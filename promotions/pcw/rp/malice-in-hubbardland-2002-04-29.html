Malice In Hubbardland

29 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/29/02 -Duke Williams is laying on the coach digging through the Hubbardville Herald when a classified advertisement catches his eye. The local Hubbardville McDonalds is hiring a store manager and entry level counter jockey.- Williams: Honey, the Micky D’s in Zombievillie is hiring!!! I’m gonna go apply. -Duke wife Jessi is in the kitchen washing dishes.- Jessi: What do you know about running a McDonalds? Williams: Come on sweetie, do you really think a manager at McDonalds practice their own management philosophy?? None of them even know what the hell college is!! Their drones who do what the McSystem tells them too. Jessi: Kinda like Nelson’s foot soldiers?? Cool titles but when you get right down too it they’re just following the manual. Williams: Bingo!! -Forty eight hours, twenty seven minutes, and eleven seconds after the last scene we witnessed. We re-join the Malice Man Duke Williams this time he’s standing in front of a man with a creepy cult like smile. No he’s not at a Scientology church but rather a job interview for the McDonalds gig.- McManager: So Mr. Williams I’ve looked over your resume and I don’t understand why a graduate of Penn State University would want to come work for a McDonalds in the small town of Hubbardville?? Williams: Well to be honest I’m settling down as a independently wealth internet entrepreneur and just want a low stress job to get me out of the house. McManager: Wealthy you say??? If you don’t mind me asking what religious denomination you belong too?? Williams: Oh no ask away!! I’m Irish Catholic but I do not practice. I don’t believe in all that the church represents. McManager: Well the reason I ask is because this town is made up mostly of Scientologists and we believe in Scientology. -Duke does his dandiest to hold back his gag reflexes as he makes the following statement.- Williams: Really?? That’s what John Trovolta is!! Battle Field Earth was probably the single best movie I have ever seen in my life!!! Do you know him? -The manager is humored.- McManager: Yes a time or two he is a very nice man!! Say Doug would you like to come join us this weekend at church? Williams: That would be an honor and a privilege. -One hundred and twelve hours, thirty seven minutes, and nineteen seconds later we rejoin Duke Williams as he starts his training to become a Scientologist. This is stage one of the TR training. – Teacher: Duke your check has cleared!! Lets get you on path to self realization and inner peace! Williams: Sounds like a jolly good time skipper!! -Duke would love to vomit right about now.- Teacher: Welcome to training you are now a TR0 in our gradient training. Would you please sit in the chair there. -Duke takes a seat in the wooden chair the teacher then sits right in front of him knee to knee.- Williams: Hey man ever hear of persoal space? Teacher: Relax, close your eyes, and clear your mind. Williams: Fine hoss, but if my zipper starts to get unbuckled I’m out. -Duke closes his eyes as creepy Scientology babble plays in the background.- Williams: How the hell am I supposed to nap with that garbage in the back ground don’t you have any Hank Williams. Teacher: Just be quite, close your eyes, and listen to the words. -Duke does so and an hour and a half later the teacher lets him stop.- Teacher: Ok now lets try stage two I need you to sit in the chair and keep your eyes open till I tell you to stop. No talking, twitching, or moving. GO -Duke sits there for about five minutes then starts to talk.- Williams: Hey skipper where’s the port I have to piss like a kid on a car trip. Teacher: FLUNK! Talking. Start Williams: Yea, yea, yea….where’s the john? Teacher: FLUNK! Talking. Start Williams: Would you be surprised if I told you this isn’t the first time I’ve flunked something? Teacher: FLUNK! Talking. Start Williams: God damn hoss, you ever hear of a dictionary?? They have new words in there to expand your vocabulary! Teacher: FLUNK! Talking. Start -Duke gives in knowing he’s not going to win this battle.- Williams: …… -Duke stays still for about fifteen minute’s then a hard booger starts to jab his nose. So he digs in to remove the poker.- Teacher: FLUNK! Body Movement. Start. Williams: I had a booger that was killing me!! Teacher: FLUNK! Talking. Start. -Duke shows the rock hard booger to the teacher.- Teacher: FLUNK! Body Movement. Start. Williams: I just wanted to show you what I was dealing with here!! FLUNK! Talking. Start. -Duke sits there for two hours with out doing anything. Finally the teacher ends the practice.- Teacher: Complete you may move. Williams: About damn time! -Twenty Three hours later we join Duke and the Teacher for round two. The teacher has Duke sit in that same wooden chair.- Teacher: Same as yesterday you will sit in the chair and stare. No movement, no talking, nothing. I will be talking to you and you need to learn to block me mentally. Williams: I’m married I can block anybody talking at me. -Duke sits still as the teacher starts the clock.- Teacher: I bet you like men don’t you pig? -Duke ignores the mans insults for fifteen minutes as they are all along the same lines.- Teacher: I’m going to come over and take your wife and do her in the ass. Williams: Really?? You want to swap wife’s, hoss? I’m game. Teacher: FLUNK! Talking. Start Williams: No really cus I’d love to do you wife in the ass! Teacher: FLUNK! Talking. Start -Duke shuts up and the process continues for two more hours.- Teacher: Welcome to TR1! Williams: Really what did I win? Teacher: The right to continue. Williams: Joy. -Duke is handed a copy of Alice and Wonderland and is asked to read the book with out laughing of showing emotion.- Williams: Don’t you have a Hustler or a Forum for me to read instead? Teacher: This is it. Williams: Jesus I need a beer. Teacher: JUST READ THE DARN BOOK. -Duke sits down and reads the book for two hours till he meets the teachers satisfaction.- Teacher: Welcome to TR2. Williams: Did I win a toaster oven? Teacher: No just the right to go on. Williams: You know it would be cooler if I won a toaster oven. Teacher: No toaster oven. Williams: Damn it. -We join Duke three hours later in his hotel room in Hubbardville. Duke opens up the drawer to the dress to find not a bible but a copy of Dianetics. Duke shakes his head in disappointment as he talks right into the camera.- Williams: What a f*cking joke!!! That’s right Kannon I’m entering your world. Alice In Wonderland?? Kind of fits in the whole irony of things. Malice In Hubbardland. -Duke laughs at his own lame words.- Williams: These simpleton’s have no idea what I’m about to do to there community. Inner sabotage is a fine art. Things that make you go hummmmm. Who in the UA is working for the Malice Crew?? Think about it Kannon you never know. I can’t wait to see you and Ellie at church once I reach the proper level. Maybe you can come over and we can study some of this science crap. Look kid your nothing but a weak minded dolt who feels the need to follow. You’re a follower in all aspects of you life you hump. -Duke takes a sniff of the desk as he takes in a line of coke.- Williams: So you want to put an unbalanced man in the ring with his back to the wall?? Six against two I bet you feel like you have balls the size of an elephant? I bet your think your super man against Pee Wee f*cking herman. Well hot shot think again. That right I want to step in the ring with all of the UA. I want to look each and every one of you choad warriors right in the face before I stick my finger in it and start swinging like a banshee. -Duke flashes a dark smile.- Williams: I know I’m going to take an ass kicking. I know some ribs will be broken. I know pain will be inflicted. I still don’t care. I’m just looking to get one good shot in on one of you bastards and break a f*cking nose and send somebody WHISKEY BENT AND HELL BOUND. Hoss I’m out. -The camera flicks off.-
