The Horror's Of Dr. No

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 20th 2002 (Mike Frampton is seen in a bright, white room. The room has rubber walls and a mirror facing in. On the opposite side of the mirror stand doctors of the mind who are studying a patient. This patient is Mike Frampton who was recently locked away for have Multiple Personalities. Mike sits in a corner with a straight jacket strapped on him. He wears no shoes or socks and his pants have no belt and are white and very baggy. Mike slowly rocks in the corner. The floor reflects the light from the ceiling towards Mike's face. Mike pushes his body to the wall. He manages to pull himself up without the help of his hands. He then slowly walks towards the mirror. He stares into his reflection. His face has stubble on it as he has not shaved in a few days. He looks at his stubble and smirks as he likes the look. Mike stares deep into his eyes. Little does he know that men in white coats are staring right back into his eyes. Mike then out of a burst of pure addrenaline headbutts the mirror. Shards of glass fly and his forehead is cut and he begins to bleed. Several men in white coats rush in with a needles. Mike is held down and sedated as the scene fades to white.) Mike Frampton: Where am I? (The scene fades back in with Mike's words. He slowly opens his eyes, still he is in a straight jacket. He is in a chair in an office. Three men in white jackets guard the door. The back of a chair stares Mike in the face. A window is directly infront of the chair.) Mike Frampton: I said, where the hell am I? (Mike kicks the desk infront of him. The chair then spins around. A man with grey hair, a moustache and a suit is sitting in the chair.) Dr. No: Mr. Frampton, I am Doctor No. Mike Frampton: Doctor No? Why am I here? And why are you calling me Mr. Frampton? Dr. No: Mr. Frampton, I think we all know the reason you are here. Mike Frampton: Not I. Toss me a freaking bone here, Mr. No. Dr. No: That is Doctor No! (Doctor No presses a button and a metal device straps Mike's hands to the chair. Doctor No then presses another button. A wave electricity is sent through Mike's body.) Dr. No: Mr. Frampton, welcome to the shock treatment facility for the clinically insane! Mike Frampton: But, I am not insane Mr. No. I... (Dr. No presses a button and Mike is again electricuted.) Dr. No: I tell you once and I tell you one thousand times, Doctor No. Mike Frampton: Alright Doctor No. But do you know who I am? I am one of the most powerful men in the world. I do not know who Mr. Frampton is but I am not him. (Doctor No's hand hovers over the shock button.) Dr. No: Then who, are you then? Mike Frampton: I am the President of the greatest country in the world! Dr. No: Cuba? Mike Frampton: Of course not Cuba. You communist pig. I am George W. Bush Jr. I think you should be more careful with that button. Any minute now this place will be stormed with CIA agents and Secret Service men. Dr. No: Really now? So George W. if that is your real name, which it's not, what year is this? Mike Frampton: What kind of question is that? And why should I answer? Dr. No: I am the one who asks the questions here! (Dr. No presses the button yet again. Mike is yet again electricuted. He jumps in his chair but is held in with his restraints.) Mike Frampton: It is the year 2002 dingus! I was elected in the year 2000 after a long voting problem in Florida. Darn old people can't read! Dr. No: What year were you sworn into office? Mike Frampton: 2001. Dr. No: What month? Mike Frampton: January. (Doctor No reclines in his chair thinking over what Mike has just said. Mike is about to interupr but remembers how Doctor No likes to shock people. Both men stare when Doctor No slams down on the button. Mike is yet again shocked.) Mike Frampton: But I didn't even say anything. (Doctor No presses the button yet again.) Dr. No: I don't like you staring at me. Tell me. Have you ever, EVER, heard the name Mike Frampton? Mike Frampton: Of course I have. Dr. No: How? Mike Frampton: He is a famous guitarist, everyone knows that. He can make his guitar talk man! He is skilled! Dr. No: That is Peter Frampton! (Doctor slames on the button again shocking Mike.) Mike Frampton: Stop that! Dr. No: You are not George W. Bush. Mike Frampton: Yes I am! Dr. No: No you aren't. Mike Frampton: Am! Dr. No: Aren't! Mike Frampton: AM! Dr. No: AREN'T! (Mike Frampton clenches his fists. Suddenly he shakes as if he is undergoing a transfermation. He then stops shaking and looks around as if seeing the office for the first time.) Mike Frampton: Where am I? Dr. No: Cut the crap. Mike Frampton: Who the hell are you? Why am I here? Someone answer me! Dr. No: Calm down Mr. Bush, that is what you like to be called is it not? Mike Frampton: Man you're crazy, I am Mike Frampton. PCW Superstar. Big Daveylicious' tag team partner and I am receiving a Rising Star title shot at Spring Fling. Now who are you? Dr. No: Are you now? Mike Frampton: Yeah I am now let me the hell out of these chains before I rip your throat out. (Doctor No pushes the button. Mike is shocked. He looks down and up to Doctor No. He stares a hole in him. He then begins to pump himself up. He exerts all his energy and rips apart the metal that is holding him in place. The three men guarding the door move in with a needle.) Mike Frampton: Now I am leaving this place and you can't stop me. (One of the large men in white leaps at Mike. Mike side steps him and throws him across the table into Doctor No. A second man charges at Mike. Mike ducks and flips the man into the air. The man crashes down through the oak table sending splinters everywhere. The last man with the needle stops in his tracks. Mike then bolts at him and tackles him. Mike grabs the needle and walks over to Doctor No. He then jams the needle in his arm. Mike then runs out into the hall as the scene fades out.)
