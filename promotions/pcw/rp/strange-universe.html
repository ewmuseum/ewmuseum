Strange Universe…

Date: Recently... [Fade In] Location: The Green Lodge.Time: Mid-Afternoon.Date: Unknown. Comic stepped onto the balcony and looked out over the garden below. There they were, training as usual. He’d always seen training as a huge part of his life, but Sydney Nerge and The Gadget took it to its limits. He sighed, looking up into the light blue sky above and rested his weight on the hilt of his newly taken sword. His armour, highlighted in certain areas with shades of emerald, was still worn from his confrontation with The Black Messiah earlier in the day, and blood - a deep shade of red - stained the blade where he had managed to slay the dastardly Violent Kajj, the former holder of the weapon. Comic looked back down at the training warriors in the lush gardens and turned to his left. Here, the balcony stemmed out into a thin stairway leading into the Green Lodge’s gardens. He couldn’t be fathomed with training today, not after such a heated battle with The Black Messiah and Violent Kajj, but he needed to speak with Sydney. He was lucky to be alive to be fair, and was extremely surprised that he even made it out of the woods with all his parts intact. Violent Kajj had held the prestigious weapon in his dark hands for a long time now. But at last it had been restored to the side of good, or at least a vague version of ‘good’ judging on how you considered the element in coalition with Comic. “Sydney!” he called out as he made his way towards the duelling pair. Sydney Nerge, the holder of the Holy blade, waved a hand for The Gadget to cease her attack. Upon seeing this, she did, and looked on as Comic approached with a frown. She hadn’t known either of the men too long, but she knew already that seeing Comic without some sort of sick grin on his face was odd. “Yes, my friend?” said Nerge, resting his weight on the hilt of his training sword. The blade sank slightly into the earth as he did. He never fought in training with the Holy sword. He was a huge man, and the odd thing about him was his sight – and the fact that he lacked it. He was blind. But he didn’t see this as any sort of disability. His hearing and sense of smell were amazingly well tuned, and thus were the gains of not being able to see. He considered these two senses his greatest allies, much like the people he grew comfortable around. Comic had yet to fully gain his respect and trust, but he lived with him. He put up with him. He accepted that without Comic’s help, his battle with Titutopolis and The Black Messiah and David Trace was inevitable useless. The Gadget was there for support in his fight too, and she, much like Comic, was a good fighter - perhaps not as established in the land, but nevertheless still a good fighter. “Our fight with the Unholy this Tuesday worries me, Sydney,” Comic sighed, hanging his head and wiping the growing sweat from his brow. “Our enemies are strong - perhaps too strong - and our lives look doubtful to exist after this battle. I must find some source of strength, my friend, or else I shan’t fight.” Nerge rested a hand on Comic’s shoulder. The warmth of the warrior’s armour was extreme, a combination of the sun’s temperament and the warrior’s own emotions. He, like The Gadget, didn’t know Comic too well, but Sydney felt his ally’s worry. “Fear not, my friend. You know too well that I have defeated Titutopolis many times before! Why, there’s no need to fret! My ally, I can smell your fear - you shouldn’t agonize yourself like this!” “I’m not afraid to fight, Nerge, I’m simply concerned that I’ll lose my blade in the heat of battle. This newly won trophy of war means a lot to me. I had to go through two of this land’s greatest warriors to take this sword and I don’t wish to lose it so soon!” Comic’s voice was stern, serious. Before he had come to reside at the Green Lodge, he had been known for his foolish and somewhat amusing ways. But today, hours after his bloody battle, his lack of seriousness had vanished. Seldom did he act like this. “What you must realize, Comic, is that we won’t lose the battle nor your glinting sword; you’re with us after all, right, Sydney?” said The Gadget confidently, nudging Nerge in the ribs playfully. Sydney chuckled a little, nodding with concurrence. “And we’re much better than those Satanic freaks.” She concluded. “Indeed.” Nerge continued, “So don’t vex.” Comic sighed, throwing his blade to the floor. “I’m not afraid! Why, I have already beaten The Black Messiah to take THAT damned blade! As I’ve said already, I just don’t want to go into this fight a winner and come out a goddamn LOSER!” The Gadget looked towards Sydney. The great fighter’s face was calm, even after the aggravated words of Comic. He was always like this. Nothing ever seemed to annoy him; he always responded to emotion with a lack of it. Slowly a smile crept over his lips. His dark face, partially protected by a tiny black piece of cloth wrapped over his mouth, beamed with delight. His eyes, ever white, looked on vainly as he addressed the jester-like character, “I do wish you’d calm down,” he said, turning his back on the man and walking towards The Gadget. Calmly taking her sword, he spun around, leapt into the air and decapitated one of the practice targets. He dropped back down to the ground, his white cape folding gently on the ground. Comic folded his arms, unimpressed. “Train with us if you’re so worried about losing your precious sword.” Said Nerge, rising. Comic scoffed, “I’ve just had a battle, squire. I don’t need to train.” The others paid him a blank stare, “…Yet, anyway.” He finished. “I’m going back to the lodge to rest up. If you want me, I’ll be in my bedroom.” He picked his precious sword back up and began to move away from the pair with a frown. Suddenly he turned back to them, “And if I lose this blade in combat, I’m blaming you guys.” It wasn’t long before Comic fell out of sight, safely residing back inside the ancient mansion. Sydney turned to the perplexed Gadget and sighed. “Such a mixed up young man. I can’t understand his way of thinking.” He raised his sword again and smiled, his attention seemingly returned to training, “Anyway, come at me, and I’ll show you a few moves.” The young woman’s head fell to the side, “What do you mean? I don’t have a weapon.” Sydney let out a laugh. “What sort of excuse is that? Sometimes a good fighter needs to learn how to defend himself/herself without the aid of a blade. Why, I remember a few weeks back when Titutopolis and I last met in battle, and that old dog swiped my blade from my hands. Why, I didn’t know what he was going to do next, so I swept his feet from beneath him, took his own sword and—” he paused, contemplating what happened next. But he failed to conclude. “So, what happened next?” queried The Gadget after a few seconds. “Oh, it doesn’t matter.” “No, cummon, what happened next?” She was determined to find out what happened after that, but Sydney didn’t seem to wish to comply with her question. He was a mysterious fellow, full of shadow and consumed within a blanket of question. You really didn’t know when to take him seriously. Like Comic, he was an amusing character. A cynical character; a man of one-liners. But he was honest, caring – perhaps too caring sometimes – and honourable. He didn’t believe in cheating his way out of a fight. And the fact that he was blind didn’t stop him from doing anything he wanted to. He was most certainly a free spirit. “No, no, it really doesn’t matter. Seriously.” He said, taking up a defensive stance. “Now, on with the training; I want you to attack me, and take special notice of my movements.” Unarmed, and definitely unprepared, The Gadget huffed a deep sigh, screamed loudly, and sprinted towards her trainer. This was very unfair. He had a sword, she didn’t, and for some unknown reason, she was the one attacking. In one fell swoop of the sword’s hilt, Sydney knocked her to the ground. “You need to be more alert, Gadget. You ran in on me then like you were some sort of idiot. And what if you needed to take your opponent by surprise? I guess you’d run in screaming?” He sighed, offering the young woman a hand, “Come on, get up.” She grabbed his wrist, pulled him down towards her and drove her feet into his stomach. She lifted him off the floor and flipped him over onto his back with a thud. From behind her, she could hear the mighty warrior laughing. “Good, good. You’re learning. But you’ll have to do better than that this Tuesday when we finally meet those beastly cretins in close combat. Ok?” Gadget lifted herself back up and nodded, dusting herself off. “Yeah, I know.” “Good. So help me up.” He raised his arm to hers and she obediently grabbed his hand. Within a few seconds she was back down on the floor, winded. “What did I just tell you, Gadget?” She clenched her chest, trying to speak. However, she couldn’t. “Be…more…alert. That was the easiest trick in the book.” He dusted himself down and began to walk away, “So, I’ll see you out here later. I’m going for something to eat in the meantime.” Five minutes past before The Gadget found her legs again. Gaining her footing once more, she sighed, looked up at the baby blue sky and then looked at herself. She was full of muck. Without her armour, she was pretty small. But she was fast, strong and flexible. These were good attributes for a warrior; well, for a certain type of warrior. She gazed over at the Green Lodge and stretched tiresomely. She was exhausted now. All that training had left her in dire need of some sleep. Sydney was a very concentrated fighter; she had seen that many times through previous battles, and through previous sessions of training. But so was his great nemesis Titutopolis. The only differences between the pair were their alignments to good and evil, and their beliefs on honour. Titutopolis was a cunning, crafty man, whilst Nerge was a much more honourable warrior. They were more alike than they knew, or would let on, however. Though Titutopolis was a small man, he matched Sydney’s size with his speed and unearthly agility. But Sydney was no typical ‘big man’; he had as much speed and agility too. Come Tuesday afternoon, the two would meet with their respective allies besides them. What a battle it would be. The Gadget smiled, considering this, and walked away, running to get to the lodge quicker… [Fade Out]
