Flying High

5 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 5, 2003 [Look at those tasty waves out there. They aren't waves that most surfers cream themselves over, but they are the kind of waves that an experienced surfer can ride without much concentration. As 'Totally Tubular' Tommy stands on his private beach watching the sunset gleam off of the ocean, he really wishes that he hadn't hung up his surfboard permanently. The waves are made only for the beginning surfer as they could handle these waves with no problem, but an advanced rider like Tommy could pretty much ride these waves in his sleep. This is why he loves the late evening for surfing because it seems like you can surf forever and nothing can take you out. It's one of the most bodacious feelings in the world to be on a wave, skimming your hand across the water as a giant rush of waves spills just inches over head. The rhythm of the waves and the cool evening breeze bring such an inner peace to one's totally radical soul. Tommy's blonde locks flap in the air as his baby blue eyes focus on the ocean before him. The sun has now become a reflection into the water as if the Pacific Ocean is about to swallow it whole. His tanned skin looks absolutely golden from the evening sky beating down onto him. Tommy wishes that one of those totally bodacious photographers were here to make this a 'Kodak moment' but he knows that there aren't. It begins to make him sad but that's when his mind begins to think about his PCW World Title. A sigh of relief is released from his body and a smile slowly cracks onto his face. He is, probably, one of the most unstoppable champions in PCW history. It's a goal he had set out to do since he stepped into a wrestling ring all those years ago. He dedicated his life to becoming one of the best wrestlers in the world and about one month ago it came to pass as he captured one of the most prestigious titles in wrestling. So many legends and radical dudes have held the title in the past, and now Tommy is making them all look like absolute crap with his totally bodacious reign. His smile is cracking wider now as he thinks about the PCW fans. They once embraced him as their hero, a compadre who could stand up for all that was bogus in PCW. Tommy finally figured out that the key to success is that he can't think about what the fans want. He needs to only think about what he really wants, and what he really wanted was to succeed. Once he stopped listening to the fans and doing his own thing he began to win big matches. It used to be a totally heinous curse that he and Harry could win but never when it counted the most. Ever since Tommy embraced who he truly was it was only a matter of time before he started winning those huge matches. He was rising to be the most bodacious dude in wrestling and with the win of the PCW Title he has finally proven that. It's just too bad that there aren't any bodacious opponents to take him on for the title] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: That sunset is far out, man. [Tommy nods with the smile still cracked onto his face. Harry now stands beside his brother with his newly purchased Iron Maiden T-shirt, his usual black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. Harry lets the warmth of the setting sun beam into his body making every cell inside bounce around madly. It sends this tingling feeling through him and when he closes his eyes he feels like he's flying through the air. That's what the effect of marijuana will do to a man, but Harry gets the good stuff. He doesn't bother with that nickel and dime stuff that gets the cretins high. Harry only works with the top of the line stuff that hits the system immediately, and it hits it HARD! There is nothing better than getting completely stoned before the sun goes down because if feels like your actually out in space watching the earth rotate. If Harry concentrates enough he can actually feel the earth spinning on it's axis and he's able to feel the gravitational pull onto his body. That's why when the warmth of the sun hits him he's able to let go of gravity of give himself that flying feeling. The sun is his power and flying his is most far out ability. Sort of like 'Super Heavy Metal' Harry] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Saying that this sunset is far out is an understatement, dude. I'd say this sunset is totally bodacious in a far out way! Harry: Yeah, man. [For some reason Tommy's mind wanders to Venneman Pendragon. Or as Tommy would say, 'Venom Pentagram'. He's thinking about the after match beating he gave Venneman. He had already defeated him in the heat of battle but then just decided that he would keep on hurting the large opponent. Tommy realizes that he's watching the sunset, feeling at peace, but he doesn't really understand this remorse that he's feeling. He put that dude in the hospital and he is not going to be able to wrestle for a while. If he would have kept going the dude could be in a wheelchair for the rest of his life. Tommy begins to think if his past enemies got their hands onto him if they would end up putting HIM in a wheelchair. What if someone like Rent-A-Hero or...Skylar Green got a chance to do that to Tommy? It could be a total disaster for the Tubular One. Tommy knows deep down inside his soul that he is not a chicken, that he isn't a coward, and especially yellow! But when one compadre makes a ton of lethal foes like that things could get totally out of hand. In fact, Tommy feels he might need a hand in making sure that he doesn't end up worse than Venneman Pendragon, or Vendekamps Penisdrag] Tommy: Harry, do you ever wonder how that Venom dude is reacting to my totally bodacious beat down to him at Spring Fling? Harry: I don't know, man. Tommy: But if you were to put yourself into his shoes for a few minutes, how would you feel? Harry: I'd hurt a lot, man. Tommy: TOTALLY!! HAHA! But I mean mentally, not physically. Harry: Well, I'd probably want to see your eyes burnt out of your skull and then stab you to death with my far out sword, man. [Tommy just keeps staring at the sunset as he shivers at the thought of being stabbed by a sword. If his own brother would even think something like that, then imagine what a dude like Pendragon is thinking. He could be plotting something extremely heavy against Tommy or others in PCW could be plotting already. There could be all those new dudes who came in plotting something against the gnarly World Champion. Tommy realizes that he needs to cope with the fact that he is a marked man wearing the highest title in the federation. He already is setting an example of what a champion should be, but he needs to honor the other side of his championship, which is to take on all comers. He needs to show these dudes that he can dominate anybody he steps into the ring with. What he shouldn't do is beat down his opponent after a totally bodacious victory, but it's REALLY fun to do it!] Harry: I wouldn't want to do that to you, man. I'd want to give you hot chicks who feed you grapes, but I was just saying what that Ventalilation guy would do. Tommy: I know, dude. I just needed an outside opinion on it. I never really thought of the totally bogus consequences that beat down would have made. Harry: You shouldn't be thinking about consequences, man. You should be thinking about your upcoming match on Turmoil! Dorn Tracer and Violence Jack are freakin' crazy! Tommy: Dude, your absolutely right about that. Even though it is non-title, which is a decision I did not make, I have to watch my back because if one of these dudes pins me it could mean a lot of horrible things. I know with these triple threat matches that any dude can come out looking totally bodacious when he's really just a heinous wrestler. Harry: Are you going to go by the usual training or are you going to go extra for something like this, man? [Tommy stands silent for a few moments, taking in the final moments of the sunset. He can already feel the darkness taking over his body and the sudden drop of the temperature. He debates about his current training which is really helping him think more clearly, but he feels that he needs more. He's hit a peak in his life, which some would call their prime, and he needs to train hard, if not HARDER! If he takes himself to the limits then there is no telling what he'll be capable of in life. Tommy smiles once again as he realizes he's just ENTERING his prime and he's already the most bodacious thing since Sponge Bob Squarepants] Tommy: I think we're going to need to take the training to the next level, Har. I get most excellent satisfaction out of my current training, but there is always room for more. Harry: Yeah, man. So, it means that I'm going to be putting on my GOOD Iron Maiden T-shirt tonight. Tommy: Totally, dude. We're going to hit that club tonight and we're going to pick up the most bodacious babes that God has ever created. Then we'll bring them back to the gnarly beach house and that's when the new training will begin! I honestly believe that I've been holding myself back when it comes to the babes, dude. When I really want something I can have it. Harry: Yeah, man. Me too. [Tommy puts his arm around his brother and smiles at the darkened sky of California] Tommy: This is the beginning of something new, dude. The beginning of something great! The beginning of something bodacious! We're totally going to rule!! Harry: Yeah, man. Fade to black
