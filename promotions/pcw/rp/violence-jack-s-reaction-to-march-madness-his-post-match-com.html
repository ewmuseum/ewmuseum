Date: Who cares, just not no-showing Turmoil, which I&#039;ve no intention of winning. The following is from an interview conducted by Cal Seaver, interviewing Violence Jack while he was being attended to by medical technicians after his match with Skylar Green. Cal Seaver enters the infirmary; opening the door, the screaming of Violence Jack fills the outside hallway and echoes through the arena. The door is closed behind Cal. Standing back carefully not to let the spurting blood touch his gray Armani suit and Cole-Haan dress shoes, Cal Seaver holds a tape recorder in Jack's direction while a camera shoots the scene. Cal: Violence Jack, that was... a match, indeed. To call it grueling would be a euphemism. What is going through your mind right now? Violence Jack may not even be listening to Cal Seaver. It is possible he doesn't even hear him. He's been crying out in pain the entire time. Violence Jack: HOLY SHIT! HOLY FUCK BITCH! SHIT FUCKING SHIT! SHIT! SHIT! Cal Seaver brings the tape recorder back to his own mouth. Cal: Indeed. Skylar took you to your limit, far more than any opponent before. Have your opinions of him changed at all? Jack continues on his string of profanities. Violence Jack: FUCKING BITCH!! HOLY FUCKING FUCK COCK SHIT FUCK! Seaver seems not to mind the cursing that would make Kevin Smith and Jason Mewes blush and continues the interview. Cal: And what about your loss of your place in March Madness? Violence Jack still expounds upon the pain, giving it words. Violence Jack: IT FUCKING HURTS! FUCK! SONNUVABITCH! ARGH! OW! OH FUCK! FUCK!! Cal Seaver nods. Cal: Interesting. You are aware that you will have to face Dante Jackson for the Continental Title on this coming week's Friday Fusion, right? Violence Jack: SHIT FUCK SHIT SHIT SHIT SHIT SHIT!! Cal: Do you feel that you'll be healed enough to compete then? Violence Jack: SHIT FUCKING SHIT BITCH ASS BITCH CUNT FUCK SHIT SHIT! Cal Seaver turns off the tape recorder, but not before getting in one final question. Cal: So, Jack, is there any chance for a rematch? Violence Jack: HOLY SHIT! HOLY FUCK BITCH! SHIT FUCKING SHIT! SHIT! SHIT! FUCKING BITCH!! HOLY FUCKING FUCK COCK SHIT FUCK! FUCK, IT HURTS! FUCK! SHIT FUCKING SHIT! SONNUVABITCH! ARGH! HOLY FUCK BITCH! OW FUCK! FUCK!! SHIT FUCK SHIT SHIT! FUCK COCK SHIT FUCK! SHIT SHIT SHIT!! SHIT FUCKING SHIT BITCH ASS BITCH CUNT FUCK SHIT SHIT! Backing out of the door, Cal Seaver shuts it behind him and the cameraman. Cal Seaver: Hmm... I think that went well.
