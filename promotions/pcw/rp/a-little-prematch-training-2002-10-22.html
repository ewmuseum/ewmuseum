A little prematch training

22 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/22/2002 *The scene opens up in a dark, murky alley somewhere in Brunei. It has been raining for several days, leaving puddles of water all around the cracked asfault ground. Laying about on the asfault, are several old pieces of trash, about a dozen rats, and climbing out of a large green dumpster a skinny Asian bum. This bum, wearing rags, starts to sift through the trash on the ground looking for any morsel of food he can find for nourishment. Suddenly, from the street, comes the one, the only, the legendary Jon Kano. He must have been walking in the rain, as his hair is sopping wet, but neatly pulled back in a ponytail. Kano is not wearing his trademark fine 10,000 dollar suit, but jeans, a 'REAL LEGEND' t-shirt, and a black leather jacket. He approaches the bum, who is still on the ground looking for food. The bum keeps searching until he reaches Kano's feet. The bum follows Kano's body with his eyes, stands up, brushes himself off, and begins to shuffle off with his head hanging low.* Kano: No, come back. *Kano reaches out with his right hand and spins the bum around.* Kano: How would you like five hundred dollars? *The bum gives Kano a very confused look.* Kano: You speak English? Bum: Yes, but I have learned one thing in my life, kind sir, is that there is no such thing as a free lunch. Kano: Quite true. You see, what is your name? Bum: Chan Ho-Li. Kano: I am Jon Kano, professional wrestler and living legend in Primetime Championship Wrestling. *Kano and Chan shake hands.* Kano: You see we are in town doing a show and I am a little rusty for the match I am in. It is an Extreme Title match, where the only rule is that there are no rules. Weapons are commonly used in these matches. I am a wrestler, not a brawler. I am a world-class athlete, too. I can rise to the occasion, and I figured people in your situation probably are experts in fighting like that. Chan: I guess you could say that, sir. Kano: I need some advice on the subject. Chan: Very well. It sounds to me like you need to be an innovator with anything you can get your hands with. Kano: So like that stick over there? Chan: Correct, sir. You would take that stick and where would you hit your opponent with it? Kano: Easy, the head. Chan: Wrong. Take your opponents' legs out, they can't defend themselves if they can't stand. Kano: How true. Can I ask you a quick question? Chan: Sure. Kano: You seem like a relatively intellegent man, how did you end up in this situation? Chan: I was once a respected member of the community until I began to not care about others. I lost every friend I had, and then I lost my job. My wife left me, my children haven't seen me in seven years, and I have been living on the streets since 1995. Kano: That is sad. Chan: Don't feel sorry for me, I am where I am because of my own stupidity and selfishness. Back to you, just always be prepared to defend yourself and never believe that you have the match in the bag. Kano: Sounds good to me. Thanks. *Kano reaches into his pocket and pulls out a roll of hundred dollar bills. He counts out five and hands it to the excited bum, Chan Ho-Li.* Chan: Oh thank you, sir. Kano: No problem, but wait. Is this how I am supposed to do it. *Kano grabs a trash can lid and bashes the bum over the head with it. Kano then slams Chan's head into the brick wall of the alley. Chan is bleeding and screaming in pain, but Kano has no mercy and hooks him and KANOPLEXES HIM ONTO THE ASFAULT!!!!!!* Kano: Seems to me like I got that down pretty well. And I think on Turmoil it will be my time to show to everyone once again why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!!! *Kano kicks Chan a couple times in the ribs as the scene fades to black.*
