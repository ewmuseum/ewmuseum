Date: not entered *The following is a writing made by Skylar Green in the weeks prior to the initiation of his issues with Violence Jack. The reader should notice the attitude of Skylar Green as he wrote this piece, the tone of his words and the relative carefree feeling that manifested itself in unstructured discussion about nothing important.* I was sitting in Coral Square Mall the other day across from the Funzone, where they have an arcade. I could hear the games that they had in there. Sounded like recent old ones like Killer Instinct, Time Crisis, and others that I used to play when I could see. I wandered in, just to hear what was going on. There were a number of people my age there who were talking about the good old days, with games like Contra and Ninja Gaiden on the original Nintendo Entertainment System. One man with a raspy voice who smelled of pizza claimed that he was the inventor of the Konami master code. At some length, the conversation shifted to the types of games they would make if they were game publishers. I didn't participate, I couldn't really. None of them believed that I wasn't born blind and that I used to play video games. But they had some interesting ideas. Here's what I remember. 1) Pokemon Hunter There was a conspiracy theorist there (or maybe he just saw that Chin-Pokomon episode of South Park, go figure) who claimed that Pokemon was just an attempt by the Japanese to take over America. I don't believe him, the Japanese could take over America if they wanted to, especially since the country screwed itself over with the nuclear non-proliferation act and since they don't want to use nukes anymore. What are we going to do, shoot the same missles at them that they have at us? Anyway, the idea of this was simple. Ash (we're talking Bruce Campbell from Evil Dead Trilogy, here) must use his shotgun and chainsaw, plus his attack-slave Pikachu, to hunt down all the Pokemon summoned to the Earth to destroy humanity by the Necronomicon, that same punkass book that's always causing trouble for people. Pokemon -- Gotta kill 'em all. 2) Free Clinic Mario Remember Dr. Mario? He was fighting viruses in a test tube with megavitamins that he'd shove down there, and the color of the virus and two portions of a matching pill had to be lined up by threes horizontally or vertically to eliminate it? That was a good game, nice Tetris spin-off (though the original Tetris is still the best; the 3-D versions just went to far). Anyway, Sho Miyamoto (Shigeru Miyamoto's 'cousin' who lived in a nearby low-income neighborhood) suggested Free Clinic Mario. It runs on the same principle of Dr. Mario, but is more graphic. Dr. Mario must battle various venereal diseases, along with malaria, botulism, and a host of other diseases, armed with Robitussin and Flintstones chewables. 'The total budget is $15 per person, and if that amount is exceeded, the person is kicked out to die. 10 deaths in a day and you're put out of business because the Crips are gonna come in and pump yo' ass with lead fo' not savin' G-Dogg.' Or something. I'd rather not try to remember exactly what this wackjob said. The chewables come in the standard Fred (orange), Wilma (blue), Pebbles (red), Barney (green), Betty (yellow), and Bam Bam (white). Each disease needs a certain mix of Flintstones chewables to be eradicated, some mixes more complex than others. The viruses need to be destroyed quickly or they'll multiply and fill up the screen. If the viruses gather up too much and you're in danger of losing the patient, you can give them a bottle of Robitussin, which will cure everything (not really, it's more of a placebo effect, but they won't die when they walk out). Of course you only have one bottle, and give them 9/10 of it each time to wipe out all the viruses. After that, the bottle is filled with water to replace the missing syrup. After 3 uses, the Robitussin is useless until you get funding for another bottle in a month. This guy was weird, yes. Interesting game concept, though. 3) Command & Conquer 2001 A man identifying himself only as 'The Caliph' offered this game, in which players are given a real Pacific island, Latin American, African, or Middle Eastern country to lead to prominence in the world military theater. He didn't go into any more detail than that. He threw a smoke capsule onto the ground and ran off. 4) Smurfect Dark Smurfette combats an intergalactic conspiracy to destroy the Smurf race. She uses a number of Smurfons designed for dispatching expendable targets, and her high-tech Smurfvices will help her in her tasks. The ultra-communist Papa Smurf gives her assignments from the center of the collective, the Smurfington Institute. 5) Scared Straight A game where you input your picture and vital stats and watch as your character gets raped, beaten, and killed in prison. 6) Super Slash Bros I shudder to repeat the concept, I'll just list who the guy said would be in the game. Leonardo and Raphael from The Ninja Turtles, Jack the Ripper, Jason, Freddie Krueger, Tusk from Killer Instinct 2, OJ Simpson, Zorro, King Arthur, Angelina Jolie (she likes playing with butterfly knives), Death, the 3 Musketeers, Ash (from Evil Dead), and Raziel (Legacy of Kain: Soul Reaver). Again, I will not give the concept of this game. But looking at the cast, what they'll do is pretty self explanatory. 7) Nothing 64 You get nothing, you pay $64. This is a tax-deductible write-off, if you want it to be. But essentially you're just handing the store your money and walking away empty-handed. 8) Svenmue Remember Shenmue? Yeah, like that. Only different. Set in Sweden, a Swedish man must uncover his father's killer and avenge his death, using the secret art of strudel combat. Settings include Iron Chef, the set of the Baywatch Reunion, and an old German airship. 9) Crazy Ambulance You follow around Axel from Crazy Taxi. Whenever he causes a crash, you pick up the victims from the crash sites and take them to a nearby hospital. 10) Jean-Claude Van Damme's 'Quest' All aspects of a challenging game are eliminated because, as you are playing as Van Damme, you cannot lose. Though there are 10 stages and 80 fights along the way, you get to the last boss in 5 minutes. He then beats the crap out of you for 3 hours before allowing you to kick him in the face three times and win the game. This game is so easy that even if you set down the controller and walk away, you'll still win. 11) BRF (British Royal Family) Rumble Various wrestlers from around the world square off in a huge tournament to be declared the heir to the throne by being inducted into the British royal family. 12) Survivor Brought to you by the creators of Highlander, Duncan McLeod romas the island with a sword, taking the heads of his fellow competitors for the prize of $1 million. There can be only one SURVIVOR.
