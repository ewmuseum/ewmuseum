Stockholm, or Something Like It.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered The sun always shone. The skies were always clear. I was never left for want or desire. My days were spent idling away at menial tasks that even the most un-gifted of trade school drop-outs could master in a few minutes. There were the lengthy shopping extravaganzas, where my and my sisterhood would fly to a prime retail district and spend days buying on my benefactor’s credit. We would binge and purge on the finest meals in the most exquisite restaurants in not only the Valley, but the entire world. I traveled, seeing the wonders of the world, all served to me on a silver platter and provided with a smile and a wink. Paris. Milan. London. Moscow. Oahu. Tokyo. No place is now foreign. Men worshiped me, begging for my attentions. In the lesser countries, tribes not accustomed to my beauty would often proclaim me as goddess, lifting me up upon thrones of gold, offering me to select from their treasures. This effect was not lost on home soils, as the cheering throngs of the arenas, there to root on their favorite wrestling entity, were turned into slobbering burlesques simply by my appearance. I would strut down the ramp, do a little turn and wave, and blow a few kisses. And they ate it up. Life was perfect. And excruciatingly dull. The intense power I held over these simple-minded Neanderthals and the meaninglessness of the rest of my existence gnawed at what was left of my mind. Oh, I know what they all say. Us blondes are all bubble heads, but it’s not true. It is forced upon us because… it all becomes too easy. I became robotic, going through the motions and turning the charm on and off when needed. But without needs… without wants… I began to lose the ambition to even do that much. Soon, the heaping attentions of men became inconsequential. The revealed secrets of Egyptian pyramids were little more than trivia. The finest cuisine from world-renowned chefs tasted like ashes in my mouth. Or maybe it was more of a honey glaze? But the point is life became boring. STUPID boring. That’s when HE came into my life. He came from nowhere, literally sweeping me off my feet. Speeding from the wild, blue yonder on his jet-sled, and plucked me from my never-ending torment of opulence and whisked me away into a world of danger and intrigue. Tossed over his broad leathery shoulder, I was helpless against his powers. Sure, his skin was green and scaly. Sure, his teeth were elongated, to the point where you could call them fangs. Yeah, maybe he tossed a few grenades around the city in fits of rage. But deep inside, I, and I alone, saw the scared little boy, hurting, inside him. I saw his desperate cry for help in the form of a kidnapping amidst a diabolical plot to hold Big Alan Steele at bay while he plotted world domination via nuclear holocaust. I saw his inner beauty. So I tried to reach out to him – show him that someone in this great, big, blue ball known as Earth cared about him as a person. I tried to show him that despite his tendencies for murder and mayhem, he was capable of love… and being loved. He resisted at first. Gun to my head, Knife to my throat. Suspended, bound and gagged over a roiling vat of acid. Threats of torture. Threats of dismemberment. Threats of disemboweling. He even tied to the warhead of his biggest nuclear missile, screaming about ‘finally ridding himself of that squawking psycho!’ I knew I was getting through to him by the way the rope wasn’t as tight. I could feel the love behind his death threats. Everything was coming together beautifully… until HE came BACK into my life. I cried out in protest. I screamed in his defense. All to no avail. From my vantage point high above, strapped to the ticking nuclear warhead poised for launch into San Jose, I watched in helpless horror as that cretin… that buffoon… that numbskull… BIG ALAN STEELE break into my honey’s inner sanctum and battle the storm troopers. That big oaf was trying to ruin everything! He waded through the swarming guards, swinging his powerful arms left and right, tossing them aside like they were rag dolls. Sweetie’s expensive equipment, equipment that he had to rob a LOT of banks to find the capital for, was getting destroyed by the flying bodies and the intensity of the fight. ‘Hi-Keeba’ this and ‘Hi-Keeba’ that… that Greek idiot was laying waste to the plans of my future husband! Finally, the most handsome man in the world came out to personally deal with that interloper, Steele. A great battle ensued. Explosions rocked the secret hideout. Hubby flew gracefully on his jet-sled, laughing that infectious laugh of his as he flung grenades at the self-appointed man-god. The battle raged on for what seemed like hours, the momentum of the fight swinging perilously to each combatant. I cheered as Steele dangled perilously from a catwalk. I cringed as my man’s sled was destroyed by a redirected grenade from Steele. It was terrible! Big Al gained the upper hand and forced my beau’s hand to play his trump card. The nuclear warhead was activated… the rocket rumbling to life underneath me. I thanked God for the opportunity to assist my love as the warhead was jettisoned from it’s silo, speeding across the perfect tropical sky from the secret island lair. Once again, everything was perfect in the world… Until I saw that simpleton, Steele, clinging to the rear stabilizing fin of the missile!! I mean, what a jerk! So, here we are – me tied to the missile with that moron crawling up the shaft. To make a long story short, he somehow managed to make it up the length of the missile and release my bonds, He fiddled with the controls for a bit, but I doubt that half-wit could figure out a poptart, let alone that piece of art designed by my one and only. He grabbed me most rudely, and leaped off, plunging us into the ocean. He turned around and took the entire force of us hitting the water, but not enough for my liking because he was still breathing. A Coast Guard rescue ship fished us out of the briny deep which brings us to the now. My hate for him grows daily as I find myself back into the routine that numbed my soul. I had tasted something different… and I liked it. For the first time in my life I knew true love – the star-crossed love of Romeo and Juliet. I felt their pains at being apart. I felt the sympathy pains as Steele laid into my hubby. And now I languish as he does – my prison being life; his being Arkham Insane Asylum. All because of Big Alan Steele. He made me what I am today… this seething ball of hate. And now he shall pay… DEARLY! “BWAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!” “Uh… Candi? Me and a few of the other Graces were talking… and… well, we are worried about you. That whole kidnapping experience has changed you somehow…” Candi growls menacingly and glares daggers at the frightened Suzi. Suzi was right: Candi HAS changed. Dramatically. While the Graces usually are stylishly casual in appearance, Candi is dressed head to toe in tin foil. She hasn’t bathed in days. Her hair is wild and unkempt. She works feverishly with a length of trilene rope, six tampons and a long expired box of a dozen eggs. Her face twitches as she snaps at her Grace compatriot. Candi: “Shut up and get me more tin foil or pay DEARLY! BWHAHAHA!!” Suzi cries out in fright, sprinting out of the room. Candi begins to mumble to herself, rocking on her hindquarters as she fashions the tampons through the eggs, tying them all together like a string of firecrackers. She holds it up, admiring the craftsmanship. Candi: “Yes… this utility belt shall serve me well in my fight against my arch-nemesis, Big Alan Steele! I’ll teach him to take my life away from me…. Yesssss…” Suzi: (rushing in) “Here’s your tinfoil, Candi…” Candi: “CALL ME BY MY NEW NAME!!’ Suzi: (shrugging) “Okay, Queen Diabolique. Like, whatever…” Cackling malevolently, Candi begins to encircle her head with the foil, poking out strategic holes for sight and breathing. Candi: “This shall ensure that Big Al can’t read my thoughts and know that I’m going to kill him!” Suzi: (scratching head) “Wait a minute here… you want to… KILL Big Al? Why?” Candi: (booming and struggling with one end) “SHUT UP AND HELP ME WITH THIS CHEST OF DRAWERS!” Startled into action, Suzi salutes and leaps forward, spurred on by the crinkling, shiny new super-villainess… “Snow and Lord Nelson shall have to wait… for now I have bigger fish to fry!” We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he stands on the back patio of Stately Steele Manor in front of a barbeque grill, slapping on it a huge salmon along with huge hunks of meat. He grins ear to ear, looking quite pleased with his grilling endeavors. Trusted manager Sue Saksnussum relaxes on lounge chair, reading the book, “How to Deal with Difficult People.” Big Al steps back and wipes sweat from his brow, looking resplendent in his toga and apron, reading ‘Can you smell what I’m cookin’?’ A chest of drawers crashes into the spot next to the grill where he just vacated. Big Al looks around, puzzled. Big Alan Steele: “Ms. Sue… did perchance the weather reports call for severe precipitations?” Sue: (absent minded and not looking up) “No, Al. Clear for the next three days, I think.” An end table slams just port of our hero. BAS: (putting finger to lips) “Hmmm… tis most puzzling, this rain of sensibly-priced IKEA products resembling my own.” A safe crashes down through on the patio deck, exploding through the wood and creating a huge hole that narrowly avoided Big Alan Steele. Big Al licks his finger and hoists it into the air, testing the wind. He looks around, and happens upon noticing the Grace wrapped in tin foil poking her tin-ensconced head out of the window. Candi: (upstairs) “Curses! My plans are foiled!” Suzi: (giggling) “Hehehe! That’s funny! Foiled, get it? You’re all dressed in tin foil and your plans are…” Candi: “SHUT UP!” The training room. Big Alan Steele is working hard preparing for his next match, squaring off against the Masochist, practicing a few power moves. Little does he know that a set of cross-hairs are trained on his chest… Candi: (hidden under the cellar steps) “Yesssss! That’s it…” The long barrel of the rifle slowly slides out between the steps. Meanwhile Steele is just completing a devastating powerslam. He reaches down to help up the Masochist… Candi: “Now… DIE!” BLAM! Steele helped the Masochist up directly into the path of the bullet. He slumps to the mat, an angry Big Al looming over him. BAS: “By the gods! There is no time for restful libations! We need to continue at once!” Masochist: (breathing heavily) “I… I think I was shot!” BAS: “Could my powers have increased so without my knowing? Have I developed the unearthly and god-like abilities of telekinesis?! Hi-Keeba!!” Masochist: “Um, bleeding pretty bad here.” BAS: “Well, are you alright.” Masochist: (checking) “No… but I’ll be alright. I’ve had worse and it doesn’t seem to have gotten anything important in there…” BAS: “Well then! By all means, let us take a 15 minute break for you to compose yourself. Allow me to assist you up.” Masochist: “Thanks…” BLAM! The Masochist again crumples to the ground, Steele looking on, confused. Masochist: (groaning) “Might want to get me to a hospital this time… or find the gunman.” An angry Candi throws down the rifle in disgust, roaring from underneath the steps. Candi: “Foiled again! I knew I should have bought more than two bullets! You have won THIS round Steele… but I’ll be back! BWAHAHAHAHA!!” Candi runs from the spot under the stair and dashes up into the house, the two looking on, confused. Masochist: (painful giggle) “That’s funny. Foiled, and she’s all dressed in foil.” BAS: “Indeed, but her attempts on my life are a tad disturbing. Perhaps we should have a talk with her.” Cut to a darkened room , where a trussed up Candi lies flat on a gurney, strapped down in a standing position with a mask on her face, ala Hannibal Lecter. Big Al, Sue and a couple of the Graces stand around Candi, pondering her condition. Sue: “I have to admit… that last attempt on your life was pretty ingenious, what with the hole, the hippopotamus, and the toilet cleanser with the foaming action.” BAS: (sour) “Can we please not encourage the super-villainess, even if she happens to be a Grace?” Sue: “I was only saying… how’d you escape that one?” Big Al gets thoughtful look… Cut to the Masochist, lying in traction on a hospital bed, wrapped head to toe in bandages. Masochist: “Stupid hippo… (sighs) I need a new gig…” Back to Stately Steele Manor. Candi: “Please… please take this mask off. I promise I’ll be good…” Sue: “Don’t do it, Al. I’ve heard of this. You see, she’s developed what psychologists call the Stockholm Syndrome.” BAS: “Ack! Germans!” Sue: “Uh, Swedes, but it’s close enough. Quite. You see, when a kidnapper takes a hostage, that hostage becomes dependant on the kidnapper for their lives. Therefore, the kidnapper becomes the caregiver to the hostage. In certain circumstances where the hostage may have a rather… weak will of their own, the hostage can actually fall in love with the kidnapper, taking their side in the kidnapping and generally being a real pain in the ass to those trying to rescue them.” BAS: “And as far as the will goes, there are few weaker than that of a Grace.” Steffi: (indignant) “Hey, do you mind?! We’re standing right here!” Candi: (sweetly) “Please, please, please take the mask off? What harm could it do?” BAS: “Hmmm.. tis a foamy problem, indeed! How shall we approach this?” Sue: “Well, its actually pretty simple from our perspective. She needs intensive deprogramming from trained, licensed graduate students of psychology in dealing with such disorders. We can’t do much but keep her from trying to kill you.” Candi: “Pretty-pretty please with sugar on top?” Sue: “At any rate, let’s consider it a training session for you. She wants to kill you, just like Tyler Nelson and Snow want to. And just like them, she’ll say anything to trick you and gain and upper hand.” BAS: “So… you want to engage in gladiatorial combat with one of my Graces? I dare say this is a Bold detraction from my normal pursuits…” Sue: “NO, you idiot! We’ll get you training as usual. But until we can get her shipped out, I want you to keep her here, and don’t listen to anything she says.” Candi: “Pwetty, pwetty, pwease with lots and lots of whipped cream and sugar and sweet things, with a cherry on top?” BAS: “By the gods, woman!! Here! I shall remove yon facial confinement… OUCH!” Candi: “BWAHAHAHAHA!!!” BAS: “She bit me, the vile succubus!” Candi: “HAHAHAHA!! I hope it becomes infected and festers and lingers for like four days! Jerk!” Sue: (sighing) “Oh, Al… when will you ever learn?’ Candi: (screaming at top of lungs) “My baby will come for me! I know it!! You can’t just stop his nuclear warhead and not expect him to kick your lily-white ass! I’m here baby! Come and get me!!” Sue: “Wait a minute… you never stopped that missile. Where did it go?” Big Al gets a slow, wry grin that spreads across his features… A mime in white face, a red beret, and your typical black and white striped outfit. The Eiffel Tower looms in the background, as the street thespian does the famous man-in-a-box routine on a bridge overlooking the Rhine River. Normally his face a mask of concentration, a strange whistling noise overhead startles him enough to look skyward… Mime: “Sacre' bleu!!” The streaking missile dives towards the ground… Cut to the grinning Big Al. BAS: (thumbs-up) “Hi-Keeba!!” So now I bide my time. I’ve danced the dance that they laid before me. I passed the tests regarding my sanity and the goofs at the deprogramming center proclaimed me cured. Cured, they said. Fools. For I was forever changed! My heart beats ever stronger for my love! I shall soon have my revenge on Big Alan Steele, but for now I must await the perfect opportunity to strike; lying dormant to catch him off guard and forever eliminate his insulting presence forever. And after that, I shall free my dark savior and together we shall rule the world!! Queen Diabolique shall HAVE the retribution she so craves… Ooooh! 50% off footwear sale at Bloomingdale’s! Gotta run! Bye!
