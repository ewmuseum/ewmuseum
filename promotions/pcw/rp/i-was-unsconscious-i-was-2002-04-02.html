I was unsconscious. I was.

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-2-02 Xavier Kannon might have sent the Krayzie One to the hospital unconscious. But, just like with every other evil being who thinks they can keep Jeremy Howard down, the Krazed Lunatic spurned their attempt when arose from his bed. He might have fallen to a knee the second he tried to stand. Yet, its the fact that he even tried to stand that amazes people. Sometimes, this man can seem inhuman. Inhuman or just plain Krayzie? If you're a true fan of Krayzie, you'd know this man is just plain Krayzie. But, two days removed from being put violently through the Snapple machine, how is Jeremy Howard doing? And, will he be able to make it to Turmoil and his Continental title match with Chef? To find out, the promo begins as the scene fades back into the Krazed Lunatic's hospital room in San Jose, California. There, Jeremy is sitting up, staring forward. Jeremy: Get that nurse to sign me out of here. The view of the room widens, showing Jessica sitting in a chair within the room. Jessica: The nurse is waiting on the doctor to check your head before she releases you. Jeremy: Damn. I have to be at Turmoil tonight. Jessica, in blue jeans and a white t-shirt with matching tennis shoes, stands up from her chair and begins to move toward the Krayzie One. Jessica: What if something's wrong with your head and you can't ever wrestle again? Jeremy: Me? Not wrestle? I don't think those two go together. Jessica: Well, they will if you don't get that head checked out. Jeremy scoffs at her statement. Krayzie and wrestling go hand and hand. One wouldn't be complete without the other. Its just sad that Xavier Kannon almost changed that last Sunday with his vile attack. Thing is, he only slowed the Krayzie One down. Xavier Kannon isn't nearly man enough to put the Krazed Lunatic out. That would take more than a piece of trash like the Gold Patron Meritorious and his bitch valet. It would take alot more! Jeremy: That does it, I'm signing myself out of here. Jessica sighs. Jessica: Your own personal health doesn't mean a thing to you, does it? Jeremy: Not if it means sitting in this hospital for six hours, waiting on some over paid asshole just to say that I can take another hit in the head. No. Jessica sits herself down in Jeremy's lap, which is now hanging off the edge of the bed. Jeremy, wearing blue jeans and the same style black 'Krayzie Till I Die' t-shirt that Xavier Kannon continues to fantasize with, wraps his arm around his lovely girlfriend's beautifully petite frame. Jessica: Sometimes, I try to believe in my heart that you're not really the psychotic lunatic that you always claim to be. But, its times like this I know I'm only hoping for that. Jeremy just shakes his head, trying to come up with something to respond with. Jeremy: You knew when you got involved with me, that I was a little wild. Jessica: A little? You just got put through a Snapple machine a couple of days ago. And now, you want to leave the hospital that is taking care of you to go and beat the hell out of the Chef and his Islamic views. Jeremy smiles, finding some sort of sick thrill in his motives. Jeremy: Somebody has to shut that man's rambling mouth up. Jessica: And it has to be you? Jeremy: If I don't, I lose my Continental title. There's no way in hell I'm going to lose my belt to Chef and miss my chance at beating a hole in Kannon's face. It's the cold hard truth. Xavier Kannon is signed up for a Continental title match at Spring Fling. So, whoever is the Continental Champion has Kannon to look forward too. If that man isn't Jeremy Howard, then the Krayzie One misses his chance for round three with his former friend now turned bitter and hated enemy. But, there's no way in hell Jeremy Howard is going to lose on Turmoil. This man can become inhuman at times. Jessica: Since you put it that way, I can see the point now. Jessica slides off of Jeremy's lap, allowing the current PCW Continental Champion to stand up. Jeremy: Then its time I sign myself out of here. Jessica: I guess that solves that then. Jeremy knods his head toward Jessica, then begins leading her out of his hospital room. As he walks, his lack of coordination is evident. Krayzie stops, standing completely still. Jessica, using what little muscles she does have, tries to keep Jeremy from falling flat on his face. Jessica: You going to make it? Jeremy puts his hand out, holding himself up by use of the wall. Jeremy: I just had a dizzy spell. Nothing to worry about. Jeremy takes his hand away from the wall, standing on his own free will again. After a short pause, Krayzie re-begins his journey out of the San Jose hospital, so that he can make it to Turmoil. Jessica grabs the door, pulling it open. Just as Jeremy takes his first step through the door to his hospital room, his nurse rushes away from her station in the direction of her 'patient'. The Nurse: Mr. Howard, I need you to get back into your bed! The Krayzie One smirks at her. Jeremy: I'm signing myself out. Jeremy pauses, glaring at the nurse. Jeremy: I have a show tonight, I don't have time to wait around all day for your doctor to make time for me. The Nurse: Sir, you could have serious head damage. Jeremy: You have obviously never watched a PCW show. I'm the guy that does whatever he wants whenever he wants! I jump through second story windows! Of course I have head damage! Jessica: He does have head damage. The nurse steps back in shock, having nothing to say. Jeremy: Now, if you'll excuse me, I'd like any paperwork I need because I'm leaving. Thank you. Jessica: Actually, just send it to the address I gave you guys. It wouldn't be a good idea to keep him. He gets hostile when he has to wait. The Nurse: Okay, I send to you there then. Jessica: Thanks. I appreciate it. The nurse knods, kind of confused by it all. Nevertheless, she steps back and lets the pair leave the hospital. Jeremy Howard is old enough to make his own decisions in his life. Therefore, he's old enough to sign himself out of a hospital. Healthy, or not. Jeremy: Lets go. I have a Chef to put out of business. Jeremy grabs Jessica by the hand, beginning to leave the hospital on his own free will. If Xavier Kannon wanted to put the Krazed Lunatic out of commission on Sabotage, he failed. This man is walking and he's walking just fine. Yesterday, he might've been unconscious and a questionable for Turmoil. But now, he's definate. A definate Scientological killer on his way to Turmoil. Jeremy: I swear, if I get my chance, I'm going to break Kannon's f^cking face. Jessica grips his hand tighter, showing an expression of love. Jessica: You should reconsider letting me go to Spring Fling with you. Jeremy: No! I have Spring Fling all sorted. If the Krayzie One can get past the Chef, much like he did back in the second round of the sixty four man tournament to crown a World Champion upon PCW's restart, then the Continental title match for Spring Fling is a sealed deal. And, should that happen, Scientology is going to meet hell. The view on Jeremy and Jessica zooms in, moving down to show only Jeremy's black Nike's walking out of the hospital and into the California sun.... to black!
