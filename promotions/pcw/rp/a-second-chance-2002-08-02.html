A Second Chance

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-02-02 These ideas and feelingsI reach for the skyBut get caught up on the ceilingNow I'm reeling in painMy body can't stand itWill things get better?That's just outlandish… The words played in my head as I awoke, pain all over my skin. Where was I? The walls were white, spotless. I tried to moved, but felt trapped. It was then that I saw the bed I was in, the tubes pumping into my body, and realized I must be in a hospital of some sort… Oh yes, the fire! The inferno, the blaze. But with this rage came the cleansing feeling of evil memories washed away. I felt new… It was wonderful, this new outlook I seemed to have. Why not a new personality? Why not a new life? So odd they seemed, my actions in the recent past. I had destroyed my Voice of Reason and listened to the evil that dwelt within. Evil that promised gold and glory, but only lead me to anguish and defeat. I didn't even have my career to look forward to… my Rising Star Title was stripped away by my pure ignorance and lack of responsibility. I didn't deserve the Rising Star Title… I didn't deserve to be in PCW. But I would not soon throw away the only hope of redemption. Nevertheless, I felt renewed. Now the evil was gone from my life, and I could begin to start piecing the puzzle of my life back together. But with a new beginning, what was I supposed to do with my life? Find three unsuspecting rookies to train into warriors? No… I could not even begin to heal the wounds from before. Instead, I would accomplish my ultimate goal… I would convert the world to my warrior ways! Oh… No, I couldn't… could I? I had tried many times before. Didn't seem possible… those that I knew I could easily change, but it took time and energy… time and energy I didn't have. I needed a way that I could reach the public, so that they would view me with respect and listen to my beliefs. Of course! How could I be so stupid? I already had that position in society… well at least the opportunity. Primetime Championship Wrestling-- the big leagues, one of the number one federations in the world. Watched by millions, fans would have no choice but to listen to my words! 'Slow down… take it step by step.' I knew that I could people to listen… if only I were at the top. So that's how it must be! I would have to get to the top in order to make a difference. So it would be done. I would make it to the top of PCW! I got myself out of the bed, found my clothes and changed into them. I then slipped out the door, and walked away. Four hours later, I had found a hotel and checked in. I wrote a check… didn't know if it was any good, but supposed so considering my success in wrestling and my little spending. Room 404… I took the steps instead of the elevator. I like control of where I am going, and at what pace I go there. When I got to the room, I unlocked the door, and looked around. A fairly nice place, not that it mattered. All I needed it for was rest, and occasional planning. Ah, planning. I closed the door behind me, and set down upon one bed the folder that I held in my hand. You see when I left the hospital, I made one more trip before coming to the hotel-- I stopped by the local PCW offices to attain information pertaining to my next upcoming match. Only now did I open the folder and take a look. Four Way DanceNumber One Contendership For Cruiserweight TitleBrandi vs. Trigger vs. Ricky Thunder vs. Syko the Goblin I was surprised! Cruiserweight Title? It wasn't my division, but it was a step up from Rising Star. I could add another championship to my list! …I could climb another rung on the ladder to success. I glanced over the names again. If I wanted to win this--If I wanted to make my way to the Cruiserweight Title, I would have to study each opponent with the utmost enthusiasm. Learn their successes, learn their stories, learn their secrets. Brandi… Ricky Thunder… Syko the Goblin. Syko the Goblin? Ah, how intriguing. He would be my first victim.
