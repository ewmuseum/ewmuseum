PCW Wars: New Hope Part 1

13 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13 - 10 - 02 A long time ago in a galaxy far, far away.... It is a period of civil war. Rebel spaceships, striking from a hidden base, have won their first victory against the evil Galactic Empire. During the battle, Rebel spies managed to steal secret plans to the Empires ultimate weapon, The Death Star, an armoured space station with enough power to destroy an entire planet. Pursued by the Empires sinister agents, Princess Leia races aboard their starship, custodian of the stolen plan that can save her people and restore freedom to the galaxy.... The vast sea of stars serve as a backdrop as a small craft maneuvers between the gunfire of the goliath ship known as the Generic Oriented Deathstar. Laser bolts swamp the darkness of space, finally hitting the smaller craft in its solar fin just as the yellow planet of Tatooine emerges. The scene switches to the interior of the ship just as an explosion rocks the two passing droids who walk unnoticed. One is a tall bronze coloured unit whilst the others appear as a metallic food blender on rollers shooting about like a remote controlled car. Rush-3PO: 'Man, what was that shit? Damn that reactor went down far too easy, a touch of the Ellie me thinks!' Rebel troops storm past the droids and take up positions either side of the corridor. Rush-3PO: 'It's a conspiracy, blame the white man, we're all going to die!!!' The smaller unit flickered into life with a series of high-pitched sounds, only understandable to that of another droid. Rush-3PO: 'Dat fine white ass of the princess won't escape this time!' The other unit again responds with another series of those annoying sounds, when suddenly a huge crashing sound filtered through. The likely hood would be the Generic Oriented Deathstar had boarded the miniscule craft. The Rebel fighters aim their respected weapons and wait patiently, then without warning the metallic wall caved in unveiling a hoard of armoured Stormbringers who make their way into the smoke-filled corridor. Within minutes the passageway is alight with gunfire as the Stormbringers fend off the Rebel force. The laser beams ricochet off the surroundings walls wildly as the two parties dive for cover, however the Rebel army have taken a number of casualties and the fearsome Stormbringers look to edge the battle. Rush-3PO: 'Damn - I'm never gonna listen to another food blender in my lif-... Guarantee!' The smaller unit counters his comrades commenting by running him over, the two droids crashing together with a thunderous thud. As the warfare settles down a dark seven-foot figure walks into the building. This Syth Lord is none-other than Darth Pharaoh, he is clad in black gowns and a grotesque facial mask that obscures his face from view. Standing out from his white minions, his presence alone creates a dense silence and the rebellious army panic, like ants they scurry aimlessly. Hidden from view and unbeknownst to the Syth Lord, the small droid has a small electronic card is inserted by a woman’s hand who remains sheltered by the darkness. Standing dumbfounded Rush-3PO looks for his traveling campadres R2 D2 who is no-where to be seen. Rush-3PO: 'Now where did that roller-blading trash can go? R2 get your aluminum ass out here....' Peering down another passageway Rush-3PO notices his companion through a thick fog of smoke, the little droid however is being tampered with by a young girl. Rush-3PO: 'Oh, lady go get your own damn coffee espresso!' The unknown female vanished into obscurity whilst Rush-3PO and R2 D2 re-acquaint one another. Rush-3PO: 'Man you’re a real lady's man... Please show me the force...' The unit interrupting with its monotone squeaking. Rush-3PO: 'DON'T LET ME MELT YOU DOWN INTO PAPER-CLIPS BITCH!!!' Suddenly the trash can resembling R2 D2 crashed into his comrade once more causing the devious duo to fall into a life raft, which accelerated from its docked position and seems on a collision course with the planet Tatooine. The scene switched back to the evil Darth Pharaoh who gripped a fallen foe by the throat, hoisting him with ease, surrounded by his beaten rivals he's about to question a dying officer when a Stormbringer intrudes. The white armoured Stormbringer bares the nametag 'Bobby' and interrupts his Lord with bad news. Bobby: 'Those photo's of Aimz aren’t-... Err-... The Death Stars plans are not in the main computer my Lord!' Almost choking the life out of the Rebel officer the sinister Empire Lord asks his initial question with a monotonous but muffled voice. Darth Pharaoh: 'Where are those plans.... Please?' Officer: 'That's a nasty throat infection, strepsil's are your best call of action. But getting back to that question... Oh-... This is a diplomatic mission, a consular ship!' Darth Pharaoh: 'If this is one of those ships.... Where's the Ambassador?' Refusing to speak the officer has his life removed by Darth Pharaoh crushing his throat with nothing more than his own grip. The soldier goes limp and his dead carcass is tossed aside like a rag doll, the Syth Lord turns to his warriors and addresses them once more. Darth Pharaoh: 'Bobby clean this mess up, nothings more irritating than dead bodies lounging about doing nothing. The rest of you, tear this ship apart, leave no stone unturned, find them plans and bring me the Ambassador.... ALIVE!' A Stormbringers hand raises and Darth Pharaoh nods to acknowledge. Trooper: 'Sir this is a spacecraft and there isn't any stones on-board....' The rest scurry around as the minion glares at the cold-hearted stare of his master allowing the scene to change to that of the Ambassador search. A lovely blue haired girl is seen huddled into an alcove; she is the Princess Anarchy Organa, a member of the Alderaan Senate. The sound of the search party grows louder and the girl’s eyes widen like an Animee character as her fear seeps through. Trooper: 'There she is guys, set 'em to stun!' Anarchy steps out of her hiding place and sets hotfooted in the opposite direction of her captors before having her progress cut-short by the paralyzing effects of a stun gun. One Stormbringer checks the victim to see whether she survived the blast and gives a thumb up. Bobby: 'Can we play with h-... Hmm-... Inform Lord Pharaoh that we have the prisoner!!!' Again the scene closes only to re-open from the cock-pit of the Generic Oriented Deathstar where the life-pod containing Rush-3PO and R2 D2 hits the atmosphere of Tatooine, burning brightly like a small comet or shooting star. Chief Pilot: 'There goes another one' Captain Mann: 'Hold your fire - There isn't any life forms but, what the hell, it'll make for good target practise!' Looking from the interior of the life-pod Rush-3PO waves good-bye to the Imperial starship as their own craft spirals through the Tatooine atmosphere, stars twinkling back like Morse code. Rush-3PO: 'Dat fighting be some funny shit, those Stormbringer dudes put on quite the show....' R2 D2 bleeping a reassuring response. Rush-3PO: 'I hope this thing is safe - Dat AA Company don't doing any others planets besides Earth!' Finally the scene resides on the saga's main star, Alexander Skywalker. The young sixteen-year-old farmer has rugged hair and a pair of strange goggles aligning his forehead looking like Tai from Digimon. The youngster stands alongside another boy who appeared about the same age, however our star remains motionless holding a pair of binoculars and looking at the backdrop of his planet. A. Skywalker: 'Yeah-... That's it-... Ewwww!' Biggs: 'Yeah that comet was awesome, don't you think?' The image shows a shot from the binoculars, watching a pair of Sand People making out in the distance. A. Skywalker: 'Err-... Arr-... Yeah that comet thingy was like sooooo bright!' Continuing Alexander Skywalker concludes the conversation; afterwards the duo walks away in opposite directions. A. Skywalker: 'Well I got to sow some seeds, basically run the family business since the Uncle is a damn alcoholic!' Again the scene changes, man it's like a roller coaster but this is how the script goes... Err-... Returning to the Deathstar we watch on, as Princess Anarchy is lead down a narrow by a squad of armoured Stormbringers. One pinches the bottom of the Princess; the recognisable assailant being the one marked 'Bobby' forcing the royal to take a swipe with an open palm almost knocking his helmet off. Bobby: 'Hey bitch wait until I find your email address, huh?!?' laughing to himself with childish giggles. The squad led the Princess into a smoke-filled room resided by the Dark Syth Lord, Darth Pharaoh who stares hard at his captive prisoner. Anarchy: 'Lord Pharaoh, I should've known. Only you could hire that one particular pervert. The Imperial Senate will not sit for this, or stand for that matter, after all you attacked a diplomatic....' Darth Pharaoh: 'Don't play games your highness! You’re not on any diplomatic mission, plus you were kinder over the speed limit for this restricted system. Anyways some spies sent some transmissions here, so give them here and we wont have to torture you, sounds a fair deal to me!' Anarchy: 'What the hell are you talking about - I'm on a diplomatic....' The Syth master interrupted the blue haired babe. Darth Pharaoh: 'Your part of the Rebel Alliance, a traitor... Take this Whore away!' Anarchy is marched away against her will and into a holding cell, just as Captain Mann turns up to meet with with Darth Pharaoh. Captain Mann: 'Holding her is dangerous, wasn't that a Michael Jackson song too? If word gets out it could generate sympathy for the Alliance if you catch my drift' Darth Pharaoh: 'I have traced the transmission here - She is my only link to that secret base and besides Michael Jackson's only big hit was 'Bad' so stop fretting!' Captain Mann: 'Hmm, good point - Oh and she'll die before she tells you anything!' Darth Pharaoh: 'Send a distress call and tell that Senate that all aboard were killed....' Suddenly an Imperial Trooper turned up with valid information with regards to the present mission. Trooper: 'Lord Pharaoh the plans aren't aboard dude, that bodacious babe must've hidden them in that dodgy looking life-pod, you know the one that had no life-forms, you know the one that contained those two silly looking droids!' Darth Pharaoh: 'So true... I know the Princess must've hid the plans aboard that jettisoned pod... Damn I'm good, no wonder I'm the boss!!!' Trooper: 'Well done sir, very observant~!' Standing smug, Darth Pharaoh turns his attentions back to the ominous looking Trooper. Darth Pharaoh: 'You have done well, now go back to the ship and prepare a search party. You shall be rewarded well, how does a pay rise sound?' Trooper: 'TOTALLY TUBULAR!!!' The scene reverts to a shot of the Generic Oriented Deathstar that looms over the planet of Tatooine, shadowing the best part of the planet with its awesome size. - To be continued -
