The 'Anti-Christ' Saga: In League

13 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 13, 2002 Handlers Note: Just so you assholes realize this, these RP's up til my next saga (which will be the Nightmare Saga if all goes right) happen in the past, between the time DarkStar was killed by Clyde and the time he was resurrected. Thanks Rev 6:1 I watched as the Lamb opened the first of the seven seals. Then I heard one of the four living creatures say in a voice like thunder, 'Come!' 2: I looked, and there before me was a white horse! Its rider held a bow, and he was given a crown, and he rode out as a conqueror bent on conquest. The engine of the white Corvette sped into Philadelphia, it was far surpassing any speed limit rule that the large city had on its streets, like the driver truely gave a damn. He had the whole world in his newly reborn hand... He was poised to clench his fingers together and make a fist, letting the liquid of the good in the world bleed out like stigmata on christ. The man smiled at the analogy. Obviously, this was DarkStar's body, he had the maneurisms of DarkStar, the blue hair, the dark, monotone speech, rarely using contractions, but yet it wasnt. The personification of the Dream Lord is not involved in this recreation in any way, this is a different person, but yet the same, this is true darkness... this is Prince Lucifer, his eyes glow a different color, his access to the realm of dreams taken away, as the realm falls into disrepair. Stopping at a red light, This Doppleganger of the Nightmare King looks out of the windshield, seeing the human beings walk by on the white pedestrian walk way, his mind flashed with possiblities. I could kill them all... just by waving my hand, or talking in a different way, I could destroy the world with the mere mention of my name... Lucifer Morningstar grinned, pale ear to pale ear. But first... we eliminate the only that shall know... The light turned green and he drove forward, his wheels leaving rubber on the walkway. --------------------------------- She sat idlely, what else could she possibly do, he was gone... and it has only been a day since the Wake, but it feels like it was forever. Could she go on like this? The thoughts of suicide and heartbreak rolled around in her mind, she knew she wouldnt be able to go through with it anyway, especially after the cryptic message she was sent the night before... She sits down and thinks, remembering it clearly and vividly... It was the night she got revenge on Courtney, DarkStar's exgirlfriend, the one that made them all like they are today, she slaughtered her and her boyfriend in cold blood (and cold steel)... ---------Flashback----------- The living room was barren again tonight. All the lights off, save the Televisions company. Blood, sweat, water, and tears rolled down her face, forming streaks in her make up. Her left hand had the remote in it. Flipping through the channels. Trying to forget. She pushed her hood back and layed her head onto the couch. Day dreaming and gazing off into the ceiling. Her thoughts all melded into one. The TV was no companion. Miss Emily did nothing for her. Nothing at all did anything. Except for what was on her TV tray. She glanced over to it, the bottles of pills. Anti Depressents, uppers, some she found in the medicine cabinet that had nothing on them. It didnt matter. It all made her forget. She flicked through the TV for a few more minutes, basicly going through the rotations. Nothing. Until she reached channel 6, ABC news in the Philadelphia region. She raised a eyebrow at the screen and the caption. The news anchor flickered in, a bit of static after a large burst of lightning. 'In other news, a tragic turn of events has happened just outside of Philadelphia in the Southern New Jersey suburbs' A graphic comes up in the top corner, next to the anchors head. It is a crime scene outline and a crashed car. Talena: Oh fuck. 'A young couple was dealt a tragic but poetic blow. In the late hours of last night, 21 year old Courtney Prescott was stabbed to death in her home, the police report states that it was a random attack most likely, based on the stab pattern it was perpetrated with a curved knife. The PYPD are looking for leads on the case... However, the tragedy does not end there. Her boyfriend, who left moments before, ran a red light, and was killed after being thrown from his vehicle. The time's of death were almost exactly at the same time. The couple didnt have to be apart for ve-...' Talena growls and stands, raising her right hand at the screen and firing. Now we see what she was holding aside from the remote, a small pistol. The TV shattered, electricity bursting outwards. She stood, the gun in her hand still and dropped the remote. Talena: And they make them out to be some sort of romantic coincidence??? I FUCKING KILLED THEM BOTH! She sends her foot into the speaker of the TV, breaking that inwards. Talena: YOU DIDNT MAKE GAVYN A MARTYR, YOU JUST LET HIM GO!!! Her stability has finally fallen over, a earthquake rocking her mind like a chair. Things began to snap as she lost it, her small frame seemed to grow to Taxi Driver proportions as she swung the gun around, yelling violently to herself. Talena: EVERYTHING I FEEL HURTS!!! WHY!!! She puts the gun to her head. Talena: WHY! WHY! WHY! She feels her grip tightening on the trigger, her eyes close but suddenly shoot open again, she kicks over a end table, her dress rides up, revealing her knee high platform boots and purple striped stockings, the end table falls, sending the lamp that was on it to the ground, it shatters loudly. She brandishes the weapon again, firing at the wall and splintering a clock on the mantel piece. Signifigantly, she has lost it. She crumples to the ground again, burying her head into her hands, the gun pointing out above and away from her, she rocks back and forth like a little girl throwing a tantrum. She looks up, her eyes filled with more tears then ever before, she knows that this is it. Life or Death... And slowly... she walks towards the right... taking the path that leads to her brains spilt onto the floor. Her hand lifts the pistol... I can see it now... 'In a tragic turn of events, the girlfriend of local hero Gavyn Drazail kills herself' She looks at it for a second. I wonder if it will be poetic when my blood is staining these dark carpets Raising it and placing it next to her temple... Head or Mouth. Head or Mouth. That sounds like sexual innuendo She keeps it on her temple, deciding. It will be the quickest... Her eyes close tight as the barrell begins to click. Ding... Dong... She flinches, thinking the noise was the gun, it drops. And she just stares at it. Looking at the piece of metal laying on the floor. The one that could end her life. She never takes her eyes off of it as she stands. Walking to the door. She looks through the peephole and see's noone. Great, I stop my self-murder for a game of doorbell di-... Something catches her eye, the fluttering of a black bird flying away from the doorstep. Curiosity gets to her as she opens the door and looks onto the rain drenched steps. Roses? She looks around, confused, and picks them up, the red roses are wrapped up tightly, a small white note card on the paper. She plucks it off with her trembling fingers, looking around again. She opens it and reads... 'But where shall wisdom be found? And where is the place of understanding? Man knoweth not the price thereof; neither is it found in the land of the living... for the price of wisdom is above rubies.' Stay Strong, Love,Gavyn Our view lingers, the pale light of the moon gives her face a blue illumination... And she dreamed... -------------------------------------- At the time she had no idea of what to make of the flowers that were sent to her doorstep that night, of course she imagined the return of her lover, and she knew that it was as possible as it could ever be, but it lingered that it was all a sick joke from Clyde... he would do something like that. She sighed and looked down at the black carpet with tear stained eyes, her mascara making black, rugged stains on her beautiful white cheeks... And the doorbell rang... She was startled, shooting her head up and shaking her black hair into place, she walked to the large door, answering it, grabbing the handle and turning... And in one burst of emotion, her eyes leaked and she grabbed a hold of the figure standing on the other side of that door... He had returned home, just like the cryptic letter hinted towards, her eyes closed and she held onto him for dear life... Love... A beautiful thing. ----------------------------------------- Lucifer looked towards Talena and feigned happiness, he feigned that his heart was mended, for he was the Great Deciever, she would never see through the guise he had right now. His eyes teared up, salt water dripping onto her shoulder, he gripped her back with his strong palms and he held her there. If the one he loved is so easy to deception, imagine the rest of the mortals... Lucifer Morningstar... this is your time He grinned through the tears, which Talena percieved as happiness, and she was half right... He was just happy for a different reason... ------------------------------------------- Talena looked into his eyes and embraced him, this was perfect... Something wasnt right though... His kiss, it felt cold, cold and empty... Maybe it was just her feelings, or maybe it was because it was so long since the last time they felt eachother like this, maybe she forgot... But one never forgets... Her smile changed a little, still a half smirk, she walked backwards, stumbling... And then she screamed... Her second sight, the pair of eyes that existed only mentally, kicked in, and all things spiritual were revealed... standing in front of her, in her lovers body, was the morningstar, and she would be damned if she let him survive... Before she could do anything however, she was attacked from behind, blindsided if you will, her head knocked onto the ground hard, her brain smacked against her skull and made her woozy, she swung, her incredibly sharp nails tore into something vinyl, but a sharp, stinging feeling clasped onto her arm, right below her wrist... she felt the sticky feeling of blood leak onto her arm and her clothing... Her eyes began to focus as she felt cold steel pierce her neck, and she saw who was sitting ontop of her, a curved blade to her neck... Fu-... ck... The female jester laughed as the blood poured from Talena's wounds... 'DarkStar' walked to her side and kneeled down, putting his hand on Talena's forhead... Lucifer: Talena... I want you to meet my princess... Harle Quinn... I take it you two already know eachother? Talena swung again, but it was feeble, she lost conciousness quickly, with Harle Quinn muttering something about payback... ------------------------------- The depths of hell... Red... fiery... warm... It is sad that, sometimes, a love is so strong that the pair share a bond that goes above physical and mental restraints, it becomes spiritual, and when the bond is broken, it only becomes stronger... He knew, hanging there like he was crucified, like he was a sacrificial lamb, that they had gotten her, that Talena was now gone... And Vengeance... Vengeance filled his soul... He ripped his arm from the wall, chunks of the barbedwire hung from his muscular bicep, and his eyes turned the familiar shade of black... DarkStar growled... a deep... animal growl as his other arm came off the wall... And his mortal return began....
