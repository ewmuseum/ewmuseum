Counsel from Isiy

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd of July, 2002 Complexities – life seems to be full of them, doesn’t it? Take mine for example. One minute you’re a normal guy, living a simplistic life in the pro wrestling circuit, next minute your main eventing two weeks in a row, thrust into a deep and sadistic feud with a maniacal Scientologist and given the opportunity of a world title shot. To some wrestlers that’d seem like the greatest thing to ever happen to them, but with me, it’s both good and bad. See, its good because I’m finally getting the respect and recognition I believe I’ve deserved for a while, but it’s also bad because the guy who I just happen to be feuding with –Xavier Kannon – has beaten up my wife, destroyed numerous irreplaceable relics from my country’s past, mummified me, shipped me off to Egypt and cost me at least five pints of blood. The things I’ve just listed, however, are nothing compared to the final proverbial ‘nail in the coffin’ that ‘The Gold Patron Meritorious’ has cast upon me, though. You see, Xavier Kannon was defeated by my good friend and bodyguard, Zen Dula, a week or so ago, and, Kannon been the asshole he is couldn’t take it. So, this past Fusion, Xavier bloodied Zen, electrocuted him and left him for dead… Now I get a chance to teach that bastard a lesson! This coming Sunday at PCW’s Summer Pay Per View, Wrestle Vessel, I get to face off against Kannon in a ‘Sarcophagus match’! So far I aren’t exactly sure of this match’s rules, but I believe the winner is the man who locks his opponent in the ancient coffin-like structure. But, as life has a tendency to do, another stipulation has been added to this match – the winner gets a world title shot later in the night… Not bad, huh? Now I’m more determined than ever to win my match against Xavier, and I have to reasons to do so - for my pride, my family and my friends AND for a World Heavyweight Title shot! So thus begins my adventure of possible glory and my possible demise. But, my friends, whatever happens I only hope I get some kind of revenge on Xavier Kannon…my most hated enemy… Our scene opens up in the confines of the great Anaheim State Hospital, Anaheim, California. Isiy and The Pharaoh had been there for most of the weekend since Zen had been brutally destroyed at the hands of Pharaoh’s worst enemy, Xavier Kannon. The room’s white walls shone with a tint of orange as the morning sun rose up over the distant mountains of the sun-kissed valley known as ‘Sunny’ California. Wires and other pipes connect up to the life-support machine that the huge monster of a man occupied entwined with colourful other wires, which led to unseen destination -, possibly even to coffee machines outside the room, but hey, we’ll never really know the truth behind that, right? Pharaoh looked over towards his wife, tired and his hair a mess. The Pharaoh: “That bastard will pay for this!” Isiy smiled, reluctantly. Isiy: “Yeah, I know, honey.” Pharaoh’s brow wrinkled as a frown appeared on his handsome yet tired-looking visage. The Pharaoh: “You really don’t think I’ll beat him this time, do you?” Isiy doesn’t answer. After all, Xavier had defeated Pharaoh three times in a row now, so what made her husband think he could defeat Kannon this time? She sighed and watched as Titu turned away from her. He looked up at the expressionless face of Zen and shuddered. The Pharaoh: “Some people have no faith in me, Zen, but I promise you that this coming Sunday, Xavier Kannon will pay for what he’s done!” Isiy tried to smile at her husband’s determined words, but she couldn’t quite manage it. The long and strenuous feud with Kannon had taken its toll on her too. Titu spoke of him all the while! A moment hadn’t really passed that he hadn’t mentioned the Gold Patron Meritorious and now it was beginning to get tiring. Isiy: “Come on, Titu, let’s go and get some breakfast--…” The Pharaoh: “Our friend needs us now! Can’t you see that?!” Isiy sighed. There really was no talking to Titu when he was in this kind of a mood. He could be as stubborn as a mule at times, and she’d learnt that it was best to leave him when he was like this. Shrugging, she rose from her seat at the side of Zen’s bed and left the silent room… The Pharaoh: “My own wife doesn’t even think I can get the job done…” Pharaoh sighed, tightening his fists. The Pharaoh: “Well I’ll prove everyone wrong, Zen! In the name of Horus I promise I’ll defeat Kannon! I will, dammit!” Pharaoh rose to his feet. His friend couldn’t talk to him anyway and he was hungry, after all. He walked out of the room and down one of the winding halls of the Anaheim State Hospital, thoughts of the up and coming PPV on his mind. He’d get a chance at the world title if he defeated Xavier…what an opportunity, and extremely out of the blue, too. In the distance Pharaoh saw his wife walking in the same direction as him, he caught up with her and smiled. The Pharaoh: “Sorry about that, but Zen’s our friend and he needs us now.” Isiy: “And what about when you go off on the road again? Where is this big PPV set, anyway?” She had a point and she was about to make an even bigger one. Montego Bay in Jamaica… Isiy would blow her top when she found out. The Pharaoh: “Well, I mean, it’s, it’s in Jamaica--…” Isiy: “Jamaica?! And there’s you telling me that our friend needs us to be there for him?!” Pharaoh frowned. The Pharaoh: “I’m going so that I can defeat Kannon, in Zen’s name!” Isiy let out a long sigh. It was clear that she had never been into the idea of Titu venturing off over the country. ‘A loving woman she was, but too protective of her husband’ was the perfect description of the Egyptian beauty. Isiy: “Look, I just don’t--…” The Pharaoh: “--Like me going away all the time?” Isiy nodded solemnly. The Pharaoh: “Look, I have something to settle; something that’s needed settling for a long time! My love, don’t worry about me, I’ll be fine, I swear to Horus that no harm will come of me!” Isiy: “But there’s always that chance that something might happen…” The Pharaoh: I know, I know…but Kannon needs to be taught that I mean business! Enough of this losing streak, O river of my aching heart, for come this Sunday at Montego Bay I shall end it and go on to defeated Big Alan Steele or Frisco for the PCW World Heavyweight Title!” Isiy looked up into her husbands deep brown eyes; they glimmered in the artificial lighting above them. Isiy: “speaking of which…” The Pharaoh: “Yeah, I know. I have a match against them, too. Isiy: “And Xavier’s going to be acting as the special guest referee…” Pharaoh scowled, he’d forgotten about that. The Pharaoh: “Dammit! Every single time I try to do something in PCW he’s there! Acting as the Referee, or interfering, or attacking me backstage! I’m sick of him! The bastard is really beginning to piss me off, now!” The anger was growing. Isiy placed two hands gently on her husband’s shoulders, a caring smile shaping on her near-perfect face. Isiy: “Calm down, Titu! If you go into that match this coming Sunday - as much as I hate the idea of you even doing so – like this then you’re going to get hurt. Don’t you think Kannon’ll know you’re going to be like this? It’s been his plan all along!” Pharaoh considered this and then sighed, easing his body and loosening his fists. The Pharaoh: “I just--… He’s, he’s been…” Isiy hugged her husband. Isiy: “I know…I know…I, myself, want to kick his wife’s ass, but do you see me getting stressed about it? No! You have to keep a cool head in situations like this, my love, seriously, or else you’ll do more damage to yourself than the guy you aim to do damage to…” Pharaoh, looking a little embarrassed, looked down into Isiy’s bright blue eyes and smiled awkwardly. The Pharaoh: “How come you weren’t around when I needed advise for my TV title defence?” He laughed a little. Never before this moment had he taken his wife’s words seriously in a situation as dire as this one; now he wished he had done times and times ago. Isiy: “Come on, I’m starving.” Isiy took Pharaoh’s hand in hers and led him down the full length of the corridor until they came to a tall door reading ‘Canteen’. Isiy opened the restaurant’s entrance as her and Pharaoh walked in. Inside the room a group of elderly men and women sat around a table playing chess. The Pharaoh: “The Gods will be doing such a thing, I would think.” Isiy: “What do you mean?” Isiy, as much as she loved her husband, had never really considered the Gods of Ancient Egypt as much as The Pharaoh had. He was, and always had been, a true believer in the power of the immortals who supposedly watched over him. Big Alan Stele was another man who considered such a thing to be his only refuge from his enemies - the Gods had always supplied useful information on difficult situations to their followers, whether they worshipped Zeus, or whether they followed Amun-Ra - and Pharaoh had always considered it with great admiration, too. The Pharaoh: “The Gods, Isiy…they decide whether I am worthy enough to win such a match as I have this Sunday, and this Tuesday for that matter! I will truly need their support on my side if I am to defeat the likes of Frisco, Xavier Kannon, and of course, my friend, Big Alan Steele. Isiy smiled. Isiy: “Well I’m glad to see that you’ve, at least, found a friend who can help you through your troubles with Xavier.” Pharaoh shook his head. It was indeed true that Big Al had raised his hand and helped him out at the end of Fusion on Friday, but could Pharaoh really trust the Greek warrior? It was a question that needed to be answered, but whether it would or not was another matter. The Pharaoh: “Whether I can trust BAS to help me in the future is still to be decided, my dearest love. We follow two completely different religions, and though we respect each other, our trust for one another has yet to be fully sealed.” Isiy nodded as she walked towards the main cafeteria area. Food, protected by huge plastic shields, was on show for everyone to see. Pharaoh looked at it in interest. The Pharaoh: “Hmm…Hospital food? I remember that O too well from my last stay here.” Isiy laughed. Isiy: “It isn’t that bad, Titu. I bet you hardly even tried it!” Pharaoh laughed as though saying: “I’ve been here and tasted the stuff, and well, my dear, you haven’t, so don’t pass judgement, just yet!” The Pharaoh: “Well we’ll see what you think of it soon, won’t we?” A man, dressed in classic chef garb, walked up to the food and smiled. Man: “Breakfast?” Isiy smiled, cheerfully. Isiy: “For two please!” Xansiv walked up behind his wife and put his arms around her waist, hugging her close to him. He’d always been a man who was easily made jealous - he’d been like that ever since Xavier first feuded with him back in NTICW. Man: “Hello, sir…” The Pharaoh: “Hello, my friend…” Pharaoh watched cautiously as the man prepared the hospital’s daily breakfast - which ALWAYS consisted of a bowl of Cornflakes and a cup of tea, along with a small selection of chocolate digestives - and put everything on two plastic trays. He handed them both to Isiy and waited… The Pharaoh: “How much is that?” Man: “Ten dollars, sir.” Pharaoh reached into his long black silk gown and produced a brown leather wallet. He opened it and took out five two-dollar bills. Man: “Thank you sir.” Pharaoh handed him them and walked towards the table Isiy had sat down at. Isiy: “When are you leaving?” The Pharaoh: “I have to be in Tampa soon, and then I’ve some other publicity shoots elsewhere.” Isiy: “Oh, right. Mr Popularity are we now?” Pharaoh smiled shyly. The Pharaoh: “Well ever since I’ve been battling Xavier, the fans have really gotten on my side. It’s a good thing really; they’re all that keep me going sometimes. Of course, they’re nothing compared to seeing your beautiful face again.” Isiy blushed as she poured a cup of tea for herself from the silver kettle that the chef had supplied them with. The Pharaoh: “I just hope Zen’s OK…” Isiy frowned. Isiy: “So do I…If you have to kick Kannon’s ass then do it for Zen, Titu.” Pharaoh winced. The Pharaoh: “I plan to…” Pharaoh raised a spoonful of Cornflakes to his mouth. The Pharaoh: “I just wish you’d believe in me more.” He ate them as Isiy looked up from her tea. Isiy: I do, it’s just that Xavier’s beaten you three times in a row now, Titu. It’s kinda’ hard for me to believe in you when he’s got that sort of record against--…” The Pharaoh: “He’s beaten me three times through cheating, Isiy. Even you have seen him do that!” Isiy: “Well I guess so.” The Pharaoh: “And this time it’s in a sarcophagus match! I like the odds of winning it because I’ve worked with Sarcophagi most of my life! I can win this match, Isiy, trust me!” Isiy nodded, smiling. Isiy: “I do believe in you, you know…” She took a sip of her tea as Pharaoh ate some more of his Cornflakes. Isiy: “I want you to win more than anything.” Pharaoh grinned, his teeth clearly visible. The Pharaoh: “Isiy, I have to win…my pride rests on this match! Xavier won’t beat me again, I won’t let him.” Isiy smiled. Pharaoh was extremely determined; she hadn’t seen him like this for a long, long time. And she hoped he’d defeat the Scientologist, she truly did. He’d hurt her too, not just Titu. She remembered the pain she felt after the vicious X-Bomb and shivered. Isiy: “Just, give him your all, and stay calm…” Pharaoh looked up. His bowl was empty. The Pharaoh: “I have to go…take care of Zen…” He rose from his seat and looked down at his wife; she smiled at him seductively. Xansiv leaned back over the table and kissed her passionately. She’d given him much advice today – advise he’d lacked up until now. Isiy: “Take care.” He was ready now…ready to face Kannon, again--… The Pharaoh: “I will…” …--And this time he’d win… The scene fades to black.
