Amnesia would be a benefit.

15 October 2002

Title: Amnesia would be a benefit. Featuring: Tim Walden Date: Oct. 15, 2002 Memories..Times may come and go, things may change, but memories gently flicker, still there in the back of the mind, the distant signals of days gone by...Needless to say, some are better than others, but sitting here in my office, television wheeled in and displaying the previous year's Mall Brawl, I know that this year I would have a lot to live up to.. 'I SHALL WIN FOR YOU, MY BELOVED EMILY!' Okay, maybe some of this isn't quite worth living up to, but the mere appearance of my first solid victory over THE Multiple Man gives me a morale boost..There was no evasion for him that time, and he never really would be able to get away from me whenever we fought..Just sort of a hopeless case, would never reach the athletic plateau required to beat me and it showed. This was pleasing for me, I could see the dawn of my era taking shape as I brought down my opponents one by one..This was just as I could hope for this time around, except with another year of experience under my belt I would not fall victim to the errors of distraction that I remembered causing my defeat before...Of all the ways to lose, leaving the mall via faux Amish strippers was both the least painful physically and the most painful emotionally, as I knew that I might've had a shot at winning, had I only hung in there instead of allowing myself to be distracted by the female form as tended to happen at the time.. 'Walden just beat one of the #1 Contenders to the World Title!' A sudden drop back into reality, as I flip out the remote and pause the footage..This is different than last year all right. But it's not all on the positive side, for in my ecstacy to see my positive showing, I momentarily ignored the reason for this sudden surge of skill....I wasn't just talented at the time, I was INVINCIBLE. That's why I took out Golden Game by myself, made the HIT tap out, and beat Ryan Knox in less than 10 seconds flat, and that's not something I can count on now...But then I realize something else. TMM, Klaymore, Jupiter, HIT, Knox, Borro, they're all gone now..Nobody whom I defeated last year is here now, so those victories are even less relevant, if it's possible. This year, I start with a clean slate in my own mind, but I just know that everyone else will be watching me, wondering if I can do it again, meanwhile I have that 10/18 nonsense to worry about..It's enough to drive a guy bonkers, the mix of self-doubt and paranoia that I'm starting to stew in...I stop the tape, turn off the TV, I need to just relax a moment here..It's not helping my mindset to get worked up over such minor things as a finger presses over the intercom button.. Walden: Greg, I'm going out for a bit, hold my calls. Voice: Will do, sir. I leave the office, no bother for a coat that I did not bring, for though it is mid-autumn, it's still pretty fair weather with the sun out..The park's only a short walk away, and I take the stairs down to the exit, then start walking to the park bench..It's a quick trip, so I stop at the newsbox and buy a daily paper, then sit on the bench and start to read..I can't get interested though, something's uncomfortable, tearing at my thoughts...I lay down, I need the rest as I drape the paper over my head, try to relax, there's nothing to worry about, you'll do fine, all that self-esteem bull. I didn't do too finely against the Chef, what's there to suggest that I won't have a repeat of my failures this week? I have to stop thinking about it.. I feel a tap on my shoulder, and pull the paper off my head.. Walden: Ugh...What is it now? Unnamed man: We need you back at the office, Mr. Walden..The network's gone down, and we need to have you around to reassure the staff that everything's fine. Gah...Can't I get a decent rest now any more? I'm not going to be in much shape for the match if I can't get this tension out...Well, at least it's not something to do with wrestling, I can't take much more of it for the moment..I groggily pull myself up and off the bench. Walden: All right, all right, may as well go and keep things productive..Beats sittin' round here, I guess. I wander back to the building, not defeated but merely drained of a will to resist..I was strained for hope though, still thinking to Friday and the battle ahead..I wanted to win badly, but I knew that I would have to put on one of the performances of my life to do it. Even if I did manage to work with the rest of the Front Office to reach the end of the match, it would still mean defeat most likely, but that would not be an issue at that point..However, I knew it probably wouldn't work like that, whoever was coming after us had obviously planned his or her attack well enough to be effective..What was I thinking?! The Front Office, lose for the fact of some screwball with a screw?! Ridiculous..But there was still a chance..That was what I would have to watch out for...Because without the Front Office by my side, what was I to do, short of making a repeat performance as Invincible Tim, and that hadn't happened since the day after my firing..But now... Times changed...Fame is faded...Though almost nothing is the same... The memories remain. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
