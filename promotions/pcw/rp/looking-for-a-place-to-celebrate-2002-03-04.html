Looking for a place to celebrate

4 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/04/2002 Boda lifts Chase up and proceeds to Boda Bomb him over the top rope and out of the ring. Chris Chase has been eliminated!!! Dave: We are down to three men. Boda spins around and looks at Mexi who smiles psychotically before leaping off the top turnbuckle and chokeslaming Miya through the table. The White Mexican and Miyagawa have been eliminated. Duke: Mexi just eliminated himself and Miya to give Boda the world championship. Boda stands in shock at what his friend just did and walks around the ring in a daze as the crowd cheers him on. Crowd: BODA!!! BODA!!! BODA!!! Dave: I never thought I would see the day that Boda would be called the PCW champion!! Jeff: And now PCW is doomed to the same fate as PCW2. Duke: I’m still shocked that Mexi would give up a shot at the title to let Boda win. Amazingly Mexi is up on the outside of the ring and hobbles over to the announce table and grabs the World Title and rolls into the ring. Mexi holds the title for a second and then passes it off to Boda who is still star struck and Mexi raises Boda’s hand in victory to a loud pop. Clay: The winner of the match in a time of one hour and twelve minutes the NEW PCW WORLD CHAMPION… BODA!!!!!! Dave: Boda has done it! What a way to finish off an excellent PPV!!! ‘I will never get tired of watching that. Man, this is what it is all about.’ Boda stops the tape of the match he was just in to win the World Title. ‘I need to go celebrate. What do you say hun?’ *It’s been only a short few hours since the PCW has embraced a new champion. But now all the hoopla is over, all the hype has come to an end. PCW is moving on to its next event. The champion [wow that feels so nice to write], and his lovely wife are back at the hotel they are staying at for the big event. The girls are asleep, and Boda, is still juiced with the adrenalin of winning the title. Stacey on the other hand......* ‘Chris, you know I’m happy for you. You know I want nothing more for you, but I’m tired.’ responds Stacey. ‘But, why don’t you go on out. I’m heading to sleep.’ ‘Well, if you think so. Okay.’ Boda reaches over and kisses his loving wife. ‘I’ll be back in a few hours. Let me go out into St. Louis and celebrate with my fans.’ ‘Be careful’ remarks Stacey, as she slides into the king-size bed. ‘I will’ Chris comforts her with his response. *Boda walks out of the hotel room, and closes the door behind him. He walks down the hallway, with a new swagger in his step. The new confidence is evident in Chris Sloboda. Finally, after failing multiple times, he wins the big one, in the biggest match ever for the title. He’s gone from jobbing to Shaq in a bad gimmick match, to becoming the World Champ. That’s the stuff legends are made off. Hell, Boda made such an impact, that Robbie Decker, the Boda-hating columnist was the first to congratulate him. Things are definitely looking good for the World Champ. Of course, the celebration only can last a few days, as in three days time, Boda has to defend his newly won title against the man that many thought should be the champion, Alexander Miyagawa. So, as Chris heads to the elevators, he is greeted by a young PCW fan. The young kid is excited in seeing a real wrestling star up-close. So, being the fan loving, World Champ that Boda is, he is more than obliged to talk with the kid, and give him an autograph. As the Champ and the little kid climb on the elevator, the kid is the first to ask Chris a question.* ‘So, Champ.... ‘ speaks the kid, ‘your going to beat Miya right?’ ‘Beat him?’, Boda looks at the kid with quizzical eyes. ‘I’m going to finish what I should of tonight. If it wasn’t for Mexi, I’d have grabbed Miya by the neck, and toss him to the outside like some Japanese trash. Instead, my friend did it for me, thus giving PCW what it truly needs. A true blue, deserving champion. And, kid, did you see Miya earlier, whining about how I needed to have Mexi to win. Well, Miya is so brilliant, he got eliminated by Mexi because Mexi was in the match. It’s not like when Miya brought Jeremy to the ring with him for nothing. Hell, Mexi being in the match was as big a surprise to me as it was to everyone. But that’s okay son. Miya can bitch, whine and cry all he wants. It won’t get him nothing more than an ass kicking and a cup of coffee. Yeah, he deserves a title shot, but he also deserves the moniker Howard Porter 2002. But, listen kid, I have to respect him somewhat. I mean come on, he tried really really hard. He just wasn’t smart enough to bet me. And Tuesday will be no different.’ *The elevator come to a stop at the lobby, and the little kid thanks Boda, and runs off to the kiddie video game room. Chris asks of the lobby man about where to go for some drinks. He is directed to the hotel bar. Chris heads that way, and looks in on the slightly busy business. Happy, drinking patrons seem to cheerfully enjoy the nectars of the liquid god, so Boda decides to join them. He walks in the door, and to the bar. Upon ordering a Bacardi Silver [official drink of PCW World Champion, Boda], the bartender notices who Chris is.* ‘Aren’t you that wrestler guy?’ asks the bartender. ‘Actually, I am that wrestling Guy.’, Boda responds, and the scene fades out. ‘I am Boda, the PCW World Champion.’
