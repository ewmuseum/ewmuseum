The Night Before Decimation (A Tribute to Christmas ... Sorta)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dec.22/02 ‘Twas the night before Decimation, When all through the arena,Not a wrestler was stirring,Not even Jon Kano or Vilas Lavagna. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ The Title Belts were hung in the Champ’s locker with care,In hopes that a REAL Champ soon would be there; ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ The superstars were nestled all snug in their hotel beds,while visions of suplexs and powerbombs smashed in their heads;And my girlfriend in her kerchief, and I in my cap,Had just settled down for a pre pay-per-view nap, When out on the lawn there arose such a clatter,I screamed from my bed to put an end to the matter.Away to the window I groggily stumbled like a snail,Tore open the shutters and let curses sail. The moon on the breast of new-fallen snowMade it hard to see due to the unbearable glow,When, what to my wondering eyes should appear,But that loser, Mark Amulet, in his wrestling gear,With his arm reared back, and the time on the caper,I knew in a moment he was throwing toilet paper.More rapid than eagles his projectiles they came,And he whistled, and shouted, And called out my name ... ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ “Hey, Mark! hey, Marvelous One! Hey, Michigan! Hey, boy!It’s me Amulet, Mark Amulet, the man with whom you should not toy!To the top of the ladder! to the top of the world!The winner will win! but the loser will be hurled!” As dry leaves that before the wild hurricane fly,I watched as roll by roll of toilet paper glided by,So up to the house-top the TP flew,Luck for me Amulet didn’t wipe them with p***. And then, in a twinkling, I heard on the roofMark Amulet had gotten a ladder, what a dumbass goof. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ As I drew in my head, and was turning around,Down the chimney Mark Amulet came with a bound. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ He was dressed in a mesh shirt, with black hair to his neck,And his clothes were tarnished with ashes and he looked bad as heck;A bundle of shaving cream, toilet paper, and fire ants flung over his back,He looked like a prankster just starting his attack. His eyes - did not twinkle! his dimples - he had none!His cheeks were so pale, his nose -it did run.His big, loud mouth was drawn up in a sneer,His stupid goatee reeked of nachos and beer; The roach of a doobie held tight in his teeth,And the smoke it encircled his head like a wreath;He had a broad face and muscle-bound belly,That resembled anything but a bowlful of jelly. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ He was a loser and a dimwit , and a little bit crass,And I laughed when I saw him, cuz he looked like a dumbass;A wad of spit on my chest it did splatter,Soon gave me to know I’d meet him on the ladder; Spoke many a word, but went straight to work,He spray painted my walls, that damn, dirty jerk,And laying his hand aside of his nose,And giving a nod, his middle finger rose; He dove out my window, and ran to his car,And away he flew, very fast, very far.But I heard him exclaim as he drove out of sight, “The ladder match is mine, so don’t bother showing tonight!”
