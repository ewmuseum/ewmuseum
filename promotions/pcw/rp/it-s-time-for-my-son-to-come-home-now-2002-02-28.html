It's time for my son to come home. Now!

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-28-02 No longer can the Krazed Lunatic talk about getting his son back. Now, he has to put up or shut up. His two year old son, the son that Jessica yearns to be home with her, is faced with being sacrificed if Jeremy doesn't do something quick. But, how can Jeremy do anything when he's been knocked unconscious again? Could this young baby, who has yet to really live life, be nearing the final minutes of his life? And, could this be the end of the Krayzie era in PCW as Jeremy Howard meets a similar fate? As fans worldwide pray for him, and his enemies smile down upon his current misfortune, the scene fades into the back of the old church. Fire rages in a large circle around a cross in the center. In the background, hardly visible because of the nighttime sky, the Krayzie One can be seen being dragged out of the old church. His head his face down, with blood flowing from his forehead. By the looks of his feet dragging behind him, it appears that the Krazed Lunatic is also unconscious. The Voice Of Keith Lipowitz: Bring that asshole here. I'll show him just who he's messing with. Suddenly, the evil foster parent of two year old Troy Howard emerges from the group of black clothed people. Keith, also wearing the same black garb as the others, walks up to where Jeremy's being dragged from and helps pull the Krazed Lunatic toward the fire and presumably the cross. Keith Lipowitz: Hold him right there. I want him to watch his very own son burn to death. A sickening look crosses the face of Keith Lipowitz. Jeremy's head begins to move. A crowbar shot to the back of the head might subdue some people and knock them out. But, this is Jeremy Howard. You can't keep Jeremy Howard down if he doesn't want to stay down. All of the sudden, much to the dismay of Keith Lipowitz, Jeremy lifts his head as the group of men bring him within ten feet of the fire. Keith Lipowitz: You just won't stay down, will you? Jeremy slowly moves his head back and forth, 'no'. Jeremy: You might have the upperhand now..... But you won't be sacrificing my son. Keith Lipowitz lunges our and grabs the jaw of the Krayzie One, jerking Jeremy's face violently face to face with his. Keith Lipowitz: That's not your decision to make, now is it? Jeremy: You try and harm my son, and it'll be your last breathe. Before, Jeremy's feet drug on the ground when he was brought out. But, as his anger begins to rise and the tension mounts between he and this Keith Lipowitz, Jeremy plants the bottom of his two feet on the ground and stands back to his feet. No longer does he need the help of these evil men to stand up. Jeremy Howard can stand on his own two feet, making him even more dangerous than before. Keith Lipowitz: Is that so? Since your so confident and think your some big shot, I want you to see this. Keith Lipowitz makes a motion with his hand to four of his men. And, before too long, its obvious what that motion meant. The four men bring grab a cage that sits off to the side of the entire procession and bring it forward. In it, lays a young child, crying his little head off. Keith Lipowitz: I'm sure this little guy looks familiar, right? This might be the first time Jeremy's seen his son since the day he was born, but every parent knows when its there child. Its unexplainable, but they just have this feeling inside of them. And, with the sight of Troy, Jeremy gets that same feeling. Jeremy: You were obviously a piss poor parent. Keith Lipowitz: Who said I wanted to be a great parent? You see, Richard O'Bannon wanted this kid dead. Why do you think he gave him to me? A hate filled look crosses Jeremy's face. It's official, the Krazed Lunatic is pissed off now. Jeremy: Because an asshole wanted to give an asshole a present? Keith Lipowitz sighs. Keith Lipowitz: There you go again, being stupid. If that pea brain of yours will let you listen for a second, I'll explain everything before I sacrifice the both of you. Jeremy just stares at him, not saying a word. On the face of the Krayzie One though, his feelings can be read loud and clear. Keith Lipowitz: Now, as I was saying. Richard and I, we go way back. High school. The Governor knows of my 'dark' side. H#ll, Richard has joined us on occasion. Who says politicians aren't fun guys? Still, Jeremy's eyes don't move from the face of Keith Lipowitz. Jeremy ears are listening to everything he has to say, but his mind is thinking. Thinking of just how he's going to carve this man's heart out and show it to his very own eyes when the Krayzie One gets the chance. Keith Lipowitz: Well, to make a long story short so I can get on with my fun, Richard asked me to take young Troy from the wonderful parents he did have and deal with him my way, since you put Richard's head through the window of my limo! Back then, only days before Jeremy won his first World title, the Krazed Lunatic came face to face with Richard O'Bannon and an associate of his in his quest to get answers to the freedom of his brother, much like he's shooting for this Sunday at March Madness. In that altercation, Jeremy put the man that would become Governor of Indiana, Richard O'Bannon, through the window of a limo. And now, it seems that's the reason why young Troy has been placed with this man and his occult of evil worshippers. Keith Lipowitz: I was happy to do it. I mean, my people love to sacrifice. Jeremy gets fully to his feet. He keeps it to himself, but now he's feeling fine. One has to wonder just how long it's going to be before this man snaps. Because, when Jeremy Howard snaps, it's dark and h#ll is hot. Jeremy: Your people? What? They forget that Halloween is only one day a year? Keith Lipowitz: You know what? Your pathetic jokes about my people are really starting to piss me off. I try to have fun with you and you ruin it. I bet this ruins your fun! Keith Lipowitz barks out an order to a few of his men. Keith Lipowitz: Take the kid to the cross and string him up. His dad wants to be a jackass, his son can burn for it. Jeremy tries to break free, but with six men holding him with everything they have, its to no avail. Jeremy looks up, seeing his son taken right through the circle of fire, into the middle. Troy appears to be fine, no harm done. But, the middle of the fire begins to become more visible. At the base of the cross, in the center of the fire, is a pile of sticks and branches. The men sit the cage, holding Troy, down on the sticks and branches. His little cries pierce the sky. Keith Lipowitz: This is going to be so cool....... I call upon the Higher Being to bless this! One of the men in the fire pulls out a large stick from underneath his garb. The man takes the stick and touches it on the fire that rages around them, catching it on fire. Then, he begins to move it toward where little Troy sits in his cage, crying. A man of elderly age, dressed in white clothing, steps out of the chuch, beginning to make his way toward the fire. Keith Lipowitz turns to Jeremy, looking him dead in the eye. Keith Lipowitz: As soon as he gets here, your son is going to burn! The man in white garb, presumably this Higher Being, gets a few feet from the church when, without so much as a warning, the old church blows up into pieces. Keith Lipowitz and his followers turn toward the church. It's nothing more than an afterthought now. The man in white garb is face down on the ground, with his white clothing blackened on the back. Jeremy: D#mn. That has to suck. Keith Lipowitz: Light that baby on fire! Jeremy turns his attention to Troy. The man with the stick re-ignites it with fire. Many of the other followers rush to help the man they know as the Higher Being. Jeremy: This is not going to go down like this! Jeremy, with everything he has, lunges out at a few of the black clothed followers, nailing them with kicks, punches and anything else that he can fight them with. Soon, the Krazed Lunatic is surrounded by these men. All hope seems lost because of how badly he's outnumbered, until help arrives in the form of the man they call Darkstar rising out of the ashes of where the church used to be. Jeremy: Now thats what I call an entrance. Keith Lipowitz: Arrgghh! Your son will die! Light that fire! The man with the stick raises it into the air. Fire still continues to rage all around where Troy sits. The stick begins to close in on the sticks and branches, when a certain Japanese-American superstar rushes out of nowhere and blasts three of the black clothed men with anything he can get his hands on. Only the man with the stick stands in his way. That certain man, better known to many in PCW as Miyagawa, rushes the man. The man with the stick tries to nail Miyagawa with it, but the wily superstar ducks the stick, then shoves his attacker into the circle of flames, setting his clothing on fire. Miyagawa grabs the cage Troy's in and races off with it. Keith Lipowitz: D#mn it all to h#ll! No! This can't be! Jeremy: I think it can be! With Darkstar disposing of many of the other followers with his special powers and various other ways, Jeremy comes face to face with the man that was going to burn his son to death. Keith Lipowitz tries to reason with Jeremy, but the Krazed Lunatic is having none of it. Jeremy lands a kick to his stomach, then a forearm to his face. Keith Lipowitz's head jerks back. Jeremy grabs him in a variation of the cobra clutch and drags him over to the circle of fire. The cross still stands tall in the center. Keith Lipowitz: What are you going to do? Killing me is a felony! You'll go to prison! Jeremys smiles, maniacally. Jeremy: Who said I was going to kill you? Jeremy looks around the area, noticing that the number of black clothed followers still standing is are less than the Asylum members present on the scene. Darkstar spots one more man running for his life through the woods. Darkstar smiles wickedly, dusting his hands off and watching the events transpire in the center of the fire. Jeremy: You like burning the cross and sacrificing people on it? I think it's about time I show you to respect the cross and my Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ! Keith Lipowitz tries to fight his way out of it, but to no avail. Probably for the sake of his fans, his son and the lovely Jessica, Jeremy hoists this Keith Lipowitz into the air and nails the Krayzie Death Slam right onto the sticks and branches that he was going to burn his son with. Then, Jeremy lifts this evil person up and ties him to the cross. But, Jeremy doesn't set it on fire. A fire rages in a circle already around this man. Instead, Jeremy jumps through that fire and begins to leave with Darkstar. Darkstar: Think anyone will find him for awhile? Jeremy looks back, seeing this man fight to break free of his current predicament. Jeremy turns his head back, smiling as he leaves Keith Lipowitz to die for all he cares on the cross. A non burning cross. Jeremy: For the sake of other people that have to deal with his sick mind, I hope not.... I just feel sorry for that cross having to have scum like that on it. Darkstar: Well, at least we got your son back. A smile crosses Jeremy's face. Jeremy: What would I do without friends like you guys. Darkstar: I don't know, but I just had a thought. Jeremy: What's that? Darkstar turns toward Jeremy, while they continue walking. Darkstar: You know when that fire burns out, he's still going to be hanging there. Wolves might eat him. Jeremy: Now that's something I could appreciate in some sick and twisted way. Finally, Jeremy gets to Miyagawa's car. And there, sitting on the hood of Miyagawa's car, out of the cage, is the two year old child of Jeremy and Jessica Howard. This might be the first time that young Troy Howard has ever seen his superstar father, but that doesn't stop him from latching on Jeremy and hugging him. Jeremy: I just want to thank you two men for everything you did. This the greatest gift a man can be given. Both men knod there head, acknowledging Jeremy. Parents and there children have a special bond that can't be broken by the powers of evil. For, in the end, good always wins.... much like it will Sunday, when Jeremy wins the freedom of his brother. The scene, a father and his two year old son reunited, fades into the night sky of Indiana.... to black!
