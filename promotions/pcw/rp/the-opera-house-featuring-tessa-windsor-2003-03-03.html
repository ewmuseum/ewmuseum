The Opera House (Featuring Tessa Windsor)

3 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/3/03 The Grand Hall was so full of life and chatter. All around people walked and talked among themselves. Suits and dresses flocked the hall. Among the people Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor make their way. They move past an old couple, admiring their love. Chris turns to Tessa and stared into her eyes. 'Tessa, I love you. But something seems wrong. What is bothering you?' he asked her. He was concerned with her and it was clear in his voice. He puts his hand on the side of her face and gently strokes her face. Tessa holds his hand and looks away from him. 'Chris, I've never been to a play before. I don't think I fit in here.' She tells him. 'I don't even know what is playing.' Chris chuckles to himself a little. He puts his hands on her shoulders and leans against her. 'It's called Phantom of the Opera. It's my favorite play. I am sure you will enjoy it.' Tessa looks down on the floor feeling rather guilty that she has no clue what Chris is talking about. To her Phantom of The Opera meant nothing more than a Phantom that happened to be in Opera and at the moment she had much greater thoughts on her mind such as the rematch that she and Chris would have against P.W.A on Friday. The ushers open the doors allowing people to enter the play house. The businessmen and woman enter slowly as Chris and Tessa make their way down the isle towards their seat. 'Yeah, I'm sure I will.' She answered quietly before taking a seat in the row closest to the stage. 'Come on, where is your excitement?' Chris chimed in with a cheery voice as he sat down with Tessa, his excitement level much greater than the level of Tessa who seemed like she was dragged here and forced to watch this production filled with people that she had never heard of before. Instead of answering Chris Tessa merely stared off into space, her mind overloaded with thoughts on what could possibly occur on Tuesday. There were many possibilities but only two mattered. They would either win or lose plain or simple and that was all that counted. The lights started to fade down as the chandelier slowly lowers. Chris grabs Tessa's hand and squeezes it lightly. He looks at her as the actors start making their way out to the stage. He leans in toward her and whispers to her. 'I love you.' He looks at her but Tessa is looking at the statues standing along the walls. Chris sighs and turns back to watch the play. The spotlight shone on the stage directing the crowd's attention towards the stage but Tessa's focus was not on the stage, instead it was on the images that were running through her mind. The last match her and Chris had with P.W.A. was nearly a month go but Tessa still remembered it vividly as if it had just happened the prior day and she still had bared the physical and emotional scars from that day in the form of a mark left on her neck from the purple creature of P.W.A., Nerezza. Chris's eyes glimmered as he watched the spectacle before him. The Angel of Music starts to sing and Chris' heart is lifted to a higher place. He looks over to his right where his love sits, hoping she was enjoying this experience as much as he was. Yet his bright smile fades like the spotlight as the curtains are drawn. Tessa isn't even looking at the stage. Her eyes were looking off into nothing, her hand running on her neck. Chris' heart sank into her chest. The stage manager walks out to center stage and informs the crowd of intermission. The Schmoopies make their way to the Grand Hall to wait for the second act. When they make it out to the hall Chris turns to Tessa and gives her a hug. She leans her head against his shoulder. 'Tessa, what is wrong? Are you not enjoying the play?' he asks her. His words are soft and light, almost inaudible to everyone else. 'Huh?' Hearing Chris' brings an end to the thoughts as she comes out of her daze and looks over at Chris who is staring into her eyes trying to figure out what is on the mind of the woman he loved with all his heart. 'You aren't enjoying this are you?' Chris retorted with a question filled with disappointment and expecting the worst. It seemed that Tessa had no interest in what was going on around her and would rather be back at home. 'No, it's fine.' Tessa simply answered. 'Tessa you don't have to lie, if you aren't enjoying this we can go home.' Chris stated, much more concerned with Tessa's feelings than his own. 'No, it's fine really.' Tessa answered once again. Chris's expression only fell lower and lower. His fist clinches up tightly. He grabs her by the arm and forces her over to the stairs that are marked off. Chris moves the rope and sits her down on the third stair. He looks into her frightened eyes. 'Something isn't fine Tessa. What is it? I want to know this instant what is bothering you.' Chris tells her. In his mind the images from Fusion play over and over again. He can see her teasing Erik Erratic. He can see her licking her lips as she stares at El Romantico. Were they ploys to make them drop their guard? Maybe, but he wanted to make sure that is what it was. 'Tessa Bear, you can tell me everything and anything. I love you that will never change. Now what is bothering you so? I want to be able to help you but I can't until you let me.' He tells her, tears' nearly forming in his eyes, his voice was cracking. 'Chris I'm fine!' She lied once more, unable to look him straight in the eyes. 'Tessa please just tell me what's on your mind.' He pleaded with her as he sat down beside her on the step. He looked over at her and noticed that her blue eyes were once again starting out into nothingness. Instead of responding Tessa remained silent thinking ahead of the challenge that stood before them this Friday in the form of the very first Pranksters Paradise match that could very well also be their very last match as Tag Team Champions. 'Tessa?' Chris said once more in attempts to win over her attention. She tries to turn away from Chris but he grabs her chin. He points her face toward his and looks at her in the eyes. He leans his forehead against hers. Tessa closes her eyes, Chris does the same. His lips press up against hers. Chris breaks the kiss seconds later. He leans back away from her. 'Let's get out of here.' Chris tells her. Tessa's eyes shoot open surprised. 'What? But Chris, the play isn't finished yet.' She tells him. 'Who cares? Let's go for a walk or something. Maybe we can hit a little café or something.' He tells her, with a little laugh in his voice. 'Are you sure? I thought you loved this play.' She asked. 'I do, but I love you more. Now come on, let's get the hell out of here.' Chris says as he stands up. He offers her his arm. Tessa gladly accepts it as the ushers start to let everyone back into the house. The two love birds walk off into the dark night. Even the pitches of black nights can not bother them though for they are together now. Tessa never told Chris what was bothering her but Chris couldn't care less. He knows she will tell him what the matter is one day. What matters the most right now is that she is happy and smiles. And so the two march off towards Friday. Although they are not sure of what will happen, they know that whatever it is they can handle it, as long as they are together.
