Know Your Enemy!

2 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02 December 'Keep your friends close, keep your enemies closer!' There was no denying that Gaz 'The Jester' Green and Noscosto Ireul Nerezza, collectively known as Pranks With Attitude had drawn a great deal of interest since debuting at Third Dawning and gracing Primetime Championship Wrestling television. From the moment they aligned themselves with Alexander Miyagawa in his re-creation of the stable 'Asylum' they became marked men from all aspects of the active roster. The lower card saw them as a stepping stone to further their own careers, after all this devious duo were little known as they learnt their trade in the pitiful promotion of New True Internet Championship Wrestling (NTICW). However Pranks With Attitude hadn't made the jump to be nobodies for a second time and all those who stepped up to the plate soon found themselves introduced to a Darker Side Of Comedy and the list soon mounted. Cowabunga Connection Duzza and Makonie Misplaced Some would argue that these weren't real threats and challenges, well if these weren't then the Front Office would surely be an uphill struggle, after all they had the man who stood atop the corporate mountain, CEO and World Heavyweight Champion Xavier Kannon. Asylum soon became the thorn in the sides of the Front Office, especially the rookies of Gaz Green and Nerezza who used their boss as their own personal stepping stone and too this day there is still a common hatred for one another. Above all these though there has been one group of individuals who could be deemed the true nemesis of P.W.A and like them they to had turned their backs on another promotion in the search of stardom. Both teams were new to the PCW scene, both wanted to make an impact and both wanted to out do the other and so began the war of Pranks With Attitude versus Playaz Inc. Through this hatred there is a spark of respect but now the feud has fizzled out, P.W.A have found themselves facing an equally impressive force-... SKITZO!!! The sound of bellowing laughter was the first indication of the opening of another Pranks With Attitude segment. The whaling was almost in unison as Nerezza laughed heavily like a Viking while his smaller associate cackled like Norman Wisdom, two polar opposites in the laughter spectrum. 'Gaz I must admit putting Skitzo on the same pedestal as Playaz Inc and Xavier Kannon was sarcasm at it's very best!' said the vampire trying desperately to control his rhythm of breathing. 'Well I fear Ghetto Prep Ellie over some demented, gibberish talking, soda endorsing-....' retorted the Emerald Menace before Nerezza hit home with an untimely interruption. 'STATUZ QUO WAS IN THE ALAMO MATCH?!?' Again the duo broke into a mass of hysterics, allowing the camera to bask in the variation of wardrobe as Nerezza sat slouched into the Jester's favourite leather reclined chair wearing his long flowing trench coat that partially concealed his black t-shirt and it's Gothic insignia. Gaz however made this day historical, long gone was the three-piece velvet business suit to be replaced by Special Forces camouflage overalls. 'We must take Skitzo seriously however, he has the advantage going into this bout, knowing full well that we have no clues to this mystery partner of his-... So in saying that, if Little Red Riding Hood should show up with a bazooka and a bad attitude I expect you to chin the bitch!' hissed the Angel Of Fear taking the threat of Mr. Psycho very seriously. The problem the dynamic duo had going into this match was the fact they were being kept in the dark over this mystery opponent or as Skitzo described him a monster. Even though they had earlier spoofed him and mocked him in front of millions of home viewers, the fact remained that Skitzo undoubtedly held all the cards leaving thousands of questions circling their minds unanswered. 'Was this supposed monster a former PCW performer?' 'If so was this person a huge star, or did he vanish into obscurity like Avalanche?' 'Could it actually be Avalanche?' 'Or bigger, some returning great like Doug Troy, The Reverend John Hull or the hardcore icon himself Ed?' Well the Champions could breathe a breath of relief at the latter. There was no chance Ed is going to return unless he had recently became a zombie thought the Jester, his question still not answered however. 'Whom has Skitzo aligned with?' Contemplating over this elusive answer, Gaz tapped his foot with frustration before moving over to an erect drawing board, which had paper strapped to it in what appeared to be some demonstration. Lifting the first sheet, he unveiled another with the words 'What do we know?' before hinting with some subtle coughing for his partner to pay a little attention. 'It's simple. We apply what we already know and deduct the possibilities!?!' roared the jovial Jester raising his eyebrows and gaining an agreed nod from the relaxed Nerezza who sat opposite. 'Well he hinted towards someone returning-....' said the Living Nightmare as Gaz folded the second paper over to unveil a pre-drawn list of possibilities and those who would be unable. 'Returning is the key here. Most of the old roster now reside in Ohmygodthatfederationreally Sucksat Wrestling (OSW) and I would bet Natalia as collateral that Jane Reagan would not allow anyone to jump ship-... So that deducts Ivan Stanislav, Alan Kriegman, the usual big name stars!' replied the Lord Of Laughter folding the paper over the top of the board once more, to yet another list, a scaled down version to those matching the criteria. 'He called this partner a monster and believe me who ever it is had that town spooked, his presence alone oozed intimidation, those people were paranoid almost to the point of-... SKITZO-ISM!!!' said the Spawn Of Evil Noscosto Ireul Nerezza. 'Exactly! Well from this list we can rule out Akiyama since he is already part of the PCW family and could you see them teaming up, maybe Cyclonica and Skitzo but not Akiyama?' bellowed Gaz Green with his own interpretation of a sergeant, portraying his act rather well before continuing. 'We can rule out Hessian himself, would he really want to destroy his own master-piece huh?!? Vampyr Nosferatu was turned into Carbonated Goats Blood at the hands of Hessian so I would be very surprised if makes a return even though he's immortal-.... Talking about being immortal that leaves Brooklyn Blade!' The very mention of FSW's 'The Immortal' Brooklyn Blade sparks a frenzy of laughter as the duo return to a pair of howling Hyena's, giggling at the man who once openly challenged the P.W.A behemoth Nerezza. Regaining their composure the Conniving Collective glanced back up to the board awe struck at the sole name that was left from their deductions Meanstreak. 'Err-... Arr-... That-... That can't be right, can it?' stuttered Gaz 'The Jester' Green, the name alone sending a cold chill down his spine, a feeling he hadn't felt since facing the hoard of greats at Alliances, greats like Tyler Nelson, Angelo Deville, Mike Marvelous and Timo Bolamba. 'Well he fits all the criteria! He's a hall of famer and any appearance would be deemed a return, nobody would argue that this thing is a monster!' replied Nerezza gulping, looking star struck at the evidence before him. Both individual's then in unison glared up at the sole name occupying the board, soaking in the prospect of being decimated at the hands of a true monster, probably hoping that they've made a mistake, but as it stands, the mystery opponent looks to be 'Meanstreak'. With their eyes fixed on the names bold lettering, bodies motionless, shells of their former selves the image slowly drew to a close, leaving nothing more than a snowy picture and the crackle of distort sound. - The End -
