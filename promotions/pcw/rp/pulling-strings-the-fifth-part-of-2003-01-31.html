Pulling Strings; The Fifth Part Of

31 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.31.03 Location: Da Volcano; Los Angeles, CaliforniaDate: January 30th, 2003Time: 12:34 pm 'Have you ever noticed that Skylar Green really doesn't have much to deal with his time? He sits down in front of a camera, thinking just what he can about what his opponents do. You can't have a life around that guy...' The voice of reason inside of Jackson heads lashes out, as on the outside, Dante sits outside of a recording booth, as the dials and buttons upon the sound check are placed at their right levels, creating an instrumental played overhead. The beat's producer, or creator Sik Wid It, a in house producer when Dr. Dre happens to not be in nods his head with a prowless as Dante cracks his neck sharply. Sik nods as a trail of weed smoke follows his lips as he takes a few puffs away at the short cigarette. 'Yeah, he's like the tweedle dee and tweedle dum of that entire triple threat match you have in New Zealand.' 'That's him, you can't change that.' Dante smirks as he leans back into the executive chair, the sheer stability coming atleast a inch away from toppling over to the floor. 'But in all reality, shouldn't he...' Sik stops him in mid sentence, 'He's a blind dude man, get over it...' 'Yeah, I know but -' 'HE'S A BLIND GUY! YOU DON'T MESS WITH THE BLIND! THEY'RE EVIL PEOPLE!' Sik screams and shouts rapid and quick quips about the blind, sending the chair crashing backwards to the floor and Dante along with it. Scratching his head, Sik stands up taking a picture of just how all that just happened. With a smirk on his face, he stiffens a chuckle. Before mulling into over blown laughter. Dante straightens himself up, dusting off whatever he got on the floor with his hands, inspection for a clean guy is modest at best although it does have its drawbacks by missing that one small spot and then you're ruined forever. All biet a situation Dante feels is inevitable, but just doesn't want to confront it yet. Just like the Triple Threat Match Up for the Continental title, lasting all of what will be about fifteen minutes or twenty, depending on how the staff at PCW Headquaters in Atlantic City think of how to build this up, sending it through the road guy through cellphone to read out to the wrestlers. Personally, its going to be a Match of the Week/Month/Year quality match up - if the three men involved stick to what they've been doing. Sik sulks back into the chair, pointing at Dante with an extended index finger, which Dante quickly jerks back and mutters, 'Ha.' Ouch was the momentary jab to the arm of Sik, real name Marcus Antonio Dobbs to those who checked up on him before he was ever hired as a Dre` protoge, anybody that went through Aftermath had to have some background rivalling that of your typical good with a mean streak type of personality. There were not going to be any 'Suge Knight' type of deals around here. There was no strong arming when it came to black folks and their brand of violence noway, remember they started the Rodney King riots because of of their own got beat on National Television and nobody became guilty for their acts. 'You gonna spit on him?' the question ran in Dante's head whether or not he should or should not do the deed he had promised so many others but had their chances revoked. The last time these two met in a ring, Dante Jackson lost by a countout, well this was the number one contenders contest that stirred the pot for Totally Tubular Tommy vs. Rent a Hero, Skylar Green vs. Rush Mason, and Dante Jackson vs. Chet King. Scratch Rush off of the plate and you currently have your Continental title scene, with many others looming in the picture. 'No,' Dante starts as he stares deeply into the ceiling above him. 'but if you analyzed exactly what he said during that entire promo, which was garbage` anyway, he pointed out that I was a superhero and that Totally Tubular Tommy had AIDS.' 'Garbage`?' Dante nods, 'Yeah, pretty but not needed garbage. My English teacher back in college called it that.' 'Do you think Green went to college?' Maurice asks. Dante shakes his head, 'There's not a college for the blind around here, a school for people waving heir heads around like Stevie Wonder and becoming wacked out superheros with modled chins and fine latinas shaking their butts in front of you, but no - there isn't one.' 'Aw...' Maurice sighs as he turns his way back to the board, starring at the keys lit up before manuvering a few that have been preset at zero. The beat harmonizes causing Dante to marvel at the sound, 'Keep it!' he mentions towards Maurice as his cellphone rings. 'Hold on a sec, gotta take this call...' He gets up from the chair, wobbling back as he takes another seat down near the main offices of the building with creme colored walls and various photos of the label's talented artists at work. 'Hello?' '...' 'Sure! So when's the tour supposed to start?' '...' 'The fifth?' '...' 'Yeah, its nice but you have to say - the fifth is a Wednesday, shouldn't you always start tours on a Friday or something?' '...' 'Right, only wack guys start tours that late.' '...' 'Who says I'm wack? I've got a better album that have of the schmose on tour now!' '...' 'He's overhyped, well I was too, but...um...I'm still better!' '...' 'Thank you very much for your insult, it has a place next to my pad and my load nine.' '...' 'I don't know why black people always carry guns, ask Charles Heston why is he still alive!' '...' 'I'm serious! Ask him!' '...' 'Because I'm also looking for his autograph, do you think he has a phone number of where I can reach him because it would be great if...' '...' 'NO, I'M NOT TOTALLY TUBULAR TOMMY!' '...' 'Just because I don't speak ebonics you think I'm a white guy?' '...' 'Well let me just go eat my watermeleon and piece of chicken then!' '...' 'Sorry, I was stuck on the black stereotype there.' '...' 'Uh huh, and you say that I have problems, listen there are blind guys out there trying to sabotage everbody in sight! Including the president!' '...' 'No, I'm not making this up!' '...' 'Fine, sue me if you don't believe me!' He hangs up the phone, before Doug, the office intern, a common white guy with all the nice guy white man features approachs Dante. Handing him a piece of paper, Dante stares at it for a seocnd before announcing, 'SUED?! JUST FOR SAYING THAT TOTALLY TUBULAR TOMMY IS A KEN DOLL?! GREAT!'
