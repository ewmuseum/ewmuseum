FUCKS of America

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26 Dec &#039;02 We open up backstage post Decimation where Chet King is walking out to his black Cadillac in the parking lot, when he’s approached by a ‘big boned’ man, wearing a plain white shit, tie, pants and glasses. On his shirt he has some sort of name tag, reading “Dr Roundstone”. Chet looks up at Dr Roundstone as he taps Chet on the shoulder. Chet King: Yes? What can I do for you? Dr Roundstone: Hi, I’m Dr Roundstone. Chet King: Yeah I know, it says so on your shirt. Dr Roundstone: Oh you noticed too? When I saw this shirt, I said to myself ‘This is you, this has Dr Roundstone written all over it!” I knew that I had to get this shirt. Chet King: Ah…yeah…that’s what I thought. Dr Roundstone: Thanks Mr King, you truly are as kind as people say you are. Chet seems pleased by this comment. Chet King: People say I’m kind? Who? Dr Roundstone: The guy who does the TV commercial for your show, he always is talking about what a kind man you are. Chet King: Anyway, what can I do for you? Dr Roundstone: Well I represent the FUCKS of America. Chet seems taken back by this. Chet King: You what? Dr Roundstone lets out a hearty laugh. Dr Roundstone: Hahaha! Don’t take the FUCKS part literally, it’s an acronym. Chet now understands. Chet King: Oh ok, what’s it stand for? Dr Roundstone: FUCKed Society. Chet King: I see… what do you want from me? Dr Roundstone: Our organization involves dealing with people with addictions, and one addiction that some people have is an addiction to wrestling. Chet fakes being surprised. Chet King: You mean people are addicted to wrestling? I had no idea! I didn’t realize that half the guys in PCW’s life long dream was to be PCW World champion. Dr Roundstone: No, you misunderstand me. I don’t mean people are addicted to wrestling, I mean there are people out there who are addicted to watching wrestling. This time Chet is actually surprised. Chet King: Seriously? Like who? Dr Roundstone: People at our clinic. Chet King: Maybe they’re not addicted to wrestling, maybe they’re just addicted to watching someone like, say, me. Dr Roundstone: No that’s not the case, in fact our studies have shown that you being involved in wrestling has caused some people to lose their addictions and never watch wrestling again. A tear appears in the corner of Chet’s eye. Chet King: Nonetheless, I’ll have to ask you for the millionth time, what do you want from me? Dr Roundstone: We were hoping that we could bring in a wrestler to talk to some of these people in the hopes that we can cure these addictions. Chet King: What would you want me to say to them? Dr Roundstone: Just something about how there’s more to life then watching wrestling, blah, blah, blah, the way to turn into a great wrestler is to stop people a great wrestling watcher, blah, blah, blah. Chet thinks to himself about this offer for a moment. Chet King: How much is the job paying? Dr Roundstone: $500 for each addict you cure, $200 for each addict you can get to stop watching wrestling for 2 months, $100 for each addict you can stop watching wrestling for 2 weeks and $20 for each addict you can get to streak at next years World Series while singing show tunes. Chet King: What was that last one? Dr Roundstone: Me and some of the other Doctors thought it’d be funny to get them to do that. Chet King: I suppose it would – you’ve got yourself a deal. Dr Roundstone: Fantastic, I have to warn you about a few things though. Chet King: Like what? Dr Roundstone: Some patients in the building, depending on what addiction clinic they’ve been attending might think that you’re an Amazon love princess. Chet King: Just remind me to stay away from them then. Dr Roundstone: You’ll also need to stay away from some people who think that you’re an Amazon love warrior. Chet King: Do those people attend the same sessions as the Amazon love princess people? Dr Roundstone: No – you’ll also want to stay away from the people who think you’re an Amazon love king, and the people who’ll think you’re an Amazon love Burger King Whopper Burger. Chet King: How many different ‘Amazon Love’ sessions are there? Dr Roundstone: About 15. They’re actually all on the third floor aka the ‘Amazon Love’ floor. Chet King: I think I’ll just stay away from the third floor all together. Dr Roundstone: Good idea. When do you think you’ll be able to come by? Chet King: I can’t come around on Wednesday night since I’ve got the Carnival of Carnage, and then I can’t come around on Friday night because I have a shot at the Continental Title against Totally Tubular Tommy, and then I can’t come around on Sunday night because I have a number one contender match for the Continental title….oh wait, Sunday night should be free because I’ll have won the Continental title by then. It’s just a pity that I didn’t win it tonight. If only I’d beaten Dante, I just got unlucky. But I won’t be unlucky on Frida….. As Chet King continues to talk to himself, he hasn’t noticed Dr Roundstone, who’s left a note on Chet’s car saying ‘Come by whenever you’ve finished talking’, running away after being scared by Chet’s non stop talking. As Chet continues to talk about how he’ll have luck on Friday and talk smack about TTT and Dante, he doesn’t notice the camera man, and thus our view of the scene, running away.
