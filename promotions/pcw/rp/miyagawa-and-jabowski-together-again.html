Miyagawa and Jabowski... Together Again

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8th April 2002 (BST) or 7th April EST. The duo reunited... on stage? The scene opens on Miyagawa, wearing his trademark goggles, but this time around his neck. For some reason, he seems to have shaved off his goatee, and also for some unknown reason, he stands there, on a white background, wearing a crisp tuxedo. A gust of wind catches his hair, and tussles it, leading to the suggestion that he is in fact standing outside. He strokes his chin for a second, hinting that he shaving was in fact a new thing. Steve Jabowski wanders onto the scene from off-camera, also wearing a tuxedo. The problem with Steve’s tuxedo is that it appears to be the same size as Miyagawa’s. That may not seem so much like a problem… apart from the fact that Steve is at least two sizes larger than Miyagawa, and the tuxedo seems rather… strained on him. Steve scratches his neck at the tightness of the collar, and eventually just gives up and pulls off the bow time, turning and throwing it off camera, and opens up the top three buttons of his shirt. Steve sighs. Steve: Why do I always put up with this c##p? Still it’s better that we’re doing something backstage at the Duck Pond than me trying to con my daughter into paying for me to watch Spring Fling... incidentally, you did get me decent tickets, didn’t you? Miyagawa pulls out his wallet from his right trouser pocket, pulls out a bill and holds it up in front of Steve’s face. Steve snatches the bill from Miyagawa’s fingers and shoves it in his pocket. Steve: Now I remember. And the tickets? Miyagawa: I gave you the tickets already you old fool, how do you think you got in already? Steve grins like an Amish with a new ploughshare. Steve: Shall I get on with the script? Miyagawa: Yep, hey Mr. Cameraman, begin taping now! Cameraman (from off-screen): Erm… I started half a minute ago. Miyagawa: Oh… ok, let’s just get on with it then. Steve: Right… well an Englishman, an Irishman and a Scotsman are all trapped on a desert island. Miyagawa grins sheepishly at the camera, like a television presenter on crack. Miyagawa: Desert Island… how queer. Steve looks a bit nervous at Miyagawa’s obvious adlibbing. Steve: Erm… queer as in unusual of course? Miyagawa: Yep, not queer as in Chris Chase… and out… and in… and… Steve (interrupting): I get the idea. Anyway, they’re trapped on a desert island, see? Miyagawa: Ok, desert island, got it. Like that Tom Hanks film? Steve looks confused. Steve: Huh? Tom Hanks wasn’t on a desert island in Forest Gump? Miyagawa: Castaway gimboid, he started talking to volleyball, didn’t he? Steve: You mean that he went that mad in a film, and he’s never appeared in a psycho’s promo for a wrestling federation? Miyagawa: Seems crazy doesn’t it… I mean Hannibal Lector has been in tonnes of Clyde’s promos… Steve (interrupting again): Well he was in one. Miyagawa: Ok, he was in one of Clyde’s promos. The camera zooms out a little, and the white background ends. In facts it looks like Steve and Miya are standing in front of something white on a hill. Something quite large and white and standing up like an advertising board. A man in a panama hat seems to be standing a few feet away from Steve and Miyagawa, wearing a panama hat. He turns sharply as he sees the camera and walks away from the camera; going off screen… his face is never seen… Steve: Right, Englishman, Scotsman and an Irishman… Miyagawa (inquisitively): Where are they? Steve (impatiently): On a bloody desert island Miyagawa (wittedly): So that would be in the red sea then? Steve quick smacks Miyagawa around the back of the head. Miyagawa raises an eyebrow at Steve, and Steve fishes the bill back out of his pocket and hands it back to Miyagawa, who shoves it into his jacket pocked quickly. Steve: Right, in the desert… they find this lamp, right… and the Englishman rubs it. Miyagawa: Ah, trust the English to always rub someone up the wrong way. Wasn’t it Prince Phillip who went to China and warned British students not to turn slitty eyed? Steve just shrugs. Steve: Ain’t got a frickin’ clue, don’t keep up with the British monarchs… however Princess Stephanie of Monaco… now she’s hot. Miyagawa: Lamp? Steve suddenly snaps out of a daydream. Steve: Oh yes, the Englishman rubs this lamp, and out pops a genie… Miyagawa: As they do. Steve: Yes, indeed. Right and this Genie says “I usually give three wishes… but seeing as there’s three of you, I’ll give you one each.” Miyagawa: Not a very fair genie is it? By all rights, the Englishman, as he was there first should have got three wishes. Steve: If you’re going by that, then the Native American people should gather the taxes across the USA. Miyagawa: And the Neanderthals should demand tribute from everyone in Europe. Go back to Poland, buttfinch. Steve looks agitated. Steve: I AM NOT POLISH! Miyagawa (calmly): Ok, Mr. Jabowski. Steve: AND I’M NOT JEWISH EITHER! Miyagawa (still calming): Did I say that your nose is more crooked than a banana? No, I don’t believe that I did… nor did I mention Shylock or started to dance around talking about a pound of flesh. Anyway, wishes? Steve sighs again. Steve: Right wishes, so seeing as he rubbed it first, he got given the first wish. So seeing as they were all stuck on this desert island, the Englishman said, “I wish I was back in England”… and poof… he disappeared. Miyagawa: Back to England, right? Steve: Yes, back to England, quiet correct. Then the Scotsman got his turn, and said “I wish I was back in bonny Scotland.” And poof, he disappeared. Miyagawa: What did the Irishman say… and was this Irishman perhaps related to Jon Carrick? Steve: Actually the Irishman said “Byjesus… may name is Jon Carrick… and I be so lonely, so I wish that the others were back here to cheer me up.” There is silence for1…2…3…4…5… seconds. Miyagawa: So what’s the punch line then? Steve just shakes his head disappointed. Miyagawa: Right well, we took up too much time with your warm up joke… so can someone throw me a script? Some technician appears on screen and throws Miyagawa a pad of paper and a pen. Miyagawa: Ok, let’s make some cuts. Miyagawa leafs through the pad, pulling pages out seemingly at random, and scribbling through the lines… he does this for a minute or so and then throws the paper back at the technician, and puts the pen into his jacket breast pocket. Miyagawa: We’ll just do that. The technician flicks through the pad and shakes his head as if he feels like a huge mistake has just been made. Technician: Ok… Miyagawa: SHUSH! YOU DON’T HAVE A SPEAKING PART! The technician nearly jumps off screen. Suddenly a plastic skull gets thrown on screen, and Miyagawa catches it and holds it ala’ McBeth. Miyagawa: For naught Corey Williams… I knew him Steve. What troubles the mind but the slings and arrows of dodgy gay gimmicks? How dastardly it can be to have a face that looks like a sphincter, and a speech impediment, which makes him sound like Kermit the Frog on cannabis with a bad case of the munchies. Miyagawa throws the skull back off screen, and in return he gets thrown a Hannibal Lecter style mask. He quickly straps it on. Miyagawa: Clarice… The guy with the Panama hat and the sunglasses appears back on screen again, and just shakes his head in disgust, before leaving again. Miyagawa quickly takes off the mask and throws it off screen. Miyagawa: There done. Steve: That was months of planning I spent writing the script for this promo, and you cut it to pieces, the plot has been completely removed. Miyagawa: Exactly, Steve this is Hollywood after all. Steve: Dude, you are one old bastard. I doubt anyone else even remembers Jon Carrick. Miyagawa: I could have said Paddy the Irishman, but I actually like Paddy. Jon Carrick was just a big green and yellow muppet. Much like Chris Chase is, except of course he’s just a big gay muppet. Steve: Did you see that advert for Spring Fling... the Old... the New...... the Current. Apparently you’re the old. Miyagawa: Not when you’re standing next to me, I’m not. Miyagawa grins at Old Man Jabowski as the camera fades out on the man who hopes to be the new Primetime Championship Wrestling Champion... and of course saving his baby’s life would be a major bonus, but since the hospital is a lot more optimistic now then they were, he’s less bothered... and of course Steve Jabowski... the man who makes everyone else in NTICW seem young. Here’s to Spring Fling... Here’s the winning... Here’s to something else as well... erm... ok... damn... cut the power already... no... I look like a prat still talking, cut the DAMN POWER ALREADY.... NO!!! CUT THE PO...
