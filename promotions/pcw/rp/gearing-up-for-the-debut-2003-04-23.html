Gearing up for the debut

23 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-23-03 The scene opens to the man in the black trench coat sitting on a stool in a pitch black room. Mystery Man: “The time draws closer my opponent. Time draws nearer and nearer to your realization. Adam Stryker, the man who I must go through to win back what I once held. The man who stands between me and my ultimate comeback! I have been in all forms of the PCW. The time is coming and the time is now. The extreme title, only the first step in my reign of terror, only the first step in my PCW domination and my stride to the top of the ladder, this will be great and unfortunately for Adam Stryker, his time is running out, his days of thinking he’s something special is running short. I say the time is now and I damn well mean it. Adam may think he’s something special, but he’s nothing compared to the greatness that is me and quite frankly ‘That’s A DAMN Fact!” The man in the black trench coat stands up and flips on a light switch to reveal a pair of sunglasses, a hat, and a long black leather trench coat covering himself, even his face. The man in the trench coat walks over and pops open a bottle of SoBe and drinks a good portion, then tosses it down on his bag of wrestling gear. The man in the trench coat walks over and takes a seat back down on the stool, looking at his locker room door as the knob slowly begins to turn. After the door slowly opens, in walks some common PCW stagehand with a clipboard in hand. Man: “What may I do for you young sir?” Stagehand: “Um I just am on my rounds checking whose all here this evening.” Man: “Yeah, you’ll find me under Mr. Surprise for now, but after tonight it’ll be something new. So you know I’m here and will be here for the entire night, until my hand is raised as the Extreme #1 Contender to the prestigious PCW Extreme Champion! I am the extreme of extreme and I’m going to prove it.” Stagehand: “You sound familiar Mr. Surprise.” Man: “Well I was the guy screwing your wife last night. She was quite annoying when she kept yelling about how she never felt anything from you and your short dingaling, said she’s glad she met me.” (laughs) Stagehand: “MY PROBLEMS ARE MY PROBLEMS!” (starts to cry) Man: “Dude! I was joking! I have a girlfriend, and god keep that crap to yourself!” The stagehand leaves the locker room crying as the man in the trench coat sighs, lays on the floor, and takes a long nap lying on his back. The man in the trench coat jumps up and locks the door then goes back to sleeping on the floor of his locker room. The scene cuts off to black. OOC- Sorry for two short pieces of crap, hopefully they’ll get better after tonight.
