Exploring the Soul Part 2: On The Run

9 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/9/03 Exploring the Soul Part 2: On The Run It was like I was some type of infection to their society. They tried to get rid of me over and over until the only option I had remaining was to run. So I no longer had a home, not like I did before, but now I found myself in a new place every night in a world where I would never be accepted. It was hard in the beginning, but I learned to cope with it. I would picture myself as a heroic nomad in the deserts of the Sahara like in those long novels that give you inspiration in life. Unfortunately it didn’t always feel that great. Every night was a hard night and a lonely night. Those nights are things I’ll never forget. How did it all begin? Eduardo Morales was his name… He was a bastard of the worst kind. He didn’t care about the struggles people were going through, he just wanted to see the newspapers praising him every morning and lowering the rates for crime and poverty. Accept he wasn’t getting rid of crime and poverty; he was just chasing us out of our homes. How so… Well it was a dry July day. Barcelona was going through a heat wave and as anyone would know in time of discomfort is when the crime rates go through the roof. The past director of the police department had been fired and Eduardo was just brought in. He was a big man with a thick beard and broad shoulders. He always had about four or five officers with him and I swear this guy never slept. He was always roaming the streets scaring the hell out of poor innocent people. Nobody liked him, not even the people he was trying to help. He came off as a real heartless bastard and everyone knew he was. His job was his entire life. I never saw him with a family or anything and he was working all the time. I take it you ran into him a few times… Not just a few times, we were practically best friends we met so occasionally. I wouldn’t go a week without finding myself in a prison cell over night. The cells weren’t very nice either. It was always filled with these drunks and transvestites. Eduardo wasn’t very fond of people who were different. He kind of reminded me of a Hitler. He wanted this little perfect society out of Barcelona, even though it was destined to fail. Did you ever think about just leaving Barcelona? I thought about it, but I could never bring myself to doing it. Even though I had no real home, Barcelona was where I belonged. I didn’t have much, but I had Barcelona and I knew it was never going to leave me. That was until Morales tried to chase me out. I was already on his list, and I was on it for doing absolutely nothing. I was just a slight dab of paint on his work of art. What he never understood was that his work of art could never fit the standards he made it out to be. We were fine the way we were and he just created more havoc. People began to rebel. It was like the perfect society versus the outcasts in society. I wish I never got involved in it. How did you get involved? Well I was pretty sick of this entire running around, so one day I decided to join the stand. What I didn’t know is that the stand would soon be revolving around me. On my way to a rally I had fallen over some laid out wire in a dirt field. I cut my lip and slightly ripped my shirt. It was a little bloody, but nothing too bad. When I got to the rally everything went as planned and I was actually excited about being involved. Toward the end I saw a camera flash in my direction, but I didn’t think much of it. That was until the next morning, when my face was the center of the Barcelona Star. There I was bloody and dirty with the caption over my picture saying “How Much is Too Much”. I’m guessing Mr. Morales wasn’t very happy? You could say that, especially since that little caption ended up causing him to lose his career. At first I was happy, but then I remembered he would recognize that face on the front cover and he’d get his proper revenge. I wasn’t exactly scared of him, but he did give off a frightening light. Maybe it was because he always had four or five men by his side, but who knows. Did the two of you ever meet again? We met again and I wish to God we never did. What happened when we met again may have changed the course of my life as I see it now. I used to be calm, tame, and very respectful. After the night of August 8th that all changed. What happened on August 8th? I don’t think I can really say what happened. I’ll just say something unfortunate happened from an unfortunate incident. After that incident I packed up and left for good. I never went back to the outer edge of Barcelona where I was born after that incident. I guess I was too afraid. I decided to take myself to the center of the city. It was huge and haunting. I dreamt of what happened on August 8th every single night. A month later it seemed like all of my emotion were drained. I no longer cared or felt like caring. I practically changed over night and I don’t know why. I went to sleep as a young scared boy just wanting to survive and woke up, as a hateful emotionless man who did whatever it would take to get ahead. What did you think of what you had become? I didn’t really think much about it, but now that I look back on it I see myself acting the same exact way that I did. Is that a good thing? I…. I don’t know.
