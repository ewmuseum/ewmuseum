Date: Tuesday, June 11th, 2002 So the day begins. I know this only because I can feel the sun penetrating the shades and warming my skin. Another day another dollar a wise man once said. The only problem is I only work on Tuesday’s, Fridays, and Saturday Nights. This day does not produce a dollar for me. -The voice of Duke Williams is heard as the shoot opens up with him laying in bed.- My head aches from last night’s freedom of woman, whiskey, and weed. The dollars have been spent, my balls have been licked, and the grass now simply grows on my brain like a tick-digging deep into skin. My own choices lead to my own paths. I need water I can feel the dehydration over taking my system. It feels like somebody has hooked up a water pump to my toes and are sucking my fluid out of me. -Duke remains laying on the bed in nothing but his boxer shorts. A smile flashes over his dry lips.- I do remember the sucking I got last night from I think her name was Tiffany. Maybe it was Tammy? Who the hell knows now. Sex should be the last thing on my battered mind this early morning. Pancakes should be the first. I could really go for some pancakes. I big glass of water too. -Duke sits up at the end of the King size bed and scratches his hairy chest after a big yawn.- I could turn on the news but will I find? War, scandals, murder, you know it really has been a wonderful year for TV. I have to take a piss then I must hit the gym. Its been a few days and I can feel the love handles starting to fill in. -Duke grabs the side of his stomach which is tight as can be.- I must eat right today. Just lean chicken on wheat bread. I have a big match against Akira on Fusion. No drugs. My be just a joint to regroup. -Duke walks over to a drawer and pulls out a joint and lights it up.- That feels just about right. Akira thinks I’m preparing to look into the eyes of darkness but little does he know he’s the one who needs preparation. Does he know who the bad guy is? A confused soul who thinks he knows all. The reality of it is he knows very little. When you think you have it all figured out the game changes and so does the scenery. I however remain consistent. The roots will always remain when the winds of time cut and chaff the rest of the world. -Duke takes an other hit then puts out the joint and heads to the fridge where he grabs a bottle of water and drinks it down.- The human body needs to be replenished everyday. Becomes rather redundant if you ask me. That’s the way he created the human body. Convenience wasn’t one of his major concerns when he started up this little experiment. Now he’s lost control of the whole game and I’m running the show. I’m still stuck with the basic design with no major overhauls planed. Too much work. I reckon laziness is my biggest flaw. You know what they say nobody’s perfect. -Duke walks towards the bathroom and stubs his toe on the way. He cusses out in pain then hops up and down till the pain subsides.- Damn it. Pain what a joke. No matter how well we tune our bodies and create optimum machines out of them they still feel pain. No way around it really except to accept it. You know that phony Jesus Christ told us to accept the things we can not change. HA that’s why he was hung on a cross and only still alive in fairy tales. -Duke walks into the bathroom and starts to urinate.- The way to combat pain is to hurt it before it can hurt you. When I step in the ring Friday with Akira he’s going to have nasty intentions of doing terrible things to me. I can crucify myself and just accept it or I can combat it with Malice. He’s looking punish, I’m looking to kill. Before he can rake my eyes I need to rip his out. Is that the new TV guide? -Duke’s attention turns to a magazine laying on top of the toliet.- It is. I’ve searched the damn house for this thing. F*ck it. How many times are they going to put Star Trek on the damn cover? Where’s the Duke Williams addition? Ahhh vanity don’t you love it. -Duke shakes out the final drops and zips up and washes his hands then his face.- Akira was exhaled from Japan. My thinking is because he was too ugly even for their standards. So why do I get stuck fighting him? Everybody know I like to fight outspoken, flamboyant, cocky kids. In the eyes of the fans, win or lose, you always walk away with more respect. They make me look better then I already am. -Duke wipes the water off his face, winks, and blows a kiss.- Akira is different. He comes across as a deep thinker and a dark kind of guy. Not my style to say the least. The kid who dresses in all black in highschool to make a statement gets beat up. The stylish jock that has a following of friends influence a lot more people while beating up the statement freak. Dark and mysterious if for the raven or the bat. In your face, honest, and a tad crazy is for the wrestling ring. -Duke walks over to a dresser and pulls out a pair of jeans, T-shirt, and socks.- I haven’t heard anything from the punk yet. Probably meditating on his next set of words. Trying to create a moving speech that will revolutionized the wrestling world. Then again maybe he’s just stuck in his pathetic little life jerking his little meat fearing the thought of stepping in the ring with pure Malice. -Duke puts on his jeans and shirt and heads to the kitchen.- I wonder if I have any poptarts left. -Duke scourers the cabinet and only comes up with eggo’s- Damn these will have to do. I can really go for a fruit filled pastry right about now. Sh*t stop thinking about food and focus on your match. I realize this match is of epic proportions in my wrestling life and to a lesser degree Mr. Akiyama’s. He’s a big son of a bitch for a jap. Towers over me, out weighs me, faster then me if you can believe that. I do however have the experience and know how on my side. This is a must win match for me. My first real singles match since my return that could end with a clean out come. I have something to prove, while Akiyama will prove to me just how bad he is. -Duke’s eggo finishes heating up. Duke places it on a plate and heads to the coach. He wants to send me to the void. Everybody knows that is Ellie~! crotch and everybody has been there. I’m going to send him on a much less pleasant trip. To a place thought to be only fiction. He’s a deep thinker so I hope he study’s what I will have to say. Akiyama will be sent home. Whiskey Bent And Hell Bound. -Duke eats his eggo as the camera fades and the narration ends.-
