The Golden Saga: Self Esteem. (Ending)

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-24-02 The shot fades in, black painted lips formed into a sneer. A smile maybe, but definately a defiant sneer. Defiant towards who? Those that doubted him perhaps. Maybe even those below him. The view pulled out and DarkStar stood smugly, leaning on his vintage Stingray. The car had been his dream for years, its been the representation of his life to this point. When he was a child, before he even knew how to spell Goth, his father had a Stingray. It wasnt vintage, but it was beautiful. Ever since then DarkStar wanted to own one. When he first signed with PCW he and Talena finally had the money for it, it was purchased with 18,000 miles on it. It was a show car. DarkStar drove it anyway. It was like a child. It was a child with his dream coming true in front of him. Its kind of ironic that the future Lord of the Dream Realm had his own dreams about such petty things as owning a car. He did though. When he bought it he realized that he finally made it. So here he stands, leaning against the perfect black car, parked in front of the Hertz Car Rentals. He tapped his black painted nails on the hood of the car, waiting to be helped by the clerk. Inside the car, Talena turns the radio up a little louder, the sound system kicks in and the vibrations from the bass cause DarkStar to stand up straight, he looks into the car and Talena just smiles. zAo is on, thats loud enough to get someones attention. DarkStar grinned as the door to the large rent a car agency opened up. A chubby middle aged man, balding of course, walked out in a cheap suit. His combover flapped about in the breeze. DarkStar couldn't help but chuckle. Talena turned the music down as the man approached. 'Hello, can I HELP you.' He said it really snidely, DarkStar wanted to punch him in the face. He must of had no clue about who he was. DarkStar pushed his sunglasses down and looked around him, feigning confusion. 'Oh, you were talking to ME like that, I thought you were talking to a common gutter trash, not someone like me,' DarkStar took his glasses off and put them in the pocket of his Illig 40' Bondage Pants, he brushed some dirt off of his trench coat and stared at the chubby man in front of him. 'you see, I should be top priority over the rest of your customers. Isnt it obvious?' The man looked him up and down, noting the various piercings and metallic spikes poking out from his wardrobe, he made a nearly disgusted face and shook his head. 'You will wait in line like everyoen else, Sir....' he emphasised sir with a heavy dosage of sarcasm. 'Listen! I am Gavyn Drazail, and my boss sent me here to get a car and store my old one, now if you don't hurry up and waddle your little dwarf like legs into that place, get me my car keys, and let me get the hell out of this... place, then I'm going to make sure that you, my friend, can't breathe without the help of a rather large contraption with metally bits.' DarkStar took a menacing step forward, well as menacing as he could be at least. The manager held his hands out and stopped DarkStar for a second. 'Drazail? Wait... you work for Mr. Nelson right?' DarkStar nodded and ran his hand through his hair. 'Why didn't you just say so!' The man motioned for DarkStar to come with him, DarkStar followed and held out one finger to the car, Talena nodded and waited. They walked past rows and rows of cars, the mans cellphone began to ring and he took it out. DarkStar looked around him, none of the cars appealed to him. The only car he ever liked was his Stingray. The man answered his phone and stopped. 'Yeah, hello sir, he's right here, no I haven't shown him yet... yes... yes... ok.... I will, right away....' he extended the phone to DarkStar, he made a face but answered it anyway. 'Hello?' 'Yeah, hows it going Darky', he recognized the voice right away. 'Hey Tyler, how are things on the boat, i'm trying to rent a car but i'm running into difficulties.' 'Dark, you don't need to rent a car, everythings all taken care of for you. You are going to realize soon how good it is to be partners with the king.' DarkStar raised his peirced eyebrow, now following the man again. He had a finger to his ear to drain out the noise from the highway nearby. 'I have no clue what you are talking about, Tyler.' There was a chuckle from the other side of the phone, a rather insane chuckle, but a chuckle. 'Let me just say this, I pulled some strings, worked some people over a little, and got this done for you. Consider it a... congratulations present, on your victory over that sad bastard Legend Inc.' DarkStar was still confused but had to stop before he ran into the back of the of this car place. He had a large smile on his face. In front of him was obviously a car, but it was under a gray canvas cover. 'What the hell...' DarkStar muttered, 'Just wait and see Dark, did he take the cover off yet?' Almost as if he heard Nelson, the cover was removed, revealing a brand new 2003 Porsche 911 GT2, beautifully painted black with the fading purple when the light hits it just right The interior was a dark shade of blue, just like DarkStar loved. His jaw dropped at the site of the machine, it was beautiful. 'Im assuming you like it, the sound system alone cost more then we pay most of the Rising Star division. It goes 0-60 in about 4.5 seconds. That should prove to be alot of fun for you.' DarkStar could barely talk, he circled the car, the fucking hood ornement was the grim reaper on his skeletal horse, the liscense plates read 'DrkStr' and... yes, it had hydrolics. A Porsche with that much power and hydrolics with a sound system to boot. 'I cant take this Tyler, you know how I am, this is way to far above me... this is... this is....' Nelson silenced him 'This is what you can do now, Dark, your the fucking PCW Continental champion, You are entitled to some perks, just because you're gothic doesnt mean you cant accept a expensive gift. It doesnt mean that these people you wrestle and see every day arent below you,' there was a pause, 'it really only means you have better fashion sense and a stronger blood lust then them' 'What about my car, I love that car.' DarkStar ran his hand over the beautiful Porsche. 'The rental agency will have it delivered to your garage, although im sure you could sell it if you wanted, its not like you will be driving it much anymore.' There was more silence. 'You better get to the boat, I have you and Talenas room saved near the top of the boat. Great view.' DarkStar looked at the interior, it really was a great color blue... 'Remember Dark, you're better then all of them, if I were you, I would act like it more. You have everything going for you now.' DarkStars black lips curled up into a smile as he opened the drivers side door, 0 miles and a full tank of gas he noted. 'Thanks Tyler, i'll see you on the ship.' He handed the phone back to the manager, the smirk never leaving his face. 'I am better then all of them.' It zoomed in to his face, the same smile we saw in the beginning. Everything fades to black with only the sound of the powerful engine behind it. fin
