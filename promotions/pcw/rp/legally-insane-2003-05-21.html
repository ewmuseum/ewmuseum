Legally Insane?

21 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 - 21 - 03 ‘I haven’t a clue why I’m here…’ Dorn mumbles more to himself than he does to the ratty looking man in the cheap gray suit and tie with black polka dots on it. Lying back on the comfortable red satin chair with his hands clasped across his abdomen, Dorn doesn’t appear to be too livid about his whole ordeal. However, deep within the recesses of his mind, it’s quite the contrary. ‘After all, It’s not like I’m a paranoid loony who thinks he’s being stalked by a murderer! Tsh…’ The headshrinker looks up from his scribbling on the clipboard and stares at his impatient… well, patient with a confused look through his broad, wide-rimmed glasses that just DEFINE geeky, ‘Mr. Tracer, the woman that recommended you be brought here told me that’s EXACTLY what your problem is!’ The glare he gets from Tracer in return, however, causes the doctor to lower his gaze and just start a line of questioning that avoids the subject of a “masked stalker.” ‘Mr. Tracer, can you tell me about your childhood?’ ‘Why?’ Dorn inquires, not too concerned by this whole thing. The doctor pushes his glasses higher up onto his nose and clears his throat, ‘I think it may lead us to understand why your religious nature drives you into a paranoid state of mind.’ The absolutely dirty look he gets from Dorn nearly makes the young, ratty man piss his pants in fear, but he quickly takes hold of his composure and takes a deep breath. He scribbles some things down on the clipboard, which Dorn pays NO attention to, and gazes at another anonymous sheet of paper that sits on a small circular table just adjacent to him. ‘Mr. Tracer, it says here that you follow a religion that some mistake for a science, which leads you to believe they are committing “blasphemy”. Can you clarify?’ ‘I guess. Try to keep up though,’ The Astronomer snidely remarks with a grin on his face. He turns over on his side and props his head up in his head, looking around at everything other than the geeky psychiatrist. ‘I make it plain and simple enough that I follow a cliental of gods known as the Tiamats, from which many stories and legends branch. Astronomers, who are distinguished followers like myself, perform rituals and make sacrifices like any other religion. Yet, morons still get them confused…’ ‘Well, it DOES happen to have the exact same name as a type of spiritual study,’ The doctor chimes in, with a hint of confidence in his voice. Dorn shoots him another dirty look and shakes his head in disgust, his eyes closed, ‘Try not to be so stupid. And besides, what does this have to do with the Druid?’ However, Dorn doesn’t realize that he never mentioned the Druid, and the confused look on the doctor’s face puzzles him greatly. ‘What’s your problem?’ ‘The Druid?’ At that moment, the Astronomer realizes his mistake and his face goes slightly pale, since he really never wanted to elaborate on the conflict with his masked stalker. ‘Ummm… yeah, the Druid. Some masked fruitcake that goes around and bothers me every chance he gets. It’s not really a problem.’ Unconvinced, the doctor gives a puzzled hum, ‘I don’t know, Mr. Tracer. The lady that brought you in mentioned ‘masked stalkers’, as you know. I think you may be denying that this is your problem.’ The doctor scribbles down something else on his clipboard and pushes his glasses back up onto his nose. ‘In any case, can you tell me a little bit about the Druid?’ ‘He’s a fucking masked stalker that has caused me nothing but pain!’ Dorn spits angrily, ‘What is there to explain?’ ‘Who do you think it is, Mr. Tracer?’ Dorn throws his hands up like he’s been defeated and stares at the ceiling, ‘Gee, if I knew, it obviously wouldn’t be a problem! For all I know, it’s the fucking ice cream man!’ ‘Do you see this so called “Druid” often?” The doctor looks down at his clipboard and then back up at Dorn every couple of seconds as the Astronomer responds. ‘Of course! He’s all over the fucking place! In fact, I saw him in the bathroom before I got here! I couldn’t attack him because I was pissing, but I’ll be damned if that fucker didn’t run scared,’ A sly grin crosses the Astronomer’s face and he drums his fingers together. The doctor looks up slowly and raises a thin eyebrow, ‘In the bathroom? Are you saying the Druid just appears?’ Dorn scoffs, ‘Well, I’m glad SOMEONE understands…’ The doctor nods and looks away, scribbling some more things down on his clipboard. After a moment, he sets the pencil down, takes the paper off the board, and hands it to Dorn. A confused Astronomer takes it and stares at it for a moment before crumbling the paper and bursting out in anger, ‘LEGALLY INSANE?!?! YOU INFIDEL! JUST BECAUSE THE DAMNED DRUID DOESN’T HAUNT YOU IN YOUR SLEEP, YOU DON’T BELIEVE ME! I DON’T HAVE TO TAKE THIS SHIT!’ Tracer storms up out of the recliner and kicks the clipboard right out of the doctor’s hand, causing him to draw back in fear. The Astronomer storms out of the office, knocking various things over on his way out, before slamming the door behind him. The doctor sighs in relief and shakes his head, ‘What a nutcase…’ [End.]
