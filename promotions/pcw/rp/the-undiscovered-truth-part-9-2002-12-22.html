The Undiscovered Truth (Part 9)

22 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/22/02 The Undiscovered Truth Part 9 They think they can find me, but they’ll never find me. Sometimes I sit and wonder what goes through other people’s minds. Is it like mine, or is it something completely different. I wonder if there are other people in this world exactly like me, and if one day we can ever meet. My only crime I ever committed was being born, and yet that simple thing has led to so many problems for me. I hate to say I wish life was never given to me, but I just think God could have used it more usefully on someone else. I’m on a plane ride to Canada for Decimation. The people next to me seem so happy and merry. I look at them and see the smiles, the tickling, and the love. Then I look at myself and see a big empty black hole. This boy and his mother will never have to go through what I went through, and yet even though were all human I’m so different that we can’t be associated in any way. They think they can find me, but they’ll never find me. They make it like I’ve committed this heinous crime. They never took the time to think about my life, just their wallets. Sometimes I try to love all people, because that’s the Christian thing to do, but then people like this fill my soul with the anger it craves. I WILL NOT GO BACK! Mark my words and tattoo it on my arms, for I will not be a subject to experiments. My self respect runs high, and my respect for the rest runs low. There are few people I can look at and see a warrior within. People try to make like their wise and have had hard lives, but those are the same people who shudder at the words of my life. Someone one day will see my name in an old paper. They’ll do some research and then see the little amount of information gathered on me. It will then become a big spectacle over my life. Then the same people who tried to kill me will come back and said they knew me well. They’ll live off of the fame of my life, and it will never even faze them to think about who I really am. I know it will turn out that way, for wasn’t Jesus treated the same way along with all other unknown heroes. Why I always think about these thoughts is beyond me, but there is just nothing else to think about. They think they can find me, but they’ll never find me. Their minds can’t think like mine. They can’t make assumptions to where I’ll be, because the places I go don’t exist in their world. My life is a mirage, and they think they’re right their and have me caught, but then they realize that nothings there. It’s not like I can disappear with a snap of the fingers, or change the appearance every time I open my eyes. It’s no magic; it’s just the way it is. I already know what they’re thinking, and I know that they fear me. Why fear me? You are probably asking yourself. Well this is why. Have you ever stuck your hand in a dark box where you didn’t know what was inside? Well for that split second you realize something can go wrong and your body reacts that way. That’s kind of like how I am. You think you can catch me like any other person you’ve been after, but when you get close you hesitate because I’m different from the rest. There have been time I’ve feared being caught, but then I’ve just remembered how little these men and women are capable of. I wish them the best in their lives, and no they’re only doing their jobs, but they don’t know what they’re getting into. I don’t want to harm, but I will when it’s for my own cause. I WILL NOT SURRENDER IN THE FACE OF DANGER! I’ll preach my words so loud that the heavens will hear them. I’ll continue my life without changing a thing. I’ll walk around like I’m any other man. I’ll say hello to the police in the morning. And I’ll scream my name through the streets. I AM EL ROMANTICO! *Everyone on the airline draws their attention to Romantico and gives him and odd look. Romantico just smiles back them and he continues his thoughts to himself. * They think they can find me, but they’ll never find me. I am indestructible. I was put on this world to live long and complete God’s goal, and by God I might not know what it is, but I will complete it. YOU will not put a stop to this, because I may help satisfy your scientific ego. I love my country and I love my people, but I abandon it all for myself. I believe in doing what is right, but if doing right is sacrificing yourself to help an endless massacre. Then I might as well put an end to it. They think they can find me, but they’ll never find me. It’s because I don’t want to be found. The lawyers, the financers, the owners, the managers, they won’t let anything happen to me, because in PCW I equal money for each of them. And even though I’ve always been able to handle things by myself. It sure is a lot better, when you have support behind you.So some on Spain, I dare you to come after me. Try and use me as an experiment, and you’ll have to fight a war in the meantime. They think they can find me, but they’ll never find me. And that is all that will ever matter.
