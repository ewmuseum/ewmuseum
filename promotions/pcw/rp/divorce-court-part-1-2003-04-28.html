Divorce Court: Part 1

28 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 28, 2003 Within the definition of a friend, it can be found that friends help other friends out. Tyler Preston and John Gargano are thought to be friends, yet the actual status of their relationship is still a little cloudy to Tyler. There hasn’t been to many instances in which John has come to save Tyler or help him out in a bind. Tyler wishes that they could be a little closer in terms of helping each other out. However, be careful what is wished for, because that very wish could come true. Tyler Preston walks into the cold confines of the Rochester Municipal Courtroom. Tyler leads Kalli as he places his hand on the small of her back. As he walks, he holds his blue tie down with his other hand. He figured that since this is a special occasion, he should ditch the bow tie and wear something more traditional. The room is made dominantly out of lightly stained woods. The sound of the duo’s rubber soles makes a relaxing rubbing sound against the multi-colored carpeting. Tyler leads Kalli all the way down to the wooden gate, which separates the benches for the general population, from the front where the participants of the case sit. Tyler opens the gate for her, and she enters. Her scarlet red suit coat brushes against his cotton blue, button down shirt. Tyler takes her arm to guide here through, and then lets the heavy gate swing shut. Tyler looks around the room. He examines the official New York state seal that decorates the front of the judge’s podium. He glances to the left to the table to see a ratty Al Sutton seated, chewing on a toothpick, and picking a piece of lint off his mother of pearl tie. It doesn’t exactly match the shirt. “So are you going to be alright with this. Taking on any type of case can be tough when you are representing yourself,” Tyler asks in concern. He wants this case to go off without a hitch so that there are no longer any more asterisks by their relationship. He also knows that Kalli’s soon to be ex-husband, Al Sutton, is a conniving man that will take advantage of even a beautiful young woman such as Kalli. “Yes, divorce cases aren’t to tough, and a lot of the time they don’t even require a licensed lawyer. The way that I see it, if Al can take on this case by himself, than I sure can,” Kalli says with a smile, offering a small jab by stating her opinion on her ex-husband’s intelligence level. Tyler smiles, mostly humoring her, still feeling a little concerned. “I’ll be sitting right over here. I know that it might not seem to professional, but if you have to, call me over. I’ve had a little experience in courtrooms, but that was mostly to fight off charges that I was being racist or inciting a riot. Can you believe that?” Tyler asks in a sarcastic tone of voice and a huge smile that spreads across his entire cleanly shaven faced. Even at stressful times, Tyler can insert a strategically placed joke to lighten the mood. Perhaps it is this fact that draws Kalli so closely to him. She grabs the sleeve of his suit coat in thankfulness. “…And you have no idea how much that means to me. Thank you so much for coming out here, and supporting a stupid mistake that I have made,” Kalli says in a serious tone, and just whole heatedly means it. She is ecstatic that Tyler does not hold a grudge against her for keeping this marriage a secret for so long. Tyler smiles a modest smile, almost proud of the fact that he did not lose his temper and end the whole relationship when he found out. “It’s no problem kid. We’re going to get through this, and we’re going to do it easily because I have big plans for us,' he says with a chuckle in his voice as he points his stubby index finger right to her noise. She giggles along with him. They both find comfort in the fact that after the mess is by them, they will be together with out any problems to think about. Only a bright future stands in front of them from this point on. “I wouldn’t have it any other way,” she says as she steps up on to her tiptoes and gives him a quick kiss on his smooth pale cheek. He smiles as she swipes her right palm across his face to vanish any residue that may have been left behind by the lipstick. Tyler swipes his own palm against his cheek to make certain, and then walks through the stained wood gate and sits in the front row of the general population benches. He gazes at the back of Kalli’s head and is lost in thought as he follows the weaves of her medium length black hair that is tied up in a bun. He leafs through thoughts and memories that they have had together, and how they will only get better once thing are mended in this area. His dark eyes wander over to the other table where a gruff looking Al Sutton sits with his chin in his hands. Tyler does not despise the man as he should on account of Al’s shrewdness, but instead, he is thankful for the fact that Al wants to get this behind him as much as Tyler and Kalli do. As Al leafs through a few papers that set in front of him on the table, a person approaches Tyler from the right side and gives him a tough bump to the hip. Tyler glances up, to discover a suited lawyer, wanting to jockey is way in to the front row. Tyler sneers and moves over. The lawyer doesn’t sit down, but stands over Tyler, casting a shadow on his whole body. Wanting to know what the hold up was all about, Tyler looks up closer to see a bushy black haired John Gargano looking professional in his three piece suit wielding a brief case as a tasteful accessory. Tyler is surprised to say the least to see his good friend showing up at a very personal event. Tyler must inquire, because he is really confused at this moment. “John, what the hell are you doing here?” Tyler asks, maintaining a balance in his voice between pure confusion and inquiry. Tyler reexamines John and wonders what his latest scam is all about. Tyler hopes that John wouldn’t soil this important case with one of his childish jokes to get back at him. “Word on the street is that your little princess needs a lawyer,” John says, attempting to lead Tyler in to understanding why his presence is being made known at the courthouse. Tyler just stares at John raising one eyebrow in still dominant confusion. “Yea, she called around for a while, but couldn’t find anyone. It looks like she’s flying solo today, but things won’t get to complicated,” Tyler replies, hoping that by answering this question, John will reveal what on earth he is talking about. John smiles at the confirmation that Kalli is receiving no legal help at all. “That’s what I thought, but don’t fear, because Robert J. Turner is at your service,” John says in almost an English accent as he executes a slight bow to an even more confused Tyler Preston. Preston scratches his bushy blonde head and begins to find himself annoyed. He has no time for childish pranks, and he wants the case to get under way. “John, sit down, you’re annoying the hell out of me,” Tyler says, giving up on even trying to figure out what it is that John is leading to. John does not follow the orders, because he knows that his services are in great need here. He knows that Tyler will get a kick out of it. He begins to explain to Tyler the deal that has been going on in his mind. “Tyler, can’t you tell? I’m Robert J. Turner from ‘Real People, Real Cases’ the movie? I’m glad you know all the roles that I have starred in. Anyway man, you and your little girlfriend are in dire need of my help. It just so happens then when I was studying for my role as a lawyer in the movie, they paid for three months of full law school. I have this little cases down, and I know what I’m doing. I can help her out,” John reveals, hoping that Tyler will stand up and hug him for coming up with such a great idea. The opposite happens as Tyler shakes his head in disapproval, not wanting anything to go wrong in the case. He doesn’t want to add more stress to Kalli’s situation. “No John. This isn’t a game anymore. This is Kalli’s life and we better just butt out. She knows what she is doing out there. I’m glad you came but lets just sit this one out and watch things happen,” Tyler pleads, hoping that John wasn’t to fixed on this idea. John indeed was, and isn’t about to take a feeble ‘no’ for an answer. With a smile on his face, he departs from the disgusted Tyler Preston. He charges through the wooden gates and plops down in a royal blue chair next to Kalli. He slams his brief case down on the mahogany table and begins to sort through papers. Kalli is speechless and she looks over her shoulder at John Gargano who seems to be out of character, as he looks professional. Actors, they obviously are their own special breed. In a blind rage, Tyler almost jogs right on John’s tale. He attempts to swing through the wooden gates just as John did, but is met at the gateway by a large African-American male. Judging by the gray uniform with a sterling silver badge, Tyler does not think it would be in his best interests to continuing moving forward. The security guard looks down at Tyler with a snarl. He has obviously had a long day, and is not in the mood for more unruly punks messing around in his courtroom. “Where do you think you’re going buddy?” The man asks in an extremely booming low voice. The security guard puts his hands on his hips to show that he means business. Tyler has no intentions on creating a scene on John’s account, so he begins to back pedal to the seat that he was previously sitting in. The security guard nods his head in approval, and is proud of another job well done. Tyler curses John out under his breath. He begins to worry about the outcome of the trial, praying that it will go off without a hitch. With John in the mix, anything can happen. John turns his head back to Tyler once he gets his papers get, and gives Tyler a little wave. Tyler experiences great anger, until he finds that Kalli is also smiling. She obviously feels confident having John in her corner. Tyler feels more at rest as he runs his fingers through the knotted blonde hair and sits back with a deep breathe. Kalli turns back around and flips a few strands of her medium length black hair back at Tyler. John adjusts his red power tie as he glances over at the sorry opposition. He snickers. “That’s ‘Old Man Sutton’ huh?” John inquires to Kalli, making sure that it is his correct opposition before he begins trash talking. Kalli nods her head to signify a ‘yes’ and John just shakes his head. “This is going to be to easy,” he continues with confidence. Kalli just smiles, hoping that John is correct. John hasn’t been right much before in the past, yet both Tyler and Kalli are beginning to confide a little trust in him. The black robed judge enters the room with a stern face and his silver eyebrows jetting down over his eyes. The door to the small doorway behind the podium slams shut as he releases it from his hand. Everyone in the courthouse rises to their feet before the security guard, who now serves as the bailiff calls everything to order. As everyone rises to their feet, the bailiff’s loud booming voice vibrates the furniture and wooden support beams. “ALL RISE. THE ROCHEST MUNICIPAL COURT IS NOW IN SESSION. THE HONORABLE JUDGE HAYES IS PRESIDING IN THE CASE OF SUTTON VERSUS STEARNS. PLEASE BE SEATED,” the man bellows out in almost a scary tone of voice. He steps back with his thumps sticking under his belt. The robed judge Hayes sits down behind his podium with a now light smile on his face as he sifts through papers. The reason for sudden mood change is unknown. John stares at him intently, not wanting to miss a beat and let his friends down. Tyler sits back, biting his nails. He trusts John, but perhaps not enough. “We have case number four hundred fifty-three with Sutton versus Stearn in a communal divorce case. Not a lot of complications with this one as I can tell by the documentation. The real issue at hand today is who gets what out of the relationship in terms of financial benefits. Mr. Sutton, I can see that you are represented by yourself and miss Stearns, I’m not sure if I have become aquatinted with your representation thus far,” Judge Hayes says as he glances over to their side of the room. He initially thought that Kalli would be representing herself, but was thrown for a loop when he caught a glimpse of the grinning John Gargano whom checks to see if his hair is still securely gelled down. “I am sorry, where are my manners your honor? John Anthony Gargano, I will be representing Miss Stearns today. I am sorry for not further notice, this was kind of a last minute thing,” John says professionally in attempts to make the right first impression on Judge Hayes. He even rises as he speaks so that hey can pay the judge the utmost respect. “I see. Please be seated then Mr. Gargano and we can be on our way,” the judge instructs in a dull tone of voice. This suggests that he has been present in the courtroom for quite sometime, and is getting somewhat tired of trivial cases such as this one. Here comes John Gargano to save the day. Even though Tyler insisted that no help was needed in this situation, John proves that he is a great friend by helping out the cause. John plops back down in the chair with an excited expression on his face, glad to be trying something new. Tyler just shakes his head and wishes that things could for once be normal in his life. Something that hasn’t happened since he met his interesting counterpart. John’s heart is in the right place, but is his legal skills?
