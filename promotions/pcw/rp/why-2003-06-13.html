Date: 06/13/03 A dark scene filled his mind.. Monotonous surroundings, the art of blood embroidered on the walls, a figure’s footsteps pounded upon the ground creating fearful audio. Abundant blades, imaging utter, malevolent, violence. The figure paused, overlooking an escritoire. A missive came folded over it; the figure gained it, flattening it. The name came with a menacing and wretch description. The scene visualized the truncated missive.. The carnage filled in the view was described as that of the precise, presentiment profligacy. All under the stoking enmity for the brutes (women). The degenerate and diabolical name to summarize the demented vision.. Stone Burns .. And it was not slander. The animosity was so deep that name became a threat. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Disturbed by the ongoing nausea produced by the itinerant PCW airship Stone had been continously flying over in. What also roiled him was the past time, the date that precisely began his misogyny trip, even though very irrelevant as he'd been musing for something else like his Fusion match in particular and his newly found foe, Lacuna Debris it still was connected as it was HIS name. 'Manuel Labor - Extreme?' he studied, twitching from side to side in the showy sofa aligned in the center of his room in the upper decks of the airship. Athwart from him, he walks over and looks through the outside viewing window. The white, cotton-like clouds swinging from side-to-side like a pendulum in a pale blue sky. Clearing his mind, he leaned over, extracting a document from a single drawer. Riffling through it, he looks at the contents, the details of his upcoming opposition, Manuel Labor. Walking away from the delight of the outside skies, he comes back in to the bleak, prosaic darkness. With the document in hand he reads through the biography.. Nothing much of a monstrosity to his size nor' weight, along with the image of his face.. nothing but a full-time, mexican janitor. He perches above the sofa, arm-rest handle and continues to riffle, almost visibly but inaudibly chuckling to the traits and physical characteristics. Dropping the document to the ground, he rested his head back over the middle of the sofa, leaving his feet over the handle. 'Manuel Labor - Extreme? HA!' he repeated, emphasazing it to the end, and gesturing his hands around his waist, literally donning an 'upcoming title' to his waist. Closing the lids of his eyes, the thoughts that were almost perpetually harming him emotionally came over him again.. The name, Lacuna Debris. A women. A possible misogyny victim that was actually disturbing him inside, along with the stomach nausea. The possible, next PCW Extreme Champion, Stone Burns continued to think for a women, the brutes, and the easy prey. Why was a women actually worrying the paragon of misogyny himself?
