The Hunted Saga Part Six: Memory pt 3

30 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/3/02 The story so far… In Part One… The hunted man known to the army as David J. Constantinople and known to the world as John Crichton, found himself lying in a piss stained alleyway, with no memory of who he is and more importantly what he is. In the last instalment he had discovered that his name was John Crichton and discovered that a company ‘Zemoff Industries’ was apparently to blame for his memory loss. In Part Two… Crichton follows the information given to him by the homeless man to the hotel, where Crichton is confronted by three men, who claim to be working for Zemoff Industries. After a brief scuffle, Crichton manages to over power them and gains a firearm and a piece of paper containing Zemoff Industries address. Crichton dons a disguise and gains access to the Zemoff building. All that is left to do now was to rescue Aeryn Sun from a heavily armed force, with no weapons and escape undetected. The story continues… The camera opens up where we last left Crichton, on the 20th floor of the Zemoff building trying to escape the clutches of the martial artist Security Guard. Crichton rounded a corner and paused for a breath. That’s when he noticed the fire alarm. Crichton broke the protecting glass barrier and pressed upon the white dot. Instantly the alarms sound, louder than Crichton could stand. Within a blink of an eye the once empty corridors became crowded all running furiously for the stairs to get their asses back down to reception. The security guard is seen walking in the opposite way from the crowd, walking ‘upstream’ if you will. Crichton looks around the place for some kind of implement, which could potentially be used to knock the security guard out cold. The security guard gets closer and closer to Crichton’s position, and then with a blur Crichton rounds the corner and makes his way at speed at the security guard. The security guard notices a foreign object in Crichton’s hands, but it doesn’t register until it’s too late, as Crichton swings the fire extinguisher, connecting with the security guards skull. He is knocked instantly unconscious. “The bigger they are the harder they fall, or something.” Crichton comments before dropping the extinguisher to the floor. Crichton then grabs the security guard under the arms and drags him into the nearest maintenance room. Crichton opens the maintenance room door and stows the unconscious body of the security guard in, placing him between a heavily used toilet brush and a very unclear mop and bucket. Crichton gingerly steps out of the maintenance room and quietly closes the door behind him. Crichton needed to think about his. He just couldn’t very well walk into Mr. or Mrs. Zemoff’s office and demand Aeryn back without getting killed. Crichton looks around, for many things can give inspiration, and Crichton’s gaze fixes upon an air duct at the bottom corner of the corridor. Crichton instantly runs over to it and attempts to gage the size of the duct, compared to his own size. Crichton then nods, as if to say the sizes are acceptable, and then begins to rip off the casing leading to the duct. After a couple of tugs, the casing easily rips away from the wall, into Crichton’s hands. Crichton quietly climbs into the snugly fitting duct and attempts to replace the covering behind him. The camera cuts to inside of the duct, as we are placed in the point-of-view of Crichton as he crawls through the duct. Crichton crawls for awhile, before coming to the first covering leading into another section of the building. Crichton stops and looks down into the room. The room is deserted, it appears to be some kind of staff kitchen, with a toaster, a microwave and a kettle, amongst other things. Crichton didn’t get too caught up in such trivialities and continued his search for Aeryn Sun. The camera then cuts to a view of a good portion of the city called Chicago, as the camera pans around to the left to reveal that we are placed in the luxurious office of the owner of Zemoff industries. Big gold writing is imprinted upon the window pains, of the companies’ title, and the room is comparable in size to that of the reception area. Twenty stories below this current room. The camera pans further down, to reveal a severely over sized desk. Just behind the desk we can see the girl from Crichton’s picture, or Aeryn Sun as he used to call her before the memory incident. Aeryn is tied to a pine wooden chair, with rope, but she is not being gagged in any such way. Then another woman dressed in an expensive looking purple suit, about 30-40 years of age, with an abundantly too much make-up on, casually strolls into shot and sits upon the desk before Aeryn’s eyes. She opens a small, rectangular gold box upon her desk, and casually extracts a cigarette and places ii her mouth. “So my dear, Ms Sun, where do you think your beloved John Crichton could be hiding, eh?” The woman asked bluntly. “I have no idea and you know that I don’t, Miss Zemoff.” Aeryn replied with venom in her voice. “Oh yes that’s right, he abandoned you earlier today, by leaping from out moving car. He hit the pavement pretty hard, and before we realised he had gone, he was too far down the alleyway slums for us to venture, without arousing suspicion anyhow.” Ms. Zemoff stated. “I’m glad he’s escaped your evil clutches witch!” Aeryn spat. “We’d better find him soon or your light will be permanently extinguished!” Ms. Zemoff replied. Aeryn just looked away, trying to hide the tears in her eyes. Ms. Zemoff intantly notices this and attempts to use it to her advantage. “Whatever is the matter my dear?” Ms Zemoff probes relentlessly. “It’s nothing, I just thought that if I were to die, it would by Crichtons…” Aeryn trails off as a hefty siren is heard. Ms. Zemoff looks around confused. “Is that the fire alarm? Carlos get over here.” Ms. Zemoff demanded. The camera follows a, Mexican looking, fellow with long black hair as he approaches Ms. Zemoff with a trott. “Yes Ms. Zemoff?” Carlos asks. Ms. Zemoff moves a little closer to Carlos, “Take the other clown at the doorway, and go and see if that was real or simply fake.” Ms. Zemoff demandingly whispers. Carlos trots off again, nodding to the other butch looking man, as breaks off his position to follow Carlos, to the reception area. Ms. Zemoff paces back and forth in front of her death muttering things. “Of all the time, it had to be now. Lousy timing.” The camera cuts back to Crichton as he continues crawling through the very refreshing air ducks of the Zemoff building. Crichton had already passed five other duct vents and had yet to uncover the whereabouts of Aeryn, Crichton had suspected that this was just like a rat in a maze, and that in this lifetime he would never find the right room. That was until he accidentally slipped down a vertical duct shaft, hitting the vent cover hard and landing in a precarious situation, with his ass indented into the vent. “Aw…hell why does this shit have to happen to me!” Crichton began “At least it can’t get any…” Crichton’s famous last words as he falls ass-first out of the air duct, nine foot to the floor, hitting the red carpet flooring with a thud. Crichton figured that he had broken his tailbone, as his eyes panned around his new surroundings. Crichton noted the style of this luxurious room as his eyes fixed upon the sight of Aeryn tied up to a chair and then fixes upon the rather attractive looking female sat upon the severely over-sized desk. Crichton painfully, managed to get back to his feet. He strolls over to the woman and stops at about ten feet away. “Hey lady, is your boss Zemoff around here?” Crichton questions bluntly. A smile comes across the woman’s face and then the expression turns to that off an innocent little girl. “No I don’t think so.” The woman replies. “Oh for god sake Crichton she is Ms. Zemoff!” Aeryn shouts angrily. A very shocked look appears upon Crichton’s face as he replies to the comments. “What? Your not telling me that…” Crichton begins. Ms. Zemoff strides forward and executes a perfect martial arts kick sending Crichton stumbling backwards. Crichton feels his nose, and looks at the red liquid that appears upon his finger. “I’m so gonna kick your ass for that!” Crichton bellows as he lunges in with a roundhouse right. Ms. Zemoff blocks the punch and replies with a stiff back hand slap to Crichton’s jaw. Again Crichton stumbles backwards. “What in the hell’s goin’ on here? Why does it always have to be hard? Why can’t it ever be easy? It feels like I’m fightin’ all three of Charlie’s Angels right here.” Crichton mumbles as he begins to charge at Zemoff. Zemoff swiftly moves out of the way, sending Crichton, crashing into the table, knocking Aeryn down with him. Aeryn’s chair crumbles and the ropes simply fall off her once bounded hands. As he rises Crichton grabs a chair leg, as Zemoff comes in for the kill. Crichton stands up and delivers the blow with lightening fast speed and accuracy, smashing the woman around the jaw with the piece of wood. Zemoff flies backwards, tripping over the upturned table as she goes, hitting her head hard upon the corner of the carpeted step. “Ain’t that a bitch when that haappens!” Crichton simply states helping Aeryn rise to her feet. Aeryn simply looks up at Crichton’s bloodied face and says “You nearly got your ass kicked by a woman!” A smile is raised upon Crichton’s face he sees the irony in her comments. “Sorry I couldn’t get here sooner, but I had amnesia…Lucy so I…” “Hey hold on…” Aeryn begins, before seeing the grin upon Crichton’s face grow larger and larger. They then kiss and like a tidal wave, the one kiss, manages to bring the majority of his memories back to him. After the embrace, Crichton wishes that some of the memories, didn’t come back to him, but it was his life and he wouldn’t change it for the world. The camera fades to black as Crichton and Aeryn leave the room.
