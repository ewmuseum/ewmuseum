The Devil Owns a Supermarket Branch – Part Five: The Whimpering of Bear Trapped Dinghies…

28 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th July The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book Three: Deus Ex Toaster…… RP#4 for PPV… (28th July 2002)… Chapter One: The Devil Owns a Supermarket Branch – Part Five: The Whimpering of Bear Trapped Dinghies… For your viewing comfort – this Promotion has been rated “M”. This Promotion is not suitable for people expecting random poetry, have a low tolerance of morons and of a seriously nervous disposition. This Promotion, however, is suitable for people expecting a cleverly random yet relevant adventure featuring ‘Saint’ Chris Chase, have a craving for off-the-wall mockery, and need something a little different from the Roleplay Durch Technic. This is your preview to Wrestle Vessel – Please enjoy without spawning a cycle of spontaneous orgasms. Thank you for your patience… -------- Once upon a digital watch, in a place right on your doorstop… “PaCHOW! --- Great Lucifer’s Toenails – I can’t believe that someone would just leave a bear trap right in the middle of a tourist zone…let alone leave half a dozen! Jesus Chris, you could have been mincemeat if you hadn’t have managed to get the rubber dinghy stuck in there – YOU BASTARDO!” “ARGH! WHAT have I told you about acting like a complete FREAKING retard Dustin? You can go buy yourself another rubber dingy from that rustic seaside shack about half a mile along the promenade. Insulting me in Spanish is no way to treat someone who just happened to get chased around an arena by a complete lunatic, only to be nearly maimed AGAIN two days following!” “Oh Yeah…Well, at least you didn’t get firebombed by that Satanist. Oh wait, that’s a good thing, isn’t it?” “…….” ::.~(Musings of Chris Chase)~ Cowardice isn’t brilliant, it isn’t particularly something that I’m not all too proud of myself for submitting to of late. The Chris Chase I used to know would laugh in the face of a mere Satanist, the Chris Chase you used to know would execute all manner of insulting barrages in some confusing way that’d probably leave the lot of you perplexed and almost entirely baffled. Taking things to the all-too-literal meaning is one minor trait of the satanic psyche…and that of Dustin. The world-famous moron has been acting up lately, not in a particularly unpredictable manner, but to the extent of having an unhealthy obsession with instruments of mass torture and with accessories of a black inclination. Paying testimony to the evils of the world with moronically inclined mannerisms, I get the feeling that Dustin’s turned Satanic Jobber on me… Or maybe the loaned Rent-A-Sense is acting up again… Not that I really care. It’s hot around here. The Summer’s finally arrived, and everyone’s buying into the whole look, feel and taste of the season. You can just smell the ice cones and sense the tan lines on the nether regions of beautiful and the not-so-beautiful but confused women of this world. This time last year, I was struggling to come to terms with my own freshness, my naivety on my also-ran status in the PCW at that time of year. I bombed out of a tournament in the first round, and generally failed to make a lasting impression on my peers. Now, I’m fighting the clichés of the Satanic Knights that I’ve been thrown against by the powers that be, and quite simply, I’m inching my way back into the form that made me World Champion. Don’t blink. World Champion. That’s what I was, albeit for five days, that’s what will be read on my own eulogy if Clyde ever manages to put my life away in the place where the devils dance on the souls of beggars. The days are long lately, longer than in Winter or even Mid-March. Except in Australia, but those bastards get to have a barbeque in the middle of the Holiday season. Anyone for Flame-grilled Turkey? The longer days mean that the window remains wider than ever…that there’s more time to kill, lazing around and do fuck all prior to major events. Montego Beach. Yes, it’s that classic Pre-Pay-Per-View promotion again, isn’t it? The infernal cliché that precedes all the larger than average. It bears such a narrow berth, and breaking the overused pattern of tradition was always the kind of thing that I managed to do best at School, or anywhere else for that matter. People have always passed me off as a down to Earth guy… I guess that’s all to change… I never really bought into all that “Be one with the average fan” crap anyhow…it’s like saying “HIT THE FUCKING HALLMARK” to myself whilst watching Xavier Kannon matches with a mocking eye… There’s a Cruise Liner docked in Montego Bay, that’s got to be the PCW Wrestle Vessel. It’s a fucking sweltering day on the beach, beating the likes of the weather early Friday afternoon, and rivaling that of the Arizona desert. I look into the brightly glowing white sands beneath me as I stand in the very center of a wide-open, desolate beach. It’s the kind of thing you want to be doing when you’re wearing sunglasses, or that should come with a health warning, detailing that if you stare into it for too often, you could possibly end up messing up your eyes. Not that that’s my intention. In these very sands, a cavernous hole will be dug, for the climatic battle between the burned man and myself as we fight for absolutely nothing, if only for the maiming of the other. People are bidding me farewell already. Idiots. There’s half a dozen bear traps and a deflated rubber dinghy surrounding me, replete with an incensed Dustin Downey… There’s nothing worse than an angry moron with their heart ripped out….:: Dustin: HOW COULD YOU DESTROY MY DINGHY? Seriously Chris, I know that the preservation of yourself is the key to dying later on tonight, but you destroyed my frickin’ DINGHY! I hope you feel ashamed at yourself, you destroyed a limited edition Cap’n Dustin Dinghy…it WAS priceless! ::. Confused? The truth is, since that run-in with the vendor at Wal-Mart not so long, Dustin’s been acting like a possessed soul…setting fire to old age pensioners, chasing ferrets with chainsaws…like DarkDustin – a side to Downey never before seen on Public Television. A lot of focus lately has been on Dustin, rather than my good sweet self, the recently returned Chris Chase. And you know, I hate being ignored almost as much as I hate being berated by such inflated egos like Xavier Kannon. The fact is, I’m impervious to being berated by other beings like myself – whether you choose to believe it or not. Surely…I have my faults – I have a tendency to wind up being mocked about the mysteries of my own sexuality, and the tendency to wind up biting off way more than I can chew. That, and I’m a pretty random guy when it comes down to it all. An enigma of modern wrestling, a devout figure for all you penniless cretins to admire and adore – you want to be me…you want to do the things that I do for humanity. Why, I’ve ridden Canada of everything that goes against the beliefs and morals of modern men…and I’ve provided an easily consumed yet challenging means of entertainment to those who wish to watch… Sure, I’m a snotty king of Bravado, the Savior to your meaningless existence, the wall to Clyde’s child molesting barrage. And I’m blatantly promoting myself again…go me….:: ….. ::. There’s a massive wall of silence between the referee and I. A rift, a wedge struck between one of the formerly solid friendships that’s ever existed in recent years. On one hand, I destroyed Dustin’s Dinghy by jamming the fifteen-foot long beast into the jaws of a bear trap…but on the very other hand, the whole incident reminds me of when Josh Hale laid traps for Downey once upon a time, back in PCW2. Maybe Dustin managed to learn something back then. Moral decisions aren’t great, they’re like the Tim Walden to my Chris Chase…irritating to say the very least. A Frisbee smacks Dustin in the head… Unsurprisingly….:: Dustin: OWIEEE! NOW WHO FUCKING WELL THREW THAT! I tell you Chris, that bastard’s gonna get some if he wants to take out his sexual frustration on Dustin Downey that’s for sure. Or, of course, we could just go pet kittens together…what a vile and evil activity for us all to do. Heck, you could too! Chris: Gyah? What in the blue blazing brilliance of Chris Chase are you on about? ::. Taking not the time to slap my own forehead, instead, I turn my head swiftly with a curious eyebrow. Moronic as he is, Dustin’s about as stiff as a ball of Play Doh. Not knowing exactly who threw the Frisbee, Dustin leans down and lifts the thing off the floor, before launching it into the air towards a pack of seagulls…nearly mowing the whole lot down in the process. With an innocent look, he jabs me in the ribs, provoking me into nearly delivering a swift backhand to his forehead. For once, I’m glad they stuck a warning label on a Chris Chase promotion…and for once, I’m actually glad that breaking tradition on a Pre-Pay-Per-View promotion is a viable deal indeed…without actually managing to dig myself into a hole in the first place….:: SQUAWK! ::. A Seagull or two falls to the utterly unterrifying weapon that is the common plastic Frisbee….:: Dustin: Whoops, I must have brought a vile end to the winged death – ah well, there’s always something evil to be accomplished around here, no matter how lame and tedious it may seem. Maybe if I took the time to urinate all over this here beach… Chris: ~(Sarcastically)~ OH THE HUMANITY!! -- Dustin, could you possibly find it within yourself to spare the very lot of us from the evil propaganda and agenda that you may have planned, no matter how trite and harmless it might actually be. The fact is Dustin, I care about so much for evil…COME ON, running around all day trying to crucify and maim small mammals isn’t a way to make a living in this world. Dustin, my very excitable comrade, let me introduce you to my friend and possibly yours…God… ::. Yes, God. Don’t look at me like that; I haven’t gone off my nut – at least not yet anyway. Millions of people can’t be wrong, of course, there was that fiasco with the election of the Argentinean President a couple of years back, but this is based on the beliefs of our fathers and forefathers…and the fore-milkmen. A belief system that has been the paradigm for Western Culture ever since the Romans and Greeks gave up on Jupiter and Zeus simultaneously. It’s at times like this when I get lazy and bone-idle that I call on God to protect me for whatever I’m letting myself in for. And apart from that time that Tim Walden managed to pull a fluke back quite some time ago now, I haven’t been failed by the man upstairs – wherever that might be….:: Dustin: Oh please, I gave up on that guy years ago…he’s the least believable out of all known unnatural phenomena. Along with Clowns, he’s the most useless invention of mankind ever since we invented sliced bread…no, say we throw out this notion of God and dig ourselves a hole? ::. Suddenly I feel as if this promo has a purpose…or is that a porpoise? Being that we’re quite close to sea, that could well be a possibility, but maybe I confused it with a Dolphin or perhaps a Beluga – I hate Marine Mammals as much as I hate Satanists. There isn’t that much diversity and they’re only have a certain percentage of human like intelligence – much like the Satanic race – if you don’t mind me insulting the rest of humanity with that statement alone. Don’t worry about me; I’m a man perplexed. Sure, there needs to be somewhat of a hole in the beach for the match to take place… But why should we do the dirty work for those low-paid manual labor types who only work to screw the looser groupies – or to immigrate to another country or state. Even our Pimply Faced Intern seems to be a long-term feature of the staff, unless someone managed to get a hold of Kriegman’s cloning machine and produced a thousand of the whining, often beaten up and punked out loser. My hand falls upon my chin, and begins to rub as if it were a habit of mine, as Dustin pokes that very pondering look at me. Pondering in an almost begging way, as if it was his lifelong dream to dig a bloody great big hole in the sand. But then again, this is a guy who lost his childhood to the evils of home tutoring - so who exactly am I to keep I from him?.:: Chris: Meh…knock yourself out Dustin…PUT DOWN THAT SHOVEL! Gyah. I didn’t mean for you to take it all that literally, again, Dustin. Now then…it’s that time again…isn’t it? Two Hours Later… ::.Two hours later…Dustin’s halfway there. Who would have guessed that a moron could be so effective at digging a hole? Not me, that’s for sure. I lie on the sands beside the hole, peering out into the wide blue yonder, the sky, the atmosphere that makes sure that we don’t spontaneously combust due to the lack of pressure in space – or suffocate to death. Death, the eternal welcoming committee to the Satanic Jobber kind, one of the four deadly horsemen that plague our very world with the things that people like me try to combat. Sure, idly sending off packets of Wheaties to Ethiopians isn’t going to slow down Famine any less, but at least I’m doing MY bit for humanity. Like any of you are willing to do the very same thing…lazy Satanic Jobber loving curs… Meh…and so we finish for now…not with so much as a bang, but a whimper that doesn’t lead anywhere until I figure out what my next catchphrase will eventually be. But for old time’s sake… And you can Justify…That! Damn Internet Connection Whores!.::
