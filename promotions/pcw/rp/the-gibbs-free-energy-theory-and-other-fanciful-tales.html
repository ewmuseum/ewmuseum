Date: .. The world seemed to be spinning wildliy off of its axis. The room was a multi-colored blur of gibbering faces, melded together in a garish collage of gnashing teeth, spittle and flesh. The walls began to run together, streaking the roseprint wallpaper across the linolium floor. All thge while, some son-of-a-bitch thought it would be a keen idea to turn up the music just a few more decibles. Khadafi slumped against one of the bleeding walls, unmindful of the gaudy garden variety flowers that were currently dribbling down his blue and white RocaWear shirt. 'So,'He querried me with a rattled tone. 'What do you know of the latest theory on Gibs Free Energy? They're saying now, that it's all just a load of crock. Some facist fuck up in northern Wisconsin is saying that it's not needed at all; all we human beings have to figure out is how to reach a state of total homeostasis with the rest of the energy in our surroundings.' He reached for his beer but missed, tried to get it again, and had his hand swallowed whole by one of the whirling face dervishes. It giggled and garbled, eyes flashing wildly, tongue lolling as it sang to the viscious beat that poured out of the house sound system. The fillings in my teeth were cracking and splitting from the raucous chorus of Judith Priest's 'Stranglehold'. In fact, I could feel several of my teeth being rattled out of my skull even as Ali struggled to remove his hand from the gaping maw of whatever humanoid fiend he had placed said member of his body in. Something came loose in the back of my mouth. Priest began screeching more, guitars screaming as the drums kicked it into high gear. I opened my mouth to tell Khadafi to forget his beer, lets go hit that Ope that Neo-Pig was bragging about, when one of my teeth fell out. Quick as a cat I caught it in the palm of my left hand and stared down at the poor, beleagured and now exhiled thing. Tossing it aside as it had been so easily tossed from my gums I turned to Ali and hooked him by the arm. 'Lets get the fuck out of here, chum!' I babbled to him in my daze. The acid tabs that we had dropped were beginning to really take hold. I knew this because you don't usually see two men like myself and Ali Khadafi stare at a Blender for going on ten minutes, as we did before we were finally able to usher ourselves out of the paralyzing grip of Mrs. Sanchez's Bananna and Strawberry smoothie. 'Gibbs Free Energy is a motherfucker. Bertil, I tell you. It will fuck you up.' Ali garbled up at me as we both burst out into the cool Minnesotta air. We were now the only two residents of a dimly lit patio, which was fine by me, because I knew that Ali had about an oregano can full of hash and I had a pipe. 'What will REALLY fuck you up is working out those Net Ionic Equataions! Holy Shit Bertil! Holy fucking horse manure!' I watched as he pulled the hash out of his jeans pocket and proceed to pack a healthy wad of that shit into my pipe. It was all fine and good for him to go rambling on about your basic chemistry, but I thought that it was quite inapropriate for the munchkin who was currently walking towards us to be so naked. I watched him for a second or two, transfixed by the little mans spry gate. I wondered how he kept from falling over. And didn't it hurt to drag the length of your phallus on the ground like that? 'What are you talking about?' Ali asked me, passing the smoldering pipe my way. 'Don't you see him, man!?!' I bellowed pointing the fuming pipe in the general direction of the porn-star munchkin. 'See who?' 'That little munchkin you fucking retard! MY GOD HE WOULD HAVE SPLIT DOROTHY IN HALF WITH THAT THING!' I didn't realize that I was screaming until the Neo-Pig kicked down the door and lit into us. With its beady eyes and stubbly snot it approached us with a menacing walk. Stuck between a massviely endowed midget and a Neo-Pig, what the hell were we to do?
