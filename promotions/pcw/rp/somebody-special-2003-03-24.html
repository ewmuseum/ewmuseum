Somebody Special

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/24/03 A dimly lit living room whose only source of light is a candle that flickers in the dark. It seemed awkward but it reflected my feeling well at the moment. I felt like I had come to a place in my life where I was trapped in a dark corner and my only way out was Chris; the only problem was the chance of the flame burning out. With the way I had been constantly letting him down and with the way he had been acting around me made it seem even more possible than before. As I stared at the flickering candle flame my blue eyes twinkled in the light emitting from the stick of wax. The focus of my eyes slowly moved from the candle to a picture that sat beside it. The picture was one of the few ones I had from my youth. It was a family portrait that had been taken back when I was fourteen. It brought back a memory of my past and the uneasiness that used to rest inside my soul. In the portrait I looked happy but that was one of the few joyful memories I had from my youth. Everyone at least once in their life claims that they had a rough youth, while I may not be able to claim that I had the roughest and be telling the truth at the same time it is also true that my youth was a much more tumultuous than the average persons youth. Though it might not have been the swinging of fists or open palms that made it so miserable it was the words that were often exchanged that made me feel lower than dirt. It seemed that at times the only friend I had inside the family beside my deceased grandfather was my older brother Brandon who would occasionally show me some pity and give me a shoulder to cry on, for five minutes. Yet staring back into the picture made me feel that at only moment I could relive this hell in the new family I had found with Chris and the three kids we were raising on our own. At any moment the one person I trusted in which was Chris would decide that my five minutes were up and it was time to move on, after all had he not done that to several girls before me? How was I to be sure that I was the one? My eyes worn out from following the flame closed slowly and before I knew it I found myself asleep, being brought back to the past. It was like I was reliving everything that happened to me nearly eight years ago. I found myself outside the house dressed in my favorite pair of shorts. They were a bit tight around the hip but just like any young fifteen year old I would sacrifice comfort for fashion. Along with the shorts I had a tank top that complemented my eyes well, sky blue in color they also went well with the navy blue color of the shorts. Outside the house with me I found my best friend Heather right by my side. She had on an outfit similar to mine. We were always told that we looked like sisters and by the way my parents treated her it felt like it as well. As much as I had loved Heather there was rivalry between the two of us because of the relationship my parents had developed with her to the point where at any given time they would insult me and give her the praise I had desired for so long. “Hey Heather.” A voice from behind me called out. Curious as to who the voice belonged to I turned my head over in the direction of the voice and noticed that it was coming from my older brother Brandon as another reason as to why I had regretted my friendship with Heather came to my mind. It wasn’t just my parents she had won over but the affection of the only person who ever seemed to care for me my brother Brandon. “Hey Brandon.” She responded with a slight smile on her face just to seem the slightest bit friendly, but when Brandon was not around she had always told me about how she despised him and was glad he was not her brother. It always made me ponder why she had even bothered to lie. Why pretend to be someone you were not? “Oh my goodness Heather dear we haven’t seen you in so long!” The rarely cheery voice of my mother called from the doorway as she walked out onto the porch barefoot embracing Heather in a hug. Surely enough just like Chris and I were attached to the hip so were my mother and father which is why surely enough my father followed right behind her. “How are you Red?” My father questioned calling her by the nickname that referred to the slightly curled head of hair she had. “I’m great. I just got home from a trip with my boyfriend.” She answered with a smile making it quite obvious that she was thinking about the man she had just mentioned. “That’s great! It’s too bad we can’t get Tessa to do something like that more often. She never leaves the house.” My mom laughed looking as me in an expression that read of to me as “you should be more like Heather” yet the thing she failed to realize was the stuff that had happened on that trip that Heather had failed to bring up. Yet Heather didn’t seem to mind the praise she was getting, she seemed to bask in the glory of it no matter how much it pained me to know that my parents would continue with expressions and words like the one just used by my mother until I was practically a carbon copy of Heather. “Yeah Tessa, you should come hang out with me some more.” Heather added in to continue to sucking up. It disgusted me the way she pretended to be so perfect when in reality she was far from what she claimed to be, but what disgusted me even more was the fact that my parents were never willing to give their only daughter praise yet here they were worshipping Heather as if she were the best thing on earth. “Yeah…” My eyes began to sting with tears as I was finally able to recall where I had felt this feeling before. This feeling of never being perfect enough for anyone was brought on by my parents and being refreshed by Chris whether he intended to stir emotions or not. Suddenly I wanted to jump out of this dream turned nightmare and never return but I found myself trapped by the ones who had created this hell of emotions for me, my parents. “Look at you Tessa; you’d be so much better if you acted like Heather.” My mother’s voice chimed in one of my ears. “Yeah, listen to your mother she knows what she is talking about.” My father’s voice echoed in the other ear. I got to wake up! Rolling around in bed and trying to pull myself away from this half awake half asleep state of mind my mind continued to scream that one line. Wake up Tessa! Nothing is going to be better until you wake up! “You should hang out with Heather more. She’s such an awesome girl.” My brother’s voice rang in the background. Shut up Brandon, you’re dead! You can’t tell me how great she is if you’re dead! My mind continued to lash out it’s anger at the image of my dead brother, it’s anger fueled by everything that I was told I was supposed to be in order to be a great person and worthy of the Windsor name. “She’s pretty.” I am pretty to others! Ask Chris, he tells me all the time! “She’s smart.” I may not be smart but I’m sure Heather could never achieve what I’ve managed to achieve in a PCW ring. “She’s got the best personality ever.” What about me? Last time I checked I took care of your kids when you died. So hush you! Go away please! I’ve got to wake up damn it! “Tessa.” I could hear a voice calling for me in the distance but it was at such a far distance that I could not distinguish who it belonged to. “Tessa.” It called it once more but before the dream that was more like a nightmare could continue any further I was able to win the struggle between dreams and reality as I finally forced my eyes back open. The sight I saw was one similar to what I saw before I had been overtaken by heavy eyes and a tired mind. The candle continued to burn and the portrait of a family I wished I hadn’t been born into was still there. “Tessa.” A voice cried out once more similar to the one in my dreams. I turned my body around and noticed that beside me on the couch was Chris, looking over at me with concern. “Wake up we have to do some training for Tuesday. You don’t want Lacuna to be the better woman again do you?” He said, taking me by the arm and pulling me gently up off the couch. Instead of answering him though I let his words sink into my mind. The better woman. Is she really? If she’s such the better woman then why does he bother wasting his time with me? Why can’t I ever be the better person? I was taught manners and all the other basic needs for a great person and I use them everyday, so why can’t I ever be the best? Maybe I’m just not meant to be the best at anything… “Tessa!” Chris shouted out once more. “Did you hear me, we have to get going! Stop the daydreaming; there is plenty of time to do that when we get home.” “Yeah…I’m coming…” I said as I dragged myself away from the couch and towards the front door. Perhaps when my parents told me I would amount to nothing if I didn’t change they were right. Perhaps that would be proven on Tuesday I would prove them right by losing to Lacuna and Jade, or perhaps I would prove them all wrong and be just what nobody thought I could be, somebody special.
