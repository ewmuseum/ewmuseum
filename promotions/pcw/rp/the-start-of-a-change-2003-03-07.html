The Start of a Change

7 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-7-03 “So, he hates person enjoying themselves does he?” the words came from Chris Storm’s lips. His tone was bitter and angry. Chris sat on the edge of his king sized bed. His arms were folded as he stared down at the floor. “He was sickened at the sight of my wedding was he? Typically attitude I’ve seen lately. Everyone here seems to enjoy watching me fail time and time again.” He said. The bitterness was clear. Unfolding his arms, he reached into his pocket and produced a small gold band. The wedding ring he was never able to slip onto the finger of Tessa Windsor thanks to the attack by P.W.A. Chris flips the band between his fingers as the light from the window hits it making the gold shimmer in the dark room. Chris clasps the gold band in his palm and squeezes his fist shut. The door opens flooding the dark room with light. Chris turns his head toward the door frame to see who is there. Tessa Windsor stands in the doorway. Her forehead is bandaged up from the attack at the hands of P.W.A. Chris looks away from her and back toward the window. Tessa senses something is wrong and takes a seat next to him on the bed. “What’s wrong Chris?” she asks him as she moves her hand toward his face. Chris just turns his head away from her. He remains silent. Tessa ask him once more, “Chris, what’s the matter? You seem upset.” Chris remains silent though. “Fine, I’ll leave you to your thoughts then.” She says, standing up. Chris’ hand reaches out and grabs hers. She looks back at him with a curious gaze. Chris pulls his hand back and stands up. Tessa opens her hand to see what he had given her. She opens her hand to see the golden wedding ring. Her breath is caught in her throat. Chris walks right past her toward the front door. As his hand touches the doorknob Tessa calls out to him again. “What is this Chris?” she asks him, fearing for the worst. Could he be taking back his offer of marriage to her? Chris lowers his head and stares down at the carpet. He shakes his head. “I wasn’t able to give that to you before. It may not be official but I wanted you to have my wedding band as a promise that one day we will be man and wife.” He said with a cold, rattling voice. Chris clinches his fist, his arms shaking with anger. The memory of the cold-hearted attack by P.W.A. stands out in his mind. The attack at Fusion, the ruining of their wedding at March Madness, and all the pranks they pulled on the two before then. All of it coming back to Chris, a great flood of pain. Tessa walks over to him and puts her arms around his waist. She leans her head against his chest. “Don’t worry Chris, I know we will. But don’t let what happened at March Madness get you down. We will bounce back. We always do.” The words strike Chris. He grabs Tessa by her sides and pulls her off him. He bends over to look her directly in the face. “I am sick and tired of having to bounce back. I’m sick of being the joke of everyone in PCW! I am Chris Storm, not a punching bag to be used by whoever wishes to. I let Shin and Mani walk all over us. That nearly cost us Alex and Christa. Then I let Jackson Rust walk all over me and that nearly cost me you. Adam and Erik was next to step all over us. Now P.W.A. is walking all over us. I’m sick and tired of it all. I want it to change.” Chris yells at her. His words get higher and colder as he speaks. Tessa just looks at him, not sure what to say. That doesn’t stop Chris from continuing though. “Since day one everyone has told me about how there was so many other Storms here. How every Storm was a failures and I am going to be a failure just because I have the name Storm. People look at me and think I am a weakling just because I show I care about you openly. They don’t see the same Chris Storm that was the most successful wrestler in the WIW: NG ever! They only see the Chris Storm that is whipped by Tessa Windsor. They only see me as a lost puppy owned by a little girl. That isn’t who I am!” Chris continues to rant. His face starts to get red. The children start to gather from around the house to see what all the noise is about. Chris looks at the little ones. “Look at me Tessa! I play daddy to three children that are not even of my blood! I spend time I could use in the gym to pick them up from day care, hockey practice, and helping them with homework! Do you think Duke Williams has to play daddy? Does Snow spend his free time cooking meals and baking cakes? When have you seen Clyde playing catch in the backyard with a 10 year old? I’m not a wrestle, I’m a house mom!” Tessa just stands there taking the verbal abuse from Chris. Tears start to form in her eyes. How had he gone from promising to marry her to blaming her for the downfall of his career? Before Tessa can get the chance to reply to him Chris storms out of the house, slamming the door behind him. Tessa cringes at the sound of the door slamming shut. Evan, Alex, and Christa all try to comfort her in her weakened state. The sharp, cold air hits Chris. Despite it being March, Massachusetts was still very cold. Chris walks over toward the minivan. He looks at it and turned away with disgust. Chris looks around for another vehicle. Seeing none he looks back at the minivan. He looks at his reflection in the driver’s side window. “Where is my Stringer GT? What the hell happened to it? Traded away for this monstrosity! What got into my head to convince me to trade away my prized car for this ugly piece of junk?” Chris punches the driver’s side door out of anger. The impact leaves a dint in the door. Chris turns away from it and starts walking off toward the town. “No wonder everyone sees me as nothing more then a joke. I don’t do anything to demand respect. But that will all change soon. I am tired of being looked at as just some ex-PTC trying to pretend to be a member of the WO. They will all soon learn that I am not someone to take lightly. They will see the Chris Storm that dominated the WIW: NG and won the Grand Slam championship. People like Chet King, Tim Walden, Nerezza, and Gaz Green, all of them will see a whole new Chris Storm.” The anger and hate fills each word. For once a smile creeps onto Chris’ face as he develops a plan. “I know what I will do. Oh yes, Wednesday will be very interesting for Tim Walden. I hope he enjoys the taste of his own blood.” “So, you finally realize the error of your way have you?” Chris looks around for the source of the words. There leaning against a tree is someone that could easily be mistaken for Chris Storm’s twin, Ein. Ein pushes off the tree and walks toward Chris. He throws his arm over Chris’ shoulder. “Chris, look you have been off to the wrong foot since you first stepped into the PCW. I mean look at your debut, you was attacked and destroyed by Angel Society. Your wedding was supposed to be the happiest day of your life and yet P.W.A. made it one of your worst. People like Chet King and Tim Walden are getting cheap shots on you left and right. The entire league wants you gone for good. They cheer every time you are making a fool of yourself not because they like you, but because they know you are only degrading yourself more and more.” As much as Chris hated Ein for everything he has done, his words where true. Chris ducks and gets Ein’s arm off his shoulder. He stares at Ein then suddenly kicks him into the gut. Ein doubles over in pain and shock. Chris drives his elbow into the back of Ein’s neck, sending him onto the ground face first. Chris stands over Ein, looking down with a smirk on his face. “I am going to systemically destroy everyone that gets in my way, including you Ein. You have been a thorn in my side for too long. If I so much as see you again I will no hesitate in bringing you down to your knees in pain and breaking you in half.” Chris says. He spits on Ein before he walks off back toward the house. “I can not wait for Turmoil. The PCW finally gets to see Chris Storm the way he was meant to be. Tim Walden will be driven through the mat like my bitch. It won’t be enough for me to just defeat him. I am looking for more then just the pin or submission here. I want fear and Tim’s blood on the canvas. I want to send a message to the P.W.A. that I won’t sit back and let them ruin my career or my life. Pretty soon they won’t be the only ones pulling pranks here. I’ll show them why I am the violent Storm.” He tells himself as he walks back inside the house. Chris looks around the house but everything is quiet. He walks toward the bedroom, where the door is open just a crack. Chris peers in to see the lights are all out. A figure lies in the bed, clearly Tessa. Chris crawls into the bed next to her. He tries to put his arms around her but Tessa shrugs him off. “I’m sorry for what I said before Tessa. I was blowing off stream. I’m just so tired of being walked all over here. Look I’m sorry. I treasure you and the children, I really do. I’m just frustrated with the fact that no one in the PCW is taking us serious. But I plan to change all that. This week they will see that we are not pushovers, even if I have to write it in our enemies’ blood.” Chris tells Tessa as he tries to take her in his arms again. Tessa rolls over and faces the window. She stares out at the stars. “I don’t want you seriously hurting them Chris. We will beat them where it matters, in the ring.” Chris puts his hand on her shoulder. “Watch Turmoil and what I do to Tim Walden. Then you will understand what I am doing is what we need. I know you have to be tired of the sneak attacks and the cheap pranks by P.W.A. just as much as I am. You have to be tired of the whole roster looking at you as some sexual object in the ring. I can change all that Tessa.” He tells her, rubbing her shoulder with his hand. Tessa just keeps her back to him. It was time for a change. Chris Storm is promising that on Turmoil the PCW will see a whole new side of him. Can he deliver or is this just another Schmoopies moment waiting to happen?
