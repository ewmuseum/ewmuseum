Fight

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th of July, 2002 Xavier Kannon: The darkness that follows me. The evil that torments me… The one who I will end… The one I want to end… Months ago I was a member of the NTICW roster. The first show it all began…the Pharaoh-Xavier hate. The hate that boiled between us could have started a war. Had we two armies we would have certainly started a war. Yes… Back then our troubles were only just beginning; they were only small. I was cocky, arrogant and naïve, much like Kannon is these days, but things change… His ego will deflate one day. His ego will diminish soon. He had me arrested as an illegal immigrant…he cost me matches…and I did the same to him. I locked him in the ancient confines of a sarcophagus in my locker room. I had Carmoon, gods bless his soul, defeat him in the ring. That was amusing…Xavier went out of his already deranged mind. He couldn’t handle the loss…so he went after Isiy, my beloved wife. But she refused his advances – or tried - until Kannon forced himself upon her under the watchful gaze of The Hive. Kannon couldn’t get the job done as usual. He formed The Hive with Ken Kaze and Rich Cannon, better known as Methodical Disorder, and he beat me down… It was around this time that Xavier began to advance his cheating ways. Every match he won, he did so because of cheating…a low blow here, a rake of the eyes there…he truly was a cheater…it was always in him. Then he brought in Ellie~!, the preppy little bitch from Duat, itself! The Gods told me to beat him…they said I had to or my honour would forever be ruined. But in the end it was Drake Maxwell who ended Xavier’s reign as NTICW world champion… And now we find ourselves in present day. Present day gives me a problem, however. You see time, as it has a tendency to do, has given Kannon the Time he needs to train…to pick up a reputation, to advance his wrestling skills. He is now more powerful than ever…He is ranking in the world title division…he has held the cruiserweight title, the extreme title, and the continental title…he’s even acted as the commissioner of PCW… yet he has done ALL of this in preparation for one singular act in PCW. My demise… But Xavier, do you really think I’m going to let you do as you wish? Do you really think I’m going to allow you to beat on my wife? …Beat on me? …Make fun of my country? …Make me the laughing stock of Primetime Championship Wrestling? Xavier I’m telling you from the very bottom of my heart that you won’t do any of those things to me. You’ll never do any of those things to me Kannon, because I will FIGHT! I will not give up…I will not sit down and allow you to pass…I will not separate the water, as Moses once did in order to let you walk through the sea…I will not take your punishment without a noble battle… Xavier… I will fight… The scene opened up in The Palace, Anaheim, California. The Pharaoh stood gazing out of the window in his superbly furnished bedroom. He wore his hair down today; it spread over his shoulders like a cascade of rich black silk, shimmering glossily in the sun. His stern eye led to the house next door where two small children fought in their backyard, it wasn’t serious fighting of course…it was just play fighting. Pharaoh watched for a while with a look that could have cut glass and then abruptly left the window before walking towards the door of the room. He walked out onto the lobby; many vases and other ornaments lined the flat surfaces of the hallway. Isiy was fond of them…Pharaoh wasn’t, but for once he ignored them, he had other things on his mind… He edged towards a room…its door was well carved and decorated in tiny hieroglyphics, much like the other pine doors in the Mansion. He stood for a moment and then knocked, ominously. “Yes?” came the voice from within. The Pharaoh: “It’s me, Zen.” There was a sound of shuffling and then the door was swung open. The face of a huge man, dark skinned like The Pharaoh and wearing a pair of silky black Adidas shorts stood looking down at Titu. It was the very same man who had attacked Duzza many weeks ago when Pharaoh had first returned to the ring. He was shaven bald, but had a thin black goatee beard spun around his chin like a serpent. His eyes were fixed and always angry-looking. But of course, he wasn’t angry with his own master…the man who had helped him through earlier life. No, Zen owed Pharaoh a debt and he was paying it as Xansiv’s bodyguard. Zen: “Is there something wrong, Pharaoh?” The Pharaoh: “No, Zen, there’s nothing really the matter, I came to tell you that this Friday I’ll be needing your help. You’ll be coming to Fusion with me. Alright?” Zen looked as though he was contemplating the thought for returning to a PCW event. The bookers had wished for him to stay away from the squared circle after the Duzza incident, but Pharaoh needed some form of backup. Titu wasn’t a cheating man, but he liked to play people at their own game. If Xavier was going to have Ellie~! and his cheating ways in his corner then Pharaoh was going to have Dula in his. Zen: (Enthusiastically) “Another chance to destroy one of your enemies, master? How could I give up a chance such as that?!” Zen was a huge man, mighty and proud His voice was like a thousand drums being beaten senselessly and his bulk was almost all muscle - all four hundred and eighty nine pounds of it – that is. The Pharaoh: “I don’t want you to attack him…I want to do that, my friend. But if he gets up to any funny business then sort him out, OK?” Zen: “Understood. But who is this ‘wrestler’ you speak of?” The Pharaoh: “Xavier…Kannon.” Pharaoh tightened his hand into a fist. Xavier truly was the thorn in his side, the bane of his life, and more importantly one of the most hated wrestlers in PCW. Pharaoh wanted to destroy Kannon and this coming Friday on Fusion he’d get his chance. With a steel chair by his side he’d get vengeance on Kannon…he had to… Pharaoh had to fight… Zen: “The bastard who attacked Isiy?” The Pharaoh: (Nodding his head) “The very same…” Zen: (Furiously) “The guy who bloodied you in the ring?” The Pharaoh: “Yes…” Zen frowned. Zen: “Then I will personally make sure he doesn’t cheat his way into winning your match on Fusion, Sire!” Pharaoh nodded. Zen: “You hate him more than anything, don’t you?” Zen had a way of figuring out what people were feeling. He knew Titu felt hate for Kannon. And who wouldn’t after the things Xavier had done? He had no right doing those vile acts! He was sicker than many had first perceived, even Pharaoh didn’t think Kannon would go to those sorts of lengths… The Pharaoh: “Yeah, but I will make sure I get some form of revenge on him this Fusion!” Dula nodded thoughtfully. Zen: “I’ll make sure that he pays… One way or another!” Titu smiled. The Pharaoh: “Sometimes we just have to fight. I have some things I need to do, and then we’ll be setting out for DeRidder, Louisiana. I have to open the new wing of the museum there…so if you’ll excuse me, Zen…” Zen: “Yeah, sure…” Zen closed the door as Pharaoh began to walk back towards his bedroom. Isiy was downstairs, most likely preparing something to eat. Pharaoh wasn’t hungry, though. He has more important things on his mind. The destruction of Xavier Kannon would truly be a bittersweet taste. To see the horror on the face of Ellie~! as her husband lay in a pool of his own blood would be extremely enjoyable…and amusing. To see Kannon laying face down, choking on his own sweat…his own blood… that too would be brilliant… And he knew he could win…he’d done it before. The Pharaoh: (To himself) “He continues to think he has a chance, but when Friday comes I will be the one left standing. For what Kannon has done he shall pay for, he will repent or else forever his soul will be harshly judged in Duat! The Gods look down nastily upon people who attack their followers.” Pharaoh edged towards the window and looked down at the brawling children. The Pharaoh: “That’s how it all begins…you fight once and you can’t stop. You can’t stop enjoying the feeling of your opponent tap out, you can’t stop feeling the greatness inside you after you’ve once again defeated an opponent.” The two children lock up and one hits a bulldog out of the tie-up. The other begins to cry, moaning that his friend hit the move too hard. He stands, holding his nose and pushes his friend… The Pharaoh: “Playing becomes serious… playing becomes conflict. Lighthearted acts suddenly evolve into fearful wars. That is how it was with us, Xavier. We began in PWA together. Exchanging friendly insults backstage. Oh it all got out of hand…and now look at us! You… you fear me, Kannon, that’s why you had to do as you did last Tuesday! One of the children takes a swing for the other; the second child ducks and kicks his attacker in the stomach. The Pharaoh: (Broodingly) “Wars start from the slightest comment, the slightest action…the slightest mistake… You made a big mistake attacking me, Xavier. “ The boys both hit each other in the face and collapse to the floor. Voice: “Titu!” Isiy shouted from downstairs, Pharaoh watched the boys for a second and then left the room again. The Pharaoh: “Yes, my love?” Pharaoh got to the bottom of the staircase to find Isiy stood with a tall, angry-looking man standing by her side. Isiy: “This is Mr Thompson, from next door--…” Mr Thompson: “Because of you my child and his friend are fighting each other!” Pharaoh looked a little puzzled. Then he ventured to ask… “And what has that got to do with me?” The man was clearly taken back. But he straightened himself up and shook his head angrily. Mr Thompson: “It all began because of that wrestling you do! They were playing in the back and then the next minute they’re fighting like a pair of hungry birds over a bread crumb!” Pharaoh folded his massive arms over his chest and shook his head to and fro. The Pharaoh: “Just because they’re fighting now doesn’t mean I had anything to do with it!” Mr Thompson: “Oh yes it does! You and your merry band of wrestling fools have converted my son into talking, walking and SHOUTING like an idiot!” The Pharaoh: (Sighing) “Mr Thompson…I have more to think about than your boy and his friend fighting, now please, leave my house…” Isiy bit her lip as Mr Thompson’s eyes bulged and his face grew bright red. Mr Thompson: “Now look here--…” The Pharaoh: “Don’t think I won’t show you the door personally! Now get out of my house!” If it were possible for anyone to do so, Mr Thompson would have blown smoke out of his ears. He angrily walked towards the front door, grumbling, opened it and left, but not without forgetting the mandatory slam of the entrance in situations like this. Isiy: *Tut* “You shouldn’t have done that, Titu! It wasn’t very nice!” Pharaoh ignored his wife, and began to walk up the stairs… The Pharaoh: (Quietly) Sometimes we have to Fight…
