Political Doubts?

12 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Political Doubts? Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: May 12. Annoyance. In the Politically Incorrect Dictionary, a picture of the Heartbreakers sits next to this term. The two Purveyors of Pink have pushed and pushed Politically Incorrect's prim and proper personages to the point of poisonous retribution. Upsetting Politically Incorrect is not the most prudent thing to do. They have a tendency for working to achieve vengeance. And, since Powell has power in Des Moines...there are easy ways to tell whom he is facing. Namely, what laws come down off the books. Against the Cowabunga Connection, he banned surfing. Now, what does Primetime Championship Wrestling's political duo have in store for their latest challenge? Try 'The Anti-Pink Act of 2002.' Yes folks.....them politicians are pissed. And they are fixing to filibuster all over the Heartbreakers... Politically Incorrect style! ::The Power That Is in Des Moines, Iowa, William S. Powell, is virtually cackling with glee from his latest act. Reginald V. Lovecraft sits next to him, his head shaking incredulously. Still, Lovecraft realizes something important. He knows his partner is in a mental state that he needs to be in. William S. Powell is ticked. And, Lovecraft knows, he should be. For, he cannot take the Heartbreakers to the Halls of Justice alone...:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: I really don't know how you manage to do this, Mr. Powell. The Anti-Pink Act? Isn't that a flagrant violation of freedom of expression? William S. Powell: Not really. Remember, time, place, and manner restrictions CAN be imposed. I am simply stating that at no time, in no place, and in no manner can the odious color pink be displayed anywhere in the city of Des Moines! Reginald V. Lovecraft: The ACLU is going to be all over you, you do realize that, right? William S. Powell: They're too busy dealing with drug testing cases right now.....so, we can do this! Reginald V. Lovecraft: But what purpose does this serve? You must realize that the Heartbreakers are formidable foes. We haven't been in training since we won the titles due to the Ultimate Authority protection. We've become lazy. William S. Powell: So? Why train when we have the Ultimate Authority at our back? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Because, I've asked them to stay away from ringside. ::Powell stands, shocked. His shock quickly turns to anger.:: William S. Powell: ARE YOU MAD?! YOU GAVE UP OUR ADVANTAGE?! Reginald V. Lovecraft: William, stop acting like a child. We won these titles on our own merits before we joined the Ultimate Authority. I have to know if we can do this. William S. Powell: But, what if we lose? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Easy! We use our powers as owners of the tag division to make ourselves a rematch! William S. Powell: Oh yeah! I forgot about that! Reginald V. Lovecraft: It's not that I think we'll lose.....but I need to see if our skills are where they used to be. William S. Powell: Yeah, I know man....we've come a long way since fighting Bobby Stormbringer and DarkStar in our first match here..... Reginald V. Lovecraft: Starting today......43 days....eclipsing the Divinity for the longest tag title reign in Primetime Championship Wrestling history. Just about a month and a half on top of the PCW Tag Team World, proving our greatness beyond a shadow of a doubt. Sure, our win loss record hasn't been the best, but that is for the good of the Authority, in beating down idiots such as Duke Williams and Jordan X Shabazz. William S. Powell: As always, Politically Incorrect takes on all comers....especially dead ones, like Hank and Ganker! :Lovecraft sighs, a look of sheer annoyance on his face.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: The time for joking and laughing is done. It's us vs. them, Politically Incorrect vs. The Pink Posse. The Heartbreakers will bring everything they can muster at us. It is up to us to hold the dam tight. It is up to us to not lose. It is up to us to RETAIN the PCW World Tag Team Titles. And we will do it the way we always have. William S. Powell: Heartbreakers, Valentine and Heart, be prepare. For today is not Valentine's Day......it is ELECTION DAY! Reginald V. Lovecraft: And, on Election Day... William S. Powell: POLITICALLY INCORRECT ALWAYS TRIUMPHS! Fade to... Paid for by The Ultimate Authority.The Most Politically Incorrect Group, Period. ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
