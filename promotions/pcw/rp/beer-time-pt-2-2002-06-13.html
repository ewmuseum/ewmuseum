Beer Time Pt 2

13 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13/06/02 So are you going to give me an answer or not? We find Ricky Thunder and The Supreme Machine still sitting in the booth where we left them last time.  The crowded bar isn't as crowded now as three hours has passed since our first instalment and quite a few of the patrons have moved on to other places or staggered home to bed.  Both wrestlers have consumed copious amounts of beer in the time that we've been gone and Ricky Thunder looks a little the worse for wear. All I'm asking for is a fucking tag match man.  Fuck!  You'd think I was asking for your first born child or something.  One match! Don't worry about Crichton, Rick.  I'll take care of him for you on Friday night. Yeah right.  You've lost your first two matches in PCW remember? Ricky Thunder leans across the table.  His eyes are having a little difficulty focussing. What's wrong with you man?  You stay locked up in that damned apartment all the time training but you're wrestling like shit.  This isn't the SuMa I know.  The SuMa I know would have mopped the floor with those two chumps. The young Australian grabs hold of his beer and takes a large gulp. I'm just taking a little time to settle in to the new job.  That's all.  I'll take care of Crichton. Look what's the fucking problem?  Those guys deserve an ass kicking for what they did during my match with Makonie. The Supreme Machine sighs to himself as he scans the bar until he sees the blonde bar attendant, Terri, delivering drinks to a table across the room.  She senses him watching and flashes a smile his way. They'll get their ass kicking Rick, don't worry.  Besides, you know what I'm like.  Ricky Thunder plonks his beer down on the table and looks at The Supreme Machine with a puzzled look on his face. What do you mean? The Supreme Machine turns back to face Ricky. If, and that's still only an if, I agree to reform The Supreme Team here in PCW, I'd want to make it worth our while. Huh? Well, if we're going to bother reforming once, why don't we go all the way and go for the titles? Ricky Thunder looks taken aback as he takes another large mouthful of beer. I don't know about that man. Well that's how it is sorry.  If I agree to face Crichton and Murray with you, you have to promise me that we'll keep going and take the PCW tag belts. The young Australian slides out from the booth and stands at the end of the table, shaking his head. I don't know.  I'll be back in a minute.  I gotta pee. The Supreme Machine watches on as Ricky turns and makes his way, a little unsteadily, through the crowd.  As he disappears through the door marked "MEN", The Supreme Machine turns back to the table and drinks some of his beer.  He looks down at his watch.  11:49 Can I get you two another drink? He jumps slightly as he looks up to see Terri standing at the end of the table. Oh... She laughs slightly as she puts the tray down on the table. Do you think you'll still be here in about an hour? The Supreme Machine looks a little stunned by her directness.  Terri blushes as she looks at him. I'm sorry.  I'm not usually this forward.  I don't know what got into me. No, no. That's fine.  Really. You must think I'm some sort of... oh, God knows what. No really, it's fine.  And the answer is yes. Yes? Yes I'll still be here in an hour.  I'm meeting someone remember? He pulls the business card she had given him earlier on in the evening out of his shirt pocket and flashes it at her. That's great.  I really don't usually go around giving customers my number.  Honest.  I just had to do it this time. Well I'm glad you did. Terri stands smiling at The Supreme Machine until one of the other bar staff walks up beside her and whispers something in her ear.  Terri quickly picks up the tray from the table and glances around towards the bar where the supervisor is standing looking straight at her. I have to get back to work or I'm going to be in trouble.  So you guys wanted a couple more beers? Yes thanks, but you'd better make my friend's a lite. OK.  I'll see you in a few minutes. He watches her as she heads back towards the bar.  The supervisor growls at her when she gets there but she just smiles and appologises.  He takes another mouthful of beer as he spots Ricky Thunder making his way back towards the booth.  He pauses on the way to talk to a young brunette woman dressed in a smart business outfit but she ignores him so he continues back to the booth and slides into his seat. Did you see that?  Stuck up bitch! He drinks the last of his beer and puts the glass down heavily on the table. And now I'm out of damned beer! Don't worry.  I've ordered another round. Excellent!  Hey, hang on.  You didn't order me lite beer like you did in Atlanta that time did you?  I can tell man.  I'll fucking kill you if you've ordered me lite beer. Of course not.  I wouldn't do that to you. Just as well.  I hate lite beer. Both wrestlers look up as the bar attendant, Terri, arrives with their beer. Here you go. She puts the drinks down in front them. This isn't lite is it? Terri glances quickly at The Supreme Machine who gives her a little wink. No. Did you want lite beer?  I can change it if you want. No, no.  That's fine. The Supreme Machine hands her the money for the drinks.  She smiles broadly at him as she puts their empty glasses onto her tray and heads back to the bar.  Ricky Thunder takes a sip from his full glass and leans over the table towards The Supreme Machine. I was thinking. And? Don't start that shit again. Sorry.  What were you thinking? I was thinking that's it's unfair of you not to help me out with Crichton and Murray. The Supreme Machine shakes his head slowly before taking a mouthful of beer. This will be good.  OK, why am I being unfair. Well, ever since you came back from retirement you've been coming down and getting involved in my matches. I promised your brother I'd look out for you. Exactly! What do you mean? You keep saying that you promised my brother to look out for me so then why won't you do it now?  Those guys jumped me, remember?  All I'm asking is for you to agree to one tag match so that I can get even with both of them at once and you give me this shit about wanting me to go after the tag titles with you.  I just want these two.  I'm not really interested in the tag titles.  You promised my brother so why won't you agree? You're drunk. So what?  Answer me damn it? The Supreme Machine drinks some more beer then stares hard across the table at Ricky. OK.  Fine.  I agree to the damned match then.  Happy now? Ricky Thunder smiles as he leans back in his seat and has a long drink of his beer. Yeah.  I knew you'd agree to it. Bastard! Asshole! The two men burst out laughing as they both raise their glasses in the air and bang them together over the table.  The Supreme Machine looks up as Terri walks past smiling at him.  Thunder looks down at his watch.  12:24. She gets off work soon dude. I know. Don't suppose you want to start the tag team tonight? The Supreme Machine glares across at Ricky Thunder. No! Hey, I was only joking! Ricky downs the last of his beer and slides out of the booth. You going? Yeah, I'd better get going.  Don't want to cramp your style. Very funny. The two men shake hands and Ricky turns to leave but he stops and turns back. Thanks man. What for? Agreeing to the tag match. That's OK.  Don't worry Ricky.  I'll take care of John Crichton on Friday night and then we'll get that tag match happening.  You'll get your payback. Thanks again man. But you have to promise me that you'll think about going for the tag titles. OK.  I'll think about it. Good. Now I'm getting out of here. The two men say there goodbyes once more and Ricky Thunder turns and makes his way through the thinning crowd towards the exit.  The Supreme Machine sits back down at the booth and has a drink of his beer before he looks down at his watch.  12:49
