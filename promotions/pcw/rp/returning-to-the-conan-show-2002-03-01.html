Returning To The Conan Show

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/01/02 The scene opens to the set of the Conan O’Brian Show. The stage is clear, and they have just finished setting up the stage area. After a few more moments Conan walks out to a huge ovation. He is smiling and is wearing his usual attire of suit and tie. He walks out to the front of the stage and begins his usual start of the show. Conan: Hello ladies and gentlemen. Tonight we have a very special show for you tonight. Our guests here tonight are PCW wrestler and returning guest Steve Johnson. And our other guest will be my former Co-Host Andy Richer. The crowd cheers wildly for the mention of the two guests. They are giving Conan a standing ovation once again. He walks over to his desk and sits down behind it. He looks over to the curtain and announces the arrival of Andy. The music hits and Andy walks out to a huge ovation. He waves to the crowd and even gives them a little bow and sits down in the chair right next to Conan’s desk. They shake hands as Andy sits down. Conan: Man Andy you got a bigger ovation than I did and this is my show! You always have stolen my spotlight however. That is why we had to terminate you. (Conan and Andy both smile and laugh about that last comment) But really Andy how have you been? Andy: Conan I have been pretty good. But you know it is not my fault I still your spotlight. It is way too easy to do it you know. Any way, I had to come back here tonight to add a little class and real comedy to this show. Conan: Really then why don’t you just prove it then Andy! You know I am the one with my own show you don’t have one so there. (Once again the two laugh about what has just been said.) Any way tonight you get to be part of what should be a historic show. Our next guest is Steve Johnson. Andy last time he was on not only, were the ratings the highest they have ever been, but the show was filled with plenty of action. Did you ever see any of that show? Andy: Yeah I caught the entire ass kicking he laid on Ben on the next days Entertainment Tonight. He really laid Ben’s ass out! It was one of the greatest things I have ever seen! I have been waiting to meet him every since he first broke into the wrestling business. He is the greatest wrestler I have ever seen and that includes the likes of Stewart Hix and some others. Conan: Well you won’t have to wait much longer Andy. Right after the break, PCW superstar Steve Johnson right here will join us on the show! The show cuts to a commercial break. The scene then shifts to a Pizza Hut Commercial. After a few moments the scene fades to the back dressing room area of the show. You see Steve Johnson walking out of his room and toward the stage. He is wearing a pair of black Adidas Pants, white Adidas shoes, and a black and green T- Shirt. On the front of the shirt are the words, “ All children’s Hero, Every Woman’s Fantasy, and Every Man’s Greatest Fear…. Welcome to My World!” In red letters trimmed in black across the front of the shirt. The scene then cuts back to the stage. The show comes back from commercial break. Conan: Welcome back ladies and gentleman of the audience. Oh yeah and all you watching at home. Can’t forget you people at home, as you are the ones that make this show possible. Any way, without further delay, every one give a warm Conan O’Brian welcome to Steve Johnson! Steve walks out from behind the curtain. He gets a huge reaction of cheers. One man in the front starts yelling things at him and Steve walks over and extends his hand to the fan. They shake hands and the man has a huge smile. Steve smiles as he walks over to Andy and Conan. He shakes both of their hands and sits down in the seat next to Conan’s desk. Andy has now moved over to the second chair. The fans continue their cheers for the next few moments. Conan: Welcome again Steve. Last time you were here you were working toward getting a title shot at the XGWO and now you have not only gotten that shot but won the title and moved on to the biggest company in your industry Primetime Championship Wrestling. And you gave Ben quite a roughing up. Are you going to do anything like this again tonight? I may need to go put on my helmet and shoulder pads. Steve (smiling): Well as long as Ben is not here running off his mouth, and you and Andy are going to keep your comments respectful, then there should be no problem at all. And thanks for the welcome its good to be back on the show Conan. Conan: Well Steve, Andy here is a huge fan of yours and was really excited about meeting you here tonight. He has been your fan since the beginning. Any way we have a few topics to cover with you here tonight so lets get started. Andy you had a question you wanted to start off with tonight. Andy: Yes. I am so glad to see you in PCW. I watched your debut last Tuesday. I was really pissed off by the ref. What was his deal? And tell us was he fired for such a lame call? Steve: Well like you said I was making my debut and got screwed by a dumb ass ref. Yes he was fired. Which is good for him, because if had intended to kick him around a bit more the next day but he had already been released. I figured being in the unemployment line was bad enough. You know Andy they really should have made this your show and called it the Andy Show you ask much more insightful questions than Conan. Conan: Hey that is not funny! This is my show, everyone knows that! Andy: Thanks Steve! That was a really great thing for you to say. Any ways go on ahead with your questions Conan. This is still your show after all. Conan: Yes it is, and as long as it is then I will ask the questions from now on. Any way Steve, we have a piece of footage here from your last match against Ripper on Tuesday. Take us through what happened and tell us about what you felt when the ref DQ you. Steve: Well Conan, this footage is of me kicking the living hell out of some one, like no one else on the face of the earth can do. I am beating the hell out of Ripper like he has never had done to him before and helping edge him closer to the decision to finally retire from his pathetic career. Let’s just take a look and watch. TV. Set pops up and begins to play footage of the match. You see Ripper landing some heavy moves for a short amount of time. Then you see Steve hit the Low shot and the ref call for the bell. Suddenly Steve and Ripper attack and kick the hell out of the ref. They double power bomb him out of the ring and to the floor. The TV screen goes black and then goes back down into the original position. Steve: See that load of crap! There I was ready to take Ripper out and that ref makes that stupid call. Then that gorilla Ripper in an attempt to save himself after the match goes after the ref with me. Well he only bought himself enough time until Sunday. Then I will take care of him as well as the other two jobbers in my match. Andy: I can hardly wait to see it happen. The new PCW Rising Star Champion!!! Steve: Then let me get you some ringside seats. You could watch all the action live and up close. Andy: What do I say? I say that yeah I’M convinced! I will gladly take some tickets. Thanks Steve. Steve: No problem man. Don’t mention it. Conan: Hey what about me? Can you get me some tickets too? Steve: No, I could not. For two reasons. One Andy is a star and you are not. And two and most importantly, I really just do not like you Conan. So lets move on shall we? Conan: Well then. On to the next question. Two of your opponents have had alot to say in the last couple of days. What do you have to say to that? Steve (with a sadistic little smirk): Frankly I find it quite amusing. Sylo and Ripper have been trying to convince everyone that they are crazy and mad men. Well great for them. Sylo climbed a mountain and found his inner beast. Buried his former self, talks all the time about taking it easy on us and God having mercy on our souls. Loads, and loads of crap. Then you have Ripper, the big bad man who beat up a woman. WOW! That is quite impressive. Beat the hell out of a woman a third his size, and even then he needed a bottle to get the job done. He is pathetic, and on Sunday they will both go home wondering why their inner beasts and voices didn’t save them when I was beating their ass! Conan: That is quite a statement there Steve. Do you have any doubts about this Sunday? Andy: Of course he doesn’t he is Steve FN Johnson, Conan. You should know better by now. Steve: Andy is right Conan. I am telling you the company made a mistake keeping you instead of Andy. You can ask some seriously stupid things. Any way, no I have no doubts. Why should I? My three opponents are two mental morons who listen to beasts and voices. The other is a paper champion. They are neither a threat nor a concern. They are all pawns and stepping stones in my quest for the Rising Star Title. They just plain suck! Conan: Well Steve, I have one more topic to go over with you. You’re new woman in your life. Where did she come from? And why now do you choose to have a manager at your side? Steve (a little smirk): To tell you the truth Conan, she has been here all along. It is only now that we decided she is ready to accompany me to the ring. She had to learn some basic self-defense things and how to conduct herself in an interview. Now she is ready to take the reigns and accompany me in public. Besides every great man should have a beautiful woman at his side. That is something that none of my opponents, especially Ripper would know or understand anything about. She is just another great thing about my career and me. As far as why now goes, because I can and I will. That is all the reason I need to have for letting her manage me now. Conan: Well this will be a classic match up in Sunday that I will enjoy watching on the TV at home. Good luck Steve and come back anytime. It has been a real pleasure talking with you again. Steve: The pleasure has been all yours Conan. On the other hand it ahs been a pleasure meeting you Andy and I will get those tickets for you tomorrow. As Steve and Andy get up and start to shake hands there comes a scream from the curtain area. Suddenly from the curtains behind Steve comes Ben Afleck running toward him with a steel chair. As he reaches Steve and swings the chair Steve has already noticed him and ducks. The chair catches Andy full in the face and put him down and out. Johnson quickly turns around and grabs a very stunned Ben and delivers a Silencer to Ben through Conan’s desk. Steve gets up and looks down at Ben who is completely out. Conan just shakes his head. Conan: Damn! And I just got that table waxed. Now they will probably give me one of those crappy ones, like Jay Leno has. Steve: Well at least you are not the one bleeding from your head like poor Andy here. The star of your crappy show besides me is down due to one of your more pathetic guests. If I were a betting man Conan, I’d say you and Ben tried to set me up here tonight. You wouldn’t have done that would you? You and Ben would not have ever really thought about trying to set up Steve Johnson would you? Conan: No. I would…. Never try that. Please you know me Steve. Conan attempts to back away but Steve quickly grabs him and delivers a Silencer to hard on to the stage floor. Then he looks down at the out cold prone body of Conan. He smirks and leaves the stage to a chorus of boos and some cheers. The scene fades away to black.
