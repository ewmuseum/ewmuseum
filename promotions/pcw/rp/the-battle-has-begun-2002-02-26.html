The Battle Has Begun

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/26/02 (OOC: I was given permission by Ryckman to use his character. Thank you.) The scene opens to the outside of the large hotel where we saw Steve Johnson leave from earlier. The streetlights all along the road are on and light up the street and sidewalks bright enough to leave shadows of the people and things around on the ground. After a few minutes of watching the very peaceful scene a very familiar black Chevy Tahoe pulls into the parking lot for The hotel. When the vehicle comes to a stop the driver side door swings open just as we hear the engine shut off. Steve steps out of the vehicle with a huge grin on his face. He walks to the front doors to the hotel and enters the main lobby to the hotel. He walks toward the elevators that are located toward the back of the lobby, but is stopped by the man behind the front desk. The man's nametag reads, 'Robert'. Robert smiles as he walks from behind the desk and stops Steve. Robert: Hello Mr. Johnson. Here are your messages for this afternoon. Have you had a good day? Steve: Well I can say it has been a rather eventful one any way. Look at all these messages from crazy ass fans and stuff. Robert (with a look just like a light bulb went off in his head): Oh speaking of crazy people that reminds me. You had a visitor earlier this afternoon. He was very strange and wouldn't leave his name or where you could contact him. All he would say is, ' The Lil man will know who was looking for him.' It was weird. Steve (smiling as he looks up from his stack of messages): Yeah I might have a pretty good guess as to who it was. No worries Robert he is not crazy. He just wants everyone to believe he is so that they will fear him. I can tell you it hasn't been working so far for me. Voice: Well maybe this new evidence will change your mind. Steve and Robert turn and look toward the entrance where two men in suits have just entered the lobby. They have very serious looks on their face. They walk up to Steve and stop. Man 1: Mr. Johnson we are from the PCW Competition Committee and we have to speak with you in private sir. It is of great importance. Steve: And what if I do not wish to hear what you have to say to me? What then? Man Two: Sir we have videotape here that we wish to have you view. It is very important to your match on Tuesday night. Steve: Well in that case we can use the one in the office behind Roberts’s desk here. Steve and the two men walk into the office and Robert closes the door behind them. The first man takes the tape out of his jacket pocket and puts it into the VCR. As he hits play he explains what they are about to watch. Man 1: What you are about to view is a security camera from the arena you were at this afternoon. I think I will let the tape speak for its self for right now. The tape begins to play the parking lot area of the arena from earlier. It scans around the lot for a moment then comes to a black sports car and a red Ford Explorer. A man is lying underneath the Explorer. The camera zooms in as close as it can get. After a few minutes the man slides out from the vehicle holding some kind of cable. The man is none other than PCW wrestler Ripper Ryckman. Steve leans forward in the chair he is sitting in behind the desk. On the tape Ripper walks over to the shadows near the rear of the building clearly waiting for some one or something. A little while later Steve walks out of the entrance and heads to the parking lot. He climbs into the black sports car and drives off. Ripper at the back of the building comes out and throws the cable down in anger. The tape then goes to static. Steve's face by now has become a bright red. His body is shaking in anger. He looks up at the two men in suits as they begin to explain. Man 2: You see Mr. Johnson the fact that he attempted to take you out can allow us not only to remove him from the match on Tuesday, but also from PCW all together. Then we would turn the tape over to the local authorities, and he would go to prison. All you would have to do is sign these forms filing a formal complaint. Steve looks at the stack of papers the man has set on the desk. The thumb through them when suddenly the anger inside of him subsides and an evil smile crosses his face. He looks up at the men. Steve: So what you are telling me is that I now hold the fate of Ripper Ryckman’s career in my hands. Man 1: Yes sir. Steve: If I sign these he would be out of the match on Turmoil? Man 2: That is correct sir. Steve: He would be out of the Rising Star Title match at March Madness, and his career in PCW would be completely over before it even began? Man 1: Again that is correct sir. Now if you would just sign these forms we will be out of your way. Then man hands Steve a pen. Steve turns through the papers to where his signature is to be placed and puts the pen to the paper. He sits there a moment without moving. Then another little grin comes over his face. He puts the pen down and pushes the papers away. Steve: No I don’t believe I will sign this form here today. I have a much worse fate in store for Ripper than a simple loss of his job and being thrown in jail. Man 2: Sir we urge you to sign those papers before this maniac hurts some one else. The man who owned that Explorer is now in critical condition at a nearby hospital due to the injures he suffered from Mr. Ryckman’s sense of fair play. Please reconsider. Steve: How long do I have to file this complaint? Man 1: The amount of time that the committee will hold on to evidence and allow a person to make a complaint on a situation is one full year from the date of the offence. So by rule in PCW you would have one year. But you really shouldn’t wait that long sir. The time to act is now. Steve: No I am sticking to my original decision and not filing. Ripper is going to wish I had filed and let him out of this match however. No if you could leave me alone, I have something to take care of. The two men start to protest again but are cut off by Steve. They just shake their heads and turn and leave the office. Steve remains seated at the desk as Robert comes into the office to see him. Robert: Did they get every thing squared away for you sir? Steve (with that grin on his face as he stands): Everything is squared away Robert. Everything I can assure you. Robert stands at the door, not sure what to think as Steve walks past him and out of the office. Then instead of heading to the elevators like he was before he heads back out of the hotel. :::::::: THE SCENE ENDS::::::: As the scene reopens we see Steve walking into another hotel room. Just as he is walking up to the front desk he spots Ryckman. He walks right over to where the man is standing, near the rest area where some people are watching T.V. The hotel security is out in force in the lobby tonight, since there are PCW wrestlers staying in the hotel. Ryckman has his backed turned to Steve as he walks up. Steve spins him around and has Ryckman face to face with him. The two both get an evil smile. Steve: Just the man I was looking for. I know what you attempted to do Ripper. Ripper: I see you are all right Lil man. What ever could you be talking about? Steve: Oh, lets just say that I now literally hold your career in my hands. You will pay for this more than you could have ever dreamed of. I was wrong about one thing though. Ripper (smile growing bigger): Yes…. Steve: You are crazy. You never should have tried to take me our Ripper. Now I will unleash a side of me against you that no one has ever seen before. The beating you take on Turmoil is no longer just going to be a technical one. Now it will be a brutal one as well. Ripper: I look forward to that lil man. Steve (a devilish grin): Hell why what. Suddenly Steve punches Ripper in the face. Ripper is rocked only for a second as he returns the favor. The two stand there trading punches to the face and head. Neither one giving the other any ground, or advantage. Just as blood begins to pour from the men from the heavy blows they are landing to each other’s faces, several security guards run over and grab the two. The first two to grab Steve get their heads smashed together. Ripper takes the first two to grab him and picks them up and slams them to the floor. Then the two go to punching each other some more. Finally about a dozen securities join the other four who are finally getting to their feet and eight grab each man and bring them to the floor. The two lands on the floor, just about a foot apart, staring into each other’s blood stained faces. They stare one another down with absolute anger and bad intention. The scene fades away to black with the two starring one another down.
