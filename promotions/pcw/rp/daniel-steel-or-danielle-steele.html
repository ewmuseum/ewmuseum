Daniel Steel...or DANIELLE STEELE??

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Yep…Friday night was a good night… Not only did Akiyama make his triumphant return into PCW…but three people felt the CELESTIAL sting of the nVg. First there was Misplaced…who were obliterated at the god’s hands by the Absolute Destiny Apocalypse and left to suffer in the ring. Then the Malice Man himself felt first hand what it was like to have your immediate destiny destroyed right before your very eyes…straight into the concrete outside the ring. How’s that for DIVINE intervention, eh hoss? Now…the Divine Prophetess and the Neo Void God had their sights set on the Dark Messiash…two individuals new to PCW who considered themselves to be the grim saviors of this place. Looks like someone already beat them to that title… =©= The scene opens upon a quiet neighborhood within some unmentionable city in the US. Dusk has fallen, and everyone was in for the night enjoying their dinner and loved ones. =©= =©= All except for the Celestial Couple, though. =©= =©= Akiyama and Cyclonica walked slowly along the sidewalk. Like always, they were in their usual black mesh/PVC/vinyl attire with sunglasses despite no sun out. Like Celestial beings really cared if a sun was out or not. =©= Cyclonica: You see, Akiyama? This past Friday…was nothing more than Divine. You went out there and showed everyone who crossed you just why you’re a god and their just fleshy blood sacks waiting for decomposition. And you taught valuable lessons to everyone. What do you do if someone’s in front of you and wasting your time? Take em out, just like you did with Misplaced. And what do you do when someone like Duke Williams is wasting your time with silly and futile combat? Simple…TOSS HIM and walk away, hoping that the record of ever fighting such a worthless specimen doesn’t ever come to light. =©= Akiyama nodded as the Divine Prophetess continued on with her appraisal. =©= Cyclonica: And now this Tuesday, phase two of our plans are going into action. Fusion was just the beginning…the herald of our Celestial Conquest. And there’s nothing and no one that’s even worth being considered a threat to your godly might! =©=The two of them turned in front of a familiar house and walked up to the door. There was a plastic cover on the door where there was a hole. Cyclonica looked right past that as she rang the bell. Footsteps began to approach as the door swung open… =©= Baka: What do you want now?? =©= Yep…good old Seibzhen…finally managed to shave off that fake Duke Williams beard, though there were many cuts across his face. =©= Cyclonica: Don’t you speak to us like that, mortal insect. You owe us your life for getting you out of incarceration. Baka: True…I was about to become jail whore number nine to Pootie the Pimp before you guys came for me…but that’s besides the point! Cyclonica: See…you owe us your sexuality too…though we don’t really want payment for that. Baka: Then what do you want? Cyclonica: We are in need of a base of operations until we find suitable facilities. Your home is good enough for now…we’re not too godly for slumming…we do it daily when we interact with you flat-scan mortals. Slumming is a definite description of the match Akiyama has with the Dark Messiahs come this Turmoil. =©=Seibzhen sighs as he steps aside, knowing full well that there’s no stopping the nVg or the Divine Prophetess once they set their minds to something. It was amazing how some people could be so intelligent while others like Steel and Stone were complete and utter ignoramuses. As he closed the door behind him, Cyclonica took one look upon the walls and dashed for the bathroom clutching her stomach. Seibzhen took a curious look as they heard vomiting echo down the hallway.=©= Baka: What’s her problem? =©=Taking the opportunity to speak with the god, he looked up with a weak smile.=©= Baka: So…how’s life been for you? You still hang around with the Void Elements? =©= Seibzhen got nothing but the trademark glare and growl from the nVg, and that was enough for him as he backed off of him. The toilet flushed as the Divine Prophetess came walking back out with a book held up in front of her face.=©= Cyclonica: Akiyama! Cleanse that wall fixture of those Malice-Man attributes!! =©=The god instantly spins around and sweeps his hand across the wall where Seibzhen’s collection of Tin Tin artwork hung. The works of Captain Haddock flies off the wall, causing Seibzhen to cringe as the pieces crash all around him. Cyclonica looks up from behind the book and nods approvingly, rubbing her toned stomach slowly.=©= Cyclonica: Thank you…it’s revolting to see Williams in person, but to see him illustrated is just as bad as in real life. I should be thankful that there’s no stench accompanying the art. =©=Cyclonica suddenly looked down at the novel she clutched in her hands. She looked up at Seibzhen with a inquisitive look.=©= Cyclonica: Baka… Baka: My name is Seibzhen…Richard Seibzhen! Cyclonica: …Baka…why do you have a romance novel in your bathroom? =©= Seibzhen looked down at the flowery book in her hands and shrugged.=©= Baka: Where’d that come from? Cyclonica: Your bathroom, you deaf primate. I thought I made that clear in the previous statement. =©= She checked the back of the spine and read it aloud.=©= Cyclonica: “Malice”…by Daniel Steel? Why…the FREAK…did one of the Dark Messiahs write a book about Duke Williams? =©= Seibzhen takes a look at the book himself, doubting the words of the Divine Prophetess…such a foolhardy mistake to make in life.=©= Baka: That’s Danielle Steele…not Daniel Steel! Cyclonica: I know what it said! You think an L and an E make a difference in the name? The facts are still clear as day! Daniel Steel…one half of the Dork Messiahs…moonlights under the name of Danielle Steele. And his pen name isn’t even a clever one! It truly saddens the likes of even me to find that a man has to resort to wrestling to fund his writing talents. =©= Seibzhen sighs heavily.=©= Baka: Look at the back cover! Does that look like a man to you? =©= Akiyama steps besides Cyclonica and they both turn the book to its back. They stare at it for a few seconds, scrutinizing the photo of the woman.=©= Cyclonica: If you squint your eyes...and tilt your head to the left...you can make out the same feminine features that Daniel Steel possesses. It's amazing what a little Photoshop can do these days. Baka: Oh geez… Cyclonica: It’s fine though…it really is. All in all…it really clears some things up. Like just how come the Dark Messiahs were going to be totally ineffective against the nVg…because one of them is a limp wrist writer and the other one is his book cover model. But you know…it doesn’t even matter. Be ye Dark or Dope, there’s one thing that they do not know of. They have no idea…whatsoever of what they’re getting themselves into when they step against the Celestial One in the ring. Daniel Steel and Stone might as well put their talents to more useful efforts...I hear the comic book store in the mall needs a couple of guys to dress up like Frodo and Gandalf from Lord of the Rings and pass out coupons for reserving a DVD copy when the movie hits the shelves. Those two seem pretty competent of that task. Baka: Am I just completely cursed by the presence of you two? =©=Hey…that got the Divine Prophetess to stop talking…the bad thing about that is…he also got the attention of the nVg. The two of them stared into Baka-I mean Seibzhen- with eyes of disbelief. Next thing you knew…Seibzhen was hanging ceiling high by the god by his collar while Cyclonica worked him over with her words.=©= Cyclonica: You call yourself cursed?? YOU?? You’re LUCKY is what you are! You’re so lucky that you’re even being allowed to EXIST, unless you want to be banished into the Void! Akiyama can make an exception to the rule just for you! If anyone’s cursed around here, it’s the two of us! We are the ones that are forced to sit here in this miserable little hell hole and coexist with you peons daily!! It’s us who are cursed by having to open our eyes everyday and stare into a creature you know will cause it’s own extinction not far from now! If anyone’s cursed, it most certainly isn’t you, little man! We’re cursed with having to smell your human stench! We’re cursed by having to go into combat night twice a week against rodents who cannot even hold a candle to the talent that the Neo Void God has, let alone hold threat to his well being! We’re cursed to have to fight a couple of wannabe dark ones who couldn’t deliver a pizza, let alone be the messiahs of anything important! You want cursed?? Stay tuned for Turmoil…and you tell us who is cursed after Akiyama shows two flat-scan mortals just why he is the Absolute Destiny Apocalypse of mankind!! SO SAYS THE DIVINE PROPHETESS! =©=Just then…the three of them turn around as a new character comes into play, staring slack jawed in a bit of surprise.=©= Woman: Richard…who are these people and what are they doing here? =©=
