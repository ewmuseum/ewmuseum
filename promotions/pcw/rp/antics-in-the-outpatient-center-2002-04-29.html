Antics in the Outpatient Center

29 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/29/02 The camera fades in… The scene is a hospital outpatient center. The Chef, Bertha, and Manuel all sit side-by-side in uncomfortable hospitals chairs in the outpatient waiting room. The vomit inducing smell of Saline (If you had Leukemia and Nausea you’d know!) and fresh hospital cafeteria chicken fried streak fill the air. Patients are whisked back and forth through the revolving doors of the hospital. Some scream for their mothers, other scream for a good lawyer. The doctors linger around the nurses’ station, making flirtatious remarks and talk about the latest “All Nighter” with the whore from Radiology. Some more passionate doctors talk to their patients and try to reassure them, but most get slapped a lawsuit, or even worse, a hand. The Chef: When will that British dude get out of surgery!?!? Bertha: The doctor f*cking told us f*ckfaces that it would take another f*cking hour to reattach his f*cking finger, ho @$$! Manuel: Daddy, he’s got a name… The Chef: Yes he does, and it’s probably one of those really long and hard to understand English sir names like “Appledodger” and “Billingsbumpkinsworthsmithintonmayberry”. Bertha: That ain’t no f*cking last name b*tch @$$ mother f*cker! The Chef: (Replying)….Times Bertha has been to England….ZILCH! Times The Chef has been to England…ONCE!!! Who’s the expert? ME!!! Bertha just lets out an angry “HMMPPPH!!!” and crosses her arms. The Chef: Well, he better get that finger attached soon, I’ve got a Frisbee here that’s just waiting to be chewed!!! The Chef holds up a Whammo! Brand Frisbee. Bertha and Manuel sigh. Bertha: If you f*cking wanted to play fetch, just f*cking get a piece of sh*t dog, d*ck @$$ b*tch!!! The Chef: Dogs, Schmogs… English Soldiers are the wave of the future! Bertha and Manuel sigh again. The Chef: But what really irks me, and probably Allah too, is that I don’t have a match for Supremacy!!! Bertha: F*cking true…The Chef: I’ve defeated Boda, Chet King, Big Daveylicious, and we’ve collectively defeated Rent A Hero and Juan Borro! I’ve on a major roll, and no one cares!!! Bertha: MMMM, f*cking rolls… Manuel: Why don’t you find an opponent Cheffy? The Chef: Well son, Allah has provided me with a buffet of opponents. Unfortunately, while I’m looking for a high quality product like Beef Wellington or a Cornish Game Hen with a raspberry marinade, I’m getting products more like Slyders and Kentucky Fried Condor. Allah willing, I’d like some real talent to sauté!!! Bertha: Well if you f*cking win the Extreme Title on that sh*tty as f*ck Turmoil, you can be at the f*cking Pay f*cking Per ho @$$ skank View, motha f*ckaaaa!!! The Chef nods. The Chef: I haven’t faced most of the men involved in the match, but with Allah on my side, there’s no doubt I’ll sauté some serious @$$! Suddenly the outpatient surgery door swings open and the Alouette’s new pet is wheeled into the hallway. The Chef: Magnificent Muhammad! Praise Allah! Kano Rocks! He’s OK!!! Manuel hides behind Bertha, still frightened from the terrifying experience at Khandahar. (See last Promo) The Chef: Welcome back boy! How is your finger!?!? The British Solider, a blonde man with substandard teeth and wretchedly hazel eyes glares back at the Chef and spreads his legs apart, revealing what’s beneath his hospital robe. Solider: Finger’s fine, now if I can just get a dame to jump on this! The Chef faints. Bertha perks up. Bertha: That’s a f*cking huge @$$ mother f*cker c*ck s*cking b*tch @$$ humping fairy tale f*cking sized giant @$$ f*king c*ck, d*ck!!! The soldier nods, he gets off his wheelchair and walks over to Bertha. He lovingly takes her by the hand and holds it against his chest. Bertha: This is f*cking hot! The British Solider quiets Bertha by placing his pointer finger on her lips, shushing her. Soldier: The name is Bond…Jimmy Bond… He takes his finger away from Bertha’s mouth, but she viciously grabs it and forces it into her mouth. She pulls it back out and licks her lips. Bertha: Well Mr. f*cking Bond, as you can see, I’ve got a f*cking good throat size and f*cking pussy…galore! The soldier quivers with disgust, but realizes that by pulling a “Don Juan” on B, he could get a ticket out of becoming a family pet. Soldier: Do you know how long it has been since I’ve felt a…(Struggles to say the next word)…woman… Bertha: Well I’m f*cking at least three women, so that makes up for some long sh*tty @$$ time f*cko!!! The soldier watches Bertha pucker up, and backs off. He continues pondering the effects of kissing those wretched lips. Suddenly!!! THUMP!!! Manuel Hung leaps from behind Bertha and kicks the soldier right in the shin, causing the former peacekeeper to jump up in the air and hop around the room in agony. Manuel: That’s for hitting on Mommy, and this is for me!!! He then grabs the Chef’s rolling pin, which the Culinary Conquistador so appropriately dropped while fainting, leaps up onto a chair, hops off and CRACKS THE ROLLING PIN OVER THE HEAD OF THE SOLDIER!!! Jimmy Bond drops to the floor with a sickening thud. Manuel: (With the stoic glance of Steven Seagal) Looks like you’ll be spending a little more time in the hospital…JACK!!! (Pause) I dis many years old! He holds up two fingers. Bertha grabs Manuel and lifts him up, staring directly at him. Bertha: F*cking thanks ya piece of sh*tty @$$ pig sh*t! I was about to f*cking get boned up the mother f*cking c*ck @$$… A tear falls out of Manuel’s right eye, saddened by the disappointment of Bertha. Big B, a big softy when it comes to kids, can’t let her anger control her, and embraces the little scamp. Bertha: AWWW, f*cking dry those eyes b*tch!!! Let’s get you some d*ck @$$ ice cream, ho! Bertha hauls Manuel through the outpatient center doors to the cafeteria. The Chef and Jimmy Bond, if that is his real name, lay motionless on the cold hospital floor. As Bertha walks through the door, Manuel turns towards the camera and shouts… Manuel: ADIOS ENEMIGOS!!! The camera fades to black…
