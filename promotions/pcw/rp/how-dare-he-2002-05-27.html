How Dare He...

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-27-02 How dare he deceive me. How dare that rotten filth of a human attempt to play mind games with I, the Infinite Warrior Trigger. How dare he think that he would get away with the plot he plans to pull off… how dare he show his face in front of mine. Minion. I have always hated the name, even for the brief period when I considered myself his friend. I despise The Insane One… I always have, and until the day I die I always will. I am a warrior, he is a warrior, and yet so many differences come between us. The most of which being our sense of morals: he the evil predator, and me the defender of good. Now don't get me wrong… I am not a peaceful person. My life revolves around hatred and battle, around bloody fights and doomed twists of fate. But deep down… if you look and see who I really am… I am not evil. What I do, I do for the better welfare of others, not for my own personal greed. I don't have to be who I am, I don't have to do what I do, but for some reason I continue to be the warrior that all know me as. I continue to be the protector of good that in my soul I am. The Insane One however… he is different. Search and search, but you will never find a drop of good blood in his sick and twisted heart. He is evil-- he thrives on the pain and suffering of others, and nothing pleases him more than to see a victim desperately struggle for survival. You may think that he is just a troubled soul. That he really isn't as bad as I make him out to be. That is what he wants you to think. That he just has some issues, that something in his past is unresolved-- it's a lie! Minion started off like many, being born into a rich family that always seemed to be around when he needed them. They dressed him up in nice clothing, they gave him all the presents he wanted, but something was just wrong with that boy. His family gave him everything, yet he just spat in their face, oozing with repulsion as they slaved to please him. The more his family tried to bring him into upper class society, the more he rejected it, growing out long dark hair, painting his nails a thick black, and speaking of praise to the devil. I do not know much more than this… for after The Insane One grew into a young adult, he left the only home he had known, resenting his family's pleas to save him. I, however, had a very different past. A family that didn't love me, friends that didn't want me, and a world that was so utterly heartless in times of need. Finding knowledge in places that many feared, I hardened myself into the warrior that I am today. I learned to ignore my conscience, abandoning any feeling that might hold me down. I closed up my heart for good, promising to never love anything or anyone. I didn't want this life. It chose me, and I simply decided to spite the world by continuing to thrive. When I met Minion, there was instant malevolence between us. I remember falling asleep with the sweetest dreams being of his death, and knowing without doubt that his were of mine. There was never any question of it… we were going to be sworn enemies whether we wanted to or not. Luckily however, we did want to be. The decade of my life that followed brought even more tension between The Insane One and I, as we followed each other around the globe, ready to fight at all times. Minion brought me to the peak of my game, and I to his. We were the worst thing for each other, and at the same time the best thing. If it had not been for Lance Minion, I would not be a warrior at all. And yet, as even more time went on, things began to change. The hatred between us grew so strong that it became a bond of friendship. There we were, the greatest of foes, but the only ones in the world for each other. The best of friends. And now, as I return to the present state of mind, I see that perhaps I should not be so upset with The Insane One. After all, he was just being himself… nothing in his current actions change how I perceive him in the long run. When he lied to me, he was doing only what he knows to do. I cannot hold that against him… can I? I realize now that Minion has not changed at all… it is me who was more willing to alter for him. Why is this? Could it be that I seek companionship that badly? In looking for any kind of friend, did I turn to the my mortal enemy because I already knew him so well? What is wrong with me? Am I so blind that I cannot see the underlying plans Minion is surely devising for my destruction? Can it be that I have been played for a fool? I cannot blame The Insane One for playing the mind games that he is so accustomed to. That is demanding something of him that is not possible. But that does not mean I will sit back and let my personal apocalypse begin. I am a warrior, and it is time I toughen my soul and get back to the one thing I am good at. It's time to get back to fighting the battle…
