Title: The TRUE Kings Of Chat : SCI'N'TOLLY F'N G!!! HUBBARD IF YA HEAR ME! Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 17-18/01/03 “Next on the Hubbard Network, Scientology’s highest rated talk show....” With the dull monotone of Scientology’s TV Network’s voice over man, or woman, can’t be sure, we hear an all too familiar intro.... *Dum* *Dum* Dyawwwwwwwww.... ... The opening of Run DMC’s “The Kings” starts up, but while once it was used by D-Generation X, now it has 2 more.... More.... Well, 2 new owners. “SCI-SCI-SCI-SCI’N’TOLLY G!!!!!“ Baseline kicking in and guitar guiding us into the song the opening credits of the Hubbard network’s very own “Sci’n’Tolly G Talk Show” flashing pictures of famous Scientologists amidst images of the host’s cheesy grins. “THE KINGS OF CHAT, WHAT? YA SEE THAT HUBBARD IS GOD, AND WHEN WE COME WE BRING THE KIRSTEY ALLEY, JOHN T. AND TOM!!!“ With more badly reworded lyrics name-checking celebrities and preaching Hubbard, we fade from the credits into a lavishly designed set that shills the hosts’ names wherever possible. “BECAUSE WE RHYME FOR ME, AND THEN WE RHYME FOR YOU, AND EVERYBODY KNOWS THAT HUBBARD IS TRUE... SCI’N’TOLLY G COMING TO YOUR TOWN AND IF YA GET IN OUR WAY, WE’RE GONNA HAVE YOU KIL-.. UM... TOLERATE YOUR BELIEFS!!!“ From the huge E-Meter backdrop emerge the 2 stars of the show, Sci and Tolly G, dressed in Whites suits that would make any tele-evangilist Green with envy. Their arrival is serenaded by prompted applause that could just as easily be coming from a CD while in the background their theme continues to play. “THE KINGS OF CHAT, WHAT? YA SEE THAT HUBBARD IS GOD, AND WHEN WE COME WE BRING THE KIRSTEY ALLEY, JOHN T. AND TOM!!!“ The music and clapping fade down as one to leave the 2 Scientologists standing in front of their set, 2 chairs and a sofa both in Black leather, as well as a glass coffee table that reflects an image of L. Ron Hubbard at the angle the lights hit it. This show looks tacky, and it looks like it will certainly be preachy. Sci : “Hel-.. Wait, I’m picking up something...” Sci, the shorter of the 2, reaches a hand up to his earpiece as if someone is talking to him through it, before looking out into the crowd and announcing. Sci : “XENU HIMSELF SAYS WELCOME TO THE SCI’N’TOLLY G SHOW!!!” Throwing his arms up into the air in jubilation, Sci prompts another round of applause from his studio audience who’re whipped up into a frenzy, Tolly G pouncing in and yelling over the claps. Tolly G : “HUBBARD IF YA HEAR ME!!!” Taking a step back, the 2 hosts know what’s coming as the sound of over 100 voices in unison shout back to them, sounding as if it’s a regular segment. “HUBBARD!!!!!!!!” The video screen to the left of the set then flashes up the image of famous Scientologist, voice of Chef from South park and token Black Hubbard follower Isaac Hayes. Looking over his dark glasses he puts on his best Shaft-theme voice to reply to the Hubbard call. Isaac : “You Daaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaamn right.” Hayes winks before the monitor goes back to the show logo and the 2 hosts head back to their chairs and stand just in front of them. Their eyes hunt for the auto-cue and lock onto it, reading their script like pros. Tolly G : “Over the last few weeks, Scientology’s NUMBER ONE rated Talk Show has brought you many top guests....” Sci : “....Many of whom are our personal showbiz friends...” Tolly G : “... And tonight we continue the trend with a VERY special guest.” Feigning bemusement, Sci looks to his co-presenter. Sci : “L. Ron Hubbard?” Tolly G : “If Hubbard could wrestle, then yes.... Because tonight, on this very show we bring YOU....” A drum-roll goes out around them as the lights flash, adding some kitsch suspense to the proceedings. Tolly G : “THE MAYOR OF HUBBARDVILLE, WRESTLING’S ONLY SCIENTOLOGIST.... GOLD PATRON MERITORIOUS XAVIER KANNON-HALL!!!” Sci : “HUBBARD IF YA HEAR ME!!!” “HUBBARD!!!” Again, Isaac Hayes’ visage flashes up on the screen accompanied by his low, gravely voice. Isaac : “You daaaaaaaaaaaaaaaamn right!” The studio’s lights flash Purple and Green as the giant E-Meter set slides apart for PCW’s owner Xavier Kannon to walk out in a smart dark Grey suit, straightening the collar as his forehead immediately feels the heat from the hot studio lighting rig. “Er.... Like, you forgot your Ellie~!” Scurrying out behind him is PCW’s preppy Commissioner, Eleanor Kannon-Hall who suddenly stops and looks out around the studio in shock. Eleanor : “Like, what is this? A TV show or something?” Hearing her voice, Kannon cringes and worries about what damage his wife could do on live television. Ellie, however, just revels in the attention of the cameras. Eleanor : “Like.... ELLIE~! IF YA HEAR ME!!!” The crowd go dead, until they all notice the scowl on Xavier’s face that prompts the studio floor personnel to gesture to the audience, getting them to play along. “ELLIE~!” Bouncing on the spot and applauding herself, Ellie~! looks to be thoroughly relishing the attention. Eleanor : “You’re, like, SO damn right and stuff! YAY ME!!!” Sci and Tolly G look to one another, wondering what they’ve gotten themselves into with Xavier’s extra baggage as they take their seats opposite the sofa, Xavier dropping back onto it and making himself comfortable as Ellie~! looks out into the audience, bemused. Eleanor : “You do, like, realise there’s all those people here, right?” Turning towards the hosts, she sees them nod and just shrugs, skipping over to the sofa. Eleanor : “Must get WAY annoying and stuff when you’re trying to get changed and have, like, SO a lot of people watching you.” A confused Tolly G goes to tell her they don’t actually live on the set but a focused look by Kannon who raises his eyebrow as if to tell the host to let it slide stops him. Ellie~! leaps onto the couch next to her man and snuggles up to him as Sci prepares to start the questioning. Sci : “First, Gold Patron Meritorious, may I say it’s an honour to have you with us here tonight...” Confused, Ellie lifts her head up off Xavier’s shoulder and stares at him with her vacuous pale Blue eyes. Eleanor : “Gold Patrowha?!?! Did you, like, do a Prince and change your name of a symbol and stuff?” His eyes narrowing and face wincing as he realises Ellie~!’s words are going out to Scientologists around the country, Kannon puts his arm around her and kisses the top of her head. Xavier : “Maybe you’d like to get some sleep, my little Poon Puppy.” Shrugging, she seems fine with the idea. Eleanor : “Okies, you 3 only ever talk about that boring cult and stuff anyway!” A Red tint comes to Kannon’s usually pale cheeks to suggest embarrassment as Ellie~! nestles her head into his shoulder groove and yawns, closing her eyes and dozing off as Sci and Tolly G try to salvage the interview. Tolly G : “So... Um... Xavier, Scientology... It rules, right?” Hopefulness in their eyes, the two beleaguered hosts stare at their guest. Xavier : “Of course, with the exception of Giovanni Ribisi we’re truly the Religion of the future. Not caught up on deities and commandments, how many wars do you see being fought around the globe that have Scientology involved?” Over exaggerating the amount of thought required to answer the rhetorical question, the 2 hosts scratch their heads and look perplexed before Sci hazards a guess. Sci : “Um..... None?” Kannon snaps his fingers, nodding. Xavier : “Correct, unless however you could the war of propaganda against us by the Government, or the war of oppression and prejudice we have to face as Scientologists each and every day in this country.” Seeing that the blatant Scientology shilling has returned to their program, Sci and Tolly G try to keep the momentum going. Tolly G : “Now, Xavier... You’re another in a long line of celebrity Scientologists who’ve overcome the odds to be successful in the public spotlight... How did you manage to overcome the prejudices of the world?” Leaning back on the sofa casually, Xavier edits out all the negative points from his answer. Xavier : “Well, it wasn’t easy as the PCW fans were never likely to embrace a man who’s religious belief didn’t fit their ‘Norm’, but all I could do was let myself as a human being do the talking and that way they’d have no grounds to be pre-” Not as asleep as they hoped, Ellie~! rolls her head on Xavier’s shoulder and looks up to his face. Eleanor : “And then you, like, SO backstabbed that Krayzie guy.... Oh, and then you TOTALLY screwed Tyler out of the company...” Frantically gesturing to their producer, Sci and Tolly G call for an advert. Sci : “Um... I’m afraid we have to go to commercials now but we’ll be right back...” Not paying any attention to the hosts, Ellie~! continues as Xavier cringes. Eleanor : “Oh, and then you used Scientology’s, like, religiousy thing to evade Taxes, and then yo-” Before she unwittingly gets Kannon into more trouble the feed suddenly switches to commercials, and thankfully that is where we leave the Hubbard Network, just before the John Travolta “Just for Scientologists” hairdye commercial. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
