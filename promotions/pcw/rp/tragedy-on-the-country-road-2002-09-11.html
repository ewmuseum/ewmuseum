Tragedy on the Country Road

11 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/11/02 It started out like any ordinary night, during any ordinary day, of any ordinary week. However, by the end, there would be a devilish twist of fate in store for the Latin Beauty, Cecilia Verucana. A long road stretches out for miles, barely any houses or buildings... actually, there aren’t any at all. Just trees, many trees, their leaves beginning to change from their luscious green to a somber brown. The first signs of fall. There is a bend in the road some distance away and through the trees surrounding this bend, a pair of blinding round lights filter through the leaves. Then, with the sound of an engine and in the blink of an eye, the car has flashed past, ,quickly racing towards it’s destination. The moon, high up in the midnight-blue sky, reflects on the hood of the black vehicle. Two women sit in the driver and passenger seats. The woman driving has shoulder-length grayish light ash brown hair. From the looks of her complexion and the striking beauty of her face, it is easy to figure out that this woman is of Latin descent. Around her neck is a gothic-style chocker with many spikes making heir way around it. ON her arms and wrists are many outrageous and fascinating bracelets. From spikes, to chains, to plain metal plates, these bracelets also seem to be in the same gothic sense as the chocker. Now unmistakably known as Cecilia Verucana, the driver looks carefully out onto the night road, while her passenger, an auburn haired, average looking woman, stares out her window at the trees zipping past. “Don’t you think you’re going a bit fast?” asks the paranoid passenger. “And not only that, it’s dark out. You could wreck...” “Oh stop being such a wuss,” Cecilia says with vigor. “I’m only going 65. And besides, who are you to criticize, Audrey? You don’t even drive!!!” Cecilia turns and looks at her best friend with a taunting smile. “Be quiet,” Audrey exclaims. “After ridding with you all the time, I think I'm a little frightened...” She turns to look back out the window and her eyes widen in fear at how much speed they’ve picked up. “Yes, very frightened.” “Of what?!?!” Cecilia demands, taking her eyes off the road momentarily. Seeing Audrey’s frightened glance, Cecilia puts her vision back on the road. “Of crazy people like you!!!” Audrey says this with such passion that both girls cannot help but laugh. Staring back out the window, Audrey feels relieved to see Cecilia has slowed down. It then dawns on her, however, that they have been on the road for quite some time. Audrey sighs. “Jesus, Cecilia, how far away is this party?” Cecilia is so busy concentrating on the road, that she is left temporarily deaf to every sound around her. Realizing that Audrey has said something and feeling her icy glare, Cecilia quickly glances at her friend, scrunches up her face, and says “Huh?” Audrey sighs in aggravation. “The party... how far away?” She says this repetition with much articulation. Stressing her anger in her words, Audrey gives Cecilia an even colder glare. This causes Cecilia to chuckle a bit. “Hey now, no need to get an attitude... You are the one that wanted me to pay attention to the road...” Cecilia laughs at Audrey’s impatience once again. “Besides, what is it to you? It’s just a bit up the road.” “It’s everything to me because you are the one making me go!” Irritated and tired, Audrey throws her body into the seat. After a few moments, she realizes how uncomfortable it is to try to rest in this fashion and uses the lever under the seat to lay it back. “Besides, it is your duty as the driver to pay attention to the road anyway.” Miss Verucana shakes her head in disappointment. “You need to get out more...” Audrey, from her laying position, tries to protest. “...And the mall doesn’t count. You need a social life, dear. And calling into American Idol to vote for some nobody who’ll only get one hit wonder status doesn’t count either.” Cecilia stops a moment, cocks her head to the side, and starts, “You also need a...” “...Boyfriend.” Audrey finishes the statement for Cecilia, before stating one of her own. “Honestly, I get tired of you saying that... I’m perfectly fine. Besides, after...” “Oh please. The man was a sleaze, we caught him cheating on you, remember? And don’t even say you're depressed about it, because you as well as I know that he always treated you like shit and that you can do much better than that.” Cecilia makes a left into a long driveway, which extends up a step, grassy hill. “So I think you are deserving to get a night out and have it to yourself, to meet someone new... a better guy.” “You don’t eve know what you’re talking about, Cecilia.” Audrey says this as she crosses her arms at her chest and throws Cecilia a smug look. Cecilia looks back in confusion, obviously taking her eyes off of the road. However, Audrey does not notice this and neither of them notice that they have gone right off of the driveway. “What do you mean,” Cecilia asks, sincerely dumbfounded by Audrey’s statement. “Well, we all know what the stereotype is for women wrestlers....” Audrey laughs at her own insult and Cecilia grows a malicious and vengeful look. She slams her fist into Audrey’s gut, who promptly responds with an “Oof” Holding back laughter because it hurts to laugh, Audrey responds. “Well there goes all the air... Look out!!!!!!” Cecilia looks up to the road just in time to see that she has drifted off the driveway and is headed for a tree. In a nearly fatal act, Cecilia slams on the bakes and spins the wheel, causing the car to slam into the tree... on the rear passenger side. Audrey breathes heavy with fair and quickly exclaims, “You see, you are a crazy driver! You never keep your eyes on the road! You could have killed me... or us... or you!” Still in a lot of shock, Cecilia brings her head up from the steering wheel and holds her hand over her heart. “Jesus... let me go check on the car...” Cecilia quickly opens her door and jumps out of the vehicle. Walking around to the passenger side, she sees that she has completely demolished the passenger side of her car. “Damnit...” Cecilia kicks a tire and sighs as a hubcap falls off. “Audrey, turn the key and see if the things starts...” “Cecilia, I don’t see how the damn thing is going to start... it’s like, bent around the tree...” Audrey argues, but then sees the angry look on Cecilia’s face and quickly follows orders. She turns the key and the engine starts, but then stutters out. Trying again, the women get the same response. “It’s not gonna work, Cecilia.” “Mother f*cker,” Cecilia spins around and throws herself at the tree, putting her head in her arms. “We’re gonna have to walk to the party and get a ride to the nearest gas station or police station or something. I don’t know... it’s so late out and it doesn’t seem like there’s anything around.... damnit!” Cecilia begins to stomp on the hubcap. “Calm down, Cecilia. It’ll be fine... lets just go get someone...” Audrey gets out of the car and shakes of her nerves. Then, grabbing Cecilia’s much larger arm, Audrey begins to pull her back in the direction of the driveway. Wandering aimlessly through the trees, the two women seem to have no clue how far away the road is. That is, until a pair of headlights appear through the trees. “Audrey, over there, look!” Cecilia says as she tugs on Audrey’s arm and begins to run towards the lights. “Come one, we’ll be able to catch the car...” The two women run as fast as their legs will carry them, until finally they come to the driveway. When they get there, they see that the car is just bout to zip past them. Throwing their arms in the air, they flag the car down and it comes to a screeching halt. A lone man sits in the driver seat and he stares at the two women in confusion. “Something wrong?” He asks in a curious tone. “Its probably not a good thing for the two of you to be out here by yourselves at night. Then again, what do I know?” He smiles and his eyes begin to dart back and forth, without reason. “Uh... our car crashed into a tree and we need a lift to the nearest.... well the nearest anything with a phone,” Cecilia says, looking over at Audrey with a slight smile. “Do you think you could give us a ride?” The man seems to recognize something about Cecilia and leans forward, examining her. “Hey, aren’t you that new Cecilia chick in the PCW? What are you doing out tonight... don’t you got some match to be trainin’ for against that... new Celt feller?” “Yeah yeah... it’s what they all say. Yes, I’m Cecilia, yes I have a match, and yes I should probably be training. So do you think you could cut the small talk and give us a lift?” Cecilia stares at the man in a pleading way, hoping that he’ll say yes. Audrey suddenly nudges Cecilia’s arm. Both women turn away from the car slightly, so that Audrey can whisper to her. “I think he’s drunk... I don’t think it’s a good idea to ride with him...” Audrey says to Cecilia in a nervous tone. “And what if he just turns out to be some psycho who take us to some broken down factory and rapes us or something? I’m scared...” Cecilia rolls her eyes at Audrey’s paranoia. “Audrey, in all seriousness, why would he even consider that? I could beat the crap out of this guy before he could even blink. Besides, who knows how far up this driveway the party is and the people there will be even more intoxicated. It’ll be okay... I promise.” Cecilia looks at Audrey with the same pleading look she gave the motorist. “Fine,” Audrey says with a frown. “But I’m holding you responsible if something does happen...” Audrey then stops and thinks. “Why don’t we just use the phone at the party?” Audrey arcs an eyebrow and smiles smugly, as if she has won the argument. “Because I know for a fact that these country hicks don’t have a phone.” Cecilia looks up the driveway. “Have you seen any telephone poles anywhere?” “So what makes you think any building around here will have one?” Audrey frowns. “And how will we even get back here? I mean, how will we tell them where the car is and stuff. Cecilia, we’re in some shit this time....” Audrey stomps her foot and turns back to the car. “So are you gonna take us,” she asks the motorist in an angry tone. “Yeah... sure. whatever...” he turns his head slowly back to the road and blinks. Before getting into the front seat, Audrey turns to give Cecilia an aggravated look. Cecilia quickly does the same as she gets into the back seat. Both women buckle their seat belts. The man continues down the road and pulls out of the driveway, back onto the ‘main’ road. His concentration and attention span seems to be really bad, because he keeps looking out his side window and at the two women who have entered his car. The car veers to the opposite side of the road every time he does this, as well. “Excuse me, sir... are you drunk?” Audrey asks this with a lot of anger. “Because if so... even though I’d hate to say it... I think you should let my friend drive.... it’s not safe.” “I’m...” The man lets out a hiccup. “... I ain’t drunk. I only had a couple.” The man begins to look around again. This scene causes Audrey to look back at Cecilia with a scared look. “Just how many is a couple?” Cecilia asks, starting to get nervous herself. “Uh.... I dunno. I don’t think I can count them on my fingers...” The man stops and thinks about this. “Maybe on my fingers and my toes...” The car swerves to the other side of the road again, causing everyone inside to be violently thrown around. Audrey turns and gives Cecilia a worried look. Returning the look, Cecilia look up to the front of the car, where she sees another car coming in the other direction. “Shit! Dude, look out you’re gonna hit that car!” Cecilia leans forward and tries to grab the man’s arms. This is done in vain, though, because it only causes the car to swerve even further onto the other side of the road. The last thing Cecilia knows, they hit the car head on and then there is black. ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~ Cecilia awakes, to find herself surrounded in bright white light. She sits up suddenly and feels a sharp pain in her shoulder. “Where.... where am I?” She asks as her eyes adjust to her surroundings. Seeing that she is in a hospital bed, she looks around violently, but then winces in pain as she feels a sharp pain in her neck. “What happened? Where’s Audrey?” A nurse standing beside her bed looks up and sees that Cecilia is awake. “You were involved in a very violent car accident. Lucky for you, you only experienced mild injuries. You should be much better in the morning...” The nurse smiles, but the smile doesn’t seem sincere. “Where... where’s Audrey???” Cecilia looks around and finds that she is the only one in the room, other than the nurse. “Is Audrey okay???” The nurse gives Cecilia a solemn look. “Was Audrey the other woman in the car with you?” Seeing Cecilia’s nod, the nurse becomes grim. “Well that makes things easier... seeing as the other three were hard to differentiate.” Cecilia’s eyes widen, taking this statement in. “What do you mean... are you saying....” The nurse swallows and lowers her head. “Honey, your friend... Audrey.... she lost a lot of blood.” Pausing for a moment, she finally continues. “She passed on before the ambulance got there....” The nurse continues to say something, but Cecilia doesn’t hear it. She seems deaf to everything. A large wave of emotion crushes her and she cannot even breathe. Staring right through the nurse at the wall, she begins to feel light headed. Then there is black....
