Semore Smoke becomes a stand-up comedian!!!

14 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 14, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up into a hot muggy afternoon. Black rain clouds that could burst at any second in the sky dropping its rain droplets down onto the earth below cover the sun. Semore Smoke is standing outside yet another big building that looks like a headquarters of some sort, but instead of it being either the Primetime Central Headquarters, or the Wrestling Tattler Headquarters, this one is the Comedy Central Headquarters. The building itself is about ten stories high and has a sort of mixed paint job of blue and red to it. The building is made to look a little comedic, but to Smoke it just screams insanity. Out front is a sign that features a few of Comedy Central’s characters, including a few of the puppets from Crank Yankers and a few painted pictures of Jimmy Kimble and Dave Atel, one the host of The Man Show and the other host of Insomniac. Smoke walks from the sidewalk where he had been standing looking at the massive thirty acre building the whole time. He’s wearing a pair of jean shorts and a white T-Shirt to try and beat the heat of the day. He walks through the short front lawn and up to the huge double doors at the front of the building. He pushes open the door and steps into a nice cool building, the air conditioner must be on all the time for it to be that cool. Smoke almost shivers from the coldness, but he gets a hold of his senses quickly. He walks up to the desk in the middle of the huge lobby and looks at the secretary behind it. She looks up at Smoke as he walks towards it and nearly passes out in her chair. She seems to be love struck or something, but that’s usual for Smoke so he ignores it. He walks up to the desk as she starts to get her composure back and he asks… Semore Smoke: Excuse me, do you know where I can find the Executive Producer of the Comedy Central station? {{{{The secretary continues to blush as Smoke answers the question. She looks better though then she did when Smoke first entered into the lobby of the huge building…}}}} Secretary: Yes sir Mr. Smoke. But first off I needed to ask you, how great was it kicking that racist asshole, Mark Makonie? {{{{Smoke smiles this time, as he didn’t realize that the people at Comedy Central were such wrestling fans, or at that Primetime Championship Wrestling fans…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yeah, it felt great to do some good for once. With me being from the south I kind of know a lot of racist bastards, and it was pretty nice taking one of them out of the picture for once. {{{{The secretary laughs at Smoke’s statement, even though he was serious while he said it. The secretary continues to laugh and point to the left as she gasps to try and get the words that she wants to say out of her mouth…}}}} Secretary: His off… hahaha! His office is down that way take the… hahahahahaha!!! Take the elevator up to the top floor and it’ll be the first door on the left once you step off the elevator, it’s right next to it. {{{{She continues to giggle, but Smoke doesn’t really see what’s so funny. But he just nods his head in thanks and follows her instructions he walks towards the left where she had pointed and into a small corridor where an elevator is. He presses the button for the elevator to come down and waits a few seconds. As he waits he looks at his watch and notices that the workday is almost through so he better get his interview over with as soon as he gets up there. Semore is at the Comedy Central Headquarters to interview for a spot on their Friday Premium Blend, which is pre-recorded most of the time, and some of the stuff they show up there has been playing for a year. Semore wants to record in front of a live audience with his comedic talent and then show it on Comedy Central’s stand-up comedy show, also known as Premium Blend. The elevator reaches the bottom floor after a few seconds and Smoke climbs onto it when the doors open up. The elevator music is that of the classic sounds of Bruce Springstein. Semore doesn’t recognize the song but he does recognize the singer for sure. He starts to hum along to the tune as he hits the button for the top floor, or the twentieth floor. The elevator starts it climb towards the top floor without a stop in between. Smoke just leans back on the rail in the surprisingly luxurious lift. He looks up and notices that the elevator is not only looking good but also is going up surprisingly fast! It’s currently at the fifteenth floor and is rising into the upper teens. Before Smoke knows it a few seconds later, the floor stops at the twentieth floor and Smoke steps off. He looks at his watch and sees that it’s nearly five o’clock and that the day is almost over. He turns after walking off the elevator towards the left and walks to the first door he sees on the left. On the front of it is “Executive Producer”. The secretary had let Smoke by the first time because he is scheduled to have a meeting, that’s why he was allowed to enter; now it was the moment of truth. He’d either find out if he was funny enough for Comedy Central, or if he would have to just go and present his skills in front of the wrestling fans on Primetime Championship Wrestling shows such as Turmoil and Fusion. Smoke turns the knob of the door, fearing what he might find as he enters the room, if he’d be welcomed as a star, or sent out like a jerk. Smoke enters the room shaking more then he had ever shaken, he’s more nervous then he was in PWA, more then he was on his first PCW appearance, more then anything. Smoke walks up to the desk and finds that the person sitting in the desk hadn’t noticed his entrance. Smoke then lets out a sigh and says…}}}} Semore Smoke: Excuse me I’m Semore Smoke, the wrestler from PCW. I called and scheduled a meeting with you today about a spot on your Friday edition of Premium Blend. {{{{The person turns around and shows a huge grin on his face as he looks at Smoke. Smoke just smiles back, and then realizes that he recognizes him! He was the guy who had took him in at Fox one day and let him do a bunch of Monty Python sketches for a Monty Python special that Fox was going to air, but what was he doing here? Smoke just has to ask that…}}}} Semore Smoke: Whoa. I know you you’re from the Fox network aren’t you. Why are you working here now? {{{{The producer shrugs and then says…}}}} Producer: The Comedy Central station and Fox network are affiliates, and well, I was offered more money to come to Comedy Central and do this job. When I heard that you were interested in a spot I went ahead and booked it because I know from first hand experience that you have comedy talent. {{{{Smoke’s face just brightens up as the words spill out of the Producer’s mouth. It’s praise that he thought he’d never be able to get…}}}} Producer: But now, the last time we met you were only working one show a week, two if you had a Pay Per View match to work. But now you’re working two shows a week and it’s three if there is a Pay Per View. Do you think you can work a weekly spot on a pre-taped Premium Blend? {{{{Smoke thinks for a while because he hadn’t really thought about his busy schedule as of late…}}}} Semore Smoke: Well to tell you the truth, not only do I have PCW to worry about but I’m also representing the federation and the inter-federation that it’s in, in a tournament called the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. {{{{The Producer nods and then ponders just like Smoke had done to begin with. He then smiles as he gets an idea…}}}} Producer: I’ve heard about this GTT thing, it ends on Friday right? Well, in that case, you work your shows on Tuesday, Friday, and even Sunday for Pay Per Views, and then on Monday’s or Wednesday’s we’ll book you for a comedy show in a town somewhere close to where you’ll be wrestling. That way you don’t have to worry about hours of traveling each week, or the strain of overworking. {{{{Smoke nods and then has another question that comes to his mind as he thinks about it…}}}} Semore Smoke: Uh… sir? When will be my first show? Producer (With a smile on his face): I’ve booked you for next Monday night to perform at the Raleigh ESA in a huge amateur production we’re doing for next week’s Premium Blend. People have been complaining about us having just old stuff from a year back, so we’ll have a lot of new names such as you up there with fresh material, you think you can handle it? Semore Smoke (With a shrug): Yeah sure, no problem, nothing can beat the pressure of wrestling live two or three times a week. Thanks, I understand that you have to get your work done before the day is over. I’ll be going now; I have to get traveling to the site of our next PCW event anyways. {{{{The Producer has not stopped smiling the whole time and he stands up to shake Smoke’s hand. He’s wearing a semi-formal suit that Smoke hadn’t noticed before at all. He shakes Smoke’s hand and Smoke just looks at the tie and has to chuckle a little bit at as it pictures Semore Smoke in a numerous pinning situations, one from where he beat Lance Perfection earlier in the year for the World title, another where he pinned Scott Taylor after a tag match. Smoke looks up at the Producer who is now chuckling as well and before Smoke can comment on the tie he says…}}}} Producer: Yeah, I’m sort of a fan of yours. I’ve followed your work ever since you were in Dream Wrestling Federation. Semore Smoke: Cool, I better get going, see you later. Producer: Same. {{{{Semore walks towards the door and opens it again but twisting the knob and then pulling. He exits through the door and then goes to close it but he hears the Producer yell from inside the office…}}}} Producer: No! Keep it open! I need some fresh air anyway! {{{{Smoke just nods and keeps the door wide open. He walks back towards the elevator smiling as he realizes that now he’s a stand up comedian, and that one of his other life long dreams might be coming true…}}}}
