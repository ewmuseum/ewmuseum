The Crusader Chronicles 3...The Escape..Sort of...

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Aug. 6, 2002 (The scene opens into the psychiatric ward at the Pentagon, where we last left our hero, Venneman Pendragon. Why the Pentagon? Where do you think all of the people who have a beef with the government go? Anyhow. The camera moves down a lonely corridor filled with identical doors. We pass Venneman Pendragon's personal shouter, Benjamin, who appears to be pushing a laundry cart. He looks a tad nervous as he scans the area. On past, the shot halts in front of an open door. The camera moves through and we see Venneman Pendragon, sitting on the floor, still restrained by the straight jacket. He looks coldly across the room at a grandfatherly gentleman that we know only as the Doc. He smiles reassuringly at the crazed Pendragon, and finally he speaks...) Doc: I believe you were about to tell me about the delusion John Crichton when last we spoke...Now. I believe that you would be wise to continue on that train of thought. Talking about the problem is the only way you will ever get better...You are quite sick. (Pendragon barks a short laugh and his eyes grow even more contemptuous. He stares coldly at the Doctor across from him...) Venneman: You guys would seem to have all the answers. And all the power. You stripped me of sword and armor. You've locked me in here like some kind of animal. Why should I tell you what you already know? It was you people who MADE Crichton! So why don't we cut the crap and get me the hell out of here? Doc: Tsk, tsk, tsk....So long as you are in denial about your problem, you will never leave! So why don't you just play along...All will be well... (Venneman leans back against the wall and shakes his head. He looks back up and his eyes appear to be cloudy, not the raging orbs of insanity we are used to...) Venneman: Very well...Crichton is the leader of an organized crime syndicate. He uses his super human powers, that YOU PEOPLE gave him to advance his power. Unfortunately for him, he is also a wrestler in MY federation, the PCW. Doc: PCW...That is the place that affects this alternate world of yours called...Gauze? Venneman: Exactly. May I leave now? Doc: So you say that John Crichton is in the PCW? Venneman: He sure is... Doc: Certainly...Feel free to go at your leisure. Thanks for the cooperation.... Venneman: So am I cured? (The Doc shrugs as he leaves the room without undoing the straight jacket.) 'Damnit! Where was I going to go? I could hardly move. What the hell...I have a match on Tuesday, and I am stuck in here...And where the hell is Benjamin???' (Almost as if on cue, Benjamin pushes the laundry hamper into Pendragon's room....) Benjamin: Ok..I have a plan... Venneman: They said I could... Benjamin: You get in here...I managed to find your clothes, so you can change after I get the straight jacket off of you... Venneman: But, the Doctor said... Benjamin: Are you worried about the Doctor? Get the hell in this hamper! It's the only way. Venneman: But... Benjamin: But nothing... (Benjamin pulls the still groggy champion to his feet and pushes him into the hamper. Pendragon tries to protest, but Benjamin shhh's him....) Benjamin: I'm nervous enough...I would appreciate it if you just let me get you out of here, my Lord... (Pendragon lets out a sigh, but he stays silent as Benjamin pushes the hamper from the room. Benjamin pushes the hamper down the hallway and does not receive a single strange look from passersby, even though Pendragon barely fits into the hamper. At last, Benjamin makes a turn and pushes the hamper through a door marked EXIT. Only then does he look around and pull the sheet from Pendragon...) Benjamin: Ok...It's safe. Venneman: I know. I was trying to tell you...The Doc said I could go. They only wanted to know where Crichton was. Evidently he has been eluding them. Goes to show you how much exposure our TV Champ has gotten himself. (Benjamin slaps his own forehead after hearing his daring rescue was needless...) Venneman: Now get me out of this contraption... (Benjamin tilts the hamper and Pendragon rolls out onto the ground. Benjamin helps him with the straps on the straight jacket and Pendragon throws the device to the ground. He then strips off his remaining clothes, and the only thing that keeps the shot decent is a bit of blurring over his 'King's Iron'. He puts his own clothes back on and straps on his championship belt. He picks up Excalibur and unsheathes it. He takes a couple of swings with it before putting it back into its scabbard and belting it onto his waist. He then claps Benjamin on the back and motions for him to follow. They walk out to the front of the Pentagon, where the adventure began, and fin Xerxes still tied to a fence. Benjamin walks over and pats the horses muzzle and Xerxes whinnies a greeting. Pendragon smiles....) Venneman: Shall we be off? Benjamin: Well, there is one more thing... Venneman: What's that? (Benjamin walks over to Xerxes and pulls open the saddle bag. He reaches inside and pulls out a miniature TV and VCR combo. Pendragon looks embarrassed...Damn, what all does this guy CARRY in there!) Venneman: Umm...I don't know HOW that got there.... Benjamin: It matters not My Lord... (Benjamin then pulls out a miniature cassette and places it into the VCR. He rests the small TV on Xerxes' saddle as he presses play...) Benjamin: It would appear your opponent has actually noticed you...On this tape, he proceeds to besmirch your valiant name... Venneman: WHAT???!!! (The tape plays and Venneman stares at the screen, his blue eyes smoldering. As it finishes, a rough grin comes to his face...) Venneman: Get the camera, Benjamin... (Benjamin reaches into the saddlebag again and pulls out a mini palm recorder. Why he carries a camera when he has a camera crew is beyond even me. Benjamin gets Venneman into focus and shoots away...) Venneman: So, Crichton. You have broadcast ill words of me from your evil lair...Obviously, you have no idea what I have been up to. I have not been going to the government to get information on you, as you have clearly outsmarted them, I solicited for another reason...I wanted to find Captain America, the original, to get some pointers. He had the same treatment as you, I hoped he could help. With your latest rants, it would appear that all I have to do is show up to defeat you. With all your superhuman strength that you boast of, you lack even the most menial human intellect! You've spent eight months running from the government? Guess what...THEY KNOW WHERE YOU ARE NOW!!! But that's not going to save you from me. How did you put it...I would be too busy dressing in my mother's clothes to notice you 'kicking my ass'? Guess again. I will be too busy laughing at your pathetic attempts to pin me to notice the tears in your eyes that are running because you will have finally realized that your life, your purpose, your very EXISTANCE is a sick joke. When you are bested by a mere mortal who should have no business being in battle with you amazing strength, what will you do? Certainly you will have nothing in PCW...Hell, I bet your loyal henchmen will turn on you when they see you are nothing more than a man... I think I have finally realized why the Doctor wanted to know about you...Anyone who thinks their life came straight out of a comic book deserves to be wearing the straight jacket that I just removed. Maybe you are running from the only people who can help you... After all, they helped ME!!! (Pendragon laughs...You know the drill...Soon he is laughing so hard that the camera starts to shake as Benjamin catches a little of the vibe. Finally, the shot switches back to the original...) Venneman: Make sure the whelp sees that tape. I want him to know that I have him pegged for the fraud that he is. If this trip taught me anything, it has taught me that I am the greatest, most important thing going right now in PCW...I will not fail Skyler, and I will protect the land of Gauze...Come on, Benjamin....We have a show to get to... (The pair both climb onto the back of Xerxes. Pendragon boots the horse into action, and the two ride off down the street into a setting sun. Were this a different genre, it would look like a scene straight out of a classic western, but with new buildings, a man in armor...Ok, maybe it would look nothing like that; sue me...)
