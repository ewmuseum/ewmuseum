A little tour

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-6-02 Brandi stands at the bottom of a steep hill leading up to a very large, stone building. She’s thankful that the boat ride over here wasn’t nearly as bad as Wrestle Vessel. The water wasn’t choppy, and she didn’t feel the need to run to the edge of the boat to relieve her stomach of its contents. Despite the sun overhead, it’s quite windy and Brandi’s hair whips around her face. She’s glad that she remembered both her sunglasses and her sweatshirt, although it is pretty lightweight. Her friend Lindsey stands nearby, peering out across the clear blue water and dreading the boat trip back. Unlike Brandi, he stomach doesn’t handle being on the water very well, even though the journey was smooth sailing, so to speak. She is also wrapped up in a sweatshirt of her own, although she was smart enough to put her hair up before they left. Lindsey looks as though she might be having trouble holding her food in, but Brandi grabs her by the arm and starts to drag her up the steep climb anyway. When they finally get to the top of the hill they’re relieved to find that the rest of the tour group hasn’t left them yet. In fact, the tour guide seems to have disappeared. Brandi and Lindsey will be spending the day with the five other people in their tour group, and with the tour guide. The group consists of a man, his wife, their son, and a couple that would make Steven Smith proud. This had been Lindsey’s idea of a break for Brandi. Of course, she hadn’t told her that a trip to Alcatraz wouldn’t exactly be the place where they would have the most fun. She also didn’t tell her that instead of taking her mind off of wrestling, it would more than likely be on her mind more than ever. She would constantly be reminded of Snow, and wondering what would happen the next time the two of them meet. If I have to keep listening to the two life partners talking about how they’re going to explain to their situation to their parents for much longer I think I’ll go insane. Oh great! Did I just hear them talking about what they were doing last night? Brandi inches away from the two men as they share their stories and laughter. Lindsey follows. Brandi is glad to see that her face doesn’t look so green anymore. Lindsey- I’m starting to think that maybe I don’t come up with the best ideas. Brandi and Lindsey both giggle, but stop quickly when a stern looking man approaches the group. He is wearing a pair of tight blue jeans, and an even tight t-shirt with the word “Guide” written on the front and back in big black letters. He also has on a black baseball cap with the word Alcatraz stitched on the front. He crosses his arms across his chest, which makes his muscles stick out even more in the shit. Guide- I am James. I am your tour guide. I will guide you. You will listen. Understand? Brandi manages to hide a laugh and she nods along with the rest of the group. When James turns his back to them, she and Lindsey look at each other and share a whispered giggle. James- Follow. The two gay men are the first to follow James inside the stone prison. As they walk past, Brandi hears them whisper something about how James’ butt looks in his pants. The son of the other couple runs after James, so his parents hurry after him. Brandi and Lindsey are the last of the group to enter the building. Once everyone is inside, James turns once again to face them. James- This is Alcatraz. Alcatraz was used as a federal prison from 1934 to 1963. Famous people were incarcerated here. Follow. Lindsey looks over at Brandi, to ask her what’s up with the way James talks, but she’s too far off in her own world, thinking. Lindsey shrugs and follows after the rest of the group. Brandi unconsciously follows along. Her eyes dart around as they walk. This place sparks an interest in her, for some reason. I actually feel kind of bad for the people that got sent here. I know they did something wrong, but I don’t think that anyone deserves to live like this. Well…maybe a few people. Those who show no compassion for others. Those who…well, those like Snow. James stops once again at the top of a flight of stairs. Brandi looks down. She doesn’t remember walking up them. Brandi looks around. They’re now in a row of cells. The large room is drafty, and has very little light. James holds on to the bars of one of the cells as he talks. James- Prisoners slept here. They lived here. A few died here. Brandi tries one of the cell doors, and is surprised to find that it slides open. She taps Lindsey on the shoulder and slips inside the cell to get a better look. On one side is a hard looking cot. On the other is a metal toilet and a sink. Other than that, the cell is empty. Lindsey leans against the cell door, watching Brandi as she inspects the living conditions of former prisoners. Lindsey slips, and the heavy door to the cell slides shut with a loud clang. James stops talking and pushes past the five others in the group. He stops in front of the closed cell, and peers in at Brandi. James- What are you doing? Brandi- Well, right now I’m standing here. A few minutes ago I was standing over there. Brandi points to a spot a little behind James. Lindsey laughs, but is shut up quickly by a dirty look from James. James- Come out. Brandi walks over to the door, and tries to slide it open, but somehow when it closed it locked itself. Brandi tries one more time, then she shrugs. Brandi- It’s locked. James- It isn’t locked. Brandi shrugs again and takes a seat on the hard cot. James tries to pull open the door, but his muscles fail him. The door truly is locked. James looks angrily at Lindsey, then at Brandi. He turns back to the rest of the group. James- I’ll be back. You stay. James hurries down the stairs in search of the key so that he can let Brandi out of the cell and continue with his daily tour. The little boy of the group runs off down the corridor. His parents hurry off in that direction. The gay couple have a seat against one of the cells. Brandi now lays down on the cot, staring up at the ceiling. I wonder if this is how Snow feels. Laying there on his hard cot, knowing that he’s stuck here. All the anger and frustration he must certainly be feeling building up inside of him. All the anger and frustration that he had taken out on both Rent A Villain and Brandi only days earlier. Lindsey stares at Brandi from behind the other side of the bars. Her face is a look of concern and worry. Lindsey- What if they can’t find the key and you’re stuck in there? What if you end up missing your Cruiserweight title shot because of me? Brandi walks over to Lindsey and rests her hands on the cold bars of the cell door. She laughs. Brandi- Look, I’m not going to be stuck in here. I’m sure they have to have the key laying around somewhere. Just in case something like this happens. Plus, I thought you were trying to get my mind off of everything. Here you are, bringing up the fact that you think there’s a possibility I could miss one of the biggest matches of my career. Lindsey- Sorry…I just saw that look in your eyes. I already knew that you were thinking about it. You kind of got that blank stare you get when you concentrate. I just figured… Brandi laughs and paces along the cell. Brandi- Well, you need to stop figuring then. Stop figuring and stop worrying. James will be back soon with the key, and I’ll be out of here. Nothing will keep me from Fusion. Nothing. Ok? Lindsey laughs uneasily. Brandi goes back to the cot. She rests her back against the wall, and sits with her knees up against her chest. A strong wind blows through the cell from somewhere, and Brandi shivers. I sure hope that James guy comes back soon. I mean, they do have to have a key, don’t they? They can’t just let someone sit in here forever. Damn you, Lindsey! You have me all paranoid. Thinking that I won’t be able to wrestle The End. I know I’ll be able to. It’s just that now I’m having second thoughts. I mean, it’s not everyday I get a shot like this. I have to take it, and give it my best. If I don’t, I won’t get another shot. Or will I? Thankfully, James plods back up the stairs with a set of keys in his hand. Brandi and Lindsey both smile, but James looks pretty unhappy about having to make the trip. He tries each key in the lock until he hears a loud click. Then he pulls open the door, and Brandi steps out. James shuts the cell door once again, making sure that it’s locked so he won’t have this problem again. James- No more trouble from you. Understand? Brandi nods. James starts walking along the corridor once again. He doesn’t seem to realize that he’s missing three members of his group. Brandi and Lindsey follow him at a distance. James- Follow. Brandi and Lindsey giggle. James doesn’t notice. He’s too busy discussing their next stop with the two gay men who have followed so closely behind him. Brandi- This is the next room. People walked through here. Follow. Brandi acts serious as they follow James into the next room. Lindsey giggles. Brandi- You listen. No talking. Understand? Lindsey giggles and laughs again. Brandi points out various locations and doors in this room. Brandi- Food was made there. Breaks were taken there. Follow. Brandi walks forward, still looking around the room. She doesn’t notice that James has stopped a few feet in front of her. She smacks right into him, and looks up. He is looking down at her with his angry scowl. James- Something funny? Brandi and Lindsey both shake their heads. James- No more talking. You just follow. Understand? James doesn’t wait for an answer from either one of them. Instead, he continues on his tour. I hate following people around all the time. I just wish I could go out on my own and find things out for myself. Hmm…I wonder if that’s how The End feels. Always having to follow someone around. Well, someone or something. Sure, instincts play a large factor, but still…I can’t help but wonder how that must feel. One thing is for sure. He does deserve his Cruiserweight gold. Lindsey- Hey, are you ok there? Brandi- Yeah, I’m fine. Why? Lindsey- You spaced out. Brandi- Oh, sorry about that. I was just thinking about… James runs over to Brandi and Lindsey again. James- No more from you two! You must leave. You have disrupted my tour too much. Go. Brandi and Lindsey look at each other and shrug. Two security guards appear out of no where and grab the two women by the arms. Brandi looks over her shoulder to throw a little wave at the tour guide, but he is too caught up in a story by one of the men. Just as they round the corner, she could almost swear that they grabbed his butt. Brandi shakes the thought out of her head as she is lead outside. Lindsey- Wow, I can’t believe it. We just got kicked out of Alcatraz. Brandi shrugs. The two women have a seat on a rock overlooking the bay. The security guards disappear to wherever it was they came from, leaving Brandi and Lindsey to wait out in the wind for the next boat to arrive.
