Root of Problems

11 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/11/03 The rounded slab of meat sizzles violently in the pan as Milo takes a silver colored spatula and pushes it underneath. He flips it on it's opposite side and steps back quickly to keep the sizzling fat from burning his arm or staining his silk red shirt. 'Why anyone enjoys eating these slabs of heart attack is beyond me. Why can't people just conform to veggie burgers? Their delicious and nutritious!' he says in a sing songy voice while turning the heat off from the pan. He walks over to the counter and pulls out some buns and puts the burger together. Just as he's finished Brianna walks into the room with a cheerless look on her face. 'Brianna I made lunch for you. It's just like you like it with the little ketchup and mustard swirls on top.' he says pushing the plate in her direction with a smile on his face. 'No thanks, I'm not hungry.' she says waving it off as she reaches in the refrigerator and pulls out a bottle of beer. 'I slaved away on this and you aren't even going to eat it?' 'Milo it's just a burger.' 'I try to be nice and this is the thanks I get?' 'Fine if you want me to eat it so damn bad, I will!' she yells at him as she yanks the plate up and marches quickly out of the room ascending the stairs to her room where she slams the door shut right behind her. Her attitude and anger at the world has still remained. She hasn’t been able to shake these awful feelings, even after Milo has worked so hard to get her back to her old cheery ways. Even after finally getting a match Brianna was still upset. The reason for her being upset was her worry for Kramer. With all of the terrible stuff he had gotten in to, he could possibly be hurt. She hadn’t really known him very long, but she felt a special connection. It hurt her every time she looked at Grimer, because it reminded her of him. “God why can’t Milo just get the hint to leave me be?” she says into the mirror as she looks at herself, a bit surprised at her anger filled eyes. “What is happening to me? Kramer is ok, he has to be….but there is still a part of me that begs to differ. How can I even begin to worry about tomorrow or Turmoil? Kramer’s more important….I need to just know he’s ok and life will be better, much better.” Brianna stands up and walks over to her well made bed, and falls back on top of it with her arms crossed over her chest. The camera switches to the opposite side of the door to Brianna’s room is shown. Standing there is Milo who oddly enough has a grin on his face. “If I find Kramer, she’ll be back to her old self!” he says merrily as he walks silently away, not wanting Brianna to know he was eavesdropping. He heads back downstairs and grabs his black jacket from the coat tree by the door. He heads outside and stops. “How in the world am I suppose to do this? He could be billions of places…..this is gonna be hard than I first anticipated, but I gotta find a way to get to him.” He starts walking and disappears down the street. The scene cuts back to Brianna who is still sprawled out on her bed. She is still very sullen and upset. “To make things worst all of my matches leading to the PPV have Skitzo in them!!! Why? Does the booking team hate me that much? I swear, come PPV I am settling this once and for all. I don’t care if it damn near kills me, this thing between me and him will be over and I will come out the major victor.” She looks as though she is going to say something else but the scene slowly starts to fade out.
