Title: This Commercial Announcement is Paid For By... Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: 12th Afghanistan. A land under much danger right now. Assassinations, daisy cutters, and major terrorist organizations have brought the priorly somewhat obscure nation into the pantheon of American vocabulary. It has also brought a lot of grief for two of PCW's tag teams. Both Politically Incorrect and the new PCW Tag Team Champions, The Celestials, have been having issues with The Chef and the Refugees as of late. Celestials? Who the hell am I kidding? The tag champions is/are Akiyama. One man......in his mind, one god. Politically Incorrect and this god have been having issues. To be precise, William S. Powell has somewhat of a schoolboy's crush on Cyclonica, Akiyama's Divine Prophetess. The two teams seem destined to collide. But before that can happen, they must unite to nip the Islamic threat in the bud, in a 4 vs. 3 handicap match. And even before THAT, the politicians have a chance to flat-line The Refugees in a Fusion main event. Once again...America's going to kick some Afghani ass. ::The scene fades into a picture of the American flag. Once more, the almighty narrator of Politically Incorrect makes his presence felt, speaking in his powerful, booming, awe-inspiring voice.:: Narrator: For nearly 10 full months, the United States of America has been battling against a faceless enemy. An enemy that often cannot be pinpointed. An enemy that has little but a name. The name is TERRORISM.. ::The screen lights up with pictures of suicide bombing scenes in Israel, one of the U.S.S. Cole, another of a pair of United States embassies, and culminating in a photograph of Ground Zero.:: Narrator: Groups such as Hamas and Al-Qaida have caused irreperable damage in places around the world, damage is that is physical, mental, and spiritual. ::A photograph of the Jewish Star of David appears on screen, in blue and yellow.:: Narrator: Many of these groups advocate a Palestinian state, at the expense of Israel. To Politically Incorrect's William S. Powell, this is a source of discontent. ::The scene fades to a picture of Powell, standing at a dais in what we presume to be his hometown of Des Moines, Iowa.:: William S. Powell: For those of you who do not know, I am a proud Jew. The proliferation of anti-Israeli terrorism in this world must be stopped. We have already experienced enough in our history. Narrator: William S. Powell is a man prepared to take a harsh stance on terrorism. It is evident that, in the wrestling world, The Chef, Alexis Parshin, and The Refugees represent the worst that there is. : A picture of the four men appear on-screen.:: Narrator: Is Primetime Championship Wrestling doomed? Is there no way to stop those who support terroristic attacks against both the United States and Israel? NO! THERE IS HOPE LEFT!! ::A picture of Politically Incorrect appears on screen, with the American flag as a stirring background.: Narrator: Politically Incorrect is ready, willing, and able to put a stop to this! But, by themselves, they can take on two men, the Refugees on Fusion. But, two men cannot stand against four... ::Slowly, three letters begin echoing, starting in a low voice, and slowly rising.:: n.......V.......g ::The letters swirl around in the ears, reverberating as the chant rises... NVG..............NVG............. Narrator: The reigning PCW Tag Team Champion, Akiyama, for one day only, will stand at the side of the politicians. Akiyama despises Politically Incorrect, but for one day they will do what they must. They will obliterate the influence of Islamic terrorists in PCW. ::A clip fires up....Akiyama lifts Alexis Parshin into the air, annihilating the Uzbek Annihilator thrice with the Absolute Destiny Apocalypse. The god raises the tag team titles high overhead...: Narrator: The tag team champion of the world.....and arguably the best tag team in the business today........it will be Politically Incorrect, to say the very least. ::The commercial ends with.....:: Paid for by the friends of Politically Incorrect Neo Void Gods.Eliminating Islamic Militants: The Only Right Thing To Do. ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
