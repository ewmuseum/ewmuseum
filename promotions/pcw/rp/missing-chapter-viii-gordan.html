Date: Today Gordan… Gordan… Gordan… I had the conversation between that had just occurred between me and Clair Rivence, friend of Hanry Claval, and partner with Ivan Chistov. In the conversation, the only information I had gotten out of Rivence was Chistov’s fourth alias. I only needed one more, his real name? All this time, I had thought that Ivan Chistov was the real name of the man I was looking for. Now I find out that Chistov is a fake. But who is behind all the different name? Gordan… The fourth alias I had received. Was it a first name, or a last name? Gordan could go both ways. In my trusty database program that had helped me out quite a bit during this mission, I had typed in Gordan. Of course, as I suspected, I got back over three thousand match names, just in America! I needed a first name. But where would I find a name? Like an angel came down from earth, a 'You Got Mail' symbol popped up in the lower right hand corner of my screen. Anxious and wondering who could possible send me mail, I double-clicked on the logo, making an email pop up. It was from the Hare Krishna! From: [detail withheld] To: [detail withheld] Subject: 'Terry' SyN, I found a word. Terry Could be a name? From,Missionary #17 That was all the email said. Terry? Now I had the words, 'Terry', and 'Gordan'. Wait a second! Terry Gordan? Gordan Terry? Both of the names made sense but could it be? If the name didn't match up, what would happen? Then what would I do. Dumb me, analyzing things again. Just put the name into the database! I first entered the name of 'Gordan Terry' into the database. I waited quickly for the information I needed to pop into the database. It didn't come. Instead, the following error showed: Error:No Matching Name Found.Please Enter Another Name To Try Again. Damn computer database, how reliable could it be? I switched the name around into, 'Terry Gordan' and waited patiently, watching the hour glass turn around and around until the name popped up. A smile spead across my face. Name: Terry Gordan Age: 35 Other Aliases: Ivan Chistov, Fred Mystique, Fredrick Thomas, ??? Other Information: Terry Gordan's past is pretty mysterious. Besides the fact that Terry was also Ivan Chistov, who was trained by master, Henry Claval, Terry is unknown. Gordan stays with assistant, Clair Rivence in an unknown location. The hideout, which Gordan has been using for about the past year, is somewhere in Germany. Besides that, no evidence has been given on where Gordan could be. Police have been looking Gordan for the last year, on private locations but with no lead's, the police are struggling. Police say that if you find Clair Rivence, you find Terry Gordan. No other information. I know where Ivan Chistov/Terry Gordan is! I was so close to him and I have to go back! To the church!
