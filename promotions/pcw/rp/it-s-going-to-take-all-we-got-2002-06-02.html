It's Going To Take All We Got...

2 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.2.02 [previously] Mike and Ron visited a local bar in Houston. On that night their best friend James was working as they talked for several hours on how they, Mike and Ron, were doin in the buisness. Several hours passes, as Mike and Ron dropped a couple more drinks before heading back home, catching up on the sleep they couldn't get. [fade in] 'Ah man..what a night,' I said to myself. 'I knew we stayed out late last night, but damn I didn't think I can sleep this long.' I get up out of my bed and look at the clock. It was already twelve thirty in the afternoon. Man how time flies when you are catching up on some sleep. I know that me and Mike will not stay out that late anymore, even if we can't get our sleep here at our house. I get out of bed and walk down the hall towards the stairs. As I'm walking I notice that Mike's door is still shut, so I figure he's still asleep and I won't bother him. I walk downstairs and goto the kitchen. I see their is fresh coffee in the pot so I grab a mug and walk to the machine. I pour the coffee as I look on the counter. What caught my eye was the wrestling magazine we had recieved in the mail not to long ago. I pick it up and walk towards the back door. I open the door and step outside on the patio. I shut the door behind me and walk towards the patio furniture. I put my coffee on the table and sit down on the chair. I open up the magazine and flip right to the page of the editorial that mentioned us. 'Since this guy has written this about us, it has only made me more pissed off and more determined to do what we I already knew what we can do and that is win the tag team titles,' I said to myself. 'I know many people see us and think that we are nobodies and that we were probably just brought in to fill the tag team divison, but people will be very very shocked once they see what we have and will do. We have competed against Cowabunga Connection and took them to the limit and walked out the victors. Some considered that an upset only cause CC have been in PCW since the demise of the 'beginning era', and were considered one of the top tag teams. Now comes our match this Tuesday on Turmoil, where we have to face the title holders of that 'beginning era', The Heartbreakers. Since the demise of the 'beginning' The Heartbreakers took the tag division by storm capturing the tag titles. Now in the 'new era' of PCW, many feel they are going to win this Round Robin Tournament and capture what was theirs, and that's the PCW Tag Team Championships. Of course that in itself sparks me and Mike more than anything. I know they held the tag titles before and are number one contenders for them right now, but in due time WE will be.' As you can tell I am getting a little fired up talking about The Heartbreakers. This is going to be a big match for us in our short careers here in PCW, cause we are defending the former tag team champions. I get up off the chair and look around in the backyard just to calm myself down a little bit, but I can tell that it's not working. 'This will be a time in where we can show everyone, that we are no joke. That we are for real. That we will be the tag team that everyone will be talking about and not just about us upsetting so many tag teams here in PCW, but how we became the PCW tag team champions in such a short amount of time!' I hear the back door slamming and a voice. The voice is Mike. 'Ron what in the hell are you doin out here yelling for?' 'Well it's about time you get up Mike,' I said to him sarcastically. 'Shut up man. What are you doin out here man?' 'I was just thinking about our match and how ready we are for it that's all.' 'I mean you were talking pretty loud Ron, that's why I came out here.' 'And if I spoke quitely you would probably still be sleeping right?' 'It's a possibly, but I'm up now. This is getting to you ain't it?' 'Yeah it is.' 'Why? You and I both know we are ready for this. We are in the best shape we have maybe ever been in. We have trained hard, we have concentrated on the task at hand and we are on our way Ron.' 'I know we are. I just want to prove everyone wrong about us. I want everyone to remember us not just as a typical tag team but as something else.' 'It's not going to just happen in one night or two weeks. It takes time bro. I'm sure many people will look at us different once we knock off The Heartbreakers and move on a step closer to the titles. Many people felt that we upset CC, and I know we did and that's what we like right?' 'Yeah Mike we do.' 'We like to be the underdogs. We like to upset the best teams in any federation and we have started that here. CC is down and the next on our list is The Heartbreakers.' 'Hell yeah! I know we had a great match against CC, but let me tell you something Mike...' 'What's that?' 'This very match on tuesday, is going to take every thing that we have. They are going to push us to the limit and we will push them to a limit that they have never experienced. We are fourty-eight hours away Mike...' 'It's going to be one hell of a ride Ron. One hell of a ride.' [fade out]
