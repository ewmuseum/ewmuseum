Ignorance... through and through

3 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd of August, 2002 (We are greeted with a landscape view of The Pyramids in Cairo, Egypt. A slight gust of wind blows past as the darkening sun beats down the day’s last rays upon the sand-covered valley below. Night approaches at frightening speed as the pale blue sky darkens to a dim hue of navy, the stars can just be made out glistening against the quickly dimming sky. Many of the tourists who have spent their day here begin to pile into coaches and other transportation vehicles that are lined up on the dusty road ahead, high hopes of returning safely to their hotels elsewhere in Egypt on their minds. Amongst the laughter, the happiness and the voices of the other tourists in the valley only two sole tones of speech stand out against the rest.) “And we’re here to do what?” asks the voice of a disgruntled woman. “We’re here, Isiy, to recruit…” Says a familiar voice. “Recruit for what?” the same agitated voice of Isiy Xansiv echoes. (We begin to focus in on the two distant figures whose voices were at the forefront of all the others. They stand looking up at the amazing pyramids, expressions of great wonder about their faces – well Pharaoh does anyway. Isiy on the other hand stands looking a little tired and mainly peeved.) “Have you seen the army Frisco has?!” says Pharaoh, startled by Isiy’s dismissal of the point in their mission. “So what?! Frisco has an army? Whoa! We’d better run for the frickin’ hills.” She replies, rolling her deep blue eyes. (Pharaoh shakes his head and begins to walk away from the colossal constructions. His footing finds its way in the direction of the coaches.) “I don’t think you’re grasping what I’m saying, my love.” “Ya’ know, I seem to be getting that impression too.” She says sarcastically. (Pharaoh shakes his head in dismay.) “Look… it’s like this, OK?! Frisco doesn’t like me, which I think is pretty damn obvious from the way he attacked me at the PPV with a steel chair, almost rendering me unconscious so that I couldn’t compete later that night to win the PCW Heavyweight Title. So now we have a problem. Frisco is my enemy, right? And Frisco has an army at his disposal to use against me, much in the manner he did at Wrestle Vessel. I, on the other hand, have nothing to guard me at all! So, Isiy, that’s why we’re here in Cairo - to get the best of the best Egyptians to help me in my fight against Frisco…” (Pharaoh sighs, looking ahead at the dust-writhed coaches. His future endeavours will surely be ruined now that Frisco has shown hatred towards him. And the only way for him to truly be able to get along with the things he wants to do in PCW is basically to hire some sort of security team…men, and perhaps women, who can take on the oncoming forces that Frisco throws at him…) “And to think that Kannon went on to defeat Frisco later in the night for the title! It makes me sick damn it! I beat Xavier, so therefore I should have had my title shot!” Pharaoh bellows, anger flowing through his bitter veins. (Isiy smiles soothingly and pads her husband on the shoulder.) “Look, you’ll get your shot eventually…” She disputes, making sure she argues gently so not to annoy Titu any further. “And besides, you’ll get your change to punish Frisco this coming Tuesday. You’re booked against him in the main event, right?” (Xansiv frowns, shaking his head.) “Yes, but I should have got my title shot right there and then!. I earned it! And then Frisco--… Arggghhh! That bastard will pay for this!! He cost me my damned shot, Isiy! Frisco’s made a big mistake, and this Tuesday he’ll get a first hand view of all my anger in one singular outing in the ring!!” (The gorgeous Egyptian looks her husband up and down worryingly.) “Yeah… I know, honey.” She says carefully. (Xansiv shakes off his anger, considering his reason for coming to Cairo.) “So as I was saying, if I’m to stand any chance of surviving against Frisco I have to collect together a group of men that I can use to help me out when his goon squad decide it’s time for me to be taught a lesson. I know what people like him are like…they’ll do anything to annoy, agitate and more importantly they’ll do anything to get in your head, Isiy! Frisco is just like Kannon; only he has an army at his command! Which makes him even more dangerous than that damned scientologist…” (Isiy doesn’t say a word; she simply continues to follow her husband towards the bulking vehicles in the not-so-distant distance, hearing him out as they walk.) “And besides, maybe its time we stocked up on a few people to act as some sort of personnel for me-- err, for us…” Pharaoh states, a lopsided smile spread over his face. “If you say so, Titu.” Isiy murmurs. (The couple finally arrive at the feet of one of dust-covered coaches and climb onboard with the rest of the dazzled tourists…) “Back to the Cairo Plaza Hotel, please.” Pharaoh says, beginning to walk away from the overweight woman driving the coach. “Well that’s the only place we go to anyway, bub.” The woman says sarcastically. (Pharaoh stops in his tracks, turns on his heels and frowns at the super-heavyweight woman in front of him.) “I’m quite aware of that, I just thought I’d be pol--…” “Just sit down, will ya’?” (Pharaoh’s eyes widen. The indecency of it! How could she speak to a fellow human being like that?! And one of such high fame as him?!) “Look, ma-am, I am Titu Xansiv of Primetime Championship Wrestling and I--…” “Look, mate, I don’t care who you are…” (The woman pulls Pharaoh towards her using the jewellery around his neck, a threatening expression about her irate face.) “…Or where you come from…you’re on my bus now, and you’ll sit down and shut your damned mouth!” (She laughs smugly and pushes Pharaoh away from her face with ease. Titu goes tumbling backwards into an old woman, who hits him over the head with her handbag in return.) “I’m sorry, Mrs. But it wasn’t me! She pushed me!” (Titu points towards the plump driver.) “And this is my bus, ya’ pussy bastard! You don’t give me the blame on my own damned vehicle!” she says, splashes of saliva flowing from her mouth. (Xansiv sighs.) “Look! I’m sure this can easily be resolved if we just get together and talk--…” “Or you just get of the coach, man!” (Pharaoh turns to see a man, long hair flailing over his calm and methodical face, raise his arm to throw a scrunched-up paper bag at him.) “Just get off the bus, you’re holding everyone up!” He shouts as the ball of paper flies through the air. (Pharaoh dodges it and stares the man down hatefully.) “What was the point in that?” (The guy shrugs off the comment and proceeds to hurl another paper bag at him. This time the bag hits Pharaoh square on the chin. Titu clenches his fists tightly, trying to stop his easily possessive anger from taking over.) “Come on, Titu. We’re obviously not wanted here.” Isiy says from behind his broad shoulders. (She begins to drag Pharaoh from the coach as he screams in anger at the passengers.) “You’re all just like the people who hate me at work!” “Yeah, and I’m thin, bub, now take a hike!” shouts the bus driver. “Yeah, it doesn’t look like that’ll ever be a possibility for you, fat girl!” Pharaoh shoots back. (The doors of the coach slam shut leaving Pharaoh and Isiy standing outside its confines with nowhere to go.) “There has to be another coach around here somewhere…” (Isiy looks around as Titu begins to wipe off the dusk from his loincloth and jewellery.) “Frickin’ assholes! Some people just really get on my nerves…” “Just try to remember you’re a big fan favourite, too. We don’t want one singular outing to be the downfall of your career.” Says Isiy. “Yeah, but did you hear that hippy guy and that fat bastard?!” Pharaoh argues, “They were giving me more abuse than I’ve ever had to handle from the public!” “I suppose sometime you’ll just have to handle that sort of thing…some people don’t like you, just like some people like Frisco and Kannon…” (Pharaoh raises his eyebrow.) “Is that even possible?!” (Isiy takes a moment to consider it.) “You may have a point there. But anyway we have to find a way out of here!” (Pharaoh looks around. In the distance the fleet of coaches and transportation vehicles fade away over the horizon, a dust cloud trailing behind them.) “Hmm…No, I don’t think there is, we may be stuck here…in the middle of the goddamn desert all because of that fat fucking bus driver.” Pharaoh says, raising his voice as he insults the driver… (Isiy sighs.) “Will you just stop abusing that woman? I could end up like that one day!” (Pharaoh’s jaw drops.) “Oh…my…Gods!” (Titu gasps for air.) “You are kidding, right?” (Isiy smiles.) “Nope…” (Pharaoh drops to his knees in the sand, a pair of tiny dust clouds erupting into the air as they hit the floor and a fearful look upon his face.) “Dear, gods, why do you mock me so?! Why goddamn you, why?!” (He beats his fists into the sand about him as Isiy rolls her eyes in disbelief.) “Geez, I was only joking…” (Pharaoh springs back to his feet and laughs nervously.) “Yeah…I know…I was just…err…It really doesn’t look like we’re going to get out of here until tomorrow morning, Isiy!” he says, changing the subject with amazing speed and agility. (Isiy looks around.) “Titu, look at it like this…” She points towards the dusty road just meters ahead of them, “If we follow the road then we’ll be back in Cairo in no time, OK?” (Titu raises his hand towards his chin and strokes it in deep thought.) “Perhaps you’re right, and anyway if Frisco can navigate over the oceans then I must be able to navigate myself along a dusty old road…right?” (Isiy nods.) “Right…” She says. (Pharaoh smiles smugly and sets off down the road to the sound of sighing from his wife.) “Err…Titu?” (Pharaoh stops halts, turns around and folds his arms over his chest.) “Yes?” he asks. “It’s this way.” (Isiy motions towards the opposite direction to which Pharaoh is walking.) “Well of course it is! I--…I…I was simply testing your navigation skills, Isiy!” (The Egyptian beauty forces a smile.) “Of course you were, honey, now common, it’s this way…” (‘The Charm’ shakes her head as she sets off down the correct route towards Cairo.) “So, just out of interest, Isiy, how did you know that this was the way back to Cairo? Not that I don’t know, obviously, but just to see if you’re using your mind correctly …” (Isiy points up towards a huge white sign reading ‘CAIRO - 3 MILES’) “That kinda gave it away.” She says. (Pharaoh nods, looking up at the huge sign. How he’d missed something like that moments ago was beyond even him. He smiles in his wife’s direction, revealing his pearly white teeth.) “Excellent use of your…err…eyes, my love. I’m sure you’ll make a good …helper… in my battalion.” He beams. (Isiy’s brow wrinkles as a frown forms upon her face.) “What do you mean: ‘You’ll make a good helper in my battalion’?” (Titu sighs.) “Isiy, you’re going to help me with important matters! Why, I’ll need someone as quick and as sharp as you to help me with my trials and tribulations!” (Isiy’s face becomes a portrait of angry shock.) “I won’t have anything to do with your…group of…of…thugs--…” “They won’t be thugs, Isiy, they’re going to be there to help me fend off Frisco’s thugs! Why, I’ll have nothing to do with anything like that at all!” he replies dismissively. (The flown on Isiy’s face remains there for a few more seconds before she gradually relaxes.) “Well…Ok…I guess you’ll need an advisor of some sort--…” “Yes, my dear, and you’re the perfect candidate!” “Well lets just see how things go first…you don’t even know that these people will want to help you!” (Pharaoh chuckles.) “Isiy, sometimes you crack me up…” (Pharaoh strolls past his perplexed wife, a humoured expression about his face, and sets off on the three-mile walk back to Cairo. The scene fades to black.)
