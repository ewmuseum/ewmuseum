Date: For maximum freshness! Work sucks. Sure there are people who’ll tell you differently, but keep in mind that there are also people who’ll tell you things like ‘Britany Spears is an artist’ or ‘Bridges of Madison County was worth watching’. Yup, lots of idiots and lunatics wandering about these days. Hi, how ya doin’, blah, blah, blah. Me, I’m Jack Noir, professional fly in the ointment, monkey in the wrench and a fully loaded, half-cocked sex pistol. “Look, I don’t like you and you don’t like me.” Can you say, ‘No shit?’ boys and girls? Of course you can. The guy with the blueberry head of unkempt hair gazed levelly at me. “But for some bizarre reason Val likes you.” What can I say? She has gourmet caliber taste in boyfriends and fast-food caliber taste in employers. “And she says you can be trusted to perform well.” Internal smirk. “What I want from you is to sort of keep an eye on him. See what makes him tick.” Offhand, I’d say large doses of creatine and protein shakes, but this was technically work so I had to at least pretend to be civil. “How much?” “Two hundred a day plus expenses.” So here I was within spitting distance of New York City. All at the behest of my closest friends, Benjamin, Grant, Jackson and Hamilton, to do a little overwatch work for one steroid junkie on another. It was my fourth day of living out of my car, and some definite comparisons between Grizzly Adams and myself could be drawn both in terms of excess facial hair and hygiene habits. To be brutally honest, I was funkier than a party with James Brown in attendance. Did I say work sucks? Because it does. To make things worse, I was parked across the way from this particular egomaniac’s temple dedicated to himself. What a waste. My six-three frame crammed in my Buick like it was the hold of a Cuban immigration ship and this swollen balloon sculpture of a creature wastes all that square footage on a mess of gaudy trophies and memorabilia that no self respecting pawn shop would pay more than twenty bucks for. Truly, there is no justice. Then again, maybe there is. Looking at his picture, one instinctively wonders exactly what kind of woman likes animals so much that she feels the need to mate with them? One thing’s for sure, this guy’s momma was a very open minded type when it came to camels. Maybe all those lumps on his body are for water storage? Enough musing, I’m on the clock. I peered again through the telephoto lens of my camera aimed at the massive picture windows of Lumpy’s training-slash-torture room, where he’s busy, AGAIN, with beating up a couplea guys smaller than himself. Very impressive, to be sure. I would look equally as good if I wiped the floor with a Cub Scout troop myself. Then again, how often can you find other lummoxes pushing seven feet tall? Other than the NBA, of course. Punch, punch, crunch, crunch… the result is as predictable as the plotline of your average situation comedy. Two up, two down, never mind that it looked like the guy cheap shotted the kid first thing. After all, X time repeating champs just don’t do that kind of thing, right? Paragons of fair play, right? Sure, and baseball players do it for the love of the game. Now he’s talking to himself, a sure sign that there’s a camera crew in the room. Honestly, who actually cares what these troglodyte throwbacks of evolution have to say? I can see the headlines now, ‘Wrestler cures cancer through bodyslams!’ Jeez, all they need to do now is combine it with NASCAR to form the perfect white trash recreation. One of these days I really have to invest in one of those real laser vibration readers to listen in on what they say instead of just showing my laser pointer to prospective clients and relying on my dubious lip reading skills. But what’s the point when all they say is “I’m really good, you’re really bad, I’m calling you a childish name and here’s my catchphrase?” So far, none of my wrestling type clients have complained about my fudging of the text, and I don’t expect they ever will. Hell, I’ve used the same Xeroxed sheet for the last two years and no one’s noticed. Dammit, out of coffee again.
