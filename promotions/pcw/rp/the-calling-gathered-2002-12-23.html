The Calling Gathered

23 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23 December All events in this series is set before the announcing of the suspension of the Tag Team Championship and the departure of Gaz 'The Jester' Green to the Front Office. Prologue: Following a letter to various collectives within the Primetime Championship Tag Team Division offering them the chance to become the number one contenders to the PCW pristine, our next episode saw the meeting of these eight individuals at the address marked on the back of said document. The legit of this letter was that Playaz Inc, Skitzo (himself), Walden/ Brandi and The Black Dragons would have the opportunity via a meeting with Pranks With Attitude to redeem themselves and get right back into the title hunt. A large Gothic mansion looms ominously in the distance, its spire visible over the tree line and the moon sits behind giving the whole opening scene a horror flick awe. Like something from a Christopher Lee movie the mansion appears gloomy, stained glassed windows and two large oak doors with a brass goblin knocker. Soaking in the detail of such an architexual masterpiece the roar of an engine draws nearer when suddenly a limousine pulls into view. A long white limousine strolls up the cobbled path and it appears as though the first batches of guests have arrived, and certainly in style, however the identity of this collective is still unknown. Parking directly opposite the buildings entrance, the doors either side of the vehicle swings open unveiling former PURE tag team The Black Dragons. Dressed casually, still in an oriental style with various dragon insignias, the duo look over the mansion with roaming eyes before breaking into a conversation based on the charade they're about to embark on. 'Failure is no longer an option, the family expects results and not excuses!' began Chi'ing removing his dark sunglasses and looking over to his Black Dragon comrade who simply nodded before voicing his own concerns. 'The Black Dragon Master has spoken!' 'Inphino Blitz?' retorted Chi'ing with a dumbfounded facial expression. 'No, not that OSW fool, maybe I should've said the Mountain Lord Pai Po has spoken!' informed Chen Lei-Chia still reflecting on his last encounter with the Asian sage, an encounter that involved the family figure head wielding a bamboo cane as an act of corporal punishment for the teams persistent failure. Ascending the concrete steps that lead to the dwelling, the duo stopped in amazement, mouths ajar, absolutely star struck as they witnessed Skitzo being carried by his duplicate, smashing a leather riding cane into his bare side the clone yelped in pain. 'AAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHH~!' The shuddering cries of pain bellowed as the Skitzos drew nearer, one crash after another, one scream after another, the pair finally emerged before the Black Dragons, dropping into a heap onto the concrete ground. 'Urgh?!?' murmured the Black Dragons in unison, their eyes widening at the spectacle before them. If this charade were astonishing enough then nothing could have prepared the duo for what happened next as the heap of Skitzos moulded together creating but one being, a sole Mr. Psycho so to speak. 'I knew we should've taken the train-... BUT-... No, you insisted that we carry one another, even though your out of shape!' argued Skitzo towards himself, his face going through the motions, firstly he cringed in anger then for no apparent reason he looked upon his opponents quite jovial. 'Be quiet - We have guests!' 'Oh, the Taiwan twins-.... Tell me, what is it like having your house trampled every other week by a giant lizard?!?' questioned the inquisitive Skitzo. The Black Dragons merely looked at one another in sheer disbelief before retorting in unison. 'Giant lizard?' 'GGGGGOOOOODDDDDZZZZZIIIIILLLLLLLLLLLAAAAA!!!!!' roared the Gatorade endorsed superstar triumphantly. However the multi-persona individual cried his answer in as though many voices sounded at once, a scream of a crowd rather than a sole individual. As the three PCW employees peered at one another the bellowing roar of an engine blared. Stuttering forward with a collection of jerks the black vehicle was the same Cadillac used by the former Champions Playaz Inc, it's engine seeping from the open top bonnet, chugging cobias amounts of smoke up into the atmosphere. Finally coming to rest before the existing two teams, the doors swung open to reveal the articulate Dante Jackson and his Ebonics ranting colleague Statuz Quo. 'Well the oddities are here, all we need now is for the comedian to show his face and we'll have a potential circus attraction!' cackled the African-American Dante Jackson towards the bigger Statuz Quo. 'LOLOLOL - Dis be another conspiracy by dat mothafuckin Jester Gazzy G, his lil attempts at keeping da Blackman from his rightful property, da mothafuckin PC-Dubya Tag Team Championships dawg!' hissed the former RUSH Syndicate Champion with passion, heard by both his partner and the surrounding parties that include both The Black Dragons and Skitzo. 'POWER TO THE BLACKMAN!!!' cried Skitzo to the snarling faces of Playaz Inc. 'AH-.... POWER TO OUR ASIAN BROTHERS!!!' continued Mr. Psycho, this time channeling some very bizarre stares from the Puro collective of The Black Dragons, prompting himself to self promote with his own cheer. 'POWER TO THE SCHITZOPHRENIC!!!' 'You be one mothafuckin strange white biatch - Holla back youngin-....' injected Statuz Quo in agreement with the nodding Dante Jackson. 'Thanks!' smiled Skitzo, pleased with Statuz Quo's outlook. As the five man strong unit of PCW personnel waltzed towards the entrance, the sound of a chopper could be heard. With a gentle breeze and clearing the dense tree line emerged a helicopter, obviously someone traveling in style, making an impression, question being though, who? With the propeller blades chopping the air above the gathered guests of Pranks With Attitude, leaves fluttering and the sound simply deafening the vehicle finally came to rest on a patch of open grass merely a few feet away. As the scene returned to its desolate array of silence the helicopters doors opened and dropping to the ground was the stable mates Tim Walden and Brandi, obviously representing the illustrious Front Office. 'I think we should tax this bunch of vermin on the grounds of being just that 'VERMIN' what do you think Ms. Wilson?' asked the blonde headed Cruiserweight Champion Tim Walden. 'Hmmmm, agreed Mr. Walden!' answered Brandi towards the Front Office Financial Reclamation Executive Tim Walden. Ignoring the snarls and sinister facial expressions of their colleagues, the Front Office members stormed past the motionless statuettes and thunderously banged the knocker of the mansion to no avail. Again they smash the brass doorknocker causing a huge bang that echoed from within, yet the door remained a fortified barrier. 'Dat mothafuckin pair of white trash, Crackaz With Attitude has played da Playaz Dante-.... Deez white homeys can fuckin rot, but dis caper was gonna be our big pay-off, back as da Tag Team Champions!' murmured Playaz Inc representative Statuz Quo, when suddenly without warning the two large oak doors opened under a heavy creak. Walking into the unknown, a dark gloomy reception area waiting. Various Victorian furnishings, dank walls, minimum lighting, all of which creating a spine chilling awe, setting a whole new scene for the seven man party. Creeping in silently the collective edged forward, teeth chattering and their bodily hairs standing erect, suddenly the lights dimly lit under the guidance of one Gaz 'The Jester' Green. 'WHAT THE FUC-....' screamed the PCW employee's in unison. Standing atop of a flight of winding stairs was the Emerald Menace himself. Clad in his typical three piece business suit, his hair spiked and his teeth glistening like a quiz show host, Gaz welcomed the collective and informed them of the task at hand. 'Welcome-....' 'Mr. Green, don't you think you have some explaining to do!?!' interrupted Tim Walden. 'To business-.... As I explained via your letters, this little excursion will offer each of you the chance to become the number one contenders for the Tag Team Championship. It's all rather simple, somewhere hidden in this mansion is the gold, all you have to do is simply retrieve it and you become the next heir to the throne-....' informed the Lord Of Laughter before an untimely interruption from the Front Office pairing of Brandi Wilson and her tax inducing colleague Tim Walden. 'Wait, these people aren't in the know, these people don't know the current state of the Tag Team division since it hasn't been officially made public-....' As Tim Walden voices his concerns towards this charade the scene abruptly closes, leaving the episode on a cliffhanger. - To be continued -
