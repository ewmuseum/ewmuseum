Scuttlebutt

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 27th, 2003 'RUAAH!' Those shouts and screams can distinctly be heard throughout Stately Jackson Manor as we join our Messiah in the midst of some after thought. After the match with Violence Jack last night back in Iraq, Jackson of course left the arena, signalling a chase of migranes after screaming his lungs out after the no contest decision. Furthermore, he'd been duped for the second time by Violence Jack, and when Jack wanted to get to three, then Jackson would have to make it sure that three was never there in the first place. His eyes are bloodshot red, as his eyes wave inward and outward and closer and closer to being completely shut. His jaw muscles tighten as the light red shaded 2003 Mazada continues to plow through the road, eating yellow road markings and various tire scrapings as the humorous sounds of The Real Sadaam by Sadaam Hussien toys with radio listeners throughout Kuwait. POUND! The Messiah slams the dashboard, knocking a few items out of the glove compartment, jarring them onto the floor. Looking over the stockpile of items ranigng from a bottle of superglue, a copy of the July 1996 edition of Playboy Magazine and not to mention a fold out poster of Pamela Anderson, Jackson continues to rummage before his hand cuts upon something. Dante: RUAAH! Rasing his finger upwards to the sky, he can see a tiny and small paper cut forming upon his right index finger. The sharpness of how he's feeling right now coupled with this newborn cut on his finger has sent him into a shockwave of pain and displeasure. He begins roraring down upon the small car, smashing up the dashboard once more before beginning to swerve off of the road, leading to some drivers on the opposite lane to start yelling obsceneties. Driver: Watch where ya' goin' assbutt! The driver skees off, leaving Dante stuck as the road continues to be gobbled up Pac man style by the Mazada's tires. As he treds on borderline insomnia at this point, he skids into a nearby ditch, before waking up to realize that he's swinging the steering wheel at a rapid rotation, trying to get the car to stop. KERPLUNK! The right end of the tail fender crashes into the hulking mass of earth, leaving Dante a bit shaken up, but still in his glorious Messiah presence. Electric jolts as aftershocks continue to rattle the man's teeth and bones, but his will is just fine. Dante: Now, all to find is somewhere to make sure I don't try hurting others...why do I sound like Arnold in T3? I wonder if anybody's even see the trailer for it, dear god! Jackson stops in his tracks, his white Nikes' giving into pounds and pounds of dirt and rubble as the Messiah stumbles upon a beat up shack with the emblem, 'Mike's Hard Lemonade' shifting awkwardly to the side. Jackson raises his chin up in an oh motion, taking a few steps away from the failed soft drink company's location in Kuwait. Dante: So that's what happened, they went belly up just like Sobe` and all those other crappy mixing drinks... 'DON'T BE DISSIN' SOBE`!' From out of the thick grey clouds, the mighty voice of one of PCW's past. As Dante continues to sulk inside of the desert, a body hovers upon him, chuckling in a simple and yet more delicate way that even he couldn't take into remote consideration of what was happening. Dante: No way, it can't be! He wondered. Pushing through his blond colored cape ensamble, the man stands placing both of his fist along either side of his torso, sending a group of young screaming teenage females to come rushing over as he gleems into the moon. Dante: Teenyboppers... He stared at the Superman logo etched on the chest of the masked wonder, smirking almost. Dante: Follow the... Dante then turns his face to stare upward, looking directly at the man whom name is responsible with the leigions of teenyboppers who firmly follow any good act on the music scene. Dante: RUSSELL MAN! Teenyboppers: OOOH, NIPSY! Nipsy Russell: NIP-SAY! Soon, a shockwave of emotion go through the Messiah at this point, causing him to clearly be overwhelmed by the appearence of Nispy Russell that even under the strong guise of courage, his knees buckle from the pressure and he slips down to the desert ground, starring once more at the King of Sobe bottles and almost owner of PCW - Nipsy Russell Man Russell. Dante: Jeez, I thought Capitan Re-run Man would never let you go. Nipsy stutters a bit, before smiling. Nipsy: Hey yo! Its not that hard son! Dante: Nispy, you wouldn't believe how hard it is. Nispy: (coughs) What is going on? I mean I could be sitting here wearing my pants below my ankles, having a high fade to the top and then be maybe one of those animal rights activist, and yet still be a superhero. Like you were, or now don't feel like to be. Dante nods. Dante: Well, have you ever heard of the Supremacy Screws A Continental Champion Rule? Nipsy: I don't listen to country. Dante: Okay, lemme explain it to you. First off, at last year's Supremacy..wait, YOU WERE IN THE FRONT ROW WHEN IT HAPPENED! Nipsy looks around, to signal if Dante were ever talking to him, but doesn't return the favor. Dante hums in uproar before struggling to manuever his hand into the pockets of Nipsy. Russell begins to struggle, but Dante overcomes him and pulls out tons and tons of PCW tickets! Dante: DEAR GOD! Nipsy looks over in shame as Dante begins to count the stockpile of tickets clutched firmly in his palm. Dante: Look at this! You've got the PCW Dream Card Ticket! The First Dawning Ticket! Hell, you've even got the ticket to that Sabotage that never aired! Nipsy: And it was a good show too, with all those jobbers. I think Rush Mason finally beat Skylar Green that night. Yeah, I think that happened. Dante: Wait, Mason FINALLY beat our new World Champion? Nipsy: Yeah! Or was that Rock Startling? They both have the same shaped head and everything. Hmm... Dante slaps his forehead in complete knowing of Nipsy's forgetfulness. Nipsy: But, I think I get it. Dante: Okay, Duke got screwed at last year's Supremacy thanks to Kannon and now this year its happened to me. Screwed over by a fat man in a metal suit. Nipsy: Yeah, same thing happened to me when I fought Dr. Doom. Dante: They always want to get ya, don't they? Nipsy nods. Dante: So, what are ya going to do to help me? Nipsy: Watch you sit here as PCW films this. Dante turns towards his right, finding cameraman located in the fake bushes and shrubery as they continue to film until Dante latches his hand onto a camera. The cameraman looks on, petrified towards almost shaking in his skin before Dante just tosses the camera away. Dante: DAMN YOU!
