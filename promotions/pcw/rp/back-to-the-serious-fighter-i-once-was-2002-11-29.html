Back to the Serious Fighter I Once Was

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/29/02 A New Beginning The sun had just set and the sky was bright pink. The birds traveled in packs as they passed me sitting on my balcony. I stood there with my breakfast happy to be alive and for what I had been granted. My week, well it hadn’t been my best. I’ve been so busy running from place to place, country to country, trying to fight gangs, and wrestling to sit back and finally relax. Though I was feeling a lot of stress slowly roll off my back, I wasn’t completely happy. The reason being: I’m not winning. You see it’s not something I’m used to. I’ve lost two matches here in PCW and they were both due to my lack of concentration. I’m arriving in stadiums ten minutes before I’m set to fight. I rush everything and the money has blinded me into thinking I’ve been doing a good job, when the truth is that I haven’t. I used to be a warrior. Not a single defeat in eighty-two matches against some of the finest fighters Spain has ever produced. And now, well now I lose to unworthy men, Men such as Vilas Lavagna. The name itself gives me a slight headache. A small and weak man has himself has come into the ring with the greatest all around fighter in the land and has made a mockery of the name people were once scared to utter. Am I losing it? Sometimes I think I am. Has my magical touch disappeared, or am I just no fighting with the passion I once had. The flame needs to be rekindled, but how. All I think about lately is myself, my own life, how I can fix it, what God expect from me. Well it’s time for me to sit down and get serious about my own reality. It’s not too late to prove to the world the great fighter I am. My reputation may be tainted, but it is not ruined. I will fight back from every defeat, and grow stronger. What am I doing here? Romantico looks around and sees the Lamborghini’s in peoples driveways, the gold worn around there necks on a casual basis, and the boats in there backyards and thinks: This is not where I belong. These aren’t the things that made me the ruthless and powerful fighter I once was. It was the old me, who struggled for every day on earth. That’s where the strength came from and I have to keep it up. Sure, the money and the glamour are nice, but when you have it you lose something. You lose something much more important then materialistic possessions, you lose the real you. I won’t lose it though; I’ll never forget how hard I worked to make it to this place. With God as my witness here today, I vow to put my all into every last fight I fight. And even if I may not win them all, I’ll know I gave it all I had. PCW won’t be able to handle the artistic flavor and Mediterranean style of a diverse and distinct fighter as myself. The era has come for the old school Romantico. Back in the day, my style was definitive. My feet were like magical possessions that would just destroy anything that came in my path. If I had to use my feet in a fight, I’d know it was a job badly done. Everything I had I owed to my feet. I guess it was passed down from my father, Borro the star soccer player and me the martial arts master. Cages.Scaffolds.Concrete.Balconies.Roofs.Swimming Pools. You name it I fought on it. I didn’t care how much damage would be done to my body, as long as my opponent’s body had even more damage done to it. I was reckless; I’d bash heads into hard objects, choke people till they coughed up their lungs, kick away at people’s faces twenty to thirty times in less then ten seconds. Back in the day I was a fighter, now I’m a wrestler. A wrestler is protected and sheltered. They fight for finesse and pins. Fighters are different; fighters fight till their opponent can’t fight back. That’s what I was used to and that’s the game I loved. Pain. Pain. Pain. Those were the only things I heard go through my head, as I would fight my opponent. Win. Win. Win. Those were the only things I heard go through my head, before I would fight my opponent. Then I was champion and things went exactly how I wanted. Every fight was my way, in a Romantico Rules Match. The first floor of the stadium would be filled with nothing but stone pebbles. It was about two feet of stone pebbles and the first floor had no fans there. Then there would be sixteen sets of scaffolds connects the whole second balcony. The fans would be right there and then when the bell rang, four officials would light fire to each of the corners of the scaffolds. We’d be fighting in the middle and someone would have to throw there opponent threw the scaffolds to win, before the fire hit the middle and they would both fall the thirty feet to the bottom. The fans loved the match and thought it was the greatest thing in the world. If only things could go back to the old ways they used to be. Starting today, I go back to old school, and even if I may not be able to fight in Romantico rules match, I can still start with the basics. Romantico then smokes his cigar and puffs it into the sunny winter air, and makes his way back into his comforting house. Will Romantico like up to his promises, only time will tell. It all begins on Turmoil. {{{{((((((T-H-E E-N-D)))))}}}}}
