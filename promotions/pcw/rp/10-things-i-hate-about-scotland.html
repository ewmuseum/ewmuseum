10 Things I Hate About Scotland

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: By A Big Black Guy Named Ben Our scene opens up on Rush Mason sitting at a small desk, illuminated by only a small lamp nearby. He’s topless, and his rippling biceps look so handsome in this light…I mean, he’s got no shirt on, and is wearing gym shorts as he is working on something on the desk. Rush: I haven’t written this much since that time I wrote that stalker letter to Pam Grier in 7th grade. Yes, our favorite Voodoo advocate is writing a letter, and the shot goes down towards the letter. Dear NATO, It has recently come to my attention, that Scottish people majorly suck. Now when I say this, I’m referring to all people of Scottish ancestry and I will now provide you with 10 reasons why Scotland should be thrown out of NATO, and I’m sure you’ll agree with me here Rush: Ten reasons?! Bon Dieu, I’ve gotta work like Jane Reagan in heat. 1) Scotland produced Andy Murray: I’m not sure if you fine people are familiar with Andy Murray, but he is, to put it in technical terms, an utter moron. He thinks he is some sort of rapper, despite the very obvious fact that he is a Caucasian. Rush thinks for a moment about his audience and then continues to write Now normally I LOVE white people. I would like to, and I don’t exaggerate here, make love to every white woman who is blond and less than 150 pounds, including children. Anyways, Andy Murray has tried to become a rapper despite his incessant whiteness and is now seeking a record deal. To let such a pathetic buffoon become a major rapper would be a disgrace even to a terrible country such as Scotland. It would make all of the four people there who aren’t drunk extremely mad. Therefore, in addition to Scotland’s banishment from NATO, I would additionally suggest that Andy Murray be either put to death or locked away. Rush: One reason so far? This letter is gonna be more bitchin’ than Hubbard on a 90-degree day. 2) The people in Scotland speak in the most ugly way possible Yes, I’m afraid that Scottish people have what we in the industry would call a dialect that reminds us industry people of a flock of goats communicating. I say that with love for diversity in my heart, but their language is quite possibly the most dreadful sound since S Club 7’s last album.Just thinking about a sound so terrible makes Rush shiver to himself, then return to his writing. Not only is it a language of word butchery, but Andy Murray-yes the terrible traits of this man continue- has made even his own dialect, called NedRap or some nonsensical thing like that. He speaks like he has his own fist stuck down his throat, which is very possible with Mr. Murray, who in addition is not the sharpest knife in the drawer. At very least, Scottish people should be sentenced to a lifetime of silence from their terrible language. Rush: So this is what whining feels like? Ah yes, I remember whining back when I was a heel. Those were the good old days… Rush realizes that the camera is rolling and just quickly goes back to his writing. 3) Scottish people are on the lower end of the intelligence scale Scots are indeed not smart as a group, as evidenced by Andy Murray. Not only does he believe in his own strange rapping, but he can’t even decide if he lives in Glesga or Glesgow. I mean, what a moron, huh? Rush looks very proud at this, unknowing that the two are the same place. Scottish people also find the need to be idiots 24/7. Some of them are born that way, others are well…born that way I guess, although the schools there aren’t exactly “crunk” as my African friends in the States would say. Rush chuckles to himself, thinking that his audience will have no idea what he’s talking about. 4) Scotland is geographically difficult What I mean here is that, to be technical once again, Scotland is what a travel connoisseur such as myself might call an “island.” It in fact is not connected to other landmasses, making it tough to bike to or drive to. And, if we can’t get there in our lovely E-Classes while we smoke a lovely Cuban cigar, is it really that important? Rush of course, doesn’t bother to look this up, not caring whether this is actually true or not. An island is bothersome to keep peace in and therefore Scotland is even more of a hassle for you lovely NATO guys. Scotland basically needs to become a regular country like the rest of us if they really want to come across as caring about the issues. That would seem harsh, but if you consider it, all they’d need to do is force the geological process of millions of years in a month or so, not much to ask when you’re looking at the big picture.Rush shakes out his hand from writing cramps and thinks for a moment, and then goes back to scrawling onto the paper. 5) Scotland’s flag is unoriginal and boring Now usual this would not be a problem, but for a country with so many other problems, you’d think that they’d have the time to make up something colorful and vibrant, right? Wrong. All they have is a stupid little white x through a blue background. I’m no political expert, but I’d say that if Scotland is as screwed up as it seems, they should’ve had a LOT of time to put into that flag, yet it still looks as though a kindergartener produced it. Not only is the flag boring, but also it could be construed as racist. It is, after all, an X going through a colored background, and us Americans are against things like that. In fact, we might have to go to war with Scotland just because of their flag. That’s how much we care. Rush chuckles to himself at his own wonderful writing. 6) Scotland’s government is also boring Not only is the government boring, but they’re so dull and monotonous that I can’t even bear to think of their names. However, I do know that they are typical Scots, talking like they’re chewing on a tire and drunk most of the time. You see, Scotland gives all of Europe a bad name because of its NATO involvement and therefore gives NATO a bad name, which would encourage terrorist attacks, but not even on Scotland, which would definitely be welcomed by most of the free world. Rush: This is so much better than that time I wrote a fake fan letter to Michael Bolton! Wait a second, did that sound gay? 7) Scotland doesn’t take responsibility for its actions Yes, Scotland isn’t a very responsible nation. As of right now, Scotland is responsible for the entire debt, all poverty and famine, food poisoning, and molestation in the world. That seems like a lot of crime for one nation, but you figure they’d fess up and everything would be okay? Wrong again. Scotland has never claimed responsibility for these matters, which has encouraged acts like the terrible 9/11 terrorist acts, which would be much more productive if aimed at Scotland, preferably Glesga or Glesgow. Rush: Bon Dieu! I just had a fabulous idea! I learned some shocking recently thorough an unknown source. The 9/11 attacks were also the work of Scottish fundamentalists. It seems impossible, but I have sources within the most important of governmental agencies giving me this intelligence. But even more striking than this is that Andy Murray was the operator who ordered these attacks. Scotland should be immediately banished from NATO, or at least put in timeout or something. Andy Murray obviously is one of the most wanted men in America and should be handed to Rush Mason, the police commander of the United States when found Rush: Sometimes, I wish I were someone else just so I could meet me. Rush takes a deep breath and then continues his writing. 8) Scotland is confusing Along with all the other puzzlement that Scotland causes to people, there is yet even MORE to add to the list. At the top of this list is a little organization known as Scotland Yard. Now get this: Scotland Yard is not even, technically, in Scotland. Scotland is betraying the trust of the people, and the postal employees too. 9) Haggis is from Scotland I’m not sure if you NATO dudes are familiar with haggis, but it is somewhat like Scottish people: foul smelling, uglier than Roseanne, and extremely stupid. Of course it’s going to be popular with these barbarians. It is the most vile thing to ever come out of the world, and not surprisingly it comes from the most vile country: Scotland. Rush: Fuck, I’m out of reasons. 10) Scotland sucks There is no 10th reason, except possibly Andy Murray’s mother allowed to become a citizen. Scotland undoubtedly reeks of sucktasticness and should definitely be banned from NATO. Thank you for your time, Rush Mason Rush: Wow, I sounded terrifically rich, snobby, and preppy. Like SO Cool! Rush switches off the light just as the scene switches- the letter is read by a white man who is standing in front of a large group of men, most likely NATO associates. Reader: Wow, that definitely came from a black guy, most likely a Voodoo following fundamentalist. The group just nods as the man throws the letter in the trash and the scene fades to black…
