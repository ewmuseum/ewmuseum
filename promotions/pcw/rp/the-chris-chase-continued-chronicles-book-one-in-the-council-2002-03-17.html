The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book One: In the Council of the Immortals - Chapter One

17 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th March OOC - DOH! The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book One: In the Council of the Immortals… RP#3 for Sabotage and #1 for Turmoil… Location: On the Road on the very outskirts of Salt Lake City, Utah. Time: Approximately 09:00 on the 17th March. Weather: Dry…relatively dry. Musical Accompaniment: The Chase Volunteer Orchestra and Edward the Strolling Minstrel… Chapter One: Hitching a Ride… “Let me tell you one thing, Dustin. And for your sake I hope you take notes. Don’t EVER invite a Strolling Minstrel to accompany us on my travels!” “What, and miss out on the truly awesome opportunity to listen to some classic Middle Ages rock and roll? Didn’t you ever see Robin Hood: Men in Tights, Chris? I tell you…my man Edward here is sure to make us some all important cash on the way to Salt Lake City…” “Whatever. I can’t exactly do anything but let this run…and hope that you’re somewhat right about this whole thing. What, with the company in debt and all…I’ve had to have my travelling privileges taken away. That damn Boda’s caused more trouble than it’s worth by destroying the ring and jumbotron…” “Art thou a Grecian fool who claims to be the son of Zeus? Thou Sucketh!” “Damn Strolling Minstrels…” ::.~(The Voice of ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase)~ You are what you are until you’re suddenly the next Big Thing. Fame can change a person, it can alter their brain chemistry in a way that is totally alien to the likes of the cretins of the general public…totally inexplicable in that sense. Egos can blow up…people can alter in the blink of an eye, and for what? The adulation of people that indulge in pop culture? And at what cost to the person? There, in the eternal history of existence, has to be an exception to the rule…something that bounces into the spotlight on the rebound, if only to treat it as if it was second nature to them. I’m that exception. Let’s face it, I’m not the one who goes around trying to obliterate everything and the kitchen sink with a demolition ball, and I’m not the one who finds himself totally altered by the conflict in Afganistan, coming home to a hero’s welcome if only to find myself praising Allah. Through it all…I remain level-headed, the same old saviour to you all… ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase. However, with circumstances as they are of late…I’m wholly surprised that I haven’t turned into a raving lunatic like half of these nimrods that just happen to be my opponents. The company itself is in absolute tatters due to the lack of any financial backing behind it, wrestler’s pay is declining due to this…and we’re currently losing the ratings war with the likes of the OSW. Being accompanied by 20 members of a volunteer orchestra/brass band and a strolling minstrel isn’t exactly making things any better for yours truly as it stands… Let me explain further… As you may well know by now, the PCW is well in debt…by half a million United States dollars. Of course, that has meant large repercussions for the employees of the company, such as the backstage crew, the jobbers and…myself. And in the name of everything that sucks massive ass, this has meant that I’ve had to travel in less of a luxurious fashion. Considering that I’ve spent 90% of my time accompanied by Dustin Downey, you’d think that I’d at least be let off lightly. But, to spite the brilliance that is ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase, Dustin’s been permanently assigned as my official Travel Buddy, and hence all travel decisions have to be verified or made by him. Whoever made this travesty of a mistake will pay in due time. Hence, as the scene opens…this explains why I’m kicking up the dirt along the straight and narrow road leading into Salt Lake City, Utah…with nothing but Dustin, a Strolling Minstrel by the name of Edward and an assortment of Wind and String instrument musicians to keep me company. Oh great, shall I start making reservations at the nearest Lunatic Asylum as soon as possible, or shall I wait for the real outward lunaticism to kick in? The dust kicked up from my shoes blinds me as I trudge along the road…watching the automobiles and coaches zoom past at tremendous velocities, sending the likes of myself reeling in their wake. Beside me skips a strangely jovial Dustin Downey. Remember, it’s already been proven that Dustin’s a loon…so I don’t necessarily have to bring that up again….:: Edward: ~(singing)~ Art thou a pretender in the faith of the land of Arabs? Thou Sucketh also! LA LA LA! ::.Edward the Strolling Minstrel is something of an enigma in this world. Unique is possibly a polite term to call this complete imbecile…I mean, heck, he gives Dustin a run for his moronic money that’s for sure. Sounding rather like something from the Middle Ages, Edward sings as he strums his Lire…no, not Liar…lire, prancing about roughly 50 yards ahead of Dustin and I…leading the way to Salt Lake City as if he were the Pied Piper. The Pied Piper is an old story, dating back to god knows when. The Piper himself was a musician who played the flute as a means of hypno-eradication of whatever town hired him. The first example of an exterminator…in the story, he rid a small town of the plague of rats that had enveloped it…but when the town didn’t pay him for his job…he lured all the children away from the town with the hypnotic power of his flute… Edward’s music isn’t so much hypnotic as it is irritating, with as much organisation as his dress sense, which is literally hundreds of years behind the likes of my early 90s grunge Pretty boy look. And people call my dress sense old. Anyhow, enough blabbering on about a minstrel…it’s the 17th of March, a day that go literally go down in history as the making of ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase. No…I’m not going to release a book, what, are you completely insane? It’s something far more prestigious than a book signing, or even a normal victory other the usual losers. Number One Contendership for the PCW World Heavyweight Title is on the line tonight, a carrot wiggling above my nose…tempting me to reach out to the sky with that confident grin glued to my face. And of course, I’m entitled to make that grab….:: Chris: Oh for MY sake Dustin! Seriously do you really think that this useless cretin is going to readily fund some decent form of transportation other than having to travel in cramped cargo aircraft? What kind of life is that for a hero to the masses, ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase…can’t I just market myself? ~(There is a crescendo of a riff from One Vision by the band behind Chris…who swings his head around with an exasperated expression on his face)~ ::. The interruption of the band at a key point of a rant is nothing less than exasperating. The city of Salt Lake City looms ahead of us…as we continue to march steadily along the road in front of us. The open leather jacket that I wear flutters in the steady breeze, prompting me to try in vain to keep the damn thing close to my body. My thoughts eventually break away from this pedestrian ritual, and to that match…THAT match. Big Alan Steele, Chef and Miyagawa better hold on to their hats…Chase is going to show all what a Cruiser-Heavyweight can do….:: Dustin: No can do, Chris…my Messiah-rifically good friend. I’ve been put in charge of the financial and travel decisions that were once made by yourself. Despite me being the eternal idiot I pretty much am, I have to make these decisions with thought and planning… ::. As yet, the constant complaint constantly crosses my mind, as Dustin digs himself into an even bigger hole. Why the Strolling Minstrel? Why must I be subjected to the whinings of a Strolling Minstrel whilst I ponder the advantages of being the Number One Contender to the PCW World Heavyweight Throne…currently held by noneother than Boda-Boy….:: Chris: But a STROLLING MINSTREL?!? ::. Yes Chris Sloboda, aka Boda, aka Boda-Boy in Chris Chase lingo. That jackass has been my enemy ever since the day he tread his former NBA foot in the PCW2, back when I was nothing more than a promising youngster just trying to make his mark in the business. I was thrown into the war waged between the owner of the company, Jon Hull and Boda’s Family Contingent…as Jon’s ‘Chosen One’…as ‘The Messiah’ to his Reverend it seemed blatantly obvious as to why that happened. Back then, my association with a certain Dustin Downey was fresh…High Impact was formed between the two of us and Mark Estep…and Chris Chase was a name associated with the term ‘Future Legend’ and ‘PCW2 World Champion’. That never happened…my luck gave way at a critical point…and I’m sure you all know the story behind that. It’s not like that really matters any longer…Boda’s now World Champion thanks to the likes of myself…and now I have to work to get my shot against him at the Spring Fling Pay-Per-View on April 7th. Dustin looks at me with that same blissfully stupid expression as per usual…before answering my demanding question in the typical Dustin sense….:: Dustin: I just think that these episodes could use a little zip for a change, I mean, when was the last time that a Strolling Minstrel was ever seen in a PCW show, let alone a promotion? Think of the marketing possiblities Chris! ::. It’s rather too late for Dustin to say that, I’ve already figured the consequences of hiring a strolling minstrel to make money for inter-event journeys. Obviously…we’re not going to travel very far on a budget comprising of any profit we make from hiring this guy. And with my position in the federation being the highest it’s been since my great ascension in PCW2, the decision made here simply cannot be concocted from a hamster loving moron with a tendency to molest Brandi in clusterf*cks. Let’s face it, what would my opponents think if I couldn’t even handle a situation such as this, I’d be ridiculed…forced to comply to anything they had to say. What kind of a dumb idea is that? The time I listen to these nimrods is the time that Boda’s actually got slightly more talent than I do…arrogant swines….:: Chris: Don’t you think I’ve done that Dustin? There is NO feasable way that this loon can possibly make us more than five dollars and twenty cents. Do you know what that’d get us? ~(Again, there is a crescendo of a riff from the Chris Chase Entrance theme ‘One Vision’ by Queen, is blasted out by the band following Chris and Dustin…a combination sounds from Violins to Trombones. Again, Chris snaps his head around…frowning)~ Dustin: A ride on a crowded bus, next to a fat woman who breaks wind every two minutes, given wind speed, direction…the orientation of the sun and the solar conditions? ::. I slap myself in the forehead…obviously….:: Edward: ~(Singing)~ Art thou a Japo-American thug who oft uses a clichéd theme music? Did thou formerly wear goggles? Thou sucketh TOO! LA LA LA…LA LA! ::. Repeatedly….:: Chris: What is with you people? Is your job to permanently annoy me, throwing me off the proverbial edge? Sheesh, could you at least do a better job of it? Not that I’m complaining of course. Yeah Dustin, that would probably be all that we’d get out of it…nothing more than a measly ride on Public Transport…the bane of commuters across the world. Now then, I have a better idea…come on, I’m just full of them…aren’t I? Dustin: Well…if you’re going to contribute for a change…I’m sure that Edward will understand… Chris: Sure, whatever. Did it possibly occur to you that I was literally a white hot commodity…a lucrative investment for a large variety of sponsors across the globe. Since I’m seemingly blessed with the gift of the gab and talents to match…you’re missing a vital part of our money making arsenal, Dustin. Me. Yours Truly…the Big Kahoona himself. Sure, you may call me arrogant, but surely I can raise more dough than this reject of society. What, with this #1 Contendership for the PCW World Title practically in the bag…I can milk this cash cow until the Boda jobs his way back to Orlando! ~(Cue another riff from the band...)~ ::. Confused yet? Desperate times call for truly desperate measures. I’ve never been one to rip people off on the basis of my own notoriety…despite being one of the most conceited wrestlers in PCW at the moment. And while this cash crisis continues to be evident, there’s nothing more for me to do than reap the benefits of success…the success that I’ve attained after defeating a former NTICW World Champion 3 times, beating a Contender for the World Title…whilst making constant statements against the possibility of a long title run for Boda… Dustin glares at me with a faraway glance…he was never one to understand the machine that is business. Even I can’t make executive decisions to the effect of the likes of Duke Williams or even Tyler Nelson…not even I’m perfect. The outer suburbs of Salt Lake City envelope us now, as we stroll along a concrete path…the hustle and bustle of the city center a mile ahead or two of us now. There is a constant nagging in my brain that this walk is sucking some of the energy out of me, energy that could be oh so vital to my hopes of becoming the #1 contender for the PCW World Title…something that means so much to me now. I finally have big fish to fry…a former mentor, all three being men that I looked up to as a small fry competitor in PCW2. And now it’s my turn to swim amongst the heavyweights of this industry….:: Edward: ~(Singing)~ Art thou a Messiah? A Hero? A Saviour? Sent from yonder to entertain us all? Art thou a wizard of the grappling arts…a high-flying star amongst mere mortals? Thou…will…winneth! LA LA LA LA LA! Dustin: Excuse me while I go slap the jolly roger out of this idiot Chris…no one insults my former Tag partner and hero to the masses! ::.Grabbing Dustin’s arm, I hold him back whilst he swings a couple of fists at Edward in vain. He frowns at my persistence in holding me back…confused as per usual….:: Dustin: But…But he insulted you, Chris! Why did you stop me?!? I was going to pummel the little bastard into taking that back… Chris: ~(Slaps own forehead)~ Idiot…he may have mused about me in a lyrical sense…but he didn’t insult me Dustin. Come on, what good is this guy shilling when he can’t promote me to save his life. It’s just about the only thing that he’ll be able to do well in his god forsaken life. The focus is on me Dustin, that spotlight is shining down upon me as I stand…gloriously. Heck, I’ll beat those ass spelunkers in THAT match tonight, I’ll be the one that faces Boda. I’m the only one that can…I’ve thwarted Boda since the day we met…and thanks to you…I’ve got to reclaim my advantage over him. Do you honestly know what that’ll mean to me? Dustin: It’ll give you that warm feeling of well being? ::. Again, I slap myself in the forehead….:: Chris: Of course not…it’ll give me bragging rights once more over that jackass. And those bragging rights will give me the right to proclaim with enough justification that I’m no false Messiah. No liar…just an average guy. Dustin, with these freaking talents…I can bring this federation back into the black…back into the safe area. Boda’s done nothing but harm our profits. It’s my appointed task to rescue and save the federation from the likes of that happening. By beating Miyagawa, Big Alan Steele and the Chef…it’ll then be obvious that I’m suited to that role. And then, I can bring that Bag of Federation ballast back down to earth… Dustin: And then it’ll give you that warm feeling of well being? Chris: No…it’ll bring a final reason for why I can Justify…that! And you can Justify…that! ::. Yeah, I can justify that…I mean, it’s a knack that I’ve developed over the two years or so that I’ve been in this business. It’s surely something that will serve me for the decades of adulation to come. So, can you Justify…that, within reason, that you can support the likes of Big Alan Steele, Chef and Miyagawa in their futile attempts to curb my ascension into the hallowed arena of PCW greatness? Can you? Well…we’ll see who’d be right after tonight…when ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase finally makes the impact that he’s been waiting to make ever since he arrived in the PCW…little more than 8 months ago…Fade to White! And you can Justify…that!.::
