Semore Smoke entertains a local Toronto Club!!!

27 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 27, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up in a cold and somewhat windy location. It’s outside a bustling part of town that has very few cars in its area. But there are several walking up and down the sidewalks on both sides, most of which are on the left hand side where a bar is sitting. Word has spread across town that Primetime Championship Wrestling Rising Star Champion, Semore Smoke, is performing at that bar or club. The camera changes from the overhead view and then to the ground view as it enters the club passing several people waiting in line. It passes the bouncer who is standing guard making sure no one breaks or tries to get in without a valid ID proving that they’re over 21. The camera goes through the corridors of the front part of the club. Then finally it enters into the main part of the club where the drinks and entertainment are served for the people allowed into the club. Right now music is playing, but Semore Smoke is seen walking around off stage, taking shots from his drink as he paces. The people within the club are all basically dressed the same. Some of them have different colored hair such as blue, green, etc. Then there are others that are wearing multi-colored outfits that obvious wouldn’t match perfectly, but are in style anyway. The music playing is that of punk and other rock, the type of music that Semore Smoke grew up listening to. Smoke looks up at the stage and sees the DJ of the club getting up from his seat at the far end of the stage to walk and introduce Smoke to the center of the stage where a microphone is set up. The DJ looks around at the people who are starting realize that something is going on because the music stopped… DJ: Everyone, your DJ here, Marty The One Man Party, we’ve got a special treat for you tonight though. Pro wrestler and current PCW Rising Star Champion, Semore Smoke, is here to show us some of his stand-up comedic talents. {{{{The crowd claps somewhat but most everyone either doesn’t care or is too drunk to clap. Semore walks on stage wearing his best gangster ware, a long black trench coat, which he hadn’t worn since he was younger and in the trench coat mafia in Wilson, North Carolina. Underneath it he’s wearing a regular black T-shirt and for pants he’s wearing a pair of white dockers, an interesting ensemble of clothes, but it seems to work in the environment. Smoke looks around at the crowd as they start to settle down at the few tables in the place, a buzz is also going around the audience, as people are surprised that the local Canadian tavern could get such a stand-up comedian who has even been on TV for his work. Semore stands on the stage at the microphone and then smiles a huge grin from ear to ear…}}}} Semore Smoke: Hello Toronto! Yeah. Most of you probably know me as the current PCW Rising Star champion. But I do have another talent that I’ve showcased on TV before, on Comedy Central’s own stand-up comedian show, Premium Blend, which is hosted by David Alan Greer. But I’m not here to plug that show, even though the encore presentation is this Friday, ha. But I’m here to do a little talking about what really eggs me on. Guys who like to sit around and label other people… like these stinking PTC people. No, not the Primetime Central, but the Parent’s Television Council, which is headed by L. Brent Bozell III. They label stuff that they themselves don’t even watch! PCW Turmoil ranked as one of its top ten shows this week that were too vulgar. First thing, Brent Bozell doesn’t watch wrestling I bet. I remember when he was pulling this same bullshit over with the WWF® or WWE® now over SmackDown! ™. The thing that got me was their poll of the show, which was done by College students at Indiana University in the states, 1123 counts of drug use! Come on! I’m the only guy in PCW who openly smokes weed, pot, and marijuana, whatever you want to call it, on TV! Yet I’ve accounted for 1123 counts of drug use per hour? All right, I tried to pull a Mick Foley and call Mr. Bozell himself, but he was unavailable, or so they said. It turned out that they also counted my tattoo on my chest! It’s just so fucking stupid!!! {{{{Some people had laughed during the spill, but to make it funnier, Smoke started to hit his head and walk away from the microphone screaming like a mad man the last sentence of that spill…}}}} Semore Smoke: Woo! Glad to get that a little over with. It’s just so fucking stupid that just because I’ve got a weed leaf on my chest that they would count it! But onto better things, everyone knows that PCW is here for a Wednesday edition of Turmoil that will be taking place later today. Well, I’m facing none other then PCW veteran, Clyde, and the pussy that I’ve already talked about before, Eddie Mandrake. Mandrake hasn’t changed, in fact when I pinned him to become the Rising Star Champion I thought I actually heard him crying. Then when he stepped into the ring with me, I would have sworn he wet his pants. But to Clyde, here’s a guy who likes to say that he’s “extreme”. Well, all right, the guy has half his face burned to a crisp, but other then that, he’s just about as a pussy as Eddie Mandrake. I remember while I was in the Primetime developing stages, seeing Stewart Hix in the PCW. Those of you who don’t remember Hix, he was the original pot-head of PCW, and others say that I’m just like him, except that I’m a hell of a lot better then he ever was. Well, Clyde here put out a promo I think either yesterday or the day before with him talking to Stewart Hix in a dream I think… well, you know, how much information can Hix give you? He’s a stoner for crying out loud! He probably led Clyde down the wrong path in his search for information on how to beat me. So Hix, if you can hear me in some freaky unexplainable way, thank you! Clyde will lose, Mandrake will lose, I’ll keep my streak alive of never being pinned or made to submit in PCW since my debut, and they’ll be forced to kiss my ass…. Well, the latter doesn’t have to happen. Oh, I forgot, you know those two from RUSH, Dante Jackson and Statuz Quo… Yeah, the ones who came out at the end of my fucking match. Those idiots don’t even act black backstage, yeah on camera they’re the baddest black dudes you’d ever encounter, but off TV, they’re just like Mandrake and Clyde, a pussy! As my hero always said… uh… I’ll think of it soon… my hero was George Carlin though so you know it’s going to be something stupid as hell, oh yeah I remember! “Cause’ you’re doing it all wrong!”… Wait, yeah that was him on Jay and Silent Bob Strike Back… you have to excuse the memory folks, it comes and goes, damn weed! {{{{Smoke grabs his cigarette case from his pocket and starts to pack on it. He then opens it up and grabs an already been rolled joint and sticks it in his mouth. He then lights the joint with the lighter and takes a long drag on it…}}}} Semore Smoke: Ah yeah! That’s the stuff! That’s what all life’s problems are made of! {{{{People start to laugh at something that Smoke had actually ripped off from Dennis Leary. Smoke looks at his watch and then drags on the joint again, and then looks back at the crowd…}}}} Semore Smoke: Whoa! Damn! Fuck! Shit! Hell! Crack Whore! Ok, I just felt like saying the last one for the hell of it. I’m late for work! It’s been great talking to the local stoners here in Toronto. You’ve been great! {{{{Smoke realizes that it lacked a little in being funny, but he doesn’t care. Anything is funny to people drunk and high off their asses so Smoke knew he could have gone up there and danced the funky chicken to “Back in Black” by ACDC and the crowd would have loved it. He gets off stage and walks around to his table, grabbing his things that he laid on the table there as he does. The crowd continues to clap and a few people yell out “You Rock!” from the audience. Smoke smiles as he heads out the door of the club…}}}}
