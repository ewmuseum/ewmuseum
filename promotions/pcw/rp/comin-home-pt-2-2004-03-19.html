Comin' Home Pt.2

19 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/19/04 For weeks he was doing just as he said he would, and that was becoming PCW's newest star. Then when he reached his main goal of becoming the World Champion, he lost focus. He'd lost matches before, but he'd never been beaten twice in a row like recently. The Buzz needed to regain his hold on his confidence, and on his goals. Which is what brought him home just a few days before the next PCW Turmoil. He'd come home to regain his focus, and to avoid failure.... He sat on the old wooden bench while the cold, Michigan air blew right through his leather jacket. He sat with his eyes poised on an abandoned lot that was littered with various 'junk' items. Chunks of wood, litter, and even an old abandoned car sat where something important to him once stood. It was late at night, around 2 a.m., and the streets were eerily quiet and was lit by a few street lamps. It seemed the only thing that was 'alive' on the street was Cal Roberts, his parked car, and memories of his past. Years ago a Gym stood where the abandoned lot was. It was an old brick two story building. The first story held a boxing ring, free weights, and a small office where business was done, and the second held a two room apartment. It was in that gym where he was raised, and in that gym that his dad made him into the fighter he was today. He was supposed to be a boxer like the old man. It'd been his father's goal for Roberts since the day he was adopted at the age of seven. His 'father' had taught him many life lessons just by beating the crap out of him in that ring, and telling him how 'proud' he was of his boy for being a tough fighter. He wasn't an abusive man by any means. He'd given a lot to Roberts to make him a happy child. It was just an aging man training his successor to live out old and out of reach dreams. Eventually he discovered wrestling on television one day, and had become hooked from day one. He wanted to use his atheletic abilities to do what they did...but better. His dream didn't go over well with his father, but soon a compromise was made. If Roberts worked amatuer fights, and could win consistently his father would find him a place to train. Roberts held up his end of the deal, and was a force on the Michigan amatuer boxing scene. His father, however, wouldn't be able to hold up his end of the deal. He came home one day from a day at school to find his dear old dad dead in the gym office. Dead of a heart attack, and cold and ice. 'He didn't think I could handle the wrestling business', he thought while running his hand over his shortly cropped hair. Buzz looked at the lot for a few more moments before standing, and move towards his black Corvette. He stopped for a second and looked at the lot, and then at the surrounding neighborhoods. Things hadn't changed much, but then again things don't change...people do. With his final thought he opened the car door, and sat down inside. The car was still running, as he had left it one while he sat on the bench. It was purring like a kitten before he popped it into gearm and squealed up the street. 'One down, two to go', he thought to himself. There were two more places he wanted to visit before heading to the airport. The place where he had trained to become a pro-wrestler, Slam Masters Gym. Then he had to see her...his true love that he chose to leave for the business. As he drove down the M-59 highway leading eastward towards the other gym of his life. He thought about his career in PCW as he drove, but soon was overtaken by a sudden urge for Dairy Queen when he saw they were still open. 'Hmm...What the hell', he mumbled whil turning right into the Dairy Queen driveway. He pulled around and in front of the menu. He looked at it when he was surprised by a loud but sweet voice. 'Welcome to Dairy Queen, can I take your order?', a young women's voice purred. Roberts smirked a bit while looking up and down at the menu. 'Yeah you'll take my foot long...I mean I'll take a foot long with just ketchup.' The women giggled a little bit before responding. 'Is that all?...I mean will that be all, sir?', she inquired playfully. 'You'll see..I mean add a Mr. Misty blue raspberry, large.' 'Pull around, sir' The Buzz began to smile, anticipating who was waiting for him in that drive-thru of hope and promise. As he pulled up he saw no one, but soon what he would see walking toward the window would terrify anyone. In fact it might scare a whole small japanese village. The Buzz looked terrified as the beastly women walked towards the drive-thru window. His terrors were subsided for a brief moment when she turned to the right. It was then that she appeared once again, but with a bag and a large drink. Without hesitation, he put the car in gear and drove away from the Dairy Queen as quick as possible. 'Sweet jesus that was one ugly heifer', he thought to himself. He laughed somewhat as he drove, and thought to himself that maybe he didn't need to visit his past. He'd never been one to live in the past, and why should he think that he wasted his life. He was financially comfortable, getting laid almost nightly, and was famous. What more could you ask for? Being World Champion and having a family he thought. He quickly dismissed the thought, and turned on the radio. Maybe he should atleast go see the Slam Master gym just for old times sake. He drove for about forty five minutes while listening to 101.1 the WRIF, which was a hard rock, heavy metal station that was famous throughout Michigan. At about 4:00 a.m. he reached the parking lot of Slam Master's Gym. As he pulled into a parking space between an Oldsmobile and an SUV, he noticed the lights were on inside. Upon further inspection he noticed a class was training at this late hour. 'Payin' their dues in advance', he thought before turning the car off. He wondered if Sully still ran the place as he got out of the car and moved towards the old one story gym. It was painted a dull white and the only thing that was remotely interesting about the place was the neon blue light that read 'Slam Masters Gym'. It was even colder than it had been earlier outside, so he hurried to the door of the gym and stepped inside. The class stopped as if someone had just been shot. He looked around the small gym that was much like his father's old place. It featured some lifting equipment to the right, and a wrestling ring to the left. The five students all stopped, and began to whisper. There were smiled on their face, and he noticed a man walking towards him. It was Sully, but a much older Sully then he remembered. The man smiled slightly while approaching, and soon held his arms out. 'Buzz! What the hell took you so long to come back!', Sully said with enthusiasm while hugging Roberts. Roberts and Sully were both glad to see each other after what had been almost six years. 'Well, Sully I've just been working pretty damn hard man. Sorta' just....I don't know. I just lost track of the time, and friends.' Sully had once been a very ripped and handsome young man, but time had turned him into a greying middle-aged man. As he stood in front of Roberts, he could not help but think of his times in the gym. Sully had been very tough on his class, and that's why some of the guys had went on to be big successes in Mixed Martial Arts, and Pro-Wrestling. He remembered all of the pranks they would play on Sully, and the consequences that resulted from those pranks. 'You're doing really well kid. I've been keeping track of you, and you've made me very proud.' Roberts smiled at the comment, and stood in silence. 'Really, Cal, you were always the best student in the class. A little over confident, but that's part of what made you a great student.' 'Well, thanks that means a lot, old man. I just thought I'd stop by, and make sure you weren't six feet under.' Sully and Roberts laughed slightly, but soon a grim look spread over Sully's face. He placed a hand on Buzz's shoulder, and shook his head. 'I'm really sorry about what happened to your girl. She was always great around the gym. I can't imagine why she'd do what she did when she had a guy like you.' Roberts was confused at what Sully was saying. Roberts hadn't seen the girl since she made him choose between wrestling all over the country, or a life with her. He'd always kept a picture of her on his wall, but at the time he chose his dream over his love. 'What are you talking about? I haven't talked to Melissa in almost five years. We broke up..' I spoked with urgency and worry, as a look of surprise spread across the face of the old man. 'Buzz, she killed herself almost five years ago.' TO BE CONTINUED
