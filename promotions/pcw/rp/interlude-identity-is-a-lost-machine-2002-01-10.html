Interlude: Identity is a Lost Machine.

10 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-10-02 Its sickening how little I know about myself. What am I? I don't even know anymore. Am I the lord of Dreams? Am I magical? Am I something specific? Or Am I a sick little goth boy who enjoys making his opponents bleed? Well, I don't quite understand. This isn't something I can explain using big words and confusion. I sit here all alone and wonder what I have become. I started off insane, thats all I was. I was crazy, I didn't have rational thoughts. I wandered in a realm where straight jackets were the normal. That was what I was for a very long time... Unnoticed... Uncared for. Then, suddenly I found my calling. It was her, the love of my life. We were the first couple of PCW. Respected and revered, feared mostly. One day I fucked it up, she left, and I had nothing. My thoughts were stripped from my body, I consoled myself in the love of a nymphomaniac clown, but that did nothing for me. I lost her and that consumed my life, trying to win her back, the heartbreak and pain I felt became what made me. I was the hopeless romantic, it broke me into the spotlight. My pain made me perfect. I became a sado-masochist. Then came the third stage, I found out that our world isn't alone, there are people who rule over thoughts, feelings, and abstract ideas. They are the Unmoving, they are the Endless, they are the embodyment of Neil Gaimans work. I took one of their fallens place, my real father, Dream. I became exactly that, I was the Lord of Dreams, I was the Prince of Nightmares. I ruled over your thoughts, I knew your feelings. However, I had to sacrifice myself, sacrifice my real feelings so I could 'filter' everything else through. I was everything and nothing. I've been everywhere man...... heh. Now, I don't know if thats what I am. I don't know if I am truely magical. Can I really do these things? Am I really a god? Do I conduct magic or am I just insane. Maybe its all a dream. What irony that is.
