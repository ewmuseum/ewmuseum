Esteppin' Out

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-26-02 **Theme Music: 'Give It A Name' by At The Drive-In** ----------------------------------------Dear Jenna, Hey. Is there ANY way to make your nephew go away? Oh, and I get another shot at LeBlanc on Sunday, wish me luck. Love,Jason---------------------------------------- [Another day in Tallahassee, another stop at the Chevy dealership on Appalachee Parkway. The sky drips intermittently, but Jason's huddled inside trying to convince a man in a suit that he does NOT need a ten-year extended warranty.] Jason: I DO NOT need a ten-year extended warranty! [See, Jason's a wise man. And a wise man knows that if you go shopping for a car on a rainy day, it is, in some instances, easier to get a good deal. The dealers have less business. It's better STILL if you get them at the end of the month, in a relatively slow month for car sales (like February, which has the EXTRA perk of also being a short month), you're AWFULLY likely to get a good deal. At least, that's the idea.] Salesman: Alright, alright...so you're sold? Jason: Alright... [The salesman tosses Jason the keys to a brand new Chevy Tahoe, 2002. He turns them over in his hands, staring into the jagged metal. The salesman drones on about signing the contract, and dates to pay the given amount of money, but Jason doesn't notice.] ::I've GOTTA get LeBlanc. I've just gotta.:: [The salesman sees Jason drift off, his attention not really on the matter at hand, but at this point in the negotiations, he's used to it. It's the only way he could sell Mr. Core a car at such a high price.] ::LeBlanc...Estep...HANK~!...these days I can't tell my allies from my enemies..:: [The salesman shakes his head, and mere minutes later, Jason is driving home in his new Chevy Tahoe. It's dark blue, CD player...the works. For Jason, who hasn't owned an automobile in about a year and a half, it's like the world's greatest convenience. Yet still, as the speakers play the At The Drive-In EP 'El Gran Orgo', Jason's thoughts still turn to his enemy and nephew. ::Man, that kid's screwed up one too many times. I'm not even going to BE at Fusion, since I'm gonna do promo work for the Pay-Per-View...I swear to God that if that kid comes NEAR my match at March Madness, I'll break him in half...:: ::Seriously, the guy needs to get down to business and pick up something that's gonna tie him down. He needs a good vocation...something other than wrestling to keep him grounded. He needs guidance...:: [Jason's eyes get wide.] ::So he's booked against LeBlanc on Friday...and LeBlanc apparently is real angry at him...and me...:: [The car swerves over a U-turn as Jason rushes in the other direction.] ::I gotta get to Fusion...like it or not, he's my nephew...and I DON'T want to see him get killed.:: [And with this, Jason rushes towards Friday Fusion, and his role as protector. After all, what else would an uncle do...? Right?]
