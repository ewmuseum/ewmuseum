A Lost Childhood/A Mother Never Known/A Burning Apartment

11 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-11-03 Home. It's a simple word. Four letters. Yet they can mean so much to a person. Home is suppose to be to where one's affwctions are centered. It's warm and cozy. Where you feel loved. That isn't always the case though. I looked up at the sign. 'The Queen Heights Apartment Building'. I looked around and picked up a small broken piece of concrete laying on the sidewalk. I looked at the broken neon sign. With a swift motion I threw the rock at the sign, breaking the neon lights in it. The broken glass falls to the sidewalk like rain. I walked under the falling glass, letting it land on me. I walked into the apartment building, pushing over the door. It fell to the ground without a problem. The hinges was horribly out of shape and the frame was rotted away. I looked around the apartment building. I was instantly disgusted. Rats was running around inside the apartment. The walls was falling down. The wall paper hung off the walls and was stained. I stepped over the broken items around the apartment. The place was a mess. It always was. Even when I was younger and could hardly remember anything, I still remember how much I hated this place. Why did I hate it though? I can't seem to remember why. Over the years my memory has slowly been coming back to me. In bits and pieces I see parts of a past I don't remember. I'll see flashes of this building. It's worn down and ugly. Horrible smells come from it. A deep feeling of hate builds up inside of me. I've always said it was for that building. Then one night I had a flashing memory. It's a rainly night. I look at at the sky and see that neon sign, 'The Queen Heights Apartment Building'. Then I walked off into the dark rain. That is the end of the memory. I'm not sure what all it meant. But I was finally decided to visit it. I don't know how long it's been though. It couldn't have been that long ago. In the memory I looked to be a teenager. 'It's been so long' I said. It wasn't really to anyone. I just needed to say it. The silence in the building was starting to get to me. It was like the place was a morgue. I looked around and walked over to where the apartment manager's office was. I just hopped over the counter and looks around. Suprisingly there was still furniture in the office. Something must have happened here then. I looked around at the different files. They were shattered everywheres. I just brushed it off and walked through the office. Some of the keys to the rooms were still on the rack. I looked at the different room keys. Something caught my eye. One of the keys was still shiney. The rest had rusted down sitting there for years. But this one appeared brand new. My foot hit a small fridge under the rack. It slowly opened. The light had burnt out but there was still old food inside of it. It was started to get strange. It seems as if everyone in the building just up and left. 'Weird.....' I said to myself. I just shook my head and left out the back door of the small office taking the shiney key with me. I looked at the key in my palm. 'Why is it shiner then the rest? Hmmm...B24, why does that sound so familiar?' I looked toward the stairs of the apartment building. They seemed to be ready to fall apart. The metal was rusted and holes was formed in some areas. In fact there looked to be bullet holes in the stairs and surrounding walls. 'What happened here?' kept running through my head. I started to walk up the stairs but after a few steps I could tell they was no safe to use. I needed another way to get to the second floor. DING It echoed in the silent building. I looked around for the source of the sound. I turned the corner toward a hallway of rooms. There was a light shining from down the hallway. I walked toward the light. How is there light here yet the rest of the building is pitch black? Things didn't seem to make any sense around here. The light was coming from the elevator. I don't know why but I walked into the elevator and pressed for the second floor. It was like the light pulled me in. I can't explain it. But within seconds the elevator was moving upward. I was taken by a calmess. The doors shut in front of me and I could see my reflection in the metal. I looked down at the key in my hand. 'This all is so weird. Was I meant to find this key? Or this elevator? What is going on here?' the questions keep coming. I couldn't make heads or tails with it. Someone or something wanted me to be here. Someone or something wants me to find something. The question is what? I didn't have much time to think about that as the metal doors slowly opened. I put the key in my pocket as I stepped out carefully, merely sticking my head out. I looked around to make sure no one was around. When I finally felt safe I walked out toward the hall. The doors paint was worn off. They looked sorry and pathetic. Looking around at this sorry ass place it made me glad I lived in my current apartment. However the memory of there came back to me. The current apartment is becoming a place full of angry and mistrust. Bitter words fill the air choking the inhabitant of the apartment. It was all because of one man. 'Jackson Rust' I said. Just the thought of him made me anger. He is trying to ruin my relationship with Tessa. Always showing off for her. Always playing the nice guy for her. I know what he is really like though. She doesn't but I do. He claims he just wants to be friends. 'Friends? Ha that is a joke.' I laughed to myself. A smile actually shined on my face. I was getting a match to where I could actually go up against Jackson Rust. I could break him in half and show Tessa she doesn't need him around. Tuesday it would be the worst night for Jackson. Just wait and see. I finally noticed I was at the room B24. Something was different. Very different. This door was painted white. I touched the door with the tip of my finger tips. I looked at them. White paint. It was fresh. 'Ok this is starting to get really weird.' I wipes the paint off on my pants and tried to open the door. 'Locked.....' Suddenly I remembered I had the key to the room. I pulled it out of my pocket and held it up between two fingers. I stared at the key. Another memory flashed before my eyes. It was me. Only younger. Much younger. I am standing in front of the door looking at the key, holding it the same way I am now. I can feel weight on my back. Is it a booksack? I can't really tell. The door suddenly swings open. There is a large man in a black suit standing there. He looks down at me through his black shades that cover his eyes. He just walks right on by me as if nothing is wrong. I look inside the room and see red stuff on the walls. I can see someone laying there, nothing moving. Who is it? I know them. But I can't place them. Just then I can feel to large hands grab me from behind. They cover my mouth, I can't scream. My heart starts to race. I think to myself, 'What is going on here? Somethings wrong.' by I can't do anything. I'm slammed against someone's chest as they have an arm wraped around my chest keeping my arms tucked in at the sides. The other still covering my mouth. I can still a prick at my neck. It all goes blank....... DING I am snapped out of the dream. The key slips from between my fingers and hits the ground. I look out toward where the elevator is. The doors close and the light is cut out of the hall way. I softly touch my neck. I could actually feel the prick again. I quickly looked around to make sure no one was around. Indeed I was all alone. I rubbed my neck and picked up the key. I quickly unlocked the room and entered it. My heart sank as soon as I stepped into the room. Bullet holes left the walls looking like swiss cheese. The blood was still there. There was a chalk outline of the bodies. There was a picture where the face of one bodies is. A knife going through the picture holding it there. I pulled the knife out and picked up the picture. 'Mom....' no. I shook my head. It couldn't be. I don't know who my mother is. I don't even I ever met her before. But somewheres deep inside told me that the picture I was looking at was in fact my mother. I pulled out my wallet and put the picture away. I looked around the small apartment. It was just like the room I remembered from my flash backs. Yet unlike the rest of the apartment building, it was completely empty. I noticed something else in the apartment. Something in the window seal. It was a piece of paper. I walked over and picked it up. My hands shaking, my heart beating. I read the paper silently. 'Add one part hate, mix with one part fire, and you have the making of one apartment building flambe'' What could it mean? It didn't take long for me to figure it out. Under the piece of paper there was an arrow carved into the window seal. I turned my head toward where the arrow was pointed to see the area that would have been the kitchen. On the counter there was a clear brown bottle three quarters full of something. Next to it is a box of matches. I opened the box of matches to find one lone match left. I picked it up from the box and struck it. I could feel the small flame burning. I dropped the match inside the bottle and watched as it exploded. Flames leap everywheres. I jump back to avoid it, caught by surprise. I quickly dashed out of the room. The flames was building as it consumed anything and everything in it's path. I looked toward the elevator but I knew it wouldn't be safe. The stairs was my only choice. I know they aren't safe. But there is nothing else I can try. Already the flames are licking at the apartment door. The freshly added coat of paint on the door is melting away from the heat. I flung open the door to the stairs and looked down the stair well. Even though I was only on the second floor the stair well still went down a bit. Too far for me just to jump down. It also went down to the basement. I looked back over my shoulder. 'Shit!' the fire was now in the hallway. I took a step onto the stairs. I found already feel the metal underfoot start to give some. The bolt holding the stairs to the concrete wall was loose badly. I needed to get out of the apartment building and quick. I closed my eyes, said a prayer, and ran down the stairs as fast as I could. I could hear the concrete break and the metal fall. I shot my eyes open and saw I was near the door to the first floor. By luck I never closed the door when I opened it before and I was able to jump through the doorway just as I could hear the stairs falling and crashing down. I rolled in the hallway and jumped to my feet. Looking back over my shoulder I could see the smoke clearing. I said another Shinto prayer and starts to hurry out of the apartment building. I walked out to the street. Flames was bursting out from the windows now. The fire was starting to move up the different floors. The only thought on my mind was that I needed to get back to my apartment. The place I call my home.
