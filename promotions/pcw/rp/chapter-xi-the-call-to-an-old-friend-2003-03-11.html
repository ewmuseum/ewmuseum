Chapter XI: The Call To An Old Friend...

11 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-3-03 ::Introduction:: Now Mark is in Mexico thinking about how did Jax know he was down here. There are so many questions Mark needs answered since he has not seen Jax in forever. Jax is an old friend of Mark’s. He was actually one of the best friends that he has ever had in his life and since he entered PCW this past time he just grew away from him since he was busy with the wrestling life. He had other people to hang out with after the shows were over and Jax was moving all over the place. Mark didn’t know where to find him and the last time he saw him was when the two took a road trip down to Florida to catch the first NTICW pay per view. Jax was the person who introduced Mark into the Neo Nazi world. He taught him everything he knows and he taught him how to hate. Now he needs to know how to hate other people and this is the perfect chance to find out how. We continue back into the past of Mark Makonie as we find out how he became to hate the spics. ::End Introduction:: Chapter XI: The Call To An Old Friend… As Mark is sitting in the back leather seats of the taxi cab he keeps on staring down at the card that the security guard gave him. This is a surprise to Mark since he has not heard from Jax in over a couple of months. Now why would he just pay for all of Mark’s accommodations and how did he know he was going to Mexico? Mark just shakes his head as these questions are mind boggling. “I need to find out what Jax thinks about with my new beliefs because he could be the one to help me start this and help me out.” Mark says under his breath so that Pedro doesn’t hear him up in the front. “He led me into this lifestyle and he should have some pretty good ideas about this and what I should do.” Mark looks up at Pedro to make sure that he is not looking back at him so that he didn’t hear him. But Mark is sure that he now knows that Mark is trying to be a spic hater and that is what he feels right now as well. Mark puts the card down in his lap as he looks out of his window in the cab. He sees the normal setting for a Mexico town. There are palm trees and it looks like there is sand on the ground and not grass. There is nothing to be in sight, just the looks of a jungle as there are wild animals running around there. There isn’t a person in sight and Mark is just wanting to get to the nearest city. “Where is the nearest city from here?” Mark asks Pedro as he leans up in his seat. “Its about five miles from here.” Pedro says as Mark has a surprised look on his face. “I thought you only spoke that damn foreign language.” Mark says as he leans back in his seat. “Well thats what a lot of people think when they see us.” Pedro starts as he is driving along the paved road. “People automatically think that we can’t speak anything else besides Spanish so I like to surprise people.” “Well just drop me off at the city here so that I can make this phone call.” Mark says as he picks up the card and he studies the number on it. “Who is it that you are going to call?” Pedro asks as he looks at Mark in his rearview mirror. “Now that is none of your damn business since I hate all of you spics I can’t share anything with you.” Mark says as he looks out the window and he doesn’t say a word. Pedro drives up to the nearby city as it is a small village town from the looks of it. Mark looks out his window and he starts to see some spics carrying around fruits and other stuff in their baskets. They are not wearing what the Americans would wear. They have all of these flashy clothes on and they all look like they haven’t taken a bath in about a couple of years. Mark puts his hand over his nose as the taxi is starting to get a body odor smell in it. Mark sniffs his own self as he doesn’t smell anything off of him. He leans up towards the front of the car and he sniffs up there and it just stinks like total body odor. “For one reason I hate spics is because they smell like shit.” Mark says as he looks out the window just loud enough for his ears to hear the message. “You can let me off here.” Pedro just nods as he pulls up right next to the curb. Mark opens up his door as he takes a pen out of his front pocket. He writes down the number on the side of the taxi so that he can call them up later to get another ride. Pedro drives away as soon as Mark copies down the number and he goes off to do some other runs. Mark starts to walk down the street of the little village town looking for a phone. “This is another reason why I hate all spics.” Mark says as he looks up at the buildings and can’t read the writing. “I don’t fuckin understand their writing or their language at all.” Mark goes up to the glass door, but can’t even read the sign. He just enters into the café that it looks like and everyone stops what they are doing and they just stare at the white man. Mark walks along the wooden boards as they creak from being set there for so long. Every step you hear a squeak sound and another one after that. “What in the hell are you all looking at?” Mark asks as he stops in his tracks at the register. The people in the café just bust up laughing as they can’t understand the English language. Mark gets an even more evil look on his face as he looks over at the guy who is running the cash register. The guy has his hair spiked up with a tint of red in it. He has a whole bunch of zits around his face and Mark just looks like he is getting grossed out by the whole thing. “Can I use your phone please?” Mark asks the guy who is standing there looking like he is ready to take an order. “Yo no entiendo inglés.” The guy says as he leans his head back and he laughs. Mark looks over the counter and he looks for a phone. He sees that they have a cordless phone. Mark grabs the black phone as he runs to the back of the café through the white swinging doors. Mark takes the card out of his front pocket as he quickly dials the number on the phone pad. The phone starts to ring as the phone on the other end is picked up and the ring stops. “Jax?” Makonie asks as he is trying to be fast on the phone. “Who is this?” Jax says on the other line with a dark voice.
