Back In Black: Bring it on Boys and Walden

26 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Back In Black: Bring it on Boys and Walden Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 12/26/02 The opportunity to get his hand on a cackle of losers approaches this Fusion. The Malice Man still bruised and banged up from Decimation sits on the coach and yells at the TV. Williams: What do you make a commission off of being not funny you fat f*ck? “The Malice Man” yells at the unresponsive Drew Carry doing his stand up act on the TV. Williams: Everybody thinks their Don Rickles and George Carlin these days. These fucking young humps got as much talent as a retarded circus monkey on PCP getting fucked in the ass by the bearded lady in the concessions booth. Duke turns off the TV and turns his attention to the camera watching his every move. Williams: What is youth?? It’s rash decisions and emotional actions. You know the old story of the old man and his son sitting on top of a hill looking down at the sheep. The little kid tells his dad we should run down there and fuck a sheep. The father said no son we should walk down there and fuck them all. You see how that works kids? That story must take place in one of them sheep looking European countries but you get the idea. Duke reaches over for his sipping whiskey and takes a sip. Williams: Gaz Green the mean machine wants to play in the main event? Now this guy is truly funny….. Duke smiles. Williams: ….Funny looking and not funny HA HA but funny queer. Funny that he thinks he can play with Malice. No humor in Malice son just a lot of pain. Like a wart on a big black whore’s ass there is nothing funny about it. Like a Tyler Nelson aids test nobody is laughing. The smile fades from Duke’s face. Williams: You think this business is a joke. Kid I’m not in the mood for a jester I’m in the mood for an execution and that’s just what’s going to happen to you. Duke itch’s his nose with his thumb. Williams: Timmy Walden I’ve been wanting to slap the smug fucking smile off your face since the dawning of PCW. You little ass kisser why don’t you take all the sucking up you do and make a living off it. Move down to San Fran and kiss ass all day and make a grand doing it. You fairy fuck. Duke sips his booze. Williams: I don’t like you and never have. You’re a fucking leech living off the success of others. A motionless speck on the penis of life. You ain’t even crabs hoss. You’re just worthless kid and Friday I going break your fucking skull. Duke smiles a sick smile. Williams: Chris Matthews??? Isn’t he that fat fucking prick who hosts the news shows Sunday morning?? You know the dork who’s a Bills fan. Tubby prick. Anywho. Duke looks over the schedule to see who else is on the card. Williams: Tyler Valentine, and Joe Bananas what is this some kind of Porno movie? Where’s Jenna?? Kiddies I’m going to have to teach you how we do it in the main event…..Malice Style!! Duke smirks a Walden style smirk. Williams: Hey Kannon what happened? You feeling a little sore? Who’s your daddy??? I kicked your ass once and now I’ll kick your teams ass. Get ready to be sent whiskey bent and hell bound….hoss Duke turns the tv back on. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
