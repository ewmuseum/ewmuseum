A meeting with the boss of Sony Music

Date: not entered [Scene opens outside the headquarters of Sony Music in downtown New York City.The building itself is grand in size, with noticeable, black marble walls reaching right up to the roof. A blue sedan rental car pulls up outside the building and parks in the handicap spot.Out steps Flaymin Aymin.He gazes up at the majestic marble walls in awe] Flaymin: Ooooh, Shiny! [Suddenly the valet walks over to Flaymin and taps him on the shoulder] Valet: I’m sorry sir but you can’t park your car in a handicap spot. [Flaymin eyes the valet up and down before turning back to the wall] Flaymin: I like this wall.I’ve got this sudden urge to lick the shiny marble! Valet: Sir, you really need to move your car unless you’re handicapped. [Flaymin sighs and then walks back to the car, with the valet in tow. Flaymin opens the passenger seat car door.Quickly he turns around, grabs the valet by the head and slams him onto the car door causing a large cut on the Valet’s head. Flaymin then tosses the valet into the car and closes the door on him] Flaymin: There we go!Now we have someone handicapped to mind the car! And you don’t you get any damn blood on the dashboard. [The concussed valet just slumps back in the passenger seat as Flaymin makes his way into the building. Inside the building, Flaymin takes the elevator up to the 7th floor. A moment later he exits the elevator and makes his way over to the receptionist] Flaymin: Hi, I’ve got a meeting with Mr Motorola. [The receptionist frowns and then looks back at her schedule] Receptionist: You must mean Mr Tommy Motola? Flaymin: Yeah close enough. Receptionist: Ok, just head down to the last office on your right hand side. [Flaymin nods his head and then makes his way down the left hand side of the hallway. Having realised that he’s gone the wrong way, he stops and scratches his head in confusion] Flaymin: Left….. Right…… what the hell is the difference? [A sexy, young executive walks past Flaymin.He quickly catches up to her to ask for directions] Flaymin: Hello, little lady!I’m looking for Mr Motorola’s office. [The young woman smiles at Flaymin.She then points him in the right direction. 15 minutes, 5 wrong turns and 3 toilet breaks later, Flaymin finally arrives at the office of Sony Music boss, Tommy Motola. He straightens up his tie, checks his breath and then walks into the room where Michelle Branch and Tommy have been waiting for Flaymin] Tommy: What happened? Michelle: You’re late. Flaymin: You guys should start handing out map directions.I’ve been walking around in circles for the last 20 minutes. [Tommy shakes his head before instructing Flaymin to sit down at the round discussion table] Tommy: Well Flaymin, it appears that you really are quite serious about performing this duet with Michelle. Flaymin: Sure am. Tommy: I hope you realise that we here at Sony are taking a great risk teaming up a ticking timebomb with one of our brightest up and coming stars. Flaymin: Michelle is a timebomb? Michelle: I don’t think he was talking about me. Flaymin: Just face it honey, no one’s perfect. Tommy: Hey, hey, listen to me when I talk!You’ve got the attention span of a 3 year old! Flaymin: Sorry boss. Tommy: Now I have faith in Michelle that she’ll be able to put together a great song.Basically you’re just here for the publicity, so you might get a line or two and that’s about it. [Flaymin slams his fists on the table in disagreement] Flaymin: Michelle and I decided that we were going to write the song together! Michelle: I’m sure he’ll be able to carry the tune, too. [Tommy stares at Michelle with a look of bewilderment plastered across his face] Tommy: Darling, sweety pie, my dear, dear Michelle…. You can’t be serious!?!? [Michelle nods her head, thus causing a wide grin to instantly spread across Flaymin’s face] Flaymin: The woman believes in me!So why don’t you? Tommy: Lets face it; you’re hardly the musical talent Michelle is.Do you know what this could do to her reputation if you screw up? Flaymin: I’ve been a screw up all my life and just look where its gotten me! Tommy: An uneducated alcoholic with one testicle? Michelle: Guys, guys, settle down! The way I see it, this is a great opportunity to gain more exposure amongst the wrestling fans all over the world. Wrestlers dig my style of music, don’t they Aymin? [Flaymin pauses for a moment] Flaymin: Uh….I think I overheard a few of them a while back discussing ‘Branch’. Or was that ‘Ranch’? No wait, I think they were just talking about a rash. Tommy: I’m really starting to have my doubts about this duet. Michelle: Don’t worry about it.Flaymin has a mysterious quality about him, he just oozes sensuality and sexiness. Flaymin: I gather you’ve seen my porno! [An astounded Michelle begins to shriek] Michelle: You’re what!?!? Flaymin: Errr… jorno…alJournal.Yeah, journal. Michelle: You keep a journal?That’s so sweet!See Tommy, Flaymin is a new age man! [Tommy sighs before pulling a contract out of his briefcase and thrusting it on the table in front of Flaymin] Tommy: Alright, you’ve got your damn contract. Flaymin: Woohoo! Michelle: You won’t regret this, Tommy! Tommy: *Groan* [Scene fades to black]
