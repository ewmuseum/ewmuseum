Resurrecting the In Ring Promo #2

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/04/02 A still scene from the previous promo with the crowd chanting for the Chef is shown. Narrator: Welcome back to another hilariously stupefying Chef promo! When we last left the Chef, he was controlling the entire crowd with a mind control device implanted in all his quiche! Will anyone be able to stop his culinary insanity!?!? Gus the Video Guy: I think so, friend… Narrator: No one asked a pathetic low life like you Gus. For Allah’s sake, the Bushwhackers could do a better job of video editing than you! (Remember Wrestlemania 7?) Gus the Video Guy: (Sobbing) I’ll be in the car sippin’ some Moonshine… Narrator: (Angrily) Get outta here! Hmmmph, now that the retard is gone, back to the magic!!! The still scene does not play. Narrator: Um…little help Gus? Damn play button, where are you!?!? Ah ha, I am invincible!!! The scene begins to play. The Crowd: VIVA! VIVA! VIVA! VIVA! The Chef raises his hand in triumph, looking at the thousands of mindless zombies in the arena, converted to his side by a simple slice of quiche. Bertha: Wow, that f*cking quiche must be really @$$ humping good f*cko! The Chef: Well it does have other four different kinds of cheeses, hearty piece of smoked bacon, a mouth-watering egg scramble, and a delicious dash of shallot! But the real surprise is in the quiche! Bertha opens up her gym bag, and pulls out a piece of the leftover quiche. She breaks it in half and finds a robotic transmitter!!! Bertha: What the f*ck!?!? The Chef: Ha Ha, Praise be to Allah for mind control devices!!! Those little doodads latch onto the top of your throat and transmit an electromagnetic signal to your brain, telling the host to obey me!!! VIVA ME!!! Finally I will have a horde of mindless zombies at my disposal, doing my dirty work, ridding the world of the “Infidel” religions, and instituting Mohammed and Allah in the minds of every single person in this country! Praise be to Allah for deception!!! The Crowd: VIVA! VIVA! VIVA! VIVA! The camera fades to black, and fades into the next part of the Chef promo series… Narrator: Let’s get on with the show! “Why Can’t We Be Friends” by War plays over the loudspeakers as a familiar face makes his way out onto the stage. The Chef: Charles Bronson!?!? Bertha: What the f*cking f*ck!?!? Bronson holds up a microphone, and speaks to the crowd. Bronson: Hey boyo…times a tickin’, let’s get busy… The Crowd violently voices its protest to Mr. Bronson, the star of many ‘Death Wish’ movies. Bronson tries to calm them down. Bronson: Hush, people… The Crowd groans in agony with each word of this man’s horrible material. The Chef: Why are you here Bronson!?!? Bronson: I ain’t tellin’… The Chef: Have you come to stop my evil deeds? Bronson nods. The Chef: Um…what’s up? Bronson nods. Bertha: I think he can’t f*cking hear too f*cking well, a$$ gobbling mother f*cker! The Chef: Oh really… (In a lower voice) Oh Mr. Bronson, are you addicted to gay porn? Bronson: No, but your girlfriend is… The Crowd: WHOAAAAHHHHHHHH!!! Bertha: F*cking c*ck @$$ sh*tty as f*ck mother f*cker!!! Dave Kern: Nice comeback by Bronson! Jeff Marx: Shut up Kern! We aren’t supposed to be in this anymore!!! The Chef grits his teeth in anger, but then a light bulb goes off in over his head. The Chef: Well Mr. Bronson, how does it feel to be the in social gutter? Bronson begins to talk… The Chef: Hold on second buddy! I’ve got a surprise for you that you just might like!!! “Ring My Bell” by Blondie plays over the loudspeakers as a voluptuous young Islamic Militant Teenage Girl, probably fresh from the streets of Khandahar, wheels out a tray full off delicious culinary delicacies. The eyes of Charles Bronson light up as he sees a plate of Beef Wellington, Orange Duck, Cherries Jubilee, Chicken Cacciatore, Baklava, Fried Mushrooms, Grilled Mahi Mahi, and Quiche. Bronson licks his chops, ready to dive in. Bronson reaches out to grab the Grilled Fish, but the Chef shouts from his microphone… The Chef: Stop! Stop I say! You’re a “face” right? You’re a “good guy” Bronson nods, holds the microphone up and says… Bronson: Sure thing, pally… The crowd cringes with pain after hearing that reply. The Chef: And you’re quite skilled on the microphone too! NOT! Bertha: Get f*cking on with it skank @$$! The Chef admonishes Bertha’s cry and continues on to an imminent culinary catastrophe. The Chef: Since you are a good guy, why don’t you let the crowd decide for you which food to choose…hmmm? Bronson steps back and looks at the crowd. Crowd: QUICHE! QUICHE! QUICHE! QUICHE! QUICHE!!! Bronson nods his head and dives into the colorful combination of egg, cheese, bacon, and onion. He gorges on the quiche, and chomps ravenously at the eggy surface. The Islamic Militant babe asks him if he’d like a fork, but Bronson waves her away… The Chef: Yes, EAT! EAT! Become one of my mindless zombies!!! Bronson notices the small metallic object inserted in his quiche and drops the culinary creation. He turns to the Chef, and says… Bronson: No dice, pally… The Chef, angered by Bronson’s refusal, tells Bertha to finish him off. The Chef: Go ahead, sauté his @$$, I don’t care anymore! Bertha: It will be a f*cking pleasure! F*CKING KIIIIII-YAAAAA!!! Bertha leaps over the top rope and lands on the ground with such an earth shaking shock that minor quakes occur throughout the city limits. She charges up the aisle, and SPEARS BRONSON, who mistakenly turned his attention away from the Chef in order to scarf down some Beef Wellington. Bertha uses Bronson like a rag doll, first firing a barrage of lefts and rights at the horrible actor, and then lifting him up and spinning him around in the air. Bertha: Mother f*cking @$$ humping f*cking your mother f*cking father in the shower with a f*cking c*ck sucking piece of mother f*cking sh*tty @$$ f*ck face god d*mn ho @$$ mother f*cking f*ck @$$ skank b*tch sh*tty as f*ck fairy @$$ed queer salad tossing d*ck up the f*cking @$$ pieces of horse sh*t on my f*cking shoes mother f*cking f*ck up you c*ck @$$ed queer as f*ck sh*tty b*tch @$$ed f*cker!!! Bertha tosses Bronson into the crowd of mindless zombies, who jump on top of him and tear him to pieces like a bunch of crazed maenads. Bronson: Dis…ain’t…over…AAHHHHHHHH!!! The shouts of the crazed crowd drown out the screams of Bronson. Bertha: F*cking poetic justice b*tch!!! The Chef: That right there is a lesson to you all, do not question my culinary supremacy! Bertha nods affirmatively. The Chef: HAHAHA!!! I am changing the world…one quiche at a time!!! MUHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! Bertha: F*cking HA! F*cking HA! F*cking HA! F*cking HA! F*cking HA! F*cking HA! F*cking HA! Dave: This doesn’t make any sense!!! Why did Bronson even want to go up against the Chef!?!? Jeff: Praise Allah for such an informative and entertaining promo!!! All I’ve got to ask is…WHERE’S MY QUICHE!?!? The camera begins fades out as the mindless crowd destroys the arena. Dave: It’s Woodstock ’99 all over again!!! The camera fades to black…
