Gimmick Search 3: Sealing the Jobber Closet!!!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/27 (FF #3) The Scene reopens with Judas Steele leaning against a wall, talking to Pete. Pete is sitting infront of him, indian style hanging on his every word. Steele: ..I wasn't really dead though. They just wanted to make it look like I was. You know, stage makeup. Pete: Wow...I never would have guessed... Steele: Really, dude, it was a fantastic time, dude. For real, dude. Pete: Tell me more...tell me about beating Brock Newbludd! Steele: Well, dude... Voice: What are you talking about, Steele? Pete and Steele turn to see another man walking from the closet. He stands in front of them, and smiles. His long hair in his eyes, he removes his sunglasses and shrugs his shoulders. Pete: Wow... Steele: Dude...it's... Voice: Bobby Stormbringer! Pete: Wow...you're a legend around here! Everyone talks about you. B.S.: Well, that goes without Kannoning the Revenant. Steele: What? B.S.: Alliances!! I shot my AIMZKannon all over that Nelson-ing son of a Kano. Pete and Steele look at each other not knowing anything Bobby is talking about. Bobby starts to babble something about a wedding, and not really being there, when another person walks out of the closet. Man: Yo! I hears you's be lookin fo' a gimmick! I gots the best one eva, sucka! You gots to sleep wit J-Lo! because then, once you done bangin her, you can get that top shelf redneck poon, and pass that skank off to someone else! Pete: Mr Showtime?? Showtime: In the flesh. SUCKA! B.S.: YOU WILL NOT IGNORE ME! I AM THE NELSON OF THIS WALDEN! Judas Steele rears back and launches a fist that connects to the skull of Bobby. Bobby flinches backwards and looks at Steele. Tears well up in Bobby's eyes as he tries to stare Judas down. Out of no where, Mr. Showtime kicks Bobby in the face. He falls backwards, and cracks his head on the floor. Blood starts to ooze out of his head as Pete just shrugs and continues talking to Steele. Pete: So... Judas: For real. You think we should like, call an ambulance or something, dude? Pete: Nah, he'll be ok. Suddenly, a low rumble echoes from inside the closet. The three men turn and look inside. The rumbling gets louder and louder as men start pouring out of the closet. Steele: Oh, shit dude... Pete: What? Steele: JOBBER STAMPEDE!! Bad Battio, Big Gooey Sac, The Preacher, Stewart Hix, Cain Manson Crow, Avalanche, and more jobbers from PCW past pour out of the closet. A siren sounds as the Jobbers run around aimlessly. Suddenly, they line up in front of the door. Trumpeters exit the room, playing a regal harmony. A man dressed in fine clothing exits the room. Man: HEAR YE! HEAR YE! PRESENTING HIS LORDSHIP...Lord Crim Griffin Dagoth... The man makes his way out while all the other jobbers bow to him. Pete stands looking over the spectacle, not really knowing what to make of it. Dagoth walks up to Bobby Stormbringer who by now has lost 90% of the blood in his body. He waves a hand over Bobby's face, and instantly, Bobby begins to breathe again. Pete steps back, scared at what's going on. Dagoth turns and looks at Pete. Slowly he walks over to him DaGoth: Are you the one that freed us from our curse? Pete: I think so...why? Dagoth: Thank you. We've been in there far...far too long... Suddenly, another siren rings out. From down the hall way, about 50 guys dressed in riot gear rush the halls. The jobbers stand and scatter, looking for places to hide. A few run back into the closet. Stormbringer grabs Judas Steele and starts slapping him in the face. B.S.: YOU WILL NOT BAN ME! DO NOT BAN ME! ARGH! Judas: Stop it, Bitch! B.S.: DO NOT KANNON MY CLYDE WITH YOUR WALDEN! Judas: Leggo of me, damnit. The Jobber Squad reaches the scene and starts beating down jobbers. They push them back into the closet. Stormbringer and Steele escape, running down the hall slapping each other. Another wave of the Jobber Squad rushes the hallway. Stormbringer and Steele are pushed back into the closet after Bobby screams more about being Nelsoned... Finally the closet is sealed with a big strip of duct tape. The Jobber Squad high fives each other for averting yet another jobber crisis. Jobber Squad leader: Who caused this? Pete: I did, sorry...i looked into the light... JSL: Didn't you get briefed on the Jobber Closet? Pete: No, sir. I didn't. JSL: Never, ever look at the jobber closet! EVER! Do you understand? Pete: Yes, yes sir. Understood. JSL: looking around Who's blood is this? Pete: One of the jobbers... JSL: Well, since you caused this, you clean it up, you got me? Pete: Yes sir. I will sir. Right away sir. JSL: Carry on. Pete: Yes sir. Pete walks over to a janitor's closet and reaches in, grabbing a mop. General Tso walks over to the blood, and looks at it, frowning. the blood starts to move, and runs up Tso's leg. Tso starts to shake and convulse as the blood enters his own blood stream...He falls onto the ground, as Pete turns around and looks at the midget sitting where the blood used to be. Pete: Bad Tso! Don't lick up the blood! You don't know where that jobber's been! Tso: ...! Pete: Come on, let's get you cleaned up. We have gimmicks to try out still. Tso: ...? Pete and Tso start to walk down the hall to the bathroom. Tso shakes a little bit, and turns around, looking at the jobber closet. His eyes flash with a green light and he chuckles slightly. Pete doesn't seem to notice as he catches the scent of a Tuna on Rye. to be continued...?
