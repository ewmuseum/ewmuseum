I Will NOT Be Oppressed By Crunk.....

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/03/02 Asylum is Dead. Xavier Kannon killed Asylum. 2 short sentences and in the aftermath of the post-March Madness Turmoil both are ringing true in the ears of PCW fans around the world. It all began with the hot-headed Scientologist venting his frustration at being dumped from the PCW Title Battle Royal turned into a full on rant about the oppression he believes he undergoes due to his minority faith. It looked like the angry up-and-comer might have been put in his place when Asylum’s leader, The Krazed Lunatic Jeremy Howard came out to calm him down but the arrival of the Continental Champion and the reception he got only furthered Xavier’s resentment and after Krayzie told him to get over it the Gold Patron Meritorious snapped and sucker punched him. Howard put it down to frustration and left it but when Kannon went on to try and interfere in Miyagawa’s World Title match Jeremy would stand by no more and amidst a backdrop of mayhem with Asylum fighting Asylum and Deuces fighting Deuces the Krayzie One and the Arrogant Scientologist severed their friendship and went at it. With a new foe looming on the horizon Xavier finds himself in a situation well known to him. Hated by the fans for his arrogance, and feeling oppressed by the world for the lack of understanding his religion gets. With all this on his plate he also has to deal with the growth of another religion, the soon to be built Church of Crunk headed by none other than The Rush, Xavier’s ex-Tag Team partner, a man Kannon is not fond of for more than one reason.....Who is also one of his opponents, along with The End at Sunday Sabotage. “Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk!” We open up looking at the US Senate building against a cold Blue sky, the bright sunlight highlighting the spectacular White domed and pillared building. “Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk!” The camera moves down from the top of the building, profiling the domed mid-riff and following the pillars down to the front steps where a crowd of around 50 are protesting, being contained in a small pocket by Washington Police and waving various placards and signs that show their anger about the new Religion, called Crunk. “Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk!” The camera moves through the crowd showing the various face of those shouting, contorted with anger from the passion of their cause, waving signs saying Crunk Do A Bunk! and Crunk Is Junk!”. We continue the tour, looking at the signs and hearing the shouting voices up close until, at the back of the pack, we see the 2 masterminding the protest. Xavier : “This will teach the Rush for insulting me like he did....” PCW’s only Scientologist, Xavier Kannon, the man who killed Asylum is standing with his arms crossed, grinning in satisfaction with smugness exuding from his bright Green eyes. The Gold Patron Meritorious is wearing a long, Brown leather coat that stops just a few inches above the White steps. Under it her has a black silk shirt, left open to show a Black round neck t-shirt with Asylum RIP written across the chest in Purple, a sarcastic message to those he shared the stable name with since Shang-Haied 2. With this he has a pair of his signature Black boot-cut jeans with Green stitching and intricate Purple embroidery depicting Scientological runes snaking down the left leg to where a pair of Black, Grey and Purple Skechers adorn his feet. Xavier : “First of all he killed the Greatest Team Alive in favour of....I will try to say this without laughing....Black Attack....” The mere mention of the name makes Xavi look like he wants to vomit, screwing his face up as if sourness itself was taking up residence in his mouth. Xavier : “Well Rush, I have killed far great things than our team.....I ended Asylum, I ripped it out of PCW’s history, I aborted it......I revealed it to be little more than Jeremy Howard’s ego boosting machine and the single most oppressive stable in PCW history!” Standing beside him, nodding her head with the same arrogant smirk that Xavier has is his valet Eleanor Hall. Ellie~! is wearing a Brown suede jacket and matching suede skirt with a hem line just above he knee. Under the jacket she wears a light Pink t-shirt with Snapple~! written across her 32B chest in white. She sneers in satisfaction at the arrogant Scientologist’s words. Eleanor : “In......PCW.......History!” Kannon nods in agreement with his valet’s words, looking at the rapidly swelling crown in front of him, carrying out his wish in sabotaging his former partner’s religion. Xavier : “That is exactly what it is.....History...All it’s former members like Ed, it’s current, well, most recent members.....Howard the Oppressor and Miyagawa.....History. Asylum wronged me, it oppressed my beliefs and it tried to silence me....But it was I who did the silencing.....I silenced it forever.” As he runs through his acts of Wednesday night his eyes glimmer with pride at what he had done, driven in a fit of anger and jealousy at being thrown out after three minutes and eight seconds. Xavier : “Now with Asylum put out of it’s misery, something else needs to be silenced.....I’m sorry I have to do this Mason, but there is only room for 1 dominant religion in PCW, the same goes for soda and they are Scientology and Snapple~!.....And I have both of them...” As the 1st couple of Scientology look upon their handiwork as the anti-Crunk protesters clash with the Police as more and more supporters join them to fight for their cause. Eleanor : “Xavier, oh Scientological Slayer of PCW Stable lore....I believe it is time....” The ex-Cruiserweight Champion nods, still grinning wickedly. Xavier : “It is that time, we have them reeling.....So let me finish them off just like I did the sorry oppressive remnants of Asylum......FETCH ME MY SOAPBOX!” Hearing the rallying call from their leader, Gold Patron Meritorious Kannon, the protesters down their placards and back away from Police barrier between them and the Senate. 2 of the Scientologists carry over a crate for Kannon to stand on and he steps up, as to tower about a foot and a half above the heads of his fellow Dianetics followers, Ellie~! standing down to his left, looking up at her man. Xavier : “Fellow Scientologists! I feel your oppression! I feel your frustration....But unlike I had to against the oppressive backdrop of Asylum you no longer have to keep it all in....Imprison it deep in your thoughts burrowing away at you in your sleep....You HAVE a voice and that voice IS ME!” His charismatic words have already charmed the Scientologists who follow his words and start their chanting again, becoming rhythmic and eerie. “Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk!” Xavier : “Why should YOU, clean living Scientologists who never do harm upon to others have to suffer yet more prejudice by having a Church of Crunk build within mugging distance of YOUR Scientology Church!” “Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk!” The Police seem to be more worried now the crowd are standing back and adhering to the words of wrestling’s only Scientologist and his speech that they can sympathise with. Xavier : “I understand your sense of isolation my fellow followers, we are forgotten about, cast aside....Just like L Ron Hubbard himself was laughed at when he invented the world’s fastest growing religion....They may mock us, they may give permission for a Church of Crunk ON YOUR DOORSTEP...But we will protest and our millions of followers will protest and in the end they will learn the one rule they ever need to know....DON’T OPPRESS M-...I mean, US!” “Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk!” The Scientologists are now facing away from the Senate building, their signs lowered as they look up to one of their faith’s symbols, not that far detached from the sermon on the mount. He reaches down and takes Ellie’s hand, helping her up beside him as she sneers in agreement with the man who ended Asylum. Eleanor : “Down..........With........Crunk!” Her repeated words erupt their own cheer causing Ellie~! to smile and look smug, giving herself a clap. Eleanor : “Yay me!” Xavier : “Just look who is the head of the Church of Crunk! Is he a moral man like me who speaks out for what he believes in? No! Is he a highly respected member of the community like me? No! He is a womaniser, he is a blasphemer...In that he shows Hubbard a total lack of respect, and on Sunday he will....I’m not even sure if I should say this, he will h-....No...I don’t think I can...” A hush descends upon the gathered listeners, waiting with anticipation. On the box Xavier rubs his eyes, feigning caring as well as he can, trying build up an illusion of caring as Ellie~! prods him, encouraging him to carry on. Xavier : “On Sunday he will.....He will beat up a BLIND MAN!” A gasp of shock is heard all around as an arrogant smirk begins to appear in the right corner of his mouth. Xavier : “You heard me right Scientologists, Police, and yes, I see you watching there Senator...He will HIT A BLIND MAN! A man who cannot see, who makes his way around using his other senses, and this so-called religious figurehead is going to knock him down and stomp any small sense of sight he may have left out of him....” Suddenly he spies something out of the corner of his eye and whispers to Ellie~! Xavier : “Go kick that blind person there or something....” Eleanor : “Only if you insist....” She smirks as she drops down and walks through the crowd to where a bind man and his seeing eye dog. She lies in wait then stretches out a leg and trips the man, dropping him so he lands on hi elbows and knees before picking up a stick and throwing it down the road so that his dog gives chase. The crowd seem shocked and sickened by the reactions of Xavier’s valet. Xavier : “Was that not wrong? I SAID WAS THAT NOT WRONG?” The assembled Scientologists mumble in agreement. Xavier : “I agree that it was TOTALLY wrong! That is what you are condoning if you allow the Church of Crunk to expand....No blind man would be safe, no other minority religion will be safe in their faith....THINK OF THE CHILDREN WOULD YOU? Is that what you want to see on every street corner? I certainly wouldn’t.” Eleanor begins to make her way back to the Gold Patron Meritorious who is continuing with his reasons for Senate action against Crunk. Xavier : “You see......Rush Mason will tell you all sorts of things Mr Senate Men, he will say you are a team and that you are tight. He will claim that he accepts your beliefs and would not do anything to insult your faith.....He will tell you anything but it won’t be true! The Church of Crunk is a only I existence to take over the World.....If you do not act now then you will destroy the World just as I killed Asylum, only the World is just worth saving!” Ellie~! hops up onto the box again as the crowd roar along to Xavier’s words, now touching the 250 mark at least. Eleanor : “Just.........Worth........Saving!” Xavier : “You Senators have a chance to save the world, you can nip this in the bud and safeguard the future of the Nation’s youth....But if you stand by and do nothing then the blood of Scientologists throughout the USA will be on your hands....Appeasing them is NOT the way to go, do not stand back and do nothing....” “Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk!” The arrogant Scientologist waits patiently for the chant to die down. Xavier : “.....So Senators, Governors.....GEORGE W BUSH if you have quite finished dealing with some Afghan obsessed chef....Join me, join Scientology and join the whole of America in the battle against Crunk or with Hubbard as my witness our country will be lost....” Eleanor : “Will.......Be......Lost!” The 1st couple of Scientology step down and exchange arrogant smiles, satisfied that their words have fuelled the fires of the crowd who have now turned back and are battling with the Police guard at the foot of the Senate’s steps. “Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk! Down with Crunk!” Xavier : “This is looking to be a very good day...” He folds his arms and watches the chaos unfold before him, Scientologists clashing with the riot shields as the City’s media begin to show up just as a follower staggers out with a bleeding cut on his head. Beside him Ellie~! stands smirking with her hands on her hips. Eleanor : “Very........Good........Day!” The shot then starts to fade out as we zoom up close on Xavier’s face, strands of Auburn hair being blown across his face by the breeze and his piercing Green eyes sparking with jubilation as we hit Black.
