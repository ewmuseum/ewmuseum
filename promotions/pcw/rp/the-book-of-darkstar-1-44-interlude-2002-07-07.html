The Book of DarkStar: 1-44 (Interlude)

7 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-7-02 The Book of DarkStar 1-44 1. In the beginning, there was darkness 2. and from the darkness of nothing there was bred hatred. 3. Hatred which stemmed from the continuing opression towards the Dark Knights. 4. One day the hatred doth boiled over to the mortal coil. 5. From the darkness was born the end alls. 6. The pedophilliac murderer was bred from the fire, 7. His lover was bred from pure evil, 8. they were called by the masses of the names Clyde and Bethany. 9. The Masses did continue to oppress and offend,10. and they all dreamed.11. the dreams of the Masses continued to become reality in their own sad lives.12. Then was born the Lord of Dreams,13. The DarkStar.14. As his queen he did take the link between the spiritual world and 'reality'.15. Thus was Talena.16. The four reigned over the mortal coil supreme,17. They drank the blood of the innocent from the skulls of the rightous.18. and they struck fear into the hearts of the good and kind.19. They found followers, many left for other rights but many stayed,20. The End-Alls and the Followers became known by a different name.21. The Dark Age.22. The Dark Age then proceeded to fall to the Masses,23. the blood was spilt and the good were slaughtered.24. One day however it was all ended.25. The Fire Knight and the Dream Lord were cast away from the Mortal Coil, they were recieved back into their creations, into the fire and into the world where only dreams and nightmares were in true existence.26. They were cast aside by the Hoss, the Cowboy, the man known to the Coil as Duke.27. Their revenge was plotted, but in the time they were forced to serve their sentance.28. They were forced to sit idly by and witness the rising of the False God.29. To the heathens of the Coil he was named Big Alan Steele.30. But they still plotted, and revenge was to be brought to those of the Coil.31. The King then spoke to the Fire Knight and the Dream Lord.32. The King looked forward into the darkness and yelled high from the mountain tops and low to the valleys, 'Come forward, bring thou hell and bring thou sorrow.'33. Forward from the darkness stepped the End-Alls. The New Saviors.34. On the first friday of the seventh month of the two thousandth and second year of the Roman calender the Dark Age was resurrected.35. The Dark Age was free.36. The Mortal Coil was overrun and defeated as the blood of the Cowboy was spilt onto the hallowed ground.37. The Mortal Coil had fallen to the End-Alls, the Dark Age.38. But the False Messiah stepped forward for the Light Hats and called his name to the heavens, he spoke in blasphemous tones about his comparissons to the Non-Christ.39. Tom Lexian stepped forward from the blinding light.40. His blood was also spilt on the hallowed ground, but he stood up, his clothes had not been soiled and his will had not been broken.41. The show down was then set for the Fire Knight, the Dream Lord, the Cowboy, and the False Messiah.42. The Dream Lord did not take kindly to this as he set foot back into his realm to create the end.43. To the one that will be called the False Messiah he spoke, 'A wise man did once say, 'Light and Dark, in both Extremes, is impossible to see through', to you I look and see nothing'44. And thus it began
