Title Time

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/24/02 Scene opens in the parking lot outside the Saddledome. Warden Riley, his security staff, and 'Swamp Dawg' Karl LeBlanc arrive for tonight's Triple Threat match with Hank and Jason Core for the PCW Extreme Title. Several reporters have gathered. Warden Riley: Alright, open up the back of the PaddyWagon and bring LeBlanc around here. Reporter #1: Mr. Riley, you all have a huge match tonight... any predictions? Warden: Yep, Hank and Core will get hurt tonight and LeBlanc will be crowned new Extreme Champ. Reporter #2: I dunno, you seem awfully confident. LeBlanc's PCW record isn't exactly untarnished... Warden: Hell's fire, are you talking about those two losses? Did you see those two losses? Reporter #2: Well... ummm... actually, no I didn't see them. Warden: Well, let the Warden enlighten you. The first loss... LeBlanc beat the living hell out of Hank the entire match. The kid was inches away from death when LeBlanc made one misstep, ran head first into a mirror, and knocked himself out. Hank just happened to fall on top of LeBlanc and get the pin. So you see, I don't count that as a loss, just a learning experience. Hank never beat LeBlanc, LeBlanc beat himself. Now, onto the second loss... that was the epitome of bullshit. LeBlanc was about to break Mike Storm's neck when some dumbass named Newbludd interferes and cracks Storm over the head. Next thing I know, the bell rings, and the damn ref disqualifies LeBlanc of all things! Can you believe that... why should that be counted as a loss? Reporter #1: Because that's the rules of professional wrestling... Warden: Well, piss on those rules. LeBlanc hasn't really lost a match yet. He has dominated every match his had so far. Tonight will be no different. Reporter #3: Talk about Hank and Jason Core. Warden: A never-has-been and a never-will-be... Hank has always been a worthless jobber that just happened to get lucky and win a damn title. A title will no longer be around his waist at the end of this night. He's tainting the Extreme Championship. It was fun for awhile to see some loser like Hank strike gold, but enough is enough. LeBlanc will redefine that division in the months to come. Hank better print out a copy of the PCW website's main page for his scrapbook now, because after tonight it will be LeBlanc's picture atop pcw.ewmuseum.com. Reporter #3: ...and Jason Core? Warden: Jason Core is not a smart man. He made the grave mistake of attacking LeBlanc after his match with Mike Storm two weeks ago at Turmoil. This kid's a new comer and the first fight he decides to pick is with LeBlanc. He'll learn real soon that Swamp Dawg is the last sonofabitch on this planet that you want to piss off. He awakened a sleeping giant and LeBlanc told me last night that his goal is to end Jason Core's career. He might do it tonight, or he might wait until the PPV. Time will tell. Reporter #4: Can we talk to LeBlanc? Just a few questions... Warden (smirks): You're just about as dumb as Jason Core. Security Chief: Here is Warden, we're ready. Let's take him inside the arena. LeBlanc emerges from behind the PaddyWagon. The crowd of reporter quickly steps backwards distancing themselves from the big, black monster. The stumble over each other trying to get out of the way. LeBlanc approaches the group of reporters and violently shakes his shackled hands in their face. The media moguls scatter frantically. Warden Riley chuckles as he grabs LeBlanc by his arm and leads him into the SaddleDome.
