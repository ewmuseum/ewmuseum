Tijuana sucks!

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/28/02 “Hurry up and toss him in the back!” “How ya wanna do this shit, Ty? We gotta sit in tha back, too!” “Damn! You’re right. Throw him in the trunk.” “Heh heh…hell yeah, nigga!” A thud is heard followed by the slamming of what is apparently a car trunk. After a few seconds there is another slam of a door and the sound of a vehicle speeding off into the distance. We see none of it and know not what is going on….not just yet at least. The picture finally opens up in Tijuana, Mexico….what a shithole. We aren’t in the tourist area where it’s halfway clean for the Americans to come and blow all their money on hookers and drugs. We find ourselves in the barrio of Tijuana….the ghetto. The streets are filled with riff-raff and trash, both human and inanimate. I guess that’s to be expected from an economically medieval country. The homes are seemingly put together with band-aids and crazy glue, collages of sheet metal and 2x4’s. The two and three story apartments run together in a continuous run-on sentence of poverty. Most of them don’t have glass in the windows, allowing the ‘curtains’ to blow freely in the stench filled air. Women gather in groups on the sidewalk and mash corn into dust to make tortillas, allowing their dozens of bratty children to roam the streets at will with little more than rags to serve as clothes. The streets themselves are composed of cobblestones and literally whatever else could be found….even some animal skulls are thrown in the mix. Truly disgusting. Only a place such as this could produce something as vile and ridiculous as the White Mexican. Only the slums of a third world nation on the brink of the stone age could export someone so pathetic and worthless as Mexicano Moe Momar. Only a situation as grave and important as the one facing Tyler Nelson could bring a man of such class and dignity to this glorified Mexican trailer park. Only the sounds of the children screaming like wild animals and the women talking like hens can be heard until there is something off in the distance. The sound of tires navigating the cobblestone and skull streets of the barrio begins to drown out the commotion that had been going on. Soon a black Jeep Liberty Limited comes around the corner at the end of the street. It’s windows are tinted nearly as black as the paint on the vehicle itself. It slowly purrs along, followed by a plain, black Chevy van. All of the local activity comes to a halt as the miracles of American know-how and monetary might come rolling through the neighborhood. The children begin to follow the vehicles as the women chatter and point at them. The Jeep crawls to a stop and the van does the same behind it. There is no movement for several minutes from either vehicle. The children huddle around the vehicles in awe, trying to peer through the tinted windows to see just who is inside. Suddenly the back doors to the van shoot open and a crazed black man comes leaping out from inside. He releases a primal scream which scares the shit out of the kids surrounding the vehicles. The maniacal black man looks at the children as if he hasn’t eaten in several days and just found his lunch. The driver’s door flings open and out pops a frantic, professional looking white man. “LeBlanc!! Don’t eat the children!! You’ll spoil your dinner!!” shouts the Warden Jack Riley. ‘The Swamp Dawg’ Karl LeBlanc looks over at the warden with a glint in his eyes. A mammoth dog collar is fastened around the thick neck of LeBlanc and has two chains dangling from it on either side. The ends of the chains appear to be broken off. The warden calmly walks toward LeBlanc as the children scatter and seek shelter from the mad man. “Take it easy, Karl. You’ll get your hands on that no good Jason Core this Friday and you can take out your frustrations on him then,” says Riley in as soothing a voice as possible. “Just stay right there.” The warden continues to walk up to LeBlanc with his hands cautiously outstretches in front of him. LeBlanc just stares at Riley and seems to calm somewhat, allowing the warden to grab a hold of the chains and fasten them to the back of the van. No sooner does he get that done then the doors to the Jeep Liberty swing open and a pair of men hop out from either side. On the passenger side is another humongous black man, standing 6’10” and weighing 350 pounds. He wears a pair of black leather pants, tan Timberland boots, and a black leather coat with no shirt on underneath. His thick platinum chain swings on his neck as he struts around to the back of the Liberty. His name is ‘OG’ Orpheus Grant. “Damn, Ty….this muthafuckin place is worse than any place I ever been to. And I been to some real shitty fuckin places,” says OG shaking his head in disbelief. “Hell, I’d rather be back in tha pen than live in this muthafucka.” The man Orpheus is talking to is the man who climbed out of the driver seat of the Liberty. Tyler Nelson, once one of the richest men on the planet Earth, steps to the back of the SUV wearing a stylish black suit with polished black shoes. He adjusts the cuffs on his suit coat as he speaks. “What else did you expect from Mexi?” questions Tyler in disgust. “The guy is a total loser and now I know why. Well, this place has something to do with it but the biggest reason is because he’s retarded I think.” OG chuckles at the former CEO of PCW’s joke. Tyler glances to the back of the van that was following them and calls out to warden Riley. “Everything alright back there?” “Yeah,” shouts the warden. “I have LeBlanc secured. No harm done.” Tyler mutters to himself. “He could only do good in this hell hole.” Tyler and OG converge at the back of the Liberty and OG opens the rear cargo door. As he does so we see a smirk come across the face of the greediest player in the game. The camera pans around to get a view of the inside of the vehicle. There appears to be a body lying in the back. “Did you get his wallet?” asks Tyler of OG. Grant looks back at Tyler with a shocked look. “Did I get his wallet? Does a ho turn tricks?” Before Tyler can answer the rhetorical question, OG answers for him. “Hell yeah I got the damn wallet! This stupid muthafucka had over three hundred dollars in it, too.” “Hmmm….must have cashed his paycheck for the month right before he went into the hospital,” wonders Tyler. “Damn, he makes a lot more than I do. Bastard.” OG digs through his pants pockets until he finds the wallet he lifted from the person stuffed in the back of the Liberty. He hands it over to Nelson who starts to thumb through it. He pulls several cards and other items from the various pockets and compartments inside the wallet, looking them over and then tossing them to the ground if they are of no use to him. Finally he comes across one he keeps, dropping the remainder of the wallet to the ground with the rest of the garbage. He looks at the card, then up at the street sign. “How about that….we’re here,” announces Tyler. “Get him out of the back and let’s go!” OG reaches into the back of the vehicle and works his huge arms around the body that is lying motionless. He scoops the dead weight up in his arms and pivots so that we can see for the first time that it is none other than Mexi himself! The White Mexican is still unconscious and comatose from his unknown illness that had him hospitalized. OG shifts Mexi and then slings him over his shoulder like a sack of potatoes. Tyler motions to warden Riley, who undoes LeBlanc from the back of the van and guides him toward the front of the vehicle. The four men walk into the building that they parked in front of and disappear inside. The camera fades out. Fading back in we find the four men we just left in a stairwell inside the apartment building. LeBlanc leads the way, followed by warden Riley who keeps a tight reign on the chains that dangle from ‘The Swamp Dawg’s’ collar. Next is Tyler, followed by Orpheus Grant with Mexi still slung over his shoulder. “What the hell is that smell?” asks the former CEO with a contorted face. “It’s like something died in here five years ago.” Warden Riley pipes up from up ahead. “It might be all the dead mice and rats that are lining this stairwell. Looks like they haven’t cleaned this place in years.” “Typical,” mutters Tyler. “And why in the hell is there no elevator in this giant shack? Hasn’t this country entered into the mechanical age yet?” The foursome trudges up the stairwell until they finally reach the third floor. They pass through the fire door, which is actually just a piece of plywood, and into a corridor. “It’s down there,” says OG as he points to the end of the corridor. Tyler just shudders from the idea of him being in such a place. The hallway is dimly lit and littered with trash much like the street was. Many of the apartments have their doors wide open, revealing the meager living conditions inside. Furniture is sparse in each apartment, with many cardboard furnishings throughout. LeBlanc snarls as he heads up the expedition, seething with every breath he takes. It’s all that warden Riley can do to keep ‘The Swamp Dawg’ under control. “Calm down, LeBlanc!” pleads the warden. “I can’t have you go and get yourself thrown in one of them Mexican prisons while we’re down here. Then you couldn’t go back and kill Jason Core on Friday.” LeBlanc whips his head around at the mention of the Extreme Champions name. A rage fills his eyes as he snorts in contempt for his upcoming opponent. He then turns and continues down the hall. The four men pass one of the open apartments and suddenly some music comes from within. The latin classic “Lowrider” by War starts to thump from an old school record player that sits atop an empty milk crate. Nelson turns up his nose in disgust. “What in God’s name is that infernal noise?!” As the music continues to pulse, a surprised “What tha fuck?!” is heard from OG. The other three men spin around to see Orpheus dump Mexi from his shoulder to the floor with a look of shock on his face. “That muthafucka is awake!” Tyler scoffs at his bodyguard’s proclamation. “You’re kidding…..right?” Grant doesn’t get the chance to answer as all of a sudden Mexi starts to writhe and twitch on the floor. Nelson’s eyes grow wide as Mexi starts to get to his knees. “Well don’t just stand there! KICK HIS ASS!!” screams Nelson. Instantly Orpheus springs forward and lands a solid boot to the side of Mexi’s head. The shot drops the White Mexican to the floor and brings a confident smirk to the face of tha OG. As he smiles, his single gold tooth catches a sliver of light and glints. “That’s what I’m talkin ‘bout, nigga.” Nelson starts to smile as well, but it is quickly erased as Mexi starts to move again and begins to rise. The music keeps beating and Mexi seems to be moving to the rhythm. “LeBlanc!” On command ‘The Swamp Dawg’ lunges forward and tackles the newly animated Mexi to the ground. LeBlanc starts to rain down with pummeling right and left hands. “Get him!! GET THAT SON OF A BITCH!!” screams Nelson at the top of his lungs. LeBlanc wears himself out attacking Mexi, then stands over him panting and out of breath. The former inmate leers down at the beaten and bloody White Mexican, a look of satisfaction on his tired face. Behind him, Nelson applauds the effort. “Brilliant!! Simply brilliant!!” cheers the former CEO of PCW. Mexi lays still on the ground as the music continues to play. LeBlanc turns his back to Mexi and starts back down the hallway. Nelson slaps him on the back as he passes, garnering a glare and a snarl from the huge black man. Tyler follows LeBlanc down the hall for a bit, then turns back around. “What the FUCK?!” exclaims Tyler as he sees Mexi slowly rising to his feet again, seemingly oblivious to the beating he just received. Nelson grits his teeth and lets loose with a ferocious growl. “AAARRRRRRGGGHHH!!” Tyler charges at Mexi, aiming to take his head off. He dives at the upright White Mexican with fire in his eyes. Suddenly the music stops. Mexi abruptly falls to the ground and Nelson goes soaring over him and into the apartment. OG cringes and covers his eyes as the sound of Tyler crashing into the record player is heard, followed by an “Ugh.” “Ya okay in there, Ty?” asks Grant. “What the hell happened? I had him dead in my sights,” wonders Tyler. “He just collapsed.” The warden walks up and taps Mexi lightly with his foot, making sure he is unconscious again. “As soon as that music stopped, he fell like a ton of bricks. Damndest thing I’ve ever seen.” After a few seconds Tyler comes walking out of the apartment, dusting his suit off. He looks down at Mexi and frowns, then gives him a swift kick to the ribs. There is no movement from the White Mexican. “This place sucks. Let’s get back over the border before some other bullshit happens. I can’t take any risks before Fusion when I totally dismantle this piece of shit for what he’s done to me. If he thinks being in a coma is bad, then he hasn’t seen anything yet.” Nelson motions to Orpheus to pick up Mexi, which he does cautiously. LeBlanc follows them with warden Riley behind him. They walk back into the stairwell and the plywood door closes behind them. We are almost ready to fade to black when mysteriously more music starts to emanate from inside the apartment. This time the tune is “Pretty Fly – Tequila Mix”. As we do finally fade out we hear yelling from inside the stairwell. “There he goes again!! Get him!!” With that, we fade out.
