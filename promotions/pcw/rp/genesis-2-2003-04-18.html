Genesis [2]

18 April 2003

Date: April 18th, 2003 Ah, now where was I? Back to sipping coffee, drinking no doze as you carried a gym bag over your shoulder, interested in looking towards a time slot inside some federation with more backwater xealots than a Donald Goines novel. Still, I saw the PWA as a stepping stone, a preak that could only be a belmont in the end. I was quickly impressing people left and right. To them, nobody had heard of me. I was just a plain out of the box black kid from New York City given a chance to wrestle thanks to a mob boss trying to kill me. It was a instant fresh idea, until somebody named Deville toyed with the thought of it after playing Grand Theft Auto about seventy five times too many. It sucked that your original gimmick had to be sub-sided with your other one, that persona of 'rapper extroadanaire' but, it was still cool to meet the likes of Jordan Crazy, Hessian, and the likes. MTV even let me have a Making The Band contest, in which I ran into this one white girl, who ended up having me run smack dab into Cordozer Stevenson. He's in PURE, or the SWF right now, and has a shot at almost every single major title you can ever think of in the federation. Alas, the PWA had its ups and downs. First off the bat, you get a Italian guy, who indeed had skills and always kept to towards himself to never be such a mob figure, just a pasty eating fat guy. He had no mic work, his best attempt at a impact move was a suplex. And thus, I dusted him off in a Bret Hart type of fashion. Five Moves of Doom. Of course, one win isn't enough so I continued plunging on, being almost like Hessian's top face inside of his branch from the promotion or academy as they keep wanting to tell us. I was soon set to with the Hellbound title, a gimmicky name for such a imposing figure as the Gothic, but it was good purpose. Hessian was a good friend, almost like Glen Jacobs only with a more serious character and could work his ass off in the ring. I think he went to NTICW, beat up a religious guru named The Josh, and went to PCW during that point. Of course, he could just tell you what happened and thus kill you for just telling you his real name. Hellbound title all set for the taking though, until a suspecting and watchful Sean Williams plucked me from there and ushered me to WWCF, an upstart federation which tried its best to either mimic its sister the XWA or try and be like the NTICW years of Jacob Yoder, which in essence were beating out the competition of PCW by like .1 in the ratings overview. The WWCF then changed its name to Rising Star Wrestling, or RSW for short. It didn't last long, as a couple of shows later I was shipped to the XWA in such a move even I had more heat than the two men in the main feud. Trigger and Deadhead. One's now the president of some federation and the other turned out to be Dorn Tracer - Shooting Star title holder. And the most active guy I've seen since a young Playaz Inc. But, once the XWA closed, it was all onto Rush from there. And maybe the longest piece of longevity I've probably ever had.
