Baby's Got A Temper

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6 - 04 - 03 “IT’S ALL YOUR FAULT, YOU DUMB SHIT!!!” The roaring can be heard for miles around, causing car alarms to go off and birds to scatter in fright as the seismic-like sound waves course through the air. Its source isn’t an earthquake, a talking elephant, or Roseanne Barr… it’s Dorn Tracer, hot off his DOUBLE loss only an hour ago. Having first been pinned by Gaz Green, then his estranged wife Tessa Windsor, the Astronomer is absolutely LIVID! His hair is strewn about and looks like it’s been run through the ceiling fan. His face is bruised, battered, and his teeth are gnashed in an absolute fit of rage. “I HATE YOU BOTH!!” The target of his temper tantrum… or targets, rather… are his tag team partners: Dante Jackson and The Pharaoh. Pharaoh just folds his arms across his chest and shakes his head while Jackson just rolls his eyes. “Chill out, Dorn.” “Yes, my friend. Calm yourself!” Pharaoh snaps, a little edgy as his partner goes absolutely berserk. However, calming down just isn’t in the cards for Tracer. “CALM DOWN?! Look what happened! I not only got pinned by ONE Green, I got pinned by TWO Greens! And the latter cost me my title!!” “I don’t have time for this. I got other shit to deal with,” Dante snorts before turning away and stomping off in the opposite direction, only fueling Dorn’s rage. Pharaoh watches Dante walk off before turning back to his partner and giving him an irritated look that lacks all sympathy. “I was NOT the one that got pinned, Dorn. You lost it for yourself.” The Astronomer, now TV Title-less, rears his foot back and boots an equipment crate off its pile, sending it crashing to the ground and nearly breaking his toes in the process. “You have to be shitting me, Titu! You’re my partner! You’re supposed to look after me! You should have nailed that bitch with a chair and knocked her off the turnbuckle!” The Astronomer takes a wad of hair in both hands and tugs violently, unable to control his spiraling fury. Pharaoh just sort of looks away, possibly EMBARRESSED by this debacle. “Dorn…” He begins, his voice saturated with annoyance, “You are like a child. Take responsibility for your actions. YOU lost, so YOU are to blame. Besides, it is merely the TV Title… I held it once. You’ve got other things to worry about.” However, Dorn isn’t exactly listening to his older, wiser partner. His eyes dart all about as his breathing becomes heavy and labored with undeniable rage. He throws his arms down to his sides and tries to compose himself, but it really isn’t working. “Like…WHAT?” Pharaoh smirks, “Brianna Washington. Forget the gold. You’re got a much bigger problem, and she’s a problem for us all, since she’s got a grudge against the entire Triumvirate. The TV Title will return someday, worry not.” Not really in an emotional state to listen to reason, Dorn rips his gaze from the ground and stares into Pharaoh’s, fire dancing in his pupils. “Worry? WORRY? Titu, I’ve just realized how INCOMPENTANT my teammates are! Now I’ve REALLY got a reason to worry! If we… or YOU, rather… don’t get your shit together, then we’re screwed!” At that, the Astronomer turns away and storms off, knocking over anything and everything in his path that he can. However, Pharaoh isn’t fazed by Dorn’s temper. “Child…” With that, the Egyptian Madman turns and strides off in the opposite direction. [End.]
