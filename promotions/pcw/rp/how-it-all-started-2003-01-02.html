How it all started

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-02-03 'How did I get into wrestling?' Chris asked himself. It seemed so long ago that he got started. Yet he remembered everything crystal clear. How the air felt weighting down on his skin. The rumbling in his stomach as he feared what would happen. The bitter and mercyless wind slamming against his body as he stood outside. He can even remember what he was thinking. Great matches of man vs. man. The mortal struggle involved in it all. Then the slamming on flesh onto hard mat. The screams of pains and echo of the crowd as they cheered for more. The glory the victor felt as he stood over his opponent. The little bit of immortality he gained as he knew at least one person in that crowd would remember him and tell the story to his son. Then his son would tell the story to his son. Thus ensuring the man's immortality. Even if it was locked in the eyes of a little boy. It was four years ago. The month was November. It was the 22nd day of the month. It was rather cold for the time of the year. Fall was coming to an end and winter was breaking ground. However in New Orleans, Louisiana winter was just a few degrees colder from summer and so the locals was surprised when the wind picked up and brought the cold winter apon them. Snow was even predicted for this year. A joy to the children yet a woe to the parents. There told a young man, not even really a man yet. He did not know of the cruel ways of the world. He believed anything he imaged he could create. But he was about to be in store for a very painful slap of reality. The young man was slightly covered. A pair of windbreaker pants that was a dim white color. A thin long sleeve dim white t-shirt that did little to protect him from the winds that slammed his body and tosses his blond hair into the air. Those innocent blue eyes stared at the giant door. The young man was tall himself and rather well built. He was slightly over six feet three inches and weighted in at over two hundred and forty pounds. So he was not a small man. But the door still made him look like he was just a lose little boy. The man summed up all his courage and forced himself to knock on the door. Nothing. The man's heart starts to race. He quickly knocked on the door again. This time louder then the last. He was getting nervous. Dispite being freezing cold, sweat still rolled down his forehead and his palms. The door finally comes to life as it slowly starts to open. A large man stands in the doorway. He was massive. Easier taller then seven feet. He must have weighted in at over three hundred pounds on a lighter day. He had a snarl on his lip as he clearly was annoyed. He man quickly reaches out and grabs the man under his chin and lifts him into the air with ease. He stares at him in the eyes. The giant's eyes are a ghostly black. As if he never felt any emotion before in his life. He spoke out with a mighty voice. 'What do you want boy?' his hand clinches tighter on the man's chin, nearly breaking his jaw. He man grabs the giant hand that is holding him up and tries to loosen the grip. He's not strong enough to break the grip though. All he can do is stare into those black eyes. The man starts to get frighten but with it comes anger. Anger that someone is able to do this to him so easily. 'Let go of me you! I'm here to ask you to train me!' he man shouts back. The seven footer just laughs and tosses the man down onto the cold, hard ground. He laughs at the man on the ground. The guy pulls himself to his feet slowly. Getting if the man decided to try anything else. The massive man just shakes his head. He grabs the edge of the door. 'I don't train anyone. Now leave boy before I have to hurt you even more.' he says as he goes to slam the door. The man quickly moves in and puts his foot in the way so the door won't close. The gaint man looks surprised. The smaller man gets his shoulder in the door way also. 'Please Genesis! You have to train me. I want to be an incredible wrestler like yourself! Please I beg of you, train me!' he quickly shouts out. Genesis opens the door a little. The guy relaxes thinking he's going to listen to him. Yet Genesis just gives a gruesome smile. With force he slams the door into the man's shoulder and foot. He opens the door a little and processed to do it again and again. Yet the man refuses to pull out of the doorway. After the fifth slam Genesis finally leaves the door open a crack. Just enough to leave pressure on the man's shoulder and foot, yet they are still able to see each other. The man's face is red from pain. He's bitting his bottom lip trying to bare the pain. Genesis just gives a lighter smile as he realizes the man won't give up so easy. He finally has to ask him. 'What is your name kid?' 'Storm!' he shouts out as Genesis applies more pressure to the door. It feels like his shoulder and foot are going to be crushed in the door frame. Genesis keeps adding more and more pressure to the door slowly. 'Storm what?' Storm's expression changes from a painful mask to one of deep depression. Genesis takes notice and lets the door open more to release the shoulder and foot. Storm backs out of the doorway. His hand hanging low. Genesis gets a curious look on his face. He's interested in the sudden mood swing of the one calling himself Storm. Storm's body starts to shake. It's not from the cold. It's his anger building up inside of him. His face tightens up and his fist clinch up. His entire body is shaking and his chest is heaving in and out. Genesis, although amused by this, starts to get a little annoyed with him. He walks up and pushes him on the hurt shoulder. 'Boy what is your name?' he ask him again. 'I don't know!' shouts the man. He punches Genesis square in the jaw as hard as he can. Genesis stumbles back but refuses to fall. He rubs his chin where he was hit. He spits out blood. He wipes his mouth with his arm and gives a smile. Genesis moves in with skill and speed, unnatural for someone his size, and shoves Storm to the ground. He hits the ground hard, scraping his arm up. Genesis walks back to his door and stands in the door way. 'You made me me bleed. Boy you better prove to me that you want me to train you. Now leave!' with that said he slamms the door shut before Storm can block it. Storm just stared at the door. He starts hitting it with his fist. After a few minutes he stops and leans against the door. The cold chills him to the bone. His body gets weak. Storm can no longer stand it and falls down. Meanwhile Genesis just walks back inside and takes a seat in front of the fire place. He goes back to reading his paper without care if the person outside freezes to death or not. He mutters to himself, 'That boy won't last long. He'll leave after a day or two. A week at top.' The days pasted. One after another. Chilling cold at night and the sun beating down on his during the day. Through horrible rain and wind he endured. The rain soaking him to the bone, the wind chilling him to the point of near death. Yet Storm never moved. At times he would slam his elbow into the door as hard as he could to let Genesis know he was still out there. At times Storm got so sick he would have to throw up near the door. But he never moved from the spot. Finally the seventh day came. Storm was still sitting outside leaning against the door. The lack of food and water was really starting to piss Storm off. Genesis was sitting down enjoying his breakfast when a slam at his door draws his attention. 'He can't still be out there?' he tells himself. Genesis finishes his breakfast and walks over to the door. He stands in front of it looking at the white door. Another slam at it. The familiar sound of Storm's elbow slamming into the door echos out again. Genesis throws the door open causing Storm to fall inside. He just lays on his back looking up at Genesis, surprised at what just happened. 'You still here?' Genesis mutters to him. Storm frowns and turns his head away from Genesis. 'Why?' Genesis ask him. Storm stands up and brushes himself off. 'Because I have no wheres else to go.' Genesis smirks at his reply. He lowers his head and shakes it while laughing softly to himself. 'You know how many people come here to train under me boy? It's not easy being a role model. All the little punks looking up to you.' Storm gets angry at his being called boy. It makes him sound weak and small. 'What makes you worthy of my training?' he asked. Without a second thought Storm easily replies. 'Because I am the perfect wrestler! All I need is someone to train me and I will be unstoppable!' Genesis just laughs. He's heard that millions of times. So what makes this one so different? 'Perfect huh? Well I do owe you one for making me bleed. And you did stay outside my door for an entire week. I tell you what. I'll give you some food and water and a place to sleep for the night then you get the hell out of here.' Genesis smirks. The guy looks horrible. His hair was plastered to his forehead. His clothes soaked, causing them to stick to his skin. The lack of food and water made him very pale. He almost looked like a corpse. 'no.....' came his reply. Genesis looked at him surprised? 'No?' he asked. Not sure if he heard him right. 'No!' Storm shouted. His voice was weak. 'Either you train me or I'll stand right here until I die. It's your choice.' he simply stated. He stomped his foot down to add to his point. A laugh filled the room. It was sick and twisted. The same laugh Genesis had all these years. It was almost his trademark. That sinister laugh that came deep from his black heart. Then with a blink of the eye Genesis laid Storm out with a powerful boot to the face. Storm dropped to the floor. Blood pouring from his mouth. Genesis stood over him looking down. He just laughed at him. Then something surprising happened. Storm managed to pull himself to his feet. Genesis stands back shocked. Course it's not long before he is over it. With a big kick to the side, Genesis sends Storm down to the ground again. He nearly rolls outside of the house. Storm grabs onto the frame of the door to pull himself up. Genesis is surprised again but smirks. He grabs the door and slams it onto Storm's hand. He lets out a sick laugh. The door swings open with might. Storm stands there, his hand hanging at his side. Blood dripping from it. He leans over and supports himself on the frame of the door. Genesis just shakes his head. 'Boy I'll give this, you have guts. But you are stupid.' Genesis claims right before he throws a punch at Storm. Storm pushes off the door frame and avoids the punch. He grabs his wrist and flips Genesis to his back onto the cold and hard ground outside. Genesis can only look up at the sky surprised. He gets back to his feet and dust himself off. He looks over at Storm with a smirk. 'Tell me your name and I'll train you.' He man just put his head down. He replyed in a low voice. 'Storm' Genesis merely shock his head. 'No your full name. I want your full name.' he asked back. A little fustrated. The man just kept his head down. His eyes locked up on the floor. Genesis, upset he won't look him in the eyes, grabs him under his chin and pulls his head up. He makes him look him in the eyes. The man weakly answers the question. 'I don't know my full name. I only know I am called Storm.' Genesis looks at him weird then tosses him to the ground. 'Well if I am going to train you, then you need a full name. I can not just call you Storm.' he thinks about it for a second. Then it comes to him. 'I know what I'll call you.' 'Chris Storm, the Perfect Storm.' The man just smiles as the name sinks in. He looks up at Genesis. 'I like it. I am Chris Storm! I am the Perfect Storm!'
