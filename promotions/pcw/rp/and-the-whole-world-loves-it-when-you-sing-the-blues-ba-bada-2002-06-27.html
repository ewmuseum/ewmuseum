And the whole world loves it when you sing the blues... *BA BADA BA BA BADA DA*

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/27/02 End:'Hey, where's breakfast?' The End walks out of the kitchen in a black bathrobe. His short hair hasn't seen a brush in 24 hours, but doesn't look that bad. His face is dry and unshaven. He stumbles out not fully awake from his night's rest. From the kitchen, the connecting area is the TV room (mainly for the Misfits), and there he finds them, watching Turbo: A Power Rangers Movie. End:'Slade, where's breakfast?' Slade pays too much attention to the television, and only whispers back at The End. Slade:'Oiasdjfopim jifasdpo.' End:'What do you mean it's your day off?' Drek Redaxe answers, but not in a whisper. Drek:'We ahsked ye fer a day oof, and ye sid th'aht t'day woos fine day indeed.' The End claps his hands.'Well, forget that. Go and make me some breakfast, you guys know I can't operate in the morning without any.' Richard:'You seem to be operating right now. Go and fix yourself some cereal.' Lamar:'Yeah, we're buzzin'.' End:'Buzzing?' Richard:'You know, just lying back, ignoring everything else the day, cracking open a cold one...' End:'I thought that was necrophilia?' Drek:'You're fooking sick bahstid.' The End steps up to Drek. End:'Since when did you talk to me in that disprespectful tone?' Drek turns away from The End. Drek:'It's me day oof.' The End grumbles about his lack of good help and goes into the kitchen to make something to eat. The End opens up a cupboard and reaches in. *SNAP!* End:'OW! What the--? Who left a mousetrap in the cupboard?' Richard:'Me. They're for the mice.' End:'That's understandable, but will someone explain to me the Burmese Tiger Pit in the middle of the kitchen floor?' Drek calls back. Drek:'Fer the rah'ts.' End:'... Rats?' Drek:'Mm-hmm. S're soome big fooking rah'ts.' The End climbs out of the Burmese Tiger Trap in the kitchen. Back with the Misfits, they couldn't care less about The End's situation, since it's their day off. The movie is at the part when Justin receives the gifts of being a Power Ranger. End:'These Coco Puffs taste terrible.' Lamar:'We've been out of Coco Puffs for a few weeks.' Drek:'We keep th' moothballs I' tha'ht box o' sireal.' The sound of The End spitting and gagging, coughing up mothballs comes from the kitchen. End:'You ignorant asses are trying to kill me!' Slade:'Klaasdoiuo, hoppasonmio oaso.' End:'Damn right I support you! You'd all be in a circus freak show if it wasn't for me!' Richard:'Who says this isn't a circus freak show now?' The End, wary of anything else the Misfits might have put in the wrong containers, forgets the issue of breakfast and goes to the bathroom. The Misfits ignore him and continue watching their movie. For a little while, everything is silent. Then... End:'Ah, crap! Who left the toilet lid down?'' Lamar quickly runs into the next room. Lamar:'Aw, man. Now we're going to have to clean that up tomorrow.' End:'Tomorrow? Why can't you do it now?' Lamar:'It's our day off.' The End shakes his head. End:'I'll just lift the lid and clean it up later.' Lamar throws himself on the toilet... Lamar:'Don't do that!' End:'I have to go to the bathroom.' Lamar:'We're growing sea-monkeys. They don't like light.' The End is silent and blank for a few moments. End:'... I take it that I wouldn't want to know what's in there?' Lamar: *Shaking head*'Nope.' The End gives up and goes down the hall to the next bathroom. End:'Very well.' Lamar goes back to watch the movie with the other Misfits. By now, the scene involves fighting the turtle monsters on the old creaky ship. Just then- End:'OW! DAMMIT! WHO THE HELL WOULD LEAVE A PLUNGER IN THE TOILET?' Richard:'You should check before you sit down.' End:'You should think before you leave a plunger in the damn toilet!' Richard:'Just take it out, sit down, and go back to whatever you were trying to do.' End:'No, thanks to your negligence I think I can hold off for another few hours.' The End turns on the shower. The water comes out lukewarm, then warm, then hot. He undresses and steps in. End:'FRICKIN' HELL! What are tacks doing on the shower floor!' Drek:'We were pootin' posters o' ye oop on th' wall.' End:'That's it, I'll die in my own house if I stay in here with you people!' The water shuts off, and in a few moments, The End storms out of the back fully clothed. He grabs his sword-cane and walks out the door. Drek:'Was he goin' soomewhere?' Slade:'Dno.' Richard:'Well, he left out the back, anyway.' Everyone's quiet. Drek:'Ye think he remembered the steep hill i'th' bah'ck of the ho'ose?' Slade:'Plmwas?' Richard:'He'll be fine as long as he didn't jump the safety railing like he always does.' 1 minute 25 seconds later, The End rolls to a stop, having fallen down a 500-foot hill. End:'Frickin'... *Cough*' The Misfits drive up in their car. Richard:'Richard figured you'd have finished falling by now. Get in the car, we'll take you back to the house.' The End slowly gets up and dusts himself off. He walks in the direction of Richard's voice and once again falls off a hill, this one about 400 feet down. Drek:'Ye gotta fookin' remember th'aht th' mountains throw yer voice all oover the place!' Richard: 'It's not Richard's fault he's blind!' Slade:'Oweoa uin asd hiqw pi j qiwef nibasd df.' Lamar:'Yeah! Stop fighting and go get him!' Richard drives the car further down to catch The End as he falls down, down, down the hill.
