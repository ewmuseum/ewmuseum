Am I free?

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-22-02 He sat across the desk from me with a look of being annoyed on his face. His thick mustache arching out hiding the smirk I knew he was trying to keep from showing. He had his hands folded behind his head and he was leaning back in his chair, listening. Listening to me and my tall of the night before, listening as if he didn’t care and would rather be doing something else. For a moment I was sure he was going to say it was time for his annual coffee break. But I wasn’t going to let him get away, not until I told him my account of what had happened the night before. “I’m telling you this guy is a psychotic freak.” The best words I could use to describe the guy that broke into my house the night before. But it still seemed as though it wasn’t quite the right words. My boss, the guy sitting across the desk from, leaned forward. “Cal, he’s just a professional wrestler. You make it sound as though he belongs in a straight jacket.” Straight Jacket? Perhaps my boss was onto something. “What, you’re saying you don’t believe me?” I know by now I must sound crazy myself. But h*ll somebody needs to know this guy is a loose cannon ready to go off. His tone grew from laid back to very annoyed. “Look, Cal your one of our top guys, one of the best we got. But maybe you mistook the guy.” What in the world is he thinking? The man broke into my house, and I’ve mistaken his actions his subtle threats…. “Tell me then, how did I mistake him being inside my house?” He leaned back in his chair again. “Did you think that you might have accidentally left your door open?” This was growing old and fast. If he didn’t believe me then why didn’t he just come out and say it. “Listen I’m only going to go over this again one last time.” Open your ears you over grown ape, I though happy that my thoughts couldn’t give me away. “I was sitting at home, drinking a beer. The phone rang, I answered the d*mn phone. People can do that in their own home, can’t they? He said my name, how did he know my name? He then told me to meet him someplace, a park d*mn it. A park in the middle of no where, when I get there nobody is there. I mean nobody, not a bum, young lovers, no f(censored)cking body. So what do I do next, I go home. You know the place where you’re suppose to feel safe. The place you go home every night to get away from the psycho’s in the world. When I get there this guy is sitting on my couch. D*mn man, he took the light bulbs out of my lambs and disconnected my phone. And you’re telling me that I mistook the guy?” There I got it all out, and without any interruptions at that. Now let’s see what he thinks. Am I really the crazy one here? Do I have something here or not? D*mn I’m frustrated as ever. After sitting there for what seemed like a long while he looked straight into my eyes. His teeth biting into his bottom lip. His wrinkles on his forehead showing the signs of his age. He seemed to be unsure what to say. Most likely thinking that I had lost my mind and imagined the entire scene, but I didn’t, did I? “Cal, maybe you should take a few days off.” This was his reply. “You could use the time away from the office.” Could I? Frustrated I stood pushing my chair back, causing it to screech on the hard wood floor, I turned and walked out of his office making darn sure to slam the door on my way out. I’m the victim here why does it feel as though I’m being treated as a lunatic on his last leg? Maybe some time off is what I needed. I was after all long due for a vacation, a vacation away from these over paid suits, and more importantly the so called superstars that carry this company. I left the downtown office building thinking of where I might go and what I might do with my time off. A vacation that would keep me busy or one where I could relax, unsure of what I wanted to do I thought about contacting a travel agency later. Although I had plenty of time to think about where I wanted to go and what I wanted to do. Before I could put in for my vacation with my bosses secretary he had already called her and told her to put me down for two weeks. What was I going to do for two weeks? As I walked to my car I got an odd sensation that I was being watched. I stopped and look behind me but I didn’t see anything… better anybody out of the ordinary. My mind must have been playing games with me. But as I turned around again he feeling grew even more. My hair on my arms began to stand on end and that’s when it happened… First it was the hand on my shoulder. Then it was, “Hi there Cal.” I knew at that moment that my assumption was right. I not only was being watched but followed as well. And now that person was right next to me and talking to me. I slowly turned towards the first that approached me. To find that I was correct in my guess of whom it was. It was none other than the Freak himself, Ripper Ryckman. “Wha… do you wan…T?” A sly smile came over his face. “What’s with the attitude Cal?” He was talking as if nothing ever happened. As if he never broke into my house. I’ve been in PCW for a long time now. I’ve seen them all in my time here. But nobody seemed as dangerous as this man does. You at least knew you were going to set one of them off. With this guy it was different. He seemed calm and collected all the time. But yet dangerous… how could this be? How could a man appear to be normal yet give off the impression that he wasn’t normal at all? “Attitude, how about the fact you broke into my house last night?” Has he forgotten about that? He appears to be confused, but about what? “I thought we had all of that worked out? You know the part about me having you spread the word that I had signed a contract with Primetime Championship Wrestling.” Yeah, I remembered. How could I forget? “Fine… what about the part of having me drive half way across the city to a park that (pointing to him) you were suppose to meet me at?” Let’s see what he has to say about that. He laughs, not a loud long lasting laugh, but a laugh none less. “I’m not sure I know what you’re talking about.” What does he mean he’s not sure? Of course he’s sure, isn’t he? “Tell me Cal, did you pass along my message?” Again acting as if we were life long buddies. You bet and a whole lot more, like you should be locked up for one. “Yeah, I passed it a long.” Acting as if it wasn’t a big deal. “I was also informed you’re first match has been signed.” A smile came over the Freak’s face. As he ran his hands together and shook his head. “Goooooddddd…” At that exact moment an almost unbelievable devilish grin came over his face. His mounds of muscles seemed to contract with each breath he took. And his eyes seemed as though they held his opponents fate already in his hands. As if he were picturing the pain and suffering that the poor individual would be going through. “You don’t have to sound as if your going to enjoy it.” At that moment he looked down upon me and I began to feel as if I shrank with each passing second that his stared was directed towards me. I then knew that wrestling wasn’t about collecting a pay check or even getting name recognition for himself. It was only about one thing, only one reason why he stepped into the center of that ring week in and week out. It was legalized violence to him. He could wrap those think powerful hands around someone’s neck and not have to worry about being handcuffed and dragged away by ten police officers. “Enjoy it, (laughs) I’ll be doing a lot more than enjoying it. That I can promise.” His look was telling me the entire story. His words just added thunder to an already raging storm. At that moment vacation didn’t sound like such a bad idea. In fact retirement sounded like an even better one. I was just ready to get away from this man. “I need to go. So if you don’t mind.” “Go, where you off to little man.” What the h*ll? Me having to, GO, put a tone of anger into his voice. What exactly did he want from me? Why was he following me? Under my breath I blurted out the words. “Yeah, I got to pack for my vacation.” Damn what did I just do? I told him I was leaving town. Big mistake Cal, big mistake. Where you going? I just knew that was going to be is next question. He was going to try and pry into my private life. Perhaps to follow me, on my vacation? He would do that, would he? Maybe I could tell him one place and go thousands of miles away from there. Maybe then I could get away… “Just of vacation.” Here it comes. He’s going to ask where. “Ok.” That’s it ok. Nothing else, no where too, with who, or why? “I’ll see you around.” With that he patted me on the shoulder looked into my eyes. Oh, Lord was that a grin, a smirk, a look of I’ll see you around. Look at me I’m nervous and jumpy. I need to get a hold of myself. As I stood there half in fright and half wondering what in the h*ll just happened. The Freak turned and began walking in the opposite direction. I turned and watched him turn a corner about a block away. He was gone. I am free. Or am I…
