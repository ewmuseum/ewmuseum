Arriving in Tea Neck, NJ

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/31/02 *Scene opens in a restaurant where Ricky Thunder is reading a newspaper and sipping acup of coffee. He shakes his head in laughter a bit as he reads an article* Since the early 1700's, the Pine Barrens forests of New Jersey have been the scene ofmany strange phenomena. These events all involve some sort of demon creature whosedescription varies from a three-and-a-half foot tall demon looking bird, to a hair coveredanimal that is over seven feet tall. More often than not, these strange occurrences are allattributed to a creature known as ' The Jersey Devil'. The legend of the Jersey Devil iswidely believed by the residents of NJ, and has even become a state mascot of sorts. There is also a movie that touches on the legend. One movie in particular entitled 'TheLast Broadcast' has somewhat of a cult status among fans for being the true predecessorof 'The Blair Witch Project'. Like in 'Blair Witch', a group of amateur filmers venture offinto the forest to document an occult haunting - never to be heard from again. Maindifference is that instead of the Blair Witch, this time it's the Jersey Devil. Many haveheard of Jersey's NHL hockey team with the same name (The Jersey Devils). This is allobviously intended as pun, but on a different note, some people take the legend seriously.They scour the woods there, trying to discover, document , or otherwise identify theelusive creature. They collect frequent reports of unexplained sightings and regularlydiscuss the nature and possible origin of it. To get a firm understanding of the nature ofthe Jersey Devil, let's take a quick look at how this legend began in the first place. Versions of the creatures origin differ slightly but here is basically where the creature reportedly came from... The story takes place in the year of 1735, there was a husband and wife who lived in theforest called the 'Pine Barrens' in Leeds NJ. The family resided in a small cottage, deep inthe woods there. They were both quite skilled in the evil black arts, and were practicingSatan worshippers, the man being a warlock, and the wife a witch. They already had 12children born to them, but the warlock husband demanded even more! The witch (MotherLeeds, as she would later be called) disdained the idea of another insidious brat, but theman persisted to impregnate her against her will once again. Pregnant and Full to theoverflowing with satanic rage - she cursed the day she met her husband and ever the dayshe conceived a child with him. She declared with her own mouth ' I wish this child to bea devil '. When the fullness of her pregnancy came to an end, there were her husband thewarlock and a midwife present to deliver the child. As she lay upon her bed in that littlecottage, it poured rain, and a terrible thunderstorm was in full force. Then the child cameforth from his mother. As he went into the hands of the midwife, she cried out in terror asthe baby disfigured and transformed right in her hands! The cries of a human baby weretransformed into the terrible shrieking of a hideous demon. The wishes of mother Leedshad been granted! The Jersey Devil was born! Immediately it destroyed the poor midwifewith it's fangs and claws, not hesitating to angrily turn it's fury and bloodlust on both Mr.and Mrs. Leeds. It is unclear whether or not Mrs. Leeds survived, but the tattered andtorn husband watched in terror as the devil flew right up the chimney and into the stormynight. Ever since, the Jersey Devil has haunted the Pine Barrens forests of Northern NewJersey. This outbreak of terror was abruptly stopped in 1740 when a certain Christianpriest was called upon to exorcise (cast out) the demon. Subsequently it was not seen orheard from outside it's immediate territory for one hundred years. There are some disagreements as to certain details, but that is the basic story passed downthrough oral tradition. Figuring that most stories are based on some element of truth, onvein of thought runs strong throughout the legend. Witchcraft and the occult. There is seemingly a pattern that has surrounded the sightings. In 1840, exactly 100 yearsafter the demons exorcism, there was a rash of reports from all over the area of NewJersey. The winged entity reportedly killed dogs and farm animals, chased people, andmade many afraid. The 'devil' appeared again several times throughout the 1800's, onceagain terrorizing people and killing animals and livestock and such. in 1899, a wave ofattacks were reported from as far away as New York State and Pennsylvania. January16th through 23rd, 1909 is know as 'the phenomenal week.' During this time, thecreature was seen every day. The entire state was in a panic, and the devil was seen inNew York and Pennsylvania. Strangely, the sightings abruptly stopped that year as fast asthey started. After 1909, over the course of years, the creature was reported several times.Some claimed to kill it and show the body but none of these were ever proven. More oftenthan not it was reported to scare some people, eat chickens or attack other animals such asdogs, then be chased away into the woods. Several times the animals tracks were found aswell. Usually the tracks were described as hoof prints. The creature's appearance is reported differently by many, with some certain attributesremaining consistent. It is was always said in it's earlier history to be between 3 and 4 feettall, have bat like wings, a horse like or dog like head and neck, a small body, and hoofedfeet. Tiny clawed limbs protrude from it's body, and it moved somewhat birdlike. In the last 20 or 30 or so years though, there has been a newer version of the animal'sappearance. A far more realistic one. Whether or not it is the Jersey Devil of legend, orsome new creature, is unsure. Some say there is no connection between this new one andthe traditional legends, but it possess some strikingly similar attributes to the creature oflegend which cannot be ignored. For one, this newer animal has a head and neck that hasbeen compared to a horse's, or a hyena's. Nobody I know of has gotten a clear look at thisanimal's feet, but the legs are reverse jointed and relatively small like the legendaryanimal's are. However this new creature is reportedly much bigger than 4 feet tall, and isreportedly quite powerful looking, being over seven feet tall, and approximately 300 pluspounds. That - and the lack of wings - are the biggest differences. The new creatures head,neck, and body comprise the majority of it's height. Proportionally it has very, very shortlegs. It has extremely long arms that could touch the ground. Regarding the short, scruffyhair which completely covers the animal, it appears in a brindle pattern similar to a hyenaor pit bull (erratic striping). One witness described the hair as white. It is said that the neckis one of the most prominent features, being at least as thick as the head at the base of it.The neck is also distinctly arched, or bent forward, with a pronounced spine bone. Isuppose that the torso, legs, along with the long arms could be compared in an overallsense with the shape of a bat . --- Other than both of these descriptions, there are alwaysthe ominous black shadows, screams in the night, eyes peering from the darkness, and allthe other usual stuff associated with the 'Jersey Devil' and other demonic hauntings forthat matter.
