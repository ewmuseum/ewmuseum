The Bellzier Chronicles: Of Dreams and Nightmares [Part Two]

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 26, 2002 DreamLandTime Unknown The night was so long and dark, I hardly knew what was in front of me when I opened my eyes. That was a nice way to wake up to your new morning, huh? That’s right, I was actually waking up at this time. But apparently, that’s the way Dream Land works. They go to bed in the early afternoon, and they wake up in the middle of the night. I had on a wrist watch, which I checked, but the numbers on my “Cool-As-Hell” Casio digital watch were going crazy. I guess watches weren’t allowed in Dream Land. I moved around a bit, remembering that I had spent the night on the floor of Fearful’s floor, the scared as hell rabbit. It was wooden, the floor that is, but extremely soft. If I didn’t know any better, I would have thought that I was sleeping in my very own bed. I rubbed the ground, thanking it for giving me such a comfortable night of sleeping. To my surprise, the floor answered me back. “Oh yeaaaaah. That’s it, that’s the spot.” The floor bellowed out to me. I, as always, had an incredulous look on my face. After all, it’s not everyday the floor talks to you. At least, not if you’re sane. I tapped my hand against the floor. “Knock, knock… “Who’s there?” I paused for a moment. This was getting awfully weird. “Tom…” “ Tom who?” “ TOM, YOU’VE GOT TO GET THE HELL OUT OF HERE! THE FLOOR IS TALKING TO YOU!” Was I overreacting? Perhaps. I was in Dream Land, so I should have suspected the weird and the lame to be following my footsteps everywhere, but I guess the floor speaking to me was just too much to handle. So I started to run for the door, which would eventually lead to the kitchen/living room and freedom. As I did, the floor underneath me began to move. It swayed me back and forth, as if playing with me, and finally tugged me down until I fell flat on my buttocks. Geez that hurt. I grimaced as I began to pick myself off of the floor and rub my derriere. Hard falls like that one on your tailbone hurt much more than you would originally believe. “Owwww…my buttocks.” I complained. The floor answered back in a tone that sounded vaguely like my mother. “Well, you deserved it. Running away like that hurt my feelings. How would you like to say hello to someone, just to have them run off like you were Freddy Kreuger or something? I mean, I’m insulted. Do I not look pretty today?” “ Look, I’ve seen Freddy Kreuger before, okay? No one is uglier then he is, trust me. Hell, I’d rather kiss a toilet then look at his ugly mug again.” To my chagrin, the floor began to cry back at me, and that’s when my cold, hard exterior began to melt. Why do I have to be such a softy? “Then it is me….I’m ugly! I knew I should have bought that make-up kit that wonderful woman, Kathy Lee Gifford was selling. She’s so pretty, unlike me!” The floor is crying. My hands, which were facing palms down against the floor, were actually beginning to become wet. I guess the floor had tear ducts too. Awfully, awfully lame. I guess my sensitive side would have to overcome all fears now. “ Stop crying, this isn’t baseball. C’mon, get over yourself.” The floor sobbed and sobbed, and between pouts continued to speak. “I can’t help it. When you’re ugly, you have low self-esteem. I am a low self-esteem floor.” Some wild, unprotected floor sex could change all of that. Could floors do that? I decided to take a melodramatic approach and try to fix the situation. That’s me – always looking to make the lives of fellow Dream Land citizens a better place. “ Look, I’ve seen uglier floors. Hell, when I was young, my dad used to take me to the race tracks all of the time. Do you know what the floors were like there?” The floor sniffled and stopped crying momentarily. “Were they smelly?” “Yes…” “Were they dirty?” “Yes…” “Were they as sexy as I am? Gosh, do I really have to answer that? I feel like a pervert if I said yes, and yet, I feel lame saying no. Uh, avoid the question. Yes! That’s it, avoid it, Tom. Be a man! “ Do you know what I think? I think you need to get over yourself, baby. I think you need to stand up for yourself. That’s right. Stand up for yourself!” The ground suddenly began to shake, and it felt like a huge earthquake was occurring underneath me. I suddenly realized that the floor was taking me literally when I told it to stand up for itself. Oops. “Okay, okay. Don’t stand up for yourself! On second thought, just stay where you are!” The ground stopped shaking. Yay, I wasn’t going to die. “Alright, let’s calm down here. Why don’t you tell me your name. My name’s Tom Lexian. Who are you?” “Silly billy, I’m the floor. I don’t have a name. But I already know who you are. Fearful told me all about you while you were sleeping. Fearful doesn’t sleep very much. He’s so paranoid about being eaten by Tiger-Man that he wastes away half the night looking outside the window with a fork in his hand. “A fork?” “You heard me! I said a fork!” “No forkin’ way!” Bad joke Tom. Floors don’t like bad jokes. I decided to put everything into perspective. I rubbed my chin and began to speak again. “Okay, let me get this straight. The man, erm, the rabbit spends his entire night worrying his wits away about this Tiger-Man, yet he only defends himself with a fork?” The floor shrugged, well, sort of. “It’s a pointy fork…uh, it cuts into food like no other fork in all of Dream Land!” “ That’s just great. Hell, if I was afraid of some tiger man, I’d arm myself a bit more heavily. Shit, I’d get like a frickin’ bazooka and just go all Wu-Tan Shaolin on his ass. Now, how do you feel about that? I mean, c’mon, a fork? What’s wrong with this rabbit? He needs a sword, an axe, a rifle, a machine gun…hell, he needs the frickin’ BFG 2000 from the best selling game ever, DOOM. Yes, BFG 2000. Whoo! I think it’s only thirty dollars at toy stores nationwide.” “I don’t know what to say. But he’s always losing sleep over it. You’ve never seen the Man-Tiger before, so you don’t know the horrors that await Fearful. He likes to eat your head first, then he takes a straw and drinks the marrow from your bones. Then he does the Macarena on your body while doing several pelvic thrusts in the air.” Wow. Dancing in such a manner denotes intelligence. Intelligence denotes an ability to reason. An ability to reason means he’ll figure out how lame I am. Look at that terrible chain reaction! “So, is he sleeping now or what? Can I speak with him?” “He’s still awake. I can still feel him pacing in the kitchen. Go speak with him. Maybe you can relieve some of his paranoia.” I walked out of the room, opening the door to the kitchen. I immediately bumped into the kitchen table. It was a steel table, with jagged edges. It was painful when I ran into it. Hell, I thought it had pierced my skin when I ran into it. The entire house was a one story cottage, and here he was, with a steel table. What is this? I spotted Fearful pacing around, just as the floor had told me. He was murmuring something to himself, and he indeed did have a fork in his hands. My, it looked dull. “ Oh my gosh!” Fearful exclaimed, jumping in the air. “You’re up already! You must be hungry…let me fix you something to eat…” He ran over to a simmering stove and began spooning the brown slop into a bowl. It looked anything but appealing to me. Better not to insult the walking rabbit when he’s holding a fork in his hand, albeit, a dull fork. I took the bowl and smiled. “Thanks, but I’m not really hungry at the moment. Maybe later.” “ But it’s my very special stew. It’s chock full of vitamins and carbohydrates to keep you running through the day. Essential eats for someone on a quest like yourself.” I stared at the stew, and I could have sworn I saw an eyeball floating in there. It disappeared back into the abyss of stew, until I could see it no longer. My stomach began to churn… “Fearful, this looks like the same stew you served me last night…” Fearful sighed and sat down sadly. “It seems as though you’re too smart to be fooled. Touche, my good man. It’s true, this is the same stew you had last night.” “That’s fairly disgusting. But anyways, tell me why exactly are you so afraid all the time?” “Afraid? Who’s afraid? Who told you I was scared?” “Well…the floor kind of told me how paranoid you are of Tiger-Man….” “Let me tell you something. The floor is nothing more than a scheming, rumor riddled little trouble maker. Don’t pay any attention to it. God knows it’s done me more harm than god! Hmph!” The floor rumbled underneath us. Obviously it heard. “Okay, okay. Maybe I’m a bit afraid, but not to the point where I would stay up all night and just wait for him to come and eat me!” “ There’s nothing to fear. This AWS man, he can’t be as scary as he sounds. Hell, no one is. If you just stand up to him, you’ll be able to beat him and earn yourself confidence! Fight the power!” Suddenly, there was an intense rapping at the door. I looked over at it, and it was shaking with a fury I’ve never seen before. I looked over at fearful with an inquisitive look. “Are you expecting visitors?” Suddenly, a loud, menacing voice called out. “OPEN THIS DOOR! I WANT TO EAT YOU!” “Oh my god! It’s him! It’s him! We’re going to die!” “ COME OUT HERE AND FIGHT! I CAN SMELL YOU ALL THE WAY OUT HERE!” I smelled my shirt. It smelled like a penguin. Waddle waddle. Fearful grabbed my shirt and looked like he was going to have a stroke. “What do we do? What do we do? What do we do?” “You said he’s big?” “YES!” “You said he’s ugly!” “YES!” “You said he’s vicious, mean, and a creature that does nothing but pillage and cause suffering to all he comes in contact with?” “YES! YES! YES!” “Is there a back door to this place?” “YES!” And then I bolted…. …like the wind, only faster….
