Transition -- History (Part 3: PWA)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered *Three weeks ago, while in FBI detention; dictated to Sandy Wong* UWWC closed, myself dying on their final show (which wouldn't have been good for ratings anyway). After that, I decided to go for the bigtime. After a little training sabbatical back at the house that my parents left for me in their will back when they had me, I acquired an application to the Primetime Wrestling Academy. I filled it in (though 'The End' was already filled in, go figure) and sent it in. They accepted me. See, this was at the time where the PWA could actually be choosy and not let any schmuck walk through the doors. I went there, spent a week in The HIT's class. Drake Maxwell was my rival in terms of skill. He had a habit of trying to start fights, but the trainers could still kick his ass if necessary. He insisted on calling me 'Xiyeau' for some reason. I don't know where he got that. Brad KnoXvill called me that once. I think because he saw my AOL screenname listed and didn't know my real name. Drake Maxwell then took to calling me that, as opposed to The End. He swore it was a Japanese war name or something. Hell, I just typed in letters when picking an SN. I don't know what, if anything, it means. Maxwell wanted to wrestle me because he, like the others before him, wanted to prove something, and he thought I was a 'mark.' 'Mark' in the sense that I was going to be used as a stepping stone for someone's success. I had been bashing and silencing such wrestlers for a while. I wrestled Maxwell on my first and last PWA show, beating him by DQ because John Crichton interfered. Crichton had no talent at that point. It was sad. Drake Maxwell was quite annoying, but there were other academy students that were just plain weird. John Crichton was one of them. My term in PWA was about 1/20th of what it was for him. He'd been in PWA for a couple of months by the time I came in, and he was there just as long after I left, rather leaving to join Global Wrestling Federation than to try to learn anything in PWA. Then again, being in PWA for so long, he would've learned something by then if he was going to. Crichton was good for a laugh. He didn't intend to be, but still. He was never too smart. No, he was an idiot. I've used the expression before, but he was slam-drunk; whenever he spoke it was slurred and erratic in topic... he must've been dropped on his head too much. He had pictures of 'John Crichton' from Farscape. We all made the connection; hey, his real name is Dave Constantinople. He would walk around like he owned the place, and have those pictures with him. About 10 of them, carried in a Trapper Keeper. Everytime he spoke, he showed you a picture. We never saw his real face, he was always flashing those pictures. He wore a mask of 'John Crichton' over his face. Even worse, at PWA shows, he had those pictures cycle through as he spoke. The pictures went pretty fast, he didn't say much. The vast extent of his vocabulary was the phrase 'Now ain't that a bitch?' He said that as a response to everything. It often got his ass kicked (especially when letting it slip when talking to his mom when she visited once). Next was Ken Kaze. First, I thought he was a day-dreamer. No, he was just an idiot. He tried to model himself after Ed, going so far as to have a trashcan named George that he brought with him wherever he went. George was the more charismatic of the two. Kaze was a clutz. He was always tripping and breaking his neck trying to do a springboard from the apron, or slipping while running off the ropes, something. Sometimes I wonder how he managed to do as well as he did. Then I remember that there is such a thing as sheer dumb luck. Kaze had that. And a trashcan named George. This somehow got him NTICW titles, and a win over a guy like Hessian. But he's gone now, and Hessian is about a month away from killing Propaganda since they're teamed up, so everything is good; the universe has been brought back into balance. Oh, as I was coming in, Itagaki Freeze and Kenji Hirose were going out. Weird, those two. About the only thing they had in common were that they were Japanese, but they tag teamed (not that they were very good at it) because a El Diablo and HIT said that PCW would probably accept them faster if they were in a tag team. Freeze liked to dye his hair white, and liked to stand outside in the snow, and there was apparently a glacier he lived on that he invited everyone over to for barbeque. We were smart in bringing a wood stove and some chicken wings. He was a rather dejected individual. Can't say much about him, other than he sucked in PCW. As for Kenji, he had a gambling problem, apparently. But again, wasn't much to him other than that he bombed out of PCW after Alliances where Akiyama was forced to try to carry a team of Japanese people who sucked. Brad KnoXville was there for a second. He was alright. Just wanted to wrestle. Wasn't the best at it, but he tried. I can't remember much about him, then again he wasn't that much of a dynamic guy. But all good things must come to an end. And, all short things that didn't have time to be good or bad must also come to an end. And thus, I left the PWA with a promotion to a PTC federation that was starting back up after a hiatus for restructuring... *Next session: EW*
