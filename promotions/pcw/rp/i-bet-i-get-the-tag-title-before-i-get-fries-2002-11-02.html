Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 11-2-02 4. causing misfortune: characterized by, bringing, or signifying bad luck (A Duke Williams omen)5. malicious: characterized by a desire to cause hurt or harm. (Duke is going to kill him.)6. disagreeable: very unpleasant (This reeks of Duke Williams!) { I've always been crazy but it's kept me from going insaneNobody knows if it's something to bless or to blameSo far I ain't found a rhyme or a reason to changeI've always been crazy but it's kept me from going insane}-Waylon JenningsThe words to an old Cole Porter song fill up my thoughts. I lie awake in the dark hours that linger in silence just before the dawn. I listen for that cold, black, train carrying old hobo’s running from something that faded like my dreams of living a normal life a long time ago. Looking for a new home but winding right back where they started with a little less to gain and to live for. I don’t pity these men but rather admire them. So free from any leash with nothing to lose nor anything to gain. They concur nothing but follow no man’s orders. Marching to a beat of a drum only audible inside their individual heads. Unlike Jesus the burden of the world is not upon them nor do they wish for it to be. Take this cross and shove it is the attitude they live by and die by. The world needs men like this. On the other hand we would never evolve into a great society if this were all we had. We need leaders, religious figures, poets, prophets, and Goth Gods. This is the tale of a man taking a stand against a system that has never been questioned. -The camera catches up with our Goth God and your President Dukestar The Goblin as he stands in a long line waiting for service. He is wearing ostrich skin boots, black sweat pants, a black nylon Starter shirt, black mascara on his face, and black cowboy hat with a skull on the front and a dead flamingo wrapped around the brim. Duke talks into the camera ignoring the stares he’s getting from the other patrons of this fine family eatery know as McDonalds.- Dukestar: People are saying that time takes care of people like me. I’m living to fast and they say I can’t last for much longer. Little do they know that their thoughts of me are my savoir. Little do they know that as a Goth God I’m going to live forever. So enough with the bullshit about this old Goth Buckaroo is too damn worn down to get the job down.-Duke scratches his evil Goth beard.-Dukestar: Let me rephrase that since getting the “job” done doesn’t seem to be the problems its getting the win that’s a Janet Reno(bitch). El Diablo in the main event over Duke Williams? Are you people smoking crack or cock when you came up with that there title match. But that’s all blood under the bridge where the homeless guy slit the joggers throat. -A mother with her child covers the kids ears and exits quickly.- Dukestar: What’s on the docket now is the Tag Team Titles. Are you ready for the Kannon-Williams connection? A bond of GM and President. Prep and Freak. Do you understand the historical quality this holds? A marriage of left and right creating an unstoppable duo. We are the Front Office and if any lesson has been learned over the last year its best not to fuck with us. -Duke gets annoyed by the slow guy behind the counter who happens to be retarded.- Dukestar: This is the first warning of pending food poisoning. Anyways the rest of the rabble rousers in this tag match up have about as much of a chance of winning as DA Bears do the Super Bowl. For you none football fuckers that calculates out to zero. -Duke sighs as the Mongoloid f*cks up another order.- Dukestar: When you get down to brass tacks who has seen more then Williams and Kannon? Who has headlined more shows then Dukestar and Xavier? The answer is nobody. Look this is like Willie Nelson stepping into a boxing ring with Mike Tyson. I love Willie, you love Willie, hell Mike might even like Willie but when you look at it realistically even with Willie’s black belt he ain’t got a chance dark hoss. -Duke Stoops his feet in protest at the slow witted servers incompetence.-Dukestar: Lets put the slong in the slot and call a fuck a fuck. Hey hoss I’m a Goth God and nobody else in PCW is. So how can we lose? Think about it for a moment I’ll give you ten seconds.-Duke looks at his Evil Movado watch.-Dukestar: Times up….the correct answer is you can’t. -Duke gets impatient and yells out to the special ed major.- Dukestar: This is ridicules its called fast food so why do we have a slow dude behind the counter? -Everybody stares at Dukestar The Goblin.- Dukestar: I mean for the love of Hubbard this is bullshit. I’ve seen fat broads chase ice-cream trucks with more speed then this. Look son it ain’t rock science. You punch in the order and some greasy faced loser makes the sandwich then some uneducated fat women scoops up some three-hour-old fries and pushes a button corresponding to the drink size. Then you shove it in a bag and where done. -The kid is ready to cry.- Dukestar: Look I ain’t got time for your tears I’m an Evil Goth God and a very important man. You need to understand this. So get somebody who ain’t all weepy eyed up here to serve me some damn food at a respectable speed. You jive hoss? -The kid looks dumbfounded as the manager steps up.- Manger: I’m the manager here can I help you? Dukestar: I’m impressed does that mean you’re the only one here with a GED? Manager: I will not take this abuse. Dukestar: Look spanky I just came here for a hamburger, fries, and an ice cold coke. What do I get? I get a retard trying to comprehend the English language while sparky in the back drools on the food and puts ketchup on a hamburger when they asked for just mustard. You jive G-E-D Jed? Manager: I’m going to have to ask you to leave. -Dukestar’s face gets red though the makeup.- Dukestar: Let me ask you a something hoss. Why do you hire a guy who can’t tie his own fucking shoes then you put him in front of everybody during lunch hour? Another thing why do you need to hire walking death to serve my food. There is no need for an eighty year old women who can’t remember her kids names to be serving me food. Put the old bitch in a retirement village and let me get my fast food fast you filthy fucker you. Manager: I’m calling the police. Dukestar: Fuck you and the police. I’m calling the health department you roach infested, geriatrics using, retard serving, pimply faced feeding, fat fried fawn frolics are going down. You can put that in your high school drop out pot pipe and smoke. I’m a God and you don’t fuck with the Papacy president. Manager: Take it up with the police sir they are on their way. Dukestar: Let I’m leaving now and it has nothing to do with your threat of calling the cops but rather the slow ass service her.
