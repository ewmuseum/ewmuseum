The Flaymin Blues...

Date: not entered “I need a drink.” Naomi plopped do2wn on the barstool with a glum look on her face. Rei looked up from polishing glasses, her brow arched at the despaired sound in her friend’s voice. “What’s with you?” The tall brunette shook her head, her ponytail swinging behind her. “Nothing… nothing at all. Just hit me with a shot of Asura.” A sigh left the woman’s lips as she leaned over the counter and closed her eyes. Her behavior now… it’s the result of losing to the likes of Flaymin Aymin. Her momentum shot to hell and back, she had high hopes for pulling off a winning streak upon her debut here in PCW, but now that was dead, as dead as Chet King’s respect for women. But she was still the PCW Cruiserweight champion… and she was going to remain that way on Fusion. Finals had just passed; tough times, studies were extra hard this time, with these new things popping up out of the blue, her time was strained more than Chet King’s manhood after he drew with Tessa Windsor… and the manhood challenged by Miss Moonlight herself. Miss Moonlight challenged PCW alone… simply by being a woman, and by being a rookie in the company. Everyone wanted to see her fail, just because of her talent and her urge to rise to the top no matter what; the cruiserweight division already hated her guts, and the men of PCW saw her as a threat to every aspect of the business itself. A near six foot tall two hundred pound woman that could lift as much weight as they could, and even had the power to press some of them overhead. She was the biggest threat to hit PCW since Xavier Kannon… Not because she was a woman… not because she was a superheroine… because she was a being determined to prove everyone wrong… and achieve her goal no matter the cost. Rei sat the small crystal shot glass down, filled with a deep red liquid. Naomi grabbed it and downed it instantly, slamming it back into the counter and nudging it into her friend’s hand. “Keep em coming, Rei-Rei.” The brown haired bartender’s eyes grew more concerned. “Whatever you say, girl.” There was another life outside of PCW3, Naomi had to remember that. The life of Naomi Kawaii… Tokyo University freshman. Naomi worked hard to get where she is, and she was determined not to fail at this point. Her parents were helping as much as they could to make sure she got her degree in International Business, and she didn’t want to fail them one second. But sometimes… it felt like she was failing them indeed. And the only reason she did was because she lived a life outside of being a college student… a life that was hidden from her parents and friend’s eyes. She lived a life of servitude, a life of protection and honor, and by a code of justice. A path lighted by the light of the Moon… the life of Miss Moonlight. The protector of Tokyo was always on duty, no matter the stress of studying for finals at school, or by the trying struggle of being a female PCW superstar one or two nights a week. evil always lurked nearby… and she swore by the moon that she would protect the city she loved. She swore by the moon she would protect PCW… “You know,” Rei began as she sat down the next shot of Asura brand wine, wiping her hands off on the black apron laced in front of her. “You can always talk about your problems with me. that’s what being friends is all about.” Naomi looked up at her, her eyes a bit glassy. But instead of opening up, she took the shot down and slammed it again. She smacked her lips, shaking her head slowly. “You know, Rei-Rei… some things just have to stay bottled up.” She flicked a finger at the sealed bottle of Asura on the counter, drawing out another concerned sigh from her bartender friend. Some things stay bottled up… while others are opened and drained for all they’re worth. Miss Moonlight was booked up this Fusion… a title shot for Brianna Washington, the former Hardcore Gadget. Now the talented woman wanted a shot at the Cruiserweight Championship of the Mistress of the Moon. The title will not change hands. Nothing against the woman… but this was a competitive line of work, and even though the both of them should work together for the equal opportunity of womanhood in PCW, on Fusion the two of them will go against each other. Woman to woman… And in the end they’ll shake hands in the ring and hug, a job well done no matter who wins. Because if they get in the ring and show all the PCW fans that women are just as good as men… that will be the heroic thing to do… and that will keep PCW safe just a little more. Brianna’s not a villain… she deserves respect, but Chet King… his mistreatment of Cindy Bi, his rude talk of women… it will not go unpunished. He has to wait for Turmoil though… First Miss Moonlight will retain the title… then she’ll defend it against Chet King one day to seal his villainous fate once and for all… by the light of the moon. Naomi laid her head down on the bar, a heavy exhale coming out from her. “Wake me in a few hours… I gotta go to the gym before I study a little…” Rei shook her head in pity. “You and your weights. All right… in a few hours.” She walked back into the kitchen, leaving Naomi Kawaii resting at the counter in the empty bar that slow afternoon…thoughts of the lecherous Aymin pinning her disturbing her lingering thoughts before drifting off into sleep.
