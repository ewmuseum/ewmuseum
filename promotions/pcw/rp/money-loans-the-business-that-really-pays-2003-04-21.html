Money Loans... The business that really pays....

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 21, 2003 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up outside of what seems to be a waiting room. In the room you hear a relaxing style of music, no words, just instrumental, but it’s relaxing to sit back and listen to. The room itself has several rows of chairs in it, with almost a quarter of those chairs filled by someone waiting for whatever the room is for waiting for. There is a window of bullet proof glass with a woman standing behind it, she seems to be a bit on the wild side though, as if she’s part of a gang or something, or just off the streets in a new type of environment. The room though seems like that of a Dentist Office, so it just seems too out of place for anything to be true or make any sense what so ever. The door on the far end of the room opens then and a man steps through the door with a smile on his face and a wade of cash in his hand. He sticks the cash in his pocket and turns around to stare at the man who is walking through the door behind him, it’s Semore Smoke! The man shakes hands with Smoke for what could seem to be the millionth time and he turns around and walks across the room, and then out the main entrance. Smoke looks at the next man in line and points for him to enter through the door with Smoke… Semore Smoke: You’re next my friend, this won’t take long at all. {{{{The man stands up excitedly and walks towards the door with a grin on his face. He steps through the door with Smoke and the room is that of a small room with just a table in the middle of it with a safe on top of it, two chairs at the table and a video camera recording everything that goes on in the room. Smoke points for the man to sit down at one end of the table and then sits down himself at the opposite side of the table. The safe is sitting right next to him and he has a key around his neck, which goes to the lock on the safe. Smoke points to the man across from him and says…}}}} Semore Smoke: So, we can start off with you telling me what your name is. {{{{The man nods his head and is still obviously excited…}}}} Man: My name is Shelton Jones, sir. {{{{Smoke nods his head and makes a curious look on his face as he asks…}}}} Semore Smoke: Ok Mr. Jones. Now, why should I lone you money? Shelton Jones: Well, I need the money to buy my wife and I our dream house in the country. I’ve got a great steady job in town and I could pay you back within months. {{{{Smoke nods again and he grabs a piece of paper that is on the table and a pen as well and scribbles some information onto the paper…}}}} Semore Smoke: Ok, well, how much money are we talking here? A thousand maybe even two? {{{{Shelton pulls at his collar a bit as he now looks nervous…}}}} Shelton Jones: To be honest, I’m looking for about ten thousand dollars. {{{{Smoke looks surprised for a second and then writes down the figure on the paper…}}}} Semore Smoke: Ok… whatever then, now, do you think you can pay me within three months? We’re speaking the ten thousand plus twenty-five percent interest rate per month. So in three months, that’ll come up to… {{{{Smoke scribbles away with his math skills at the piece of paper, working with the fraction and then multiplying it by the gross number and then adding the final result to the ten thousand dollars…}}}} Semore Smoke: twelve thousand five hundred dollars, will you have that money by July 21 of this year? {{{{Shelton nods his head…}}}} Shelton Jones: Yes sir I will. I’ll give you the address of where I’ll be living then so you or your guys can come collect the money, or I could hand deliver it personally? Semore Smoke: No, I’d like to deliver it personally, I might want to see what my money bought you in the process. You do know though if you don’t have that money in three months what will happen, correct? {{{{Shelton shakes his head, but does nod his head in understanding for his next statement…}}}} Shelton Jones: I do know that it’ll be something that I won’t like, so I’ll be sure to have that much money in three months, I’ll be able to get a better spot in the office by then and I’ll be making that much in about a month’s time alone. {{{{Smoke nods his head and then slides the piece of paper over to the man and hands him the pen…}}}} Semore Smoke: Well, then I’ll just need your signature down here, and then you can have your money, it’s as simple as that. {{{{Shelton nods his head in excitement as he scribbles his signature to the piece of paper. Smoke meanwhile takes the key around his neck and takes it off the chain around his neck and then uses it to open up the safe. In the safe you see several wades of cash, enough money to make a grown man cry almost, and it almost makes Shelton Jones cry when he spots all the cash in the safe. Smoke grabs a few bills and counts them. He then hands them to Shelton who in turn snatches it from the table and puts the money in his pocket and then stands up sliding the paper back to Smoke in the process. Smoke looks at the paper and then stands up himself as he closes the safe and then puts the key back on its chain around his neck. Smoke then reaches out to shake Shelton’s hand and the two then shake. They then head towards the door to leave and Smoke speaks to him…}}}} Semore Smoke: Well, if you pay me on time, then I’ll be sure to remember you and you could get a discount on any money you borrow from me, but remember, borrowing from me ain’t like borrowing from the bank, if you don’t pay me, I’ll break your fucking legs and stick them straight up your ass. {{{{This makes Shelton’s grin fade away to a frown, but he then reassures Smoke that he’ll pay the money in three months, and as Smoke opens the door and the two exit, they shake hands yet again, and the man exits the building just like the man before him did. Smoke points to the next person in line and says with a grin on his face…}}}} Semore Smoke: Next? {{{{The scene fades as the next person in line enters the ring and Smoke closes the door behind him…}}}}
