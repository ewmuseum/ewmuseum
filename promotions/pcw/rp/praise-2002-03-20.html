Date: 20/3/02 Praise the LordPraise the Lord from the skies.Praise him high above the earth.Praise him all you angels.Praise him, all you armies of heaven.Praise him sun and moon.Praise him, all you shining stars. Psalm 148: 1-4 (*) What is it that keeps you going, allowing you to live your daily lies day to day. Whether you are a businessman or unemployed, rich or poor, you have something that makes you tick. For some it is a daily dose of caffeiene, others use alcohol or drugs, some people however live their daily lives on one fuel...God's love for his creation. The simple premise that Jesus died on the cross so that us humans can meet with God and praise him and talk to him is one that two billion people believe in. Prayer can be a daunting thing, how exactly do you address an omni-present God who you cant see? Do you talk as if talking to an old friend? How do you show the respect that God deserves? Everyone needs love, like sunlight love is something that keeps you living. Without love you are not living, you are existing. You love your friends, you love your family, you love God. Love is free to give but it is the greatest gift to recieve. The greatest thing you can ever learn in life is to love and be loved (**). 'All You Need is Love' is the lyric sung by the Beatles, when you love you live. In a world which today is full of hatred it can be hard to love, terrorist actions, murders, robbery, rape, mistrust, hate, conflict, war and many over evil things happen on this earth, but through it all love shines through. If everyone loved each other there would be no war, no hatred, no mistrust, surely this is what heaven must be like, except on an incomprable scale. In professional wrestling it could be argued that there is no love, a sport where you go and try to hurt someone should obviously be devoid of the most complex human emotion. 'You stuck by methrough thick and thinYou helped mewhen my world caved inyou motivated meto be a better personyou encouraged mewhen things seemed to worsennow I love youand you make me strongeri wish i could tell youand be alone no longer' (***) The love in wrestling might not be so obvious but it is there, the love of the fans, the love of the adrenaline, the love of the fame. This love is framed by a loneliness, a loneliness which Benjamin John Pailthorpe knows all too well. Every man needs a woman, Ben hasnt had a woman for three years. 'I wish you could seehow much I care about youI wish you would agreeplease dont bid me adieuJust say the wordand I would be by your sideno matter what had occuredi would be your guide through the darkness and strifewe would get through togetheri would give my own lifebecause i'll love you forever' (***) Below his tough exterior Benjamin John Pailthorpe is a sensitive caring man, although he may not show it he has a lot of humility for a sport where egos run rampant. He doesnt have confidence in his own ability, he doesnt blow his own trumpet, he only hopes and prays that his God given talent will be used wisely...and that there will be a crowbar nearby if something goes wrong. 'I need to find the strengthto tell you how i feeli could talk at lengthand for a few moments stealyour precious timeand make it my ownyour voice so sublimefor a few moments postponethe onset of lonelinessthe inset of hatei wish i could expressi wish it was our fate' (***) A life without love is not a life, Ben J. Pailthorpe is currently exisitng with the love from God. This love fills the huge void in his soul, but it does not comfort the feelings of longing for a female companion. As Ben goes into a match against Bobby Stormbringer, The Pharaoh and Big Daveylicious he has alot of thoughts running through his mind, and alot of burdens on his soul. Will he cope?
