Title: Outcheating PI?! IMPOSSIBLE! Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: Today Survival of the fittest. Primetime Championship Wrestling has long been controlled by the rules of Darwin. Only the biggest, nastiest, and hungriest survive, right? Wrong. For, there's something Darwin could have forseen developing. After all, when cornered, a mouse doesn't use a vicious wood twig shot to stun a snake, or an ant does not use a blade of grass to choke out opponents. In PCW, however, smaller craftier men have long kicked the crap out of tougher opponents with the infinite power of cheating. Hell, if Xavier Kannon cheats well enough at the PPV, he's your new PCW World Champion. As of late, the Pretty Cool Guys, seemingly clean cut young popstars, have embraced the ways of cheating happily, and seem to be attempting to formulate a plan for victory. Unfortunately, the Pretty Cool Guys have forgotten a simple maxim. NO ONE can outcheat Politically Incorrect. Ask the Cowabunga Connection. Heavy Metal Harry and Totally Tubular Tom still haven't fully recovered from Spring Fling, the pay-per-view in which Xavier Kannon helped Politically Incorrect retain the tag titles. It could certainly happen again. The politicians have connections far and wide. Further, there's a whole locker room full of PCW stars that, for the right price, would not be adverse to administering a timely interference on Politically Incorrect's behalf. Get ready..for at WrestleVessel, corruption looks to rule the day once more.. ::In their stateroom aboard the spectacular WrestleVessel itself, Politically Incorrect sit sequestered. They appear to be in here for the long haul, as indicated by the locked door, and the two plates of food in the room. One holds a perfectly cooked lobster, with claw cracker nearby. The other holds a sumptious filet mignon. For the time, the lobster is safe. However..the poor steak is not.. *Stab......slice......chomp-chomp...* :The stabber, slicer, and chomper is none other than William S. Powell, who is greedily devouring his meat, as Reginald stands, looking not at his lobster, but at Powell.: William S. Powell: Yum! Thish foodth ith greath! Reginald V. Lovecraft: For the love of Enron executives, CHEW AND SWALLOW YOUR FOOD BEFORE YOU SPEAK! Damn Northerners, no sense of manners.. ::William carefully swallows a particularly large piece of meat before answering: William S. Powell: Thank you, Miss Manners. Now, do we have any plans to discuss, or are you just going to sit here and critique my dining? Reginald V. Lovecraft: No, indeed, Mr. Powell, we DO have important business to discuss. It surrounds the PCW Tag Team Titles. William S. Powell: What a shock. It ALWAYS surrounds the tag titles! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yeah. I know, but that's because we are the dominant force in tag team wrestling in PCW, despite what Akiyama says. And why? William S. Powell: BECAUSE WE CHEAT LIKE HELL! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Exactly! We have gotten to where we are today through cheating, deception, and the Ultimate Authority. Yet, one must never forget: We WON the PCW Tag Team Titles against five other teams. And, in the end, my partner soared off the top rope onto the now-deceased Juan Borro with the most insane aerial maneuver in this industry, Election Day. And that was all it took...people underestimate us because of the Ultimate Authority connection...and those that have have paid dearly! William S. Powell: But, we are more than willing to cheat our way to victory. I know that, and our opponents know that. Unfortunately for the Pretty Cool Guys...they are going to have to come up with one hell of an elaborate plan to outcheat Politically Incorrect. As a certain Neo Void God might say, attempting to outcheat us is like fucking for virginity. Utterly, and completely pointless. ::Powell dives back into his filet, allowing his partner to speak, amidst loud cracks as Lovecraft breaks into his midday meal.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: You must remember, gentlemen, we've been to the dance before, so to speak. We've BEEN the tag team champions. We've been ready to do this for months, because we know how to do this. We know how to ascend the mountain, and stand at its peak. Experience truly is the best teacher. And the Pretty Cool Guys, as tag title virgins, are far too inexperienced to match skills and wits with Politically Incorrect. William S. Powell: In battle, and in politics, there are winners and losers. Winners are those who succeed, losers are those who do not. It is that simple. And, aboard this fine vessel, on Sunday, you two will discover that sad fact for yourselves. For, as our theme song says, we are seeking no truth, for winning is all. You may find that as grim, true, or real as you want, but one fact remains. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Election Day beckons, and, ON ELECTION DAY....POLITICALLY INCORRECT ALWAYS TRIUMPHS!
