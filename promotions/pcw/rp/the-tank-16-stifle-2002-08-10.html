The Tank 16 - Stifle

10 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 10, 2002 ﻿********* The Tank __16— ********* Stifle Boilingwhere water shouldn’t go.Lines wrinkle sharply and I crave your bloodlike nicotine. ~ Leigh Donovan Wednesday, August 7 - Day 34 “Where the hell have you been!?” Doug Hattoni exclaims as Jason Snow wanders into his fiveby nine cell, shortly after the morning roll call - there’s no sign of Doug’s cellmate, TimMcDonald. “They stuck me in the hole,” Snow says quietly, entering the cell. He looks tired from his threeday stay in the small lonely cell coupled with his post-match brawl last night on Turmoil. “Theygot me out in time to go to Turmoil.” “The hole?” Doug says, narrowing his eyes at Snow. “Don’t you fucking know how dangerous itis right now for you to abandon my side. Harvey Blake’s been itching to put a fucking blade inmy back for two weeks now, and you, my main muscle, go and get yourself locked in thehole!” “Hey!” Snow snaps back at Hattoni, stunning the fat old man. “You may be able to talk toeveryone else in this fucking place as you please, but when you’re talking to me, youwatch your God damn mouth!” Obviously, Snow is still upset over the state he saw his sister-in-law in just a few days ago. No one, not even Doug Hattoni, is welcome to cross his line today. Doug Hattoni, however, isn’t used to being spoken to like this - even when the man speaking isJason Snow. “You ungrateful bastard!” Hattoni fires at Snow, fanning the flames in the youngerman’s eyes. “I’m keeping you alive in here, and you have the nerve to tell me to watchmy mouth?” Hattoni is lucky - Snow is only a split second away from snapping on the old man and slappinghim in his chubby old face. He thinks better of it and rubs his face in an attempt to calmhimself. Hattoni realizes that Snow should be calmed too, before something unpleasant happens. “Hey,”Doug Hattoni says gently, putting his hand on Snow’s massive right arm. “Don’t worry about it -I’m just blowing off steam. These are stressful times in here, kid. As much as I hate to admit it,Harvey Blake’s more dangerous than any hot-headed bastard I’ve ever taken on.” Snow wipes a bit of angry sweat from his brow as he cools himself. “He’s always a threat, kid,” Hattoni says quietly as he sits down on his bottom bunk. “And asmuch faith as I have in my boys - without you around, I’m a sitting fucking duck. And ifanything happens to me, all hell’s going to break lose in this fucking place. I’m the only thingkeeping the whites and the blacks from killing each other off in here, you know. If I go, it’sgoing to be a fucking power struggle like you wouldn’t believe.” Snow knows that Doug’s circling the main issue - drugs. Doug brings drugs into the Tank, andthat’s the lifeline of the place. Everyone keeps the peace in fear of being cut off from theirdrugs, or cutting off a dangerous man from his. “You’ve just got to watch your step, kid,” Doug says to Snow gently. “Just sit tight until I giveyou a job to do. Until then, keep your nose clean. That way, everything will be alright.” *** Prisoner Profile File Photo: Coming Soon...Name: Jake Joseph HooperPrisoner Number: 690009Nickname(s): noneRace: White Born: 02-01-1978Height: 6'2Weight: 215Hair: NoneEyes: BlueFacial Hair: noneTattoo(s): Various White Supremacy symbolsBuild: muscularFrame: largeIdentifying Characteristics: Rather large, no visible hair on body, various Aryan tattoos.Current Sentence(s): 2002 - Murder (Sentenced to forty years, up for parole in eighteen)Previous Convictions: 2000 - Assault (Served thirty days in prison)Family: none From the Office of the Warden “Jake Hooper isn’t like the rest of these stupid motherfuckers running around with HarveyBlake,” sneers the Warden. “This guy is the real deal. He’s just like Blake. Before he came tothe Tank, he believed in all the Nazi bullshit that he’s accepted for here. In fact, that’s whatlanded him here in the first place.” “In May of 2000, Jake Hooper was involved in a white on black brawl in LA. From that pointforward, he was recognized as one of the leaders in the White Supremacy movement. He waswilling to do anything for his beliefs... even murder.” “And that’s why he’s here. A couple of months ago Jake killed a black barber in the middle ofhis shop - in broad daylight. Was it a prank? A joke? Some demented political statement? Idon’t know, and I don’t give a damn. But until he’s a middle-aged Nazi, he’s myproblem.” *** Thursday, August 8 - Day 35 Over the last few days, Jake Hooper has become Harvey Blake’s right hand man. He’s smart,strong, and conniving enough to perfect any task that Harvey Blake might throw at him. He’sstill new to the Tank, newer even than Jason Snow, but he quickly learned that he would fit rightin with Harvey Blake’s White Nation. “Snow’s going to Fusion tomorrow night,” Jake tells Harvey’s men as they sit in the cafeteriaeating today’s choice of slop and tasteless bites. Harvey sits among them, watching his newfound soldier speak - he feels so proud. He wishes young Jake were his own son sometimes. The young man seems so driven. “Hattoni’s going to be sure to get his daily shit taken care ofbefore Snow leaves the prison.” “So we have to get Snow stuck back in the hole,” says Harvey with a smile. “The more time wecan get him away from Doug Hattoni, the better.” “What’s so fucking special about this punk?” asks one of the skinheads. “I mean, Christ, he’sjust one man. How can he stand up to us?” “He can’t,” says Harvey with a scowl. “But that doesn’t mean he can’t break a couple of ournecks and cause enough commotion to get the whole fucking mafia up our asses before we canget a hand on Hattoni. Think, damn it! Any other fucking mobster Doug can pick as abodyguard, we can take. But he knows that. That’s why that fucking lion is around.” “Right,” says Jake Hooper. “We all know Snow’s not indestructible, but I’d much rather takemy chances with any one else in Hattoni’s clan than the one who kicks ass for a living. Andremember, we have to keep it quiet. The quicker and easier the better. Snow won’t let either ofthose things happen, so we have to get the fucker out of the way.” “How are we going to get him sent back to the hole?” asks another anonymous Nazi. “Step on his toes,” says Harvey Blake, his lone blue eye fixed on Jake Hooper. “Since he gothere, we’ve been watching our step around him. Well fuck that. One of us is going to get ourass stomped, and not fight back while it’s going on. Whoever that man is though, will be greatlyrewarded.” “And then,” Jake smiles back at his mentor, allowing the older one-eyed Nazi to finish. “Hattoni’s all ours.” *** From the Office of the Warden “A feared man is a hated man,” starts the Warden. “Why do you think so many people havebeen trying disprove the existence of God for the last millennium or two? People fear God, andpeople don’t like to fear things. People then tend to get rid of those things that they fear - theyeliminate them. In God’s case, the only way to eliminate something you can’t see, smell, taste,or hear, is to proclaim that it doesn’t exist.” “However, in the Tank, the things you fear are real - very real. You can see them... you cansmell them... you can taste them... you can hear them... The prisoners in the Tank don’t have theoption of proving that their fears don’t exist - so they use other means ofelimination.” *** Snow sits at one end of the empty chess board and stares blankly down at the pieces, his eyesovertaken by the images in his mind. The most prominent image is his last sight of Krista,sitting on the opposite side of the cracked glass, staring blankly and stoned at him. There wasnothing innocent about her in that moment. And now as his mind traces her etched features, hecan still find none. “Hey big guy,” a voice breaks his horrid thought - almost in a rescuing gesture. When Snowlooks up, however, he finds that the intentions of the speaker were far from saving him from hisown thoughts. “Want to have a game?” Snow is silent as he glares up at Jake Hooper. All the skinheads look alike to Snow. With theirshaved heads and Nazi tattoos, the only thing that really separates them is their current amountof facial hair. Jake Hooper has no facial hair. In fact, Snow can’t see a single hair on any exposed part of theyoung Nazi’s body - not even eyebrows. Physically, Jake is rather impressive by every daystandards. He’s tall, muscular, and moves with a confidence that suggests he knows how tohandle himself in physical situations. “You don’t want a game?” Jake asks with mock politeness. “I’m just looking for something todo, man. It’s fucking boring in here, you know, and half these motherfuckers are to stupid toknow this board is for anything other than checkers. So come on - lets have a game.” “I’m not in the mood,” Snow says before glancing over his shoulder at the pool table. As hesuspected, Harvey Blake and his assorted Nazi companions are watching with smirks andchuckles. “Not in the mood?” Jake asks, leaning back in his seat. He knows Harvey Blake is taking noteof his bravery in this situation. Jason Snow has quickly made a reputation as one of the mostdangerous men in the Tank, and Jake is luring him into a fight. “How can you not be in themood?” Snow is silent but his eyes are locked on the young Nazi in front of him. “What else are you going to do to pass the time?” Jake asks, his tone growing angry. “It’sfucking prison, man. Ain’t like you got better shit to do! Unless you’re planning to go see thatcute little girl again.” Jake follows up the statement with a smile that he knows will crawlbeneath Snow’s skin and burrow into his brain. “What the hell are you talking about!?” Snow says quietly, yet harshly. “You know,” Jake chuckles, “the cute little thing that I saw you with in the visitation room. Andif I may ask, do you happen to know where she got whatever shit she was on? I tell you, man - Ihave to get me some of that!” By now, Snow knows exactly what Jake Hooper is trying to do. He’s trying to set Snow off -make him explode. This only makes Snow angrier. He wants to make Jake reap what he sews. “I know what you’re doing here,” Snow says sternly as he stands up from the table,simultaneously kicking his stool away. “But what you don’t know is what you’re gettingyourself into. You fucked up now, Nazi!” Snow grabs reaches across the table and grabs the young skinhead’s denim vest. Just before hethrows a punch, however, Doug Hattoni’s raspy voice booms throughout the Unit. “Snow!” Every man in the Unit turns his attention to the fat Italian standing outside his cell on the cellbalcony - every man but Snow. The angry young lion doesn’t turn away from Jake, though hisfist never does lash out. “Fuck!” Snow screams in frustration, turning away from Jake after kicking the table over. In afurious tear, he stomps up the stairs toward Doug Hattoni’s cell where he’ll no doubt be scoldedfor his short fuse. Leaning on the pool table, Harvey Blake glares up at Doug Hattoni’s cell with utter contempt. It’s only a matter of time, he thinks. He says quietly to his companions, “Snow can’t protect thefat bastard forever. We’ll get him.”
