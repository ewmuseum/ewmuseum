I'm no stranger to the rain.

7 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/7/02 -Ostrich skin boots sit high on top of a desk. They belong to the man, who’s kicked back in his chair wearing black jeans, a black Johnny Cash T-shirt with John flashing the finger, chrome shades, and a black Stetson hat. The man is Duke Williams who has spent the last four hours fueling up on cocaine and whiskey. A cigar sits from his lip as he talks on the phone.- Williams: Yea the first kid is about 21 standing 6’3 and weighs about 239…….yea…….electric torture is what I had in mind……HA he thinks he’s a bad ass now……no problem………ok hoss. -Duke takes a sip from the bottle in front of him.- Williams: The other guy’s age is approximately 34 years and the perp will be with a young girl with a bad attitude…..ahh he’s about 6’2….265 pounds….burnt face………..yea ugly as sin………execute him on site…..ok…………the other kid is young about 26 preppy, 6’2………about 230……….leave him alone……..really…………….ok Winston thanks…………..bye. -Duke hangs up the phone and stares into the camera.- Williams: I'm no stranger to the rain. I'm a friend of thunder. Friend, is it any wonder lightning strikes me. I've fought with the devil, Got down on his level, But I never gave in, so he gave up on me. -Duke rubs his thumb against his nose.- Williams: I'm no stranger to the rain. I can spot bad weather, And I'm good at finding shelter in a downpour. I've been sacrificed by brothers, Crucified by lovers, But through it all I withstood the pain. I'm no stranger to the rain. -Duke ashes his smoke.- Williams: Its too bad Keith Whitely drank himself to death. It’s too bad I don’t get the respect that’s owed to me. It’s too bad that some of the kids of today have given in to the devil and his ways. My army is growing through misperception and parallel direction. The dark kids have more in common with me then they will ever know. Nobody believes it, but I am pure evil. We both demand respect. We both do what ever it takes to accomplish what we need to get accomplished. Darkstar, Clyde the only difference between me and you guys. I deserve the respect, while you kids think it should just fall on you. That’s b*llshit! -Duke smiles into the camera- Williams: Has society carried out injustices towards you and your people? Yea they have! -Duke starts to clap his hands and laugh.- Williams: HA HA HA and I applaud them. Freaks, dark hippies, scum, abnormalities, are all words that describe you humps to a tee. Should we in fact feel sorry for you? F*ck no. Should we pity you? Not in this lifetime. Should we outcast you and alienate from the rest of us? Damn right! Should we hang, mutilate, and systematically wipe out these freaks? Hell yes. What do they offer us as a human race? -Duke rubs his beard pondering the loaded question.- Williams: Does Clyde really contribute anything meaningful and productive to our society? Is Darkstar really going to add or season our lives? Then why do we keep them around? -Duke slams his fist on the desk.- Williams: You drug me on my show, slap my half their body around, and ruin my match? Who the f*ck do you kids think you are? HUH? Do you know who your messing with. Clyde you ain’t never met a motherfucker like me. I’ll get down on your level. Hell I’ll go deeper. I’ll go sicker. I’ll get more twisted then a then a game of twister with the Manson family. WEEEEEEEEEEEEE let the games being hoss. Chalk one up for the Clydesdale. But now it’s the Malice Man’s turn. Pain, suffering, torture…round one Alex’s there is the daily double. Lets bet it all. Bet it on black. -Duke takes off his chrome shades and his dark brown apache eyes make you feel what is coming.- Williams: Bethany you cute little plumb you. Damn how did you know I like it rough. Now a lot of ladies have tried a lot of different techniques to get that 13 inches in there hands but that was the hottest. If only you were just a tad older I’d bend you over and do you Malice Style while you squeezed my sack. -Duke likes his fingers and makes a hand motion like he’s tuning a radio and playing with some nipples.- Williams: I’ll keep your number on file honey. Ummm the way you jabbed me that was hot, hot, hot. Clyde you lucky dog you. You may be a sick freak but hoss you got a honey. -Duke flicks his tongue in the air.- Williams: Oh and I’m not forgetting you Talena. No. Your one fine piece of ass!! 5’8 just the right size. Ladies love out laws like babies love stray dogs. So that explains why Bethany is with her stray dog but what’s your story? Why don’t you drop the freak and bring it over to the sheik Malice Man. I’d treat you right honey. I’d love to take you behind closed doors. I have to admit its going to be hard to concentrate if you’re at ring side. Oh yea it will be hard. -Duke winks at the camera.- Williams: You like it rough too hossette? Do ya? IF you don’t and you keep hanging around with your dark scum friends you may find it rough. Beauty only takes you so far then my Malice has to kick in. Its called survival. Get out of the picture, join me, or feel the wrath of Malice. It’s that simple. You cross my path I’ll break those hot legs of yours. -Duke puts his shades back on.- Williams: So that leaves us to a so called legend. -Again Duke puts a thumb to his nose.- Williams: In the industry of making a legend. That is what I get out of Tommy’s name. Very selfish and egotistical if you ask me. That’s something you earn not manufacture. He can win all the tournaments and titles he wants. The fact is he’s not the real deal. I’m not saying I am but at least I have a storied career behind me and a bright further in the coming years. -Duke spits into a bucket.- Williams: But I need to put that aside. What is that? Maybe jealousy, maybe envy, or maybe just anger. Kinda like when you see Rosie O’Donnell or Carrot Top on tv. That unexplained rage that goes down your spine. That’s what I have. But not this Tuesday. No. This Tuesday he is my partner. I will respect the kid as my partner. He needs to hold his end of the deal thou. He can’t slack off or act like a rookie. I ain’t got time to bleed hoss. -Duke smiles as he holds up the peace sign.- Williams: Turmoil Tommy and Duke will send the dark jobbers back home Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound and hoss that’s the honky tonk truth!! -The camera fades on Duke’s logo of the flying w.- =w=
