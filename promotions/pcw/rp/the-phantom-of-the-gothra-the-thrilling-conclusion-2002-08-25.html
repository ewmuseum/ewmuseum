The Phantom Of The Gothra-The Thrilling Conclusion!

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-25-2002 ..::/::..When we last left our vile villain, he was exploring the depths of the gothic novelty store known as 'Warm Subject' looking to embrace and further understand the mysteries of gothic culture in an attempt to be prepared for the grueling No DQ, Continental Title 3 way between himself, the vicious Darkstar, and the hardened criminal, Jason Snow. Unfortunately, his plans to a drastic turn when his beautiful interest, Brandi Wilson, disappeared shortly via trap door after stepping into a changing room to try on one of the 'Warm Subject' outfits. Unbeknownst of what awaited him below, our vile villain showed shades of his old heroic self and took the long plummet down the trap door in a desperate attempt to recover Brandi and find out who it was that kidnapped her. With that said, we return to our story as we find none other than PCW's only villain, Rent-A-Villain, proving gravity right as he plummets what seems to be endlessly down a dark corridor, his clear pants and his streaked hair flapping in the wind as he folds his arms, almost bored by the continuous plummet downward as he looks to the camera and comments..::/::..Rent-A-Villain:'BY THE INFOMERCIALS OF STACKER 2, IT'S ABOUT TIME YOU GUYS CAME BACK!! You realize I've been plummeting for the past few hours? This 'to be continued' stuff isn't all it's cracked up to be ya know!' ..::/::..His long plummet finally comes to a halt, our hero finally hitting what seems to be bottom as he crashes into an unknown expanse of water, splashing violently up in the air as Rent-A-Villain shoots up, soaked from head to toe as he looks about at his new surroundings. Looking about, our vile villain comes to find he is within a strange expanse of catacombs somewhere under the mall, waterways flowing every which way and torches dimly illuminating stone pathways throughout this strange expanse. Rent-A-Villain trudges through the cold, glacial water as his body shivers angrily, the vile villain pulling himself up on one of the stone walkways and shaking his head, water sprinkling from his hair as he continues to look about..::/::..Rent-A-Villain:'GREAT CATACOMBS OF CATWOMAN'S CURVATURE!! There are hidden catacombs under The City mall....but what could possibly be down here? Some type of intergalactic alligator monsters? Kool-Aid web shooting spiders?! Or perhaps beautiful Amazon women with fish gills....mmmmmm....fish gilled Amazon women....WHOA WHOA WHOA, mind on business Renty. Where do I go?!' ..::/::..Rent-A-Villain turns to his left, shaking a little more water out of his hair as he walks over to a fork in this particular stone walkway, two archways before him with two respective signs for each. One reads,'To Safety-Smart People, Black People In Horror Movies, and Virgin's this way.' The other reads respectively,'To Danger-Dumb Blonde's, Scooby-Doo and His Gang, And Confused Superheros Turned Supervillains Looking For Women this way.' Rent-A-Villain looks to the right archway, placing his hands on his hips and brandishing a bright smile before exclaiming..::/::..Rent-A-Villain:'I GUESS THAT'S ME!! ONWARD!!' ..::/::..With that, Rent-A-Villain wastes no precious time as he traverses down the dark corridor, very little light presenting itself as Rent-A-Villain squints, slowly continuing down. It is not long before the eerie silence within the tunnel is shortly destroyed....replaced in favor of cheesy organ music which grows increasingly louder and louder as he continues down the corridor, our vile villain noticing light towards what looks to be the end of it as he quickens his pace..::/::.. Rent-A-Villain:'Dark corridors....chessy organ music....torches....secret passageways....WHERE DO YOU ORDER THIS STUFF FROM?! I've been looking in 'villain home improvement catalogs' all over for this stuff! I wonder if he'll give me the name of his decorator after I thrash him with my SUPER SAIEN STRIKES O' GO....I MEAN EEEEEEEEEEEEEEVIL-NESS!!' ..::/::..Reaching the end, Rent-A-Villain is in utter awe of a grand circular room reminiscent of a traditional Greek theater, the marble pillars of the theater decrepit and cracked and the floors warped and browning with age. The walls are decorated with what look to be hand-painted drawings of dragons, tigers, and other elegant yet strong creatures littering the wall, adding an antique beauty to the worn theater. The cheesy organ music continues to play, it's location unknown, but Rent-A-Villain seems unconcerned with it's whereabouts as he fixates his eyes upon what he came for....the lovely Brandi Wilson, tied by her hands and dwindling from a pillar at the top of the cracked and worn stairs before Rent-A-Villain. Her clothes have been altered, Brandi in a black lace, gothic style dress with the cleavage low on her tight and well-built body as she looks down to her villainous other, calling out to him with her soothing voice..::/::.. Brandi:'RENTY!! UP HERE!'Rent-A-Villain:'I'm coming Brandi, how did you end up in this appalling predicament? And where did you get that dress from? You know it is illegal to shop lift oh fair Brandi....'..::/::..Brandi rolls her eyes, Rent-A-Villain quickly scaling the stairs as he approaches the marble pillar, getting a close look at the rope tied to a hook on the pillar which keeps her bound up like a kite in the high wind. Quickly, Rent-A-Villain unties the knot in the hook, Brandi letting out a slight scream as she quickly plummets from her dangling position, but our vile villain is there to catch her in his strong arms as she puts her arms about his neck, brushing back a strand of her soft brown hair and flashing a smile to her savior as she says..::/::..Brandi:'My hero....I mean villain.' Rent-A-Villain:'And don't you forget it! Now what happened?' Brandi:'Well...I was getting changed and about to try on that outfit, when suddenly the door fell out from under me....I landed in this weird waterway and I got snatched up by some guy in all black and he knocked me out...' Rent-A-Villain:'WITH WHAT?! CHLOROFORM?! NOXIOUS GAS?! BRUTE FORCE??!' Brandi:'Nah....that cheesy organ music, after a little while it reeaaaaaaaaally makes ya just want to go to sleep. Anyways, he said his name was...'Evil Voice:'THE PHANTOM....OF THE GOTH-RA!!! **!!!THUNDER STRIKES!!! WOMAN SCREAMS!!! R.KELLY RAIDS A PLAYGROUND!!!!** ..::/::..Brandi and Rent-A-Villain look about for that weird sound sequence, but that search stops as they look over to the classic church pipe organ where they find a fit man in black slacks, a dark silk vest, and a black mask....his piercing dark eyes looking down upon out two protagonists with his arms folded and the organ music picking up in pace, raging on non-stop in the background..::/::..Phantom:'Welcome to my lair said the spider to the ladybug...' Brandi:'Don't you mean 'fly'?' Phantom:'Oh yeah...sorry, I always mes....I MEAN, SILENCE!!! So, you two now know the secret of the PHANTOM OF THE GOTHRA!!' **!!!THUNDER STRIKES!!! WOMAN SCREAMS!!! BILLY GUNN HAS A BORING MATCH!!!!**Rent-A-Villain:'CUT IT OUT!!' ..::/::..Our vile villain angrily picks up a loose rock on the ground, hurling it upwards off screen as 'OW' can be heard shortly, Rent-A-Villain brushing off his hands in a job well done before saying..::/::.. Rent-A-Villain:'Continue...' Phantom:'So...I know what you must be thinking, 'Why am I inhabiting these catacombs'...well..' Rent-A-Villain:'Actually, I was wondering why you've got that dumb organ music playing in the background...' Brandi:'You couldn't find any Robert Miles or some Creed?' Phantom:'SILENCE, I PAID $14.99 FOR 'THE BEST OF BORING ORGAN MUSIC'...I'LL PLAY IT WHENEVER I FEEL LIKE IT! NOW, as I was saying, when the whole 'Goth' revolution came along...I thought I would have fit into it perfectly. I saw the Crow more then 30 times.....I have BURNT copies of the Misfits CD's.....I EVEN STOLE MY SISTER'S MAKE UP SO I COULD PAINT MY FACE WHITE AND BLACK!! But noooooooo...I was never goth enough....so I secluded myself to these catacombs under The City, searching for another who I could share my lonely, not-Goth-enough days with....that and spying on the girls trying on bikini's in Gadzooks, I mean hey, I gotta take advantage of these catacombs somehow. UM UM, anyways, I've been under this same mall for...' Rent-A-Villain:'LOOK, WHAT DO YOU THINK THIS IS, A JOHN CRICHTON RP?! I DON'T HAVE 20 PARTS TO WASTE ON YOUR WHOLE BACKSTORY, THIS IS A TWO-PARTER YOU KNOW?! Can't you just tell us your evil plan, try to kill us, let me foil you, and we can FADE this thing out so I can get some sleep?' ..::/::..The Phantom Of The Gothra storms over to Rent-A-Villain, throwing a tiny temper tantrum before the PCW's vile villain like a little schoolgirl, completely breaking his 'goth' character and exclaiming..::/::..Phantom:'WHAT IS WRONG WITH YOU SUPERHEROES THESE DAYS?! You show NO patience anymore for the obligatory 'long-winded villainous speech'! Well fine....I BET YOU I WILL DEFEAT YOU!!' ..::/::..With a cocky swagger, Rent-A-Villain replies with the classic, yet childish, line in this situation, Brandi watching on a both men and shaking her head in disdain of the childish antics...::/::..Rent-A-Villain:'I bet you won't... Phantom:'WILL!!' Rent-A-Villain:'WON'T!!' Phantom:'WILL!!' Rent-A-Villain:'WON'T!!' Phantom:'WILL WILL WILL WILL!!' Rent-A-Villain:'WON'T WON'T WON'T WON'T!!' Phantom:'WILL TIMES INFINITY!!!!' Rent-A-Villain:'WON'T TIMES INFINTY PLUS ONE SMARTY PANTS!!!!'Phantom:'I KNOW YOU ARE BUT WHAT AM I?!' Rent-A-Villain:'TAKES ONE TO KNOW ONE!' ..::/::..This childish exchange is mercifully ended by a swift punch to the Phantom's jaw by Brandi, the Phantom's eyes crossing before he limply falls to the concrete. Brandi checks her nails quickly, making sure she didn't break one, then looking to Rent-A-Villain and saying..::/::..Brandi:'That Tae Bo does do wonders...anyways, can we go now?'Rent-A-Villain:'BRANDIIIIIII!! HOW COULD YOU DO THAT?! I WAS GONNA GET HIM WITH 'I'M RUBBER YOU GLUE, WHATEVER YOU SAY BOUNCES OFF ME AND STICKS TO YOU!' NO ONE COMES BACK AFTER THAT ONE!!' ..::/::>.Rent-A-Villain pouts, folding his arms and standing disappointed he couldn't spout off his comeback as Brandi simply giggles, taking Rent-A-Villain by the hand and asking..::/::.. Brandi:'Can we just go home and play PS2?'Rent-A-Villain:'You know the way to a Kryptonian's heart my dear....I wonder which one of these catacombs leads to Babbages though. Reserve a copy of Smackdown 4....OH NO NO NO, NOT IF I SNATCHER ME ONE!!'Brandi:'Your so sexy when you speak about inane subjects.' Rent-A-Villain:'Why thank you...it takes years of practice.' ..::/::..With that, Brandi and Rent-A-Villain walk down the stairs, exiting the decrepit theater and leaving the Phantom fo the Gothra laid out upon the steps as the two head back home, the mystery of the Phantom of the Gothra solved and this story proving one day.....we REALLY are lacking QUALITY villains in the year 2002! FIND OUT NEXT TIME WHEN RENT-A-VILLAIN DOES THE SAME THING HE DOES EVERY RP...TRY TO TAKE OVER THE WORLD....ONE DUMB SCHEME AT A TIME!!
