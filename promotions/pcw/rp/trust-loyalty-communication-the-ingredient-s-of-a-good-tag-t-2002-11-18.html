Trust, Loyalty, Communication (The Ingredient's Of A Good Tag Team)

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18 - 11 - 02 It was only days ago when Gaz 'The Jester' Green defeated and humiliated Eddie Mandrake in the first round of the Cruiserweight Championship Tournament. Setting a new record in the process, Gaz now has the fruitful bout waiting against former nemesis, Cowabunga Connection's Heavy Metal Harry. From there Gaz may advance to Alliances where a ladder match awaits in the final. The biggest Pay-Per-View of their lives will see Pranks With Attitude defend their Tag Team Championships in a fatal four-way elimination match, the first bout of the gauntlet. From defending the gold, Nerezza will embark in an Alamo match in a bid to make 'Rezz Bull' the officially endorsed product of PCW before re-aligning with Gaz Green for the Main Event. However the Main Event personifies the Pay-Per-View as the roster unites into teams in what could best be described as pure carnage. From all four corners of Wrestle Tattler, the cream of the crop will align. The best of RUSH, FSW and OSW will slug it out with the rest of PCW, with over 60 combatants, 15 teams and 5 rings, but through this chaos one notable entity will hope to be crowned victorious. Gaz 'The Jester' Green, Noscosto Ireul Nerezza, Vilas Lavagna and Skitzo. TEAM BASTARDOS~! Returning to the command post of Primetime Championship Wrestling's Pranks With Attitude we are on the brink of yet another caper involving the current Tag Team Champions as they embark on what could possibly the most eventful week in their short tenure with the company. 'We lack something squire?!?' murmured the distinctive voice of Gaz Green. The scene opening within the confines of the pranksters apartment, a spectacle in the heart of his hometown of Las Vegas Nevada, better known as the City Of Sin, and for obvious reasons. Gaz peered from his balcony at the hoard of entertainment venues, casino's, bars, lap dancing clubs, a losers paradise thought the Emerald Menace as swarms of people flooded each in turn, leaving one for another. Many of these people loved gambling and its adrenalin rush, others loved scantily clad women baring all but the Jester got his kicks from misery, the misery of others. 'You must really hate people with a passion, but why?' questioned the other half of this Conniving Collective, Noscosto Ireul Nerezza with a hiss as he sat watching his partners incoherent state. 'People are like drones, power hungry, back-stabbing, self centred-....' answered the Lord Of Laughter before an untimely interruption from that of Nerezza. 'It's human nature but as you listed those negative characteristics I generally thought you were describing the attributes of Xavier Kannon. Hubbardville's little boy was hungered by that of power but now he has sole control of PCW there isn't anything left to accomplish and if he should recapture the World Heavyweight Championship then he'd be complete-....' 'Well I know he'll let the rest of the Front Office take the rap, back stabbing is part of his genetic make-up, they call it Scientology!' 'And-....' continued the Jester, stalling he allows Nerezza to intervene and voice his concerns with regards to his partner who has recently acquired possibly two of these stated attributes. 'Gaz I'm worried!' The Jester merely peers at his colleague dumbfounded. 'Greed and power go hand in hand and if you should be victorious in this Cruiserweight Tournament then you may be laden in gold, providing we retain in the opening match!' 'Hold on a second. I never once asked to be in this Cruiserweight Championship Tournament, and see this is an opportunity to prove the critics wrong!' retorted Gaz firmly. The tension builds and negativity swamped the room, almost choking both parties, certainly thunder has struck paradise. From Nerezza's point of view his long term friend has succumb to greed, the dark side if you would while Gaz looks upon this as an act of jealousy. Walking into the most important event thus far, and defying the word 'Alliance' Pranks With Attitude are having trust issues, something that would need to be dealt with before they try to defy further odds, namely the Tag Team title defence scheduled for the Pay-Per-View. '.... Gaz, what critics?' said the inquisitive vampire. 'Let's face it everybody thinks I'm the supporting player of Pranks With Attitude and Nerezza has carried me from day one, so I see this tournament as an opportunity to go it alone and win the BIG ONE!!!' replied the Prince Of Pranks as he prepared mentally for the task at hand, in this case the second round of this tournament. 'WAIT!!! This has been a team effort, no supporting players or team captains but one unity that has seen us through The Front Office gauntlet, why care what others think?' roared the Angel Of Fear. 'WHERE DO YOUR PRIORITIES LIE?' Gaz turned to that of a stuttering mess, mouth ajar, the Jester struggled to build a sentence and this only infuriated his partner further as the end looked nigh for two members of the Trickster Team. Eyes flamed over, teeth grinded almost to his gums, Nerezza looked a picture of built up anger as he patiently waited for Gaz Green's response, a question that has rocked the very foundations of Pranks With Attitude. 'My priorities lie with us retaining the Tag Team titles!' finally murmured the comic. Gone has his jovial expression, in its place a look of concern as the days pass by, Alliances2K2 looming in what looks to be a make or break situation. The answer appeared to sit well with the brooding vampire but the time of response was the real disturbance and as Nerezza prepared to question his associate further the apartment door creaked open unveiling the eye candy that is Natalia Miyagawa. Standing in a stern, yet feminine pose, Natalia had obviously heard the best part of this discussion. Enraged maybe a little excessive, the Russian beauty was certainly disgusted by the low morals of the P.W.A camp, something she has put time and effort into, which should entitle her to a say. 'So do I get too have a say in this matter?' snarled Natalia, her head swaying over both that of Gaz Green and Nerezza. 'This has nothing to do with you!' retaliated the Living Nightmare, feeling Natalia's contribution is insignificant. 'Really - Well I thought we were discussing the Tag Team titles and stop me if I'm wrong but didn't I play a part in you securing them from the grasp of Playaz Inc?' injected the the seperated wife of former PCW competitor Alexander Miyagawa. 'Err-... Arr-... Well-... Hmm-...' a stuttering response was all Gaz and Nerezza could manage as they felt the wrath of their female valet, who feels both have displayed sef-centred attitudes. 'GROW UP, HUH?!?' 'This is a team and Alliances should be the catalyst in strengthening this team but you'd rather squabble between one another, if it isn't over gold, then its proving points!' cried the recently acquired manager towards her clients. Both members of Pranks With Attitude however, take this in, soaking in every word spoken and begin to look solemn. Dwelling on the facts, Natalia is indeed right and that Alliances is all about opportunity but the main event should be the fatal four-way elimination match and not some soda endorsement or Cruiserweight Tournament final but tightening their strangle hold of the Tag Team division. 'Ahem-…' coughed the PCW diva, indicating for the dynamic duo to call a truce. 'It's water under the bridge!' voiced the Jester. 'No hard feelings-...' concluded Nerezza, but Natalia however wanted a little more assurance, hinting for the pair to bury the hatchet, not in one anothers backs but by a friendly hug. 'Well-....' 'Can't we just shake hands, it was a misunderstanding, not a gay rights protest~!' joked the former NTICW comedian with a grin, colour finally filtering into his cheeks and looking close to the man he once was. With a hoard of hysterics from the three-man party as they looked at the lighter side of this escaladed problem the episode drew to a close and P.W.A's future may very well have been cemented by that of an outsider, Natalia Miyagawa.
