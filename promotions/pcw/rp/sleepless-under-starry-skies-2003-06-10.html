Sleepless under Starry Skies

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.10.03 His feet slapped noisily on the dry cement as he rounded the pool while his nose quickly grew accustomed to the hovering chlorine aroma rolling off the top of the clear water. Despite the white towel held fast to his shoulder and the black swim trunks pulled to just below his navel, he had no intention on diving in. His mind was elsewhere, furiously working into the late hours of the night. Non-stop, it’s pace only quickening and not taking a break for sleep. That was why he found himself wandering poolside on the first deck of the airship at 3:00 in the morning. The sundeck was deserted with the exception of an attendant at the juice bar who glanced up at Silvio for a brief second before returning to his task of scrubbing invisible smudges from the spotless white countertop. Boring late night work but someone had to do it, Silvio supposed. Solace turned out to be in the arms of a large cream-colored tanning chair, fully extended and facing outwards to the night sky. Oh, what a beautiful night sky it was. The stars randomly dotted the sky and twinkled, offering their little glimmers of hope with extra gusto. The moon sat nearly straight above where Silvio lay down, hanging low in the sky and appearing larger than ever and letting its warm yellow glow fall gently to the sundeck. If the hanging lights of the sundeck had been turned off, the moon would have naturally lit the area beautifully. An uncontrollable sigh slipped through his lips as he stretched out and stared up at the night sky. His mind, keeping with its mile-a-minute pace, began flaring with ideas and sending quirky little ideas and analogies racking his mind. Amazing… The sight WAS amazing. Perhaps even more amazing was the simple fact that Silvio had seen it a million times and then some during his lifetime but it never failed to take his breath away. What was amazing was how the setting had taken place. He was on the exclusive airship of the Primetime Championship Wrestling as a WRESTLER, not as another on-looker, reporter, or hopeful. Well, maybe not so amazing. Hell, like they could’ve prevented me from getting boarded on if I really wanted to. A little added bonus never hurts, though. A random tidbit flashes in his mind, some worthless scientific general knowledge, and disappeared as quickly as it came. “The light from the nearest star takes about 4 years to reach Earth. Heavens, for all we know, the stars we look at in the sky at night could have gone out over 3 years ago!” said his elementary science teacher. Man, his mind was a raging machine. But it made perfect sense, in his mind. It was the perfect analogy… somehow. Dorn Tracer is not the true astronomer of the PCW, I am! It’s so obvious; it’s right in front of our faces! Good thing it wasn’t a snake, I would be chock full of venom right now! Matt Jackson may be a ‘star,’ but he went out a long time ago! But his light still shines down to earth. He lives on that light, but he’s not producing anything new. He’s got no solid grounding! He’s just composed of harmless rays of light, he’s burnt out, he’s nothing new, and he’s nothing new! Okay, perhaps his mind was more tired than he’d realized. But it made perfect sense. Matt Jackson had been performing lesser than what he had once done. A Rising Star title ‘reign’, if you dare call it a reign, that most thought would have lasted forever, ended abruptly after only three days. He’d lost to Abner, another rookie within the federation. Gunshot wound or not, ‘THE AGENT’ Matt Jackson never lost to a rookie! …Or did he? Oh yes, he did. And he will again on Fusion! Fusion… The thought was on his mind again. Maybe it was pre-match jitters of a severe variety, considering the match was still four days off at the present time, but he could not shake it from his mind. Rochelli’s eyes gazed at the cosmic blanket again, seeking and probing for more answers. While not believing in the power of the stars, tarot cards, a God, or anything along those lines, Silvio could recall his aunt whom was a fanatic for such things. She often commented that the stars offered council and ‘that man on the moon is one wise motherfucker!’ Needless to say, her viewpoint had been taken with a grain of salt and laughed at before, but now he was curious. His tired state had rendered him stupid. So tell me, Mr. Moon, how much do you know? Give me some answers! And, magically, one-by-one, the answers came to him through the stars. His mind raced back and forth from press conference to present time. He’d had a lot to say about Matt Jackson, he still did and he always would as long as the little government squirrel decided to meddle in his affairs. But, like the reporter from the back of the room had asked after Silvio had blown up in his small fit of annoyed rage, what about Tyler Preston? Yes, Tyler Preston was not one to be forgotten about. Silvio knew he could handle Matt Jackson, it was no contest despite what the Agent had seemingly convinced himself. Who can blame Matt Jackson for thinking he’s going to beat me? He belongs to the United States government. Hell, you can’t get a job for them unless you’ve got a fucking doctorate in brain washing! This little ‘my mind is strong and my confidence is high’ act is going to crumble beneath this feet and he’s going to collapse! And even the most ‘strong-willed’ of government agents, like Matt Jackson, can’t keep the distraction of that hot little Russian piece Yulia out of his head. Pathetic fool! His mind is in disarray! This is no longer a matter of HOW or if I CAN beat Matt Jackson, it is a matter of how badly I choose to put him into his place. ‘Yeah, but what about Tyler Preston?’ The reporters questioned popped fresh back into his mind, blinking like a neon light. Silvio closed his eyes and sighed once more. He opened them; with a new found difficulty caused by sleepiness that was starting to overcome him and found his eyes trained on Orion’s Belt. The three little stars making up a band, sitting in a row in the sky. Three of the brightest stars in the night sky, the answer to the question ‘What about Tyler Preston?’ Tyler Preston is a lot like a star in Orion’s Belt. When you put him next to his other little star friends, he means something. But when you put him all by himself, he’s just another star whose name you don’t know. NO ONE knows that the names of the three stars are Alnitak, Alnilam, and Mitaka but EVERY ONE knows they belong to Orion’s Belt. Just like NO ONE knows who the hell Tyler Preston is, but every one knows just who E2 is. A problem? No, but he should go back to the tag team division where he came from. He’s a conniving little bastard. He’ll cut your throat with the knife during your victory celebration. Well I got eyes in the back of my head kid. Don’t’ try your shit! Lay down the easy way, or lay down the hard way. Either way, you’re laying down. ‘And the Warrior, what about him?’ Ah yes, the Warrior. The Warrior? Silvio glanced up and a smile crept over his lips. During his lifetime, he must’ve seen millions of stars ‘go out’ and he’d never see them again. But he could never know which stars had suddenly disappeared from the night sky. This was the scenario of The Warrior. He’s there one night and gone the next. And no one really gives a damn, no one really feels guilty, because there’s so many others to look at it. HA! Oh well, now the kid can say he’s been in PCW and not be lying. Good for him. Too bad his friends will never get to hear the part about how he was decimated on his debut night and sent out. But we’ll all know. Heh heh… Laughing sleepily, his eyes began to droop heavily. He popped them open, struggling to stay awake but ultimately failing as he drifted off to sleep in the tanned colored folding chair underneath the brightly speckled night sky. Sandman: 1. Rochelli: Zilch!
