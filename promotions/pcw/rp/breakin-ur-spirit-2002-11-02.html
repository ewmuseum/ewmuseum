Date: 11.2.02 | A shadow of ill-fortunate dawned over me like judgement day before God after a bad lived life. My adreniline shot cold, felt heavy on the skin. Foot steps racing my beating heart added to the pure fear trapping me. Like a reaper after a soul, I was the prey. Crept tight underneath a desk, darkness surrounded me. Only dark cloths made me blend to become some what invisiable. Flash lights, dogs, people in police suits; I felt it, they were after me. Two bags of damned money, I kept stached in two tight hands not even the jaws of life could pry off. Pitty stealing, it wasn't my style, I didn't need this. So why did I do it? Just for the thrill. Till my home boy shot out from underneath the desk screaming and shooting. | 'Wut da fuck?! Nigga, u gon' get yo ass shot!' BOOM! I didn't wait for his body to fit the floor, quickly I threw as much money I could hold, jumped over the desk, and took off hauling ass down the maze-like hallway. | Walls turning to no end, doors on every side not knowing what would conceal my savory or my damnation. Finally reaching the spiraling stairway, I swung open the door and ran quickly down in a slanted circle. Gun shots popping near me with every turn, foot steps progressing with each movement. A door lead to the outside. Not so much ground level, but still an escape. Making it out, there was no escape. Just wind bitting away at my face like rapid fire ants. It was over. I saw thoughts, memories, the resolution of my after life era. Blood poured down my hole from my head, they had finally caught me. The thrill was over. | - The corners of dark creep upon usLike the sun that rises beyond fields- It consumes everything with lightBringing life to its knees- As blood replaces angerAnd fills the river that once flew without a flaw- A father walks outTears seep through a child's hands- Like a bullet through a victim's fleshOut conscience without its legs- A plague of voilence sweeps through the childrenLike a bomb on softened soil- Life is snatchedLike a rose pulled from it's roots- Soiled words slip through the lips of our leadersLike a child coming from its mother's womb- But we keep quietBut we keep still- We stand in silenceLike a lion catching its prey- We keep creatures in the burrow of our mindsAs we slowly die- As we walk awayAs we walk away - - - 'This bitch don' put me to sleep. Go't damn.' Slied out to Sheeca who set on my left. 'You are so embarrassing! Through her 'Bullet in the head' poem, you keep on shouting about being shot in the head by some cops! Damn AJ, you're embarrassing! And don't tell me you were asleep either! I know you were messing around! Embarrassing me all up in here like this!' Each sentense, the level of his voice would increase a bit. People started looking our way; she then knew it was time to just sit back and shut up. 'Her last poem gave me a damn nightmare, datz a damn shame.' Adding on. 'Shut up, she's about to cite another poem!' Shunning me like she was the Queen of, somewhere, for lack of better word. - The old lady spoke ancient. Mystic, enchanting, moving her mouth while projecting the feeling with movement to us. Black hair draining of color at the back, black smooth skin, time evolved into wrinkles. 'This is another poem I wrote. It's called Manipulation.' The one's who actually cared clapped, I looked at Sheeca and sighed. - - - because you are nothing without it the first step is to take its world away,not by brute force, but by kind ridicule.suddenly the world isn't good enough. Disease the result is multi-faceted:no distractions keep it from you,and you are surperior, the master of insight Coward when you are aloof, it will weep;it is in ecstasy in your grasp.You are god manifest because it is nothing without you - | De-ja-vu; an occurance in which something happens exactly the same twice. In otherwords, the crowd gave the same ovation for this, as did last poem. | 'Wut da fuck iz dis? A damn illusion?' Dream real, I spoke of it. Tired of my commenting, 'Don't you have a match or some sort of shit like that to get ready for anyways? I don't know why you came with me if you didn't want to her it!' Lady in the middle row, can you please shut the fuck up! The old poem teller told as she smoked a ciggarette and flipped through the pages of her time worn book. Of course I bursted out laughing with a collective few. Pissed reigning in emotions, shown by facial expression and fire in Sheeca's eyes. 'Don't you have an important match to get ready for?!' Thinks about Fusion... 'No, not really.'
