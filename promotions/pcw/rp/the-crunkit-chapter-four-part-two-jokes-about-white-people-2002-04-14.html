The Crunkit:Chapter Four(Part Two)Jokes About White People

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/14/02 Despite an awful showing back in the real world as Rush, during which he somehow lost to a non-RPing Red Rock, but hey how’s ya like dem apples? Despite that showing, Rushbo was still in a worse place. As you might remember, or might not care, the last time we saw the amazing Crunkit, he and his dwarf companions had been kidnapped by a shit lot of white people. As he was carried off by a goblin/white person, he had some choice words. Rushbo: Hey! The white guy still didn’t answer so Rushbo tries again to yell at the large monster. Rush: Hey, you! Still, Rushbo continued to try and get the attention of his kidnapper. Rushbo: Hey, earth to the great white hope! Goblin: What do you want, ya stupid Negro? Rushbo: Do you WANT to die, dumbass cracker? You messed with the wrong 3’7 machine! The goblin just ignored Rushbo pounding against his large frame, because the large white monster could barely feel it. Rushbo: Listen, if you let me go, then I can give you whatever you want. Goblin: What I want is to eat some nice dark meat tonight. The goblin licked his lips and ran his pale white tongue over his sharp teeth as Rushbo gulped in fear. The goblin walked over to the crack and stepped through as the first goblin to go through the crack. He yelled out to the rest of the goblins to follow. Goblin: Follow me, my wonderful friends. We need to carry the evildoing trespassers to our leader! Rushbo: What in the name of Rush Mason was that? Goblin: What, you’ve never heard the ancient language of Caucasia, the lost continent? Rushbo: Whiteybonics, that’s sad, just sad. As the shit lot of white people thundered through the crack, Rushbo could see a flash of light from where he just came from, and tried to angle his body so that he can get a quick peek, but the goblin’s grasp was tight and ddnt’t allow him to do anything of the sort. Rushbo: Listen, I left my uhhh…friend pheasant back there, and you know that us darkies need our fried food, so do you think I could go get it? Goblin: Yeah, you’re right little darkie; you do need those fried birds to live, don’t ya? Rushbo now resisted the urge to hit the pale man, and bust out the 3’7 kung fu ninja skills, and of course he could defeat all of the white people, but he didn’t want to hurt any innocents, so he decided to go along with this for the goblin’s safety. Rushbo: Can you just open that crack for me one more time? Goblin: Sorry, but only the Caucasian Goblin King can do that one, and he’s all the way on his throne, and I won’t be able to hear him, and my dog ate it… Rushbo: So, you’re going to let me die? Goblin: No, I’ve got some food that you can have. The Goblin reached into his pocket and pulls out the most disgusting, inedible product ever known to man: haggis. He held it out to Rushbo, who almost gagged at the sight of the seafood product. Goblin: Want some? The goblin could barely keep his own appetite at bay, looking at the haggis intently. His eyes were large and his grip on it was very firm. Rushbo: No thanks, somehow, I’ll make it through. Jeez, they give me a whole bunch of British goblins, the only thing worse could be Canadian jobbing goblins. The goblin who is holding him chomps down on the haggis, the disgusting smell coming from the fish product thing that is an English delicacy. Goblin: Fellows, where in the bloody hoo-daw are we? I’m absolutely flabbergasted! The language of Caucasia emanated from the lips of the lead goblin, as the goblin holding Rushbo had been identified, and Rush had to once again restrain himself from smacking the big white dude in the face. Rushbo: No offense, but why do you have to be so…white? I mean, you talk to me like a fairly normal uhhh… large monster. Goblin: Well, goblins have been taught the ancient language of Caucasian because, well actually just because it’s a lot easier than for Rushbo’s handler to write that to write grammatically correct Ebonics. Rush stayed quiet for a while, even though the loud smacking lips of the goblin eating haggis can still be heard. The goblin carried him by his toes though, which was surprisingly lucky, because his head was so far away from the terrible smelling food. The goblins walked through the extremely rocky terrain of the cave towards a strange red light that seemed to be getting closer to the group. The goblins then broke out in song. Clap! Snap! The white crack!Just like a rhino, we’re all albino!And down down to Goblin townYou go my friendly wino Crush! Bam! Howdy folks!We eat haggis and call each other blokes!And down down to Goblin TownOn a bike without any spokes Swiss Cheese! Cheese is good!Goblin housewives suck on our wood!And down down to Goblin TownAway from the Negros’ hood The sound was truly terrifying for Rushbo and the dwarves, for the amount of Caucasian goblin energy in the cave at that time could’ve produced its own Phil Collins concert. However, this song meant more work for the goblins, because the leader goblins pulled out their whips, and whipped the white people. That’s right, they whipped the goblins, because they knew that the Crunkit might just have to whip all their pale asses if they tried to pull that slavery shit on him again. Wait, no they whipped the other goblins because they needed to move with speed to get to Goblin Town, which was sounding less appealing to Rushbo every second. AND he still had no idea where Coren, Sethin, Trevin, or Juandalf were. However, he could hear dwarf tongue somewhere, and he was beginning to dread that his companions were being tortured, but all of those thoughts left his mind as the entire goblin company stumbled, it seemed, right into a huge cavern. In the middle of the cavern was an enormous bonfire, and there were goblins on every side of the fire. They stood around, looking like they were eager to get hunting and screwing their sisters, well maybe, but maybe not. Who knows? Maybe they were hillbilly goblins. As the goblins saw the new meat coming in (the dwarves and Rushbo) they laughed and cackled, they stomped their feet and clapped their hands, but of course not in rhythm because we all know that white people have no rhythm at all. Rushbo was let down by the large goblin hands and he scurried along the floor, avoiding the whips as best he could, towards where the dwarves were standing, feeling very, very small. He reached an area near the fire where Coren was indeed standing with Sethin and Trevin. Rushbo: Coren! Sethin!Trevin Coren: Hello there burgular, it looks as though we’ve gotten ourselves into quite a situation here. Rushbo: That doesn’t really do this situation justice, but yes, you have gotten into quite a situation. Coren: Oh, I got us here? Rushbo: Your friends were the ones who got us into that cave and now Juandalf is probably dead, making me once again the only person of color here, which leads me to believe that you will feast on my amazingly chiseled and muscular delicious tasting body with these goblins! Sethin: Dude, we’re as much food here as you are. Rushbo: These people want steak, they don’t want turkey. They want some dark meat, and they want to marinate it with the blood of those ponies. The group glanced over toward the terrified animals, which had been brought into a small corner, where the goblins eagerly watched them. The goblins also had taken it upon themselves to go through the baggage of the group and smell and touch everything in those bags. Trevin: Dude, those ponies are gonna be the first thing they eat. They can’t keep their fingers off those things. Sadly, that would be the last time they ever saw the ponies, because they ran away and joined the Church of Scientology, but then committed suicide when they realized that Battlefield Earth was a good book, but a terrible terrible movie. However, the original band of dwarves and Rushbo didn’t get too long to enjoy the company of one another, because soon they were shackled and put in chains. They were dragged through the large cavern to the end, with the small Crunkit in the tailing end. On that end of the cavern was a huge stone, on which sat a enormous Caucasian goblin with a huge head, and stuffing haggis into his mouth with amazing speed. The strange thing was that he had a Scottish accent and Rushbo’s handler was pretty sure that Andy Murray signed up for this RP set, so he looked rather like Andy Murray would if he had a huge head and ate haggis all day, so just check out Andy Murray’s poser and you have the exact same picture. Around the large Murray-esque goblin were dozens of armed goblins who carried axes, knives, swords, glocks, Elian Gonzalez, etc. There were also instruments of torture, including a TV stuck on the Disney Channel movie Tru Confessions. These instruments of torture had most likely been created by goblins, because no one else could come up with a stupider idea than putting Shia LaBouef in a movie where he’s a retard. Anyways, goblins didn’t particularly hate dwarves, but against Coren’s folk they held a special grudge because of a war long ago, and goblins don’t really care who they capture as long as they are defenseless. Of course this is not true in real life because only every white person born from the beginning of time until 1950 felt this way about black people(OOC: Of course I am joking, but divide the number by two and you’ve probably got a fairly good statement about the people of the US) The Goblin King confronted the company, Coren in particular. King: Blaaaaaargh! Who are those that stand before the great King Blargh! Rushbo: Your name is Blargh? King Blargh: Silence! I am speaking to the leader of this merry band. Who are these festering piles of fetid compost? Rushbo: Um…excuse me but you’re the one eating- Rushbo is quickly cut off by a swift kick to the shin from Trevin, which stops Rushbo from talking at all. Guard Goblin: They are intruders, Great Blargh. We found them sitting on our Front Porch. King Blargh: What do you mean coming into goblin territory like this? You were probably thieving, spying, or looking into the business of my people! Well, what do you have to say? Coren: I am Coren the dwarf. We sheltered from a storm in this very cave, not knowing that it belonged to your people. We didn’t mean to inconveniece you in any way. The king goblin thought thought this over for a moment, scratching his giant chin repeatedly. King Blargh: Possible, but why were you traveling up in these mountains anyway? They are not safe for little people. Where were you coming from and going to? Tell me, Coren Oakenshield, or I will slice your throat myself! Tell me the truth and I will kill you quick and painlessly Coren didn’t even flinch at the mention of the death of he and his comrades. He stared into the eyes of King Blargh and boldly lied. Coren: We were on a journey to visit family. You see, Shenaynay just had her baby daddy run off, so- Guard Goblin: They lie, Great one! One of their kind struck us with lightning when we invited them down to Goblin Town and they still haven’t explained this. Rushbo took this moment to interject his own thought. Rushbo: Well maybe if you didn’t call it Goblin Town you’d get more visitors. I mean, who wants to go to a place where they might run across Sykbo the Goblin? Why don’t you call it Happy Land or something? The king roared back at the little man. King Blargh: What do I look like, a fag or something? In the meantime, the guard goblin held out Coren’s sword to the king, which sent him into a fit of rage. He howled like the new Fleetwood Mac CD had been delayed for 2 more weeks. His soldiers too, gnashed their teeth as though the sword itself had caused black people to be so much better at basketball than white people. For they knew it was Orcrist, the goblin cleaver. King Blargh: You are murderers and elf-friends! Even worse, you do not accept our haggis! KILL THEM! SLASH THEM! EAT THEM! The king screamed at the top of his lungs, except that no one was really paying attention anyway, but at this very moment, there was a flash of light a poof of smoke and the great fire vanished. In the dark, Rushbo whispered out. Rushbo: Well, if we have the choice to eat that nastyass haggis or die, then call me the dumb black guy and hand me the sword, I’ll cut my own throat. Coren: Burgular, we do not have time for your antics. Rushbo: What antics, I’m serious? Of course, the rest of the conversation couldn’t be heard because of the loud noises coming from the goblins, who probably were just a shade of white too dark to glow in the dark. Shrieks of a nature never heard before were heard and sparks burnt clean through the goblins. The smoke from the fire clouded the entire room so that it was almost impossible to see. Soon the goblins began to fight amongst each other so that utter chaos had broken out. As the fighting began, a sword flashed in the smoggy room. Rushbo watched it slice right through King Blargh, and his eyes had a dumbfounded look on them even as they slowly fluttered shut for the last time. The rest of the goblins fled from the sword Orcrist, that had killed their king. The sword was sheathed and a voice cried out. Voice: Let’s go, and Rushbo, no jokes about white people until we’re out of reach of the goblins This put somewhat of a damper on their escape, for Rushbo would’ve really liked to make a joke about the white people shrieking like rock was banned or something, but he kept to himself as they ran from the scene. A faint light led them and the voice called out to them again. Voice: Be careful! They will pursue us as soon as they can relight the torches. Trevin: Dude, one second. Trevin, being the kind dwarf that he was, picked up the small Rushbo and carried him on his back, as much as it displeased Rushbo, because the Crunkits small body, however immensely attractive it was, wasn’t fast enough to keep up with the dwarves sleek quick bodies. Then a light appearead and so did a familiar face. It was Juandalf who had helped them escape. He held the sword Orcrist and CrunKiller, both in his hands. He made sure all four were safe and then they headed off again. They began to hear goblin noises and such, but they kept on going, even with no real idea of where they were going. This sent them going even faster, but goblins are faster than dwarves, and they were gaining with every passing second. The goblins also knew the way better, and they were furious. Rushbo: Why in Crunk’s name did I leave the hole? Of course, he didn’t know about the thieving of his home and the occupancy of it by the two thieves, but he still wished he was home. Trevin: You should be asking why you ate so much before you left, dude! You weigh a ton! Soon they had to turn around and swords flashed and Juandalf flashed his wand many a times, smoking holes through goblins. Soon, the goblins had to stumble back the way they had come, dejected. However, when the torches went out again, they put the softest shoes on their fastest runners and went out in search of the party. No one ever heard them coming or saw them coming. Suddenly, Trevin was grabbed from behind in the dark and Rushbo fell off of his shoulders and hit his head, and he didn’t remember anything after that…
