The Call

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-9-02 (We fade in on The Refugee's who are in their Detroit home. Ali's voice can be heard yelling across the house and Qaboos is nowhere in sight.) Ali:Qaboos!!! Just then Qaboos comes running down into the home carrying a bag of groceries. He has a big cheesy smile on his face and he looks very happy. He runs in and gives Ali who is still in shock a hug. Ali remains still even as Qaboos lets go. He then places the bag down on the kitchen counter and turns on the kitchen television and acts as if nothing has happened. Ali is worried about this joyous Qaboos and decides to investigate. Ali:What's with you? Qaboos:I just had possibly the greatest food I've ever had slide down my throat. Ali:Some sort of American dish? Qaboos(laughing):Now why would I eat a dish? Qaboos continues watching television and Ali shakes his head and leaves the room. He goes into his bedroom and lies down. After relaxing for a bit he hear's Qaboos's loud and annoying voice. Qaboos:Hey Ali! Ali mumbles something loudly and begins to doze off again. Qaboos:Ali!!! Ali:Ye.. Is all he can say. Qaboos:Someone's on the phone. Ali:Tell I ca' back... He says closing his eyes. Qaboos:I really think you should talk to him. He receives no answer and sighs. He then charges up the stairs and shakes him awake. Ali begins to mumble and toss but still doesn't awake. Qaboos:Alright fine then. I'll just tell Hamid you are too busy for him. Qaboos smiles and walks out of the room. He counts to three and the exact moment he says the word three Ali comes charging out of his room and runs downstairs. Qaboos(laughing):Works like a charm. Ali looks around for the phone but cannot find it. He see's Qaboos playing ping pong with half the table up. He dashes over to him and practically pounces on him. Ali:Phone?! Qaboos:What phone? Ali:Where is it? Qaboos:Wherever you left it. Ali:But you answered it. Qaboos:Oh yeah. It's in the grocery bag. Ali runs back into the kitchen and reaches into one bag and pulls out a can of tuna. Shuddering his tosses it aside and this time has more luck and pulls out the phone. Ali:Hello. Karzai:Hello, Ali. Ali:How are you, Mr. President? Karzai:Mr. President? Well that's a new one. Ali:Well why have you called? Karzai:Well I've wanted to talk to you for awhile now about Afghanistan and such but something happened yesterday and well I'm not sure how to tell you this but... Ali pays little attention and is focusing on Qaboos who is watching Fox News. All of a sudden he drops the remote and is still for a moment he then turns at Ali and motions for him to come looks at the television. Ali waves him off but loses track of the conversation. Karzai: ...and we're all very... Ali:I don't mean to be rude but could you start from the beginning. Karzai:(sigh) Just when he is about to begin he is cut off by Ali once more. Ali:Oh I forgot to tell you did you hear about how I am in PCW? Karzai:Yes, I remember. I came up with the idea for a National Holiday in your honor remember? Ali:Oh yes, that's right. Karzai:Now anyway... Ali:And did you hear that I am in a stable with The Chef? Can you believe it?! Karzai:Yes I can because some of my own money went to providing our country with Afghan flags with you two and Chef's picture on it. Ali:Really? Gee, I must be popular back home. Karzai:Yes, you are but that's not what I am trying to tell you. Ali:Oh and I got a manager, a Saudi named Isa... Karzai:Ali!!! Ali whimpers into the phone and Karzai after a moment of silence, Hamid speaks. Karzai:Sorry. I do that sometimes. Ali:Quite alright. Karzai:Anyways, if I could finish what I was going to say. Ali:Certainly. Karzai:No interruptions? Ali:No, none. Karzai:Seriously? Ali:Seriously. Karazai:Okay then. Ali:Okay. Karzai:You can stop now. Ali:Alright. Karzai:Anyways, here is what happened. Do you remember Haji? Ali:Haji?! Yes of course, he came back to us after working for the Taliban. Karzai:Indeed. Anyways, he was appointed Vice President of Afghanistan. Ali:Well you chose a good man. Karzai:Thank you. But he was shot and killed yesterday. Ali sits in silence for a moment then looks over at Qaboos who already has tears streaming down his face. Ali looks over to the wall and looks at a picture of his father with Haji. Karzai:Hello? Ali:Sorry about that Hamid. Karzai:I understand. Anyways, the people are miserable down here again and I know this is a pretty big favor but we would like you to come down here and give them some support. Ali:I will think about it. I think we need some time to talk now though Hamid so I'll let you know. Karzai:I see. Well it was nice talking to you. I hope to see you down here. Have a good day. Ali:Bye. He hangs up the phone. His emotionless face which knows no joy but only death and pain looks over to Qaboos and he says. Qaboos:Call Isa. We must talk.
