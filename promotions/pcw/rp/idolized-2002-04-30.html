Idolized

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-30-02 Too soon were you taken from us. How could the one you loved, the one you looked up to do such a thing? He was the one that led you into this mess, and now you can never get out. Such a bad influence he was. Why did I let you watch that filth? It’s his fault entirely. I can’t do much, but I promise he will pay. He can’t get away with this… … He can’t… Why did you have to leave us so soon? Was it something I said? Did I play a part in your fatal decision? Oh, if only you could answer me. Never again will you though. You were my life, my everything. How can I live without you?! I can’t… Sleep well my child for the lord holds you in his arms now. *All she could do was cry. She had lost the only thing that mattered to her. In the pouring rain she sat at his grave and wept as her tears blended in with the rain. She didn’t care about how she looked sitting out in the cold. She didn’t care about anything anymore. But what was to blame for her son’s death? There was no question at all in her mind. The answer was obvious.* *Luke was a young boy at about nine years of age. He was always jolly and had an aura that would light up the most dim of spaces. He had a pair of eyes that charmed even the cruelest of people, and a smile that could thaw out and melt even the coldest of hearts. In the past year of his life, he went through many changes. His family was tearing apart before his very eyes. Rarely could he ever get by a day without having to hear his parents fight. Worst of all, at some points those altercations became physical. He wasn’t dumb despite his age either. He knew it was only a matter of time before the inevitable would take place. The boy needed an escape from a reality so harsh. But first he needed the moral consent to do so.* *His parents were sleeping that fateful day. He was just flipping through channels on his TV not truly expecting much to excite him. Then came one station. Three letters and one number on the end soon would hold the boy’s fate in his very hands.* PCW2 *As the show went on, he watched many different presentations from the new flood of superstars. Little things we call, “promos”. Then, much to his wonder his eyes feasted one longhaired man standing in the dark alleyway. Just by looking at him the boy could tell he had the same outlook on life as himself. That bleak glint in his eye told it all. Even those cryptic words that spilled from his mouth had painted a landscape of pain much similar to the boy’s own.* *A few days passed and Luke finally got his chance to see the man in action. It was a four corners confrontation between himself, Badge, a jobber named Scotty Killer and a no-show Draven Lazerus. Spinning and flying through the air, showing a near unprecedented scientific expertise, his actions excited Lucas, keeping him on the edge of his seat. Finally, he found someone to relate to. Finally, his hero had arrived.* *Week after week passed and the boy grew more and more fond of him. He began to idolize Stryfe, emulating his everything he did. Luke’s obsession with the man had soon grown to a very unhealthy level. It climaxed one morning as his family sat at the table for breakfast.* “Thanks for breakfast dear.” The father thanked his wife. She took his comment very kindly as she replied, “Thank you Frank, it was no big deal really.” As the boy took a bite of his cereal, the sweet taste reminded him, “Y’know, Chex was Stryfe’s favorite cereal, now it’s the only kind I’ll eat.” The smile on his father’s face soon was reversed as he quickly stood up and snapped at his son, “Y’know what? I’ve heard enough of this ‘Stryfe’ crap! Stryfe this! Stryfe that! An’ I’m starting to get sick of this shit! Wrestling is a bad influence for you and you should have a more positive role model in your life! So shut the fuck up ‘cause I don’t wanna fucking hear this shit anymore!” “Hey!” Donna shouted back to her husband, “You don’t need to talk to him like that! He’s just a boy! Let him like who he wants!” “You just don’t get it, do you? You never do. Luke is growing up and he needs to start knowing better.” He argued. “Of course I get it. I’m not your stupid bimbo and I don’t need to put up with this!” “You don’t fucking talk to me like that!” *The boy couldn’t take anymore. He ran out of the room with tears streaming from his eyes. It was a familiar experience to him, nothing new at all. No longer could he be the same innocent child that encased his soul since birth.* *Another PCW2 broadcast taken place that day, and of course young Lucas would be sitting in his favorite chair to watch it.* *Time passed and the program went on. Then he saw on camera his idol once more. A large quantity of powder was spread out on the table in front of him, and it sure as hell wasn’t flour. Stryfe completely dived face first into it to snort up. Luke had just learned about this stuff a week ago. Jeremy’s face soon turned to a large smile hiding under his shaggy hair. And then it hit the boy. He had found an answer to his problems.* Maybe now I can escape this… *Just days later with one puff, his whole world went up in a cloud of that smoke. How good it felt to finally float above all of his cares in the world. He was no longer of this world, he was drifting in his own. But soon those people back on earth would start to miss him, as he would grow just as homesick. The problem was however he had no way to return home from his trip. He started to finally drift closer and closer to his homely state of mind only to get knocked away again. Marijuana wouldn’t do the trick anymore… It turned to crack… Which turned to heroin… Which turned to LSD… But how could a small boy possibly get the money for such expensive things? He had to steal. Every little penny within sight he claimed as his and even resorted to shoplifting items from nearby stores and selling the goods. Either way, he went to any lengths possible to get his hands on the bittersweet taste of salvation on his tongue.* *Amazing… just at the tender age of nine and already he was doing hard drugs. It was unheard of. He finally found the perfect getaway from life’s challenges and he could leave anytime he want…. Or perhaps not. It kept calling him. He had to stay. With every second that passed his marriage with death grew stronger and stronger. This soon became a full-blown addiction. It was only a matter of time before his family played a part in this deadly game of roulette. One night, just before Luke had fallen asleep, his mother knocked on the door of his room.* “Can I come in?” She softly asked behind the wood. “OK.” Her son answered. She sat down on the foot of his bed with a very concerned look in her eye that obviously couldn’t be a sign of any good news and told him, “There’s something very serious I need to talk to you about.” Luke asked, “What’s that?” “Have your teachers been talking to you lately about drugs?” “Yeah, I’ve heard about them for a while now.” She confronted him asking, “What about those marks on your arms? Do you know why your behavior has been very erratic?” “What do you mean?” Luke asked in response She explained to him, “In the past couple of months you’ve been acting very strange around us. Do you know that these actions are much like the effects of drugs?” “Are you trying to tell me I’m doing drugs?!” Luke shouted back. “I already know you are.” “No, I’m not!” “Just come out and say it. I’m not dumb.” *A long silence filled the house as Lucas prepared himself to proclaim the shocking revelation.* He took a deep breath and answered back, “Alright. I admit it.” “Why would you do such a thing?” Donna stammered with tears filling her eyes. “Because I couldn’t handle all your fighting anymore.” He soberly answered. *There was nothing left for Donna to say. Her world had just come crashing down on her with that one fateful arrangement of words and she now knew that at least to some extent she was to blame. She ran out of his room. She didn’t ever want her son to see her cry, but it was too late. All she could do that night was drown in her tears.* *Things only began to decline more and more from then on. Friends were lost and enemies were gained through his behavior. His performance in school and socially altogether were affected. There would be days he would just lock himself in his room the entire day. His parents were still reeling and couldn’t think straight as to find a solution.* “My mom says I can’t be friends with you anymore.” “Young man, we may have to hold you back a grade.” “What’s become of you?” *It was quite a scary sight to see a once bright future dimmed. The child was now nothing more than a shell of his former self. Scarier however was the fact that his friends and family could only stand by and watch the horrors unfold. It wasn’t long before everything came to a horrid climax just like another bad dream. But there was no waking up from it.* *He had lost everything in life. There was nothing left in this cold gray world to satisfy him and add color to his cerebral vision. There was no longer any reason to keep withstanding this and it was time to put a stop to it. The jig was up. He had lost his battle and war. The metal felt surprisingly warm on his tongue, yet it sent cold shivers up his spine. The taste of lead so sweet began to fill mouth as he began squeezing the trigger. His parents walking past his room were able to witness the last moment of his fragile life about to be shattered in a pool of blood and brains.* -CLICK- NNNNNNNNNNNNNOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! -BANG- And that was all it took… *Luke’s suicide took it’s toll not only on those close to him, but also the man that introduced him to his drug-induced hell. Stryfe read the front page article just three days later.* HAS WRESTLING GONE TOO FAR? *The title spoke novels of incidents past. But when things got to this magnitude, it was an entirely different story. Jeremy couldn’t even walk outside without hearing those distorted voices blaming him; Those twisted, broken fingers pointing.* Son of a bitch! Murderer! Killer! Junkie! Bastard! *From that moment when death struck the young boy, Stryfe knew he would be forever haunted as the man who threw that lightning bolt. The one that killed the child…* *He sat in his room alone, much like the way the boy did. Other than taking a peek out of his window to see the people below shouting and throwing various items at him, he did nothing to associate himself with the outside world that loathed him.* *But then, the most miraculous of events occurred… The sky ripped open and a heavenly figure from above descended upon the mob below. It’s ghastly presence suggested that a direct servant of the lord came, but the face, the soft glow from its eyes said otherwise. It was the spirit of the boy.* “Do not blame this man.” he spoke with the powerful bellow of what seemed like god himself. “The decisions I have made were my own and nobody else’s.” *The crowd as well the man locked inside could only stare on in a state of pure shock as to what had befriended their eyes and ears. Truly this was a miracle of some sort.* He continued, “What kind of world do we live in where people go and blame others for decisions made, right or wrong? Those close to me have asked me, “What has become of me?” So I ask you in response. “What has become of you?” This man is innocent and has done no wrong. So I ask of you to please refrain from blaming my childhood idol. The only killers here are yourselves, you have killed off faith in fellow humanity. I am proud of my past and to have looked up to him.” *With a great flash on the horizon, the spirit disappeared and everything apparently has now been set right. Still in shock, everyone knew not what else to do and returned to their everyday lives.* *Everything was looking clearer than ever before for Jeremy. No more clouds were obstructing his view of a bright future ahead of him. He sat in silence for over an hour before reality smacked him in the face again.* “I have a match to get to!” He remembered as he sprinted off into the distance to prepare for another battle. “With a clear head there will be nothing to stop me from crushing Syko for what he has done to me.” *All had been forgiven for now. But who would forgive him for the sins in the future he would bestow upon his adversary?* Perhaps nothing…
