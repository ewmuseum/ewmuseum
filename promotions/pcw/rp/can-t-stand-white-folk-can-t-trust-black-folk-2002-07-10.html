Can't Stand White Folk...Can't Trust Black Folk...

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.07.02 The scene opens up in some dingy, $20 a night motel in a downtown area. The motel is just 2 stories up and is attached to every building in the long street. There is a flashing “Motel” sign above the entrance with the O and L not flashing. There are no lights on in the motel, but some of the windows look like there is a television flickering in there. The scene moves into one of the rooms. The room is very small, a 10 x 10 box, with one TV in the corner, a bed which looks like it could break at any moment, one window that looks over a road and a painting of a river hanging over the bed. There is a bathroom, but out of fear for giant cockroaches and spiders no sane person would use it. So naturally, the first time you see Duzza, he is coming out of the bathroom. He is hardly wearing anything. He has a pair of baggy white boxers and nothing else on, exposing his muscular physique and his crucifix tattoo on his right shoulder. He has his hair set back in long cornrows. There is no light in the room, so Duzza switches on the tv and lays on the bed. The first channel that comes on, is some news show and the topic is the R. Kelly sex tape. TV person: “R. Kelly, guilty or not guilty? I think we all know he’s guilty” Duzza see’s this on the TV and a look of total disgust comes across his face. He puts up the middle finger and then switches of the tv, while muttering to himself. Duzza: “Can’t a brother get a break? People dissin’ him on the radio, in the street, on the TV, now in the fucking wrestling ring. Give the brother a freakin’ break” Duzza looks into his bag that is next to him on his bed, he pulls out a CD player and his favorite CD “I Wish” by R. Kelly. Duzza closes his eyes and for a few minutes is lost in the song, singing along, reminiscing of a time where you could openly support R. Kelly in public and not get your ass kicked. Sadly, those days are gone, and now it’s hard for a brother to even wear a R. Kelly t-shirt with out getting looks of disgust from passers-by. After the song is finished, Duzza gets up and looks out the window. Out of the window, he can see nothing but hell on earth. He can see a big fight going on between a few guys, after realizing that they’d probably come up and kick his ass if they saw him watching them fight, he walks over to the bathroom, talking while he’s walking. Once in the bathroom, he flicks on the light switch and you can see the cockroaches scatter, he slowly walks over to the mirror. The reflection is one of a battered and beaten a man. Duzza’s bruised body stares back at him in the mirror. For, Duzza was the victim of a brutal and unprovoked attack by men a day before he would of called his allies. Apparently The Uprising, the latest black stable in PCW, had something against Duzza and his boy R. Kelly. Now you would think that as black men they’d be able to support a brother who was in trouble with the law, especially since there is little doubt all three members of The Uprising have been in trouble with the law before. You would expect them to support a brother, at least until he is proven guilty, but no, these brothers, one who thinks he is Malcolm X, one who thinks he is a Voodoo Queen and one who no one really knows about, are three judgmental brothers. And apparently, just supporting R. Kelly was enough of a reason to give Duzza a beating, a public humiliation. But Duzza knows what goes around, comes around and he’ll get his own back. Duzza: “Mother fuckers… them mother fuckers. How they gon’ play me like that? I’m Duzza damn it! I’m Duzz, I’ve been around longer than all them fools, without me, there would be none of them, fuck them and…wait… why the fuck am I talking to my reflection?” While Duzza is thinking about why he is talking to his reflection, his cell phone starts to ring, with the ring tone as “Fiesta” by R. Kelly. Duzza quickly picks up the phone. Duzza: “Yeah yeah, who this?” The voice at the other end is a familiar one, it is a voice of a young, successful brother who can acknowledge what Duzza has done for his people. It is the voice of RUSH superstar Statuz Quo Statuz: “Yo yo bro, it’s ya boy Statuz” Duzza: “’Sup State?” Statuz: “Nigga, I jus’ done watched Turmoil, I saw what happened to yo ass” Duzza: “Oh, you hear to take the piss like the last seven phone calls I got where?” Statuz: “Nah dawg, I’ma jus’ callin’ to say I got yo back dawg. What them fools did to you was bang out of order y’know” Duzza: “For real?” Statuz: “For real bro” Duzza: “Well, finally. A brother who has got his mind right, cos y’know I’ma get my own back on them nigga’s” Statuz: “No doubt dawg, and if you need it, I got your back, Playaz Inc. too” Duzza: “That’s a great offer bro, but I’ma get them fools back on my own. They wanna dis me like that? Man, they can spit on me all they want, but when they make R. Kelly jokes, see then it’s personal” Statuz: “No doubt” Duzza: “You see the problem wit’ these nigga’s is, they ain’t got no damn respect. I opened the gate for these brothers, where was they when I was getting flamed for callin’ all the white boys ‘crackers’? Where was Jordan X Shabazz when I was in PCW2, representing NYC to the fullest? Where was Rush Mason when I was kicking ass all over EW? See, they wasn’t here for any of that. I fought a one man struggle bro, it was me against the world” Statuz: “I hear ya” Duzza: “And then as soon as the struggle is over, all these nigga’s start popping up, like white guys at a Barry Manilow concert. I opened the gate and they are walking through it, I done so much and what do I get?” Statuz: “An ass kicking” Duzza: “Exactly! I get a damn ass kicking. When Jordan X was called Jordan Crazy who had his back? Me, that’s who. He probably don’t know it, but I done had Rush’s back since the day I laid eyes on him. See, I knew they was gonna be stars, but why they making stars out of themselves at my cost? Weren’t they man enough to go solo and get to the top? No, they had to give me a three on one ass kicking to get people to notice them fools. Well fuck them nigga’s, fuck them” Statuz: “For sho’, man I remember back in the day when I was getting hate left and right, you was the first guy to tell me you had my back and you never turned your back on me, for real” Duzza: “See, that’s how it goes. People turn on me, I don’t turn on no one. But I tell you one thing dawg, and I mean this, I’ma get ‘The Uprising’ back. I don’t care what I gotta do, ain’t no one spittin’ on Duzza and dissin’ the good name of R. Kelly and getting away with it. When R. is found innocent they will eat their words, and I will get them fools back, I swear to God, the Uprising will pay for what they did to me bro, they will pay” Statuz: “Well good luck to ya bro, good luck” Duzza: “Luck? Please, they’re the ones who gon’ need luck man. Duzz don’t need luck…” With that, Duzza puts down the phone, chucks his phone down and sits on his bed, no doubt thinking of ways to get back on The Uprising.
