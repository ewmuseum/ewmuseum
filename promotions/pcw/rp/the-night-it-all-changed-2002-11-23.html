The Night It All Changed

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Night It All Changed Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 23/11/02 He hates them. Wherever he takes PCW on the road they are that bit of grit in his eye that he just can’t get out, that aggravates him everytime he blinks. They’re that little cut on his finger that stings whenever he touches something. They’re that little itch square in the middle of his back that just can’t be appeased. They’re always there, and although they pay for it, he hates them. They’re the PCW fans. “Peons.” Had they never cared about him in the first place, they may not jeer so rabidly. If he hadn’t betrayed them all in March they might just dislike him rather than detest him. But he was the one they thought they would still be cheering for today, their new Golden Child. “I am nothing of theirs.” Once there was a time that they used to cheer for him. ‘KAN-NON! KAN-NON! KAN-NON!’ His name vibrating through their vocal chords right up to the rafters, filling each arena with the sound of their praise for him. At Shanghaied they loved him, he saved Miyagawa, he helped Jeremy Howard, he found a place in their hearts. “I can’t think of anywhere I’d rather not be.” Their respect was truly earned shortly after, when Justin Howard was kidnapped on the day he finally earned his release from Prison. The End and Drake Maxwell taunted Asylum with him, sending Xavier off on search of his mentor’s brother... But he found them and his body paid for it. Beaten to a pulp, blood blinding him and starting to dry, encrusting his eyelashes, he crawled through the vast expanse of backstage to try and tell Jeremy where they last were... His path marked by blood stained palm prints, not really knowing where he was going but knowing he had to be going somewhere. He still didn’t make it, he collapsed just short of Jeremy’s locker room but it was something, Justin was whisked away that night but Xavier couldn’t have done anything more. “Every drop of my blood, wasted.” From that night, the fans saw Xavier differently. Unfortunately, from that night Xavier saw everything differently. He just couldn’t appreciate the respect, the cheers, the love being lavished upon him by the PCW crowd... Jaded and bitter, feeling that what he was given was no compensation, his downward spiral began. Ironically, the fans popped so much for him, that they never noticed. “Nobody did.” There was no broad grin on the Scientologist’s face as he walked out to be lavished with applause, there was no happy feeling inside that so many had taken him to their hearts... All he noticed about the cheers is that they were quieter than those for Howard and Miyagawa. These were the same cheers that Ellie~! would have normally lapped up, but when she saw her husband that night... Having his bloodied face mopped with a wet towel, stitches perforating his skin. How could she be happy after seeing that? “She saw them as the same selfish peons I did.” “Rock is Dead” hits. Fans cheer. Automated response. He had everything a PCW rookie could possibly have wanted, and he just couldn’t see how lucky he was. It had almost become an insult for him to be cheered. Every clap from every fan had become an insult, rubbing it in that he was the guy who takes the beatings. But he sat on his anger, almost admirably. The fans cheered and he just ignored them, not the Xavier we know today who’s always ready to insult them and everyone they know. It didn’t last for long. “It lasted too long.” Come March Madness, Xavier was again fighting Asylum’s corner... Watching the back of Miyagawa in the Main Event, coming in towards the end and giving his all, like he always did back then, for as long as he lasted. 3 minutes and 8 seconds. While helping Miyagawa he was dumped from the biggest match of his career. In 3 minutes and 8 seconds. To make the chances of his Asylum stable-mate winning a little better, he again was sacrificed. In 3 minutes and 8 seconds. “You can stop saying that now.” Those 188 seconds would prove to be the end of Asylum, and PCW as we know it. Even in the 3rd Age, everything PCW has begun, every cent in the PCW bank accounts can be directly traced back to the moment both Xavier’s feet touched the ground that March evening. The moment the fans stopped cheering for Xavier can too. “Then those 188 seconds are very dear to my heart.” Turmoil, everything changed. PCW was transformed following March Madness and Xavier was at the center of it... He was the pin that when removed led to things falling apart, he was the one who set things in motion, set the ball rolling. Stopped the cheers and made them jeers. A new side to Xavier, something PCW fans hadn’t seen before emerged. He was no longer the valiant Asylum member, laying his body on the line for the good of others. Bitterness had infected his bravery. Jealousy had replaced respect. “I was me again.” That Sunday he had seen the fans jeer when Miyagawa was eliminated, a stark contrast to the silence he received when blind sided and tossed. Watching from the back, his eyes and ears took in the scenes of jubilation when Jeremy and Justin Howard were reunited... ... Not a mention of the blood he had spilled for the cause. It wasn’t the fan’s fault. It wasn’t Asylum’s fault.... But can it all be Kannon’s fault? “I told it how it was, they made their choices.” A man of strong faith, Kannon went from using it as an inspiration to using it as an excuse. No longer was Scientology the thing that drove him on, gave him strength, and made him at peace with himself... It was something to keep his down, a religion that people didn’t know nor understand that he could easily claim people hated. It wasn’t his fault, it was all theirs. “Yes, yes it was.” The oppression claiming began... He lost a match, bias referee. And it continued, leaving a trail of destruction in his wake.... The most notable casualty being PCW’s oldest stable, Asylum. Xavier Kannon sucker punched Howard, the cracks began, and once Miya won the World Title... Asylum was dead. The Scientologist had succeeded in his first aim... But now he had the fallout to begin with. In one night he had gone from being worshipped to detested, admired to disdained. “I much prefer the latter options.” Jeremy Howard still thought he was worth saving. The Krayzie One was wrong and there was no way back for Xavier. Now he bleeds only for himself and his wife. Now he has no reason to prove himself to anyone. Now he is no longer an errand boy. He’s Xavier Kannon, they’re the PCW fans. He hates them, they hate him. “And so it will stay.” That it will. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
