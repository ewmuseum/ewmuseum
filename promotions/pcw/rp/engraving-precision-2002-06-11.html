Engraving Precision

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.11.02 6.11.025:14 pm.Baltimore, Maryland-- From the start of time, man has been an inquisitive creature. May it be curiosity about the surroundings, others around them, and even about what may lay ahead around the corner…he or she is trying to peer into the unknown and trying to explain occurrences that were felt to be, at certain time periods, “unexplainable”. Most thoughts seem to hinge on being predictive or warning thoughts being mysteriously sent to the person; be it fate, luck, chance, or bad luck having this omen or foretelling sent their way. It would appear that our ancestors had all the bases covered in omen carrying thoughts. Birds, four legged animals, clouds, stars, other people, and even insects…the listing of items could almost be enough to encourage one not to leave the home to lessen the risk of an encounter or an omen being delivered. Being creative, sayings and thoughts developed over what you did or came upon within the home, or how something arrived to your home…or so that’s the way the legend goes. As I look outside, green leaves fall from the sky like confetti, hitting the ground with somber impact. Tall and elevated trees etch the surroundings as the sun sinks rapidly; the silvery light fading from the bare boughs and the watery twilight setting in. My nostril, long unused to it, was quick to detect the smell of wood smoke in the air, blending with the odor of moist summer earth and the saltiness that came up the river with the tide. You know…my name has always resembled what I am. A complete Burden. By no means, would you ever think that such a name would be such an important role of your everyday life. Sometimes it seems like I was put here to carry on the name and yet, I am the name. That’s just the brunt of it, nonetheless. Everything that I try to say, my mentality devours. I try not to talk to people…this is just my mind and how I think. Believe what you may…Block out what you don’t. This is just how I reside. Perhaps the things which happened could only have happened to me. I do not know. I never heard of things like them happening to anyone else. But I am not sorry they did happen. I am in secret deeply and strangely glad. I have heard other people say things--and they were not always sad people, either--which made me feel that if they knew what I know it would seem to them as though some awesome, heavy load they had always dragged about with them had fallen from their shoulders. To most people everything is so uncertain that if they could only see or hear and know something clear they would drop upon their knees and give thanks. That was what I felt myself before I found out so strangely. My life has always been a living hell, and there wasn’t much I could do about it. Destiny played its role...Hell if I believe that. I was only 15 years old when my parents departed this life. Now, do you call that destiny? -- There she was…lying in a pool of her own sweat. All I could do was stare, naive of what was to become. Beside her insipid body rest two bottles of painkillers, Zannex. Her face pressed against the rigid wood floorboards. I knew she was dead as soon as I looked at her. It wasn’t the fact that she wasn’t breathing…it was the innocent stare in her eyes. “Mama…?,” I knelt beside her bed with tears streaming down my face like a waterfall, afraid of what I may stumble on. “Mama, get up…,” my patience was almost running out. I just wanted her to get up and smile, telling me everything was alright. “I don’t want to play any games…come on, get up,” that was it. I broke out in tears and threw my hands over my eyes, trying to hide the corpse of my dead mother. “Mama…please…mama…,” I cried to no avail. I moved my squelchy palms from my sopping eyes, looking at the forefront of her palm “Morphine…?” I picked up the baggy of the liquid and brought it to my nose, breathing in the white fluid. Definitely morphine. I stared at her motionless body as my eyes began to swell up again. I rubbed my wrist over both eyes, trying to keep the tears from falling. The sniffles wouldn’t stop. I got up from my kneeling position, wiping my hands on my khaki shorts. The first thought that ran through my mind was, “What in the hell am I supposed to do with no parents, now?” Just 12 days ago my father, Michael Burden, was shot and killed by an Iranian on his way back to the United States from Iran. Everybody was so jolly about how the war was over…but how in the hell was I to be happy when my father was shot and killed? Now look at my mother, Laura Burden, lying in a pool of slobber and sweat. She didn’t want to live anymore. She didn’t want to live for me any longer. The death of my father brought this on my mother. Suicide. From my standing position, staring at my mother’s corpse, a bloodstained letter stood out at the end of her pale feet. Reaching for the letter, my eyes widened, as I read it aloud, trying to keep in the tears. -- There’s no reason to read the letter again…for it replays in my mind every second of every hour. Who do I blame for what has happened? I blame you. You are one of them, the ones that make things like this happen. I remember the poem that I wrote later that week…the day of my mother’s funeral. “Angel of death come light a candle with me, take my heart and set me free. Broken wings show me the way, misery and despair the company we keep. The candle burns but dark and bleak. Shadows of light so soft and deep, ashes and dust we sow what we reap. Shadows and shimmering light, just cuts on my wrists a bloody sight. Pools in crimson red, as I bow my head to the darkness where I am led. As the angel of death sets me free, as the price is paid for darkness belongs to me. The angel of death takes me, embraces my soul, immersed in the pool of blood that now shimmers in the candle lights glow.Take my hand, my friend of misery, for together we are one of a kind.Fuck the world, fuck society, and fuck life.Angel of death come light a candle with me, take my heart and set me free.” I believe that I have a reason to feel like I do. Everything that I’ve ever loved has been taken from me…I just want my time to come to its closing stages. Call me mad, you have your own opinion. It’s all like a bad dream that won’t end…and yet, you are the nightmare waiting to start again, beginning a new legend. As I close, I want you to remember this. Real legends are made for what they are and what they have done. All the others, the false legends, are a tale of what they wish to be. So, end this nightmare and start my legend over again. The thing is…which legend is real…and which one is just a counterfeit? In the end, all will be decided. In the end…all realism will be attested…to prove who is the one Rising Star of this humanity that we reside in. Kris Burden The black ink drips off of the feather pen as I sign my signature and close the journal… End
