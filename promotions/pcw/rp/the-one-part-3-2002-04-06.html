The One Part 3

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 6th, 2002 You never give up, but you gave up on me. Now that I'm older you seem to stalk me but as a child you neglected and never spoke to me. You left me in a strange new world without a helper. Now you persist and try to be my buddy. WELL NO WAY! Now you back away. Last time I checked I thought you ran. Well what do I know about you. After all I have never known you until, well, NEVER! I am 24 and I never met you. Most people can not say such a thing but I guess I am not most people am I? AM I? A tear? A tear in your eye. It rolls slowly down your cheek. Do you have the right to tear? Do you have the right to be upset with me? Do you have the right to come back into my life. I never left you, damn it, YOU LEFT ME! WHAT DO YOU WANT FROM ME? Not money? Not respect? Not love? But forgiveness? I could have given you money. I never though will ever forgive you. How could I? How could I bring myself to forgive a man like you. Just look at you. YOU'RE NOTHING BUT A DEADBEAT! Now it's my turn. I lived my life alone. Shunned from the world. When I had friends they were never good people. When I had love it was short lived. My anger and torment got me through life. My life has been nothing but a lie. I am a lie and you are the perpetrator of this lie. END IT! What do I mean? WHAT DO I MEAN? KILL ME! You created me, you can destroy me. Now damn it put a bullet in me and take me from this world. You will never receive anything from me but now is your chance to take one last thing from me. MY LIFE! You won't do it! What a surprise. You never have done anything for me in my life. I never knew you. When I ask you to do one simple thing you back out. ONE THING! I asked for one damn thing and you could not even come through. You want forgiveness and you can not even do me this one thing. WHY? Love? You never knew love in your entire life. Don't try and put love on me. You never loved me or anyone you knew. Now if you don't take my life then leave me to do my own bidding. Do it or leave. You won't? THEN LEAVE ME ALONE! ALONE LIKE I ALWAYS WAS BECAUSE OF YOU!
