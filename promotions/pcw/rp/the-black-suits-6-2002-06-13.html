The Black Suits [6]

13 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 13, 2002 The Agency HQ Time Unknown 'Uhhh...where am I?' I asked the question, not really expecting an answer. My head felt like it had been through a car wash, and was spinning around faster than the tea cups at Disney Land. I wasn't sure if I had a concussion or not, but there seemed to be a large bump on my forehead where I had attempted to headbutt Sporty. Of course, I couldn't be sure since my hands were bound down across a chair. I struggled against them, but I found that the more that I struggled, the more it hurt to move. These weren't just small ropes holding my hands together either. They were steel handcuffs, with iron chains clinging them to the chair. The chair itself wasn't very comfortable either. It was solid steel, and I was afraid to move, for fear of cutting myself. A chair like this must have cost the Agency a bundle. Its good to have budgets. I opened my eyes, glad that neither had been bruised shut. If anything, I loved the ability to see. A lot of people took it for granted, but I knew how valuable it really was to my every day life. In the struggle with Sporty, I was pretty sure he had beaten my face into a pulp, but I was glad to see that I still had most of my feeling there. I looked about the room, seeing as though they hadn't been clever enough to bind my neck as well. I was in a completely white room, and nothing was of ill color except for myself and the grey, steel chair I was locked up against. The walls seemed smoother than any material I had ever seen before. It was at that moment that I realized that the walls were sloped in an oval shape. I was sitting in a rectangular room, but a circular room. It did a number on my senses, and suddenly I started to get dizzy. I wasn't usually claustrophobic, but watching the room curve made my stomach turn. The only other time that I felt this way had been on airplanes, trying my best not to throw up on the person sitting next to me. That's why I enjoyed taking the bus to different wrestling events the best - less chance of feeling dazed and confused. The door to the room suddenly opened, and the old white man in a suit walked in. He looked rather calm, not angry or upset, nor happy or joyous. I wasn't sure if he was here to interrogate me or execute me for what I had seen....I was a bit afraid to ask. 'Well, if it isn't Agent Old Man...' I said, still struggling against the chair. I had said that in spite, although I knew I was in the worst position to be making insults. Leave it to me to dig my own grave. 'You are quite the jerk, aren't you? Even tied up to that chair, you still can't shut that mouth of yours, can you?' I shrugged. 'We're not perfect, are we?' He didn't sit, he just stood there looking at me. When he finally spoke, he placed his hands in his pant pockets and began pacing. 'My name is Agent Sourpuss. My partner was Agent Chopsticks...' 'Wow, great names. Did you guys come up with that yourselves, or did the Agency make them up for you?' 'They are codenames. They reflect our own personalities. But you don't have to concern yourself with that, because hopefully you'll never have to see us again after this.' 'Awesome.' Although it had come out sarcastically, I had truly meant it. I didn't want to see these guys again if I could help it, although I had a funny feeling that we would all somehow meet again. It wasn't a happy feeling, and then again, I could be wrong - I wasn't a psychic or anything. 'The Agency, which you have met inadvertently, is a secret organization. Very secret...' 'Like, government protected, right?' 'Something like that. Although, not quite like the Men in Black, which has a similiar theme to what we are. You see, there are things in this world that would probably make your skin crawl and question your very own morality. We are not alone in this universe, Lexian. Do you understand that? I'm sure you do, because you've witnessed it first hand. What attacked you was not a normal alien...he's dangerous.' 'Sporty, huh?' I said, raising an eyebrow. 'That's a great name for such a dangerous butterfly.' 'His kind come from a world fairly close to Earth. For the most part, they are a peace loving race. They hate to leave their world, but some of their kind are a bit...adventurous. Mostly their criminals. Sporty came here about ten years ago, looking to cause trouble and finding it with a string of bank robberies. We put him away, but not before he could brought back to his own world for trial. Now he's running around Earth again...' 'And looking for that crystal...' I said that, not realizing that the crystal wasn't in my back pocket anymore. I felt around for it, but realized that it was gone. I looked up to see Sourpuss looking directly at me. 'It's in a safe place, don't worry...' 'What's so god damn important about that crystal, anyways?' I asked, feeling defensive. I didn't like things stolen from me, especially when I didn't know how important they really were. 'That crystal is not from Earth,' Sourpuss explained. 'That crystal is used to help Sporty's race procreate. With that crystal, they can proliferate...' 'So what's the big deal?' I asked, not truly understanding the meaning of the crystal. 'So he wants to have Sporty Jr. Who are we to stand in his way?' 'You are truly a simple creature, aren't you? Don't you see? That crystal is only found on their world. If Sporty is able to procreate here, he and his offspring could quite possibly take over the world...' My eyes seemed to bulge out of their sockets. 'Aren't you being a bit melodramatic? I mean, can he really take over the world with that one crystal?' Sourpuss shrugged. 'Eh, probably not. But still, he can cause a lot of damage if he is allowed to multiply. That much is agreed upon, correct?' I nodded in agreement. After hearing that the crystal was nothing more than Sporty's genetic link, I decided that it would be in my best interest to keep it away from him. After all, I did spray him in the face with the Super Soaker 2000. That brought up something. 'Hey, what did you do with the crystal?' 'It's in a safe place,' Sourpuss added, not spilling any more details about where the actual crystal was. 'Don't worry, after we erase all of your memories, you'll be free to go.' 'All of my memories?!' I shrieked. The very prospect if losing my precious memories caused me to scream out like a girl. It was fairly embarrassing. 'Pipe down,' Sourpuss shouted back. 'I didn't mean all of them, just the ones from the past few days. You can't remember any of this. We've already gotten your friend from the arena, and a couple of bystanders that saw a bit too much. You're the last one. Geez, I can't believe you're a wrestler...you're such a cry baby.' 'I am not a cry baby!' I shouted, feeling a bit hurt. No one has ever accused me of being such a thing, although, I had never been hunted down by a rogue butterfly alien before either. 'Anyways, we've got to get back down to business,' Sourpuss said, opening the door. As he did, a group of men walked in carrying a small stretcher. Two men came behind me, untying the handcuffs from the chair, and then forcing me to lie down on the stretcher. I struggled, but even these guys were too big for me to handle and fight back against. I was trapped. They placed me on the stretcher, clamping my hands down to it, and binding my feet at the ends. I tried to peek my head up, but they had also restrained my neck using a brace. The only thing I could see was Sourpusses face, which looked down on me as if I was an animal they just had too much trouble with. That scared me, considering they usually put those kind of animals to sleep. I could feel the stretcher being moved underneath me, and I held my breath for a moment as I realized that I could be setup. What if everything that he had told me back there was untrue? What if they didn't intend to wipe my memories as he said? What if...they were going to try to kill me? It would be the logical thing to do, considering I had seen everything, and what I hadn't seen, Sourpuss had told me. Thats the way those secret agencies worked...damn it, I should have known better. After all, I had watched plenty of X-Files episodes to know about secret government shit. The stretcher was taken out to the hallway, with Sourpuss in tow. I wanted to tell him to screw off, and that if I ever got out of this stretcher, I would beat his ass. Unfortunately, I was tied down and I knew he had a gun. I may not be the most intelligent guy on the planet, but I knew when I was beat. I stared directly up at the ceiling, counting the lights that passed my eye. There must have been at least one hundred until I heard voices overhead. The stretcher suddenly stopped, leaving my count behind. I forgotten what number I was on - damn this one track mind of mine. I recognized the voices overhead. It sounded like Chopsticks and Sourpuss were talking. I wondered what exactly they were talking about, but I doubt they would answer me. I was about to get my memory erased - no reason to waste a breath on some memory-less guy. Good for me. That's about the time all hell broke less. Suddenly, the lights all dimmed, causing a shadow to be cast throughout the entire hallway. A red alarm light began to spin, and the alarms began to blare throughout the entire complex. A voice over the intercom continued to shout, 'Intruder alert. Intruder alert.' I knew I was in a lot of shit, and now I was going to be right smack in the middle of the war. I had no weapon, no idea where I was, and I was strapped down to a table. Oh god, how do I find myself in these predicaments? I just hoped they didn't offer me as a sacrifice to whatever was intruding on The Agency's HQ. Whooooosh! I felt a large brush of air pass by us, and all of the attendants, including Sourpuss and Chopsticks, hit the ground immediately. I tried to do the same, but of course, I had forgotten I was strapped to a table. Thats when something hit the stretcher, causing it and myself to fall to the floor. Guns fired, and I heard a familiar scream....Sporty was in the hallway with us. I was on my side, unable to move, only listen at what was going on around me. I saw bodies fly, and guns fire, but soon I realized that Sporty had gotten the advantage. I saw him take two gunshots to the chest and still fly away....something told me I needed to get out of here. I noticed that during the fall, my wrist guards had been shaken off a bit. I could now move around my hand, and I was able to free them both. I then worked over my feet and neck restraints, until I fell from the stretcher and looked up. Sporty was nowhere to be seen, along with Chopsticks or Sourpuss. Bodies lined the ground, telling me that Sporty had obviously been successful in his assault. I saw a body nearby....he was unconscious, and his gun was lying next to him. I picked it up, placing it in my own hand. It was still warm - it had been fired recently. I checked the man's pulse to make sure he was still alive, which he was. It appeared that Sporty hadn't been a killer butterfly, although I didn't check anyone else. I decided right here and now, that I was going to make a stand against Sporty myself. I still had no clue where I was, and I would need the Agency's help to escape. I couldn't do that if Sporty takes them all out, and especially if he starts procreating on me. But this gun would not be enough. I would have to find another form of offense, and I would have to do it soon. So I crept slowly along the hallway, hoping to be a hero...not a memory...
