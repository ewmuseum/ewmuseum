Dinner....(Wizard of Gauze part 3 of 4)

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-2-2002 (The trees had finally pulled away from the winding yellow brick road, and now our adventurer Venneman Pendragon, along with his guide, Lyco the Joblin, was making his way through a vast field that seems to be filled with roses. The sun is high in the sky, and Pendragon is actually smiling. He looks around at the beautiful scenery, quite a change from the previous promo's twisted landscape, and takes in everything. He smiles at Lyco and pats his stomach like he just had a good meal. Lyco does not look amused...) Venneman: Hell of a dinner. I gotta admit; that went down pretty easy after all the travelling I have been doing as of late. Don't you agree Lyco? (Lyco says, nothing, he merely continues to walk. Pendragon chuckles a bit at the non-reply. What happened, anyhow? *******FLASH******* (We are back in the twisted landscape and Pendragon still lies on the ground, laughing. A strange duck has made its way towards them and it stops in front of Lyco. Lyco looks at the duck, with lust in his eyes. He leans down and begins to stroke the duck's feathers.... Lyco: There's a good girl...How's my baby doing? Couldn't get enough of daddy, huh? Had to come back for round two? Well...I got all you can handle and more, if my friend will give us some privacy... He shoots Pendragon a look that Pendragon completely ignores. Venneman: You know what goes nice with a lot of travelling? A good dinner. And I gotta say, that duck is looking pretty tempting...And I can see your mouth watering as well. Lyco: Of course my mouth is watering...This is Yarkstar, my lover....Aren't the piercings lovely? Pendragon peers at the duck, and he sees that the duck does in fact have piercing. Pendragon blinks in surprise...What kind of duck has piercings? Pendragon looks from the duck to Lyco, and then back to Yarkstar. He shakes his head in disbelief... Venneman: No way. You guys are an item? That means the similarities between you and Syko run even DEEPER!!! You guys are practically joined at the HIP! Regardless, I think that tonight we shall feast on duck! Venneman moves quickly towards the Duck and draws Excalibur. He draws back to strike and Lyco screams, in as much as his rasp allows him to... Lyco: NOOOOOOOOOOO!!! I LOVE HER!!! SHE IS ALL I HAVE!!!! Venneman: She is also the only edible thing that has presented itself so far in this damned land... With that, Pendragon lops the head of the duck off with one swift motion. Lyco crumples to the road, sobbing. Pendragon wastes no time as he snatches the duck up and skins it quickly. He then walks to a tree by the side of the road and cuts off a branch that he strips and sharpens. He rams this through the duck and props it against said tree. He then returns to Xerxes and reaches into a saddle bag. He rummages around a minute before he pulls out two metal stakes with circles on one end. He places these next to the rabbit and takes off into the woods. He returns with an armload of firewood and kindling he drops the kindling to the ground and proceeds to dig a hole with his sword. After he has the hole at a depth of about one foot, he dumps some kindling in and pulls a piece of flint and a piece of steel from his pocket. He kneels down and repeatedly strikes the two materials together. Sparks fly and soon the kindling catches fire. As the fire grows, Pendragon starts to add larger pieces and soon his fire is of a nice size. He jams the two rods into the ground on either side of the hole and places his rabbit on the stick between them...Instant rotisserie. Soon, the sound of sizzling meat is heard as Pendragon turns the spit. He looks over at Lyco and shakes his head. Finally, he gets up and walks over to his horse again, and again rummages in the saddle bag. He pulls out a cooking thermometer this time, and he walks over and sticks the duck with the thermometer.... Venneman: All right...Ready to eat. Can't be too careful with poultry... He pulls the stick from between the rods and walks over to his saddlebag again. He reaches in and pulls out a couple of plates and some silverware, and a bottle of wine. I guess he is always prepared. He walks over next to Lyco and takes a seat on the road. He pops the cork from the wine and takes a deep draw from the bottle. He cuts the duck into a few portions and offers some to the sobbing Lyco. Lyco pushes it away and Pendragon shrugs as he digs in to his own plate of food. He smiles as he chews the delectable meat, and soon his plate is empty. He reaches over and takes the plate from in front of Lyco and eats it too. He finishes the wine, stands up and takes the dishes back to his saddlebag. After a yawn and a stretch, he hops into the saddle... Venneman: If the master wants you to bring me to him, I think we had better be moving along. No sense crying over spilled milk, or roasted duck.....ON YOUR FEET NOW, WHELP!!! Startled from his depressed revelry, he wipes his eyes and stumbles to his feet.... Lyco: Very well...But when the Master gets what he wants from you, then you are mine...My dear Yarkstar...I can't believe it!) *******FLASH****** (We are back in the field of roses, and Pendragon smiles as he thinks of the wonderful meal. Lyco is silent, and in the distance, a huge reflective tower can be seen peeking over the horizon. Pendragon sees this and looks at Lyco, who does not acknowledge it.) Venneman: SO is that the abode of your 'master'? Lyco: I it is. Now if you don't mind, I don't want to talk to you...Murderer. Venneman: Don't fret about that...I am sure you can find a new 'one and only'. And I did enjoy eating her, though maybe not the way that you have enjoyed her. (A look of rage passes over the child-man's face. He stares straight ahead as Pendragon laughs at his own wittiness. He looks a tad uneasy as he looks toward the tower....) 'Where was this git taking me? I wasn't sure if I was walking into a trap or a party. Everything in this world confused me. Why was there a Syko around every corner? Why was everything in the land a larger and more distorted version of my own reality? Was I really dreaming, or was this land a true danger to me? It is hard for me to comprehend a land wherein my authority is not absolute...A land where I am not the sovereign. This was a land such as that. I only wondered who this Master was, and what he wanted with me...' (Finally, the pair reach the tower. An immensely tall, smooth monolith, the whole thing seemed to be made of glass. A beautiful, lone sentry in an immensely flat landscape. There appeared to be no door, but Lyco walks up to the tower and places his hand in a certain spot. Instantly, a door appears, and a hole opens in the door. From inside comes a voice...) Voice: Who is it? Lyco: Lyco...I bring the hero to the Master. (The hole disappears and the door opens. Lyco walks through to the darkness, and Pendragon draws his sword and follows, slowly and cautiously...) 'I hate the unknown, and this most certainly qualifies. I mean, what the hell was I doing? Trusting a being whose friend I just ate? Was I crazy? Perhaps a little. With pure genius and power, sometimes insanity is merely a stone's throw away. I figured I was about to be embroiled in some kind of struggle for supremacy...Maybe for this entire world, maybe for all worlds. This tower could certainly qualify as a dimensional axis. My only hope was that I could take care of business here and wake up, or bounce back to my own reality...Although I have met a couple versions of Syko the Goblin in this realm, I had the real one to deal with back in my world and PCW...The man wanted my title...He wants me to feel pain. Neither want is gonna come to fruition for him. Greatness is my destiny, championship gold is my birthright. I wasn't about to let some neo Goth punk take what I had striven so hard to attain. That would not happen. In a fantasy world, everyone could be a champion...In my world, only a chosen few hold the right. And I am chosen. I guess I could end with some kind of catch phrase, but I need to concentrate on the trap that awaits me....' (As soon as Pendragon is through the door, it disappears as if it never was. The shot pulls back and shows the whole tower, which now almost seems to glow as the color of blood...) >>>>TO BE CONTINUED
