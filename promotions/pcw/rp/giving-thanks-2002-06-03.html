Giving Thanks

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/3/02 From the Words of Skylar Green: So, I'm the PCW World Champion now. Funny how I could reach this before some of the people who were around before me, and those who got here after me. Funny that I could reach this when there were actually many doubters. 'A blind guy can't get anywhere.' Nice try. 'You're not talented enough.' And you are? I'm not one to brag, so I won't. I'm just pointing out the flat fact. But I feel like this is the time that thanks are necessary. So here it is, my list of those whom I owe thanks. Sandy Wong: Yes, jackasses, I know she's dead. This is more a salute to her memory. I've never been more focused since she's been gone. Before then I was really just hopping up the ladder to the top without much of a real direction. Bless her heart, she never minded. She was there the whole time supporting me. With her passing, I had some business to take care of, what with making Violence Jack my bitch and all (and I proved that I can psychologically screw people up without raping them; Villam Ender take note). With that out of the way, I went straight for the top. Using The Pharaoh as a diversionary tactic to force Tommy into the offensive, I was able to get the World Title match that I wanted in the first place and I was able to beat he and Titu. I feel like Sandy was there in spirit, so thanks. Rush Mason: Feels weird thanking you, but I will. You were more motivation to win. After all, after upstaging you twice and retiring you from PCW (effectively, the wrestling world since you never wanted to work anywhere but PCW), to win the World Title right in front of you felt good. You wanted to get to this before me, and twice you attempted to use me as a stepping stone toward that goal. I can't blame you, it's instant credibility to CLEANLY defeat me, but I beat you back with a stick and stayed on course. I 'kept my eye on the prize', so to speak. Tania, Rafaela, and Ines: I'm flattered that you'd like to give this family-thing another shot. You've lost a lot, what with Julio getting gunned down, then the same thing happening to Marcus a little later. Those two were collectively 'Dad' to you girls; Tania especially. We tried before, what with me taking legal guardianship of you as things were supposed to be written down in your fathers' respective wills. They tried to get out of the ghetto, using DJing, rapping, and wrestling as their means of escape. Sadly, they lost their lives before they could build you a better life. They were old friends of mine from a previous federation we'd been in together, and I must've come off as a really trustworthy guy for them to entrust your protection to me. At least now, they can rest easy knowing that the best thing that they wanted for you, an escape from the same dangerous streets that took their lives, can be fulfilled. Julio Garcia and Marcus Jackson, the original Rhythm & Rhyme: Back when I was Enigma/Seppuku in UWWC, you guys came in as a tag team recruited to help out with the poor doubles scene. The federation was down to two regular teams that traded the belts back and forth. The fans were starting not to care (what little fans there were, anyway). You two came in and quickly became the talk of the town. Well, the five towns that we visited anyway. Julio, you were 5'10' 212 at the heaviest, but your strength and shootfighting techniques made you seem bigger. You were more the rapper anyway. Marcus, you were 6'10' and 295 pounds of muscle. You could've tossed anyone from pillar to post, but instead you were the high-flying submissionist of the team. You were a DJ, a walking sound factory of a beatbox, and just seemed to be the more fun of the two. For the little stereotypes that UWWC had, you two broke them all. You weren't a tag team of identical partners, you weren't the same old 'Bill and Ted' or 'Gothic' tag team. You knew what you wanted, you knew what you were good at, and you knew that wrestling was how to get it. You won those Tag Team Titles with your whole family in attendance. That was the first time I'd met your daughters. I couldn't tell you what they look like now, but they seem to be growing up fast. Tania has taken responsibility for her younger sisters much more than before, when she just considered them a nuisance. It was you two that gave me your family. In turn, you've given me something to fight for outside of myself. I thank you and I promise that I'll keep them safe. PCW: I'd like to thank the federation, all of its fans, and all of the workers that never get any spotlight time. I'd like to thanks the technicians who make sure I'm aware of everything that they leave lying around, and give me the specs on the ring ramp and the little variations in the size of the ring from place to place. I'd like to thank those camera people who made sure all these 18 months that if I had something to say, that they dutifully recorded it for posterity. Thanks to the caters, whomever they are. Those little mini-pizzas you make are fabulous. Thanks to the commentators for giving their opinions. Thanks to Byron Calver for paying PCW so much attention that he got released from PWC. Thanks to everybody. My friends and partners within PCW: Everyone who's agreed to team with me -- James Edwards, Miss Moonlight, most recently Tessa Windsor (I'll leave her name at that as a courtesy to her). I've left off Gaz Green's name. I'm aware of that. Anyone who's backed me up, whether physically or even just in spirit. Everyone who gave a heartfelt congratulations when I won the World Championship, those whom I can talk to in the back without getting an empathic sense that they're plotting my downfall... thanks to all of you. Some would say that PCW is filled with nothing but a bunch of selfish pricks. I call those people 'the competition.' It's a singular effort in PCW as much as it is a team effort. I'm not going to go Red Army on anyone, but we all know that one person isn't going to carry this federation, or the shows, so I want to thank and congratulate you on helping to keep the PCW product at the top of the TV rankings. And lastly.... My opponents: Whatever I may let on, remember that I respect you, and even if I seem to take you lightly, I still recognize your ability in the ring. So I say to you, thank you for not sucking. Seriously. I can't look good if you all are horrendous at this, so thanks for having talent. That concludes my list of thanks. If I have time, I'll get around to the list of people to whom I owe no thanks.
