The first REAL Promo(Part 1)

23 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 23, 2002 [We are in beautiful Denver, a city with a mountain back drop. It’s nice and cool over there. However, the rains have hit, and it is rather damp. The area that we are in is actually quite the nice neighbourhood. Several large, warehouse type building are within a few acre lot. All of the buildings are painted in a very heartless, cold, and unforgiving grey. Cars are heading in and out of the main gate. We can only assume at this point that this is some sort of television studio. Several cars are limousines, some are exotic sports cars. A car barrels around a corner, heading for the studio. The guard, who is manning the gate control, sees a speeding blue Subaru WRX. He sits there eating an apple as he waves everyone through the exit gate, the enter gate is down. The WRX seems to be heading at the gate, at approximately 75 miles per hour. The WRX reaches the gate, and breaks through it; the guard immediately scrambles a security team and hits the alarm.] Guard: SCRAMBLE, SCRAMBLE! INTRUDER BREACHED THE FRONT GATE, HEADING FOR STUDIO 3B, INTERCEPT IMMEDIATELY! [Meanwhile at Studio 3B, we see James Gallagher, the man who Eddie Mandrake’s staff representative, and his assistant Steve, standing outside the main entrance door of the studio. James is wearing a PCW Golf Shirt and Dress Pants, while Steve is wearing a Hawaiian shirt with a Miyagawa T-shirt underneath. James is pacing impatiently and Steve is drinking a Vanilla Coke.] Steve: Damn, this is good [BEEP] James: Please, this is important; Nelson will have my ass on a cross if Eddie doesn’t get here soon. Steve: He’ll be here. James: You do realize he’ll have your ass is on a cross as well. Steve: (Slurping) PFFFT! (Cough) WHAT?! James: Yeah, you better start worrying. [SCREEEEEECH!] James: What the hell is that? [The blue WRX rounds the corner in a power slide, and finally hits the brakes near James’ position. The security guards block the exit of the area, as Studio 3B has a dead end. Security pulls out their guns, and points them at the WRX.] Security Guards: FREEZE! [The WRX remains still, engine still running, left turn signal blinking, brake lights on. The driver, who we can’t see, remains in the car.] Lead Security Guard: Alright, step out of the car, and place your hands on your head. [The driver leans to the passenger side, and comes back up. The driver steps out of the car, and it’s Eddie Mandrake, but he’s holding the MP5 that he had from his previous job! He aims it at the group of guards!] Eddie: Uhm…You were saying? [The guards drop their weapons and run for the hills. Eddie lowers his, and shoots it into the air…It was unloaded. James just looks at Eddie. Steve drops his coke.] James: What the hell do you call that? Eddie: Road Rage Emergency Kit. Steve: Does it work? Eddie: It does. You’d be surprised. James: You are almost an hour late…you have to work on the punctuality portion of your… Eddie: Look, I can kill someone on national television with incredible timing. Yet, you talk to me about punctuality. Don’t even bother. [Eddie walks into the building, James, and Steve follow.] James: Today Eddie, you will learn the art of the “promo.” Basically explained, it is a way to hype a match on television. Most wrestlers do this for house shows to hype their match, or to promote the card when they enter the town. Eddie: Uh-huh. Can you explain to me why I have to do this? Steve: Everyone here does it at one time or another. Eddie: Great. [Eddie enters his “Trailer” which is just outside of the main stage that James has booked for Eddie. Eddie eyes widen as he sees all the things that he has in the trailer.] Eddie: Wow, everyone gets all this crap? James: For the most part yes. The higher in the card you get, the more popularity you receive, the more stuff you get in the trailer. [Eddie heads in and sits down in what looks to be a very comfortable chair.] Eddie: Oh man…This chair feels NICE! James: You should see the ones that the Main Eventers get. All in good time my friend. I’ll give you a few minutes to get used to this, and then, we’ll get started. Eddie: What do you mean get started? James: On your promo of course, feel free to raid the mini-bar…but make sure you don’t get too drunk. Only Duke Williams raids the mini-bar and PCW pays for it. Eddie: I see. So, I’ll have to pay for my booze? Steve: Yup, any and all soft drinks, such as Vanilla Coke and Sprite are free however. [Steve cracks open another can of Vanilla Coke.] Eddie: You’re gonna look like a can of Vanilla Coke when you finish. Anyway, come back in fifteen, by then I’ll be ready for any teachings you may have. James: Alright. Steve, let’s go. [James and Steve leave, as Eddie remains seated in the incredibly comfortable Laz-E-Boy as we fade to black…for now]
