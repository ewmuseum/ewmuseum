Deep thinking, throw in a little fun to the mix, you got Hank~!

29 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-29-03 ~ Mobile, Alabama home of the Mobile Civic Center, the exact place that PCW Tuesday Turmoil takes place only about a half-day away from now. The scene opens to Hank~! Sitting in a bar, his elbows on the counter, leaning forward, and eyeing the beer bottle in front of him. Hank~! However just pushes it away and stands up from where he is sitting and digs into the pocket of his jeans. Hank Leaves a couple of dollars on the counter then exits the bar. The scene shifts to Hank walking down the sidewalk along the street, hands stuffed into the pockets of his long black leather trench coat. Hank comes to a crosswalk, many cars stopped and many cars going. Hank takes a seat at the foot of met pole that is the base for a stoplight. Hank watches traffic go by him for a few moments then stands up, as he is sick of people just staring at him. Hank then crosses the street that takes him to his hotel. Hank walks over to his Black Van in the parking lot and unlocks the sliding door and sees his girlfriend Jenn still asleep, which is normal for somebody to be asleep by three in the morning. Hank climbs in and kisses Jenn on the forehead, then crashes on a pile of clothes beside her. ~ Hank: “Good-night my angel.” ~ Hank begins to nod off, but can’t fall asleep completely. Something is running through his mind that keeps him awake. Jenn eventually slowly awakens and looks up at her boyfriend Hank with a tired gaze in her eyes. ~ Jenn: “Why are you still up hun?” Hank: “Jus’ thinkin’, go back to bed I don’t want to keep you up.” Jenn: “Well I’m partly up now.” Hank: (smiles) “That you are and I apologize if it was I who awoke you.” Jenn: “No, just couldn’t stay asleep.” (yawns) “You lock the door?” (Hank nods) “That’s good to hear, I wouldn’t want anybody to die upon smelling your socks.” (flashes a smile) Hank: “Ha, ha, you’ve managed to survived this long babe.” Jenn: “Yeah well I’m used to them.” Hank: “Oh how true a statement.” Jenn: “So are you going to get some sleep for your match?” Hank: “I thought I’d jus’ hit the gym when it opened.” Jenn: “What all did you do when we parted ways yesterday evening?” Hank: “Pretty much sat in a bar and made a bottle of Corona last hours upon hours. I jus’ took the time to do some deep thinking about where I stand in the world, PCW to be more precise.” Jenn: “And what conclusion did you come up with?” (sitting up and wrapping her self in her blanket) Hank: “That I’m jus’ a small hardcore fish in a large salty ocean. There are sharks, there are whales, there are other things bigger and better than me and I can’t let them get me down. I am who I am and there is no changing that. No matter how hard I try to avert it, there is no changing that fact.” Jenn: (nods) “Any thinking about your match at all?” Hank: “Everything I jus’ said was the basis of my match. Each one of my opponents today are bigger fish than I ever was! They’ve been around and know the ropes, not me, I’ve been away from the Primetime ring for quite sometime now. They all have an edge on me, so you could call them ‘the sharks’ if you will. I’m the small fish that has to dodge, outsmart them even.” Jenn: “So you’re the veteran returning to the ring to reacquaint yourself with the new talent?” Hank: “No, I’m here to beat an old school beating into the newcomers. I’m here to show all the guys in the back whom came after me, the cruiserweights whom don’t realize a man like me hath paved the way for their damn success. I’m in this match to prove the newcomers need to study the veterans, need to know their damn history or they’re damn well set to repeat it.” Jenn: “You are one of the PCW’s finest.” (smiles) “Finest in general as well.” Hank: “Well I am Hank~! After all.” (smirks) “Anyway I’m going to get a few hours rest before I hit the gym. Feel free to drive around, you most likely won’t awaken me once I fall into a deep slumber.” Jenn: “Don’t let the fumes kill you.” (snickers) Hank: “Will you cut it out woman! It don’t smell that badly back here!” Jenn: “Yeah it does, we’re just used to it.” (smirks) Hank: “That’s it after tonight’s match me and you are coming back here and having our own match to even this score my way!” (grins) Jenn: (smiles) “Works for me, you’re on!” ~ Hank falls a sleep as Jenn lies back down, lying her head on his chest. The scene cuts off to black. ~
