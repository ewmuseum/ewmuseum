Battle of the opposites.

Date: not entered [i]This Sunday two men will step into a ring together and fight for their careers. [/I] There is something about Jon Kano, a man of only 26 he is already a veteran and a legend. The fact that he is incredibly talented has been totally outshone by his ability to survive; it is that ability for which he has become a legend of PCW. Never before has one man's career been under imperilment so many times, never before has one man had so many enemies in such high places. Enemies like former PCW Heavyweight Champion Alan Kriegman, enemies like CEO of PCW and former Heavyweight Champion Tyler Nelson. They too have both endeavored to end Kano's career, to stop this incredible run of his and they too have been unsuccessful, despite all the power they possess. This Sunday Corey Williams has an opportunity to end Jon Kano's career, a probable chance, which is better than anyone else ever has. The way that Williams got the chance that an evil super-genius and the most powerful man in the sports entertainment business never got is by putting it all on the line. Tyler Nelson and Alan Kriegman used threats, connections and constant beatings to try and get rid of Kano, but never did they put their career on the line, never did they go all the way. For possibly the first time in PCW history, someone has a chance to end the career of PCW's TRUE legend, indefinitely. If Jon Kano loses this Sunday it is quite simple, he will not come back. If Jon Kano loses this Sunday we will never see him in a PCW ring again. After all that has befallen the man who rocketh the mosteth, this may be the first time there is no backup plan, there is no survival clause. Despite overcoming the actions of so many, including the afore mentioned geniuses and CEOs, Jon Kano's career could be ended on Sunday in the most ironic fashion. It is ironic because the man that has the chance to end his career is his complete opposite. Initially, Corey Williams and Jon Kano appear very similar, both are tall, large and both have long blonde hair and an incredibly athletic physique. However Williams is a little slimmer and taller whereas Kano has a thicker yet shorter body. Both men are learned in the art of technical wrestling; however Williams' style leans more towards highflying whilst when the chips are down Kano relies on a powerhouse of strength to help him stay alive. Both men have struck it rich outside of PCW with their companies, CCW Enterprises and Kano Enterprises Inc. respectively. However whilst Kano used his popularity to help his company become successful, Williams uses his company to help him become popular and successful in the sports entertainment industry. Corey Williams was born with rich parents whom he rebelled against and went to live in the humble streets but Jon Kano is a self-made man who has worked his way up from humble beginnings to get himself to where he is today. Both men are, surprisingly enough, of similar ages, only some two years apart which happens to be the very age of PCW. However, because of Kano's slight seniority he fights for the old school of PCW whilst Corey Williams fights for the new school of PCW, claiming to be its King. To sum it up, Corey Williams has always talked the talk whilst Jon Kano has always walked the walk. [I]This Sunday two men who are complete opposites will step into a ring together and fight for their careers. [/I] Opposites though they are, the manner in which they are opposites is notably complementary. And as such Jon Kano and Corey Williams, complete opposites, can consider themselves equals. [I]This Sunday two men who are complete opposites will step into a ring together and fight for their careers, but they will do it as equals. [/I] There have been two eras in PCW so far. Jon Kano made his name in the first of those eras; he lived through Meanstreak's glory days, the Media takeover at Shanghaied and Stewart Hix's title reign. Corey Williams made his name in the second era; he lived through the Old School stable, Judas Steele's 'big return' and the Wild Deuces/Asylum split. At the Third Dawning, all that is in the past must be left behind as nothing more than fond memory as two men will face each other for the right to live through the third era. [I]This Sunday two men who are complete opposites will step into a ring together and fight for the right to live through the third era, but they will do it as equals. [/I] The third era is the prestigious era, one which Corey Williams himself has hailed, though usually in reference to his presence in it, as the 'golden era' of PCW. It is an era where new legends will be created, where the past will be left behind. It is an era in which dreams will be made or crushed; a tradition which will start at its dawn with many superstars putting their dreams on the line from the hopeful rising stars right up to the owners in the main event. All of them are fighting for their dreams to come true in the third era. The first era was old school, the second one was new, but this third era will eventually transcend every concept of time as it smashes record after record and replaces all history and legacy both old and new, replacing them with superior characters, storylines and philosophies. It truly will be a golden era, one that any wrestler will want to be a part of, particularly Jon Kano and Corey Williams. Jon Kano believes that if he makes it through to the third era, passing his greatest test ever, that he is a certifiable legend destined for the PCW record books, no longer to just be forgotten like the rest of the has-beens. For Corey Williams, making it to the third era means success. In his sick and twisted mind the third era and success mean the same thing to him. He truly believes that if he beats Kano at Third Dawning his career will instantly do a 180 and he will become the single most successful wrestler in PCW history a short time thereafter. Whether or not you believe what they do, there is no denying that both men has as good as reason in their mind as ever to win this match. The third era is the promised land for both men and they will do anything short of forty years of wandering to reach it. [I]This Sunday two men who are complete opposites will step into a ring together and go to any lengths for the right to live through the third era. They will enter as equals but one man will leave with it all and the other with nothing. [/I] Should be one hell of a fight.
