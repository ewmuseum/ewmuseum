Together We Stand, Divided We Fall

9 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/9/02 Time: 1: 15 P.MLocation: New York CityScene: Apartment “All in all were just another brick in the wall.” The scene opens with Juan Borro lying back in front of the television set. Juan has been sitting there the whole day trying to remember parts of his past. There has been little movement from him the whole day and Juliet seems a little nervous. Juliet looks over at Juan, and sees how strange he’s acting. She decides to give Mandy a call about what do to. (Juliet dials Mandy’s number, and Mandy answers the phone.) Mandy: Hello! Juliet: Hi! Mandy it’s me and I have a little bit of a problem that I have to talk to you about. Do you have a few minutes? Mandy: Sure I do, tell me what’s bothering you? You seem really nervous should I come over there. Juliet: No, I just want some reliable advice. You see Juan has been seeing things the last few days and I’m finding it a bit odd. Mandy: He’s been seeing things? Juliet: Yes. And I don’t know what to do? All day today, he’s been sitting on the couch and not saying anything. Mandy: Why don’t you just let him have his peace. Sometimes Kevin likes to have his own space also sometimes, so we should just let it be. Juliet: Okay, if you say so. Well thanks Mandy I think I’ll take a little bit of a rest myself; it’s probably just nothing. Mandy: Yea, and don’t be phased by it, it’s nothing. Well I have to get going, I’ll talk to you later. (The two hang up and the scene goes back to Juan Borro who is still sitting on the couch seat.) Juan Borro is sitting there practically lifeless, staring at a blank television set. In Juan’s mind a bunch of things are going on. Daddy's flown across the ocean Leaving just a memory (Juan Borro has flashes of his father and the last time he saw him as a child, right before he left Juan’s life. Juan remembers the nights he’d wait up up for his father to come home, when in the back of the head he knew he would never be returning.) A snapshot in the family albumDaddy, what else did you leave for me?Daddy, whatcha leave behind for me? (Juan has flashes of the taxmen coming to the house and demanding the bills. He remembers them taking him out of his house and bringing his to an orphanage.) All in all it was just a brick in the wallAll in all it was all just bricks in the wall (Through all of the hardships and hell he was put through, nobody cared about how he felt and what was going on in his mind. They never asked him how he felt, because to them he was just another lose soul they had to take care of.) Juan’s focus then goes over to the times where he lived in the orphanage, and how the people there treated him and the other children so poorly. We don't need no educationWe don't need no thought control (Juan remembers the lessons they were taught, and how they brain washed the other children into always giving back to the orphanage. Trying to make all of us believe that if it weren’t for them, we would be nothing. ) No dark sarcasm in the classroomTeacher, leave them kids aloneHey, Teacher, leave them kids alone (We had absolutely no rites. We’d try to make things fun and happy, but they wouldn’t give us the satisfaction of that. In the nights there were the troubled children and I’ll never forget the screams they screamed from the beatings they received.) All in all it's just another brick in the wallAll in all you're just another brick in the wall (We meant nothing to them, and we started to feel like we would mean nothing to anyone. That’s when I decided I had to get out of that place, and had to get out quickly.) Juliet becomes worried as she hears Juan roaring and grunting in the other room. She tries to ignore it, but just can’t. She rushes over to the living room, and puts her hands on Juan’s shoulder. Juan turns around, and stares, but doesn’t speak a single word.) Juliet: Juan, I don’t exactly know what’s going on, but maybe you should go take a Tylenol or something, because you don’t look very good. (Juliet puts her arm around Juan and gives him a gentle kiss on his cheek. She then looks in his eyes, but he turns away. Juan: Don’t look at me like this. I don't need no arms around me and I don't need no drugs to calm me. Juliet: Juan what are you talking about. What has been going on with you the last few days, you seem almost like your possessed. Juan: I have seen the writing on the wall don't think I need anything at all. Juliet: Juan, what are you saying? Are you trying to tell me that you need something, because if you need anything I’ll be glad to get it. Juan: No. Don't think I need anything at all (Juliet begins to become very emotional over Juan and the way he is acting. Juliet doesn’t know what to do, but when she looks in Juan’s eyes she doesn’t see the man she first laid eyes on. Something happened the last few days, and she wants to know what it is.) Juliet: Juan, can’t you just tell me what’s going on in that head of yours, I’m afraid for you and I’m afraid for myself. Juan: All in all I’m just another brick in the wall. Juliet: Juan, I can’t take this, we need to do something and something quick. I’m going to call Kevin over. (Juliet calls Kevin to come over. About thirty minutes later Kevin arrives nervously hoping his friend is all right.) Kevin: Where is he? Juliet: He’s out there. (Juliet point outside in the cold weather where Juan is just standing and looking straight up into the sky. Kevin walks down the aisle of flowers, and whistles to get Juan’s attention. Juan turns around but just looks back up at the sky.) Kevin Klaymore: Hey you, out there in the cold, getting lonely, getting old, can you hear me? Juan doesn’t answer so Kevin continues to speak. Kevin Klaymore: Hey you, standing in the aisles with itchy feet and fading smiles can you feel me? Juan doesn’t answer again. Kevin Klaymore: Hey you don't help them to bury the light. Juan finally turns around and acknowledges Kevin’s existence. He stares at him stunned, almost like Kevin knows exactly what he is feeling himself. Juan draws closer to Kevin, who he looks eye to eye with. Kevin Klaymore: Don't give in without a fight. Juan: I don’t know how you know, but it almost seems like you know the feeling I’m going through. Kevin: I do know what you’re going through Juan. I’ve had my fair share of bad moment in my past, but you can’t let them haunt you, because they’ll do what ever they can to make you go crazy. Juan: But it’s almost like I can’t go on when having all of these feelings. I need to find a way to get rid of them, but I don’t know how. Kevin: I know how. Juan: Then how? Kevin: Find something to love. Whether it be Juliet or your career and don’t just think about it, but let it guide you life. Let it be your light into the future and no matter what don’t let the past hide the light. Juan thinks about it for a little while before answering. Juan: Kevin, thank you. You may have just saved me from complete depression. I don’t know what I was thinking feeling sorry for myself. People out there could only wish they had the good fortune I had. Kevin: Well if you ask me, you better go back inside, and patch things up with Juliet, because she looks very lost. Juan walks inside the house, into the corner of the room, where he sees Juliet on the phone with Juliet, with tears rolling down her eyes. Juan: Hey you, out there on your own sitting naked by the phone would you touch me? Juliet comes up to Juan, and wipes the tears off of her eyes. She takes her hand and gently puts it on his chest. Juliet: Hey you, with your ear against the wall waiting for someone to call out would you touch me? Juan returns the act of love, as he puts his hand on her chest, and looks deep into her beautiful eyes. Juan: Hey you would you help me to carry the stone? Juliet: Of course I will Juan. You know I’m here for you whether it be in times of thick or thin. Juan: In that case, open your heart I'm coming home. I’m sorry Juliet, I just felt so bad about my friend who died for me in the prison. All of his dreams were only fantasy. The wall was too high, as you can see. No matter how he tried he could not break free and the worms ate into his brain. Juliet: It’s sad to hear Juan, but this man doesn’t seem like e had much to live for. Maybe, he felt helping you would be his good deed before death. You did say he died with a smile on his face. I will always pray for his soul, because if it weren’t for him I wouldn’t be here with you right now. (The two rejoice, with a long kiss. The two then hold hands and run outside before Kevin leaves in his car.) Juan: Hey you out there on the road, always doing what you're told can you help me? Kevin: You know I’m here to help Juan. And don’t you tell me there’s no help at all. Juan: Because together we stand, divided we fall. Scene Fades to Black.
