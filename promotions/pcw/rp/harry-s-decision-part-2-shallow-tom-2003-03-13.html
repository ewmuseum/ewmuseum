Harry's Decision - Part 2 - Shallow Tom

13 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 13, 2003 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Harry, what in the name of bogusness are we doing? Is this some kind of twisted This Is Your Life type of thing? Because if it is you can just KEEP ME AT HOME!! I can do much more bodacious things there. 'Heavy Metal' Harry: You could do that, brother. You could sit out back by the pool bathing in the sun with two beautiful women at your side while your sipping back a nice cool iced tea. In fact, you could have some Beach Boys tunes going in the background as the two women are feeling your chest and whispering sweet nothings into your ear. You could be doing THAT instead of being with your brother, who after March Madness is leaving to pursue his own ventures, and going on this 'bogus' mission. [The blue mustang is going at a constant sixty miles per hour with Harry behind the wheel. His brown hair, tied in a pony tail, flapping back in the wind. Tommy sits in the passenger seat with his blue silk shirt opened to expose his tanned, muscular chest as he has an orgasmic look on his face] Harry: Are you still with me over there? [Tommy just stares forward with his eyes wide open and his mouth moving as if he's talking but there are no words coming out. Harry curses himself for trying to prove the point because Tommy probably stopped listening once he got to the part about the women] Harry: Your Rent-A-Hero's bitch. [Immediately Tommy turns his head and points his index finger in his brothers face. Harry doesn't even flinch] Tommy: Hey! There are a lot of heinous things you can say to a man but THAT was totally uncalled for, dude. Harry: I know but I just wanted to get your attention. Tommy: Well, that's your fault to begin with. You had me on the bodacious beach with some radical babes. What did you want me to do? Just ignore it? [Harry turns his head to Tommy and begins nodding] Harry: Yes, I did. For a guy who had sex this morning, then had sex with a woman in an elevator at the Los Angeles Mental Hospital, and then got a hummer at the gas station you sure had your mind of sex. [Tommy shrugs] TTT: Dude, I've got a most important night coming up. A title defense and a shot at the World title all in one night. It's going to be a most difficult task for The Tubular One but everybody, including myself, knows that I'm going to pull through with the victory. Rent-A-Jerkface, Jackson, and Venneman Pentagon won't even phase me as I march to a gnarly, and successful, title defense. Then it's a battle royal. All I have to do is NOT get thrown over the top rope...I mean...how hard is that?! But in speaking of sex...why are we going to Mindy's house? You haven't talked to that babe in like eight years, dude. [Harry sighs as his thoughts span out to the past. Mindy Hughes was the first woman that he ever fell in love with. Even though many things are hazy from those days he does remember the wonderful times he spent with the lovely Mindy. Many didn't think much of her back then, but Harry saw so much more in her than everybody else. Maybe it was that, but maybe he was just so messed up that he just went with the flow. Either way he still spent some great times with this girl and he needs to see how she's doing. Maybe she wants to see him again, or maybe wants to see Harry in an unmarked grave. Honestly, he just wants to know why the two of them ended because that's one part that he doesn't really remember. Tommy TRIED to explain it but all he did was insult the poor girl and that just got Harry's search for answers to a screeching halt] Harry: For some reason I remember having some strong feelings for this woman, Tommy. I don't really see her face but I can see her spirit and I think that's all I really need. [Tommy rolls his eyes] Tommy: Well, thank GOD that you only see her spirit, compadre. If you saw anything else you'd probably be losing that vegetarian lunch that you ate an hour ago. May I say that having all vegetables is MOST BOGUS! Harry: She can't be THAT BAD, bro. You make it sound like she is a female gorilla. Tommy: Well, SHE IS like a female gorilla! [Harry just sighs knowing that he truly can't take his brothers opinion on many things. But what if Tommy IS telling him the truth about her? What if she is really disgusting and makes you want to spew? Can he really handle that? Could that be why things ended between the two of them? Maybe she will never forgive him for the way that he treated her one night or something. Jeez, all these questions are really beginning to give Harry a headache and he doesn't have an aspirin with him] Tommy: You know that movie...Shallow Hal.... Harry: Yes, I watched it the other night. Good flick. Jack Black is very entertaining. Tommy: That dude is pudgy. Harry: Well, so am I. Tommy: SHHH!! Let me explain something to you. Harry: Ok. Tommy: Hal got that most bogus hypnosis and he saw heinous babes as bodacious. Now that most righteous Gweneth was a hottie, but really she was just some really disgusting fat babe. But Hal was the only one that saw her as this totally bitchin' honey! It was CRAZY! Like how she weighed down the boat and everything! He should have TOTALLY suspected something! He must be a complete moron! Jeez...talk about bogus.... Harry: What's your point? That I'm under hypnosis? Tommy: Yeah. It was called weednotized, dude. You were so high that you never saw her for what she really looked like. Harry: So, I saw a beautiful woman and really she was a two hundred pound gorilla? Tommy: TOTALLY! [Harry sighs and shakes his head. It sort of DID make some sense but not entirely. Even if he was THAT high he probably could have seen what she looked like. Apparently that didn't really matter as he still does have some strong feelings for her. Maybe that romantic, non-judgemental side came out for a short time when he was with her. Either way he's got to see what happened between the two of them. He takes exit 110 and heads toward Stephens Road. As they pull onto the very suburban-like street Harry can feel his nerves really getting the best of him. The car stops underneath a large oak looking tree and is just across from the Victorian home with the Roman pillars and blue shutters. Her home looks very beautiful and, who knows, maybe she is already married with children. She could really be secure in her life, or maybe she used her wonderful mind for something and got rich. Too many possibilities and they ARE going to be found out one way or another] Harry: Are you going to stay here or come with me? [Tommy looks up at his brother with a smile on his face] Tommy: I have GOT to see this, dude. Harry: Ok. [The two men cross the street and walk up the brick pathway to the door. Both of them look down at the welcome mat with a picture of a cat on it that says 'Welcome' on it. Harry sort of chuckles to himself as he remembers how much she really liked cats. He then knocks on the door as sweat pours down onto his brow and it reminds him that he should fix up his hair...just in case she still IS single. The door opens slowly and a very large, obese woman stands at the doorway. Her face is filled with pock marks and her thick glasses don't add to the blemishes on her face. Her double chin is beginning to grown a third one. With another cheeseburger it should come in quite nicely. She wears a very large floral patterned dress that looks like a kind sized bed sheet really. Harry keeps a straight face as he forces his best smile] Tommy: SWEET MARY AND JOSEPH, DUDE!! [Tommy leaps behind a pillar and hopes that the monster will not eat him] Obese woman: Hi...uhhh...can I help you? Harry: My name is Harry Gordo and I'm here to..umm...see a Mindy Hughes. [The fat woman nods her head and smiles. As Harry looks her over again, which is hard to do, he is trying to get used to her looking like that. This is a woman that he's loved] Obese Woman: Let me go get her... [The obese woman disappears from the doorway and Harry's hopes rise up from their shattered state. Tommy looks from behind the Roman-style pillar with a confused look] Tommy: That wasn't Mindy? Harry: I guess not. [As Harry finishes his statement a very striking woman comes to the door. Her pale skin shines underneath the California sunlight and her hazel eyes sparkle with life. The waves in her black hair are curled to absolute perfection that Harry finds himself wanting to immediately touch them. He soon realizes that the smile on his face is probably one of a pre-pubescent boy seeing his first nude woman] Harry: Hi..uhhh...I'm...you know....that guy. [Tommy walks up besides his brother at the sight of the bodacious babe] Tommy: He's Harry, and I'm TOMMY! You will remember me from such bodacious waves as the Los Angeles Lip where I totally rode it the wave like a bull!! Yeah, you were there...I remember. Mindy: I was, Tommy, and if I remember you almost drown afterward. Tommy: Pft. That's because a totally heinous shark was after me. Mindy: Of course there was. [She turns to Harry, looking him over and her eyes just seem to be a bit surprised] Minday: Harry Gordo...I never thought I'd see the day where you'd retire that Twisted Sister T-shirt and finally find OTHER clothing. You look good. Harry: Thanks...you look good too. [Tommy leans over and whispers into Harry's ear] Tommy: Dude...say something nice about her eyes, dude. Say, 'Your eyes remind me of one of the most bodacious sunrises'. [Without thought Harry does as he is commanded...] Harry: Your eyes remind me of one of the most bodacious sunrises... [As he finishes he realizes what he's just said is absolutely corny and totally uncalled for] Mindy: Well, thank you for telling me that. A 'how are you' would have been nice but you go right for the throat, now don't you? Harry: Uhh...yeah, sorry. [She just laughs in the doorway as Harry collects his thoughts. Tommy taps on his watch to let Harry know of the schedule they are on. Tommy needs his workout time and the brothers need their hang out time before Harry leaves] Harry: Mindy, I came here today to tell you that many things have changed in my life over the past few weeks. I've discovered WHO I am and what I want to do with my life. I keep getting these thoughts of you in my head and I just wanted to come by and see how you were. Mindy: I'm great. I'm working as an image consultant for a few movie stars, making some good money, and I'm going to be getting married in the summer. Harry: Oh...that's fantastic! Mindy: Yeah, my partner answered the door... [Harry and Tommy both looked completely confused] Mindy: I'm a lesbian, Harry. [A sigh leaves Harry's body as he soon realizes that it WASN'T his fault that he lost the love of his life...it was nature] Harry: So, that's why things ended between us? Mindy: Yes. I found out about my lesbianism and I fell in love with Wendy...who answered the door. I didn't look like this back then though, but looks aren't everything.... [She shifts her glance over to Tommy] Mindy: ...right Tommy? Tommy: Babe, looks are EVERYTHING! Harry: Tommy...cool it. Tommy: Cool what?! She asked me a question. Harry: Well, I'm going to be leaving California to pursue a new life. After March Madness I'm catching a plane to New York City. Hopefully I can find a new beginning there. Mindy: Well, I wish you the best of luck, Harry. I always knew that you had a lot of potential in you, but you just needed to quick smoking all that weed! I guess you finally stopped. Harry: Well, we have to get going...tight schedule and everything. Maybe we can get together for a coffee or something before I head out? Mindy: Sure...I'd like that. Harry: Sweet. Well, goodbye, Mindy. [Mindy smiles and Harry smiles as the old feelings begin to reemerge from all those years ago. Even though she's a lesbian it still doesn't stop the feelings from overtaking her. She can literally feel the pressure on her heart the feelings are so strong. She just remembers how much Harry talked about one day becoming a successful writer and having a family of his own, and just be happy together. It was so simple and he never asked for much. Plus he knew how to treat a lady and that's something rare in men these days. She watches the two men get into the Mustang and she closes the door before she begins to cry...] Tommy: She became TOTALLY bodacious, dude! Harry: You can say that again... Tommy: She became TOTALLY bodacious, dude! Harry: And that ain't no lie, bro. At least I didn't mess anything up...I guess even being so high that I couldn't even mess up love. Tommy: It just sucks that she's a lesbian, dude. I mean that is just a waste of woman!! It'd be one thing if she were with another bodacious babe, but to be with Jabba The Slut...IT'S HEINOUS! Harry: You really are a pervert. Tommy: I'm proud of it. Harry: At least someone is. [Fade to black]
