Keep Your Job Idol

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/08/02 “Can you believe this payroll? It’s ludicrous!” With this financial exclaiming from the new CEO of PCW, Xavier Kannon, we fade into one of the executive suites in the PCW Offices where the 1st couple of PCW are sat at a wooden table. Kannon is observing a long list of names with occupations and salaries beside them. Xavier : “There’s a Lear Jet hidden in here if we can just get rid of those worthless employees!” The table is all the furniture in the large room that is decorated in Egg Shell. The carpet is a fluffy White and about 10 metres from the table at which the couple sit is an “X” that hasn’t gone unnoticed by the prep. Eleanor : “So, are we, like, digging for treasure or something?” For a second Kannon wonders what his naive wife is talking about until he sees the X. Xavier : “No my little Poon Puppy... Something far more rewarding.” His bright Green eyes shine with anticipation as he runs a hand through his long mane of Strawberry Blonde hair to rid his bearded face of any loose strands. Xavier : “... Ritual humiliation!” These 2 words suddenly inject the preppy Commissioner with some enthusiasm as she rubs her little hands together, awaiting the fun to commence. Xavier : “ENTER!” Through the main doors to the suite walks one of the Janitors that work at the PCW Offices. He is dressed scruffily in faded Blue dungarees and who’s shaggy Brown hair looks as if he has just crawled out of bed. The CEO casts his eye disapprovingly over the hard working, if rather unkempt employee. Xavier : “So what is it you do again.... Gary.” Looking down the list Kannon finds the name an marks it off with a pen before looking back up at the employee with a look at oozes authority... One he has seemingly been practising. “I’m a Janitor.” Kannon sneers at the basic response to his question. Xavier : “Really? I could have easily mistaken you for someone in the Particle Physics department.” The CEO flashes a smug grin at the employee as Ellie~! chuckles. The Janitor, hardworking and proud of his job makes a dry comeback. “We don’t have a Particle Physics Department, sir.” His emotionless response causes Kannon to lock his Green eyes onto him for a few seconds, not appreciating the retort levelled at him.... And causing the already petty owner of PCW to be even more so. Xavier : “Tell me, as a Janitor... When did you last empty the bin in my office?” Gary takes a moment to think it over, such menial tasks not featuring prominently in his timeline. “I dunno.... Maybe an hour ago?” The CEO taps his pen on the desk, taking in Gary’s answer. The body language exhuded from the Scientologist doesn’t bode well for Gary, the hostility now being shown from both of the couple is clear to see. Xavier : “An hour ago, huh?” Gary just raises his eyebrows and shrugs. Xavier : “A man as important as me can create a lot of rubbish in an hour, Gary. In fact, 15 minutes ago my bin was nearly full.” Still Gary stands silent, having no reply to Xavier petty knit-picking and hoping the whole frustrating ordeal at the hands of his new employer will be over soon... But Kannon’s hair-trigger temper cuts in ad the CEO yells out, admonishing the Janitor. Xavier: “DO YOU THINK I LIKE BEING SURROUNDED BY GARBAGE!?!?!?!?!” For the first time, Gary’s eyes drop down to the X on the floor as Eleanor looks on with glee at the crestfallen Janitor who speaks back without looking up. “Do you want me to go and empty it again, sir?” For any other man, breaking someone like this would be enough. But this is Xavier Kannon we are talking about, and the presence of Ellie~! only makes it worse as she whispers enthusiastically into his ear, causing a grin to appear from ear to ear. Xavier : “All in good time, Gary. First I have a special task for you.” The signs do not look good and Gary knows it. He looks up with his neck limp, as if to tell Xavier to just get on with it. Xavier : “I want you to sing.... What is it called again?” He turns to Ellie~! who whispers into his ear again before reclining, arms crossed. Xavier : “I’m a little teapot.” The Janitor just stands there in disbelief. “Are you serious, sir?” Again, Kannon doesn’t appreciate the signs of resistance, no matter how subtle. Xavier : “As serious as your family being out on the street if you don’t DO WHAT I SAY is.... Gary.” In the Janitors brain a battle is fought between his own dignity and the well being of his family, which the CEO can read a full dossier on in his note on the table. Gary drops his head and begins to sing in a muffled voice. “I’m a little tea pot....” A smug grin so wide it doesn’t look as if it can all fit on Kannon’s face breaks out as Ellie~! watches and listens, enthusiastic at seeing her humiliating idea carried out. Eleanor : “Like, do the actions and everything!” Xavier : “Yes Gary... Do the actions.” Kannon leans back in his chair and watches on with a warming sense of authority was Gary now does the hand movements to accompany the song too... All to keep his job. “Here’s my handle....” Gary’s eyes are emotionless, his feelings numbed as he carries out the demeaning task... Or punishment, however it should be looked upon as. “Here’s my spout....” Luckily for the Janitor, Kannon then calls for him to stop, issuing the order through his laughter at the Janitor’s willing self deformation. Xavier : “You can stop now.... How can I not keep you on knowing you’ll be a source of entertainment like that when I’m bored.... Now go empty my bin.” The once defiant Gary walks out of the suite a broken and humiliated man another victim of the degrading actions Xavier and Eleanor find entertaining. Xavier : “You know what’s even better than watching a Peon like that whore his dignity out to appease us, my little Fuck Ferret?” Ellie~! looks back at him, eager to know the answer. Xavier : “We still have 37 to go!” The 1st couple of PCW exchange a look that only they can... A look that says they both take perverse pleasure in seeing others devalue their own opinions of themselves just to entertain the spoilt couple.. The shot fading to Black on the 2 pairs of eyes meeting.. The Green of Kannon’s exchanging the information with Ellie~!’s pale Blue pair. “NEXT!”“
