The final score (Part 4: Featuring Bullet Time!)

15 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 15, 2002 [Fast forward a few hours after the buy at AmmuNation. Eddie’s MP5 lay on the dresser, still in its case. The room is empty, and the Air Conditioning is running non stop. It is in the middle of the desert after all. The door can be heard opening, as Eddie Mandrake enters the room. He has a towel around his neck, so it looks like that he has been working out. He releases a huge sigh as he enters the very cold hotel room. He takes off his running shoes and his socks. He shivers as his bare feet touch the marble floor. He runs over to the thermostat and turns the temperature up a little, but not too much so the heat will kick in. He heads over to the phone, which has a blinking light. He hits the “Voice Mail” button, and listens to the messages.] Machine: You have…2 New Messages…First Message: Mr. Mandrake. This is James Gallagher from Primetime Championship Wrestling, I need you to call me back immediately. Thanks. *Click* End of Message, to erase this message, Press 7, to save it Press 9. [Eddie presses 7] Machine: Message Erased, Next Message: You have one hour after hearing this to get ready for the job…You wuss out, and I’ll kill you personally. *Click* End of Message… [Eddie immediately presses 7 and the Star key instinctively. Eddie then goes to the dresser and gets his clothes. He closes the door, and with the magic of television, he exits the room, changed into his war gear. Urban Camouflage Schemed shirt, pants, black boots are the form of Eddie’s war gear. Eddie takes the case and exits the room, locking the door behind him.] A few hours later [Enter morning. A few hotels down the strip, closer to the MGM Grand. We see the stage where the presidential address will take place. The secret service is patrolling the area, F-16s from Nellis Air Force Base are patrolling the skies, and well…Security is so uptight, it’s not even funny. Eddie drives into the area, with security checkpoint. They stop the car and Eddie rolls down the window.] Security: ID Please? [Eddie hands his ID over to the guard. The guard with a leering look stares at the ID, and moves his eyes up to Eddie at the wheel of his WRX. The guard gives the ID Back.] Eddie: I’m headed to the MGM Grand to win me a lot of money. Security: You sure know how not to clash, sir. Okay, move along. [Eddie rolls up his window, and enters he manages to enter the area. He drives into the parking lot of the MGM Grand, parks his car, and steps out. He heads to the trunk, and gets his steel briefcase. He closes the trunk and enters the hotel. He remains calm the whole time, while LVPD members and Individual Security makes their way to their respective positions. Eddie heads to the information desk. Eddie: It’s time. [The Clerk hands Eddie a key to a room, on the key ring it says “1611”. He takes the key and leaves the desk. Eddie enters an elevator, and hits the “16” button. The doors close, and the elevator begins its ascent. The “muzak” in the elevator is so annoying, Eddie punches the speaker and it actually shuts off. The Elevator stops on the sixteenth floor. Eddie steps out, and goes into the first room on his left. He sees all the communication gear set up, as well as the stuff to rest his rifle on. Eddie adjusts a few things to his liking, and then sits down on a chair near the window. He places the case on the ground, and opens it. The gun glistens in the sunlight. Eddie’s eyes contract because of the reflection from the weapon. Eddie takes a deep breath, and begins to assemble the weapon. The view from the stage is absolutely perfect, over the crowd that has assembled, and over the heads of the security personnel. The communication device begins to flash traffic.] Other End: Shooter, come in please. [Eddie ignores the traffic, and continues to assemble the weapon.] Other End: This means you, Mandrake. [Eddie takes another look, now knowing he was to respond, picks up the comm. Radio and responds.] Eddie: yeah. Other End: Okay, when you’re set up, I want you to make the shot, right after the beginning of President Bush’s Speech. Go for either Powell or Bush. Either one will satisfy the requirement of the job. You miss, you fail. You cower out, you fail. Go for the one shot, and you better make it count. Eddie: Alright. I’ll do what I can. Other End: Carlo and Jimmy are on stand by in case you do fail. No one fails my boss, he will be on hand for the ceremony, so you better make it count. Eddie: Alright, don’t get your panties in a bunch. I’ll do it. Other End: I will contact you when you should make the shot. Boss 1, out. [The comm. Closes off and Eddie gets himself set for the shot. The television is on, because Eddie wants to see the aftermath of his dirty work. Eddie gets the scope assembled, and places it on the aiming portion of the weapon. The man begins to take aim, just as “Hail to the Chief” begins to play by a local High School Marching Band. Eddie begins to breathe more slowly, trying to gain calm, and ease the anxiety that is clenching his stomach.] Organizer: Las Vegas, Please Welcome The President of the United States! [President Bush and Colin Powell step onto the stage to a massive amount of cheers. They wave to the crowd, and slap skin with people in the front row. It’s very much like a wrestling show, with Bush and Powell as the faces. Eddie adjusts himself in his seat again. The comm. Pops on again.] Other End: Green Light, take the subject out. [Eddie remains poised…however, he doesn’t move. Bush is now well into his speech, and Eddie remains ready. The comm. Returns again.] Other End: Green Light, take the subject out. Eddie: Copy. Other End: Don’t give me [BEEEEEP] copy! PULL THE DAMN TRIGGER! Eddie: Forget it. I’m out. [BEEP] you, and your money as well! Other End: CARLO, JIMMY! TAKE HIM OUT! EDDIE! YOU ARE DEAD!!! [Carlo and Jimmy break the door down; Eddie sees them into the corner of his eye… ENTER BULLET TIME! Carlo and Jimmy pull out their Berettas…Eddie turns around with his MP5, at the same time Eddie begins to make a move for some cover. Both parties fire their weapons…Eddie hits Carlo in the arm, causing blood to fly everywhere. Carlo and Jimmy hit the wall, the TV, and the window, causing all round damage. EXIT BULLET TIME! Other End: KILL HIM! KIIIIIIIILLLLLLLLLLLLLLL! ENTER BULLET TIME! Eddie jumps out from behind the bed and fires his weapon. The bullets pass through Jimmy and Carlo’s respective bodies and hit the wall behind them. The blood spatters on the wall like cheap paint. Jimmy and Carlo fall limp on the ground. EXIT BULLET TIME! Eddie (Thinking): I got to get out of here before the forces come. [The comm. Opens one last time.] Other End: Mandrake, if you come back to Caesar’s, I will make sure you leave in a box! You hear me! A BOX! [Eddie picks up the communication device] Eddie: You first asshole. [Eddie pumps the communication device full of bullets before exiting the room. Eddie comes into the hall.] Voice: HOTEL SECURITY, FREEZE! [Eddie sprints down the hall and into the stairwell. Security forces fire their guns at Eddie, but they end up hitting drywall. Eddie manages to through the stairwell, but the security force manages to seal off the main exit. Eddie, thinking quickly, uses the butt stock of his rifle, and smashes open a near-by window, and flies out through it. He sprints to his car, and gets away. Hotel Security is left dumb founded back at the MGM Grand. The WRX speeds off down the strip.]
