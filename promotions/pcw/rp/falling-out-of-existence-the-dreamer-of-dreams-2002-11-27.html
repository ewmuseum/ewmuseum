Falling Out of Existence -- The Dreamer of Dreams

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 27th, 2002. Continued from Falling Out of Existence – The Prologue. “What dream?” Aislinn looks down to her friend, Amber, who in turn is just getting out of her bed, standing up and stretching in the cool morning air. She looks to Aislinn, who is straightening her long blonde hair out, wrapping it around her shoulder. “I had this weird dream. I suppose it was the one that woke you up, but it was weird. I could have sworn I knew the person who was attacking me. He looked familiar.” Aislinn studies Amber carefully, looking at her face, and the exposed skin that isn’t covered by the nightgown. Nothing but her delicate, soft skin can be seen. “What happened in it?” “In what?” “Your dream, silly.” “Oh…well, what I can remember of it was that I was attacked in my sleep. A very big man came, and yanked me out of my bed and pinned me to the wall, threatening me about something. Saying that “they” want something of me. It was very odd, and creepy. I almost FELT the pain.” With a concerned look on her face, Aislinn sits Amber on the bed, and plants herself next to her. She strokes Amber’s dark brown hair, while hugging her. “Are you alright?” “Yes, I am. It was more of a nightmare than a dream, I believe.” “It sounds like it.” Amber nods her head slowly, reminiscing on her past dream while Aislinn continues playing with her soft hair. “You know what I was thinking, Aislinn?” She stops toying with her friend’s hair, and puts her hands on her slender legs before responding. “No…I’m not a mind-reader. What were you thinking, Amber?” Amber smiles, and pushes Aislinn back playfully while winking. “I was thinking that maybe that dream was a message or something. You know?” Aislinn sits back up promptly from Amber’s little push to look her in the eyes, furrowing her brow. “You mean you think that someone might attack you in your sleep?” “You never know, Aislinn…there’s a lot of crazy people in the world we live in.” Aislinn nods in an agreeing manner. “So true, so true.” Both women fall into silence, as the next few moments play out to Aislinn playing with Amber’s hair again, putting a few strands into a French braid, while Amber sits with her hands on her legs. Aislinn breaks the silence after a moment. “Why don’t you go back to bed, sweetie?” Amber turns around, as Aislinn lets her hair go and fall gracefully onto her back. “I can’t. If I wake up in the middle of the night, I can’t go to sleep.” “Same with me, Amber.” “Plus, I had that weird nightmare…I won’t go back to sleep until tonight, I presume.” Aislinn smirks. “Sucks, doesn’t it?” “Yeah…another sleepless night in the books.” Aislinn shuffles herself to the edge of the bed, before standing up. She turns around and looks at Amber. “I’m going to try to sleep. Maybe I can break the curse, heh.” Amber looks up, before standing to a vertical base, wiggling her bare toes on the carpeted floor. “Alright, good night. I hope my little nightmare won’t cause you any problems.” “Let’s hope not.” Aislinn turns around, and walks out of Amber’s door, which has yet to be closed. Amber continues to sit up on her bed, not moving much at all. A few moments pass before she lies down on her bed, pulling the sheets up to her waist, ready for another sleepless night. « § » Taken from the memoirs of Amber Dyiasse. As far as my life goes, I’ve had some pretty weird dreams. I’ve been taken on fabulous adventures in my sleep, but recently, I’ve encountered a dream, rather, a nightmare that may lead to something in the future. I’m frightened to think it might even happen, but there are endless possibilities. I could almost feel the pain the man in my dream dealt to me. I almost felt being slammed against my own bedroom wall. I almost felt like I was gagging with his strong grip wrapped around my throat. I almost did… I suppose that it is sort of an odd thought, but I almost recognized the man from the nightmare. Tall, stout, and strong. He reminds me of someone, but I can’t put my finger on it. I will be making a spell to cast this dream person away. Considering sometime next month, a waning moon is occurring, I have plenty of time to find out who the man is. Here beside me now, I have an airtight glass jar, and several magical herbs; ash tree leaves, lovage, clover, lilac, and garlic clove. Through the process that took me five minute, no more, no less, I have filled the jar with water, and dashed each of the herbs in it. I have a black string, and a fully functional freezer, and a piece of parchment. All I need now is to find out this man’s name and birth date, and I have myself protected for life. I am realizing that the only way to find this information out is to combat him in a series of more dreams and nightmares. If I feel pain, so be it. It will be well worth it if I am able to find out this crucial information to use my Wicca spells on him, and to banish him out of my life. « § » A lone candle flickers in the small breeze blowing through the unglazed windows of the dark, damp church. The candle burns proudly on top of the altar, positioned quite obviously down at the front end of the church. A set of eyes focus on the intense burning of the candles flame. It burns with a great pride. It brightly lights up the massive interior of this church, does this small candle. It adds a sense of warmth to this otherwise unsettling picture. It makes us feel warm in a cold place. The light shining from this candle is strong. The burning halts. The main door of the church opens and a great gust of wind blows in. The candle flame has been extinguished. We feel cold. In the church doorway, we see a figure. Drenched in black, as mysterious looking as its surroundings. The menacing looking figure takes a few small -- almost timid -- steps into the church but we still can’t see this person clearly enough. He stands still for a moment, and then a bellowing voice fills the church and echoes off the walls. The voice is low but very loud, and otherwise, creepy. “Dreams…” The man quickly turns his head from side to side; he then turns back to where he entered the church from and dashes for the door. But it slams shut. The man pounds on the door a few times, before reluctantly giving up. As the eyes stare right into this man’s face; she, Amber Token discovers something. He's not menacing! He looks scared. Fear is written all over his face. He actually looks pretty pathetic. Tears trickle down his cheeks. He's scared out of his mind. But would you be any different? The poor man slumps to the ground, leaning against the door. It is then that we notice the collar. This man is indeed the priest of this church. The bellowing voice is heard again. The priest clutches his head in his hands, understandably scared. “Dreams are of an odd type of vision. Dreams, we feel. Dreams, we see. Dreams, we touch. And dreams, we hear.” Finally, the priest gathers the courage to speak, although his voice is rather jumpy. “Who...who is in here...with me? Who is...” The booming voice cuts him off. “Dreams are thought to be the fragments of our mind being thrown around as we toss and turn throughout the night, settling to our slumber. It is thought that dreams are simply voids in our minds, which are opened as our eyes close, and our minds switch off.” The priest is rocking back and fourth. He looks to be losing some sanity; literally, losing sanity. His eyes widen. He foams at the mouth. The voice continues, mercilessly, torturing the priest’s holy ears. “Some, however, believe dreams have a greater importance and a greater purpose. Some believe that dreams decide our fate. Happy dreams, happy life. Horrid dreams? Get worried.” “WHO ARE...YOU?” The priest screams at the top of his lungs, but the voice ignores him, cutting him off again, speaking in the low, unsettling tone that has been getting into the priest’s head quickly, and efficiently. “Your fate was decided many thousands of years ago. You can’t alter your fate.” A long, disconcerting pause takes the place of the voice as the priest continues to rock back and forth, worrying for his very own life. “You can’t alter your dreams.” The priest is now on his knees. His eyes dart around, surveying the darkness that fills the church, ever since the candle was blown out. “Please! Just show your face and no more shall be said on this matter. T-This is the house of God! This is no place for c-childish games!” The priest pauses himself, expecting the unexpected, waiting for an answer. The low, mystic voice roars over him again. “So, you wish to see me?” Another unsettling pause occurs, as nothing is said between the priest, and the hidden owner of the voice. “Very well then. And then we shall see if you judge your dreams as ‘childish’. I shall show myself to you, Father Priest.” The candle then relights the room, which is perched atop the altar. Standing there next to it though, is a man who has an ice-cold look about him, taking any warmth away that the candle had offered us. The man looks to be roughly 6'05” and a little on the lean side. His cold blue eyes penetrate what it is he focuses on. In this case, he is focusing on the priest, who is in great shock as he stares back, still on his knees at the other end of the church. This mystery man gives out a cold smile as the priest spurts out words, which can barely be heard. “My God. My...Son. I thought you had...” “Passed on? No! I'm still alive, Father. I bet that troubles you.” “Where have you been, Da...” The unknown man walks slowly from the altar to the back of the church, slowly approaching the kneeling priest. “I have been thinking deeply about my revenge. I have been thinking deeply about how you, and the world treated me. I have been thinking about the way you always used to be ashamed of me as a child, just because of my ambitions! What kind of father would shun his little boy just because of the ambitions he had?” “It was for your own good! The things you wanted could serve no purpose to this world. To be a living nightmare. To be a fighter. To be a hit man. What good would become of you? I was merely trying to shape you...for your own good!” The unknown, tall man, still in an open vest from Amber’s dream stands over the kneeling priest. “And what happened when you couldn’t shape me? What happened when I became a burden to you? What happened when I became so different from you that you became ashamed of me?” The priest’s mouth opens, but no words come out as he stares in hopeless fear at the man looming over him. “You tried to get rid of me. And now you shall suffer! I've been dreaming of this moment for months! Feel my wrath...” The man grabs a sword from the side of his hip, from inside a container for the sword. The man holds the sword high above his head. The priest’s eyes widen before he falls to a heap on the ground passing out from fear. The man drops the sword to the ground and looks down on his Father. “Fool! I would not have struck you! Pitiful man! I did not need to strike you to make you fall into slumber. You did it all by yourself and now your dreams shall haunt you, whilst I on the other hand, shall pursue my dreams. My dreams to strike fear into everyone’s dreams! A dream which you, my father, failed to let me achieve. But you can hold me back no more. I shall truly turn my dreams into reality! I am the dreamer of dreams. I am the usher of reality. I am Darien Ranier, your flesh and blood…and everyone’s horror story.”
