Revenge Saga Part 16: I Like Your BRA! And other Incredibly bad chat up lines...

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/6/02 Revenge Saga Part 16: I like your BRA! And other incredibly bad chat up lines… Fleming sat behind his mahogany desk, his feet up, eyes closed with some classical music playing in the background. A faint crackle is heard from the intercom, which connects his newly acquired office to another section of the Zemoff mansion, on the East Wing of the building. At sound of the crackle, Fleming opens his left eye and looks around suspiciously. Carla: Sir! [Pausing] Sir! [Voice becoming more urgent] SIR! Fleming picks upon the stereo control from the table presses the ‘off’ button, simultaneously pressing the button allowing him to speak to Carla. Fleming: Yeah? Carla: Sir, The Thunderbolts have arrived. Fleming: Well send them right in! Carla: That may be a little difficult sir. Fleming: What makes you say that? Carla: Atlas won’t fit in the building sir. Fleming: [Sighing] Okay, send them to the back, I’ll talk to them on the testing field. Fleming rises from his seated position, and grabs his suit jacket from the back of the chair. He carefully slips it on, before heading out of his office door. For anyone else, simply walking to the back of their houses took mere seconds, but for Fleming it was very different. He would have to go through at least seventeen doors, the last being a security clearance door. In essence it would take him at least five minutes to get his ass outside, and that’s if he was walking fast. Earlier that morning the deal with the Russians had been concluded and Carla had managed to summon The Thunderbolts almost immediately after the Russians left. The money was there, so he didn’t expect the deal to be too strenuous or to last very long. Fleming reached the very last door, and punched in his personal security code. The door opens almost immediately and Fleming steps out onto the beautifully green grass before him. ************ It didn’t take Crichton long to find a bar, as entered he realised that he had picked a decent bar to say the least. The bar was dark, with musky odour, but he quite enjoyed it. He walks straight over to the bar and sits upon a stool, next an old man. He examines the rooms interior, and is slightly impressed by it. Metal road signs pinned up on the wall, along with lithographs of various American towns and cities. Hmmm…An all American bar, I’ve found my second home. The woman behind the bar heads over towards him, a sexy grin upon her face. I’ve still got it! He silently declares. Woman: What can I get you? Crichton: A condom…I…er mean J.D. and Cola. Woman: [Resisting the urge to laugh] Coming right up! Crichton: [Muttering] Yeah [Looking at his pants] more than likely! Woman: I’m sorry. Crichton: Oh…nothing. The woman simply smiles and turns to fix him his drink. Crichton admired the woman’s very shapely figure, especially the short skirt and a really tight scarlet top. Then he feels a tap on his left shoulder. He turns to see the old man glaring at him with a wide smile upon his face, his false teeth literally hanging out of his mouth. Crichton shivers and tries not to look at them. He was also wearing a dark blue suit, his war medals on show for all to see. Old Man: Hello Boy, my name’s Reginald. Crichton: [Scrunching his face up because of the alcoholic smell of the man’s breath] Hi, my name’s John. Reginald: I bet you’re a real good boy… Crichton: Errrr….what? Reginald: I bet you’re a real decent and generous guy…am I right? Crichton: You’re drunk aren’t you? Reginald: I’ve had a few Chardonnay’s, what of it? Crichrton: Nothing…nothing…just making sure. The woman walks over and places Crichton’s drink in front of him, he extracts a five dollar bill from the pocket of his knee-long leather jacket and hands it to her. Crichton: [With a real smarmy look upon his face] Keep the change. Woman: [Muttering] Cheap ass… Crichton: Pardon? Woman: Nothing. Reginald: I like your bra! Woman: [Sighing] Nice try Reginald, but again I’m gonna have to decline… Reginald: It’s the age thing isn’t it? Woman: No, it’s just that I don’t I’m good enough for you. Reginald: I don’t think you are either, but I’m willing to give you a try! Woman: I’m gonna have to pass. Reginald: Well suit yourself then! ************ One hour and three J.D.’s and cokes later, Crichton was still sat at the bar, eyeing up the very tasty woman. Reginald has passed out over by the duke box, Crichton suspected it had something to do with the pint of absinthe he had finished off prior to collapsing. Crichton had spent the hour trying his best to get into the woman’s pants. Crichton: So come on, what is the worst chat up line you’ve ever heard? Woman: Besides this one and the one earlier by the man himself, Reginald? Crichton: Yes. Woman: Right okay, I’ll pretend to by the man and you pretend to be the woman, okay? Crichton: Kinky! Woman: [Not rising to the bait] Awwww…I don’t feel well. [Whispering] You say ‘what is wrong’. Crichton: Okay, what is wrong? Woman: I’ve got a fever for your beaver! Crichton: [Laughing] I’m writing that one down! Woman: Indeed. Crichton: How could you resist? Woman: It was difficult! Crichton: I’m sure! So do I have any chance of scoring?
