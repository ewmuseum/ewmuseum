Darkness Infinite

16 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Oct. 16th 2002 HateDeathLongingFEARALONE!VOID!!!!!!! Reborn Anew... I am now Celestial... The words echoed in his head as he remembered. Alone in the dark alley Shin Yagyuu stood staring at blood covered hands. His head slowly lifting to the sky.. -=- Location: The Void Alone in the dark swirling hell known as the void he lay. Sprawled out in constant physical pain Shin Yagyuu stared at the blackness around him. When will the suffering end. When will I be set free... When will I die.. When will I be reborn? Will I suffer here forever? Memories like pictures.. Filling the blackness. A samurai stands alone in a field. Two hundred men begin to surround the lone warrior as he stares them down. Voice: Shinsetsu Yagami! You will die this day! The man who stands alone stares at the man speaking. Shinsetsu: Oda! Your lies will not corrupt the Japanese Shogunate! If it takes my last breath you will die! Oda: Hollow words mean nothing after you die... CHARGE!!!!!!!!! The warriors surrounding the lone Samurai begin to rush him. The man in the center charges the closest warrior and cuts him down with hard knee to the stomach a swift swipe of his katana follows to take the man's head. Blood splatters the dirt as Shinsetsu spins around and lunges his blade deep into the next attackers stomach. The events play on the pictures as the lone samurai is slowly overwhelmed. Enemies on all sides I kept trying... I took them from two hundred to fifty before they broke me... The man stares at the images as his body lays beaten on the ground. Oda: Yagami! You will be made an example! The men surrounding Yagami's body lift him up and carry him to Oda Nobunaga. Oda: String him to my horse... I will drag him back to Gifu Castle. If he is not already dead then the humiliation will be shattering. The men nod and tie Yagami's legs together.... ENOUGH!!!!!!!!!!! STOP SHOWING ME THIS! Shinsetsu Yagami floats in the void screaming at the nothingness. I KNOW MY PAST I FOUGHT FOR HONOR AND DIED A SAMURAI! WHY TAUNT ME WITH THINGS I AM PROUD OF DEMON! Because you are not proud. We see into your mind Yagami... NO! I EARNED MY HONOR! Yes you did... But you are more than a samurai... It was a personal disgrace to die that day. You wanted more and we can give it to you... You did not survive that battle on pure humanity... You were then and are now a Celestial.. Why do you think your death brought you to us... What... What do you want of me... Akiyama requires assistance... You are the strongest of the Celestial Warriors... -=- Shin's blood covered hands drip clean as it begins to rain. The man whom he killed was nothing but a fool.. A fool who had challenged the wrong fighter at the wrong time. Shin's hand slowly clenched into a fist and re-opened. The silver barcode flashed bright on Shin's left shoulder blade as a 6'8' figure stepped from the darkness. The two figures simply staring at eachother in the darkness. The alleyway is no place for us.. Cyclonica stepped from another dark corner behind Shin. And though you may find it fun senslessly slaughtering 'them' is below us. Shinsetsu.. The black mask shown in the darkness as Shin looked over to the woman speaking to him. Shinsetsu Yagami.. The blood flowed off his enemies head as Shin turned to Akiyama and bowed slightly before exiting the alley leaving Cyclonica and Akiyama alone. FADE TO BLACK
