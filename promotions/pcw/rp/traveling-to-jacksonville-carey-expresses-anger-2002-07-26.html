Traveling to Jacksonville: Carey expresses Anger

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/26/02 Last time we saw Misplaced they were visiting Carey’s parents at there extremely colored house. Today they have a match on Friday Fusion against the Cowabunga Connection. This is a big match that could decide the fate of their careers. If they lose this match they will be looked at as complete jobbers. That shouldn’t happen. Now we see Carey J. Nowski, Stephen Cairns, and Jackson “Hoe” Pallish on an airplane. Of course Carey’s biggest fear is flying, so he’s gripping to his seat. Jackson is listening to a Jay-Z CD, and Stephen is sleeping. It is a very lively team. The plane is an ordinary coach section with many people cramped into one small area. Hostesses are shuffling through the aisles, and most people are sleeping. Outside the weather is perfectly clear. Inside Jackson is taking off his headset and putting it into his carry on duffle bag. Silence ensues for a while. Jackson: “Yo’ Carey! Wut’ up? You doing aight’? You look like you be strugglin’.” Carey: “Yeah, I’m perfectly fine. I just don’t really like flying much. I’ve always had a fear of flying. It’s the worst part of my job. I hate it. It’s making me go nuts. This is horrible. Damn! I hate this!” Jackson: “Whoa! Calm down yo’. We’ll be off of dis’ bitch in about ten minutes.” Carey: “I know, but we could be dead in that time. You have no clue what might happen! Flying is a conspiracy set up by the government. Those pilots are really probably crooked government officials. You have no clue what’s really going on.” Jackson: “You goin’ through somethin’? You’re actin’ really weird. You need to just chill.” As they’re talking the plane jerks a little bit, which really startles Carey. It also wakes Stephen up and he sees Carey grappling the seat. He just gives Carey his normal “you’re insane look”. Stephen: “Afraid of flying I presume? Or is it just set up by bad people?” Carey: “Listen, I really know what’s going on but nobody can see that. It’s all set up. Everything is somehow set up. It’s amazing that the normal citizen has no knowledge of what the government does. That man across the aisle could be a secret agent spying on all of us.” Stephen laughs and leans across to look at a very chubby colored man eating a brownie. It really does look like a government official. Stephen: “Excuse me. Excuse me sir. Are you a government secret agent?” The man takes a while to respond, and he has a very confused look on his face.” Colored Brownie Man: “Ha! You think I’m a secret agent? That’s ridiculous. I’m just a chubby old black man traveling to Africa.” Stephen: “This plane is going to Jacksonville not Africa. I think you probably got on the wrong plane.” Colored Brownie Man: “What? Jacksonville? I thought this was to Africa. Holy monkey droppings!” The colored brownie eating man runs to the back of the plane and starts to cry while still screaming “Holy Monkey Droppings”. Meanwhile, Stephen just continues to laugh at Carey’s strange ideas of government conspiracies. Stephen: “Buddy, you really need to get in the real world. Not everyone is a bad person, and not everything is set up. You’ll get some crooked people, but most people are just normal. Try to straighten up the way you think.” Carey: “Nobody ever believes me until it happens to them. I know what’s going on in the real world. I’ve spent years researching this while my best friend was always a Fox Mulder doll. I’m extremely dedicated to what I look into, and I know what’s going on.” Stephen: “Yeah but you’re a wrestler now Carey. We have other things to concentrate on rather than dancing aliens and crooked cops. We have to worry about bogus surfers. God I hate talking like that.” Carey: “Just because I’m a wrestler I refuse to change my life. I will reveal the truth to the whole world one day, and people will come to their senses.” Stephen: “Whatever you say buddy. You’re just living in a different world now though.” Carey gets upset and drops the conversation. They both just slouch down in their seats and attempt to relax. A hostess comes through and gets all of them a drink. Stephen starts to fall asleep again, but Jackson is still sitting there. All is still silent on the plane with many people asleep. Jackson: “Yo Carey, you lookin’ tense again. We landin’ pretty soon.” Carey: “It’s not even that anymore Jackson. Nobody believes me. I know I’m right. Nobody in PCW believes me conspiracy theories, and nobody on this plane would believe me. They all take me as a joke, and I’m just about sick of it.” Jackson: “Yo’ cracka’, I believe you. Man I dun’ know what’s goin’ on up in this shiznit. Some mothers could be plottin’ an attack on us and we jus’ be sittin’ here. That be as ridiculous as my mama’ tryin’ to cook dat’ monkey one time.” Carey: “Interesting comparison. It’s nice to see that somebody actually has a sense of what’s going on though. Most people just think the world is a jolly place. Well it’s not! It’s not!” Carey grasps his hair and starts to tug on it. He then remembers the thing that settles him the most. He reaches into his bag and pulls out his Fox Mulder doll. With a sigh of relief, Carey just stares out the window. While they’re all just sitting there the colored chubby man starts running up and down the aisle very upset that he caught the wrong plane. Hosts are trying to get the man to sit down, but he doesn’t seem very willing. As he continues running Stephen sticks out his leg, which knocks the man down. Innocently Stephen pulls his leg back into his seat. An announcement comes over the speakers. Announcement Attendant: “Ladies and Gentlemen we are now preparing to land. Could you all please fasten your seatbelts immediately?” Everyone fastens there seatbelt. Carey nervously starts to shake as the plane goes down to the runway. Carey: “This is were everything goes wrong. They probably forgot to put the tires on that they use to land. We’re all going to die!” Jackson: “I really doubt that yo’. We be safe.” Carey sighs and continues to stare out of the window. The plane makes its landing, and eventually everyone can exit that plane. Now Misplaced is getting off of the plane into the airport. All they need to do is get their luggage and head off to the hotel that they’re staying in. Fusion isn’t long from now. Stephen: “Well it looks like we’ll have a hellish time getting out of here.” The airport is packed. People are scrambling to get to their planes, and the security lines are backed up what appears to be miles. There’s no room to move, and it’s going to be hectic to find directions to the luggage rack. Luckily there is a map directing them. Stephen: “Alright, according to this map we want to go right over there.” Stephen points to his right were there is a hallway. They all travel down the hallway until they reach the luggage rack. Right away Stephen and Jackson’s bags come out to them, but Carey’s isn’t anywhere to be found. They have a seat on a bench to wait for Carey’s bag. The crowd in the room starts to thin out, and the rack starts to empty. Now there are no bags circling around on the conveyor belt. Carey: “They stole it. The god damn company stole my bag! I had everything in there to. My clothes were in there, all of my money, my laptop, and all of my files. We’ve been set up.” Stephen: “Maybe we have, or maybe they just lost your bag. Lets go to the lost baggage counter right over there. Maybe they can help.” Stephen and Jackson start to walk but Carey just sits there pounding on the bench in agony. Finally Carey gets up and walks along side Stephen. They make it to the lost baggage counter, but nobody is there attending it. So they have no choice but to sit and wait for an attendant. Carey: “They don’t want us to get my bag. They want to keep it all for themselves. That’s all they want! It’s a conspiracy! The whole damn flying thing is a conspiracy and I’m sick of it! I want my bag!” Stephen: “I’m telling you this as a friend Carey. You’re going insane. You really need to calm down and wake up. They just lost your bag. There’s nothing we can do about it. Nothing!” They just sit silent for a while with Carey showing deep anger. After so long a young female dressed in red, white, and blue sits a bag down with “CJN” carved on the side. Carey jumps up knowing that is his bag. He runs over without questioning and tackles the lady. The lady struggles to get free from the very angry Carey. Neither Jackson or Stephen are paying attention until they hear a struggle. Stephen quickly jumps up to go hold back Carey. Stephen: “What the hell are you attacking a lady for man? Get off!” Stephen and Jackson both drag Carey off of the lady and then help the lady up. The lady is crying a little bit, but she holds strong. Jackson: “Yo’! What you doin’ Carey? Jus’ go back there and chill out man.” Carey: “This lady stole my back. She stole it! I know she did it on purpose. I can tell from the look on her face. She thought she could get away with it, but her guilt caused her to bring it back!” Stephen: “Just go sit down.” Carey shakes his head and walks away. Stephen and Jackson are still concentrated on the young lady. Stephen: “Are you alright? He went a little nuts on you back there. Don’t worry about him, he’s a weird person.” Blonde Lady: “I…I think I’m alright. He jumped on me pretty hard. I took this bag by mistake, and I just figured I’d return it to here…” As they’re talking a security guard approaches with a confused look on his face. Security Guard: “What’s going on here? What happened?” Stephen: “Our friend over there went a little crazy over this lady taking his bag on accident. It’s no problem now though.” The security guard takes a glance at the scene. He locks onto the blonde lady as if he knows her. The lady gets a frustrated look on her face almost as if she’s done something. She gets up and starts to walk away, but the security guard doesn’t allow it. He chases after her and locks her in handcuffs. Security Guard: This isn’t your ordinary blonde innocent lady. We’ve been looking for her. She’s done many cases of baggage theft. Your friend over there must’ve recognized her. Stephen’s jaw drops as he remembers Carey’s comments about the lady taking it purposely. The security guard takes the lady off. Stephen walks back over to Carey who is seated on a bench looking through his suitcase. Stephen: “How did you know that she was a crook?” Carey: “That’s the point I’ve been trying to make to everyone. I know more that you all do. I have knowledge on people and what they do. Maybe one day PCW, and even you will come to realize that I have the knowledge.” Stephen takes a seat on the bench next to Carey in order to catch breath. After a little bit they get up and head out of the baggage area. They continue to walk down that hallway until they’re out of site.
