Resurrecting the In Ring Promo #1

2 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/02/02 The camera fades in… We see a shot of the PCW ring inside some arena located somewhere in America. A crowd of thousands unknowingly awaits a Chef promo. A man in the crowd tries to warn the rest. Man: You all are just here for a Chef promo! Go home now! Nothing good will come of this! Another Guy: Wait a second; maybe you’re just trying to take our seats! Man: No, I’m looking out for the good of humanity here people! Do you know that Chef promos cause cancer in lab mice!?!? Yet Another Guy: You’re full of crap dude! They promised us Kano! KANNNNOOOOOO!!! Man: I’d rather get the cancer... Even Yet Another Guy: Are you badmouthing Kano!?!? Man: Oh, and I suppose you’re a Karl Leblanc fan too!?!? Yes, Even Yet Another Guy: Hey, I’m a “Swamp Dawg” fan!!! Man: (Sighing) Listen folks, there’s going to be a Chef promo in just a few minutes here, so I’m leavin… Before the man can finish the sentence, the Muslim Call to Prayer, descending from the heavens of the arena’s speaker system, falls upon the crowd. Woman: The guy was right! Run! Some Other Guy: Kano ain’t worth this! As hundreds flee the arena for their cars, the Chef and Bertha walk down the aisle towards the ring. The Chef is wearing his trademark camouflage Islamic Militant garb with the black army boots and a pitch-black fluffy beard that probably took at least three good Rogaine baths to perfect. Bertha is wearing the usual black sweats with a white T-Shirt with the red text “I Survived Sven’s Smorgasbord” on the front, and on the back “1996 Sauerkraut Stomaching Champion”. In one hand she holds a giant turkey leg, and in the other, a gym bag. The Chef hops onto the apron, and separates the top and middle rope for Bertha. After estimating the Bertha body volume to rope distance ratio, the Chef decides to cut all three ropes with a Portable Electric Carving Knife to allow the self proclaimed “Rhino of Roanoke” through. The Crowd: BOOOOOOOOO! Dave Kern: Hello folks, and welcome to another engaging Chef in ring promo! Jeff Marx: Praise Allah for the resurrection of the in ring promo! ***Duke will not be appearing on this promo because frankly he has better things to appear in than a Chef promo*** Dave Kern: You said it pal, we are in for a treat! Now if this Islamic Militant gunman would just stop pressing this incredibly hot pistol of some sort on the side of my head, I would be able to commentate without any bias to those Chef haters! Click Click… Dave Kern: You know what, I really love the Chef, I do… I love Allah too… Jeff Marx: I didn’t hear you Kern? Dave Kern: I...love…ALLAH! Marx waves the gunman away. Jeff Marx: That’ll do… Well Kern, you said at least one righteous thing tonight! Dave sighs quite possible a world record-breaking sigh. The Chef tries to quell the enormous amounts of boos, hisses, rants, chants, spitting, profuse use of the word “F*cker”, and the plethora of gestures. The Chef: Shush people! This is rude, crude, and unacceptable! The loud noises, most likely a symphony of farts, belches, shouts, coughs, blown noses, and teeth grinding, reaches a deafening level. The Chef: I think I hear Allah preparing a lightning bolt for row 241! The Chef turns to Bertha. The Chef: B, this crowd won’t quiet down, and I have some very serious stuff to talk about!Bertha: (Munching on a turkey leg) Ain’t my f*cking problem… The Chef: Aw, come on Bertha! Honey? Sugar Lump? (The Chef painfully gets this one out) Sex-y? Bertha: Ok, Ok, you f*cking sweet talker! Bertha takes the Chef’s microphone and gives the crowd some of that Bertha magic… Bertha: Shut the mother f*cking @$$ humping f*cking your mother f*cking father in the shower with a f*cking c*ck sucking piece of mother f*cking sh*tty @$$ f*ck face god d*mn ho @$$ mother f*cking f*ck @$$ skank b*tch sh*tty as f*ck fairy @$$ed queer salad tossing d*ck up the f*cking @$$ pieces of horse sh*t on my f*cking shoes mother f*cking f*ck up you c*ck @$$ed queer as f*ck sh*tty b*tch @$$ed f*cks!!! The Crowd is now silent. Bertha hands the microphone back to the Chef. The Chef: Ah, thank you my darling… The Chef pauses for a moment, and then dives into what will no doubt be a rousing conversation. The Chef: Greetings fellow gastronomers, culinary school dropouts, infidels, transsexuals, and Michael Jackson, who doesn’t fit any of those categories, I think. The King of Pop, who’s standing in the front row of the crowd, replies in some sort of adlibbed song… MJ: (Singing) I’m an in-fi-del…HEH! I’m a gas-tro-no-mer…YOW! I’m a trans-sexy-sexy-sexy-sexual…HEH HEH!!! The Chef: (Rolling his eyes) Yeah, now I know why you were recently tops on the Billboard Charts for…two weeks was it? MJ: HEH! YOW! HEH! YOW! The Chef sighs. The Chef: For some reason, Allah hasn’t smote you yet, but I’ll take it upon myself to do his work for the benefit of the world… The Chef signals for one of his Islamic Militant cohorts to escort MJ away. MJ: But I can change! HEH! I’m not a crim-i-nal! YOW! The Chef: Yeah, but I think that anyone who bought “Blood on the Dance Floor” disagrees…Adios Enemigo! The Chef and Bertha wave as two men with possible ties to Islamic Fundamentalist groups drag the King of Pop away to rid the world of his horrid sound. The Chef: Oh, did I mention? I am hereby instituting a “Cleansing Policy”. To make this as bluntly simple as possible, I’m going to have people killed when they make me mad. Sound good? The Crowd begins to boo. The Chef: Oh come on people, death is a part of life, you know the “Circle of Life”!?!? More boos come from the crowd. Bertha: (Angry at the crowd) You f*ckers are telling me you never f*cking saw the f*cking sh*tty as f*ck Lion King?!?! The Chef starts to point and direct his Islamic Fundamentalist guard in the direction of crowd members who need a good smiting. The Chef: Smite him, and him, and her with the purple hair, and that guy with the “Cheffy Sucks” sign, and that guy with a “Mexi Sucks” T-shirt, give him a quiche on me! As the soldiers for hire escort the crowd members out of the arena, the Chef makes a very amusing announcement. The Chef: Just kidding! HA! I’m not going to kill you! Come on folks, I’m not a “Dirty War” public massacre terrorist; I’m a “sending young men off to be suicide bombers” terrorist! Men, send them back to their seats and give everyone a free quiche!!! HUZZAH!!! As one man in the crowd gets his quiche and takes a bite, he shouts, “VIVA CHEF!” The Chef: Ha Ha, and ol’ George W. thought I could never get over with the crowd! Bertha: That’s what f*cking free food can f*cking do, sh*t for brains… The people who were taken away return to their seats, but are still quivering with fear from the enormous shock of having a gun pointing at their head. Upon receiving the free quiche, their eyes light up and take a bite. After swallowing the first bite, they stand up and shouts, “VIVA CHEF!” The Crowd: VIVA! VIVA! VIVA! VIVA! The Chef raises his hand in triumph, looking at the thousands of mindless zombies in the arena, converted to his side by a simple slice of quiche. Bertha: Wow, that f*cking quiche must be really @$$ humping good f*cko! The Chef: Well it does have other four different kinds of cheeses, hearty piece of smoked bacon, a mouth-watering egg scramble, and a delicious dash of shallot! But the real surprise is in the quiche! Bertha opens up her gym bag, and pulls out a piece of the leftover quiche. She breaks it in half and finds a robotic transmitter!!! Bertha: What the f*ck!?!? The Chef: Ha Ha, Praise be to Allah for mind control devices!!! Those little doodads latch onto the top of your throat and transmit an electromagnetic signal to your brain, telling the host to obey me!!! VIVA ME!!! Finally I will have a horde of mindless zombies at my disposal, doing my dirty work, ridding the world of the “Infidel” religions, and instituting Mohammed and Allah in the minds of every single person in this country! Praise be to Allah for deception!!! The Crowd: VIVA! VIVA! VIVA! VIVA! The eerie scene of thousands practically worshipping the Chef is suddenly paused, but by whom!?!? Gus the Video Tape Guy: It was me, friend… Oh, ok, well on the to narrator, wait, that’s me! What is going to happen to the thousands of people that were given mind control devices by the Chef!?!? Will they be loyal to him!?!? Will anyone come to stop this downright heinous deed!?!? We’ll find out…Same Chef Time…Same Chef Channel!!! NANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANA…THE CHEF!!! Dave Kern: Wait a second! What the heck happened to us in the promo!?!? Jeff Marx: It's all apart of Allah's divine plan, or just a goof by the Chef's handler... Dave sighs again. The still scene fades to black. Gus the Video Tap Guy: Y’all come back now…
