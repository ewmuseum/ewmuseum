He Doesn't Listen Very Well, Does He?

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-23-02 Brandi stands, surveying the office. Towards the center of the room is a decent sized blood stain, and there are little spots scattered about the room as well. On the wall behind the desk is a small dent, with a blood trail going down it towards the floor. She walks towards the wall and sighs. Brandi- You sure you’re alright, Tim? Sure looks like an awful lot of blood. Walden- I’m fine. It looks a lot worse than it is. Brandi- No headache or anything? I mean, you don’t have a concussion, do you? Walden- Nah. The doctor said I was fine. Just needed a few stitches. I’ll be alright. Brandi- Alright. Hey, I’m sorry about what happened. That’s the last thing I would have expected from him. We were kind of arguing earlier, but then I thought he was fine so I asked him to go help you…so, I guess in a way it was my fault. Walden- Your fault? Brandi, you can’t blame yourself for Nathan’s jealousy. I didn’t expect it from him either. Before this happened, I considered him a friend. I just hope that I can still consider you one. Brandi- Of course! Nathan isn’t going to break up our friendship, or The Front Office for that matter. I wouldn’t know what to do without you guys. Besides, I left him back in Sacramento. He shouldn’t be showing up anytime soon. Walden- You left him there? Brandi- Yep. I wasn’t about to bring him back here with me. Walden- Are you ok? Brandi walks towards the door, having to step over the stained carpet to do so. Brandi- …yeah, I’m fine. Walden- Are you sure? Brandi- Yeah, I’m sure. I just…don’t know. I feel bad for what happened, and I almost feel bad for leaving him without a ride or anything. Almost, but not quite. Enough about that. Let’s just forget about him and try to forget about what happened. You sure you’re alright to wrestle? Walden- Ha! Just because I’ve got stitches doesn’t mean I can’t wrestle. Come on, I’ve had a lot worse. Brandi- You’re right. Walden- Course I am. We just gotta concentrate on Alliances now. I still wanna be Tim Walden, Taggerweight Champion. Brandi- You will be, Tim. Think about it. Those bitches Tomas and HIT won’t stand a chance. There’s no one to worry about in that match. The whole tag team division is a bunch of jobbers, and sometimes I don’t even know why I let them keep their pathetic little jobs. Then, there’s the main event. With Kannon and Duke on our team, you know we have nothing to worry about there either. Walden- Of course we don’t. There’s a light knock on the door and Walden looks in that direction, suspiciously. Walden- Don’t answer it! Brandi- Geez, Tim. Nathan really has you on edge, doesn’t he? Relax. I told you, I left him back in Sacramento at the funeral. He wouldn’t have been able to get back so quickly. Besides, I called a cleaning service earlier to have them come over here and clean this mess up. Can’t have you working in a blood stained office now, can we? Brandi giggles and opens the door. A young woman stands outside with a bucket of cleaning supplies. Brandi turns around and flashes an ‘I told you so’ glance towards Tim. The woman steps inside and is shocked by the sight of all the blood. Brandi- Thanks for coming by on such sort notice. We’ll leave you to work in peace. Brandi grabs Tim by the arm and leads him out into the hallway. The cleaning woman takes a bottle of something from her bucket, pours it on the stain, and begins to vigorously scrub it with a brush. Walden- Now where are we going? Brandi- I wanna go grab a soda real quick. You coming? Brandi starts walking down the hall, leaving Walden standing there by himself. He shrugs and walks after her. Walden- I guess I am. I could go for an A&W Root Beer right about now. When Tim reaches her side, Brandi playfully pushes him towards the wall, sticks out her tongue, and runs down the hall. She rounds a corner and disappears from sight. When Walden reaches the same corner, Brandi still is no where to be seen. He looks around and continues walking down the empty corridor. Walden- Brandi! Brandi? Hey, where’d ya go?! As Walden looks around, a door quietly opens behind him. Brandi emerges from the room, and shuts the door as quietly as when she opened it. She giggles to herself, and tiptoes towards Tim. When she gets close enough to him, she pokes his ribs with her index finger and whispers in his ear. Brandi- Boo! Tim jumps and screams loudly. He runs down the hallway, and quickly goes into an empty room, locking the door behind him. Brandi leans up against a nearby wall and laughs. When she finally manages to calm down, she casually walks over to the door that Walden disappeared behind and knocks. Walden- Go away! Brandi- You want me to go away? Walden- Brandi! Watch out! Nathan’s here? Brandi looks around with a grin. Besides her and the vending machine at the end of the hallway, it’s empty. Brandi- Uh, are you sure Tim? No one’s around but me. There’s a click as the door unlocks, and opens slightly. Tim timidly peaks his head out in the open and looks around before leaving the safety of the room. Walden- You’re right. Brandi giggles and shakes her head. Brandi- Some champion you are. The littlest thing scares you and you run off to hide. Not to mention that scream. Sheesh, Tim. Don’t think I’ve ever heard someone scream like that before. Walden’s face turns red and he starts walking down the hall, towards the soda machine. Walden- Leave me alone. Brandi- Awwww. I’m sorry. Didn’t mean for ya to get all embarrassed and everything. You alright? Walden- Forget about it. It’s fine. Brandi- I’ll tell ya what. The soda’s on me. One root beer coming right up. Brandi runs to the vending machine and puts two dollars in. She fiddles with the buttons for a few minutes, before bending over and retrieving the sodas. She hands one of them to Tim, and opens the other for herself. After a long sip of the cool liquid, Tim sighs and seems to relax a bit. Brandi- See. Now you feel better, don’tcha? Walden nods and the two start back towards the office. Brandi- As soon as we get that place cleaned up, I’ll help you get your things straightened out. As they round the corner, Brandi almost drops her soda in shock. Standing in front of the door to Tim’s office is Nathan. Tim looks up and sees the man standing thee at about the same time Brandi does, but his reaction is much different. Instead of being shocked, his eyes fill with anger and Tim throws his soda bottle at Nathan’s head. Nathan ducks, and the bottle bounces off the wall. Nathan- Brandi, I need to talk to you. Tim, I came to apologize. Brandi- Nathan, I told you not to come here. I have nothing to say to you, and I don’t feel like listening to you right now either. Nathan- Well, surely you’ll accept my apology, won’t you Tim? Nathan holds out his hand as a sign of friendship towards Tim. Walden looks at it for a moment and walks towards Nathan. He pauses for a moment, as if he was going to shake the outstretched hand, but instead Tim pulls his arm back and punches Nathan square in the nose. He drops straight to the floor and Tim holds the office door open for Brandi, who quickly goes inside. Walden- Why don’t you give security a call real fast so we can get this garbage picked up? Tim laughs and follows Brandi inside.
