Ego

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/11/02 “ARGH!!!!!!!!!” The screams of a man echo forth in a black chamber. Deep within the bowels of the earth it lies, dank, cold...........a place of misery. All along the walls mold grows, covering, devouring, robbing.............threatening to take what little life exists. Upon closer inspection we can see not only does this green abomination grow over the brick, but over the lifeforms strapped to them.......... And yes these bodies are alive..........humans, or at least vaguely human shaped. Their features undistinguishable........lost forever to the grime. They are the faceless. They are a reflection of you. But it is not upon these, the broken that we focus now............instead it is upon the man from who the tormented screams actually originate, since his captives are far beyond the point of making noise. A demon is he..........a killer is he. A monster is he...........but he is a very, very special kind of monster.For you see, some serial killers are driven by hatred..........some by lust. Some by a cause, whether justifiable or not.......... This monster is driven by all of these things. But even more so he is driven by his own overwhelming ego. Clyde: ARGH!!!!!!!! From her position on the far wall, a little girl looks up. Her name is Bethany..........the killer’s lover for quite some time. She too has the benefit of being totally bereft of morals..........however beyond that there lies something else. Another kind of hatred, it differs from Clyde’s maniacal ways......... She waits. Very, very patiently she waits............for an opportunity to kill him. From this spot on the moldy wall, she sits crouched while picking at the flesh of one of the faceless captives. A knife in her hand, she absent mindedly flays the skin from his body............only mildly disappointed that he is no longer capable of screaming. That is always such a pleasure......... Why? Why does she take pleasure in the torment of her fellow human beings? For the answer to your question, you need only look around. Take a glance at a random person, tell me what you see...........do you see something that makes you happy? Do you see something that makes you smile? No, you do not..........you see agony. You see stupidity. You see things that should not be...........so why is it immoral to simply be rid of these things? She takes pleasure in their torture because in a way they torture her..........in that respect, her motivations for killing are much the same as Clyde’s. However now the pleasure of torment is interrupted by the yelling. The little girl shakes her head.........this is one thing that is frustrating about living with the monster. His little quirks. Bethany: Clyde, what the hell is your problem? Clyde: ARGH!!!!!! With a grunt of frustration, Bethany throws her blade to the ground. It clinks off the cold dungeon floor as she stands, dusts her dress off and marches over to her burned lover. Clyde sits on the floor as well, his face illuminated by the glow of that eternal staple of torment, the television. His expression is one of fury.........exasperation.......... Clyde: ARGH!!!!!!! Bethany: For God’s sake Clyde laxative is inexpensive, you don’t need to go through such torments. Clyde: ARGH!!!!!!! No. Bethany: Then what is it!? Clyde: Look. The burned man points to the small television, from which an extremely long orange extension cord protrudes and snakes it’s way all the way out of the dungeon. Bethany looks.............and sees the face of a young man. Bethany: Bobby Stormbringer? Clyde: Yes...........look at him! LOOK I SAY~! Bethany does look...........stares intently. And finally............seeing nothing special she turns away. Bethany: He’s just rambling on about nothing like 90% of the jobbers in PCW. What about him? Clyde: I just realized he has a match with me. Bethany: Really? I thought you were on temporary leave due to your being fired. Clyde: (Putting a finger to his lips) Shhhhhhhhhhhh, kayfabe! Bethany: What kayfabe? You cut two damn promos yourself about it nimrod! Remember the Verizon quality control team who beamed down and fired you........... Clyde: (Cutting her off) ANYWAY, that’s not the point. The point is this moron Stormbringer..........now I can deal with jobbing. Hell I can deal with jobbing to Jeremy Howard after Miyagawa had kidnaped me three days earlier, thus trapping me in a plothole I couldn’t get out of.......... Bethany: (Muttering) And you shush me about kayfabe. Clyde: But what I cannot deal with, is NOT BEING MENTIONED IN A JOBBER’S DAMNED PROMO! ARGH!!!!!!!!! Almost as if on cue a fellow with sunglasses strolls into the room............smirks and does an RVD point to himself. Slade: Slade! Clyde: BAH! With a shout the demon leaps upon the former PCW jobber and bringer of the plague that was Sladerade upon us all, ripping and tearing. Clyde: My ego must be satiated! Pay attention to me! PAY ATTENTION I SAY~! Slade: Mommy! A short distance away Bethany regards all this with her arms folded. Bethany: Nice to see you have your priorities straight, Clyde. Not looking up nor changing his expression, Clyde calmly rips the skin from Slade’s body as he continues. Clyde: I know. Now if Mr. Stormbringer would have just mentioned me in the slightest I more than likely would have continued along my merry way, watching what little money I have drain until I spiral into homelessness........... Bethany: Kayfabe! Clyde: (Tearing) Shush. But now I’m mildly irked. Bethany: Really, I couldn’t tell. Clyde: Job me............murder me............beat me into the ground. I do not care...........but I WILL NOT BE IGNORED! Slade: (Bleeding) Dude, that hurts........... Clyde: (Slashing) Quiet Slade! Slade: ARGH!!!!!!! Bethany: Truly your ego knows no bounds. Clyde: If a man has no ego, what does he have? Bethany: If that man is you, he has no testicles and no job. Clyde: Shut up. And as a demon massacres a jobber, a girl looks on and ego is sustained by any means necessary, we mercifully fade to black...............
