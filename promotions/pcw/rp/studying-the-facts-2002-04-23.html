Studying the Facts

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd April So many questions El Diablo has to ask himself now, with his wife gone and brother's grave tarnished. Flaymin Aymin has basically left El Diablo's life in ruins, and now it is up to El Diablo to pick up the pieces and make them fit. A stack of papers are seen, but when examined carefully, they are all from letters sent through the post, there relevance right now is unknown. The letters are placed on a brown and smooth desk, along with a coffee mug, a night lamp and many pencils resting on a notepad. Sat at the table is none other than El Diablo. He has on his new found face paint and is letting his long dark hair run freely down his back. He wears a white t-shirt and has on black denim jeans, other than that; it looks like his night planned out at that desk. The desk must be in a study in his home in Chicago, Illinois; also in the room are several shelves of books a radio and a telephone on the window ledge. The curtains in the room are closed and the only light comes from the night lamp on the desk. To finish the rooms furnishings are a nice navy blue carpet and brown painted walls. It really suits the motif. 'Damn Angel,' says Diablo to himself whilst searching through the pile of letters he has. Angel has since moved out after leaving El Diablo, and her whereabouts are unknown to him, but he has a sneaky suspicion that Flaymin Aymin will know where she is. 'I swear, this better not come to a divorce,' he says aloud in annoyance, 'I'm not losing half of everything then getting kicked out of America by damned immigration,' he finishes while searching through more of the letters. It becomes apparent that he may be looking for a loophole somewhere if Angel does indeed file for divorce. Which, no doubt Flaymin Aymin is already pushing for her to do, if he is in contact with her. 'Funny, I always thought it would be a woman that would ruin my life, but no it's that damn Aymin,' Diablo bitterly says, 'that bastard,' concludes the Mexican Devil. He takes another letter off the pile and skim reads through it. 'DAMN!' exclaims Diablo in shock, 'She spent $90 on leather boots, that's my freaking money not hers,' he says whilst gritting his teeth together, 'There best not be any payments to porn shops or anything, then I'll know that is Aymin's fault,' finishes Diablo, in obvious confusion as to what lays next for him. His whole life in the last two months has fallen down around him, more change has occurred in those two months for him than the prior two years. Strange how things can change, and it seems El Diablo may even change some more to get his life back on track and lose the omen of Flaymin Aymin once and for all. The time now is near 10pm and El Diablo has spent pretty much all day searching through old letters, for god knows what reason, and has had no success in whatever he is trying to do. 'I may as well give up on this soon, I have had found nothing of use,' speaks Diablo in frustration, 'I should really concentrate on my next two matches anyway, Juan Borro and Flaymin Aymin,' finishes Diablo. Despite everything that has happened El Diablo still has to wrestle. Despite the mess his life may be in, he still has to compete at top quality. And despite everything he still has to cope, no matter how much of a hard task it may be. El Diablo gets knocked off his trail of thought by the phone ringing. He stands up and walks over to the window ledge where the phone is situated and picks it up after just three rings. 'Hello,' answers El Diablo! 'Ah, Mr Diablo, this is Adam Grazier, president of Taco Bell,' replies the voice at the end of the phone. Diablo smiles. 'Oh hello, why do you call so late?' responds Diablo hoping for some good news. He hasn't had any good news in a long while now. 'It's about your first commercial with us,' says Adam. 'What about it?' asks the Metaphor of Magic hoping for a positive answer. 'We want to start filming it as soon as possible. Whenever you're free from wrestling is the best time,' he responds to Diablo's delight. Diablo smiles again and sits down on the window ledge. 'I can do it tomorrow actually,' El Diablo responds in glee. 'Excellent, we'll have everyone ready in Mexico for this,' replies Adam jubilantly. Diablo smirks while running his free hand through his hair. 'We'll talk payment later right?' asks Diablo cleverly. 'Of course, I'll fax you the details later tonight,' says Adam, business like. 'Great, bye then,' says Diablo. 'Bye,' finishes Adam. El Diablo then hangs up the phone and smiles before standing up. 'At last some good news for me,' he says with pride and walks back over to his letter pile on the desk. When suddenly his face turns to shock as he picks up one of the letters. He looks at it carefully for a moment. 'Oh shit,' is all he says as he walks out of the room. What could the letter be? And what could it possibly say to get a response like this from El Diablo? Only time will certainly tell.
