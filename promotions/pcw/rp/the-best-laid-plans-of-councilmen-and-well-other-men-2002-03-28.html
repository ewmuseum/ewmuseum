The best laid plans of Councilmen and, well, other men...

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The best laid plans of Councilmen and, well, other men... Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: March 28th, 2002 Happiness. The opposite of anger. A feeling of great joy. Right now, William S. Powell is angrily happy. He has disposed of a person in his way, and is looking for a replacement. He is angry it came to that, yet pleased with the outcome. It seems like quite the paradox. Then again, Politically Incorrect is a paradox. The name is paradoxical. A politician should always be correct....yet these two men are not. Hence the name. They themselves are paradoxes. William S. Powell is a 28 year old politician from the Midwest, in Iowa. Reginald V. Lovecraft is a 47 year old delusional man who thinks he is the governor of Georgia. They have nothing in common. Well, they're both Republicans..but other than that, nothing. They should not be able to get along, yet they do. Paradox once again. Reginald V. Lovecraft, though, is quite happy. He is on his way to the airport now, with lovely intern Katherine Willis at his side. Despite Lovecraft's age, the two seem to get along quite well....as friends, that is.. What will happen when Lovecraft proposes......something more? Sit back, relax, and enjoy the show... ::The long silver limousine of Reginald V. Lovecraft is the site of today's little pathos play. Unbeknownest to the elder statesman of Politically Incorrect, William S. Powell has set up a webcamera in the limousine, and is watching his every move from his own limousine. As we look at the screen of Powell's laptop over his shoulder, via his own camcorder, we hear him laughing maniacally.:: William S. Powell: OH MAN! This is TOO GOOD for words! I have to keep this on tape. With me commentating his advances, I've got blackmail material for YEARS! YEARS I TELL YOU! ::He smiles, as on the screen in front of him, his comrade, Lovecraft, begins to make his move.....:: ((Henceforth (( )) shall denote what is happening on the webcam)) ((Reginald V. Lovecraft: So.....Katherine, how are things? Katherine Willis: Things are well..)) William S. Powell: Ooh! Great leadoff line by the old guy! ((Katherine Willis: So...are you ready for your match tonight? I've been watching PCW for a while now....whether or not they are on the same page, Stormbringer and DarkStar are the real deal. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Maybe so....but William and I are prepared. Powell can handle Stormbringer quite easily. He left the man lying in a pool of blood at the Playboy Mansion after all! No, there is no doubt in my mind that Councilman Powell can handle that. The question is, can I handle DarkStar? ::Reginald straightens out his suitcoat, its red shade making a sharp contrast with his intern's long black dress. Yes, I said long BLACK dress. We're not going THERE yet.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: There is no doubt in my mind that I can! Observe this DarkStar if you will! He was a member of Asylum! He was a member of the Dark Ages....and he was a lackey in both! A man who never had any ambition to lead! DarkStar is simply that: a man without ambition. Now, Powell and I DO have ambition. Our ambition is to be the greatest tag team here in PCW. Bar none. Katherine Willis: Oooh.....Ambition is good...)) William S. Powell: Nice save there by Lovecraft! He seems to really be picking up the pace, but when will he make his move?! ((Reginald V. Lovecraft: Katherine....there is something I've been meaning to talk to you about for a long time... Katherine Willis: Yes? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Would you.....umm.....this is so difficult....)) William S. Powell: COME ON! SAY IT! SAY IT, AND I HAVE THE ULTIMATE POWER OVER YOU FOR THE REST OF YOUR LIFE! Just imagine! I could distribute this to CNN! No wait, I hate CNN! C-SPAN! Yes! That's it! I'll send it to C-Span! I'LL MAKE MORE MILLIONS THAN I ALREADY HAVE! This'll be more fun than fighting that Ganker idiot on Sunday....oh! There's another reason I'm better than Lovecraft! I have a singles match and he doesn't! ((Reginald V. Lovecraft: Would you want to....wait a cotton picking second....the heck is that? ::Reginald's eyes have wandered northward, as he spots the webcam set up by Powell in the northern corner of the backseat.:: Katherine Willis: What is what? Reginald V. Lovecraft: (under his breath): Powell........he's the only one who has access to this limousine....he's going to pay for this... Katherine Willis: What are you saying? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Nothing! Nothing! ::Reginald reaches up, discreetly disconnecting the webcamera, much to the chagrin of Powell.::)) William S. Powell: Damn! There goes a great plan. Next time I'll have to remember to rig one on the bar in there. Guess all that's left to do is worry about the match...Driver! Driver: Yes! William S. Powell: Get the CD out of the glove compartment, my special mix. Track 13. Driver: Yes sir, Mr. Powell! ::The strains of '...And Justice For All', Politically Incorrect's theme music, begin to waft softly into the backseat where Powell sits. The music is low, almost as an oddly soothing backdrop for the young politican's mood.:: William S. Powell: You see, as the song says.....we are seeking no truth. Winning is all. And simply put, Lovecraft and I are accustomed to nothing but victory. Sure, his victories are delusional; my theory is its advanced syphilis due to choosing some bad interns, but hey! I could be wrong! ::Powell's eyes light up at his jest at Reginald's expense.:: William S. Powell: DarkStar, Stormbringer, hell, even you too Ganker....when one helps run a city, he has many things on his mind at once. He has public utilities on mind. He has expenses on his mind. He has ten, twelve things to think about at once. Keeping that in mind, what's a mere two? Two matches...one tag team, one single. Each a different test in its own unique manner. The so-called Perfect Storm, the Lord of Dreams, and a jobber's helper....three men that are as different as you can possibly find in a wrestling promotion. Yet, for all three, the end result shall be the same. Because, Friday and Sunday, they are both Election Day. And, on Election Day....Politically Incorrect is ALWAYS triumphant. I'll see you..gentlemen soon enough. ::Powell frowns as we fade out to Powell listening to his music...and a new message appears on screen.:: PAID FOR BY THE FRIENDS OF POLITICALLY INCORRECT. ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
