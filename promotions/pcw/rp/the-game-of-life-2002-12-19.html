The Game of Life

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/19/02 The wailing and knashing of teeth ring forth. Here in the dark plains of Hell, hope vanishes and flits away like so many broken dreams. 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!' The voice shrieks, piercing the gloom. 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!' 'NO NO NO!!!!!!!!!!' 'Will you shut up?' 'Make me!' The other voices do as well. We march on through the darkness, past lakes of fire to witness a rather odd sight, a man hunched over the outstretched palm of another fellow in a cloak. Skitzo: I'll win this game yet! Beneath the hood, the dark eyes of Clyde glimmer. He smiles knowingly. Clyde: Pick a pearl, any pearl. Mr. Psycho gingerly reaches out, touching one jewel. It sinks, receeding into the demon's hand. Clyde: Hmmmmmmmmmmmmm....... With his free hand Clyde scratches his chin. He then touches a pearl of his own, as nearby a commotion rises. Clyde: (Looking over) Bethany what the hell are you doing? In a pit not far away, the homicidal child wrestles with what looks to be a small winged creature chomping on a highly smelly cigar. Bethany: Come on asshole! Clyde: ......is that Minion..... Skitzo: Pay attention to the game! Skitzo: Yeah, my soul's on the line! Skitzo: My soul's more important than yours! Skitzo: Shut up! Again Skitzo touches a pearl, causing it to sink into Clyde's flesh. Skitzo: How do you do that? Clyde: Perks of being the replacement Satan. Clyde watches the eyes of Skitzo, then does a double take and glares at Bethany again. Clyde: Will you leave poor Minion alone..... Minion: Feisty little bitch. Bethany: Eat death! While the demon is distracted, Mr. Psycho makes his move. He switches several of the pearls around, faster than the eye can see.....then just as Clyde turns, he sits down again. Skitzo: Your move. The burned man glances down at his palm quizzically, raising an eyebrow. Clyde: Now I know you cheated, but really, usually when one cheats they make an effort to move one of the objects from their original position. Skitzo: (Slapping his forehead) You idiot! Skitzo: Am not, you are! Skitzo: Cut it out! Skitzo: Make me! Shaking his head, Clyde removes another pearl from his hand. Skitzo: Argh. Skitzo goes, then Clyde. Then Skitzo, then Clyde, then Skitzo. Clyde: (Smiling) You lose. Your soul is mine. The crazy looks at the now empty playing field....er, palm in shock. Skitzo: You cheated! Clyde: Actually you cheated. Skitzo: I did not, he did! Skitzo: Stop lying! Bethany: DIEDIEDIEDIEDIE! Clyde: Will you knock it the hell off!? On his small chair, Skitzo fumes. He begins to shake, turning a furious shade of red. Skitzo: You haven't beaten me yet anyway! Clyde: (Turning again) Huh? Skitzo: Me either! Skitzo: Asshole! Skitzo: You'll never beat me! Skitzo: Me either! Skitzo: Or me! Skitzo begins vibrating slowly. Then he gradually picks up speed, until he is a blur. Clyde: ......huh? Faster, faster Mr. Psycho moves....until he seemingly fades into two forms. Then another, then another, then another. Clyde: Hm. More, more copies of Skitzo form.....finally 11 of him stand before Clyde, while a single Skitzo remains moping a bit away. Skitzos: (In unison) We have not yet begun to lose! Skitzo: You twits. Skitzos: Shut up. Souless bastard. Skitzo: Meh. Clyde: Ugh.....very well. Slowly standing, the demon extends his arms. Muttering in Latin, he begins to vibrate as well....groans of pain escape his lips. Clyde: Urgh....urgh..... Bethany: I told you not to eat so much cheese. Minion: Get the fuck over here! Bethany: Agh! After nearly thirty seconds of chanting, Clyde suddenly ceases. He smiles warmly.....then promptly bursts, literally exploding into chunks. Skitzo: Well that was interesting. Skitzo: I've seen better. The pieces of the demon lay still on the ground for a moment. Then they stir, growing....growing into larger shapes. Skitzo: Hmph. Finally the bloody bits reach full size, each shifting into the familiar form of Clyde. He stands, now 11 strong. Clyde: There. Skitzo: Gimmick infringement! Skitzo: We didn't have to blow ourselves up to do it either! Clyde: Bah! The burned man snaps his fingers, then holds out all 11 of his palms. In each appears the series of pearls. Skitzo: Let's go then! Each of the Skitzos begin to step forward....however the final, soulless Skitzo yells. Skitzo: Huddle, fools! Skitzo: Huh? Skitzo: What's his problem? Very grudgingly, the patrol does an about face. Marching around their 'brother', every Skitzo in attendence whispers. Skitzo: Psst psst psst. Skitzo: Psst psst psst. Skitzo: That idea sucks. Skitzo: Shut it! And at last they return, looking much more sure of themselves. Skitzo: Alright, let's go! Smugly, the Mr. Psychos begin the game anew. Each Skitzo moves, as the demon twins counter. Though they smile at first, gradually their mirth fades. Clyde: Hm. Another move, then another.....the Clydes scratch their heads. Clyde: Um.... Clyde: Uh..... More moves, then more....and slowly, ever so slowly, the tide shifts. Clyde: It can't be. Skitzo: Ha ha! Skitzo: You can't beat our strategy! Clyde: And what strategy was that? Clyde: Yes. Clyde: Do tell. Skitzo: Easy. Skitzo: One of us makes a move. Skitzo: The other sees if it works or not. Skitzo: If it doesn't we try something else. Skitzo: You can't win! Skitzo: Ha ha! Behind the mass of Skitzos, the lone souless Skitzo slaps his forehead. Each of the 11 Clydes smirk again as they take their turns. Clyde: Well. Clyde: Certainly works both ways doesn't it. Clyde: Really I should have thought of that sooner. Clyde: But I win. Skitzos: (Simultaniously) HUH!? Clyde: All your souls are belong to me. Skitzo: You idiots. Skitzo: Who're you calling an idiot? Skitzo: Now he owns all our souls! Skitzo: Not until we die. Skitzo: Yeah, that's demonic law. I think. Clydes 1 through 7 scratch their heads, while 8 through 11 scowl. Clyde: Well, technically. Minion: COCKSUCKER! Clyde: Shut up. Clyde: Bah. Clyde: Oh well. And as 11 Clydes stand muttering as 12 Skitzos prance their way straight out of the underworld, we mercifully fade to black.....
