Never Know

15 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/15/03 Brianna was sitting on her sofa with a mirror in hand, a blood stained face looking down at it. She checked her nose and had established it wasn't broken, which was always a good thing. She stared for several more seconds before turning her head and looking dead at a quiet Milo, something he barely ever was. 'Is it broken?' he asked with much concern while playing with his shirt's cuff link. 'No, it's not broken, just bruised.' Brianna replied as she turned her head from Milo, setting it back on the couch looking up to the ceiling. It's a surprise that her nose isn't broken, because when the door slammed in her face it knocked her back a few feet. Milo had been in a rush to get to work and hadn't realized that Brianna was on the other side of the door. Next thing you know Brianna's on the floor with blood streaming out of her nose at a fast pace. 'So are you mad at me?' Milo probes, not wanting Brianna to be mad at him. 'No. It wasn't your fault and I understand that.' Brianna replies taking a red hankerchief from her pocket and dabbing away at her nose to remove some of the blood. She goes to get up and falls to the floor as a swift dizziness overcomes her. 'Oh my god, are you alright?' Milo rushes over towards Brianna with worry on his face and helps her get off of the floor and back onto the couch. 'Should I call a doctor?' 'I don't need a doctor, just rest.' Brianna says while finding a comfortable position on the couch, closing her eyes. Milo rushes out of the living room and comes back a few minutes later with a blanket and pillow in hand. He gently places the pillow under Brianna's head, and puts the cream colored cover over her motionless body. Even though Brianna had said she didn't blame him, Milo still felt highly responsible. He had just moved back in a couple days ago after him and Brianna had met up to chat. He had found out everything that had been going on with her and the whole mafia ordeal, but he still wanted to move back in. Brianna had tried to talk him out of it, scared for his safety, but he wouldn't take no for an answer. He continued to ponder things as he sat in the chair watching over Brianna before he was disrupted by a knock at the front door. He got up slowly and walked to the door. He opened it up and his face turned sullen as Thomas was on the other side with a smile on his face. 'What in the hell do you want?' Milo spits out. 'I don't believe that's any of your business as I am here to see Brianna.' Thomas throws back. 'That's too bad, because she's resting right now.' 'Resting? You would think a young vibrant woman like that would be up and about at this hour.' 'She got in a little accident and needed to lay down.' Hearing the word accident filled Thomas with worry. 'What happened? Is she alright?' he throws out trying to get into the house to see. 'She'll be fine, now go away.' Milo says blocking his entrance. 'I really must talk to her.' 'I'm sure even if she was up she wouldn't want to talk with you. Tell me what you want to tell her and we'll have a good laugh over it later.' 'You know Milo, I don't really appreciate the attitude right now. But I have no more time to waist with your little games, so give this to Brianna.' Thomas reaches into his black leather jacket pocket and pulls out and envelope with 'Brianna' written on it. Before Thomas can get any more words in, Milo slams the door and keeps the letter in his hands as he heads back into the living room. He turns on the lamp to the right of him, as the room was in total darkness to give Brianna a more peaceful sleep. He lifts the flap of the envelope and pulls out a folded, white piece of paper. 'What's this?' Milo says quietly as he unfolds the paper, finding a long letter. He briefly reads it and then sets it down on his lap with a look of confusion etched on his face. He wants to be with Brianna again? This can't happen, I can't let it happen. She told me how much pain he put her through after she decided not to marry him the first time around. She told me how badly he made her feel, constantly setting her up for failure. And now this jerk wants to get back with her? Reading this letter will probably force Brianna to give it some thought, because Thomas was always a smooth man with the right words to say. This may pull her in, and I can't let it happen! If I give this to her I will be making her life a living hell...... Milo gets up abruptly walking towards his room. He goes in and closes the door behind him. He sets the letter on his bed while he searches through his drawers pulling out a lighter from within. He then walks back towards the letter lifting it at and angle. He runs his finger over the top of the lighter, and a short blaze of fire emerges egniting the letter. Within seconds it's engulfed with flames and Milo walks it over to his bathroom and runs water on it. 'What are you doing?' Milo jumps as the sound of Brianna's voice can be heard behind him. 'Uh nothing...' 'Well could you do that nothing somewhere else? That smell is killing me.' 'Sorry.' he replies as he throws the charred, water soaked letter into the waist bin. She'll never have to know. Milo thinks to himself as he ushers her back to the couch as the scene slowly fades to black.
