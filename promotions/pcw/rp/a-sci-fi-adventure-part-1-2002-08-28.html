A Sci-Fi Adventure! (Part 1)

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 28, 2002 CHARGE!!! A voice screamed out from a large group, standing in the ruins of a large hive city. This group is decked out in spiked armour and tall helmets, coloured in dark violets, and stained on a few, stained with the blood and giblets of their victims. Many are carrying somewhat large weapons, most are carrying rifles, adorned with more spiked than the armour, and a bloodied bayonet. Some are carrying pistols, some carry close combat weapons. The opposing force is just cutting down their victims with incredible speed and efficiency. A group of people, trying to run are firing while falling back, wearing dark green powered armour. They have standard machine guns, trying to take as many opponents out as they can while trying to make it back to the last Armoured Personnel Carrier. Some rounds just bounce off the charging force, some actually penetrate and slow them down, some actually penetrate and floor a few. They are running as fast as they can, while wearing almost sixty pound powered armour. Their footfalls are extremely audible as they run away. One of them, not wearing a helmet, but bald as a plucked chicken, is leading the charge away from the units. “Almost There, men. Keep running!” The unhelmeted shouts. As they enter an alley, the chasers stop at the entrance of the alley. Some of them are out of breath, but the leader, adorning silver shoulder spikes, removes his tall helmet showing his rather tall hair-do. The hair is golden, but soaked with perspiration. “Sire, are we not chasing after them?” one of them spoke. “No. We have larger plans for them.” The leader said. “But sire, how?” another one spoke. “Their escape route is blocked by a squad of Scourges, and a Ravager. They won’t provide us with trouble.” “Excellent Sybarite…Absolutely excellent.” Another spoke aloud. Sure enough, back in the APC, the driver is navigating the curves that come with the alleys. It speeds by building, kicking up the toxic puddle that was left after the last acid rain storm. On a set of ruins just above, there is a fluctuation of light…revealing a humanoid, clinging to the wall. This one has irregularly shaped spikes on the shoulders, forearms, and shins. He touches a button near his neck. “Mandrake 4 Delta to Archon, they’re taking a different route out. They’re headed approximately 3 more miles north than expected.” The humanoid reports. “Get to your Raider APC trooper; you’re going in with the rest of your group” The radio states. He quickly gets off the ruins and sprints to a floating vehicle. It, like everyone else, is adorned with sharp edges, and it includes a cow (or human) catcher. As soon as the lone scout is in, they speed off after the APC. They are moving at perhaps the fastest rate of speed for any such armoured personnel carrier on the battlefield at this moment, simply because it’s on open road. The wind whips around the open topped vehicle as the contents of it hold on. One of them, the only one with hair for that matter, and has silver tipped spikes. He is our villain, Eddie Mandrake. “Well? How did it go, my brother?” Eddie Asked. “Well, it was great to get some exercise finally after sitting on that ship for a few months” The Mandrake Unit responded. “Don’t worry, my brother. You’ll get a lot more exercise, when we take captives here! When we present them to Master Vect after we return…He will reward us, by allowing us the honour of torturing them!” Eddie stated. He slams his shoulder armour as they continue to speed down the open expanse of land between hives. Meanwhile, in the APC, the soldiers that remain from the defence force that helped defend the remnants of the hive city on this planet. The sergeant of the group enters the drivers’ compartment. The drivers are wearing just standard uniforms of the Imperial Military. “Hey, how long until we reach dust-off?” The sarge inquired. “Approximately five minutes, sir” the co-pilot responded. “Whatever those things were, they simply tore us apart, and we couldn’t get any sort of weaponry on them. When we got into close combat, they just ripped us to shreds.” “No need to worry about that. We are fully armed and protected from what’s out there.” The sarge returns to his seat and closes the door. The pilot rounds one last corner and sees daylight. However, almost lightning quick the Raider APC of the opponents darts in front of the human APC. Like two freight trains colliding, the wreck is massive, debris flies everywhere; the Raider APC literally splits in two pieces, the other APC slides to a stop. Both halves of the Raider explode into a ball of blue fire. Inside the human APC, everyone has been shaken up, and there is a massive piece of shrapnel from the Raider piercing the door of the driver’s compartment, and there is blood leaking through it. “Aw shit, everyone out!” The sarge barks at the other apes in the transport. They gather their gear up and get ready to charge into combat, thinking their enemy is close by. The sarge opens the door, and the apes run out of the transport. Weapons with their safeties off, they dart out of the wrecked carrier. However, they see nothing. They look about the area, and see absolutely no signs of enemy activity. “What the hell? Check the wreck; they may have been killed in there” the sarge orders. Three out of the group of eight approach the main impact of the wreck. In the wreck, are two dead humans, fully intact, and a few limbs, detached from the torsos. The three soldiers stand there thinking… “Sarge! I only see parts from two aliens here!” “How come I don’t like the feeling of this?” the sarge says under his breath. Soon after he says that, about ten soldiers, like the scout that gave away the APC’s change of plan, emerge from their camouflage, weapons drawn to the group. The troop of humans immediately draws their weapons. The sarge reacts in kind. “Hold your fire! What do you guys want?” the sarge inquires. “Very simply, we want you. Surrender now, and we will spare your lives, and after a short stay as our guests, we will return you to your Imperium. Any one of you fires a single shot…and we will kill every last one of you where you stand” Eddie orders. “Well…you heard him boys. Don’t fire a shot” the sarge orders. Soon enough, most of the troops put their weapons down…but raise their knives to their enemies, and begins attacking them hand-to-hand. They however, don’t stand up very well to the even better trained enemies. Two go down with their own knives plunged into their heads. One of them has their neck snapped clean. The rest of them now point the big guns at them. The remaining troops that are alive look down the barrels of the weapons, and simply drop their knives. “I’m glad you chose wisely after we had to make three examples. Now if you please, step into the raider now approaching and please don’t make a fuss about it. We don’t have to make any more examples. Heh Heh Heh.” Eddie orders as the marines fall in line. The sarge, however, sprints right for Eddie, in one final attempt at glory. Eddie pulls out a Splinter Pistol (A spiked pistol, the smaller version of the spiked rifle or Splinter Rifle). He pulls the trigger as a round sails right into the head of the sarge blowing out a piece of his skull. The sergeant’s charge fails miserably, as he drops to the asphalt. Eddie places his weapon back into the holster. He walks away without a second thought. “Bob, load ‘em up!” Eddie Orders as the Raider arrives. A few hours later, at the dust-off point Back at the dust-off point, all the captive human prisoners that are all in shackles are being led into a very nasty looking space craft, it looks like it has wrought iron fencing on the edges of the craft. Many troops are wandering the dust-off point, on patrol. Eddie is standing near-by on a hill, looking out over the massive hives where human civilians are safe…for now. Someone approaches from behind Eddie. Eddie looks back and sees his commanding officer. Archon Rapheel, a rather tall individual, whose blood lust is comparable to Eddie’s in many respects. The battles that Rapheel has been in however outnumber Eddie’s two-to-one. The scars show on his face. “You did well today. A few more slaves for our masters” Rapheel says. “More blood for the wine, more fear to feast on. It never ends” Eddie says. “You want out of the troubles?” Rapheel inquires. “Not at all. I’m just saying that it never ends. We return to the Comorragh with fresh souls, and many spirits to break down. When they die, we head out looking for more slaves. Every damn mission we go on is a fucking slave raid. I want more action than capturing these…fragile creatures” Eddie utters. “I see. So, you want more competition?” Rapheel queries. “Actually, yes. We can go anywhere in this galaxy, and we always target the humans. We never target anyone else. We know where everyone is hiding, it’s just a matter of finding them.” “Who did you want to go after?” “Well, The Tau are always fun. I hear it’s even easier to capture them than it is humans. The Orks are always a glorious battle; I hear that their strength in close combat is renowned.” “Uh-huh. I see. Well, I will talk to the Lord. He will most likely grant a reconnaissance mission to another sector that is not Terran.” Rapheel concludes. Rapheel walks off and boards the space craft. Another member of Eddie’s team approaches him. “Sir? Do you have any orders?” The Mandrake unit that performed so admirably during the mission asked. “Go relax, play with one of the slaves, something. I just need to think right now.” Eddie answered. The unit goes off and boards the ship. Eddie spits onto the dry toxic ground of the planet before boarding the ship himself. It goes through all the checks and takes off to return to the Comorragh with it’s newly won prizes.
