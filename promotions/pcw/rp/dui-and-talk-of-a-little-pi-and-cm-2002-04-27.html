DUI and talk of a little PI and CM.

27 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/27/02 -A 1978 black F-10 pickup truck is hauling ass through central Illinois doing about eighty five in a fifty five. The Allman Brothers Band is cranking out of the radio with “Ramblin’ Man” and the gentlemen behind the wheel is tapping along with the beat on the steering wheel. The man is wearing a PCW staff varsity jacket, chrome shades, and a black and white Stetson hat. He is “The Malice Man” Duke Williams. We get a side profile of him as he drives and speaks.- Williams: This Sunday night PCW gets to run free and wild Malice Style in the main event. How cool is that?? Christ it’s been awhile, I wonder if I should get my hair cut or maybe tan??? F*ck it I am who I am and must silly bastards can give two sh*ts. So whom are the victims when Legend Inc and myself step in the ring for a good old fashion country style bashing? First lets start with Nelson’s tag team Politically Incorrect. Now I would dig these chumps if they weren’t anal catchers in Nelson’s World Series. -Duke pushes in the cigarette lighter and lights up a Punch Rare Corjovo cigar and starts to puff away.- Williams: Lets start with the old man, well hell the guy’s only 8 years my senior. Gramps I don’t know who the f*ck you are or why you think it’s a positive career move to step up and join the Ultimate Authority but I can speak from experience and tell you it’s not hoss. From one Midwestern Republican to another if you got half a brain use it and get out. Since I know that’s not going to happen let me drop this kind hearted image and get down to brass tacks. You call your self the governor? The governor of what?? The governor of who?? The governor of bullsh*t and lies? The governor of cooperate propaganda and smear tactics? Who the f*ck do you think you are hoss? Well Governor let me introduce my self in case your too dense to know…..I’m the president!!! Not some self made title that’s catchy to the eye but the actual president of PCW. Peon your nothing more then one half of the tag team champions. Who I notice don’t have the ball’s nor the nerve to put the gold on the line. That’s ok though I’m not interested in tag belts just beating the hell out of ANY members of the UA. -Duke ashes his cigar before he takes another puff.- Williams: Reggie let me ask you this, where do you get off calling yourselves politically incorrect? Do you think your more PI then me?? You think you tell the world what it doesn’t want to hear because its right? You think you can even match the politically incorrect statements I rant off about on my radio show yet still keep an audince? Reggie you’re an amateur compared to this old hawk. Old man after you step in the ring with this aged warrior your body will have more holes in it then a Gary Condit alibi and your career will crash faster then Al Gores did after the beard -Duke changes lanes to avoid traffic.- Williams: That leaves your little buddy in arms Billy Powell. Kid you call you self a master of debate? I call you a masturbater, cus you like to jerk your opponents around with words laced with spin. James Carville may have d*ck on you, but the Malice Man’s got your number. You want to debate with me then bring it. You know where to find me every Saturday night on fancast.com, so be there. Lets debate junior. Who’s had more p*ssy in the state of Iowa?? You or me? I got my money on the malice man who’s taken many young Iowa corn goddesses right in their corn holes. Sure I’ve been cultivating the fields for a few more years then you but kid my soybean hybrid is top of the line while yours is just not up to par. To quote my good friend and Senator form the fine state of Arizona John McCain “Sacrifice for a cause greater than your self-interest and you invest your lives with the eminence of that cause.” You see that’s your problem kid, I represent the working man and all that is right in this world. While you and your boyfriend are nothing but a pawn in a corporate machine looking out for your selves. That hoss is very Political Correct. -Duke smokes down some more Punch smoke as he dives down the interstate.- Williams: That brings me to a new breed. Just when you thought the machine of the established would reign supreme the kiddies step back up and make a name for them selves. A little bit cocky and arrogant the new breed is the future. Unfortunately for them the future is not now nor is it Sunday. These grenades are wrestlers who do not want to pay their dues like the rest of us did but want gain immediate admiration from their peers and fans. What a crock of sh*t if you ask me. You can’t drive a car till you learn where the gas is. You can’t f*ck a lady till you figure out where the hole is. And you sure as Jessi Jackson is black can not be a star till you pay you dues. You following the old mans advice kiddies? A firework is a great sight as it blasts high in the sky and provides us with a gorgeous visual that’s spectacular for our eyes to see. However just as quick as the wick is lit the show is over. A campfire takes time and energy to build, provides warmth and light, and is the basis of life. You’re nothing more then a cheap Chinese fire cracker while I’m a burning log of Malice. It took time to get my fire hot but its going to last a whole lot longer, just ask your wife. -Duke laughs at his cheap joke.- Williams: Corey Williams you call yourself Controversial? First off let me make it clear to the viewing public that I am not the father of Corey and the only reason for that is his mother was a cow! If rubbing a poor defenseless women’s head in vomit is controversial then kid you guys take the cake. In my book that ain’t controversial its ignorant and p*ssy like. If you tried that in my bar your ass would be hanging from a tree the next morning. An eye for an eye and a punk for a punk it’s time to get an old school wrestling lesson. You know something Cory you’re an embarrassment to all the fine Williams families out there. Have you ever thought about maybe taking a few bottles of Aspirin washed down with whiskey and going to sleep? Give it a try a kid you might find that you like it. I sure wouldn’t mind. -Duke adjusts the heat in the truck.- Williams: That leaves just one character and that would be Chris Chase. What the f*ck is your deal??? I don’t even know who you are. I know you’re in the new breed and you’ve been around for a few months but other then that I know nothing about you. Do we really pay you?? Have you ever sold a T-shirt? The unknown Messiah is what you are. That’s about the equal of being the leader of the Heaven’s Gate cult or a leader in the Scientology movement. Your Canadian?? How come all you young wrestlers all come from Canada what’s in the water up there hoss? HUH? We’ll hell hoss I really don’t care cus that ain’t my bag up there ah. Well kids bring what you got cus the Malice man is ready to fight and that is what I do best. I’m unstoppable and nothing can bring this bad ass to a halt. -From behind the gum rollers pop on from a highway patrol.- Williams: Ahhh sh*t the heat man!! -Duke slows down the truck and comes to a halt on the shoulder as the cop pulls behind him and approaches the cab slowly.- Officer: Licensee and registration please. -The cop shines his flashlight in Duke’s eyes then uses it to point at the PCW camera man who’s been filming Dukes rant.- Officer: Who’s this?? What’s the camera for. Williams: That’s Bob Saggot and this is America’s Funniest Home Videos! Smile for the camera will you hoss. Officer: A smart-ass I see. Ok you want to play hard ball lets play. -Duke hands his papers and license to the cop.- Williams: Sure lets play, I have a bat in the back shall I grab it. Officer: Step out of the truck sir. Williams: Step on this!! -Duke grabs his crouch. The cop becomes irate as he opens the cab up and drags Duke out. His partner has his gun upholstered.- Williams: If your going to touch me like that you should by me a drink first hoss. -The cop slaps a pair of cuffs on Duke wrist behind his back. Then takes off his hat and glasses.- Williams: Hey asshole those are my things!!! Officer: Have you been drinking tonight sir? -Duke thinks about the question for a moment then answers.- Williams: Yes your wife’s breast milk. -The cop turns red as he searches Duke’s pockets and pulls out a water pipe.- Officer: What’s this for? Williams: You obviously not a bible thumper. Officer: Answer the question. Williams: Well I think its used for smoking dope in. Officer: Is that what you use it for? Williams: No I use it to please your wife I hear she’s used to small pipes. -The cop slams Duke into the hood and starts to read him his rights. Duke starts to yell out loud.- Williams: Rodney King….Rodney King….Rodney King!!!!!! Officer: Calm down sir or I will have to restrain you further. Williams: I bet you’d love that you f*ggot. Officer: Your under arrest for suspension of DUI, drug paraphernalia, and resisting arrest. Williams: Cool!! I’m I going to be on cops? Officer: No your going to jail. Williams: Well f*ck you then!! Officer: No it looks like your f*cked. Williams: This isn’t my first trip you know. Officer: There’s a surprise. Williams: I have to piss like a racehorse. Officer: Your going to have to hold it. Williams: No I need you to hold it my hands are cuffed and I have to go now. -The cop turns his attention to the PCW camera man.- Officer: SIR TURN THAT CAMERA OFF. -The camera flips off fast and the transmission ends.-
