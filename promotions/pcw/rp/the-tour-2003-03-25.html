The tour...

25 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 25, 2003 OOC: This was gonna go up tomorrow, but since I found time, I figured I'd put it up tonight instead, so I went ahead and dated it for tomorrow...---------------{{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up backstage at what appears to be the arena where Tuesday Turmoil is to be aired. It’s daylight and the sun makes it look as if its noon. The sky is cloudless and the scene is that of a back parking lot outside the arena. A limo pulls up through the driveway and into the parking lot. The limo pulls up outside the arena and stops short of the backstage entrance. Both doors of the limo in the back of it open up and the two guards that have been seen accompanying Smoke as of late step out as well as Semore Smoke himself who is wearing a regular dress suit. In his mouth seems to be a cigarette, not the usual joint that you’d normally see and in his hand is a duffle bag that must be his things, just in case he’s booked in the last minute to appear on TV. Smoke walks away from the limo as it pulls away and pulls around the arena and out of site. Smoke walks up to the back door and enters the arena with two PCW guards standing inside checking passes to make sure that the people are clear to enter. They see Semore Smoke and realize who he is a move out of the way. Smoke points to his two henchmen who are in close follow of him and says to the two PCW guards... Semore Smoke: They’re with me. {{{{The two guards nod their heads in understanding and Smoke continues to walk forward down the corridor following signs that say “singles locker room”. Smoke turns the corner of a corridor and walks down a bit, following the signs as he walks down the corridor. Then at the end of the corridor, two signs point in two opposite directions, one points to a door on the left and the other points to a door on the right. The one pointing to the left reads “Males locker room” and the one pointing to the right reads “Females locker room”. Smoke turns to the left and enters the male locker room. Expecting some other early bird wrestlers to be in the locker room when he enters, he finds that the room is in fact empty and that Smoke is the first to arrive. Smoke drops his things off on a nearby bench a then looks around the vast locker room with a smirk on his face...}}}} Semore Smoke: Well guys, welcome to the backstage area at a wrestling event, here’s where everything normally goes down that doesn’t go down in the ring. We’ll be staying back here unless someone high up wants us to go out and do something, but I have plans myself to go out and do something today, so I expect to be on TV tonight. Now, you two will not be going out with me tonight, but you will stay back here and just become familiar with the people I hang with backstage and talk to, and maybe Friday or next Tuesday you’ll make your on screen appearance as my guards or henchman, now any questions? {{{{The two men shake their heads “no” but then the one on the left looks at Smoke with a curious look on his face...}}}} Henchman: Will El Diablo be back here tonight? {{{{Smoke looks over to a far wall and nods his head in disappointment...}}}} Semore Smoke: Unfortunately, yes he will be. But that’s not that bad of a thing, you two can taunt him for me tonight, heh, show that the Mafia comes in numbers and stays in numbers, and that if he wants any chance in defeating me this Friday, then he’ll have to get through all of us, even though I don’t plan on you two being out on Fusion, I do plan on playing a mind games to get AJ Trinidad and El Diablo off guard, what a night tonight and Friday will be, heh. Now, to show you guys the catering area where you can get food all day long... {{{{Smoke exits the locker room with both his henchmen in tow behind him. Smoke turns a corner and walks the opposite way that he came in from the main backstage entrance. He sees a familiar blue curtain and walks up and around the curtain. Behind it you see several empty tables, but there is one big table lined up on the wall that is lined with drinks, food, and other items that every growing boy needs, such as cigarettes. Smoke points to the food and says...}}}} Semore Smoke: Go ahead, eat all you can, it’s unlimited and better yet, it’s free! I’ll be right back, I need to take care of some business... {{{{The two henchmen look happy that they’re getting a free lunch and they both grab plates and start stacking up on food. Smoke turns around and exits the catering area and walks back around the corridor and heads back to the singles locker room where he was before. He enters the door and finds that the room is still empty, he walks up to his bag and opens it up and brings out a picture of something and puts the picture in his pocket. He looks around again and then looks at the mirror in the room with a grin on his face...}}}} Semore Smoke: AJ Trinidad is gonna shit his pants tonight when I lay down the law on him with what will happen in the coming days. Hahahaha! {{{{Smoke breaks out into hysterical laughter as he stares at the mirror. The scene fades on the mad laughter of Smoke and the grin on his face...}}}}
