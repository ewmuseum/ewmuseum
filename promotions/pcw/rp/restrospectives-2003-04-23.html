Restrospectives

23 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/23/03 It had come to this: Skylar Green, fully-clothed in the shower. He didn't feel victorious. He didn't believe himself to be triumphant. Why should he? Sure, Skylar didn't really defeat Violence Jack, but he didn't have to. Violence Jack was the one who had to prove that he could beat Skylar. Unable to do it on his own, Violence Jack had Dorn Tracer and The Pharaoh come out and disqualify him. A cheap sham of a victory, it earned Jack nothing, especially since Skylar gave him the sole ownership of the Continental Title before the match even started. Shanahan's gods would not be happy. There was no great battle won, no blow struck at the heart of Skylar Green. The net result was that Jack got hit in the head with steel chairs by his comrades from his religious alliance. Skylar just sat there in the shower, he hadn't even removed his ring attire yet. His black leather bodysuit that absorbs the impact of chops, weak kicks, and short falls, the white denim long-sleeved jacket he has worn as an accessory to his ring attire tonight, the black mask he covers his face with... all of it still on his body. The thin leather boots he wears with the shoe sinks in them so his natural height of 6'8' isn't increased by an inch or two when he wears anything with a sole. The gloves over his hands... it's all still there. He didn't even bother to get a post-match cup of hot chocolate like he usually does, didn't even bother with the autograph session that fans usually ask for in the lounge areas of the arenas. {Shanahan is defeated in spirit. Physically, he lives. His spirit is crushed, though.} His eyelids are shut, the synthetic eyes under them as hollow and empty as Skylar himself feels. At Sandy Wong's deathbed, at her grave, he swore to destroy Violence Jack. He swore to put Shanahan through a pain worse than what he had done to Sandy. Sandy's physical body was completely demolished. The heart within would not have been tainted by Bruce's pure evil, but she was still taken from this world. Taken from Skylar. It hadn't hurt as much. Skylar was used to being alone, he had been most of his life. But still, Sandy's departure from this realm had hurt Skylar as he hadn't before. Skylar had never given his heart, his love to someone. Not until her. There had been acquaintances and there had been dates; but Sandy was different. A restless man in Skylar became calm and content with her around. She had subdued the Sleeping Dragon, faster and more efficiently than anyone ever could. And she did it with love and caring, rather than with hatred and violence. It still hurt, but not as much. That must be what could account for Skylar's feelings at this time. Feelings of dissatisfaction. Of unfulfillment. {It doesn't hurt as much anymore. I wanted to... to avenge you. But I can't if my heart is not in it. Something in me didn't want to kill Shanahan. Is that what you wanted?} Skylar, his head buried in his knees as he has his arms wrapped around his legs, sitting in a position that is almost vertical fetal. {Tell me what to do. Tell me what I'm doing wrong.} Shanahan had lived, and beat himself up in the process. He had his Continental Title, given to him by Skylar himself before the match even started. Skylar didn't need the Continental Title so long as he had his driving ambition, but that was starting to fade. And without that, there was no need to be associated with Violence Jack, or any title held by Violence Jack. Skylar was the stronger of them, without a doubt. But his powers seemed manifested in anger. Skylar was at once his most successful, and his most vulnerable, in the times when Sandy was at his side. But with her gone, he had become something of a loose cannon, nearly destroying anyone who got in his way. March Madness is still said to have seen one of the most brutally devestating sub-main events in the venue's history. {Where do I go form here? What do I do?} But that is far in the past now. Tonight there had been enough happening to occupy Skylar's mind. A mysterious 'druid,' having powers that no one in PCW before it had, made its appearance only an hour earlier. It can form itself into a human figure, or a cloud of mist; has superhuman strength and pain tolerance; can levitate objects and persons, and make them fall unconscious with a simple command and a touch of its hand. Who is this druid? Why did it help Skylar? {It whispered as it passed by. 'Just a memory...' The only memories I've had for the past two months are those of Sandy.} Skylar's head rises from his knees, up to the ceiling. The hot water from the shower, the same water that's been running for the past hour non-stop, falls into his open eyes. The irritation isn't much with the synthetic eyes, Skylar doesn't close them. {Is that you? Is this your way of guiding me? Helping me on the right track? My target was only Shanahan... are you directing me to expand my scope? To defeat not only Shanahan, but Titu and Dorn as well? I hadn't any real desire to fight them. My battle was not with them.} Rising up from the ground, Skylar turns off the shower water. His clothes, soaking wet and saturated, continue to drip water. {But if that's what you want, that is what I will do. I no longer have one target. I have three. I may need your help. Are you still going to help me? I can feel you, like you're still there. Have you come back to aid in bringing Shanahan to an end? Sadly, I'd prefer you not to get involved. You were so innocent. So pure. You had nothing to do with this. It was my fault you were dragged into it. My fault I couldn't protect you. It's up to me to make amends.} A deep sigh. {But you must have your own demons. You can't be anything but restless. Not the way you died. You must be in this for your own reasons, as well. If you must, then so be it. But please let me support you.} Stripping down from his ring attire, Skylar wraps a towel around himself. {And please support me.}
