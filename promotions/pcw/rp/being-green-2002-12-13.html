Being Green

13 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-13-02 Think back long ago. Think hard and long. I want you to remember your first match. How did you feel when you stepped through the curtain for the first time ever. The crowd cheering as you walked down the ring. Think back to how you felt before the match. Was you scared, excited, nervous? Or maybe a little of all. Do you have that memory in your head? Good, hold onto it as you listen to what I am about to say. I can remember my first match ever. It was for the WIW almost three years ago. I was a little scared. Hell who wouldn't be scared? I had to walk out there with my mentor, Genesis. Genesis was a legend in the WIW and word spread out quickly that he trained me. I went against my opponent move for move and I finished him off with the Perfect Storm. I can still remember that day as if it was yesterday. The pride I felt when the roaring crowd cheered for me. The utter joy I felt when I heard the announcer say, 'And the winner by pinfall; Chris Storm!' Backstage I was so excited and scared I couldn't tell the difference between the two. I was just a green hand going against another rookie. Yet people was expecting so much from me because they heard and knew a legend had trained me. They all expected me to be just like him. To pass him one day. Man, I must have jumped around for hours that day. And now? With Adrenalin just a mere two days away. I am jumping up and down again. I feel just like a rookie again. I will step through those curtains and it will feel just like the first time all over again. This single moment is the moment I have waited so many years for. The match that I have waited for all my life. The challenger that can truely bring me to the next level. Sure you have your Angelo Devilles, your Ivan Stanalvis, and your Tempest. For me? It's only one person I want to face. Josh Starwind. The man that nearly took my sweet Tessa from me and ruined my perfect little world. And PCW gave me that. At Adrenalin they are giving me the chance to face the man I hold on a level above the rest. To make matters even dearer for me, they are making it my first ever PCW match. I can't believe it. What luck! I just couldn't keep all the excitement to myself. I was overjoyed. I know, it's weird. I will be the first to say I hate the guy. I think he is a virus. Anything near him is thrown into a world of chaos and pain. It happened to my dear, sweet Tessa. When I step in the ring it will happen to me. But right now my life could use a little bitter chaos and pain. I will be right there dealing it back to him. 'I'll prove to her I am her protector!' I shouted to myself! 'I'm the strongest. Not him and his stupid sword!' I yelled. I turned to the mirror as I hopped up and down, warming myself up for the match. I gave my reflection a cocky smile and flashed him my pearly whites. 'Damn you are a sexy son of a bitch. No wonder you are doing to rip Josh a new asshole!' I told my reflection. I threw a few punches in the air. I let my body move in slow motion as I could see the hair begin to rise up. My excitement was creating goosebumps. I started to laugh. 'Show him just what you are made of. Go into that match a crazy little bastard that will throw everything and the kitchen sink at him. Left, right, hook, uppercut, kick to the knee!' I laughed as I followed orders. A left punch, right punch, hook jab, uppercut thrown in followed quickly by a kick to the knee area. I jumped up high and stomped onto the ground. I stopped suddenly and pointed to the mirror with my hand in the shape of a gun. 'Bang! Bang!' I grabbed my chest and acted like I was shot. I stumbled backwards and fell on the bed. I started laughing. I heard the door open and I lifted my head up to see who it was. Little Christa walked in the room with a curious look on her face. Clearly I was making a little too much noise I guess. She walked over to the edge of the bed and looked at me. With all the innocents she could muster up she simply asked, 'What are you doing?' I looked at her and softly laughed. I rolled over onto my stomach and looked at her. I put my finger to my lips signaling for her to be quiet. I pointed over toward the mirror at our reflection. 'You see that guy in the mirror? I'm telling him why I am going to beat Josh Starwind on Sunday.' she looked at me confused. She stared at the mirror then at me. Once again with innocents of not understanding she said, 'But Chris, that is you in the mirror.' I simply sat up on the bed and put her in my lap. I looked over at the mirror and pointed to our reflection. 'You think?' she nodded her head. 'I don't know. I think I look cuter then him. Yeap. Definately. I'm much cuter then that guy. Don't you think so?' I asked. I was trying to do my best to keep from laughing again. I just stared at her with a smile and a calm look. She finally understood what was going on and started to giggle. 'I think he's cuter. Your hair is messy today Chris. Yes, I have to say he is cuter then you. I'm sorry. But don't worry I still love you.' she hugged me. I was laughing now. I hugged her and held her out a little from me. In a playful manner I asked her, 'Oh you think so?' she nodded her head again and smiled. 'Oh I see then. Well that this.' and with those words we started to playfully wrestle on the bed. I tossed her onto a pillow and looked at her. 'You can be my warm up match against Josh. Although I think you might be a little tougher then him.' I tried to jump on her but she rolled out of the way. I acted like I was hurt and let her take advantage. I guess we must have made too much noise as Tessa walked into the room to see what was going on. Christa and I both see her at the same time and stop wrestling. Me with a pillow in my hands ready to hit her and her with the blanket ready to throw it over me. I dropped the pillow and she ducked under the blanket. Tessa looked at the two of us and laughed. 'What are you two doing in here?' she asked us. Christa stuck her head out from under the blanket and looked at me as I looked back at her. I quickly turned toward Tessa. I collected myself and was ready to tell Tessa the truth. 'Tessa, I didn't want to say anything but I'm breaking up with you. Christa and I are dating now. But don't worry, we can still be friends Tessa.' I said with a completely calm mannor. Tessa looked at me completely shocked. Christa crawled out from under the blanket and hugged me again around the neck. Tessa looked at Christa and frowned. 'Oh I see. Well I guess I'll just have to move out then. I don't know where I'll stay but I guess I can manage on the streets for now.' Tessa said as she started to walk out of the door. Christa started to laugh. 'Bye-bye Tessa.' she said as she waved to her. I couldn't take it anymore. I started to laugh at the little eight year old. I fell backwards on the bed laughing. Christa fell with me. Tessa walked over to the bed and looked at both of us. 'You know, I don't think I can give up Chris without a fight.' she said to Christa. I instantly jumped up and got off the bed. I stepped back from them and looked at them with complete seriousness. 'I knew it! It's because I am just so darn cute!' I proudly said as I turned toward the mirror. 'Ha in your face Josh Starwind! I'm cuter!' I said with a mighty laugh. I placed my hands on my hips as I gave another mighty laugh. Tessa looked at me and put her arm around my shoulder. 'I don't know. . .' she said as she looked at my reflection in the mirror. 'I think he is a little cuter then you Chris. I mean your hair looked a little messed up today. You slept on it wrong or something?' Christ shot up from the bed. 'See I told you Chris!' she shoot out. She was now kneeling on the edge of the bed looking at both of us. I turned toward her and wrapped my arm around Tessa's waist. I pulled her close and gave her a kiss on the lips. I looked back at Christa. 'Just wait and see. On Sunday when Josh and I wrestle I'm going to beat him. Then we'll see who is cuter.' I told them. Tessa put her hand on my chest. She looked at me with a questioning look. 'And what about me and Mani?' I looked at her like I was confused. 'And? Are you two wrestling or something also?' I asked her. She looked at me hurt. I forgot she was wrestling that night too? How could I? She pushed off from me a little but I kept my hold on her to keep her close to me. 'Chris how could you forget? This is my first match in PCW! You have to be out there with me. What if Shin comes out with Mani are trys to attack me during the match?' she asked me. She looked disappointed that I had forgotten her match. I gave her a good laugh and she looked at me confused. 'Tessa I didn't forget you had a match. Don't worry, you'll do great. You are very talented and you was WIW's best Intercontintal champion ever. Sunday will be a night for both of us to always remember. I promise.' I told her as I hugged her tightly. I kissed her once more on the lips. The kiss was interupted by the 'ewww' of a certain little eight year old. Tessa and I both turned and looked at the giggling little girl. We laughed and jumped on the bed. Alex, the young ten year old boy soon came in the room to see what was going on. Before he knew it I picked him up and tosses him on the bed also. The four of us playfully wrestled on the bed as we laughed. All the excitement got me in a very playful mood. I can't wait for this Sunday. Christmas maybe the 25th but Sunday was like a whole new Christmas for me. I will wrestle my first PCW match and I will get to have it against the one man I was never able to face in the WIW. The one man I wanted to face and wanted to defeat. Now I have my chance and I am ready. I'll throw punches, add in a kick for good measure and suplex him out of his boots. I'll lock in armbars, leg locks, hammerlocks, wristlocks, ankle locks, and countless other locks and bars. He won't know what is hitting him or from where. But say. . .isn't there someone else in this match though? Oh well I don't care. My focus is just on one man. Josh Starwind. To hell with the other person. As far as I am concerned, he doesn't even exist to me. I only have eyes for my goal. The prize is within my reach. I will defeat Josh and I will prove everything I have claimed. What else can I say? Oh yes, 'Beware the Perfect Storm. I strike hard and fast. When it's over you'll understand why mother nature is a bitch!'
