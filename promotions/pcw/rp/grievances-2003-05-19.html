Grievances

19 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-19-03 'Insufferable!' The Pharaoh, standing tall over his smaller guards, all of whom are dressed like extras right out of The Mummy and Stargate (actually, they are). Pharaoh takes off his Egyptian headdress and slings it across the Triumvirate locker room, past the miniature Stonehenge that functions are Violence Jack's altar and a statue of En'Flarim for Dorn Tracer's spiritual sanctuary. The otherwise empty locker room is thus decorated with Pharaoh's headdress, some cow's blood, and Dorn Tracer's black 'Rome Essential' luggage full of street clothes. The guards stand in line, all five of them wielding simple wooden bo staffs, averaging 5'7' in height and not fluctuating much either way. Their heads shaved bald at the command of The Pharaoh (so as not to have better managed and better looking hair than Titu Xansiv himself), the halogen lights above reflect off of them. Pharaoh paces back and forth in front of his guards, his white Egyptian ruler's robe dragging past him on the ground while the lights above brighten up his muscular torso, rather dry considering he was just wrestling a match five minutes ago. 'That Druid ruined everything! That blasphemous Tracer and I were beating the heathen's tar out of the large fat one, then the Druid comes out and takes out my partner!' Pharaoh points an accusing finger at the guards. 'Why weren't you out there to stop it?' The middle guard speaks up, nervousness in his voice. Guard 1: You didn't tell us to do anything about The Druid. The far right guard in front of Pharaoh volunteers his opinion, more sure of himself in his reply. Guard 2: In fact, you specifically said that Dorn had to deal with his own problems, and that neither you nor us should be saving him. The Pharaoh shakes his head, stepping toward the second guard. 'You should know that what I said only applies to times when Dorn is on his own! Of course we're not going to be his own personal security force in times where it doesn't involve myself, but this was a tag match and I unfortunately had to have that astrological tart in my corner to win! Which brings me back to my first question: where were you?' The smallest guard, at 5'6', raises his left arm at the elbow while the spear is rested vertically against his right arm, the same as all the other guards. His thick moustache covers his top lip, and makes him look like someone out of a late 70's cop show. Guard 3: We were waiting back here in case you were in trouble yourself. Tilting his head to the side, his medium-length black hair falling upon his left shoulder, The Pharaoh's eyes go wide as he moves over in front of the third guard and looks down into his eyes. 'Speaking of which, I was under attack from Totally Tubular Tommy AND Heavy Metal Harry. AND YOU WERE STILL NOWHERE TO BE FOUND!' Guard 2: Sure we were. You could've found us back here. 'YOU WERE SUPPOSED TO BE RUNNING INTERFERENCE ON THOSE TWO!' The second guard pulls out a piece of papyrus from his robe where a pocket has been sewn. The Egyptian-style is folded into a scroll. Unwrapping it, the guard reads the message inscribed in ornate hieroglyphics, written with a 'Hieroglyphics to English key at the bottom.' Guard 2: Your words were, and I quote, 'I want nothing to be used as an excuse for Heavy Metal Harry backing out of the match at Turmoil, and Totally Tubular Tommy saying that I have him at an unfair advantage. Therefore, unless someone is holding a gun to my head, if they run in on my match this Friday, I'll take care of them myself.' The guard closes the papyrus scroll, a smirk over his face. This is a newer guard, rather headstrong. Being around Pharaoh long enough will change that, though. 'You know what I really meant when I said that!' The Pharaoh shouts at all of them. 'If you aren't going to actually do anything as my bodyguards except for use up my precious oxygen, what am I paying you for?!' A sixth guard at the far left raises his left arm, an index finger in the air. He stands 6', the tallest of the bodyguards. This one isn't even remotely Egyptian. He strokes his braided head, not yet at the head-shaving part. Sucking his gums, he shakes his head and raises a point. Duzza: You ain't paid us yet. I ain't doin' jack shit till I see some cream, yo'! 'Silence!' Pharaoh yells, 'or I'll sacrifice you to Mark Makonie. And there isn't a coalition of black people to save you, either.' The guard at the far right agrees with Duzza. Guard 2: That is a good point. You've been threatening to pay us for months now and we've yet to receive a single check or even cash under the table. The Pharaoh stands back and raises his left arm to the air, forming a fist. 'That's because you've yet to earn a single penny!' Isiy Xansiv, sitting in a loveseat in the corner reading up on spring fashions in Egypt, raises her head from the magazine. Brushing some hair out of her eyes, she rests the magazine down on her lap. Isiy: Sadly, it doesn't work like that. 'What are you talking about?' Pharaoh says, turning back to Isiy. 'Moses, the stupid ragtard, never told the pharaoh Give my people fair wages.' Isiy stands up, her white queen's dress with golden lining draping to the ground. Isiy: They have to be paid simply by virtue of being on your payroll. Defeated on the issue with Isiy siding with the slightly less-than-disgruntled (but certainly annoyed) guards, The Pharaoh leaves the locker room. Two minutes later, he returns with five paychecks. 'Here, a paycheck for each of you.' The Pharaoh hands out the paychecks. Each bodyguard's face shows momentary excitement, then disappointment. Guards: $6.15? Pharaoh nods his head, standing a few feet centered in front of the middle of the group, his arms folded across his chest. 'You've done about an hour's work total since you've started work for me, and that is the minimum wage in this backwards country.' Guard 2: This is a ripoff. 'Work harder and you'll earn more. EARN being the operative word.' Duzza throws his spear on the ground, spitting at The Pharaoh. The saliva falls short, landing about 4 inches from his feet. Duzza: At least ya'll got paid. Where's my money? The Pharaoh waves him off, making a 'pfft' sound with his lips. Pharaoh: You're lucky I even allowed you to make a cameo appearance! The agreement was a cheese sandwich and you've already had thirty-eight of those! The guards look at Duzza. Duzza: ... I was hungry. And besides, what about those three? Duzza points off to the side, where cartoon versions of Jay and Silent Bob stand next to Charles Barkley, wearing his old Phoenix Suns jersey. 'I have no idea where they came from,' Pharaoh says with a shrug and confused facial expression. Jay turns around and sees Charles Barkley. Jay: What in the hell are you following us around for? Charles Barkley leaves, his feelings hurt yet again. 'You two may exit as well,' Pharaoh waves them away. Jay: Snootch to the nootch. And thus the crossover with the cartoon 'Clerks' ends. The Pharaoh begins his pacing once more, his arms folded behind his back, keeping his head turned toward his bodyguards. 'But do not fret, you shall soon have a chance to earn your keep. There are two matches coming up that I shall actually need you to work for. The first of which, the match this upcoming Tuesday Turmoil. My wife and I shall be facing Cowabunga Connection. I want you five--' Duzza: What about me? 'Get the Duat out of here!' And now everyone is gone who isn't supposed to be there. 'Repeat: I want you five to guard her closely. Watch her like a hawk. Don't even let me near her. She must be kept completely safe until the match. And once that happens, you are to stay just beyond the curtains, and if anything not a part of the match (or part of the rules of the match) happens to her, I expect you all to attack anything and everything that's not Egyptian at the ringside area.' The bodyguards all nod their heads. 'I expect Tommy and Harry to try something underhanded. When they're not cheating, they are both incapable of wrestling me. They fear a frontal attack against me because they know that they will fail. So I'm more concerned about an attack on Isiy outside of the match.' Guards: Understood. 'And then there is the matter of the pay-per-view: Supremacy. A fitting name indeed, as I shall reign supreme over all and bring down the Wrath of Horus upon all heathen and nonbelievers!' The guards are inspired by The Pharaoh's words and raise their spears into the air. Guards: Yeah! Pharaoh's gaze sinks down to the floor. 'All... but one.' Everyone looks at him puzzingly, save for Isiy, who merely reads her magazine, seated in the corner. She shakes her head, a smirk across her face. 'Skylar Green. He's been a constant thorn at my side with all these little challenges. I hate to say it but he could be a serious threat to my World Title aspirations. This entire time since Spring Fling a month ago now and I've yet to win a match against him that Tommy hasn't helped me to a win!' Silence in the room, as The Pharaoh moves over to the mirror at the side wall adjacent to Isiy's position on the black leather loveseat. He stares into the mirror. 'For all my greatness, my strength and cunning, and the backing of the most powerful gods known in history... and I couldn't beat Skylar Green, or anyone he chose to assist him in wrestling me! I've lost to two rookies in Miss Moonlight and James Freaking Edwards! I wouldn't have lost if they were on their own... but something about Skylar inspired them... it gave them a harder drive to win.' The Pharaoh pounds his fist into the mirror. 'They beat me. Skylar beat me. Why?' Pharaoh turns back to his bodyguards. 'I'm doing everything right, everything that the gods have told me, and I still haven't been able to beat Green! Something's not adding up! In everything I try, everything we possibly compete in, Skylar ends up beating me or outlasing me... he just does better than I.' Pharaoh sinks to the ground on his knees. 'I'm considering just giving up on trying to defeat him.' The Pharaoh turns his head back to Isiy. 'You talked about making a change. That would be a big change. I'm not one to quit, but everything else I'm doing just isn't working.' Isiy shakes her head. Isiy: No. I've known Titu for too long, and he would never quit, even if he felt he was going to lose. The Pharaoh enters the room. Pharaoh: What the Duat is going on in here? Skylar stands up to his full height of six feet eight inches. 'Just messing around.' The Pharaoh stomps into the locker room and slams the door behind him. Pharaoh: You're wearing MY clothes. Conversing with MY wife? PAYING MY BODYGUARDS?! 'Had to do something to pass the time,' Skylar says, shrugging his shoulders. Duzza sticks his head back into the locker room. Duzza: Can I have a few more sandwiches. Skylar, The Pharaoh, Isiy, and all of the bodyguards shout at him. Everyone: GET THE HELL OUT! Duzza: Damn... Duzza closes the door. Pharaoh: As for you, Green, kindly stay away from me and everyone I know! We'll meet at the pay-per-view. Until then I'll take any more of these 'visits' as your stalking of me. Skylar walks out of the door. The Pharaoh is left with Isiy and his bodyguards. He steps forward to his guards, still sweating from the fight he was in minutes ago. Pharaoh: You couldn't tell me from a tall heathenous half-breed? The guards all look down at their feet, muttering under their breath. Pharaoh: Say something? Guard 1: We never paid that much attention to you. Pharaoh: I knew it! Isiy stands up again, approaching The Pharaoh. Isiy: You must calm yourself, dear. You'll give yourself an anurysm. The Pharaoh turns back to Isiy. Pharaoh: And what have you to say? We've been married all this time and you couldn't tell the difference? Isiy rolls her eyes. Isiy: I knew it was him, I'm not a fool. Pharaoh: Then why didn't you tell the others that it was Skylar and not me? Isiy: As I recall, it's not my responsibility to make sure that the bodyguards are aware of who you are. The Pharaoh's hands go to his forehead, slapping upon his head. Pharaoh: Insufferable!
