Mine!

10 May 2003

Date: 5/10/03 BEEEEP BEEEEP BEEEEP BEEEEP The annoying high pitched ringing of my alarm clock only seemed to grow louder the more I sat in bed and peeked out at the sun which was just rising. I reached out to the alarm and smacked the snooze button, hoping for another ten minutes sleep but just like many of my wishes it wouldn’t be granted with an early riser such as my mother living in the house. Just as I had expected the silence after the alarm was not long lived, as the door separating my room from the living room flung open as my mother charged in like a drill sergeant waking his troops first thing in the morning. “Time to get up Tessa.” She proclaimed as she peeled the pink cotton sheets off the bed and away from my grasp so that I could not curl under them and fall back asleep. “Fine, I’m up.” I answered in a rather annoyed tone, hoping to convey the message to her that she should leave and allow me some time to get changed and slowly wake up. I was slightly glad though that she had woken me up, considering that it would give me time to study my opponent for Turmoil, Dorn Tracer, and let me plan some strategies of my own on how I could possibly defeat him and take from him a possession that was of value to both me and him, the television title. “Good, now that you are up you can get out and finally find me a room. I’m tired of sleeping on that wretched couch.” She groaned as she successfully found something that she could complain about just as I suspected she would. Yet to me it made no sense since there were two empty bedrooms in the house. If she wanted a bed so bad why didn’t she take one of those rooms? “A room?” I began to question wondering why the rooms I offered her were not being used for her room. “Mom there are two rooms open, what do you mean find you a room?” I finished my sentence as I looked over at her as she paced around my room looking it carefully up and down. “Tessa the bed in one of the rooms is too small, surely made for a child.” She answered without looking at me as she continued to browse through my room. “You can get a new bed for it, you’ve got the money.” I replied as I took the sheets that had been taken away from me and tossed them on the bed so that I could make it seeing as though if I left it unmade I would never hear the end of it. “No need to bother, it’s your house so the way I see it if you want to put a new mattress in there for me then it’s coming out of your check, after all this is your house.” She replied in a snobby manner as if it was my responsibility to buy her a mattress of her choice. I thought to snap back and tell her to buy her own but then I remembered that there was still the guest room. “Then why don’t you sleep in the guest room, there is plenty of space in there.” I said, hoping that there wasn’t something in that room that she could complain about, but of course there was. “The guest room? HA! How do you expect me to sleep in there when the man you call a boyfriend has his junk littered all over the place.” She retorted as she turned her nose high up in the air like a true snob. “Oh my god I forgot.” I exclaimed as I remembered that Chris had taken the room because he claimed that it was no longer right to sleep beside me considering I was a Green now. I had totally forgotten that he had still not come back for his materials which really had me convinced that something bad had happened to him that no one wanted to tell me about, or that his opinion of me had suddenly been yanked in another direction and he totally hated my guts. “Perhaps you could switch though. It seems that you have no problem with the mess left by that pig and your room is just perfect for me!” My mother continued on as she ignored my words and continued to attempt to manipulate her way into getting me to say yes by using Chris as a way to get to me. “Fine…” I gave in, knowing that she would hound me relentlessly until I gave into her. Besides it couldn’t be too bad. The room was only slightly smaller than mine and I could easily just pack Chris’ stuff away and make room for my own. “I knew you would see things my way.” She replied in a pert tone as if she knew she would not even have to try because of the influence she had on me as a mother. Although that was not the case I let her stand there in awe and believe it as I silently slipped out of the room and walked down the hall to the guest room and made myself at home there among the items Chris had left behind. I walked into the room which was cleaner than I had expected it to be considering the comments my mom had made about it. The bed was made, everything was in place, and it was spotless. I don’t know what my mom was complaining about. The things that Chris had were all placed away in a closet, on a dresser, or tucked away in draws. I sighed as I fell on the bed thinking about how much I missed Chris and how much of a drama queen my mother was. She had made it seem like he was a total slob and had left a pig style behind for me to clean up, yet here he was more organized than even her. “Why can’t you come back Chris?” I whispered to a picture of him that was placed in a frame on the small table beside the bed. I had missed him dearly and just about everyone knew that, yet there was nothing I could do about it. Then to top it off my only other ally, Jackson Rust, had decided to just up and leave. It seemed that I was on my own now and the only way I was going to make it was to hope that I didn’t find myself too many enemies, or else I might find myself cornered with no way out. I sighed and closed my eyes realizing that even though it could be dangerous to have no allies this would also give me a chance, a chance to prove that I could make it on my own and prove myself as not only a great tag team competitor but a damn good singles competitor as well. No longer would it be Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor, it would be just plain Tessa Windsor showing the world that I could make it on my own and that I didn’t need Chris’ help to become a success. “I love you Chris, but this is my room and my time to shine now.” I whispered to the picture once more as I slipped a picture of myself into the frame, slowly blocking out the memory of Chris. If there was something I needed to do to succeed on my own it would have to be block out the memory of Chris and focus on my own career. Chris had left and that as his choice. I should learn to accept it and move on, and the time for that was now. If Chris wanted to just abandon me the way he had done to many other girls in the past that was fine, he would simply miss out because now it was my time to shine and show the world just how great I could be and it would start on Tuesday with my match against Dorn Tracer. After all, what better way to prove yourself a successful singles competitor than to win the television title on your first shot?
