The Sage of A Champion: Part 15

4 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-4-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!Part 15” Chapter 5: Mission Of Violent Ray UnfoldsDay 6: The Insane One, Mir, Begins without BladeD :: The scene is picked up from where we left off. Slowly, but surly the plans are unfolding and the final staging of planning are almost finished which means it is almost time for BladeD to get down to business. :: General Powers: Has Jessica been informed about any of this? BladeD: No she hasn’t. I haven’t gotten the chance to tell her yet, and really I don’t want to tell her. General Powers: Well, you can’t just run off without telling her because the first thing is going to do is call me up or find some way of getting a hold of me, and then when she does I won’t have any other choose, but to tell her where you are. That would make it even worse, so you are going to have to sit down and talk to her about it. BladeD: Yea, but that make’s her all nervous and crap, so when I go out and the mission is started the only thing I can think about is how worried she is. You know I can daydream while doing certain things… :: Before he can go on General Powers cuts in. :: General Powers: Yea, you seem to daydream all the time around me and around people you work with. BladeD: Funny, funny. Anyway, I can daydream doing certain things, but during something like this it is never good to daydream and I hate having to do that because it can throw my game off very quickly. General Powers: Well, I don’t know what else to you I mean you have to tell. Point blank. BladeD: Well, are we finished now? General Powers: Well, we still have Main Plan B and Main Back-Up Plan A to go over. BladeD: Oh. General Powers: If you do for some reason have trouble getting to Sergeant Mills, then you would need an alternate plan correct? BladeD: Correct. General Powers: Well, in the area that you are going to be in, there should be a building called “Engaged Forces.” It is basically a library of sorts… Well it isn’t quite a library, but it is place you can go to in order to have access to all sorts of information that you wouldn’t normally have. From there you can find out every detail about the area you are going to be in, like all the technical stuff, people that work there, ect. Really the only thing you won’t be able to find out is classified information. One reason why I told you about this place is because Sergeant Mills may recommend you to this place to get other information. BladeD: I am guessing I am going to be carded like crazy to get in the place. General Powers: Actually you won’t. BladeD: What? Why is that? General Powers: You are going to have to remember that the area you are going to be in is all enclosed. Like I said. Think of it like an air force base. At the only entrances in side they are going to card you. Once you get through that you are not going to have to worry about being carded for anything else, you just have to make sure you have your id and all that like a normal person would. BladeD: Yea, I understand what you are talking about now. General Powers: As for a main back-up plan if all that fails… You are basically screwed unless you can find one for yourself. BladeD: :: tries to hold in laughter, but fails:: Anyway, why do you say that? You always have a main back-up plan. General Powers: Well, Russia has always been a part of the world that I have always tried to avoid. I never really liked it that much, so Sergeant Mills is really the only person I know in that area. He is basically it. You will have to find everyone else on your own if you are going to need help from anyone else. BladeD: That is odd of you general. Usually you know everyone and their second cousin. General Powers: Yea, well… I don’t know. Russia just always seemed like an odd world to me because my only visit there I didn’t like. I didn’t like the way the people talked, walked, or ate. I mean there wasn’t one bit of Russia that I liked. From the time I got there to the time I left that was all I thought about, was leaving, so I never wanted to re-visit Russia and any chance I got, I always find a reason not to go, so because of that happening I never really met anyone in Russia at all. The only people I did met it was my only time talking to them. BladeD: I have been to Russia one time and I enjoyed it for the most part. General Powers: That is because you enjoy every place from home that you visit. BladeD: I guess that is true, but Russia didn’t seem bad at all to me. General Powers: Oh well, it is because of that reason that you aren’t going to have much help. It makes me mad because Violent Ray is the United States’ biggest threat and it had to come from the part of the world that I know least. I have some really good friends that have all kinds of contacts in Russia, but I haven’t been able to get a hold of my friends. I know if I could get a hold of them I could probably get us some valuable information that would save us a lot of time, but the problem is that I don’t have much time in order to get into contact with them… BladeD: Yea, that kind of sucks. I don’t anyone in Russia because like you I have only been there one time in my life and I don’t know anyone that knows anyone from Russia, so I am out of luck both ways. General Powers: Well, that isn’t your problem either because that isn’t in your job description. The only way that could be your problem is if you were supposed to have gotten contacts in Russia or whatever for whatever reason. BladeD: I understand what you are trying to say. I didn’t realize it was in your job description to get contacts. General Powers: Well, any place that I go to for business I am supposed to get contacts of the most important people that I met up with and say in contact with them as much as possible and it is because of times like these, why I am supposed to do that. BladeD: That is why you are usually constantly on the phone with people that don’t relate to really much of anything then. General Powers: Ha, Ha! Yes if you want to put it that way. That is why a lot of the times I am on the phone; I am on the phone b-s-ing. Basically, anytime that I have free while at work I am supposed to get on the phone and talk to people, but we are beginning to get totally off subject and need to talk about this later. BladeD: Yea. :: laughs :: Well, I wish I could have that job. General Powers: It is ok I guess. BladeD: Well, I still hate the fact that I am going to have to tell Jessica about all of this. I mean I really don’t want to, but I guess I really have no other choose, but to tell her.General Powers: Well BladeD. You become a stronger man every time you do these kinds of things, so in a sense, they are good for you. BladeD: Yea, I guess you are right, but they aren’t easy things either. I know right when I tell her, she is going to start having tears in her eyes and there is nothing I can do about it, but tell her it is going to be ok. General Powers: Trust me BladeD. Jessica will be ok. :: The scene cuts there. BladeD has, I guess you could say, a tough task ahead of him before he goes on this mission. I would hate to tell a girl that I love and that also loves me that I am about to go on mission that I may not return from. It isn’t the easiest thing in the world, but sometimes stuff like that just has to be done and this is one of those times for BladeD. I guess it just shows a little bit of his softer side, which isn’t a bad thing for someone that always has to act so tough. ::
