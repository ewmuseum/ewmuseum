Date: October 3, 2002 ﻿*********The Tank __29—********* Questions and Questions From stumps and stumblesthe brain enters regions of wonderousnothingness. I’ll answer you soonand maybe it won’t be what you’re waiting for. ~Leigh Donovan Saturday, September 28 - Day 86 Shortly after role call, Tim McDonald grabbed his towel and headed toward the shower, but inreality, he ducked into one of his loyal Italians’ cells and waited there until Vincent Giovannileft. Now, Slick Timmy McDonald has returned to his cell where he is currently rummaging throughhis bullish cellmate’s belongings. “Motherfucker!” he curses when he can’t find the small notepad that he’s searching for. Tirelessly, he searches, all the while keeping one eye fixed just outside the cell in case Vincentreturns. “I know you from somewhere, you motherfucker,” Tim spits about his cellmate as he continuesto file through the large man’s drawer, and then beneath, even in his shoes. “I just have to figureout why...” Before long, McDonald gives up his search, cleans up the mess he’s made, and slams his wayout of the cell to the main floor of Unit B. Saturday, September 28 - Day 86 “Harvey,” a firm, cold voice breaks through the Aryans’ usual pool table conversation. Themiddle-aged one eyed Nazi turns away from his game to find himself starting at the bearded faceof young Jason Snow. “Well, well, well...” Blake says with a sarcastic smile. “To what do I owe the pleasure.” “Shut the fuck up, Blake,” Snow says with complete disregard for Harvey’s entourage of ratherlarge Aryans. “I want to talk to you about Leigh Donovan.” Witha slight chuckle, Harvey purposely turns his back to Snow and leans over the pool table totake a shot. “What about him?” “I want him out.” “Out of what?” Blake asks as he sinks the three ball into the side pocket. “Out of you sick bunch of fucks here,” Snow says, gesturing to the skin-head men surroundingthe balding and one-eyed Harvey Blake. “I want you to let him go. Reject him.” “You want me put him back as J-Thug’s bitch?” Harvey asks, turning back to face Snow oncemore. “I’m the only thing he has, Snow. Maybe you can get by without a gang in this hell hole,but that kid’s lucky to have lasted as long as he has. He needs me, Snow. I’m his family now.” “You sick fuck,” Snow says sharply while shaking his head. “No one in this place needs youor this bullshit you preach, you old cocksucker.” Snow takes a step toward Harvey,glaring at him through his cold blue eyes. “I’m warning you to stay away from him, Blake. Idon’t want to be listening to that fucking Nazi shit every time I walk into my cell.” Harvey doesn’t break a sweat under Snow’s heavy glare. He advances toward the larger man,and the two end up chest to chest. “Remember who you’re fucking talking to, son,” Blake says,his one blue eye fixed on Snow’s lion-like face. “I’m not some fucking playmate you can justchew out whenever the fuck you don’t agree with something I say or believe. You step out ofline with me, Snow - and you’re a dead man.” “If you want to try something,” Snow says, lowering his brow to intensify his glare, “I fuckingdare you to.” With that, Snow turns his back to the one-eyed Nazi and stalks off, leaving Blakeand his entourage watching. “Are we just going to let him disrespect us like that?” Jake Hooper pipes up in Harvey Blake’sear. “He fucking turned his back to you Harvey! He fucking mocked you!” Harvey watches in silence as Snow makes his way to his cell. Sunday, September 29 - Day 87 Despite the fact that he’s in the gym, Vincent Giovanni has no intentions of working out. Thebullish man sits on a weight bench holding a bottle of water while keeping an ever-watchful eyeon the door. The gym is relatively quiet at this hour, which is why no one is questioning Vincentoccupying a bench he has no plans to use. Finally, the man he’s been waiting for walks through the heavy steel doors - the young red-hairedman known only as the Rat. “You,” Giovanni says when the freckle-faced youth enters the gym. “Come over here a second, I want to talk to you.” “Sure bud,” the Rat says with a smile, approaching the Italian bull. “What do you need?” “Information,” answers Giovanni. “My specialty,” the Rat smiles, his freckled cheeks puffed out to either side like a food-packingrodent. “I’ve head,” Giovanni says seriously. In this relatively secluded area, he’s completely droppedall signs of his passive disguise. “You’re going to tell me some facts.” The Rat chuckles. “Sure,” he begins, “I’ll tell you whatever the fuck you need to know if you’vegot the cash to pay for it. If not, then you can, as they say in Italy - go fuck yourself.” “Hey!” Vincent says, grabbing the nineteen year old’s skinny left arm. “I’m not paying youshit!” “Then I’m not telling you shit!” The Rat holds his ground. He soon regrets that, however. Unlike most of the people that the Rat deals with, Vincent Giovanni isn’t affiliated with any ofthe Tank’s gangs that might value his information or life. Instantly, the Rat finds himself reelingin pain as Vincent twists his arm with one mighty tug. “You still not going to give me what I want, you little prick?” Giovanni asks, still seated andforcing the redheaded youth to his knees. “Look at me! Fucking look at me!” The Rat raises hiseyes long enough to get slapped hard in the face by Giovanni’s merciless broad left palm. “Youdon’t fuck with me, kid - you got that? Maybe you think that because I don’t run with one ofthese cocksucking gang-bang groups that you don’t have to give me any respect. But let me tellyou something you pathetic little bastard, I’m Vincent Giovanni - Vinny fucking G! And when Iask something from you, you’re going to respect my wishes! Do I make myself clear.” “Yes,” the Rat’s voice cracks in pain and Giovanni finally releases his bone-crushing grip. “Good,” Vincent Giovanni nods. “It’s nice that we’re finally seeing eye to eye on this.” The Ratgets off his knees and stands in front of the bullish Italian, about to be mugged of his onlyvaluable possession - information. “What do you want to know?” the Rat says, his will shattered. “I need some information on Doug Hattoni,” Giovanni answers. “More specifically, how hedied.” “Why?” the Rat asks curiously. “You planning to make a move on Tim McDonald for control ofhis old operation.” Suddenly, Giovanni lashes out once more, and as quickly and ferociously as ever - he slams ahard right fist into the Rat’s thigh, crumbling the younger man back to the gym floor. “That’snone of your fucking business!” Giovanni bursts out at the floored young man. “Jesus!” exclaims the skinny redhead as he clutches his aching thigh. “You broke my fuckingleg!” “It’s a charlie horse you God damn pussy!” fires Giovanni. “Now, lets get back to business! Iwant to know everything you know about what happened to Hattoni.” “Ok, ok!” pants the Rat while squinting his eyes through pain. “Whatever you fucking want,man. Whatever you want.” “First and foremost,” Giovanni starts, “who did the deed?” “I don’t know, man,” the Rat says in a last ditch effort to escape Giovanni’s persistence. “You know,” says Giovanni before digging his heel into the Rat’s aching leg. “You know andyou’re going to fucking tell me!” “Ok, ok!” the Rat exclaims. “It was the Aryans, all right? The Aryans had it in for Hattoni for afucking month!” “Now we’re getting some where,” says Giovanni with a smile. “But too bad for you, I alreadyknew it was the fucking Aryans. I know Blake gave the order - I just want to know who did thefucking dirty work.” “Blake did it himself,” answers the Rat. “A couple of guys helped him, but word has it, Blakewas the man who smothered him.” “Heart attack....” chuckles Giovanni, thinking of the story that had originally explained Hattoni’sdeath. “How in the fuck did Harvey Blake get into Hattoni’s cell if there was a war between theItalians and the Nazis? I thought that bad motherfucker, Jason Snow, watched Hattoni’s ass likea hawk.” “Officially,” Rankin starts, “Snow got caught up in a fight with the Aryans and Hattoni gotclipped in the commotion. Unofficially, that’s bullshit. Snow didn’t have a fucking scratch onhim when it was all said and down. I don’t care how fucking tough you are - if you get in a fightwith half a dozen blade-touting Nazis, you’re going to come out bloody.” “Snow wasn’t Hattoni’s only guard though,” Giovanni says. “Of course not,” the Rat shrugs. “But the other ones were no where near Hattoni’s cell when theshit went down.” “So you’re saying,” Giovanni tries to put things together, “Snow sold out Hattoni, but someoneelse must have been on the inside of the Italian gang must have been in on it?” “You’re smart as a fucking whip, aren’t you?” the Rat says sarcastically. He continues beforeGiovanni makes him pay for the remark. “There’s conspiracy all over this thing. And I haven’teven told you the real kicker yet.” “Oh?” “There was a riot in Unit E, right?” the Rat starts, beginning to get into his story now. “And abunch of fights broke out here in Unit B when all the guards rushed off to help out.” “Wait a minute,” Giovanni interrupts. “You’re telling me that all the guards rushed out of UnitB to go help in Unit E? Isn’t that against some kind of code?” “A bunch of them,” says the Rat. “A couple of C.O’s running over to lend a hand - sure. But wewere left in Unit B with what? Half a dozen of the cocks? That was no accident, my friend. Imay be young, but I know how things work around here. And when you see a dozen cocksrunning out to get involved in a riot - something’s fucked up.” “You’re saying they were paid off? By someone in here?” “Maybe not all of them,” shrugs the Rat. “It’s possible that someone got to the head C.O. Hewas, after all, screaming like a bitch to get as many people as possible over to Unit E. So, Iguess you’re left with the question - who had the money, opportunity, and balls to bribe a C.O? Other than Hattoni.” Giovanni doesn’t squeeze the Rat for any more information. He has all he needs to know.
