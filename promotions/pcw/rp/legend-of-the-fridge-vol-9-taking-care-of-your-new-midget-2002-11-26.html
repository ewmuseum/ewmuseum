Legend of the Fridge Vol. 9: Taking Care of Your New Midget

26 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/26/02 [The man known simply as Pete sits at his computer in his small one bedroom apartment. He stares at the blank monitor with the television remote in his hand. He's pressing buttons on the remote, pointing it at the computer.]Pete: Work, damnit. Come on! Turn on![Pete slaps the remote. He opens the battery case to reveal that the batteries are missing. Disgusted he throws the remote to the ground, where it bounces, and somehow hits the power button for the computer. The Computer whirs to life as Pete stares at the remote. He shrugs and looks at the monitor.]Pete: Strange. Anyways. Let's see. Where's the internet?[Pete looks on the desktop, and double clicks an icon. Internet Explorer pops up.]Pete: Let's see. I gotta find something about midgets...let's try...Mi..jet? yeah, that's right. Enter.[Pete presses enter, and watches the results pop up.]Pete: What? Not spelled right? Mi-Jet? M-i-j-e-t. Huh? 'Did you mean to search for Midget?' What the hell? There's a 'D' in Midget? Since when?[He clicks the link that says 'Midget' and watches the results pop up.]Pete: Hmmm...Rock Midgets? No...Scary Midgets? No...Ah-ha! Here it is! Slave Midgets![Pete clicks the link. Instantly hundreds of windows pop up each showing a more perverse act. Pete looks on in horror as he sees Midget love, Midget menage-a-tois, Midgets and Horses, Midget Tossing, Midget Abuse, Chinese Midget Torture.]Pete: Well, that wasn't it. Damnit. Where the hell am I going to find out how to take care of a midget?[Pete reaches down on the desk and grabs a large sandwich. He puts the meaty treat to his mouth, and opens widely. Pete takes a giant bite out of it, chews sloppily, and makes funny gurgling noises.]Pete: mmmm...My Moof des big smammich...[Pete puts the sandwich back down, and closes all the windows that popped up. He goes back to the search engine and looks again for a site about midgets. He clicks another link and is taken to a page about an auto race.]Pete: No, I'm pretty sure Midgets aren't supposed to drive. Man, can't you find anything on the internet?[He surfs around a bit more, and looks at another few pages, each having less and less to do with midgets.]Pete: This is useless. I wonder if the library would have anything? Hey, Tso?[General Tso walks out of the bedroom, around the corner and into the kitchen. He looks at Pete.]Tso: ...? Pete: Come on, let's go to the library. We need to find out what to feed you. Tso: ...! Pete: What do you mean, you eat normal people's food? You need a special diet. Tso: ...? Pete: Don't talk back to me. Tso: ... Pete: That's better. Get your jacket and snow suit. It's cold out there.[5 minutes later, Pete's walking down the street with General Tso, headed towards the library. Around Tso's neck is a collar, and connected to the collar is a leash. Tso does not look happy, and is dragging behind Pete. Pete watches the cars go by, and pulls the leash.]Tso: ...? Pete: Yeah, you need the leash. I don't want you getting away you know. Tso: ...! Pete: What do you mean, you're not a dog? Of course you're not. You're a midget. I just don't want you running away. Tso: ... Pete: Yeah. I think so too. You promise not to run away? Tso: ... Pete: Fine. Take it off.[Tso takes the leash and collar off. Pete and Tso finally reach the library and go inside. Pete walks up to the snotty looking librarian at the front desk.]Pete: Excuse me. Librarian: May I help you? Pete: Yes. I need a book about midgets... Librarian: [Obviously disgusted] Don't you mean 'Little People'? Pete: No, I mean midgets. Librarian: Sir, the politically correct term is 'Little People.' Pete: I'm a Democrat. I need a book about midgets. Librarian: [More disgusted] Over there.[She points to a large section of books. Pete tries to shake her hand, but she just looks at him, rolls her eyes, and returns to her librarian type work. Pete shrugs and walks over the the section. Scouring the shelves, he looks for his book. He grabs one off the shelf, and opens it. The title reads 'Midgets for Dummies'. Pete walks with the book and sits in one of the large chairs. General Tso walks around grabs a book on 'Living with Fat Men for Dummies'. Each man gets comfortable, as they start to read.] [1 hour later] [Loud, horrid noises eminate from the Midget section of the library. General Tso stands, staring at Pete, who's fallen asleep, and is now snoring. Library patrons rush out of the library, fearing that the building is about to collapse. The disgusted librarian walks over to Pete and Tso, and shakes her head. Tso starts to shake Pete to wake him. Suddenly, Pete's eyes spring open.]Pete: Broccoli! No! NEVER![Pete grabs Tso around the neck and starts shaking him. The Librarian screams, and runs over to her desk. She picks up the phone and calls 9-1-1. Pete suddenly comes to. He puts Tso down, and looks around.]Pete: What the? Oh. Just you. Sorry. Tso: ... Pete: Wanna go grab a bite to eat? Tso: ...? Pete: Sure. I didn't know Midgets like Taco Bell. Tso: ... Pete: Me too.[Pete and Tso walk out of the library as stunned patrons stare at the oddest of the odd couples.]
