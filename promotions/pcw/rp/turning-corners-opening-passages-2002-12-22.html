Turning Corners, Opening Passages

22 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd Dec A light snow gently falls from the sky laying softly on the already vast amount of white on the ground, shadows of trees are cast upon the snow and ever so softly more snow drops to the ground from the trees. Standing atop these trees are several magnificent birds, trying to find warmth somewhere in this freezing weather. A large ball of snow drops from another tree and crashes onto something metallic, emphasized by the loud 'clinking' sound. It becomes clear that the ball of snow has hit and laid on top of a vehicle. A red car, practically covered all over in snow, except for a few blotches where the red shines through into the dark cold day. In this vehicle are two people, one male one female; it's El Diablo and his wife Angela. Both look absolutely freezing, and it's no surprise, considering how cold it is outside and that the car engine is not on. Luckily though, El Diablo and Angela brought blankets with them and are wrapped up in them, the best they can, but the cold is still apparent by their red faces and shivering bodies. Of course the car must have frozen up and not be working, as why else would two people stay in a freezing car in the freezing cold for so long? 'Think it'll run yet?' Angela mutters out words, trying not to lose any heat from her mouth. Steam from her voice heads Diablo's way before finally disappearing. 'I don't think so. I think we'll have to wait for help,' Diablo tries to remain optimistic, but is freezing rapidly. Angela sighs, 'But that could be hours!' The Mexican Devil nods slowly, much to Angela's concern. Angela moves closer to her husband so they can share their warmth, and so they can be closer physically anyway. He wraps his arms around her underneath the blanket and plants a kiss on her cheek. Angela smiles, 'You're lucky, you're wearing two t-shirts, a warm top, padded jeans, and a big belt,' Diablo smirks, 'I earned this belt though, this PCW Title has many uses, such as now. If you want, you can wear it a while to get warm,' El Diablo's act of kindness prompts Angela to kiss him passionately on the lips, before removing the blanket, so her husband can place the title around her. Angela is only wearing a tube top and a short skirt, so as El Diablo places the Title around her waist it is a welcome warmth, but surprisingly he doesn't stop there. He takes off his top and slowly puts it on his wife, caressing her body in the process to allow her to get warmer. Angela's face lights up with a smile, and again she plants a loving kiss on the lips of her husband. They then get back under the blanket and snuggle up to each other once more. 'Are you warmer now?' he asks her politely. Angela nods, and to acknowledge that El Diablo kisses her on the cheek. Outside the car, the snow is coming down heavily and large balls of snow are crashing down on the roof of it, and there are no cars near them to try and help. 'This Title really is warm,' exclaims Angela. This prompting a sick smile from El Diablo, which Angela notices and worries her a little. 'I love this Title,' states Diablo caressing the Title under the blanket, 'It's ours, it's yours, it's mine, it's Mexico's! And at Decimation it will legally be ours. We will have it all then Angela, PCW, the Title and respect! Respect from everyone in PCW that is below me, respect from the vermin that come to watch me week after week. This will be a defining moment for us Angela, I couldn't have got this far without you,' Angela smiles and again kisses her husband. 'I love you,' she whispers in his ear. 'I love you too,' he whispers back, 'But, I also love this Title, and I will not give it up,' Angela looks up at him with some concern. 'I won't give up this prestige for anyone or anything! No one can take this Title away from me, simply because I won't allow it,' he rampages on. Angela breathes heavily, in an effort to gain warmth, as more snow piles on the top of the roof of the car. 'What do you mean?' she asks warily. The Lord of Mexico smiles dementedly, and wraps his arms around Angela's stomach, making sure he's touching the Title. 'I mean, I will do anything to keep this Title with us. I don't care what I have to do, just as long as we have this Title. By any means necessary baby, by any means necessary,' Diablo replies with malice in his voice, just at the thought of no longer having the PCW Title in his possession. Angela looks nervous and El Diablo notices this, more clear than the snow blocking all the windows on the car. 'Are you sure? What if you get hurt? You could get injured and your career could be over. We know what Clyde is capable of now, and I don't want anything bad to happen,' she speaks full of worry. Diablo kisses her forehead, 'Nothing will happen, my love. If anything does happen, it will all fall upon Clyde. We will keep this Title Angela, no matter what!' Angela shakes her head in disappointment. 'OK, but I'm just not so sure as you are Raul,' she says softly. El Diablo looks at her, but with worry in his eyes, maybe he doubts Angela. 'Don't you believe I can win?' Diablo asks his wife. Angela freezes on this question for a few moments, but to her rescue comes a knock on the driver's window, which directs El Diablo's attention away from her. He slowly winds the window down to find a Cop staring back at him. 'Need any help sir?' the brown haired Cop asks, now that the snow is ceasing to fall. Diablo looks back at Angela for a moment before turning back to the Cop. 'We could do with a ride actually, I think the battery is dead,' he responds, intent on getting out of this snowy mess. The Cop looks to his car and nods. 'I can take you to the station and arrange for your vehicle to be towed,' the Cop states. Diablo nods as he goes to open his car door. 'Thank you,' Diablo replies.
