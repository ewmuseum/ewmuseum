Who Is Behind Me?

27 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/27/03 Cloaked by the shadows of the tall trees around her, the woman staggers away from them. Her head, constantly looking over her shoulder, to make sure they aren’t following her. When she is satisfied they aren’t, she slows down her pace. Allowing herself to jog and catch her breath. This is the first time in hours that she has stopped. For the entire length of a night’s time she had been running from them, trying to get a way and when they kept coming, she would run faster. It would be dawn soon and with the coming of light, she knew that they would be on to her trail again and her only friend in the world was no where to be found. The question of if they were even friends anymore was still in the for front of her mind. She had let her friend down just a short while ago and did nothing to make up for it. Her eyes causally scan the area to make sure her pursuers were nowhere to be found and at this moment they were. It looks like she out smarted them once again and soon she would be able to return to her newfound lifestyle. She was finding it quite nice to live the high life, even though it really wasn’t her own life. It was more of a stolen one, which she was claiming as her own. She probably wouldn’t be able to hold on to it for much longer if things kept up the way they had been. “I can’t stop for to long,” she whispers, trying to convince herself to keep running. Nothing is there; she can’t seem to move her legs. They have carried her for so long and now, they feel as if they can’t go on anymore. She knows that they are getting closer, but nothing is there. Her motivation is gone and it seems like no one wants her around. “Run you stupid bitch,” she yells out, forcing her legs to move again. Within a matter of seconds, she is once again in a full run. She will be able to avoid them for a while longer now. She knows that she can’t keep this up forever and soon, they would catch her and it would be all over. She didn’t want to let it end like this. When she first started everything seemed so bright and perfect and now it just seemed as if it was fading away. She is glad no one is around to see her like this, her mind is a mess and she is slowly becoming more paranoid by the second. This isn’t how she envisioned things so long ago, but it was the way they were. She had made some bad choices and now was paying dearly for them. No one could help her with this situation, not even her friend. They knew where she was at all times and no matter how fast she tried to get away, it just never seemed to be enough. As she turned out of the park and on to the city streets of LA, she knew she needed to stop soon. Her air supply was running short and she needed to take another break. The sun was almost at full rise now and that meant that it was getting close to 6 AM. Bolting down the street, she passes a few people who pay her no notice and pass her off as another morning jogger. Her eyes rapidly dart from side to side, trying to find some sort of hiding spot. Nothing seems like it will be good enough for her to hide in. Everything is too noticeable, but she needs to rest. Then she spots it, a place no one would think she would be. She picks up the pace, as she gets closer to the high-class apartment building she saw. “No doorman, that is even better,” she thinks, allowing her mind to fade away from the paranoia for a while. Ducking in to the glass doors, she slumps against the polished stonewall behind her. Her eyes look over the lobby that now lies out in front of her. It is just like her friends house, so lavishly put together. The freshly waxed tile floor, that glimmers with the overhead lights. She can’t believe the size, it is so much bigger then anything she has ever seen. It offers her a sense of warmth and belonging. It is the best she can do on such short notice and she hopes they won’t find her here. If she can just stay away from them for a few more days then everything will be all right. She had gotten this far on just stealing and conning people; maybe she could do it for a while longer. **Haley Hilton greeted the warm LA morning by stretching in bed and looking over at the alarm clock that was on the table several feet away. It was only 6:00 AM, but the heat of the day was all ready beginning, as she arose from the ornately design that encompassed the frame. Pushing the silk sheets away from her slender body, she puts her feet into the fuzzy pink slippers that she left next to the bed last night. She slowly pushes herself up off the queen-sized mattress and walks over to the window to the right of the bed and pushes it open. Allowing the morning sun to bleed its red tint into the room. The ocean could be heard in the distance. Waves crashing against the shore, cleaning off the debris and giving the beach a new start on a new today. Taking a deep breath of the fresh air that is starting to fill the small bedroom, as she makes her way towards her vanity counter. She glides gracefully over the hardwood floor below her, like a ballet dancer in the biggest performance of their lives. Once she makes it to her goal on the other side of the room, she quickly pulls the wooden chair out and plops down in it. Her sparkling blue eyes are fixed on the person she sees in the mirror before her. This isn’t the same Haley Hilton that she was a few days ago. She wished things could be like that- before the PWA. Her body is throbbing in pain as she sits staring at the person she has become. The person looking back at her is something she never believed she could become- broken. Reaching for a few of the small glass containers, she begins the daunting process of putting on her make-up. Her body is throbbing in pain through her blue pajamas as she goes through the motions. First lipstick, then blush, and finally eye liner. The eyeliner causes her the most pain, but also brings her the greatest joy when she is done. She covers up her black eye and once again look attractive, at least in her own eyes. Things have been hard for the princess over the past couple of weeks. Taking the beatings day after day, only to wake up and do it again. Her normal pampered life was slowly being taking down, not all at once, but like taking a small piece of a puzzle out and then another- so it can’t be whole any longer. She was wishing that she could go back home and be waited on hand and foot. It couldn’t be like that though; she was living on her own now. She didn’t want that anyways, she was just doing fine on her own. Stupid rich people, always using people and never getting anywhere on their own, I don’t need to live like that. She thought with a smile, twirling her long blonde hair between her fingers. She knows she should do something with it, instead of just letting it hang over her face, but she doesn’t care. I will fix it before I go out with Matt tonight. That is the only time that it matters and I like want to look my best for him because tonight is like going to be so totally great. A smile crosses her tiny lips at the mention of her new boyfriend. He has been the only thing she has been able to turn to as of late. He rescued her from fighting alone in the PWA, just like a knight in shinning armor and now he was helping her break away from the horrors that are her boss Rick. He was doing everything right and trying to put the puzzle back together. She still wasn’t happy about what they were doing. Xavier Kannon was trying to destroy countless peoples jobs and ruin a sport for countless spectators. All the while, Matt was going along with it and helping him in his evil tinkering. She secretly hoped that she was going to be able to talk some sense into them, but it didn’t seem possible. There was no way around helping Matt now either. Last Tuesday was her best chance to get away. Not to say that she didn’t want out, but for some reason she knew she wouldn’t have been able to turn her back on Matt. That is the reason she put her name on that paper and that is the reason she is going to stand by and see him through this. Even with not knowing him long, she was falling in love with him a little more each day. She was starting to hate it, but every waking moment of her day was filled with thoughts of wrestling and it was becoming her life. Haley jumps out of the small wooden chair and back over to the bed. She plops down and stretches herself out on it. Enjoying the almost fully risen sun, she glances over to the clock one more time. The dull red digital numbers read 6:30 and her day hasn’t yet fully started. She lets out a sigh and begins to let her mind wonder. She replays the events on Turmoil over and over, letting them unfold in her mind. Playing like a movie she had never seen, she noticed something new about it, each time she pictured them. Xavier’s reasoning for wanting to bring down the PCW still didn’t make sense to her. Jeremy Howard was just trying to make things better in a time that Kannon turned his back on the organization and the worst part about it was that he was dragging Matt right in with him. What about Jade and Debris? Her mind called out to her, taking her away from her analysis of Turmoil and bringing her thoughts to this weeks Turmoil. She hadn’t given much thought to their match after it was announced. She didn’t know what she was going to do, wrestling had never been her thing and the only reason she was even in the PCW was because Matt needed an assistant. He will protect me, she tells herself, allowing the reassuring tone to relax her a bit. Wrestling was the major downside of Matt and Kannon’s deal. She knew Matt didn’t mind it, but he had done well in the PWA and she hadn’t done anything except take a beating and ice her body when it was over. She was hoping that she could pick a little up over time and use it when she really needed it. As far as she was concerned Matt would be the main one doing the wrestling. I can handle Debris. She is nothing more then a rich little snob. She like sits around all day and does nothing. I will totally show her what being a true princess is like all about. She smiles and stands up from the bed once more and cheerfully bounces out of her bedroom for her morning run. ** Haley saw the lady before she exited her apartment building. She seemed out of place, but Haley couldn’t be too sure. She tries her best to forget about it and continue on her jog. She was wearing the same workout attire she always had. The tight black running shorts and the small tee shirt, it was the perfect outfit to keep her cool, on the hot LA days. The building and people around her were blurring together as she ran by them. Her brain couldn’t stay on one subject for long. Going from her date with Matt, to their mission, to Jade and Debris, and then back to Matt. She knew once she saw him tonight everything would be fine and she would be happy, but right now she had to focus on running. She needs to be in shape for Turmoil because that is going to be the only thing she has going for her. Others before her have done it, but the others before her were trained and ready to step into a ring. Haley was smaller then most and knew nothing about wrestling. Everything would turn out for the best when she saw Matt in the corner cheering her on though. She would do it, she would do it for him.
