A Certain Poignance [Bed Of Spiders] (Part 5 and Final)

Date: March 25th, 2003 I think [ poign·ant adj1. Profoundly moving; touching: a poignant memory.] A W A K E Many stars shine. The water flows. The shore is golden. The sky is blue. The grass is green. The world is beautiful. Eddy was in his bed, dreaming the thoughts of nature and beauty. A poignant beauty so strong and painful it was loving as well as hateful. Maybe it was the feeling that he'd not have the chance to experience a beauty ever, ever again so strong and vivid and he'd never have the experience again. There was many reasons why beauty held it's traits like it did. I will lay where I fall.Littles matters anymore.No need to fight the tide.All that I need is inside. Eddy was in an insomniac mood. He tried to sleep but his mind drifted off into many things. The ceiling fan clicked and clacked as in spun continuously in circles with an annoying beat that could cause anybody to not sleep. But that wasn't the problem. Petrified. Never did Eddy want to feel as vulnerable as he did now. He felt anybody could jump through his window and massacre him, but that clearly wasn't the case. He felt so open to pain he could barely realize. He didn't know how to feel, and had so much confusion towards Earth. Lost Eddy stood up to get water. He stood up from his bed, causing a clump of the bed to wad and become messy but he didn't notice. The carpet caused static cling as he touched the doorknob and walked out of his room and into the kitchen. He grabbed a see through green plastic cup and filled it with water from the tap to the top. It wasn't cold, but he didn't care. He started to head to the restroom and sat his water on the sink counter before looking straight into the medicine cabinet. He looked beaten and worn out, for sure. He eyes had bags under them but they looked awake and alert. He never felt so inhumane in his life. Paranoia He walked back into his room to try and get sleep, but he couldn't. He didn't know why he was in the mood he was in, but he was. He tried to calm his mind with serene thoughts but couldn't. F L A S H B A C K 'I hope you get that bastard to pay for the shit he did. That fucking racist asshole...He judges everybody on the skin that protects them from pain. Without skin, everything we'd touch would hurt like hell. It's just fucking bullshit we have to be judged by looking at what protects us from being hurt', his father said in a more stronger voice, almost raising to a bigger climax with each word. 'I will dad. I agree with you', said Eddy, almost baffled and confused. His dad then reached up and tugged on Eddy's shirt hard, almost choking Eddy. His father had a fracture left wrist, and he was using his right. But his dad held with so much agression is was shocking. Eddy could feel his green sweater stretching and his father tugged. 'I mean it son. Make him pay for what he did. Make him feel miserable for what he did to me...actually, fuck what he did to me...Make him pay for what he does to everyone else who isn't white. I'm an iota of importance, so forget I said anything about me. But make him pay. Do it son, please...For the sake of everyone.' 'Okay Dad...', said Eddy really confused at this point. His father didn't let go, but loosened his grip. 'But dad, I'm fighting Skitzo this week for the Extreme title...Makonie isn't of importance.' His dad's eyes bulged. Eddy was startled. 'Eddy...Prevail over Skitzo, and I have to tell you something...Makonie will come. He's gonna run in and attack you like the asshole he is. He's going to fight you and try to make you lose. Know that. But Eddy, if he does this, take all that Extreme weaponry use and use it. Lift up your fucking chair and clock him over the head with it as hard as you can hit.' 'What if Skitzo blitzed me with his 20 dildo?'. Eddy felt stupid asking that. 'Look out for him too. Beat Skitzo, but beat Makonie too.' His father let go of Eddy's shirt and laid to rest. 'But dad...I don't want to attack Makonie if I don't need to...' 'Don't NEED TO?!!! You think that Makonie curbs all these African Americans, insults Latinos, tease Asians and doesn't DESERVE to get his ass kicked?' 'Well...That's true.' 'Once you hold that Extreme title, Makonie is going to attack you. But don't let this stop you. Fight. Kick his ass...Kick that bastard Skitzo's ass and win that God damned title. Holding that title is like telling Makonie 'Haha, I'm Hispanic and I hold a title unlike your sorry ass'. That's all the proof you need to tell him he's a bastard for being to bigot he is...His bigotry is not the nicest thing, and prove to him any race can do just as well as any other race', his father said. 'Okay Dad...' 'Hold that title forever son. Don't let it go. It's yours. You earned it. Beat that sorry ass guy named Skitzo, and kick his ass, and kick whatever 10 alter ego's he has.' Eddy nodded. 'I'll try.' 'That's what I like to hear, son...You can leave now. Train hard and long.' A W A K E What do you fight for? What do you fight for Eddy? Glory? Inspiration? Fairness? Unity? Eddy sat there in a pile of his own thoughts, wondering answer to his own question. 'For......' Do you have a reason? That's when it hit Eddy. What was he trying to do? Eddy was inert. Stationary. His facial expression didn't change. He was a body lying still on the ground of some room, staring face down on his bed. Like in the book 'Johnny Got His Gun', a soldier loses all his limbs in a land mine and is paralyzed completely (except his neck) and is only a torso. He loses every sense: Smell, sight, hearing, taste...But was left with feeling and pain, but didn't even have the fingers to touch. He had no point to live but was living. He tried to tell the doctors in morse corse by tapping his neck on his pillow he wanted to be killed, but legally, they couldn't do that, so the soldier was faced with this life. Eddy was in this feeling now. Eddy hoped it was tenmporary, but it was so intense he didn't know what to understand. This was a very odd night for Eddy. He wanted to understand everything, but was faced with the fact he couldn't. It was heartbreaking. Lord, save me. Give me clarity. In the end I may be nothing. In the end I may mean nothing. In the end there may be nothing. Oh God, I am so overwhelmed in emotions I don't know where to start... Eddy then realized something. He went down to visit the Indian areas to understand the culture, and was very curious. The respect for nature, the respect for everything, to good use of everything...Eddy respected it. Eddy then stood up, grabbed a coat that wasn't visible in the dark room. Tossing it on and grabbing keys from his nightstand, he looked outside at the creek that ran behind his house. It was tranquil and glimmering with light of the moon and stars. Eddy divulged in the beauty, and turned with one glance, and walked outside.
