A Turmoilic Time

11 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th October 2002 We go backstage at Tuesday Turmoil a couple of nights ago, in the Asylum Locker Room. I just wanted people to note that this isn’t in the ring. Thus it’s not an in-ring promo, thank the lords. It’s after the show, and the full evening is over with people getting ready to leave. Nerezza has already gone to the car leaving Gaz and Miyagawa standing in the dressing room. Miyagawa is putting some stuff away in a kitbag on one side of the room while Gaz is doing the same on the other. Gaz: So Anarchy is out then? Miyagawa silently bobs his head to a yes. Gaz: I guess we’ll be looking for a replacement for our Alliances team. I mean we gotta keep that one. Miyagawa grins for a second as he remembers last year’s events. Miyagawa: Just hopefully the replacement won’t stab us all in the back immediately afterwards and try to take the world title shot. Gaz: Huh? Miyagawa: Last year at Alliances, Timo tried to take Jeremy Howard’s World Title shot even though we had agreed that Timo was to get the TV title shot. Gaz: So what did you get? Miyagawa: An Extreme Title shot. Which I didn’t get till the last week in December. And Badge ran away too so I didn’t even win the title. Gaz grins. Gaz: So who gets the Extreme Title shot this year? Miyagawa: JJ if we’re that freaking despirate. But first things first… I have got to beat Frisco on Friday and then see if I can bring anything around for the Continental Title on the week after at the Mall Brawl. I came second last year. Gaz nods and looks curious. Gaz: Oh right, who to then? Miyagawa snorts. Miyagawa: Frisco. Which is why I just must beat him just once on Fusion. I’ve never pinned the guy, and he’s beaten me far too many times. He’s like my total anti-opponent. I have this theory… everyone has a single opponent that they cannot beat. I think mine is Frisco. Gaz: But if that was right then there’s no way you could win on Fusion. Miyagawa: Well, I’m not good at theories. Gaz laughs. Gaz: You could say that. Gene Roddenberry and Walt Disney don’t share a fridge. Miyagawa: I’m never gonna live that down, am I? Gaz: Nope, you sure aren’t. Miyagawa finishes up packing and slings the bag over his shoulder and goes to the door. Gaz looks like he suddenly remembers something. Gaz: Natalia was here, I just remembered I was to tell you. Miyagawa turns around. Miyagawa: She’s here? That’s weird, she usually calls me when she turns up at shows… ever since the whole Boda thing during the summer. **pauses for a few seconds** I never saw her. Gaz: Well I only saw her the once, she asked where you were and we told her that you went to get a drink. Didn’t she her again. Miyagawa raises an eyebrow. Miyagawa: Did you tell her where the machines were? Gaz: Yep. Miyagawa: That’s just strange. I was down there talking to Anarchy, got a Pepsi, signed some photos for a crewmember’s kid and then came back here where I’ve been since. Miyagawa shrugs. Miyagawa: She’s probably gone back to the hotel I expect. Probably to check on the nanny. Gaz: You guys have a nanny? Miyagawa: Yeah, gives Natalia some free time. Gaz: To do what? Miyagawa: She has hobbies. Not sure what they are, but she has them. And with that, we depart our little scene as Alexander Miyagawa heads off back to the hotel to see if he can find his wife there.
