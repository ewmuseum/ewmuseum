Semore meets THE REAL Jay and Silent Bob!!

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 24, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up into another sunny day that Semore Smoke has become accustomed to up north. The weather is that of a mild day, not too hot and not too cold. Compared to the weather that he’s been having in his hometown for about the last month it’s a lot cooler though. The scene is just about the same as yesterdays, it’s in the outdoors area surrounded by a lot of noise of car horns beeping and people yelling, you can even hear the distant sound of a siren wailing in the air. The cars are speeding by on the road as usual, and the people are shoving others left and right, and there are small tourist groups walking down each side of the road. Semore walks out from the ally, which he had walked into earlier that day. He looks both ways and then turns towards his right and then heads up the sidewalk. Dodging people as they run back and forth on the sidewalk, he even sees a man steal a woman’s purse and then he runs past her and right past Smoke. The woman is screaming as the robber runs down the sidewalk and gets away with her purse. Semore tries to avoid the yelling woman as she’s starting to kick and punch at people who could have stopped the robber while he ran past them. Smoke is able to avoid the woman as she is busy with one man who is much bigger the Smoke who could have stopped the robber with one swift punch of his fist. Semore is low on weed though, of course he was able to smoke the blunts that are free at the club. But he isn’t allowed to take any of the weed outside the club itself. So he’s looking for the nearest store that would have any sellers. Smoke as a former seller himself realizes that all Quick Stop convenient stores have sellers who stand in front of them during the day, and Semore had seen a Quick Stop only a block away from where he was. Semore turns the corner when he gets to the intersection and heads towards the right again. He then walks about twenty feet, dodging people again as he walks and he then sees the convenient store he had seen before. He turns towards his right again and walks up to the convenient store, there standing in front of it, is a slim guy with long blonde hair wearing a yellow overcoat, underneath he’s wearing standard street clothes. Standing beside him is a somewhat shorter and pudgy fellow with long brown hair, he’s got somewhat of a bushy brown hair beard he’s wearing a greenish overcoat. Smoke looks around and walks up to the two standing there and says… Semore Smoke: Yo, I was wondering if you guys wouldn’t happen to have any stuff on you. {{{{The blonde haired guy looks around and then at Smoke and says…}}}} Blonde Haired: Yo, yeah my name is mother f*uckin’ Jay, this is my bitch Silent Bob. We’ve got some sh!t for you. How much are you willing to pay for it, and how much do you want? {{{{Silent Bob just sits there and nods as Jay says his spill. Semore nods and smiles as well…}}}} Semore Smoke: Well, I’ve got as much money as I need. I just want a quarter though, enough to get me through two days. Jay: All right dude. I’ve got the shi!t you need, better fork over the money though, it’ll cost you a hundred f*uckin’ dollars. {{{{Smoke pulls out his wallet and then pulls a crisp hundred out of it and hands it to Jay. Jay takes the bill and then looks around and then hands Smoke the quarter, which he pulls out of his pocket, all ready wrapped. Then Jay looks at Semore with a confused look on his face and thinks for a few minutes. Semore does the same thing towards Jay then they both come up with the same thing to say…}}}} Semore Smoke & Jay: Hey, wait a minute, I know you! {{{{They both start to chuckle as they realized they said it at the same time. Jay then points at Semore to go first and Semore does…}}}} Semore Smoke: Aren’t you two the same Jay and Silent Bob from movies like Dogma and Jay and Silent Bob Strike Back? {{{{Jay and Silent Bob both look down at the ground as Smoke says this and then Silent Bob speaks up as he looks up at Smoke…}}}} Silent Bob: Actually it isn’t. That idiot, Kevin Smith stole the idea of Jay and Silent Bob from us and copied us into characters of his own. He ripped us off pretty much. Semore Smoke: Oh, sorry to hear that, but you guys are like famous though. So, what were you going to say Jay? {{{{Jay actually looks like he’s about to laugh now, might be the reefer he’s been smoking that day or something…}}}} Jay: Ha! You are him you’re that Semore Smoke guy, I saw you on Premium Blend Friday night and you’re also a wrestler for PCW! I know you dude! You’re the funniest guy I know! {{{{Smoke nods with a smile and then says…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yeah, that’s me. I didn’t realize so many people watched wrestling, I knew it ad a vast fan base, but I didn’t realize so many potheads watched wrestling. {{{{Jay starts to chuckle again…}}}} Jay: Yeah, you’re like our hero man! You openly smoke weed and you get paid to do it on TV! You do what all of us “potheads” as you call us want to do in our wildest dreams! The last people to have done that were Cheech and Chong! {{{{Silent Bob continues to nod his head as Jay talks. Semore is just smiling, and of course is blushing as well…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yeah, Cheech and Chong are like my heroes to me. I never knew I was looked upon as a hero to potheads in the world. How about this, if you guys show up tomorrow, I’ll give you two free tickets to see the wrestling show that’s in town. {{{{Jay and Silent Bob both are surprised and happy, they look at each other with surprised looks and then at Semore and both nod without a word. Smoke puts the weed that he had been holding in his hand the whole time into his pocket and then turns to look behind him and then back at them again…}}}} Semore Smoke: I better get going, I’ll look for you guys being there, this will be awesome! {{{{Jay and Silent Bob just stand there silent, which isn’t odd for Bob, but for Jay it is. Smoke turns and walks away from the convenient store with his weed in his pocket and a chip on his shoulder. Now it’s time to head for the arena to get ready for his match the next day for the Rising Star championship…}}}}
