Bob Vurdo? Fine, but first CUTTING A PROMO AGAINST PLAYAZ INC!

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/4/02 A red oriental carpet covers the floor, with two tan leather loveseats set opposite each other though facing the camera. Seated upon the right chair is the stereotypical ghetto black man: wearing black Enyce sweatshirt, black Levi's jeans, and brown Lugz. He's got a goatee, dark skin, shaved head with a black do-rag over his head. Wears sunglasses over his eyes inside, a band-aid over his face, chewing on a toothpick. The white man has his hair slicked back in a standard 'Geek cut.' He's wearing a tight (though not flattering his atrophied physique) white shirt, black pants, and black dress shoes. He's got black bottle-frame glasses over his face pasty white face, and... A POCKET PROTECTOR! The two men are played up with neutral Latin jazz, and the white man talks to the camera to start up the show as the music fades out. White Man:'Welcome. Neither me nor my cohost are much for introductions so let's get right down to it.' THE TOP 20 THINGS THAT SCARE WHITE PEOPLE 20) Affirmative Action Black Man:'Whether it's picking crops, or cleaning rooves, white people don't like affirmative action. Most of the time, all it guarantees black people is a low-level service job with no possibility of advancement and the ability to walk into a country club with OUR OWN set of clubs, but even THAT seems to be too much for white people.' White Man:'We established those country clubs, and even though we don't want those jobs we don't want you to have them either!' 19) Denzel Washington He's a strong black African American leader, a role-model for others to follow in his footsteps. His movie roles scare white people. White Man:'He went against the white establishment in Glory. Can't have that. Black Man:'The f*cking wanted shoes!' White Man:'Black people went barefoot in the jungles of Africa, they can go back. In fact, why don't you all just go back to Africa?' Black Man:'It's your fault we're here in the first place, jackass! Tell ya what, we'll both go back to where we came from. I'll learn how to hunt elephants, and you get used to Blood Pudding and women with armpit hair.' White Man:'.... Or especially Denzel Washington in Training Day. Scary as all heck!' Black Man:'Nah, you're probably more scared of him from Pelican Brief. He was close to Julia Roberts's tits a helluva lot.' 18) Hip Hop White Man:'It's giving opportunities to inner city people to express themselves and make money, and I don't like it.' Black Man:'Man, you're disturbed.' 17) UPN Tuesday Nights White Man:'I can't channel surf UPN without fear of getting all-black programming, especially on Tuesdays! You people watch TV anyway, why do you have to see black people?' Black Man:'Why do you have to see white people? Ass...' 16) Oprah White Man:'She's one of the 500 richest people in the country, and that's not good. Not only that, but she's a major influence on white women and I'm worried about a Lysistrata situation.' Black Man:'You talkin' about my mama? White Man: *Shakes head*'Not to mention that she's head of the National Book Mafia.' 15) Incarcerated Black Men White Men:'This speaks for itself. They're scary, are in for some serious offenses, and they want to take our white women!' Black Man:'Seriously, dawg, there's something wrong with yo' head.' 14) Non-incarcerated Black Men Black Man:'Don't even say it...' 13) Basketball White Man:'It used to be clean fun for white kids until YOU PEOPLE took it over.' Black Man:'If ya'll ain't no good at the game YOU INVENTED, ain't that YO' FAULT?' 12) Anything considered 'ghetto.' Black Man:'It's an expression. The ghetto, yeah, dat's a scary place for EVERYBODY. But for something to be ghetto just means it's... um, of poor quality like you white people say.' White Man:'EXACTLY. White people aren't used to poor quality anything. We eat Oreos, you eat Hydrox. We have pork chops, you have pig knuckles. We have gated suburban communities, you have one-room rent-controlled properties.' 11) Black people behind white people in the ATM line at 11PM. White Man:'YOU CAN'T TELL ME THIS DOESN'T SCARE WHITE PEOPLE!' Black Man:'Even I'm a little uppity about anotha' brotha' behind me at the ATM.' 10) BET Black Man:'It's owned by white people now.' White Man:'Still, it's all about encouraging black people and rap and hip hop. Scary stuff.' Black Man:'Damn...' 9)Black people trying to buy guns White Man:'There would be no drive-by shootings if black people didn't have guns. And YES, I'm counting the Armed Forces.' Black Man:'White people have guns and are scary too.' White Man:'But white people can use guns prudently.' Black Man:'COLUMBINE.' White Man: *Shakes fist*'Damn you!' 8) Black People who already have guns. White Man: *Nods* Black Man: *Flips White Man off* 7) P. Diddy Black Man:'Whatchu got against P. Diddy?' White Man:'Crossover appeal, having sex with many women I CAN'T EVER HAVE, Jennifer Lopez, Dream, Making the Band 2 COME ON!' 6) Mike Tyson Black Man:'I can understand that.' White Man:'If he wouldn't talk, he'd be public enemy #1. You can also put O.J. Simpson in this slot if you're a 36-year-old white female.' Black Man:'That was just wrong...' 5) Louis Farrakhan Black Man:'Huh? Black men in power always scare you white people. You betta hope that assassinations and presidential succession don't lead to Colin Powell in the Executive chair.' White Man:'Colin Powell hasn't been black since he was 20.' Black Man:'YOU TAKE THAT BACK!' 4) The Source Awards White Man:'Who knows what evil, devious activities your black people are up to on your own awards show.' Black Man:'All we're doing is giving India.Arie an award for her hard work when ya'll Grammy bitches be givin' it to some 70-ye'old country cracka' who speaks mo' messed up English then Master P! I couldn't understand a single fucking word comin' out of his mouth.' White Man:'You listen to his music?' Black Man:'No. You?' White Man:'I'm more Tony Bennett myself.' 3) Large groups of black people. White Man:'That just creeps me out.' Black Man:'Look, if we happen to be there, we're there. You're only lookin' like an ass to skip the movie or drive another 15 miles for gas because there's 5 or more of us hangin' around.' White Man:'Why can't you get a job and stop hanging around?' Black Man:'How come ya'll white people don't say that to Tony Hawk?' 2) LARGER groups of black people. White Man:'This is even worse.' Black Man:'I'll bet.' AND THE #1 THING THAT SCARES WHITE PEOPLE) The Million Man March White Man:'It was led by blacks and heavily populated by blacks. And where they were... too close to the White House. Scary stuff, they could've taken over the White House. Then there'd be corruption and incompetence... nevermind.' Black Man:'Haha.' THE END (Join us next time for the Top 20 Things That Scare Black People!)
