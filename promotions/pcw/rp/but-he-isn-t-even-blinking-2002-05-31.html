But He Isn't Even BLINKING!!!

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31/05/02 Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Hall have always been a high profile couple. Many valets just walk to the ring with their “Beau”.... Posing for the cameras and shouting the odd word of encouragement... They are the eye candy... Not really there for much purpose other than to look vacuous and maybe mutter the occasional word in a promo. Eleanor Hall is different.... While most valets enjoy their TV time and moment in the spotlight... She craves it.... Even to the point of now having wrestling matches.... But now she is more than merely a valet. On Turmoil, after a sickening tribute to the acts she has carried out to help him, Xavier proposed to the Prep and in her own Preppy way, she answered Like..... SO YES!!! However, their moment of happiness did not last long as some of Kannon’s actions came back to haunt him... It was he who ended Asylum... It was he who drove Jeremy Howard from PCW... And Asylum member and Howard’s best friend Miyagawa had something to say about it. With Kannon’s happy day ruined, the Scientologist and the wacky Japo-American will both be placed in the same School at Fusion with the first one to escape the grounds declared Extreme Champion.... But as we know, Kannon doesn’t take defeat of any kind well, and in their war of words Miyagawa won a convincing victory... ....How will the Gold Patron Meritorious deal with it.... Or would he even deal with it at all? “HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!” We are only shown a Black screen, as a familiar voice bellows out breathless laughter, only pausing to gasp more air into the lungs to continue. “HA HA HAHAHAHAHA HAHAHA HAHA!!!!” A wheezing gasp of breath is taken in followed by more laughter, the sound of the lungs nearly emptying themselves of all air heard at the end of it as it becomes almost a whisper. “HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!” Our shot starts to fade in on the Red face of the usually pale Xavier Kannon, he has just finished another bout of laughter and is holding his ribs, gasping to get air back into he lungs. Xavier : “Oh Hubbard..... That is the funniest thing I have ever seen!” We fade out to see him lying in the bed of his Greenwich Village apartment with a remote control in his hands. The Scientologist searches in the poor light to find the rewind button then rolls the tape back then hits play. “Just $5 a month will help supply a village with clean drinking wat-” Xavier : “HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA.... Oh, it just gets better!!!” “-er and allow them to grow their own crops...” Xavier : “THERE IS A FLY...... CRAWLING OVER HIS EYE!!!.... And he isn’t even blinking! HAHAHAHAHA.... He isn’t BLINKING!!! HAHA!!! ELLIE~! you gotta see this!!!” Out of the bathrooms comes his valet, Eleanor Hall who is towel drying her long, wavy Brown hair. All she wears is a towel around her, and her face, even without make-up is stunning. Eleanor : “Um..... Like, what are you laughing at?” Kannon turns excitedly to her, gesturing with the remote to the flat screen, wall mounted TV on the wall opposite where he is laying in bed and she puts her hands on her hips, waiting to see what the commotion is all about. He rewinds and hits play at the point where he last watches it from... Xavier : “Better hold on to that towel, this will have your ribs aching...” “Just $5 a month will help supply a village with clean drinking water and allow them to grow their own crops...” Xavier : “HAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! LOOK.... THAT KID HAS A FLY.... “ The Gold Patron Meritorious takes a gasp of air as Ellie~! looks at him, worried... Xavier : “WALKING ALL OVER HIS EYE!!!! HIS EYE!!! AND HE ISN’T EVEN BLINKING!!!!” The look on Ellie~!’s face can only be described as anti-climactic... As if she was hoping for a little bit more than a fly, an eye and the lack of blinking. Eleanor : “That’s it? He doesn’t like, stumble around and step on a land mine or anything?” First of all Xavier looks puzzled that Eleanor isn’t laughing her head off like him, but then thinks about it... Xavier : “I don’t know..... I’ve never played it past the.... He sniggers and blurts it out through his laughter. Xavier : “FLY ON THE EYE!!!” The Prep shakes her head and rolls her pale Blue eyes, looking at the screen as Kannon un-pauses it and they both watch the rest of the aid commercial, neither seeing what they wanted. Eleanor : “Okay, so there is no kid stumbling onto a land mine and getting blown into thousands of bits..... So all it has i-” Before she can get her observation out, the Scientologist has rewound the tape and is watching his favourite bit. Xavier : “A FLY CRAWLING OVER HIS EYE BALL.....AND HE DOESN’T BLINK!!!“ The valet perches on the edge of the bed and look to her man, worried. Eleanor : “Is it really that funny.... Or are you just trying to put what Miyagawa did to the back of your mind?” The bright colour fades from his cheeks and the smile drops from his mouth... His eyes that were tear soaked from the heavy laughter narrow, and his entire face contorts into one of abject disgust. Xavier : “This has NOTHING to do with Miyagawa.... He doesn’t matter to me.... It was just a VERY funny commercial because the Fly crawled over his eye and HE......... DIDN’T........ BLINK!” As usual, his valet sees right through him and doesn’t cease in her quest to find out why he finds it so funny. Eleanor : “Are you sure.... He did verbally bitch slap you on Tuesday, then he mocked you, then he got the last word in and left you speechless.... Then suddenly you find something that is mildly funny at best and think it is hilario-” The seething Scientologist snaps, slamming the remote down onto the bed so it bounces a full 3 feet back onto the air and Ellie~! hops off the side of the bed. Xavier : “IT WAS FUNNY! THE FLY WAS CRAWLING ACROSS HIS EYE AND HE DIDN’T EVEN BLINK!!!” The Prep shakes her head. Eleanor : “Like..... What... EVER!!! This isn’t good Xavi, you have to fight, like, 10...Or was it 15...” She pauses for thought for a second as Kannon slowly turns his angry face to his fiancé. Eleanor : “Well, let’s say A LOT of people on Friday and that tape will not help you win the Extreme Title... Even if he doesn’t blink... You should be harnessing your hate for Miyagawa, it should be fuelling you to win the Extreme Title.... All that frustration at him getting one over on you every sentence... Use it...” As she goes on and on in her whiny, preppy voice he gradually gets more and more angry at the mentioning of how Miyagawa got one over on him at Turmoil, ruining his engagement and snaps. Xavier : “DAMMIT THIS IS NOT BECAUSE OF MIYAGAWA! IT HAS NOTHING TO DO WITH HIM RUINING THE BEST DAY OF MY LIFE AND INSULTING BOTH ME AND YOU, MY FIANCÉ... IT HAS NOTHING TO DO WITH HIM SAYING I WAS TALKING SHIT OR STEALING MY CATCHPHRASE....IT IS BECAUSE THE FLY WAS CRAWLING ACROSS HIS EYE AND HE DIDN’T BLINK!!!!“ He angrily grabs the remote control and rewinds, showing a young African boy sitting motionless as a fly crosses his eyeball, seemingly the boy doesn’t notice, or really care that much. Xavier : “HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! Look, he nearly has one on each eyeball! HAHAHA!!! Who would want to listen to Miyagawa run his mouth at me and insult you when they could see that!!! Look... HE ISN’T EVEN BLINKING!!!!“ As Kannon again laughs heartily his valet despairs, muttering to herself under her breath. Eleanor : “Not denial again....” She shakes her head and wanders back into the bathroom, still talking to herself. Eleanor : “I hate it when he gets like this.... There’s no talking to him...” She looks at herself in the bathroom cabinet mirror before smiling in satisfaction at what she sees and reaches for her tooth brush when Xavier booming laugh starts up again. “HAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!! HE STILL ISN’T BLINKING!!!!” The Prep sighs and shakes her head as the shot begins to fade out. Eleanor : “Looks like I’ll have to find a way of giving him an electric shock again....” As she rolls her eyes and wonders how to snap the temperamental Scientologist out of it we hit Black. “HAHAHAHAHA!!! THAT FLY IS WALKING ACROSS HIS EYE!!!”
