Blitzo 2: Bloodhunt - Introduction

24 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24-10-02 A cold and dark night has befallen the city of beautiful Prague. The scene settles on an external view of a local community blood bank. As a tram passes by, a young, rough looking man walks into view, rubbing his hands together to garner some form of heat, before walking across the street into the blood bank. Inside, the sounds of coughing and spluttering fill the air as many of the donors inside wait to give blood. The pale, rough man enters the building and nods to a security guard at the front, before taking a ticket from the little ticket machine. Looking at the big, bold and black “#76” on the ticket, the man scoffs as the machine overhead reads “#31”, before walking across to the waiting room and sitting next to a rather sickly looking man. Gazing around the same old drab, run-down and flickering building, he turns his attention to the man next to him; clad in a ragged black outfit, with silver-grey fingerless gloves, his hood covering most of his face, but what the man notices is a long, thin scar running down his chin. Rough Man: Hey, you know they pay for blood here! There’s no limit to how many times you give, hell they even buy it in the jar! The sickly man coughs into his fist again before nodding reassuringly at the rough man. As #32 appears on the ticket reminder box overhead, a young looking woman enters the room carrying a clipboard. Her facial expression is that of stern, strictness, as she calls out into the waiting room. Clipboard Woman: JARED HËSSAK! The sickly man coughs again and stands up, nodding at the woman. Nodding again with a little smile on his face, the man known as Jared Hëssak walks over to the woman as she turns and takes off through a door behind her. Walking alongside her, the rather large man coughs again as they make their way down a long, dark hallway. Clipboard Woman: Have you recently visited a tropical country? Hëssak: No… Clipboard Woman: In the past twelve months have you gotten a tattoo, non-acupuncture, or undergone any ear, skin or body piercing? Hëssak: No… Clipboard Woman: How did you get that scar on your chin-… Hëssak: Childhood accident… The woman with the clipboard walks through an open door and down another hallway; Hëssak coughs again trying to keep up with her as they pass many medical supplies lining the hallway, from centrifuges to aphaeresis devices, and so forth. Clipboard Woman: I see from your questionnaire that you don't have any immediate next of kin? Hëssak: Not that I'm in contact with… Clipboard Woman: Nobody to call in case of an emergency? Hëssak: I told you…No…wait, does that mean I can't be a donor? Clipboard Woman: It depends. You see we came up with some unusual results on your blood test… Hëssak follows the woman to a steel door were two security guards await them. Both look a little bored, paying little attention to the monitor, which offers a view of the examining room beyond. There is also a small window with safety glass. One of the guards opens the door, following Hëssak and the woman with the clipboard inside. The other guard remains behind, manning the hallway. Inside the woman with the clipboard ushers Hëssak into the room, indicating for him to sit in a kind of reclining dental chair with arm and headrests. Hëssak notices a security camera mounted above the door as he enters. Hëssak: ………How unusual? Without responding, the woman places the clipboard with Hëssak’s details on it aside and stands before the chair, clearing her throat as she The Nurse sets aside Hëssak's file, looking uncomfortable. Woman: It seems your blood has a very rare phenotype, one that's quite valuable to people like us… Hëssak: Us? What are you talking about? As Hëssak replies, a kind-faced doctor enters the room, nodding to the guard with a kind of twinkle in his eye. Doctor: It's a good news-bad news scenario, Jared...Good news for us, bad for you! The Doctor and woman smile, as the woman pulls up some kind of blood extraction machine, inserting her fingers into what looks like a medical version of Freddy Krueger’s claw, but with needles instead of blades. The woman, doctor and guard laugh, suddenly baring their fangs. The realisation suddenly hits that these are vampires, while Hëssak sits it the chair looking rather frightened. Hëssak: What the-… Jared Hëssak is cut off suddenly as the guard grabs him around the throat, forcing him into the chair as the woman advances on him with the needle glove, all three vampires laughing out as Hëssak whimpers in the chair. As the Guard restrains Hëssak, his hand brushes against Hëssak's jaw, the flesh of which briefly separates along the scar, almost as if it were a seam. As the woman prepares to administer the needles, Hëssak begins to laugh! The vampires seem confused at this reaction from their victim, until Hëssak starts to shake and twitch, as though he is having a fit. Suddenly the whites of his eyes bleed red, and Hëssak throws his head back, laughing out as the scar seems to separate on his chin…until a pair of larger, much more deadlier looking fangs than the vampires have extrude from his gums, and with a large, evil laugh, Hëssak lashes out, knocking the doctor off his feet! The woman screams as Hëssak jumps out of the chair and grabs her, forcing her against the wall and biting into her neck, a shower of blood glazing the wall. The security guard rushes Hëssak, only to be thrown into the wall and buried under the debris that falls. The doctor scrambles to his feet and tries to exit the room…until Hëssak turns and with one swift swing, grabs him by the throat and slams him up against the wall, just under the security camera. Screams suddenly echoes throughout the room, and outside the other security guard tries to get in, only to find the door locked. Running for the security office, the guard looks at the camera to see Hëssak massaging the doctor’s face, his hood suddenly falling back to reveal his bald head, consumed by veins and a sickly yellow skin, his eyes glowing a red evil. The security guard gasps as Hëssak looks up at the security camera, and smiles sinisterly, the scar on his chin continuing to apparently spasm. Hëssak: Vampires……… Hëssak looks away from the camera for a moment and stares into the doctor’s horrified eyes, before stroking his head and grinning sadistically at his face. Hëssak: ………I hate vampires… The doctor screams out as Hëssak smiles at him, and the scar on his chin separates fully, his “jaws” widening back fully to his ears to reveal something beyond petrifying closing in on the doctor’s face and his soon-to-be-over life… ------------------------------ Blitzo Voiceover: Forget what you think you know…Vampires exist. My name is Blitzo…I was born half-human…half-vampire. They call me the “Daywalker”. I have all their strengths, none of their weaknesses...except for the thirst. 20 years ago I met a man that changed that…Astler. He taught me how to hold the thirst at bay, taught me the rules and gave me the weapons to hunt with: Silver, Garlic…Sunlight! 2 years ago he was attacked, they took him and turned him into the thing I hate most…I should have finished him off, now I’m hunting him…I will find him, and nothing with stand in my way! Blitzo: You realise of course you’re only going to spend the first five minutes of the film doing what you said there and spend the other god-knows-how-long stopping the bad guys in the MAIN PLOT! Blitzo: Shut up this is for atmosphere… Blitzo: Ok so… BLITZO 2: BLOODHUNT ______________________ Starring: Skitzo……………………………………………………………..………………BlitzoAsa Fountain……………………………………………………..……………….AstlerSemore Smoke……………………………………………………………Semore ScudHessian………………………………………………………….……………….HëssakCharlie Loc……………………………………………………..……………Nyssa LocNerezza………………………………………………………….………...DamaskinezzGaz “The Jester” Green………………………………………….………………..GazadRush Mason……………………………………………………….………….RushhardtAngelo Deville………………………………...…………………..…………ChupangeloJason Marah………………………..…………………………...The Irish VampirechaunMarvin Harris……………………………………………………..……………..GasmanTempest…………………………….……….…………………………………TempaineIvan Stanislav………….…..………………………………………………SicklehammerThe Fan………………………………………….……………………….Fat Fanpire #1Joey Flash…………………………………….………………………………Joey FlashD-Kay Calyx……………….……………………………………………...D-Kay Calyx ------------------------------ BOOM! A second-story door flies open and five obvious vampires run out, their fangs protruding from their gums and their hissing echoing through the night. They race along an alleyway; scared shitless from whatever is chasing them. As they speed along, a dark figure appears from around the corner, with a Mach pistol in hand. Looking down another corner, the smooth, intimidating face of none other than Blitzo shines in the streetlight, before he continues chasing the five vampires along the street. As Fat Fanpire #1 passes two nooks, he calls out. Fat Fanpire #1: IT’S BLITZO! GET HIM! GET HIM! From the nooks, two leather-clad vampires emerge and aim their weapons at Blitzo, who immediately disintegrates the vampires with the silver-tipped bullets from his Mach. Looking up at the overhead scaffolding, Blitzo suddenly leaps freakishly high, disappearing from the vampires sight and running along the scaffolding as two of them stop at the end while the other three sprint off to the left. Mumbling between themselves, they tremble looking for him in the silence…until the black cape-wearing hero drops down from above and fires the Mach, turning the vampires to ashes in a matter of seconds. Turning to the left, Blitzo catches the three vampires standing at the end of a hallway on a balcony. Fat Fanpire #1 looks down the two-storey escape ladder to the ground, and slams a fist onto the railing. Fat Fanpire #1: FUCK THIS SHIT! Without wasting another second, the first vampire slides down and drops off at the bottom, running away into the distance. Fat Fanpire #1 follows, tripping up on the bottom rung and fumbling to his feet and running off for a red R1200 motorbike as the other vampire drives off in a tricked-out Panhead Harley chopper with ape-hanger handlebars. The first vampire has taken off, starting up and driving off as the third vampire slides down the ladder, his skin burning from the friction as he slides. Eventually succumbing to the pain, the vampire dives off and lands on his back, immediately jumping up and running off after Fat Fanpire #1 and the other vampire. As he runs after them in desperation, on the second storey Blitzo charges along the alley and straight for the balcony. As he reaches the balcony, Blitzo leaps into the air over the railing and dives down the two-storeys to the ground, flipping majestically in mid-air and landing in a cat-like stance, immediately drawing the mach pistol and firing three bullets with deathly precision. The first bullet whizzes through the air and catches the vampire in the chest, starting the disintegration process. As his torso turns to ashes, the second bullet hits his left shoulder, jostling his arm and reducing it to dust. Finally the third bullet hits and strikes in the right shoulder, as the legs disintegrate leaving nothing but a skull and spine to slowly fizz away in the ground. The vampire on the black bike races forward along the street and skids to a halt at the end of the road, cursing as a black Charge blocks his path before turning back. Blitzo turns in time to see the vampire and now Fat Fanpire #1 speeding back towards him, and dropping to a hunched position, Blitzo unveils a double-bladed boomerang in his grip, and twisting back to his full stance, spins and releases the boomerang, watching as it spins through the air, and between the two bikes. While Fat Fanpire #1 wobbles on his bike and remains on a steady course straight ahead, the other vampire turns right into a thin little alley, yelling out as sparks fly from the handlebars and side of the bike. As Fat Fanpire #1 speeds towards Blitzo, the Daywalker leaps into the air as Fat Fanpire #1’s bike slides on the ground, and as he slides underneath Blitzo, the Daywalker kicks back, slamming two heavy boots into FF #1’s head and sending him spinning to a halt. As Fat Fanpire #1 steadies the bike, Blitzo grabs the returning double-bladed boomerang and hides it behind his back as Fat Fanpire #1 sizes up Blitzo before speeding towards him again. This time, Blitzo spins out of the way as the bike zooms past him, but manages to jam the boomerang in the spokes, sending the bike twisting and spiralling in the air, and Fat Fanpire #1 with it. As the bike and FF #1 smash into the ground, Blitzo turns around in time to catch the other vampire speeding out of the alley towards him. Taking a step back, Blitzo leaps into the air, spinning around and landing directly on top of the bike behind the driver. Pulling out a retractable garrotte wire, Blitzo slices the head off the vampire and pushes the disintegrating body to the ground, before taking control of the bike. Blitzo: Am I the vampire, or am I the fucking vampire… Blitzo: No I am… Blitzo: There’s still one of them bastards left! Blitzo & Blitzo: ATTAAAAAAAACK!!!!!!!!! Blitzo speeds along to Fat Fanpire #1 skidding before the pimped out vampire and looking at him through his stern gaze. Fat Fanpire #1 can only look back through a crimson mask as Blitzo dismounts and walks up to him. Grabbing Fat Fanpire #1 by the helmet, Blitzo leans close to his ear and whispers in his own growling voice. Blitzo: Tell me where he his…and I'll consider you a loose end… Fat Fanpire #1: B-Blitzo…you are too cool…you glasses they never even flinch when you fight…you super badass…I will tell you…I will tell… The scene fades out as Fat Fanpire #1 confesses all to Blitzo, having no idea of what misery is to befall him. Blitzo growls as inch by inch, Fat Fanpire #1 confesses the whereabouts of the only being Blitzo truly cares about………Astler. To Be Continued
