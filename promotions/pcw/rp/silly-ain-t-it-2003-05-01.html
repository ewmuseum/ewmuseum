Silly, ain't it?

1 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-1-03 When last we left our resident Adonis, Dan “Big O” Osbourne, and his friends had recently entered a new and exciting world. And now they have to save it. But alas, there seems to be some problems. “How am I supposed to use this stupid hat?” “Put your arm in it, Jack, say something that rhymes and pull out something that helps us. It’s easy.” “Okay, Mikey, since you seem to know so much, what do the rest of these weapons do?” “Well, Dan, I can fire arrows of energy, Liz uses her club to bash things, Molly can turn invisible, (and apparently instantly teleport. I never understood that), Jack pulls seemingly useless items out of his hat that wind up being useful after all, and your shield can withstand any blow.” “What about this sword?” “I don’t know. Other than making sense, I can’t think of what it does.” “So let me get this straight,” Molly timidly questions, “with these weapons we can go beat this Viniger guy?” “Yup. Pretty easy isn’t it?” Dan nods his head. “Seems that way. Is there anything else we need to be aware of?” “Well there is one other thing, but I doubt if it’s really going to be a problem. It’s pretty rare that it shows up.” “Pretty rare that what shows up, Mikey?” “Well, there’s this five headed chromatic dragon named Tiamat that breaths fire, ice, lightning, poisonous gas, and one other that I can’t remember at the moment.” Mikey scratches his head. “I’m sure it will come to me at the most inopportune time.” “Joy for us.” “Don’t worry, Liz. With these weapons we can take her. But as I said, the likelyhood of us actually running into her is a million to one.” Suddenly, a large, deafening roar is heard behind the group. Everyone turns to look at Mikey. “Okay, so we’re going to Vegas after we’re done.” The skies turn black. The earth trembles with the advancing terror. Clouds roll towards our adventures who are preparing to face the onslaught. The clouds get closer and closer. Then they part revealing the horror of… a half man-half woman creature that looks vaguely familiar. “Tiamat?” The female creature half begins to speak, “Actually it’s Tia and Matt.” “What?” Dan speaks for the group and all their bewilderment. “You know. Tia Leoni and Matt LeBlanc” Mikey steps forward. “I don’t get it. What happened to Tiamat?” “Dead.” “So where did you come from?” The scene goes into a flashback sequence. We see Tia Leoni and Matt LeBlanc climbing aboard the rickety old roller coaster. The coaster rolls, bright lights, yadda, yadda, yadda. We see the pair standing in front of Mickey Rooney. Matt speaks up. “How you doin’?” Tia promptly smacks him in the back of the head. “Sorry. Hi. I’m Tia Leoni and this is Matt LeBlanc.” Mikey Rooney scratches his chin. “Tia? Matt? You know, it just might work.” With that, Mikey waves his hands and the couple merges into one being. Back in the present sequence… “You’ve got to be kidding me. Mickey Rooney turned you into Tia/Matt? Why?” “Well, there needs to be a third, neutral party to terrorize both Viniger and whatever adventuring party happens by.” “You? A terror? That’s just funny. Please, tell me how you plan on doing that?” Tia/Matt spits a green fluid onto Jack, destroying all his clothing with the exception of his magic hat.” Mikey slaps a hand to his forehead. “Acid! That’s what I forgot!” Liz doubles over in laughter. Dan draws his sword, Mikey draws his bow, Molly vanishes, Liz charges, Jack shivers and Tia/Matt attacks. An epic battle ensues. Lots of flame, and ice, and lightning, and poisonous gas, and more acid. Lots of screaming occurs, weapons fire, or clang, or poof, or whatever they happen to do. Liz goes ape shit. Finally Tia/Matt falls. Umm… let’s see… umm… we’ll say Mikey fires the fatal blow. Tia/Matt dies, says something funny, everyone’s happy. Okay, so let’s move on. “Mikey, what are you doing?” “I’m looting.” “Looting?” “Yeah, Dan, looting. Seeing what Tia/Matt here had on him or her, or whatever it is.” “You’re robbing the corpse?” “No. That would be wrong. I’m looting.” “Spoils of war, Dan.” “What’s wrong with you people!? We just killed Tia Leoni and Matt LeBlanc. We’ve ended their careers, and more importantly, she was hot. And now, you’re digging around stealing their personal belongings. That’s just sick.” “Hey! I found a ring of stamina!” “Give me that!” “Problems, Dan?” Molly places her arm around Dan’s waist, “None.” After a good time of laughing and mocking each other our adventures set off to find Viniger. Will they find him in time? Can they defeat him without me having to explain it in too much detail? We’ll soon find out as I have to wrap this silly thing up before Dan’s first solo match on Friday.
