The Wishmaster --- The Last Hurrah, Part 2 (Joint RP with Mani Shigeru)

27 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/27/02 Sunny, about 72 degrees. Perfect weather on this, the day after Christmas. The beachfront is occupied by busy tourists and residents alike, hoping to get in the water and get a tan, all things that very few places in the country could comfortably provide them with all with the convenience of having entire malls and 15 story Marriott hotels set up right across the street. McDougal's Jazz Pub and Coffeehouse sits on the intersection of Pleasant Avenue and 6th Street. Jeff Kashiwa makes an appearance playing 'Voices'off of his 2002 CD. The smell of irish creme coffee fills the air around the establishment, bringing a relaxed feel to the surrounding area. That, and the music, is one of the reasons that The End frequents this indoor/outdoor coffeehouse. And after Adrenaline, when he retires, he'll have even more time to do so. The End will never again wrestle in any ring. Not professionally. Not amateur. The End will not make appearances in other federations. He will not make appearances of any kind. To the rest of the world, The End will have dropped off of the face of the earth. And that's just how he wants it. Sunday will be his last match. His heart wasn't in the Carnival of Carnage, The End isn't a tag team wrestler and never had any desire to win the suspended belts. No, that was just routine. More and more his wrestling career had become just that... routine. He couldn't enjoy the competition of it, because there always ended up being some extraneous X-factor involved that took away from that. The feud, the match with El Diablo was to get The End to star in a movie. The End, good or bad, has no intention of acting for a living. Not his style. He doesn't like pretending to be someone he's not. He's a bad liar, doesn't suit him. The feud with Rush Mason was a glorified question of his heritage. The End never questioned anyone else's heritage, why should that be placed upon him? A senseless question anyway, one that ultimately didn't matter as The End trounced Rush Mason using the very Celestial heritage (the powers he had been granted by birth) to win the Last Man Standing Match. Most recently, The End got into a tiff with Totally Tubular Tommy. This got way out of hand, issues on two fronts: the one, Tommy knew that The End was a direct threat to his Continental Title ambitions; the second, Heavy Metal Harry, who has more influence on Tommy than the latter realizes, wanted Sandy Wong. He always wanted her, but now that she's taken... forbidden fruit, you know? Yes, everything had become routine. It could never be just about the wrestling. It could never be just about the sport of it. That, more than anything, is why The End has decided to retire after about a year of faithful PCW service. He couldn't just wrestle. He'd be absorbed into outlandish feuds and ridiculous storylines again and again until he went mad. Just like El Diablo. He had to get out. Had to... That was for Sunday. He would win his final match as a proper goodbye, and once he won the #1 Contendership to the Continental Title, he would choose a worthy successor to take his place in the match. The End doesn't normally guarantee victory. He may joke about it, but he respects opponents enough not to underestimate their skill. But at Adrenaline, for once, it's not about the wrestling to The End. It's about going out on top. It's about going out with some pride and dignity. It's about going out in a non-heelish way to counter Chef's last-minute turn of the mind. Damn cripples... But today, The End will relax. After all, he's got a cup of coffee, a bowl of peanuts and pretzel sticks sitting at the white-clothed table at the edge of the road which is blocked by bushes that were perfectly cross-bred with violets, he's got the soft, upbeat music in his ears and the smell of creme in the air... Sandy Wong is off looking in the shops down the street. Today is about relaxation. Not just for The End, but for his guest as well... The End:'Mani... glad you could make it.' In his red shirt and jeans, The End stands up to meet the coincedentally-identically dressed Mani Shigeru, the only person he told to meet him here... Mani: '.....' Mani nods and then raises a hand in objection.. The End:'Very well then, we'll skip the customary handshake and settle for a hug and kiss greeting.' Mani shakes with silent laughter and put out his hand for a shake... The End: *Shakes Mani's hand*'Would you like some coffee? Usually just saying I'm thirsty would bring someone running but... you know.' Mani nods his head 'yes' and they sit down. Then he looks around confused before gesturing to a nearby table with a grunt in that direction. The End picks up on it. The End: 'Ignore Storm and Windsor, they're just here on a date. Listening to the music. Jeff Kashiwa. Good musician, you should check his CD when you have the chance.' Mani looks up a bit shocked and notices Windsor and Storm... Mani slowly shakes his head a bit defeated... The End:'Anyway, it's not about them tomorrow... it's about your other opponents. Jon Kano, Brandi, and Flaymin Aymin. You've got a shot to challenge Teresa Tomas for the Television Title, and there is a very good chance of your winning.' End signals for someone to come take Mani's order. Mani nods his head and smiles... As a waitress approaches. Mani grabs a pen and writes down double cappucino on a peice of paper... On a corner Mani write down the message 'Open your Mind...' Waitress: *Confused*'Open your mind?' The End: *To waitress*'That was to me. Just get him the double capp.' The waitress is given $10 and walks away. Mani grins lightly and begins to speak with his mind. Note any matchwriters, or just those people who need to pay attention to Mani's character as I say SPEAKS WITH HIS MIND, meaning telepathic communication that I'm just letting you in on for the purposes of the story. Not that I don't think you pick up on these things. It's just... you know, the months on end of reading 'The End looks up and sees...' NOTHING! He can't see! Preserve the freaking character! But it's too late now. The End is retiring. Just don't do the same for Mani's character. Thank you... Mani:'Good, at least she said it out loud. Nice to finally communicate with you, Skylar.' Mani slowly turns to look at the door where the Waitress passed through to get his coffee. The End:'Your inner voice seems distracted. You must like the woman.' Mani smiles lightly his face fully exposed for the first time... Mani:'It was her eyes; light green like Kiana's were.' The End:'Ah. It's always something like that. Doesn't have to be the body. Sandy's got the most beautiful voice I've ever heard. But that's beside the point. How is the training for your match coming? It'll be people like you who need to hold up The Order with myself leaving.' End takes a sip of his Mocha Hazelnut decaffinated coffee, stroking his long hair out of his face. Mani:'Well.... Honestly training is what it has always been... Not neccessary... My lifestyle away from this sport keeps me in shape.' Mani's eyes slowly go from the dark blue of non-aggression to the bright green of full aggression as Windsor and Storm leave. Mani's eyes return to dark blue as the double cappucino sits in front of him... Mani:'My life outside the ring is troubled and you know that better than most should.' The End:'If you listen to Duke Williams, a good bottle of Jack Daniels, followed by 20 more, cures whatever life problems you have. Of course, you'll need a new liver after that. Anyway, calm down. You're transmitting all your thoughts to me instead of just what you wish to say.' Mani's laugh breaks between vocal and silent as he shakes his head. Mani:'Alcohol is not my forte... It's bad for the body... Even my body... As for my thought... I apologize.' The End:'That's alright. Anyway, don't worry. You're not facing particularly difficult opponents. They're the least of your problems.' Mani sighs. Mani:'As for Kano, Wilson, and Aymin.... I don't know what to think.. Against three PCW legends I'm looked upon as the underdog by all those who don't know me...' Mani breaths in heavily, seemingly taking in not just the air, but the whole world around him. Mani:'But more important.... Than that match... Any title... Is enjoying whats been given to you... And not regreting whats been taken away.... This is the first time since my re-birth that I have not worn a mask... All my masks have the familiar smell of blood.' The End shakes his head as he puts down his coffe cup. The End:'I tell you I used to have that same problem back when I was a semi-dark-gimmick. Mix lemon juice and Febreze in the wash and it'll come out. Anyway, how does it feel to have the mask off?' Picking up a pretzel stick set in a glass jar by the flower vase in the center of the table, The End takes a few bites while Mani responds. Mani:'Nice... I shroud myself in darkness as much as possible... I have no memories of my not wearing a mask... Except this one...' Mani tilts his head down and touches his lips. Mani:'Although its been burned my skin feels fine... And the wind feels good on my lips...' A pause in projected telepathic speech, before Mani goes onto a tangent. Mani:'Skylar..... Can I ask why you are retiring?' Mani turns and looks at the waitress again. Mani:'I wouldn't dare.... My world is a bit too dark for relationships...' The End gives a shrug. The End:'I just feel that I've done all I could. What I've been doing, these past three months. That's vintage End right there, I can't give any better than that. That's why I'm hanging it up. I feel that I could only go down from here, and I want to leave on top.' End gestures in the direction of the waitress. The End:'Anyway, nothing's keeping you from a one-night stand.' Mani:'I believe the old phrase goes... Better to burn out than to fade away?' The End:'The person who said that was a hobo who may have had a past but no present and no future. I have a legacy that I don't want to shovel dirt on by doing progressively worse in the ring. Leaving now will leave my career, my achievements intact.' Mani smiles lightly. Mani:'Sky my affection for her is false... Plus, the process that made one of the raZed Angels... Took away my drive for such needs... But hearing your explanation I understand your retirement.' The End:'Heh. They have pills and such for that. Besides, it's time for the new people to come in. I've been in PCW a year now. Unlike others who've been around a long time, I know when to step aside and let the new guard move in.' The End pretends to cough. The End: 'CoughKANOCoughCLYDECoughDARKSTARCoughCHETKINGCoughFLAYMINAYMINCough!' Mani:'Coming down with a cold, are we?' The End gives a shrug and sips more of his coffee. The End:'Perhaps. It's that bitter headcold that you get when you burn out. I have burned out. I can't give anymore. That's why I'm leaving. So, I'll win the match at Adrenaline. And holding the #1 Contendership, I'll be in a position to decide who gets that shot in my place. Do well enough and I might choose you.' Mani laughs this time fully vocal. Mani:'At this rate I might be able to speak by the time we leave....' Mani takes a sip of his coffee and smiles.. Mani:'Onto another subject... Skylar... What should I do with Shin? I want to save him but he deserves death...' The End: Were it me, I'd tell you to go to OSW and settle your stuff there under the larger NSW/OSW dealie. But that's not gonna happen, so I'll forget about it. Anyway, that's the funny thing about the universe. Leave things alone and they tend to correct themselves. Disregard Shin. The Celestials did. Whatever happens to him is the path that he has chosen and the fate that he deserves. Intervention would produce universal reverberations that could be even worse than whatever the situation with him is now.' Mani shakes his head. Mani:'If he kills another innocent I can't stand by.... I may be a killer Skylar but I have my reasons for each of my hits..... Shin is moraless and a murderer.' The End:'He has his reasons, as well. Vague as they may be. No one, human or otherwise, kills without purpose. Even the insane have reasons that are, to them, valid. Mani:'Yes, but my fear is that his purpose is sick pleasure.' The End:'Then stop him. But realize that the universe has balance which must be preserved. If not, it is a pendulum. Whatever void you leave by killing Shin will be filled by a much stronger, more evil force.' Mani:'I know this.. Which is why I am vexed about stopping him...' The End:'Don't eliminate him, just contain him. That is the only way to truly stop him. Shackle him to a rock at the bottom of the ocean, bury him in the Gobi Desert. But killing him won't stop the madness. You'll doom yourself to an eternity of it.' Mani begins to laugh Mani:'Shin could survive anything.. I've attempted to stop him twice.. I never have been able to...' The End:'Third time's a charm, right?' Drinking down the rest of his coffee upon hearing Sandy call him 100 feet to the north, The End wipes his mouth and shakes his head with another shrug. Shrugging and shaking his fist, they've become more his catchphrase than anything he could possibly say... The End:'I can't really tell you what to do. Look inside yourself. Isolate yourself, deep meditation. You hold all the answers you need, just may not be looking deep enough. Do what you have to, is the only advice I could give. But I also advise you to not lose the focus of the #1 Contender's match at Fusion. Winning that and the Televison Title would be a boon to you. You like competition as much as I do... And the better your own ranking, the better the competition you'll have access to. Don't let Shin paralyze you from living your life. He is more than mortal, yes, but he's constricted to the same thoughts and emotions. And that's what can be his weakness.' Mani nods his head. The End:'But he won't be a factor in your match at Fusion. So whenever possible, don't fret over him. When the time is right, you'll face off with Shin and end it. Until then, don't rush things. Everything happens for a reason. Just find the reason, figure out the plan. When you do, you'll be ahead of the game.' Standing up again, The End shakes Mani's hand one more time and as Sandy Wong approaches he gives Mani a goodbye. They've both got important matches coming up. Mani, a match that could potentially decide his future in Primetime Championship Wrestling. The End, his final match, his last hurrah. Their time will come. But for now, relaxation....
