Chucky Cheese Hell (short, worked off from Akiyama's)

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-3-02 Chucky Cheese Hell: Akiyama had just finished up a birthday party. Where you ask? Chucky Cheese. Sylo had went along with this whole 'party' idea until some freak dressed in a cow suit wouldn't quit bugging him. He had power bombed the cow through a table out of sheer anger. Soon after the party ended, the balloons had deflated and guest went home Jay Sylo found himself with Akiyama. He was angry, angry because time had been wasted in CHUCKY CHEESE HELL! Precious time he could have used training, or meditating, or anything besides listening to masses of small children yelling while some pedophile in a suit danced around. All one big waste of time....time he didn't have. In less than 24 hours he had to face off against three men for the rising star title. He wanted that title but more importantly he wanted to win. He wanted to stand before the masses with the rising star title held high as they all cheered for him, for only a moment time would stand still just for him. It wasn't a want by now it was a need, like a crack head needs a hit. So now we join as Jay Sylo speaks with Akiyama before they are off to the arena where the Divine fighters would surly walk away with gold. Tonight was the night where the world watched them and cheered for them with all their glory. (The wind had a slight chill in it as it blew through the short hair of one Jay Sylo. His eyes were narrowed and ice blue, he snarled a small bit as the EMT's could be heard helping the man in the cow suit from the background. Sylo looked to the man and snarled once more sending a signal of fear down the mans spine. He then turned his attention back to Akiyama who didn't look really happy.) 'So are we ready?' Sylo asked looking in Akiyama's direction. 'We are ready are you read?' Akiyama replied. 'I've been ready to get the hell out of here since Yuu told me we were coming to this god forsaken place,' Sylo snarled as he said this. 'Titu are you ready to leave?' Akiyama said looking to The Pharaoh. 'Are you kidding me? Hell yes I'm ready,' (The three, along with Yuu all climbed into one car. Yuu was driving, Akiyama rode shot gun, and The Pharaoh along with Sylo rode in the back. Sylo stared out the window as the car pulled onto the high way and headed toward the arena. His heart was heavy for he knew there was something more to tonight. He let out a sigh which came only from the depths of his body as he continued staring out the window as cars, signs, and trees flew past.) 'Do not worry,' The Pharaoh said looking to Sylo. 'It's not me worrying about the match. I mean it's only Lance Bell, Steve Johnson, and Ripper Ryckman, what's so hard about that?' He said turning his attention to Pharaoh. 'You do have a point but something is bothering you....what?' 'I feel a great evil, I haven't felt anything like this before....I do not know what it could be but I feel it, deep down in my bones,' Sylo said as he turned back looking out the window. 'You have more important things to worry about. You must win the Rising Star title, then all of the Divinity will have gold. Next you must face that social reject Karl Leblanc. He is nothing more than talk, you will defeat him with ease,' Titu said with a smirk. 'Yes Karl Leblanc is nothing to worry about, the Rising star title match is nothing to worry about but there is something, I can feel it deep down inside. I know I told you this but I just can't help it.....it's so strong,' (Akiyama turned from the front seat looking back at Sylo. He raised an eyebrow while staring deep into Sylo's soul.) 'Worry about tonight and Leblanc, if this 'evil' you speak of does show up we will quickly take care of it. Your main concern is beating Steve 'I am a retard' Johnson, Lance 'I'm really a homo' Bell, and Ripper 'I hear voices because no one will be my friend' Ryckman,' Akiyama said with a smirk. (All three men joined in with a laugh. Akiyama was right, he did need to concentrate on the match at hand and on Wednesday day when he beat Karl Leblanc worse than the prison beatings he usually gets.) 'I didn't get the joke,' Yuu pipes up. (All three men let out a groan as Yuu kept asking 'what?'. Sylo snarled and turned his attention back out the window. At the same time Yuu flipped on the radio as some boy band was playing.) 'LETS ALL SING!' She yelled. 'Oh dear god kill me!' Sylo yelled. 'KILL YOU?! WERE TRYING TO KILL OURSELVES!' Akiyama yelled as he tried to jerk open the door. 'AHHHH MY GOD LIKE EARS!' The Pharaoh yelled as Yuu snapped one of her free hands to the music singing along. (The car continued to drive off down the road as the three men were tortured by the music. Hopefully Akiyama and Sylo could recover in time so they would be at their best when their March Madness matches rolled around. But for now we leave them, ready to shoot themselves in the head.)
