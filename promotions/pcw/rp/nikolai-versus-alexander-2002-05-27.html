Nikolai versus Alexander

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27th May 2002 We return to the little house on the prairie right now. Ok, so we don’t. So sue me. Instead we return to an underused room in the Asylum Gym... the dining room. Probably because there’s no television in there, but it does seem perfectly suited for this situation... and if you don’t know about the situation then read the last promo you jackass. Moving on, Miyagawa looks a little tense as he sits on the dining chair around the wooden circular dining table that also sits the pregnant Natalia in a typical pregnancy dress and this new comer, this Nikolai that Natalia seems to know from way back. Natalia looks towards the table, trying to avoid the gaze of Nikolai, a person she never expected to ever speak to or meet again. She doesn’t know what to say to Miyagawa as this whole thing could bring a level of mistrust into the relationship which is exactly what she didn’t need after the whole debacle with Chris Sloboda and the arrival of her younger sister, Lara. Nikolai smiles at Natalia, and glances over towards the bellhop who opened the door. I guess maybe that last night in Russia could explain the bump, but who is the Japanese looking fool in some silly wrestling t-shirt. Maybe he’s one of those estranged fans who think that it’s all real. Whoever it is, maybe he could get him to go make some coffee or something. Nikolai (Russian accent, remember?): Can I get a cup of coffee or something? Miyagawa (Retorting quickly at the apparently smartass Russian bastard): Starbucks is down the road. If you were paid in pickles I think they might trade. Natalia (looking shocked): Hey! Miya, I’m Russian too, don’t forget that. Miyagawa: Who is this moron? Nikolai did you say your name was? Nikolai: Da; that is my name. And if you don’t serve the coffee in this Gym, then perhaps you fold the towels. So can you go fold some towels and leave Natalia and I alone, we have things to discuss. Miyagawa stands up from the table suddenly with rage in his eyes. Miyagawa: FOLD YOU OWN FUCKING TOWELS! Natalia leaps to her feet as best she can and steps between Miyagawa and Nikolai, who got to his feet after that last outburst by Miyagawa. She sticks her hands up to keep the duo apart, but neither man goes close enough to force Natalia to actually have to keep them apart. Natalia (with her Russian accent sounding more and more American every day): Boys, calm down... the pair of you. We can talk this over, but first I think that we need something of an introduction. Both men nod at her, but neither takes his eyes off the other. Natalia: Ok, good. My Former Fiancée Nikolai Rubens, meet my husband and the father of my unborn child, Alexander Miyagawa. Nikolai: Former? Natalia looks at Nikolai, straight in the eyes. Natalia: Yes Nikolai, my former fiancée, as not the current one and not ever will be again. You should have not come here. Nikolai looks saddened if by the shock of her words wounded him somehow. He bows his head and takes a step back from the confrontation. Nikolai: I’m sorry... I shouldn’t have come. I thought that you had run off to America to seek fortune... I finally landed a decent corporate job, so I flew over here to take you back with me to Moscow. Natalia turns fully to Nikolai, so that she stands just to the front and left of Miyagawa, although half a foot shorter so that they seem to stand together against this Russian invader. Natalia: You shouldn’t have come Nikolai, and I am going to have to ask you to leave. Nikolai: Ok... I’m sorry. To both of you. I wish you all the best in the future. That would have meant some more if Nikolai looked up at all through saying that. He walks towards the door with the front door to the opposite side of the corridor. He walks up to the door and opens it himself and steps out into the sunshine. He locks eyes with Natalia for a last moment before closing the door himself. Miyagawa (solemnly): And they said “May you live in interesting times.” Natalia looks over her shoulder at her husband, rather curiously. Natalia: And what do you mean by that exactly? Miyagawa: It’s a Chinese curse. Basically means that I hope many unexpected and unwanted things happen to you often. Someone said it to me once and I’ve expected the unexpected ever since... but I didn’t expect that. So, is there anything else I should know about your past? Natalia: Not much. I was once a man. Silence. Nothing said from either of them. And no movement until Miyagawa puts his arm around her shoulders. Miyagawa: Oh. Is that all? Natalia bursts out laughing at Miyagawa’s calmness about what she just said. Natalia: I was kidding! I would have thought you would have seen straight through that. Miyagawa (keeps straight faced): Oh... it’s just that I was once a woman. Natalia turns around to look at him while he keeps a perfectly straight face. Natalia: You are joking right... tell me your joking... wait, you have to be, or else I wouldn’t be pregnant. Miyagawa (suddenly grinning): I had you there for a second. Natalia punches him softly in the arm before wrapping her arms around his chest while he holds her head softly into the crevice under his chin as the camera fades out... Natalia: Maybe I deserved that.
