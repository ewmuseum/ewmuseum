The Calling - Conclusion part 1

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Christmas Day Prologue: Seven Primetime Championship Wrestling combatants have all gathered together in an extraordinary bid to become the Tag division’s number one contenders, a throne dominated by the uncanny force of Pranks With Attitude. Asked via letter, the details of this charade have been shroud in secret with the magnificent seven's only knowledge of forth-coming events was for them to gather at a location found on the back of the letter. We last saw Statuz Quo, Dante Jackson, collectively known as Playaz Inc, Chi'ing, Chen Lei-Chia, The Black Dragons, Front Office representatives Tim Walden and Brandi Wilson standing unified alongside Mr. Psycho, Skitzo within the confines of a gloomy mansion. Each oozing with ambition, an ambition to become the PCW Tag Team Champions, however everything about this 'Calling' has been mysterious and those with the answers have yet to draw any light on the subject. We last saw the collective as they entered the belly of the beast. A dark, dank dwelling covered in Victorian furnishings. An episode inspired by Alfred Hitchcock, claustrophobia, a sense of fear, unpredictability, the venturing into the unknown and with the troops doing exactly that these emotions were apparent. Without warning their host, Gaz 'The Jester' Green emerged atop of a winding staircase; the Emerald Menace would rectify all uncertainty. And it is from here that we pick up on the next installment of this ongoing saga-.... 'As I explained via your letters, this little excursion will offer each of you the chance to become the number one contenders for the Tag Team Championship. It's all rather simple, somewhere hidden in this mansion is the gold, all you have to do is simply retrieve it and you become the next heir to the throne-....' informed the Lord Of Laughter before an untimely interruption from the Front Office pairing of Brandi Wilson and her tax inducing colleague Tim Walden. 'Wait, these people aren't in the know, these people don't know the current state of the Tag Team division since it hasn't been officially made public-....' The Front Office collective of Tim Walden and Brandi Wilson took up smug stances as the surrounding parties of Playaz Inc, Skitzo and The Black Dragons looked at one another in confusion. With the Jester saying one thing and the dynamic duo of the Financial Reclamation Executive and his colleague retorting another the proceedings appear at a stalemate. Gaz meanwhile returned the evil glints of his co-workers with an unfazed facial expression, obviously having a trump card ready to play. 'Cretins of Primetime Championship Wrestling, you have been deceived by your former Tag Team Champion Mr. Green-....' proclaimed the blonde headed Tim Walden before the murmurs of those present exploded. 'Former?' 'Former Tag Team Champion?' 'What do you mean by 'FORMER' Tag Team Champion?' questioned Dante Jackson, speaking on behalf of the masses, a question that lay on the tips of every tongue, more so for this seven man collective. 'Two days ago, it was agreed by the board and issued from the CEO himself that the unit known as Pranks With Attitude would be stripped of the PCW Tag Team Championship taking immediate effect, however it has yet to be made public!' informed Brandi Wilson, spurning some rage fuelled glares and like a pack of wolves the group headed by Tim Walden ascended the stairs. Suddenly with a thunderous crash the doors swung shut, momentarily side tracking the angry mob. With a few well-bought seconds Gaz 'The Jester' Green played his second trump card, a simple twist to the safety-rail's circular finishing piece and the stairs retracted creating a slope, which sent the seven individuals into a cluster on the ground. 'Oh-... I forgot to mention this-... The doors are electronically locked and can only be opened with this specially designed key thus making this a game of cat and mouse. I bid you happy hunting and welcome to the Jester's private Alcatraz!' roared the trickster in a jovial manner, brandishing a key, which he placed in his jacket pocket before setting hotfooted from the crime scene. Rolling from the heap and returning to their vertical bases, brushing themselves down and looking a little ticked off the team embark up the stairs shuffling along the left-hand side, grasping the banister dearly. Like a group of mountaineers they ascended the challenging slope, their legs swaying freely from the polished flooring, one by one they emerge atop of the stairs and onto the landing. 'Where be dat mothafuckin pussy of a Jester, huh?!?' hissed the Ebonics ranting Statuz Quo un-amused by recent events. A giggle bellowed from across the landing, seeping from a set of doors merely feet away. Like a runaway locomotive the Playaz Inc representative Statuz Quo raced towards the source when suddenly the African-American male flipped into the air for no apparent reason, crashing back to earth with a sickening thud. - CRASH - 'STATUZ!!!' cried Dante as he raced over to his fallen comrade. Almost in a moment of de ja vu, Dante Jackson elevated up into the air and crashed alongside his stable mate. Yelping in pain and holding various limbs, Playaz Inc remain wounded in the face of battle as Tim Walden stepped from the shadows, inspecting the walkway he noticed the area had been rigged with a tort piece of fishing wire. Stepping over the pranksters handiwork, the fellowship proceeded slowly over to the source of sound, the giggling continuing to emit as they drew nearer. Creeping forward like a Special Forces unit, trying desperately to minimise their sound and looking to have accomplished just that when without warning the safety rail occupying the second floor of the house gave way sending Skitzo plummeting fifteen feet to the ground below. 'WHAT THE FU-....' gulped the Black Dragon representative Chi'ing as he peered at the life-less carcase of the 'Mental One' Skitzo who lay motionless, his limbs twisted in an inhumane manner. Suddenly as the group gathered their composure they looked down at the body as a mark of respect but to no avail. The body had vanished, yet tackling the gruelling obstacle of the stairs and the trip-wire was Skitzo who joined the side of those yet to be incapacitated. Taking up the infamous Hamlet stance, Skitzo knelt before the broken safety-rail, sympathetically talking to the ground he once laid upon in a crippled heap. 'Fear not number nine - I shall avenge your untimely death!' Almost immediately the psychopathic Skitzo raced down to the first floor, re-positioning his body back to its crippled state. Violently jerking like a victim of epilepsy Skitzo twitched his head and spoke up to the group in a solemn voice. 'Don't let my death be for nothing-....' Almost a repeat, the body sprung back up to its vertical base and waltzed past the obstacle course of the stairs and trip-wire, finally standing alongside his dumbfounded group of peers. Gazing star struck, absolutely astonished by the events that have transpired minutes earlier Skitzo broke the silence, his eyes watery with tears, he rallied the troops back into action. 'Don't we have a Jester to catch?' Joined by the battered Playaz Inc, the magnificent seven descended upon the source of these child-like giggles. Pushing open the door to a sleeping quarter, they stormed the room in force, with no exits and certainly no escape they crept up on a chair that concealed the laughter. A chair had been positioned with its back to the entrance and Gaz being 5' 11' could easily cram his small stature onto it, with his legs tucked to his body you wouldn't be able to tell otherwise. 'NOW!!!' roared the authority figure of Tim Walden. Storming the room, rage fuelled and still a little ticked off by this whole charade they threw aside the large decorative chair, yet no Gaz Green, the sound filtering from a tape recorder carefully positioned for full effect. 'Dis mothafuckin white boy of a Jester is playing you'se guyz - We don't need dis shit, so in true nigga fashion me and ma boy Dante are gonna do what you crackaz can't, holla back youngin!' ranted Statuz Quo as he pointed towards his partner, both leaving the room, pursuing their own attempts at capturing the elusive Gaz Green. 'Must be a little pissed at us for not asking how was his trip!?!' grinned the Cruiserweight Champion Tim Walden smugly. 'The gibberish talking peon has a valid point Mr. Walden. Instead of combining our efforts we should split up, thus covering more ground, after all we came here in groups of two in a race to take from Pranks With Attitude the PCW Tag Team Championships-... I see no difference, instead of gold we're now in the need of that key!' injected the Front Offices Brandi Wilson, spurning a sinister grin from her stable mate and the acknowledging nods of The Black Dragons. Scurrying from the scene like a swarm of ants, the Black Dragons and the Front Office vanished leaving but Skitzo to himself, a disadvantage and handicap for the man known as Mr. Psycho. 'I wonder where they keep the kitchen?' murmured the schizophrenic. 'Yeah I'm thirsty, I hope they've stocked Gatorade!' 'Must we drink that shit all the time?!?' 'Mind your own FUCKING business number six, huh!?!' 'Can we all just get along guys!!!' concluded persona number twelve with his innocent and child-like voice. As Skitzo walked from the bedroom the scene slowly drew to a close, distorting the sound and leaving just a black screen of emptiness, ending another chapter in this ongoing saga featuring Pranks With Attitude. - To be continued -
