Soul Searching...The Rugged Outdoorsman Type Way

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 3, 2002 There was a handful of clay in the bank of a river. It was only common clay, coarse and heavy; but it had high thoughts of its own value, and wonderful dreams of the great place which it was to fill in the world when the time came for its virtues to be discovered. Overhead, in the spring sunshine, the trees whispered together of the glory which descended upon them when the delicate blossoms and leaves began to expand, and the forest glowed with fair, clear colors, as if the dust of thousands of rubies and emeralds were hanging, in soft clouds, above the earth. In the eyes of The Heartbreakers, a thousand rubies and emeralds were hanging in the soft clouds, in their dreams to be more specific. However, it wasn’t rubies and emeralds they were after...it was pure gold. They had already surpassed round one, defeating what some were calling the “next big thing” in PCW. The next “Heartbreakers” or “Politically Incorrect,” if you will. The Dark Messiahs were a great team, but obviously they had their flaws. It seems that the Dark could not compete with the Light, or PINK!, for that matter, and the Dark Messiahs were sent packing to the bench. Well, now it is Suicidal Tendencies that will get their chance to dethrone the former champs from their winning streak. Suicidal? Hell, those two clowns might as well commit suicide if they are going to stand next to THE two hottest things to hit PCW since...well...their debuts. The flowers, surprised with the joy of beauty that The Heartbreakers brought to the outdoors, bent their heads to one another, as the wind caressed them...at least that’s what Jack and Johnny figured they were doing as they passed by. “Look at us,” Jack Valentine started, “All of this nature and beauty.” “Yeah,” Johnny Heart continued, “At least Mother Nature still has the kind decency to recognize when a greater beauty steps into her field. The beauty of our faces...” “And our asses,” Jack finished. It seems as if Suicidal Tendencies want to focus on the past. They want to focus on their one measly victory over a team that couldn’t even get the balls to face The Heartbreakers in Cowabunga Connection. Hell, even HANK~! could pull off a victory now and then over someone of the caliber of Cowabunga Connection. They seem to be hypocrites though when it comes to the past, saying that they are great and they are the team to beat because of their past victory. However, they will not concede to the fact that the Heartbreakers were, and still are, in the hearts and minds of all the little Heartbreakers that will be in the stands this Tuesday, the PCW tag team champions. You know, The Heartbreakers may agree with the Suicidal Losers on one point. There is a sadness of youth into which the old cannot enter. It seems unreal and causeless. But it is even more bitter and burdensome than the sadness of age. There is a sting of resentment in it, a fever of angry surprise that the world should so soon be a disappointment, and life so early take on the look of a failure. It has little reason in it, perhaps, but it has all the more weariness and gloom, because the man who is oppressed by it feels dimly that it is an unnatural thing that be should be tired of living before he has fairly begun to live. William S. Powell and Reginald P. Lovecraft should take a long look at that last statement. Those old hounds are falling apart at the limbs. They lack the discipline, and respect for the tag team division that they will need if they ever want to become PCW Tag Team Champions again. They are out parading their new stable around like they are hot sh*t. The Heartbreakers don’t need to remind the Political Inbreeds that it was Jack and Johnny that dethroned them of their titles and sent them on the downward spiral towards a lowly singles career. Aside from the usual ranting about the other tag teams, the main reason The Heartbreakers were out here in the great outdoors were to do some soul-searching. In the past, The Heartbreakers have been known as the cocky, self-centered, egotistical maniacs that we still see today...except back then no one respected them. Now, the fans love the Heartbreakers and their cocky ways don’t seem to hurt the fans as much as they did in the past. “I just don’t get it...why do they like us now, and they didn’t like us before,” Johnny asked, very sincerely, to his partner Jack. “Is it because we’re better wrestlers?” Jack began with a smirk on his face. “No, I don’t think that’s it Jack,” Johnny smiled back, as if this soul-searching thing was a big hoax. “Is it because we’re nicer guys?” Jack continued. “Na...I don’t think that’s it either Jack,” Johnny responded. “Do we look any better than before Johnny?” Jack smiled. “N-...well, we do look a little better, but I don’t think that’s it either Jack,” Johnny laughed. “Well...I wonder what it could be,” Jack began, as he reached into his camping bag. Jack and Johnny were wearing camouflage gear and hiking boots. They actually looked like two normal guys for once in their lives and they weren’t dressed like their normal flamboyant selves. However, Jack reached in that bag and pulled on two bright pink vests. They continued their mockery of a “soul-searching,” by continuing on with the conversation...it was pretty obvious where this was going. “I just can’t figure this out,” Jack smiled. “Me neither,” Johnny continued. Johnny began unzipping his camouflage gear, and Jack was soon to follow. It was a one-piece type piece of gear and they both zipped about halfway down, so they could throw on the PINK! vests. “Any idea yet Johnny?” Jack said. “Nope.” Johnny retorted. With their PINK! vests on and the camouflage gear still covering up their legs, the Heartbreakers turned around and shook their heads, jokingly puzzled at what on earth the fans could like them for. They slowly bent over, pulling their pants down to their legs revealing hot PINK! underwear. Jack, standing on the left had “It’s all about...” on his tights, and Johnny, of course on the right had, “the PINK!” on his tights. The camera zoomed in revealing their trademark catchphrase: “It’s all about the PINK!” Jack and Johnny turned around and looked at each other, still pretending to be puzzled. “I guess we’ll never know,” Jack said, shrugging his shoulders. As the humorous bit wraps up, the camera fades to PINK!
