The Thriller is Hungry

30 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/30/04 ‘So this is the big time? I’m not all that impressed. I was backstage at Turmoil, and watched all the action. It was good…. But not what I expected.’The scene opens to show a small, skinny, but muscular man sitting in a booth at a diner. The man sports a tank top, blue in color, and a PCW hat, with his black hair poking through the back in a pony-tail. The man is young in age, roughly 18 at the most. But he talks as if he’s a 10 year wrestling veteran. ‘Of course, I put my name in the Face to Face challenge. I got what it takes to be a World Champion. I maybe only 5’6…. 185 pounds, but I can hang with the big boys. That’s why I challenged you, Boda. You’re so big and bad, as you proclaim every time you get a mic in front of you, well, this Giant Killer is going to show you you’re just bad.’‘Excuse me, you ready to order?’ An elderly, grey-haired waitress steps into view. The young man gets visibly upset. ‘Listen, can’t you see I’m talking?’‘To who dear? You’re eating alone.’ The senior woman responds.‘Damn you woman, this is a promo. I’m talking to the camera there, and explaining to the fans why I challenged Boda this week.’The woman doesn’t understand. ‘What? Are you looney?’‘No woman. I’m Nathaniel Oscar Seller. I’m The Cruiserweight Sensation, gonna rock the nation. I’m a Thriller, Giant Killer, no need for filler. I hit low, and fly high, I’m hell of a guy.’‘So, what are you eating?’ The woman, seemingly unimpressed.‘FINE, you old bitty. Get me a hamburger, with cheese, and mayo. Curly fries and an unsweet tea.’The rest of the patrons turn and look at N.O Seller as if he committed murder.‘WHAT?’‘Honey, you’re in North Carolina. There is no such thing as unsweet ice tea. This isn’t the Northwest, where only pansies drink their ice tea with no sugar.’ The waitress informs the cocky little cruiserweight. ‘Fine, make it sweet. You sour old hag.’The waitress leaves to place the order. All the patrons turn back to their food. Nathaniel continues his charade.‘Okay, so listen, Boda….I know what you’re thinking…Who the hell is this kid? What kind of balls does it take for this kid to challenge me? Well, this week, on Turmoil, I’m going to show you, and PCW, that I am the GREATEST cruiserweight EVER. I can take down any giant, even you Boda. You better be prepared…Because Nathaniel Oscar Seller is coming for you, and you’ll never know which direction I’m flying in from.’The scene fades out, as the waitress brings Nate his sweet tea.
