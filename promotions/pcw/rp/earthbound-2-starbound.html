Date: Bleh BOOM!!! The bright, colorful, rolling hills of a little city tremble at the might of an unknown force that sends a shockwave surging through the ground. Flat, yet 3-D, houses quake and shake before the power of the earthquake-inducing intruder, which is unknown. A small, but bustling city of flat, colorful people sits beneath a huge cliff, which contains houses at its peak. The various flora is also rather… flat, but extremely colorful. The greens, browns, yellows, and other shades of colors beam in morning sun like fluorescent lights. It’s a rather peaceful city, but too bad the peace has been broken. The sirens of police cars pick up as little flat people with stubby legs and monotone expressions scurry from the action. The cars wind up a rode, strangely only facing one direction, and drive off along the cliff. They pass a couple of houses that are right next to each other, and these are our points of focus. The houses look virtually the same, with the only difference being the color of the mailbox in the solid green color that is probably supposed to be grass. The house on the right, somewhat bigger than the other, contains the mighty hero of this story. DORN TRACER! Let’s see just what the Astronomer is up to since the crashing noise. The house is 3-D, but extremely low quality. Everything is ‘flat 3-D, and a flat, gray, shaggy dog sits next a purple sofa and snores loudly. A blonde woman wearing a frumpy red dress scurries to and fro, never leaving the path she’s on. Back and forth... back and forth… like a robot. Upstairs is where the bedrooms are kept, and where we shall go. The door on the left side of the light red corridor is that of Dorn’s sister’s, and the one at the end of the hallway is his. Inside the room, darkness fills it like milk does a cup. Nothing is noticeable except Dorn’s bed, but it doesn’t matter, since there’s not much in the room anyway. We amble on over to the bed and see the Astronomer sound asleep, despite the rumbling. His snores sound like a slow-moving two man saw and he seems to fidget every now and then, like something’s bothering him. Dorn, in his sleep: Ugh… die…Ackart…Erratic… Bush… He turns over towards the window adjacent to his bed and continues to snore… but suddenly… WHAM! “Hey rump ranger, get up!!
