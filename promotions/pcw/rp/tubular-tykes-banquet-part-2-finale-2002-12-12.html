Tubular Tykes Banquet - Part 2 (Finale)

12 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 12, 2002 [The camera's flash and the record buttons are pushed on the mini-recorders. The Tubular One, PCW's most prized possession, is about to spell the year's fate for his Tubular Tykes program. His satin blue blazer shines underneath the bright spotlight, which Tommy has become quite accustomed to after all these years of fame. His blonde hair looks so golden, as if he's not really a human being. His tanned face gleaming so perfectly, radiating that special light to all the bodacious babes in the audience. Their legs trembling and hearts racing with desire. Tommy knows this, and he loves it. He is only a few feet from the podium as he waves his hand to the crowd, watching them clap their little hearts out. It's a good ovation, but not nearly as good as what the little Humping Boy got for his gnarly rendition of the alphabet. Pretty bogus, if you would ask Tommy. Harry is still hanging out by the bar, a little further down now as some middle aged looking man is cursing like a madman, trying to dry some kind of liquid from his white shirt...well, it looks bogusly yellow now. What did the Harrmeister do now? Oh well, it doesn't matter beause in a few moments Harry is going to be scampering for the door like a totally bodacious rabbit going to it's home] Tubular Tykes: WE LOVE YOU, TOMMEEEE!! [Tommy gives the Tykes a thumbs up and a smile, just playing it for the cameras. All the totally gnarly news stations are in attendance for what will be a totally earth shattering announcement. Channel 4, 8, and 12 are lined up with reporters spouting off something that they probably didn't even come up with. Tommy lets the camera's and the lights radiate into him, as if it's making his blood flow faster. It's adrenaline, dudes. The gnarly feeling that every eye is on him, knowing that he is the most tubular dude in all of the world. Surfer. Wrestler. God. It's that simple. That's why these people in attendance, and the people who will watch it on the news or read it in the paper, need to understand Tommy's reasons. Harry shakes his head toward Tommy, signaling for him to not do this. Not that Harry really cares about the announcement, he just doesn't want to look bad in the eyes of the lovely Sandy Wong once Tommy gets through to that unfar out guy, The End] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: DUDES ANd DUDETTES!!! [Tommy has both arms raised up, sort of like Richard Nixon did on that airplane all those gnarly years ago. The people slowly quiet down as Tommy's arms slowly make their way back down to his sides. His large frame is so amazing in the spotlight. Nobody has really noticed that Tommy was such a big guy, but he is. Standing at 6 foot, 4 inches and weighing in at 240lbs. He's no lightweight, not by a longshot. The people have come to silence as the camera's still flash wildly to catch every move that he makes, make sure they can capture something that sells newspapers. If you don't snap fast enough you may miss something important, something incriminating, something funny, or something sad. You never know what could happen at any given moment] TTT: I'd like to bodaciously welcome you all to Tubular Tykes 2002. It's been a most righteous year and we've marked in some of our highest donations EVER! It's only been three years of giving, but it seems with each year we get bigger AND better. Thanks, compadres! [The crowd claps and they all look to one another, pleased that they can give, save themselves from looking like greedy fools, just like the surfer boy on stage. The professional wrestler that could grapple any one of them up and break their bones. The man who is about to destroy dreams, and break souls. The Tubular One is about to show why he's got a nice corner picked out for him in hell. Tommy looks across the room to the hidden mirror that displays the written words for him to read. He's been going with the script that was written by Brandon Higgins III so far, but that will change in a few minutes or so] TTT: With two new centers being built we've got to really think big on the future. With the donations that you make tonight you can really contribute to some big, something radical. We can expand even more and in the next five years there could be Tubular Tykes Centers all over AMERICA! Think of the gnarly possibilities!!! [The crowd 'Ooo's' and 'Ahh's' as if there were a man holding cue cards, but there isn't. Watching some of the richest people in the world pretend can be most depressing, but there are a few that actually care...that's what will make this bogus announcement tough to make] TTT: The tykes have been making progress like you wouldn't believe. With the teachers taking time out of their schedules to committ to overtime and extra work, the tykes have been breaking barriers that scientists could never have predicted. That's why the human spirit will always prevail over those totally bogus scientist dudes. Our tykes are strong, never wanting to give up on their hopes and dreams. They are sometimes ridiculed and made fun of, but they just laugh back, not taking the insecurities of the totally bogus dudes and dudettes. So, it's time that the Tubular Tykes Foundation...moves on.... [Ralph, Tommy's agent, and Brandon Higgins III look at each other with quizzical eyes. That last sentence was not in the script, or maybe Tommy was just adlibbing because he missed it. The teleprompter is moving pretty fast] TTT: The Tubular Tykes Foundation must be closed.... [Gasps and looks of horror have filled the banquet hall. The retarded children laugh like maniacs, not really even paying attention to what TOMEEE is saying. He just looks cool. Tommy's agent and Brandon just look on in horror, trying their best to get to that podium to apologize for Tommy being an absolute FOOL!] TTT: Tubular Tykes will move on to a bigger and better plane. The children will be placed in with the regular tykes of this world to function as normal. [Higgins looks over in pure anger at Ralph] Higgins: He's gone retarded!! Ralph: That happened A LONG TIME AGO!!! TTT: With the gnarly progress that has been made over these three years, it's proven to your truly that these retarded...errr...SPECIAL COMPADRES can function in regular society. We can no longer segregate them from reality, try to get telling them that they are something that they are not. That's IS TOTALLY BOGUS TO DO!! I mean..did you ever see that movie Artificial Intelligence with that little dude from the Sixth Sense?! That little dude had some totally heinous head problems, and so will these little compadres! We need to make sure that they lead normal lives, and the only way to do that is to let them loose back into normal life. [Higgins and Ralph make it to the stage, and they cover up the microphone. Both men stare at Tommy with a vengence, really wanting to knock the sense into him] Ralph: Tommy, you cannot end Tubular Tykes! We signed all those building permits and contracts to start construction on the new buildings. I know you weild the power, but IT MAKES NO SENSE!!! You just can't end something out of the clear blue sky and WHAT ABOUT THESE CHILDREN?! They don't have a place to go, it's in the middle of the school year!! Higgins: I've worked too damn hard on this project for your dumbass to screw it up! I've made this my life for three years, making sure these little retards got the proper care. Then you come on stage and wipe that ALL OUT?! I should kick your dumb ass!! [Tommy just smiles arrogantly, way ahead of the totally bogus dudes. Tommy motions toward the back right corner of the banquet hall to reveal a very handsome man in a black leather jacket, with brown slacks on. The man smiles brightly with a toothpick in his mouth and points at Ralph. Tommy's agent has his mouth dropped in horror, knowing full well who that man in the corner is] Ralph: That's Mitch Granato...the super agent!! [Tommy's smile grows as he nods his head, stepping back up to the microphone as Ralph passes out. Higgins can do nothing but check Ralph...because they are gay lovers after all] TTT: With the help of my NEW AGENT, Mitch, we've put into works some TOTALLY BODACIOUS water parks!! They'll be opening up this summer for your enjoyment!!! [Tommy gives everyone thumbs up as they just look on in horror. Some want to mob the stage and take Tommy out. They've been donating to his water park fund. Tommy has millions and he steals other's money to build his water parks...trashes a charity to make a water park over top of it] TTT: Dudes and Dudettes, I did not do this for greed, I did this for the gnarly children. They deserve to live normal lives AND THEY GET LIFETIME FREE PASSES TO THE WATERPARKS!! Now THAT is totally bodacious. [The little children celebrate, hearing the only important words of the night to them. Free and water park. That's all that they needed to hear. Higgins stands up in a blind rage, ready to attack at any moment. He wants to rip Tommy's throat out and shove it straight where the sun doesn't shine. He wants to see his tanned face contort into horror, beg for mercy, just DIE! Tommy took everything that he was working for...TOMMY MUST DIE!!!] Higgins: I'LL KILL YOU!!! [Higgins charges at PCW's hottest new singles star, but he's stopped.....] Kid in football helmet: DUUHHHHHH!!!! [The boy puts his head forward and cracks Higgins directly in the crotch. The man keels over in absolute pain as the other children celebrate around the boy in the helmet. Tommy laughs hysterically on the stage as the crowd is just awestruck, they have no idea how to react. Is this a horrible thing? Is this a good thing? Is that man's testicles going to be all right? Who really knows!! Tommy walks off the stage smiling from ear to ear, knowing that he's going to make a ton of cash on this new water park idea. He passes Mitch and pats him on the back as Mitch gives Tom a thumbs up. Harry isn't at the bar...] TTT: Harry? [A beefed up security guard in a tuxedo points to the exit] Security Guard: Your boy took off a while ago. [Tommy laughs and shakes his head] TTT: Totally bogus, dude. [Tommy jogs outside as a couple of the news stations follow him, others are just sort of shocked as to what happened. Did Tommy really sell out the contract to a water park? Can he even do such a thing? Well, according to him he just did it. With one fail swoop The Tubular One, the man with a golden heart, took everything away. Now he'll be dubbed a greedy, evil man by the press, just like he was before he started Tubular Tykes. In Tommy's eyes, he was damned if he did and he was damned if he didn't. He'd rather be known to what he really is. Greedy, full of himself, and filthy rich....and being a TOTALLY BODACIOUS WRESTLER!! That part will be taken care of on Friday Fusion and at Decimation when he takes out The End once and for all, gaining Sandy Wong for his brother Harry. Tommy makes it to his blue and yellow stretch limo. There are four bikini girls hanging out of the sunroof waving at Tommy wildly, giggling like a bunch of school girls. Tommy smiles, happy that he doesn't have to go cruisin' for babes, it's already been done for him. He'll have to TOTALLY tip Jeeves five bucks for the babes...most generous of the compadre] TTT: Babes, your looking most bodacious tonight. [They laugh wildly to each other as they slither back down into the limo. Tommy enters the car and Harry is in there wearing chain mail] TTT: DUDE!!! What in the name of Medieval Times are you doing?! [Harry looks like he's seen a ghost] Harry: I'm prepared for the attack, man. TTT: Dude, they aren't going to kill us! They have no right. We just sold out, that's all. Harry: Really, man? TTT: Totally. [Tommy looks over at the four beatiful bikini women] TTT: Selling out is so awesome, isn't it? Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy smiles at the women as they slide over his way, playing with his hair...caressing his chest] Harry: Dude, punch me in the chest, man. [Tommy looks at Harry with a stern look] TTT: Punch yourself in the chest, dude. [Harry looks at his chest, then at Tommy] Harry: Ok, man. [Harry slams his own right hand into the chain mail. His face contorts with pain as he looks at the throbbing right hand before him] Harry: OUCH, MAN!!! [Fade to black]
