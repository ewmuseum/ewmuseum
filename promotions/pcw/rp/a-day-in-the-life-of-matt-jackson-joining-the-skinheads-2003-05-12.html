A Day In The Life Of Matt Jackson, Joining The Skinheads

12 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/12/03 The pieces of Jason Hilton’s plan have been going exactly like he wants. Nothing can stop the lawyer turned gladiator from getting his daughter back…he has hired the best people. Men who have broken Matt and worse yet…men who have caused him to doubt his resolve and give up faith, when he didn’t think he would. The pieces of the puzzle are finally dropping into place, missing only the biggest piece. Jason Hilton doesn’t have to worry because it seems like the final piece if about to fall into its spot. The events of last Fusion really have Matt questioning Haley’s loyalty. At the same time only fueling his hatred for Mark Makonie more…making him want to get his hands on the nazi, but so far he has had no luck. Makonie has been doing everything in his power to avoid the black ops agent and he has been very successful. It’s all going to stop soon though, no matter how much he tries to run, there isn’t going to be any escape for him. I will to kill you Mark…take away your life for the acts you have done over the past few weeks. Who is going to stop me from doing it? Jason Hilton…certainly not. He has no reason to want to help you anymore. I did what he wanted, I left my princess and now she is going to be out of your hands for good. The only thing left for you now is to die…to lie at my feet as another nameless face. Everything can begin now that they have freed me…no more captivity. Matt paces the bedroom of his hotel room. He is dressed in his normal attire; a grey suit, that is form fitting to his muscular 5’8” frame. His spiky brown hair is covered with a flesh coloured swimming cap, not letting a shred of it be revealed. Pulling open the blazer, he makes sure the leather holster is in place, holding his black plated 9mm pistol. Letting the jacket fall back into place, he continues to pace around his room, listening to the sounds of the South Koran nightlife outside the open window behind him. Stopping, he takes a look at himself in the mirror in front of him…fixing the cap to make certain that everything is in place for his meeting. Taking a step back from the vanity dresser he plops down on the queen sized bed behind him, ruffling the sheets and causing the comforter to conform to his body. “Well it looks like Society does have a good idea. Making things conform to you is nice. I might have to try to use this plan on Turmoil,” Matt says aloud, letting a smile split his angular face in half, revealing his perfect white teeth. They are no longer caked in blood and look good for a change, as does the rest of his body…he cleans up nice and not being around the captors is letting him rest his body…the beats over the past few weeks have done a number on him, but they are starting to become just memories, letting him focus on Turmoil. “Everything is going to be so much better…after I take care of the cancer that is Mark Makonie,” he continues, letting his words fill the tiny bedroom. It’s not an elaborate bedroom, but it gets the job done. Having a bed at the head of the room, with a small metal nightstand next to it and in front of it a vanity center. On both sides of the bed are two medium plywood doors; the one on the right leading to the bathroom and the one on the left leading to the bigger area of his hotel room. Resting his hawk like brown eyes for a moment, he shuts his eyelids, remembering the massive amount of bruising and cuts that are around them. This only makes his smile brighter, knowing that the beatings are no longer coming…knowing that he is free to keep PCW open, going behind the CIA’s back to ensure that his own share in the company is safe. Making sure that it isn’t forced to conform to people like Society or crumble under the pressures of the new power stable…putting an end to VJ on Turmoil. Hearing a knock on the outer door the Chameleon jumps off the bed, and surges forth over the white carpet below to the door on his left. Jerking it open, Jackson crosses the enormous room and climbs a few steps to the heavy double doors. Making a few final straightens to his suit, Matt takes a deep breath as he grips the gold handle and opens the doors. “I can’t believe I am doing this…there has to be some other way.” Matt thinks. “There is no other way…you failed Matt and now it’s time to release Christian MacManus, time to release the Chameleon” Christian MacManus barks, pushing Matt out of his head. “I got the job done in Paris and in Hong Kong!” Christian MacManus wins, as he stands staring at the bald headed man in front of him. The man nods and smiles, letting himself breeze past Jackson into the hotel room. Walking to the middle of the room, the man sits down on a leather couch in the center of it and adjusts his jeans and white tee shirt to make himself more comfortable. Walking over to a small table behind the couch, the Chameleon grabs to glasses and sets them on the table. “Can I get you anything to drink Mr. Manger?” Matt questions, pouring a glass of bourbon for himself. Dropping a few ice cubes in, the operative turns to his guest. “I would like that, but I am afraid I didn’t catch your name over the phone,” Manger replies, a sense of power in his voice. He spins his bulky body around on the sofa to get a better view of the man who called him and paid for his flight to South Korea. “It’s Simon,” Jackson quickly lies. “I am sorry I was so brief over the phone, but I felt something like this should be saved more for in person…that’s why I put together this little face to face meeting. I figured we could get a lot more done this way and even enjoy a few drinks.” “The face to face meeting is fine with me Simon…wish it could have been someplace else other then this gook infested place, but I believe we will be able to make do here,” Manger bellows out, some laugher in his tone. “I can’t believe he just said that…there is no way I can go through this.” 'Shut Up,” MacManus interjects…don’t blow this. It’s our only ticket to making the man we so hate pay. I should have never let you take over Matt…you made me soft.” Trying not to show his disgust Matt cringes a bit, pouring another glass of bourbon for his guest. Picking up both glasses, the Chameleon glides across the white-carpeted floor over to the couch. Forcing himself to sit down next to the skinhead, Matt places his glass on the wooden coffee table in front of him and hands the other one towards his guest. Manger grabs it and takes a few sips and sets it down on the table next to Jackson’s. “Now Simon, it’s not everyday someone contacts me and flies me to South Korea…you must really be interested in the movement,” Manger begins, locking his black eyes onto the young man in front of him…cracking a small smile at the agent to try to relax him a bit. Nodding the Chameleon crosses his arms over his barrel of a chest. “I am very interested in the movement sir. I don’t believe that there is another organization that can better suit me out there. It will be refreshing to be around a group of people, who share the same believes and ideals as I do.” I can’t believe I just said that…it must be done though. “That’s what I like to hear Simon…your commitment shows. I mean never before have I been flown out of the US. There is something in your eyes that shows you have the drive I can see it. You are going to make a great addition to the team,” Manger boasts, moving his arm more towards Jackson, so he can clearly see the swastika tattooed on his arm. “Very impressive sir…one day I hope I can have the same symbol on my arm. I would wear it proudly, just as I will serve the cause proudly,” the Chameleon lies, clinching his fist and hitting his chest. His guest only smiles at this jester. “What is it you do again, Simon?” Manger asks, trying to probe deeper into his new recruits life…making sure that he is perfectly ready for the job. “Have you ever seen the PCW sir?” Jackson responds, hoping that Manger will give him the answer he wants. “I can’t say that I have,” Manger tells him, reaching out for his glass and taking a few more sips of the alcohol. Setting it back on the table, he turns his full attention back to the operative to his side. Jackson stretches, grabbing his own glass and finishing it off in full. “I do a lot of back stage work for them and I sometimes wrestle,” Matt starts. “It’s just as of late the fucking niggers have been holding me down and I am sick of it. I am sick of them raising over the white man and taking what is his,” he adds in, looking to get the full affect of Simon, the newest man he is letting himself become. I can’t believe I am saying this too him…I am becoming the man I hate, I am becoming Mark Makonie, but it’s the only way I can trap him…the only way to kill him. “The naggers seem to do that Simon, but don’t worry you are taking a step in the right direction. You know by joining us though, you are going to have to be fierce in the ring, make your beliefs known and topple the damn coons.” “I understand totally sir,” the Chameleon lies once more. “I will do anything it takes to make our race strive over them all. This is the era of white power and I am going to be pleased to usher it in. With you by my side, I know that I can put those niggers in their place.” Yeah right you dumb fucker…you can’t control my actions...I can't be conformed. You won’t watch over me in PCW. I am just using you in this deadly game and your giddy ass is to blind to see it. Soon everything will once again be mine and everything will be back to normal…Makonie won’t know how or why, but it won’t matter. I am proving why Stone Burn’s is nothing, but a murder and I am an assassin. Reaching out his hand Manger smiles. “I am glad to have you aboard Simon, but it’s getting late and I think I should go down to the room you reserved for me. I will be in touch, but I would like to tell you about a concert we are having to promote the cause.” Grabbing his hand the Chameleon nods. “Please sir tell me anyway I can help.” “Well if you weren’t to busy with this PCW thing, we would like you to come to Norticfest, small concert back in the states…Shane Weathers will be headlining it and singing a lot of music to get our message across.” “Tomorrow we have a show, but I am free after that. So, I should be able to make it to this concert. Seeing as you aren’t leaving until tomorrow, maybe we could fly back and talk more on the plane about our future,” Matt hisses, letting a smile cross his face. Standing up, Manger nods his head and walks towards the door. Matt listens for the door to shut and quickly jumps up punching the couch. He begins to pace the room in front of him and feels his face scrunch up in disgust. I hate you Manger…your movement and beliefs make me sick, but it’s the only thing I have left. The only thing that I can use to salvage my life.
