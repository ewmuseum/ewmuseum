Dan: Pizza’s here. (Dinner…….) -The Williams family sits down for a nice Joe’s pizza dinner.- Dallas: Pizza again? Duke: See!! Jessi: F*ck you both. Dallas: Good then I’ll move out. Duke: Next year when you start college you can be gone. Dan: I’d be happy to go for ice-cream dad. Dallas: Suck up. -Duke shoots Dallas the glare from hell.- Duke: I hate f*cking kids. Your dad would kill you if he found out. Dallas: Last Christmas I took my earnings from modeling and got these. -Dallas lifts up her shirt and exposes her perfectly bought breasts. Mary shows them off like a Price is Right girl does to a new car.- Dallas: When my father saw them he damn near popped them. Mary: Its was wild Mr. Williams’s vein was sticking out of his head. I have never seen anybody get that red. Dallas: But it was ok when he got a facelift last year….whoops did I say that on camera. -Both the girls laugh.- (At the mansion……..) -We find Jessi and Duke sitting next each other watching TV, sitting in the love seat.- Duke: No but really we have some good kids. Jessi: They may have a foul mouth but at heart they’re all angels. Duke: Even though Dallas and I fought she’ll be home at midnight like she’s supposed too. They truly are well behaved. (Back at the parlor………) -A dirty tattooed man is holding Dallas’s left breast as he lines up the piercing gun.- Dallas: Your hands are cold. Tattooed man: You have some nice breasts. Don’t worry babe in a minute you won’t be worried about my hands. -A scream is heard as the gun puts a hole in the nipple of the daughter of malice.- (On the love seat back at the mansion……) Williams: We’re not quite the Cosby family but we’re pretty damn close. (Back to the Parlor…..) -Mary plays with the new ring that protrudes from Dallas’s boob.- Dallas: You can play with it tomorrow right now it hurts like f*ck. (At the Mall back in the burbs…..) -Dan and his football buddies are at Randhurst mall looking to pick up fat chicks.- Dan: Dude look at that cow over there!! I’ll give you ten bucks if you go hit on her. Tim: F*ck you man she’s like 40 and fat. Dave: Lets keep it under 18 we’re more likely to drive them to suicide. Dan: Good point. Hey baby. -Dan walks over to a fat assed 16-year-old girl. He puts his arm around her.- (Back on the love seat with Mr. & Mrs. Williams……) Jessi: Dallas can be rough, but Dan is perfect. He is the sweetest thing on earth. The kid has a heart of gold. Duke: Sometimes I wonder how somebody as mean as me can produce something so pure as him. (Back at the mall with tubby…….) Dan: So what’s your name? Fat chick: Sally. Dan: You have a boyfriend Sally? Sally: No? You’re hot. Dan: I know. So do you want to be my girlfriend? Sally: I would love too!! Dan: Then lose about fifty pounds you fat bitch. -Dan’s friends jump in and all start oinking as the girl runs off crying. They all laugh hysterically. (Back on the love seat with the Malice parents…..) Jessi: The nicest kid I’ve ever seen. Nothing like his father. (11:58pm……….) Jessi: Dan is asleep and Dallas just got home. -Duke points at the clock.- Duke: See right before midnight. A real trooper she is. Jessi: We are great parents aren’t we? -The couple kisses as the screen fades to the credits.- =w=/MTV/PCW
