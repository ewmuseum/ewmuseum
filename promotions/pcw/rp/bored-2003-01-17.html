Bored...

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17-01-03 Things couldn't get more basic than this. The scene fades into a black room, sitting under a bright light silhouetting them somewhat, are Skitzo and Pete!. They are motionless, as the camera zooms in on them; the finer details are picked out, until finally they are as black and golden shadows. Skitzo coughs, and Pete! clears his throat. Pete!: (eats fried chicken) Hey, Skitzo, Skitzo, Skitzo, Skitzo, Skitzo, Skitzo, Skitzo, and Skitzo. What's the Dilly, yo? Skitzo: Niggaz in da hood my fatboy brutha...motherfuckers can't stick to their own so they gotta invade the white man's land and get punked out, now they all bitchin' ass foo's! Skitzo: I'm ok personally... Skitzo: I got a little drunk and wanked to some Spanish donkey porn myself! Skitzo: Dirty bastard! Skitzo: Fuck off at least I didn't try to run for British Prime Minister… Pete!: (blinks) Fo' Sheezy. Pete!: (looks at place of Mac n cheese) Mmm. Cheezy. Skitzo: Apparently we're booked on Adrenaline, I ain't seen a promo from that cracka hater in a long while, how's your bone collector lookin'? Skitzo: Dark Gimmick collects penii? Skitzo: Silence... Pete!: yeah he does. Right up his bony ass...ever see how wide that thing is? It's like a fuckin’ cave. Pete!: (drinks a 40) Fo' Sheezy. Skitzo: I ain't taking no part in fuckin' with Clyde's daddy-o, Hoss...he can like...kill people! Skitzo: You wouldn't challenge Ghandi to a thumb war you pussy Pete!: Yeah. Pussy. Skitzo: youdie... Pete!: youdie. Skitzo: Fuck you then I'm outta here, I get no fucking respect! Skitzo: Skitzo leaves the light, then in the darkness can be heard twitching and walking back as though nothing had happened. Skitzo: AND DON'T COME BACK!!! Pete!: YEAH! Skitzo: Well, it's Friday, I'm bored as fuck but the last time I didn't do any promotional material for a match.........I won...I think... Pete!: (clearly confused) Skitzo: Which means we gotta get off our fucking asses and pretend we're on some wild adventure? Pete!: Yeah. Let's go thuggin. Looks like fun. Skitzo: Can't be assed big man, I'm really bored as fuck for once. PCW sucks when you're feuding a little bitch that won't put up after continually getting beat down... Skitzo: And there in lies the fucking problem. Skitzo: Here, have a Gatorade! - Official Drink of PCW! Pete! turns around and puts on a Raiders hat, and some sunglasses. He grabs the Gatorade, and chugs it. Pete!: Awwww...come on, my niggers. Let us go and bust some caps into some thuggish ruggish dudes. For Real. Skitzo: Skitzo chugs down his Gatorade while Pete! suddenly eyes up a nice chicken leg resting on his left tit. Skitzo: You sure do get distracted easily, hm? Pete!: (eats chicken leg) Mmmph. Skitzo: So.......how 'bout them Raiders? Skitzo: Shut up and say something racist! Skitzo: Nigger. Skitzo: Don't be so black, man! Pete!: Anyone got any crackers? Skitzo: Quo does, locked in the trunk of his '69 Chevy. Skitzo: Playa hata... Skitzo: Skitzo yawns and coughs in the direction of the camera. Looking for something, he finally sighs and sits back down on the stool provided, while Pete! rests amply on four, two for each cheek. Skitzo: Cameraman, got any ideas for a promo? Pete!: (picks his nose) Skitzo: Kannon really fucked up signing this match. Skitzo: It's gonna be a walk in the ghetto. Pete! nods and eats some watermelon. Skitzo: Where the fuck are you getting all this food from? Pete!: Where you getting all those people from? Skitzo: Look at the flab man; it's like a humid fridge just waiting to be raided! Skitzo: Hey fuck you, you're fat and I'm schizophrenic... Skitzo: And you can lose weight... Pete!: But I won't. Skitzo: Or get liposuction, fatty. Skitzo: Hey I got an idea, wanna game of slaps or thumb wars? Skitzo: Oh so Ghandi isn't good enough, but this fat fuck is?!?! Pete!: What's slaps? Skitzo: Clasp your hands together, and take turns slapping one another. If your opponent moves their hand and you miss, it's their turn...game keeps going 'til we decide our Adrenaline match warrants a decent promo. Pete!: I thought it was when I did this. Pete! stands up and starts slapping Skitzo in the face with all the force of a feather… Pete!: HOW'S THAT BITCH? HUH? YOU LIKE THAT? HUH? ANSWER ME! Rather quickly, he runs out of energy, and sits back down, panting. Skitzo: ............................................Yah I'm fucking bored too. Pete!: yeah.........(pants)........why don't.....(pants)....we kick......the camera guy's ass...? Skitzo: Do you think anyone is still watching? Pete!: (looks around) I won't tell if you guys won't. Skitzo: Y'know... Skitzo: Dark Gimmick might be able to kill people, and...and Quo might be able to be black... Skitzo: BUT I CAN ROLL MY TONGUE THREE TIMES OVER!!! Pete!: whoa...(watches) Skitzo: I'm not doing it, Porky... Pete!: Do it. Pete!: Now. Skitzo: Skitzo rolls his tongue three times over, and Pete! gasps at the amazing spectacle that is a rolled tongue. Pete!: mmmm...burrito... Skitzo: Hey if we continue to sit here, do you think PCW will give us a decent match instead of one that entails LOLing and dying? Pete!: (looks at watch) How much longer are we supposed to sit here? Skitzo: I don't know, but how about we have a sitting contest... Skitzo: See who can get deep vain thrombosis first! Pete!: I'm down! Sitting's my specialty! Skitzo: READY! Pete!: SET! Skitzo: GO! Pete! sits down, and falls off the stool, hitting the floor head first. Pete!: Ungh... Pete!: (starts to bleed) ..... Skitzo: I.....can't......move.... Skitzo: I...WIN! Pete!: (starts to gurgle, still on the floor bleeding) ga....ga........ba.a..... Skitzo: ............. Skitzo: ........... Skitzo: ............. Pete!: (starts to convulse) Skitzo: ............. Pete!: (foams at the mouth) Skitzo: ................ Pete!: (coughs) Skitzo: ............ Pete!: (blinks) Skitzo: ............. Pete!: (sits up, stands and gets back on the stool) Skitzo: Statuz Quo and Dark Gimmick got no fucking chance against us man! Pete!: (nods) Skitzo & Pete!: ………………… The End!
