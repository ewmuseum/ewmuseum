The 80's [3]

15 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15.01.03 'Hello?' a voice calls out with much of a timid response. The calling for words happens to be a quite bit over, as the parties are not situated with their surroundings. Behind the group of boardwalkers happen to be Jackson Rust and The Masked Mullet, conversing while starring at a tanned woman's body from far away. Pertaining to the knowledge, we are currently in New York, preferbly the beaches of New York City back in the year nineteen eighty-six. The beats of Aerosmith and Run DMC blasts through boom boxes set upon alongside corners and marketplaces where it is customary for people to set up circles of poping and locking, not to mention other forms of breakdancing upon mats and small rugs. Many a shop keeper would run these young hooligans off with a broom or some other weapon, but no. Not in New York. Now, you would just shoot people for saying something wrong. Inside of this group happens to be none other than Rent-A-Hero, Vanilla Skillz, Shin and Mani, not to mention the only person that is truly deserving of this group, Dante Jackson. Although, something is missing from around his neck. 'Hero, GIVE ME MY CHAIN BACK!' The Hero twirls around in a Hurricane typ eof fashion, smiling at his opponent before tossing the chain over the Vanilla Skillz, who flaunts it without much disregard. 'YO HOMESLICE, I BE PLAYA WITH THIS!' Dante grumbles, before snatching the chain away from him, pushing him into the fray of black men dressted in Kangol caps and Addidas warm up suits with the Superstars to match. 'Yo, what are you doin' here man?' one ask towards Vanilla, obviously his attention not parted towards the man. 'You lost?' 'Naw, I be pimpin' like dem hoez from the ninth flo'!' Skillz shakes off the words, before Dante pulls him from the arm. Skillz smirks towards Dante, but the Lyrical Messiah waves him off quickly. Dante shakes his head, 'Look, time to lose the ebonics for a second, you have to mix it now.' Skillz raises his eyebrows, the eyes bulging out in complete fear before turning back to the group, starring at the black men. 'I don't kno' any reg Englizh!' A sigh hits the face of Dante, but he turns back to the group. 'Look, this is a white boy. He doens't have any kind of spitting skills on a microphone, but he's is good with dancin'? Cool?' 'Hey man, just as long as he stays on beat, he's cool.' the leader of the group reports to Dante who nods with affirming tongue. Skillz steps back into the circle, before the tune of 'Rappers Delight' by The Shugar HIll Gang begins pumping out of the small portable box's speakers, well and nicly enough for Skillz to hop in the circle as the others look at him. Soon, he drops down, beginning to spin on his head with relative ease as the others begin cheering him on. As Dante smiles, a figure spins him around shaking him out of the relative groove. 'Yo, why you ain't speakin'?' 'Cause, I gotta mess with the white folks man, you know how it be like!' Dante chuckles as he turns around to face his Playaz Incorperated breathern, Statuz Quo. The six-foot five, former Syndicate and Tag Champion of RUSH and PCW smirks, wearing a red hoodie with black pants and boots. He stays incognito a bit, but right now he's like he is always. 'High?' Dante stares into the small, yet slanted eyes of Quo. The blood shot sparks of red are shown easily as Quo breathless lifeless smoke out of his lungs. The raised eyebrow effect from Dante doesn't help matters either. Quo nods to Dante, 'Ya know.' Shaking his head, Dante takes a seat over towards a bench, before spotting Skylar Green walking around the boardwalk with nobody even paying him mind. Wearing a black metallica T-shirt, its obvious where 'The End' is going. Dante whispers to himself, before turning to Quo, crying on the inside. 'I wish I had a T-shirt like that.' he mocks Skylar from afar as Quo looks around Dante suspiciously. 'Cops?' 'No, Oprah. She was doing a show here and found my ass out of the muthafuckin' blue. Dat bitch is HUGE!' Statuz remarks, moving away from the crowd as he begins to run away, hands folded into his pockets while cautiously checking the scene out behind him. As the shot sppeds away from Dante, we rejoin Jackson Rust and The Masked Mullet, checking out more women on the boardwalk. Smiling rather boyously, the two men begin playing paper, rock, scissors as the scene zooms in a bit on what they're convering on. 'Okay, 'Jackson starts with his left palm open and his right fist planted in the shape of a 'rock'. 'Same time, ready?' 'As always, but if the person wins, they've got to go get those girls numbers.' Mullet responds with a smile on his face, he's determined, afterall in high school he was the national RPS player of the year, all four years he was in high school. That has to mean something against Jackson Rust, a mere newbie to the sport whose picked up on it after a few drinks in various clubs and night parties. Jackson raises his head to face Mullet, 'And if they lose?' Mullet thinks for a second, playing 'Seek and Destroy' by Metallica in his head. 'I've got it! The loser will have to strip NAKED, and go get those girls numbers!' 'You're on!' Jackson cries with an ethuiastic appeal as the two men drop down rock, rock, before Mullet switches quickly to paper as Rust keeps his rock. Looking at it for a second, Rust shudders as Mullet raises his hand over the playing area, covering the fist of Jackson Rust shaped into rock. He's lost, meaning he has to go in the buff, to talk to females while Mullet uses what he knows best. 'CHICKS DIG THE MULLET!' 'Preaching books for sale!' The boyhood wonder from Atlanta, Georgia is standing back on a corner post, nice whire suit with black shirt on the interior, before quickly handing out copies of some unheard of book. Rent A Hero comes up to him, Chet counting his money like a crazed fan. 'One, two, three, FOURTY!' he cries before stuffing the faded dead presidents into his pocket. Renty shakes his head, 'Aren't you that seriously good guy from Atlanta that I have to save every time Totally Tubular Tommy and Dante Jackson bumrush you?' 'Not now son, I'm busy.' the former Continental champ replies. 'Dawg, I seen you - you on the ground like a lil' bitch before I come to the scene, kryptonighting err' mutha around there on the set. THEY BE SET TRIPPIN YO'!' the Hero shouts as others from the break dancing group join him. 'YEAH!' Chet shakes him off, 'If you want an autograph, you're going to have to wait.' Rent turns his back on Chet, 'Man, if mah Krypton powers don't booty short slap yo punk ass, then I don't kno' what will!' Chet clicks his tongue, before looking at his book, 'Self Help for Old Wrestlers?' Dante chuckles from afar, 'White people man!'
