It's all just a dream.......right?

10 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/10/02 Things certainly have changed in PCW....and none for the better. Jealousy and envy has bred some serious contempt for the man the made PCW what it is today, Tyler Nelson. The man that created the Primetime Championship Wrestling juggernaut has been taken down by the very people that he made famous. He gave them careers when no one else would let them in the door. He paid them for being worthless pieces of this when others shunned them. Hell, he was donating to charity every time he made out the payroll. And how did they repay his generosity and good will? They stabbed him in the back. They lied in a court of law. They gave false information to the Department of Justice that incriminated an innocent man. All this.....because they were jealous. Jealous of the money Tyler Nelson worked his fingers to the bone to earn. Jealous of the power that Tyler Nelson possessed as a result of that hard earned money. Jealous of the respect that Tyler Nelson commanded and so justly deserved. Jealous of the man....Tyler Nelson. Everything happens for a reason, they say. The reason for these individuals to bite the feeding hand of Tyler Nelson is still unknown, but they will pay nonetheless. Jealousy and envy are strong character traits, difficult to defeat when used to their full potential. But nothing, nothing at all on this world, can withstand the power of greed. Moving to this past Friday....Tyler Nelson emerges from the rear of the Strahan Coliseum in San Marcos, Texas. The time is not too long after his little “talk” with the President of PCW Duke Williams. Tyler exits the arena dressed in his orange prison issue jumpsuit, with only one flip-flop in his ensemble due to losing one while kicking the hell out of Duke. He has what looks to be a dry cleaning bag slung over his shoulder and a rather satisfied smirk on his face. Tyler marches out into the parking lot, towards where the infamous black Hummer limo would normally be parked. I say normally because it’s not there due to the verdict handed down by that crooked son of a bitch Judge George Jefferson. Tyler walks past the empty spot shaking and bowing his head slowly, paying a sort of homage to what once was. He continues on until he reaches the end of the parking lot, then stops. “How in the hell am I going to get to Dallas from here?” questions Tyler to himself. He reaches back and digs in the back pocket of his jumpsuit, pulling out his wallet. He flips it open and peers inside. “Nothing. Not a Goddamn dollar to my name!” grumbles the former CEO rather disgustedly. “How can anyone survive like this?” Tyler ponders his own question for a few seconds while he stands near a street corner. “This just won’t do. A man of my means cannot and should not be made to live like this!” proclaims Tyler. “Something must be done, and quickly. There is no way that Tyler Nelson, the most powerful man in the universe, can be reduced to living like Ned.” Tyler snorts in disgust at the thought of the homeless former PCW wrestler. “Perhaps this is all just a bad dream that I haven’t woken up from yet,” wonders Tyler. “That’s got to be it! It’s just not possible that all this is really happening to me. Hehe...I can’ believe I was getting all worked up over a dream.” Tyler takes in a deep breath and exhales loudly, sighing at the end. A short chuckle at himself and Tyler is on his way, walking down the sidewalk in San Marcos. The streetlights barely illuminate his path as the former CEO shuffles along, one foot bare against the cold pavement due to losing a flip-flop. A chilly wind picks up behind him and causes him to flip his collar up around his neck. “Boy,” says Tyler with his teeth nearly chattering together. “It sure does get cold in dreams. You would think that I would want to fantasize about some tropical place where naked women serve you on the beach and there are no poor people allowed within miles of the well-to-do folks. Instead I’m dreaming about a rat infested hell hole that is home to rednecks like Duke Williams.” Tyler stops dead in his tracks and gets a sober look on his face. “If this is a dream....” Tyler rubs his fingers along his chin. “Then I really didn’t just kick Duke’s ass in that arena.” A rather discouraged expression falls over Tyler’s mug, but is quickly replaced with a devious grin. “I’ve [b]got[/b] to remember to do that when I wake up. That was a simply magnificent display of ass-kicking prowess. That’s what that jobber JJ is in store for on Fusion as well.” As he continues to stroll down the sidewalk, the neighborhood begins to look worse and more rundown. Buildings are vacant and dilapidated, looking as if there is no way that they could support a comfortable lifestyle. There is no other activity on the streets....not really a surprise to Tyler. “What in the hell is this?” says Tyler looking around. “I can’t be seen around here. There are losers and poor people that live in this kind of neighborhood.” A gust of wind comes up from behind Tyler and whips the dry cleaning bag up and over his head, covering his face. “Goddamn it!” shouts Tyler as he struggles to get the garment bag off his face. He is finally successful in freeing his head from the bag, and as he huffs his displeasure he realizes that he is no longer alone. “Who are you?” asks a young child now standing in front of Tyler. The small boy, probably about 8 years of age, has spiked blonde hair which is brown with filth. His young, innocent face is covered in dirt along with his rags for clothes. He looks up at Tyler with a faded glint in his eye. “I’m the CE-...er, what the hell do you care?” snaps Tyler, causing the young boy to hang his head in fear. “This is you, isn’t it?” asks the boy quietly. He raises a piece of newspaper up from his side and into a ray of light. Tyler’s face contorts into a look of anger as he looks at a picture of himself on the front page with a single word emblazoned in bold, black print. “CONVICT?!” Tyler shouts. He snatches the newspaper from the boys hand and crumples it up in his hands. “Where in the hell did you get that you little bastard?” The boy doesn’t answer Tyler, but just hands his head down kicking at the air with his foot. The former CEO becomes enraged at the boys refusal to answer and scoops him up with one arm into the air, raising him to eye level. “I said, where did you get that?” Tyler seethes. The boy looks up with tears in his eyes, then swiftly kicks Tyler in the groin forcing him to drop to his knees and release the boy from his grasp. “You little prick.....” says Tyler in a rather raspy and winded voice. He clutches his groin and grimaces in obvious pain as the boy takes off running into the darkness. Tyler reaches out in vain trying to catch him, but fails miserably. “I’m dreaming....so why in the hell does this hurt so bad?” Tyler is left to wonder about his place in reality as the pain pulses from his crotch throughout the rest of his body. A tear wells up in his left eye as he struggles to his feet, doubled over in pain. He takes several long, deep breaths before standing up straight. “Something isn’t right here,” Tyler says as he picks up the garment bag he was carrying. He dusts it off, but as he finishes he hears several footsteps moving toward him at a rather fast pace. He turns around and squints into the darkness, straining to see what is approaching. Suddenly there are voices heard in the black night. One of them is the one belonging to the young boy that had just kicked Tyler in his manhood. “He’s this way!” directs the young boy. A slew of voices can be heard next, sounding like an angry mob. Tyler squints harder and can finally make out the shadows of several men running his way, led by the boy. “I think my welcome in the slums has run out. Time to leave.” surmises Tyler. He spins around and is just getting ready to take off running when all of a sudden a black Lincoln Towncar with dark tinted windows comes squealing around the corner. It screeches to a halt and a voice comes shouting out from inside the vehicle. “Hurry up and get in!” says the unrecognizable voice. Tyler pauses and tries to see who is in the car, but he cannot make them out due to the darkness and the tinted windows. Tyler is undecided as he debates the validity of the offer. As he does, the footsteps from the angry mob grow closer. “C’mon before they get here!” urges the voice. Tyler shrugs his shoulders and makes a mad dash for the car. The rear door pops open and Tyler dives inside just as the mob reaches where he was standing. The driver of the car punches the gas and the car speeds out of sight. We follow the tail lights the best we can until they disappear into the night. Then the camera fades out.
