In The Attic...

2 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02-01-03 The sounds of a man possessed echo through the shabby little attic. Items and objects are slung over his shoulder as though they were simple rocks, aimed at another stood further behind him. A glint in his eye as he found a small chest, and upon opening it a smile graced his face as though he had seen the gates of Heaven themselves open, beckoning him into their warm and comforting grasp. Stood behind him was another, a worn man, one of a broken mind. The chest came alive with pictures, a thousand and more words bound to a single portrait. Why, if such beautiful works could talk, they would say-… Skitzo: OHHHHHOHHHHHHHHHH FUCK ME!!!!!!!!! Skitzo: MY PORN COLLECTION!!! Skitzo: I thought you lost it? Skitzo: Only that week Cousin Goober came to stay… Skitzo: Cousin Goober…I’ll remember that for when Stro fucks off and I need someone to run with… Behind Skitzo stands the great jobber himself, a man of many talents…most including jobbing…a man that has sweated blood and the ejaculate of the bookers he blew for a push, spent his entire life to gain nothing but $5 and the chance to be a temporary manager of the One Man Gang… Stro: You know SKITZO! The Stro thinks maybe we should learn to WRESTLE a bit, for you have a match on SUNDAY NIGHT ADRENALINE-ADRENALINE-ADRENALINE! Rush Mason is your OPPONENT and THE STRO really thinks-… Skitzo: I think you should shut the fuck up… Stro: But why? You hired me… Skitzo: I hired you to shut the fuck up! Adrenaline can fucking wait, I’m going to pop by Fusion on Friday and say Hi to some people…after…what happened…last week on Adrenaline… Skitzo: Dis week we gon’ kill dat nigga…heh…heh…heh… Skitzo: Hey, Mark Makonie?! Chill…ooooh look at this! Skitzo holds up a roll of film, and blowing a thin layer of dust from the label, makes out the name of the movie printed by the sunlight shining through the web-ridden window above. Skitzo: Blitzo 2??!! I wondered where the fuck this went…oh well, out with the old in with the penis I always say, HA! HA! HA! HA! BOOM! BOOM! Skitzo: That wasn’t funny… Skitzo: So it’s only Stro and us in here, it’s not like tens of people have the opportunity to just look in on what we’re doing and think it’s-…oh…fuck… Stro: Wh-… Skitzo: WHO SAID YOU COULD SPEAK, FUCKER?????? Stro: …………………… Skitzo: In fact, you know something Stro, Maestro…whatever the fuck you’re called. You’re a shit. A big shit. Your website is shit. Your wrestling school is shit. You once tried to engage in cybersex with a Canadian named Byron Calver…YOU’RE A FUCKING WASTE OF SPERM!!!!!!!!! Stro: ………I DON’T HAVE TO TAKE THIS FROM YOU!!! THAT’S IT!!! FIVE BUCKS OR NO FIVE BUCKS…WHEN STRO WEARS A STRAP…SOMEONE’S GONNA-… THUNK! With precise aim, Skitzo lobs the ‘Blitzo 2’ film at Stro, and watches as the reel slices through Stro’s skull, embedding itself into his pulpy purple brain. Collapsing to the ground, another Angel gets its wings as another jobber dies… Skitzo: So what else do we have here… Skitzo: Are you aware you are now a murderer? Skitzo: OOOH!!! SO FUCKING SUE ME! Skitzo: That’s the plan… Rummaging through the chest Skitzo removes rather oblique object, covered in dust and oozing with some kind of scum, looking as though it hasn’t been discovered in a very long time indeed. Skitzo: OH! I FOUND EL DIABLO’S HEAT!!! Wait…what’s this-… Skitzo: A pink slip? Skitzo: What are we even looking for? Skitzo continues rummaging while mumbling to himself…as only he can. Skitzo: DarkStar’s work rate…no that ain’t it………where the hell could it be? Skitzo: WHAT?! Skitzo: Well, Statuz kicks my ass on Sunday and now I’m drawn against Rush, I need some kind of Negro repellant… Skitzo: THIS IS THE FIRST TIME IN YOUR LIFE YOU’VE ENCOUNTERED BLACKS IN WRESTLING!!! Just fucking wrestle them, they’re not a plague… Skitzo: EUREKA!!! Skitzo: John Leslie? Skitzo emerges with an object in hand. Upon closer inspection, it appears to be a dusty old action figure. Cleaning it up, Skitzo finds himself staring eye-to-eye with what can only be described as… Skitzo: My Samuel L. Jackson figurine! Skitzo: It’s a doll… Skitzo: FIGURINE!!! Finally I have the power to defeat two promos from Rush Mason! Skitzo: I wish I knew what channel those things aired on… Skitzo: Are you kidding me? A Samuel L. Jackson doll will NOT beat Rush Mason…he’s a fucking former Continental Champion! Skitzo: So was Scott Naket but look at him now! Skitzo: He’s dead Jim… Skitzo: Meh, who cares…let’s put this plan into action! Kicking the chest back into a pile of miscellaneous junk and hiding Stro’s body behind a mannequin with orifices…Skitzo exits his attic and soon finds themselves on the porch, the radiant sunset beaming across the land, a calm so’ westerly breeze sending chills down Skitzo’s spine as he holds in his left hand the Samuel L. figurine, and in his right a needle… Skitzo: As you can see, the figurine has been dubbed “Rush Mason” on the back with tippex…therefore making it an exact copy of the man himself… Skitzo: You fucking idiot… Skitzo: AND WITH THIS NEEDLE!!! I, SKITZO…SHALL-… Skitzo: Simulate bum sex with a needle and Rush, engage! Skitzo jams the needle into the Samuel doll and forces it into and out of the newly forged hole in the figurine’s posterior…1400 miles away on a charter flight to L.A., Samuel L. Jackson is conversing with Ben Affleck over the success of Changing Lanes when… Ben: So yeah, this bitch has me on a leash man. I can’t rub one off without her running in, perfume squirting and camel stroking telling me that I can’t-… SPLAT! Samuel: OWWW MUTHAFUCKA!!! Ben: What’s up? Samuel: Muthafucka, it-…it felt like sumbawdi stuck sumptin up ma jacksee…ye dig? Ben: No… Samuel: I feel…invaded… Back in Louisiana, Skitzo removes the needle from the figurine’s posterior and looks at it in a funny manner. Skitzo: Hmm…maybe I need more than his name for it to work… Skitzo: Why not just grab something long and hard and hit him with it IN PERSON??!!!?! Skitzo: That’s so…up front and personal… Skitzo: BUT IT’LL HURT A FUCKLOAD MORE THAN THIS PISH VOODOO!!!!!!!!! Skitzo: Hey if it works for him it’ll work for me… Skitzo: Trying to mimic Rush’s voodoo work is like giving Statuz Quo talent and an English Dictionary…it just doesn’t work! Sitting motionlessly for a moment or two, Skitzo stares blankly at the sun until it drops beyond the horizon, completing another cycle. Sighing, Skitzo stands up and looks at the figurine, pursing his lips and taking in a slow breath and again sighing. Skitzo: Ah well…if Voodoo won’t work, I’ll just have to beat his ass down Louisiana style! Skitzo: But the booking says Rush beats you… Skitzo: …………………………………………………………Go fuck yourself… Skitzo: Ohhohoh…that can be arranged! Skitzo: …………Please don’t let me get me… Skitzo: If I had a gun I’d shoot you for that…time for a nap before the Ten Minute Freeview rolls around! HA! HA! HA! HA! BOOM! BOOM! Walking back into his humble abode, Skitzo tosses the Samuel L. Jackson figurine into the nearby Bayou D’Arbonne, and closes his door with authoritah as the scene fades out to black while in the background a plane flies overhead. SMASH! A window bursts open and the body of a man soars through the air, crashing down onto the Bayou as glass fragments fall all around him. Upon closer inspection, the twisted and contorted body of Samuel L. Jackson moans in agony… Samuel: Ohhhhhh…muthafucka! The End!
