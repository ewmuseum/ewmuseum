Revenge Saga Part 21: A SHORT encounter with Wolverine...pt1

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/7/02 Revenge Saga Part 21: A SHORT encounter with Wolverine… It had been almost a week since Fleming last met with The Thunderbolts…a week since he attempted to negotiate a deal with them. Now they had called, wanting to see him in person, they said he was finally going to meet the man who was ‘pulling the strings’ as it were. Fleming gingerly place the receiver of the phone back on the hook, then sat down on his luxurious brown leather chair, resting his feet upon the desk. This was not good. Not good at all. He knew what the meeting was about…he knew what the guy who was ‘pulling the strings’ wanted from him. Fleming had unwitting walked into the trap. Now that Fleming had shown his wealth, his weapon technology and his power, he figured that the guy in charge of the Thunderbolts would want Fleming to become their permanent backer, no questions asked. If whomever it was in charge was to maker the ‘offer’, Fleming was in no position to refuse. For one he didn’t want the US government to know anything about his deal with Russians, and if he refused the offer by the guy who was ‘pulling the strings’ he could guarantee that this information would some how find it’s way to the government officials. Fleming also knew that the guy in charge would do to ‘persuade’ him to finance their little organisation. He had the necessary muscle to force Fleming to do what he wanted. He hated situations like this…being backed up against the wall with limited choices to make. He realised that this was where he differed from John Crichton. John Crichton would chose to fight out of the corner no matter the costs, but Fleming could not and would not…he wasn’t John Crichton. Plus, he knew that surrendering to their power was the only way he might get his revenge…for if he tried to fight he would die…if he didn’t die straight away they would hunt him down and he would die anyway. Crichton was a man who took those chance everyday of his life…and survived…Fleming on the other hand didn’t believe in luck and even if he did he was assume he didn’t have any to speak of. If Fleming had walked into this situation open-eyed, rather than clouded by his inevitable revenge he would have seen this coming…he would have seen a lot of things coming, but revenge was his life now and he sure as hell couldn’t ignore it. He had already made the decision before getting into his black, open-top convertible…he would agree to the madman’s demands…it was the only conceivable option. ************ Even though Logan no longer had his adamantium claws anymore, he was still a tough customer, who could hold his own against the majority of super villain. The new bone claws that began to grow out of his hands when the adamantium was stripped from his body were more than adequate to take out the average super-villain. Through he publicly known codename “Wolverine” he was feared, revered and respected in the super hero and super villain world. They knew of his rage, they knew of his powers, they knew he wasn’t an easy foe. He’d undoubtedly had a hard life. Especially since joining up with the X-Men. With the X-Men he had to fight for his life day in day out. There was only so much one man could take, even if you did have super healing powers. So he took a leave of absence from the X-Men, to get some rest and ‘heal some wounds’. Not too long ago he even had a run in with the ‘devourer of worlds’ Galactus…and survived. Nobody could argue that he had earned this break. As soon as his break was confirmed by Professor X, he packed a few things, then jumped into his jeep and headed for the open road. What next? His luck…all of it bad. No sooner had he left the gates of Professor Xavier’s school of gifted youngsters, did it happen. Wolverine turned the corner to see some guy being led by some sort of handbag. The Handbag was literally dragging the wimpy looking man through the air. Logan put on the breaks as fast as he could, but it made no difference, the wimpy looking man hit the car…and hit the car hard. After the impact he simply slumps down the bonnet of Logan’s jeep and crumples to the floor. Logan sighs, before jumping out of his jeep and walking around to the front of his vehicle, where the wimpy looking man is just lay there, the handbag sat on top of him. Logan: Hey bub, what’s the deal with the handbag? Guy: Uhhh…that’s m..yy..my enchanted handbag. Logan: [Face filling with confusion] Enchanted Handbag? Guy: Y…uhhh….eah…I’m the super hero known as The Handbag Warrior… Logan: Say what now? HBW: [Dragging himself up from the floor] The Handbag Warrior…My enchanted Handbag homes in on villains with the greatest of ease, and dispatches them with mightiest of blows. Logan: [Nodding his head…attempting to stifle his grin] Really? So, why the hell did you mess up my paintwork, bub? [Absently rubbing a scratch upon the bonnet of his jeep] . HBW: It was the bag…it was in full control, trying to find help… Logan: [Eye’s widening] Help? HBW: Yes…my enchanted Handbag found the villain known as John Crichton… Logan: Say no more! Take me to this guy! HBW: [A confused look upon his face] But, who are you? A grin appears upon Logan’s face as he extends his arm out, and jams his fist just before HBW’s face. HBW’s face fills with even more confusion at this action. Within a blink of an eye, Wolverine’s bone claws extend from his fist, startling HBW immensely. HBW cowers back from the claws, screeching like a girl. Wolverine retracts the claws and looks at the cowering HBW with confusion. Wolverine: What the hell kinda super hero are you anyway? HBW: Erm…an extremely effeminate one…You’re Wolverine aren’t you? Wolverine: [Nodding] How did ya guess? HBW: Do I detect a hint of sarcasm there? Wolverine: I should hope so I was layin’ it on pretty thick! Listen boy…or girl…whichever you are, tell me where John Crichton is hiding and I’ll go take him out! HBW: I’ll do better! I’ll show you where he is! Wolverine: [Shaking his head] I only work alone! Besides the way you screech you’ll scare him away before we even get there! [Turning to get back into his jeep] HBW: [Grabbing Wolverine’s arm] No! I’m going with you! Wolverine: No your not kid…this guy is way outta your league! HBW: [Hands on hips and his left foot tapping the floor] It’s because I’m gay isn’t it? Wolverine: [Eyes widening] You’re gay?…aw hell I’m getting my ass outta here! [Getting into his jeep and turning the key in the ignition.] HBW: He’s in the Heart o’ the city motel near… Wolverine: I know where it is! [Pulling away from the curb] …and thanks… ************ Less than an hour after The Handbag Warrior left the motel room, literally running like a girl, Crichton had his bags packed. He wasn’t scared of HBW, but if any other superheroes found out his location he would have one big fight on his hands. He didn’t want to fight any superheroes anymore. He wanted to fight Jordan X Shabazz. Some people would think this running…but the truth was if he tried to fight anymore superheroes any more he would lose. His fight with the Avengers was nothing more than lucky…but the reputation the fight brought was overwhelming to say the least. He wanted no more confrontations with the men and women who saved this world from evil, he simply wanted revenge and to wrestle. Luckily in his case these two objectives collided. He beat Jordan X Shabazz for the title at the last PPV…Gold and Glory…whatever the man believed about him…the truth was HE, not Shabazz was the Television Champion. Yes, he ‘cheated’ to win the title, but getting that gold back was one of the only things that guided his life recently…especially since the death of Aeryn Sun. He one the title by any means necessary…and now it seemed Shabazz had taken the philosophy to heart himself. Since Crichton kicked his ass at the last pay per view, Shabazz had been trying to get another shot at the title…by any means necessary. So far Shabazz and his thugs had jumped him on more than one occasion and they had destroyed his new car. He spent a fortune on that car and Shabazz and his gang of thugs destroyed it and spray painted their logo on it. Shabazz tactics could be argued to be sound…bother your opponent until he gives you another shot at the title…Crichton didn’t agree with those tactics. He believed that Shabazz should be made to get to the back of the line and let the other competitors have their shot at the TV title. However, he wasn’t the one making the decisions and tomorrow night on Tuesday Turmoil, Jordan X Shabazz had his rematch. The question was Would Shabazz try to get the victory by any means necessary? Or would he fight the way he did at Gold and Glory? Either way Crichton realised that he had a tough fight on his hands. To Be Continued…
