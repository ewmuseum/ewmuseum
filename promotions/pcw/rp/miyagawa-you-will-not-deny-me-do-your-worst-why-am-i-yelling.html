Date: Now With start with an excerpt from Tuesday Turmoil...... 'Dave: Miya and Williams are still going at it! Miya has Williams in a headlock. Williams low blows him, breaking the hold. Williams takes off running, then tackles Miya into the steps that lead back toward the ring. Both men continue to roll down the steps. Step after step. Finally, they reach the bottom. Neither man seems able to get back up. Dave: WE'RE OUT OF TIME FANS! WE'LL SEE THIS FRIDAY ON FUSION! Jeff: Nobody won. Duke: Nobody can stand up. Jeff: I wanted to see more blood! Dave: SEE YOU ON FUSION! The PCW copyright logo pops up in the lower left hand corner. Before the scene fades to black, the camera picks up a shot of each man. Of Chase and Boda laying in the ruins of what used to be tables. Out cold. Of Williams and Miyagawa, laying at the end of the steps, unable to get back to their feet. Fade to black!' So that was it, Chase down, Boda down, Miya down and Corey down. The four men fought to a stalemate, in and out of the ring, a gruelling battle. And you'd think they'd have a little respect for each other now, wouldn't you? But this is Chase and Williams we're talking about, the New Breed, the cockiest and most talented athletes in PCW, the newest and most exciting prospects professional wrestling has ever seen. You think they're gonna stop to think about some old-farts clinging desperately to their spots? HELL NO! Chris isn't gonna respect some washed-up Ivan wanna-be whom he OWNED in PCW2, and Corey ain't about to get on one knee and 'give honour' to some goddamn nip. ::Our scene opens up in a car, a limo to be exact. Of course, this being a Corey Williams promo, and Corey Williams being a PCW HEADLINER, you'd probably expect to see Corey Williams laying back in the backseat of the limo, sipping on champagne, enjoying a life of luxury. But because of the fact that he signed a damn contract with Tyler Nelson the day after he and the New Breed had appeared on PCW, using the fans blind love to take it from the hands of Management. Ole' Ty wasn't too happy then, and neither was Corey when he saw the amount of zeroes on his check, or lack there of. So who'd expect to see Corey Williams, the man on everyone's lips, the most controversial athlete in sports-entertainment, to take on limo driving as a second job? Well that's exactly what's he's doing, after all, one becomes addicted to the lifestyle of the rich and famous. And right now, CCW ain't rich, but he is famous, and luckily, one good-looking guy...... not that I like him or anything, at least, not in that way.:: Corey: Miyagawa called me a homosexual....a gay, a fag, a reamer. ::Corey gives a short laugh.:: Corey: I'm surprise he had the balls to say that, after all, your balls were in the mouth of Jeremy Howard at the time... ::Corey sneers into the camera.:: Corey: No, Miyagawa, I don't get off on men, but I do have what you might call a sick fetish. I get off on giving pain Miyagawa, listening to that crowd 'ooh' and 'aah' me as I deliver sickening blow after sickening blow....it turns me on. Especially when the person is an arrogant Jap like yourself. The pain I brought you on Turmoil was just the start of it, just wait until Spring Fling. You and I WILL fight at Spring Fling, whether you want to or not. ::Corey flips a switch on the door of the car, it causes the sound proof divider that separates the front cab of the limo from the passenger seats to close so that Corey may have a little more private conversation with the camera, whom he is speaking through at Miyagawa.:: Corey: And you've tried everything to get out of the match, haven't you? You've tried questioning my sexuality, you've tried to claim that I'm a racist, you've tried drafting yourself to PURE, you tried to end my career, hell, you even went and used that little sob story of yours..... ::Corey puts on a sad face and the fake tears start to come in mock of Miyagawa, complete with over-exaggerated hand gestures.:: Corey: Oh! My wife is dying and I need to face Boda at Spring Fling because he's the only person in the whole entire world who can save her! ::Corey stops fake crying, and the sick smile returns to his face.:: Corey: Well go tell someone who gives a damn Miya, coz I don't buy your little excuse. After all, that's all it is, isn't it? You don't want to face me because you know that, with all you've got going for you, your wife, your money... (Corey shudders)... your fans, even despite alllll that, you'll never have a big fat American c*ck! You'll never be quite white enough, quite good enough, to make it to where I am, PCW HEADLINER. So you thought you'd just quietly slip out of my grasp...... NO F*CKING WAY! You will not deny me Miya! You will not deny me of the pleasure of kicking your ass, of proving in front of millions of fans that there IS a superior race, and that I AM a superior human being, and that you are nothing but Jeremy Howard's bitch. ::Corey pauses to lick his lips.:: Corey: Try what ever you want, do what ever you want, but you will not stop me from having my moment in the spotlight at Spring Fling! ::Corey looks away from the camera and begins to focus on the road once again.:: Corey: Where to Mrs. Anderson? ::Suddenly a head bobs up from Corey's lap and onto the camera. It is none other than blonde dream girl Pamela Anderson wearing Corey's chauffeur's cap.:: Pamela: Just drive around the block a few times, from the looks of things this is going to be a big job, and I DO mean BIG. ::Like I said, he ain't rich but he sure is one good-looking man.:: Corey: Your wish is my command, M'am. ::Corey says this whilst pushing Pamela's head back down. Corey turns and winks at the camera.:: Corey: Homosexual indeed!....... ::Corey's focus returns to driving, whilst he receives pleasure from Pamela Anderson. But even a BJ from Pammy cannot measure up to the pleasure Corey will get from beating up that slanty-eyed freak, Miyagawa. Corey has seen all the excuses, all the plans, nothing has impressed him. No one can stop Corey from obtaining his destiny.... The scene fades out before this becomes too much like a Badge promo.:: !The End!
