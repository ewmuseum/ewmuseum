One Preppy Valet With A Headache & One Scientologist With Some Explaining To Do.

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/04/02 Ellie~!’s Prologue : Um....Like...OWWW!!! I hate Duke Williams, I hate Jordan X Shabazz, I hate their families and I hate every one they like and Xavier is going to kick both their ungrateful asses! I am just a poor innocent valet, I do nothing wrong, I never interfere in matches or be mean to anyone...But still poor little me gets hit on the head by some woman beating backwards redneck who SOME HOW got to b President! This is..Like...SO not a YAY ME moment...I’m lucky to be alive, and if it wasn’t for the excellent treatment available in this new City we moved into...I may be dead...Something I said I’d do if we ever moved to Wisconsin anyway....Maybe my knock to the head effected me but I kinda like it here...YAY HUBBARDVILLE! “OOOOOOOOOOOWWWWWWWWWWWW!!!” Our shot opens up in the Kelly Preston wing of the L Ron Hubbard memorial hospital in the north-west corner of Hubbardville, just off Wehatenicolekidman Avenue. We are shown a bed in a private room where PCW’s number one valet and preppy Commissioneress Eleanor Hall whining softly as she holds her head. Eleanor : “Oooooowwww....Like...Stop hurting!” She slams her hand into her head angrily but that just causes more pain and Ellie~! winces. Eleanor : “Oooowww.....Even that hurts.....Someone give me something to stop my head hurting....Like a new head, but one as good looking as this one...” ........Duke won’t get away with this...Xavier will, like, hit him.....Then hit him again.....And then keep hitting him and I can kick him...And keep kicking him...Yeah...... Ellie~! is wearing a hospital gown and laying on her back, her faces etched in discomfort after taking a Duke Williams chair shot square on the top of the head while she was humiliating Jordan X Shabazz. The top of her forehead has a huge bruise developing that is just visible from under her mane of long, wavy brown hair with blond highlights. She reaches to her left, trying not to move her head too much and grabs a black TV remote control and she aims it at the TV set in the corner of the room and searches through the buttons until she finds the channel number she wants. Eleanor : “Duke Williams can hit me over the head and try to kill me....But he will never make me miss Party of 5...” .......See Duke, you can hit m, you an try to oppress women like you do Scientology people but you will never shop me watching my favourite TV shows...Hey, I just noticed...These gowns are, like, SO unflattering..... She turns it on but the volume has been turned up too loud and the opening theme, “Closer to Three” blasts out at near full volume. EVERYBODY WANTS TO LIVE, LIKE THEY WANNA LI- She hurriedly turns it off as she grabs her head, in the course of which the remote goes flying across the room and hits the floor, the batteries flying out and rolling along the floor until they hit the wall and stop. Eleanor : “Owwww....Damn it! I hate you Duke Williams, I hate you Duke Williams, I hate you Duke Williams! First you deliberately try to put me in a wheelchair just because I’m a woman and you hate us all...But now you made me miss the start of Party of 5!” She reaches out to her side, he arm only making it barely 2 feet over the edge and about 10 away from the remote control but still he keep reaching. Eleanor : “Eugh....” She shuffles over in the bed and reaches further but still she is about 10 feet away from it, a fact that doesn’t seem to deter her as she shuffles across a little bit more and promptly falls off the side of the bed and drops in a shower of sheets and pillows down onto the floor. Eleanor : “Owwwwwwwwwwies!” She holds her head which is throbbing after the jolt and then tarts to smack her fists into the pillows, drumming away at it in anger. ......Eugh....I HATE headaches...They like, really make my head ache........ Eleanor : “Damn it! Damn it! Damn it! I HATE Duke Williams, I HATE Duke Williams ...I..... HATE...... Duke....... WILLIAAAAAAAAAAAMS!” Her own screeching cuts right through her head and she grabs it again, wincing at the combined pain of a chair shot and her own annoyingly screechy yelling. Eleanor : “Where’s my button....Ah...” She starts to crawl through the pile of sheets and blankets she landed within and over her pillows to the small bedside table where she reaches out and presses a small red button before dropping back down onto the pillows. Eleanor : “After going through this I might actually feel sorry for Jordan Shabazz after Xavier and that guy with not much hair and the weird cross tattoo show him what happens when you defy the Ultimate Authority...In fact...I won’t feel sorry for him...I’ll laugh...But only when my head hurts less...Owwww....” “The Panic Button went off in the Hall room!!! Suddenly through the door comes bursting a team of Scientology’s best Doctors and highly skilled Nurses, reacting to Ellie~!’s pressing of her panic button. As the doors fly open Eleanor ducks her head under the pillows and huddles up under the covers. .....You’d think after someone just got hit over the head with a chair they’d be....You know...A little more QUIET? So what if the red button is for if people die or get attacked or something...I, like, fell out of bed....... The Doctors and Nurses look to each other and approach the preppy valet slowly. Doctor : “Um...Miss Hall? You called for us? The red button?” As his words reassure her, she pops her head out from under the pile of covers on the floor and looks nervously out with her pale blue eyes. Eleanor : “I fell out of bed....” The nurses help the preppy valet up and back into bed as she moans at her headache as the Nurses put the tower of huge pillows under her head. As the Doctors and Nurses then leave her, the right hand door s stopped from swinging closed as Xavier walks through them, checking over his shoulder nervously and himself sorting a bruise on the back of his head where he was nailed with a ball bat. Xavier : “Are you okay my little Fuck Ferret? I saw the rush to get in here...” The Scientologist and one of the men responsible for the very city the hospital stands in is wearing one of his black hooded sweatshirts with Gold Patron Meritorious emblazoned on the front on flaming green and purple. With it he has on a pair of black worker-cut jeans with Purple and Green depictions of Scientological runes snaking down the left leg. Eleanor : “He........Hit.........Me.....” As she speaks in a quiet, whiny voice, Xavier is still looking over his shoulder, his bright green eyes wide open as if startled and a cold sweat is breaking on his forehead, matting some of his long auburn hair down. Eleanor : “With.........A.........Chair.....” She sees that Xavier is more focused on what could happen to him then his valet who is lying in the bed, something she doesn’t seem to happy about. .......Okay, good things about being ill...Grapes, Grapes SO rule.....Sympathy, Sympathy rules too...... Eleanor : “Um....Like...Hello? Lavish me with sympathy please...Chair to head....How did it happen by the way? My memory is a little blurred. Kannon gestures to one of the security guards to watch the door before he walks hesitantly into the room, contorting as he walks to look under the tables before taking a seat by his valet’s bed and holding her hand. Kannon’s eyes shift around, wondering how to explain the events of Sabotage. Xavier : “Um...Well...As we held Shabazz up for you....Duke, being the uncultured animal without a shred of moral fibre that he is....Decided that he hasn’t got what it takes to battle the males of the Ultimate Authority so he decided to take it out on you my little Poon Puppy...” He tries to sound as convincing as possible and Ellie~! nods along still moaning softly and tiredly through her headache before turning to Xavier who is running his hand through his hair in relief. Eleanor: “I remember you...Like, being in front of me and then the next thing I remember was the chair, like, SO hitting me on my pretty little head....” The Gold Patron Meritorious thinks on his feet, hoping to try and shift any focus away from his cowardice that caused Ellie~! to b waffled with the chair. Xavier : “Yeah...Er....Jordan was struggling and I...Um...Fell, that’s it, I fell. Unfortunately for you my little Shag Squirrel, that gave Duke a clean shot at you which he took being the low life scum he is....Him and Jordan ‘Even though you do everything for me I’ll stab you in the back’ X ‘Fine, I’ll just turn my back on everything others did for me’ Shabazz!” Ellie~! slowly nods in recognition at Xavier’s response and in a rather laboured mood rolls over to face him and speaks to him in a soft, whiny, sleepy voice. Eleanor : “Like...Pass me that mirror will you....” Kannon turns around and grabs a hand mirror off the table before passing it to her and she holds it up to her face and her jaw drops. Eleanor : “I’m..........Like..........Disfigured!” .....Bruises, like, belong to the highest order of things that suck. They are, like, THERE...And they SO never go away quickly.....Like Duke.... She prods around the bruise on her forehead, wincing every time she touches it. Eleanor : “Um...Like, SO get me a plastic surgeon or something! I can’t go out with THAT on my forehead.....It’s like....EUGH!” Kannon looks closely at the bruise and tries to comfort his valet, almost starting to show some guilt for his part in Eleanor’s injury, ducking the chairshot from Duke Williams knowing that his valet was behind him. Xavier : “Don’t worry my little Fuck Ferret.....Your bruise will be gone in a few days, and if Duke says anything about it they we can just mock his wife...Unfortunately nothing on her face will fade in a few days.” His valet grins at the prospect of this mocking, something she hasn’t done since her constant slurring of Jessica, girlfriend of Jeremy Howard. She then slowly rolls back over and closes her eyes. Xavier : “I’ll leave you to get some sleep, but don’t worry, Duke will pay for his unprovoked, sick, vicious and unjustifiable attack on you....A woman no less.....Me and Mark Makonie will put an end to the inevitable gloating from him and Shabazz...Don’t you wo-” As he rants on Eleanor cuts him off, holding her hand up to him. Eleanor : “Um.....Like....SO trying to sleep here....When I wake up you can show me the video of how it happened....” She fidgets slightly to get comfortable ready for her sleep as Xavier turns pale at the thought of Ellie~! seeing the tape. Xavier : “Um...Yeah....That tape......Er.....About that...I...” Ellie~! groans and mutters to Xavier as the shot starts to fade out. Eleanor : “Trying........To.........Sleep.....” The shot then fades to black on the Scientologist’s worried face, wondering how he can keep the truth from his naive valet. .......Like.......The........End.
