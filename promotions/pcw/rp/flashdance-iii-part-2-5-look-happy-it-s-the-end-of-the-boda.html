Flashdance III - Part 2.5 - Look Happy, it's the End of the Boda World Title Reign

Date: 6/7th April OOC Note – MGB Junkie Chris Chase here, thanks to my parents cutting the power every two minutes, my workrate’s been a little impaired of late. Meh, well…as far as the Matt Good theme for this RP goes…I’m going with the song “Look Happy, it’s the End of the World” off the Underdogs album. The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book One: In the Council of the Immortals…… RP#5 for Spring Fling… Location: On exit from Tucson, Arizona…beside a derelict motor vehicle. Nah, not there. The large forboding Radio tower in the distance. Time: 18:00 Saturday evening, April 6th… Chapter Eight: Flashdance III – Part 2.5 – Look Happy, it’s the End of the Boda World Title Reign. “What? Don’t look at me like that…that speech on Fusion was just fine, apart from when those freaking idiots decided to rain on my parade. But one thing still puzzles me…why the hell did I let Boda and Kannon win?” ::.~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ Friday Fusion was nothing less than confusing, at least from where I stand in the whole manner of things. It began with not so much as a bang, but with a whimper…the promotion of fibreglass, the promotion of the Pay-Per-View…both of which could have been done off of PCW Television. Promoting all these worthless idiots who lucked their way onto a Pay-Per-View event…well, they HAD to get somebody in to do it. Like I’m really interested with that, heck, it’s about as interesting as watching paint dry and grass grow in a Boda promo – and I really don’t need to elaborate on that. My destination awaits me, my destiny awaits me…fact is, I’m far more wrapped up in the blatant self-promotional issues at hand to even worry about what else is going in the federation right now. I’m establishing the new era…flushing away the remnants of the shoddy old, and bringing about a change that’d revolutionalize the way that folks look at the PCW. Come on folks, look happy…it’s time that Boda got tossed off his pedestal like the foul pretender that he is. Where’s Akiyama when you need him? Never before have I been this sure of myself…heck, the production line of Chris Chase action dolls has been tested to the limit ever since I became #1 Contender for crying out loud. Yet, in this infernal world of ours…the same old cliché pops up again…I’ve got to (shudder) prove myself. Like I’ve said a bazillion times this week, Spring Fling marks the kick in the pants that the PCW needs. New beginnings, and if I’m going to be THE New Champion…it’s all well and good that I’ve got to prove myself to be a worthy contender. That’s something that at least 9 out of 10 retarded ass spelunkers recognise officially…and to meet the cretins’ demands, I must supply, supply, supply. Sheesh, and who said that economics was lame? I think that was me….:: “You let two schmucks of that magnitude, who just happen to be your most reviled enemies over the past year, win? Were you completely out of your mind, or did that little Therapy session conducted by the little Japanese freak put you into a drowsy state from which you couldn’t do sod all about it? In other words Chris, you screwed up!” “Oh please, screwing up is actually one of the things that I don’t do very well…” ::.’Screwed up’ – see also: messed up, fouled up, royally screwed up, Miyagawa, Boda…Doctor Healy…Chuck E. Cheese. Dustin’s law of Morondom… Let’s face it, I rarely screw up…even if I do screw up, I’ve usually got a good reason or excuse as to why I’ve screwed up. Last night was no exception really, there were a great many things that I could blame…for example, I could blame the very fact that it was indeed, my first night as a Special Guest Referee…or the fact that I’m more of a wrestler rather than a referee, and I prefer to break or bend rules as opposed to enforcing them. A referee’s job isn’t exactly hard to do, but it’s hella tedious. I might as well just set up a chair in the middle of the ring and applauded all the carnage going all around me…occasionally taking a moment to send another wrestler on his way back to his lockerroom. The fact that I allowed two of my greatest enemies in the past year to win is something that I’ll be beating myself up for, for most of this next year. I had the chance to rub in the fact that Boda and Kannon were royally punked out by Jeremy Howard on his lonesome…but I blew my chance. Meh…there’s always next year…or tomorrow… It’s the eve of Spring Fling, Saturday April 6th 2002. A day prior to the proverbial popping of my PCW World Title cherry. The sun’s setting on a desert background…the winds whistling around my ears as I stand on the gantry of a radio tower, my hair floating in the breeze as I look to the distance. Below the tower is my transport, and a figure who is scrambling around…chasing some small object on the deserted road beside the radio tower…until he stumbles into a lamp post. Today sees the return of Dustin Downey to Chris Chase promotions, the real Dustin Downey as prefered to some idiotic reproduction in a dream. Dream folk are never like their real life counterparts, Boda should know that for one. Chris Chase isn’t the type of guy to f**k a goat…and exactly why was Boda dreaming about Beastality publicly for? I guess we can only guess. To my right, is Serenity Downey…cousin to the unconcious fella below…and strangely smart for a Downey….:: Dustin: ~(In the Distance)~ WHOOOO! The Road-runner! Run from the Coyote you bastard…RUN! RUN! RUN! RUN, DAMNIT! Don’t you sit there just looking at me like that, RUN! Damnit…I’m coming after you, I’M THE WILY COYOTE!! SMACK!! Dustin: ~(In the Distance)~ Jeez…who put this lamp post right in the middle of this freaking desert… ::. Of course…I sigh. Dustin’s moronic antics have been missed by a lot of folk ever since he was canned for the second time in his career. Now he’s back under PCW contract, exactly what implications will that mean for yours truly? With the return of Snow and Corey Williams imminent, I can hardly say that New Breed’s dead…there’s just too great a many things to think about. Anywho, as I sigh, slapping my forehead as per usual…there’s a tap on my shoulder. The type of tap on the shoulder that I’d usually shrug off as if it were some jobber by the name of Tim friggin’ Walden…but of course…like that wack fellow Canadian, the tapping is ever persistent… There always has to be someone wanting my attention…whether it’s the media, or my superiors, or my allies, or my opponents…there’s always someone. Not that I don’t appreciate the constant requirement of Chris Chase in their daily supplement, I mean, who wouldn’t want to have the single most spectacular individual that the world has ever laid its beady eyes upon as an ally, an opponent, an employee or a subject? It just gets a little, well, frustrating that I can never have any time to myself…to ponder what ails the world other than the superficial ‘Reign’ of one Chris Sloboda. That jackass has had time, at least a month of it, to make anything more of himself…but he’s just ended up being the generic big man…there for sluggish power and sluggish power alone. Even Miyagawa can’t prevent that from continuing, after all, he lost the World Title to Boda straight after winning it. Only Chris Chase, the man who thwarted Boda time and time again, last year…can prevent Boda from continuing that ponderous and downright inane trend that would only result in the PCW going under… Chris Chase… The Hero… Interrupted by a Downey family member…tch….:: Serenity: You’re in an unusually thoughtful and ponderous mood, are you sure that you’re not suffering from any permanent damage caused by that freakish Miya-schmuck? Sheesh, if his wife wasn’t pregnant, I’d be the first woman to take exception to her and her poor choice in men. Damn Japo-American schmucks, they’re beginning to become the wart on the toe of the true heroes in this Anti-Boda conflict…aren’t they, Mister Chase? Promo Breaks – Ideal when a Handler is dying of thirst… ::.Yes, the Anti-Boda conflict…a war that’s been raging on and on ever since Boda first stepped into the PCW itself…ages ago…way before Chris Chase was ever heard of. The jobber Boda, the younger, perhaps faster version of his pondering self. At least then, he caught on to his foul ways, and attempted to change, becoming ‘Hardknocks’ Chris Allen…possibly an all too embarrassing version of himself. Maybe I’m just opening doors that should have remained locked ever since they were closed. Besides, it’s time to move on…to change…despite the return of an old friend to the foray… Fourteen hours on from the initial incident at the radio tower, which in any case is a break from the tiring issue of being on the road, thanks to the numerous pit stops made by my idiot of a former Tag partner…we haven’t got any closer to our final destination. I say our regretfully…somehow, Dustin managed to arrange for us to pick up half a dozen nuns, two gopher hunters and a hitchhiking strolling Minstrel who seems a little too familiar. A gas station on the outskirts of a city, a place to rest…for the thirtieth time in two hours. Dustin’s weak bladder must be playing up more than usual…I think to myself as I lean against a phone box, watching the attendant fill up the petrol tank to the rental van that Serenity had managed to arrange for as our lift. To my right, kids beat each other up…imitating the wrestling styles of three familiar folk…as Serenity approaches me with a frown on her face….:: Kid #1: YOU WILL DO THE JOB! And you can Justify…that! Serenity: Hey, don’t get angry…at least they haven’t caught wind that you’re actually here, in some desolate little gas station. These schmucks aren’t going to figure that some famous Professional wrestler would visit some kind of place like this…after all, it isn’t your kind of place now, is it, Mister Messiah? Huh? Huh? Chris: Heh…you’re smart for a Downey…normally it’d take Dustin two days to figure that one out…and I’m probably one of the more frustratingly difficult people to figure out…heck, I don’t even think that I’ve got myself figured out. But you’re right, this place is no place for an athlete of my calibur…of my moral standing and of my stature. At least I’m being idolized in some form of another by these kids…these great children who worship great wrestlers…namely a man by the name of… Kid #2: GAAAAAAAAAAAAH! I WILL KILL YOU!! Chris: ~(Sighs)~ Did I neglect to mention that this place sucks more ass than Miya-Jobber? ::.Agitated, I smack the wall with my fist…only to be answered by a shout from the owner of the gas station from inside the building itself. Watching the kids act out a match scheduled for tonight’s PCW Spring Fling event with keen interest…I sigh, mostly at the fact that the kid acting out as Miyagawa has the kid who was unluckily dumped with the Boda moniker in an imitation of the Therapy whilst the little clone of yours truly just stands there…waiting for his chance to grab either of the two, and job them with much ado. Serenity elbows me in the ribs, which really aren’t that sore from that Boda assault on Tuesday. Frowning, I glare at her….:: Chris: So I was wrong! Big frickin’ deal…I’m going to win tonight, I can just… Serenity: ~(Cutting in)~ Feel it? Chris: Sure, I can feel it…and heck, I know it. For I’m Chris Chase, ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase, leader of High Impact…current leader of New Breed…leader of all that surround me. I set the trends, they follow. I punk Boda out…they shun him. I beat Miyagawa…they ignore him too. But darn it, I can’t help the feeling that I’m being oh so cliché right now. Come on, beating the pulp out of these guys is what I’m going to be doing anyway, so why brag about it? Couldn’t I just brag about my impeccable record over Xavier Kannon, the fact that I’ve won a greater percentage of my matches than Boda has…and the fact that I’m going to be rebuilding a federation around myself, very much in a similar way that the Reverend was planning to do way back in PCW2 before I was canned… ::. And as I ramble on again aimlessly…there’s one other distraction that’ll prove unavoidable for you folk….:: Boda Kid: Err…ROOOOOOOOAR! Darn, where’s a catchphrase when I need one… Chase Kid: Oh for my sake kiddo…take this you freaking jobber! Boda: ARGH! YOU MADE ME BLEED YOU BASTARD!! ::. Ideas to be continued in the next promotion. And now, Fade to White….::
