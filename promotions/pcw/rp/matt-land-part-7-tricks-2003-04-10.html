Matt Land, Part 7- Tricks

10 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/10/03 “You know you can’t keep this up Mrs. Clarkson,” Matt tells her, his voice wrought with contempt, “You are going to give me the information on your father soon.” Across the steel table sits a torn dress Sarah Clarkson, staring through bloodshot black eyes, she can’t help but wonder how long this is going to continue. Pushing her long black hair out of her face with her free hand, she leans back in the metal chair and begins to quietly sob. Matt leans back and crosses his arms over his barrel of a chest, making his grey suit sway like a sail in the wind. Waving his hand, he brings the petite girls attention back to him. Matt is being lax with her today, coming off of his master plan at Turmoil, it put him in a rather good mood and over the past few days he has been able to keep it up. “I know you want to tell me Sarah and once you do everything is going to be better I promise,” he calmly tells her, shooting a smile showing off his perfect white teeth, trying to let her know that he is sincere and when she gives him all the information he wants, everything will be better. “The only reason you are keeping me alive is because of the information I have. Once I tell you, you are going to kill me,” she quietly says, staring down at her legs, looking at the destruction that is her dress. The past few weeks have been hell, lack of sleep, lack of food, and worst of all the beatings. She is enduring them all though and the constant pressure to break hasn’t hit her yet. She is holding strong and it was going to take a lot more to sell out her life and her fathers. “I am not going to kill you Sarah, you are too vital to the success of my operation. You hold all the keys and I am going to be the one to unlock all the doors,” he says, standing up and pacing around the small interrogation room. His black dress shoes clicking against the stone floor, “You see Sarah; I have taken a liking to you. I may have lost my temper with you a few times, but you are a beautiful girl and I want this to work out for both of us.” He is using the oldest trick in the book, trying to butter her up and make her like him. “You see Sarah, under different circumstances. I could see us together, but you were my objective and that is one thing I never mess around with,” he tells her, shooting another smile her direction. Trying her hardest not to, she gives him a smile, revealing her blood soaked teeth. This is the man that has caused the blood to flow from her mouth so cleanly and now she is giving him a smile. She almost couldn’t believe it herself. After, everything he has done to her, showing his human side opened a new world to her. She is attracted to the man that she was growing to hate and now against her better judgment she is considering helping him. “This is the situation we were placed in and I don’t think you have handled it bloody well,” she responds, trying to be strong and not let him know that she is growing soft. She loves her father and doesn’t want to give him up, but the ex-Ranger is becoming too much for her. He is a trained professional in situations like this and she knew she couldn’t hold out forever. Now might just be the right time to release the information and quite possible with his new found likening for her he might spare her life. “You have to understand Sarah, I didn’t want to do this, but they placed me on it because I am the best at what I do,” he says kindly. “I am going to tell you something against my better judgment. I am going to tell you my name.” This is a big risk in the human game of chess, but he is going to tell her to try to convince her anyways. It doesn’t matter much to him anyways, because when it is all said and done, she is going to just be another victim. A nameless face that blurs in with the others that he killed before her. It is his way of life, his survival and he will continue to do so. Christian MacManus more commonly known as Matt Jackson is trained to do this, he knows nothing else. “Really? I didn’t think you would be able to do that. Wouldn’t that make me liable to ID you later?” She questions, happy that he is finally going to show her some type of trust. The pieces are finally starting to fall into place and the ghost is going to get exactly what he wants by the next time he comes in here. “Yes, I feel that I can finally trust you. I am Christian MacManus,” he tells her, trying to hide that fact that it is no longer his name. Then again, he can’t be sure it is even his real name because he has had so many names and allies he can’t even remember. His birth name could be anything he wants it to be. He knows the ways to change that and without parents or family to say something different he knows that he is safe. Walking beside her, he gives her a small smile and gently kisses her on the lips. Allowing his tongue to feel the cuts and taste the blood that he produced to his objective. “I am going to go handle some other business. I will be back later with some food.” He brushes his brown hair back and lets a stone look return to his brown eyes. Giving her one last quick kiss on the lips, he makes his way toward the steel door and walks out. Turning off the lights to let her get some well deserved sleep. “I can’t believe she bought it. Soon she will end up like Tommy and will be out of my hair once and for all,” he tells himself aloud, licking her blood from his lips. A smile crosses his face as he looks across the Washington, DC warehouse to the body of Tommy. “Time to get rid of you.”
