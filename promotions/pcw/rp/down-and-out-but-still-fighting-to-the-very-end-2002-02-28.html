Down and out, but still fighting to the very end!

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-28-02 It's time. It's time for the Krayzie One to get his son back from the evil occult that holds him hostage from his rightful parents. But how? The last time anyone seen Jeremy Howard, he'd been knocked unconscious from the white van slamming into the side of his black BMW X5. How is the Krazed Lunatic going to overcome the odds against fifty men that believe in evil beings, when he's been knocked out cold? That powerful question needs an answer. And, to that, the scene fades into a close up of the side of the white van. By now, its late in the day, with the sun beginning to set. Suddenly, the door on the side of the white van opens. There, six men, all dressed in black clothing, pull the Krazed Lunatic from within the van. Jeremy, bound behind the back by rope, tries to fight back, to no avail. Jeremy's feet land on the ground. Mysterious Person One: You're coming with us. Our leader wants to see you. Jeremy spits on the man's face, creating anger within the body of the mysterious man that was just speaking. A grin forms on Jeremy's face. Jeremy: Your leader can go and f^ck himself. A different mysterious man walks up to Jeremy and sends his fist into the stomach of the Krayzie One. Mysterious Person Two: That'll be enough of that. Anymore outbursts and we take you for a swim in the lake first before we see the Higher Being. Jeremy stares at the other mysterious man, a glazed look coming over his eyes. This man's threats toward the Krazed Lunatic don't faze him. Threats don't faze Jeremy Howard. This man is a twenty one year old wrestler in Primetime Championship Wrestling. He's used to idle threats that most opponents, like Drake Maxwell, lose the balls to back up once they step toe to toe with the Krayzie One. Jeremy: Go ahead, keep on threatening me. Don't think for a second that I'm just going to let you drag me into your little get together without having my say. Mysterious Person Two: Just shut up! We'll sacrifice you, like we are that baby, if you get out of line. Sacrifice a baby? It might be quick to jump to conclusions, but could this man be talking about Jeremy's two year old son, Troy? It's a widely known fact that the Krazed Lunatic is trying to get his son back. Could this evil occult be trying to rid themselves of any evidence of having the former two-time World Champions son? It's a good possibility. But, they'd better hope they don't set off that firey rage within the soul of Jeremy Howard, or death might be there best option. Jeremy: You're just like this guy I know, Drake Maxwell. Mysterious Person Two: Oh yeah? Jeremy knods his head, 'yes', to the mysterious man while the grin resurfaces on his face. Jeremy: You both use threats like there an appendix. They're there, but have no use. Crack! Obviously a different mysterious person of the six grew tired of Jeremy's backtalking and chose to crack him over the head with a crowbar. The Krayzie One's head falls forward, unconscious. All six of the mysterious people, that took him from his black BMW, grab him by a limb or elsewhere and drag him toward the old church that begins to come into view. Suddenly, the scene cuts to awhile later in the night. The sun has gone down, making visibility hard. Moments later, the Krayzie One comes into view, sitting all alone in a dark room. Each arm is bound to an arm on a chair, with him also being unable to use his legs. A single window sits to the left of the Krayzie One's position in the room. Jeremy can see fire. Jeremy: (talking under his breathe to himself) This doesn't look good. Jeremy watches out the window some more, trying to take in as much information as he possibly can. Jeremy: (talking under his breathe to himself) What have I got myself into now? Jeremy pauses, still watching out of the window. Its at this point, as the Krayzie One sees a cross burning within the fire, that his raw emotions begin to kick in. The same raw emotions that often put people in the hospital with bodily injuries. Jeremy: Whatever it is, I have to get out of it. I have a son that wants to his father win the Continental title this Friday at Fusion. And, by God, he's going to see it! All of the sudden, as Jeremy finishes his sentence, the door into the dark room bursts open. Standing in the doorway, are a horde of men. With it being so dark, the Krayzie One can hardly see all of the men that stand there. But, he does notice one of them from earlier. It's the one he was arguing with before he was knocked out cold from the crowbar. Jeremy: I suppose you've come back to blast my head with a crowbar again? The mysterious man from before shakes his head, 'no'. He seems unamused by Jeremy's direct statement. The Mysterious Man From Before: When you get done acting like an immature asshole, let me know. I've got a few things to talk with you about. Jeremy flexes his muscles, trying to break free of the ropes that lock him to the chair. The Mysterious Man: I don't think that's going to work. They use that kind of rope in the military. It's almost unbreakable. Jeremy: But your face isn't. The Mysterious Man: Alright. Forget it. I won't talk to you about your son. The mere mention of Jeremy's son clicks something within the soul of Jeremy Howard. It's obvious this man knows something about Troy. Just how much does he know, though? Jeremy: What about my son? The Mysterious Man: Let's just say, that if you hope to even gain a glimpse of your precious son before I sacrifice him, you'd better keep your mouth shut and stop being such an arrogant nuisance. Jeremy snaps. His anger takes so control of him that he snaps the wooden chair into pieces. The adrenaline of this man is sky high. Men, all clad in the same black garb, rush into the room and try to restrain the Krazed Lunatic. It's hard for Jeremy to fight back with all of the ropes that are still tied around him. But, nevertheless, the Krayzie One fights these men with everything he has. Jeremy: I'll get to you, you motherf^cker! Crack! Again, Jeremy's dropped to his knees because of a crowbar shot to the back of the head. This time though, is different. No crowbar is going to knock Jeremy Howard out cold. Jeremy drops to his knees, dazed and confused. A number of men grab him, lifting Jeremy to his feet. The Krazed Lunatic is hardly able to stand. The mysterious man from before walks to just before where Jeremy stands. The Mysterious Man: You obviously don't have a clue as to who I am. Jeremy's head just falls forward. Any normal person would have been knocked unconscious. If this would have been Chet King or Drake Maxwell, they would be out cold. If this were Snow, he'd be running for his life, leaving his kid to suffer a fate far worse than anything else. Not Jeremy Howard. The Krayzie One is fighting the pain with everything he has. He tries with everything he has to lift his head, getting it halfway up. The mysterious man grabs him by the jaw. The Mysterious Man: I'm Keith Lipowitz! I'm Troy's foster father. Not that no good for nothing whore wife of mine. I am! He's mine. With what little strength he can muster, Jeremy shakes his head, 'no'. Keith Lipowitz: You don't think so? The mysterious man that was just found out to be the Keith Lipowitz, that Jeremy was looking, turns and looks to the faces of a few of his men. Then back to Jeremy. Keith Lipowitz: You might be his biological father, but you won't be nothing if I sacrifice you too! You and your son can die together..... Jeremy's head falls again, as a number of men begin to drag him out of the dark room and seemingly toward the circle of fire much like what was seen before. Just why does this Keith Lipowitz want to sacrifice the baby boy that he called his foster son? That's a question that might not ever get answer. That is, because an ever bigger question needs to be answered. How in God's name is Jeremy Howard going to get out of this bit of trouble that he's gotten himself into and save his son? Hopefully Jeremy knows, because as the scene fades out, the Krazed Lunatic is being dragged to imminent doom..... to black!
