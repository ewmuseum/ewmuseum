Publicity Stunts Prologue: An agents work is never done...

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/6/03 “What in the name of Greek Buggery…happened?” Blind Rage asked, slamming his fist down hard upon a very expensive looking table – effectively smashing it into two. Lusty Linda’s eyes widened at the damage and as soon as the realisation hit her, she stomped over to Blind Rage and stood face to face with him. “Would you mind NOT destroying everything in site you big stupid oaf?” She yelled, bending down in an attempt to retain some of the valuables from the destroyed table. Blind Rage visibly grew angrier. “I’ll show you oaf…” he said, pausing immediately after the words left his mouth. “What the hell is an oaf anyway?” Lusty Linda shot him a look as she tried to piece together a smashed ornamental figurine. “I don’t know,” She replied, “What were you saying again?” Blind Rage paused for a second, his eyes rolled upwards, trying to remember his angry words from just a few short seconds ago. “What the hell happened…on Turmoil…with the superheroes?” Linda stood up, with all of the figurine fragments in her hand, and shot him a puzzled look. “What superheroes?” “Two other guys pretended to be superheroes and helped out one of the ring crew.” Warrior explained, not taking his eyes off the television. Linda glanced to her left at the couch, which had been strategically placed directly in front of the television, for watching her soaps. “How did you know they were pretending?” Warrior looked half turned, careful not to take his eyes away from the TV. “Well we’re the only superheroes in PCW,” he said, pausing slightly to shoot Linda his very own version of the evil eye. “Aren’t we?” Blind Rage took to staring at Linda in an accusing manner. Linda looked at both of them innocently. “I think so…” Blind Rage’s eyes widen with anger once again. “What do you mean, you think so?” he bellowed, “You better damn well make sure.” Linda held her hands out, almost in a plea to Blind Rage to trust her. “I was in a very intense meeting with the PCW chairman the other day and he assured me that you’re careers were safe within the PCW and that you will be the lead contenders to the Tag Titles.” Warrior developed a confused look. “What intense meeting? Shouldn’t we have been involved in the meeting?” he said, pausing slightly. “Well damn! I don’t think I’m getting my memos.”“What meeting?” Blind Rage asked, ignoring Warrior’s comments completely. “I had a top level meeting the other day with the chairman of PCW. He wanted to see if I was adequately maintaining my assets.” Blind Rage sighed. “By assets, I assume you mean…breasts?” Linda slowly nodded. “And that’s when we delved into rigorous discussions about you’re future in the PCW and how you would soon be PCW tag team champions.” Blind Rage sighed again. “Meaning you slept with him to ensure that we get title shot?” Linda gasped in shock. “How dare you! I wouldn’t sleep with anyone to further your careers!” Linda declared, she felt the eyes of the superhero tag team glare at here unconvinced of this statement. “Well not again anyway.” “So what did you do?” Warrior asked, “Just give him head then?” Linda nodded. “Yeah. Nothing major. He didn’t mention anything about any other superheroes either, although there are undoubtedly a minority of superheroes jumping on the band wagon since our success hit the new stands.” Warrior developed a puzzled expression. “What success?” Blind Rage sighed and tapped his tag partner on the head lightly, sending him flying off the couch, only stopping as he hit the wall. “We’ve been successful,” he replied, taking Warrior’s place on the couch, “It’s just that you spend most of the time unconscious, instead of actually witnessing the success first hand.” “Yeah, during my ‘in-depth’ discussion with the PCW chairman the other day I managed to secure Justice Inc a Tag Title shot on next the next edition of Tuesday Turmoil.” Blind Rage automatically stands up and jams his hand up in the air in excitement, obviously forgetting his height and strength. His hand soundly creates a hole in the roof and the falls down onto the floor, narrowly missing Linda. “I swear, if you break just one more thing in this house, I will break your face!” Linda demands, forcing Blind Rage to cower back slightly. “I’m sorry. I’m just excited that we’ve actually got a shot at the tag titles and the match seems to be fait…how can we not win?” Blind Rage replied. Following his statement, Rage looked at Linda, Linda looked back at him and then they both looked over at Warrior who was still uncomfortably propped up against the wall. “I’m so NOT tagging that idiot in!” He added, as he retook his position on the couch. Linda smiled at the comments, grabbed a large, A4 wad of papers from her only remaining table and sat beside Blind Rage on the couch. Obviously, a little too close for him, as the mammoth superhero shifts an inch or so away from her. “So leading up to Turmoil, I’ve organised a few publicity stunts.” Linda declared; leaving the heroic tag team stunned slightly. “Publicity stunts?” both Blind Rage and Warrior enquire; almost in unison. “Yes. Tomorrow, Warrior will teach the students of Willmore High, how to defend themselves against bears…they seem to be having a mass problem with them for some reason.” Linda said, sifting through some more of her notes. “ And you Blind Rage will test the durability of demolition balls.” Blind Rage developed a confused look. “Demolition balls? How exactly will I do that?” “Well they will swing them at you, obviously, continually, until one of them breaks or until…erm…well…you break.” “WHAT?” “Exciting isn’t it?” Blind Rage then got angry, again, and so lunged forward in an attempt to strange his manager / PA. “Why I oughta…” However, Warrior manages to step in the way. “You can’t do that?” Blind Rage developed a confused expression and Linda’s scared expression, morphed into a smug smile. “Yeah, listen to your partner here…” she said. “And why not?” Blind Rage asked. “Because I want to do it!” he replied, lunging in for the kill. Linda however, managed to slip underneath Warrior’s agile body and then made her way to the door of her apartment. “After all the men I’ve slept with for you two!” The chase continued.
