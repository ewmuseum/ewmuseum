The Wrong Schmoopie (Part Two)

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/03/03 The convertible, once silver, now mud stained Mercedes Jade had supplied so graciously to Lacuna (However, Lacuna believed she paid for it herself anyway) slowly ground to a halt on the wet gravel outside of her American home with a skidding crunch. Giggling from the two women was clearly audible above the roar of the engine as they sat in the luscious leather interior for a minute. They had just returned from the incident at the Carnival, and the spirits of both women were as high as a pair of kites in the sky. “Did you…”Lacuna begins, but she collapses into a fit of laughter “Did you see the look on their faces? Sheer brilliance! We should do this more often!” Lacuna was clearly hyped; the adrenaline flowed through her body still as the event failed to settle into her somewhat preoccupied mind. It had been a long time since she had enjoyed herself that much, and it was going to be her only topic of conversation for a while. Back in the car, Jade smiled to herself. Her plan was working and Lacuna was finally opening up to her a little. It was how friends should be. They would now start swapping fashion tips and the like, just as true friends should. She had even succeeded in getting Lacuna to reach into her pocket a little and spend some of that money she cherished so damn much. “Yeah, it was fun. Even if I had to nearly break my arm in the process” Jade rubbed at her bicep, which had taken a battering from the strength of the arm wrestling carnie. “Oh stop moaning, I brought you a present to make up for it didn’t I?” Indeed she did, a cute little hipflask which Jade had not hesitated to accept while pointing out what brand of Vodka she wanted it filling with. It wasn’t exactly cheap either. Jade remembered about this, and removed the little brown leather container from her pocket and took a deep swig, swallowing noisily and offering the bottle to Lacuna who shook her head politely. They climbed out of the car and Lacuna flicked the key at it, the lights flashing to recognise that it had been locked. The top lifted itself up and rolled over, covering the insides neatly as they walked towards the front door of the house. “You might want to get that cleaned in the morning” mentioned Jade, who gestured towards the mud coated sides with a loose arm “Why wait until the morning?” questioned Lacuna, with a genuine surprise to her voice “There are so many things you need to learn about around here1” “Not before I teach you the art of living, ‘Cuna” Jade giggled slightly as the front doors swung open and out three of Lacuna’s black-clad servants, two carrying huge spotlights and trailing an extension lead, the third carrying equipment for car-cleaning. ”That’s amazing! They just do that without you asking?” “Well, that what I pay them for” Lacuna smiled wryly. ”At least, that’s the principle of it. You have to keep the well trained though, they tend to slip a lot” stated Lacuna matter-of-factly as they entered the hallway of the grand house. Brook was on hand to remove the ladies’ coats as usual. His distaste in the time and Lacuna’s attire was obvious to all. “So you decided to come home then, Lacuna? I hope that thing didn’t stop you enjoying yourself” by ‘that thing’ he meant Jade, and emphasised it by pointing an arm at Jade, who scowled back evilly. “Yes Brook, I have had a good time, and it was all down to my friend Jade. Now would you instruct Brown to bring beer and snack foods to the pool house as soon as possible? Oh, and bring Quoth along, with a fresh supply of eyeballs. I am sure Jade would like to witness the feeding of the raven” Tonight, Lacuna was not in the mood to fool about with a stuck up Brook. She knew his intentions were good, and that he just missed the old her, but the old her was dieing, and he needed to accept it. She was a new woman. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - The pool house, one hour later. The pool was empty, or at least in terms of water. The tiled floor was coated in a litter of beer cans and crisp packets, as Lacuna and Jade lat flat on their backs staring at the sky for the second time in as many days. The ceiling was glass and they could see through to the beauty of the pale glowing full moon that illuminated the grey clouds with an eerie blue-ish light. Jade sat up suddenly holding her abdomen and groaning. Lacuna looked up with concern, the black sunglasses on her caramel face slid down her nose. Jade sat up in a heaped sitting position. Lacuna dives up and Jade lifts her hand telling her to stop. Jade smiles briefly before groaning a large belch out of her mouth. Lacuna looks at Jade with distaste before they both fall back into that familiar pattern of giggles once again. “Your disgusting, you know that?” said Lacuna through her giggles, or rather, tried to say lacuna through her giggles but the large amounts of alcohol in her system converted it into one long slur, strangely enough, Jade seemed to understand. “I’m not, but, okay, I am” “Don’t speak like that, I’m pissed, I don’t understand things that long” Lacuna spat out, quite literally, as she coated the tiles in a layer of spittle. “I think you should go and fight Tessa drunk, it would be so funny!” she giggle furiously, but Lacuna becomes stern, her face becoming steely and plain. “I don’t want to face Tessa” 'You want to do a bunk? Yeah that'd be fun...lets go to Britain!' Jade said the most stupid idea. But was easily misconceived mind you. The three feet of beer cans and crisp packets are looking mighty good to Jade as she looks over the edge of the pool. 'Water looks nice at this time of day!' Jade enthused. Lacuna dived up. 'Jade no!' Jade had already ran across the diving board and into the empty pool. The clanging of beer cans and crisp packets fill the room. Jade hums loudly. Lacuna laughs heavily and wanders over to the pool, sitting down on its edge. Jade was lying face down in the bounty of cans and crisp packets, still humming loudly. “What –are- you like, you stupid fool!” “Hmmm Hm Hmmm Hmmm” “ I meant it you know, I don’t want to face Tessa” she was becoming moody again, maybe it was the beer talking. “It’s the wrong Schmoopie” “Why do you keep saying that?” “Because I want Storm, not Tessa” Jade's lips quiver together. Chris Storm was a good friend like Lacuna and she didn't want to be caught in the middle of that! She tries to change the subject albeit rather unsuccessfully. 'Lacuna you are so obsessed.' 'Am not!' Lacuna replied. 'Are too' Jade fired back a quick reply. 'Am not! Am not!' Lacuna was being childish but Jade could do that just as well. 'Are to! Are to! Are tooooooo!!' Jade places her fingers in her ears and does a quick round of; 'La La La La' to herself. “I know I’m drunk, but I really think I mean this. He beat us, him and that Zero guy.” 'You need to stop dwelling on the past and look towards the exciting future.' Jade ordered Lacuna. 'Then you will become everything you wanted and more OK sweety?' Jade scoots Lacuna up a bit on the sun lounge and rested her head against lacuna's shoulder. Maybe she was right. Maybe she didn’t need to dwell on the past. She didn’t need to destroy Storm, she would destroy Tessa instead. It sounded funny now, but all she wanted was Scmoopie blood, and Tessa could pay as well as Storm ever could.
