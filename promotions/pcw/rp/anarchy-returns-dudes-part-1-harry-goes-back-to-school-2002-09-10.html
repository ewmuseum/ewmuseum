Anarchy Returns, Dudes - Part 1 - Harry Goes Back To School

10 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 10, 2002 ['Heavy Metal' Harry looked up the word 'Anarchy' in the dictionary and came up with: '1. The complete absence of government and law. 2. A condition of disorder or confusion'. All that really did to the Heavy Metal One was confuse him even further. As he sits in between The Olsen Twins, holding Websters dictionary in his lap his bloodshot eyes need answers] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I don't get it, man! [The scene zooms out, showing that Harry is participating in a classroom with many other 16 year olds who are at their learning facility. The young teenage faces look back at Harry with curiousity, disgust, and confusion. The one totally feeling bogus about it all is the old, fat woman with her gigantic floral patterned dress on. Through his double thick spectables she gives Harry a look of pure evil] Teacher: What don't you get, sir!! [Harry shrugs] Harry: Anarcy, man. I don't get it. Teacher: We are talking about the Emancipation Proclamation, sir! [Harry looks up from the dictionary looking quite lost] Harry: Premature Ejaculation, man? [The teachers face contorts in absolute horror. She can't believe that has spilled out of this mans mouth. The other kids in the classroom erupt in laughter. Mary-Kate and Ashley laugh loudly, seeming proud of their boyfriend. Harry just looks lost, not knowing what he's really said] Teacher: SIR!! It is a travesty that you are in my classroom. I gave you a chance here, and now YOU MUST LEAVE!! [Harry slams the dictionary looking rather sad] Harry: I just wanted to spend a day learning with my chicks, man. [The teacher then points to the door as Harry slowly makes his way out of the classroom. His head is held low as his black cowboy boots clunk against the smooth marble floor. The children in the classroom chuckle here and there still feeling the shocks of Harry's comment. Harry stops at the door and then looks back at the teacher looking rather remorseful] Harry: Was it that Premature Ejaculation comment, man? [The classroom erupts in laughter, scaring Harry. The teacher has rage ravaged across her fat, wrinkly face] Teacher: OUT!! [Harry quickly opens the door and jogs down the hallway away from the classroom. The halls are empty as all of the students are in their classes back to learning after a most bodacious summer. Harry seems to be quite upset about being kicked out, but it certainly brought back a lot of memories from his days in high school] Voice: DUDE! [Harry turns around to see his brother and tag team partner, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, at the other end of the long hallway. Tommy is dressed in his Hawaiian button down shirt, khaki cargo shorts, and black sandals] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Dude, why are you here? [Harry points toward the door where he came from. Tommy looks in to see the students taking notes about the Emancipation Proclamation. Tommy then notices two special girls near the back of the classroom, The Olsen Twins] TTT: Dude.... Harry: What, man? TTT: You've really got to call this bogus relationship quits. It's most disturbing. Harry: No way, man. TTT: Well, you don't go to school with them, that's for sure, dude. Harry: They wanted me to, man. [Tommy then sighs as he rolls his eyes, looking back through the window. The twins have made eye contact with him and Tommy quickly looks away] TTT: Bogus...made eye contact. [Harry waves through the window as Mary-Kate and Ashley wave back] Harry: They are far out, man. [Tommy grabs Harry by his Twisted Sister T-shirt and pulls him away from the window and against a wall that contains a bulletin board. Many flyers advertising Back To School dances and fund raisers litter the board] TTT: Dude, this is just totally bogus. Your in a relationship with two 16 year old babes who used to be on a sitcom in the early ninties. Your a 23 year old man...do you know how totally heinous that looks. [Harry seems to think about that] Harry: That's not heinous, man. TTT: YES IT IS, DUDE! [Harry then seems to realize something, slightly pushing Tommy off of him] Harry: I get it now, man. [Tommy looks confused] TTT: What is it, compadre? Harry: Your just jealous of me, man. TTT: WHAT?! Harry: Yeah, man. Ever since I've had Mary-Kate and Ashley you've been negative about everything. You even tried to take them away from me when I first had them stay over night for a far out pajama party... TTT: A pajama party?! Harry: Yeah, man. A pajama party! We had fun, we played truth or dare, and then we had lots of hot, steamy... [Tommy holds his hands up with a very disgusted look in his eyes] TTT: DUDE!! Just stop right there. Harry: See...your jealous, man. You can't handle to hear how happy I am. TTT: Listen, compadre, we have a most brutal match coming up on Friday Fusion against a couple of newcomers called Total Violation. I've never really seen them in action, but we know that you should never underestimate a couple of compadres, especially after competing in the GTT. [Harry rolls his eyes] Harry: You and your GTT, man. TTT: You could have entered it too. [Harry decides to changed the subject instead] Harry: In speaking of Total Violation, man....I was violated by that teacher, man. [Tommy looks in the window and sees the overweight, wrinkly teacher smacking his yard stick against the table. Tommy looks back at Harry with his eyes wide open, knowing that she must be a totally bogus teacher] TTT: What did you do, dude? [Harry shrugs] Harry: I asked her about Premature Ejaculation, man. [Tommy then looks back in the classroom] TTT: Sex Ed is totally my cup of tea, dude. [Tommy opens the door and walks inside the room. The smile on his face is bright and shiny as many of the students know him from PCW and his great performances in the GTT] TTT: So...is this the gnarly class about Premature Ejaculation? Teacher: RAHHHH!! [She chases Tommy with her yard stick and he bolts through the door grabbing Harry by his arm and they run toward the nearest exit] Teacher: Stupid punk kids trying to take me down. YOU CAN NEVER TAKE ME DOWN! I am Mrs. Murray and NOBODY TAKES ME DOWN!! [From behind her a young man carrying a brown paper bag silently runs up behind her and whacks her in the head with it. Chunks of brown goo goes everywhere, including her hair and her floral dress. The man trots away in silence...the only glimpse caught of him is his 'Tool' T-shirt] Mrs. Murray: What is that smell??? [She sniffs the air] Mrs. Murray: DOG POOP!! OH MY GOD!!! [The man at the end of the hallway is shadowed in darkness] Adam The Anarchist: Not Dog Poop, My Dear.....MY POOP!! [The scene fades on the laughter of Adam The Anarchist]
