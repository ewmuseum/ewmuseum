Bud Ice sure sounds Nice

8 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-8-02 RP 1 Turmoil 9 I’ve done it! Teresa yelled these words as she makes her way to her pick up truck after the big pay per view. A huge party is in order and she knows just where to go. In a club near her hotel, there will be a live band and half price long necks all night. Now Teresa isn’t the one to jump onto the dance floor and flaunt all she has. She usually the girl at the bar or siting at a table with about three or four guys exchanging jokes and sharing conversations most do after having a couple beers or so. With Celt beat, there is only on more step to reach the gold. The Title Belt is so close to her reach now. Her one obstacle is Cyclonica. She’s a tough opponent, which isn’t a mystery. After the surprising family reunion Sunday night, perhaps the blue haired so-called goddess will be a bit vulnerable. The sheer thought of the possibility brings more hope to Teresa. I need that belt. I want the title. I have to prove myself worthy. By now Teresa is stepping into her hotel room. She could’ve freshened up at the Arena but the idea of sharing the locker room showers with the other women just didn’t tickle her fancy. It’s bad enough to listen to their constant whining about a sore leg or aching back. Hello? That comes with the business. You will get hurt. Yeah, Teresa is pretty sore. Her back feels like a semi truck had just rolled over her, she has a splitting headache and she is so damn hungry. As she undresses for her shower, the last person she would think of at a time like this approaches her thoughts. This man is non other than the Tax Man himself, Tim Walden. Sure his shocking kiss brought the rumors flooding in backstage. Teresa didn’t even expect it. Hell, she didn’t expect him to sling her across the table two weeks ago. He did seem to be enjoying himself as he took his time exploring her mouth with his tongue. She can still remember the taste of mouthwash that he left lingering on her tongue. What little Timmy doesn’t know is that not many men who have taken advantage of her walked away unharmed. True, Walden did walk away that night. His time is coming. Teresa is like a lioness on the prowl. She is waiting silently for the right moment to attack. Then the young lioness will devour her prey bite by bite. Good things come to those who wait, so it’s been said. For right now, there are more important matters to deal with, like winning the Cruiserweight Title Belt. The shower is nice and hot. Teresa finds herself standing under the showerhead, as the streams of water beat against her body like tiny pellets. The idea of celebrating her win against Celt alone in her hotel begins to sound more tempting. She would simply drive to a store and buy a six pack and return to the hotel. She’ll spend the night watching cheesy horror flicks. Large crowds and loud music isn’t what she wants tonight after all. The beer is cold, the horror flick is sick and all Teresa needs is a good hard…well one’s imagination can figure out the rest. She thinks of Scott. The Bud Ice must be talking to her. Four empty bottles rest on the side table next to the king size bed. Dozens of memories flash through her mind and she falls back onto the bed. Her eyes stare at the ceiling as she thinks of their last meeting. It was sheer accident but she feels someone or something brought them together that night. She was at a bar enjoying a few shots with a guy she met there. They were exchanging jokes and mild conversation. Her cell phone rings. It’s him. The sound of Scott’s voice caused her to forget what’s-his-face she was talking to and she stepped off the bar stool and moved to a corner where she could hear better. After a moment of conversation, she finds that he is in the same bar. Suddenly, the bar scene didn’t appear so entertaining. They meet at the door and leave the bar. They walk arm in arm to a pizza parlor. There, they talked over a large pizza and a pitcher of Coca-Cola. They held hands for the first time in several months. She felt the fire for him rekindle within her and with his words and the look in his eyes, she saw the fire in him as well. Without warning, she felt his lips caress hers. She gave in and the kiss lasted long enough to get a few stares. When they broke from the kiss, neither was much for words and shortly, she finds herself leaving the pizza parlor with him and to his hotel. A deep breath exits Teresa’s lungs. That one night feels like a dream now. She hasn’t seen him since and isn’t sure when they will meet again. She isn’t one to show her weaknesses. This man however, is her main weakness. No one will know how he affects her. To the world, her game face will remain. The world will see her as Teresa Tomas, THE American Woman that isn’t to be reckoned with. Deep down, this tomboy who isn’t afraid to get her fingernails dirty, is a woman with female emotions and female thoughts. Wednesday night, she will put on her game face, show the world who she wishes to be, and continue to hide the female she is damned to be.
