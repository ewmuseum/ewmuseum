The Greatest Sin of All...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered A lot has happened since Inphino Blitz returned to PCW… … well… not that much, but significant things has happened. A friendship as strengthened between two old friends… A rivalry has brewed between the new and the old… and a new evil has tried to stand against him. And out of the four that started the grand madness, the great pursuit for the PCW world title… only ONE remained in the end. Skylar Green, then The Buzz Cal Roberts… then Inphino arrived, not too far off in front of Propaganda. The four men were the top of the game, still ARE the top of the game in PCW. But a new battle in this never-ending war for gold has come. Inphino won the first battle… and now, new players to the game think it’s their turn for victory. “It is finished…” Inphino was wrongfully stripped of the belt by Alan Zoober and Dick James. The fans still acknowledge him as THE champion, however. And the fans would love for it to see the Black Dragon Master once again hold up the PCW championship as his own. But… there are a number of men that now stand in his way. “I will no longer persist… I shall not pursue.” Familiar faces line his path… Darkstar, a man who’s danced around Inphino for years, yet the two have never met face to face. Cal Roberts, the newcomer who’s persistence in the world title scene has developed a bit of a rivalry between him and Inphino Blitz. Kanton is yet another older name that has not faced Inphino in the past…and Jesse Ramey, he is one who became the first and only PURE world champion. It would be interesting for the only PURE champion and the only PURE legend to meet face to face… quite a sight. “I’m sorry… it just cannot be.” And then… there’s the goliath, the big man in PCW to date… Propaganda. Accompanied by Eric Sagar, Propaganda has been on a tear through the opposition and through the affairs of Duke Williams, Boda, and John Kano. It was Kano, however, who alongside Kris Kanton, and Brian Arson, brought the holy pair of Propaganda and Sagar into the alliance known as Primetime Players. Such a fitting name some would think… some… “I know that’s what you want, but I just won’t do it.” Now the mighty fiend stands not alone, if you could say that. Sagar would claim that he is never alone, yet alongside the angelic host the Lord’s sent to protect and assist him in his mission. And Propaganda and his entire alliance are within the new world title tournament. Ironic, some would say… fixed said others, and a few merely state it as coincidence. Pick whatever answer you desire. Things don’t look good for the other fighters in this new battle. “I’ve done what I wanted to accomplish. I’ve succeeded. A greed for victory shall clearly be one’s defeat.” Amidst the list of seeded talent… amidst the challengers… is there one to stand against the Players? … We shall see… The scene opened upon the pretty face of Mina de Margo. She sat at a pine table, a shiny wood finish. From the table up, she was seen wearing a white button down shirt and a white hair band holding her black and red hair back. She stared ahead as someone with a tired look, her hands cupped around the coffee steaming from her mug. Blinking slowly, she kept her eyes ahead on whoever it was. Sitting across from her was Inphino Blitz. He himself sat with his arms folded across his chest, his hair combed back into a neat masculine bun. With a black tank top on showing off all the definition of his arms, he looked off to the side as in front of him a cup of tea awaited him. The sliding doors were open as the two of them sat there in their Japanese style parlor silently, the sound of the outdoors in the background. Soft running water from the waterfall of their garden, the hollow bamboo clack of the shishi-odoshi as it pours water into the pond. Birds flying past and calling out… some would call it a peaceful day. But inside, there was quite a silent ruckus, the tension between Inphino and Mina thicker than Eric Sagar’s Bible. Mina sipped her coffee, glancing down for a minute at it before looking back at Blitz. Inphino didn’t look back, only off into the garden as he picked up his own tea to drink. They both sat their drinks down at the same time and returned to silence. Then Mina exhaled audibly, almost sigh-like as she slouched over, propping her head in her hand. Blitz didn’t notice; that was one thing about Inphino that worked out for him inside the ring. His focus was unrivaled, and you had to physically get in his way in order to distract him, and that if happened didn’t happen long as most distractions were dispatched with a quick Inphinite kick. But Inphino’s focus had no use in social situations, when he had to deal with a person in conversation. Mina knew this for a fact, and decided to break the silence the old fashioned way. “You’re weird.” That blatant statement caught Inphino’s attention outright, his brow wrinkling as he looked to her. “Hm?” “You heard me,” she said as she looked up at the ceiling. “You’re weird.” It was just something you weren’t expecting, at least he wasn’t. “Please, explain.” She smirked slightly as she sat up and looked at him. “Your mission has, and always will be to train your abilities, right?” “Correct.” A nod from her, “But when you came back, you wanted nothing but to gain what you lost the last time you were in PCW, the world title, as well as the respect from the fans. True?” Another nod came from Inphino this time. Mina smiled slightly again as she trailed her finger around the mouth of her mug. “So… now that you’ve completed your side venture, why aren’t you continuing on your life journey?” Blitz stared at her for a while, a blank expression on his face. That was normal, so Mina wasn’t too sure if she had hit a mark or just struck out with her point. Then Inphino reacted, a sigh as he turned his gaze back out to the garden. Mina sighed as well, going back to drinking her coffee defeated. “You know…” She perked up as he responded slowly. “… according to Christian morals… we’re living in sin.” Well, THAT was something. Mina looked at him with wide eyes, blinking dumbfounded as Blitz kept his gaze at the garden outside. “Uh… what’s that got to do with anything?” “Stating a fact is all…” he said casually. “You, living here alongside me out of wedlock… it’s a sin in the Christian church.” This was definitely getting strange. Mina’s eyebrow quirked upwards as he looked back at her. “We live in sin… don’t you feel ashamed?” “No, not really.” She said before sipping her coffee. “Should I feel ashamed?” “According to Sagar’s teaching, we should be.” He said, shrugging his shoulders. Mina rolled her eyes. “I’m not screwing you or anything. We’re roommates, coworkers is all. There’s nothing in the Bible saying anything about a man and woman being roommates.” “Hmm…” he nodded slowly, returning to the garden outside. “What’s this about, huh?” Mina asked suddenly. “What are you thinking?” “Not much…” was all he said. Mina cut her eyes at him, aggravation settling in. “Now you look here,” she said, placing her palms flat on the table and rising up slightly. “I don’t know what’s going on in that head of yours, but let’s get things straight here. Eric Sagar is by no means a creditable authority on what’s right and what’s wrong, you hear me?” “Is that so?” Blitz responded while turning back towards her. “Yeah, that’s so.” “How so?” Mina sighed again, this time out of irritation. “Because the guy’s nothing more than a redneck bigot. I mean, he probably can’t tell the difference between Chinese, Japanese, or any other Asian out there.” “Who can though?” Inphino shot back with a sad, but accurate point. “Most people have no clue themselves.” “That’s beside the point!” she was getting riled up fast. “He feels that foreigners are wrong, and that his big dorky freak of a servant needs to punish them. If anyone who sees anything different than what their narrow little minds are used to, then they’re wrong. Case closed.” “Hmmph… it seems that things are narrow everywhere.” He gulped down the rest of his tea calmly, Mina standing up looking at him with a confused expression. Blitz looked up at her and gave a slight smirk. “Sagar is obsessed with control,” Blitz began as he sat his cup down. “In his position as a reverend, he has power over the masses within his congregation, but that’s not enough in his mind. He wants more in his congregation, but he is but one man, and he cannot convert everyone in the world. Angered by that, he deems everything that’s not conforming to his beliefs… wicked. Other cultures are guaranteed to be deemed wicked in his eyes, especially if he cannot understand them.” Inphino stood up and continued to explain things to Mina. “Propaganda is no more the tool of God than you are living in sin with me. He only believes so because it came from Sagar’s lips. Sagar has a powerful hold over Propaganda psychologically, which makes me wonder what could have happened in Propaganda’s life that would break his independence so much. But to keep on track, Propaganda acts in sin… believing he’s doing holy work, but he does nothing but Sagar’s work. In analysis… Propaganda serves Sagar… and he who raises himself higher than God shall be damned to the utmost, along with their servants.” Blitz took his mug and walked back to the kitchen. Mina stood there alone, staring ahead pondering the words he just spoke to her. Coming back into the room with a fresh cup of tea, he stood in front of the open sliding doors and looked into his groomed garden. “Mina…” She snapped out of thought. “Yes?” “Issue a challenge to Propaganda,” he said, raising his tea to his nose. “I shall face him in this tournament.” That got a raise out of her, her smile beaming wide. “Hell yeah! But why now?” “Out of the four of us fighting for the belt, he is the only one I haven’t beaten one on one.” “But you did pin him at Shanghaied in the triple threat.” She added. He shook his head. “It’s not the same. Also… this Primetime Players issue must be addressed, and while most like to take on the weakest link first… I go for the strongest. When one leaves the strongest defeated, that leaves everyone questioning just how much strength he possessed in the beginning… including himself.” “Yeah, sure,” she said as she backed away into the hallway. “… whatever. I’m just glad you’re fighting. First Propaganda, and then we take out the rest of them Primetime Player bastards! I’ll book a flight to the US right away!” She ran off to work on getting the match arranged, leaving Inphino looking out into the garden. He sipped his tea slowly and nodded. Inphino knew what is meant to sin. He’s looked into the mirror and at times seen different faces. As he stands the Black Dragon Master this day, he sees Propaganda and sees a man looking into the mirror and seeing what he is told he’s seeing… seeing what Sagar tells him he’s seeing. When Propaganda and Inphino Blitz step into the ring, when it’s all over Blitz hopes that the mirror will be shattered, and Propaganda will see who he truly is… and what Sagar truly is… We all live in sin… some live greater sins than others…but the sin of putting yourself before God… is one of the greatest sins of all…
