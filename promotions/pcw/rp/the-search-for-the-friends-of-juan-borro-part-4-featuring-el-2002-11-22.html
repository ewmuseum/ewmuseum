The Search for the Friends of Juan Borro (Part 4) featuring El Diablo

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/22/02 {Fade} Tears.Heartbreak.Suffering.Pain. The shock had come, Juan’s death was revealed. Everything was felt, but nothing was shown. Shock lived within her soul, sought out through her eyes and swayed into Romantico like a bullet. He held her in his arms. He grasped her like she meant something to him, because she did mean something to him. A tear had rolled down his eye. How it happened he may never know, but he felt it deep inside. That phenomenon is something he may never completely understand. Thank You. Excuse Me. Juliet stared up at Romantico wiping the tears from her moist eyes. Nothing. Do you want to go inside, maybe rest a little? Yes. Romantico brought Juliet inside and El Diablo followed. He sat her down on the couch and just let her sit there silently for a few minutes. Are you all right? I’ll get better. May I ask you how you know Juan? I’m his brother. Ju…. Juan had a brother? Yes. It wasn’t something many people knew, nor did he, but he did. How did this all happen? He was killed at a PCW PPV. Oh My God, who would do such a thing. A very sick man Romantico murmured those words with anger in his soul and he tightly gripped the pillow next to him. I’m sorry you had to find out this way. I’m happy I at least found out. But oh God I miss him. Juliet lowered her head in sadness, as both Romantico and Diablo didn’t know what to do. You know Juan once told me that love isn’t something you do, but something you feel and that nothing could compare to it. He always did have a positive outlook. Do you mind if I ask you why it didn’t work out? A lot of things, it was just something that was the best for the both of us. Do you truly believe that? I don’t even know anymore. Juliet clutches her hands together and nods her head. I loved him and that’s all I know. I wish it could have worked out, oh god I wish we could have just been together one more time. Faith isn’t always friendly. Faith is never friendly. {FLASHBACK} Romantico remembers the times when Juan would come to him and tell him about how great his life was going. Juan: You have to meet her man; she’s the most beautiful thing you’ll ever lay eyes on. Nothing could compare. Romantico then stared at the crying Juliet. She is! What did you just say Juliet finally wipes away her tears. I was just thinking about how much Juan really did love you. I loved him just as much. He was a great man, and you must be honored to have had such a great brother. I am. Romantico knows the dreaded moment is coming and decides to just spit it out soon than later. He breathes out through his nostrils and proceeds. This isn’t the only reason I came here to talk to you. It isn’t? No. I need to know something very important. You see I lead a complicating life and I need to meet with Juan’s father. I believe you’ve met him. Yes, he’s a very torn but wise man. Juan introduced me to him a few months ago. Well you see I need to find him, to learn a few things of my past and I’ve come here to learn where he lives. I’m sorry but I don’t know the exact location. I know he’s now in a small town west of Lisbon that starts with S. It’s a small house that’s all white and you’ll notice it because it’s right near the largest house in the entire town. I’m sure if you look it up you’ll find him. Thank you so much, you don’t know how much this means to me. Romantico is in a state of happiness, and he is about to just go run out the door and find his father, but then realizes he has to do something to help comfort Juliet. He turns around and goes up to her. Remember Juan. I will. It will be hard to ever forget him. Give me you hand. Juliet placing her hand into the palm of Romantico. Romantico then closes the palm and tells her to shut her eyes. He then opens her palm. Now open your eyes. When Juliet opens her eyes and she sees the gold pendant that has “JB + JS” engraved in it, and then it says “Forever Together”. How…. how did you get this? Don’t ask questions; just accept it as a gift. With that said Romantico and Diablo exit the house and make there way to the car. How did you do that? Diablo asks Romantico looking up at him in awe. I didn’t do it, a greater power did. {FADE} TO BE CONTINUED!
