PCW wrestlers seemingly get ahead of OSW... but which OSW car is still leading?(Part 3)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sometime last week! {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up within the blue Dodge Viper that Semore Smoke and The End are driving across the country in the Cannonball Run. The End who was driving before is now sitting in the passenger seat with a joint sticking out of his mouth. Semore Smoke is driving the car and is driving about thirty over the speed limit. The End seems to have fallen asleep with the joint in his mouth so Semore reaches over and grabs the joint from the man dressed up as Hamburgular from McDonalds. Semore has abandoned his Ronald McDonald costume and now has it stuffed in the back. Smoke sticks the joint into his mouth and takes a puff from the almost out cherry and it starts to re-ignite. He smiles as the green smoke seeps through his body and out his nose. Smoke turns to the CB set-up in his car that sits right below the stereo set-up. Smoke turns the knob on and puts it on the signal number that all the PCW wrestlers agreed to use against the OSW wrestlers. Right when Smoke turns it to channel eleven, the channel they’re all supposed to be at, he hears the voice of Gaz “The Jester” Green over it… Gaz Green: Yeah, I know what you mean Surge. But don’t think we’ll be friends in the ring. This is just for this one chance at kicking some OSW ass. {{{{Smoke picks up CB microphone and pushes the button in and speaks…}}}} Semore Smoke: Hey guys, Smokester here. You guys wouldn’t believe what I did about ten hours ago. Jim Surge: Hey Smoke! What did you do? Semore Smoke: Heh. You’ll find out when you watch this thing on TV. But I might as well tell you. I went to the Parents Television Council headquarters and broke in… all right, I didn’t break in, the door was unlock, but I went up into L. Brent Bozell III’s office and checked his computer, which was on and all already. And on the computer I found plans for him to take over the world! Ha! Imagine that! I also found in his office two flags and a huge picture. One flag was a nazi flag and the other was a communist flag. And the picture was of Joe McCarthy! Ha! {{{{Semore Smoke is surprised by the next voice that comes through the CB…}}}} Mark Makonie: What the hell is wrong with a nazi flag? You damned nigger sympathizer! {{{{You can hear in the background as Duzza slaps Makonie over the back of the head and snatches the microphone away from Makonie…}}}} Duzza: That stupid fuck! Why Lord? Why did you give me this dumb fuck!? Yo, Smoke. Where are you now? Semore Smoke: I’m about halfway through Tennessee. Duzza: Good, we’re close to you. How fast are you going? Semore Smoke: Like a hundred and ten. Duzza: Damn boy! Slow da fuck down! Look I need some weed, that’s why I want to catch up to you. This damn idiot there is starting to piss me off. He keeps thinking that he’s Jon Lovits from Rat Race driving Hitler’s car… Semore Smoke: Ha! I’ll slow down to about ninety then so you can catch up. Duzza: Aight. That’ll do. Gaz: Hey, Semore. Did you do anything to get rid of Ivan Stanislav yet? Semore Smoke: No, I haven’t seen the guy. Gaz: Damn! He’s got stealth stuff all over that damn van of his. It’s frustrating you can’t tell where he is at all. They travel at night with the lights and all off and during the day they travel with the invisible stealth on. No cops will see them. Semore Smoke: Damn Russian technology! {{{{Gaz must have held the microphone button down, but didn’t speak. You can hear his partner, Nerezza in the background…}}}} Nerezza: Hey! Tell them that we just spotted Scott Taylor and Tyler Davidson. Gaz: You hear Rezz? Semore Smoke: Roger. Gaz: What? Semore Smoke: Roger means I did. Gaz: Oh. Don’t know any radio talk! What da fuck!? {{{{The scene leaves Smoke who has a confused look on his face after the last statement and it switches to Nerezza, who is driving, and Gaz “The Jester” Green, who is navigating in the passenger seat. They’re driving a huge purple hearse, and on the front dash you can plainly see several buttons that let out different traps. They catch up to the huge monster truck in their way. One side of the truck is painted pure black and the other side is covered in green sequin. Nerezza kicks the hearse into high gear and speeds past the slower monster truck. Tyler Davidson, the driver, honks his horn as P.W.A pass them. They then turn in front of the monster truck and stick to a moderate speed of about eighty-five miles per hour. Nerezza then looks at Gaz and smiles a wicked smile, Gaz nods as Nerezza reaches forward to the dash and presses a button. Suddenly out of the back of the hearse you see a huge flamethrower blowing out towards the monster truck. Tyler swerves the truck as fast as he can to the other lane to avoid the flames and does so as the flames run out. Nerezza then hits another button, and a blade sticks out of the driver side of the hearse. He then looks into the rear view mirror and sees that Tyler is in the left lane still. He then slams on the breaks with the sharp blade sticking out of the driver side of the hearse, and as the monster truck passes it, the blade slashes both the front tire and the back tire on the passenger side. It begins to swerve as both tires go flat automatically and the huge truck swerves and spins out off the road into the median of the high way. Nerezza and Gaz both high five each other as Gaz gets on the microphone of the CB again to tell of the good news…}}}} Gaz: Haha! We just got rid of them sum bitches! Just slashed those big-ass tires and sent them spinning! Semore Smoke: Great job! One OSW car down and how many left to go? Jim Surge: Four more to go. Semore Smoke: Damn… how many PCW or miscellaneous do we have left in? Jim Surge: Well, counting us… six. Good chance someone from PCW or another fed will win, instead of OSW. Semore Smoke: Sweet. Jim Surge: Ha! Spotted Hessian and Minion… damn…. I don’t want to go anywhere near that scary car. {{{{Ben Campbell speaks up from the driver seat…}}}} Ben: Head on back of car seems to breath fire. Must have drunk firewater. Jim Surge: No, it’s just called lights, still freaky as hell. {{{{The scene changes to Jim Surge and Ben Nighthorse Campbell’s car. Ben is seated in the driver seat dressed in his Native American best and Jim is dressed in regular street clothes beside him. They’re riding in a black hummer, the scene changes and you now can see Misty, their valet, sleeping in the back of the hummer. Ben hits the gas and speeds up to about ninety miles per hour and drives up along beside the scary vehicle, driven by Hessian. As they drive by, Jim looks out his window at Hessian, who looks back with a look of anger on his face. He slams his car into the next gear, and in doing so the transmission skips and the car begins to slow down. The hummer passes the car easily as Hessian tries to jack rabbit back into first gear. As he does so, he loses valuable time. Ben flips a switch on the shifter in the hummer and an oilcan sticks out from underneath the hummer. It then spills across the road and lifts up and back into the hummer again. Hessian finally jack rabbits the car and is now in second gear and gaining speed quickly. He hits sixty miles per hour and then hits the oil slick. The car spins and swerves from side to side as Hessian tries to keep control, but he can’t keep control as the car swerves and stops dead in it’s tracks off the road with steam rising from the engine, or is it from Hessian’s anger? Either way, they’re out of the race for a little bit. Ben and Jim both laugh as Jim gets over the CB…}}}} Jim: Hah! We got rid of them as well! They’re engine will need to cool off now! Gaz: Great! That’s two OSW cars down and three more to go. The other two must be far ahead of us now though, either that or far behind us, either way, it doesn’t matter where you’re seated in position from the other drivers, it’s when you started and how long it takes you to get across. Semore Smoke: Yep. {{{{A new voice joins the conversation…}}}} Statuz Quo: Yo! How’s my PCW boys chillin’? Semore Smoke: We’re doing pretty damn good Quo. Statuz Quo: Smokester! That’s good to hear. I just passed that fucked up monster truck driven by both Scott Taylor and Tyler Davidson, seems da trucks tires are slashed. Gaz: We did it! Statuz Quo: Hah! I figured! Damn… there goes dat scary ass car of Hessian’s, which of you did that? Jim Surge: We just did! Heh. We’re already getting close to dominating this PCW style. But you do know in the long stretch between all of us it’ll be every man for himself? Semore Smoke: Yeah, but in the past half hour or so that we’ve all been chattin’, I’ve made it to the Arkansas state line! Ha! Statuz Quo: Damn you quick son of a bitch! Semore Smoke: Catch me if you can! Hah! {{{{The scene changes back to Semore Smoke’s Dodge Viper as you can see it speeding down the road, now at a moderate ninety miles per hour speed so some of the PCW wrestlers can catch up. He’s only got Ivan Stanislav to catch up to now, but can he catch up to the almost invisible car? Who knows. The scene fades on the car as it speeds down the road and you can hear the bitching voices of Makonie, Statuz, Nerezza, and Surge over the CB…}}}} TO BE CONTINUED…
