Live from the Crucible Theatre...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Live on the 7th of May, 2002 The nail biting tension. Live from the Crucible Theatre somewhere in England is the final frame of the world snooker championship at the moment. Peter Ebdon sweats as he watches Stephen Hendry line up to get the first point of the game.... The Red Misses somehow. Miyagawa leaps out of his seat and screams at the television. Miyagawa: GO ON PETER! KICK HIS ASS! BREAK THE CUE OVER HIS NECK!!! Syko looks at him rather worried as Ebdon takes to the table and tries a long red pot to the bottom right corner of the table... and it bounces back out of the pocket. Miyagawa slumps down into the chair as Hendry lines up again and this time manages to pocket a red followed by a quick blue into the left middle taking the white up against the top cushion and then back down for another red. Miyagawa: God dammit... WAIT!!! Stephen Hendry misses another red. The pressure and weight of this tournament on his shoulders means that out of the five few points that the world’s two best players are missing easy shots. Syko: Dude, chill. It’s like big pool. Miyagawa: This isn’t Pool, fool, this is Snooker... DAMMIT!!! Says Miyagawa as Ebdon messes up another red, this time using the rest of aim it into the bottom left pocket unsuccessfully. Syko: Who are these two anyway? Says Syko as Hendry chalks the cue after going 7-4 in this final frame with an easy red.... before screwing up a long blue into the top left with amazing surprise as the crowd groan in surprise. Miyagawa: JESUS! Syko: Jesus? He play’s Snooker? Miyagawa: Silly goblin. Miyagawa looks at the clock, it reads ten to as Ebdon pots a red and lines up perfect for the pink in this crucial point building frame. Miyagawa breaths slowly as Ebdon takes the pink down for its important six points and going up 11-7 against the Scotsman, Stephen Hendry. Syko: Aren’t they meant to be better then this? They’re hardly potting shots that even I could get. Miyagawa: You would be too short to reach across the table. YES! Screams Miyagawa as Ebdon doesn’t mess up on a red to the corner. He chalks his cue carefully as he lines up for the re-spotted Pink and sinks... the pink for it’s six points again, going eleven points clear at 18-7 against the now seven times champion. He pots another red to Miyagawa’s relief. Miyagawa: It’s not about skill now. This is the big stage, it’s about bottle. It’s about not messing up. Seeing it through, all of that. GO YOU BASTARD! Says Miyagawa as Ebdon pots a blue easily, his nerves having seemingly dissipated and following up with a quick chalk and a red. He lines up on the pink again... and it’s in. Syko: Why does the guy in the tux put the pink ones back but not the reds? Miyagawa: The reds all have to go first before the colours are removed in sequence, yellow, green, brown, blue, pink and finally black. Black gives the most points at seven. Syko: Ah. But it’s out of play at the side of the table, that’s why he’s going for a pink, right? Miyagawa: Yeah. And it has to be in sequence, red-colour-red-colour until there is no more reds and then the colours in sequence. Everything has its sequence. While Miyagawa has been talking Ebdon was at the table clearing the balls into their pockets, going for red-pink-red pink-red-blue and on... now forty four points ahead with seventy seven points possible on the table for Hendry should Ebdon mess up. Syko: He’s doing much better now. Miyagawa: He’s got into the flow of things. Into sequence and he’s forgotten his nerves. He’s just out there now against his opponent and sees only the balls. The occasion is forgotten and he is against himself now. Just like me and Boda on Sunday. Syko: Ah, so that’s why you’re watching this then? Tension practice? Miyagawa: No. I just happen to like snooker. Gah... Syko: What? Ebdon pots a red, but spins the white too much and leaves himself placed in a bad position and has to go for a brown. Miyagawa: This is it. If he gets this red, Hendry needs snookers to win. If he pots this red. IT’S NOT THERE!!!! Miyagawa: NOOOOOOO!!!!!! DAMMIT!!! NOOOOOO!!!!!! DON’T LET THAT SEVEN TIME CHAMPION BASTARD WIN IT!!!! Stephen Hendry comes to the table knowing that if he manages to clear the table with getting red-black all the way then he can win... but the black is out of position and he gets a red, but has to go for a pink... and follows up by snookering Peter Ebdon. Miyagawa: COME ON!!! DO THE HIT THING!!! Syko: Hit thing? Is that a snooker term then? Miyagawa: No, HIT, mathematics. Ebdon touches the red. Hendry goes for another snooker... and pots the white. Miyagawa: YES! GO! Ebdon needs to pot only one more red to win... It’s there. Miyagawa: YEAH BABY YEAH!!!!! He’s won it! He’s won it! Syko: So why isn’t he being congratulated and stuff? Miyagawa: Because it isn’t over yet, he has to miss before it finishes. Ebdon pots a red and goes for a green... and misses... and the crowd erupts as Peter Ebdon becomes Embassy World Snooker Champion... of the World. Meanwhile in the Anaheim Dungeon’s Television Room back in Anaheim... Miyagawa: YEAH!!! YEAH!!! AND IT WAS HENDRY’S OWN FAILURE THAT CAUSED IT!!!! Syko raises an eyebrow at the whole thing. Syko: So that’s about the big occasion... about not loosing your nerve. About winning the big occasion against a seasoned veteran... right? Miyagawa: No. I just happen to like snooker. And with that, we end it here. This promo was filmed live as the final frame of Stephen Hendry vs. Peter Ebdon was played ending at midnight here in London, England. And thus the ball plays are accurate.
