Where Has He Been?

20 March 2002

Date: 03-20-2002 (OOC:THE MULTI-PERSON RP BEGINS NEXT RP, EXPECT IT!! THANKS A BUNCH FOR THE TURNOUT!!) . . ::/::. .After a long long hiatus, our story begins within what looks to be a seemingly normal small town in the subarbs of Sova Ncotia, Canada. The houses look extremely primitive for this day and age, made of gray brick and holding up golden straw roofs. A dirt road weaves through the this town, made of of an estimated 30 or 40 so establishments as a handful of the town's citizens lead their lives with the sun shining down on them with it's warm smile. The townspeople look to be completely happy with what some would call a 'primtive' existance as the conservative women,dressed in their flesh covering dresses or long sleeved shirts and blue jeans, run errands and gossip within the streets while the men are hard at work in the background as can be seen at the local lumber factory, a chorus of chopping heard from far away with the ominous sounds of trees falling for the 'chorus' ' hook . Children play in their choice of the bright green grass or the dusty dirt roads as the tustle and play around with not a care in the world, all looking to be quite well in this town. . . . all looking to be happy with their peaceful and simple lives. Shortly, all of this is changed as the sound of tires srunching up on the dirt grows louder and louder in the distance, drawing attention from the gossiping females on the outskirts of town as all turn their heads to see what is going on. As the dust begins kicking up in the background, now the children stop their playing as they look on in interest of what it is that could be approaching their town, captivated whenever anything or anyone new makes its way in. Their questions soon are given answers as it looks to be a sporty, jet black Viper GTS soiled with red dirt all over the bottom edges and tires as it begins to slow, the children and women stepping aside and off the road to give the car room as their eyes stay glued to this new entry to the town. Within seconds, the Viper halts in the center of the road at a complete stop as we are able to see none other than former 2-time PCW World Heavyweight Champion Jeremy Howard at the wheel with his lovely wife Jessica by his side in the leather covered passenger seat. Jeremy shifts on the brake and cuts the car off, both he and his significant other stepping out from the Viper and closing the doors as Jeremy looks about at the town with question, taking off his shades to reveal his deep dark eyes as he runs his fingers through his untamed and long black hair. Jessica joins him at his side, fixing the wrinkles in her short strapless flamey dress and kicking off the dirt from her knee high boots before saying. . ::/ :: . . Jessica:'Jeremy baby, you sure this is the place? This si starting to remind me of that Amish paradise video. . . .' *shudders at the thought* Jeremy :'Now babe, what have I told you about trusting me. . . . I know it looks like crap, but he's gotta be here.' . . ::/:: . . The common women and children eye the two strangers with wonder, scared to approach them as Jeremy and Jessica look about at them, trying to find the right thing to say to them. One of the little children, a scrawny red-headed boy with freckles, approaches Jeremy's car and runs his hands across it softly as if he were petting a horse before saying in utter awe. . ::/:: . . Boy :'Nice wheels mister. . .we don't see too many fo these here.' Jeremy : 'It is nice isn't it huh lil' buddy?' *smiles* 'So tell me. . . . is this Sova Ncotia?' Boy :'Your in the right place mister...the land of wood, butter churning, and simple boring life sir. No doubt aboot-(NOT A TYPO! HEHEHE, CANADIAN SLURRING RULES!!)- it!' Jessica:'Awww Jeremy, that is sooooo cute, he said 'ABOOT'. . . . say it again please?' *smiling*Boy :'No.' Jessica: 'Please?' Boy :'Nooo!' Jessica:'I'll give you a pack of Starbursts. . . .' *opens up her purse and reaches in, unearthing a pack of Starbursts and wavering it in front of the boy* Boy : *mesmerized by the candy* 'Pride in my nationality. . . . .candy. . . . Canadian loyalty. . . .sweet sweet candy. . . .ughhhh, I hate being a kid. . . .ABOOT ABOOT ABOOT!!' . . ::/:: . . Jeremy shakes his head in shame as Jessica hands the boy the pack of Starbursts, his little fingers ripping into the foil as the other kids watch on jealously. One of the women steps up to Jessica and Jeremy with a friendly smile tattooed on her face, her hands at her waist as she asks with her friends watching on cautiously. . ::/:: . . Woman 1:'What brings you two to our town, eh? It's not common we have visitors. . .' Jeremy :'Well, we're looking for this guy actually. He disappeared maybe. . . . . .four or five months ago and we have a credible source AKA 'Smelly Joey' from Wrestletattler.com, who's gonna take a serious a** kicking courtesy of the Krayzie One if he's wrong, that he is here.' Woman 1 :'Well what does he look like?' Jessica:'Well. . . . he's about 6'2. . . . 221 lbs. . .covered in tight spandex . . . and is always yelling silly catchphrases like 'HOLY. . .' Far Off Voice:'HOLY HORSE RIDING LESSONS, WHOA FOUR-LEGGED MEANS OF TRANSPORTATION TYPE THING!!!' . . ::/:: . . The heads of all the townspeople, as well as Jeremy and Jessica, turn sharply to the right as the sound of rapid hoof beats can be heard drawing closer and closer. Drawing closer, we come to find a white horse wildly galloping off the dirt road and into the surrounding grassland near the houses as the leather reigns about it's neck fly about loosely, the rider being none other than our long time missing hero of the PCW. . . .RENT-A-HERO!! Shockingly, Rent-A-Hero is without spandex costume and has instead traded up for a red and dull gold MOUNTIE outfit of all things, complete with shoulder decorations and black knee high boots. . . the only thing still 'hero-ish' about our hero being the purple Hawkeye mask he covers his face with, still obviously believing in his secrecy as always, Rent-A-Hero looking in great shape as always. The townspeople collectively gasp as our hero desperately struggles to control the horse to no avail, yelling loudly in his usual heroic tone for the untamed animal to stop in its tracks and shortly it does. . . which in turn hurls our hero off the saddle as he soars through the air and crashing into a stack of barrels labeled 'Candle Oil', the barrels smashed into wooden shards as our hero is drenched in slick candle oil and buried in a makeshift grave of broken wood. . ::/:: . . Jeremy : *sigh* 'I think we found him. . .' *mumbles* 'And now he's not just A idiot. . .he's a CANADIAN idiot.' Jessica:'Jeremy, how come you always come down with a bad case of the mumbles whenever Rent-A-Hero's around. If I didn't know any better, I'd think your insulting him or something.' Jeremy : 'Come on honey. . .now why would I do that?' *mumbles* 'Da** it, I think she's on to me. . .' . . ::/:: . . The entire group of women, children, Jessica, and Jeremy rush over to the aid of Rent-A-Hero as he begins unearthing himself from beneath the destroyed wood, still managing to crack that pearly white heroic grin as he exclaims. . ::/:: . . Rent-A-Hero:'ALL IS WELL CITIZENS!! YOUR HERO EMERGES UNSCATHED, slightly embarassed, YET UNSCATHED!! It always could have been worse, I could have landed in those crates of 'Sunlight Activated Matches' after I landed in this candle oil. . .*points to the crates of 'Sunligth Activated Candles' conveniently set a few feet away from the now damaged barrels of candle oil* . . . .then I woulda been in soem real trouble liek the Negaborgs of V-17 Sector. . . boy was that a good read. Oh well, your mountie will no arise and continue his duties good citizens, carry on, eh? Hehe, I said eh?. . .' ALERT, SLAPSTICK COMEDY AHEAD!! DON'T SAY I DIDN'T WARN YOU!! . . ::/:: . . Before anyone can say anything to our oil-drenched hero, he arises to his feet to only step in a large slick of candle oil, sliding dangerously fast towards the crates of 'Sunlight Activated Matches' as the crowd turns their heads to the side as they all predict the inevitable. Shortly, that inevitable fate happens as a loud explosion can be heard after Rent-A-Hero crashes into the crates, the crowd turning their heads back to him and opening their eyes as our hero run about like a crazed lunatic as his body is enwrapped in blazing flames, screaming like a dumped schoolgirl. . ::/:: . . Rent-A-Hero:'HOT HOT HOT!!! GYAAHAHAAAAA!!! HOT HOT HOT!!! THE ELSATIC IN MY SCOOBY DOO UNDIES IS MELTING!! HOT HOT HOT!!'Boy :'Mommy. . . . is the masked mountie hero man supposed to be burning like that?' . . ::/:: . .The crowd collectively looks at the elderly mother of the child, who in turns looks at her child and hugs him protectively, saying to him in that comforting voice only a mother with child can possess. . ::/:: . . Mother:'Yes dear. . . .Mr.Hero is going to be just fine, he's just doing his Human Torch impression. . . .' Boy :'Can I do the impression too?' Jeremy : 'No son, I don't think that's a good idea. . .not unless you wanna have 'crispy franks and beans' like Mr.Hero over there. . .' Jessica:'Shouldn't we go help him J?' Jeremy : 'We will. . . . I just wanna commit this moment to memory for when I'm having a bad day.' . . ::/:: . .The crowd continues to watch on in awe as our newly found hero kicks, screams, and runs about in the grass fields of the small town continually yelling in pain as the flame sears away at him. . ::/:: . . LATER THAT DAY. . . . PSM and EGM magazines litter the floor and a glass coffee table is within the center of this humble living room, a circular brown mountie hat resting atop it. Jeremy reaches over with his free hand and picks it up, spinning the cap in his hand playfully as he says. . ::/:: . . Jeremy :'I used to wonder if an aptitude test was required to be a mountie. . .with Rent on the force, my questions have been answered.' Jessica:'Oh you are so bad Jeremy. . .I don't know why I'm with you.' *grins* Jeremy:'You know. . .just like you knew who's name it was last name. . .' Rent-A-Hero:'OOO, WHAT NAME?! DID YOU GUYS PLAY CLUE OR SOMETHIN AND SHE GUESSED RIGHT!! MAN THAT MUSTA BEEN WIZZER GUYS!' . . ::/:: . .
