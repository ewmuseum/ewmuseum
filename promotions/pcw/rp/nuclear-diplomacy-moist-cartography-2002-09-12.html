Nuclear Diplomacy - Moist Cartography

12 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12th September The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles Book Four: Distopian Fantasies…… RP#1 for Fusion and Turmoil (Versus D-Kay Calyx/Mandrake...)… ChaseTopia Chapter Three: Nuclear Diplomacy 2 - The Sequel - Part One... 'Life is like trying to commit suicide with a toothbrush. You're all geared up to do it but decide to brush your teeth first.' ~(Narration as read by Chris Chase)~ Did you miss me? Seriously now, did you really miss me?Of course you didn't miss me, I was never gone in the first place. It was almost as if I was watchingover you like some feverish pervert, except that I'm not trying to peep on you whilst you're in thebuff. No one's trying to, who in their right mind would want to? Put that damned thing away...it'snot that impressive. It doesn't stand out like a soldier in a crowd of seagulls, it doesn't equate in theslightest to the grand country of ChaseTopia. Does my ego look big in this promo? Or have the guysat production using a distorted lens for a change? Thirteen bullets, thirteen fallen citzens, thirteenpissed off militants, thirteen more bullets, thirteen pissed off and very dead militants...ChaseTopia isat war. The declaration of it wasn't as drawn out and pathetic as any of us had first thought. In fact,it was pretty gratifying to airmail a guerrilla off-shoot state a picture of my ass and another of a verymiddle finger. It was the 'Fuck you' to end all 'Fuck you's - or was that the mother of all 'Fuck you's?Just because I'm a Smart ass, it doesn't mean that I can't confuse myself... Thirteen bullets was all it took. Thirteen measly pieces of lead...it might as well have been a hundredand thirteen for all I cared. Thirteen bullets from a small group of Argentinian Guerrillas, who had given up on the state of economic turmoil in their home country, and whilst in a drunken stupor, created a Militant state in the forests of the Andes, pissed off at the rest of South America. Including,for some strange reason, the small flourishing young state of ChaseTopia, five-hundred miles off thePacific coast of South America. A lonely little island supposedly ruled by an egomaniac of a professionalwrestler and his moronic referee buddy, a dictatorship without the pitfalls of Nazi Germany or thecomplications of the Castro regime in Cuba. That's what drugs will do for you, they'll take your senseof logic and set it out to sea on a blazing pyre. Instead of being a real dictatorship, ChaseTopia becamea flourishing, if small, Capitalist state. In the space of two months, it had became a multi-nation country,complete with immigrants from all over the world that made up nearly all of the population...all ninetythousand of them. Thirteen dead citizens of ChaseTopia. It prompted the largest uproar in the history of the state - it waspreceded by the uproar caused by Dustin Downey's faltering hygene problems. It was the poisonoustree frogs that were to blame for the latter. The Argentinian Guerrillas didn't have a problem with treefrogs, they just had itchy trigger fingers. Pissed off that UN Sanctions meant that any attack on a SouthAmerican state would result in a severe shitkicking, they took it out on a little known state that no decent politician would care too much for. The Independent state of ChaseTopia, instead, was a possiblymore beneficial target for them. And so, the thirteen militants that made up their first, if small, barrageagainst ChaseTopia, sped across the South Pacific in a modified speedboat at the dead of night... '~(Mumbling mouthful of untranslated Spanish. Do I look like a Spanish-English dictionary to you?)~' 'Subtitle: Muahahaha! By our South American Gods! These Tacos are the greatest thing since...Tacos! What the fuck do you want with my gun? Don't make me pop a cap in your perverted ass, you freeloader!' 'Subtitle: Would the two of you quit arguing? The whole idea of being stealthy is that we don't let theseinfidels know that we're coming. Do you two want to die? Sure, death would be like a vacation for the pair of you; in that you'd be stuck in a strange place with only one pair of underwear...' 'Subtitle: Would we be given full-board accommodation and access to an all-night lua bar?' 'Subtitle: How should I fucking know? I haven't died yet...and I'm not willing to die that soon. So shutyour mouth before you force me to get the tape!' The South Pacific can be unforgiving for those who are stupid enough to frequent its waters in faultyand wholly inadequate transport. But adrenaline was never a great friend with the neurons of the head,and war can remove the sensations of intelligence from even the most apt men. They say that the confusioncan leave a man groping at an increasingly distant reality, absorbed in the swampy, thick soup of war,clutching a rifle with shaking hands, watching comrades fall to the ground in front of them. The scarsremain, like incessant whiners pining for success in any form, at any cost. The Argentine troops knew alltoo well about the mental detoriation that can only ever be derived from Xavier Kannon promos and war,a putrefying mess that drives its victims into a deranged stupor, but they didn't necessarily care. By the timethey were on the boat, it didn't matter to them how they'd return to their homelands, instead, they founda better tonic - they argued. Arguing was second nature to them - waging war was just a poorly timed result.One day, it'd cause them to self-destruct, to fail...to ultimately die. And they'd be martyrs in their own eyes,idiots in everyone else's. Greed can consume men. It never corrupted me. I'm just a lonely operative of the grappling arts, I don'tbelong here. Pushed over the brink by the desire to be more, pushed into self-annihilation by the realisationof an inadequacy that should have been poisoned like a mutated rat or poor communist leader. I was backto square one, back among the crowd, back where the unidentified bodies fall. Fucking obscurity, I knewit like a hated enemy. It has been my most effective enemy, it was ACTUALLY capable of ending myexistence here, in this deposed form. The only match to the brilliance of Chase. What was I saying aboutgreed consuming men? Ah...I remember now... Water throws itself against the rugged metal sides of the converted speedboat, it's engine gunning awaymoderately as it races down the sea, underneath the stained steel sky above. Thunder claps in the distance,large squalls kick up in the presence of a chaotic storm... 'Subtitle: Jesus fucking Christ, this weather is enough to make anyone reconsider an attack on a smallisland nation - wouldn't you say, Captain?' 'Subtitle: MEH!!' 'Subtitle: What in blue fucking skies of El Dorado is that supposed to mean, fuckhead? Do you thinkthat even for some reason that swearing in a wrestling promotion is viable these days? I'll be fucked ifI can ever figure out why a pair of sweaty over-grown men would want to throw each other aroundwhilst cursing like a drunken Scotsman. Aye Caramba, is that a Gopher that you've got there?!! (Hey,if you've got a problem with the translator, take it up with the producers and financial benefactors!)' If there's one thing that I've learnt from the past five minutes, it's that something always goes wrong.In that case, it was the case of the whole debacle with a faulty translator. But look closely. Cartographysuffers from some major fall-backs - not all maps are water proof. Not all people can read them. Notall people can afford to take the time dealing with the meticulous and sometimes over-baked planningparticipated around a map for several hours. Torrents of Sea water pour over the side of the boat as itbolts through mountainous swells. Swells that could topple a man and bury him with 100 metric litres ofpounding water. A navigator holed up in the innards of the boat angrily shakes his head as water spillsover onto his map board, drenching the paper with a crescendo of salty moisture. Sexual frustration hadleft the navigator with only one passion. He had an extremely intimate relationship with a carbon stickand papyrus pulp. Nothing beats the classic techniques, at least in the sense of tradition and blatantinadequacy. The minute grains of carbon on the map were simply eroded away by the continual hammering from the infernal compound of Hydrogen and Oxygen - that drove the Navigator over the edge. 'Subtitle: THAAAT'S IT!! I've HAD it! This is the LAST frickin' time that these cretins forget the fuckingpolyurethan and perspex!' Grasping a rifle hanging on the rack beside him, he limbers up and out of his seat...heading for the outside.Perplexed crew members look on...perplexed. Well, what exactly do you expect? Wouldn't you be confusedif one of your seemingly sane, yet map-obsessed comrades had suddenly, without prior warning, transformedinto some inanely wanton lunatic toting a loaded rifle and a reinforced frown? It's the natural reaction, toflinch like a shy elementary-schooler from his female High School Baby-sitter - People put themselves in thatkind of position...obviously. Crew Member: ~(Subtitled)~ Hey, HEY! Put that DAMNED gun down! Navigator: ~(Subtitled)~ ...Random Censored Profanities... Obviously we're dealing with an inferior production team here...BANG! BANG! BANG! That felt better. The last thing I want is a bunch of f**king inconsistencies that seem to litter wrestlingpromotions like shreds of the morning newspaper. Whoops...I take those asterisks back. PCW2 was a meccafor cocky assholes and seething dirty hicks. That, at least explains the presence of myself and a certain ChrisSloboda respectively - but not D-Kay Calyx. Need I mention 'Black Attack'? It's killing my credibility to thinkthat he actually wants a match with me. Although, considering how damned bone-idle I've been over the pastthree months, it's not difficult to come to the conclusion that my reputation has already been dragged througha sodden bush about a million times. Ooh...that's fucking original. Pick on the has-been, why don't you? I can't tolerate hypocritical asses like those who run this fricking federation, the incessant annoyance is drilling into my skull with a blunt utensil. Working for Xavier Kannon is comparable to beating yourself with a spoon - it's both stupid and utterly pointless. And therein lies the difficulty in avoiding the problem - my livelihood is in the balance.What a time. It kills the motivation in a vicious manner...beating all effort with a shit-kicking comparable tohaving a piano dropped on your forehead. Your life stops with ABS. Unfortunately enough, that doesn't necessarilymean that you'll skid into a brand new life all of the sudden - it just gets stuck in the emotional traffic... The same happened to the navigator... Crew Member: ~(Subtitled)~ Look Juan, I know that you're pissed off. I can understand that, but killing the restof us isn't going to do you any fucking favours, is it? Without a crew, you'll be at the mercy of the seas. And bythe name of El Pollo Diablo, the demon of the waters will engulf you in flames of watery death! Juan the Navigator: ~(Subtitled)~ I'd rather face the demon of the waters than be forced into bringing downa fucking CANADIAN! A FUCKING INEPT CANADIAN WITH A DEAD FUCKING CAREER!! So what if hebeat Xavier Kannon THREE times, so what if he's defeated some of the great PCW wrestlers - they say that he'sFUCKING GAY! GAY! You know, 90% of the people on this planet are morons... Crew Member: ~(Subtitled)~ You believe anything, don't you fuck-head? Juan the Navigator: DON'T....BANG!...CALL...BANG!...ME...BANG!...FUCKHEAD!!! BAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAANG!! It's safe to say that the navigator's sexual frustration had gone to his head, literally. Gunshots ring the air as he firesupon his crew member's lifeless body, putting a pound of lead into the blood riddled corpse of the fourteenth crewmember...and that's how the thirteen men that were to land aboard ChaseTopia came to be. But that, will cometo pass in the second part of this drama... To Be Continued... Never underestimate the ability of your peers to take your apparent laziness seriously... And never underestimate their ability to fabricate such idiotic bullshit... Ho-Hum...and D-Kay succumbs to the brilliance of Chase - the Eternal Underachiever...
