Confessions.

15 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/15/02 Confessions “Hello, My Child, how long has it been since your last confession?” Like the words of Godliness pouring through my empty veins. Waiting to burst in sin I stay there motionless with words being the only way to express ones self. So there I was, on my knees, praying for forgiveness… Bless me Father, for I have sinned, it has been about one week since my last confessional appearance………… “And tell me in what ways you’ve sinned, my child?” The priest’s confident and repetitious tone utters. I have been distrustful, to myself and to my peers. I have acted in a manner of which any other man would hold his head in shame. I have lost what dignity I had. I have tried to play with people’s minds, when the only mind being played with was that of my own. I have looked past difference, but only realized that I’m the only one that’s truly different. And I despise God for that reason, and yet I know it is a sin that I do so, and I repent on the obvious. I have lusted, like so many times before my body has struggled for the love that I have not received as a child. I have tried to use my mind, but my mind seems to be just a blur in these situations. For not using the gifts God has granted me, my mind, my heart, my conscience. I have these gifts, but yet I’m too incompetent to utilize any of them. God, grant me the strength to be stronger, grant me what I desire, and I shall stay faithful to you for all time to come. “My son, you seem confused to say the least. You act as if you’ve let down the Lord, when all you’ve done is showed him how great of a man you lived to be. Your sins are minor and your desires are expectable. Live not to ask of what God can do for you, but for what you can do for God. With that said, go now my son. Say three Hail Mary’s to grant to help your realize the struggles that the Virgin Mary went through to work through her temptations. Say an Our Father to show God that he is the primary male in your life and that you still want him there to guide you. Then, reflect on your life and if your mentally fit to believe in yourself and those around you.” As the priest finished Romantico took a deep breath and recollected on what he had just been told. “May I ask one more thing of you, Father?” Romantico asked a little bit confused and puzzled. “Of course my son.” The priest answered kindly and showing concern. “Why can I not feel emotions?” I’ve never asked this to a priest before but I felt I had to. “No emotions you say, that’s quite odd. Are you sure you don’t feel anything?” The priest asked obviously not knowing an answer. “Yes, I’m sure. I don’t have feelings, and without feelings I sometimes can’t control myself. It’s not that I’ve never been taught morals it’s just I don’t have the heart other seem to have.” Romantico replied. “All I can tell you son, is that God will grant you your pleas. He’s hearing you in heaven and your good deeds won’t go unearned. And believe me, he understand your situation and why you have to do what you have to do. Just remember that God created us all, and there’s nothing about our lives he can’t answer. Prayer is the path to your destination.” The priest answered giving Romantico a new more promising hope. I left the confessional and proceeded to the alter. I looked up at the crucifix and realized what it symbolized. I wished to one day become a symbol, a symbol of greatness and spiritual purity. To do so I’d have to make new strives and conform to the new life I’ve been granted. I proceeded with my prayers and spoke them with all of my heart. When I left that day, I did as the priest asked and reflected. And so comes the story of my life. Week after week in heart-felt confessional, I spill out my guts to an interpreter of God. The man high above is listening to me, and even though I know of what I do, and realize that God may not see them fit, I still apologize for having my life lead this path where I am destined to sin. My sin is sometimes impossible to control as over and over again my body triumphs over my mind. The day I’m able to control it is the day I become the man I set out to be on this earth of ours. The spiritual cleansing I need affects my mind in so many ways. I need to get everything I possibly can off of my chest, so that I can live guilt free. This week, well this week, it’s not the same. Everything is different you see. My ego has had the best of me, and what I once thought of as fiction is true. El Romantico has lost. Words to a simple man, but yet disgrace to a prideful man. I did not want to go out in that way. I am a fighter not a coward. I blame myself for the events that occurred. I as stubborn as I was, tried to play mind games and show off my talent. Instead I just fell in a pit that I’ll now have to climb myself out of. THE MISTAKE WILL NOT HAPPEN AGAIN! I repeat for I am a man that holds no room for mistake. Mistake is in the past and that’s what Turmoil is, the past. THE FUTURE IS EVERYTHING! I will make sure I treat it like everything. I’ll do my best to overcome the predictions and prove my self once again as a dominant force. I am manIn my path are different disturbancesI am a manI look past them and live in confidenceI am a manBut sometimes they get the best of meI am a manAnd they blind me so I can’t seeI am a manBut when that road becomes completely clearI am a manI can look ahead from year to yearI am a manWhat it takes to be the bestI am a manIs to distinguish yourself from the restI am a manI am a fighterI am a loverI am a followerI strive to perfectI fight to protect I am everything God made me to beAnd that is why he hears my pleasI do everything that I canFor I, oh I am a man.
