I can't even go to the movies without trouble following me

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-5-02 The room is dimly lit by a few lights along the walls and by the lights outlining the stairs leading to the upper rows of chairs. Other than the many rows of chairs, and an empty screen, the room is empty. Footsteps are heard walking down the empty hallway, and Brandi rounds around the corner carrying a drink in one hand and a small bag of popcorn in the other. She carefully scoots down a row of seats and sets her drink down in one of the holders. Brandi looks around the empty theater, shrugs, and takes a seat next to her drink. She adjusts her skirt and crosses her legs, waiting for the movie to start. Brandi tries to relax. She lays her head back against the seat and eats the popcorn slowly. She has a cut just underneath her hairline on her forehead that is starting to heal. It’s a reminder of the brutal handicap match with Snow. Since then, her head has been hurting more often, and she can’t really seem to concentrate on anything for very long. Damn this headache. Actually, damn Snow. No, it’s probably my fault. I shouldn’t have jumped in front of that chairshot. It’s just…I couldn’t bear to see him hurt Rent A Villain anymore. Of course, I didn’t stop him. Well, at least I didn’t have to watch it. I probably should go get my head checked out though. I might have a concussion or something. Nah, I can’t go see another doctor. If I keep going in there for stupid things like this, they aren’t going to let me wrestle. I can’t afford to not be able to wrestle on Fusion. Not with a Cruiserweight title shot. Wait…what movie am I here to see? Ahhh, that’s right. Signs. Some alien movie that Lindsey came so see. She said she couldn’t sleep all night. So why did I come see it? I can’t exactly afford to lose anymore sleep. She said she couldn’t go see it again, so here I am. Sitting in some empty theater, all alone. Signs, signs, everywhere the signs. Blocking out the scenery… Brandi can’t remember the rest of the words, so she just hums the tune. She didn’t realize that a few other people have joined her in the room, and that they’re sitting a few rows above her and off to the side. Their eyes are burning holes in the back of her head, she so turns around to face them. Brandi- Hi, is there something I can help you with? The two men just look at each other and laugh. Then they look back down at Brandi and shake their heads no. Brandi sighs and turns back around. They’ve never seen a woman at the movies before? They just can’t go anywhere without staring and gawking, I guess. Typical man for you. Well, at least not all men are like that. Rent A Villain’s face floats through Brandi’s mind, causing her to smile. Yeah, not all of them are like that. Brandi giggles. She stops when something hard bounces off the back of her head. She turns around to see the two men who where staring at her laughing. Brandi puts one arm on the back of the chair and turns around to confront them. Brandi- Do you have a problem? The men laugh again and shake their heads. Brandi- Well, then would you mind not throwing things at me? The bigger man shakes his head, and then laughs some more. The other man follows along and laughs loudly. Brandi shakes her head and starts to turn around, but stops when one of the men starts talking. Man- Hey! Do we know you from somewhere? Brandi shakes her head and turns back around, thankful to find that the men leave it at that. She checks her watch, and settles back into her seat with the popcorn in hand. At least the movie is starting soon and I won’t have to deal with these morons much longer. The movie theater is filling up more quickly now. It’s filled with the chatter of whispered conversations. In two seats close to the door, a young couple sits holding hands and listening to each other attentively. Brandi looks over at them and smiles. Signs, signs, everywhere the sign. Blocking out the scenery… Once again, she starts humming the rest of the song. I’ve got to find out the rest of the words to that song. After the movie I’ll go home and put the CD on, relax in a nice bath, and then maybe call up Rent A Villain to see how he’s doing. Brandi reaches up to brush the stray hair out of her face and tuck it carefully behind her ear. Her hand brushes over the cute on her forehead, and she frowns. Damn, that still stings. Thankfully it’ll be gone soon. I just can’t help thinking that I could have walked away with a lot more than just a couple of cuts…or I might not have walked away at all. If it hadn’t been for Rent A Villain I don’t know what I would have done. Oh! And then Darkstar running out to save RAV. I really should thank him for that. I just don’t understand why he helped… Something else bounces off of Brandi’s shoulder and comes to a stop on the floor a few rows away from her. She turns back around. The two men look at her, wave, and laugh. Brandi- What the hell is your problem? The bigger man laughs at the seriousness on Brandi’s face. Man- Ya hear that, Carl? The ‘lil lady wants to know what our problem is. Carl- Yeah, Vic. I heard her. The two men laugh again. Brandi- What? The two of you come to the movies to throw things at people? Brandi grabs a handful of popcorn and, out of anger, throws it in the direction of the two men. The popcorn, not being very aero-dynamic, falls short of the men. As it falls, they laugh. Vic- You’re gonna have to do better than that, sweets. Brandi- Sweets? Brandi stands up to better face the men. Vic- Yeah, sweets….Hey! I know you! You’re the one that’s pickin’ on that blind dude. What’s his name? Brandi- I’m not picking on anyone; I just have a match with The End. Vic- The End. Yeah, that’s the guy. Carl- Whatcha doin’ picking on a blind dude? Ain’t ya feeling bad for it? Brandi- No, I don’t feel bad for him. He’s doing perfectly well. He’s held the Cruiserweight title for awhile now, and I’ve watched him wrestle. He can definitely hold his own in the ring. Vic laughs and looks Brandi up and down. He stands up and takes a few steps towards her. By this time the rest of the people in the theater have heard their argument and are listening and watching them carefully. Vic- I betcha you aren’t so bad yourself. Why don’t we go back to my house and you can show me a couple of those moves of yours? This comment feeds Brandi’s anger and she reaches down, grabs the bag of popcorn, and hurls it at the large man. It seems to go in slow motion. The popcorn flies out of the bag and hits the man right in the chest, leaving smears of butter all over his black muscle shirt. The bag hits him on the chin, and falls to a rest at his feet. Brandi giggles, but the man’s face grows red with anger. At his sides, his hands make fists and he takes a few more steps in Brandi’s direction. She moves out of the row of chairs and stands on one of the steps. The man is now a few steps above her. A strong smell drifts down the steps to Brandi. She sniffs the air and makes a disgusted face. Brandi- Jesus! Don’t you ever shower? Brandi grins, knowing that will only feed the man’s anger. He steps down to the next step. Vic- You’re dead, b*tch. Brandi laughs, but realizes that the man has every intention of harming her. He makes a sudden lunge for her, and Brandi sidesteps back into the row of chairs. The man tries to catch his balance on the step, but he’s standing on one of his untied shoelaces and falls headfirst down the rest of the stairs. The other man runs down the stairs to his side. Carl- Vic! Carl starts towards Brandi with revenge written on his face. She shrugs and starts down the stairs towards him. She gets to the bottom of the stairs and looks down at Vic. Brandi- Hey, I didn’t mean for that to happen. You alright? Carl swings at her, but Brandi blocks it. Brandi- Hey! He was the one coming after me! Carl swings at Brandi again, but she manages to block that one two. A few other men in the theater manage to grab Carl and pull him away from Brandi before he is able to take his frustration out on her. They quickly drag him out of the theater in search of an usher. Vic stands up carefully and looks over at Brandi. Vic- Sorry. Vic slowly limps out of the theater after his friend. The others in the theater clap. Brandi winks at them, takes a bow, and sits back down in her seat. She takes a long drink of her soda and settles down to watch the movie. As Brandi walks through the dark parking lot towards her car, she happens to glance up at the sky. She stops dead in her tracks and peers through a cloud. She’s almost positive she saw some bright orange lights disappear into the cover of the cloud. Brandi shrugs and starts walking again.
