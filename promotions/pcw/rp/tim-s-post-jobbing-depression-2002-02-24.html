Tim's Post-Jobbing Depression

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Tim's Post-Jobbing Depression Featuring: Tim Walden Date: Feb. 24 The scene opens in a bar..Yes, it is rather lacking of distinctiveness as far as description goes, its mahogany namesake being tended to by a 40-some year-old, balding and greying man, the rest of the bar somewhat darkened as Tim Walden, clad in the typical Wild Deuces shirt and blue jeans, sits upon the second barstool from the left, just past the empty one, as it seems as if most of the patrons have drunken themselves under the table..A window beyond the bar reveals the bitter darkness of the outdoors....Tim's looking almost somewhat tipsy, his eyes glazed over with absolute depression upon his face...It's been a cold, heartless night for him, his gaze piercing, but not effectively as per usual, merely in an almost pitiful manner. He's obviously still suffering the wrath of his own mind's disappointment in the body that it wrestled with, first failing miserably to finally bring him the victory he'd been drained emotionally, mentally, and psychologically in his earlier Aymin matches, all to get to the point where one victory would bring him to the World Title match at March Madness...And coming up not merely short, not robbed again by the illegal activities of International Incident or some new foe, and not even losing as the result of an illegal pin or a satisfying disqualification with illegal object, the defeat purely his own failure to earn the opportunity he long dreamed of...The chance to go to a PCW pay-per-view and fight for the gold, the prestige of the World Title belt..The chance taken away from him by his own failures. Tim would naturally be depressed by this fact...But more happened that night to harm his fragile ego. Later on in the night, Tim had his first-round match in his second Global Title Tournament..He'd hoped that he would not repeat his previous GTT performance, in which he lost to D-Kay Calyx in a mild upset, but he'd been very confident going into his match with PWA wrestler and all-around underdog Kevin Sleeper...Sleeper battled merely fairly, but the advantage of not having come from a match earlier in the night eventually wore down Tim, and he again lost..To a PWA wrestler, in a match he'd been heavily favoured to win, in the first round of a major tournament..This would explain the almost-finished shot of Scotch sitting in front of him, as Tim's head shifts from being held high on his shoulders...To falling, faintly hitting the bar face-down as Tim, normally stoic but released by the alcohol, lets out a loud sob...The bartender turns.'What's the matter, kiddo?', the sympathetically curious voice queries to the back of the head laying stiffly on the counter and blocking the path of the dishcloth...Tim raises his head, the tears in his eyes now trickling down his cheeks; it will obviously be a tough thing for him to retrace his crestfallen state's history for discussion. 'Well,' he sniffs, 'I'm Tim Walden, a sort-of well-known pro wrestler from the PCW...If you didn't know, of course. Tonight, I had two really important matches, you see..Firstly, I finally got the opportunity to defeat my long-time opponent, this Australian porn-star who'd beaten me twice before, both times under either extenuating circumstances, firstly with a third man battling in our match, then with one of his cronies interfering against me to cost me the opportunity to save the viewership of the fed from being further represented by a pile of moral dung without any redeeming values to his existence, let alone to the general well-being of the people he represents and their ethical standards..I failed the people though, giving it everything I had, and failing to put up a fight nonetheless..My endurance having given out on me, I fell to him, losing respect, self-confidence, and a World Title shot in one 3-count...Then, just an hour or two later, I was upset by a kid who's still learning how to wrestle in a match everyone'd thought I could win..It's just so damn much to have to live with, falling so far, so quickly...' The bar owner inhaled deeply, realizing this man would be a difficult case to handle...'Maybe you just had a bad night or something, you just need to try and relax, a couple of good days to release the stress'll do you good..' His optimistic advice met with a low-pitched groan. 'I can't do that! You see, I've been signed up for two matches..Firstly, tomorrow I've got to catch a red-eye flight to Los Angeles and make an appearance in a big match in the Playboy Mansion to prevent trouble for my career..I probably won't make much of an impact now any more, but I want to at least give it my all and hope that my technical skills'll be back to their usual quality, and maybe I can just luck into the NTICW Extreme Title and spite my friend's ex who runs the fed..But then I'd be too busied up defending the belt to make it to my PCW schedule, so I probably won't go for it if it wouldn't make Mexi happier than any of the guys that are left. THEN, after that match, I have to speed up back north, into Canada to face my old stablemate, goes by the name of Big Daveylicious and hasn't done anything worth noting since he came into the PCW 4 months or so ago..' This drew a look of confusion from the middle-aged man. 'Wait, he hasn't done ANYTHING of note after four months in PCW?', he quizzically asked.. 'I mean, it's got to be pretty tough to go that long without managing to win SOMETHING...There's what, 7 or 8 titles to fight for, 3 shows a week, and 17 weeks in the time he's been there? That'd mean that in 51 shows, probably with a good deal of title shots, he STILL has nothing to his name, not even some sort of moment that improved his star?' Tim nodded grimly. 'Big Daveylicious? No way...The first group I joined into, Thugs 4 Hire, Big Daveylicious was constantly getting the hype, the attention from Badge, the guy running the show...And what'd he do to deserve it? Not damn much...He just repeatedly blew title shots constantly, first for the Rising Star title, which with my friend 'Red Rock', and that new kid Stormbringer being the exceptions has been competed for by worthless pieces of crap..THEN, he got some opportunities to go after the Tag Team Titles working with Tom Walczak, and they lost every time..But look at what that Polish guy's up to now, making a big splash and big cash in that OTHER fed, then look at Big Daveylicious, who spent his next couple months trying to get favors from, or at least beat up the women of PCW...And usually failing miserably. Eventually, he finally managed to get a fluke pin over the then-Extreme Champ..Only to immediately get beaten up and lose the elimination match, to nobody's surprise, and America lost interest in Big Daveylicious...Or at least what interest they had...But now, just as the TV Title is on the brink of salvage, as someone felt it proper to award me another fair title shot in 7 days' time, albeit not another chance against that porn-star son of a bitch but rather to take on the world-famous Miyagawa, a man who has my respect, I am threatened for the right to truly claim for the gold in my match with Big D, the winner being declared #1 contender for a future title shot..And now, at this point, I worry that he might be able to defeat me in my time of weakness...' The bartender starts to speak, but is soon cut off..'Wait...I think I've finally figured out what's holding me back....It's not poor stamina, lack of talent or drive, or anything like that......This is a case most truly disturbing for me, a foe that I've faced down once before and defeated...But only temporarily, as I won by running away that time, and I've come back to find that it didn't lose, but merely lied in waiting....Now, I must face off against it one more time, and I may not truly claim victory until I have managed to run it out of my life once and for all..It is not human, it is not animal, vegetable, or mineral, it is not easily blocked...I must leave immediately, hail a taxi, and catch a quicker flight to near a place of safety in Los Angeles...' Tim gets up, and walks out towards the door, leaving a 20-dollar bill, and the drink, unfinished, before turning briefly back toward the bartender... 'I must flee....the effects...of The Syndrome.' And with that, Tim Walden had disappeared beyond the front door of the bar. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
