The Tunnel

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12th March 2002 The walls begin to shake and pulses begin to run through the floor. Two bright beams of light illuminate the darkness and in a matter of seconds a train whizzes by. A white light beaming out of each and every window as people take their usual paths home. Whether it be reading the paper, talking on the cellular phone or just sitting with a briefcase on their lap, the citizens of New York are stuck in the same old boring routine of everyday life… Papers begin to fly around from off of the stained grayish/white concrete tiles of the subway platform as the breaks on the train squeal and grind against the tracks as the train slowly comes to a halt. The train eases itself to become stationary and aligns itself perfectly in between the pylons that support the roof of the platform, there is yet another loud gushing sound as the electronic doors which are evenly spaced along the trains carriage’s open. It’s like a floodgate, a million and one people flood out of the doors all at once, in a flurry of hands, feet and briefcases! Suddenly the dead station fills with life and the atmosphere begins to grow, but all of that fades away in a matter of seconds! All the people who just got off the train disappear up the flight of stairs somewhere behind the platform and the only remittance of them ever even being near the platform is the soft clattering of footsteps up the stairs which slowly fade off into the distance as the last straggler finishes his trip up to the ground level! Once again the subway station is dead, all except for one man squatting down on a bench which is directly in line with one of the subway doors. The train seems to notice the man squatting on the bench because it just stands there, lifeless with its doors hanging open as the man on the bench just sits and stares! The man who has his head hung low raises it a little and out of nowhere exhales a huge cloud of smoke, revealing himself to be none other than Charlie Loc. After exhaling the huge cloud of smoke, the platform seems to get a little darker as Charlie stretches and steps down from the bench. Tap~! Tap~! Tap~! Tap~! The sound of footsteps echo off in the distance and sound as though they’re coming closer as Charlie quickly looks left to right…as he does this the footsteps seem to be getting closer and the echoing becomes louder and clearer. Charlie shakes his head and pulls the collar up on his black trench coat before tucking his hands in his pocket and putting his head down low and walking through the doors of the train…. His boots clank on the metal floor of the subway carriage as he grabs ahold of one of the overhead rails and looks outside where he just walked in. As he looks outside the tapping of the feet becomes rapid and closer and as it does a strange grunting noise can be heard coming from somewhere in the same direction. 'Ehhhhhh….WAIT!' The shouting of a gravely voice can be heard in the distance as out of nowhere a huge, dirty looking man appears on the other side of the platform, running and holding a brown paper bag. The familiar whooshing sound of the doors beginning to close starts and the lights on the train flicker as the dirty man on the other side of the platform stops to catch a breath before taking a swig out of something that’s concealed in the brown paper bag. Just as the doors are about to close, Charlie reaches out and sticks his foot in between the two door flaps and they remain open, stopping the train from taking off. The dirty man who has now consulted to walking smiles a huge toothy grin as he walks slowly up to the doors that Charlie is holding open. As the dirty man grabs the doors, Charlie removes his foot and the man stumbles around, his feet clanking on the metallic floor of the subway cart. Just as the man stumbles in the doors…WHOOSH~! They slam shut with the sound of rushing air and literally make the dirty man jump, collapsing in a crumpled heap on the floor as the engine of the train churns and begins to hum as the train takes off! 'Ahhh shit! Why does this always happen?!' The dirty man mumbles to himself as a reddish liquid trickles out of the brown paper bag and runs into the ripples of the metal on the floor. The man turns to his side and spits a glob of reddish blood onto the floor next to him as he pulls himself upright and touches his lip and feels the bristle on his face. A pinkish colored tongue appears out of his lips and comes to lick the spot where his hand was just moments before. The man chuckles to himself and looks next to him seeing the brown paper bag lying on its side and he quickly puts it upright…. Suddenly the carriage vibrates and the bottle topples over once more and the man falls back onto his ass. A pissed off look crosses over his face as he begins to mumble words to himself yet again. 'Just my fuckin’ luck! Lost my alcohol AND I’m stuck on a subway with a fuckin’ rice picker! What are the odds of that?!' The man shakes his head and reaches down for the toppled over bottle inside the paper bag and a smirk crosses over his face. The lights flicker in the carriage for a split second as Charlie walks over and steps up onto a blue bench before squatting down and resting his head in his hands. The dirty man in the background lets out a chuckle as he picks himself up to his feet and swaggers a little with the bottle in his hand….only this time the carriage was perfectly still! Clearly intoxicated the man lets out a roar before launching the bag over in Charlie’s direction, causing it to crash on the ground. 'FUCKIN’ BASTARDS!' SMASH~! The bag with the bottle inside it crashes on the ground at Charlie’s feet as the lights flicker once more as Charlie looks up into the direction of the dirty old drunk. Yet another huge toothy smile crosses over the drunk man’s face as he pulls up his pants and scratches his balls before making his way over to Charlie who is still looking, emotionless at him! 'Wutcha think your lookin’ at ya Rice Picker?' The man slurs his speech as Charlie continues to look at him with a stone cold glint in his Asian eyes. The man chuckles yet again and scratches his balls for the second time as he waits for Charlie to reply. The train carriage rumbles as the drunken man begins to speak again, 'What’s the matter ya…ya…ya Chink? Can’t cha speak English or sumthin?! Answer me or I’ll….woah~!' The man stumbles and falls on to the seat that Charlie is squatting on causing Charlie to jump up and step onto the ground and size up the alcoholic, smelling, dirty old man, to which he gets yet another huge bellow. 'What you call me?' Charlie stands in front of the drunk with his hands in his pockets staring up at him as the man indeed towers above him. 'You heard me ya bloody goo…gook~!' The man slurs his speech once again before letting out another loud bellow, opening his mouth wide and giving Charlie a smell of the alcohol he just had and taste too as he spits when he talks! Charlie turns to the side and wipes away the spit on his nose with his shoulder before looking directly back up at the man who’s making himself look bigger by puffing his chest out. 'I lookin’ at you….white boy!' Out of nowhere Charlie let’s out a smile and tilts his head onto the side, turning it into a lop-sided grin more than a smile. The drunken man gets a glint of anger in his eye and opens his mouth to speak before stopping and leaning back with a confused face. Outside the subway windows, amidst the darkness of the long tunnel a neon sign can be seen flashing on the wall…'Time Square Station, 1.5miles' All of a sudden the man lurches forwards into Charlie’s face before letting out yet another huge bellow of a laugh. He smacks his stomach before talking. 'Ain’t you that superstar Chuck Loc or something? The one on Television?' Charlie just stares at the man and lets out a deep breath of air through his nostrils as the man continues to speak. 'Hah, it is you! Wait…if it is…why the hell are you riding a subway when you gots them fancy limousines? How come you ain’t gettin’ laid by all them famous playmates?!' Charlie just sits and stares intently as the train slowly begins to slow down as it makes it’s way to the station. 'YOU THINK YER SO GOOD DON’T YOU?! YOUR NOTHING BUT A WRESTLING GOOK WHO DOESN’T KNOW HOW TO FIGHT FER SHIT! PUT EM UP YA FUCKIN’ ASIAN!' The man yells out the top of his voice, blurting it out from literally nowhere. The man screws up his face and leans back before dropping his right arm and swinging a huge right hook at Charlie! As the swing continues the man’s face becomes red as he concentrates all of his power into the punch, to which Charlie steps to the side and sinking one of his own right into the drunken man’s mid section, forcing him to double over as he begins to start taunting the man. 'What you laughin’ at now?! Ha ha ha! I think this fuckin’ funny! White boy think he can fight, then he get dropped by Asian!' A sinister smile crosses Charlie’s face as grabs the man by the man buy the back of the shirt before removing his left hand from his trench coat pocket and sinking a left uppercut into his face. The blood makes a splattering sound as it hits the wall of the carriage as the objects whizzing past outside the subway windows begin to slow down and the grinding of the trains breaks in through the sound of the fighting. The man lets out a bellow of pain as Charlie looks down at him, 'What the hell? You call me Rice Picker again? HUH?! WHITE BOY?! APOLOGISE OR I DROP YOU AGAIN….NOW!' Charlie screams his little lungs out at the drunken man as all he can do is blurt and mumble as he spits blood onto the floor, which bounces onto Charlie’s boots. Charlie looks down and the sinister smile on his face turns into a psychotic one and he grabs the drunk by the hair and pulls his face up to look at his own. The mans face is a bloody mess as his eyes roll to the back of his head and he begins to groan. Charlie begins to yell at him again. 'WHAT THE FUCK?! I BUY THESE BOOTS SO I KICK ANTON’S ASS, AND YOU FUCKIN’ PUT MORE WHITE BOY BLOOD ON THEM?! HUH?! WHAT THE FUCK YOU THINK I’AM?! MADE OF FUCKIN’ MONEY?! HUH?!' The carriage continues to rumble….only not from the sound of Charlie’s screaming! The subway continues to rattle and squeal as the train slowly comes to a complete halt at the 'Times Square Station' Charlie can be heard laughing as the man groans and there is another loud slapping sound as he sinks yet another hard left into his face. The familiar whooshing sound of the doors opening and closing due to the air pressure can be heard yet again as they swing open, almost inviting the two men to step off of the train and onto the platform to continue their little dual! Charlie sees the doors swing open as yet another huge grin crosses his face! He grabs the man by the shirt and turns him back towards himself as a sick thought fills his mind…. 'You know what White Boy? HUH?! I SAY YOU KNOW WHAT?! You seen what I do now, you think it all good! You tell your friends you get bashed by Charlie Loc, the Asian Muh-fuckin’ gangster! But what I do now, nothing compared what I do tonight and Friday! This nothing compared to what I can do….' Charlie finished his sentence. 'Actually White Boy….YOU NOTHING~!' Charlie leans back before sending a huge blood-stained boot right, square into the ass of the drunken man, sending him directly through the opened doors and forcing him to land with a thud, unconscious on the platform. As the doors whoosh closed yet again, the train slowly starts to pull away from the platform as Charlie can be seen cracking his knuckles and taking a look at his boots from inside the carriage. As the train slowly pulls away from the platform Charlie tucks his hands back into his pockets and steps back up on to the seat he was sitting on before, squatting down and looking around the now dead and blood-stained carriage as the train drifts off into darkness….
