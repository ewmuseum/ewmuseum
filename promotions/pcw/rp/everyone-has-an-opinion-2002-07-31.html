Everyone has an opinion.....

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-31-02 Freedom of speech is in the declaration of independence as right for the people of America. A human right, the right to speech freely on anything or anyone you wish to talk about. Since 1776 freedom of speech has cause much controversy since it was issued as a right to humans living in America. Freedom of speech has brought views of people and placed them in the forefront of a long discussion between many peoples. People like the Martin Luther King Jr. brought how they felt about African American’s rights to the American public and brought change to a nation. People in the music world let politicians and people know how they feel in their songs. The sixty’s is a good example of freedom of speech in music as many artists were talking about peace not war and there views made other peoples views change about the situation or it strikes a argument between two sides, fighting to prove there point or there opinion on the situation. Freedom of speech has brought much debate into different subjects, which may cause anger, agreement with the opinion or even war. Opinions or freedom of speech have come along way since they were issued in our declaration of human rights in the United States. Talk shows have brought dispute on different topics from teen pregnancy to girlfriends who cheat on their boyfriends with their boyfriend’s father. The news channels also take advantage of there right when they have shows such as “Hardball” or Bob O’Reilly’s show, their talk shows are hosted by two cocky fifty-year-old men that think they know everything in the world. Opinions have caused many wars between countries or religions. The current state of the Palestine and Israel conflict over land given to Israel after World War II once owned by Palestine has been going on for fifty years now. The Holocaust brought a nasty and sadistic view of someone’s opinion as Adolf Hitler believed that Jews were the root of all evil and were the causes to all the world’s problems. So Adolf Hitler exercised his right of speech to bring followers along with him to try and destroy a single race of Jewish decent because Hitler clearly did not like them for no real reason except he felt there were the causes to the world’s problems. Hitler’s opinion is not one I do not agree with nor will I ever and in the end he paid the price for his opinion because people did not like him throwing around his weight. Another clear example of opinions of curtain view that have started conflict and war is the recent tragedy of September 11th, 2001 where members of the terrorist organization, Al-Quieda attacked America because they did not like what America was represented which is life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness. Freedom is something they did not like so they inflicted the way of thinking, their opinion and attack my homeland of America. America just like the terrorists responded with there opinion of the situation which was “we stand united.” America came together and let the terrorist feel the wrath of their opinion of their organizations killing the roots of the terrorists. Opinions can cause actions and sometimes the actions can cause an all out war between two different views. Arguments strike at the roots of opinions. When an opinion is stated in front of a loud audience or even your own living room most people will respond on your thought, giving their views of the situation. Then the opinion just snowballs from an argument to a feud to a fight. Opinions cause a sense of urgency from someone to respond if they feel that what was said was false and does not meet with their values. I believe opinions are the nerve in everyone that sets people off with the snap of their fingers and that’s why I love pushing people’s buttons when I speak. I know there is people that have heard me speak and they want to strangle my throat. As they get pissed off it feeds my reason to keep pushing the buttons. Controversy is something that runs through my veins, pushing the buttons is the only way people can make themselves self destruct and the people I piss off have all this built up tension inside from what I have said and they just want to blow up and kick my ass. Which is even more reason to sit back and relax, laughing at the torture these people put themselves in. I can be compared to controversial rap artist Eminem because what I say is what I feel whether people don’t want to hear the truth or not. People also compare me to Judge Judy because I’m ruthless in what I say and what I say is what I feel and is based on my opinion of the situation. I’m not a normal person that puts their tail between their legs and just smiles when someone says something they don’t agree with. Those people are insecure and they are pressured into something they don’t believe in, they are a follower and not a leader. I on the other hand go by my gut instinct and I consider myself a leader into what I believe in. I don’t get pressured into something I feel is wrong, stupid or something I don’t agree with. There are people out there that think that they can manipulate one soul out there and change there way of life and those people that try and manipulate another person is someone that should look into a mirror and admit that they have a problem. Feelings are hurt, friends might be lost, family might never speak with you again but as long as you feel you have succeeded with what you feel then there is nothing wrong with your right to speak freely about what you want when you want and whom it is about. In life more friends are made then enemies but it is felt that someone has a opinion that you do not agree about and is consider a friend then you should tell there person your view on the situation. I think people today do not express themselves through voice. It is usually spent through art or clothing. I believe that expressing how you feel through those ways is good but it discloses what underling subjects the person is feeling. Now this is not what I was going for, I sound like a physiatrist which is not what I was going for, my point was to give the history of stating a opinion and bottling up inside and then after that all hell broke loose when I started stating how to express yourself and shit. Opinion is a powerful thing in the world. It can be the root of all evil or the root of change within a society. Opinion starts arguments and could patch friendships. Opinion is a tool that is more powerful then a fact because it’s how you feel about the situation and where you are coming from on the subject. Everyone has opinion, seem people don’t use that power, I on the other hand do and I use it for the best of my ability and that could be dangerous for those that want to test the ability…….
