Substandard Living Conditions

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/11/02 “What do you mean he QUIT?! What the hell is that supposed to mean?” demands Tyler Nelson as the scene fades in. The former CEO of PCW stands flabbergasted in his hotel room inside the local Dallas Super 8 Motel. He holds the phone close to his ear as he stands next to the extremely uncomfortable single bed. The suit that was given to him by William S. Powell and Reginald P. Lovecraft hangs from the curtain rod across the window. Tyler’s prison issue orange jumpsuit is off his torso and dangling around his waist, as if he was in the process of changing when the phone rang. He listens for several minutes, shaking his head. “Just like that, eh? Well I don’t need that little bastard anyway,” seethes Tyler disgustedly. “I have plans already set into motion that didn’t have room for a half-pint teenager and his hooker girlfriend.” Nelson pauses once again as he listens to whoever is on the other end of the phone line. After a few seconds he chimes back in. “I don’t know what I was thinking either. I thought I could finally make something of that cookie cutting bastard, but he turned out to be a dreadful disappointment and a huge waist of my precious time,” Tyler concedes. “I mean, I should have known better than to take someone under my wing that moonlights as the Super Duper Masked Jobber King.” A few more brief exchanges take place, mostly with Tyler nodding in agreement and giving the generic “Uh-huh” response. Then the conversation begins to wind down. “Thanks again for the ride the other night,” says Tyler. “That punk kid and his gang of jerk-off’s would have gotten their asses kicked if you hadn’t shown up. Then they probably would have tried to sue me for money I don’t have.” A short pause. “Exactly. Anyway, I’m getting things set up for all of us and the plans are getting set. Don’t worry about a thing, because the greediest player in the game is on the case. Uh-huh.....bye.” Tyler hangs up the phone and stretches his arms above his head, releasing a long yawn. “Rrrrroooaaaaarrrr!! Aaarrrgh!!” growls Tyler as he turns and looks around the motel room. “This room is extremely below my high standard of living. This is somewhere jobbers like JJ stay while on the road.” Another short pause by the former CEO and now convicted felon. “Damn! This doubly sucks because this IS my hometown and I’m staying in the roach motel!” Let’s take a look around the room, shall we? It’s roughly the size of a large closet, with only enough room for the bed, a nightstand, and a dresser at the end of the bed. On that dresser is a small black and white television that only gets in two channels. The mirror behind the dresser is cracked and dirty, which happens to match the walls which were meant to be white but now have a pale brown tint to them. Continuing around the crackerjack box of a room we come across the curtains that cover the window. They are a burnt orange shade with tears and rips throughout. Before we can move on we see some sort of insect scurry out from behind the curtains and dash across the wall to safety behind the dresser mirror. A little farther around we see the doorway to the rooms bathroom, which is lit by a flickering florescent light. As the camera moves past we come straight to the sour face of the former CEO, which is a light shade of green and contorted in disgust. “This is ridiculous. A man of my means should not be reduced to staying in a place such as this. If only half my meager paycheck wasn’t donated to charity I might have afforded an upgrade to the ‘tolerable living’ floor,” says Tyler. “I need to find a way to make some quick money, and fast. The bidding for PCW is going to start soon and I need some liquid assets. I’ll show all those backstabbers that did this to me that I am that much better than they are. They won’t keep me down no matter how hard they try, even placing me in the ring against the likes of JJ. Hell, JJ jobbed to Jeff Marx for Christ’s sake.” A snort from Tyler is heard as he moves around the small, single bed toward the bathroom. He walks in the room and we can get a short glimpse of the accommodations. There is a sink, a toilet, and a bathtub with showerhead. All three fixtures have rust stains and calcium deposits at least an inch thick. All that remains of the bathroom mirror is a slim sliver about three inches wide and a couple feet long. The paint peels off the walls like skin off of a snake, with the pale sea green color painful to see. Tyler sighs as pulls out some toilet paper and wipes off the seat on the toilet. “Ugh.” We fast-forward a few minutes, skipping Tyler’s shit and shower. He exits the restroom with a towel wrapped around his waist and walks over to where the suit is hanging from the curtain rod. He looks over the nice looking dark blue suit with satisfaction. “Finally something I can be proud of. A nice suit, not the quality I am accustomed to, but it will certainly do over that orange thing I was forced to wear,” says Tyler vindictively. “Can’t expect much more from the cheapskate taxpayers of this country.” Just as Tyler reaches for the hanger the suit is on he takes a sudden step backwards and his mouth falls agape. “What in the HELL is that?!” gasps Tyler. Zooming in on the suit we see what looks to be a cockroach crawling across the left lapel. Tyler frowns and slaps the bug off the suit. It flies across the room and collides with the wall, then trickles down to the floor. The former CEO grabs the Bible off of the nightstand and springs across the room, bringing the good book down hard on the roach, crushing it instantly. Tyler picks the Bible up from the floor and looks at the bottom with a satisfied grin on his face. “Take that you fucker,” sneers Tyler sadistically. “That’s what happens to little pests that want to screw around with me. Take note JJ, cause that’s what will happen to you if you decide to show up on Friday.” Tyler turns back to the suit and proceeds to put it on. Of course we don’t see the actual putting on of the suit, we just fast forward to Tyler looking at himself in the dresser mirror. He pivots from side to side, admiring how he looks. “Damn this feels good!” exclaims Tyler. “There was a time where I didn’t think I would be able to wear a suit again. This is just the first small step toward my return to dominance in PCW. The company is going down the shitter without me. The shows aren’t selling out, revenues are down, merchandise sales are down, wrestlers are jumping ship left and right, and I’m not getting enough television time. I am PCW’s savior, I’m the one who brought PCW out of the depths of despair the first time when the Phantom Booker let it go to shit. Now we got this fucking hillbilly in charge and it’s happening again. I bet the son of a bitch can’t even count to twenty on his fingers and toes, let alone run a multi-million dollar company. PCW needs me, and right now I need it.” The former CEO of PCW straightens his tie out and winks at himself in the mirror. Then he heads for the door and leaves the room, allowing the camera to fade out as the door closes behind him.
