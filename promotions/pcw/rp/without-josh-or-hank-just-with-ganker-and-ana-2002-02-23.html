Without Josh or Hank, just with Ganker and Ana...

23 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-23-02 --- Ganker and Ana are sitting on a park bench in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Ganker with his arm around Ana and Ana with a smile on her face. Ganker is wearing blue baggy jeans, a black RingSide Entertainment t-shirt, and a black leather jacket. Ana is wearing a white cut off sleeve t-shirt and black tight jeans. Ganker then stands up as he helps Ana up and places his arm around her shoulders and Ana has her arm around Ganker's waist. They then walk over to a small cafe` and enter and take a seat in a booth, opposite sides, so they can look at one another straight on, they take one another's hands and begin to talk. Ana: So, you seem to have a big match this Tuesday, your debut. It's even your specialty, a tag team match with your one and only tag team partner Hank, what do you think? Ganker: Honestly? (Ana nods) I think it's a load of bullsh*t, now sure it's one of my many specialties, but I want singles action. Not jus' motha' f**kin' tag team action. I mean what has AFL-CIO done that I Ganker have not done already? (Ana shrugs) Win PCW gold? Tag gold to be more specific. It kind of pisses me off to see such wastes of space such as the AFL-CIO havin' the right, no, the honor of steppin' into the ring wit' such an athlete as myself and Hank~! Team Nirvana is legendary and AFL-CIO needs to earn their spot. They need to earn their chance to face the best damn tag team ever created, but the best damn two superstars in PCW and in any federation to date. AFL-CIO, they nothin' but wannabes and this Tuesday on Turmoil when I make my debut, when I Ganker Ransolver, the God of Wrestling debuts, I'll debut and lay the AFL-CIO down with the Screw-Over and then nail the RSE-Vengeance and show the world that RSE lives through me for as long as I have breath in my lungs. Ana: Why don't you like tag team matches as much as you used to? I mean when we first met you were all psyched when I'd bring up the NZW tag team title division. Now you're hating the division that helped establish you. What's up, what's the deal? Ganker: Yeah, it helped to make me, it molded me and the character I portray today, thanks to the tag division, I am not half of what I could have been. See Hank, he got the shot that I should of had. I should have been the first man of Team Nirvana accepted into PCW, I should be the PCW Extreme Champion right now. I'm Ganker Ransolver damnit and yo know what? (Ana shrugs)(thumb thing) Ganker Is God~! I'll prove it this Tuesday on Turmoil teamin' wit' Drum... I mean Hank for one more time in the legendary team, Team Nirvana. Then myself and Hank go for the tag team titles of PCW, the titles we should have been handed upon my damn arrival, there is no better team and there will be no better team. (runs hand through hair) Cause, (thumb thing) Ganker Is God~! Ana: If you don't like the tag team division, then why do you want to hold the PCW Tag Team Championship so badly? That's kind of strange. Ganker: I'm a strange person babe. (they share a laugh) Really though, the tag team division may not be my division of choice, I may not respect it as I do the singles, but gold is gold and I'm in this sport not cnly cause I love it and will do it until I can do it no longer, but to win as much gold as I can, from different federations. Besides, PCW gold belongs around the waist of Ganker Ransolver, don't yo think? (Ana nods and smiles, Ganker just smiles at her) Yo look so beautiful in this light, yo know that? (Ana blushes) Let's say after my workout today, we have a nice romantic candle light dinner and then we go back to the hotel and really celebrate? Ana: Sounds nice. (smiles Ganker: By the way, yo know if Janelle is actually pregnant wit' Hank's kid? Damn it's even scary to think that Hank will be responsible for one kid growing up and the kid no matter what will have something of Hank in him. (shudders the though) Ana: No, it was a false alarm, but I too shuddered to think that Hank was going to actually going to be a father. I mean Hank is a lot of things and good at a lot of things, but father, Hank? I definately think not. I think a stick of celery would be a better father than him. Ganker: I bet AFL-CIO think celery is fruit, no, they probably think it's a sliver of meat. Ana: (laughing) I bet they do. Ganker: Damn people here are slow to get to your table, maybe I should go up and get something, damn slow punks, keepin' my lady waitin'. (Ana smiles) Ana: That's sweet, put me before yourself, I don't deserve it. Ganker: Yo bet yo do~! Now if yo don't mind I'm goin' up to get us a couple of burgers, fries, and drinks. Mine Sobe of course and yo drink a Coke. Ana: You know me well Ganker. (laughs) Ganker: That cause I love yo babe and want to know all bout yo I possibly can. So, yo want me to drop yo off at the mall while I hit the gym wit' Hank to train for his match for Sabotage ando our match for Turmoil? Ana: Nope, I want to come to the gym to watch you workout, because I love you too and want to be with you all the time I can manage. Besides, what good's the mall if I can't have you there to spend your credit card and cash. (laughs) Ganker: I personally don't care as long as I get the damn free show to watch yo try sh*t on. (laughs) I bet AFL-CIO aren't interested in that sorta thing though. (laughs with Ana) Ana: How long have you known Hank, and how well? Ganker: Been so long can't remember and very well... Unfortunately, nah, I'm jus' playin'. Hank's a very cool person. Weird at times, but so am I. Ana: (laughing) Sometimes, try all the times baby. You're the weirdest person I know, from the first moment our eyes met, I knew you were a rare weird one, and a great catch. That's part of the reason I fell so deeply in love with you. Ganker: Cool... Shall we head off to the gym now? Ana: Yup, weren't you gong to get up and get us food though? Ganker: No, this place sucks. Ana: (laughing) Okay. --- Ganker helps Ana up out of her seat as he places his arm around her and her arm around his waist. They walk out of the cafe` and towards the gym, to get Ganker in a good workout for his PCW debut match.
