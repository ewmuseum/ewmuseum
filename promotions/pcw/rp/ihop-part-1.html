IHOP (Part 1)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: I hate doing RPs in parts, but sometimes you are left no choice... “Ah! This is nice, Big Al. What a great idea! Heck, I didn’t even know IHOP had their corporate flagship in San Jose!” We now rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he grins across a huge stack of Belgian waffles at his trusted manager Sue Saksnussum. The Masochist grunts his approval as well, his mouth stuffed with generous portions of the Rooty-Tooty Fresh and Fruity. The Graces are also, of course, in attendance within the impressive restaurant. The outside walls curve in one big circle around the center of the restaurant, one big pillar through the center where the kitchen and other offices are located. Shaped into a big disc, the giant IHOP rotates slowly around the center, grandly displaying the stunning bay city of San Jose through huge picture windows. Sue takes a long drag on her cigarette, while sitting back into her plush seat. Big Al sprays syrupy waffle bits across the table as he booms to the group at hand. Big Alan Steele: “But of course! This grand affair caters to all my breakfast-anytime whims, much like the Roman banquets of lore! More waffles! More heated real maple syrup!! Ha ha ha ha ha ha! Hi-Keeba!!” Masochist: “Yeah, they just erected this up last year, enticed by the new mayor’s plan for business in San Jose. Personally, I voted against it, but I have to admit, this place rules! Even if security was so tight downstairs… but I guess you have to be extra careful these days. They wouldn’t even let me take my keys upstairs!” Candi: “Yup! You can’t be too careful nowadays… terrorists are everywhere! But this place rocks! I mean, just look at that view!!” Everyone does as bidden and take in the scenic overlook, contented smiles on their faces. Only Big Al scowls. BAS: “Harrumph! All I see is crime and immorality to be cleansed by the mighty, non-abrasive safe for your counter top non-bleach anti-bacterial sanitizing cleaning agent of the gods, Big Alan Steele!” Debbi: “Huh. I see the ocean.” BAS: (growling) “You’re not looking hard enough…” Jodi: “No… I see ocean too.” BAS: (waving arms frantically) “Wait until the city once again comes into yon views!! By the gods, you exasperate my patience!” Sue: (dry) “Oh yeah, I can see what you mean. All those palm trees and sunny boulevards and shopping districts all at a comfortable 80 degrees. It’s a veritable slime pit down there.” BAS: “But you have not seen the horrid underbelly lurking beneath the tranquil façade of calm that rests on fair San Jose! The villains I have faced and the horrors I have seen…” Masochist: “You know, its funny you should mention that. Some people say that it’s the superheroes that ATTRACT the super-villain criminal element. You know, the whole ‘To be the man, you’ve gotta beat the man…’ kind of deal. In fact, there was an editorial in the Chronicle lambasting us today, saying that we should hit the bricks.” BAS: “Wha… ? Such an ungracious attitude!! Would he leave the fate of such a city in the hands of lesser heroes or, may Zeus prohibit, in the hands of mere MORTALS?! Such a condition could only end in catastrophe and doom! Doom, I say!!” Masochist: “Yeah. They should take it from ME, it’s not like we go looking for trouble.” BAS: (grinning and flexing) “I do! Hi-Keeba!!” Steele stabs his fork into the stack of waffles and, with a superhuman move, slams the entire column into his gaping maw, munching with abandon. The table looks on in awe, while Sue leans back, enjoying her Salem. Sue: (mellow) “Hey Al, don’t eat too much… you have a big gauntlet match tomorrow. You’d better be prepared.” Big Al smiles, showing waffles. Reaching into the folds of his toga, he pulls out a long feather. BAS: (muffled from waffles) “Which way to yon vomitorium, so I may purge and rejoin the festivities?” Everyone at the table blanches. Sue: (frowning) “I’m not a big fan of that kind of eating habit Al. Bulimia can be very addictive.” BAS: (confused) “Bulimia? I know her not! But if you could point me towards the vomitorium, so I may entreat myself to the great Roman traditions of hedonism, I would be most grateful.” Sue: (sighing and pointing) “I wish you would just call it the ‘Little Gladiators’ room, or something.” Big Al salutes, and pushes his bloated stomach away from the table, heading off to the restrooms. Meanwhile, in the kitchen area… Head Chef: “Cmon! I need a rush on waffles to table 16! Move it!” The glinting stainless steel is spotless, as innumerable waiters, waitresses, bussers, short order chefs, and other personnel bustle throughout the kitchen. Through all the activity, a man carrying a large sack of batter slides through, making his way to a huge mixing tub. The man places the sack next to the mixer, and taps at the side of his nose. The man running the machine smiles and sidles next to the bag man. Mixer: (goatee and British accent) “Did you have any trouble?” Bag man: “Naw… they don’t suspect a thing!” Mixer: (nodding towards the bag) “That the stuff?” Bag man: (looking around) “Yeah, there’s enough BATTERITE there to take over this place! All you have to do is expose it to heat… the grill, a body, anything will do!” Mixer: “Excellent. Everyone is in place. We begin in 2 short minutes!” Bag man: “Yes sir!” The mixer grabs the bag and cackles evilly, opens the sack and begins to slowly pour the BATTERITE inside the machine, which roars to life, giant beaters stirring the mixture. A small ripple of excitement shivers through some of the workers, all wearing goatees and dressed impeccably in IHOP uniforms. The ring leader, the mixer, dips a bowl into the mix and brings it over to the malevolent short order cook. Mixer: (smiling) “Brent… make me some weapons!!” The chef chuckles and dips a spouted cup into the mixture. The batter flows onto the piping hot griddle, the batter popping and sizzling, the rounded pancakes taking on hardened edges… Back inside the restaurant… Sue checks her watch, looking impatiently at the restrooms. Sue: “So… how many more times do you think the Big Guy will ‘indulge’ in his waffle appetites?” Masochist: “I dunno. He’s already on four, so I can’t see his throat holding up much longer. Those purges bring up an awful lot of stomach acid.” Cindi: “Don’t we know it!” Sue: “Still, it is a nice place, and anything that puts Big Al at ease before a big match is a-ok by me. I’m just glad you guys aren’t ‘slumming it’ tonight again. He’s gonna get hurt bad one of these nights…” Masochist: “Hey, you and me both. Now I can spend some quality time with the woman and not have to worry about excessive blood loss.” Sue: “Wait… I thought she was the Sadist?” Masochist: (winking) “Yeah, but she doesn’t make me bleed TOO much, if you know what I mean!!” Sue: (quizzical) “No, I don’t.” FWOOM!! The entire restaurant is thrown into chaos as a huge explosion of waffle batter that covers a couple near the kitchen. The batter hardens on impact, creating a twisted sculpture of jutting edges and frozen in screams, trapped in BATTERITE. People get up and try to run, but find the exits blocked by kitchen help bearing razor sharp pancakes hardened to a deadly consistency. The mixer steps ahead of the snarling IHOP kitchen help, a batter bomb in his hand. Mixer: “Greeting, ladies and gentlemen. I am Marcis, and I shall be you host this day. Move, and you shall be killed… or worse.” Marcis gestures towards the screaming couple trapped in the batter, eliciting screams of panic from the less-hearty in the crowd. Sue groans and finishes off her cigarette. Sue: “Just great. (to Masochist) Can’t you two go ANYWHERE and not have to have some huge, near-world ending battle to end all battles?” Masochist: “Hey! This never happened to me until I joined up with your little group!” Terri: (sadly) “Does this mean that brunch is over?” A busboy comes over, and grabs her hair, yanking her out of her seat. Busboy: “Yeah love! No more for you today!” Terri’s eyes go wide before she reaches across the table and almost casually grabs the pot of coffee, tossing the steaming hot liquid into the terrorist’s face, who collapses, hands clutched to his face, screaming in terrible pain. A whole cadre of kitchen help rush over to Big Alan Steele’s table, expecting a fight. Marcis raises an interested eyebrow and waltzes over. Marcis: (with a sinister tone) “Are we having any problems with the service here?” Terri stands, folding her arms across her ample chest and raising an indignant chin. Terri: “Harrumph! I just got my hair done. He was asking for it.” Sue: (relaxed) “Hey, it’s all settled. You’ll get no trouble from US, Mr. Man. But, could you bring us a new pot of coffee, seeing as it was your uncivilized minion caused all this.” Marcis: (grinning) “Such courage in the face of such overwhelming odds! I like your attitude. (snaps fingers) Reeco! Get this lady a fresh pot of Gevalia at once! (to Sue) Now… how is it you can be so calm as yourself and your friends find yourselves in such dire circumstances?” Sue: (smarm) “I’ve got an Ace up my sleeve.” Marcis looks across at the Masochist, who gulps at the leader of the men. Sue gives a tsk-tsk as she nonchalantly raises a fresh cup to her lips. Sue: “Not him, I mean, look at him…” Masochist: (dejected) “Thanks a lot…” Marcis: (steeping hands, sitting interestedly) “Really? Tell me more.” In the bathroom… Steele looks up into the mirror with red-rimmed eyes. Looking disgusted, he runs the water of the sink and sticks his face under the stream, sucking up as much water as he can and gargling. Spitting the water down the drain, he looks back into the mirror, rising up to his full height. Rubbing his belly and patting it in satisfaction, he grins ear to ear. BAS: “By the gods, I feel refreshed!! Bring on the fifth course!” The explosion rocks the revolving IHOP, shaking Big Al around in the bathroom, knocking him off his feet. Steele’s eyes open wide in alarm, as he immediately recognizes the muffled sounds of the terrorized citizens of San Jose outside in the dining room proper. He balls up his fists, shaking in anger, when a new sound emanates from outside the restroom. “Take a few men and check out the restrooms! Make sure everyone is out in the dining room where we can keep tabs on them.” Steele narrows his eyes, and slowly backs his way into the last stall, silently closing the door behind him. Just as he settles on the toilet, making sure his feet cannot be seen, just as a group bursts into the restroom. Waiter: (snarling) “Check’em all.” Big Al watches the shadows on the floor, and makes a mental not of how many men have arrived, and where they are. He counts three faux IHOP employees – the squad leader near the door, and the other two men checking the stalls in tandem, backing each other up as they kick in the doors of the stalls. Al grins and rubs his knuckles. He likes his odds. The men make their way to the final stall. The dishwasher rears back and kicks into the door with great force… “HI-KEEBA!” The hinged door explodes off it’s moorings, back into the startled man kicking. The unexpected force drives him back against the sink counter, bashing his skull of the fixtures. He slumps into the darkness of unconsciousness, as Big Al slowly emerges from his hiding spot, cockily kicking his limp form under the sink. His eyes steeling on the two remaining men, he folds his arms against his chest, daring the stunned men into action against this toga wearing demi-god. BAS: “So, it has come to be that Zeus has called upon Big Alan Steele once again to provide a measure of structure to this uncivilized rock called earth. So be it! Though I have come merely for grain-based food stuffs, I find myself to soon be feasting upon the glories of battle!! So come, and face the awesome power of Big Alan Steele! Hi-Keeba!!” The other dishwasher shakes off his amazement and draws out two hardened pancakes. Big Al laughs out loud at the seemingly meager display, the man charging forward, screaming a war cry… RRIIIPPPP!! Steele manages to turn his body and dodge the oncoming attack, but the man’s reflexes proved quicker than thought, tearing a huge swatch of material from his toga. Al is horrified at the razor edge of the pancake, as he begins to dodge the flapjacks in earnest, the dishwasher swinging out in vicious arcs with the precision of a modern day ninja. Big Al begins to gain the advantage and uses the palms of his hands to turn aside many of the more deadly blows, before managing to get inside the man’s defense and hit him with s sclancing blow. But then again, such a blow from Steele is like an ordinary hit from an ordinary man. The criminal falls backwards, yet manages to maintain his footing, rubbing the side of his jaw in awe. Leader: “Get out of the way! I’ll seal him up!” Dishwasher: (snarling) “I got this! He’s NOTHING!!” BAS: “Then come and prove your mettle against Zeus’ favored son!” The terrorist screams in rage and charges… and is lifted up vertically into the air from an uppercut from Steele that perfectly intersects the man’s defenses and send him into the waiting arm’s of Morpheus. But even as the man is lifted, Big Alan Steele notices the man behind him biting at the ring of a grenade and lobbing it at him. A batter bomb. Following through the uppercut, Steele leaps up and desperately grabs at the heating duct, tearing it open with his raised punching hand and grabbing up into the duct system. The batter bomb explodes before the unconscious terrorist hits the ground, the hardening batter rushing around the airborne man, suspending him in BATTERITE. Big Al just barely manages to pull himself inside the duct works as the light below is abruptly cut off by the rushing pancake batter. Back in the kitchen, Marcis holds court with his men, taking stock of the current situation. Two men have Sue held tightly, her hands bound behind her back, and one with a pancake disc-blade at her throat. Marcis speaks into a radio-receiver. Marcis: “Have you tripped any of the locks yet?” Radio: “Affirmative. We have 2 of the 5 down already, though that last electromagnetic one is going to take a miracle to get through.” Marcis: (smug) “Relax, Ovid, for today, I am your fairy godmother. You shall have that miracle. Keep me informed, over and out.” Sue cocks an eye at Marcis, and puffs at her smoke, which she refused to relinquish from her lips. Sue: “Locks? You mean the safe? This is all about money?” Marcis: “Not that you deserve an explanation, but yes. The International House of Pancakes is, as stated in its nomenclature, International, thusly having many fiscal holdings across the world over. Today they experience a market crash, courtesy of my sheer brilliance.” Sue: “Yeah, whatever. Then why do it like this, huh? Kidnapping and terrorism is hardly the best way to go.” Marcis: “Ah, but it is when they don’t THINK that it’s about money. I’ve already contacted authorities and made demands, courtesy of the People’s Liberation Front of Judea.” Waiter: “I thought we were the Judean Front of the People’s Liberation?” Marcis: (sighing) “What does it all matter? It’s only a mere ruse to screw with the authorities.” Sue: “You’re pretty sure of yourself, telling me all this.” Marcis: “Ah yes, back to you, Ms. Sue of the Ace up her sleeve. Perhaps we should now go into great detail of your little card tricks…” The man from the bathroom bursts into the kitchen. Man: “Sir! Rodney and Gregor were just taken out by some HUGE guy in the bathroom!” Marcis takes a sideways look at the captive Sue, who is smiling. Marcis: “Oh? And what happened?” Man: “Well, he knocked them both out like they were children!” Marcis: (growing angry) “Well, did YOU manage to neutralize him, because if you didn’t, your presence here would be quite an affront to my sensibilities…” Man: (gulping) “Oh, oh yeah, no problem. I hit him with a batter bomb and froze him in BATTERITE.” Marcis: “Did you SEE him covered over?” Man: “Well… no. But you know how those bombs work. They flow all over the area! He was trapped like a rat in the corner! There’s no WAY he could have survived!!” Marcis: “And my men?” Man: “Ah, well, they were, ah, unfortunately trapped in the bomb blast.” Marcis looks mad for the briefest of instants, before regaining his composure. He turns and smiles warmly at Sue, the color drained from her normally tanned visage. Marcis: “I take it THAT was the Ace up your sleeve? Tut tut tut, Ms. Sue. I was hoping for a little more of a challenge than that.” Sue: (indignant) “Well, he got two of your men!” Marcis: (laughing) “Well, of COURSE he did! And I expected as much! In an operation such as this, you cannot expect to make an omelet without breaking a few eggs, so to speak. A very apt statement here, if there ever was one.” Sue: (growling) “Don’t count your waffles before their cross-hatched, Marcis. I wouldn’t count Big Al out so easily…” Marcis: “Big Al? As in Big Alan Steele?! A mere WRESTLER?! Preposterous, woman! I’ve done the research on this pitiful town! The only superhero registered is a fellow called ‘The Masochist,’ whoever the feeble soul is. And by the time any REAL heroes are called, my plan will have been carried out FLAWLESSLY! And no one can escape from my BATTERITE!! Boys, take her back out to her table… I doubt we’ll be hearing from her Ace anymore today…” Big Al grits his teeth as Sue is led away, watching from a small duct way overhead in the kitchen. Shuddering in rage, his face crunches up into a visage of pure anger. Heaving, he breathes out loud, but soft enough to not be heard below… BAS: “Diabolical!!” ~BAS!~ Be sure to tune in next time for PART TWO of… IHOP: The International House of PAINcakes!
