Date: 12.12.02 I may miss my family, and I may miss what I hope to know and attain about certain things, but I'm still who I said I was before. When you look in the mirror everyday, you seem to tihnk that your family is perfect, not shattered by any moments of tragedy or something to hurt you in the end. I once wanted the life I see my son happens to be living at the moment. Was it all a dream those lonely nights in New York where I trudged along in the unpacked meat shelters, hoping for sure that the promoter of mine at the time; Frank Smooth happened to choose who he wanted to go over and who he wanted to put down. The same vision, the unjust nightmare. I can't look back at it now without those painful and searing shots through my head, probably air bubbles killing themselves as I drink myself stupid. Its not uncommon in life, everybody happens to take a dose of the way out. Whether it be by drugs, drink, or a gun - we all have a way of dying out in the past. Now, I'm not scared by anything I may have done personally, but I've done things to scar my son, and my daughter. The latter of which I've never been able to see in contact, whether by phone or looks by the way her hair moves in the wind. Her tears are because of me, I've messed up that life, and now I must go along and tell you this. Smile, I've done it a thousand times before, but now its different. You can't smile upon that which you've hurt yourself and everyone around you. Sorry. Date: August 14th, 1977Place: Buffalo, New York (Hanson Civic Center)Time: 9:07 PM (EST) It had been a year for which Eric Jackson wasn't the man to be dealt with. Sure, he'd been up and down the New York wrestling scene, taking in pay jobs and putting some fans in their seats. For three years, he had gone out for this dream of his since watching Stan 'The Man' Musial and 'Nature Boy' Buddy Rogers entertain the crowds back in the sixties. Now, it was his turn to run away with the show. Backstage at a NEW, Northeast Entertainment Wrestling, the weekly 'Immortals' television show was broadcasted throughout homes in the New York area, whether it be the city or the state. In Buffalo, they would get all the action from ringside, for this is the place where the federation happened to be born. 'I'm a champion, basically the very best in the federation if you don't want to believe me,' a young black man with an army shaved head told the field reporter sitting in the lockerroom. A light chuckle from him as the wrestling slug his arm to the opposite side of the lockers, picking up the NEW United States Championship belt that he happened to have coveted four months earlier. 'A champion.' The reporter nodded once again, before switching questions on the wrestler. 'So Eric, want to tell the world how you'll do tonight?' He cracked his neck powerfully, as his shirtless body fed from the air condition in the lockeroom. Leaning back, he smirks a bit before answering the question. 'Its real simple, I go out there and I give the fans what they want - nothing to Ron Chambers, or anything but he knows what I bring to the table. It was him who I beat for this title anyway, he knows not to mess with the superstar of NEW.' Rousing cheers could be heard from the arena inside, as the cameraman focused his lense into the face of Eric, a untainted look of determination upon his face. 'Because...I am the one and only 'King Of Wrestling'.' 'Ironman' by Black Sabbath hit the sound system as Eric got up from the lockerroom, the title belt placed snug along his waist - the traditonal way. Soon as he shut the door, the cameraman lowered the camera to the floor, flicking the off button on the camera to send it fading out of the picture. Somehow, the fans ate it all up, defening cheers as the referee stepped into the ring, waiting for the fighters he'd hold for the verdict. Cue up: 'Stairway To Heaven' Led Zeppelin. The fans went nuts when they heard Robert Plant sing the somewhat howling lyrics, but stopped those cheers dead right when Ron Chambers stepped out, his blonde hair down to his face with firebird red lycra shorts. Giving the fans a shit eating smile, he walked down to the ring, hearing his name called by the ring announcer as the fans retorted back at him with glaring swears and obscene taunts. 'Eat shit Chambers!' 'Jackson's going to kill you!' 'You fucking suck Ronny!' He took it all in however, as he parted through those ropes and that charasmatic smirk on his face. It rolled off of his body, every word they happened to say, the entire world would watch as he came to defeat Eric Jackson for the NEW United States title. Cue up: 'Ironman' Black Sabbath Maybe the largest cheer of the night came right here and now as Eric Jackson stood in the entryway, the gold plated United States title placed fittingly around his chest, not able to come off until he released it from its hooks. Walking towards the ring, he gritted his teeth, before echoing 'THIS IS MY SHOW!' to Chambers, who only looked straight ahead at the man he was to face as soon as the timekeeper rung the bell. Ding
