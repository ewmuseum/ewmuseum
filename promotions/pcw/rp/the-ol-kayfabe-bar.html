The Ol' Kayfabe Bar

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: (Joint RP w/ Jordan) Our scene opens up in what seems to be a dusty, dank, bar, where smoke rises everywhere and the loud raucous voices of many men can be heard all throughout the bar. It’s basically a regular bar, nothing seeming out of the ordinary at first glance. However, there is indeed something extra ordinary about this bar. Sitting at the bar are none other than PCW’s own Jordan X Shabazz and Rush Mason. Rush is wearing a black down vest over a gray Ecko t-shirt with black baggy jeans over silver Air Forces. Jordan is wearing a black Phat Farm sweatshirt with matching black Phat Farm sweats on over low-cut all white T-Macs. Rush: Well Jordan…it was a pretty good night. Jordan: I’m surprised to hear such a confused dark man say that. Rush glances over at Jordan, who chuckles at his own joke, while Rush surprisingly reaches into his vest pocket and pulls out a cigar. He lights it with a small Zippo from his pocket as Jordan looks on questioningly. Jordan: Since when do you smoke? Rush: I don’t. Hey, aren’t I allowed to celebrate a good night once in a while too? I’m only human, you know. Jordan: Hence the death of the Crunk as Hell God? Rush looks around for a moment, puffing on the cigar once. Rush: I think my minds returned to normal, although I’m still not one hundred percent sure what I’m doing. I guess that crazy bitch was right…the Rush is dead. It’s just Rush Mason now. Jordan: You really think that dropping a jobber moniker will help you get any better? Rush looks over at Jordan and shakes his head while Jordan laughs once again at his own joke. Jordan: Say, why did we come to this bar anyway? You’re usually dead tired after Turmoil, just that bump from Parshin should’ve tired you out. Rush: Yeah, that guy’s pretty old, but they’re teaching the old dog new tricks. He’s learning the business quickly. Jordan: I suppose that’s always good. But while we’re on business, we do have a match on next Turmoil. Rush takes another puff of the cigar as he laughs out loud to himself. Rush: Well…sonofabitch! I finally got booked, hmm…looks like we’re tagging then. Jordan: Yeah, against Murray and Crichton. Ever worked with either of them in the ring? Rush: Nope, I guess we can talk to those two tomorrow at the gym. They easy to work with? Jordan winces at thinking of a certain memory. Jordan: I’m not sure you’ll be able to pull the usual quality match. Crichton’s tremendously athletic, but he’s stiffer then a motherfucker when it goes into clutch moments. Rush: Dammit, Global Wrestling will do that to you. Jordan: As for Murray, I’m not quite sure, I’ve heard that he’s got great feet for his height and is one of the best punch-feigners in PC-Dub. The bartender walks over just as Jordan finishes speaking, and addresses both men with a high, whiny Irish accent. Bartender: Would’ja laeke saemethin’ ta drink? Neither man fully comprehends the man’s strange dialect, but between them, they can obviously figure out that he’s asking both men if they need a drink. Jordan: Yeah, I guess I’ll take a Cosmopolitan. Bartender: Alraety, and what would’ju laeke to have, lad? Rush scratches his stubble-filled chin for a moment and then replies back to the bartender. Rush: Can I have a Bailey’s Irish Cream? Bartender: Shaere ya can, but I’m waernin’ ya. ‘Tis strong! Rush smiles back at the bartender. Rush: It’s okay, as you can see I’m a pretty big guy. I can handle most drinks pretty well. Besides, if I get drunk than at least I’ll kick everyone’s asses in here quickly. Both Rush and the bartender chuckle at this, a rare sign of actual affection, although it’s not surprising considering this promo being laced with kayfabe cracks, not even really a promo. Jordan: Irish Cream? The last time I came out here with you got a straight vodka. You really like that hard stuff, huh? Rush: Well, I don’t have time to drink on the road and I’m still trying to keep my heart rate down. I don’t want all this working out to ruin my life after 35. Jordan nods, in the way of showing his respect for a peer in the business. Rush: Plus there’s all this extra workout that I have to do to gain some weight for the whole powerful Voodoo gimmick. I gotta gain about ten pounds, but I gotta keep it as all muscle too. The training’s been a son of a bitch. Jordan: Yeah, well the training for PURE ain’t exactly a cakewalk either. The jet lag is a bitch, and some of my matches were taped as house shows if they coincided with Sabotage, though that’s not a problem anymore. Rush takes one last puff of the cigar, and the puts it out in a nearby ashtray, smushing the ashen end of the cigar in. Rush: Yeah, this new two televised cards a week things is much better. I mean, before the company shutdown, I was afraid that I would burn myself out. Jordan: Not even six months in and a burn out? People would scream steroids and the tabloids would be the town crier for them. Rush nods once again as the bartender returns to where the two men sit on the end of the bar. Bartender: Aye, there’re yaer drinks. One Irish Cream and another Cosmopolitan. The two men nod in thanks as the man sets the two drinks down on the table. Rush picks up the small shot glass and sniffs at his drink, then reeling back. Rush: Wow, those Irish may have produced a clumsy oaf in the ring, but damn can they make liquor! Jordan laughs as Rush jokes on about Andy Murray. Jordan: So you’re gonna work a two week program with Murray for the Pay Per View? Rush nods and speaks back through a sip from a glass of water. Rush: Yeah, I figured it would probably help with getting this whole new gimmick thing over, and the kid’s got some heart, even if he’s still learning some of the rest. Jordan: Well, the gimmick looks interesting, and will probably give you a feud w/ either Kannon or Chef, can’t be that bad. Rush: I’m not sure about that. Chef’s got bad knees and he’s on his last set of legs probably. I’ve talked to him and he says that he wants to go out on a good note. Of course, I never mentioned anything about a feud, but I got the feeling that the guy wants a hero’s retreat, which he deserves. Jordan: Oh no doubt. I’ve seen the tapes from early last year and the guy’s one of the greatest technical wrestlers to ever hit the ring. But what about Kannon? Rush: Kannon is, and always will be, a wild card. The guy’s funny as hell and one hell of a worker, but he’s gonna be big, who knows where I’m going… Jordan: Well I hope that we’re both going to bigger and better things. Rush: Heh, seeing as I’ve been here six months on Friday, that sounds like a good plan. We’ll see where I’m goin’ in another six months. Jordan: I’ll drink to that. Both men raises their glasses and toast each other, then sip the drinks down quickly, Rush cringing at the sour drink burning in his throat, though the sensation gives way to a small buzz quickly. Rush: C’mon Jordan, let’s hit the hotel. We can start the road to those bigger and better things tomorrow. Both of the Malice Crew members leave more than enough for a tip to the bartender, who smiles and waves back to the men as they leave. They walk down the street back towards the PCW’ers hotel, fading off as the scene goes to black…
