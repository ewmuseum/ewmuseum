Missing: Chapter X: Project 84

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Chapter 10 Here I was. Still amazed at what I was watching. You don't know where I am? Then go catch up cause you know nothing. I was standing in the entrance of a doorway leading to a basement area. Only twelve steps down, there was a women, Clair Rivence, and an unknown man. This was my chance at getting some vital information to complete my mission of catching Ivan Chistov. I only needed one more of his aliases and I was sure that when I got it, I would have enough information to stop this criminal. Stop it! I had to focuss at the conversation down stairs and not worry about Chistov's other names right now. Man: Clair, is Project 84 ready? Clair: You know that I am never late with my deadlines, sir. Man: Good. The plans are ready and now all we need is our hit man. Clair: What is he going under now? Man: You mean the name to sign on the documents? Clair: Precisely. Man: Terry Gordan. Clair: Very well, Claval. Project 84? I had never heard of this project being made. What was the purpose of the project, and what could it possibly do? If only I had my database program with me, I could figure it all out in a second but I had left it in my hotel room to charge up. I had been using it a lot lately and the battery was dead. The thing wouldn’t go ten minutes without freezing up and shutting down. So Chistov was the hit man. Could that be why he had stolen the four million dollars from the Hare Krishna, to help contribute the building of this mysterious Project 84? It all fit together and why else would Chistov want all that money? His alias for the project was Terry Gordan, I may need to know that for later on. If only I could figure out his real name. All of this would be done and over with but NO! His real name was top secret, and for what reason? One thing that absolutely blew me away was what Clair had called the name. “CLAVAL” was what she referred to him as. Could it be that the man downstairs was the same Henry Claval that has been in an unknown location all these years? Maybe he wasn’t hiding at all. He was just hanging low until the right moment. Where else to hide out than in a small, peaceful, German town? Clair: Speaking of which, where is Gordan currently hiding? Claval: He’s in his hometown, about one hundred miles away. He’s ready for me to give him any orders I want. Clair: Oh Henry, you have too much power in this world. So it was Henry Claval! Henry: Exactly. That power has kept the police away from me for five years. Claval: (Laugh) Henry: But back on topic Clair, when is the project set to take off? This was the part I had wanted to hear. I needed to find out when something big was to go off and attempt to stop it. To make sure I could hear every word possible, I tried to move closer. IN a daring effort, I slowly moved my foot onto the first step without and noise. I moved the second foot onto the step and I cringed. SQEAK! Henry: Did you hear that? Clair: Lets go. I was caught! I quickly ran up the stairs, not making too much noise. There was a bush about five feet to my left and I dove in it and stayed motionless. The footsteps climbed the stairs and were at the top. Clair: HELLO? Henry: Anybody there? I stayed as quiet as I could. Clair: Maybe it was just the wind. Henry: Probably. Our nerves our on high all because of the project. Let’s go back down. Clair: Actually, I’m starved. You want to go in for a snack before we come back out? Henry: Sure. The two turned and walked in the unlocked back door of the house. I hadn’t been caught. This was my chance. I dashed at the stairs to the downstairs and quickly ran into the room at the bottom. The room was basic. There was a pool table in the corner of the room and a TV with a couch in the other. In the middle of the room, there was a plain table and on it, a stack of papers. There was nothing else in the room. Without thinking, I grabbed the stack of papers, hoping to find some information in them. I hurried up the stairs for fear of someone coming. When I was positive there was no one outside, I ran around the house. I ran all the way to my car before I opened the door and placed the papers down in the passenger seat. In a few seconds, I was gone from the street. I figured that Claval and Clair would panic when they realized the sheets were gone but I didn’t care. How were they to know that I was there? As I drove away, I glanced on the top paper from the stack of about twenty. The words on the sheet read: “PROJECT 84” This was it…
