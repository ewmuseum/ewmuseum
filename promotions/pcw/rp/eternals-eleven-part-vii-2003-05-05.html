::~Eternals Eleven~:: (Part VII)

5 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/05/03 (...Continued from Part VI) The City of New Jersey is even busier, the middle of the city center is full of shops, clubs and other places where local people like to spend their evenings. In the centre of this very town is a small public bar called ‘Franchetti’s Bar’, the place is quite big with many tables and chairs, aswell as a large bar area. The place is not busy, nor empty, there are some tables filled with people lauging and drinking for hours upon hours. At the bar sits Shane Wethers and Mikey Fishmore, they sit on separate stools drinking beer from large pint glasses. There is nothing new in appearance with either of them, Shane is wearing his typical attire consisting of black baggy jeans with a chunky silver chain hanging from the pocket, a red t-shirt with the words ‘New Era’ in bold black letters, a pair of grey and red trainers and Shane’s hair is in the trade-mark spikey-messy style. Mikey’s attire is pretty much the same as he always wears, a cheap second hand suit with a distasteful shirt and tie. The scar below Mikeys left eye can be seen and Shane mysteriously bright blue eyes stand out in the shadows. The two veterans lean on the bar whilst sat on their stools, Mikey has a cigarette in his mouth and puffs away on it as if he was on a mission. “ Mikey Fishmore docks his cigar out in a silver ashtray “What are you worrying about?” Mikey asks, puzzled about the whole situation “You’ve got five members of security now and if your dumb ass cousin agree then that will make it six!” Shane grits his teeth and takes a drink from his pint glass. He then puts it back down on a beer mat “I just don’t like the thought of given my distrustful brother a chance, I should really cut the guy to ribbons” Shane mutters. “Well you can’t and it not even worth bothering about.” Mikey advises him “Just let him have this opportunity and then do what you will”” Shane nods his head “I suppose so” Shane admits. Mikey grins “Anyway,” Mikey says, briefly changing the subject “this Tuesday you get to pick up your forth victory by winning that four-way battle, your mind should be on that” Shane scratches his head “I’m not worried” Shane re-assures him. “Adam Stryker was a wise-ass muppet just like them four pickledicks, but I soon shut his mouth when I beat him in front of the world” they smirk as they drink from their beers again “I wonder who will get the invitational for an Eternal Asskicking at Turmoil” he adds. “It could be anyone, but they can be sure that they will get the beating of their lives. I can almost feel the rush of blood to my feet as I’m rising up that roster. Now it’s time to prove myself again by stepping in that ring to face a bunch of kids with more grease on his hair than a Sunday morning English Breakfast!” Mikey stands up and waves a $10 note at the guy behind the bar “Do you want another beer, Guv?” Mikey asks “All this talk of destruction is making me thirsty.” Shane stares into space “Yeah,” Shane replies with a smirk “thirsty for giving out the ass kicking off a lifetime.” Mikey laughs as the barman brings the beers across “This Tuesday is gonna rock,” Shane says in an eccentric tone “so society better get reading his commandments and take notice of the eleventh!” Mikey looks at Shane with a bewildered look “I though there was only ten” He asks. “No you fool” Shane snaps, “The eleventh quite clearly reads… ‘Thou shalt not mess with thee Eternal Punk unless thou wants a foot in their ass!’” Mikey grins and applauds “I couldn’t possibly agree more!” he says. Suddenly, the door of the bar opens and in walks Silus, followed by Huey and his associates. There entrance kinda remind you of an episode of the Soprano’s. Shane and Mikey turn around “Well if it ain’t Cheech and Chong along with the chuckle brothers!” Shane adds with a cocky expression “The question is ‘which one of you are the chuckle brothers?’” they all walk over to a table to the left of the room and take a seat on the black wooden chairs “So what answers have you clowns brought?” Shane asks. The thug-like associate takes his gun out of the holster again and points it at Shane. Shane just looks at him with a look of zest “Oh c’mon, I’ve seen it all before.” Shane groans, “Are you auditioning for the Godfather or something? Want some cotton wool to cushion your mouth or will my fist do?” Huey looks up at his associate and gives him the nod. He puts his gun away and sits down. “Anyway…” Huey says, trying to be the ‘Kofi Annan’ of the two sides, “Silus has got something to say. Since he’s been on day release from jail after trying to kill you, I think he might be willing to help you out if the price is right.” Shane looks over at Silus “So what’s your offer?” Silus asks. Shane sits silently from a couple of seconds before reaching across the table and grabbing Silus by the front of his collar. Mikey Fishmore and Huey Lucio grabs hold of Shane around his shoulders, trying to restrain him. Shane roughly shakes off Huey whilst Mikey continues to attempt to calm Shane “Ya see, that’s why I won’t help you out” Silus shouts across the table. Shane grits his teeth and forces forward as Mikey keeps pulling him back “You expect me to visit you after you tried to kill me you anal wart?” Shane protests “You faked your own death when I was 8 and you turn up 18 years later, you caused grandpa to have heart-failure and Nana mysteriously dies in her sleep a couple of days after you framed me for an expired visa. If that wasn’t bad enough, you ended my relationship with Elektra and make my whole life a mess! A favour would be for me not to kill you here and now!” “I didn’t kill our Grandparents” Silus says sympathetically. “I made a mistake and now I’ve learnt my lesson.” Silus looks at Huey “C’mon Huey!” Silus says in a quiet tone, “You know I’m telling the truth, right?” Huey shrugs “For the right price, please Shane” he pleads. Silus bows his head in Shame. “I’m sorry ok?” he says apologetically “bail me out of jail and I will help you” Shane sits there and bites on his bottom lip “ok…” Shane says “it’s a deal” Shane gets up and walks away as Mikey follows. Huey sits with his associates and looks at them as Silus walks towards the door, the shadow falls upon his face showing a suspicious expression
