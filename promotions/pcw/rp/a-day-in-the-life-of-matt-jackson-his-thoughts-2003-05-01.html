A Day In The Life Of Matt Jackson, His Thoughts

1 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/1/03 Killing someone can weight very heavily on a man’s mind…causing another person’s death isn’t an easy task. It takes an above average person; someone whose mind has been changed so much that he doesn’t care…it takes a man. Someone who knows how to kill…who knows how to cover their tracks. You can’t just kill for pleasure or hatred…you must kill out of a want…a need. There is a fine line between a killer and a murder. Stone Burns is nothing more then a mere murder…killing for no other reason then a hatred. He isn’t a man…a trained professional. He is nothing more then a man who lives in fear…fear of his problems and misfortunes. He kills defenseless women to hide away from the pain…pain of rejection. The only problem is he doesn’t consider the true problems of this…the sickness. He is nothing more then a lost man…looking to make an impact by killing. Thinking that he can strike fear into those who don’t understand…I understand and I am not scared. I will stand in the ring with him on Turmoil…looking into his murderous eyes and I will allow him to look into the eyes of a trained killer…a professional. There is also a level of revenge involved in it…not for the face he kills because I have done it countless times myself. I don’t fear him…knowing that he isn’t the trained man I am brings me a sense of relaxation. Revenge for the mere fact that he kills defenseless women…out of hatred. Christian MacManus never killed without a reason…out of hate. He only killed when he felt it was needed…striking out against those who opposed him. Killing a woman isn’t the same as pulling the trigger on a man…at least not for me. It always makes me cringe when I do it…it happened when I pulled the trigger on Sarah Clarkson and countless times before that. Women are a whole new ball game when you are dealing with a live or death matter. Knowing that they are the one’s who brought you into this world…Stone Burns. He had a mother at one point and now he strikes out against the whole race. It makes me sick knowing that he is just another Mark Makonie…different beliefs, but the principles are still there. The same thing drives them both…fear. Each one fears something different, but in the end it’s still what pushes them. Stone Burns fears failure and rejection…knowing that he isn’t the best in the PCW has to be tearing him up inside, giving him the fear he needs to go into Turmoil and crush me…rejection. It’s the one thing he felt against me the last time we faced. I might not have beaten him personally, but when the deck was evenly stacked…he just didn’t have it. He just couldn’t over come me and now he expects things to be different this time. He is hoping that is dreams will come true and he will be able to topple the great Christian MacManus…not inside a wrestling ring, but in my own element….in killing. He can’t! He won’t! Unlike him I am not some dime a dozen masked murder. I am not to be compared to Ted Bundy or Jeffery Dahmer or even Stone Burns…murders. You can find men like this all over America and they all have one downfall…they can’t keep it up forever. They get cocky and unsmart letting their crimes be known…failing in every respect of the word. It’s only a matter of time before Stone Burns gets caught up in something…not being able to get out of it. Being rejected by the one thing he has become accustom too…failing. Slowly letting it become his second nature…his vice. He is going to learn failure once again at Turmoil…he is going to pay for his crimes. I am not going to be the one to cleanse him and purge him…I will leave that for Shane Robertson as I am a sinner myself. Mark Makonie isn’t much different…his hatred just spreads out. He is the man that I have grown to hate…to kill. He is the man that has forced me to call back Christian MacManus my former self…the killer. Stone challenged him, but got Matt instead…Makonie forced the little bit of Matt that was left in me out and now he is dealing with it. He is going to be feeling the full effects of his actions on Fusion…revenge. I am getting it in a different sense. Not the type I would like, but it’s still my chance to strike him down for all the pain he is causing me and Haley…it’s the only thing I can do for now…until I break free of this hell hold known as captivity…getting back to the one person that matters to me.
