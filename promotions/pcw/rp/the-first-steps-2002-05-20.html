The First Steps

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20 May 2002 Insanity....... Insanity is an illness of the mind, an illness that has no cure. Many people suffer from insanity each day, some mild forms, some more drastic. In the dictionary Insanity is described as 'a permanent disorder of the mind.' Insanity eats away at its victim's mind until there is nothing left of them but a dribbling vegetable...... The difference between insanity and genius is measured purely by success. Some people called Albert Einstein insane, but others called him the most intelligent man on earth. People have their own opinions of insanity, but have you ever thought about the opinions of the insane??? Do the people who are actually claimed as insane think of themselves as insane??? It is quite ironic that if you think about these questions too much that it would actually drive any man insane.......... Anyway I bet you are wondering what all this talk of insanity has to do with The Dark Messiahs. Well I would have thought it was rather obvious, as The Dark Messiahs are missing a few screws themselves. Anyway the last time we heard from the 'Twisted Ones' they were taking part in some sort of dark ritual. Now we join them in the aftermath......... There is a cold chill in the air, the wind is howling through the forest's trees. The ground is covered in dry wet leaves, which rustle with the wind. It is daytime and the daylight streams through the forest branches, lighting patches of the forest. It is not an intense light but it is enough for us to see what is going on. At this moment in time the forest is relatively quiet with the only sound being of the tweeting of a solitary bird and the rustles of the leaves. Suddenly this relative silence is broken by the shouts of men close by. Suddenly into our view run Daniel Steel and Mike Stone, they are running at a tremendous speed, as if running for their lives. Indeed they are running for their lives as they are quickly followed by 10 or so cloaked men running after them holding daggers and swords high into the air, and shouting with all their might. We now run along side Steel and Stone. Their upper body is uncovered and the cuts they received earlier are still bleeding. They run bare foot and their trousers torn. They are obviously in immense pain, but they know if they stop they will never get out of this forest alive. Stone takes a quick glance over his shoulder to see how close the Dark Lords are to them. They seem to be pulling away from the Dark Lords but do not dare stop. Stone glances over to Steel, whose face is nothing but concentration. They continue to dodge tree roots and branches and continue to pull away from the Dark Lords. The cries of the Dark Lords can still be heard, but they are now out of view. Stone and Steel eventually stop close to a riverbank. The river flows gently like liquid silver. Stone takes another look in the direction of which they have just run. Stone turns to Steel who is panting and puffing....... Mike Stone: (Out of Breath) Listen.....Daniel, right??? Steel looks up at Stone Daniel Steel: (Out of Breath) Yeah, Daniel Steel. Mike Stone: (Getting Breath back) Ok listen Daniel I have a plan but........ Before Stone can finish the cries of the Dark Lords are only a few metres away. Stone's eyes become wide with freight as he frantically looks at Steel. Steel looks back with a calmer look, and his eyes dart towards the river..... Daniel Steel: (Surprising Calm) Quickly into the river!!! Without any questions asked both men jump into the river with a huge splash, and hold on to the river bank, with only their eyes surfacing the water looking up over the river bank. Just as the river settles back to its gentle flow Master Wolf (who we saw earlier) appears along with the familiar apprentice...... Master Wolf: (Angry) Where the hell are they??? How could you let them run off like that Apprentice?!?! You promised me they were secure!!!! Apprentice: (Feeling like a failure) I'm sorry master they were just to quick and strong for me!!! Master Wolf: (Shouting) I don't care for your feeble excuses, the state they are in at the moment means they are highly dangerous not only to other people but to themselves as well. We must find them before it is too late. With that both Master Wolf and the Apprentice exit the scene. After a few seconds Stone and Steel emerge from the river, drenched through but glad to have fooled the Dark Lords. Steel touches the cut across his chest and winces with pain, before looking over to Stone........ Daniel Steel: (Curious) So what is this plan you have in mind??? Mike Stone: (Serious) First let me ask you a question. Have you any family??? Daniel Steel: (Still Curious) No I lost them in a car accident a few years back. Why??? Mike Stone: It doesn't matter why, the important thing is we are going to have to lie low for a while, those guys are out for our blood and if we are going to survive we need to keep a low profile. I know a place just on the outskirts where we can stay for a while. But I need to know that you are willing to leave behind your old life. Believe me if we don't do this now then we won't stand a chance. Now are you with me??? Daniel Steel: (Pacing and thinking) My life isn't worth anything at the moment, and if I join you now then my life is still going to be nothing. So the question is, is there any point in me joining you??? How would we keep ourselves alive??? Neither of us have any skills!!! Mike Stone: (Smirking) Not necessarily. Daniel Steel: (Confused) What do you mean, 'not necessarily'??? Mike Stone: (Thinking) I mean we do have 1 skill. Remember the wolf??? Daniel Steel: (Still Confused) What about the wolf??? Mike Stone: We killed that wolf together. We worked as a team, together we are stronger......... Daniel Steel: (Interrupting) What's your point Mike??? Mike Stone: We go into wrestling. Daniel Steel: (Laughs) You serious??? Stone nods...... Daniel Steel: How the hell can we get into wrestling??? We are only 14, we are too young. Mike Stone: (Laughs) I know we are too young at the moment, but that can be to our advantage. We have nothing holding us back, we have years of training ahead of us. Whilst we lie low we can train until we are ready for the wrestling world. So what do you say, you in??? Daniel Steel: (Smiles) I'm in!!!! Stone and Steel shakes hands but as they do, they both grab their heads and scream in pain as the scene fades to black......... 2 Weeks Ago....... The scene opens up in an apartment somewhere in the windy city of Chicago. The apartment looks quite modern with the widescreen TV and leather sofa. On top of the TV is placed a camcorder, which has been connected to the TV. Hanging on the brick walls are some modern art pictures, which look like they are worth millions. In the centre of the room is a glass coffee table, which has an assortment of snacks in bowls arranged on it. The floor has been panelled with polished oak wood. From all this it is quite obvious that we are in the lounge of the apartment. The sound of the hustle and bustle from the street below streams through the open apartment window. Suddenly the doorbell sounds, and this voice comes from the room next to the one we are in..... Voice: Just a second!!! There is a sound of a flushing toilet and the running of the tap before into our view strides a youngish man. This man has long blonde hair, which reaches shoulder length and dark brown eyes. He also has a wispy beard and his face is of an oval shape. The rest of his body is draped in loose clothes. He is just doing up his jeans as he reaches the door. He looks through the spy hole in his door. His face is one of disbelief........ Man:(Disbelief) It can't be.......can it??? The man quickly swings the door open........... Man: (Smiling) Mike is it really you???? Suddenly through the door walks Mike Stone, looking a lot older now from when we last saw him. He smiles at the man who is a few inches smaller than Mike, since he stands at a huge 6'11........ Mike Stone: (Smiling) Hey Derek, long time no see. Derek:(Shocked) Long time no see??? That has got to be the understatement of the century. 8 years it has been, 8 damn years, where the hell have you been??? Last time I heard from you, you said you were going into some sort of forest!!! Mike Stone: (Head bowed) I know, I know, I’m sorry for not contacting you for all this time, but before we get talking I want you to meet someone. Derek: (Intrigued) Who??? Daniel Steel's head appears around the door as Mike Stone waves him in. Steel puts a smile on his face as Derek looks at him........ Daniel Steel: (Smiling) Hey Derek, it's nice to meet you. Steel extends his hand towards Derek. Derek studies Steel for a while, before grabbing Steels hand and shaking it vigorously. Derek: (Smiling back) Nice to meet you too, Daniel. So what brings you 2 to Chicago??? Mike Stone: (Serious) Work. Derek: (Understanding Tone) Oh you guys work together. (Pointing to the sofa) Sit down and take a load off..... Steel and Stone look over to sofa and nod to Derek thankfully. They sit down with a sigh in their voices. Derek shuts the door and stands by the side of the sofa looking at both men. Derek: You want anything to drink, coffee, beer, anything??? Mike Stone: (Thinking) Erm.... I'll have a coffee, no milk, and 2 sugars. Derek: Okay.....and what about you Daniel??? Daniel Steel: Coffee as well, please, a little milk, and no sugars, thanks. Derek: Okay, won't be a moment. Derek moves out of the lounge, and obviously into the kitchen as the clatter of cups and the splash of water can be heard. After a few seconds Derek's voice comes from the kitchen, bot Steel and Stone look in the direction of the kitchen.......... Derek's Voice: So what do you 2 work as now??? Daniel Steel: (Apprehensive) Erm, well we don't actually 'work.' Derek's Voice: (Confused) How do you mean you don't actually 'work'??? Everyone works.... Mike Stone: (Laughs) What Daniel means is that what we do isn't exactly work, it's more of a lifestyle for us. Derek's Voice: (Anxious) So come on, what do you guys do??? Daniel Steel: We wrestle....... There is a moment of silence, until Derek's Voice is heard again....... Derek's Voice: (Unbelieving) You guys wrestle??? Mike Stone: (Offended) Yeah, is that so impossible to believe??? Derek's Voice: (Apologetic) Sorry didn't mean to offend, just wondering how you could have got into wrestling??? Daniel Steel: (Silent Anger) We'd rather not talk about that, it's sort of our secret. Derek's Voice: (Sighs) Okay, fair enough, coffee is nearly ready be there in a second. As the pouring of coffee can be heard, Steel and Stone suddenly grab their heads in pain. Their mouths open in pain but no screams come out. They both fall to the floor holding their heads in pain beginning to shake. Derek walks in with the coffee on a tray. He keeps his eyes on the coffee as he speaks not knowing of what is happening....... Derek: (Cheerful) I hope you like this coffee it is my........... Before Derek can finish he looks over to Steel and Stone, and sees them in pain, he quickly puts the coffee tray down on the floor and moves over to kneeling down at their sides. Both men are in such pain now they don't even acknowledge Derek's presence....... Derek: (Worried) Guys, guys, speak to me, what's wrong.......... As Derek tries to get through to Steel and Stone, the scene fades to black..........
