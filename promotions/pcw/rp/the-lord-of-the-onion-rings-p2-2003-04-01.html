The Lord Of The Onion Rings (P2)

1 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/04/03 (The camera opens up, everything is still and motionless. You can see The Overweighter on the floor with a tree right behind him. The tree is going to stamp on him. You start to hear a heart beat. It's getting faster and faster, everything seems to be slowly coming back to normal. At the moment everything is going in slow motion and getting faster. The Overweighter rolls over on to his back and sees the tree is just about the crush him. He rolls out of the way and everything quickly goes back to normal. BANG, the tree missed him. He gets up and starts to run again. The tree tries to crush him again but misses him for the second time. The Overweighter and Throwdough runs into a cave, it's dark and gloomy. You can feel the spider webs break over your body as you walk through it.) The Overweighter: Do you know where you're going? Throwdough: Yeah of course, at the end of this cave is another forest but it's a good one. It's where the meeting is going to take place. The Overweighter: How long will it take for us to get there? Throwdough: Don't know depends on how long it takes for use to fight the bear in the cave. The Overweighter: There's a bear in here!!?! Throwdough: Keep it down, he might be sleeping. (The Overweighter starts to walk in the opposite direction.) Throwdough: Where are you going? The Overweighter: away from that bear! Throwdough: Don't you want your onion rings? (The Overweighter turns straight back around.) The Overweighter: Come on hurry up. (The Overweighter starts to jog and Throwdough starts to get in the way.) Throwdough: What are you doing? The Overweighter: Getting out of this cave. (He picks up Throwdough and starts to run as fast as he can.) Throwdough: Wow we are at an amazing 10mph. The Overweighter: Shut it! (They reach the end of the cave. Overweighter runs straight out and sits straight down on a rock.) Throwdough: I knew if I said something about the onion rings it would make you forget about the bear. The Overweighter: Oh about that, that wasn't the reason why I came back. Throwdough: What was then? The Overweighter: I saw the bear, it was following us. Throwdough: That's why you were speeding up. (Suddenly you can hear a loud roar. A bear comes running out of the cave. The Overweighter and Throwdough begin to run again. They both run up a hill, Throwdough gets to the top first. The Overweighter slowly gets there. They both stand next to each other. You then hear a creaking noise. They both look down and see they are standing on a wooden platform. The wood is wet as well.) Throwdough: Get off quick! (The Overweighter is to late the platform breaks and they both fall through it. They start to roll down the other side of the hill. Throwdough hits a tree, while The Overweighter carries on going down. He gets to the bottom. He falls through some bushes and lands in a water fountain. There is a table with people sitting around it. There are also guards all over the room. They are now seizing The Overweighter. There are loads of guards all pointing at him.) Head Guard: Who are you and why are you here!?!! The Overweighter: I…I…I…I…I…I'm… (Throwdough comes rolling through the bushes and lands next to The Overweighter.) Head Guard: Throwdough! What in heavens earth are you doing? Can't you see we are just about to arrest this man! Throwdough: Let this man go, I brought him here. He is after the Lord of The Onion Rings! Head Guard: Really? Well it's up to the masters if he can help. Throwdough: He's from heaven! The Overweighter: Why did you have to tell them that? Leader of Gills Fish & Chips Restaurants: Heaven hey maybe we can send him on his own if his from heaven. The Overweighter: You could do that but the all mighty wont like it. Leader of Bursa Kebabs Restaurants: We will send you and no one else! The Overweighter: Okay, I will go and tell him now then shall I? Head Guard: You're not going anywhere! The Overweighter: Okay, okay I will go on one condition? Leader of Gills Fish & Chips Restaurants: What's that? The Overweighter: Someone comes with me. All of The Leaders: No! Leader of Bursa Kebabs Restaurants: We are not wasting are men on your safety! Throwdough: I'll go with you. The Overweighter: Thanks but I don't think you will make a difference. (All of a sudden someone falls through the bushes and lands next to Throwdough.) Head Guard: Seize him! (The person looks up.) Throwdough: Sam! Head Guard: Sam Ganja!! What are you doing here! Sam: Sorry, I was following Throwdough and then I lost him, man. I carried on going but then I fell down this hill, man. Throwdough: Well you found me. Sam will come with me. The Overweighter: That still wont make and difference but oh well. Leader of Gills Fish & Chip Restaurants: Come over here and take a seat we are having a meeting. (The Overweighter, Throwdough and Sam get up and walk around the table. They all sit down.) Leader of Gills Fish & Chips Restaurants: This is the leader of Bursa Kebabs. This is the leader of Chinese Restaurants. This is the leader of Mc Donald's Restaurants. This is the leader of Burger King Restaurants and last but not least the leader of Kentucky Fried Chicken Restaurants. The Overweighter: Colonel Sanders! What's the secret recipe? Colonel Sanders: I didn't sell my secret recipe for $1,000,000 so why would I tell you for free? The Overweighter: Because I'm your best customer, I have brought over $10,000,000 of chicken from KFC. Colonel Sanders: Okay, I will tell you later. The Overweighter: Woo Ho! Throwdough: When is the leader of all leaders going to arrive? Leader of Gills Fish & Chips Restaurants: Soon, once he gets here we will start the meeting. The Overweighter: Okay, so why do you all want to kill the Lord of the Onion Rings? Leader of Gills Fish and Chips Restaurants: He stole all my peoples Calamari. The Overweighter: Is that a god or something? Leader of Gills Fish and Chips Restaurants: No it's squid rings! The Overweighter: Oh squid rings, umm they are nice. What about you? Leader of Bursa Kebabs Restaurants: Stole my peoples Onion rings. The Overweighter: You? Leader of Mc Donald's Restaurants: Onion Rings. Leader of Burger King Restaurants: Onion Rings. Leader of Kentucky Fried Chicken Restaurants: Onion Rings. The Overweighter: What the hell did he steal from you? Leader of Chinese Restaurants: Sweet and sour balls. The Overweighter: He stole Sweet and sour balls? Leader of Chinese Restaurants: Yes he has stolen all of are Sweet and Sour balls! The Overweighter: Okay…? He stole all of Big Mouths Burger Bars Onion rings. Head Guard: The Leader of all Leaders will be here very soon. The Overweighter: The Leader of all Leaders? Does that mean his not a leader to me? Throwdough: Your from heaven your our Leader. The Overweighter: Really? Why don't I get the big cool chair? Head Guard: Because that is the Leader of all Leaders chair. The Overweighter: Well I'm his leader so he will understand. (The Overweighter has a massive smile on his face. While he gets up and walks towards the big cool chair. He sits down on it.) The Overweighter: Wow this is comfortable! Throwdough: How long will he be! Head Guard: He has arrived now but he has other business to take care of at the moment, so it will be 5 minutes. The Overweighter: What's the difference between Roast Beef and Pea Soup? (Everyone sits around the table silent.) The Overweighter: You can Roast Beef but you can't pee Soup! Everyone: … Hah The Overweighter: Laugh! Everyone: Ha, ha, ha, ha. The Overweighter: That's better. Sam: Throwdough tell us a joke, man. Throwdough: Okay. I got sent home from school one day because the person next to me was smoking. My mum said the boy next to you? Why didn't he get sent home? The Overweighter: Because it was you that set him on fire! Throwdough: How did you know? Everyone: Ha, ha, ha, ha. (Everyone is suddenly silent. It's lasting for a while. The Overweighter looks around the room. He is looking at the old paintings on the wall.) The Overweighter: Hey you what's your name? Leader of Chinese Restaurants: How hi. The Overweighter: I'm seven foot and 2 inches, but what's your name? Leader of Chinese Restaurants: How Hi. The Overweighter: I have told you! Throwdough: No you don't understand. How Hi is the china man. The Overweighter: About 5ft. Throwdough: No! How Hi is the china man! How Hi: My name is How Hi. The Overweighter: Oh sorry, you lost me for a minute then. Throwdough: I thought you were meant to be smart to get into heaven? The Overweighter: Yeah but the test was multiple choice and I'm a good guesser. Head Guard: He has arrived, everyone get one get on one knee. (Everyone gets on one knee, when The Overweighter goes to put his weight onto his knee you can hear a huge crunching sound.) The Overweighter: There goes my knee bone again! (The Overweighter falls onto his ass. He gets back up, sits down on the big cool chair. The Leader of all Leaders walks in. The Overweighter looks at him. His eyes start to pop out. He starts to form an angry face. His looking at The Overweighter in he's chair. The Overweighter is looking at The Leader of all Leaders neck in anger. He has loads of gold chains and is wearing a Nike hooded jumper. He is also wearing loads of gold rings and is making his hands form into a W shape. He is wearing a cap on his head, it not properly on his head it's pointing upwards and is at the back of his head.) Leader of all Leaders: Wat you think you'ze doin in my chair? Head Guard: His from Heaven and he insisted on sitting there. Leader of all Leaders: I iz sorry man. I not know you iz from up above. I iz sitting ere, does that mean we iz wicked? The Overweighter: A Townie! AHHH!!! (The Overweighter gets up and runs out of the room. He runs straight out of the front door. Throwdough and Sam follow him.) Throwdough: Overweighter wait for us! (The Overweighter stops and waits for them.) Sam: What's wrong with you? The Overweighter: The thing I hate the most is Townies! Throwdough: What's a townie? The Overweighter: You know townies, they look like your Leader of all Leaders and I hate them! Throwdough: Okay…? The Overweighter: They always say Wicked or Sound as a pound or Ideal or Init. They don't know how to wear their caps properly and they are basically assholes! Sam: How dare you call our Leader of all Leaders a Donkey Hole, man! The Overweighter: I don't care about your Leader of all Leaders. I just want my Onion rings! Throwdough: Well we will help you wont we Sam! Sam: Yeah, man. The Overweighter: Good lets go then! Throwdough: It's this way to his home. The Overweighter Throwdough can you promise me that I won't see anymore townies? Throwdough: I'll try and avoid them for you. (The Overweighter, Throwdough and Sam all set off up the mountain to find The Lord of The Onion Rings and all the onion rings steal them back from him. They slowly go into the distance until they are out of site.) Will The Overweighter get his beloved Onion rings or has The Lord of The Onion Rings eaten them already? If he has what will The Overweighter do? Will The Overweighter kill The Lord of The Onion Rings or will it be the other way round? Find out in Part 3 of Lord of the Onion Rings!
