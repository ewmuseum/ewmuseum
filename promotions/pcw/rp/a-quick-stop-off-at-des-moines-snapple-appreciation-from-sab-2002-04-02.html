A Quick Stop-Off At Des Moines + Snapple~! Appreciation From Sabotage

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/04/02 The shot opens up on picturesque Des Moines in Iowa, the site of wrestling’s only Scientologist Xavier Kannon’s own Church of Scientology. We see the remains of the old people’s home that was demolished to become the site of the church, and the ploughed up school playing field that will become the car park. “I can almost invisage it now my little Poon Puppy, a shining glass spire shooting up into the Iowa sky, looking down on the rest of Des Moines, just as we shall look down on all who still shun our far superior religion!” Into the shot wander the 1st couple of Scientology, Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Hall. Kannon is staring with pride upon the rubble that once housed the elderly of Des Moines, Ellie~! is looking a little confused. Eleanor : “What is with the WE? You do know that I am not actually a Scientologist don’t you? Preps and minor religions do not go together...” His preppy valet is wearing a skinny fit Pink Snapple~! t-shirt with Silver writing across her 32B chest. Along with this she wears a pair of Blue flared jeans with floral embroidery down the sides of each leg. Her long, wavy Brown hair with Blond highlights is loose, bouncing with each step and around her wrists she wears a plethora of bands and bracelets along with a chunky watch. Xavier : “I mean WE as in SCIENTOLOGY....You are simply one half of it’s 1st couple.” The Gold Patron Meritorious is wearing a long, Brown leather coat with an open Black silk shirt under it revealing a “Krayzie ‘til I Die” t-shirt worn by Jeremy Howard. He also has on a pair of Black worker-cut jeans with Purple and Green embroidery snaking down the left leg to his pair of Black, Green and Purple Skechers. Eleanor : “1st as in best.....YAY ME! I’m not even a Scientologist and I still get to be in the 1st couple! Um....Change of subject.....Why are we here?” She looks around the sleepy corner of Iowa wondering why so close to Xavier’s match with Brock Newbludd at Turmoil they are walking around in Des Moines. Xavier : “Because we’re going to be spending a lot of time here when th-...My Church is finished so I’d like to get a good look at Des Moines, meet the people, know the sites....Work out where the next one can go...” As they stand at the bottom of the steps that once led up to the old peoples home and now look up to a worksite ready for the ground breaking ceremony with all the machinery and prefabricated buildings in place a loud engine is heard getting closer and Xavier’s bright Green eyes flicker into life. Xavier : “Ah...It’s here!” They turn around to see a lorry with a Yellow and Orange checked design on the side, yes, this truck belongs to Snapple~! and it pulls up out the front of the work site. Ellie~! looks at it, her pale Blue eyes transfixed as she grins. Eleanor : “Ah, Snapple~! Gives me such fond memories of Sunday when you put Jeremy right through the Snapple~! machine...I bet Jessica cried a few tears for that one!” The driver hops down out of his cab and looks quite familiar at Xavier, who tilts his head in vague recognition. Driver : “Here is your shipment Sir, 11,000 bottles of Snapple~!“ Kannon is still looking him over, thinking he knows the man, a fact that troubles him as no right minded Scientologists should know a lorry driver. Xavier : “Um...Thanks...Aren’t you Mark Estep? You know the guy who signed for PCW, then left, then came back again, then took time off, then came back again, then got fired?” The man looks from side to side, “Um-ing” and “Ah-ing” to try and squirm out of the question. Driver : “Um...Er....Um...No...I...I don’t know who that is. Anyway, if I was then you’d be using m-..I mean Estep, without my...I mean his permission.” The Scientologist looks at him suspiciously as the driver lets out a sigh of relief. meanwhile Ellie~! looks at both of them, wondering what they are talking about. Xavier : “Oh...Okay, because if you were I was just going to rib you a bit, boss you around a bit more, have a few words to Snapple~! and get you fired...But as you’re not that waste of space jobber I have no need to. The driver scowls. Driver : “I am not a job-...I mean, no problem Sir. Where would you like the Snapple~! moved to?” The Scientologist points up to the site, and a special pre-fab structure in Yellow and Orange checks that sits on the site. Xavier : “There is a special Snapple~! house for the workers. I feel that working all day on building a church of purity and knowledge they won’t want Beer, they won’t want something pumped with Carbon Dioxide...No, they’ll want a refreshing fruit drink made with 12% real fruit juice....They’ll want Snapple~!“ The driver nods and starts his arduous task of transporting the Snapple~! crates from the lorry, up the steps and across the building site to the special hut. As he gets on with that task the 1st couple of Scientology walk towards the rest of Des Moines. Eleanor : “You just can’t resist a good shill can you?” Xavier : “Nope....If only more people would just put their prejudices aside then they would realise that Snapple~! just like Scientology is the embodiment of all that is RIGHT in the world. Snapple~! has Water, the purest of all substances, it has Sugar that gives energy to carry out good deeds and it have Fruit, nature’s candy, all 3 of those make a taste combination that is so beautiful it could cause a tear to flow down the cheek of the late L Ron Hubbard!” As he speaks, Ellie~!’s pale Blue eyes shoot a curious look at her man, wondering if he is going a little overboard with his Snapple~! endorsing this close to 6 days of important matches. Eleanor : “Er...Maybe you should, like, leave the Snapple~! thing for this week, you have Brock tonight, those people on Friday and then....” He face contorts into one of disgust and appears to have to force out the words. Eleanor : “....Jeremy...Eugh...Howard....On Sunday...” She almost gags at the saying of his name such is her disdain for Jeremy, and more importantly, his girlfriend Jessica. Xavier seems to ignore her concerns, grinning confidently. Xavier : “Don’t you see, those matches and Snapple~! are not separate entities, they are linked...” Ellie~!, who is normally confused by Xavier’s sayings and philosophies anyway looks even more bemused with this one as she tries in her head to link a Snapple~! bottle to those 3 matches. Eleanor : “Ya Wha?” Xavier : “Look at Brock Newbludd....A drunk, someone who’s penchant for alcohol has undoubtedly ruined his career...Imagine what he would be like if he converted to both Scientology AND Snapple~!? He would once again, through the teachings of Dianetics become the most innovative wrestler in PCW, and with the fruity goodness of Snapple~! rather than a couple of 6 packs inside him, he would be at he peak of physical fitness to carry them out.” His explaining of it seems to explain it to Ellie~! as she nods away, understanding Kannon’s point. Eleanor : “That’s fine and good for Brock, but he is the...Well...The....Lets just say that match doesn’t matter! What about Friday? Or Sunday?” Xavier : “Um....Okay, maybe Snapple~! can’t be used to explain everything...But at least with Howard I have blind hatred and a burning desire to put him in hospital to fall back on....As you do with Jessica...” Ellie~! shows her evil grin as Xavier reverts back to his loathing of Jeremy, he same desire to inflict pain that Eleanor, his valet has towards Jeremy’s valet Jessica, the mother of his child. Xavier : “....Friday and Sunday will not be matches, they will not be competition...They will be FUN...They will be a chance for me to end Howard’s oppression of PCW talent, they wi-” “Hey, aren’t you that Scientologist fellow?” He is interrupted by an elderly voice followed by many voices agreeing in response to it and as the 1st couple of Scientology turn around, aggravated by the rude interruption they are faced with a group of 10 angry looking old timers brandishing their walking sticks and zimmer frames aggressively. Old Woman : “He’s the one who had out home knocked down!” Old Man : “He’s the reason I have to live in my son’s damp basement!” Old Man #2 : “Because of him I am sleeping in a kennel!” Old Woman #2 : “He’s the reason my Grandson has no playing field to use!” Xavi and Eleanor stand looking at the angry crowd before looking to each other and shrugging. Xavier : “Yeah....That’s me...” His admission causes another rumble of contempt to erupt from the elderly crowd as they express their anger at his actions in collaboration with Politically Incorrect. Old Man : “Well sonny, what are you going to do about it?” Such a simple questions seems to upset the Scientologist more than it should as he drops his head, shaking it in disappointment as he lets out a sigh of despair. Xavier : “What IS it with you people? My Church of Scientology is here to make Des Moines BETTER! It is here for the future, it will stop your Grandson living on the street and turning to drugs and guns...And here are you only thinking of yourselves!” His method of trying to make the elderly guilty isn’t working as they reject his accusations. Old Woman : “We want our home back!” Old Man : “My Son is a Lawyer!” Old Man #2 : “We have the people on our side!” Ellie~! looks at he old people and shakes her head, muttering under her breath at their unwelcome distraction. Eleanor : “Hate........Old........People!” Xavier : “Um...You, old woman...Who is saying you can’t still sleep where the old folk’s home was? It’ll just be a bit more noisy, cold and muddy! Old guy, your Son is a Lawyer? Wow, I have all 547696 Lawyers that Scientology needs at my disposal...And you...Old man...You have support? I HAVE THE GUVNA! More importantly, I OWN THAT LAND! So what are you going to do about it?” Surprisingly the elderly mob rise up to the Scientologist’s challenge and mutter loudly amongst themselves before all turning to face him. Old Man : “CHARGE!!!!” In unison the old people charge...Slowly...Very slowly. They all hobble along towards the couple, using their sticks and frames. Xavier : “Scientology has arrived just in time, our new anti-gang policy is much needed given the way the elderly intimidate people on the streets.” The 1st couple of Scientology turn and walk back towards the church site calmly as the old folks follow them as fast as they can...Which is very slowly and the shot fades to Black. “Hehe...Wait...They are quite funny...Can I keep one...Can I?” “NO!”
