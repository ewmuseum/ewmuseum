You ever notice how Kannon likes his lackeys young and male?

16 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/16 -The camera opens up in the hallways of the Primetime Championship Wrestling offices. The walls are covered with posters of current stars like Big Alan Steele, Jeremy Howard, Jason Snow, and Tim Walden. All of a sudden a well built man in black wranglers, a Jon Kano T-shirt, chrome shades, and a white Stetson hat, rounds the corner whistling Dixie. The man is none other then Duke Williams. Duke finds his way to an office with the name plate “Marketing Director Mike Adams” written in gold. He knocks then opens the door to a spacious office with big windows that overlook the streets below. Mike is on the phone.- Mike: I have to go………..yup……….ok……..ba-bye. -Mike stands up to and greets The Malice Man with a hardy handshake.- Mike: Duke how are you? Williams: Everyday above ground is a bonus. Mike: Ha Ha Ha yes….yes…so what can I do for you Duke? Williams: Well unless you missed Sabotge I’m back. Mike: When were you gone? Williams: Back in the ring hoss!! Fighting for the little man and getting drunk doing it. Mike: Sounds like a tough job! So you’re here for some merchandizing ideas? -Duke sits down in the seat right in front of Mikes desk where he puts his boot up.- Williams: Yes sir, what have you got so far? Mike: Aside from the Malice Crew shirts we have a new Duke Williams cotton shirt on the front it has your face and read’s “Hitting the bottle again” on the back it reads “and doing it Malice Style”. -Duke has a smile on his face.- Williams: I like that. -Duke reaches into his pocket and pulls out a Punch cigar he places it into his mouth but doesn’t light it up.- Williams: What else you got? Mike: The ever popular Duke Williams shot glasses. Williams: Eight ounces of pure Malice? Mike: Hell no that was the old ones, these are twelve ounces!! -Duke gives Mike a high five.- Williams: Speaking of beverages you ever have that Snapple stuff? -Mike makes a grimace with his face.- Mike: The stuff tastes like piss. Williams: That’s what I’m saying! Let me ask you does Snapple fit our image or demographics? Mike: No…. Williams: Are our views fruity tea drinking homo’s? Mike N…. Williams: Well maybe Xavior Kannon’s viewers are, but the rest of us wrestlers aren’t. Why isn’t a man’s drink the official drink of PCW?? Mike: I think he won…. Williams: I’ll tell you why cus a bunch of queers are running the show. Let me ask you a question. Mike: Ye….. Williams: Are we the PCW or the Icecapades? Mike: PC…. Williams: Damn right we’re the PCW!!! Jack Daniel’s should be our drink of choice not Snapple save that for Mickey on ice!! I mean look at the man he’s pathetic!!! He drinks fruit drinks, talks about El Ron Hubberb…..I mean who’s that, El Nino’s cousin? Mike: I think its L. Ron…… Williams: A Mexican storm, that’s what this guy praises a Mexican storm El Ron Hubbarburto!!! He has that hot piece of ass standing next to him and all he talks about is not so tasty beverages and Mexican weather fronts. There is not a shadow of a doubt that he’s a little….you know. -Duke waves his hand in a shaky motion.- Williams: If you had that young ladies ass at your finger tips wouldn’t you be banging it instead of just showing it off? Hell yes you would!! Stand on it son! I think it’s a sham relationship, she’s in it for the money he’s in it to cover his cock craving. I mean he joins up with a notorious homosexual in Willie Tyler Nelson, and have you ever noticed how he always has young male lackeys? Things that make you go hummmm. He enjoys fruit drinks and next week on Sabotage he wants to have a man lick an ass on live TV. You need anymore proof?? -Duke lights up his cigar- Mike: Those are some pretty convincing facts!! Williams: Oh it doesn’t end there. One time on my radio show when I was half stoned I let Kannon co-host and guess who calls in? -Mike shrugs his shoulders- Williams: His gay homosexual lover who emptied his ball sack live on the air for Kannon!! Oh yea he a little…. -Duke whistles this time as he shakes his hand in a gay like motion.- Mike: He’s going to have a guy commit a rim job on TV?? -Duke shakes his head in disgust.- Mike: I thought you were kidding but I think your right he is a gay. You have to stop that filthy act from happening. Williams: AS president I will. -Duke pulls a little note pad out of his pocket and looks it over.- Mike: You have any other shirt ideas? Williams: Just a few, I want a T-shirt that reads “Tyler Nelson Has Aids” with a picture of me pinning Nelson for the Presidency. -Mike jots the idea down on some paper- Mike: Ok I like that. Williams: I want a picture of Kannon with his lackey that reads “Kannon is a Faggot”. Then to get around the PC police put a cigarette under the picture. Mike: Ok, I may have to get approval on that one. Williams: Then I want a black T-shirt that reads in big what letters Ultimate Authority with a big white arrow under it pointing to my crotch. -Mike busts up laughing- Mike: That one is a definite, I see it being a big seller. Williams: I would think so!! Since my jewels are the Ultimate Authority!! Mike: HA HA HA ok Duke I’ll take your word for it. Williams: Well hoss, I got to run some cops want to question me on some Snapple attack on a church. Mike: Good luck. -Williams gets up and starts to head out the door.- Williams: I don’t need luck I got Malice and Crunk. Kannon needs the luck when I send his ass Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound yet again. -The camera fades on the exiting Malice Man-
