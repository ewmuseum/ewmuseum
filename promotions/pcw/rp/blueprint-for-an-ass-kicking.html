Blueprint For An Ass Kicking

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: By A Heel...YAY! OOC: Of course everyone knows that I’m not racist, and if you feel honestly offended by my use of the word ‘Chink’ then…there’s nothing I can do. I’m not going to use it a lot…or probably ever again…it’s just to get your hatred of Rush across. It’s all for you people :-) Our scene opens up on a strange sight. We see none other than Rush Mason and Jordan X Shabazz dressed in white lab coats and protective goggles, efficiently making them look like the geek police from plant Dork. However, fashion is fortunately not the flavor of the day. Rush: Okay, T Roc, put the first diagram up on the screen. Next, our shot shows Thug Roc, wearing yet another white lab coat sitting at a projector, fiddling with the controls. Thug RoC: What the hell does this blue button do? Out of nowhere, a diagram of the male reproductive system flashes up on the screen as Jordan and Rush hurriedly turn away. Rush: Damn! If I wanted to be blinded, I’d look at this wonderful gold that I now possess. Rush gives a fake smile, with a digitally added in glint to his smile, as he shows off his Extreme Title. Jordan: T Roc, you got that shit fixed yet? Thug RoC fiddles with a few more controls, before he nods in response as Rush and Jordan turn back towards the screen, which now shows a full bone structure of a human being. Rush: Now, nimrods of America, today’s lesson is about certified kicking of a body part that we in the business refer to as the…ass. Jordan nods, grooming his new and improved facial hair and trying to adjust his glasses so that he looks “cooler” even though to most he looks like a terrorist. (Shh…don’t tell Jordan that, he’ll have a hissy fit.) Rush: Yes, within the next week I get to kick the asses of four different individuals. First, we shall start off with the man known to the typical wrestling fan as Charlie Loc. Thug Roc does some more fiddling, muttering to himself something about damn Chinks not being worth his time. Jordan: T Roc, you need to stop with the…well never mind…they’re only dirty Asians who always tell us to ‘hurry up and buy.’ You know Wang, I’ll buy whenever I please! Rush chuckles to himself as T Roc switches the image to a picture of Charlie Loc. Thug RoC: Okay, it’s working now. Go ahead, y’all.Rush and Jordan nod in approval and then begin yet another schpiel. Jordan: Okay now here we have Charlie Loc. He is a man of slightly above average stature and far above average stupidity. He goes about spouting moronic sayings like, “You Nothing!” He can’t even form a sentence, what a moron! Rush nods, and T Roc switches the image to the bone structure of a human, and it somehow must have something to do with whatever the Uprising is trying to prove. Rush: This is approximately the bone structure of Jackass #1 aka Charlie Loc. Now as you see on this diagram, the bones in his body are very close together. Therefore, to properly kick Chink Loc…errr…Charlie Loc’s ass, you will need to be extremely light and quick. Jordan: I suppose that would make me perfect for the job? Rush: Jordan… we’re not going to waste our time on Wang Chung. He can fight with his little Chinese friends, while we go work on more important things. Neither Jordan nor T RoC has any real problem with this, as neither really wants to bother with Charlie Loc. His problems are just too multiple to list. T RoC: Aite, now we’ve got John Crickton. Jordan: Actually his name is- Rush: IT DOESN’T MATTER WHAT HIS NAME IS! Ehh…sorry. What I meant was that his ass kicking will be so bad, he won’t even remember his own name. Thug RoC brings the diagram of John Crichton’s bone structure up onto the screen. Rush: John Crichton suffers from denial that he wishes he were black. He’s obviously jealous of Jordan…and I think that Jaden might not exactly enjoy his “inferior capabilities.” T RoC snickers yet again, while Jordan clenches his fists. Jordan: Crichton is mine. A dog named…nigger. It’s not funny at all. In fact, it’s downright insulting. That Caucasian coward will find that the Uprising will not stand for his racism!T RoC and Rush both nod at this, very willing to help kick the ass of John Crichton. Rush: Next, we come to…bleh….Andy Murray. T RoC, if you want to, you can go ahead and kick his ass…or chube, whatever the hell that means. T RoC looks up from the projector and smiles, now switching the image to Murray’s body structure. Thug RoC: Yeah, I’ll take him out. He’s never seen anythin’ like an Uprising ass-kickin’. Rush: Sounds good to me…and if that poor chicken-eating Indian tries to interfere, I’ll knock the maize right the fuck out of him. Jordan: I don’t understand what’s wrong with those people. I mean, we’re so obviously superior in race that they should understand that we deserve the titles in PCW more than anyone else. All three men have to nod in agreement at this fact. Thug RoC: Rush, who’s the last person on here, there’s another picture. Rush shakes his head in disgust at the thought of the next person. Rush: The next person is Malek Williams, the most disgusting type of man in the world. A pedophile who disgraces my skin color…he makes me physically sick. The bone structure of Malek Williams aka Duzza comes up onto the image screen, as all three men shake their heads. Rush: I can’t even talk about that disgrace. Jordan: Neither can I, but I do have an idea. Thug RoC throws the projector to the ground and comes to join his other two fellow Uprising members. Thug RoC: Damn thing was pissin’ me off. Jordan, let’s get out of here and figure out how to get rid of the virus that infects the black population. Rush: Yes…let’s figure out in what way Bon Dieu wishes for Malek to be destroyed… The three, newly evil men, turn and walk out the room, without divulging anymore of their plan, and the scene fades to black…
