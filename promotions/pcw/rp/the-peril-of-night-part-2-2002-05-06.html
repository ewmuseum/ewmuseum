The Peril of Night Part 2

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/05/02 As she passed into the forestrial domain, all that was around was the scent and scene of a thousand flowers. Intermingled with the dense dew that rode the wind like a twisting airborne cornucopia of beauty, hitting her in the face and trying to force her backward and out, away from her goal and destiny. However, she swiftly darted several paces forward and regained a sturdy stance. She could do no more than look up and awe at the magnificence of the forest, the moon's liquid beams of fluorescence, filtered by the canopy of this natural dome fell through the thick underbrush and into the palm of her hand. Her hand quickly withdrew, as she raised her head from the fascination of the spreading beams that seemed to grow from the floor of the forest, as the clouds parted and presented the naked moon. She looked on, forward into the depths of the night that almost seemed to open up, as the moon’s light fell through the branches of the trees illuminating the beauty of it all, that she failed to see. Beauty was something Anna would only find in the maiming, mauling and murdering of children, she would see beauty in a neck broken in the perfect fashion and would smile into the face of villainy, as this was all she had ever known. With her lack of interest in the potency of such magnificent natural beauties, she simply walked on trailing through the vines, leaves and dirt deeper into the heart of the forest. As she continued to walk, the trees became darker, their textures became more irregular and the moon’s guiding light was scattered and differed among the coiled and twisted branches. The night’s air bit with greater force and tainted her skin, the purple now became a blue, as the pigments were buried by the shadows of these towering trees and nothing was as it seemed. She took a moment to stop, endure, listen and to simply observe what surrounded her. The scent of dead nature passed her sinuses and caused her eyes to swell with water, which then bled down her cheeks and slid into her mouth through her blackened lips. She saw nothing more than a sleeping forest, no vibrancy or movement, not even the whispering of the wind would stir the resting giants of the trees. Their leaves hang still, like dead bodies, hung from poles, with a deadly blood curdling stillness about them. Feeling somewhat intimidated by the strong feeling of power that she sensed within her heart, she went on. With her every step the floor would crackle, croak and snap beneath her weight and careless feet, despite the constant distractions and need she felt to turn to her left and right with every step to look for something she went on her way. As she hastened her movements, wanting to end the experience as soon as she could, for never had she felt such a presence of cold-heartedness. One that not only matched the level of her own villainy but defied any logic of evil that she had ever known. To feel such a power truly scared her. As she broke into a sprint a scent that she had never encountered before was forced up her nostrils in the thick and now foggy breeze. The harsh snapping she heard in her ears, the black trees enwrapped with shadow and the whispers of the wind were all set aside because of this scents potency and unique nature. This scent was fear, her own fear, dripping from her body as she perspired. She sprinted, but she could not escape herself, she could not run and so with a sigh she rammed her filthy left heel into the dirt. With a jolt and a jerk she stopped, within her unfamiliar surroundings, she could do no more than wander to a lone tree. She leant upon it with her arm, scratching the delicate lattices of her lacy sleeve, but this was not her concern. Her head leaning against her arm, both cold, from the icy breeze that encased every square inch of her body, twirling through her body and freezing her from the inside out. As the wind hastened, she closed her eyes in pain from all what had happened, physically and mentally she was a living case of turmoil and suffering. With fists clenched and her eyes forced shut, as some sort of shelter from the biting cold, she felt the frost, it slowly ran from her hair, every strand was still, each with a coating of frozen fear, as the perspiration became icy as it exiled from her soul. As it froze the fearful scent now became a relentless biting upon her, as she squirmed to evade it, there was no use for there was no escaping what this was, its power was simply too great for her. Just then, as the jaws of time itself snapped shut upon the moment, the forest, the winds, even the hastening of her own heart was drowned out by the moans of a thousand fiends that became a roar within her head. Her eyes like pierced balloons seemed to explode from their lidded seams, her hair blustered up and danced in the wind like the flexing of a mad eye. As it darted into the enigmatic tempest of the wind the flashes of moonlight that now pierced the canopy of the forest focused upon her, she almost seemed to draw it to her, with some form of Godly power. She swiftly span around 180 degrees to stand within the clearing between trees, which stretched for miles around. Silence became her, but so did power, for as she did, what seemed like golden flakes began to cascade from the flowers and as she raised her arms high into the air, as if they were beacons of evil the black specks of dark matter began to almost glide into her hands. She felt the invigorating warmth that seemed to flow into her body as this mysterious matter sailed into her every inch of skin. Her hair exploded as more and more of this darkness flowed into her body, filling her veins with black blood and filling her head with thoughts of despicable atrocities that would kill many simply from the horror, which was buried within them. Several minutes passed, the flowers and the grass became white, from their emptiness and now purity. For she was sucking away the darkness, which they held within them and making the pure. Not for their benefit, but for hers. For something more sinister had come from this, she now felt the power surging within her. The strength of one million warriors, the force surged in a spiraling dart to her brain, as the lenses of her eyes flickered at a speed that many could not comprehend. She laughed insanely as she simply stood and looked up to the moon, snarling at it and grinning as well, with the knowledge that eventually she would hold the force and the power to destroy it all. As the final traces of blackness was wept from every tree and flower, the forest became of pure white, no texture or colour remained for as far as her eyes could see. Anna snapped and span around with her teeth grinding, as she prepared to unleash the power. She leant forward and raised one arm before her, holding it up as if offering to the heavens. However, as she did this she began to speak in tongues: “Metsu ledu ara, kimi oma. Seta opa KAYA!!!!!!” With this final explosion of words, a fire began to grow within her hand. She raised her other hand and repeated a similar phrase: “Empu leda yura, kimi oma. Seta opa NAYA!!!!!!” With this phrase the fire grew larger and then Anna stood. An evil smirk passed over her face, as she glanced into the enticing flames, she felt no burning but could see the fire as real as it was. Seemingly useless natural beauties, which she despised, had created this blaze, which she admired. She simply glared in absolute pleasure into the heart and soul of the fire and she knew exactly what to do. She knelt down and began once more to speak in tongues: “Hemla, essa rota. Kella mi anuma felcha despia!” With this solemn prayer she placed her hand upon the floor and from the single flame came many. For as it touched the ground the blaze darted into six directions, each of equal length and distances apart. Every trail then cross another and linked with the other, until the blazing star was formed. She stood at the center knowing that she had created this and as the flames grew higher she simply cackled insanely with her newfound powers. However, from nowhere the blaze vanished, as it her actions themselves had simply been deleted, they were gone. She struggled for breath, as the harsh truth and confrontation became a reality. For as she looked onward surely enough, there stood the figure. Cloaked, with no more than a black figure among the white underbrush it stood there and although it stood still, that was what made it most frightening of all. Had it been attacking her or walking, it would have been natural, but it was the unnatural relaxation of this being that frightened her most. She froze upon the spot as she heard a deep voiced faint trickle of laughter pass through the visor of the figure. Before it spoke. “Hello Anna…”
