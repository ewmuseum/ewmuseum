I remember you like yesterday,like I remember yesterday. Fading is mandatory of remembering. ~Leigh Donovan Monday, July 29 - Day 25 Harvey Blake stares up at Doug Hattoni’s cell as he leans against the pool table. “These fucking Italians,” he says, “they don’t believe in shit. Fucking money - greed... that’s all these cocksuckers know.” Hattoni’s cell is the only one in the unit that’s housing an inmate at the moment. It’s 9:30 am, and by this time, most of the inmates are either working or taking advantage of their free time. Hattoni, however, rarely leaves his cell. “Why don’t we just whack the fat fuck?” asks one of the many young skinheads loitering at the pool table with Blake. Harvey Blake turns to the young skin head and has to restrain himself from slapping the young man’s face for his stupidity. “Jesus Christ!” he erupts. “Hasn’t anyone told you this is a fucking war? What the fuck do you think is going to happen if we try to take out Hattoni? Huh?” The young skin head is silent. “Let me tell you,” Harvey says, grabbing the young man, who’s half his age, by the arm. “First of all, why don’t you take a look over there.” Harvey jerks the young man’s arm to the left and points at the television where several inmates have decided to lounge. Two Italian inmates, however, sit away from the rest, chatting casually while staring in the direction of Hattoni’s cell. “He has people watching him every second of the day. Usually it’s only a couple, but since all this shit over Bruno’s death, he’s got every fucking Italian in this Unit, along with the rest of his lackeys, watching his fat ass all day long.” “Jesus, Harv,” the young skinhead protests against his verbal beating, “it was just a fucking suggestion.” “I’m not through!” Harvey says, shoving the young man away. He steps back, away from his companions, letting them know that his words are intended for all of their ears. “Doug Hattoni has the best protection in this fucking prison. It wouldn’t surprise me if he had people checking his toilet before he takes a piss! And every fucking time you look at the old cocksucker, he’s got one of his boys watching - either that or that God damn lion he keeps for a new boyfriend, Jason Snow, is roaming around.” Harvey shakes his head in disgust of the fact that Hattoni’s gang is so much more organized than his own. “He ain’t so fucking tough,” says another of the young skin heads, this one a rather large man who’s weight is more attributed to fat than muscle. Harvey smirks, his voice speaking in tones of sarcastic anger. “You think so?” Harvey says, “You want to go try him out? You want to see how tough that son of a bitch is? Fuck, he kicks ass for a living! From what I can tell, for the past five years since you’ve been in the Tank, all you’ve done is talk tough and then suck the right dicks when the chips come down!” The chubby young skin head blushes. “Fucking big talker!” Harvey Blake looks around at all his companions and widens the accusation. “That’s all any of you are! Well, let me assure you, things are going to change around here. We’re going to get organized. This thing with Hattoni, it’s a fucking war - and we’re going to treat it like one!” Blake raises his voice a little more, “Do you know who I am you motherfuckers?” he asks his companions rhetorically. “Do you? I’m Harvey fucking Blake! For years, white men haven’t been able to keep big nigger dicks out of their asses without knowing me. I’m the most important man in this fucking prison! Every fucking one of you needs me to survive! And from now on, I want the same treatment as Hattoni. For every one person watching his ass, I want two watching mine. I will not let the supreme race fall victim in this prison! I put an end to it years ago, and I’ll not have that shit happening again.” Harvey Blake has a way of turning anything and everything into an issue of race. This is no different. The young men around him make up only a small portion of the actual gang of Nazis that are loyal to the one-eyed Blake. He knows, however, that word of this speech will spread to every man with an Aryan symbol tattoo’ed on his skin. “It’s time to get pure, boys,” says Blake with a smirk, “time to let the world see who we are and what we believe. Time to show your nuts. From now on, I don’t want to see any of you interacting with a nigger, spick, Jew, an Italian or anyone affiliated with the Italians. Any white man that you see doing so, for whatever reason - he will no longer be one of us. He’ll be one of them. And they are the enemy.” The young skinheads begin nodding to each other - proud of their leader’s determination. “When I came to the Tank,” Blake says with a sigh, “I had to fight to make it safe for whites. I had to make us equal to all the rest of the dirty fuckers in here. Well now, equality just isn’t good enough. We’re going to prove that we truly are the superior race, boys - we’re going to run this fucking place. But to be king,” Blake says, turning his one eye back to Hattoni’s cell, “you’ve got to take his throne.” *** From the Office of the Warden “Harvey Blake is detestable,” says the Warden while shaking his head. “Despicable. Deplorable. But he’s also one of the most respected, and feared, men in the Tank. Why?” “Because he’s driven,” the Warden answers himself. “He’s got charisma that other men look up to. If Harvey Blake were black, he’d probably give J-Thug a good run for his money as leader of the Thugs. If Harvey Blake were Jewish, he could probably persuade half the world’s Christians to convert. But Harvey Blake is white, and a white supremacist... God help us all.” “He speaks a message that appeals to middle class white people, especially those who don’t enjoy a lot of freedom, or knowledge about other cultures. In the Tank, black men outnumber whites heavily. Therefore, when Harvey Blake comes along preaching his message of white supremacy... well, to a poor white boy who fears being bullied, he may as well be Martin Luther King. They believe in him. They buy into his bullshit simply so they can survive.” “But of course... not everyone buys in.” *** “Open ‘em up!” bellows one of the C.O’s escorting Snow from the main entrance of Peter J. Tankerton prison to Unit B. Upon his order, the heavy electronic barred gates slide open. “Cuffs,” the C.O says to Snow, nodding to the young man’s wrists. Snow raises his hands and the C.O quickly unlocks the chain that binds them. Without another word, quietly slips into the Unit that has been home to him for nearly a month now - complete with serial killers, racists, and all other form of major criminals. He still can’t believe Miggy’s dead - been dead nearly a week. He can realizes that Harvey Blake is capable of terrible things, and killing Miggy was by no means below him. The part that Snow has trouble dealing with is the fact that one of Blake’s followers, a young man Snow knows only as Derek, gave up all social interaction with the other prisoners to kill Miggy and thus perform a favor to Harvey Blake. How much control does he have over these people? Snow is finally able to get his mind onto other things by the time he gets to the television, where he plants himself in the closest unoccupied chair. His head is still pounding from last night’s hellish match with Rent-a-Villain. He can’t help but wonder how his opponent, who was on the more painful end of the match, must feel this morning - considering the fact that his own head, along with the rest of his body, is throbbing. But that’s always been part of the job. You take your shots and try to give the harder ones. Snow’s very good at his job. He knows how to fight - how to hurt people. But now, with all of his fellow inmates watching his match, and the knowledge that it is important that they fear him, wrestling is more than just a job - it’s a key to survival. “Jason Snow,” says a voice from behind the young lion just as he gets comfortable in front of the television. He looks up and sees another C.O. “I signed in at the door,” says Snow, referring to a few moments ago when he had to sign himself in, as a prisoner, after his night at Wrestle Vessel. “This isn’t about your little outings, maggot,” says the C.O, trying to intimidate like he does all prisoners - especially more dangerous ones. “You have a visitor.” *** From the Office of the Warden “Visitation,” chuckles the Warden. “It’s a lot like masturbation. You get a little taste of everything your craving. For a prisoner, everything he’s craving is the outside world. He gets to hear about it, think about it, and get information about it, all from a person who actually lives there.” “But in the end, when he goes back to his lonely cell, he realizes - it was just a visit. Masturbation. It wasn’t real. All the pleasure he felt came from his own mind. And now, he has to go back to dealing with his everyday, sexless, life.” *** Snow wonders, as he walks down the long corridor to the glass-divided visitation room, who could possibly have come to visit him? It wouldn’t be his wife as he specifically told her not to come here - he doesn’t want her to see him in this place. Despite her arguments, she eventually agreed that she would not see him until his release, whenever that may be. So who could it be? One of his co-workers? That’s unlucky, since the majority of them don’t visit Snow in his locker room - and with good reason. Snow’s relationship with Doug Hattoni may have been formed within the walls of a maximum security prison, but it has changed the way he conducts himself everywhere, even on the outside when he’s fulfilling his duties to PCW. Another of those duties will have to be fulfilled on Wednesday night when the young lion takes on the Chef - a PCW legend. Snow knows very little about this man, and Wednesday’s battle will be their first. He doesn’t think much about it. He knows that in his present condition and mind set, there may not be a man in PCW who can out-do him between the ropes. These distractions part ways with his mind as he steps up to the C.O responsible for the visitations room. “Jason Snow - Unit B,” Snow says quietly, peering out into the room. From his position, he can only see a few of the glass-divided booths, and doesn’t recognize any of the occupants. The C.O, a fat bald man who looks completely disinterested in his work, looks down at his clipboard. “Number seventeen.” Snow nods to the C.O and ventures down the long, narrow room. It’s quite full on this Monday morning. Prisoners are speaking through telephones with their families, friends, or other acquaintances, looking teary-eyed at their company through the bullet-proof glass. There she is. Krista. A lump forms in Snow’s throat and he nearly turns around and leaves her there. He doesn’t want to talk to her - he doesn’t know what to say. It was her ex-boyfriend who Snow beat senselessly to wind up within these walls to begin with. He did it for her, of course, even if it was something she never wanted him to do. At the time, he did it for her. At twenty years old, Krista - Snow’s sister-in-law - is only four years his junior. But when they exchange glances, it’s obvious that they’re lifetimes apart. He cares for her like a daughter, but Snow is a naturally violent person, which is why his caring and protection left him rotting in the Tank. “Hey,” Krista says with a smile a moment after they both pick up their telephones. “How are you?” Snow asks, genuinely curious, but also looking for a way to break the thick tension. “I’m ok,” she says, tears welling up in her pretty brown eyes. “How’d you get here?” Snow asks - California is a long way from New Brunswick, Canada. “I decided that a trip would be good for me before school starts back up,” says Krista. “What better place than California?” “This is no place for someone like you,” says Snow, speaking of the contrast between Krista’s naturally sweet personality and the harsh realities of California cities. “Are you here alone?” “A couple of friends,” Krista says quietly and quickly. There’s a long quiet moment before Snow finally speaks his mind. “You shouldn’t have come here, Krista. I don’t want you here.” “Jason,” Krista says quietly, “it might be a surprise to you, but there are a lot of people back home who care about you. Even when you’re... in here... we care about you, Jason. That’s not going to change.” “I said before I left,” Snow says, tapping an authoritive finger lightly on the small table in front of him, “I didn’t want you coming down here to see me.” “You told my sister not to come see you,” Krista says with a smile that tries to be playful. “You didn’t say anything about me.” Snow leans back in his seat, angered horribly by her comment but trying not to show it with his face. Everything in him wants to throw the phone at the glass. Maybe if he tries hard enough, it could break through. “She’s worried about you, Jason,” Krista says with sad eyes - Snow knows that she’s talking about his wife. “And I’m worried too. You haven’t called. You haven’t written. My sister - your wife - watches the news every day just to make sure you’re not dead!” “I can’t deal with this, Krista,” Snow says, leaning forward and putting his face in his free hand. His blond beard prickles his palm. “You can’t deal with what?” Krista says, her voice rising. “Jason, you have to get help for your problems - you really do. Don’t they have programs in here? Something? You can’t control your temper, you seclude yourself from your family - it’s not normal, Jason. And we love you.” “This is what I can’t deal with!” Jason Snow says loudly, slamming his fist down on the table. Behind him, a C.O puts his right hand close to his nightstick, ready for anything. Snow continues, “You don’t know what it’s like in here, Krista - you don’t fucking know! The last thing I need right now is to have my mind on ‘being a better person’! Better people get fucking killed in here, Krista!” “Jason,” Krista protest, “I only meant -” He interrupts: “This isn’t helping me, Krista! You can’t come down here. It doesn’t matter how much you want to see me! It doesn’t matter how much I want to see you. It’s not going to help anything!” His voice is booming now, and Krista cries openly. This isn’t how she wanted it. She thought that they could have a nice, quiet little visit. She hoped she’d be able to talk him into calling his wife - her sister - the way she was always able to talk him into doing things. But the man in front of her is different than the man she knows. She’s seen the violent side of Jason Snow before - the wild, uncontrollable beast with no sense of logic or reason. Now, it seems that the beast has completely consumed Snow. Seeing her tears melts him, however. He puts his fingers lightly against the glass and says, “Krista, when I get out, we can be a family. You, your sister, Haley, and me... we’ll do something. Maybe I’ll take some time off from work and we can go somewhere.” His words ease her pain a little and she manages a small smile. Snow grants her a small one of his own and continues, “But not now, Krista. Not yet.” Krista nods and cries harder, accepting Snow’s words for gospel. Without looking, she reaches up to touch her side of the glass where Snow’s fingers are, but when she looks up, she finds herself alone in the booth. Snow is already heading back to Unit B toward the life that he has been forced to accept.
