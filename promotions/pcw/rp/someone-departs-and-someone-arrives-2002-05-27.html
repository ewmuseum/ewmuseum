Someone Departs, and Someone Arrives

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27th May 2002 There comes a time in your life when you must look back on all your achievements and rate them in order to see what exactly you just feel that you have learnt, rather than achieved. Of course, Miyagawa is far too flippant to do such a thing, but hey, I thought it was worth mentioning it. Such times come at times of great change, great instability and great anguish. Miyagawa previously had such a moment of great anguish, where he was waiting for his near wife, Natalia, to awaken after the brutal attack of Chris Sloboda some two months ago. He sat in that hospital corridor outside her room, not even wanting to see her before she was awakened by her own comatose state. He just couldn’t bear to see her in such pain and this changed Miyagawa... he might not show it, but he was deeply affected by this and made a resolute promise never to let such actions happen to anyone her cared about again Although I guess it’s not that bad then if he just threw Syko out of the house for breaking a pot plant while trying to play squash against himself in the kitchen with a rubber ball and one of Miyagawa’s wrestling boots. We cut to the exterior of the Anaheim Dungeon, which is once again being repainted white to become the Asylum Gym of Anaheim once again. The door suddenly opens widely and out flies a goblin. A goblin named Syko. Syko dusts himself down, his jeans and oversized shirt (probably one of Miyagawa’s) collects some dust off the pavement. He stands up once again and promptly gets hit in the chest by a flying rucksack that also comes out of the door; however in traditional style he no-sells the suitcase and then promptly sings the Russian National Anthem. Ok, so maybe he doesn’t sing the Russian National Anthem, but I’m sure a few people out there will get the joke, and sing it just for fun... in a Syko styled hi-pitched voice. Miyagawa (from inside the house): DON’T COME BACK!! Syko: But Miya, I was just having a little fun. You remember fun... when we played table tennis with baseball bats and a tennis ball... over an eighteenth century dining table in an antiques store... come on, I had another boot, you could have played squash too! Miyagawa steps into the doorway so that he is now visible. He simply wears a pair of black training pants with a couple of thin white lines down the outside and a black t-shirt that reads “School’s in for Summer” in white with “Old” scrawled just to the left of “School” in bright red handwriting type script. Miyagawa: It doesn’t matter. I have a five month pregnant woman running around the place... well waddling around the place anyway. She could have walked into the kitchen to eat one of her cravings and been smacked in the face with a rubber ball by an enraged goblin playing squash with one of my wrestling boots! Syko: She was a asleep! Miyagawa: She was woken by the constant thudding and then the smash as you broke a valuable Tabernaemontana Divaricata. Syko: I did no such thing; I broke a pot plant, that was all. Miyagawa: That is the damned plant you damned goblin. Syko: I’ll come back in a few months when you’ve cooled down... maybe you might name your kid after me, I’d like that. Miyagawa: No child of mine is going to be Syko Miyagawa. Get a grip, and good bye. Miyagawa slams the door on the Goblin in his driveway. Syko stands there a little depressed for a second before slinging the rucksack up and onto his shoulder. He turns about and walks out of the driveway onto the pavement outside the house. A finely dressed man bumps into him on the pavement. Syko: Mind where you’re going. The finely dressed man looks at the kid with the backpack and the bat face paint on him and raises an eyebrow. Man (in a deep Russian accent): Would you excuse me, I am looking for Natalia Schevchenko. I was informed that she lives somewhere around here. I was told the Anaheim Dungeon but there are no castles or forts in Anaheim. Unless you count that little castle over in Disney Land, da. Syko raises a bat face paint covered eyebrow back at the man who just said that. Syko: The dungeon is the Asylum Gym once again and it’s that place there. (Nods at Miyagawa’s gym which was just freshly painted white back from the Anaheim Dungeon’s dark black [only real old school PCW people will get that reference]) Man (still with the accent): First she lives in a dungeon and now she is in an asylum... deeply worried for her, I am. Syko (muttering): Bah. And with that, Syko brushes past him some more and strides off down the pavement away from him and away from the Dungeon/Gym. Meanwhile, the finely dressed man in the grey suit walks up the path to the front door of the residence of Alexander and Natalia Miyagawa. He knocks on the door thrice and waits. The door swings open inside and Miyagawa stands at the door looking impatient, expected it to be Syko. Miyagawa: Yes? Man (still with the accent): I am looking for one of your... well... da... yes, patients. Miyagawa sighs and shakes his head annoyed at the last few minutes. Miyagawa: Wrong type of Asylum. Man (accent again): What other type of Asylum is there then? Natalia walks past Miyagawa behind him, she doesn’t really pay attention to who is at the door, but the man immediately recognises her. Man: Natalia! She turns her head and looks at him and immediately a look of recognition dawns over her features as she realises exactly who it is. She pushes past Miyagawa and hugs the man in a warming embrace. Natalia: Nikolai! Miyagawa’s eye brow immediately rises. Miyagawa: Nikolai? Man: My darling fiancé, I have missed you! A look of shock comes across Miyagawa’s face right now. Miyagawa: Whoa. Wait a darned second. What do you mean “Fiancé”? Nikolai and Natalia look at Miyagawa as Miyagawa looks between the duo... as the camera fades to black on the trio.
