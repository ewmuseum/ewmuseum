Comedy a dream? Or is it a Nightmare?

17 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 17, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up into a whirl of colors, red, yellow, green, the whole rainbow. A picture of Semore Smoke whirls into the concoction of colors and it continues to whirl, as if someone were on a trip. Then the words “Semore Smoke Live!” whirl into the scene and land hard on the colors, stopping the swirling of colors, the picture seems to sink down under the words and then it all lightens up into a ball of light, and then disappear into nothing. Semore Smoke’s theme music, “Clint Eastwood” by Gorillaz, hits in the scene and it changes to that of an auditorium set up. The stage is set up with the same title image that was scene when the production started. Semore Smoke steps onto the stage to a huge cheer from the crowd wearing an un-tucked gray dress shirt that is buttoned to all the way to the top except for the collar button. He’s also wearing a pair of stylish blue jeans and a pair of his finest Timberland boots. He looks at the crowd with a “stoned out of his mind” look and then cracks a little bit as he starts to smile. He walks up to the microphone set up on the stage and stands they’re letting the cheers suck and spotlight seemingly suck into his body. A stagehand walks onto the stage with a stool and sets it beside the microphone; he in turn leaves a bottle of water and a pack of cigarettes on the stool. Smoke’s music stops playing over the PA system in the auditorium and Semore Smoke speaks up on the microphone… Semore Smoke: Hello Raleigh! Ha! You know what, this is the first time I’ve ever been able to stand up in front of a crowd like this and entertain without being a little physical, but give me some time, I’m still an amateur. Speaking of amateur, how many people here watch PCW wrestling? About half of you eh? Well, have any of you guys been watching this whole Syko The Goblin and Vennemon Pendragon situation? Yeah, I’m starting suspect that there is more to that then there seems. Yeah, I saw them both backstage talking one day and the words “Amateur and action” appeared in their conversation a little too much, makes you think you know. Don’t think that’s funny? I thought it was funny, so does the whole bunch of guys in the back. How about this, lately I was accused of trying to smuggle marijuana into the United States. Well, as you know I was proven innocent. Well, make a long story short, when I got home I had several calls on my answering machine, I listened to every single one of them and they were all from my supplier threatening to come and kill me. Don’t think that’s funny either? {{{{The crowd is starting to boo Semore now, and Smoke realizes that he’s losing his crowd completely. He then thinks fast and does something that most people wouldn’t normally do. He pulls out a cigarette and steals a few things from the famous Dennis Leary…}}}} Semore Smoke: You know, you really shouldn’t smoke. But for someone like me who’s been known to do other drugs, it’s a good way of stopping. Cigarettes are like my savior though, as is alcohol as well. As much as I love alcohol, I love these just as much! {{{{Semore starts to take huge puffs on the cigarette and then let them out, he does this several times and it gets a few laughs from the crowd…}}}} Semore Smoke: But you still shouldn’t smoke, it’s smoking that leads to such love. You don’t really love it, you need it, you crave it…. Argh…. {{{{Smoke starts to drool like he’s Homer Simpson looking at a donut, he then does his best Homer Simpson voice…}}}} Semore Smoke: Argh… Cigarette… ahh…. {{{{This gets great laughter from the crowd and Smoke realizes that they’re now eating out of the palm of his hand. He then pulls a deck of cards out of his pants pocket and he starts to shuffle them in the air…}}}} Semore Smoke: Another love of mine has to be card playing, especially when there is money at stake. Best night of my night involves cigarettes, beer, cards, and friends with a sh!t load of money. But not only am I good in cheati… I mean playing card games. But I’m also good at card tricks. Let’s take this first card here. Ah, it’s the Ace of Spades, my favorite card. Let’s take this card, tear it up like so… {{{{Smoke tears the card up into a good ten different pieces, he then drops it on the floor…}}}} Semore Smoke: All right, now let’s take the next card… man, it’s the Ace of Spades! {{{{This gets a sound of “ohh” from the crowd watching and Smoke tears it up and drops it like he did before…}}}} Semore Smoke: Next card… Ace of Spades! I’ll do the same with this one… oops! {{{{Smoke drops the cards and they fly everywhere, most of them land face up and you can see that the whole deck is full of Ace of Spades’. Smoke just smiles as if he didn’t mean to do it, but he actually did. The crowd is laughing at his faked failure and Smoke just lets it set up into him…}}}} Semore Smoke: Thank you, it was great! {{{{As Smoke is stepping around to get off stage he realizes that he’s not wearing any pants anymore, in fact he’s completely nude! He can’t realize why, the crowd is all booing too, they’re not laughing like he thought they might, and he happens to look down and sees that he’s missing something! Then the scene changes and Smoke is seen sitting up in his bed sweating like he just had a nightmare, which he just did have. Smoke turns his legs to the side of the bed and he stands up in the hotel room the PCW had paid for so he could be close to both the arena for Tuesday Turmoil and his show on Wednesday night for the taping of Premium Blend. Smoke had just realized first hand that he was scared to go up there now onto the stage. He didn’t realize how tough it would be, he’s on TV every week, but he has to entertain the people with jokes, not with punches, kicks, or slams. Maybe he could work in some wrestling acts into his routine, but right now Semore Smoke can’t think of a single thing that he can do. He thought being a wrestler was hard, but being a stand-up comedian would be harder. He could be booed as a wrestler and still be considered over, but he can’t be booed by a crowd during a stand-up performance of his, that’s just wrong, if he’s booed then he won’t be able to live out his finest dream, the dream of being a comedian, the dream of making people laugh, the dream of getting over in a different way…}}}} TO BE CONTINUED…
