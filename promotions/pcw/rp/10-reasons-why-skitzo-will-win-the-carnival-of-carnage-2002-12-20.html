10 Reasons Why Skitzo Will Win the Carnival of Carnage

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20-12-02 10 Reasons Why Skitzo Will Win the Carnival of Carnage 1. Skitzo has already had success in two previous clusterfucks, namely the Black Friday Mall Brawl whereby he helped Syko The Goblin win the PCW Continental Title, and at Alliances 2K2 during an Alamo Match where Skitzo himself won the PCW Soda Endorsement Contract for Gatorade! to be named the Official Drink of PCW! 2. Skitzo was the Hide-And-Go-Seek Champion between the ages of 7 – 11. Albeit Skitzo played the game by himself, he was still the best in Louisiana! 3. Skitzo is not a Negro, a Vampire or a Jester, therefore with his combined talent of H.A.G.S. Champion status and Schizophrenic attributes, when he finds the Tag Team Championships – yes both – he will not take them to the Pawn shop to be melted down into “G’s fo’ his grill”, try to suck the gold from out of the belts or attempt to trip someone up in a very prank-like fashion with them. He will wear them with Pride and Dignity…as only Skitzo can. 4. While 60 men are competing in the Carnival of Carnage, Skitzo has the ability to split into 12 separate entities, thereby increasing his chances of finding the belts first…and ganging up on various members of the roster taking part in the match. 5. The rest of Skitzo’s opponents are all wankers and will not find the belts because they all have herpes, and God hates every one of them. 6. Skitzo finding the Tag Team Championships will deem him the second man in PCW history to hold the Tag Titles as a one-man team…although Akiyama doesn’t count because he’s a big gay boy who wears queer armour and has sex with his own children. 7. The match takes place inside a theme park, the theme being Christmas. It is common knowledge that Saint Nicholas always saved the biggest bits of coal for “Niggaz In Da Hood” and retarded kids to keep them warmer over Winter, and as a schizophrenic, Saint Nick should take pity on Skitzo and help him on his quest by delivering the belts which will put more 0’s on Skitzo’s paycheck and enable him to buy the best in heating technology. 8. Skitzo will win the match because…if he doesn’t there are going to be a lot of bowlegged elves running the Park come Christmas… 9. Skitzo’s temporary manager, The Stro, has never seen any Championship belts in his long and “not-so” prestigious career as a Jobber. Winning the belts will not only ensure a warm Skitzo this winter, but also Stro’s chances to be near a Champion and Championship belt. 10. Skitzo can and will win the Tag Team Championships on Turmoil in the Carnival of Carnage because let’s face it…he is The One Man Gang and he is…The Best There Is…The Best There Was…And The Best There Ever Will Be………at being a Schizophrenic Clusterfuck Champion! The End
