'So concerned with your own desperation the you forgot about mine'

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Now ::Our scene opens up china....town, that's right, Chinatown. A little slice of Asia right in our very own country. Our camera fades into our scene, were we see Corey Williams walking down the street with a look of disdain on his face.:: Corey: Great, these Japs get their own town now. What's next? Are we gonna give them our land? Treat them as equals? It disgusts me how quickly everyone forgot World War 2. 'Hey Corey! Wait for me!' ::Corey turns around to see Dustin running to try and catch up to Corey, Corey is even more disgusted than before.:: Dustin: Quit trying to ditch me Corey, Chris said you have to look after me. Corey: Actually his exact word was 'baby-sit', and it's not my fault that you are not in the great shape I am in. Look at the potbelly of yours, reminds me of Boda for Chase's sake. You know, that God fella may not be quite as good-looking as me, but he sure as hell knows how to pick a chosen one when he sees them. I mean not everyone can be blessed with the perfect athleticism, smaller than Boda and his potbelly, larger than Miyagawa and his frail Asian body, but of all the people God coulda chosen, he chose me..... And to tell you the truth, that was the best decision he's ever made. Dustin: ...Yeh, well Ch-- Corey: I mean, he coulda chosen Miyagawa or Jeremy Howard for that matter, but what good would have that done? What's the point of having superior individuals who 'care' about those inferior to him? Dustin: ...Well the thing is that y-- Corey: I mean for the love of Chase, they'd just waste the gift trying to help the common man... instead of SAVING the common man like I do. Dustin: I don't know if I exac-- Corey: I mean, without my controversy, millions of people would go through life without knowing true joy! Dustin: Uh-hunh... Corey: I'm an American hero damnit! (Corey looks up to the sky) I should be worshipped throughout the cosmos, praised throughout the universe. I shouldn't have to put up with these comments from some punkass nip! I should be treated with the respect I deserve, the respect I will rightfully TAKE at Spring Fling when I'm standing over the lifeless body of Miyagawa and, at that moment, the fans finally realise who the REAL good guy is! ::Corey stares up into the sky imagining the way he believes he should treated, but then he looks down, back to reality, back to these ungrateful idiots. And what does he see? Asians walking around without chains or prison uniforms and Dustin not even paying attention to him, busy picking his own nose.:: Corey: And instead I have to put up with this sh*t! ::Dustin Downey's eyes turn to Corey idiotically, as if noticing him for the first time. This lack of the attention and respect he deserves is enough to send him over the edge.:: Corey: ARGHHHHHHH! ::Corey reaches back to slug Dustin, but Dustin wags a finger, the finger not up his nose, and says to Corey possibly the smartest thing he's ever said.:: Dustin: Uhn-Uh Corey, I don't think Chris would like it if you went and hit his favourite....um......friend-guy. ::Corey stops himself, sweat dripping down his forehead. He unclenches his fist and relaxes.:: Corey: ...Don't think I'm doing that because I'm afraid of you, or Chris for that matter. I just happen to be in a.... good mood. ::It's quite obvious that Corey is NOT in a good mood. From what we've seen it seems that Corey is in one of his most foul moods ever. With Miyagawa making insinuations about his sexuality and disregarding him in general and Chris constantly switching between fan favourite and rule breaker, tensions are high and if we continue on this path eventually something is gonna crack... will it be Corey? Either way, there's no doubt that comment was full of shit, but Dustin does not take advantage of it, his short attention span drawing him to something else. He follows up one of his smartest comments ever with one of his dumbest comments ever.:: Dustin: OOH! A puppet show! Can I go watch Corey? Please please please please PUH-LEEEAAAASSEEE!? ::Corey waves a hand absent-mindedly, sighing.:: Corey: Be gone with you..... ::Dustin skips out of screen, going to his puppet show. Corey is left alone, despite all his talk about how much people should worship him, this is how he likes it. For, much to Corey's dislike, people are too opinionated. That won't let someone make the decisions for them, even if that person is particularly better at... well.... everything and therefore the world would be a better place if he controlled everything.:: Corey: I'll tell you what, these Asians got one thing right, democracy doesn't work.... but that's not to say communism does either. What's with all this equality? People should not be payed the same wages when others are obviously better than them, and people's votes should not count the same as the people who are making the RIGHT vote. ::Corey looks around, surrounded by Asians, none of them even noticing him.:: Corey: ....God I'm hungry. ::Corey looks around, still no one notices him. Corey sighs and begins to walk over to a fast food noodle store in the middle of the street he leans on the counter and looks at the man behind it.:: Corey: Yeh I'll have a cheeseburger and a large fries, with a regular coke. And... Old Man Behind Counter (in broken English with Asian accent): No cheeseburger, only noodles. Corey: What the hell did you say to me? ::Corey stares into the man's eyes, the man cautiously repeats himself, slower. Old Man Behind Counter: No cheeseburger.... on-ly noo-dles. ::Corey kicks the wall of the store in frustration.:: Corey: ....Kannon gets to meet Adolf Hitler and Mike Tyson, two of the most controversial people of this century, and this is what I get.... Frikken Jap Town. ::Corey turns around to look back out at the street, these gooks are still going on with their everyday lives.:: Corey: ARGHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! ::Corey's yell is both out of frustration and an attempt to gain people's attention. It works, for a second, a few heads turn but after they see that it's just a man yelling his head off and they return to their everyday lives. This Asians that Corey has insulted so much now seemingly don't even care. Corey stops yelling, his eyes snap around, looking in each direction only to see people ignoring he and his controversial ways. Corey breathes heavily, and faster and faster. And then suddenly he kinda.... snaps (for lack of a better non-Walden term):: Corey: ARGHHHHGHHHH! ::This time Corey's yelling is backed with actions, as he turns a 180 and grabs the Old Man from Behind the counter by the scruff of his shirt and flips him out over the counter and onto the hard, concrete floor of the street. Corey then picks up the man, sets him on his feet and then takes a few steps back before hitting a 'Truth Hurts' superkick. The man drops like a sack of shit. Corey stands there, his eyes fixated on the man. Suddenly he hears a lot of Japs 'ooh'ing and 'ahh'ing, Corey slowly comes out of his trance his eyes fluttering open. He looks up and around at the street, everyone is looking at him, not in admiration and praise, but in shock and fear. And to Corey that is the same thing. A grin slowly comes onto his face, followed by a laugh. The laugh is, at first, short and broken. Done in a kind of forced way that is so sick it makes you want to vomit. But eventually the laugh becomes more natural and flows until Corey is laughing his ass off, evilly, of course. He stops and his head slowly moves in the direction of the camera, he speaks, this time, with everyone in China Town hearing him.:: Corey: Controversial words can only get you so far Miyagawa, controversial actions are what the people wanna see, that's what they're gonna remember. Miya, you were so concerned with your own desperation that you completely forgot about mine. You think the fact that you want your wife and her baby to live can ever match my want and NEED for respect? I will have that respect Miya, and you WILL be the vessel through which I get it. You cannot escape me, me and my destiny. Because after I've beaten your ass at Spring Fling, your own people, just like right now, will TURN on you. The will finally realise my greatness, they will finally turn ON you and turn TO me. ::Corey kneels down to the old man on the floor and notices that he is bleeding from the forehead. Corey reaches down and wipes some of the old man's blood off of his forehead with his index finger, he brings the finger back up to his mouth and suck the blood off of his finger, before looking to the camera once again.:: Corey: Now you just try and tell me you're gonna stop me. Just try and stop me from claiming my destiny. Claim that you're gonna be in the main event. You have nothing to gamble with? Well, let me be honest with you, I'm poor as hell and I get no respect. With all your mansions and your caviar, you don't even know the meaning of having nothing to gamble with! All I have, Miya, are my looks and my wrestling superiority, and as far as Spring Fling goes..... I'm putting it ALL on the line! I cannot lose that match, I WILL NOT lose that match, because if I do, it's the end of me.... ::Our camera gets a close-up on Corey's face, determined and desperate, in an almost psychotic way. The camera zooms out to include the old man's unconscious body in the shot, is this how Miyagawa will be at Spring Fling? The camera zooms out one more time, this time to include the people looking on in shock. Will the people finally realise that Corey is deserving of their praise.... or at least their fear. The stakes cannot get higher, Miyagawa is desperate to get out of the match so that he can save his baby's life, but Corey is desperate to keep the match going, because if he doesn't, his once promising career is seemingly over. Our scene fades to black...... the colour of death.:: !The End!
