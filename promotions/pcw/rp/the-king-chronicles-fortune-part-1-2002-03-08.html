...The King Chronicles: Fortune [Part 1]...

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8 March 2002 'In a lifetime, you can only make something out of yourself, bringing someone along for the ride is making the world a better place.' CXVI: Despair ...When will it ever end? Once I thought everything different among the darkness, never realizing what I do in life reflects upon my parents, my goals in life, my moments of not living and spaced out moments in life. A prophet for the people in which everyone that buys my music, listens and enjoys my music. Nothing though, it couldn't come at my face even harder, even longer trying to figure somethings out. Ozzie bit the head of a live bat and people praised him for it, causing his mainstream name to grow out farther than his days of Black Sabbath, into his own enigma which many embraced. Even music was blamed for murders in San Diego. There is a difference, when music leads to the occurrence of death, shallowed by thoughts of ending life. Believe it these people are legends in their own right, it is something all of us would like to accompany in life, the point of which you can become bigger than what your name allows you to. Kurt Cobain found his name in death, Buddy Holly, Marvin Gaye when the man who spreaded the seed which spore him used a bullet to end him. Pure savage outlets from which instinct is born and bred in which the stronger must survive. Within the rain clouds, many show their true feelings for one another, inside the fire ring that surrounds us, the people that looks with intent eyes who feel they should entertain, the sweat glistening off of the brow, trying to concentrate on whether skill, determination, or even heart and will power shall get the treatment of a god. The eternal feeling...I feel the words reeping against my soul, yearning for new and none of the old attachments that bind me to what calls for the sake of defense. Defense is overrated saying that it wins championships, I believe that to be a bunch of talk created by football guru's who were tired of looking at the offense continue to hold victory after victory pummeling the opposition into the ground, feel it when something different occurs. Settings change, people change. When a cold front is sweapt away with heat, people become sick, when legends change, people instantly turn away from the television and the new legend emerges. And the process starts all over again. CXVII: Opiate The smoke blurtted out of my mouth as another drag on the cigarette took me by surprise. It wasn't like a person my age to smoke, but still if the perception of reality hadn't killed some thought into fearing death, this was a quicker way to reach it. There is no fear, not a single amount of it as I take in another drag and get ready for the match ahead, Legend Inc. Within a cause of feeling, I stand inside of a room the listening sounds of Motorhead, 'Ace of Spades'. It was all like a card game, each card was played on a level playing field, until the larger dissects the smaller and absorbs it. Much like wrestling and the talent pool. Rebellion has such a taste in my mouth, something I can burn off of, for now if you once thought I were different than you then you have that respect. Days turn into nights and abandonment is harsh, only if you don't know how to deal with it. Its not like I truly care about some people, they are only apart of my story, and my story is only part of theirs. We all intertwine at some point in time, something out of Six Degrees of Seperation. I take another drag, flicking the ashes into a near by ash tray, watching them roll into the container and give one last look of life before its exouds from life. My eye only catches the points of trying to smile and get ready for tonight, a callous smile came upon my face as the television remote control sat right next to me. My hands, holding on in the grasp of my palm, my index finger pushing different buttons for different controls. This is your life at age 20, waiting for something new, and I can't even offically drink beer. I know it sucks but really when wrestling is the adrenaline supporting the drug of anxiety, do you care? Nope, and right now you're looking dead into my head, loving my thoughts- for its all you can see. Exits were sealed, and now you're freakin' stuck. I raise the cigarette to my mouth and let another drag of nicotine into my system of capillaries, blood stream, aortas and veins. My eyes look deep into the television, visions of claymation television blur into my pupils, creating a burning glaze in my eyes. The smashing of body parts, the sick look of flesh impaled through a piece of metal, music television. The clicking of the door's handle hadn't taken me off of my game, trying to relax and let the rage build, my preformance getting more close to the ounce of perfection by every second. I didn't hate Legend Inc. no, more of a determination wise prepareness. The End had created a new hatred inside of my heart, and it was starting to get on my nerves. We appear on this box three times a week, bringing in more revenue than a soap opera. The person walking through the open space happened to catch my eye, a white and black blouse came in the picture, somewhat a smile was on the person's face. A long silk flower dress covered her undergarments from her waist down and the hair wrapped in chop sticks in a Japanesse fashion, when I flicked the channel once more, New Japan Pro was on, subtitled in English text. She laid down on my lap, letting her elbow land softly against the arms of the love seat as I gazed into her eyes. My seed, deep bureid within her was to be out the cycle six. I could hardly wait as the mask of the Japaneese crossed the pixels of television as computer graphiced flames circled around it and the New Japan Pro logo flew onto and landed itself at the top portion of the television. Barely could I hold my composure as a smile crossed her lips, a watery smile that made knees fall out of their correct placement. That mysterious aura around her, it defended to an even louder range. Rain dropplets continued to patter against the window placed closed to a second door painted mellow yellow. I dropped the handle of the remote control back into the tray in which every small remote for every electrical appliance in the room was placed. Searching around blindly, my hand grasped a small doll, with an exact likeness to an opponent of mine. Voodoo. 'Won't you ever give those dolls a rest?' she asked me calmly, a state of ecstasy hanged in a blue hue over her emotions. I smiled at the doll and shook my head 'no', finding amusement in this. The doll had a piece of glass sticking out of it, deep buried within the abdomen. 'This one...Thomas Lexian?'a question peered from her mind, my heart slowed down the tempo of everything trying to regain the composure I once had before she came in here. 'Yes,' I told her with a sharp pain to the answer as a small gulp came into my throat. 'The one who shall be subjected to the martyr lifestyle. Not in death, but in the simple realization that loss is apart of career processing.' A smile came across her face, to the point of which I could only look at her with a smile of my own. A slight chuckle came from my throat as I tried to move, but she wouldn't budge. The voodoo doll still had a piece of glass in its abdomen, yet another piece has been added this time to the hand. 'What are you meaning by this?' she asked quizzically, I didn't pay attention towards her, just continuing my look at the small cotton doll. 'Meaning by this, what are you?' I flipped the question around, a click of her teeth showing her disapproval of what I said. 'I mean, its something you find picturesque right?' 'Right, but why?' she asked reaching over the love seat to grap a hand full of Lay's Potato chips. I took some from her, trying to even the load. As I stuffed my mouth, all I did was mumble words that got a quizzical answer from her. I started chewing on the chips, the small parcels of food becoming smaller and smaller in my mouth as my words became more clear, 'Mental preperation.' Slipping out of the chair, I took the doll with me, smiling to myself and picking up my black leather jacket with a hood over it. She asked the question of where was I going, but I gave her no answer. As I looked down into the eyes of the doll, I pushed the glass deeper into a new insertion, his head. Red liquid began to start coming down his head and all I could do was keep my eyes dilated at a open position. Blinking twice, I shook my head and placed it out on the floor as the thumping of the doll hitting the floor. My footsteps continued down into the stairwell as she came out of the door asking me where was I going once more? 'To work.' I replied as she shrugged while I continued my descent into the first floor of the hotel inn complex. On my way to good fortunes.
