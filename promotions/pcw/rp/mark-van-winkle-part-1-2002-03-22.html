Mark Van Winkle-Part 1

22 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-22-02 OOC Note - AIM me at [detail withheld] and MSN me at [detail withheld]. Well PCW, im on a pace to break all injury records. Im back for a good while this time.... hopefully for good. But, with Spring Fling coming up, its time to get myself noticed and onto the card!! Hope you like this, after sitting through school for 4 weeks straight without role-playing, i have come up with some ideas... heres one... Rip Van Winkle... one of the more well-known characters in fictional history. Remember him? The guy that slept for 20 years. He's an icon to all American working men. I mean, think about it, after working 6 days a week, 10 hours a day, for 5 years, wouldnt you love to take a 20 year nap? You could go another 10 years without any sleep at all. Well, this story is about what happens when you take that story to seriously... Voice:'He used to tell his story to every stranger that arrived at Mr. Doolittle's hotel. He was observed at first to vary on some points every time he told it, which was doubtless owing to his having so recently awaked ... Some always pretended to doubt the reality of it, and insisted that Rip had been out of his head, and that this was one point on which he always remained flighty. The old Dutch inhabitants, however, almost universally gave it full credit.' Bobby Stormbringer:The End... The End:What? Bobby Stormbringer:Not you... The End:You just said my name, now tell me what you wanted to say.... Mark Estep:He wasn't talkin to you, calm down kid... The End:Who said that? Mark Estep:Um, me.... over here... The End:Hmm, direction doesn't usually help a blind guy!! Mark Estep:Oh yeah... [The scene opens up with a shot of Mark Estep driving his huge semi truck down the highway. In the passenger seat is his fiance Amber, and in the back of the truck you can see a resemblance of The End and Bobby Stormbringer. They are bickering over what Bobby was talking about.] Mark Estep:Alright guys, shut up! Bobby Stormbringer:Sorry... The End:Bah! Mark Estep:Thats it!! [I swirve over to the shoulder and stop the truck. I get up to go to the back and kick some ass, but before i can even unbuckle my seatbelt...] Amber:Ill handle this... [She stands up and stops me with her arm and climbs into the back of the truck cab, and shuts the dividing curtain. I pull the truck back into traffic and after a few seconds i hear a large boom and a few screams. I look back as the curtain opens and i see Amber sitting on top of The End and Bobby Stormbringer.] Amber:One... Two... Three... New champ... Mark Estep:Nice one babe... but you cant become champ by beating two jobbers.... Bobby Stormbringer:Hey!! Im in the sweet sixteen of the Global Title tournament... The End:And i beat you once Mark!! Mark Estep:Thats because i got robbed of the first round win, and because i let you End... Bobby and The End:Uh huh... Mark Estep:Shut up... Well, we're almost there... Amber:Where? Mark Estep:Jake's bar en' grill.... Amber:Figures... Mark Estep:What'd I do now? Amber:Ever since you got this new attitude... [She uses her fingers to quote the air] Amber:All you want to do is drink and party. Mark Estep:So whats wrong with that? Bobby:Yeah, whats wrong with that? The End:Oh no... Amber... You've beaten me blind!! [Bobby, Amber, and myself all look at the End awkwardly...] The End:Oh, nevermind. [Bobby and I both laugh as i pull into a parking lot with my semi and i park sideways in 4 spots. Bobby and The End both get out the back hatch and they shut the door.] Amber:Babe, i really dont want to be here.... Mark Estep:Well, then what do you want to do? Amber:Take me home... Mark Estep:Hah! Amber:[A stern look on her face].... Mark Estep:Yer kiddin', right? [She gave me the look... You all know the look. The look that isn't good, and every man knows it.] Mark Estep:I ain't taking you all the way back to Chicago, you must be crazy. Amber:Where are we anyway? Mark Estep:Albequerque, New Mexico... Amber:Seriously?!!? Mark Estep:Yeah... Amber:Grr!! All this stress can't be good for the baby!! Mark Estep:Baby?!? Amber:Erm, i mean... Mark Estep:Are you gonna have my baby? Amber:Well, umm, yeah i guess Mark Estep:You guess? Amber:I mean, yeah i am!! Mark Estep:Oh, ok... Well, how about this, i cant take you back to Chicago, but ill call you a cab and you just go to a hotel and call me when you get there. Amber:Alright, i guess that'll work for me... Mark Estep:Good, well im gonna go in here and have a few drinks with the guys, ill be at the hotel in a couple hours... Amber:Well, have fun while im sitting in the hotel room.... all alone... [She drops her head, and then looks up momentarily to see if I'm watching, but then drops her head again. ] Amber:With nothing to do... Mark Estep:Alright... [I get out of the truck and shut the door behind me as Amber looks at me with an angry look on her face. I hurry towards the bar like a young kid, sprinting through the gates at Disney World. As i walk through the front door, i see and old western type bar, with saw-dust on the floor and a jukebox in the corner. Tim Mcgraw's 'The Cowboy in Me' is playing in the background as i walk through a group of about 10 tables, with people sitting on most of the seats. Bobby Stormbringer is sitting at the bar talking to other women with a glass of orange soda sitting in front of him. The End is also sitting at the bar, but he is being ignored by all the girls equally as much as he is ignoring them. I walk up to them and sit down next to Bobby Stormbringer who is being submerged in a wave of women. 5 of them, at the least. A part of the song comes on and i begin to sing it....] Mark Estep:I guess thats just the cowbooyyyy, in me! The End:God, i hate this music!! Bobby Stormbringer:Ya know, i dont normally dig this 'down home' type of music... [He uses his fingers to quote] Bobby Stormbringer:But this song is pretty cool... And thats... The End:No B.S., yeah we know... Bobby Stormbringer:Why dont you let me talk? The End:Could it be, because im sick and tired of hearing that stupid catchphrase? Bobby Stormbringer: [Standing up] Do you want to make something of it? The End:Yeah! [When The End stands up, he faces the wrong direction so BS sits back down as all the women laugh. The scene temporarily fades out at this point, but is to be picked back up again at a later time] To Be Continued...
