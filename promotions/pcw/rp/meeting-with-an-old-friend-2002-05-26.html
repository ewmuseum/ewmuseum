Meeting with an old friend...

26 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th May 2002 We start off our standard day’s tour of the life of one Alexander Miyagawa with a quiet walk with him and his pet dog. Ok, so he doesn’t actually have a pet dog anymore, and he’s less walking and more driving through LA right at this precise second in his fantastic BMW Z3 in British Racing Green Metallic. The radio is blaring loud, although it’s turned to some dodgy talk radio station so there’s not exactly a great deal to boast about on that. He drives carefree through the town, with it being quite warm today, he has the hood down and the passenger windows wound down too. His left arm hands out over the door and he raps his fingers on the metallic door to the tune of a song that only he can hear. Where he is going we do not know, but as with all these attempts at irony, I expect that we shall find out within the next ten lines or so. Radio: And Jeremy “Krayzie” Howard will be making an appearance at Dougal’s Bookstore in the centre of LA today, so make sure you all head down there to meet this PCW legend in the flesh while you can. Miyagawa’s eyes glance off the road and down at the radio and nods his head slightly. Suddenly he makes a swift right and heads down a less major side road along a row of shops. He pulls the car up to the sidewalk and stops outside a bookstore. He hops out and turns the alarm on with the little keyring gismo and heads inside the bookstore. The bookstore is like any regular bookstore. For example, it has a large display of AD&D books and a coffee shop tucked into one of the front corners of the shop. A rather over helpful customer service representative stands near the door and spots Miyagawa immediately, although Miyagawa’s disguise is rather ingenious... ok, it isn’t... he’s just wearing a pair of shades and a Kid Wonder T-shirt. Although no one would guess it was Miyagawa wearing a Kid Wonder T-shirt. Customer Service Rep: Hi sir, can I help you? Miyagawa nods towards him and heads over to ask him where Jeremy Howard was. Miyagawa: Yeah, I heard you had a book signing or something today? New Autobiography that’s out I think it was. Customer Service Rep: Ah yes sir, you must mean our celebrity book signing, it’s over in the coffee shop at the moment, in fact the queues aren’t too big. He’s a great guy to talk to and I’m sure you’ll enjoy the book... do you already own a copy? Miyagawa: No, I don’t. Customer Service Rep: Ah, let me get you a copy then sir. Miyagawa nods at him once again. Miyagawa: Thanks. The Customer Service Rep heads over to a large display and takes a copy of the Howard Autobiography off it. He heads back to Miyagawa and hands the book over. Miyagawa: And the coffee shop is where? The Customer Service Rep points over towards the tables and the cups of coffee less than ten meters away. Somehow whenever someone becomes a customer they immediately loose all sense of direction and Miyagawa is no exception. Miyagawa heads over towards the coffee shop and sees Jeremy Howard wearing a leather jacket and signing some books from a rather small queue. Behind Miyagawa, the Customer Service Rep suddenly shouts. Customer Service Rep: Oh, HAVE A NICE DAY, SIR! Miyagawa (muttering): Yeah, whatever. Miyagawa hops in the back of the queue of the handful of people. The queue moves fairly quickly and within a couple of minutes, Miyagawa is at the front. He places the book down in front of Howard and sticks his hand out to shake his hand. Miyagawa: Heya Jerry, how’s it going? Jeremy (in a thick Birmingham accent): What? Miyagawa: What do you mean what? Jerry: What does yam mane, what does yam mane what? (What do you mean, what do you mean, what?) Miyagawa raises an eyebrow and then removes the sunglasses from his face. Miyagawa: Jerry, it’s me. I know the t-shirt is a cunning disguise, but it’s me? Jerry: An' who am yam exactly? (And who are you exactly?) Miyagawa raises an eyebrow once again at Jeremy Howard speaking gibberish. Miyagawa: Eh? Jerry: I said An' who am yam exactly? Miyagawa: Jerry, you seem to have picked up a dialect problem. Rush must be doing shows way out in the sticks... it’s me, Miyagawa, Jerry. You know... former stable mate and all... you know... me? Jerry: I yav naaa idae who the hell yam. (I have no idea who the hell you are) Miyagawa: What? Listen, Jeremy Howard, speak proper you hear? You can’t go around shouting all this gangster lingo all the time when no one can understand you! Jerry: I oy jeremy howard. (Brummie for “I am not Jeremy Howard”) Miyagawa: I know your Jeremy Howard. But... Jerry: I oy jeremy howard. Miyagawa: Yes Jerry, I know you are. Dammit Jerry, phone me later when you’ve stopped being such an ass. I own a fed too Jerry, and it doesn’t make you any better than I am. Jerry (As Miyagawa turns around): Noggen yedded grummy. (Stupid Idiot)Miyagawa turns around from Jerry who signs the book anyway. Miyagawa storms out of the shop and gets back into the car. He glances over towards the bookshop and notices that it’s called Douglas’ Bookshop. Miyagawa: That’s strange. Jeremy was at the wrong... oh crap. Says Miyagawa as he sees the massive board advertising Ozzy Osbourne signing there today...
