The Beginning

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/04/03 In everybody’s life, be it man, woman or child there comes a time when change is a necessity. The way we live our life is not longer acceptable. Mistakes repeat themselves and you are left with only one door left to walk through. The one marked change. This is where I am. No longer can I take the heartache so I must learn from my mistakes in the past. I am all alone, nobody is holding my hand. My path is a limited one that I must walk alone. My only companion is my mind and it is telling me that I must win. So far I have survived on luck but this will eventually run out. I am in the right place at the right time and this Friday I will finally get my chance to fulfill my father’s legacy I can't complain. I hopped aboard and enjoyed the ride without a moment's hesitation. Who wouldn't take advantage of the best possible situation for their career? Fate usually lets us enjoy success because we follow it but some of us follow a higher power. God is my constant companion and ever since my fathers death I have prayed that all my dreams will come true. I plan on jumping in with both feet. Life happens for a reason as do mistakes. These are all presented to us so we can learn from them and pass on to our children what we learn in life. It is obvious to me that my learning is not complete and even now I realize that I must take on the mantle of responsibility for myself and my accomplishments. The free ride has long since ended but I don’t mind. Now is the time for me to stand up and be counted. I am ready as the time has come for me to rise and defend what I believe in. There are no exceptions! My mind tells me there is a reason for me being in PCW. I came here to win, and I will. I am here, I have seen the talent and I will beat all opponents put in front of me. Unfortunately though my path has faltered. I am not nearly as strong as I once was. People question my position right and left. I question the validity of my current position… PCW's Janitor Does fate lie? Would it tease me with the sweet taste of success, only to watch me fall on my face? I cannot believe that this would be true. I can't possibly be finished. I feel the need to prove that I belong here; I must prove that to myself and anyone that doubts me. My pride, my identity, and my hard work all rely upon this. I realize that I have many options in my life at the current time. Here, at the top of my dreams, I will not allow myself to go back downwards. My only option, the only way out, is up. I have set my goals higher. I won't allow myself to fall. Nothing you can say will frighten me from this next leg of my journey. You can throw what you like. You will be wasting everyone's time, however. There is no way for you to 'rape' my dreams and goals. Nothing you can say will send me into retirement or severe depression. My mission is to succeed. I won't stop until I do. I am not yet finished with this match…this title…this federation… I am not afraid of you El Diablo The Mexican Devil.
