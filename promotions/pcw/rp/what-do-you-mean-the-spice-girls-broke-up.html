WHAT DO YOU MEAN THE SPICE GIRLS BROKE UP!?!?!?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [Scene opens in Flaymin’s house. In the living room dancing around with a microphone in his hands as The Spice Girls ‘Wannabe’ blares out of multiple speaks situated all around the living room, Flaymin grooves to the beat] Flaymin: If you wanna be my lover…You have to get with my friends…Make it last forever…Friendship never ends… [Having forgotten the rest of the lyrics, Flaymin looks at the lyrics sheet in his hand to see where he was up to.Unable to find the spot he was up to, he decides to just dance some more instead….Grooving to the right…..Break Dancing a little on the left….And moon-walking in between… However in the midst of the moon-walk, Flaymin slips over and bangs his head against the hard tiled floor. He grabs his throbbing head and winces in pain while his fingers run over the emerging bruises in his skull. Meanwhile, Flaymin’s new valet, Angelique, walks into the room and notices Flaymin on the floor writhing in torture] Angelique: You tried to moon walk again, didn’t you? Flaymin: Err…. NoI was doing the Macarena. Angelique: To a Spice Girls song? Flaymin: I was fusing together two different art forms: Brit-Pop and Spanish-Pop! Angelique: And what did you come up with? Flaymin: Iggy Pop! [Angelique rolls her eyes before she momentarily exits the room and returns with a large box] Flaymin: What’s in the box? Angelique: I bought you a Karaoke machine.Now you won’t need to write down the lyrics on pieces of paper anymore. [Flaymin walks over and begins to unpack the Karaoke machine. He then walks over to the TV and hooks it all up] Angelique: So what music do you want to do first? Flaymin: I think I’ll continue my ode to The Spice Girls. Angelique: Again? Flaymin: I still have ‘Stop right now’, ‘2 Become 1’ and ‘Spice World’ to cover. I can’t wait until their new album comes out! [Angelique frowns for a moment and scratches her head] Angelique: What do you mean ‘New Album’? Flaymin: You know- Posh, Baby, Sporty, Ginger and Scary doing it all again. Angelique: I’d hate to be the one to break it to you, buuuuuut….Ginger left the group to make yoga videos.Posh got married to a poof.Sporty is a lesbian.Scary is divorced.And Baby… well she got lost in the whole mêlée. Flaymin: What does any of that have to do with the music? Angelique: News Flash: They broke up a while back. [Tears instantly begin to well in Flaymin’s eyes] Flaymin: Say it isn’t so! Angelique: I’m sorry, but Girl Power is dead. [Flaymin’s jaws drops and he crumbles into a heap on the floor] Flaymin: I can’t believe it….. Music is officially dead!The airhead pop music….The mini skirts….The bad teeth….The horrible accents….The feminism….The lesbianism…. It’s….. All…. Over….. Angelique: Kaput.Finished.Dead and buried.Ding! Ding! Ding! They’re down for the count!Go home folks, there’s nothing to see here…. Flaymin: ALRIGHT, I GET IT!! But I’m still in a state of shock.This is like being told that The Jackson Five have broken up! Angelique: Uh… now that you’ve mentioned it…. [Flaymin grabs his ears and begins to yell] Flaymin: NO! NO!NO MORE DISASTER STORIES!!!Leave The Jackson Five out this!Michael… Janet…. Tito…err… Skeeto…. Angelique: Skeeto? Flaymin: You know, the one with the afro! Angelique: They all had afro’s! Flaymin: The guy with the big, black, curly one!It kind of looked like my one! [Flaymin lowers his head and points to his curly afro] Angelique: Right… that really narrows it down…. Flaymin: Bah!Nevermind woman, just go start the car and I’ll quickly pack my bags. [Angelique looks at Flaymin in surprise] Angelique: Where are we going? Flaymin: London! We have to reunite The Spice Girls before some other earth-shattering disaster occurs… like Milli Vanilli breaking up!!! [Angelique raises her hand to correct Flaymin, but then wisely opts not to question the madman as the scene fades to black…..] In the next instalment: Flaymin goes to London……
