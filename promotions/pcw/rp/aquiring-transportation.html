Aquiring transportation.

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Aquiring transportation. Featuring: Politically Incorrect Air travel. An industry that has taken a sharp nosedive recently, pardon the pun. Because of this, costsaving measures must be taken. To say the least, Politically Incorrect are rich men. Combined, they are estimated to be worth a quarter of a billion dollars due to industry, inheritance, and of course, politics. Today, they are going to make a major purchase. A local billionaire has fallen on hard times, and is auctioning off his belongings. Politically Incorrect needs a way to get to Spring Fling this evening, but they need a way to get there. What do air travel, billionaire, and auction indicate? Yep, a small jet is available for auction. The state believes that it will revert to them, as no one could possible afford the ten million dollar minimum the man is asking for. They obviously don't know Politically Incorrect. Or they would know that Politically Incorrect love amenities. For years, Powell and Lovecraft have wanted their own 'Air Force One', so to speak. And now, the chance to get it has arrived. They're ready for the jet to be in their possession. The only question is, what will they name it? ::Politically Incorrect pulls up to the McCoy estate on the outskirts of Des Moines. They are in a odd-looking red limousine, a vehicle of Lovecraft's, custom made. They get out, Powell holding a briefcase, as their chauffeur parks the car off to the side. They begin to head over to the area of the auction, talking as they go.:: William S. Powell: You ready for tonight? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yeah, I just hope there's not some guy here richer than us. We need to fly to get to Spring Fling, and I'd really rather not fly on commercial aircraft. William S. Powell: Afraid of terrorism, Lovecraft? Reginald V. Lovecraft: No, it's just the average air traveller disgusts me. I'd rather be on my own plane, with my own bathroom, thank you very much. William S. Powell: Heh, don't worry. We'll get there. No way anyone can beat what we have in this briefcase. And when we DO get there, we've got three teams to defeat. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Two of them came up after us from NTICW, whence Powell came. Sure, Fear Factory defeated him in his last NTICW match, but that was when he had Damian Beckett for a partner, and had already gotten the call up to PCW. There was no point in fighting a match for a worthless NTICW tag title, when he already had the opportunity to go after the PCW ones. William S. Powell: The vaunted tag division of PCW seems to be a bust already. We plowed through everyone promoted from NTICW in one night. Fear Factory, Misplaced, the Cowabungas...the only tag teams left in PCW we haven't faced are the Sensation of Annihilation and Suburban Uprising, both of whom suck. The Surburbans for the Civil Disobedience ripoff name, and the Sensation because they consist of BIG DAVEYLICIOUS and MIKE FRAMPTON. Hell, the dead corpses of Jobbers Inc. would make better opponents that those two! Reginald V. Lovecraft: I'm sure the two aforementioned teams are up next after we win tonight at Spring Fling. Then, we will have done as we said we would along. We will have, and are right now, dominating the PCW tag team division. ::They smile, as they walk on, approaching the auction area. They pull up two chairs, as the auctioneer bangs his gavel to signal the auction's beginning.:: Auctioneer: Alright, let us begin with the ridiculous. Lot #0001 for today is this jet airplane. ::Powell and Lovecraft both gasp, looking at the plane, painted gold and silver. It is a truly magnificent craft.:: Auctioneer: The asking price is ten million dollars, so I think we can move o-- ::Powell raises his paddle, indicating a bid.:: Auctioneer: Ten--ten--ten....we have a bid for ten million dollars. Going once, going twice, sold, to the gentleman in the black suit in the back. ::Powell and Lovecraft smile as they go up to the podium, opening the briefcase, and handing it's contents, ten million dollars in hundreds, to Mr. McCoy. Powell removes a can of paint from the briefcase, as the auctioneer hands him the deed of sale.: Reginald V. Lovecraft: What are you up to? William S. Powell: It's time to christen our craft. ::Taking a paintbrush out of his pocket, Powell paints 'POLITICALLY INCORRECT ONE on the side of the craft in black paint.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: It's lovely, but who are we going to get to fly this thing? William S. Powell: I'll give a pilot a call. You call Roger Schultz, and tell him to meet us at the McCoy residence in about half an hour. We should be ready to leave Des Moines for Spring Fling at about 11:40 AM, 11:50 in case there is traffic causing Schultz and the pilot to be late. ::Both men go off to make their respective phone calls, and return.:: William S. Powell: Pilot's on his way. Reginald V. Lovecraft: So is your campaign manager. William S. Powell: This is perfect. We should arrive with plenty of time to prepare for our contest tonight. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yes, Mr. Powell. There is nothing that can stop us from our goal. We ARE the most dominant tag team in this era of Primetime Championship Wrestling. Forget Golden Game. Forget Cowabunga Connection. Forget Fear Factory. Forget any other pretenders to the throne. Tonight, we prove that EVERY DAY IS ELECTION DAY! William S. Powell: And, on Election Day, POLITICALLY INCORRECT ALWAYS TRIUMPHS! ::Both men walk over to their new plane, admiring it, as we fade to the usual.:: Paid for by the Friends of YOUR Tag Champs, Politically IncorrectThe Conquerors of the Tag Team Division
