Fusion Fallout: A Pope Chronicles Interlude

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30th June 2002 Allah-person is a religion fighting machine, fighting single handily the anti-Islam warriors of the free world. The Rabid Rabi is the single person who can turn back the hope of a free Palestinian state and keep the Jewish faith alive... plus he also regularly gets invited to lunch with lots of old Jewish women to meet their daughters... and the axe of circumcision is a deadly accurate weapon of mass destruction. The Popeman is the ultimate of all the arch rivals of all those that align themselves with cults such as Scientology. The Cardinal, the follower of the Popeman and the chief nemesis of all of those such said cult members. But now as we rejoin them in the Popecave suddenly an unexpected call is made. Director: That’s a wrap people! Good job! The Popeman and Cardinal walk past the camera, in fact so much so that we switch to another camera, one this time that seems more “real”. The cardinal removes her small red mask and the Popeman removes his cleaver facial disguise... a small white bowl placed upon the top of his skull. Den/Cardinal: That is such a lame disguise. Miyagawa/Popeman: It beats a pair of glasses, however. Den: I stand corrected. So can we go get some dinner or something, I’m ravenous. Miyagawa: I thought you were meant to be cardinal? Den: Hilariously funny, please do try to keep it up. Oh yeah, your wife called this morning. Miyagawa looks over at Den, with peaked interest. Miyagawa: Oh right, I guess I’d better call her back then... erm... she didn’t see Fusion did she? Den nods happily. Den: Apparently. And she said not to bother to call. Miyagawa gulps. Not just any gulp. One of those Vince McMahon “I practise this in front of a mirror” gulps. Miyagawa: Ok, now I’m in the shit. I’d better call her. Voice (from off camera... with a Russian accent): I wouldn’t bother. Miyagawa turns about and the camera swings behind him, showing Natalia Miyagawa in her fully pregnant glory wearing a pretty bog standard maternity dress, but with Miyagawa’s old leather jacket over the top of it. Miyagawa tries to smile and look happy at her, even though you can see in his eyes that he’s fully expecting to be shouted at. Den looks at Miyagawa and then at Natalia. Den: I’ll leave you two to it. Miyagawa looks over at her sharply. Miyagawa: Hey! You’re meant to be my bodyguard, guard me already! Den chuckles to herself before retorting back right at him. Den: My contract doesn’t cover acts of god or stupidity. Den walks off quickly, slowly removing parts of the rather rigid Cardinal costume as she goes along until she is finally out of sight. The camera looks back to Natalia who is still standing there quiet. Miyagawa walks over to her, still smiling, still expecting to be shouted at quite heavily. Miyagawa: You’ve had a haircut... it looks great. Natalia (shaking her head): Nope, I haven’t. Miyagawa: Erm... it’s a manicure... **sees Natalia shaking her head still** new makeup... been to a spa... signed up to a gym... ok... now... about Fusion... Natalia looks quite firm, as in she isn’t smiling, but then again isn’t frowning either at this point in time... notice I said at this point in time. Natalia: Yes. About Fusion... Miyagawa does another “Vince McMahon” gulp. Natalia: You went to a strip bar. Miyagawa immediately realises he should just back down straight away. Miyagawa: I know... I’m sorry... it was for that Ladyknox joke thing... really... honestly... I’m sorry. Natalia: I’m proud of you. Miyagawa opens his mouth to continue apologising and then stops. He looks at her suspiciously and then goes to say something but stops again and looks at her again. Miyagawa: You are? Natalia: I am. Miyagawa: Erm... why? Natalia: You went to a strip bar. And you didn’t even get a lap dance. I know the business your in, that was all for the show right, you wouldn’t normally go to a strip bar when your out of town Less than one tenth of a millisecond passes before Miyagawa responds. Miyagawa: NO! Sorry... no, of course not. I wouldn’t do that to you, hell, I didn’t even do it before we met, because I’m just generally not a strip bar sort of person. I don’t have the perverted genes in me... Steve on the other hand, he loves them. But they’re just not my thing. Natalia: Ok good. Now are we just going to stand around here all day while you apologise, or are you going to get changed out of your Pope costume so we can go get something to eat? I’m eating for two here you know. Miyagawa: Sure. The camera ends on a close up of Miyagawa’s grin. Just after the camera fades out a trailer for Wallace and Grommets “A Close Shave” plays. Is that just a co-incidence or just the writer trying to be subtle? Who knows?
