The tour.

28 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-28-03 “You’re kidding right?!” We find ourselves in the gym of our resident Adonis, Dan “Big O” Osbourne. Obviously dressed for a workout, he is arguing with his manager, Mikey. “No, Dan, I’m not.” “How can they book me for a match with Stone Burns?! He’s a thug. No style. No real skill to speak of. What are they thinking?!” “I’m sure it’s just intended to see how you perform before they give you the bigger matches. You should know you can’t just leap into the mail event.” “Yes I can! I’m Dan Osbourne! I’m the best technical wrestler in the business!” “Don’t you mean, TACTICIAN?!” “IT’S A REAL WORD DAMN YOU!” “Hehehehe. That’s what makes it funny. Anyway, you may be the best, but they don’t know that yet. You’ve got to remember you’ve been gone for almost two years. Most of these guys don’t remember you.” “Fine. If I have to go through these trials to get noticed again, I will. And Stone Burns will be a character witness when I’m done with him.” With that, Dan goes into an intense workout. Weight and speed training, then tears through a few sparring partners. Drenched with sweat, Dan wraps it up and heads to the showers. As Dan showers we get a view of our surroundings. The entire bathroom is done up in black and white marble with gold plated highlights. Black towels hang from the gold racks. The monogram on both reads Big O. On the marble sink sits numerous hair care products and a bottle on ‘High Karate.’ A figure passes by the camera dropping a robe to the ground. The camera pans up just in time to see the shower door close. As the two bodies merge, the cameraman wisely vacates. He’s been shot at enough to know that with Dan, discretion is the better part of valor. The cameraman steps out to see Mikey, arms folded with a scowl on his face. “Are they at it again? Those two. I swear. Well, they’ll be busy for a while so I’ll give you a tour of the grounds.” With that, we begin our tour. Starting in the grand entryway, the camera pans. The windows on the front wall of the room are, in all actuality, the walls themselves. With the exception of a few feet of support, the glass covers the entire surface. The polished white floors lead to twin cascading stairways. They meet at either end of the top walkway overlooking the wide-open foyer. From the walkway, we can see that the highly decorated foyer resembles the vision of the home of the Gods themselves, Mt. Olympus. Moving into the master bedroom, the sudden change in colors is an onslaught on the eyes. Red silk sheets cover the extravagant four poster bed. The cherry oak furniture complements the whole feel of the room, calming the brightness with a warm contrast. The beige carpeting also helps to break the colors and give the room a larger look. Finished with the bedroom, Mikey leads us into the entertainment wing of the mansion. A full sized theater, across from the four lane bowling alley, runs any movie desired, via a computer controlled storage and projection room. Further down the hall the game room rings, beeps, and chimes with the pinball machines, stand up video games, and wall sized television screens. Large, comfortable chairs sit in front of multiple televisions with multiple gaming systems attached to each. Across the hall is the dance club, which is a scaled down version of Dan’s actual nightclub, ‘Afterglow.’ Mikey then leads us into the most expensive part of the complex… the garage. Starting with the custom Jesse James chopper, the motorcycle wing is stocked with twenty-seven different bikes. Harley-Davidson classics, colorful bullet bikes, and custom choppers from multiple manufactures cover the floor. In the next room, Dan’s pride and joy sits in the center of the room. A canary yellow, 1969 GTO Judge, with a monster blower stinging out from under the hood, sits ready to pounce on 21” tires with chrome mag wheels. Surrounding the Judge are numerous cars, ranging from exotics like the white Ferrari Testerossa and neon green Lotus Esprit to classics like the ’57 Chevy, ’68 ‘Vette, and many, many others. “Well you’ve seen the entire place now and I think we’ve killed enough time with flavor text so let’s go see if Dan and Molly are done yet.” After quite a while of looking, Mikey finally locates Dan in the interview room talking with a reporter from PCW magazine. “So Mr. Osbourne, can you tell us a little about your relationship with your valet, Molly?” “Without a doubt, Molly is the single most important thing in my life.” “What about your manager, Mikey?” “My best friend and also the greatest trainer in the industry. No one knows more about wrestling than he does.” “What are your goals in PCW?” “My goal is the same as anyone who puts on their tights and steps into that ring. I plan to be the Champion.” “Well, you have to admit, that is a task easier said than done. Do you really think you have what it takes to get it?” “Well of course I do. Anyone who doubts him or herself should just give it up now. If you don’t think you can be the absolutely best in the business then you hinder yourself to a point where you’ll never succeed.” “Wise words. Well Mr. Osbourne, thank you for your time.” “Always a pleasure.” With that, the interviewer leaves the compound. Dan and Molly hug Mikey and wish him well as they get in the Ferrari Enzo and head out for a night on the town. Mikey finds the nearest maid and instructs her to clean the bathroom, training room, and interview room. For the first time, the cameraman speaks. “So, like what, are you the butler too?” “Basically, yeah. Dan and Molly are rarely home, and when they are, they usually wind up together like they were today. It really hinders Dan’s training, but he’s happy I guess, so I’m happy for him. “Sounds like there’s a bit of resentment you’re harboring there.” “When did this turn into my interview? I won’t lie to you. I wouldn’t mind seeing less of Molly around here, but it’s more for Dan than anything else. She’s a distraction, and distractions get people hurt.” “But on a personal note, are you jealous of the time she and Dan spend together?” “Not really. They go out all the time. They don’t need a third wheel so they leave me at home. There’s enough around here to keep me busy, and it’s not like I don’t have a social life of my own.” “Anyone special?” “Special? Not likely. I like to drink and shoot pool so that’s what I do.” “Do you ever head out to Dan’s nightclub?” “I like to relax. That club is far from relaxing. Too many beautiful people doing beautiful people things. Look at me. I’m not a great looking guy. I don’t really fit into a place like that. I’m more of a biker bar kind of guy. Give me a good pub and I’m happy.” “Well, sir, I think I’ve been here long enough. Thank you for the tour and the impromptu interview.” “No problem. It’s not that often that anyone speaks to me.” As the cameraman drives off, Mikey stands in the doorway, “I hope that doesn’t come back to haunt me.”
