Mayor for a Day.

28 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 28th, 2003. The scene reluctantly opens up to reveal the inside of a Saturday Night Live-like official Capitol building of some sort, with Jackson Rust leaning in a big leather chair, his feet propped on the desk that lies in front of him. Kimberly types away at a desk of her own parallel to Rust, as Al and Steve play a rather shoddy game of ping-pong. Jackson smirks as he turns the swivel chair around, facing the open window…revealing… Jackson Rust: Ah…Weedville. Rust sighs in relaxation as the ole Weedville sun dawns upon him as he looks out of the window; there’s people walking down the street, cars driving slowly so the dumbass children have time to run their soiled asses off of the street…several men in black masks and black jumpsuits taking their turns shooting the innocent… Jackson Rust: Yep…it’s the shit to be the friggin’ owner of it. Kimberly: You don’t “own” it; you just run it… Jackson sighs as he pushes off of the delicate windowsill, turning around in one awesome swivel movement, before facing Kimberly rather lamely… Jackson Rust: Shut it, Narrator… Narrator: Make me. Jackson Rust: I wi—never mind. Narrator: Continue with how the promo was planned. Jackson Rust: Fine…Al, Steve, keep planning ping-pong (rather shitty, too), and— Al: Hey! We heard those parentheses! Steve: Y-yeah…a-ass. Jackson Rust: LIKE I WAS SAYING! Kimberly, repeat your last line after me. Kimberly: Okay. Jackson Rust: *ahem* Yep…it’s the shit to be the friggin’ owner of it. Kimberly: You don’t “own” it; you just run it. Jackson Rust: Okay then…I suppose you don’t “own” that diamond necklace I bought you…you just run it for me, right? Kimberly: Fine, you own Weedville. Jackson smirks, leaning back in his big time chair as Al and Steve stop hitting the little orange plastic ball back and forth, and place their cheesy, red, graded Fisher-Price paddles down upon the green table with little lines to make it seem they’re playing actual tennis…some kids are fucking stupid to believe that. Kimberly: You don’t have to drag down everything AND children’s imaginations, too, do you? Narrator: It’s my job… Kimberly: Oh, very well...drag down anything creative that you wish. Al: Do we, and when I mean “we”, I mean Steve and I, play any significant part in this promo? Steve: D-do w-we? Jackson Rust: Does Mother Teresa shit in the woods? The scene immediately cuts to the nearby Weedville National Forest, of which does not exist, showing one, Mother Teresa, attempting to take a squat next to a small family of black bears, as Paul Mycock, from a previous, and I mean OLD Jackson Rust promo, comes onto scene, blocking the horrid visuals that would be if he hasn’t been standing there. Paul Mycock: Ladies and gentlemen, I’m Paul Mycock, reporting for JacksonCO news. Yes, I have recovered from the malicious attacks by Gentle Ben in my last news report, and I am suing him with a hefty fine; and now, here I am…reporting with Mother Teresa…and another family of bears. The bullock that is Paul Mycock approaches the excreting Saint as she winces in pain; as well do the bears. He bends over a bit, and puts the microphone in front of Mother Teresa’s mouth. Paul Mycock: Now, Mother Teresa…do you normally shit in the forest? Mother Teresa: Only on Tuesdays, and Sundays…those are my…eeeeeeeeoww; erm…wild days. She winces in more pain as she tries to push. Paul Mycock: Now…I have a question that all viewers want to know…which is seemingly the last question I asked before I was attacked the first time: are you Catholic? Mother Teresa: Yes I am, you dumb fuck. The entire bear family takes that as cue before leaping over Mother Teresa and maliciously assaulting Paul Mycock, as he screams in pain and agony, before the scene cuts back. Jackson Rust: I suppose you DO get a significant part. Al: Cool and all, but dude! Jackson Rust: Yeah? Al: Mother Teresa has been dead for a bit… Jackson arches his eyebrow. Jackson Rust: Bad example, my fault…so, let me get a new one then. Rust quickly opens one of the varnished drawers of his Weedville Mayor desk, and grabs out a small notepad, labeled “Analogies Meaning Yes”, and flips through several pages. He sighs as he pretends to know what the hell he’s doing, before randomly stopping on a page, and looking at it quizzically. Jackson Rust: All right then. Do you get a significant role? Ha! Does Winnie the Pooh shit in the woods? Narrator: Why is everything involving shitting in the woods? Kimberly: Very good question. Just as a scene cut is beginning to happen, sounds of gunfire go off before Rust, as everyone ducks down for cover. Rust shrugs, and motions for the scene to cut with a simple wave of his hand. Voice: Fuck, no! A bruised, battered, and bloodied Paul Mycock stumbles onto the scene, some of his skeletal frame showing through the right side of his head, as he holds his left leg in his arm, trying to keep balance after the assault by the bear family and Mother Teresa. Paul Mycock: No bears! Please no! *SMACK* Narrator: Woooooo! I have asterisk SMACK asterisk powers here, too! Mycock grabs onto a tree, keeping himself off of the ground as he pants for air, and loss of blood. Paul Mycock: At least this is a gentle bear… Narrator: You thought the same about Gentle Ben. Paul Mycock: Shit… All of a sudden, from above the tree the dismembered, and disfigured reporter has a bucket of something dropped on his head. He frantically peels it off of his skull, with honey slowly falling from his hair. Paul Mycock: Winnie? Are you up there? Winnie the Pooh: They don’t call me Pooh for nothin’! Paul Mycock: Huh? *SPLAT* Paul Mycock: YUCK! YOU FUCKING BASTARD BEAR! I SHOULD KILL YOU AND YOUR MOTHERFUCKING GAY ANIMAL FRIENDS! Winnie the Pooh: RAR~! With one leap, the yellowish animated bear leaps from the tree, and smashes a beehive over the head of Paul Mycock, dropping him to the forest floor, before dropping an elbow across the sternum. The bees flutter away from the action, before all looking toward Pooh. Winnie the Pooh: Not this time, guys. Pooh whispers to the bees, as they form a circle around his body; they buzz in agreement, before turning about face as Paul Mycock gets to his foot…with his other foot in his hand. He looks up to see the angered hive of bees, before signaling to cut the footage, but the bees attack first. Paul Mycock: Oh, fuck. He waves a simple goodbye as the bees swarm him, Winnie laughing along as he gets stung repetitively until the screaming stops, and the scene cuts back to the Weedville Capitol building. Jackson Rust: Better analogy? Kimberly: Much better. Al: Dude, does that guy even work for you? Jackson Rust: I should hope not… Steve: H-he’s…l-li-like d-dead. Jackson shrugs uncaringly. Jackson Rust: Yeah, I know that. But I have more important things to worry about. Narrator: Being what? Jackson Rust: Running Weedville, dude! Kimberly: Why the hell didn’t you make that a top priority before? Jackson Rust: Because I felt that the people should have a chance to run their own city…*mumbles* then they fucking turned it into an hierarchy of anarchy…the bastards. Al: What was that? Jackson looks up from his mumbling ways, and quickly tries to think of something. Jackson Rust: CORPSE! Rust points in a completely random general direction, before turning around in his swivel chair, not allowing Al to see him after he turns around from looking for the corpse. Al: Where’d he go? Narrator: *sly smile* He jumped out of the window. Al looks at the undamaged window. Al: There was no shattering… Narrator: He opened it before jumping. Al: Oh…shit! Jackson, wait! Al quickly runs and slides across the Mayor’s desk, before spinning the swivel chair back around on accident, still not enabling him to see Rust, who is sliding down in the chair, and leaps through the glass, and out of the third story window. Jackson Rust: SHIT! Steve: W-wh-what? Jackson Rust: He broke my frickin’ window! Kimberly: He thought you committed suicide. Jackson Rust: The dumb bastard… All of a sudden, barging through the door on the opposing side of the large room, a slight woman; a secretary, comes running in, and leaning over Rust’s desk, giving him a nice shot as she frantically speaks. Secretary: Mr. Jackson, Mr. Jackson! Jackson Rust: What is it? Secretary: Al just jumped from the third story window and landed… Jackson Rust: Good. Secretary: …on Paul Mycock! Jackson Rust: EVEN BETTER! Secretary: We’re going to airlift him to a hospital. Jackson Rust: No need to. Secretary: Why? Then, Hulk Hogan barges in through the door, with Triple H and the Undertaker following close at hand. Hulk Hogan: Because we taught him how to no-sell, brother! Triple H: I taught him how to no-sell, because I’m that damn good! The Undertaker: Whatever, fools. I taught him how to no-sell, ‘cause it’s my yard, and I’m the Deadman! The entourage ignores the verbally abusing WWE superstars, as they go back to talking. Feeling left out, the aforementioned Superstars fade away suddenly, due to lack of attention, hyping, and licking of the nuts. Jackson Rust: Anyway, he’ll be up in a moment…probably didn’t feel a thing. Secretary: How is that so? Jackson Rust: He’s too fuggin’ dumb to feel pain. Secretary: Oh. She shrugs, and turns around, leaving as Al barges through the door, crushing the secrtery behind it (but not before hitting her in the head with it). Al: Shit, that was a long plummet! The scene mercifully closes.
