The 80's [1]

2003

Date: 04.01.03 'Dante....you're not thinking of going back in Kriegman's basement again are you?' said the sweet and professing voice of Tracy Ellis-Jackson. The next ideal for the Lyrical Messiah other than figuring out just how to knock Shania Twain off of the top of the Billboard charts happens to be sneaking into Alan Kriegman's basement, known to be the Neverland of gadgets and weaponry. The scene arrives at our destination of Pleasantville, New Hampshire. The fairly suburban neighborhood is flocked with men and women with cheer on their faces and their bodies contorted to not only water their grasses, but talk to others as well. Looking up upon a grassy knoll, we can see a monument built right in the middle of the battlefield in which Alan Kriegman and Dillan Meyers, collectively known as Goovil waged war in the first and only Pleasantville Deathmatch, in which Alan Kriegman literally 'died'. Fortunately he came back and did alot of evil things in OSW, but as another figure steps onto the plane, he kicks at the statue of STREAK getting blown to bits by its own missles in a psuedo Metal Gear Solid 2 moment (check out the game it rules!). Dante Jackson is bitter, bitter to the point that even the mere mention of Alan Kriegman has him hiding and cowering in fear. 'Okay, so he TRIED to kill you!' the designated cheer of his wife resounds through his ears as the couple sits upon the knoll. 'You're still here aren't you?' Dante sighs as he seems out it, although two nights ago at the final Turmoil for 2002, he defeated Rent A Hero, in a fast paced, techincally sound match up which is probably his biggest singles win to date. 'No,' he sulks down, his platinum and diamond encrusted medalion sits on his chest as he breathes in some of the New Hampshire air. 'I was meant to save The End from blindness, and DAMMIT I'm going to do it!' 'You mean Xavier Kannon right?' the contemporary vixen replies. Dante chuckles, 'Right, I meant the BOSS of the company. Jezz, Tracy you sure don't get out much.' 'Or maybe you don't remember when The End beat you into a trashcan and left it over your head. Remember at his party? When he changed his name?' - FLASHBACK FLASHBACK FLASHBACK! - Now that The End is blind once more, Alan Kriegman takes the opportunity to walk up and put the pen and contract in his hands. Kriegman:'Simple enough, Skylar--' The End:'Xavier.' Kriegman stops, the supergenius thinking he's heard wrong. Kriegman:'What?' The End:'My name is Xavier. Xaiver Kannon.' Kriegman:'What the hell, when did this happen?' Giving a shrug with a smirk, The End pats out the smoke in his hair while answering Kriegman's question. The End:'Friday, 5 PM.' Kriegman:'And why on earth would you change your name to Xavier Kannon?' The End nods his head to the left to the RUSH roster members, Chris Matthews, Tyler Valentine, Stimpo. The End:'A going-away present to them. The Resistance. And anyone else who doesn't like the owner and CEO. Remember my Christmas Wish show?' Kriegman:'How could I forget? I planned so many ways to destroy the set and you with it.' Though a disturbing statement, The End isn't surprised and keeps going. The End:'Anyway, Matt Elia wrote a letter asking me to help out with the Resistance. But truthfully there was nothing I could do for him. I felt bad, I couldn't fulfill his wish. But I wanted to make it up to him somehow, do the next best thing.' Kriegman:'So you changed your legal name so that when you retire they can see the headline Xavier Kannon Retires from PCW?' A nod of his head. The End:'It's the little consolation I could give. They'd like to see that happen, and it's the best I can do.' - END FLASHBACK! FLASHBACK! FLASHBACK! - 'Oh yeah,' the African American wonder smiles cheesly, before getting up from the knoll. A brisk winds sweeps up behind him, twirling him around as he wears a ensamble closely to the look of Kid Rock, in his 'Badwitaba' video. As the couple walks down the knoll to the inhibitants of Pleasantville, something stops Dante dead in his tracks as he looks ahead. 'What is it?' his wife asks him tugging on his arm with much resistance by Dante. He replies, 'Chet Frickin' King.' His eyes hit the center of the street as the figure known as Chet King stands in front, marvelling at the houses and their regular inhabitants. He smirks before waving towards Dante, 'HEY DANTE!' 'Dammit, dammit, damn!' Dante mutters the cypher, before Chet walks up to him, a slight smile on his face as he folds his arms along his chest. Wearing a Seattle Mariners T-shirt with blue jeans, he smirks once more at Dante, before turning towards the little lady. Chet's eyes light up, 'Well he-.' 'She's not Cindy Bi, Chet.' Dante cuts him off. 'Anybody else from your permecium seed around here?' 'Well,' Chet scratches his head before turning around, checking out the people down below. 'There's Vanilla Skillz down there battling with some average mute guy, Chris Matthews is about to deck Marvin Harris, Mordred - ' 'Who's Mordred?' Dante cuts Chet off once more, before Chet points towards the RUSH member. 'Had to be the goth one eh?' Chet just shakes his head, 'Chris Collins, Bryce Dolan, even Jackson Rust - they came here to buy summer homes, or to disturb with the old folks, whatever the case might be.' Dante chuckles, before walking over towards the center house, parked with a small 1956 Compact, a few gnomes placed in Invader Zim fashion, warding off some of the visitors and a oval shaped ball placed at the top of the garage. Around it, from a downward view there are burned spots into the ground, places Dante wouldn't dare take a step towards. The front door of the house seems to still have Christmas ornaments and a small reef placed on the glass in a spiral decoration. With much a smile to his face, Dante sneaks around the side of the house, creaking before a house warning sensor pops out from under the ground. Starring at Dante blankly, the mechanism swirls around, catching Dante's body in a full gaze as the Lyrical Messiah takes a few steps back, before the mechanism shoots a red laser aimed straight at Dante's head! Dante stands there, stupid look and all before Chet King pushes him out of the way. The laser shoots, misfiring by hitting the side of a conjoined house only seperated by the lawns and driveways. Dante gwaks at the sight of the laser bouncing off a plant pot before burying itself into the ground, but as he turns around the gwak grows even wider. 'EW! GET THE HELL OFF OF ME!' Chet pushes himself off, 'I just saved your life!' 'No you didn't my reactions did, you aren't even worthy of MENTIONING MY LIFE in the same sentence as I referring to you!' the African male barked at Chet, before picking himself up. 'Saved by Chet King, not happening!' 'Look,' Chet started as he began dusting himself off. 'I've been inside of this federation for THREE Shanghai`ed's now, and I don't plan on dropping one. Now if you'd please just shut up, maybe we could get into the basement.' Dante stares at him before speaking fastly. 'What you talkin' bout Chet?' 'The basement? ALAN KRIEGMAN's basement?' Chet only states the obvious as the two men stare into the pad lock oak covering the jewel of United States Weapons Technology. Alan Kriegman's Super Basement of Weapons and Gadgets. Knocking the lock over with a few well placed kicks, the lock unlatches from the chained guard clenched tightly around the door handles, before Chet pulls the leftmost one open. Looking in, Dante sighs heavily, whistling as if the find inside were a Californian Gold Mine. 'Jesus!' Dante cries out before walking over to the Destructo Head Disc seated upon a pile of weapony labeled, 'Used into Turning Dillan Meyers EVIL' before Chet turns around, holding up a piece of equipment that looks abit like a sphere from Final Fantasy, you know the one that they kept messages from Jyscal and Jecht in? 'Dude, what's up with the Final Fantasy log?' 'The writer has been playing it three days straight, ever since New Years, and he's now JUST about to get to Zanarkand. Excuse him will ya?' Dante asks, before taking the sphere out of Dante's hands. 'Hey! I found that!' The two men rush out of the basement, before a voice can be heard from the stairs, 'Somebody better not be wacking off to my Breasto Impanto machine....' Rushing out of the basement, Dante carries the sphere towards the center of the street, a smile on his face as he looks at it oddly. Chet taps him on the shoulder, as Dante turns, giving him a STONE GAZE! Okay, he just looked at him okay? Geesh, you readers are SOOOOOO wanting to believe these metaphors - errr...um...anyways the others around them, particularly Jackson Rust looks at the two PCW wrestlers, co-workers of his before chuckling. 'Off playing video games again?' the former DWO World Heavyweight champion jokes, before Dante begins to sit Indian style, facing the sphere as others join around him. 'Dante, if you do something - ' 'Shush, its a sphere, what more harm could it - ' Before the others can say anything, Dante taps the sphere causing a rift to appear out of materialized air. A swift wind picks up, as the rift grows wider and wider, before it encapsulates all of the members around it. Tracy walks over towards the sphere, looking at it strangely, before shrugging her shoulders and picking it up. Inside the small warp, the grunting voice of Dante Jackson could be heard, screaming and crying for dear life, 'SAVE ME CHET!' 'I'm in here with you idiot.' Dante in a coy fashion turns away from the Georgia native, 'Yeah....I knew that. So where are we?' Soon, from behind the group, Mani Shigeru stands, cold and callous as Dante looks at him, thinking he'll say something to spark interest as the group happens to transfer from whereever they are currently, to a new destination. 'Well Mr. Mute, got anything smart to say? Come on, I know you went to JAPAN HARVARD!' Dante screams at the mute, who only stands there, not saying anything. His tag team partner Shin twirls Dante by the collar. 'He doesn't speak, deal with it.' Dante pushes himself away, 'Look, go back to Vanilla and Mullet okay, I hear they're hiring for some Mortal Kombat sequel!' 'OOH! CAN DIS PLAYA BE JOHNNY CAGE?!' Skillz pops from out of nowhere yelling at the mere mention of Mortal Kombat, all his gas mask co-worker Marvin Harris can do in the meantime is smack him good behind the head. 'Well, looks like we're going somewhere...'
