Matt Land, Part 4 - The Void

undated

Date: 4//403 Where am I? Matt asks himself, searching the recesses of his mind for an answer. Nothing comes forth; all his thoughts and memories are gone. His past is non-existent and his beginning hasn’t ever happened yet, he doesn’t even know if he is alive, but then again he doesn’t even know what life is, or what his purpose is going to be. He is just moving along, not against his own will, but he can’t stop it. He has no control over what is going on here, he isn’t the creator and he doesn’t understand any of this. His brown eyes peer into the distance, only to be met with darkness, it is all around him. Encompassing him and bringing him in, making his black suit seem just to disappear and blend perfectly in with the blackness- the void ness. As he continues forth, he isn’t sure if he is floating or walking, his legs seem to be making movements, but every time he looks down to see, there is nothing there. “Hello?” He yells out, awaiting a response that he knows isn’t going to be received. He is the only one in this place and he is enjoying the solitude and solemnity of being alone. He still doesn’t understand how he is moving, like a puppet with his master pulling on the strings he keeps trekking forward, almost as if something is pulling him in the direction he is going. A smile forms on his stone face, revealing his white teeth, that some how seem to sparkle in the darkness. “Welcome to my world Matt Jackson,” a voice booms out, filling his ears and shaking him down to his very core. It sounds like it is carried on the wind and without a second thought; it quickly fades away to the bleakness to where it came. He tries to spin himself around to see if anything is behind him, but his control over his legs is void and he is forced to keep walking the way he is now. He pushes his arms up to his brown hair and pushes back, making sure he has some control over his body. In a fit of anger, he tries to stop himself from going forward, but every muscle in his body tightens and makes him lunge forth on his path. “I am the Neo Void God,” the voice starts up, with a certain power in his voice. He is the ruler of this black world that Matt is now a part of and he wants to make sure the young agent understands that. “Where am I?” Matt questions, hoping to finally be able to get some resolve to the question that has been plaguing his mind ever since his inception into this world. He knows that he should be feeling some sort of emotions, but instead there is nothing except emptiness. He feels a since of belonging, almost as if he should be here. “You are in my world Matt,” the voice assures him, letting him try to figure out what exactly is going on. “My partner and I created it and you are our first creation.” “Why did you make me?” He asks, wanting to know exactly who he was and what his purpose was, in this place. He knew that he should be here, but he just didn’t understand why and he didn’t understand the timing. “We needed a strong person to hold order over everything when we are completed. We needed someone who was going to be able to take charge,” a female voice chimes in, answering his question. Her voice is more a whisper and not booming like the original voice he heard. “Aren’t you guys the rulers though?” “Yes we are, but we needed people to worship us. We needed people to fear us and know who we were. We are the god’s of your world Matt,” they both tell him, in perfect unison. “Then show yourselves to me!” His voice is full of contempt and as soon as the words left his mouth, his mind began race. All his past memories and thoughts coming back to him, he knows who he is and he knows why he is here. They aren’t gods, they are just failures, people looking to rule over defenseless people. “No one will stand over me!” At the sound of those words the couple appears in front of him. The man an enormous physical presence and his long black hair is hanging out. With robotic arms he reaches for his creation, but Matt jumps out of the way. Feeling inside is jacket for his automatic, Matt pulls it out with force and points it towards the Neo Void God and pulls the trigger. BOOM! The sound echo’s in the hollow world, as the bullet passes through the man’s robotic suit and he disappears. At the sight of her partner falling, the woman rushes forward, her blonde hair hidden behind a bandana and her tight leather outfit clinging tightly to her body. She swings at Matt and knocks him down, with an impact like no other he has ever felt before. “You can’t beat us. We are the perfect team,” she yells out. Swinging at him more and more, lashing out in hate for the anger of her downed partner. “This is our Universe, we created it and you can’t stop us.” The blood starts to spill from his forehead from her repeated blows and Matt knows he is going to soon be dead. The stronger more powerful woman is going to destroy him. He has one last chance and that is to make her lie before his feet like so many others. Pulling the trigger of his pistol the sound once again rings out. BOOM! She falls back and he gets back on to his feet. Taking a step in her direction, he notices he is in control of his legs again. Pulling the trigger again and again making sure that she isn’t going to get up. He smiles as her crimson life force pours out adding color to the once black place. “No one is in control of me. This is my universe and I am the creator!” Jerking out of his dream, Matt smiles and looks around, glad to be back in his own room. Everything is in its place and the blood that was on him a few seconds ago has faded. “I am the next Continental Champion,” he tells himself, with a smile and rolls over to get some sleep before Fusion.
