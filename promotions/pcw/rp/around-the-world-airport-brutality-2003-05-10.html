Around the world.. Airport Brutality.

10 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/10/03 Losing again.. and my nuts really hurt. I thought and still recovering mentally and physically from the captivity. But no excuses for my fiasco in the PCW but I just continued on, leaving South Korea from Athens.. Entering the airport with my duffel bag shouldered, and my blade propped in the storage of my tux coat pocket. Strolling past the fellow travellers, I spotted a few harlots.. and thinking of my kidnappers I just felt like slicing their skin off and then burning the inside of their flesh. But I garnered my hostility for a second and went on. The process of receiving my ticket had already been consummated and all I needed to do was go on to my gate, not too far from where I was positioned. The airport surroundings looked much the same as in America, high heavens, glossy white floors, boutiques and antique shops in a profusion and the presence of thousands of people. Finally, I approached the security, fine dressed males with the addition of a female, and I could sense her fresh attitude from her makeup as she waited there, tapping her foot with her heels. People in front of me placed their bags through the screen-check, and after that I came.. placing my one and only bag, with no difficulty.. I'd had no jewelery or anything I needed to remove at the time so the process was pretty quick, but passing the metal detector it went off. I confusingly harked at the sound as one of the officers, in a choppy American accent said.. 'Sir please step back through de' detector.' and I followed.. The other officer frisked me from shoulder down, and then felt an object in my pocket.. removed it. Hey.. You can't do that! I shouted in effort to pull back my blade that I had gone through no trouble in the past (somehow).. the women security officer stepped in with a nonchalant look on her face and the firm grip of her palms on her hips. 'We can do whatever we want.' she claimed as I was readying myself to choke her until' she bled from her ears but held myself back as the security officers examined my blood caked blade.. arching his eyebrows in puzzlement. 'What're ya doin' vit' this?' he questioned as I simply knew the answer in an instant.. I carry it around I retorted as he laughed along with his partner and the woman.. His laugh turned in to a frown, and then a bleak expression. I pushed my lips to the left, and left a single dimple on my face waiting for what was to happen.. Both officers checked out the blade and just shook their heads as one came forward.. 'You c'not carry this bag' on de' airplane and in da' bag. We will have to put it through a special delivery luggage and you can obtain it in South KORAYA', is it?' Hearing the ridiculous comment I just shook my head and simply said No. and managed to jerk back my blade with a single pull but they both instantaneously tackled me as I thought I was trying to pull of an act of terrorism.. the women had called for backup and additional airport security and seconds later they had me shackled, and dragged out in to a special room, in the remote areas of the airport. There were two guys there, behind a sleek ivory table.. they pounded their fists like they were tough but actually they were tough... but I acted smooth even though I had known nothing about this room or what I had gotten in to. Finally, I figured out they were going to thwack me around with punches, kicks, slams, and all sorts of corporal punishment. Moments later I felt the harsh and heavy fists of two ponderous males hitting me and assaulting me heavily. I fell to the ground as they continued a ground assault of stomps and kicks.. they were ruthless as they'd stomp on my head and then on to my nuts.. finally, knocking me unconscious by a driven knee in to my temple. I'd woken up in the same room, this time those two men had gone and a moderately tall man with black ray ban glasses perched upon his nose had appeared there.. I never knew i'd get a reaction so bad from a minor incident.. especially from a local airport but it was a foreign one after all. All he said was.. 'Act normal and leave your blade to us.' and then he left as I just dropped back down to the ground, later going to forget what he had announced.
