The best laid plans.....

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/04/02 The Excelsior Hotel…..not quite what Tyler Nelson is accustomed to, but a far cry from the uninhabitable dwellings he has had to suffer through the last month or so. Cockroach infested, Cracker Jack box sized hotel rooms with little to desire, other than the lumpy bed, after a long day of kicking ass. Even those rooms were an upgrade from the jail cell that the former CEO of PCW was confined to during his incarceration. What a low point in anyone’s life it must be to be imprisoned, but for a man the means, pride, and pedigree of Tyler Nelson? Almost deadly. Fortunately Tyler is a man of strong will and a hunger for revenge and retribution that must be quenched. It drove him to keep his sanity…..yes, he did indeed remain sane throughout that most disturbing time in his life. While he did things that were…shall we say, not of the norm…he knew at every step of the way what he was doing. Everyone thought he had lost his mind. Every move, every statement, every moment was calculated and predetermined to manipulate a few select people to do what he wanted. And they did. So now where does that leave us at the moment? Spring Fling is coming up this Sunday, the new owner of PCW is about to be announced, and Tyler Nelson will face the White Mexican in a ‘Loser Quits PCW’ match. Was this all part of Nelson’s plan of revenge? Did he manipulate Mexi into a match where Tyler could finally get rid of the man who cost him everything? One has to wonder just how far Tyler planned this thing out, wouldn’t you say? Back to the Excelsior Hotel, room 6051 to be exact. It’s a lavish hotel in its own right, classically decorated in white and black. The sun brightens the room as it gleams through the patio door. The black carpeted floor is plush and soft, running into bright white walls. Black leather furniture fills the living area, featuring a long, comfortable couch along with a chair and ottoman. They are arranged around a glass top table on top of a chrome frame. Track lighting runs along the ceiling to serve as the artificial sunlight at night. The soft sound of running water can be heard from the master bath as we focus on the door leading to the bedroom. The black door is slightly cracked, letting a sliver of light slip through. Soon the water is turned off and then the light suffers the same fate. The door is pulled open and out walks the greediest player in the game, Tyler Nelson. He wears a pair of expensive dress slacks, black, with matching leather shoes. The former CEO of PCW does not have a shirt on, and pats his face with a towel as he walks. His face sufficiently dry he takes the towel and flings it over his shoulder, allowing it to rest there. Tyler walks over to the dining area which sports a similar table to the one in the living area, glass top with chrome frame. Around the table are six chairs covered with black crushed velvet. Draped over the back of one of the chairs are a white button-up shirt and a deep red colored tie. Tyler picks up the tie and holds it up in front of him. He shrugs his shoulders and drops it on the table top. He then takes the towel and does the same with it before grabbing the shirt off the back of the chair. As he sticks his arms through the sleeves he stares out through the sliding glass doors at the beautiful landscape. “Pretty soon,” starts Tyler. “Things are going to be back to normal pretty soon. I’ll have PCW back under my control, I’ll be the most powerful man in wrestling once again, and I’ll be back to my mission of burying OSW.” A smirk sneaks across Nelson’s cleanly shaven face. “And I’ll even have the added bonus of ridding PCW of that fucking Mexican like he should have been a long time ago. Someone with that little talent has no place in PCW…..MY PCW!” Tyler finishes buttoning the shirt, then digs in his pants pocket and finds his cuff links. He fastens them on his shirt cuffs and then arranges them just right, popping each one when he is done. Next comes the tie, which Tyler scoops up off the table and wraps around his neck. He ties it perfectly, then pulls it tight to his neck. Then Nelson undoes his pants and tucks the shirt neatly inside them, fastening them back when he is done. Looking dapper as usual, Tyler smiles coyly to himself. “Sunday is going to be a great day…..the ULTIMATE day,” says Tyler confidently. “I can’t wait!” Suddenly the phone rings, causing Tyler to spin around on his heel. He paces casually over and snaps the receiver up off the base. “This is Nelson,” answers Tyler. We can hear a soothing female voice on the other end of the line. “Yes Mr. Nelson, this is Cherie down at the front desk. There is a Miss ummm…..,” she pulls the phone away from her head and covers the mouthpiece. Her next question is muffled somewhat, but still decipherable. “What was your name again, ma’am?” “Tell him Mona is here,” is the reply in a seductively sexy voice. Cherie returns to the line. “Yes, a Mona is he-“ “Yes I heard you. Send her right up,” orders Tyler. “Very well,” says Cherie as her voice tails away while Tyler hangs the phone back up. Tyler pauses and looks down at the phone with a puzzled look on his face. “Unexpected, to say the least.” Another shrug of the shoulders and Tyler heads back to master bedroom, pushing the door open and flicking on the lights. He disappears into the room and we can hear the closet door slide open for a few seconds, then slide shut. Tyler reappears in the doorway, pulling on the matching coat to his suit. He pulls the sleeves of his shirt down and straightens his tie one more time. As soon as he is done there is a knock on the door. Tyler looks at the door and then heads over to it, pulling it open. “Well hello there,” coos Tyler. On the other side of the door is Mona Moamar, ex-wife of the White Mexican. She stands there, seductively leaning in the doorway, wearing a hot red leather mini with matching halter top. Her mocha skin is speckled with body glitter and the aroma of sweet perfume permeates the air. “Hello yourself, cutie,” flirts Mona. “I have something for you.” A big smile spreads across Tyler’s face. “Oh you do, do you? Please come in,” says Tyler as he steps back. He pulls the door open and motions for Mona to come in, which she does with a sexy switch in her hips. She looks around the room with a glint of mischief in her eyes. “Nice place…..where’s the bedroom?” asks Mona as she softly sits down on the comfortable leather couch, a sly smirk on her face. Tyler looks down warily at the beautiful woman, “Is this a business or pleasure visit?” “Could be business. Could be pleasure. Could be both. Up to you,” purrs Mona cryptically then runs her tongue across her luscious lips. Tyler places his hand on his chin in contemplation. He looks Mona up and down, then glances toward the bedroom, then back at Mona. She shifts on the couch, crossing her legs the other way and giving Tyler a brief glimpse of her pantyless crotch. His eyes widen and he chuckles to himself. He snaps his arm out and looks at his platinum Rolex watch, then shakes his head in disappointment. “I wish we could have a little fun, but I have to be at a very important meeting in twenty minutes,” explains Tyler with a hint of sadness in his voice. “This will have to be relegated to a business meeting for now.” Mona shakes her head. “That’s too bad, Ty. I was looking forward to a little escapade with you.” Mona sighs heavily and then produces a micro disk that had been nestled in her cleavage. She tosses it over to Tyler who almost doesn’t see it as he was locked in to where she got if from. He fumbles it around and finally corrals the disk. “That should be helpful in your match with my ex-husband. A few….tidbits….about him that only I know.” Tyler holds the small disk up in front of his face and smiles big. “Thanks, doll. You’re a sweetheart.” Mona reaches her hand up and Tyler quickly grabs a hold of it, helping her up from the couch. She stands and the two of them are face to face, their body’s touching. She looks up at him seductively and smiles a sexy smile. Tyler looks down slyly and gives her a wink. “Some other time,” says Tyler. “This meeting absolutely cannot be missed.” “Awww….that’s too bad,” pouts Mona. She climbs up on her tip-toes and plants a peck on Tyler’s cheek, then heads toward the door. Tyler turns to watch and admires her, excuse the pun, assets. “You gonna be in town long?” calls Tyler. “Maybe….maybe not,” replies Mona without turning around. She gets to the door and waves at Tyler without looking, then leaves without shutting the door. Tyler shudders and then looks at the disk again. “As if I needed more help in taking out that worthless piece of shit, now it’s a guarantee that I will make Mexi quit PCW on Sunday,” sneers Tyler. He takes the disk and drops it in the breast pocket of his suit coat. Then he walks over and grabs a few things off of the dining room table before heading out of the room. He pulls the door shut as he leaves, allowing the scene to fade to black.
