Wrestling is a Game of Poker

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dec. 1992 “Hehe, Stormy Boy, eh?” Ericko cackles as he takes a swig of his lavish wine. “Does that guy ever learn?” The fiery in his eyes extorts confidence and swagger, just like a human that knows he cannot be trampled or defeated. The five cards in his hands contains life and death situation that only few men knows, or even comprehends. He was one of those few men. With these five cards came an authority beyond all intellectual capacity. Five cards that wasn’t brought in his hands by fate, but by skill. King of Hearts – a man in-loved to his own ego. A man bequeathed by a precious lady; a lady he dreamed of being with and finally is. Perfection is in his red heart. Love and perfection came together as Siamese twins like conquerors amidst the unforgiving storm. King of Spades – Blackened by its own strength. A man less popular than the King of Hearts but has the same heart to succeed. Underrated by the masses, but under extreme conditions would stryke at will! This King is capable, more proficient than the King of Hearts considers him to be. Eventually, The King of Spades would use his shovel to bury the King of Hearts alive and finally let humanity know that he is more than just a second-rated sovereign. King of Diamonds – the most gleaming of them all. An intellect of immaculate substance and unblemished understanding of the other kings. Shining with astuteness, he often appears erratic to other Kings. He acts by his will and with little regard to what the other monarch would reflect of him. That’s what makes him unpredictable. Always new, always iridescent. Queen of Hearts – the King of Hearts’ teddy bear. A beauty so magnificent, even the King of Diamonds is attracted to. She’s a wind soaring through the heavens like a white feather, free and beautiful. But with her beauty, came an impiety that the King of Diamonds detests. With that same exquisiteness, came a conniving, manipulative witch in disguise as the Queen of Hearts. Jack of Flowers – the son of farce and wit. The Queen of Hearts’ protector. With his sense of humor, came a flowery benevolence to the laypeople. Such an appealing character, that’s what he is. The holder of the rising star designation. The label, The King of Diamonds’ would soon acquire. “Three of a kind…” Ericko suggests to his adversary, still shining with poise and a cocky look in his eyeballs. “AGAIN!” the opponent snarls vehemently, obvious that he is not having a good night – or a good game. “Shit, man. Whenever I get two pairs, you always get something fucking higher.” As the opponent complains of his fate, Ericko’s hands grasps the money in the center of the table, slowly taking it to his vicinity with an arrogant smile on his face that assures him that he is indeed insuperable. “So exactly when will Stormy Boy come back to claim his pride once again?” he changes the topic as he counts the money he just won before him. “Does he really think he can beat me in this game?” The opponent strikes the table with his fist as he looks at the area of his table, sucked out of its money after seven hours of playing; making it even worst is listening to Ericko’s superciliousness. “You want to go for another set? Or are you finally going to summon your friend to save you?” Ericko hides his smirk and adds, “Or prolly borrow some more dough. Hehe.” “Screw that. I’m out’ta here.” Ericko’s antagonist stands up and continues gazing at Ericko and the enormous amount of money he just won. The frustration in his face is palpable. The man takes his eyes off the money and heads toward the door of the underground casino. “Hey, Jack,” Ericko beckons after him, perceptibly wanting to make a laughingstock out of someone. “Here you go,” he utters as he throws him a dollar coin. The defeated man leaves with disgust and essence for revenge in his face. On the other hand, Ericko sits there in satisfaction, knowing that he prevailed yet again; laughing, patiently waiting for the ideal andperfect storm to come and claim his throne as the King of Diamonds.
