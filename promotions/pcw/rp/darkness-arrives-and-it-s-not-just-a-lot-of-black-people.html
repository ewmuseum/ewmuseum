Darkness Arrives...And It's Not Just a Lot of Black People

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: By Will Otto The scene opens up on a large bonfire, the coals burning ferociously. As the camera pans outwards, we can see that there are a group of about 20 people gathered around the bonfire, though their intentions are unclear. However, now at the fire, we see none other than Rush Mason, standing alongside his girlfriend Melanie, whispering while a loud noise can be heard around them. Rush: Are you fuckin’ serious?! Melanie: What’re you whining about now? Rush: Sorry, Mel, I can’t talk. I’m praying to Satan and his evil goats of doom if you haven’t noticed. Melanie rolls her eyes as the noise around them becomes clearer. It seems to be words, but is obviously not in English. Close scrutiny reveals them to be some sort of eastern European tongue, sounding somewhat like Polish but definitely having Latin, French, and other languages tossed into the mix. Rush: Are you taking notes? Melanie looks at him, surprised and confused. Melanie: What? Rush: Taking notes? If we’re supposed to learn how to speak Satan, we’re going to need to take notes. Rush’s sarcastic tone has continued and now Melanie just sighs as the chanting stops and a figure, not very clear under the shadows, steps up towards the middle of the bonfire and the “congregation”, if it can be called that, and starts to speak to the entire group. His voice hisses out of his mouth like the air out of a plastic playground ball. Voice: Brothersss…we have come tonight to celebrate our newessst victory… His speech is frightening to hear, his pronunciation of S’s almost makes him sound like a snake. Rush, of course, being Rush, has to discuss this. Rush: Oh great, there’s a bunch of sheep here, dark sheep nonetheless and they follow a snake! Melanie: He’s not a snake, there are certainly people who just have speech disorders, there’s no need for you to be such a dick about it. Rush tries to apologize but Melanie just turns her back as Rush looks up at the sky and holds out his hands. Rush: What the hell did I do to you? There, of course, is no answer to this question, so Rush just turns back towards the large bonfire and the mysterious figure, listening to the strange speech. Voice: Tonight, our plansss to make ourssselvess known will be told to all and then we will make it known to those who opressss usss. Rush can now be seen looking up at the voice, eyebrows raised. Rush: Wow, these dark jobbers are like SO original! Melanie just looks at Rush with the same look of confusion and surprise that he looked at the mysterious figure with. Rush: Did that sound gay? We switch back to the strange tongue of the mysterious figure who speaks even louder, even though the crowd is still perfectly silent. Voice: Now we have new infiltratorsss amongssst usss tonight. New ssservantsss of our Lord, the Fallen Angel, he who shines brightest. Rush gulps and looks at the sky once again with one eye and puts his hands together in a praying motion and whispers to himself. Rush: Please don’t pick me, please don’t pick me, please don’t p- Voice: Here he isss, Russsh Masssson! Rush looks around, feigning a smile while looking at Melanie for some sort of help, but she just smiles maliciously back at him for his earlier actions. Rush walks up to the middle of the bonfire, and starts to twiddle his thumbs nervously, and finally he can see the mysterious figure in the light of the fire. Rush: Whoa, that dude looks like HE needs a major fixing-up. He thinks for a second, and then mutters to himself. Rush: Did that sound gay? The man’s appearance is a bit disturbing. He looks a bit like Corg, but there is no black paint, in fact there is no sign that he is alive, except his chest moving. His skin is perfectly white, with black spiked hair and black pupils in his eyes. Voice: Isss there a problem, friend? Rush quickly replies, very apparently afraid of the figure. Rush: Nope, no problem, definitely not a problem here. He turns out to the crowd, looking at all of the strange looking faces. He takes a deep breath and then starts to speak. Rush: Umm…brothers, I’ve come here tonight to experience the rush, no not me of course. Rush chuckles quietly to himself while the crowd is completely silent and he starts over. Rush: I came here to experience the rush of the dark side, the uhh… amazing feeling that worshipping the dark lord gives to one, and now I will take my knowledge back to PCW and ummm… fight for the dark lord, I guess. The last part of his sentence is cut out anyway as the entire crowd begins to clap, while Rush just smiles, still looking confused. The crowd begins to file out, but the figure stays behind to talk to Rush, as Melanie walk up the small hill towards the bonfire. Voice: As Damien, You have done well as hissss ssservant, he will be pleased. Rush: Lucifer? The figure just looks at him blankly as Rush quickly changes topic. Rush: So, is there anything else you need, I do need to get back to spreading the word of evil in PCW, ya know? The figure just nods as Rush begins to walk away, but the camera stays with the figure, who walks down towards the woods. He walks down towards another mysterious figure, but the other figure is larger, though only his lower half can be seen in the camera. Damien: He is ready, master, for your teachings. Mysterious Man: Is he? How can you be sure? Damien: He is vulnerable and nervous, afraid of whom he is. Mysterious Man: Well, soon he will not even be himself… The camera then shows a split screen of Rush and the darkness surrounding the mysterious figure as the scene fades out to black…
