Date: not entered (And once again we return to secret, hidden lair of THE Multiple Man, where grim and unfortunate experiments continue unabated.) Tmm: “Hi there! Hi there! Hi there!” (He sits down at the kitchen table, then gets up and sits back down, then gets up and sits back down.) Tmm: “I’m here to tell YOU, Violence Jack, that I’m gonna kick your ass tonight! I’m here to tell YOU, Kramer Wild Wing, that I’m gonna kick your ass tonight! I’m here to tell YOU, Mike Frampton, that I’m gonna kick your ass tonight!” (He sips from a conveniently placed glass of water… and sips again, and once more.) Tmm: “Not only am I going to kick your ass, Violence Jack, but I’m going to list EVERYTHING I’m going to do to your carcass in the ring! Not only am I going to kick your ass, Kramer Wild Wing, but I’m going to list EVERYTHING I’m going to do to your carcass in the ring! Not only am I going to kick your ass, Mike Frampton, but I’m going to list EVERYTHING I’m going to do to your carcass in the ring!” (He unfolds a LONG list, flap, flap, flap… the bottom hits the floor.) Tmm: “All right, hang on a sec.” (With much trouble, he refolds the list and lets it unwind again. Repeat.) Tmm: “There. Now…” (Keira walks into the frame. POW! Her haymaker lifts him clean from the chair and deposits him ass over appetite onto the floor, unconscious.) Keira: “Tell me when he wakes up, I’m gonna hit him twice more.” (Cut to exact same scene. Briefly, an 11 appears in the corner.) Tmm: “Hi there! I’m here to tell YOU…” (His mouth moves weirdly, but eerily silently.) Computerized voice: “Violence Jack, Kramer Wild Wing, Mike Frampton.” Tmm: “…that I’m gonna kick your ass tonight! You should know…” (Again, his mouth moves utterly out of synch with the words heard.) Computerized voice: “Gonzo, George Washington, Mr. Ed.” Tmm: “… that I’ve had my eye on you for a long time? The hell?” (A frown creases his features.) Tmm: “All right, who’s been messing with the voice track?!” (Cut to exact same scene, though now Tmm is sitting down and staring into space. Briefly, a 17 appears in the corner.) Tmm voiceover: “I had sat at my table, incredibly focused, thinking and sitting. Sitting… thinking. Being incredibly focused. With my table.” (Cut to a wrestling ring surrounded by a capacity crowd! Briefly, a 22 appears in the corner as Tmm runs down the ramp and grabs the microphone!) Tmm: “Yo! Listen up all you peons, ‘cause I’m only gonna say this once!” (Freeze frame and pull back to show Tmm and Steve looking very worriedly at the previous scene.) Steve: “Listen, not to hurt your feelings or anything, but these are REALLY bad. We’re talkin’ on par with a Drew Barrymore poetry reading here.” (Tmm practically buries his head in his hands.) Tmm: “I know! It’s horrible! I have all these cool ideas that turn into THIS crud!” (He hits play on the VCR, showing a low wall with sky behind it. A sock puppet walks into frame.) Puppet: “Hey kids! Can you say ‘Violence Jack, Kramer Wild Wing, and Mike Frampton can eat my goat scrotum?’ Let’s try! All together now! ‘Violence Jack, Kramer…’” (Mercifully, he pauses it again.) Tmm: “That ISN’T what we did! It isn’t even close! Where’s the elegant setting? The perfect posture? The crystalline pure dialogue?” (He picks up a random tape, labeled 32.) Tmm: “How could this happen?! These tapes are all direct copies of the original footage! We never filmed ANY of it!” Steve: “Well, SOMETHING had to happen to them! Tapes don’t just change their contents by themselves!” Tmm: “It’s sabotage, I tell you! They’re out to get me!” Steve: “’They’ who?” Tmm: “The syndicate, the group, the secret people that rule the world! Whatever they call themselves! They’re out to get me by secretly changing my wonderful promotionals into garbage that’s what! They’re out to take over everything!” Steve: “If they rule the world, why do they need an insidious plot to take over everything?” Tmm: “Shut up! I’ll bet you’re not even Steve are you, Xon-Far the impostor! Take off that cheap costume, for I’ve found you out!” (Tmm dives on Steve and tries his best to tear Steve’s face off.) Steve: “Leggo! Ow! Stop! Help!” (At that moment, Val walks into the room lugging a stout walled leaden box about the size and thickness of a largish dictionary. Using a pair of very long tongs, she tears away a videotape that has been velcroed to the underside of the table that all the strangely converted film footage has been stored. With quick, sure movements, she plops the tape inside the lead box and secures the lid. Only then does she take out a Dictaphone and click it on.) Val: “Frampton radiation experiment number one completed as of 20:42:15 P.M., June 25th, 2002. Initial effects seem to indicate that the close proximity of pre-recorded Frampton material to have a detrimental effect on nearby non-Frampton material. Full sensor logs follow…” (Click! She turns off the little audio recorder as the camera fades out.)
