Pulling Strings

24 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01.24.03 If music had never left his ears, Dante Jackson would not have a single thing to do with himself. Of course, there is wrestling in all of its glory and surprises to bring upon him. Although both of things treated him nicely, he didn’t portray it around the house, because he’s basically a two-leg pony, wrestling and music – one or the other. “I feel like shit…” The roar and ache in his body is nothing compared to what he feels on the outside. Yes, you can say he’s a nobody, a wannabe thug feeding off some image that’s been left off of the radar ever since blacks started targeting Primetime Championship Wrestling with an intertwining thought to get at the very top, even if it meant staying at the bottom. Even if it meant him staying back in RUSH before the transferred occur. He was on the brink of his sixth month in PCW, two titles won in that span, classic battles all of which have received their various accolades, but he still can remember what happened back in RUSH, August the 18th of two thousand and two, the aptly titled “Crossroads”. It wasn’t something played off from the Britney Spears movie that did meteorically at the box office, but more of a battle royal to see where the number one contender ship to the now almost defunct RUSH World Title happened to be. On that night, Chris Matthews finally defeated Cassius Deamont after Chris Sloboda left him at the ring, just like a wife at the altar. Tyler Valentine defeated D-Kay Calyx in an effective ladder match that some belief was the turning point in the now current World Champion’s career. And Brock Newbludd won the battle royal. Yet, in the mind of Dante Jackson, he knows he should have been rather higher than what he was at, a boring and yet one-sided feud with Andi the Magnificent in which Dante toyed, and completely dismantled him from limb to limb. That one moment where the battle royal happened, that was when Dante decided to leave. Elimination by Boda himself made sure that the deed was signed and a meeting with Tyler Nelson would be orchestrated the next morning. “You remember, don’t you?” he sits inside of the Casper, Wyoming Arena locker room, a few hours after Turmoil had ended, him viewing over what he should prepare for; Chet King on Sunday night in Japan. “Remember what?” The first voice happened to be from his wife, Tracy Jackson as she is referred to now as she leans back along with him, sweat glistening off his body as manly odors form and begin to swirl the room. “ ‘That’s it, I’m gone to PCW.’ ” She was paraphrasing the same monotone Dante used to tell Boda that he was leaving. Maybe because of personal direction, personal actions that he thinks should have been taken and brought upwards into Main Event scenes when Jeremy Howard was running the show. Howard had promised that Dante was to bloom, not from a former Xtreme Wrestling Association member and roster prize from the closure of Rising Star Wrestling, a product ran by Sean Williams, former owner of PURE. For a moment, this was beginning to ring true for Dante, him and Statuz Quo along with Dark Haze formed Playaz Inc. the rising star stable that still runs currently but with members Ali Khadafi and Duzza, but in his eyes, Dante feels that the group is on life support, everybody is doing a different thing. “You used to take care of those guys, Khadafi, Quo, you helped them through their times.” Tracy nudges him on the shoulder, trying to help him get back into the previous mindset he was in, but its not there. The memories flush back into his head like a pool of running water forming under a sink. “Where are they now?” “I have no clue,” he remarks dejected as he sniffs in phlegm with his nostrils. “Not a single clue. But whatever they’re doing, good for them.” Tracy sighs hard, more of a grunt than the usual purr that Dante’s used to from her. As he turned to face her, the attention of her was focused upon television, male supermodels she could have gone with that she had known back in high school. Dante raises his Adams Apple, “I see you…looking at them like there’s something missing from the heart.” “Nothing, you’re all I need, and that’s more of a fact than these men who are only glamorous because they say someone else.” “And I didn’t see someone else?” Dante quietly responds, but Tracy turns her head with sharpness towards the television once more. “You’re more into that TV that your own husband. Matter of fact, just where is Simmone anyway, I thought you kept her?” Tracy stifles, “Off playing with Rent A Hero, she’s like his second favorite wrestler other than her ‘da-dy’” He nods, before looking over the small piece of paper inscribed in front of him, ‘Your Next Move…’ A quiet rumble sits in his stomach as he turns his head over, trying to figure out what does his future old. “Just drive back home, I’ve got to think…” “What?” Gasping for breath, Tracy sits back a bit shaken up with the words said by her husband. “You, want me to go?” Dante nods, “What I said, what goes.” He picked himself up, no pain in sight as he squinted his eyes before walking away. For the first time, but not the last.
