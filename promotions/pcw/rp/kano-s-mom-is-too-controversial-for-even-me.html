Kano's Mom is too Controversial for even me!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Now ::The scene opens up in the streets of Dallas, Texas. It's a magical sunny day, the birds are chirping and the leaves on the trees are..... there. We see 'Controversial' Corey Williams walking down the streets, a little jig in his steps and a smile on his face.:: Corey: Ahh, what a magnificent day! The smell of pollution in the air, the sounds of babies crying..... god it feels good to be alive. ::Corey crosses the street at a crossing, but stops halfway through it. Walking by him are an old lady on his right and a distinguished gentlemen on his left. He grabs the distinguished gentlemen's top hat, and the old lady's walking stick and uses the two to do a little tap dancing jig in the middle of the road whilst the horns blare loudly. Once finished, he throws the hat and walking stick away, and runs to the other side of the street.:: Corey: Ain't it great to be controversial? ::Corey walks past a fat kid, whom he stops.:: Corey: Hey fat kid, I had sex with your Mom last night, and lemme tell ya, she was FINE. ::Corey walks forward once again, leaving the fat kid crying behind him. As he walks on, he sees a fine looking young lass, whom he stops too.:: Corey: .....Observe. ::Corey looks as if he's about to choke, but instead he spits a small live frog out of his hand. The woman claps with admiration.:: Corey: And now for my next trick.... ::Corey reaches down the girl's top and pulls out both her bra and panties in one fluent motion. The woman is shocked and tries to cover herself up. Corey moves on with her underwear in his hand. He again sees another young fine looking woman, this one is black. He stops her and hands her the underwear.:: Corey: Here, I bought these for you. ::The black woman doesn't know what to do, but before she can react Corey licks her face.:: Corey: Mmmm.... tastes like chocolate. You can be my sex SLAVE anytime. ::Corey walks on with a smile on his face, leaving the black woman shocked. Corey walks past a ladies clothing store, and he grabs a ladies dress off the rack as he walks on by, without even a second glance. He stops and puts the skirt on over his leather pants. He then walks over to a vent shaft on the pavement and stands over it doing his best impression of Marilyn Munroe. He bats his eyelids and tries to keep his skirt down, the flashes from camera flashing on his face. A round of applause comes from the observers and Corey takes off the skirt and backs away to a nearby door.:: Corey: Thankyou, and goodnight! ::Corey takes a bow and then goes through the door. Again taking with him the controversial atmosphere that he had seemed to lose after going out first in the March Madness Battle Royal. He went off the rails a little, his controversy replaced with frustration and anger, but now he seems back on track. The delight he takes in the displeasure of others has allowed him to claw his way back up, whilst others fall to the ground either sound, leaving a clear path in front of him. The camera swings around and we suddenly realise that Corey's coming through this door was no random decision, as much as his actions looked random, you can never know what is planned and what is not when it comes to Corey and controversy. And it seems that his direction at least, was certainly purposeful. For we are now in an expensive cafe, not that there is really such a thing in Dallas, Texas, but close enough. And there, in the corner of the cafe, flipping thumbing through a book, the Ace, the Messiah, the Celestial Cowboy, Chris Chase. Corey approaches the table at which Chase is sitting, Chris looks up just in time to see the approaching Corey.:: Chris: Corey! How's it going, son? Corey: Not bad, not too bad at all.... ::Somewhat of an understatement, for these two men are on the lips of every sane man and woman in the globe. Chris returns to his book. Corey observes him as he readjusts his reading glasses.:: Corey: Uh, Chris? ::Chris looks back up to Corey, looking rather smart in those glasses of his.:: Chris: Yes? Corey: You don't wear glasses. ::As if suddenly realising this Chris grabs the glasses off his face and throws them across the room and to the floor, again without a second thought.:: Chris: Yeh, I know, but I thought they'd make me look more intelligent than I already I, it's hard to have the looks of a sex god AND have people take your intelligence seriously y'know. Corey: Uh hunh, but that's just one of the problems guys like us have to deal with. And the book? ::Chris turns the book over to inspect its cover.:: Chris: Something about Napoleon, can't see why someone would write a book about him though. If that's all it takes to get a book written about you, then someone should be writing my book right this moment, I've done thrice the things this loser did. Hell, he couldn't even win a damn war! Corey: What do you expect Chris, he's French. ::Corey sits down and picks up a menu off of the table.:: Chris: Say, did you happen to catch Boda's last promo. ::Corey puts down the menu and nods, a smirk on his face.:: Chris: Like husband, like wife, eh? Boda wants to expose my oozing innards, whilst the last person to make my oozing innards CUM out was...... Stacey Sloboda! ::Corey stifles his laughter, after all, it's not nice to make fun of Boda at his expense...... although it's very fun.:: Corey: Yeh, Boda wants to know why the fans turned against him, well let me put it this way Boda, there's only so many clotheslines the average fan can take in one match. Did you ever think of trying to put a dropkick or a superkick or a splash or SOMETHING into your repertoire? Oh I forgot, that would take effort, and Chris SLOBoda doesn't like to make an effort. You think it's hard to get the fans to like you? What did you ever do in the first place? Beat up a few jobbers, that's all. You think it's hard to get the fans to hate you? But what have you done so far, beat up some Mexican guy that the fans love coz he's an underdog, but what they don't realise is that there's a REASON he's the underdog, because quite frankly he's not very good. No, it's not hard to be cheered by the fans. No, it's not hard to be booed by the fans. Chris: But being cheered AND booed, loved AND hated by the fans, now THAT'S hard! Corey: And THAT'S controversial! ::A waitress comes over with a notepad and pen.:: Waitress: What can I get you. Chris: I'll have a latte. ::The waitress writes it down and then looks up and nods to Corey. Once again, Corey can't help but notice that she's black.:: Waitress: And you? Corey: I like my coffee the same way I like my slaves, straight... black. ::The black waitress lets it go, after all, she needs this job, it's not like it's been raining jobs for black people since slavery was abolished, especially not down here in Dallas.:: Chris: So you ready for Kano on Friday? Corey: God I love making jobbers tap, it's just so satisfying, that moment when they beg for their own well-being and the moment after that where the begin to think you're not gonna let go....... and maybe you don't. ::A sick smile comes across Corey's lips.:: Chris: Well, Kano has got one advantage over you. ::Suddenly Corey is concerned, after all, Kano DID get an upset victory over Nelson not too long ago.:: Corey: What's that? Chris: Well, he's so old that, as controversial as you are, not even YOU would dare say that you f*cked his Momma. ::A sly smile creeps onto Chris' face, the look on Corey's face is priceless. He shudders in disgust, for Kano's Mom is too controversial for even him. Scene fades out.::
