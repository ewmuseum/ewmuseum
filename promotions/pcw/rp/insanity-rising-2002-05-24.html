Insanity Rising

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-24-02 Introduction: After what seemed like a normal beginning, Trigger's former enemy and co-trainer of the WITs has taken the Warriors In Training, and the Infinite Warrior had an idea where. In haste to reach his destination, the Infinite Warrior had tracked down a cab and was now cruising down the roads at top speed. PCW headquarters were downtown, he had heard, and it was important that he get there quickly. Minion and the WITs could already be gone. Sitting in the ripped leather seats, Trigger forced himself to stop worrying and get a handle on the situation. He thought back to last night… such a wonderful night. Everything was perfect; everyone was happy. The WITs were prepared for a grand celebration, Minion and Trigger seemed to have finally set aside their differences, and it didn't seem like much could change that. But there was one thing that the Infinite Warrior knew could change anything… betrayal. A quick conclusion, yes, but Trigger had always been one to think on his feet. Early in the morning, The Insane One Lance Minion asked him to go to the store and pick up several items. The Infinite Warrior accepted, but was flustered when he returned to the abandoned shop only to see that Minion and the WITs had disappeared. Knowing Minion and his evil ways, Trigger had determined that The Insane One must be up to something, and speedily thought up what. In the taxi, the Infinite Warrior buried his face in his hands. Of course he knew what Minion was up to… if he had just looked a little closer, he would have known all along. Why would an independent soul like The Insane One want to be a 'back-up' trainer for the WITs? No, that was not the case. All along the clues had been there, but Trigger had been ignoring them. The explanation was unmistakable: Minion intended to steal the WITs away! Trigger slunk back in his seat, mentally yelling at the driver to speed up. They were going to the PCW local headquarters. Why again? Because that's where the WITs wanted to go. They wanted to become warriors; they wanted to become Primetime Championship wrestlers. If the Infinite Warrior could see this, then it was a given that Minion could, and if The Insane One could see it, then he would use it to his advantage. All at once, the cab pulled up along the sidewalk, and upon looking out the window Trigger determined that they had arrived. He quickly paid the taxi driver, and hurried out the door. There it was, the PCW local headquarters. He headed right up to the door, ignoring a homeless man begging for change, and pushed the handle. It was a small building containing only one floor… fairly new with only slight graffiti defacing the paint. Immediately inside, however, it was chaos. About five police officers were in the front room, sternly writing down notes in their violation booklets, and nearly a dozen workers crowded around with nervous expressions on their faces. Just then, a stretcher came hurried by, held by two medical workers. Atop it lay a middle-aged man with a large bandage wrapped around his head. He was unconscious. Trigger was confused. What catastrophic events were happening to cause all of this? He that if Minion and the WITs were here, they had not left yet. Suddenly, the Infinite Warrior got a sick thought. He spotted a receptionist standing around, and practically ran up to her. 'Excuse me,' he started, 'what's going on here?' She turned to him with a frightened look on her delicate face. 'Four men came in here… they wanted to join PCW. When we told them that applications were closed, one of them went crazy, destroying thousands of dollars worth of equipment, and injuring several of my co-workers. Three of the men seemed afraid, though, like they didn't know what was going on. I don't think it was a robbery… they didn't take any money. It just seemed like the one man, the ring leader… it seemed like he was ver angry!' She continued on, but Trigger wasn't listening. Four men had entered the building to fill out applications... One man had attacked… Who wanted to desperately get into PCW? Well, the WITs did. But who would be capable of attacking an office room with innocent people? Minion! Trigger knew he had to do something. This in part was his responsibility. After all, it had been he who introduced the WITs to the terrible evil that was Lance Minion. After all, it had been he who had introduced Minion to PCW. After all… Lance Minion was The Insane One, and Trigger knew this perfectly well. How could he let such a menace upon society? He had to find Minion and the WITs immediately. He had to figure out what to do that would make things right again. But this time he wasn't going to spare anyone. Minion's insanity had hit, and now it was time for extreme measures. The Infinite Warrior turned and sprung out the door of the building. He kept running, right into the street, unaware of the high-speed car coming right at him, unable to stop. Trigger turned, just in time, but there was no way to escape…
