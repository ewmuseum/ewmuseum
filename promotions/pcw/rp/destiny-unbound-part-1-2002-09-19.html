Destiny Unbound (part 1)

19 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th September 2002 Jim and Ben nestle themselves back in their Wisconsin hotel room after their dominating victory over the reigning PCW World Tag-Team Champions on Turmoil and Jim moves over to the counter and happily pulls a fax from the squeaking, whirring fax machine still positioned on the counter in the tan and yellow kitchen area. Jim looks down at the paper and quickly a bitter frown crosses his face as he crumples up the fax and throws it at his Apache half-brother. Ben drops down into the couch and puts on the tube which is still showing Turmoil highlights and promos for the upcoming Friday Fusion. Jim: Did you see this crap? We’re not booked on the next Turmoil! We just destroyed the reigning champions in less than 2 minutes and now we’re not even booked?! Ben unfolds the now crinkly piece of paper and gives the list a quick scan. The enjoyment and happiness is not yet ready to leave the high-flying member of Redemption Song and as he flattens out the paper a wide smile crosses his distorted mouth. Ben: Man, you always focus on the negative. That’s why every woman you get involved with leaves. It’s a small miracle that I can even put up with you with all your bitching all the time. ‘Where’s Misty? Why would she leave us? Blah, blah…’ Jim: Ugh… Ben: Instead of complaining, just think – we pulverized the PCW World Tag-Team Champions!!! In our second match they couldn’t last two minutes with us! We now have an unbeaten steak running close to 3 months between CHZ and PCW! Jim (interrupting): Okay hotshot, you’ve made your point. Ben: PWA deserve the shot, you said so yourself before we went into Turmoil. Jim (annoyed): I know what I said but you obviously didn’t hear me. I said I get what you’re saying. Ben: Good. Then stop whining like a girl and lets figure out what our next step is. Jim: Well, the next step is finding our damn new valet. Ben: Actually, the contest will continue in a about a half hour. We’ve got a girl coming up here tonight, I made the arrangements from my mobile phone when we were getting a ride back to the hotel. Jim: Nice. What’s the deal with this one? I’ve heard a few more guys have applied. Ben (grinning slyly): Oh, this girl is very much a woman. Her name is Destiny and she responded to an add I placed when we first got to Wisconsin for a stripper needed for possible acting opportunities! Jim: Thank friggin’ God. After the Mr. Powerful fiasco, I don’t think I could have dealt with trying to explain why men just don’t make good valet material all over again. Ben: Well, don’t worry about that either; we’ve got a couple more guys lined up to look at but not just yet. Jim sighs and walks over to the couch and slumps down with a tall cool glass of refreshing root beer. Ben: You shouldn’t drink that stuff you know. It’ll rot your teeth. Plus you haven’t had anything to eat since before the match it might upset your stomach. Jim (annoyed again): Jesus, who the fuck are you Mr. God Damn Rogers? Ben (feigning hurt feelings): Awww… I’m only trying to look out for you bro. Jim shakes his head and runs his hands through his extremely short dirty blonde hair, giving his own scalp a massage more than doing anything else. Jim: Yo, tell that damn hooker to bring us a friggin’ pizza. If we’re not booked for a week I damn sure am going to eat some pizza and look at hot hookers. Ben: Well Jim, I’m not sure how ‘hot’ she’s going to be exactly… Jim (narrowing his eyes): What the hell is that supposed to mean? Ben: Well, the girl is from Wisconsin after all. Jim (relieved): Well hell, I thought you were going to say that you called some hooker service that specializes in fat chicks or transvestites or something nuts like that! Ben gets up and puts his hands on his hips and smiles widely. Ben: Man you are a crazy man. A little paranoid, eh? Not everyone’s out to get you bitch! Now let me give Destiny a call. Maybe if she’s not too close to the hotel she can pick us up a pizza. Jim (happy like a schoolboy): Hells yeah! Ben walks off to the kitchen area to get away from the noise of the television and Jim’s thoughts immediately take a turn back to the black as a picture of Totally Tubular Tommy is brought up on the screen and a picture of Redemption Song’s former valet Misty is brought up as well. In between the two is a big red heart with a question mark inside of it. Jim’s face immediately turns red as he presses the mute button on the television’s remote control. And before he realizes what he’s doing, pain shoots up though Jim’s right hand as the glass from which he was drinking his root beer shatters in his hand, sending a nice shard deeply into the palm of his tanned, leathery hand. Jim: GOD DAMMIT!!! Ben: Shhhhh… I’ve got Destiny here! She’s going to bring up a pizza but she said only if we eat it off her. Jim: Do you see what happened here? Jim shrugs his shoulders and turns his right palm out for Ben to see as blood drips quickly from his hand onto the hotel carpet. Ben quickly finishes up his conversation with Destiny and hangs up the phone. He then motions for Jim to come over to the sink. Ben: Wash this out as soon as possible. Is there any glass lodged in there? Jim: No dickhead, it’s bleeding because I’ve received the stigmata. Ben: You really have to calm down man… Jim (thick western drawl): Calm down? Calm down? Misty’s running around with a loser surf bum that loves retards and I’m just supposed to say that’s okay? Ben (evilly): I didn’t say that it was okay. I said for you to calm down. Jim: Alright man, I’ll bite, what do you have in mind? Ben: Isn’t Cowabunga Connection going to be at Fusion? Jim: Yes. Ben: Let’s increase our reputation as career enders. It’ll be… Jim (interrupting): Sweet #5, yes I know! Ben: Okay then, its settled. Jim smiles as he finally puts his still-bleeding hand under the kitchen faucet and begins to rinse it out. After the hand is cleared of blood, Jim pulls the large piece of glass from his pulsing wound and blood beings to flow again, much more substantially than before. Ben goes to the bathroom and comes back with a large amount of gauze and an athletic bandage. Ben: Wrap that shit up, Destiny should be here any second. Let’s ask her what we should do to Cowabunga Connection! Jim: If that slut says suck their dicks I ain’t doin’ it. Ben: Funny. Jim: Truth. Suddenly there is a knock on the hotel door. Ben goes up to the door and looks out the peephole. Ben: Holy crap! Destiny’s here already and she’s got a pizza! She doesn’t look half bad either! Jim: That was fast as hell! Ben: I guess you have to suspend your reality a little bit, man. Jim: Yeah, whatever just get her ass in here, I’m starvin’! Ben grins, unlocks the lock and opens the door. (to be continued…)
