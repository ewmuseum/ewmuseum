Cleansing of the sins

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-29-02 OOC: I'm pulling a Stormbringer *wink wink* Listening guide: Disturbed 'Glass Shatters' Enjoy. Cleansing of the sins: His eyes were glassy and his heart was beating at a pace faster than usual. His vision had went from clear to blurry like on a foggy night. Inside his head it felt as if a thousand tiny men were pounding away with sledge hammers but where was he? He lifted one of his massive arms to wipe away the blur, slowly he wiped and blotches began to take shape. A large table sat before him with old men sitting in various wooden chairs which seemed to be three hundred pounds and had carvings of old in them. The table was heavy, metal, and a light was shining down hitting him square in the eyes which made it even harder to see. He scanned a little more finding that his Celestial Star Title was laying upon the table surrounded by some blue, glowing aura. He was confused and upon finally gathering all his preverbal 'marbles' he reached out to grab the title. Pain flashed through his nerves inside the tip of his finger and sent a thrill to his brain which shot down through his spine. His body shivered with the sensation of pain as a loud groan moved through his cracked lips. 'Do you know why you are here?' An old voice with many years behind it boomed. 'Huh?' He was caught off guard thinking this was just an illusion. He thought maybe he had stumbled and bumped his head, maybe he had a car accident, maybe he was even dead but he didn't think this was real. He had even pinched himself a few times to make sure that it wasn't a all to real dream. Upon further inspection he knew this.... Was no dream. 'I will repeat myself no more. We bring you here in light of your Celestial Title,' the old man boomed once more, he appeared to be the 'leader' with his gray beard and wise expressions. 'What about it?' Sylo snarled in his usual snappy voice but with a groggy twist due to him not being fully composed yet. 'It is in great danger of being lost! You know that you are the only one fit to hold the Celestial title, you are the only one that can bring pride to it but yet you flaunt it around like it's a toy!' The elder's voice was none to happy. 'SILENCE!' another elder boomed. His eyes shifted like a radar trying to pick up enemy spy planes. The oldest elder stood with his silken silver hair and golden threaded robe mixed with white. His beard seemed to go on for ever running almost to his knee caps as he leaned on an old wooden cane. 'Your actions bring shame to your family's history. Your a warrior Sylo not an evil being. You slay the sinners but lately you have turned into nothing more than a henchman and for that you must pay for YOUR sins,' The elder boomed. 'I only did what I had to do to survive! Can you not respect that? Now if you excuse me I have a match against John Crichton Sunday for MY Celestial Star title that I WON ALONE! Who are you to tell me what I must do or what I must not do? Where were YOU when I was close to death all them times?' 'ENOUGH!' Boomed the older elder. 'You WILL complete our training course to clean you of your sins. If you do not pass you will perish it is your choice,' 'You can't do this to me!' Sylo growled slamming his fist on the metal table which echoed through time. And with that he was left in darkness..... It had seemed like a year at least. What was this place? Did time actually have a meaning in this hell. A crimson sky provided a blanket for the barren waste land which surrounded every possible aspect. Sylo's clothes were ripped in various locations, he was battered, bloody, and it seemed close to death. All of this was to pay for his sins, to cleans himself of them. He had seen horrors that the human imagination could never imagine. Blood shed and war was all that surrounded him like a massive colas of destruction and despair. It was something that not even a Hollywood producer could think up. He was on his knees, his most vulnerable point since arriving to this hell. Blood stained his Ice blue hair along with his clothes which resembled dirty wash rags. The eyes that had made him famous still held their icy glow but dirt red blood covered near. In front of him, right as he felt the life escaping his bosom the image of Jade appeared. He felt it was a mirage as his soul burned to die but once he felt her soft touch he knew she was there. 'Your sins...' Her soft voice felt like silk on his ears. 'Ye....yes have I not paid?' His voice was scruffy and full of pain. 'Your final task is upon you. You must kill your own sin,' She touched his face cuddling his cheek whispering her silk words caressing his ears. 'I am near death. I do not fell as if I can take anymore for I am weary and I shall just die. My sins were great but death seems greater,' 'NO! Do you hear me you can do this. Your elders had to test you, they had to show you what you did was wrong. Look deep inside you hold a power which wasn't given to you by some 'celestial' it was given to you by birth, Unleash it and you shall know the true purpose of your life,' Her words were sharp, stabbing his ear drums with the truth. 'But I don't understand!' He cried out. 'You will...' And with that she was gone. He was left alone in this hell once more with only questions and fear on his mind. He stood up but the ground began to shake with a force of god. His knees trembled and he surly thought this would be the end. He could feel death breathing down his neck raising the small hairs on the back of his neck making them stand in attention and all he could do was close his eyes and bare it. He reopened his eyes. Standing before him was himself. The duplicate snarled and waved a large fist; this was his sin. Sylo's eyes turned normal as the duplicate charged forward smashing into Sylo's sternum crushing him across the desolate plain. Blood dripped from his mouth as he spit a bit to the side as he clutched his now probably broken ribs. 'So your my sin?' The duplicate only snarled. Deep down inside he began to feel a power drawing from a well deep in his soul. It flowed into his veins giving him power, expanding his muscles and turning his eyes back to their solid Ice Blue color. He snarled back and leaped back up to his feet. 'Now let's dance,' Sylo snarled charging toward his own sin. The two began to throw punches and kicks which they both blocked. They knew each others every move but the duplicate through a new one and nails Sylo in the chest sending him sprawling back only for him to leap back up and take a fighting position. He stuck his hand out telling the duplicate to come on. Quickly it charged as Sylo scanned his surrounding with laser precision. A large, jagged bolder laid behind him and as the duplicate leaped with a roar which would send fear down any normal mans spine Sylo ducked. He grabbed the duplicate by the leg and drove him face first into the rock. Blood splattered the waste land mixing in quickly like camouflage. He hovered over his now dying sin. And smirked down but quickly turning it into a snarl. 'Step up because your the next one in line to be killed,' His voice boomed. And with that he drove his large black boot into the skull of the duplicate splattering him like a ripe melon. His vision was blurry once more but this time his eyes quickly adjusted to find the elders sitting with smiles upon their time ridden faces. A little skittish he scanned the room and standing by one of the Elders was Jade. Sylo smiled and then looked up to the oldest Elder who held the Celestial Star title over his shoulder. 'Very good, there for a while I thought you weren't going to make it,' The other elders clapped as the head one said this. 'Never doubt me....for it will lead to your downfall,' He snarled but quickly smirked. 'I believe this is yours' The oldest elder laid the Celestial Star Title down on the table and slid it over to Sylo who looked down then back up at him throwing the title on his massive shoulder. A portal opened behind Sylo as he turned to walk out. Jade stepped beside him looking at him with her beautiful green eyes. He took a few massive steps forward before a voice cut through the air striking his ears with an sound he thought he would never hear. 'Sylo,' It was the voice of the oldest elder. 'Yes?' Sylo turned asking. 'Kick Crichton's ass!' A smirk moved over his old lips. Sylo smirked and looked at the other elders who clapped and gave the thumbs up. Sylo turned back to look at Jade and she nodded with a beautiful smile. 'Crichton will step up cause he's the next in line for me to kill,' and with that he disappeared into the portal.....for now.
