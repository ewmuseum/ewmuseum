No, play time is over. Now it's time to get serious. FF#3

13 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-13-03 A black E 55 AMG Mercedes-Benz creeps along a road. The familiar license plate STRYKER comes into focus. The lights turn on as the darkness begins to overtake the light of day. It comes to a stop in front of the popular kid’s restaurant Chucky-E-Cheese. The passenger’s side door opens as Jaslene steps out into the dusk. She’s wearing a pair of jeans with a ’Got Stryker?’ shirt. She takes in a deep breath, ”Ahhh….what a beautiful evening don’t you agree Adam?” ”Yeah sure,” Adam replies still sitting in the car, ”Do I really have to go through with this?” Jaslene turns back around and looks into the car, ”Of course,” she says with a sly grin on her face, ”I mean after all you were hot to trot at Rohan’s Rassilin’ World, why should Chucky-E-Cheese be any different?” ”Jas look around,” he answers, ”My car is probably worth more than this dump. Don’t get me wrong Rohan was a cool guy and all but this is…well this is a Chucky-E-Cheese for Christ’s sake! It’s embarrassing!” ”Look I don’t care what it is! You agreed to do this so come on let’s go already!” she snaps back at him. ”But you agre-“ she slams the door on him, ”Oh Goddamit! This night is going to suck, I can already tell.” Inside the restaurant is just a mess of bodies, young and old, big and small with just about everything you’d expect to find in a place like this. Kids running around screaming as parents chase them, one kids makes a Bee-Line for garbage can just as he is about to throw up. The staff looks much like you’d expected, young, tired and immensely overworked. A couple of guys busing tables look up just as Jaslene walks in. ”Dude!” one exclaims to the other. ”She’s freaking hot as hell!” the other guy answers back, ”You don’t think she’s a M.I.L.F. do you? I don’t see any kids.” ”Are you stupid?” he answers, ”Come on Brad she barely looks a day over twenty six! Use your brain before you speak.” Brad looks again, ”Yeah you’re right Sean. Good thing she didn’t hear me say that huh?” Both of them laugh to themselves before moving along to the next couple of tables. Sean takes a better, longer look at Jaslene before realizing something. ”Hey wait a minute,” Sean says thinking still, ”I think I know her from somewhere…” ”FOR REAL?!?!?!” is the excited reply from Brad, ”Dude you’ve got to introduce me to her so I can work the magic!” Sean flicks his dish towel at Brad, ”Not like that you idiot! And besides you have no magic to work!” ”It’s sad because it’s true.” Brad sighs. ”If anyone is going to work some magic on this babe it’s going to be me! I know who that is now! It’s Jaslene Myst from the DWO!” Sean says confidently, ”Dude could you imagine how could it would be to hook up with Jaslene Myst? Everyone at school would know you’re name, You’d be the coolest kid in school!” Brad says with enthusiasm. ”You’re damn right I would be,” Sean says thinking things over in his head, ”I’m going for it!” Sean stands up and wipes his clothes clean. He starts walking toward Jaslene running his hand through his hair as he goes. Just as he is about to approach her Adam comes in through the door. He walks up behind Jaslene and gives her a kiss on the cheek. Sean quickly turns around and heads right back toward Brad. He picks up his stuff and looks to Brad. ”Yikes! It’s Stryker!” he says before heading toward an employee only door. ”Eep.” Brad says as he follows Sean. ”Can I help you?” a young woman’s voice says from behind. They turn to see a young blue haired teenage girl standing there. She’s wearing a Chuck-E-Cheese uniform. ”Oh uh yeah,” Adam sputters in a bit of a daze, ”I’m Adam Stryker. I’m supposed to be doing a public appearance tonight.” The girl seems unimpressed, ”Yeah, yeah whatever…which party are you here for?” Adam looks confused and a bit shocked. Jaslene quickly cuts in, ”He’s here for the Taylor party.” Adam looks at Jaslene, she just smiles back at him. ”Ok…that party starts in about twenty minutes,” she says adding, ”Changing rooms and whatnot are in the back there.” She points to a darkened hallway. ”Whoa! Whoa! Hold on…TIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIME OUT! Changing room? What do I need to change for?” he asks. ”You don’t wear makeup? Come to think of it I don’t see a bag of props or costumes with you either. What kind of clown are you anyway?” the girl calmly responds. Adam’s eyes bug out of his head as he exclaims, ”Clown!? What are you talking about? I’m not a clown!” ”Well only when AJ isn’t around.” Jaslene says snickering. ”Not funny and certainly not helping Jas.” he says to her. ”Aren’t you from the PCW though?” the girl asks. ”Yeah,” he responds, ”Primetime Championship Wrestling.” ”Huh? Primetime Championship Wrestling? What’s that? I was talking about the Priority Clown Workers. They are the best!” the girl says with a smile. Jaslene’s snickers turn into a full blown laughter as she bends over forward holding her ribs. ”AH! Priority…haha…Clown…hehe…Workers!” she exasperates, ”She-she thinks you’re a clown! How funny is that?” ”It’s not Jas!” Adam says with some anger. He pushes his way past the young girl and to the outside. He steps out into the cool night and paces around for a bit. Jaslene finally comes out of the building, though still showing signs of her amusement. ”Adam what’s wrong?” she asks coming to his side. ”This is wrong Jas. I’m no clown,” he answers looking down to her, ”I should have known something was wrong. This is an unusual place for a public appearance to begin with.” ”Yeah but come on Adam, it’s still the right thing to do.” she says putting her arms around him. Adam looks down into her eyes and contemplates his choices. He could either be a cold hearted bastard and leave or give one kid the thrill of his life. After a couple of moments, a smile comes to his face. ”Come on, let’s go back in.” he says pulling Jaslene by her hand. A FEW HOURS LATER. Adam is standing at an arcade game. A blue plastic gun is in his hands. A small group of kids stand around him, the oldest being no more than twelve. All look at him with awe in their eyes. ”Heh watch this.” he says as he points the gun toward the game. The kids ‘oooh’ and ‘ahhh’ as he fires the gun in rapid succession. He steps down on a pedal at the base of the game. Jaslene comes walking over with a towel in hand. She’s now wearing a Chucky-E-Cheese shirt and a pair of shorts. She wraps the towel around her wet hair and rubs it as she watches him play the game. ”Oh God no…Time Crisis Two! We’re never going to get out of here now…” she thinks to herself. Adam steps away from the machines and thrusts his arms up into the air. ”Yes! Victory is mine!” he exclaims. He turns to see Jaslene just glaring at him. ”Uh-oh.” he says. ”You’re damn right ‘uh-oh’!” she says to him in a fierce tone. The parents of the birthday boy come walking over. The dad shakes Adam’s hand as the mother hugs Jaslene. ”Thank you both so much for taking the time to be here tonight.” the father says. ”Joseph is never going to forget this birthday. Having Adam Stryker and Jaslene Myst showing up just made him so happy!” the mother says before hugging Adam and giving him a peck on the cheek. ”Really it was my pleasure. I enjoyed myself tonight more than I thought I would to be honest with you.” Adam says with a huge smile. The two parents say one last ‘thank you’ before leaving. Adam’s huge smile quickly disappears as he once again sees Jaslene glaring at him. ”What? What did I do?” he says trying to look and act innocent. Before Jaslene can answer the young girl from before comes walking over with a bag in her hand. ”Here you go Miss Myst. You’re clothes probably won’t be stained.” she says handing the bag to Jaslene. ”Thanks Melissa!” Jaslene smiles as Melissa starts to walk away. When she’s gone the look of anger quickly returns to Jaslene’s face. ”What gave you the thought to throw pies at me for a good two hours?!?!?!” she yells. ”I needed to entertain the kids somehow and you know how much kids love to see people get in with pies! Besides I figured since you got me into this mess, you’d at least help me get out it.” Adam says trying not to laugh. Jaslene stomps her foot into the ground. ”Oh come on Jas!” Adam says, ”You can’t be all that upset, all the kids had a great time.” ”Yeah because you let them throw pies at me too!” she fires back. ”Well I needed to get the kids involved somehow! Don’t be such a spoilsport Jas. You would have done the same thing to me.” Adam says confidently. Jaslene lightens up a bit, ”Yeah probably…” ”See? There you go. Now come on, are you ready to go home?” Adam asks. ”Yeah let’s go.” she answers. ”Well here’s some bus money because there is no way I’m letting you sit in my Mercedes with pie all over you.” Adam says cracking up. She lets out a yell before chasing him out of the building. Adam is laughing the whole way out. He stops just outside and waits as Jaslene comes bursting through the door. He picks her up and aqueezes. ”I was only playing Jas!” he says through laughter. Adam places her back on the ground and takes the bag from her. They head toward the car as Adam pulls out his keys and disarms the alarm unlocking the doors. ”You were in a pretty good mood tonight, does this mean you aren’t going to be so serious about the PCW anymore?” she asks as they come to the car. Adam opens the door, ”No, play time is over. Now it’s time to get serious.” He gets into the car and turns it on. The moment Jaslene shuts her door he takes off leaving nothing but dust.
