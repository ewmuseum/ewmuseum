An Old Friend

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/02/02 The news couldn’t be true could it? No way one of my best friends in the world could be dead. Could he? Sometimes when we hear things, we tend to put them away in disbelief. That is what I have done for the last week. Certain that reports were mistaken, some how misprinted. But the fact remains that they were correct; and my dear friend is now lost to me forever. The rain falls fast and hard like it may never end. The night is gray and very dreary because of the bad weather. Outside in the cold wind and pouring rain stands Steve Johnson. He is standing next to a grave. The dirt on top still looks fairly fresh despite the rain making it all muddy. He stands there with his head down looking toward the grave and his hands inside the pockets of his large black coat. His long black hair is left down tonight as he stands there with no emotion showing on his face. It is as if he is in a state of shock. As he kneels down and sits on his knees in the front of the grave the camera catches its first glimpse of the writing on the grave. “Stewart Hix 1972-2002”. The grave of the most hated yet respected wrestler of the modern era. Johnson continues to set there in silence for what seems like ten or fifteen minutes. His head still down, reflecting the pain he is feeling inside that he will not show on the outside. Finally he looks up and begins to speak. Font color=blue>Sure was a hell of a run wasn’t it my friend? Too bad I wasn’t there for the end of it like I should have been. Should have been on that plane with you that day. But oh no, stupid me I decided that not letting down the “fans” and doing one more autograph session was more important. I should have listened to you when you always told me to never let the fans get in your head. I am sorry, but I failed to listen closely enough and now I have let “them”, cause me to lose my friend. (Johnson sets back onto his rear in the mud. He doesn’t even seem to care.) It has been a great couple of years though. I remember the first time Michael introduced me to you. You were your typical arrogant and smart-ass self. I hated you when I first met you. Thought you were everything that was wrong in the world; personified. (Johnson chuckles a bit) But your ring knowledge and great technical skill showed me something totally different. You weren't arrogant and cocky. Well maybe you were but you have every good reason to be. The first time I actually saw you wrestle, I knew right then that you were what I wanted to be in the ring. I began to respect your talent and skill and therefore you. Even though I haven’t always agreed with everything you said or did with your life outside of the ring; I could never disagree with your success in the ring. Stew you are what every good technical wrestler wants to emulate. You had all the right ingredients it took to be not only a great wrestler, but also maybe the best technical wrestler ever. I see now what I must do clearly. I have already begun forsaking the ones who I never should have been listening to in the first place. The turn coat, fickle fans that will forget you the moment you walk out of the doors. But they will not ever forget you Stew. No they will always remember how much they hated you. And that is what I will strive for as well. To be the greatest wrestler of all time, and to make it a personal mission to have the fans despise me and everything I do. No Stew I know right now there are fans celebrating the fact you met your fate. Let them celebrate to their hearts content. This Sunday they will celebrate no longer. With every chance I get I will beat the crap out of all that oppose me and do everything possible to destroy what the fans love best. Their hero’s who have won against all odds and pressures. Now those men have more problems than they ever could have dreamed of. I have always been focused in my career. Always strived to be the very best there is. But now with this loss the world and especially PCW superstars will see an even more determined and unstoppable force. My anger and pain will serve as fuel to destroy all that oppose me. Sunday night at March Madness my friend I get my first opportunity to walk away with some PCW gold. My three opponents have all get various other concerns that will be their down falls. Ripper and Sylo going around whinnying about the voices they hear and the pain of the loss of people they never had. Neither of them knows what true loss or pain is all about. But Sunday I will give them an advanced course in it. I have all my skills, both physically and mentally to get the job done. And against these three jobbers that would be more than enough. But with this added pain and suffering I now must live with for the remainder of my days on this forsaken earth, I have the added elements of caged aggression and mental anguish to unleash as well. The pain and mental torment given to me by my disgusting desire to please the legions of wish they were somebody’s that we call fans. The ones that one-minute love you and then the next minute is cheering for some jobber like Ripper, Sylo, or Bell. Well no more will that be a problem. Let them have their jobbers to cheer for. It will not matter or make one difference. (Steve stands back up and looks over to the head stone again. A small smirk comes over his face): There is no doubt that I finally have learned the last lesson I needed from you old friend. The one that tells me to look out only for myself from now on and do only what is good and profitable for me. Your loss has hit me like a ton of bricks, but that is nothing compared to the feeling those three will have win I take all their hopes, dreams, and title aspirations and cram them down their cramped little necks. The time has come for my “Reign of Pain Tour” to begin. And begin it shall on Sunday. It will start with eliminating that paperweight of a champion, Lance Bell; and continue on as I eliminate the other two wish they could be and take the Rising Star title for myself. I will make you proud Sunday Stew. I know that even though you are no longer here you will be with me as I make yet another triumphant move toward the history books. Goodbye for now my good friend, I have some other things to do and other people to kick the hell out of. Rest In Peace my dear friend. You truly were the best who ever lived. Steve walks away from the grave and starts down the path toward where he parked his Tahoe. As he walks down the path, he comes across a middle-aged man who is wearing a PCW T-shirt. The young man is the Pimply Faced Intern Kid. A huge fan of Hix during his PCW days. The Kid stops Steve who has no idea who the kid is. Kid: Hey there Steve. I guess you must have came to visit Stewart’s grave too huh? Guess so. Should I know you or something? Kid: Not necessarily. I am a PCW reporter and have always been a big follower of Hix. Therefore I am also a big follower of yours; on the account of you and Hix being such good friends. I am really bummed out about the loss of him. He always made me laugh. You know I was there the night he won the PCW World Title for the very first time? Yeah I remember that. I was at the event to watch him gain his first World Title that night. I am really surprised that Nelson hasn’t fired you yet. Since you are the one that counted the submission and rang the bell causing him to lose his precious World Title. No matter I guess he has more than enough now, and he does have the chance to get the belt back on Sunday. Kid: Speaking of Sunday I see you have a Four Corners elimination match for the Rising Star title. Do you have a strategy for the match? Yeah to eliminate all three of my opponents and be the last man standing in the middle of the ring. That sound like a good enough plan for you? Kid: Sure. I am not the one that has to execute the plan so anything you say would work for me. Any special plans in honor of Hix if you win? Don’t you mean when I win? But no I have plans for any special celebration or speech in his honor when I win. He is dead and I wish to let him rest in peace. His brilliant career speaks for itself. Besides Stew will be there right along side me the whole time in spirit anyway. That is more than enough for the both of us I think. Now I have to go and get ready to break the bones of three jobbers standing between my Rising Star title and me. Steve walks past the Kid who continues up the path toward the grave of Stewart Hix. Steve walks the rest of the way with his head down until he finally reaches his Tahoe. He get in start up the engine and lets it warm for just a minute before he pulls out of the parking spot and out onto the road leading out of the cemetery. His taillights growing dim in the darkness until they are completely gone. The scene then fades away to total blackness. In memory of the best performer of all time...Stewart Hix!
