SH****************T!!!!

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/25/02 “They WHAT?!” With that we fade in to see the CEO of PCW Tyler Nelson with a cell phone pressed to his ear. He stands dumbstruck in the grand foyer of his suburban Dallas mansion. Shock covers his face and infects his voice as he speaks. “For how long?” questions Tyler. “Why wasn’t I called sooner?” He is silent for several seconds, his face growing more and more pale every moment that passes by. “Why didn’t you stop them you idiot? A warrant? FOR WHAT?!” Tyler listens for a few more seconds, panic starting to settle in. “Have Keene call me ASAP! And start emergency plan Delta!” barks the CEO. He removes the phone from his ear and obliviously presses the ‘END’ button. His gaze is fixated off into space, with no point of focus. Tyler’s eyes begin to glaze over as he begins to realize the gravity of the information he just received. “SHIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIT!” The single word echoes throughout the spacious mansion, bouncing off of the marble walls and crystal chandeliers. As the echoes fade there is silence. Tyler continues to stand there, stunned into a stupor. “This is bad........verrrrry bad,” mutters the CEO nearly incoherently. “I.....I gotta.....shit. I have to get there fast. CAL!” On command Cal Seaver, the CEO’s personal driver, comes dashing into the foyer. The part time reporter for PCW comes to a sliding stop in front of Tyler, panting for breath. “Y-Yes Mr. Nelson?” “I need to get to Greed, INC headquarters right away. Bring the car around front,” says Tyler rather calmly. Cal snaps to attention, “Yessir!” The chauffeur spins on his patent leather shoe heels and takes off in the direction from which he came. Cal’s shoes clap against the marble floors as he disappears out of sight, leaving Tyler alone once again...alone to think. “Think Tyler....think. There’s got to be a way out of this.” Tyler begins to pace in the foyer, tapping his finger against his temple. “The first thing is to fire those fucking lawyers.” Soon the sound of the black Hummer limo is heard coming to a stop outside the front door to the mansion. Before Cal can exit the car Tyler is out the door, motioning for Cal to get out. “Get the hell out of there! I’ll drive myself.” Cal clamors out of the driver’s seat with an uneasy expression on his face. “Are you sure about that, sir? It’s not a problem for me to dri-“ Cal is interrupted by the back of the CEO’s hand as it smacks him across the face. “OWW!” Cal clutches at his cheek as he stumbles out of the way. “Listen to what I say, Seaver,” Tyler warns. “I’m the fuckin CEO and there’s nothing that anyone can do about it.” Cal shrugs and backs away as Tyler climbs in the front seat of the limo. He turns the engine over and revs the engine several times before dropping it into drive. The all-terrain tires squeal for a split second as the Hummer springs into motion. Cal stands and shakes his head back and forth as he watches the limo race out of sight. It cruises down the long drive leading up to the Nelson estate and arrives at the wrought iron gate at the end. Tyler doesn’t slow down and proceeds to plow through the gate, destroying it and doing considerable damage to the Hummer limo. The noise chases off the birds resting in the surrounding trees. The limo travels down the road toward the Greed, INC Headquarters dripping numerous forms of fluids and leaving it’s front bumper behind. “Got to get there fast.....” mutters Tyler to himself. Looking down at the speedometer we see that it is reaching 80 miles an hour and the CEO’s foot isn’t getting any lighter. He passes a large billboard on the side of the road doing 85. Sitting behind the billboard is one of Dallas’ finest who proceeds to flip on the cherries and siren and take off after the CEO. The police car, a Camaro version, quickly eats up the distance between itself and the Hummer limo. Tyler glances up to the rear view mirror and drops his head in defeat. “Goddamnit! I don’t have time for this shit!” Tyler contemplates not stopping, but the Camaro is right on his tail now and there is no chance of outrunning it. The CEO pounds the steering wheel. “FUCK!” Tyler slowly reduces speed and veers off to the side of the road with the police officer close behind. The CEO turns the ignition off and taps the gas pedal with his foot. The officer just sits in the car for a few moments, in all likelyhood running the plates on the limo. “Come on you fucking pig!” exclaims Tyler. After a few more moments the driver’s door of the police car swings open and out steps a rather large and typical looking officer. His chest is thick with a bullet proof vest, and his hair is cut to a flat top. The officer’s eyes hide behind a pair of dark tinted sunglasses as he looks around the area before heading up to the CEO’s vehicle. “This is ridiculous,” says Tyler. He presses the down button for the window and it starts to lower slowly. “Will you hurry the hell up! I don’t have time for this right now!” orders the CEO. The officer smirks and stops at the window. “Good day, Mr. Nelson. I need to see your license and registration for this vehicle.” “You must be kidding,” scoffs Tyler. “I’m the CEO of Gree-“ “I know damn well who you are, sir,” interrupts the officer. “Just get the Goddamn license and registration out NOW!” Tyler is speechless as the officer scowls down into the limo. Finally he regains his composure and lashes back at the officer. “Who is your superior, junior? I’m going to have you busted all the way down to shit patrol, following the mounted police around with a bucket and shovel!” The CEO grins at himself. “If you don’t get that license and registration out you’re going to-“ The officer is cut off by Tyler. “Look Buford T. Justice....how much is it going to take?” snarls the CEO. The officer does a double take. “Excuse me? Are you offering me a bribe?” Tyler rolls his eyes before responding. “Brilliant deduction Einstein. You should be a detective. Now what’s the number? One thousand? Two thousand?” “You can’t buy me off, sir” the officer says sternly. “Pffft! C’mon, how much? Five thousand? Ten thousand?” Tyler pulls his wallet out and starts to remove wads of hundred dollar bills. Although we cannot see the officers eyes behind his sunglasses we can tell by his raising eyebrows that he is starting to get interested.” “Ummm.....” Tyler begins to fan the money out in front of the officers face. “Fifteen thousand? Hurry up and decide because I have shit I need to take care of.” The officer looks around suspiciously and then suddenly snatches the money from Tyler’s hands. “Don’t tell anyone, and I mean anyo-“ “Shut up! I gotta go” demands Tyler as he turns the ignition over and slams on the gas. He drops the Hummer into drive and peels out on the shoulder of the highway, spraying gravel and dirt all over the officer as he stands there with a fistful of dollars. Soon the officer and his car are nothing but a speck in the rear view mirror. Tyler taps his thumbs on the steering wheel nervously as he approaches 85 miles per hour once again. “The audacity of that leech to pull over the CEO. I should have HIM arrested! Just like that low life White Mexican. He thinks he can attack me and get away with it? Nobody, and I mean NOBODY, does that to the CEO. This Friday I’m going to kill that taco eating son of a bitch.” A short pause from the CEO. “This is all his fault” Tyler seethes. The CEO’s rant is interrupted by the ringing of the car phone. Tyler snatches it up from the base, “Hello?! It’s about Goddamn time you call me, Keene! What the hell happened there today?” Tyler listens intently and his face turns a shade of red that can only be brought on by extreme anger. “I’ll be there shortly. If any more of those Justice Department pukes show up, lock em out!” Tyler slams the car phone back on to the base and presses the gas even farther to the floor. We get a view from outside of the Hummer limo as it weaves in and out of traffic. It moves at a high rate of speed down the highway and out of sight. The scene fades to black.
