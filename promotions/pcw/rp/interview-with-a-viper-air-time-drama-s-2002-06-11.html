Interview With A Viper (Air-time Drama's)

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th June 2002 Dum Da Da Dum Da Dum Dum Dum~! The techno beats pulsate through the television set filling the room with sound as the drums and the rest of the music cut in and the 'SBS Television' logo appears dead center in the middle of the television screen. The music continues to beep away as a huge picture of the Golden Soccer 'World Cup' flashes in front of the logo and begins to rotate clockwise in all its glory, causing the Gold to shimmer and shine in every direction filling the television set with it’s radiance as the words 'Korea/Japan World Cup 2002' appear below it and the reporting scene slowly begins to materialize behind it. 'Qualifying Round World Cup Highlights Will Begin In Just A Moment, Until Then We’ll Start By Showing You The Classic Close Match Between Senegal and Uruguay' a stern and well spoken man’s voice can be heard as the music slowly fades out and the network shows the scheduled programming as everything becomes ready behind the scenes. Behind the logo and the theme music there is the flashy scenery for the World Cup 2002 show in all its glory! Strobe lights line around the desk and the pylons on either side, the blue, green and red lights giving off a soft glow and warm feel to the set as a huge television screen built into the background begins to glow as well and play the highlights that had just been announced. As the highlights begin to play, the lights are slowly turned up on the set and everything begins to glow more than before as a white light overhead is flicked on, casting a professional look over the entire set, which now looks pretty first rate and impressive. Although the lights over the set and the television are on, there are a million and one people hustling and bustling around in the darkness in front and behind the set. Camera men wearing hats and earphones are busy setting up equipment and readying their cameras, posh looking women are talking to men dressed in suits while others are busy powdering other men’s faces while they sit on big chairs, men in suits are walking around talking on cell phones and looking important and finally a few other men are rehearsing lines whilst pacing backwards and forwards, trying desperately to memorize the script! The scene behind the scenes can only be described as PURE chaos! But somewhere amidst all the backstage hustle and bustle a young spiffy looking man wearing a blue suit and a red tie with slicked back, black hair struts onto the lit up set holding a script in one hand and beckoning someone to follow behind him with the other. 'Don’t be shy, you’re the guest the camera is going to be focused on you for a fair bit of the show Mr. Loc!….Mr. Loc?' The man talks towards the front of the set before turning around to look for the 'Guest' with the familiar name. As he turns around, standing in the center with everyone working like dog’s around him is none other than PCW Superstar, Charlie Loc, admiring everything around him and not listening to a word that the reporter is saying. 'We don’t have time for this Mr. Loc, the show begins in little more than 3 Minutes!' The man impatiently says towards Charlie in a professional voice, Charlie still doesn’t seem to pay any attention but looks in the mans direction. 'Grrr,' the man shakes his head and walks across the set behind the desk, his feet clapping on the tiled white floor as he sits down on a stool and corrects his posture, facing the front of the set as Charlie aimlessly walks across the front looking around him in awe as he does. The wide eyed Charlie still doesn’t seem to get the picture as the everyone backstage seems to suddenly revolve around him as he continues to walk aimlessly around the front of the scene as the camera men begin to communicate with themselves through the use of hand signals and weird phrases. 'Are you good to go Mike?' A woman’s voice can be heard as she pops her attractive blonde face onto the scene for just a second. With one hand she removes the microphone away from her face before staring wide eyed at Charlie as the reporter gives her the thumbs up before shrugging his shoulders as she smiles, shakes her head and disappears off into the darkness of backstage. The reporter begins to prepare his notes as Charlie Loc finally notices him sitting behind the desk and begins to walk over behind it, still admiring the pretty lights and beautiful scenery. His baggy jeans make rustling sounds as he walks and sits down on a stool behind the desk next to the reporter as the two begin to have a last minute conversation 'Do I have to run through what’s going to happen during the show with you again Mr. Loc or will everything be fine? You can never be too prepared!' The reporter mumbles to Charlie as he straightens his sheets of paper. Charlie just stares back at him blankly as the reporter gets the message and continues to talk. 'Look I know you’re on Television in the States like every week, but this is different! This telecast is World Wide and it’s not Wrestling we’re talking about…it’s Soccer! You do know what Soccer is right?' The man finishes his sentence taking Charlie for a complete dumb ass. Charlie just shakes his head in amazement and begins to crack his knuckles by his side clearly getting frustrated by the man when the 'Guest Appearance' hasn’t even begun. 'Just answer my questions in English and everything will be fine! By the way my name is Mike Ramone and I’m the SBS World Cup fill in reporter! I’m sorry that we skipped formalities before! I can’t say I know who you are but you must be important if you’re on here right….' The reporter trails off as Charlie just shakes his head and continues to stare at him with a look of hatred in his eye. 'And were on in a Minute people! Quick somebody come and powder that Charlie’s nose! Get some more light on Mike and everything is good to go! Chop Chop!' The woman’s voice from before can be heard booming over the set as two attractive females run onto the set. One of them begins to powder Charlie’s nose as he squirms and the other fixes Mike Ramone’s tie as they do their job and quickly exit the scene leaving Charlie and Mike sitting facing the front of the set awaiting the telecast to come onto air. 'And were on in 5…4….3…2….1…..' The cameraman’s voice trails off before the SBS logo and theme music hits the air. CLICK~! There is a loud clicking sound as a huge spotlight floods onto the set which is now on Air as Charlie stares at the camera dumb founded as Mike Ramone begins to speak and 'Korea/Japan World Cup 2002' appears on the television screen behind him. 'Hello and welcome to the SBS highlight show for the Korea/Japan World Cup for 2002! I’m Mike Ramone and sitting next to me is the famous Vietnamese Wrestler from PCW…Charlie Loc!' Mike’s voice trails off as he reaches to shake Charlie’s hand. Charlie doesn’t take any initiative and just nods his head at him as his hair flops over his face. Mike manages a weak smile as he turns back and the woman with the headset slaps her forehead as Mike begins to speak. 'Sorry for this folks, we had tried to get Juan 'Sunshine' Borro in for talks but it seems he would be in hospital due to recent circumstances! But getting over to Soccer Senegal and Uruguay just drew 3 all in a great match-up that was just telecast before! What’d you think of Mr. Loc?' Mike looks at Charlie with a gleaming white toothy smile perfect for the camera. 'It not Vietnam so I not give a *BeEp*!' The last word of Charlie’s sentence is bleeped out as he leans back on the stool with a smug grin crossing over his face as Mike begins to go red with embarassment. 'Uhh, yeah getting onto the Vietnam situation then! Why do you believe that Vietnam didn’t qualify for the World Cup? Wasn’t the team strong this year?' Mike asks matter of factly. 'Vietnam not have a team….no give a *BeEp* about Soccer! I only come on here so I could make a fool out of that drug *BeEp* Juan *BeEp* Borro!' Charlie states back as Mike looks towards the camera and begins to become flustered. 'Well, uhh, I’m sorry but could you refrain your language please Mr. Loc? The censors are having a hard time keeping up and could we please keep this conversation on Soccer?' Mike nearly pleads with Charlie. Charlie looks at him as the sinister grin crosses over his Asian face as he shakes his head and Mike gulps and manages a cheap fake smile at the camera as everyone backstage begins to look at each other. 'I not give a *BeEp* if you want to talk about Soccer…you do it in your own time! Right now this my time! You paying me to do this, I do this my way! So if you want Soccer, change the *BeEp* channel because Charlie not going to give you soccer!' Charlie yells towards the camera and leans forward over the desk. Once again everyone backstage begins to go in to an up roar as the woman can be heard asking for a commercial break only to be told they get one every 15 minutes. A few groans can be heard as Mike tries to keep himself calm as the Vietnamese Viper literally takes over the show. 'The Portugal Soccer Team think they hard-core because they back up Juan Borro, but what they not realize is they just make things worse for him! Athlete’s should be drug tested, if they were, Borro probably never even make it onto a playing field in his life! I tell him myself, you slip up once I going to make you pay! He slip up that one time and now I making him pay, World Wide! But it not him to blame for this, no it not! It the Portuguese Soccer Team because they the ones who come out drunk! They the ones who lost and caused him to do this! They the ones who should be drug tested and kicked out of the World Cup!' Charlie finishes his spill as everyone is left looking at him in complete shock as he continues. 'I not give a *BeEp* about Soccer! If Vietnam even get a team together there going to be no competition anyway! There nothing the rest of you can do! No one can stop me! No can make a fool out Charlie Loc! You hear?!' Charlie begins to yell his little lungs out as everyone slaps their fore heads! The attractive woman looks towards Mike Ramone as he shakes his head and shrugs his shoulders before looking in Charlie’s direction and asking him a question, trying to make everything look like it’s a big part of the script. 'And Charlie, what do you have against the team of Portugal? They’re making a great effort this World Cup, with or without Borro! I think you should just give them a break and….' Mike gets himself cut off….. 'Mike, you shut the *BeEp* up! I not like you, I not even want to be here! But let me tell you now! This not going to be the last time you see me! This not going to be the last time I mess up things for Borro! I telling you now this World Cup, it only the beginning! You all nothing….' Charlie screams his last words into Mike Ramone’s face before beginning to laugh sadistically. The camera begin to pan in as Mike begins to fan himself with his sheets as Charlie Loc shakes his head and stands up off of his stool before reaching down and picking it up. Mike Ramone turns around and throws his hands up in defense. 'SECURITY! SECURITY! SECURITY!' Mike screams like a little baby as Charlie laughs and sends the stool cracking down over his head, sending his notes flying around him like a whirl wind! Charlie doesn’t seem to want to stop there though! He tries harder to get his point across as he grabs the stool and sends it crashing into the television set, sending shards and sparks flying in every which direction as the cameras all pan in on Loc who is destroying the SBS set. The attractive and probably head woman can be heard shrieking as she faints in the background as all the fancy looking men and women go to comfort her and a few cameramen quickly flee their cameras as Loc spots them and waltzes over. He grabs the camera by its lens and pulls it up close to his rather un-attractive Asian face before speaking right down the throat of it. 'Look at this, I prove you not *BeEp* with Loc! No one *BeEp* with me! *BeEeEeEeEeEeEeEeEeEeEeP* This not the last you see of me! This just the beginning! JUAN BORRO, PORTUGAL, SOCCER, SBS….YOU NOTHING~!' Charlie screams and nearly shatters the camera lens by doing so as he laughs and rears back. Out of nowhere everything seems to go suddenly into slow motion as Charlie’s small, yet huge, scarred Asian fist can be seen flying at the lens of the camera cracking the glass and showing the image of the final moments of Chaos as the screen fills with brightly colored lines and long beeping sound. The SBS logo fills the screen as a replay of the Soccer match which was shown before comes back onto the screen as underneath the green and perfect Soccer field a note is being displayed in white writing on a black background on the bottom of the screen. 'Due To Technical Difficulties, The Soccer Show Will Be Back When Everything Is Online And Well! Thank-you For Your Patience And Sorry For Any Inconvenience' The scene slowly fades out as Senegal scores it’s first goal in a matter of minutes leaving the television buzzing with the World Cup atmosphere and leaving everyone wondering just what is happening on the set of SBS Soccer Show as Loc was left continuing to show his rage and cause a complete Storm.......
