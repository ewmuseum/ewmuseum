Good News

26 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 26, 2003 Just as the sun was coming up we had finally managed to find our way back to my Chatham, Massachusetts home as the children slept silently in the back. The new day had not only reunited the house that I hadn’t visited in nearly a month, but it also reunited Chris and Jackson in friendship. The reason for their grudges being dropped so suddenly was unknown but it didn’t matter to me. All that mattered was that they were getting along once again and that there was no more senseless fighting that I was caught between. “Should we wake them up?” Chris said, unfastening his seatbelt and climbing out of the driver’s seat side of the car and into the biter cold winds of a New England morning. “Nah, let them sleep for a little bit. They’re probably more exhausted from the trip than both of us together. Beside a little piece and quiet would be nice even if it were only for five short minutes.” I said, following the same steps as Chris as I climbed out of the passenger sear of the car and walked towards the trunk where there were several boxes waiting to be brought inside to be unpacked. While standing there beside the trunk Chris pops the hood of the trunk up, Being in a rush to get the moving process over with I reached into the trunk grabbing the first box I can find but before I could even lift it Chris’ voice cut in. “Tessabear, don’t you worry about the boxes I’ll get them as long as you hold the door open for me.” “It sounds good to me.” I said while walking away from the trunk and towards the front door as Chris stands there gathering as many boxes as he can in his arm. “Kami, these boxes are heavy. What did you pack in them Tessa?” He says as he peers over the top box to see where he is going. “They aren’t my boxes so how should I know?” I said as I pulled out my pair of house keys, unlocking the front door for Chris, allowing him to step into the house. “Where do you want me to put them?” He said, stumbling around like a drunken man looking ready to fall at any moment. “Just place them beside the stairs.” I said, shutting the door behind him to keep the cold air out. “So I don’t suppose you’ve heard the good news yet, have you?” He said as he walked away from the boxes which he neatly stacked against the bottom stair. He was such a perfectionist while I was the total opposite, tossing anything where it looked like it would fit. It made me wonder how on earth we’d be able to deal with each other once we started to share a bedroom if that time ever came. “Good news?” I questioned as he walked back towards the front door. “Yes you heard me correctly.” He answered with a smile that told me it was news that would give the morning a good start for a change. “And what’s this so called good news? Are you and Jackson going to kick the crap into Erik in return for his actions at Shanghaied?” I asked while opening the front door once more and walking back out into the numbing winter weather towards the car where two more boxes waited along with three sleeping children. “Well as great as kicking Erik ass would be that’s not the news, but it’s something even better.” He said, following me out into the cold but warming me with his charming smile as he teased me some more still not telling me what this good news was. “What is it?” My anxious voice cried like a child wanting to know ahead of time what they got for Christmas or their birthday. “Well how do these words sound, Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor: PCW Tag Team Champions.” He smirked knowing that I would surely squeal like a little girl and surely enough I did. “Oh my god, are you kidding me Chris?” I squealed gleefully but yet almost positive that there was some twist to this considering it seemed much too good to be true. “Provided we beat P.W.A on Tuesday, then no I’m not kidding you.” He said as he walked back over to the trunk taking the final two boxes in his arms and walking back towards the house with a smile on his face knowing that his words just made my day twice as good as it already was starting out to be. “Yes!” I cheered so loudly that I had awakened the children who were still inside the car. The car door opened and out came three small figures with morning hair. The smallest of the three came over to me taking me by the hand and then looking back up at the huge white house that stood before him. “Is this our new house Tessa?” He said as the rising sun twinkled in his blue eyes. “You better believe it is.” I answered as I ran my hand through the child’s darker brown hair, pushing it down to make it look slightly neater. “It’s awesome.” He said with nothing more than a smile. “It sure it, but you know what would be more awesome? Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor as PCW Tag Team Champions and Evan you better believe we are going to do our best to become just that.” I smiled as I patted him on the head. He exchanged the smile with me before heading towards the front door where Chris was standing. Chris knew just what I had said to Evan by the smiles that would not leave our faces and I knew he was hoping for the same which is why going into Tuesday I knew we were going to have to give it nothing but our all. The time for fun and games were over and it was time to finally prove that Chris and I were a lot more than a happy couple. We were also a team with a goal, a goal to win those PCW Tag Team titles no matter what it took.
