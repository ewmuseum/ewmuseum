In Portugal visiting The Golden Toe...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Meh.... Our scene opens up in the infamous city of Lisbon, Portugal. Currently we look over this brilliant place from a bird’s eye viewing point. Slowly but surly we skim above the creamy white clouds of Portugal giving us the perfect sight of the beautiful city. Abruptly we begin to plummet back down towards the earth, seemingly plunging straight for a head on collision with a huge apartment complex. As we grow almost too close to the building’s surface we seem to slide easily through its very walls to arrive with a new view, that of an extremely well furbished apartment. As we look around we find there to be many shelves full of gleaming golden football trophies, four white leather sofa’s, a polished wooden floor and a wide screen TV set. We’re obviously in the lounge of the apartment. We continue our journey through this place until we find another room, of which is occupied by a tall, mousy brown haired man in his mid twenties. This man is none other than PCW’s very own, Juan Borro. The room that the former cruiserweight champion resides within is obviously his apartments kitchen. As we gaze about we observe many pots and pans hanging from some metal girders strung up to the roof. A metal table, which shimmers in the morning light of the Lisbon sun, locates itself in the middle of the areas floor, which is made of black and white tile-designed lino. The former cruiserweight champion sits behind the silvery table, sat upon a small light brown chair, reading a black and white paper whilst enjoying a mug of steaming hot coffee. Juan’s concentration is stirred when suddenly there is a harsh knock at the apartments door… Juan: What the hell? Juan looks down at the watch upon his tanned wrist. Juan: Who could be calling at this hour? Why its only 9: 30 in the morning! There is another vague knock at the door; this one is a little lighter than the last. Juan looks on in puzzlement to who could be calling at this hour and then carefully puts down his coffee and newspaper and heads for the kitchens exit. Moments later he walks through the lounge and then into a short corridor, at the end of which we find a door; presumably the apartments entrance. Juan grasps the doors golden key and then turns it; there is a slow clicking sound signalling the unlocking of the door. Borro then places his hand upon the doorknob and opens it. The Golden Toe is taken back by surprise when he is met with the face of Titu Xansiv…better known to PCW fans as The Pharaoh! Juan: PHARAOH?! Pharaoh smiles, looking a little dazed and a little drunk too… Juan: What’s the matter with you? The groggy Pharaoh grins drunkenly and then blurts out the words… The Pharaoh: I had a drink with Flaymin Aymin… Juan: One drink?! Pharaoh’s drunken state now makes perfect sense. As you may remember, the last time we saw The Pharaoh he and his two person entourage of Isiy and Wimlog had been invited into the living-room of Flaymin Aymin’s whitewashed beech house. The Flaymin One had promised a few “brews” to the Egyptian trio and so obviously Pharaoh had been delivered with a few more “brews” than he had first anticipated. The Golden Toe looks on in shock… Juan: Pharaoh, you of all people should have known not to drink any of Aymin’s beer…its damned strong stuff…I mean it was brewed in Australia dammit!! Unexpectedly a disgruntled-looking Isiy pops her head around the door… Isiy: Juan…can we come in, please? Titu wanted to talk to you, BUT judging from the state he’s in, I doubt that’ll be happening! Without question Juan offers them to come into his home with a friendly hand gesture…Isiy pushes Pharaoh in through the door, and follows him with an angry sigh. In next walks the disfigured Wimlog Mogre, the Pharaoh’s recently hired advisor. Juan leads them all into his living-room and offers them all a seat on one of his white leather sofas. Each of the Egyptian’s take a seat with great expressions of gratitude present upon their faces… Juan: So what brings you all here? Pharaoh is about to blurt some sort of drunken sentence out but he has little time to do so before Isiy interrupts him. Isiy: Well, we WERE here to talk about that match you guys have coming up, I believe its scheduled for tonight? Juan: Yeah! The eight-man tag team match! Should be a great-… The drunken Pharaoh interrupts… The Pharaoh: -Chance to kick some ass!! Juan laughs, obviously humoured by The Pharaoh’s down-to-the-point view on the eight-man tag team match. Juan: I was going to say that it’ll be a great match…I mean, we have some tough opposition! The Pharaoh: Tough?! Ha! One of the four men we have to battle is Jason Core! Don’t make me laugh Juan, why I almost broke his leg during our TV title match this past Friday on PCW Fusion! Juan looks at Isiy doubtfully. The gorgeous Egyptian enchantress sighs… Juan: I’ll go and make him some Coffee… Isiy: Yeah, best idea really… A few hours later, and fully back to normal, thanks to the ingenious amount of caffeine that Nescafe manages to store within a few teaspoons of coffee granules, The Pharaoh is once again himself…thankfully. The Pharaoh: By Horus, Juan, I’m dreadfully sorry that you had to see me like that… Juan chuckles… Juan: Not to worry, Pharaoh…it happens to the best of us at least once in our lives…so anyway, Isiy was telling me about why you came here today…you wanted to talk about our match, right? Pharaoh nods in agreement. The Pharaoh: Yes, Juan! Now the thing is I want our team to be a united force! Do you know what I mean? Together we can win this thing, my friend…all we need to do is use teamwork! Juan: And I know about teamwork, Pharaoh, I mean I’ve held the PCW tag team straps twice with old Kevin Klaymoore! I know damn well that we can win this thing! Pharaoh smiles in response to Borro’s enthusiasm, it looks as though Juan is fully geared up for the bout… The Pharaoh: Well I’m glad to see that you’re ready for the match! Plus you get a chance to take out that Jobber, Rent A Hero! Juan: Rent A hero is no jobber, Pharaoh; I have the utmost respect for him! Pharaoh nods, accepting Juan’s admiration for PCW’s caped cruiserweight crusader. Suddenly a flicker of a time long past glints in his royal brown eyes, he strokes his chin thoughtfully and then asks a question that possibly only Juan could know the answer to. The Pharaoh: Speaking of jobbers, how is Kevin Klaymoore doing these days? The Golden Toe rolls his eyes in uncertainty. Juan: Meh…I wouldn’t know! I really haven’t kept up with him since he left PCW for some strange and unknown reason. When I think back I truly regret teaming with him again… Juan sighs and shakes his head in disappointment. Juan: The single worst career move I could have ever made… Pharaoh nods in accord. The Pharaoh: Yes, I agree… Pharaoh turns to Wimlog… The Pharaoh: Mogre! Wimlog: Yess, Masster? The Pharaoh: What time is it, foul cretin? Wimlog grinds his teeth in frustration of Titu’s insult…he then looks down at the watch upon his thin wrist and reads out aloud what he sees. Wimlog: 12 O Clock, milord… Pharaoh sighs and then looks back at Juan. The Pharaoh: Well Juan it looks as though I’m going to have to get going…I have one more place to visit before our match… Pharaoh gets up from his seat, as does Juan. Borro offers Pharaoh a hand seemingly in signal for a handshake. This is just what Pharaoh wants…a team united cannot be stopped after all… The Pharaoh: I bid you farewell, Juan… Juan: It’s been a pleasure having you around, Titu… Pharaoh grasps the Portuguese superstar and shakes his hand with a sly grin. The Pharaoh: Oh, and once again I hope you’ll forgive me for my drunken state earlier on… Juan chuckles. Juan: Hey, don’t worry about it, Pharaoh… Juan shows the three to his apartments door… The Pharaoh: And remember, Juan, if we lose this match then we have to kiss our opponents asses…I don’t think any of us wish to do that…I know I’d hate to kiss that damn Jason Core’s ass, or that wretched Mexican, El Diablo’s for that matter! By the names of the great Egyptian Gods, I’m being forced to face him in a TV title defence this Friday on Fusion! Juan: Ah well, I guess that there is a good side to facing Diablo… The Pharaoh: There is? Juan: Yeah! You’ll easily win! Therefore giving you another name to add to your ‘defeated’ list! Borro and Titu laugh as the Egyptian trio walk back outside onto a thin, red-walled corridor of the apartment complex. Titu, Isiy and Wimlog begin to walk away from the kindly Juan Borro’s home as he Golden Toe waves them on until they’re out of sight. The scene fades to black…
