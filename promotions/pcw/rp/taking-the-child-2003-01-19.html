Taking the Child

19 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-19-03 The cold was falling apon the people of America. Everywheres people was suffering from the cold weather that is sweeping the nation. New York is no different. The people of New York turn up their heaters and stay indoors. At the apartment of Chris Storm the heater is in the 70's and the blankets are passed out. Everyone is sitting in the living room. Tessa with Alex and Christa on the couch and Chris Storm sitting in a chair with Evan near them. They are watching Lilo & Stitch. The kids are laughing as Tessa and Chris enjoy themselves. Suddenly there is a knock at the door. Chris picks up Evan from his lap and passes him to Tessa. She lets the little one rest in her lap as Chris goes to answer the door. With each step Chris wonders who it is. Although he knows deep down who it is and what they want. But he holds onto that little piece of hope in his heart that it isn't that. He takes a deep breath and silently says a Shinto prayer before he puts his hand on the door knob and opens the door. 'Oh no....' His heart skins deep into his stomach. He has to place his hand on the door frame to keep his balance as his legs feel weak under him. Tessa turns her head to see what the matter is. She lets out a gasp as she sees who is at the door. Chris looks over his shoulder and sees her looking. He quickly steps outside and shuts the door behind him. Chris just leans against the door. 'What can I help you with officer?' he ask them. The two cops look at him and take off there hats. Chris was expecting and waiting for this. He knew what the police officers wanted but he was hoping against fate that is was something different. The officer on the left just shook his head. Chris lowered his head. All hope he had vanished. 'You are here for Evan aren't you?' was all he could manage to say. The cop on the right let out a sigh. Chris pushed himself off the door and stood between the two officers. 'I'm sorry. But we have to take him.' Chris did not want to hear those words. He may have just meet Evan a yesterday but already he was attached to the child as if it was his own. Now Evan was being taken away from him. Chris took in a deep breath and got ready to talk with the police. He needed to know, 'Do you know what will happen to Evan?' the cops looked at each other. Chris knew it meant trouble. Sweat started to forum on his forehead. He was getting worried about Evan. 'Evan will be placed in a foster home for right now. Until relatives can be found for him.' the cop informed Chris. Foster home? Chris started to think quickly. Tessa has started to notice Chris has been gone for a long time and gets up to see what is the matter. She stands in the doorway and sees the cops. Her breath gets caught in her throat. Chris spots her and quickly moves in front of her. She looks at Chris worried, trying to see over his shoulder at the two cops standing outside the apartment. 'Chris what is going on here?' she asked him. Chris kisses her on the lips to quiet her. 'They have came for Evan. But don't worry. I am going to talk to them. Now go back inside and watch the kids. I'll be back in there shortly.' he tells her. Tessa seems to accept his words and returns inside. Chris lets out a sigh and turns back to the cops. Both of them seem impatient now. Chris starts to plead with them. 'Look please listen to me. If you are going to place Evan in a foster home then leave him here with us. Tessa and I already raise two children together. Not to mention we have stable jobs and a warm home for him. He's already been here for the past day. We have grown attached to him. Please don't take him away from us.' his words fell on the cops ears. They felt sorry for the child. Something like this was never easy on a boy. Yet the law was the law. They had to take him away. The cop on the left put his hand on Chris' shoulder. He just kept his head lowered. He hated to do this. 'I'm sorry sir. But the child must come with us. It's the law. There is nothing I can do about it.' Chris merely nodded in defeat. He looked up at the cop. 'Can you at least let me bring him out here so the other children won't know what is going on? Also, can Tessa and I tell him good bye?' he pleaded with him. Tears were starting to form in his eyes. Chris was trying his best to fight them back. The cop on the right nodded in agreement. Chris turned to go get Evan but the cop of the right placed his hand out and stopped Chris. He looked around and then at the other cop. 'If you want to request that Evan be placed in your care until a home is found, then we can bring you and your wife down to the station to fill out the proper paper work. You two can even start on the adoption papers if you want.' Chris smiled. Maybe there still was hope. He had to tell Tessa. Chris asked them to wait and he goes back inside. There still might be hope for Evan. And Chris was going to try his best to be sure of it.
