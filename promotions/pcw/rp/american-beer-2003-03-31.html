American Beer

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-31-03 I pledge allegiance to the flag of the United States of America and to the country for which it stands, one nation under God indivisible with liberty and justice for all. These words started the school days for children all over the U.S. for years. Did they really understand what they were saying? Most Americans also forget these same words as they become adults. Sadly these same words of patriotism and its meaning will be forgotten with the sands of time. To be a true American, one must realize the true meaning of freedom and not take for granted this great nation and the men who sacrificed their own lives to give us what we have. I’m not saying to take your pride to your head and make yourself better than those unfortunate enough to be born an American. Be thankful and appreciative of what God’s grace has given to you. What’s this? You don’t believe in God? Shame on you. How can you label yourself a true American when in truth, your Constitution was built on the foundation of the belief in God our creator? Even the quarters you carry in your pockets reads ‘In God We Trust’. An Atheist has taken prayer out of our schools. Through the years guns and knives have replaced religion in our school system. Feels free to exercise your freedom of no religion but please don’t take my freedom of religion away. To those that don’t support our President or the war I say this, ‘COWARD’. There is no such thing as a world without war, and as long as there is human beings breathing on this Earth, there will be wars. This may not be pleasant to hear, but sometimes my friend, the truth hurts. I’m glad we finally have a president with a backbone and that is willing to not only serve, but protect our country as well. What would you do in his place? Could you even face the pressure, stress and responsibility that come with such a position? A true America supports the soldier that sacrifices his or her way of life just so that the rest of the country can keep theirs. To the protesters I say this, GO HOME. Don’t you people understand if we don’t do something now, the future could be a lot worse that what you’re seeing now. Support your troops; don’t discourage them with your anti-war bull. With a push of a button, Teresa turns off her television. She takes in a deep breath and sits back in the black leather couch, which sits in the living room of her new Louisiana home. For more than a week, the TV has been airing nothing but news broadcasts on the war. After so long, she assumes, people will get tired of hearing about it unless they have a loved one overseas. “I need a beer.” The war isn’t the only thing that has been on Teresa’s mind. True, her soldier brother Eric has taken most of her thoughts, but a more obvious circumstance consumes the rest. This being Friday Night Fusion, March 28. She escorted Rent-A-Hero down the ramp, stood at his ringside corner then out of nowhere gave his angles a gentle tug and before he realized what was going on, PCW’s favorite superhero lost his Cruiserweight belt. What went over Teresa? What caused her to turn on her spandex buddy? The world will know soon enough. For now, Teresa grabs more than one beer from her fridge. She takes three. Tuesday, the former long reigning TV champ is going one on one with a woman who has beat her once before. To Teresa, that match doesn’t count. If it weren’t for Brandi and her interfering, Teresa would’ve had Tessa pinned. That was then. This is now. It’s time to put that schoompy in her place. To this thought, Teresa pops the cap off a bottle of Bud Ice and takes a long drink. “Here’s to the true Americans.”
