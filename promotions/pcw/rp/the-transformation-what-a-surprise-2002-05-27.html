The Transformation: What a Surprise!

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/27/02 The Scene opens with Jaden sitting up in her queen size bed because that what she is, fiddling with her pen and pad think about her oncoming relationship with Big Daveylicious. Did she make the right choice? Is he the right one for her? Is there someone better for her? She did not know. The only person who could answer that for her was her child’s father for 3 years, Sylo. She put her writing utensils down and began to call Sylo. An awakened voice answers the telephone. Someone who was asleep but was just awaked by the ongoing ringing of the telephone. It was a soft but lovely voice, very petite. She knew this could not be Sylo. “Maybe Sylo moved. Did something happen to him,” Jaden began to wonder. “Hello,” the half sleep voice cried out with a slight yawn. “Hello, my I speak to Sylo?” “He is not home,” the voice replied. “May I ask whom am I speaking to,” Jaden ask wondering who is this woman in Sylo house, maybe in his bed. “This is his wife, Kelly! Whom am I speaking to?” There is a pause. Jaden lost the breath of words… “Just tell Sylo that all hell will break lose…that I will preach heaven down and KNOCK THE HELL OUT OF HIM,” Jaden says screaming at Kelly. Kelly did not know what to say but to say, “ok!” As soon as Kelly and Jaden finished exchanging words, Sylo walked into his bedroom holding Jaden and his child Jasmine. A beautiful 3 year old little girl with ice blue hair with black high lights. She is just a unique and gorgeous child like her mother. “Did I get any calls while I was gone?” “Yes, one strange one…some girl called, I think she said her name was Jaden. She said, just tell Sylo that all hell will break lose…that I will preach heaven down and knock the hell out of him!” Sylo was in shock he just stood their mouth wide open, jaw hitting the floor and the baby falling out of his arms. Kelly tries to interrogate Sylo, trying to find out who is the Jaden who called while Jaden begins to change her actions and herself. Jaden seems to be transforming herself everyday, changing her imagine, surprising people all the time. People see her as the girl that does not do anything wrong, smart, goes to church, the perfect angle, but she is about to open their eyes and show the truth because the truth hurts! Jaden walks over to her closet and picks out her outfit for her date with Big D. that is what she calls Big Daveylicious. She finds the shortest and sexiest thing she can find, something that will turn people’s heads. She walks back over to the bed, grabs her little journal, and begins to write her daily thoughts for the day. Dear Journal, Hi, it is I, Jaden! I called your friend today. You know Sylo. I find out the bitch is married. I guess you can say that our relationship is over. I guess you can say it worked out for the best. Maybe Davey is the one for me. He is somewhat cute, too. Maybe I will have to him on a trip around Jaden’s block if you get my drift (SMILE)! Well I have to go and take a shower to get ready for tonight. I have to take care of the essentials…hair, makeup, nails, etc. Love-yea always, XOXOXOXO, Jaden R. Lopez PSIsn’t it funny how everything works out Diary!
