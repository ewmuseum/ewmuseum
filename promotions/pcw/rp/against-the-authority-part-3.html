Title: Against the Authority- Part 3 Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: -- The protest. It is Monday morning, outside the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid's classroom. His 25 students are ready to enter, carrying signs. For soon, Principal Susorch will enter, to address the class. The now-bare classroom is the scene for a protest. A protest against the authority that attacked Mr. Trapezoid. A protest against the forces a morality. A protest that comes out strongly in favor of F.U.C.K.ing! *DING DING DING!* Looking up at the clock, The HIT sighs... The HIT: The bell... one of the few constants in my life. In the ring, or in the classroom, the bell signifies 'go time'. The sounding of that bell means its time for either kids to come streaming into the classroom, or for me to go out into the arena and kick some ass. The ringing means I'm at home.....and I'm happy. But damnit, right now, I'm not happy. The authority in my life is forming a tag team to kick my ass, at school, and in the ring. The key parts of my life are converging....and there's only one way out. Delta Pi. The kids slowly come in....carrying their signs. Trapezoid smiles to acknowledge them entering, then goes back to speaking, in a lower tone. The HIT: Delta Pi isn't just my cockamamie scheme to get rid of hostile school personnel. It's what I have to do in PCW as well. The Front Office is trying to take me out, so I have to take it out. Systematically. Intellectually. As only the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid can. With Teresa Tomas at my side... I WILL hold tag team gold shortly..but, as nice as that is, it's not the main goal....that comes on Friday. The students have nearly all filed in, as a cruel smile lights the Trapezoid's features... The HIT: The PCW Television Title...the belt I defeated Kid Wonder for on the beaches of Nice, France....and the title I lost to a FUCKING BUCKET OF CHICKEN! I swore that I would avenge that loss....and now, I cannot, since Kramer's gone back to the Purdue farm. Now, the only way I have left is to hold that title....and do it the justice it so richly deserves....okay kids! Calm down! The principal will be here shortly! And, what are we going to do when she comes? The children raise their signs, and begin to chant... Class: HECK NO! YOU'RE A HO! HECK NO! YOU'RE A HO! The HIT: EXACTLY! *sniff* You kids are so good at this... Class: THANKS MR. TRAPEZOID! The HIT: I just wish I could be here for this... Jimmy: You can't? The HIT: No....they won't let me be here while you meet the new principal. Class: AWWWW! The HIT: I know. So, I'm going to get on my flight to Turmoil. YOU GUYS ARE THE LEADERS NOW! She'll be in there in ten minutes..and it is your job to give that miserable old woman hell like only The HIT and his students can! Class: YES! The HIT: Have fun......it's time for teacher to go kick some ass... The HIT turns to leave, closing the door behind him. As he walks, his fellow teachers are assembled outside, pointing and laughing. The Trapezoid walks through the gauntlet, head held high, whispering one phrase over and over again. The HIT: They will not break me.....they will not break me... His refrain rises, reaching its crescendo, as he turns to scream at his co-workers.. The HIT: YOU WILL NOT BREAK ME! I DO NOT CARE WHAT AUTHORITY YOU SET AGAINST ME! BY THE TIME I RETURN HERE ON WEDNESDAY, I WILL BE THE #1 CONTENDER FOR THE TAG TEAM TITLE! AND, WITH TERESA TOMAS, I WILL TAKE THOSE DAMNED TITLE BELTS AND USE THEM TO MAIM EACH AND EVERYONE OF YOU UNGRATEFUL SHITS! The teachers laugh, feeling quite secure. After all, it is the Trapezoid who is being censored...the Trapezoid who is being reprimanded. Still, HIT inwardly smiles, walking out the hall door, and takes one step into the outside.. The HIT: Poor principal.....she doesn't know my kids. She have no idea as to what they are capable of! Just like those teachers don't know...any of them. They want to mock me, insult the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid? They, like the Front Office, will discover quickly how I achieve my payback... Trapezoid walks off into the lot.....looking for his vehicle as we fade away, ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
