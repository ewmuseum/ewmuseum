A Plan Is Unfolding

12 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: A Plan Is Unfolding Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 5-12-2002 ..::/::..Our story begins in the living room of the Campbell's, a living room nicely furninshed with all types of exotic tapestries and items from Chinese vases to French paintings, everything in that living room having some kind of unique origin and story behind it...but we're talking about PCW's favorite superhero here, not a Ming vase thank you very much. across from the wide-screen silver Sony TV set that dominates the living room are three seats...a large black leather sofa and two matching plush chairs seated around each other with a beautiful glass table in the very center holding up a vase of fresh red roses, in full bloom as they perk up the already grandeur living room. Filling these seats are Forrester and Beverly Campbell on the sofa, the parents of Rent-A-Hero's true love and OSW starlet, Amy Campbell....the aforementioned Amy Campbell sitting in one of the black leather couches and none other than PCW's superhero extrodinare...RENT-A-HERO, in the other black leather seat. Standing before the four of them is none other than the man from the last installment of Rent-A-Hero's adventures...Glen Pierce, a multi-millionare who seems to have had a romantic past with Aimz...that relationship untold as of yet. He primps his pinstripe suit, still speaking to the four of them with Aimz looking completely detested by Glen and all he stands for as he says..::/::..Glen:'It's good to be back in the old neighborhood though, nice to see things are still the same Mr.Campbell.'Forrester:'Glen, the neighborhood certainly hasn't been the same without you..'Aimz:'Yeah your right dad...that sh**ty smell just disappeared along with him.' ..::/::..Forrester glares over at Aimz, not liking the comment at all she just made, but Aimz firmly standing by it as she folds her arms. Glen takes a sigh and leans against the eggshell white walls of their house, smiling as he looks over to Rent-A-Hero. Our hero looks back to him, consuming a small bag of Haribo Gummi Bears happily as Glen says..::/::.. Glen:'Well Rent-A-Hero, you certainly have my jealousy...you've got yourself a fine catch, you better hold onto her you lucky dog you.' Forrester:'He better be watching how he 'holds' onto her or I'm gonna crack his Kryptonite filled head...' Beverly:'FORRESTER!!' Forrester:'What dear? I'm just not gonna let nobody 'scoop out' my pumpkin...especially not condom boy over there!' ..::/::..Amy, Beverly, and Forrester break into an uproar about the comments just made, but our hero seems not to even be taking notice of their primitive yelling...even though he is the subject matter of what they are arguing about. Our hero simply bites the head off of a clear Gummi Bear before popping the rest of the chewy candy in his mouth before saying..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'GREAT SILENCES OF SPINDERELLA!! HUSH YOU ALL, arguing is incredibly bad. I'll have you know Mr.Campbell that I treat your daughter like a princess and with the utmout respect and curtosey...never would I dare disrespect her in private, around you, or aroudn anyone else...that's just NOT WIZZER sir. As for holding onto her Glen, I certainly shall...no way a catch like her is getting away from this hero. Just liek the PCW Crusierweight championship shall NEVER escape the grasp of my hands at PCW Supremacy when I score a MAJOR goal on the Golden Toe...Juan Borro. Me and him shall clash in a battle of the lightheavyweight titans and the strongest shall survive. That match is all about want...the need to want to beat the other man the most. We both have it there and if there could be two winners...I'm sure it would be that way. But there is no such thing as a 'double winner'....one man will rise above his fallen foe with that belt high overhead, while the other will look down and taste the nasty rotten Gummi Bear of defeat...I know what that taste has been TWICE...it shall not happen again for a third time. Just like in issue 838 of Superman Adventures...the third time IS the charm and I will make it so when we square off face to face, man to man and finally come to the conclusion of who the BEST PCW Crusierweight champion is and who will be the flagship of the division for after that pay-per-view...I love that belt more than my own life, and I probably will come clsoe to giving it many times just to hold onto the 10 pounds of gold that shines brightly on my waist or shoulder. That is where it has found a home and that is where it will stay, HANDS DOWN. So with the force of an Arkillian battle crusier...the strength of the Incredible Hulk, the speed of the Flash, and the intelligence of Mr.Fantastic...I will sue every tool I have to take home the ultimate 'Golden Cup' from Juan Borro...and I will have more than just a title...more than just a victory...I will have his RESPECT.'Glen:'You get off subject VERY easily don't you?' Rent-A-Hero:'Teehee...it's a gift Glen.'..::/::..Glen looks to his platinum Rolex, obviously startled by the time as his eyes widen. He looks to the Campbells and Rent-A-Hero with a look fo sincere regret, smoothing out his suit once again and addressing them in his silky smooth voice that demands respect, but does it in a calm fashion..::/::..Glen:'Well I must be off, Bill Gates needs another loan again...' *laughs like it's another ordinary ordeal on his agenda* 'Anyways, I'm having a little bash at one of my homes, it's near the beach. Why don't you and Amy make it a plan for tonight eh Rentie? I'd be honored to have you two as guests.'Amy:'A party...I like to party, but why would we-' Rent-A-Hero:'A PARTY, WIZZER!! WE'LL BE THERE LIKE A VALLEY GIRL AT THE LOCAL MALL GLEN!! It's time for Rent-A-Hero to shake his groove thang!' ..::/::..Our hero follows that spirited remark by jumping up out of his seat, running quite unrhythmically in place in his best version of the 'Running Man' as the Campbells look in, Beverly and Aimz giggling at our hero's cute antics while Forrester still continues to seeth with utter dislike for Rent-A-Hero. Glen just smiles and waves to everyone as he prepares to head out the door, graciously bowing out by saying..::/::..Glen:'Goodbye everyone, thank you for having me as it's always a pleasure. Amy, Rent-A-Hero...I expect to see you both at my party. Goodbye all.'..::/::..They all wave as Glen closes the door behind him, his smile transforming into a devilish smirk as the door closes and he heads for his car. He unearths a minuscule little cellular phone from his suit pocket, flipping the lid on it and dialing a series of numbers briskly as he heads for his car. Not even two rings pass by as someone picks up on the other end, Glen speaking in a business manner to whoever it is on the other end, serious about the plans he is detailing..::/::..Glen:'Yes...they're coming. Look Elise, just do your job. This guy is NOT gonna have Amy. I made a mistake long ago yes, but if I can't have her...NOBODY will. You are gonna be what it takes to get her to fall right back into my arms. Just work your charm and everything will work out fine, just make sure your in the room I told you about. You will be taken care of for this, I promise you. Just make it happen...what does he look like? Trust me, he's gonna be the only idiot dressed in spandex and jumping around thinking sweetened Kool-Aid is a hard drink. Elise...make it happen. See you tonight.' ..::/::..Glen hangs up his phone, a serious look of evildoing in his eyes as his chauffeur opens up the passenger door, this shrewd business man ready to carry out a plan to ruin the relationship of Amy and Rent-A-Hero...only time will tell if he can make it happen, but thanks to the miracle of RP fast-forwarding...you won't be in suspense for long..::/::..At The Beach Party... ..::/::..Loud trance music and wild screams overcome the beach, the blanket of the stars and the night sky going along perfectly with the crashing indigo waves as we focus in on the large and spacious beach house of Glen Pierce, the site of a wild party taking place. Inside, intense bumping and grinding, hard drinking, and serious mingling and flirting are taking place amongst various people, the party going way past 'wild' a few hours ago. Near the punch bowl we find none other than Rent-A-Hero, rocking his head to the beat and looking extremely suspicious as he begins pouring a strange red drink into an almost empty punch bowl from a clear plastic container. He looks about to make sure he isn't caught during this act, finally emptying the container and smiling sneakily as one of the male party-goers pulls up a cup and dips it into the mysterious drink that Rent-A-Hero has poured out. He says..::/::.. Partygoer:'What is this guy? It sure does look good...'Rent-A-Hero:'Try for yourself and be amazed by it's PLUTONIC POTENCY PARTYGOER!!' ..::/::..The man shurgs and downs the drink quickly, upon finishing it looking extremely confused as he looks to Rent-A-Hero. Our hero is still smiling and nodding his head like he has done something great as the man yells..::/::.. Partygoer:'THIS TASTES LIKE UNSWEETNED KOOL-AID MAN!'Rent-A-Hero:'MAN, YOUR RENT-A-SENSES ARE SHARP...looking for a sidekick position?' ..::/::..The man tosses the cup down and walks away angrily, Rent-A-Hero shrugging in confusion as he leaves the table, weeding through the crowds of partiers like a snake through tall grass. He probes his eyes about the party looking for Aimz, concern overcoming his eyes as he tries to look for her. Finally, he catches sight of her, talking to Glen on the outside balcony near the beach, the two of them seeming to be sharing intimate conversation away fromt he party. Our hero looks to be a little hurt by this sight, nearing the wall as he says..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Holy Privacy....why is Aimz talking to him...ALONE?! Oh well, this ain't the time to become a green monster about it...I need to just enjoy the part and not be jealous...so why am I still talking about it? Okay, stop worrying about her now....okay NOW! OKAY NOW!! GRRR!!' ..::/::..Suddenly, while he is engaged in this self argument with himself, our hero hears a loud scream from one of the bedrooms down the hall he is on. Our hero's head sharply turns behind him, his urge to save overcoming him as he exclaims..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'EDGADS~! SOMETHING'S GOING ON!! THIS LOOKS LIKE A JOB FOR....RENT-A-HERO!! AWA I G...' *Falls and trips on the floor* 'Away...I go...owwie...to the floor...owwie.' ..::/::..Our hero slowly rises back up to his feet and limps down the hallway, the scen fading as Glen can be seen from far away, smirking at the sight of our hero going down the hall. WHAT'S GOING ON?! WHAT PLAN HAS GLEN SET UP FOR OUR HERO?! FIND OUT, SAME RENT-A-TIME, SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!! ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
