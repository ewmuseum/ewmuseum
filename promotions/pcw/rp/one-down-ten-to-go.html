One down ten to go

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: today Bob Vurdo stands in a crowd of people gathered at the first press conference for the up and coming election. Peter Simmons will be attempting to convince the public to vote for him as mayor of Amber Hill with his 'innovative policies'. Bob has decided to ensure he knows everything that is going on with the election so he can be sure of victory and the crowing of Bob Vurdo as mayor of Amber Hill. Finally Peter Simmons appears on stage wearing a formal tuxedo and carrying his speech in his left hand. Peter walks over to the podium set up with a microphone and smiles at the audience. 'Hello and thank you all for coming.' A few of the crowds members applaud Peter as he prepares to deliver his speech by clearing his throat. 'Residents of Amber Hill will know who I am. I have been the mayor of this city for the last four years and I plan to continue running this beautiful city for as long as I can. However I do not plan to continue running things like they are now. I know the residents of Amber Hill love the way this place has been run but I think I can make it an even batter place by improving a few things.' Bob looks around at the crowd when he makes eye contact with an old lady standing next to him. The lady is small and wrinkled with a droopy face and thick gray hair. She smiles at Bob before taking a step in his direction to talk to him as Peter continues his speech. 'Hello.' Bob smiles reluctantly as he wants to listen to what Peter has to say instead of some old lady. 'Oh I don't know he has been running this town for a while now.' Bob pretends not to hear her but the lady continues anyway. 'You know I heard there was someone involved in the Mafia running for Mayor.' Bob turns his head in a curious fashion. 'I know I know that would be chaotic and believe me everyone is scared of it happening. I have spoken to quite a few people and it seems he won't be voted in anyway as everyone knows he's a hitman.' The lady obviously doesn't know much about the Mafia. If everyone finds out Bob is in the Mafia he will have no chance of getting voted in. Bob knows this very well and is already thinking to himself about what he can do to thwart those rumors and win the election. Sure he has the election rigged but if people become curious Bob could be put away for a long time on Corruption charges. 'So who do you think you will vote for?' The old lady continues but this time Bob doesn't care about being rude he just ignores her. 'I think I will vote for Mr. Simmons.... He hasn't done a bad job running this town that's for sure.' 'Now you may have heard rumors about the election being run by the Mafia or about me being in the Mafia but none of these rumors are true. There is however someone running for Mayor who is suspected to be involved in organized crime but I do not want to disclose his name.' Peter smiles as he looks through the crowd and notices Bob Vurdo staring angrily up at him. 'Thank you for coming here and I hope you all vote for me in the election as it is the only smart choice.' Peter then smiles at the crowd and walks back off stage as people begin leaving. Bob Vurdo however doesn't leave with the other people he just stands in the area where the audience is and waits for everyone to leave so he can have a word to Peter Simmons. Bob waits there after the rest of the people have left for a few minutes waiting for Peter to come back down stage and walk over to his car in the car park. Bob takes another look over at the car in the car park by the basketball courts. It's a red four wheel drive that has been perfectly polished and doesn't appear to have any imperfections whatsoever. Bob then takes a look at the steps that lead up onto the stage before deciding to go and find Mr. Simmons himself. Bob walks over to the stairs and takes another look up at the stage with the curtain drawn accross. Bob climbs the stairs and walks over the stage to the curtain. He opens up the curtains with two hands and looks into the back room where Peter Simmons sits curled up against the wall. He looks up at Bob in an intimidated fashion. 'Please don't hurt me.' Bob smiles an evil smile as he realizes how scared Peter is of him. 'I won't hurt you if you do something for me.' Bob takes a few slow steps toward Peter who is cowering against the wall. 'What is it I will do anything.' 'I want you to pull out of the election.' Peter pauses for a second. The election is everything to him it's all he cares about. 'You have 24 hours before...' Bob is interrupted by a car pulling up in the parking lot. Bob cautiously walks over to the curtains and pokes his head out to see a police car pulled up in the parking lot. 'Twenty four hours.' Bob steps down to the top step as the policemen get out of the car and see Bob. The men walk over to Bob as he walks toward his car in the parking lot. 'Excuse me who are you?' The police officer pulls out a notepad. 'Oh I am Bob Vurdo.... Peter is acting all weird.' The police officer nods as he jots something down on his notepad. 'He thought I was trying to kill him.' Bob laughs as the police officer smiles to Bob. 'Thanks for your time sir.' The two police officers then begin walking toward the stage which is set up in the park. Bob walks over to his black Mercedes and gets in the car. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The clock reads 7:00 on the wall above the television Bob watches as he sits on the couch puffing on his fine Cuban cigar. The opening credits begin for Amber Hill Local News as the boring news music blares through the speakers set up around the room. The scene on the television cuts to a lady sitting at the desk. 'Tonight in local news Peter Simmons has gone crazy? And has Amber Hill become over populated.' Bob laughs to himself as the news reporter continues in telling the story about Peter Simmons. 'Following Peter Simmons press conference today he called the police and claimed a 'madman' was stalking him. When police arrived at the scene they found Peter curled up in a ball shaking.' The lady takes a break for the news to sink in. 'He was then taken to the police station to cool down and when he finally did calm him down he ran out of the station and went to the council building across the road where he removed himself from the election.' The lady pauses for another moment before continuing. 'Is this pure coincidental? Has Peter Simmons gone crazy? Or is there really a madman stalking Mr. Simmons who wanted him to remove from the election.' 'The general public where quick to point the finger at the suspected Mafia involvement in the election however these are purely rumors for now.' Bob turns off the Television and smiles to himself. One down ten to go.'
