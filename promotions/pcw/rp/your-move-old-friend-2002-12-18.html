Your move, old friend

18 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/18/2002 'Ahhhhhhhhh, breathe that clear, cold, crisp air. Really feels good in the lungs,' states Jon Kano as he deeply breathes in the cold air of the frozen tundra of Northern Canada. As Kano exhales, you can see his breath rising into the air. Kano, with his Television Title draped over his left shoulder, is dressed in a parka, Carhardt pants, and a pair of heavy duty winter boots. The current PCW Television Champion is in the middle of nowhere, somewhere in Canada. There is nothing around, just the frozen white abyss. The only thing that can be seen is Jon Kano, and only then because of the contrast of his brown attire to the pure white foreground and background. 'I was born and raised in Las Vegas where the average temperature is somewhere in the nineties. So many people are surprised to hear me say that I love the cold. I thrive in it. I love it. I spent about nine months of my Army career in Alaska and Siberia, training with Russia's winter corps. Ever since then, I have been addicted to the cold harshness of winter,' reveals Kano with a slight smile across his face. Jon Kano begins to walk through the knee-high snow. 'Teresa Tomas needs to recognize just who the hell I am. I was headlining cards with Alan Kriegman in the Tokyo Dome before she even thought about becoming a wrestler. I was rolling with the likes of Tyler Nelson, Dan Hollywood, Chet King, and Chris Sloboda while she was wrestling in some hick town's high school gym on Sunday afternoons. I am not some run of the mill wrestler. I am one of a kind, there has never been someone like me nor will there be anyone like me once I am gone. I am a certified, living, breathing legend,' touts the self-proclaimed legend that is Jon Kano. Kano finally reaches a freakishly powerful looking snowmobile with a huge 'SKIDOO' logo on its sides and hood. Kano hops on it and begins to get his custom-made snowmobile gear on. 'Teresa is without a doubt talented, but she is no Jon Kano. She may have Abner, but I have a question for those two. Who is Duke going to side with?' asks Kano with a sly little look on his face. 'Duke and I have history. We have history of drinking each other underneath the table on a nightly basis. We have a history of standing side by side taking on all comers. We have a history of being legends here in PCW. He and I have had our differences, but we still have a lot of history of friendship. My question is when the chips are down and Duke has the opportunity is he going to knock my teeth down my throat? Or is he going to hesitate or not take any action at all? Teresa, I think you should have a nice long talk with our friend Duke about this because if he doesn't get involved, you stand no chance in heaven, hell, or here on earth because I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!!' warns Jon Kano as he fires up the snowmobile. Jon Kano takes off like a bat out of hell, actually pulling a wheelie, on the snowmobile until he is seen no more as he becomes just another faint object in the distance of the great white abyss.
