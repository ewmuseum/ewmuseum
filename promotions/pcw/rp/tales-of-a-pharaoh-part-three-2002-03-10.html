Tales of a Pharaoh: [Part three]

10 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/3/02 Tales of a Pharaoh: [Part three] Location: Town with no name, TexasTime: 1: 30 AM It was a bleak and hopeless looking site. Beholding it seemed useless. The place was dry and dusty and not many, apart from the odd couple, lights twinkled in the night sky. From a mile or so away it had seemed that this little village had been brightly lit but things were different close up. Now that I gazed upon the barren town my hopes, once again, we dashed… “Where am I?” I said, so long had I been out walking across the tough terrain of Texas that I had truly forgotten where I was. Everything, up until this point had looked the same… I gazed about. What hope would I find here, I had lost almost everything…Jay and Aki, my tag team title, that Akiyama had taken with him after fighting with the bikers back in the restaurant, Isiy, she was at home, back in distant California, and I hadn’t requested Carmoon to join me on this trip…I was alone, oh so alone…in the Town with no name…a place that seemed to have no motion about it, a place that seemed bleak and depressing… I breathed a sigh of heavy disbelief and then looked around again, I had no hope of viewing anything different but my instincts told me to do so. My eyes tensed and it seemed that a few miles away a tiny cloud of dust had settled over the horizon… The Bikers! I thought as I watched with strained eyes. It could only be them…it had to be them… If anyone knew the whereabouts of my two celestial friends it would be those greased up, leather-clad Mortal idiots… Without hope I began to run, shouting and crying after them…it wasn’t that I wished to find Akiyama and Jay for company, oh no! It was the fact that on Sabotage Akiyama and I were to fight Legend Inc. and his newfound Tag team partner Frisco…if Akiyama wasn’t about to aid me in defending our titles then how would I be able to compete? Not only was this a strenuous problem but also Akiyama had both our titles with him…it would seem extremely odd to go into Sabotage by myself AND bearing no sign of the titles I have to defend…what would Duke Williams say? Something obscene and foulmouthed I guessed but the point of this whole speech was that Akira was no where to be seen and I needed his help at Sabotage… I ran a few metres after the cloud and then dropped to the dusty ground on my tired knees…my cries of “stop” and “Hey you” directed after what I believed to be the bikers had seemingly woken much of the dreamy town. How did I know this? Well put it this way…after I had dropped to my knees knowing that I had no hope of catching the attention of the supposed bikers I looked up to find a man around six feet tall, wearing a pair of blue and white striped pyjamas bearing, in his hands, a… double barrel shotgun… I sighed and almost began to laugh…so much had gone wrong that this moment was comical…especially the fact that only one day ago I had been put in this very situation…a double barrel shotgun placed in my face… “Who the hell are you?” said the man. I got to my feet and bowed politely, I wished not to be shot and I was still trying to get to the arena for Sundays show, time was of the essence so I concluded that meeting this mortals demands was quite essential. “I, good sir, am The Pharaoh Titu Xansiv,” I replied with a smile, not cocky just friendly… the man, even though he still held onto his gun, placed it down by his side, but it was quite noticeable that he was still wary of me. “And what’s your business here, many strange people have passed through these parts lately but you…you look the most strange,” he said, at these word I was curious. Was he speaking of Akiyama and Jay, they truly were an odd site to behold. “What do you mean, good sir?” I started, “What do you mean “many strange people”?” The man hesitated slightly and then began to tell of a strange force that had wreaked havoc upon his village… “Just last night a strange and terrible force came upon my land. Men, and I use the word ‘men’ sparsely, cos’ they looked nothing like men I had ever seen, clad in dark brown and black leather came into our town upon beastly things; they had a look of motorbikes but they were alive! Like nothing I had ever seen I can tell ya’ know! Horrible mutant things…anyway, they came into town and trashed the place, when dawn comes you’ll be able to see the destruction caused by them…it was terrible…” he said. I looked on over the horizon, the dust had gone; already it had settled back on the ground. It must have been them…and something had happened to them on the way…but what? From the sounds of things they had been transformed into beastly fiends…a punishment from my fellow god, Akiyama? Perhaps but things weren’t clear yet, I’d have a better idea of things in the morning. My eyes were beginning to grow heavy; even us Gods need sleep you know. I turned to the men with a weary smile… “I can assure you I am not at all here to cause any kind of hate or desecration on your humble town, I only wish for a nights rest and then I shall be gone…I promise…” The man sternly looked at me, his eyes were full of fury; obviously a cause of the hate that had already been brought upon his town… “I ain’t fond of you strangers but you seem ok to rest here. Ya’ can take refuge in my house; my wife and me have a spare room that you can take lodgings in for the night. But it’ll be an early wake up call in the morning; cos’ as a repayment for my generosity of letting you stay here you can help to tidy up the village tomorrow…” I agreed to the mans proposal and followed him back to his house, a small cottage-style place just a few yards away from where he’d found me. Many people looked on from their windows as I passed by some of the lodgings in the town. Many seemed frightened, but the majority seemed angry and callous at heart. I began to feel uncomfortable, it wasn’t me who had destroyed some of their village and still they looked at me with hating eyes; it had obviously been the bikers, Mutated or not… The man led me to his house and, using a small golden key, unlocked and opened his door. I stepped inside the place to find a fairly well decorated house with a large log fire burning in the corner of the room we stood in; obviously the kitchen judging from the pots and pans hanging about the place. The man motioned for me to follow him out of the kitchen and into a long, thin lobby. A steep staircase could be made out in the distance and we moved towards it will relatively slow pace. “You said you had a wife…” I began “What’s her name?” The man turned to me with mistrustful eyes. “What is it of your business, stranger?” he replied gruffly. “I was just making conversation,” The man was quick to reply “Well don’t; strangers aren’t usually welcome about here; its lucky that I don’t see you as a trouble maker…or else you’d have had to continue your walk until you came upon the next town in these barren lands…” Sarcastically I pondered a thought to myself ‘I had always heard that Texas was hospitable and not as barren as it first appeared; obviously I had been misled…’ The man turned back from me now and continued to lead me towards the staircase. Before he reached the bottom he stopped suddenly and pointed towards the steep casing of multiple wooden steps. “You’ll find everything you’ll need up there. Just go through the door that ya’ find at the top and get inside the room; its our spare bedroom…of course you’ll find a bed, a fireplace where you can light a fire to keep ya’ warm and a small black and white TV…” I thanked him and, under the mans watchful eyes, walked towards the feet of the steps before beginning to strenuously climb them…once I reached the top I looked back down to the bottom to see the man still standing, watchful as ever. I sighed and turned back to the door before opening it and edging inside the room…it was dark, yet the moonlight gave away the shapes of the things inside. I was too tired to care for my hunger and I immediately dropped on top of the bed. The next morning I awoke covered by a thick white quilt. I sprang to my feet and gazed about the room. Not royal enough in comparison to my usual surroundings, but homely enough for my liking… I yawned and then walked slowly across the room, daring not to make a sound, towards a small glass window that looked out on the village. Wreckage lied about the streets and small sheds and such had been ripped down along with the occasion house. I turned away pondering what may have happened. Could the bikers have done this? The man had given an account of a group of creatures on the backs of mutated monsters that appeared to be motorbikes…a strange tale I know, but being the god I am I had seen my share of monstrous beasts…it could be true…yet I knew the up and coming day would give me more of an idea of the events of the previous night… I walked out of the room via the door and made my way downstairs, I was automatically hit with the strong smell of pancakes; A dish that I was not too keen on. I cringed and even went to the extent of holding my nose until I entered back into the kitchen where I thought it polite to show some form of “liking” the food that was been dished up. A youngish woman, around her early thirties, late twenties was stood cooking away, preparing a meal for her husband. I wandered into the room as she swung around to greet me… “Hello! Did you sleep well?” she said. It was highly noticeable that she had a better temperament than her husband… “Yes, thank you…” I answered. I hated being so polite to mortals but at least she was being alright with me…her husband had seemed very cautious and untrusting; even after I had greeted him graciously. “Excellent, will ya’ stay for breakfast?” I hated pancakes but I was so hungry! I had a decision to make…fast… reluctantly I agreed to eating the dreadful food…well at least one good thing would come out of all this…I wouldn’t be hungry for much longer. After I had eaten my share of the pancakes, which seemingly came along with a small portion of actually quite delicious golden syrup, the man from the night before and the young woman’s husband entered into the kitchen. He smiled kindly to his wife and then gave me a stern look. “You ready to get down to some work, boy?” he said. I smiled arrogantly “Of course!” The man grunted suspiciously. I couldn’t believe he was still wary of me; I was a God for goodness sakes! I decided that the only way I’d get his approval was to help out and clear up part of the town. Should be easy…my Godly powers would be able to aid me in the labouring task. “Come on then, Titu, was it?” asked the man. “Yes, that’s me name, but you can call me The Pharaoh, most other Mortals do…” The woman suddenly sprang up from her seat. “As in The Pharaoh from PCW?” I smiled confidently. “Yes, that would be me, why do you ask?” The woman smiled as if she’d seen her idol and walked towards me “Our Son is a big fan, but he isn’t up yet. Will you not come back and speak with him later?” she asked. “Of course I will…I’ll talk to him of my next opposition in PCW! Legend Inc. and Frisco!” I broodingly said in a hating tone. “Come on, Titu, work has to be done!” said the man, once again catching my attention. “Oh yes, come… let us go!” I bellowed in an enthusiastic voice. We left the house and entered back onto the streets of the town; I had grown accustomed now to calling it the town with no name. It certainly was a desolate place to see, and the turmoil that had taken place the night before had wrecked most of it…a small gathering of men had began to swarm to certain places that required immediate attention. I looked into the mass and suddenly a face from the past loomed against the faces of the other mortal men… Akiyama! To be continued…
