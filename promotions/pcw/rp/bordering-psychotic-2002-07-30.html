Bordering Psychotic

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30th July The last time we saw El Diablo he had just reconciled with his estranged wife Angel, with the promise of marriage counselling. Would PCW's Mexican Devil go back on his promise of marriage counselling? Or would he go through with the whole horrifying ordeal? And with a new PCW Champion being crowned at Wrestle Vessel in Xavier Kannon, El Diablo now knows who he will face if victorious on Tuesday Turmoil, and will that be a distraction when in counselling? Lets see! The scene opens up in an office of sorts, with white wallpaper and a gray carpet. The office is of medium size and has a large window at the front of the room, with a view into the city. In front of the window is an armchair, a glass table and then leant against the far wall is a couch. The chairs are of a matching set and are made out of blue cotton- at least on the top anyway. There are a few plant pots varying in places around the room, and in a the far left corner is a silver filing cabinet, which El Diablo and Angel will soon be a part of. And in the near right hand corner is a television on a stand in the upper corner, it must be there for when the counsellor has nothing to do. The office is currently empty, almost like it is mirroring the way El Diablo and Angel's marriage was. Suddenly the office door opens and in walks a large, chubby fellow. He has light brown, wavy hair and wears small spectacles attached on some thin rope so they won't fall off of him. Following behind him are El Diablo and Angel. Angel has a smile on her face, whilst El Diablo looks very unattached from her and the whole scene. He is most probably thinking about his upcoming wrestling matches. 'Ah sit down, sit down, please,' says the chubby man to El Diablo and Angel. They listen to the man and go and sit down on the couch, not next to each other but apart, leaving the middle seat on the couch free. The man then goes and sits in the armchair and pulls a notepad and pen out from his pants pocket. 'We're almost ready to begin, just let me make some prior notes,' the man says, revealing himself as the counsellor. For a counsellor he seems a little too casually dressed for El Diablo's liking, who is already looking around nervously. Another concern seems to be that the man is only in his mid thirties as Diablo seemed to be expecting a man in his late forties to early fifties. The man is dressed in loose black pants and a woollen jumper with an eagle pattern on it. Meanwhile Angel is wearing a black tube top and a black leather mini skirt with black high-heeled boots, as she wears her dyed black hair down. El Diablo though is wearing a white shirt and his typical black denim jeans and black sneakers. Oh and of course his Mexican style face paint. 'Ok, are we all ready to begin?' asks Counsellor Stephens to them both. Angel nods, while El Diablo scans the room with his nervous eyes. 'Are we ready Mr Diaz?' reiterates the Counsellor. Diablo nervously looks up at this behemoth of a man. 'Mr Diablo will do,' comments Diablo with a sinister look at being ordered to answer. Counsellor Stephens looks curiously at Angel for reasoning on Diablo's response. 'Oh, he's a professional wrestler! It's his wrestling name,' reassures Angel. 'Ahhhh,' states the Counsellor at understanding the reasoning behind his response. The Counsellor begins to add more notes to that notepad he got out at the beginning of the session. 'So, explain your troubles with me, one at a time,' states Counsellor Stephens with a mark of authority. Angel thinks for a few moments, looks up at the Counsellor and begins to respond. 'Well it all began when we first got married. I'll be honest, I didn't really love him at the time, he just needed a Visa really bad at the time, so I went through with it for him, as a friend,' answers Angel, before taking a breather. Counsellor Stephens nods and continues writing in the notepad. 'Why would you marry him for a credit card?' asks the Counsellor. El Diablo sighs loudly. 'Not a credit card, you dummy, a freaking document allowing me to work and live this country. You know like a green card!' yells Diablo at the Counsellor in anger at his stupidity. He must be wondering how Angel can be putting her trust in a man who doesn't know what a Visa is in that instance. 'Ahhh, I see,' responds Stephens whilst making more notes. Angel looks across at El Diablo before continuing with her story. 'That's when the troubles all began, and it didn't help when his hobbies got weirder,' continues Angel on her husband. 'What hobbies are they?' asks the Counsellor. Angel coughs as El Diablo sits there feeling like a vegetable as he gets talked about. 'He got really into Ancient Babylonian History and began to act rather strangely as he got more and more into it,' finishes Angel as she adjusts her hair. Counsellor Stephens looks up and at El Diablo while making more notes. This only makes Diablo more nervous, as not only is he being spoken about, he seems to be getting written about too, not in a positive way, but very negatively too it would seem. 'So what is your story, Mr, um Diablo?' the Counsellor asks whilst twiddling his thumbs. El Diablo sits up and coughs before starting. 'I think it all went wrong when Angel cheated on me with a man I was in an insane battle with,' comments Diablo with anger. He looks at Angel with malicious intent as he says this. 'You were in an insane battle with someone?' asks the Counsellor curiously. Diablo notices him making more notes about him and simply sighs. 'Yes! He was the man that caused the death of my brother! But I have closure now,' states Diablo as he stares at Angel with a look of disgust. Counsellor Stephens nods his head and scratches the top of it. 'Is that all?' asks the Counsellor impatiently. Diablo almost scoffs at one the man has said. 'Is that all?' mocks Diablo angrily. Angel simply blushes and puts her heads in her hands in embarrassment of her husband. 'She slept with another man! And you ask if that's all?' he continues on his tirade, 'Well, for your information no it's not!! She then left me for another woman!' finishes Diablo, as he moves further away from Angel on the couch. The Counsellor drops his pen and notepad on the notepad and looks at Angel in a seductive sort of way. 'Oh really?' he asks. Diablo nods slowly as he watches the Counsellor eye up his wife and trying to look at her cleavage. Angel notices this too and just smiles at the attention. 'OK!' shouts Diablo, 'THAT IS ENOUGH! WE'RE OUT OF HERE YOU SICK PERVERT!' screams Diablo as he stands up and looks down at the perverted Counsellor. Angel gets pulled up to her feet by her arm by El Diablo. All she can do is blush in embarrassment. The Counsellor backs up a little in his chair and looks up at Diablo in worry, and taking glances at El Diablo's beautiful young wife. 'WE WON'T BE COMING TO SEE YOU EVER AGAIN,' Diablo continues shouting, 'Although you can come as much, or as little as you like,' says Diablo taking a snide comment at the Counsellor. Counsellor Stephens blushes and leans out to get the notepad, but before he can get it, Diablo stamps his hand down on it and lifts it up to look at it himself. On there he discovers some scribbles of writing about him and a sketch of boobs. No doubt what he drew as he looked at Angel. As he looks over the notepad, the Counsellor looks more nervous than Diablo did to begin with. 'Mr 'Diablo' Diaz, seems like an insane man who does not deserve such a beautiful wife,' says El Diablo reading from the notepad and looking over the notepad to look at the Counsellor. 'Give it back,' says Counsellor Stephens in panic and stretching his arm out for it. El Diablo though steps back and pulls Angel back with him. 'In fact, he sounds and looks like a borderline psychotic,' continues Diablo, before throwing the notepad back to Counsellor Stephens. Angel stands behind her man and looks ready to leave. 'PERVERT!' shouts Angel to an El Diablo smile. Angel and El Diablo then turn their back on the Counsellor and walk out of the office, slamming the door behind them. 'You really do care about me!' says Angel to her husband. 'Of course,' states Diablo while staring her in the eyes, 'I'll always protect you,' Angel wraps her arms around El Diablo and begins to kiss him softly. Is this a new El Diablo we see? Is he really a borderline psychotic? And if so, will that have any bearing on his upcoming matches on Turmoil and Fusion? but most importantly to El Diablo, he has his wife and life back.
