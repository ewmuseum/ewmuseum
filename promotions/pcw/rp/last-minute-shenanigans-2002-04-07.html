Last Minute Shenanigans

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/07/02 The camera fades in… The Chef sits behind a makeshift desk in front of the camera. Behind the Chef is a flag displaying the International Incident logo (which contains a burning “I2” text separating an already burning Old Glory). The Chef coughs violently before the camera, holding his hand up to block the flow of mucus to the desk. He then wipes the sweat from his forehead and begins… The Chef: Greetings fellow gastronomers. I don’t want to take up too much of your time tonight, since I know that Burt Reynolds is featured on tonight’s episode of the X-Files. I just want to talk about a few things before my match tonight against Big Alan Steele. There will be no Bertha tonight in this promo, so if you are looking for her you might as well just… “HELLO F*CKO!!!” The Chef: Bertha!?!? What are you doing here!?!? Bertha: Just f*cking giving this sh*tty @$$ bland as f*ck promo some mother f*cking spice! Bertha dances before the camera. The fluid movements of her blubber are practically hypnotic. The Chef: Must…resist…urge…to…look……don’t…want…to…believe…I’m…a…chicken…again… The Chef shakes his head, dispelling any visual memories of the wavy flab. The Chef: Moving on, I would just like to say to Big Alan Steele that… Bertha: Look at this, I can do a f*cking cartwheel, b*tch @$$! Bertha takes a step back and then lunges forward, performing a somewhat adequate version of the cartwheel. Unfortunately her flab distinctly obeys the law of gravity and begins to sag to her knees as she wraps up the cartwheel. Camerman: MMMPPHHH!!! I think I’m going to wretch… The Chef: Bathroom’s the third door on the left… The camera begins to tilt back down towards the ground as the cameraman rushes to his sanctuary away from Bertha. The Chef walks up and tilts the camera back up so it faces the desk properly. He then walks back over and sits back down, ready to address his Islamic Militant fans.The Chef: Ok, now where was I before I was so rudely, crudely, and unacceptably interrupted? Bertha jumps in front of the camera again. Bertha: Hey f*ck face! Pull my mother f*cking finger! The Chef: I dunno, we wouldn’t want any more acid rain to hit the city… Bertha: Yeah, that’s f*cking true! Bertha pulls her hand back and begins whistling the Classical Theme to the PBS show Masterpiece Theater. The Chef: Excuse me!?!? Bertha: What’s up f*ck daddy? The Culinary Conquistador angrily answers Big B. The Chef: Ahem, I was the one that staged this promo! I am trying to get something off my chest and vent my frustrations to the world! The Chef slams his fist on the table. Bertha: Oh, you are gonna f*cking talk about your b*tch a$$ sh*tty f*cktastic wee-wee size, ain’t ya f*cko!?!? The Chef: I’m not going to talk about… The Chef pauses to ponder Bertha’s response. The Chef: Why did you bring up the subject up my…um…cooking utensils…yeah that’ll do…when I said I needed to vent my frustrations? Bertha: Oh, I didn’t f*cking mean THAT, if you were thinking I was mother f*cking @$$ humping insinuating about your small @$$ Mongolian sh*t bird c*ck… The Chef slaps his forehead. The Chef: Bertha, just leave… Bertha holds up her hand as if to tell the Chef to “Talk to the Hand, cause Bertha don’t f*cking understand”. Bertha: Sure, I’ll f*cking go, but I’ll be taking the sh*tty as f*ck queer @$$ party with me, ho skank! Bertha walks away and the Chef sighs a concerned but ultimately satisfied sigh. The Chef: Since Pandora is gone, and taken all the evils of the world with her, let’s get down to business shall we? Suddenly the door bursts back open. Pimply Faced Intern Kid: Hey Chef, it’s time for your match! Get going! The Chef sighs again. The Chef: But…but…but…? Pimply Faced Intern Kid: NOOOOOOOOWWWWW!!! The Chef jumps back and rolls up into a quivering little ball like a terrified Chihuahua. The Chef: OK…I’m coming… The Chef shakes his head in disbelief. He’s a PCW Grand Slam Champion, and some little punk with more acne than all of the American high school bands combined is bossing him around like he was a nobody, or even worse, Scott Naket… The Chef faces the camera and leaves the viewer with this message. The Chef: Adios Enemigos… Tonight is the night for Allah’s Champion to take that step forward… Pimply Faced Intern Kid: AHEM!!! The Chef hurries to pick up his rolling pin and kitchen knife. He then scurries out the door, with PFIK following behind him. The camera fades to black…
