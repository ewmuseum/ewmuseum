The End Of The World As We Know It?

4 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The End Of The World As We Know It? Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 04/10/02 “Need....... More......... Snapple~!“ Our shot opens up on the tired eyes of PCW’s preppy Commissioner, Eleanor Hall as they stare through her Black rimmed glasses.... The reflection of the work happening around her reflecting in the lenses. Eleanor : “Need... Top... Up... Evil... Super... Genius... Powers!” The wife of PCW’s Champion looks weary after a full day... Well, a few hours... Of actually having to do something. Eleanor : “Snapple~!.... Machine..... Near...” She slowly walks over to one of the new and bizarre contraptions that are dotted around her Super Lab far under the surface of Hubbardville, Wisconsin. On the top of it are a series of buttons. Eleanor : “Ellie~!.... Lack... Strength...” Her hand slowly rises towards the panel, even this looking an effort. Eleanor : “Ellie~!... No.... Like... Gravity....” With her left arm she tries to help lift her right.. The Pink and Silver varnished nails getting closer to panel right. Eleanor : “Damn... That... Guy... Who... Was... Hit... By... The... Apple... For... Inventing.... Gravity....” Slowly but surely her hand creeps up to the panel, the prep looking unsteady on her feet as the Evil Super Genius prep powers fade from her body. Eleanor : “So... Tired... Haven’t... Said.... Like... Or... SO.... Once!” Finally her hand reaches up to the height and drops down onto the panel. Eleanor : “Snapple~!... Near.... Then... Ellie~!.... Have... Power!” With all the remaining strength she can muster, she presses the buttons on the panel.. Well, TRIES to press the buttons but she doesn’t have enough force for it to register. Eleanor : “LIKE.... HELLO?!?!?? You are, like, SO my machine!” With a sudden burst of frustrated energy she kicks the machine and slams her little hands down onto it before collapsing over the device.... The 4 hour day sapping every last bit of strength from her little body. Eleanor : “Ellie~!... Too.... Preppy... To..... Die!” She turns her head despairingly to the left as she sees someone approaching, their shadow being cast over the little prep’s spent body as it lays draped over the machine. Eleanor : “YOU!... Ellie~!.... Snapple~!“ “I’m pleased to see you too, my little Poon Puppy!” The camera pans away from the Commissioner to shown a confused Xavier Kannon looking at his nearly sleeping wife with his bright Green eyes. Xavier : “By Hubbard, you look exhausted. What have you been up to?” With her long, Blonde hair hanging over her face, she turns her head and just manages to lift it up to stare back through the dense forest of Blonde strands at her husband. Eleanor : “Been... Awake... Six.... Hours.... No.... Nap!” A concerned look crosses Xavier’s face. Xavier : “In Xenu’s name... Are you trying to kill yourself!” From under the veil of hair Ellie~! snaps. Eleanor : “Just... Like... Get.... Snapple~!“ The Scientologist shakes his head and pulls his wife off the machine, lifting the little prep up in his arms as he presses the button marked “Snapple~!“ on the machine and from out of nowhere, a bottle of Mango Guava comes into existence. Eleanor : “Power... Surging.... In... Ellie~!” The tired eyes of Ellie~! now begin to sparkle in their pale Blue wonder, glowing from under the mane of Blonde hair covering her face and her whole body shakes as Kannon undoes the bottle. Xavier : “You know, my little Fuck Ferret... This is kinda worrying!” He pulls the hair from over her face and slowly moves the opening of the bottle to her Pink lips and pours.. The sugary liquid dribbling into her mouth with immediate effects. Eleanor : “That is, like, SO better!” As more Snapple~! is drunk, her Evil Super Genius powers are topped up, and the high concentrations of dissolved sugar give her instant energy. Eleanor : “Now I can TOTALLY execute PLAN E!” With seemingly limitless energy, the prep leaps from her man’s arms... Not a good idea as she has to land in high heels but Kannon quickly reaches out and corrects her stagger. Xavier : “There were plans A, B, C and D?” The prep straightens herself up and once again ties her hair in a neat bob behind her head. Eleanor : “E stands for ELLIE~!“ The Scientologists nods and lets out an Ahhhhhhhhhhh. Eleanor : “Because, that’s, like... My name, and stuff.” With a huge whirring noise, a video screen lowers from the ceiling and covers the OPERATION, LIKE, KILL THAT CLYDE GUY.. AND STUFF! banner. Kannon looks on in amazement at what his wife can do with the power of Snapple~!. Xavier : “I’ll never understand all this...” On the screen is shown the planet Earth from space. Eleanor : “I’ll, like, go slowly for you seeing as you SO aren’t an Evil Super Genius like me...” Xavier raises his eyebrows at his wife’s words but she is already scurrying over to a console and pressing a glittery Pink button which sets of an alarm and a computerised voice starts to speak. 10... 9... Eleanor : “I found out that Clyde, like, live here!” Not being too specific she just points to the image of Earth. 8... 7... Eleanor : “On a planet called EARTH!” 6... Looking at her with a completely blank expression, Kannon mutters under his breath. Xavier : “She can form objects from stray matter.... And still manage this.... That scares me.“ 5... Eleanor : “Now, we, like, want Clyde dead.... So, I’m blowing up his home planet!” The Scientologist desperately waits for the hint that it’s a joke. 4... 3... And keeps waiting... Still, nothing but a happy grin. Eleanor : “Cool, my TOTALLY great idea has you speechless!” 2... 1... LIFT OFF!!! Eleanor : “Two.... One... Lift Off!” The whole complex then shakes as on all the monitors around the lab a rocket is shown shooting off into space... Kannon’s eyes watching them in horror as she repeats the same thing under his breath. Xavier : “Hubbard help us.... Hubbard help us..... Hubbard help us....“ On the huge view screen the rocket is shown reaching orbit then breaking off it’s casing to show one huge battery. Eleanor : “That’s, like, a gun and stuff!” On the screen the tip of the weapon starts to glow Red with a huge build up of power. Xavier : “Hubbard help us.... Hubbard help us..... Hubbard help us....“ Ellie~! looks to him, giggly at the impending death of Clyde... And the World. Eleanor : “HELLO!?!?! I, like, SO don’t need Hubbard’s help... That thing will slice the Earth in two!” Xavier covers his eyes as the mass of Red energy build up and is released..... Eleanor : “Um, like, WHERE IS IT GOING!“ As the Scientologist peers out through his fingers the thick Red beam shoots off well wide of the planet much to Ellie~!’s disapproval and Kannon’s relief. Xavier : “Would you look at that? It missed... Thanks, L. Ron!” Ellie~! bounces on the spot, flapping her arms about in frustration. Eleanor: “Dammit! Dammit! Dammit! Dammit! Like... SO... DAMMIT!“ She kicks the console then bounces on the stop holding her foot. Eleanor: “Eeeeeeeeep... Need more Snapple~! to make bigger gun!” She goes to scurry back to the Snapple~! materialiser but Xavier cuts her off and picks her up. Xavier : “I think it’s time I cut you off...” The Scientologist heads to the exit, Ellie~! flapping around in a desperate attempt to get free as the shot fades to Black. “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
