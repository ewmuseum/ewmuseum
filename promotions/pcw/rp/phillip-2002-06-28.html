Phillip

28 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-28-02 Phillip Heaven Rayne appears strolling alongside of the in ground pool in her cousin, Erika’s backyard. She sports her turquoise-blue bikini and matching flip flops, wearing her long blonde hair tied back in a bun, with her brown sunglasses resting on top casually. With her new tan forming, Heaven looks like a golden brown beauty, the color of her skin highlighting her bright,cornflower blue eyes. Heaven is heard laughing as the scene focuses in on her talking loudly with her cousin. Erika is tall with shoulder length, jet black hair, almond brown eyes, and tanned skin. She has always been a raving beauty. She sports a skimpy, hot pink bathingsuit. “Ahhhh,” shrieks Erika, “You’re getting my hair wet, Heaven!” She stands in the pool, giggling as Heaven walks along, pulling a raft towards the ladder, and splashing Erika intentionally as she goes. The two of them always have such a good time when they are together. However they haven’t seen each other in quite some time. Heaven and Erika are the same age, and when they were in the seventh grade, Erika’s mother lost a long fought battle of Terminal Cancer. Erika was stricken with grief and her father even more so, to the point of being incapable of coping. Consequently, Erika moved in with Heaven and her parents. They had a blast, and Heaven somehow made Erika forget the pain for a while, until one day when she woke up, she found it had dulled slightly. The girls became so close that when three years after Erika moved in, her father was stable and wanted her to come back, it was extremely heart breaking. By then, they were about to go into the tenth grade. At first they tried to keep in touch, a few cards and letters, and one long distance call, but sooner than later they drifted away like the leaves in the fall. That was a little over four years ago, and oddly enough, Erika and her new husband just moved into the same neighborhood as Heaven. They were delighted to discover, and almost instantly became tight again. Heaven climbs onto the silver raft and lays back, and sliding her sunglasses down and over her eyes, as she attempts to increase her sun tan. Erika goes under water and swims underneath of Heaven’s raft, submerges from the water, turning Heaven face down in the pool. Heaven goes down surprised, and comes up, coughing, spitting water out of her mouth, and gives Erika an annoyed face. Erika finds it funny and is laughing hysterically. Eventually Heaven laughs too. She is in such a good mood because she is with her cousin again, that it is hard for anything to anger her. Plus she was away from Erika for so long that it is difficult to get cross with her. The girls’ laughter gets louder and obnoxious as the two begin wrestling in the water, splashing and kicking, acting like they did when they were thirteen. Unexpectedly, the gate to the wooden privacy fence is thrust open, and a tall, lean, pale man with strawberry blonde hair appears, standing in his green swim trunks. “Hi there, beautiful,” he says to Erika. “And who is this?” He asks smiling and referring to Heaven. As soon as he entered the backyard, Erika’s smile evaporated, and she crossed her arms as if in an effort to cover her breasts, from the way they were popping out of her tiny pink bikini. Heaven thought this strange that she would do such a thing, in front of whom she assumes is Erika’s new husband. “Phillip, this is my cousin, Heaven. We grew up together.” “Hi,” Phillip says, “I’m Erika’s brother in law.” “Nice to meet you.” Heaven says, but she is surprised. She was expecting him to say that he was Erika’s husband, since he has just called her beautiful, and considering the way he keeps looking at her like she’s a chocolate cake. “Erika, can I go inside?” asks Phillip. “Sure,” she answered, a bit uneasily. “Can you join me for a second? I’d like to talk to you for a moment.” “No Phillip, I cannot,” she spits back, suddenly becoming angry. Heaven looks surprised, and Phillip’s face turns crimson, as he looks embarrassed now. “Ok, well, if you change your mind, I…I’ll just be inside,” he stutters before turning and going up the back porch and into the house. As soon as he is out of sight, Heaven turns to look at Erika in question, who is looking with her head down. “What’s the deal with that guy? Why did he just come by your house, and why did you get so upset with him?” “Did you see the way he was looking at me? Did you hear him call me “Beautiful?” Ugghhh…that guy makes me sick to my stomach!” “Where is your husband, what’s his name again. I want to meet him.” “Gavin will be home in about two hours. I can’t wait for him to get home, because you can meet him, and so that Phillip will leave.” “Well, he’s Gavin’s brother, why would he leave when he gets here? Why does he come here when Gavin isn’t home?” “Heaven I have to talk to you...as close as we were before I left Vegas, I know I can trust you.” “What is it, Eri? You can trust me.”“Oh Heaven, it was awful,” she cries, her tone of voice suddenly changing and turning into a tone of fright, tears pouring down over her cheeks now. “Erika, I have a feeling you are about to tell me something very bad about Phillip, something that would make him furious if he found out you were telling me. He’s still here, inside your house. Let’s just get in my car and go for a drive, while you tell me. Ok?” Erika agrees and the two of them get into Heaven’s silver car, driving away.
