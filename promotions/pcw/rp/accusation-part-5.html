Accusation, part 5

Date: Thursday 26th June :: The detective picked up the receiver of the phone and placed it into the now sweaty palms of Sivasian Krikorian. Crusty and dried blood laid on his hand, but he received it with pain. The detective pushed his face up to Sivasian’s. Krikorian could uncomfortably see the spit coming through his teeth and mouth. Sivasian swallowed hard, his throat hadn’t been watered in hours and he was hungry as anything now. Maybe the detective was trying to beat him into submission. :: Detective: You have ONE call, and only one call sir, if the person isn’t in, you defend yourself at the trial. :: Sivasian nodded with his bright blue eyes meeting the detectives and took over the receiver. He hadn’t seen his friend for some 15 years, but again assumed they were still thoroughly friends. The Italian he had met in the Ukrainian army was now a lawyer in America, he had made millions, but sometimes Sivasian felt as though he was thoroughly jealous of his state as an idol back in the Ukraine, when the Italian, now a Ukrainian citizen, and had been for 25 years, barely got any recognition. Sivasian dialed the number he remembered of a luxury millionaire flat . The detective leaned forward and spoke into Sivasian’s ear. :: Detective: You had better hope that he’s in, otherwise, say hello to Mr.Jail! :: Sivasion shakes his head and tries to ignore the detective as he hears the ringing tone. Indeed, could he be heading off to jail, could his wrestling career and probably his life be wasted on this one phone call. Things were shady however, even if he did pick up. His ‘friend’ who he hadn’t seen for at least 15 years was in the Ukrainian army with Sivasian, and then went on to take a law course and become a major lawyer over in America. The phone rang as the rest of the room fell silent. Sivasian’s heart missed a beat when a click came on the other end, had the police service in Iraq hung his call up. Suddenly though, a thick , slightly annoyed Italianised English accent came on the other side. :: Reciever: Ello, ow can I elp you? If you want a meeting about a case, please leave your name und number, and I vill note it down. :: Sivasian’s throat felt dry, but he wiped saliva from his mouth and spoke. :: Sivasian: Allesandro! You must help me, please! :: A few seconds silence from the other side of the phone as Allesandro tries to recognize the voice. :: Allesandro: Sivasian, is that you? Sivasian: Yes my friend! I need you to help me to help myself, so to speak. Allesandro: And what is the problem? :: Sivasian takes a second to think about wording the situation right. :: Sivasian: Look, I know we’ve had our spats in the past, you’re wife ran away with me, but I think you still owe me a favour, remember back in the field in 81? :: Indeed, Allesandro remembered and owned his friend a favour. A young rebel was running towards a young and injured Allesandro, when suddenly a blazing, last minute shot from the rookie hands of Sivasian Krikorian had saved the day! :: Allesandro: I remember, and I DO owe you a favour, what is it I can do for you, what’s the case. Remember, we’re still icy after the whole wife thing. :: Sivasian nodded, even though Allesandro couldn’t see hi, he understood his frustration and annoyance. :: Sivasian: That is fine, I’m in Iraq, in a military camp , being somewhat held hostage. :: The detective nodded towards a security guard who raises a large blunt object over a weakened Sivasian’s head. Sivasian gulps and rephrases. :: Sivasian: I’m being held fairly and appropriately, can you help me? Allesandro: Can I help you? Why, I’m the greatest lawyer in the world! Sivasian: You’ll do it then? :: Another slight pause, this time for Allesandro to light another cigarette. :: Allesandro: Of course I will, why wouldn’t I do it for an old friend? Sivasian: Oh great, you’re my saviour Allensandro, I’ll see you soon then, the trials in three days. Allensadro: I’ll be there, trust me. :: Sivasian sighs a big sigh of relief as he places the receiver for the phone down. :: Detective: You’re lucky, VERY lucky. Usually no one answers or can be bothered to come to Iraq, but you won’t be lucky in the court, you’ve got the hardest judge, and so much evidence against you, you can’t possibly win, even with the greatest lawyer! :: A large crackling evil laugh comes from the detective as another security guard comes in and escorts Sivasian from the room. ::
