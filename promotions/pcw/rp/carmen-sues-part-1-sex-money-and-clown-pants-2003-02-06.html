Carmen Sues - Part 1 - Sex, Money, and Clown Pants

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 6, 2003 [Arrogant, Greedy, and Selfish are quite the adjectives to describe the current PCW Continental Champion. Clueless, Dumb, and Pooface are other adjectives used by people to ALSO describe PCW's Continental Champion. His blonde locks of hair sold for five thousand dollars on E-bay, and so did the candid 'Tommy/Carmen Electra' bedroom secrets tape. Carmen is seeking lawsuit, but Tommy only seeks more footage. He received one of those bogus pieces of paper from one of those lawyer dudes. He's supposed to go down to court and maybe have one of those heinous plea bargaining things. Tommy's lawyer, Buster Snyder, has come up with a most bodacious game plan that will not only get Tommy out of the rut he's in, but it will get him back in the good graces of Carmen. And when that happens, it's Bedroom Secrets Part 2: Bodacious Bed of Roses. After the telephone conversation with Buster, Tommy can only smile and plan a gnarly evening with Carmen. His blonde hair pressing against the leather passenger seat of his 2002 Mustang convertible. The California air rushing on his tanned face as his smile suddenly fades into a look of fear...] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: HARRY!! WATCH OUT FOR THE TRACTOR TRAILER, DUDE!! ['Heavy Metal' Harry, Tommy's manager and brother, barely swerves the Mustang in time as not to kill them, ending a bodacious wrestling legacy. Harry's bloodshot eyes are wide with fear as his long brown hair whips around like tentacles. Harry is a stoner, a large one at that. He coped with the fact at a young at, 5 to be exact, and he's dedicated his life to making sure he's the best there is. Second to that is his managing skills. There are great managers, and legends, but Harry would like to go down as the ultimate. The heavy metal rocking stoner who got his half brother, Tommy, to the biggest prize of them all...The PCW Heavyweight Championship. As Harry swerves past the other drivers on the road speeding like a madman he's coping with a few facts right now. Number One: Rent-A-Douche Bag is STILL a thorn in their side. Number Two: Tommy is a supervillain and not even OTHER supervillains like each other. Number Three: Harry's usually too stoned to come up with a far out plan to even take care of the other two problems] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Sorry, man. TTT: Dude...I forgot that you can't drive. Harry: I drive my golf cart all the time, man. TTT: Well, that's totally different from my most excellent Mustang. Besides, we'd never make it to the courthouse in time with that thing. [Harry turns to his brother and has a look of a scalded child] Harry: But...it has a new engine in it, man. Plus, it's got cool new Twisted Sister decals! TTT: HARRY, WATCH OUT!!! [Harry turns and they are on a collision course with a Los Angeles bus. Harry swerves left and ends up on a collision course with pedistrians on the sidewalk. Shoppers just minding their own business about to get run over by a stoned loser. Tommy nabs the wheel and swerves them back onto the road, but now they are heading for a construction zone. The only thing that the two brothers can do is scream like little girls. A PCW Champion and a heavy metal rocker screaming like two teenage girls watching a horror movie. The car hits a dirt pile and flies over the construction zone, the workers watching on in amazement. The Mustang floats through the air with grace and speed. The two men still screaming like little cheerleaders. The car clears the construction site and slams onto the road and spins out into a parking space, directly in front of the Los Angeles courthouse. The screaming stops, the two brothers peel themselves from the leather, and then Tommy commences to place two quarters into the parking meter] TTT: Give me the keys, dude. Harry: Ok, man. [Angrily, PCW's resident super villain takes the keys from his brother and strolls toward the gothic-like entrance of the courthouse. Tommy needs to seek a bathroom immediately. He was too scared to soil his pants and soon the fear will set in...that's when he'll soil himself. Unfortunately, Harry already did that and he's seeking a new pair of pants] Harry: Hey, bums. [Harry approaches two homeless men, looking at a pair of pants that were thrown away by some really rich idiot. The two bearded bums look to each other and begin to take more of a hold on the pants] Harry: Those are really nice pants, man. Bum # 1: They are mine! Harry: Of course, man. But I'll give you fifteen bucks for them. [The bums pause for a few moments with no emotion on their faces. Harry can't take his eyes off of the pants which he hasn't gotten a really good looking at, but he just knows that they are pants] Bum # 2: Fifteen eh? Harry: You know what, man. I'll make it twenty since your Canadian. [The two bums look at each other with confusion and then look back to the stoned man with the piss in his shorts] Bum # 1: Deal. [Harry tosses them the money and they toss him...clown pants. Harry stares at them for a few moments as the bums run down the street in celebration of their new found wealth. See, Harry didn't give them a twenty dollar bill, he gave them a stack of one hundred dollar bills that TOmmy gave him this morning] Harry: FAR OUT, MAN!! [Inside the courthouse Tommy has already begun consulting with his lawyer, Buster Snyder. The thirty something dirty lawyer is only 5'2' and weighs 260lbs. He's a fatty without a doubt but the guy can play a bodacious round of volleyball! Buster has his brown hair spiked upward really high as if he wear a Japanese Anime character and his red silk suit only makes him look like a silky tomato. Tommy decides to ignore the totally bogus look of his lawyer and get right down to business. He wants to go over what is going to happen, and exactly how things will be done. He doesn't want to look stupid in front of other people, even though it happens every single day. Tommy still can't believe Carmen would do something like this. The entire free world already knows that the babe is a total hose hound and will have sex with anything! Plus, the babe makes enough radical dough to support herself, like a couple thousand benjamins is going to really miss out. SHE HAS ENOUGH MONEY! Well, so does Tommy but he's the one who took twenty minutes out of his day to post that on E-bay, ship it, and collect his money. HE'S on that tape too. It's not like she was the only person who appeared there. Harry even makes a breif appearance...well, that's because he wanted still shots for his 'personal' collection, but it's not like Carmen was the only person. Sheesh, women and their totally heinous pride] TTT: Buster, what exactly is going to go down in there? Buster: Well, Thomas... TTT: TOMMY! Buster: ...Tommy, her lawyers are going to bitch on and on about some copyright non sense. About how she was a victim and how emotionally traumatized she is...then...they'll probably say your some kind of sex fiend who has mental issues. We all know that's a farce. [Tommy seems uneasy and takes his eyes away from the fat Tomato of a lawyer] TTT: Of course, dude. Buster: Then I'll nail the SOB's! I'll talk about her former relationships with certain SINGERS and ROCK STARS! And how she bled them dry like a WICKED black widow spider. I will demand that they drop any kind of charges against you because, quite honestly, you can beat the living poo out of them. They'll be up against the ropes... [Buster jumps back and forth, trying to impersonate a boxer, but his fat just jiggles up and down. Tommy looks away from the Anime Tomato just so he doesn't lose his gnarly breakfast that he had] Buster: ...and we'll go for the knockout blow. I will announce that you DID NOT know that the camera was there. That some tourist had it planted in there, sold it under YOUR name, thus framing you for something you really didn't do. In fact, we'll try to prove that you didn't have sex with her in the first place. That some cult cloned you, planted your clone in the bedroom while you were off helping an old lady across the street... TTT: Buster, I'm a super villain...I don't help heinous old ladies across the street. Buster: Oh...well, I'll improvise on that. But, your solid. You won't have to say a thing and you'll be out of here in no time. TTT: Good. The faster the better, dude. I have to get back to the beach house for some totally bodacious training! I've got an eight man tag match on Fusion and I need my head in the right place. I'm going to be teaming with Walden, Violence Jack, and that black dude. Buster: Dante Jackson... TTT: YEAH! Dante Jackson! And we're going against Teresa Tomas, Skylar 'I'm not really blind' Green, Venom Penishead... Buster: Venneman Pendagram. TTT: Whatever...and, of course, the most bogus of them all. The totally bogus loser of them all, Rent-A-Jerkface!! UGH! That guy makes me so mad! He can't take the fact that I totally kicked his ass at Shanghaied and now he expects rematches and stuff. It's like...can a totally bodacious super villain catch a break here?! I GUESS NOT! [Harry walks up to the two guys with a large smile on his face. The Anime Tomato and Tommy stare at Harry oddly] Buster: You look like a heavy metal, white MC Hammer. [Harry looks down at the shiny clown pants, which are extremely baggy, and then looks back up at a man who has insanely spiked hair] Harry: You look like Goku from Dragonball Z, man....only fatter. Buster: I hate you too. [Buster escorts Tommy toward the plea bargaining room as Harry just looks at Buster with confusion. Harry shrugs and walks with the men as his pants swish as he takes a step. They enter the room to only be confronted with something that they did not expect. Buster notices the smug, handsome Johnathan Roberts...super lawyer. He's won cases that many other lawyers would have lost. The sly lawyer makes sure that he gets his man in the end, and Buster has NEVER won anything against him. Not even in Dragonball Z trivia!! Tommy can't believe that he's seeing Carmen Electra, the slut hottie, with Kelly Slater at her side!! Kelly...the professional surfer who tried to take Tommy's mantle of surfing God from him!! That BITCH!] Kelly Slater: Dude, you look like MC Hammer. [Harry smiles and nods to Tommy, who can only look on in shock] To Be Continued...
