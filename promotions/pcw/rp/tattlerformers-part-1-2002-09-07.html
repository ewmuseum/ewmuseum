Tattlerformers - Part 1

7 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 7th 2002 Millions of years ago, in the Earth's infancy, life had already originated on other planets. Life that was not composed of flesh and blood, but of freezing cold steel and electrical emotions. These beings have crash-landed on earth, bitter rivals whose war with each other has driven them all into an accidental landing on a pupating Earth. One legion seeks power and destruction, the other bears the demand for peace and protection. The heinous Deceprimetimers, led by the sinister and oil-thirsty Nerezzatron wage war against the righteous and good Oldskoolbots and their commander, Gazimus Prime. Regardless of their chosen loyalties, they are robots in disguise. They are more than meets the eye. There are Tattlerformers... The year is beyond humanity's birth. A time when Dinosaurs are mere newts and plant life has only just begun. Across the sky, a blazing slash of light plummets towards a volcano, currently in the middle of erupting and spewing masses of ash and smoke into the air. It ploughs into the side and rips through the layers of rock and lava, hurling magma and debris around as it buries itself at the base, covered and smothered in an orange ocean. Inside, the battle still wages... 'They've broken through the final barrier!' Yells a green coloured robot, whose body is angular and almost totally lacking curves of any kind. 'Oldskoolbots, prepare for battle!' As the words leave his face, the dull grey interior is lit up by a blaze, with the security door flying from the last defensive obstacle and embedding itself into the computer screen at the cockpit. As the smoke clears, a purple monstrosity walks in wielding a gigantic pair of glistening daggers that seems to be composed of light. 'Give up, Gazimus Prime! This ship is ours! Surrender now and you may retain your sparks!' he roars. 'Never, Nerezzatron! I'll give this up over my dead chassis!' Prime replies, pulling a huge rifle from a compartment on his leg. Through doors at the side, a group of Oldskoolbots rush in, all brandishing futuristic weapons and aiming towards Nerezzatron. As if on cue, a squadron of Deceprimetimers swarm to the aid of their leader, lining up their crosshairs with Gazimus. A stand off situation ensues when suddenly, the ship's damaged computer kicks in, talking in a distorted female voice. 'Warning! Warning! Status Lock will now commence!' 'What? No, NOT NOW!' the violet Deceprimetimer yells, rushing towards his nemesis. His run is cut short as Gazimus steps forward, taking the giant down with an extended arm. As the two brawl, their armies remain still, awaiting the command. Prime grips the wrist of Nerezzatron and slings him into a wall, just before the entire ship radiates a red warning light that causes everyone on board to howl in pain and collapse. Gazimus is forced to his knees and Nerezzatron falls flat on his face as another attempt at a charge ends in failure. 'Status Lock completed. Anomaly - life forms adapted to planet detected. Scanning alternate modes now. Fzzzt.... scanning... scannning... scannnnnnnnning......' the computer says, displaying a hundred and one different DNA helixes and chemical composure on the screen. Nobody is moving a gear... Four million years later... '...ing. Scanning complete. Status lock removed. All systems operational.' Around the ship, everyone is still coming around. Lights flicker as diagnostics run and rebooting takes place. The two immense behemoths that started the battle slowly rise to their feet, this time with the recovering Nerezzatron standing up first. Catching site of his enemy, he begins to shake out the loose connections and gestures towards the still stunned Gazimus. 'Prepare to enjoy your last few seconds, Prime! I shall.... what the....?' he croaked, looking at his arm, which now had a huge set of claws at the end instead of his daggers. His chest bears the head of a vampire bat and his legs have metallic wings protruding from the back. Looking down at his foe, he does a double take as Gazimus now has a set of huge wheels on the back of his legs and the chest now seems like the cab of a truck. 'What in the name of Cybersextron?' he yells 'All surviving Tattlerformers have been adapted to earth technology and life-forms. Inadaptable subjects have been protoformed for further use due to anomalies.' the ship's computer says. Slowly, around the two leaders, the few Tattlerformers who were able to undergo the adaptation began to stand, while at the front of the ship, a stack of what looked like cube shaped boxes sat - the essence of the Tattlerformers that couldn't be adapted at the time. One by one, they stand to their feet... 'Rebooting systems for Mingstro, Stimpotron, Endlock and Hotimo Rod.' As these heroes assemble, the ship's computer speaks again, just as the Oldskoolbots come online... 'Now rebooting: DevilleTron and Mortificus.' 'I have only two soldiers?' Nerezzatron remarked. 'Gah... men! Grab some protoforms, we cannot allow the scales to be tipped in their favour! CHAAAAAARGE!' The three Deceprimetimers rush forward towards the blocks, shielded now by the quintet of Oldskoolbots. 'You're not getting your hands on these, Nerezzatron!' Gazimus proclaims. The two rivals meet up and lock into a collar-to-elbow tie up, grappling each other for possession of the stored soldiers. Devilletron, an intimidating red and black robot steps forward and removes a pitchfork from behind his back, twirling it in his hands and aiming it at one extremely short robot that hovered in the air and a rather camp looking machine with dragon wings sprouting from its back. The flying one pointed at the crimson and quirked a metallic eyebrow. 'Who the devil are you?' he squeaks. 'THIS is the devil I am... DEVILLETRON! TRANSFORM!' With that, plates of red armor clamp around his body and the pitchfork's points develop panels that transform it into a spade shape. The fork attaches to his lower spine and two horns spring out from his head. Finally, massive claws swing around from his hands and he roars at the two blocking him. 'That's nothing... Stimpotron - TRANSFORM!' the tiny one shouts, merely spinning his body around once to face away while his arms and legs turn the opposite direction. His face is now a rosy-cheeked grin and in his hand is a small energy wand, fizzing with electrical sparks at the top. 'And if you liked that... Mingstro! TRANSFORM!' yells the pink partner, as a dragon's head bites down over his. The wings double in size and his legs combine to form a tail. Finally, his hands disappear inside his arms and punch the ground in unison to form his new legs. Devilletron runs forward with his horns lowered, roaring. The two Oldskoolbots stand their ground... ... ... before running like cowards. On the other side of the ship, a black and silver metal Tyrannosaurus Rex guards the cubes while besides him, a brightly coloured and shiny robot points an arm full of laser cannons at the menacing looking green machine in front of them. A snake's face sits on his chest and his wrists seem to have circular domes on the sides like the end of a rattlesnake's tail. 'May asssssss well do what I ssssssssee... Mortificusssss! Transsssssform!' he hisses, before his arms slide down to join his feet and form one long structure and his chest plate engulf his head for for the jaws of a huge serpent. The mouth remains closed, save for a tiny opening in which a forked tongue dashes in and out. 'Watch him, Hotimo Rod...' the T-rex growls, lashing his tail about. 'I got him, Endlock, no worries...' the grand-looking robot utters, looking cool and composed. He fires a stream of laser bolts at the snake, who effortlessly winds between them with great skill. Realizing his strategy failed, Hotimo Rod leaps into the air and tucks his arms and legs close. 'Hotimo Rod, TRANSFORM!' he calls as his legs fold up behind him and his chest flips up over his face. When he lands, a shiny Lamborghini begins to rev up, screeching his way towards the snake. Before it even knows what happened, the reptile flies over the back of it, landing in a crumpled heap. A tail lash from Endlock sends him flying across and into a wall. The Lamborghini then circles and spins past, throwing a cloud of dust from the ship's floor at him. Infuriated, the snake perches on its coils, rearing back and spreading its hood. 'HOTIMO, LOOK OUT!' Endlock screams, dashing to protect his ally. Mortificus belches out a stream of burning flame towards the car from his fangs. In the nick of time, Endlock swipes his tail to knock his friend out of harms way but as he turns, the stream of fire engulfs his eyes, forcing a huge yell of agony to ring out. 'Sssssssssssucker!' Meanwhile, Gazimus seems to have Nerezzatron beaten, locking him into a complicated hold and twisting his legs around. Nerezzatron is unable to counter in any way. 'Give up, Nerezzatron, your days of tyranny are over!' he shouts with pride. 'NEVER! I'll shatter your lenses, Prime!' the evil machine replies. 'HEY!' a raspy voice calls out from at the front of the ship. 'This is all we can carry!' Nerezzatron looks up to see Devilletron holding two of the huge cubes and Mortificus in robot mode with arms loaded full of machinery. 'HA! The Deceprimetimers win again, Gazimus!' Nerezzatron gloats before his arms slide underneath his body. 'Nerezzatron, TRANSFORM! Suddenly, a pair of razor sharp wings spring out, forcing Prime to release the hold before Nerezzatron somersaults and flips over into the air. His fall never takes place as now, a huge metal bat floats in his place. 'You LOSE, Gazimus! Farewell!' Flapping over to his henchmen, Nerezzatron picks them both up in his claws and turns to the hatch. He pulls his head back, then whips forward and opens his mouth as a deafening scream emits, shattering the door off the supports and blowing clean through the rocks that buried them. 'We can't let him get away! They have the adaptation equipment, without it we can't ressurrect those protoforms!' Mingstro cries. Literally. 'Gazimus - TRANSFORM!' the Oldskoolbot shouts. His legs swing underneath his green cab and his head folds inside the seats. From behind, a trailer seems to appear from nowhere and attaches it to the back. 'Hotimo, look after Endlock, I'll try to stop the Deceprimetimers!' His wheels spin and a cloud of smoke is blown back as the semi rolls out of the ship, chasing after the rogue robots and leaving Hotimo, Stimpotron and Mingstro to look after the fallen Endlock.'I... I can't see a thing!' the tyrannosaur groans. 'Let me see what I can do...' Stimpotron says, taking his wand as Endlock's chest panel opens up. Turning the wand around, he inserts it into the complex circuitry and sparks leap from the tip. The dinosaur lies still, wailing lightly as the fairy modifies his systems. 'Good thing we brought medics along...' Mingstro sighs, looking over to the pile of remaining cubes. 'But they took the one thing that's preventing us from bringing our comrades back.' 'All done!' Stimpotron interrupts. 'Try Rebooting.' Endlock's systems shut down with a whirring noise, before a few beeps confirm his re-activation. 'Can't he come back any faster?' Mingstro impatiently whines. 'He's using Lenses XP. It's more of a smiley face from the creator than a product. Turn your head sideways.' Stimpotron, Hotimo Rod and Mingstro all tilt their heads to the left, to see a face closing it's eyes and sticking it's tongue out. 'Damn... the kid's right!' Hotimo gasps. Shortly, Endlock stands up, now looking around. 'All feedback sensors re-routed. Me Endlock hear better, me Endlock know better!' the dinosaur growls. '....' Stimpotron is lost for words at the neanderthal sounding mech. 'Well, I had to sacrifice some parts of his processor so he could at least tell what's around him. His hearing and touch-analyzing is now super-tuned up, but he can't transform.' 'Me Endlock like this!!' the Tyrannosaur grinned, showing rows of steel teeth. With a happy chuckle, he turns around and marches off, accidentally clipping Stimpotron with his huge tail. * * * A vast wasteland of dry ground stretches out for miles but the picture of the enemy is prominent as the metallic thieves fly off. Gazimus chases after them at full speed, determined to reclaim his allies. 'Stop right now, Nerezzatron!' the green truck shouts. 'Bossssssss, we have a follower!' Mortificus warns his transport. Looking over his shoulder, the giant bat spins around and spreads his wings wide, screaming another sonic boom towards the ground and making a crater. The brakes slam on but too late as the trailer swings around, slipping into the obstacle and taking the cab with it. With no time to lose, Nerezzatron and his crew zoom off over the horizon, leaving a dented and damaged Gazimus Prime to disconnect from his back half and once again transform to his robot shape. 'Damn him... I'd better check back on the Oldskool bots.' he says wearily, before starting to push his trailer out of the pit. 'The matrix only knows what they have planned...' * * * The Deceprimetimers are surrounded on all sides by barren desert. Harsh winds blow sand about, irritating the joints of the giant robots of evil. The sun beats down, giving a true test to their coolant fluid. Regardless, they are putting their scheme into action... A small sphere like a disco ball rolls around in Devilletron's hands, shining a hologram out of itself while Nerezzatron presses the image with a claw. A series of animals, vehicles and everyday household objects flickers past, none of which seem to take the liking of the commander. 'No... no... no... NO! There must be a good adaptation for these protoforms!' In rage, Nerezzatron punches the hologram. His fist flies past and smacks into the ball, which begins to shake rapidly. he takes a step backwards warily with his lenses opening wide 'Wha... what's going on?' 'I think... it'sssss gonna blow!' Mortificus panics, his flickering tongue frozen at the tip of his jaw. 'Every machine for himself!' Devilletron drops the ball and ducks down behind a ledge, quickly followed by Nerezzatron and Mortificus. The ball shudders and points towards the protoform, producing beams of light which begin to mould the plain metal cubes into shapes... animal shapes... and then stops dead. The same voice from the ship then speaks. 'Adaptation complete.' After a moment of peering over the ledge, the three Deceprimetimers look up above the sandy ridge to see a metallic rat and a reptile of some kind, maybe a lizard, standing there. For some reason, the rat is sitting on the lizard's head. The rodent giggles wildly while the lizard opens its mouth. A torrent of acidic green slime drips out, sizzling on the sand. 'Incredible!' Mortificus exclaims. 'A rat and a komodo dragon... sssssssimply marvellousssssss!' Nerezzatron steps forward. The two look up, almost in synchronization. 'You... state your name! Who were you!?' The domineering machine commands. The rat grins and leaps off the komodo dragon's head. Instantly, it seems to blink as though in a trance while it's furry partner dances all over the ground, then jump up into Nerezzatron's face and gyrates. 'The name's Minion the Impimus! Who the hell are you!' he squeals. A flailing Nerezzatron grips him by the tail and yanks him off his face, shaking his head. 'Don't you EVER rub your nuts or bolts on my head again!' he bellows, throwing the rat to the ground and turning his attention to the komodo dragon. 'And you, what do YOU answer to?' The creature remained silent, still in a trance. As soon as the rat jumps back onto his head, he blinks again and looks up... ... ... and stays silent. 'Useless. Why did we bother wasting our time on y-' his speech is cut off and the komodo rears up onto his back legs. His head swivels and a neck extends out, forming the arm with the dragon's head replacing the hand. His other arm then shoots out with the tail forming a huge lance. The body turns around and the feet spin around until the body has finally filled out - although it lacks a head. The rat then hops up and his body opens up and folds back, with the tail stabbing into the komodo's neck area. The final parts of the rat's body reform and a face appears in what should be his ribcage. Two red eyes light up and the mouth opens, with a totally different voice speaking from the rat's squeal. A gruff guttural tone comes out as it introduces itself. 'WE ANSWER TO HESSMASTER.' It booms. The fully changed robot stands taller even than Nerezzatron and looks for more intimidating. 'Incredible... two have fused together in symbiosis!' Nerezzatron remarks. 'What do we do with him? He could be part Oldskoolbot!' Devilletron says, still hidden by the ledge. 'Silence!' Nerezzatron growls. 'With HessMaster... the Oldskoolbots stand NO chance!' Just then, a snake slithers along the ground, gliding past HessMaster. With a lightning quick strike, his right arm shoots out, devouring the wriggling morsel in a mouthful of acid. 'Mmmm.... delicious.' It snarls. Mortificus quivers behind the ledge at the sight. 'I think it's time we took him for a test run... don't you agree?' Nerezzatron asks, looking back at his henchmen. Replies of 'Oh yes, absolutely, why not?' come back, spreading a leer across the bat's fanged face. A cold, conquering, cruel leer... To be continued...
