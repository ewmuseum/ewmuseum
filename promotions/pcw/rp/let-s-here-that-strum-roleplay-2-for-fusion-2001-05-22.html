Let's here that strum (Roleplay #2 for Fusion)

22 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd May 2001 A loud strum of a distortion bass guitar is heard. The screen is as black as nightfall. A second strum is heard, this time more high pitched. The screen remains black. Then, three loud knocks are hears on what seems to be a door. The camera comes into view. The sky is a clear blue with very few clouds. The air is as fresh as a cow isn’t. The placement is set outside in front of a small bungalow house. The location is yet to be known. The outside walls of the house are a whitely painted wood; as the front door is a bright blue color. We are just inches away from the blue door so not much is being revealed from the house. Upon the door, placed in the top-center position above a gold colored brass doorknocker is a small double glazed glass window. Below the doorknocker in a small golden brass text prints ‘64’, revealing the house number. From behind the camera reaches a clenched fist, followed by a long blue polo shirt sleeve. The fist knocks on the doorway three times. Given a couple of seconds, the doorway slowly opens up. Standing in the doorway with a metallic guitar plectrum in his mouth as a man looking to be in his early twenties. He oddly has spiked hair very similar to that of Chet Price with only one difference. This guy’s hair is a dark purple color with blonde streaks. He is wearing a black vest and a pair of black jeans. His footwear is not seen. He looks strangely familiar. He opens his jaw widely and begins to look astonished. He speaks, slightly stuttering. Man: “Oh my God man, what are you doing here?” A typical-American male voice comes from behind the camera. Second man: “I’m on town, I thought I’d come and see you Frankie.” The first man is now recognized. He is Chet Price’s best friend, Frankie Jefferson. Frankie: “I don’t believe this Chet, you’re here.” Chet: “My brother is too, he’s around the corner parking the car.” Frankie: “Michael’s with you too? What are you guys doing in Atlantic City?” It doesn’t take Albert Einstein to figure out that Frankie Jefferson is talking to one of the chosen PCW stars, Chet Price. Chet: “Our next show is here in Atlantic City. I remember you were saying how IGW is basing itself up here and how you had to move here. I looked you up and here we are.” Frankie: “I’m glad you’re here man.” Frankie reaches out an open right palm and waits for Chet to shake it. Chet does, hugs him and pats him on the back. Michael Price then appears from around the side of the house from which he parked the car in the driveway. Frankie: “Hey Mike, how’s my favorite little superstar?” Michael: “Hey man, I’m fine.” Frankie: “Well I don’t know about you two, but I’m not gonna stand out here all day. Let’s get inside.” Frankie walks inside as Chet and Michael follows. The camera fades to darkness before we can see what the brothers are wearing. The camera re-opens inside the small house. There is a black television set to the far right corner of the room, a fish tank beside it and a fireplace at the far wall. Over to the left side of the room is a large brown lather three-piece suit. All five seats are lined up beside each other. The wallpaper is a clear white and the carpet looks to be a fluffy red. Sitting on the couch part of the three-piece suit are both Chet and Frankie. Chet is wearing a white tank top covered by a short sleeved black shirt. The silk shirt is open. He also has a pair of black jeans on and a pair of black loafer shoes. Michael on the other hand is wearing a plain white polo shirt, which he has fully buttoned, a pair of white jeans and a pair of white training shoes. He is sitting on the far single seat of the leather three-piece suit. Frankie: “Chet, you still play guitar?” Chet: “Always do and always will.” Frankie: “…because there’s a red lead guitar in my bedroom if you wanna go get it. It cost me $350, so don’t break it.” Chet: “Cool. The bedroom’s…” Frankie points toward a doorway. Frankie: “Right there.” Chet leaves the room and within seconds; comes back with the red lead guitar in both of his hands. Examining the guitar, Chet compliments Frankie. Chet: “This is a sweet guitar man. How much did you say? $350?” Frankie: “Yeah man.” Chet: “I’m starting to think you got a bargain, it’s sweet.” Frankie: “Here…” Frankie stands up and heads toward the black television set. He reaches behind it and fiddles about with some of the wires. After a couple of seconds, he stands back up straight with a think but long black cable held in his right hand. Frankie: “Bring that guitar over here Chet.” With intentions to see what Frankie is up to, Chet walks over with the guitar. While he holds the guitar, Frankie grabs the bottom of it and plugs the wire he has into the small socket upon the guitar. Frankie pulls the metallic plectrum from his teeth, dries it off on his tank top and hands it to Chet. Frankie: “Here, strum the guitar. It comes out great with this new TV set I have.” Chet, without hesitation strums one deeply distortion type note. The large sound surrounds the one-floored house. Chet: “Jesus, that rocks. Let’s have some fun.” Chet begins to strum frequently upon the strings of the guitar playing the tune to The Price Brothers theme tune; ‘Forgotten’ by Linkin Park. Just as Chet begins to enjoy himself, his brother slams his hands over the strings bringing the music to a sudden stop. Chet: “What are you doing?” Michael: “Give me the guitar Chet, I’ll show you how it should be played.” Chet: “You can’t play guitar!” Michael: “Dad taught me. I pick up very easy. I can play chords easy as well as lead.” Chet willingly hands the guitar over to his brother smirking to Frankie hoping Michael will mess up. Michael: “Frankie, can you turn the distortion off on this?” Frankie: “Yeah, but why would…” Michael: “Just do it.” Frankie uses the small remote control, presses a few buttons and turns back to Michael. Frankie: “Try it.” Michael takes the plectrum from the hand of Chet and a basic ‘2-2-1’ chord. A soft sound plays. Michael: “Sounds fine to me. OK, here we go. I’m gonna get some real music going.” Michael rests his right leg on the television cabinet with the guitar neatly pressed on. After a couple of seconds, Michael begins to strum. He’s begins to play chords which are recognizable, but are one of those things were you can’t quite put your finger on it. Then it becomes pretty obvious. As he is playing the guitar, he is also singing to the song. Michael: “’She loves you! Yeah! Yeah! Yeah! She loves you! Yeah! Yeah! Yeah!” Both Chet and Frankie stare in horror! Michael: “With a love like that…you know you should be glad…” Michael closes his eyes and begins to sway his head as he plays. Chet begins to yell at Frankie. Chet: “Turn that damn thing off!” With the irritating sound of The Beatles blasting so loudly, Frankie just barely hears Chet. Frankie immediately turns around toward the television set and points the small remote at it. With just the click of one button, the sound draws to a holt. Michael: “Hey man, I was jamming to that.” Frankie: “Michael, there is no way in Hell I will have a song written by The Beatles played in this house. No way whatsoever.” Michael: “God, you guys have no taste.” Chet takes the guitar back from Michael and places it back in the bedroom. Within seconds he’s back. The three men sit down on the three-piece suite in the same arrangement as before. Frankie turns to Chet and opens up a new conversation. Frankie: “Chet I was meaning to ask you. I heard you made it into PCW, is that true?” Chet: “It most certainly is. Four of the hardest weeks of my life paid off. I’m there.” Frankie: “You got a match booked yet?” Chet: “As a matter of fact, I do. It’s against a tag team called Politically Incorrect.” Frankie: “Tag team? They put you in a handicap match for your debut?” Michael, realizing Frankie has been misled decided to clear up the confusion. Michael: “Handicap? Frankie, you’ve got the wrong idea. It’s a tag team match.” Frankie: “You mean, you…” Frankie points at Michael. Frankie: “…and, you?” Frankie points at Chet. Michael: “What’s so hard to believe about that?” Frankie: “Well, you two argue over the littlest things. I don’t know how you managed to become a tag team. I thought you, Michael were still in the academy.” Michael: “No, I got brought up with Chet.” Chet: “Once again, another job well done by me.” Frankie: “What? What do you mean?” Chet: “Michael here hasn’t wrestled yet.” Frankie: “In PCW?” Chet: “In anywhere! He is still yet to have a pro wrestling match.” Michael interrupts angrily. Michael: “Well I must be doing something right. PCW don’t just hire every Tom, Dick and Harry. Don’t be jealous Chet…” Frankie: “Tom, Dick and who…?” The scene ends on a hot-tempered mood.
