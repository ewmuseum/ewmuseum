The War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part IX]

27 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 27, 2002 Valentine was full of the joys of PINK!. As he came back into the hotel the old man behind the reception desk gave him the bill for their stay so far, handwritten in blue biro. Valentine put down what he was carrying and put the bill in the breast pocket of his tweed bright PINK! jacket. He vaulted up the stairs two at a time, and knocked on the door twice before entering. It was apparently a special knock, and meant that The mystery man was unlikely to overpower him when he entered the room. However the risk of that was pretty minimal. The mystery man was sleeping the sleep of the dead, or alternatively the sleep of the seriously exhausted and hungover pretending to be dead, and definitely denying being hungover, because they never get hangovers. The mystery man stirred slightly, and Valentine tossed over papers to him. What the hell was going on? Why was Jack back talking to the Mystery man again? What was in those papers? Jack and Johnny have a huge match tomorrow and the PINK! was just starting to look strong again...now Valentine is spotted with the PURPLE~! The Mystery man uttered something obscene and turned over. Valentine made himself a lunch of french bread and PURPLE~! beets, the beets were a bit on the bitter side, but he was hungry and it was not too bad. He switched off the modem which was still twittering and fluttering its little lights on the floor. The mystery man laid there in bed...he was certainly a tough cookie. Valentine pulled out his wallet, and opened it out flat. Inside was an old black and white photo of two children, You could tell it was old, reality just did not photograph like that anymore. The children were very alike, the older had his arm around the other’s shoulder, they were both smiling. It was a very young Valentine and his sister. Valentine had almost forgotten about her, until he had borrowed the photo from his mother. His sister had been doted on, loved. His sister was the one who had always got away with everything and was never out of mischief. Looking at the photo again he realized that he had loved his sister, loved her as a part of himself he could never quite express. Valentine was not quite whole, his sister had made him complete. He looked at the photo again and put it away, thinking of his partner Johnny Heart. Then looked over at the sleeping The Mystery man. After his lunch, Valentine decided to head out and take in the local Art Gallery, it was supposed to have a good PINK! collection. By this time The Mystery man was a bit more coherent. Mystery Man: “Where’s lunch Valentine?” Jack Valentine: “There’s some bread if you want it, some beets, half of a fruit, you can even heat up some vegetable soup on the ring if you want.” Mystery Man: “What no meat?!? Are you trying to starve me Valentine. I need my meat...long and hard and salty!” With that The Mystery man turned over again and went back to sleep. Shifting scenes to find out what Johnny Heart is up to... It had been rotten day for Johnny Heart so far, and it showed no signs of getting better. Gravity would find its own way though...last night he showed just why he and Jack were tag team champions, by knocking out that huge bald guy at the pub. Johnny then felt the ground hitting him hard, and rolled down the slope of the corrugated asbestos roofing. He blacked out momentarily, the whole world was twisting and his bones ached, as if they were trying to leave his body by force. He opened his eyes again. The sky was moving, he could see it moving there was a cloud, there was a bird, and there it jumped, then it drifted that way, and again it jumped out of position again. He got up, a member of the Rainbow Squad was lying face down next to him, he felt for a pulse in his neck, and found one, he cursed. It would have been mighty convenient if Smith, the Rainbow Squad member, had died in the fall, it would have solved a lot of problems and evened the odds a little by getting the sissy man out of the picture. The pair of them were just on the edge of a flat skylight to a factory below. It looked like they were on a mission for the PINK! forces. Up above in the portacabin office, The Village People were organizing his men. The Cowboy was gone, the other four were all okay, if a bit battered and bruised. They were not terribly bright; burly, loyal, strong, vicious, a lot of things but not bright. They did not like Indian being the leader, but they were happy enough to follow his orders for the moment. There was PURPLE~! everything in the filing cabinet, but they needed loading, this took time. They each took an extra pocket full of PURPLE~! spray. The stolen PINK! that it looked like Heart and Smith were trying to recover, was shoved into a metal cabinet, and it was locked. The front door of the portacabin was locked, to the casual observer there was nothing to arouse suspicion. They went down by the wooden stairs. The Indian let the others take the lead, while he tried to see what had happened and where he should head for. The Indian was useful to Smith (Not in that way sicko!), and the arrangement suited them both. Smith ruled his men with a steel rod, and as long as a...okay, we'll stop with the gay references...as long as he was alive The Indian was safe. You see the PINK! were not killers, they were just trying to spread the word about their...situation. They fought for everything they believed in, but they didn't use violence if they didn't a have to. With Smith dead he was at risk from the PURPLE~! element. Too many Village People after a quickie for him to play the double game for long. But Smith was big enough and smart enough to play the game just so that it would benefit the pair of them. The men were on the roof, it was a series of ridges, and once they reached roof level they could see nothing. There was a fire door for the factory at one side, and The Indian gestured one of the men to go through it. He pointed the other two in the direction of the broken skylight. With that he headed down the stairs and then clambered over roofs to the skylight. The men were perched on either edge of the skylight, they could see the broken glass below them. The Indian remained impassive, Smith moved, and The Indian allowed himself a grin. Heart was nowhere to be seen. The Indian and one of the men headed back across to the fire exit and left the other man to see if he could see Heart moving, he was to follow the others once they came into sight. He fingered the catch on his PURPLE~! shooter. He had never used it before, but the principle was simple enough, point and fire, he could manage that. Heart had dropped down through the skylight himself. He had once again checked Smith for a pulse, the bastard was still alive. Hopefully he had broken something. The bastard had the luck of the gay man on broadway. The factory was large and dark. A lot of industrial sewing machines filled the place. It seemed very dark and dirty for a factory that was still in use, but he had visited a few of these places rounding up PURPLE~!, they all looked like this. He had by now lost his sense of direction. He wanted to head in the opposite direction of the metal bashing shop that he had come through, with Elton John in danger and the Village People after him, led by crackshot Indian, he was in deep trouble. If he was captured in this, one of the last missions of his career, he would not get a shot at the gold on Sunday against the Pretty Cool Guys, Akiyama and Politically Incorrect, and would be stuck in a PURPLE~! prison forever. He saw a door opening, and the first of the Rainbow Squad’s men coming through it. He dropped down to the level of the sewing tables. If there was a man here already, he could assume that there would be Rainbow Squad at the skylight already or, there would be before long. Excluding the direction that would take him to the man he could see, and avoiding the skylight left precious few options. He tried to crawl a bit further but it hurt too much. It seemed as if the PINK! was hurting Johnny Heart. Johnny was bruised and battered and for what? The PINK!? Was it even worth it...giving up a healthy body in a tag team championship match just to fight for the PINK!? Of course...it's all about the PINK! Johnny rolled himself under one of the cutting tables, he could feel his lungs filling and exhaling. His ribs hurt, his vision started to blur and narrow, his field of vision started to narrow, it was like a film, not real at all, all arthouse effects, not happening to him at all, his field of vision narrowed until all he could see was a few square yards of the roof, a fluorescent light was swinging gently, he could only see one end of the light, it was at the end of a long dark tunnel, it seemed closer, but infinitely unreal, it was getting further away, the dark tunnel was getting longer, the light at the end went out... Back to Valentine's neck of the Caribbean... The Mystery man was feeling rough, he felt like shit, he looked worse. Shaving would be a bad idea, he ran his hand over his face, it was rough like sandpaper. He made a cup of tea and drank it while sitting on the toilet, and reading the papers that Valentine gave him. He had produced a huge battleship of a turd, it seemed too good to flush away, so he left it floating there in the pan. He had some of those little flags that you get on the end of cocktail sticks, which he’d picked up at the bar the other night. One was a union jack. he stuck it on the middle of the turd. That would sure as hell annoy Valentine. Valentine was not one for the gross stuff in life. He splashed some cold water over his face and got dressed. If there was no meat in the room, that equaled no good. It was too early for chocolate, and anyway it was bad for the complexion. He could get something at a local pub. Shifting back to the PURPLE~! warehouse where Johnny and the Rainbow Squad are... Smith had regained consciousness, The Rainbow Squad were loading him onto one of those trolleys that porters used to use to move luggage when they still did that sort of thing. The rest of the Rainbow Squad took their positions around the warehouse but when they opened the door to where the Village People were meeting...the Village People were gone! Where had they gone to? With only one day left before Wrestle Vessel, was this really a good idea to let them run freely in the Caribbean? Would they make an appearance at Wrestle Vessel? And what was Jack Valentine doing with the mystery man from the PURPLE~! Stay tuned for Part X to find out more in this ongoing saga between the PINK! and the PURPLE~!
