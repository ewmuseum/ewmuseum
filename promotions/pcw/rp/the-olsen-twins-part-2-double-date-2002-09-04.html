The Olsen Twins - Part 2 - Double Date

Date: September 4 [Pranks can be bodacious. When pranks are used to cost the Cowabunga Connection the tag team titles and lock 'Heavy Metal' Harry in a storage closet with two jailbait babes, then pranks are TOTALLY BOGUS! Nezerra and Gaz have officially entered in the realm of heinocity. A couple new compadres, trying to make names for themselves dare to mess with the Connection. Pranksters will just be another category that they toss out of the PCW tag division. Politicians, skateboarders, mad scientists, madmen...the Connection has faced them all and walked away with their totally gnarly heads held high. This will be no different. Pranksters With Attitude will face the waves and they WILL WIPEOUT!] [Mary-Kate and Ashley Olsen, the two babes who have captured the 'HEARTS' of so many teenage boys. They started out as two little dudettes on Full House, as the little baby who every gave ice cream to. The show had its bodacious moments, all except John Stamos and his totally heinous acting. Other than that, the show seemed to do it's most excellent job. After Full House the babes moved on to making movies. Nothing that dominated box offices, but those direct to video movies that you don't really hear much about. Next thing you know the babes start hitting puberty and appearing in their very own video games. It seemed quite rad that these dudettes were doing something with their careers. Then, the 16 year old babes emerge looking quite bodacious. Yes, the Olsen twins ARE BODACIOUS!! Nice jugs. Nice bodies. Nice faces. Wait...that just isnt' right. It's TOTALLY HEINOUS] [The White Lion is one of the fanciest, expensive restaurants in Los Angeles. The cream of the crop comes from all over California to dine at this fine facility. The thin, bald maitre' D greets the customer the second through the doors. His thin mustache wiggles snobbishly] Maitre D: WELCOME! Did you have a reservation??? [He looks at the customer quizzically, waiting for a response. His bug eyes make you want to punch him in the face and then beat him with his own black dress shoes] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Dude, we have most excellent reservations! [One half of the Cowabunga Connection smiles bright in his blue and yellow Armani suit, which was hand tailored just for him. Shailyn, his current girlfriend, stands next to him with a smile just as wide. She is wearing a beautiful glimmering blue evening gown. Her brown hair is combed perfectly straight, flowing past her shoulders] Maitre D: The name, sir? [His eyes grow even wider, looking most heinous] TTT: 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, party of five. [The Maitre D looks down his list and stops about 3/4 down the list and then smiles brightly, folding his hands together] Maitre D: Yes, Mr. Tommy, welcome back. I thought you looked familiar. Right this way.... [Tommy and Shailyn follow the thin man through the main hallway and into the dining area. It's a very large ballroom like atmosphere with mini-balconies where the dining tables are placed and a gigantic area for dancing. Tommy smiles at the dance floor, pumped to get Shailyn out there for a gnarly romantic time. They part through many business men, unwinding from another days work and head to a booth in the back of one of the balconies. From there they have an excellent view of everything in the dining room. The lighting is slightly dim, which is perfect for the atmosphere. The violins, cellos, violas, and timpani's rumble together for a beautiful song as many couples enjoy a dance before their dinners arrive. The smells of steamed vegetables, cooking oils, and meat fill the air. Tommy smiles to Shailyn, giving her a kiss on the cheek] TTT: Babe, you are looking EXTRA bodacious tonight. [Shailyn smiles brightly, looking very flattered] Shailyn: Thank you, Tommy. [Her face then turns to confusion] Shailyn: How come Harry was so discreet about this double date thing? Is his date some kind of Hollywood actress? [Tommy looks extremely uneasy as the events earlier in the day come back to him. The Olsen twins, half naked, on his bed. He was kissing them. They were kissing him. They almost made it down to his twig and berries, but luckily Harry came in for a much deserved save] TTT: Well...ummm...babe, did I tell you that you were looking bodacious? [Tommy smiles brightly, hoping that his charming good looks will make Shailyn forget about her question. Tommy then realizes that Shailyn is not any ordinary babe. She is most intelligent and can resist her sexual urges. That's why she is TOTALLY Gnarly!] Shailyn: THOMAS!! [Her stern voice comes across playfully as she looks at Tommy with puppy dog eyes. Tommy cannot resist the puppy dog look, it is one of the most bogus tricks in the book by babes all around the world. It is the male kryptonite] TTT: Babe.... [Before Tommy can let it out, Harry arrives with his date...err DATES...in arm] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: It's time to PARTY, man!! [Harry still wears his Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. There is a strict dress code that makes men dress in blazers and ties while dining at the White Lion. Harry obviously forgot in his stoned state. Mary-Kate and Ashley look TOTALLY BODACIOUS...err...BOGUS!! Wait...bodaicous...ahhhhh!!! They wear evening gowns as well, with slits down their backs, running to the tops of their butts. It just looks rather odd on the OLSEN TWINS] Mary-Kate and Ashley: Hi, Tommy. [Tommy nervously waves up at them, not making any kind of eye contact with them at all. Shailyn smiles and stands up from her side of the booth, shaking hands with the two teen actresses. She seems to be cool with the situation, but who would be cool with Harry dating the 16 year old Olsen Twins. Yes, it's bodacious but at the same time it has JAILBAIT written all over it] Shailyn: Nice to meet you two. I loved your work on Full House. Mary-Kate: Thanks. [The two twins bounce their way into the booth as Harry sits on the edge, his face beaming like a lit up Christmas tree. Tommy feels kind of guilty looking down on this bogus situation. He's never seen Harry this happy before. It's amazing how these totally bodacious babes...or bogus...or gnarly...ARGH!! These BABES have come into his life and given Harry some happiness for once. Usually it was just Harry puffing the magic dragon, or banging away to Twisted Sister tunes, but now he's got The Olsen Twins. He's one happy compadre] Harry: This place is far out, man! Ashley: FOR SURE!! OH MY GOD!! I've wanted to come here for SOOOOO long! Mary-Kate: ME TOO!! [The two grab each other's hands and writhe up and down. Tommy seems frightened, Shailyn just smiles, and Harry is too happy to notice the weirdness] TTT: Dude, I made advanced orders for us all. The shrimp platter is most bodacious!! Harry: Shrimp is far out, man. Mary-Kate: Shrimp? UGH!! I can't take that stuff!! OH MY GOD! Ashley: FOR SURE!! You guys just DO NOT understand. She cannot have shrimp or she'll just get TOTALLY bloated. [Harry now looks concerned] Harry: Not far out, man. Maybe we should skip the shrimp, man. Chicks, what do you want here? [The twins smile] Mary-Kate and Ashley: We want you, Harry! [Harry's eyes bolt out of his head. The twins then pounce on him, pushing him away from the booth and toward the dance floor. Tommy looks on with a frozen look of shock. He was looking forward to sharing a shrimp platter with his most bodacious babe, Shailyn] TTT: I guess shrimp is out of the question now. Shailyn: Tommy, there will be other days for the shrimp. They seem really nice and they are making Harry happy. It's really amazing how upbeat he's been. When he called me I nearly fainted at how positive he seemed. It was like talking to a different person. [Tommy nods, watching the Olsen twins freak Harry out on the dance floor. For starters, it is freaky seeing two 16 year old Olsen's freaking his brother and they are freaking to Beethoven. Not a gnarly thing to do] TTT: He does seem like a different person. I really hope this doesn't affect his gnarly ring work. We've got to face Duzza and Makonie AND Pranksters With Attitude for the tag team titles this Friday. It's yet another gnarly shot for the titles, babe. We've been screwed so many times that I have lost count. We can't get screwed Friday, its our most excellent destiny to reign supreme as the most bodacious tag team champions in PCW EVER! Shailyn: I don't think it'll affect him in a bad way, Tommy. I think if nothing else he'll perform much better with a little joy in his life. [Tommy watches as the two twins kiss Harry all over his gruffy face, feeling up his beer belly. Tommy then has to turn away for a few moments feeling a bit disgusted, then he slowly starts looking up again to take a look. He watches as Mary-Kate places her butt on Harry's crotch and rubbing it there. Tommy then turns away again in disgust] TTT: Babe, I say that we dance! [Shailyn smiles brightly] Shailyn: I thought you'd never ask. [The scene fades to black]
