Burnin' Down tha House! (Rp#2 for TT and #2 for FF)

29 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/4/03 The flames surrounding him did not bother Blind Rage. His body had a higher tolerance for pain than an average person. He was more focus at his task; saving the trapped family from death. He stood in the doorway of the, once white, suburban house and calculated the possibility of the floor collapsing under his weight. The team had spread out, each finding a different way into the building, it was the quickest way to locate the family and that was the main goal here. Badger Man had gone around the back door, Warrior had scurried up the drain pipe and The HandBag Warrior had flew in via the upper rear window. He had received no communication from them, so he had to assume that they were facing the same problem as he. Blind Rage looked over his shoulder at the crowd, who had begun to develop behind him and wondered which of them made the anonymous phone call. Anonymous phone calls often made him become suspicious. What was so wrong with coming forward? It was a suspected arson case he was positive. The police had cordoned off the immediate area, blocking off the crowd and the odd PCW wrestling fan with barriers. Blind Rage sighed. The entire firemen for this area had been called away to fire at the dock. It was reported to be big, but he couldn’t see the flames. Precious time was wasting away, he had to move and move soon, before it was too late. He ventured a nervous foot forward onto the fire-ridden floor boards. It seemed to be okay, so his right foot followed. Now is whole wait was displaced upon the floor, Blind Rage heard an uneasy cracking noise. Before he could move the floor had apparently dissolved underneath him, he fell almost seven feet to the cellar all of the burning limber buried him. He could hear the crowd gasp as he feel, but there was no need, he didn’t even have a scratch. Again, he sighed. “Today really is going to be one of those days!” he exclaimed, with great anger. *** Warrior had scurried up the drain pipe with a considerable ease and once he was standing on the roof of the back porch below he was decidedly uncertain what to do. He didn’t want to dive through the window like the star of some bad action movie, because prior to contrary belief broken glass does actually hurt. So Warrior provided his own method of entry, He quickly detached one of his laser axes from the right side of his belt and instantly pressed the engage button. With surprising speed the green-coloured blade shot out the end of the shiny, metal bar and formed a solid axe-like shape. The laser blade effortlessly penetrated the wooden panelled wall, allowing Warrior to gain access. Upon entry the thick, black smoke caused by the fire hit him like a ton of bricks. Regardless of this set back, Warrior ventured onwards. The room he was in appeared was empty. It was a child’s room. The small bed scattered with dolls made that fairly obvious to him. With his right hand over his nose and mouth he began to search the room. After a minute or so he came to realise that nobody was there, it made sense, a little girl would be with her parents in this situation. Warrior opened the door to the room, to see the ever illusive Badger Man striding up the stairs. He was better equipped for the situation. He had a face mask, so he didn’t have to parade around with his hand over his mouth and he could see in the dark, and despite the sun glaring in through the windows the thick, black smoke prevented Warrior from seeing much. Badger Man looked over at Warrior with a smile. “Didn’t fall on your ass trying to climb the drain pipe I see.” “Good to see you too!” He simply replied, with sarcasm in his voice. “We’d better hurry up. You and Blind Rage need to get back on the road. You wrestle tonight and then again on Friday. Let’s wrap this thing up quick, eh?” “It has to be wrapped up quick to gain any success in the event. I know this isn’t the time for this, but ‘Rage shouldn’t be taking these freebe missions.” Badger Man lead Warrior into the adjacent room to the one he was just in and together they searched. It appeared to be another child’s bedroom, but this time Warrior guessed it was a little boy. Not many girls collected GI JOE, all except his cousin Susan, but that was something he’d rather not think about. He shook the thoughts from his head and realised that Badger Man was speaking to him. “…you know as well as I do that the majority of superheroes are out of town at the moment.” He began. “And all of the fire units were on the other side of town, dealing with that massive blaze.” “I never saw a massive blaze on the way over hear!” “Neither did I. It could have been some hoax call or something.” Warrior developed a confused look, as he bent down to look under the bed. “Perhaps, but they surely would have been here by now if that was the case.” “Probably.” “I thought this whole thing was fishy from the start…and I don’t mean the smell coming from between Linda’s legs either, it was something else!” “There isn’t anybody in here…” Badger Man’s comments were cut off by a scream. Warrior held his hand up, signalling for silence. Upon hearing what appeared to be the scream again, Warrior recognised what it was. The Handbag Warrior shouting in his extremely camp voice. “I’ve found something.” The homosexual superhero bellowed for a third time. Warrior and Badger Man nodded at each other in agreement, then they both scurried out of the room and into the next one. Upon entering the room, they were faced with The Handbag Warrior, with both children draped over his shoulders, with the mother lying unconscious on the floor. He glanced up at them as they entered the room, with a decidedly hurried expression upon his face. “Badger Man get the woman! Warrior there’s another one of the family in the Wardrobe, alive I think, you get them.” HandBag Warrior barked flying off through the very sizable hole in the wall. Warrior figured that’s how he got in. Warrior flung the door of the wardrobe open expecting to see an unconscious man. However, what he got was very different. The Alsatian leapt from the wardrobe, barking like crazy, as rapid as a wolverine. A combination of shock and the force of the leaping dog, knocked Warrior down, the dog gnawing at his right arm. Warrior let out a yelp at the pain, whilst desperately trying to find the laser axe upon the left hand side of his belt. With great difficulty his fingers finally located the device and so he unhooked it. Then he viciously hit the dog over the bag of the head with it. Nothing happened. Well, nothing good. The dog dug his teeth in, deeper into the arm and Warrior was beginning to lose consciousness. However, this was not because of the dog. The smoke from the fire was beginning to get to him, he estimated a maximum three minutes of conscious time. He had to make them count. He hit the dog again. Nothing. He hit the mutt a third time. Again nothing. The fourth blow bore fruit, however, the dog was finally unconscious. The problem was, the dog was unconscious on top of him and he didn’t have the strength to push him off. Warrior’s last thought as he reached unconsciousness was; that’s irony! *** Blind Rage quickly stood from the burning debris that had once engulfed him, he patted down the small fiery outbreaks that had started on various places on his costume. Once done, he let out an almighty grunt, before leaping up form the cellar and finding firm ground upon the hallway. Without a second thought about the amazing feat he quickly rushed through the flames and debris, up the stairs, he rushed even more when he heard the screams of Warrior. By the time he burst through the bedroom door Warrior was unconscious…and so was the dog. Trust Warrior to venture into bestiality during a life and death situation! he silent joked, picked the dog up in with his left hand and Warrior up with the right. He then leapt through the sizeable hole in the wall, Blind Rage presumed was created by the very powerful handbag of the Handbag Warrior. He hit the grassy ground with a distinctive thud, the rushed around to the front of the house. Warrior would need medical treatment. *** Blind Rage sat on the grubby, brown couch in the living quarters of the Justice Inc building. Handbag Warrior sat on the couch opposite him and Badger Man on the armchair to the right of him. Both seats the same grubby, brown design. “So what you thinking’, sugah?” Handbag Warrior asked with a concerned look upon his face. “I’m thinking that, this little fire escapade was a set up.” Blind Rage replied. “Warrior, was thinking on the same lines.” Badger Man informed, taking a sip of ice tea, from his rather large glass. Blind Rage smiled. Even though he and Warrior had very different perspectives on the world, their business, and their wrestling career, it pleased him that they at least were thinking on the same lines. “Now we have to figure out who and why. I believe it was the same person who leaked the extra-strong mint addiction a couple of weeks ago.” “Yeah, but like then, we got no leads on the suspect, sugah.” The Handbag Warrior stated, fiddling with the strap of his enchanted handbag. “We do have a lead.” said Badger Man with a smile. “The missing firemen. They had to have seen what happened, there had to be a reason why they took so long, because there definitely wasn’t a fire on the docks. I asked a worker. We won‘t make a move until Warrior gets back.” Blind Rage explained. “Where is he anyway?” Badger Man inquired. “Hospital.” Blind Rage simply answered. “What the hell is he doin’ there, sugah?” The Handbag Warrior asked in a concerned tone. “The doctors are trying to pry an unconscious dog from his arm!” Blind Rage replied with a chuckle.
