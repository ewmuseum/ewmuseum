David Arquette Syndrome - Part 1 - The Shaman Healer

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 7 [It's a beautiful day. The sun is up. The temperature is at a comfortable 65 degrees, perfect for the morning. The tide is looking to be totally rippin' today. Looks like it's going to be a nice long day of surfing and letting some most bogus frustration loose. After being totally dogged by that heinous dude Randy Raggamuffin, Tommy has to let off some steam. He broke his television and stereo, but that's a totally different story. After hearing the wise words of a Homeless Man, who freakishly disappeared, Tommy knows that Shailyn wants Tommy. Maybe she is fooling around with Raggamuffin but deep down inside her innocent little Christian heart she wants The Tubular One] [Harry is having a bad morning. He sits frozen on the leather couch looking all around himself. He's been diagnosed with David Arquette Syndrome. That's right. When stress gets a hold of you and your accused of being gay, the David Arquette Syndrome takes over your body like some sort of cancer. You scream like him, you walk like him, and you make horrible jokes like him. Unfortunately in this state you don't suddenly run into a Courtney Cox look a like and shag all night long. This is serious, this could be career threatening to The Heavy Metal One. Just what fan in their right mind would want to be entertained by some long haired freak that looked like David Arquette? NOBODY!! Harry would be booed out of the arena and costing them the tag team title for sure. The Heartbreakers will get an easy victory because of this illness] [The Therapist suggested a wise Shaman healer who lives in the outskirts of Los Angeles. He lives in a cave and likes to pretend that he's Mexican. The Cowabunga's were told that he was quite good at what he does. He actually made a blind man see before, and it made it on the front page of the Inquirer. Unfortunately the man saw a David Arquette movie and leapt to his death less than 24 hours after his cure. This is why the syndrome is serious and this is why the Cowabunga Connection, PCW's most bodacious tag team and number one contenders for the tag titles, must travel to that cave. They must meet this Shaman healer who likes to be Mexican. They MUST SAVE HARRY!!!] [The blue '02 Mustang skids onto the dirt path, just feet from the entrance of the cave they must enter. The forest is kind of scary with the trees looking all dead and a bunch of skeletons hanging from them. Shrunken heads stabbed through stakes are on each side of the cave entrance with weird tribal symbols written above with blood, or at least red magic marker. Tommy, dressed in his Hawaiian button down shirt, blue and yellow swim trunks, and black sandals, looks at the place in absolute horror. This place is totally bogus in a most heinous kind of way. They need to get out of here and look in another forest because this dude is no healer. He's some freaky bogus cult leader] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: We need to seek another place to find this Shaman dude at, compadre. [Tommy turns back to the car and looks at Harry kind of funny. He doesn't seem to be like himself since they hit the freeway. His eyes aren't bloodshot anymore, his face isn't as pale, and his mannerisms seem totally goofy. It's like he's shapeshifted into a totally different person] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: The names Dewey. I'm gonna be a cop soon, I'm working my way up. We need to get you under some protection, sir. There is a ghost face killer running around this town and we don't need you turning up dead like that hot blonde girl did. [Tommy now looks in real horror. The syndrome has now spread. No longer does Harry just scream like Arquette, now he's taking over his movie roles. Too perfectly!!] TTT: Dude!! We have to go inside there. We need to get you cured no matter what it takes. We have to be ready for The Heartbreakers at Fusion. We don't need some totally bogus David Arquette dude ruining our most bodacious shots at the titles. [Harry/Dewey taps Tommy on the shoulder and whispers into his ear] Harry/Dewey: The names Dewey. [Tommy leads the way into the cave. He's pretty hesitant going inside knowing there might be some kind of bogus booby trap to stop them. Hehe...booby trap. Wouldn't it be bodacious if a bunch of boobs just come down and covered them. It would be a totally gnarly wet dream come true!!! This is reality though. They are entering a dark and heinous cave to what seems to be their doom. If they don't cure Harry then it's complete doom for them anyway. The tag titles mean the world to them and with Harry like Arquette that chance will not even come close] Harry/Dewey: Stop. Let me lead the way. I'm a police officer and I'm in love with Gail...the news reporter. There is a ghost face killer on the loose and you don't need to get killed. There has been plenty of blood shed. [Tommy just shakes his head, not knowing how to cope with this totally bogus disease. Harry/Dewey leads the way through the deep and dark caverns of the cave. It seems that they are walking forever until a dim light starts to become brighter and brighter. Tommy feels that this is it, this is the healer that can save his half brother from turning into one of the most bogus actors of all time. An eerie chant is heard as the two men grow closer] Eeire Chant: Hum A Hum A Hum A... [Harry/Dewey turns to Tommy and whispers] Harry/Dewey: Stay here. If I'm killed then run like hell. [He leaps out into the source of light with his finger pointed out like a gun. His voice screeches through your head like nails just like David Arquette] Harry/Dewey: FREEZE, GHOST FACE KILLER!!! [Tommy ignores his orders and appears into the source of the light. It is really an amazing sight. There are tons of candles burning throughout the cavern, the smell is TOTALLY bodacious. Posters of naked women hang on the walls, along with a few posters of masked lucadores. Tommy is very impressed with the decorating. Could use a little more blue and yellow though] TTT: Bodacious. This has to be the place, dude. [A figure rises in front of the cluster of candles. He is a robed figure with his arms out stretched like some kind of God. He stands frozen in that position as the Cowabunga's can only look on in total disillusion] Figure: Ma Gusta Frijoles!!!!! [Tommy then looks in confusion. He failed Spanish class. Harry failed Spanish class too. So did David Arquette according to his movie 'Ready To Rumble'. It's all confusion] TTT: Most gnarly dude. We're to understand that your a Shaman healer that can help my brother out. He's also my bodacious tag team partner and we need him cured so we can go face a couple of Pink dudes for the PCW tag team titles!! [The man steps forward to reveal himself to be wearing a red and black Luchadore mask] TTT: GNARLY!! Harry/Dewey: GHOST FACE KILLER!!!! [The David Arquette wanna be begins pumping his thumb down like a madman, trying to shoot the Shaman healer. Apparently the dumbass thinks he's the Ghost Face guy from the Scream movie. Talk about being totally embarrassed] Figure: Ma Gusta Frijoles. TTT: Dude, I don't really know Spanish so could you like maybe speak English. At least maybe write some stuff down on a piece of paper. We need to get this done ASAP, compadre. Figure: Frijoles. TTT: For Free?!?! GNARLY!! Shaman Healer: Ma Gusta Frijoles. [The healer begins circling Harry/Dewey and chanting 'Frijoles' over and over again. Tommy looks on with most attention, hoping to pick something up and use it on his own. Maybe he can curse Raggamuffin to have his coc---] Shaman Healer: Frijoles...MA GUSTA!!!!! TTT: Ok.... [Tommy looks to Harry/Dewey, and Harry/Dewey looks to Tommy. Then they both look to the Shaman Healer in the Luchadore mask] TTT: Harry? Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: SUCCESS!!!! [The Shaman Healer smiles brightly under the mask and throws his arms in the air like some kind of victory] Shaman: Ma Gusta Frijoles!!!! Ma Gusta Frijoles!!! Harry/Gordie: WRESTLING'S NOT FAKE!!!!!!!!!!!! [Harry/Gordie, in total David Arquette screaming spears the Shaman Healer and begins bashing his face in with repeated rights and lefts. Tommy immediately pulls him off of the Healer and to the side. Tommy sees that the Shaman Healer has been knocked unconcious but Harry's barrage of blows] TTT: This was a total waste, dude. Harry/Gordie: Listen, Shaun, we have to go to Atlanta. We have to Save....JIMMY KING!!! TTT: Totally....Bogus..... To Be Continued
