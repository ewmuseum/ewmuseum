'Alliances', Haven't Had Much Luck With Them, Have You Ty?

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: 'Alliances', Haven't Had Much Luck With Them, Have You Ty? Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 18/11/02 We see the smug face of Xavier Kannon. Stubbled features all in unison to promote the image of total self-satisfaction. “Tyler Nelson.” The Strawberry Blonde hair is neatly tied back, the ponytail not seen behind the Scientologist’s neck that is bordered by a White dress-shirt collar. “All this used to be yours.” Under the collar and falling down the middle of his torso is a Black tie, flanked either side by a memento of the old CEO’s... A waistcoat patterned with dollar bills. Greed is still good in PCW, it’s just another man’s. “Must be hard?” The classic tale of the student learning from and surpassing his tutor. Learning from the best makes you the best, and also leaves the former-best slightly redundant. ‘Redundant’... A perfect word for the neutered former PCW talent lurking in FSW. “Crawling out of 1 & A Half Star Wrestling back to the bright lights of ol’ Primetime, huh?” When Kannon opposed Tyler Nelson is was not a battle of the egos. Not a battle of Greed. Some would say it wasn’t even a battle of power. Looking further down Kannon’s body, his arms are crossed across his front, PCW cufflinks keeping his cuffs in place while showing off once again that he is ‘Mr. PCW’. Not the Phantom Booker. None of Media. Not Greed Inc... Not Tyler Nelson. “But still, good to see the old you back.” Last time Tyler and Xavier faced there was no Armani suit on the then-CEO’s back. No silk ties, smart shoes of well-tailored shirts. Then Kannon was faced with was pale body, Black writing snaking it’s way around his adversary’s form, blackened eyes, Black leather pants... As to battle Kannon’s power hungry alliance Tyler needed specialist help. The Dark Age. “Though you must feel like a Tramp who just crawled out of a New Jersey Gutter and found himself in Vegas.” They could have ruled PCW together. But as it was Tyler Nelson’s famous... Or infamous... Greed that propelled him to PCW’s summit nearly 2 years ago... It was that same Greed that wrenched it from his fingers. “We made a good team, while it lasted.” The Ultimate Authority looked as if it would crush PCW under it’s power show by show. Tyler, Xavier, Tim Walden and Politically Incorrect... All 5 men buying PCW from it’s creditors and immediately stamping their mark all over the Fed. 4 days of UA rule, Jeremy Howard and the White Mexican both gone. Tyler poised for World Gold. Kannon with the Continental Title around his waist thwarting the power of President Duke Williams. “Then, certain things happened... Didn’t they?” At Supremacy, Kannon did what he does best. Thrown and Beaten about by Duke Williams, he pulled out the win in a way Tyler would have been proud of. Run-Ins, Interference and Foreign Objects led to the Scientologist standing over the PCW President, Title safe in the UA’s grasp. Nelson didn’t have such a good night. And boy, did PCW know it. “I’ve heard of taking a loss badly, but don’t you think that went a bit far?” Later that night, the bell rung to mark Tyler Nelson becoming 3-time PCW Champion, but there were no celebrations. 2 bells later, Big Alan Steele was standing bold as ever in the middle of the ring, Tyler’s World Title in his hand. What came next shocked everyone, including a certain Scientologist who was just getting used to his power. “You caused the first crack right there, Ty... And from that slightest of flaws... It all fell apart.” Firings. Mass Firings. In a fit of rage, Tyler fired the whole PCW roster. From the lowliest curtain jerker right up to his friend, ally, Commissioner, the Continental Champion. Before Kannon fired Nelson, Nelson fired Kannon.... Remember? “After that, it was never the same.” The Ultimate Authority rode again, but mistrust was rife and jostling for power was continuos. Everyone wanting to carve themselves a bigger slice in case it all fell apart again. Fall apart again it did. “You got rid of Walden, Lovecraft and Powell left.... But Nelson and Kannon were still tight.... Right?” Tyler tried to rebuild. The more allies around him the better, so he thought. The Chef & Alexis Parshin. Aaron Goeren. Frisco. Corey Williams. One again the core of Tyler and Xavier rallied re-enforcements around them, but just as before they left and were stripped away... And with the lure of more power, more say, more influence... ... Xavier made his move. “Me, the Argie and the Pantyhose-headed freak... An adequate alliance while it lasted.” 10 steps across a ring changed PCW forever. From Tyler Nelson’s side, where he had been from the beginning of the Ultimate Authority, Xavier slotted in between Frisco and the Phantom Booker... Tyler was on his own, and Kannon had found himself with essentially 50% of PCW. From 15% to 50% in just a few months. If only a Bank could offer such competitive interest rates. “For once, you just couldn’t get it back. I kept that power elusive from you, until the time was right.” Many matches have high stakes. Be it a Title, Title shot, staff position or just pride. But at the dawn of the 3rd Age, a match was made with stakes the like of which had never been seen before. Each side with their motives, each side with their own goals. “Wasn’t that meant to be my salvation, Tyler?” PCW World Title. PCW CEO position. Commissionership of PCW. 100% Ownership of PCW. And supposedly, Xavier’s salvation were all put on the line in an epic match that coincided with the start of PCW’s 3rd Dawning. “Didn’t really work though did it? However, I got what I REALLY wanted so it’s all good.” Inside the cell they battered one another, they made one another bleed. They rolled through barbed wire and they plummeted down to earth. But at the end, a Hallmark shifted the balance of power in PCW and for once, Tyler had nothing. “You helped groom your own successor Tyler, and may I say you did a great job.” At Alliances, Nelson will walk back into a PCW arena. He won’t be walking to the executive suites, or the Skybox, not even a makeshift CEO’s office. The FSW locker room will be the only place Nelson has access to. “Welcome back, Nelson.” Sitting next to his new ally Angello Deville, wondering when his new charge will backstab him, wondering what might have been, plotting what still could be, the memories, the rivalries, the matches, the fame, the spotlight... “Don’t make yourself too comfortable.” ...
