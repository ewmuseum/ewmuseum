[Flaymin holds the plectrum and positions it between his thumb and index finger] Flaymin: I don’t know about this, I mean I don’t need no plastic thingy to do some ‘Dick Flinging’. Michelle: Quit your yapping and do it.Now with your free hand, position it at the far end of the guitar over the 4th string. Flaymin: 4th from the top or the bottom? Michelle: From the top. [Flaymin stares closely at the guitar and begins to count down to the fourth string] Flaymin: I don’t like the fourth string, can’t I put my finger on the second string? [Michelle throws her hands up in the air in frustration] Flaymin: What!?! Michelle: Just put your finger on any damn string!!!! Flaymin: For a music teacher you sure do have a short temper. Michelle: Aaarrgghh!!Remind me again why I’m doing this? Flaymin: Dick Flinging. [Michelle shudders at the thought of Flaymin strumming his mini- member] Michelle: Right….. that. [Flaymin shrugs his shoulders] Flaymin: I don’t know, I was just wondering if you knew any of them. Michelle: What does that have to do with anything? [Flaymin pauses for a moment and his eyebrows begin to twitch] Flaymin: What were we talking about again? Michelle: *sigh* I was in the midst of teaching you how to play a guitar. Flaymin: Right, and I was in the midst of lobbying for right to play the drums instead. Michelle: And I was in the midst of saying that we already have a drummer. Flaymin: And I was in the midst of… being in the midst of something…. Michelle: Fine!Fine!You want to play the drums, go right ahead! [Flaymin smiles giddily and gets up and walks over to the nearby drum kit.He sits behind the drums and rubs his hands in anticipation. He then looks at all the different drums and cymbals.He picks up the drumsticks and shakes his head in disapproval] Flaymin: Screw this, Dick Flinging is nowhere near as difficult as this crap! Michelle: By difficult you mean banging drumsticks against a drum? Flaymin: Exactly.I specialise in a different sort of banging, if ya catch my drift.
