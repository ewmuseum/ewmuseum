Date: BOOYAH! Vote Skylar Green for PCW Continental Champion! Why should you vote Skylar Green for PCW Continental Champion? Why not? How could you NOT trust this face? Skylar Green will be running on a platform that's about 3 inches off the ground. Actually, it's a treadmill. After all, the guy's blind, we don't want him hurting himself. He has no actual campaign agenda, but I'm sure we can BS one here. *Bobby Stormbringer pokes his head in the door.* No, not you. *Stormbringer leaves to return to the EW 24/7 chat so that he may complain and/or gloat about PCW Spring Fling April 2002 some more.* Anyway, we'll run down the list of things... that I-- okay, honestly, I just made this stuff up. But anyway, let's run down the list of things on Skylar's campaign platform and see what he actually hopes to accomplish as PCW Continental Champion, and that he'd like to do with your support. 1) PCW Security will be given new kickass uniforms. In addition to these wonderfully stylish skull costumes (complete with the color green, to represent the man who gave them the costumes, and black since that's part of the heritage of Skylar Green and it's hard to assign a color to the Japanese), PCW security will be provided with Jester Scepters, which shoot magic beams when used. In the event of melee combat, the scepters can easily crack a person's skull. In addition, these security guards will be given official 'enforcer' duties, making them the closest thing to PCW's own Secret Police. FEAR THE PCW SECURITY! 2) As Continental Champion, Skylar Green promises to limit live performances by Dante Jackson. He will also limit performances by Totally Tubular Tommy. But you will still get plenty of performances by Skylar Green. Hey, he works for free, PCW gets as many bookings out of him as they can. 3) PCW will have trainers to keep everyone in tip-top wrestling condition. Frontrunners for the job are Pete! and Slimer from the Ghostbusters. They will be in charge of coming up with exercise plans, meal plans, and even total schedules for such activities as sleep and sex. Not that Totally Tubular Tommy or Dante Jackson will need sex scheduled into their programs. We couldn't find anyone to touch him. This isn't an actual part of the agenda, but Skylar Green's childhood friend is Baywatch star David Hasselhoff! Do you know David Hasselhoff? Do you? Well, neither does Skylar Green, but there's no harm in saying so, right? Right? 4) Skylar Green wishes you no particular harm. Isn't that right, Violence Jack? *Insert Evil Grin Here* 5) Skylar plans to increase appropriations for goat cheese. (Are you happy now, Moby? HUH?) 6) Skylar Green is prepared to offer everyone who votes for him a brand new 2003 Acura RSX. (Blue color shown, open road setting not included.) Isn't this the most repulsive thing you've ever seen? Yeah, Xavier Kannon's been looking bad since that beating by Akiyama. Word of advice, folks, stay away from the nVg! 7) Skylar Green will release Totally Tubular Tommy's plans to take over the world in true supervillain fashion. First, I'll breed hundreds of thousands of totally gnarly mouse-hunting cats and stuff. I'll plan to unleash them upon a third-world country, wiping out the rat ppopulation and in turn depriving those starved natives of their only source of meat, which will be completely bogus for them. Then I'll demand $100 dollars per person to get rid of the cats. My awesome calculations says that $100 dollars times 1 billion people equals $100 billion dollars! Heavy Metal Harry will inform me that the natives would merely eat the cats. I slap my head and shot, 'D'oh!' Back to the drawing board, right before Skylar Green drops in. I capture him and place him unsupervised in an easily escapable trap. Skylar Green gets out and destroys my hideout and my sidekick, Totally Tubular Mini-Tommy, but I escape. I'll shake my fist at him from space, where I'll hide for two years until the sequel where I'll come back for another failed plot to take over the world. Dude! Whoa... Excellent!! *Plays air guitar while fondly remembering Bill and Ted's Excellent Adventure.* 8) Skylar Green promises you that Violence Jack will NEVER EVER MATE. EVER!!! That's right, ladies, he's on your side. Convicted felons, the clinically insane, the California Raisins, and Hollywood actor Harrison Ford would vote for Skylar Green. Wouldn't you?
