Welcome to Flaymin's House of Torture~!

16 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/4/02 [Scene opens at Disneyland. A black Four-Wheel Drive with tinted windows drives through the backstage area of Disneyland.The car goes past several warehouses and iconic Disneyland rides. Finally it comes to a stop outside a fenced-off construction site.The back door of the car opens and out steps Flaymin Aymin with El Diablo’s mask in his hand.Flaymin spits on the mask and then wipes off the phlegm by sliding the mask on the ground.Flaymin looks at the mask and grins, before he throws it back into the car. Flaymin’s assistant, Porn Queen Jenna Jameson, opens the other door of the car and steps out. She walks around the car and over to Flaymin] Jenna: Why are you grinning? Flaymin: Oh…. Nothing. Jenna: You spat on El Diablo’s mask again, didn’t you? Flaymin: I couldn’t help it. [Jenna shakes her head and then they both walk towards the main doorway of the construction site. At the doorway is a man in a sharp business suit.He greets Flaymin and Jenna by shaking their hands] Walt IV: Hi!Welcome to Disneyland!I’m Walt Disney IV and I’ll be filling you in on all the info about ‘Flaymins House of Torture’. Flaymin: What happened to Walt the 2nd? Jenna: And where is number 3? Walt IV: They both work at Disney World. Flaymin: And you got stuck with Disneyland huh? [Walt begins to look uneasy when responding to these probing questions. He claps his hands together and puts on a fake smile] Walt IV: Enough about my two older brothers!Why don’t I take you for a tour of ‘Flaymin’s house of Torture’. [Walt opens the door of the construction site and walks inside.Flaymin and Jenna follow him inside. The three of them walk past several construction workers who are busy welding together various parts of the ride] Walt IV: Firstly, I just want to say that I’m ecstatic that you decided to endorse your ride here at Disneyland- this will really piss off my two brothers at Disney World. Flaymin: What? Walt IV: Uh… Never mind.Lets pretend I never said that. Flaymin: I didn’t know what you were talking about to begin with. Walt IV: Anyways, to sum things up thanks for joining the Disneyland family. Jenna: Mr Walt Number Four, how come you’re creating such a violent ride?I thought Disneyland was a ‘Family Fun’ kind of place. Walt IV: I’ve had enough of that Family Fun shit. I’ll let you in on a little secret:Right now I’m in the midst of creating a whole new world here at Disneyland- ‘Pluto’s Porno Paradise’!! Flaymin: Pluto’s Porno Paradise? Walt IV: Hey maybe I’ll get you and Jenna to promote that too! Flaymin: You might as well give me my own section of Disneyland.I’ll fill it up with the finest prostitutes, drunks and psychopaths money can buy! [Walt nods his head approvingly] Walt: I’ll think about that. But for now why don’t I show you the features of the ride. [Walt, Flaymin and Jenna walk into the unfinished ride.They stroll over to a section where five construction workers are busy installing gigantic, electronic blowtorches] Walt: This part of the ride is called ‘The Firefly’.I love watching PCW shows in which you pull out your blowtorch and set your opponent alight.Just like last week when you burnt The Multiple Man’s face- that was sweet.And we would love to bring that level of intensity to the ride. Basically, the people on the ride will walk through this door.Instantly they’ll begin to smell the stench of burning flesh and before they can realise what is happening……. WHOOOOOOOOOSHKA!!!!!!!! 10 thousand litres of kerosene come gushing out from all corners of the room. [Flaymin begins to laugh in amazement] Flaymin: Ha!!That would rock! Walt: You bet. Then the ride-goer begins to panic because you know… they’re like on fire and stuff.So they then run through this door over here, still on fire of course, and they start to roll on the floor to put the fire out. Then, they begin to hear a rumbling noise…. Flaymin: More fire? Walt: Nope, a Tractor! [Walt points to one side of the room] Walt: This wall over here slides away and behind it a tractor comes charging out at 200 miles an hour. The person then has to duck out of the way of the Tractor and avoid the petrol leak. Jenna: Petrol Leak? Walt: Yep, the way I see it, the person would already be on fire from the blowtorch and by this stage the fire would be wearing off. So to kick-start the flames for a second time, when the tractor bursts in here, it will splatter petrol all over the place and that will pretty much cause the flames to sky rocket once again. Flaymin: I’m loving this ride more and more! [Walt then leads Flaymin and Jenna through another door] Walt: Ok in this room we have the exploding beer kegs. [Flaymin frowns at the thought of an exploding beer keg] Walt: I’m sorry to say this, but that time El Diablo rigged that Beer Keg with explosives only to see it explode in your face was pure genius. So I figured that the Beer Keg explosions would scare the hell out of everyone as well as put out all the fire that is engulfing the people. Flaymin: A Beer Shower? Walt: Yep, a Beer Shower will dampen all the fire. By this stage, the people will be suffering from Third Degree Burns. They’re tired.They’ve inhaled a truckload of smoke.And they’ve been run over by a tractor. What they’ll be looking for is a safe haven, somewhere to sit down and relax. That’s where this carriage comes in to play. [Walt puts his hand on a nearby carriage and pulls it towards Flaymin and Jenna] Walt: Ok, so the people will jump in here to get their breath back. But we don’t want that. Flaymin: Hell no. Walt: So the carriage begins to roll into the next room and it is in this next room that 200 staple guns will begin to fire out round after round of staples. This idea is taken from the time you pinned the ears of your opponent to the mat in order to win your first Extreme Title. Jenna: But what if the staples miss the person’s ears? Walt: That isn’t a problem.If it doesn’t connect with their ears it will most likely get them right between the eyes! So there is a chance the individual might wind up with a popped eye socket, now ain’t that grand?!?! Flaymin: Sounds like fun! Walt: Of course the ride isn’t finished yet, but when it is I’ll be sure to give you the full tour. Here, as a token of our gesture please accept these two free Mickey Mouse Body Bags!These are top of the line and we expect them to be best sellers once we start stocking them in our merchandise stores. Jenna: What are we supposed to do with them? Flaymin: One Body Bag for El Diablo…..And another one for his shitty little mask!! Walt: You’d waste an authentic Mickey Mouse body bag on that stupid Mexican Midget?You could just fit him inside a pillowcase! [Scene ends as Flaymin, Jenna and Walt share some laughs at El Diablo’s expense]
