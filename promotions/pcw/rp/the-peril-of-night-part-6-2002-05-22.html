The peril of night part 6

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/05/02 The fur of this white giant, as it glowed and shimmered beneath the newly born sunrise, as Anna’a face painted an expression that would read as the dawn of her life. Tremoring with the ground as the huge beast continued to form, her perspiration almost seeming to explode with growth, as her heart pumped faster and faster, as if it were trying desperately to flee from the manifesting evil that lay in her path. However, the mass of pure fear fixated her, unable to move with terror freezing her like ice, she could do nothing more than stand and await her destiny. The entire continent shook beneath the tremendous pressure and force created by the beast, such enormous power could not be viewed by mortal eyes, yet Anna’s supernatural abilities allowed her to see exactly what this thing was, although for once mortality seemed inviting to her. For nobody with sane flesh in their body would look upon such things by choice and those who dare to tempt their curiosity would lose their lives, as they were pillaged and torn into pieces by the slashing winds of darkness. The being grew claws of perfect ivory form. They shone with great barbs and glistened with the energy residue that encased them. Forged in the blood of dragons and wielded thus, like a great sword they danced in the surrounding fires, as energy was hurled into every direction, destroying all in it’s path with the force of a raging inferno that equaled the fire of a million chimera’s. Streaks of grimacing red painted slowly across the once silver sky, the rims of the clouds frowned a grey, which instigated doom and pain into the hearts of those who viewed it. Energy from all around was absorbed into a great broth of creation that seemed to center with this beast, with every second it grew and the roar of the flames that penetrated the ground itself became louder, as the seconds passed. She stood alone, with not an ally by her side. Only her dueling forks for an alliance, but they were no longer of use. As she lowered her arms with the rising depression in her heart, with nothing but a recurring sense of defeat and desire for surrender, she simply watched on. With pastel painted tears, melting down her soft move cheek as she stood before the terror that was this beast and for the first time, she knew what it was to feel. True fear. As the temperature rose and all she could see was the purity of fate’s formation, she sighed the sound of defeat, as a fanfare of tears, like pearls slid and cascaded into a pool of forlorn hope beneath her tired eyes and her weak lips. No longer could it be stood. The pain, the sadness, the hurt. She wanted them all to go and knew no other way to rid her weary soul of the discomforts that plagued her very existence. The sands of time grew thin for her, but as fate had mocked her, now she would tamper fate in one final sealing action to ensure that not one more person would suffer as she had. As Anna fell to her knees, with an action of prayer implied by the movements of her cold and bitter fists, she rocked back and forth digging a hold into the soft earth below her. The beast now lost its glow. Emerging a behemoth from the tempest. It’s white fur shimmered beneath the newly born sunshine, as it pierced the gray and displayed the purity and evil of this enigmatic creature. Mighty claws at its side, it wavered slightly before her, moving two steps toward her and stopping in the dust. With a deep growl it caught her attention, with fearful eyes, she stared upon its unforgiving face, with a glare so menacing that even the demons of hell itself would curl up and die of depression, as they would lament that fact that such horrors existed within what was once such a pure world. The time was now, she would have to do it to put the final blow upon this twisted beast and the powers that locked her away in the darkness for so long, but with the new sun came a light that would pierce the fog of confusion and it would all drift away from her mind. For so long she had been convinced of atrocity and falsifying promises of supremacy but now she saw, that all of them were no more than lies. The bleeding of her sins would commence and once and for all it would be over. As the beast roared and gripped her throat, she did not react or struggle, but simply allowed the beast to lift her high into the air, as if she were planning something. Her heart no longer pounded. With sterile emotions she did no more than look deeply into its eyes, hiding her planned demise for this twisted and chaotic thing, beneath an exterior of hate. The beast gripped tighter, sounding her cue to end it all there and then. Anna raise her hand high, as she was drawn closer still to the beast and drew a sword from a case of red leather and encrusted diamond. Forged with the fires of the hearts of all that she had crushed, she knew this was her only chance. As the beast simply smirked and continued not to acknowledge the sword, which towered two meters in height above her head, with her hair whipping into a frenzy and flailing like the compulsive rush of adrenaline that overcame her. With one moment, once action and a menacing assault. Into the heart of the beast she plunged it, striking its center an almighty roar came from the beast, as it began to weaken the grasp upon her neck. Falling to the floor with a thud that caused volcanoes to erupt and valleys to be born, she rolled aside into a heap of twisted stone. Standing on the spot, she looked at the beast, which held is bleeding chest, as the mighty blade glistened as it stood erect within its body. She smiled for she was now near certainty that all those who this creature should have hurt, would now be safe from the harm and torment that had overrun her own soul and despite her sickness and dysfunctional actions, only her humanity could destroy the desire to cause this evil to happen to people. She walked upon its carcass, as it slowly breathed, she drew the sword and held the blade at its throat. “Time to die…” She growled in a low and seemingly confident tone. She said this whilst standing atop the monster of a being and it simply looked up at her and snarled. The creature then responded, perfectly clearly, with Anna telepathically, as it was once more the man now, as he had lost his transformed state and he lay battered upon the floor. “This Anna is not the end. It is only the beginning…” As this sound recurred and echoed through her confused mind, the figure disappeared and once more everything was as it had been before. She stood in the dark neck of forest by the county roadside holding the sword. She knew not what to make of it all, for she was tired and confused. However, as she thought about it more, for closure she looked into the puddles of blood upon the floor and cast a bloody reflection of herself back into her eyes. Then she did no more than look into the heavens and wondered: Would it ever end?
