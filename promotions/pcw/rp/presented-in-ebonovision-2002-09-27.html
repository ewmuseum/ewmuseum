Date: 27/09/02 - This RP is brought to you by Ebonovision. Ebonovision is a product of Gazcorp and no offense is made to those of a racial division. And anyway, IT'S JUST P.W.A MESSING AROUND! - The scene opens in a rough looking neighbourhood. Terraced houses make up the street, most of them boarded up and covered in graffiti. A few children play around a fire hydrant, laughing happily. They continue to dance around until they catch sight of in incoming vehicle. Gaz: STOP! Narrator: What? Gaz: This is in Ebonovision! Now start doing it like you're supposed to! Narrator. Fine, fine... The scene rewinds again and starts over, this time somewhat differently. Da scene opens in da hood. Dere's shit all over da street and some kids are doing their thang by a hydrant. They soon look up as a ride comes by. A purple hearse comes into view with two mean muthas inside. On da left, yo ass is gonna see a guy dressed in green. I ain't talking no suit, dawg. I mean green wifebeater, green shorts in denim, green headband. Shit man, his hair is green. Da man on the right ain't in green. That biatch got long hair and it's frickin' purple. He ain't wearing no shirt and he got these crazy ass sunglasses on with some jeans where da fly goes down to his knees, brutha. Yo ass sees they be packing heat too. Even if it is water guns. Both da crackaz be splashed in gravy too so dey don't look like crackaz no more. Dey be cruisin wit' some groovy ass track blastin' too - Bizasement Jizaxx: 'Yo yo, where yo head be at, bitch?' Da man says something and I'm telling ya bro, he ain't smiling. 'Why the hell did I let you talk me into this...' Rezz sez. The short guy gives him one mean elbow and he says sumpthin' sumpthin' again. 'What da fuck is dis shit, brutha?' da vamp says. 'Because we gotta get inside the craniums of da Playaz Inc. if our asses want any chizance of beating them, G.' Da Jester hollaz. 'So why you drag my grey ass into dis? I coulda bin kick it wit' da Asylum homies, but you steal my ride and now yo ass wants me to look inside the crazy heads of those biatches so we can go 1-2-3? That's twisted, man.' the bloodsucka bitches. 'Look, da way I see thangs, da #1 law up in here is know thy enemy. So I say we find out what's going on by asking da li'l guyz what's cracking. Den we know how to beat Playaz Inc.' Gaz da man wit da mastaplan says. 'Dey ain't gonna give it up for free, bro. What you gonna give dey asses, I ain't handing over no bling-bling!' dat vamp whines. 'Fo shizzle ma nizzle. Dat's why I brought some bizackup. Now let me do my thing and Holla Back, Immortalin!' Da clown man reaches into the back, pulls a sheet off some shit and brings it to the front. The ride stops moving and da men step out. Dere's Lugz on dey feet and Rezz gut Fubu that go in li'l piles round his ankles. 'Ok, I've had it. From now on, I'm doing my parts in Crackavision.' Nerezza steps out of the picture, leaving Gaz looking puzzled. Moments later, he re-appears, clad in his usual outfit of a sweeping red coat to his ankles, regular dark blue jeans and a black T-shirt. The gravy that once marked his skin is gone, leaving the pale colour behind again. He follows Gaz as the Jester marches over to the children. 'Yo, kids... sup?' Gizaz says, sporting some kinda shit in his hands. Looks like the man got some kinda book. Da tallest of the kids takes one look at his ass and hollaz: 'What yo fake ass want?' da kid snaps, seeing Gaz wearing dat counterfeit shit. 'Yo, chill homeboy. I just wanna offer yo ass some good stuff.' Gaz flashes a big-ass smile. 'No way dude, I'm only 6 years old. Momma says I gotta be a good boy else I'll end up as a pro-wrestler, so not for another year!' da li'l guy turns away. Gizaz shows da kidz what he got. He gives each of the li'l guys a book. Dey look at it and begin to read: 'Dr Seuss in da hood?' Da oldest kid don't have a damn clue what's going on. Another of the kidz reads what da books say out loud. 'One Bitch. Two Bitch. Dead Bitch. Blue Bitch? What's this shit about?' she ain't happy. The third of dem ain't happy either. 'Da Cat In Da Backwards Baseball Hat?' '...Gaz, what the HELL are these things? I will not diss green eggs and ham... I will not diss them, Sam I am.' Nerezza reads aloud. He throws the book back into the hearse and climbs in. Gaz's smile fades as the children move away, unimpressed with the books. 'Okay, put dis shit back into Crackavision.' Gizaz commands as he walks out of the shot, re-emerging in his snappy green suit. Nerezza opens the door for him as he hops into the car, looking miserable. 'Don't worry, Gaz... I'm sure we'll find another way to beat them without transforming into Fresh Prince rejects.' the vampire says, turning the keys. 'After all, we're part of the Asylum. Part of the A-Team.' Suddenly, The Jester's eyes light up as the grin re-appears. 'Rezz, you just gave me an idea. It's time to call in some professional help...' The hearse speeds off out of the street, leaving the children to rip the books up slowly, one by one. The Biznitching End.
