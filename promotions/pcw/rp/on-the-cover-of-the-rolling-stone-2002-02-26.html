On the Cover of the Rolling Stone

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb 26 [color=red][i]It’s been quite some time since we last saw the Killin’ Villain, Jason Snow. And when we rejoin him, we find that he is still hanging around with his current flavour, Tammy Hart. She’s as gorgeous as ever, her long brown hair hanging down past her shoulders, pouting at Snow the way she does so often. The scene is a photoshoot taking place somewhere in the United States. We see Jason Snow lazing on a bed, his naked body covered only by bed sheets. His head lies in the lap of the equally nude Tammy Hart, who’s also covered only by bed sheets. A photographer does laps around them, snapping photographs all the while...[/color][/i] Photographer: “Beautiful! Beautiful! Gimme more! More! This is for the cover, you know!” [color=red][i]Lying in front of Snow is the PCW Heavyweight Championship belt. Gently, his hand brushes it while the photographer snaps another photo. The photographer is a surprisingly short, hairy man, covered in gold. What cover is this for, anyway?[/color][/i] Photographer: “Rolling Stone, people! Rolling Stone magazine!” [color=red][i]We have our answer.[/color][/i] Tammy: “How long until lunch, Joe?” [color=red][i]The photographer, Joe, pauses to check his watch. He then sighs...[/color][/i] Joe: “How about we take lunch now? I’m going down for a coffee, I’ll be back up in a few minutes.” [color=red][i]The little camera-wielding man heads off screen, leaving only Snow and Tammy Hart. Suddenly, a wild look comes over Tammy’s lovely brown eye...[/color][/i] Tammy: “Well - he’s gone. You know what I want.” [color=red][i]Snow glares stares at the young woman with a confused expression...[/color][/i] Snow: “What you... want?” [color=red][i]Tammy flops back on the bed, letting her arms sprawl out at her sides...[/color][/i] Tammy: “Take me, Snow! Take me now! NOW!” [color=red][i]A stunned expression comes over the Killin’ Villain’s face. He looks at Tammy, and then points back at the camera... She really was not expecting him to make any arguments - there are many sides to this Killin’ Villain...[/color][/i] Snow: “But wench - er, I mean, Tammy... the camera man is standing right there!” [color=red][i]Tammy shrugs her shoulders...[/color][/i] Tammy: “I’ve done worse.” [color=red][i]Snow knows that she has and so do we. We’ve all heard the stories. Snow, lying naked in the bed and covered by nothing but a bed sheet, puts his head in his hand and chuckles...[/color][/i] Snow: “You’re serious?” Tammy: “Of course I’m serious!” [color=red][i]Snow is silent for a moment...[/color][/i] Snow: “So it’s true...” Tammy: “What’s true?” Snow: “Giving you orgasms of a physical nature is much like throwing a hot dog down a hallway.” Tammy: “Excuse me?” Snow: “I was informed that’s the new way to call someone a slut.” [color=red][i]There’s a long moment of silence as the pair stare blankly at each other. It seems that this is their first tussle as a couple. Snow’s cold blue eyes are harsh as he speaks - it seems that her spell is finally wearing off. After a while, Tammy finally breaks the silence by laughing aloud...[/color][/i] Tammy: “Ok - whatever you say Snow. All I know is that we have a couple of spare minutes, so stop playing this morality role and get over here.” Snow: “Are you, wench - and YES I CALLED YOU WENCH - implying that I, the Killin’ Villain, am an immoral creature!?” [color=red][i]Tammy half chuckles - she thinks he’s joking. Snow, however, is anything but joking. His eyes are growing bloodshot as he glares hard at her. She does nothing but laugh... and suddenly... he hates her...[/color][/i] Snow: “I’ll have you know that I... I... ...” [color=red][i]He can say nothing. He lowers his head. What’s going on with the Killin’ Villain anyway? We’re not used to seeing him this way. He’s nearly shaking. Are we seeing, at this most inopportune of moments, a breakdown of the Killin’ Villain. What can be going through his mind. And then, when he looks at the nude woman, covered only by a bed sheet, we realize... he doesn’t want any of this anymore. The lights... the glamour... things were so much better when they were simpler...[/color][/i] Tammy: “What’s going on with you anyway...?” Snow: “By Greatness... I’m not sure...” [color=red][i]It’s a rare occasion to see Snow in an honest state. With that, he stands up from the bed, holding the white sheet around his waist, and looks down at the surprised young woman...[/color][/i] Tammy: “What are you doing?” Snow: “Leaving.” [color=red][i]Tammy sits up and her face is finally serious...[/color][/i] Tammy: “WHAT!? Leaving? Where are you going?” Snow: “... as Great as I may be... I’m not too sure...” [color=red][i]And with that, the young PCW Champion turns away and makes his exit. When he gets out the door, we assume that he is spotted by on an on-looker because we hear his stern voice...[/color][/i] Snow: “What in the he1l are you looking at!? Have you never seen a Superior Individual before!?” [color=red][i]And that’s the last we hear from him, though his final words are much more along the lines of what we’re used to. At that moment, the photographer returns, surprised to find that there is no more Killin’ Villain to photograph... fade to black...[/color][/i]
