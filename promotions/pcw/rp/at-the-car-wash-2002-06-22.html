At The Car Wash

22 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/22/02 The scene opens up outside of Fred's Car Wash. It's a rather small car wash, but clean, which is located right behind Speedway, a gas station. Kramer's rather familiar black truck pulls up behind a dark red Saturn that is next in line to get a scrub down. Realizing it might be a while, Kramer hops out of his truck and walks into Speedway to get a bite to eat. He would look decent if it weren't for the backwards Bugs Bunny baseball cap. Instead he just looks like a fool with his white t-shirt tucked into his baggy blue jeans. Oh and did I mention the big chain necklace circling around his throat? Identity crisis anyone? Anyway.....Kramer takes a few candy bars and some gum and plops it down onto the counter. The young lady behind the counter, looking quite mellow in her pitch black hair and black ensemble, looks at the food and starts registering it on the cash register. Lady: That'll be two dollars even. Kramer takes out a wadded mass of dollars from his pocket and pulls out two singles. Being the nice man he is, he tries hard to smoothen them on the counter before handing them to the lady. As he hands it to her his hand brushes hers which sends a tingly sensation up his spine. Kramer quickly steps back from the counter and stares at the woman for a minute trying to figure something out. Lady: Is something wrong? Kramer: I....uh....I read in a book that said if you felt a tingling feeling after you touched someone, then you were meant to spend the rest of your lives together. You felt it too, right? The lady looks far from amused at Kramer's antics, but manages to stay cool. Lady: Yes I felt it. Hearing this, Kramer gets a little overjoyed. He takes a huge step back up to the counter and leans on it a bit still checking her out. Kramer: So was it as good for you as it was for me? Kramer gives her a full smile as she slumps her head forward shaking and laughing. Kramer: What's so funny? Lady: Have you ever heard of static electricity? Kramer: Yes. Why? Lady: You dufus. While you were shuffling your feet on the carpet getting your junk together, you were building up the electricity. Because this countertop is a form of metal, when I reached out, and you reached down, you caused an electric shock. Kramer: Oh.... Kramer's eyebrows shift down as he thinks about it. Then it finally clicks and he grabs his junk food. Kramer: So you still want to get together sometime? Lady: Listen. Even if I did like men, you aren't my type. Kramer: Oh we got a lot of women like that in PCW, or so I've heard. Before the lady can ask what PCW is, Kramer is back outside and to his car. Since he had taken so long in the gas station, a few cars cut ahead of him, but it doesn't seem to bother him as he gets back in his truck and just waits. He picks up a Snickers bar and peels off the wrapper. He takes a huge bite consuming half of it at once. As he is munching down he remembers that he has Grimer with him. He pulls down the glove box and looks inside. He sees little Grimer, in his teeny wife beater shirt, munching down on huge grain pellets. As the camera is zooming in on Grimer, we are blown away by what it looks like inside. Inside there is white silk cushioning all around so Grimer doesn't get hurt with a sudden stop or something of that nature. There is also a little bed and of course food and water. Grimer finishes eating and looks up to Kramer who holds his hand out. Grimer crawls onto his hand, and Kramer pulls him out in front of him petting Grimer lightly on the head. Kramer: Hey little bud. A huge smile etches itself across Kramer's face as Grimer runs up his arm and rubs his head against Kramer's soft chin. It's quite weird, but you can see the joy that Grimer and Kramer have when they are in each others company. The way their eyes light up with the mere sight of each other is just indescribable. As Grimer runs back down Kramer's arm, the silent atmosphere is blown to bits by a loud car honking from behind. Kramer looks ahead and realizes that its finally his turn so he puts the car in drive and pulls in the opening. From his baggy blue jeans he pulls out a pocketful of quarters and starts loading them into the machine. After paying the right amount, and pushing a lot of buttons, he checks the windows to make sure that they are both fully rolled up. Then he presses on as Grimer takes a seat on the passengers side. Instantly the windows are engulfed in an army of suds. Then they come up to two huge spinney things that soak the suds deeper to the car making sure to get all of the dirt. The suds and dirt disappear though, as huge blasts of water, from both sides, spray down the truck. Finally they go through the dryers and are back outside with a brand new looking truck. Kramer: Now only if you could wash away life's problems like that. Pay a few quarters and BLAMO! your problems are washed away down the drain. He drives past some cars and is back out on the highway where he quickly picks up speed, narrowly missing a car to his right. He looks down at Grimer only to see that he is fast asleep. Not wanting him to get hurt, Kramer scoops him up and sets him in his bed and closes the glove compartment while at the red light. Needing some form of company, Kramer turns on the radio and starts surfing the channels waiting for something interesting. He stops at the mention of wrestling and turns the volume up interested in what they are talking about. Man: Little Speedo got slammed. I thought he was going to die when Big Bad Mean Guy tossed him into that pit of hungry lions. Other Man: Who in the hell needs two arms anyway? Man: Moving on....oh what about the big PCW pay per view coming up? Other Man: Gold and Glory isn't it? Man: Yes it is. Other Man: That is going to be rocking. I mean where else could you see angry black men, Nazi's, Indians, and super heroes? The only place I know of is PCW. Man: The match to watch will be the trailer trash brawl. Violence Jack is just going to dominate. Other Man: It should be interesting but what about the steel cage match? That's just going to kick ass no doubt...... Before we can hear anymore, Kramer turns it off. Kramer: What's so great about this Violence Jack guy anyway? And everyone else involved for that matter? Do they have KFC backing them? I don't think so!! Do they get hulked up on crispy pieces of chicken? I don't think so!! Only I do that! Since it is in a trailer I am going to have to stash some chicken somewhere, otherwise I could be royally screwed. Ah...but that's a little over a week away. What I should be concerned about is Tuesday Turmoil. Yet again I get the chance to regain the TV title. I just have to find a way to plow through Frampton, TMM, and Violence Jack. Wouldn't that be ever so cool if I held two titles at once? I would be the talk of the town!! While he is talking up a storm, he almost hits another car, but manages to swerve away. The scene slowly fades to black as Kramer continues to babble on.
