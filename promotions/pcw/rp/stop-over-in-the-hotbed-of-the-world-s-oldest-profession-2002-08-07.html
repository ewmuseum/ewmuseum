Date: 08/07/02 *THUD!* The End falls flat on his back, out cold. A little dust flies out away from The End, but quickly settles. The ground is so eroded, there's not even enough dust on the ground to scatter. The houses in the area are horribly maintained. Some still don't have basic amenities. They houses are made of straw or wood; perhaps even clay if the inhabitants are wealthy. Young women of all ages roam the streets, looking for a tourist to turn a trick. The sky is gray, the unpaved reddish-brown ground beaten into a misshapen grid of parallel and crisscrossing footprints upon the earth. The Misfits now have lost their bags; though they are a great distance away from where they were before, they haven't changed clothes since the cruise ship. Everyone's clothes are dirty now, and they smell, but not as much as the place they have come to today. What will happen today? Find out… Lamar pinches his nose with his right hand, standing away from the group and looking around at all the flies that seem to LOVE the area. Lamar:'It smells like fish here.' Lexicon has his leather jacket around his waist. Despite the gray skies, it's very warm here. He stands behind Slade, who is sitting on the ground trying to read the map of the area in his possession. Lexicon:'Slade, since you bear the US Navy map, tell us where we are on the globe?' Slade looks the map over. Hard to believe he can read a map even though he's always got his eyes closed. But anyway, he makes out six letters, which explain both The End's condition and the strange smell. Slade:'H-A-I-N-A-N.' (Slade can string together individual letters to form individual words, but only at an extremely slow pace. When he needs to say sentences or entire paragraphs, he terribly slurs together his speech. But he's lucky that he is able to say anything at all. All his life, he's been asleep, with his subconscious mind acting for him in the conscious world.) Sandy:'Hainan? The island of Hainan near China?' Richard:'Appears so.' Richard turns his attention back to The End. Richard:'Richard has some smelling salts. Richard will wake End up.' Richard reaches into the pocket of his white (but not for long) Bermuda shorts and pulls out a tiny packet of smelling salts. Slade dives in and slaps the salts out of his hand before he can use them on The End. Slade:'Adouihserfoiuref!' Richard:'What did Richard do wrong?' Lexicon:'Don't you get it? He has a heightened sense of smell. If he got knocked out by the less-than-overpowering, but still pervasive smell of fish in the air, the smelling salts could kill him!' Richard looks at the salts in his hand. Richard:'Oh.' Drek plugs The End's nose and pounds on his chest with his small, but muscular arms. After a little while, End comes back around. End:'What happened?' Lamar:'You just fell out of it. It was funny.' End sits up and rubs his head. End:'How long was I out?' Richard:'Not too long. Richard tried to wake you up with smelling salts.' The End looks up at Richard in a 'What were you thinking?' expression on his face (he wears his basic wrestling attire underneath some his University of Miami sweats, and the mask is in the pocket), but not wearing his mask right now). End:'You're kidding, right? That stuff would kill me. Sensory overload; brain would burn out, not a good thing.' Richard just stands there until Sandy Wong jabs Richard in the arm with her elbow. Richard:'Richard apologizes.' The End takes a deep breath. The foul air rushes into his nose and lungs and he nearly passes out again. End:'HOLY--!!' He exhales quickly and makes a resolution to breath through his mouth for the remainder of their time here. Coughing, The End stands up, and slowly adjusts. End:'It's alright. Man, this place reeks.' Sandy:'That's probably why you passed out in the first place.' The End shakes his head. End:“Where are we?” Lexicon:“Hainan.” End:“The island world-infamous for its rampant prostitution?” Sandy:“Yeah.” The End turns his head to Richard. End:“I thought we revoked your navigation privileges?” Richard shakes his head. Richard:“You gave them back when Richard saved you from the quicksand in the Congo.” Drek:“Ind ah’m still try’n t’figr oot how we goht ther’.” Rmembering his Cruiserweight Title defense, The End consults Slade. End:'Slade, what time is it back home?' Slade looks at his watch. Slade:'Muenoadkniwe ulaehjno iodjs.' The End slaps his head. Though he's wearing his trademark white sunglasses, The End still opens his eyes wide (force of habit). End:'11:25?! We've got to get back! The changes in time zones throws me off everytime. I will have to relinquish the Cruiserweight Title to Brandi if I don't get back in time to defend it.' The End starts walking off to the North. The tree stops him. End: *Rubbing nose*'Frickin'... does anybody know where we're supposed to be going?' Richard is about to say something, but some young Asian girls (a good possibility that they're not even 18) come up and tug at his arms, trying to pull him into a nearby building. Richard:'Where are you taking Richard?' Richard doesn't put up a fight, and they take him away. Drek:'Do we 'ave t'wait fer 'im?' Lexicon:'Yes.' Drek:'Dahmmit!' A seedy-looking Asian man who bears a striking resemblance to Hoobastank comes and grabs Sandy Wong by the arm. Asian Pimp:“Where you been? I got customers waiting! American, rich!” Sandy jerks back, trying to get loose. Sandy:“Who are you? Let go of me!” Before anything else can transpire, The End grabs the pimp from behind and puts him to sleep with an reverse neck crank. He crumples to the ground. Sandy backs away, shocked and disgusted. Sandy:“You killed him!” The End doesn’t say anything, he just steps away from the pimp and Sandy and walks off. Drek explains for The End. Drek:“Nay, joost knocked ‘im oot.” Sandy:“Did you have to be so violent about it?” End:“Did you want to be forced to perform sex acts at his command?” She walks up to The End, who is leaning against a wooden sign waiting for Richard to rejoin the group. Sandy is still stuck in place from what she just saw. Lexicon comes up to Sandy, attempting to set things straight. Lexicon:“Shall you be alright?” Sandy:“I guess. I’m not used to seeing a guy I know just casually walk up to some stranger and crack his neck. I asked to be his valet just to stay in familiar PCW territory, but I don’t know if I can go along with being a valet to someone so violent.” Lexicon shrugs. Lexicon:“In the defense of my friend and comrade, he was protecting you more than he was acting with malice toward the asian pimp. Believe me, I’ve known The End for nearly a year, even before he came to PCW. The only time he would actually harm someone is if it happened in a wrestling ring. As intimidating as he may seem, he wouldn’t hurt a fly.” That reassures Sandy a little bit, but not much. Lexicon continues. Lexicon:“And anyway, you should cut him a little slack. He’s not really very experienced with women. He’s not really sure how he should be around you. He doesn’t want to offend you, but he isn’t sure how not to.” Sandy looks at Lexicon with an oddly inquisitive expression. Sandy:“Really? I’m worth that much to him?” Lexicon nods and whispers to Sandy, not so much for secret, but just to emphasize the “FYI” nature of his next statement. Lexicon:“Indeed. To speak but the truth, he’s actually had a slight crush on you for a little while now.” Sandy:“A crush?” Sandy thinks, “Aw, how cute.” Sandy:“Did he happen to mention what he liked about me?” Lexicon:“Not too much. He did say that you had a nice voice.” Sandy thinks better of her previous response. She walks over to The End and places on hand on his shoulder. Sandy:“Um... thanks.” She leaves it at that. In a hut around the block that contains nothing more than a small bed with the stained white sheets serving as the door, Lamar is looking down south at a naked Asian woman, who’s lying down on the ground with her legs spread. He points to her vagina. Lamar:“What’s that?” Slade sticks his head in the hut. Slade:“Jeuwemasdh!” Lamar:“I don’t know, she said she wanted to show me something.” Slade:“Dmer jernfiluhc iope rqwem!” Lamar:“She’s a prostitute?” Lamar looks down at the teenager. Lamar:“You’re a prostitute?” The teen nods her head. Lamar looks back at Slade, confused. Lamar:“What’s a prostitute?” Slade steps into the hut and drags Lamar out. There are screams from the village people outside. Slade comes back into the hut, finds Lamar’s pants, and takes them back out to Lamar. 10 minutes later... *Laughs from the Misfits, aware of the narration* Richard:“Richard apologizes, but Richard could not last a moment longer.” The Asian girls laugh, though disappointed, as Richard stumbles out of the hut, unable to walk straight. Drek:“Heh, mahn kint wolk. Must’ve hah’d ix’prts woorkin’ on ‘im.” Lexicon:“No, I thin kit’s just his own lack of stamina that’s the problem.” Richard crawls over to the group. Richard:“Richard is having trouble seeing.” End:“Hey, that’s my gimmick.” Back in the hospital, Kay Fabe gets the wrong chart left at his bed. The nurse wrote in the wrong care information. I won’t subject you to his punishment, let’s just look at the chart. Kay FabeDiagnosis: Various Injuries; too many to listTreatment: Enema w/ HCL (hydrochloric acid) Slade:“Asdio mrfnu v wernuce.” Richard shakes his head. Richard:“No, it’s not that. Richard also has aches in his joints, and there are strange cysts in his pubic area. Look.” The Misfits and Sandy Wong shield their eyes. Drek:“Ey! Thah’ts nought soomethin’ we want t’see!” Lexicon looks up at The End standing next to him. Lexicon:“Sometimes I envy you, my friend.” Sandy grabs Richard by his arm and drags him over to a clean, well-maintained computer standing in the middle of the filthy cesspool that is Hainan. The End and The Misfits follow. Sandy turns on the computer and opens up the main virus detector application. Sandy:“This computer here is set up for Western tourists who come to... get their freak on. They type in their symptoms, and the computer will tell them if and what STD that they have.” Sandy tacks away at the keys, much like the writer of this RP is doing right now. (Kay Fabe is safe, only breaks of kayfabe by the characters results in his injury.) Sandy:“Blurry vision, aches, cysts...” The computer processes the information. It takes a while, during which a bus of tourists stops in town just a few feet away. A “Ding!” and the computer is done, and gives a report by a computer voice. Computer:“You’ve got syphilis!” Richard snaps his fingers. Richard:“F*CK!” “That’s what we come here for!” says the eavesdropping Sebastian Toomes of OSW, who stands in the middle of the square and drops his pants. His extremely large penis hangs down to the floor, and he smiles in anticipation as six Asian women flock to him.
