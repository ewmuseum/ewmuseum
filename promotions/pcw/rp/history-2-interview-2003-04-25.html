History #2 Interview

25 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/4/03 Belief is something that gets you through the world, without belief in anything there would only be doubt and a human soul simply cannot work on hope alone. There has to be belief pushing you through. You must always believe. The weather had changed drastically in just a few days. James blamed the meteorological office; secretly he thought they owned a special machine, which they used to control the weather, then broadcast the complete opposite just for their own personal pleasure. The rain was lashing violently against the window of the office block which James was currently sat in. The room was sparse to say the least, a few tasteless wall hanging and a random assortment of chairs placed almost randomly around the room. James leant back in his chair and sighed deeply running his hand through his hair as he did so resting them on the back of his neck as he stared at the ceiling fan spinning rapidly letting out an annoying hum. James moved his had down and glanced at his watch. It was now two-twenty seven in the afternoon. James had been sat with the humming for just over two hours now and it was beginning to wear away at him, it made his flesh crawl. “You better get used to that hum pal, you are going to be hearing it for a while yet they always do it. I always consider standing the humming to be the first test” the voice emanating from a man in front of him. James looked straight ahead and was greeted by the sight of some muscles attached to a body. He could not believe he had not noticed him when he had entered the room. To say that he was well built was an understatement. Every aspect of his body was over developed, this in itself caused James to be a little more than intimidated causing him to momentarily question his current choice of career. James knew there was no way that he would be able to stand toe to toe with a person built like that for a start his muscles would prevent that from happening. “So you’ve done all of this before then” asked James attempting in vain to maintain some degree of manliness by keeping eye contact with the man mountain before him. “Have I done this before? If I say my name you will know just who I am and you will be thankful to be in the same room as me for I am Ehren Davis former PCW and OSW world champion” boomed Ehren his voice full of pride and passion. James stared blankly at James before the corners of his lips curled upwards into a smirk, then a smile before he finally broke out into a hysterical laughter. Ehren climbed out of his chair and moved angrily towards James flexing his muscles as he walked. James fell backwards off his chair due to the uncontrollable laughter and began to struggle for air. “What the hell is so funny, who the hell do you think you are laughing at a hall of famer” scowled Ehren which set James off again laughing uncontrollably once more rolling around on the floor, struggling to pick himself up James slumped back in the chair and sighed happily. “Thanks man I needed a good laugh. I was really worried about the whole interview process but now I’m good. Got a few questions for you though mate. Who exactly did you beat to become world champion in both PCW and OSW just out of interest of course” quizzed James as Ehren’s red face of anger began to fade into a red face of embarrassment. “Also if you were a world champion and a hall of famer surely you wouldn’t have to go through the interview process the feds would be all over you they would fight for you and welcome you with arms wide open, one more thing I’m sure that PCW, OSW all of the big feds favour home grown talent” said James a huge grin on his face as he realised he was cutting into the man mountain and a huge rate of knots. “My talent is all my own” roared Ehren, a few particles of spit landing on James’ cheek as he did so James slowly raised a hand to his face and wiped it off. “Thanks for that. Your talent maybe home grown but there is no way in hell that your muscles are. That’s probably why you keep failing. You are so roided up that you muscles are just for show, they don’t hold any real physical value anymore. They are little more than aesthetically pleasing and they don’t do that very well. “Commented James who was taking an unusual amount of pleasure in pointing out the downfalls of the person who just a few moments ago had intimidated him so much. “They’ll be more than enough to crush a worm like you,” snarled Ehren as he stepped forward and swung viciously at James’ head. James let natural instinct take over as he leaned backward avoiding the initial swing. He than feinted to his left crouched before exploding with an uppercut to the jaw of Ehren, which sent him sprawling to the floor. James stepped backwards rubbing his fist. It was the first time he had actually hit another man and the adrenaline rush from it was a strange feeling that he had not experienced in quite some time “There’s another reason you were rejected you loser you can’t even take a punch” spat James in disgust as he gathered his belongings and headed towards the exit slamming it behind him. In the corner of the room a small video camera had picked up every thing that had happened. Upstairs a few floors a group of executives were watching the events on a large plasma screen along the wall. “I told you he had spunk. He has the fire in the belly and the aggression I say we get him and we try to hold onto him” spoke one of the executives as he sat smiling at the video screen. “How can we sign him? Look at the evidence. He has no background in wrestling. He held his hand after punching someone. How is he going to react to being hit with a piledriver or backbreaker he’ll run home screaming to his mother.” Replied a second executive who didn’t like the look of James from the first moment he saw him and was extremely reluctant to change his view. “I have to agree with Clive. James may have a good physique and a good head on his shoulders but when push comes to shove he is not a wrestler he is a failed astronaut at best he can we possibly consider implementing him onto our roster”. Said a third voice sprouting authority with every word. The room fell silence until a voice at the head of the table spoke just a few short words, which changed the situation entirely. “Because I said so.”
