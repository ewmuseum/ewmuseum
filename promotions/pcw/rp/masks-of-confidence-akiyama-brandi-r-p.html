Masks of Confidence (Akiyama/Brandi R/P)

Date: Sometime this week... The sun rained down with nice, warm…skin-caner-festering…rays of goodness. The waters sparkled from the said rays, the waves crashing softly against the hull of the cruise ship. There was a line of people trying to get on board; their luggage nestled snugly under their arms, their feeble little muscle straining to hold a bundle of fabrics. All of them…littered in wild outlandish colors that some would call tropical. I don’t care who you are…pink is not a color you should wear…especially if you’re some big sweaty guy with blubbery arms who looked like he swallowed the whole cow. Moving aside from fat slob tourist number twenty… On board the boat, the already settled passengers roamed about freely, enjoying the leisurely activities of the cruise liner. They played shuffleboard, swam in the pool, took up Broadway shows…all that cheesy expensive crap not worth the money. There were some PCW celebs around as well…wining and dining with the commoners. Look there…Referee JJ is playfully jobbing to the three year old in the overalls. Head of Security Sandy Spitz handing out little taser glowsticks to the passing kids… Interviewer Sandy Wong is posing for pictures with some of the male passengers… What a voyage this will be… “I detest this salty smell…” At the back of the boat, leaning against the railing…we find our two world-destroyers… Cyclonica and Akiyama. Despite the hot temperature, they’re still decked out in the tight black vinyl pants (Akiyama’s with many zippers along them), black leather boots with silver lacing them (Cyclonica with her silver stiletto heels), Akiyama with his see through black mesh shirt and black taped forearms, and Cyclonica with her ice blue PVC halter underneath her black mesh see through shirt. Both of them glowered with their arms folded across their chests as the breeze fluttered past them, their streaked hair fluttering a bit in the breeze. “That smell…it’s so pungent and irritating. It’s liable to give me headache.” Her silver-lenses glance over to her right, staring into the couple in their swimwear holding hands as they stared out at the waters. Once again her nose wrinkled up in disgust. Was the Divine Prophetess upset with the water? “Ugh…humans…” Maybe not… Two kids came running along, their smiling faces flashing brightly as they played. The god let out a growl as his eyes followed them around the corner. Another frown came upon her perfect face. “And they’re…having fun. No suffering…no pain…no anguish…nothing! And do you know why that is, Akiyama?” He looked at her with a stiff nod. She nodded back, a small gleam coming into her eyes. “Of course you do! You’re the Neo Void God…omniscient and omnipotent. These flesh-bags are so pleasant because they think that since they’re on vacation, they have all right and reason to lose their worries! They think they can engross themselves in their own little pleasures for this little voyage…and never again think of the horrors that real life have to offer! Those 6 buffoons constantly dismiss the pains and sufferings that they know will fall upon them at your godly hands! They refuse to receive you as their one true GOD OF TAG…and their disbelief shall be their own undoing!” Akiyama smacked his fist into his palm, glowering at the utter disrespect of his superiority. Cyclonica placed her hand on his fist, drawing his attention. “But first…we have the DIVINE Duty…to make these people on this boat…realize that there is no vacation from the wrath of the god.” Her face bred an evil look…one to send shivers into the hearts of the courageous. Shadows fell upon them as they glared ahead, only them knowing what evil and dark plans lay in their minds… …Then the sun came back out from behind the clouds… Cyclonica looked at the nVg and nodded. “Let’s go…” ¤ Meanwhile ¤ “Dude, look at those legs! She’s like super-HOT!” “I know!! I can’t believe I’m seeing her this close!! And IN THAT!!” “She’s a goddess!! And check out those hubajoobs!! They’re perfect!” Three youths in their late teens stood at the edge of the corner peering around at someone. They were dressed in various swim trunks of tropical design. From the words they spoke, it sounded like they were staring at a certain Divine Prophetess we know… The red headed one grinned a perverse grin. “Dude…go over there and say something!” The blonde shook his head nervously. “No way! She’s like so out of our leagues!! She’s on TV and everything!” The brown haired kid laughed at him. “Sissy! I bet you couldn’t even stare at her shmanky-shmank if she showed it to you!!” A shadow fell over the chuckling teens from behind…an advancing position that the Heartbreakers frequently use. They turned around and frightened looks came over all three faces. “What…are you chumps doing?” The brown haired kid lowered his expression, changing it to one of annoyance. “Hank! Come on now…you scared the sh!t out of us!!” WHACK!! “Watch your mouth, bro.” The large brunette glared down at his brother, the younger one rubbing his arm. “Anyway, what are you guys doing?” “Look around the corner and take a peek!” the red head said. Hank looked around the corner, then looked back at the trio. “What? Her?” “HER?? HER??? She’s more than just a HER!! She’s the goddess of PCW!! I should smack you for undermining her and her superior posterior!!” SMACK!! Hank’s brother held his face as Hank cracked his knuckles. He sighed as she stepped away from the corner. “You kids…I’ll show you all.” He walked around the corner and stopped in the middle of the hallway. He looked down at someone and smiled confidently. “Hi…I’m Hank. And you are?” We basked our eyes on a vision of beauty, long silky legs, her entire body glistening from suntan lotion. The woman wore a sky blue bikini tied around with strings. On her face was a pair of light blue sunglasses, which you could see her eyes were closed from within them. She let out a tired sigh as she brushed some of her brown hair out of her face. Then she looked up at the muscular man with a polite smile. “Brandi…but I’m sure you knew that already.” Hank looked a bit flustered as she sat up and looked at him. “Um…yeah, I did. I was trying to be polite I guess. How’s it going?” She looked away for a second, in thought. “I was doing fine…until now.” “Oh?” Hank didn’t like that statement much. Brandi looked back at him, politely smiling at him again. “You said your name was Hank right?” He beamed, “Yep!” “I knew a Hank…I liked him better.” And the smile faded from his lips… “Anyway, you’re in my sunlight…so if you don’t mind, I’d like to go back to my nap.” She laid back down, leaving our boy Hank fuming with embarrassment. He walked back towards his corner, where his brother and friends were snickering at his failure. “And Hank?” He turned around, hope filling up in him again. “Tell your buddies to get lost…I’m not in the mood for an audience.” The trio’s faces dropped as they disappeared from the corner, Hank glumly following behind them. Brandi smiled to herself as she got comfortable again. But after a few minutes again…her sunlight was blocked again. She huffed as she sat up again. “Okay, I was polite the first time…now I’m going to have to make you leave me alone!” But her voice squeaked off when she realized who was standing there. Yep…the Neo Void God… She screamed as she jumped off her beach chair. She turned to run, but ran smack dab into the Divine Prophetess standing behind her. She sat on the ground looking up at Akiyama and Cyclonica, overwhelmed by their presence. Cyclonica’s eyes pierced her own. “Those flat-scans were bothering you…” Brandi watched as she looked to Akiyama, and then the two of them walked away. She stood back up and watched as they disappeared around the corner. “HEY!” Hank and the trio turned around to find the Divine Prophetess standing there at the corner leaning against the wall. She motioned with her finger for them to come closer. “Yes?” Hank was a bit more interested in her, or anyone that would give him time. Cyclonica held up a life saver towards him…not the candy, the life saving doughnut. “Hold this in front of your face, and I’ll give you something that’ll knock you for a loop.” The wink she gave him was enough to convince him. He grabbed the preserver and held it in his face. He closed his eyes and puckered up. “Ready!” Cyclonica looked around him and nodded at his friends, all three of them watching with eager eyes and bulging shorts. Hank wanted to take a look at this magic moment for himself…so he opened his eyes… ¤ HAND OF FATE!! ¤ The fist of the god went straight through the foam doughnut into Hank’s mouth…and down he went out cold. His brother and friends watched in shock as Cyclonica stood back up and grinned in his unconscious face. Akiyama then picked up Hank and dropped him overboard. “Mortal overboard…” Cyclonica gloated as the two of them walked away. Hank’s brother and the two all flocked to the railing, where Hank was floating away with the life saver locked around his head. Brandi watched in awe as they came walking back to her. She just stared at them, her mouth agape. Cyclonica glared into her irritated. “Close your frickin mouth…it’s almost as if you were inviting men to have oral.” She snapped her jaw shut, looking a bit annoyed at her. Then Cyclonica and Akiyama walked away, silently. Brandi watched for a second, but started running after them. “Hey!” she called out. “Hey!” Turning around… “What now, woman?” “Why’d you do that?” it was strange…no one questioned the god…not without expecting to be destroyed afterwards. But she was genuinely curious. Cyclonica shrugged. “They don’t know the fear of the god. So we showed them…as we will show those tag teams soon.” She leaned in close to Brandi, frowning in her face. “You…are a just an exception.” “What? Why?” Brandi asked on, thought she should have known that getting a clear answer out of the Divine Prophetess was harder than Jack Valentine looking at a guy in Speedos. “Because…everyone in this world has done nothing but to go out of their way to stand in the way of Akiyama…even you at some point. But you did it unaware of what you were doing. You’re still a part of the problem…” Akiyama took a step forward, bearing his presence down upon Brandi. She stepped back, taking up a fighting stance…who knows anymore why humans foolishly try to step against the god. Cyclonica stuck her hand out between him and her suddenly. “You…on the other hand…are a weaker part of the problem.” She turned on her heel and walked off. Akiyama glared down the smaller PCW star and walked off. Brandi watched them…until… “Come along!” ¤ Soon… ¤ “You see them? They look the same to you, do they not?” Cyclonica, Akiyama, and Brandi all walked along the boardwalk of the boat. Cyclonica pointed to the people standing there having fun, enjoying life as it is. She was about to break it down for the former Extreme champion. “If you think that, then you are more than wrong, Wilson. Take a look over at that couple…tell me what you see.” Brandi placed her eyes upon a man and woman with their child posing for a picture. They all smiled as the cameraman began to take their pictures. “Looks like a family taking a picture…what’s wrong with that?” “Happy they seem…but you know nothing about the anger the man holds for his wife…for having child…ending his freedom… locking him into a lifetime of the prison called marriage. Do you hear their cries for escape? I do…” “That’s nonsense.” Brandi uttered. Cyclonica shook her head at her words. “You think so?” she snapped her fingers, and Brandi had a sudden sense of being watched. “You feel that? Feels bad doesn’t it? Take a look back at your happy couple.” She looked back, and to her surprise, she found the man standing next to his wife drilling holes into Brandi’s behind. She shuddered, and noticed that his wife’s eyes were also caught on something. She followed the trail, finding her glance left upon the muscular frame of Akiyama. Cyclonica snapped her head back and hissed at them both, setting them both straight in one motion. Then she looked back as they continued walking. “Shows what you know.” “What’s the point in all this?” Brandi was getting upset with the presence of the Celestials…much as everyone else did. Akiyama gave the Divine Prophetess a look before looking back at Brandi. Cyclonica waved her hand back at her. “The point…dear woman…is that things are not always as they seem. Everything looks peachy on the outside, but inside there’s more turmoil and strife. With everyone trying to put up a mask like everything is peaches and cream, they’re only forcing the goodness out of themselves, allowing their own negativity to take over from within…ending everything in chaos. Our enemies…yours and ours…they put up masks of confidence before them to try and psych us out. Those politicians…those singers…and those girls…false hopes and confidence…” Brandi lowered her eyes, taking in that info for her own personal reference. Then she stumbled back as she ran into the back of Akiyama. She looked up and found the two of them looking at her. Brandi didn’t like the way things were playing out at this point… Cyclonica’s dark eyes looked upon her…not an evil look this time…surprisingly. “The thing about us is that we have no reason to put up masks for our enemies…what you see is what you get. It saddens us truly that these humans have to do so in order to motivate themselves…instead of having true belief in their abilities. Remember that…for it is one of the sorrows of humanity.” Akiyama held his huge fist out at her, causing her to flinch a bit. Then she looked back up, finding his hand held out before her. Brandi looked into his face, and for a split second, she wasn’t as scared as she used to be. “We are here to bring this realm into Universal Harmony…but until then…take care of yourself.” “Strange words from the destroyers of the universe.” Brandi smirked. Cyclonica shrugged her shoulders, a non-caring look on her face. “Yeah…sure…whatever… maybe this whole face thing has finally gotten to us as well.” Brandi giggled as she tapped her small fist against Akiyama’s. Akiyama gave her a short nod of his head before stalking off with the Divine Prophetess. Brandi watched as they headed towards the end of the boardwalk, where a crowd of people were coming out. As she turned around, she heard a voice call back at her. “Yo…flat-scan.” Brandi looked back, and saw Cyclonica facing her from the peak of the crowd. “All of you humans wear masks…maybe it’s time you took yours off…grab yourself some individuality.” Brandi’s eyes grew wide before she saw a wink of the eye of the Divine Prophetess. Then she nodded eagerly, a smile spreading across Brandi’s face. Cyclonica turned around, sighing as she joined the god’s side. “So says the Divine Prophetess…” The people encompassed around them as they entered the building…
