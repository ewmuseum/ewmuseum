Star light... star bright... First star I see tonight. I wish I may... I wish I might... Wish upon this star tonight.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday, December 13, 2002 It's Monday night. A shot of the Shelbyville night sky shows us a star. A lone star sitting up in the sky. Not another star even close to being seen. The star twinkles up there by its lonesome. Down below, lying in an open field... in the grass... is a large skeletal figure. The figure dons a dark, heavy cloak. The figure is none other than new PCW Extreme champion, The Dark Gimmick. He lies down and blankly stares at the single star in the sky. Gimmick: So... quiet. So... peaceful. So... depressing. So... so... so... so stupid. Dark Gimmick lets out a dry moan. He rolls his eyes into the back of his head. Gimmick: The torture. Urge... to kill...... rising. Dark Gimmick's eye sockets close. His expressionless face suddenly cracks a small smile. Gimmick: Think... happy thoughts. As The Dark Gimmick attempts to dream a little dream... which will likely involve death of some sort... another figure steps into the picture. Voice: It's a beautiful night, isn't it? The figure is that of a young woman. She stands about 5'3 and appears to be somewhere in her 20's. The lady wears some blue jeans and a blue coat to keep herself warm while out in the cold air. Woman: With a pretty sky and all... you just can't help but to look up. The Dark Gimmick keeps his eye sockets closed for he does not want to even look at this good soul. She, at the same time, doesn't look down at him. Instead, she just talks and talks. Woman: You know, you should make a wish. Dark Gimmick is unresponsive. The lady smiles and looks up at the star in the sky. Woman: You know how it goes. Star light. Star bright. First star I see tonight... Dark Gimmick: (thinking to himself) Die! As the lady continues rambling, more stars start to fill the sky. The lady finally decides to stop talking and look down at The Dark Gimmick. When she looks down, all she sees is the hood of the cloak covering Gimmick's face. Woman: I'll... I'll let you sleep. And with that... the woman walks away. Dark Gimmick's bony right hand pulls the cloak from over his face. With the woman out of sight, Dark Gimmick decides to sit up. Gimmick: (aloud) Die! Up in the sky, the original star in the sky turns into a shooting star. The star shoots through the Shelbyville night. Gimmick watches the star as it blazes by and heads off in the same direction as the young woman was walking. Gimmick: Die! The shooting star disappears. A couple of seconds later... off in the distance... a loud scream can be heard. A moment after the scream, a loud crash is heard. Again, a small smile cracks the skeleton's face. The Dark Gimmick lies back down in the open field and looks back up at the Monday night sky. Gimmick: That's better. The eye sockets of The Dark Gimmick shut once again as we fade to black.
