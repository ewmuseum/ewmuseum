Jordan's house party, PTC style!

22 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/22/02 Four months. That may not seem like a long time to you, but when you think about it, it is. Jordan entered the PWA four months ago to little fanfare and to be honest, I don’t think anybody cared. And why would they? He was just another young wrestler trying to pay his dues and make it in the hard business of professional wrestling. It’s hard enough to make it in the business, but when you are in a huge conglomerate of federations, it’s ten times harder. But Jordan had attained the goal that almost every wrestler in the PTC had once strived to reach… Jordan was granted his PCW contract by Tim ‘F^cking’ Walden. Walden didn’t grant Jordan the contract because he liked him…Hell, I don’t know why Tim Walden gave Jordan the contract. But either way, Jordan’s a PCW wrestler now, sure it might piss off some people who are in feds below the PCW, sure some people might think that Jordan X Shabazz isn’t ready for three cards a week, plus house shows and promotional spots. There are also some people who think that Jordan isn’t even worthy of the rumors that state Jordan might join Jason Core, Legend Inc. Duke Williams, and Rush Mason in the Malice Crew… But, Jordan knows, in his heart, the PCW is the only place for him… The scene opens up in Toronto, Ontario Canada. It’s the city and country that Jordan grew up in. We span throughout the city seeing it’s great landmarks and beautiful sights, such as The Sky Dome, The Air Canada Center, and perhaps the most famous thing in Toronto… The CN Tower. Birds and seagulls fly all throughout the skyline. We now zoom into a house in the suburbs of Toronto, the city of Scarborough to be exact. We see a line of identical looking large houses all with different colored garages… Some nasty ass colors we see. Anyways, we stay looking at one house in particular that seems to be buzzing with action. Numerous cars line the outside of the home, and cars are covering the side. This is the house Jordan grew up in; apparently he’s holding a party for him getting into the PCW or something of that nature. It’s pretty much a private party Jordan is holding for his close family and friends. It’s still around mid afternoon, so the sun is still shining brightly. We now enter the house. It seems as if the house is full of people. But these aren’t any ordinary people, oh no, it’s DRUNK WHITE WOMEN FOR EVERYBODY! Heh. Just kidding there. Honestly though, there are a lot of drunk white women. We see many wrestlers in the PTC in the home. We see “The Rush” Rush Mason talking to two drunken women, we see The OSW’s Epitome Of Evil sipping on a large bottle of Hennessey. We now go downstairs to the basement where we see Drew Hostyle with a fine looking young lady, they are rather close, and they look like they are about to get their groove on. There are many other people in Jordan’s home, but they aren’t anybody in Ron owned feds so they don’t really matter. We now go upstairs to what appears to be a bedroom. The entire place, including the bed, is covered with paperwork. As we see some of the paperwork, we see some of the print on the papers, most all of them read ‘Sign Here’, and then below that ‘Sign here’ and so on and so forth. There’s one desk with a computer and a whole load of papers. Now we see that on top of a stack of papers Jordan and some young lady are passionately making out. They roll through the papers and continue to make out…Suddenly, Jordan pulls away. He looks at the girl, who has shoulder length blonde hair and beautiful green eyes, and he speaks. Jordan X Shabazz: “I think you should go downstairs now.” God this pains me so much. I can’t believe I’m actually doing this. If this was some other time I’d hit this girl and then I’d biz ounce. But, now that I’m in the PCW, I’ve got to be careful where I plant my seeds. Something I don’t want is to get a phone call in nine months with some white trick saying ‘Jordan, I’m pregnant.’ And you know them white tricks to eh? They’ll hound you for child support FOREVER! And I don’t want to be no deadbeat daddy… That’ll look bad on my resume. The look on Jordan’s face is obviously one of pain. It’s almost as if he doesn’t want to tell the girl to go downstairs. She gives him a surprised look. Woman: “And why is that?” Jordan X Shabazz: “I got some work to do up here, there’s all these papers I’ve got to sign, plus I’ve got some work to do regarding this new radio show I’m going to host…And, I’ve got to write up my speech.” Woman: “Speech?” Jordan X Shabazz: “Yeah, speech, I’ve got a press conference coming up soon.” The woman who Jordan was just getting hot and heavy with looks at Jordan once again. She shakes her head slowly and she walks out of what appears to be Jordan’s bedroom. Jordan shakes his head, almost as if he still can’t believe how into this Civil Rights movement he is getting in to. He gets up from his bed and he takes a deep breath as the cool air-conditioned room lightens as Jordan turns on the lamp. Jordan picks up a few of the papers and he begins reading them quickly. He begins signing most of the papers at a rapid rate. Then he comes across a group of papers stapled together. Jordan begins to read them aloud. God damned contracts, always drowning a playa in this hoes. The only reason I like paper work is when I’m doing some Civil Rights things. I LOVE getting white people in trouble unnecessarily, I swear, it’s better than sex… Jordan X Shabazz: “What’s this? Hmm… If Jordan becomes injured in away way shape or form Primetime Championship Wrestling is in no way legally obliged to pay him any fees… Dada dada dada… AHA!” Jordan leaps into the air almost in excitement. Jordan X Shabazz: “I bet Xavier Kannon and Mark Makonie put this in here. And they tried to put it in small print…they thought that since I was black I was automatically stupid and I wouldn’t read it…” Jordan sits down in his chair by his brand new computer. It’s a Dell, black, and looks like it cost a lot of money. It has a red bow, probably a gift from somebody. Jordan shakes his head quickly and reads aloud. Jordan X Shabazz: “If Jordan does not complete a physical within twenty-four hours of signing his contract, his contract will be null and void. Hmm… I think I should get this done now.” Jordan bounces up off of his wooden chair and he quickly enters his closet. When he gets out he’s dressed in a large black Malcolm X t-shirt, some baggy blue jeans, and a white Nike visor. He looks all of 18 years old, but it doesn’t faze him. He walks down stairs as the intoxicating smell of Marijuana and alcohol stuns Jordan. But he manages to make it down the stairs. See what happens when the wrong people come to your house party? Watch the popo’s come in and kick my door down and start arresting people. Possession of narcotics isn’t too good is it? Jordan gets down to find some people he didn’t invite down there. They look to be friends of Drew, so Jordan doesn’t question them. Jordan walks into the living room to find everybody that he invited to the party damn near drunk, and screaming. Random Person: JORDAN! CONGRATULATIONS ON GETTING INTO THE *Hic* PC *Hic* W. It’s *Hic* The person makes a vomiting sound and he is no longer heard. We now see Jordan’s older brother, and current OSW superstar, Drew Hostyle stand up on a chair. He appears to be perfectly sober and he shows it. He wears a proud grin on his face. He taps a fork onto a glass of Heineken, and he speaks. Who the hell does he think is going to clean that carpet up? He’s lucky this is my summer house, I swear, my foster parents are probably turning over in there graves. Drew Hostyle: “Ladies and gentlemen, can we get a round of applause for Jordan? He made the PCW in about five months, Jordan, we’re all proud of you.” The room erupts in cheers as Drew finishes his toast to his baby brother. Jordan looks around and tries to hold in the smile and happiness. He has to go to the doctor before they close, his physical is very important. Jordan smiles at them and begins walking slowly towards the door. Suddenly, Drew screams. Drew Hostyle: “Jordan! Relax, stay, have a drink.” Jordan turns back around to face his older brother as the crowd cheers him on. Jordan looks like he really needs and wants to leave. Remember that peer pressure bull$h!t them white people talked to you about in High School? All that, walk away stuff? I was I had listened, I don’t know how to properly defuse this situation without pissing Drew off. That’s the last thing I want to do, this nigga is still trippin’ over that trick Aimz and there match on Sunday. I’m sure he can pull it out, but he’s… Not normal. Jordan X Shabazz: “Drew, I can’t.” The look on the face of Drew Hostyle is an angry one. Before they were split up by Child Protective services when they were young, Drew had always bossed around Jordan. And Jordan never stood up to Drew. In fact, I would say Jordan was afraid of his older brother. While Jordan wrestled as a clean baby face, Drew wrestled as a nasty heel. Drew Hostyle: “What? The PCW’s newest wrestler is too good to chill and have a drink with his older brother?” GOD DAMN IT DREW WHY ARE YOU ALWAYS STARTING THINGS! I pray one day I have the balls to stand up to this cat…But he’ll probably kick my ass if I talk any mess about him. Oh how I remember my short childhood with my real parents… The entire room cringes with ‘Good God, no the fuck he didn’t go there.’ Jordan looks around the room and sees all eyes on him. It doesn’t exactly make him comfortable. The PCW physical was the most important thing to him at the moment, but he didn’t want to anger an already angry Drew Hostyle… I mean, come on, what’s one beer going to do? Jordan slowly closes the door he had slightly opened while Drew was talking, and he begins walking back to the large group of hostile black folks. Jordan walks towards the table where Drew had set up a bunch of refreshments. It was a table full of history, immediately after Jordan and Drew reunited in New York, Jordan flew Drew down to Toronto to meet his foster parents, they ate their first dinner together on the table…Anyways, Drew had set up a whole bunch of Heinekens in Styrofoam 8 ounce cups, and Jordan picked one up. Do they really expect me to drink this entire thing? Heh. I’ll probably pass out after a sip or two…Then they’ll leave me alone. Jordan wasn’t much of a drinker. But he took a sip of the beer anyways to a huge pop from the crowd. They began to chant ‘CHUG! CHUG! CHUG!’ at Jordan. Jordan didn’t chug it though, he sipped on it…almost as if he was a British man sipping tea. Drew still has a sour look on his face as Jordan finishes down his first beer in less than twenty minutes. Drew, who’s attired in formal clothing for ‘gangsta’s’ like himself, baggy khakis, a brown Phat Farm dress t-shirt, some brown Lugz boots [which are untied] a New York Mets baseball cap, and a black doo rag, walks up to Jordan. Drew Hostyle: “Oh come on Jordan, drink some more. Let loose, you are acting as if you’ll get fired if you get drunk tonight. What happened with you and Sarah [The girl who Jordan was with earlier in the room.]?” Jordan X Shabazz: “Well, I was going to go to the doctor. I need to get a physical and pass it or my contract with the PCW is null and void. Plus, today’s probably the only day of the week I can go in and get a walk on physical. It’s never busy on Tuesday’s like this.” Drew looks around, and checks his shiny Rolex wristwatch. Drew Hostyle: “What time do ‘dem crackas close?” Jordan X Shabazz: “I think at like…ten, or eleven.” Drew Hostyle: “Okay, cool, I’ll take you there later tonight. Trust me, relax and drink some more, we can party and shit. Getting into the PCW is a huge accomplishment and I think you deserve to celebrate.” I guess I do deserve to celebrate, not everybody is as talented as me. Not everybody can wrestle in the PCW…And not everybody is badass enough to be considered a member of The Malice Crew. Jordan X Shabazz: “Alright dog, but you best take me to that doctor’s office tonight. No matter what condition I am in.” Drew nods solemnly as the rest of the room goes back to normal. Epitome Of Evil is sippin’ on some Bacardi while reading a book called ‘The Art of Bludgeoning White People Until You Win A Match.’ written by, of course, Epitome Of Evil. Jordan surveys the room as he continues to sip on some beer. *** 2 hours later. Time: 9:45. The small family owned doctor’s office closed in 15 minutes. The scene reopens with the now empty house except for Jordan X Shabazz and his older brother Drew Hostyle. There are beer bottles, chips, and greasy chicken bones that litter the floor. Drew Hostyle is obviously sober, and proud that he and his brother just threw a badass party. Jordan, who is obviously not sober, remembers the physical he has to get. Jordan X Shabazz (Wobbling around and walking towards the door) : “Drew, we’s got to bounce over to the doc *Hic* tor’s office. I need *hic* this physical. Drew looks around. Drew Hostyle: “We need to clean this place up.” Jordan X Shabazz: “Why? It’s a *Hic* summer house, it’s not like I live *Hic* here anymore.” *Hic* Drew nods. Drew Hostyle: “Pass me your car keys, I’ll drive us there.” Jordan went into his pocket and he threw his brother Drew the keys to his rented Jeep. The PCW would kill him if he wrecked the car. Drew walked towards the car and he opens up the passenger side door. Drew walks around to the other side and he gets in. He puts the keys in the ignition as Jordan slowly but surely closes the door to his summerhouse, and he locks it. Jordan stumbles towards the car as Drew Hostyle obviously gets more and more impatient with Jordan and he screams. Drew Hostyle: “Come on you jackass, GET IN THE CAR.” Jordan stumbles some more, but he makes it into the vehicle. Drew checks the time and notices they have five minutes to get to the doctor’s office that is a good ten minutes away. Drew puts the peddle to the metal (Or whatever they say.) and the car begins to speed on the residential streets of Scarborough, Ontario Canada. After about three minutes of blowing by stoplights and blowing by stop signs, Jordan and Drew reach the doctor’s office. Jordan looks as if he’s about to throw up, but he handles himself correctly and makes sure he doesn’t throw up. The doctor’s office is a decent sized office, but since it’s family owned, and understaffed, it appears as if it’s always empty. The large parking lot in the back always has quite a few open spaces due to the fact that not many people head to this particular office. Just as Drew runs up to the door the secretary (Or Nurse) closes the door in his face. Drew checks his watch, then he checks the time inside the small office. Drew Hostyle: “Say bitch, you best open this door. I’ve got two minutes.” The nurse turns around, and she faces an angry looking Drew Hostyle. She opens the door and she calls to Dr. Stevenson. Nurse: “Mr. Stevenson, we’ve got one more patient here.” As Drew walks through the door, Jordan stumbles behind him. Drew leads Jordan towards the doctor’s office. Drew opens the door without knocking to find Dr. Andrew Stevenson working on some paper files and looking all of 80 years old. The office seems to look like it has been renovated and it looks decent. Leather swivel chair for the Doctor, a nice looking small operating table, and a table covered with white paper where his patients usually sit or lie down on. And a clean sink in the corner of the office. He’s surprised to see Jordan X Shabazz, a kid whom he remembers from a few years back. He notices Jordan is drunk and tries to get them to leave. Dr. Stevenson (Standing up): “Well, Mr. Copeland (Talking to Jordan), this is quite the surprise. I think I might have to see you tomorrow or something. You don’t have an appointment and you are obviously under the influence. It’s not right.” Jordan X Shabazz: *Hic* “Alls I need it a *hic* physical. We can do this quickly.” Drew Hostyle: “Yeah, and sit your cracka ass down.” Dr. Stevenson quickly walks to the sink and he washes his hands. Dr. Stevenson: “Okay, it’ll have to be quick.” Drew nods at Jordan and he goes to the waiting room. Jordan stumbles towards the Doctor. The alcohol on his breath is disgusting, almost like looking at Will Otto’s face. Jordan X Shabazz: “Say, doc wanna hear something funny as hell?” Dr. Stevenson doesn’t want to anger the already drunken Jordan X Shabazz, so he obliges with Jordan. Dr. Stevenson: “Sure.” Jordan X Shabazz: “A Mexican, Indian, a brotha and a white person was all standing on a top of a building. The Mexican said 'this is for my people' and jumped of the building, The Indian said 'this is for my people' and jumped of the building, The black person said 'this is for my people' and pushed the white man off.” Dr. Stevenson does a nervous laugh as Jordan begins cracking up. Jordan reaches into his back pocket and he pulls out a piece of paper. It’s obviously the physical that the doctor has to sign. Jordan pulls a pen from his ruffled clothing and he shoves it in the face of the doctor. Jordan X Shabazz: “Sign here please. I’ve got a big tag team match coming up on Friday, plus a Pay Per View match coming up in May, and I don’t need a cracka like you tryin’ to hold a brotha down.” Dr. Stevenson: “But Mr. Copeland, if you were to get injured during the match I would be liable for malpractice.” Jordan’s face doesn’t waver. He appears to be sobering up, but he’s got a pounding headache, and the Doctor’s battles aren’t helping. Jordan X Shabazz: “Sign the freakin’ papers so I can make Mark Makonie and Xavier Kannon pay for degrading the black man.” Dr. Stevenson feels as if he has no choice, he quickly grabs Jordan’s pen, and he signs the paper. Jordan nods at him, and he walks, slowly, feeling the wall for support, to the waiting room where Drew Hostyle stands up, with a beaming smile…Something you don’t usually see from somebody like Drew. Jordan holds up the paper to Drew almost as if you are showing your dad your first ever ‘A+’ on your report card. Drew Hostyle: “I’m proud of you boy. Now alls you need to do is get rid of them crackas in The Malice Crew, Legend Inc, Jason Core, and Duke Williams, then, you’ll be like me.” “White people…You can’t live without them…WE CAN LIVE WITHOUT THEM!” Fade to BLACK!
