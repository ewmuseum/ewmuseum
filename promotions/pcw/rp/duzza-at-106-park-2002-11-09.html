Duzza At 106 & Park

9 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.11.02 The scene opens in the BET studio’s in New York, New York. Where BET’s biggest show 106 & Park is being filmed. Outside the studio, it is very hot and the sun shines brightly, inside is a different matter completely. The studio is calm and very busy. People are walking around everywhere, camera’s are moving around the studio, but it is all quiet, as a taping is taking place. AJ and Free the shows hosts are sitting on a couch, infront of a audience of diverse young hip hoppers. AJ, is a tall man, with braids and a fashion sense that is somewhat lacking. Free is a stunningly beautiful black woman, with her hair died Orange. Today she is wearing her hair tied back. Free: “Thanks for joining us on Thursdays edition of 106 and Park” Just then, the camera’s skip to the audience, who start cheering and clapping loudly, before dieing out. AJ: “Man, have we got a hot show for y’all today! Apart from the top 10 countdown, we’ve also got J’Lo and Justin Timberlake! That’s right, J’Lo and JT on the show! Today!” Free: “That’s right and not only that, but we have a exclusive interview with everyone’s favorite Asian emcee, and Freestyle Friday hall-of-famer, Jin!” AJ: “And… we have another special guest, in the form of Free’s boyfriend! Haha, PCW star and R. Kelly associate, Duzza!” Free: “He ain’t my boyfriend! Yet…haha, well let’s bring the ol’ handsome fella on! Ladies and Gentleman, Duzza!” A funky hip hop beat plays and the crowd cheer as Duzza walks onto the stage. He is dressed in a baggy white “XXL” hoody, baggy white pants and Timberland boots. He has a big red-gold chain around his neck. There is a big “R.” at the bottom of the chain, which stands for Rockland, which is a small record label Duzza is part of. His hair is plaited tightly in long cornrows that reach to the bottom of his neck. Duzza is just looking so fresh and clean. He gives AJ daps and gets a kiss that seems to have a hidden meaning from Free, before they all sit down on the couch. AJ: “What up Family?” Duzza: “Hey, thanks for having me” Free: “It’ll be my pleasure.. believe that” Duzza: “haha, I do” AJ: “Well, Duzz, let’s get straight to business, let’s talk about your boy R. Kelly. How’s he doing?” Duzza: “Well, R. Kelly is my boy, so I don’t really wanna talk about him on TV or anything, but the man is doing good. Holding his head up, and just shaking of these false allegations. Kellz, got a album dropping December, heard some of it, peep it fo’ real. Hot stuff” Half of the crowd claps while the other half boo’s Duzza: “Damn…tough crowd” Free: “Ok, well, let’s move on. What music you really feeling at the moment? Who’s in your CD changed?” Duzza: “Ah, gotta be my boy Jin. Reppin’ China Town. I know he ain’t released a album yet, but he’s signed with Ruff Ryders, and I got my son to download me all his BET freestyles and a couple of his songs, and he’s hot” AJ: “Yeah, Jin’s a cool guy, you know him personally? Ever met him?” Duzza: “Naw, never met the dawg, but he’s got mad skills. Big respect to the dawg… I know, in the ring and on tv, I got like a whole anti-white personality going on, but ya know, it’s cool to see diversity in hip hop. Hip hop is the voice of this generation. Black, white, Chinese, Indian, Latino, I don’t care, they all welcome in hip hop…for real” Free: “For real, one love, one love” AJ: “Well, family, you got any video you want us to play?” Duzza: “Um, yeah for sho… I Wish by my man R. Kelly” Free: “Ok, well, R. Kelly will lead you to the break, we’ll be right back after this” The show goes to a commercial, before coming back Free: “Ok, Duzz, tell me, hows the pro-wrestling life going? I hear you and Playaz Inc are holding it down” Duzza: “Oh, for sho. We’re reppin’ to the fullest. There’s a lot of hate surrounding us, most of it seems to be aimed at my man Statuz Quo. Y’know even vegetarians got beef wit’ us! For real. Everyone from player haters like that Russian dude in the other fed to Xavier Kannon seem to go beef wit’ us. But it’s all good… we fear no one” AJ: “Ah, well what you got planned? What’s coming up?” Duzza: “Well…after this show, I’ma take out Free for some dinner and dining, nawmean? Ha, but before that, I hear Justin Timberlake be on this show? Well, he’s gonna catch a kerbing like his name was Mark Makonie… for real” AJ: “Well…on that note, thanks for coming Duzza….and here’s the number 10 video… “Work It” by Missy Elliot” With that, the scene starts to fade into the Missy Elliot video as Duzza sits on the couch, trying to look hard…
