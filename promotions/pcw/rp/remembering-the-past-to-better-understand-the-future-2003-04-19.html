Remembering the Past, To Better Understand The Future.

19 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/4/03 Where exactly have you been? Where have we been? Well, you need to think outside of the box and understand that not all crime happens in the New York area, despite what Stan Lee claims. We have been working out of town. Way out of town. Due to governmental restraints, we cannot possibly divulge that information, but needless to say we kicked ass and got back to our home in one piece. Yes, but while you have been gone your life has crumbled around your spandex-clad, ego’s. Your wrestling company has apparently become defunct, the government have repossessed the Justice Incorporated building and you have lost credibility with the majority of the public. Yes, boy these matters do concern us, Jenny, but this situation was essentially unavoidable. We had no control of the situation and therefore were unable to plan for the absence. For this we are sorry, but like I say it was unavoidable. So what future does the Justice Incorporated company have? A very bright one. We have built the company up from the ground before, we can do it again, only this time it will be better. We have already received an offer from a very reputable wrestling company, and have accepted this offer. The self-publicity will continue and the tag team of Justice Inc will reform and continue to kick ass in the wrestling industry. This is law. This fact. This is Justice Inc. ----- At a comeback press conference Two Days ago. Justice Incorporated. A team of two superheroes standing up to evil wherever they may encountered it. The members; Warrior, Blind Rage, Badger Man and the Handbag Warrior. Their goal; to stand up for truth, justice, and the American way. For a profit, obviously. Initially, the whole concept of the superhero duo breaking into the wrestling industry was purely in aid of business motives. Their superhero business was going through a rough patch and essentially they needed some self-publication. At the same time they needed to make some money and obviously a normal advertising campaign was totally out of the question. So the found the wrestling industry. This could offer the team good money, easy, self-publication and a healthy fitness regime. It was the perfect solution to an almost impossible situation. After a couple of months their financial situation drastically improved, earning them more gigs than they could possibly imagine. In fact, they were receiving more bookings than they could ever have hoped for. Soon, they moved from their old, run-down, building and moved right into a brand new Justice Incorporated headquarters in the better part of the New York area. Then there was a need to recruit more members as the demand for their time grew. It wasn’t because Blind Rage and Warrior couldn’t cope, it was just the demand for work was far too much for them to handle. So two new members were convincingly decided upon, when they both demonstrated their powers to Warrior, who didn’t feel the benefit of such actions the next day. Essentially, there was no need for wrestling anymore. They had publicised their company as much as they needed to, this new public interest gave them greater revenue and so all their reasons for entering the wrestling industry had become, essentially, defunct. Life was prosperous. Life was good. All right up to the scandal that was. It seemed the wrestling industry would not let them leave quite that easily. The passion and the need for it was in the blood of both Blind Rage and Warrior and it was next to impossible to give up. Blind Rage found it the worst to cope with, however, Warrior managed to overcome the problem quite convincingly. Blind Rage drilled him over the subject and found that he had become addicted to something. To what he didn’t say, initially, but this was all that was needed to convince the public of wrong doing. Somehow, the press managed to gain this information and manipulate it to their own advantages. “Still can’t get over the crave of adrenaline huh? I sorted that out a couple of weeks ago when quitting the wrestling industry became a realistic option.” “Really? What did you do?” “I…huh…I…can’t tell you that!” “Why what is it, sugah? What takes the place of the adrenaline rush for you now, hon?”, The Handbag Warrior quizzed. “It’s none of your damn business, man!”, Warrior replied, getting very cagey indeed. Blind Rage walked over to Warrior with a concerned look upon his face, taking his former tag team partner by the shoulders. “Warrior tell it to me straight. Don’t lie to me. Are you taking some kind of drug? Is that what you’ve replaced the adrenaline rush with?” “No! It’s not!”, Warrior replied with conviction. “Tell us the truth, hon!”, The Handbag Warrior demanded. “That is the truth, you limp wrested bastard!”, Warrior replied with venom in his voice. “No you’re not!”, Blind Rage said. “Yes I am. You just won’t accept that the truth doesn’t involve my intake of drugs!”, Warrior replied. “Well if it’s not drugs sugah, why don’t you just tell us what you’ve compensated the adrenaline rush with?”, The Handbag Warrior asked, looking extremely confused. “Because it’s no business of yours! It’s my business.”, Warrior replied, leaving the room in a huff. “Well…that went well.”, Blind Rage stated sarcastically, a stern look etched upon his face. ----- A copy of the recording, which the press manipulated to their own advantage. So, Justice Inc were therefore forced to begin a new, in order to accomplish what they originally set out to do, whilst trying to figure out how the hell the press got hold of the conversation. Despite all the hype and the controversy, Warrior had become addicted to nothing less than Extra-Strong mints, but the addiction had been blown out of all proportion and therefore the public refused to believe this explanation of the event. The Justice Inc team were just re-establishing themselves in the minds and hearts of the national once again when it happened. The team were abducted by aliens and consequently played a huge role in an intergalactic war. Basically, they kicked ass then come home. Come home to nothing. Their home; repossessed. Their company: dissolved. Their wrestling career; in the gutter. It was time to turn over a new life, to build their company up from scratch once again. It was a job of epic proportions, but they were superheroes. If anybody could do it, it’s them. The contract offer from Primetime Championship Wrestling was a beginning, a beginning they would never, ever forget. This tragedy began when they turned their back upon the wrestling industry. That would never happen again. Ever.
