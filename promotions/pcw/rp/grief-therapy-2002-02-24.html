'Grief Therapy

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb. 24, 2002 Chef was dead. Akiyama still couldn't believe it. Chef was dead. His leader, his friend. The only guy he'd call Sir without that little extra in his voice that turned it into an insult - or sexual innuendo, depending on how cute the other person was. Yeah right…what was he thinking?? Depressed beyond belief, Akiyama trudged off the elevator onto the 53rd floor and down the corridor, so down that he barely had it in him to goose a secretary as he passed. She shrieked and hit him with the files she was carrying, but it didn't have its usual thrill. Tyler Nelson had summoned Akiyama, Yuu, and Darkstar to his office for some kind of meeting, one they weren't allowed to bring weapons too. Shuffling to the door, he stopped and sighed, bracing himself the meet with his boss. Grabbing a flask of liquor out of his rumpled jacket, he took a deep pull and put it back. Fully braced now, he went inside. 'You're ten minutes late,' Tyler informed him coolly. 'We are?' Akiyama looked at his watch. 'Damn. We'll be back in another twenty.' 'Get back here!' Tyler yelled. He was a young man, no older than his employee, with brown hair that reminded Akiyama of a loaf of bread. He wore a pinstripe suit that tried to make him look like his shoulders were broad, but only succeeded in making his head look like a pomegranate. It was about the same color as one too, Akiyama noted. 'I swear!' Tyler snapped. 'If I weren't already short one jobber in this MM tournament, I'd shoot you!' Instantly, the sound of bawling filled the room. 'Aw, for god's sake, Yuu, shut the hell up!' Startled, Akiyama looked over at the corner. Sitting on the couch, dressed in a blue dress, was a woman with black and blonde hair. Long, flowing brown and black hair, like a mop. Akiyama did his traditional shudder and looked away as she blew her nose on a damp hanky. On the other side of the room was the last of the four people in the room. Darkstar was smaller than he was and thinner, standing impassively in his gothic attire with his eyes hidden behind sunglasses. He looked completely unaffected by what had happened, though he winced slightly as Yuu's wails reached new levels in loud. 'Oh, Chef!' she cried. 'Shut up!' Tyler screamed. Yuu dropped down to basic sniffling and he let go of the edge of his desk, the wood creaking as he released it from the near-breaking pressure. 'Now,' he said, visibly calming himself down. 'Since Chef died-' Yuu started blubbering again. 'SHUT UP! Since Chef died, I’ve been thinking about death and how it affects PCW’s wrestlers. And since you three have technically died, the three of you have been fucking useless! ” He pointed at Yuu. “You've been a whiny cry-baby.' He frowned. 'Actually, you've always been a whiny cry-baby.' she glared as he turned to Akiyama. 'YOU have become a no account drunk god- never mind…' Akiyama grinned. 'And Darkstar has-! Okay, Darkstar isn't any different than he ever was.' 'Great. We're still losers. Meeting's over.' Akiyama started for the door. 'FREEZE!' Akiyama looked back at him over his shoulder. 'What is it, man? It's nearly happy hour at the docks!' He hesitated. 'We mean we have some training we need to do. Sir.' Tyler gave him a glare that would have been terrifying if he wasn’t so ugly. 'You three morons are going in for grief counseling.' 'What??' 'But it's happy hour-' 'Am I getting paid for this?' 'No arguments!' Tyler shrieked. 'I sign the checks, I decide who lives and dies and I make the decisions!' 'Oh, if you put it that way…' 'Tin pot dictato- we mean yes, sir!' 'I better be getting paid for this…' Tyler took a deep breath, the vein on his forehead throbbing. 'Now, the three of you are going to shut up, Yuu is going to get rid of her hanky. Akiyama is going to stop talking like that for once in his goddamned life and comb that rats nest he calls hair, and Darkstar is going to… be himself.' He straightened up, arms crossing. 'In room 5464 is a doctor. A psychiatrist. He is going to talk to you about your fucking grief and I'm not going to hear another word about it. Is that clear?' 'Who is this doctor?' Akiyama asked. Tyler looked upwards, mouth working in a silent prayer for patience. 'Don't worry about it. Wonder recommended him very highly.' 'This is supposed to reassure us?' 'GET OUT!' Tyler screamed. Shrugging, Akiyama turned and slouched out of the room, followed by Yuu and Darkstar. Behind them, Tyler gasped for breath, watching them go, then sat down and reached into his desk drawer. Taking out a pink bottle he took a healthy swallow. 'Goddamn dark jobbers…but now I have to go identify the body.' he muttered, and then phoned the med center to book another appointment to get his ulcers checked. Room 5464 was a conference room with windows looking out onto the corridor, covered with curtains. The three wrestlers paused before the door, their reflections showing in the glass. Yuu with her even damper hanky, Darkstar with a bored expression and ever present sunglasses, and Akiyama in a suit that had been slept in and long red hair pulled back into a messy tail. 'Do we want to go in there?' Darkstar asked at last. Yuu dabbed at her eyes. 'Well, we did promise we would.' 'No we didn't,' Akiyama groused. 'We just got yelled at and told to show up.' They kept staring at the door. 'Are we going to go in there?' Darkstar asked a few minutes later. 'Not us,' Akiyama muttered. 'Last time we went first we ran into a couple of reamers in action, which lead to us getting struck by lightning…darn Zeus.' 'I'll go,' Yuu interrupted. 'I'll just find out what the doctor's like. Then you guys can come in.' She walked to the door, opened it and went in, closing it behind her. Akiyama and Darkstar stared at the door. 'You feel as much like a wuss as we do?' Akiyama asked. 'Yup.' 'Glad we’re not the only one.' They kept staring at the door. 'Guys!' Yuu opened the door and leaned out. 'Come on in, the doctor is sweet.' 'This is supposed to reassure us?' Akiyama muttered as he wandered in, Darkstar trailing behind. The conference room was long and rectangular, a table with chairs around it in the centre, windows overlooking the city of Midgar on the wall opposite the one with the corridor windows. A coffee maker and a small tray with donuts sat on the table, along with a box holding something that looked to be foam. A skinny man in a white lab coat and glasses beamed at them, hands clasping under his chin. 'Oh, at LAST! I was getting so WORRIED!' Akiyama and Darkstar hesitated, eyeing him. 'He almost looks like Hojo,' Darkstar muttered. “Hojo?” “Yeah…that whacko doctor from Final Fantasy 7 that made Sephiroth.” 'Yeah,' Akiyama whispered back. 'Only scarier.' The man scurried forward. 'Welcome, welcome! My name is Doctor Fred Woo Hakase, but you can call me Fred.' He shook Akiyama's hand vigorously. 'It's so WONDERFUL to finally meet you, Darkstar!' 'Um, doc, we’re not-' Fred spun on Darkstar. 'Akiyama! You're just like Mr Nelson described you! Don't worry, we'll get right to talking about that speech problem of yours in a minute.' 'But WE’RE Akiyama!' Fred turned to Akiyama, his eyes sad. 'Oh, how sad it is to see a conflict of personality in one so young. Come, have a donut!' To Akiyama's surprise, he found himself holding a honey glazed donut while Fred hurried around the room, yammering on how therapy was healthy and they would all be happy and content with themselves once they were done. Yuu sat in a corner sniffling and eyeing him a little warily. Akiyama looked at Darkstar. 'Run?' 'Sounds good to me.' They both bolted for the door. It was locked. 'Shit!' Akiyama cursed. 'Darkstar!' Fred gasped. 'Swearing is UNHEALTHY!' Darkstar raised an eyebrow above the level of the glasses. 'Why do I think we're in deep trouble?' 'Because you're a wise man,' Akiyama muttered, still yanking on the door and wishing he'd brought his weapons. Typical of Tyler to order them to show up unarmed. He'd get the pubescent little prick for this. Fred had pulled four chairs into a circle. 'Come, come! Sit!' His eyes filled with tears. 'It's time to talk about Chap.' 'Chef,' Yuu corrected and immediately started bawling again. 'Have a tissue, dear.' Fred dug a Kleenex box out of the big box on the table and handed it to her. 'Thank you,' she mumbled, taking it and sitting down. Reluctantly, Akiyama and Darkstar joined her, sitting down warily on either side of her since Fred had taken the only other chair, beaming at them all even as he wiped a tear away, then folded his hands in his lap. 'Now, Mr Nelson tells me that you're a close nit group, correct?' 'Absolutely.' 'Kind of.' 'Hell no.' Scene showed the backstage area at the recent Fusion. Darkstar was seen walking down the hall with a bored look on his face. Then coming down the opposite end of the hallway, drinking on a bottle of water. As the two passed each other, words were exchanged. 'Hey Darkstar.' The NVG gave him a short nod. 'Go to hell.' The Lord of Dreams muttered. Akiyama stopped right in the middle of the hallway, staring back at him with a peeved expression on his face. 'Yeah? Well...We CAN go to hell! We can go anytime we want because We OWN HELL!' he bellowed. 'We have the deed right here in our wallet! SEE??' The Neo Void God's hand slapped against his backside of his black vinyl pants a few times, where his wallet is kept. Suddenly his peeved expression changes to one of fear. 'Oh sh!t!!' Akiyama spun around and dashed down the hallway. Following behind him closely is none other than Precious Peter Love, Ultimate Warlord, Boca Del Inferno, and Shadowman. Ahead of them all, Akiyama's friend Yuu was walking reading a magazine. Akiyama stopped in front of her as his hand reached for the doorknob of the janitor's closet nearby. 'Hiya, Aki!' she beamed. 'Yuu, we're going to hide in this closet for a few minutes. If anyone asks for us, tell them we left the building, okay?' 'Righty-O!' Akiyama muttered a thanks before he dove into the closet. Inside, he could hear the muffled footsteps of the Ream Team approaching. One of them asked where Akiyama was...he couldn't tell. They all sounded as gay as Nephiliam...whatever that was. But that wasnt what freaked him out...it's what Yuu did. 'Akiyama's hiding in the janitor's closet! Oops!' 'It's a good thing we pulled Avalanche out of the Void and substituted him in our place.' the NVG explained at the end of the story. 'Sometimes it's good to be a god.' 'Testify.' Darkstar tapped fists with Akiyama in agreement. Fred sighed happily. 'How wonderful.' His face fell again. 'But I also understand that you've all gone through a deep personal loss recently.' Yuu started blubbering again while Akiyama felt a tightness in his throat and Darkstar looked down. 'Dare I say it? The death of your leader Chaff.' 'Chef!' Akiyama shouted. 'Now, Darkstar, wait your turn. It isn't polite to interrupt.' Akiyama leaned back, fuming. Yuu patted his shoulder for a moment and he fought to relax. He wasn't going to let this idiot get to him. Grief counseling was a good thing. They could use it. None of them had been there when Chef died and all of them felt guilty for it. He'd been everything to each of them. Fred sighed again. He sounded like a wheezy kettle. 'Now, from what I understand, Chat-Chat died at a place called the Temple of Gourmets, when a horrible unhealthy man stabbed him over and over and over, making him bleed and scream and cry again and again for someone to help him. For ANYone to come to his rescue and save him from such a horrible fate. But none of you came! None of you were there for him in his hour of need!' “Heh…isn’t that the gay bar around the corner?” Darkstar muttered. “You would know, wouldn’t you?” Akiyama remarked. DS glared at him hard. “I’ll make you regret existing, Akiyama!” “Bah! You’re in for a Void Banishing!” 'How sad. How terribly, terribly sad to have failed a man you worked with.' Fred wiped away a tear. Yuu howled, sobbing madly. Darkstar grabbed one of her tissues while Akiyama felt sick. Eyes wide with horror, he stared at Fred. 'Well, let's all see the good side!' 'G-good side?' Yuu sniffled. She was halfway through the box of kleenex already. 'Of course!' Fred cheered. 'It's brought all of you closer together!' 'R-really?' 'Like hell.' 'Are you stupid? You're stupid, aren't you?' Fred leaped up. 'Let's all turn our frowns upside down, because death is a HAPPY thing! It gives one a chance to see ones own worth as a living person! To hear the joy of the will! To wear very slimming white. I hate to say it, but black doesn't suit any of you. Especially you with the orange hair, Darkstar.' 'Our hair isn't pink!' Akiyama yelled, still reeling from the 'death is a happy thing' bit. 'And we’re Akiyama!' 'Darkstar, Darkstar, Darkstar,' Fred mourned, kneeling before him with his hands on the NVG's shoulders. 'Don't resist your own identity. 'This brashness, the toupee, it's not you at all. You know that?' Toupee? Akiyama thought. 'This is our real hair!' 'Oh, Darkstar, don't deny it. It's not HEALTHY to lie to yourself. You're bald as a cueball, aren't you?' For the first time since he'd known him, Darkstar sniggered. Akiyama glared at him. 'Great, NOW you decide to get a sense of humour.' Fred spun on the other wrestler. 'Now, Akiyama. Laughing at your teammate just shows your inadequacies. That's why you drink, isn't it?' Darkstar blinked. 'Admit it, you think you're inferior to the others, don't you? Are you impotent? Feel free to share.' 'NOW WAIT JUST A MINUTE HERE!' Akiyama yelled. 'Not now, Darkstar.' 'Um, do you know you're getting the two of them mixed up?' Yuu ventured uncertainly. Fred stood up, sighing. 'I see we have a lot of work to do, beyond helping you get by your grief for Chet.' 'CHEF, DAMNIT!' Fred ignored him, pulling something out of the box. 'I think we all need to address our grief directly.' He pulled a tab on the object and there was a fizzing, inflating sound as it filled out. When it finished, Fred put it in his own chair. 'Now, why don't we all pretend that this is dear departed Chart come back to listen to ALL your little problems.' 'Uh, doc, do you know that that's an inflatable woman?' 'She's very, um, pointed, isn't she? Could I have some more Kleenex?' 'I used to date a woman who looked like that.' Fred stepped behind the doll, holding it up. 'Hi,' he squeaked in a falsetto voice. 'It's me, Clef, back to see you!' Predictably, Yuu started crying, grabbing tissues to wipe her eyes with. She was surrounded by a forest's worth of them already. Fred turned the doll to her. 'Tell me what you think, Yuu.' 'I-I m-miss you!' 'Freak, Yuu!' Akiyama snapped. 'It's just a damn doll.' The doll spun, smacking the real Darkstar across the face. 'That's not healthy behavior, Darkstar!' 'Shit, doc! You just about put my eye out!' Fred looked at him sadly. 'Now, if you cannot participate, Akiyama, you will have to wait outside, is that understood? It would be for your own good.' Darkstar grinned, sensing salvation. 'Great! Can I go now?' His face even sadder, Fred pointed to the window. 'Then take a five minute time out. And don't feel that we don't love you anymore.' 'Uh, doc, that's a window.' 'Because we really do. So skip off and cool down, it's healthy!' 'Uh, doc, we're 54 floors up.' 'Hurry up now, or you might miss the donut break!' 'Yeah?' Darkstar sneered. 'How ya gonna make me, you skinny freak? Hit me with your damn doll again?' Fred was awfully fast with a needle. 'Now, Darkstar, why don't you tell me how YOU feel about my being all dead and rotting?' the doll squeaked. Akiyama looked at the window dubiously. Darkstar was outside on the ledge, drunkenly strolling along, spinning on one leg to turn around with his arms pin wheeling, singing loudly all the while. 'He's going to fall,' he noted, wondering precisely what was in that needle Fred used, and how to avoid getting one himself. 'Darkstar is just expressing himself in a safe environment while allowing the rest of us to get on with this wonderful therapy,' Fred assured him. 'Now, focus! Dead people! Think about dead people!' Akiyama resisted the urge to rub his throbbing temples. 'We don't think the ledge outside of the 54th floor of an office building is a 'safe environment', doc.' 'But of COURSE it is!' Fred insisted. 'Just LOOK at that VIEW!' 'Woohoo!' Darkstar shouted outside, arms spread. 'I can fly!' Akiyama bolted for the window, half diving through it himself as he grabbed for Darkstar’s leg, barely catching it as the bluehead pitched off the ledge face first. Fred sighed. 'Well, if Darkstar is going to just run off like that, I guess we'll have to focus on YOU, Yuu dear.' Yuu blinked. A frown on his face, Tyler glared across his desk at Sandy Wong. The woman shifted uncomfortably, trying not to sweat. Tyler' frown deepened. 'I'm not sure your attitude is correct for this company, Wong,' he said slowly. 'You seem to have a very unhealthy belief that 'people' matter.' Darkstar swung upside down past the picture window. Wong stammered, sticking a finger in her shirt collar and pulling to loosen the constriction. 'Sir, I can assure you, these rumors are completely inaccurate. I'd never do anything to betray Nelson.' Darkstar swung past in the other direction, waving. Tyler leaned back in his chair, fingers steepled before him. 'Go on, convince me.' He wasn't sure Wong was doing anything against company interests; in fact he didn't have any proof whatsoever, but his gut told him there was something annoyingly 'wholesome' about this woman. Kremmen had trusted him, but Tyler wasn't his Kremmen. 'I've always worked loyally for you, ever since I first started here. I've worked up through the ranks and profits are up more than…' Her voice trailed off as she watched Darkstar swing by the window, scrabbling at the glass and yelling something unintelligible. Tyler followed his gaze and gaped at the wrestler, mouth open. Wong threw herself to her knees before the desk, hands clasped. 'PLEASE, SIR! I DIDN'T MEAN TO SELL OUT PCW AND PRETEND I WAS A MECHANICAL DOG OR HELP OSW OR ANYTHING! PLEEEASE DON'T HANG ME OUTSIDE LIKE HIM! I'M AFRAID OF HEIGHTS!' Tyler didn't hear her. He was already out the door. Cursing repeatedly, Tyler Nelson ran up the stairs, too pissed off to use the elevator the one floor needed. What the HELL did that man think he was doing? He'd seen Darkstar do some pretty stupid things before, but that had to be the topper. If he fell, the company would be terribly embarrassed. 'Top PCWer does nose-dive off office building'. The news would eat it up. Still cursing, he banged through the doorway and into the crowded hallway. It looked like half the company was jammed into the corridor in front of the conference room, where the curtains on the windows to the hall were wide and the glass slid open. Threats of pink slips got Tyler through the press of men and women until he could reach the door and see what they were all hooting at. Darkstar was back in the conference room and doing a strip tease on the table. 'WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU DOING??' Darkstar grinned at him, his eyes sparkling with a strange sort of glaze. He had his coat off and as Tyler gaped, stripped off his shirt as well, swung it over his head as the watching women and not a few of the men cheered, then flung it out into the audience. Infuriated to where he thought his head was going to explode, Tyler turned his attention to the rest of the room and just about swallowed his own tongue in shock. Yuu was sitting in the lap of an inflatable woman with the largest breasts Tyler had ever seen, stroking her cheek and sighing happily. 'Oh, Chef!' she babbled happily. Akiyama was only a few feet away, having abandoned any pretence of stoicism or control as he throttled a skinny man in a lab coat who looked unnaturally like a Professor Hojo/ PHD crossover. 'Wait!' the man gasped. 'Darkstar! (wheeze) This is very (choke) unhealthy!' 'Diediediediediediedie-' Akiyama told him, squeezing tighter with a crazed leer on his face. Tyler saw the reputation of PCW going straight down the toilet. 'STOP IT!' he shrieked. 'I forbid this!' They all ignored him and his blood pressure shot up even higher. 'I am Tyler Nelson and I will NOT see such behavior in my company! I will be treated with respect!' Half a second later, Akiyama's pants wrapped around his face and the crowd cheered. Tyler tore them off. 'THAT'S IT!' He pointed at Akiyama. 'LET GO OF HIM NOW!' Reluctantly, Akiyama let go. The doctor wheezed a moment longer, then beamed. 'How wonderful! What a healthy chance for everyone to admit publicly to any secrets they've been hiding. Yuu! Do you have anything you'd like to finally get off your chest for everyone? Feel free to let it all out.' Yuu cradled her doll. 'Chef and I are getting married.' 'Wonderful! Darkstar?' Tyler was too busy trying to adapt to that pronouncement to realize that it was Akiyama who answered to the name Darkstar. 'I want to kill everything and everyone, and see the world as an airless, lifeless VOID.' 'Wonderful! Akiyama?' 'I've slept with everybody in this room except for two.' Darkstar launched his underwear somewhere into a corner as everyone gaped at him in shock, except for Fred. 'Wonderful, Akiyama! And to think your file said you didn't interact well with others!' 'Darkstar! You told me I was the only one!' Yuu cried, though she didn't relinquish her hold on the doll. A few of the people muttered in agreement with the Yuu but Akiyama just grinned, doing the limbo under an invisible pole. 'We don't believe this,' Akiyama muttered. 'Two timing little wh-' His voice dropped off and he abruptly turned to glare at Tyler. 'He said everybody but TWO,' he growled. Realizing everyone was staring at him, Tyler went red. 'Um… he must have slept with the doctor?' 'Uh uh. Ain't had the private therapy sessions yet.' So much for reputation. 'THAT'S IT! EVERYBODY'S FIRED!' Everyone started complaining about that except for Yuu, who was too involved with her doll; Darkstar, who was busy propositioning the tall potted plant in the corner and Akiyama, who got a truly psychotic look on his face and started cracking his knuckles as he eyed Tyler. 'Um, okay, everyone but Akiyama’s fired.' Akiyama looked up. 'We’re not? Cool…' Fred looked at Tyler, his eyes filling with tears. 'Oh, how sad! What personal inadequacies are you trying to hide behind your need for control?' 'He thinks he isn’t as good as his KREMMY!' Akiyama yelled. 'Shut up!' Tyler yelled, going red again. 'It's true too.” Darkstar remarked, giving a lap dance to an empty chair. “He's not HALF as good a lay…' 'I SAID SHUT UP!' Fred put his hand on Tyler' shoulder. 'Oh, Mister Nelson, I'm here to help you. CONFIDE in me! It's HEALTHY!' Tyler jerked away from him. 'Are you insane? After what you did to my wrestlers? They're useless to me now! Chef wasn’t even dead!! When I got there to identify the body, they had some fat biker looking jerk with beer and chips in his beard and his @ss reamed to bloody hell! Didn’t even look like the Tali-twerp!!” “What’s the difference?” A sudden thought occurred to Akiyama. He yanked a sheet of folded paper out of his pocket. 'Here, this is the addresses of the International Incident members. They really need your help.' Fred took the paper, looking at him worriedly. 'They do?' 'Oh yeah,' Aki lied. 'Their leader keeps getting down on his knees for someone called Allah. Real issues. They all think this Mexican they've got is the devil and one of them even has a heart and toe of gold. They're seriously disturbed, every last one of them.' Fred's eyes filled with tears. 'Oh, how horrible! I have to go to them immediately.' 'Heli-pad's on the roof. Just try not to talk to the pilot. Tyler may need him later.' Fred scurried off, taking his box with him. 'When you come back, we'll tell you about someone named SNOW!' Tyler yelled. 'He's got a major self esteem complex. You'd like him.' Nelson turned to glare at the three wrestlers. 'I hate you all.' 'Does this mean we Fusions off?' Akiyama asked. Tyler just sighed and went back to his office. Darkstar woke up lying on something hard and uncomfortable, feeling a draft. 'Mmwa?' he mumbled, lifting his head. The conference room was dark, his clothes scattered everywhere, Akiyama sitting in a chair under the window with his eyes closed. There was no sign of anyone else. 'What happened?' he slurred and looked down at himself. 'And why am I naked?' 'The doctor drugged you and you did a strip tease on the table,' Akiyama told him in his usual intellectually raspy voice. 'Chef wasn’t dead. Yuu ran off with the inflatable doll. Fred is after International Incident. Tyler fired everybody else.' 'What, again? That's the third time this week.' Darkstar sat up gingerly, feeling like he had a hangover and sniggered. 'Yuu with the doll, eh? Wish I could remember that. Guess Fred turned her little brain into a pretzel. Glad to see you're still normal though.' 'Yessss…' Akiyama nodded slowly as he stood up and headed for the door. “Anyways, see you at the March Madness, boyo.” Darkstar blinked, suddenly nervous as Darkstar stood up. 'Uh, hey man, what are you talking about? Er…uh oh.' The door closed behind him, the sound of a deadbolt locking behind him. The name on the back of the door read “REAM TEAM CLUBHOUSE.” ~~~THE END~~~
