Inner Demons or Outer Demons? Part 5...A Visit To Yonder Shrink....

19 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-19-2003 “I stand on the brink of insanity. Conversing with mineself, this prophecy has left me unknowing what is reality, what is fancy. Am I the Knightly King, as I have so long believed? Or, is there a purpose more grandiose for mine life? Regardless, I need some answers. I’ve asked around, and since spiritualism answers me not, then I must try another path. A path I had long neglected, and for good reason. I shudder to think that I have an appointment….” Pendragon stands before a large building, a forlorn look upon his face. Excalibur seems to even sag a little with a sense of dejection, and it is a damned sword! A bit of snow falls from the sky, and his blonde hair shifts in the breeze. Today he wears a white cloak….Hey, even Kings get cold. The shot pans up to the door and we finally see the source of his displeasure…. Dr. Vander Hoff-Psychiatric Counseling “Aye…Yonder headshrinker. But what choice have I? This damned voice. The prophecy. Perhaps the chap will have a little bit of insight into what my problems are…” AYE…AND PERHAPS OUR MEMBER WILL SPROUT WINGS AND FLY! Pendragon: And who asked thee for thy opinion…. A man who emerged from the building looks startled as he hears Pendragon speaking into the air, but then smiles a knowing smile and pats the giant man on his shoulder… Man: Don’t worry. They’ll help ya out in there. Doc really knows his stuff. Pendragon brushes his shoulder where the man touched him and frowns down at the man… Pendragon: Dost thou believe I have a problem, whelp? The man swallows hard as his eyes grow large. Pendragon raises one gauntleted hand and points down the road… Pendragon: You really should be going. GO! The man practically sprints away, and runs right into a large woman carrying a hot-dog. He falls to the ground and she commences to beat the hell out of him with her purse while at the same time eating the dog and shouting a flurry of obscenities. Pendragon chuckles a bit as he ascends the steps into his own personal hell… AYE BUT ‘TIS A LARGE WOMAN! Pendragon: Aye, ‘tis. He pushes through the revolving door and heads to the counter. A pretty secretary sits there and she perks up as the large and noble man approaches her… Secretary: Hello. May I help you sir? Pendragon smiles as he looks at her. Hell, she is pretty, and Pendragon isn’t a stone. He places his gauntleted hands upon the counter and speaks, his voice deep and melodious… Pendragon: I have an appointment. Should be under Pendragon… She nods and looks at her computer screen…. Secretary: Hmmm….The Lord King Venneman Pendragon? Pendragon: The one and only, lass… After reading the name he booked himself under, she eyes him a tad warily now. Secretary: Down the hall. Door on the left…It’s a waiting room. He’ll get you when he is ready… Pendragon: I thank thee, lass… He nods and turns to walk down the hall. Finally, he comes to the door, opens it and walks into what appears to be a waiting room. It is empty, so Pendragon smiles…. “At least I shouldn’t have to wait long for the quack…” AYE THE PLACE IS DESERTED…. Pendragon just shakes his head and takes a seat. He picks up a magazine and begins to flip through as the shot pans over to a clock on the wall. 10:30. The scene wavers and the clock now reads 12:30. The shot pans back to show a very annoyed looking Pendragon thumbing through an issue of Family Circle. He looks up at the clock, and his look hardens. He throws the magazine and heads straight for the door to the Doc’s office. He kicks it open and suddenly a ghastly look comes across his face. The shot heads into the office, and there on the floor is a man on his hands and knees barking like a dog. The doctor sits in his chair with a box of milk bones…. Pendragon: what travesty have I stumbled upon? AYE….THIS IS AN ADOMINATION! Pendragon: Can thou be quiet for two seconds? Doc: Ummm….This isn’t what you think…And who were you talking to? Pendragon: Never thee mind…. The “dog man” crawls over to Pendragon and raises his leg above Pendragon’s boot. Pendragon frowns down at the thing and smiles. He then boots the man across the room. The man whimpers, just like a dog. Pendragon moves quickly and from a coat rack he grabs a collar and leash that just happens to be hanging there. He steps to the cowering man-dog and places the collar around it’s neck. He then jerks it from the room, gives it another kick and wipes his hands. He walks back into the room and slams the door. He removes his gauntlets and tucks them behind his sword belt, glaring at the surprised shrink the whole time. Pendragon: What was that about? Dost thou run some kind of fetish shop rather than a head doctor’s office? Doc: Umm….No. He thinks he is a dog. I was trying to help him. Pendragon: Thou means to tell me that thou were feeding the crazed bastard dog food for the better part of the morning? How does that help him? Doc: Positive reinforcement…. Pendragon: Did thee teach him how to sit? Speak? Why am I here? THIS is crazy… Pendragon turns to leave, but the Doc begins to speak to him… Doc: I know why you are here. I think I can help, just from what I have seen so far. You really do need help….You know that right? You cannot go on the way you have been…. Pendragon freezes and his head drops a tad… “The man is right. With two voices speaking to me, I need to do something. And this fellow does seem to think he can help me….Perhaps I will give him the benefit of the doubt….” Pendragon turns and takes a seat on the couch… Pendragon: You have 10 minutes, whelp. I doubt that thee can help, but thou believes that thy help will be beneficial. I will listen… Doc: Great. The first thing I see wrong with you is your clothes. Why do you dress in that kind of attire? Pendragon looks down at himself, golden armor, leather pants, boots and a cloak… Pendragon: This is what I wear! What problem has thee with mine clothes? Doc: They went out of style CENTURIES ago! And the way you speak….Archaic! And what is with the sword? I cant believe you are allowed to carry it around! Pendragon, getting noticeably angry, begins to wrap his hand around the hilt of his sword… Doc: I mean, you need to get with the program…This Delusion of being a knight…that is your whole problem! Pendragon’s eyes cloud over with anger, and his face turns a tad red…. UH OH! He leaps from his chair and grabs the doctor by the throat. He lifts him into the air and draws his sword. He holds it against the Doc’s chest before he regains his senses and lowers the small man into his chair. He sheathes his sword and smiles at the terrified shrink… Pendragon: I knew that thy help would be unwarranted and unwanted. I am torn by a prophecy and all thee can do is spout sacrileges at the Lord Pendragon! Thou would propose to me that my problem is who I am? What sense is that? I AM a Knight…I AM thy KING! I AM THE LORD OF LIGHT….KING OF NIGHT…LORD OF THE DARK!!! Pendragon: SILENCE INFIDEL!!! This fool serves to mock me and all that I stand for! And you merely chant? I AM THEE…THOU ART I!! Pendragon: Head shrink….I thank thee for thy time. Perhaps thou art good at what thee does, but I can have none of it… He storms from the room, ripping the door from its hinges as he goes. HOW WILL THOU DEFEAT THE TUBULAR ONE? TOMMY STANDS BETWEEN US AND OR KING’S GOLD!! Pendragon: And the fool cares nothing for our match. He takes me lightly, shrugs off the great Pendragon… AND HIS IGNORANCE SHALL BE HIS UNDOING! Pendragon: My might shall be his undoing. Period! I care not if he prepares for me or not. I will break him. The fool’s reign ends with Pendragon… AYE…THE GREAT LORD PENDRAGON…. Pendragon: Heaven help the souls in mine way… He passes the secretary who gives him a funny look as he walks from the office carrying on a conversation with himself. He walks through the revolving doors, and disappears into the flurry of snow that falls into the darkened world…. “The weight of the world seems to rest on mine shoulders. And what can I do about it? I face a lesser man for gold on Friday…I fight with myself for control of mine actions daily. What destiny holds? How do I know? How do I choose? This golden warrior prophecy is starting to affect me. Friday…Friday I take my place among the greats once more. Gold around mine waist…Fine times ahead…..” AYE! Pendragon: Can thee say nothing else, man!!!??? AYE!! TO BE CONTINUED
