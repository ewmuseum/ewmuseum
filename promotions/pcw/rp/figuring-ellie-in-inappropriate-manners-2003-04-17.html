Figuring Ellie- In Inappropriate Manners

17 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 17th, 2003 The scene fades in upon a large parking lot, cars dotting the parking lot everywhere. A single camera pans around, showing many different types of these vehicles before coming to a specific rest on a black Cadillac Escalade. A few seconds pass, then the camera pulls back a bit revealing the license plate: KRAYZIE. Moments later, both the drivers side and the passenger side doors open to the sight of Primetime Championship Wrestling Owner, Jeremy Howard and his wife, the lovely Jessica. Jessica: Again, why are we going to the mall and not shopping for me some clothes? A sly grin crosses Jeremy's face. Jeremy: Because I'm on an official business trip. Jessica: For PCW? Jeremy: Yeah. Jessica, dressed in tight fitting blue leather pants with a matching top, rolls her eyes. Jessica: I told you I didn't mind this short stint in PCW if it DIDN'T interfere with us. Jeremy: This won't. I get to make fun of Ellie and you get to enjoy watching. Jessica: I thought you were trying to get Kannon to redeem himself? Jeremy: I am. Doesn't mean I can't have fun doing it. Jeremy lets out a slight laugh, while continuing toward the mall entrance with his wife. Jeremy, clad in blue jean shorts and a white tank top, wraps his right arm around the love of his life, a couple made in heaven on a pretty sunny day in Mishawaka, Indiana- a city ten miles west of Elkhart. Just over top of the loving couple sits a sign with intricate designs. In the middle of it, in bold block letters, it reads University Park Mall. Jeremy reaches for the door, opening it for his wife. Jessica: Well, Mr. Business man, where we going to first? Jeremy: To see some of Ellie's close family. A look of confusion forms on Jessica's face. Jessica: At a mall? Okay, now you've lost me. The two march on, coming to a bit of a crossroads with paths. In front, a shoes store. Left or right, a long, winding path of specialty stores. Jeremy, ever the one to cause drama, leads his wife to the right two stores, coming to a place known as The Pet Shoppe. Jessica: A pet store? Jeremy: I know I can find Ellie's mother in here! Laughter begins to fill the insides of Jessica at the realization as to what her husband was getting at. Jessica: You know, if it is one thing I miss about PCW, its the crazy ass adventures we used to go on. Jeremy: You ready, cause this one's about to get real fun! We're about to get to know Ellie even better. I mean, if she's going to be the referee at Spring Fling, I feel I should know her a little better. Jessica: Whatever you say, honey. Whatever you say. Jeremy leads Jessica into the Pet Shoppe, choosing to search up and down the aisles for what the Krazed Lunatic believes is Ellie's mother. Birds, no. Fish, no. Suddenly, Jeremy comes to an abrupt stop on a dog that appears to have a smashed in face. Jeremy: There she is! Jeremy points his finger right at the lovable pooch as Jessica stops dead in her tracks, shaking her head out of disbelief, but laughing. Jessica: I don't know if I should spit on it because it could be Ellie's mother or say hello out of pity. Jeremy: Um, I'd feel sorry for their father..... He's got two dogs in the family! Jessica's mild laughter begins to increase. Jeremy: I bet everything was done doggystyle in that family! Jessica: That's..... Uh..... Gross. Jeremy: No, Ellie licking her dad's ass is gross! Jessica sighs. Jeremy: Hmmm, you know, maybe that dog isn't Ellie's mother. That dog's too cute. A somber 'acting' Jeremy lowers his head, appearing to be distraught. That is, of course, until he finds his next subject to poke fun of Ellie with.... A snake! Jessica: Oh, you've got to be kidding me. Jeremy: No way! It's got to be! Ellie's second mouth opens just as wide as that snakes mouth! Jessica: Second mouth?..... Wait..... Nevermind. I don't want to know. Jeremy: You know damn well a whore like her could fit our Escalade up in that hole. Jessica: What the hell are you doing thinking about the size of her hole? Jeremy's face tenses up, a feeling of being 'busted' showing on his face. Jeremy: Um, Duke told me after he tore that up! Jessica: Yeah, sure. Jeremy: It's true! Jessica just rolls her eyes. Jeremy: But honey, I love you. Jessica: Yeah, we'll see how much you love me sleeping on the couch tonight. Jeremy: Wha? A few seconds of dead air pass. Jeremy: Damn, lets get out of here before I cause anymore trouble. Jessica: Too late. Jeremy, still sporting a sly grin, grabs Jessica by her closest hand and leads her out of The Pet Shoppe. Amidst the constant looks of adoring fans, the loving married couple press on until they come to their next destination, Hot Topic. Jessica: Uh, I doubt we're going to find any of her so called 'family' in here. Jeremy: I know. But, I have in on good authority that Ellie's a freak! Jessica: A freak? Jeremy's smile begins to widen. Jeremy: Yeah...... In bed! Jessica: I can't believe you! An upset growl sparks from Jessica. Jessica: I'm out of here! Jeremy: But honey. Jessica: But honey hell. This escapade is all yours. Jeremy just shakes his head as Jessica storms off. This, of course, doesn't stop the Krazed Lunatic though from continuing his little mission.... This just gives him more freedom. A Store Employee: Hello sir, may I help you? Jeremy whips around to find a lady, with jet black hair and nose piercings standing before him. Jeremy: Uh, yeah. I'm looking for some freak repellent. Store Employee: Some what? Jeremy: Well, you see, I'm a wrestler and I have this match on Sunday. The referee for the match is the freak named Ellie. I need some freak repellent to keep her away from me. Store Employee: (sternly) We don't have freak repellent! Jeremy: Wha? How the hell you expect me to be safe in the ring with her when she's prone to attacking any man that gives her a look. Store Employee: Uh.... Sir.... This is a store for the specialty dressed, not preppy sluts. Try Victoria Secret. Jeremy: Ah, thats right! Preppy whores dress there all the time! Jeremy, keeping with his insane ideas, races down the mall to Victoria's Secret. Upon his entry, a beautiful young brunette approaches him like all those hounds in stores always try to do. Young Brunette: May I help you find something, sir? Jeremy: Yeah. I'm looking for something to wear inside the ring this Sunday in my wrestling match. The young brunette looks at Jeremy, weirdly. Young Brunette: For you? Jeremy: Yes! Ellie's going to be in the match and I wanna be just like her! I love her! (under his breathe) Like I like the thought of syphilis. Young Brunette: Um, well, sir, I don't know if I have anything for someone like you. Jeremy: What do you mean?!?! I wanna be just like Ellie when I grow up! Young Brunette: By wearing intimate apparel? Strange. Jeremy: Yeah, if I look like a cheap slut, everyone will love me too! Young Brunette: SECURITY!!!! The young brunette begins backing off Jeremy, apparently scared. Jeremy: Wha? I just wanted to have fun. Jeremy shrugs his shoulders. Jeremy: Oh well. The scene begins fading out as Jeremy rushes out of the intimate apparel store and toward his next destination, wherever that might be with his deranged mind..... to black!
