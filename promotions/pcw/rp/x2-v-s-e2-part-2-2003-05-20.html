X2 V.S. E2: Part 2

20 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 - 20 - 03 1 Year Ago…….. Gargano: How much farther we got?! Preston: Don’t worry G-Baby! I mean I know that walking isn’t the fastest way but…. It’ll get you into shape for the big movie; remember if those Hollywood up and ups don’t see you as fit then I can’t be in the movie either. Forget about that bro we’re on a mission! Gargano: [Sarcastically] yes to save the princess from Coopa’s palace! Hahahaha! Preston: [Chuckling under his breathe] Hey! I thought I was the one telling all the jokes around here! Gargano and Preston chat here and there about this and that all along there long walk down to the X2 set. The long walk begins to take its toll on Gargano as he hunches over leaning on a hot dog cart much to the dismay of the vender. Vender: Yo! What are yaw doing? Yaw buying or what? I don’t have all day. Gargano: What did you say to me? Do you know who you’re talking to? I am….. With John still leaning on the cart the vender abruptly cuts him off by tipping his cart and sending John stomach first into the warm concrete scarping his chest on impact. The vender just stands there and laughs as Tyler stands back watching in amazement and anger. John sits there in a fit of rage; his face is a flush of steamy red like the ketchup that is lying on the ground next to him. As the vender bends over to pick up all his spillage John’s temper gets the best of him as he stiffly kicks the vender flat in the face sending him flying down on his back with a sickening thud a lot louder than John’s. The crowd that gathered to watch this spectacle of an Actor and Vender’s argument cringes and lets out a slight Oooh chant similar to those heard in most wrestling venues. John gets up and quickly dusts himself off like nothing happened. Tyler just stands there in utter shock at what he just saw as he begins to get a slight smile across his slender face. Preston: Man! John! I didn’t know you had it in yaw! There’s promise for you after all. Tyler pats John on the back as he runs over to the spilt cart and pops open the refreshment lid and reaches in and pulls out two water bottles. He then reaches deep into his pocket and pulls out two dollars and runs over to the unconscious vender and throws them down on his chest laughing. Preston: Well that’ll do it! Let’s go John. Tyler begins to walk away slowly pushing thru the crowd of spectators like nothing ever happened. John just looks up and shakes his head as he runs thru the crowd starting to diminish. Gargano: That felt great! Preston: Yeah I know! I just gypped that guy for like a $1.25! John just stands there and watches Tyler walk by slowly sipping on his pride and joy of the moment his water. John shakes his head and chuckles slightly as he goes to catch up with “The Golden Boy” wandering ahead. Preston: Well we’re here! Both John and Tyler gaze up at the enormous tanned building in awe. Both men run over to the gate in anticipation to get in and see the action but are suddenly pulled aside by an overweight security guard with a jelly doughnut stain on his shirt. Preston: [Sarcastically] you bleeding there slim? Tyler turns towards John who has his hand over his mouth trying to withhold his laughter from the joke. Security Guard: Ok?! Where are your passes? Gargano: Oh right here guy, Ty? Tyler is fidgeting around his pocket searching for the Hollywood pass to enter thru the gate. Tyler looks up suddenly and slaps his head then pulls John to the side. Gargano: Don’t tell me! Preston: I must have left them on the kitchen table. We’ll have to go back. Gargano: No way! I am not walking another 50 blocks! Tyler scratches his chin for a couple minutes then pulls John down to whisper his idea to his ear. Preston: Just follow my lead. Tyler walks over to the edge of the building out of the security guards vision and whispers for John to run over as well. Preston: What?! Free doughnuts at the refreshment table! And wait is that what I see? Can it be? Yes! It is! It’s an anisette!!!! The security guard perks up and looks down at his watch and then in a hurried pace quickly runs away from his post passing right by John and Tyler sitting in the corner. Preston motions for Gargano as he begins to run past the gate and into the studio. John stops half way there and then turns around and looks back the other way. Preston: What is it?! Gargano: I want an anisette!!! Preston: There are no anisettes! I lied to…..Oh never mind! Preston walks away from John and into the studio. John hurries up and makes the decision to forget about the fake anisettes and follow after Preston on there mission. After about fifteen minutes they finally arrive at the end of the building. And John is still breathing heavily after only that little walk. Gargano: You know what I could go for right now? Preston: What? Gargano: An anisette. Tyler just shakes his head and walks away from John still searching for the right room. As John calls his name again Tyler turns around and looks at John. While looking at him he runs right into a heavily steel plated door. Gargano: I meant to say watch out for that door! Tyler grabs the tip of his nose in pain but suddenly realizes the irony of it all. Preston: John the door! Gargano: Yes….The door Preston: John we’re here! It’s………X2
