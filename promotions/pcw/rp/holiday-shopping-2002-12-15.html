Holiday Shopping

15 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-15-02 Laying on the bed I looked up at the ceiling. A soft sigh escaped me. Christmas was coming and I still had not gone shopping for Tessa or the kids yet. 10 shopping days left. I really didn't know what to do. I mean I had never gone Christmas shopping before. I never had anyone to shop for. I can not remember my parents. I have no idea if I have any family or not. Needless to say with my ruthless attitude I use to have I never made many friends in this business. Everyone was worried I would turn on them at the drop of a hat. So every Christmas I would spend just like any other day. I went down to a gym and I worked out. Normally the owner of the local gym down the road from my apartment kept a key hidden for me to find. I openned it up in the morning and I stayed there until night time. Same thing every year. No exchanging gifts, no egg nog, no mistle toe hanging above that I had to worry about. Just me and the sound of weights being lifted and my body being pushed to the next level. However I can not do that this year. I have someone I care about and wish to spend Christmas with. So what am I suppose to do? I don't even have a tree or any thing to hang on it. I needed to get all this stuff and fast. I jumped up from the bed and threw on a jacket from my closet. Of course it supported the only baseball team that matters, the New York Yankees. I looked around the apartment to make sure Tessa and the kids see me. I wanted this little trip out to be a secret. I don't want them to know I have yet to buy them anything for Christmas. I ran into the kitchen while the kids and Tessa was watching TV in the living room. I quickly grabbed some food that I cooked this morning for breakfast and quietly snuck out of the door. I waited for a few seconds to make sure they didn't hear me. I started to walk down the hall toward the elevator. 'What should I get them? I have no idea what they even want.' I told myself as I pushed for the bottom floor. Having never gone Christmas shopping I don't know what people want. I never thought about it before now. 'I need something really special though. Something that shows her how much I care.' I said. I didn't even know where to start. I guess I should go to the mall or something. 'Hmmm, this might be tougher then I thought.' I told myself as the elevator stopped. I stepped out into the cold air. The wind wrapped around my body as I walked down the sidewalk. I looked around at the different people out today. Women with bags on their airs covered in large coats. Men in business suits walks slowly about. Some kids that no doubt show have been at school right now. I payed them no mind though as I continued to walk through the concrete jungle. 'Jewelary? Women love that stuff. Maybe I should get her some diamonds.' I said out loud. Yeah that was it! Diamonds. What women doesn't love getting diamonds? But then it hit me. 'Oh wait, she doesn't wear that stuff. I mean she can't wear it when she wrestles.' well then I guess the diamonds are out. I needed something with serious thought behind it. I looked up to see the mall in the distance. I still hadn't thought of something to get Tessa or the kids. As I thought my mind slowly started to drift off. A chill came over me. I stopped dead in my tracks. My eyes was wide open. An image had snuck into my mind. I looked up at the mall. The events between Josh and Tessa played out before my eyes. Like a memory play I watched them. The silent audience taking in all he sees. The explosion. The fear in Tessa's eyes as the madman was getting ready to deliever the final blow to her. The sight of Christa in harm's way. The madman holding onto her with a gun to her head. 'NOOOO!!!' I screamed out loud. I threw a punch at the madman but to no avail. My fist just went through the image and I stumbled forward, nearly falling on my face. People around looked at me and stared. I did not even see them anymore. The entire area before me vanished into the sight of my fears. I could see the mall stores. The smell of blood hung in the air and danced on my nose. I could hear the groans of the people that was caught in the cross fire. The suffering and pain before my eyes. How did I remember all of this when I wasn't even there? I heard Tessa's noice scream out. 'Tessa!' I yelled. I looked around for her. By now there was a rather large gathering watching me. People stared and pointed, making comments to each other. I was too lost in my fantasy to even see them. My head was poisioned with images of this haunting moment. I was crippled to the real world, unable to shake these visions before my eyes. I looked around for my Tessa. I could hear her calling out but I couldn't see her. I howled out for her. Calling her name as I franticly searched for her. I moved past the dead bodies hoping that each one isn't her. With each foot step I took my hate for Josh Starwind grew a little more. My heart was slowly being consumed by the black hate. My rage was building inside of my, begging to be released. He had caused all this. The pain, the suffering, the lost. It was all his fault. His curse had causing innocent people to die. He was no better then the madman doing all this. He is the plague casted down on us for our sins. The monster dressed as a human. He had to pay for his crimes against man. And I was going to make him pay with my own bare hands. One of the people watching had decided he was going to see if I was ok. He touched my shoulder from behind but he was unfortunate enough to be on the receiving end of a punch to the jaw by me. He fell to the ground and the people quickly backed away from me. That created a large enough hole for me to run through. I pushed my legs hard. I was running as hard as I could. I had to find Tessa and Christa. Their voices rang in my ears calling for me. But I couldn't find them. I had to get to them before the madman did. If I lost them I don't know what I would do. Then I saw her. Tessa was being held by the madman. He had his hand around her throat holding her in the air. My blood boiled. My vision got blurry as my anger begun to peak. The only thing in life I cared about was being hurt by this person. The man looked over at me with an arrogant look. I finally saw his face for the first time. He looked just like Josh Starwind. My breath was caught in my throat. Then I hear Tessa cry out in pain. Everything went black as I rushed the man. I dove in at him for a shoulder tackle. I could hear metal and bone meeting. I hit the cold ground. I slowly begun to open my eyes and see where I was at. The image had finally left me. I slowly gathered myself and got to my feet. I stumbled around for a few seconds before I regained my balance. My shoulder was killing me. My eyes finally openned fully. My vision cleared and I saw what I had hit. I had driven my shoulder into the side of a parked car. The side door was crushed from where I had hit it. I was dizzy as my head had hit the car too. 'ptt..ptt...ptt...ptt' I looked around for what was causing that noise. It was like a soft pin drop. I looked down to see a small puddle of red blood. Blood was dripping into it making it larger and larger. I touched my temple to discover the source of the blood. I had busted my head open apon hitting the car. I looked around to make sure no one saw me. Pulling out my wallet I grabbed a couple bills and put it under the windshield wiper. I walked off like nothing happened. After all, I still needed to go Christmas shopping for Tessa and the kids.
