Pressing Matters? Or just a really cheap pun?

6 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Pressing Matters? Or just a really cheap pun? Featuring: Tim Walden Date: December 6, 2002 The scene opens upon what appears to be a mildly crowded press room, reporters speaking at a frenetic pace in hushed tones in their seats, some standing around in front of the blue background...Suddenly, Tim Walden walks through the oak door in the corner of the room to the right of the main position, walking briskly to the lectern, which reads PCW on the front of it as the crowd quiets a little, camera bulbs flashing and giving him brief disorientations...He taps the microphone, as they fall into relative silence. Tim: Is this on? Okay, good. Now, as you all know, I am competing in two major matches in the next few weeks. This conference is to address the first of these matches. Tomorrow night, I am participating in a Cooking Utensils hardcore match against four adversaries, 'The' Rush Mason, 'The End' Skylar Green, The Hardcore Isoceles Trapezoid, and Totally Tubular Tommy, for the vacant Continental Title. This is being known as the Chef Memorial Match, though he isn't dead so much as retired, having felt himself unable to defend the title against me, but this is irrelevant... Tim steps back for a second, recovering his train of thought, before stepping back to the lectern. Tim: In recent days, one of my opponents, notably The Rush, has taken it upon himself to make public announcement regarding his participation in the match and his opinions of his opponents, notably myself. Now, while I respect the efforts of Mr. Mason in regards to his ability to wrestle in an extreme setting, I cannot allow the public to be subjected to his factual inaccuracy. While I have lost my fair share of matches, he ignores the fact that the majority of these losses were not of my own fault, as they have come of illicit pinning positions, run-ins by people with vendettas against my opponents, and on over five different occasions illegal involvement by the HIT himself. However, dealing with him is a different story which may be covered later on.. A few overeager reporters are reaching their hands up at this point, and Walden looks over the crowd with ambivalence. Tim: Yes, if you could please save your questions for afterward, I'll get to them if we have time here at the Front Office, after all, I have work to do and flights to catch. Now, anyway, though I have lost my fair number of matches, there were very few that I can honestly say I was beaten fairly within. He eventually went on to unveil his match for the Trapezoid, and for once, I'd have to say that he failed to give the HIT enough credit. He is a math teacher, not merely some poor schlub who has demonstrated the ability to use the Newtonian method of square-root approximation. In fact, my qualifications to take care of PCW's financial situation even surpass that sort of mere grade-school demonstration. I am rather ashamed to see how few people take me seriously as a threat, in spite of my recent string of successes. I assume this has something to do with the fact that the Trapezoid's recent comments to the effect of his intent to commit hideous acts of violence against me, taking advantage of the situation. However, I assure you all, I will be ready by the time of the match to take anything he throws at me and attack him as brutally as need be to win, as well as everyone else who gets between me and the title which I earned at the Alliances match with my performance defeating the Trapezoid himself, as well as El Diablo, Duke Williams and even the PCW World Champion, Clyde, that makes three of the four men competing for the World Title on Sunday as a matter of fact, inside one of the most hellacious structures in all of wrestling! This in itself should make it evident that while I will have my work cut out with the opposition in the match, I am fully capable of beating them, and with that in mind I will do all that I can to win. I'd like to open the floor to questions at this point... Immediately, several hands shoot upward, and Tim sighs... Tim: Firstly, I'd like to point out that the washrooms are in the hall, coming out and walking left, third and fourth doors on the right. And secondly, I'd also like to point out that no, PCW is not considering hiring wrestlers who double as reporters right now. Several hands go down amongst the little grumbling of the gallery, while about 20 percent of the people leave, and PCW's Financial Reclamation Executive looks a little disappointed. Tim: I had hoped that wasn't actually necessary this time..Oh well, Mr. Davis from the Primetime Herald. A 30-something man stands up from where Tim points, clipboard in hand, assumedly Mr. Davis... Davis: Thank you, and good luck on the match by the way. Now, my question is in regards to your feelings about your match Sunday, as you've been signed to tag with Brandi against the Trapezoid and Teresa Tomas once more, and I'm wondering not about this much, but rather what your thoughts are about fighting alongside Vilas Lavagna a mere five days after your victory over him for the Cruiserweight Title, not to mention against El Romantico.. Tim: Well, it's simple. I'm aware that Vilas lost to me when it counted, but he put in a good effort in defeat, and I applaud that, seeing as how his victory over Romantico solidifies the team as simply better than that which we have to face on the first truly PCW/RUSH edition of Adrenaline, and that's what really matters. He had better just not go letting his inexperience get in the way of our victory, or life might get rougher for him. Next question? Yes, Gerald from theblockwrestling.com? Gerald: Yes, I'm just wondering if you'd like to comment on reports of a, er, hospital stay that supposedly happened a couple weeks ago, apparently as a result of a large gash on your forehead? Tim stops dead for a second, rather stunned, perhaps to the realization that someone found out that much...He inhales, then exhales deeply before pulling his head back to the microphone. Tim: Well, much as I was trying to keep this one out of the press, there was a breach of security in PCW offices, and an outsider who I was unable to identify, obviously having been someone with a dislike for my management strategies, this outsider was able to gain access to my office as I moved into a new building, then proceeded to attack me from behind with what turned out to be one of my own paperweights while I was distracted. This is all I really know on the situation. Okay, aaaaaaand...Jeff Rogman, Wrestling Week in Review. Jeff stands, as Gerald had earlier, but they didn't merit their own separate lines of dialogue for their obvious action.. Jeff: Do you have any messages to pass on for your opponents? Tim: I think it's relatively simple. For the other three men, I simply point out that if they stay out of my way against the Trapezoid, I won't be forced to take extreme measures greater than that which they will need to go through for me to simply win the match as I plan. Mere days before I headed to the developmental academy PWA, I witnessed the victory of Frisco over Ivan Stanislav and Jeremy Howard to gain the vacant Continental Title in addition to his Cruiserweight gold at PCW Supremacy. I come into Friday's match with a plan, and that is to make history repeat itself, to whatever costs need to be taken within reason, to become the second Cruisernental Champion of PCW and seal my place within the record books, the histories that people like you will put together. And for the Trapezoid, it's even simpler. I've taken away a lot from you since you dared to show your face around here, and I've even backed you into the corner, to the point where you had to give up the one thing that set you apart in the Front Office's rage just to get one last chance to get your revenge...You took the chance, and you've put the war on your field, but I'm not backing down until I'm either victorious or unconscious, and it's going to take a hell of a lot to get the latter. All riiiight.... Walden's slowed speech is probably related to the underwhelming number of hands still raised...Zero. Tim: Well, I'd hoped there'd be a little more enthusiasm here, but it's all for the best, I guess, since I really would rather be working in all honesty. Well, bye folks. Tim walks away as the reporters begin, or more accurately continue to file out from the room...And he promptly stumbles on a cord. Tim rears up his foot to stomp down upon the impediment, but thinks better of it and opens the door, leaving the room as the scene fades back to black.. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
