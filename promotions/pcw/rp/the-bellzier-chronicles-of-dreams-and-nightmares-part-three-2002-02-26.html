The Bellzier Chronicles: Of Dreams and Nightmares [Part Three]

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 26, 2002 DreamLandTime Unknown I was screaming at the top of my lungs. Oh, the pain. “Soooooome Onnnnnnnnnne Heeeeeeeeeelp Meeeeeeeeeeee!…..!!!” There I am, falling away by the wayside and not coming up. Where was I falling from? I guess my own world, who knows? It’s been awhile since I’ve actually had a grip on reality. Falling has never been a problem of mine….getting up – well, that’s the real challenge. I was falling backside first into some dark, cylindrical hole. They always have to be dark and dank, don’t they? So now you’re wondering how could it possibly be cylindrical when I can’t even see anything? Well, gravity tells me that I should be going straight down, unless I was thrown into this hole at an angle which would continue building up an acceleration towards a specific direction. As far as I know, I was thrown straight down, but I was bouncing around on the walls like a pinball is some really bad rip-off movie pinball machine. That’s how I know it’s cylindrical! Whoo! Beat that. Anyways, I fell and fell…until I finally hit something hard. Okay, it wasn’t that hard. “Aww geez….” I said to myself, rubbing my head and looking around. “What a fall. Where the hell am I?” I looked around myself for a few moments, trying to orient myself to where I was. Location is crucial, you know? People don’t like wandering into places they know nothing about. The sky above me was a beautiful sky….green. Green….hmph. Now, the last time I checked, the sky was blue, wasn’t it? Yes, yes…I’m right. The sky IS blue, unless I’m on some other planet. Oh no…. I looked down on what I was laying on. I was falling for at least three or four minutes, so I should have gathered enough acceleration to lay me flat as a quarter on the ground. That is, if the ground was solid. But I didn’t feel a bump at all. In actuality, I felt better than ever. That’s when I looked down, and saw that I was sitting on a great pile of green Jello. Bill Cosby would have been proud. I pulled my hands out of the green blob, looking at the green mess on my hands. “Gosh, this stuff is sticky….” “ Hey you! Get out of the lake! There’s a monster in there!” I looked around to see where that horrible voice was coming from. Terror….fear….excitement? No, no, strike that last word. It was mostly terror and fear. After dealing with the big, bad Tiger-Man, I knew that wherever, I was, I would be in trouble. Let’s see, the sky was green and I landed in this pile of Jello. Three guesses on where you think I am, smart ass. So here I am, desperately digging my legs out of the Jello and trying to make my way to solid ground. The entire Jello lake was about the size of a small house. It was like quicksand, pulling me underneath. Funny, it wasn’t doing that before. Beginning to fear being eaten up by some Jello monster, I started to swim. It was surprisingly easy, as I dashed my arms in and out of the Jello, tearing it up and actually making good time. Finally, I reached the Jello shore line, where I was cold, tired, and my entire lower body from the waist down was green. I hate ironic finishes. “I hate Jello. I hate it so much.” I looked up, to see a little girl looking down at me and smiling. I don’t like that. Whenever a female is smiling at you, it either means they like you or they’re about to do something incredible horrible to you. Since females don’t seem to jive my innards, I was assuming she was about to pull out my toenails or something. “Hello, little girl,” I said, looking scared. “Who are you, and how are you going to make me suffer?” She cackled loudly. “You silly goose! I’m not going to make you suffer. My name’s SGItL, otherwise known as the Smartest Girl in the Land! But my friends call me Sherri. I’m sorry for scaring you like that, but I find it funny when I watch people trying to swim through the Jello lakes like that.” Trying? I think I just broke the 50 meter freestyle record, and there was a hell of a lot more resistance in Jello than there is in water. “Whew, well, that’s a relief. I actually thought there was a Jello monster that was going to eat me or something…” I wiped the sweat off of my forehead and admired my surroundings. It’s not everyday that you find yourself knee deep in a pool of jello. “Well….there sorta are. You see, these Jello lakes were a practical joke, okay? You know that Maples the Caterpillar rules over all of Dream Land, right?” I knew it. I knew I was back here in Dream Land, a place that I completely loathe and hate. I’m lame, myself, but this place just annoys me. Everything was like my own world, except flipped around and distorted. “I believe Fearful was telling me about him. He’s some sort of a tyrant, right? Rules with an iron fist and controls everyone?” Sherri just shrugged. Odd for someone who’s supposed to be so smart. “Well, I guess – considering that caterpillars don’t even have fists, but oh well. Besides being somewhat of a meaniehead, he also has a weird sense of humor. One of his latest creations were Jello storms. He has it rigged so that once every few weeks or so, instead of rain, we get Jello coming down from the heavens. Well, this was fine and dandy with me. Sure, it was annoying and all, but not something I would cry about. I think it was at this time when people started realizing that Jello does not evaporate away. For the most part, it’s a solid material, so it just started accumulating. So much, in fact, that these Jello lakes started popping up everywhere.” Couldn’t someone just take it away? Don’t you people have homeless people that could take advantage of these Jello lakes? Sure, it’s not exactly healthy, but food is food, right?” “Homeless? What’s a homeless?” She asked, with an inquisitive look on her face. “We have Less Homes – it’s so sad. They’re everywhere nowadays.” I turn around, and I see a house walking around aimlessly. It’s a one story flat, painted white all the way around with sky blue paint around the windows and on the door. Suddenly, all of the windows bust open…. “ DOES ANYONE NEED A HOME? PLEASE? I’M AVAILABLE! ANYONE? OH GOD, SOMEONE LIVE IN ME!” The house sulked away, and I actually felt a tear at my eye. Oh no, I wasn’t feeling sorry for the home – I just realized that I ruined my Nike Awesomes shoes by swimming in so much Jello. So, so sad. Sherri started speaking again, something I couldn’t do after watching a home sulk away like that. “Anyways, this accumulation isn’t even the biggest problem. The biggest problem is actually the Jello….becoming civilized.” I had a curious look on my face as she finished. “What exactly do you mean….civilized?” “They say it got smart,” Sherri said with wide open eyes. Smart? Smart Jello? My idea of smart Jello was the kind that was 10% less sugar and you could make it in less than ten minutes. If she was talking about Jello that could do derivatives and solve complex derivations in Calculus….well, that was just down right scary. “I’m a bit sketchy on that last part. Could you elaborate on how Jello could become frickin’ smart?” “No one even saw it coming. Not even me, and I’m the Smartest Girl in the Land. They just started coming out of the lakes, in human form, and come out lusting for….” “Blood?” I said, interrupting her. “No! Worse! …… Shoes!” Oh dear god, that’s lame. I tried to contain my laughter, but a few snickers seem to pop out of nowhere. “Uhm, shoes? They hunger for shoes? Is there any particular brand that they like? Reeboks? Adidas? Payless specials?” “They don’t discriminate, they just take without permission. I, personally, have lost eight pairs to the monsters. Thankfully, I have created a weapon against them.” She pulls out a large weapon, which looks like a rifle. It’s completely metallic, with odd knobs and buttons all over the gun. On the side, I notice it has a label on it. I turn my head to read it and it says, “Jello Killer 2K1 Beeyotch.” I wondered if that name was copyrighted. “My, that looks awfully dangerous. Something that some dangerous mad scientist would create, not a pretty girl like you.” Sherri pumps the gun’s handle, pointing it at me threateningly. I began to back up slowly. That’s what I get for trying to get laid all the time. “This is not a toy. It generates enough heat to melt any piece of Jello into gelatinous form. No Jello warrior could ever survive one blast of this.” I stared at the gun admiring it. “Well, I’m sure you could even market a video game and a movie about this. You could be rich!” “Yes!” Then I actually thought about it. “Well, probably not. Movies about Jello might not be a hit success. Hell, I remember a movie about this giant lizard named Godzilla, which couldn’t even make money if it had Britney Spears dating that lizard monstrosity of theirs. But still, it could be marketable.” “It’s more than just a gun, you know? It’s also a great hand drier, and I use it get rid of nasty Jello stains. Let me show you…” She fired the gun at my pants, which were stained green from the Jello swim I had. It slowly began to peel away, falling away at my feet. I began to smile, as I thought the pants were lost for sure. Hey, this chick was smart! “Hey, thanks for that. I thought I might have to throw these pants away and run around here in my underwear.” “No problem. Anyways, we should be getting out here. It’s starting to get late, and those Jello warriors like to come out during the middle of the day.” I started to nod, but something hit me in the head. It didn’t really hurt, but it was sort of embarrassing. I looked down, to see that a green glob of Jello had hit me in the head. I looked up, and I saw a medium sized Jello man was standing there. He had a mean grimace on his face, and if I didn’t know any better, I’d say he was upset at me. He had one of those looks that said that he had an antelope shoved up his butt and he just couldn’t get it out. Ouch. That really hurts. Anyways, needless to say, I was a bit at a loss for words. What else can you say when a five foot Jello man is standing in front of you? “What’s that, Sherri?” She looked afraid, and I didn’t blame her. “It’s them…it’s the Jello men.” “Them? I only see one…” Suddenly, the Jello man broke off in half and there appeared two Jello men before me. They both duplicated again, and suddenly there were four Jello men. One more duplication, and suddenly there were eight! I just had to open my big mouth…. “ Sherri….do something!” I exclaimed. “What are they going to do us? Eat us? Kill us?” “Weren’t you listening before? I told you what they want…they want your shoes! No stand back and let me do my job!” Sherri began firing her gun, which cut down two of the Jello men immediately. They fell into a green mess on the ground, slopping away to whatever Jello afterlife laid before them. The other men began to run off, obviously afraid of what was going to happen to them. So, they were full of Jello, but perhaps they had some brains after all. Well, with them on the run, I wasn’t going to let them get away so easily. I ran after them, faster and faster, and leapt on top of one. I was laying on his stomach, and immediately cocked my fist back, slamming it into his, uhm, face. Much to my surprise, my hand went right through, creating a hole in his head. I brought my hand back out, which was now covered with green goop, leaving a permanent hole in the poor guy’s head. He still, however, could speak. Sherri walked up to him and shoved her gun in his face. “DON’T MOVE SCUMBAG, OR YOU’RE JELLO MIX AGAIN!” The jello man threw his arms in the air and pleaded for her not to shoot. “No! No! Please god, don’t hurt me! I swear, I wasn’t going to take your shoes! I just wanted to look at them!” I can’t say I felt sorry for the guy, since he did jump us. I wondered what his intentions were, and why exactly he had thought it necessary to take us out. I wanted answers, and I wanted them now. “Tell me who you are! Tell me why you attacked us!” “Okay! Okay! I’ll tell you, just don’t hurt me!” He took a deep, green breath, and sighed. “My name’s…my name’s…well, I don’t have a name. I’m just a Jello warrior. I was sent here by King Coverdale to kidnap you and take you back to his hidden lair.” Sherri snickered. “King Coverdale? Hah! He’s been dead for years now! The only thing left are his eyes, which are always floating around, peeping in on people while they’re showering.” “He hasn’t been dead, just hiding. And now he’s back, and he wants control over Dream Land again. The only way he can get it back is with the outsiders help.” The outsider….that was me. I needed to know the whole story, but I assumed I’ll be getting it soon. Sherri looked over at me. “The outsider must be you.” My, she’s a smart girl, isn’t she? “Is he the one who brought me back? Is he? Did he send me back to this world?” “Yes, he did. But he assumed that you owed him something for allowing you to go back to your world for your match. He knew you were desperate to go back, but he knew just as equally that you would not make it for your match. He made it possible for you to return, if only for a short while. Now he has a new task for you, and he expects you to repay him in full.” “That reminds me…I still have no idea who you are.” Sherri said, arching one eyebrow at me. I just stood there and sighed. It was a really long story.
