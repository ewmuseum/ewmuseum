Cockney Geezah!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th June! “Who are YEE!?” [Slowly, our scene fades in to a loud, noisy Glasgow street. It is a dull and gloomy day in the far-from beautiful city, and spittle’s of rain fall gently from the sky. People dodge in and out of each other, trying to get out of the rain for fear of sudden downfall. Upper classes, businessmen … Basically, none of the people around here are on the pond life level of Andy Murray and Rab. Speaking over out favourite neds, we zoom into the closed doorway which looks like it leads to a small set of apartments. Andy and Rab stand in the doorway, sheltered from the rain, watching all the people try and escape it. Andy is of course wearing a red and white Adidas shell suit & baseball cap, and Rab? He’s wearing a black and white number.] Andy Murray: Hoho … Look at awl these toffs min … Rab: Aye min … Watchin’ toffs getting’ soaked is pure mental man! [Watching the unfortunate “toffs,” Andy and Rab laugh at their misfortune, as if they held a higher place in society because they’re sheltered.] Andy Murray: Look at that chube over there min! [Andy points across the street, where a smartly dressed man in a suit is walking, suitcase over his head to shield it from the rain. Suddenly, a Stagecoach bus drives buy, sending the contents of a nearby puddle flaying up onto the man. Andy and Rab chuckle.] Rab: That’s pure magic by the way! Andy Murray: Hoho … Stupid toffs. Rab: Aye, stupid toffs. [Suddenly, the delightful duo’s oh-so intelligent conversation is interrupted, when an unknown person barges between them, seeking shelter under doorway. This person is wearing casual clothes, that really are far from trendy. Old blue jeans, weathered brown boots, and a checked jacket that just yells “Lumberjack.” The man stops, and rubs his hands together in the cold, Andy and Rab exchange confused glances.] Andy Murray: Ho! [The man turns to look at Andy and Rab.] Man: Oh, sorry lads. [COCKNEY! Andy and Rab have encountered a cockney … In Glasgow?!] Rab: WHO ARE YEE!? [The man stares at Rab for a moment, before snapping into action, and shaking his hand.] Man: Oh right, my name is Dave, yorself? Rab: Ah’m Rab. Dave: Rab, short for Robert? Rab: No, just Rab, ya chube. [Dave turns to face Andy, before shaking his hand also.] Dave: And 'oo are yer? Andy Murray: How do you dinna ken ma name? Ah’m a pure famous wrestler from the tele, by the way. Who are ye? Rush Mason? Ye follow Voodoo? Is that why ye speak like a pure bampot? Dave: Well no, actually, I'm from London, right, that's where I cop the accent from, yer see. Andy Murray: Accent? Is that one ‘o they new vegetarian diets. Dave: No, it's ... uhhh ... Nevermind. [Andy again looks at Rab with a confused Rab, there is an uneasy aura between the three for a second, before Cockney Dave breaks the silence.] Dave: So lads, yer don't 'appen ter know where I can find a post office, do yer? Rab: Post Office? What’s ‘at? Andy Murray: Ah think that’s one of they places where you send emails and stuff. Dave: Actually it's where yer send letters and postcards and fings. [Andy glances a look of disgust at cockney Dave, as does Rab.] Andy Murray: Don’t you get all cocky wi’ me pal … Or ah’m gonnae pure hammer ye for actin’ like a chube, ye got me!? Dave: 'ooa, right, no need ter get mad lads … Rab: Shut it, ya chube. [Andy and Rab back Cockney Dave into a the corner of the doorway, Dave seems mildly scared.] Andy Murray: Did I tell ye to speak? Dave: Nope. Andy Murray: Then shut up, before I chap ma fist off yer heed. [Just when Andy looks like he could hit Dave, Dave points out to the road.] Dave: 'Ave a look! Struth! It's a llama! Right! [Neds being neds, Andy and Rab turn around, of course there is no Llama, but they still look around for it. Suddenly, Dave darts between the two, and makes his escape, oblivious to Andy and Rab.] Andy Murray: Ho! There isn’t no llama! Rab: Aye … [Andy and Rab turn around, expecting Dave to still be there, and of course, he isn’t.] Rab: What!? Where’d that chube pure run awa’ to? Andy Murray: Where’d he go? [After looking around the doorway for Dave, Andy and Rab surprisingly cannot find him.] Andy Murray: Chube … Lets go pure write our rap song now min! Rab: Aye! [The scene slowly fades out, as Andy and Rab places their hands in their pockets, and head back to ned central – Andy’s apartment!]
