Has Kannon Seen The Light?

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/05/02 Denial is a wonderful thing. Don’t want to accept something. Deny it. Don’t feel like accepting something. Deny it. Get bad news you don’t feel like coming to terms with. Deny it. When the CEO of a company decides to fire you along with the rest of the roster. Deny it. When that CEO is Tyler Nelson and you are his Commissioner. Deny it. That’s right, Xavier Kannon was in denial. After his firing he refused to accept it, despite what others told him, or what he read, in the face of plentiful evidence.... In his Scientological head he never lost his job, Tyler never lost it, and he was never stripped of his titles. However, all good things must come to an end as Ellie~! has decided to break the news to the Gold Patron Meritorious.... The question is how will he take it. Currently no bets are being taken on “Well”. “WHAT DO YOU MEAN I WAS FIRED? THEY’VE GOTTEN TO YOU HAVEN’T THAT? I KNEW WHEN YOU SET THE AIRPORT ALARM OFF IT WAS BECAUSE THEY CHIPPED YOUR HEAD.... CAN THEY HEAR ME NOW? ARE YOU WEARING A WIRE?” We open up in the open plan kitchen of Xavier Kannon’s Hubbardville residence, situated in the exclusive Jenna Elfman Court development. Amidst the reflections of stainless steel surfaces and hanging herbs the Scientologist isn’t taking the news to well. Xavier : “Don’t worry Ellie~!, I’ll remove it for you....” He turns around and opens a draw to pull out a long bread knife before lunging at Eleanor with it, much to her.... Well....Let us just say she runs. Eleanor : “ARGHHHHH!” The prep turns on her heels and runs around the kitchen, Xavier following her, crazed with paranoia and holding the knife up in a stabbing position as he gives chase. Xavier : “I’ll free your mind from their commands!” As they continue their Benny Hill-esque chase around the kitchen, Ellie~! running I fear and Kannon, his judgement impaired by his heightened denial.... ....On the 10th lap of the room, around the table in the middle, Eleanor grabs a griddle pan and turns around, connecting it with Kannon’s forehead and knocking him cold. Eleanor : “YAY ME.....” She then looks down at the fallen Scientologist and covers her mouth.... Gasping and regretting her aggressive action. Eleanor : “Oh Hubbard.... I’m going to be on the news.... Or worse... Judge Judy!” As she looks down agasp at Kannon he slowly comes to..... Holding his head and blinking at the light that comes flooding down into his eyes. Xavier : “*Groan....*” He shakes his head and continues to hold his forehead, patting it for blood and grimacing at the pain ringing and throbbing throughout his skull. His preppy valet knees down beside him, looking apologetic and stroking Xavier’s hair back. Eleanor : “Um....Like....Sorry?” Xavier’s eyes gradually become less glazed and he tries to clear the cobwebs and focus on his valet. Xavier : “As you would say.... Like, OWWWWWWW!!!” He slowly stumbles up to his feet, steadying himself on the kitchen counters and doing his best to keep his balance through the grinding pain in his head and the numb feeling in his limbs. Eleanor : “Do you remember anything? That was a mean swin-....I mean, FALL you took there... You just fell right over and banged your head!” He stretches and blinks repeatedly in an attempt to clear his vision before laying eyes on Ellie~! Xavier : “Er.... Ellie~!.... YOU HIT ME WITH A BLOODY CAST IRON GRIDDLE PAN!” She looks down to her right hand where the makeshift weapon is still grasped and gulps, trying to look innocent. Eleanor : “Oh....Yeah.... I suppose I did kinda.... Would you buy that it was an accident? One of those kitchen accidents that happen....... Wait, what else do you remember?” This question causes Kannon to pause for deep thought. Eleanor : “Try and remember things from.... Say.... 9 days ago and stuff, then that’ll save me the arduous task of having to, like, tell you!” The dazed Gold Patron Meritorious staggers back and takes a seat on one of the stools that stand infront of the breakfast bar and tries to remember back to the time Eleanor has set, still struggling through the extra barrier of his sore head. Then suddenly, a light seems to flick on from behind his bright Green eyes and a grin crosses his face. Xavier : “YES! I remember now!” Miss Hall looks at him, rather confused and mutters under his breath. Eleanor : “Wow.... I, like, SO didn’t expect him to take it this well!” Xavier : “I BEAT DUKE! I can recall it as if it was yesterday! I grabbed the drunkard and planted his hairy, disgrace of a face right into my title.... Then won... I REMEMBER! I BEAT DUKE!.... Wait a minute....” As his face changes expression a little to fast for her liking, Ellie~! cringes... Guessing he has now remembered. Eleanor : “....And here it comes....” The camera focuses in on Ellie~!’s face, already screwed up and expecting a tirade from the Scientologist. “WAIT A MINUTE....... I WAS FIRED! MY TITLE.... MY COMMISSIONERSHIP.....GONE!!! HOW IN HUBBARD’S NAME COULD NELSON FIRE ME! I HELPED HIM BUY THE DAMN COMPANY!” For his outburst, Ellie~! covers her ears and screws her face up before opening one eye to see if he has finished and finally un-clamping her head. Eleanor : “So you, like, remembered then?” Before she has even finished pronouncing the “N” she again screws her face up and clamps her head, predicting another outburst.... ......She isn’t wrong... Xavier : “OF COURSE I REMEMBER! IT’S KINDA HARD TO FORGET SPILLING YOUR BLOOD TO WIN A MATCH ONLY FOR THE PRIZE TO BE PULLED OUT FROM UNDER YOU!!! I EVEN BOOKED NELSON IN THAT DAMN TITLE MATCH AND THEN HE GOES AND FIRES ME JUST BECAUSE THAT BIG GREEK RETARD BEATS HIM!” Even with her hands pressed so hard against the sides of her head her nails turn White, Ellie~! nods along as her man vents his frustrations. She goes to relax her hold but then suddenly clasps tightly again. Xavier : “I BEAT BIG ALAN STEELE!!! I PINNED HIS SHOULDERS TO THAT MAT.... BY ALL RIGHTS I SHOULD BE THE ONE FIRING NELSON! IF HE THINKS I AM GOING TO BE ALL FRIENDLY ON FRIDAY HE CAN SHOVE HIS NON-SCIENTOLOGICAL CEO TITLE UP HIS NON-SCIENTOLOGICAL ASS!” While Kannon continues to rant, Ellie~! turns to the camera and tries to shout over him, barely managing to be heard. Eleanor : “I think you’d better go now.... These things can last hours....” She winks at the camera and the shot starts to fade out, as does Kannon’s bellowing voice. “I KNOW..... I AM NOT A SCIENTOLOGIST HENCE I AM NOT PERFECT SO I’LL FIRE EVERYONE! NOTE TO TYLER.... CONVERT.... IT’S A LOT EASIER!” Black.
