The War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part X]

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 5, 2002 It was a PINKtastic night. It was PINKriffic. It was pure PINKness. Funny that the night was revolved all around PINK! when in fact the Heartbreakers brought home the gold. Wrestle Vessel was a night to remember for the Heartbreakers, where Jack Valentine pinned William S. Powell to regain the tag team titles that were so wrongfully stolen from them on May 12 at Supremacy. Now, on their second reign as champions here in PCW, they have two goals in mind. The first is to destroy Politically Incorrect's reign as the longest champions ever, and the second goal, which may not be accomplished because The Heartbreakers would like to think that they will hold the belts for all of eternity, but the second, is to become the first ever 6-Time Tag Team Champions to grace the face of a PCW ring...eliminated Da Westsidaz and the Blood Brothers from the record books. It may not be an easy task, but the buck starts Friday on Fusion when The Heartbreakers have their first title defense against the new number one contenders, Cowabunga Connection. In the past, The Heartbreakers have had nothing bad to say about their bodacious buds from the Connection. The Heartbreakers know how to hang 10 just as well as the next dude. Hell, Jack and Johnny have been said to hang a lot more than 10 when they are in private, but that's another story. Cowabunga Connection is not filth-covered in greed like the dirty Politicians, and they aren't ridden by their egotistical musical talents like the Pretty Cool Guys. They are just surfers. Albeit, they have never been tag team champions, and the Heartbreakers will do their best to keep it that way on Friday, they will get their shot, and Jack and Johnny will be ready. Last we saw Johnny and Jack outside of the wrestling ring though, they were in trouble. In trouble with both the Rainbow Squad and with the Village People from the PURPLE~! Everyone thought that the injuries instilled by the events that took place in the war would affect their performance in the ring last Sunday, but it didn't, and now they are stronger than ever. They will be ready for anything and everything that the PURPLE~!, PINK! or anyone else has to offer as our story continues... Back at the Warehouse where Johnny Heart and Rainbow Squad Member Smith were last seen... Johnny Heart could hear voices, the whole gang must be here by now, what time was it, it was too painful to try and look at his watch, it was not worth it, the time was not important. He could hear the whine of a hand operated drill. One of those cordless ones. It was bad enough that they were armed, but they were armed with PURPLE~!. His face was on the concrete floor. He tried to look around him, there must be a solution. He had no weapons, the exits must be guarded by The Village People, he could barely move. He must have some advantage, some tool, or ability that he could use to his advantage. Always look on the bright side, a trifle difficult, but there must be something. He rolled and twisted so that he could see in the other direction. A big PURPLE~! rubber...tire...was there. The men were still busy with Smith wheeling him about on some trolley. All that, was somewhere behind him. If he could get to the tire he might be able to do something. He crawled painfully towards it. There was no point in trying to avoid being seen. The quicker he got to it the better. What had tires like that, he crawled. He could taste something in his mouth, something had got in his mouth, it was warm and smooth, but sharp at one end. He spat it out. A tooth. The first thing that came to his mind... Johnny Heart: 'Damnit! I've gotta get this fixed before my dearest fans see me in this atrocious state...' He then remembered what he was there for...to save Smith, and destroy the PURPLE~! squad. Checking in on The Mystery Man and Jack Valentine The streets were packed with people, there was some sort of parade going on. The Mystery Man usually relied on his instinct to find bars. It seldom failed. The pub was called the 'Tool-shed.' He sat at a window seat, the silhouettes of the floats and crowds flickered across the far wall. The waitress was preparing the PURPLE~! punch for him. He would have asked for more, but that was all the PURPLE~! they had. He was contenting himself with a packet of saltines now, he had already finished his starter of a few shots of the punch. Valentine was nowhere to be seen. He was folding the saltine packet in half lengthwise. Then again. Now he folded the long rectangular strip so that it formed a right angle. He folded the arms of the right angle across each other, till left with a single tuft. He tucked it into the fold already provided by a previous fold, making it look like a big...er...lovetoy. He then remembered that he didn't have any cigarettes. Back to the Warehouse... Johnny Heart had finally dragged himself over to the tire, it was attached to a fork lift truck. A heavy base unit, and a dirty pallet on the forks. It was an old diesel one, there was an old familiar name built into the base. The keys were in the ignition. It seemed to have the right number of pedals and a strangely horizontal steering wheel. The fork lift was in a side room, which was why The Indian and the other Village People had not spotted it yet. It had just been luck that Johnny Heart had seen it. He could do with some luck by now. He hauled himself into the seat, turned the keys in the ignition. How did you work the gears on this thing, was it like a car, the fork lift thrummed into life of a sort and started to move, now was not the time to be fussy or wimpy, he pressed what he assumed to be the accelerator to the floor. He jerked backwards sharply, of course some idiot would have left the fork lift in reverse gear in a PURPLE~! place like this, always puts the fear of god into new staff. He pulled hard at the steering wheel, uncertain of what he was trying to do. He could see The Indian and the Village People running towards him, one of them fired of a shot of PURPLE~!, but the others just ran towards him. That must be the accelerator that he was pressing, the fork lift was picking up speed, he began to try and remember what was behind him. The heavy base of the forklift hit the thin skin of the brick wall hard. It bulged like a dam about to break. Outside a little boy pointed out the bulge...in the wall you SICK FREAK!...to his mother. She picked him up and ran, the crowd outside started to scatter. The wall burst outwards, and the forklift jumped out into the evening air of the 'special' parade that was going on outside. Johnny Heart could taste the air in his mouth, and felt his cuts sting in the fresh air. He took his feet of the fork lift pedals and slipped sideways off the forklift. Someone snatched him off the pavement and pulled him back. Occasional bricks were falling like autumn leaves. The crowd formed a hemisphere of space round the punctured brick wall. Inside Johnny Heart could vaguely see The Indian and the others vanishing. Johnny Heart picked himself up, the crowd was packed tight, beyond them a parade and floats, for once seeing only the back of the crowd. He could hear police sirens. Now was the time to get out of here. He was hemmed in tight in the crowd. He managed a few hundred yards through the crowd, and saw a familiar face in the distance...it was The Mystery Man!!! Things were beginning to intertwine now, and we are all seeing how the PINK! and PURPLE~! are finally relating. Until now, Johnny had not met up with the Mystery Man in quite sometime. It had always been Jack having the secret meetings and Johnny sticking strong to the PINK! The Mystery Man looked up to see an old friend who had very obviously fallen upon hard times. Johnny Heart looked like a man who had lost more fights than is physically possible. Johnny Heart got the waitress to order him a taxi, and with PINK! lemonade in hand hauled himself over to sit opposite The Mystery Man. They knew each other from way back, they’d both walked out of the same talk at a PINK!/PURPLE~! conference years ago, and bumped into each other every so often. Neither was exactly hard to miss. The Mystery Man proffered the bag of crisps. Johnny Heart shook his head and slumped back, his head rolling back lifelessly. His breath wheezed noisily. The Mystery Man went back to eating his saltines. About half an hour later the taxi had still not appeared, the traffic was virtually impossible, the parade was still in progress, with men jumping about as fairies in the middle of the road. Johnny Heart had recovered slightly and The Mystery Man was listening to his tale of woe, though it did lapse in and out of audibility and comprehensibility. The Mystery Man said little. He just let Johnny Heart talk. Johnny Heart: “I’m dead meat, what with The Indian and the betrayed Rainbow Squad member Smith after me, there is nowhere to turn, between them, that pair can do practically anything they like. They must have this town stitched up between them. If I go to the police I’ll get gardening leave till they decide what to do, in that time Smith will have me thrown out. They can’t afford to leave me alive, even if no one believes me, no one will, I know and they can’t leave me alive. That whole thing was a set up, the gang got wiped out, and The Indian had a pop at me too as part of the deal. Now Smith has got everything, the only fly in his ointment is me, and he’s not going to let that last for long. Maybe it would have been better if they’d just finished me off. Getting out hasn’t exactly done me any good.” Johnny Heart’s eyes were glassy and out of focus. He seemed to be drifting further and further away. Maybe it was because of the men in the parade, but most likely it was because of the trouble he was in. Mystery Man: “I’ll tell you what, if you can stay alive for forty-eight hours, which shouldn’t be difficult what would it take to get you out of this?” The Mystery Man was curious, these sort of situations were an everyday occurrence to him. It seemed he was sort of a median, or a double-agent for both PINK! and PURPLE~!, but which side was true to his heart? Johnny Heart was too exhausted to doubt the plausibility of what he was hearing. Johnny Heart: “If Smith were gone, that would give me the breathing space I need. I could tackle Village People and the PURPLE~! through channels after that, it would take half of forever but it could be done. With Smith still around I’m just on borrowed time.” Mystery Man: Well that’s not too difficult...” Johnny Heart: “You don’t know who Smith is and what he can do. He was trained by the best in the Rainbow Squad.” Johnny Heart could see that the whole thing was impossible. Mystery Man: “You don’t know who I am and what I can do.” To The Mystery Man nothing was impossible... What in the hell was going on? Now Johnny Heart was in talks with the Mystery Man? And where was Jack Valentine? With Cowabunga Connection only a few days away, the war between the PINK! and the PURPLE~! was getting rather intense to feel safe in the ring in a PCW Tag Team title match. And with what Politically Incorrect did last week on Turmoil, The Heartbreakers were just a bulls eye on the dartboard of DOOM~! Which side was the Mystery Man on? We will find out in episodes to come of the War: PINK! vs. PURPLE~!
