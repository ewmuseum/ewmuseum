State Troopers...Gotta Love 'Em...Travels, part 4 of ?

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-26-2002 (The scene opens to show our hero, Venneman Pendragon, trotting his war horse Xerxes down the side of a lonesome highway. Today he wears a flowing white shirt and the normal black boots with breeches. Excalibur is strapped to his left hip and the PCW Rising Star title is slung over his right shoulder. Following close on his heels is his Minstrel, Edward, riding a spotted donkey. While Pendragon looks to be lost in concentration, or merely just bored from the travel, Edward cannot help but kick up a lively tune with a combination of his voice and his instrument of choice, the lyre...) Edward: And closer now than yesterday, the Lord was on his merry way....He has a date with destiny, by order of the Company...He rides on through the day and night, anxious for his chance to fight...With plunder never on his mind, there is no other of his kind...LORD PENDRAGON!! He is... (While Edward sings, a Pendragon voiceover starts and drowns out the happy words...) 'Travelling was never something I particularly enjoy, and I have to say that for the most part, this publicity trip for PCW has been no different. First, in New York, the commoners looked at me as if I were crazy when I asked for them to support their sovereign. Hell, they already neglect the paying of their taxes to their King, the least they could give is some respect...And then, Zed and Zeke of Booneville fame. Seems those two wanted to get a 'piece' of royalty...I did hate to leave them breathing; it is most unpleasant sometimes to have to operate within the confines of law. The only high point thus far was being able to put a 'Champion: of the lance to shame. And now I ride into Alabama. I wonder what else could possibly go wrong...' (As if in answer to his question, an Alabama State Patrol car pulls up behind the horse and the donkey and turns on its flashers. Pendragon reins up his horse and Edward stops with his singing. Xerxes takes two steps sideways to get on the shoulder, as is proper protocol when you are a horse and you get pulled over. After a momentary pause, the trooper steps from his car and puts on his Smokey the Bear style hat. He saunters up to Pendragon, who is still seating on his horse. He starts talking in that 'I'm a Statey and I'm better than you' tone that they teach in asshole 101, and it is amplified because of his Alabama twang...) Trooper: Ahh'll need yore lascense and registration, sir.... (Venneman's eyes get wide as the trooper asks this question. He looks at Edward who just shrugs...) Venneman: My apologies officer...I seem to have left my wallet elsewhere. I didn't realize you needed a license and registration to ride a horse in Alabama. (The trooper tilts his shiny sunglass-wearing head towards Pendragon...) Trooper: You makin light a me boy? I don't take kindly to you Yankee tourists comin down har and tellin me how to do my job. (Pendragon's eyes flare for a moment, but he holds down the insane rage that HAS to be boiling up inside. He replies calmly...) Venneman: I am no tourist, nor a Yankee. I am on a promotional tour for my company, PCW, and for myself, Lord King Venneman Pendragon. It is time people leaned how to display the proper respect for their betters... (The trooper laughs out loud before quickly composing himself. He takes off his sunglasses and peers up at Pendragon through squinted eyes...) Trooper: King, eh? Holee hell...Here I thougt I was just stoppin some crazy lookin guy on a horse, and it turns out yore a king! Wonders never cease...I get it now...Why would you need a lahcense? I should know who you are already, seein as how yore my king and all, huh? (Pendragon looks at the trooper, ill pleasure writ large upon his face...) Venneman: Exactly. Now, officer...If you have nothing else, I must be on my way. After all, I have broken no law. Trooper: I'll be watchin you buddy...You screw up once and I am gonna ship you off to the looney bin you escaped from...You hear that? (Pendragon pays no heed as he kicks Xerxes into motion. He pauses a moment in front of the cop as Xerxes releases a huge pile of, well, crap, that lands directly on the trooper's shoe...) Venneman: You have a good day, officer... Trooper: Well gawd damn! (Pendragon chuckles softly as he keeps on riding his horse down the highway. After a little while, Edward cannot resist and he starts to sing again...) Edward: My lord he showed the cop, why he's the wrestler at the top...And Xerxes knew just what to do, when he shat upon the shoe!!!! Lord PENDRAGON!!! HE IS.... (Once more a voiceover cuts him off...) 'People show no respect to their betters...That is one thing that truly saddens me about the world today. I wish there was someone who was my better, just so I could show people the way of proper respect. Unfortunately, I am alone at the top. And Friday, I must teach a man named Syko respect...Goblins and Knights...Natural enemies. One could make the argument that it is my inborn duty to rid the world of the creature. I say that it is my duty to show the fools who would stand against me at Wrestle Vessel exactly what I am capable of. To completely destroy an accomplished opponent will certainly raise my stock in the minds of all of my competitors, as well as the incompetents who will fall on Sunday.' (Pendragon stops his horse and reaches into his pocket. He pulls out a brand new map, evidently not wanting to end up lost again, and scans it. He nods and places the map back into his pocket...) Venneman: Edward...Just a few miles to go until Mobile and the TV station. We oughtta make it right on time... Edward: Lord PENDRAGON!!! HE IS LORD PENDRAAAAAAAGOOOOOON!!! 'Poor Edward...He does mean well. And I have to deal with another Podunk TV station. These southern rednecks really like their wrestling. I guess everyone has to have something to keep them going. But what if your name is Trigger? What keeps you going then? You lose the title that defined you to a far superior being, and then you cry about the past. Trigger...Do you REALLY think that anyone ever looked upon you as a warrior? Because all I have seen is a whelp. A foolish man who justifies his mediocrity by displaying a lack of will power. Why do I say that? Because WITH will power, you could be whoever you want...The 'Voices' would not matter. As it stands, face the facts; I dropped you with The Fall of a Legend and did not break a sweat. Pity you rolled from the ring, else you would have found out what true pain is...unfortunately, that honor was left to Scorpion. Yeah, I remember him from my CCW days. He felt The Last Crusade AND The Fall of a Legend, and he also took the pinfall that gave me MY title. And now he gives us a revelation....THIS MAN HAS NO WILL POWER EITHER!! He is a gambling addict. I guess you would have to be to get back in the ring with me after BOTH of my finishers, so I cannot lay blame. You keep doing what makes you happy, boyo. I just hope it doesn't take food from your family's mouth. Thunder? All I have to say is that your run of bad luck is not over yet. Just wait; if you thought the muggers' BAT was rough on you...Wait till you get a load of me...The only other fellow who deems mention is Syko, and that is only if he is able to walk after our match on Fusion. But, if I am unable to rid the world of him there, fate has seen fit to give me another chance on the Wrestle Vessel...Some people say he is there for star power; I say he is there to take a beating at my hands. I am not arrogant, that is just the way it is...To all involved, Prepare for the pain...' (Venneman draws his sword and lets out a holler as he boots Xerxes into a sprint and dust rises behind the galloping horse. He flies by a gas station where two old men sit idly out front, watchin the procession...) Man 1: What in the hell was that, Earl? Man 2: I don't know...Kinda looked like somethin out of a history book...Was it a knight? My memory ain't so good anymore...But that was an awful big belt...Maybe he is here for the rodeo! Man1: Damnit Earl...I don't care what he looked like. I just wonder if he has a lahcence(license) to ride that horse... Man 2: Maybe we should call trooper Bob... Man 1: Maybe so, Earl...but I ain't a'gettin up... (A license to ride...Only in Alabama...) >>>>>TO BE CONTINUED
