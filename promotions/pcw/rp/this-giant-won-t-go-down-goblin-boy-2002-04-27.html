This Giant Won't Go Down, Goblin Boy.

27 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/27/2002 “Syko? Are you fucking kidding me? I got a match against Tiny Fucking Tim! Bah.” ‘Chris..... calm down.’ “Stacey.... you want me to calm down? Please, give me a reason to calm down.” *The scene of our promo opens in a very familiar location. The sun is shining, the weather is beautiful, and the Orlando Magic just lost a tough playoff game against the Charlotte Homeless [hopes someone gets the joke]. Of course, we are outside the TD Waterhouse Center in Orlando Florida. Formerly, The O-Rena, a place where Boda himself spent 4 years wrecking the boards, and jammin the ball down the throats of his opponents. But, on this sunny Saturday afternoon, Boda flows through the exiting crowd of disappointed fans, making his way towards his Lincoln Blackwood, he recently purchased. Of course, the random fan stops him, and asks for an autopgraph, which the Big Bad Boda Daddy is more than happy to fufill. Once able to continue, Boda and his lovely wife head for the parking garage across from the TD Waterhouse. Stacey looks at her much larger husband, smiling lovingly at her love of her life. Chris looks at her, and tries to smile, but he is obviously giving a lot of thought to the upcoming PPV. This of course becomes the center of conversation.* Stacey: ‘Come on.... all I asked was how this week looks for you, and you blew up at me.’ Boda: “Stacey..... sorry, I just got a lot on my mind. Hell, you realize what I’ve gone through alot as of late? Lost the title last PPV. Found out I have a long lost sister, and now, I may be wrestling in my last PPV in PCW, ever. I gave PCW all I can give it, and I might be walking into my last match come next Sunday. So, yes hun, excuse me if I’m a little uptight as of late. Especially since they book me all damn week. First, against the little munchkin reject. Then in a big 8 man tag team match, and against Kano and Miya, in a triple threat match. Come on..... I got things to do, besides waste my time in little matches that mean JACKSHIT to my PPV match.” Stacey: ‘Come on Chris.... relax. It’s not like you’re going to lose to Miya. I mean, you’ve beaten him evertime. You’re bigger and better than he is. It’s his time to go, not yours. Besides, these matches are just warmups for you.’ *This gives Boda a quick laugh, as he opens the doors to the Lincoln Blackwood.* Boda: “A warm-up? Come on girl. First off.... it’s SYKO. I mean, last time I faced him... he got what? 4 offensive moves in on me. Come on.... he’s like walking the dog... I won’t break a sweat. Hell, I willing to bet you that diamond necklace you wanted, that this match ends in less than 4 minutes. I’ll give the little bastard some credit... he has heart. But, unfortunetly, Stacey, for him.... I’ll pop his little heart.” *Boda and his wife climb into the 2002 Lincoln Blackwood, and Boda starts up the vehicle, as a all to familiar song is playing on O-Rock 105.9.* Stacey: ‘Aint this Miya’s song?’ *Indeed the Linkin Park song, ‘One Step Closer’, is playing. Boda changes the station, to a pop rock station.* Stacey: ‘Why you do that? I liked that song.’ Boda: “Because, I’m home, from the road. I don’t want to be reminded today about Miyagawa.Come on, it’s bad enough we’ll have to go to family reunions, and see him now. But, I don’t want to listen to his music, and get unfocused on the ass whooping I am going to throw at him on Sunday next week. I don’t care that he’s my brother in law, PPV time... it’s gonna feel real good Stacey.... to send him out on his back, out of PCW.” *Boda puts the Blackwood in reverse, backing out of the parking spot, and merging into the traffic exiting the parking garage. The former World Champ drives out onto Amelia St, and heads for I-4. It’s about a 45 minute drive home from the TD Waterhouse Center. Of course, that’s after fighting all the traffic leaving the game. So, as Boda sits behind the wheel, watching the traffic in front of him, Stacey leans the seat back, wanting to take a little afternoon nap.* Stacey: ‘Remember to get the girls on the way home. Stop at my mom’s house, and grab them. I’m taking a nap. Ok?’ Boda: “Sure, no problem.” *Stacey closes her blue eyes, and Boda is left to drive. But, being the man he is, his mind starts to think about the upcoming week. His match tomorrow, the PPV.* Well, looks like I’m trying to be ran out of PCW. I mean, I’ve got a loser leaves PCW match at the PPV, and all week, I get booked into grueling matches. Syko tomorrow night. Tuesday, the 8 man tag match and of course, Friday, me, Miya and Kano. *Boda explodes in laughter. Stacey doesn’t even budge* Come on..... who the fuck books these matches? Boda against Syko. Come on.... I really feel sorry for the kid. I mean....... he’s David, I’m Goliath, only this time, just like last time, the big man will come out on top. I give the kid credit... he doesn’t show fear. At least last time we met, he didn’t. Until I lifted him high into the air, and just as I brought him slaming to the mat, the look in his eyes was that of fear, death and pants pissing. I understood though. If I was wrestling someone that outweighed me by 200 pounds, and stood almost 2 feet taller, then yes, I’d be scared, and I’d probably shit myself to. Speaking of shitting themselves when they face the monster.... an 8 person tag match on Turmoil. Come on allready..... it’s not fair. I mean, any team with me on it is bound to win. So,it’s not fair to the team of Miyagawa, Jason Core, Rent a Zero and El Dukey. Hell, even if my partners got the flu, and stayed home, I could handle it. After all, it’s only Core, Miya, Hero and Diablo I’d be facing. Not the cream of the crop. Oh well, warm up match at it’s best. Break a sweat? Maybe. Break some one’s little neck.... highly likley. Then.... my so-called best friend gets to get his ass whipped, again, by me on Fusion. Only this time.... Miya gets a bit of pre-PPV action in this one too. GOOD. Maybe Miya will learn what he’s going to get at the PPV, and stay his little punk ass home, hiding, as usual, behind my sister. *Boda switches lanes, as the exit he needs to get off is quickly coming* Speaking of Miya.......it’s almost time to pit the two greatest stars of PCW against one another. Sure, Snow is a 3 time champion, but his last two title reigns, were simply a mockery of the past greats. Miya, as much as I despise him, is a true warrior, a complete competitor. He is the biggest threat to my career right now, but not for long. No way, no how, am I going to lose my career to him. Not this time. His little bit of Therapy, not going to matter. Becuase as I sinch in WHO’S YOUR DADDY, and he’s forced to tap, his remaining days of life will be spent elsewheres, not in PCW. I am PCW. People associate PCW with Boda. And come next Sunday.... Miya will be on the outside looking in. *Boda slows the Lincoln Blackwood down, as he gets ready to turn into the million dollar home he bought for his in-laws last year. There, playing on the front lawn, are his two daughters. They wave and smile as the Blackwood comes to a stop. Boda gets out, and grabs his girls, loading them into the truck. He waves bye to his mother in law, then heads off down the road for home.* Syko, sorry about your luck boy. I’ll try not to hurt you this time. But, you know... it ain’t personal, it’s business. Keep dreaming about topping the giant, little boy..... because tomorrow night, your dream will become a fucking nightmare. *And so, the promo ends. The Black Lincoln Blackwood creeps down the road, towards the Sloboda family home. Tomorrow morning, Boda flies out to Columbus Ohio, to wreck the dream of the Goblin king. Let’s hope Miya tries something, so Boda can get a week early beating in on the man he has grown to not only dispise, but respect at the same time.*
