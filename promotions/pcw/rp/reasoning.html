Date: not entered International Incident, one man down and never to return again. That was the major occurrence of Sunday Sabotage this past week, and the man that exited International Incident was none other than El Diablo. The Mexican Devil has claimed never to return to International Incident ever again. What possibly went wrong within the group that forced this loyal Mexican out? El Diablo, now no longer has to put up with the pressures of living up to International Incident's standards and finally live up to his own standards that he wants. It would appear that the whole of what happened on Sabotage was El Diablo's own concoction and a very successful one it would seem. 'Angel,' shouts El Diablo. Her footsteps are heard as she walks into the room that El Diablo is seated in. 'What is it?' Angel asks with a look of annoyance on her face. El Diablo looks up at his wife. 'Sabotage went down well,' he tells his wife. Angel smiles a little. 'Well, some of it,' she nervously responds. Diablo stops looking at her a moment and just looks down at this shirt he has on. 'You see this?' he tells Angel while pointing at his shirt Angel looks at El Diablo's shirt. 'See what?' she replies. El Diablo quits pointing and looks back up at his beautiful wife. 'I'm free from I2, I'm no longer tied down by anything or anybody. I'm back to being myself,' states El Diablo eagerly. Angel smiles a little. 'It's great you feel like this, but Sabotage still didn't going down perfect,' Angel states clearly. El Diablo has a look of seriousness on his face. 'Sure it wasn't improbable, but lets just forget about Akiyama and the attack. Just look at the positives of that night,' El Diablo tells Angel in glee. Angel sits down on a stool near the seat that her husband is seated on. Angel is wearing a red and black tube top, and a black leather mini skirt as well as her leather boots. Her dyed black hair is loose and she has her black and white cosmetics on. 'And what positives would they be?' asks Angel in an obvious doubt. El Diablo looks at her as he adjusts the leather mask he wears. He is also wearing some black sweatpants and a black short sleeve shirt. His dark brown hair runs freely from the top of his mask. 'How I left that chump Aymin alone in the ring, how I voiced my opinions on the politics of International Incident publicly and my personal favorite setting aflame the shirt with the I2 logo on, it burned the very core of I2 with that one moment,' El Diablo joyfully recalls. Angel smiles across at El Diablo. 'You're right, I suppose it was a successful night and maybe you'll be able to bring that success into Turmoil,' states Angel to her husband. Diablo smiles with a sinister thought. 'I defeated Anton The Kid last Fusion so I see no reason why I cannot beat him again on Turmoil,' he says confidently. He was indeed successful on Fusion against him, but only by disqualification when a vengeful Neo Void God attacked the Mexican Devil in cold blood. But in the end, it did guarantee El Diablo a spot in the Main Event for March Madness, so it did work out for El Diablo, just like leaving International Incident will work out for him, he hopes. The only worry the Mexican Devil has to deal with now is when he will be drawn into this event, an early number could destroy his hopes, however a late number could really give him an opportunity for success at March Madness. However, El Diablo knows that he cannot look past Turmoil and will make every effort to be victorious once more, in what has been a rare successful week for him. 'Turmoil won't be easy, as I'll have to look over my shoulder constantly for any revenge, but otherwise I can defeat Anton again, I know that,' he tells Angel. Angel smiles at her husbands' confidence and intelligence. 'Are you scared of the backlash from them?' she asks him. El Diablo laughs loudly, which seems to disturb Angel a little. 'I'm not scared of anything from them. If they want revenge, I'll take it like a man. I will not conform to any feelings that they would want me to feel. I'm just not like that,' he reassures her. At March Madness, El Diablo will be making only his second Main Event appearance, and ironically both his Main Event outings will have been for the PCW Heavyweight Title. His prior shot was in a four-way dance, back when Ivan Stanislav was at the helm of PCW. The uniqueness of March Madness is that 20 men from many different backgrounds in PCW get a shot at the grandest and greatest prize of all and even the lowest of people in this match have a realistic shot at being victorious due to the draw, albeit a long shot. But El Diablo relishes the match and just wants a decent outing like he usually does at pay-per views, despite his absence from the card on the last two pay-per view events, Decimation and Shang-haied 2. 'And what about March?' asks Angel to El Diablo. 'March, I prove myself,' he replied steadily. El Diablo stands up. 'I am glad I left that political mess International Incident, it was about time I voiced my real feelings and acted upon my gut feeling. Aymin was the worst of them all, a beer guzzling fool. Klaymore? Where the hell was he ever during I2? Juan was an ass that would just look out for himself, and Chef couldn't care less about I2,' states El Diablo about International Incident. Angel stands up also and stands by her husband. 'But, that's all in the past now, Angel, I have a future to look forward to, unlike the joke of International Incident. And my future starts on Turmoil in defeating Anton The Kid once again, and then onto March Madness where I shall prove myself again,' he rants to his wife. They both walk out and exit the room in which they were in. El Diablo is just looking forward to his future, but what does his future hold? Success or failure from now on? And will he be able to survive any form of backlash from International Incident? Only time will answer those questions.
