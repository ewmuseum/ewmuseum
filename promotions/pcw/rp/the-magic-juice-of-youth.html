The Magic Juice of Youth

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/8/04 The Rite Aid pharmacy stood before the Abyssmal Warrior. He reached up and adjusted the trucker's cap on his head, to cover his bald head. For the past week, anytime he ran into wrestling fans he'd been greeted with laughter, and jeering. He was here at the Rite Aid to put an end to that. He was here for something that would restore the power he had lost when he lost his hair. Much like Samson he needed his hair to be a true warrior, and without it he was nothing. The brutal beating he received from Buzz the previous week had proven it. He walks towards the white building, and as he moved towards the door, a pretty woman stepped out. She looked at him, causing him to grab at his covered bald head out of embarassment. Nothing was said, and she continued to walk by the once great warrior. He opened the door, and emerges into the building. As he walks in, he can feel the eyes of a thousand non-existant stares. The truth is no one gives two shits about him, as the majority of your average joes aren't wrestling fans. He moves towards the nearest aisle with his hand, non chalantly, covering the side of his face.The Abyssmal Warrior(Thinking): The Warrior must be quick to get what he has traveled a great distance for. The fountain of youth, if you will.The Abyssmal Warrior moved through the aisles looking at the items for sale as he passed. Ky Warming Liquid, Q-Tips, Snickers, but no magic youth juice. He began to panic, as he reached the last aisle. His worst fear was confirmed as he reached the end of the aisle. It was not in sight, he would have to ask for house.The Abyssmal Warrior(Mumbling): The forces of evil hath struck again. They are trying to keep me away from my power.The Warrior shuffled out of the aisle, knocking over items along the way with his overly broad shoulders from years of 'juice' abuse and iron pumping. He moved towards the check out, and once there the young man behind the counter looked up at the Warrior in awe.Clerk: May I help you, sir?The Abyssmal(Quietly): Yes, I'm having trouble finding the great juice of youth....Clerk: Excuse me, sir....The Abyssmal Warrior: You know..the juice. It makes you feel and look younger....The young man looks the Warrior up and down, and is afraid to say what he is about to say to the massive man.Clerk: Sir, Rite Aid does not supply steroids....The Abyssmal Warrior(Indignantly): NOT STEROIDS I HAVE PLE......No, I meant.....The Abyssmal Warior leans in close and the young man jumps back in fear. The Warrior lets out a hearty laugh before motioning that it is ok for the man to move in closer.The Abyssmal Warior: The magic juice known as Rogaine..He points at the bald head that was covered with face paint and a hat. The young man begins to chuckle before reaching down and pulling out a box of Rogaine. The Warrior moves to the left to keep the box out of view of anyone in the almost empty store. He nervously looks around like a crackhead before leaning in once again.The Abyssmal Warrior: I'm going to need about 10 boxes....Clerk: TEN BOXES OF ROGAINE?! YOU'LL KILL YOUR FOLIC..*Interrupted*The Abyssmal Warrior grabs hold of the young man, and pulls out a picture of himself from his peak. He drops hold of the man's shirt, and places the picture back in his pocket.The Abyssmal Warrior: The planets, stars, and moons must align by Saturday...for me to get my hair back...The last scene we see is that of The Abyssmal Warrior carrying a bag stacked with Rogaine, stealthily out of Rite Aid as the sun sets in the distance. If the planets, stars, and moons do indeed align, and if The Warrior does no poison himself with Rogaine..it will be a tough night for Boda....................................................................................................................................No, no it won't...
