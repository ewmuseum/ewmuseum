Date: Today? Pretty Cool ChroniclesChapter #3: Winning our… re-DEBUT~! It was an outcome that I don’t think many people would have come to expect, especially with regards to two pretty big jokes like Ryan Moore and myself. We’d been telling everyone that we were going to win… that, we were going to storm through everything like they were nothing, but… deep down inside, we didn’t quite feel the same way that our moves moved.I’d told Ryan that even the best teams in the World will lose sometimes, that, even teams that were as dominant as the ‘P.W.A’ would sometimes experience that little thing that is loss. We’d spent a few days gearing up toward that fated outcome… and yet, what happened wasn’t what we would have expected. A victory… and one that was pretty dominant at that? How could we have been able to gain such a victory against teams that were scarier than the depths of Hell and the Candyman combined? It seemed like somebody had planned for us to win, like somebody had… almost pushed us toward the finishing line with the expectation of taking us down if our confidence was high the next week… E2? Could they have been the collaborative duo that went out to make sure that the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’ would be running into Tuesday Turmoil with their confidence blinding their actual skill and talent? I don’t know for sure… but its not something that I’m going to ignore too easily… -- Production Start Production Date: 26/04/03Current Location: Everyone’s favourite ‘Death Bar’… Ronald McDonalds~! “You know, I never realized that PCW would have been so kind as to fund our very own party after winning our match against two really, really tough teams last week…” I said, before making a chewing noise, that being the sound of a double cheeseburger being munched into… and then, chewed into absolute nothingness. “Yeah, I mean… I knew that our opponents were going to be like, so hard, but the way we just managed to run through them, it almost felt like we were fighting Russell Sanileg and Jayson Frost all over again.” Ryan managed to spew out, pushing a chewed up piece of burger to the side of his mouth so he could say everything without too much getting in his way. The scene had slowly begun to open in the confinements of a McDonalds restaurant, one of the favourite places for the newly rehired Tag Team of Ryan Moore and I. In all honesty, we hadn’t expected PCW to pay for our buffet… and, they didn’t pay for any of our food at all. Instead, we just got one of their little executive producer people to come along and make sure that all the “Happy Meals” and standard orders were on him.Both Ryan and myself had decided to wear something a little more interesting than the usual pop gear, opting for baggy jeans and official PCG Hockey Jerseys. I was wearing the one that’d be used when we’re performing on Turmoil… Moore decided to wear the one that’d be used when we’re performing on Fusion. After taking another bite out of my delicious snack, I turned my head to look at outside the side window of the complex. “… You know, I’m always wondering why we’re the apparent icon of millions of teenage girls across America… and the World. Its almost like we’re supposed to be role models for these people… and yet, I’m not even a Girl or anything…” I said, looking somewhat confused for a few moments before taking another bite out of the delicious McDonalds prepared food. Ryan turned to look out of the window afterwards and have gave the female entourage a little wave… sending three of the Girls at the front to faint and fall heavily down.. “… Rich and Ryan, when do you think you’re going to be finished with all this food? I know you said that you wanted me to foot the bill for your celebrations and everything, but… don’t you think this is going a little too far?” the executive asked, looking down at the table with a little worrying hovering within his beady little eyes. It was like he didn’t really want us to spend all his money or something on all this food. “Dude, you’ve got to recognize the sheer importance of this celebration… and you know, we’re also getting another one of our celebrity friends to drop by. Who knows, you might even be able to get an autograph from them or something!” I said excitedly, looking toward the doors of the restaurant to see of the guest of honor had made their arrival. Sighing, I turned back around and looked to Ryan, “Hey, what time did you tell William to come and meet us here? Please say that you mentioned PM instead of AM.” Ryan looked stopped eating and pulled back the sleeve of his jersey to reveal one more of the many official PCG items that’d come back on sale following the duo’s rehiring. “… Hmm, well, at the moment my watch reckons its about 2:30pm and I’m pretty sure that I told William Smith to come around at something like… 2:30pm. I guess he’s just going to be running a little late or something?”I nodded my head somewhat in response and got back to finishing off the rest of my food, which didn’t actually take that long at all. Upon finishing, I shrugged and looked up to the executive, who could only return my sudden gaze with a brief but, forced smile. “Is there anything else that you’d like to have?” he asked, sounding very nervous and worried about what kind of foods I’d be looking to order next. My lower lip cupped over my upper lip and my eyes rolled upward, looking toward the ceiling, a look of immense thought beginning to cover my face. I now had to come up with the next ordering selection of food that I was going to have before ‘Big Willy’ came along and started up the party for real. “Uh… actually, I think I’ll hold off ordering anything else until William comes along…” I began to say, my voice slowly beginning to fade out toward the end of that sentence. I was beginning to lose hope in the appearance of our Rap/Actor friend from the “Fresh Prince of Bel Air” among other things. Ryan could sense the lack of support I was having for the arrival of the greatest Rap artist in the whole entire world. He leaned over the table and put a reassuring hand on my right shoulder before saying, “… Its all good, Rich. I’m sure that Will’s just held up in traffic or something… you know how it is with these big shot celebrities. Sometimes, they just don’t have the time to give back to the ‘Celebrity’ friends that they know.” I shrugged my shoulders and nodded a little, pretending to understand everything that Ryan had said with regards to an excuse for Willy. The PCW Executive looked around his immediate area for a few more moments, seemingly happy that I’d held off my order… but, I soon changed all of that happiness when I grabbed his attention and started to demand more food to compensate for this annoying upset.“… Actually, I think I’ll have another two chicken nugget ‘Happy Meals’… and make sure the toys aren’t the same, because like, I really don’t like it when I’m forced to endure the same toys.” I said, not even turning my head to present my order to the little man. To respond to such a thing, he just nodded his head and sighed loudly before turning around and joining the shortest possible queue. I looked up from the table and stared across at Ryan, waiting for him to finish eating some more of his food before saying what I wanted to say. “You know Ryan, I didn’t think this is the way that our victory celebration would have turned out, ya know? I mean, its like we didn’t actually win in the end… its like, we were just given a victory and then this crappy party to make us realize that winning isn’t everything in the world…” I said, sounding somewhat like a realist about everything, without even realizing it. Ryan sighed and dropped one of his fries back into the box, looking up toward me with a coy expression resting on his face. “Well, personally, I think we should just be happy that we even have something to celebrate… even if the party with Will Smith isn’t actually going the same way as we would have really liked it to go. I mean, we managed to get this awesome victory against two teams… and now we’re going to be facing a couple of newbies with the number one contendership of the Tag Team Championships on the line…” Ryan began to say, but I felt the need to cut him off as he mentioned the Tag Team Titles. For those of you that don’t quite understand the terrible plight of PCG the last time they were employed by PCW… it was those dastardly Tag Team Championships that proved to be the final nail in their unfortunate coffin. “Ryan… don’t you remember what kind of pain and suffering we had to endure the last time we wanted to hold onto those Tag Team Championships? Don’t you remember everything that happened with those two Championships… and how they, stopped us from performing at our very best?” I said, sounding like one of those Oscar actors that is trying to sound out the danger of the quest that a mystical hero would have to float upon. “I know and understand the consequences of old… but this is almost a year later and a lot of things have changed. I mean, for one… we’re fighting newbies to get a shot at the two guys that just happen to be holding the Tag Team titles at the moment. One of them… Gary Gray, just happens to enjoy making people laugh with his comedy act…” Ryan began, myself being the one to cut him off and explain the situation a little more. “Ryan, I don’t mean to sound like some kind of moron or anything… but the guy is called, “Gaz Green” and I don’t think he won all those Tag Team championships by just, running around like some kind of clown… making people laugh or anything… and then, there’s that Vampire dude that still runs around from place to place and stuff, making people die or something.” Ryan Moore seemed to enjoy mocking people… and a lot of the time, I also enjoyed walking around insulting people who happened to be less unfortunate than ourselves, however… he didn’t quite get the point of everything that’d been happening so far here in PCW. Unlike the last time, when we were awesomely better than every other team… in terms of both, wrestling talent and dancing talent, we didn’t have to contend with a scary looking Vampire and his little friend… or former friend as the case may be. With those two now factored into today’s equation and the hate that all the regular people seem to have for the music that we like to create, I don’t think anything is really going to help our own personal cause. “Ok, Ok, Ok… so, perhaps this isn’t going to be the easiest match that we’re ever going to face, but what about if we do that dance we did when back in Raccoon City? Or, what would happen if we were to copy Michael Jackson’s ‘Thriller’? Wouldn’t that make everything all better and the Vampire guy… erm, want to dance with us for a little bit?” Ryan suggested, thinking that everything that happened in the Raccoon City incident with the Zombies, would actually work with a smart Vampire. I thought about it myself for a few moments and wondered exactly what kind of results would happen in that scenario. “Hmm… you know, I’ve always wondered that myself and I think I have something of an idea that would even help us figure out if the Thriller dance would help us to gain control of a Vampire… but, what about this contest with those new people? Should we really be thinking that we’re going to walk through them?” I asked in a curious tone, actually feeling a little worried about everything now. “… Now that I’m stopping to think about how bad and ugly they could really be, I’m not sure if they’re a couple of people that we’re going to be able to handle alone… but, then again, because this is their ultimate debut within the walls of PCW… they could suddenly become starstruck or something and bow down to our own personal greatness?” he suggested, bringing even more thoughts to my mind, but nothing that would really settle as a solid plan. I knew that this would be the first opportunity of our opponents are actually make a name for themselves in Primetime Championship Wrestling, but well… I didn’t want to be the people that really made them, if you know what I mean… “Rich… Rich… I’m back with everything you wanted!” called the voice of the Executive Producer from the queue, the individual himself, stumbling over everything as he tried to get to PCG’s table without dropping the two happy meals on the floor. You’d actually wonder how anyone could come close to stumbling whilst holding nothing more than a single Happy Meal in each hand… but this was America and this was a story containing PCG. “Dude, are you totally sure that you didn’t get me the same toy… are you THAT sure that you didn’t get me the toy for girls either?” I asked, almost demanding that everything had I wanted had been attained and made certain. In all truth, I didn’t mind the whole deal with the same toys… since, being a fan of Star Wars, I liked to recreate little mock “Clone Wars” scenes… although, if I were to find a Barbie or something in my ‘Happy Meal’… despite that little plastic girls plush boobies, I really don’t think playing with them is an activity for a grown man like me. The executive nervously handed over the ‘Happy Meals’ placing them on the table infront of me like they were the lost treasure in that ‘Disney’ movie, “Treasure Planet”. I looked over their paper bag packaging with great interest before opening up the first bag, peering inside, desperate to break through to the first toy, whatever it may be. “Hmm… what do we have today?” I said quietly, pulling out a plastic bag, the little ‘Happy Meal’ flyer covering the body of the little toy-thing. I held it high and pulled open the bag from its top, reaching in before pulling out the surprise. “Hey, you’ve got a pretty decent Hot Wheels car there!” Ryan called from the otherside of the table, admiring the craftsmanship on this great little metallic device. I placed it down on the table and nodded with great approval. Victory was supposed to be sweet… and now, I was getting the sweetness of last weeks victory against Justice Inc and those Significant Others. I didn’t bother looking in the second bag this time, instead, I just reached in and pulled out of toy, once again unable to see it due to the flyer that coated the entire body of the thing. Ripping open this bag with even more joy than the last, I reached inside and felt around for the toy… my fingers rubbing across two… less than subtle bumps. “… Ergh, would you like me to change the toy for you?” the executive asked, sounding almost scared to death after realizing that I’d been unfortunate enough to get the one toy that I desperately made clear couldn’t be in one of the Happy Meals. I removed my hand from the bag and pulled out a rather lush looking plastic Barbie doll. Not to insult her too much by saying that she looked mingin’ or everything… but in all truth, she was a little too small and lacking of pulse for my liking. “You know, I made the smallest of requests about the toy that I would have liked to avoid having my meal… just one small request about the one toy, that somebody of my stature, didn’t need to have in their meal! Outside, I have all the girls that I need in a fanbase… why do you think I’m going to need a plastic doll to replace all of them?” I shouted, starting to throw one of those hissy fits that us major league pop stars have been more than known to throw in times of annoyance. “Yo Yo… G’s over there, what’s kickin’ homies?!” yelled a voice from the other side of the complex. I turned away from the executive and looked over to see everyone’s favourite homie, Will Smith! He stood there dressed in a sharp black suit, white shirt and stuff. Ryan looked over too and started screaming any excited fan would when they saw their own personal icon. I probably would have screamed too, but well… my own personal icon would be everyone’s favourite playa, Karl LeBlanc~! Anyway, moving away from that little piece of the story, Will slowly made his way over to our table, lowering his black shades before pulling up a seat, pushing the executive out of the way to sit down. He slumped forward and started shuffling through our food for something to eat, picking up one of my happy meals and pulling out the bag of chicken nuggets. “Sorry about bein’ all late on y’all… I was held up by those hotties outside… D’amn, I ain’t even seen those before, ya know what I’m sayin’?” he said, chuckling out aloud, Ryan and myself doing the same… even though all of those people had been standing there in accordance to our arrival for the last few hours. “I heard you two managed to win your first match back in PCW… well, from the boys over at the Recording Studio, you know… I’d like to say, ‘Congrats’ on doing your thing. Its always good to see two members of the entire PoP industry… making those pansies, N’Sync look like they could have actually been respectable people.” He said, starting to fill his mouth with some of the chicken nuggets. “Hey Will,” Ryan began, looking a little curious before continuing, “… you know, we’ve been looking to add an extra element to our music. You know, like how the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’ were before in that other company with… whahisface… Steven Smith? Yeah, him… we were wondering if you’d be interest in coming along to be our backing singer or something?” Ryan looked kinda excited and honoured about offering Will Smith a spot within the ‘Pretty Cool Guys’, especially on a day that was a great as our victory celebration. I nodded my head approvingly, trying to get Will to see the light within the darkness of PoP prejudice and hate. I mean, the guy was black and everything… so, being that his thousands of brutha’s and sista’s had been discriminated against all those years ago, perhaps he could be our own personal Martin Luther King and preach the music that is PoPular? “You guys want Me to drop my whole… clean ghetto rap thing, to just… come on down with the two of y’all and get my ass shot trying to make out PoP as being… cool and hip and, actually good?” he responded in a queer tone, sounding very surprised that he’d been asked to do something of that nature. I mean, if I were in his position, I’d be overly excited… but, I guess things were going to go the same way they did when we offered a dude called Elton John the chance to be part of our heterosexual PoP musical duo. “Well, you don’t have to drop your entire Rap act and everything… but like, it’d be so cool for you to come on around and perhaps add a little to our remix’s and general ‘Drastic Improvements’ of music videos like, “Quit Playin’ Games” and O-Towns track, “Liquid Dreams”!” I said, continuing to try and swing Will Smith over to the good side with some more benefits of things that we could do to make our own personal profile that little higher across America. “… Ok, Ok, Ok… but like, what about this match that you’re going to be having with those E2 fella’s this week on PCW’s little Turmoil show? Do you think I’m going to have to get in the ring or something to help you two? I mean… I’m not a wrestler or anything and I sure as Hell can’t do any of those fly looking moves.” Will tried to say in his defence, not realizing that he was actually making himself look quite the wimp to us. “What’s so hard about doing a few moves for us? I mean, if you break your neck or something… those PCW Crewmen will be ready to help you along the way whilst we win the match!” I think that was actual turning point in Will’s decision about joining us at our Victory Party. I don’t think he actually minded the whole ‘Proving PoP is cool’ part, but when faced with the possibility of helping us to win against E2 this week on Turmoil… and then being told that if he broke his neck, we’d just sit back and continue with the contest in question… he really didn’t want anything more to do our movement to the stars. “You know, I think I’m just gonna take this Happy Meal… and maybe frisk up that little Barbie Toy you got on the table… and hey, if I decide that the ring is going to be the place for me to die, then baby… I’ll know who to kill, aight?” Will said, slowly getting up from his seat, flashing Ryan and myself these cheesy grins before waltzing off into what could only be described as a sunset, but in reality… the entrance to McDonalds. “How in the Hell… did we ever convince ourselves that Will Smith was actually cool?” Ryan cried out aloud as the scene slowly began to fade out into the confinements of darkness. Yet another day of nothingness had passed for the Pretty Cool Guys… although, all things aside… we’d been able to get ourselves a few free Happy Meals and stuff. -- Production End Well, what can I say at the end of that interesting little day? However many of you thought that William Smith might have had a few shreds of coolness and whatever left within him… how can I break the news of its falseness without breaking your tender little hearts? … Anyway, regardless of that little mishap, we still have our Tag Team contest against E2 this week… and then some, huge Cruiserweight Championship Main Event. Do we think we can honestly win the Tag Team match this time around… No Do we think that, by some twist or fluke… we’re going to walk away holding any form of Cruiserweight Championship? Probably not… but even the greatest of artists can dream, can’t they?
