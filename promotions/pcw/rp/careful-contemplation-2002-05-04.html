Careful Contemplation

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/4/02 [Fade In] [Darkness except for faint glow caused by the sliver of moon high above, and the tiny amount of illumination given off by the stoplights hanging over the street like tree branches. A few cars mumble by, but it’s late and most people are not out and about. A sign high above, flickering green reads ‘Holiday Inn’ and far below the sign leaning against the building that the sign belongs to is a figure.] [As the shot zooms in on the area in which the figure leans, we see the sidewalk scattered with various forms of trash and yellow newspaper print. The figure’s back is against the wall, and so is his left foot. Zoom closer, it’s none other than one of Primetime Wrestling’s newest additions, Ryan “Streak” Stephens. He’s wearing mesh shorts, and a white T-shirt. His hair appears to be wet, though it’s hard to tell in the poor lighting. It’s tossed about his head as though he’s just come from a shower.] [Alone in the dark with nothing but his thoughts, it is obvious Stephens is contemplating something, perhaps everything. The intensity of his eyes can be seen despite the lighting. His stern jawbone clenched tight gives way to his chin, which currently features a little stubble of hair; most likely reflecting his occupied state of thought and lack of time to worry about much else.] [Two matches this week. Two matches that may invariably decide whether or not Stephens will be able to compete with the top wrestling personalities in the world. Two matches that could ultimately set a tone for a solid career within the likes of PCW, or perhaps two matches that could leave Stephens soul searching for what went wrong.] [Doubt and fear are two things that never slipped into his mind three years ago when he broke into the wrestling world. He was poised and confident, never flailing or grasping for opportunities. They came to him, and he capitalized, always. But now, three years later, he has been through so much. He often finds himself alone, contemplating everything, out-thinking himself, worrying too much.] [The fire, the passion, the heart, and the determination. They all still live within him, but now he’s haunted by his inner demons, his doubts, and his fears. He must get on a roll, and he must gain the respect of the fans, and even his co-workers. He came to PCW for a new challenge, and it will be nothing less. It’s his time, but is he prepared?] [Stephens removes his foot from the wall, and let’s out a small groan as he stretches his arms high towards the heaven’s above to work the stiffness out. No telling how long, he’d been out here thinking. He takes a few steps to his left, and swings open a glass door, obviously that of the Holiday Inn.] [Fade Out] [Fade back in moments later] [Stephens has entered his hotel room.] [Stephens is sprawled out across the bed. His fingers are interlaced and placed behind his head so as to prop him up. A silhouette of his figure can be seen above the back of the bed and is caused because the only light in the room is a dim lamplight perched atop a nearby bedside table. We follow his eyes to that of an attractive female sitting Indian-style at the end of the bed. The female is his wife, Dana. She wears men’s boxer shorts and a large T-shirt, which hangs off her so much that her bra straps are visible through the sleeve holes. She is a petite woman who is short, and rather slender. Her blond hair is curly and pulled back into a bun. She speaks.] Dana: I’m glad you decided to come in baby. You’ve been out there almost two hours. [short pause, you can hear the concern in her voice.] I’m worried about you. [Stephens looks at his wife. He doesn’t want her to be worried, but he doesn’t know what to tell her. He’s scared. He’s unsure of himself.] Dana: You can’t worry so much honey. If you win respectably and you lose respectably, and you lay it all out there. If you give everything you have, these people are going to respect you, these fans are going to respect you, and you will conquer this here challenge that you seem to think is so important for your career. [Stephens’s eyebrows cave down towards his eyes. He’s been offended. This challenge, succeeding in PCW, it is important for his career. Slightly irritated he responds.] Streak: You sound like my high school baseball coach. [mocking her] Give everything you have. [And now she’s offended, but she’s trying, she loves him, she decides to be the better person this time. She sighs and speaks again.] Dana: I’m sorry baby, I just know that you’re capable of so much, and I have so much faith in you, that it hurts me to see you worrying about it so much. You’re acting as if this new stint in PCW is the most pivotal point in your whole career. I’ve seen you at the top, and I’ve seen you at the bottom, and you’re so relaxed at the top, you don’t over analyze ever little aspect. [She stops and crawls over next to him. She lays her head on his chest, and wraps her arm around him.] Streak: I didn’t mean to get flustered with you baby, you were just trying to help. It’s just that I don’t think you can comprehend how important these two matches I have this week are. They’re going to set the tone for my PCW career. I have to win these matches. [She adjusts her head so that her eyes look out over her forehead and into his deep blue eyes.] Dana: No baby, that’s where you’re wrong. You don’t have to do nothing of the sort. [He tries to interject, but she continues on.] You said it yourself when you decided to come here. These guys, and girls for that matter are the best, the absolute best in this sport. Well baby, you are too or you wouldn’t be here, but the fact is, if these wrestlers are truly the best as you say they are, then a solid, heart-filled performance will earn you respect, and THAT will set the tone for your PCW career. [She looks at him; he seems frustrated, putout, but not mad. He never gets mad at her.] Streak: I suppose you’re right baby. I just want this so bad, and I’m scared that maybe I don’t belong here. Dana: [her voice is stern, and seems strange coming from her petite little body] You do belong here, or they wouldn’t have signed you. Sometimes I wander why you thought you had to do this, but you did it baby, and I’m right behind you, like I’ve always been. [He hugs her tightly, and gently kisses her forehead.] Streak: All my life, I just wanted to be the best. I wanted to please the fans, shine in the spotlight, and uphold the good virtues of man. I was doing all that in the NWC, but something was missing. There was an absence in my career. I knew in my heart, I wouldn’t be the best, not until I came to PCW and proved myself to them. Not until I ascended the ranks of the best wrestlers in the world, and claimed their top prize for myself. Dana: And you will baby, you will, but you have to let it come. Standing outside in the middle of the night worrying about whether or not you’ll succeed won’t get it done. Training hard, being familiar with your opponents, and relaxing your mind until the time of the match will. You have to be confident baby. Streak: You’re right, and I know you are, but it’s just that… [She interrupts] Dana: It’s just that nothing. You’re going to step into the ring Tuesday against Stryfe and completely exhaust your mental and physical strength, and if you do that then you’ve done all you can do. Then you’re going to drag yourself out of that ring, and backstage to me. Where I will hug you, and tell you that you did a good job, and then you’re going to rest your body, and your mind and start preparing for Anna Keyson the following day. [You can sense a lightening of her voice.] Dana: [In a playful tone.] But you’re going to lose that one, because females are just better wrestlers than men are. [The mood has lightened, they both laugh. Stephens concerned look is no longer occupying rent on his rather handsome face. He rolls over, and takes his wife into his arms. They wrestle about the bed, and wrinkle it all to hell, before Ryan finally lets his wife pin him.] Dana: [slapping hand against bed] ONE! TWO! THREE! [She kisses him as we fade out. You all don’t get to see what happens next.]
