Extreme Jailbait (Part Two - Final)

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-1-02 **OOC: You know what? I'm going to shamelessly imitate our bookers, and my opponent Mr. LeBlanc, and stop counting my RP's. So there. Oh, and this is rated R. At least.** **Theme Music: 'Rock You Like A Hurricane' by the Scorpions** ----------------------------------------Dear Jenna, I'malittle drunk right now. If I doanything stupidd tonite, fergive me. LoveJase---------------------------------------- [Our scene comes up where the last scene left off, Jason...fully attired in a toga a la Big Alan Steele, attending a Phi Kappa Psi fraternity function. Standing on a table, being handed alcohol to keep his mouth shut...and now just told that he's going to be sold to a college girl this evening. To most people, this is NOT a problem.] ::Oh no.:: [But as you know, Jason is NOT a normal person. He's still hung up on his dead wife. He hasn't been with anyone since Jenna died.] ::This is a train wreck waiting to happen. One, I have no intention of having a date, relationship, or any liason with a young woman here...:: [A pretty young blonde thing in a togakini winks at Jason as she adjusts the top of her togakini.] ::Except her.:: [Jason is handed a beer, absently drinks the whole thing, and absently hands it back to Joe. He signals a pledge to get a refill.] ::Second of all...none of these young girls would want a guy like me. I'm ancient, compared to the rest of the guys here. I'm a banged-up former athlete, I have no money to speak of...I mean, come on...:: [Another beer, another drink, another worthless pledge sent to get another cup full of beer.] Mark: Well, are you ladies ready to bid for the future PCW Extreme Champion!? [A loud chorus of whistles and such come from the females in togakinis and togas.] Joe: You guys wanna start the bidding at...twenty-five dollars?? [Instantaneously, seven to fifteen hands shoot up in the throng.] ::That many? Odd.: Mark: Fifty? Seventy-five?? [As the bidding proceeds, several dozen different young ladies make their bids...and Jason manages to down three or four (he's starting to lose count about...now) more cups filled to the brim with some horrible keg beer. The bidding has jumped ahead to...] Joe: One hundred and fifty dollars? [Two young women have continued to bid, a DROP DEAD GORGEOUS blonde togakini girl (the same one who tried to take a peek up Jason's toga earlier) and a very attractive youngish-looking redhead in a homemade toga, surrounded by what appear to be her sorority sisters, who keep feeding her wads of cash.] Joe: One hundred and fifty dollars...going once... [The redhead manages to scrape together a little more money from her friends before yelling...] Redheaded Girl: TWO HUNDRED DOLLARS!! [The blonde girl shoots a look at Jason saying 'Sorry, stud.' and lowers her head a little.] Mark: SOLD! To the redhead who we don't know in the back! [The girl jumps up and down, and hugs her friends, before scurrying up to the table.] ::She's cute, but she looks a little young. I'll just dance with her a little, make her feel pretty, and find somewhere to pass out...I'm starting to feel a little tired...:: [Jason steps down from the table, carefully, another beer is fed into his hand, and the red-headed girl stares deeply into his face.] ::Uh-oh...I know that loo-:: [Before Jason can react (alcohol slows the reflexes, kids), the redhead mashes his lips with a deep and serious kiss. The crowd goes wild.] ::Oh. Shit.:: [Jason gently but firmly pulls away, but the lithe young woman seems pleased. She starts dragging him back indoors, and Jason...ever the gentleman, not wanting to embarass, just goes along with her.] ::This is NOT going to end well...is it.:: Jason: Uh, look... [The redheaded girl looks at her drunken prey with smoldering green eyes.] Jamie: Call me Jamie. Jason: Uh, right, Jamie... [She stops momentarily, and puts a finger to Jason's lips.] Jamie: Not a word, sweet thing. [She continues to drag him back to the rear of the frat house, but our scene cuts to Mark and Joe talking to a few of Jamie's friends who were giving her money earlier. They look a bit young, and excited to be here.] Mark: So your friend won Jason Core, huh? [One of the girls, 'Marlena' for argument's sake, answers.] Marlena: Yup. It's good, none of the other guys at this party are suitable. [The patented quizzical expression is on both frat boys' faces.] Joe: 'Suitable?' [Another girl ('Gena'?) gives a 'Well duh!' look.] Gena: See, Jamie's picked tonight to lose her virginity. [Marlena nods.] Marlena: She wanted to make sure that it was someone safe. Gena: ...and nice! Marlena: And well endowed! [We cut back to the earlier scene of a girl trying to check under Jason's toga. Sure enough, that was Marlena. End mini-cut scene.] Mark: Whoa...I guess Jason's getting lucky tonight. Joe: Yeah...a virgin. Go him. Gena: Yeah, she doesn't look like she's sixteen, does she? [Stop. Mark and Joe's faces drop into stunned amazement, framed by the giggling of the girls.] Mark: Sixteen? Joe: Sixteen? [All the girls around nod.] Mark AND Joe: JAILBAIT! [With this, the two frat boys bolt indoors, leaving the girls in stunned amazement.] Gena: Men! [The scene cuts back to a small room in the back of the frat house. Dark. Secluded. The door opens, and Jamie leads Jason in.] Jamie: And...here we are! ::Uh-oh. Here we are? Man, this isn't like wrestling. How do I get myself out of a situation without hurting my opponent...nicely, but firmly.:: [Jamie rubs her hand up the front of Jason's toga.] Jamie: Mmmm...nice and firm. ::Okay, NOT NICE AND FIRM!.:: Jason: Jamie, stop, hon. [Jamie giggles in delight.] Jamie: Oh, playing hard to get, huh? [The gives him a playful shove, and he lands on the couch.] ::I don't frickin' believe it. I'm virtually getting raped by this girl. Now I know how LeBlanc felt over in Amaranth.:: Jamie: You're gonna help me become a woman... ::Become a woman...oh man...oh...oh, jeez. I'm kinda tired all of a sudden...:: [Jamie mounts Jason's toga covered body, and slight snoring sounds begin to emanate from Jase's closed mouth.] Jamie: Oh...oh well. [She feels around under Jason's toga.] Jamie: Good, he can still operate! [She fishes around under the toga for a moment, and then pulls out...well, we don't know. Our view is obscured by a bottle of Budweiser. No matter what anyone says, Bud is the backstage Official Drink of PCW. But alas, Jamie desires something else to quench her thirst.] Jamie: Yes... [The door blasts open, revealing Mark and Joe.] Joe: NO! [They take a brief look at, uh...the beer bottle, have a brief look of jealousy cross their faces, but it's replaced by steadfast determination.] Mark: Suck not that d*ck! [Jamie stands up in amazement, and the two guys rush over to the sleeping Jason and toss him over their collective shoulders.] Joe: Something's digging into my shoulder, man. [Mark and Joe both make disgusted faces. As they leave, our rapist, Jamie...calls out to them.] Jamie: Now what do I do? Mark: Find a 16 year old, tramp! I'll have Jason give you Anton The Kid's phone number. [And the men exit, victorious, having saved Jason from certain peril...for the police were already on the way to break up the party. A minute or so later, and Jason would never make it to March Madness...never win the PCW Extreme Title.] Joe: We're not getting paid enough for this. [We see Joe and Mark carrying the #1 Contender for the Extreme Title down Pensacola Street, past Doak Campbell Stadium. Mark reaches out and smacks Joe's arm.] Mark: Does $150 ring a bell? Joe: Oh yeah... [And the two men trudge down the street, with a content, sleeping Jason Core on their shoulders. Now, come Sunday...we know Jase'll be relaxed and ready to fight. Could he BE more relaxed.] Jason: *snore* Joe: Dude's heavy as hell... [That's a nope. Does the 'Swamp Dawg' have to worry about Jason? He better. Not only is Jason relaxed...but he IS focused on his match for Sunday. He's gotten his family issues straightened out...he's got a powerful desire to win his first PCW title...and worst of all, if you're Karl LeBlanc...] Jason: *snore* [On Sunday, he's got one MOTHER of a hangover. The scene does that 'fade to black' thing that you know so well...see you on Sunday, kids. Peace out, PCW.]
