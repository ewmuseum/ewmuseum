Bullshit and Lies

7 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Bullshit and Lies Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 2/07/03 -The Malice Man sits in a leather chair wearing blue jeans, a Johnny Cash Middle finger shirt, and a John Deere hat. He has a glass of whiskey in his hand is ready to rant into the camera like always.- Williams: Jon what the fuck are you doing eating at McDonalds? Nothing but retards and burn outs working there and they will spit in your food. Man, there in the back drooling all over you food hoss. -Duke adjusts his large, chrome frame, Elvis style glasses.- Williams: But I’m not here to compare fast food notes. I’m here to tell you to relax, chill the fuck out, get high, get laid, do what ya got to do. I mean in the end what does it matter anyway hoss? -Duke thumbs his nose.- Williams: I asked to go out with dignity, respect, and purpose something I feel I’ve earned. When nobody else was working the fans and we had nothing but heels who carried the ship?? HUH?? In the two years I’ve been here have I not earned anything besides being the most over rated wrestler? Tell me Jon, you’ve ridden this ship too. You’ve been around longer then I have. Where’s your gold watch? Huh? They editing your segments too? Fucking bullshit if you ask me. I am I pissed right now?? You’re damn Skippy. -Duke downs some whiskey.- Williams: My contract runs out at the end of February and at this point I don’t have ANY interest in making an appearance at the PPV. Why should I? To put some young gun over who’s being pushed up the card to damn fast. Tell me Jon, did you enjoy watching El Diablo headline two consecutive ppv’s getting shots at the world title. Do you think the viewers were interested in that? -Duke’s lip curls.- Williams: How about the new Owner Jon, does that at all make you want to work harder. You enjoying the storyline? Fucking Skitzo or some shit? What a fucking crock. Did they offer the position to you Jon?? God knows you’ve earned it. Hey, I'm not braggin' or complainin' I'm just talkin' to myself man to man here. -Duke sighs and shakes his head.- Williams: When Steve Austin walked out on that other promotion I didn’t understand it. I didn’t understand why he wouldn’t take the dive to Lesner and move on. But after jobbing to Violence Jack I understand completely. It’s a matter of respect, principle, and dignity towards the business. Here I am in the final month of my career working towards my last PPV and what do they do?? -Duke spits on the floor.- Williams: They stick me in a match with a returning talent they’re looking to push. I match they know I can’t win and if I do it ruins this guys return. So fuck my “Month Of Malice”, fuck my going out in style, fuck the respect I’ve earned. Lets use Duke to push the young guys. What a fucking treat. -Duke laughs a sarcastic chuckle.- Williams: I had a segment where I beat the fuck out of Jack with a bat. Showing my characters unstable nature in his final month. What do they do?? They cut it out of the show. They cut it out of the show and then book me to lose in the main event. You know something Jon it’s refreshing to speak my mind. -Duke downs some more whiskey.- Williams: Then to add insult to injury they put me in a match with you. A guy who’s my friend. A booking that makes no sense. I kick the shit out of Kannon at the PPV but yet I don’t get an automatic entry into the Rumble. Suck on this fat cock. Where’s the justice in that? -Duke again shakes his head.- Williams: I’m going to lay down for you tonight Kano. I have no interest in winning the match and having to be at March Madness as anything more then a commentator. My contract is over in February 28th and that’s that. You remember when this federation used to respect those who’ve put there time in? Remember how they let Boda ride out in style. Hoss them days are over with. -Duke smiles a sadistic smile- Williams: I’m going to let you win the match. It’s not really that big of me since you have no chance at wining March Madness. They’ll just use you to put over some undeserving asshole that will be gone as soon as his push dies. I don’t know how many times you and I have sat through that. You think they would learn by now. -Duke arches his eye brow.- Williams: You think they would buy a fucking clue. I don’t know about you but if I see one more Kannon vignette I’m going to fucking vomit. You ever hear too much of a great thing hoss??? Gives me the fucking shits like drinking too much tequila. -Duke downs some more booze.- Williams: Johnny I never been nobody's idol, but at least I got a title. And I Take A lot Of Pride In What I Am. I have enough pride to say fuck you to PCW and walk out on my own terms. If I were a smarter man I would have read the writing on the wall saying PCW wasn’t going in my direction. I should have walked out to OSW when that opportunity presented its self a few months ago. But I bought into the bullshit they fed into my ear. I let my ego and ambition overshadow my good sense. -Duke tosses off his shades and looks directly in the camera.- Williams: I made a bad career decision. Plus I wanted to live on my name and not have to pave another path. Don’t make that same mistake Jon. Don’t do it. Fuck everybody else in that locker room cus the only person they are looking out for is himself or herself. You do the same Jon. Don’t left them rape you the ass Malice Style. Don’t put anybody over you don’t think deserves it. Fuck’em Jon. Fuck’em Hard. -Duke finishes off the bottle and throws it down on the floor.- Williams: Take your legendary status to heart. Don’t let them take that from you Jon. Don’t let them defecate on your character. Did you see what OSW did to me? -Duke looks seriously into the camera.- Williams: They offered me a chance to go over there. Told me they would give me a win over Mexi. What do they do? They have Ivan toss me out of the ring and push there own fucking agenda. Made me look like shit. I guarantee you that’s not how it was sold to me. -Duke smiles- Williams: The bottom line is this business if full of bullshit and lies. Quit while you’re ahead and don’t look back. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
