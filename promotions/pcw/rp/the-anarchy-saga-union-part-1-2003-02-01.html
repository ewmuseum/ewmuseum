The Anarchy Saga: Union Part 1

1 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-1-03 Can it really be a State of the Union address? Look around, there isn't much left anymore. A Union means this; un·ionn. 1. The act of uniting or the state of being united. 2. A combination so formed, especially an alliance or confederation of people, parties, or political entities for mutual interest or benefit. You see, thats what the union is ment to be. Together as one, pride, all that. Now, tell me where in America you see that? Tell me... just spout something random that you think is a union. Marriage you say? No, not marriage. It is a union in the sense of the law, and a union in the sense of catholiscm, but is it a permanent Union, like our 'glorious' nation suggests? No, 50% of all marriages end in divorce. FIFTY PERCENT! Half of everybody who falls in love and takes the time to get married just forgets about it, they just cast 'love' aside and eliminate it from their vocabulary in reference towards the other person. That says almost nothing about 'Americans', it says that they are non commital and fickle, that one minute they can look their lover in the eye and promise the world, then the next they can look the judge in the eyes and say it wasn't a gift, or that they want half of it. Thats perfectly fine in the current America. Tell me how that constitutes a Union? Got anymore? Oh, Actual Unions? Like the ones you join to better the workplace? Bullshit. Unions are corrupt ways to get more money from you that you work for, just like taxes. Unions make you believe that they are doing something for you, in a way they are you might be saying. Or thinking, if you even do that. You see, Unions tell you that they keep you from getting fired for no reason, help you get overtime and benefits. Uhhh... Can't the workplace provide you that safety anyway? In other countries maybe. Not in our 'free' America. The union ties you down and rapes your pockets. Dues, initiation, any little thing. For what? Security? We have all seen what a republican government can do for 'security' haven't we? Nothing. They have made America weak. Well fuck that. Fuck the Unions. The people can run themselves if they wanted to, they have been lulled into this mistaken belief where all things are provided to them by the Unions. The unions are just namesakes for the bigger corrupt Union, the government. Anymore? Oh, you want a rest? I apoligize that my brain has overpowered yours despite my american education. Education, thats another joke. Well, look, I'm going to sit here in my chair, drink my european coffee, and wait for you to recover. Come again
