Undercover Brother, incogNEGRO [Chapter 3. 'DUCK DARKIE!']

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/21/02 Let me fill you in on what happened in Chapter 2. While Rush Mason slept in the single motel room in the seedy side of Oklahoma, Jordan X Shabazz was forced to sleep in the car. While sleeping the car, Jordan had a nasty nightmare in which he was a white man. As Jordan woke up from his deep sleep and in a deep sweat, there was a knocking at the door. Thinking, that at the door was his friend Rush Mason, Jordan X Shabazz threw on a ski mask that Rush had brought on the road trip in case it got cold in Jersey when they arrived at the PCW Pavilion for Fusion. When Jordan opened the door, much to his surprise, three KKK suit clad men met him. They, assuming Jordan was a man whom they were waiting for at their little gathering across the street named Jack, told him to get out of the car and to start 'N***** hunting.' Our scene opens up again with Jordan seated in the backseat of the rented Cadillac Deville 2002. The three KKK members in full KKK regalia stand before Jordan, smiling through their masks, thinking like the ignorant rednecks that they are, they think the man wearing the ski mask is their friend Jack. 'So, Jack, let's go hunting, we'll get us one tonight, Stacy, in the motel over here, says she got two of 'em come in tonight,' states one of the men. Jordan, having no alternative to stay alive, gets out of his Caddy. He stands up straight, and wonder how he'll survive when he speaks hardcore Ebonics, and dresses like a black man. Dresses like a black man... Oh shit! Jordan just remembered that he's attired like any regular black man would be, baggy khaki shorts, a white t-shirt, a Nike basketball armband, and some Lugz boots. Before Jordan can think anymore, he is interrupted by one of the men in the dark KKK suits. 'Ah! Great job, Jack. You're dressed like one of 'em. He'll think you're just another darkie ready for some, how do you say it? 'Chillin''? The rednecks burst out laughing at their cheap attempt a humor. Yeah, redneck racism at it's finest. Jordan nods at the three rednecks as he ruffles his clothing. Still drenched in his own sweat, Jordan decides not to speak yet, he's trying to make sure that when he speaks, and he doesn't say something that he'd normally say. He doesn't want them to think that he's black... As strange as that sounds. Suddenly, from the motel that Rush was sleeping in, a young, vibrant, beautiful looking woman comes out. She has shoulder length blond hair; she's wearing some KKK pants and a white t-shirt with a design and logo that Jordan can't see... She looks rather familiar to Jordan... Wait a minute.... IT'S THE REDNECK, HICK, FARMER'S DAUGHTER FROM INSIDE THE MOTEL! Obviously, the way she talked and dressed before was a disguise, perhaps she was waiting for Jordan and Rush to arrive at the station, but how in the hell would she know? You'll find out later... Jordan watches in awe as the girl reaches inside her mouth, and pull out several teeth covers, revealing a beautiful set of white teeth. Meaning, that she really did have more than 12 teeth! She calls out to one of the guys 'Bubba!' One of the men dressed in full KKK clothing, runs over to his friend, and they share a hug. Jordan's assumptions were right, she doesn't appear to be from the area. It's completely obvious that she isn't from the area. Jordan can now read what it says on her shirt... She wears a t-shirt that says University of Columbia! But that's in New York... People don't just where t-shirts with Universities on it for no reason, she's obviously an outsider. Jordan stops thinking as the woman who he now knows by the name of Stacy, leads them across the street where the other members of the KKK are waiting hungrily. This branch of the KKK hadn't gone hunting for some black people in a long time, because of the fact that very few black people frequented the area. But now, thanks to Stacy, and somebody working on the inside of the NAACP, it has been discovered, that one, or more, black people are in the area. As they all gather around Stacy, who appears to be their leader, she speaks. 'It has come to my attention, thanks to one of my friends at the NAACP, that we've got two of 'em right here in this city. Two of 'em came into my motel earlier today, one of 'em is asleep now, and the other one went to sleep somewhere in a car. We're going to find them tonight!' She screams angrily, almost as if she's trying to incite a riot. The crowd of KKK members, a small group for their standards, about 30, cheers backs! Jordan becomes even more uncomfortable as the three KKK members that pulled him out of the back of the Cadillac Deville he slept in. Before he could think anymore, the entire group of KKK members begins walking towards to the motel, with Stacy leading the charge. All of the KKK members have their hoods off, showing a display of faces. Young, old, ugly... Uglier. Several of the members notice Jordan wearing his hood, and he's not even wearing the one they provide. Nor is he wearing any KKK clothing on his body, in fact, he's wearing 'their clothes.' Luckily for Jordan, he's pulled up his socks to hide his black legs, but that wasn't much of a stretch, because the shorts were baggy and came down to his shins. His shirt, however, did reveal his arms, though he managed to snatch up a sweater on the way out and put it on. They have arrived at the motel; Stacy leads the men into the motel room of Rush Mason. They kick down the door to find... Find... Find... Nothing? Da fuck? Rush was gone. They all looked around and began murmuring. 'Where would a N***** go in the middle of the freakin' night?' 'KFC!' One redneck shouts. 'Church's!' Another one shouts. 'Shut the fuck up!' Jordan shouts. Oh shit. Dumbass. Blew your own muthafucking cover. Like committing suicide... Damned Civil Rights Extremists, you can tell they aren't smart just by the freakin' arguments this people make. Anyways, now Jordan's got a whole bunch of white, unblinking eyes on him. Suddenly, a big dark blur... 'DUCK DARKIE!' OOC: I hate to leave y'all in suspense... Wait... No I don't. Please come back for Chapter 4.
