Chapter V: A Thought Process

10 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-2-03 ::Introduction:: Mark at last won in PCW again and hopefully will keep on doing that. When he wrestled Teresa he looked untouchable, even with the referee change the outcome didn’t. He actually did beat the Television champion in PCW with a respectable win and is now in the battle royal at March Madness. There he will bide for a World Title shot and then he could be walking away with the World Title in less than a month. What a high goal to set for himself as he has been back with PCW for a couple of weeks. But he can’t look past what is offering him on Fusion this week. He is going to be going up against a few other wrestlers for the Extreme Title. But a lot of people don’t think Mark is that extreme, well after this match hopefully they will find that the Nazi can show you extreme. ::End Introduction:: Chapter V: A Thought Process Mark is trying to stand up on his two feet as he presses his hands against the wall trying to use his own arm strength to get him up off of the ground. Mark staggers as his right leg is giving out on him every time he puts pressure to the leg. He finally gets his body stood up on both of his legs and he starts to walk into the distance as the yellow street light shows a slight light on Mark’s body as he walks under it. “What do I do with my life now?” Mark asks to himself as he walks some more going in the direction of the main street. “I don’t have a job and no one really believes in anything that I do.” He is now putting his hand out on the rough brick to hold himself up as the pain is excruciating. The expression on his face is rough and he doesn’t look to happy. Mark stops as he hears some music coming from across of the alley. Mark then heads over towards the music and he sees that there is a window at the room. Mark holds his hands over the window pane and he peers in the window. “Maybe that’s what I should resort to.” Mark says as he looks into the window more closely. “Maybe I should just let my emotions break down and forget all of this Nazi stuff.” Mark sees two people in the little apartment together. The music that is coming from the room is some Barry White and the two people just look madly in love. They are both dressed for special occasion looking real spiffy and the guy pulls out a nice diamond ring from his pocket. The lady is in tears and Mark can’t take this. “No! Mark Makonie does not need a lady in his life to make him happy.” Mark says to himself as he punches at the rough red brick. “I can make myself happy with just being myself and destroying the whole coon nation while I am at it.” Mark steps away from the window slowly from his leg being messed up and he starts to walk down the alley some more going to the main street. As Mark reaches the main street he pauses before he goes out into the open. He is making sure that the cops are not on him anymore as if they were he just would hide in the dark for a while. “Good there are no more of those damn coons following me anymore.” Mark says to himself once more as he starts to go out into the open. “I don’t even know why people call them cops. They should just be called rent a cop as far as I am concerned on the issue.” Mark walks down the dark street as the street lights are shining white lights down on the gray cement. Mark sees something in the pavement that looks like it has been sketched in with a stick or someone’s finger. “Here is a tribute to the greatest African American man that served in the USA.” Mark reads as he tries to get closer since the light is so faint. “That man is Martin Luther King Jr.” As Mark reads that he gets saliva built up in his mouth and then all of sudden Mark gets behind it and he lets it go out of his mouth. The build up of saliva hits the pavement as it makes a dark spot on the sidewalk where the tribute is at. Mark then gets up and he continues to walk towards the spot where he is heading to. “That shit should not be in this town.” Mark says aloud so if anyone is on the street they will hear it. “I just don’t get when this town is going to learn to stop worshiping the coon community and have the whites take over. I have already done as much as I can for this town, damage wise.” Mark is shaking his head as he goes past the old candy shop that once stood back in the day when Mark was a little kid. He remembers going in there with his grandfather and getting his macadamia nut cookie and eating it with a big tall glass of cold milk. Mark glances as what is a barber shop now that cuts the coon’s hair for a regular price. Mark remembers when this town was just filled with the white community and the blacks weren’t even around. His grandma always was telling him about the stories when there were separate toilets for the blacks and whites and Mark wishes he would have lived back then. “I think its time that I get out of this town and explore more of my life.” Mark says as he sits down on the bus bench waiting for the next bus to come by. All of a sudden a cold rush of wind comes in from the East and Mark is only wearing his black jeans and his untied leather boots. Mark wraps his arms around his body as he bends over to try to keep himself warm till the next bus is coming. The cold air on his body feels like he is dieing since his body is so cold and he feels that his mind is freezing up. Mark just bears the cold as he closes his eyes and waits for his future to begin.
