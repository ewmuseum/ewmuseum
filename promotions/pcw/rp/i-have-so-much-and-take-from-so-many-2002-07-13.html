I Have So Much, And Take From So Many.

13 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13/07/02 To anyone looking in on his life..... Xavier Kannon has it all. At the age of 26 he has a high ranking in the World’s most profitable Religion, a 20% stake in PCW, a Bank Account that with proceeds from PCW, Snapple~! and Scientology runs into the mid 8 figures and a beautiful wife in Eleanor Hall. But with everything he has, he has taken so much from others... He tried to have Jeremy Howard’s son taken from him, his orders have taken loved ones from their wives, parents and children.... And on Friday night he took the Pharaoh from Isiy when the bloodied Egyptian was Mummified by the couple then driven out of the arena... After such an act, others would be in hiding, or even attempting to explain their actions in hope that the repercussions would be less severe.... But this is Xavier and Ellie~! we are talking about, so a celebratory meal is in order. “Here’s to an Egyptian shipped back to where he belongs!” *CLINK!* With the sound of glasses toasting we fade into “Elfman’s”, the most expensive and exclusive restaurant in Hubbardville where are a quiet table, all alone and not surrounded by the other guests sits a happy couple... Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Kannon-Hall. Eleanor : “That, like, SO RULED!!! Like, when he woke up and was all... Hey, I’m like a Mummy! That, like, TOTALLY rocked my world!” One side of the table sits the giggly and happy figure of Ellie, her Preppy mind actually enjoying the fact they bandaged a brutally beaten and bloody Titu Xansiv. The preppy wife of Kannon is wearing a classic little Black dress, and her long, wavy brown hair with subtle Blonde highlights is untied and falls down her bare back. Across from her, wrestling’s only Scientologist grins almost bashfully.... Not a natural emotion by any means. Xavier : “Want your World rocked? Wait until I get you home...” He winks to his Wife who goes Red and almost looks embarrassed.... Both of them using their act of pure evil against a defenceless man as an aphrodisiac. She looks back at him with her Bambi-esque pale Blue eyes, taking a small sip from her glass of Moet. Eleanor : “So...... Can’t...... Wait!” As the sickeningly happy couple play footsie under the table, Xavier glances out at the rest of the restaurant, where other couples sit across from one another at the other tables, yet none of them quite seeming to have the chemistry that Xavier and Ellie~! have. Xavier : “I can just imagine the Xansiv house tonight.... That little shed in a Sand Pit somewhere hiding from the authorities until they get their Green Card.... Isiy bawling her eyes out because she has no idea where her husband is....” His bright Green eyes glimmer with menace as he imagines the Pharaoh’s wife, her husband disposed of by the Scientologist, probably out of her mind with worry. Xavier : “... Beside her that huge bumbling idiot of a Bodyguard.... That is if they got him breathing again trying to comfort her.” He then mocks a North African accent, making his voice deeper as to mock Zen Dula, bodyguard of the Pharaoh... Xavier : “Oh Eeeeeezey, he bee okay, Titu be tuf mahn!” As soon as he finishes impersonating the 7 foot giant he shakes his head jokingly, nearly cracking himself up as he takes another sip from his Champagne. Xavier : “Correct me if I’m wrong my little Shag Squirrel...” Across the table his doting wife nods, enjoying her husband’s ranting. Xavier : “.. But aren’t Bodyguards, you know... Mean to PROTECT their employer? I mean the 5 guys I have do a damn good job stopping any Peons getting near me...” The Scientologist gestures to a group of 5 men in Black tuxedos that are positioned just down the small flight of stairs from the couple.... One of the Bodyguards nodding back to him in assurance... Eleanor : “Those are, like, YOURS? Wow, I thought they were like REALLY lazy Waiters!” “HEY!!!!!!!!!!!!!! YOU KILLED MY SON!!!!!!!!!!!” Suddenly the screeching voice of an enraged Woman is heard and out of nowhere comes a woman seemingly in her late 50’s who comes tearing at the couple’s table, pure hate in her eyes. Eleanor : “I DIDN’T..... HE DID!!!” Ellie~! drops off her chair and rolls under the table as Kannon looks between the Woman and his security guards... Both of whom are rushing towards him, the woman still screaming at him. Woman : “YOU HAD HIM BEATEN AND THROWN IN A RIVER TO DROWN YOU BASTARD....” Despite the rage on the woman’s face and his wife cowering under the table, Xavier sits there calmly, a smug grin on his face as he looks right back at her and waves, under the gaze of the entire restaurant who have turn to see the scene. Xavier : “Do I know you?” His attitude only angers the bereaved mother more as Ellie is heard talking, panic in her voice from under the table. Eleanor : “Have they shot that fat mad woman yet!” She continues to storm towards Kannon who is now mocking a yawn, leaning back and crossing his legs as she gets within 10 metres. Xavier : “I killed him, so what.... He cost me a STRIKE, stop being so selfish!” The woman’s face is now Red with anger as Kannon now toasts her with a glass of Champagne as she gets within 5 metres.... Ellie~! peering out from under the table at her, and the rest of the restaurant’s patrons all watching with anticipation at the event surrounding their fellow Scientologist. Xavier : “Can I get you a glass?” The smirking Gold Patron Meritorious now holds the bottle of Moet up to the furious and grieving mother, distraught at the attitude of the man who’s order caused her to outlive her own child. Woman : “I’LL MAKE YOU PAY FOR WHAT YOU DID TO HIM YOU BAST-....” *OOOOOOOOOOOMPH!!!!!* Just as her hands get to within a foot of Kannon’s throat the Scientologist’s Bodyguards pounce and all 5 of the burly men tackle the woman down onto the floor as she writhes around, trying to get free to avenge her son’s death. Woman : “YOU CAN’T STOP ME!!!!!!” The entire restaurant looks at her and mutters under their breaths what they think the scene is about. “She said he killed her son....” “Kannon got a Strike?” “She’s that man from the river’s Mom?” While the furious mother is still being subdued, Xavier holds his hand up to the people as Ellie pulls herself up from under the table and back onto her seat, adjusting her dress. Xavier : “Miltant Christian...” Kannon explains, pointing to the woman who is being dragged away by his security. Xavier : “..... I converted her son to be a Hubbard follower and she thinks I KILLED his Christian Soul.. Some junk like that!” Eleanor : “Junk....... Like...... That!” The collective sound of the restaurant “Ahhhhhhh-ing” and getting back to their meals is heard as the woman is dragged out through the kitchen door shortly followed by the sounds of her screaming and metal implement after metal implement hitting something soft. Xavier : “Not a great time to be a Peon huh? Isiy loses her husband, well at least until someone finds his bloodied and bandaged body...... She lost her Egypt loving punk of a son.... And Rent-a-Villain lost his little Ginger slut of a girlfriend!” Returning to their meal, Ellie~! smirks at the thought of Renty’s heart being broken. Eleanor : “Ginger....... Little........ Slut!” The look on the face of Kannon is one of complete contentment... Sitting in his own city across a table from the wife he loves as so many have lost those who they care about. Xavier : “While we’ll go home tonight to our marital bed.... Pleasing each other in such Scientological ways that few have discovered.... That dressed up freak will be mourning a picture Aimz and trying to convince himself he is a “Villain” when thoughts I have in my sleep have more dastardly intentions than his worst plans..... If that woman survived she’ll have to live with the fact she’ll ever avenge her son.. And Isiy, well she’ll probably just nail that big dumb bodyguard!” As Kannon takes another sip of his Champagne, another conceited grin crosses his face as we start to fade out to Black... Xavier : “With Hubbard as my witness....” He puts the glass down and nods to himself, in his head thinking of all he has and all he has taken from others. Xavier : “It’s great being me!” His Green eyes glow brightly with joy, the last things we see as we hit Black. “Great........ Being....... You!”
