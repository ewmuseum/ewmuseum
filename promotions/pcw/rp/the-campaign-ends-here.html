Date: unknown =©=Cutting to an interjecting scene totally different from the last scene, we find the Akiyama and the Divine Prophetess standing in the back of a crowded subway car. They both stood their ground as the car jerked and heaved back and forward from stop to stop. There was a wrinkle in the brow of Cyclonica as she stared straight ahead into the dark walls of the underground tunnels whiz by.=©= Neo…new…fresh… It’s not rookie…not infantile…not innocent… Not like Dark Messiahs… Neo… Void…nothingness…negativity…morbidity…eternity… It’s not satanic…not gothic…not demonic… Not like Dark Messiahs… Void… God...lord…sovereign…master… It’s not delusional…not freak…not mortal… Not like Dark Messiahs… God… …I wish the best for those brave men who go out and face hardships everyday…risking their frail bodies and their meager lives to strive for some sort of common good for man. But what good are men that claim to be saviors of darkness committing? Two mortal men who don’t have a single thing dark about them except maybe their hair color. Weak…foolish…generic dark wannabes. Brave…yet foolish men… Foolish for thinking that there’s any type of good left to be done in this world. Foolish for thinking that the good they do is really good…when it is more evil than anything the Dark Messiahs can claim to have committed. Dark indeed…they have a lot to learn… Just what makes two humans like them dark? Being violent? Hell…if that’s the case, then the wrestling industry’s filled with dark gimmicks. Try again. Being sadistic? Nope…sadism is an art that these two have no way of mastering in their career. Next? How about being left in the dark about not really being worth a Divine d@mn between the both of them? How about being left in the dark about the talent that they lack to conqueror the tag team division? How about being left in the dark about the pain and suffering that they’re about to face at the hands of the Neo Void God come this week? If that counts…then sure…they can be dark all they want. And they’re messiahs as well…can’t forget that little tidbit. What are they the messiahs of? What are they saviors of? “Steel: A handicapped match, is the best match we can get…” No…the best match you can get will be matching your dental records after being beaten into a pulp. Deliverers of dumbness? “Steel: …Akiyama doesn’t stand a chance.” Chances…like you know anything about chances. How many chances have you squandered in your big championship hunt? Saviors of stupidity? “Steel: …but he is just one guy.” Oh geez…this guy knows nothing about Akiyama after making that statement. Messiahs…of the Moronic…I think… Sure…everyone needs to be saved every once in a while. One question though…on Turmoil…who’s saving the Messiahs from their Absolute Destiny Apocalypse? “Stone: I mean, he calls himself “The Neo Void God.” Is that supposed to scare us or something?” Not as much as the Dark Messiahs strikes fear into the hearts of men. With a name like that, I bet Akiyama really wishes he cut in front of you guys in the nickname line…sorry, pardon my sarcasm. “Stone: I mean you see what this guy used to dress like, he didn’t look scary he looked stupid, and he even looks stupid now.” All in all…the fear of these two men shows in their dialogue…they must be scared if this is all the material they can say against Akiyama…lest these two are truly dimwits. “Stone: And without anyone to protect Akiyama…” I hate when opponents think they know everything about their opponent by just skimming over their biography. It makes their insults so generic, and they sound completely stupid when they make comments like this. If they knew anything about the god, they’d know that Akiyama needs no one for protection…others have to find protection from him…protection from banishment. “Stone: …I felt sorry for him because at Turmoil he doesn’t stand a chance.” I feel sorry for myself…for ever thinking the Dork Massagers had anything worth listening to. I feel sorry for Akiyama…because he’s the one that’s got to fight two lame humans who can’t take a few hours out of their “busy” campaign to learn more about their foe. As a matter of fact…I feel sorry for those two lame brains as well…I know Akiyama’s going to be pissed when he sees for himself personally just how much of a couple of dummies they are. “Steel: That d@mn freak Akiyama doesn’t stand a chance!” They speak a lot of chances here…that must be fans of the game of chance. Like for future reference…if you get the chance to have yourself booked against the nVg…waste the chance and hope for something that might not cost you your lives. Can’t waste the chance, you say? Why not? I’m sure you’ve wasted so many others before trying to get the belts…practice makes perfect. Best method of survival…you get the chance to run, you had better run…because if you don’t you’re giving Akiyama the chance as well as the permission to make sure that no one walks away after the match is over. Mortals always see things in numbers and never actual individual potential. Two against one? Big Divine Deal…with the combined talent between the two of them, Akiyama’s the one who should be tying his hand behind his back to even the score. But even then…I’d be surprised if either one of them was fast enough to dodge the Hand of Fate with their “fine” reflexes. Handicapped match means one thing in our eyes…one goal… Winner wins when he leaves his opponent…HANDICAPPED. But hey…don’t worry…when you’re neck’s snapped and you’re paralyzed after screaming in The Darkness, you’ll be able to get all the cool parking you want. Know this now…becoming champions isn’t about pledging and contributions. It’s about being worthy of being champion…something that you two wont be ready for…not for a long time. Instead of wasting time trying to speak to another mortal like yourselves, you should be training your weak little bodies in hopes of actually gaining enough strength and skill to be considered worthy of the Neo Void God’s time. You think you’re in peak condition now? No…your species knows not the true limits of the organic machines you possess. =©=The train begins to slow down to another stop. Cyclonica feels a nudge against her arm. Looking up, she finds the nVg walking out of the car into the station. She follows, her mind still set on the previous thoughts at hand. =©= Yes…a complete shame how their mortal minds totally delude themselves from their own reality. They’re just simply not good enough to defeat one like Akiyama…one man they say…sure, if they’re lucky, they can take on one man. But this is not just one man…this is the GOD…the Neo Void God…one man doesn’t begin to scratch at his might. Until they can comprehend any of that…they’re just another flat-scan. =©=The Celestial Couple steps out into the city, standing out from the rest of conformist society. They look around their surroundings, the night lights blazing down upon them and reflecting off their glasses. =©= Turmoil…the campaign comes to a halt…it ends here for you messiahs of the dark. You will have yourselves a personal taste of what it truly means to be dark… a personal view of what a messiah of pain is as Akiyama delivers suffering unto you. The Dark…shall be with us always…but you two will not…being that you are nothing but poseurs. In PCW…there’s only one person who is dark…and that’s Akiyama. He stands alone…just as he does against you two insects. And just as he will reign in victory…he will rise from your broken carcasses, using your skulls as stepping stones as he crosses over into the path. Don’t like it? Too bad…because you’re destiny has just been prophesized…SO SAYS THE DIVINE PROPHETESS. =©=The two of them disappear into the city…walking forward towards their fate…knowing full well that the campaign…is dead….=©= =©=
