10 Things I Hate About You That Make Me Want Your Kid To Be Taken Away

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/03/02 We open up in a Welfare Officer’s office a the Indiana state offices. In it, the man who’s office if is stands behind his desk opposite the first couple of Scientology, Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Hall. The man checks the document in his hand, looking up from it for a split second to eye up the 2 people in front of him who have brought the complaint. Man : “Look, Mr Kannon, we are here in the interests of the Children, but I don’ see how we can act on what you told us...” He finishes off reading it and puts it on his desk to be met with an icy glare from Eleanor and a steely gaze from Kannon, both burning holes in his own and causing his bottom lip to quiver. Man : “Okay...Well you’ve came here in person so you may as well reiterate it to me....What did you want to relay to me?” With that sentence the 1st couple of Scientology break off from their glare and revert to their normal expressions, Xavier preparing to speak. Xavier : “Simple, I, Xavier Kannon wrestling’s only Scientologist and the future of Primetime Championship Wrestling with prove to you....By giving 10 reasons, why Jeremy Howard is NOT a fit father!” Wrestling’s only Scientologist is wearing a full length Brown leather coat with an un-buttoned Black silk shirt. It falls open to show a Black “Krayzie Till I Die” t-shirt like those worn by Jeremy Howard, taking another stab at his ex-Asylum team mate. With this he has a pair of his usual Black boot-cut jeans with Green stitching and Purple embroidery depicting Scientological runes snaking down the left leg to his Black, Purple and Grey Skechers. Ellie~! leaps in to back up her man, stating the last words to the man again. Eleanor : “A.........Fit.......Father!” Eleanor Hall is wearing a Pink skinny fit t-shirt with White collar and sleeves and Snapple~! is glittering Silver across the chest. With it she has a rainbow patterned fabric belt and Green tinted faded jeans with Gold sequinned stars down both legs to her Tan Converse sneakers. Her long Brown hair with Blond highlights is neatly ties back out of her face and on her wrists she wears a chunky watch and various bands and bracelets. The Scientologist goes to continue when he hears what his valet said, sighing and breaking his momentum to turn to her and make the correction. Xavier : “No my little Poon Puppy, NOT a good father! Ellie~! thinks about it and realises she was wrong so thinks it over for a second and looks back to the man who stands bemused by the presentation occurring before him. Eleanor : “Not.......Good......Dad!” He nods along with his valet’s words of support, emphasising the point to the man before them. Xavier : “Exactly, a second perfectly sane person says the same as me!” The man they are trying to convince just stands their un-moved, wondering how long this is going to take out of his day. Man : “Er....Okay, so what are these reasons?” He is given a slight sneer for his question from Xavier who is now forced to launch right into his 10 fold explanation as to why Howard should have his Kid taken away. Xavier : “Reason One.....Numero Uno.....THE GUY HIMSELF ADMITS HE IS KRAYZIE! That one is so simple even that stupid bitch wife of his could realise....Wait, she hasn’t....Oh well.....” His words start to trip him up so as ever Eleanor jumps in on his behalf to steer him in the right way. Eleanor : “Use the murderer line....That was a good one...” The word murderer finally breaks the man out of his apathy for a brief second or two. Man : “Murder?” Now he realises he has the man’s attention, the Gold Patron Meritorious launches on. Xavier : “Yes.....If someone admits to killing someone they don’t go Oh, wow, he kills people....That’s marketable...NO THEY DON’T, they say that man is a killer therefore unfit to look after his child, and you should do the same!” Eleanor : “Yeah! Do.......The.......Same!” The man still looks confused, not sure what Xavier is getting at in his quick fire reasoning. Man : “So he hasn’t actually killed anyone?” They stand silent before him for a moment or tow, wondering how exactly to respond until Ellie~! looks to Xavier for inspiration. Eleanor : “As.....Good.....As!” With the added moment of Eleanor’s words to think Xavier starts back up with his biased reasoning, thinking of some more ways to contort the truth. Xavier : “When people send in their adoption forms and put ‘Krazed Lunatic’ on it do you accept them? No...Want me to tell you why?” Ellie~! cheers him on, acting as the one girl cheerleading squad and sneering at the man before them. Eleanor : “Tell him why....Go on, tell him....” Kannon pauses and the welfare officer takes the chance to try and put an end to the lunacy transpiring in his office. Man : “Well, ye-” Before he can even get to end of the second word Ellie~! leaps in and puts him in his place, staring right at him with her pale Blue eyes. Eleanor : “He’s going to tell you now!” With that Xavier bursts into one of his new-found moments of madness. Xavier : “BECAUSE A.....THEY CAN’T SPELL KRAYZIE SO MOST LIKELY CAN’T KEEP A JOB AND B....THEY ADMIT TO BEING A LUNATIC!” Eleanor : “Being......A......Lunatic! How would your Bosses react if you did that? Huh? Huh?” Even with Xavier’s passionate words the welfare officer remains un-impressed and tries to end the fiasco as quickly as possible. Man : “Look, Mr Kannon....You don’t seem to ha-” The Gold Patron Meritorious misinterprets his response as a cue to move onto the next point. reason two why Howard should lose his son. Xavier : “I can see you are eagerly waiting for reason Number Two so I will not disappoint....” Eleanor : “Will......Not.......Disappoint!” He leans forward, causing the man to flinch but instead of attacking him he pulls his hear back to show cuts and bruising on his forehead. Xavier : “See that? How do you think I got that? And I can assure you that is real!” Eleanor : “If that were any more real it would be there twice just to show how real it is!” The man takes a closer inspection, seeing that they are real and out of politeness he enquires. Man : “So how did you ge-” Again his doesn’t get a chance to finish as the eager Scientologist names the man who marked him. Xavier : “I got it because Jeremy Howard....A man allowed to look after a CHILD did it! Last Tuesday he hit me, last Friday he hit me some more and on Tuesday HE DID THIS!!!!!!” Eleanor holds up her hand, looking between both men. Eleanor : “Can I just add.....Unprovoked!” Kannon leans back and gestures again to the head wound. Xavier : “He cut my head open! I did nothing to deserve that....I had not even seen him that night and he drew my blood!” Eleanor : “If he lost another 8 pints he may have died from blood loss!” Maybe if it were not for the previous wild accusations the welfare officer might actually take heed of his words but is now tired of Xavier’s pathetic display. Xavier : “Imagine whet would happen if his Kid cried one night.....HE’D PROBABLY GIVE HIM THE KRAYZIE DEATH SLAM!!!!” Eleanor : “To a Baby! Hear that? To a Baby!” Again he tries to put an end to Kannon’s vain attempt. Man : “Mr Kannon, once again I reiterate that I can not cat without evidence, may-” The Scientologist’s face is masked by a look of pure contempt, as if he had just had the biggest insult possible delivered to him. Xavier : “Excuse me......Do not oppress ME! My taxes pay for your house, my blood pays for the food you eat so don’t you dare say that to me EVER again!” He doesn’t know how to respond to this new line of questioning and dithers as his right hand cups the edge of his desk and pushes a Red button. Man : “Um...Er..” With the welfare officer now quite Xavier resumes with his character assassination of Jeremy Howard. Xavier : “Now, even though those 2 reasons should be more than enough for you to burst into his home and prise his child from Jessica’s arms, enough to make her run out onto the front lawn and sob as that little brat is given to a decent family!” Ellie~! does her best to try and shift his point back to the welfare of the child. Eleanor : “Not that it’s more important than the Baby though....He doesn’t mean that!” The welfare officer now appears to be stalling for time, urging Xavier to tell him more when before he just wanted it to end. Man : “So is there a third point?” Xavier : “I AM GETTING TO IT OKAY! DO NOT HURRY ME!” The man takes a few steps back from his desk, retreating from Kannon’s harsh words. Eleanor : “Yeah, don’t oppress his right to take time to say something!” Xavier : “Reason 3....” Just as Xavier goes to speak the door of the office bursts open and 3 security guards burst in. 2 grabbing an arm each while the other takes up position between the Scientologist and the welfare officer. Xavier : “What.....WHAT IS THIS!!!!” He struggles to break free but is gradually pulled towards the door. Eleanor : “HEY! Like....Um....SO GET OFF HIM! He was like TOTALLY framed!” Xavier : “YOU ARE OPPRESSING MY RIGHT TO HAVE SOMEONE’S SON REMOVED! WHEN THE DEMOCRATS GET BACK IN YOU’LL PAY! WE HAVE INFLUENCE!” Eleanor : “GET.........OFF........HIM!” The more Kannon struggles the more guards show up, 2 now grabbing his legs and physically carrying him out of the room as the shot fades to Black. Kannon : “YOU PUT THEM UP TO THIS HOWARD......YOU!”
