Upside Down Tommy

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 10, 2003 [The scene opens on the tanned face of 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. The scene is turned upside down on the former surfers mug. His face seems a little reddish, maybe from getting some extra rays from the wonderful approaching summer sun. Maybe it's from embarrassment of a situation that he's about to enter. It could be pure, unadulterated anger ready to explode out of his perfectly toned body. With all of the options it will be interesting to see how it turns out. Fun in the sun, an awkward situation, or anger. Lately when Tommy hears about anger he begins to think about The Incredible Hulk. It seems that everywhere that he goes there are posters, toys, and food of The Hulk. So, naturally Tommy suspects that when one gets angry that he turns a certain shade of green. But, alas, when Tommy thinks about the phrase, 'a certain shade of green', his mind wanders to the rock band Incubus. It is a track off of their album titled, 'S.C.I.E.N.C.E.'. It's truly amazing how someones mind can just float to different thoughts in such a little amount of time. Only thirty seconds ago he was thinking about bodacious babes completely naked on his bed. Now he's thinking about Incubus. Naked women to Incubus. It could be a hell of a title for an upcoming book that he will write. It will explore the thought process from Naked Women to Incubus. In fact, it could be like a science experiment and Incubus's one album is called S.C.I.E.N.C.E.! Tommy realizes that this couldn't be some kind of coincidence, that it was a destiny] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: That's just not far out, man. [The scene switches to Harry standing upright with an agonizing look on his face. It's almost as if he's the one upside down with the reddish face. That's one good quality about Harry, he ALWAYS puts himself in the others persons shoes...even when it's not necessary. Tommy could tell a few stories about those naked women and Harry's odd quality of putting himself in anothers shoes. Peeping Tom comes to mind, but it's not the words that Tommy would use] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: It's totally bodacious. Harry: Your hanging upside down, man. Tommy: Batman does it. Harry: But Batman is a crime fighter. Tommy: Who's to say that I'm not a most radical crime fighter? Harry: Me, man. Tommy: Ok...but this helps with muscle growth. It will only make me more gnarly. [The scene zooms out to show Tommy hanging upside down on an odd device with foot latches. Harry stands next to the contraption trying to figure out exactly how it's holding his older brother in there. Tommy moves his body forward and backward to get a little swinging action going on. Harry takes a couple steps back, afraid that he may be hit by the swinging surfer] Harry: You do know that you have a match against James Edwards, man. Tommy: Huh? Harry: A match. It's against James Edwards, man. Tommy: He's not in the Universal Title Tournament! These dudes are TOTALLY HEINOUS with this tournament stuff. First they tell you that you only have to take on seven compadres and then the next thing you know they are throwing a bogus dude with a regular name at you. Pft, I tell you what bodacious bro...things are going to start changing in this wrestling business and I'm going to be the rad dude to do it. Harry: Far out, man. [Tommy continues swinging back and forth with his index finger tapping his chin in deep thought. Harry's eyes move back and forth with Tommy's momentum just waiting for Tommy to fall off of the machine at any moment] Tommy: I've fought guys like Skylar Green, Venom Penisgram, The Pharaoh, Rent-A-Jerkface, and Dante Jackson. How does this TOTALLY BOGUS dude think he's going to take me? James Edwards...just say that name a few times. Harry: James Edwards....James Edwards....James Edwards.... Tommy: Doesn't that sound so totally plain to you? Harry: James Edwards... Tommy: Totally! You'd think that the guy would try to come up with some kind of heinous nickname to make him sound cooler. Maybe something like 'Stone Cold' James Edwards...wait...I think that name is already taken by some bald, broken knee loser. How about 'Stone Hot' James Edwards...wait...that one was taken by ANOTHER bald, broken knee loser from NTICW. Maybe something like 'Tommy's Bitch'... Harry: James Edwards... Tommy: TOTALLY! Now THAT is a gnarly name. [Tommy begins to swing his body back and forth again to regain some of his momentum that he lost from earlier. His face is of utter satisfaction as he's glad that he has come up with a more interesting name for James Edwards. Apparently this is the man who he will face on the upcoming Turmoil card, but this all brand new to The Tubular One. As a former World Champion he feels that he shouldn't have to take on such petty opponents. Should he even show up at the event? Would it even be right for him to do so? Would that make him look like he's afraid of this James Edwards? Meh. It wouldn't look like anything because all those wrestlers and all those fans know that Tommy is the best. They all know EXACTLY what he's capable of. Why bother even showing up at the event? It'll only be a waste of damn time to Tommy. He has more hanging upside down to do and more bodacious babes to get with. Better yet...he'd rather put his surfing trophies in chronological order than go to Turmoil and face 'Tommy's Bitch' James Edwards] Tommy: So, how about that new Metallica album? It's MOST bodacious. I have to admit that I am not a fan of the heavy metal genre but those guys can sure get down and dirty...I TELL YA! Harry: James Edwards... Tommy: Yes, I know that is my opponent on Tuesday. In fact, I probably won't even show up now because I have to organize my socks. Wait...I don't even WEAR socks! That's kind of bogus...but I know there are other things that need organized. I could clean the living room, but that will only remind me of all the bodacious babes out on the beach. Why would cleaning remind me of bodacious babes, you ask? Well, EVERYTHING reminds me of bodacious babes! HA! Harry: James Edwards... [Tommy swings more vigorously so that he can get closer to his brother. With each upswing Tommy takes a swing and slaps his brother in the head. Harry doesn't even seem phased by the blows at all...it's as if he's in some sort of weird trance. Tommy just slaps away and giggles like a little boy] Tommy: James Edwards is going to be Tommy's Bitch! Harry: James Edwards... [As Tommy goes for the killing blow the ankle supports give way and Tommy slams onto the hard wood floor on the living room] Tommy: OW!! [Harry looks down at his fallen brother, crumbled up in a ball] Harry: James Edwards... Tommy: Dude...I'm totally going to take that guy out now.... Fade to Black.
