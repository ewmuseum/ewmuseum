Hunted Saga Part 17: The Final Round up pt1

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/4/02 Fleming was not surprised at being asked to come to the Military Base just outside of Chicago, Illinois, after all it had been the best part of three months and he hadn’t even come close to capturing Crichton. He also suspected that General Tranter had figured out about Dan Lyons, a tragic mistake on Fleming’s part. He should never have trained him up and supplied him with all the up to date weaponry, he should have realised that the hate burning inside of Lyons’ soul was to great for him to over come. Fleming in effect had give him the means to reap revenge, all that Lyons had to do was follow through and Fleming had no doubt that he would. He had also seen recent reports of a confrontation between the rich Industrialist and Lyons himself, and Crichton was supposedly involved in the situation. Fleming flashed his clearance pass at the base security and then carefully listened to the parking instructions for the soldier at the front gate, not that he needed to, he had been here once before and he knew exactly where General Tranter’s office was. Fleming carefully steered his black Mustang into the appropriate parking space. Fleming takes his keys out of the ignition and opens the Mustang door. He steps out onto the cold, concrete floor and scans the area a little, for reason in particular, more instinct than anything else. He then enters the building through the heavy glass doors and makes his way to General Tranter’s familiar receptionist. “I’m Mr. Fleming…” He begins. “Yes, we have been expecting you, go right on into the office.” The receptionist interrupts. Fleming simply shrugs this off and enters General Tranter’s office; however, once he gets inside he gets a shock of a lifetime. Standing in various places around, admiring the lavish office, were the well-known group of super-hero’s who call themselves The Thunderbolts, hero’s who sell their services to the public. Fleming could guess what was going on here and he didn’t like it one bit. He recognised the Thunderbolt members Mach 1 who was Idly looking out the offices extravagant sized window, the massive frame of Atlas however, was blocking most of the sunlight, Songbird was talking to Moonstone in the far corner of the room, while sat on the desk Jolt and the leader of the group Hawkeye, simply look on as Fleming enters the room and closes the door behind him. “Where is General Tranter?” Fleming asks, not being daunted one bit by the six hero’s for hire before him. A man wearing the full army regulation wear of a general, steps up from behind the dark brown mahogany desk and takes a few steps closer to Fleming. “General Tranter is no longer in charge of this project anymore Mr. Fleming. I am. General Bernstein. In fact General Tranter is no longer in the United States Army anymore.” General Bernstein informs. Mr. Fleming steps back in shock at this announcement. “Why? What happened?” he questioned. General Bernstein smiles a little. “It’s ironic really, the man left in charge of an AWOL case, going AWOL himself.” Bernstein reflects. “Tranter is AWOL?” Fleming asks, not wanting to believe this statement. “Yes I’m as surprised as you, but when the guys up top decided that he was no longer effective in bringing to justice this AWOL case, he decided to go AWOL himself. We believe that he is independently trying to catch the prey to prove that he can.” Bernstein explains. “So they’re basically throwing me off the case?” Fleming replies, the slightest hint of anger showing in his voice. Bernstein nods ion disapproval. “I don’t know what they think Fleming, but I believe that you are good at what you do. However, I also think that this John Crichton is very good and possibly a greater force maybe required to stop him.” Bernstein informs. Fleming sighs. “I guess that’s where these guys come in, eh?” Fleming questions already knowing the answer, but wanting confirmation of his suspicions. “Indeed. The United States Army has hired the very capable Thunderbolts to capture Crichton. They should have all the firepower they need to take him. Tell me, is the tracking device you attached to his car still there?” Bernstein further inquires. Fleming’s mind wanders slightly to the very first time that he encountered Crichton back at the bank robbery. It was there he implanted the tracking device upon Crichton’s car… Flashback… “I’m from a special government agency, hired to track you down John. Now come quietly and no blood will be spilt.” Fleming replies. Crichton nods, “Ah right!” Crichton simply says as he knocks the direction of the barrel of the gun away from him, then whacks Fleming across the face. Fleming stumbles backwards momentarily in shock at the speed of Crichton’s attack. Crichton follows it up by booting Fleming’s wrist, forcing Fleming’s gun out of his hands. Crichton then plants a knee into Fleming’s gut before, ramming his face into the metal boot of his white car. Fleming doesn’t get up from this, but Crichton doesn’t wait to find out and gets into the driving seat of the car and starts the engine. Fleming pushes himself up off the ground and slumps over the boot of the car. The car then begins to move and in a second the car moves away at speed. Fleming simply slumps to the floor on his back. A smile grows upon his face and it slowly develops to a maniacal laugh, as the camera fades to black. It was at this very point in time, Fleming implanted the homing bug upon Crichton’s car. He had to assume that Crichton hadn’t discovered the bug, because each and every time they’d followed the bug they had found Crichton close by. Fleming also figured that Crichton had to be getting a little suspicious, he must at least be wondering why people were finding him over and over again. Fleming himself had given the appropriate frequency of the tracking device to Lyons and he had assumed that Tranter had given the frequency to Ms. Zemoff, because she had a tendency to find Crichton too. “But I take it that I’m out of business now?” Fleming further inquires. “I’m afraid so Mr. Fleming, but if you give me the frequency for the tracking device you placed on Crichton’s car I’ll give you the money that the army owes you now. $500 000. Bernstein bargains. Fleming hears a clear whistle to suggest that $500 000 was a lot of money, which of course it was. This placed Fleming in a dilemma indeed. On one hand he could use the money, but on the other hand he had professional pride. He had wanted to capture the ever-elusive John Crichton himself, using his own merits. However, the point of this was, he could take the $500 000, but nobody said he couldn’t hunt Crichton still. Fleming smiled. “I think we have an agreement!” He declared, bringing an equally big smile appear upon Bernstein’s face. ***** The punch bag situated in the middle of the gym, which is based in the Firestar Center in Cincinnati, was not the best Crichton had ever used, but it was adequate for the purposes he required. He had left Aeryn in the locker room to sleep off the strenuous series of events, which had taken over their lives recently. In theory he should be resting up too, but he felt like taking some of his anger and his frustrations out upon the punch bag. It was just one of his ways to keep the dark impulses raging within him under control; at least for the time being. During the time of the Inner Battle, which was undoubtedly ravaging his body at that very moment, he had come to figure that anger was the vent, in which the dark impulses used to escape to his consciousness. At first he thought this as an hindrance, but looking back at the events that forced these impulses to his consciousness, they had helped him out of a few sticky situations. His mind was undecided, whether to embrace these impulses or continue to fight and repel the impulses. However, he feared that his match tonight may force him to decide this prematurely, because he would undoubtedly get angry and itch to gain revenge on all three of his opponents. Orpheus Grant and Karl LeBlanc mercilessly attacked Crichton after his match a week last Friday against Kramer Wild Wing and as a result Crichton lost the match and was beaten to a bloody pulp. The way Crichton saw it, what comes around goes around, and tonight on a level playing field; a situation where the likely hood is he won’t get triple teamed, he had his chance to reap his revenge. Just the merest thought of revenge made his blows upon the punch bag impacted harder. The rest of the people in the room look around in astonishment, as Crichton’s training schedule becomes a little distracting to the other people, to say the least. Crichton stops as a hand gently rests upon his shoulder. He’d already decided that if this was Miyagawa, he was simply going to attack. As he had heard of his effeminate ways from various worrying sources [payback’s a bitch ain’t it]. Crichton quickly turns to see Aeryn standing next to him. “Oh, hey honey, how’s it going?” Crichton inquires. “Fine. Your hitting those punch bags a little too hard and people have been complaining.” Aeryn informs “Really?” Crichton questions generally surprised. “Yeah, I think you’d better rest up before your big match tonight, don’t you?” Aeryn prompts. “I suppose so. I need all the energy I can get to reap my revenge!” He states, heading towards the exit of the gym.
