Money Pt. III

25 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25 Feb 03 Jade did it. After five minutes of serially hanging up on the PCW secretary she gave her name. Thankfully the woman already knew who she was from her runs in the DWO. An Interview was arranged for the next day, which went successfully and her application was announced triumphant. Jade was to be booked as soon as Tuesday, a little extra surprise for her was that she would be facing an old friend. That old friend turned out to be Lacuna Debris. Out of the other three ladies involved in that match that competitor worried her greatest. The other two didn’t even seem to matter. As soon as Jade got the phone call she went upstairs to her dungeonesque room where her most intense training of all came under light. Yet she just sat on her ass with her knees to her chest and her chin resting on her knees. God it has been so long since I have even been near her. It is so long since I have been able to smell her expensive shampoo float near my sense of smell. Her elegant clothing in plain sight as her taught frame moves backwards and forwards as she beats the snot out of her opponents. Lacuna haunts me like a ghost from the past, violently shaking me in my dreams to the very groundwork of my own temple. She is a bad dream which is going to haunt me until I get away from her that’s what should happen but I know perhaps better than most that getting away from her isn’t some thing anyone could get away with everyday. I’ve had a fruitful and busy past with Lacuna. The Tag Team Tournament is the thing which brought us closer, after my infernally idiotic ‘friend’ Angelus Sorrow asked her to fill into his place. What a total surprise that was to see her wrestling in my corner. I remember that match like it was just yesterday. I told her that this wasn’t where the refreshments were held, I laughed nervously as she ignored me and turned her head. When she started the match off I was utterly gob smacked as she floated around the ring like a bloody fairy. It was a devastating defeat but things begun to go into line once we got onto the same page. Once we realised that when we got back into that tournament things were going to change, they did. She helped me out with a lot of things on a more personal level. It took me a while to figure out how to get her interest but once I did it was easy. All I had to do to get her to help me deal with my rat bastard of a cousin was to act like it was in her interest to help me. It was really as she is such a training freak, I mean it is like she is obsessed with making the already huge physique of hers expand. She’ll soon rival that monster Callisto Bane! She helped me to get the club, which I guess is officially half hers. Not that I’ll tell her that, Jesus the last thing I need right now is that interfering busy body staking her claim, I bet that bitch would do it just to annoy me! That Prime Time Central challenge match was terrible. My newly broken fingers were all over the place and she kicked my ass! Ha! That won’t play any difference when she trotted down to the fatal four way match up. I am determined! What will happen is there is going to be a whole lot of determination, well when I can be bothered getting off my ass and training. Stupid training, what good does it do? I mean other than the health. Then there are the other people who’ll be ‘sitting in on this match.’ I say sitting in because I just know all the action is going to be coming from my fine self slamming her head off of the mat. I’ll show her what it means to have the Blood Lust. Jade rolls onto her hands and her bruised knees, grunting as she does so. She stands on her feet and rises into a fighting position, her fists screwed into balls she walks over to a wooden board spread on two stools. The chairs are bent slightly. Her hand is free from her cast and taped up tightly. Jade begins pounding downwards on the wooden beam, the chairs moving more than the actual beam does. She beats down on the wood again. This was the first and often the most painful part of her training. Usually she just left most of these to her other Job, you know what with her being a kleptomaniac she kept herself on her toes with her often unprofessional approach made her run the extra mile while she was on the job. The chairs buckled under the pressure of an axe kick. “Stupid chairs, always breaking on me. Stupid chairs” She grumbled to herself. Jade got distracted by a noise on the balcony. She nearly tripped over herself as she scrambled out of the room. She hopped down the stairs leading to the second floor and opened the glass double doors leading to her veranda. A black figure dives of the balcony and into the street with a great example of etiquette and grace. “Oi!” Jade shouted after them. It was definitely the body of a male, Jade thought to herself. “Perhaps it was Lacuna!” She giggled to herself, pushing the worry to the back of her mind for a change. Jade slinked into a leaning position and rested on her elbows against the metal railing. Jade looked up at the orange sky full of the wonder of the night and smiled, this was the first time she had managed to relax in a day. I imagine what will happen in the future. How things will turn out with Lacuna. Perhaps when I beat her down to the ground she will be able to swallow her pride and accept a hand shake. If not then I could always beat her back down. I doubt there will be any more showing of Blood Lust in the future. Unless she wants, no! No! NO! The last time she just bailed on me after we suffered our second loss. Bah screw her! She was the weaker link. I think she was the weaker link. We did get through to the quarter or semi finals I don’t really remember it was a hectic time for me. That was when I decided that I needed a change for myself. To stop pesky things like grey hairs getting me before my time has come. There were a few things in this world I have ever found calming. My parents, bless their souls, Switchblade, at one point, Lacuna, yeah there’s a shocker, Carys, baby face, a darling who should be here, and this view. This view was magnificent, seeing other people chasing their tails in the great hustle and bustle this place seemed to reflect. The roof tops empty, unclouded, so high up all your troubles seem so far away. I seem to float like a queen looking down on her mighty empire. One day this will all be mine. Looking over Pennsylvania you’d see the cars in a rush pumping out their smelly fumes. They were beeping noisily on their way. The sunset simply capped off the view as a slight purple reflection changes the mood. It gives Jade another boost of adrenaline. She gets a little hyped as she paces back and forward tapping her fingers on her thighs as she does so. She is enthralled in peace which is an entirely new experience for her. The silence does, however bring back a painful thought, Money. Will things change for her? Will PCW really make that much of a difference in her accounts? Nathaniel expects so and she thinks he knows his business. If it did would that mean she would not have to be so illegally involved in things? She doesn’t think she would be able to give up the thrill even if she could. Given the choice she would rather re-break her fingers. Another thought makes way in the front of her head. Her little protégé was missing again. “Where are you Carys? I need you here with me when I go through these tough times and I need you to be safe. I know I can’t concentrate on mostly anything when I don’t have you here. I swear if this is that ‘boy friend’ who is keeping you away then I will deal with him. Oh yes he will have a whole lot of Jade to deal with. It could be fun.” Jade sighs as she walks back into the building, pulling the door closed with her as she does so. Lost in thought. -[Fade]-
