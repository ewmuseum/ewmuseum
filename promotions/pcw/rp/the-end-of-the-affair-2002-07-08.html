The End of the Affair

8 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8th July 2002 It is a nice relaxing Sunday in the Miyagawa household. Well; the Asylum Gym... although I guess that is rather the Miyagawa household after all. Not that it really matters. Anyway, back to the subject at hand... it’s a rather slow day here in the Miyagawa “household”; Natalia appears to be out at the moment and Alexander himself is lounging around in front of the television set while wearing a black t-shirt and a pair of black shorts... and no socks. He’s got his feet up on a stool in front of the couch he’s sitting on and is enjoying a frosty beverage in a glass, held in one hand. On the television screen is shown what appears to be am episode of Stargate SG-1, in fact it must be because an Asgard just turned up on screen talking to Colonel O’Neil. Suddenly onto the screen comes Natalia wearing a jacket and carrying a bag in here hand... Miyagawa sees her and immediately swings his legs off of the chair and sits upright in much better posture. Miyagawa: Good day, darling? Natalia looks at her husband suspiciously and then sees the foot marks on the foot stool. Natalia: Don’t use that for a foot stool! How many times do I have to tell you that? Miyagawa sighs, knowing now that she did in fact notice. Miyagawa: But it’s a foot stool. What else exactly are we meant to be using it for? Natalia places her shopping bag on the floor and grabs the foot stool and places it on it’s side... showing that it is in fact some piece of Russian art that Miyagawa hadn’t noticed before. So much so, that Miyagawa looks slightly shocked at what it is... although he’s not sure exactly what it is, but it’s defiantly not a foot stool. Miyagawa: Oh sorry. I didn’t know. Honestly. Natalia: Appearances can be deceptive... why on Earth do you think I went and married you? Natalia sticks out her tongue at him and Miyagawa chuckles to her quick joke there. Miyagawa: So what have you been buying then? Natalia: Well I remember you mentioning that you didn’t have any footage of the End in action, and that might put you at a disadvantage on Tuesday because he’s the only person with the build... how did you describe it... Miyagawa: Lanky bastard. Natalia: Da, Lanky bastard. So I went out and got you his videos. Miyagawa: He actually has videos? Natalia pulls a video out of the bag and looks at it for a second before handing it to Miyagawa. The camera zooms in on the video... it’s a copy of “The End of the Affair” starring... well... let’s just say that PCW’s The End isn’t in it, nor was involved in the production of it. Miyagawa looks at what he thought was a footstool and then at the video and begins to smell a set up... then looks at his wife and works out that if he accused a women with only five weeks to go of her pregnancy left of such a setup, it might not be good for his health... in the fact that she might just try to poison him. Miyagawa: **Looks at video** Erm... **looks at wife** thanks. Natalia: My pleasure... I got a few more as well. Miyagawa: **pretending to be containing the excitement in his voice** Really? Natalia: Oh yeah, they were having a sale of these in the video store. Miyagawa: **Once again pretending/saving his life** Now why would they want to go and do that then? Natalia: You know, that’s what I thought. Natalia hands him another video, this time a copy of Arnold Schwarzenegger’s “End of Days”. Miyagawa: **This time completely honestly** Ooh, actually I’ve seen this one... and actually I kinda liked it. I thought it was one of his better films. Natalia: Who? The End? Miyagawa: No, Arnold Schwarzenegger. Always wanted to meet him... I wonder how often a day he has to say “I’ll Be Back”? Natalia: Why would he say that at all? Miyagawa: It’s his catchphrase. Natalia: Oh... anyway, I got this as well... I think I found his first name with this one. She hands him a copy of “Howard’s End”. Miyagawa nearly groans then remember the whole “not upsetting the wife” thing and looks rather pleased. Miyagawa: How many more of these did you get? Natalia: only another four or five... And so we leave Alexander Miyagawa as he relives his own personal video hell... oh, and Natalia in a few minutes is going to suggest that they watch them... all. Now. I certainly don’t want to stick around for twenty hours of bad movies, do you?
