Clinching

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Clinching Featuring: Tim Walden Date: November 5, 2002 Victory has come back into focus..The will to reign supreme pulling back into synchronicity with the means to do so, as the Front Office dodges yet another bullet from the Trapezoid's efforts to cause a rift.. First, he costs us a World Title shot from Duke's decency to have a little concern for his co-worker, then going and running in on a perfectly good match to hurt a referee and distract a competitor, leading to the near-end of our tag title hopes before they started! But still we move forward, unhalted by the poor sportsmanship of our opposition, the Front Office unified... The shifts of attitude since Friday had taken my hopes from relative certainty to a sense of hopelessness for a time, feeling the doom of abandonment, that even I would struggle to defeat two people at once, especially that lousy cheating four-sided shape and the Southern bitch, Teresa Tomas, and I would be left to feel the stinging agony of defeat and its repercussions stretching for a full 30 days..The agony of the lingering uncertain failure to come had ground at my mind for a few hours then, but I should have had more confidence that Brandi would come around and realize that there was nothing to be gained from her grudge, after all it was the rational thing to do, and who would be more likely to act rationally than an executive? Now that we were back on fair terms, we couldn't be stopped so easily as the HIT had been planning on. It would take everything they had to come close against us, and tomorrow night, close isn't good enough to win..We have the advantage on nearly every side of the battle, each separate aspect working in our favour to improve our hopes yet further and keep them underdogs, a label they would have great difficulty shedding to our opinion, no matter the effort.. I could beat the Trapezoid. Even if the first victory was mildly tainted, I know I could still kick his ass three ways from Tuesday when we meet once more in this vital battle, then five more on Friday..However, when he attacks from behind or in other illegal fashion, he always seems to get the upper hand on me..I need to remain watchful for his attacks, as though the man tries to pass himself off as the good guy in all of this purely on the fact that the fans for whatever reason dislike me, he still makes a multitude of behaviours that shame the dignity of the federation with his disobedience of our rules...But perhaps that is why they like him, perhaps they hold such awe-inspiring anger toward us that they would rather ignore the immoral behaviour..Like a man who stands by and cheers while another is being mugged. To exterminate him from PCW is the true aim of my effort at this point, for he has gone from a mere thorn in my side to a full-fledged aggravation, but I have the feeling that I will not be able to get rid of him as easily as I'd like. He is very cunning so far in choosing to make his attacks and seemingly vanish from the building as soon as his work is done, but when confined to the wrestling ring, his skills at sneaking about are severely hampered, and we regain the advantage when the Icoseles Trapezoid cannot be as Hardcore as he needs, while our technical skill remains fully legal..The match's implications are not about him, however, merely hope that he will get injured somewhat to make the Television Title hunt easier.. The match this Friday is one which pits me against several opponents, most likely none of whom have an interest in keeping me in the match..Venneman dislikes me for my actions toward Teresa, the Trapezoid holds disdain for our power as a group, and Teresa's evasion has led to a growing, deepening mutual disrespect...The one man I can possibly trust to aid me is Rush Mason, who is probably aware that should it come down to him and the other three, he would be the one to take the fall, even though his former goons have been given such rich treatment recently by the Front Office in trying to take care of the HIT, and while failing as wrestlers still being obviously against him and Teresa..Yet he is against them, and against Venneman who is thought to be on the same side. The alliance system here is confusing, but I have no other choice for the moment but to trust the man..At least until I hold the Top Rope Temptress under my power. Teresa Tomas, the thorn firmly entrenched in my side for the past month, who has had the gall to not merely reject the taxation but to constantly try to get one up on me, breaking her agreement in an attempt to harm my manhood and knock me out, trapping me in my office without the majority of my clothes and humiliating me in front of Brandi, and now her decision to work with the Trapezoid for only their good, ignoring the needs of the Front Office while using him to chicken out of a fair and honest singles match full knowing that it would only prove my superiority. The hopes of her and the HIT against us as a team aren't that much greatened, but his sly maneuvering is enough to lose the battle, as I learned Friday in what could reasonably have been an ending situation to the match, though with Bob calling the match it would be much less likely..Should it happen again, though, I would be powerless, owned by another, a position I cannot bear the thought of... I would be the lackey again, that in itself a bothersome idea, but this time in the employ not of someone whom I could use to my own personal gain, instead that of one who holds a great contempt for me in Tomas..With her deciding what I could and could not wear and giving the orders, I would be something entirely new to my knowledge, a powerless man in an executive position, unable to do what was best for me and having to relive my humilation for 30 days of PCW television, knowing she would undoubtedly want to shame me in front of the world-wide audience...This was a lot to lose at once, and I would not take the risk of becoming the laughing stock of wrestling in this fashion if it was any greater than necessary ...I need a plan, some way to win the match not just with the 97 percent chance that Brandi and I had with Bob on our side, but with absolute certainty to take victory over the evaders and make her wish she'd just paid the taxes in the first place... ...The only question is how? ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
