Rush Mason? Nay...Mr. Helpful!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: #2 FF “I hate white people, I hate black people, and I really hate blind people!” This would be the somewhat usual opening to a Rush Mason promo, some sort of whining statement that involves being better than other people. Of course the scene hasn’t faded into clear view yet, but we can tell that Rush is complaining, as usual. “It’s not like I hate people….I just hate the fact that they suck.” That’s the kind of loving statement that is usually expressed by Rush when referring to people other than himself. The scene finally fades in, as we see Rush sitting in some sort of waiting room, talking basically to himself, although several other unknown people sit in the room as well. “Naaaaayxt!” The word ‘next’ is stretched out for three syllables from the other room, a loud Spanish female voice shouting out, as one of the people in the room with Rush walks into the next room. Rush continues to sit, irritably, looking around the room for some sort of amusement. Rush: I can’t believe this! I’m being treated like a fucking second-rate nimrod regular citizen! Meh…I wonder what it’s like to have flaws and stuff? Rush’s near-shouting has attracted some of the attention of others in the room, but they just mumble under their breath something about him being a psycho and return to their waiting with a nice Field & Stream magazine from 1994, telling how to finally catch that dream bass. However, Rush takes no notice and just continues to think out loud. Rush: I bet I’m sitting in a row filled with flawed people! I bet that guy has some weirdo terminal illness! She’s infertile! He’s got a penile implant! Screaming penile implant will usually get you attention, and it doesn’t fail for the Voodoo Villain, as now the whole room throws hurried confused looks towards him, but of course, being his lovely naïve little self, he still doesn’t pay them any mind. However, the confusion in the room subsides for a moment as the screeching sound comes again. “Naaaaayxt!” Everyone, including Rush, covers their ears this time, trying not to hear the nails on a chalkboard voice. However, it only takes a few seconds for another person to get up and head into the next room, as the person from before walks out. Rush: I think I’m getting flawkemia! I COULD GET A FLAW! I can feel myself sinking into normality! And of course, now everyone is watching Rush’s spaz attack, not even bothering to disguise their amazement. Rush is beginning to sweat profusely, many beads sliding down his shaved head. However, before Rush can completely lose control of himself, we hear the inevitable… “Naaaaayxt!” Once again, every ear in the room is covered immediately, as the screeching voice hits. Even the last woman before Rush to arrive covers her ears as she heads into the room, while a sorrowful looking man leaves, holding a briefcase. Rush’s eyes search all around the room. One man sitting near Rush whispers to him, trying not to let others hear. “Buddy…chill out!” Unfortunately, this is not exactly what Rush wants to do, and he begins shrieking at the man who just tried to console him in a small way. Rush: Do you think Bon Dieu chills when he is about to sink into a land of plague?! Do you think that the Lights of Loa chill when they’re about to enter a fetid wasteland!? Of course, this doesn’t make sense really, but Rush doesn’t think of this as he starts to act like a madman, but luckily, he is saved from life in a straight jacket by the most unlikely thing. “Naaaaayxt!” The evil voice causes him to cover his ears, but fortunately, he gets to leave the waiting room and heads into the room where the voice of death is coming from. He opens the door, and sees an office-like room, where a middle-aged Spanish woman with glasses and long pink fake fingernails is sitting, playing with her hair. “I assume you Mista Rutch Masone?” Rush tries to smile as he walks in, but then his smile fades as her Hispanic accent butchers his name and he just throws a glare at her as he sits down. She looks him over for a moment, then doesn’t bother with a handshake, but introduces herself. “I’m the help lady, what can I help ju with?” Her Hispanic accent makes the word you sound strange, but her English isn’t bad, although Rush would probably describe it somewhat like “butchering of the language worse than a household pet could do.” However, Rush, for once is a tiny bit polite. Rush: Yeah….I’m Rush, and I came here about a job application. Rush spits the word Rush, glaring at her, but she doesn’t pay attention at all. However, it doesn’t make very much sense for Rush Mason, PCW wrestler, to be looking for a job, but Rush isn’t exactly Mr. Sensible. The ‘help lady’ responds quickly. Help Lady: I’m sawry, but we’re not takin’ applications right now, sir. She begins to get up and show him the door, but Rush quickly cuts her off from the action by spitting out a rebuttal. Rush: Well…I see that you don’t have a Mr. Helpful… The lady gives him a blank look, as though he just told her how babies are made, and continues to play with her hair, as Rush waits for her to pick up what he has been saying, but he ends up having to suggest it yet again. Rush: I think it would be great if there were a Mr. Helpful to assist these fuckta…erm…citizens in need. The women looks like she’s thinking for a second, but then hits her head with a fingernail, and blurts out to Rush the obvious. Help Lady: Ju want to be the Mr. Helpful? Rush nods solemnly, and follows this with his explanation for why, which better be good, because there are not a lot of people who want Rush analyzing their problems. Rush: Seeing as I am the Future of PCW and a tremendous specimen of an elite human being, I could probably gain some mindless servan…erm…give valuable help to people. The help lady looks at Rush with the same blank look, and yet again, takes a few seconds to process the information. She looks at her hair for a moment, then to Rush, and then back to her hair, and puts a finger up, having yet another brilliant observation. Help Lady: Ju want to be the Mr. Helpful? Rush tries to smile, but his teeth are gritted underneath, as he continues to explain to the ‘help lady,’ who seems surprisingly unhelpful for someone who is paid to be the opposite of that. Rush: See…I am what you would call ‘El Supremo.’ I’m a prime example of a first-rate guy. I’m not your average nimrod, and I really need to get some slaves around my house, and people who have problems can’t think for themselves, so I can just think for them! Unfortunately, the help lady is performing the daunting task of looking out the window at every car that passes, and of course can’t be stopped for a little thing like human contact. Rush: You know what?! I can’t take this! You second-rate plebeians are all the same! I can’t interact with you without feeling like some sort of brain-damaged amoeba! Do you know how hard it is to make a genius like me feel flawed like you?! With this, Rush storms out of the room, fuming that he can’t be Mr. Helpful, and the help lady realizes about a minute later that he is gone, looking around shocked, as the scene fades out, but not before the help lady has a startling epiphany. “Ju want to be the Mr. Helpful?
