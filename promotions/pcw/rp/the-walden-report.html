Title: The Walden Report Featuring: Tim Walden Date: Nov.19 Several days had passed..The strategy had held firm so far. But still, there was another test to come, and not in the least the most out-of-sight of his concerns. The win, for once, was not the thing. He could afford to take the loss and declare it a mere lack of effort more than he could afford to win the semifinal berth, if he let her catch him once again... Tim looked out the window of his hotel room with a stressed sigh, rather less relaxed than he was on Fusion. The work that went into taking his Front Office duties on the road for the time being was an aggravation, but it was worth it to be sure to keep his freedom, even if it left a muddle of papers on his bed for the majority of the day...As long as the housekeeping staff came in and took care of the room every day, things would be sort of close to being livable, and it wouldn't be long anyway, just another 16 days and he could go back to the office. However, for the time being, he was left with no other option but to stay out of sight and avoid the obvious, hoping that the damn Trapezoid wouldn't go ruining things for him on Turmoil. Probably a rather hopeless hope after what he did on Fusion...If the first run-in hadn't caught the ire of the shape, the work he'd done against him for his tournament match would surely have grabbed his attention. You just don't throw buckets of blue paint on a guy and brutalize him with a chair and get no anger out of the man ...Or at least one would think, yet from what had been heard of his ranting since, he seemed more concerned with spreading propaganda about Xavier than anything regarding Walden in the past few days...Perhaps it could be seen as arrogance on his part, perhaps just impatience in his war against the Front Office..Either way, it was evident from Tim's expression that he was not pleased with recent events, as he turned back from the window and pulled the miniature tape recorder from his pocket.. Now was as good a time for a bulletin as ever. 'Good morning, PCW fans. It is November 19, 2002, and I am as always Tim Walden, speaking from my usual secret location on day 14 of the thirty days of being Teresa Tomas's valet, and only the seventh day in my 23 Days of Strategic Avoidance Initiative. As you may know, tonight this initiative hits a major snag, as my booking in the tournament has situated me in the position where the necessity of winning my match with Boca del Inferno to not look like a laughingstock has worked against me even in victory, having to meet Teresa one-on-one in the ring and all.' 'However, rest assured fair and honest wrestling fans...' A smirk made its presence known, almost realized in his delivery. '...She will lose. And she will lose badly. When I get through with that overhyped bimbo Tomas, there won't be anyone left to give me orders, and there will be no confidence remaining in my other opponent for the night once they have had the discomfort of watching me throw her to the mat like a rag doll, then slap on No Evasion and give her exactly that. No evasion from her suffering....No hope to get away from the months of her blatant disregard for the Front Office's wishes and the good of PCW...No mercy, for I am no longer sure whether it would be best to let go if the match ends and avoid the disqualification...' 'I will hold onto her until the referee has to pry me off if needed. I will show her the edge of destruction, I will show her how close she is to having her bones shatter at my hands if need me, I will put the fear of the Front Office into her...Of course, that's what I would do in most cases.' Tim's grin suddenly faded slightly, as he looked back around at the papers scattered around the room, a simple but effective reminder of his situation... 'However, there is the issue of her power over me. I must have a little caution going into this match, for should she manage to remove my in-match earplugs, designed for concentration, she would be able to give me orders again, compromising the mission's integrity. I must keep her in check while managing to avoid the inevitable offensive of the Trapezoid, in a proper preparation for Alliances. However, I have faith that with the Front Office on my side, and Miss Wilson especially holding personal issue with the two evaders, I will not have to worry about any sort of interference against me that won't be nipped in the bud by PCW's crack security squadrons and my teammates, and thus I have all reason to believe that I can win my match as well as to defeat whichever of the two possible semifinal opponents I have who cannot compete fairly without another man in their corner, all without failing to maintain my security regarding Tomas. This concludes the Walden report for November 19, 2001, morning edition, and in representation of the Front Office, I bid all who hold support for what we have done for PCW the warmest parting possible. From France..I believe..Adieu.' A click of the stop button...Another daily job well done... Too bad there was still the taxes to take care of. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
