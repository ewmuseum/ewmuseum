The Publicity Stunt Part Two: Ninja's aren't part of the act!

12 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/6/03 The whole entertainment deck of the PCW Airship was buzzing with action, as civilians, wrestlers and superstars alike poured into the room, hurrying to take their seats. Warrior pulled the curtain back a little and popped his head around to look at his audience. “Oh shit, oh shit, oh SHIT!” he exclaimed, nervously. Lusty Linda has arranged the publicity stunts months ago without bothering to mention it to Warrior nor Blind Rage and the stunts would all end in disaster. It was like a calling card to every criminal in the world to come and have a pop at them for no reason. It was worse when they had that bounty on their by the ‘Outrageous’ Owl-Man. The only difference was the criminals actually knew where to look this time. What made things worse was the crowd. Celebrities, who had seen it all and could do much better themselves would soon watch him perform various karate manoeuvres, using his deadly laser axes. And when he looked at the audience through the curtain his fears were doubled. “Jackie…freakin’….Chan’s in the audience!” He added, to anyone who would listen. Linda stubbed out a cigarette on the side of the wall and walked over to Warrior. She placed a concerned hand upon his shoulder in an effort to calm him down. “Calm down!” She said. “It’ll be alright!” Warrior glared at her uneasily. “How will it be ‘alright’? I’m going out there to perform martial arts moves to Jakie Chan…who quite frankly would kick my ass!” Linda sighed. “But he doesn’t have laser axes though, does he!” “But he could easily beat the shit out of me and take them” “Stop being such a baby and get out there!” Warrior wished that he had been crushed by a car, like Blind Rage, and forced to stay in his cabin by Linda, despite there actually being nothing wrong with him. Warrior sighed and walked through the curtains and onto the stage. * * * * * It was night time. They were at one with the night; their home was with the shadows; their very being bred for murder. The PCW airship was the target, and they reached their target with minimal effort. Their ship was cloaked to the airship’s sensors and so it was relatively easy for them to slip onboard. They were members of The Hand, a group of deadly ninja assassins from Japan. They were here to take out Justice Inc, starting with the one named Warrior. The seven of them pierced through the decks of the ship, until coming across the superhero in the entertainment deck. They were quite amused to see Warrior performing various Martial Arts manoeuvres. They went in for the kill. * * * * * Warrior had just performed an excellent round house back kick, and the audience clapped as a resultant. Warrior suspected that they were doing so just to humour him, but that didn’t deter his performance in the slightest. He was just rounding things up, when it happened. The doors to the entertainment deck burst open and five or six ninja’s burst in, wearing red costumes. The crowd cheered, believing it to be just another part of the show, but Warrior hadn’t known about this. Warrior glanced at the side of the stage where Linda stood. She glared right back at him and shrugged. She didn’t know who they were either, which meant only one thing; these really were ninja’s. With a variety of summersaults, flips and elaborate cart wheels, all of the ninja’s made it to the stage; they almost surrounded Warrior in an arc-like shape, about a meter or so away from him. “You…Warrior…of Justice Inc…prepare to die…so says The Hand.” One of ninja’s declared. The Hand? Warrior recognised that name. It was a guild of ninja’s who were nothing more than mercenaries. Deadly ones at that. This was going to be tough. “What the hell do you want me for?” Warrior asked in response. “We have received orders to kill you. There is a bounty on your head…I’m sure you’re aware of this!” “Oh great. More mercenaries out for the kill.” The crowd cheered for what they though was all part of show and Warrior noticed Jackie Chan giving the two thumbs up for the performance. Warrior sighed, he was about to get his assed kicked, but at least the audience liked him. If it wasn’t one thing…it was another. The first ninja attacked, attempting to hit a powerful looking kick to Warrior’s chest. Warrior blocked the kick with his left hand and cracked the ninja on the jaw with his right. The ninja stumbled backwards falling into some of the other ninja’s by accident. The audience applause. A ninja to Warrior’s left hit a flying kick, which caught him on the left side of cheek, sending him stumbling to the right. The audience boo the ninja. The ninja comes in for another attempt, kicking at Warrior, but Warrior managed to kick the leg away with his left foot, then hits a thrusting kick to the ninja’s chest with his right, sending the ninja toppling backwards. The audience cheer again. Then one of the other ninja’s smoothly withdraws a sword from their sheath and charged. Warrior quickly extracted both laser axes, blocked the initial lunge with metal axe frame in his right hand, flicked the ‘on’ switch on the base of the laser axe in his left hand, and then sliced the sword in half. However, this left Warrior open for a punch from the ninja’s other hand, which sent Warrior reeling backwards. The audience boo. Warrior stops himself from falling off the side of the stage, by propping himself up against the wall, however, he then stumbles near the edge of the stage, as the ninja charges at him. Warrior ducks and hits a back body drop, sending the ninja off the side of the stage and straight through a table. The audience cheer. The two ninja’s who were unintentionally knocked down in the initial attack, get back to their feet and menacingly stalk Warrior. The ninja on his left attacked with a thrusting punch, Warrior blocked and pushed him backwards, as the ninja on the right attempted much the same manoeuvre. Warrior blocked the second ninja’s attack and pushed him backwards as well. The audience gasp. Warrior dives through the gap between the two ninja’s, hits a forward roll as he hits the wooden stage floor and then leaps back to his feet, once again ready to fight. He retracts the laser axe sabre on both of his axes and opts to use the steel handles as weapons to handle his foes. The first ninja charged, Warrior sidestepped him, and then hit him, hard, on the back with his steel axe handle, sending him flying off the other side of the stage, forward flipping through another table. The audience applaud. The last ninja charged at him in much the same fashion, except this time, Warrior hit him in the gut with one the left handed axe handled and then clobbered him on the back of the neck with the right one. The last ninja fell to the floor unconscious. The audience gave him a standing ovation. Warrior glanced at them, a little out of breathe, a little disturbed at the audience not realising the threat was real and a little surprised that he managed to take out so many ninjas. Quickly, Linda made her way up the stairs and onto the stage, stepping over an unconscious ninja along the way. She took the mic from the stand and waited for the audience to stop applauding. “That was Warrior, from the superhero team of Justice Inc, wrestling aboard this very airship this Friday!” The crowd cheered again.
