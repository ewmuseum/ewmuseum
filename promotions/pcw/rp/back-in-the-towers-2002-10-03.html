Back in the Towers

3 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/3/2002 *The scene opens up inside of Kano Towers, located about three miles due east of the legendary strip of Jon Kano's hometown of Las Vegas, Nevada. As the camera begins to show Jon Kano's office for the first time in close to a year, Kano is seen at his desk, relaxing a bit with his leather, executive style chair reclined and his feet up on his desk. As the scene zooms in to focus on Jon Kano, it shows Kano's vast collection of memorabilia of his own career. It shows the pictures of Kano wrestling in the Tokyo Dome against a large Oriental man (obviously prior to his career in PCW). It then shows a picture of Jon Kano shaking hands with then President Clinton. Finally, the scene comes to focus on Kano.* Kano: Well here I am in my favorite place in the world. I have always loved coming back to Vegas. I was born here, I was raised here, I live here with my beautiful wife, and soon I will begin to raise my unborn child. Four months, and I am a father. Now that is a scary thought. Jon Kano, the living legend at the age of 26, and I am going to be jointly responsible for another's life with my wife. I feel bad because I know that I will probably not be there when my child is born, and I know that I might not be there a lot while he or she is growing up, but I pray to God that the child knows that I do love him or her and I do what I do so they never want. I grew up getting my needs met by my father and mother, but I always yearned for the finer things. My child will never need nor want, because his father will be on the road 250 or 300 days out of the year, killing himself to provide for his family while his mother stays at home, taking care of him. *Kano reaches into his desk drawer and pulls out a cigar. He cuts the tip, and puffs on it until it lights.* Kano: Last Friday, on Fusion, I showed everyone that I am back to my old form. I took an able wrestler, Kramer Wild Wing, and damn near crippled him. He will never be the same, I gave him a therapeutic back massage and made him suck canvas. And when I took him off the top rope and KANOPLEXED him straight to hell and felt his body go limp on contact, I had this feeling go through my body. This feeling was pure euphoria, everything in the world was right. Everything made perfect sense. There was no war, no famine, no disease, nothing bad at all. This feeling was better than sex. I felt like I needed a cigarette right then and there. I couldn't talk, I couldn't think, I couldn't do anything except for making the cover. Then this week, Carl Cash steps into the ring with yours truly. Now Carl could be something somewhere, but not now, and not by beating me. I am the Man who Rocketh the Mosteth, and he is just known as That 80s guy. I remember the 80s. The music sucked, the culture sucked, and the whole damn decade sucked. Am I the only one who remembers the crap bands from the 80s like 'Frankie Goes Hollywood' or the infamous 'B-52s'? The 80s were the era of the power ballads and talentless heroin addicts singing about rocking me like a hurricane. Jesus, who in their right mind would want to be known as the 80s guy. Didn't they try a show like that? Didn't it last like six weeks? I am personally surprised that Carl Cash is actually lasted this long, but I am also surprised he is here on his second hurrah, but his second hurrah will be his last and his second hurrah comes to an end this Friday in MY CITY, and that is WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!! *Kano's phone rings and Kano picks it up.* Kano: Yeah, Jon Kano. Hey honey, yeah I'll be home in about an hour, just finishing up a little business and I'll be there. See ya then, love you too. *The scene fades to black as Kano hangs up the phone and puts out his cigar.*
