Theory 101: Skylar Green, Reality, Drake Maxwell, and other matters

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 13-14 of 2003 A.D. So much to cover, so little time, and just where to begin? Typically it’s easy to say start at the beginning, but in this case I don’t think that rule factors in. Besides, beginning is relative. Who’s to say what beginning is? Certainly not me, and especially not you. There actually is no distinction, contrary to what ‘reality’ may tell you. Yes, I’m sure that confuses your feeble, unenlightened little mind. But it won’t in a bit. Stay with me here, child, for I intend on going rather quickly. Skylar Green’s past had been well documented; from his tenures in various lesser wrestling promotions, to his heated rivalries with the strongest of foes, very little of The Artist Formerly Known As ‘The End’ is still an enigma. But nonetheless, I find it troubling that so many points have been neglected and dismissed about the past of the blind man. Whether this is out of memory blackout, ignorance or the sake of stifling outrageous truth from public eye is a topic that could be endlessly scrutinized. But for sake of time, let’s not get into that one, eh? This whole philosophy has incited me into thinking and I have other things to address here. So, as another offbeat theory, could it all be in the endless search for ‘realism’? Now that one requires some reflection. What does humanity know about ‘realism’? It was once said that we exist on a placid island of ignorance and were never meant to journey far, lest we drive ourselves mad with the bleak realities existing outside the comfort-glossed Utopia that is our own society. Some things are simply better left unsolved and shouldn’t be delved by human speculation or research. It’s as prevalent a concept in professional wrestling as it is else where in civilization. And, unfortunately, it’s also all the misbegotten truth. Yep, remember all those times you terrified your children with tales of the boogeyman and monsters in the woods, then hurriedly reassured them that there was no such thing as monsters? How about those tales of evil things composed by such disturbed minds as Edgar Allan Poe, Stephen King, or H.P. Lovecraft that we eventually laugh off knowing of their absurdity? Or better yet, how about that night you spent walking home in the dark along that remote road, all the while feeling that sense of dread inherent in the notion you were being watched by unknown eyes in the trees? You knew there was something there prowling in the shadows, but of course you shrugged it off when you reached the solace of the porch-lights and just attributed the whole scenario to too much of that Nightmare on Elm Street marathon? I could tell you to walk in there right now and kindly inform little Mindy and Tommy that monsters do in fact exist and that their favorite snack is human flesh. I could acquaint you with facts about many of the more notorious masters of the macabre and the mystical visions reported as plaguing their sleeping hours, the very dreams and nightmares that formed the imagery scrawled in their manuscripts; through this means I could relate to you the nightmares inflicted upon one Howard Phillips Lovecraft, pulp writer turned both vessel and reporter of clandestine forces of universal evil. Better yet I could burst your comfortable little bubble by saying that that feeling that chilled your spine in the woods that summer evening was no more your imagination than it was Robert Englund in a felt hat, striped shirt and razor-fingered glove, and that everything you pictured in your imagination wasn’t far from the truth (if not worse). But actually, I won’t tell you any of these things. It’s better this way. It’s more to your advantage to believe you linger near the top of the cosmic food-chain, behind only God, his angels and the (imprisoned, of course!) demons and minions of Hell. It’s human nature to flee your fears and seek sanctuary for protection (hence the impact of religion on all culture). Fight or flight, the intuitive alarm system in us all; it’s nothing to be ashamed of. Unfortunately when Azathoth sired us from raw chaos, he never believed we would go running to some second-rate divinity and a make-shift savior for refuge. A simple flaw in our creation by no fault of his own. Wait, wait……you still believe Jehovah God created us? Pfft, have you not been listening? Well…..I can overlook it on this one occasion. The next time though, you may end up taking a forced glimpse into the true nature of the universe. Daoloth himself will revel in your ensuing madness, trust my words. Anyhow, some people are simply content with their own evaluation of reality. They hear tale spun of Celestials, Great Old Ones, prophecy of Earth’s annihilation buried in folklore, and tomes of horrid knowledge penned by madmen in the distant corners of the Earth. They dismiss it all as insanity, the simple ranting of those poor demented misfits of the social collective. These so-called ‘lunatics’ are locked away in padded rooms as they gibber and speak in obscene tongues originated of dead races, and it’s all just “delusions manufactured in their twisted mind”. Forget warnings of the theological institution and let forces of the unknown be damned. Blame it on psychological regression, shoot serums into his arm, drown him in sedatives, wrap him in a straitjacket. I speak from experience, it’s a farce. Judgment contrived of bitterness you say? Alright, have it your way. How about some history, eh? Friedrich Von Junzt composed Unaussprechlichen Kulten and was deemed mad for its revelations, furthermore cast out as a pariah. For his desire to study those mysteries and blackest of cosmic perceptions which man could not solve through practical means, and his lack of faith in conventional science and logic, one of the world’s greatest theosophists was regarded as an outsider. Alas the infallible disciplines of science and logic failed to determine probable cause for his death following a return from Mongolia when Von Junzt, barred and confined in his lodgings in Dusseldorf, was found dead with the mark of talons around his throat. Alexis Ladeau, after piecing together the mutilated pages of his colleague’s final manuscript and reading their contents, burnt the documents into ash and cut his own throat with a razor. Never read that parable in your history books, huh? Applaud science. Another victory struck for the upkeep of ‘our ‘reality and the suppression of the ‘obscene’ reality. ‘Realism’, my friend, is just a red herring. What you know is a diversion from truth. The Matrix, that little Keanu Reeves diddy made a few years back? That is your life. This is all an illusion; your world is an elaborate hoax produced by proverbial directors and script-writers with identities and forms beyond our comprehension, all brought to life on a stage so immense we have yet to fathom its prodigious depths. Now, bearing all this in mind, what exactly separates the average Duke Williams or Chet King spiel from the documented exploits of this industry’s more ‘bizarre’ and outlandish denizens? Fear and denial don’t typically expel ideas from existence, but some are apparently near-sighted enough to believe so. They’re content in their reality, and who am I to shatter the little Zion where they ignorantly dwell? I sympathize with the common blue-collar workingman as much as the next guy, but hell….mongrels like Williams don’t even bow before the right altar. And you trust that yokel’s views? Sheesh. I should have put that boy out to pasture when I had the opportunity. Perhaps I could have redeemed you in the process. Those imbeciles can continue their verbal attacks from in front of a camera until they get some debilitating form of cancer as far as I’m concerned. I’ll be content with haunting your dreams with what I’ve come to know. The powers of technology mean little in the face of divine influence. Those cameras won’t be attached to their derrieres forever, god forbid. But I’ll be sure to keep you updated on my life as I see fit, and as long as you require sleep, you’re mine for life. How can I do this you may ask? Wipe the miserable tears from your eyes and I’ll tell you. Done? Good. Well, having access to the Library of Celaeno has its advantages, believe you me. Man, you wouldn’t believe some of the things Jehovah God himself made his vassals avoid discussing in the Bible. Trust me, no one would revere ‘His Holiness’ if they knew of his ambitions. I just thank Azathoth I’m not among those irrational fools blindly having dedicated a life of service to Him. Sorta makes me feel sorry for both Duke and that degenerate offspring he sent off to defile ‘that other company’. Speaking of which…..ah yes, Skylar Green’s history. Well after seeing that he’s sufficiently bored you with enough trite stories to send Barbara Walters into a coma, I’ll digress and get to the meat of the matter here quickly. But first I thought I should note one detail of Green’s long-winded chronicle: wasn’t the Drake Maxwell he spoke so highly of the one and the same ex-PCW Continental champion caught up in a scandal involving deceit and fraud during one of his parents company’s more prestigious tournaments? There are smarks and fanboys out there, I’m sure they can back me up on this. The same Drake Maxwell hailed as a company saviour before being exposed as a sham and subsequently lynched and dismissed of his contract? Pfft. And people say I’ve never done anything profitable or productive for this company. Fuck the smarks and the horses they rode in on. If anyone knows the current whereabouts of Mr. Maxwell then please enlighten me. Last I heard he was headlining at a gymnasium somewhere in Peoria, Illinois with an asking rate of $75, a Latino whore with a foot fetish and a night’s supply of Jim Beam. But hell, I’m sure he’s well worth it and all. Just a shame he wasn’t blessed with the ‘assets’ of some of his peers to avoid such a fate. Speaking of which, caught any DWO programming lately? Anyhow…..Skylar Green. Well it appears Mr. Green has been labeled as the Sleeping Dragon, one of those fabled beings of ridiculous Celestial lore. According to the archives of the Library of Celaeno, a convention was held only months ago to discuss the crisis of “The End’s” existence and time-bomb capacity in this realm. It appears the freak has been imbued with monstrous power, and possesses the aptitude to render this universe back to its original state: a seething nuclear chaos where all matter is rearranged by the will of great daemon-sultan Azathoth alone, where all that we know has been extinguished from being. In essence, Skylar Green is the Celestials’ equivalent to a cosmic reset button. His existence is a hazard not only to all we commonly know, but to the sustentation of all the Old Ones across the vast nebulas. My purpose has become clear and the method is evident. Green must not be allowed to subsist on this world or any other. His wretched carcass will be sent screaming into the great beyond and deposited in the abyss where his own screams shall be all he hears for illimitable aeons. Once there the only thing that mongrel will capable of annihilating will be his own demolished husk of a body, as he will rot in solitude for eternity out of reach of this universe. A proper elegy will be prepared in honor of his exile, rest assured. Ah, but I grow weary this night. You may rest now, dear child. Tomorrow is a new day, and with its dawn shall come new experiences and pleasures. They are yours in which to partake. But don’t forget your hours of slumber still belong to me. And bear in mind my words of earlier; know that few entities take pleasure in mockery of the human condition like the Render of the Veils. Pleasant dreams, heathen. Ia Shub-Niggurath!
