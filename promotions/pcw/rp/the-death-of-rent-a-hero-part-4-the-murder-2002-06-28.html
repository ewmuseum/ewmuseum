The Death Of Rent-A-Hero Part 4-The Murder

28 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Death Of Rent-A-Hero Part 4-The Murder Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 6-28-2002 Well...it's you again.....here you are, sitting on a stool in some filthy pawn-scum bar somwhere in the nameless city you live in. All you know really about today is that you wasted most of it sulking in bed over the loss of Amy, coming to grips with the fact that she just isn't coming back... So you washed yourself up... Got nice and clean...Got your best costume out of the Closet O' Costumes....this one being the rare Scarlet Spider edition of Spiderman suits worn by Ben Reilly.. And you left your now dismal apartment in search for the man they call Death. Problem is...where does he hang out. I mean it's not like he has a pager number or anything. 'Hello Death, come kill me around 9:00, thanks bud, bye.'You realized earlier that you beign the hero you are, you battle things head on...waiting and retreating was NEVER your forte. So you decided to go out and search for the man shrouded in darkness, the carrier of lost souls, the collector of lives...Death. You figure, why sit around and wait for Death to just BARGE IN YOUR HOME and disturb you at your earliest discomfort. No better thing to do then charge your Energon, get right in Death's cold face, and say... 'Groovy...' So with that mentality in mind, your just sitting...awaiting Death to come right inside this haven for sleazy whores, sick inbred rednecks, and underhanded hustlers....and take you off this Earth by his hand. You ignore the terribly annoying country music playing... You ignore the sleazy whore taking up a proposition from a shady man, fumbling through his pockets to get the proper payment for her 'services'... You ignore the pretty bartender with the dangerously low neckline on her shirt and a scandelously high mid-driff as she gives you alluring 'Screw me now' looks... All your Rent-A-Senses can focus on is how Amy could forsake you like she did and when Death is coming. And your impatience for both answers is wearing thin as you tap your spandex-enwrapped foot against the bar stool leg... You haven't made it a point to keep track of time and you could care less about your obligations right now....The fans are an afterthought... Tag belts, whatever... Crusaders Of Light...not even worth a breath of air to burst into a insanely long monologue about how you intend to crush them alongside Jason Snow... The world stopped when that redhead left your home with no explanation and left you laying after the deepest love making you've ever had... You knew it woulld be the last time....but you were dumb enough to believe that she loved you enough not to leave. You allowed yourself to give into her heavenly body. You took the bait hook, line, and sinker. You gave her the best of you and then some and the only feeling that could match how it felt inside her when you two shared in the act of the creation of physical love...was that feeling you got the very next day when you looked over and reminded yourself how lucky you were to have her, and then kissed her so she'd awake like Sleeping Beauty, except you were the spandex clad Prince Charming with various Rent-A-Powers and she was a redheaded punk princess with a anarchist fashion sense...the perfect fractured fairy tale everytime you thought of it. But when you rolled over and she was gone...all traces of her disappearing from your life...The tombstone and the grave site was erected...all it needed was a superhero's body to fill it... So you irked Death on...you badgered him...you defied him by sitting in that bar, knowing you had no place there...practically ASKING, BEGGING for him to come and end you right there and maybe he too would take the bait just like you did with Amy...Suddenly...after hours of patience.... Hours of standing out in this spawning ground of evil like a a vanilla sprinkle on a chocolate cake... Death sent his messengers...and you couldn't be more happy. His messengers, were in the form of two burly thugs, dressed in dingy clothes unfit for real humans to wear and not even worth describing, you weren't one for describing trash. They took tiny sexpot shots at the lovely bartender, who simply blew them off with a routine raise of her middle finger...that was when they then turned their attentiosn to you. Slight snickers overcame their face... It reminded you a lot of the snickers you recieved from elementery through college... Those same half smile snickers with the eyes that probed your spandex superhero replica, little jokes being passed between the little cliqus...whether it be the jocks or the preps girls...it didn't matter, they all had something to say. You always knew humans always disrespected what they could never understand...and these two were no different... One of them, scratching his scruffy beard when he looked at you grinned, finally mouthing words to you as his hot breath bombarded you like gamma rays to the Incredible Hulk as he asked.. 'What are you...a giant fu**ing condom or something?'So...he greets you with such a scornful insult...usually, the pipper and chipper HAPPY RAH would look to him, smile, and answer his insult with a quirky greeting or an offering of peace. They often say when your close to death, you get senile...consider what comes out of your mouth next the most SENILE thing you've ever said... 'Yes I am...I would have a condom for you but I don't think they make them in 'microscopic organism' size.' That was the breaking point.... You think that made him your friend?15 minutes ago, you were stressing and losing your mind over Amy... Now your in a dark alleyway getting your a** handed to you by your 'two new friends'. They're working you over good... Hard punches to the jaw... Stiff shots to the gut... Kicks to the head... And NO ONE to help you in the darkness of the night. Even though you know deep down someone COULD have helped....most humans are creature who tend to mind their own business and they'll pretend they don't hear or see a man in distress if it means no conflict. It didn't matter to you though...ever punch they landed on you, every kick they bent you over with, every wall they slammed you in... Made you feel so much better. ...This was therapy for you, and the pain was just what you needed...you cried out for sweet release...the ending of life without what you loved in it and you didn't care how you got it. You weren't going to DARE fight them back, they were doing just what you wanted...ending you as painfully as possible. Your Kryptonian blood leaves stains everywhere you land, painting a beautiful mural of chaos as you continue to get tossed about the dingy alleyway....all the pain about Amy...the pain with being used...the pain of being a FREAK being shut out with the mroe and more punishment you took...and you LOVED IT... Finally the finale...one of them smashes you with a intense left hook across the jaw, sending you crashing into a heap of trash...old newspapers, food scraps, and other unidentifiable materials making a makeshift grave for you...a fitting ending for a real superhero...Now..you just needed them to end it. You were broken, beaten, bleeding...yous suit torn all over and your spirit broken...now was the time for them to end it and you gazed upon the two burly thugs as they approached you slowly, a blood laced smile overcoming your face as you prepare for sweet release... Then everything just had to SCREW UP FROM THERE...Why did they pick NOW to have a conscience! One of the thugs looked down on your beaten body, completely in shock by just how much he had beaten you....his eyes speaking for themselves as he can't believe that after the complete DESTRUCTION you were jsut given, you smile for the end. The two of them start to back away...your eyes and mouth quivering angrily, not enough strenbth to rise from the trash heap and provoke them to finish the job, so you just yell at them angrily...'FINISH WHAT YOU STARTED!!! END ME!!! RELEASE ME!! DEATH IS WAITING FOR ME!!! END IT!!'..::/::..But no matter how hard you yell...they can't help but back away in fear of what they've just done...and you realized...you can't even DIE like a normal person. You were SUPPOSED to quiver, beg for your life....but noooo, you had to sit there and smile....and you can't help but hate the world... The world can see the logic in taking Amy from you... But not the logic to have your life ended...And as you sit in the alley, your body an utter snot rag of blood as you lay in the trash where you feel belong, taking notice of a used condom among the trash. You and the condom have so much in common actually... The condom and you were important for just long enough to give the user pleasure...and when they were done... They discarded you without another care in the world and you would soon be replaced. Funny what you think of when you get the crap kicked out of you... You lick the blood from your lips, swallowing your life force with slowed pride as you look to the sky, saying to yourself in the emptiness and loneliness of the alley...'Rent-A-Hero...is...dead....at...last...' That thought lingered in your head with great joy, making you laugh with your mouth clogged with blood. Your dead...you have killed yourself...now... It's time to bury him...Bury him for good... Bury the pain and suffering that came with the name Rent-A-Hero... You'll do that....just as soon as you can feel your legs again... So until then, you wallow in the trash at the thought that you are officially deceased and you think about what you want on your funeral program....would it be appropriate to print...'HE DIED SAME RENT-A-TIME...SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL'... Hey it's your funeral...give it a whirl... ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
