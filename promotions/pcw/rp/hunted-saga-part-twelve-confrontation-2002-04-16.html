Hunted Saga Part Twelve: Confrontation

16 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/4/02 Previously… “Crichton, I know there is something wrong, you must tell me. I might be able to help you!” Aeryn pleads Crichton begins to shake his head. “No. I cannot reveal what is happening to me, but I can tell you that I am sorry and one day I will tell you what occurred on this day.” Crichton explains. Aeryn simply shakes her head in disgust and storms away into the car. ************* “You know Dan, I could use your help capturing the man behind the diners’ destruction, if your interested in revenge.” Fleming declares. Dan nods in agreement. “Why me?” Lyons inquires. “Because Crichton always sees us coming for some reason, and if you attack him he’ll never suspect a thing.” Fleming explains. “Well Mr. Fleming that seems very acceptable, very acceptable indeed.” Lyons replies. “Excellent!” Fleming simply states. And now… Lyons was secretly hoping Fleming would offer him this opportunity and since they met, suspect that he would ask for his help. However, Lyons wasn’t planning on playing along forever, because when he caught up to the man called John Crichton, he wasn’t going to capture him, he was going to kill him! The busy streets of Spokane, Washington, hummed with the clutter of so many people and so many vehicles passing by. Crichton had made it to the place in which Tuesday Turmoil was taking place with hours to spare and he had decided to take Aeryn shopping. He hated it, like all men usually do, but he was trying to make it up to her after his previous outburst upon the muggers. They had begun shopping three hours earlier and Crichton had been dragged to at least five shoe shops and Aeryn hadn’t purchased a single item. Crichton was quickly losing the will to live, but Crichton brought this punishment on himself and was determined to see it through until the end, which Crichton believed wasn’t all that long away. However, he was glad that his mind was occupied, because now he didn’t have to worry about the Inner Battle he was having between his conscience, which stood for everything good and his subconscious, which wanted to bring his dark impulses up to the front of his personality. The battle was still raging and there was no foreseeable end in sight, which worried Crichton a lot. He hardly realised the wooden bench he was sitting on, in yet another shoe store tremble slightly as Aeryn sits beside him, trying on her fifth pair of shoes in that shop. “Come on liven up, it ain’t that bad!” She states rather angrily at Crichton’s bored look. Crichton shrugs a little. “Your right it ain’t that bad, compared to cutting my penis off with rusty bread knife.” Crichton declares. Aeryn begins to grin slightly. “That can be arranged you know!” She replies with a mischievous look upon her face. Crichton looks at her for a second attempting to figure out whether she was joking or not. After a minute or so he decided that it wasn’t worth pondering that much. Aeryn takes her shoes off once more and stands up again. “Ya know I don’t like these shoes, I think I’ll try on those over there!” Aeryn declares more to herself than to Crichton. Crichton visibly sighs. “The punishment continues!” He mutters to himself. **************** The special tracking methods provided by Mr. Fleming had worked according to plan, but when he reached the car, Crichton and Aeryn wasn’t anywhere near it. So he reasoned that they cannot be far and decided to roam the streets looking for them. He had spotted them in a shoe shop about half an hour earlier and had been watching them from a far ever since. How he was going to get them in a quiet place was another matter all together. Dan Lyons was so far from his home for one thing and one thing only – revenge! Mr. Fleming had told him that this John Crichton character was the one directly responsible for his wife and his daughters death, when his diner exploded. Mr. Fleming had provided Lyons with all the gear he needed to capture Crichton, guns, gas grenades the works, but he didn’t intend to capture Crichton. All he wanted was to kill Crichton nothing more, nothing less. He only accepted Fleming’s proposal in order to locate Crichton and all of the equipment he was provided with was simply a bonus. Lyons realised that if he killed John Crichton, the army would come looking for him, but he didn’t care at all, all that he wanted to do was to reap vengeance and the time for vengeance was soon at hand. Lyons watched on as the couple finally left the shoe stop and began walking along the street, in the opposite direction of their car. The only option that he could think up right now, was to follow them back to their motel and then smite them. Lyons decides that there is no point watching them any longer, he might as well prepare for their inevitable confrontation and then simply follow them with the tracking device. **************** His punishment was nearing an end, or at least he hoped it was, there was a good chance that Aeryn would change her mind, but many argue that that’s a woman’s prerogative. Crichton tended to agree heartily with this line of argument, but not in Aeryn’s presence. Aeryn had finally chosen a pair of shoes that she likes, which also wear well with the majority of her outfits. Crichton did his best to persuade her that these shoes suited her very well, a manipulative tactic in order end his punishment faster. Finally Aeryn agreed and Crichton bought her the shoes. He then managed to usher her out of the shop to prevent her from seeing anything else that would potentially catch her eye. Only when they got back to their car did Crichton feel that his punishment had finally ceased. He started up the engine and pulled out of his parking space next to the curb. “Shall we now venture to the motel before the show starts?” Crichton questions, feeling a giddy sensation about having the opportunity to fight for the PCW TV Title. “Might as well. I’ll get the arena to send the wrestling tapes of the Pharaoh over, so you can get back on the job.” Aeryn states retrieving her mobile phone from her coat pocket. Crichton sighs. “No rest for the wicked!” Crichton stated. **************** Lyons eyed the white car as it pulled away from the curb and started in the vague direction of the motorway. Fleming had given him some very helpful advice on how to follow somebody without making it too obvious, but that wouldn’t matter too much anyhow because the tracking device would take care of that. In all theory he could chose to follow them when ever he wanted, but he felt the thirst for revenge too great of a temptation to wait any longer than he had to. Half an hour later he followed Crichton’s white car into a motel parking lot and parked at the other end of the lot from the car, to keep suspicions to a bare minimum. He watched as Crichton and Aeryn make their way to the motel reception area, presumably to get themselves a room. Lyons decided that this was the perfect time to prepare himself for the task ahead. He swiftly exits his car and makes his way to the boot, he opens the lock with his key and pushes the boot up to it’s full height. He takes out the shotgun and runs his fingers over it with a sickening delight. “Justice is about to be served pilgrim!” He idly states as if talking to his gun. ****************Aeryn looked at the very disturbed look upon Crichton’s face and he again checked the mirror, which showed him the rear of the car. Aeryn knew Crichton and became very worried herself. “Crichton, what’s wrong” She questions trying to study his face for answers to her question. He briefly looks at her. “Oh…huh…nothin’, nothin’ at all.” He declares, clearly trying to concentrate on some other task. It was very clear to Aeryn that something was wrong and even though she may come to regret it, she decided to push him for answers. “Crichton, I can tell that there is something wrong, so why don’t you just tell me!” Aeryn almost pleads. He looks at her again, and decides that there is no point avoiding the subject because his suspicions were all but confirmed. “I think…” Crichton began, almost hesitating to tell her. “I think we’re being followed.” Crichton responded trying to gauge the look upon Aeryn’s face. Aeryn felt her stomach churn and the familiar sickly feeling of danger come over hern as she contemplates the true meaning of Crichton’s current words. “Oh hell, what will we do?” Aeryn questions, panic clearly etched upon her face. Crichton shakes his head a little as a sensible battle plan enters his mind. “We can do nothing at the moment, we must not show any sign that we know that he is following us otherwise we might force him to do something rash. So when we get to the parking lot we’ll both head to the reception and plan our next move from there.” Crichton explains calmly. Aeryn simply nods her head and prepares herself, as they turn into the motel parking lot. A car behind them predictably follows them. Crichton sighs to himself. “Looks like I was right.” Crichton declares sombrely. He parks up the white car across the way from the motel reception area and takes his keys out of the ignition. “Are you ready?” he asks in a very caring voice. Aeryn nods a ‘yes’ and they get out of the car and head into some new untold danger. **************** As soon as Lyons prepared himself with necessary array of weapons, for Fleming told him that this Crichton was slippery, very slippery indeed, he decided to just go for it. He didn’t much care who saw him do it, he just wanted vengeance there and then. So he begins. He begins the ever so long walk towards the reception area. He slowly stalks the building, not trying any army tactical manoeuvres just a straight, true path. Lyons entered the reception with dea6th in his eyes, as he sees Crichton stood there with a pistol generally pointed in the direction of the reception door. Undeterred by this Lyons takes another step forward. **************** Crichton takes his pistol retrieved by soldiers a while ago, out of the back of his pants and aims it at the entranceway of the door. He was warned by Aeryn, who was acting almost as a look out, that the man was heading their way. Luckily the reception area was deserted when they got there, otherwise this whole thing coulda got messy, messier Crichton corrected himself. Crichton saw the big figure, almost Zombie like, make his way up the road and toward the reception. Crichton read the mans eyes and found nothing more than hate, a need for revenge. With Crichton’s current problem, the inner battle, he could identify with the man, but there was something about him that seemed familiar. As if by cue, Aeryn gasps. Crichton looks at her, prompting her to explain. “Crichton, that’s the diner owner!” Aeryn stated. “I thought he was dead, with the rest of them.” Crichton replies. “Me too.” Aeryn simply declares. Crichton felt a sigh of relief, relief that people may have survived the accident, but the realisation suddenly hit him. The vengeful look in his eye meant that somebody close to him had died and Crichton was willing to bet that they died in the explosion that he was part responsible for. He could do nothing about that now, all that he could do was keep himself and Aeryn alive and that meant threatening him and persuading him to leave them alone. The diner owner edged his way through the door. Crichton decided that this was going to get very messy indeed.
