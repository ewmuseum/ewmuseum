DC/LX/VI Saga: Unwanted Revelations

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/11/02 'Dear JeremiahThe committee and myself trust you are doing well, and taking good care of yourself. Lord, or maybe Lucifer, choose your preference, knows that none of us are getting any younger. Then again you've never let that stop you.Anyway, onto more pressing matters. We are no longer assuming that you know of our current plans and leading events, or rather THE event, we are assuming that you already know. This plan has been in motion for decades upon decades, and we still plan to go forth with it. The wheels are in motion as we speak. Now, we are aware that even though you have left our organisation, we took it upon ourselves to merely politely inquire if you would wish to change your mind and rejoin us. We would be more than willing to welcome you back with open arms, you were after all a key figure in our organisation when it started. It would be a shame not to have you aboard with us as our plan finally goes into action and succeeds. How is the young Gideon by the way? We heard you had taken it upon yourself to teach and guide a student, we here at the committee are more than sure that under your wisdom and guidance he will turn out to be an exceptional man and a gifted talented warrior. It would be a shame if something were to happen to him just as our 'event' were to take place. People may ask questions, it may appear to be too much of a coincidence.Nevertheless, if you wish to rejoin us Jeremiah, we will look out keenly for your response in the mail. If not, then please do not waste your valuable time sending anything to us, we will more than understand. Kindest regardsDC/LX/VI' * * * * * * * * * He read through the letter again. Though really it wasn't a letter, it was a blatant threat covered up nicely by persuasive polite words. It reeked of foul play, it reeked of the end of something. Whatever this 'event' was Gideon was willing to bet it wasn't a high school reunion. 'What are you up to old man?' he muttered to himself as he read through the threat another time under the candlelight. DC/LX/VI, they were Roman numerals of some sort, but they didn't concern him right now, though maybe they should have. 'You won't find out who they are from that letter' croaked a familiar and shrill voice. Gideon turned his head sharply to look over his shoulder. The small candlelight cast a deathly shadow over one side of his face, only allowing one of his rage filled eyes to stare at the elderly evil known as Jeremiah O'Barr. 'They move in mysterious ways, like the good Lord himself you might say.' Gideon rose from the chair he had been sitting in and stared down at the petit man. ' I think you have some explaining to do old man' The dark puppet master nodded slowly, but with a menacing grin over his face that showed off his set of white rotting teeth. 'Oh surely my dear boy, that letter itself must have explained a lot for you, more than enough in fact. I think you've had more than enough explanations for one day.' 'I've been searching for explanations for the last three years, now that I get a small hint of one I think it's time I cashed in the rest of the overdue ones.' The age old evil slithered into the room, his long white hair and beard made him appear like some small twisted version of Santa Clause, and the small staff which he used to help himself walk make him appear like one of the mythological characters you read about legends. By appearances alone, Jeremiah O'Barr could easily be mistaken for something not from this world. 'My hands are tied in this matter I'm afraid, you read the note yourself. I now know as much about this as you do' 'You've been working for them for years, or at least you did, that much is obvious, you must know more than just what is said in this note!' 'Are you that blind boy!' snapped the elderly evil. '............... Who are they Jeremiah, and why do they want you back with them. More importantly, what did you do when you were with them?' 'Things which you need now know. However I will tell you this, I wish not to return to them' 'Their threatening to KILL ME if you don't!'. Gideon threw the politely covered up threat down to the floor as an exclamation point to his comment. 'I thought you welcomed death my dear boy' croaked the elderly evil with a slick sly smile. 'Oh I do old man, but on my OWN terms, not on yours, and certainly not on the 'committees!'' Gideon slowly walked to the corner of the small wooden candlelit room and slumped down in the corner amongst his only friends in life, the shadows. He let his head drop nonchalantly forward, allowing his long black hair to smother his face. 'Why this constant torment old man? Why don't, just for once, stop being the wrinkled up old wretch that you are and shed some light on this burdened relationship that you have forced upon me'. There was an unpromising lonely silence, before the croaked voice of the old man creaked through it. 'I suppose, if you were to find out about them yourself, there would be nothing wrong with that.' Gideon looked up slowly, his eyes connecting with the old mans. For the first time in years he seen that the old man was troubled, nervous, and most surely afraid. 'Look for ancient prophecies, mainly with a three digit connection within them leading to something at the end of this year. I can tell you no more.' With that the dark puppet master turned sharply and left the room with his head hung low. Gideon almost felt sorry for him, the clues that he just told him had mentally hurt him to tell. He could indicate that he wasn't merely informing Gideon to look for clues if he WANTED to find out about this committee. He was telling Gideon to seek out the answers, and to Gideon, that was the most worrying part about all of this.....
