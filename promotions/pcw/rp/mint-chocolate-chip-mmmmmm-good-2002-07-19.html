Mint Chocolate Chip....Mmmmmm Good!

19 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/19/02 It had been about a week since Kramer had been seen or heard from. Many thought he was so depressed about losing another important match that he was going to just toss in the towel and leave wrestling forever. Or at least that is what many were probably hoping......But all of that was laid to rest when Kramer approached Rush Mason on Turmoil demanding a title shot. After an exchange of some more words, Rush gave Kramer a reply, but not one that he was really counting on. Rush had replied that the only way he could get a title shot was if he defeats Thug roC on Turmoil. Of course he threw in the stipulation of him being the special guest referee!! So it seems that Kramer may be at another loss. But before he starts to worry about Turmoil, he has direct his attention to Fusion where he takes on the Flaymin one and Ryan Stephens....... The scene opens up inside of a Greater's ice cream shop. It's quite small, but well kept, with three unoccupied tables off to the side. There are a few customers at the front counter, ordering different variations of Greater's ever so famous ice cream. The camera shifts to the entrance though as Kramer walks through wearing a white t-shirt and black shorts. He gives a little smile as the cool air blasts him as he proceeds through. He takes his place in the line and waits for his turn to order. Unfortunately he doesn't notice who he has in front of him, which is a squad of little league baseball players. So naturally, it takes each kid several minutes to finally decide over what flavor to pick. Finally Kramer hears the word that he had been waiting impatiently for. Woman: Next! Kramer steps forward in one quick motion and looks around at the charts pondering between Mint Chocolate Chip, and Double Fudge Cookie Dough. He opens his mouth, ready to order, but he never gets the chance. A masked man weilding a gun bursts into the shop causing everyone great panic. Kramer doesn't notice anything though as he tries to get the ladies attention at the counter. Kramer: Excuse me! Ma'am! I need some ice cream..... The masked gunman steps behind Kramer and tries to push him aside. Kramer: Hey wait your turn buddy. Kramer turns around to confront the man, and gets the gun shoved in his face for his trouble. Sweat begins to form on Kramer's brow as he slowly steps aside never letting the gun out of his sight. Kramer steps to the far corner and watches as the man yells at the woman. Man: Give me all of your money or you get your damn head blown off! Her shaky fingers tap on the keys to the cash register which finally opens up. The man hands her a bag and orders her to stuff all of the money inside. Hesitantly she takes all of the money and places it in the bag. Man: Now get me a double dip of Mint Chocolate Ice Cream now!! The woman walks over and peers into the tub and slowly looks up to the man. Woman: There's only one dip left sir. Man: Fine then give me one scoop of that and Rocky Road Ice Cream!! Kramer stands up abrubtly unable to control himself as the last scoop of Mint Chocolate Ice Cream is calling out to him, beckoning him to eat it. Kramer: Uh can you have the Rocky Road and something else? Man: Are you kidding me? Mint Chocolate Ice Cream is my favorite! Kramer: Well you see it's my favorite as well, and in actuality it should be mine anyway. Man: How so? Kramer: I was in line in front of you. Man: I guess you won't need ice cream if you are lying on the ground now will you? The man brandishes the gun towards Kramer again warning him to back off. Kramer: Hey you never know. The man reaches out to grab the cone which the woman is handing to him still shaking nervously. Kramer looks at the man's hand and notices that he is an African American. With all of the recent events Kramer just goes off yet again. This time he steps up to the man who has foolishly left his gun on the counter. Kramer snatches it up and tosses it out of the way to prevent anyone leaving with a bullet embedded in their skin. Kramer: You know I am tired of you black people stealing crap from me!! Man: What in the hell are you talking about? Kramer: First that asshole Rush Mason steals my damn Extreme Title and now you steal my damn ice cream!! Man: Rush stole jack from you. Now you better sit your ass down before I do it for you. Kramer: I am not the man to be messed with at the moment. Man: Oh really? Well guess what? I am going to do to you what Thug roC is going to do to you on Turmoil. Kramer: You don't have the guts! You are just as much as a pussy as the rest of The Uprising! Woman: What are you guys talking about? Man: PCW wrestling bitch! Woman: Was bitch really necessary? The black male lunges at Kramer with a right fist. Kramer wisely swerves to the side and knees the guy in the gut. Pretty soon both men end up on the floor rolling around throwing more punches. While all of this is going on, the woman dials up the police. Within a matter of minutes they arrive and take the robber away in handcuffs. Kramer stays, black eye and all, to get ice cream. Unfortunately during the scuffle, the Mint Chocolate Ice Cream was smeared all over the floor, so he had to settle with Cookie Dough. The scene slowly fades to black as Kramer happily licks away at his ice cream cone.
