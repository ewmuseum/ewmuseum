Turmoil Wars - Matt Jackson (Part II)

26 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/26/03 (OOC: A continuation of Turmoil Wars - Part I) Stone knelt down, ripping the blade out from James Edwards's flesh, and standing up on his feet; face immensely perspired, and lightly mutilated. With all the disadvantages accompanying Stone, Matt Jackson, his second and penultimate opposition, came forward smiling sadistically, clad in a dark black suit, that of an agent's suit. He hoisted his bludgeon, the weapon he would be using. Stone lethargically brought his blade up in the air, the incarnadine blood trickling downward like an empty faucet. The fancying of Stone came with other obstacles this time taking place in the confines of his immured chamber in Bainbridge. Stone looks around, as framed artwork and photography blanket the room, those of his daughter Emily and Anna Blasch, both victims of his heinous homicides. Matt Jackson mercurially motioned with his feet, as he mysteriously dissolved in to the air. Athwart Stone came non-visible yet audible footsteps, and then unexpectedly adjacent to his position was the penumbra of Matt Jackson, who then once again appeared and thwacked Stone across the head with his club-like instrument. Stone fell to his side viciously, unconscious, with the blade falling from his possession. With the crafty tactics of Agent Matt Jackson, he came over Stone silhouetting his shadow with his rather small figure, yet still overwhelmingly, and largely projected. With the now rather usual fiend appearance of objects came a thin rope, in an inspissate state more and more as he firmly gripped it. He tethered Stone's motionless hands, and attained his blade, now holding his bludgeon and Stone's blade. He basked in his success in overcoming the misogynist. Suddenly, a barrage of winds sluiced the sealed chamber, knocking Jackson down to his buttocks. An unusual mystic feeling came over the chamber as the mind of Stone Burns had slight modifications of apparitions soaring in the chamber, confusing Matt Jackson who kept his position and looked on. The specters were those of Emily Burns and Anna Blasch but with an abrupt flash they left, as the room quieted but changed. Stone stood conscious and well, as Matt Jackson was out, tethered with rope. The positions had changed as Stone now held the bludgeon and blade; Matt Jackson was placed back down, but regaining consciousness although not majorly debilitated but enough to leave him stone still. Stone had no other choice but to come forward along with his blood caked blade and a grim scream.. 'AHHHHHHH!' He came down with his blade only to halt by a few words coming from a sudden emergence.. 'Let me do that for you.' Stated a masculine figure, firing a bullet in to the head of Agent Matt Jackson, 'ending his days' with a single shot. Stone brought his attention to the figure and it was that of the Ukrainian born Soviet Sniper.. Sivasian Krikorian, Stone's next and last Turmoil Wars adversary. TO BE CONTINUED
