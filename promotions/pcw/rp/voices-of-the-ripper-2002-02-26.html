Voices of the Ripper...

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-26-02 I Sat there breathing hard, my muscles ripped and bulging, and sweat giving my skin that glossy look. I had just finished lifting weights trying to work everything out in my head and release some of the built up frustrations that have made their way into my life since my last visit with the Doc. I had heard that after my match this Tuesday on Turmoil that I have a match at Primetime’s up and coming pay-per-view, March Madness. What a very fitting name. What a very fitting place. What a very fitting time. What a very fitting match… Four corners, four men, and one winner. Lance Bell, Jay Sylo, and my opponent tonight on Turmoil, Steve Johnson. There’s many things I enjoy in life, but getting to destroy a man’s career. His hopes. His dreams. His… LIFE. Is one of the most enjoyable moments for me. And on March 3rd 2002 I get to destroy careers, hopes, dreams, and lives. Three of them in fact. Three men who will be changed. Three men who will never see the world the same way again. Three men who will known nothing more than that they weren’t good enough. Strong enough. Fast enough. Smart enough to take out the freak. Three men would all be changed by the hands of the Ripper. The suits of PCW had helped into three of their superstars despise. Does it matter that Primetime official suits came down on me? Does it matter they warned me? That they threatened me? That they told me to leave him along? The man I’ve been following? The man I wish to dispose of? No, it matters little to me. I’ll take my built up and over flowing frustrations I have for PCW’s upper so called businessmen out on three individuals at March Madness. PCW officials saved… Cal Seaver. From my hands of wrath. But only for the time being… I reached to my left for a toweled I had laid their earlier. When my hand didn’t come into contact with the towel I turned my head to help my hand along. But it wasn’t that my hand was in the wrong place. The towel wasn’t where it was suppose to be. And like a demon out of nowhere stood a man. With my towel draped over his shoulder. A man that seemed familiar. A man that I was sure I knew. A man that appeared to me only a few times before. A man that nobody knew existed except for myself. A man that resembles me in more way than just one. This man was… me! I began remembering things at that instance. A time months ago. Months before my PCW arrival. Months before my forced visits to the Doc. A time when people considered me… normal. ***Flash Back*** I was sitting across the parking lot waiting for her to get off work. It was nearing that time as I had made sure before I had shown up outside. I didn’t want to make people suspicious by staying out here half the night. I had planned it all perfectly. Now it was all going to come together. She was going to see how much I cared. How much I wanted to be with her. Nobody else but her. Nobody could give me what she could. Nobody else could put a smile on my face like her. She was the one. I knew it and soon… very soon so would she. She walked out of the bar. It was a rather chilly night therefore she was wearing jeans and an over coat. She had bundled herself up tight as to keep the wind out from her small fragile frame. She didn’t even bother to look around the parking lot as she made her way to her blue convertible. Why should she have too. Everybody that came here knew her and liked her. Nobody would harm a pretty little hair on her head. As she opened her car door my emotions and feelings of the past few weeks began to play in my mind. The frustration, the anger, the pure hate for a woman that did what she did to me. How could she? What was she thinking? She was playing me for a fool. A man that had no sense. She flirted with another guy when she was mine. She shouldn’t have done that. She should have known better. But she didn’t, did she… Start the car… The voice in my head was speaking to me. Follow her… As if unable to ignore the voice I started the engine on my car. Placed it into drive and followed her. Her house was only a few miles from the bar. I kept back far enough that she wouldn’t know she was being followed. Why didn’t she notice? Why couldn’t I ignore the voice? Once she pulled up into her drive. I parked across the street. She got out of her car and slammed the door. She stop and began digging into her purse, for what? Why didn’t she hurry and get out of the darkness of the night? Why didn’t she hurry to the confines of her home, where it was safe? Open the door… Put my hand on the door release but halted. Trying to buy time, trying to give her more time. More time to get away. Get out… Her time was up. I had no other choice but to get out. Once I opened the door I slammed it harder than needed to be. Trying to get her attention. Walk towards her… Run… Emily run… get away… please… She just stood there in the pale light of the moon. As if destiny couldn’t keep us away from each other. But she didn’t know. She didn’t know that… he was with me… inside my head… As I stepped up into her yard she stood her ground. I wanted to yell out to her. I wanted to warn her of the danger. But he wouldn’t allow me to open my mouth. He was in control… I did as he said… no matter what it was… Her eyes were dark with fright. She must know. Why else would she be afraid? Why else would she tell me to stay away? Why else would she change her phone number? Why else would she change her locks? She had to know he was in my head. But she had to know that I, I loved her. Once I stopped a few feet of her she began to get angry. “What are you doing here?” She was practically yelling at me… at him… “I…I…I’m… not sure.” I was here because of him. Did she truly not know this. “Not sure, your not f(censored)king sure why you’re here?” She should watch herself. She didn’t want to make him upset. “I…want to talk.” Maybe she’d calm down. “Talk, I thought I’d made it clear. I want nothing to do with you. I want you to stay away from me. Now leave before I call the police.” She did it… she made him mad. Listen to her… She’s frightened… She hates you… Destroy her… “NO! I WON‘T” I yelled out loud. As I did so she jumped back. I had frightened her. She was afraid. But she asked. “You won’t what?” Do it or she’ll never be yours… After that I don’t remember anything. It was as if he had complete control. As if he went from controlling just my mind to my entire body. I remember crying, pleading, begging, it all came from her. But I couldn’t stop…him. He was in control. It was as if I wasn’t even there. I just remember sitting in the car with my hands handcuffed behind my back. She was lying on the ground just a few feet from her living room door. If only she wouldn’t have stopped. If only she would have gotten inside. Maybe I could have just left. Maybe she wouldn’t be hurt… hurt because of him… and my weakness to control him… That’s when it happened the first time. That’s when I had seen him for the first time. He was there standing over her. An evil grin over his smug face. Him rubbing the blood into his powerful hands. Slowly he began to turn. Slowly he looked at me. I was frightened. It was… ME! I tried to yell, to warn somebody, he was right there. Why couldn’t they see him? Why was I locked up in the back of a police car when he was loose? Why had they not seen that he was actually the one to hurt her? It wasn’t me… it wasn’t me… ***Flash To Present Time*** The voice had always been there. It had always been inside my head. Guiding me telling me what to do and when to do it. But never before, never had he actually revealed himself to me. Not until that day… and now here he was again… I stood and turned to face him. Eye to eye, nose to nose, we stood there staring each other down. My fiercest enemy ever… myself… The grin on his face was the same as that tragic day. It was as if he still had her blood stained on his hands. But why… why show himself now, after all these months? (To Be Continued…)
