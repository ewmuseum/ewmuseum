Metal Gear Spoofage 2 (part 12)

4 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/4/03 'Show me what you've got!' In a small chamber beneath the faux-off shore cleanup facility known as the Big Shell, Raiden looks about frantically. He then ducks just in time to avoid being impaled by a flying dildo from the bizarre one named Argy. Raiden: Ack! The sex toy imbeds in the far wall.....the vampire does a ballerina dance, leaping gracefully all the way up to the rafters. Raiden: This is not good....AH! Again our hero dives out of the way just as several of the phallic objects whiz out of the black. Argy's chuckling voice rings forth. Argy: I'll spear your ass. Raiden: That's what I'm afraid of. NO! Once again Jack dives, just avoiding getting his ass speared (ahem). He rolls into the water, hanging on by his fingertips....then recalls what his foe said about its contents. Raiden: Ewwwww! Quickly Raiden climbs out, frantically trying to knock the orgy water off of himself. He then turns and yelps as Argy runs straight for him! Argy: Your ass is mine. Raiden: Never!!!!! Covering his ass with both hands, Raiden begins running around the room. Hot on his tail (ahem) Argy follows......then flies into the air as an explosion rocks under his feet. Raiden: (Holding a claymore mine) Argh! Argy flips, plopping into the disgusting water and again drenching Raiden. The hero looks over to find his foe swimming about like a very large androgynous fish. Raiden: (Glancing about) How can I.... Before the sentence can even be finished Argy leaps from the pool, landing before Jack. He smirks, waving yet another dildo in his hand. Raiden: That's really gross. Argy: Catch! The vampire launches his weapon straight at the gaping mouth of Raiden. Just before it can hit him he throws himself to the ground....but then as he tries to stand, he finds he cannot move. Raiden: (Struggling) What....the....hell....? Our hero looks behind himself to see a very strange sight. His own shadow appears to be holding onto his foot. Raiden: (Kicking) Hey, let go! Unfortunately the young man's words have no effect, the dark form continues to hang on. Cackling silently, it begins crawling over him. Raiden: No, down boy! Down boy! The shadow looms closer, making its way over his body. Nearby Argy watches and drools. Argy: Heh heh....AGH! The blood(?)sucker screams as in total desperation Raiden pulls out his pistol, leans back and fires a wild shot that catches him right in the bullethole on his forehead. He stumbles backwards drunkenly, does a final pirouette and splashes into the water. Raiden: (Kicking his shadow) Bastard, whose side are you on!? Shadow: (Yelps like a puppy) Raiden: Meh. Shaking himself off, our hero glances up to find an odd sight. A rather large fellow in glasses sits in the corner watching him, eating a bag of Doritos. Raiden: Hey wait a minute..... The man notices Raiden, quickly putting his chips down and getting to his feet, dusting off his hand and extending it. Man: Oh hi, I'm Brian Chapman. Raiden: You're....Bobby Stormbringer's sister? Brian: Er, brother...he's weird. Bobby: (Bursting over the Codec) Lies! He's a her! He's a her and I want her! Raiden: Ewwwwwww. Brian: Well anyway, what brings you to this rapidly flooding off shore cleaup facility. Raiden: Well, I was here to save the President, but he suddenly died. Then I tried to save an agent named Aimz, but she died. And now I'm here to save you! Brian: .....I'm not reassured. Raiden: Eh? Brian: Never mind. Let's go. Chapman begins to walk off, leaving Raiden to scratch his head. Quickly he trots after him, towards the exit of the chamber. Raiden: So....can you tell me what's going on here, Brian? Brian: Well, I'd say it's probably all the work of the mods. Raiden: The mods? Brian: Yeah.....hasn't anyone told you ANYTHING? Raiden: I think Argy told me he wanted to have sex with my corpse. Brian: ......ew. Raiden: (Blushes) Brian: Well, basically the mods control everything. Haven't you noticed any weird stuff happening lately.... Raiden: Well, recently it felt like something like five months had passed from the time I found Argy to the time our fight began, but it couldn't have been more than three seconds.... Brian: Exactly. That was all the mod's doing. They control everything.....screwing with post counts, right down to the general degradation of what used to be a pretty cool community into the mess you see today on the boards. Raiden: Huh? Brian: Yup. Hey I can't swim, would you mind giving me a lift? Chapman abruptly leaps onto Raiden's shoulders, hanging on to him in a piggy back position. Raiden: (Stumbling) You're....choking.....me.... Brian: It's okay, I'll just listen to your heartbeat and we'll be fine. Hey, why's your heartbeat slowing down? Raiden: (Turning blue) Help....me..... Jack takes one more step forward, then trips and falls headfirst into the water, sinking rapidly to the bottom. Raiden: (Gurgling) AGH!!!! AGH!!!! AGH!!!! Snake: (Over the Codec) Kid if you survive this I'm so killing you dead. To Be Continued
