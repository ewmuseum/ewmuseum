Pissing Off Boda...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Bah! Our scene opens up on the view of a marvellous crystal clear swimming pool. A hot sun beats down upon us and as we gaze about we find that the surroundings of the pool are lined with tall flourishing green trees. A huge grey stonewall is erected around the pool and its length seems to continue for miles around the absolutely huge premises. In the background a vast house of amazing craftsmanship can be made out and as we once again begin to look around ourselves the distinct shape of a well-built man, with spiky blonde hair can be made out lying on a blue and white striped beech towel by the pools edge. We close in on him to find that this person is in fact former PCW world heavyweight champion, Boda. Boda relaxes by the pools edge under the watchful eye of the burning hot sun. He’s obviously asleep and seems pretty heavily in the land of nod, stirring every now and again and breathing in patterned sequences. Suddenly the sound of footsteps upon the stone slab floor of the mansions patio fills the air. Into the scene swiftly walks a man in a dark navy butler’s uniform, he looks down at the dozing Boda and coughs in an attempt to wake the Czech. As assumed there is no movement from the Big Bad Boda Daddy and so once again he Butler coughs; this time a little louder. Slowly Boda stirs before sluggishly flipping over onto his back and sitting up, still a little dazed and sleepy… Boda: What the hell do you want? Boda yawns… Boda: Can’t you see that I’m trying to rest?! Butler: I’m sorry to interrupt you, sir, but three people just arrived; a tall male Egyptian-looking man says that he needs to speak with you urgently… Boda sighs and then gets up off the beech towel… Boda: The Pharaoh! Dammit! What does he want? We’ve already had our damned match! Boda walks away from the swimming pool, seemingly headed for the huge mansion in the foreground. The butler follows closely behind… Boda: Did he say why he was here? Butler: No, sir, he didn’t. Boda: Dammit! Minutes of walking later Boda finally reaches the giant-sized house and opens up a tall glass door, which allows good access to the back of the house where of course we find the beautiful swimming pool…Boda walks indoors and immediately we are metaphorically ‘knocked down’ by the vast amount of ‘Orlando Magic’ basketball merchandise that litters the walls of the mansions colossal walls. Boda walks down through a short well-decorated corridor and opens a door at the halls very end. We follow him and now find ourselves standing in what appears to be a hallway of some sort. There in front of Boda now stands The Pharaoh. An arrogant smirk swipes his handsome face and his wife Isiy stands by his side. Boda: What do you want, Pharaoh? We’ve already had our damn match…you do realize that you’ve woken me from my rest, don’t you? Pharaoh seems not to be intimidated by the massive Czech monster and simply smiles as though not even in doubt of Boda’s potential mood swing… The Pharaoh: Boda, I do realize what I may have done, and yes, I know that our match is well behind us, but if you cast your mind back a little then you’ll remember that I was suppose to come here and cut a promo anyway… Boda: That was meant to be on Tuesday Morning, Pharaoh! You never got here! Titu smirks again. The Pharaoh: Your words speak only the truth, Boda. But alas I was in Lisbon cutting a promo with Juan Borro. Perhaps you saw it? Boda: I had better things to do, I’m afraid… The Pharaoh: Well if you had watched it then you’d know very well that I was headed here after the promo had finished. But. As it does, time grew upon us and so I couldn’t arrive here in your marvellous home before our match… Boda frowns doubtfully. Boda: So you’re here now? But why? Your next match is against that idiotic Mexican fool, El Diablo! What have I got to do with him? Pharaoh smiles broodingly. The Pharaoh: Nothing, my friend, but your home… Pharaoh pauses for a moment. He gazes about the former PCW heavyweight champion’s home and then breaths in heavily before continuing his speech… The Pharaoh: It seems to be the perfect place to set a promo, don’t you think? Boda frowns… Boda: No, not really! And anyway! I don’t have time for this; I have to prepare for Miyagawa at the PPV! Please, leave this place now! The Pharaoh: I’m afraid I can’t do that, Boda. For you see you signed some papers that said I could cut at least ONE promo here at your home. You never said when, and so I wish to cut one right now… Boda grinds his teeth. Boda: Why you disrespectful Egyptian Bastard, I should grind your bones! I need my rest! Can’t you see that? And I need to train in the gym! My day isn’t even finished there either, for I need to damn well get back on the road for the up and coming shows!! Pharaoh sighs… The Pharaoh: Look, this will be over soon, I promise! Reluctantly Boda finally agrees for Pharaoh to cut his promotional vignette, but not without grinding his teeth in annoyance of the former Egyptian king’s wish. Boda: Fine, but this had better not take long, Pharaoh! Pharaoh walks towards Boda and places a reassuring hand upon his massive shoulder… The Pharaoh: It won’t, my friend. I promise you that much! Boda: Fine… and take your hand off of me! Boda shrugs off Pharaohs hand and walks towards a set of stairs across the hallway, obviously these lead up onto the second floor of the mansion. Boda sits down upon them and watches as Pharaoh begins to act out his promo… The Pharaoh: Hello, my fans, and welcome to another Titu Xansiv promotional vignette…now as you may or may not know, I am here within the confines of the Boda mansion. Yes that’s right I am here in the Big Bad Boda Daddy’s home! Now right over there… is Boda… The cameraman filming the whole ordeal turns his view to the goliath Boda who unenthusiastically waves to the camera. The cameraman slowly returns to the face of Titu Xansiv, PCW’s TV champion… The Pharaoh: Now what a pleasure it is to have permission from Boda to let us venture forth into his amazing home-… Suddenly Pharaoh is unexpectedly interrupted when Boda steps into the scene again, this time shaking his head to and fro… Boda: Look, Titu, this is all wrong! The Pharaoh: What? Boda: You’re doing this all wrong, man! Pharaoh frowns in puzzlement and then looks at Isiy. The Egyptian Enchantress shrugs; obviously unsure of what Boda is talking about, before Titu turns back to Chris… The Pharaoh: Boda, what are you talking about? Boda: Well look at this?! This isn’t how you cut a promo! You look like you’re cutting a TV show, dammit! Pharaoh puts his hands upon his sides and watches as Boda continues to give him advise on Promo cutting… Boda: I might as well goddamn give you a tour around the house; it’ll be better than what you were about to do! Boda shakes his head again in disbelief and then signals for the cameraman, Pharaoh, and Isiy to follow him… Boda: Come on; follow me…you can talk about that Mexican freak while we go! Boda begins to walk back in the direction he just came, that being the bearing of the pool… The Pharaoh: Boda, I see little to no reason why you’re doing this! Boda: Well that sucked back there, Pharaoh! You should be ashamed! Pharaoh grinds his teeth as suddenly a tiny laugh fills the air…Pharaoh looks down behind him and we find Wimlog Mogre by his master’s side. His small stature has seemingly made him unnoticeable before this moment and from the looks of things the freakish little man is as ugly as ever! The Pharaoh: What the hell are you laughing at, cretin? Wimlog immediately ceases his laughing. Wimlog: Nothing master… Titu frowns at him angrily… The Pharaoh: Good! After a few moments Boda stops In front of the three Egyptians and begins to speak… Boda: Here is a selection of the trophies I won while in college playing basketball… Boda points up towards a trophy case whilst smiling, seemingly recollecting on past days. The golden gleaming cups and medals all shimmer in the bright sunlight from outside… Boda: Yeah, some of these were the last things I won while I was still in the basketball circuit…before I came to PCW2 or PCW I played for the Orlando Magic team… Pharaoh looks around the walls of the room in surprise, he is as shocked as we were when we first saw it, the countless pieces of Orlando Magic merchandise is simply astonishing… The Pharaoh: Boda, what has this to do with my match? Boda: Nothing really, but your promo was gonna really suck, Pharaoh, thankfully I’ve brought some prestige back to your sketch! The Pharaoh: But this has absolutely nothing to do with El Diablo, Boda…it’s pretty damn well boring! Chris frowns… Boda: Look, if you don’t like THIS, then you’re free to go elsewhere and cut your promo in a different place to MY home! Pharaoh frowns… The Pharaoh: Oh don’t worry, Chris, I’m going! Pharaoh swiftly followed by his entourage head back down the corridor they just came up. In the background Boda laughs boomingly… Boda: Finally they’re gone…now back to some sunbathing! From down the hallway Pharaoh shouts… The Pharaoh: Oh and by the way! I just hope at the PPV you do what I did on Turmoil…that’s pin Miyagawa…then again, Boda, I doubt you will! Boda frown scornfully and then charges off after Pharaoh as the sound of the mansions front door being slammed shut fills the air of the vast building. Boda: Get back here, you Egyptian piece of crap! On these words the scene fades to black…
