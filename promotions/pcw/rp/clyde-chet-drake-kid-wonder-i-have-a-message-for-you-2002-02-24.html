Clyde, Chet, Drake, Kid Wonder: I have a message for you!

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-24-02 Three days, two birds, one stone. Ever since losing the World title belt to his former best friend, Jason Snow, at Shanghaied, the Krazed Lunatic has had an up and down journey. He's weathered the verbal beratings of Kid Wonder, all the while chasing his dream of being the first man in PCW history to complete the title sweep. Add to that, the heart wrenching battle against Drake Maxwell for his brother, and one might think that Jeremy Howard is about ready to just give up. If that's the case and you're one of those people whose beginning to place doubt within the Krazed Lunatic, then you need only to remember back to Decimation in December and the six weeks after. Many men, freaks and overall evil beings tried with everything they had to break the Krayzie One.... but failed! And now, with another battle of psychotic minds with Clyde on the horizon on Sabotage, a chance to add another title, the Continental title, to his amazing list of accomplishments this coming Friday Fusion and the fight of a lifetime for the freedom of his brother at March Madness next Sunday, the Krazed Lunatic can't afford failure. The only thing Jeremy Howard can afford this week, is for the heart and determination that led him to winning the World title twice, to carry him to three blockbuster victories.... Or a miracle? Whatever it takes, the Krazed Lunatic is going to need it if he plans to prove to everyone that has ever doubted him why he is one of the most prolific wrestlers to ever grace a Primetime Championship Wrestling ring. Something that eats away at the soul of Jeremy's arch-enemy, Kid Wonder to this very day. So, just where would the Krayzie One go to sort his mind, with so much going down in his twenty one year old life this week? With millions of fans worldwide asking that same question, the scene fades into the modest sized, yet impressively decorated living room of Jeremy Howard. There, the Krazed Lunatic can be seen staring at his thirty five inch Sony television, wearing a pair of FUBU blue jeans, a white t-shirt and a blue flannel shirt over that. Jessica: I think we have a problem. Jeremy stands, still staring emotionless at the television screen. Jeremy: We have no problem. Just a bigger list of things to accomplish up until March Madness. Jessica: How can you sound so confident with an unbelievable amount on your plate coming up? Suddenly, the scene is graced with the beautiful presence of the lovely Jessica, clad in white jeans that stick to her sexy body and a matching white shirt. Jessica is seen making her way toward Jeremy, from the entrance to the living room. Jeremy: Because, I don't have time to doubt myself. Jeremy pauses from speaking, while pushing the power button on his television. The screen on the television goes black. Jeremy: Already tonight, I have to deal with Clyde. Did I not beat this sick f^ck already? The Krayzie One raises his hands to his forehead, as if to ask 'why even bother?' It was only a week ago that the Krazed Lunatic beat Clyde and Vampir Nosferatu for the number one contendership to the Continental title. Now, a week later, Jeremy has to battle Clyde again. One on one. Jessica: There has to be a reason why this match was booked. Jeremy: Of course there is! Kid Wonder probably licked Tyler Nelson's ass until he signed this match, just so he'd have another chance at attacking me from behind. Jessica: Then you'd better watch out for that tonight, Friday and next Sunday. Sadly for Jeremy's sake, that's a very real possibility. As of late, its been no secret that Kid Wonder utterly loathes the former World Champion. A burning hatred runs so deep within the Quintessential Teen Idol, that Jeremy can never rule out the probability of Kid Wonder ruining his chance at the Continental title or freeing his brother. Unless, of course, the Krayzie One can find away to slow down Kid Wonder's efforts somehow. The questions remains then, how? Jeremy: That's alright. I think I have the answer to all of Kid Wonder's problems solved. Jessica turns to Jeremy, looking at him with a question mark in her facial expression. Jessica: You do? Jeremy knods his head, 'yes'. Knowing the mind of this man, there's just no telling what he's thinking about, though. For Kid Wonder's sake, he'd better pray its not about murder, mayhem or the like. Jeremy: I know someone that owes me a favor, that I'm sure that'd be willing to help me out one last time. Jessica: Who? Jeremy: Well if I told you, then it wouldn't be a surprise to Kid Wonder, now would it? Jessica knods her head, 'no'. Whoever this man is that seemingly owes the Krayzie One a favor, he's going to have a tall order to fill. There's not one, but two vital matches to the Krazed Lunatic's career coming up. After Clyde on Sabotage, the next piece of Jeremy's title picture comes up on Friday against the impressive Chet King. Beating this man one on one will be a task in itself. Beating this man with Kid Wonder lurking around will be a task that's nearly impossibly. Then again, remember this is Jeremy Howard. Jessica: Don't forget about Chet King. He did beat Mexi at Shanghaied to retain his title. Jeremy walks backwards over to his well designed black couch and takes a seat, still continuing to stare at his television screen. Jeremy: You know, I have nothing against Chet King. He's never done a thing wrong to me or had a bad word to say about me. But, he is standing in the way of my destiny. Of my legacy. Jessica: Honey, it'll be a match to remember against two men that don't have to cheat to win. Jeremy lifts his right hand, stroking back on his hair one time. Jeremy: I could only hope so. I still can't forget about Drake Maxwell. You know d#mn well that he's going want to get his little face in the spotlight before March Madness. Jessica walks over to the window, taking a look outside on a sunny Elkhart day. Jessica: It's sickening how he's clinged to everything you've earned and built for yourself just to get noticed. It's truly sickening. Jeremy: No! What's going to be sickening is March Madness. Locking me up inside of a cage with that jackass is like locking a caged animal up with a piece of fresh meat...... Deadly. March Madness might be the night that most people pay there money to see the twenty man battle royal to crown a World Champion or for one man to retain. But, for Jeremy Howard, its the night that four years of h#ll could finally come to an end when he steps into the ring with Drake Maxwell in a match to try and free his brother of a h#ll far worse than prison life. The Krazed Lunatic is going to attempt to free his brother from the dark grasp that Drake Maxwell holds over him. Jessica: You're not planning on doing anything that'll get you sent to jail, are you? For the first time since the cameras started rolling, the Krayzie One takes his eyes off the blank television screen by lowering his head. Jeremy: I'm going to do whatever it takes for my brother to feel the warmth of freedom. Jessica: I understand that, but..... Jeremy: But nothing! Jeremy leaps to his feet, burning with rage inside of his two hundred and sixty four pound frame. Jeremy: If that f^cker thinks that he's going to keep trying to manipulate me and my life, he's dead wrong. I'm sick of Kid Wonder's constant beratings and Drake Maxwell's hold on my brother. Jeremy pauses, long enough only to catch his breathe. Then..... Jeremy: This week, sh!t is hitting the fan and the bullsh!it stops. For, someone is going to meet a psychotic ending! I don't care if its Clyde, Chet, Drake or Kid Wonder. Someone! Jessica just stands still, trembling at the fearful sight of Jeremy Howard. If Drake and Kid Wonder were trying to get under his skin, they succeeded. But, at what cost? Jeremy: And I think its going to start tonight! Now lets get out of here. I'm sick of watching Drake Maxwell promos. They have nothing to do with me and they bore me to death. Jessica, somewhat hesitantly, grabs the right hand of the Krazed Lunatic as he begins to lead her out of the living room and out of the house. If you're an enemy of Jeremy Howard, then you might want to take Sabotage off from screwing with him. He's one pissed off twenty one year old man. Knowing that, and hopefully sinking into the minds of a few people, the scene fades out to the front door of Jeremy's house closing shut..... to black!
