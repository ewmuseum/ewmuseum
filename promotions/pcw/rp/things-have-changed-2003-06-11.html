Things Have Changed

11 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/06/03 Things Have Changed… -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Change is all around us,Often it’s all we see.As days go by and the sun does set,It is the centre of our being.Some say that it’s inevitable, That change in life will never end. And we fight to constantly adapt, As life takes us around each bend. It can strike us in the heart, It can turn life upside down. For better or for worse, It often makes one frown. But change, change is needed, Through each and every day. Change it is essential, In each and every way. It effects who we are, Changes who we will become, Gives us meaning to stand up and fightUntil this life is done. Change, it is quite simple, It’s message is quite clear. Be ready… be prepared..Because change… change is here… -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Part One… Some say that change is a good thing. Me? I’ve been forced to adapt to change all of my life. But it wasn’t until now that I realized just how much a little change can have such a huge impact on an immense amount of people. It’s no secret that my life has seen it’s fair deal of change over the last few months. The way I looked at the world… changed. The way I looked at my own life… changed. And then there was light. All it took were a few simple words. Your winner… and the NEW Primetime Championship Wrestling Extreme Champion… Manuel… Labor… It was here that the true change in life had begun. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- [ I find myself walking the busy streets of my adopted home town of Thamesville, Ontario, Canada. Night has taken over the light of day, as I walk among the very same people I have lived my entire life with. There is a certain feeling in the air on this night. Although they are all running here and there trying to find the perfect item in the shops for those they care about, everyone seems to have a second to stop and say hello. To give a simple greeting. To act the way we all would if this was a perfect world. ] But we’re very far from living in a perfect world… [ We walk among one another every day of this journey called life. Mixing in the crowds, we attempt to gain acceptance. We want to be accepted. We need to be accepted. But here, in this time and place, no one worries about acceptance. Why? Because we join together as one. We are all accepted. I guess it’s the magic of the Summer season that puts a smile on everyone’s face. The same magic that makes every child anxious and excited for the night never to arrive, just so he or she can keep playing out in the yard . The same magic that lights up every man, woman and child’s life every single year. The same magic that seems to be almost contagious. The same magic that turns any grown man into the smallest child. Magic is all around us. ] [ I haven’t got any shopping left to do. If I had someone to buy for, then maybe that would be different.Those thoughts don’t bother me right now. Nothing can throw me off at this point in time. ] [ You see… I AM that grown man whose heart is filled with the magic of Summer. I AM that man whose life has been lit up by a ray of hope, and happiness. I AM the man walking that common path of acceptance. I have found myself. I have found acceptance. ] [ I really have no reason to be out here this late. But the feelings inside me when I see these people during the Summer sun… they consume me. I simply walk among them, to be a part of this feeling. To join in. To share the contagious magic of the season with all of those around me. To give back to the small town that helped me become the man I am today. This is where it all began. People had been saying that I couldn’t do it from day one. I was looked down upon as having a stupid dream. It would be too hard. I couldn’t handle it. Wrestling was a joke anyway. This is where it all began. ] [ The resilience…. the adversity… the doubt… the magic. ] -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- [ Everyone I pass has a smile, a nod, or a few kind words for the young man who was once thought to be incredibly ridiculous for having an absurd dream of becoming a professional wrestler. I remember those days. The days when I trained young aspiring wrestlers who had the very same dream I had lived with all of my life. The days that made me realize that I had a long road in front of me, yet, I was very privileged at the same time. The days where people looked down upon me for having such hopeless ambitions. I had served my community well, but I was heading on a one way road to the middle of nowhere. ] That was then… this is now… [ Now I stand amongst them, not a disappointed face in sight. I’ve become the hometown hero that most of us can only dream about. I have pride in my roots, and always have, but now that I know how many others feel that pride inside of themselves for the small town boy who is making his dreams come true… now I know this is truly what I was meant to do. ] And I don’t plan on stopping any time soon! [ The sun is warm. I doubt a single person even notices it on this evening. Light is streaming profusely in every possible direction, but it only adds to the sentimentality of this seemingly perfect night. I look around, and it’s as if time stands still for a few seconds. Small children walk hand in hand with their parents, their faces glowing with smiles. Teenagers run through the streets heaving baseballs at one another. Young lovers walk hand in hand admiring the beauty around them. An older couple sits on their front porch, getting some fresh air and sharing the magic of the season with one another. Smiles all around. Best wishes from everyone I see. It’s as if a switch has been turned on inside of everyone, reminding us of what life is truly about. The little things. ] It’s the summer. The best time of the year. Peace Tranquility Happiness Why can’t life be like this every day?
