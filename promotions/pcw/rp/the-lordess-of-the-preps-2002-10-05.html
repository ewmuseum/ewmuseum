The Lordess Of The Preps

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Lordess Of The Preps Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 5/10/02 “Eugh... Just typical of how people, like, SO judge.. Um, like, other people...” We open up on the shaking head of PCW Commissioner, Eleanor Hall. The little prep is standing in front of a giant rack of DVDs and in her hand holds the casing to one particular film that has a certain empathy with her... ... Clueless. Eleanor : “I mean, like, HELLO?!?!? They SO make people like me out to be.. Um...” She stops, trying to think of the word.. Trying harder and getting more frustrated, it showing clearly in her body language. Eleanor : “Like, stupid and stuff!” Beside her a customer walks up to the rack and browses it quickly, grabbing a copy of Clueless himself and going to walk off when Ellie~!’s voice is heard behind him. Eleanor : “Er.. You....” He turns around, wondering what the woman behind him wants and is met by the PCW Commissioner standing with the hands on the hips of her navy Blue mini-dress, staring at him with her pale Blue eyes, her head slightly tilted in annoyance. Eleanor : “You, like, should SO boycott that DVD!” The customer looks to his DVD then back up the aggravated Ellie~! Eleanor : “It is TOTALLY detrimental to preps!” Looking confused, the man then starts to chuckle to himself, pointing the DVD casing at the PCW Commissioner. “This is a publicity thing, right?” She looks back at him blankly. “You know, like when they had the Lord of the Rings, all these people dressed up as Dwa-” Upon hearing the title, the little prep’s look morphs from aggravation to intrigue. Eleanor : “Lord of the Rings.... I SO need some new jewellery!” Not knowing quite how “Ellie~!” Ellie~! can be when she really puts her lack of mind to it, the customer just thinks it’s part of the dumb Blonde act and points to the special rack set up behind her. “It’s there...” She looks to it, then back to him. Eleanor : “Okay, fine.. Go buy that or something.. Ellie~! has something else to deal with.” Walking off towards the pay desk, the man shakes his head and chuckles to himself while the prep scurries over to the Lord of the Rings display. Eleanor : “I hope it has some stuff with Sapphires, my eyes need highlighting.” Her pale Blue doe-esque eyes gaze at the DVD case, confused by what it shows. Eleanor : “Like, what’s with all the stupid looking people on the front?” Undeterred she picks up a case and walks off to the counter. Eleanor : “They must be, like, the people who make the rings or something...” Reaching the desk, she is stuck at the back of the queue of about ten, and after a few seconds the little prep starts to fidget, bored of waiting. Eleanor : “Er.... HELLO?!!? Service? This is, like, a SHOP isn’t it?” Those in front of her tut and shake their heads at her whiny, impatient voice. Eleanor : “Hey, I’m, like SO wanting to buy something here!” She stands on her tip-toes, still not elevating herself enough to see over the people queuing in front of her... She then starts to jump on the spot to see over them, still failing. Eleanor : “Must be self service of something then.” The prep shrugs and peels off from the queue, making her way to the exit where she reaches into her purse and pulls out one of the numerous Credit Cards and hands it to the Security Guard who stares back at her dumbfounded. Passing between the 2 detectors, Ellie~! sets the alarm off, forcing her to cover her ears. Eleanor : “Er.. HELLO?!?!? I know I left, like, no reason to remind me...” As the Security Guard walks out through the doors, looking at the disappearing back of the prep, Credit Card in hand and a look of bemusement in his face we fade to Black. “YOOOOOOU SHALLLLL NOT PASSSSSSSSSSSS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!“ With the thunderous roar of Ian McKellen’s “Gandalf”, our shot fades back in on the totally blank look of Ellie~!’s face... The reflections of the movie shown in her pale Blue orbs that pass for her eyes. Eleanor : “What in....” On the wide screen TV in front of her, the Wizard stands on the bridge in the Dwarf Kingdom, the giant fire demon before him. Eleanor : “.. Charisma Carpenter’s name....” The demon falls, but makes a grab for the Wizard, bringing down more of the pass and sending Gandalf plummeting... Ellie~! seeming totally unmoved by it all. Eleanor : “Does this have to do with JEWLWERY!?!?!“ Her expression stays blank as she sits curled up in a giant armchair, the only light being cast coming from the TV. Eleanor : “I’ve only seen, like, one ring and they SO have no concept of how a model should look...” Still her eyes watch the screen, confused yet transfixed as the shot again fades out to Black. “MY LITTLE POON PUPPY, I’M HOME!” With the sound of a door slamming and his voice booming out, we fade in on PCW Champion Xavier Kannon as he arrives home to a dark house. He flicks the hall light on before wandering in further, waiting for a reply from his wife. Xavier : “Ellie~!? You didn’t accidentally lock yourself in the Fridge again did you....” As the Scientologist opens his kitchen door he is in for something that he never expected... *KABOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM* *WHIZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ* As he pushes the door open a bright Orange thunderbolt shoots right at his face. Xavier : “IN XENU’S NAME!!!!!!!!“ He quickly hits the deck as it whizzes over his head and crawling into his kitchen he sees Ellie~! in her robe, standing over a large Black saucepan with some bright liquid boiling in it.. Orange and Green energy whizzing all around her. Eleanor : “Oh, hey.... I thought I’d, like, try and kill Clyde with a spell... It smells of Limes!” As another huge explosion echoes around the kitchen to accompany hundreds of miniature Green fireworks going off at once that again cause Xavier to grab his head and duck down. Eleanor : “I, like, SO don't know why we didn’t do this before... “ Her eyes looking at all the bright explosions around her that continue to dart through the air, the little prep doesn’t notice that the saucepan is reaching a dangerous boil. Xavier : “Maybe it’s becau-...” Before the Scientologist can finish a massive eruption happens from the pan, sending out a blast of Green and Orange explosions that send the little prep scurrying to the floor where she holds her ears and screeches at the bright balls of fire and light that skip through the air above her head as the shot fades out to Black. “...-se of things like this....” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
