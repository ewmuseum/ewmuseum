The New Manager and The Internet

30 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30-12-02 Bob Vurdo can't go out in public at the moment well at least until the election is over. He has to pretend he is scared of the person who is corrupting the election so it doesn't become obvious that it is Bob Vurdo himself. He is now one hundred percent certain the election will be won by himself and Bob Vurdo will be the mayor of Amber Hill. He only has to wait until Thursday to begin his reign of corruption on Amber Hill which will end up making him one of the most powerful men in America. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Bob Vurdo walks into the joint kitchen and dining room with the local paper in his left hand. He walks over to the table where he places the paper and sits down. The dining room is relatively big with various painting hung up on the walls which overlook the dinner table which is set up in the middle of the room. The kitchen bench is the only thing that separates this room from the kitchen where Mrs. Vurdo is standing as she prepares dinner for the two. She greets Bob with a smile on her face as he sits down and begins looking at the paper. 'How are you Bobby?' Bob looks up at his mother who is wearing a black shirt with an apron over the top, her lower body is covered by the kitchen bench. Bob smiles at his mother who means so much to him. 'I'm great mum and how are you?' 'Ah okay.' Mrs. Vurdo's voice trails off which suggests she isn't that happy but isn't going to complain about it. 'What's the matter mum?' 'You know bob I get quite bored nowadays with nothing to do' Mrs. Vurdo takes a deep breath in to suggest she is sick of the boredom. 'Why?' 'I stay at home all day and all I do is cook, drink and watch television.' Mrs. Vurdo walks over to the oven where she checks the lasagna. 'That's what most people do at your age.' Mrs. Vurdo turns around looking quite angry at Bob. 'You know I don't want to become some old American retired woman with no life bob. I want to be able to do things with my time and have fun.' Bob decides to back away from the insults. Mrs. Vurdo is an Italian woman and the old people in Italy live life to the fullest. 'Do you have anything in mind?' 'I would like a paper work job but not a whole day paper work job just bits and pieces and maybe attach some action to that.' Bob begins to realize what she is saying. He doesn't want his mother to get involved in the business as she would be horrified to find out all the things they do. 'No Mum you are not joining the Vurdo family you mean to much to me for you to do that.' 'No you know I would never do that.' Bob is relieved to hear she doesn't want to get involved in the family. 'Bob I would like to well..... You know your wrestling?' He looks up curiously at her to see where she is going with the wrestling. 'Yeah' 'I was watching you the other night and I noticed that another wrestler had a manager.' Bob then realizes what she is talking about. Mrs. Vurdo wants to be Bob's manager in PCW. PCW is a tough place where people can end up killed like Kai and Bob doesn't want that for her. 'No mum its not a good idea you could get hurt I don't want that.... I don't want to have to look out for you as well in the ring.' 'I wouldn't get involved in the matches I would just watch it would be something for me to do.' Bob tries to think of something to rebut but can't find any other logical arguments. 'Muuuuuummmm,' groans Bob. 'I'm not going to make you it's just that I get really bored sometimes you know and it would give me something to do.' Bob can't believe how easily she manages to manipulate him into agreeing. He doesn't want his mother being bored at home and besides she could stay away from the ring. 'Ok then you can come with me to Fusion but I don't know whether I want you out at ringside.' 'Thank you bobby and we will deal with that issue when it arises.' Mrs. Vurdo smiles before going back over to the oven where the lasagna is cooking. Bob sighs before going back to the paper that is sitting on the table in front of him. He begins turning the pages while reading the headlines of each article to make sure there is nothing about the election. The ringing of Bob's cell phone then interrupts him as he pulls it out of the pocket of the usual sharp black suit he wears. 'Hello.' 'Yeah Bob it's Tony.' 'Heya Tony.' 'Yeah the elections going well.... Good job.' 'Yeah we will have ourselves a new mayor on Thursday.' Tony laughs menacingly, 'Yeah I got a proposition for you.' 'Yeah?' Bob is curios to the proposition as Tony doesn't like to do things over the phone. 'Yeah you know the Internet?' 'Vaguely why?' Bob doesn't have much to do with computers other than working out the finances. He has only heard of the Internet but has never personally gone on it himself. 'I worked out we would be a lot better off if we ran things over the Internet.' Bob is confused by this he has no idea how fast technology is moving with computers. 'How would that work?' 'Well there are many programs we could get to talk to each other and page each other through the Internet. It would also be easier to plan things and get information.' 'I don't think I could do all that.' Bob is skeptical of his ability to get all this stuff when he doesn't even know what the Internet is like. 'I can get someone to set it up.' Bob thinks for a second and decides he can't turn down a safe offer when he doesn't know the full potential of what Tony is proposing. 'Okay well I'll give it a try for a day or two and if it runs well then we will keep it but I don't know if that will happen.' 'That's all I ask.' There is a slight pause in the conversation. 'Okay well I will cya later.' 'Cya,' Bob replies as he hangs up the phone before going back to the paper. He doesn't know about this Internet thing but he will see how it works out.
