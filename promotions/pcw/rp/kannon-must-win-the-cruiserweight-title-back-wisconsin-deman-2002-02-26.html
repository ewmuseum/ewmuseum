Kannon Must Win The Cruiserweight Title Back...Wisconsin Demands It...

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/02/02 Alliances 2001 will be remembered for many things. Maybe the return of Darkstar to cost Clyde the World Title, some would say the creation of the Wild Deuces, others Jeremy Howard winning the main event.....But to most it will be remembered for Alan Kreigman winning the Soda Endorsement match to make Snapple~! the official soft drink of PCW. Since then there has been much controversy as to whether an exclusive deal is in the best interests of PCW or whether Snapple~!‘s lack of bubbles mean it cannot be classed as a soda, and even in the face of competition from his Stablemate Darkstar’s Carbonated Goat’s Blood, Snapple~! has endured, just like L Ron Hubbard when he was first putting Scientology on the map and all the hates wanted to put him down. Today is an important day in the year of PCW’s official soft drink, for just like the march Madness Battle Royal where 20 of PCW’s top guys come together, it is the day of the Milwaukee soft drink convention, the time of year when the big soft drink retailers come together to shill their wares and products. We open up in the Milwaukee Convention Centre the evening before PCW’s Tuesday Turmoil. Dotted all around the floor space are numerous tables and exhibits housing the various Soft Drinks affiliated with the show, the top event on the Soft Drink calendar. As we take a quick tour of the floor we see the main players, Coke, Diet Coke, Pepsi, Dr Pepper, Fanta etc. trying to steal the show with their floor space, using huge cut outs to attract attention to themselves. Surrounding the centre stage brands are the smaller ones, Mountain Dew and most of the independent drinks, amongst them, PCW’s official drink....Snapple~! “What do you mean ‘You like Bubbles’?“ comes the quizzical voice of wrestling’s only Scientologist who is shown yelling at someone before him with a bag full of free soda samples, “You come here, you ask about Snapple~!, you say you are a PCW fan and see it advertised...Then you decide that you don’t want Snapple~! in your life because we don’t pump it full of Carbon Dioxide.” the Scientologist just shakes his head as the fan tries to explain, “No.....You’ve said enough to disappoint me...Please go...Please...” The Scientologist is dressed in his usual full length Brown leather coat with an open Black and Purple striped shirt under it, revealing a Black and Green Snapple~! t-shirt beneath it. With this he has a pair of Black boot-cut jeans with Green stitching and Purple embroidery down the left leg. His hair is neatly tied back to make him look more presentable as he liases with the public. The PCW fan lowers his head and walks off, upset that his thoughtless words have insulted Xavier Kannon, the next PCW Cruiserweight Champion. “That’s it......Go....Go support Chris Chase and drink Mountain Dew or whatever, that stuff looks like piss!”. he sighs and shakes his head at the loss of another prospective Snapple~! drinker. “Wait until you see all the new flavours you have given up kid!” Kannon then shouts after him, “Lime Green Tea! Very Cherry Tea! SNAPRICOT ORANGE!” The Scientologist collapses back in his seat in front of the elaborate Snapple~! exhibit with it’s large cardboard back ground of Yellow and Orange checks with pictures of the various flavours bottles attached to it. “This really isn’t going how I planned,” he mutters to himself, “Snapple~! is the official soft drink of PCW, I guess there just aren’t that many wrestling fans here today.” As Xavier talks to himself a man in a bright Orange suit with Black shirt and Orange tie walks over to him, a Tango logo on the back of his jacket. “Haha.....Look at the mighty Snapple~!“ he jibes at Xavier, “It looks like you don’t know when you have been Tangoed doesn’t it! Haha! Well, I don’t have enough time to stay here and laugh at your bubble-less drink, we actually have people to talk to! Haha!” Kannon shakes his head as the man dressed in bright Orange walks away. “Fine, look down on us...” he says to himself again, due to the lack of prospective Snapple~! drinkers, “Just like the New Breed look down on people, it is you who will be shown up. Chris Chase is living his last day as PCW Cruiserweight Champion and Tango will fall from grace long before the sugary fruit-flavoured elixir that is Snapple~!“ The Gold patron Meritorious starts to turn around one of the free bottles of Snapple~! in his hands, reading the label’s small print and nutritional value to pass the time. “Hmmmm, contains real fruit juice....” he mutters as he hums random tunes, looking down at the bottle. “Can you help me?” comes a voice to awaken him from his boredom as he looks up to see a pretty young woman, somewhere in her early 20’s and dressed in a Purple sweater and Blue jeans. “I am looking for a drink..” she ten adds. Kannon puts the bottle down and stands, smiling and showing off his piercing Green eyes to try and work his charms on the woman. “Well if you are looking for a drink, this is the place to be!” he gestures to the brightly coloured backdrop, “Look at the vast array of fruity goodness that we have on offer....You look like a Pink lemonade woman to me....” She takes a look at the selection of drinks on offer and narrows her eyes, trying to back out of it. “Um.....I was actually going to ask you about another stand?” she says nervously as the enthusiastic grin on Xavier’s face drops, “I was trying to find the Tab Clear stall...I don’t know if you know where it is?” “Tab.......Clear?” asks Xavier back at her, as if he had just been levelled with the worst insult possible. “You mean Tab Clear as in the clear Cola that didn’t taste of Cola but just tasted of something disgusting and....Well, it didn’t taste like it has natural fruit juices in like Snapple~!“ The woman backs off slowly, worried that Xavier will blow any second, before turning 180 on her heels and walking quickly through the crowds to get away from the frustrated Gold Patron Meritorious. Xavier gets up and walks towards the small Red curtain in the Snapple~! back drop and shouts through it. “Ellie~! It’s time for Plan B!” he yells before waiting for a reply. “Ellie~! we talked about this...We need to show Chris chase that he hasn’t won, that he may have taken Snapple~! out of Canada but we don’t care....America is the home of PCW’s official soft drink.....Ellie~!.....You said you would do it for the love of Snapple~!” He takes a few steps back as something tries to squeeze it’s way through the curtain, causing Xavier to have to hold the background to stop it falling over. “Fine then......” sighs the whiny voice of Ellie~! “I’ll do it, but is someone laughs at me then I’m going to jump on them and squash them like a big fat sumo!” she speaks with straining in her voice. Finally a giant object forces it’s way through the curtain, a giant foam Snapple~! bottle suit that stands about 7 feet tall and 4 feet wide. About 5 feet up is the face of Ellie sticking through where the neck of the bottle starts, looking none too happy about being in the middle of a giant bottle of Snapple~! Apple. “This suit makes me look fat!” she complains as she stamps her right foot on the ground, holding what parts of her arms emerge from the suit out to her sides, her pale Blue eyes showing the despair of her lost figure, “I look like......I look like....A SEVEN FOOT TALL BOTTLE OF SNAPPLE~!“ Xavier looks at her, trying not to laugh as she tries to walk around from behind the table. “You look fine my little Shag Squirrel!” he says, “I barely noticed it, honest, I just thought it was a new outfit you went out and bought this morning! Honest....I did.....Go get’em Tiger!” The Prep looks unimpressed as she waddles around the table in her big foam suit and stands in front of the table, swaying from side to side as she tries to keep her balance while waving people towards Kannon’s table. “Roll up, roll up.....See PCW star Xavier Kannon who is like, SO going to be kicking Canadian ass on Tuesday Turmoil when he totally beats up Chris the Canadian Chase!”. Despite her efforts this doesn’t seem to have livened the crowd out of their Snapple~! apathy. “Okay so you don’t hate Canadians....Hmmm...” she ponders what would get the people of Wisconsin riled.... ....Then, she has an idea. “Watch the next Cruiserweight Champion queer bash Dustin Downey’ GAY LOVER!” she boats as the 7 foot bottle of Snapple~! surrounding her shapely figure gets more attention. “Hmmm, a bit too far north for homophobes....I GOT IT!” she rejoices, doing a little leap on the spot and nearly losing her balance. “Yay me!” she adds after steadying herself. “LADIES AND GENTLEMAN! THE NUMBER ONE CHEESE FAN himself! Xavier Kannon!” the mention of dairy produce suddenly gets the crowd’s attention and they put down their cans of soda and flock around the Snapple~! exhibit. “He is the friend of the dairy cow, and is advertising Snapple~! which is the OFFICIAL drink of ALL dairy products! They are eve developing Snapple~! Milk, and Cheese flavoured Snapple~! Not to forget Milwaukee Buck’s Fizz Snapple~! the official soft drink of YOUR basketball team!” As Ellie~! continues her boasts a big crowd is forming, drawing away from all the other drinks and even the kid put off by the lack of Carbon Dioxide and the Tab Clear woman are back to join their fellow diary state natives in support of the Cow-friendly soft drink. “Um.....Yes......That’s right!” says Kannon, trying to follow on from Ellie~!’s un-planned link. “We at Snapple~! are long time allies of Wisconsin.....During the great Milwaukee soda famine of......Er......1927?.....We supplied cases of Snapple~! to keep the inhabitants well stocked in sugary flavoured alternatives to water! In fact, it was the Snapple~! regiment, the Snappers, they fought for Wisconsin in the War of Independence and virtually drove the British out of your fine and cheesy state!” As he speaks Ellie~! points over to him and works up the crowd. “Tell them what the so-called Messiah, Chris Chase said about the FINE state that this convention is being held in?” she questions, overstating each word and nodding through the suit to the crowd as she speaks. “Well, I didn’t want to offend these people with his......Harsh and spiteful words...” the Scientologist replies, looking innocent, “But.....They have the right to know I suppose...Chris Chase, is indeed....” he bows his head and shakes it, taking a sip from his Snapple~! “....He is A VEGAN! You heard it.....Chris Chase want’s the Wisconsin economy to die! He wants to see you punished for ever milking a cow!” “Milking............A...........Cow!” supports Eleanor, working the crowd up. Xavier clears his throat. “I’m sorry for saying that.....But you had a right to know, after all, when a man wants to destroy an entire state it’s inhabitants have a right to know.....So where are you going to be on Tuesday Milwaukee?” he says, standing up as he announces it. “TURMOIL!” they cheer in support of Xavier, the Snapple~! representative who blew the lid of Chase-Gate, the greatest scandal to hit the dairy state. “I’m glad to hear it!” Xavier continues, “I WANT your support.....I LOVE cheese.....In fact, do you know what I had for lunch today? I had a CHEESE sandwich......With BUTTER.....Then I washed it down with a glass of MILK....Um, and some Snapple~!” he claims, “Do you know what Chris Chase probably had....I bet he had a SALAD roll....With GLUTEN FREE bread.....He doesn’t support your economy, in fact, I bet he’s out liberating cows right now, forcing them to keep their milk inside them because it DISGUSTS him!” Ellie~! nods, moving the entire top few feet of her Snapple~! bottle suit, “It.......Disgusts......Him!” she sneers, sickened by the thought of the man who deported Snapple~! “He has already deported this fine drink from Canada, lets not let him do the same with dairy from Wisconsin....As the Doors once sung.....The time to act is now....” he states, rallying the troops, “ARE YOU WITH ME WISCONSIN!” he bellows, grinning as they yell back in support, “Do you LOVE your Cheese?” again they shout back positively, “So what, apart from Milk......ARE YOU PEOPLE GOING TO DRINK?” “SNAPPLE~! comes the resounding response from the Milwaukeeans as Xavier & Ellie~! smile and bathe in the crowds adulation as the shot fades out.
