Date: ... “ALRIGHT, MS. HARRIS!! I’ll pay my freakin’ rent, already!” “Just be sure ya do, you fuckin’ lowlife! And take this piece of aged garbage with you!” A gangly, nasty senior woman picks up the dusty tome of the unknown and flings it at poor Dorn Tracer, who manages to duck it time before its leathery surface smacks him in the face. The book goes spiraling to the cement of the outside sidewalk and opens upon impact, flipping to some random page. The belligerent walking mummy of a woman slams the shaky door of the motel and disappears within, leaving poor Dorn out in the cold. “Stupid blasphemous woman… I swear, she needs to pull that stapler out of ass…” Dorn thinks to himself, with a look of disdain across his shaded face. Dorn adjusts his new black leather trench coat so it sits comfortably on his strange new armor, and steps down from the stoop of the motel. Looking back for a moment to look at the run-down shit box, the Astronomer can only wonder what his life has turned into. The place is pure brick, with three windows lining either side of the front of building. Crammed between two abandoned business places, the shoddy motel is nothing to look at and little to use. But since Dorn’s been on a ‘leave of absence’ and drawing from his former RUSH contract, it’s been all he can afford. “And besides,” Dorn says to himself, in a trance of thought. “It’s not like I don’t have it anymore.” Consider this moment the beginning of your journey, my warrior… The eerie, woman-like voice of that dragon spirit suddenly echoes from deep within Dorn’s consciousness and sends the confused Astronomer into an instant state of panic. His eyes burst wide open and he begins to turn his head this way and that, ignoring the wondering stares of the people that pass him by. Even people in vehicles stopped at the traffic light give him a look, wondering just what is wrong with this guy. “LEAVE ME ALONE, DEVIL WOMAN!” Dorn screams, practically to the sky and urban landscape around him. His trench coat flails about as he searches desperately for a physical source that he knows isn’t there. Accept you destiny, Dorn. I am in control now. “Accept THIS!!!” Dorn dashes to the nearest street lamp pole, grabs a hold of it, and proceeds to smash his forehead right into the cold, hard steel. The blow sends him sprawling to the cement like a tree of flesh. Curious spectators jump back in response and some laugh while others just stare blankly. After a minute or so, the pedestrians resume their journeys to where ever while Dorn just lies on the cold concrete… out cold himself. The voice, however, doesn’t cease. You cannot resist, my star-born savior. En’Flarim, your Goddess, pleads to you. You have to help us. The Tiamats are falling out of favor. This ‘God’ that the mortals speak of is nothing but a figment of their imagination, but he’s still our level of faith. You must help us… one thing at a time, hero… read the book… The book, Tracer… now, awaken. Almost as if on cue, Dorn awakens with a headache with the power of 1,000 suns and a nice big bruise on his forehead. Already swelled and purple, it’s definitely going to need ice. Having heard everything En’Flarim said, Dorn can do nothing but wonder what it all means. ”My stars… hero? Destiny? Tiamats?? This crap is becoming too weird,” Dorn grumbles through his teeth. “I DEFINITELY need to see a shrink.” The book… “ALRIGHT! Hold your comets…” Dorn slowly climbs to his feet bruised, dazed, and confused. He quickly shakes his head to clear out the cobwebs and takes a quick gaze around just to make sure he’s not in an intergalactic void being attacked by a golden dragon. In his survey, he notices this ‘book’ that En’Flarim was probably talking about. Something tells him not to look at it… something tells him it’ll only lead to trouble. “But this En’Flarim thing probably won’t let me sleep peacefully unless I do… the woes of being an Astronomer.” Dorn then sighs, slowly walks over to the open book, and stares down at the uncovered pages. “I suppose a peak wouldn’t hurt…” The Astronomer leans towards the strange tome and as he gets closer to it, the text becomes clearer. Something about Gods and Goddesses is all he can make out from his distance. Finally, he picks it up and holds it out in front of him, staring at the pages. Since they make absolutely no sense, he decides to turn to what he assumes will be the table of contents. Instead, at the top in strange, black letters, it reads: Astrology Elders and HeroesBy: Jonathon Marley A little perplexed, Dorn continues to read further down. To this day, I still have no idea how the Great Tiamats came about, as they have no recorded history. Scholars and Scribes of Astrology have tried to document the stories passed on by the Tiamat descendents, but it’s been mostly their opinion. My attempt, namely this book, is to deal with the facts and merely put forth who the Tiamats were, and not what they did. With the letters being large, the page ends there. It seems to be this guy’s personal opinion of his own book. Having heard something about ‘Tiamats’ from En’Flarim, Dorn nods to himself and flips to the next page. Chapter 1: The Tiamats Here I shall list the Tiamats in hierarchy form, as that’s how they supposedly divided themselves. The Tiamats were the lords and Gods of Astrology, starting the science/religion itself and actually created a race that followed it. They died out long ago, but on with the lost. Further explanation shall be later. En’Flarim (Inflariam) – Goddess of All, She, The Creator, The Destroyer. The lead Tiamat. En’Flarim can take many forms, but is usually seen with her golden reptile beast form. Currently captive within the confines of the Great Star. “So that’s who this En’Flarim creature is…” Dorn says to himself as his eyes scan the rest of the list. Z’en (Zenander) – Lord and God of Matter. The second in command, below En’Flarim. Z’en controls the very bits of matter floating about, from the stars to the surface of planets. He can only change, however. He cannot create like En’Flarim can. Tartus (Tartorus) – Tartus the Vain. The Warrior Tiamat. Battled for many millennia before succumbing to the might of -----------. Former second in command. “Why is it crossed out?” Dorn thinks to himself with a puzzled look on his face. He scans down through the list and finds nothing else crossed out. Perplexed, yet interested, Dorn continues. Desima (Desimimuta) – Goddess of The Way. ‘The Way’ refers to the power of gravity and the force of pushing and pulling. She seems to control how the planets in our current Universe are aligned, and rotates them like toys. Sheor (Sheorzotam) – Sheor the Plague. Sheor represents everything bound to the ultimate force known as Chaos, from which the Tiamats were born. Despite this, Sheor is low on the Tiamat ladder. Disease, destruction, strife, and insanity are his forte. A very recent addition to the Tiamats, due to the coming of man from En’Flarim. These 5 are the only recorded Tiamats that the Astronomers know of. They all have secondary meanings and purposes, but their lesser gods, which I shall list next, fulfill these. And the page ends there. Dorn shifts his eyes to the next page, more intrigued than before, and sees what these lesser gods are all about. Chapter 2: The Lesser Gods These ‘Lesser Gods’ are just that: Underlings for the all-powerful Tiamats. Although they have their own purpose as well, they don’t have significance as big as the ones that Tiamats possess. Dormoral (Unknown) – Dormoral is a mysterious Lesser God, serving under both Z’en and En’Flarim. She controls the strings of Time and Space, piecing things together and creating whole Universes. No one is quite sure how this is done. At’Kellam (Atikelliam) – Lord and Lesser God of Being. The Lesser God responsible for the many races of creatures that inhabit the thousands of worlds across the Blackness. When a race dies out, it is At’Kellam’s doing. The Lesser God of Being serves under Sheor. Viticet (Viticet) – Goddess of Life. Viticet is responsible for the very essence of positive energy, which is used to create many things like the elements and water. She serves under Desima. Virus (Virus) – Not exactly a God, but a hero. Virus was a humanoid before humans were conceived. Virus was the one to topple the reign of terror brought forth by the Great Star before it settled and became a beacon of life. Virus was then inducted to the legions of Astrological Gods and Goddesses as a reward for subduing the power of the Great Star. “This is some messed up crap!! I knew Astrology had all-mighty beings, but this is almost too much!” Dorn says to himself, with a hint of irritation. He never knew the science/religion he followed had such a broad and strange background. “In fact, I don’t know I WANT to know anymore.” Dorn closes the book without thinking about marking his place and jams it into his coat. He then buttons the jacket up, jams his hands instead the leather interior of his coat pockets, and strolls along the sidewalk, walking to nowhere in particular. His own thoughts are the only things keeping him from zoning out. “Tiamats… Lesser Gods? HEROES? This is too much, someone could honestly go crazy from all of this. And if this Jonathon guy is telling the truth, then the head honcho is camping out inside of my mind!” I see you’ve discovered your masters, Dorn… “GRAH! Not you again!” Dorn yells at the spirit, right out loud as well. People turn from all directions and stare at him as he argues and shouts at himself, pretty much. Do not be afraid, nor confused. Things shall be revealed as your quest continues. Now, if you please, approach some means of transport. “Hold on a second, En’Flarim!! I’m not going another step without getting some damn answers from you! You’re supposedly the Goddess of All and yet you’re living inside of my head! GO POSSESS SOMEONE ELSE!” You shall know why I have chosen you. You have a purpose now, my warrior, and you shall fulfill it. If not, I’ll have to make do with something else. At the sound of this, Dorn practically smiles. He gives as sly, clever look to the air and nods, understanding perfectly. “So if I don’t want to do whatever this quest is or CAN’T, you’ll leave me alone?” Incorrect. I shall consume your entity and absorb whatever life you have. Suddenly, Dorn’s plan to get rid of En’Flarim melts away before getting a chance to take shape. His jaw drops in shock and he shakes his head, not wanting to believe it. “This has GOT to be a dream…” It is reality, and it is the will of the Tiamats that you follow my every instructions. Now, find us transportation. Though he definitely doesn’t want to, basically because he doesn’t know what to expect, something tells him to anyway. His feet move without him thinking about going anywhere… his body takes motion without Dorn himself sending the brain waves. En’Flarim is controlling his body… she’s MAKING him do her bidding. Dorn can do nothing but be shocked, frightened, worried, and angry… “I have such a bad feeling about this… I can’t do it…” “This thing is going to kill me.” [End]
