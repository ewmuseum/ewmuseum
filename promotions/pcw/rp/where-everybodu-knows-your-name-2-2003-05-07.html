Where Everybodu Knows your name (2)

7 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/05/03 The door creaked as Manuel let it close behind him. Taking off his jacket he turned and placed it straight onto the hook. Skylar Green could have done it no better. For Manuel everything was slipping back into place, it was like riding a bike. All the sights smells sounds and locations were things he would never truly forget. He quickly walked up to the bar and although he was recognised the barman wa spreoccupied and quickly got him his drink. Manuel downed it in one swift motion before requesting another from the barman. This drew a glare from the short balding man behind the 2 feet of solid oak. Spinning on his heel he quickly refilled Manuels glass and with a tilt of his head directed the Lucha Legend to a room on the other side of the bar. Labor grabbed his drink and walked to the door. This room passed for the VIP room in the place, it was more of a safety issue than privacy, he knew that. He knocked, causing the old, oaken door to vibrate heavily. The hinges were weak, the wood old. He heard a voice inside and grunted a reply. The door swung open and a huge man stood before him, his extra large suit hardly able to contain him. He looked him up and down and motioned him inside. Once there he knew the routine, he spread out his arms as the guy patted him down You'll want to check his right shoe. He heard the voice, he knew who it belonged to. Immediatlly the bodyguard checked his right shoe, easily finding the small knife he hid in the sole. Manuel grinned sheepishly and reached into his back pocket, removing the his mobile phone he and handed it to the bodyguard. He took the items and walked out into the bar, shutting the door behind him. The room was small, and there was just enough space to squeeze in a table and several chairs. At said table was Khaled....He was a little older, a little heavier, his long hair wasn't as vibrant as it once was, but it was definetly him. He had wrinkles around his eyes, both of which were staring straight at Manuel. 'And here I thought you wouldn't show up at all. I here you finally got your dream? Your father would have been proud! His grown son wrestling with other men who THINK they are Vampires and Spacemen!' Manuel clenched his fists under the table, not rising to the old mans bait. 'I almost didn't, but I want out' ~:: Khaled wrinkled his nose at him ::~ 'Well you might as well have a drink, lemme guess, Tequila? You always did drink that tapwater.' Manuel sat down at the table and downed his glass, letting Khaled pour him a new one. Leaning back he looked straight into the older mans eyes. Manuel- Listen, I don't really know where to start... Khaled- Start at the beginning, I said I'd give you 5 minutes, I figure you deserve that. Manuel- That wrestling shit you are talking about is my life, it used to be yours, at least have so fucking respect for it. I know it's not really an excuse, but that's why i wasn't there that night to back you up...That's why... They both lowered their eyes and crossed themselves..It was still to painful to say aloud Khaled- Why didn't you just call us? We would have understood. Manuel- I don't know why, maybe I was scared. I was facing a very real possibility of making it to the big game. Getting asked to dance with the most beautiful girl at prom, all that shit rolled into one! I was fucking scared man. No excuse, I know, and you can hate me if you want, but I had to get this off of my chest. I just hope that somehow we can put this behind us. He saw Khaled inhale sharply...He knew Dante was not one to make rash decisions Manuel-Look, I said my part, if you ever wanna talk you got my number, if you don't, that's fine, like I said I needed to get rid of this weight, I got a fresh life, I've gotta clean up my past. Mnauel got up and left Khaled to his thoughts. He picked up his knife and phone and walked back to his car. Just another piece of the puzzle put back into place. He felt better, his pain was gone.... now onto Fusion!
