The End: The Trailer (An El Diablo Film)

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/10/02 *Music Cue: Brahms’s Symphony #2, Allegro non troppo* *Fade into a black screen with white letters that appear in the center of the screen by typing. Cue the creepy voice that seems to do the narration for every movie trailer done inthis country today.* Voice:“The year is 2066.” *The camera pans up from the darkness to a bluish gray sky. Tall, wide buildings of gray metal reach to the upper levels of the atmosphere. Anti-gravity cars in all different colorsand shapes fly in orderly lines through the grand alleys and chasms that the sky-high buildings create.* Voice:“There is no crime. No war.” *A perfect utopia, tranquility in a scene of people of all races, sexes, and religions walking along on a long raised platform, the white walls and ceilings illuminated by an almostdivine light that shines down upon them all equally.* Voice:“And humanity,--” *Cue an approaching picture of Pharaoh sitting at his presidential desk in the Oval Officeat the White House, wearing the standard navy President’s suit. The sky outside is sobright that only the light is distinguished on camera through the single large window in themiddle of the wall. The maplewood desk is simply organized with a but a single flatscreenpop-up LCD imaging computer, and room to place personal belongings (mainly picturesof his mother and father). The American flag sits on a golden flagpole in the backgroundto his left, and a fern is on his right.* Voice:“-- Cyborgs,--” *Cue a quick single flash of Hessian, wearing his old PCW mask. His metal-braided hair flings the water off as he quickly turns his head from down the dark, rainy brick alley toward the camera. His eyes glow a scary red, the light emanating from them actuallyleaving a trail on camera.* Voice:“-- And androids--” *Cue to a picture of The Rush, running, almost gliding, quickly through the streets past computer-generated people, wearing a white skintight jumpsuit that makes him look like a futuristic version of the Good Humor Man. The streets are clean and dry. The storefronts have neon signs and automatic doors that allow people easy access. Electronic imaging replaces windows, and shows what merchandise is inside.* Voice:“-- Live in perfect harmony.” *Next, the camera cuts to a scene in which Pharaoh, Ivan Stanislav (who is dressed in his red flannel shirt, black pants, and suspenders, though his face is made to look metallic with eyepieces and a faceplate over his mouth), and Minion the Imp (who plays the AndroidDrax-600, dressed as Minion would normally appear in OSW shows and with his skin digitally altered to be blue) shaking hands.* Voice:“The only way things can go from here...” *Atop the U.N. building early on a cloudy morning, a fleet of re-outfitted Chinook helicopters lands on a helipad.* Voice:“... Is bad.” *The entire building explodes into a reddish-orange fireball. Bits and pieces of it fly far into the distance. The scene changes to Hessian, in his OSW attire, standing in front of a legion of followers.* Hessian:“The humans created us, but unlike Dr. Frankenstein, who was vastly disgusted by his creation, these humans are jealous of us, and all that we are capable of!” *A right scrolling shot of the camera takes the view past the level of the leather couch in the Oval Office which covers up the view, to a zoomed-out look at Pharaoh, Jesse Ramey, and Kwik FX (the Vice-President) arguing.* Pharaoh:“I don’t want to start a war here.” Ramey:“Sir, it’s too late for the olive branch approach.” Pharaoh:“It’s never too late to prevent war!” Kwik FX:“When are you going to realize that we’re already at war! We have been ever since we created those things!” *A greasy spoon diner with a white suede seating theme shows PWA cadet Charlotte sitting behind the counter wearing a white apron and pink shirt under it that shows enough of her cleavage to give the NTICW’s Scott Taylor (wearing a khaki bubble vest, jeans, and blue flannel) a good reason to gawk. The counter is impeccably clean.* Voice:“The blood of the innocent shall be spilled...” *A mere shot of blood spattering against a stainless steel wall, the light source coming from the camera’s POV. There is no silhouette, and nothing else in the shot save for thebright patch of light on the wall, and the blood trickling down.* Voice:“And a mysterious stranger...” *The End, standing in the middle of the street wearing a black trenchcoat, unbuttoned revealing his ring attire, which is also his movie attire. He holds an CGI-electrified sword. He is standing in the middle of a crowd of cyborgs, which seem to be shouting andcheering. A part in the crowd allows the camera to start with a far shot, and then move in closer until The End’s body takes up the entire screen from top to bottom.* Voice:“May be our only hope.” *Music Cue: “Bombshell” by Powerman 5000* *On a snowy day out in some pristeen countryside, five cyborgs charge The End with their backs to the camera. He cuts them down one-by-one with speed and precision. The cyborgs are flipped for a loop after each cut, landing on the ground with a snow-crunching thud.* *A window with red curtains, and two military guards standing at either side, rigid in their stance and holding their guns straight along their shoulders. The End bursts through thewindow and kicks them down.* *A dark street, with anti-grav cars parked along dirty buildings. All the buildings are made of old brick, and boarded up. The End runs down the street straight toward the camera, which moves back at the same speed. Behind him is an attack pod which fireslasers at him. The lasers miss, destroying the street as they hit the ground.* Voice:“This spring,World War 3 begins.* *El Diablo, dressed in dirty brown clothes and with his unkempt hair making him, look like a hermit, runs down a corridor in a sewer-like complex from far top right to near bottom left. There are four equally spaced gratings on the left wall, and pipes running along the ceiling, dripping water on the ground. As El Diablo runs past each grating, it blasts off the wall, narrowly missing his body.* Voice:“The fate of humanity hangs in the balance.” *Aimz falls out of a tall building on a stormy night, the tallest building around. Rent-A-Hero lunges out of the window, grabbing her arm to keep her from falling to her death.* Voice:“Some people have everything to lose.” *Ricky Thunder, his hair black and slicked down, wearing a gray suit, is standing behind his black computer-desk, his arms crossed behind his back, looking out of his window on an orange sunset over an entire city which he owns.* Voice:“And some have nothing to lose.” *In the same sunset miles away atop a grassy hill, Syn rises from his one knee and dusts off his red pants, zipping up his black jacket as a breeze starts to kick up. Syn slowly turns his head up to the sky.* Voice:“Will humanity be victorious?” *A huge battlefield in the old city of Los Angeles, with hundreds of humans standing and cheering with their guns raised in the air.* Voice:“Or will this truly be... The End?” *The screen fades out with a close-up of Hessian’s face.* Voice:“An El Diablo film. Coming Soon to a theater near you.”
