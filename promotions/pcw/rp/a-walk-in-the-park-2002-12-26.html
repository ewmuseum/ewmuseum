A Walk in the Park

26 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-26-02 Snow drops fall on New York softly. The white snow blanketed the land making everything look pure. All is peaceful. All is silent. Central Park looks untouched under the white snow. The sound of crunches breaks the silence however. A figure wrapped in coats and a scarf walks through the snow, crunching it under his black boots. The figure stops as a snow flake lands on his nose. He twitches his nose trying to get it off but sneezes, causing it to fly off. He rubs his nose and pulls off his hood revealing it's Chris Storm. 'I can't believe I failed.' he said. He looked down at the snow. He gives it one good kick tosses it in the air. The wind picks up and causes the snow to dance in the air. Chris starts to walk again as the images of CoC comes back to him. He can't help but smile as he remembers him and Tessa sitting together on the bench. Chris picks his head back up and looks up at the sky as the snow flakes continue to fall. 'She looked really cute dressed in the helper costume. I hope she kept it. Might come in useful later.' Chris laughs to himself. He walks over to a tree and places his hand on the bark of it. Chris looks out over the white park. No one else seems to be in the park today. Everyone must be home with their families. Chris would be home too, but after getting the news about the next Turmoil he had to escape the house. He had to get away from Tessa. 'They are making us face each other? How can they do such a thing to us?' he told himself. He balled up his fist and punches the tree. 'They know I can not hit Tessa! I refuse to touch a single hair on her head. They just want to try and break us up. But I refuse to let it work.' He yells at the tree. Chris punches the tree again and again releasing his anger. He closes his eyes as the blood inside him begins to boil. The blackness begins to consume him. Chris tries to open his eyes but he is still engulfed in the darkness. He tries to look around for something, anything. 'Tessa!' he yells out in the vastness of it all. The scream falls on deaf ears. He quickly starts to panic. Chris looks around for someone, anyone. Finding no one he yells out again. 'TESSA!' still silence. Chris falls down on his knees. The energy drained from his body. Then a white light shines. Chris has to cover his eyes, the light is so bright. He is finally able to look at the source of the light. His jaw nearly hits the floor, if there is one in this endless pit of black, and his energy returns. Chris sees the one thing he wanted to see more then anything else. Tessa. 'Tessa!' he yells as he runs toward her. 'I'm so glad to see you.' but he stops dead in his tracks. Something isn't right about Tessa. She isn't looking at him. Instead her back is turned to him. Chris can see someone else in front of Tessa but he can not make out who it is. Chris walks over to Tessa and tries to put his hand on her shoulder but his hand just goes through her. Chris nearly stumbles onto his face but he is able to keep his balance. When he looks back up he can see who the other person with Tessa is. 'Jackson Rust?' he mouthes to himself. Chris is trying to figure out what is going on when Jackson suddenly puts his hands on Tessa's shoulders. They look into each other's eyes. Chris stands back shocked. 'Oh Jackson! Thank you for saving me.' she tells him. Jackson smiles and bring on hand under her chin and lifts it up with a finger. 'Do not worry Tessa. I'm just ashamed to see Chris left you in your hour of need.' he tells her. Tessa frowns. Chris however is very livid. He raises his fist to Jackson. 'You bastard! I didn't leave her! We decided it would be best to split up. So don't go saying I left her all alone! I would never do anything like that. Tessa knows it.' he shouts at Jackson. But his words are like a pray and can not be heard by them. Tessa looks into Jackson's eyes and smiles. 'Tessa, I will always be there to protect you. I promise.' he tells her. Then he brings his face closer to hers. Chris tries to stop him but it's useless. Jackson Rust plants a soft kiss on Tessa's lips. 'Noooo!!!' Chris yells at the top of his lungs. He starts throwing mad punches at Jackson but they all miss him. The blackness starts to crack all around him. Chris continues to try and attack Jackson. He goes for a Spinning Heel Kick but lands on his face. The darkness all around cracks more and more as Chris tries to hurt Rust. Almost as if it is taking the damage. Chris doesn't even notice the abist breaking up. He is too consumed in his own rage. Jackson slips his arms around Tessa's waist and pulls her in closer for a more passionate kiss. Tessa's eyes light up in surprise and excitement. Chris sees this and starts slamming his fist onto the black ground. 'Oh Jackson!' Tessa lets out. 'Jackson, just hold me. I feel safer in your arms then anywheres else. I know you won't leave me like Chris did.' she tells him. Jackson looks directly at Chris. Chris' blood turns cold as his body freezes. Jackson gives him a smirk and starts to hold Tessa closer to him. Is Jackson looking right at him? The utter blackness soon begins to turn a crimson red. The cracks in it appear to bleed. Chris begins to breath deeply as anger fills him. He grabs his head and looks at them again. Chris lets out a mighty roar. 'STOP IT!' The endless stretch of red shatters and Chris's eyes shoot open. He sees he's back in the white park. Chris looks around the park setting. He lets out a breath of relief. 'It was just a nightmare.' he tells himself. Chris leans his head against the tree and lets out a laugh to calm his nerves. Chris looks around and suddenly turns pale white. There staring at Chris is the image he sees. The one that looks just like Chris. Only he has scars on his face. The one was still wearing army camo gear. The image laughs in a manical voice. He looks directly at Chris. 'You are weak. Letting that man walk all over you like that. I thought Tessa was your pride and joy? And yet you just let him come right on in and win her over. How sad. You truely are a failure.' he tells Chris. The cold words are like daggers to his chest. Each one a blow to Chris' pride. Chris' head falls again. 'You are right. But I won't give up without a fight. I will turn that friendship match into a blood bath when I make Jackson bleed in the ring! I'll show Tessa he is too weak to protect her. That I am the only one strong enough to ever protect her!' Chris shouts. He looks around for the image again, but it disappeared into the wind. The only reminder Chris has that he was there. That and the cold feeling Chris has deep down in his heart. The bitter wind starts to get to him as he wraps himself up in the scarf again and starts walking back toward the apartment. Tuesday will come and fates will be decided. Will Chris lay a hand on Tessa? Will she strike at him? What effect with Jackson Rust have on this match? Only time will tell and that time is ticking down.
