If I am a Gorilla, what is he?

24 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th Feb 2002 [It has been one rough ride for the Samoan Savage, from the breakdown ofManagement, to the on going war with the New Breed, everything seems to bea burden. Though thanks to Boda and his victory over the New Breed Leaderin Jason Snow, not only has the spotlight been taken away, Afwa now hasthe chance to get back into the heart of things and show that he can reallylook out for himself, regardless if his best friend Timo Bolumba is therebeside him or not. Though once more the week ahead is to be a tough one, for two of the threeevents will see this wild samoan in action and for one of the events, itwill place Afwa and Timo together, though Friday shall come, but before then,Afwa Kilmanjaro has to mae sure he is focused on the task that Tuesday Turmoilplaces in front of him, that of Jay Sylo, a man whom Afwa has no knowledge of,though since Sylo's entrance into the PCW, Afwa can see that he is on a missionand it just so happens that this mission involves one stumbling block in the formof a mountain, that of Afwa Kilmanjaro. As we approach Afwa today, he has justcome off a tremendous victory along with Timo Bolumba and Boda over the New Breed,so you'd think we would at least be happy, I guess not, for as the cameras get closerAfwa is seen grunting..] Afwa: Grr.. Timo: Afwa, what is wrong? Afwa: Afwa no gorilla! [Obviously Afwa Kilmanjaro is alluding to the comments that Jay Sylo made about him.] Afwa: Timo, Afwa monkey? Timo: I think you mean are you a monkey. Afwa: That what Afwa say! [Timo chuckles.] Afwa: You laugh at Afwa? [Afwa not smiling as he can't belive that his true friend is laughing at him,though he forgets about it and just pats Timo on the head.] Timo: No, I was not laughing at you! I was laughing, at you thinking youcould possible be defined as a monkey. Afwa: Afwa no monkey! Afwa savage! Timo: Afwa, you should not take what people say too serious. I don'tthink Jay Sylo literally meant you were a gorilla, it's just a figment of speech. Afwa: Afwa no like Sylo! Sylo be meanie to Afwa! Timo: Afwa, Jay Slyo is a very disfunctional man at the moment, I would notlisten to a word he says. [Afwa sighs.] Afwa: He hurt Afwa! Timo: What? Afwa: Afwa make Piggey wiggey pay! Sylo Afwa make squeel like piggie. Timo: Afwa, Jay Slyo is not a pig, he is a human being and if you do nottake him serious then on Tuesday night, he will cause you a lotof pain! Afwa: Jay Sylo, many a man have tried to get into the world of the Samoanknown as Afwa, so many end up flat on their face, will it be thesame for you? Timo Bolumba speaks wise words to me, has taught mea few words of English and its these words which no matter how they are spoken will not be the key to destroying you. [Afwa amazed at the words he just constructed seems now to be on a rolland decides to continue.] Afwa: Jay Sylo, to understand Afwa, you have get under my skin. You have todo such evil things not even you could contemplate doing, unlike me.Jay Sylo, you say that Afwa is not your intended victem, though youdo not care, for in your eyes, even though I am innocent, you willtry to abuse Afwa, in the intent of doing better for your own needs. Timo: What are you going on about? Afwa: Comedy gets you so far, today, I decided to take a serious approachand leave the comedy behind. Jay Sylo, just like you, me, Sylo, youinitaially were not my intended victem, for you are not the New Breedand my war is against them, though you call Afwa bad names and nowAfwa show you who big, who strong and why he can and will CRUSH YOU! Timo: Afwa, are you sure you know what you are doing? Afwa: Afwa ready.. Afwa fight Piggie! Timo: Oh. [Timo Bolumba looks at Afwa as if to say, I sure hope you know what youare getting yourself into, for I sure don't. Whilst Afwa just standsthere smiling and acting as if he is a big kid who is about to getto play with a new toy, only this time, the toy is not new it's aa small pigee in the form of Jay Sylo. If only he knew, if only. We all fade up and then it is black..]
