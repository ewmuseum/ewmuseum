Date: not entered Night life… never in your life will you see a more splendid sight. The darkness envelopes the light like a silk cocoon around a caterpillar. The darkness is welcoming…draws the citizens out from their homes to enjoy the wares that the city of Tokyo only have to offer when the sun sets. These wares are numerous… but they all have the main purpose of entertainment. Clubs, dance halls, bars, movie theaters, strip clubs… they all seek to please the people. The night… pleases the people… a trait in common with one Miss Moonlight. She was perched on a rooftop watching the streets intently, her hand cupped over her brow to make her scan more precise. She was there to please the people… protect them from the night they so enjoyed. Because as much as they liked to play when the darkness came calling… evil knows only to lurk when the sun comes down. “By the Moon!” she muttered disappointedly. “There’s not a crime in sight! How will I train my skills against those who wish to do wrong under the watchful eye of the MOON?? By the eye of the MOON if there’s no villainy to trounce, there’s no way I can efficiently train for my Cruiserweight battle royal!” She sighed, dangling her feet over the edge of the roof and leaning back on her hands. She looked up at the sky, her eyes growing bright as she looked into the wide, round full moon surrounded by stars. “All I wanted to do was be the best I can be,” Miss Moonlight whispered to herself… or in her own personal conversation with the Moon. “I wanted to help the people…make their lives better in more ways than one. I want to protect them…and I want to entertain them…but if I can’t keep myself sharp, then what good am I as a heroine and a wrestler?” -------=------- The moon shined down brightly in crescent form, a sideways smile beaming down at the one…maybe the only one… who spoke to it every night. “Dear Moon… please look after my mummy and daddy, both who work hard at what they do. My mommy tries hard to raise me right, and my daddy fights bad guys night after night to make the world safe. Please protect them both, and keep them well and safe. And please let me grow up to be as strong, caring, and good as they are. Thank you.” “All right sugarbee… it’s time for bed.” A small child looked up from her dolls, a raven haired pixie with familiar bright brown eyes. She smiled at the man’s voice, crawling across the room and submerging herself under the blankets of her futon mattress. Her head poked back out, a smile on her face as well as a black haired stuffed rag doll cradled with one arm. “Will you tell me a story, daddy?” the girl asked in an innocent voice, almost as epitome of total purity. That was a rare thing in this world of heathens… everyone only thinking of themselves, no one else thinking of society around them. It was almost sad to see the world turn so selfish… and in this battle of many numbers only one could win… But if that one person won for the people… it could really make a difference. “Another story?” kneeling down next to the bed was an young looking man, about in his late twenties, with short black hair. He wore a pair of black slacks with bare feet and a white police officer’s shirt. He ruffled the little girl’s hair and smiled warmly at her. “Didn’t I just tell you one last night?” The girl giggled at her father. “You tell me one EVERY night, daddy!” He scratched his head with confusion in his eyes. “I DO?” “DADDY!” He laughed along with his daughter. They loved their special little time together at night, when it was just him and her, and the moon shining down through her window. This was her time when her father was a hero, the only man in the world that could take down Godzilla himself with but a stern look! She wanted to grow up with his courage and strength… because it was the best thing she knew existed. “All right… well today at work two people tried to break the law, but I caught them and showed them a thing or two!” Her eyes widened with attentiveness. “What did they do??” He pointed to his nose and winked. He would never tell her what crimes they committed, leaving it to her imagination what despicable things they could’ve done. But the story always ended the same way… no matter what. “And in the end… justice prevails!” The child clapped with joy, loving each bit of it as her father told it. Then he leaned over and kissed her on her forehead, smoothing her hair and smiling at her once more. He then got up and walked across the room to the door, where he looked back once more at his pride and joy… his only daughter. “G’night daddy…” she said softly, sleep beginning to set in. His smile never wavered. “G’night… Naomi.” ------=------ Miss Moonlight smiled heartily at the moon, old memories fluttering in and warming the heart and soothing the soul. She had a lot to prove, not only to herself and to her parents, but to the city of Tokyo and the fans of PCW. Rent A Hero and Mang A Hero hand picked her themselves to be the sole protector of Primetime Championship Wrestling… and she wouldn’t disappoint them both. Both of them were great heroes, now taken out of commission by the harsh war on evil they fought. Now it was her turn to take command on the never ending war… it was her turn to become a war hero. And the first battle of the Jr Heavyweight Royal… would be fought on a squared battlefield… bordered by cable ropes… body after body being flung off the field in the melee, and in the end… one soldier will be left to claim the prize. Miss Moonlight knew it would be her… she was certain of it by the light of the Moon. Tires screeching below broke her thoughts. She sat back up and peered over the edge, watching a car pull up to the building she sat on hastily. She watched the three people get out of the car, and instantly she knew that the Moon was watching down on her, shining her grace and mercy upon her. “Alas… suitable training partners for my match! The Moon is a generous being!” And so she ran off to intercept… -------=------- “I swear, I’ll never go to that bar again!” Shun walked over to the light switch and flipped it up, casting down a spotlight on a single round table in the center of that small dingy apartment. Eiko nodded, sighing as she sat down on the stool in front of the table and laying her head down on it. “Miss Moonlight whipped our asses… AGAIN!” “I don’t know how you two let her beat you down! I mean… you had weapons for Christ sake!” Ryo kicked off his shoes and stomped over to the table, taking at seat on a small chair. He turned his cap backwards and lit up a cigarette. “I mean, come on now! I thought you knew how to use those things!” Shun flipped the middle finger at him. “We DO know how to use em! It’s just that we can never get a clear shot on that Moon bitch! She’s just too quick!” “As soon as we swing, she’s waiting to throw us to the ground and make us beg for mercy.” Eiko said, her face still pressed into the table. “I swear she’s like a machine! You can’t land a punch!” “You can’t land anything for that matter.” Ryo’s voice was to the maximum of hopelessness… for some odd reason whoever Miss Moonlight fought against, their voice took that drastic turn for the worse and began to grow a despondent tone… like there was no chance on earth of defeating her. Heh, maybe they were right. Shun tossed a few cold cans of beer to his allies from the mini fridge in a corner of the dank and barren room. “Enough of the “WE CAN’T BEAT MISS MOONLIGHT!” sh-t. Let’s just divide up the goods we got tonight.” He poured out a sack of stolen goods onto the table; wallets, clips of money, credit cards, cell phones, watches and jewelry. The mother load of pickpockets and lifted merchandise. Their eyes gleamed at the bounty before them, almost salivating in a cartoon manner of greed. You couldn’t look down on these three… it’s not like they were the scum of society that deserved to be shunned and cast aside. As a matter of fact…they were just like anyone else you would meet. Hard luck people trying to live life the best they knew how… and the best way they knew how was by doing the wrong thing. You shouldn’t pity them, you shouldn’t boo them… just accept their acts for what they are…because it’s not your place to judge them. They were everywhere… including in PCW. You can’t even count on your fingers and toes how many times someone cheated or stolen a title in a match, snatched it away from the clutches of he or she who was seconds away from winning. It’s and injustice… it’s a fluke. The people want to see the right person for the job win the title… and that person was Miss Moonlight. And rest assured…she’ll get the job done… she’ll win the Cruiserweight title. “Okay,” Eiko started out, rubbing her hands together in traditional greediness. “As usual, I get the jewelry.” “Why’s that?” Ryo protested. “These pieces will put off a nice price on the streets!” “Because if I’m gonna start pullin in a better level of mark in my line of work, I need better jewelry!” Shun chuckled to himself. “Which means she has to look like a HIGH class skank to bring in HIGH class “johns”.” Eiko glowered hard at him. She must’ve been sensitive of her street walking scam… but hey, it brought in the cash like you wouldn’t believe. “At least I have class PERIOD…unlike you and your brutish ways.” “Prove it!” Ryo folded his hands and grinned. “The common brute has a certain dialect that makes them appear more threatening… which means low brow speech like “OH YEAH” and “C’MERE A MINUTE”.” “OH YEAH!!?” Shun curled his finger towards Ryo, his fist balling up at his side. “C’MERE A MINUTE!! I’ll show you!” His sat there motionless as Eiko and Ryo stared at him, satisfied looks on their faces. Shun slumped back in his seat and took a sip of his beer. “F-ck you both.” Outside… Miss Moonlight landed on the balcony gracefully, watching inside at the three hoods argue amongst themselves. She catches her breath before leaning against the balcony railing in a provocative position. “The Moon watches down on us all…” … no response. She blinked, watching the trio pay no attention at all to her. She clears her throat and says it once more, this time with feeling. “The Moon watches down on us all…” Still nothing. She stepped out from her pose and put her hands on her hips, an annoyed look coming over her. “The MOON… watches down on us…ALL!” Miss Moonlight sighed, slumping her shoulders and watching the three ignore her still. Inside the arguing continues, curses and insults flying at all three of the alleged friends. In the background on the balcony, Miss Moonlight hopped up and down, making a commotion as she tried to get their attention…yet failing at because of the soundproof glass that blocked her. “---WATCHES DOWN ON US ALLLLLLLLLLLLL!!!!!!!” She hunched over to catch her breath from screaming. Then she sucked in a big breath of air, standing up straight. And then she simply knocked on the glass door. Eiko, Shun, and Ryo snapped their attention to the balcony. Fear froze on their faces as she stood there, her fists balled up at her sides and an irritated look in her eyes. “What does she want?” Eiko whispered, barely moving her red lips. Shun leaned over to her closely. “What do I look like, bitch…a psycho?” “That’s psychic, moron.” Ryo muttered lowly. “I got an idea.” Shun said. Then he smiled, waving at Miss Moonlight through the window. “WHO IS IT?” Levels of exasperation rose within Eiko and Ryo that humanity never thought existed. Shun looked at them and shrugged his shoulders. Ryo shook his head and looked over at the heroine. “What do ya want, huh?” Miss Moonlight motioned towards the knob on the door, an impatient look on her face. “Uh… it’s open.” Eiko answered. Miss Moonlight reached down and turned the knob on the door, sliding it open a crack. Her face lowered to embarrassment as she scratched the back of her head. “Oh…” she said sheepishly. “Thank you.” Then she hopped back and drove her foot through the window, sending shards of glass showering into the apartment. The trio shielded their eyes, looking back to see Miss Moonlight step through the shattered balcony window frame and flash the MOONLIGHT symbol, an M at her midsection with her hands. “The MOON…watches down on us ALL!” she grinned. “YEAH!” “Are you crazy?!” Eiko shrieked at her. “What do you think you’re doing here??” “I saw you three fiends outside, and thought I’d come in here and teach you a thing or two about being law abiding citizens!” she said confidently. Shun curled his finger at Miss Moonlight, raising his fist to strike. “Oh YEAH?? C’mere a minute!!” Ryo smacked him in the back of his head. “That NEVER works! Stop saying that!” “I think you three will be suitable training partners for my big match this Tuesday!” Miss Moonlight braced her self into a fighting stance and smiled. “Now… who wants to get rehabilitated?” ------=------ Ryo was draped over the edge of the balcony, groaning in pain. Eiko was slouched against a wall, her head lowered from being unconscious. And Shun… he was out in the hallway… lying on what was left of the front door. Miss Moonlight stood tall in that dark apartment, her hands on her hips laughing heartily. “Ahahahaha!! Three foes shown the LIGHT of the MOON! But… they weren’t that much of a test for my LUNAR abilities at all! I need better training if I’m going to become the Cruiserweight Champion this coming Turmoil! And I will become the champion to give the people a righteous protector of the Junior Heavyweight division as well as PCW and Tokyo! I’ll do so BY THE LIGHT OF THE MOON!!” Just then sirens began to wail off in the distance, growing close to the apartment building. She looked around nervously before inching away towards the front door. “But for now… Miss Moonlight will take her leave… lest the police catch me and take me downtown for some STELLAR questioning…” Miss Moonlight ran off down the hallway, disappearing into the stairwell… disappearing until… the moon shines down on us once more…
