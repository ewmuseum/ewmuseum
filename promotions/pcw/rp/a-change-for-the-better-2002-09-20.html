A Change For The Better?

20 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/20/02 The scene opens up to the sound of laughter. The person bellowing out doesn't seem like they have just heard a hilarious joke, but instead it sounds as though they are having a great deal of fun. As the camera comes into focus we see Kramer sitting on a light brown carpet in a comfortable looking outfit consisting of a baggy shirt, baggy pair of shorts, and socks. At first we can't really tell what he is having such a great time over. Suddenly a silver object, resembling a rocket, comes into view as Kramer pulls it through the air with his right hand making noises to symbolize a real rocket. Kramer: Vrooom!! He wiggles it around in the air before pretend crashing it onto the carpet. He pulls open a section of it to reveal Grimer who is expressionless in his little astronaut outfit. Kramer: It seems as though Grimer Armstrong has just landed on the moon.... Kramer takes Grimer slightly around the waist and pulls him upright. He then walks him along the carpet pulling him up every once in a while as though he were really on the moon fighting the nongravity. Kramer: This was one step for rodents, and one giant leap for rodent kind. Grimer pulls away from his grasp and runs into his cage straight for his food. Grimer starts gulping it all down not leaving a single crumb. Kramer: Good idea little buddy. I'm going to go get something to eat as well, and when I come back we can continue where we left off. Grimer stops eating for one second and turns towards Kramer. He then runs towards the opening to his cage and slams into the side causing the flap to come down shutting the cage off. Kramer: If you didn't want to play all you had to say was no. Kramer stands up laughing a bit at not only the genious of Grimer, but also his choice of expression. Kramer walks down his steps and walks through the living room towards the kitchen only to be stopped midway by the sound of the ringing doorbell. Kramer slides towards the door in his socks and opens it up. Seeing the person on the opposite side of the door causes his jaw to drop. Kramer: Nancy?! What...what are you doing here? Now I swear I sent the vet the bill for Scruffers several weeks ago. Nancy: It's funny, I am used to a simple hello but whatever you prefer. She giggles slightly causing Kramer to fidget at the door as he looks over her slim figure in her tight blue jeans and red sparkly shirt out of the corner of his eye. Kramer: I'm sorry. How about we start this all over? Kramer shuts the door part way and pulls it open. Kramer: Well hello. Nancy: Now that's better. She laughs a bit more and as she does the wind picks up a bit causing her to hold her arms shivering a bit. Kramer: Would you like to come in? Nancy: Sure. She steps in and stands in the hall as Kramer shuts the door. He then directs her towards the couch and offers her a cup of coffee which she politely refuses. Kramer: So what brings you here? Nancy: Well I am getting ready to move out of my apartment and I realized that I didn't have the muscles needed to move all of the big stuff, and I don't have the money to hire movers, and I didn't know anyone who could possibly help me. So I was wondering if it would be possible for you to help me out? I mean if you don't all you have to do is say so....and I would be willing to pay you. Kramer: You don't need to give me money, I'll happily do it for free. Nancy: That's great! Thank you so so much. She jumps out of the couch and wraps her arms around Kramer's shoulders and gives him a big kiss on his cheek leaving light red lipstick in it's place. Judging by the look on Kramer's face, he is highly shocked by that show of affection. Nancy: Just come over to my place on Sunday around noonish. Kramer: Certainly! Kramer walks Nancy to the door and shuts it softly behind her. He turns and leans back on the door and slowly slides down with his eyes wide open and a smile on his face. Kramer: That woman is so magical! He snaps out of it several minutes later and looks down at his watch. Kramer: I guess now would be a good time to get my crap together for Fusion. The scene slowly fades as he stands up, not thinking about his match against the returning Tim Walden and his cheating tactics, but the ever so beautiful Nancy......
