The Fear and Loathing Saga: Skeptic...

Date: 10/??/02 I can remember going through my cars glove compartment like I was a child trying to find the last bit of candy he dropped under the seat cushions. This was the salvation I needed. My lover may be insane but she definately hit this one right on the head. She put one thing in my contract that would let me completely stick it to whoever fired me... a sort of pension plan you can say. I always kept my important papers in the glove compartment. For anyone else that would work, but to apparently to me old McDonalds wrappers are important papers. Talena swayed back and forth in the passangers seat as I leaned over her, she had taken another line off of my dashboard while I was franticly searching for my revenge. Sure, I could have much more easily used my god-like powers to destroy the minds and bodies of those who opposed me. But hey, where is the fun in that? Thats kind of missing the forest for the paper factory, you know? Why cause simple pain when you can drain a company of its ever important life blood. Money. 'Fuck yes!' My hand ran across the indented Primetime Stationary that my contract was printed on, I pulled it out of the cramped glove compartment and let it breathe in the night air. It was quite a sight, one hand gripping this seemingly useless bundle of papers while the other held 240 dollars worth of blotter acid. 'Talena, hunny, I found it. What am I supposed to look at?' She grabbed the papers from me and leaned them on my head, I realized I still hadn't moved from the position of leaning across her, she flipped a few pages and read down the next one. 'Right here, your pension plan.' I sat up and nearly dragged my head on the Porsches roof. I tried to comprehend the jargon used, I guess this is why Talena was my little gothic attorney... 'Where the hell is the Party of the First.' I really didn't know, thats the sad thing. Talena gave me a very patronizing look and sniffed idly. 'As a result of your pension plan, you get a all expenses paid trip to Las Vegas, and in return you have to write an article about it for PCW Magazine.' I blinked. 'There is now PCW Magazine anymore.' She made a face, 'Well, yeah, but the money is still there. This part of your contract was never nullified.' In essence this plan was genius, there were just so many loopholes that I didn't know how to react to it. I mean, really. I love her. But couldn't she have negotiated for more money instead of a fucking trip to Vegas for a week. Thats the dumbest thing I have ever heard in my life. 'By expenses Gavyn, I mean everything... Legal or not.' 'Well thats all f-... wait. What?' The other shoe had dropped.
