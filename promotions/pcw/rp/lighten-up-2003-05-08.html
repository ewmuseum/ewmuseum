Lighten Up

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 8, 2003 E2 has been running on a high after teaming up with ‘Pranksters with Attitudes’ to learn the art of executing pranks. One aspect that the duo failed to recognize is the concept of karma. This theory states that ‘what goes around comes around’. Although John Gargano and Tyler Preston have been feeling invincible, it would only be a matter of time before some touches them and brings them back to reality. There are repercussions, much to their dismay. The scene fades in to the interior of John Gargano’s apartment room. Tyler and John hang out in the family room in front of John’s big screen television. They gaze at the latest news that is being broadcast as they lay on their stomachs, sprawled out on the light blue carpeting. It is obviously ‘chill time’ for the duo as the sport some scrubby sweat suits. Hints of perspiration can be seen on John’s hairline, which suggests that Tyler was putting him to work again, getting him in shape for the upcoming movie ‘To the Ring and Back’. Their peaceful stupor that they enjoyed as they watched television is broken as Mick comes charging through the cream colored door and shuts it with a slam. He drags his dark red brief case and waves a crumpled piece of paper in his hand. He looks very angry at something. Tyler and John look up at him with wide-eyed surprise. “Gentlemen, I am not very pleased about this little piece of paper that I hold in my hands. It just proves the point that I have to watch your every move and treat you like little children,” Mick says, beginning politely but moving towards hostility in his voice. He waves a tattered piece of paper that has obviously been subjected to folds and hand exchanges. “Hey, if that’s from the ‘Laugh Factory’ you can just tell them to kiss off. I didn’t show up last week because they are behind on their payments and I’m done doing charity work for them,” Tyler says in defense. He doesn’t want to be labeled as an unjust man, so he attempts to clear the water right away. Mick shakes his head, informing Tyler that this isn’t even the correct subject. “No, its not from the ‘Laugh Factory’, this is your own personal business. This little memo that I received concerns both of you. This is direct at E2,” Mick says. This catches the attention of both men. Even though they value their careers outside of the ring, they especially value the ones inside. They grow nervous knowing it concerns their PCW careers. They don’t want things to be ruined causing them to be a flash in the pan tag team. “What is it Mick? Have we been promoted?” John says in hopes. He pushes out the wonders of possibly being demoted or released from the PCW. He takes the optimistic approach as Tyler shakes his head, knowing that this can’t be anything good. “Are you kidding me? I think the PCW has had enough of you jokers to be promoting you guys. After all, you are lucky that you still have jobs there,” Mick explodes. He is appalled that John would even suggest such a thing. John leans back with a frown on his face. He is unsure of what he may have done to anger Mick so much. Whatever it was, it begins festering in the minds of Tyler and John. “Hold the bus, what are you talking about? We show up on time every single day and we already won our first match against those worthless ‘Pretty Cool Guys’. Hell, we even show up to the arena when we don’t have a match. What’s the problem?” Tyler asks, annoyed that Mick is keeping it from them. Tyler figured that he has done nothing but contribute in a positive way to the PCW thus far. “That is precisely the problem. Maybe you should stop going to the arena when you aren’t assigned a match. All that comes out of it is trouble,” Mick says sternly, like a father would say to his son. John nods his head in agreement, wanting not to make anymore waves. Tyler just chuckles at Mick attempts to order them around. “Maybe you should get your nose out of our business. I’m not sure if you are aware, but E2 flies solo. We don’t need some legal agent acting like our mother to succeed in the PCW,” Tyler states, putting Mick in his rightful place. Tyler and John are both in agreement that E2 has wrestling matters under control, and Mick is beginning to wear on them in a negative way. “Is that right pal? Well I’m making it my business. This concerns one of my clients. Any negative publicity that reflects of John is going to screw me,” Mick snaps back in an angry tone. He gets right up in Tyler’s face but Tyler doesn’t back down. Instead he lets out a huge grin. John gets more nervous, thinking that this may affect his acting career and he still doesn’t even know what it is. “I don’t really see what the problem is. We haven’t done anything that bad around the PCW. We usually just hang around with Gaz and Nerezza,” John says, trying to convince even himself that he has not committed any acts of violence or crime. John begins to shiver, thinking that all he has worked so hard for may be lost. “Oh great, that’s just the type of people I want you hanging around. John, how do you think you look when you are running around on national television with a pack of thugs?” Mick says, criticizing the Pranksters with Attitudes. Tyler and John both frown, knowing that PWA has been nothing but a great support for them ever since they entered the PCW. “They aren’t thugs, they are pretty good friends. They’re showing us the ropes while we get adjusted to the new PCW life. Its not as easy as it looks you know,” John states, coming to the verbal aid of PWA. This shows great courage as he finally stands up to his own agent who intimidates him for the most part. John and Tyler are both glad those words were put on the table. “He’s right, and I doubt simply hanging around with Gaz and Nez is going to ruin John’s acting career, as if he had a huge career to speak of already,” Tyler reinforces, putting John’s modest acting career into prospective in the situation. “It’s not the fact that you are hanging around those two misfits, the matter concerns the influence that they are having on you. They’re turning you in to Neanderthals,” Mick says, thinking back on all the things he has seen Tyler and John do on Turmoil and Fusion. Mick shakes his head at the mere thought of it. “You’re kidding me right? What have we done that is so bad?” Tyler says with complete innocence in his voice. He knows that they have pulled a prank or two, but were they really that bad? “Why don’t I just start reading this memo. It’s pretty packed to the brim of bonehead things you have done thus far with the PCW. This has to come to a stop if you ever want to succeed John,” Mick says as a warning and at the same time, insults the duo. Tyler losses his patients. He is sick of this man taking control of everything in his life, so he snaps back at him. “Read the damn thing Varelli, I’ve had just about enough of your lip,” Tyler explodes. He settles back in to his chair and listens to Mick as he reads select parts of the memo in a disgusted voice. He is faking it somewhat, trying to make things sound worse than they are. “Very well Tyler. You both are guilty of vandalizing, recreating murder, and food poisoning. That’s quite a list of credentials men. The PCW is not very happy with you at this point,” Mick reads. He knows that the legal terms sound a lot harsher then the actions they really did, but he figures it will have more of an affect on them. “If that’s how they are going to be, then f--- the PCW. They need to lighten up,” Tyler says. He feels great anger. All he ever did was pull a few pranks backstage just like the PWA has been doing, and now he gets yelled at. For a brief moment, he almost thinks that he would quit the PCW. He doesn’t see any point in staying if they are going to act like that. “Bite your tongue Tyler, this is the opportunity that we have been waiting for. Let’s not blow this apart just because of a small misunderstanding,” John says, putting Tyler back in to the right mindset. Tyler settles down, realizing that he is being incredibly drastic by making such a harsh decision in such little time. “You’re right, but that frustrates me. We try to make a difference in the PCW and we catch nothing but flack for it,” Tyler says, feeling as if the PCW has turned on him. Mick continues to shake his head and is on the receiving end of a harsh sneer from Tyler. “I can’t believe you managed to do all that within your first month with the company,” Mick says in amazement. He believes that is quite a list, and is uncertain of what they were thinking. He folds up the memo and puts it back in his pocket. “You know what Mick, that’s what we had to do to get where we are buddy. We almost killed Nerezza, we made fools out of ‘Pretty Cool Guys’, and we made the super heroes of ‘Justice Inc.” run to the bathrooms for mercy. I call that a good job, and if the PCW can’t see that, then I don’t know who will,” Tyler says for a small speech. Mick just laughs, thinking that Tyler sounds both like a little kid, and like a very confused professional wrestler. “Yea, you’re getting yourselves somewhere. You’re getting yourselves closer to jail. You guys have turned in to a bunch of thugs,” Mick says behind chuckled laughter. Tyler and John frown at this constant nagging from Mick. Tyler finally gets up to leave. “If all you’re going to do is mock the way that we do things then I’m out of here. We can do this with out you. John can even make a living as an actor without you,” Tyler points out, delivering a heavy blow to Mick’s confidence and ego. Mick just laughs, thinking that the statement was false. “Oh is that right? John, are you going to go with your little friend and play childish pranks on people? Think of what is best for your career Johnny,” Mick coaxes with a personal touch to it. John looks at Tyler, and then gazes over in Mick’s direction. He is torn. He wants to excel in both aspects of his career. “I can’t pick between you and Tyler. I want to do both, can’t we all just be friends?” John suggests, hoping that this rivalry will be solved just by the simple suggestion of John. John wishes that he never introduced Mick to Tyler. Ever since they met, there has been nothing but arguing between the two. “John, just ditch the guy. He’s a loser. The only thing that he is going to do is get in the way of our fun. He’s going to be acting like our parents,” Tyler convinces. Mick has already spoiled their fun. Tyler was having a great time executing the pranks, and now Mick was raining on his parade. “Yea Tyler, but he helps me get the roles in movies. Just give each other one more shot,” John says, assuming the role of the peacekeeper. Tyler just laughs at the mere suggestion of such a stupid idea. Mick rolls his eyes at John’s attempts to mend things back together. “While you’re being indecisive, I’m going to get ready for a big night at Fusion. Tune in Mick, I’m pretty sure that there will be a fresh batch of great pranks coming in,” Tyler says with a smile, trying to agitate Mick further. Tyler walks out the door and slams it behind him. John sighs as Mick begins to pack up his things also. The party has suddenly come to an end, and John is going to be the odd man out. “Come on Mick, let’s talk about this summer’s movies,” John says. He wants at least one friend that can give him some benefits. Mick just slams his brief case closed and jets out the door. John sprawls out on the couch and stares up at the ceiling, feeling as if he has no more friends in the world. He knows that Tyler will never stay mad at him, and things will be cured at Fusion, but the fact that he ticked him off for the moment doesn’t rest well with John. John lays motionless on the couch and closes his eyes. He envisions the times where he was striving to get where he is now. He recalls how unhappy that he felt, but at the current moment, he has to ask himself ‘is he any happier’? Big decisions have to be made, and they need to be made soon. The scene fades out as John falls in to a deep sleep.
