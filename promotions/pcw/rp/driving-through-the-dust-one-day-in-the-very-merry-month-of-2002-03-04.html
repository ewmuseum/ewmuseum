Driving Through The Dust One Day... In The Very Merry Month of March...

4 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4th March 2002 We return to the extremely dusty road in Texas. Unfortunately the situation hasn’t improved one bit and it looks like a man with extremely bad dandruff is standing in front of the camera. All of a sudden a BMW Z3 in British Racing Green Metallic zooms past. Guess Who? We appear inside the BMW that was previously mentioned, although had I just mentioned it again it probably would have just been a whole hell of a lot shorter. Anyway, we appear inside and sitting there driving (I win the $1 prize for stating the obvious) is none other than Alexander Miyagawa, formerly known as Isawa Miyagawa. Sitting in the passenger seat trying to open a can of peanuts is Steve Jabowski. Steve: Dammit, I can’t get this open and I really want a peanut! He’s pulling at the ring-pull on the can and it’s not budging. Miya diverts his attention from the road to the can and back again, watching through the holes in the dust that the windscreen wipers are creating. Miyagawa: Don’t open that in here whatever you do. They’ll go everywhere. Besides I keep them in there for a special occasion. Steve: This is a special occasion, I want a peanut. Miyagawa: You don’t always get what you want, so put them back in the glove box right this instant. Steve: But they’re out of date in a week. Miyagawa: Good. Next week I’ll track down Boda and give him a peanut. He’ll enjoy it until he realises that they’re out of date and then he’ll do something that’s he’s good at... he’ll choke. I’ve just got one major problem with this whole Boda thing, you know? Steve: I know I just want a peanut, but go on. Miyagawa: I’m a face, right? People cheer me and I say nice things about people. For instance, Jeremy Howard has rather good sunglasses. Kid Wonder’s sister Amanda is quite pretty. See? Nice things. But Boda is a face, right? Yet... he’s... well he’s a frickin’ gimboid. How can a muscled up gimboid like that be a face? Steve: By eating peanuts before their best before dates? Miyagawa carries on as if Steve had said something there that wasn’t based solely upon pure sarcasm. Miyagawa: And what’s more... Akiyama had to check if he was a good guy or a bad guy? He’s the guy; shouldn’t he know what he’s doing? He wanders around in his void armour crap like he’s Robbie the frickin’ Robot and asks people if they think if he’s nice or nasty. Steve: He did say please. Don’t suppose you’ve got a can opener in here, do you? Miyagawa: I’m not sure what’s worse... Boda winning the title or me taking it off of him after three days. Sure Boda would be a former champion, but not exactly what I think he had in mind. Maybe I am a heel. **Pauses for a second** Dammit, no... I am a face, just I’m a face facing a jackass. That is what this is. Face versus Jackass. See, it’s simple. Steve: Maybe like a blade of something... a penknife would do. I told you to steal the ones from Kid Wonder’s last birthday party that he had in a plastic packet but Noooo... that’s too simple, so you went and you stole the ones in the can. And now I can’t get the damned thing open. Miyagawa: Now Mexi... Mexi I just don’t get. A few months back Tyler Nelson did this whole thing with me too, so what did I do? Steve: You drove over his limo three times and pissed in his coffee, which was then stolen on television by another company. But the important this is this... can we stop so I can get a rock to crack this damned thing open? Miyagawa once again carries on regardless of Steve’s second sentence there, ignoring his plight with the peanuts. Miyagawa: Exactly. So what did Mexi do when I pointed that out to him? He interferes and becomes the person who caused the Boda World Title Win. Mexi has guaranteed his place in history and every former champion to grieve for the poor title. This is the start of bigger things to come, you just wait... Hardcore Anthony Cay will be back now and then the crap will really hit the fan. Steve: Shit. You mean the shit will hit the fan. And it will if I... wait, I know... Steve tries to bite the can open, before recoiling in pain. Steve: Damned thing! You sure you haven’t got anything like a pair of scissors or something. Hell, even something large and blunt will do now. Miyagawa: Life is full of problems. It would appear that I am one of Mexi’s and Boda’s. At least I can rely on Duke Williams to back me up because as far as I am concerned there is no difference between Tyler Nelson and the White Mexican. One is a trumped up loser who sister screwing jackasses and likes the taste of power... and the other one is Tyler Nelson. And that’s the final thing I will say on the subject. Steve: I’ll just wait another ten minutes for you to start ranting again, shall I? Just at that point the ring-pull finally releases the peanuts... all over the inside of the car in a shower of nutty rain. Steve reaches forward and takes one off the dashboard and eats it. Steve: I only wanted one. Miyagawa and Steve collectively look around the car and the peanuts are everywhere. Steve just grins at Miya sheepishly. Miyagawa: And another thing. Why are the tech guys showing my car with all this crap. Don’t they know this is meant to be Texas, not Dune? Steve: You already said that. Miyagawa: Yeah but this time I’m sure I can see Sting and a bunch of worms over there in the distance. Just what the heck are they playing at? The camera pans upwards through the roof of the BMW using some flashy special effect probably first used in the Matrix and upwards off the sound set up to an office on the next floor up. In that office is stacks upon stacks of rows of filing cabinets. There is also a trapdoor in this room; open. And next to this open trapdoor is a filing cabinet also opened marked “Emergency Situations”. Standing over this is a rather young intern fresh out of college who probably doesn’t know exactly that he’s throwing dust down into the sound stage before as he rummages through the section marked “Champions”. He stops at a folder marked “Miyagawa” and pulls it out. He opens the folder and there is a small note in there marked “Buy Cake and throw balloons about. Then let Chet fire him if Chet is still President.” That is all written in Tyler Nelson’s own script. The intern tips the folder out unleashing more dust... and a small green party balloon through the trapdoor. The Intern shrugs and throws the folder behind him where it joins other notes and folders... one marked Hank has a note inside reading “Panic” while another marked Hix has a note marked “Shoot with attached pistol.” And it too has a pistol attached. The young boy finally seems to get what he was sent to find... a folder marked “Boda”. He opens the folder and more dust than any of the others falls out and a small box with a big red button on it; plus of course a note which reads “Push Button”. The kid pushes the button... and nothing happens. He shakes the box and it sounds empty. Kid: Damned electricians. The guys we hire are cowboys. The camera cuts to a small room cluttered with electrical crap and a man is standing in the middle of it looking rather panicked in a white shirt and blue overalls and a cowboy hat. Man: If I don’t find that button and wire it to this radio to call in the Ass-Ass-Ins to take out Boda I will be fired. This is not a good thing, this is a bad thing.
