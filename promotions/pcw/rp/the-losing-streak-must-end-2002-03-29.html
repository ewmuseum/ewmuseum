The losing streak must end.

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/29/02 “This is pathetic. Five in a row, I’m a joke” Chet King says in frustration to no one in particular as he walks out of the Pan American Center following Tuesday Turmoil. “You’re right, it is pathetic Chet” states Cindy Bi who is walking next to him. “Thanks for the support, Cindy” “Hey, you’re the one who said it was pathetic to start with. This has got to be the worst losing streak you’ve ever had.” “It probably is. I don’t think I’ve ever lost five matches in a row before” a depressed Chet King sighs. “What’s worse is the fact that you’ve lost some of these matches to guys like The End tonight.” “I know, I know. It’s shocking. I never thought that I’d job to The End.” The warm Mexican air blows over Chet King’s and Cindy Bi’s faces as they walk down the street outside of the arena, heading towards Chet King’s black sports car located a block away from the arena. “The thing is Chet, that you haven’t won a match since a month ago, since you lost the Continental Title to Jeremy Howard.” Says Cindy Bi. “I know that’s the thing! There’s no need to rub it in” replies Chet King. “And one month ago was when you acted strangely one day for no reason, complaining about life and how much you hated it.” Cindy Bi remembers. “Huh?” Chet King acts confused, giving Cindy Bi a weird look “What are you talking about?” “Don’t act surprise, you remember as well as I do about that day when for no reason you talked about how you hated life.” “I’ve never said I hate life, I love it.” “Don’t play stupid” yells Cindy Bi “You remember that day.” Cindy Bi stares in dead straight in to Chet King’s eyes as she reminisces about what happened. “It may of only been for a period of five to six hours, but those five to six hours were weird. You told me about how you hated life and I tried to find out why, but you never told me. And this happened just a day before you started your losing streak, and I have a feeling that this may be why your losing streak began.” Cindy Bi explains. Chet King looks at the ground and doesn’t say anything. “So you remember the day?” questions Cindy. Chet King continues to look at the ground and doesn’t say anything. “Do you remember or not?” Cindy yells at Chet King. Chet King slowly looks up at Cindy and stares in her eyes. “Yes, I remember” he says. “I knew you did. Tell me what that was all about.” “Don’t worry.” “Tell me!” “Don’t worry about it, let’s just concentrate on how I can end this losing streak.” Cindy Bi has a very frustrated look on her face as the two walk down the road. “To end your losing streak I need to know why you hated life that one day, because I feel that is why you’ve been having bad performances in the ring.” Cindy Bi tells Chet. A look of anger shoots across Chet’s face. “Forget about it. It’s got nothing to do with anything.” Shouts Chet. Cindy Bi looks surprised at Chet. “What is wrong with you Chet?” asks Cindy. Chet King appears to take offense to the question “Nothing is wrong with me! Just drop the whole thing.” “You want me to drop it? Fine I’ll drop it. I just hope you can deal with what ever your problem is by yourself.” “I don’t have a problem.” “Alright, whatever.” “Okay, now do you have any ideas on how I can break this losing streak without referring to what we were just talking about.” Chet King wonders. “Not really, although I’ve heard what some of the other wrestlers have been saying about your poor performances.” “How does that help me? I don’t care what others think, I just want to know how to break this losing streak.” “So you don’t want to know what others think of you?” “Not particularly.” “You sure you don’t want to know what others think of you?” Cindy Bi asks again. “Yeah okay, I want to know, tell me.” “Corey Williams said that although you appear to be moving up the card…” before Cindy Bi can finish Chet King interrupts her. “What does he mean moving up the card?” asks Chet. “How can all this jobbing mean that I’m moving up the card?” “Technically you are moving up the card since you’re ranked the number five contender to the world title” “But I was number for a couple of weeks ago, which means I’ve moved down.” “True. Anyway what Corey said was that although you appear to be moving up the card, he thinks, to quote him, your stock has dropped tremendously as of late.” “As much as I’d like to disagree with him, I can’t.” “He said that he thought you were at your best when you were feuding with Mexi, and when you had the Continental Title.” “Obviously I was at my best then, I had the Continental Title! Some one who’s not at their best can’t hold on to the Continental Title!” “Heh, yeah well he said that you’ve failed to catch his eye while the Asylum and Wild Deuces splits happened, and amongst the financial crises of the company.” “Luckily I didn’t catch his eye or he would of seen me jobbing! Too bad this is January 2001 when I was one of the men fixing the company’s financial crises.” “If it was January 2001 I wouldn’t be with you.” “And why would that be bad?” Chet King jokes. “That comment is going to cause me to go straight in to new comer Rudolph von Hackenmen’s comment about you.” “I presume that means it’s a harsh comment.” “Indeed it is, He said just five words, and those five words were ‘Chet King is falling fast’” Chet King sighs as the two get nearer to Chet King’s car. “I can’t say I’m falling that fast at the moment if I’m ranked number five for the world title, but I’ve got to stop this losing streak so that I don’t fall any further.” Chet King wishes. “If you really don’t want to fall, and you want this losing streak to end, then this Sunday you better be serious going in to your match against Clyde.” “Don’t worry, I’m going to be as serious as I’ve ever been going in to a match.” “If you lose on Sunday you will truly be falling, and climbing back up will be a very hard task.” “I realize this.” “As far as I’m concerned, this is a must win match for you on Sabotage.” Cindy states. “As far as I’m concerned, this is a must win match for me.” Chet also states. “Then on Sunday, no matter what problems you are having which you won’t tell me about, you’ve got to put everything else aside and go in to this match knowing that losing is not an option.” “That will be exactly what I’m going to do, I just hope that Darkstar doesn’t show up to help his sex master, Clyde. That little bitch better not cause me to continue my losing streak.” “I’ll do everything in my power to make sure he doesn’t.” “Thanks Cindy, but even though Darkstar’s a little bitch, I don’t think your power will be enough against him. So if he, and or Badge shows up it’s going to turn in to a handicap match and I’m going to have to take on all of them.” “Will you be able to?” “With the knowledge that I must end my losing streak, I should be able to as I will not let myself lose this match.” Says Chet King “And if Darkstar does show up, it might not be too bad as I’ll get to beat his bitch ass down before our match at Spring Fling.” Chet King and Cindy Bi reach Chet King’s black sports car. As the moon shines from the sky on the long stretch of road ahead of them, Chet and Cindy hop in to the car. “Remember Chet, Sabotage is a must win situation.” Cindy Bi tells Chet. “I know, Cindy. I know.”
