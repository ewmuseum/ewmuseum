The Bachelor III: The Concept

22 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/22/03 The Bachelor III: The Concept The scene opens in a large dark room with a big black leather couch and 54 inch wide screen Sony Digital television. The walls were painted deep red with gold frames of hundreds of different famous television stars. I was in a place of enchantment and prestige, one with a colorful and provocative feeling to it. I was in Hollywood. Of course the United States gave me quite a sour taste in my mouth. It reminded me of stubborn and snobby Anglo Saxons, juvenile delinquents, a sick blend of disgusting cultures, and old perverts with too much time on their hands. Surprisingly, this marvel of a state by the name of California was different. The weather was consistent, never too hot, and never too cold. It reminded me of the soft and cool Mediterranean weather. The people were laid back and active. They weren’t the lazy obese bums, which I pictured most Americans as being. I remember Juan telling me one thing about America that stuck in my head. “The people are fat, the food is greasy, and always double up on condoms.” Even though it seems as if he may have been a bit off the mark, well accept for the possible use of double condoms, I still was a little defensive. After this morning though, I felt completely relaxed, for this morning I met a man by the name of Gerald Harrison. Mr. Harrison was quite the impressive one and made me quite the offer. So here I am. After getting an offer of five hundred dollars just to step inside the doors of this renaissance style home, I decided to take a chance and see what I was being offered. So I wait in this cool bright pit of good feeling wondering what lies ahead for me. That’s when the same man I had seen earlier enters through the front door. I stood on my two solid feet and walked patiently toward Mr. Harrison shaking his hand thoroughly. A good grip by this wise looking man showed me that he meant business. What I was about to see was definitely business. El Romantico, Romantico, El R, what ever you prefer to be called I don’t know. You see what I’m offering you here is not only an instant pop of worldwide fame, but also something I believe you’ve been quite fond of finding, love. Excuse me. Don’t get me wrong. I see you are highly capable of getting any woman you so desire. I’m sure you’ve even had woman grabble at your feet just for you to sigh their name. Now that’s quite a success, but imagine that success being viewed by the whole world. I don’t quite understand what you’re getting at. Well I’m not a man of long speeches and different propositions, so I’ll sum this all up in a short little tape. So may I draw your attention to the rather wide screen in front of you? You may like what you see. Romantico makes himself comfortable as the video slowly begins. The lights suddenly dim and then the room grew silent. 30 Women 1 Man No rules and no regulations. One woman will win the heart, while the other twenty-nine will slip away in sorrow. Are you man enough to step up to the challenge? Do you think you have what it takes to please the likes of thirty of the Unites States’ most beautiful singles? The applications will need to be impressive, the record needs to be clean, and the look needs to be immaculate. And if you manage to be the chosen one, you will be able to live seven luxurious weeks within the beautiful Caribbean confines of Bermuda. The tape finally fades to black. Let me get this right. You want me to go on a reality sitcom where women will be fighting to go out with me. Well you would need to apply first of course. You see the other week I was offered a deal. I was one of fifty Fox representatives to try and go out and find the next bachelor. The first two editions of the show have led to stunning successes. They went on to have a Bacholorette and recently Joe Millionaire has aired and made quite the ruckus. In this of what seems to be the era of Reality Television, I thought you would be an exceptional candidate. And why would I be such a great candidate. Well not only are you already popular around Europe and in parts of this country for being the PCW Cruiserweight Champion and Underground God in wrestler, but your also one of the most desired men in the world. The Bachelor is looking for a more International star, a European novelty. This is something beyond the all American schoolboy. When I saw you walking down the streets of Hollywood earlier today, a light went off in my head and I knew you were what America is looking for. I agree with everything you are saying, but I’m still not very sure. Well here’s the application and here’s my number. Get back to me if you decide that this is the future you’re ready to embark on. Mr. Harrison handed me a couple papers before escorting me politely to the door. My mind was wandering as to what decision to make. I wasn’t sure if this was the path I may have wanted to take, but I was sure ready for what ever may have lied ahead. Something “New” begins here.
