A New Friend...and the Return, of an Old Flame - Part I

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 11, 2002 Christina Livingston. The gorgeous supermodel that the PCW fans adore, and it infuriates Jack every time he hears them cheer for her. The woman that has been in Jack’s life ever since he signed a contract with PWA last year in May. The woman that led Jack to PCW, and the woman that has helped Jack get to where he is today. But ever since word broke that Jack would be returning to PCW after recovering from the near life-threatening injury, Christina has yet to make an appearance. Could there possibly be problems between Jack and Christina? Last time we saw Jack at the hair salon, it seemed as if there was a little tension whenever the hairdresser mentioned the name “Christina.” Take that for what you will, but the only thing we truly know right now is that Christina hasn’t been around in PCW for a long, long time. The fans of PCW are anxious to see her stunning beauty back in a PCW ring sometime soon, but if she’s not coming back with Jack, will she ever come back? With that said, the camera fades into Jack Valentine, sitting at his ever famous kitchen table inside of his now prominent mansion. Wearing his pink robe, he sits at the table just looking at the ceiling, thinking…wondering…pondering. What he is thinking, who knows? Maybe he is thinking about his loss on Fusion to Andy Murray, or his newfound hatred towards Mike Frampton, or his upcoming match at alongside of his new partner Johnny Heart, or maybe…just maybe, he is thinking about the old flame. Christina Livingston. Jack Valentine is a very complicated man. Sometimes you can love the guy more than anything in the world, and other times you can’t even stand the sound of his voice. Jack goes through these times, where you just can’t help but feel bad for the guy…and this is one of those times. Jack gets up and paces around the room for several minutes before a knock is heard at the door. Jack somberly walks towards the door, and peeks through the window very slowly. He looks out and smiles. A taller, amazingly big man is standing their in black sweats. He is sweating and it looks as if he has just gone for a long run. Jack props open the door and invites the man in. “Come on in Johnny,” Jack says. Jack’s tag team partner, Johnny Heart, enters the room and is introduced on PCW TV for the first time. Johnny Heart was an instructor in PWA for about 3 months last fall and hasn’t been seen since. Apparently, in Johnny Heart’s absence as an instructor in PWA, he was helping to retrain and restructure Jack’s career in PCW during his injury. The two have been close friends for a long time now, yet not many have known about this friendship publicly. For those who don’t know, Jack Valentine & Johnny Heart have been causing ruckus in the tag team division, ever since Johnny Heart arrived here in PCW. Johnny and Jack have been interfering in different tag team matches and have come to grow a newfound hatred for the so-called Sensations of Annihilation. The fans have fallen in love with the energetic pair, and they haven’t even had their first wrestling match as of yet. This Sunday on Sabotage, Jack and Johnny, The Heartbreakers, will go head to head with their new foes in the Sensations of Annihilation. Christina Livingston is supposed to be by their side, but will she even be there? Jack hasn’t even talked to her in weeks…and it looks like trouble. Aside from all of the new fuss in Jack’s life, he continues his conversation with Johnny Heart in the hallway, and invites him into the kitchen. “Where the hell did you go to get this sweaty?” Jack questioned as he and Johnny made their way to the kitchen table. “I just ran for miles upon miles man, all over the city. Maybe if you worked out a little more, you could be as big as me…” Johnny sarcastically stated. “Maybe if you were as tone as me…you could have a girlfriend,” Jack responded. “Yeah, that looks like it’s going real well for you right now kid,” Johnny humorously stated to Jack. Jack stares down Johnny with an evil eye and the two continue the small talk at the table for a few minutes. Johnny Heart looks up at the ceiling and sees a huge mirror, covering the entire ceiling. “When did you get that?” Johnny asked. “Just a couple days ago…” Jack retorted. The two begin looking at themselves in the ceiling and then stand up. They each take their shirts off and walk back and forth in the kitchen, staring at themselves in the mirror. They continue this for what seems like an eternity. Then, they decide to start posing competitively against each other. The entertaining pose-off provides a few laughs for the viewers as they both poke fun at each other during the pose-off. Finally, after the laughter recedes, Jack and Johnny sit back down at the table. “So what’s the deal…are you going to be ready for our match Sunday Johnny…you haven’t wrestled in PCW ever and this a big deal for you,” Jack inquired. “Will I be ready? You’re the one who hasn’t won since you came off of your injury. Don’t you remember anything I taught you Jack??” Johnny solicited back to Jack. “Did you get to the Elite 8 of the PCW title tournament?? I didn’t think so…besides, we’re wrestling Mike Frampton and Big Daveylicious, how hard is it gonna be…especially when I look as good as I do,” Jack laughed as he mocked the comment that Johnny made. “Well, will you be ready Friday night for you Fatal-4 Way match…I won’t be there to carry your ass that night,” Johnny snickered as he took a sip of water from the bottle he had in his back pocket. “Do you realize who I’m facing? Andy Murray, Violence Jack and Kramer Wild Wing. These are three of the ugliest men on the face of the planet…don’t they know that I’m Jack Valentine…I made it to the Elite 8 of the PCW title tournament…” he said. “Enough with the PCW title tournament…that was your 15 minutes of fame…now my beautiful ass is going to carry you to the top and bring you some PCW gold for a change in your life. Something you could never do on your own,” Johnny chuckled. “Why do you ha-” As Jack was trying to utter out a rebuttal to Johnny’s harsh comments, the sound of a door creaking is heard in the background from the front door.
