The Prodigy Animal Kidnappings (part 3, final)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/29-30/02 The scene reopens and Kramer has his foot practically glued to the gas pedal not stopping for anything. As he zooms passed all of the cars a small formation of sweat begins to form on his brow. The look in his eyes speak of only pure anger. Jason on the other hand is still not really believing all of this is happening. The look in his eyes show worry that the cops might pull them over which could make or break the situation with Grimer. Jason: Kramer don't you think you should slow down a bit? I mean I realize this is a crisis but honestly, do you want to get pulled over by the cops? Kramer: I don't give a damn!! Jason shakes his head in disapproval as he tries to stay calm. Amazingly enough, the whole way back, Kramer doesn't get pulled over by any cop cars. That could be because they are too busy sitting on their fat behinds in their squad cars munching on jelly donuts. Kramer sees the big bucket of chicken on the roof, and the rotating KFC letters and makes a sharp turn pulling into the KFC headquarters parking lot. He quickly turns off the car and rushes into the building pushing pass anyone in sight. He finally spots Bobby Smart who is busy writing down some notes and races over to his side. Kramer: Mr. Smart. Smart: Wait a second please. Kramer: Mr. Smart! Kramer's voice gets a bit edgier and louder which forces Smart to look up. He sees the heat in Kramer's eyes and is almost taken back at it thinking it's because of him. Smart: What's wrong? Kramer: Have you seen Grimer? Smart: Well....I......hmmmm..... As you may have remembered from earlier, Mr. Smart sometimes forgets things. This just causes Kramer to fidget awaiting an answer. Smart: Oh right. I saw him. The last I saw, you had handed him to Tom, one of our new guys. Personally I didn't know that they were on the staff, but I guess you always need pet groomers on hand. Kramer: You said they. There were two men? Smart: Yes, brothers. Quite the opposite those two are. One's tall and skinny, while the other is short and fat. Kramer: Do you know where I can find them? Smart: First tell me why there is such a need to ask so many questions. Kramer: I believe Grimer was kidnapped and those two men are the prodigy animal kidnappers. Smart takes a moment to think and remembers hearing that somewhere. A big light bulb then forms above his head as he remembers hearing about poor Trident and Rocky. He then gives Kramer a sincere look. Smart: I don't know where they were going, but there is a camera in the back and front lot, so when they left the camera might have caught their license plate number. Kramer: Great! Smart motions for Kramer to follow him as they both take on a quick pace to get to the tapes. Smart leads Kramer to a room marked Restricted Area. They enter and we see screen shots from all areas of the building, and shots of the outside perimeter. Smart presses a rewind button and a screen in the middle starts to rewind the footage. Smart stops as he sees Tom and Marvin first stepping outside. He slows down the footage and Kramer manages to make out the license number. And as soon as he has it in his mind, Kramer races back outside leaving Smart in a cloud of dust. Kramer gets into his truck and revs it off. Just as he had entered, he speeds off into traffic. Jason: What did you find out? Kramer: Jason I need you to call the police right now and tell them that we might have the prodigy animal kidnappers. Jason obeys and whips out his cell phone and dials 911. As he is talking to the operator, Kramer gets stuck behind a white van that nearly cuts him off. Kramer shouts some profanities as he honks his horn. The van in front speeds up and when Kramer sees the license plate his eyes light up. Kramer: It's them! Jason: Who? Jason looks ahead as he puts the cell phone down. Kramer: Those bastards have Grimer! Kramer speeds up the car and Jason looks at him with a look of udder fear. From Jason's view, Kramer looks like he has gone completely mad. Kramer gets closer and closer to the back of the van and taps it. The scene switches to the inside of the van where Tom and Marvin look terrified. Marvin: What's wrong with that guy? Tom: I don't know, but I don't like road rage too much. Marvin: Try to change lanes or something. Tom takes the car to the right but is surprised to see the black truck behind him change lanes as well. Yet again the truck taps the back of the van but this time with more force. Marvin: Tom please pull off of the road and let this loony tune past. Tom takes the van off to the side of the highway in a bunch of grass. He is suprised to see the truck follow him. Tom: What in the world is this guys deal! Tom gets out of the car and starts walking towards the truck. Jason looks a little petrified not sure whether or not they have a weapon of some sort. Kramer on the other hand has a small grin on his face as they are doing just what he wants them to do. He makes sure to conceal his face a bit with his hand so that they don't recognize him. Tom comes up to the window and puts his chubby elbow on the door. Tom: I need your insurance info buddy, because you are for sure going to pay for the damages to my van. Kramer takes his hand away from his face, and Tom looks on in shock unable to speak or move. Kramer reaches up and grabs Tom's head and pulls it into the car where he proceeds to slam it against the steering wheel. Marvin sees that his brother is being attacked so he runs to his aid. Kramer pushes Tom out of the window and as Marvin comes around Kramer opens up the door and hits Marvin with it which causes him to fall to the ground in pain. Kramer then gets out of the car and starts kicking both men in the sides sending them the message that they picked the wrong guy to screw with. Kramer stops a moment and ducks his head back in the car as Jason looks on in awe not sure what to do or think. Kramer: Call the cops back and tell them are location. Jason: Kramer look out! Kramer turns around and is pummeled by a fury of fat fists courtesy of Tom. Tom then lifts up his right knee and shoves it into Kramer's midsection. Marvin comes up behind Kramer and grabs his arms preventing him from fighting back as Tom hits him hard in the face. Tom brings his fist back again and as he brings it forward, the scene goes to slow motion. We see him hit Kramer and Kramer's head jerks to the side violently as he spits out a mouthful of blood. Tom: That's for messing up our van, and this is for banging my head on the damn steering wheel! Tom grabs Kramer's head and slams it against the door. From that point the fight is over as a group of police cars swarm the area. Police officers step out and draw their guns at Tom and Marvin yelling at them to put their hands up. They comply and the cops move in and cuff them. As a police officer walks over to get a statement from Kramer, Kramer rushes away and opens up the back of the van. Inside he is delighted to see the cage containing Grimer as well as Rocky and Trident. The scene slowly fades to black as Kramer pulls Grimer out of his cage and gives him a big hug. In just a matter of days Kramer had experienced a lot of drama and action. And one would speculate all of this would keep his attention away from Gold and Glory. But always in the back of his mind, Kramer has been thinking about it. He has been thinking about the fact that a rare few think he can pull out with the win. He is thinking about how exactly he can get himself the win. And most of all he is thinking about each individual opponent. BloodAngel, to him, is no competition. I mean where has this guy been anyway? Then there is Duzza who like just about anyone has his highs and lows. But yet again Kramer doesn't see that spark of competition with him. Next is TMM with whom Kramer has had a lot of confrontations. But still Kramer doesn't see as big a competition, although he will be watching out for him. Last are the two favorites to win, Lovecraft and Violence Jack. Violence Jack as some may say has the name and fire that a true Extrime Title champion needs. He does have the talent, but can he pull out with the win? Finally there is the Lovecraft. A man who doesn't have the regular image that we would expect to see in an Extreme Champion. When we think extreme we think of young and vibrant. Even though Lovecraft is far from either, he has managed to keep a stronghold on the title which should be applauded. And even with all of the bashing he has done to Kramer, Kramer still has some respect for the man. Heck Kramer has respect for just about everyone as long as they prove they are deserving. But everything has to come to an end sooner or later, so after twenty three long days, it's time for Lovecraft to give up the title to a new champion. And that champion is none other than Kramer Wild Wing! After the PPV Kramer embarks on the chance at being a double title holder, as he takes on either Crichton or Shabazz for the TV title. Though Kramer doesn't have a preference on whom he faces, he feels that that match is his. Can it be done? Can Kramer, a bare mid-carder (if that), do something that so few people have done in the past? Hey it's PCW, anything is possible!
