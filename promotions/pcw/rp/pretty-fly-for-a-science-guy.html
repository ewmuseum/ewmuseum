Date: not entered [Scene opens in the waiting room of a Doctor’s office.Flaymin sits calmly alongside a few other waiting patients, just casually flicking through a 6-year-old magazine.He then reaches over and taps the shoulder of the elderly woman sitting beside him] Flaymin: So who do you think will win the Atlanta Olympics this year? [The woman stares back at Flaymin before shaking her head and continuing to read through her own magazine] Flaymin: Not much of the talking type, huh? [The woman raises the palm of her hand and places it on the side of her face to block out any vision of Flaymin. In response, Flaymin shrugs his shoulders and continues to read about ‘The Upcoming 1996 Atlanta Games’.But this thrilling piece of literary art is interrupted by a brief ringing sound and a flashing red light above the Secretary’s desk] Secretary: Aymin, Flaymin?Or is that Flaymin, Aymin? Well whatever the heck your name is- the doctor is ready to see you. [Flaymin hops up off his chair and walks into the doctor’s office. Inside the office, the walls are painted bright pink, and the coffee table resembles a gigantic ass- with the ‘asshole’ in the middle of the table filled with pretty flowers. A man, dressed in a tight shirt, velvet pants and a tartan scarf appears from behind Flaymin and claps his hands together] Herma’an: Darling!!Long time no see! [Flaymin turns around and smiles at the queer looking man] Flaymin: Hey Herma’an, its been a while huh? [Herma’an reaches over and starts running his fingers through Flaymin’s afro] Herma’an: Oh darling, I just love what you’re doing with your hair! Flaymin: You mean not cutting it for 8 months? Herma’an: Whatever it is, I just love how bushy and curly it is!It reminds me of pubi… Flaymin: Woah!Spare me the details, buddy. Herma’an: Hmmmph!So what brings you here today? [Herma’an walks over to his desk and sits on his chair. Flaymin sits in the patients chair and puts his feet up on the table] Herma’an: Shooo!! Shooo!!This pinewood table cost me a fortune, darling! [Herma’an shoves Flaymin’s feet off the table] Flaymin: Right, I forgot about your thing for wood. Herma’an: *Sigh* You never were much of an Interior Decorating Expert.Wood is the most beautiful material on earth!Its…. Its…. Flaymin: Brown? Herma’an: Uh yeah… that too.So enough chitty-chatty, what brings you here today? [Flaymin reaches into his pocket and pulls out a photo of Jamie Celt and hands it to Herma’an] Herma’an: What is this? Flaymin: Its my wrestling opponent for this week. [Herma’an looks at the photo, gasps in horror and places his hand over his mouth] Herma’an: He’s… he’s…. Flaymin: Hideous? Herma’an: I was going to say retarded but I think ‘hideous’ is a good enough description. Flaymin: You see, I feel sorry for the poor guy.He looks just like my High school Science Teacher and he has about as much style as a Drag Queen at a funeral. [Herma’an continues to shake his head] Herma’an: I feel sorry for him already!Darling, he is what we in the homosexual community call a ‘Major Faux Pas’.His dress sense is beyond horrific and that hairstyle is oh so Woody Allen!Tell me, is he a nice guy?I mean, maybe a personality of some sort can be salvaged from this human disaster? [Flaymin sadly shakes his head] Flaymin: Nope, he’s as boring as bat shit.I really couldn’t get past his dull exterior. [Herma’an raises an eyebrow] Herma’an: Tell me, is his posterior any good? Flaymin: Well I didn’t get the chance to take a good look at it but I’m pretty sure its in more disastrous condition than his dress sense and general appearance. Herma’an: Uh huh.And what were you supposing bringing his photo to me would accomplish? Flaymin: Well Herma’an, I know that you’re the most styling-est person in all the lands and I ask for a very big favour- Transform this prissy little bitch into something appealing! [Herma’an looks at the photo of Jamie Celt once again.He stares at it for a good 30 seconds before sadly shaking his head] Herma’an: I’m not sure about this.I’ve never had to transform something so repugnant into a beautiful rose in all my time. [Flaymin places his hand on Herma’an’s shoulder] Flaymin: Just give it a shot, please? Herma’an: Hmmm, ok, ok.I’ll do what I can but I don’t know if this will work. Flaymin: I have faith in you. [Herma’an walks over to a nearby drawer and pulls out a makeup kit.He takes the kit over to the table and opens it up, revealing dozens upon dozens of different colours of mascara] Herma’an: Here, which colour do you think is best? Flaymin: I envision the new Jamie Celt tearing away the shackles of his nerdy Science Teacher look and…. Wearing purple mascara. Herma’an: Oooh, I like it! [Herma’an pulls out the purple mascara and begins dabbing it on the photo of Jamie Celt] Herma’an: What about lipstick?Do you think he needs some good lipstick? Flaymin: AbsolutelyYou can’t possibly call yourself a man if you don’t have that bright oozing colour of lipstick shining bright.I suggest yellow!! [Herma’an strokes his chin] Herma’an: Yellow is nice, but I was thinking more along the lines of Gold.Gold lipstick and purple mascara go together like Vegetable Soup mixed with Coke. Flaymin: I like it. Herma’an: Next on the list is that awful hairstyle.Do you have any suggestions that would fit in with the styling we’ve already given him? Flaymin: I’m a huge fan of the mullet.And even though they haven’t been in fashion since 1982, I’m sure they’ll be making a huge comeback in the very near future. Herma’an: But Aymin, the mullet isn’t stylish. [Flaymin ponders that thought for a moment before he clicks his fingers together] Flaymin: I got it!We’ll give him a flashy headband with sparkly lime-coloured thingies splattered all over it.That would make the mullet look cool. Herma’an: And he’d certainly stand out from the crowd in the dark. Flaymin: He’ll have men just crawling all over him in no time! Herma’an: I agree.But now there is only one thing left before the transformation is complete. Flaymin: And that would be? Herma’an: The man himself.You go get him and I’ll finish off the final touches of our masterplan. Flaymin: Fantastic!I’ll go get him! [Flaymin dashes out of the room to find Jamie as the scene fades to black]
