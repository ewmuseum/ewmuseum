The PI Press Conference, and a Warning to the NVG

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The PI Press Conference, and a Warning to the NVG Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: Today Hollywood, California. The film capital of the world. Many a star-struck man or woman has headed out West with dreams or delusions of grandeur and greatness. Recently, films such as Lilo and Stitch and Scooby Doo have debuted. However, an even bigger film is about to debut. It is not a film that will likely be sent to theaters, due to its length. Then why make it? Because, its producers and costars have the cash to do so. Who are these mysterious men? If you said 'Pretty Cool Guys', shoot yourselves. Of course, these two men are Politically Incorrect. Yes, the politicians have produced their own feature film. And, with some string pulling and arm twisting, have gotten some of Hollywood's best to star in it alongside them. And so, news media have converged upon a small theater on the outskirts of Hollywood. Roger Schultz faces down a veritable army of press figures and journalists, as he waits for his charges to be ready. ::The reporters seem to be growing more and more anxious by the second. Schultz does his best to keep the men at bay, before he finally cannot prolong the suspence any further. Roger then grins, stepping up to the microphone.:: Roger Schultz: Ladies and gentlemen, without futher ado, I introduce to you...the next Primetime Championship Wrestling World Tag Team Champions, and Hollywood's newest stars.......'The Master Debator' William S. Powell....and the reigning Primetime Championship Wrestling Extreme Champion, 'The Governor' Reginald V. Lovecraft......collectively........POLITICALLY INCORRECT! ::Powell and Lovecraft march out, impeccably dressed. Powell is, as always, in all black, Lovecraft wearing a red suitcoat and black pants, the Extreme Championship draped over his shoulder.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: We'll take questions. ::A reporter from the local ABC station rises: ABC Reporter: Before we get into this movie of yours, Mr. Lovecraft, what do you feel about your chances in your Extreme Title Match Sunday? Reginald V. Lovecraft: I feel my chances are excellent! I mean, just look at who is in this matchup with me! Violence Jack, a man I know little of, other than he is embroiled in a CLASSIC battle with Daveylicious, at least until Big D. left for the fine pastures of FSW. ::He chuckles slightly:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Then, we have Blood Angel, a member of the Phantom Phive, Phour, Phree, or whatever we're down to after disgruntled garbageman Clyde killed Supreme Machine. I think we showed our respect for the Phive after we initiated The Great Debate last Tuesday, and the Phantom Phive fell before the Political Machine.. ::Powell interjects himself, on the behalf of his partner.:: William S. Powell: And then, Duzza. Enough said right there. And of course, Kramer Wild Wing. Master of the ever-deadly and dangerous FERRIS WHEEL OF FUCKING DESTRUCTION. Kramer, ARE YOU ON CRACK? WHAT MENTAL GENIUS NAMES HIS FINISHER THE FERRIS WHEEL OF DESTRUCTION? Besides, the move is a Giant Swing. In a Giant Swing, you spin someone in a HORIZONTAL CIRCLE! A FERRIS WHEEL SPINS VERTICALLY! WHAT TYPE OF MORON ARE YOU?! ::An NBC affiliate decides to speak up.:: NBC Affiliate: While I realize you each have important matches at Gold and Glory, I'd like to ask, what made you get into the film making industry to start? William S. Powell: Well, actually, it WAS our matches at Gold and Glory that inspired us. After watching that idiot Rent-A-Hero flail around for months, it gave us an excellent idea. Super villains SO get the shaft in movies! I mean, they ALWAYS LOSE! It's discrimination against evil people! Reginald V. Lovecraft: And so, we believed it high time that we introduce a motion picture, from a villain's point of view. And thus, we are proud to announce our film, which will be starting in a few hours, is entitled... ::The two politicians smile, before gesturing skyward and screaming: Both: CORRUPTION MAN! :At this, Roger Schultz pulls a cord, revealing the Corruption Man title logo on the backdrop behind Politically Incorrect:: William S. Powell: We feel that this concept will revolutionize the way the average person looks at the superhero/supervillain genre. It shall be a new beginning. ::A Los Angeles Times reporter raises his hand, and Powell calls upon him.:: LA Times Reporter: On another subject, what do you think about the tag team battle royale? William S. Powell: Tough. All of the teams in it seem to be good, and have the ability to win, except maybe Misplaced. Although, there is one thing that quite upsets me. Akiyama..trying to win the title on his own. Akiyama.....a man who refuses to speak for himself, instead letting a 'Divine Prophetess' speak for him. Cowardice? Most likely... ::Lovecraft's ire too has been aroused by the Neo Void God, and he takes his turn to speak.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Some God....Akiyama.....I do not take kindly to those who mock religion. I am a Southern Baptist, a very devout one indeed. You see, it is false prophets and Gods, such as yourself and Cyclonica...that are the cause for the evil in this world. You claim to rule over the Void...I warn you now, Akiyama, cross my path, and you will be presiding over quite a different Void....a Void of blackness....a Void of death. ::More journalists clamor for a question, but Roger Schultz cuts them off.:: Roger Schultz: Ladies and gentlemen, the film is about to start. Any of you with press passes may enter the theater.. ::As Politically Incorrect enter the theater, the camera fades to black, with no catchy advertisement.:: ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
