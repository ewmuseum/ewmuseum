Where ever I go, action is never far behind. (#1TT)

14 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-14-02 Seclusion, isolation, imprisonment from that what you crave. Could you stand living without it? It’s calling you, second after second, you stomach whales at you to take it back. You want to feel it again, you want to be in the middle of what you desire. You want it like never before… yet you cannot get it. The feeling is none-describable. It is like hell personified. Your body stretches with mixed emotions, you never thought you could ever miss it like this… yet you do, like never before. I sometimes wonder how I could miss wondering the streets of Boston night after night. Almost lifelessly pulling the trigger of my handgun as a bullet shot out of it and chased its prey. I wonder how I could miss get the crap beat out of me by twenty men at once. Or how I could miss using my bare fist to pummel a man’s face until it was nothing like before, until it was covered in a crimson liquid and had a toothless expression on it’s face. I am good, I have always been good, and I don’t desire evil, so why would I miss things such as that. I believe in justice and prevailing over what’s wrong in the world. Yet, there were so many good sides to it. Saving the innocent, living with my best friends and extended family around Boston. And just general day-to-day living. It was simple, yet it is a life I love, and cherish. Now it is gone, and I would do almost anything to get it back. Jamie Celt walked down the busy streets of Wisconsin. The streets were packed with busy people, getting on with their busy life. Of course there was always a laid back, slowly, tramping alone individual spotted but they came few and far between. Jamie seemed to be walking in the opposite way of them all. He was hit on the shoulder a few times by inconsiderate citizens who just want to get to their destination and don’t give a damn about anyone else. The sun was beaming down on the city and people had perspiration formatting on the top of their shirts due to the sweltering heat. Though heroic clouds were coming from both ends to stop the sun’s attack on the earth. Jamie walked through Hubbardville; it was fast becoming a large city, filled with prestige and class, with little crime and a very high level of luxury throughout. Jamie, however, did not feel home is such a place. He felt home in Boston City, with his family and friends, doing what he has done his whole life. Though, due to his extremely busy schedule with PCW, it will be extremely hard for him to spend much time their. He had to train day and night, spend ours on promotional propaganda and of course, attend events, and battle to reach the top of the game. Jamie wore a long t-shirt, the sleeves stretched down to his elbows; it was green and black, saying ‘CELT’ is sharp writing, it was Jamie’s first promotional top for PCW, he was proud to wear it. He also had on baggy, dark blue jeans and his hair was as always spiked up, with a wet-look to it. Jamie continued to walk down the cleanly streets of Hubbardville. He wondered about his upcoming match, the following night against Cecelia Verucana. It would be his first battle against a woman. He had no idea how he would handle the situation. He in no way liked to treat woman as a ‘weaker sex’. Though from the statistics he had ‘dug up’ Verucana is what most of Jamie’s opponent’s are not, much lighter than him. She is around 178 pounds while Jamie is 222. He would have a big advantage in that department. Though she was a very powerful woman, and her highflying skills were exceptional. All in all Jamie was prepared for a wrestling match. And that’s when he realized, he was not fighting a woman, he was wrestling one. That gave him great relief, and believed his moral standing in the match was much higher at that stage. He felt like PCW would be his big and only break, he knew he had to make a success out of it. And he would start on Cecelia Verucana. He was disappointed his match against Trigger was discontinued on Turmoil, and people did not get to see Jamie to his full potential, but the Last NTICW Champion was intending to show his skills to their full, and on next week he would go into his number one contendership match for the TV title with confidence and would be willing to sacrifice every vital part of his body in order to achieve success, he needed success, he wanted success, he thrived upon success. Jamie continued to walk down the cleanly streets. He was not out for any reason in particular, he had just checked into a hotel and decided to take a walk, to clear his mind, so to speak. Jamie walked into a small local shop. He looked around; it was a small cramped little place. Jamie went over to the magazine’s section and glanced through them, not really paying attention to what he was looking at. He though again about how much life had changed for him since moving away from Boston. He wanted to go back so badly. He wanted to be a part of the Celtics again, and fight against rival gangs, and save people from the clutches of evil, as corny as that may sound. He felt empty inside without it, though he loved wrestling, he did not know how long he could go without the adrenaline rush he got that came with everyday life in Boston. Jamie is startled and awoken from his deep though but a big, rough looking man who comes into the shop. Jamie immediately gets nervous as he sees the man wears a balaclava. Jamie was one of four other customers in the shop. The others were an old man and woman, holding hands, obviously married, as soon as they saw the man the flinched back in horror and the old man grabbed his wife’s hand tighter than before. There was also a boy of about 15 he looked up with a blue bubble jacket and looked startled at the sight. And lastly were a young girl with a child in her arms. She dropped the basket of groceries she was carrying at the vista of the man. The cashier was a man slightly older than Jamie, of Indian background quite obviously. He looked at the man is horror and back away from the counter. The man came right up to the cash register, and then too everyone is the shop’s horror he pulled out, what looked to Jamie like an A-45 pistol. “NOBODY MOVE!” The man said with a deep, yet slightly quivering voice. It was then that Jamie felt less fear. The quiver in his voi8ce told Jamie he was nervous, and would more than likely not dare pull the trigger. Jamie calmly hid behind the isle and couched down. “Get all the money out of the register now!” He said to the cashier. The young Indian boy did as he was told and began putting the money into a small green bag given to him by the thief. He was nearly crying is he unloaded the money. And when he was finished the thief quickly took the bag. He looked around at the people; Jamie could see fear in his eyes. The thief did not know what to do next. “Uh, everyone on the floor, now!” He says. The all do as he says, the Mother was crying and the old man was holding his wife tightly. “Okay, now don’t move!” The thief said shuddering. He then ran out of the shop and down the street. Jamie got up and chased him. He knew the thief would not dare fire a shot at him. The thief ran for his life but Jamie was in hot pursuit. He chased him, knocking people out of his way as he went. The thief ran into an alleyway but Jamie was catching him fast. Finally the classic seen appeared. A steal gate separated the thief from freedom and capture. Jamie would not give up. The Thief leap over the gate but somehow Jamie catches the bottom of his right trouser. Jamie then climbs over himself as the perspiration falls from his face. Jamie gets up and throws the man against the wall, and takes off the balaclava. The man was young, and seemed to have little morale in his dark complexion. “Nice try man, but next times do it right.” Jamie said coolly as he struggled to find his breath. The man did not reply. Jamie pushed him onto the ground where his fall was broken by black rubbish bags. Jamie grabbed the green bag and climbed back over the gate, not even looking behind him to see what the man did, and how he responded to losing his money. As Jamie walked back to the shop he realized he did not need Boston to do what he loved doing, action was everywhere, and Jamie would never have to go looking for it, somehow it always managed to find him first. He hoped, he prayed though that leaving Boston for PCW would be a good move, time would only tell, and his time was Fusion, for Cecelia Verucana would be Jamie’s next form of trouble.
