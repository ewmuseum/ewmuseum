Saying Goodbye - (short)

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-26-02 'I always lived thinking my mind was free. Never knowing the restraints I held only thinking inside the shell. Finally it was unleashed, my mind expanded further than most. I now walk the earth and notice how blind I really was, I walk this earth and notice the ignorance which floods the land. I walk this earth alone, but always alert.' Jay Sylo...he now knew his kidnappers identity, none other than the one people call Akiyama. What was Akiyama's purpose? It was quit simple, Sylo had saved him and now he was helping enlighten Sylo's mind, turn him into something more than human. After his transfer from a mortal to a celestial he felt different. Sylo was now taller, stronger, and faster. A machine in ways but still held his human side close. He had one more thing to take care of before he could finally move on. We join him now but remember his up-coming matches press hard into his mind....but it seems now that he isn't as worried as he used to be. (The ground; a cold waste land. The stone drove into the fresh dirt acted as a marker looming with a shadow over a fresh plot of dug up earth. The sky was a mix of thick gray and white gravy with no life moving over head. Fifty paces to the north an old, cold, dead tree stood like an old man who had seen better days. The grass was a dead, dark brown color with weeds sprouting for freedom at different points. A slight, chilly wind blew through the air, howling out songs which only it could understand. Over the lot previously pointed out a dark figure loomed like a spirit watching over the ones he loved. He was a rather large man, very muscular, short trimmed hair which stood on ends like blades of grass but looked as sharp as shrapnel. His clothes hung from his body, baggy to the fiber. It blew in the breeze like a flag in the night, howls from animals blasted through the air as the figure slowly ran a large finger over the fresh dirt, swirling it around. Slowly he raised two grains of the fresh brown dirt in between two of his fingers rubbing it slowly. Almost like he was inspecting it for something.) 'Today I let you go,' The voice slowly said looking down. (He ran his hand across some letters which were engraved in the stone. The letters were blurry, you couldn't really see what words they made out but you knew they were there. His hand dropped like a lifeless corpse as his eyes were an ice blue color all around which matched his hair and goatee.) 'But now I look to the future and now I am more than human. I will be better now....pushed by a force greater than you or I ever knew,' The voice said again. (A name followed this figure, the name? Jay Sylo. The 'Sylencer' himself. The same man who would fight for the rising star title against Lance Bell, Ripper Ryckman, and Steve Johnson.) 'Three men who walk the road which leads to different paths. Lance Bell is the champion but he is not a champion. His ignorance and cockiness will be the downfall for him and once you go up the way down hurts even more,' Sylo said under his breath toward the grave stone. (His eyes continually shifted up and down almost like they were on guard for something, scanning every fiber of motion that moved. His heart beat in a rhythm as he continued staring into the endless gray head stone.) 'Ripper, an individual who seems to be battling many different demons within. His soul isn't free and it blocks up his senses. He thinks he can destroy that which he doesn't understand but it will be he that is destroyed. I do not play games anymore, and this shall be shown Tuesday,' Sylo said stopping to take a closer look at the headstone. 'So long you and I traveled together but now you rest six feet in the soil where you will be safe. Safe from the world you always wanted to leave behind but I am left here to fight. March Madness will be the beginning of the end for all the evil for now I am Celestial...and nothing can stop me,' He said staring to the grave once more. (He pulled back a small bit of the dirt and reached down deep into his large pocket. He withdrew two metallic looking dog tags. He ripped one off and placed it in the smaller plot he pulled back, and then like a bull dozer he pushed the dirt back forward sealing the tag in.) 'I know you will treasure this....even in the after life. I know you will always be with me so when I go into battle you will be there. Even if it is against Lance Bell, Ripper, or even Johnson we will always come out victorious because now no one....can stop me,' (A small smirk moved across the cold lips of Sylo. Something not seen in some time. His eyes seemed to shimmer almost like glitter on a bright day as he held the smile across his lips.) 'Soon, there will be a new champion holding their title. Soon the world will have a hero, and soon the world will be free. I thank Akiyama for what he has done. He has enlightened my mind and for that I am grateful,' He said through the smile. 'This new power drives me....it pushes me and I will become known throughout the world as a man you don't mess with, as a man who will only help the innocent and strike down the evil, I am the one they fear,' Sylo replies. (The gate behind him slowly slammed open and closed. It's old rusty metal screeched on each exchange. Sylo looked back and closed his eyes...memories rushed through his head and it seemed to leave just as fast. He looked down at the headstone which read 'THE OLD JAY SYLO') 'Goodbye my friend....I am the new version of us and I have been reborn. Now to move on and finish what was started so long ago. May god have mercy on the souls of Bell, Ripper, and Johnson....' (Slowly he stood up dusting off his pants as he walked down the old dirt path. Sylo headed out the rusted gates past the old rotting tree. And like that he was gone...but not forgotten.)
