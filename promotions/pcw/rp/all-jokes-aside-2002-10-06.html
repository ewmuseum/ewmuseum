Date: 10-6-02 Friday Fusion - September 27th It was only a few hours after the end of Friday Fusion, with them once again defeating the tandem of Gaz Green and Nerezza, solidifying a second consecutive victory over the Tag Team champions. After the cameras had shut off and the fans had gone home, well satisfied with the carnage and actions of the night, Statuz Quo sat back against a couch, a white towel drapped over his head collecting up sweat and blood. His back still glistens with perspiration from the night's work. The sheen goes nicely with the patches of dried and not so dried patches of blood covering his body. The PCW Medical Staff has done their job sewing up the wound on his forehead. Grasping his head, a painfull expression hangs from Quo's face. It was an easy reminder that this was the most painful night in Statuz Quo's career. All in all, the night of Friday, September 27th was maybe the most influential night in the career of Statuz Quo. For one, the victory over Pranks With Attitude had been a sweet one, maybe the exact fit towards what the outcome might be at Red October. Over the course of the night, twenty minutes brutality were dished out over the course of one match. That 5 Man Handicap match was the entry point into the eye of the storm. A PCW style handicap match has its differences that regular handicap matches, because there are no rules. Its basically survival of the fittest team. Rush went along to face The End in the crowds, and Dante Jackson happened to be entangled with Gaz Green, leaving Quo with the dreaded Nerezza alone. For over 15 minutes he fought alone, no one to watch his back and let them receive the two-on-one helping hand. He was pulverized through a table, his skin shredded as he bled heavily. He was bashed in with chairs, blasted with planks of wood, but he refused to give up. It too both teams to knock Quo out, putting out Nerezza with the Flat Broke, but being caught with a steel chair to the skull, courtesy of Gaz Green. Any noteworthy idiot can see that Statuz Quo's body has been torn to shreds. All of the shit that happens to be brought upon Quo can now be bullshit. Quo's busted his ass not once, not twice, but in three straight matches and almost killed himself in the process. And to show for the bruises, a championship just waiting to drop into his lap. Red October is a hearted event, something cherished by every man and woman in the lockerroom, and whomever has walked down the hallowed halls of PCW. It test boundaries, makes something out of the people that you didn't think were going to make it. For Statuz Quo, its a time to show just who in the hell he happens to be. Not some banger out of Atlanta, but his old actions in RUSH, showing why he's in PCW now. Then he was the second best man in the federation, a trait he tends to acompany everytime he places on the red pants. He's a dreamer now, seeing the future almost as bright than everyone else. The evolution of Statuz Quo as a man, not just a wrestler. The thoughts of the night protrude his mind, even if Rush did leave them, they overcame it and proved to Pranks With Attitude who the real Tag Team Champions should rightfully be. Anybody could handle Duzza and Makonie, but remember - Playaz Inc. were the #1 Contenders. 'Shawty!' Quo screams as he lets the towel float from the sky, sailing majestically from the ceiling and all the way to the floor. The door to the lockeroom swings open, allowing Dante Jackson, the beta member of Playaz Inc. to make his way into the ring. Taking a step inward, he smiles before tossing a bottle of Cristal onto the sofa, making Quo jump. 'The fuck's that for?' he picked himself up from the couch and began to eye the bottle for all of its glory. That shade of emerald green struck a cord in the eye of Quo, a smile appearing on his face. This was the just reward for all of their sacrificing over the past two months, and finally it delivers. 'After tonight, you just have to understand that you and me are officially the Pranks With Attitude asskickers!' Dante boasted as he began to unravel the shroud resting above the opening of the bottle. For the most part, Jackson isn't as bad as Quo happens to be right now, but is still radiant in his own sweat from the night's actions. A shallow abrasion on his right bicep has been stitched together and bandaged. Jackson looked at the wound as only a joke, something only to attract women, he guessed. Slowly, the smoke began to rise from the bottle of Cris` as Dante clanked together a wine glass, anticipating the bubbles against his tongue. Suddenly he stops himself, before looking over exactly what's placed in front of him. 'No.' he mutters, dropping the bottle to the ground, Quo eyes his partner, shallowly dropping the bottle to the floor. Quo bellows out a resounding grunt before catching the bottle, seconds before impact. Quo dusts himself off, placing the bottle on the table. 'The hell's the matter with you?' 'WE JUST CAN'T DO IT!' he squealed, before beginning to beat away at the couch. Quo raised an eyebrow towards his friend, before laughing, faking the truth of the true problem. 'Yo ass can't be serious...' Dante raised an eyebrow, 'I'm dead-fuckin' serious.' 'Well, how bout you just go to jail, end up like Jason Snow and jester to every muthafucka in the lot?!' Quo darted at Jackson, who sideswepped him out of the way. Jackson grunted, before pushing himself out of the room, slamming the door behind him. Jackson huffed, his eyes fuming as if he looked possessed, a man within rage and uncontrolled anger. Its been two months, all bet seven months since the journey had started for both men. It all changed them, that solitude of being by themselves, including the source of gaining a championship. Their blood was boiling, with Quo looking back at the door, but finding only empty space and that Jackson had been gone a while ago. He shuts the door, before beating the wall with a solid fist. Yesterday, matter of weeks ago, the two of them didn't have that meaning full object or purpose for victory. Just win, and get your name out was the motto. A title changes them, it changes anyone. Quo takes a few paces around the room before lighting himself a blunt and calming his nerves. Whether or not, the quiet emotion burning between the both of them is running on a boil. Statuz Quo knows it, and Dante Jackson knows it as well. All a matter of time. Kimora Gym - October 5th Every single push he continues to make, its a step closer towards defeating Gaz Green and Nerezza for the PCW Tag Team titles. Its a goal he's been wanting for over a year, ever since he took baby steps in the Primetime Wrestling Academy. He strained slightly, before pushing the bar past the bridge in his arm and elbows. Just above him, a spotter stands, cautiously watching him lift the weights, before setting the bar back at a common resting place. 'HIT THE DAMN BAR!' The man's booming voice is enough for Dante to take the bar into his grip once more, lifting it off the hinges and beginning to push up once more. Its been only an hour, and Jackson has upgraded his max from 250 to 335. He begins his breathing motion, sucking inward as he drops the weight slowly on his chest, before blowing outward as he pushes up. The strain isn't that much as he quietly begins to speed up the repetition, slowly counting out his progession louder and louder, making it more of a boast than a tell of when he's finished. The sounds of 'Lose Yourself' by Eminem begins to play in the gym as the physical test of strength is gaining on Dante as he rest the bar once more. '30!' Its a decision for him to stop that satisfies him a bit more than beginning happened to. 'Well done,' he repeats to himself with a slight chuckle, 'well done.' As the bar snaps back on the railing, Dante sits upward, starring at his picture in the mirror. The definition of a champion has been hard to define in his opinion. All Dante has ever seen from people are their victories, their chances awaken and his torn off into the dust. Not that it mattered much, but Jackson longed for a championship. He wanted one to call his own. Something that would take him out of hte picture as just a 'great wrestler'. His young lion tenacity continues to make him grow more of an ego, even indulging in that which may not benefit him, but instead elevates the people around him. He placed the black #1 Playa cap over his head, as he took his duffel bag, slug it over his shoulder and began to walk out of the gym. Slowly, the sounds of footsteps approaching caught him off guard as he squinted his eyes to check what was going on behind him. Statuz Quo. 'Hol up, hol the fuck up!' The much larger Quo happened to stare into the eyes of Dante, for the better part of a week, the words they would usually part each other with were gone, replaced by silence and their very own reasonable doubt. As much as they were friends, that rivalry back in RUSH before Playaz Inc. even formed. 'So, you still think we can't take those two boys?' he questioned Jackson solomenly, before relaxing his shoulders. Dante smirked, 'I've changed my mind on the subject, but I think...' 'You think what?' 'Something, just something.' Quo stared at Jackson, 'The fuck's that supposed to mean?' 'You know,' Dante started up another cooking scheme of his. 'They've gotten Mr. T as a manager thinking he'd just scare the shit out of us because he's their random strong black guy right?' 'So?' Quo wondered. 'Is that supposed to fucking freak me out?' Dante sighed, 'No, Its supposed to make you think!' 'Fuck.' Quo uttered that word, before Dante turned to face Quo's direction. Slowly but surely, the small form of motorcycles began to take notice in both men as the small spectrum rose into a pack of hungry men and one single woman riding shotgun on the back of the pack leader. Dante raised his eyebrow, and slowly he tapped Quo to get his attention, 'What the hell is that?' 'Aw fuck yeah!' he began to get a little excited, bouncing up and downward on the pavement, starring into the spot that happened to show those bikers gaining upward infront of the two men. The humming increased, before the shallow skid against solid cement widened Dante's eyes. 'Gang members?' he focused his attention on Quo who shot back, 'I guess, or maybe some dumbass bitches wanting their heads screwed the fuck in.' 'I'll agree with that.' The two men chuckled, before one of the bikers caused them to pipe up. 'SILENCE!' 'I guess that's the crazy motherfucker.' Dante whispered as one by one, each of them began to remove their helmets. All of them happened to carry a dubious distinction to one another. As they dismount their bikes, in the eyes of Statuz Quo - its was all easy to fuck these people up. One, standing out in the very front happened to have a green streak riding down the middle portion of his hair. The red rooster haircut shaped on his head, along with the spiked necklace and the piece of barbed wire sticking from his eyebrow made Dante wonder, who is he? 'Let me introduce myself...' Green hair broke the chatter. 'I am....K-PAX!' The two men infront of him stand there, before looking at each other and bursting out in laughter. K-Pax as he happens to be called stomps his foot on the ground, shaking their laughter to complete silence. 'You dare mock me?' 'Well, yeah.' the Lyrical Messiah commented for the group. 'I mean, you call yourself the name of a horrendous moive starring Kevin Spacey, and well, you kinda look like a Gaz to me.' Statuz Quo begins to stare into the man's face, arching his eyebrows a bit before nodding, 'Yeah, in the mouth he does.' Dante turns to Statuz, 'And how in the hell would you know that?' 'Because,' Statuz winks. 'I beat his ass on Fusion, a punch in the damn mouth can do very devious somethings.' 'Like what? Fucking hoes like a speeding bullet?' 'Not quite, but it'd get us a spot on UPN. Or maybe FOX, you know how those dumbasses like having failed reality experiments.' 'SHUT UP!' K-Pax interrupts the two men as Dante and Quo turns their heads to face the man. 'NO YOU SHUT UP!' K-Pax is taken a bit awkward by this as Jackson and Quo continue their conversation. The fumbling leader of the pack pivots his foot, before charging ahead at the two men, who happen to slide over to the right, making the small man stumble into the concrete. Blood begins to start dripping from his nose as he gets back up, stepping on the puddle and turning to face the two men once more. '-okay, so what other form of beating white people is there?' Dante asked, strutting around Quo who held his distance in the middle. 'We can't just come on television, beat them up and then leave.' 'That's Parental Advisory and Black Attack-ish.' Statuz nodded at Jackson, who laughed at it a bit, before patting down his throwback Nikes. 'How about we come on there, act like them before whupping their asses?' 'Naw,' Dante stopped Quo's idea with one word. 'We'd be making asses out of our damn selves!' 'Aight, aight. But its hard to think when there's a crazy dude right behind you, trying to cut off your lung support and shit.' Quo turns around, to find the man known as K-Pax with CIA wiring, heading right in his direction. The larger and more powerful man lifts K-Pax by his jeans and hoists him in the air. Screaming for his comrades to help him, K-Pax struggles to make his way out of the tight bind that Quo has forced him into. 'Dante, you get him from the back, I got the front.' he told his partner in a war games like method, the move of catch and catch can was a devious tactic, and not many could master it. But when you're on the streets like Statuz Quo happens to be all the time, then its only natural that you master it. 'SUPER WEDIGE!' A loud rip can be heard as K-Pax howls out in pain, the stretch marks from the elastic underwear too much for him to bear. The Hanes portion of his underwear are long enough to be placed over his head as Quo drops him to his feet. The small man can barely walk right with about three inches of cotton white shoved in his crack. 'Lets go...' he mutters as tears begin to flow down his face. 'Let's go!' 'Nobody can defeat Wedgie Power!' Dante laughs. Pranks With Attitude. Since that night when we defeated them for the #1 Contendership, its been well known that we were the better team. They're given the shots against Makoine and Duzza. Same thing - the better team wins. But, in retrospect, its nice that they won those titles. Its nice that we've defeated them not only once, but twice. Tonight, we do it for a third time and secure our right and our claim to those Tag Team titles. But them, we haven't touched the other two specimen have we? Redemption Song... Cowabunga Connection... The Redemption Song is composed of Apache Chief and Robert Ulrich, two goons from the times of cowboy movies and indian scalpings that don't know that they're in for the night of their lives tonight. I guess when you've been searching for a female for months and months now, you lose track. Cowabunga Connection is not different, with Heavy Nuts For Honor and Totally Twatty Tally, they make up the only team that has defeated us before. Well, maybe because we weren't ready for the spotlight, whatever you happen to mean. But I know for certain that if they make it into the finals, then low and behold, they'll be facing us. Tom Cruise, Richardo Moltablan, 'Eddie' from Frasier, and even Adam West can be a little stupid, or filler what ever the case might be. Tonight is our night. The night, of Playaz Inc. Ricardo Moltablan: When ever I'm needed, I fill in because I can bring in, the raitings!' The echo of a gunshot can be heard as the former star of Police Squad lays on the floor, bleeding.
