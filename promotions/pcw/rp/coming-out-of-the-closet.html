Coming out of the closet.....

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-4-02 ::Introduction:: It looks as though Ryan 'Streak' Stephens won the battle of the two. With all the trash talking he did how can he not back that up without winning. It could've been a political matter, but he could've just have won just because he was the better wreslter that night. With Mark being in the dark all week he is now beginning to come out of the closet from his defeat. He feels that he has not put up the performance or attitude that PCW really wants. Now he wants to improve and be the best wrestler that he can be. What can Mark do to improve since he just had a reality check last Sunday night? He can come back on Fusion and defeat The End. But with what The End has put up already it looks as if Mark will lose yet again and his chances for a belt are gone for yet another month. He will the have to resort down to the low card again and work up from there. Mark needs to start and make a name for himself and hopefully it will start on Fusion. ::End Introduction:: The scene opens up outside of a small California town where it is getting dark. On the dusty alleyways there is a 4th of July carnival where there are food stands and there are game stands. Some of the basic rides are going with the lines pretty much just widling down since the fireworks are going to start soon. Mark has a hot dog in his hand and he starts to look over the game stands. There is the stand where the bottles are set up and you have to throw one ball to knock em all down. Mark goes up to the stand and a man with a couple of teeth left stands there laughing. 'Step right up and try your luck at this sir.' screams the man as Mark is standing right next to him. 'How much is it?' Mark asks as he is looking at the man's missing teeth and his grayish brown beard. 'It is $1 for one throw.' The man says as he points to the sign that shows the price on it. Mark goes down deep into his blue shorts pocket and he pulls out a wrinkled $1 bill. He hands it over to the man who is working the game. He reaches into the bucket with balls in it and he pulls out one of them. Mark goes up the the line where he has to throw the balls. He aims at the balls and he finally winds up and throws the balls at the milk bottles. One goes down and with another one, leaving the bottom right one standing. 'Close that was very close there bud.' The attendent says as he scratches his hairy chest with his fingernails that haven't been cleaned in a couple of weeks. 'Step up and try it again.' 'No all of you people just want to try and steal my money saying that this game is easy, but all you do is rig this up so no one can win.' Mark says as he is looking at the man with a bite out of his hot dog. Mark goes off while the man is still talking with the Mark. Mark starts to walk down the alley that have the food stands and some more games. Mark is not thinking about much right now except for just relaxing and having fun at the carnival. He finishes up his hot dog while he is walking and he goes over to the ticket booth. 'How many tickets sir?' asks the lady who is inside of the ticket booth. 'Just give me enough to ride the Scrambler.' Mark says, talking through the little screen hole. 'Five dollars sir.' The lady says as she types that into her cash register. Mark again goes deep into his short pockets and he fumbles around the change making noise. Then he pulls out the five dollar bill that he was looking for. He slides that under the hole cut out into the booth. Then the lady hands him three blue tickets. Mark takes those tickets and he heads over to the Scrambler ride. The Scrambler is a ride that goes arouind in circles as it doesn't go that fast. Mark hands the three tickets to the man attendent and then he goes to find a space for him to sit. A little kid who is by himself goes over to where Mark is sitting. 'Can I ride with you sir?' asks the little kid looking up at Mark with his dark brown eyes. 'Hop on in.' Mark says as he notices it is a white kid and not an African American kid. The two sit in and the ride is staring out slow. It starts to go around in a circle, but then it starts to speed up. Mark is just sitting there with a slight smile on his face just enjoying the ride and not worrying about The End or anything else. The ride is going a decent speed and Mark looks over at the little kid who is getting a kick out of the ride. He probably thinks that this is a fast ride and enjoying life to the fullest right now. He is not worrying about politics or what color you are, just being a kid. 'I want to go again.' Says the little kid as the ride starts to come to a stop. Mark just smiles seeing the kid smile. Mark lifts the pin up as he swings the door open letting the little kid out first. Mark hops out and he exits the ride. It is getting dusk out as the sun is going to the other side of the Earth. Mark is heading to the field where the yearly fireworks are getting fired off. Mark goes over and there are silver, metal benches set up for groups of people to sit on. Mark just goes over to the right of that and he finds an old crate that he can sit on. Patriotic music starts to play over the loud speakers that are set up on the light poles all over the field. The fireworks start to go off and Mark starts to realize a lot of things. 'It is good to just come out here and relax to get some things off of my chest.' Mark starts to say as he watches the first fireworks go off into the dark night sky having it light up the sky like day. 'I have been holding a lot of feelings in about who I am and how I feel. Yeah I know that I am a bad person, but people just look at that and not how I act. So what if I don't like African Americans that still doesn't mean I won't like other people. People don't know the kind of history I have been though as a kid.' Mark starts to flashback to his early childhood in Pasadena, California. He is sitting in the family room watching the old 25' television that is set up on the Cartoon Network. He is just enjoying himself on the couch relaxing. Then he hears his brother outside talking to his buddies and then he hears a car with loud music come by. Mark just ignores the sound and he goes back to watching the Roadrunner run through the desert. A couple of minutes later he hears some gun shots being fired just outside so he moves to the front of the couch, crouching down. After a couple of minutes Mark gets up from the couch and he goes out front to the house. There lies on the front porch Mark's brother and his friends in a pool of their own blood. Mark leans down to his brother and he puts his head on his chest. He hears not beat and tears start to roll down the side of his cheek as he is realizing what has happened. Back to the life right now Mark starts to get emotional and he the leans back in the crate and he watches the rest of the fireworks go off. It was ten years to date that Mark's brother was killed and now he is ready to dedicate this match with The End to his brother who meant so much to him. 'I just realized how empty I have felt in the past years without my brother who I spent all of my time with when I was a kid.' Mark says as he sees another firework go up in the sky. 'I just wish he was here but that is beside the point. I need not to think about that or anything because I am here to relax and just think about my upcoming match with The End.' With that said Mark is starting to get back into his Neo-Nazi mood as he watches the grand finale of fireworks go up in the air. Mark is thinking about Fusion with The End and about some other things on his mind that will be revealed soon in a matter of time.
