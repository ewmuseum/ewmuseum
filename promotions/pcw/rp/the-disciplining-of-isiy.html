The Disciplining of Isiy...

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: After last Promo... '...B!tch...' “Where am I?” '...B!tch.' “B!tch??” '...Dont stop crying like a b!tch with a skinned knee...' “Oh... no please...” She was lying upon a hard, rough surface, but couldn't see through the darkness that surrounded her. Her face was wet... she been crying, it seemed. She wasn't bleeding... nor were there any marks that she could see from the needles or otherwise, and that both filled her with relief and frightened her at the same time. She tried to push herself up... but found she had no strength. She could barely roll over to her side, which she wanted to desperately because there was a sharp jagged rock under her, digging into the small of her back. As soon as she managed to do so, she heard footsteps nearing... and quite suddenly, was aware that she could see through the darkness. It was funny darkness, in that when she looked down at herself, she could see herself clearly; but couldn't see an inch in front of nose otherwise, to look at the ground for instance. The footsteps walked around her... and she could see that whoever stood before her wore black boots with silver buckles. That was all the observation she got to make before one of those boots reared back and slammed into her stomach. All of the air rushed from her stomach in a great rush... but before she could even begin to recover, a hand snaked down into her hair and lifted her head up. She heard a voice that was remarkably familiar. “Little girl... if you don't stop crying like a b!tch with a skinned knee... we’re going to choke you until our hands get tired!! For every tear you shed in our presence... we are going to smack the FRICKIN’ taste out your mouth and then smack it back in!! That's your problem... you show too much weakness!! Too much!!! What kind of a goddess are YOU?? And then... in what is probably the greatest fallacy of all since Pharaoh decided that he wanted to be just another LOSER and try to defy us... you actually believe to the bottom of your heart that you're strong…you actually believe that your husband’s strong!!! Please... give us a frickin’ break!! As we look at you now…you and your bastard husband... what we see is about as strong as a tissue condom!!! And everybody knows it... except for you...” Her eyes fluttered as she began to regain her lost breath... but the person whose face was only inches from hers remained a blur through her tears. She continued to speak... her voice taking on a mocking quality. “The truth is... if it weren't for us... would have been broken a long time ago... broken and cast aside for the little useless piece of garbage that you are... and it's d@mn funny to watch you think otherwise. But we can see how you would come to believe all these things. It's not too late for us to fix it... and fix you... and show you exactly why you're husband’s so pathetic...' Her eyes began to clear as the tears flowed down her face... and the man that spoke to her (it was undoubtedly a man by the roaring) began to come into focus... but was abruptly sent into a blur again by a sense-shattering slap across her face that sent an uncomfortable numbing sensation across her cheek. The voice suddenly became much more vicious and dripped with unveiled derision. 'The Great Titu Xansiv... known and feared for his ring skill... the only mortal to have ever held the Divine Tag Titles with the ONE true God of PCW... and a complete and utter waste of good air. You think you're pretty d@mn good, don'tcha, Isiy? You think you're hot sh!t!! But you see... if your husband were half as good as you claim to be... then you wouldn't be here right now getting your @ss kicked just like your husband!!... ' .... At last... the man's face came into clarity before her terrified eyes...which grew wider still when she found that the face was none other than the Neo Void God. His lips were twisted in a wry, one-sided smile that spoke of both bemusement and menace. As she looked at Akiyama, she felt like a bug caught trapped in a bottle, watching her captor lighting a Jumping Jack firecracker to put in the bottle with her to see if she'll survive its explosive dance. The man released her, and stood up... the god reached down and grabbed her by her stringy black hair, and forced her up to her knees... then released her and began to circle her, like a jaguar circling its prey, that horrible lopsided smile still on his lips. “Now... who are we?” The question caught her off-guard... her mouth worked, but no answer came out. The NVG grabbed her hair and forced her face upwards. .... 'GODD@MNIT B!TCH, WE’RE TELLING THIS NOW!!! WHEN WE ASK YOU A QUESTION, YOU BETTER D@MN WELL ANSWER!!! NOW WHO ARE WE?!! She found her voice finally... it came out as a tiny, wavering, scared thing. ...You're The Neo Void God... Aki tossed her head away as if throwing an unsavory object that dirtied her hands... causing her to nearly fall over. He began to circle round her once more, his taped hands behind his back. 'Yeah... that's right! Meaning that you... as well as your husband…are beneath us!! You are nothing more than a simpering little girl that believes that she's a force to be reckoned with simply because her husband can beat up a bunch of men who should've had 'Loser' stamped on their heads at birth! You believe that because Pharaoh’s so great... then that makes you someone of importance. You believe that he’s hard as a rock and strong as a redwood... when in reality... he’s as fragile as a soap bubble…JUST LIKE YOU! Now... how do we know all this? We'll tell you how we know: each and every time that he has been in a match... always there has been a point where he’s been laid out as easily as anyone pleases... wondering if he could go on... lying there in fear that it would all come crashing down so quickly. And every time... he’s wanted to go ahead and give in to the hurting... just lie there and give up. But all those times, we was there to pick your husband’s weak, soft, pathetic carcass off the mat so that he could continue fighting. Each and every single time... we were there... HE just didn't know!' .... 'Oh! So you didn't know? Oh, we get it... you thought that it was he who found the will to go on!! Well, we’re sorry to disappoint, child... the only thing he have a will for is taking what are undoubtedly the greatest beatings we’ve ever seen! Really... he must have the pain threshold of a godd@mned rhino!! ' She tilted Akiyama's head up towards her by the chin. 'Well?' Yes... 'Can't hear you.' Yes!! 'With conviction, Isiy!! You always try to act like you got a pair... just because your husband doesn’t have a set in his crotch! So say it like you actually do!! Say it like you're proud of it!! Repeat after us: We’ll gave the enormous, walking, talking d!ck known as Pharaoh an @$$beating that will be so d@mn bad that the crowd will go home and have nightmares about it!!! I will give the enormous, walking, talking d!ck known as Pharaoh an @$$beating that will be so d@mn bad that the crowd will go home and had nightmares about it'! As soon as the last syllable left her mouth, Akiyama's face was rocked with a vicious slap. 'WE!! WE!! Bullsh!t, you b!tch... you never did jack sh!t!! Don't lie to us like that ever again!! That night Akiyama will be to Pharaoh what Kryptonite is to Superman!!! Do you know how he will able to survive the massive beating that he will take? The reason why will shock and amaze you... but it's because it's the one match Pharaoh doesn’t have the Neo Void God to watch his scrawny back!!! That will be the match that the entire world will find out a fraction of what 'The Neo Void God is truly capable of! Do you think that he might last more than a minute against me in a match?? Yeah... that's the problem... you actually do!! You believe he can take on anyone and anything... and we are the only reason that he’s been right so far. Now... let us further clarify our differences with a question. What is the single driving force that sends him out to the ring night after night to further make an @$$ of himself? What's your motivation, so to speak?' She avoided Akiyama’s stare. It's the fans... he loves the attention... he likes to entertain them... he likes to-- The NVG shook his head as he cut her off with a wave of his hand. 'All right, that's quite enough. Shut the f*ck up, 'cuz you're really and truly about to piss us off. You have no idea how much you sound like a stripper, apparently. Let me tell you something about your beloved 'fans'. Do you realize, that for every single solitary genuine Pharaoh fan... that there are approximately eighty genuine grade-A Akiyama anti-fans? Of course you didn't... because you're blinder than Mr. Magoo on a starless, moonless night!! Look at him…he’s defeated some of the more established stars of wrestling... due mainly to us... but he defeated them nonetheless! In the minds of the fans... he’s a fallacy. An oxymoron, you see. An offense. It's not supposed to be possible for someone like him to be able to win matches against these losers…because he’s an even bigger loser... he’s not supposed to win a single championship belt. He isn’t supposed to have anything like that. And yet, somehow... the fans love him for it. Oh, you think we’re lying. Isiy... one thing that you'll find out about The NVG is that we never lie. We haven't told you anything yet that wasn't the truth... nor am I going to tell you anything that's not true. And the truth is... you've got an 80:1 ratio that says Pharaoh Punk@ss sucks the rigid c*ck of Satan more than he has a chance of defeating us!! An 80:1 ratio of hatred...but this hatred is for us…but they still are more favorable of your husband than of us! Despite the severe brain trauma caused by greasy breaded foods and high alcohol consumption…the fans still know who’s better out of the two…and that is from the very fans that Pharaoh says he loves so much and work so hard to entertain!!' Akiyama's voice was small. ...Then why is he cheered?... The NVG scoffed. 'Look at yourself, little girl. Your chest has been cheered. Your face has been cheered. Your @$$ has been cheered. Your legs and what's between them have been cheered. You, on the other hand, have always received the type of ovation that a pin drop could be heard through... but just never got a chance to hear it!!' She sniffled... which immediately caused Akiyama to kneel down and get in her face. 'Yeah... that's right... cry about it. You let loose a single clear tear and we swear we will strip you naked go to work on your backside for the rest of eternity!!! How does it feel to know that your husband’s so-called career has been a big joke? How does it feel to know that you're both the laughingstock of the wrestling world? Not too good, huh? Now... what are you going to do? You gonna cry about it... or you gonna be a big girl for once in your life and accept it... and get over it?' Of those two choices... she chose crying. She couldn't help herself... everything that she'd believed in had been violated. Great sobs wracked her body as she sunk to the ground... The Neo Void God stood over her for a while before kicking her in the side of the head, screaming. 'WEAK!!!!!' “WEAK!!!!!” “THEY’RE ALL WEAK!!”
