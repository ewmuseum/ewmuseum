He who couldn't...

8 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/10/02 I’d failed… I’d failed my Gods. I’d failed my fans. I’d failed my love. I had failed. At Red October I tried. I tried to defeat the monster. I tried with all my might to overcome Akira Akiyama… …But it was to no avail. The God defeated me instead. But one cannot relish in the past, isn’t that what they say? Well, I am not everyone else. For failing in my duty, I would suffer the consequences, and if my Gods did not lay them down before me then I’d make sure I suffered from my own self-inflicted penalty. In failing to beat Akiyama I had let down everything and everyone I loved. The fans wouldn’t look at me under the same light again, and neither would Isiy. She’d probably think I was an idiot; a stupid fool; a man who thought he could beat a God and couldn’t. And if that weren’t enough, I’d also let down the Gods of Egypt. Horus and the other deities weren’t forgiving. They would probably shun me now; ridicule me for losing to Akiyama; silently insult me from up above. I truly had failed. And for that, I would punish myself… ~**~*~**~ California slept under a blanket of darkness; night had descended upon the city and with it so had misery for one man. Titu Xansiv hadn’t slept easily tonight. Ever since he failed in his task to defeat Akiyama at Red October sleep seldom visited him anymore. He felt that everything had left his side. He wasn’t even capable of sleeping next to his wife now. He didn’t feel that he was worthy. The smell of defeat still lingered over his tanned body and the memory remained…that same o so terrible nightmare. Titu looked out over the world asleep. Why had he ever let the thought linger into his head? Why had he ever believed he could beat Akiyama again…? He felt sick. With a deep sigh, he slid from the windowsill and dropped to the floor, its cold surface awakening his mood. He had let a lot of people down, he knew that, but how could he repay them? For the past day he’d wondered how, and finally he knew what he had to do. Titu moved his weary eyes towards the table in the room’s corner. He remembered when he’d bought it and wished now that he hadn’t. His positions meant nothing to him now…he just didn’t care anymore. So much so was his entrapment in this depressing mind painful that he didn’t want to think of anything else. Akiyama…he sighed. No thought came…his misery was growing. He edged slowly towards the short table and pulled up a chair. Sitting down, he drearily picked up a long, thin pen and took to a sheet of bone white paper. What to write? He didn’t quite know the answer to that either. So many questions rampaged his head that he couldn’t think straight at all. Titu looked over at the peacefully sleeping Isiy. She had yet to notice his new habit, that being that he seldom fell under the watchful gaze of sleep. She didn’t sense that there was something wrong with him, but he knew he couldn’t put her through any more pain. He knew he had to go. And so finally gaining enough energy and inspiration to write, he set about the paper as he saw fit. ~**~*~**~ The morning sunlight swept through the open curtains, a light breeze moving the drapes to and fro poetically. The restless form of Isiy Xansiv stretched outward as her light blue orbs adjusted to the sight before her…an empty room. Edging forth from her silk white sanctuary she found no sign of her husband. Her feet touched the carpet lightly, her brow furrowing, the whereabouts of her husband flooding her brain. A thought occurred abruptly. Perhaps she’d slept in and he was downstairs? She walked out of the bedroom onto the stretched hallway. “Titu?” she shouted to the world in general, her voice full of hope. As she’d trusted was not the case, there was no answer from downstairs. She walked back into the master bedroom, still trying to wake her body from the hours of sleep behind her and peered about the empty space. It all seemed like a dream. Everyday she woke up next to Titu, and now a break in a chain of consistent events had taken place. Titu Xansiv was nowhere to be found in The Palace and Isiy was growing worried. “Titu?!” she screamed, her mind, body and soul finally awake. But her scream was in vain, for The Pharaoh was long gone… ~**~*~**~ A bronzed man wandered slowly down a winding road, his mind full of confusion, and his soul slowly contemplating the events of the past few days. Titu Xansiv wasn’t an angry man. It was true that, like everyone, he got annoyed from time to time, but unlike Xavier Kannon, Tim Walden or Violence Jack, Xansiv didn’t take out his frustrations on the weak. He wasn’t like that… he was better than that. He’d had to face a lot of hardships in his time, and through most of them he had succeeded in coming forth victorious. The pain he felt now was unknown to him. Never had he felt so emotionally distraught in the past. He felt as though he could take out his anger on a person of weakness at present, but after some reconciliation, he thought better of it. Akiyama had defeated him…there was no way of getting around a fact like that. How he wished it were him who had gone on to compete in the main event later that night, but Akiyama had defeated him… …And there was no way of getting around a fact like that… He had had to leave Isiy. There was no way on this earth that he could possibly face living with such a beauty when he was a ‘fall short’. The Pharaoh Vs Akiyama was meant to be his. That match was meant to be his time; his time to show the world that he could take things to the next level; his time to show everyone he could still beat the God. He sighed as finally he came to a stop outside a lonesome bus station. Surprisingly, there weren’t many people about - an unusual occurrence considering the time of day. He’d decided against taking the car to wherever the heck it was that he was heading. He wasn’t even worthy of driving that… At length, a bus rolled out of the sunlit valley and pulled up by his side. The automatic doors creaked open and a man peered out, his eyes reflecting the beating sun. “Where you going, bub?” he said, a toothpick hanging loosely from his bottom lip. Pharaoh stared at him, blank-faced. “Who knows where any of us are going?” he uttered, no sarcasm present in his voice whatsoever. The driver responded with a furrowed brow; he didn’t quite understand what on earth the strange looking fellow in front of him was talking about. “Just take me anywhere.” Titu uttered after a moment. ~**~*~**~ ‘Dear Isiy, I have come to a conclusion. For the past few days you may have noticed how down I’ve been… then again, maybe you haven’t. But the fact of the matter is that since the demon Akira Akiyama defeated me this past Sunday, I have not felt myself. I feel that I have let everyone down…and more importantly that I have let you down. I write this note a torn man - a man of total perplexity. I look about the world now and finally realize how much is wrong within its supposedly “safe” confines. I have no place at home now, Isiy, and so I leave you, and shan’t return until I have found my true self. I can only hope that you accept my change of character, and as abrupt as it is, I hope you can still look upon me eventually in the same light as you did before I failed… Do not think that I don’t love you anymore, for I still do with all my heart. But I am not a worthy man and I won’t be until I have discovered the real me… Obviously I’ll still be attending the shows, but don’t come to the arenas; I couldn’t bear to see you so soon…it would only break my heart further. I have let you down. I have let the fans down. And I have let my Gods down. I am a failure and I can’t accept that… I love you… Titu…’ Isiy sat looking down on the note, a tear forming in the corner of her eye. She’d found the message around an hour ago and the news had hit her hard. She couldn’t believe it…she really couldn’t. She loved Titu, and up until this moment she thought he knew that. A tear fell from her eye, blotching his words of blue below. At length she wiped her eyes and decided that the loss was the last straw for him; he’d taken it hard. And now he was gone…gone for a long time… She rose from the chair beside the table and walked into the lobby… …She continued to weep… ~**~*~**~ The TV stations were already advertising his next match. And once again it was against Xavier Kannon. The bookers just couldn’t quite get enough of the Pharaoh/Xavier saga, could they? Xansiv looked into the shop window, his face staring back from behind the glass. “And in the main event at the special edition of Turmoil held this Wednesday we’ll see a classic bout as Xavier Kannon faces off against The Pharaoh! This match is set to be a classic, folks! Don’t miss it!” said Dave Kern from a PCW studio quite possibly set up in Japan. Pharaoh looked away and sighed. He wished he’d stayed in Japan too, but he needed to see Isiy before he left for good, well…until he felt he could face her again. Now the orient beckoned him again and he needed to answer the call. He set off down the road to his left, a mind not fully settled, and a soul still very uneasy. He had to get back on track…he needed to defeat Xavier… Akiyama truly had set his emotions running wild. He felt weak…he felt depressed. He hadn’t wanted to leave Isiy. But he felt that in doing so he had made the right decision. And now he was set on a long and ever-winding road towards finding himself. It could be a long trek…he knew that already, but so much so did he feel that he needed to take this journey that he did not doubt it at all. And so the Egyptian walked onward…a nomad now…
