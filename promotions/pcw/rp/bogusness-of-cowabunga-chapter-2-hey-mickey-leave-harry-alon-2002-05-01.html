Bogusness of Cowabunga - Chapter 2 - Hey, Mickey, leave Harry ALONE!

1 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 1 [The scene opens on Disney World's coolest landmark, Epcot Center. Many children have enjoyed childhood experiences here, experiences that they will remember for a lifetime. The place where Mickey Mouse, Minnie Mouse, Donald Duck, Goofy, and so on, all live and come to life in one magical place. It's so amazing the first time you see Disney with your own eyes. It's like you've entered some sort of fantasy land just like in so many of the Disney movies that you see on the big screen] [As the crowds begin flocking in for another day of fun and excitement, two men seem noticeable and they are 'Totally Tubular' Tommy and 'Heavy Metal' Harry, the Cowabunga Connection. They both wear Mickey Mouse ear hats, actually showing a bit of pride about it. Tommy also wears a Disney World logo T-shirt, his blue and yellow swim suit, and a pair of black sandals. Harry wears his Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. These men stand out in the crowd since they aren't with any children, just with each other. Most people in the crowd walk by questioning their sexuality, but the Cowabunga Connection doesn't care one bit because this is their first visit down into Disney World. The two men enter through the main gates and capture all the beauty that is Disney World] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: It's bodacious... 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Far out....man.... [The two men are just frozen in their Mickey Mouse ear hats. All of their childhood dreams are quickly coming true. After watching all those Disney movies and always wanting to visit the Magic Kingdom, they finally have done it. Tommy stops and then looks at Harry as if realizing something] TTT: Didn't we come here when we were in NTICW, dude? [Harry stops and then looks at Tommy] Harry: Oh yeah, man. TTT: So this isn't the first time we've been here. Harry: No, man. TTT: How come we thought it was the first time we've been here, dude? Harry: Because you jobbed to Rich Cannon and blocked it out of your memory, man. TTT: I didn't job to Rich Cannon. Harry: See what I mean, man. [The two men then shrug and walk through the park. They check out all the most bodacious rides and attractions that make this park one of the best in the world] TTT: This could be the last time we hang out at Disney World as the Cowabunga Connection. Harry: Why, man? TTT: Because of that most bogus Roulette of Death match on Sunday. Nine compadres face off in a battle royal, and the winner gets to keep his job. Then the rest square off in tag team matches and the only losing dude will get fired from PCW. Isn't that totally bogus? Harry: Why would they do something like that, man? TTT: Because the Ultimate Authority is heinous is a most bogus way! Harry: Oh yeah, man. [The two men pass by the line for Splash Mountain. They go to wait and there is a sign that reads '4 Hour Wait, Sorry'. The two men look at each other with very sad eyes] TTT: This is the most bodacious ride here, Harry! I don't think I can wait four hours to experience the gnarlyness of Splash Mountain. Harry: Me either, man. [They slip out of line and continue on their way through Disney World] TTT: I was saying earlier that this could be our last times together as the Cowabunga Connection...because if one of us loses then we are gone from PCW... Harry: That's not far out, man. TTT: Yeah, and it's bogus too. Harry: Don't worry about anything, I'll make sure I carry on the legacy, man. TTT: What makes you think that I'm going to lose, dude? Harry: I don't know, man. Just seems logical. TTT: What if Darkstar loses? Harry: Then Darkstar loses, man. TTT: What if Akiyama loses? Harry: Then Akiyama loses, man. TTT: There are seven other compadres in that match, and the chances of us getting eliminated are...2 out of....um...2 out of.... Harry: Three, man? TTT: No, higher. Harry: Eleventeen, man? TTT: Is that a number? Harry: In Europe, man. TTT: Gnarly... [The two men then come to a large open area that is shaped like a gigantic circle. There are tons of games around where you can win most bodacious prizes. Many of Disney World's personalities are dancing around the circle trying to do their best to make the children happy. Harry and Tommy both look at each other with wide, happy eyes. Mickey Mouse!!! GOOFY!! Two of the most gnarly and far out characters in Disney EVER! They are upclose and personal and ready to meet and greet PCW's most bodacious tag team, the Cowabunga Connection] [The two men push children aside and knock them over to get over to their heroes. Harry stands in front of Mickey as Tommy stands in front of goofy. The two Disney Icon's stand motionless now, wondering what these two dorks want] Mickey Mouse: HELLO!!! Harry: ... Goofy: HI THERE!! TTT: ... [Mickey Mouse then pats Harry on the shoulder] Mickey: All right!! LET'S GO PLAY! [Harry looks over to Tommy and whispers] Harry: Mickey Mouse touched my shoulder, man. TTT: Radical... [Mickey and Goofy begin dancing with the kids again as Harry and Tommy still stand motionless. Harry has a bright smile on his face, getting touched by Mickey Mouse. What a feeling!! But why is he so happy that a ficitional cartoon character touched his shoulder? He really can't explain it. So, Harry walks over to Mickey and decides to take action] Harry: I've seen Fantasia over 400 times, man. [Harry begins shaking Mickey's hand, getting in the way of the gleeful children. Mickey leans in, whispering to Harry] Mickey: Your ruining it for the kids. Harry: Ruining what, man? Mickey: Their fun. Harry: What about my fun, man? I'm a paying customer. Mickey: That you are, sir, but the kids are our number one priority. You had your chance as a kid, and now it's their turn. [Harry's heart seems to be breaking] Harry: But I never got a chance as a kid, man. I only had fantasia....and forty hits of acid.... Mickey: Acid is very bad. Harry: Thanks for telling me that now, man. Mickey: No problem, now move along and let the kids play. Harry: No way, man. I'm staying here until I get my picture taken with MICKEY MOUSE!! [Tommy walks over hoping to maybe add some order to this chaos] TTT: Settle down, Harry. There is no need to be bogus about this. We'll get you one of those gnarly Mickey Mouse ice cream bars. [Harry turns to Tommy with complete rage in his eyes] Harry: I don't want F*CKING ice cream bars, MAN! I want my picture taken with MICKEY F*CKING MOUSE! Mickey: Hey!! Those kids don't need to hear that kind of language, and neither do I. [Goofy now waddles on over to see what is the matter] Goofy: What's going on? TTT: Nothing, Goofster, we were just about to bodciously get out of here. [The man behind the Goofy suit seems to know who these two men are] Goofy: HEY! I know you guys. Your the Cowabunga Connection for PCW! I've seen all of your matches and I saw all of Tommy's matches from his NTICW days. TTT: REALLY?! So how tubular are the matches? Goofy: They were horrible! I've never seen such horrible wrestling in all of my natrual living as a fan of professional wrestling. I don't even know how the hell you got a job with such a prestigious company like PCW. My right nut can wrestle better than the two of you can. It's no wonder that your put in a match where your jobs are on the line...hehe. If I were PCW I'd just let everyone in that battle royal beat the living crap out of you, and then fire your lame asses. Mickey: Now that's the best idea I've heard all day! [Harry and Tommy look at each other, then look at Mickey and Goofy, and then once again look at each other. They give each other a nod and begin to walk away. It seems that they'll take their tongue lashing and go home broken men. Goofy gives a high five to Mickey, celebrating his great use of words. Just then Tommy turns around and nails Goofy with a stiff kick to the neck, which would be the person's face underneath the large mask] TTT: Now who sucks, dude! [Harry leaps on Mickey and begins driving elbows into his chest. The children all around begins crying and screaming to their parents. They can't believe that these two men are beating up Mickey and GOOFY!] Harry: NOW YOU WILL DIE BY MY HANDS, MAN!!!! TTT: Chill out, dude. Harry: I AM THE REAPER OF MOUSE SOULS AND YOU ARE MINE, MAN!!! TTT: Your putting a bummer on our ass kicking, dude. Harry: YOU WILL BOW BEFORE ME AS A MOUSEY PEASANT AND GIVE YOUR WIFE TO ME, MAN!!! TTT: Dude... Harry: I WILL RAPE HER AND SHE WILL BEAR MY CHILD, MAN!!! TTT: That's gross, compadre. Harry: IT WILL BE THE FIRST HALF MOUSE HALF HUMAN AND WE WILL RULE SUPREME, MAN!!! TTT: STOP IT, DUDE! Harry: .... [He gets off of Mickey Mouse and looks down at his hands, and then down at Mickey Mouse] Harry: Sorry, man. TTT: Let's get out of here before security arrives. Harry: Far out, man. [The scene then fades to black]
