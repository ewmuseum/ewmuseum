Albino Power

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17 Feb &#039;03 The scene opens up with Chet King sitting where the audience would sit if an episode of the Chat King Wrestling Show was on the air. Next to Chet is one of the producers of the show, dressed in Armani suit, just like Chet is. Chet King: Who is it you want me to interview on the next episode of the show? Producer: Well it isn't anyone involved in the wrestling business, but he is a very interesting person that could create some big ratings if he were to appear on your show. Chet King: That's a bit of a problem then. Considering my show is supposed to be about wrestling! That's what makes it different from other Jerry Springer and Ricky Lake type talk shows. Producer: I know, I know. But this guy could draw big ratings if he were to appear. Chet King: I don't care. I'm only have people on my show if they're involved in the wrestling business. I don't mind if they're wrestlers, referee's, ring maintenance guys, popcorn vendors at wrestling arenas or even just fans of wrestling. Is this guy a fan? Because if he is then I'll consider it. Producer: No, he doesn't watch wrestling. Chet King: Then I don't want to hear any more about the issue. Producer: Come on Chet, just give it a chance. Meet the guy and then decide. Chet King: No, it's not happening. Producer: Let me just tell you about him, then you can decide to meet him as he's in the back some where at the moment, then you can decide if you want him on the show. Chet King: Well... Producer: Come on Chet, let me tell you about him. Chet King: Alright, tell me about him. Producer: Well his name is Red Whit, and he's the leader of the Albino Power group. Chet, naturally, seems a bit shocked by this statement. Chet King: Excuse me? Producer: He's the leader of the Albino Power group. It's a radical extremist group who believe the only people who should live on the planet are albino's. Chet King: So this guy won't like me because I'm not an albino? Producer: He'll hate you! He hate's white's, black's, Asian's, Middle Easterners, Pacific Islanders and everyone else, unless you're an albino. Chet King: He sounds interesting, I'll meet him. Producer: Excellent, you'll love this guy. Chet King: Why would I love an extremist who doesn't like me? Producer: Because he's a funny, crazy, nutty guy! Chet King: I don't find any extremist organizations funny, even if they are an Albino extremist group. Producer: Alright, well let me bring him out. The producer leaves the studio and goes backstage, a minute later he brings out a guy with white hair, paper white skin and red eyes. The guy is wearing white pants, and a white t-shirt, with red letters on it that say 'Albino Power'. The producer leads him over to the area where Chet King is sitting. Producer: Guess who this is Chet. Chet King: Uh, Red Whit. Producer: How'd you know? Chet King: Because he's the only albino I've seen in the last five years. Red Whit doesn't seem happy about this comment from Chet King as he sits down next to Chet King and shakes his hand in an aggressive manner. Red Whit: Good to meet you Chet King. Let me just tell you that I'm not happy at all about your previous statement. It just makes me sick that you and the rest of your people think you can treat us albino's like that. Chet King: Good to meet you too Red, and I'm sorry about that comment. I didn't mean any harm by it. Red Whit: Sorry's not going to cut it Chet. You and your people are always holding the Albino man down. But no longer will we stand for it. We've been held down for too long, we're not going to fight for the Albino people, because we are superior. Chet King: What do you mean by fighting? Physical or mental fighting? Red Whit: I want to get on your show so I can spread the word about the Albino people ready to rise up and take the earth. We will first use our mental ability to rise up and take over the world by appearing on shows like yours and Maury Povitch's. Then if the case need be we will use force to capture the world. Chet King: Do you have the power to do that? Red Whit: Indeed we do. We've managed to frame Saddam Hussein and the Iraq government. We've got George W. Bush and the American government believing that it is the Iraqi's who are stock piling weapons. But in reality it is us, the Albino's! Chet King: How've you been managing to get away with it? Red Whit: While the Americans have been paying all their attention to Iraq, we've managed to sneak weapons into an abandoned XFL stadium. Heck, when the XFL was still operating we were sneaking weapons in, it didn't matter, no one turned up to the stadium. Chet King: I'm sorry Red, I'm sure your ideas are very interesting, but I don't think I want a radical extremist group leader on my show. Red Whit: Are you saying what I think you’re saying? Chet King: If you think I’m saying I don’t want you on my show, then you’re right. Red Whit: You know what I can do to you? One phone call and I’ll have more then a hundred albino’s here ready to beat you down to a bloody pulp. Chet looks worried. Chet King: Well, what if I were to cut you a deal? Red Whit: What sort of deal? Chet King: You won’t appear on the show, but you won’t get your extremists to beat me up either. Red Whit: What do you propose? Chet King: I’ll tell you the name of a person who’s been making derogatory comments about Albino’s, and in fact has told me personally that he hates Albino’s. Red seems furious about this. Red Whit: Who is this person? Chet King: If I tell you, do you promise you won’t set your followers on me? Red Whit: I promise, we won’t have time anyway, we’ll be spending our time trying to find this person and let them know about Albino power! Chet King: Alright, well his name is Gaz. Red White: Gaz? Where is he? Chet King: I’m not sure, but no doubt he’s out insulting Albino’s. Red Whit leaps to his feet and runs out of the studio yelling as he leaves. Red Whit: I’m going to hunt down this bastard! As Red leaves Chet laughs and looks at his producer. Chet King: Heh, now that’s a prank! Poor Gaz, he’s going to be taken out by Albino extremists before we even get to fight at Turmoil. Producer: Well I doubt that will happen. Chet King: Why’s that? Producer: Well Red didn’t even bother to find out what Gaz’s last name was. There’s got to be thousands of Gaz’s out there. Chet King: Hmm, he’s not that smart of a guy is he for an extremist leader, is he? Producer: But he is dangerous, afterall he appears to have planted an explosive device on that chair he was sitting on. So I suggest you run out of here like I’m going to. Chet and the producer get up and leave, fearing the worst about the red tube type thing left on the seat by Albino extremist leader, Red Whit. Oh don’t worry, it wasn’t really a bomb, it was just a tube with some posters inside it. The posters were propaganda for Albino Power and looked completely plain with nothing on except for the words Albino Power written in red letters, but that’s only if you weren’t looking closely. For if you were looking closely you would of seen, that on top of the white background there was a picture of an Albino’s face.
