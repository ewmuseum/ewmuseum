Feeding the fire, and letting the fumes fly sky high.

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-11-02 --- The segment begins with Hank and Ganker shown in Ray Lota's Seattle Bar at the counter drinking a bottle of Corona. Hank is wearing some black jeans, black Nike basketball t-shirt, and a long black leather trench coat. Ganker is wearing a blue jeans, plain black t-shirt, and a black leather jacket. Hank then looks to his left after swigging his Corona and spots a Red Rock poster hanging up. Hank grins as he turns back at Ray who has a dart laying in his hand. Hank grabs the dart and throws it and nails the poster where Red's eye is. Hank laughs as Ganker starts to edge away, raising one eyebrow at Hank in the process. Hank then grabs another dart and aims for Ganker's nostril as Ganker comes up and grabs the dart and tosses it at the Red Rock poster, nailing it right in the kneecap of Red Rock. Ganker laughs as he sips his Corona. They both take their seats back at the bar.Hank: 'So Ray, yo hear bout our match this Friday against Metallyx?'Ray: 'Yeah, I hear bout your match boys, and I'm proud of the boths of yous. Always knew those beatings I gave you in my backyard wrestling ring would payoff someday. Never knew they'd get you shots at PCW titles, let alone PCW contracts. I'm proud of yous two and just to prove it, free beer for the rest of the week.'Hank: 'We get free beer from yo anyway man?'Ray: 'Oh yeah, must have slipped my minds, I'm used to paying customers coming into my humble pub I paid all of two dollars for. Who are the members to this tag team you're facing?'Hank: 'Uh, erm... Red Rock and... Uh help me out here Gank.'Ganker: 'Hell like I know, uh D-Kay Calyx?'Hank: 'Close enough boyo.'Ray: 'Red Rock, the man sounds familiar, Brendon Red Rock?'Hank: 'Sure... Uh, whatever.'Ganker: 'Jus' for yo bro, I'll leave Red Rock to yo and I deal wit' D-Cal.'Hank: 'Thanks man, I always wanted to get my hands on Red Rock since our last match, when I was so screwed out of that match. Clyde, cost me that damn match, but I don't care bout that psychotic freak, I care bout how Red Rock took advantage of the situation and go the cheap victory. Now times for me to even the score and come out on top as the best between myself and Red Rock. The score ain't settled until I say it is, and to me, that's never!'Ray: 'If only I Ray Lota were in a championship ring like yous two boys, I'd show the world what The Bounce is all about. I've always wanted to show, but never got the chance, so do me a favor and bring this victory home and win it for me, and all your Seattle Fans.'
