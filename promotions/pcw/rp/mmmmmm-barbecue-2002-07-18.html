Mmmmmm...Barbecue.

18 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/18/02 Tuesday Turmoil RP #2. To say that I used to be a workhorse is an understatement. I remember when I used to break my back to impress the PCW higher ups, I would get into the gym everyday that I had the time to, and working out. I worked out with weight training, and I worked out in the ring. That was my attitude ever since I stepped into a PCW ring. I always thought of the place as a sacred sanctuary where only the best were allowed. But it was soon revealed that that was far from the case. PCW rings were infested with a bunch of white guys, who had little to no talent, and were only hired because another white guy, felt like giving one of his buddies a job. Case in point, BloodAngel. There is no way in hell he made PCW because of his talent. I used to be buddies with Ryan Stephens when we were in the Malice Crew together, and he always told me that if BloodAngel decided to bring his A game to the ring, he could defeat any PCW wrestler. ANY PCW wrestler. ANY DAMN PCW WRESTLER!! Heh, what a waste. All BloodAngel did was hang around here, and collect a paycheck, and what the hell, why shouldn’t I do that? Seriously. Screw training. Screw putting in the extra few hours in the workout regiment because you think that you have a better chance of winning. If PCW has taught me anything, it’s that the best wrestler doesn’t always win. For instance, if you can remember back to the PCW’s last Pay Per View, Gold and Glory. I finally got my Television Title shot with John Crichton. It was obvious that John Crichton hadn’t brought his A game. His physique was laughable, and his mind didn’t seem to be on the match. Instead, he was worried about his sexual escapades with some guy named Fleming and Ms. Zemoff. I had the title within my grasp, and just as I was about to become the first black PCW Television Champion, disaster struck. Jaden, a two bit, stanky booty ho ass, man looking whore, decided that she/he wanted to get his/her stupid ass on the apron. While I was yelling at that ho to get off the apron, Crichton takes advantage, and gets the pin. I remember every time I went somewhere, a public appearance on the radio, walking down the street, or just whenever I talked to a wrestling fan, they would always say “Yeah man, you got screwed,” as if I already didn’t know. It’s not to say that I won’t train and get in shape for matches, especially now with this injury, basically what it means is that I’m going to spend more time chillin’ away from the ring. I am making a pact, and this includes Pay Per Views, no more than 3 days of training. Keep in mind that training for me is like an hour and a half. So for ever match, three days is my limit, because I’m not breaking my back to get screwed again. But I don’t like to complain about wrestling like that, I usually keep my complaints with N.I.G.G.A and The Uprising. I’ll tell them what happened, but I won’t tell the public, because I wouldn’t want to expose the WHITE charade of a company that the PCW is. That just goes to show that talent doesn’t decide matches anymore. Because PCW officials could have easily shown a replay of what happened, and ordered a restart. Sure it’s irregular in sports now a days, but GOD DAMN IT, THIS WAS A FUCKING TITLE SHOT!! MY TITLE SHOT!! When I say ‘Kill Whitey’ now, I mean it. That’s the message I want to send to all the youth of the world; if the white man is screwing you in the ass without KY Jelly, like is happening to me now, make sure that they remember your name. Make sure that you take ‘em out. (The following break in has been brought to you by Whitey: Kill him.) *** The scene opens up in the plush mansion of Jordan X Shabazz. His hard work, and his putting N.I.G.G.A on the New York Stock Exchange, pays for this home. The place is loaded. DVD player, wide screen TV, leather couches, PlayStation 2 (along with other game consoles and video games), and even a portable fridge, adorn Shabazz’s living room. We now go to the vast backyard of Jordan X Shabazz. We see the pool, and some women in the pool playing around, some of them appear to be rather young. We see Jordan dressed in his normal dress pants and dark shoes, but this time he wears no blazer to top it off, today he wears a blue dress shirt, which is tucked into his pants to give off the look of a smart man. Jordan’s thick rimmed glasses were on his face, and his hair was picked out in the form of a small afro. He sits in a lawn chair, surveying the action. To his left sits Rush Mason, not dressed as heavily as Jordan X Shabazz, but dressed in baggy North Carolina shorts, some baby blue Nike cross trainers, and a white Reebok shirt. His nappy hair is unbrushed, and he doesn’t appear to care much. Jordan leans to his right side and pulls out a novel by John Grisham called ‘The Brethren.’ After a few minutes of reading intently, Jordan calls out. “HEY, THE BARBECUE IS DONE!!” Jordan was calling to his two ‘butlers’ (Read: slaves) that worked for him. He paid them each about twenty dollars a day, and they were on call every minute and second of the day. They could work 24 hours and make only 20 bucks, which is below minimum wage, but when Shabazz hired them, he knew what he was getting into. Two intelligent runaways who needed work. Steve Bird and Scott Armstrong. Scott was the one who came outside in a hurry, he went towards the barbecue where Jordan and Rush’s food was being cooked. As their food was being prepared Jordan and Rush decided to have a little conversation about their opponents on the upcoming episode of PCW Tuesday Turmoil. Rush Mason: “They’re an odd couple, but they both have beefs with us. Jordan X Shabazz and Mark Makonie go way back. Their feud in the NTICW was the stuff of legends. Shabazz and Makonie went at it in a variety of different gimmick matches, each hurting the other, and fighting for their race. In Shabazz’s last NTICW match, he defeated Mark Makonie and won the flag match. But in PCW, when Shabazz and Makonie stepped into the ring and had a match, it was Makonie who came out on top, albeit with the use of a weapon, he holds a definite victory over Jordan X Shabazz. Rush Mason and Duzza have a different story. It wasn’t too long ago that Rush and Duzza were good friends. But with the addition of Thug roC to the PCW, Rush changed. He grew a hatred for the man that he once supported. And the whole thing escalated when Rush, along with the rest of the Uprising, poured a bucket of white paint onto the man known as DUZZER! Now the feuds that have sort of been put on the backburner are coming to a head, Jordan X Shabazz-Rush Mason-Mark Makonie-Duzza. Jordan X Shabazz: “Screw them, they can’t work together.” Can Duzza and Mark Makonie work together despite the fact that Duzza’s father was killed by skinheads? Can Mark Makonie and Duzza work together despite the fact that Mark Makonie loathes every race that isn’t his own? Will Duzza and Mark Makonie fall victim to The Uprising? We’ll have to wait and see…
