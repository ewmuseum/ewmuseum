Attack of the Preps!

16 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/16/02 Tick…Tock…Tick…Tock… The time is ticking down to a match that could have a huge impact on Misplaced’s career. They have struggled in the beginning of their career in PCW. That was a while ago. They have yet to become successful. It’s rather disappointing considering that they were once one of the most hyped up tag teams. Once. Now they’re considered the jobber tag team. They’re that team that you toss in the match just to have them there for the sake of it. That’s all that they’re seen as right now. This upcoming match on Fusion could decide a lot. As of late Carey J. Nowski has been acting strange towards the public and towards his teammates. He’s not the bashful Carey that everyone once knew. He doesn’t seem to be as much of a coward. The man can now go out in public and speak his mind. That may not be a good thing for him. He carries many odd thoughts through his mind, and some people will just blow him off when they hear of them. Respect is something that Misplaced could never gather. They still aren’t pulling it in. Not that they have deserved it, but it’s not coming. Meanwhile, we have this Misplaced tag team, truly Misplaced. Carey has been acting strange as of late, and Stephen seems to be the saner one of the two. I don’t think either member of the team has an idea what is going on. Tomorrow night on Friday Fusion they face the Pop group of PCW, The Pretty Cool Guys. They have met before in the ring, and Misplaced has never come up successful. They’re looking to change that this week. Carey: “We absolutely must win tomorrow! If not, I think we can safely say that this team isn’t working.” Carey’s voice can be heard, but nothing can be seen. After a while we can finally see the duo walking through what appears to be a park in Oklahoma. This is of course where Fusion will be held this week. The day is sunny, the wind is blowing, and the people are enjoying the park. Children are running around and playing on playgrounds. Adults are sitting on benches with there significant other. Then you have Misplaced walking through the park on a dirt path. It’s such a peaceful scene. Carey and Stephen finally get tired of walking. They find a nearby bench, which is located by a playground full of screaming children. It sounds like they walked into Pee Wee’s Playhouse. As they’re sitting they notice a group of teenage girls talking about The Pretty Cool Guys. One is a blonde girl, the other a brunette, and the other has red hair. Preppy Blonde Girl: “Those Pretty Cool Guys are like so hot! There voices are totally sexy! I wish I could get a piece of them!” The two can’t help but chuckle at these young teenage girls talking about adults in such a way. They move to a bench a little closer to the girls. Preppy Red Head Girl: “I know! They’re like so…like smooth! That Rich Sherwin…oh my! You wouldn’t believe how cute he is! Oh my god!” The girls chuckle, and so does Misplaced. Preppy Brunette Girl: “Plus they’re athletes, so they have killer bodies! You wouldn’t believe it! I saw Rich Sherwin in a magazine before with a swimsuit on, and oh my god! He’s so sexy!” Stephen gets up out of his seat to go talk to the girls. Stephen: “Hey you girls! Who is this that you’re talking about? They sound like quite a group.” The girls jaws all drop in amazement. I wonder why? It takes them a while to gain there speech back to respond. Preppy Red Head Girl: “Are you serious? You’ve never heard of The Pretty Cool Guys? They’re like so totally the best group around! Plus they wrestle, so they have killer bods’!” Stephen plays along with the girl. He just keeps nodding his head and smiling as the girls whisper among themselves. Stephen: “So these guys even wrestle?” Preppy Brunette Girl: “Oh my god! Yes! They’re the reason I watch that wrestling thing! Who cares about the matches? I just love the tight suits!” Stephen chuckles again, and so do the rest of the girls. Preppy Blonde Girl: “So…are you…” The girl pauses a moment to get her question out. Stephen appears to be expecting the usual “Are you those wrestler guys on TV?” question. Stephen: “Yes? Am I…?” Preppy Blonde Girl: “Are you like gay or something? I mean you’re here with a guy, and you’re talking to us about two sexy guys.” Once again Stephen can’t help but laugh at the girls. Stephen: “That’s a good one. No, I am not gay. Neither is my partner over there. You see, we’re wrestlers. We’re going to fight The Pretty Cool Guys soon.” The girls all go silent. They look back and forth to each other pondering over what to say. Preppy Red Head Girl: “Oh…my…god! You’re going to touch my beauty Rich is his special spots! You’re so lucky! For you being gay you’re getting the best!” Stephen: “Damn you little preppy whore to hell! I’m not gay!” Preppy Blonde Girl: “Chill out dude. You’re the one who came to us. We can’t help it that Rich Sherwin and that other guy are so sexy! Just look at them!” The blonde girl holds up a CD with The Pretty Cool Guys on the front of it. Stephen looks at it for a moment. Stephen: “May I see it please?” The blonde girl nods, and hands the CD to Stephen. Stephen looks at the case for a moment, and he then takes the CD out of the case. Suddenly a grin appears on his face. He takes the CD and breaks it in two halves. After that he hurls the two pieces as far as he can. The CD goes flying into nowhere. The three girls get upset looks on there faces. Preppy Brunette Girl: “That was so un-cool! I just bought that CD yesterday! You’re like a total jerk!” The brunette girl grabs her purse and starts to walk away. The other girls follow her, but Stephen just stays for a moment. He’s done on one knee figuring that these preppy girls are gone, but suddenly he feels a whack to the back of his head. A rock comes rolling in front of him. He’s being ambushed by a group of teenage girls! The blonde girl runs and smacks him with her purse, and the red head follows with the same attack. Stephen grabs both of there purses and stands up. Stephen: “Back off now or I hurl the purses into the air! The two preppy girls slowly back off, and Stephen gets a grin of success on his face. He has defeated the preps, or so he thinks! Suddenly he feels a kick to the back of his leg, which causes him to stumble. He turns around to see the brunette girl in position to smack with the purse. WHHHHHHHHHHHHACK Stephen takes a devastating blow to the face from a purse. He grasps his nose in pain. Meanwhile, Carey finally takes off his music headset to notice Stephen being attacked by the preps. He walks behind the blonde and the red head slowly. He’s trying to be as silent as he can to grab the purses, and he does so. Carey: “What’s going on here?” The girls all turn towards Carey, and they all chuckle at the conspiracy nerd. Preppy Blonde Girl: “This jerk was messing with us, so we taught him a lesson! He like didn’t like The Pretty Cool Guys and he broke our CD!” Stephen: “They jumped me! It’s all their fault!” Stephen walks over to Carey and grabs the purses out of his hands. He takes one purse and hurls it into the thick wooded area to the side of the. The other purse goes hurling into the playground somewhere. Stephen: “My work here is done! Lets go Carey!” Stephen and Carey walk down the path to a bench further from the girls. The girls are all screaming obscenities to Stephen, and they keep saying The Pretty Cool Guys rock. Stephen finally ignores them and they sit down on a bench with the girls out of sight. Carey: “That had to be the most pitiful thing I’ve ever seen. You were just jumped by three teenage girls! They looked like they’re each thirteen!” Stephen: “Hey! They ambushed me! There’s nothing I could do about it. Besides, they didn’t really hurt me. One got a lucky shot with their purse to my face. That’s about it!” Carey: “I repeat…that had to be the most pitiful thing I’ve ever seen!” Carey laughs to himself as he continues to get his insults in on Stephen. Carey: “We should have a really tough time winning if you can’t take the preps!” Stephen: “Do…ah…do you think I’d really fight them? They’re just little girls!” Carey just busts out laughing. This is unusual from the man who’s usually the tortured one. He has that look of sweet insulting revenge in his eyes. Carey: “So they can just torture you? That’s hilarious! Finally I have something to gauge at you about! Yes!” Stephen: “You need a life Carey. You really do.” Carey: “Silence devil man! At least my life doesn’t involve being beat by teenage girls! It’ll be scary to see what grown men can do to you!” Stephen: “It was three on one! That wasn’t a fair match! Also, what happened to Mr. I hate the world?” Carey goes silent for a moment, and his face gets more serious. Carey: “Well…” He tries to keep a straight face, but he starts to laugh again. Carey: “Well, I’m still out to prove the whole world wrong. That’ll come in time. This opportunity is just too grand to waste though! I must keep laughing at you.” The two just stay on that bench for a while. Carey continues laughing, but Stephen starts to tug at his hair. Yes, this is that Misplaced team. So soon they have a match against The Pretty Cool Guys. As said before this match could decide their fate. People can only hope that Carey doesn’t snap in his match trying to spread the truth. You can also only hope that Stephen actually fights back against these upcoming preps.
