In Search of a Joblin...Part 1.

28 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-28-2002 Belle’s Tavern was, in appearance, your ordinary bar. Waitresses lugged huge mugs of ale to patrons who were a tad too friendly with both their gold and with their wandering hands. This, of course, resulted in a number of faces being slapped; on the other hand, it also made for some batting of eyelashes and heavy flirting on the part of the waitresses. The thing that separated this tavern from the rest of the taverns in Falmdora was that this was THE place that one would go if he were looking for information; doesn’t matter what kind of information either. The people in here have ears everywhere. You could find out as much as you could want about the local government officials as, say, a local thief. This was a place full of unsavory characters, and they DO make the best stoolies. Tonight, the tavern is packed to capacity, and the waitresses really have to hump it just to keep up with the customers demands for “MORE ALE!! MORE WINE!!! The money flows freely from hands that just stole the coin this night and from those who may actually have earned their gold. A raucous band made up of a flutist, a harpist and a crude looking string instrument bang out cheery tunes that keep the tavern’s patrons lively and jovial. There is not a somber, or sober fellow in the place, save one. In the far corner sits a large man in a black cloak. Venneman Pendragon. His hood is pushed back to reveal his noble features and somewhat insane eyes. He sits with a goblet of wine in front of him, watching everything that happens in the place. He smiles as a pretty waitress bounds over to him, eyes fluttering and hips swaying… Waitress: Can I get ya any more wine, guv’na? Or might I interest you in something else? Pendragon looks her over quite favorably before he replies… Venneman: Indeed I shall have more wine, and I also seek two things. A room and also information. Ye dost have rooms available, do ye not? Her eyes light up when she hears that Pendragon will indeed be spending the night in Belle’s Tavern… Waitress: Aye, course we have rooms. You just wait here and I’ll fetch your wine and find out which room you can have for the night…Or will it be for longer? Pendragon smiles and leans back in his chair… Venneman: Now, I should guess that that remains to be seen. Could be a night, could be five nights. Depends on how fruitful my business is here in Falmdora… With that, she rushes off with a huge smile upon her face. Pendragon sighs as he watches her go… “Falmdora. I know little of the border town that lies in the outmost reaches of the kingdom of Gauze. I have been under the false assumption that Gauze was all encompassing of the land, but now I know differently. When I came back here, I found mine army scattered and disbanded. My captains have gone into hiding, and the general consensus is that I died in the prison of Lyco the Joblin. At least, that is the rumor that has been spread. I hoped to gather answers here, in the trading town of Falmdora. Falmdora; home of traders and thieves. Both people were valuable when it came to information. Who travels and speaks to more people than a trader, and who would keep their ears to the ground for any important information more so than a thief? I could think of none.” The waitress saunters back over to his table in the corner with a full goblet of wine. She smiles as she places the goblet on the table and hands Venneman a folded piece of paper. Venneman places the paper on the table without looking at it. He pulls out a fat gold coin and flips it to the waitress… Venneman: What is thy name, lass? Waitress: ‘tis Jenna, my Lord… Venneman: I need something, Jenna…I need you to send me someone who knows things. Someone who travels and keeps an ear to the ground. These are fell times in the Land, and it would pay well to deliver information to the needy. Jenna: Hmm…I believe I know just the man. He is a tad uncivilized, but he does seem to have a finger in everything that goes on. I’ll send him over…Umm…Might I know your name, good sir? Venneman: ‘Tis Roland, my lass. Roland Stern, from in-country. She runs off once more, and Pendragon unfolds the paper. One word is written in an unsure hand…”MIDNIGHT.” He smiles as he tucks the paper into his cloak and waits for the man that Jenna has gone in search of… “ I hated to use another name, but twas not the safest place for me to be in the Land once more. And the name I chose is noble in its own right. Perhaps this place would be more than I had hoped. Someone who might tell me that which I need to know, and Jenna could probably provide some much needed diversion for me. All in all, it was turning out to be a good night…” A fierce looking man in leather armor strides to Pendragon’s table and looks confidently at him. Hard blue eyes and a jet black mane set this fellow apart from the other patrons in the tavern, and he has the untamed look of the wilds. He was not a man to be trifled with. Venneman liked him instantly… Man: I am Kona. I hear you seek information. Venneman: Indeed I do. Please, have a seat, Kona. Allow me to introduce myself….I am Roland Stern, of the Gilden-Lands. Kona: Well met we are, Roland of Gilden. Though I must say you bear a resemblance to one I have only heard stories about. He would be too intelligent to return to this land, though, when so many seek his head. Venneman: Aye. I am sure that he is, whoever it is the man you speak of. Kona takes a seat opposite of Pendragon and waves his hand at Jenna, a gesture that surely is a request for more ale… “The man knows me for who I truly am. Indeed, this lad was a rare find. Just enough civility in him to be able to function in society, but enough of the wild to make him fierce. He and I were a lot alike.” Venneman: I hear that thou art a worldly man who might possess information about a man that I seek. Kona: I have been known to know a thing or two now and then… Venneman: I seek a man by the name of Lyco. He holds the appearance of a mere child, but he is truly more than his appearance would belie. Kona: I have heard of the man. He is known by many names, but I know him. Lyco the Cruel. He is a killer of children and a defiler of women. Though I am a barbarian of the wilds, even I do not partake of such things as that vermin. He is also high in the ranks of the army of Crazier Dannon. Not a man to trifle with…Why do ye seek the monster? Venneman: He…Owes me. I intend to collect. Kona: Indeed. I thought it would be for something that the creature would not like. Very well. From what I heard, he was last seen courting a beautiful woman, though none could tell if she were a willing target. He is in the northlands right now, and he was last seen in the city of Domfilla. Nice town, lots of work for a man in my field. That though, is all I know. Venneman nods and pulls out a leather pouch. He tosses it to Kona and he catches it with a clink… Kona: Thank you, good sir. If you need me for anything else, I am Kona. People know me. Just ask. Venneman: Do not speak so soon. I may be in the market for someone with thine obvious…Talents. Kona smiles and stands, walks away. Pendragon downs his goblet of wine and pulls out an ornate golden pocket watch… “It was already near midnight, and I had something that I would enjoy waiting on me. I know that Jenna will not disappoint. I only hope that she is able to keep up. ‘Tis been some time since I allowed myself the pleasure of a woman. I needed not the weakness involved. This though, would satisfy a need of the flesh, not of the heart. I was also sure that I would indeed call upon Kona again. I needed an extra sword if I was going to accomplish what I was setting out to do. It seems that Lyco always has some kind of help, and I needed the odds to be in my favor. This was delicate business, and I needed to take after him in a controlled and planned manner. But, as for now, duty calls to me…And what a duty it shall be!” Pendragon chuckles as he stands and heads for a stairway where waits the beautiful Jenna. She walks ahead of him as they disappear into the darkness of the hall…. >>>TO BE CONTINUED
