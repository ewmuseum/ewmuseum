Multiplicity.

Date: not entered “Halt, wretched villain, or dare taste the two-fisted justice of Big Alan Steele!! Hi-Keeba!!” We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he bursts noisily through a wooden door, splintering it into toothpicks. A grinning man with a bald head and gleaming golden quasi-futuristic clothing, turns his head, stooped over the controls of a technological wonder – a gate standing seven feet high with a bluish, swirling vortex of suspended seemingly out of nowhere. The crazed man’s right index finger hovers threateningly over a large green button as he throws his head back in diabolical laughter. “Fool! But you are too late to stop Chronos, MASTER OF ALL TIME!!!” “Your time is short, murderous scum, and growing shorter by the minute! This I swear!” “Didn’t your father Zeus ever teach you not to swear?” grins Chronos as his finger trails circles around the button, “Manners aside, you are just in time to witness my ultimate victory, Big Alan STOOGE!! Behold, as I activate my Time Portal! My time grows short? My dear, misguided Greek god, my time is only beginning!!” Big Alan begins to stalk forward, growling, “You and your devices pose little threat to me, as I have proven time and time again!” “It is true,” admits Chronos, “That it was you that first placed me in Arkham. It was you that brought my criminal empire to its knees the first time. And it is also true that you have worked your way through my most recent labyrinth, unraveling my latest plot to gain control of the city… but you underestimate me! It’s funny you should mention ‘time and time again’, because THAT is JUST what is about to happen!!” Chronos cackles with insane laughter as he slams his fist down on the green button. A rainbow of colors strobe in the vortex, as light dances through the backroom of the lair. Steele leaps, arms outstretched… GRZZZZZT!!! Steele roars in surprise and pain as a beam of light envelopes him from the rear, catching him in mid-leap and holding him suspended in midair. Chronos twirls and dances in glee… as Chronos walks out from behind Big Alan Steele, stasis gun in hand? … the Hell?! The second Chronos walks over to the first and they exchange high fives, doing a little victory celebration. The first bows grandly to Big Al, a smile firmly etched onto his face ear-to-ear. “Well, I do believe you have lost to the better armed man in this battle of wits, as you hover before me, trapped in your moment in time by my Stasis Beam! You can talk, Mr. Steele, so please, tell me how it is to fail so utterly miserably?!” Steele’s eyes move about furiously, assessing the grimness of the situation, “Diabolical!” “Indeed it is!” agrees the second Chronos, “You see, this was not a plan spaced out over moments… this was plotted over DECADES!! Take a close look at me, Steele…” Big Al does as bidden, taking in the features of the second Chronos. A slow dawning of realization comes across his features… “You… you are OLDER!!” “He can be taught!” laughs the first Chronos. “Yes, Steele, I AM older. Twenty years, in fact. You see, our first meeting here at this spot went rather poorly. You were the victor in my timeline, putting me in Arkham for twenty, long suffering years. Each day I would sit in my cell, listen to the screams echo down the halls and smile… smile knowing that revenge would be sweet in coming. Once released, I immediately went to my Time Machine and punched in the coordinates for this very moment. I knew you could defeat me… but could you defeat TWO of me?” “BWAHAHAHAHAHA!!!” screams the first Chronos, “Sweet INDEED!!” Big Alan Steele tests his body, but cannot move in his time-space prison cell, “Fiends!! Then the green button…” “Oh, that. Just some special effects I whipped up. I’ve actually been here the whole time, waiting for the right moment,” says the second, older Chronos. “He, I mean I, told myself exactly EVERYTHING that would happen here today!” “NOT SO FAST, Hades-spawned demons!” The two Chronos’ turn in unison, gasping at the sight before them… ANOTHER Big Alan Steele walking haughtily through the door. He guffaws loudly as he walks right before the two dumbfounded masters of time and grabs the Stasis Gun out of the second Chrono’s hands. This Big Al grunts briefly, as he snaps the weaponry in half, the original Big Alan Steele tumbling out of his cell made from time and space. The first Chronos is stunned, “Inconceivable!!” The first Big Al quickly stands and looks on with a rather dumbfounded look. Soon enough, though, realization hits and he and the second Big Al clasp hands and forearms. “Brother in arms!!” “Actually, I am you… from the future!!” “Hold it!” cries out the second Chronos, scratching his head, “What the Hell happened here?” “Tis quite simple,” begins the second Steele, “For a span of fifteen years I was trapped in yon containment cell, placed on display for all your cohorts and vile evil friends!!” “I bet you looked GREAT as a centerpiece…” muses the first Chronos. “Indeed I did, but the scorn and ridicule of a criminal underworld so willing to see me in such a deplorable condition only fueled a vengeance that only now becomes sated!!” The second Chronos ponders aloud, “But how? How did you escape the separated layer of time and space that I created for you?” “How did I, indeed?” mocks the second Big Al, a sly grin. The second Chronos continues his deep thought… and finally comes to a decision. Shock and horror crosses his face as he smacks the first Chronos upside the head. “Ouch!! What did you do THAT for?!” “Fool! You’ve doomed us both!! Your power cels in this time period have a maximum life of only fifteen years!! YOU FORGOT TO CHANGE THE BATTERIES!!” “I didn’t do ANYTHING yet!!” The two Big Alan Steele’s look on in sheer amusement at the squabbling Chronos’ as they crack their knuckles in anticipation of a fight. “You idiot! In my time my cels last for EONS!!” “But… I haven’t done anything yet!” “Enough!!” booms the first Big Al, startling the two time masters, “Your chicanery has gone on long enough! Prepare for severe retribution…” “FREEZE!!” screams Chronos, a new Chronos, popping in through the doorway, “Up against the wall, both of you! I’m not afraid to use this time disintegrator, capable of separating your very atoms into individual time dimensions!!” “Omigod!” cries out the first Chronos, “What happened to you?!” The latest Chronos reaches up and wipes the blood running down his chin. His face is bruised, his left arm hanging useless and broken. He spits a loogie of crimson on the ground, grumbling. “Well, we resisted these two jokers, and they beat us pretty badly. Fortunately, I was able to make it over to the Time Machine, sending me back to this very moment. In a straight up battle, they can take us, but with just ONE more of us… I KNEW we could win!!” “Well then?!” screeches the second Chronos, “KILL THEM!!” “HI-KEEBA!!” A meaty fist flies from nowhere, crunching into the jaw of the most recent Chronos, and sending him flying across the room, crashing into the original Chronos’. “It can’t be!!” “Ha ha ha ha ha!!” laughs the newest Big Alan Steele, looking resplendent in his toga, “In the flesh!” “But…” “Simple! While your evil compatriot managed to send one Big Alan Steele to the land of the Elysian Fields, I took the opportunity to dive headlong into the Time Machine!! Transported back to this moment in time, with the obvious aid of the gods upon Olympus, I am now enabled to end your nefarious schemes, with the help of myself!!” “Huzzah!!” yell out the other two Big Al’s, throwing up their fists in victory. “I wouldn’t count us out yet!!” says yet another Chronos, running from out of nowhere, surprising the Big Alan Steele’s… but he is somersaulted head over feet by a vicious clothesline from another Big Alan Steele. “It’s okay!! I’m here to even the odds!” says a new Chronos. “And I as well, Hi-Keeba!!” pumps up another Steele. “Wow,” says the original Chronos, rubbing the back of his head in embarrassment as he watches the influx of Steele’s and Chronos’, “This is getting out of hand!!” “DIE! DIE! DIE! DIE!!” screams a predictably new Chronos, his latest device countered by yet another Big Alan Steele. The original Chronos edges his way over to the Big Alan Steele side. “Er, are YOU the original Big Alan Steele of this timeline?” Big Al’s eyes narrow dangerously, as he takes in Chronos, “Indeed I am…” “Look,” sighs Chronos, “This seems to be following a pattern here. Whenever I win, a new Big Al appears. Whenever YOU win, a new Chronos comes along.” “I believe you are correct,” muses Original Al, as he watches a new Big Alan Steele kick a Flux Decelerator from a relatively new Chronos. “Right, so what do you say we make a deal?” “What sort of deal, as I have no intentions of bargaining with such a menace as yourself!!” “Nothing like that… I say we let our battles play out in the future. Whichever side has the last member to show wins. If we have one more, then we win. If you have an equal number of Big Alan Steele’s, we’ll all go quietly… deal?” “I do not know,” says Steele, rubbing his chin in thought, “I savor the taste of battle as tis sweet upon my lips!” “Listen, I’m allergic to pain, okay? You get the bad guy and everything turns out okay, unless, of course, in the future I win.” The Big Alan Steele of this time-space dimensional plane looks thoughtfully at a cackling, new Chronos as he joins the side of evil, and as a new Big Alan Steele yells out a warcry in warning to the burgeoning ranks of their foes. “In the future our battles wage on?” “Probably in the near future, yes,” agrees Chronos, watching as new members of himself appear in unison with members of the Steele’s. “Then… it would not be boastful to brag of achievements garnered in a future time span, agreed?” The room is starting to become quite crowded as more heroes and villains of similar makeup force their way inside. The original Chronos rolls his eyes, “Yeah, if it’s THAT important to you…” Steele sniffs in derision, “Like you would not…” “OKAY, OKAY!! Do we have a deal or not?!” Big Al nods, offering a hand to seal the pact, “I wish you luck in this game.” “Thanks, you too,” returns the first Chronos. They lean back and watch as Chronos after Chronos, Big Alan Steele after Big Alan Steele, make their way inside the now cramped laboratory. The choruses of ‘Hi-Keeba’ and maniacal laughter are deafening… But soon the stream of endless time travelers slows, the moments in between newcomers becoming more pronounced. “I do believe the battle of the future has ended,” says the first Big Al. “Moron,” sighs Chronos, “They never began!! But, it DOES seem that our doubles have ceased in coming. Looks like it’s time for a headcount.” “Okay!!” shouts Big Al as he gestures to all, “All worthy adversaries to THIS side of the room, and all sons of Zeus to HIS side of the enclosure…” Some slight murmuring as the crowds part, eyes shooting daggers at the opposing side. A wall of togas to the right and a glittering embankment of gold to the left. The original opponents of this battle walk out in the middle. “Now, should you miscount…” growls Steele menacingly. “What good would a miscount do?” shoots back Chronos angrily, “All you’d have to do is attack!!” “Indeed,” said wryly. The counting begins. We now re-rejoin our Big and Bold heroes, Big Alan Steele’s, as they all banter about aimlessly, thoroughly enjoying each other’s company back at Stately Steele Manor. Trusted manager, Sue Saksnussum, looks on in utter disbelief, her hands shaking as she quickly sucks out cigarette after cigarette. “Fifty-seven Big Alan Steele’s!” she chokes out in between drags, “And I could barely manage with one…” “But think of the positive boon upon society we shall bring!” says a Big Alan Steele, “With this great amount of me, super villains of the world shall have great cause to reconsider their vile actions!!” “Huzzah!” cheers a Big Alan Steele. “Yes, no longer shall the world need be in fear of unknown villainous assassins!” agrees a Big Alan Steele. Sue begins to weep deep, bitter tears. “Ah, the noble lass,” says a touched Big Alan Steele, “She cries tears of happiness at the dawning of a new era!” “But…” sniffs Sue through her depression, “But wouldn’t this destroy the fabric of time and space itself? How can there be so MANY of you?!” “As the Lord of Time, Chronos, put it,” explains a Big Alan Steele, “Each continuum goes on without us.” Another Big Al sighs heavily with emotion, “Yes, as we now work together to purge THIS Earth of evil, other dimensions have lost our empowering watchfulness. In yon dimensions, you, Sue, have lost me forever! Yet here YOU are with fifty-seven! Talk about luck!” “Yeah,” states Sue dryly, “Real lucky. Can’t you just… you know… go back? The Time machine was RIGHT THERE!!” “Alas, I am afraid not,” says a Big Alan Steele, “As we have now converged upon this time continuum. Were we to use the time traveling device, we would only be subject to THIS continuum.” “We can never go back,” agrees a Big Alan Steele. “Huzzah!!” cries out another Big Alan Steele, “Brothers in arms forever!!” The roar makes Sue cringe, “Hi-Keeba!!” “Wait,” begins Sue, a slow smile on her face, “What if we were to… you know… hold a Holy Tournament or something? In praise of Olympus…” The Steele’s fall silent. “Continue…” one says. “Well, the world really only need ONE Big Alan Steele, as you guys are SOOOO effective in controlling crime…” Murmurs of agreement spur Sue on. “So, what if we were to have a gladiatorial battle in praise of Zeus with the FINEST warriors of all time… YOU! The winner of the contest would truly be deserving of his place here on Earth?” A pregnant silence. Sue bites her lip as the words settle upon the sons of Zeus… and the grins are unmistakable! “By the gods!! A truly magnificent battle of bloodsport of quality unheard of in this realm!” “Not since the long bypassed days of Roman times has such an idea been possible!!” “Right!” Yells Sue in encouragement, “THERE CAN BE ONLY ONE!!” “ONLY ONE!!” chant Big Alan Steele’s in unison, “HI-KEEBA!!” They all begin to pour out of the house to the backyard, selecting dangerous implements of death. A stray Big Alan Steele comes up to Sue, placing a hand on her shoulder, tears in his eyes. “Your commitment to the old gods is nothing short of remarkable! You are to be commended for such a wondrous idea! And besides the glory upon high, there can be no better training to prepare for PPV matches of such magnitude than taking on dozens of the current PCW Champions! You are truly a boon to me, Ms. Saksnussum…” “Aww,” says Sue coyly, with a wave, “Think nothing of it.” “Hi-Keeba!!”
