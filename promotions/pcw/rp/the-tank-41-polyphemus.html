Date: The Second of the Final Three RP&#039;s of the Tank Series ﻿*********The Tank __41—********* Polyphemus If I take everything from youit’s because you did it first. ~Leigh Donovan Friday, November 22 - Day 141 After the fight in the gym, life in the Tank didn’t go back to normal as Snow hoped it would. Prisoners stared at him wherever he went, staff members who knew nothing of the fight wouldquestion the cuts on his face and the bruises all over his body. He wonders if anyone wouldnotice tonight at Fusion? The thought of parole never seemed so sweet as now. He can’t bare this existence much longer,with everyone either looking at him as a monster or a fluke. Tomorrow’s parole hearing can’tcome too soon. Life in the Tank has been hard on Snow, to be sure. He sits alone in his cell, as he has as muchas possible since the epic brawl, until he surprised by the sudden appearance of his cellmate,Leigh Donovan, just back from the infirmary. “Look who it is,” Snow says without a smile. Leigh, of course, is incapable of smiling, speaking, or any other activity that involves movementof the mouth. His jaw is fractured and he has barely any teeth left in his mouth, which is swollento twice its normal size. In response, he raises his middle finger to Snow. This gets a chuckle out of the bruised lion. He runs his fingers through his long blond hair andrealizes that both he and Leigh are the members of the same club - all of the bruises on theirbody came at the hands of J-Thug, who now, has inevitably taken Leigh’s place in the infirmary. At last word, J-Thug’s jaw will likely never fully heal without extensive plastic surgery, andeven then, there’s the possibility that he’ll never speak right again. When the brought him inyesterday, he had lost a lot of blood through his mouth and nose, and there was some concern forhis life. Snow was relieved to hear that J-Thug lived through the experience - to date, he’s neveractually killed someone, and is in no rush to cross the line between aggravated assaultand murder. Suddenly, Tim McDonald pokes his head into the cell, his smile as radiant as the sun that noneof the occupants have seen in quite some time. “Hey mute,” he says playfully to Leigh, “get thefuck out. I’ve got to talk business with your bruise buddy.” Reluctantly, Leigh exits the cell, partially because he has no way of protesting. “Jesus,” McDonald says after taking a good look at Snow’s exposed arms - they’re both coveredin dark purple bruises. “Your face came out ok, well, considering what you looked likeyesterday, but I had no idea you took such a beating to the rest of your body.” “What’s up with Giovanni?” Snow asks, cutting directly to the point. “It’s taken care of,” McDonald says confidently. “Well, everything other than the actual fact...”he hesitates. “Snow,” he says, his face straightening, “the time is right to make a hit on him. You just made the fucking guards in here a shit load of money and most of them have no ideawhat’s going on in here anyway. It’s time to take the fucker out. Now.” “What about his...” Snow tries to think of the best word, “friends... on the outside? I thought yousaid they would get suspicious if Giovanni suddenly got taken out. “Not any more,” McDonald smiles. “I fixed things. They told me to have it done.” Snow is shocked but he doesn’t question it. McDonald masterfully framed Giovanni for rattingout several high ranking mobsters, a crime in the underworld that commands death. McDonaldwill be happy to provide that, since Giovanni’s death will clear both he and Snow of anywrongdoing. “I just want to finish this shit,” Snow sighs. “I can’t have my family in jeopardy.” “Finishing this is what I’m here about,” McDonald says, treading lightly. In the end, he decidesthere’s no better way to bring it to Snow than by straight-forward means. “Snow, you’re the guyto make the hit.” Snow’s eyes turn to stare at McDonald. “I’m up for parole tomorrow,” he says. “You want meto commit my first murder the day before I try to convince people that I’m rehabilitated?” “I don’t think you have a choice here, Snow,” McDonald says. “But actually, I was thinking itwould be best to wait until tomorrow morning.” “The day of my parole hearing...” Snow chuckles at the irony. “You’re the only man for the job, Snow - the only man I can trust. Giovanni would rip me toshreds.” Oddly, Snow accepts the inevitability of what he must do with relative ease. He never imaginedthe decision to murder, in cold blood, being so easy. Then again, he has yet to tap what he maybe capable of in cases where his family is in jeopardy. “Alright,” he says quietly. “Whatever I have to do. My family’s not going to be in danger whenI step out through that fucking gate tomorrow.” He hopes they allow him to step out. He can’tbare the alternative. Friday, November 22 - Day 141 “Hey Snow,” Jake Hooper’s voice breaks the silence that Snow has surrounded himself with fornearly an hour. Jake’s is an unwelcome presence in Snow’s cell, but lately, he can have nopeace. His eyes wander to Jake’s smiling face. “Your buddy’s got himself into a little trouble inthe gym. We thought you might want to know.” He laughs after he speaks and then disappearsbehind the far wall, at least from Snow’s point of view. What has Leigh gotten himself into, wonders Snow. It’s lunch time, so most prisoners are eitherin the cafeteria or on work detail, but in these days where the Unit is run by overworked C.O’s,many strays get left behind. Despite his better judgement, Snow decides to head to the gym tosee what trouble Leigh has found. He can’t talk himself out of it, Snow jokes to himself as he pushes open the heavy steel door tothe gym - the Unit is almost completely vacant. Snow finds himself gazing at a horrible sight, much worse than anything he had feared. LeighDonovan is being restrained by a Nazi twice his size while Harvey Blake, the leader of the Aryangang, laughs in his bruised face. Snow’s immediate thought is that Leigh is being punished forfailing to kill J-Thug earlier in the week. “Let him go!” Snow commands, quickly taking steps toward the scene. Harvey looks over at Snow awkwardly, and then, very slowly a smile creeps on his face. Peculiarly, the other Nazi releases his hold on Leigh. Suddenly, it becomes all too clear to Snow- it’s a trap. Before he can react, he feels a blunt pain in the back of his head and sees a flash of white. Amoment later, he’s on the floor - Jake Hooper had come from behind him and struck him withsome unknown object. The Nazi’s pounce on him, and suddenly, half a dozen more come rushing from out of thelibrary - one has a blade, Snow notices. Jake Hooper fires several straight right hands down atSnow to keep the young lion stunned while his companions get in position to hold him down. There’s a Nazi on each of Snow’s limbs to go along with Harvey Blake, Jake Hooper, and thetraitorous Leigh Donovan. “You bastard!” Snow sneers as he struggles to free himself. His cold blue eyes are locked firmlyon Leigh’s bruised purple face. If he didn’t know better, he’d think Leigh was smiling - despitethe broken jaw. Yet another thunderous shot slams cleanly into Snow’s forehead, where a bright red spot isforming with the indentation of Hooper’s thick knuckles. “Give me the knife!” Harvey says to his blade-wielding companion. The designated skin-headhands the sloppy blade to Harvey Blake, who leans close to Snow, glaring at him through hislone eye. “Snow,” he says, “I’ve told you countless times that I am your only means ofsalvation in this hell hole. Did you think I wouldn’t hold true to my word? Did you think that Iwould really let you walk the fuck out of here after all the disrespect that you’ve givenme in the last half year!?” “You sick Nazi motherfucker!” “Even now,” Harvey says through clenched teeth, “you haven’t learned to mind your fuckingmouth.” With that, he slams a right hand directly into Snow’s mouth, causing the skin onSnow’s lips to break against his teeth. Blood runs down his chin and onto his chest. “You prey on these people!” Snow says in a voice laced with horrible anger. “Not a singlefucking one of them can stand on their own! That’s why they need you, you sick fuck! And that’s why you need them!” “We’re a brotherhood!” Harvey argues, his lone eye blazing on Snow. “We’re strong separately,and stronger united. We have common values.” “You don’t give a fuck about them!” Snow fires back. “Not a fucking one of them reallybelieves your bullshit! They cling to you because they think it’s the only way they can survive! They don’t care about your Nazi bullshit! They just don’t want one of J-Thug’s boys fuckingtheir ass for the entirety of their sentence.” Harvey doesn’t know how to answer this, and it seems that a few of his Nazi companions arehoping this ugly and strained conversation will soon end - end with Snow’s death, of course. “You know what?” Harvey says with a mocking smile that changes the subject. “You look like afucking hippy. I always said that. I think it’s time to do something about it.” Harvey reaches down, grabs a fist full of Snow’s long blond hair, and with one quick motion,runs the knife through it, cutting it away. The skinheads laugh while Snow tries with all hismight to defend himself. Harvey then reaches down and cuts off a second portion of Snow’shair, then a second, and then a third. Some of it, he simply rips away from Snow’s skull with hisbare hands. “There,” he says when Snow’s blond crop is left looking horrendous - cut in various lengths allover his head with blood swimming through it from Harvey’s slips of the knife as well as thetimes he didn’t use the knife at all. “You look much more respectable now. You’ll lookgood at your funeral.” The Nazis chuckle in approval as Harvey presses the blade against Snow’s exposed through. “Just a swipe to the side,” Harvey says as he applies more pressure, “and it’s all over.” A dropof blood runs down Snow’s neck from a tiny cut made by the knife; It leaves a crimson trailbehind it. “Wait a minute,” Harvey says, thankfully pulling the blade from Snow’s throat. “Iwant to make this fun - a real initiation.” He turns to Leigh Donovan and holds the blade out tothe young Australian. “You do it.” Leigh accepts the knife, almost reluctantly, and he’s surprised by how heavy it suddenly feels. There, below him, is his prey - Jason Snow. He leans in close with the blade, inching it toward’sSnow’s exposed hairy neck. “One fluent stroke,” Harvey says with a nod. “You don’t want to get his nigger-loving blood allover you.” The one-eyed Nazi chuckles to himself as he steps back and folds his arms across hisheavily tattoo’ed chest. Leigh presses the blade to Snow’s throat, finding it hard to hold his hand still. The suspenseseems to amuse the Nazis. Snow’s eyes are wide and trying to look down far enough to see the blade that’s about to end histwenty-four year old life. It seems like such a young age, suddenly. He’s always thoughthimself an old man. He never did get along well with those his own age. “You’re not one ofthem,” Snow hisses at Leigh after noticing the young man’s broken jaw is trembling. “This isnot where you belong! Leigh!” “Kill him!” commands Harvey. “Now!” Leigh hesitates, his mind eating away at Snow’s words. Months ago, when he’d first entered theTank, he never dreamed that he’d join a Nazi gang. But they were his only opportunity forsurvival. But as he stares down at his pending victim, he can’t help but think that if it hadn’tbeen for Snow, he wouldn’t have survived at all - it was Snow who pulled Leigh from the mightyfangs of J-Thug, who no doubt would have ended the young Australian’s life. J-Thug hadresented Snow for the help, but now, he realises that help is all that he’s been looking for - ahelping hand that he only saw in the form of Harvey Blake. All along, the help has been there,in his own cell, sleeping right above. It is too late to get help from Snow now with his parolepending tomorrow - but it’s not too late recognize it. It’s not too late to do what he’s wanted todo since the change he’s gone under since his several trips to the hole - it’s not too late to finallystand on his own. “No,” Leigh answers sternly, pulling the blade away from Snow’s throat. Harvey is stunned. “W-what...” he stammers as his face grows mean. “What did you say to me? PRAG!?” “I said,” Leigh hisses as he backs away from Snow, “NO!” With a sudden burst, Leighlunges at Harvey Blake, swinging the blade wildly with his right hand. The blade runs acrossHarvey’s cheek and blood splashes from the wound instantly. “I am not a PRAG!” Leighscreams, taking another swipe with the blade, too quick for Harvey to defend against. This time,the blade slashes across Harvey’s chest. “What the fuck do you think you’re doing!?” Jake Hooper bellows, rising to his feet to defendhis mentor - he’s too late. The third swipe of the blade proves to be the most harmful. It firstmakes contact with Harvey’s left ear, and then runs along the upper portion of his face andslashes directly through his lone eye. The aging Nazi screeches as he drops to his knees and clutches his disgusting face. Blood, andother sickening fluids run through his fingers and drip to the concrete floor. “I’m blind,” hecries, his entire body shaking. “I’m fucking blind!” The shock of the events is enough to distract the Nazis long enough for Snow to break free andflee from the Nazis, who don’t even make an attempt to restrain them. They’re concern is fortheir leader, who’s been left blinded and helpless on the gym floor. Leigh Donovan takes thechance to disappear behind Snow into the main room of Unit B. Harvey Blake removes his hands, unmasking his crimson face and ripping his eyepatch off in the process. Two gaping holes remain where his eyes had once been, his newly blinded socket bleeding hatred for his attackers, his life, and his newfound uselessness.
