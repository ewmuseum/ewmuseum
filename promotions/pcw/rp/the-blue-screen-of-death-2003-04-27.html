The Blue Screen of Death

27 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4 - 27 - 03 BEEP! BEEP! BEEP! 'Grah... shut the fuck up...' A tired hand suddenly bursts forth from its cloth casket of blankets and swipes the annoying digital alarm clock right off the mahogany dresser, sending it crashing to the blue carpet like a beeping meteor. The irritating repitition of beeping noises stops when the clock splinters in about four different pieces, and from then on, Dorn Tracer's morning ritual begins. The warming anacondas of quilts and blankets are thrown off his body almost like a reflex and the Television Champion rises from his slumber like a zombie from the Deadhead, rubbing the crust from his right eye by grinding his fist in. 'Just needed ten more minutes...' He groans with a venomous hint to his voice. 'Fuckin' flight doesn't leave until 8 at night anyway.' The morning rituals are always despised by the Astronomer, but he knows they're a staple of his day. If he doesn't prepare properly in the morning hours, then he fucks up the rest of the day. The reasons are beyond his knowledge, but at the same time, he could really care less. The recent events that have transpired on PCW television haven't exactly brightened his days, and they're slowly getting worse. They're hanging on his mood like bloated hemroids on his posterior, threatening to burst from pressure at any moment. Yes, it's that bad. The PCW TV Champ rotates his body and hangs his legs over the edge of the bed, then stretches them out at full length. The entire rest of his body seems to straighten and stretch as well, almost like a reflex. His face jumbles up and twists from the muscle bunching, then when his joints give a satisfying CRACK, he relaxes his person and sighs happily. 'Ah... much better...' With that, he rises to his feet like a bear from a year long slumber and ambles on into the small bathroom connected to the hotel bedroom. Nothing better than a warm morning shower to start the day, He thinks to himself with a hearty grin, the very thought of it teasing his satisfaction nerves. Dorn presses a hand against the bathroom door and slides it open, letting some morning sun pour into the white-tiled shitter room. He reaches over and lets his hand explore the wall before finding the light switch and flipping it on... before letting his eyes get adjusted. The ceiling light flares to life like an atomic explosion and sends Dorn staggering out of the room, shielding his eyes. 'AH! FUCK! MY EYES! MY VIRGIN, LIGHT-SENSITIVE, BAT-LIKE EYES!' He cries, in a rather comical manner. After a moment of letting his eyes recover, Tracer blinks rapidly for a moment or so and steps in, shutting the door behind him. There's silence for a minute, followed by the slight groaning of the glass shower door opening, then the unmistakeable choir of running shower water and Dorn's horrible singing voice. 'OOOOOHH THERE'S ONE-TWO-THREE-FOUR-FIVE-SIX-SEVEN-EIGHT-NINE-TEN-EIGHTEEN WHEELS ON A BIG RIG! AND THEY'RE ROLLIN', ROLLIN', ROLLIN'! ROLLIN', ROLLIN', ROLLIN'!' ****************************** About half an hour later, The Astronomer comes striding out of the bathroom holding a Ninja Turtles bath towel around his waist. A satisfied smile adorns his handsome face and he smacks his lips in an arrogant fashion, expressing that said satisfaction. His body glistens in red from the heat and his hair dangles down like a messy black forest of vines, covering his face and shoulders. The Television Champion walks over to the generic hotel dresser and gazes upon the very golden object of his immense pride: The PCW Television Title. Its beautiful golden face manifests itself on his crystal blue eyes and a sudden wave of pride washes over his already wet body. 'Ah... the Tiamats have truly blessed me. My limits go beyond the skies, and my victories are proof of that,' The Astronomer says out loud, but to no one but himself. His voice rings with pride and dignity as he sticks out his chest and nods. 'The recent influx of new blood is NOTHING compared to me. Only devote zealots such as myself truly shine, while the blasphemers burn in opener hell. Heh...' A sinister look graces Tracer's face as he turns away from the belt and walks over to his Pentium Laptop, a very fine machine he just purchased. It's open and sitting on, casting an electronic glow across the floor in a thin column. Without even drying off fully, Dorn pulls up a chair and sits down at his laptop, gazing with an intense eye at the screen. It seems as if he's been downloading something, as a thin blue bar EVER SO SLOWLY makes its way to the right of the screen within a little dialogue box. In the middle of the left region of the dialogue box, the total download time thus far is displayed: 11:45:25, and counting up from 25. He's been downloading whatever it is for about ELEVEN straight hours. Dorn's tired eyes gaze upon the Time Remaining display and suddenly beam with excitement when it reads that only 15 minutes are left. 'Good... I can't stand waiting,' Dorn blurts out, as if someone asked him. 'Better check up on the Turmoil card while I'm waiting. I'm sure it won't do any harm.' Dorn grips the mouse and moves the pointer to the little underscore button on the corner of the dialogue box, pressing it and minimizing the download box. He pulls up Internet Exploror and his hands suddenly go to work at the keyboard, typing rapidly. His skilled fingers become blurs and the rhythmic sound of key-pounding echoes through the room, like a steady machinegun. A white text box labeled Address at the top of the screen suddenly contains these words: http://pcw.ewmuseum.com. The screen flashes and suddenly, the Spring Fling logo appears in the middle of the browser display. Dorn sighs to himself and clicks on it, bringing up the Primetime Championship Wresting site. The Astronomer uses other hand to stroke his chin while he gazes at the site. 'Let's see now... gotta find the roster page... Oh wow, they found Nerezza. I figured that vampire douche wouldn't stay buried.' Dorn cracks a grin at his own claim and shakes his head. He finds the pull-down menu leading to the card preview and pulls the pointer down with the button depressed, but accidently lets go on the Roster selection. 'Doh!' He cries in a mock fashion, not really wanting to see the Roster. When it appears, his eyes scan the various 'Coming Soon' picture shots and notices they heavily outweigh those WITH pictures, like himself. 'By the Tiamats...' His voice is baited with surprise, his eyes wide with confusion. The fucking greenhorns are more than we are! That's alot of spotlight to distribute... The very thought of his hard-earned spotlight being given to one of the newbies sends a furious look across the Astronomer's face. The recent influx of new blood has been STAGGERING! The newbies now outnumber the veterans, and Dorn's non too comfortable with it. 'Damn young fucks are gonna run us out if we don't do the same to them first...' Now done with the tainted roster page, Dorn pulls down the menu again and clicks on the selection that reads 'Turmoil Card'. It seems to take a little longer than usual, but Dorn deduces that it's the fault of his download, which is only 10 minutes from being done. The Turmoil Card finally appears and Dorn just skips over most of it until he finds his match. 'John Gargano...' A puzzled look crosses the Television Champion's face as he looks at his match, cycling it through his mind again and again. 'John Gargano... what in En'Flarim's name is a John Gargano... Some fucking unknown pitted in a match with ME! The Television Champion! I represent the 3rd most prestigous title in PCW and they're booking me in a match against a fucking GREENHORN! It's blasphemy!' In a fit of anger, Dorn pounds the wooden dresser, causing it to quake violently, then shakes his head. A look of rage is still cemented on his face as he reads the match again and again, still not believing that he's facing some nobody. He eyes trail along the rest of the card and down to the taskbar, where his 11 hour download now reads 5 minutes. The thought of the video being downloaded puts a smile on Dorn's tired face and he gives a satisfied sigh. 'Jenna Jameson's Greatest Hits... Tiamats be praised for such a vision of lovliness!' The Astronomer moves the point back up to his match and stares at John Gargano's name for a minute, wondering if he should actually be worried. A sly grin crosses his face and suddenly, he begins to rapidly press the pointer on John Gargano's name, though it doesn't do anything. His clicking gets faster and faster, and his eyes widen with savage glee. Faster...and faster...and faster... until... 'WHAT THE?!?!' Like the light of Sheor himself, a daunting blue light suddenly emits from the screen, with white text imprinted. Dorn's confused eyes read what it says... 'Press Enter to continue... CTRL-ALT-DELETE to Restart... Well, that's obvious!' He slams the Enter key, but the same blue screen comes right back. Thinking maybe he missed the Enter key, The Astronomer looks down and presses it this time... same blue screen reappears. His pressing of the Enter key becomes frantic now, nearly shattering the plastic button. 'No...! It can't be...! Please...' After pressing it about 10 more times to no avail, only one thing sticks out in his mind: CTRL-ALT-DELETE. 'THE BLUE SCREEN OF DEATH!!! NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!' Dorn SLAMS the laptop shut in a fit of rage, picks it up, and hurls it against the mirror on the far wall with amazing force! The mirror shatters into a million pieces and the laptop gives a mechanical groan before shutting off. The Astronomer jolts up in a furious rage, knocking his chair down, and drops to his knees, raising his fists to the sky! 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOO!! DAMN YOU, SHEOR! DAMN YOU STRAIGHT TO PLUTO! AHHHHHH!!!!!' On that note, Dorn buries his head in his hands and promptly begins to weep... [End.]
