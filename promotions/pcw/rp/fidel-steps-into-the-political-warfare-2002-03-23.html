Fidel Steps Into the Political Warfare

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/23/02 Time: 3: 18 P.M Location: New MexicoScene: Raul’s Farm “ Political Warfare Begins to Grow” The scene opens with Juan Borro and Kevin Klaymore entering Raul’s farm. Raul was one of Juan’s old prison mate friends. He needed somebody who owned a large sum of land where the government wasn’t present, plus not far from United States borders. Juan goes up to the door and signals for Kevin to wait behind for a few moments. Juan knocks on the door, and after a few moments an old lady answer the door, and looks at Juan curiously. She then begins rambling on in Portuguese. Juan is about to respond, when he sees Raul running down the steps. Raul jumps in front of his mother and greets Juan very happily. He then invites him in. Raul: Come on in Juan, I’m just watching some television. Raul then looks back and sees Kevin wondering whom it is. Raul: Who’s he? Juan: Oh that’s my friend Kevin Klaymore. We wrestle together in PCW. He’s here also to make sure Fidel makes it to Sunday night, without being deported. Kevin Klaymore goes up to the door and greets Raul. Raul is blown away by Kevin’s physical physique. Raul: Man Kevin, you sure are one huge essa. Tell me how you guys managed to get the head Cuban Honcho, Fidel Castro to even think about entering this county man? Juan is about to answer, but Raul interrupts him. Raul: Oh wait, how rude am I. Come in and take a seat guys. Me Casa Su casa. Just don’t take ya socks of essa, cuz that stuff stanks. The men take a seat, as Raul’s mother comes in wondering what’s going on. Raul’s Mother: Raul, who these men? Raul: They’re friend’s mamma, go upstairs and get some sleep, ya look tired. Jus wring ya bell and I’ll be up there. Raul gives his mom a hug and a kiss, as she goes upstairs to sleep in her room. Raul then comes back down and takes a seat nervously. Raul: Sorry guys, my motha needs me, ya know? Juan: It’s no problem Raul, but you see, when Fidel comes she may see it as awkward. Are you sure it will still work out. Raul: Sure it wills my motha sleep like an angel. The men wait as they watch television for an hour or so. Raul goes over to the kitchen to finish up preparing the meal, when all the men hear a helicopter landing in the back of Raul’s farm. The men charge outside as the helicopter lands. All three men’s eyes are glued to the exit of the helicopter, until they finally see Fidel come walking out on a red carpet, with a fine leather overcoat to wear over his fine Satan and silk suit. He walks over to the men with a Cuban cigar in his mouth. He puffs out and keeps going up to them until they are nose-to-nose. Juan, Kevin, and Raul remain silent not knowing what to say, so Fidel is the first to speak. Fidel: So this is where you had me come. Nice little place, reminds me of Cuba, except for the smell and feel of this God forsaken land. Kevin: The smell could just be me I didn’t shower. Fidel gives Kevin a weird look. Meanwhile Juan slaps Kevin in the head leading Kevin to do his usual “What was that for?” stance. Fidel: So this match of mine is tomorrow I presume. I’m looking oh so forward to breaking bread with those American politicians. Juan: Believe me, sir. There is going to be no bread breaking tomorrow, it will be all out carnage. We’re confident you’ll have our back, and do it well. Fidel: Of course I will I’m Fidel, and child how many times must I tell you to stop calling me sir. No need to try and impress me, I’m already impressed by your squads resume. It’s very impressive work. Juan: Thank you we pride ourselves on that. Tomorrow’s match will be how do you say, a blood bath, or at least that’s what the Americans call it I believe. Fidel: That’s great to hear. So what countries did the two of you actually come from? I presume you are both foreign? Juan: But of course, I am from the beautiful and friendly Portugal. Kevin: And I actually lived in United States, but also lived a lot of my childhood in Pampelona, Spain. I have ancestry back there. Fidel: Well you sure are one large Spaniard. Where you come from sounds nice, the both of you. I know Spain and Portugal are two fine countries in Europe. Iberian Peninsula is very desirable. Juan: Thank You. Raul interferes in the conversation, as he steps in front of the men. Raul: Sorry to interrupt, but I just want you all to know the food is ready inside, so we can eat dinner if you want. Fidel: Ah! Dinner sounds great it has been ages since I have had a home cooked meal. It isn’t easy being me. They all go inside and sit down for dinner. Raul grabs the shrimp and lobster he made and puts it on the table. Everyone takes a little bit of food, and begins eating. Fidel: So this Primetime Championship Wrestling business seems very interesting, every man need to be able to fight. Juan: I agree, I actually came to wrestling to prove to the world that a soccer player and foreigner who is small could be just as dominant as everyone else. Fidel: Yes, you are that huge soccer star. I knew your name sounded familiar. Kevin: And I pretty much went into wrestling, because I thought of the fighters as heroes and types of legends. Fidel: You men sure do deserve the title of legends. Any man willing to fight for a cause should be a legend as is hero. That’s why I can’t wait to step in the ring. Juan: It’s great to see you’re so anxious. Fidel: How is this Politically Incorrect team? Do you face any others? Kevin: Politically Incorrect seems like smart men, but their physical attributes are quite weak. It’s funny that they can even seem to compete. They can talk a good game, but they can’t back it up. Juan: That’s true and later on this week we face a barrage of teams. I believe it’s Fear Factory, Misplaced, Cowabunga Connection, Jobbers Inc., and Metallyx, that we face including Politically Incorrect. Fidel: Those sure are some odd names. Do any of those teams worry you in any way, shape, or form. Juan: Oh not at all. Fidel: That’s the spirit! Real men like myself are not afraid of anything, we anticipate it instead. Kevin: That sure is true. Fidel: When do I get to meet the rest of this International Incident team? Juan: You’ll see them all tomorrow. You’ll also meet you’re fellow lumberjack Chester Bin Laden. Fidel: Yes! I am quite excited, even though I must say I don’t agree with Bin Laden’s ways it doesn’t mean his cousin is the same. Plus, a powerful man is still a powerful man, inDEED. Juan: Yes, the two of you will get along fine. Fidel: Well in that case, I should be getting some sleep I’m just about finished here. If you could guide me to my room than everything could go along splendidly. Raul: Sure follow me. Fidel leaves the scene and Golden Game clears the table, completely prepared for their upcoming matches physically, mentally, and POLITICALLY. SCENE FADES TO BLACK.
