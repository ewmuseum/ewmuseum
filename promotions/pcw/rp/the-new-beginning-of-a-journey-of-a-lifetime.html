The new beginning of a journey of a lifetime

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [Scene opens in the hotel room after Flaymin’s defeat to Violence Jack in their just completed Extreme Title match. Smouldered with dry sweat and a variety of bruises, Flaymin just slumps his battered body against the wall with his hands on his forehead, leaning his elbows onto his knees. He sits there in silence for a few minutes, not even moving a muscle…. Just staring into space. Suddenly, Hirraani walks into the room and looks at the exhausted Flaymin.She puts her hands on her hips and frowns] Hirraani: Hey you gotta get up- we need to clear out of this Hotel room in 15 minutes. [Flaymin momentarily raises his head and mutters underneath his breath] Flaymin: Screw it. Hirraani: Look, I understand your devastated- but there’s nothing you can do it about it. Flaymin: I almost killed myself on that f’n Wrestle Vessel.And for what? To drop the title just like that to a mentally challenged, bald freak? Hirraani: Mr T? Flaymin: No!I meant Violence Jack. Hirraani: Right…. Him.Oh well, life goes on! [Flaymin looks up at Hirraani with an evil gleam in his eye] Flaymin: Forget it, I’m staying here.You need to move on and do your own thing. [Hirraani gazes back at Flaymin, suspiciously] Hirraani: What are you trying to say? Flaymin: Me…. You….. its not working.I’m holding you back from your destiny.And as fruitful and bright as your yellow brick road is, somehow I don’t see myself skipping along merrily beside you. I’m heading in a different direction. Hirraani: And where would that be? [Flaymin looks around the room momentarily and then shrugs his shoulders] Flaymin: I haven’t figured that out yet. But I’ll know when I get there. [Hirraani shakes her head in confusion] Hirraani: But if you don’t know where you’re going and you don’t know how you’ll get there- how will you know that you’ve achieved what you set out to do? Flaymin: I don’t know…. I’ll buy an Atlas or something. [Hirraani walks over to Flaymin and places her hand on his shoulder] Hirraani: You’re distraught.And exhausted, both mentally and physically. Maybe you just need time to clear your head? Flaymin: No, the only way I’m going to clear my head and find my way in the world is to run.I’ll see the world as I pass it by and when I finally discover that haven that offers me tranquillity- I’ll stop running.Because the world will finally make sense. And I’ll understand what my role in PCW is meant to be. Hirraani: Don’t be so optimistic.Because while you’re out there exploring your journey to freedom you might suddenly come to a halt and finally realise that I was the one for you.Not everyone gets a second chance to right their wrongs, you know. Flaymin: Well then that’s an opportunity I’m willing to take. I can’t just stand around, with one testicle, watching my career go down the shithole. Hirraani: You don’t have to stand!You’ll be more comfortable sitting down. Flaymin: Spare me your wisecracks. [Flaymin and Hirraani suddenly become silent once again. The silence fills the room for almost a minute, until Flaymin finally speaks up] Flaymin: Well?I see you’re still here? Hirraani: Is this what you really want? [Flaymin pauses for a moment as he begins to strain his intellectual abilities in deep thought….] Flaymin: Yes….. I think you should leave.If I’m going to start a new beginning, it has to be without you by my side. [Hirraani cries out with one final plea] Hirraani: But we were made for each other! Flaymin: No we’re not.We’ve been living a sham. Where I’m headed- there’s no place for you. Hirraani: BUT YOU DON’T EVEN KNOW WHERE YOU’RE HEAIDNG!!!! Flaymin: I told you, I’d buy an atlas. [Hirraani coldly stares into Flaymin’s eyes. Without saying a word, she picks up her bag and walks to the door.She opens the door and then looks back at Flaymin before exiting] Hirraani: This is your last chance. Flaymin: Have a nice life. [Hirraani hangs her head in shame before finally walking out the door and out of Flaymin’s life- for good. Flaymin slowly looks around the room and gazes out the window at the dark, starry sky… and at his unpredictable future. [Scene fades to black]
