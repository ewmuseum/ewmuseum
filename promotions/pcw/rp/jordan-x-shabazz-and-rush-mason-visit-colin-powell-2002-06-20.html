Jordan X Shabazz and Rush Mason Visit Colin Powell...

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/20/02 “Let’s go man, what the hell is wrong with you?” The familiar voice of a professional wrestler screams. It’s Rush Mason. He and Jordan have drawn the short stick and are traveling with one another to PCW Tuesday Turmoil. Neither Rush nor Jordan were supposed to wrestle on this card, they were just there to provide some support for their buddies, Jon Kano and Duke Williams. None of the Malice Crew was wrestling this night, but they had to be there in case something went down. Rush wore a gray Ecko sweater, baggy blue Phat Farm shorts, some white Nikes, and a doo rag and baseball cap to cover his nappy ass hair. Jordan X Shabazz came into the view of the camera, he wore a large Malcolm X t-shirt, some Phat Farm jeans, a Nike wristband, and some brown Lugz boots. Shabazz was dressed for combat. His hair was picked out into a small afro, not nappy, like Rush’ hair, but finely groomed, like an intelligent black man would have his hair. Rush and Jordan were in Washington, DC. It was Jordan’s dumbass idea to march to the white house, it was like the Million Man March…with two people. Why were they marching you ask? Three reasons, all relating to Jordan’s opponents on Friday Fusion. The first reason, was because Jordan X Shabazz wanted to offend his opponents. And because Jordan X Shabazz was delusional enough to believe that the President of the United States, busy as he is with counter-terrorism and whatnot, that he would listen to Jordan ramble. Jordan stands on the corner of the street right beside the white house, it is now very secure, cops patrolling the street. Shabazz stands looking at a huge space of lawn that is well groomed, yet not used. It’s behind a supermarket. Jordan appears as if he’s sizing the place up. Rush: What the hell are you looking at that for? It’s just grass Jordan, they have it in Dallas too, don’t they? Jordan X Shabazz: That’s not the thing Rush, it’s the possibility, think of the publicity N.I.G.G.A. would get… Rush: Possibility of what? Jordan turns to Rush, and flashes a big grin, showcasing his white teeth. Jordan X Shabazz: A big ass statue of Malcolm X, with Martin Luther King to the right, and Jordan X Shabazz to the left. Rush sighs heavily. Rush: Good Lord, please tell me that Jordan hasn’t taken to speaking of himself in the third person. Or is it first person? Jordan looks at Rush and he gives him a death stare. Fans of the Shabby one know why, Jordan wants African Americans to portray themselves as intelligent, and what Rush said was the epitome of what Jordan didn’t want to see. Rush: Hot Damn Jordan, if you wanted to carry on an intelligent conversation you should have talked to Skylar. Jordan X Shabazz: End? Heh, I can’t carry on an intelligent conversation with anybody outside of the Malice Crew, and especially if you’re a white boy. Rush: He’s half black. Jordan ignores Rush’ statement and he begins his trek towards the white house. The security that borders the white house is stiff, ever since 9/11, it is very hard to get through into the actual white house (Not like it wasn’t before). But this is different, Jordan X Shabazz has connections inside the white house. That’s right, you heard me, Jordan X Shabazz has connections, inside the white house. Who is his inside man you ask? HOW MANY FREAKIN’ BLACK GUYS WORK INSIDE THE WHITE HOUSE?! Sheesh. It’s none other than Colin Powell, the only black guy working inside the White House. Secretly, he’s a fan of professional wrestling, and a bigger fan of Civil Rights. So when he heard that Jordan X Shabazz would be in town, he decided to invite Jordan and a friend of his choosing into the white house where they will discuss Jordan’s concerns about the safety of Americans. Jordan and Rush walk through a metal detector, where they are thoroughly searched for drugs and weapons because they are black, and are visiting another black guy. Jordan and Rush walk through the lavish halls of the white house, they are two of the 6 black men that have ever walked these halls. The cops look at Jordan and Rush extra-weird, like they’re ‘Black people! Hide your wallets!’ Shabazz and Rush try to ignore them. After walking up numerous flights of stairs, Rush and Jordan reach Colin Powell’s office. Shockingly, music is pumping from Colin Powell’s room loudly. Jordan and Rush don’t even knock. They just open the door. And they are completely shocked to see Colin Powell smoking a cigarette, loud rap music pumping, and a bunch of women dancing. Jordan X Shabazz: “Colin, Colin, Colin! What the hell is going on?” Jordan X Shabazz and Rush barge through the door, Jordan takes a seat on one of the many nice leather chairs that adorn the office of Colin Powell. Colin puts his cigarette out and he gets the music turned off. He tells the women to leave the room as well. Colin Powell: “Well Jordan, welcome to the white house! I’m prepared to discuss your concerns.” Jordan goes into his pocket and he pulls out a crumpled piece of paper. He unfolds it, it is stained with chicken grease and watermelon juice. Jordan smiles as he begins to read his suggestions to the Secretary Of State (Okay so what? I’m a freakin’ idiot and I don’t know what Colin Powell’s actual job is). Jordan clears his throat and he begins to read, Rush Mason holds his head down, knowing that Jordan is about to make an ass out of himself. But they are teaming on Turmoil, and the two men have to spend some time together to make sure that they are on the same page and ready for the competition that can give them some momentum heading into PCW Gold and Glory, where Rush Mason will take on Andy Murray, and where John Crichton and Jordan X Shabazz will do battle for the vacant PCW Television title. Jordan X Shabazz: “First off, I would appreciate if you closed the borders to from Scotland and Mexico to the United States. Second…” Colin Powell is surprised, so surprised that he has to cut Jordan off. Colin Powell: “Whoa, whoa, whoa, slow down there buddy. Close the borders off to Mexico and Scotland? Mexico is one of our leading trade partners, and Scotland has given us such talents as Andy Murray, and Groundskeeper Willy!” Rush Mason is off in the corner, drifting into his insane world of voodoo. Jordan X Shabazz: “Andy Murray… a talent? HA! That’s laughable, and destroys your credibility, Colin.” Colin Powell takes a deep breath, not knowing exactly what to do with his Civil Rights friend. Jordan X Shabazz: “Mexico brings in a lot of car thieves, and a lot of illegal immigrants. I swear it, most all Mexicans are drug dealers. Like this El Diablo for instance, his name means the devil… That’s right, the devil! And it’s logical to think that the Devil could actually be a drug dealer. So I say, check his papers up on the computers. And if he’s not legal, DEPORT HIS MEXICAN ASS!!!” Jordan is now sweating profusely. Rush has put his head down and is now praying to some weak ass Voodoo God. Jordan is looking very crazy, Colin Powell has a stern look on his face, as if considering Jordan’s idiotic demands. Jordan X Shabazz: “Now I know I won’t get you on the Scottish part, because Groundskeeper Willy is very respectful, but I’ll move on. I ask you, to now, find every single blind person in America, and take away their walkers, canes, radios, dogs, and anything that they use to connect to the outside world.” Colin Powell looks appalled at this. Colin Powell: “TAKE AWAY THEIR CONNECTIONS TO THIS WORLD?!” Jordan nods solemnly. Jordan X Shabazz: “Check the Malice Crew buddy, there’s no blind people. But when you check my opponents for Fusion, I see one blind person. And you know how much you love to see me win, Colin.” Colin sighs heavily. Colin Powell: “I’ll see what I can do, Jordan.” Jordan grins and continues to read, albeit slow, from his crumpled piece of paper. Though he claims to be the intelligent black man, years and years of power bombs and body slams have taken their toll on the Civil Rights’ Activist’s brain. Jordan X Shabazz: “Now, this is the most important, I know that we are at war and everything, but could you, possibly, kinda, really, *Says really fast* TAKEAWAYAFEWBILLIONDOLLARSFROMTHEMILITARY!” Jordan thinks he’s slick, eh? Did you see that? He’s asking for Colin Powell to take money away from the military in perhaps the most important for the military in the entire 21st Century. Colin Powell takes a few minutes to comprehend what Jordan just tried to slip by him, and when he does find out, he’s surprised. So surprised in fact, that he has to stand up. Colin Powell: “JORDAN, HOW IN MY RIGHT MIND AM I GOING TO TAKE MONEY AWAY FROM THE MILITARY?!” Jordan looks down at his paper, as if he’s thinking. Jordan X Shabazz: “John Crichton was in the Military once… He’s my opponent for the Pay Per View, Gold and Glory, and you know how much I would like to have that Television title around my waist. Think about it, you could have your favorite Civil Rights Activist on Television everyday!” Colin Powell: “But I do see Jesse Jackson on television most everyday!” Jordan is shocked, and angered. Jesse Jackson, one of the financial backers for Jordan’s Civil Rights group, N.I.G.G.A. Jesse Jackson recently decided to pull the plug on Jordan’s project, though the company still ran from The Malice Crew’s money, and from the backing of Jonnie Cochran, and The Reverend Al Sharpton. Jordan X Shabazz: “Okay, I’ll let that slide buddy. I know that taking money away from the military would be hard for you to do without being caught and getting in trouble for. Because whenever shit happens they blame it on the black guy… but… Can you investigate a murder for me?” Needless to say, Jordan’s plans aren’t going too well, and his friend, Colin Powell isn’t taking these requests too well. But this one, has got to be the most ignorant one. He’s asking Colin Powell to look into a murder for him. Colin Powell: “Murder of whom?” Jordan X Shabazz: “A woman named Ms. Zemoff. I have reason to believe that at the Pay Per View, I’ll be wrestling a murderer.” Colin Powell takes off his glasses and wipes his eyes. Jordan was putting some unneeded stress on his shoulders. He should be smoking a cigarette, and partying, like all black men should. Colin sighs heavily again. Colin Powell: “What makes you think that he did it?” Jordan X Shabazz: “Well I believe it’s just too coincidental that the murder of his last lover, Aeryn, happened at the hands of this woman, Ms. Zemoff, and her husband, who’s name I have conveniently forgotten.” Colin Powell: “Jordan… I’ll see what I can do.” Jordan X Shabazz: “REALLY?!” Colin Powell: “Yes.” Jordan jumps up and down like an idiot. But his europhia is ended when Colin Powell asks him a question. Colin Powell: “Are you registered for selective service?” Now if I’m not an ignorant Canadian, I believe that Selective Service is when at the age of 18 you sign up for Selective Service with the United States government, in case there’s a war, and you need to be drafted. Jordan couldn’t lie to his friend, he just couldn’t do it. Jordan X Shabazz: “No man, I forgot. It must have been on the day of the Million Man March.” Colin Powell: “I heard rumors that you would take a bullet for somebody in the Malice Crew, would you do that for the United States?” Duke Williams had recently gotten the Malice Crew closer, and stronger with the additions of Snow and Rent A Hero. Jordan X Shabazz had a love for the Malice Crew that can only be described as the love for rednecks and beer. It was real. Jordan X Shabazz: I draft dodging, flag burnin’, and I ain’t running to Canada. You want to send me to jail? Fine, you go right ahead, I’d rather be in jail for four hundred years then go to war. I ain’t goin’ no 10,000 miles to murder and kill other poor minorities. If I want to die, I’d be like one of them crazy ass white people and jump out of a plane…. Wait… that’s not the line.” Obviously that was a play on Mohammed Ali’s famous speech about the Vietnam war. Jordan hadn’t seen the movie ‘Ali’ in a while, so he forgot the line, and improvised. Before Colin Powell could question him further, Jordan said his goodbye. Jordan X Shabazz: “Rush, let’s go, we’ve got to meet up with the boys, we’re going out for a night of fun! Colin, I’ll holler at you sometime in the future, take care.” Jordan and Rush are out the door, Jordan had achieved his goal. He had given his ideas to his good buddy Colin Powell, and he was now out to meet Rent A Hero, Snow, Jon Kano, and Duke Williams, for a night out on the town, which will be shown on this very RP board in a few days.
