Shabazz Undergoes a Serious Change (Small character trait changed....Well half gimmick change)

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/05/02 If you read the last installment of the tales of Jordan X Shabazz, you would have seen Jordan dressed as a regular black man, a street thug so to speak, you would have seen the Shabazzonator in a store called ‘Gucci’ there he disrespected a female saleswoman and riled up some of the security, in the end, Jordan was cuffed, but not arrested. N.I.G.G.A was off the ground, Jordan’s Civil Rights group had been growing. He had a good amount of employees, and he was really happy. He had been getting financial backing from Jesse Jackson, Al Sharpton, and Johnny Cochran, all of them decided to put their own personal money into Jordan’s new organization. But after Jordan X Shabazz held a heart to heart talk with one of his financial backers, Jordan has decided to undergo a change. He has called in a press conference and meeting for all of the members of N.I.G.G.A, and for the national media. He is expected to make a life-style changing decision. 20/20 called Jordan to do a follow up story, but he decided that enough was enough and it was time for a change. *** The scene opens up in an indoor and well air conditioned room. It is very large. At least twenty decent sized white tables with four chairs around them are there. They are filled with various members of the media and workers of Jordan’s N.I.G.G.A organization. On the stage stands a large podium with one chair there. Jordan X Shabazz, dressed in a baby blue suit and a baby blue top hat, sits. He also wears some reading glasses that give off the impression that Shabazz is very intelligent. He looks very serious. His usually happy face is replaced with a much more grim one. As the people settle into their chairs, Jordan gets up to the microphone and begins to speak. Jordan X Shabazz: “Ladies and gentlemen, I brought you here today for a life-style changing event that I believe you all should follow. I’ve finally realized the error of my ways, and I know what I have to do to help the black man in this day and age.” Jordan smiles at the crowd for the first time as they give him a good and warm welcome of cheers and hoots. Jordan X Shabazz: “Now I know that quite a few of you in this audience here are workers from my N.I.G.G.A organization, and I want you to know this… As of today, NO EBONICS WILL BE SPOKEN IN MY BUILDING!” A collective gasp comes from the audience as Jordan’s face has turned from one of happiness, to one of serious anger. He appears to have more. Jordan X Shabazz: “Also, there will be a dress code in place. AND NO MARIJUANA OR DRUGS WILL BE USED ON THIS PREMISES!” Jordan had always been a lax and laid back boss. He had allowed his workers to wear whatever they wanted. And he allowed drugs to be used openly. Marijuana was smoked in nearly every office, and Jordan often decided to partake in these parties. But now, he has seemed to change a little bit. Jordan X Shabazz: “Now I know you are all probably wondering ‘why Jordan, why?’ Well the reason is simple, my heroes, Malcolm X and Martin Luther King Jr, neither of those two men spoke Ebonics or wore baggy clothing. They spoke perfectly well dignified English, and they dressed as all black men and women should be dressed, like they were going to church.” Some of the old timers in the crowd and some of the people who have read about the lives of Martin Luther King Jr and Malcolm X cheer in the crowd, Jordan nods and waits for them to finish their cheering before speaking again. Jordan X Shabazz: “And I realized, that what I was doing was wrong. I portrayed the black man in a negative light. I am the head of a large organization, I’m a professional wrestler, I’m a public figure, I am seen all over the damn world… And I spoke horrible English, and I wore even worse clothing. I was a disgrace. Malcolm X and Martin Luther Jr were both turning over in their graves, the future of Civil Rights Activists was bleak.” Jordan takes a deep breath, and then a sip of some mineral water that he has bottled up. His eyes are burning with anger for some strange reason. Jordan X Shabazz: “We have fought through slavery, we have fought through discrimination, but now, we have to fight through the stereotypical ‘Gangsta’ image that plagues us. It seems as if all of the black men out here nowadays portray themselves as ‘gangsta’s.’” To add emphasis on the word ‘gangsta’ Jordan puts his hand in two mock quotations. Some of the people in the crowd are getting into the speech and they are hanging on Jordan’s every word. Jordan X Shabazz: “But these ‘gangsta’s’ go around robbing people, attacking defenseless people, and generally making us black men to look like criminals.” Jordan shakes his head vigorously, showing that he does not support ‘gangsta’s.’ Jordan X Shabazz: “In fact, next Tuesday I get a chance to show you people what I want to do to every so called ‘Gangsta’ on this planet. My former friend, a man that I used to be close with, Duzza, is one of my opponents next week. And I plan on making him pay for all the sins of the black ‘gangsta’s’ have made.” Shabazz gets angrier and angrier as the speech moves on. Jordan X Shabazz: 'The white man wants to see us acting 'thuggish' they want to have a reason they can arrest black males and beat them while in custody. They want to say it's 'Self defense.' When Jordan says the word 'Self Defense' it's obvious that he's using sarcasm. Jordan X Shabazz: 'Fuck the police, and fuck the white people. The only way we can fight these two enemies is if we use our brains, go to University, and get our education. Black people were enslaved because they were not educated. Now that we are getting educated, the white man cannot control us any longer. N.I.G.G.A will help with that. Shabazz realizes that he doesn't want to bore the crowd with more chatter, so he decides to go in for the finish and conclusion of his short speech. Jordan X Shabazz: “In closing, I just want you all to know that it’s time for a damned change. Black people need to be portrayed in a good light. Act intelligent, don’t do drugs, do wear baggy clothing. Don’t pretend to be a ‘gangsta.’ I got my degree from The University of Toronto, and I plan on using it. Changes are going into effect in this world, and I want the black man to be fully equipped in this. So everybody, with me as your leader, the black man will move to a completely new era in this day and age.” Some of the people watching break out into cheers as Jordan smiles proudly. He is dead serious about these changes apparently. Before we leave this small and short press conference Jordan X Shabazz throws his right fist in the air. Jordan X Shabazz: “FIGHT THE POWER!” The scene fades to black with photographs being taken of an angry Jordan X Shabazz.
