Still Thinking

29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 29th, 2003 It was revealed at Supremacy that Brianna was in fact the Druid, who had for several weeks, driven Dorn up a wall and back. She proclaimed her dislike for his persecution of people, and for supporting a murderer such as Violence Jack. This has now made life another challenge. Since Dorn knows who she is, he is going to be gunning for her to repay the torture she bestowed upon him. The scene opens up to that of the soothing sounds of the Beatles belting out “Yesterday”. The camera comes to life revealing Brianna sitting motionless on her living room couch. We can see the fading bruises on her face from the punches delivered by Dorn after their Supremacy match, and a cut on her lip, which is on it’s way to healing. Even though she’s not having her best of days regarding looks, she still manages a smile, somewhat creepy albeit. “Yesterday….all my troubles seemed so far away..” she belts out in tune as she stares at apparently nothing. Brianna continues to sing moving around somewhat to break her motionless stance. A minute later exactly, the song cuts off and all the sounds in the room are driven to silence. Brianna stands up and walks to the stereo located on the far right wall. She stands there and starts to laugh, almost maniacally. “This should be Dorn’s new theme song….he thought that after Supremacy things had shifted in his favor. What a highly optimistic point of view coming from such an evil human being. In actuality the ball never left my court. It’s now just have and half. He makes empty threats and I mess with him…simple as that!” She is obviously in the know of things Dorn has said and continues to express her feelings on them as though nothing else really matters. “On Turmoil I proved that it wouldn’t be the end. And itching powder should be the least of Dorn’s problems as I have much much more packed up my sleeves. It will all be unraveled slowly and once again Dorn will surge with questions like he already has, and it will all build up ultimately driving him insane.” Brianna swings around on that note and is startled at seeing Milo standing there staring at her with a raised brow. “Were you talking to yourself?” he asks quizzically. “No! Well yeah I guess you could say I was, but it was more….oh I can’t explain it.” “Anyway…I got a call while I was on the phone with Antoine and it was PCW executives.” “What did they want?” “They were just calling to give you your schedule for the week. On Friday you are up against SOCIETY and Turmoil you’re off yet again.” he says as though he were used to being the messenger boy, much to his dislike. “SOCIETY?” Brianna says trailing off a bit worried. “Yuppers. Hey do you mind if I borrow your car? Antoine’s car is in the shop and we really wanted to check out this new bar downtown.” Milo steps towards Brianna with an outstretched hand, waiting for car keys to occupy the space. “You know this would be so much easier if you got a job and bought your own car.” “What’s mine is yours Bri. And vice versa.” “Says you.” Brianna retorts. “I can’t help it I can’t keep a job long enough to save up money. If people weren’t so ignorant I would have my own car by now.” “If you would stop being so opinionated then you would be able to keep a job!” Milo places his well-manicured hands on his hips staring at Brianna who is rearranging the disks on top of the stereo. “Well they should hear me out. It would make their lives a lot better.” Milo pouts like a little kid. “Think about it. At that marketing agency you were fired because you called the lead executive a moron all because of his clothing! Now how ridiculous do you honestly think that is?” “He should know that green and red contrasts creating an ugly sight for the eyes. You don’t have to be a fashion genius to know that!” He replies flailing around his arms. Not wanting to go on anymore, Brianna fishes out her keys and throws them to Milo who barely manages to catch them. “Thank you.” “Just remember, you make a mess, you clean it up. No more beer cans and other junk!” “Gotcha Bri. I’ll be back by midnight.” Milo high steps out of the house and Brianna remains by the stereo placing loose cd’s in their proper cases. “SOCIETY huh? A monster of a man taking on little ol’ me? It’s gonna get ugly…” The scene slowly fades out. Brianna has to worry about The Conformist on Fusion. She’s going to have to focus all energy and strength on the behemoth before she can even worry about the astronomer. But how can she tackle the big man all by herself? He’s yet another man that Brianna has dislike for due to his oppression on people to conform to his beliefs and followings. Will this factor be fuel enough to drive her to victory? It sure would be an upset for the 138 pounder to defeat a man almost two times her weight.
