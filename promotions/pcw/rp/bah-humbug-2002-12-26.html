Bah Humbug

26 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Bah Humbug Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 26/12/02 “Grrrrrrrrrrrr.... I should sue Christianity for severe annoyance...” Our shot opens up on the Scrooge-esque face of PCW CEO Xavier Kannon as he stands looking out of the floor to ceiling windows of his office at the main PCW building in Atlantic City, New Jersey. Xavier : “At least it only comes but once a year.” Behind him, through the open door of his darkened sanctuary, the PCW Christmas Eve Office Party is in full swing with moronic party music, seasonal singing and the odd cheer sneaking through Xavier’s ears. He snarls at the commotion behind him, something only worsened by the group of Carol Singers who appear at the foot of the building’s steps and burst into song. “GOOD KING WENCELESNESS LOOKED OUT, ON THE FEAST OF STEPHEN....” His eyes roll as those downstairs filter out to watch the spectacle of 10 or so not that great singers bursting into song while wrapped up warm in the bitter night. The magic of Christmas is lost on the Scientologist who only admires the Capitalist machine that runs it. Xavier : “Scientology REALLY needs a holiday season, so much money going to these peons that we’re missing out on.” His attention is then grabbed by a loud noise from the hallway outside his office, the thudding of feet in stereo. “COME ON AND DO THE CONGA, COME ON AND DO THE CONGA....” A row of PCW employees, led by the Pimply Faced Intern Kid dressed as one of Santa’s Elves makes it’s way through the door to his office and does a lap of Xavier’s space before heading back into the brightly lit hallway, leaving streamers and party poppers strewn over the Scientologist’s Red carpet. Xavier : “Peons.” His head shakes as the last of the Conga train leaves his office, feet kicking either side in time to the music being blasted from the open plan office just down the hall from his. The bright Green eyes roll back into his head while he surveys the mess. Xavier : “I’d be getting the Pink slips out now if I could actually remember any of their names.” Slumping back in his large Black leather chair, Xavier reaches over and plucks a file off his cluttered desk, the top sheet of which is marked all over in Red ink, circles around figures in the ‘Expenses’ column. Xavier : “I just hope these morons buy our merchandise this Christmas, something needs to pay for these new offices.” The hold-ups on the new PCW Hubbardville offices seem to be spiralling out of control. Vast sums of money to architects and building contractors are marked with Red, and the usually clean brow of the Scientologist is furrowed. Xavier : “Time to ‘transfer’ some funds...” A trademark smirk crosses his face before he turns to the door and bellows. Xavier : “PIMPLYYYYYYYYY!!!!!!” As his booming voice echoes around the office, he screws up the top sheet and throws it over his right shoulder towards the corner of the room where it ricochets off the 2 walls then drops into the trashcan, clipping the side on it’s descent. Xavier : “Time to put this spotty little moron to use...” He turns and faces the door, waiting for the Intern to arrive. A scurrying is heard in the corridor above the distant party music before the PFIK bounds through the door. Sighing, the Scientologist shakes his head and mutters. Xavier : “What in Xenu’s name do you look like...” The tired looking Pimply is decked out in a Red and Green velvet Elf costume complete with a hat that a Gold bell hangs off. His cheeks are rosy from doing the Conga and his entire body is dotted with streamers. PFIK: “Thanks for the great office party, Mr. Kannon, sir.... I think Cynthia from Accou-.” Suddenly the sly look on the CEO’s face turns to anger, the Intern looking as if he’s just put his foot in it. PFIK: “Oooops, what did I say? Was I meant to call you Gold patron Meritorious?” Still, Xavier doesn’t speak, his face turning a deeper shade of Red as his teeth grind. Xavier : “I paid for you worthless morons to act like retards in the bad music section of Peons ‘R’ Us?” Taking a few steps back, the Intern nervously answers. PFIK : “Um.... Er.... Mr. Duke said to have a great time for Christmas and pay for everything on your account to reward all our hard wor-... Oh, he lied, didn’t he?” Slowly, and not speaking, Xavier nods at PFIK’s question, the Intern continuing to backtrack towards the door while Kannon continues to snarl with burning Green eyes looking bloodshot. PFIK : “I think I should.... Er.... Maybe go and cancel the $1000 Santa ice sculpture if it isn’t too late...” The fists of the CEO clench so tightly that his thumb nails go White as the door slowly closes shut behind Pimply and only a whisper escape from between the locked teeth of Xavier. Xavier : “Grrrrrrr... Somehow, some way, Duke has to go.... No more meddling, no more tricking.... No mo-” As he quietly rants, the door creaks open again and his head shoots up, angry face yelling at the door he thinks the PFIK is sneaking back in through. Xavier : “STAY OUT UNTIL YOU FIND A WAY TO CHARGE DUKE FOR ALL THAT CHRISTMAS SHIT!!!” A voice speaks, slightly taken aback, from behind the door. “Well, like, SO Merry Christmas to you too and stuff!” The frustrated look on the CEO’s face melts as he hears the voice of his wife, PCW’s Commissioner Ellie~! She pokes her head out from behind the door showing she’s wearing a Red velvet Santa hat with White furry trim atop her mane of long Blonde hair. Xavier : “Not you my little Poon Puppy, sorry, come in...” A smile on her face, the little prep skips festively into the room showing she has matching Red and White mini-dress, long gloves and thigh boots to go with the hat. Kannon perks up and she leaps into his arms. Eleanor : “Merry Christmas, Xavikins!” Despite his loathing of the festival, Kannon can’t help smiling as Ellie~! starts to nibble away at his neck. Xavier : “Merry Christmas to you too, my little Fuck Ferret. Reaching around him, Ellie~! hangs from his neck as she looks into his eyes with her Bambi-esque Blue pair, a cheeky grin on her face that sparkles in Red glitter. Eleanor : “So.... Do I get an early Christmas present?” Looking right back at her, he tilts his head apologetically. Xavier : “They’re all back at the appa-” Her middle finger, decorated with Red nail varnish crosses his lips while her teasing eyes look to his desk as she giggles. Xavier : “-...and I think I can let you unwrap something a little early....” His frosty Scrooge-like visage dropped, the CEO backs Ellie~! up to the desk where he sits her on the edge and the shot fades to black on the preppy Commissioner being slowly pushed backwards onto the wood and papers as the Carol Singers outside continue their song on the street below. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
