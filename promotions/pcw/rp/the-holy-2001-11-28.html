The Holy

28 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/28/01 Awake to asleep.......movement to still All along the way huddle the crowded masses.............lined up one by one, two by two. In seats carved from wood, their expressions hidden by shadows......... As we glance around it becomes unmistakable that we are in a church.........along the walls various depictions of our Lord and Long Dead Savior Jesus Christ hang. Though he may be moldering in the dirt amazingly enough his sheep still gather........ And gather they have. Always on the same day, always to the same place........entering this “holy” abode. Worshiping little more than a hippie peace lover.........who somehow convinced the world he was a God. No idea of where the true power lies. The true power lies here on the Earth. The Devil owns it. What’s more, the Devil is right in front of your face. From somewhere hidden, outside the church a light shines. The sun brings it’s illumination through a large stained glass window located above the altar......breaking apart the shadows and revealing the faces of the congregation for the first time. Panic stricken........ropes bind them, holding them to their seats. A truly captive audience. One by one we glance over their faces........man, woman, child.........slowly, ever so slowly. In the background an organ grinds it’s haunting melody........dark and foreboding. And eventually, we reach the front of the church........behind the pulpit stands a nightmare brought to life. Clyde: Worshipers. The demon smiles at his gathered flock........smiles his terrible, tainted smile. Full of evil humor........he knows what they think. That part of the gift never left him..........in fact it is stronger than ever. He feels their confusion. He thrives on it. Clyde: I know why you are here, sheep. All your thoughts........all your dark desires. They float unbidden through my soul........ The burned man holds his head........ Clyde: I can’t make them stop. Your worthless, petty thoughts.........I truly believed it was a gift at first, to know all your desires.........the easier to prey on you. But no.......... Clyde slams his fist on the pulpit, garnering slight shrieks from the gathered. Too frightened to do much more, he discerns....... Clyde: You worthless vagabonds. Your inanity drives me MAD! Again the demon brings his fist down.........again, again. Splinters dig into his flesh........blood seeps down, staining the church floor. After a brief moment though, he smiles once more......... Clyde: But I digress. Do you kind folks know the true reason you’ve come here today? The burned man looks about........from face, to face, to face. Inside all of their minds........searching. Searching for a flaw a sign. Something......... And there it is. In a young girl.......the thought passed through her head. Faith. The thought that she came here to be closer to God. Clyde: BAH! In this seat on the front row, the young lady’s eyes grow wide. All she did was think........she didn’t say anything........ It doesn’t matter. In a flash the demon is in front of her.........his eyes are glowing. Glowing red. The fires of Hell burn behind those eyes........not human. Utterly not human. The blaring of the organ grows faster.........faster.........more maniacal. Reaching a fever pitch........and then......... ..........he smiles. Clyde looks to the girl with tender affection. Gently he cuts through her ropes with his long, sharp and dirty fingernails. Freeing her........ Clyde: Arise, my child. Abruptly the organ music ceases.........he takes her hand. Up they go, in front of the onlookers who have long since ceased to attempt to find any logic in this horror they have been made privy to. All they can do is watch.......... The pair reaches the pulpit. With a somehow sweet smile, Clyde bows deeply.........kisses the girl’s hand. As she stares at him with wide eyes he speaks........... Clyde: You are somehow different from the rest. The girl says nothing.........no sound emerges. In fact no thought passes her mind........but it does not upset Clyde. Clyde: Look at the sheep out there, woman. The young lady does as asked........looks out to them. Out on their faces........their faces shocked into numbness. Clyde: All of them.......they came here originally to worship their false messiah. But as soon as I took them out of their routine what did they do.........where did their thoughts go. Can you guess? The woman looks blankly.........then finally shakes her head no. Clyde: I will tell you then. As soon as things started to stray from the original plan their thoughts went away from their God..........went immediately to their own selfish thoughts. Their own means of escape....... These last few words are spit from the burned man’s mouth..........full of disgust. Clyde: The one who saved them.......brought them from the darkness. Held them up to the light and rescued them from a life of pure torment. The burned man sneers. Clyde: Or so he would like them to believe. Suddenly Clyde takes the girl’s hand.........to the melody of the ever present organ he leads her down the steps and into the congregation again. Clyde: Everywhere are people like them. It eats at me........take this one for example. Clyde kneels next to a middle aged and rather chubby man.........touches his cheek, feels him tremble in terror. Clyde: This one........he only came here because his wife forced him to do so. No faith........and as soon as I turned the tables he betrayed his master. Onward........through the aisle, to an elderly woman. Clyde: You..........oh my dear I’m glad you can’t read her thoughts. No she actually came here because she finds the preacher sexually attractive. The burned man clutches his forehead with laughter........tears stream from his eyes, red as blood. He falls to one knee, still holding the hand of the lass who looks at him peculiarly. Clyde: Can you imagine..........this old whore, lusting after the tub of goo preacher? Can you imagine? Oh ye of little faith. The elderly woman begins to say something, but the words die in her mouth as Clyde snaps his head up and glares at her. Clyde: You too.........you have no faith. No faith in me. With this odd choice of words, the demon marches back up to the altar fairly dragging his unwitting accomplice behind him. Up behind the pulpit they go again..........reaching the somehow odd blank wall at the rear of the church. Clyde looks to his right.........upon the ever familiar face of the Virgin Mary. Her look of solemn grace a beacon........... Clyde: Faith. Family. One and the same...........neither are real. The burned man looks at the girl.........looks through her. Doesn’t really see her..........yet he speaks. Speaks to no one and to everyone........... Clyde: I had a family once. Yes even a demon must have kin...........and what a brood. A true Dark Age we were to bring.........and just when we were on the cusp......... Clyde snaps his fingers. Clyde: Just like that. Right when we were on the verge of bringing the whole rotten place to it’s knees.........one of my own sabotaged us. Flames seem to leak from Clyde’s eyes.........he seethes as he breathes one word. Clyde: Darkstar. The demon whips the girl around to face the blank wall...........suddenly it changes shape. Growing into a black void.........matter swirls and is vacuumed into it. He takes her in his arms, leaps through.......... in this vortex she sees she cannot move but she sees her body is here, but she is not she looks up she sees his face on top of her the grinning demon so full of hate breaking her never ending years pass an eternity passes no relief shame stop Suddenly the girl is back in the church.........tears run from her eyes. Her clothes stand torn and tattered..........but she knows. Knows instinctively the worst is yet to come..........the demon stands over her cackling as the organ music takes on a maniacal pounding rhythm. Without cadence. Without sense or logic. Clyde: (Sneering) He succeeded in the first part of his goal, did Darkstar........he ripped our Satanic coven apart from the inside out. Where once there was darkness.........he brought LIGHT. He allowed COMPASSION to enter the sheep! He brought GOODNESS! He brought PEACE! At this point the demon shouts his words, each exclamation augmented by another mad pound of the infernal organ. The girl attempts to tear away but his grip is like iron. Then her look of mortification takes on another shade........one of complete and undying morbid confusion. The statue of the Virgin Mary begins to cry. More specifically, it begins to cry blood. It turns to face her.........in that moment her sanity is torn from her mind in a single, terrible swipe. She watches and giggles absently as the statue comes to life..........awkward at first, as if it is not accustomed to it’s newfound freedom. Then slowly it begins to walk.........out towards the congregation. Clyde: (Laughing) Goodness.........peace. Darkstar.........you took what we had accomplished and ground it underfoot for your own selfish desires. The Virgin stomps out to the followers..........as the poor girl watches on in insane bemusement it grabs them and one by one tears their flesh from their bones. Clyde: But the past is the past.........you cannot correct things. You cannot save yourself.........you have sealed your own fate. Blood cascades across the church floor as the statue of Mary rips the bound parishioners to shreds. The organ blares ever louder, the music of Hell...........finally the young lady looks to it. Her eyes bulge as she sees a tiny little girl spring off the keyboard, firing a last mash of noise into the hectic air. As the demon child leaps upon her in a flailing of teeth and claws, the demon looks upon it all and laughs. Clyde: The Dark Age has just begun. In the rain of blood and the laughter of one far, far beyond the realm of sanity, we mercifully fade to black...........
