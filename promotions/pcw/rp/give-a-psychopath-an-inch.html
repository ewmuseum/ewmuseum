Date: not entered 'Mommy! Mommy! Look! Its Daddy!' Sharon snapped out of her fugue, shaking her head. She looked down, and the macaroni water was boiling. The elbow noodles were settled at the bottom of the pan, and she stirred them with the plastic spoon, letting them swirl around, floating up towards the roiling surface of the water, then sinking in a circular pattern to the bottom. 'What, honey?' Sharon asked her five year old daughter, Carrie, who was in the living room, watching television. 'Mommy! Daddy's on television!' she shouted. Sharon stirred the noodles once more, then wiped her hands with a dish towel, walking into the living room, where Carrie was perched on the coffee table, watching wrestling for some Godawful reason. 'Honey, get off the coffee table.' she admonished, but Carrie was too engrossed with the television. Sharon walked closer to the set, and saw... Saw him... He was wrestling some black woman. He wore the same white shirt and tie he always wore to work - that is, before they fired him. They wrestled in that ring, a PCW logo on the bottom corner of the screen. People, voiceovers, were making smartass comments. Carrie looked at her mother joyfully. 'Daddy's on television, mommy! Look! He's like The Rock! He's wrestling, mommy!' Sharon stood there, the dishtowel curling between her fists. That fucker, she thought. Don't tell me he doesn't have money for child support... Sharon turned and went back into the kitchen, not wanting to look at his face anymore. Bernard. The worst mistake she ever made... Possessive, psychopathic, jealous... Bernard was a monster. Thank God she got out of that marriage when she did. She may have done it in a less than honorable way, but it was done and over with. She just wanted that bastard out of her life. Yes, she cheated on him. What do you expect? He was horrible in bed. He was repulsive, unattractive... He was more concerned with having a family, something to fill his empty life, more concerned with that than keeping her happy. She was 17 when she was married, for goodness sake... He was a Staff Sergeant at Fort Bragg. She thought it was sexy... back then. But when he left the Army, any attraction she had for him died. He was sick in the head... He reminded her too much of her own father, a wife-beater and a drunk. Bernard didn't drink or hit her... but the similarities were too close for her comfort. He never let her go out. He was insanely jealous. He never let her go out and have fun with her friends, go to bars and just forget the stress of being a young mother. She loved Carrie, but just turning 18 and having a 4-month old child, that was stressful. He would always question where she had been when she would stumble in at two in the morning, which was perfectly acceptable. The bars closed at 2, she left about an hour early anyway. Maybe if she had gotten home at dawn, yes, that would be acceptable, acceptable for him to be concerned, but what the hell, you know? He was there for the baby. Why can't she have some fun before her youth is gone? And besides, it was her life. Having a baby is his responsibility too. And he was a psycho... Always going on about how America is going to shit, and how she should stop buying R&B albums, how they were 'encouraging a lifestyle of drugs, sex, racial stereotypes' and blah, blah, blah. She liked the sound of it, why shouldn't she use the money she got - it was both of theirs, really - to buy something she liked, lilke music? Everything he makes was half of hers, too. That's what marriages are for. So what if she used some of the paycheck on the bars and albums? Its not like they were starving anyway, and shit, what's a couple of CDs a month gonna hurt? He was racist, she knew it everytime he came home and she was watching BET on the couch. He would bitch at her for not having the house clean or not having dinner ready, or not changing Carrie's diaper. If Carrie was dirty, she would let me know, you know? It's like he stepped out of the '50s or '60s, all stuffy and asshole-like. He fucked her, he got her pregnant, and he married her. If he didn't like her tastes in music or what she did for fun, why did he marry her? But it was better when Henrique, one of the guys in Bernard's old unit, came by while Bernie was at work (he had a job at some contractor job on base). Henrique was Hispanic, very tasty. And he was fun, too. Great in bed. He would make her call him Papi, and he would call her Mami while he fucked her. He was fun, and for a couple months, he would take off during lunch and fuck her at her house, almost everyday. From 11:30 till one, he would fuck the shit out of her, and she loved it. But then Bernard had to fuck that up to. He forced her hand to get a divorce. How the hell is a woman supposed to live with a psychopath like that, spying on her and shit? What kind of psycho is he? Fuck him. Bernard is a psycho. And now that he's a wrestler, I'm gonna get more Child Support, she thought as she stirred the macaroni. Suddenly, outside, a loud bang. She looked out the window, and LuAnn's husband, Cyrus, was leaving the trailer in a huff. Hmpf, she thought. Another asshole. Men are pigs. She continues to stir the macaroni. Like a good mother, cooking. She smiles. A good mother.
