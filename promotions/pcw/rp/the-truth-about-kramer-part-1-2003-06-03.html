The Truth About Kramer (part 1)

3 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/3/03 The glass glistened in a shimmer of light, as Brianna lifted it to her lips to take a sip. Not used to have any form of liquor in her system, Brianna happily gulps away enjoying every last drop. She takes her eyes off the glass momentarily to look up at her partner sharing the table, Kramer Rinc, who looks quite handsome in the dim lighting with a clean shaven face and his short, flowing brown hair, which has evidently been cut. There they sit, quietly, in the booming night club, not really taking notice to the people around them. 'Great band.' Brianna throws out trying to make small talk. 'They have a nice pop rockish sound.' Kramer replies looking toward the stage where three men in their twenties, wearing baggy clothes, bang away on their guitars and drums. 'So...it was really nice of you to take me here. I'm either always at home slaving away in my lab, or traveling for PCW shows. I honestly can't remember the last time I've been to a club.' 'It's good to get away from all the stress every now and then.' “It’s weird though.” Brianna throws out readjusting her black skirt under the table. “What is?” Kramer asks looking dead at her, twirling his glass in the air, before sucking out the remaining liquid. “Well it’s been weeks since I hear from you yet again, and all of a sudden you just pop up and offer to take me to a club.” Kramer looks at her for a few more seconds before waving down a waitress to ask for another scotch on the rocks. Brianna passes on a second glass and goes back to staring at Kramer, awaiting an answer. “Things have been complicated, and I just needed to break away from it.” “Break away from what?” “Well I guess it doesn’t matter coming out in the clear as I am now starting a new life, putting behind all of the secret service crap.” The last statement catches Brianna’s ear. “Secret service? Your involved with the FBI?” “Technically, not anymore, but yeah I was a fed. The stress level was just constantly building and I couldn’t handle it anymore so I resigned.” Brianna looks at Kramer for a minute examining him. He sits their nonchalantly and sips away at his new glass of scotch that the young waitress has just brought back upon his request. “That’s not the reason.” Kramer looks up at Brianna. “How can you be so sure?” “I’m a woman, I know these things.” Upon saying this, both share a laugh as Kramer rubs at the back of his neck nervously looking around. “Does this have anything to do with those mob guys a long while back?” Brianna asks intrigued. “Those bozos? No. All of them are locked up securely in their own jail cells making some new male friends.” “Sounds like something Milo would love. I know he’d drop the soap instantly.” Again they share a laugh before Kramer straightens up finishing his second glass of scotch. “The reason I quit….was…..well it was because of you.” Brianna stares bewildered at Kramer, thinking this to be another joke. She slowly comes to realize that his eyes are full of sincerity. “Me? I’m afraid I don’t understand.” “It all started that one day we accidentally met and I left Grimer with you. Since then we’ve gotten really close and I have found that I have more than a friendly attraction to you. I can’t get you out of my head, and I can honestly say I’ve never felt this way before. Being in the agency I barely ever got a chance to see your beautiful face and I couldn’t take it.” He cuts off giving Brianna a chance to take this all in, awaiting her response to the whole ordeal. “I guess I’m kind of flattered, but….” “But?” Kramer chimes in eagerly. Before Brianna has a chance to reply the scene fades to black leaving us at suspense, or something of that nature…..
