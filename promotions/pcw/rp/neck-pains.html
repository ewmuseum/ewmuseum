Neck Pains....

Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin had discovered the art of cracking the muscles in his neck.But one crack too many was enough to ensure pain and suffering.How is PCW’s most ‘hardcore-est’ wrestler going to deal with this shooting pain in the neck? Watch and learn…. [Scene opens along a dusty suburban street.With rundown houses lining both sides of the street and prostitutes rubbing up against lampposts, Flaymin walks down the street looking at a business card in his hand. After walking past a number of houses, Flaymin comes to a stop outside a particular house.Flaymin looks at the business card in his hand once again, before nodding his head in approval] Flaymin: Yep, this is the place. [Flaymin puts the card in his pocket and walks up the driveway of the shabby house. He looks around and notices a bunch of rusted cars scattered all over the front lawn as well as a number of strange holes that have been dug all over the place. Nevertheless, Flaymin walks over the top of the holes and knocks on the door. A moment later a man dressed in tight lycra (like a professional cyclist) answers the door] Lycra Man: Can I help you? Flaymin: I’m looking for Barry. Lycra Man: That would be me. Flaymin: You’re the chiropractor? Barry: Assassin… Chiropractor- it’s all good.I’m multi-talented! And you would be? Flaymin: Flaymin Aymin.I was referred to you by a mutual friend. [Barry clicks his fingers together] Barry: Right, now I remember!Come right in and we’ll talk business. Flaymin: What business?I just want a neck rub? Barry: Heh, Heh, you can call it what ever you want. [Flaymin shrugs his shoulders and walks into the house. He takes a seat in the living room as Barry walks over to the windows and closes the blinds. Barry then sits down on the couch and speaks to Flaymin] Barry: Right, now that this place is totally secure you can say whatever you wanted to say. Flaymin: I just want a rub!! Barry: Absolutely.Who would you like me to rub out? Flaymin: Oh, it’s just a pain in the neck. Barry: Yep, we get a lot of those.Do you have a photo of this ‘pain in the neck’? [Flaymin stares at Barry with a perplexed look on his face] Flaymin: Why would I have a photo? Barry: How else would I identify the victim? Flaymin: IT’S THE CIRCLE THINGY HOLDING UP MY HEAD!! Barry: A circle thing holding up a head?You mean you want me to hang the person? [Barry momentarily strokes his chin] Barry: Gee, I haven’t used a noose in a while but I’m sure I can work something out. Flaymin: No, no, its just a shooting pain in the neck. Barry: Shooting!Now you’re speaking my language!What are we talking here-AK47?Or would just prefer the good ol’ silencer placed on a pistol? [Confused by all this murdering talk, Flaymin turns around and points to his neck] Flaymin: Can you see this? Barry: Your finger? Flaymin: No, my neck! Barry: Oh right, gotcha! Flaymin: Well that’s what I want you to rub.That freaking shooting pain in my neck is what I came here for. Barry: You want me to whack you? Flaymin: Whack me, rub me, do whatever the hell you guys do to make this pain go away. [Barry, gets up and walks over to a nearby wardrobe.He opens the door and pulls out some black gloves and places them on his hands. Next, he reaches into the lower realms of the wardrobe and takes out a black suitcase] Barry: Are you sure you want me to do this? Flaymin: Yes, yes, do whatever you can to make the pain go away. [Barry opens the suitcase and pulls out a sawn-off shotgun.He loads it with two bullets and then closes the bullet holster back into place] Barry: By the way, this will cost you $800. [Flaymin slams his fists on the sofa armrest in shock] Flaymin: 800 dollars for a freaking neck rub!?!?!?I don’t think even God charges that much! Barry: Well you’ll be able to ask him yourself in just a couple of minutes….But besides that, this will be a high quality hit.I’ll make sure its quick and fast and that you won’t feel a thing. Flaymin: Fair enough. Barry: Ok, just lean your head against the sofa and relaaaaaax. [Barry gently pushes Flaymin’s head down onto the armrest of the sofa.With his free hand he places the pistol on the back of Flaymin’s head] Flaymin: Hey that feels nice, but the pain is in my neck, not my head. Barry: I’m sorry, I just can’t do this!! [Flaymin raises his head in surprise] Flaymin: Why not?Just try and give it your best shot! Barry: No, no, I just bought this sofa from Ikea three weeks ago.Do you know how hard it is to get bloodstains out of Swedish furniture?? Flaymin: Don’t worry, I’m hardcore. Barry: We’re going to have to do this outside.My mother will kill me if she’s that I’ve tracked blood into the house.Sometimes she can be a real pain in the neck, herself. [Flaymin rubs his neck soothingly] Flaymin: I know exactly what you mean. [Flaymin and Barry walk out into the backyard. Suddenly in the corner of his eye Flaymin notices the gun in Barry’s hand and begins to freak out] Flaymin: Whoah!!What the hell is that!?!? [Barry looks at his pistol and then back up at Flaymin] Barry: It’s the gun I was going to use to remove the neck pain…. Flaymin: And blow off my f*cking head in the process??? Barry: Hmm.. I never looked at it that way, but you are quite right. Flaymin: Damn straight. Barry: Since I’ll be doing to jobs at the same time I think I’ll have to charge you twice the price. [Flaymin’s eyes boggle open and slowly he begins to step away from the lycra-wearing assassin] Flaymin: I…. Uh… think I’ll be going now…. [Barry sadly empties the bullets out of the gun] Barry: Suit yourself, but if you decide that you want me to finish the job, I’ve just upped my price to $2000. [Flaymin gazes at Barry with a stunned look on his face] Barry: You ok, sonny? [Without uttering another word, Flaymin dashes out of the house, but along the way he trips over the carpet and slams his head into the floor. Flaymin grabs his neck and then begins to grin in astonishment] Flaymin: The pain!…. Its gone!!!I can see the light again! [Barry walks into the room and whips out a calculator] Barry: Lets see….You ran through my house and tripped over MY carpet which means you still owe me money for my services. Flaymin: You’ve gotta be kidding me! [Barry whips out his rifle and aims it at Flaymin’s head] Barry: Sure I’m kidding…. Just tell it to Mr AK 47….. [Scene fades to black]
