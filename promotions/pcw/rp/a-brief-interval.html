A Brief interval...

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Tuesday evening... (The scene begins to fade in from black as a voiceover soon grows perceptible…) Voiceover, ominously: “The following recording was captured backstage after the March 11th edition of PCW Tuesday Turmoil…” (He marches back and forth across the room, his hands tightly concealed behind his back and the occasional sick, deluded grin flashing in and out across his lips. He murmurs words of an age long language under his breath sharply and sometimes grasps at his skull in a forceful manner. His body is still strewn with sweat from his match earlier, and the presence of now dried tears lay evident across his cheeks.) The Pharaoh: “Damn him…” (Coming to an abrupt stop in front of the regulation wardrobe mirror, The Pharaoh flashes a painful smirk and drops to his knees, his eyes sorrowful and distant.) The Pharaoh: “Damn him.” (Once again, like the previous two encounters we, the audience, have had with Titu Xansiv, he appears not at all in a fit state of mind.) The Pharaoh, angrier: “Damn him!” (He beats his fists into the carpet and begins to laugh a quite pitiful chuckle. His eyes well up with ears once more and his hands find his face, clawing at the skin as though troubled by an intense rash.) The Pharaoh: “DAMN HIM!” (The pain he feels is evident now. He scowls, gritting his teeth. He punches the ground until his knuckles are raw. …He stops, looks around, and curls up, an unseen darkness consuming him.) The Pharaoh, quietly: “…Damn…him…” (Seconds turn to minutes and minutes soon turn to hours. He lays there, unmoving as countless clerks shuffle into his locker to tell him of PCW’s departure from Vegas. He cares not for their ever-wearier words…he cares about only one thing…Clyde.) Voice of The Pharaoh: “Look what you’ve done to me. Look at what you have MADE me become. Take a GOOD look, demon, and LAUGH. Ever since our paths crossed at Shang’heid you’ve been out to get me, and congratulations, Clyde, you’ve FINALLY gained what you desired. The past weeks have been unbearable. You have broken me like no one else, and now I give you a chance to take a good long stare at what you have achieved…I feel that I have no sanity left. The plagues have troubled me beyond any wrestling, fiend… You have snapped me; manipulated me; played my mind to your advantage, and spliced my grasp on reality. …But I won’t give up, demon. I won’t let you overcome me at March Madness, oh no! I won’t let you destroy the first-born, I won’t allow you to pin me after everything you’ve done, and I won’t permit your evil! I shan’t ALLOW any of it! You’ll FEEL my wrath, demon! You’ll feel the WRATH of the Gods!” (His eyes sore, his skin dry, and his voice cracked, he sits up, staring into space. He gapes directly at us, unable, it would seem, to say a single word. But we are soon found misjudging…) The Pharaoh: “You make me sick. You make me visibly ILL! You have done everything there is to trouble me…but I’ll have my revenge. I’ll give you your comeuppance! I’ll grind you down and play you just as you have me! No longer shall this go on…” (He gains his footing and edges across the room, taking a seat upon a rickety bench in the far left-hand corner. The cameras view him from a distance, his face facing the cold wall in front of him and his voice as drone-like as it was a moment ago. He seems not to watch the world from the normal person’s view, but from his own personal psychologically dysfunctional outlook.) The Pharaoh: “You may be the only one who KNOWS me now, Clyde, but I aren’t LIKE you. I never will be! I’ll never be as fucked up as you are. NO! I shan’t act in the strange ways you do – the purely EVIL ways you do. I know now why the wrestlers here fear you, demon. They’re as scared as I am going into our match this Sunday EVERY day. But I’ve seen you firsthand, Clyde. I KNOW what you’re capable of. I know you’re THE sickest MONSTER in this disturbing void and—” (He pauses, looking on.) The Pharaoh: “—I know that no matter how disturbed you are, I can better you come the PPV.” (As before, another clerk knocks upon the door and enters quickly. He seems surprised to see Pharaoh sat upon the bench and splutters a little. He stutters some more, coughs it off and eventually begins to speak.) Clerk: “This is the last time, Titu…we HAVE to go. (To Titu his voice is barely audible…) Clerk: “Mr. Xansiv! We must be off. Make haste!” (Without a word, Pharaoh gets up, grabs his pre-packed bag by his side and moves towards the locker room’s exit. He pushes past the man and walks on down the hall, his eyes still dreamy and cold…oh so cold.) The Pharaoh: “And after you, demon…after you, that title doesn’t look such a bad thing, either. The Gods themselves are with me this Sunday, and with the power of Ra I shall vanquish ALL who oppose me! It doesn’t matter how much I feel the fear, Clyde; it’s how well I’ll overcome it which counts.” (And with these words, the scene fades, the clerk looking on perplexed with Titu’s sudden motivation…)
