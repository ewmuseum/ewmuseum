Undercover Brother, IncogNEGRO [Chapter 2. Getting Settled... And Getting Discovered...]

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/20/02 If you missed Part 1, let me give you a rundown right quick. First, Jordan was brought into the offices of Debbie Barker. A very important and well paid NAACP official, decided to give Jordan the job of head man in a small group of people that were to sabotage a bunch of racist groups in the dirty south. Jordan was all up for the challenge and he even found himself a backup partner, Rush Mason. When we last left Jordan X Shabazz, he was standing in a Greyhound Bus Station. That's where our scene reopens. Jordan stands, wearing baggy khaki Phat Farm shorts, some brown, untied, Lugz boots, a white Pure Playaz t-shirt, and a black Ecko visor. Jordan left home with the bare essentials to survive on a road trip. Clothes, drinks, and Discman. That's about it. So as Jordan stands, pondering whether to walk the few feet over to the Arab owned shop to buy some cigarettes and food... But before he can, the darkest of the dark, the blackest of the black, the man who puts the TA in Tar, former NTICW Television Champion, and former NTICW tag teag Champion, 'The Rush' Rush Mason, appears with a grin on his face. Hell, I'm sure if he wasn't smiling Jordan wouldn't know that he was even there. 'Darkmaster Flex!' Rush says with a smile to the man he considers his apprentice. 'Great to see you.' Rush drops his bags at Jordan's feet and he embrances Jordan in a Malice Crew 'What's up my nigga.' type of hug. Their is some dead air. Almost as if Rush is trying to get Jordan to take his bags with him to the car, and knowing Jordan's past with Xavier Kannon, Rush actually thinks Jordan will. 'So, aren't you going to take my bags to the car?' Rush asks seriously. 'F^ck no. What are you trying to do? Put the blacks back to slavery?' Rush laughs as he picks up his bags. Jordan leads him to the small Arab owned shop that smells of curry and has numerous proud banners of their God in the background. Jordan goes into his pocket and reaches for his money. The Arab man begins screaming. 'You Mothervucker! You want to bust cap in ass? NO! I call Nine-Eleven! POLICE! HELP!' The Arab man screams to some of the police officers roaming the area. The officers immediately walk over to the shop and spot the flabbergasted Jordan X Shabazz, and the laughing his ass off Rush Mason. The officer is a rotound individual with short, receeding and balding hair. He doesn't appear to be happy, he looks like one of those guys who just got told they have AIDS or something. He's dead serious. 'Are these men causing trouble Mr. Akbarrosslawler?' the officer questions the owner of the shop. 'Yes, very much so! These two man come to me, they run up and pull gun! They vant to vob me!' The Arab man screams loudly with anger in his voice. 'Man hold up,' Rush begins. We weren't doing anything! We're just minding our business, when bin Laden over here goes nuts.' Rush finishes while pointing accusingly at the Arab store owner. 'Okay, all I want is for you two men to leave. There is no need for you to stick around. Head on over to your bus... And make sure to sit at the back,' the officer says to Jordan and Rush with a chuckle. Jordan doesn't say anything. He just stares at the police officer with an intense look of hate on his face. In some areas of the dirty south and in the hip-hop community it is known as 'Mean Mugging.' Jordan nods at Rush as Rush picks up his bags, the two men walk towards Jordan's rented Cadilac Deville, which he was picking up the tab on. Was this a sign of weakness? Was Jordan's not arguing with the obviously narrow and stereotyped minded Police Officer and Arab store owner. Jordan and Rush methodically walk back to Jordan's Cadilac Deville, 2002. Rush seems angry at the fact that Jordan didn't stand up to the store clerks. Jordan was once a badass who didn't take anything from anybody, especially anything from somebody that he thought to be a part of the inferior race. Now, it appears as if Jordan has developed a tolerance, albeit small, for white people.... Well, when you think about it, he had to. He's partaking in his first ever PCW Main Event in a match against The Ulitmate Authority, a group of power hungry and power totting wrestlers, Mark Makonie, Xavier Kannon, Tim Walden, and Tyler Nelson. But, when you see his partners, you see Duke Williams, a white man, in fact, one of the whitest men in the planet, Jon Kano, another white man. That's two white men on your team. It's hard to wrestle as a team when you've got people on the team that you do not trust. There have been tag teams in the history of wrestling that consisted of two men that didn't exactly like each other, but when you think about it, those teams didn't last. Duke Williams said it just a few days ago, before, the Malice Crew was just a group of wrestlers who hung around away from the ring. They talked, shot down some drinks, and generally had a good time, all the time. But now, it's changed. Duke Williams, Jon Kano, Rush Mason, and Jordan X Shabazz are not only a bunch of friends away from the ring, but a bunch of friends in the ring, that watch each others backs, and makes sure that the Ultimate Authority, with all their power, don't take advantage of any single wrestler. As Jordan and Rush make their way back to the car, Rush whispers over to Jordan, 'Say, what happened in there?' 'Typical ignorance and racism my tar-like friend,' Jordan says without changing his facial expression. 'This country is a cesspool of rednecked police officers, and stereotyped to the bone Arab store owners. Same goes for those damned Koreans, I'll never forget when...' Rush cuts off Jordan. 'Bah! I don't need to hear this, you're the human racial statistic machine, you're probably going to go into some story about a black girl getting shot in the back because she tried to steal some orange juice, and the Korean lady getting probation.' Jordan shakes his head. He knows that Rush just read his mind, but that's exactly what these two men can do... Well... Not all the time. Jordan's mind works one way, everything to him is racial. There's never a single thing you can do, or say, and Jordan not accuse you of being racist... Unless of course, you're either black, or a member of the Malice Crew. As Jordan and Rush make their way to Jordan's car, Jordan can't help but think of all the times he's experienced racism. From police officers stopping him for every little traffic violation, the kids not letting him play hockey with them, people locking their car doors in front of him. Little things like that, it tends to build up in somebody. Jordan is not one to come out and tell everybody his past, you'd have to pry through several thick child service briefcases to find out where this man has grown up. So not releasing his emotions and feelings, has led to this large amount of pent up agression and anger... Don't believe me? Listen to Jordan whenever he calls in to talk to the Phantom Booker on PTC Malice. The wrestler known as Shabazz enters his car as Rush shoves his stuff in the backseat. Rush gets beside Jordan in the seat known as shotgun, and the men begin their hour and a half drive to Norma, Oklahoma. ***** Two hours later. The scene reopens with Jordan X Shabazz and his buddy on this top secret mission, Rush Mason, leaving the Cadilac Deville that Jordan had rented. Jordan and Rush take their bags out of the trunk and the backseat, respectively. While on the drive, Jordan's head, Debbie Barker, sent them a text message through Jordan's two-way pager, that they were to stay at a small motel, called 'Bertha and Bob's.' Obviously a home of the hick. Jordan and Rush walk up to the small motel, that smells of putridness. Urine, and feces are clearly seen on scrawled on the walls of this place. The windows are cracked, probably from break ins, the walls are cracked from the wear and tear of the weather. Jordan covers his face as he walks into the motel. Rush opens the broken glass door with black bars behind it and he follows Jordan. Inside, is not much better. Their is a young, unattractive, pimply-faced, teenage girl working the desk. She has shoulder length blond hair that is uncombed. She is wearing nothing but a sweat drenched and disgusting sports bra, accompanied by some hoochie looking jean shorts. She stands partially behind a desk, and partially in the way of the entrance for the workers of the motel. She greets Jordan and Rush with a smile, which showcases her few teeth. In all, counting top and bottom, about tweleve teeth. This really is a hick town. Rush looks afraid of her ugliness, and he shoves Jordan to the forefront. 'May I help you two fine gentlemen?' The redneck hick farmer's daughter type looking girl asks. She speaks perfectly well, with no southern accent. Almost as if she's not from the area. 'Yes, we're looking for two separate rooms,' says Jordan to the woman working the front office. She turns around, and spots only one key. She takes it off the rack and turns around to Jordan and Rush. 'There's only one key left sadly.' 'Bah! Darkmaster Flex, get your ass back into the car, that's where you'll be sleeping for the night,' Rush says while grabbing the key out of the hand of the woman working the front desk. 'Now get to steppin' darkie, I've got sleeping to do.' Jordan sighs deeply. He doesn't argue with Rush, he knows that there's no point. When Rush speaks, especially to Jordan, Jordan listens. That's how much respect Jordan has for 'The Rush.' As Rush takes his keys and runs like a little girl to his room, Jordan walks outside into the brisk cold Oklahoman night. The smell from the cracked down motel is a bother to Jordan, but he continues walking to his car. As he walks to his car, he notices that a few houses across the street from a motel, there seems to be a gathering of men in dark sheets. Jordan, knowing his history, realizes that a gathering of the Ku Klux Klan is happening right before his eyes. Bah! But this professional wrestler is tired, he just drove a few hours to a small hick town and now was forced to sleep in his own car. Jordan opens the back seat door to his car, he lies down, and within minutes, he falls into a deep sleep. (Dream In The Eyes Of Jordan) I am running.... And running... And running. I see a cliff in front of me... I'm going to jump... I've got to jump.... This will end the pain... The pain... The pain.... What pain? The pain of being white... (Back to regular narration.) There's a tapping from somewhere. Jordan looks around [In his dream], then he wakes up in a cold sweat. His clothes are drenched in his own sweat, he is wearing the same clothes that he put on earlier in the day. Jordan assumes the tapping on the window is that of his friend, Rush Mason. So Jordan throws on one of the ski masks that Rush brought with him for when the two men go to the PCW Pavillion for Fusion. Jordan opens the door and is shocked to see three men wearing dark colored hoods and a dark suit. It's obviously those members of the KKK that were across the street and a few houses down having a gathering. Jordan quickly checks his Timex watch and realizes that's it's three AM in the morning. He looks back at the three KKK members and quickly tries to collect his thoughts, were they going to jump him as some sort of an inatiation? Were they going to beat him brutally and tie him to the back of their pickup truck? Were they just going to stab him and then hang him? Those are the thoughts that ran through the head of the Civil Rights Extremist. Before Jordan could react further, one of them spoke. 'Hey, Jack! Where the hell were ya? We've been waiting since 1 o'clock. We were just about to go N***** huntin' without ya! It wouldn't have been as fun though,' one of the cloaked, redneck, KKK members whispers. 'Great get up Jack, you look like one of 'em, 'specially the mask!' Oh shit. Jordan thinks to himself, he's got to pretend he's this white guy, a freakin' member of the Ku Klux Klan?! Oh damn, where's Rush' dark ass when you need him? To Be Continued...
