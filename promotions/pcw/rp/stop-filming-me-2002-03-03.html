Stop Filming Me!

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/3/02 *Monologue* “All the hype, but doesn’t perform well.” Words of D-kay. Hype? Yes, Big D gots a lot of hype. Winning the FSW World Heavyweight title was his hype. It was his ticket to the PCW. Just like the others who came before him. Mike Marvelous, Jacob Jackson, Carlton Carter, Jake Keeton, Big Gooey Sac, and Token Weed . All have held titles in the FSW and their ticket was their success in the FSW to allow them in the PCW. But all did brief stints with the PCW. When Big Daveylicious joined the PCW he vowed he wouldn’t be like the other FSW wrestlers who came before him. Yes, he did just that. He went through the PCW World Heavyweight title tag title tournament with Poppa Plump as his partner and he carried that lazy slob with him to the third round to get what? To lose due to Poppa Plumps laziness to two PCW main eventers, Kid Wonder and our current World Heavyweight Champion, Snow! Big D didn’t lay down for Wonder or Snow. Plump did. If hype isn’t getting gold than that is Big D’s problem. Sure, he’s beaten some pretty damn good guys. But what are they know as to D-Kay and Walden? A fluke, luck, or even accidental. Call it what you want it’s called winning, beating ,overcoming, prevailing, triumphantly over his opponents. Big D doesn’t barred his country with a so called jobber virus. Not like that CJS virus crap which Walden used. You know the kinda crap that’s on the nose of D-kay Calyx after being up Walden’s a$$ or the kinda crap where the fans leave to go to the bathroom when a Karl LeBlanc promo airs. (Finally, the scene fades to a white stucco, three-story beach house’s front door in Miami, Florida. We can hear the Atlantic Ocean come on the shore of the beach. As we stare at the door a man’s hand knocks on the door. Suddenly, the door opens for us to see the owner of this extravagant home, Big Daveylicious. He is wearing a black and white Hawaiian shirt, khaki pants, and Oakley sunglasses. We can see the reflection of the cameraman holding the camera towards Big D.) Big Daveylicious: What in hell are you doing here?! Cameraman: I’m here to film on of your promo’s, sir. Big Daveylicious: Did I schedule one for today? Cameraman: No, but... Big Daveylicious: No, buts about it! (With that Big D slams the door on us. Leaving us facing the front door of Big D’s house.) Cameraman: But what am I gonna film...oh, crap. (Once again we see the hand of the cameraman's knock on the door. The door opens again with a view of a pissed off, Big Daveylicious.) Big Daveylicious: What do you want now?! Cameraman: I’m sorry, Mr. Daveylicious but I need money for a taxi. (Suddenly, a smile comes across Big D’s face and he laughs.) Big Daveylicous: A taxi, huh? No problem. (Big D reaches for his back pants pocket but stops and looks at us with furious look.) Big Daveylicious: Wait a second! You owe me some money. Cameraman: But I needed it for a hotel room. Big Daveylicious: Yeah, but instead you wasted it on a one legged hooker from Cancun! (Big D shakes his head and slams the door making us get used to this view of his door.) Cameraman: Great! I got nowhere to go and nothing to film... (Suddenly, we hear a car pull up. We pan behind the front door of Big D’s house to his driveway to notice a station wagon a little Girl Scout pops out of the car trailing off towards us with two boxes of Girl Scout Cookies. We move towards her stopping her from completely walking to us. She stares at us and than looks back to her mother who is the driver of the vehicle. Than smiles towards us.) Girl Scout: Hi, want to buy some cookies? Cameraman: How’d you like to be filmed little girl? Girl Scout: Mommy says I shouldn’t talk to strangers because they’ll trick me with goodies and kidnap me. Than they’ll do no no’s. Cameraman: C’mon little girl? I got to film something. Girl Scout: Wanna buy some cookies? I got chocolate chip and peanut butter cookies. Cameraman: Uh...no. (Suddenly, the girl stamps her feet on the driveway and throws her brown girl scout sash and her green girl scout hat onto the ground along with the boxes of cookies.). Girl Scout: Fine than! You can keep the stupid cookies! I hate Girl Scouts!(She than runs off to the station wagon and climbs in it. It speeds off out of our view. We look back at what the girl scout left. Suddenly, the cameraman grabs the items as the scene fades to black than it fades to a vertical view of Big D’s front door with the cameraman walking up to the door with a girl scout brown sash, green hat, and two boxes of cookies. He knocks on the door and the door opens to revel a furious with a beer bottle in hand. But Big D stops calmly and smiles. He appears to be drunk.) Cameraman: Hi, want to buy some Girl Scout cookies? Big Daveylicious: Wow, your a tall Girl Scout... Cameraman: Your that Big Daveylicious guy. What are you gonna do to D-kay and Walden? Big Daveylicious: What’s the flavors? Cameraman: Flavors? Well, I’m vanilla. Big Daveylicious: I meant the cookies, girl. Cameraman: Well, I got chocolate chip and peanuts. Big Daveylicious: How much? Cameraman: $5.00 or $2.00. I don’t care. Just tell me what your gonna do in the elimination match? Big Daveylicious: Hold on let me get my wallet, hon. (Big D walks out of our view but comes back with his wallet in hand.) Big Daveylicious: You said you wanted to know what I was gonna do in the elimination match, right? Cameraman: Oh, yes. (Big D kicks the cameraman in the nuts. Causing him to fall to his knees holding his genitals.) Big Daveylicious: That’s what I’ll do! Now, Stop filming me! (With that a beer bottle from Big D is heading towards us with that the scene fades to black.)
