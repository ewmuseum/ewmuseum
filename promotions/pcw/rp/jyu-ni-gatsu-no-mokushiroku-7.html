Jyu Ni Gatsu no Mokushiroku {7}

Date: December/March Botan looks out the window, peeking past the white curtains to see what the commotion is outside. “Oh…Big Aki Yama’s here. Thank god…now I don’t have to fight those retards anymore. Though he’ll be quite busy with all of em. Hope he throws em all out too…” She closes the blinds, wiping her face with a warm cloth. “Yep…throw em all out of San Diego.” She walks past the den…where things have gotten a little out of control with Yuu and Molly’s game of twister. Botan cursed under her breath as she tripped over Yuu’s skirt. “Jeez, can’t you two watch where you leave your clothes??” Leaving the fits of sexual lust from the two girls, outside bAy and Sonata stand frozen in shock as the Divine Prophetess stands before them, her arms folded across her chest dominantly. “I’ve waited for this day a long…long time Big Aki Yama.” Her voice echoed into the air, white vapor coming from her lips. “I’ve dreamt of the day when I would stand over your beaten carcass and bask in the victory! And now…that day has come!” Sonata cut her eyes at her. “Um…you haven’t beaten him yet, Divine Swine, or whatever your name is.” The Divine Prophetess glared hard at bAy’s manager. “That’s DIVINE PROPHETESS!! Don’t get it mixed up! And I don’t need to beat him yet…because I know I will defeat him this day!” “So what are ya, psychic or something?” The Divine Prophetess reached into her cloak and pulled out a card, holding it proudly for Akiyama and Sonata to read. They both leaned forward, reading the small print on the card. “Official psychic license of the Muse Council,” the god read slowly. Sonata’s eyes squinted at the print. “With a learners in oracle work, doctorate in prophesying, and masters in mind raping.” Aki points with his pinky at the card, looking up at the Divine Prophetess. “You know this expires in a week, right?” “Of course I know!” she snatched the card back, draping her cloak around her tightly. “But I’m not gonna renew it…it’s even more EVIL when you’re unlicensed. Plus those dues will screw you up the ass.” “And we all know how much you hate that.” Rolling her eyes, Sonata nodded slowly, her disinterest thick. “So…we gonna fight here, or are you gonna chew our ear out like Xenu over there?” To the side Xenu could be seen talking on a cell phone angrily. “I don’t care what you say, by the power of Scientology and the power bestowed upon me by the Grand HUBBARD himself I command you to fulfill my command!! Do you understand what I’m saying you filthy haired peasant?? GOOD!! Now…this is the last time I will repeat myself: …a LARGE pepperoni with extra cheese. And no anchovies…the great princess Ellenore can’t swallow them. Everything else…but those just wont go down.” He stops for a minute, looking into the phone. “NO, In the name of L RON HIMSELF I DONT WANT ANY BREADSTICKS WITH THAT!!” The Divine Prophetess sighs, snapping her fingers with a look of disgust. Matrix comes walking up to Xenu from behind with a coil of wire wrapped in his hands. The Divine Prophetess cuts across her throat, gritting her teeth, and Matrix lowers his head, pocketing the wire and leading Xenu away by the arm. “O…kay.” Sonata says before she scraps through her pockets for her cigarettes. The Divine Prophetess nods slowly. “Right, now where was I…” she taps her foot on the pavement for a second before snapping her fingers. “Oh right, NOW TO DESTROY YOU BIG AKI YA--.” “What in the name of Hubbar--!!” A slicing noise could be heard in the background. The Divine Prophetess snapped her icy eyes to the streets instantly. “MATRIX!!” “Yeah?” his voice came out from behind the bushes calmly. “Step out where I can see you please.” “Uh…give me a minute.” “NOW, MATRIX.” There’s a rustling in the bushes before the SON OF RALLY MONKEY steps out from behind the bushes. He stands there, Vogue-ing stiffly in front of the Divine Prophetess, Big Aki Yama, and Sonata. There were three disgusted stares. Matrix steps out from the bushes, his face covered in bloodspray. He slaps Son of Rally Monkey in the back of his head roughly. “Told you it wouldn’t work! Get the hell outta here!” Lowering his head, the monkey pitifully walks off down the street. He turns the corner, Matrix still pointing for him to leave. After a few seconds, the monkey pokes his head back out from behind the corner. “GO!!” Matrix bellows. And with that said, the monkey departs. The Divine Prophetess turns back to our heroes with a nod. “Now where was I…” “Leaving?” Sonata said with a sly smile. “Nice try, flatscan. NOW I WILL DESTROY YOU BIG AKI YAMA, AND I SHALL FINALLY HAVE WHAT I ALWAYS DESIRED!!” bAy raised his hand like a student. The Divine Prophetess, with arms raised from current rant, looked down at him. She lowered her arms, her diabolical smile lowering as well before she pointed at him. “What?” “Well…what is it that you want from us anyways?” She gave him a stupid look, the same one any bystander would give them. “What else? To claim my rightful position over you in PCW!! To become the dominant Akiyama in the entire world!!” Sonata matched stupid looks with the Divine Prophetess. “Hey, haven’t you heard? PCW hates you! Pretty much most of the wrestling world hates you.” The Divine Prophetess’s eyes flared up at her words. “WHAT. DID. YOU. SAY??” Sonata nodded as she reached around in her pockets, searching still for her cigarettes. “Yeah, after that Cruiserweight title deal, you pretty much overstepped your boundaries as a secondary character… from there everyone pretty much resented your existence. That’s pretty much why you can’t hold down a job.” “THAT’S NOT TRUE AT ALL!!’ she yelled at Sonata. The black and blonde haired manager of Big Aki Yama nodded slowly with a smug smile. “Sure it is. That’s why you didn’t return with Akiyama this time to PCW. You were a waste of space. Especially since he has vocal chords for himself…” The Divine Prophetess clutched her head, shaking it over and over trying to ignore her words. “LIES!! ALL LIES!! I know the truth! I’m PSYCHIC!!” “If you’re so psychic…how come you didn’t see that sledgehammer coming, eh?? Tomas sneak in under your radar or something?” “I’M NOT LISTENING!! LA! LA! LA!! DA! DA! DA!!” The Divine Prophetess yelled loudly trying to drain out Sonata’s accusations. Sonata grinned at Aki, giving him the thumb’s up as the blue haired woman tried to repress the truth. Matrix sighed, standing from afar with a despondent expression. “Geez, how many times do I have to do the job myself?” He walked across the street and opened the van door, reaching in with both hands. He came back out with a tan sack in his hands, SANDBAG printed on the side. “When the job calls for results…the job calls for SANDBAG!” Matrix walked back into the yard of bAy, passing the screaming Divine Prophetess and sneaking up on Aki and Sonata. He dropped the sack and spit in his hands, rubbing them together before hefting the bag up over his shoulder and aiming. “Two for the price of one…here we go!!” Just as he took swing…SON OF RALLY MONKEY jumped into the way, jerking around and doing the Harlem Shuffle! Matrix’s eyes bugged out as he tried to swerve the sandbag…and INADVERTANTLY BAGGED HIMSELF IN THE FACE!!! Aki and Sonata turned around, looking down at the KO’ed Matrix, stars in his eyes. They look to Son of Rally Monkey, who shrugs his shoulders in a dumbfounded expression. Sonata looks up at the god with tired eyes. “This really is a sad day for villains.” “SILENCE, MORTAL FOOL!!” The Divine Prophetess pointed her finger to the skies suddenly. “I SUMMON THE SATANIC FORCES OF HELL TO UNLEASH THE WINGED DEMON OF DARKNESS FROM ITS SLUMBER!! PREPARE TO BE BANISHED!!!!” Sonata just shook her head at the woman. “Oh, so we’re Dark Gimmicks now? Look how low you’ve sunk.” Slowly a huge shadow begins to come over Aki and Sonata. Sonata ignores it and keeps talking. “And then you’re gonna use BANISHMENT against Aki Yama? That’s just the most uncreative thing I’ve heard of! I swear, every time you’re on camera you totally screw up the plot not to mention the entertainment value of promos! If I had any pull in this, I’d---HOLY MOTHER OF HUBBARD!!” Sonata dived out of the way just as a huge feathered beast drops down and SWALLOWS BIG AKI YAMA WHOLE!!! Sonata jumps back up, a shocked look on her face. “WHAT THE DUCK??!!” A huge seven foot near 400 pound duck stood upright, its head jerking around quickly before letting out a roaring burp. “QUWAAAAACK!!” Sonata was finished with it all. She walked past the duck and stuck her finger in the Divine Prophetess’s chest. “That’s it! You’ve gone too far!” The Divine Prophetess looked at her innocently. “What?” “Don’t what me! First you have an unreasonable goal of becoming bigger than your father in PCW…well THAT WONT HAPPEN! People don’t like you! And not because you’re a heel, its because YOU SUCK!! You suck so much that even when you’re not on PCW TV the ratings drop because you’ve tainted it with your fishy vile scent!! It’s not Syko dropping the ratings, its not Kannon and Ellie sucking up all the air time with inept preppie talk…its not even the insane gimmick matches done in drunken stupor where a number of wrestlers go out of character and some get anally raped by large animals!! IT’S BECAUSE YOU SUCK CYCLONICA!!” Cyclo- The Divine Prophetess was shocked. Even the giant duck stood there, bill gaped open and eyes wide. Son of Rally Monkey’s hands were clasped over his mouth in total amazement. Botan, Yuu and Molly watched out of the window with shocked expressions. NEXT DOOR, Magus and Clone Borg 39 looked out the window, joining in with the surprise. Cyclonica’s mouth closed slowly, her jaw slacking as she looked away for a second. Then she came back and pointed her own finger in Sonata’s chest. “What gives you the right to say that? Huh?” “How about because out of all the women that’s been in his life I’M the one he cares about the most! I grew up with him, I was his best friend. I’ve known him longer than anyone else in this world! I say when enough is enough!” Unimpressed eyes shone dark in the Divine Prophetess. “Please. Like I’m supposed to just stop everything because you have some irrelevant sentimental bond with him. Haven’t you realized by now that he cannot coexist with humanity?? His physical bearings are too much, and his unstable mind frame will surely lead to the annihilation of everything around him, not to mention himself! What we’re doing here is preserving his existence for the well behind of the world, not to mention his own well being!” “Oh, by taking away his freedom? By taking away his brain! His mind! His soul??” Sonata wasn’t buying any of her words like the god wouldn’t buy anyone stopping him from getting the world title after he returned. Determination was a grand thing…even when it came from the ones that had no hope of success. “He had no soul to begin with.” Cyclonica ripped off her cloak and stepped up to the woman, her fists clenching tightly. “And what soul he has we gave him. We’re finally taking back what is ours…me, the Department of Divine Examination…and the country of Japan.” “Don’t make this into a patriotic deal! This is because you can’t bear for him to exist unless you’re pulling his strings! Akiyama suddenly starts to think for himself and his Divine Prophetess can’t handle that, so she puts a sledgehammer in the back of his head and gets the entire Japan Special Forces to biologically make him your slave!! I’m not standing for it?” “OH?” Cyclonica looked around, an amused smile on her face. There was no way this woman could stop her…she was the Divine Prophetess after all. Twice her size, twice her skill, and she had the god’s blood running through her veins. Nothing could stop her. “Give me one reason why I should stop everything right now? Give me ONE!” “Because I LOVE HIM!!” …the whole world stopped…literally. The giant duck…no more. Melted into a puddle of dark ooze. Deep within that ooze….Akiyama sat in darkness. Banished…in his own Void. In normal attire of black vinyl pants, boots, and black short sleeved mock tee, he sat there, head hung low, arms at his sides. A defeated image many men would hope and dream to lay eyes upon in their realities. Maybe an image set to appear soon… Voices echoed around him….stirring echoes of hollowed voices, souls…commanding him over and over with different orders each time. “Destroy them all.” “Kill everyone that stands in your way.” “You’re a god? Look at yourself! Get your act together!” “Is this how you want to be? A wash up? Pathetic? Nothing??” amidst all those voices…one gentle…calm voice silenced them all. The voice was innocent, a girl’s voice that was quite familiar to the pricking ears of the god. “Aki… get up.” Slowly the nVg’s head rose, his dark eyes cast upwards into the Void skies. “Get up.” He obeyed. “Akira Akiyama…do what your heart desires.” The god threw his arms into the air clawed, his head thrown back with a mighty roar that quaked even the Void of his mind. The darkness shattered…and light flooded in. Blinding light… Truthful light…
