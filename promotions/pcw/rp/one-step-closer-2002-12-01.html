One Step Closer

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dec 1st 2002 November 29th, 2002America – Mexico Boarder10:23 p.m. Vilas road to the Cruiserweight championship lead through this boarder between two countries. Coming off what some would say, a rather large upset was the last thing on Vilas mind. Even the championship or even Tim Walden for that matter, was in the back of his head. His thoughts were on something more then just winning. They were on the change that he would take if he succeeded. Would I have to change who I am, just because I am someone different? Are winning the best I can do at this moment or is there something much more then what I am seeing? It troubles me to think I could very well turn into the normal human being. I am one inch away from becoming a sell out to the system that makes me different, and stepping over that line of my own personal well being. Vilas watched as cars by car rushed past, entering the United States and rushing into Mexico. So many lives being effected as each motor roared on, toward the major cities. It was easy to see that life was not as picture perfect as some made it out to be. To Vilas, each one of these people had their own story of lies to tell. They were not moving because of enjoyment. They saw a chance to make riches and took the opportunity. Vilas rested just outside the boarder, waiting and hopping for so sort of sign. Was this a mistake…? Or a chance of a life time…? Do I really want to turn into one of them, pondering over my riches and praying the next day I can make more money and be greedier? This wasn’t my life style and I should be living in it. Vilas turned around and headed back toward the highway leading away from the boarder. Something drew him back though and again he found himself only a few steps from the edge leading back into Mexico. He waited and took a deep breath, crossing the boarder with one step. It was his goal… It was his destiny to succeed… There was only one thing that mattered now. Winning and surviving till my path was complete. It was now or never and there was no turning back. Thailand believed in me and so did Safina. Now I must go on alone and prove I have what it takes to SURVIVE… WIN… Conquer my fate… This would become a day now one would soon forget. Life would move on and the greed of Primetime Wrestling would know the name Vilas Lavagna soon enough. This was no longer a joke in my eyes but rather a calling. Walden knew it and so did Romantico. A new uprising brought to the table… A new beginning… I was One Step closer to succeeding… I already took that step…
