Date: Dec.13/02 The air was bitterly cold, and reeked of rotting flesh. As I walked down the pitch black hallway, I could hear my heart pounding. I had never forgotten the last time I was in a situation like this, and that’s why I was more cautious then I had been as a child. No more running into walls . . . no more tripping over my own feet . . . I had to do this carefully, cautiously, slowly . . . I had to face my fear while calm, cool, and collected. Suddenly a large, fire breathing clown jumped out in front of me . . . ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ I had finally come face to face with my greatest fear . . . The terror that had haunted my dreams since I was a small child . . . The terror that still struck fear in this young man’s body, mind, and soul . . . The fire breathing clown let out a monstrous roar, then began to advance on me. I turned to run, but the hallway that used to be behind me had been blocked off somehow. There was no escape . . . I was locked in that small room . . . with nowhere to run. I frantically searched the walls for a switch to open the hallway up, but alas, there was no switch. As I stopped to gather my thoughts, I could feel the intense heat of the clown’s breath searing the back of my neck. I turned my head slightly, only to come face-to-face, nose-to-nose with the clown. With yet another monstrous roar, the clown leaped forward, grabbing me by both shoulders with his enormous hands. This was the end . . . I had faced my fears, and I lost . . . The clown reared back it’s ugly head, as if preparing to bite a whole in my head and chow down on my brains. It was at that moment, that it all came together . . . ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ With his hands busy holding me and his head pulled back, I saw my chance to escape. The clown had left it’s chest wide open . . . so replaying my training as a wrestler in my mind, I jerked my head forward and landed a solid headbutt to the clown’s sternum. The clown yelped in pain, and released me from his grasp. I dropped down to one knee, and readied myself for a second attack. As the clown stumbled backwards, it gasped for breath . . . and as he got himself ready to come after me again, I charged forward and landed a vicious heart punch to the clown’s rib cage. The clown’s exterior was not what I had expected. It wasn’t skin at all . . . but rather some sort of papier-màche . . . and located under the clown’s chin was some sort of speaker. This was no fire breathing clown . . . This was some sort of trick . . . Some sort of costume . . . I’ve been had! ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ A I stood above the motionless corpse of the clown, I huffed and wheezed to get my breath back. My attacks took all the energy I had . . . as I wanted to end the terror, once and for all. I leaned over, and took hold of the clown’s head, which with little effort, slid right off. Underneath the many pounds of papier-màche, I found the Haunted House Operator. I slapped him a few times to wake him up . . . my heart punch really knocked the wind out of him! When he came to, I asked him what the hell was going on. Him simply said . . . “How else would be able to overcome your fear, if you never had to face it again?” . . . and he was right. What that man had just done for me was the greatest gift someone could have ever given me. I had faced my fear . . . and I triumphed . . . I kicked it’s ass. I was now ready for the “House on Haunted Hill Match” at Fusion. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ After apologizing to the Operator for hurting him and giving him quite the reward for his help, I hoped in my car and headed for home. The whole way home, all I could think about was my match . . . The Extreme Title . . . The lack of fear . . . The chance to fight Mark Amulet once again . . . Oh yes, I haven’t forgotten about Mark Amulet. He and I still have a score to settle, and I can’t imagine we’ll be settling our differences anytime soon. As for the rest of the poor souls who are taking part in the “House on Haunted Hill Match” . . . they have one simple rule to follow. Stay away from me, and they won’t get hurt . . . well actually there are two rules they must follow . . . Mark Amulet is mine, and mine alone . . . if anyone gives him a beating . . . it’s me . . . and only me! ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Alas, that is the end of the tale . . . the quest for courage . . .
