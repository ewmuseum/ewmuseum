A Year In The Making - Nerezza (Part 3)

22 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22 Jan 2003 Recap: The opening of this cluster of events saw the graduation ceremony unfold as many of Wrestle Tattler's high-profiled names sat in attendance to bare witness to the four inductee's of Primetime Wrestling Academy's graduation list, which holds some of the most talented individuals in the industry. Names etched in stone, names from the past that may have gone yet never forgotten and names still riding within the community to this very day, Ivan Stanislav, Jason Snow, Hessian and Xavier Kannon to name but a few. Many were there to show their respect because on this very day Angelo Deville, Nascosto Ireul Nerezza, Gaz 'The Jester' Green and Amy 'Aimz' Campbell were about to be inducted into this private circle of friends and co-workers. Already the shenanigans of Amy Campbell have been documented as she stormed from the building in a flood of tears and now the time has arrived for Nerezza to take the stand and speak before his peers. 'Well Amy's departure was like her OSW career... Short and sweet... So as we bid the Firesnatch good-bye our next graduate was a hand groomed student of mine and maybe one day 'The Malice Man' Duke Williams will actually pronounce his name correctly... NEREZZA~!' roared the distinctive voice of Old School Wrestling's satanic goliath, Hessian. With the room dropping into darkness a spotlight beamed overhead, finally coming to rest on the behemoth Nerezza who stands erect, blinded by the piercing white light he snarls before proceeding towards the altar. Draped in a crimson red funeral suit and his hair neatly tied back into that of a ponytail the vampire shuffles forward aggravated by the light as those present watch in awe. 'A fitting colour for his suit, one might say?' murmured Xavier Kannon. 'Come Shanghaied the colour of his suit will be the least of his worries....' retorted the Emerald Menace with a subtle smile. His facial expression and the roll of his eyes indicated that the Jester was covering information from his Front Office stable mate, which spurned the Scientologist into a question asking frenzy. 'Did you have something to do with that Aimz speech?' Kannon began. 'What else have you planned for tonight’s proceedings?' '.... Your keeping things from me, Mr. Green!' concluded the inquisitive CEO. The Corporate Jester merely shrugged his shoulders as he reverted his attention over to the pedestal where Nerezza stands smugly, the murmurs slowly faded as the lights once again rose. Natalia and Ellie~! continue to exchange dagger-like-stares to the amusement of their respected clients who peer over to the patient Nerezza with shear ignorance... The Front Office... A soap opera of its own, a worthy challenger to the Eastenders throne. 'Ladies and gentlem.... Ahem, sorry. GOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOD EVENING, WESTERN CONSUMERS!' began the man labelled Primetime Championship Wrestling's Living Nightmare as he hit home his killer punch line to the positive response of the crowd. 'Well, what can I say? 7 months ago, I was languishing in an Indy federation, still human and collapsing under financial strain. I ran a company, some might say into the ground. The bottom line is that I was a fighter, not a businessman. These hands, these... Claws... They are for fighting, not holding a pen. So I had to find a way into the ring again. I applied to many places, but the one that sparked the most interest was a school, not a federation. Having been away from the ring so long, I decided I needed to train again. And that's where I first met the man named Hessian. At first, he objected to my ways and my smart mouth. Well, we all make mistakes and I was also still just a human back then. And then in that fed, something changed me forever. It was a combination of the tutelage of Hessian, the enhancements that my new life gave me, the provoked spirit of competition from Angelo Deville and the confidence from Gareth Green. It was a revelation. But enough of my unlife story....' continued the Angel Of Fear to his congregation so to speak. 'I want to thank a few people who have helped me. Hessian, who has been more of a surrogate father to me and raised me as his own. Dorn Tracer, wherever he may be, for being one of my first opponents. Angelo Deville for being my first ever tag team partner in PWA and sparking me back into fighting. And now, to people I owe no thanks to - A stinking, traitorous, slimy-green snotball of a human being. And Elvis, who is just dead. Thank you!' concluded the former Pranks With Attitude behemoth to a desolate silence as the onlookers watched dumbfounded. Hessian parted his hands to gesture his applause when suddenly a clap filtered prompting a mass turn of heads as the assailant was identified as Gaz 'The Jester' Green who clashed his palms together sarcastically knowing that those open palms would substitute for clenched fists come 'Shanghaied'. Grinding his teeth as a retort, saliva swishing around his mouth like a washing machine, Nerezza looks at his former partner and friend with evil intent as the 'first Blood Match' looks to have been brought forward. A stare down follows before Xavier Kannon intervenes. 'Gaz, keep it for Shanghaied, I can't afford to let this happen before the Pay-Per-View!' The intensity is certainly there, the hatred is present and correct, everything points to their contest being a possible showstopper, as they can't spend a night’s company together without their feelings hurdling common sense. Gaz Green never to be out-down, and certainly not by the man he claims oppressed him for the tenure of their time together stands erect, when.... Suddenly bursting through the twin door entry-way like a battering ram was Sarah Michelle Gellar, or in this case Buffy The Vampire slayer wielding a wooden stake with some neat handiwork. A few spin kicks and showboating was enough to draw those in attendance to look in disbelief as they gaze at the antics of the blonde headed babe. 'HUH?!?' being a general comment murmured. 'DIE BLOODSUCKERS!!! Err... Arr... No bloodsuckers?' questioned Sarah as her eyes roamed the dwelling. A shower of heads waved to indicate 'No' leaving the actress looking rather embarrassed, as she blushes in claret red. 'So this isn't the Buffy Convention then... Me bad...' finished the stake wielding wife of Freddie Prince Jnr. as she tip-toed from the scene, passing the litter of debris of her entrance and vanishing from sight. Talking about vanishing acts, Nerezza has himself disappeared, as the altar lay bare. The vampire performing his own re-enactment of David Copperfield as he vanishes without trace, leaving another graduation speech done and dusted, ironically Nerezza himself wasn't dusted... Hopefully... Taking centre stage once more the cloak wearing fiend Hessian steps back up to the microphone and bellows down the equipment with a harsh tone as he introduces the third inductee, be it the mobster Angelo Deville. 'Between the emotional departure of Amy Campbell and the disappearing acts of the great Nerezza it would be nice if someone would actually stay... To the end... So I am putting in a lot of faith to our next guest, hopefully he will show us his party trick of concreting peoples shoes, maybe his own... Wrestle Tattlers Universal Champion, Angelo Deville!' Again the lights dim but instead of the emergence of Angelo Deville we go to our first stop, the reserved table of The Front Office as the PCW CEO converses once more with his Corporate Jester, reflecting over recent events (Very recent...) 'Two down, one to go...' smirked the Emerald Menace. 'Mr. Green I hope you haven't been making a nuisance of yourself?!?' asked the Hubbardville native with a slight smile. 'Hey... Those Buffy Convention directions could have lead poor Sarah to a Rob Zombie concert but alas they directed her here, and what a time to make an appearance, during Nerezza's snore-a-thon!' replied the evil genius sarcastically through the darkness when without warning the spotlight again rose paving the way for Angelo Deville to make his long awaited appearance. - To be continued -
