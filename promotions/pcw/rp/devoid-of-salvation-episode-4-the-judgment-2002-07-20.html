Devoid of Salvation: Episode 4 – The Judgment

20 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 20 The abomination known as the Crawling Chaos beamed with pride as he looked down on the disheartened mortal that had so aggravated him in recent months. Much of the progress that those fools of the ‘Coalition’ had made in recent weeks, notably advancements in the deterrence of Great Cthulhu’s ‘Deep Ones’ and the widespread eradication of Hastur the Unspeakable’s cult followers in the Liverpool region of England, had been perpetrated by this ingrate. Shanahan’s night-born revelations, guided further by his incredibly strong will, had been the catalyst behind a great deal of these developments. Subordinates of the loathsome Nyarlathotep, including the deity himself during periods of extreme boredom, were responsible for many of these; He had not counted on the human being so indomitable and zealous in light of his rampant hatred for the society he now claimed to protect, and as such had revealed a rather generous portion of the Old One’s plans feeling there was no harm in doing so. Their objective was merely to stupefy and shock the bitter street-bred ruffian into pulling out of the Coalition, but the plan had backfired enormously. Shanahan had thwarted numerous schemes by the underlings of the abundant Old Ones, with information bestowed by the Chaos himself. But now, he had the mortal right where he wanted him. On his knees, a dagger to his breast. He looked downright pitiful actually. Despondent and dejected, the rage gone and replaced by a deluge of utter helplessness. Far from the sadistic, destructive machine he attempted to portray on television across this miserable planet. Far from the ‘Violence Jack’ persona he depicted to the common TV-viewing flunkie. The pharaoh-morphed celestial rose one still hand, clenching the bony fingers into a mailed fist. A vicious grin crept across His face as the smoldering eyes locked onto the lowered head of his adversary, sparking as if taken from the deepest hellfire. “End the pain, my friend,” urged the cosmic demon, his voice embodied by that distinct echo of a thousand voices. “What benefit does this Earth still hold for you? Plagued by war, infested with disease and famine. Mankind’s existence has degenerated into being that of a mere pestilence. Yet you vainly attempt to rescue them from a fate of their own making. What is the purpose?….” The voice trailed off as Shanahan’s eyes closed momentarily, the assertions of the demon drowned out into silence as visions began to flood the darkness he saw through lowered eyelids. He saw the mass graves found in wake of the infamous Holocaust, and bodies littering the streets of Mogadishu, Somalia during their 90’s civil war. Pictures of the gang-wars and drug-inspired street violence he was all too familiar with, the aftermath of bombings throughout war-torn Israel. He saw in graphic detail of rapes and various perversions, the butchered fetuses of the unborn as they were wrenched from the womb. These all flashed through his mind like that of some deranged commercial, all finally culminating in an image of the September 11th tragedy that resulted in worldwide outrage and the deaths of thousands of innocents. Suddenly it all vanished, clicked off as if the plug was pulled on some internal television feeding the inside of his eyelids. Shanahan’s eyes fluttered and opened wide, gaze leveled on the intricately-crafted celestial blade poised at his own chest. “It is not your responsibility,” continued Nyarlathotep. “Not your duty to mend the wounds caused by the recklessness of the human race. I am offering you a chance to be rid of this grief once and for all. No more persecution, no more pestilence. Only the undying serenity and solitude offered by Her Lady Death. You need only take the next step. It comes no easier, and no less painfully….” Bruce sighed deeply, his pulse racing as thoughts raced through his obscured mind. The god was appealing to his reasoning, feeding the convictions that Shanahan had grasped for years. It really was almost too easy. Where Craven and McCarver’s supposedly ‘generous’ God had failed to bid him entry, the representative of his greatest enemies now offered him the key. Salvation, deliverance…..peace at last….. Shanahan realized what needed done. And this was the time to do it. He gripped the hilt of the dagger, its onyx-carved form adorned with the emblems of many now-familiar deities. Hands trembling, beads of sweat now slithering down his brow, he thrust the blood-tipped blade into the pink flesh. * * * Sensaki and Norton slid through a low dip in the cavern wall, the belly of the slope like that of a bowl as it allowed both men to pass out of the Cthonian-infested chamber and into the next without traversing the maze-like tunnels. Sensaki rolled his gun across the floor with a clatter as a thunderous crashing emanated from the room from whence they had just come. “They’ve gone under again,” growled Norton as Sensaki crawled across the floor to snatch up his weapon. “Bastards are everywhere, this entire cave system is crawling with the f*ckers. Try to raise the captain again, me and ole’ Serena will keep ‘em at bay.” Dropping his carbine to the rock floor with a rattle, the burly soldier withdraw a multi-barreled firearm from the bag slung over his shoulder. Sensaki grasped his radio and knelt to the floor, plugging one ear as Norton mumbled a barrage of curses while feeding the belt into one side of the weapon’s chamber. * * * Stryker pivoted on his left foot, raising his rifle to waist-level and unleashing a volley on the serpent-like beast darting toward him. The beast jolted but kept coming, Stryker already racing to keep up with his towering wingman. Ahead was yet another fork in the tunnels, neither any more promising than the other. Both were relatively silent at the moment, yet would be crowded with the squid-like fiends as the word spread to their kin the human invaders were in the area. “Left,” Stryker said without hesitation or consideration, darting down the hallway with Slade in tow as his radio suddenly crackled with static. He heard Slade bellow a fervent war-cry and gunfire roared yet again, Stryker seizing the sweat-strewn device from his belt and raising it. “Stryker here, but I’m a bit busy at the moment….” “Chief! Jago here, Christian ….ssss…… and I are pinned down in the …..ssss…… southwest quadrant! As far as ……ssss…… I know the entire team stationed here has …..ssss……. been annihilated! No one except …..ssss…… our unit is left!” “Vanessa and Bruce!” shouted Marcus over frenzied fire from Slade. “Where are they?!” “Lost contact with them about three minutes ago, chief! This place is crawling with those things! We up and walked ourselves into a damn nest!” “Get yourselves to the north entrance, ASAP!” demanded Stryker, switching magazines. “The area’s too hot and we don’t have the manpower to maintain this operation. F*cking GeoCo….” “AHH F*CK!” Sensaki’s panic-stricken voice permeated the radio for moment before being eclipsed by the thunderous, steady hum of Norton’s minigun. Several blasts from Sensaki’s own Winchester sawed-off penetrated the drone of the minigun and all went to static again…. * * * (Excerpted from the memoirs of Bruce ‘Violence Jack’ Shanahan) How did it feel Aymin? How did it feel to nearly have your second-rate career ended at the hands of the executives’ worst f*cking nightmare? And how did it feel a few days later to feel the flames lick at your skin and sear your flesh before crashing to the ground in a blaze of glory? Better yet…how does it feel to realize its not over as long as your continue to foolishly proclaim yourself ‘extreme’? And Brandi…..what was it like coming back into Primetime Championship Wrestling after leaving as a virtual goddess, and walk right into the wrathful arms of Boston’s Bastard Son? Your every technique cast off like a mosquito bite, your neck nearly fused into your spinal column, your overrated past tenure in the organization spit on and rewarded with your near obliteration. The latter which would have come to pass if not for that one-balled wretch attempting to save face after the beating I dished out only days prior. Every trial put in my way I have passed, every worthwhile opponent placed in my path left a twisted pile of trodden flesh. Every night I step between the ropes I give those bewildered fans a sense of where I came from. Whether it be the war-zone that is the Orient or the inner streets of slum-ridden Boston Mass, I give the fans and that uptight admin exactly what I promised during my negotiations: I give them that edge the ‘Extreme’ division has been lacking. I give them violence, unbridled and unchecked. I provide them with bloodshed, merciless and unforgiving. I take brutality to a level they haven’t seen since before I myself began watching Primetime Championship Wrestling programming. And still, it’s not good enough. For my efforts, how am I rewarded? I defeat that ‘extreme’ fraud, Flaymin Aymin, for the number one contendership to the title I seek to restore respect and honor to. After that I take Brandi, the ‘Wonder Woman’ of PCW in days past, and nearly destroy her once and for all. For once, it seems a white trash thug is getting some respect. Some prestige in the illustrious world of PCW. And what happens? Kramer Wild Wing and Thug RoC, a pretty boy and a nobody from Nowheresville, are put together in a #1 contendership match. #1 contendership for the Extreme title to boot. I can almost feel the sting of the collective front office hand striking my face. Must I redeem myself? For what purpose have they decided to nullify my right to take out that disgraceful Rush Mason at Wrestle Vessel? Bah….fine. If I must redeem myself, then so be it. Either way, I will get whats coming to me on the 28th. By hook or by crook. But the blood will be on the hands of every sorry f*ck in the corporate office. Every board member, every director and representative of any and every council will have to answer for what happens if I don’t get what I have earned. It is out of my hands and into theirs. For now, it’s simply wrong place and wrong time for TMM. THE Multiple Man. PCW’s resident nutcase. They say the guy’s lacking a bit upstairs. And I won’t dispute that, not after seeing what he’s done and having been in the ring with him. But then again, we all tend to go a little crazy sometimes. Especially when we’re deprived something we deserve. A sad thing indeed. Especially for the poor sap on the receiving end…..
