And Willie begat Tyler

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 10, 2002 Spring Fling has found its place in the PCW record books, and once again, Jason Snow is a member of PCW. But then again, not many people knew that he was actually considering retirement, not just at home recovering from a knee injury. So much has changed for Snow in just a month. He and his wife are once again together and living happily. Things would probably have been happier had the young PCW Superstar never returned to the ring, but as time wore on, they both realized that was out of the question. One thing hasn’t changed, however - Jason Snow and Chris Chase seem to be as close as ever. At Spring Fling, Snow helped his former stablemate win the PCW Heavyweight Championship of the World, the highest prize in all of wrestling. And now, this coming Friday night on Fusion, the pair will team up against Boda and Miyagawa... everyone in the ring will be a former champion, except Chris Chase, who currently holds the title... Snow: “Is my car down here yet, damn it!?” The scene opens inside one of the most luxurious hotels in Anaheim, California. The young PCW Superstar, Jason Snow, slams his fist down on the check-in desk, startling the young clerk on the other side of it... Clerk: “Uh, no sir, but we just got word that the car is, indeed, on its way.” Snow: “On its way!? That’s just not damn good enough, peasant! What the hell am I supposed to do down here in this hell hole of a state without a car!? Sit in here and stare at your pathetic face! Hell no! I’m the Killin’ Villain, damn it! I want to get out from time to time! I hear they’re having a sale on Snake Skin across town!” Clerk: “Well, sir, there’s a bus that arrives every half hour at the front gate.” Snow: “A bus? A BUS!?!? By Greatness, did you just suggest that I, the Killin’ Villain, use PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION!? What in the hinges of hell do you take me for, peasant!? I’ll not subject my-Superior-self to the stench that comes along with riding a...” Snow cringes at the thought... Snow: “...bus...” The young Hotel Clerk doesn’t know what to say to the angered Killin’ Villain - this is obviously his first encounter with Snow... or anyone like him for that matter. He looks fairly young, probably fresh out of college, and has a freshly shaven face and short-clipped black hair. In a shakey voice, he responds... Clerk: “I can get you a car rental service on the phone, if you would like.” Snow: “Damn it, plebe! That’s worse than your ‘bus’ idea! Have you no sense!? Why would I, the Killin’ Villain, in all of my Greatness and Superiority, want to drive a car that has been driven by the low-lives of this hell hole of a state!” Clerk: “Well, Mr. Snow, it does normally take at least four days to drive from New Brunswick to California. We’re already chancing speeding violations.” Snow: “You’re also chancing an ass-kicking from the Greatest wrestler ever to step inside a ring! Where the hell is your inferior driver, anyway?” The young clerk cringes, not wanting to give an answer to the Killin’ Villain. Already, he’s learned that unpleasant answers, regardless of truth, lead to unpleasant insults. Snow is not much good for accepting bad news. Lucky for the clerk, however, Snow’s attention is immediately diverted to an older man who is entering the hotel carrying a bag of golf clubs... Snow makes his usual comments... Snow: “What in the hell is he supposed to be?” The Clerk gives Snow an odd look... Clerk: “You mean... you don’t recognize him?” Snow: “Do you expect me to recognize every piece of trash that blows through the front door!?” Clerk: “But Mr. Snow... that’s Willie Nelson!” Snow: “Willie Nel... Did you say... Willie NELSON!?!?!” Clerk: “Yes sir, Willie Nelson!” Snow: “By Greatness! What luck! Look at those golf sticks! He must be rich! Why, this is the opportunity of a lifetime!” Clerk: “Y-you’re into country music?” Snow doesn’t seem to understand the Clerk’s words. He spins around and glares at the young man... Snow: “What the hell does that have to do with anything!? Damn you, peasant - you’re inferior intellect astounds me!” Willie looks exactly like he always seems to look. Long, braided grey hair showing little of the blond that it did some thirty years ago. He wears hippy clothing, like always, and is carrying a golf bag in his right hand and a guitar case in his left. As he approaches the front desk, Snow begins to panic. The Killin’ Villain whirls around and points a finger in the young Clerk’s fresh face...Snow: “Don’t say a word about this, plebe! As far as you’re concerned, I was never here!” With that, the young PCW Superstar quickly steps out of our view as Willie Nelson steps into it. With Willie speaking to the young Clerk at the front desk, the scene fades slowly to black... Later... The scene opens inside Jason Snow’s luxurious hotel room. The room is decorated in light blue, and there is plenty of evidence lying around that proves Snow is, indeed, staying here. We’re in the living room, which is rather large and spacious. There’s a large television on one end, a blue leather sofa on the other... and in the middle... is Willie Nelson... Willie: “I can’t say I understand what all this is about.” Snow: “Oh! Sure! Play ignorant, plebe!” Willie: “Plebe? Huh? Look, I think you got the wrong fella, son.” Willie would no-doubt try to run if he weren’t tied to a chair. He doesn’t appear to have been harmed, which is the only good sign in this entire situation.... Snow: “I have no such thing! How many rich Nelson’s can there be on this planet? Considering the ‘stupid’ gene that they all seem to exhibit!” Willie: “I really don’t know what you’re talking about, son.” Snow: “You can deny it all you want, peasant! But I - with my Superior Intellect - know the truth!” Willie: “What truth!?” Snow: “That YOU, peasant, are the father of that low-life scum, Tyler Nelson!” It’s official. Snow has snapped. The thought that a man like Willie Nelson, and a man like Tyler Nelson, can exist on the same planet is unbelievable enough. The thought that two men, so opposite, can be related... only in the twisted mind of Jason Snow... Willie: “Tyler? Well, son, I’ve got a lot of kids, to be honest with ya. But I can’t recall any Tylers...” Snow: “Don’t deny it, Nelson! You can’t lie to me! I’m the Killin’ Villain, damn it! I’m a Superior Individual! I can see through your lies! Why, by Greatness, look at you! You’re a splitting image of that has-been bastard!” The camera closes in on Willie’s face... how Snow can think they look ANYTHING alike is beyond any of us. Willie is short, skinny, and only the most desperate of women would think him good-looking. Tyler Nelson, on the other hand, is tall, handsome, and in as good shape as anyone... Yet, to Jason Snow, they are apparently as identical as twins... Willie: “Look, son, I really don’t know what you’re talking about. This... Tyler... I’ve never met him before in my life!” Snow: “Oh! Then how in the hell do you explain your money!?” Willie: “I’m an international country superstar!” Snow scowls... Snow: “Don’t lie to me peasant! If you really sang ‘coun-try mu-zik’ then you would no doubt be wearing one of those silly cowboy hats!” Willie: “You don’t have to wear a cowboy hat to sing country music!” Snow: “What kind of a bafoon do you take me for, Nelson! You’re not getting out of here! Not until... not until...” At this moment, Snow realizes that he doesn’t have much motive behind kidnapping Willie. He never actually considered what kidnapping the aging hippy would do for him. Still, he decides that it can’t be a bad thing to hold a relative of Tyler Nelson captive... Snow: “Until...” A brilliant idea strikes Snow... Snow: “Until you FORCE your evil spawn to hand over control of PCW to ME!” Willie gives Snow a peculiar look - he thinks the young former PCW Champion is crazy. Well, lets be honest, he’s probably not that far off the mark. Snow’s more loonie than a Canadian dollar... Willie: “Until I what? Look, I keep telling you - you’ve got the wrong guy! I’m Willie Nelson! You know, Willie Nelson!? The guy who sings all those old, catchy, country songs?” Snow: “I’ve never in all my Superior days seen a man lie through his smelly yellow teeth like you! Admit it! You are the father of Tyler Nelson!” Willie: “I don’t even know who Tyler Nelson is!” Determined that Willie Nelson is lying, the former PCW Champion tries hard to devise a plan. Moments later, he believes that he’s found one... Snow: “Ah Ha!” Willie: “What?” Snow: “I suppose that if you really are who you say you are, then I suppose there would be a guitar in here!” Willie: “Well, yes, but there’s also...” Snow smirks... Snow: “Well, we’ll just see about... what the...” Snow opens the guitar case and finds that there is a guitar within its confines, however, it is Willie Nelson’s guitar - anyone who’s ever seen Willie Nelson’s guitar knows that it is riddled with holes and the strings point out in all directions. Even the wood looks rotted... Snow picks up the guitar... Snow: “I- ... I thought you were rich! What the hell is this thing!?” Willie: “That’s my guitar. It went to ‘Nam with me.” Snow: “I’m sure it did! The damn thing looks like it won the Purple Heart!” Willie: “No, no! Not the war, man! Farm Aid! We had Farm Aid in ‘Nam, way way back.” Snow: “I’m not going to even pretend to give a damn what Farm Aid is! Are you trying to tell me that you actually play this thing!?” Willie: “As often as I can.” Snow: “OH MY GREATNESS! This is the most inferior excuse for a guitar that I’ve ever seen! And another th... ... ...” Snow looks into the guitar case, his face a painting of curiousity... Snow: “What’s that?” Willie: “Oh, that’s ... uh... that’s nothing...” Snow reaches into the guitar case, which we thought was empty, and produces a small bag of marijuana... Willie Nelson’s face turns bright red, hoping the Killin’ Villain doesn’t turn him over to the police - then again, Willie could always turn Snow over for kidnapping. Well, at least now they both hold something over the other, so maybe Snow won’t be going back to prison... Snow: “By Greatness!” Snow holds the bag up for Willie to see... Snow: “Someone put a bag of kitchen spices in your guitar case!” Willie breathes a sigh of relief as Snow tosses the bag aside - though we do catch Willie making sure he knows exactly where the bag lands... for further reference. Snow sets the guitar down in front of Nelson and then proceeds to cautiously untie Nelson’s hands. After putting the guitar in Willie’s lap, Snow adds sarcastically... Snow: “Well, if you really are a country superstar, and I doubt that there even is such a thing, play me a song, cowboy!” Willie: “Well, I’d be glad to...” Wille strums an odd-sounding chord on the beaten guitar... Willie: “This is one that Carlos and I used to play...” Willie begins playing a semi-slow tune. When he sings, however, Snow is in for a shock... Willie: “Where’s my pal? Where’s my friend? All good things must ha-ave an end...” His words go through Snow’s head like a nail! Obviously, the Killin’ Villain wasn’t ready for Willie Nelson’s “unique” voice. Immediately, he begins pleading with Willie to cut the music... Snow: “OH MY GREATNESS! You sound like a rat, screeching for its life while being devoured by a drowning cat!” Willie continues the song... Willie: “Oh, they all went... to Mexico...” Snow: “Damn you, peasant! Stop that horrid racket!” Willie: “I guess they went, to Mexico...” Snow turns his back to Willie, holding his hands over his ears to stop Willie’s singing from penetrating. Willie, being the sly opportunist that he is, spots the chance to escape. Now untied, and with Snow’s back to him, the country music sensation leaps up from his chair and cracks the Killin’ Villain in the back of the head with his prized guitar! For a moment, it seems that the blow doesn’t phase Snow... Snow: “And another thing! No halipeno peppers! By Greatness, if I should find but ONE hali... ... ugh...” Suddenly, Snow collapses in a heap on the floor - unconscious. Willie, gripping his demolished guitar like a baseball bat, waits for the fallen wrestler to get up. When it’s apparent that Snow is down for the count, the country music sensation looks at his shattered guitar and shrugs... Willie: “Nothin’ a little duct tape and gum won’t fix.” Quickly, Willie runs out of the room, leaving Snow lying in a pool of his own drool on the floor... ...A moment passes... Just as the scene is about to fade to black, Willie comes rushing back in - his guitar still in hand - grabs his bag of marijuana, and then makes his second exit. With Snow sleeping soundly on the floor, and Willie’s footsteps echoing in the hall, the scene fades to black...
