Concrete Parents

15 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-15-03 “It’s been a long time. Sorry about not visiting sooner but some personal things came up that I have been trying to deal with.” The voice rang out as the evening sun was just starting to set into the horizon. The skies were painted with colors of orange, red, and yellow as if heaven itself was burning. There was a deep sigh. Peering down, the back of Chris Storm can be seen. He is standing in a cemetery in front of two head stones. He is kneeling down in front of both of them. Chris was dressed in a white shirt with black tribal patterns running along it. Pressed white pants covered him followed by polished black dress shows. A pair of sleek black sunglasses covers his eyes. “I really wish you was both here now. Maybe if you two where then things wouldn’t be so screwed up for me now.” It’s clear now Chris is talking to the two tombstones. Looking closer, both of the tombstones are unmarked. No name, any time of death, or messages from beloved family members; just cold, hard concrete. However it would seem from the way he is talking that he knows whom these two people where. “Guess what though! I have good news. I just was informed that Tessa and I broke P.W.A.’s record for the longest Tag team title reign. I think its like 53 days holding onto the titles. That means we are in the PCW record books. I am so proud of Tessa. This is her second award, also being known as the first ever, female tag team champion. Isn’t that great?” His voice is more cheery now as he thinks of his recent success. Chris grabs a gym bag from beside him. From inside it he pulls out his half of the Tag Team titles. He holds it up in front of both of the tombstones as if they could actually see the title. “You know they can’t hear you.” Chris slowly turns around to see who said that. In the corner of his eye he sees something coming at him. Chris dives backwards to avoid it. A metal baseball bat hits the left tombstone, breaking the corner off of it. Broken concrete rains down almost as if in slow motion. Chris just stares at it, his jaw dropped. His head tilts to see who is behind the baseball bat. The smiling face of Ein is standing there greeting him. Anger starts to boil up inside Chris. “You!” he shouts. The twin image of Chris, Ein, stands over him looking down. The baseball bat in his hand still rests on the head of the tombstone. “See what you made me do?” Ein growls at Chris. Ein takes the baseball bat and swings it once more at Chris. Chris has to roll away, avoiding the tip of the bat by just inches. He scrambles to his feet, getting into a fighting stance against Ein. Ein swings the bat back and forth, holding the point of it at Chris Storm, an angry look on his face. Chris keeps backing up, trying to avoid Ein’s wrath. Ein keeps circling around slowing making Chris back to facing the tombstones. When Chris finally has his back to the tombstone once more, an Ein lung forward and manages to catch Chris by surprise. The tip of the bat is shoved right into Chris’ throat, forcing him to lean back against the tombstones. “So little brother, come to visit mom and dad did you?” Chris’ eyes grew to an amazing size when Ein said that. His jaw dropped and seemed to hit the floor. All he could do was stutter back, “little brother…mom and dad…” A slight laughter rolled from Ein’s lips as the information sinks into Chris’ head. He was enjoying this so much. Ein moves the bat up, tapping Chris on the chin with it. “Oh yes baby brother you heard me right. You were just talking to our dear old mom and pops there. Don’t tell me you didn’t know we were brothers? We look exactly the same. Thought it was just a rare thing? Why do you think you heard me in your head? We have a bond that is closer then blood.” Ein’s words came out like acid to Chris. He tries to cover his ears to keep from hearing them but Ein shoves the bat even harder into his throat. Managing to turn around, Chris stares at the two blank tombstones. He can feel his knees getting weak as his most hated enemy stands before him telling him about his past. Chris just had to ask though. “Do you know what my real name is?” That sinister smile crosses Ein’s lips. “Oh you mean you don’t know? Well isn’t that interesting. I think that is something you need to discover on your own brother.” Ein just laughs as he shoves Chris back more with the tip of the bat. Not getting the answer he wanted, Chris becomes angrier and grabs a handful of the broken concrete still resting on the tombstone. With a quick flick of the wrist, Chris throws the concrete into Ein’s eyes blinding him. The bat goes loose from Chris’ neck and he takes the opportunity to rush him. He lifts him up onto his shoulder making him drop the baseball bat. Chris drives Ein right into a nearby willow tree. He repeatedly slams him into the trunk of the tree, harder each time. Trying to fight back, Ein brought down both hands onto Chris’ back in an Ax Handle Smash. At the same time, Ein brings his knees up and into the chest of Chris Storm. Chris is lost in his anger however, as he continues to try and break Ein’s back. Chris finally throws Ein to the side, away from the tombstones. He backs off and gets into a fighting stance he uses for Teal Chi fighting. “Come on Ein, I’m about to show you just why they call me the Perfect Storm!” Chris shouts at his twin brother. Ein manages to pull himself off the ground and stands upright laughing. Chris’ eyebrow rises as he stares at Ein in disbelief. Even after all that Ein still made it seem like he wasn’t even bothered from it. “Foolish boy; you still don’t seem to understand do you? I’m your superior in every way!” Ein screams back at Chris. He gets into his own fighting stance, one very dissimilar from Chris’ own. Slightly startled but Chris stays strong. He and Ein charge at each other and start to swing blows at each other. They block each others punches until Ein manages to snake his foot and kick Chris’ shin. Losing his footing for a moment, Chris becomes victim to a palm thrust to the chest. He stumbles back, as Ein comes in and hits him with a backhand slap followed by bringing that elbow in and coming back, smashing it into Chris’ face. At the same time his other hand comes up hitting him with an Uppercut Palm Strike. Chris flips back and lands on the hard ground. Ein laughs as he stands back up straight. Ein moves his head side to side, popping his neck. The cracking echoes through the silent graveyard. He unbuttons his black silk shirt. Red and white dragons are embroidered on the front and stretch around the body of it. Red dress pants roll down his legs meeting his fine black shoes. He looked so much like Chris yet there was so much different about him. The way he stood, the way he breathed, the face expressions he carried. So much hate and loathing in all of them that there was no way it could be Chris Storm, not after finding the love of his life in Tessa Windsor.Finally starting to move again, Chris looks up at Ein standing there so cocky. The flames of hate inside Chris start to burn more intense. He comes back to his feet. Rather then say anything, Ein just motions with his hand for Chris to come at him. Chris starts to run at him once more but Ein smirks and takes two steps, kicking forward sending dust into Chris’ eyes. Once Ein sees that Chris is temporarily blinded, he punches him closed fisted in the throat. Chris clenches his throat as he gasp for air. Ein drops his knee into the back of Chris’ knee bringing Chris down to the ground on his knees. Ein grabs his spiked blond hair and slams Chris’ face down into the tombstone of his parents. “See this Chris? Your parent’s tombstones! You caused their death!” Ein shouts at him as he rubs Chris’ face into the concrete slab. Tears and blood roll down Chris’ face. He just closes his eyes as his older twin brother continues to drive punch after punch into the shoulder blades. Everything all around just goes silent. The birds settle down, the crickets stop playing their song, the air gently stops moving. Only the sound of Chris Storm’s breathing can be heard. Then the silence shatters like glass to a loud snapping noise. A heart beat starts to pick up and become very rapid. “What the…?” is all Ein can say before Chris brings back his elbow into his stomach. Driving his elbow in three more times, Chris is able to break free from Ein. He comes up and nails a Mule Kick on Ein sending him back away. Ein doesn’t fall though as he keeps his footing. He looks up at Chris shocked and confused. Chris just slowly rises back to his feet. Blood and dirt are smeared on his white clothes. Chris simply brushes some of the dirt off him and turns to face Ein. His face is already showing signs of bruising and blood clings to his face and hair. With a flick of his head, his hair flings back behind him moving some of the dirt and blood out of his eyes. Chris just looks at Ein with those hate-filled eyes. It was at that moment that Ein knew this fight was just starting… TO BE CONTINUED!!!!!
