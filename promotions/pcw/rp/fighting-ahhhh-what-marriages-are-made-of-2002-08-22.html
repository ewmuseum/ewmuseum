Fighting, ahhhh, what marriages are made of.

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/22/2002 *The scene opens up inside of the Jon Kano's beautiful home. To be more precise, the living room, decorated with various articles of Kano propaganda (pictures, posters, t-shirts, logos, etc.). Jon Kano is decked out in baggy blue jeans, black Doc Martins, and a tight black t-shirt with 'WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF' across the chest, which exposes every single muscle in Kano's upper body. Kano's wife, Kristin, is dressed in tight black pants and a royal blue shirt. Jon Kano and his vivacious wife are standing facing each other about eight feet away from each other, in a heated dispute.* Kristin: God damnit, Jon, you promised me you were done. Kano: Shoot bullets through me, I lied, it appears now. When I told you that, I really did think I was done. The doctor told me I was history. Then last week, I went to the best doctor in Vegas, probably the best doctor this side of the Mississippi, and he told me that I still could do it. My body just needed a little rest. Kristin: I am so f*cking tired of worrying about you, Jon. The last six years is all I have done is worry about you. Do you think I like to watch you on television or when I am backstage taking a chair shot or getting dumped on your head? You think it is fun for me to wonder if the next day will be the last time you are actually functional? You think all you have to do is take care of yourself in the ring, what about me? You are so selfish. *Jon Kano walks over to his wife, and gets about three inches from her face, with a face full of anger.* Kano: WHAT DID YOU SAY? WHAT THE F*CK DID YOU JUST SAY? DO YOU THINK I DID ANY OF THIS FOR ME? DO YOU THINK I FOUNDED KANO ENTERPRISES FOR ME? DO YOU THINK I DID EVERYTHING FOR ME? DO YOU HONESTLY THINK THAT I AM GOING BACK FOR ME? I AM DOING IT FOR YOU AND THAT CHILD OF OURS THAT IS IN YOUR STOMACH RIGHT NOW. *Jon Kano walks past his wife and out the front door, knocking the door straight off the hinges as he slams it behind him. Kano hops into his brand new 2002 Nissan Altima 3.5 SE and roasts his tires out of the driveway as the scene fades out.* *The scene fades back in inside of Jon Kano's Altima, parked on a cliff on the opposite side of Las Vegas from which his home sits. Kano is laying on his hood, looking up into the dusk sky.* Kano: God damnit anyway. I swear to God I love her more than anyone or anything in this world, but she knows how to piss me off. She thinks I am going back for my own pride. Yeah right. I am going back so she doesn't have to hear from people that her husband was a pussy because he retired. I am doing it for my unborn children for the same reason. I don't give a f*ck what people say about me, because I know that I was and am everything I am could be. I gave it my all, and I have no regrets. I don't regret not kissing Tyler's ass for all those months, because I was a man. I wasn't like Jeremy Howard who sold out and then turned his back when he got what he needed. I wasn't Xavier Kannon who got where he is today by kissing ass. I stood on my own two feet and told people to bring what they had. Duke and I stood together and defended the honor of tradition in PCW. When Corey Williams threw me off the stage, I knew that if that ended my career, I knew that I did all that I could do. I never once aspired to be the biggest name in professional wrestling. I never once believed that I could be as big as I am now in the world of pro wrestling. When she and I met, we were just kids, 15 years old. We met when I was playing baseball for a summer league team. Her father was an umpire and she was down there to get a little money off him. I went to bat and our eyes locked in on each other. She liked what she saw, and my God, I loved what I saw. I caught up to her after the game and invited her to this beer bash me and a couple of my friends were throwing. As they say, the rest is history. *Kano moves from laying on his hood, to sitting on it.* Kano: But as much history is between her and I, as much as I love her, as good of a husband as I try to be, as could of a father I will be, nothing in this world can stop me from going into Third Dawning and kicking the hell out of Corey Williams. That son of a bitch threw me off of a stage, fifteen feet to a concrete floor. He wasn't aiming to hurt me, he was aiming to end my career and cripple my ass. When I was falling to the floor all I could think about was her. So my whole motivation for coming back is and was her. *A smile crosses Kano's face.* Kano: But I would give every cent I have to see that expression on Corey's face when he spun around and saw me standing right there in the middle of the ring. I am not sure, but I think he shit himself because he knew a whole lot of hurt was coming his way on that night, and on Sunday night, I ain't gonna stop with hurting him, I am ending his career. His career is done after Sunday, and I will be the one standing tall and proud over his broken body with thousands upon thousands of Kanomaniacs will be chanting, 'Kano ROCKS~!' I can't stop there, though. After Sunday, Kano is back in PCW, bigger and badder than ever. Sunday is truely the dawning of a new era, an era that has been two years in the making, it is time, my friends, for the era of Kano supremacy in PCW. God have mercy, because I won't. On Sunday, whether the match is happening or not, I will prove to Corey Williams and to everyone else once again why I am what REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!! *The scene fades to black as Kano continues to stare out on his city, Las Vegas.*
