Thoughts Of Gold Are Working Out

10 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-10-02 One! Two! His muscles pump and the heavy bar goes up. Down. Up. Three! Four! Sweat starts to roll down his face. But his eyes are focused in at the ceiling. Nothing can break his concentration. He is in the zone. Not feeling the pain. Not feeling the burning in his arms. Six! Seven! Such pain! Such agony! But he is no where’s near it. No… his mind is far away, thinking of the past, the future… and opportunities that await him. This man… he is not a man. He is more than a man. Twelve! Thirteen! He is strong. Too strong—he cannot be stopped. In his mind he is invincible. Eighteen! Nineteen! He will not back down—he cannot back down! He is a warrior—he is the warrior! …He is the Infinite Warrior. He is… Trigger. Trigger: I knew right away that he wasn’t scared. Too cocky, too ignorant—that’s what he was. He never really contemplated the fact that he could be upended; defeated by the one he so underestimated. Xavior Kannon. What a foolish name. What a foolish being. How can he be so sure? How can he not even prepare for a possible loss? Oh…it is befuddling, yes. But I can bide my time, fore tragedy strikes those who least expect it all the harder. Thirty-One! Thirty-Two! He snaps back to reality. He realizes the sweat pouring down his body. He realizes the writing pain in his arms. He keeps going. Thirty-Three! Thirty-Four! Just one more… He pulls all the strength inside of him. Just one more… Thirty-Five! He rolls the weight to its holder behind him, and gets up. He looks around… a quiet day at the gym, it seems. A usual occurrence, seeing as it is so early in the morning. If he minds, Trigger doesn’t show it. He spots a treadmill and makes his way towards it—no hardships are spared when the Infinite Warrior feels the need for training. As he starts up the contraption, he thinks back to when he began wrestling. To the training that he endured then in relevance to now. He finds himself breathing harder, and struggling to keep up with his regular routines. Although minimal are the differences, he knows he is getting older… Trigger: My body is wearing out. I am deteriorating. Yet in my wrestling career—in PCW—I am in the position of a newcomer. Why are my goals unfulfilled? I should be on top of the world at this very moment. I’ve earned it. I’ve suffered through many long, hard years… I’ve waited for my chance… but it never came! Until Tuesday… Tuesday I will have my chance, my one and only chance, to prove to the world that Trigger is not washed up. Trigger: The PCW World Championship. With what other prize comes instant fame and glory? I’m not in it for the glory. Only the respect. Some may say I am too materialistic. Why would I need a belt to signify my glory? I don’t. I am perfectly content. It’s the others I am worried about—the fans! They cannot respect a man unless they know his name. Come Tuesday, they will know my name. And my face. And my talent. He steps off the treadmill, still not bothering to wipe the increasing sweat from his body. It keeps him going. The pain, the nausea, the fatigue… it only encourages further intensity. Stumbling around, Trigger spots his next workout—a punching bag, suspended from the ceiling. Boom! His blows go into it like a freight train, taking out all of his anguish… delivering all of his fury. Pound! Pound! Such intensity! Now… he drifts away… back to his zone… back to his thoughts… Trigger: Ironic, isn’t it? No, not really. More like fate. It was only the other day when the new plan came to my mind. I was in the gutter, down and out, but I had one more idea…Perhaps I had been wrong all along. Fulfillment of life was not entirely about being a warrior. But what was it about? I had to find out. So I began this journey—this path of searching. And although I have yet to find the meaning, I am on the right path. Isn’t it funny? I was in the worst possible spot… and now an opportunity arises. An opportunity so great, so grand, its glory is priceless. The PCW World Championship. Nothing—to that! I must be doing something right. He must be doing something right… the thought lingers in his mind as his body slows down and his head comes back to reality. He looks around. The morning has progressed, and now people are filling up inside the gym. Trigger stands there, breathing heavily for several seconds. He heads for the door… but not before muttering under his breath… Trigger: I will do something right…
