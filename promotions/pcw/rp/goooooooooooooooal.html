GOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOAL

undated

Date: Just Now, Dammit Total apocalypse. Is this a grim look into a horrible future of some kind, where our own technological creations have gotten the better of us? The horror seems to stretch as far as the eye can see. The terrain is rough to cross, as it is a massive graveyard; simply human bones layered upon human bones. You must be swift, and steady, because one split second on your face, and you’ll be a victim of a bomb shell, ventilated into the atmosphere. In fact, anyone with half a mind would advise you to avoid moving in a straight path for more than a ten seconds. Even more awful than the bomb shells, the most frightening of all the chaos is the emotionless, unrelenting, mechanical monsters that roam about, targeting any living organism that moves. Racing through the line of fire a small battalion of war ravaged men have somehow breached the enemies defenses, a clever sneak attack into unseen territory. Warriors with nothing to lose, they stride on fearlessly, each one knowing deep in his heart that if he were to die this day... Perhaps, his contribution could be just enough for his comrades to achieve the greater good. They are only nine, and with such weak numbers left, these valiant men know they must succeed, lest all will have been fought for in vain. They’re mission, easier said than done, is to deliver a single item to a destination somewhere in the direction they are heading. To confuse the robotic devils, they alternate the item between them, but unfortunately, no man has made it as far as they have, so they don’t know how to find this drop off point. Thus, they must stay on the move, and work as a team like never before. Maintaining their numbers could be helpful, as well... An ear shattering explosion overhead alerts the invaders that they are not undetected anymore, and viciously levels the straggler in the rear of the pack to the ground. Only one fool dares to compromise it all, turning back for his ally... Both are wiped out in a hail rapid fire. The rest don’t even look back, afraid to even think about it. Allot of good men gave up everything to get them this far, and to risk they’re efforts is sick. Driving forward, a right wing man obtains the item, and breaks away to the head of the pack. The hair raising sound of a falling bomb shell echoes above, its high pitched screech indicating they must flank right, or risk traveling directly into its impact point. Instincts lead them to the right, guiding them straight into the aim of a robot firing squad. They are trapped, and for a deadly moment they stop, looking to the man with the item. He needs to decide on the spot which is the right way to go, and with haste. Sadly, there is no right way, so he launches the precious item the furthest he can before his untimely downfall. Two more follow him... The remaining four, stunned by their own still pumping hearts, stride on harder than ever before. Followed by a wave of unleashed ammunition that narrowly misses them, they close in on the item that still is in motion. Only now, their darkest fears have come true. The metallic opposition has now targeted their item, rather than its carriers, and the entire operation rests completely on that item! A final flurry of hope, one of the four thrusts himself into the chaotic onslaught that surrounds the item, taking several hits in the chest for it. Meanwhile, passing his downed friend, one of the final tree intercepts the item right before another is vaporized by a sudden pulverizing attack from behind. Only two left, they look back to see the behemoth shock tank lingering behind them, crushing the bony surface beneath its weight. It’s cannon still smokes from the last shot fired. With the added initiative to pick up their pace behind them, this duo’s prayers are finally answered with a sighting not far ahead. It’s what they came to find, and heaven help them, they’ll deliver this item to it. THEY WILL KICK THIS SOCCER BALL INTO THAT GOAL IF IT’S THE LAST THING THEY EVER DO! Giving his teammate the signal to go all the way to the goal, he dribbles the white and black ball between quick feet, to throw the chasing team off, and at the last possible second, he delivers, punting the ball to his partner who follows up with a kick right into the GOAL! Celebrating such a victory, the two sprint in a collision course, embracing one another with tightly squeezed arms. This is their moment, and it is only glorified by the cheers of millions behind them. Yet, the moment makes them completely oblivious to the fact that all gunfire has ceased, and blind to the presence of no goalie... Terror overflows the hearts of all, as the goal dissolves from it’s solid state to liquid. Chrome water could accurately describe it. The steel toned puddle bubbles for a minute, before rising up into its true form. Now a metallic golem sporting a fearsome photon cannon appendage, the juggernaut casts a shadow over the unsuspecting two, right before firing the fruits of their labor into they’re very own... “GOOOOOOOOOOOOAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAL~!” Brazil-99Portugal-0 The seats are packed to the maximum, all eyes watching intentively as this wacky, in fact disturbing fiasco of a game unfolds before them. This happens to be one of the biggest soccer games of the year, and an important one to Juan “The Golden Heart” Borro. His old teammates are being slaughtered on his old turf, and guess who’s behind it... We find the maniacal Goblin on the sidelines, conducting one of his most evil schemes yet. The appearance of this particular scenario is questionable, but facing the facts, it’s easy to conclude no one ever predict what Syko will do next. Disguised in the Brazilian Soccer coach’s uniform, the runt pecks away at a hand held black box of some sort. Syko the Goblin: “Yes, my invincible KILL-BOTS! Destroy the pathetic Portugal pansies! And be sure to capture that girly essence that makes this spectacle so mockable!” Kill-bots? Perhaps that might explain the oversized, steelclad brutes that surround Syko. It’s a wonder they can fit into a Brazilian uniform, let alone pass undetected by the referees. Cut to a scene of one of the referees sitting in front of a television screen that projects the image of the Goblin. The poor man holds his face in his hands, sobbing uncontrollably. Syko on TV: “IF YOU EVER WANT TO SEE YOU DAUGHTER AGAIN...” Back on the field, the same official is approached by another who seems slightly suspicious of the Brazilians. Catching a glimpse of Syko pretending to slight his throat with his thumb out of the corner of his eye, he just shrugs, pretending to not see anything. Syko the Goblin: “MUWAHAHA! Pitiful, tool! HAHAH!” Feeling a tap on his shoulder, Syko turns to see one his kill-bots pointing out towards their goal. Kill-Bot: “Lord Syko, they are attempting another strike.” Indeed, another gallantly charging group of Portugal pukes are heading towards the goal. Outraged by a second break in his unstoppable defense, Syko’s face goes red. Syko the Goblin: “Bah, you swill! How come you didn’t tell me sooner?” Kill-Bot: “There is a reason I am always on the bench...” Syko the Goblin: “GAHR! I’ll do it myself, then!” In rapid succession, Syko pegs several buttons on his little black box, snickering to himself. Out in the field, one the pursuing kill-bots stops, falling to all four limbs; each of which having a drill reaching far into the ground, to keep it unmovable. Bent in half and stood up like a four legged table, the mechanisms “back” begins to spread apart. Emerging from the new gap a slender, sharp tipped missile protrudes. Transformed Kill-Bot: “Count down in T-minus 5...” At this point, anyone remotely close to it has scrammed, anticipating total annihilation from a safer distance. Transformed Kill-Bot: “...4...3...2...1...Fire!” Moments later, the Brazilian goal is reduced to ashes, making it impossible for anyone to score. Half time Down in below the seats of the soccer stadium, there is some serious tension inside the Portugal locker room. “WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON OUT THERE?! DO YOU ALL HAVE NO PRIDE?!” Almost talking to himself, the Portugal head coach paces back and fourth before three players on a bench, who clutch one another in fear. This is whats left of the team. Shaking violently, their faces ghost white, they give off the impression that they are slightly... frightened. Player 1: “Hey, coach... those Brazilian bastards! They aren’t human! You can’t blame us!” Coach: “What do you mean?! I know a bunch of Brazilian’s when I see them, and those ones just kicked your asses!!” Player 2: “P-p-p-pu-leeze! I have children! I don’t...” Suddenly the door to the locker room opens, and a fourth player limps in, a coat of sweat over his body. Immediatly his teammates are surrounding him, trying to figure out what happened. Player 1,2, & 3: “SERGIO!” Sergio: “No time! Coach... I must show... you...” Soon Coach: “Well, this is odd...” The five of them, the last of team Portugal are pressed up against a hallway wall, spying undetected around the corner at two kill-bots with fire arms standing guard over a door marked, “Brazil.” Coach: “Why would they need guards by their locker room?” Sergio: “They... ... secret... (Releases an exausted gasp.)” They’re friend collapsing to the ground, they huddle together, only whispers passing between them, as they try to formulate a plan. With little time before the second half the three able bodied players pull out a soccer ball, and cunningly roll it around the corner. Kill-Bot Senty’s: “KILL BALL!” Opening fire, the robots attention is drawn to the target ball giving the soccer players just enough time to... Coach: “NOW!” Moments later, a few seconds to spare with the stunned kill-bots down, they slip into the locker room to find... Pitch black. Feeling the wall, the coach finds a switch, illuminating a room full of bound and gagged Brazilian’s in nothing, but the clothes God gave them. Speechless, they stare at the room in awe, unsettled until a succession of loud clicks break the silence. There is an unholy pressence behind them... Syko the Goblin: “Borro... this one’s for you! MUWAHAHAHA
