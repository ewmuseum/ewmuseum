Just another day....

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/24/02 -Duke Williams is sun bathing on the roof of a Chicago apartment.- Williams: You ever get the feeling of not wanting to work? -Duke takes a sip from his frozen margarita.- Williams: You every just not want to cut a promo, or work out, or call a match? -Duke gulps down some more of the frozen booze loaded treat.- Williams: That’s the way I feel right now. So I’ve decided to do it while relaxing and see how that works out for me. -Duke turns down the radio which was cooking out a Haggard tune.- Williams: So what should I talk about first? I see I’m way behind in work with Miya, Legend, and Kannon running there mouths like it’s a f*cking marathon. Slow down boys you only have one life to live. The old rule of thumb in wrestling is the fans only remember a week back. So shut up and wait for that week. Here it is now the sleeping giants been awoken. -Duke growls.- Williams: Lets run down the hairy assed hillbillies standing in the way of my Continental title. First lets start with the basics or history, wrestling, and odds. History and facts are not in my favor. In the last five years of my career and especially here in PCW when it comes to PPV title matches I have always ended up on the short end of the stick. Maybe I don’t want it as bad as the others. Maybe I get the runs, eat too much, drink to much, or just plain sleep to late. -Duke squirts some water on his chest to cool him down. – Williams: Hell if I know but the truth is PPV title matches are not my strong suit. Then lets get to the history of things. Stories are a big thing in wrestling. What’s the story here? Miya and Kannon and the wedding. Legend and Ole Duke are just an added bonus to the match. We don’t belong. People who don’t belong tend to lose matches they’re not invited to. So how do you correct that? -Duke smacks his belly.- Williams: Make some f*cking waves man. That’s just what this old buckaroo is going to do. How? Stay tuned hoss. -Duke lights up a punch rare cojova cigar.- Williams: That brings us to statistics. Since I’ve returned from retirement I’ve obtained a winning streak similar to the Cubs. Being a Sox fan that upsets me a bit. For those of you who aren’t baseball fans surly you know the Cubs tend to lose more then they win. It sucks watching my win/lose average slipping to below respectable standards. Miya, Legend, Kannon, this is the stat that should concern you the most. I’ve lost some many fights I’m bound to win one someday and that someday is Sunday. -Duke ashes his smoke and takes a swig from a bottle of whiskey next to his margarita.- Williams: So that brings us to the cast of characters starting with the one I’m most familiar with Xavior “Bitchalot” Kannon. Now this hump got a win against me in last months PPV. Here we our again. Thought you would move on didn’t you bitch? You thought that loss you gave me would send me back to retirement. Back to the basics of life. Kid your way off base. So you’re marring that cum rag. That same used tampon who gave you the win during our last continental go round. Congratulations, you two dip dongs are perfect for each other. Both are annoying, ugly, out of your minds, and chicken shit’s. No really it’s a fact. I don’t make shit up. -Duke opens up a dictionary laying next to him. He flips to the center of the book where somebody wrote in thick black marker “DIPSHIT’S” under it is a poorly pasted picture of Kannon and Ellie.- Williams: See you hairy assed hillbilly, I ain’t lying. -Duke tosses the book and takes a sip of his frozen concoction. A big grin is on his face.- Williams: That moves me right along to Legend INC. This kid was in the Malice Crew for like a week. When I drafted up the original plan he was in it. Then a week later he disappeared. Now I’m not one to spread nasty false rumors but what I heard is he was in court fighting the priest who molested him as a child. Now I have no proof of this but I am a respected PCW journalist. If you remember about the time he disappeared is about the time the Catholic Church scandal broke. Things that make you go hummmmmmm. -Duke sips down some more frozen booze.- Williams: This so-called good guy left the Malice Crew high and dry. This so called fan favorite was nowhere to be found when he was needed by the people. The Malice Man has always been there. Weather its been reporting the inconsistency of the The Red Army as a PCW journalist to fighting the battle for control of this company. I’ve taken them all on. Where were you Tom?? Huh? Social injustice, criminal activity, run-ins, run downs, you were nowhere to be found. -Duke rubs some lotion on his shoulders.- Williams: You claim to be a good guy but this is a now society and we all want to know right NOW what have you done for us lately? Huh? What have you given the people to cheer for? You even going to acknowledge me in your promos or are you going to live in your fantasy world? Tom in your mind you’re a star and a hero. This isn’t your mind however this is the real world. A world in which you’ve accomplished nothing. This is PCW. Primetime Wrestling Hoss. Are you ready for Primetime? You’ve dropped the ball once lets see you do it again. Forget your past, don’t worry about the RP God, and lets find out what your future in the Prime Time is all about. This is a test and Sunday you need to pass. However the only thing you will pass is through is the ring and into hell as I send you Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound. -Duke swats a fly- Williams: That brings me to the final competitor in this little caged continental show down and that being Alex Miayagawa. Now I have no history with Miya as matter of fact our paths have always been kind of vertical and have never intersected. Now I’m no dummy like Kannon. I’m no rookie like Legend. So I know what I’m up against when I step into the ring with this talented kid. However Alex, that just means I’m going to come at you extra hard and then let you and Kannon brawl it out. You have a pre-scripted agenda. I don’t. I’m not there for revenge against Kannon, to teach Legend a lesson, or to prove I’m the best by beating Miya I’m just there to win the Continental title. You see the secret is to keep it simple and that’s what I’ve done. -Duke takes a shot out of the whiskey bottle.- Williams: So PCW has decided to put us into a double cage extreme match. Two layers of danger and possibly death. HA I love it. That’s what I call malice style hoss. Final something falls in my favor. So we have two, maybe three pretty boys in a cage with a Malice Man?? Damn dog its liking like I see some sunshine and not in a Borro kind of way. -Duke smiles as he cracks his neck.- Williams: Enough about Gold and Glory we’ll have enough time to yack on dat in da next week. Lets talk a little turmoil. Not the kind of Turmoil an Xavior Kannon Honeymoon will create. I’m talking Tuesday Turmoil when the Malice Man, The Man Who Rocks, and Miya take on that piece of trash the Chef, Kannon, and some other guy. The best part about it is Ellie is the ref!! -Duke smiles as he adjusts his shades.- Williams: Now you think I would be upset about being in the same ring as the little anal stretch mark. No its not a problem. -Duke takes a sip of his lemon lime treat.- Williams: I’ve been wanting to get in the ring with her since our last dance. Now what in theory does this match mean?? Does it really make a difference who wins and who loses? My only purpose in this match is to spank her little ass Malice Style. Beat the hell out of that little c*nt. Show her a real bad time while I’m in a real bad mood. So Ellie I’m glad to see your smiling face in this match. I’d also like the personally thank you for signing up to help out the PCW. You’re a real trooper babe. -Duke shines with hillbilly charm.- Williams: Ellie have you ever been Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound? Have you ever dance with the devil in the pale of the moonlight? You want to rock hun, and I want to roll. You dig my hoss jive? Dunce your in for a lot of pain. A LOT OF PAIN. So jiggle that ass for me baby its Malice Time. -Duke gulps down from the bottle.- Williams: So who do we have in the ring if this turns into a wrestling match? -Duke scratches his beard.- Williams: We have Kannon who I’ve already talked about. The dummy is going to get a taste of what’s going to happen to him at gold and glory. The only thing is if he really wants to taste it he’ll have to lick it off Ellie cus that’s where I’m delivering it. -Duke smiles into the camera and sends a wink in Ellies Direction.- Williams: Then we have the Chef. Man have I wanted to get my hands on this guy for a long, long time. A trader to this great land of ours is not a human in my book. This man is lower then dog sh*t. Every dog has its day. However dog sh*t don’t do days, ya dig? -Duke counts on his fingers to three.- Williams: That brings me to the third guy. Who are you again? Alex something? Damned if I know and I’m to lazy to go look. Rookie listen to me. You don’t impress me much. Give it up hoss, cus you don’t impress me. -Duke signals for the camera to cut and it does.-
