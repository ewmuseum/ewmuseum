El Hossa and I?

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Thanks to El&#039;s backstage link! -Duke Williams is out side with a rifle by his side. He is in the back woods of Montana where nature is still the boss and not man. He is wearing a hunting jacket, black jeans, his chrome shades, and a John Deere hat. Duke stands in front of a dead deer which he just shot.- Williams: Dinner for the next week hoss!! -Duke grabs a cigar protector out of his jacket pocket.- Williams: A ten point buck….not a bad kill ah? -Duke pulls out a cigar and sticks it in his mouth.- Williams: Kills…death….bang….bang….no longer. -Duke lights up his cigar.- Williams: If that’s what its going to take to get the job done then so be it. -Duke ties up the deer’s legs.- Williams: I a little pissed off right now. Hell I’m more then pissed I’m steaming!! Clyde thinks he can walk into a PCW arena and f*ck with me?? God damn it I’m the Malice Man!!!! Trust me what I say. -Duke takes off his shades so you can look directly in his eyes.- Williams: Trust me when I say the death that was struck into this deer is a pleasant experience from what I will do to Clyde. I once knew a guy named Clyde. Clyde played electric bass….played it with finesse and grace. Sat on the porch ain’t got no shoes but damn that boy could sing the blues. The blues baby. That’s only half of what you’ll be singing when I get my hands on you! F*cking hump. You and your little cackle of pinmonkeys. -Duke hoists the deer onto his shoulder and starts to walk back home.- Williams: Then who can forget the continental cage match at the ppv? -Dukes cigar hangs out of his mouth- Williams: How the hell did Legend Inc win a match with Xavior, Miya, and myself? He’s not that f*cking good. . A flash in the pan. A one win wonder. Generic. Plastic. A Fraud. -Duke shakes his head.- Williams: The history of that title is very sound. However last Sunday that history was sh*t upon. An undeserving, non-mentioner, non-traditionalist won the title. That makes me gag, vomit, puke, blow chunks, get angry. That makes me want revenge, vengeance, a chance to kill him. I can achieve just that with a victory over the Ultimate Wetback. -The cigar drops out of Dukes mouth.- Williams: Hey Holmes so you’re the Mexican Devil? -Duke makes a thinking face.- Williams: Wow that’s the equivalent of being the president of Uganda. Or the quarter back of a CFL team. Nobody gives a rats ass. Kid I hate to break it to you but I’m the American Devil….The Malice Man. You know I always thought cutting my front lawn was hell. Maybe I can hire you and your super evil power to hedge my bushes hoss. Or is it El Hossa. -Duke laughs at his lame joke.- Williams: So tell me vato who does your makeup? You look like taco bell is running an Ultimate Warrior promotion. Get some soap. I know they don’t have that in Mexico but in the US we have soap. Try Irish spring its my favorite. Take this soap stuff and a washcloth and scrub that gunk off your face. Hell I’m embarrassed to step in the ring with you. -Duke shakes his head in disgust.- Williams: Listen up holmes. I know why Mexico only brought five hundred to the Alamo. They only had two cars. No really come on lets get serious huh? I know things are tough for you. We just passed the most confusing day in Mexico….Father’s day. I told you to stay serious El Taco now pay attention. -Duke spits a big snot rocket.- Williams: You see kid you have the wrong match, on the wrong day, at the wrong time. This isn’t just a big break for you. No. It’s going to be a big break for you body. -Duke kicks a rattle snake out of the way.) Williams: Kido, I want revenge on Lexain and I want it bad. I want to kick his undeserving ass. You dig me El Hossa? You can’t beat him. You can’t beat me. So just give it up. Huh? You ever think of retiring and starting up a landscaping business or maybe retire back to Mexico and sell fruit. I bet you’d do a lot of business with that half ass makeup job. -Duke makes a face of disgust- Williams: Could green, red, and white be the ugliest set of colors ever? I’ve seen better looking things in Plumpers the magazine. Hey hombre no offense but you just cut the enchilada. You may cut the cheese on occasion but that’s not what we’re talking about. -Duke throws the deer into the bed of his pickup truck.- Williams: Fact is fact and the fact is your in my way to the continental title and I’m going to move you. Nothing will stop me this time around. Not Clyde, Kannon, Nelson, Legend, God, or the Mexican Devil. As a matter of fact kid I’m going to send you some place you should be familiar with. Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound and hoss that’s the Honky Tonk Truth. -Duke gets in his truck and drives away.-
