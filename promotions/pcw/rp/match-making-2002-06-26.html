Match Making

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/06/02 Our shot opens up on a busy.. Well, okay not REALLY busy but a street with a fair amount of hustle and bustle lets say. *SREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEECH!!!* The mellow hum of activity is sliced wide open by the high pitched screeching of car tyre’s rubber on city street asphalt that makes an incision through the ears of the pedestrians before cutting right through their skulls.... Metaphorically of course, this isn’t a Clyde production. *THUD* Just as the screeching starts to lower in volume, the sound of metal crunching into metal booms out from the point of collision, followed by an encore of glass shattering and the final jubilant applause that is the car horn. “Oooooooooooohhhhhh....” The pedestrians cringe at the time of impact, most keeping an eye open to see the collision that if it were on TV they would watch with lustre. In a city home to 10’s of 1000’s of cars, all eyes are now on these 2 as their respective drivers stagger out, both seemingly un-harmed. “What the fuck are you doing?” That is un-harmed and pissed off, may I add. “What am I doing? YOU could have killed me!” Both men throw their hands up in frustration.. And the thought of their insurance premiums going up, and they both stare at their damaged cars, the looks of pity making one think it was their children or something. “That was my present to myself for surviving 25 years in my fucking brain killing job!” The Silver Mercedes the man refers to’s front has been crushed, buckled from the collision as it bounced off the side of a less glamorous looking Brown Station Wagon. “At least you HAVE a job. I need my car to get to interviews!” The heated and intense body language between the two men only intrigues the watching crowd more as they wonder which, if either will throw the first punch. “Wow, a car crash... Those, like, SO rule!” Now there is a new arrival to the crowd, amidst the large gathering of businessmen on their way to work and early morning shoppers, a preppy little woman makes her way to the front. Her pale Blue eyes are transfixed on the car wreckage before her as she directs some words at one of the bystanders. Eleanor : “Anyone, like, die?” The man standing tall above her shakes his head to her question before he carries on to his place of work, satisfied there is nothing more to see. Eleanor : “Cool, then I didn’t miss anything yet.” The 2 men continue to share heated words, quarrelling over who’s fault it was as the crowd of on-lookers is slowly eroded away but replaced by a larger queue of motorists to are all beeping their horns, adding to the tempers of the 2 drivers. “How is this MY fault? You T-Boned me!” Seeing the 2 men come close to shoving each other with their body language, Ellie~! looks on pursing her lips and waiting for them to start fighting. “What? You think I’d want to smash up my Merc?” The pair involved in the crash are now using aggressive gestures to exclemate their points... Their hot heads only heated more by the gathered crowd and car horns sounding all around them. “Look, man.. I’ll give you my insurance details and we can let them sort it out.” The face of the little prep sinks as she sees the 2 men begin a peaceful solution to the problem and the ring of humanity around the accident scene starts to diminish as all hope of some road rage violence seems to be gone. Eleanor : “No fight? That, like, sucks!” With everyone starting to go about their business again, Ellie~! takes it upon herself to intervene.... Oh dear. “Yeah, that’s fair. I suppose we were both off the ball.” As they go to grab their documents from the glove boxes, their eyes are both distracted by the petite woman in a White Peasant top dirty Blue denim and flared jeans that is approaching them. “Er... Hello...” The Merc driver greets her, not knowing who she is or why she is now standing with them. Eleanor : “Um.. Like, HELLO?!?! He SO smashed your totally stylish car with his rust bucket... I’d say it was, like, out of jealousy or something.” The seed of suspicion now well and truly planted in the fertile soil of the man’s doubts causes him to look at his own vehicle... A shiny machine of engineering perfection when he left off for work and now a crumples mess for the most part. “Yeah... He did cut in front of me....” The man’s doubting gaze is now cast upon the Brown Station Wagon which looks like it hasn’t been washed since the last rainfall as the other man approaches with his insurance documents. Eleanor : “You should fight him.” The Station Wagon owner drops a document and pauses while he retrieves it from the clutches of the light breeze.. The Mercedes’ driver not taking his eyes off him. “I can’t be seen fighting in public, my company would fire me on the spot.” Ellie~! frown briefly before in one quick burst she springs into life, bouncing excitedly on the spot. Eleanor : “But I’m, like, Commissioner of PCW. so I’ll SO make it a match... YAY ME!” The Merc man looks at the giggly little prep in thorough bemusement. Eleanor : “It means they, like, can’t arrest you... Say, how cool?!” He seems to like the sound of this and again locks his eyes on the other driver who is now approaching with his insurance papers and a look of contentment at a solved problem on his face. “Whooo, nearly had them blown away th-” *SMACK!* To a look of pure pleasure, delight and glee from Ellie~!, the Mercedes’ driver lands a punch right to the chin of the man who just 2 minutes ago crashed into him. The little prep bounces on the spot too cheerlead as the Station Wagon driver staggers back then charges back, sending both men to the floor. Eleanor : “GO ON! Like... HIT HIM! YEAH! AGAIN!!!” The crowds who had departed are now back. Transfixed by the 2 men in suits brawling on the street to the joy of Eleanor. Eleanor : “Making matches, like, SO rules!” By her feet the 2 men rolls around, grabbing onto one another’s suits to stop any punches being thrown, much to the little prep’s now annoyance as she kicks one of their arms away so the other can land a punch to their face. *SMACK!* Eleanor : “That’s better... Now hit him back!” *WHEEEW WHEEEW WHEEEW WHEEEW WHEEEW WHEEW!!!* Around the corner comes a Police squad car, obviously called the scene of the accident but is now confronted by 2 brawling men and a woman egging them on with her won preppy brand of encouragement. Eleanor : “Come on... You may be a balding middle aged loser but you can beat this guy!” The car pulls up and the 2 Cops emerge and rush over to the scene, Ellie~! walking towards them and gesturing to the fight. Eleanor : “It’s okay.. I, like, made it a PC-....HEY!... ME!” The 2 Policemen rush past her and pull the 2 men apart, pushing them against the wrecked Mercedes and handcuffing them while they try to squirm away to continue fighting. Eleanor : “My..... My match!” Watching as the 2 men are forced face down on the asphalt, Ellie~! stamps her feet and frowns, not looking too pleased. Eleanor : “And I usually like screw-job endings too!” She folds her arms and walks back to the crowds in a huff as our shot fades out on the little prep merging with the on-lookers once more.
