Date: December/March “Aki! Wake up!!” -What? - “Get up you lazy sack! Do you know what time it is?” -Should we care? - Akiyama’s eyes opened, looking up to see Sonata standing over him. He sat up, looking around the room at the white marble polished floors, the sun shining in through the white curtains. Sonata was dressed in a yellow halter top and a long black skirt with sandals, her midsection exposed to show her navel gem. “What is this madness?” he asked, yawning loudly like a lion with cooked brain from the blazing suns of the Serengeti. “Why do you rush us?” Sonata gave us an annoyed look, her hands already folded across her chest. “What are you talking about? Do you know what today is? You gotta get ready!” She reaches behind her back and pulls out a pack of cigarettes, placing one in her mouth and muttering to herself about the incompetence she deals with daily. Akiyama just wondered when she started smoking. “Sonata! Hold thy tongue and explain thyself! What is today? What’s going on? When did you begin to smoke?” “Christ, how hammered did you get last night at that party?” she blew out a puff of smoke and popped him in his forehead with her palm. “Today is the day of your big showdown with the Council of Darkness. San Diego lies in the balance if you don’t stop them, got it? I’ve been smoking since I was 12! But that’s none of your business, now is it?” Akiyama climbed out of bed, looking down at the black and silver kimono he wore. He looked up at Sonata with wide eyes, his hair braided in a silver ponytail behind him. “What sorcery is this, Sonata?” She shrugged, taking another puff of her cigarette. “Beats the hell outta me. I don’t complain about you wearing kimonos, your braided hair, or your black fingernails as long as you save the city. The PR work trying to push your manliness is more than enough work, but at least you get the job done. Now get up and get to training!” Aki lowered his head, walking out of the bedroom towards the door. He walked down the hallway, passing by a den where three women sat watching Saturday morning cartoons. He passed by the doorway, but few seconds later came back with a quizzical look on his face. “Pardon me ladies…” The lavender haired woman, the brown haired woman, and the blonde all turned around and smiled at once. “MORNING, BIG AKI YAMA!” “Morning, now who are you thre---.” His statement dropped once he realized what they called him. “What…did you call us?” The blonde smiled. “Big Aki Yama. Same thing we always all you. Same thing the world calls you.” In the house next door, a pale man with light purple hair kneels down in a dark room, candles surrounding him. An altar sits before him, where he bows his head and prays. “O great Lavos, I beckon thee, come forth and serve me well!” Wind begins to pick up inside, lightning bolts crackling as a small rip in time begins to open. The pointy eared man stands up, tossing his cloak back and pulling out a scythe. “YES!! Come on out, beast! The black wind howls… and Magus will get him some sweet, sweet PAYBACK!!” The rip opens further, Magus peering in and seeing a spiked beast roaring from within its dimension. Just then …a huge yell of pain echoes out from the house next door. Magus looks back, and just as he turns away a huge spike impales him before the portal closes shut!! Magus drops down to one knee, a glower on his face. “Damn. This ALWAYS happens!” He pulls a cell phone out of his cloak and hits a button, raising the piece to his ear. “Yo…it’s me again. get your ass over here with your machine so you can make a few…changes again.” Back in the stately estate of bAy… “This is a nightmare. Nightmare indeed.” Aki sat at the kitchen table, his head held in his hands as the blonde, brown haired woman, and the lavender haired woman patted him on his back comforting. “It’s okay, BAY.” The blonde cooed softly in an innocent voice. “We all have bad dreams sometimes.” Aki looked up at her, his eyes already tired of her. “Who are you anyway?” “I’m Molly. And this is Botan and Yuu.” Yuu smiled, the brown haired woman finger pointing to her dimpled cheek. “We’re your FATES!” The god sighed. “Great Celestials, can this get any worse?” He clasped his hand over his mouth, his eyes rolling as he smacked his head into the table. The Fates tried to comfort him once more, but Sonata came walking into the room glaring at them. “Get the hell off of him and go back to watching Kirby…or whatever the sh-t you watch.” The Fates scurried off, Aki raising his head to watch Sonata pour herself a cup of coffee. She turned back around and gave him a rebellious look before taking the steaming cup down at once. “Don’t that hurt?” he asked. She shook her head before pouring another cup. “Took a class down at the Y. Swallowing, by Ellie. Teaches you how to swallow hot liquids…though I think she was teaching on a different curriculum…” “Whatever…” Aki smacked his head back down into the table and sighed. Sonata shook her head, walking over to him and smacking him in the back of his head. “Don’t act like that! You gotta be in tip top confidence to face those Council of Darkness goons! You wanna go train some?” Aki looked up from the table, a small nod coming from him. “Yeah… might as well.” Sonata wrapped an arm around him, scolding him again. “That don’t sound like the Big Aki Yama I know!” Raising his fist into the air, he gave out a battle cry of “HI KEEBA----AWW HELL!! KILL US!” He plunked his head back down on the table, bringing another exasperated sigh from Sonata. “This is going to be a long day…” -=---=-Meanwhile…-=-=-=-- In front of the Bowl-A-Rama, the scene focuses on Contra arcade game in the corner of the arcade. Past that…lies darkness… “Villains and Villainesses… I thank you all for joining this council meeting.” A triangle table sits underneath a single bulb; men and women of various backgrounds sit glaring at each other. Standing at the end of the table was a man in all black, cybernetic implants on his face. “First and foremost, let us deal with new business before conducting out main purpose of assembling. First, we’d like to welcome our newest member, Clone-Borg, to the council.” Gruff welcomes come out at the metal clawed robot in the corner. It beeps a few times, its head twisting around. “For your first assignment…we want you to go and take care of our most nefarious enemy…BIG AKI YAMA.” The robot motors out of the room through a sliding door. As soon as it closes, the entire room bursts out into laughter. “Yeah, he wont be back.” one voice says. “Okay, we’d like to thank Jester personally for the free passes to the Comedy Cuisine last night. And like you said…the real joke there WAS the food.” The Jester sits back in his chair cackling madly, decked out in green bell cap and suit. “Gut-bustingly funny! OHOHOHOHOHO!!” His stomach lets out a bubbly urp, wiping the smile from his face as he runs off from the table. The man in black shakes his head before moving on. “Anyway…onto our more serious business… BIG AKI YAMA.” Low murmurs of displeasure come out from the people. The man calls for order and proceeds. “First, I’d like to call Professor Gutierrez to stand and give an analysis of the situation thus far.” An tanned older man with black and white bushy hair and a white lab coat stands nodding to his evil colleagues. “Thank you, gentlemen, and thank you, Mat.” The man in black jumps back up, pointing a laser pistol at the doc with the thick tropical dialect. “FOR THE LAST TIME…MY NAME…IS MATRIX!!! NOT MAT!! MAAATRIIIX!!” Just then a large monkey appears from the corner of the room, a smile pasted on his face as he starts doing the hustle. Matrix points the pistol at the monkey violently, his eye twitching. “NOW…IS NOT THE TIME, SON OF RALLY MONKEY!!” The Son of Rally Monkey lowers his head, backing up into the darkness of his corner again. A voice clears from the darkness, causing the entire room to go silent. Matrix sits back down, holstering his pistol and calming down slowly. Professor Gutierrez looks around before beginning again. “Er…okay, as I was saying, Big Aki Yama has been a thorn in our sides for a number of months since he moved to the San Diego bay area. Crime has dropped at least 45% in the first week alone!” A digital screen projects in the center of the table, showing a pie chart saying 45% crime down, with two other slices cut into the pie. “Our crime rate has dropped to a mere 15%!” A pale looking man with fangs raises his hand. The professor points to him. “Vampirezza?” “That’s only 60%. What happened to the other 40%?” “Winona Ryder shoplifting crime spree.” The council nods in understanding, the professor shaking his head. “Yes, you can’t stop that spunky little vixen. It’s been a slow few months for us. But we’re gonna change all that! Now, I’ll begin to go over the composition of bAy!” He pulls an organizer from his lab coat and begins to type out instructions. Next, a 3D skeleton of Akiyama appears, spinning around slowly for each to see. Matrix raises his hand before the professor can continue. “Where’d you get that snazzy organizer from?” The professor glares at him hard. “Made it. Cost a lot of money, took me years. Don’t worry about it.” Just then, a beep goes off from Matrix. He motions for the professor to hold on as he reaches into his pocket and pulls out the same organizer. He checks it, the professor giving him an evil look. “Where’d you get that from?” Matrix looks up, holding the silver organizer with a smile. “This? E-Bay. Paid only ten bucks too.” “You son of a bit—.” Another clearing of the throat silences the two of them. The professor continues on, pointing out the physique of the god. “You see that bAy is quite muscular, and powerful to bat. It’ll be a task to stop this man.” A hand rises from the corner. Leaning in, a man in a suit with slicked back hair takes the floor. “Yeah, Xenu, long time member, first time bAy conspirator…what exactly is Big Aki Yama’s superpower?” The council grows silent as the professor clears his throat embarrassedly. “Well…actually his strength is natural. And his strange ability to not feel anything is due to the extremely near dead pain receptors in his body.” “So what you’re telling me is that he’s just a strong retard? No powers whatsoever?” when the professor nods, he shakes his head in disappointment. “I didn’t invest all this cash to become Platinum Saint Debonair in the church of Scientology AND this council to find out that this guy’s not even a god, let alone a superhero!” “Well I’ll have you know we’re about to settle things here and now! We’re gonna attack him today..with everything we have!” Xenu stares hard at him. “If you haven’t been able to stop him thus far, what makes you think you’ll succeed this time?” The professor rolls his eyes. “BeCAUSE…being a part of evil villainy is the overbearing confidence in one’s success no matter the circumstances. It’s in the handbook we gave you, right between explain your evil plot in triplicate to the hero at his moment of defeat, and never employ henchmen that are more intelligent that yourself.” “Beesaiydes…e’ry one of dem taimes we fught b-A-y we’s was bai ourselves! We’s as a team should bes able to stup ‘im!!” Everyone looked to another side of the table, where a bearded man in a cowboy hat sat with cigar in hand and whiskey bottle in another. Xenu points across the table at him with a scowl. “Who the hell is that?” Matrix shrugged. “Texas Pete Williams.” “He supplies the booze and smokes for after the meeting.” Vampirezza whispers to Xenu under his breath. Then a glass shatters, Texas Pete holding up the remains of his bottle. “Ah’m tired of ya hosses always underaresstimatin’ me! My genieous is supeereeeorr!! Why, ah’m the rootinist, tootinist, rip-roarin’ sumbi--.” Another clearing of the mysterious being in the far side of the room comes out. Everyone settles down, holstering their weapons and sitting back down slowly. Texas Pete looks down, tears beginning to swell up in his red eyes. “Poor Jack…ah’m sorry ah had ta spill ya guts all overs the table…” Texas Pete hunches over the table, slurping up the last remains of his whiskey. The professor stares at him for a moment, giving Matrix a tired look. The half man/half computer motions for him to continue. “As I was saying…that’s why we have this appointment to duel with bAy today at 3:09 pm. Together, our superior minds shall DEFEAT…BIG. AKI. YAMA!! MWAHAHAHAHAHA!!” He remains the only one laughing in the room, drawing him to stop and look at them pleadingly. “Come…LAUGH WITH ME! MWAHAHAHA!!” The room bursts into laughter…all except one voice. The one mysterious voice that sits in the back of the room… …ice blue nails stroking along a feathery side… TO BE CONTINU--- Just then a door swings wide open, light flooding into the room. The whole load of them hiss at the light, a small boy standing in front of the Contra arcade machine with wide eyes. “Oh my gawsh! A super secret lair of deranged evildoers right in the center of my local bowling alley!” Matrix glares at the kid with a black face. “Close that door.” The kid lowers his head, jerking the door closed with all his strength, and after a few turns he snaps it shut again. Matrix sighs as he looks back to the professor. “We gotta change passwords. I mean come on now, who doesn’t know UP UP DOWN DOWN LEFT RIGHT LEFT RIGHT B A START?” Son of Rally Monkey hovers behind Matrix, doing the Macarena. Matrix turns around ready to swing, bringing the rally monkey to flinch. “BACK OFF!!” TO BE CONTINUED.
