A duel with Ginger Spice's Intercom....

Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin had arrived at London- Step 1 in the mission to reunite The Spice Girls. Now its time for Step 2- Track down The Spice Girls….. [Scene opens in a Taxi rank outside Heathrow Airport.Flaymin walks over to the nearest taxi and indicates for the driver to pop the boot. Flaymin then puts his bags into the boot and closes it, before hopping into the passenger seat in the taxi] Taxi Driver: Where ya off too, lad? [Flaymin reaches into his pocket and pulls out the supposed address of Ginger Spice a.k.a. Geri Halliwell.Flaymin hands the piece of paper with the address to the driver. The driver looks at the address and nods his head before handing the paper back to Flaymin] Driver: The swish part of town, eh? Flaymin: What?Oh no, I don’t think she’s Swedish. [The driver frowns in bewilderment.He then shrugs his shoulders and drives off towards the address of Geri Halliwell] Driver: So you here on business or pleasure? Flaymin: Strictly business.If all goes well, then I might indulge in a little pleasure, if you catch my drift. Driver: I hear ya loud and clear, sonny!So what is the nature of this business visit? Flaymin: Operation Girl Power.I can’t really say anymore than that. [The driver nods his head in interest] Driver: Sounds important.Are you some sort of secret agent, like FBI? Flaymin: Fijian-Born Indian? Driver: Uh, no I meant the other term for FBI…Federal Bureau of Anal Inspections or something like that. Flaymin: That sounds like fun, I wonder if they use gloves or do it the good ol’ fashioned way by using the Jaws of Life to separate your ass cheeks. Driver: Crikey, I wouldn’t know! [A couple of minutes pass before the driver finally pulls up to the kerb outside a mansion] Driver: ‘ere we are! [Flaymin looks at the majestic house in awe] Driver: Pretty thing, ain’t it? Flaymin: Wow…. look at the size of that satellite dish!I bet you can reach Kazakhstan with that thing. [Flaymin pays the driver, pulls his bags out of the boot and walks over to the main entrance of the grand mansion as the taxi drives away] Flaymin: Something isn’t right…. [Flaymin thinks it over momentarily before he suddenly clicks his fingers together. He then reaches into his bag and pulls out an ill-fitting, old and faded ‘Girl Power’ t-shirt and slips it on] Flaymin: Done! [Having completed his preparation, Flaymin walks over to the intercom and presses the button. A moment later, a posh sounding voice responds to Flaymin via the intercom] Voice: Hello and welcome to Geri-ville.If you are a member of the paparazzi- you better RUN.If you are selling food- Keep walking.If you would like Geri to autograph your album- Go f*ck yourself.If you would like to commend Geri on her Yoga videos- Err… you’d be the first. If you’re still standing here, a Sniper will be aiming a rifle at your head momentarily- thank you for bringing your own body bag. [Flaymin looks at his travel bags] Flaymin: Dammit, I knew I forgot something. [Flaymin presses the button on the intercom once again] Voice: YES?? Flaymin: Um, I forgot to bring a body bag.Can I use my suitcase? Voice: Can you fit in a suitcase? [Flaymin scratches his head in confusion] Flaymin: Why do I need the suitcase, again? Voice: *Groan*Stay right there, Sam the sniper will be with you in a moment to put you out of your misery. [Flaymin raises an eyebrow and then reaches over and presses the button on the intercom once again] Flaymin: Hi, I’d really just like to speak to Geri. Voice: This is Geri. Flaymin: Really? Voice: Would it make you feel better if I said that I was? Flaymin: Yeah sure. Voice: In that case, I’m Geri.What do you want? [Flaymin reaches into his pocket and fumbles around with a notepad. Finally, he finds the page he wants and begins to read from it] Flaymin: Geri, it’s a pleasure to meet you.Kiss her hand and don’t get horny….. Oh shit, did I say that out aloud?!?!?! Voice: *sigh*No harm done, keep talking. Flaymin: Well Geri, I’m a huge Spice Girls fan and I when I heard that you guys broke up I was like ‘OH MY GOD!’.And then I went through a period of resentment….Followed by a period of denial….Followed by a brief period of anger…Followed by…[Flaymin is abruptly cut off by the voice who seems to be yelling at someone] Voice: SAM, DID YOU FETCH THAT DAMN SHOTGUN YET??? Flaymin: Excuse me? Voice: I’m sorry, I was talking to my inept employees. Flaymin: Where was I… Voice: Going home. [Flaymin’s face lights up with glee] Flaymin: You mean I’m allowed into the mansion! Voice: No, I meant going back to your home. Flaymin: You want to come over to my place? [Flaymin begins to wink at the intercom and stroke it gently…. Until he realises its just a machine…. Before the voice can reply, loud shooting sounds begin to fill the air as bullets zip left, right and centre. Flaymin ducks for cover and uses his travel bags as a shield. Meanwhile, one stray bullet hits the lock on the Main Gate of the mansion, causing it to bust open and the doors to swing wide open. Not one to pass up such an opportunity, Flaymin quickly gets up and runs past the open gates and onto the front lawn of the mansion as the scene fades to black…….] In the next instalment….. Flaymin and Geri come face to face in an epic showdown!!(Well not quite, but it should be interesting)
