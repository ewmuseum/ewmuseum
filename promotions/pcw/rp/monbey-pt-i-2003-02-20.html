Monbey Pt. I

20 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20 Feb 03 The sweet smell of female sweat clouded the room which had crusty yellow wallpaper stuck to it inefficiently. There were to fearsome female warriors clashing with the other’s body parts viciously, swinging in all manners and techniques. Grotesque grunts pouring from their mouths like water from a tap. The Raven haired lady profusely sweating, as she attacks the smaller blonde haired girl, with her right hand and sweeps the hair out of her eyes with style with the other hand. The smaller girl blocks the attack with her right hand and kicks the shin of the larger, more muscled woman. The raven haired woman, Jade grunts and dives forward, she also lifts her right hand. The tiny girl goes to block the right hand but as the Raven haired lady takes away her right fist and swaps it with her left knee into the girl’s kidney, the girl, Carys, is left open for the vicious attack. Jade shakes her head. The training had lingered on for hours. They had worked their way through the bar, the kitchen, the stairs, the landing and into Carys’ bedroom, leaving a path of demolition in their tail. A ghastly sight as trash, decorations and such crept through the rather large Pennsylvanian building. That moist humid smell had filled the entire building as they beat the living hell out of each other. Jade’s hand had healed nicely in the past seven days as Carys had soon found out as each female thrashed the snot out of their friend. Adrenaline producing music thumping in the background as the jukebox in Jade’s sleazy strip joint thudded and shook the walls. Carys grinned as she managed to get inside Jade’s inner space. This was Carys advantage and she knew it, Jade was large and packed with those long, strong appendages. To get close to her was the way to her defeat. Carys, although slightly winded as she was, kicked Jade in the sternum and forced her against the wall. Jade ducked her head slightly just in time to evade a well balanced kick into the wall where her head existed briefly. Carys forced the tip of her foot against the wall. “Ouch, couldn’t you have left your foot there for me instead of me kicking the wall? I think I have a broken toe!” Jade smiled as Carys fell on her ass onto the dirty floor. She was rolling in agony as she holds her foot. Jade lifted her protégé off the ground with her right arm and proceeded in dusting her young friend off. They then take a bow of respect and Carys walks out of the room. Jade wipes her brow again and heads out of the now littered room and into her own. This bedroom was much more extravagant and luxurious. There was a large queen sized four poster bed which lies in the middle of the room, littered in finely woven black silk sheets and fluffy pillows. “Jesus, what a mess.” Jade walks through the disarray and towards a one way mirrored door and into the En Suite bathroom. Her clothes dropped behind her in a trail into the room. Feelings… Jade was pleased to have her young friend back, there were no doubts for her that was true. Something about the way Carys fought bothered her. She had been a lot more efficient in her capabilities than she had ever been before. That was just the start of her worries. Carys had been gone for near a week after that huge argument over little things. Carys came back without so much as an explanation. Without as much as a hug! It was screwed up; she seemed so impassive and blank. It was like Carys had something on her mind. Jade tried to ignore it for the time being. It seemed the best option. It however was not ALWAYS the best idea to ignore matters which seemed to constantly press at your thoughts. When she began to get the final warnings through her mail box about her ever increasing debts produced by her disproportionate disbursement of cash placed on the Club she was building. Here she was once again having a shower, like she could wash away her feelings. She wished she could, and then she wouldn’t have to put her colossal plan into action. A plan to she had to make up for her recent unemployment status which she was going to be eradicating. The Plan which will commence tonight at 1AM. Although she regretted having to steal again, she did not regret having to feel the adrenaline pumping through her veins. -[Fade]- Jade had to put up with the torment of Carys lying to her earlier in the day which had wound her tight like a spring ready to pounce on anyone who dared step in her way. She met Carys’ ‘Boyfriend.’ There was no way he was her boyfriend, the uncomfortable ambience those two created when Carys touched his hand was easily seen. Jade once again ignored them, something which, like the bills, was ever harder to do the more they banged on in her head. That wasn’t really the thing beating her up inside right now. It was more the fact that, to Jade, her plans seemed to fall into line too easily. She had a fair idea that only a part of the security features were wrote down and so she was now once again going to go over her plan, alone. Jade walks into the empty and musky storeroom which was at the back of the strip club, next to the Manager’s office. She pulled up a chair to the large oak desk, its colours hidden apart from the little bit shown where the spotlight reflects on the polished surface of the desk. There was no light pouring in from cracks under the doors, windows or even the air conditioners. Jade pulled out a large rolled up blue print. On the paper were several white drawings of a multi storey building. The blue print was covered in multi coloured post it notes. She pulls up a chair and takes a seat. “Now, where was I?” Jade ran her long bony fingers through her pitch black and white coloured hair. She began ticking things on the post it notes which had been marked several times as the plan had been gone over. Jade got to a certain part in the plan and her eye brows furrowed. This had been the part which got her confused, something faintly rang a bell about the whole situation and it freaked Jade out a little. “I have gone over this a hundred times and still nothing makes sense. Well, just fuck it!” Jade snapped and threw the whole blue print against the wall. Jade reached under the table and pulled out a bottle of vodka. She began drinking as she slumped backwards into her chair. Jade’s eyes opened after a second and she threw the whole bottle of vodka into the waste basket. “I got it! I should always drink on the job!” “I think the phrase is I should never drink on the job!” The voice spoke softly from behind her. In Jade’s realisation of what was going down she had not heard her friend and chauffer, Nathaniel; walk in through the sound proofed door. His blue eyes almost lit up the room as the light reflected back at her. He stepped towards the table and grinned as Jade bent over to pick up the papers from the floor. Jade spread them out on the table and they both glanced over the plan. She runs a black painted nail across the drawing and points to a certain position. “Are we closer to completion on the plan? I certainly hope so seeing as the whole thing goes down tonight.” He questioned Jade; he was obviously a part of the whole thing. “Mani is involved in this whole thing.” She referred to her cousin. He had many times tried to get her in trouble with the police. The person she bought the security plans from is obviously doubled up as one of his cronies. “This whole thing does not have to go down the drain though. I’m going to take him out of my plans once and for all. All we have to do is get the police involved.” Nathaniel looked up miffed as that would mean them not being able to get their hands on the item. “OK, I know what is missing, it occurred to me when the alcohol slithered down my throat, it really clears my head some times you know?” She fired the excuse at him; he looked at her in incredulity. “Don’t look at me like that! There are bars which drop from these points here, and here.” She went on, informing him of the entire change. They were in place for a huge win fall, and to eliminate a insignificant little runt. It was a good night for Jade indeed. -[Fade]- Jade pulled her hair into a tight knot behind her head and pulled a black balaclava over her head. That added to her black body hugging cat suit and she was ready to go. Her utility belt, if you like, completed the outfit. Nathaniel walks into the room, the lights from the bar glistening in his blonde gel-slicked hair. Jade turns around and warns Nathaniel. “Now, sweetie I have a warning and if you do not heed it then there will be great trouble in result. You will stay back; you will NOT follow me in there. I am the professional here; I don’t want to put you at risk. Do you understand?” Nathaniel rolls his eyes. “Don’t play with me man! Is it a yes or no?” Nathaniel nods his head in understanding. “Good, warm the car up!” Jade was enthused now. Jade headed out of the nightclub, picking a holdall up on the way out. A loud exhalation of air leaves her mouth. Carys runs to dive into the convertible Chevette waiting for her. The car screeches from it position and sets on its way down the street. ‘Wake up late’ by Korn blasts through the huge speakers lining the back streets. Jade hits the mute button and shouts at Nathaniel for being such a jerk and leaving the music on. It made them look unprofessional and incompetent. Eventually they got there. It appeared to be some sort of Museum, definitely pre 1900’s architecture which is the kind of thing you see two security guards on the barred doors. There really SHOULD have been guards there. There should be guards everywhere. Especially with the mother load which was going to be transported here shortly. Jade needed to get ready and she reached into the holdall behind the back passenger seat. There is a rope and a grappling hook and she fastens them securely, pulling harder when the rope is between her teeth. “Could you Kurn the engine off!” Jade muttered as she tried to speak with the rope is there. Jade pulled it out of her mouth and climbed out of the car. Jade walked up to the steps and still saw no guard in sight. She huffed and swung the grappling hook around her head and lets go of it. The rope flies through the air catching around the head of the gargoyle; it wraps around with fierce intensity and gives it a quick yank. The gargoyle’s head breaks off and falls to the floor. “Shit!” Jade hisses under her breath. She knew something was wrong but somehow couldn’t seem to make herself care that there were still no guards around her. The second time she tried she had a significant amount more luck as she managed to grip it to the ledge of the building. She slides the rope through the hook which is fastened to her belt and she steadies herself. Jade was about half way up the building when she looked into the window where the ‘prize’ was situated. There were four guards stood at arms length to the jewel. “Hmm.” Jade smiled, she seemed to feel a lot more comfortable knowing that she was going to have to go through four guards to get it. Even though there was not a single sign of a proper security system. Jade scrambled up the building without the same hesitation she expressed before, almost double pace. Jade finally got her hands on the top of the building and yanked herself onto the ledge. Something caused her to lie on her side; she needed to be avoided being seen. Her body paused, not even letting out a breath. “Son of a whore. This can’t be happening. After all this planning it is going to fall through for me?” Jade saw a man standing over the ventilation ducts. He was securing a wire which was, unbeknownst to Jade, which was already carrying three other men. Jade looked around and pulled a soda can from her utility belt. “May not be top of the range but it does work.” Jade whispered under her breath. She threw it across to the other side of the man as she ducked behind a glass skylight and the man searched around clueless. After being satisfied that this wasn’t a professional operation she began to crawl across the floor towards a different, smaller ventilation pipe. Once upon the pipe she dives onto the still wary man, her arm wraps around his throat. “Want to still be breathing in thirty seconds?” Jade’s right hand slips over his mouth and muffles his cries of shock. She allows him a little room for him to nod his head. Jade unclips her rope and ties it around his waist with her one arm. When she doe that she tapes his mouth up and kicks him in the ass. He stumbles over the ledge and he remains tied up on the edge of the building. Jade rubs her hands together in the cold and picks up his two way radio which keeps the people in touch. “So there’s more than one of you here then.” The angst in her voice rang true. She was nervous as the last real theft she tried was screwed up, her barely even capable of escaping from the police. It had been the best part of four months ago that little fiasco and she really did not want a repeat event of last time. She felt so exhilarated. Jade decided that since she had used her rope to tie and sling her new little friend over the top of the building that she would have to use the rope that at least one other guy had to have used. “Oh great, I have a direct route to me getting captured.” Jade rolled her eyes and shrugged her shoulders as she grabbed the tight black wire to go down into the building. “I can’t let my treasure escape me without putting up a fight on the way.” Little did she know that is exactly what she would have. -To be continued.
