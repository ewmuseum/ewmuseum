One Blink

9 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/9/03 One Blink That’s all it takes… Did you know the average human being has their eyes closed through thirty five percent of the day, including sleeping and blinking. That’s thirty five percent of their lives going right down the drain. The world is waiting for you to wake up to begin, but yet it always keeps moving. Some people just need sleep. You can’t go without sleep; you’ll become tired, weak, disoriented. It’s a shame you know. Missing out on that thirty five percent might just entirely change your life. You think, well since everyone else is asleep or blinking then why can’t I. Well have you ever thought to yourself why you sleep and blink. It’s pretty simple if you think about it. Some people just don’t want to be seen. Our eyes are controlled one hundred percent by our body. We can force our other body parts to do things, but forcing those eyes is one of the hardest things to do. We were made to not see things at certain times because God doesn’t want us to see some things. Well you see I, I don’t blink. Hell I don’t even sleep. But yet I never feel the need to. I could if I wanted to, but why miss out on the world around me. I know more now about the world than people fifty years older than me may know. I look at this gift and wonder, “What does it mean?” Life so many other things that make me different this is the one I always find myself wondering about. In the Bible I remember a saying in Matthew 20:33 reading: 'Lord,' they said to Him, 'open our eyes!' If you look at it in a more literal sense you think, oh they must be blind. And that is exactly what it states in the Bible. That wasn’t what Jesus was referring to though. Blind was used in the sense of they were able to see, but haven’t been able to see what he wants them to see. If you were to read the Bible you would notice a lot of blind reference. Blind is referred to fifty five times in the Bible. One out of every five pages in the Bible most likely has the word their. God wants to heal us of our blindness, and so he had. That’s when he realized some things shouldn’t be seen. Hate.Crime.Injustice. These things sometimes ruin people and pollute the world. So to rid them, we close our eyes to all of this that is happening. Would it help if we never witnessed all the bad things in our world? Maybe. The stuff everyone else doesn’t see, I witness every second of my life. The hate, discrimination, and separation are all around me. My eyes aren’t yearning to close because they’ve seen enough, but yet they starve for more to expand my mind. I’m not a scholar.I’m not a genius.But I know how the world works. You give me, I give back, but don’t anything with what I gave you before I gave back, because it might make me want to take it back without you giving it back. Confused? You should be. I’m not here, alone, in a dark room talking to the world of wrestling because I want to make you better people, but yet I want you to experience the world in the way I look at it. You see if you could spend one day seeing what I had seen, then maybe you could understand the man under the hidden body. I’m not differentI’m not a freakIf you look hard enough you’ll find the real me. That’s right, all it takes is for you to keep looking. If you glitch, blink, sleep, whatever, you name it, you’ll miss something so important. The worst part of it all is you’ll never even realize you’ve missed something. Life is knowledgeKnowledge is keyUnderstanding is everything Life is for fun I guess some of you might say. Fun puts a smile on your face; knowledge opens you to a whole new world. Maybe some aren’t up for it, but I’m sure a lot are. Men like Tim Walden, Xavier Kannon, Duke, they don’t know this other world. THEY DON’T WANT to know this other world. So keep your eyes openKeep your mind clearThink before blinkingAnd don’t just listen with your ear. *El Romantico leaves the empty black room as the red light on the video camera disappears. *
