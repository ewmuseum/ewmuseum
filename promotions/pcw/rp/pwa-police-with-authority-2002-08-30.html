PWA - Police With Authority

30 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30 - 08 - 02 It's downtown New-York at the dead of night with the moon being the only source of lighting as the council's street furnishings have been vandalised countless times before and have yet to be replaced. This particular part of town is a shadow of the city, which can offer entertainment and intrigue compared to the squalor bestowed on the natives who have no choice but to fight for survival with gang wars being a common tourist attraction. Walking down the road Reservoir Dogs style were the devious duo recently assigned to the Primetime family, Pranks With Attitude. Nothing appeared out of the ordinary with the mountainous Nerezza in full vampiric glory with his two piercing canines in full view and his fashionable attire of a crimson red sleeveless trench coat present and correct. The cocky strut belonging to Gaz 'The Jester' Green was in full motion as he showcased his emerald green business tuxedo, hair held firmly high and his eyes sheltered beneath dense sunglasses they waltz down the street together. 'So how do you plan we make an impact?' grinned the conniving comic with a sinister smile. 'First, this isn't just Pranks With Attitude no more. We are going to make an impact courtesy of the Asylum!' groaned the towering behemoth Nerezza. 'So tonight we prank a name for ourselves....' continued the Living Nightmare. 'And pranking is my speciality, squire!!!' roared the Jester with an untimely interruption. Revealing the Jester's infamous bag of tricks, the content baring misery on the occupants of New York he lays it down and presses aside the fasteners. With a spring loaded effect the case opened to unveil cobias amounts of tinned spray bonded together by elastic and various other devices of the Jester's arsenal. 'So what should we focus on tonight then Jester?' questioned the Angel Of Death. 'Well there's a children’s play area around here....' retorted the velvet trickster coming to the end of the street and peering over to a simplistic park, furnished only by a set of swings, a slide and seating arrangements. Entering the children’s play area Nerezza soaked the slide in a transparent glue, which would appear invisible under the more familiar sun commonly associated with New-York whilst his comrade Gaz Green loosened the bracket of the swing creating a potential hazard. The whole charade looked like something from Beadles About as pieces of framework were dismantled and what once was a place of joy had now became a death trap courtesy of Pranks With Attitude. The mischievous Jester left the confines of the park, still being worked upon by the evil Nerezza who crippled the foundations of the benches used by parents to watch over their siblings as they run riot. Gaz however has armed himself with two spray canisters and decided to create his own version of a zebra crossing; lines of white and black perched to the existing road waved unevenly. 'We've single-handily demolished a part of New York!!!' bellowed the jovial Jester with glee. 'There'll be hell to pay if this ever comes to light~!' injected the Gothic superstar, grinding his teeth in the process. 'Yeah - But who's going to find out it was us?' retorted the Lord Of Laughter as the wails of a police vehicle cloaked the area leaving the duo to call in an early night. Nerezza taking titanic strides forward, leaving Gaz to languish behind hurtled down a pitch-black alley that lay hidden from sight. The alley itself had enough space to drive a car along and was primarily used for the front end occupants to place their rubbish or a possible escape route from the law but our dynamic duo were now going to shelter themselves there until the danger had past. With the sirens drawing nearer and the quivering Jester looking a shell of his former self, Nerezza hatches a plan that would ensure the both would avoid further trouble with the police. 'Listen Jester.... I want you to put on that straight-jacket you brought and act normal' whispered Nerezza. 'Act normal? Charming, I'm sure' replied Gaz Green with a little look of confusion. 'No Problem - We haven't much time so listen. If the police catch up with us you play the mental patient whilst I pretend to be your brother who is trying to look after you since you've been released from your asylum!' continued Nerezza. Gaz started to place on the straightjacket with incredible effort, requiring the service of his partner to buckle up at the back. Suddenly the alley became a lighting spectacle as a cars headlight shone intensely through the dense darkness and the roaring engine could be heard over the thick silence that blanketed that part of New York. The car slowly drew nearer prompting Nerezza to leave his space of confinement and confront the officer over the behaviour of his supposed brother Gaz who has decided to bounce about whilst screaming the word asylum. 'ASYLUM! ASYLUM! ASYLUM....' yelled the King Of Komedy as he threw his body mass about like a crash dummy. 'Officer come quickly it's my brother?' cried the titanic man-mountain like a distraught victim. The car eventually came to a halt and the driver’s side door swung open with nothing but a silhouette behind the windshield as the intense light blinded Nerezza's squinting eyes. Then without warning a foot exited the car, followed by the body of a stocky individual clad in a navy blue police officers uniform. 'What’s the trouble here?' said the officer as he moved into the light. 'ASYLUM! ASYLUM! ASYLUM! ASYLUM!' continued the mental Jester, Gaz Green. 'What's this fellow ranting about?' questioned the officer, looking in disbelief at the troubled Gaz Green. 'He's not use to the outside world but I'm settling him, the doctor said it's a question of time!' proclaimed Nerezza, still portraying the false brother to his tag partner. 'Well ok then.... I was on patrol and had a call about a couple of vandals matching your descriptions but I guess it can't be you since your brother is a little incapable should we say!' joked the officer obviously suckered in by the whole episode of 'Of Mice And Men'. 'No worries officer, easy mistake. Well good luck in bringing the felon to justice!!!' blurted Nerezza as he tapped the officer on his back, secretly placing a sign on his back with the message 'I'm a bent Copper' 'Well gentlemen I bid your good-night' yawned the officer before reversing his automobile back the way he came in. 'I AM THE AMAZING STEVEN HAWKINGS~!' screamed the lunatic Gaz Green, still we into his character. As the car slowly reversed out, the team of Pranks With Attitude crept away in the darkness, trying their best to look inconspicuous when suddenly they hear the thunderous bellow of the officer who confronted them minutes earlier. 'WHAT THE F....' 'Hmm, maybe we should get out of here!' said the trembling Jester as the team rode hotfooted from the alley with the car possibly coming back from the other end in a Benny Hill style finale.
