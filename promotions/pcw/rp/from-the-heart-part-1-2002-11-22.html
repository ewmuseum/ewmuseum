From The Heart (part 1)

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22.11.02 The scene opens up in a small, dark studio. Duzza has been filming MTV Diary “A Day In The Life of Duzza” and if you have ever seen that tv show, you know that there are always small breaks from the footage of the day, where the artist speaks about what has been happening. Well, it is in this dingy little dark room with one camera pointing at them, with one light for lighting and a chair to sit on, where the artists spill out their guts on national television. Duzza sits in the room staring at the camera. Earlier in the day he was phoned by his closest homie Statuz Quo to say that something had gone down and he had come home to find all his possessions in boxes and his girl telling him that they had to move. Duzza was given no reason as to why his homie had to move and had know idea when he would be back, all he knew was that Statuz Quo had told him to take control of Playaz Inc. And Duzza wasn’t about to let down his homie. But now he sits in the dark room, wearing a big black hoody, with the hood up. He stares into the camera. His face is unshaven and Duzza looks tired. This time last week Duzza was on top of the world. He knew that Playaz Inc was running from PCW, he knew that nothing could do wrong. But now it has gone wrong. Statuz Quo, the leader has personal issues to address, Ali Khadaffi hasn’t been seen for days and Dante Jaackson hasn’t returned Duzza’s phone calls. Duzza stares into the camera, his face shows signs of rejection and he doesn’t really know his next move. Eventually, he starts to speak… Duzza: “I don’t know why everybody’s talking about us. As in Playaz Inc. See they want us to fail, they book us in a match where all four members of Playaz Inc are fighting each other? The Friday before a pay per view where we was the bookies favourite to win? So they put us in a match with each other. I can see these fools on their knee’s just praying for Playaz Inc to break up… or for Playaz Inc to falter. Well… I guess some of y’all got your wish… because it seems, we’ve fallen… but we will get back up… believe that. See, I don’t know why this world got so much hate dawg, if there was this much love, then it’d all be great. There would be no killings, we wouldn’t be getting ready to go to war, and we wouldn’t have a gay ass president who is getting egged on by his dad to take his people to a war, where they will get killed… But, all this hate aimed at me and my people, it’s just making me stronger y’all…making me so strong… See, there are a couple of key issues that I gotta address and hella bullshit that I’ve just gotta get of my chest. First of, Playaz Inc are still going strong, ignore the rumours and the matches against each other, Playaz Inc are still down with each other. Playaz Inc for life. Now every mother fucker and their mother got key issues they wanna contest and express yeah, but ya see, I listen to a few of them and just ignore the rest. See, some confess we’re hot, while the rest say we don’t deserve what we got? Excuse you? Don’t deserve what I’ve got? I’ve worked and hustled every single day of my life since my father passed away when I was a kid to get what I got, and now you’re saying I don’t deserve what I’ve got? Fuck you. I’ve probably worked harder than any other mother fucking, just to get this far. And I ain’t finished yet. See, I’ve done a lot of things and seen a lot of things. I’ve been betrayed by more people than George W. Bush has executed. From Rush Mason to Jordan X to Thug RoC, every one has turned on me. Until I met Statuz Quo. See, when I first met Statuz, he reminded me of myself. He has a passion and determination like no other, and he was getting hated on by everyone, and it sickened me. So I decided to throw my hand out to the brother and we just connected and now, he’s the only mother fucker I know has my back. Dante Jackson and Ali Khadaffi, they be my friends, but Statuz? That’s ma dawg for life. See, all it took for me to get it here was hard work. Well, that and a unhealthy obsession wit’ fame. But you ask around, ask around, who’s nice? And they’ll mention my name. I’m as nice as they come. Believe that. But y’all can’t talk about me. Y’all don’t know what I’ve been through. But look at me now. I just wanted to be famous and get ma family out of the hood, now look what I’ve got myself into. But is it me, or are my own people waiting for me to fail? Is it me, or don’t my own people even believe me? First y’all complain, saying there ain’t no Harlem kid in wrestling, well now there is one, and y’all say I sold out? Sold Out? Fuck you. I’m the realest mother fucker alive. But what do I have to do to prove to y’all I’m not a gimmick? Because I ain’t. I’m real. This is the real me. The Duzza you see, throwing gang sides, with braided hair, listenin’ to R. Kelly and rolling in a black jeep? That’s the real me. I ain’t no carbon copy or nothin’, I created this whole gangsta genre man, before me, ain’t no gangsta’s up in the wrestling biz, then I blew up and now look? Every other mother fucker is claiming the hood… See, y’all don’t hate the fact I’m wrestling, y’all hate the position I’m in… you wish you could be in my position. I am a mother fucking pioneer, and I deserve my own chapted in the freaking wrestling history books. Believe that. oh and by the way in case you didnt notice, all the hating you doing is keeping me focused besides hating is bad for your health…” End Of Part 1
