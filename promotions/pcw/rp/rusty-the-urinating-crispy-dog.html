Date: not entered [Scene opens at the local fire station. It’s a slow day at the office and so the Firemen are busy sitting around playing poker when Flaymin Aymin knocks on the door. One of the firemen, a large man named Earl, walks over to answer the door] Earl: Can I help you? Flaymin: Hi, I’m Flaymin Aymin! [Suddenly all the men sitting around the table playing poker, drop their cards and look up at Flaymin] Earl: THE Flaymin Aymin? Flaymin: The one and only! Earl: The same Flaymin Aymin who set my dog on fire last week? Flaymin: Oh…. that was your dog? Earl: Poor ‘Rusty’ is still in intensive care. Flaymin: Yeah well, he shouldn’t have pissed on my leg. Earl: YOU pissed on him first!! Flaymin: Look, we can sit here and try to work out who did what to who and when they did it and where it happened and why and blah, blah, blah… or we can just forget the incident with the ‘Hot Dog’ and move on with our lives. [Earl eyeballs Flaymin and then speaks with a mean tone in his voice] Earl: Well, what do you want? Flaymin: I’ll be involved in a Flaming Tables Match soon and I was wondering if you guys had any tips on how I can avoid getting burnt? Earl: Here’s a thought, asshole- how about you *gasp* stay… away… from…. the…. fire… Flaymin: No, but that’s the purpose of the match! Earl: What kind of match is this? Don’t tell me that you’re pissing on people now! Flaymin: No, no, I haven’t done that in months.This is just a match put together by my mortal enemy after I kinda f*cked his wife and crushed his brother’s grave with my tractor…. [The firemen stay eerily silent.They all look at each other trying to comprehend what kind of drugs Flaymin must be on. Earl puts a hand on Flaymin’s shoulder and speaks in a calming voice] Earl: Look sonny, you probably have a promising life ahead of you- but if you keep snorting that stuff you’ll totally lose it. Flaymin: I ain’t snorting anything!I’m serious. Earl: Uh huh…And your ‘mortal’ enemy is after you? [Flaymin nods his head] Flaymin: That’s right. Earl: I see.And all this started because you nailed his woman? Flaymin: Yeah, I mean what’s wrong with that?My philosophy is, if you can’t please a woman then you better step aside and let someone else have a crack at it. Earl: Listen here boy, you’ve come to the wrong station. Just down the road is the POLICE Station- they’ll take care of you…. and make sure that you don’t piss on any more dogs…. [Flaymin thrusts his hands in the air out of frustration] Flaymin: I knew it!!You and your f*cking dog, you just can’t leave it alone!! Earl: Hey, you should see the condition of poor Rusty, with all these burns everywhere!! Flaymin: Rusty?I think ‘Crusty’… or ‘Crispy’ would be a more suitable name.You can carry him around and- SLAP!! [Earl raises his hand and swiftly slaps it across Flaymin’s face. Flaymin stumbles back in shock] Flaymin: What’s up with that!?!I come here asking for help and….. I get bitch slapped!?!? Shame on you. [Flaymin looks around the room at all the other firemen] Flaymin: SHAME ON ALL OF YOU!Denying a citizen when he comes asking for help is the ultimate crime! Yes, I pissed on the dog, but that little son of a bitch won’t cross Flaymin Aymin for a long time as a result! Earl: BOY YOU BETTER GET OUTTA MY STATION BEFORE I FETCH MY SHOTGUN!! [Flaymin shakes his head in disgust and walks out of the fire station. A minute or so passes before Flaymin returns to the fire station.He walks back into the room and taps Earl on the shoulder] Flaymin: Hey Earl, I think your Firetruck is on fire!! Earl: You no good, stinking, son of a… Flaymin: Ha! [Scene fades to black as Flaymin strolls out of the fire station and the firemen rush out to put the flames out of their firetruck]
