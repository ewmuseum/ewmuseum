At the office. Making plans for Spring Fling.

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/01/2002 [OOC note: this is not a good rp, by any means, as far as I’m concerned. I’ve been entirely busy in real life, and in running the XWA. So, please excuse this crap. I will pull out Boda-esq rps this week. I promise.] *The scene opens outside your normal, everyday office building. Tall, multiple floors, lots of windows. And, this being a sunny Orlando day, the reflection off the windows from the sun is not only bright, but also blinding. We are taken inside by a rooming camera. The plush, red walls that lead us to the main lobby, are about as easy on the eyes as watching Chris Chase wrestle. Once in the lobby, the camera pans in on the lobby directory, a stone tablet, mounted on the far wall between the elevators. A reflection is seen out of the corner, coming off the silver elevator doors. The camera turns, and sees it is only a $5.00 an hour rent-a-cop. Nothing to worry about. Well, except that the rent-a-cop is more intimidating than Chris Chase. Hell, Miya’s half-dead wife is more intimidating than the ‘Messiah’. Any ways, back onto the descriptive part of this promo. Ignoring the well-dressed, under paid protector of these hollowed halls, we watch as the left elevator opens, revealing the everyday, run of the mill, elevator. Once inside, the doors slowly shut. Plain as day, the writing on the wall reveals.....* BODA=PCW CHAMPION FOREVER *Guess there is a liberal graffiti right in this office building. Of course, it would, being that this building houses the very business that Chris Sloboda, that’s right, the PCW World Champion, not the ‘Messiah’ Chris Chase, runs.* DING *The elevator doors open, revealing a long, white hallway. At the end, a single door. What could be in that room? Sex Toys? Corey Williams Blow Up doll? Child Pornography? No, we already stated that this is not a Chris Chase ran office. Taking the long walk towards the single door, takes less time than an actual match against Chase himself. The door itself, black, with red letters going diagonal down it.* X.W.A *XWA? Xtreme Wrestling Alliance? Why would we be brought to this place? What is it that makes us wonder what is on the other side of this door? Glancing up, we see the exact reason on the glass of the door.* Chris Sloboda , OwnerBetter than Chris Chase in every way *Ah, this is the office of not only Chris Sloboda, the professional businessman, but the new head office for XWA. That’s right, Chris Sloboda, the PCW World Champion owns, runs and controls the Xtreme Wrestling Alliance. Boda, the man that next Sunday, in front of thousands and thousands of Chris Chase fans, will make them all go home crying. Balling their eyes out because the man they believe in, the one that put their asses in the seats, will go home EMPTY HANDED. Boda, now is a busy man. Not only running XWA, but being World Champion, a family man, a man hated by millions. Trying to keep that last statement in effect [hated by millions] takes alot of work. But, what kind of work does Boda do in his office?* Boda: “Look at this, a pile of applicants, all wanting to get into my fed. Hell, it’ll take me a week to read all of them. But it’ll take me less than 5 minutes to find someone with more talent, more athletic ability than Chris Chase. Who knows, maybe the guy I hire today, will be beating Chris Chase tomorrow. In this business, one never knows.” *Boda is sitting at his mahogany desk. His feet kicked back, he is relaxed, reading the stacks and stacks of new applicants. Across from him, XWA vice-president, OSW star, Jesse Ramey. Jesse too is reading apps.* Jesse Ramey: “I don’t know Chris. There are literally thousands to look at. And you have that upcoming PPV match to worry about. Tell ya what......... I’ll go over these. You worry only about Chris Chase and Miyagawa.” *Boda drops an app on the desk, and looks at Jesse, almost quizzical* Boda: “Me? Worry about Chase? Give me a damn break. Wrestling Chris Chase is like pitting the L.A God Damn Lakers against the Seminole Spring Elementary school in a game of basketball. And as for Miya, I’ve got him by his balls. He can’t touch me, he can’t touch my family either, or little Natalie is a goner.” Jesse: “Don’t forget, he’s in the main event against you now on Sunday.” *Boda gives a look of ‘oh fuck’.* Boda: “Dammit, I did forget. Of course, Jesse, it’s not like it matters any. I mean, I’ve beaten Miya, on many occasions. I’ve beaten Chase too. I’m at the top of MY GAME, and no one, especially not some little miscreant like Chase, is going to knock me off my pedestal. Now, excuse me Jesse.... I’ve got a phone call to make.” *Jesse stands from his chair, and grabs the stacks of applications.* Jesse: “Okay. I’ll be in the other office if ya need me.” Boda: “Thanks.” *We watch as Jesse Ramey leaves Boda’s XWA office, and walks to another room. Boda gets up, and closes the door. Boda walks back to his desk, grabbing the phone as he sits down* Boda: “No, what was the phone number again? Oh right..........” *Boda dials a phone number and waits for the other end to pick up.* Voice: “Hello, thank you for calling Anaheim Memorial Hospital. How may I direct your call?” Boda: “I need to talk to the ICU please.” Voice: “One moment sir.” *Some cheesy hospital type music comes on, as Boda is put on hold. Boda is seen bopping his head back and forth listening to the music.* Voice: “I.C.U. May I help you?” Boda: “Oh, yeah.... thank you. My name is Cal Seaver. I work for the Primetime Championship Wrestling organization. You haven a patient up there by the name of Natalie Miyagawa. I am calling to get an update on her condition please.” Voice: “Let me see Mr. Seaver.” *There is some more of that boring, hospital music playing in the background. Then the voice comes on again.* Voice: “Mr. Seaver. Did you forget you just called yesterday?” Boda: “Oh.... yes actually. I mean, I did call, but I lost my report for the website and didn’t think it’d be a problem recalling.” Voice: “Fine. Ok. The condition on Mrs. Miyagawa is she is out of her coma. But without the much needed blood donation, she will not likely live past this coming Monday.” Boda: “And as for the baby?” Voice: “Same thing. Mrs. Miyagawa suffered blunt trauma to the head and back. Much blood was lost internally and with out the needed blood donation, the baby will not live.” Boda: “Well, thank you for your time. And, I guess the morgue will be busy next Tuesday morning.” *Boda hangs up the phone before the voice on the other side can say anything. Boda laughs, until his phone rings again. Boda is hesitant to pick it up, as it may be the hospital calling back. But Boda does indeed grab the handset and answers the phone.* Boda: “Hello?” Voice: “Have you seen the bookings this week? Especially for Fusion?” Boda: “Xavier? That you?” Voice: “Yep. What’s up champion?” Boda: “Nothing. And no I haven’t seen the bookings.” Xavier Kannon: “Well, the main event is you, me, Frisco and The Chef taking on Jeremy Howard, Miya, Big Alan Steele and Legend Inc. It’s a 8 man tag match elimination style.” Boda: “Damn, and Chase gets to stay out of it. Lucky bastard. Three days before his biggest wrestling opportunity, and he’s not even booked.” Xavier: “Wait.... it gets better..... Chris Chase is the referee.” *Boda starts to laugh* Boda: “That’s cherry. Good. Miya in the ring, and Chase the referee. Time to make some noise before the PPV. All right Xavier. Thanks. See you on Friday.” *Boda hangs up the phone, before Kannon could finish. Boda pulls out his World Title from the top desk drawer, and stares at it.* Boda: “So.... one week away from me defending you. One week till I get to annihilate Chris Chase once and for all. 7 days from the time Miya thinks he will get my blood for his beloved wife. 7 days. In 7 days, my friend.... you will still be mine. And I’m going to make sure of it.” *Boda grabs his cordless phone, and dials a number. He walks towards the camera, and shoves the cameraman and camera out of the front door, and slams it shut. As the scene fades out, all we can here is part of the conversation.* Boda: “Yeah, listen.... about this coming Sunday.... it’s still on. Be there.”
