The second half of the date

13 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-13-02 RP 2 Fusion 15th Looking over the menu, Teresa couldn’t read much of it. Even reading the fine English print under the French words tells her little of what exactly the entrees are. Being the laid back person she is, she literally closes her eyes and points to the menu. She ends up getting a plate fit for the rich snobs that thinks eating is a sin. Teresa pokes at the small piece of meat with her fork and glances to the waitress. This is it? The waitress stuck her nose in the air and walked away. In return, Teresa flips the French girl the bird. She glances across the table to Nerezza’s face once again hidden behind his menu. She smiles and makes an attempt to at least try whatever she ordered. Nerezza finally places his menu down and picks up his fork ready to eat his bloody plate of whatever it is. Does most women gesture the waitress like that? Nerezza asks before stabbing the very rare meat with his fork. Teresa tries not to stare. She lacks some etiquette but she’s not rude unless she chooses to be. I’m not what you consider most women. The waitress returns offering the two a bottle of champagne. She avoids Teresa only speaking with Nerezza who seems to have a natural charm. The thought crosses Teresa’s mind if he even realizes it. He accepts the offer and the waitress pops the cork and fills his glass ignoring Teresa’s all-together. Nerezza now begins to believe their waitress is rude as well but decides to keep these thoughts to himself despite Teresa giving the waitress the finger for the second time. Must you do that? Do what? You know what. The bird? Yes, the bird. Yep. Teresa takes a moment to look around the restaurant. She can see what the customers are paying for. It’s not the food, but the decorating. There are red velvet like curtains over the windows, chandeliers are hung every so many feet along the ceiling. Maybe it’s the lighting, but everything seems to shine and sparkle. And what of this date you may ask? This isn’t a real date and she isn’t out to impress anyone so one can guess it’s doing ok. He isn’t looking for sex and she isn’t either so that’s a plus. She hasn’t bee on a date since she last seen Scott. She’s been too involved with her career; she hasn’t given much thought to her personal life. Finally after a few long minutes of eating and drinking the expensive champagne, Teresa finally breaks the silence. She is feeling a bit buzzed having drunk two glasses already. Are you really a vampire? Nerezza places his fork beside his plate and raises his head. Through his red shades, his eyes meet her baby blues. The stare brings a chill up her spine and she finds herself readjusting in her seat before taking another sip of her champagne. It may be the alcohol, but she’s beginning to feel a bit feisty so she continues. Vampires have fangs right? So, let me see or do I have to give you a taste of Southern Comfort and find out myself? And yes, I do mean my wrestling move so wipe that fake confused look off your face. Nerezza simply shakes his head finishing up his plate. He reaches for the champagne bottle only to find enough for one glass left. He wonders what her tolerance level is. Having seen her temper in the ring with other Roster members, he chooses not to ask. Rumors in the locker room are she’s a ticking time bomb and no one knows when she’ll go off. If you’re finished, I’ll show you, but not here. Teresa doesn’t waste any time in standing up. The waitress notices they are leaving and approach the table. Nerezza pays the bill as Teresa adjusts a few wrinkles in her dress. Soon they are out the door. It is nearly twilight and the sun can be seen setting over the horizon. Its reflection makes the fountain they had met at shimmer in many shades of red and orange. Follow me Teresa Nerezza leads her toward the alleys, the places where you usually don’t go after dark. Teresa begins to wonder what he is up to but she’s a tough woman. She wrestles men twice her size nearly twice a week and is sure she can handle this so-called vampire if need be. He stops inside an empty alley. Teresa leans against one of the walls with her arms crossed over her chest. This better not be a joke or I swear you will be hurt. Nerezza smiles to Teresa sensing vulnerability in her. It comes to no surprise. He’s felt the same sense of fear and even seen it coat their eyes before he feed upon them. She is on his turf now. What’s the matter? Scared of the dark? No. Come closer then. Teresa unfolds her arms and steps closer. His lips part and reveal his pointed teeth. Teresa steps back with eyes wide and she reaches to touch one but he closes his mouth quickly and walks past her ready to leave. It doesn’t take one step past her before she grabs his arm stopping him. Wait what about your wings? Nerezza really gives Teresa a confused look. You know, vampires have wings. Can I see yours please? He searches her face for a sign of sincerity and finally sighs. Promise you won’t laugh? I promise. If I do, you can kick my ass on Fusion this Friday. Hesitantly, Nerezza takes off his jacket handing it to Teresa, and then his white tux shirt comes off revealing his well-built upper body. Having been used to seeing well-built men on a daily basis doesn’t really phase Teresa that much though the average female would probably go ape. This doesn’t chance that she is female and under the circumstances, she can’t help but to take a longer look than usual. That’s when she sees them. Two large black wings are barely seen expanding behind him. Teresa’s mouth drops. A pedestrian strolls by the alley in the nick of time to hear Teresa’s reaction. The poor guy cannot see anything, for his sake that is a good thing. OH MY GOSH! HOW HUGE! I have to touch them. No please, this is the first time I’ve showed them. Please? I promise. I won’t hurt you. I just want to touch. Teresa steps even closer persistent on touching his large wings. Instead, Nerezza folds his wings and grabs his shirt from Teresa. After putting it on as well as the jacket, he takes no time to button either and runs past Teresa and out of the alley. The pedestrian takes off running away from the scared vampire yelling. RAPE! RAPE! SHE’S TRYING TO RAPE ME! In the meantime, Teresa is left watching him in disbelief. What? No kiss good night? Hmmph! Men…
