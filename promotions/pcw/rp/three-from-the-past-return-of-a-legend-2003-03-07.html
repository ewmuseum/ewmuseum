Three from the past...Return of a Legend...

7 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-7-03 The screen illumines and we are met with the visage of an ancient appearing man. Clad in a red hooded cloak, he stares hard into a blood red pool. A full moon illumines the scene, and his look is one of consternation. Behind him stand three men, clad in red and black and golden armor, each carrying a large broadsword, belted at his hip. The ancient grimaces and shakes his head before turning to his companions… Ancient: Aye…The three of you have been summoned to perform a deed for the order…A deadly deed, but one that must be carried out. Once finished, you three may return to the void from whence thee were summoned from… The three nod their approval, eyes not leaving the visage of the ancient. He smiles and looks at each in turn. He faces the largest of the three first. This man is clad in blood red armor, and a red plume rises from his helm. His face is noble, yet his skin is pale as parchment. He stands with his arms crossed, waiting for the words, but with questions in his otherwise empty eyes… Ancient: Aye…Count Johansson. The finest warrior ever to ravage the countryside of Poland. Your swordsmanship is known through the ages as being that of a master. You shall be dispatched first… Count: But why? Why am I sent after the only semblance of the ancient bloodline? Why am I brought from my peaceful rest to perform a deed for the likes of the dark Druids? The ancient smiles warmly and shakes his head. With a flick of his wrist, a blue flame engulfs the warrior. He screams in agony as his body glows with the blue. The ancient lets out a cackle, then flicks his wrist once more, and no sooner than the flame appeared, it is gone. The Count falls to one knee and shivers, with the ancient staring down upon him… Ancient: Because I deem it so. You are to do my bidding. That is the way this will go. Otherwise, I will torment your sold for an eternity. I believe that is price enough. Perhaps you need another look at your foe… The Ancient sweeps his arm over the blood red pool and the shot zooms in to see. The water ripples and we see our hero, Venneman Pendragon. He rides upon a snow white horse, and with his sword, that appears wholly as a lightning bolt in his right hand, he smites down different visages of evil. His eyes glow with a red fire, yet he fights for goodness and honor. One can see it in the evil in the eyes of his enemies… Ancient: Next, tis you Sir Richard. The man the Ancient’s gaze falls upon now stands resplendent in golden armor. His face is that of a noble, and his beard is cut quite short. His strong jaw shows his defiance at the task the Ancient has awarded him… Richard: Thou wouldst believe that I might smite Pendragon for thee? Thee knowest me not… Ancient: I know thy acts well, Lionheart. And you should know that if thou should fail me, an eternity of hellish torment awaits thee. Thou has retaken on the human form thanks to me, and I should get some gratitude. I ask merely a small task, one that surely a great warrior such as yourself should be able to complete. Richard: Nay….. The Ancient cuts him off… Ancient: Silence FOOL!! Ye do what I say, speak when I say ye can. Unless…. The holds his hand up and a blue flame dances across his palm. Richard shies away from the contact and the Ancient smiles…. Ancient: I thought not…And last, we come to you…. The third man is perhaps the noblest looking of the trio. His golden hair is tied behind his neck, and his beard is also the same luxurious color. He stands in black and silver armor, yet his scabbard at his side stands empty. His blue eyes remind us of our own hero, and for good reason… Ancient: Arthur Pendragon. Fitting that should the others fail, the task shall fall to thee. After all, you are the start of this mess, it is only just that you should be the end of it as well. Arthur: I shall not slay mine kin, evil one. Ancient: I know it will be hard, but what would you give to hold Excalibur in your hand once more? To rule the world as you once ruled England? To feel the great life and energy that that wonderful weapon can bring to thee? Arthur: And how would it be that I might have mine weapon back, darkling? Ancient: Come…Look with me… Arthur steps up to the pool and peers in. Venneman Pendragon is shown in all his armor and glory, dropped into a defensive stance with Excalibur extended before him. Arthur reaches towards the pool before realizing it is only an apparition. He frowns at the Ancient… Arthur: Mine sword is still in mine family, and tis actually within my reach. We shall see, darkling… Ancient: Aye, we shall. And sooner than thou would think…. *************************************************************** The scene cuts to an airport, and we see Venneman Pendragon walking through to a terminal for his private jet. The attendant smiles and pays no mind to the sword or to the armor. After all, it is his plane. He walks through the gate and the shot again switches to show him seated and belted to a couch in the jet. He frowns as he looks out the window to see the world flying by, and he appears a tad unnerved… “If man were meant to fly, he would have wings, not arms and legs…” AYE. Venneman: Still with me? AYE. NOW THAT THEE HAVE MADE THE DECISION TO BE COMPLETE. Venneman: I will miss the intelligent conversation though. ‘Tis hard for one as myself to find another to confide in. AYE, I KNOW. I TOO WILL MISS THEE… Venneman’s eyes close for a moment before flying open. He has a crazed look in his eyes as he seems to stare at nothing and everything at the same time… Venneman: Something is wrong… AYE, I FEEL IT AS WELL… Venneman: I fear that in the world, I am no longer alone…And I know not the ramifications of that feeling, not how it could be. I am the last of the ancient bloodline, but I feel now as though I may not be… GOOD CHRIST! Venneman: Aye……. The scene fades as Venneman looks around nervously in the jet, seeming to believe that at any moment an enemy will beset him… TO BE CONTINUED……
