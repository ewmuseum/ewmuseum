Will Kano be at Fusion?

20 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/20/2003 ~What will you do if hell actually freezes over? Well it never will. It is just an expression. But this widely-used expression could be applied to what has happened as of late to one Jon Kano, the 'REAL LEGEND,' if you will. Mr. Kano has always been a stand-up guy. He was a company man. He did everything he could for the better of the whole, the whole in this instance being Primetime Championship Wrestling. Ever since PCW was formed it seems as if Kano has been there. Kano has been to the top of the ladder and to the bottom of that same ladder, and everywhere in between. Not many have done this, but those who have rarely have done it with such grace and dignity as Jon Kano. Kano has had his very public spats with management throughout his legendary career here in PCW. Up until recently, many thought it was just part of Kano's gimmick or part of an on-going storyline. The truth is up until very recently, it was. Now Kano is enraged with PCW's direction, both creatively and behind the scenes. I guess Hell hath frozen over.~ *The scene opens up inside of Jon Kano's huge villa on top of the highlands surrounding Las Vegas, Nevada, Kano's hometown. Jon Kano is sitting in his living room, which consists of several black leather reclining chairs, two black leather sofas, a huge stereo system, three glass top coffee table with gold frames, and a 60 inch flat screen plasma television. Kano is sitting in front of the t.v. on his reclining chair watching 'The Best of 'The REAL LEGEND,' a highlight video of Kano's PCW career, available from PCW shopzone on February 23.* Kano: Those were the days. Look, PCW wasn't corrupt then. Granted, we were all wrestling in big gyms, but it was pure and good. There wasn't a CEO here whose only purpose is to destroy PCW. He doesn't see PCW as a company, but as his own personal toy. I look at PCW and I see a company with tradition and history. These traditions are what made PCW a legend's yard. Traditions like honor, valor, hard work, paying your dues, and respect. It hasn't been that way for a long time, too damn long. And while other decent CEO's would go out and seek a man to help save a company, this worthless, pathetic CEO finds a man to destroy it. Jesus, Mary, and Joseph, what has become of the company that I helped build? *Kano's cell phone, which was sitting on the table in front of his couch, begins to ring. Kano picks it up, takes a look at the caller id, smiles, and flips his phone open to answer the call.* Kano: Hey Duke. What's up, my hillbilly brother? *Kano pauses for his long time friend, Duke Williams, to speak.* Kano: He did what? He booked me for a f*cking match? That son of a bitch. *Kano once again pauses.* Kano: Well, at least its against Chet. Chet and me are tight. We got a long history in PCW. Christ, all the way back to MEDIA. I don't know, Duke. I might be there, I might not. *Kano pauses as you can hear Duke's tone of voice, but not make out the words.* Kano: Alright, Duke. I'll be there, as a favor to you. I am not promising that I am gonna give a huge match. I might just pull Chet onto me and hold him there for me to get pinned. I still am not too sure what I am going to do. PCW just isn't what it used to be, when it was pure and good. Yeah, I'll seeya Friday. *Kano shuts his phone and tosses it back onto the table.* Kano: Chet King on Fusion, I can handle that. I guess I'll just have to show not Chet King, but Mr. Syko and Mr. Kannon why Mr. Kano is what REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!! *The scene fades to black as Kano goes back to watching his video.*
