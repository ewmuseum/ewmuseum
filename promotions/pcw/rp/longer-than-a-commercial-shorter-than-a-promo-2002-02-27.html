Longer Than A Commercial, Shorter Than A Promo

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/Feb/2002 We open shot on D-Kay Calyx stood inside his home in Chicago, Illinois. He's dressed in a pair of black jeans, a black Red Dwarf t-shirt bearing the slogan 'SMEG OFF, YOU ANNOYING, SMEGGING SMEGGER!', and a pair of protective gloves on his hands. The camera freezes on Calyx, before panning around the room we are in. We see a variety of computer and audio equipment in one corner of the room, while the other hosts a television and video, and a black armchair. Panning back to Calyx, the shot starts again, as D-Kay walks over to the chair and sits down. Turning the television on, Calyx immediately turns to one of the many comedy channels, half way through an episode of The Simpson's. D-Kay watches for a few minutes, but is interrupted by a telephone call on his cell phone. D-Kay Calyx: 'Yo...' Voice: 'D-Kay, i got us a vehicle...' We recognise the voice of fellow Chicago native, and PCW wrestler, Mark Estep. D-Kay Calyx: 'Can it hold 6 people?' A slight pause follows, before Mark answers the question. Mark Estep: 'Uhh... I'd think so.' D-Kay Calyx: 'Good... Alright, I have to get ready to go....' Mark Estep: 'Alright man, I'll pick you up in about an hour...' D-Kay Calyx: 'Cool, later dude...' Calyx hangs up the phone from Mark, turns off the television and walks over to the equipment in the corner, lifting an amplifier off of the table, and placing it on the floor. D-Kay Calyx: 'I hope to God this gets there ok. She'll kill me if it doesn't.' We watch on as Calyx takes the amplifier, a mixing desk, and various other bits of computer and audio equipment, before placing them in cardboard boxes, and leaving them near the door. D-Kay then removes the gloves, and sits back down in the armchair, grabbing his cell phone again, and punching in a few numbers. D-Kay Calyx: 'Hello? Becky? It's Darren. The equipment is on the way. I've got Mark Estep bringing it up on the way to March Madness. Listen, I can't talk now. I have to go. Speak to you soon babes.' D-Kay hangs up the phone, and sits back, before dialling another number. D-Kay Calyx: 'Hey. Red? It's D-Kay. Listen. I got something to ask. Friday Fusion, we have to make sure Walden wins the match. Are you ok with that concept? ... What do you mean 'What's a concept?' ... Are you totally dense? ... BOOLIE! You moron. Put Red on the line.' Calyx shakes his head slightly as he waits for Red Rock to come onto the line. Red Rock: 'Hey D-Kay. What's up?' D-Kay Calyx: 'Red. When did Boolie start doing such a bloody good impression of you?' Red Rock: 'Since the bank started calling me at home.' D-Kay Calyx: 'OK...? Whatever man. Listen, I need to speak to you about Fusion. We need to ensure Tim gets that belt.' Red Rock: 'Why?' D-Kay Calyx: 'So I can take it from him on Sunday. Then, with us both having won singles gold; we can concentrate on those tag belts. That is if you're still up for that?' Red Rock: 'Metallyx is still strong. As is the Wild Deuces. I can't stay long, Boolie's walking near the television with a screwdriver. I'll have to speak to you later.' D-Kay Calyx: 'OK. I'll talk to you on Friday.' Both ends of the line suddenly click dead as D-Kay returns his phone to his pocket. Calyx looks up again, and moves towards the boxes, carrying them into the hallway of the large house in Chicago. As he places the last box on the floor, the doorbell rings. D-Kay answers straight away, and is met face to face by a courier from DHL, complete with DHL uniform, and a packaged 'Jiffy' bag. 'Mr Calyx? Please sign here sir.' D-Kay takes the pen from the man, signs the documents, and opens the jiffy bag as the man walks away. Calyx empties the contents onto the top of one of the boxes, and looks at the small number of computer discs with a smile. D-Kay Calyx: 'Ah. Rebirth, Fruity Loops, Prologic, Akai. Excellent. Becky now has the software as well. Now...all I need is for Mark to turn up.' Fade to black...
