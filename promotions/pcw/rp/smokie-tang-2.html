Smokie Tang #2

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 1, 1993 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens into a sunshine filled day and the year “1993” scrolls across the bottom of the screen. The scene is that of a rundown street that seems to be vacant, other then the scandalously dressed streetwalkers who plague almost every street in downtown Wilson. The buildings on this road are all condemned and broken down, yet several people come bustling out of one of the buildings, with cigarettes and liquor bottles, which some of them start throwing onto the straights. They’re all screaming and raising hell like most people in the southern town of Wilson, North Carolina. But these people are drug dealers. Among the dealers is Smokie Tang with two women on his arms and a joint in his mouth. There are exactly three drug dealers, excluding Smokie Tang who has two women with him, so six people walk out of the building after making a deal. They walk out into the street and car, which is a bleached clean ‘76 Chrysler, rolls up from around the corner and drives up in front of them. Some of the dealers start to put away their cigarettes, which were actually joints, and Smokie Tang just keeps his out. A cleanly dressed white guy, opens the driver’s side door and steps out of the car. He is wearing a white fur coat, which is extremely clean, and underneath that is a pure white suit, which is also extremely clean. Also getting out of the car on the passenger side is a guy dressed in a solid royal blue suit with a black tie and black boots on. He’s got a carrot in his mouth and has crumbs from the carrot and what looks to be celery all over his suit. He steps around the front of the car and stands in front of the clean man. Two more people get out the back seats of the car, each one is wearing a black suit and black hat with sunglasses on, the Dark Messiahs. They step around the back and stand behind the clean man to give him back-up protection. Smokie smiles at the clean guy and walks forward to him calling him by his name as he walks around him… Smokie Tang: Clean P! Well, if it is Clean P Corg. How long has it been since we met? {{{{Clean P Corg just frowns and snaps his fingers. The guy in the blue suit steps over and grabs Smokie and pulls him away from Clean P Corg, and escorts him back over to his women…}}}} Smokie Tang: Whoa! Sloppy Makonie, what happened between us man? We used to be such good pals. {{{{Sloppy Makonie lets go of him and he then just frowns at Smokie…}}}} Sloppy: Our friendship ended when you continued to do your fathers work. {{{{Smokie smiles at him, it could be the effects of the weed, or he might just feel like smiling at him. Smokie turns around and looks at the dealers, whom have been edging there way away from the group…}}}} Narrator: Huh? Thought I wouldn’t pop in on this one didn’t you? Well, what would this story be without Smokie’s best friend, Dante Jackson, in fact there’s me coming into this scene right now. {{{{Dante Jackson walks onto the street from an ally way and sees the commotion. He walks over to Smokie to give him moral support…}}}} Narrator: I had to give Smokie moral support, I mean, if you know the drug dealers around here, you’d know that they’re whips when it comes to snitches, which Clean P Corg is one of the biggest snitches out there. Dante Jackson: What’s the commotion over here? {{{{Smokie turns around and his smile turns into a huge grin as he sees Dante walking up. Dante is wearing a solid white mesh shirt, and blue jeans. He has on black sneakers and looks to be in the mood to raise hell, much like what Smokie and the dealers were doing before Clean P Corg and his gang showed up…}}}} Smokie Tang: Ah, Dante, my best friend. Clean P Corg here showed up for some reason. I think he wants a whooping from my cane. Speaking of which, did you get my cane washed? {{{{Dante lifts the cane up, which he was holding the whole time, but hadn’t been seen in the scene. He gives it to Smokie and smiles as he says…}}}} Dante Jackson: It’s waxed and ready for you to use. What are they doing here anyway? Smokie Tang: I don’t know, they just showed up for no reason, we were here just having fun and here comes his gang in his ’76 Chrysler and all. What are you doing here Clean P Corg? {{{{Corg looks around to make sure his back up is around him and then he answers…}}}} Clean P Corg: I’m here to see what you’re up to. You see the kids around here look up to you for some reason and you’re a bad influence to them. I’m here to make you turn straight. And if you don’t turn straight then to jail you go, and I mean that. {{{{Smokie just smiles and then drags on his joint as he starts to swing his cane around…}}}} Smokie Tang: You and what army? You think your guys can stand up to my high twirling action with this cane? I don’t think so. {{{{Clean P Corg snaps his fingers and Sloppy runs at Smokie, Smokie whips around the cane and tags Sloppy with several shots to the head and body. Sloppy falls over in pain and then Clean P Corg snaps his fingers again, and this time the Dark Messiahs run forward and try to double team Smokie. Smokie just twirls the cane in the air and lands a blow on the first fighter, and then nails the second fighter, he did it all in one twirl, they both land hard on the ground and don’t get back up, but writhe in pain. He then looks at Clean P Corg, who looks around himself and notices that all his back up is down…}}}} Clean P Corg: You might have stopped me this time Smokie, but next time I’ll be the one who’s laughing when you’re in jail. Or better yet, when you’ve changed and turned over a new leaf. Ha! I’ll be back Smokie! {{{{Clean P Corg gets into his car and starts it up. Sloppy gets up and limps over to the car and the Dark Messiahs do the same. They get into the car through they’re respective doors and then Clean P Corg hits the car in reverse, and then turns into an ally way, which leads to the main street of Wilson. Smokie just turns around and high fives Dante, by this time the dealers are gone and Smokie’s ladies are all that are left there. Smokie twirls the pimp cane and then takes the arms of his ladies, and he and Dante walk down the street towards Smokie’s downtown apartment…}}}} Narrator: You see, Smokie is one bad mother watch your mouth. You don’t mess with Smokie when he has his cane. But what was destined to happen later we weren’t prepared for. It seems that Clean P Corg would come back, and next time he would have a better chance. {{{{The scene changes to a black man in a business attire. He is standing in the middle of an office, and above him it reads “Calyx Inc.” in bold letters. Around the sign that reads that, are carrots and celery and other such food products. He is standing in front of a chart that has the production levels of several years, and he starts to point at them. The scene zooms back and shows the entire office and it’s a board meeting. All the men are in business suits and are either fifty or older, except for D-Kay Calyx that is…}}}} D-Kay Calyx: These are our production levels in the past ten years. You see how high they were when my father was running this place. Yet, look at the steady drop every year. We’re losing interest in the children community. We were the top corporation around North Carolina and now we’ve sunk to one of the lowest. It seems that kids are into drugs, guns, and un-nutritional food. We need to spark an interest in they’re wanting for nutritional food, juices, and other such good products. Now does anyone here have any suggestions to how we could go about this? {{{{No one holds their hands up initially, but then one guy puts his hand up, and this guy, who was not noticed before, seems younger then most the group, no older then thirty. D-Kay acknowledges his hand and points for him to stand and give his suggestion. The man stands and says…}}}} Executive 1: Well, I believe that the biggest influence on the children today is Smokie Tang. He’s been endorsing drugs, alcohol, snack foods, and other such products for years, and even at age 17 now he’s still influencing the kids. {{{{D-Kay thinks for a little while, and then finally smiles and gets an idea…}}}} D-Kay Calyx: Well, I’ve noticed that this Smokie Tang endorsed such products. Is Clean P Corg still on our pay roll? {{{{Another executive answers up and says…}}}} Executive 2: Yes he is sir. D-Kay Calyx: How about our residential virgin? Executive 2: You mean Charlie Loc sir? D-Kay Calyx: Yes. {{{{The executive opens up a notebook that was sitting in front of him and he then skims down and then says with a smile…}}}} Executive 2: Yes he also is sir. {{{{D-Kay gets a huge grin across his face and then says…}}}} D-Kay Calyx: I’ve got a grand plan to take care of Smokie Tang and also bring up our productions. Here’s the plan… Narrator: Uh oh! This isn’t good, with this big corporate executive going after Smokie Tang, and with Clean P Corg at his side? Smokie Tang is in big trouble! I can only wonder what they’ll do! {{{{The scene fades from the corporate executive office and then fades into an apartment complex. Smokie Tang and Dante walk up to the building and then look up. Smokie’s suite is at the top of the building. Smokie and Dante walk up the steps and into the front door of the semi-rundown building, which is barely still livable. The front hallway is narrow and barely big enough for someone to breath in. A musty smell fills the air, which smells like rotting flesh and rat sh*t mixed together. Smokie and Dante just plug their noses and hit the elevator call button. The door automatically opens up since the elevator was already at the first floor. They climb on and click for the floor number twelve, which also has a label next to marked as “Smokie”. The elevator climbs up the building extremely fast, faster then you would expect, and then just a few seconds later they’re all ready up to the Smokie’s floor. The elevator door opens and fills with fresh air. The floor is surprisingly clean and looks to be in top quality shape. They walk onto the floor and through a doorway, suddenly a green smoke filters out through the door and into the hallway. Smokie steps into the room and sniffs the air and just smiles. There in that room are several whores that work under him, or dealers who are were waiting to do business with Smokie. He just sits down beside one of the dealers and smiles as he sits down…}}}} Smokie Tang: So, what do I owe this special occasion? Dealer: Well I’m here mainly to exchange several ounces of marijuana for some hours with some of your hoes. Your hoes are some of the best in the world you know that right? {{{{Smokie smiles, and looks at Dante who starts to smoke on a bong, which is being passed around…}}}} Smokie Tang: Yes I know that. I’ll accept your offer. Who do you like in this room, and where is the weed? Dealer: The weed is already up here in this apartment building, and in your storage room, and the hoes I want are all the ones in this room. {{{{Smokie looks around and sees that among his current whores is also Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey, who once was a man, but had a sex change. He then points at her and says…}}}} Smokie Tang: You can have them all but her. She isn’t up for sale here. Dealer: All right, but I want the rest of them. Smokie Tang: Deal. {{{{The two smile and shake hands. The dealer and some of his gang grab a few girls and walk out of the room, the room now is virtually empty, except for Smokie, Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey, and Dante Jackson, who are just sitting back smoking pot. Smokie sits back and smiles and says…}}}} Smokie Tang: Life is good. Narrator: Yeah, life was good at that time. Even for me, look at me, look how wasted I was getting, but hey, that’s the name of the game around Smokie Tang. But how long would life be good for Smokie Tang? Only time would tell.
