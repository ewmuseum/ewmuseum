Free At Last...(Part 1)

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-17-02 Oh how sweet it is, the brief nectar of relief. When I sigh, my problems drift away… and for a swift moment, even I, the Infinite Warrior Trigger, may feel contentment with the world, contentment with life. Oh, how I long this feeling would stay. How I long I could forever more be comforted by the satisfaction of a perfect world. But as the momentary feeling wears off, in comes the trouble, like a swarm of ants. Right then, right when you think everything is perfect, the trouble comes and eats you up inside. That was just the case with the events of yesterday. Even though in the summer, it was a cold day, with the wind rushing into my skin, sending shivers down my spine. I was prepared for the worst… but craving the best, for I was about to face a seemingly indomitable challenge. I was about to face the justice system. I was ashamed of the actions that led me to this point, but could do nothing about it. The past is in the past, and the only way to look is ahead. However I couldn't help but reminisce of the heartless deed that might have possibly destroyed my future. I couldn't help but remember how on a cold day such as this, only a few short months ago, I had taught WIT 3 a lesson. He wanted to be a warrior, but he had such an attitude… an attitude that needed to be broken. And so I broke it. I locked him in the trunk of a car, and I drove him off a f*cking bridge! Such passion I feel when speaking of the crime… such impulses flood through my body like liquid fire, eating at my soul. But nevertheless, it was wrong. Nevertheless, I got caught. And nevertheless, I would have to pay. I had arrived at the county jail early in the morning, for a meeting with my court appointed attorney. Kirk Frinkle was his name, a small man with slick black hair and broken eyeglasses. He had pointed me towards a chair in his office, and then nervously opened his briefcase, eyeing me all the while. He didn't believe in me; I could feel that right away. The lawyer was afraid of me--of me… when in fact, I was afraid of him. I was afraid because he was part of the system; the very system that had tracked me down and now brought me to justice. This fear would not get in the way though, because in the end the only thing that could help me prevail was my wits and my wits alone. 'Is there any… is there any proof whatsoever that you did not commit this crime?' he had asked, prying the door of my mind open for prodding… unfortunate in his disapproval. 'No. Because I did commit this crime.' Mr. Frinkle had looked worried. Nervous or not, he knew that my honesty was a fault, possibly one that could cost him the case. One that would leave a blemish in his hopeful career. I knew right away he was a rookie. However he had tried, the snake-like venom did not seep through his being. He had not been hardened to the toughness that all workers must face. Many would think this is the worst kind of lawyer. He was young, inexperienced, and obviously unsure of himself. He wouldn't know what to say… he wouldn't stop when he should… and when it came down to spew the inevitable untruthfulness--he would break. Many would think that he could not succeed in proving my innocence. Many are wrong. In fact, this was the perfect kind of attorney, because with no self esteem, he could be molded… molded into something quite like a warrior… The meek, fearful defendant sunk back into the chambers of my soul, as the Infinite Warrior emerged. I had a mere hour before the meeting was over. That would be a short amount of time, but it would be enough. Because you see, in my life I have realized that things do not come easy. If life can throw you a curveball in any way, shape, or form, then it will. And dodging won't work. You've got to step up to the plate, and come right at your frustrations and problems… to attack them head on. This is what I was doing here. I was taking a situation that couldn't get any worse, and finding a spark of light inside it. Kirk Frinkle was not the warrior type, nor had the inner strength of a warrior. His aura would not withhold pain, and his battle cry would not be heard. But I was stuck with this man to guide me out of utter darkness, and had no choice but to accept. Under the circumstances, I did not give him a chance either. Kirk Finkle was about to become Warrior In Training #4… To Be Continued…
