So ya' wanna know about Sivasian? Gather round...gather round.

21 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21st April, 2003 You come to me to ask about the history of Sivasian Krikorian. Can’t you go and ask his brother, his only surviving relative, he’s at home isn’t he? You tell me he’s in the states? Strange that, never usually goes further than the farm which seemed to be his home since he was fired, or rather quit from the Ukrainian army. You haven’t heard that bit have you? Well anyway, you want to know about this rather weird but almost always serious man. Before we begin, why is he in the states? WHAT? He’s joined one of those wrestling federations? Oh man, which one? PCW? Never heard of it. So anyways, I’ll tell you more if you buy me a coffee…….. Two sugars, white….err…..not too strong please, thanks. What you want to know? Some basic information you say to start with? Well, I’ll tell you. He’s a sniper, excellent with the gun, has been ever since he was brought into the world of rifles from his dad at the age of 4. Don’t ask him about it though, he doesn’t like talking about his father like that, it doesn’t feel right to him, to talk about someone who had such a huge impact on his life, but only for 8 years. Yeah, yeah he died, weird incident, no ones really ever been right down to the bottom of it, he’ll tell you very little, you’ll get almost nothing from him about it, but I’ve heard various rumours myself. I’ve even investigated for some 10 years of my life, learnt absolutely nothing. I do know, however, that Sivasian tells it straight and up. When the girls used to try and cheer up our poor hero here, they’d ask him, ‘Oh Sivasian, how do I look in my newly brought frock?’. He’d shrug and tell them to go away, say that looked ugly in western clothes, tell them to put on their home knitted tops and long , dull, grey boring skirts. Hell, you ask him how things are in his beloved Ukraine today and things are only going to be said as they are, you’ll get the odd ‘OK I support…’ from him as he ponders how to soften the obvious blow of admitting his country, his absolute love in the world, more than his wife, his two children, more than anything, is doing so bad. How this place needs UN aid to help it going. How the sports world is full of corruption. To win in the world of sports in the Ukraine you just pay the officials more than the other team or player, simple as how it is. Everything’s a shambles there, and in the end he’ll mutter, ‘Bad…but things will definitely improve…’ that’s what he’s been saying for the last 20 years anyways. I think that communism busted the country up real mind, and no matter how honest or straight forward Siv was, he wasn’t going to find a job doing practical world in terms of cheap mass produced western rubbish easy to swallow. He couldn’t do the average family life, he couldn’t simple go home to a wife, have 2 kids, bring them up to do the same, and so the circle continues. He couldn’t take the cheese and ham sandwiches the wife had made him and then go and have them at 1pm when the shift supervisor made them stop work. If they were allowed they wouldn’t have those 15 minutes off, such is the poor, or rather lack of a high one, in that torn down country. Ever since Hitler took his tanks, his army and his damn pride and ego into their, it’s been ripped up and shredded time after time. The government didn’t want a repeat, however, of such a war, and so , as many of their fellow communist states had, including their strong ties with Russia, they set about building a vast empire of tanks, ships, aircraft and anything else that could fire something at another country. Sivasian saw his chance to shine ya’ know. When ya’ know things are just right, that something feels right deep down inside, how you could push through and be the best at something. Well, Siv wanted to be the best, the best in the world. He could shoot, he’d been doing it for 15 years when the chance came up. He had nothing from his supposed ‘education’, in which he learnt that the West blew and that the communist states ruled, almost along the same lines as Nazi propaganda worked, he was warped, had his mind twisted. Then there was something called the Iron Curtain. The United States of America, one huge military force, pushing against the other, the USSR, battling it out for supremacy of the planet. Each one tried to gain the upper hand on one another, from 1945, to the late 80’s the cold war stood, until the USSR was no more, and the countries separated from each other. Sivasian was destroyed inside, he’d trained to be the BEST sniper in the world if you will, fighting for his country, but now he couldn’t get his chance to do what he’s always wanted, to kill an American. I know you’re shocked, but he’s never told me why, never explained. I can’t penetrate that well guarded brain in their, I can’t tell WHY he hates the States so much, in fairness during the cold war they protecting the rest of the world, all of us from total disaster. You want to find out more? Go find him yourself, you might find something out that I haven’t already told you…now go. What’s that? You wanna know what he looks like? Well fine. He has thinning wispy hair, a kinda light brown almost greyish tint to it. His eyes are piercing bright blue ones, they’ll go right through ya like bullet holes. He has scares, scares on his hands, body, his feet, his face. Each one has a different story to tell, and he remembers just about how he got everyone. Just because he’s in his 40’s, has grey hair and has scars doesn’t mean the man’s weak though, far from it, far , far from it.
