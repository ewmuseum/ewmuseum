Devotion

4 June 2002

Date: 6.4.02 6.4.02- Present Time -6:27 pm.Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. There’s nothing like a beautiful sunset to dwell upon after an eventful day. The purple radiance from resting sun mixes in with the pink, and the pink mixes in with a light blue. The clouds stay far from the sun, almost as if allowing it to have its attractive moment of fame. Down on the surface of earth, mankind goes through with their habitual routines of doing the same thing over and over again. It is like they’re all working together, painting an abstract work of art on a canvas, making it as perfect as possible, trying to keep the serenity for many lifetimes. As the weather station stated earlier this morning, “Gooooooooood morning, Pennsylvania! Rise and shine, because today is going to be a great day, Pittsburgh! It’s gonna be a scorcher, so have your air conditioner on full blast!” I walked down the sidewalk with a straight face, searching my surroundings. Sporting my usual see-through mesh shirt and my black baggy PVC pants, I don’t seem to burn up on the hot day. I find myself reaching a corner, and a man in torn clothes and unhealthy hygiene holds a sign that reads, “Will work for Food.” I take one look at him wearily, thinking about what a couple more bucks in his pocket may mean…before rolling my eyes and walking on. As I would have expected, under the man’s breath he mumbles foul words. I hear them…but I walk on. I took a right and found myself on Gordon Lane. Four preteen kids play hide-and-seek, trying to keep their mind off of the warm summer day. They laugh as they run from each other, trying to make it to base. Like most instances, the chubby kid is “it.” On the side of an Oak tree in the yard that the kids run in, is a white thermometer. The mercury almost seems to boil in the sunlight as I read the engraved fluid of the thin red line. “One Hundred and Four Degrees…” Damn, it was sizzling hot. But, if you ask me…it was better than being indoors. There was nothing like a little fresh air after not seeing the sunshine for two days. Most of the time, I would rather stay inside. Ok, well all of the time. But today, I just felt like taking a walk. A little bit further down Gordon Lane, a white coloured house comes into view. An elderly couple swing back and forth on the portico in a wooden swing, staring deeply into each other’s eyes. They smile and giggle occasionally, speaking of old memories. I find it quite unfair how a couple can stick together so long. It’s inequitable that they can still be in love so much. Look at them. They’re just so perfect. The woman smiles and tilts her head back as the man gently tickles her, before kissing her cheek. He whispers something in her ear, and her eyes seem to swell up in tears, as she smiles, embracing him into her arms with pure love. I turn my head and walk toward the curb, taking a seat. I heave a sigh as I gaze at the setting sun, yearning that everything was normal again. I remember back when I was in love... --2.26.937:03 am.Detroit, Michigan. “Kris, honey, I have to go,” the words of my fiancé, Erin Saunters. She was scheduled for a business meeting in New York City, today. “If you need anything, it’s in the cabinet. I put the chicken noodle soup in the microwave for you, if you’re feeling any better later.” “Ok, baby, hurry up and go. I don’t want you to be late,” How much I loved to hear her words and didn’t really want her to go, I knew that this was the opportunity of her lifetime. She had finally gotten the promotion that she was looking forward to. All she had to do to finalize it was to have a special meeting with her manager at the World Trade Center offices. It was originally set for February 25, 1993, but she managed to move it up one day because of my ‘unique’ plans for her birthday. “I love you, Kris,” those words tingled my spine, every time. I couldn’t let her go just like that. I heard the door open. “Wait!” I finally said it. “Yeah…?,” she said as I heard her foot impatiently tapping the tiled floor. “I love you, too,” I never knew that those words could mean so much in so little time. Soon, I would find out. After I said that, I think I could hear her break a smile, as I heard the door shut. I was still in my P.J.’s, which consisted of a white t-shirt and blue pants with white stripes. I dreaded the things, but if it made Erin happy, then I’d wear them. It didn’t really matter to me if I was still in my bedtime clothes or not, though, as I made my way out of bed with haste. As I reached the front door, I ran outside, hoping to get another glance of my beautiful soon-to-be. I always seem to have bad luck. As I reached the doorstep, the black Pontiac Grand Dam took a left, and drove out of sight. I just shrugged and crossed my fingers for luck, praying that everything would go good with the job. I reached down and picked up the daily Detroit newspaper, and whistled a lively tune of melody, as I walked back into the house. Reaching the stair banner, I hold onto the rail. I sneeze two times and laugh. I almost forgot that I was sick. That’s the only reason that I didn’t go with Erin. Ah, she knows what’s best for me though. I remember the chicken noodle soup that she was talking about and begin to walk to the kitchen. Before making it to the kitchen, I groan in pain, as I reach down and grab what I stubbed my toe on. Holding my pinkie toe in anguish, I pick up the small black object. “Damn it, Erin. You’re always forgetting this. Why do you think we bought it? It must have fallen off of the banister,” that was her, alright. She always left her cell phone at home. I couldn’t be mad at her, though. Sometimes people are forgetful. This is the woman that had taken my mind off of the bad memories of my childhood. I’m only the way I am now because of her. If I wasn’t with her right now, I would be a man of complete misery. At the time I didn’t know that the thought would foreshadow of what was to come. 2.27.93- Next Morning -8:23 am.Detroit, Michigan. I must have fallen asleep. As I woke up and glanced at the alarm clock, I wiped my eyes. I couldn’t believe that I had slept the whole day and night with no intentions of waking up once. I must have been sicker than I thought…I grabbed the cordless phone beside the bed and began to press some buttons, searching for a phone call from Erin. “That’s not like her at all. Even if she left her cell phone, she would have at least called me from the hotel. It only takes her abut 2 hours to get to New York,” I shrugged and put the phone down. I knew she was ok…that’s the way my baby is. I rested my head on the pillow. I grabbed the remote while yawning and pressed the power button. Switching through the channels I didn’t really find anything that seemed to catch my eye. Finally reaching the news channel, I saw something about some World Trade Center bombing…so I turned it off. After sitting there for about a tenth of a second, my eyes widened in fear as I quickly turned the television set back on. I could already feel the tears…scared of what I may discover. “The United States was under attack yesterday. The Federal Bureau of Investigation has not yet gave any information regarding the situation…hold on…we have just received word from the FBI stating what has happened. The twin towers of the World Trade Center were aimed to be attacked, yesterday. Two terrorists, described as Arabs, drove a rented van from Ryder Truck Rental Company into the parking garage beneath the World Trade Center. They parked and got into a new car, driving away from the scene. Inside of the van was a homemade 1,200-pound urea-nitrate bomb. The truck bomb exploded just after noon, but it failed to topple the World Trade Center or the twin towers. The lower end of Manhattan, including Wall Street, is virtually shut down. This resulted in economic losses estimated at $650 million. The terrorists are still at large…the truck bomb did considerable damage, destroying several levels of the parking garage, killing six people. Over one thousand people ended up with an injury from the blast’s effect. That’s all we have for now…and we will keep you updated as we get more information.” Six people killed…six people killed…the words rang through my mind. How I knew that she was one of the six was beyond me…but I knew. -- 6.4.026:36 pm.- Present Time -Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania I get up from the curb and look back over at the elderly couple on the porch. I sigh again and make my way back down the road. I couldn’t stop thinking of how we were to get married a couple days later…but it was all put to an end. I miss her and still love her more with every passing day. “Where was your God for me? - ...What a load of bullshit.” End.
