Fate and The Dirty Politicians

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 26, 2002 OOC: Again I use language that is somewhat hard to follow and you have to read into it to get the true meaning but I think it makes for a more interesting roleplay. No one knew when The Heartbreakers began its slow and heavy uprising in the tag team division, that it was held and guided by the great hand of fate. Fate alone saw the meaning of the web they wove, the might of them, and their place in the making of a tag team history. One thought but little of the domination brought upon by the new tag team, calling them by other names and lighter ones, for the time unconscious of the strength of the team thrown across thousands of miles of doubt, disrespect and lack of reverence. Fate and a little bit of luck planned the uprising, and it seemed mere circumstance which guided the team to a better day -- the teams that were lined up to face the Heartbreakers have all diminished within the PCW organization. However, between the two men on Turmoil, there was no will to draw nearer. Each held apart. Those who had rebelled against that which their souls called tyranny, having struggled madly and shed blood in tearing themselves free, turned stern backs upon their unconquered enemies, broke all cords that bound them to the past, flinging off ties of name, kinship and rank, beginning with fierce disdain a first title reign. You see, Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart would be opponents this upcoming Turmoil. The team that has proved to be such a force in this organization will undergo there first real test. They will have to go face to face, alongside of several other PCW greats in a battle for a title that Jack Valentine has failed to conquer in the past, and Johnny Heart has yet to get the chance to. Hopefully those who, being rebelled against, found the rebels too passionate in their determination and too desperate in their defense of their strongholds to be less than unconquerable, will sail back haughtily to the world which seemed so far the greater power, the world of the tag team. Plunging into new battles, they added new conquests and splendor to their division, looking back with something of contempt to the half-savage. Valentine built his career going through PWA, learning the ropes and putting them to the test. He has gotten to where he is today from working from the bottom up. Heart on the other hand is along for a free ride at the hands of Valentine. A former PWA trainer who never learned the hard way. The two have a lot in common, but this Tuesday, their will be major differences. But while the two worlds were held apart, The Heartbreakers had to keep their focus…they have to remain focused on the tag team division, namely Politically Incorrect. They joined forces for one reason and one reason only, to capture the tag team titles. The journey being likely to be made once in a lifetime, both of their subliminal intentions were to capture titles in PCW, regardless of how they did it. Johnny aligned himself with Jack because Jack was a name in PCW, a person he could hang on the coattails of until he made a name for himself. Jack aligned himself with Johnny because of his size and brute strength, one with that much size has to be good for something, so he thought. The time now was a far cry from the time when The Heartbreakers, having shot to and fro, faster and faster, week by week, weaving new victims into their web each card, has woven warp and woof until they bind once again. The scene opens in the back of a stretch pink limousine, Jack and Johnny’s own personal limousine that Jack paid for at no expense from his bodybuilding days. The two are exchanging some meaningless small talk until Johnny makes a smart ass remark. “You do realize that you don’t stand a chance against me on Turmoil Jack, right?” He asked with a smile. “What? You think I don’t have a chance against YOU? You do remember I placed second in the Sea World Brawl, second in the campus brawl and have always been at the top of the ranks in the Extreme Title division here in PCW,” Jack responded with his nose held high. “Second Place? That’s fine with me…because it’ll be me who eliminates you for the victory,” Johnny sarcastically said. “Well, we’ll see about that…we’ll see if you can make it that far. One thing is for sure though, we can’t let this interfere with our tag team title shot coming up in the future. We have to keep our focus on the right thing, not some meaningless title held by a man that was too afraid to come down to the ring and face us,” Jack said. “That’s fine, we can win the tag team titles. Then they will introduce us as ONE HALF OF THE TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS Jack Valentine and THE OTHER HALF OF THE TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS AND EXTREME CHAMPION Johnny Heart,” Johnny says with hearts in his eyes. “The only way you could win that Extreme title is if I help your ass, and you can rest assured that I’ll be trying my best to win the title myself,” Jack said. “Well, we have to work together, that’s for sure. It’d be an interesting final to have Jack Valentine against Johnny Heart for the Extreme Championship! A real draw!” Johnny said. The sound of crickets chirping is heard after Johnny Heart’s remark about the “draw.” The two sit in silence for several minutes before the limousine stops near a local toy store where two little kids dressed in suits are standing, handing out flyers for a local politician. Jack and Johnny decide to get out and see what this is all about and spread their fame around the town. They always love to sign autographs and get noticed due to their egotistical attitudes. Jack walks over to one of the kids to see what he is passing out. He is handed one of the flyers and looks at it. It says something like “Support Politically Incorrect: The Choice of America’s Youth.” Jack looks at the kid who looks right back at him with a devilish grin. “Why are you kids supporting those losers,” Jack questioned. “Because they are our heroes and the greatest politicians I have ever seen,” one of the boys responds. “Greatest politicians?” Johnny inquired with a laugh. “Those guys are evil, corrupt and someone needs to put a stop to them. How do you even know about politics anyway at such a young age?” “No one can stop them, that’s what is so great,” the other boy said. “They are the greatest tag team ever in PCW, better than Goovil, better than Da Westsidaz. Hell they’re only competition is some fags named the Heartbreakers and they would break them in half…” “Break them in half?” Jack questioned. “Do you know who we are?” One of the boys circles Jack and Johnny and puts a finger on them like he is touching something special. “Are you God?” The little boy sarcastically responded. “The Pope? Santa Claus? Elvis? Or just a couple of fags dressed up with hearts on your pants…” Jack tries to go at the kids but Johnny restrains him. “We are the Heartbreakers…” Johnny said, “And you boys should learn a little bit better lesson in life. NEVER TRUST POLITICIANS. You should have role-models who are strong, independent and good human beings. They should also look good too. Hell, I know of two people that you little rascals could look up to…” “Who?” A boy asks. “Well…US!” Johnny said. “Ha,” the other boy said, “You guys remind me too much of the Heartbreakers. Two fairies who couldn’t wrestle your way out of a paper bag.” “Fairies? Where do young kids like you get off using that kind of language. And for the record, WE ARE THE HEARTBREAKERS! And you should know, IT’S ALL ABOUT THE PINK!” Jack screamed. “Figures…” The boys says. One of the boys spit in Jack’s face and the other one kicks Johnny in the shin before they sprint off down the street. This time Johnny tries to run after them but Jack restrains him. “They’re just kids Johnny, besides I think someone put them up to this,” Jack said. “…and I think I know who,” Johnny articulated as the scene fades to pink.
