Chang's Fears

14 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 14, 2003 Begin Transmission. (The Order of Society and Logic’s world tour continues. Over the ancient continent of Europe, to the Orient in South Korea, Afghanistan for Fusion, and now continuing onward towards Supremacy in the newly liberated Iraq. SOCIETY is tired of plane travel, which has become a regular part of his life. He now stands in Kabul, with Michael Chang at his side.) “Forgive me, Mr. Chang, for my efforts Tuesday evening.” “There is no need to be sorry. Your shoulders were not pinned to the ground. It is my fault. I could not have foreseen Violence Jack losing to Brianna. I expected him to make short shrift of the woman.” “We must learn to not make such mistakes again,” SOCIETY said. “Twice, underestimating our opponents has cost us victory.” (Chang nodded ruefully. He had expected this jaunt into PCW to be easy, and SOCIETY to rise quickly to the top. But, SOCIETY has had a difficult proving ground. He has defeated the Ahutmoth, Krikorian, and Robertson, yet lost to Shane Wethers, Brianna Washington, and Matt Jackson. 3 men he has defeated, two men and one woman he has lost to.) “Well, regardless,” Chang said, “This matchup on Tuesday is your chance to cement your spot in the match at Supremacy. You are in fourth as of now..but a victory in this match could boost your status tremendously.” “I know. I know this, Chang.” (SOCIETY’s answer was surprisingly flippant for the big man, a man who usually followed the tenets of respect to a T.) “I don’t like that tone in your voice, SOCIETY.” (Chang looked into SOCIETY’s eyes. They were soulless, emotionless.) “My apologies, Mr. Chang, but I meant no disrespect. I am just tired. Tired of being looked down upon. Tired of having to put up with people say I am not good enough to be victorious, when I know I am. Tuesday shall prove this for a fact. Tuesday, any in my path shall be made to CONFORM!” (The President of the Order of Society and Logic looked away from SOCIETY, knowing that he meant no harm.) “I…apologize, for my questioning, SOCIETY.”“Think nothing of it.” (He did think something of it, however. The problem was blatant. He had insulted his charge. He thanked the Order that SOCIETY was in a good mood. Chang lived in constant fear that his mood would alter..) “So, Mr. Chang, Friday looms as well.” (Chang had nearly forgotten. SOCIETY had his handicapped match on Fusion.) “Indeed it does SOCIETY. Indeed it does…” (The perceptive SOCIETY noticed Chang’s lack of words, something the President is not often without.) “Why are you so quiet today, President Chang?” (Michael could have filled a book with the answers. He had to admit it to himself. The President of the Order of Society and Logic was terrified of his own creation. Afraid of what he had wanted.) “Oh, no reason especially…” (He silently, desperately hoped that SOCIETY would not press him further. To his great relief, SOCIETY did not.) “Alright then. Well, I have a match to prepare for. Will we be going to the gym to train?” “Not this time, SOCIETY.” “Why not?” (Chang had another idea…) “I need to discuss impending plans with you. These plans will carry us through after Supremacy.” “You mean we are planning for me NOT winning at Supremacy?!” (SOCIETY was shocked, but Michael quickly diffused the situation.) “Not at all. This is a grander plan. Something to rise you in the eyes of all…and more importantly, make more CONFORM!” (SOCIETY smiled at the thought..)“Yes! We must make all in PCW CONFORM TO SOCIETY!” “Good, SOCIETY. Now, we must leave.” (The massive SOCIETY turns and leaves, followed closely by Chang.) End Transmission.
