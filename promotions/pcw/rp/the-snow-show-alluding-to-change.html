Date: Shanghai&#039;ed #2 ﻿************************************The Snow ShowSeason I, Episode 12“Alluding to Change”************************************ Location: Hattoni Mansion - New York City Date: January 16, 2003Time: 10:30 pm Snow stepped into the large, looming, office of Justin Hattoni, the Don of the Hattoni family. Justin was waiting for him in his familiar place behind the massive oak desk. Petey the Vicewas leaning on the desk. Neither man smiled. “You wanted to see me?” Snow asked. He was surprised how muffled his voice was in thisroom. It was said that the room was guarded twenty-four hours per day, just in case the Fedstried to plant a bug. “You’re here, ain’t ya?” Petey said, finally breaking the mould of his face into a smile. Snow nodded, and returned a smile, though it was far weaker than Petey’s. He put his hands inin the pockets of his dark blue suit - the one that his wife said brought out his eyes. Not that hecared for the compliment, but Petey had told him to look sharp, and he trusted his wife’s eyemore than his own. “Alright,” he said, pushing his yellow hair back - more to make sure it was still away from hisface than to fix anything about it. “So what do you need me for?” Justin’s eyes lit up - he admired Snow for the fact that no one, nor any situation, could intimidatethe young man. Even staring into the eyes of one of the most dangerous men in the world, JasonSnow remained casual, and on eye level. He was unbreakable, thought Justin. “It’s very big,” Petey piped back in. “And very dangerous,” Justin spoke for the first time. “Very dangerous. But don’t thinka job so important will go unrewarded.” Snow knew better than to inquire about the reward. “Have you ever heard of John Mossilano?” Petey asked as he stepped away from the desk andmoved to his familiar window-side post. He lit a cigarette casually. “No,” Snow answered plainly. “Good,” Justin remarked. “I don’t want you getting intimidated on the day of the job.” “I don’t get intimidated,” Snow replied in a flat voice that swore the truth of his statement. Justin smiled. “John Mossilano is the Don of the Mossilano family, which has had a verywonderful and profitable relationship with our own family. Lately, things between theMossilano family and the Hattoni family have... gone sour.” “And?” Snow lead Justin on. “And I want you to kill John Mossilano.” Snow stared blankly at Justin, who’s eyes assured the seriousness of his words. “You want me to kill a boss?” Snow said in a half whisper. “You want me to kill aDon!? I thought you guys were against this kind of thing. Aren’t you all about order andloyalty and the structure?” “We’re all about respect,” Justin said coldly. “It’s apparent that John Mossilano has no respectfor me, and therefore, no respect for this family. And if we can not get him to respect this familythrough peaceful means, then we’ll have to resort to... alternative options.” “What do you think?” Petey asked Snow from the window. “I think I need a drink of water,” Snow answered. *** Location: New York CityDate: January 19, 2003Time: 10:15 am “So, tell me again,” Raven said as she stared over the steering wheel of her small car, “why arewe going to the drugstore?” Krista rolled her eyes and gave a smile, “Feminine products.” “But Krista,” Raven said with a wrinkled nose, “it’s another two weeks before...” She didn’tfinish her sentence. Secretly, she was suspicious that Krista might be hiding something fromher. She feared it. When Krista woke up this morning and immediately asked if Raven coulddrive her to the drugstore, the older sister’s intuition sparked. “Is there something you’re nottelling me, Krista? I mean, you’re not sick, are you?” Krista rolled her eyes at the question as she had the last one. She “A lady must be prepared,” Krista told her sister and then turned to the window. With her faceout of her sister’s view, her smile faded. *** Location: Marty Verrelli’s Apartment - New York CityDate: January 19, 2003Time: 1:40 pm When the knock came at Marty Verrelli’s door, he immediately sprang to his feet and pulled thegun out from the back of his pants. “Yeah, who is it?” he asked, cautiously approaching thedoor. Ever since he had began dating Krista, though their meetings were secret, he had becomeincreasingly paranoid that her brother-in-law would one day arrive with a hot head. ConsideringJason Snow’s temperament, it wasn’t so far fetched an idea. “Open the fucking door!” boomed a low gruff voice from the other side, and instantly, Martyrecognized the voice of his father. He stuffed the gun back into his pants and pats down hisshort black hair. The Dragon entered the room with a bright smile in the middle of his thick black goatee, and heimmediately embraced his child. He was a few inches taller than Marty, and his beerbelly madehim seem twice his son’s size in girth. “What are you doing here?” Marty asked his father as he cleared a few magazines from the sofa. The Dragon sat down and pulled his jacket off, revealing his heavily tattoo’ed arms. A greendragon is inked on his right forearm, and it’s tail wraps around and up to his bicep. “Just dropped by, boy,” The Dragon said to his son. “Can’t a father say ‘hi’ once in a while.” “Sure,” Marty smiled, but secretly, the answer didn’t satisfy him. He stared in awe at his father -the man was a legend, even to his son. There was so little difference between the man Martyhad heard to stories about and his father. He had imagined there would be more. “So you told me you had a new girlfriend?” The Dragon said to his soon as he got comfortable. “When’s the old man get to meet her?” “I don’t know,” shrugged Marty. “It’s kind of tough for me to see her. We’ve got to sneakaround and shit, you know?” “Why?” The Dragon perked a suspicious eyebrow. “How old is this girl?” “Dad,” sighed Marty, “It’s not that. It’s... she’s got this asshole brother-in-law. He doesn’t wantus together and... well, you get the picture.” “Yeah,” nodded The Dragon. “I’ve heard a few things about the guy. Heard he’s a tough fuck.” “That’s what they say,” Marty rolled his eyes. “Truth is, I don’t see what’s so tough about him. He’s a big guy, sure - but that don’t make him tough.” The Dragon’s eyes sagged because he knew that his son was lying. He was always disappointedwhen Marty tried to act tougher than he was, just because he thought that’s what his fatherexpected. He was, after all, Marty Verrelli - The Dragon’s boy. Big things were expected. TheDragon wasn’t one of the many who expected things from Marty. Not in that way, at least. Hehad high hopes from his child, and secretly, he was dreadfully disappointed when he learned thatMarty had followed in his father’s footsteps when choosing a career. The younger Verrelli just wasn’t cut out for this life. “Funny you should say that,” The Dragon said. “I was at the Don’s place a few days back - theyseem to think this kid is tougher than fucking nails.” Marty glanced up. “Justin put me on a job with him,” The Dragon said as he looked down to his hairy, tattoo’ed,forearms. Marty realized that Snow must be moving up faster than the speed of light to be puton a jobber with his father, who was a legend in the Hattoni family. “A big job. That’s what Icame here to talk to you about.” “What kind of job?” Marty asked. “I can’t say,” sighed The Dragon. “But I’ve been around this business for a long fucking time,you know? And I know the way things work in this family.” He sighed again. “Marty,” he said with closed eyes. “If something goes wrong with this job, or if we fuck up, oranything... I ain’t coming back. I know that, Marty. That’s the way things work in thisbusiness.”
