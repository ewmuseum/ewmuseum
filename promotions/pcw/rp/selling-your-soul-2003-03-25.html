Selling Your Soul

25 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/25/03 'No. I simply refuse.' A gruff voice complains in the darkness. Anger seeps from his hidden lips, as one attempts to persuade him. 'Oh come on Clyde, it's not that bad.' 'Not that bad!? Look at me!' We fade in on the small form of the homicidal child known as Bethany, as she chuckles with her arms folded in front of a closed changing room door. Bethany: I can't look at you if you won't come out, dear. Clyde: That is the point, dear. No out. Bethany: Bah! It's not my fault you can't even beat a guy who's soul you already took 64 times. Clyde: Shut it. Bethany: It's not my fault either being a serial killer pays next to nothing. We need cash! Clyde: Why do we need money? We're demonic hellspawn! Bethany:(Screeching) I WANT NEW SHOES! Clyde: I can materialize you new shoes. Bethany: I don't want your crappy materialized shoes! Clyde: And what is wrong with my materialized shoes? Bethany: They suck like you! Clyde: Sweetie, from experience, you're the one who sucks. Bethany: Pervert! No sucking ever again then! Clyde: Oh like I care. Bethany: Er.....no biting ever again then! Clyde: ......you wouldn't. Bethany: I would a thousand times over. Clyde: ......I still don't care. I will not come out! Bethany: BAH! The child stomps her feet in anger, fuming. Clyde: This is the worst idea you ever had. Bethany: I don't know, dating you ranks up there with universal all time great blunders. Clyde: You're telling me. Bethany: Grr! Bethany wheels back and kicks the dressing room door as hard as she can, but only succeeds in hurting her own foot. Bethany: (Yelping) OW! Holding her toe in pain, the young girl hops up and down. Clyde: That was bright. Bethany: Shut....up....and.....get.....out.....here.....now! Clyde: No. Bethany: Why not? Clyde: Because it is embarrassing. Bethany: I'm sure there's a mirror in that changing room, look in it and honestly tell me if anything can be more embarrassing than looking like you? Clyde: ......point. Bethany: Thank you. Clyde: But I still refuse to come out. Refuse I say~! Bethany: What would it take to get you to come out? Clyde: Me not wearing this thing. Bethany: That is out of the question. Clyde: Why is it out of the question? Bethany: BECAUSE WE NEED THE MONEY FROM THIS STUPID PRODUCT AND IT'LL NEVER GET OFF THE GROUND IF YOU DON'T DO THIS STUPID COMMERCIAL! Clyde: Why else is it out of the question? Bethany: DIE!!!!! Bethany leaps, producing a huge and wicked blade out of nowhere. She attempts to tear the door to pieces with it, but only succeeds in causing sparks to fly off of the metal frame. Bethany: (Stabbing) DIEDIEDIEDIEDIE! Voice: Five minutes Mr. Clyde! Bethany: Do you hear that!? FIVE MINUTES UNTIL WE LOSE THIS DEAL!!!!! Clyde: Oh I am so heartbroken. Woe is me. Bethany: Grrr!!!!! Tapping her foot and holding the knife at her side, Bethany ponders. Clyde: ........ The odd silence causes our hidden demon to pause. Clyde: Bethany? You still out there? Nothing greets the monster's words. Clyde: ......Bethany? Bethany: What if I said I had an idea. Clyde: What sort of idea? Bethany: An idea that would lead to a whole lot of bloodshed and screaming people not feeling very good. Another moment of silence. And then.....the door pops open a bit. Clyde: I'm listening. Five! Four! Three! Two! One! Action! Cheesy Voiceover: Wondering what to get the little ones for the holidays? A little boy appears on screen, demanding and bawling. Cheesy Voiceover: Wondering what to get them when they just won't shut up? A little girl appears, similarly being a brat. Cheesy Voiceover: Why not give them an old familiar face? As a weary mother sits in a lounger in front of her two crying toddlers, her doorbell rings. Mother: I wonder who that could be? Boy: I want a train set! Girl: I want a pony! Boy: I want! Girl: I want! Boy: I want! Girl: I want! Mother: Hang on. The tired woman stands, making her way to the door.....she peers through the hole, then looks on in surprise. Mother: Kids, guess who's here! Girl: Elmo! Boy: Clyde! Mother: No, even better! With a turn of the knob, the mother opens the door. And inside steps Clyde.....wearing what looks like a giant chicken suit with Elmo's head stuck to the top of his own. Clyde: (Smiling in horrible pain) Hi kids! Girl: Wow! Boy: It's..... Mother: That's right kids, it's both! Clyde waddles up, looking on. Voice: (Off stage) Do it!!!!! Clyde: (Mumbling) ......no. Voice: (Off stage) DO IT NOW!!!!! Clyde: ......fine. Muttering and cursing under his breath, Clyde produces a knife, bends his knees and begins to do a dance. Clyde: Clyde'll sacrifice a chicken, Clyde'll sacrifice a duck, STAB STAB STAB STAB! The burned man swings his blade up and down as the children and their mother sway to the music. Clyde: Spill the blood of a goat, spill the blood of a calf, STAB STAB STAB STAB! Doing a somewhat distorted version of the Funky Chicken, Clyde swings his blade again then strikes a pose and forces himself to smile. Cheesy Voiceover: Dancing Chicken Elmo Clyde. In stores now, get yours today! Voice: (Off stage) And......CUT!!!!!! Bethany: (Trotting up) Hehe, now that was something. Clyde: Shut up. Bethany: Oh come on, that wasn't so..... Clyde: Shut up. Grumbling, Clyde begins to stomp away. Right behind him, Bethany follows as the director walks on stage. Director: That was brilliant! We'll sell millions! With money in his eyes, the man holds a small toy in his hand. A miniature image of Dancing Chicken Clyde himself, knife and all. Mother: Will you? Director: Yes indeed, they're already preordered to every kid's home across America! Mother: You're kidding. Director: Well I sent free samples, but once the kids start buying we'll make millions! Mother: That's not the best money making strategy. Director: (Pressing the button on the toy) I don't pay you to think. The gentleman giggles and dances a little as his creation sings. Dancing Chicken Clyde: Clyde'll sacrifice a chicken, Clyde'll sacrifice a duck, STAB STAB STAB! Director: (Dancing) Brilliant! Brilliant! Suddenly the doll stops though. Director: ......eh? Is it out of batteries? Our director shakes the toy, putting his ear to the back of it. Director: But I juat changed themAGH!!!!! Suddenly the small doll springs to life, stabbing the man right in the cheek!!!!! Director: OW!!!! Yelling for help, the man falls to the ground and tries to crawl away but the doll attacks him! He reaches in the direction of the mother actress, only to find her lying on the ground similarly being stabbed by an insane toy. Director: No, no!!!! A bit away the two children lie dead as well.....in horror, the director gazes up at a monitor. Cheesy Voiceover: Dancing Chicken Elmo Clyde. In stores now, get yours today! Cheesy Voiceover: Dancing Chicken Elmo Clyde. In stores now, get yours today! Cheesy Voiceover: Dancing Chicken Elmo Clyde. In stores now, get yours today! The director's own voice echoes in his head as well, as his life fades away. Every kid's home in America...... Every kid's home in America...... Every kid's home in America...... The theme to the Twilight Zone begins, as the dolls continue stabbing and singing, and we mercifully fade to black.......
