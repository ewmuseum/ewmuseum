Awoken in a strange parlor

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Saturday, April 03, 2004 The buzzing sound of the near by alarm clock roared through the crisp atmosphere of the early morning as I was awoken from my slumber in the large sized bed. Bare chest was heaving up and down underneath the soft blankets of the bed, the sheets rubbing softly over my battle hardened flesh. Only thing draped over my body, other than the sheets of the bed of course, were my aviator sunglasses that covered my dark blue eyes. Looking over to my left was the tender body of some woman I had meet on the road to the next arena…back in my home state…Joe Mac Country, were Nature Boy’s and Real American Hero’s are born and bred. She was a journalist for a paper…or that’s the best my slightly intoxicated mind could remember. As a Marine I could hold my liquor, but everyone was their max and I just happened to have crossed mine. “What a night…you go out for something to drink and I end up with a journalist in a bed…her bed?” Looking around the room I could tell that I had no clue to where I was, the only thing I knew for sure was that my clothing was scattered about the room as if a tornado or hurricane of some sort and run through and blew everything to hell. Looking over to my right again I scanned the body of the lovely woman beside me and decided that it wasn’t that bad of night if I ended up with her…I could have been giving the inside scoop to Barbara Walters, that was a thought that would make any man shiver for days on end. Getting up from my place on the bed I walked over to my articles of clothing scattered about on the room and started to pick them up, piece by piece, and started to place them back upon my body, from my combat boots to my United States Marine Corps Leather Biker’s Jacket. Decked out again in my fashionable attire I proceeded to write a note to the fine lady of bare in the bed and stuck it under her pillow as she slept, brushing some hair out of her face before heading towards the door…opening it and closing it with ease. As I headed down stairs I looked out of the windows and looked at the fine morning it had become…the sun had barely let it’s presence start to be known and I was one of the only American’s up at this hour so it was all the more beautiful for me to behold. My combat boots hammered into the stairs as I walked down the to the ground floor, in hopes of getting the hell out of the place and back to were I belonged…the PCW Arena. As I pushed open the door to the hotel I walked out into the night and to the PCW Arena in the distance…reaching into my pocket I pulled out a small handheld voice recorder and turned it on, preparing to offer a speech to the next victim of my wraith. “Since arriving in the PCW Roster I will admit to the fact that I haven’t been, as the Army use to say, “All I can Be.” Instead I have been back on the backburner listening to the rest of the roster bitch and moan over this and that until I can’t take it anymore and I just walk out and wash away all their nagging voices with the sweet taste of beer, or I nice shot of whiskey. But I have decided that it is once again time to pick up the ball and run with it again, it is time for the fans Real American Hero to put all these losers to rest and show them what a true World Champion is like, what a man is suppose to look and act like. None of the other talent can hold a candle to the caliber of warrior I am trained to be. I am The Real American Hero, The Tyrant of Terror, The Nemesis, The Isolator…I am Joe Mac. I am a Former DWF United States and World Champion, Last IWF Hardcore Champion, Last ECFed World Champion, MWA United States Champion, I have a fine record for the short time that I have actually been in the business and my record is ever growing, ever multiplying.” The wing blew hard as I walked along the concrete, looking up the tall buildings and into the swirling skies of dark blue starlight. Clouds formed in different places, getting darker and heavier as more and more moisture thickened them. My camouflage pants rustled against my legs as I walked, my Y-Tank Top brushing against my toned chest as I gradually made my way down the sidewalk. From outside I could here couples screaming at one another, new born babies screaming to be fed, parents waking up to prepare for work. I shook off the feeling that I needed to lay waste to a few of the on coming hobo’s that I saw in the streets, these urges had to be suppressed on a day by day basis, but they were firmly under control at all times. “Soon enough the PCW Locker room will bear witness to the truth, that I am absolutely flawless. My opponent this week doesn’t seem to believe in the hype that I lay forth but that is fine, for he will be the first to be laid to rest as I climb the ladder to my place at the top of the Wrestling Food Chain. Brian Arson will know true pain as I desolate him inside the squared circle and make him tap like the bitch that he is. No one will hear or witness pain in the eyes of another as they will see in his as he screams fro mercy. Turmoil is your gravesite Arson, and I am it’s groundskeeper. You best be training your damn best Arson, because from one man to another, you are surely going to need a lot of it.” Walking down the stretch of street I pressed stop on the recorder and listened as the tape screeched to a stop. Licking my lips to moisten them, stretching my neck and shoulders as I roll them around I walk briskly down the street with my hands in my pockets and my eyes focused on the arena as I walked closer to it. Spitting on the street I smirked to myself and ran my hand over my freshly shaved head, what a week it was going to be…and what a contest I was going to make it be.
