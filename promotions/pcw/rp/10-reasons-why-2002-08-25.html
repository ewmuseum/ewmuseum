10 Reasons Why.......

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th August Over several weeks it has become known that Akiyama and El Diablo don't like each other, this was brought to the surface during two matches mainly, Akiyama vs Chris Chase vs Rent-A-Villain vs El Diablo for the PCW World Heavyweight Championship #1 Contendership and Akiyama vs El Diablo for the PCW Continental Championship #1 Contendership. Of course Akiyama won the former and proceeded to assault El Diablo when the match was done- much to The Mexican Devil's anger! El Diablo won the latter with a brave display to defeat the beast that is Akiyama. But still Akiyama felt the need to dismantle El Diablo. Two #1 Contenders and one battle between two growing enemies and a #1 Contendership up for grabs at Third Dawning. Akiyama is the PCW Title #1 Contender and El Diablo is the PCW Continental Title #1 Contender! Both men are at the top of their games and it all comes down to what will be one final battle. Akiyama vs El Diablo #1 Contender vs #1 Contender God vs Devil The latter is what has deemed to be the concrete hatred each man has towards one another. Akiyama believes he is the God. El Diablo believes he is the Devil. The God is evil and the Devil is good! A battle of biblical mismatches! How can a God be jeered and a Devil cheered? How can the God be full of such pure evil and the Devil full of good? Akiyama has been the hunter and El Diablo the prey, but leading into Third Dawning, neither man will be hunter or pray, neither man will be at an advantage, however both men will be God and Devil respectively, with one thing on their minds only- victory! Not Akiyama's current #1 Contendership. El Diablo despises Akiyama! Akiyama has only toyed with El Diablo, and has destroyed him on more than one occasion just because he wanted to! But El Diablo doesn't know how or when to give up! The God created the 10 commandments, and so The Devil made a list of his own: 10 Reasons Why The Devil Is Better Than The God 1. Hell is always warm Yes, Hell will always be warm while heaven will always be cold physically and metaphorically. 2. Devil needs not follow commandments nor worship others No stupid laws to follow and no religious figures to worship or attempt to be! No Jesus, no Moses, no Abraham, no Noah, no Adam and Eve! Devil looks out for number one and only respects number one! 3. Hidden GOOD is better than hidden EVIL Devil hides his goodness but still has it! God hides his evil, then lashes out with floods, earthquakes, hurricanes! Not very nice at all. 4. Devil's can easily scare and intimidate Why else would he get thrown out of heaven? And Turmoil proved this point to the 'T' The big Akiyama taken off his game by the superior El Diablo aka The Devil. 5. Devil's win fair. God's do not God's cheat to gain certain victories and are somehow worshipped for this whereas Devil's win by fairness yet are made to look like martyrs because of it! All due to stereotypical opinions. 6. Devil's always have more loyal followers than God's It's been proved throughout time that those in atheist occults are more loyal than any religious groups. Just like El Diablo has a loyal fan base yet Akiyama is despised the World over. Go figure! 7. Devil can speak for himself. He needs no lackeys to talk for him unlike God! The Devil, Satan, Lucifer, El Diablo whatever you want to call him, can always speak for himself! He needs no other to do his talking for him, unlike The God who has used Jesus, Moses, the Bible, Cyclonica to name but a few have spoken for him. 8. 666= Fear The symbol of numbers in 666 has always sparked fear into the hearts of most people worldwide! nVg does nothing but make you want to laugh at the insignificance of it. 9. People fear The Devil and not The God! Even mentioning the word Devil can be cause fear in some countries around this globe, and all the word God does is warm your heart! Gods just aren't feared, whereas Devil's are. And Akiyama WILL fear El Diablo! 10. No praise or worship is required by The Devil The Devil does not command praise or worship from anybody, but still gets it, due to the respect that is deserved. Meanwhile The God does command praise and worship, and in PCW that God just doesn't get it, even though he'd thrive off of it. Ten simple reasons why The Devil is better than The God! Ten reasons why El Diablo is better than Akiyama! Ten reasons why El Diablo must defeat Akiyama at Third Dawning! And ten reasons why El Diablo is a future PCW Continental and World Champion! Third Dawning draws nearer and the life long battle of God verses Devil will commence.
