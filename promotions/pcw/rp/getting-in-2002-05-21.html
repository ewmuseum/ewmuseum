Getting in...

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21-05-02 Primetime Championship Wrestling...The biggest company known to man...Its the one place across America that many people attempt to get jobs in... its that one little place that so many people across the World will fly to, just to watch their superstars for a one night only bash. You know, PCW is one of the places where legends are born.. and with the new look roster being announced over the last few days, there is alot of opportunity for the 'newcomers' to prove their value. We've seen quite a few Tag Teams get contracts with Primetime Championship Wrestling...The Dark Messiahs...The Price Brothers...Cowabunga Connection...Three teams alone that will be able to set the standards that so many teams will only dream of being able to follow... however, of the eight Tag Teams that managed to get contracts within PCW, one duo stood out from the rest... one tandem, stood out from all of the competition and in some relation... That team... they pranced around the Tag Team ranks... calling themselves... 'Pretty Cool Guys'... or 'PCG' for short! 'No... not them...''Please... anything but those idiots...''Oh my God... they're coming back to my TV set... aaaaarggggh!' We can sense your fear at having to contend with these 'guys' again... we can sense your anger rise, your hatred increase and your interest drop down to the lowest point since ITV Digital went into liquidation! Why in the world are a team like 'PCG' allowed to even step foot into this place of supreme talent? The answer to that question... nobody really knows anything about it. However, its not the time to wonder about why this team were picked up out of the hundreds of applicants that tried to get into the company... no, no, no... instead, its time to wonder about the proposed contract signing that should go down. Now, its obvious that PCG aren't quite important enough to garner a specialist contract signing on Television... but 'Boo-Hoo'... why worry about the wonders of Television when you can simply project your contract signing right here... on this very spot? 'Hmmm...', you wonder, trying to decide whether you could actually deal with the context of this contract signing after all... Oh well, who cares what any of you really want to think? We don't... so meh to that!... and now, we present you with a tale filled with horror... a tale filled with disgusting events... a tale filled with nothing that has just been mentioned. We here, are just going to get on with the show... regardless of your thoughts... Thank you... and GOOD NIGHT!!! | - ^ - ^ - | Chapter 1 : The Beginning of a PoP era...Installment I : Meeting the Reps... Hello...Hello...and Hello..Welcome to the first installment of what should be the most magnificent journey through time and space... welcome to what should be the most amazing twenty minutes of clusterf*ck trash that has ever been created by the specie known as 'Man'. To be formal, I will introduce myself as Rich Sherwin... Don't forget to announce me Sherwin... ... OK Ryan... I won't forget to announce you on the show... hear that viewers? That was my most esteemed Tag Team partner in the history of the business... Ryan Moore. Are you happy Ryan... you got your little shout out... Tttthannnnk yyyoooouuu! Sometimes I really wonder what the deal is with him... but I guess it shouldn't really matter too much. If we can still bring out the product for the three fans that we have managed to obtain... then the problems aren't anything that we need to worry about too much. Anyway, I think I've spent enough time with the introductions and in all honesty... I'm sure the rest of you would really prefer it if I stuck down to what I'm supposed to and bring upon Installment number.... ONE!!! | - ^ - ^ - | The picture slowly begins to open inside the confinements of an office.... infact, its inside the confinements of a relativly large office. You wouldn't know exactly who this office belonged to or what the general use of this office would have been. The office... eh, oh well... scratch all that and take it from the top again... Erm... the picture opens... yeah, its inside some big assed office... right, thats about all that I've covered at the moment. SO, here we are... opening up inside this office where everything is actually set up in almost the neatest fashion known to man. I was pretty proud walking into a room like this... it brought back memories of what life was like for me in the Mansion of Dreams... where I would walk into my fathers office and see it in the same context as this... oh, those were indeed the days... Regardless of all that mumbo-jumbo about my childhood, the setting that we were currently in looked very nice, packed with everything that a normal man could ask for... and alot more for that matter! As we (meaning Ryan Moore and I... or is it I and Ryan Moore... well, both of us!) walked through the door into the office, everything seemed to suddenly light up... at first, this brought back memories of when we were in the PoP business 24/7, performing at all of those concerts around the world. I looked ahead of me... directly ahead from the door and stared at the magnificent structure that was a solid oak table. The shine coming from its body, informing both myself and my fellow singer that polish had been applied recently (Well, for all I really know, it could be polish that was applied days ago... but saying that it had been 'applied recently'... that kinda gives me the intellectual advantage that I've always wanted to attain.). Upon this table were a few items.. nothing that I could really consider to be of any specific value to me... but they were items that are required for the types of work that leave you stranded in an office from 9am until 5pm. Oh the casualness of such hours... I would have to kill myself if I was forced to work a day job like that... after all, I am indeed the singer that everybody wants... and the PCW Superstar that will get even more bookings with such an intellectual name... 'Joy' is all I can say to that! Despite this feeling, we both looked at the table and peered around at the items with an amazing amount of interest... although, the thing in the room that caught my interest the most was a very large leather chair positioned slightly infront of the table. At this very moment in time, I wasn't able to determine if this seat was filled with anything... the main reason for this lack of viewing was all down to the basis that the bloody thing was turned around... turned around so that I could only see the back of it. This was something of the moment that Ryan and myself were kinda beginning to dread... why you wonder? Well, it isn't hard to pay attention to the movies every so often is it... when two guys are walking through an office building, looking to sign a few contracts that will make them official members of the Primetime Championship Wrestling roster... and suddenly, BOOM... they come into an office and pull around a big black chair... to find the body of the man that should have presented them with their esteemed contracts! My heart began to race as we walked deeper into the office... single step... by single step... by, well, you should have been able to get the picture from the second mention. 'Welcome.... welcome... welcome to my... OFFICE...' The voice came from the chair... Ryan and myself stopped in our tracks... almost paralysed with fear at the feat that came along with talking chairs! I had only been able to read about such a marvel in my monthly issue of 'Freaky Talking Chairs Volume VI'... and fortunently, I had a cunning eye for situations that involve these freaky creations of damnation... I shrugged Ryan Moore off my shoulder and walked forward a few paces... confidence begining to ooze from my sides... I felt like a superstar, I felt like one of those characters from that kid show 'Dragonball Z'... they had their power coming off their body in bright lights... while I had to make do with the comfort of invisable bright lights of confidence. I smirked as I got the chair in my sights... this was going to become the damnest scene of pain ever... it was humanoid against chairoid... it was the body and might of man against the leather and feather stuffing of a chair! 'Ha, Ha, Ha...', I started... chuckling as the chair came into my view fully. I looked around the room briefly before returning my cocky glare to the item in question and continuning with the statement that I was about to make, '... oh Mr Chair... how you look so alone there. Well, since you are alone and everything... why don't we just have a little battle eh? Why don't I bring in my favorite little item and totally make your leather coating horrible and diseased looking? Would you like that?' There was a significant period of silence between both myself and what I had assumed to be a 'Mutant Chair'... after all, how many other chairs can talk to you... and even welcome you to their personal domain? The last time I checked 'Freaky Talking Chairs Volume VI'... the last case of a talking chair had been found in the outskirts of Romania! However, after this essential period of silence between the two of us... the chair began to reply.... 'What on earth are you talking about... I'm not a chair!', the voice bellowed... this was starting to turn into one of the more unfortunate cases located within 'Freaky Talking Chairs Volume VI'. To my knowledge, this kind of horrible case was also known as 'Inability to realize the truth'... a horrible case where the chair in question has come to the point in life where they are unable to determine... if they are a member of the chairpeople race... or if they really are one of us humans. My gut was telling me not to believe a word that this chair was spluttering on about... but my 'Chair Killer' instinct was informing me... that I shouldn't even listen to the gut... that I should just eliminate it and pretend I heard nothing. 'Do you have any idea of who you are actually messing with?!', I asked in the crazed voice tone that really would only be assosciated with the people that need mental help. At the time, I wasn't too sure if this was the tone that I should be taking with a chair... after all, I also read in 'Freaky Talking Chairs Volume VI' that they had magical eating powers... turning people into cookies before eating them for tea... really, the fate for all humans who would try and take on the 'Leather Chairs of Doom' and lose was something far worse than death itself. Moments after dishing out such a warning to the chair of doom, I decided that it would be in my best interests to continue on the verbal path, '... Exactly... your silence leads me to believe that you had no clue I was the number two 'Chair Finder and Killer' in 'Freaky Talking Chairs Volume VI'... did you?... DID YOU?!' There was another silence between both human creation and leather chair... the tension was starting to amass and the intensity of the battle could be seen upon my face. I'd love to be able to have said the same for the chair... but they don't have faces and therefore, I can't... just a useless bit of information there for all you people who think you'll find Killer Chairs with faces. Regardless of this small detail, the silence remained thicker than anything... but moments later, Ryan Moore walked up behind me and tapped my shoulder a few times. 'What?', I whispered, making sure this damnation didn't hear my voice... or the probable plan that was going to come from the brain of my trusted ally. Ryan Moore stepped another pace forward and began to whisper his message to me... 'Psst... how do we know this is even a chair... its not talking in that croaky tone that 'Freaky Talking Chairs Volume VI' says all chairs will have....' I stopped for a few moments and began to think about this possibility very carefully. Could it be at all possible that the thing I am going to start a war with... is really a human and not a pathetic little leather chair? Could it be possible.... HELL NO! Damn... 'How could it not be possible?' is all that passed through my mind at the time in question. Immediatly, I turned to Ryan Moore and clipped him around the back of the head with an open hand. He let out a small shril, indicating that the pain was sharp. 'How dare you even come close to insulting my extreme intelligence... I'm a God Damned intellectual and intellectual people are always right!' I barked out, ready and willing to make the point sound harsh so that I would never be bothered by his idiotic claims again. And then... the moment finally took place... the moment that everyone around the world had been waiting for... it happened right infront of our very eyes. The screeching of the chair as it slowly became to turn into view... Ryan Moore and I were drawn to its unmissable pitch... we were captivated by its horror... Who or what is this chair?Why did they really claim to not be a 'Killer Chair'?When will our PoP Icon buckos be able to sign the pieces of paper that will officially grant them entry into Primetime Championship Wrestling? All the answers to these questions and more... in the next episode of 'Pretty... Cool... Guys!'
