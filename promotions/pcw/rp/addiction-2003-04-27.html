Addiction?

27 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-27-03 The scene opens to Adam sitting in a small room on theedge of a bed. Water can be heard running in thebackground. The room is dark except for the glare fromthe TV and one small lamp in the back corner of theroom. Adam is holding a remote in his hand, flippingthrough the channels. “Christ, not only is Mobile one of the most boringplaces in America,” he starts dropping the remote tothe bed, “There’s not even anything decent on TV.” He stands up and stumbles forward. Adam pulls hishands up to his head and rubs his temples. “Damn...” Adam says through his teeth. He staggers forward and props himself up on the smalldresser next to the TV. Adam works his way down thedresser. He picks up a small paper cup with Marriotwritten across it. “Shit...this is bad...” he says dropping some ice intothe cup. He looks back to the closed door, the water can stillbe heard running. Adam turns around and heads for asmall fridge. He pulls it open and produces a smallbottle of water. Taking the bottle of water and thecup, he closes the fridge and places them both on topof the fridge. He turns and heads back across the darkroom. He comes to a bag and opens it. “Come on...come on...where is it?” Adam says pullingsome clothes from the bag. “Ah!” Adam removes a smaller black bag and takes it backover to the fridge. He takes the water and the cup inhand and takes a seat in one of the chairs in thecorner. After putting the cup and the water down on asmall table in front of him, he opens the bag andpulls out a couple of smaller bottles. “Vicodine? Percocet? or Codeine?” Adam says sittingthree bottles in front of him. “Eh I guess it doesn’treally matter since they all pretty much do the samething.” Adam picks up the middle bottle and looks it over.Percocet is written in black letters across themiddle. Adam Dyas appears underneath it. He pops thetop off and drops a couple into his hand. He looksthem over but returns them to the bottle. “These things are way too strong for me to be takingnow.” Adam says looking to the still closed door,“Besides Jaslene wants to still go out. There is noway I’d be able to go anywhere if I popped a couple ofthese tonight.” He places the bottle back to the table. “But Christ I have to have something. Rust’s DDTkilled my head.” Adam says rubbing his temples again,“I can’t take this, I need something.” Adam picks up the bottle marked Vicodine. He pops thetop on it and drops a five of the small pills into hishand. With his free hand, he pours the water into thecup. “Well he goes nothing,” Adam thinks to himself poppingthe pills into his mouth. He’s quick to follow that up with the water. Adamswallows the pills in one big gulp and takes anothercup of water. He crumbles the cup up in his hand andtosses it into a small garbage can sitting at the endof the dresser. The water turns off. “Shit!” Adam says to himself hearing the water stop.“I have to get this stuff put away before she seesit.” Adam quickly puts the bottles back into the black bag.He zips it closed and crosses the room. Kneeling downin front of his suitcase, he jams the smaller bag backinto it. He picks the clothes up off of the floor andstuffs them back into the suitcase as well. He closesthe zipper just as the door opens. Jaslene steps outwith a towel wrapped around her head wearing abathrobe. She sees him kneeling down in front of hissuitcase. “What are you doing?” she asks rubbing her head,drying her hair with the towel. Adam looks back to her, “Uh...nothing...” He just kind of trails off. Jaslene raises her browand just shrugs her shoulder. “So what were you saying to me before I got into theshower?” she asks taking a seat on the edge of thebed. Adam plays dumb, “I don’t know what you are talkingabout.” “You were saying something about your head hurting?”Jaslene says picking up the TV remote, “And how Rustreally rocked you with a DDT.” “OH! Uh...that...” Adam stutters for a bit, “I wasjust saying that Rust got me good for a jobber. Iwasn’t sure if I was up for my match this Tuesdayagainst Smoke but I’m feeling better now.” “Are you sure about that Adam?” Jaslene asks flippingthrough the channels, “You did take a hard shot.” Adam takes a seat next to her. “Nah I’m fine now.” He says with a smile. “Are you sure about that? You seemed out of it on theway over here.” Jaslene says turning the TV off, “Godthere is nothing on. It’s all a bunch of crap.” “Really Jas I’m fine, it’s cute that you worry so muchbut I’m going to be all right.” He says putting hisarm around her. Jaslene chuckles and nods. They touch their headstogether. “So where do you want to go tonight?” Adam says, “Ithink the nicest place they have for miles is that KFCwe passed on the way here.” “Adam!” Jaslene exclaims. “What?” It’s the truth!” Adam defends himself. “That’s not nice.” Jaslene says in a condemning toneof voice. Adam stands up and stretches his arms out over hishead. He yawns as he brings his arms back down. Adamturns and walks to the door. “I just need to take a leak and then we can gosomewhere.” Adam says turning the door knob. Jaslene stands up, “Uh...hello?” She points to herselfmoving her hands up and down. “Not dressed yet!” Adam stops, “Well best be changing that and soonbabe.” Adam is heard laughing as he closes the door behindhim. Jaslene is quick to move, just as the door closesshe makes a move toward his bag. She kneels down infront of it and unzips it. “What are you hiding from me?” Jaslene says under herbreath. She manages to pull out the small black bag out of thelarger suitcase. “What the...” Jaslene bites her lip as she unzips thebag. One of the bottles falls out and this the ground. Shepicks it up and reads it. “Adam Dyas...Take Two a day asinstructed...Percocet?!?!” Jaslene really bites downon her lip. Hearing the toilet flush, Jaslene scrambles to put thebottle back into the bag. The faucet turns on as shestuffs the smaller black bag back into the suitcase.She zips it up and stands up. Just as the door opensshe moves to the coat rack area and pulls a suit bagfrom it. “You’re still not dressed?” Adam says in a halfsarcastic, half serious tone. Jaslene just sticks her tongue out at him as she dropsthe bag down onto the bed. She unzips it and pulls acouple of different outfits from the bag. “Unlike you slovenly men,” she says picking up one ofthe outfits and looking it over, “Women can’t be readyto go in a matter of minutes.” “Sure you can!” Adam responds, “See? That’s a niceoutfit in your hand there. Put it on and we can go getsomething to eat.” Jaslene frowns dropping it back to the bed, “I don’twant to look ‘nice’ I want to look cute.” Adam groans to himself. “You might as well take a seat and get comfortable.This could take some time.” Jaslene says with a smile. Adam trudges back across the room and takes a seat inone of the small chairs behind the table. Jaslenetosses him the TV remote. “Here entertain yourself.” she says with a chuckle. Adam turns the TV on and puts it onto ESPN. Jaslenelooks to him as he concentrates on the TV. ”I wonder if he has a problem? I’ve never noticedanything before but it would help to explain a lot. Imean the work he did in the DWO was done withoutskipping a beat. I need to get some more info before Istart to jump to conclusions.” Jaslene thinks toherself as she looks through her choices. Adam sits back in the seat and closes his eyes.Jaslene takes notice. “How do you feel about your match with Smoke?” sheasks. “I feel that it has been done to death but then againI’m not a booker so what I think has no bearing onit.” Adam says sarcastically. “That’s not what I meant and you know it Adam.” shefires back. He opens an eye and looks at her. She scowls back athim. “Sorry Jas...I feel good, I mean look what Smoke hadto resort to on Fusion. He’s a sorry man with a sorrycareer.” Adam says with a half cocked smile. “Yeah but he is a serious competitor. What ifsomething happens? I mean you are in line for anExtreme Title shot right?” Jaslene inquires. “Oh man I don’t even want to think about that rightnow.” Adam says leaning his head back, “The ExtremeTitle is the last thing on my mind right now. I justwant to focus on my match with Smoke on Tuesday andthat’s it. If something happens then I’ll just bounceback like I always do.” “You’re probably right,” Jaslene says picking upanother outfit, “I’m probably making a big deal out ofnothing.” “It’s all right Jas, at least I know you care.” Adamsays with a chuckle. The scene fades out as Jaslene removes the towel fromher head and starts to undo the knot on her bathrobe.
