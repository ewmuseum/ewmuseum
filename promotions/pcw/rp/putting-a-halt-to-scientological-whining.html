Putting a Halt to Scientological Whining

Title: Putting a Halt to Scientological Whining Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: 29th Morning. After a night of what we presume to be passion between Reginald V. Lovecraft and Miss Katherine Willis, we see the two sitting up in bed, holding hands. Little do they suspect the camera hiding in a room corner, or that Mr. Lovecraft's tag partner, William S. Powell, is hiding in their closet. Reginald has, in between training, seen a great variety of promos from a great number of athletes on the subject of this match. He notices the fire in Miyagawa's eyes....a legendary PCW superstar who, despite all his success, never became the Kingpin of Extreme. Needless to say, Reginald relishes the opportunity to go head to head with such a man. However, he notices one man amongst them is quite bitter towards Lovecraft. That man? Ultimate Authority member Xavier Kannon. Mr. Kannon seems upset that Lovecraft did not aid him in his battle against the Malice Crew and Phantom Phive. One must remember that Reginald V. Lovecraft is a politician. As such, he will not risk his neck anymore than is necesary. But, if Xavier Kanon is feeling a tad frisky....Reginald Lovecraft is more than willing to go one on one. The Governor fears no man. If the former commissioner and his preppy bitch want a battle, he will be ready to oblige. For, when push comes to shove, it will be KANNON that is put out...of....commission. ::The imperious Reginald V. Lovecraft sits atop the bed in the Emperor's Suite of Caesar's Palace. Next to him is Katherine Willis, loyal companion, and now presumed sexual partner. The pair seem to be staring off into space, although, for someone who just lost his virginity, there is a violent glint of anger in Lovecraft's eyes.:: Katherine Willis: What's wrong, Reggie? I thought last night went wonderfully! ::Reginald tries to smile, but finds himself unable to.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: It's not your fault...it's....it's... Katherine Willis: It's who? Reginald V. Lovecraft: It's that jackass, Xavier Kannon, and his prep bitch Eleanor Hall. Katherine Willis: But, aren't you partners with them in the Ultimate Authority? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Maybe so...but, I cannot stand their attitudes anymore..at times, I cannot determine which one of them is more spoiled and brattish, Eleanor, or Xavier. ::Lovecraft's anger is evident...he likes to consider himself a team player, and yet...he despises working with those who do not work for their own place. Such is Eleanor Hall and Xavier Kannon...two people who achieved positions of power solely based on Tyler Nelson, money, and power:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: At the very least...Powell and I achieved our PCW Tag Team Championships on our own. We defeated five of PCWs greatest tag teams to win that distinction. And, we did it fairly, on our own. You, Xavier Kannon, needed the help of the Snapple promoting prep who belongs on an episode of Clueless and a set of handcuffs to defeat Jeremy Howard for the Continental title.You're a fucking parasite, Kannon...who leeches off others in order to get what he needs. Katherine WIllis: Why are you saying this? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Because, I'm sick and tired of this shit! Nelson and Kannon were probably the guys who fired Walden, too! Typical shit from those two. Nelson and Kannon have a symbiotic relationship. Kannon sucks Tyler Nelson's ass, and Nelson provides Christian Xavier Kannon with the power and prestige that he craves...he surrounds himself with people that feed his ego. Hell, he refers to Eleanor Hall as his 'poon puppy'. To Xavier, the leading woman in his life is nothing more than a sexual object. ::He gently places an arm around Katherine, who snuggles up closer against him.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: In contrast, Katherine here is my companion...my employee...my best friend..and my lover. She is everything to me..much more than the little Scientological fuck toy that is Eleanor Hall! ::Katherine smiles at him. The Governor smiles back, before continuing his diatribe against PCW's only Scientologist.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: You are a cancer in PCW, Xavier...flaunting your religion everywhere you go...creating this Hubbardville place, a figment of your own imagination. I have nothing against your religion, hell, when I worked in Washington D.C., I passed the First Church of Scientology every day on my way to work, but flaunting it as much as you do is ridiculous! You seem to fancy yourself the Messiah of your religion...in that case Kannon, when we meet this Friday, I shall force the crown of thorns... ::He pauses, realizing he will be battling in a school.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft:..the.....the.....I SHALL FORCE THE TIMEOUT CHAIR UPON YOUR HEAD, KANNON! And then, as you sit in that school, looking at the wall, crucifying yourself to a wall of concrete...you will know in your heart that The Governor is, and will be, the only man in the Ultimate Authority worthy of the title 'Extreme Champion.' ::He smiles again, clearly getting things off his chest he has wanted to say for a great while.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: I have HAD IT with your whining and complaining Xavier! Come Friday, THERE WILL BE NO EXCUSES FOR YOU. YOU WILL NOT BE ABLE TO TAKE ON THE POLITICAL POWER OF REGINALD V. LOVECRAFT! For, you shall be introduced to my strongest belief, Campaign Finance Reform. And, when that happens, your dreams of Extreme Title glory WILL BE VETOED! Fade to...
