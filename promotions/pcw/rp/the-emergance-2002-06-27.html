The Emergance

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 27th Which came first, the chicken or the egg? Is the glass half empty or half full? If a tree falls in the woods and nobody is around to hear it, does it make a sound? Are you dead or not living? The questions of a person who knows not when to stop asking. The answers; The egg came second, the chicken was created as man was. The glass is half empty because you are drunk and you can not see the bright side of things. The tree makes no sound because you didn't hear it. You simply aren't living because when you are dead, you're dead, not coming back. Is life fair? How can life be fair, it's not a game nor a contest. Life is life and you can not win, but you can succeed. In my mind I have succeeded, but on paper, I have not. In his mind, the neighbourhood hobo has succeeded because he did not do anything against his will to reach rock bottom or the top. To see one's future is to cheat oneself. If you see that you will win the lotto, does that mean that you had seen the future which let you win, or by seeing you won make you lose by altering the future? I saw my future and altered it. My future was golden and now I have to settle for less, revenge. Revenge tastes sweet. Revenge is a woman in a red dress. Revenge is the devil. Revenge is the pinnicle of hate. I have finally reached this point. Losing my mind in such fashion could do me well. I had it, infront of me. I had one man left to beat but I was out before it began. Now I have learned to live by the moment. No more fortune telling. No more using smoke and mirrors. It's time to change. Enough is enough and it's time for a change. I need to become a winner. I need to be a winner. I need to be the first winning Frampton. Time to be cutthroat. To be a winner. Time to go against all that I have tried to represent. Time for revenge. Time to keep people down low and let nobody rise up. It's time to go corporate. It's time to take no shit and time to become an agressive individual. It's time I lose my image. It's time to be what I always have been. A loose cannon. All these years I have been holding back. All these years I have been a main eventer trapped in a mid carder's body. It's time I evolve. It's time I leave my cacoon for the outside world. I have earned my wings. I have become my ultimate form. I don't believe that anybody feels the way I do. How can you feel like I do? Have you ever taken a moment to ask how the hell Frampton is today? Have you ever walked a mile in my shoes? No! You people resent me and now you will be punished. Punishment is coming. So many names that I have answered to. P2K, Mikey, Punisher, Punisher 2000, Frampton, Mike Frampton, the list goes on and on. Soon you will meet the one name I will only answer to. He's coming. He's coming to reach into your chest and remove your heart. The future is here. Jump aboard or be crushed by the iron hoarse that is being powered up. I don't know if I care that you think life's not fair. Do you realize that I refuse to allow my emotions to compromise my actions. I feel one emotion, anger. To finish this I will offer my final plea. Accept who I am or else face the consequences, the unleashing will bring terror upon anyone who crosses my path. *Monologue* Enjoy your last moments Jack. You may have screwed me but time is a wasting and the clock is a ticking. Frampton is dying and a new man is emerging. You should have finished the job, now you will be faced with the end result of your actions.
