Krayzie's laid up in a hospital? Can he be revived?

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-1-02 Now, Xavier Kannon has gone too far. On Sabotage, Kannon set the trap and Krayzie fell for it. When it was all said and done, The Krazed Lunatic found himself laying a pool of his own blood, having been thrown straight through the casing to a Snapple machine at the hands of PCW's very own Scientologist, Xavier Kannon. One week before their epic showdown at Spring Fling and the Gold Patron Meritorious has crossed the line. If this were chess..... wrong move! If Jeremy Howard can shake his head clear before Turmoil, Xavier Kannon's hell might start as soon as Tuesday. Can the Krayzie One recover from Sabotage's attack? To find out, the scene fades into a hospital room. A nurse, in their customary white clothing, standing over the body of the Krazed Lunatic, who lays in the bed, his head bandaged. Standing on the other side of the bed, stands Jeremy's girlfriend and the love of his life, Jessica, in tears. Jessica: Is he going to be alright? The nurse has a smug look on her face, like she's unsure. The Nurse: He took quite a hit to the head and he lost a significant amount of blood. Jessica: How could Kannon do this?!? Jessica bends forward, laying her head on Jeremy's chest. In the background, the sound of a heart monitor beats loudly. There's no telling if this is what Kannon had intended to do when he put the Krayzie One through the Snapple machine, but this is the severity of what happened. A man, a former two time World Champion and current Continental Champion lays in a hospital bed, unconscious. Jessica: I knew I should have been there. Then that evil bitch would have gotten hers instead of my boyfriend. The Nurse: Ma'am, I'm going to have to ask you to step away from the patient. Jessica leans her head up, glaring at the nurse in disgust. Jessica: What the hell?!? This man is my boyfriend. He has a Continental title match in six days, he's unconscious and you want me to just step away from him like nothing's wrong? The nurse, elderly in age, knods her head, 'yes'. The Nurse: If you touch him the wrong way, you could seriously hurt him worse. Jeremy: muttering Kan..... non. He doesn't make so much as one move with his head and his eyes are still closed completely shut. Jessica and the nurse turn toward Jeremy, staring at him. Jessica, hoping for the best, grabs Jeremy's hand out of love. The nurse checks Jeremy's vitals and such. Jessica: Jeremy, are you there honey? Jeremy doesn't make so much as one movement. The Nurse: Ma'am, I'm really going to need you to step back. Jessica: And I'm really going to need you to shut the hell up! The nurse is caught by surprised, her eyes widening. Jessica: This is my boyfriend. My love is his strength. I'll stand here as long as I wish! Jeremy: muttering Kan..... non. The nurse and Jessica, again, turn toward Jeremy to check for any signs of improvement in his unconscious state. Even with Jessica's hopes, nothing. Jessica: See what I told you! Now get out of my face! The Nurse: Fine. Suit yourself. Disgruntled, the nurse storms out of the Krayzie One's room much to Jessica's pleasure. As she hears the door slam shut, Jessica puts her head back on Jeremy's chest, pleading to him to wake up. Jessica: Come on, honey. Wake up. The fans need you. Jessica lifts her head up, staring directly into the face of Jeremy. Jessica: The fans need you... Justin needs you... Troy needs you... I need you... Jeremy: muttering Kan..... non. Jessica: Yes! Kannon needs you to kick his ass at Spring Fling. You just have to wake up honey. Suddenly, the hand that Jessica is holding firmly makes a sudden jerk. Jessica looks down at Jeremy's hand, then back toward his face. Off and on, the hand jerks at given times. Yet, there is still no change in his facial expession. The Krazed Lunatic is still out cold from going through the Snapple machine. Jessica: Honey, you just have to wake up! You have to fight the Chef on Turmoil. If you don't show up, they'll strip you of your title and your chance to fight Kannon. Jeremy's head moves from one side, then to the other. His eyes continue to stay closed, but the sight of movement in Jeremy's head is enough to excite the love of Jeremy Howard's life. Jeremy: not muttering, but faint talking Kan..... non. Jessica: Thats it! You're doing it! Wake up so you can shove Chef and his Islam preaching back to Afghanistan where all the other assholes of this world reside! Jeremy: talking slowly Kan.... non. Jessica watches Jeremy's head continue to move back and forth. That is, until the Krazed Lunatic lifts his right arm into the air, clenching his fist to Jessica's amazement. Jessica: Come on Jeremy! Wake up! You're almost there! Jessica leans forward, planting a kiss on his lips. Jeremy's eyes open. Jessica: Jeremy! You're alive! Jeremy: Where's..... Kan..... non? Jessica smiles, eager to answer Jeremy's question now that he's awoken from his unconsious state. Jessica: I don't know exactly where he is right now, but he'll be at Turmoil fighting Brock Newbludd. Jeremy: And.... I have..... a Continental..... title match..... with..... Chef? Jessica knods her head, 'yes'. Jeremy: Have the nurses get my sign out sheet. I'm signing myself out of here. Jeremy goes to raise up from the hospital bed like a little kid who just had a nightmare, bolting for mommy and daddy's room. Bad idea. Jeremy falls right to a knee, unable to coordinate himself. Jessica: Whoa. Slow down honey. You took a hell of a hit to the head. Take things slowly. Jeremy: I don't take anything slowly. Jessica: This might be one time to change that. Jessica, with what little muscles her petite frame can manage, helps Jeremy back to a sitting position on the bed. Jeremy just sits there, glaring out of the hospital window at downtown San Jose, California, the site of Sunday's Sabotage. Jeremy: Alright, I'll take a rest today. But tomorrow, I'm going after Kannon! Jessica shakes her head, agreeing with Jeremy. Jessica: Just lay down for now until the nurse checks you out. Jeremy: I don't need to lay down. I need to get ready for Turmoil. Jessica, sort of confused in the face as to what to think about her man, just knods her head. Only a few minutes ago, this man was laying on his back in a hospital, unconscious. Now, he's sitting up, plotting his revenge on Xavier Kannon for Tuesday Turmoil. Is Kannon ready for the hell that's about to be unleashed? Hopefully, Scientology has a back up plan as the scene fades away to Jeremy, once again, trying to get back to his feet to stand.... to black!
