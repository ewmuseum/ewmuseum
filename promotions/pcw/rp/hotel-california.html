Date: Previously Recorded: April 15, 2003 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up within what seems to be a hotel corridor. With rooms lined up on every side of the wall. The hotel seems to be one of the high dollar hotels that the Primetime Championship Wrestling powers at be pay for, so that their wrestlers may be able to stay somewhere with comfort on their mind, before and after their matches that they participate in. With this being the week of a Pay-Per-View however, and being that this weeks Turmoil and the Pay-Per-View are being held in the same city, it’s a lot easier for the wrestlers, as they can stay in the same hotel room and not worry about leaving the entire week until Sunday night after the Pay-Per-View to head to the city where the customary Wednesday night Turmoil will be held, wherever it may be. The scene turns for you to view the room number “765”. The door to the room opens and the scene steps into the room through the door. On the floor you see several pieces of clothing thrown astray from the bag that they were once in. The room itself is huge however. With several parts of it, it seems to be that of a rich man’s suite then any other normal hotel room. It has in the first room that you reach a small refrigerator that must contain several food items and maybe some alcohol products as well. Beside it there is a full-length food bar complete with salads and other types of foods, this bar is restocked every day when house keeping comes in and cleans up the room. Across from the refrigerator, you see a complete surround set up with a big screen TV complete with your DVD fixings and other home entertainment wants, the TV is currently off however as it seems no one has been watching it. In front of the TV is an expensive leather sofa and recliner, something that any man needs to relax after a hard days work. The camera scene turns and you walk into the next room of the huge hotel and you find that this is the bedroom itself. Complete with a huge king sized bed and it’s on smaller but still fairly beg color TV. The room is huge however, and there is a window in this room that allows you to look out upon the city, with the room number being “765” that must mean that the room that is currently being shown is about 7 stories in the air, not a lot, but certainly enough to get somewhat a view, since 70 feet can sometimes be considered a long way up or a long way down, whichever way you look at it. The room is then complete with a bureau to keep your clothes in, which obviously this person is not making any use of. And then you see a walk in closet. There is another door across the room however which is open and you hear the sound of a shower curtain opening. The door is open and the view you see in there is that of a mirror looking right back at you. It’s obvious that the room is the vast bathroom that completes the entire room. Smoke steps around the corner with a towel on and it looks as if he’s just stepped out of the shower. Smoke walks over to the bed and sits down, as soon as he sits down, the phone beside the bed rings. Smoke picks up the phone after the first ring and answers… Semore Smoke: Hello? {{{{Smoke sits there for a few seconds as someone speaks to him…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yes, it’s me. Thanks for calling man, oh, and thanks for the nice room. I couldn’t have upgraded myself any better, most everyone else in PCW is down a couple of floors in the cheap rooms, but I got a room that is just like Ellie’s and Jeremy Howard’s. Only a person with the money that I have could afford a room like this. Uh huh… {{{{Smoke reaches over and grabs a remote for the TV that is sitting on his bed and he turns on the TV and then changes the channel to the news…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yeah I’m watching it now. So, they’ve busted everyone but Georgie and Fankie eh? Ok, they’ll head to the other place then, yeah, the place where I can always call home. They should be there in a day or two, so I’ll call them then. What about the London thing, did you get in touch with the family? {{{{Smoke listens to the man on the other line…}}}} Semore Smoke: All right, well, I’ll bail all of you guys out then when I get the time and money to do so, you could be in that federal jail for at least a week. All right, well, to be sure this isn’t traceable, I’ll have to get off right now, call me again later on tonight if you’re allowed to… you’re not… ok… then get one of the other boys to use up their phone call then. I need to keep updated on all ya’lls situation later my man. {{{{Smoke hangs up the phone and turns to look at the news on the TV. The reporter is reporting the cut down on the organized crime wave that has suddenly been hitting the east coast of the United States. The reporter is speaking of Smoke’s family though, which was busted as soon as he left Atlantic City just the other day…}}}} Reporter: The latest crime family to fall victim to the new FBI policies was the Bordon family of Atlantic City, New Jersey. The Bordon’s boss, John Bordon was murdered just months ago by an unknown killer, something that police have been looking into since the murder. But the new crime boss is a virtual unknown to the crime industry and is currently still unknown to the FBI. The FBI is now interrogating all family members that they were able to catch in their Sunday raid of the Bordon headquarters. We’ll keep everyone posted on this though if anything comes out on who the boss is and if he is among those who were captured or if he was one of the three who escaped capture. {{{{Smoke turns the TV off and stands up and walks over to the other room where he picks up a pair of pants and a shirt from the floor. The scene zooms in on his face as he smirks…}}}} Semore Smoke: You can search all you want you won’t find me… Ha! I’ve got too many places to hide. {{{{The scene fades on the smirking face of Semore Smoke…}}}}
