A powerful start to the valet search

17 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th September 2002 Jim Surge and Ben Nighthorse Campbell sit in front of a large table set up in the large Main Barn where the Cochise County Fair hold it’s annual best hog and sheep competitions. Jim looks warily at his half-brother and can sense that Ben cannot shake that “I’m completely out of place” feeling. Jim’s older brother Virgil, an older leather-skinned cowboy with a thick salt and pepper moustache, takes every opportunity he can to indirectly insult Ben by complaining to Jim about “their father” and how he still can’t believe that he would ever sleep with a Native American woman. What is supposed to be a light-hearted affair and the beginning of Redemption Song’s contest to find a new valet has turned into a stomach-knot filled morning with a lot of tension flying around the barn. Jim: So Virg, how many people do you have here for us to look at? Virgil: Well, we only got one on such short notice. Plus don’t ya’ll have to catch a plane back to Wisconsin for Turmoil? Jim: I suppose you’re right. Ben, what do you think? Wanna get this thing done? Ben: I guess. Ben raps his fingers lightly on the table in front of him and pours himself a glass of water. Ben: Do you really think we needed to set up the whole barn just for one chick? Virgil: Chick?! You two didn’t say anything about it having to be a woman! Ben: Valet!!! Valet!!!! What part of that don’t you get? I thought you were an ex-wrestler! Virgil takes a deep breath and slowly exhales to keep him from saying anything nasty in return. Feeling as uncomfortable as ever, Ben tries to keep it in mind not to really say anything that might be considered a verbal attack against Virgil. Ben manages a half-smile back at Virgil and then lowers his head. Jim shakes his own head in disbelief and pours himself a tall glass of cold water. Jim: Yeah Virg, really, a female valet is what we were going for with this contest but if you’ve only got a guy, I think we can just pass and say thanks for all the trouble but that’s not really what we’re looking for. Virgil: Hell boys, its 2002! Virgil leans back in his chair and smiles widely before lighting up a cigarette. Ben immediately winces at the smell of cigarette smoke. Virgil: What’s the matter boy? I thought all you injuns enjoyed a fat peacepipe every now and again. Ben: I’d appreciate it if you didn’t call me ‘boy.’ Besides we normally use sweetgrass in the pipe and not tobacco. Virgil: Sweetgrass?! Hell, and you’re worried about having a male valet? Jim: Okay, okay, can we just get on with it. Who do you have for us? Virgil brings his chair back down and gets up from his seat and smiles widely. After a long drag on the cigarette, the cowboy raises his voice to a booming level… Virgil: Don’t ask me how he got out here so quickly, but here he is, the hottest free agent in wrestling today, MR. POWERFUL! Mr. Powerful walks into the barn confidently in a grey suit and waves to Virgil, Ben and Jim. He keeps walking forward to the table and extends his hand to shake hands with the half-brothers. Jim rises quickly and points to the center of the barn where they have set up a small platform. Jim: Ahh… that’s okay Mr. Powerful, you can just stay over there. Mr. Powerful: If that is your wish, good citizen. Mr. Powerful gets up onto the platform and Virgil grabs his camera from the table and takes a few photos of Mr. Powerful - two side profiles, one from behind and one front-on. Jim sits back down in his seat and Ben drops his head into his hands in shame. Jim: Okay, Mr. Powerful… Mr. Powerful (interrupting): Please, just call me Mr. P. That’s what my friends call me. Jim: Okay, Mr. P., we just have a few questions for you. Won’t take but a minute. Ben (whispering to Jim): I guess we’re going to skip the swimwear portion of the contest with him? Jim (laughing): Shhhh!! Okay then, Mr. P., if you were Redemption Song’s valet, would you be willing to do whatever it takes to help us win a match. Mr. P.: Of course Mr. Surge. I would do anything in my power to help you win, except break the rules of fair play and honest sport. Jim looks at Ben who starts shaking his head. Jim: Well, by ‘doing whatever it takes’ I was implying that sometimes you need to bend the rules in order to achieve a favorable result. Mr. P.: I do not believe in that philosophy at all. Favorable results should be earned. Ben: Mr. P., do you know what purpose a valet serves in professional wrestling? Mr. P.: In my experiences, a valet is usually a beautiful, intelligent woman who is commonly used as a distraction for her wrestler or wrestlers. Jim: Do you know that you are currently applying to be a valet? Mr. P.: Yes. Yes I do. Jim and Ben look at each other and then over at Virgil, who is nearly on the floor laughing! Jim shakes his head yet again but valiantly decides to continue. Jim: Mr. P., out of all the applicants we are bound to get… …Jim rolls his eyes and Ben kicks his chair for him to continue… Jim: …tell us why you’d be the best valet for us. Mr. P.: Well, because no one can stop Mr. Powerful is the main reason. If anyone tried something illegal against you I could perhaps step in and deliver a scoop slam. Plus I will help you win fans over. Ben: Mr. P., do you know that we’re heels and literally hate people who pander to the fans? Mr. P. (sad): I didn’t really know that, no. Virgil: I got one – Mr. P., do you suck dick?? Jim and Ben burst out laughing but then quickly pour themselves water and drink it down to repress the laughter. Mr. P.: Is that one of the duties of a valet? I don’t even wear queer spandex in the ring and I’m not going to start now. Jim (smiling at Virgil): I think he was making a POOR JOKE Mr. P., I apologize. Mr. P.: That’s fine. Would you like for me to change into my bathing suit now? Ben: Actually Mr. P., that’s okay. I think we’ve really seen enough. We have a lot of other applicants to review, plus we have to catch a plane in about 15 minutes to get back to Wisconsin! Mr. P: No problem, gentlemen. I really don’t think you’ve given me equal consideration though. Just because I’m not a sexy woman does not mean I wouldn’t make a great valet. I am very conscious about footwear you know, I could design you custom footwear. Jim: I think we’re good man. Virgil gets up and escorts Mr. Powerful off of the platform and out of the barn. Then he comes back in with a wide grin on his face and hands Mr. Powerful’s portfolio to Jim. Virgil: April fools! Jim: Shut up man, its friggin September. Ben: Well that sucked. Virgil laughs and brings Jim and Ben over to his black Ford Bronco. Virgil: Get in you two losers I’ll take yer sorry asses to the airport. Jim (waving the portfolio): What the hell am I going to do with this? Ben: Ahh… keep it. Maybe we’ll call him back for the finals. If nothing else maybe just the sight of him will distract people. Jim sighs and gets into the truck and the three take off for the airport.
