Title: Against the Authority- Part 2 Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: -- It's a Sunday morning. Yet, students are pouring into Brigham Young High School? Why? The answer is quite clear. The twenty-five youngsters entering the building are the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid's students. The only thing they know is that their teacher has been greviously wronged, and that a plan to rectify this is about to begin. In his classroom, the HIT stirs, having written two massive characters on the board in red chalk. The Greek letters: Delta, and Pi. Yes, Operation Delta Pi is about to begin. But what does it entail? And, what, if anything, does the Trapezoid plan to do about his two massive matchups this week on Turmoil and Fusion? As always, we're about to find out. ::Walking through the hallways of Brigham Young High School, five and twenty teenagers have determined looks on their faces. Their teacher has always been there to help them when they needed it, and now, they are eager to return the favor. The door to their classroom is open, and they immediately notice what The HIT did when he entered, and are equally appalled.:: The HIT: Welcome everyone...I'm sorry that none of my things are here, but you'll find out why shortly. :The students file in slowly, in one neat row, taking their positions in their seats, as The HIT prepares for a lecture unlike any other..: The HIT: Kids.....last night, the school board of Salt Lake City somehow got into my classroom and took all of my, no, our stuff. They even took the math books....the math books I wrote myself. :The kids murmur amongst themselves...: The HIT: I told you all in the beginning of the year that I worked for a year and a half non-stop to create those textbooks. Other than you kids, they are the things that I am most proud of. And now, the school system has tried to take them away from me. Jimmy: Damn man.....that sucks! What are you going to do about it? :Trapezoid looks at his pupil, and smiles.: The HIT: Well, Jimmy, you see, it's like what I'm doing in PCW right now. When you object to the leadership, you have to find a way to get rid of it....but, only if that leadership is unjust. You see, in PCW, the leadership of the Front Office is that it taxes and opresses the roster without their consent! It's like the old colonial argument....no taxation without representation.....yes Samantha? Samantha: But, even if the colonists would have gotten representation in Parliament....they would have still rebelled! They wanted indepedence! The HIT: Samantha, I'm a math teacher. The history department's on the OTHER SIDE OF THE BUILDING! Samantha: Duly noted... The HIT: Anyway...guys, I would do this myself, but I have a lot to think about. I have a match for a shot at the tag team titles on Tuesday, and a PCW Television Title Match on Friday. So....to help me out here....I've enlisted you as part of Operation Delta Pi. :The students simultaneously look at each other, and say..: Class: WHAT THE HECK IS OPERATION DELTA PI? The HIT: Operation Delta Pi is my plan to bring down all those in the school system who aren't receptive to my brand of teaching. Class: How can we help though? The HIT: Well, I need to prepare for my matches..and besides, they won't listen to me. School board people, however, WILL listen to students on occassion. The school board has hired a new principal to replace Principal Zuzman...they fired him because he liked my teaching style. :Everyone gasps, feeling genuine sorrow, as they all liked Principal Zusman.: Jonathan: Who's going to replace him? The HIT: Some woman named Principal Susorch...that the board put in because they think she can 'tame' me....as if. Anyway, it's time for us to go and do a good old fashioned PROTEST! Everyone: YEAH! The HIT: That principal is going to be here soon, to get acquainted with the school! I've brought in some art supplies, and we're going to give her hell, DAMNIT! Everyone: YEAH! The HIT: NOW! TAKE YOUR MARKERS, AND YOUR CARDBOARD, AND LET'S GET TO WORK!! :The students quickly rise, grabbing their art materials and begin to make their creations. HIT looks on, a tear in his eye..: The HIT: Those kids....they make me so proud. Coming here on a weekend to help me like this...they're some of the best kids I've ever taught. I really should find a way to thank them...wait! I know! :The Sultan of Sides stands, calling out to interrupt the work.: The HIT: Alright kids! Announcement! For all you're doing to help me.....I'm taking you all to Friday Fusion with me! Kids: YAY!! Jimmy: Is that where you get the shot at the TV Title? The HIT: Yes....against four people. John: Five guy match? That can't be fun.. The HIT: Yeah, Venneman Pendragon...the TV Champ and mythical hero, Rush Mason, voodoo expert...Teresa Tomas.....my tag team partner, and Walden. Jimmy: Tag team partner? Is that another one of your cutesy euphemisms for someone you're having sex with? The HIT: No, she's literally a tag team partner....if we can beat Brandi and Waldo though, we get a shot at the tag team titles. Not bad, huh? Jimmy: Not bad at all, if all goes according to plan. The HIT: And it will. When was the last time something I came up with didn't go according to plan? Jimmy: Well...there WAS that time last week when.. The HIT: Err, it was a rhetorical question... Jimmy: And I gave a rhetorical answer. The HIT: Just be a good student and go back to work on your signs, okay? Jimmy: Sure! :The HIT smiles, as he looks out over his students. Vengeance, in both wrestling and teaching, would be very very sweet indeed...: Fade to black ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
