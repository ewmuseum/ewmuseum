40 A-Ok (Part 3)

16 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept. 16, 2002 In the nightmare that is the psyche of a Mandrake, hundreds of thousands of lost souls torment them in their nightmares. Perhaps there is a price to pay for being the most brutal unit within the Dark Eldar fighting force. Most units dismiss as background noise, and use this rage and torment against their enemies in battle. Some can’t handle it, and commit suicide; others become members of the entertainment troop. The people who kill slaves in gladiator style combat. Eddie is not immune to these kinds of dreams, and his dreams are worse than the champion Mandrakes. In these dreams, the tortured souls that have been killed by Eddie and his kindred are screaming at him. Moaning in pain, and groaning in agony, blaming him for their death, and of course, their right. No, God, Please, NO! AAH! NO!! Why? Why did you have to kill me?! WHAT DID I EVER DO TO YOU! That is a sample of what goes on in his mind. Amongst pictures, and moving swirls of colours and light, there is not much else to the constant nightmares of Eddie. He begins to stir from the visit by his sub-conscience. He sees a rather large figure sitting on the edge of his bed. He blinks to try to get his vision back to normal. He notices the large figure and delivers a small smile. “Hello there. It’s been a long time” says the tall and dark one. Eddie continues to blink briefly. “I thought you were dead or worse captured by the humans” Eddie states. “Heh, you aren’t that lucky my friend. You still have me to watch your ass for now.” “Hmm…How did you get in here?” “Oh, the wench let me in. She’s a keeper, man. Don’t kill her unless she really screws up” The tall one advises. “I see, allow me to get you something…” Eddie says as he gets up from his bed and heads into the living quarters, and sees the slave that Eddie saved from certain death already fixing something out of the leftovers in the refrigeration unit. “SLAVE! Ah, good, you anticipated, whatever you’re making, make more.” “Um…can I be introduced?” “Oh, please excuse me, you humans and your ‘manners’. Very well, allow me to introduce Bre’ll Brak, Lord of the Kabal of the Rendering Eye. I served under him for years, and I was rewarded in kind with many slaves and souls for my own consumption. He was believed to be captured by the humans, tortured for information and dumped on their garbage world of Dustball…” Eddie explained. “Ah, I see, a pain to meet you” the slave quipped. Brak just looks into the slave’s eyes. The slave looks away. “Eddie, did you put a restrainer chip in her?” “No, I have not had the chance to take her to processing” Eddie replies. “That explains the sharp tongue. Anyway, there is a reason why I came. I know you are not happy with the Black Heart, and I also know of your longing for more slaves that aren’t human…” “That I am Bre. That I am” Eddie states. Before Eddie continues, the slave serves the grub, A mass of grey slabs in a steaming brown sauce. “That looks so good!” The Lord says as he grabs food. He simply shoves it in his mouth. “Damn that’s good! So I am offering you a position in my Kabal. You’ll be able to get more slaves of other species. We are planning a slave raid near Ork territory soon. I can give you a new Kabal, and a new lease on life” Brak offers while chewing his food. “What do I tell Vect?” Eddie inquires. “Give him a punch to the head and let him know you’re leaving. He may send his own retinue after you. Maybe even your former Mandrake unit will come after you until you reach my end of Comorragh…at that point, I have them.” “Come on, it can’t be that easy…can it?” “Sure. He won’t send his Haemonculi after you unless you make an attempt on his life. Just give him a punch and be on your way. I’ve done it a few times” “You have?” Eddie says with some shock in his voice. “Hell yeah. He didn’t mind it, but I was given a beating by his retinue. Nothing lethal, it just hurt like hell” Brak states. “Hah! You know something…I’m gonna go for it” Eddie says. The Lord cracks a massive smile. “Meet me in my territory in two hours. We will launch when you arrive” The Lord says. “See you in a bit sir.” Brak leaves the apartment, and Eddie gathers all his weapons and loads them into a bag. “Slave, hold the fort while I’m gone. Lock the door, and you are free to use the quarters as you see fit, but make sure you clean up after yourself.” Eddie says as he locks the door. A few minutes later at the Kabal of the Black Heart Headquarters In the throne room of the Kabal of the Black Heart, we see Asdrubael Vect toying with a slave. He’s hanging him in the middle of the room, and the occupants are taking bets on how long it will take for the slave to die. The slave is squirming, trying to survive; everyone is taunting the slave, trying to make him lose focus. Vect finally gets bored… “Bah, fuck it.” Vect takes the six-foot pole blade from one of his incubi, and throws it at the slave. It plunges and penetrates into the slave’s body. It enters through the chest, and exits through the back with a pile of blood spewing out of both ends. The slave finally goes limp and dangles from the barbed rope. “Well…I’m bored. Get me another slave, let’s devise another trial” Vect states as he points to the door. The door opens and in walks Eddie, with the slave’s head twisted 180 degrees, basically so he can look at his own spine. Eddie charges in, and delivers a thunderous right hand to Vect’s jaw. Vect with his throne fly backwards and his personal Wych cult scatters to the shadows from whence they came. “Okay, I’m leaving the Black Heart and joining the Rendering Eye, you worthless pile of trash” Eddie says with much anger and rage in his voice. “Oh really? That’s fine, go!” Vect says. “Say what? I thought you would destroy me.” “No, just go, I already have your replacement in the wings” Vect says rather confidently. Eddie just turns and leaves the room. Vect stands in his throne room…and nods. Behind him a cloak of invisibility destabilizes showing a mandrake with red eyes and armed to the teeth with blades coming out where blades aren’t normally placed. He’s breathing heavily with intensity. “Follow him, to wherever he goes. When the time is right…eliminate him from our picture. I don’t want him coming back to Comorragh, you understand?” The mandrake nods as his breathing rate continues to be heavy, as he re-cloaks himself and returns to the shadow realm. If he thinks that he will pop me in the jaw and get away with it, he must think again. I will show him that you do not cross the Black Heart! Vect thinks to himself as we leave this dark and dismal scene
