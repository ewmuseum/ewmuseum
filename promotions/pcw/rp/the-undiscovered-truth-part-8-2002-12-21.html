The Undiscovered Truth (Part 8)

21 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/21/02 The Undiscovered Truth Part 8 Being different, sometimes it’s good, but most times it isn’t. Acceptance is something everyone lives for, but some live without ever feeling it. What happens to these men and women you probably wonder? Well they fade away from the public eye. They live in the prior week and stay that way till they die. They wish and hope they can change, but at the same time know everything will just stay the same. These people aren’t useless. They were put on the earth for a purpose, but in response the earth treats him or her so badly that they never realize their purpose. The world taints us. People can’t accept those who are different. Everything needs a reason, and if people don’t get a reason they quickly jump to conclusions. Conclusions, a funny word eh, something that’s completely a guess from observations, but yet people live off of these conclusions as if they were written in stone. Why do people believe things they know are wrong? Because they’re too scared to stand up and support it in front of the same people who are too scared for the same reason. If people could see other people’s thoughts, they’d realize how similar they all are. Our instincts and thoughts are all similar yet the ways we carry them out are a world’s different. People will never accept what they can’t comprehend! *The scene opens in a science lab, where the focus is on a plastic bag with a paper label on it, that says “Romantico Blood Sample”. There is a female nurse and male research scientist in the room; both are wearing extremely protective suits that look something like space gear. * Hand me the sample. The nurse grabs the blood sample with pliers and hands it to the scientist. The scientist slowly drains the blood into a tiny beaker. He places the beaker over a heated stove, to see the chemical reaction. This is quite strange. What is it? The blood is expanding condensing from the heat. Usually cold condenses a liquid or solid. The blood is also hardening, to almost form a protective shield. Come over here and look at the texture. It’s almost in a satin form. The nurse comes over all looks over the blood. With even her little knowledge and experience, she notices the change in the blood’s appearance. Do me a favor and get me the laser microscope. Set the lens to sixty-two, I want to see what’s causing this satin shield. The nurse brings over the microscope and the scientist toys around with it and the sample until he gets a clear shot. He sees that the cells within the blood are moving at such a rapid speed that they have actually come together to form a giant cell. It’s a miracle that this man is still alive. Why is that? Most people would die from the pain of this type of reactions. Plus it would clog up the nerves, leaving little feelings to go through your heart. Maybe that would be the reason why they say this man has little emotions. It’s more than that, for this man is incapable of emotions. Do you realize how much this much affect this mans mind frame. I have no idea. I believe your familiar with the way our body reacts to sun as to that reaction to rain. When there is sun it lifts our spirits. Well you see this Romantico man never has that sunlight, for him it is always dark and he sees it no other way. The environment and nature cannot change the way Romantico feels, so therefore he is incapable of most normal human instincts and feelings. So does this make him the non-human everyone says he is. That’s hardly true, for the rest of his body is that of anybody else. The strange part about him is this unknown substance streaming through his blood. I’ve done my research and talked to other scientists all around the world and none of them can identify it by characteristics. This lab here has just furthered my wonder. Will we ever be able to come up with a conclusion? The only way we’ll be able to come to a conclusion in this one, is if we get this man and go through a surgical operation. Other than that, this cannot be comprehended. So that’s just it? It’s in the countries hands now. *The scene goes over to the country of Spain’s Federal Office. The room is filled with officers holding notebooks, as the head of the police unit walks in. Pictures are taken for various newspapers as the police general begins his speech. At 2:30 this morning, the country has declared for a new member among the countries Wanted List. This man is to be sought for but not harmed in any way. Any sign of punishment or abuse to this man will end up in extermination or stiff penalty. The Countries Science Development Institute is asking for the cooperation of a man who goes by the name of El Romantico. It is determined that the man is living in the Barcelona area, and is known by his alias. The man is said to be dangerous so be careful. We do not want to turn this into a violence issue. The man is needed for a project involving the development of human life. It is of extreme importance that this man stays within the boundaries of this country and that he is tested. Since the only name we are aware of is “El Romantico” it will be extremely difficult to trace through information. We’ll have to go out their, ask around, and gather information. The reward for those who have seen this man will start at fifty thousand dollars. DISMISS! *The hall of police investigators and officers clear the room as everyone is now on the search. * The next day in big bold letters the newspaper of Barcelona reads” Former Barcelona Fighting Hero Wanted!
