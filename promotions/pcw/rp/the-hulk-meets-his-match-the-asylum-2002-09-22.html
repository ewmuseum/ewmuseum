The Hulk Meets His Match... The Asylum

22 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22 - 09 - 02 Prologue: We have seen Gotham's crime lords take their escapades from the heart of the City to the dark and mysterious mansion that homes one of the worlds richest people in Bruce Wayne with a parody of miss-haps. The three super villains we have became accustomed to were adamant that the millionaire was the 'Dark Knight' better known as Batman, finding their speculation to be more myth than fact they hold onto a comment he made before their inevitable exit. The scene opened as the second part of this trilogy concluded with the three Gotham super villains fleeing the Wayne family mansion. A Cadillac swerves along the dirt trail that lead from the architectural master-piece that looms in the background shrouded in darkness the car follows the road at a steady pace. The moon continues to generate the only light source besides the obvious headlights of the Cadillac that stretch forward offering minimum visibility but visibility nonetheless on a night where tragedy is only a stone throw away. The car looked fixed on its destination, presumably the residence of Bruce Banner. The trio who occupy the automobile swing the car around a sharp right, an almost blind corner that takes them into an enclosed area surrounded by dense forestry woodland away from humanity. The car is thrown about like a mere bicycle as the terrain gets increasingly harder to tackle, with no road or nearby suburbia who would want to live in these hazardous surrounding? And better yet, why? As the suspension jerked up and down the car stopped abruptly with a heavy screech from the wear of the brake pads in an open clearing with a wooden cottage set aside from the rest of the open world. Gaz Green still in his full portrayal of the Joker steps out of the vehicle with a nasty snarl, 'Who in their right mind would live in such squalor?' His two comrades also stood erect from their seating arrangements allowing the camera to bask in the choice of wardrobe. Nerezza again is submersed in the world of Mr. Freeze with a heavy, metallic suit and glass dome head guard whilst Miyagawa masquerades as the Riddler with his lime green business attire that could do with touching up courtesy of Eleanor Kannon-Hall. 'Someone with serious paranoia!' replied the frosted felon Mr. Freeze. 'Either that or someone with major social problems... Unless it's a government cover-up, huh, can we say Area 51...' injected the Riddler before an untimely interruption from the jovial Joker. 'You and your conspiracy theories, maybe being a super villain isn't for you, how about auditioning for the X-Files?!?' Slowly creeping from the shadows Gaz Green showcased his speckled coloured tuxedo that would rather benefit a clown more than a criminal force like Gotham's leading nemesis. The collective who've been branded the unlikely name of 'The Triads' join their efforts in a group haggle like a football team prep talk. 'What makes you think someone lives out here, let alone a scientist who can maybe help us save Cat Woman?' questioned the green lantern wannabe Riddler, scratching his head in the process. 'He has a point... Bruce Wayne turned out to be a dodgy stock market investor and not the cape crusader. Realistically what is the chances of finding a scientist out here, we're more likely to encounter some raged fuelled mass of muscle!' said Mr. Freeze, siding with his green colleague. 'Guys a little faith here, I know the Bruce Wayne episode was a case of false identity but...' roared the Joker; rallying his troops when suddenly a thunderous 'Bang' startled them. The scent of a fired shotgun filtered over the flourishing green land and the three villains slowly turned to face the source of the intrusion. Standing before the three captors was a scrawny gentleman holding a shotgun with smoke pouring from the barrel. He gazed over his three trophies with a dumbfounded glare trying to fathom the attraction before him. 'Err-... Hmm... Has the circus come to town?' asked the stranger. 'Would you be Bruce Banner?' said the inquisitive Joker his hands high above his head, catching the gentle breeze. 'I'll ask the questions... What's with the colour scheme of your clothes?' retorted the unknown man, this time with a touch of authority. 'Hey! I'm the Riddler hence the question marks, this is Mr. Freeze, long story but he requires that suit to stay alive and this is the....' again the possible Bruce Banner interrupts. 'The Clown, I get it' 'Not really, I'm the Joker... Now that gun makes me feel a little uneasy but are you Bruce Banner?' murmured the Lord Of Laughter. The man acknowledged with a simple nod and threw his hand back offering the Triads into his home, setting the weapon down behind the large oak door that adorns the front of the building as the four man party enter. The place is little furnished with just the necessities on display with most items personally built from the surrounding woodland giving the room a fresh smell. 'So why have you tracked me down?' said Bruce Banner. 'Your a scientist right, which could be of valid use to us!!!' grinned the behemoth Ner-... Mr. Freeze. 'I 'WAS' a scientist, old news, an accident and no job...' sighed the former professor. 'Well you folks look a little famished, I'll put the kettle on!' continued Bruce Banner as he set off into the kitchen, as the sound of running water filtered over the silence. 'A FORMER SCIENTIST, FAT LOAD OF USE, SO WHAT NOW HUH?!?' roared the Joker towards the contemplating Mr. Freeze and fidgeting Riddler. The Jester, Err-... The Joker paced back and forth trying to fathom the position they were in. As hope faded away he leant over a torch that burnt on the wall and it suddenly turned to one side unveiling a secret passage as the sofa dropped back on a hinge with a flight of stairs leading into a cavern below. 'WHAT THE F....' bellowed the three super villains in unison. The group descended into the unknown via the stairs and stumbled upon a secret laboratory with huge mechanical equipment all active as their dials flashed randomly in various sequences. Mr. Freeze peered at the machinery with amazement, the Riddler look surprised and the Joker stood motionless almost foaming at the mouth but as looked on star struck the distinguished voice of Bruce Banner was once again injected. 'You shouldn't be down here~!' 'You have some explaining to do, Mr. Banner... A lot of explaining if you still regard yourself a former scientist!!!' hissed Mr. Freeze. Bruce looking a little on edge decided to tell his shallow story, 'Well, like I said I had an accident that cost me my job and amongst other things, then I developed... Well let's call it a temper and I'm trying to reverse the effects hence this equipment, but this isn't any temper, you do understand?!?' 'So with this equipment I shall revert to my former self, it isn't too complicated, powered by negativity, better known as the Neo Void-...' continued the calm Bruce Banner before Gaz Green intervened. 'Neo Void, Hmm, Akiyama is the Neo Void God....' whispered the Prince Of Pranks. 'WHERE'S ANARCHY, TELL US OR THERE'LL BE HELL TO PAY!!!' yelled Alexander Miyagawa still in full-blown Riddler mode. 'Anarchy? Huh... Who's that?' said a rather confused Bruce Banner. 'YOUR USING THE NEO VOID AND YOU'RE TRYING TO TELL US YOU DON'T KNOW ANAR-... CAT WOMAN?!?' groaned the cold assassin Mr. Freeze. 'SPEAK UP MR. BANNER....' bellowed the Joker, his arc smile reduced to a look of disgust. 'I don't know what you people are talking about, honest!' pleaded the scientist. Suddenly Bruce Banners eyes turned a pale shade of green and his body mass began to amount into muscle, coinciding with his skin colour to alter almost momentarily. Bruce Banner had transformed into a hideous green monster before the very eyes of the Triads as they gaze in terror. 'HULK SMASH!!!' growled the creature. 'HULK SMASH - HULK SMASH - HULK SMASH.... YOU!!!' The party of three looked as the creature drew nearer, slowly pacing towards them but the Joker was adamant he'd get the relevant information from the beast. 'WHERE'S ANA-... CAT WOMAN, HUH?' 'WHAT HAVE YOU DONE TO HER, YOU AND THAT EVIL AKIYAMA!!!' 'WHERE'S ANARCHY....' - Suddenly the scene switched to the bedroom of Gaz 'The Jester' Green - The scene opened with Nerezza leaning over his sleeping comrade Gaz Green, almost throttling him with his bare hands trying to bring him out of his slumber. Suddenly the Jester's eyes widened as the sweat washed across his face and in bated breathe he say's, 'Where's Anarchy?' Nerezza looked again with confusion, scratched his head and replied to the zombie like state of his partner. 'Old news, Anarchy is safe and well... Don't you remember we rescued her, Gaz are you alright?' 'AYE - BAD DREAM SQUIRE' proclaimed the emerald menace before hitting the pillow hard as he returned to his dream like state, eyes firmly shut. Nerezza watched the episode before turning and waltzing through the open door like the Angel Of Death mentioned during the Bible.
