Cannonball Run Final!! Who wins? What happens?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Two weeks ago {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up with the now familiar scene, of the blue 2000 Dodge Viper driving down a highway in the Cannonball Run. The weather is still looking good, it's a partly cloudy day with a few dark clouds off in the distance, but none of the weather looks to be coming towards Semore Smoke at all. Certain vehicles have announced that they're out of the race. Tempest and Stimpo, who were driving the blue Mini S Cooper and Scott Taylor and Tyler Davidson who were driving in a black and green monster truck. All other people are somewhere driving camera contact has been lost with Duzza and Mark Makonie, as well as with Jim Surge and Ben 'Nighthorse' Campbell, and Timo Bolamba and Mike Marvelous. The highway that Semore Smoke and The End are currently driving on doesn't seem that bad traffic wise. They're now inside the California state lines with about half an hour's drive left to go. It's around noon now on the last day of the race and Semore Smoke and The End are leading the race. The scene enters the car and you can see that Semore Smoke is still driving the car and that the speedometer tells us that Semore Smoke is speeding at least forty miles per hour over the speed limit of sixty-five at a hundred and five miles per hour. Semore turns towards The End who is sporting a new mask now that he found in Las Vegas. The new mask covers his mouth only and looks somewhat like the masks that Scorpion, Sub-Zero, or Reptile from Mortal Kombat might wear. He's no longer wearing his sunglasses, so his white blind eyes are looking out at the road, as if he could see where they were going. He turns to where Smoke is sitting and asks… End: How much longer do we have now? {{{{Smoke takes his eyes off the road for a second and looks at The End and answers…}}}} Semore Smoke: Well End, we've got about a fifty more miles to go. I'd say we'd be there within half an hour, as long as we don't run into any trouble. {{{{End's voice is somewhat muffled as it comes through the mask, but you can understand it clearly…}}}} End: Ah… Well… you don't have to call me End anymore… I think of us as pretty good friends now, why don't you just call me Skylar. {{{{Smoke turns back to the road and grins a grin from ear to ear…}}}} Semore Smoke: Ok Skylar, no problem. {{{{Smoke reaches down into the ashtray of the car and pulls out a half smoked blunt. He sticks the blunt into his mouth and pushes the car cigarette lighter in so it heats up the cherry inside. A few seconds pass and not a word is said, a car is heard honking in the background though, but it's coming from a few cars behind Semore Smoke. The cigarette lighter pops out and Smoke grabs it and lights to blunt. He takes a puff from the blunt and holds the smoke in his lungs for a few seconds. He then lets the smoke out through his nose and mouth and smiles another grin as the effects start to get to his head. Smoke can now taste the taste of victory. The scene changes, but it doesn't fade in and out, the camera just pans away from Smoke's car and goes a few cars back where you can see the familiar red van of the Russian spy and his manager, Alexei Ruslan. Ivan is still driving the van and still in the passenger seat is Alexei Ruslan. Obviously they were able to catch up to Semore Smoke after being pulled over earlier that morning. The camera begins to pan back again, but as it pans away, you can hear Ivan yelling in a deep Russian accent at the top of his lungs…}}}} Ivan: Dahmn Jahpahnese piece of sheet! {{{{Obviously Ivan Stanislav is still having troubles with the FSB equipment that the Russian spies had installed in the van. The camera pans just two cars behind the red van with Soviet license plates and you can see the 1978 Cadillac that Joey Flash and D-Kay Calyx switched to so they could lose the cops, that gave them an escort through the race until they reached Las Vegas. D-Kay has taken over driving, since the caddy is his Aunt's. Joey is sitting in the passenger seat, and both are smiling as they continue to speed up towards the Russians. The camera pans back yet again and you can see the McLaren F1 Chromed out of Statuz Quo and Dante Jackson. Statuz is driving and Dante is sitting in the passenger seat of the car. They must be wearing microphones as you can hear their conversation that they're having…}}}} Dante: Nigga… look up ahead… Dem boys think they can out run us? Statuz: Hell no… You can bet dis damn thing can go a lot faster…. {{{{Statuz double touches the gas and it must have a Nitro system hooked up there because the car suddenly gets another burst of speed and it speeds up past the few cars in front of them that were separating them from the Russian van. They make it up to the van, but as they pass the van, Ivan is seen smiling. They pass the van and swerve in front of the van and you can clearly see Ivan press a button on the dash, all of a sudden, what seems to be a missile shoots out of the front of the van towards Statuz Quo and Dante Jackson. As the missile gets close to the car it breaks apart and a net is exposed. The net then shoots down towards the tires with amazing speed and as it gets to the tires, spikes somehow break out of the net and attach to the tires of the McLaren F1. The air of the tires is sucked out of the tires automatically and Statuz can't slow down to get control back. The car swerves from left to right, and then swerves left one last time and spins out. It hits a light poll on the far side of the road as it goes off the road, and flips over to the roof of the car. Statuz and Dante aren't hurt though as they cut free of their seat belts and roll out of the car to cuss away at the speeding away red van. Speeding past the two black people, is the purple hearse that is the car of PCW's tag team, P.W.A. Driving the hearse is Nerezza and sitting in the passenger side is Gaz 'The Jester' Green. They continue to speed forward towards the Russian van. As they drive up to the back bumper of the van, Nerezza hits a button on the dash, the front of the hearse opens up and two huge clamps reach forward for the back bumper. The top clamps hover over the top of the back bumper, the bottoms of the clamps are under the bumper, Gaz reaches over and hits another button and the clamps close. Nerezza then goes to slam on the breaks, but as he slams on the breaks, the car keeps going. The camera pans back a little so you can see the cab of the van vaguely and see Ivan sitting in the driver side seat. Ivan hits a button and the bumper detaches at the back. Ivan then speeds forward as the bumper explodes after it detaches and it sends the purple hearse hurling off the road. The hearse stops off the road as the engine was blown to pieces, but both people in the car are ok as they exit the car to look around. All of a sudden, another bumper pops out of the back of the red van and hooks back in place, the license tag is the same as the other one before it and it's as if nothing had happened to it. The camera turns to show what's ahead up the highway and you can now see a huge city on the horizon. Both the blue Dodge Viper and the red van pass a sign that reads that LA is just two miles away. Both cars continue to speed forward and the scene fades…}}}} {{{{It fades back in to see Semore Smoke and Ivan Stanislav now driving through the city. A minute has gone by since the scene had faded and nothing had happened so it was edited out. Semore swerves past several cars, the traffic picked up as both drivers entered the city and now they have to avoid cars left and right. The distant sound of sirens is heard as cop cars must be speeding to their location. The scene changes to the inside of Smoke's car and you can see him smiling as he looks ahead. He turns to Skylar in the passenger seat and starts to yell at him…}}}} Semore Smoke: The finish line is less then fifty yards away! {{{{Skylar is overcome by joy in the passenger seat. And sure enough, as the camera looks forward, you can see the finish line, with a banner marked finish, and several banners around the parking lot that is the finish that say 'Cannonball Run'. But… in a twisted turn of events, a car pulls out in front of Smoke and Smoke can't stop in time, he hits the car in the driver side door of it at sixty miles per hour. Both cars spin and crash into opposite buildings on the side of the two-lane road. The red van doesn't get away either, as the other two cars are spinning off the road, a car that swerves to miss Smoke's car swerves right in front of the red van. The red van crashes into the car and is sent spinning off the road and into the same building that Smoke hits to stop. Smoke and Skylar are unharmed though by their crash and are getting out of the wreckage with their slip to punch at the finish line. They are starting to sprint forward, Smoke is holding Skylar's hand so he doesn't get lost as they run towards the finish line. Ivan is out of the van holding his head and Alexei climbs out the same door. In his hand is a slip, they both start to run as well towards the finish line and now it's a dead heat between the two teams as they both are running side-by-side towards the parking lot finish. People are seen screaming and rooting for the two teams as they are limping/running towards the finish. Smoke shoves at Ivan who shoves back hard, both aren't giving up any ground as they continue to run. But Smoke looks at Skylar and yells at him as he gives him the slip of paper…}}}} Semore Smoke: Just run straight, you'll run right up to the table, ask the lady to punch the card for you, once it's punched, we win! {{{{Skylar nods and takes the punch paper and begins to run forward towards the parking lot with great speed. Smoke then turns to Ivan and Alexei and jumps up into the air in a tackle, and tackles both men to the ground to slow them down. Skylar continues to run towards the table and as he is, Smoke looks up and is smiling, he starts to yell for Skylar as well….}}}} Semore Smoke: Go! Go Skylar! Go! {{{{Skylar arrives at the table and the card is punched, a whistle is blown and fireworks go off. Sitting off away from the table is a pile of money that must be a million dollars or so. Smoke is jumping up and down in the air as he realizes that they won and he gets up to run towards the parking lot. Ivan is cussing out his partner, Alexei, about how if the equipment hadn't had failed then they would have won. Smoke runs up to Skylar and grabs him with a huge hug. Skylar looks at Smoke and says…}}}} Skylar: We did it! Semore Smoke: Yes we did! Skylar: Can I have a joint to celebrate this win? Semore Smoke: Sure! {{{{Smoke and Skylar let go of each other and Smoke then searches in his pockets and grabs a joint and a book of matches. Skylar lifts the mask a little and sticks the joint into his mouth. He then takes the book of matches and lights the matches. Smoke turns to him and tells him…}}}} Semore Smoke: Now how does that taste? {{{{Skylar lights the joint with the match and then throws the match away, the match lands off the scene and you can't see where it lands. Skylar takes a puff of the joint and then takes it out to answer Smoke's question…}}}} Skylar: It taste great! {{{{Flames are seen coming into the scene now and people are screaming. Smoke turns as does the camera and he screams too. He turns to Skylar and the scene can now see that the match had landed in the million dollars, and now the money was fully engulfed in flames. Smoke turns to Skylar and looks at him with a dumbfounded look on his face. He then turns to the money and jumps towards it to try and put it out, but he doesn't get anything out, but only gets a few burns of his own on his arms. The money continues to burn as Smoke can't believe that he had done all that for nothing… Smoke then turns around and just walks away from the parking lot as if he were a loser, laughing in the street are Ivan Stanislav and Alexei Ruslan. Smoke just bypasses them as he can't believe it. He's lost his car, whatever money he could have made from the race, and now… his dignity. The scene fades to black, but as it fades to black, you can hear a voice over done by Semore Smoke…}}}} Semore Smoke: Skylar… End… whatever I should call you now. That was a few weeks ago… I'm glad I'm facing you this Tuesday for my Extreme title… {{{{A puff is heard as Smoke must be smoking a joint as he does the voice over…}}}} Semore Smoke: I get to burn you like you burned my money… You will pay for my car, my money, and my life…. You will pay… oh you will pay! {{{{The voice over ends as you hear the sound of a chair rolling back and someone getting up. You hear the sound of another puff on a joint or cigarette, most likely a joint. And you hear a door slamming after that. Then the scene completely fades…}}}}
