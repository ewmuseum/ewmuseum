The Olsen Twins - Part 1 - Waking Up to a FULL HOUSE

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 3 [The morning sun sometimes isn't that bodacious. When you are beaten like a bloody pulp by a bunch of totally bogus dudes called The Asylum, you sort of need your rest. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy and 'Heavy Metal' Harry, The Cowabunga Connection, have been faced with this situation for a couple weeks now. The Asylum has gotten the better of the two bodacious dudes, and there really isn't much that they can do to stop it. Even through the heinous beatings the Connection defeated Playaz Inc., teaching those totally bogus new dudes who the big boys are in the tag team division. Even through that gnarly victory the duo still has one new team to deal with. The bogus dudes who cost the Connection the tag team straps at Third Dawning...PWA] [The PWA, Nezerra and Gaz, tricked the bodaciously dynamic duo on Turmoil a week ago. Tommy faced Miyagawa in a gnarly battle that could have cemented the Connection as top contenders in the tag division AND singles competition. Unfortunately those heinous dudes made sure that didn't happen and Miyagawa defeated Tommy, but it wasn't an easy task for the compadre with goggles. While Tommy was beaten to a bloody pulp, his tag team partner was locked in a storage room with two very odd babes, The Olsen Twins. The little dudettes who played in that weird sitcom, Full House. They were just little dudettes during that time and were cute dudettes at that. These days with puberty in full effect these babes have taken on a much new mold. Is it bodacious? Yeah. Is it bogus? Most definitely. They are babes, but they are still most young babes. Harry spent some most unnecessary time in a storage room with these babes. Harry seems to not remember much, being unconcious, but Tommy knows some most bogus stuff went on] [Tommy sleeps peacefully in his queen sized bed. The silk blue covers give him warmth from the chilly nights that California has been receiving lately. His blonde hair is all over the place against the blue pillows and his bright smile shines even in his sleep as happy dreams fill his head. Two shadows come over his body, taking the sunlight away from him and it disturbs his slumber somewhat as he shifts his body to the right. Two woman shadows above him, as his eyes open he spies two red heads standing at the end of his bed. His slightly smiles not really remembering taking home bodacious babes from the night before. Really, the only thing he can remember is the vicious beating from The Asylum members. If these babes want to get some lovin' on, then its fine with Tommy. He extends his arms out to them and the two women approach on the bed, slowly crawling to his arms. Tommy's bright blue eyes begin to slowly focus now, starting to see the slender, shapely bodies of these two red heads. They are looking most bodacious] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Babes, we're going to have to take it slow. I'm feeling most bogus and don't need to further injury myself. [Tommy begins sliding down the strap of one of the babes dress. Her creamy white shoulder looks most excellent as he lightly kisses her shoulder, smiling up at her. His eyes see her tiny, slender face now and this babe looks oddly familiar. She looks pretty young too, but Tommy then lays a couple more kisses on her shoulder. The girl giggles, obviously quite ticklish] Other babe: Tommy, don't ignore me! [Tommy smiles and turns to the other babe, who looks EXACTLY like the other one. THEY MUST BE TWINS!! TOTALLY GNARLY! Tommy then pulls down her strap and lays a couple soft kisses onto her shoulder. She does the same exact giggle as Tommy laughs to himself, knowing that this is going to be some most radical fun] TTT: Babes, go to work. [Tommy puts his arms behind his head to let these two red haired twins go to work on him. They kiss his chest and begin slowly working their way down his body. His insides burn with passion, loving the feeling of a woman working her way downstairs, and with two babes doing it it's twice as good. ESPECIALLY TWINS!!] TTT: Yeah, that's what Tommy likes, babes. [Tommy has his eyes closed now, enjoying the two women kissing down his body] TTT: Your almost there, babes..... Stoned Voice: WHAT ARE YOU DOING, MAN?!?! [Tommy's eyes open wide, knowing that is the voice of his brother and tag team partner, 'Heavy Metal' Harry. Tommy looks at the two babes and sees that they're...] TTT: THE OLSEN TWINS!!!!!!! [Tommy leaps up and stumbles off of the bed, smacking his head off of the nightstand. He rubs his head, staggering toward his freeweights near the edge of his bed and stubs his right big toe on the 35 lb dumbbell. Tommy begins hopping in the air in agonizing pain and then trips over his desk chair back onto his head again. He lies in pain rolling to the corner of his room, look at Mary-Kate and Ashley Olson with pure terror] TTT: What in the name of FULL HOUSE are those two dudettes doing in here?!? [Before Harry can say something, Tommy decides to go on some more] TTT: Why are they in my room??! Why were they kissing my chest?!? WHY WAS I KISSING THEM?!?! BOGUS!! TOTALLY F**KING BOGUS!! [Tommy begins feeling sick to his stomach as he spits on the ground. His pain has turned to disgust now but still with a tiny hint of attraction in there for the twins] Mary-Kate: Tommy, I thought that you wanted us! TTT: Ugh! [Harry seems a bit angry as he stands in Tommy's bedroom doorway in his Twisted Sister boxer shorts. His small beer but hanging over them as his eyes are completely bloodshot, as they usually are] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: You stole my girlfriends, man!! [Tommy looks at Harry with terror in his eyes] TTT: GIRLFRIENDS?!? [Harry smiles as the twins approach Harry and he places an arm around each one] Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy is mortified by this site. IT'S THE OLSEN TWINS!! WHAT IS WRONG WITH HARRY?!] TTT: Dude, you realize how old these dudettes are? [Harry shrugs] TTT: They are only 16, dude!!! Harry: Far out, man. [Obviously the news didn't strike Harry the way it should have. Tommy slowly rises to his feet and stays in the corner, as if the twins are some sort of evil monsters] TTT: Dude, what happened in that storage closet on Turmoil last week, dude? Harry: Some far out stuff, man. First I started coming to and Mary-Kate was stradling... [Tommy puts his hand up and wears that face of disgust] TTT: STOP!! DUDE!! I thought you didn't remember what happened?! [Harry shrugs] Harry: Well, they told me what happened, man. Ashley: That we did. We shared something special in that storage closet and we decided that it would be best to start dating after sharing so much. Harry is too much man to just have one of us, so we both decided to go out with him. [Harry smiles and gives a thumbs up to Tommy] TTT: Dude... Harry: Check it out, man....to celebrate our upcoming title match I called up Shailyn and we're going to go out on a double date tonight, man! [Tommy is trying to breathe deeply, maybe make some sense of this insane bogusness. He then pinches himself to hope that it's some kind of nightmare, but it's not. Harry has his arms around two very illegal babes and he looks like he just won some kind of most bodacious lottery, but...in a weird, craptacular way...Harry did. They may be illegal, but lets face it these babes are hot. WAIT!! What's going on here?? Totally bogus, dudes] TTT: Double date.... Harry: Far out, man. It's on. [Fade to black]
