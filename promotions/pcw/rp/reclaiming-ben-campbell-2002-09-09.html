Reclaiming Ben Campbell

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th September 2002 Jim Surge and Misty sit in the front seat of their red KIA rental car with Ben Nighthorse Campbell smiling widely in the back seat. The three members of the Watkins Group are about to stop at a “scenic overlook” that they’ve noticed coming up on the stunningly beautiful Oregon coast. All of the trees, grass and shrubs in the area are lusciously green due to the regular rainfalls and serve as a magnificently colorful contrast to the dark blackish slate of the rocky coast and deep blue of the Pacific Ocean. “Well, well,” Misty says as she cranes her head back to spot Ben’s child-like grinning, “looks like somebody’s finally happy!” Ben Campbell, dressed in long tan pants with a leather drawstring belt and a light long-sleeved shirt with Apache geometric designs on it, nods his head affirmative, as if the little jaunt out into nature has replenished something deep within his soul. “I don’t really get to see much of this stuff back on the east coast,” Ben replies after a few moments of silence to carefully consider his words. “It feels like all of the hustle and bustle of New York and Montreal just don’t even exist out here.” Jim adjusts his dark sunglasses as he pulls the pretty tiny and pretty tinny KIA into the scenic overlook parking area. The car comes to a stop and Ben is out the door before Jim and Misty even have their seatbelts off. “Ahhhh…” the Apache Ghostrider says as he closes his eyes and fills his body deeply with a breath of truly fresh air. “This is how people are meant to live. Not crammed into whatever shitbox they can squeeze into.” Jim and Misty get out of the car and also drink in the fresh air with a bit of a weary smile that usually accompanies being inside a car for a few hours. Misty’s nose immediately starts to run and Jim opens the car door back up to grab her a tissue. Jim and Misty wanted to take Ben Campbell out to the coast while they were in Oregon for their match against Misplaced on Turmoil because the high-flying member of Redemption Song just hadn’t been acting like himself lately. “It must be beautiful here later in the fall,” Ben says, “I feel closer to my roots and the Great Spirit out here.” “AAAH-CHOO!” Misty lets out a high-pitched sneeze as Jim returns to her side with a tissue; the stunningly gorgeous valet of PCW’s hottest new tag-team apparently suffering from a minor bout of allergies. “It’s good we could do something here for you bro,” Surge says with a slight Western accent. “We had the whole day and have been at the gym all weekend. Today was perfect for a break.” Ben walks over to the ledge of the rocky coastline and sits down with his legs crossed (uh, um) Indian-style. He slowly removes the handmade leather headband from around his head and carefully places it in front of him on the dirt so as not to crush any of the 6 black and 6 white feathers that hang from it. Ben’s movements are slow and deliberate as if he was trying not to offend someone or taking part in some sacred ritual. In fact, he was doing both. “We’re going to just give you some time there Ben,” Jim says as Campbell completely ignores him. Misty blows her nose and nods but Ben doesn’t even look back at either of them. “Come on Mist, let’s go take a little walk down the path near the water.” Misty nods and she and the Tombstone, Arizona cowboy leave the other half of Redemption Song sitting perilously close to the edge of the cliff. Ben closes his eyes and begins to hum a chant to himself and starts to rock back in forth in his sitting position. After a few moments, everything around him fades into a solid wall of purple and for Ben he feels as if he is no longer on solid ground but floating in a vast ocean of unfathomable energy. With his eyes still closed, Ben notices the fact that he can see himself sitting of the cliff’s edge and he examines his red-skinned body and jet-black hair from all angles as if performing some sort of inspection. As he looks at himself Ben can sense a black energy near his body’s heart and it manifests itself on his body in the form of a tattoo of a black stallion on the skin over his heart. He watches carefully as the stallion races down his body and splits in two when it reaches his legs. The twin dark horses make their way to his feet before stopping and then splitting in two again. This time, two of the horses stay at his feet and the two new ones run back up his body and come to a rest on his cheeks. War paint… markings of the sacred Nighthorse… this is some sort of battle preparation letting me know my mind and body are not at peace. Must take a deep breath and roll with it. Become it. Bring the two incomplete sides…. Ben’s mind drifts off in several directions at once. He concentrates on Jim Surge, on Misty and on Redemption Song’s former manager Tom Core… trying desperately to find himself through the jumble of their energy. Ben has to concentrate completely to forcibly keep his eyelids shut as they twitch in an uncontrollable manner as beneath his eyeballs roll back deep within his skull. He takes another deep breath and focuses his attentions on the rolling and constant noises of the waves crashing against the rocky coast and the odd gull squawking every now and then. A dazzling beam of light makes the purple scene wash away from Ben’s mind and the Apache notices a sunbeam breaking through the clouds and hitting the still-closed eyes of his body. Still sitting at the edge of the cliff, Ben again feels as if he is floating. This time, the entire scene is white. Ben feels a strong sense of premonition, as if he is somehow close to Tom Core. Tom? Toooooom….. are you out there? Ben’s body convulses slightly and jerks while struggling to remain in the seated position. Flashes of a skunk, a snake and a porcupine appear brilliantly silhouetted in his mind, burning themselves into his consciousness. Skunk… snake AND porcupine….. these are all betrayal totems, shape-shifting totems… Tom what the hell are you up to? Ben struggles again to battle hard to keep his visions and the vastness switches to a sheet of blood red which descends through the blazing whiteness and wraps itself around the Apache’s body. Blood… my blood… finally… HOOOOONK!! HOOOOOONK!! Ben is snapped out of his trance and brought back to reality with the harsh honking of a car horn. Ben opens his eyes and runs his hands through his hair, picking up his headband and placing it back on his head. Looking behind him, Ben sees two young children sitting in the front seat of a gold SUV looking at him and waving before slamming on the horn again. The children are having some fun as their parents have jumped out to use the restroom at the scenic overlook. “Little assholes,” Ben mutters under his breath. Suddenly Ben grabs his head in what seems to be absolute agony and falls flat on his back, completely exhausted. As luck would have it, Jim and Misty return back to the scenic overlook, their walk and private bonding time complete and Misty looking as if she had just learned that her best friend had been killed. However, as soon as they spot Ben flat on his back, they rush over to him with concern the only thought in their minds. “Ben, Ben, you okay man?” Jim says, his Western accent more noticeable as it always is when he is in any kind of agitated state. Ben Nighthorse Campbell lets out a slight groan before Misty and Jim help him back up to his feet. Jim puts Ben’s arm around his neck and keeps him up while Misty goes to the KIA rental and picks up a bottle of water. “You’re not worried about our match are you?” Jim says, trying to say something positive but really unsuited to the concerned brotherly role. “We’re going to destroy those little…” “No…” Ben gasps in while exhaling as his legs buckle beneath him. “I had my first Ghostwalk in quite some time. Something big is going to happen. I think Tom is trying to betray us somehow.” “Somehow?!” Jim says in an annoyed voice as he uses his strength to keep Ben on his feet. “He already ditched us what the hell else is left for…” “STOP!” Ben says, grabbing his forehead with his hand to apply pressure and help soothe the pounding sensation. “There’s something else. Something with Misty we have to deal with first.” Misty returns with the water bottle and pours some over Ben’s forehead. The Apache reacts instantly and shakes the excess water from his skull by running his hands through his scalp. Jim catches Ben’s eyes for the first time as he opens his lids wide. Something in his expression gives Jim the feeling that he shouldn’t say anything else. “Let’s help him to the car, Mist,” Surge says as Misty lets out a big sigh. Misty and Jim help Ben back into the backseat of the KIA and then take their places back in the front seat. Ben immediately passes out into a deep sleep and Jim looks over to Misty. “Everything okay there Mist?” Surge asks his ex-girlfriend and current valet. “I guess so,” she answers in a completely deflated voice, “I don’t really want to talk about it if that’s okay. Maybe when we get back to the hotel.” “Sure, sure,” Surge says as he pulls out of the scenic overlook parking area and back onto the road to Portland. “No problem.” The little red rental speeds off into the Oregon afternoon.
