Hunted Saga Part Twelve: The Inner Battle...

Date: 15/4/021 Previously… The camera cuts to back inside the diner as Crichton warily gets to his hands and knees and clicks the safety off his rifle. One thing was for sure, he sure as hell was gonna go down fightin’ Crichton thought as he briefly pops up from behind the counter to unleash a few shots, forcing the soldiers to dive into two separate cubicles for cover. Crichton then grabs Aeryn by the hand and dragging her into the kitchen with him. The camera follows them as the zigzag through the surprisingly clean metal kitchen to the back door. The soldiers get up from their cover and quickly follow them. Crichton kicks the back door open and pushes Aeryn out ahead of him. Crichton notices all of the gas tanks directly to the left of the door, but it doesn’t register in his head. Crichton gets to about 100 yards away from the diner before the soldiers appear at the doorway and begin to fire. Crichton ducks and rolls instinctively fire a shot at the soldiers. However, the shot passes the soldiers and impacts upon the gas canisters. The reaction is instantaneous, the gas canisters explode, taking the guards and half of the diner with it. Crichton has a shocked expression upon his face. “Aw…that ain’t good.” Crichton remarks. Aeryn simply looks at him blankly, no realising the full implications of the explosion. “Why? It took out those two soldiers didn’t it?” Aeryn questions. Crichton simply looks at her for a while. “Yeah, but what about the other people inside? The owner and the waitresses?” Crichton points out. The realisation then visibly appears upon Aeryn’s face. “Aw, Hell your right!” Aeryn states. The camera cuts to the front side of the Diner as Crichton and Aeryn look at the damage caused by the gas explosion. The whole front side of the diner has caved in and whoever was on the inside didn’t look like they were getting out in one piece. Crichton and Aeryn had first been wary of coming round the front, but they had found the army helicopter deserted. Meaning that Fleming had gone inside the diner, and Crichton suspected he went in after he heard gun shots. “We can’t do anything for anyone now! Nobody could have survived that!” Crichton states sombrely. Aeryn nods in agreement. “Yeah. Besides we’ve got a Pay Per View to get to!” Aeryn replies. Crichton simply looks at her and then back at the destroyed building. “How come every time I get into trouble, stuff blows up?” Crichton rhetorically questions as the camera fades to black. And Now… The mood in the car was sombre to say the least and the silence was almost unbearable, but neither he nor Aeryn planned to break the silence anytime soon. They both had things to consider, things to ponder, mainly about all of the carnage they left behind. Immediately after the accident they had discussed a few things, but after that discussion a silence period of one hour and counting developed. After this they both had to figure out their feelings of the recent events and re-evaluate whether what they were doing was right and just, but this was always the way when innocents were killed. Aeryn figured that the diner owner, his wife and his daughter, who was the waitress, had been destroyed by the blast. They mourned for them, but didn’t have the heart to search through the wreckage for survivors, because they knew there was only one outcome: Total Destruction. On the bright side, they’re tormentor, the mysterious Mr. Fleming, was also killed in the explosion. Crichton knew the army would eventually send some other government agent, whose expertise is hunting down ‘special cases’ with no regard for the law whatsoever, but at least Fleming’s death would give them a little time for rest and relaxation. A little time to prepare for his up coming TV title match with The Pharaoh on Tuesday in Washington. It was a long drive to Washington and Crichton wasn’t looking forward to it at all, mainly because the atmosphere between himself and Aeryn was uncomfortable to say the least and would continue until they talked about the situation fully. Crichton wasn’t looking forward to that either, and so the uncomfortable feeling would remain for the entire journey. This was only the minor thing playing upon his mind at the moment though, the main thing scared him beyond belief and he couldn’t believe he even housed the thoughts or was even capable of housing these thoughts. Ever since the accident at the service station diner, an inner battle had been developing within Crichtons’s psyche that would not be undone easily. On one side there was the part of Crichton that felt guilt and sorrow for the victims of his incompetence and Crichton solely agreed with this side of him, but on the other side, his subconscious had decided that he did in fact enjoy the carnage created at the diner and indeed wanted more carnage similar to this. Crichton had been forced to do some less than pleasant things whilst he was in the army, but nothing like the destruction that he was responsible for at the service station diner. Crichton was almost disgusted that a little part of him enjoyed his actions and wanted more. Crichton also knew that this side was winning the Inner battle and he felt his mood change. He now was craving for violence and destruction; he was almost becoming malevolent. Crichton as sure as hell didn’t want let Aeryn in on his little secret, because she would confirm his suspicions, that he was losing his mind. ************** It had been two hours since the bullet hit the gas canisters and the explosion soon ensued. Most of the fires within the building had ceased, through their own volition and the most dangerous looking thing surrounding the building was the jet-black smoke, the by-product of the fire. The movement at the far edge of the destroyed building is barely noticeable at first, but becomes ever more noticeable as a rather large piece of concrete is tossed to one side and two bruised bodies emerge from rubble. Figures stumble away from the wreckage and collapse once more, not far from their starting point. The clothes of these survivors are all torn and their features are masked by black smoke residue. As a resultant of the smoke they are coughing uncontrollably. Mr. Fleming gets to his feet after his final spluttering cough and pulls the other man up. “You’re the Diner owner right?” Fleming questions, with a plan developing within his mind. “Cough…cough…Yeah…My wife and daughter are still in there!” The diner owner states trying to make his way back into the rubble. Fleming holds him back and inattentively shakes his head. “I am sorry, sir, but your daughter and wife did not survive the blast, I checked on them. Fleming lied. The diner owner begins to shake is head defiantly, as tears begin to roll down his face. “Wha..what happened?” The man questions. “Well that man we were chasing when we came in, he’s escaped from the army for some unknown reason and he will do anything and I mean anything to prevent himself going back. Needless to say he is very dangerous.” Fleming explains. “Have you ever gotten close to killing him yet?” The diner owner questions. Fleming shakes his head once more. “We are not allowed to kill him, we have to capture him and take him in for court martial” Fleming lies once again. “I see” The diner owner declares. ************* The mood in the car was no better over two hours into their journey to Washington, still not a word had been spoken between Crichton and Aeryn, still none of them planned to break the painful silence anytime soon and still the inner battle was raging inside Crichton’s head. He really didn’t want to think about it at the moment and the alternative was to break the silence and reinforce the uncomfortable feeling between he and Aeryn. However, the inner battle wasn’t going well for his conscience and the dark impulses had started to get the better of him. “So, have you any tapes of the guy I’m fighting this Tuesday?” Crichton inquired, hoping Aeryn would partake in the conversation. Aeryn visibly breaks her thinking mode and looks at Crichton with a puzzled look upon her face. “Wha…what was that?” She questions. “I just said, have I any tapes to study on this Pharaoh guy our match this Tuesday?” Crichton asks for the second time. A sudden realisation appears on Aeryn’s face and she begins to smile. “Yeah, but because we’re on the road all of the time, the officials simply sent them to our locker room. We’ll be able to watch them when we get there.” Aeryn explains. “We’ve both seen him in action, do you think he’s good?” Crichton questions. Aeryn moves her head to one side, as if to be pondering the question intently. “Yeah he is very good. After all he is the Television champion and he did beat Akiyama for that title.” Aeryn informs. Crichton sighs. “Damn. So he is good then? Well, beating Akiyama don’t mean he’s good Aeryn!, but I will be pulling out all of the stops for this one.” Crichton declares. Then a distressed look etches itself upon Crichton’s face as he looks down one of the alleyways of the city. Crichton couldn’t recall which city they were in, but knew it was still a long way to Washington. Crichton looks on as three guys mug another man. This was the turning point for his inner battle, the subconscious advocating a more sadistic side to Crichton came forth and possessed his actions. Crichton pulls over to the curb and turns the engine off. Aeryn looks at his with a questioning look upon her face. “Why are you stopping?” She asks. Crichton looks at her with a wild look in his eye. “I’m gonna teach a few guys a lesson, they’ll never forget.” Crichton answers, opening the car door and stepping out into the road. Aeryn soon follows her man as he heads in the fowl smelling alleyway in which the man is being mugged. As soon as Crichton enters the alleyway and the smell of piss brings back all of the unwanted memories, from the time when he had short term amnesia a month before. He stands there a while and the muggers don’t show any interest in him as they continue to beat the helpless victim. “You know, you guys should find a better pass time, because your hopeless at this.” Crichton declares, successfully attempting to gain the muggers attention. “Listen man, we ain’t got no problem with you at all, so why don’t you just turn around and pretend you didn’t see a thing!” One of the muggers suggests. Crichton shakes his head a little. “Sorry no can do. Not while an innocent is in danger.” Crichton declares. Crichton was even starting to believe in some of the bullshit that was spouting out of his mouth. He cared very little about the poor victim, in reality he was just trying to pick a fight and by the looks of it he was successful. He had chosen these three candidates because they were bad people, the inner battle was still raging and Crichton’s conscience would never let him harm an innocent. Three muggers then instantly let their grasp of the victim fade and they all turn to face Crichton. The former victim takes his chance and flee’s, further into the maze of alleyways. A smile breaks across Crichton’s face, as the realisation presents itself to him, he now had a platform to release his primal fury and he didn’t want to disappoint the muggers, they started the day wanting a good fight. Crichton swiftly places his body in his martial arts stance, awaiting the first attack. The man standing on the left of the apparent ring-leader steps forward, grinning knowing that he is at least two times bigger than his opponent. The mugger pulls a punch, Crichton swiftly avoids the blow, by moving temporarily to his left, dodging the punch completely. Now that the mugger was off balance he struck, with a short left hand, setting up the mugger, immediately following this up with a hefty right hand, both shots directly on target, with the muggers jaw. The mugger falls lifelessly to the floor, and Crichton continues the punishment and unleashes more of his boundless fury, by booting the fallen mugger in the gut over and over again, until one of the other muggers steps forward to fight. Before the mugger can attack however, Crichton unleashes a martial arts kick which knocks the mugger back against the wall where he hits his head and enters the dark warmth of unconsciousness. Crichton looks at the third and final mugger with the wild rage showing in his eyes and the mugger turns and runs away. After the encounter with the thugs, Crichton’s conscience temporarily ceases his wild rage and leaves Crichton feeling remorse for his actions. “Crichton, I know there is something wrong, you must tell me. I might be able to help you!” Aeryn pleads Crichton begins to shake his head. “No. I cannot reveal what is happening to me, but I can tell you that I am sorry and one day I will tell you what occurred on this day.” Crichton explains. Aeryn simply shakes her head in disgust and storms away into the car. ************* Fleming’s plan had being developing in his mind ever since he pulled the diner owner out of the destroyed building. He planned to use the diner owner’s rage to persuade him to confront and capture John Crichton. The plan would work convincingly, because although Fleming had his own way of tracking Crichton down, Crichton always spotted the soldiers coming for him and he now recognised Fleming. The drive out of Nevada and into the city was almost over and Fleming decided that it was time to use his manipulation skills to gain the diner owner’s (Dan Lyons) trust and use him to capture Crichton. “You know Dan, I could use your help capturing the man behind the diners’ destruction, if your interested in revenge.” Fleming declares. Dan nods in agreement. “Why me?” Lyons inquires. “Because Crichton always sees us coming for some reason, and if you attack him he’ll never suspect a thing.” Fleming explains. “Well Mr. Fleming that seems very acceptable, very acceptable indeed.” Lyons replies. “Excellent!” Fleming simply states. Lyons was secretly hoping Fleming would offer him this opportunity and since they met, suspect that he would ask for his help. However, Lyons wasn’t planning on playing along forever, because when he caught up to the man called John Crichton, he wasn’t going to capture him, he was going to kill him!
