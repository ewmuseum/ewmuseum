Bob Vurdo can wait. First, Scientology needs to get school by BYRONTOLOGY!

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/3/02 'I like SO love you all-zzies!' We open in the office of Her Commissionership, Nellie Bannon-Dahl as she addresses her faithful workers. Ultimate Shoot Fighting Championships, it's odd to be a member of her religion and be a figure of authority in this federation. Especially her husband, Xander Bannon-Dahl, who sits in the back corner going over tax-cut forms. Nellie, wearing a white nun's outfit with a very uncomfortable chastity belt underneath, cheers on her workers as she gives them money to go on a shopping spree. They all parade out of the office, happy about the management. (You can tell this is a complete parody, huh?) Nellie:'I love helping the worker-zzies. Being nice to others RULES! I am TOTALLY glad we switched from that punk-ass Scientology to Byrontology. Our lives-zzies have been so fulfilling now.' Xander:'Yeah-zzies. I got that Japanese fighter the championship match he's been working so hard for. And you now Clive? Well, he's had this negative Burned Guy stigma attached to him for too long-zzies, so I'm springing for reconstructive surgery! And guess what, we've been invited to he and Emily's wedding-zzies!' Nellie:'Oh, it's so nice to see those to lovebirds tie the knot-zzies.' Xander gets up and stretches. He walks over from his seat at his plain desk through the sparsely furnished room (containing nothing else but a poster advertising the next World Puzzle championships and a list of the rules of Byrontology) and hugs Nellie. He can't really feel her through the thick white robes covering his body, but it's a thoughtful gesture anyway. Xander:'I was thinking, maybe we should go on our second honeymoon-zzies. Maybe we could even sleep in the same bed this time, my little Poontang Princess.' Nellie:'Now, Xandi, you know any form of skin-to-skin touching is strickly against the rule-zzies in Byrontology.' Xander nods his head and releases his embrace. Xander:'I know, my freaky-deeky flamingo. I'm just joking. After all, they're good rules-zzies. Byron Calver wouldn't still be living a long and healthy social life for our sins if these weren't the best standards to live by.' The eyes of Xander and Nellie shoot over to the 4 Pillars of Byrontology. They state: 1) Chastity MalesYou may NOT touch a female's boobs with any part of your body. You may NOT touch a vagina with any part of your body at all. In fact, you may not have direct skin contact with a member of the opposite gender in any way. You will not refer to 'Gender' as sex because sex is a dirty word. Keep eye contact at all times. Wear heavy clothing and body condoms. FemalesYou may not touch a man's penis with any part of your body. In fact, you may not have direct skin contact with a member of the opposite gender in any way. You will not refer to 'Gender' as sex because sex is a dirty word. Keep eye contact at all times. Wear heavy clothing and full body condoms. 2) -zzies You must randomly add '-zzies' to the end of words that you say. There's no reason for it, but just do it. 3) Intelligence You must be smart. Competing in World Puzzle Championships helps. 4) Kindness You must be kind to others. This includes redheaded firesnatches, jerks, Stormbringers, and everyone else that the popular community likes to make fun of. Hosting chats is a plus, or at least being the figurehead of the chat. Nellie:'They're good rules.' Xander:'Indeed. To this day I'm a virgin. Watch!' Xander opens up the door to the side of the poster. Inside, a naked Jennifer Lopez spreads her legs for him. Xander remains perfectly unaroused and points. Xander:'What's that?' He closes the door and turns back to Nellie. Xander:'See?' Through the door, Derek Gilliam comes in. He's dressed like a gangster, but he's a Shakespearian in speech and action. Gilliam:'Hark! Tis my fellows in possession, good morrow! How doth thou day receive thee?' Nellie:'You SO talk funny. But I-zzies accept and respect our differences.' Gilliam:'I say, whateth the fucketh dost thy wench speak of, Lord Hossman?' Xander:'Um... Here's-zzies some money.' Reaching into his robe pockets, Xander draws out many $100 bills and gives them to Gilliam. Gilliam:'My word, this would be, as the elders say: Sweet-eth! Xander walks over to the door. Xander:'Now, if you'll excuse me, I have to make a trip to read to the local orphans-zzies and check up on that American Hero chap who's on paid injured leave.' Nellie and Gilliam wave to the departing Xander. Xander:'Oh, and while I'm gone, give those black workers the payraise they deserve....'
