Smoke tries to get rid of the rust...

18 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 18, 2003 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up in yet another section of Atlantic City. It seems that it’s down near the boardwalk and near the gambling sector of town. Several skyscrapers are again seen, and the scene narrows down to a single street in the section of town. The familiar black limo that Smoke has been riding in lately pulls up in front of what seems like an old dilapidated building. The limo passenger door opens and Smoke climbs out of it and steps onto the broad sidewalk. He’s wearing a pair blue sweat pants and a new T-shirt of his that reads “Got Pot?” on the front of it in gray letters with a marijuana leaf in front of it. Smoke walks up to the front of the limo and the scene changes to a zoom in of the front window of the limo rolling down. The driver looks at Smoke and asks him... Driver: Do you want me to stay here and wait for you to finish? {{{{Smoke shakes his head no and pulls out a wad of cash from his pocket and takes a few hundred dollar bills from the wad and sticks the rest in his pocket. He then hands the driver those bills...}}}} Semore Smoke: No way John, just take this and head up the road and have some fun. If you get in trouble at all then tell them that you’re my driver, that’ll shut them up. Now, go have some fun, I’ll call for you when I’m finished with today’s session. {{{{The driver smiles as he sticks the cash in his pocket...}}}} Driver: Yes sir! {{{{Smoke steps back and watches as his limo drives off into the distant sunset on this beautiful Tuesday night. He then turns around and heads towards the dilapidated building and opens up the front door. He walks in and searches for something on the near wall, near the door, he finds what he’s looking for, and hits the switch to cut the lights on in the room. When the lights come on, you see a huge gym room! Everything in the room looks brand new though, even if the building doesn’t. In the middle of the room is most likely the most crucial part of the set up, it’s a wrestling ring that looks to be about 12 feet by 15 feet in size. It’s definitely smaller then the 20 feet by 15 feet ring that PCW uses, but it serves it’s purposes. The most notable parts of the ring are it’s black cables that are used as ropes, and the ring apron that reads “ECW” on the side. It’s obvious an old ring that Semore Smoke had bought from ECW when it fell under. He walks up to the ring and sets his bag down right outside of it. He then rolls into the ring and looks around it with a smile on his face...}}}} Semore Smoke: Been a long time since I stood alone in a ring like this... How I miss the action... but tomorrow, I’ll have a chance to revisit that action, and try to put a small roadblock out of the way as well. That roadblock going by the name of AJ Trinidad... {{{{Smoke reaches into the pockets of his sweat pants and brings out a blunt. He smells the blunt first and then sticks it in his mouth. He continues to reach in the same pocket and brings out a lighter. He lights his marijuana blunt and takes a huge puff off the magical thing to try and calm whatever nervousness he is feeling. A huge bang is heard and Smoke turns around to see who it is. He sees another man standing at the door and the bang he had heard was the door slamming....}}}} Semore Smoke: Shit! Man you scared the fuck out of me! {{{{The camera zooms on the face of the man at the door and it’s Gideon Poe of PWC!}}}} Gideon Poe: Ha! I’m sorry about that man. I got your phone call and I thought I’d come and train with you, considering I’m in PWC, which is locally based here in New Jersey, so I always use a good work out. Semore Smoke: Good, I’m ready for one right now. I really need to work on getting this back into better shape, and the most important thing that needs working is my high flying skills, I have a feeling that they’ll come in handy tomorrow night. {{{{Gideon has walked up to the ring and he’s stepping up the old wooden steps to climb into the ring. Smoke puts his blunt out with his fingers and then drops it over the ropes of the ring to his bag below. Gideon steps through the cables and stares at Smoke with a grin on his face...}}}} Gideon Poe: Ok, well, I’m ready when you are, I’m just gonna let you hit a few spots on me to check and see what you can do. {{{{Gideon walks up to Smoke and Smoke sits there and thinks on what he wants to do first...}}}} Semore Smoke: Well, I’ll start off light, how about a simple suplex. {{{{Gideon shrugs and Smoke hooks him into a front face lock and lifts that extra arm over his head. He then lifts Gideon up in a vertical hold and holds him in the hold for a few seconds. Smoke walks forward a bit and then falls backwards hard with the slam. Smoke gets up and helps Gideon up to his feet as well...}}}} Gideon Poe: Damn, you’re stronger then an ox! Semore Smoke: Tell me about it. I’ve been working out as part of my rehab and I might have lost twenty pounds from my last time in a PCW ring, but I can definitely lift a lot more then I could before. Gideon Poe: That’s a good thing, gives you an upper hand against those powerhouses, both speed and strength. {{{{Smoke smiles and then allows Gideon to walk up to him again...}}}} Semore Smoke: This time I want to do a T-Bone suplex. {{{{Gideon nods and Smoke hooks in for the T-Bone and lifts with all his strength, he then slams Gideon back hard with the suplex. Gideon gets up and shakes off the slam...}}}} Gideon Poe: Damn, with a T-Bone suplex like that, you could really do some damage. Semore Smoke: Sorry man, I tried to make it not hurt as much as it would have. Well, I promise there isn’t that much more I want to run, just another suplex move and my set-up and finisher. {{{{Gideon nods again in understanding and then walks up to Smoke again holding his back for a bit, but then he just rubs whatever pain he’s feeling away…}}}} Semore Smoke: I’ll need you to turn around, I’m going to try and hit a German suplex, this’ll be the most painful move for me to hit most likely, since I have to fall back straight on my back and neck. {{{{Gideon turns around and lets Smoke hook around him with the waste lock. Smoke then lifts up and slams Gideon down onto the mat with the suplex move and bridges for a second, maybe checking to see if he could bridge without much pain. Smoke lets go and crawls around to his knees and then up to his base. Gideon is up as well again and he looks unshaken from the move…}}}} Semore Smoke: That worked pretty well, I didn’t feel any pain in the move what so ever… was it effective enough against you? Gideon Poe: Yeah, it’s a good move, and if you can hit it like that all the time, then you’ll have no problem. {{{{Smoke nods taking in the positive criticism…}}}} Semore Smoke: That’s the only problem, I know that things are working now, but once I get in that ring, I’m worried that I may not hold up all that well, I just have to trust in myself more I suppose. But… anyways, time to move on this session, it seems like time is flying. Gideon Poe: I know what you mean. {{{{Smoke points toward the top rope and says…}}}} Semore Smoke: Time for the good ole Smoke Alarm, I haven’t hit this Tornado DDT in a long time. Gideon Poe: Just be careful with the both of us. Semore Smoke: Oh I will, I may throw in another move by the way, after this one, just to check and see if I can still fly. {{{{Smoke walks up and sits on top of the top rope. Gideon walks up to the corner and stands there. Smoke hooks in a front face lock or a DDT hold as most people call it and stands up on the second rope. He then jumps off the second rope and twist around into the DDT! Both men go down hard onto the mat, but both seem to get up almost instantly and look ok and not hurt at all…}}}} Semore Smoke: Are you ok? Gideon Poe: Yeah, thanks for tucking my head away that could have hurt a lot. Semore Smoke: No problem. {{{{Smoke points to the mat and says…}}}} Semore Smoke: Just two more moves, I need you lay down for this next one. Gideon Poe: Ok, no problem. Are you going to jump off with a splash or something? Semore Smoke: Not off the top rope, now, just a springboard moonsault. {{{{Gideon lays on the mat and nods his head…}}}} Gideon Poe: Oh, well I’m ready if you are. Semore Smoke: Ok, this won’t hurt a bit… {{{{Smoke runs and jumps over Gideon’s body, he then hits the ropes and jumps onto the second rope, he then springboards off and does a back flip moonsault landing perfectly on Gideon Poe! Smoke stands up after hitting the move and then helps Gideon up to his feet…}}}} Gideon Poe: I knew you weren’t going to hurt me, you didn’t have to reassure me. Semore Smoke: Ha! Who said I was reassuring you? I was reassuring me that nothing would happen to me, I knew you were ok. {{{{The both of them chuckle for a bit and then Smoke looks at him as to say it’s time to finish the job though…}}}} Semore Smoke: Time for me to hurry up and finish this, I think it’s been about thirty minutes or so and I didn’t have all that much time before I had to get to my flight. Just my finisher is left, the double under hook powerbomb. {{{{Gideon nods and walks up to Smoke, Gideon bends down in between Smoke’s legs and Smoke hooks both arms in a double under hook, as if he were going for a pedigree or something like that. He then lifts up and with all his strength, he hooks the powerbomb hold in, he then slams Gideon down into the mat, but tries to not put too much strength behind it, making the move sort of sloppy, but effective. Gideon gets up after the move and stares at Smoke with a smile…}}}} Gideon Poe: You didn’t have to take any off of it, I could have taken it. Semore Smoke: No, there’s a reason why it’s my finisher and not just a move of mine, I can almost drive someone through the ring with that move if I do it with all my strength. {{{{Gideon nods…}}}} Gideon Poe: Oh… Ok… I understand then… {{{{Smoke looks at a clock on the wall and sees that it’s about 5:30 PM EST…}}}} Semore Smoke: Well, I better call my driver and get him back here, I’ve got to get to the airport by 6:30 for my flight to San Jose. I wish you luck with PWC my friend. Gideon Poe: And I wish you luck with PCW. {{{{Smoke steps out of the ring and walks over to his bag. He puts the blunt in one of the front zipper pockets so it’s not wasted and pulls out his cell phone from the bag. He dials a number on the phone and hits “send”. He then puts the phone to his ear and waits for an answer, he gets one and then says…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yeah, John, I’m ready for you to come pick me up. {{{{Smoke waits for a second…}}}} Semore Smoke: Tell the girls that you’ve got to go, I need you here now. You don’t want to get me late or angry, do you? {{{{Smoke waits again for a second…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yeah, that’s what I thought. Now get your ass down here now. {{{{Smoke hangs up the phone and then turns to Gideon with a smile on his face…}}}} Semore Smoke: Limo drivers, ha! What can you do, you can’t live with them, and you can’t live without them… {{{{The scene fades on Smoke’s grinning face as he knows that he’s ready to get back into the ring and face AJ Trinidad in a match on Wednesday Turmoil…}}}}
