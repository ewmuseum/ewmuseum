Mentally & Physically- Pt 2

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 2, 2002 ::It’s around 8:20 p.m. on a Monday Night. Shawn and James have left Shawn’s old house in Palm Beach, Florida. And now has one more thing to do, to get totally mentally prepared for the upcoming important week in his young PCW career. James and Shawn are located in Atlanta, Georgia. Just about 24 hours after being in his old house in Florida. Shawn is the only one, out of the two, that knows why they are here. James only knows that Shawn wants to get rid of something of his past and finally put it behind him. They are heading down a long road, once again in James beat up car. Shawn is on the passenger side, and James is driving again. They’ve spent most of the day in the car making it to Atlanta to take care of this problem. Now, in the same clothes they were in yesterday, they make their way threw Atlanta. The conversation starts as James opens it up:: “So. .uh. . why the hell aren’t you telling me where we are going?” James says for about the millionith time in the view of Shawn Mosely. “James” Shawn says as he takes a quick glance at him. “This is just something I have to take care of” James just gives a big sigh as he seems quite disappointed in the Golden One. “I’m the one that thought this whole getting rid of the past is the key to your success idea came from” James says enthusiastically. “Yeah. So you’ll understand. *Pauses* Can’t we just talk about PCW or something?” Shawn asks. James looks at Shawn and then back at the road. “I can’t believe you Shawn” James replies. James and Shawn are quietly for about the next two minutes. Then James opens his mouth, again. “Alright. Fine! What? Will you beat Andy Murray?” James says out of nowhere. “Huh?” Shawn replies. “You said you wanted to talk about something else.” James says. “Yeah. But why would I want to know from you if I could beat a jobber? That’s like asking if PCW is better then OSW. The answer will, and always will be yes. It’s just the pure facts of life. And now if you could stop asking me sarcastic questions, maybe we can get down to business” Shawn replies. James stops at a stop sign. No cars are anywhere near them, but he still takes the time to turn to Shawn and let out a big smirk.“What?” Shawn asks. “It’s finally happening Shawn” James replies as he turns the car onto the new street which will lead to the place where Shawn wants to go. “Uh. . . whats finally happening, James?” Shawn asks. “It’s coming back” James replies. Shawn looks to be getting frustrated as he wants James to get to the point. “Ok.. .what is coming back?!” Shawn asks with emotion. “Your cocky, arrogant Shawn Mosely ways.” James says. “What? Where’d this come into your head?” Shawn asks with great confusion. “Shawn. Did you hear yourself before? You referred to Murray as a jobber.” James says with a smirk on his face and his mind attuned to where they are going, still. Shawn also gives a great big smirk as he just realized what he said a few minutes ago when James brings it to his attention. “Yeah. I guess this confronting my past thing is really working.” Shawn replies. “And when you see it working, it will only get better. Your on your way to success Shawn, your on your way.” James says with a smile on. James realizes that he is closing in on the number of the house that Shawn wanted to stop at. “So, we’re almost here Shawn. I’m I coming in with you?” James asks as his head follows the number of each house they ride past. “Heh. . no. This is something I must do alone. You can wait in the car.” Shawn replies. “What’s that number you gave me again? 517. . 519?” James asks. “No. 515 Hyers Street.” Shawn replies. James nods his head and sees the house number he was looking for. He turns into the driveway and looks down it with amazement. The driveway, or should I say road was extremely long. With all the small tree’s around it was impossible for James and Shawn to even see the house from the beginning of the driveway.James drives the car slowly up the driveway, as they eventually come in eye view of the roof of the house. They keep getting closer until they edge around a small corner which leads to a view of the entire giant mansion. James sits there in aw of what he sees. Shawn, on the other hand, has seen this before and looks like he wants to get straight down to business with someone in there. “I want to meet this person. He has to be rich.” James says with a smile on. “It’s a girl.” Shawn replies. “A girl?! What are you talking about?” James ask with shock. “Don’t sound so shocked. I had a very close girlfriend before I left for South America.*Pauses* Her name was Stacy.” Shawn replies. “I am sort of shocked. But Shawn, please don’t tell me you came back here to heal the wounds.” James says. James obviously doesn’t want no girl ruining the potential Shawn has. But even more of what the money he can make. “Of course not. I just want to get things straight so I can put it behind me and concentrate on getting my new life back together.” Shawn says with a serious look on his face. “Alright. Let’s go.” James quickly says as he tries to get out of the car before Shawn could say another word. “Hold On! Your not coming. Wait!” Shawn replies with the same serious look as a minute ago. James nods his head with a disgruntled look on his face and proceeds to sit back down. Shawn finally gets up and leaves the car and proceeds to walk to the main entrance of the house. He stands about 25 feet away from the car as he looks back at James before ringing the doorbell a few times. He looks at the door, and then back at James. The door is finally answered by a woman. She’s in her late 40’s as some of the grey hair indicates, but she is also well dressed in clothes, and in expensive jewelry. Shawn turns around to greet the woman. “O. Hello. Can I help you?” The woman asks. “Uhh. . I’m looking for Stacy.” Shawn replies politely. The woman looks at Shawn with a curious look. “Uh. .”. . .the woman is at a lack of words for some reason. “You alright, mam?” Shawn asks politely again. The woman’s eyes begin to fill up with tears. Shawn senses something bad has happen and asks. “What’s wrong? Did something happen to Stacy?” Shawn asks with a demand for an answer. The lady wipes off a tear that was able to break loose from her eye and make it’s way down her cheek. Then lashes out at the young Shawn Mosely. “She left. She disappeared! We haven’t heard from her in 6 months. . all because of a guy. . .” The woman says as she begins to cry. ::Shawn, meanwhile, stairs at the woman is pure shock. The woman backs up until her back meets one of the walls in the house. She grabs her face and just collapses on the ground in which her head is on her knees as tears flow out of her eyes. Shawn runs his right hand threw his long dirty blonde hair and gives a big sigh. He gives the woman a little wave goodbye and begins to walk back to the car. Is this finally past Shawn? I don’t know, neither does Shawn. But, he now makes his way to Arizona to face Andy Murray::
