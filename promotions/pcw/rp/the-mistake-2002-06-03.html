The mistake

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-3-02 The room is small. Its tiles are white and a sickly looking green. It contains a small, uncomfortable looking chair in one corner. Opposite from the chair is a flat, top looking seat, if you will. The walls are painted white and are almost bare. A few things hang here and there. Next to the door is a counter with a sink and various medical tools. Brandi paces up and down this small room, growing more and more impatient as time goes on. She stops in front of the counter and picks up a small rubber hammer used to test reflexes. The door opens and Brandi drops the rubber hammer in surprise. It bounces off the cold tiles a few times, before finally coming to a rest. An older doctor walks in the room, clipboard in hand. Brandi quickly picks up the hammer and replaces it on the counter. The doctor squints through his thick glasses, trying to read the carts he’s holding. Doctor- Why don’t you have a seat? Brandi sits down at the edge of the flat seat. She looks around the room nervously. The hospital gown she had worn just earlier, lay in a corner. Brandi was glad to get rid of it and get her normal clothes of blue jeans and a black t-shirt back on. It seems to Brandi as though the doctor is taking forever to read through the charts. She starts swinging her feet nervously, without realizing it. One leg comes to a rest on the metal cabinet beneath the table. Both Brandi and the doctor jump at the loud crash. Doctor- Please, Miss. Just sit still. I’m almost done. After what seems like an eternity, the doctor takes off his glasses and sets the charts down. He leans up against the counter and looks Brandi up and down. Doctor- Well, it looks like everything is fine with your examination. You’re fit to wrestle. Brandi lets out a sigh of relief and the doubt rushes away from her face. Brandi- You don’t know how good it is to hear you say that. This whole time I was wondering what would happen if you didn’t pass me. I’m just glad I’ll be able to step in the ring again. Doctor- Yeah, you’ll be able to wrestle Jaden after all. Brandi- Good. I’ve been looking forward to it. It should be a good match for me to get back into the swing of things. Doctor- Now, unless there’s something else you wanted to tell me, you’re free to leave. Brandi thinks about it for a second. The doctor heads for the door, expecting everything to be fine. He’s just hoping everything is so he can get home early. Brandi- Actually, there is one thing. The doctor looks disappointed, but doesn’t dare show this to Brandi as he turns around to face her. Doctor- Nothing major, I hope? Brandi- Nah, I don’t think so. It’s just…I get these headaches a lot. Do you think there’s anything that you can give me for them? Like a painkiller or something? Doctor- I’m sure there’s something I can get you. Hang on and I’ll write out a prescription for you. The doctor leaves the room once again, leaving a worried Brandi sitting alone on the examination table. She jumps down and moves across the room to the chair, where she sits for only a moment. Brandi- I hope he doesn’t use my headaches against me in my physical or anything. I’m coming back to PCW. The doctor comes back into the room with a small piece of paper in his hand. He gives it to Brandi and holds the office door open for her. Doctor- Just give this to one of the girls on the way out of here, and they’ll fill it here for you. Brandi- Alright, thanks. Brandi heads towards the front of the building. She looks down at the scribbled note. It’s hard to tell, but it seems like the note reads “nathylenedioxytian”. Brandi hands the note to a chubby looking woman up at the front desk. She reads it quickly, then disappears into the back. She comes back out with a small bottle of pills, which she hands to Brandi. The bottle reads “methylenedioxytian”. It also has a few warning stickers on it, which read “Federal law prohibits the use of this drug by anyone other then for who it was prescribed” and “May cause drowsiness or hallucinations if not used as prescribed”. Brandi doesn’t pay any attention to the labels. She just quickly shoves it in her purse. Nurse- Take exactly one of these a day. Take no more, and no less then one. Understand? Brandi- Yep. Nurse- Ok. That’ll be fifty dollars. Brandi- Fifty dollars for some freaking painkillers?! She digs through her purse and sighs before handing the money across the counter to the woman. Nurse- Thanks. Have a good day. Brandi mumbles something and walks back out into the street. It’s a typical day in July. Very few clouds are in sight, and the air is hot. People push past one another on the sidewalks, attempting to get to their destination quicker. Meanwhile, on the streets, cars are backed up and drivers are growing more and more angry. The air fills with the sounds of road rage, people cursing, and horns blaring. The automatic sliding doors spring open and Brandi walks inside the nicely air conditioned store. She grabs a shopping cart and pushes it out of the other customers’ way. Brandi pulls a list out of her purse and reads it over carefully. She feels one of her headaches coming on, so she grabs the bottle of pills from her purse and takes two out before putting the bottle back in its place. She pushes the cart towards a small refrigerator in the front of the store and cringes at the sound of a squeaky wheel. Brandi pops the pills in her mouth and grabs a bottle of water from the refrigerator. She takes a long drink of it, before throwing it into the cart. People hurry to the checkout lanes, eager to get home with their purchases. Brandi looks at the clock on the wall. Brandi- Five o’clock. I should probably get started on dinner pretty soon. Brandi weaves her way in and out of the shopping carts and angry people in line. She makes her way to the produce section of the store and looks at her list again. Brandi- Bananas, apples, oranges, and grapes. Brandi pushes her cart around the many fruit stands choosing which fruit looks the best before carefully placing them in the shopping cart. Brandi stops the cart and looks around the large aisle of the store. It’s empty except for a hunched over woman who appears to be in her 70s. She’s leaning on her cane, picking through some apples. Brandi shrugs and keeps pushing her cart towards the grapes. She suddenly stops again. Voice- Brrrrrrandiiiiii. Brrrrrrandiiiiii. Brandi looks up and down the aisle once again, but it is still empty except for the woman. She doesn’t appear to be calling to her, so Brandi keeps walking. Voice- Brrrrrrandiiiiii. I’m talking to youuuuuuu. Brandi stops once more. She leaves the cart where it’s at, and walks over to the old woman. Brandi taps her on the shoulder, and she turns around slowly. Woman- Yes? Brandi- Did you need something? Woman- No, I don’t think so. Thanks for the offer though. It’s good to see that you young people still care about us. Brandi looks at the woman, puzzled. Brandi- You didn’t just call me? Woman- No, I didn’t. Brandi- Are you sure? Woman- I think I’d know if I called you. Brandi looks confused. She turns around and walks back towards her cart. Woman- Are you ok? Brandi ignores the woman’s question and tries to continue on with her shopping. She stops her shopping cart at the grapes, and picks through them, trying to find the right bunch. She turns around to place the grapes in the shopping cart, when she hears it again. Voice- Brrrrrandiiiiii. Brandi drops the grapes and stiffens up, straight as a board. The voice seemed to be coming from right behind her, so she turns around slowly. No one is there. Voice- Dowwwn here, Brrrrrandiiiiii. Brandi looks down at the grapes. Voice- You’re going to lose, Brrrrrandiiiii. You and I both knowww it. Brandi is shocked to find Jaden’s face on each one of the grapes. She falls backwards and bumps into the shopping cart, sending it rolling down the aisle. The grapes seem to be laughing at her. Voice- What’s the matter, Brrrrrandiiiii? Surprised to seeeee me? Brandi closes her eyes and rubs them. When she opens them, the faces are still there. Voice- Yessss, I’m still here. Brandi approaches the grapes slowly, cautiously. Brandi- What do you want? Voice- I jussst wanted to tell youuu that you that you are doooooomed. You have no chance on Friday night. You will lose. Brandi- No, I won’t. I will beat you. The grapes laugh. Voice- Oh, how little you knowwww. You can not beat me. Brandi punches one of the piles of grapes. The juice gushes through her fingers. She pulls her hand out to find that it is covered with grape juice, yet the faces are still there. Voice- You can’t get rid of meeeee that easy. Brandi grows frustrated and picks up a bunch of grapes, throwing them on the floor. She stomps on them repeatedly. She smiles at the sound they make when the squash under the weight of her feet. Voice- I’m stillllll here. Brandi turns back around and grabs another bunch of grapes, this time throwing them across the aisle. They crash into the wall and slide down to the floor, leaving a trail of purple on the white wall. She picks up another bunch, throwing them towards the back of the store. They hit the old woman in the back of the head. She grunts in shock. Before she realizes it, Brandi is throwing the grapes all around the store. Management quickly runs over to her and pries the grapes out of her hands. Brandi collapses to the floor, while management runs over to apologize to the woman. Brandi starts crying quietly to herself. Brandi- I’m…I’m sorry. I don’t know what happened. The grapes no longer have Jaden’s face on them, leaving Brandi to wonder if she’s gone crazy. Baggers quickly run to the aisle to help clean up the mess, and Brandi is led outside of the store by the management. One of them hands her a towel, which she uses to wipe the grape juice off her hands. Manager- What happened in there? Brandi- I…I don’t know. Manager- You caused us a lot of trouble. I’m sorry, Miss, but we can’t allow you to shop here ever again. Please stay away from our store. If you don’t, I’m afraid we’ll have to take legal action. Do you understand? Brandi nods her head. She still doesn’t understand what happened. Brandi- I’m sorry. Really I am. I just don’t know… The two managers look at each other oddly. They don’t know what to think about Brandi’s actions. Manager- Miss, I don’t know what happened in there, but I have to ask you to leave the premises. I’m really sorry. Brandi- It’s ok. I understand. Brandi still doesn’t know what to make of it all, but she turns around and starts the walk home anyway. Brandi- Jaden…
