Revenge

5 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-05-02 Akiyama gets in to the ring with the chair and cracks it over Bell's head as he has the sleeper hold locked on Sylo. The referee doesn't see this as he's concentrating on Ryckman and Johnson's fight. Akiyama slides out of the ring as Sylo covers Bell and calls for the referee to count. Dave: Akiyama just took out Lance Bell. Duke: Either he's out here because he has a problem with Lance Bell, or because he and Jay Sylo are forming an alliance, because he's just allowed Sylo to get the pin. Sylo pins Bell 1...2...3! ELIMINATED FROM THIS MATCH: LANCE BELL! Dave: The champion has been eliminated, which means that no matter what, we're going to have a new rising star champion by the time this match is over. Duke: You can thank Akiwakermarkerya for that! Jeff: Duke, if you don't know his name, just call him Akijobber, you'll remember that! Akiyama slides in to the ring again with the chair, and this time hits Steve Johnson over the head with it. Dave: Akiyama has just taken out Steve Johnson as well, what buisness does he have out here in a rising star contest? Jeff: Since rising stars are jobbers, and Akijobber is a jobber, it makes sense for him to be out here! Akiyama tells Sylo to pin Johnson, but unfortunately for Sylo and Akiyama, the referee has this time seen what Akiyama has done, and has also made the guess that Akiyama is helping Sylo, so he calls for the bell to eliminate Sylo via DQ. ELIMINATED FROM THIS MATCH BY DQ: JAY SYLO! ***Flash Forward*** I walked through the curtain with my head held low and my newly won Rising Star Championship slug over my left shoulder. The announcement of the ring announcer still ringing in my ears. 'THE WINNER OF THIS MATCH AND NEW RISING STAR CHAMPION: RIPPER RYCKMAN!' The crowd and their chants faded away as I made my way further into the depths of the arena. A few guys in the back, ring technicians, sound guys, even a few of the other wrestlers congratulated me on my victory as I headed towards my locker room. The feeling hadn’t hit me as hard as I was sure it would once my adrenaline stopped pumping through my veins. As I pushed back my locker room door I draped the Rising Star Championship over the back of a chair sitting next to the entrance. I walked over and sat on the bench. I picked up a bottle of water I had left there before my match. I took a big drink then poured the rest over my head. I still couldn’t believe what had happened. Jay Sylo the only guy I thought would be a challenge in this match got himself disqualified. How could this happen? How could I gain yet another victory over somebody by another disqualification? I had won my first match over Steve Johnson via disqualification. Now tonight Sylo got himself disqualified thanks to his buddy Akiyama. They first took out Bell the reigning Champion. Then Johnson which lead to the disqualification. Leaving me with the only thing I could do, pin Johnson and win the Rising Star Championship. I don’t really care how I win my matches. But d@mnit, I want to brutally punish them. I want to make them never forget who they stepped into the ring with. How am I suppose to do that if these cowards keep getting themselves disqualified? How am I suppose to prove I’m the brute force here in PCW? I can’t not without a clear cut brutal victory. I began to unlace my boots when I heard a knock on my door. “Come in.” I yelled. A man wearing a PCW logo shirt pushed the door open. I was unsure of what he wanted but I was sure it would be something I wouldn’t like. Maybe PCW has some sort of thing they had all their new Champions do. Sign autographs, promotion stuff, or whatever else they could think of. “Ryckman.” He called as if not knowing who I was. “I got a message for you.” Then what is it moron. “Well spill it. Can’t you see I’m trying to get undressed.” “Um, sorry.” He said. “This Friday on Fusion you have to defend your title.” Great. “Against who?” Another nobody. The man gulped. I imagined he thought I wouldn’t like his response. “Jay Sylo.” I could tell that my devilish grin scarred the man half to death. He backed up and hurried out the door which he had came. I couldn’t help but laugh. I couldn’t help but feel overjoyed. I’m going to get my hands on Jay Sylo, again. And this time… I think back to the match. Where my hands where around the neck of Steve Johnson. I was choking the life out of him. He was fighting, trying to get me to break the hold. What he or the fans don’t know was I wasn’t trying to win the match. I was trying to choke the life right out of him. He had put his hands on the Freaks jewels last Tuesday on Turmoil. A tactic that caused my sudden rage during tonight’s match. Now on Fusion next Friday Jay Sylo has to face that same rage and try to find someway to… SURVIVE… Surviving for Jay Sylo might mean nothing more than Akiyama. I wouldn’t put it past him. I wouldn’t expect anything less than for Jay Sylo to try and find a way out of this match. He has fear in his eyes. I had seen it tonight during our pay-per-view match up. March Madness may be gone. But the fear in Jay Sylo’s eyes will continue to grow with each passing day. Until we meet on Friday. That day Sylo might decide to stay home, hide behind his living room sofa. Jay Sylo is a coward, a coward that is hiding behind Akiyama. As I continued getting changed I thought of how confident Sylo was. How sure he was. Jay Sylo thought he had the Rising Star Championship in the bag. Sylo thought he could overcome his three opponents. He had his plan all layed out. Him and Akiyama set their plan into motion. The first stages had worked. Akiyama got to the ringside, he got a chair, and he took out the Champ Lance Bell. But then things started going wrong. Akiyama, Sylo’s so called master plan, failed when he got Sylo disqualified after hitting Johnson with the chair. Which did nothing less than lay the Rising Star Championship into my lap. Great plan, huh Sylo? I finish getting changed and look up at the clock on the wall. Thinking I got a few hours to kill. I can walk out and watch some of the on going action in the ring on one of the monitors backstage. After all sometime in the future I’d be facing some of these guys I’ll see here tonight. ***Flash Forward*** Instead of going to break, we cut to the back where Ripper Ryckman is giddy over his title win earlier in the evening. Dave: That's Ripper Ryckman! Jeff: Wasn't seeing this jobber once bad enough? Now we've gotta see him again. As Rip is walking, a figure runs into the picture and blindsides Rip, knocking him down. Dave: OH MY GOD!! THAT'S DAMION XAVIERS!! Jeff sighs Jeff: Another newbie jobber. Dave sighs Xaviers puts the boots to Ryckman. A plethora of security guards run into the picture to pull Damion off of Ripper. Dave: Damion Xaviers makes his debut in PCW with a bang by taking out the new Rising Star champion. Jeff: That's two jobbers fighting over a jobber belt. Who cares? Dave sighs Dave: Let's go to a quick break. ***Flash Forward*** Two security guards helped me back to my locker room where I now laid on the floor. A paramedic had just left after checking me out. He told me to just rest and try not to move around to much. But he didn’t see anything particularly wrong. I just had a knot on my head. Lay here and rest? Rest after someone put the boots to me? How in the h*ll did this? I didn’t get a look at their face. The coward did a hit and run. Scarred that if he stood up to me face to face that I’d rip his head off. That I would of freaked him right there in the backstage arena. But no he had to hit me from behind. And the medic expects me to lay here and not try and find the guy, bullsh!t. I pulled myself back to my feet. My head began to throb and pound as if my heart was in my head pounding against my brain with each beat. I found that once I was on my feet I had relative ease walking. I made my way out into the hallway. As I slowly walked down the corridor I found my first suspect walking towards me. “Hey! You who are you and what are you doing.?” He sharpness in my voice caused the man to jump. “Nobody… I mean I work for the sound crew.” He was a bit afraid but didn’t hightail it and run. “Can I do something for you?” “Damn Straight you can.” I put my hand to the back of my head. “Who in the h*ll took a cheap shot at me” He shook his head. “I don’t know but a few guys where talking about it back my the refreshment table.” He pointed down the hall. I pushed by him and pointed towards the direction that he had pointed. I picked up my pace as the anger raging inside of me made the pain num out a bit. Once I found the table I see to guys standing and talking as I over heard one of them as he looked at me. “There he is now.” I walked up to the guy and stood almost right in his face. “Who did it?” I sprayed spit into his face. “And where is he?” He looked at the guy standing just off to my side. “Hey, stupid don’t look at him. I asked you a f(censored)king question.” That brought his attention back to me. “I… I don… don’t know.” “What do you mean, don’t know?” “I… mea… mean I haven’t seen him around before.” He was visibly afraid. “If you don’t know they guy just say so. But you better remember which way he headed.” He pointed into a direction. “He went off towards the parking garage.” I turned and began walking towards the parking garage. As I had walked off I thought the guy made a smart @ss comment. But I had somebody else to catch at the moment. When I finally reached my destination I found a guy standing there smoking a cigarette. He looked at me as though he had seen what happened earlier. “Where is he?” He looked at me strange. “Who?” I grabbed him by the collar. You d@mn well know who. The f(censored)ker that attacked me from behind. He seemed to remember who real quick when he thought I was going to rip his head off. “He got into a car and pulled away about five minutes ago.” “Who was he?” He shrugs his shoulders. “I don’t know. Never seen him before.” I turned and headed back to my locker room. Thinking that somebody real soon was going to get an @ss whopping and be shown a whole new world of hurt. First Friday Fusion. Next… REVENGE…
