Self-Help Is The Last Help

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Self-Help Is The Last Help Featuring: Tim Walden Date: October 26, 2002 Two nemeses...One past, one present. One pathetically nice, the other a borderline psychotic. One I have never defeated, one who only now has made himself a thorn in my side...Both of whom share one common aspect: They're making life more difficult than it should be for us, but one I didn't have quite the concern regarding.. Rent-A-Hero...The man whose Rising Star Title reign came at my expense...But yet, the man whose victories I could explain away as relaxations of the rules, ever since the beginning holding out the hope that this time around, it would be different. This time, I would win. This time, there would be no questionable behaviour on the referee's part, as had been when Speedy Riggs mysteriously ignored the countout rule and slow-counted what should have been my victory. This time couldn't be a screwjob like the shot I had at Back To Old School, where the ref ignored blatant interference and my opponents ganged up on me...But that was the era of my former boss, the unreliable one who would and often did screw over his own partners if it could improve his stake..One couldn't hold out much hope for the time, but as the days grew on, I gained recognition while Rent-A-Roni slipped back..Then I got fired for someone else's agenda, and suddenly he had the ball back in his court, he was free to ascend the ranks of the federation...And that brings us to the turning point, that point in his career when he decided to fight the wrong 'evil' and assume that his girl'd be behind him every step of the way...The one assumption that would prove most fatal to him, and not a very smart one just a year after she kicked his ass once before..This would be the initiating incident in my eyes that put him on Xavier's bad side, and I am pleased by the thought of helping take him out once and for all... His luck has run out already, but his partnership remains an issue...The HIT's meddling in our business, as we learned from that confrontation on Turmoil, makes them a difficult duo to topple, even moreso in my mental state..I still don't have my confidence back from the Mall Brawl, though I try, and it becomes more and more necessary to feel positive about my hopes with the match made for Tuesday, perhaps my biggest match yet...It's hard to feel comfortable with your abilities with such precious little time to train and so many tax forms to go over..I remove the cassettes from their package, and place one into the tape deck, slipping the headphones over my ears as I lay back on the bed... 'Hello, and thank you for purchasing the Brad Helms Confidence-Boosting series of motivational tapes. These 6 cassettes will help change your life, inspire new self-confidence, and lead to a brand-new you.' Well, that's a little overdoing it..I just want to be a better wrestler, not discover a hidden talent for needlepoint.. 'In this, the first tape, I want to help you get to the bottom of your lack of confidence and make a connection to your negative energy...Now, what I want you to do is to imagine the situation that has you most anxious.' The ring... 'Have you got it?' Of course not, you lunkhead, I'-- 'Don't worry, I'll give you a little time to get the image straight.' Oh, all right then..Xavier sends the HIT across the ring and into the turnbuckles with a thud I can feel from the apron...Then reaches out to me...The tag is made, and I step in while he staggers out of the corner, trying to regain his bearings... 'Now, you probably should have the image by now. Press your mental pause button, and think about what the major conflict here is, not so much external as the internal conflict..I'll give you some time to think about it.' ...Maybe that was it..This doubt wasn't about the Trapezoid's skill, but moreso about my own ability to combat his efforts, or inability as it was..The memory of the Mall Brawl was haunting me somewhat, as if my insecurities were determined to take control of me by promoting my failures over my successes..Where I should be congratulating myself on punishing that stupid shape for the better part of half an hour, I find myself complaining about one little error of concentration...I can take him, I know I can, I'm better than the HIT and Xavi's better than Rent-A-Brain, so even mathematically speaking with the addition of the parts, we're stronger than them..But I couldn't manage to kick out of one lucky pin attempt...There was no excuse, and it's got me more than a little bothered... 'Okay, now at this point, we're going to go through this starting out the easy way..Envision the best-case scenario, the way this would happen if things went absolutely perfectly.' ...The Trapezoid staggers back, directly into a Taxplex! And another! Rolling through for one more! Then rolling into a Penaltyplex! Meanwhile, Rent-Boy is prevented from entering by Xavier, who plucks him off the apron and overhead, pulling him over and into a Hallmark on the outside, as Bob Charlie quickly counts the 3..No, scratch that, I'll let him go and give him No Evasion, make that teacher tap the mat so fast his hand would burn...All right, what next...The best-case scenario shouldn't take THAT long...What's taking this tape? I start to reach my hand toward the cassette.. 'Now, here's the difficult part of our start..Let's imagine the worst-case scenario, the one you most fear coming up as you're in this situation..Not necessarily the truly worst possible thing that could happen, but the one that you specifically have become anxious about. After all, one of the best ways to beat your fears is to face them head-on.' ...Too easy...HIT staggers out of corner, I go for the Taxplex but he slips around behind me, is it Sine? Cosine? Tangent? Worse...He gets an inside cradle.......1......2.....I want to kick out, but there's just no apparent possibility....3...Like that, it's over. Did I try? Yes...But was I capable of winning? Obviously not in the least...I can't let that happen...What's taking it so long? Bah, I'm turning this off, I don't need these tapes right now, it's not worth worrying over if you aren't sure that it'll happen...It's not like this match matters for anything yet...There's no stipulation, the important thing is that I beat the sides right out of the Trapezoid, whatever else happens is a mere technicality....A victory would be sweet, and perhaps a great boost to give me the winning spirit for my match on Tuesday...Defeat, however...Defeat could damage my spirit heavily if it comes in inopportune fashion...One more roll-up would prove my weakness..Would show me reason to be pessimistic against my hopes for Turmoil and leave me in a more distracted position than before, as the realization that I'm helpless to kick out would just bother me enough to render me even less able to do so...But then again, I could always just assume it was something about the Trapezoid himself and his ability to frustrate my efforts... I do not wish to think about it any more, I've got better things to do than waste my precious time fretting over a match without consequence...The tape shoved aside, the confidence still weakened, but growing ever slightly..Tuesday is what I should be concerned about...If the first fall were to take the title shot, I couldn't worry at all..The numbers advantage is of good enough use in an elimination match, but where the Front Office's unity can take full effect, it's no longer 25 percent probability of my going to Alliances to face the champion, but an even 50-50 chance at my ascension to a new level... And it doesn't take a Trapezoid to figure that out. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
