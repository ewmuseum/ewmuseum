The War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part XIV]

9 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 9, 2002 Jack Valentine: “Where did you get this car from then Elton?” Of course Valentine was admiring the car interior. Elton John: “You don’t want to know. When you’ve been in the 'business' as long as I have you’ll find out that you just don’t want to know.” Elton overtook a large Ford Transit van, checking the mirror as he pulled away. Jack Valentine: “You’re in a strange mood. I thought that things were panning out okay now. You were right about Smith, it was fair enough helping him out. And you solved your money worries. You’re a free man now. Make a clean break with the PINK! Department After all, you were on the PURPLE~! side when I met you as the mystery man.” Elton John: “No, that was only a distraction. The committee crossed me, it galled never getting promoted while brainless straights from the right schools went up the grades, but I got 'shafted' over that Union Street incident (The War: PINK! vs. Purple: Part I). It wasn’t my fault, they could have told the truth, these things happen when you are in our type of business. It would not have made us popular, but Liberace would have stood by the committee, and they could have weathered the storm.” Elton John: 'You still intend to clear everyone on the committee then?” Jack Valentine: “Yes, I’ve done plenty of people for them, err...you know what I mean. What do they expect me to do when they piss me off like that? I’m going to get every last one of them. But Liberace was not involved, it was one of the Village People who chaired that committee. So I’ll leave your precious Liberace for you.” Jack Valentine: “You must be mad, you’ll never be able to get them all.” Elton John: “Half of our security is there to protect the committee. They know the risks, I know the risks. Let’s just view this as a challenge to see if I’m as good as I think I am. Anyway if it is impossible I’ll die in the process and just prove to them that they were right anyway. If this whole thing is so impossible they can hardly be in much danger can they. Valentine, this is all getting boring. I want to listen to some music.” Jack Valentine: “There only seems to be a CD player, no cassette player.” Elton John: “Shit if I’d know that I’d have stolen another car. I suppose I can get rid of these then.” Elton tossed a handful of cassettes onto the back seat of the car. They had no cases, and a brown spaghetti of tape wrapped itself around them. Jack Valentine: “There must be a record store in the next town, you go in and get some CDs. We’ll have to spend all that money on something. Someone has got to get this economy going and start the consumer spending boom again, right? ” Valentine came out of the record shop after much deliberation. Elton John: “Okay what did you get then?” Elton look greedily into the polythene bag. Elton John: “Fuck you got five CDs. I gave you a hundred dollars to spend. What the fuck will I need to give you to get a decent selection of music. I’ll send you in with the suitcase next time. They must have seen you coming.” They were back in the country, surrounded by fields of bright yellow oilseed rape. Whatever happened to the green and pleasant land. Elton John: “Okay put on the first of your mystery CDs then.” Valentine pryed open the case and put on the first CD. The measured piping notes of an Tupac piece filled the car with a moment of PINK! Elton John: “Jesus H Christ, fuck, shit, damn, shite in a bucket, what is that rubbish.” Elton exploded into an equally measured and piping commentary on the merits of the music. Jack Valentine: “It’s rap!” Valentine said it somewhat defensively. Jack Valentine: “And do you have to swear so much. I have enough stress to deal with, considering my tag team titles are in jeopardy tonight!” Elton John: “Okay, okay, I’ll never swear again seeing as it offends you so much. I like music fine, but that is just a waste of vinyl. You don’t honestly like it do you?” Jack Valentine: “It is very popular, a lot of people do like it.” Valentine was now being very defensive. Elton John: “So you don’t actually like it yourself, it is just other people that like it.” Elton had spotted a weak point in Valentine’s defenses and steamrollered through it. Jack Valentine: “Take it out of the player, yes go on take it out, open up the sun roof. Right you chuck it out to the right, high as you can, as far as you can.” Reluctantly Valentine did as he was told, the CD flew, an intermittently sparkling disc of light. With an instinctive move Elton bent forward slightly, his right hand was on his...gun..PERVERT! It was in his hand, the hand was pointing out the open car window. A car backfired somewhere, the CD now flew upwards at a fresh angle and speed. The gun was back in the car, now smoking slightly and Elton wanted to know what the next CD would be. Jack Valentine: “Jesus Elton are you crazy, we’re not supposed to be attracting attention to ourselves out here. What do you think you are doing?” Elton John: “Valentine you just look round you, no one saw anything.” Sure enough despite a desperate examination of people in cars ahead, behind, passing on the other side of the road, in fact everywhere, it was obvious that no one had seen anything that concerned them, in fact most people seemed to be half asleep. Valentine was beginning to think that he had imagined the whole thing. Jack Valentine: “What would you say to ZZTop, Eliminator?” Valentine was still apprehensive about the fate of the remaining CDs. Elton John: “I don’t know, give it a spin, and we’ll see. The jury will let you know it’s verdict soon enough.” Elton patted the breast pocket over his heart, and more relevantly his gun. Eventually they had heard the entire CD collection that remained. Elton then decided that he could do better at the next record shop. Valentine remained in the car listening to David Bowie's Hunky Dory. Elton was coming out of the shop remarkably quickly. He had an unpleasant look on his face. They set off again, Elton unburdened himself. Elton John: “I cannot believe that that is supposed to be a record shop. They did not have a single decent CD in the place.” Jack Valentine: “I find that a little difficult to believe Elton.” Now it was Valentine’s turn to give Elton a hard time. Elton John: “Look they did not have anything by myself, me or mine.” Jack Valentine: “Probably because no one has ever heard of you around here.' Elton John: “Where do they get the staff for these shops. That so called assistant in there had never even heard of the Yellow Brick Road.” Jack Valentine: “Would you credit that.” Elton John: “I went in there with a hundred dollars, and if there had been anything decent I would have bought it, but no. If I had wanted to buy all that crap that they have on CDs these days I would of bought it in the seventies when it came out the first time. Hell is there a music industry in this country, there seemed to be nothing but a load of old rock dinosaurs, honestly who listens to all that rubbish.” Elton proceeded to rant on for the next fifty miles. It did nothing to improve his driving. He was what close friends call an aggressive driver, and the police call a bloody maniac. Somehow you knew that Elton was not going to die in a car crash. He traded rather heavily on this fact. With Johnny sound asleep in the back of the car, The Heartbreakers, along with Elton John, were on their way to the arena to meet up with Cowabunga Connection the biggest match of their tag team title history. One would just have to wonder if they would encounter anymore adventure on the way there. Hopefully the PURPLE~! is nowhere to be seen, but you never know. Stay tuned to part XV to find out!
