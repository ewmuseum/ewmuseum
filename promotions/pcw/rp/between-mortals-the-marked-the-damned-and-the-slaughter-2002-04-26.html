Between Mortals, The Marked, the damned, and the slaughter.

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-26-02 Between Mortals, The Marked, the damned, and the slaughter. And so it began.... And so it was written that when the clouds formed in a grand meeting in the center of the sky, when the warmest water turned a bitter cold, and when your enemy takes shelter with you that he would arise. The monster of god, the destroyer of the condemned, and the killer of the damned will arise from the ashes and go after not only a hardcore title, not only 21 other men, but the one they call Akiyama....Akiyama has been marked for slaughter and this behemoth will be the one to deliver. For a month now the one known simply as Sylo has laid healing his wounded body, mending his broken spirits, and most of all plotting the exact time and date when he would extract his revenge upon the one known as Akiyama. And like when Moses sent the plagues in the desert Sylo will strike Akiyama, his other opponents, and everyone else who steps in his way. His heart does not grow fond for them even though he is expected to be a father and the mother is none other than PWA's Jaden. But now he seeks guidance for his next mission from the man above and he knows there is only but one place to find that and so he goes. It felt like him.... as he walked through the large, oak doors of a well defined church. Each step through felt like a leap into the depths of history, the spirit of saints passed through his soul and he felt as one with them. The old stone walls seemed to cry with age as light of a dimming sun shined through the stained glass windows emitting colors of yellow, purple, red, green, and blue. His ice blue eyes seemed to glimmer in what light was being shown as the sounds of an organ slightly filled the air. A long line of wooden pews lined each side of a marble floor with a single red carpet flowing down to the door. In front of all this is a pull pit and hanging high above it close to the golden plated ceiling with the indentions of angels in them hung a large, wooden cross with a replica of Jesus Christ hanging from it. As he watched he could almost feel the pain of Jesus when he died for everyone's sins and this made him angry, angry at people like Akiyama who claim false god hood, people who deny the one true god and now he was beginning to change himself. A gentle breeze blew through his short, spiked, blue hair as he crouched down at the end of the first set of pews. His eyes still glimmered as a low growl rose up from deep down inside, it felt as though his soul had released all it's pain and anguish with one deep growl. The sounds of the organ were soon drowned out by the breeze which picked up and with a great force behind it sent the large frame of Sylo to his knees. A feeling swept over his entire being, his eyes began to glow and snarls began to arise from deep inside once more. The replica Jesus turned it's head and stared down upon the big man as a tear ran down it's cheek. 'I feel your pain,' his voice was dark but strong. It echoed off the walls and met his ears once more. The statue turned down glaring a hole into his soul. He could feel it enter his body like a bad drug and when he raised his head he found the statue crying tears of blood. He touched his own eye and found blood running out of the side as he collapsed to his hands and stared down at the fibers on the carpet. His body began to shake as his massive arms began to expand and the veins stuck out with the warm red blood pumping even faster from his heart with each breath. His lungs expanded and deflated rapidly and soon he was fighting to breath. Images of death, pain, famine, plagues, hate, greed, they all filled his mind. He watched as people were killed, as people were tortured, as people were cheated, lied too, and swindled. Thunder began to bang so hard from the heavens that it shook the stain glass windows and the organ music began to become darker, louder, and it was now clear that God was angry with the claim of a false god....and he must be punished by this new destroyer. As his head slowly raised from slumped position the muscles in his neck began to stick up and tighten. The growl pushed it's way out with much more force as his eyes began to glow more than normal. A strange power ran through his entire body with a great jolt which sent him flying back arching his back and throwing his arms up as a loud roar echoed through the church, out the door, and up the steeple making birds fly for safety. Once he controlled this strange power the statue turned and looked back down at him. He looked up and both made eye contact which locked onto each other like magnets. Sylo only nodded but that wasn't the end. The statue kept glaring down as it's eyes flashed white, staying that way. 'I bring you orders from the god on high, brought to you by the son of the one true god. The one known as Akiyama has been sealed, he must pay for his blatant blasphemy of the one high and true god. Go forth destroyer and do what your lord commands you! All in your way may be spared, only attack those marked by the seal of one true god! Journey to the east and find Ezekiel, you will know him when you see him for he is an angle of god and will tell you more of what you must know. Now go now and may the one father stay in your soul for eternity! ' The statues lips never moved as it spoke, he could hear it from in his soul. He slowly began to rise to his feet as the words of the son of man rang through his soul. A priest walked by placing his old wrinkled hand onto the massive shoulder of Sylo. He only stared forward almost as if he had heard the conversation and nodded. Then with a firm clasp the priest let go and made his way to a door at the side of the pull pit where he soon disappeared like a phantom. 'It's time for my return,' He spoke solid but under his breath. 'Everyone who stands in the way of me shall be crushed and left to die in their own sin. May god all mighty have mercy on their soul.....' He turned making his way down the long red carpet which lead to the exit of the old church. He glanced over his shoulder to take a gander at the replica Jesus only to find it back in it's place as if nothing had ever happened. He knew he wasn't crazy, he knew he had heard from Jesus Christ himself, and he knew what he had to do. He now had to journey on toward the east to find Ezekiel, the angel which would give him more information but he knew what he had to do and he was ready without anymore help. Sylo, a man violent by nature now turned toward god to hand down punishment to those marked.....but God never said he couldn't rip their spine out and jump rope with it. And as he stepped out into the bright day sun he felt a sudden cold feeling which spread through his veins like ice. He snarled and walked into the sea of sin which surrounded him every day of his life. The only difference now was he would be their executioner and everyone would pay for sin, even if it meant death. May god have mercy on their souls...... 'And so it was written that the slayer would find his way out amongst the masses and yell to them, 'Hark for I am the one sent to bring down punishment' and with that the masses fell to their knees and begged for the forgiveness of the destroyer.....he could only look and tell them 'May the lord all mighty have mercy on your soul,' The Book of Sylo: 57:2
