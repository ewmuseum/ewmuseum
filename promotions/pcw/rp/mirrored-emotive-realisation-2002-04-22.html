Mirrored Emotive Realisation

22 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22nd April A dark wind blows, leaving a black haze over El Diablo's life. There once stood a tombstone in remembrance for Pedro Diaz, but it was literally shattered by a sick Flaymin Aymin. Ironically it was Flaymin that caused the death of Pedro to begin with, but this is only one of the causes that Flaymin has had on his life. The graveyard rests in a secluded area in Juarez, Mexico; several hundred graves lay in this resting place. All with headstones of memory and love, minus one, the one that Flaymin shattered, like he has shattered Diablo's life. Although without a headstone, the memory and love for his dead brother Pedro live on within El Diablo. The wind gently blows the blades of grass, and El Diablo's long locks of hair as he stands by his brothers grave looking down at the tarnished ground, where the thong that Aymin's valet Jenna placed their remains. The day mirrors Diablo's feelings, a cloudy and dark sky at 1pm with little droplets of rain falling from above with a gentle but dark wind, spiralling thoughts of hate and dissolution. Is this what we expect to see of El Diablo now? The now unmasked Mexican, has his hair loose, and wears the face paint, to keep his face a mystery to all, still. He wears a black suit and pants with black shoes and a black tie. It's like he is going to funeral all over again, and perhaps he is after what chaos happened when Flaymin Aymin ran over the grave with a tractor, shattering the headstone into a million pieces. And at that precise moment, El Diablo's heart must have shattered into a million pieces, where he has still yet to find the real him. In his family, Pedro was the only one that really cared and believed in El Diablo, maybe that is why all this hurts him so much, especially with it being only four short years ago since that accident. 'There will be vengeance my brother, I promise,' speaks El Diablo in a low voice full of remorse. The gentle rain droplets get heavier, again mirroring the pain that El Diablo's soul is going through right now, and maybe has been for the last four years. 'Your memory will always live on me, you are always in my heart,' he beams with emotion, 'You knew wrestling was my true calling and let me pursue my passion, and that is why my Turmoil match against Juan Borro is dedicated to you,' Diablo says while looking down at the grave, in a strong stance. The wind picks up and begins to blow Diablo's hair wildly, and the rain gets heavier by the moment, with dark clouds filling the sky whole. 'This has always been about you! When you died, I promised myself I would make it, and you would make it with me, in here,' Diablo says with a passion whilst pointing to his heart, 'Sure, I've made some wrong decisions, but with you within me, I have made them right, and beating Juan on Turmoil will certainly be a right for us both,' he finishes in a much more sorrowful tone than before. Thunder crackles in the sky, and yellow strikes of lightning are seen in the distant. It almost shows all of El Diablo's pain and suffering, yet he stands tall at his brother's grave. The horrendous weather fails to move him from the place he most wants to be, by his loving brothers last resting place. 'The wrongs that I have committed in the past do not matter now, as all I see is the future, and in that future I see a change, a change for the good and righteous. There will be a new El Diablo; I just haven't found him yet. But, trust me Pedro, I will make all my wrongs, right and then you will believe in me, like I believed in you,' he roars with emotion while looking straight on now. The weather continues to get considerably worse by the second. An unphased El Diablo though, stands high and mighty by his brother's grave, taking all that the elements have to throw at him, while he speaks with pride to his deceased brother. 'I have found the man that caused the accident, and he will pay a tremendous price, I owe you that. Turmoil is just an obstacle, and Fusion is chance number three I have to destroy him, but it will not be the last, not by a long while my dear brother,' Diablo says in pride and passion that he has not had for a long time. The thunder gets louder, although the lightning ceases, the rain and wind pick up. There is no one else in the graveyard, there only stands El Diablo, reminiscing of his brother and fixing all the previous wrongs he has caused. 'He took your life, he took my wife, he has taken everything that ever mattered away from me, so right now I have nothing to lose, Pedro,' he speaks in pain of all Aymin has caused, 'You could call me kamikaze, I don't care, I just need to rectify my life and destroy anothers,' he finishes with a light gleaming in his eyes, like that of fire. The weather begins to die down slowly as Diablo kneels down and picks up the pink thong from on top of the grave. He holds it a second and looks at it, before throwing it quite a distance away. 'No more embarrassment, no more tarnished emotions, I will find the real me, I promise,' he stands back up to his feet and takes a step back from the grave. The thunder and rain ceases, like that of El Diablo's emotions right now. 'The new Diablo is almost ready to make an impact,' he promises his brother, 'Shadows of abyss, heathens of my soul, there is a new me, and it starts with victory on Turmoil,' he finishes with pride. He turns away from the grave as the sun comes out to shine down, again mirroring El Diablo's thoughts and feelings, only this time El Diablo is at peace with himself.
