A Trip To Downtown Hubbardville.

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/04/02 Ellie~!’s Prologue : Hubbardville is, like, SO awesome! It’s like a City right...But, like, without all the crappy bits that nobody likes...I think they sent all that stuff to Chicago or something...Anyway, we like live here now so Xavier can be free of all the oppression in the outside world, which I’m fine with because people always seem to do everything we ask here....hey, if it wasn’t so un-preppy I could SO be one too..... “Why was that bag, like, SO heavy? I mean, I thought that Battlefield Earth was, like, the best film ever...I’m sure you told me that, but that sack of hate mail looked, like, WAY heavy!” Our shot opens up in the greenery of Jenna Elfman Gardens that marks the centre of Hubbardville, the city in Wisconsin purpose built for Scientologists to escape the oppression of the outside world. Amidst the landscaped gardens and maze of brick pathways we see the 1st couple of Scientology, Xavier Kannon and Eleanor Hall, making their way from the busy centre down the path that leads into metropolitan Hubbardville. Eleanor : “Because if...You know, it was REALLY good and then nobody would hate it, then wouldn’t the hate mail be really light because there...Um, like, wouldn’t be any...Right?” As they head towards the open iron gates bearing the name of Jenna Elfman, the famous Scientologist who is famed for her appearance in the sitcom Dharma and Greg as well as movies such as Keeping the Faith, directed by Ed Norton...Who although isn’t a Scientologist was in American History X, and is thus linked to Mark Makonie ending this cycle once and for all. ......Makonie is, like, weird. Normally tattoos and shaven heads are, like, a SO total no....But then he was, like, WAY less pathetic than Shabazz so I suppose he is okay....His beard could do with a trim though...... Xavier : “Incidents such as this are why this fine city around us was built. You won’t find a single person within the city limits of Hubbardville that would criticise the film because their brain didn’t function on the number of levels that ours does...Hubbard, Duke Williams probably went to see some no-brainer Arnie movie ahead of the visionary masterpiece we appreciate.” The Scientologist is wearing a black Gold Patron Meritorious t-shirt with gold lettering over a grey long sleeve t-shirt. With it he wears a pair of black, worker-cut jeans with green and purple embroidery snaking down the left leg to where a pair of black, purple and grey Skechers adorn his feet. His long, auburn hair is neatly tied back and his bright green eyes sparkle as he takes in the surroundings. Eleanor : “Oh....Okay, because I was thinking that it was because it really sucked but you lot didn’t want to admit it....Looks like I’m wrong.....Oooh....What is down there?” She points to one of the yet unexplored streets in the glorious city that leads down by the hospital at which she was recovering on Sunday night. Eleanor : “This place....Like...RULES! I was SO bored of knowing everywhere but here there are still places I haven’t seen yet....Wait, when I explore all the places here can we build a new one?” The PCW Commissioneress is wearing a skinny fit pink t-shirt with Snapple~! across the chest in silver and a pair of sandblasted blue jeans with silver sequinned stars down the legs. Her long, wavy brown hair with subtle blond highlights is neatly tied back and as usual, she wears a large array of bands and bracelets around her wrists. .......That is, SO, what would be better than Hubbardville...TWO Hubbardvilles! Or maybe they could call the other one Ellieville...That place would rule, a haven for all preps across he United States....Yeah...That’s rule..... The Scientologist tries to avoid the question of building a whole new city just so his valet doesn’t get bored so promptly answers her first question. Xavier : “That is Wehatenicolekidman Avenue, follow it and you get to the L Ron Hubbard Memorial Hospital and then if you follow that you get to the Tom Cruise-arama Video Reprographics Emporium on the outskirts of the financial district.” The stroll through the gates bearing Jenna’s name and out onto Wehatenicolekidman Avenue, named after the gold digging talenetless actress who married Tom Cruise only to get herself a higher profile. Eleanor : “Is that, like, on foot? These shoes are one of the 5 pairs I bought yesterday as a pick me up and they are, like, SO not made for walking....Taxi?” Suddenly, as soon as the fist syllable is pronounced, 3 Hubbardville licensed Taxis pull up in front of them, all looking clean both on the inside and outside, with an air freshener that actually works and a driver who is well presented and isn’t wearing a dirty vest. ........Taxis......Arriving quickly........Hubbardville........Totally Rules!....... Xavier : “See, another thing that makes Hubbardville far superior to any other city in the United States...Say, Chicago, is things like this...The people. Even a Taxi Driver, one of the lowest castes in Scientology are more presentable and more...How shall I put it...House-broken than Duke Williams. His valet sneers at the mention of the man who has made her the butt of his jokes and made it his new hobby to rip into PTC’s top prep at any opportunity. Eleanor : “Than......Duke......Williams...” The driver steps out and politely tips his cap to the couple before opening the door for them to climb in before closing it carefully behind them and getting back in himself where he turns around to them, un-intrusively making eye contact. Driver : “Where can I take you Gold patron meritorious Kannon, Miss Hall?” The couple are pleased with the politeness of the driver, not at all what they are used to in the rest of America where they would have to sit in a dirty car with a rude driver who resembles a man such as Duke Williams more than the clean cut drivers of Hubbardville. Xavier : “If you can drop us off at the corner of Dianetic and Ribisi.” .........Wow......I SO don’t sense the usual smell in here....You know, like the Duke Willaims what dies in here? Smell....Hubbardville SO does really, like, rule! YAY HUBBARDVILLE!..... The driver raises his eyebrows at the mention of Ribisi Street in the South quarter of Hubbardville. Driver : “Ribisi hey? Slumming it are we? Down there I heard the average income is only $45,000....I even heard a rumour that they have less than 3 parking spaces per house as well, someone even said the rubbish is only collected 5 times a week!” The Scientologist seems undeterred by the rumours of the southern borough of Hubbardville, but knowing the high class of person who frequent the purpose built beacon of tolerance in Wisconsin he knows it will not be as bad as rumoured. Xavier : “Yeah, it is for the least fortunate of Scientologists, but we are heading down Dianetic Drive so we’ll be okay. Say, what did you do before coming to Hubbardville?” Out of politeness Xavier decides to initiate a conversation rather than having one forced upon him inside the stuffy, sweaty confines of...Lets say a Chicago cab. Instead the air conditioning is on and the cab has a nice breeze, pleasant temperature and no bodily odours at all. Eleanor : “Coming......To.......Hubbardville?” The car, rather than bolting through the traffic recklessly at the expense of the customer’s comfort, the driver makes his way slowly and surely through the centre of Hubbardville. As the camera looks out of the window we see no homeless people sprawled across the sidewalks and more importantly, no fast food stalls set up selling greasy hot dogs infested with all sorts of bacteria. Driver : “I used to work in the dirtiest, most disgusting conditions....And I’m not talking about the old, out-dated cab I used to drive...I’m talking about Chicago!” The naming of the Windy City, home of Duke Williams causes a smug grin to break over both of the Scientologist’s faces, thinking back to what they have seen of the filthy squalor ridden place that spawned the Malice Man. Xavier : “Then you must appreciate Hubbardville more than others...Coming from the awful, oppressive, foul-mouthed disgrace of a city that you do....Luckily I have only seen it on film....The unsavoury characters lining the streets, the un-hygienic food stalls....The Cowboy Hat wearing anti-social, dirty layabouts that frequent it’s amenities....Hubbardville must seem like what the Christians call heaven to you!” At the mere mention of all of these things, Eleanor’s face turns sour as anything related to Duke Williams is not her favourite subject. Eleanor : “Heaven......To.......You!” The driver nods along, not looking away from the road to make sure he is still driving safely, something he could not to by leaning over his seat and talking is an infernally annoying Chicago accent whilst scratching his arm pits. Driver : “You ain’t lyin’!” As soon as the Driver says the words he realises his mistake and looks uncomfortable as he squirms in his seat, looking guilty and ashamed of himself. .......Eugh, I hate the way he looks, I hate the way he speaks, I hate the way he smells and I hate the way he is always MEAN to me....I....... Hate...... Duke..... WILLIAMS!...... Driver : “Er...Sorry, my apologies Mr Kannon, Miss Hall....I only moved here on Sunday and as much as I’ve tried some Chicago is still coming out in me...I’m hoping through an intensive course of medication and auditing sessions I can become a fully fledged Hubbardvillian without any throw backs to my former life. In the back the Commissioner and Commissioneress loom like they will go easy on him, not holding his unfortunate background against him. Xavier : “That’s okay Driver, I know as well as anyone how Chicago can infect a person, I will not hold it against you....And as a personal favour I will not even report you to the appropriate authorities....Take it as a reward for your shunning of your hometown.” The Driver looks grateful that Xavier has decided to spare him for his rejection of Chicago, the city that personifies Duke Williams, and in Kannon’s head that isn’t a compliment. Driver : “Thank you Sir, I appreciate it. When I was told I was getting out of Chicago it was he happiest day of my life, the oppression I was subjected to as a Scientologist drove me to despair and people never seemed to want to better themselves...But I want to, I don’t want to be some 40 year old walking around in a Cowboy Hat listening to dead old country singers...I want to be the future.” The 1st couple of Scientology nod in approval as they listen to the man repenting in the driver’s seat in front of them, pleased that they have influence over the man’s life. Xavier : “No....I’d hate for anyone else to turn out like that...Hopefully Duke Williams will be a one off and Scientology can save the poor directionless souls that frequent that Hubbard-forsaken city.” The driver finally reached the corner of Dianetic and Ribisi where the glittering thoroughfare that is Dianetic Drive stretches out as far as the eye can see, and the less glittering Ribisi Street, that would still pass as one of the better neighbourhoods of any other city as our shot begins to fade out. Driver : “Duke who?” The couple smirk arrogantly at his lack of knowledge of the man who supposedly speaks for the Driver’s hometown as they exit the Taxi and look down the street ahead of them and the shot hits black. Eleanor : “Like.....Duke.....Who? Hehe...Ya You!”
