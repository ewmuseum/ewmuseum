Las Vegas Lifestyle

9 June 2003

Date: 09/06/03 Casinos will take your money if you're not careful. Craps. Slots. Keno. Roulette. All have odds in the house's favor. That's how they work; the glam, women, and glitz get you there, and you empty your pockets thinking that has long as you have fun, it's well worth it. I don't come here to enjoy myself. I came here for the same reason I came to PCW. To win. Blackjack can be beaten. The odds can be in your favor. Dealing out of a six deck shoe, double down, and a 3 to 2 payoff on Blackjacks, and you have a game ready for the whippin'. Can you play the game, Gaz? It's also called Poker 21. You get two cards, and you try to get closer to 21 than the dealer. Go over, and you bust. Aces can be 1 or 10. I'll spare you the rest of the rules; chances are, you know them like you know a wrestling ring. Do you know how to turn the game from the house advantage...to yours? I do, Gaz...and I'll tell you. Learn to play the odds. If you sit on a 12 against a dealer's 13...you stand. Hit on a hard 16 against a 9. Two aces? Split them...double your money. This is called Basic Strategy. They've been proven and re-proven again and again...every dealer up card has a play. Learn them inside and out. Stand on anything 17 and over. If you have a 'soft' 17...that is to say, a six and a ace....then you hit if the dealer shows a ten. Play the odds against me, Gaz. I'm fast. I'm not a veteran of this ring. I'm not as hardcore as PCW gets. I won't say I don't have skills...but I will say...you're dead if you underestimate me. I've played Blackjack for some time, and I've learned a basic counting system to count down the six deck shoe. Nothing fancy...I'm a Texan, not a math whiz. Counting the cards means keeping track of how many small or large cards are still in play. If the deck is rich in face cards and aces....the advantage is yours. The dealer has a greater chance of busting, and you have better odds of hitting the Blackjack. A deck full of small cards is not good...for the opposite reasons. The dealer will not bust often, leaving you to soak up bad hands. The trick is how to bet your money. Count the cards like so; face cards and aces = +1. 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6 = -1. On 7, 8, and 9 you count nothing. So if I land a 10, 6 and the dealer's upcard is a 10...my count is +1. +1 -1 +1 = +1. Are you seeing this yet, Gaz? I want you to know what you've gotten yourself into. No legendary tales. No nicknames. I'm Manuel Labor. I'm filling you in on the details. The addition above is called the 'Running Count'. You keep this in your head when you order drinks, talk to the pit boss, hit on the waitress, so on. It takes focus. The RC is then divided by approximately how much of the six decks is left in the shoe. This gives you what's called the 'True Count'. If I have a running count of -12 and I estimate there to be 4 decks left....-12 / 4 = -3. My deck is now at -3. It's not good for me to bet big here. My chances at good hands are nil. I will play out my strategy on my belly. No insurances. Hit like you're suppose to. Double down sparingly. I would have to wait this run out until better odds. Drink the beer, flirt with the waitress. In time, I'll have a better count, and then...I can increase my bets. I liken it to a match as well; you will get your runs in, with your cheating ways. I have no doubts about talent when I see it. I will wait out your offense until my chances look better. Now...my running count has jumped up to +8. 2 decks left. +8 / 2 = +4. This is a good attack count. I have a good chance at blackjack here. Has I bet, my count gets more positive. I'm rolling now. The odds are in my favor. My mind is honed. You, as the dealer, cannot catch a break. At the table, I'd set two bets, 500 each. In the ring, my moves would become increasing complex and more devestating. You think you know me...but do you know my speed? Could you counter 8 Seconds before it does it's damage? Could you stop me when I'm on a roll? Problably not. The odds are too much in my favor. By the time I walk away, and I will when I'm ahead...the match will be over. You will have lost. Another Certified Public Asskickin' for the Prince of Pranksters of PCW. But look at the bright side...now you can go to a casino and maybe WIN for a change. There's one thing I forgot to tell you about casino security. The eye in the sky sees all. Using my method and counting like I do...you'd be a tiger among zebras. They would spot you before your second beer arrived. What then? How do you beat that? Hey, these are my trade secrets here. I win. That's what I do. Ask anybody. Your bad luck was gettin' a match with Manuel Labor, end of story. Beating me is a lost cause. Beating the casino, and covering your ass while you win...that's a different story. But call me after the match...I'll be willing to tell you. Later, Gaz.
