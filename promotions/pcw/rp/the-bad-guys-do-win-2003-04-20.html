The Bad Guys Do Win

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Bad Guys Do Win Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 4-20-2003 The hero doesn't always win... This I've found out the hard way. For close to years…I’ve given the PCW the BEST of me. I’ve given everyone as much of me as I possibly could….I’ve fought the forces of evil, no matter what form they came in. Goth, evil militant leaders, evil soccer players, megalomaniac’s….it didn’t matter, I truly BELIEVED in the way of truth and justice. And when you believe in an ideal so strongly, so whole-heartidly….you become a different man when you find out your whole reason for living, your whole reason for fighting… Is a lie.I was always taught that if you did the right things, stand up for what was right, kept evil out of your heart and vanquished it at every turn…lived your life pure, you’d get the best of things…. They lied.I look at the top of PCW and I find “Totally Tubular” Tommy. He cheated, scammed, did all that was wrong to get to the top…And damn if he didn’t succeed.It took weeks upon weeks to realize, the ideal I’m fighting for is lost. THERE IS NO GOOD TO SAVE ANYMORE!! All there is is corruption, evil, and destruction. There’s nothing I can do about that…nothing I can do to vanquish that kind of evil. I’ve tried since my very entry into PCW and every time, I’ve fallen short. How many times do you fall before you just lay there? How many times do you get hit before you just take the beating? I got the hint, I am no superhero….I am not the one meant to change things here in PCW. I’m no Kryptonian…..I’m just average. The average human being with no hope to save a lost cause. I was lied to when I was told that you just had to “say your prayers and take your vitamins.” I feel betrayed, knowing that the only way to get ahead in the game of life….is to become the same villain I grew up despising. If that’s the case… I’m tired of fighting. I’m tired of trying…..and I’m tired of believing. Reality is hard to swallow…but now I’ve stomached it, and the taste has left something sour in my soul. And a heart…that once believed that all the good shall be rewarded… Has been shattered…knowing that notion is simply not true…. This is my reality… This is my pain. ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
