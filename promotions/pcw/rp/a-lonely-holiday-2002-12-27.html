A Lonely Holiday

27 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/27/02 A Lonely Holiday The night was Christmas Eve. Hopes were high, and success was inevitable. I took initiative, outsmarted the rest, and never even took a bruise for my actions. I felt like I was on top. I held the precious gold the brother of mine had slaved over for nearly a year. I hold it with honor and prestige. I figure nothing more is needed when such valuable gold is here weighing down my fingers. But I can’t say that. For I desire something of greater meaning. No, not the cruiserweight title, even though that would make for a great gift from God. You see the thing I desire most is the same thing I can have with absolutely anyone I desired. Love. All you need is love a wise man once said. And by God he’s right. You see love comes in different shades, but the shades that have reflected off of me are ones that do not fulfill my inner wanting. I wish for the day that someone can listen to my problems, work them out and smile in the end. There have been thousands of woman I had lust. They have given their full bodies to me, and I took it with neglect, letting them be trapped into something they wanted, but also something that I detested. I gave them what they wanted, and then they were off to tell the world about the experience of a lifetime. Men say “Wow, a man who can go on forever and please a woman until they actually pass out. Now that’s a man I want to be”. Well that wasn’t the man I wanted to be. I’ve tried to hold back the temptation but my body starves for it. It’s like I’m a vampire dying for the bite. It was just a little while ago, when I was able to hold off. Wrestling was my new feed for releasing energy. And it worked fine. But now…. Now I desire something special. So here I was. It was two hours after I had six hours after I had become a world tag champion, and I was back in Spain. I expected the cheers and love of all of my fans to swarm me the second I returned. I hadn’t been in Spain since the day before Decimation, and since then I believed I’ve proved a lot to the world. But that wasn’t the way it turned out I got nothing. When I first lifted my head up, I had high hopes and when I looked up and saw nothing, those hopes sunk into a pit of quick sand. Suitcases are left in station B, Mr. Romantic, is that your name? It’s Romantico; thank you. Not even the flight attendant knew my name. I thought my life had made an impact, but it didn’t, I was just another step in a never-ending staircase. I continued through the streets that day and saw all the decorations and merry attitude. The stores were closed and everyone was inside their homes together near the fireplace with the whole family. The family I never had. I guess I envied that I never received such a Christmas. Suddenly I, who was holding about fifteen pounds of gold, was jealous of a five-year-old child who was eating at a table with his family around him. At that point I realized the thing I had been starving for. It wasn’t a piece of scrap metal or gold, but yet it was something of greater importance. I starved for LOVE! The feelingThe aromaThe majestic tune of the word I wanted it and I was willing to do anything for it. The right person was out their waiting for me, and I was just standing here moping. One day I could have a family, and maybe we can all sit around the fireplace on Christmas. And even though it may seem like something small, it would mean the world to me. So I continued walking down the street that night and then I spotted a young man, who couldn’t be any older than 18 years of age. He was stumbling down the street, with a bottle of vodka shaking within his grip. His eyes were lowered, and his face was red through the cold air. He stumbled to the ground and just lay there. When I looked over at him I saw myself. I never liked these holidays, and even though it celebrates the birth of Christ, it celebrates happiness as well, happiness I didn’t have, and one I wanted to run away from. So I would usually drink the night away, or pop a pill to let my mind wander. I regret doing that, maybe if I saw this kind of things earlier, I could have changed half way through the horror. So here I am, older and more delicate. I fear the thing I want most, and yet I don’t know the first step into getting there. I walked up to the young man with the vodka in hand and whispered some silent words. I know how you feel. He stared at me in return and didn’t say a word. I knew sometimes the only thing you want is for someone to notice you and feel what you’re going through, I know that’s what I wanted. I continued that night seeing many different people in many different situations. I tried to deal with them and use them to my advantage. Gold may make the eyes glitter, but it won’t make the heart float. A Merry Christmas to All!
