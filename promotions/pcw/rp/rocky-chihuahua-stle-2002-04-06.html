Rocky- Chihuahua Stle

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6th April A fine rescue of the Taco Bell Chihuahua by El Diablo was heralded all around Mexico after his brave efforts to regain the dog from the menacing Flaymin Aymin. When last seen El Diablo was rushing the overweight and very diseased dog to a veterinarian to see if the dog could indeed be rescued. El Diablo is shaking the hand of some man in a white coat. 'Thank you very much,' says El Diablo in glee, 'I'll be sure to put him on a strict diet and exercise him back into shape,' he finishes whilst smiling. They cease shaking hands, as the man in the white coat, obviously the veterinarian, looks ready to speak to the masked Mexican. 'In my thirty years of veterinary practice this is the worst case of physical health I have ever seen in a dog,' he speaks seriously. The vet scratches his thinning white hair and then rubs his mixed gray and white stubble on his face. El Diablo eyes up the vet and notices how short he is, but ignores it and just decides to take the vets advice. 'Well, he was dog knapped by some sick man, and he did this to him,' he excuses himself, 'He over fed him, fed him the wrong food and made him drink beer, he even tried to turn my dog gay,' exclaims Diablo with a look of horror on his face. The vet looks astonished. 'He really did all that?' he questions in some sort of shock. El Diablo nods. 'Yeah, he had gay porn movies in the VCR running non stop and made the dog sit right in front of them,' replies Diablo trying not to snap on the horror of it all. The vet shakes his head and tuts. 'I meant about the food and beer, although that would explain his boner,' reveals the vet. Diablo stares at the doctor in surprise. 'He has a boner?' questions Diablo with a look of sheer disgust on his face. The vet nods and motions at the dog's genitalia. 'Yes, see here?' says the vet. The Masked Mexican nods his head in a state of disbelief. 'Well, that's it,' responds the vet. Diablo gulps hard and slowly so that his gulp can be physically seen going down. 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO,' screams Diablo, 'Damn you Flaymin!!!!,' he shouts some more, 'You've turned my dog gay,' he finishes with a look of horror on his face. The veterinarian stares in embarrassment at El Diablo. 'It really isn't that bad,' states the vet. El Diablo looks at the vet in disgust. 'How's that?' he asks in intrigue. 'At least it's not bigger than yours,' the vet says, 'At least I hope not,' he finishes as he eyes El Diablo up and down. The same look of disgust remains on the face of El Diablo for a few minutes before he shrugs and smirks. 'Hey, you're right,' Diablo says in some glee. El Diablo walks over to the table that his beloved Chihuahua is laying on. He picks up the overweight dog and holds it in both his arms with his arms shaking due to the magnitudal weight of the dog. He looks down at him. 'Hey Andy, we're going home to get you in shape,' says Diablo to his dog. He responds by burping loudly in El Diablo's face. El Diablo sniffs it to get the smell. 'Chili-cheese burrito's?' questions Diablo to the dog, 'He fed you that?' he finishes. The vet, getting ready to go to work on another animal looks across at the Mexican Devil. 'You have a better nose than some sniffer dogs,' he jokes. El Diablo smirks and heads to leave the veterinary surgery. 'Thanks doc! And now to get you back in shape,' he thanks the vet and talks to the dog. Diablo opens the door and then walks out, but before the door closes the dogs fart can be heard loudly. The vet shakes his head. 'Pet owners nowadays,' he grudgingly says. (as usual) Time Elapses 'Come on you can do it,' says El Diablo's familiar voice. The Chihuahua is on it's back, struggling to breathe while El Diablo attempts to make the dog complete some sit-ups. 'Come on!' he yells at the dog through a megaphone, 'You can do it,' he finishes trying to motivate the dog. The dog completes one then collapses onto his side. El Diablo looks disappointed at his overweight dog. 'Fine, maybe this will help you then, Andy,' says Diablo to his dog. He walks over to a shelf he has in his garage and takes off a small radio that is battery powered, he then grabs a tape and puts it in the cassette part of the radio and presses play. The Rocky Theme 'Flying High' starts to play. Writers note: At this point you know this is the rip off of Rocky. The dog rolls on his back and starts to do some sit-ups to the delight of El Diablo, who is yelling orders to the dog through a Jimmy Hart style megaphone. The next shot, the dog has a white sweatband around his head and is running whilst on his lead as El Diablo holds it while driving a car at about 5mph, which is quite fast for a dog. A wider shot and several cars behind him are beeping their horns at El Diablo and throwing many profanities at him. In the next shot El Diablo is squatting the dog as it lifts up a doggy bone whilst on his back, an obvious weight lifting exercise. A few more shots of the dog weight lifting doggy bones and then mauling a scarecrow with an afro on. The last shot is of El Diablo and the Chihuahua on a leash running up a large area of steps and then reaching the top, El Diablo heavily sweating, the dog just panting with his tongue out. El Diablo, hoists the thinner dog up in the air and starts jumping up and down as the Rocky theme 'Flying High' peaks. 'We did it,' shouts Diablo out loud to the dog. The shot widens and there were only about seventeen steps and many passers by are looking at El Diablo oddly. 'We're a success,' he yells loudly as the dog carries on panting and the music stops as the scene slowly fades out.
