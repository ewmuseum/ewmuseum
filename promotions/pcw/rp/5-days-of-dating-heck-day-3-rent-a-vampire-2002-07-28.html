5 Days Of Dating Heck-Day 3....RENT-A-VAMPIRE?!

28 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-28-2002 Day 3 ..^../ We return to our story where we last left off with Rent-A-Villain strapped to a wooden rack within the dungeon of Velina Mystique, a beautiful vampress our villain was meant to date for a TV dating show...yet things went quite awry upon meeting the vampiric vixen. With a stiff brass candlelabra shot to the head, we now find our hero in this predicament, the buxom beauty of the unded standing before him with a wide smirk on her face as she plays proverbial piano on the bare chested skin fo one Rent-A-Villain. Looking him up and down, licking her full ruby red lips with the utmost desire to feed off of the PCW star, she looks our vile villain eye to eye...her dragonskin green eyes staring back at the sea blue eyes of Rent-A-Villain, she then says ..^../ Velina:'Well...it looks like your fade out is over...now I can prepare my feast...' Rent-A-Villain:'CURSES OF DR.DOOM, THOSE FADE OUTS DON'T LAST AS LONG AS THEY USED TO! Well know this Velina....you can take the crimson essence from my veins...but you'll NEVER take my soul.' Velina:'That's embedded in the blood my dear...it comes with the price of admission...' Rent-A-Villain: *sighs* 'Is anything sacred these days...' Velina:'Nope, I knew that when they started already started preselling J'Lo's a** genes on E-Bay. Now....where should I take the first bite of my little Rent-A-Treat?' ..^../ Velina begins to run her soft, pale hands all over the loosely clothed body of Rent-A-Villain, probing his neck, shoulders, chest and arms in search of the perfect spot to sink her razor sharp teeth into. Curious at the moment while our villain is bound to the rack, hooks a finger around the elastic waistband of his smiley face boxers, pulling them towards her and taking a slight peek at his *ahem* 'Kryptonite Rod'. Startled, she steps back, letting go of the elastic band as it smacks Rent-A-Villain's tight waist, drawing a slight 'ouch' from him. With an inquisitive look upon her gothically gorgeous face, Velina asks herself ..^../Velina:'Now how am I gonna fit THAT in my mouth?' ..^../ Velina licks her lips, ready to sample her blood filled prey right before her, opening her mouth wide as her dangerous set of fangs detract with a shiny gleam in the torch lit light. She nears her mouth towards Rent-A-Villain's bare neck, our villain trying to stay strong and keep from trembling as he can feel her warm breath drawing closer. He closes his eyes....ready to accept his ominous fate as he can feel her sharp teeth scraping his skin when suddenly.....^../ !!~DING DONG~!! Velina:'DA** IT!! The one time I want someone to use that da** thing and they do it NOW!!' *looks to a bound Rent-A-Villain* 'Now you just stay right here...Velina is gonna give you all the suck treatment you can handle after I get the door....you sexy little piece of meet you.' *giggle giggle* ..^../ Velina picks up the train of her curve hugging black dress, exiting the dungeon quickly for a few moments while leaving Rent-A-Villain for a few moments. He looks about frantically tugging at the leather bonds on his wrists and ankles, growing desperate to gain his freedom as he looks up to the sky exclaiming to....ME?! ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'Come on evil narrator man, toss me down a knife or some hedge clippers or something, I can't escape.' ..^../ I'm just a narrator, I'm not supposed to get involved in the story Rent-A-Villain, I'm sorry...that'd be against the rules...there is a narrator's union you know! I could lose my job ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'OH COME ON!! Remember that time I FedEx'd you some cash when they said they fired you the first time narrator man....haven't we been through some rough times together? Come on, be a sport...help me out here!' ..^../ *sigh* I hate it when you run me for a guilt trip. *ahem* A rusty butter knife falls from some unknown expanse from above, unknown as to where it came from as it rips through the leather bond of our vile villain's left wrist, freeing him from his bondage as he frantically rips off the right wrist bond, followed by the two ankle bonds. Sliding off the rack, he strikes a 'whooping crane' martial arts stance, standing on one leg with the other leg bent in air and his two arms outstretched as he looks to want to battle Velina in only his boxers, exclaiming ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'NOW THAT I AM FREE FROM MY BONDAGE, I SHALL NOW SMACKER THAT BLOODSUCKING WENCH!! How dare she attempt to end my life before I go to face Snow and end his chances fo EVER hitting a PCW ring again after I've thrashed him with my hefty move-set of EEEEEEVILY named moves and such. I shall not be defeated...never will I taste defeat from him because he will know he has met his match in villainy ONCE AND FOR ALL!! When's the last time HE'S ever stolen candy from a baby...villain my boxer covered behind!' *Looks down, realizing he is only in boxers again* 'GYAH!!! MY CLOTHING!! OOOOOH THAT WENCH!!'..^../ After completing that statement, the steel dungeon door swings open violently, Velina rushing through the door before slamming it shut behind her, her pale face growing ever whiter as she looks to be extremely startled and worried about whatever it was that rang the doorbell ..^../Rent-A-Villain:'SCARED STRAIGHT OF THE BEATING I'M GOING TO GIVE YOU...OH YOU SHOULD BE EVIL VAMPRESS!! PREPARE TO BAT...' Velina:'Please wait stop...I'm sorry. Please calm down for a moment this is urgent.' ..^../ Velina approaches Rent-A-Villain, her eyes shifting about nervously as she looks to writhing in stress as she looks to our vile villain, putting aside his anger for the vampiric vixen to hear what it is she has to say, still posing that Rent-A-Hero heart within him even though he claims to be a changed person. ..^../Velina:'My daddy came all the way from Austria...I can't believe it's been a millennium already. Fact of the matter is, I told them by then I'd have a good man in my life to share bodies and blood with and as you can tell....I'm still single and sucking. So...if you do me a slight...little....eeeny weeny favor...I promise to make it all worth while.' Rent-A-Villain:'What is this favor...' Velina:'I'll explain it on the way up...follow me...'..^../ Velina slowly strides away from him, walking slow enough to allow Rent-A-Villain to follow her as he says one their way out of the dungeon ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'Last time I did a teeny weeny favor...I couldn't tinkle for three weeks...thank you again Aimz.' Shortly... ..^../ We now find ourselves back upstairs in what appears to be the living room of Velina's ghoulish castle furnished with yet more elegant tapestries, suits of olden 13th century armor, a raging fireplace with the fire burning steadily, and antiquated furniture from what appears to be the 15th century. Sitting in a plush rocker is an elderly, yet fit man. His hair is a distinguished shade of silvery gray, his body amazingly fit and could easily disguise his age if not for the plethora of wrinkles that swarm his face and body. His suit is fine, tailor-made blue silk and he holds a cane with a pewter sculpture of a bat on the very end. His soft brown eyes show years upon years of intrigue within, looking in them only giving you a mere glimpse of the things he's lived to see. His quiet sitting is shortly interrupted as Velina enters the room, smoothing out her tight black dress as her face lights up with joy, rushing over to the elderly man with arms wide open. He slowly makes his way up just in time to open his arms to her, the two with a warm and loving embrace as the elderly man says with delight ..^../Elderly Man:'Oh my dear, you look wonderful. You grow more and more beautiful as the centuries pass. What are you...20....21 thousand years old now?' Velina:'21 thousand daddy, and I couldn't be happier.' ..^../ She brandishes a bright smile for her father, his joy shifting the downward wrinkles on his face to straightened ones as he releases her from the embrace. He looks at her eagerly asking ..^../Velina's Father:'So...where is my son-in-law...I'd like to meet the vampire that was worthy of your hand in immortal marriage....or at least until that Buffy woman comes along and kills one of you...God I hate that show.' Velina: *nervously* 'Well daddy...I guess he's...' Off-Screen Voice:'BLAH BLAH BLAH!!!' ..^../ Velina and her father turn to the right, Velina shocked with amazement and her father smiling with fangs outstretched as they both look to the archway of the living room to find Rent-A...VAMPIRE?!?!?! I kid you not viewers, our vile villain stands before the two wearing his usual red and black vice versa mask...but his wardrobe has changed astoundingly as he stands within a jet black suit with white undershirt and matching white and black dress shoes. His usually spiked up hair is slicked back and he smiles proudly, revealing a pair of obviously glued on fangs that would make WWF's Gangrel's fangs look totally natural in comparison. To top it off, he wavers about a black cape with red inside as he whips it about his body as he slowly stalks over to Velina and his father. As if they've been together for years, Rent-A-Villain takes Velina by her soft waist and gives her an innocent kiss to her soft lips, holding her in a loving manner that Velina goes along with as our vile villain says with a horridly ridiculous Count Chockula rip off voice ..^../Rent-A-Vampire:'HONEY I'M HOME....BLAH BLAH!! I'm sorry I'm late, but I was too busy counting the victims from last night. 1....AH-AH-AH....2 AH-AH-AH.....3 AH-AH-AH.'Velina: *slightly nudges our villain, quietly whispering with clenched teeth* 'Where did you learn that stuff?' Rent-A-Villain: *quietly* 'Bad Dracula movies and reruns of Sesame Street...' *looks to Velina's father* 'So your Velina's father...BLAH BLAH...COME HERE FATHER-IN-LAW, BLAH!!'..^../ Velina's father looks to be pleased by Rent-A-Villain's performance, buying into it completely as he hugs his 'son-in-law' happily, patting his back. He then lets him go and says ..^../ Marius:'Please my son, call me Marius from now on, your family. So tell me...how did you meet my lovely daughter?'..^../ Rent-A-Villain looks back to Velina, Velina motioning to our villain to make up something as he nods, looking back to an eager Marius as he begins to make up a story off the top of his head to play it off ..^../Rent-A-Vampire:'Well Marius...it was beautiful....it was a dark night in Transylvania and I just got finished with a box of Count Chokula cereal...and there I saw...she was being chased by...by....umm....VAMPIRE HUNTING VIRGINS!!'Marius:'THOSE ARE THE WORST KIND!! Virgins don't get any sex, so they don't think about anything else But killing us....continue.'Rent-A-Vampire:'Of course...so I saw your lovely daughter in trouble and besides the fact that I was enraptured by beauty AND her booty, it was my duty as a self-respecting vampire to SAVE HER! So, I thrashed those virgins with lefts, rights, all kinds of moves until they ran away. Your daughter thanked me and from then on, we just hit it off! It was love at first bite...BLAH BLAH!!'Marius:'HE'S A KEEPER DEAR! How about you boil us up a virgin woman so we can have some 'virgin daiquiri's'...'strawberry' of course. I'd like to get know him better.' Velina:'Right away daddy...but virgin's are getting harder to come by these days.' ..^../ Velina heads out of the living room, Marius scooting closer to our posing vampire protagonist as Marius puts an arm around Rent-A-Villain...asking him in a quiet tone..^../Marius:'So tell me....shes quite the sucker ain't she, huh?' Rent-A-Villains:'Well you know...she always swallows. BLAH BLAH!!' ..^../The two laugh as the scene begins to fade, all being well for Velina as we inch closer to the Wrestle Vessel pay-per-view where Rent-A-Villain will face Snow.
