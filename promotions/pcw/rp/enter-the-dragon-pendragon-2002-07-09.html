Enter the Dragon....Pendragon!!!

9 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/9/2002 (The scene opens to show a jet black Bentley convertible zooming down the highway. The sun beats down on the ragtop, because the top is up, not down. *Flash* We are now inside the car, and we meet the driver. AN imposing looking fellow with medium length blonde hair and a short trimmed blonde beard, he looks as though his mind is elsewhere...One look into his burning blue eyes, though, and we can easily see that this man is quite close to being insane. Suddenly, we are hit with a voiceover...) 'That's me...Venneman Pendragon; 'The Legend'. This hasn't been an easy time for me. It seems that every time I become established, the federation folds or politics take over...' (The shot switches to show the same man, speaking into a cell phone in a hotel room. He looks quite concerned about the conversation...) Pendragon: So, what you are saying is that CCW is dead?...............Management has given up?.......Another fed folds with me as a champion....... (Back in the car, Pendragon looks almost remorseful as he relives the memory...) 'I almost thought that I could single-handedly keep the fed going because of my skill...My immense popularity. Sometimes though, even the greatest of men can make a mistake. I admit, mine are few, but they do happen once in a great while. Now though, I have given myself a true challenge. I have stepped up to where the supposed 'Big Boys' play. I'll admit, it wasn't my first choice, but the contract was too lucrative to pass up....Plus, all the exposure I am sure to recieve.' (Pendragon shakes his head and smiles. Evidently amused, the voiceover picks back up...) 'Do not get me wrong...I am not saying that the talent here in PCW is higher, I am merely saying that there are some established names...None that rival Pendragon, but some that I might glean honor from nonetheless. You see, it is a dark day for the talent of PCW. You fellas have to deal with the mightiest warrior in wrestling today. And yes, I am a warrior. Each time I step into the squared circle, only one thing is important...The Win. With the win comes the glory, the honor, the prestige that my name so richly deserves. In the past, warriors performed heroic deeds on battlefields and the waterways ran red with the blood of the fallen...Nowadays, a warrior must choose carefully his medium of destruction, though I do still respect the old ways...' (The scene suddenly changes. We are met with a shot of an overcast sky. We can occasionally see a flicker of lightning through the clouds. As the camera pans down we see that we are some distance from the ground. In the distance, we can see a man slashing his arms too and fro. With each slash we catch a glimmer of shiny steel. The camera zooms in closer and we are met with the hulking form of Venneman Pendragon. Barechested, he practices the forms of swordplay flowing gracefully from one position to another. With a look at his face, he seems almost to be a throwback to the past. His strong jaw, made stronger by a short trimmed blonde beard, is set in grim determination. His hair is wet due to a mixture of sweat and the light drizzle that falls from the heavens. His skin glistens from the sheen of the moisture and each muscle in his heavy arms and chest are clearly defined with each strike at each inner foe....) 'I realize that the sword is an archaic battle device, and before you foolish children start in on me, I realize that I will be unable to run anyone through with the blade because of the rigidity of today's laws...If only I had been born earlier. No matter, that is not the point. The point is this: It seems the best fight I can get anymore is one with myself. Each slash of the sword is representative of the cutting away of my personal weaknesses. By means of deep meditation in these bouts with my inner demons, I have learned that to be truly complete, to be at one with yourself, you must accept the dark as well as the light. The darkness balances the light in my own soul. Not many people can accept that they are both inherently evil AND good at the same time. One would have to be nearly insane to even believe in such a notion, but it is rue. By accepting myself, I have learned to concentrate upon the things that are important...The blood I spill in the ring; the bodies I send broken from the arena; the glory I get from beating an opponent until he can be beaten no more. These are the things that are important. I live to destroy the enemy...To see him driven before me. I bask in the lamentations of his women, his family. Like music to my ears, each scream I cause makes me smile inside, for I know I have accomplished what I set out to do...And that is to be the best...' (The scene now cuts to the inside of a gym. Pendragon is shown practicing in a ring with men even bigger than he. They know to bring their best, because practice to Pendragon is just like the real deal. He does not hold back. Each man who steps in the ring hesitates a bit as they stare into Pendragon's burning gaze. Each man leaves on a stretcher. It is hard for Venneman to find good training partners because of his brutality, but he pays well, and he seeks the best...) 'I've done some homework on the PCW. You boys like to think that you are untouchable....That the PCW is a far superior product than anyplace else...Past accomlpishments mean nothing to you boys....You know what that means to me? You guessed it....I don't give a damn either...So what if I have been able to rule damn near everyplace I have ever been? So what if I just happen to be the most talented wrestler in this federation? So what if I look around and all I see are men who would give all that they have just to experience being this great for one day? So the question is, what can I prove by taking down the no talent, unskilled, mediocre wrestlers in the fed that I now call home? How can one get honor and glory from defeating lesser men? The answer? It is hard for Pendragon to find a man who is not a lesser being. It is impossible to find one who is as skilled. Glory and honor go hand in hand with victory. Just because the competition is weak, it does not mean that victory will not be as sweet. It just means that I will rule the federation quicker than even I ever thought possible...PCW is in for a world of hurt...' (The shot switches to a frontal view of the man in the car. A crazy grin lights his face and his eyes burn with a ferocity unequalled by other mere men. His voice is deep and commanding, though tinged with a hint of regret...) Pendragon: I guess that I am the new guy, however. Perhaps I should not be so hard on you fellas. I guess that I will have to start somewhere...After all, the champions of PCW sure don't wanna go down in defeat to a new guy. There is something in my contract about the Rising Star division...I guess that is where I am required to start...Don't worry though...I'll be coming for the likes of Steel and Legend Inc. soon enough. I just don't expect you guys to pony up with enough sack to face me.......PCW....Prepare yourselves...Prepare yourselves for my skill, my intelligence, my superiority...Most of all, though...PREPARE FOR THE PAIN!!! (Pendragon starts to laugh. Softly at first, but rising until he is bellowing. The crazed laughter gets louder and louder as the scene fades to black.....)
