A romantic little experiment

12 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/11/02 (OOC - This RP is a Point-of-view type focusing on an idea Teresa and I had) 'It's a wonderful evening in Paris, France. The City of romance is truly sparkling. Aside from the occasional hitch, everything's gone right. I've learned a little more about why humans behave the way they do... thanks to a little experiment I attempted earlier.' Nerezza places down his pen and leans backwards in his chair. The vampire places his hands behind his head and casts a faraway look out of the window. From his hotel, he has a perfect view of the Eiffel Tower, complimented by the sweet smell of the roses behind him. Despite his usual perch being as far above the city as he can get, he leaves the massive tower alone. The evening he'd had was already excitement enough. The living nightmare glanced at his clothing. His rocker appearance had been dropped momentarily for a suave and sophisticated look. A deep, dark red tuxedo covered him, only showing a flash of red in the light, like a two-tone effect. His boots were forsaken for smart shoes, reflecting the image of the lamp in them. His hair was looking exceptional, purple but well-brushed and combed. The only part of his usual clothing that remains is the pair of red spectacles balancing on the bridge of his nose. He even has a pair of pristine white gloves on. The vampire leans back over the table and looks down on his diary. Resting his head in his left hand, he picks up the pen in his right and the sound of paper being scratched by pen whispers to the room. So after a little spying... well, I had to get my information somehow... I set out to make my little 'accidental' meeting with Teresa. I don't know why I chose her, really... I guess it's because she's the only one on the roster that I know that doesn't hate me in some way or another. Cyclonica and Anarchy... well, Cyclonica... she's not bad looking and she dresses nice, but if daddy asked for her to be home by 11 and I failed, I don't fancy an Absolute Destiny Apocalypse. Plus, she's not exactly the type to go dating, I imagine. Anarchy's out of the question. Brandi... front office, enough said. Bertha... Ugggh, not even Vorador looked that bad in the ancient paintings. Ellie's married anyway, but for a successful test I'd have to make it through the night without killing her. I don't think I could do that. Which left me with Teresa. Well, we knew each other from NTICW, so at least we had some background. Plus we have a mutual friend in Scott Taylor. So after calling the Pimply-Faced Intern Kid and asking where she'd been staying, I had to wait around for her to leave the hotel. Time passes pretty quickly for vampires, seeing we have such long life spans. Everything seems so much faster to me than when I was alive. I spotted her leaving the hotel. She's rather good looking, I'll admit that. She's been a lot more aggressive recently, I guess it's my luck. Anyway, I managed to move through the crowd without her seeing me. Thousands of years of predatory stealth and evolved stalking and here I am, trying to keep my head down. She's taking pictures, not paying attention to me anyway. How to make contact... She stopped next to a nice big fountain. I had a look at it myself, it was truly fitting of Paris' reputation. I then made my move, pretending to walk past her looking at my watch. I just clipped her, but I guess after the other night at Fusion, I don't know my own strength. Either way, she almost went in but that girl has fast reflexes. When she turned, I was ready to block a punch, but thought it'd be better to take one. She stopped though, just turned around and told me to watch where I was going. The words just came out of my mouth like I was a kid again... 'My apologies, Miss Tomas. I'm conducting a little experiment - would you care to help my study on human romance?' I could tell from the look in her eye that she only heard half of what I said. She was concentrating more on holding in her anger for me nearly sending her into the water. No harm done, thank Vorador. I think my smile... or the words I used if she heard them... made her laugh, I must have looked and sounded a total goof. She started to charm me, running her hands through her hair. It's near ineffective, seeing vampires can secrete a chosen level of pheromones and charm the birds from the trees if they like. But I guess seeing I'm going to act human for a while, I may as well play along. She blinked - it looked sort of... cute... and then cracked a little joke. 'Okay, just don't bite me or anything. Where we going?' ... damnation. Nice, carefully prepared plan of how to get her cornered so I can ask and I hit a wall. Can't let her see that I'm stumped... I bow to her, slowly and use the time to look around. As luck would have it, there was a rather charming little restaurant. The waitresses wore those ultra-stereotypical maids outfits and I could have sworn one of the guys had facial hair that had more attention and gel put into it than his normal hair. Either way, I stand and point to it without considering my wallet. It's times like this I really regret pulling pranks to add to the 40% we lose anyway. 'Meet me at the front, say, in an hour? Let's dress appropriately, too.' Again, I could tell she didn't catch everything I said as her eyes were fixed on the building itself. Can't blame her, I'm a sucker for art and craft myself, if you'll pardon the pun. She skipped away - perhaps she was just as lonely as me? I wish I knew how to read minds like the vampire who taught me all about what divides us from humans... makes me wish I hadn't killed him so quickly. Either way, I wandered back to the hotel. Gaz still hadn't come back and Natalia wasn't around either. If there's one thing I WILL praise about this city, it's that they know how to build good hotels. Perhaps I should actually condemn that - after soaking inside the bath for what seemed to be forever, I must have drifted off... I woke up underwater. Good thing we don't need to breathe unless it's for making sound or smelling. I could have staked myself when I saw the time - I was already 5 minutes late. After drying off like a madman, I managed to make my hair look better than usual and sprinted towards that restaurant. Then I forgot that you probably won't get far inside a restaurant wearing a red vinyl duster coat and jeans. Luckily, Paris seems to have everything you need in one small space, so it didn't take me long to dash into one of their multiple stores and rush for a decent set of attire. Sadly, it also proved to be more of a sucker on my wallet than I could be on someone's neck. Not to mention that I then had to run like hell ALL the way back to the hotel, change and make it back to the restaurant as quick as I could run. I'd already lost a lot of fuel after Friday when I decided enough was enough and finally let loose all my rage. Now I'm doing the marathon just to learn about romance. I finally made it and there she was... I have to admit, it's not often that you see a female wrestler in an evening gown unless it's some sort of pathetic match where they're made to wear one... minutes before having it ripped off to win the damn match. But Teresa sure looks good in one. If ever Kannon gets off his Scientological high horse or Ellie develops the right brain cells, I may bribe one of them into booking her in a match just to see Teresa in this kind of dress. Dark emerald green, just like Gaz's favourite colour, peeling back on one side and showing her leg. Her hair was in an appropriate French braid too and despite the fact that she looked uncomfortable standing and walking in them, i could tell she'd worn those heels to make an impression. I smiled at her as we met but before she could say anything... which might have been for the better - a waiter showed us to our table. One of those little booth ones, the kind you'd expect in a movie. Charming little place, France. It's not Akihabara, but I've lived in Wales, England, Canada, Detroit and Las Vegas. I think I'll keep my house in Japan. We sat down and I kept my head pretty low. Impressions or not, the guilt that I felt for making her wait was greater than the time I murdered a human for the first time. She spoke to me out of curiosity rather than concern. 'So, Nerezza - you're telling me you've never been on a date?' I replied like a teacher would answer a child. 'That's correct.' She wasn't done. After taking the finishing moves of The Front Office, Playaz Inc and even the time Angelo Deville used my OWN finisher on me back in the rookie days... I'd never felt so humiliated as having to admit this. I prayed to Vorador that Teresa wasn't the gossiping kind and that her attention would be kept on whoever she was facing in the ring. And right now, she had a submission hold on me that was tighter and more uncomfortable than my own Death Marionette. 'And you asked me to come here so you can get a taste of what a real date would be like?' I had to tap out and change the subject. 'Correct again. So, Teresa... now what?' She was hiding her face with the menu. It was an act of pure teasing and I found myself falling for it. Her eyes peeked over the top in a way I've only ever seen in my victims as they corner themselves before I drain them and it was that same, defensive look that had my heart almost beating again. 'First we order our food. After dinner, we take a walk around the city then rent a hotel and have wild monkey sex all night.' I froze. Wild... monkey sex? I had images of her in a leopard-skin bikini like something out of Tarzan, swinging from a creeper on a tree. My face plunged into the menu like an olympic diver to hide my expression. A set of red polished fingernails tugged the menu down. Now I was the victim and she was closing in on me. In lowering myself to the level of humans, was I going back to the pathetic man I used to be before that night in Detroit? Without my fangs sinking into the skin of another, would everything that the Ireulim inside me offered be taken away? Regardless, she elegantly covered her mouth and chuckled. 'I'm just kidding. If you seriously want to know, just do what you feel. Be yourself and if you’re with the right person, the romance will fall into place like a puzzle.' Then she sunk back into her chair, slowly like thick liquid. Beauty, it seemed, had tamed the beast. But... what did she mean? Is romance something to do with our blood? Out of all the thing's I'd learned, I knew that the chemicals in someone's brain control that indefinable thing we call emotion. Since my... change... I knew that some of the chemicals that control my feelings had either gone or disappeared. Could I actually learn about it and redevelop those chemicals with the right trigger, or is emotion something completely unexplainable? Of the blood I'd taken from various people, perhaps I had the matching kind for someone else? Whatever she meant by the right person, I'd just have to carry on the experiment and hope the results that concluded were satisfactory...
