What a life...

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-6-02 ::Introduction:: Mark and Duzza have not been getting along very well as everyone can see that in PCW. They are a very odd team and probably they will be known for that team and not their individual wrestling. Now Mark has a chance to win the PCW Extreme Title on Turmoil. All he has to do is to pin his opponent and bam he gets the title. Sounds easy enough right? Not exactly. He has to team with his partner, Duzza and make sure that he doesn’t pin the opponent if they do win. With what Duzza did on Fusion, losing the match for us, Mark was not pleased at all. Now Duzza will say that he didn’t lose the match, but he did get pinned so therefore he lost the match for the team. Now this is Mark’s chance to prove that he doesn’t care about the team and that he will do anything to hold a title in PCW and to try and make it all the way up to the top. This is only the first step into getting up to the top of the ladder. ::End Introduction:: Mark is sitting down on an international bus that is taken him into a country that is unknown. But the country is known to Mark, but most of the PCW wrestlers do not follow what this country does or is. He is on this bus so that he cannot be bothered by his partner, Duzza. He is sitting on the bus just staring out of the window thinking about what has gone on in the past few weeks. He sits there and he thinks about what direction his career is going and how he can help it out. He thinks that if he wins this belt than he might actually be known in the other feds outside of PCW, maybe he will get more fans, but all of this doesn’t matter to Mark. All Mark cares about is that he proves that he can do this on his own by himself. Yes sure he is in wrestling to kick all of the African Americans asses and to get back at them. But he is seeing wrestling in the ring as a new style. A style to try to win and try to get a belt won by the time he gets out of here. “Violence Jack and Flaymin Aymin are teaming together to take on Duzza and I.” Mark says to himself as he is looking at the ruins of the first concentration camp through the window. “Those two hate each other about as much as Duzza and I, wait I think that Duzza and I have a little more hatred towards each other. But if some way that Duzza and I can co exist then I might have Flaymin Aymin and Violence Jack hating each other in the match and that could open the door for my move into the spotlight.” Mark then starts to think about the times he spent with Violence Jack in ICW a low fed in the wrestling world. ::Flashback:: The scene opens up to the backstage of an arena. There are vendors around, but not many. There is a crowd shouting, but not as big as PCW has. Then there are the wrestlers, who are not as good as the competition in PCW. This is where it all started for Mark and Violence Jack, going to sweatboxes, but it was only Violence Jack getting the glory. We see the usual Chris Havenstorm (The Owner) and Violence Jack in their locker room getting their preppy uniforms. Then the wife of Havenstorm comes through the door of the locker room. “Oh you both look like you are twins.” The wife says as the two men turn around smiling at her. Then the wife points to them and has them follow her out of the locker room. They both follow her as they head out of the locker room with huge smiles on their faces. The wife then looks around outside of the locker room and she sees that no one is around. The crew goes around the corner and then Mark Makonie runs right into Violence Jack and his preppy uniform. “Looks like you guys are going to Harvard.” Mark says as he checks out the uniforms on both of the wrestlers. “Actually we look like we are professional in the business unlike you Mark.” Chris Havenstorm says as he looks Mark right into the eyes. “Well if someone wouldn’t kiss someone’s ass then maybe I would be up to a higher spot in the card.” Mark says as he looks at Violence Jack. “Yes it is true Mark, you suck and that is why you are as low as you are on the card.” Violence Jack says as he looks at Mark. “Actually aren’t you the wrestler who has won only one match, and is like the second to last wrestler in this fed?” Mark doesn’t say anything as he heads past the crew and he goes back to his locker room. He goes back into his locker room and he changes out of his wrestling attire. He then puts on some street clothes and he heads out of the arena to his black Audi car. ::End Flashback:: “I remember the days that I was the low carder in ICW.” Mark says as he is getting off of the bus. “He was the one who was getting the entire spotlight and he always was with the owner twenty-four seven. He went on and won the ICW Title and that just gives me more motivation to beat him.” Mark hits the streets of Germany as he is looking around to what is around. There are some German food shops around with some ancient buildings around. Mark starts to walk down the street back the way he came so that he can get a look at the first concentration camp ever built. “Now for Flaymin Aymin, this is the guy who lost his nut in a wrestling ring.” Mark starts to chuckle as he says that and he thinks that is pretty funny. “Now if I lost one of my nuts I would be pissed, but Aymin just goes and lives his life with one nut. And now does he not get as much pain down there when he gets hit? Well probably since he is about a twig and a berry away from having the women parts. Now before that ever happened it seemed as if Aymin was a pretty popular guy with the ladies. He was I have heard a porn star in the early days. Now I now know the real reason why Aymin is not a porn star anymore, he can’t deliver with one nut.” Mark laughs some more as he disses on his opponents even more. But he knows that this will not be enough to win in PCW since he is not a very winning wrestler in PCW and he knows that he is not that known. “Now I don’t like Aymin for one reason only and that is because of his hair.” Mark says as he looks through the gate of the concentration camp that is in the ruins now. “Why would a white man grow out an afro like an African American? Well I know why. He just wanted to see if since he would grow an afro out that he can probably be like the African American culture. Now that in my opinion is very wrong for a white person to do.” Mark starts to head into the gate of the concentration camp and he starts to see smoke rise from the ruins. There are the pits that are covered up by dirt where they keep the bodies, and that is giving off an odor along with smoke. Mark is thinking about Duzza and the ways how he could screw Mark over so much. Mark wishes that that wrestler would just go away for one match so that he might actually win. But no they have to schedule Mark with Duzza all the time. ”Now if Duzza decides to mess up in our match on Turmoil and has the other team win the belt then I am going to severely go off on him.” Mark says as he starts to head into the direction of the horizon. “I really don’t care about him anymore as if he screws up, he just gets yet another beat down by his favorite friend. We will see who comes out on top at Turmoil for the belt, although it is a tag team match.” Who is going to win at Turmoil? Who knows as this could be the first taste of gold Mark could have in PCW. But will this be enough to beat the other team? No one knows only the management of PCW does, and hopefully Mark will be on top of his game coming up on Turmoil.
