Date: 3/21/03 The trees swished around in the cool breeze emitted from the sky. It was yet another beautiful day outside, as the cold winter of ’03 was almost a thing of the past. This was good for people like Brianna Washington who enjoyed spending hours upon hours out in the hot sun, soaking up the beauty of nature, while getting a slight tan at the same time. “You know what we need Bri? We need a pool.” Milo chirped as he sat at the kitchen table with a glass of cold lemonade in his well-manicured hand. “A pool? We have nowhere to put it.” Brianna replied emerging from the other room wearing a pair of khaki shorts and a light red and white striped spaghetti strapped shirt. “Work your dohickey magic and make a pool that folds into a pocket size or something.” “Milo it’s not going to happen. Besides whatever happened to the YMCA? Didn’t you say you enjoyed going there all the time? Isn’t that where you met Dante?” Brianna throws out trying to get the thought of a pool far from his mind. “Dante?! That man was such a waste of time. Besides he was a weirdo. Remember me telling you his obsession with Harrison Ford? The man had all the walls in his house covered with Ford’s face!!! He had Harrison Ford bed sheets and pillows for crying out loud! I tell you it’s quite scary to wake up and having hundreds of Ford’s staring up at you.” “I thought you said you never slept with him?” “Well ah…” “Yeah, save it.” Brianna says while giving him a slight jab on the shoulder laughing to herself with him feeling a bit embarrassed by his slip up. “So it’s a no on the pool?” he inquires, following her out the door to the back yard. “Didn’t I already specify? NO!!” Brianna replies, taking a seat in the lawn chair under the umbrella, she had already set up earlier. Feeling defeated, Milo turns to walk back inside with a frown across his face. Just as he reaches the door, Brianna throws out a question which causes him to stop dead in his tracks. “Milo, have we gotten anything in the mail from Thomas? He keeps calling me and asking about it but I don’t know what he’s talking about.” “Huh…you…you know how Thomas is, always bothering people. He probably didn’t send anything at all.” “That’s what I figured. I just really wish he’d leave me alone. Sometimes I get the feeling he still hopes we could be together, but then I think about the way he treats me, and it just doesn‘t make sense.” “Forget about everything. Just enjoy your time in the sun and get rested up for your wrestling show tonight.” This time Milo makes a successful retreat back into the house. He goes to sit at the table pondering over the letter Thomas had given him while Brianna was sleeping a few days earlier. Was it such a bad thing that I burned the letter up? I was just protecting Brianna after all. Besides everyone knows what a sleezeball he is…..but even so, I still feel my conscience eating away at me. Telling me to tell Brianna….but I just can’t. Brianna comes walking through the door and startles Milo. “Done with the sun already girl?” “No, just came in for a nice cool glass of lemonade and my sunglasses.” Tell her about the letter now! She won’t be mad, she understands how I feel. “I uh….umm….I” he tries to find the words but can’t. “Cat got your tongue Milo?” Brianna asks quizzically as she pours the lemonade into a tall glass. There’s no use. If he cares about her as much as he put in the letter, then he will write another. Besides I wouldn’t doubt that the majority of it was BS anyway. Just a horndog of a man wanting something he can’t have. “I got my shades and lemonade, now I’m back off to tackle the sun. While I’m out could you go up and check on Grimer?” “How many times do I have to tell you I don’t go near rodents? They give me the heebygeebies!” Milo says shivering at the mere thought. “Sometimes I really doubt your manliness.” she says as she goes over to him giving him a hug, before making her way upstairs to check on Grimer. The scene fades out…..
