The Ho Nam Diary

5 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/5/02 ”The PCW Chronicles” Part One Location: ‘Me Love You Long Time’ Motel in “China Town” San Francisco, CaliforniaDate: ThanksgivingTime: Around 9 p.m. My name is Chiang Ho Nam. I have served under the Black Dragon family since I was seven years old and underwent the 36-Oaths when I was twelve. I have been through many promising times along with many troubling ones. I have lost two brothers, who also served in the Black Dragon family, in gang wars. I have made numerous friends, although a third of them have died in the past twenty-two years, I have had my voice box crushed preventing me to speak ever again, I have been shot, stabbed, beaten and humiliated but I regret nothing! With all the bad, comes the good… I have kept the friends I have made in my heart and in my mind, I have traveled all of China, Japan and now America, I have trained and trained and trained and I am now in a place called Primetime Championship Wrestling. I am here in America once again, while many families celebrate a thing called Thanksgiving, Chen, Chi and myself sit here in a cheap motel. The other two watching cheap porn while I write my thoughts out in this little book. They don’t understand why I write in this book, but they don’t realize how lucky they are for the ability to speak! Many people think I am so quiet because I have nothing to say, while other think I am only quiet for that imposing figure impression. Little do they know that while I am quiet on the outside, I am screaming at the top of my lungs on the inside! Being paired with these two guys is a blessing but in itself a curse. Their maturity level is as high as one would expect of a five year old. But their abilities in the ring are more than enough to make up for their behavior and these are the only two guys I could actually trust my life with! I fear that I may have to entrust those same friends with my life yet again… The three of us are in PCW and the wrestling industry on the commands of the Mountain Lord. Three simple things… One, money equals power! Two, connections equal influence! Three, Gold equals superiority! Combine each of these things and not only do you have power, but you have territory! Our lives depend on our success! Failure is NOT an option! So here we are on Thanksgiving, spending our time watching and waiting. Like so many others on this special day I am going to close with what I am thankful for… “I am thankful for the opportunity to… WHOOP GAZ & NEREZZA!” “Dragon 101” Location: Unmarked room in an old abandoned School in San Francisco, CaliforniaDate: December 1st, 2002Time: Noonish I am surrounded by IDIOTS! I sit here wondering, why! Why am I here? Why am I friends with Chen and Chi? Why did the Mountain Lord send us here to teach younger members of the Dragon family to be “more” Dragon-like? Why? Then it hits me! I sit here and see so many intrigued faces! These kids, literally seven years of age to twelve years of age, they sit and absorb every word Chi and Chen say! It is some of the stupidest things ever but they are eating it up! If these kids are the next generation of Dragons, then we are in trouble! Preparation for the PCW take-over is now underway. We have set up a sort of base of operations, where all our “missions” will begin. We have set up connections through the Black Market, the Underworld, and of course the Police! We have set up pickups for both drugs and weapons; hopefully someone in PCW will be interested… Chen, Chi and myself have made a list of people who we think would be suitable connections and of course who we think would make perfect victims! The only thing to do now, is to test our list and see if we are right! Tomorrow we return to the gym and prepare for Sunday… Gaz and Nerezza… PCW Tag Team titles… Our first real challenge… What will my role be in this battle? What ever happens this Sunday, I know it will begin the “P.W.A. Massacre!” “The Return” Location: “Me Love You Long Time” San Francisco, CaliforniaDate: December 2nd, 2002Time: 10:00 p.m. Now this is what I am talking about! No matter what happens to me in my life, being in the gym, training my ass off makes everything seem right. Beaten by my own father, blamed for his death by my mother, blamed for my brothers deaths, being beaten by rival gangs, losing my ability to talk, losing my one and only after she was raped! Everything just seems to disappear when I am in that gym… Pushing myself to the limits and beyond, being in my own little world, forgetting everything except the task at hand! Today I watched as my two best friends and partners in crime hit the ring for their training. Every time I see them in the ring, I see perfection, harmony, bliss, the pure art that wrestling is, these two bring it out for every one to see. I can’t picture anyone beating these two but they can’t always win… Can they!? This Sunday, I am reminded of the importance… The first challenge we face in this new chapter of our lives. Gaz Green and Nerezza are both great athletes. They are not the Tag Team champions for nothing! They have beaten enough people in their stay in BMW to earn my respect… But that is just between you and me. * Wink * * Wink * Redemption Song was a walk through the park! No challenge, no sweat, no problem! But I guess that’s what they all do, did Rocky face Apollo Creed from the get go? Did Muhammad Ali duke it out with George Foreman right off the bat? Better yet did my father begin beating my mother or did he start out with someone else? The point is, Redemption Song was a warm up! They served their purpose and can now be forgotten. The question now is… Are P.W.A. just fodder for the hungry Dragons? We’ll find out Sunday, won’t we? The P.W.A. Massacre is underway!
