The Death Saga: Soul Road

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-1-02 His mouth was agape as he stared out towards the casem, the road... this 'Spirit Road' as Harle called it, it just keeps going and going. He couldnt speak, words couldn't flow out of his mouth, but he knew this was what he had to do. To get back to earth, to gain revenge for his death... To get back to Talena. He hadn't even put much thought into her, everything has been so hectic, with the death and all. But now that he has her in his mind, he couldnt help but think. Oh, how she feels now... I told her not to come to Shanghai, just in case, I didn't want her to witness anything bad happening... call it a premonition, she must just be finding out.. His mind was a blur, the image of Talena sitting by the phone, getting the phonecall from the PCW front offices, and hanging up, crying... I have to do this... if it involves any chance of me getting out... I have to... DarkStar: Harle... so if I walk the entire length of this road, I can be alive again? Her smile seemed to hide something, but DarkStar was too hopeful to notice. Harle: ...oui. He mentally pumped his fists, but didnt show anymore elation then he wanted too, a sly smile on his face as he looked over, parts of the road seemed to just float there, with nothing holding them up. The farthest part he could see seemed to glitter in the moon light. Harle wiped under her eyes, somehow not smearing her make up as she did. DarkStar glanced over to her. DarkStar: Will you be accompanying me? She shrugged, her hat jingled. Harle: Et iz written that oi will, later on, but until zen... Thiz ez our aur revoir for a toime... She reached her hand out, and DarkStar took it lightly, feeling the satin feel of the glove she wore. DarkStar: Until then... its been grand Harle. She leaned up on her toes and kissed his cheek. Harle: 'opefully, oi will see 'ou again, no? It was DarkStars turn to think. DarkStar: Of course... Unless I get out of this 'hell' hole luv... god that was a horrible pun He adjusted his coat and began to walk towards the bridge like area that is Spirit Road, down here, it could seem a day and be a eternity... he had to be careful. DarkStar walked... Night became day... And day too night again... Spirit road was made from nothing but lost souls, as he walked, each one of the damned screamed out to be helped. This was rough, but he had too... He knew that that smiling face would be there when he got home... Back to Philadelphia... Back to the mortal plane... He was the Dream King... The Lord of all that was mystical, the holder of both dreams and nightmares... He was a filter for all that is evil... He filtered it through his body and made it pure... sacrificing his own sanity... YoU CanT Do IT DrAzAil No... He helped through his harming, every murder was planned before him, he was a filter... Not a filter Drazail His rational mind kicked in. Not a filter... He started to stumble. He was the king... Puppet... No... a KING... Nothing but a puppet... A Lord... of... Of what... You have no powers here of... The Lord of Nothing! NO! ...What? DarkStar screamed, and the wind blew around him whipping the leaves up into almost a vortex, birds and bats screeched from the tree tops as his energy was apparent. His eyes turned white and focused as he looked forward. The Lord... ...of DREAMS His sick smile was plastered on his face as the view changed.... This time it was behind him, seeing what he could see... The castle... the palace... Of the Morning Star!
