After Retirement, what is left?

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/26/02 After Retirement, what is left? It was a question I asked myself countless times. What would I do after retirement? I know I couldn’t continue through all of the ridicule and humiliation, but what would I do if I didn’t. Either way I can’t go back on my word, so come PPV it’s all over. I just wish I had something to fall back on. Portugal will never let me come near their soccer team again, my father left the country, my mother is dead, and I’ve left the only woman I truly loved. This is the truth behind the feeling of Juan Borro. Not the legend, not the myth, not the fable, BUT the truth. *Flickering* cameras flashing, press chasing, hype surrounding, am I such a superstar? Mr. Borro what a game today, everyone is anticipating your match against United States coming up shortly. Oh well I’ll just show them once again, why Portugal is going to take the World by storm in 2002 World Cup. (That was one year ago. I was so young and so arrogant. I didn’t know much of anything accept I was better then most other people at the sport I loved. But the nights were different, when there was no hype on me, I got to find out the truth about myself.) *MEMORIES! * Painful hurting, father crying, mother dying, I’m left to wonder why? God, why can’t someone be here with me? I wish I could still be with my parents, why did you have to take it away. I just wish I could trade all of this fame and fortune for the people I love. (I blamed God for what happened sometimes, but I knew it wasn’t his fault. It was no ones fault actually, just one of those things that happened. Well the next day I was in United States for my matchup and everything changed.) *Rowdy* Fans screaming, teenagers leaping, men drinking, warriors fighting, I wish I was one of them. Hey son, let’s get to our seats, do you all have the posters you made with Frisco on them, that way your mother can see us when watching on television. I glanced at the sign and it said PCW. It was men fighting each other all for one prize, the prestige, the honor, and the devotion. I was tough, why couldn’t I do it? (That’s how PCW started, I announced a temporary retirement from soccer to pursue a dream. It was a short lived dream, but one that will stay with me and lift me up when O get down.) *Business chatting* Corporate meetings, publicity speaking, photo shoots happening, and trainers training, I wanted my chance to be heard. Here you are son, were starting you off here at PWA, we’d like you to meet your roommate Kevin Klaymore. Handshake Me name es Juan Borro. Nice to meet you Juan, I’m Kevin Klaymore. (It started their a dynasty, a tag team genius by the name of Golden Game. It made me famous and brought me to a future destiny.) *Light flashing* PWA, EW, NTICW, Tag Gold, front page of papers, magazines, I was part of the big show. Hey sunny, how bout you some up and work for us at PCW, we need some new tag teams and your just the thing were looking for. Me….me….think good We’d love too. (That was my start to PCW; I knew I’d love it right from the beginning. God was giving me a gift; he knew he took away two people I loved, so he rewarded me with something else I loved in their place. I couldn’t have been any happier.) *bleeding* Title wins, title losses, teams begin, teams die, international corruption, prison time, all along my mind went blind. You are sentenced to 4 years in the Chinese prisons, before eligible for parole. *crying* (My life took a change. The woman I love drifted, my father who I almost got back drifted as well. I was left sitting there with no one to be with me. International Incident came and past so did my emotions it was all over.) *sweating* Working hard, getting far, becoming a star, doing everything your told, all to win that coveted gold. GOLDEN GAME HAS DONE IT!!!!!!!!! TWO TIME! TWO TIME! (I can’t express my feelings at that moment, but it seemed winning it for the second time, made everything fall in pace. All I thought about was wrestling at that point. Anything else was secondary, and even soccer fell behind. I loved those titles and if I could I’d die before I let them go. Thing just didn’t turn out that way, Klaymore was wondering and I was left to fend for myself. I know I couldn’t expect myself to do it all, but I did anyway and I was greatly disappointed.) *honk honk* Foreign relations, stress no patience, wanting more, but getting less, it was now up to me to put myself to the test. 6 Men 1 Gold! A cruiserweight Champion will be chosen tonight. Juan Borro defeats Charlie Loc! Juan Borro wins the Cruiserweight title for the second time. (Just my luck another deuce. Two Time Tag. Two Time Cruiser, It’s just too bad I can’t make it to three. I still hold that belt I won back then, and I love it strongly and will hold it to the end. Whether it is Charlie Loc or Heaven Rayne, I will not end my title reign. I’m Cruiserweight Champion, why am I depressed, I’ll tell you now, what drove me over the end.) *Screaming media* So many rumors, calling us losers, feeling so cheap, wanting to weep, we tried our best and came up short. You let us down Juan. You said you’d get this team in shape and you made us the joke of the World Cup. We underestimated our teams, and the referees didn’t help. Don’t blame it on them, it’s your fault your team wasn’t composed, and it’s your fault guys like Jao Pinto and Couto may never play soccer again. When I shift the blame I shift it on you. What does this mean? It means your fired, and I don’t want to see you anywhere near Portuguese soccer again. (That was it and my heart was crushed. I always said I’d fall back on soccer in Portugal, but now my own people don’t even want me. All I am is one big joke, SUNSHINE. Well call me what you want, but this Sunday I won’t make a joke of myself. I’ll fight for my life, and in the end I’ll either be living with my belt or just plain dead. Nothing in between what so ever.) SCENE FADES TO BLACK!!!!!
