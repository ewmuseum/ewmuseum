Pretty Cool

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-25-02 Trigger: Do you ever feel like you're so close to something… that you're being led on, and any second you could achieve greatness? Do you ever feel the frustration of being so near the warm glow of success, yet so far that you almost give up hope? The Infinite Warrior wishes, so longs that he could speak to another person. He desires the comfort of knowing an intelligent soul is listening… thinking… responding. He laughs. He knows that desire will never become reality… no, he chose the life of a warrior, and vows to stick to it, no matter what it costs him. In truth, what more could it cost him? His dignity? Pride? Self-respect? All gone. All shriveled away from years of selfless deeds… interpreted as selfish deeds… conjured by himself. The realization hit him like a sack of bricks. Trigger was unhappy. He didn't know where along the line it happened, or how it happened, but it was pretty obvious that it happened. All this time with the quests and the journeys, and getting caught up in being a warrior, Trigger had forgotten to enjoy himself. So determined to find the meaning of life… yet he has forgotten to live his own life to its fullest. The Infinite Warrior apparently was not the only one who noticed this travesty. He had heard people talking… mainly at his place of work, Primetime Championship Wrestling. They all felt the same way, the fans and the wrestlers. Trigger is too uptight. Trigger doesn't know how to have fun. Trigger isn't entertaining. The last hurts him the most… he remembers a time when wrestling was not about that kind of entertainment. Trigger is a hard worker. Trigger pushes himself to the limit. Trigger has talent. And yet… the fans don't care. Trigger: Why should they? Nowadays, all they care about is gimmicks. Angles and gimmicks. He speaks so hastily of the fans, but in his heart remains true to them. Some day they would learn to respect him. It didn't matter that they laughed at him. It didn't matter if they called him a jobber. Feeding Trigger to the monsters of the industry (Xavior Kannon, Akiyama), the promotion didn't care about him either. It got him thinking… would it be such a crime to entertain? Would it be such a crime to have fun? The warrior didn't want to breach his character. Never would he don spandex pants and a sparkly cape as he shouted meaningless catchphrases into a microphone. Trigger: I am a warrior. …The only quote that counted in his opinion. But nevertheless, something has been missing from his life for a very long time. Happiness. So he would go have fun, he would go enjoy himself. The reasoning for so many actions of so many people is 'just to have fun.' Happiness--loyalty to the self; the never-ending pursuit of life. The meaning of life? Trigger: Anything's possible. We zoom out on the location. A parking lot… In his hand, Trigger holds a slab of paper. A ticket to a show. It had been Friday, backstage at PCW Fusion that the Infinite Warrior had been given this. Pretty Cool Guys In Concert… The opportunity was not one that appealed to the warrior at the time… even now he was weary. But the Pretty Cool Guys tag team seemed to enjoy themselves. Their lives were all about fun, no ridiculous obligations or self-tormenting punishments. They told him he'd have fun there. They told him he'd have the time of his life. Trigger sincerely doubted the fact. He never liked much music… especially pop-rock. They told him this was different. They told him he'd enjoy himself. He took their word for it. Oh, how Trigger longed to feel how they did, finding endless joy in such petty things as music. He wishes he could just get lost in the mind, forgetting about everything important, all obligations. Even PCW. Just thinking of his upcoming match on Friday Fusion, the Infinite Warrior cringes. Even a warrior can feel shame, and the shame was certainly felt falling down to an opening match after two weeks of main eventing. Not that Trigger despised his opponents so. Scorpion, he was a talented one. In preparing for the match, the warrior remembered their previous encounters. Bernard Colt… who was he again? Oh yes, the psychopath. Standing in the parking lot, Trigger smirks. Trigger: These days, anyone who wants a little attention will act as if insane. The attention I must give him will surely enforce his sanity… this includes his fear. He chuckles, but then stops. Why is it that such demonic thoughts run through his head? Why can't he just relax, be free, and take life one day at a time? He stares down at the ticket in his hand. He doesn't want to go to the concert. But he has to… perhaps he does want to. Fun. Happiness. Joy. Such common words in the English language, yet so foreign to the Infinite Warrior. It would not be like this anymore. He would go to the concert. He would have a good time. Maybe he would even like the music. After all, they had said it would be pretty cool.
