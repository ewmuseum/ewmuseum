Date: not entered (A wrestling column done by Skylar Green only hours before his main event at Friday Fusion, the 7th of February. Note how innocent and carefree he seems to be at this point in time...) For my inaugural Ear to the Ground, in which I poll the PCW wrestlers and crew on random questions that probably have nothing to do with nothing, I decided to keep things simple. After all, if I get to the tough questions right away, there's nowhere to build up to, right? Of course, that's the way things work, start off small. The first times I went up and asked people questions, I asked them what they thought about Syko the Goblin as out new owner. But I had to start off somewhere. That, customarily, is what a greeting is for. I notice that I formed the greeting in a question when approaching others. It was always 'How's it going?' And I'd always get some sort of answer that was either great or not so great. While I was thinking it over, I asked three people what they thought about Syko, with Sandy to help me (according to her, one monkey covered his mouth, the other covered his eyes. The one that was to cover his ears was out sick, and instead they put Jeff Marx in a monkey suit and he was spending all his time marking for whatever Front Office member happened to walk by. That didn't go anywhere. I tell you, Duzza and Bob Vurdo aren't nearly as helpful as they pretend to be. Anyway, once I decided to scrap that original poll, I just went around asking 'How's it going?' and I catalogued the responses. Beforehand, I wrote down the four typical responses I expected (fine, all right, okay, and good), and went out to poll. The table below shows my results after polling all 111 of the PCW wrestlers and crew onhand for the event. Fine 14 people 12.6%All Right 12 people 10.8%Okay 13 people 11.7%Good 32 people 28.8%Other 40 people 36.8% Of course, there's a 0.7% margin of error here, but still. As you can see *ba-dum-bum-ching!* from my little table here, the majority of them did indeed respond as I expected. Approximately 29% of them could attest to having a good day. Then, in decreasing degree of pleasurable experience, we see that as they go down from 'good' to 'fine,' you get people who may not have had a good day but were just saying something to get me to leave them alone and get out of the women's showers (all right). Someone who had a decent day answered 'okay,' whereas someone who had a day just above 'bad' on the scale of How Your Day Went answered 'fine.' Then, I examined (in thought, jackasses) the figures more closely. As I had nothing better to do, I ruminated the numbers in my mind until I came up with the following conclusion: THERE IS AN ALIEN HIVE BRAIN TAKING OVER PRIMETIME CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING AND IT WANTS US TO THINK THAT EVERYTHING IS GOOD! The 29% of workers who answered 'good' to my question are completely under the contrl of the hive brain. Going down in order of control, are the 'okay's', which are moderately afflicted; the 'all right's' are less affected; and the 'fine's' are the least udner control. The workers who answered otherwise are pretty much unaffected by the curse, but be wary as the brain will not stop until each and every one of us is a pawn to its whims of world domination and an appearance on Larry King Live. Personally, I believe the hive brain to be something like the 'brain bug' from Starship Troopers, (I remember seeing that before I lost my eyes to the Cesspool of Suck [Eternal Wrestling]) but I went to Pharaoh, codenamed Anubis, and Rent-A-Hero, aka Twitch from Spawn, two of my fellow crusaders in the fight to overcome the alien menace, for their opinions. Had they give me an answer instead of throwing me out of their houses while having sex (actually, RAH might've been masturbating), I'm sure it would have sounded something like this. RAH: I think [the hive brain] would [be] a central gelatinous mass. It will [take us over] slowly. Pharaoh: It's a hive of some sort, how in the depths of Hell would I know? Special Agent K, who wished his name not to be revealed, (credit to Young Tiger for coming up with such a weird alias for himself... oops) went so far as to suggest Tim Walden as the source of the evil curse of the alien hive brain. This is, of course, all mere speculation, as the only freedom fighter to actually have been in the presence of the hive brain is Big Alan Steele, and he is no longer with us. For now, all we can do is hide in the shadows, waiting for our moment to power morph into our mega forms and destroy the hive brain, free the afflicted workers, and save the world! Yeah, I'm bored. If PCW would give me a challenge in my matches, I might train more. Adam Stryker is from DWO and thus has a bit to prove to me. Anyone I've faced for like the last 5 months has only beaten me through cheating... man, things are getting slow around here. Maybe they should just give me the World Title. IT'S WHAT THE PEOPLE WANT! Or so I assume. I haven't talked to 'people' in so long. But, if they like me, then I'll put on the blind people's boots, walk down the blind people's ramp, get in the blind people's ring, and proceed to whip every jabroni's candy ass, because I am the game, and you can shut the hell up, assclowns! Now get out of my yard because Skylar 3:16 says, 'WOOOOO! Who's Next?' BANG! Oh, man, I just lost SOOO much money on those copyright violations. Oh well, it's not like I'm poor or anything.
