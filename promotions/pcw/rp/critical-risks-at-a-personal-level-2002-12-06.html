Critical Risks at a Personal Level

6 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Critical Risks at a Personal Level Featuring: Tim Walden Date: December 6, 2002 It was gratifying, for about a minute. Victory was almost completely upon me, the defeat of the Trapezoid becoming evident, but for the Continental Title match Friday, he was signing away the right he'd cheated to earn and once that was over with, I would be free to remain dominant... I really should've known better. The HIT wouldn't just give up so easily. This was the man who had put Samuel Potright through hell and back, this was the man who had show in and show out put his fullest effort into frustrating the Front Office..This was no ordinary adversary by any means. I knew I could beat him on the mat, or in that ladder match, but I know now that it was a closer call than I was comfortable with.. And the calls were just going to get closer now. As if the possibility of being forked to death or balled like a melon didn't already send alarm bells ringing in my head, this new thought would be yet more fearsome... Trick or Treat: Three words that would likely strike fear into the hearts of any rational man, yet to me were almost strangely comforting in a way. The match would be hardcore and almost certainly, but I would hopefully have become used to it by that time with having to battle with so many foes in the past year and some that I had spent in rings...I would be in decent condition for it, that shape would not want it any other way..He would hopefully show me mercy on Friday for that reason. And therein lied his mistake.....I could attack harshly as necessary, I still wanted the Continental Title, and I would not let any of the four men get into my way... They all held skill to be formidable opponents, and this was what had me bothered. Where I would usually be able to take it easy against someone like Boca or Vilas, I had reasons to worry on Fusion...The Rush had been in the federation for about a year straight now, and he'd gone crazy on his Turmoil opponents. I still had mild respect to give to him, but I doubted that he'd be interested in helping me, considering how little he valued having someone on his side when we last met along with the Trapezoid...Of course, that time I could have won if not for Tomas, and she wasn't going to be a factor now...She could be no more a factor than Brandi would be, than anyone would be in Versailles. This would be among the five of us purely... Should I win, it would have been earned on my part, through bloodshed and grit, the usage of brutal force against my opposition if need be, there was nothing illegal and nothing I really could do to be disqualified. I was like Lady Macbeth...Well, except the lady part and the being Scottish and all, and the royalty, but still, I had to cleanse myself of my sympathies, my urges to mercy, my very fibers of compassion toward these four men...They all exemplified different skills. Rush's hardcore sense, End's well-schooled technical work for a man without sight, Tommy's obvious high-flying overtones.. ...The Trapezoid's mixture of all three into a seamless style of wrestling...It almost made him the biggest threat by default, but without the killer instinct, it didn't matter so much. Heavy Metal Harry had failed to beat me, why should I hold any more concern for his surfer pal's efforts? Granted, he was more focused usually, but it wasn't a big enough difference to help him. Skylar Green's recent Extreme Title reign would have given him credibility in my mind if not for his gaining it from Semore Smoke, then losing it to Bob Vurdo, and his 'Christmas Wish Campaign' showed a generous spirit that neither Celestiality nor hardcore were very consistent with..His compassion would be too great for the match as well.... But I had to drain my compassion away as well, to have a hope to win..What had these men done that they deserved to be heavily battered and broken by me? I was going to take myself into a state of utter hatred...Yeah, that was what I could do. I could try and empty my mind of the pleasant thoughts, imagine the violence bred by my rage, the reasons for this anger... I laid back, mind struggling to attain a state of anger toward them....What could make me so heartless to attack with whatever cutlery I could find? Beyond the fact that it was either them or me...I had no real connection to Mason, Green or...Whatever the hell Tommy's last name was. I had to find a way to take my rage toward the Trapezoid and reflect it upon all of them...They were going to get in my way otherwise. But if they got in my way......Then I would have to go through them to inflict pain upon him. It was too easy to expand upon my anger...He had done anything and everything to hurt me. He had ended my hopes in every shot to attain a major title, be it World, Continental or Tag, or even the TV Title..But it was in good hands now at lea--I needed to stop thinking the positive thoughts for the moment, this was about the want to hurt him, just as I had been hurt so often... He had hit me with Parallel Bases II and III so often that I almost anticipated at least one of them happening on a nightly basis, though the pain of it didn't decrease much with each successive application, I knew that I probably got in just as much agony on him physically...He didn't just stop there though. He cost me gold, he cost me glory...But I was hurting him just as much, Alliances had not been a kind time for his hopes to achieve success against me....Maybe it did come down to her after all. I didn't wish to consider the meaning too much, but much as I fought through to retain the gold, it still felt hollow afterward upon discovering the repercussions. I hadn't known until watching the tape. Parallel Bases III was really not that much different from other moves when you saw the victim minutes later, slowly getting up..But to see it on tape, seeing her head hit the steel with such utter force, the contempt of the Trapezoid showing as he could probably have put her through so much less to prevent interference, but he obviously meant to send a message, he chose to pull out the super-finisher and strike my nerve. I wished it hadn't worked, but it just plain couldn't not. It was lonely at the top, and she made it more bearable by providing the closest companionship I had for the time..I didn't want to think of her as anything more, but part of me just wouldn't let it go...Long-term, I knew it would only bring more pain to try to put the friendship in such intimate terms with myself. I had to stop thinking about it, it would provide me distraction in my wrestling, it would just lead to pain and suffering on a nightly basis, awkwardness in the daily life, goodness knows I couldn't afford to have Nathan on my case again, two psychotic characters in my life and an infatuation that would bring me eventual heartbreak one way or another.... It wasn't worth it, couldn't be worth it, wouldn't be worth it... The pain was too great. ....But right now, I was about the pain. The greater of mine, the better to drain my sensitivities to that of the others...I could let my mind go off the deep end, just for one match...The Continental Title could bring me back to my senses, to be the only man since Frisco to hold it with the Cruiserweight belt simultaneously would give me some joy in my life to replace whatever I felt was leaving me without being able to get to her, there were probably tons of girls who would be attracted by the lure of double gold...Or it could bring merely more distraction from reality, the allure of my fortune having displaced my attention to the point where I could forget to stop myself, possibly culminating in ultimate self-destruction as one by one, the consequences of my attractions would crash down around me..... I wasn't confident enough in myself, obviously. I'd come through the roller-coaster of the past 20 months of my wrestling career, indy slumming suddenly coming to fruition, the power in my hands, the collapse of the summer...I was still 90 percent as emotionally intact as before. I should have no trouble pulling myself away afterward, I knew it couldn't be so bad as my worst-case scenarios... I could do this....I would do it. For me...For the Front Office... ...For her. The pain was great enough. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
