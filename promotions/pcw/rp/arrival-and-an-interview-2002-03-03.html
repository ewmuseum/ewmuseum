Arrival and an Interview

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/03/02 Well I told you it would be continued. Now we are even closer to the beginning ofMarch Madness. We are closer to a battle between 'Amazing' Mike Storm and Brock Newbludd that is for the Television Title Number one contendership, but it is for more. Both men seem at each other's throats. Both men have had bad things to say about each other, and both see to be anticipating their match up. So now we continue on with what Mike Storm has to say. Scene: Camera opens as the hummer 'Amazing' Mike Storm was driving pulls into aparking space. The engine dies and Storm gets out from the driver side carrying his bag, he is still wearing a hooded sweat shirt and matching gray sweat pants along with his mask. He walks into the arena, and he is passing some crews guys when one calls out. PCW Crew guy: Mike Storm. Scene: Storm turns around as he catches up. 'Amazing' Mike Storm: Can I help you? PCW Crew Guy: Well I was here to inform you that Cal Seaver was looking to get aninterview. Mike Storm: Good, I will go meat up with him soon, I wanted to say a whole lot of things any way. You see him, tell him I will come find him after putting my stuff into my locker room. PCW Crew Guy: Will do. Mike Storm: Thanks man. PCW Crew Guy: No problem, just doing my job. Scene: Storm heads off to where is locker room is. He finds it, and opens the door. He drops off his bag in the room, and then leaves it in search of Cal Seaver. He is walking around, but can not find him, he runs into PCW official, Bob Charlie. Mike Storm: Hey, Bob, have you seen Cal Seaver? Bob Charlie: Yeah, I just saw him back that way. Scene: PCW referee, Bob Charlie, points back to the direction he was just walking from. Bob Charlie: We was right around the corner. Mike Storm: Thanks Bob. Bob Charlie: Glad to help you. Scene: Storm heads away from Bob Charlie. Storm heads the way he was directed. He turns the corner, and sure enough there is Cal Seaver talking to the PCW Crew Guy Mike Storm had just seen. Storm walks in there direction and the PCW Crew Guy points out Storm to Cal Seaver. Storm reaches over there. Mike Storm: There you are Cal, thanks man. PCW Crew Guy: No problem, now I will go get back to work. Scene: The Crew Guy heads off as Cal Seaver starts his interview. Cal Seaver: Well, Mike Storm, here we are, the arena for tonight's March Madness, this was your first pay per view just a year ago. You remember it? Mike Storm: Do I remember it? The Pay Per View, and the events of that night would haunt me for the rest of my life. I mean that was the beginning of the Storm curse. The beginning of me being associated with the low talent level of my two younger brothers. No offense to them, but the fact is, I am above them and any other Storm ever in a PCW ring. I went from a top prospect, to that masked one in the Storm Brothers. I mean the worst part of it all, was they did not even succeed in their purpose. They were supposed to help me if Slade and whoever had been dressing as me came out to the ring, but they could not even do that. Cal Seaver: In all fairness, they had to face off against four men, in Slade and the three Storm impostors. Mike Storm: So? They were unsuccessful, that is the bottom line. They hurt me to two ways. My chance for the PCW Cruiserweight Title was ruined that night, and my PCW career and my stock in this company was destroyed. I have worked to rebuild it. I defeated AJ and ran him out of PCW, luckily Jay has not showed up here, and hopefully he never will, because I do not want to have to deal with him too. It will waste a good match on you. I ran Kory out of PCW, and made him stop using my last name. Luckily I am done with that, luckily I have finally been allowed to move on to the next stage of my career, and it only took me a year. Cal Seaver: Okay well that brings us to today, you are currently competing to become the number one contender for the television title, and you face of against Brock Newbludd. Mike Storm: Yeah, and you know what? Cal Seaver: What? Mike Storm: You do it every time. Cal Seaver: Sorry. Mike Storm: What I was saying is how Brock Newbludd has been complaining about being considered the underdog in all his matches. About people going against him. Well, Brock, let me tell you why. It is because you suck. As far as I remember, you have not won an actual match since you returned. I mean look at it. First match back, I pin you in the center of the ring, 1..... 2..... 3, one of PCW's few clean victories. Then you go on to face Bobby Stormbringer in the first round to get to the main event tonight, and as expected, you were miserably defeated. Then there were a bunch of no decisions, so basically as far as I remember, you have yet to win since you returned. Unless you had some other unimportant match here. Well, this is why people do not predict you to win, because you have yet to actually win any matches that people know of. Mike Storm: Let us face it though. No one really gives me much respect, or a chance to win. The fact that I am predicted over you by people shows how much you truly do suck. I mean I am starting to feel sorry for you almost.... Actually no, I really do not care about that, the fact the thing is you will lose. I will be the one to stand victorious in the ring as you lay a bloody mess on the floor just outside the ring as my hand is raised in victory. I will show the whole idea of a Storm curse to sleep for ever. Cal Seaver: Well you made comments about him under estimating you and over looking you. Mike Storm: That is obviously true. They went to a strip club first off all. Which is sad in two aspects. They are married, so obviously their wives are not letting them get anything. Their wives are obviously smart women who married them for their large PCW Checks, because everyone in PCW makes good money, no matter how unimportant they are. Too bad for Blood's wife that is he is not working anymore, then again, maybe she is just a complete whore or some he met that failed one of those twelve step programs with him. Then there is the fact they had to attack a bouncer together to get into a strip club. That they could not get in themselves. It was truly pathetic. Mike Storm: Not only that, but then he sits there with him, playing video games, and planning an after party. Well, there are two things that will make that party terribly pathetic. One is that fact that Brock will lose, and will just be drowning your sorrows instead of celebrating a win. The second thing will be the fact that the only people that will show up are Brock and Stephen, because no one really likes going to a party for a loser. Cal Seaver: Well.... Mike Storm: You know, something else that bothers me about this guy. He is always with his brother. I think I have figured out why though. You see, he and his brother are known for their tag team title reigns and success as a tag team, but the fact is that they can not do a damn thing in a singles career and nobody cares about them. Now that they show up together every now and then, Brock thinks it will help people give a damn about them. He thinks it will help get him noticed by the people. Help him get a little bit of a reaction besides indifference when he walks out to the ring. That is why he always has his brother show up. Cal Seaver: Well Brock did way that your attempts to clean PCW of bad things are a waste. Mike Storm: Oh really? A waste. Think about that. I already helped PCW by eliminating AJ and Kory. Now that is far from what is needed. It is only a start, I will continue to try and get rid of people like you, to try and eliminate, or at least expose the problems of PCW. Now you call attempting to eliminate problems as a waste of time. Well, let me gladly prove you wrong. I mean are the police officers must be wasting their time then. Arresting rapists, murders, and other criminals is just a waste, I mean because that is getting rid of the bad, and according to our friend Brock that is a waste of time. Cal Seaver: I do not think he meant it like that. Mike Storm: No one asked your opinion. This is what Brock said. Now then when buildings are on fire that is bad right Cal? Cal Seaver: Of course. Mike Storm: Well why do firemen waste their time putting out fires if according to the wise Blood Brothers, getting rid of bad is a waste. Cal Seaver: Again I don't think... Mike Storm: Again I do not care. The fact is that attempting to get rid of bad is far from a waste, sure I may never be completely successful in my task, but I will never stop trying. I will keep eliminating and nullifying people like Brock Newbludd and their actions would help PCW greatly, and I will not stop, until I retire. Cal Seaver: Brock said he would be sober for this match. Mike Storm: He has to be, otherwise he can not climb the ladder. You know what though. I doubt Brock has been sober since he came back. By sober he means he will not drink as much I bet. Let us face it, no one believes it. No one thinks he will not drink, that is like a kleptomaniac saying that he did not take it, or Jon Kano deserving to be in tonight main event. It is simply not believable. Cal Seaver: Then he said he was not jealous of you. Mike Storm: That is just loser denial. He then went and said I should be in the Super Friends. Funny, they do not make that show anymore, they make a new version of it though, it is called Justice League, and I would not mind a guest appearance on that show. Cal Seaver: You know this? Mike Storm: Yeah, I seen it a few times. Cal Seaver: And people call me gay. Mike Storm: Hey, I was just flicking during commercials and I saw the Flash and Green Lantern and I had to watch. They made up a new character, this Martian guy, it was pretty cool. Cal Seaver: You have got to be kidding me. Mike Storm: No there really is a Martian guy. Maybe they can bring in Rent A Hero to the show, it would be nice to know he is still making some money. Cal Seaver: I don't believe this. Let us get back to Newbludd. He did say your first win meant nothing and he would win this war. Mike Storm: Not at all. I have already stated me Blitzkrieg plan. I let him know what is coming his way. He will not be able to handle it, even though he should know what to expect. He can not handle it. Cal Seaver: He said you are over looking him. Mike Storm: How the hell could he accuse me of that? I do not ever, over look anyone. I have been more focused then anything, than for any match I have ever been focused on. I want to be Television Champion, I still want to save that title, because the division has recently been in the crapper. Miyagawa is no doubt a man worthy of the belt, and a serious contender tonight in the main event, but he is not the man to restore the Television title's value. He does not seem to really care for it, he seems to think he is above it. He seems to thing he is better than the TV belt, and I do not like it. Cal Seaver: Anything in closing? Mike Storm: I am pumped for tonight. Last year I was screwed over at this pay per view, this year, it will actually help my career. It will be like starting over. It will all start over with me beating Brock Newbludd. I will be victorious. I will not allow someone who does not deserve it to win the shot get it. It is that simple. Brock Newbludd fits into the role of that someone. I will not allow you to get that shot Brock. You do not deserve it. You are going to learn that I truly am, simply AMAZING! Scene: Camera fades out as Storm walks away from Seaver.
