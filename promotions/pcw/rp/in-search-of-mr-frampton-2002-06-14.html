In Search of Mr. Frampton....

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-14-02 ::Introduction:: After the loss to Ricky Thunder by DQ, Mark was not mad at all. Actually he was happy that some of the members of The Political Machine came down and helped him beat down Ricky. As everyone knows he is the next big thing to happen in PCW. But that is later to come and right now Mark has a whole new wrestler to think about for Friday Fusion. He has to worry about Mike Frampton. Mark doesn't know much about Mike Frampton, only knowing that his dad beat him a lot when he was a kid and that Mike was a orphan up in Canada. He doesn't know a lot about the past of Mike Frampton in PCW, but hopes that he will pick up a victory. Mark has not picked up a victory since the re-signing of PCW and that is probably because of his workrate. His workrate has been down, but that is not going to bother him at all as a new era is going to come upon him soon. ::End Introduction:: Thinking about Frampton on Fusion Mark has an idea about his next setting for this promo. He thinks about Frampton's family and about how his dad was an alcoholic. So Mark is in search for the nearest alocoholic place that people who have a problem go and they talk about it in meetings. They are there for a while until they can get their problem under control. Mark flies down to Dallas, Texas to where Frampton's family is located and hopes to find Frampton's dad there as well. 'Do you know where the alcoholic place is here for people with a problem?' Mark asks to a local cowboy on the street of Dallas while Mark has an innocent look on his face. 'Well I recon that you take this interstate over to this street and then take a right and then take a left and then...' Mark cuts off the cowboy as he is getting sick and tired of hearing his Southern voice. 'Nevermind I will go and find it myself.' Mark says as he turns away from the cowboy and starts to head in the other direction. 'I knew all of the Southern people were no good, either they are black and lazy or cowboys who can't even give simple directions to a place where you need to go. What is the world coming to?' Mark says loudly that the cowboy heard him. Mark keeps on walking down the street as he hears the cowboys voice through the streets once again as he is getting annoyed by hearing it. He is passing African American people left and right throughout this hillbilly town. 'This is a disrespect to me and to my fans that I have, which are not that much, about how many people are actually dumb down here in America.' Mark says as he passes another cowboy, then another African American. 'These people are the disgrace of American and Frampton probably grew up with this. That is why he is like he is, dumb and has that stupid accent that no one in the PCW can stand. I am going to whip that accent right out of his voice that he will be talking German after his match with me. But that is too long to come right now, I have to go and find his daddy that he cherrish so much. I love going around the US and looking for wrestler's family. I mean I give them a little reunite after they see this on PCW.' Mark is still walking down the major interstate of Dallas and he sees a sign posted up on a light pole. It reads: Alcoholic Anonymous (AA) is starting back up this week. If your drinking is ruining your family then we can help you, we have the best staff and the best facilities around the state. See you there.Directions ---> 'That is the place in where I will find Mr. Frampton because I know that probably his family didn't put up with his drinking, who would they are all Southerners.' Mark says as he is making up his jokes as he goes along, but knows they are not funny only to make fun. 'He has to be sitting there in the cell that they have sweating because he has not had a drink in the past 10 minutes.' Mark puts the flyer in his pocket as he follows the major interstate down Dallas, cars passing him like Lincolns, Mercedes, and Chrylers on the busy interstates. Mark comes to the side road in which the directions tell him and he starts to take that side road down the dirty streets of Dallas. This part of Dallas looks like a dump with trash scattered outside everywhere in front of buildings and homes. Toys still left out with rust formed on them from the rain that has been here in the past few days. It is just sad to see the community down in the South like this, see out West people that are like this are helped into a helping home so that they can actually have a home. 'Where in the hell is this place at?' Mark wonders to himself as he is out of breath bent over. 'I have been up and down this street at least three times and I still haven't found this damn place. See the Southerners back at the directions, they can't even write them.' Mark pulls out the neon green sheet that has the directions to the AA place. He checks them out on the map of what they drew. Mark saw it wrong and he sees that he has to go down another side street to get to the place. 'I swear if they would've made the map more clear then I would've been there by now.' Mark says as he is upset to think that he was wrong. Mark folds up the neon green flyer and sticks it right back into his back pocket of his blue jeans that go right to his toes of his black boots. He starts to walk down the side street that will lead him to the AA place. He starts to pass red brick buildings with some moss growing on the sides of the buildings. He then sees some buildings with some aluminum siding coming off of it. He gets to the building with the big letters of AA right above the entrance of the building. The building has gray bricks laid out with some red bricks here and there on the building where they had to replace the gray ones. Mark heads inside the two glass doors that lead to the front of the AA. 'May I help you sir?' asks the lady at the front desk who is arranging some files. 'Yes I am here in search of a member that might be here in this alcoholic manor.' Mark says as he walks up to the desk and he looks at the lady. 'Well tell me the name of the gentleman or woman that you need to find and we can look them up in the computer.' the lady adds as she looks up and sees who is right in front of her desk. 'Well the last name is Frampton.' Mark says as he is surprised that they have a computer set up instead of just having it on file. The lady rolls back in her chair in front of the computer and touches the mouse to wake up the monitor. She then goes into a program with all of the members names and she puts in Frampton in the place where it says last name. She clicks and then she reads off some of the names that are listed. 'There is a Dan, Jack, and Dewey that are in here from the Framptons.' the lady adds as she is doing her job and trying to get this for Mark. 'Well Dewey sounds like a Southern name so I would like to see him and talk with him.' Mark says as he hopes that it is the right man of Mike Frampton's dad. 'One moment please.' the lady says as she starts to pick up the phone to page an attendent. 'You may have a seat over there and someone will be here to assist you.' the lady says pointing to the chairs with padded seats on them and that is all. Mark knocks his fist on the desk and then starts to head over to the chairs. He sits down in one and he starts to think about what he is going to say to Dewey and if this man is the right owner of Mike. Mark sits back comfortable in the chair and he puts his fist up to his chin to look smart in thinking. Mark then gets out of his deep thoughts when he hears a women's voice. 'Would you like to come this way so that I may show you where Dewey Frampton is located.' a Chinese lady says as she sits down right next to Mark. 'Sure would!' Mark says excitidly to see if this is the right man who beat Mike. The lady gets up from the chair and signals to Mark to come and follower her to see who Dewey is and where he is located. They go thorough a door which a code is needed to enter. The door shuts when they both enter that sounds like a tight seal is on the side of the door. Mark and the lady head down the hallway that is lined with rooms with room numbers on them. It is just like a hotel, but probably won't be that nice on the inside. 'Here is the door to Dewey Frampton.' the assistant says as she points to the door which is numbered 10. Mark nods and the assistant goes down the hallway back to the coded door. Mark then knocks on the door of the one the assistant told him to. 'Who is it?' a deep sounding voice says from the inside of the room. 'I am a man that wants to talk to you about your son if you have one.' Mark says hoping to get the man to let him come in. 'Hold on.' the man yells as he sounds that if he is drunk. You can hear the man inside coming to the door and can hear the television going at the same time with some kind of sports game with the crowd cheering. The locks come undon and a man in a white t-shirt and blue jeans is standing in front of the door. 'What do you know about my son?' Dewey asks wondering what he knows. 'Do you have a son who is Mike?' asks Mark as he is hoping to get a yes out of Dewey. 'By a matter of fact I do.' Dewey says as he looks into Mark's eyes. 'Why don't you come in for a little bit. Mark steps inside, right next to Dewey who gets inside more. The place that he is in is small, it only has a bathroom and a bed with a television. It is like prison just without the bars. Mark goes in and sits down on the bed where the television sits in front of. Dewey comes back from locking his doors and he sits down right on the bed next to him. 'What do you know about Mike since I left him at the orphanage in Canada?' Dewey asks as he seems interested and sobered up. 'Well I wrestle him in a match tonight and I just want to get your input on how it was just hitting your own son?' asks Mark as he is wondering what Dewey will say. 'Well it felt great when I was drunk and when I was sober.' Dewey explains. 'But that wasn't too much, the sober part, I just got out my belt and thwhack right down on his back. Sometimes I just put my fists to him and bam right to the ribs. I am glad that I got rid of that little carcas because he was always bugging me for money or some other shit like a skateboard.' 'Well it sounds that you haven't changed from what Mike has said.' Mark tells Dewey as the watch some of the World Cup on the television. 'I got into this clinic because my wife was not happy with me and I wasn't happy with her.' Dewey says as he starts to get emotional. 'I just wish I could be sober all of the time so that we can get along and actually have a family. That is why I had to get rid of Mike because the family was falling apart. I just want a family!' With that said Dewey bends over in his knees and he starts to cry. Mark sits next to him thinking about Mike and how he is going to wrestle him with what his dad said. Wondering how Mike felt getting beat up by his dad, but Mark doesn't care if it hurt or not because Mark has no heart at all. All Mark is thinking about is Friday Fusion right now and that is it. (Scene Fades to White)
