Partyin' Wit' A Nazi

23 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23.08.02 The scene opens in a nice suburban area. The houses are all kept tidy, with lawns mowed and the street clean. The sun is shining, but it is going down, as it is about 9.30pm. Children are just starting to come home on their BMXs and the lights in the houses are just coming on. The street is pretty empty, what with all the cars parked in the garages and drive ways. A few cars come up and down the streets, along with the occasional child who is rushing home to make sure he doesn’t break his curfew. Now it is about 10pm and the sun has set. The scene moves to a white Volkswagen Golf, which is parked half up onto the curb and half in the road. Inside the car are two men, one African American, one European American ;) . The two men are Duzza and Mark Makonie. Makonie is behind the steering wheel, while Duzza is in the front passenger seat. Duzza is dressed in a pair of baggy white pants and he has a big baggy black hoody on. His hair is set back in long cornrows. Makonie meanwhile is dressed in some jeans and has a white t-shirt on. Duzza: “A’ight bro, now tell me, why the fuck am I sat in a car with a nazi in the ‘burbs?” Makonie: “Well Duzz… since we’re teaming now, I thought perhaps we could try and get closer” Duzza: “Closer? What kind of sick perverted white boy are you!?” Makonie: “Not like that. Man, what’s the point, your all the same, get out of the car Duzza… see you at the arena” Duzza: “First off, I was joking and second of all I am in the suburbs. It ain’t safe for a black man to walk around the suburbs foo’! All I gotta do is trip over and the next thing you know I’ma ‘bout to get a curbing” Makonie: “Yeah well… it wasn’t a funny joke” Duzza: “Now, for real, why we here?” Makonie: “Look I told you, I want to win gold, I cant do that with a tag partner I hate, so I got to thinking, maybe we could hang out. Just this once, and then that’s it” Duzza: “A’ight then playa, you wanna get all chummy then..” Makonie(interrupting): “I don’t want to get all chummy with you Negro. I just want to do well as a team, ok?” Duzza: “A’ight man, whatever. Can we get out of this place? These places give me the creeps?” Makonie: “What? The Suburbs?” Duzza: “Yeah… now let’s go!” Makonie can’t help but laugh at the thought of this big tough thug being creeped out by the suburbs. Makonie starts up his white, German car and drives away. Duzza: “So… you ready for our match wit them two white boys?” Makonie: “Yes. I am always ready Duzza. Are you?” Duzza: “Who me? Please…” Makonie: “Why am I not surprised” Makonie parks the car in a dark, gloomy part of town. There are lots of people on the streets, all of them white. Most of them have skinheads Duzza: “Where da fuck are we man? I ain’t never seen so many white people” Mark Makonie: “This is where I like to spend my time” Duzza: “So… what is there to do around here? Beat up a innocent Negro? Mug a poor Mexican?” Makonie: “Nah, we leave that stuff for television. Let’s go to a club” Duzza: “Err… you don’t actually expect me to get out the car do you?” Makonie: “Duzza, don’t be a faggot” Duzza: “Faggot? Please. I am surrounded by neo-fucking-nazi’s. I ain’t trying to catch a curbing” Makonie: “Listen, I am well respect around these parts, you’ll be fine” Duzza: “Cracker, you fuck me over and I’ll come back for you, belive that” Makonie: “Ok, Duzz whatever, let’s go” Duzza, slowly and very nervously gets out of car and puts up the hood on his sweater. Duzza’s father was murdered by neo-nazis years back, and the thought of getting killed by them now, must be playing on his mind. As he steps out of the car, it seems like everyone of these white boys is looking at him, the hate in their blue eyes is not hard to see. Makonie looks over at all of his fellow nazi’s, and looks back at Duzza, he calls Duzza and then walks into a small club. Duzza looks around at all the nazi’s, before quickly walking into the club. Inside the club, in one corner is a stage, with a area that could be a mosh pit and a bar in the opposite corner. Makonie and Duzza sit at the bar, Duzza looks around, watching his back for nazi sneak attacks. Cos you know, them nazi’s are sneaky folk. Makonie: “Can I get a beer for me and my fri… acquaintance” The barmaid looks at Makonie as if he has gone made, before getting the drinks. Duzza: “So…errr… nice place…” Makonie: “It’s not bad… been coming here for years” At that moment, a ignorant ass combination of badly played guitars, screaming and drums starts coming from the stage. A bunch of people in the club go to the dance floor and start moshing. The band are obviously some sort of a nazi-rock band, as they are singing about “killin the niggaz” Duzza looks at the bunch of skinheads moshing away to this ‘music’ and notices Makonie nodding his head to the non existing beat and a look of disgust comes across his face. Duzza: “What da fuck is this?!” Makonie: “It’s music. Not, none of that rap crap you listen to, this is real music” Duzza: “Damn white folk… y’know rock music was created by blacks don’t chu?” Makonie: “What? Shut up. Rock is pure white” Duzza: “Rock is about as pure white as Harlem!” Makonie: “Whatever Duzza, you know, you really shouldn’t piss me off. I walk out of here, leave you here, and your dead… don’t act like it isn’t the truth” Duzza: “Ah, whatever… yo…check this out” Makonie: “What? What are you going to do? Duzz? Get back here” Duzza gets up from the bar and walks over to the big ass mosh pit that has formed, somehow he manages to make it to the front of the mosh pit, like a man with more money than sense, he jumps onto the stage, shouts something which can’t be heard over the ignorant shouting of the lead ‘singer’ before diving onto the mosh pit. Duzza is trying to stage dive into a floor full of neo-nazis. Mark Makonie watches in shock as Duzza dives into the crowd and then suddenly disappears into the crowd. No doubt getting his ass kicked. Makonie: “For fucks sake…” Barmaid: “Shouldn’t you go help out your buddy?” Makonie: “He ain’t my buddy… he’s my partner” Barmaid: “Well, he’s about to become your ex-partner” Makonie looks over at the most pit, and although he hates Duzza, hell he hates everyone who isn’t white. He rushes over to the mosh pit and a few moments later, he emerges with Duzza, who looks quite beaten, but doesn’t seem particuly bothered about the ordeal he just went through Makonie: “Fuck was you thinking Duzza” Duzza: “It seemed like a good idea at the time… man…let’s get out of here… I know a place to chill” Makonie: “Where?” Duzza: “A hip hop club on 110th street” Makonie: “You must be joking” Duzza: “Hey, you took me to this nazi shithole..now I’m take you to the hood…” The scene fades as Duzza and Makonie quickly leave the bar and get into the white car Makonie owns. Will Makonie be dumb ebough to go to the ghetto with Duzza? Will Duzza even make it out of the white land alive? Who knows… (End of Part One)
