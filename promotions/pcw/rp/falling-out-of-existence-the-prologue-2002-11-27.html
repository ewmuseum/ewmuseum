Falling Out of Existence -- The Prologue

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 27th, 2002. Almost inaudible rustling is slightly heard through the darkness. The house is quiet with the Gods looking over it, and its occupant, Amber Token. She is peacefully sleeping in her queen-sized bed; which is overcrowded with pillows, and blankets, and quilts of the entire sort, making it a very comfortable and warm sleep. The rustling gets a little louder, as it is obviously nearing Amber’s bedroom, which is left ajar during cool nights much like this one. With the stars’ light the only thing illuminated the house, the shadow of a stout figure swiftly passes through a maze of hallways and rooms. With the shadow flashes off the partially lighten walls, the figure makes its way into Amber’s room, opening the door a little more, enabling himself access to the room. “Poor girl…” The manly voice fades into the darkness as the stout figure slowly walks from the seven foot doorframe, toward Amber’s bed. The figure drops down to one knee on the edge of her bed, as the figure leans over and strokes Amber’s hair, pulling it slightly to his nose to inhale her feminine smell. “Too bad she has to go…permanently.” The figure chuckles a deep laugh as he continues toying with Amber’s hair, while taking off one of – the presumed – his gloves, revealing naked flesh. He brings his hand up to her face, stroking her cheek with his index finger, as if wiping away a tear. “What’s wrong girl? Can’t you fend for yourself?” As he whispers to Amber’s sleeping body, he shivers; the cool air running down his spine, and the adrenaline running through his veins. He shakes his head, cracking his neck, easing his tense body. He slowly rises to his feet, and looks down on Amber. He quickly lunges his arm forward, and grabs Amber by her tender throat, waking her up abruptly. “Wha—“ His grip gets stronger as he easily lifts Amber’s light frame out of her bed, and pins her against the wall. Now, only in a white, silk nightgown, Amber remains petrified as she looks at the attacker. Through the darkness, she can make out the crew cut hairstyle he wears, as well as the goatee that covers his chin, and goes right under his bottom lip. Trembling, she starts kicking and yelling at him, planting a kick to his gut through the open black vest that he is wearing. He doesn’t budge. “Your pathetic little kicks do nothing to me. You’re futile. And I, and the rest of us are far superior to you, girl.” He releases his grip from around her throat, as she falls to the carpeted floor with a thud. Her head slams against the wall, and she quickly reacts by holding it in pain. The man leans over to her, brushing his black cargo pants off as the minimal amount of light gleams off of his black combat boots. “Useless, girl. And you will be punished for it.” She looks up at the man, tears rolling down her cheeks as her eyes start to turn red. Amber solemnly curls up into a ball, holding her legs close to her body as she tries to speak, gagging a little from the death grip that was around her throat. “W-w-what…do you want from me?” He laughs for a moment before looking around quickly. He pulls on Amber’s nightgown, drawing her closer to him. She tries to back away, but the man overpowers her. “Where’s the damn light switch. If you want an answer, I’m the type of man who will make sure keep eye contact…and what’s this? I can’t see your beautiful little eyes in the dark, can I?” She shakes her head ‘no’ cautiously. The man smiles in the darkness, as Amber points to her right, where the light switch is located in the bedroom. The man pushes Amber back, slamming her against the wall again as he stands up. “Don’t move…or it will be the last time you do so.” Amber, now obviously very frightened, stays against the wall as the man walks to the right, where Amber pointed to the light switch. He flips the switch upward, causing the room to flood with light. The man turns around to see Amber, still huddled over herself, whimpering with tears rolling down her cheeks. “Scared, are you? Perfect.” The man walks back to Amber, and leans over, pulling Amber up by her shoulders, and pinning her against the wall, yet again. Amber looks up to the man’s cold gray eyes. “What do you want from me?” The man laughs again as Amber turns her head to one side, avoiding eye contact. The man stops laughing, and his face turns to a stone-faced expression. He slaps Amber across the face, causing her head to go straight, and her eyes to make contact with his. “It’s not what we want from you…it what we need of you.” She struggles to get her left arm free of his grip, to hold her face in pain. With slight confusion, she looks up to him again. “What do you want of me, then?” He stares directly into her eyes, almost penetrating her soul by doing so. She tries to turn away, but the man grabs her by her jaw, and turns her head to him. “If you have to ask…you’ll never fully understand.” She looks at him for a moment. The man releases his grip of her, and stands up, turning around and walking to the door. Amber slides up the wall with her back, but the man turns around. “If you tell anyone about this…you won’t be in existence much longer.” « § » “Wake up!” A soft, gentle voice calls out to her, as a tanned arm is shaking her awake. Amber rolls onto her side from her original position; sleeping on her stomach, but keeps her eyes shut. “Huh? What is it? Is it time to go?” The same voice beckons her to wake up, again. “Wake up, Amber. C’mon…you’re scaring me, here.” Amber opens her eyes up; no man trying to hurt her, just her old friend. Her friend is dressed out in a silken nightgown, much like Amber’s, but silver rather than white. Her ample breasts create sort of a mountain on her chest, as the rest of her body is slender, and curvy. Her light blonde hair dangles down onto Amber’s arm, irritating the skin in some ways. Her icy blue eyes look down onto her friend as she speaks gently to her. This is Aislinn, Amber’s long time friend from Brazil, originating from Manchester, England. Her accent is still with her, although mixed with a more American sound. “Wake up…wake up…” Amber sits up, throwing the blankets to the floor, and looking up to her friend. She looks around the room; it is the same as it ever was. “Where were you?” Aislinn looks at her quizzically for a moment. She looks around the room, following Amber’s eyes. “What do you mean where was I?” Amber looks to her friend before raising her index finger, about to speak. “I mean –“ Aislinn cuts her off. She looks at Amber with a worried look on her face. “I was sleeping in my room, like always. I heard you screaming, and I thought you were in trouble.” Amber furrows her brow. “What time is it?” “Three fifteen in the morning. Are you alright?” Amber sighs in relief. She feels the back of her head for any bumps, or bruises. She looks down at her body and tries to find any sort of pain, using her Wiccan powers to search without looking. “It was all a dream…”
