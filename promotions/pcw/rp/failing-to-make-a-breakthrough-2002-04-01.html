Failing To Make a Breakthrough

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/04/02 The shot opens up on a quiet, sedated street. The sky above is a lingering Grey which seems to dull the entire scene with no sunlight breaking through to shine onto the trees that tower over either side of the street. “Why are we here again? It’s, like, a Sunday...I thought that was a Scientologist’s day or rest or something?” The serenity of the scene is then shattered in one sentence from the whiny Eleanor Hall as she, accompanied by Xavier Kannon walk into the shot, strolling down the secluded street before crossing the road that only 1 car is driving down. Eleanor, dressed in hippie style 60’s chic is wearing a plain Green tank top with a pair of flared pale Blue jeans with floral embroidery. Her long, wavy Brown hair with subtle Brown highlights is un-tied and falls down her back, only a Red floral head scarf helping to hold it in place. Xavier : “No my little Poon Puppy, that it’s us....”...And it’s because I want to show the world what bad rolemodels can do to a nation.....And there are no worse examples to our country’s youth than Brock Newbludd and Jeremy Howard.” The Scientologist is wearing a long, Brown leather coat, the tail of which dances off the sidewalk, just skimming on the concrete surface. Under it he has a Black silk shirt that hangs open to reveal a Black t-shirt with Kannon 3:08 across the chest in Purple, a throwback to March Madness where he believes his problems with Jeremy Howard started. With these he has a pair of Black, worker-cut jeans with Purple and Green embroidery down the left leg. Xavier : “...And it’s because I want to show the world what bad rolemodels can do to a nation.....And there are no worse examples to our country’s youth than Brock Newbludd and Jeremy Howard.” Kannon wanders in a direction that only he knows where it leads to as his faithful valet trundles along behind him, not looking too enthused. Eleanor : “How do you plan on doing that?” She stops and after a few steps Xavier turns around to look at her, standing in the middle of the deserted road with her arms out stretched, spinning around so she can look all around her. Eleanor : “Look....There is NOBODY around!!!” Despite the fact that for once his valet is correctly pointing out the obvious to him h does not sigh, instead he nods his head, agreeing with her. Xavier : “EXACTLY!“ Ellie~! stops spinning and stumbles, trying to get her bearings back. She pulls her hair out of hr face and looks at him, even more confused than normal. Eleanor: “Ya wha?” Xavier : “There SHOULD be people out here! There SHOULD be families wandering these streets! There SHOULD be couples sitting on the chairs outside that cafe reading the Sunday pap-” As soon as he mentions this Ellie~! walks past him towards the cafe he was referring to. Eleanor : “Great idea...Let’s skip the papers though...” He watches his valet walk off and sensing he isn’t following her, Ellie~! turns around and look at him curiously. Eleanor : “Well?” She gestures with a movement of her head to the quiet cafe where round tables sporting Red and White parasols line the street by it’s windows. Xavier : “We can grab a coffee later...I have to get this done...” She folds her arms and trundles back over to him, muttering under her breath. Eleanor : “Now look who is ruining society...” She reluctantly follows the Gold Patron Meritorious as he continues his stroll down the nearly deserted street, looking with disappointment at all the is around him. Xavier : “Why do you think there is nobody else around at 10:30 on a Sunday morning?” Behind him his valet is slowly catching up, burning holes in Xavier’s back with a stare of contempt. Eleanor : “Um...Probably because it is 10:30...Like.....IN THE MORNING! Maybe because they are all in bed, still asleep...Generally, SO not being out here!” The Scientologist pauses his progress and lets his tired valet catch up, still with a sour look on her face, resentful at being woken and dragged out of bed for this. Xavier : “If only that were the reason my little Fuck Ferret.....Unfortunately there is a far more worrying reason for our solitude out here, on a usually bust street....One that I will try to put right!” As Kannon speaks his valiant words his valet’s face lets on that she is becoming less and less enthused as she rubs her pale Blue eyes and yawns, stretching her arms up above her head. Eleanor : “It isn’t the whole...CHURCH thing again is it? Storming into them and shouting at the Priests really isn’t a good idea, the last time that Gospel choir nearly caught up to us...” As she speaks Xavier appears to be remembering the incident, shuddering. Xavier : “No, for once Christianity cannot be blamed, and in a way we are allies...For we all know that Jeremy and Jessica are living in Sin with their bastard child born out of wedlock, by all rights the Christian church should be aiding me, the Pope should be making L Ron Hubbard a saint....But there is a bigger menace afoot, and it stands just a few feet away from us.” The sleepy valet looks around them quickly, still not knowing what Xavier is on about. Eleanor : “Trees? Is it trees?” He shakes his head then walks towards a heavy wooden door and turns the handle. Xavier : “Not this time...Follow me....” He opens it fully and the coupe walk into a smoke filled bar that even at the early time of day has a group of 5 sitting along the bar, all with a drink in front of them and a cigarette in their hands. None seem to be dressed that smartly and a look of revulsion crosses Ellie~!’s face, for a collective dislike of the smell, decor and clientele. She coughs, adjusting to the atmosphere. Eleanor : “Okay....I SO need a shower now, can we go?” The Scientologist takes a few steps in and gestures to the row of men on bar stools who are ignoring the 2 strangers. Xavier : “You know who is to blame for this don’t you? Brock Newbludd! All the time he has been in PCW he has been endorsing this kind of drunken lifestyle, condoning these slob-like morals and promoting this far from pure lifestyle!” As he dissects their lives the men at the bar turn around and look at the Auburn haired stranger, not taking kindly to his judgement f their lives. Man At Bar : “Hey, we don’t judge your way of life so do-” He is cut off as Kannon springs into life, animatedly shooting his words down. Xavier : “How dare you say that you don’t oppress my beliefs! What if I were to tell you right now, openly, that I am a Sci-” Man At Bar : “Fag?” The Scientologist face turns sour and he stares back at the man in disgust. Xavier : “NO I AM NOT A HOMOSEXUAL! I am GOLD PATRON MERITORIOUS XAVIER KANNON from the CHURCH OF SCIENTOLOGY I will have you know...I am here to tell you what is wrong with your lives and provide you with a new, more suitable rolemodel for your lives!” Ellie~! walks up beside Xavier, now holding her head scarf over her nose and mouth to combat the smoky atmosphere the bar has. Eleanor : “For........Your.......Lives!” The 5 men all turn on their stools and look at the Scientologist, bemused by his strange words. Xavier : “I look at you people, drinking on a Sunday morning instead of playing with your children, smoking cigarettes that will put you in the grave earlier, and that cost money you could be giving to your children...But I am a fair man, I do not judge you...I pity you....And I pity what I see in you...” The wall of blank faces looks back at him, seemingly more eager to hear what he thinks than to beat the living daylights out of him. Xavier : “I look at your glazed over eyes, and I see Brock Newbludd.......I see Jeremy Howard...Is that REALLY how you want to live your lives? A slave to alcohol and video games, or the father of a bastard child? Scientology can be your salvation, and I am her-” The man nearest him raises his hand,, cutting off Xavier who looks to him, hoping that he has seen the error of his ways. Man At Bar : “Look buddy, you are entitled to your point of view...But we are happy with our lives so if you don’t mind...” The man politely gestures towards the door which infuriates Xavier who stomps his feet, causing Ellie~! to hop backwards. Xavier : “What is WRONG WITH YOU PEOPLE?!?!?! I am OFFERING you a BETTER LIFE! Don’t you get that...BETTER! MY WAY IS BETTER SO WHY DON’T YOU LISTEN TO ME?!?!?!?” His emerald Green eyes look out hopefully to the five men who sit silent. Xavier : “WHY WON’T YOU JUST REALISE THAT I AM RIGHT?!?!?! I KNOW I AM RIGHT...WHY DON’T YOU?!?!? DO YOU WANT THE REST OF YOUR LIVES TO BE THIS WAY OR DO YOU WANT TO BE LIKE ME? DAMN IT AGREE WITH ME!!!” He stands after opening up his heart to the men, staring with wide eyes at them, getting his breath back but they all just turn back and carry on smoking and sipping their drinks to Xavier’s devastation. he turns to Ellie~! with a tear welling up in his right eye...Stuttering. Xavier : “Wh-...Why won’t they listen t-...Listen to me Ellie~!?” He looks at her with dejected eyes as she puts her arm around him and the couple walk to the door. Eleanor : “That would be because it is 10:30...The 10:30 in the morning.....On a Sunday...” Eleanor explains it to him as they walk back out onto the street and the shot fades to Black.
