It All Becomes Clear?

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/Feb/2002 'Hey Mike! Did you get that CD-R I sent you?' 'Yeah man. Awesome! They've been sent upstairs, they said they'll have a decision by Monday for you.' 'Thanks Mike.' The scene fades in on two men in a large office. The man addressed as Mike is walking away as we open, and we see the back of his brown haired head, white shirt and black pants. The other person in question is none other than D-Kay Calyx. Dressed in a pair of cream jeans, and a blue shirt, the black PCW star is sat down on a leather sofa outside a separate part of the office. We look on at the filing cabinet next to D-Kay, with a busty blonde putting away a large amount of paperwork, catching the eye of not only D-Kay, but also half of the rest of the office at the same time. After a few minutes of ogling, including the highlight of the film reel when she dropped a sheet of paper, she finally walks away, leaving D-Kay sat on his own again. The door next to him, that looks like a closet or interview room due to the lack of windows, opens, and a middle aged man with black hair and alight grey business suit exits, and calls D-Kay in. The camera goes to follow, but is stopped at entry. We fade to black for the time being. ***** We re-open onto the scene after an undisclosed period of time, with D-Kay and the businessman walking out of the room, and shaking hands, leaving D-Kay and the cameraman to walk down the center of the office, and into the streets of Chicago, Illinois. As we step out, we are greeted by D-Kay's first trainer, and former best friend, Joey Flash. Joey is wearing a pair of black trousers, and a black dinner jacket, with a midnight blue shirt on underneath. His blonde hair is sat neatly on his head down to his shoulders, and his first movement is to pass D-Kay a small brown envelope. D-Kay opens it, and shakes out the contents, revealing a bundle of bank notes. 'There you go D-Kay. $1,000. Up front, pending receipt of the goods. Becky's desperate for these. She wants to know they're in good hands.' The former 'happy' personality of Joey Flash seems to have disappeared behind the steely face. D-Kay shoves the money into his pocket, and the duo turn to walk down the street. 'Thanks Joey. I appreciate this. What does she want those for anyway? 'Well, there's an amplifier and a mixing desk she can get her money back on, but it's the computer and software she wants. She knows that her husband gave it to you Darren, and she knows what's on there. Even if you don't. He was released on bail due to unconvincing evidence, and is due to face a re-trial in a few weeks, and she knows that there is evidence on that computer that will put him away for a long time. The FBI are still sorting out the conspiracy to defraud the government, but the smuggling details and a lot more are recorded somewhere in the .exe files. Which is why it has to be delivered safely. It needs to be undamaged so the FBI can have a proper look. Hence the fee.' Joey and D-Kay turn the corner into a car park, and Joey pauses to let that bit of news settle in. 'D-Kay. You'll need to testify. Just a few questions about what was on the computer, why it was in your possession etc. So I need to make sure you're ok with that? The FBI have asked me to ask you, before they contact you.' Again there is a pause as D-Kay takes in all of the information given to him. After a few seconds he nods his head. 'Yeah Joey. I'm ok with that. I never knew it was this damn important though. It's a good thing I've not gone with a known company to deliver.' 'Who have you used?' 'Mark Estep. He's a trucker, and he'll be carrying it up. Don't worry though, it's on the way to the PCW Pay Per View, March Madness. I'll be travelling with him, and he'll have a few friends coming to give support. It'll hold a few of us, and if anything goes wrong, I'll be there.' 'Just make sure it gets to St. Louis.' D-Kay nods his head again as Joey climbs into a red Chrysler and drives off. D-Kay watches the car leave, and takes out his cell phone, dialling a number from memory. 'Hey Mark? It's D-Kay. I need to speak with you again concerning that delivery. Meet me after Fusion. I need to sort this.' D-Kay hangs up the line and climbs into his own car, the blue BMW 850i parked at the rear of the car park. The view changes to an internal one as the BMW cruises through central Chicago, on it's way out of town, presumably to D-Kay's house on the outskirts. As we leave then main streets behind, D-Kay removes his cell phone again, and clips it onto the hands free speakers in the car, before dialling another number from memory. We wait a few seconds before an answer phone kicks in, announcing the line as one belonging to a person called Clive Dewhirst. 'Clive. It's Darren. Listen, I went for that interview with EMI International and didn't get it. However, our tunes have been passed onto the top men for a possible release. I'll call in next week and see how you're doing. Laters.' D-Kay hangs up the phone line, and immediately dials another number, and gets another answer phone. 'Hey, Invincible Tim here...I can't get to the phone at the moment, but please leave a message after the beep, unless your name is Chris Chase.' D-Kay looks puzzled, and waits for the beep. 'Hey Tim. It's D-Kay. Listen, I may not make it to Fusion, so good luck against Miyagawa. Make sure you have that belt by Sunday. Oh, and let me know why Chris can't leave messages. Later days.' D-Kay again hangs up the phone, and curses to himself as he misses the turn off to where he is going. 'Damn. Now how in hell's name can I turn around on this road without being caught. Sod it. I'll make one last phone call, then sort it out. Off with the cameras.'
