The Sage Of A Champion: Part 6

8 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-8-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!Part 6” Chapter 3: The Battle To Destroy BattlePowers BeginsDay 5: Taking a Break…. Bowling Time~! {BladeD does exactly what his brother tells him to do and decides to go bowling to get his mind off of things. Considering you have to concentrate the whole time you are bowling it will make it hard for BladeD to have his mind anywhere else then on the lanes. He drives up to the local bowling alley and parks in the parking lot, then gets out his bags and goes into the alley. Once in the alley he goes up to the front desk and gets a lane, they give him lane 21, which is on the left side of the two pairs of lanes. Once he gets to the lane he opens his “storm” bag and pulls out two bowling balls… His first ball is a Storm Core Power HRG in a black and gold mixed color and his second ball is a White Dot in a brownish color. He sets his balls on the rack and sits down to enter his name in the computer, then he puts on his pair of white Brunswick 11 and half shoes, then he beginnings to ball} It has been about two weeks since BladeD has bowled, so he is a little rusty, but not too much. He steps out to the front set of dots with his Core Power and takes four slow steps outward, then throws his bowling ball from the 3 board out to the 2 arrow, but it hooks way too hard and the shot is brookline. The bowl clips the head pin and goes on through leaving the six pin standing completely still as if it never got touched. BladeD waits a few seconds for everything to set back up again and grabs a hold of his White Dot, then throws it out to the two board and lets it hook in enough to pick up the six pin. 1. The score is nine with a spare. BladeD steps back up, but this time he moves a little more over to the left. He throws his ball out to the one board and the bowl hooks right into the pocket. X 2. The score is 19 on a strike. BladeD steps back up and does the same thing only he gave the bowl a little more power this time, so it didn’t have enough time to hook in far enough. The bowl just barely missed the head pin leaving BladeD with the 5, 6, and 7. BladeD swings his White Dot and picks up the spare. 3. The score is 33 on a spare. BladeD steps back up and tries the same thing, but once again has a little more power than he needs and the result is similar only instead of leaving three pins BladeD leaves the 4 and the 5. This is an easy pick up that BladeD has no trouble with. 4. 49 on a spare. BladeD steps back up and moves to the right a little bit… He does the same thing with the same amount of a little power throwing his ball out to the five board, but the only problem this time is that the bowl goes too far brookline going from the middle arrow on out and leaves BladeD with a 5, 8, 9, 10. BladeD has no trouble picking it up though. 5. 61 on a spare. BladeD steps back up only this time he throws out to the four board with a little more speed and the bowl doesn’t get much time out all to hook up till the end when he hooks straight into the pocket. X 6. 81 on a strike. BladeD steps up and gets two more strikes in the seventh and eighth frame, then on the ninth frame BladeD gives the bowl way too much power and the bowl goes straight into the middle almost leaving BladeD with a split, but he gets lucky and only has to pick up the 6 and 7 pin which isn’t a hard task at all. 7. X 8. X 9. 8/ The score going into the tenth frame is 169. BladeD goes into the tenth frame throwing with more and more power each time because he gets more and more excited with his performance and getting all excited is ruining his performance. He doesn’t seem to realize that, but it is. He throws his Core Power in the tenth and ball about gives him a 7/10 split, but with some kind of miracle both pins fall down giving him a strike. He always does the same thing the second time around, but is lucky and doesn’t split. He leaves the ten-pin and picks that up. His final score for the day was a 218. BladeD goes on the bowl two more games, but it is nothing special… It is just a really good day for BladeD and he is keeping a lot of bad stuff off his mind for the most part and that is what this whole day was suppose to be about. His brother would be glad to know that BladeD is doing good because all that was going through Saber’s mind is how bad BladeD was going to do because he wasn’t going to be able to concentrate on bowling since he has so much on his mind. Somehow BladeD managed to keep everything off his mind and end up with a 190 average for the day. That is really, really good since he doesn’t get to bowl as much as he would like too… It makes Saber mad because he throws a 160-170 average and he bowls all the time… Saber gets so jealous of BladeD at times and it can be really funny at times watching them too fight over BladeD being better than Saber at everything… There has never been anything that Saber has been better than BladeD at… When new games and stuff come out Saber will be the first to play them and be better then BladeD then, but once BladeD gets the feel to the game Saber gets blown out of the water. It is really sad, but it is the sad truth… Not does the truth hurt, but sometimes the truth is sad and this is one of those times when the truth is very sad. Yet for some reason all we can do is just sit him and laugh at the sad truth… Isn’t that just strange when people can laugh at something that is sad? Well strange things like that happen all the time and is not something that is new to the world… That is something that is sad and strange at the same time. ::= face:: BladeD puts all of his stuff up and walks up to the front desk, then pays nine bucks for the three games he bowled, which is kind of a rip off, but that is how the world is today, and leaves the bowling center. The last screen we are left with is BladeD rolling out of the parking lot on 24s…. Trailblazer… with 24s… ::laughs:: You don’t even have to guess what he is listening to because you know right off the bat he is listening to Nellyville, but only to Air Force Ones… After that song is over with he switches cds and puts in the disc two of the two disc xXx soundtrack and begins to listen to Nelly (featuring Toya) – “Stick out ya wrist.” Which is one of BladeD’s new favorite songs, but I mean, any new song of Nelly is BladeD’s new favorite song… That is just how BladeD works. He likes anything and everything that Nelly has to offer. The screen fades to black from there.
