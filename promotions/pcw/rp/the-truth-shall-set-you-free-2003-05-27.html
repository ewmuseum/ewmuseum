The Truth shall set you Free

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/05/03 Honestly… is there any validity in the statement? Just how does the truth go about setting one free? It doesn’t seem to add up. There’s got to be something missing. Either that, or I’m missing something. But wait… To examine just how the equation works, maybe we need to take a better look at the values we’re dealing with. What is this thing we call “the truth”. Many of us hide from facts we don’t want to admit. It’s human nature. We fear. We wonder. We lie. We want to be happy. If the truth distorts that happiness… we duck for cover. It makes us feel good. Or does it? I’ve learned to accept the truth. Lord knows each of us in this business are under harsh scrutiny, observation and criticism on a daily basis. So what is it that I have accepted you ask? What do I see as “the truth”? Life is a gift. True. You only live once. So you damn well better make the most out of it while you still can. True. We all have a meaning. A role, if you will. Each of them is just as important as the next. True. We fit together like the perfect jigsaw puzzle. Exactly like the family we call… Primetime Championship Wrestling! True. Adam Stryker is a no-good, self-centered, egotistical, backstabbing son-of-a-bitch! You guessed it. True. But that’s beyond the point. I live in a fantasy world. We live in a fantasy world. To quote “some other guy”… life is a dream. And in this profession… it is just that. It’s a dream that often seems unreal. There are good times… and there are bad. There are ups… and there are downs. But I’ll tell you this much. If this really is a dream… do me a favour… don’t wake me up! This life I lead seems more and more like a fantasy world every day. When you have a chance to live a lifelong dream each and every day… when you have the chance to walk among men you’ve admired for all of you’re life… when you wake up every morning feeling like one of the luckiest men in the world, despite your battered, bruised, torn and absolutely broken body… it’s… well… different. When you can smile in the condition I’m in right now… it’s kind of scary. But none the less… it feels good. Adrenaline is a blessing from God to the likes of myself. I’d probably be unconscious right now if not for the grace of the natural high this industry truly is for me. All true. Sometimes I wonder when the dream will end. Will it come to a stop? And what if it ends abruptly? What if I’m not ready for it? What if I don’t want it to end? What if I feel I still have more time? More goals? More to accomplish? Will my dream just… end?! No. Not if I have anything to do with it… If you ask me… the dream never ends. Once you get a taste of this business… the thirst is never ending. You always come back for more. This business is in my blood. Without professional wrestling… there is no Manuel Labor. Without professional wrestling… there is no man behind Manuel Labor. There’s nothing. I’ve got a lot to celebrate in my life, but I’ll be damned if this business isn’t the most important and significant slice of this pie I call my life! I’ve always said… that you can take Manuel Labor away from wrestling. You can kick me out, send me packing and strip me of my pride. You can take me completely away from the love of my life. But you can NEVER… never… take this business away from me! It just doesn’t work that way. True. How do ya like them apples… Stryker?! Is the equation getting any clearer? Or do we need more interpretation? You see… Adam Stryker seems to think he’s could spoil my party with his recent antics. I know it. He won’t say it… but he has a master plan. The fact that I’ve solidified a spot for myself at the top of the Extreme division does not sit well with the DWO reject. True. Does he know something we don’t? Or is it merely a truth that can not be denied… Is Manuel Labor a shining star? Or in a more literal sense… is his legacy… written in the stars? I don’t think these questions need to be answered. Soon enough… the truth will be known. Soon enough… I hope he keeps watching… because no matter what he does… no matter what he tries… no matter what anybody else does… I won’t turn my head and pretend I don’t know what’s going on. I see it all. I know it all. Stryker and I… we’re not through… No… we’re just getting started… So now I walk into Wednesday Turmoil … a mere 20 hours away… I’ll stare the newly returning Abner right in the face , with my PCW Extreme Title on the line. Abner… you’ve got an opportunity of a lifetime awaiting you this very Wednesday night! Take it for all it’s worth and don’t let it go! You’ve been there before. You’ve played the game before, my boy. And you played it well. Think what you will about me… I have the utmost respect for you. But when the bell rings… respect is thrown out the window. Do what you’ve gotta do Abner… work my ribs… break my body down… work my back… fire away at my hands with every chance you get. But in the end… like I said before… chances don’t mean shit. Do everything your power to eliminate me . Do it all. Don’t hold back. END MY CAREER!! Try it! Put me on the shelf! And when all is said and done and all the dust clears… you’ll find… that it just… didn’t… work! I won’t stay down. I won’t give it up. And I won’t forget. No… Stryker… I sure as hell won’t forget… True.
