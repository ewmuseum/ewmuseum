5 Days Of Dating Hell-Day 4-Amy's Birthday Party....Okay It's Not 5 Days But This Day Counted For Two

28 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-28-2002 You really wonder what in the blue hell your doing here.... Mettallica booming in the background... Alcohol getting drank like it was water...All staples of an Amy Campbell party.... Why the heck are you even here? Couldn't you be doing better things with your time, like burning pictures of that redheaded devil that has caused you so much pain these past few weeks...part of the cause of why your even feuding with the man your fueding with now. You got some dumb invite from her in the mail, heh, your suprised she even remembered. You looked at it, pondered it, wondered just how friggin arrogant someone could be to ask you to a celebration fo their life when they didn't even have the courtesy to say 'good bye' after a passionate night together...not to mention leaving you with NO explanation as to why she'd ditch you after a good 6 months of your life for a redheaded OSW guy with a monkey for a best friend. Celebrate her life... Heh.... If you gave into your rawest emotions awhile ago... You would have ended it. But instead, here you are like the utter dummy you are, sipping red Kool-Aid from a beer mug while sitting on a bar stool, definitely standing out from a crowd in your clear baggy pants, smiley face boxers, orange and red vice-versa mask, spiked black and blonde hair, and elaborate body art of a roaring lion on your bare chest. The all oak 'classic' style bar is jumping, the music blaring as the many friends of Amy dance about, mingle, drink, act like complete idiots....and there they are....The loving couple... The two you wish you could destroy the most.... Reno and Amy.Isn't that sweet, slow-dancing to a hard rock song....that sure makes you wanna show everyone what you had for breakfast awhile back without hesitation. Look at them....embracing, Amy's soft cheek buried in Reno's strong shoulder as he holds her tight to him, Amy looking so warm and protected in Reno's arm's as he closes his eyes tightly as if he were in the sweetest dream....What was going on around them didn't seem to matter... As long as they had each other....And you hated it...you loathed it...you wanted to torture them both slowly and painfully as you sipped your red Kool-Aid with yet more anger, the cold taste of the drink not even enough to cool the warm blood you were pulsating now. You wanted revenge so bad...You leaned against the bar, watching the DJ dim the lights in the bar as they dropped low, the crowd seemingly just in awe of the 'cute little couple'...Just about 7 months ago, it woulda been you slow-dancing with her...Cuddling her...Running your fingers through her long, soft strands of crimson red hair... Laying with her quietly for hours....just holding her naked body to yours, in ecstasy at just being with her....All that has happened is you were cut out of the picture, and Reno was pasted in your place without a second thought. During the slow dance between the two lovebirds, still drawing eyes from the crowd at the pool table to the goof-off's at the jukebox trying to get the female partygoers to take off their tops all night, you notice a strange thing...Amy's eyes.....piercing you...staring at you unbeknownst to Reno. You lock eyes with her mere seconds, all that seething hatred rising up in you again...the urge to lose it tempting you many times as your hate grows just looking into her eyes. 'Hahah,' her eyes said to you....if eyes could speak, that's what hers would say. You knew deep inside you were a fool for coming here just to let her torture you slowly like this..... But deep down...You lived with a scary revelation...You still cared enough about her to let her torture you like this....it was sick. You grew tired of this mental torture...you just couldn't take watching her take what was left of your heart and crushing it under her boot hell, spitting on it for good measure. You raised up abruptly from your bar stool, heading right outside with no further word after downing what was left of your Kool-Aid, stomaching all you could with this 'happy little couple' crap.... You breezed through the side door of the bar, out into the cold night air of the alley as it was littered with loose pieces of paper and other garbage, ironic that was JUST what you were feeling like. You couldn't even get phased by the fact that your body was writhing with chills as you pressed your back against the wall....looking up to the night, star laden sky as you contemplate what cruel god would leave you on the earth broken like this....a shell of what you used to be.... You couldn't even be alone with your thoughts long enough before THOSE were broken....interrupted by the redheaded siren you once had as your own...Amy. When you looked down, there she was....standing before you....decked out in a tight hot pink 'Bad Gurl' belly shirt, baggy cargo pants, and filled with piercings as you remember her.... Her lips were still as enticing and as sweet as you could ever remember...you had to fight the instinctual habit to kiss her when you saw her. There she was, you and her...face to face....what was there to say?' 'I'm sorry Rentie....I'm so sorry....I didn't mean to hurt you,' slipped from her mouth, in a sweet endearing tone you knew too well. Maybe it may have eased your raging insides...had it not been what you expected her to say.... Cookie cutter apologies weren't gonna do it right now... 'No....just use me up right? How's the new model? Better then the old one you turned in, huh Amy,' you fired back with caged anger, aching to ask her that after so much time has passed. She just stood silent, no words coming from her as you stared at her....wanting to feel sorry for her...but you knew she wasn't feeling sorry for you when she was between the sheets with 'Monkey Boy'....why should you care? Easy, don't. You had better things on your mind....like how you were gonna get back at now for all the pain and trouble he's caused you...not this bull with some inconsiderate female who obviously faked all feelings for you. She rubbed her hands against her shoulders for warmth, still looking to you....her lips trembling as she tried to release what she wanted to say...and she did....and it was completely unexpected.... 'I still love you Rentie....I don't know how you feel about me anymore....I don't....but if I had it to do over again....there would have been no good-bye. Good night...' With that...she leaned in...and she pressed those soft, satin lips against your cheek. You didn't fight it and I think she knew that you wouldn't....for that was quite the daring move on her part for saying you were seething with hatred for her....
