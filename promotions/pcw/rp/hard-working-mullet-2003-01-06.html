Hard Working Mullet

6 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 6, 2003 [There comes a time in a dudes life when he's pushed to the edge. It could really be a number of bogus things that force a man to snap. Work, family, lack of sex...or it could just be totally heinous pressure of EVERYTHING. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy seemed like a good hearted, fun dude. He did the right thing, helped the right people, and was adored by all who knew him. Lately, Tommy hasn't been so nice, or adored. He's become unbelievably heinous and has turned on everything that he used to be for. Why would a man do this? It's simple. The undeniable pressures of being a professional wrestler and a wealthy business owner. Being a vital part of PCW's tag team division for so long, and getting the bogus screw had finally taken it's toll. Then Harry suffers a back injury at the hands of The End, and that was the last straw. Tommy snapped and lost total control of himself. He began shunning the people he loved, kicking them to the curb like yesterday's news. He destroyed a children's foundation so he could make water park's that have tons of half naked bodacious babes dancing around. He denys fans autographs because he feels if he uses his hand too much he won't be able to perform in the ring correctly. The PCW fans have had enough of Tommy's ways and they have turned on him. He doesn't even care either, that's the sad part. That after all those months of being one of the favorites, the team everyone looked up to to save the day, that Tommy can be one of the most hated compadres in PCW. All because of the pressure] [The scene focuses on a man with a blonde mullet in his suit and tie. Behind him are many awards, most of them Employee Of The Month, but it still seems most intimidating. The man's slightly wrinkled face contorts with a cold look and his dark eyes dart directly forward melting whoever sits across from him. The man with the mullet has worked at the Best Buy since it opened years ago and he's put forth all of his efforts to make sure that his customers get the satisfaction that they need. He deals with every problem personally and uses the force that only HE knows how to use] Man with mullet: Are you retarded? [The scene zooms out to show 'Totally Tubular' Tommy and his esteemed colleague and brother, 'Heavy Metal' Harry, sitting in front of the large oak desk. Tommy doesn't seem to even want to acknowledge the fact that this mullet guy is even talking to him. Harry is just happy that he has COMPLETED his Twisted Sister collection, thanks to the far out Best Buy. Tommy's sweat soaked PCW T-shirt is beginning to let off a very musty smell that leaves a sour face on the mullet man, but Tommy doesn't care to notice] Harry: Yes...he is retarded, man.... 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: I am not. [Harry shakes his head] Harry: You are too, man. TTT: Whatever... Harry: It's true, man. [Harry contorts his face as if some large man punched him with brass knuckles. He scrunches his right arm up into his chest and them makes a lot of odd movements with his lips and tongue] Harry: WETARDED!!!! TOMEEE WETARDED!!! [The mullet man slams his fist on his desk with pure anger] Man with mullet: ENOUGH! [Harry is still in his mentally handicapped mode] Harry: SAWWWW RRRRY!! [The man with the mullet stands from his plether chair and begins pacing back and forth. He knows that he works hard to make his living. He's putting his son through college and making sure that boy gets all of the chances that he never got. It's to make the future better for those that will come next, that's all it's about. He needs to make sure his son is well taken care of, and this job is the way to do it. The mullet man takes pride in the work he does and makes sure that it's done properly, without any disruptions, just like the one Tommy caused earlier in the day] Man with mullet: I work hard at this place. I make sure that each and every customer goes home with a smile on their face, even if they purchased something or not. That's what retail is all about, boys. I don't worry about sales or stocks or all that other horse poopy. No, I don't worry about anything technical. I just worry about making my customers as happy as possible, and when they AREN'T happy that's when the poopy hits the fan, gentlemen. Harry: I'm not happy, man. Man with mullet: Your also retarded. Harry: No. That's Tommy. [Tommy just stares off to the side, looking out of the two way mirror overlooking the entire Best Buy store. People are moving around everywhere and Tommy seems to have spied something] Man with Mullet: I'm not going to beat around the beehive here. You boys disrupted other customers from enjoying themselves at my store. That's why your here. Plus, Chip has a busted nose. That's not kosher, gentlemen. Harry: I'm sorry, man. Man with Mullet: Really? Harry: Yeah, man. It's tough being Jewish. [The man looks confused] Man with mullet: What? Jewish? Harry: Nevermind. [Tommy still stares out of the two way mirror, probably forgetting that he's in trouble] Man with mullet: You must be a heartless poopy face to rob a child of fun. To humiliate the boys father, making the child think that his father is an absolute nothing. Being a father myself, I find this act to be cowardly and uncalled for. I don't even see how you can walk this earth calling yourself a man!! Harry: He does have balls, man. Man with mullet: No...he doesn't. [Harry's eyes grow as wide as silver dollars as he slowly looks over to his brother. Tommy, too busy staring out of the window, doesn't even notice what's going on] Harry: No balls, man? What did you do with them?! Man with mullet: Nothing. It's just a figure of speech, Rain Man. Harry: Tommy needs his balls. The Swedish Bikini team is in town and they are in heat, man. Man with mullet: That's nice, but I don't give a poop about that. What I care about is that you two pay for the pain you caused that man and his little boy. Harry: But Tommy has no balls, man. [The man with the mullet rubs his brow and then leans onto his desk, staring Harry dead in the eyes] Man with mullet: He has his balls, all right? Harry: Really? Did you give them back? Because I didn't see you give them back to him, man. Man with mullet: You know...I really want to smack you silly. [Harry goes silent] Man with mullet: The boy is fine and knows exactly who the two of you are. I guess it's your lucky day that you boys have some fame, because he just wants an autograph, a picture, and a friendly conversation. You go ahead out there and do that for me then I won't have the cops come in here and haul your surfing poop face TO JAIL! [The man with the mullet now stares a hole through the Tubular One, just waiting for the man to crack and apologize like the poop face he knows the guy is. But Tommy isn't even paying attention to that stupid mullet guy. He's staring down at the mass of people shopping to keep the depression away for a couple hours. He's dreading his upcoming tag team match because Rent-A-Hero is going to be there. For some reason he has this sinking feeling that he is in way over his head. That Rent-A-Hero is somehow going to get a shot at his title and take it away from him. The people will support it, they want to support their superhero and make sure that he destroys the dastardly villain. Is that what Tommy has come to? Being the dastardly villain that the crowd wants to see lose? It's bogus, but it's true. Tommy just knows he's going to have to take it one day at a time. It was silly playing all those video games just to prove he was better at them than Rent-A-Bogus-Hero. Talk about some lame crap, dude. Tommy has another idea......board games] Man with mullet: Are you even paying attention? POOP FACE! Answer me you steaming pile of feces!!! [Harry sniffs Tommy] Harry: He's funky, but no where NEAR feces, man. Man with mullet: SHUT UP!!! SHUT YOUR MOUTH YOU JACKASS!! Harry: I am NO jackass, man. Man with mullet: Is this the game your going to play? Your going to play stupid with me, is that it? Harry: Who's playing, man? [The man with the mullet walks around his desk and comes within only inches of Harry's face, he whispers] Man with mullet: It's punks like you that ruin this economy for hard working people like myself. You probably sit on your ass all day and do absolutely nothing but do drugs and listen to heavy metal music. You see, that's not what life is about. Life is about working hard to make the future a better place. It's about getting down, and getting your hands dirty so that the generation after you doesn't have to do it so hard the next time. Your payment will come in heaven, that's where the real challenge begins, my friend. But...you wouldn't know ANYTHING about heaven because, let's face it poopy pants, your going STRAIGHT TO HELL. Harry: FAR OUT, MAN!! I'M GOING TO HELL! [The man with the mullet shakes his head and laughs. He tries his best to disguise his frustration, but he cannot hold it back any longer. He begins choking the hell out of Harry with his dark eyes full of rage that it seems he is about to committ a horrible act of murder. All of that heaven and hell talk doesn't make much sense now as the hard working man is about to write himself a one way ticket straight to Satan himself. Now talk about HEINOUS] Man with mullet: I'LL FUCKING KILL YOU!! Harry: ACKKKKKKK..... [The police kick the door in with their guns pointed at mullet face] Cops: FREEZE! [Tommy looks away from the window, looks at the police, then looks at the mullet faced bandit] TTT: Totally bogus for you dude. [Tommy gets up and walks out of the room. Harry then raises up slowly and exits as well] Man with mullet: I did nothing, officiers...it was those two. Cop: Of course. That long haired guy was choking himself...with your hands. Cop # 2: What are you...retarded? Man with mullet: ARGH!!! [Fade to black]
