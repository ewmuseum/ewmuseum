Family Choices

7 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-7-02 Blood and gold: The air around you escapes your lungs, water can only be felt. You feel like your dead but yet your still alive. Long enough to endure the pain as your raspy lungs reach out trying to grab even the slightest relief. The pressure gets greater, you feel as though your lungs are about to explode as your eyes bulge from their sockets. It begins to hurt, pain strikes every inch of your body stabbing you with needle like precisions. Blood seeps from every possible spot it can escape from, you can feel your life beginning to slip away. Soon the pain is replaced by a numb feeling, your close to death. Your skin begins to turn a dull pail color and soon your body becomes limp. The grip around your lungs is getting worse and finally with one last squeeze one lung explodes. Your life slips away faster and faster. Your head begins to throb as it feels someone is slamming into the side of it. A choking sensation moves over you grasping a hold of your neck not letting go as you choke on your own saliva. Your eyes roll into the back of your head as blood begins to pour out. It hurts but there is nothing you can do but live through the pain. Your bones become brittle and slowly snap under your own weight causing so much pain running through your body you pray someone would kill you. Your cries go unheard because you cannot speak. Finally as you lay their thinking you can take it no more your heart explodes from all the pressure. The life force inside escapes you as you die. You have just fell victim to 'The Sylence'. The 'sylence' gets us all sooner or later. When? No one knows their time but it does come and when it does the pain is unbearable....it keeps you alive long enough to wish you were dead. What kind of man can actually tame this force? That's just it, no man can. It takes a special being, a special soul to contain it but you have to be willing to devote your life to it. One man can tame this force and uses it when extreme prejudice; His name? Jay Sylo. The man who destroyed AFL-CIO, the man who beat the mountain himself Kilmanjaro. But you know he's been thinking. His whole goals to destroy evil have been useless thus far. Could he and should he become the black knight? Only time can tell for this but for now Sylo trains because Friday night on Fusion he has to fight that scum Ripper Ryckman one on one for the rising star title. He wants to beat Ripper, wait no, destroy Ripper. Teach him who the best of the best is and show the world that he cannot be stopped. Now that he has this beast inside he's willing to do anything to win. He has the desire to be the best and the best only now. Slowly like a virus it's beginning to eat away at his soul, he truly is turning into a monster. ('Drip, Drip, Drip,' The sound of blood dripping on cold concrete could be heard. The current surroundings were nothing to look at seeing as the walls were made of stone; cold, gray, unforgiving stones which stacked up stories to make a structure surrounding. Inside, a few lit candles burned. They were white like ghost and to match left a haunting aurora through out the room. Laying in the middle on the old stone floor was a shadowy figure. As the light picked up you could see it was a large man with short spiky hair. The only light coming from him seemed to be his eyes which glowed an eerie ice blue. Snarls could be heard coming from the figure as it slowly rose to it's feet, standing a good 7'3'. He stammered forward toward an old wooden table with old marble plates sat out. The dripping noise of blood picked up more and more as the figure moved closer to the table. Slowly he plopped down in one of the rickety wooden chairs which were located near the table. The figure snarled once more and out of the darkness stepped Akira Akiyama.) 'So, how did things go?' Akiyama's voice boomed. 'Look at me!' Sylo shouted. 'I'm busted up from head to toe! How am I supposed to win my match for the rising star title when I'm nothing more than a bloody mess!' (The blood continued to run down the emotionless face of one Jay Sylo. He snarled from deep down within as he looked to Akiyama who only smiled.) 'The training is good for you. It build character and plus teaches you to unleash your true powers,' Akiyama's voice carried out through out the stone walls. (Letting out another snarl Sylo banged his fist on the table standing up. The table continued to pivot back and forth due to the impact as Sylo walked away finally coming back with a towel. He wiped the blood from his forehead and spit out some from his mouth.) 'Why are we at this castle?' Sylo asked as he continued to wipe the blood away. 'This is the home of your ancestors. This is where they lived, this is the place they defended, and this was the place they died in. It's walls tell the stories of many centuries but you should know that more than anyone else,' Akiyama replied with a smirk. 'Hmm,' (Sylo ran his large hands over the cold stone of the castle walls. He closed his eyes for a brief second and when he opened them the castle took on a new life. Inside was his ancestors, one being Sir Sylo. The knight he had heard so much about but never had the chance to talk to, not even in his dreams. He approached with caution as Sir Sylo leaped up from the chair still decked out in his battle armor. He extended a hand and placed it onto the shoulder of Jay slowly patting it, almost as if they had known each other for years. He looked confused as Sir Sylo stepped back taking a seat in one of the old wooden chairs.) 'Lad, it's good to see the Sylo's progressed so far,' Sir Sylo said staring forward. 'I've had dreams of your battles....what does it all mean?' Jay snarled. 'You and I have a strong bond, even in death we are together. You hold apart of me as long as that barbarian lives inside of you,' Sir Sylo said in a reply. (A maid with a long dress with her long brown hair pulled back and bosom heaving, brought in food for Sylo to eat. Sylo scanned her carefully as the maiden only curtsied and walked off smiling.) 'Who was that?' Sylo said turning back to his ancestor. 'That would be my wife. A great lady, I don't know what I would do with out er,' 'Why am I here?' Sylo asked looking Sir Sylo dead in the eyes. 'You have a personal demon. I felt it inside, you don't know weather or not you should turn your back on all you believe in. Let me tell you though lad, if you worry about others more than yourself you will never have the treasures you desire,' 'You mean the rising star title?' Sylo asks. 'Aye that would be it,' Sir Sylo replied. (Sylo turned his head a small bit staring off into the distance as the same white candles burned. He was lost in the silent slumber of the flames finally brought back by the sound of Sir Sylo's voice.) 'There comes a time in every mans life when he has to choose, now is your time. I did once, it was so long ago. My home was in danger but then a force reached out and lifted me from the ashes of despair...all he wanted was my alliance with his dark army,' Sir Sylo said taking a bite of something off a marble plate. 'But you rejected the offer correct?' (Sir Sylo smiled a devilish grin as he looked to his future.) 'They tried to make me into a good man didn't they? They told you of my journeys but when it came down to me defending my family I would do anything and everything. Yes I joined the dark army but in return I became stronger than any other man. I also got everything I want, so give up your fight and just give in...' 'NO!' Sylo yelled. 'I DO NOT NEED YOU NOR ANY BLACK ARMY TO WIN! I WILL WIN THIS RISING STAR TITLE ALONE!' (Sylo stormed away and soon found himself laying on the ground. 'Drip, Drip, Drip,' The sound of his blood could be heard as he stood up once more. He had stepped into the future and just now returned. Akiyama sat at the old wooden table looking down at Jay.) 'So how did the training go?' Akiyama asked. 'It went fine....I now know what I must do to win the Rising Star title from that fat headed jack ass Ripper Ryckman. His time will come to an end....may god have mercy on his soul,' (Sylo let out a snarl which would turn any man's blood cold as Akiyama smirked. The old stone walls seemed to generate different emotions into Sylo as his beast side took over once more. Soon every thing went dark and the last image we were left with was the glowing eyes of one Jay Sylo.)
