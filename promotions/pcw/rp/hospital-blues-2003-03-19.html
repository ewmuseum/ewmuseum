Hospital Blues

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/19/03 'Argh.' A very frustrated nurse, bedecked in a form fitting suit of white fumes. Nurse: I refuse! I simply refuse! Across from the pretty young woman, a doctor stands with arms folded. Doctor: Remember your duty to the patient. Nurse: But....but this is unfair! Voice: I want my godforsaken blood!!!!! We move down to see the 'patient'.....a very unique patient, indeed. Nurse: I told you sir, we don't serve....ew, blood! We only have green jello! With a large bandage wrapped tightly around his head, the skeleton known only as the Dark Gimmick shakes his bony fist. Dark Gimmick: Give me my blood, ya cotton pickin skinbag! Nurse: Doctor, please. Doctor: (Sighing) Mr. Gimmick, please try to understand..... Dark Gimmick: Understand nothing! I want my blood. Our medic hangs his head, holding one hand to his brow tiredly....then looks up to his aide. Doctor: Nurse, would you mind donating.... Nurse: (Aghast) I will not!!!!! Dark Gimmick: It has to be VIRGIN blood! Look at her, she hasn't been a virgin since eight months before her birth! Nurse: .....well I never. Dark Gimmick: You give me blood this instant or else! Nurse: Or else what? Dark Gimmick: Oh, I'll show you or else. Or else indeed....grr..... The old bag of bones flails his arms, attempting to sit up. Dark Gimmick: AGH!!!!! A wave of pain shoots through his broken head though.....DG clutches it and lays down again. Dark Gimmick: I'm old, bend over here and put your throat in my mouth. Nurse: No! Dark Gimmick: That's it, I'm suing for malpractice, call my lawyer! Footsteps trace through the white lined hallway. A voice, distant from inside the room is heard by an aide.....brief mumbling, then agreement. Doctor: (Turning) You don't REALLY have a lawyer, do you? Dark Gimmick: Moron, I am the physical incarnation of evil. I AM a lawyer. Sue you good I will! The footsteps continue onward.....the doctor steps out, his words muffled. Then after a brief instant, he pokes his head back in. Doctor: You have a guest, Mr. Gimmick. Dark Gimmick: I hope it's a walking talking gun. Blow the rest of my head off so I don't have to hear you babbling anymore! Doctor: .....I'm leaving. Nurse, take care of him. Nurse: Fine. The practitioner leaves, as DG's guest enters the room. Clyde: Hi dad. The burned man walks up to his father, holding a bouquet of flowers in his hand. Dark Gimmick: Hello, worthless seed of my loins. Nurse: .....you have a SON!? You're his FATHER!? Clyde: So he claims. I have no way of telling otherwise, now do I? Not like we can run blood tests on him, now can we? Dark Gimmick: Bah, I'm your father and your mother was a bastard! Now massage my spinal column! Clyde: No. I just came here to visit, dad. DG extends a hand, snatching up the flowers and sniffing them. Dark Gimmick: Deadly nightshade. You know I hate that. Clyde: .....it's your favorite. Dark Gimmick: Bah, favorite shmavorite! I hate it almost as much as I hate you! The skeleton opens his maw, cramming the entire plant inside and chewing it up. Clyde: ....... Dark Gimmick: (Munching) Nurse, my bedpan needs changing again. Nurse: (Groaning) You're a skeleton, how do you keep filling it up!? Exasperated, the young woman stands and reaches beneath DG's covers, pulling out a metal pan filled to the brim with all manner of horrid things. Worms writhe and bugs crawl from it, as she wretches. Nurse: Oh God, I'm gonna be sick.....hurrrrrrrgh! The poor girl sprints in the direction of the bathroom, as Dark Gimmick howls in laughter. Dark Gimmick: Bwahaha.....now that's funny! BwahahaOW my head. Gimmick again grabs his broken and bandaged skull, shutting his odd glowing eyes in pain. Clyde: Yes, very amusing. Dark Gimmick: So why are you really here? Come to borrow money? Need new sets of surgical tools? It's always something with you, isn't it? Clyde: .....no, I came to visit. Dark Gimmick: Bah! You can't fool me, worthless. I know your game.....you're the 'Devil'. Oooooh, scary......prince of lies! I am shaking! Quivering in my cloak! Oooooooh......ow! Once again DG clutches his head in pain, as Clyde looks on. Clyde: Guess I should be going. Dark Gimmick: Damn right you should be going! Never spend any time with your dear old dad.....too busy doing nothing with your eternal life. Clyde: I just killed every child in Egypt just last Sunday. Dark Gimmick: (Mockingly) Ehhhhhhhhhhhhh I just killed every child in Egypt just last Sunday.....bah! Where were you when I was getting my head busted open by some dime store stripper? Clyde: I'm sorry I can't babysit you every instant of the day. Dark Gimmick: Babysit ME!?!? Why you little..... DG again sits up in bed, flailing his arms and trying to get at Clyde's throat but the IV wire stuck in his bony arm holds him just short. His nurse meanwhile finally emerges from the bathroom.....takes one look at his skeletal rear protruding from his opened hospital gown, turns and heads right back in. Dark Gimmick: Bah, girls these days have no taste. Clyde: I'm sure. Anyway, I'll be going. Dark Gimmick: Go on, ya lazy bum.....you're no son of mine. Why back in my day we killed ALL the kids in EVERY country six times before supper, and we didn't complain, by gum! Then we'd stop by Calvary and kick that Jesus hippie in the shin before heading home! Then we'd..... Suddenly DG stops short, falling flat on his back and lying still. Next to the bed, Clyde rises.....a steady 'beeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeep' rings forth as the small plug for Dark Gimmick's life support machine dangles in his hand. Shrugging, Clyde tosses it onto his father's dead body and leaves the room. 'Baaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaah! A faint cry rings forth.....very painfully it seems, a misty figure rips forth from DG's prone form. As it moves free, we can see it appears to be a spiritual form of Dark Gimmick himself, dressed in a white robe, carrying a harp and fluttering a pair of feathery wings on his back. Dark Gimmick: I'll get you for this......aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaargh! With an irritated wail, the form of evil incarnate is dragged up towards the ceiling, floating straight through it and off towards the heavens as we mercifully fade to black.......
