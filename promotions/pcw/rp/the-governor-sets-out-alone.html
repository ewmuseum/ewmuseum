Title: The Governor sets out alone. Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: 25th Humiliation. Abject public degradation. Such are the emotions being felt by Politically Incorrect, one day after being upset by The Price Brothers, based on some miscommunication involving Powell's infamous gavel. William S. Powell is off in a bar somewhere in Atlantic City, drowning his sorrows in fine wine and liqeuer. However, Reginald V. Lovecraft is not. 'The Governor' is instead, putting a call into the Primetime Championship Wrestling brass. Powell had his singles match early in PI's tenure, fighting to a draw with Ganker after Kid Wonder attacked both men with a steel chair. Now, Lovecraft wants his... 'School's Out' Match....a traditional PCW Extreme Title clusterfuck. William S. Powell is too upset to compete. But Reginald is not. Adversity breeds opportunity. And, with intern Katherine Willis at his side, Lovecraft is preparing for his first ever Extreme match. PCW, get ready. Because the Georgian Gentleman is getting ready to go extreme upon the remainder of Primetime Championship Wrestling. ::In the Emperor's Suite of Caesar's Palace, Reginald V. Lovecraft and his intern, Katherine Willis, are enjoying a glass of champagne. William S. Powell is nowhere to be seen, and Roger Schultz is no doubt with him, trying to stop the impetous young man. Regardless, The Governor seems to be relishing the moment, despite the events of Fusion.:: Katherine Willis: I cannot believe that you and Mr. Powell lost that contest! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Neither can I...neither can I. It was a complete and utter lack of communication between us...the gavel shot was meant for one of the Prices. Still, I think our point was proven to the Price Brothers. We demolished the two of them for most of the contest...and they managed to squeak out a victory. Congratulations, Price Brothers...but know this. My partner is nowhere near as calm as I am about this. Politically Incorrect does not forget or forgive. Down the road...when you least expect it, you WILL pay for this. After all, revenge is a dish best served very, very cold. ::Katherine smiles, giving her boss a caring, consoling hug. I think we can tell by now that they care about each other, can't you?:: Katherine Willis: So, where to next for Politically Incorrect? Reginald V. Lovecraft: I don't know what my colleague, William S. Powell has planned, beyond drinking beer and staring at women in some infernal strip bar in this city, but, I'm going to compete in the 'School's Out' match next Friday on Turmoil. The PCW Extreme Title is on the line. Katherine Willis: Extreme Title? But you don't know anything about being extreme? At least William fought in that odd Playboy Mansion Match back in NTICW. You know nothing about being extreme? Reginald V. Lovecraft: On the contrary, Miss Willis. I grew up in the deep south. If you want to know what extreme is, join African-American leaders in civil rights marches, and have fire hoses and dogs let loose upon you. ::Katherine bows her head slightly: Reginald V. Lovecraft: That is the price I paid for my beliefs...for being a tolerant person, despite the general beliefs of the South. THAT is extreme...Extreme is combatting a hostile world around you, a world that doesn't believe in freedom. A world that has different standards for different people. What do you think it was like growing up having black kids as friends among the Southern aristocracy...hell, the aristocracy my family belonged to! Katherine Willis: Gosh, Reginald..I never knew.. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Now you do, Katherine..now everyone does. The story of Reginald V. Lovecraft is one shrouded in mystery, and many paradoxes...the stereotypical Southern gentleman is not stereotypical at all. For years, I used my political influence to fight for change in my native Georgia. Nowadays, things are better....but not perfect, unfortunately. Although now, Reginald V. Lovecraft battles for glory and power, not change, I still give it all I have. Katherine Willis: I understand.. Reginald V. Lovecraft: So, Katherine, what do you know of these hardcore matches? Katherine Willis: Well, I know that they usually involve guys hitting each other with weapons like garbage cans and barbed wire... Reginald V. Lovecraft: This one will likely be a bit different...it takes place in a school building. I doubt that they will be able to find barbed wire in a school building. Katherine Willis: But, they have lots of dangerous things, like blackboards, and chairs....and gym equipment! My goodness, be careful! Reginald V. Lovecraft: I will, Miss Lovecraft! There is no reason to fear! I am 6'7', 299 lbs.! I am fully capable of handling myself, despite being the oldest man in Primetime Championship Wrestling! I'm ready to go back to school, if you will. The entire roster knows of the Master Debator...but I am considered the silent member of the team. The serious member of the team. I am all of these things. But, I will prove that what I lack in the high flying skill my partner possesses, I make up for in general street fighting ass kicking ability. I will enter a school building the unknown member of Politically Incorrect, and I will LEAVE the grounds the NEW PCW Extreme Champion! Ladies and gentlemen, the Governor of PCW is in office. No pardons will be issued...none at all. ::Fade to:: Paid for by the Friends of Reginald V. Lovecraft!Your NEXT Extreme Champion. ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
