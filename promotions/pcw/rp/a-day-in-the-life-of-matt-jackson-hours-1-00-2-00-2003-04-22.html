A Day In The Life Of Matt Jackson, Hours 1:00-2:00

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/22/03 Location: CIA Main Floor, Langley VADate: April 22, 2003Time: 1:00 am The main floor of the Central Intelligence Agency is enormous, housing over thirty employees at any given time and right now all of them are working. Sitting behind his desk at the head of it all is Matt Jackson, wearing his normal grey suit. His brown eyes scan over some documents, as he gradually tries to piece together the puzzle that is the sleeper cell. Looking out over the office, he sees everyone in their class cubical working, not slowing down. Each like a warrior, wanting to overcome the beast that is terrorism, and the weapons they use are computers and paper. His attention is taken away from the documents, as the beautiful brunette that he met about half an hour ago makes his way towards her desk. She is wearing a pink dress, with white buttons and black heels. Her slender body moves with the grace of a professional dancer and her body curves in all the right places. Matt smiles as he sees her making her way toward his desk. Leaning back in the leather office chair, he crosses his arms over his barrel of a chest and a smile splits his angular face in half. Tabby gives him a smile of her own and continues walking across the concrete floor, too Matt’s desk. Running his hands through his spiky brown hair, Matt grabs a pen from the cup on his right; ready to write down anything important Tabby has for him. Leaning over his oak desk, the young field analyst gives the agent a great view of her chest, which he casually takes, trying not to let her know exactly what he is doing. She pretends not to notice, finding her boss to be ruggedly handsome, this lets her know that he might be slightly interested and maybe have a chance with him. Moving his eyes back up to meet her green ones, he cracks another smile, “Did you guys get anything new for me?” Nodding, she smiles, “Yes, we got a list of possible groups that might be involved and I figured we could go over it and work out some sort of plan of action.” She starts to move to the other side of the desk with the list in hand and she drops it on the desk in front of Matt. He looks up at her and she only looks back at him smiling, allowing him to get a smell of her perfume and brush him every once and a while. Is she coming on to me? I can’t believe that she is even interested. “I know some of the names on this list from my past missions, but some of these I have never heard of,” he tells her, letting the since of question linger in the air. “Some of them are small time organizations. I don’t think they would even have enough to fund a cell project. I mean a smaller one I am sure they do, but this cell is highly trained from all the information we have gathered this far. He is in every form of the word a sleeper cell. He was trained and then sent to America with no recollection of his past, other then the fact that when he was awoken it would all come back.” Matt rubs his thumb and forefinger against his chin, “I wish we knew what they were planning, and it would make everything so much easier. Right now all we have is a profile about a guy who is trying to do something, that isn’t very helpful.” “I am sure we have sometime though and we will get to the bottom of it,” Tabby offers, in a reassuring voice. It doesn’t do much to ease the nerves of the ex-Ranger. His face is one of stone as he gazes over the list, trying to make some sort of connection, but nothing comes of it. “We need to get something done quickly, because I am going to be leaving for Turmoil soon and even only being gone for a few hours, I still don’t want to not have anything before I leave,” Matt tells her, hoping that it will light a fire under all his new employees. “Turmoil?” Tabby asks, tilting her head to the side like a confused puppy. Matt smiles, giving him some momentary relaxation. She doesn’t know about PCW or my life outside of the CIA. I would have figured the rumours would have started by now, but I guess that some people still don’t know my agency work has been slacking because I was caught up in wrestling. “Well, I am working deep cover in the PCW. It is a wrestling organization, where I dominate and basically just prove why I am the best,” Matt quickly lies, trying to sound better then he really is to impress Tabby. She smiles and gives him a flirty laugh, boasting his self-confidence greatly. Knowing he has to get back to work though, he tries to fight off his urges and dismiss her. “I think I should look over these groups and see what I can’t figure out. So I say we should keep trying to gather information and meet back in an hour,” he squeaks out. Forcing himself to look back down at the list, so not to be tempted anymore. “Okay, I will be back in an hour,” she says, letting her voice flirt with him even more before walking away. Matt never taking his eyes off her as she walks away, letting his mind wonder away from the case and into fantasyland with Tabby. *** Location: CIA Main Floor, Langley VADate: April 22, 2003Time: 1:30 am I still don’t see the connection here, all thought I am not totally focusing on what is going on. I never thought I would look at another woman the way I did Haley. I never thought someone as beautiful as Tabby would come along and now I don’t know what I am going to do. I have to get my mind back on this, because I need a solid base before Turmoil. I need my mind to be able to be on The Overweighter all the way and I need to rise to the PCW Rising Star Tournament. It is my one chance to prove myself as a serious threat. Soon I will have my chance to prove myself, but now I need to get back to finding this guy. Matt freezes, staring at the list in disbelief. He can’t believe that he didn’t figure out this before. Quickly drawing a few lines on the paper, he starts to piece the connection that has been missing. Scribbling down a few notes, he works like a finely tuned machine, letting everything come together a little at a time. His mind like a steel trap, holding in what he needs and letting go what he doesn’t. Grabbing the phone, he calls Tabby’s extension and tells her to quickly rush to his desk, it is almost time for him to dig deeper into this sleeper cell and finally end it once and for all. Not taking his eyes off of the documents, not even for Tabby does he look up. For the first time in over a month, Matt is pushing his skills as an agent farther then ever before. The only thing I don’t understand is why they are backing this. I am going to have to go to the company and find out myself. Something isn’t right about this whole situation and there are too many questions that are being left unanswered. “What did you need Matt?” Tabby asks him, rubbing his arm ever so slightly; trying not to let him notice exactly what is going on. Putting her head down acting like she is trying to see what Matt is working on, she lets her brown hair touch his face and it causes him to smile a bit, but his mind is to focused on what is going on. “I got a lead Tabby, though it isn’t much I am going to follow up on it and see where it takes me. Don’t get too excited though, because we have hardly scratched the surface. I just wanted to make sure you guys were going to keep working and doing everything you can while I am gone.” Tabby nods her head like any little schoolgirl with a crush and then continues to stare at her boss. Matt tries to push her out of his mind as he opens up the top desk drawer and pulls out his automatic. Checking over the clip to make sure everything is all right, he lets out a smile and than pulls out a silencer from the same drawer. With a few quick twists it is applied to the Berretta, which he places in the holster inside his jacket pocket. Who knows what is going to be staring me in the face once I get there, but you can never be to over protected. I have been in situations like this before; it is best if you always have a back-up plan just in case everything else doesn’t go, as you want it. The Berretta is always my back-up plan and so far it has never let me down. “Also, I need you guys to get me some information on this business,” Matt barks out, letting Tabby know that play time is over. “I need security, office hours, workers, shifts and anything else that you can get me. I want you to send it to my palm pilot and I will check it on my way there. It should take me about 10 minutes to get there and if everything goes as I have planned it won’t take me long to get inside.” Without saying another word, Matt jumps to his feet, pushing the chair out from behind him. His 5’8” frame matches up well with Tabby’s 5’7” build and for the first time in a while, there is a woman who isn’t taller or shorter then him. Letting a small smile form at this notion, Matt makes his march out of the CIA office and to his Explorer. *** Location: Office Building, Langley VADate: April 22, 2003Time: 1:45 am Quietly standing outside the building, Matt lets the cool night air nip at his face. Everything is dark except for the tiny bit of light that is shining on the building. The clouds cover up most of the moon tonight and for the most part it is a pretty bland night. Matt smiles as he stares at the tower of a building in front of him. It is molded so perfectly, the glass held together by the large metal beams and from what Matt can tell the whole building is made of glass. This shouldn’t be hard to break into, it is just a matter of being silent and stealthily Of course it is a matter of being stealthily you dumb ass. I can’t believe I just said that, why wouldn’t you be stealthily when you were breaking into a building. This job is really starting to take its toll on my brain. Taking a step back, to get a better view of the structure. Matt decides it would be best, just to cut the power and walk in the front. From everything Tabby sent to his palm pilot that would be the way to do it. A magnet locks the front doors and the security system is wired to the power box. So by cutting the power he is able to kill two birds with one stone and that is right up his ally. Crouching down a little, he makes his way to the right side of the building. Listening carefully to make sure no one is around. The only sounds he hears are birds making a small rustle and his own shoes splashing across the pavement. Reaching the right side of the building, he finds the small green power box and yanks it open to reveal ten small wires, each a different colour. Some are red and blue and others are green and yellow, but none the same colour and none notable. It is too hard to tell which one goes to which. I guess I will just cut them all and hope for the best. Taking a pair of small wire cutters from his pocket, Matt finishes off all the wires in one quick swipe of the sharp cutters and starts to make his way towards the front of the building. Walking with a little more swagger in his step and a little less worry. He isn’t taking this as seriously as he was a minute ago and it is starting to show. The famed agent is letting his own fame go to his head as he walks up to the doors of the of dead office building and pulls them open. Look at that, I got them opened in one try I really am that damn good. That is the last thought Matt thinks, as he feels volts of electricity run through his body. In a matter of mere seconds his body is limp against the hard tile floor and two men appear from behind him. Their features are to hard to make out in the darkness, but it doesn’t make a difference, they incapacitated their target and soon it was only going to get harder for him. 2:00 AM
