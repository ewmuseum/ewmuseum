Interview With The Devil: The Death (Part 9)

4 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-4-03 We find ourselves located in the same place as before. Shane is still on his knees with his arms spread out to the heavens as tears continue to stream down his face. His head tilted down looking at the tombstones before tossing it up to the sky. “Why?!” Shane shouts out the heavens in a very shaky voice, “Why did you have to take them away from me?!” Shane then dives face first into the grass growing on the mounds and runs his fingers through them, “The only people in my life that meant anything to me; and they had to be taken away from me.” Shane grabs a handful of the grass and jolting back into his upright position tosses it into the sky, “For what reason?!” Shane stands to his feet and begins kicking at the ground and shouting at the top of his lungs in frustration, before finally coming to a halt by falling to the ground breathing heavily and face still full of streaming tears, “If only you would stop playing games with me and just open up my eyes.” Slowly calming himself down Shane just begins to just look up to the heavens, wiping a few of the pools from his eyes, “That’s all I want, Lord. I want an answer for all of the problems and everything that has caused this in my life. I’ve always been what you’ve wanted me to be, haven’t I?” Shane closes his eyes and begins to shake his head, “You’ve kept me here wondering for so long what my calling was and then when I think I’ve found it out, you let Satan bring down all of this warfare on my life. How much longer do I have to go before you’ll intervene with it all?” Suddenly everything grows quiet and a scene appears before Shane. A blurry mass appears and through it we begin to see something that couldn’t possibly be here in the middle of this area. Shane begins to focus into the hole with a gaping mouth. He gradually brings himself to his feet and begins to approach it; and before he knows it, he has been sucked right into the mass. On the other side, we find ourselves right outside the cemetery. The day has become night now and in the distance a couple can be seen walking down the street. Shane begins to shake his head, “No…Why this?” The couple continues to walk down the street, as Shane begins to run at them screams a very painful scream. The couple keeps on walking, as if Shane isn’t even there, until he reaches them and as he goes to grab one, he comes crashing right down into the sidewalk. His face becomes bloody from the impact, and as he turns he hears a screaming. Shane closes his eyes as the tears begin to stream once again as we can now see that a man had jumped from the bushes along the side of the cemetery and had slit the throat of the man of the couple and then proceeded to stab the female. “I didn’t want to see this,” Shane says as he lowers his head; eyes closed, “I asked for answers, but I didn’t want to see this.” Shane slowly begins to come to his feet once more and begins to walk over to the scene. On the ground lay the couple, brutally murdered, as the killer was seen running off in the distance. Shane kneels down beside the couple and begins to weep as he tries to place his hand on their heads, “You bring me here to show me the deaths of my parents and in return I’m not even allowed to morn properly. Are you trying to get me to run from you?” Then suddenly a voice emerges from the sky, “No, my Son, but I am trying to get you to understand that this is your destiny from me, but you need to get your life in check before I can have anything to do with. So, go and it shall be done!” ~Series to Continue~
