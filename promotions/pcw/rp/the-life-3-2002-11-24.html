Date: 11.24.02 'That's mighty nice of you to say.' 'Oh really, was nice of you to abandon me when I called for your help, now didn't I. Seems like the tables have turned now haven't they?' A Heart Monitor beeps slowly as the sunlight creeps through the tapeless blinds set up into the room. Here lies Vincient Calvary Browne II, on his back with IV tubes sticking from his wrist. The sharp needles protrude his veins, but relax him nto the feel the pain. Its been like that for four days now, as his little sister Dawn Browne happens to stoop in, her eyes catching her brother in this sort of predicament gives her a half hearted smile, a jerk on what side of love and happiness to be on. 'Thanks,' Vincient muttered as his sister brought him a glass of orange juice. He sipped it from a straw, thanks to the fact that he couldn't lift himself up after almost being burned along with his girlfriend Dana Smith. 'Yet you didn't answer did you? I called out and you left me.' 'Hey!' she snapped at him with cheetah like speed. 'Remember how you left me the first time.' 'I know,' he grumbles, 'and you were the lifeless bait.' 'Shut up.' She eyes her watch, starring deep into the diamond studded face. She blinks a few times before casting a glance at the window. In her silence, Vincient breaks it. 'Oh, you weren't good enough to pay your big brother a welcome visit? Who ya watin' on?' 'Nobody' she told him coldly. Rather she wasn't one of the type of people to visit the ones not on the move or have something active to lend their lives towards. Dawn was your typical life of the party, without that, she's quite lost out in the world. Vincient adjusted himself, tapping a red button located on the side panel of the bed for his upper torso to rise, catching a better look of how Dawn stares into the window. There's a difference from her first glance, as she blinks sighing in the process. In all of her past anguishes, she was an enigma - one of those unwrapped pieces you find under the tree at Christmas. 'I know you sis.' 'You don't -' Vince cut her off, 'I do, don't lie. No need too.' 'I know,' she stopped her malice hatred for only a second, letting some red to run in that heart of hers. 'But he's different.' 'Oh, so its a guy thing?'Vincient suddenly got more interested in what his reporter for a sister had to say. He struggled shifting upward as Dawn turned away from the window, her full attention towards the man in the hospital gown and bed. Vince was bald, six foot three in length and stocky in build. He'd gone to the gym, worked out twice a week and was starting to grow bigger than what puberty gave him. He smirked. Dawn couldn't resist for that, no woman he'd ever met in life ever did. 'Didn't know you still dated. Thought you left men out of your lives.' 'Maybe if you weren't so distraught over whom I did have for boyfriends I wouldn't be in this mess.' she told him venomously. 'Or maybe -' 'I wouldn't be in this bed?' he answered her question for her. She knew the statement like a well known ploy given out during a juicy drama read out at the Cinema. 'Yeah, but Dana's different. She has that New York lifestyle, crazy but sexy as hell at the same time.' 'You just don't get women.' 'I never got you.' he reminded. She raised an eyebrow, 'Big brothers aren't supposed to worry about their little sisters.' 'Duh.' She smiled, before dragging over to him a glass of water. First it was orange juice, earlier delivered by the nurse. Vincient thought of her to be sweet, tanned legs, curves to go on curves, walnut complexion - fine looking sister in his opinion. Plus, a heaving chest also makes you think something like that. A nod from him as Dawn flicked her soft brown hair back, 'Like'd you care for a good woman anyway.' 'I'd care for any woman who'd put her desires there and not want to fuck me up in the process, that's what I'd want.' 'Yeah, right.' She stepped out of the room, not shamed, nor oddly taking back by her brother's words. Simply enough, Dawn didn't care enough for it to be all the more personal. It was just business, since Dante Jackson was in the lobby suite, she'd might as well make a stop here. It wasn't that she happened to not care for Vincient, but two different lives tell two different tales. Its all the more certain down the road, as Dawn let another pass of her life go, Dante Jackson took a few steps in, smiling at his baby sister April.'Well, hiya.' 'Dante!' the former doll of the unknown Jackson family welcomed her brother with open arms. The former RUSH Heavyweight title contender struggles back, smiling as he grasps onto her. 'Now when did they say you'd get out of here?' 'In a few days, hopefully to catch Alliances. Wanna see my big brother win something out there.' She was right, just about everybody in the Jackson camp wanted a win from their favorite son. The Tag Titles were one he personally wanted back since his Cruiserweight dreams went up in smoke with a Turmoil loss to the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid, taking a finals spot against Tim Walden. Its more than just representing a drink, is sort of life to Dante. Of course he's not going to shill out the drink he chooses to love or he'd be addicted right now. Then, you have Alliances. In Dante's mind, this is the big one, the one nobody can take or try to take away from him. He needs this, badly. If you're on an opposite squad, you're getting tossed over. You are getting beat. You will meet your Genocidal Overdose. 'A few? Why not today?' he asked her. He took a seat down, his knees weak enough to try and give out on him in the midst of all this. She smiled, knowing her big brother would watch her, just in case some more insanity occurred around her. 'Its all right, I mean its good if you want to win the matches, but three? Isn't that barbaric?' 'Yeah,' he told her, rocking his shoulders back. 'Sucks that they want me there before six.' April nodded, her cuts healed up, yet the test and analysis of the medical workers have been truthfully stalling her, so she can be only one hundred percent. Not anything else below that. As Dante begins to get up from the seat, April calls out. 'Promise you'll come back for me?' 'Promise.' He smiles toward her as he shuts the door behind her. She sighs, resting her head back on the pillow as Dawn begins to step into the corridor, a not pleased smile on her face. Dante catches her, raising her attention. 'What's wrong?' 'You've got a call.' * * * I thought back, hours before the exact venue and conversation, to the place where I found out that I'd advanced. It was seriously nudging me like a mosquito bite I had to scratch, what to do next in life? Remembering back to a place in which, maybe my entire ego began, the original source. 'Class, take your seats.' Back in Brooklyn, New York. It didn't feel like it was the time for idol memories of the past to get to me. It was the ninth grade, a burning feeling logged itself into my mouth, a feeling I get almost everytime something bigger than what the original brought to the table. I ate at this feeling and didn't feel morbid towards it. But first, let me set up the scenery before we get into all the psychology. This was a public school, housing and rent were very high as so was the school's prestige. Having atleast three floors to its credit, all of them housing the various grade levels, it was unrare for pickings and hazing to occur. It happened, more often than usual. Quiet chime pitched bells rang at the end of every class, a ode to a heavenly choir, or something. We all stepped out, my friends and usual partners in crime happened to treat it as somewhat of a joke, although we still found it cool in places. It happened to dawn upon me then, that freshman year changed everything you thought about yourself before. Before I was just a fourteen year old scrap from Brooklyn using my voice to get around the block. By the end, I'd be maybe valedictorian, or Prom King. Both of those happened, but I wasn't excited by them. Particularly one day, March 16th 1996, I think this is where it all began, and it started out innocently. That day, things went around pretty normal. A day away from Spring Break and everybody had the itch for getting out of school. Me and my friend Bishop (you'll learn about him later.) happen to start pulling pranks on people, most notably people that happened to piss us off or do something that we hadn't come up with yet. We both shot for the one single goal: Legend. During third period, things took a turn for the better as I dozed off in my Bio-Chemistry class. The teacher, an old Caucasian woman of fifty five with short brown hair in curls had a pink sweater and some faded blue jeans on. Me and Bishop called her 'Turtle' because of her tendency to slouch and move slow like a turtle. So one of our tricks happen to have one of those, cherry bombs in her frog experiment, sending frog excrement everywhere. It happened to land in the face of the biggest jock in the school. Gary Owens was the 'freak' athlete of the school, a body fat of 3% and a neck the size of a thick belt. A crimson flushed his face, causing my laughter to cease at that very moment. He glared at me, silently motioning to me that it was me and him, at the parking lot at the sound of the final bell. It didn't fear me that much, it kinda made a sick smile come across my face. Bishop, a few seats away from me looked at my smirking face and looked in distress. 'Are you out of your damn mind? He's going to fuck you up!' His obvious answer dealt with atleast some thrown in foul language. It his nature for him, and probably why he's in the amount of shit he's in right now. My only conscience towards it all wasn't how this man was going to tear me inch by inch and limb by limb. It was on the part of whether or not he would be able to do it period. 'He ain't doing a damn thing to me.' I chuckled at Bishop who shook his head in disgust. I could have found it only for me to be the underdog in such a situation, but its my role. Better yet, its my lifestyle. Shit, this has to go as I plan it, or that ape is going to crush me!, I thought to myself, tapping a blue ink pen on the lab table. Tracy was only a few seats away from me, thinking about me or something other. 'You dared him, didn't you?' 'Dared him?' She seemed like it was a rhetorical question for what I did, she had brains, but no common sense. 'That went off without my planning!' I sulked down in my seat, playing the role to a perfection. Everyone inside of the room thought I was the underdog going to into this. The bell rang, students filed out of their seats, starting up hype and build up for the fight. It went click in my mind, I had to do something in order for this entire thing to blow over. I bald up a fist. The sun was at full mast, glaring down with the unsafe rays on us, as a circle of people locked, barring escape from either myself or this 'ape' known as Gary Owens. He grunted like an ox, pacing around and waiting for me to pounce on him with tendency. Enough to say: I got my ass kicked. A few bruises and welts on my chest and a small knot on the top of my head, I kinda found it out that I'd cut my lip by taking one of his knees to my face. I thought he'd break my nose from the feel of it. He had a broken nose from a punch of mine and a concussion from a series of stiff kicks to the face. I felt good from that. 'OW!' I shouted out in the nurse's office, painfully as the alcohol pad was placed along a cut on my head. 'Stop yer squirmin' and hold still.' she had a deep Cajun accent to her, a mixture of Creole and Anglo Saxon for her background. The nurse uniform stuck out in my mind. Deep inside, I could feel a build up, clogged up blood from spilling out of my skull. Easily, she should have told me to find a Band Aid and leave, but this was a office stay, just to prove to people that I wasn't a wimp or a shrimp or a chicken by far. I'd just fought to a draw with the biggest person at school and she wants to kick me out of the nurse's office? So damn callous, nurses are cruel. Then I was the underdog, you could have placed the Superman esque suit on my body right then and there, but it wouldn't have made difference in the next outcome. Stepping out of the nurse's office with a smile on my face, she shook her head. 'That der boy has a screw loose or two.' * * * 'Let me get drunk tonight.' That's what I told myself, walking in at one o'clock in the morning with a slight jig to my step and a veer towards the bar. Not one of those lacy clubs hangouts you would find the young stars of Hollywood getting trashed, but a low budget place playing old Miles Davis classics out of the jukebox. A pool table rested in the center of the establishment, with the pool balls racked in a diamond shaped entrapment, pool cues slanted along the edges. 'Hit me man.' The bartender shook his head with deep wonder in his mind. 'With what?' I tried to play stupid with him, 'A bourbon, on the rocks.' Yeah, then follow that up with a lemon twist and a mixture of codeine and cough syrup. I wanted him to figure out that I was off of my game for the night, I wanted him to fall in to the trap. What kind of a plan was it to get drunk at one in the morning? I didn't know, weren't a soul in between me and the jukebox for a twenty foot radius. After that I gulped down the burbon shot and asked the man to hit me again. A damn night is only for one person, I could have been anywhere in the world, but a Boston pub suited me well, and I still had the championship effect to play upon...
