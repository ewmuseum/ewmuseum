Dating Miss Willis: Batman, The Ride

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Dating Miss Willis: Batman, The Ride Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: May 27th Romance. It is a concept that all of us experience at different times, in different ways, and with different people. Yet, all of us will concur that true romance is perhaps the greatest of all human emotions, but perhaps the most difficult to attain. After all, the difference between love and lust can be, at times, amazingly thin. Where are Lovecraft's emotions for his intern, Katherine Willis? He's not quite sure. Would you be? We've all felt as if we've been head over heels in love with a childhood crush before, but looking back now, we can realize our actions as childish infatuation. Reginald V. Lovecraft is a 47 year old man. Still, he is capable of childish emotions...but in his heart, he knows that what he feels for Katherine is beyond lust, after all, he has known her for roughly two years now. And now, he has her to himself. No Powell to make depricating comments about his technique. No Powell to interrupt his date. Yes, this would be a perfect day... They had spent the early morning driving to their destination. Not somewhere Lovecraft would have chosen normally, but it still sounded like fun... ::The Politically Incorrect limousine is parked in a prime parking spot outside of Six Flags Great Adventure, the largest amusement park in the world. It is known for its eleven roller coasters, which shall surely test Mr. Lovecraft, due to his lack of roller coaster experience. For once, no one is in the vehicle, as Lovecraft and Willis have already entered the park.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: WE'RE GOING ON THAT?! ::Katherine Willis stands, looking at Mr. Lovecraft, a smirk on her face. She is clad in somewhat less formal attire, a pair of jeans and a pink t-shirt, making her look more her twenty-three years. Lovecraft's clothing looks no less odd, as his khakis and polo shirt are quite a step down from his loud red suit. Katherine sees the look on Reginald's face, and decides to answer.:: Katherine Willis: Yes, Reggie, we're going on that. ::'Batman, the Ride' comes into view. It is one of Great Adventure's flagship rides. The rider's legs hang from the bottom of the seat, as he or she is taken through multiple inversions, also known as 'loops':: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Did...did...did you just call me Reggie? Katherine Willis: Of course I did, Reggie! Reginald V. Lovecraft: May I ask, why? Katherine Willis: It's a term of endearment, silly! :She grins, and hugs him.: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Well, I think we've found more ways to enhance my training in extreme. Katherine Willis: How do you figure that? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Because, I figure that with all of the upside-downing and right-sideuping and twisting and turning that we seem to be on pace to do today, I'll probably feel like I've been through a goddamned hardcore match by the end of it! Katherine Willis: That's the spirit! Reginald V. Lovecraft: I must admit...all of these men joining up for this thing is quite an intimidating row. We have Miyagawa, a former World Champion, former Television champion, and a former Cruiserweight Champion.... Katherine Willis: Yeah...you ARE a tag team wrestler. What do you do if you get caught against someone who has accomplished singles skills? Reginald V. Lovecraft: I brawl them. I may not be as proficient a wrestler as my partner, but I am a far better brawler, which is what this match will be. A schoolyard brawl. I know how to use the fists that God gave me, and I now know how to use the kendo stick that Miss Willis gave me. So, if someone like Miyagawa comes over with intentions of putting the Governor down for the count, these fists will be a'flyin! He may be a former World Champion, but I am a former Georgian Golden Gloves winner. If I can get him into a situation of Marquis of Queensbury rules, I can take him down... I know it. Katherine Willis: I hope so...you certainly seem to be putting a lot into this match. Reginald V. Lovecraft: I am, Katherine...this is my chance to prove that I'm not just a tag team guy with Powell....you know, I'm getting up in years. I'm already the oldest guy in this company, and there are never any guarentees as to how this body of mine will hold out. Powell and I always discuss how much we do want singles titles...despite our tag team successes. It's a matter of pride...to prove we're more than just a tag team. Riptah and Short Circuit only won one singles match between the two of them. We are the most complete tag team in existance. Now, it's just a matter of time before I prove it.. Katherine Willis: Wow.....I had no idea it meant this much to you... Reginald V. Lovecraft: William S. Powell is in his 20s...he has 20 some odd years before he gets to this point in his career.. I do not. If I want to get a singles title, it is here and now. This is my greatest opportunity, my one chance. I must become the Extreme Champion of Primetime Championship Wrestling... it is my one goal. With Powell taking care of personal business, I can focus on my OWN career. I can watch out solely for Reginald V. Lovecraft. Until William and I have another tag team encounter, I am focused 150 percent on this 'School's Out' match. Katherine Willis: Good for you, but we're almost to the front of the line for Batman. ::Lovecraft shudders:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Ah well, let the training continue.. PCW, Reginald V. Lovecraft is ready, willing, and able. And, come Friday Fusion, I am the next PCW Extreme Champion. The Governor's office is open. And, I am afraid that everyone else's efforts to become champion will...be........vetoed. ::Lovecraft, with Katherine's encouragement, steps up to the ride, as we fade to...:: Paid for by the Friends of Reginald V. Lovecraft!Promoting the Aristocracy of Extreme. ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
