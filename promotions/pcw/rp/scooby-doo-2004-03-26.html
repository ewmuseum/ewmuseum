Scooby-Doo

26 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th March 2004 It’s a cold evening in Clarksville, Tennessee. Eric Sagar sits in his office. Its part of a block of modern flats that overlook the busy Clarksville night life. Eric Sagar’s office is very classy. He’s just finishing up some paper work. It’s in preparation for Turmoil’s match for his client Propaganda against new comer Jimmy Diablo. Eric’s office is pretty bare. He has a green plant in the corner, a desk, a chair behind it and a chair in front. On the desk he has nothing but pens, an intercom, a phone, a fax machine and a paper weight. In his draws he has stationary and of course a bible. That’s it. The walls are coated in a hazelnut brown paint which is illuminated by the moonlight that shines brightly through the window behind Sagar which has horizontal blinds draped down over it. The moonlight battles through the slanted blinds. The other source of light is the bright lamp hanging from the ceiling. Sagar’s office is like something you’d expect to see in a 60’s detective series. He has his trademark stylish grey suit on, white shirt and his grey tie. He looks as smart as ever but his slouching body language and his relaxed expression tells you that his winding down about to finish. His door swings open and his middle-aged security, Mrs. Robinson steps out. She has a light tone of shoulder length blonde hair that is going grey at the roots. Her face doesn’t look worn in comparison to Eric Sagar’s who’s bulldog like face looks like a weather map. Mrs. Robinson is wearing a lilac suit jacket, matching ankle length dress and matching shoes. She has thick black spectacles. Eric Sagar lifts his head and takes a sniff of the air. “My word, that’s a delightful scent Mrs. Robinson.” A compliment, especially coming from such a pathetic misogynist like Sagar. “What is it?” “It’s obsession.” Mrs. Robinson replies in her good old Southern girl dialect. “No, I was just wondering.” Sagar defends. “No, silly. It’s Calvin Klein’s Obsession. It’s a scent.” Mrs. Robinson tells him. “Oh. Alright then.” Eric Sagar continues his work. “You preparing for the match tomorrow?” Mrs. Robinson enquires. “I am.” “Do you think Propaganda will win?” “Of course.” Eric Sagar scoffs. “I mean he is against some small fry named Jimmy Diablo.” Eric Sagar drops his pen and pushes his work away from him as if to say “I’m done” he continues to speak. “I mean the mans name alone shows how much of a sinner he is. Diablo is Spanish for the Devil. The Devil is the figure of all evil and all that is wrong in the world. He is what possesses the world to do wrong. He is what possesses the world to break the ten commandments. And what’s worse is its SPANISH! A dirty, good-for-nothing Spaniard word. He is scum. Truly scum.” Eric Sagar rants. “I see…” “And look at Propaganda.” Eric Sagar continues not acknowledging Mrs. Robinson’s statement. “He’s a seven foot monster! A messenger of god to rid of all that is wrong in the world, like foreigners, like people who are named after the devil. Diablo is a nobody. Do you hear me?” “Yes?” “A NOBODY!” “Ok.” “He will at the power of brother Propaganda, the brains of me and the undeniably blinding power of the Lord.” Sagar preaches. “Can I get an A-Men, Mrs. Robinson?” “A-men” she responds half heartedly. “Jimmy Diablo is nothing but a stepping stone, which is stupid enough to challenge Propaganda after he made an EXAMPLE of Inphino Blitz, a supposed PCW legend. Some claim he is a dead cert to get into the hall of fame. To Propaganda he was nothing more than simply fresh meat.” “I agree.” “Of course you do, Sister Robinson!” Eric Sagar exclaims. “Because I’m right!” Silence fills the room. “So are you off now, Mrs. Robinson?” Sagar asks generally. “Yes, if you don’t mind. John and I are taking the kids to the cinema.” Mrs. Robinson is pleased to answer this light-hearted question that is diverted from the topics of religion or wrestling. “You are? What film are you seeing?” “Oh just the new Scooby-Doo film.” Sagar drops his pen on the floor with a clatter and looks dumfounded at Mrs. Robinson. He is not impressed. “Is there something wrong, Mr. Sagar?” Mrs. Robinson cautiously asks knowing she has done something wrong. “Scooby-Doo?” The reverend blurts in disbelief. “Ummm… Yes.” “What sort of kids are you trying to raise?” Eric Sagar barks. “Good, well respecting Christian kids.” “Then why are you taking them to see that Satan supporting drivel?” “It’s just Scooby-Doo.” “JUST Scooby-doo? Do you know how many pro-Satanism hidden messages are in that tripe?” “No…” “Well let us start with the name. ‘Scooby-Doo’ is the name dirty homos use for an activity where they strip down to their flesh, excrete everywhere and roll around in their own poop until they orgasm or are arrested.” “Oh sweet Lord in heaven! I FEEL ILLL!” Mrs. Robinson moans. “Did you know some sodomites save their own faeces in plastic bags for weeks just for such an occasion?” Eric Sagar says, he is awfully well informed. If not too well informed. “That’s disgusting.” “It is, indeed. And look at the characters? That ‘Shaggy’ fellow. He is nothing more than an undernourished drug addict who spends all his money on drugs. He is always hungry and he is always paranoid. And these ‘Scooby Snacks’ they speak of is nothing more than a concoction of heroin and cocaine. He would get so high he would think the dog is talking to him. And DOG! That’s God spelt backwards another perverse way of promoting Satan. I have been told if you look closely enough that you can actually see scars from where Shaggy has injected himself with liquefied forms of ‘Scooby Snacks’. It’s disgusting.” Eric Sagar yaps like an angry Jack Russell. “I never thought of it like that.” Mrs. Robinson is getting disturbed. “And Thelma. Nothing more than a dyke. An ugly, unloved, unwanted feminist dyke. I mean look at what she does. Read books and command people around!” Eric shouts. “And this Fred. It is blatant that he is nothing more than a faggot! A sodomite! I mean his camp clothing alone is enough to point a big ‘Queer’ arrow over him.” “But what about Daphney? She’s okay isn’t she?” Mrs. Robinson asks. “Ah, Daphney…” “She’s okay?” “As okay as a common whore can be. She does nothing more than smile a strumpet’s smile and look at her ridiculously short skirts exposing her bare legs! HER BARE LEGS! It burns my eyes! MY EYES, DAMN IT!” Sagar is going over the top with his incessant whining. “Oh dear.” “They travel in a van, which is an enclosed vehicle that suggests that inside it is abnormal deeds are going on.” Eric Sagar tells her like he’s telling a child a story. “But don’t you and Brother Propaganda ride in a van.” “Uhhh…” She has Sagar stuck. “Let’s just leave that, then. Scooby-Doo is nothing more than a deviant form of propaganda for homosexuals, Satanists, drug addicts, prostitutions, feminist and the fun you have when you take drugs. And you should not take your child to watch this.” There is silence. “Looks like a quiet night in then.” Mrs. Robinson says. !~The End~!
