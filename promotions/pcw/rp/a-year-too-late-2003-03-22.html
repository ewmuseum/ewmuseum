A Year Too Late

22 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: A Year Too Late Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 22/03/03 I bet you cheered, didn’t you? I bet you leaped out of your tatty, aged easy-chairs and jumped up and down in joy so your belly flopped over the waistband of your cheap jeans. I bet some of you fat slobs even had to hold your chest and wait for the pains to ease afterwards. For the sake of natural selection, I hope some of you just dropped down dead. Future generations will benefit from your death. So, Jeremy Howard is back. I guess you think that I’m going to be cowering in fear, huh? Krayzie’s back so Xavier has now been put in his place. The Scientologist won’t dare pull anything now the Krazed Lunatic is back. Geez, if you people were any dumber I swear you’d forget to breath. Because it hardly happened like that the first time, did it? I don’t recall one year ago where Jeremy Howard was running me out of PCW, that would be because it didn’t happen. I beat him, remember that, I won the battle and I won the war. Just as I beat Drake Maxwell, Token Weed, Juan Borro and Impact... I beat him. If any of those came back would you expect me to cower? If anything, I’m pleased he’s back. Throw a cat a mouse and I’m sure he’ll be pleased to. But, as always, I’m afraid you fans will expect too much. Why? Because you’re a bunch of idiotic Peons. Okay, so that isn’t the reason... Still true though. You expect Jeremy to come back in a blaze of glory and rid PCW of the Kannon menace once and for all. Not for the first time, you kind of missed the point. Then again, when your own lives have no point, it must be hard to see the point in other’s lives. I was gone, I was about to walk. PCW was about to see the last of me. No more Xavier, no more Scientology. Then, as the saying goes... “Just when I thought I was out, they dragged me back in.” I’m still in PCW because of Jeremy Howard, so, by my own logic... Which is about the best kind you’ll ever know... Anything I do to, in, or with PCW now is all his fault. After all, if it wasn’t for him I’d be far away. What’s he going to do? Fire me? Fine me? Suspend me? How can you threaten to fire a man when a few days ago he quit? How can he make me pay the fine when he as no threat to force me? What punishment is a suspension when it sounds pretty good to me? Face it, I’m smarter, I’m better, and I just got the cosiest deal I’ve ever had in PCW. I have a free roam, I can do as I please, I can walk down a corridor and cripple the first guy I see... Why? I have a huge bubble around me named “Lawless”. Thank you Jeremy, it’s a very nice gift you gave me. So far I’m liking your mission to “Save me”. Maybe if you treated me this good in the first place, I wouldn’t have succumbed to the lure of Tyler Nelson. Maybe if I was given this freedom instead of being your lackey last year, I wouldn’t of destroyed your legacy. It’s still debated fiercely as to whether “Tough Love” works, beating someone, depriving them, punishing them to help in the long run. But “Soft Love”, getting all sappy and touchy-feely is NEVER going to work. “Oh, Kannon... You have to say sorry because one you were a nice guy!” Once I was a naive moron. Once I was no better than anyone who watches PCW, and I’m never going back there. I have nothing to apologise for, I made a great life for myself and I owe it all to me. What do you want me to say “Sorry” for? “Oh, Mr. PCW Fan, I’m sorry I’ve worked had and long to make a great living for myself and my wife.” Get real. I will not say sorry. I will not be saved. I will not repent, I will not falter. I will not weaken. I will not buckle. I will not stand down. I will not give in. I will not change. As a Scientologist, I’m used to others trying to impose their views on me, but it has never worked. You can’t MAKE someone be sorry, you can’t CONVINCE a man he has done wrong when he hasn’t. If anything, it’s Jeremy Howard who should be apologising. Why? Because he made me the man I am. His selfishness drove me to find other allies. Where was he when I needed him? Where was he when I was at my lowest? WHAT DID HE DO TO SAVE ME THEN?!? He did nothing. You’re a year too late Jeremy. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
