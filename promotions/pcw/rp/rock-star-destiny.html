Date: RP #1 For Friday Night Fusion,House on Haunted Hill Match Sitting in the cool darkness of my friends parlor aleviated thunder rumbling in my brow. My right eye was swollen shut. It looked like a small grape fruit, with a light skim of blood sealing it shut. The blood had formed a scab blinding me in one eye. That was alright by me. I didn't want to look at myself in the mirror anymore. I could feel the light rustle of magnolia colored silk as it moved along my chest. It was a nice shirt, a big red carp chasing a similarly colored dragonfly up my chest. It felt good to have the light material swath my body. It took my mind off the fact that there was a rash of bruises decorating my ribcage. It helped me to forget the racketing cough that shook me every few minutes or so. Then there was the blood that I had coughed up onto Quo's vanilla colored shag rug. It was drying right next to my tan Lugz boots. Hope he doesn't mind the thick globs of crimson laced black on that carpet. Didn't he tell me he just got that? Fuck 'im... Not my problem I got a couple of loose ribs. I did, have a problem with the silver Motorola cell-phone ringing on the little oak table next to my chair. It split the milky half-light of the room. It split my reverie like an axe would split wood, if you're digging that cheesy fucking analogy. Got that one off my buddy Eddie Mandrake. He's got millions of cheesy analogies. I flipped the cell up to my diamond studded left ear, wincing as I realized who was on the other side. 'Yo.'- Voice I let out a long, tired sigh. This I did not need. Not after scraping myself out of Quo's guestroom bed and throwing myself into a pair of jeans, my nice shirt, and boots. Not after that struggle. Putting on my drawers had been hard enough. Half-blinded because some scrawny little motherfucker thinks he can throw a punch. Piece of shit. I hope Celt liked those knees I threw in there. I hope he's feelin' that match as much as I am. The aches, the pains, the knotted muscles, the splitting migraine. The same migraine that the bass voice of Dante Jackson was only helping to worsen. 'What up, meng.' I shot back, running a caloused hand over the tender area of my face, noticing the rough scratch of stubble on the line of my jaw. 'Not much. Just kickin' it... Wonderin' if you gonna make the scene on Friday.' I slouched down into the soft, ancient leather of the chair, knowing what was going to be headed my way. Dante Jackson, Spiritual Advisor for Playaz Inc., was about to lay a sermon on me the likes of which Jesse Jackson would be proud of. Wasn't that cheesy? Can I get an 'Amen!' on that one? What the fuck is wrong with me? 'I don't know. Maybe. Depends on whether or not I get my prescription filled.' Maybe that would get him off my ass. He knew that I had taken a hellacious on the last Adrenaline. Well, 'hellacious' is pushing it but it was a continuation of a trend that was beginning to bother me. A trend I'm sure my buddy, Dante Jackson was sure to point out. 'You gotta be there, Ali. I'm not fucking around, dude. You've got to be there. You're turning into a fluke. You've got people sayin' you ain't worth shit.' His voice held a tone that said he was relaying something very important. As if I couldn't hear what those little fuckers were saying behind my back. Those snickers, jeers, and dead stares as I walked the halls backstage; I knew what they were for. I knew and I didn't need the self-righteous prick on the other end of the line to tell me about it. He, obviously, thought differently. 'You know, you're letting me and Quo down. Every time you go out there and lose, you make Quo look like shit. You make me look like shit. We brought you into the PCW. We did it... We brought you out of PURE after you were fired from RUSH. We saved your ass. Why would you go out there and make us look like fools for bringing you in? Do you want us to sound like dicks when we tell people that you're mint? What the fuck, Ali...' Lord, please take me now. Dante's sentimental crap only worsened my headache, making me wish for more Vicodin. Is that what I had come to? Waiting for more little pills- all color coded so you can tell which piece of candy you get to pop first- and taking shit from 'The Lyrical Messiah'? Oh how I wished that I could just lay down and die. Wrap myself in the warm grip of the soil and sleep for a bit. just a bit, not much. Just until Jackson shut up... 'So what do you think, Ali? Think you can at least put an effort into Friday. Not make an ass out of yourself?' What do you think about me sticking a fork in your eye? Think it would help if I punched you straight in the mouth? Think back to the days when we were in RUSH together, Jackson. Remember how many men I put away with just my fists alone. I was dropping 'wrestlers' left and right. Ali Khadafi can still throw down better than anyone else in the game; keep that in mind, Chuckles. That's what I wanted to say. 'Sure thing, Dante.'- What I really said. What a pussy... 'Alright, Ali... That's what I like to hear. I got your back if you need anything, dude. I'm here for you.' He replied with surprising sincerity. 'Eat my ass with syrup.' I quipped. I cut off his startled reply with a flick of my Rolex adorned wrist. Fucking idiot, calling me up and telling me what to do. Calling me a failure... Like I recreate 'Lord of the Rings' and expect people to be entertained. In the immortal words of Cedric the Entertainer: 'Nigga please!
