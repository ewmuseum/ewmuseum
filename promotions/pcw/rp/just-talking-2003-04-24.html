Just talking...

24 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-24-03 ~ The scene opens up to Hank~! Leaning against his black van with his arms crossed. Hank is wearing a long black leather trench coat, black jeans, and a white wife beater. Jenn approaches Hank and wraps her arms around his waist. Hank wraps his arms around her and kisses her on the forehead. Hank then opens the side cargo door of his van and sits there on the floor of the van. Jenn takes a seat next to him. Hank has one foot in the van and on foot out side with Jenn lying her head on his chest, lying on the floor of the van. ~ Jenn: “You lost Hank.” Hank: “I know this, why are you telling me?” Jenn: “I dunno, just thought you’d be more disappointed than this.” Hank: “Ah when you lose as much as me you tend not to be as disappointed.” Jenn: “Well I’m sure you’ll get your shot hun.” Hank: “Yeah, probably will sooner or later. I’m one of the best performers they got!” Jenn: “You don’t have to convince me Hank.” Hank: “Well it obviously won’t be very easy convincing them. Ah well, jus’ means I won’t have much of a boring week.” (smiles) “Brianna annoys me, to think a woman is holding the belt I should be holding! No offense to women or anything but that’s my title and she’s only disgracing it wit’ every damn second she’s holding onto it!” Jenn: “I’m glad the women wrestle the men here.” Hank: (scratching his head) “Um, me too Jenn, I jus’ want my damn title back and I’m willing to do whatever it takes! I’m willing to climb the ladder all over again and take on jobbers like Tim Walden. Nobody, not even you, understand what this business means to me! I breathe every aspect of wrestling, I want to be the best I absolutely can be!” (sighs) “Then you have people like Clyde who have the longest Heavyweight Championship reign and are going to look to do the same wit’ the television championship now. Look at me, a man who has a true passion for this industry, and look where it’s gotten me?” Jenn: “To eight world title reigns in eight different federations dear.” Hank: “Oh right.” (snickers in embarrassment) “I nearly forgot about those. I guess they don’t mean as much to me as becoming Extreme Champion of PCW. Representing everything that tis be extreme. I like being the man to out beat in an extreme environment! There’s nothing I like more than being beat and beating people over the head with chairs, sledgehammers, and ladders. There’s not a man like me in this industry.” Jenn: “Well maybe not as stupid.” (laughs) Hank: “Hey now!” (smiles0 “Don’t be going there now. I may be stupid, but not as stupid as some of the PCW talent I saw last night performing.” Jenn: “Well to be honest your match wasn’t all that, and I really blame your opponent, a man who you pretty much had to carry throughout the entire match.” Hank: “Yeah well he’s the one wit’ the number one contendership and not me. I guess that’s all I am is a guy to put the others over.” Jenn: “Well you just keep working at it Hank and I’m sure you’ll win back your extreme championship.” Hank: “Yeah soon or later.” (sighs) “Then again I should go after other titles like the Rising Star, Cruiserweight, or maybe even Television championships. Maybe I shouldn’t be living in my PCW past. I dunno, I guess I’ll jus’ go wit’ the flow as I normally do.” Jenn: “Anyway I have to do some shopping, so I’ll see you back at the hotel?” Hank: “If you see me drive my van into the parking lot then yes.” Jenn: “That’s right you sleep in the back of your van now.” Hank: “Yeah, it’s quite comfortable surprisingly enough. I thought it was going to be a lot more uncomfortable.” Jenn: “Well you want to give me a ride?” Hank: (sarcastically) “Right, l’m jus’ going to let my girlfriend walk a few miles to the mall?” Jenn: “No, your limo is over there.” Hank: (turns red with embarrassment) “Oh, then use it. I wouldn’t want to deprive you of a limo experience.” ~ Jenn gets up and kisses Hank then walks to his limo across the parking lot. As Hank watches her, he stands up out of the van and closes the door. Hank walks over to his locker room with his wrestling gear bag in hand. Hank enters to find Tiffany Jennings sitting there waiting for him. ~ Hank: (setting his bag down on the floor) “Hello Tiffany.” Tiffany: “So you were the guy in the hotel.” (smiles) “It’s nice to see you back here Hank, it’s a real pleasure.” Hank: “Yeah it always is and you’d do good to remember I have a girlfriend whom I love very much.” Tiffany: “Anyway since I’m in here you think you could spare me an interview?” Hank: “Sure, I have a moment.” Tiffany: “Cool, thanks!” ~ Hank sits down beside Tiffany in a metal folding chair. ~ Hank: “First off I’d like to apologize for the accommodations of my locker room currently. You know I haven’t been here long enough to make the money to fully furnish in expensive leather, glass coffee tables, and big screen televisions.” Tiffany: “It’s more than alright Hank, I don’t need to sit on leather furniture to give an interview.” Hank: “Anyway you may start when ready.” Tiffany: “Well your first match went pretty well I thought considering you’ve been out of the ring for awhile. You lost, but you looked good. So what I’m trying to ask is how it felt for you to finally be back into a PCW ring after all of these years?” Hank: “Pretty damn well, I guess I was jus’ caught up in the moment and that’s why I lost or at least the excuse I keep telling myself to make me feel better.” (snickers) Tiffany: “So will you pick up where you left off , fighting in the extreme championship division?” Hank: “I’ll do whatever jus’ so long that I can be in that ring. Titles aren’t meaningful to me like most superstars. Jus’ being in that ring and entertaining each and every night is enough to satisfy me.” Tiffany: “How do you feel about losing to Adam Stryker your first match back?” Hank: “Tiffany, apart of me is angry, but then the part of me that thinks more logically kicks in and I realize it was jus’ that, my first match back. I had a lot to prove yes, but I also had a lot of ring rust. I’m sure Adam Stryker won’t forget the name Hank~! For as long as he lives if you ask me.” (snickers) “ I put up more of a fight then he thought I would. So you can say my feelings are mixed. One hand I knew I was going to lose or had a good chance. Then on the other hand I really wanted to believe I couldn’t lose and I was going to win no matter what.” Tiffany: “Do you think you and Adam Stryker will lock horns again down the road?” Hank: “If he wants to tango wit’ me then that’s cool, anytime and anyplace I’ll take him on. I’m always up for a fight, I’m always up to beat the crap out of somebody for the hell of it. He jus’ need remember it won’t be so easy next time.” Tiffany: “Why did you return Hank~!?” Hank: “Well I took a look around the PCW locker room and saw what was going down and jus’ knew they needed a man like me to grace their federation, sign that damn contract one last time! I returned because I needed to get back in the ring as much as the fans wanted me to get back into the ring. This is purely about what the fans wanted over what I wanted. I returned for my fans.” Tiffany: “Well Hank~! Good-luck in your PCW comeback and I guess I’ll see you around.” ~ Tiffany gets up and exits the locker room as Hank~! Flips on his small television monitor to watch some of the recent promos from PCW superstars. The scene cuts off to black. ~
