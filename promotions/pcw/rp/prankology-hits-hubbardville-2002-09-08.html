Prankology Hits Hubbardville

8 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08 - 09 - 02 'Stupid Jester thinks he can.. Jes-.. Um.. Jeste-.. Play a joke on Ellie?' said the murmuring voice the belonged to Gaz 'The Jester' Green. 'Think? I know I'm capable of pulling off yet another stunt on the Tara-Palmer Tomkinson of Primetime Championship Wrestling!' continued the jovial Jester, yet to be unveiled to the home audience. Our shot opens with two devious looking strangers homing in on the infamous property that belongs to the first couple of Xavier Kannon and his dizzy other half Ellie. Situated in the heart of Hubbardville, located in the area known as Kelly Preston our scene is set for yet another caper involving the dynamic duo of Pranks With Attitude. 'You do know that it'll be our heads on the chopping block if this ever comes to light?' hissed the distinctive voice of Nerezza. 'You worry to much - After suffering the Hallmark, I plan sweet revenge on the Front Office and the very man who has bled my wage packet dry Xavier Kannon!' bellowed the Original Prankster. Both individuals stood erect before the structure known as Hubbardville House clad in tight fitting black outfits that did neither member justice in the fashion sense. Even with their concealed faces both members of the trickster team could be identified as the shorter scouted ahead allowing his partner to pick at the fabric with its durability tested to the max, as his broad shoulders left no room for movement. Gaz ushered to the front and set his bag to the ground with a shallow thud, removing what appeared to be a drill like instrument the Jester sampled through the choice of drill ends. 'Why not put your foot through the door?' groaned the behemoth. Gaz looked at his pale compadre and simply rolled his eyes as he went to work on the hinges of the beautiful complex, his nimble fingers removing the frame slowly trying desperately not to disturb any of the interiors. 'After you.... Squire!' said the Lord Of Laughter, removing his mask and gesturing the titanic Asylum member to enter first. Suddenly a thunderous voice echoed from one of the connecting rooms, followed by a scrawny gentleman covered in overalls ridden in smears of plaster. 'Who's you guys.... Mr. Kannon didn't mention any contractors to me!?!' 'Hmm, we're here about the termite infestation, one helluva situation!' roared the PCW comic. 'Termites huh.... Can't stand pests and bugs, give me the damn willy's!' croaked the paid help, coughing in the process. 'Yeah - Well good news for you then. The boss tells me you'se got the rest of the day so that you don't get under our feet....' proclaimed Gaz Green still in full portrayal of a construction worker, before being untimely interrupted upon. 'Err... Ok... But how come you guys didn't knock first, hell you took the damn door right off its bleeding hinges?' questioned the plasterer looking every bit the interrogator. 'Hmm... Well... We can... Nerezza being the foreman can you explain our earlier actions?' stuttered the conniving comedian, looking at his partner for help. 'Termites in the doorframe, being wooden it be an ideal place for the little critters!!!' grinned the spawn of evil, literally pushing the poor contractor through the gaping hole that was once adorned by a door. Watching as the stranger drove off in the distance of the tranquil Hubbardville the uncanny connection of Gaz 'The Jester' Green and his vampire accomplice Nerezza stepped back into the confines of the family home set to make an immediate impact on the assailant who robbed them of their dignity during Friday Night Fusion. 'LET'S GET THIS SHOW ON THE ROAD!!!' roared the Prince Of Pranks as he looked over the dwelling. Nerezza decided to concentrate on the higher levels of the mansion leaving the founder of 'Prankology' to weave his magic on the war zone created earlier by none other than the dizzy diva Ellie. Gaz leaving the premises momentarily returned ladder under his arm, tool bag present and correct and loosened the light fixtures whilst rigging them to a trip wire set to crash over the victim stood beneath. 'Ellie likes being on her back.... Well let's see how she likes it when the weight is a little more than Kannons, say 200 lbs more!' chuckled the sinister Asylum member as he hoisted the lighting back into position. Not contempt with that one mere prank, the Jester used an old time classic he unveiled during his days at NTICW involving electricity. Hooking a battery pack and crocodile clips to the metallic guardrail he created a potential death trap, concealing the power source under a wooden desk furnished by a vase and flower arrangement. 'After Kannon's shock showing at Fusion, nailing me with his Hallmark I'm going to go one better... Give his missus a new hairstyle free of charge, no dipping into the tax payers money this time!' hissed the irate comic basking in the knowledge that his nemesis would fall victim to this charade. 'GAZ - I'VE FINISHED WITH THE BED! NEXT TIME THEY DECIDE TO GET DOWN AND DIRTY THE THING WILL SPRING LIKE A VENUS FLY TRAP~!' screamed Nerezza from upstairs. 'A touch of 'Malcholysis' I think....' murmured the Jester, over heard by his comrade. 'What are you muttering about now?' Gaz ignoring the latter spoken words of his partner pulled from his bag of tricks a long white drape splashed with lettering and stamped with the Asylum's insignia. Looking at it with glee he reads the message out loud. 'KANNON'S BORDELLO OF SCIENTOLOGIST WHORES!!!' Rolling the fabric up he fixed it to the main wall that hadn't lay siege to Ellie's attempts of pranking and again laid a trip wire that would allow the message to descend before his very eyes. Unbeknownst to the Jester, Nerezza waltzed down the stairs in a cheery mood unaided by the banister but as he came to the bottom he placed a firm palm over the guardrail, electrifying himself in the process. 'GAZ - ARGH - I'M GOING TO - ARGH - KILL - YOU~!' cried the Angel Of Fear. Falling to a heap before the stairs Gaz offered his services of first aid, which only fuelled the vampire’s rage. 'Sorry - I can easily rectify the problem!' unveiling yet another power-pack and repeating the process once more as Nerezza tried to return to his vertical base in a sluggish manner. Nerezza literally crawling through what was the door left the Jester to re-attach the door with the aid of his trusty tool kit. Screwing the frame back to the hinges the place appeared as though nobody had bombarded it with pieces of misery but the team of Pranks With Attitude however knew otherwise. The scene drawing to a close as we await the aftermath....
