Dating Miss Willis: Confession

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Dating Miss Willis: Confession Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: May 27th, 2002 Sheer, utter terror. Reginald's done with Batman, The Ride. Although horrified, he learned something from it. Fear is easily conquered. When you're strapped in to a couple hundred tons of metal, it is nigh impossible to escape facing your fear. When one is forced to meet what one hates, loathes, or fears, one tends to pass the test with flying colors. Passing begets confidence. Reginald's confidence is at a new high now, as he walks with Katherine into a frontiersy part of Great Adventure, headed for an old west themed resturant for lunch. Reginald now has a new definition of extreme. Extreme is being willing to go to an amusement park that has a double digit amount of roller coasters with a twenty-three year old that has limitless amount of energy, Although, I'm sure Reginald is reminding himself that youthful energy is one of her positives.. Oh, come on. Certainly, Mr. Lovecraft is a gentleman, but even he must have thoughts turning to the lustful by now.. Katherine is indeed a good intern. But she is also a beautiful woman, who DOES, in fact, think of 'Reggie' in more than an employer-employee type of relationship.. As they order their meal, it would be evident to any of those in the restaurant that they are a quite unusual couple. However, they make each other happy. And, isn't that really what matters most? ::Sitting down to plates of barbecued chicken and corn, Katherine Willis and Reginald V. Lovecraft smile at each other. Their day has been an interesting one thus far, as they have chatted cordially about a wide variety of topics. Still, the more serious conversation is yet to come. Romantic talk has never been a staple of their friendship, although....it is about to be. Let the games begin...:: Katherine Willis: Soooo....have you ever taken a wife before? ::Lovecraft is taken aback by the question, but he does his best to stammer out an answer.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: No....not at all. Although, my family wanted me to at one point....it was even arranged. I was engaged to be married twenty five years ago on August 6. Katherine Willis: What was she like? Reginald V. Lovecraft: I'll be damned if I know...it was to be a marriage of convience. Back in my native Georgia, the Lovecrafts were a well known clan, especially in the 1970s. All I knew about her was her name. Jackie Hampton... The Hamptons were another well known family in my region at the time. We never met each other, except once, on the eve of our would be wedding. Katherine Willis: What happened? Reginald V. Lovecraft: We knew our hearts weren't in it, so we met, and agreed to leave that very night seperately...she headed off for the North, and I went down to Florida for a brief time. Katherine Willis: What did your family say? Reginald V. Lovecraft: They knew I had left, but they didn't know where to. Both of my parents died later that year...my mother of a heart attack, and my father of a broken heart. Katherine Willis: A broken heart? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Medically, a severe stroke...doctors say it was brought about by stress. My other relations told me how much he missed her.. Katherine Willis: You were able to patch things up with your family? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Mostly yes...many of my aunts and uncles understood...but still, the pain was there. Katherine Willis: So, you were never married...how many girlfriends have you had? ::Reginald blushes slightly, as he knows full well his answer is about to unleash a torrent of questions.: Reginald V. Lovecraft: None. ::A shocked look crosses Katherine's features.:: Katherine Willis: None? In forty-seven years? :Reginald nods: Reginald V. Lovecraft: I was sent off to the Prescott Academy for Boys for my education from kindergarten to twelfth grade. My parents then sent me to an all-male university, although its name escapes me at the moment...they did this so that they could have their arranged marriage with no impediments. I just haven't been around women much...there are far too few females in the political field. It is a field dominated by men, and as such, I haven't come across anyone. Except... :Katherine smiles, and offers a simple question.:: Katherine Willis: Except? :It is Reginald's turn to smile.: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Except you, Katherine.. :Happiness shines in the eyes of both, as they lean in and share an awkward kiss. It is almost as if both are thirteen again, as neither can stop their nervous giggling. It is Katherine who first speaks.:: Katherine Willis: So, you've never had a girlfriend before? :An elated Reginald speaks.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Never. Katherine Willis: Then, that means that I shall be in the driver's seat this evening.. Reginald V. Lovecraft: But Katherine, we never drive our own limousine! That's why we have a driver! ::Katherine realizes that, yes indeed, she is dealing with a quite innocent and sheltered man.:: Katherine Willis: Oh, never mind...never mind.. :Reginald shrugs his shoulders: Reginald V. Lovecraft: So, Katherine, which roller coaster are we off to tame next? I need more Extreme training! Katherine Willis: Actually, Reggie, I think we're done with extreme training here. Your training is off until tonight. Reginald V. Lovecraft: What happens tonight? ::Katherine smiles, as innocently as she possibly can.:: Katherine Willis: Oh...nothing... Reginald V. Lovecraft: Soo, back to Atlantic City then? Katherine Willis: Yes! After all, we're staying in the Emperor's Suite at Caesar's until tommorow morning. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Then, let us be off. Tonight, I will continue my training again...and with it, I shall become PCW's Extreme Aristocrat! ::The two leave arm in arm, as we fade away to...:: Paid For By The Friends Of Reginald V.
