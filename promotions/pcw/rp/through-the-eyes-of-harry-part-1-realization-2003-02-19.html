Through The Eyes Of Harry - Part 1 - Realization

19 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 19, 2003 ['Heavy Metal' Harry stands at the sliding glass door of the bodacious beach house that he and his brother habitat. The sun begins it's rise and it leaves a golden reflection on the far out ocean. That scene is completely ignored as Harry's senses are only drawn toward the large tanned man sticking a cross into the middle of his private beach. The man's blonde locks looking in need of some conditioner as sand is clumped in his hair. His silk blue shirt is torn at the seams, completely useless at this point. His bright blue eyes filled with sadness as he silently says one finally goodbye...to a statue] [Harry shakes his head not really sure why he's even watching on. Honestly, he feels twice as dumb for watching Tommy bury the statue than to be helping him. It's over with now and Tommy will probably forget about it in a few hours. Then he can go on talking about how he's going to destroy Rent-A-Hero, rename the Continental Title to the Tubular Title, and then find some stupid chick to go score with. Why are things suddenly so clear to Harry at this moment? It has everything to do with being sober. Harry hasn't smoked any weed in days, ever since the statue incident! He never realized it, but reconstructing the statue took his mind off of weed. He's completely looking at reality, and honestly, he DOESN'T like what he sees. What the hell happened to he and Tommy? They were adored and loved be everyone they came into contact with, and now they are receiving threatening letters...FROM CHILDREN! Harry looks down at the card he read only hours earlier, looking at the way Tommy's stick figure has a pool of blood underneath it, and it makes Harry's stomach feel a bit uneasy] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Har, I think we should have a priest here. The statue needs to go out the right way, dude. [Harry looks at his brother, half brother actually, with an emotionless stare. There are people who die everyday who never get to go out the right way and Tommy wants to give a statue a freakin' funeral? Who else is going to show up besides Tommy, Harry, and the priest? It's not like this naked lady statue came to life and started making friends. No other naked statues are going to show up. But...knowing Tommy...something like that could happen] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I'll see what I can do. [For the first time in days Tommy lets out a smile. It looks almost pathetic, really] TTT: Thanks, dude. [Tommy collapses in the doorway as the brisk California morning air blows into his face. His tanned face looks as if it's age ten years, which means that he's in desperate need of some sleep. Harry knows that if he suggests it, Tommy will only deny it. So, Harry decides not even to bother with it] Harry: You know you have to defend your Continental Title on Fusion. It's against Venneman Pendragon. [Tommy's face contorts into this odd confusion. It's not that he hasn't heard the name before, it's just the way that it sounds. Venneman Pendragon. Try to say THAT three times fast while breakdancing, Harry thinks. He can only giggle to himself at his thought as Tommy's confusion runs even deeper. Harry stifles his small laughter at the expense of his dim witted brother] TTT: Ven-a-Pen-a-whatta, dude? Harry: Venneman Pendragon. TTT: Ven... Harry: ...neman. TTT: Pend... Harry: ...ragon. [His face still wears that confusion as he looks back out to the beach. No doubt he's looking toward the grave site of his naked lady statue. He's probably trying to channel some kind of answer from it, hoping that it will give him the answer to all of his problems. He's ALWAYS been looking to something else for answers, never really trying to experience anything for himself. He just wants things right up and front, no trying. Harry can't really blame him for it, but sometimes it's better to be an independent man then to rely on others...especially objects that are buried in the sand] TTT: Why should I even be worried about some totally heinous dude like him, anyway? I can't even pronounce his name! Harry: Tommy, in his first match he won the Rising Star Title. Then a VERY short time later he's holding the Television title. He is a force to be reckoned with. [Tommy looks away from the grave site, and then looks at Harry. He stares at his shaggy looking stoner brother with a bit of suspicion] TTT: How come your not saying 'man' after everything that you say? [Harry seems a bit confused himself now. Does he REALLY say 'man' after everything that he says? He doesn't really ever recall doing anything of that nature. Well, he HAS been stoned for years upon years with only a few breaks in between. Who knows what he's said] Harry: I don't know... [Tommy's suspicious look deepens] Harry: ...man. [Tommy then shrugs it off, like he never even suspected it in the first place. Well, it's probably because he's too preoccupied at the moment. Harry knows that he's going to have to snap him out of this spell that he's under. Pendragon will take advantage of this and won't let Tommy get any kind of second chance to his head back into it. If Tommy keeps this process up he's going to end up losing that title that he wants to rename. That prescious PCW Continental Title that has catapulted his career into a completely different direction] Harry: Friday is only a few days away. We need to get down and dirty with training and getting to know this guy. This isn't some guy that you can just walk over and not give a second thought to. Pendragon is a CONTENDER! He's a vital threat to your title and your going to have to put that statue behind you for now... [Tommy gives Harry that suspicious look once again] Harry: ...man. TTT: Har, I've already said it...I can't even pronounce his NAME, dude! How bogus is that?! I'm not even going to worry about some heinous compadre like that. Lets face it...this funeral is a lot more important than getting ready for some heinous dude like that. [Harry can do nothing but shake his head. How did he even win that damned title in the first place? Somehow he overcame The End and then went on to win that ladder match, and only God knows how he managed to pull that off without making an ass of himself. Harry looks at his saddened face and does give Tommy credit for being a great wrestler. He took to it with such grace and speed. All of his trainers and peers were spellbound at the rate he was improving his skills every single day. Harry doesn't really remember much after that. Just some stuff from NTICW, mostly Tommy dressing up in a Panda suit, but everything else is pretty fuzzy] TTT: Weren't you supposed to be calling that priest, dude? [Harry nods and picks up the phone as he digs through the black book on the dresser. Papers scatter all over the hardwood floor as Harry does his best to make numbers out in the mess the book presents. All that Harry can find are numbers for 'bodacious babe's' and 'excellent honey's'. He doesn't find any sort of number for a priest or anybody who can give Tommy some kind of goodbye ceremony for the statue. It's just numbers for women that Tommy banged since he had the capability to] Harry: I can't find anything for a priest....man. TTT: Dude, look under the letter 'P'. [Harry decides to give it another shot, knowing full well that there isn't going to be a number there that says 'Priest'. That reminds Harry, he needs to reorganize his Judas Priest collection] Harry: All that I see is Priscella, Pamela, Patricia, and Poontang Sally. [A very bright smile flashes over his face as he is taken back to the time he met Sally] TTT: I haven't seen her since I was fifteen, dude! Harry: Me either...uh...man. TTT: Wasn't she the chick with the big butt? Harry: Yeah, the one that you gooed on. TTT: Well, of course, dude!!! Harry: NO!! Not THAT kind of goo. The kind where you literally put goo on her! [Tommy tries to take himself back to his early teenage days. He doesn't recall putting goo on Sally, well, HIS goo, but not some goo out of a can or anything. Harry puts the book back on the coffee table, placing the phone back into it's cradle. The operator was getting annoying with that 'Hang up and try again' crap] Harry: We'll just pray for your statue later today or something. [Tommy shrugs and turns his head back out to the grave site, but something is different. WHERE DID THOSE DOGS COME FROM?!] TTT: GET AWAY FROM THERE YOU BOGUS MUTTS!! [Tommy shakes his hand in the air and takes off after the dogs. They immediately burst into a full sprint as Tommy dives on the grave site. He pushes the sand back over top as his body shakes with anger and sadness. It's pathetic to see what Tommy has become out of the fear that is inside of him. He's afraid to face what he's become, what he's done to all of the people around him. Now he's just falling deeper and deeper into himself. It's so sad to watch him go this far, to mourn over a statue that he only got a month ago. He better snap out of his spell, and he better do it fast, because Pendragon is waiting in the wings. He'll swoop down and take the only thing that is keeping Tommy from completely cracking...the PCW Continental Title. May God have mercy and let Tommy somehow pull the win out]
