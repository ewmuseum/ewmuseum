Recipe for Vengeance

7 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7 August 2002 Recipe for Vengeance: Take one egg and one healthy amount of cream and mix them together vibrantly. Now that the fun part’s over, allow ingredients to sit inside the oven for a while (several months always produces the best results). When it starts to rise, remove from oven. Let it fester for some years, ripening to aged perfection, then dish out. Serves two…and two only… -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The sky cleared up that morning…the night’s rain passing away like the rest of those sorry jobbers that got their faces bashed in last week. Sun rays beamed down upon the shores of Haiti, making the island look like a paradise. But that place was about to become a battlefield…in due time… A black speedboat shot out into the waters like a shark. At the helm was the Divine Prophetess, taking the wheel like a pro. The waters spayed up into their faces as she took a sharp curve. At the front of the boat stood Akiyama. His arms were crossed over his chest, his sunglasses over his face hiding an ominous look of hatred. The braided black and silver ponytail fluttered in the wind. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “You really think that they’ll eliminate Fantasy for you?” Equinox stood within the shack peering out at the island that night. Back in the middle of the room the woman known as Glacia stood in the center of the room. The light from the lantern hanging above fell upon her ice blue appearance, shimmering the entire room in a light blue hue. She ran her hands up the side of her ice blue leather pants, slowing her pace around her hips and up the side of her plastic halter top. It was like she enjoyed the touch of her own hands… “He will…” she replied in a soft whisper. “I know Akira…he’s led by honor. Even when he was a part of Maxis, Inc, he was known as the honest Yakuza hitman. He’s responsible for how Fantasy is now…and he knows that it’s his responsibility to make sure he finishes the job.” Equinox shrugged at her explanation. “I don’t know. He acts differently than how you described him. And then there’s the factor of that woman with him. She may be onto us.” She chuckled a bit at him, drawing up the hairs on the back of his neck. “You worry…too much. Akira will finish Fantasy off for us…but not before the plan is complete.” She glided over to him and wrapped her arms around his neck. She smiled a smile of wickedness as she peered out at that island. “Indeed…all is according to plan.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The boat was reaching closer and closer to their destination. The eyes of the Divine Prophetess were sharp underneath those ice blue glasses… -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “I hope you know that this is about more than just eliminating another link to your past.” Cyclonica lounged across the plush sofa in the sitting room of the hotel room PCW had furnished for them. Standing in the middle of the room behind the sofa was the nVg, gearing himself up for battle. He glared up at her as he tightened the silver buckles on his boots. She countered his glare with a glower and continued. “Don’t you give me that look. You know just as well as I know that no one comes all this way just to have you stop some flat-scan mortal from stalking them. If they really wanted this man dead that much, they would have gunned him down themselves. If they have the resources to come out there, they have the resources to pop a cap in that peon!” Akiyama turned his eyes away from her to tighten the black tape around his forearms. Cyclonica sat up and propped her elbows on the back of the sofa. “Those tattoos that you cover up with that tape…they’re not just a reminder of your past. They’re not a tapestry of future events. Just like a well-scripted recipe for disaster. And speaking of which… Chef is about to get a hearty portion of pain real soon!” A growl came up from the god as he measured out the exact amount of tape to apply over the first layer of black tape on his right arm. She sighed as she leaned back against the arm of the sofa. “You know very well that once is forgivable, but twice is punishable! We already showed our complete domination of this recipe renegade when he was one of the Allah Boys with the Neo Void B!tch Alexis Parshin! And now he wants to step back against the nVg after we took his tag titles, and tossed him into a pile of steel like trash? Dear god…the time is upon us.” The Neo Void God gave a small nod as he tested the durability of the tape on his hands. Then with a crack of his knuckles, he powered his fist out like a piston, the mere velocity of the HAND OF FATE sending currents of wind forward and shaking the items on the wall and tables. Cyclonica yawned, stretching herself out. Then she stood up and slipped on her sunglasses. She reached into her other pocket and handed the nVg his black shades. “Might as well get a spot of training in before having to pummel another mortal…shall we?” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The shore looked bare…not a sign of life anywhere. Akiyama kept his eyes focused on the island for any sign of movement as the boat rocked up and down against the surf. Things were getting quite intense at this moment…as they were almost upon the island’s coast. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Glacia, Equinox, Cyclonica, and Akiyama all stood upon a rickety peer looking down upon a black speed boat. Equinox jumped down on the machine and pointed out various items to the Divine Prophetess. “This is the wheel…you turn with this.” He informed her. “This is the throttle…and this is the engine. Are you even listening to me?” All he got from her was a roll of her eyes in reply. “Your associate must learn to be more cooperative if she is to go along with you, Akira.” Cyclonica glared back and saw Glacia run her finger along the broad chin of the god. She hissed in their direction, drawing a small glance from the icy vixen. “You are to refer to him as your Neo Void God, human.” First strike against Glacia. “Second, I am aware of how to drive a speed boat. I know a lot of things you do not. Don’t let my appearance fool you.” Glacia smiled. “I wouldn’t dream of it, young lady.” “DIVINE…PROPHETESS…CYCLONICA.” Teeth gritted at the blatant disrespect of her title. Glacia seemed to pay no attention to her as she turned back to Akiyama,. “You must deal with this part of your past before you can move on as you desire.” She said to him softly. “I wish you luck.” Cyclonica’s eyes narrowed as she jumped into the cockpit of the boat. Equinox climbed back out and watched as the nineteen-ish looking woman revved the engine to life. Then she cut her eyes back at the rest of them…including the nVg. “Let’s finish this before I get violent.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The boat ran up along the edge of the beach to a halt. Off stepped the nVg, with Cyclonica following closely behind. Everything was silent at that point…no wind, no birds, no surf, nothing. Akiyama’s eyes scanned around the tropical foliage for any sign of their target…his former friend Fantasy. He stood there, with his eyes locked in a single direction trying to listen for life. Cyclonica followed his eyes in that direction. Eyes focused on the duo from the treetops. They were locked dead in the direction the eyes were looking from, but had they spotted the person? If they had spotted the watcher, they made no notice. It was a momentary staring contest between the observer and the nVg and the Divine Prophetess. Then as the god moved from his spot, the watcher dashed off into the brush. Cyclonica grew close to Akiyama, her arm pressing against the back of his arm. “I can feel him…his eyes…his presence…he’s close.” As soon as she said that, she dived towards the edge of the water as the god whirled around. He took a huge swing at a colorful figure that had appeared out of nowhere in the air. The figure did a backbend in mid air and evaded the attack with ease. Then he landed in behind the god in a slouched position, his hands touching the ground and his entire body hunched over. He appeared to be dressed in a pair of ragged blue jeans and a torn red tank top, his entire body torn with scars and some carvings looked to have been made by hand. Akiyama turned around and glowered at the man. Cyclonica watched as the man slowly looked up from amidst the mass of ruby red hair. His voice spoke from deep within him…like a clear voice of a child whose innocence was stolen from him. “Akiyama…Akira?” The arms of the nVg were lowered a bit, looking upon the man that called out his mortal name. Cyclonica stepped forward and called out to him. “You are the one called Fantasy I presume!” she made it clear she got his attention, calling the name out. “You have two options: one is to go off and live your life and forget you ever knew Akiyama existed, or the Neo Void God can personally beat the life out of you and make sure no one ever knew YOU existed! What will it be flat-scan?” She expected him to refuse to give in…most mortals didn’t give in when the odds were obviously against them. They had something called…pride, willpower, determination… the Divine Prophetess just called it stupidity, foolishness, and ignorance. Everyone stepping against the god fell in those three categories…just because they stepped into the ring with him. But she didn’t expect to hear what he said to her. “So…you’ve got some nerve to show your face around me…” “Pardon?” she responded, an ounce of curiosity in her voice. “The both of you…have nerve to stand before me like you have no guilt of your sin. After what you did to me all those years…” his voice started to waver as he pointed towards Cyclonica. “You…you were my wife…but you became his whore…” his green fingernail turned to Akiyama next. “And you were my friend…and you turned into a fiend! If you’ve come here for forgiveness…then you’ve wasted your times!! But…” He drew up to his full height, nearing about 6’4 and looking to be 250 pounds of muscle. He tossed his hair out of his face, showing a handsome, elfish man with red stubble over his chin and piercing emerald green eyes. “…If you’ve come here to DIE…then I WONT DISSAPPOINT YOU OLD FRIENDS!!! HYAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAH!!!!” He let out a huge yell before pouncing at the god with full force. Akiyama stumbled back as the man tried to tackle him to the ground. Then he reached underneath him and heaved the man into the air. Fantasy came falling back face down, but he rotated backwards and managed to nail a falling dropkick to the face of the god. He nipped back up to is feet to find Akiyama a few paces away, shaking his face. Then he ran towards him and dug his feet into the abs of Akiyama, using them as a springboard to hit a moonsault double kick right under the chin of the nVg!! But still, Akiyama only stumbled backwards and shook the pain off! Fantasy landed on his feet in front of the god, insanity pumping through his mind just as fast as the adrenaline pumping through his system. With another crazed yell, he ran towards the nVg with amazing force. Akiyama took a huge swing at him, and almost knocked his head off with a Clothesline from the Void. Cyclonica watched with intrigued eyes as Fantasy came falling back into his crouched position after being knocked clean off his feet by the stiff lariat. The god let out a snarl as he cracked his knuckles and raised his fists in a martial arts stance. Now when’s the last time you’ve seen Akiyama go into a martial arts stance? It was THAT serious, folks. Once again Fantasy took a run at the Neo Void God, this time with fists balled ready to strike. He intercepted the oncoming attacker, and the two of them broke out into a huge frenzy of fisticuffs. They each traded lefts and rights with each other while circling each other on the beach, a waltz of walloping it could be deemed. Cyclonica watched as the man took blow after blow from the god, and with each discoloration that appeared on his skin from the punches, he still kept going on as if he felt nothing. “Insanity is the key to invincibility…” she sighed under her breath. “And so we shall lose our minds to become omnipotent.” Akiyama spun at him with another huge lariat, but Fantasy ducked underneath and came up with a hard right uppercut to the god’s jaw. Akiyama countered with a spinning elbow to the sternum before dashing around him, locking his arms in a full nelson, and crushing his head into the sand with a spike DDT. The multi-colored man pushed himself out of the ground like it was nothing and ran at Akiyama once again! He leaped into the air with a jumping tackle, but the nVg ducked underneath. Fantasy landed on his feet, but as he turned around, he got up close and personal with the running boot of the god! He smacked back into the ground, but after a few seconds, he kipped back up and head-butted Akiyama! He shook it off and let out a roar as he gored him into the mat! When he got back up, he turned to place a sound stomp in the man’s chest. Fantasy was instead up across from him, and running at him once again! Akiyama drew his arm back and prepared for the Hand of Fate. Just then…Cyclonica stepped into the fray. “Fantasy! Stop!” The ruby haired man skidded to a halt and glared into the Divine Prophetess. She looked at him with a strange look…an affectionate look. She stepped slowly towards him, her arms up in peace. “I’m sorry…I never meant to hurt you.” She said assuring. Her voice seemed tender, not like any other time when she’s emotionless. “I should have never betrayed your trust. I was stupid…foolish…and a jerk…and I apologize.” Fantasy’s eyebrow cocked up at her words. His battered face looked up at her with anger at first, but it started to melt away like butter upon the hot griddle. “I…I don’t know…” Cyclonica held her arms out for him to hug her. “Please…can you ever forgive me for all these years of torment?” He looked confused, looking back at the god poised to strike. But then his eyes turned back to Cyclonica…and they grew warm. He slinked slowly out of his hunched position to stand normally. And slowly…slowly…he took a step towards her. She smiled…something rare from her. “That’s it…you know I always loved you…” Slowly but surely, Fantasy entered her embrace. The two stood there and hugged each other, Fantasy clutching Cyclonica tightly, and the Divine Prophetess shushing him calmly and rubbing the back of his head. He closed his eyes and allowed her to rock him into comfort. They rotated in each others arms, the feeling like the both of them floating in each other’s warmth. His eyes slowly fell closed, and Cyclonica looked up at the god…her smile fading and her eyes growing cold. Then as a huge smack ripped through the air, Fantasy’s eyes snapped open, his mouth dropping open and slack. His arms fell from around Cyclonica… The Neo Void God’s fist was lodged into the base of Fantasy’s head, having struck with Fate’s Hand. Cyclonica removed her arms from around him, allowing him to fall down to his knees. Then she locked her ice blue talons into his throat and watched his mouth drop open for breath. “You are a fool…” she told him as if he should have known that by now. “…And now your life will be sacrifice for the god.” Crimson poured from the incisions where her nails pierced his skin. She looked up at Akiyama and saw that he had turned his back on the scene playing before her. This man was his friend and partner in crime…but now he would be dead…another foolish person that got in the nVg’s way because they knew no better. El Diablo…Chef…Kannon…Fantasy… Just then, her jaw goes slack in shock. The hand of Fantasy gripped into her throat tightly, causing her to release hers a bit. He slowly began to stand and stare his angry emerald eyes into her. “Again…you…BETRAY ME??!?! GLACIA, I’LL KILL YOU WITH MY LOVE!!!” Fantasy’s other hand came back to strike at her, but suddenly he found himself hoisted into the air. Akiyama held him on his shoulders facing the sky and roared as he spiked him down on a fallen log head first!! His entire body flopped off the tree and crashed face first into the wet sand. Cyclonica looked over towards the still Fantasy her hand tending to her throat. She looked as the god walked over towards him and rolled him over. Fantasy laid there, his eyes half closed and his entire body a solid bruise. “Why…” he moaned, pain creeping into his voice. “We were friends…” Akiyama opened his mouth, as if he were about to respond. But before he could answer, a shot cracked through the air…and a hole ripped open in Fantasy’s chest. The god turned back with fury blazed in his eyes. Standing at the edge of the tropical wilderness with a loaded pistol in hand…was Glacia. Equinox stood by her side like a devoted bodyguard as she glared into Fantasy and Akiyama with coldness… Cyclonica stood up with her eyes locked on the ice blue haired woman. “What is the meaning of this, woman??” “There are meanings that even you cannot understand yet, young lady!” she hissed in her direction, with her eyes and gun still locked on the god and Fantasy. “This is about more than a little adultery! Much more…” Akiyama stood up defiantly before her, his body in between Fantasy and her aim. Glacia raised her gun for his head, her eyes never wavering from aim. “As a matter of fact, adultery lead to this entire thing…while I have no problem with it, the repercussions of it is what brought us all here today!” “What are you talking about??” patience was a virtue…one that became absent when the Divine Prophetess was edgy. “What I’m saying is that one of these two made me PREGNANT!!” Silence blew through them all like a chilling wind… Akiyama and Cyclonica stared at Glacia with blank looks, where she glared back with jaded anger. “All this is because one of them knocked you up?” Cyclonica asked in annoyed disbelief. “Where is the kid now?” Glacia shrugged, “Dead most likely…who knows.” “So you planned to kill both Akiyama and Fantasy because they got you pregnant? What kind of motivation is that?” “Because!! It’s a violation of my own body!! You have a child and tell me how you feel afterwards!!” she was ready to snap, but her voice remained chilled, ethereal. Cyclonica knew there was something more to this than what she was saying…her logic wasn’t complying. “I advise that you lower your weapon or things will end up complicated.” “Kiss my ass.” And with that blunt order given, she fired on the god. Metal hit metal, and the god remained vertical. Glacia looked in bewilderment as his forearm was held before his face. The black tape covering his arms was slashed open from the bullet, exposing the alloy texture of the Void Armor. The gauntlets were wrapped under that tape… She aimed again to fire, but Cyclonica sprinted in with a kick, sending the pistol flying. Then she dropped her back onto her behind with a roundhouse snap kick to the side of her head. Equinox came towards her to counter, but Cyclonica stopped him in his tracks with a kick to his throat, stopping the silver spiked stiletto heel a hair away from his Adam’s apple. “Take that ice b!tch and take off…lest you two take a honeymoon in the Void.” Equinox responded with a wary glare as he helped Glacia to her feet. They backed into the jungles and disappeared, in which Cyclonica lowered her leg and turned to Akiyama. He was looking down at the man called Fantasy with not angry eyes…but melancholy eyes. The Divine Prophetess shook her head at the still man in the sand…a valiant battler struck down in the midst of combat… “We’re through here…” was all she said… The two of them climbed into the black speed boat and sped off, neither of them not really content with the outcome that just unfolded before them…like putting a load of effort into baking a cake only to find it fallen in the oven. There were also a lot of things to think about now that Glacia made that statement…that she was pregnant with either Akiyama or Fantasy’s child… it left a residue in their minds like a bad taste in their mouths. But for now, they wouldn’t let that bother them…Chef needed to be taken care of…along with El Diablo and Xavier Kannon. They will learn that the Neo Void God and the Divine Prophetess are nothing to toy around with…their recipes for success…will cook up nothing but trouble. Meanwhile… A bloody hand held a bullet in hand, tweezed in between two fingers. Two green nailed fingers… “She…was pregnant?” Fantasy sat hunched over in the sand, his head lowered and his hair hanging in his face. The silence of the island was broken as a mad laughter echoed through the air.
