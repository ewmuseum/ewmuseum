With any luck I’ll never have to come to Roswell again (Part 3)

28 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th of July, 2002 Mere hours remain until The Pharaoh Titu Xansiv finally gets his shot not only at Xavier Kannon, his greatest foe, but the PCW World heavyweight championship too. Last Tuesday on PCW Turmoil, Titu managed to defeat Big Alan Steele and Frisco in a three-way dance match. If this is any indication of what lies in store later on tonight at Wrestle Vessel then one can only explore the possibilities of a big Pharaoh win…But Pharaoh STILL has to get out of Roswell…and apparently Yoda knows the way… As time draws on towards Wrestle Vessel The Pharaoh is found with his newly found friend, Yoda, in the back alleys of Roswell. Yoda says he knows someone who can help the pair to get out of Roswell AND, as an added bonus, make them something good to eat… The only problem is, the man that Yoda speaks off is in fact the father of Titu Xansiv! As we saw last time in the ending minutes of the gripping second part of our mini-saga, Yoda announced that he was hungry and knew where he and Pharaoh could stay and eat. Pharaoh inquired to whom Yoda spoke of and, as bluntly as an overused knife, the creature replied ‘He’s your father’… WHAT LIES IN STORE AT PHARAOH’S DAD’S HOUSE?! WILL YODA GET SOMETHING TO EAT, AND WILL IT BE GOOD ENOUGH FOR A GRUMPLY OLD JEDI LIKE HIM?! WHAT REACTION WILL PHARAOH HAVE TO MEETING HIS DAD AT LAST…AND MORE IMPORTANTLY, WILL PHARAOH EVER TRUST YODA TO NOT PLANT BRIGHT PINK THONGS ON HIM AGAIN?! Find out RIGHT NOW in the final part of our tale from Roswell…ENJOY! Yoda led the way. Pharaoh had noticed that the little green alien, who starred in such great films as ‘The Empire Strikes Back’ and ‘The Return Of The Jedi’ seemed to know his way through the alley’s and slimy caverns of Roswell’s city like the back of his little chubby green paw. It was quite unusual really, but it was fine by him, for you see, if Yoda HADN’T known the way then they would have surely been lost by now and not so long away from been found by the strange city folk. Yoda: “Almost there, we are!” The Pharaoh: “And you’re sure he’s my dad?” Yoda had claimed that the man they were on their way to see was indeed his father. But how could that be? Or more appropriately, how could his dad possibly KNOW Yoda?! It was preposterous! PREPOSTEROUS I SAY! Yoda turned to him, his beady little eyes looked blankly up towards his. Yoda: “Very sure, I am!” The Pharaoh: (Sighing) “And what am I suppose to say to him when I see him, huh? I haven’t spoken, seen, or even thought of him in ages. I’d be surprised if he EVEN recognized me…” Yoda looked the young Egyptian up and down, stroking his chin in deep thought. Yoda: “Wearing that loincloth was probably the wrong choice of dress for a moment like this…” Pharaoh sighed. The Pharaoh: (Rolling eyes angrily) “Cummon, the sooner we get there the sooner I can say hi to him the sooner I can leave Roswell and get to the Wrestle Vessel!” Yoda tuned on his green heel and hobbled his way down the alleyway. The Pharaoh: “So, like…back there…I saw you doing all that martial arts stuff with your sword, right? So how come you walk with a walking stick? Yoda spoke as he walked… Yoda: “My back isn’t what it once was, young Titu…” The Pharaoh: (Frowning) “Yeah, but I saw you flying off the walls and doing flips and you wielded that thingy…light-sabre? With excellent--…” Yoda: “…--Do not question me…” Pharaoh sighed. The Pharaoh: “I aren’t gonna’ get a straight answer, am I?” Yoda didn’t answer, he simply continued walking. The Pharaoh: “Fine, then!” The couple speedily walked on, guided by the mind of the old Jedi Master. At length they came to an old building. From the outside it looked derelict, but when one was inside you saw things in a very different light. The house, as you may have already guessed, belonged to Xansiv’s father. But NOW we’re rushing forth into the story…so lets calm down a bit…and get back to normal… Yoda knocked upon the old wooden entrance with the tip of his stick and patiently waited for a reply. The Pharaoh: “Is this where he lives?” Yoda: “Indeed, it is…” After a moment or two a small wooden slot opened up and a pair of dark brown eyes peered out. Voice: “Who goes there?” Yoda: “It’s me, Mr Xansiv…” A brief pause followed in which time the eyes seemed to focus upon the tiny green shape standing on the other side of the door… Voice of Mr Xansiv: “Is that you, Yoda?” Yoda: (Sighing) “Look at me will you! Who else around here looks like this? Of course it’s me, Mr Xansiv!” The eyes looked upwards to view the face of Titu. Voice of Mr Xansiv: “O…k…and who’s that?” Yoda: (Tenderly) “Come as a surprise, this may. But that is your son…” There was silence and then the sound of a bolt been slid out of its lock echoed from within the house. Pharaoh gulped as the door opened and out walked a man in his late fifties, dark skinned and stern in expression…just like his son. The Pharaoh: “…Dad?” Titu smiled but his grin was quickly wiped off as he was met with a hard right hook to the jaw from his dad… The last thing he remembered were the words: “Where the heck have you BEEN all this time?!” He remember the sound of shuffling feet approaching him… “Knocked him out, I think you have…” said the voice of Yoda… “Ah cummon! I didn’t hit him THAT hard…” And that was all he could recall… Pharaoh woke back up in a tall room. It appeared to be some sort of bedroom but it resembled a lounge more than anything. He moved around and found that he laid in the confined of a massive bed. Could THIS actually be the inside of the derelict house he’d stood outside earlier…before his ‘dad’ punched him in the jaw… The Pharaoh: (Rubbing his jaw) “What in the unholy name of Osiris did he do THAT for?!” He stood up and walked towards a door, carved delicately from mahogany. He turned the golden handle clockwise and the huge door opened out onto a long, winding corridor at the end of which, behind another door, came voices… Of course he recognized them immediately. One was Yoda’s and the other belonged to his father. Voice of Mr Xansiv: “A way out, you say. Hmmm… And without the people seeing you too…” Voice of Yoda: “Right you are, my friend.” There was silence for a while and then: Voice of Mr Xansiv: “Hmm… Well there IS the old ‘SWITCHEROO’ trick…” Voice of Yoda: “Oh? And what’s that, then? A shuffle of paper followed… Voice of Mr Xansiv: “What we do is this…Oh by the way, before I forget, how’s your chicken?” Voice of Yoda: (Biting into his marinated chicken leg) “The sauce is strong with this one…” Voice of Mr Xansiv: “Figures. I added a smidgen of red pepper juice to the concoction for that batch… Just then the door swung open and in walked The Pharaoh… The Pharaoh: “What did you hit me for?!” Mr Xansiv looked up from the plans in his hands. Mr Xansiv: “Son, can’t you see that we’re discussing important matters here?” The Pharaoh: Dad--…” Mr Xansiv: “Charles! Don’t backchat your dad!” Pharaoh sagged a little and then, realizing that he too needed to get out of Roswell, straightened up and said: The Pharaoh: “LOOK! I need to get out of here, too, ya’ know! I have to be--…” He looked down at the watch upon his wrist. The Pharaoh: “ …---on the Wrestle Vessel Cruise Shit in twelve frickin’ hours! And I’m still here in bloody Roswell!” Pharaoh’s dad looked towards Yoda who nodded – his eyes closed, as though meditating...well at least enjoying the marinated chicken leg, anyway… Mr Xansiv: “What do you need to be there for?” The Pharaoh: “Dad, I’m a wrestler now…” Mr Xansiv: (Shocked) “You’re a WHAT?! Last time I saw you, son, you were a damned archaeologist! What the hell happened to you?!” Pharaoh’s head drooped. The Pharaoh: “I wish not to speak of it, father.” Pharaoh’s dad sighed. Mr Xansiv: “Very well…In any case, I suggest you come over here and look over the plan with us…” The Pharaoh: “Well as long as it doesn’t involve bright pink thongs I think I’ll be OK with it, dad…” Yoda frowned. Yoda: “Ah dammit! I guess we’ll have to take THAT bit out of the plan…” Titu sighed. The Pharaoh: “You weren’t actually going to use a bright pink thong in the plan, were you?” Yoda blushed. Yoda: “Onward, we must go…” It took then an hour or so until they finally came up with a plan that fitted the expectations of everyone in the three-person circle. As they walked towards the garage of the massive house Yoda looked on and said the wise words… Yoda: “Time is a virtue, we must spend it wisely.” And thus the others looked at the wise little man and said: The Pharaoh and Mr Xansiv: “WHAT?!” Yoda: (Looking up at their puzzled faces) “Bah! It matters not…” Once the three men were in the car Mr Xansiv unzipped the bag he’d brought with him – the one this writer meant to mention earlier but forgot – and showed the others it’s extremely, enormously, overrated and far more importantly, its mysterious inside. Mr Xansiv: “This, my friends, is what we’re going to use to get past those insane Roswell natives!” Yoda: “The plan will be a success, I can see it now…” The Pharaoh: “The distraction will be amazing…AND it doesn’t involve thongs…” Yoda: “Look, shut up about the thongs, will you?!” The Pharaoh: “OK, Yoda… I’ll forgive you for that incident…happy?” Yoda: “Yes…” Mr Xansiv looked at the other two with puzzled eyes. Mr Xansiv: “What are you two talking about?” Yoda and The Pharaoh: “NOTHING!!” The three men, err… the TWO men and a … what the hell is Yoda anyway? Does anyone know? … Nobody cares? Oh OK…well anyway the TRIO sat ready in the car as Mr Xansiv – who’s real name is, for the records, Gregory Xansiv (This was explained in part 1, but I know what people are like) – turned the key in the ignition and started up the car’s engine. Moments later the vehicle was driving through the town square in Roswell. To Pharaoh’s utter surprise his fans were ACTUALLY still waiting patiently for Titu to return with Master Yoda. The Pharaoh: “Good Gods! They’re still waiting for me?!” Mr Xansiv: “Son, they won’t be waiting for you soon…AND anyway we wanted them to be, here right?” The Pharaoh: “Dad, people could get hurt in this plan, I think we’d have rather NOT had them waiting around to be blown up…ya’ know?” Mr Xansiv: “Meh!” Yoda looked out of one of the car windows and smiled broodingly. Yoda: “Die, this vermin must!” As the car rolled on through the square the fans began to take notice of Pharaoh’s presence and flocked, almost hypnotically, towards the vehicle. Yoda: “Seen you, they have, Titu.” Pharaoh gulped as his dad turned to him, gave him a brave wink and uttered: Mr Xansiv: “Now lets blow this thing and go home, kid!” Yoda frowned. Yoda: “Blatant, that was!” Gregory looked around to the back seat. Mr Xansiv: “What was?!” The Pharaoh: “Look we don’t have time for this!” Mr Xansiv nodded in agreement. Mr Xansiv: “Titu’s right! We’re gonna have to make this short, sweet, and snappy!” Pharaoh reached towards the unzipped back on the back seat next to Yoda and reached into its mysteriously complex recesses. His arm remerges holding what appears to be a soft plush doll resembling Yoda. Mr Xansiv: “Its all yours, kid!” Pharaoh unwound the window and tossed the doll out of it. Immediately a cheer went up in the crowd, as they believed it to be the real Yoda. Yoda: “NOW GO, GO, GO!” Gregory put his foot down on the pedal propelling the vehicle at blinding speed off towards the desert area of Roswell. In the background a HUGE explosion went up filling the air with a mini version of a mushroom cloud! Yoda: (gravely) “I sense that at least twenty souls were just silenced… (Lightening up) Good work, team!” The Pharaoh: “Now we have to get out of here. I can’t afford a court case! So I advise we go before the cops get here!” Mr Xansiv: “Err, so where to now, then?” The Pharaoh: “Tampa, dad, I’ve got a title to win…” And so ends our tale from Roswell. And though it was a shitty ending to such a ‘great’ story I can only say that it was an ending at least…yeah I know…it WAS still a shitty one, but I’m sticking to my story…it was an ending nevertheless…and any old ending will do, dammit! So good night, folks, and PLEASE don’t bug me about the quality of my work, OK?! THE END…
