Medieval Times....Part 3 of ?

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-25-2002 (We find ourselves once again drawn into the epic world our hero, Venneman Pendragon, calls home. Tonight, it looks like we are passing into Atlanta, Georgia...The green welcome sign on the right tells us so. As we progress further into the city, we come face to face with gigantic buildings. The car that holds the camera weaves through the streets before coming to a halt across from what would appear to be a castle...A huge sign out front proclaims it to be 'Medieval Times', a famous and fun restaurant chain that boasts live jousting tournaments. It appears to be opening night, because there is a red carpet laid out and different characters decked out in armor stride this way and that, entertaining some of the VIP guests that are on hand. The camera then pans to the left, and we see Venneman Pendragon, riding towards the castle on his war horse Xerxes. Behind him, on two donkeys, ride his Minstrel, Edward, and his Shouter, Benjamin...What they are doing we have no idea, but the donkeys are a nice touch. For the affair, Pendragon wears black leather pants, high black leather fold down boots, and his golden chain mail shirt. As usual, he has Excalibur sheathed at his left hip, and he wears the PCW Rising Star Title around his waist. He walks Xerxes right up behind a line of limousines; looks like he is gonna take advantage of the establishment's valet parking service. He watches as celebrities such as Bruce Willis, Michael Jordan and even Arnold Schwarzeneggar step from limos and enter the restaurant amidst the flashes of cameras. Finally, the line moves on and Pendragon with his entourage pulls his horse up to the carpet. He hops gracefully from the saddle and hands the reins to a flabbergasted Valet. Edward and Benjamin follow suit with their donkeys, and the three walk down the red carpet. Pendragon shakes some hands as Edward strikes up a lively tune...) Edward: Lord Pendragon of the might and truth...Nary in him a bone that's uncouth...Champion of all he sees! And the ladies he will please....LORD PENDRAGON!!! HE IS LORD PENDRAGON!!! (Edward cuts off with a look from Venneman, who is one to usually keep things in a respectful manner; no perverse speech. A small boy tugs at Pendragon's shirt and Venneman bends down and picks him up. Always a camera hound, Pendragon smiles broadly as the flashes go off and catch this touching moment. He then puts the boy down and continues into the restaurant. Edward and Benjamin follow, and soon.... ******FLASH******* The shot is now inside the restaurant, whose dining area is situated around a large jousting field; a long straightaway split by a fence. In the front row sits Pendragon and his retainers, Edward and Benjamin. A young lady also shares his table. Blonde and quite pretty, she is new to us. Let's eavesdrop...) Lady: Why, yes, I do enjoy my work for KWSH TV in Atlanta.... Edward: Penny Johnson of the TV fame...Don't you know she's quite a dame!!! Penn... (I guess we know who she is now...) Venneman: That will be enough, Edward... Penny: Does he always do this? Venneman: Song is the only means by which he communicates...He is a minstrel. Penny: I see...And my notes say that you are a...Let me see...Knight King? Venneman: Correct. Penny: I see why you picked this place...Anyhow, my network sent me down here, because a large number of our viewers here really enjoy your, um, sport, and wish to know what you think about a few certain...Things. Venneman: Ask me anything, Madam...I am an open book. (The food arrives as Venneman finishes, four orders of the leg of lamb; and this is real stuff...No fancy silverware and what not. It is to be eaten the way it was intended; with the fingers...) Penny: First off, when you have a match in Jamaica on Sunday, what are you doing in Atlanta on Thursday? Venneman: Well, I actually have a match in Florida tomorrow, and I am making my way South to do battle there. As to why I am in Atlanta, I do enjoy the fare in these restaurants. Reminds me of my own home. Excellent atmosphere. Besides, after my last couple of days, I needed to be able to relax a little. Penny: Humor me here...What kind of 'battles' do you have coming up? (Venneman smiles as he speaks, because next to winning, speaking about himself is the next best thing in his mind...) Venneman: First, I go to do battle with a Goblin. He is a vile creature who must be vanquished from the innocent world of America. I fight him alone on Friday. After that, it is time to get on the boat. My battle Sunday is on PPV, available from your local cable company, call for details on Wrestle Vessel. Shameless plug notwithstanding, it will be an epic match. I wrestle six other competitors for MY title...Seems kinda odd that I have to defend my gold against so many, but that is the way it is. Penny: And these men...Who are they? Venneman: One, actually, is a wench...No offense intended, Penny...The other five are Syko the Goblin, Scorpion, Ricky Thunder, Kris Burden and Trigger, the previous title holder. Penny: The Goblin again, huh? Venneman: Exactly. But names are unimportant. What is important is this: I am the champion. I am the Lord King. What is mine, shall stay mine. What belongs to others, will become mine. Those who stand against me in battle will fall. It is inevitable. The deciding factor in the match up is me. Who can stand against my wrath? Penny: Oh come on, Venneman...Tell me what you REALLY think about yourself. I only hope that boat is big enough for your ego! (Pendragon's eyes sparkle insanely and he begins to laugh, as he is often prone to do. Penny looks worried but Benjamin and Edward have seen it before. As soon as he started, he abruptly stops....) Venneman: Try the lamb, Miss Johnson...It is superb. (As he takes a bite of his lamb, the arena spotlight seeks him out. The restaurant announcer then gets on the PA system...) Announcer: We truly have a treat for you tonight...Descended from Arthur, master of the Sword and the Lance, PCW's own, VENNEMAN PENDRAGON!!! If we are lucky, we can get 'The Legend' to come out here and go a round with our house jousting champion, Sir Jonathan. Pendragon everybody!! (Metallica's 'The Small Hours' blares through the PA, and Venneman stands up to acknowledge the crowd. His sigil unrolls in a banner from the ceiling, and he smiles broadly, the lights reflecting off his shirt and his belt. He leaps over the railing and onto the arena floor and takes a bow. He then shrugs and walks to the backstage doorway to the left. Looks like he is gonna give the crowd a show. ******FLASH****** In the back, Xerxes is saddled and ready to go. Pendragon scans the wall that is filled with various weapons, lances and swords and the like. He grabs a lance, hefts it, testing the weight. He frowns and breaks it over one knee....) Venneman: This will not do!! These look like prop lances....EDWARD!!!! (Edward appears at his side....) Venneman: Run back to the hotel and get me one of MY lances. This must be done right. (Edward runs off and Pendragon climbs atop Xerxes to wait. In short order, Edward is back. Evidently their hotel is close by. He hands the golden, dragonhead tipped lance to Pendragon who smiles and nods his head. He then walks Xerxes through the large door and into the arena proper. Benjamin stands in the center, and Jonathan sits his mount on the other side...) Benjamin: A treat indeed! Your Lord Pendragon has graciously agreed to a show of his strength...He is a champion in every since of the world, and he has the title to back it up...He is the Man to be reckoned with, The Legend of the Lance....THE MYTH OF PRO WRESTLING....Ladies and Gents....VENNEMAN PENDRAGON!!! (The crowd goes nuts and Venneman raises his Lance in salute. He lines Xerxes up along the fence and points the lance at Jonathan. Benjamin makes his exit and a white kerchief falls from the ceiling. As soon as it reaches the ground, Jonathan takes off, his lance leveled at Pendragon. Pendragon kicks Xerxes into motion and the two riders race towards one another. Venneman levels his lance, but Xerxes, unused to the hard floor, stumbles a little and Pendragon loses his aim. Jonathan does not and he hits Pendragon in the chest with his lance. The lance explodes into a million splinters...Prop lance, it does not even stagger Pendragon. The two riders take their places once more, Pendragon down one point to none. Again the kerchief falls, and both riders spring into action. This time, Xerxes keeps his footing and Pendragon plants the lace tip in the chest of Jonathan. Jonathan flies from his horse as the lance breaks, and he lands in a heap on the ground. The crowd cheers more as Pendragon takes a victory lap around the arena. He dismounts and strides to the struggling Jonathan and offers him a hand. As he pulls him to his feet, he gets even more cheers. As the crowd roars, Pendragon leans in close to Jonathan...) Venneman: Just like the men I face this weekend, you are no champion. You should go home instead of putting on a charade for these paying fans each night. Find something you are good at...Life will not always throw a prop at you. Sometimes, you get the real thing. You need to be prepared. (Pendragon backs away and draws his sword. He points it skyward and closes his eyes. Instantly, the scene fades...)
