Hallowe'en - christmas comes early

31 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31/10/02 October 31st, 22:42. I love Hallowe'en. It's the one night of the year when the ladies dress in MY kind of clothing. I don't mean red coats either. Like YOU, there... Nerezza sits in a tree. His attire is different from usual. He wears a black, hooded robe made of plain canvas, some deep blue baggy pants of some sort, a pair of sandals and his entire body has been painted purple. He is masquerading as none other than Minion The Imp from OSW, despite being six feet tall. Illuminating the left side of his body is a bright light, leaking from a window adjacent to him. Nerezza gazes from his vantage point inside. The room is mint green and spacious, occupied by only a large oak desk, two chairs and their occupants. Behind the desk is a police officer, dressed in full uniform. In front is a woman, also dressed up. Although somewhat different - her outfit is a black vinyl dress with matching boots that climb to her knee and sport high, platform heels. Also on her legs are red fishnets, that co-ordinate with the red streaks in her hair and novelty devil horns. Needless to say, her figure and pretty face are enough to warrant her wearing the outfit. Even he can't take his eyes from your little teal oceans... 'So you say he was... how tall?' the officer asks, gazing at the girl. 'Around 6' tall, maybe 6'1' or so.' she replies. Her voice is sweet and quiet, the total opposite of the racy clothing she wears. 'And what was he wearing?' The detective continued, breaking his enchanted look for only a moment to take notes. 'Umm... a really, really long coat, to his ankles. Red, too. And... and his hair was all scruffy and purple too.' But mine's so well-groomed...' 'Any other major distinguishing features?' 'He... uhh... had vampire teeth. Fake looking ones, they were even glowing in the dark. OH! And he had sunglasses on. Blue jeans, black shirt... that's all I can remember, except the way he...' she breaks off into a sob. Tears mess up her make-up, staining her face with a dark trickle. The officer opens up a drawer and fishes inside until a box of tissues comes to hand. He offers one to her with a sympathetic look. Outside, Nerezza watches with relish as her tears seem to feed him. He taps his teeth with his index finger to test their authenticity. Real enough to me... 'So what exactly did he do?' He asks in a voice that matches hers. 'Well... I was on my way to a club to meet some friends... and I walked past this alley... then he pulled me into it and pushed his hand across my mouth. I couldn't bite him... and then...' The policeman's eyes narrow in disgust. 'For the benefit of the tape, the young lady is shifting the strap of her dress to one side and there appear to be teeth marks and severe bruising to the shoulder.' Must have been one hell of a bite to get through those fake teeth... A wave of sniffling and sobbing then erupts as the girl bursts into a full wail. The policeman clicks the stop button on the dictaphone, ending the recording. Nerezza melts past the branches and lands on the ground among some red and brown leaves. Looking left and right, he emerges from around the side of the police station, looking as innocent as an angel. Well... I have a party of my own to attend to... * * * October 31st, 18:23 The streets are bustling. Kids, left, right and centre. All dressed up. Some are Spiderman. Some are astronauts. Some are... themselves. Ugh, goths... humanity's pathetic imitators and poets. The sun is just peeking over the horizon, ready to hide itself. Adults mingle with the youths, some protecting them, some on their way to parties. Nerezza walks among them in costume, dressed as OSW's resident Pimpingest Imp this side of the Mississippi. The mask over his face protects his identity on one of the few days of the year he feels in his element. He stops for a moment. The smile on his face almost breaks through the rubber covering it. Christmas comes early every year... His trail of thought is distracted as someone brushed past him. The small eye holes prevent him from seeing whoever it was until he steps in front again....except it's no he. Staring at the rubber mask is a pretty girl, european looking with eyes that sparkled green-blue. Her shiny blonde hair ran with slashes of red and came down to her chin, framing her lovely features. 'Sorry about that... hey, nice outfit... are you a dwarf?' Nerezza froze up inside. Despite his usual dislike for humans, this one stirred something inside him. No sounds came from his cold mouth and he merely stood there as the girl shrugged and continued past. Nerezza's eyes scanned up and down her clothing... what there was of it. Black... shiny... sexy... TALK! Still, he said nothing. Hypnotized, he began to follow. It was to him as though they were the only two in the street. Like a puppy, he followed her. Through packed terraces, across roads and over a bridge, drawing closer to the town centre. The air smelled of various hot food, including garlic as a Hallowe'en promotion. Garlic... guaranteed to keep ANYTHING away. The stink finally breaks him from his trance as the girl he was stalking steps into a side street. Still wearing his Minion outfit, he follows, now aware of his surroundings. He notices a small alleyway up ahead and begins to quicken his pace as his mouth begins to water. Hm... good enough place to grab... Suddenly, his racing mind is interrupted as the girl suddenly whisks sideways into it, seemingly against her will as a yelp escapes quickly. The vampire tenses as he looks left and right. The alley is deserted with the exception of the girl. He sinks his claws into the wall and begins to scale the face of it as fast as he can, sending chunks of stone to the ground into a messy pile. Once he heaves his weight over the top and onto the slanted roof, the view that greets him sends his eyes bursting through the mask. Standing on the ground and trying to restrain the girl is a fairly tall man, wearing a red duster coat, jeans, boots and a black t-shirt with a head full of purple hair.... It's.... me? he thinks, gazing down at someone wearing clothes simular to his. Despite the fact that the coat has the sleeves intact and there is no armor or gloves, the outfit is rather convincing. Too convincing. His name and image was on the line. If any blood drainings were to be done, then the genuine article was the one to do them. Regardless of the flattery, Nerezza begins to haul his weight over the edge behind the struggling pair. Again, mounds of masonry tumble to the concrete, but the frenzied gyrations and sound of rubbing clothing blot out the falling debris. Nerezza slowly makes his way down watch, but the scream that escapes through the attacker's hands as he bites his victim's shoulder makes him freeze up for a moment. The sound takes him back to a simular scenario, back to when he was Matt Jay in PWA, just after meeting Gaz Green, before he was a vampire... In Matt's mind, he had gone out to the streets of Detroit, Michigan in search of accessories for his new gimmick - ironically, a vampire. Without much luck, he was lumbering home after the search only turned up red contact lenses. A sound on the otherwise silent night alerted him to a lane nearby. Peering in, he saw a woman being pinned to a wall by a man clad in black with a balaclava over his face. Stepping out, Matt tried his most convincing vampire impersonation. Although within range of the man's knife, superstition had overcome him and the red eyes of Matt sent him screaming from the scene. The damsel in distress then gave her knight in shining armor a kiss... one that drew the blood of them both and turned the UK-born Matt Jay into Nascosto Ireul Nerezza, the anti-human in every respect... So.... familiar.... Snapping back to reality, a split second had passed between the wandering of Nerezza's mind to the next octave oozing through the parted fingers of the imposter. Dropping immediately from the wall, Nerezza did something only Minion could do... the vampire took a step backwards, scratched his leg with the opposite foot, then took a jog forward and swung his leg upwards, planting a sandal between the legs. As the assailant's grip loosened, the girl ran as fast as she could out of the alley, not even looking back as the attacker crumpled onto the floor in an ungraceful heap. It appears I'm quite taken with her... I guess I have time for a quick exercise before I follow that perfume scent... he thought to himself. Despite being incapacitated. the man kneeled down in a way that still left him open to attack. Nerezza inched forward and reveled in the grunts and groans of agony. He savored in the pain that was in the air and could almost feel the damage spreading throughout his new prey's abdomen, through the intestines, through the stomach... before moving closer. The last thing the man felt was another force slamming into his damaged testicles, so hard that he flipped head over heels and ended up staring up at the sky. The last thing he heard was a maniacal, yet high pitched laugh echoing up and down the confined space. The last thing he saw before blacking out was the rubbery purple skin that belonged to a demonic little imp... * * * November 1st, 15:11 Nerezza awoke to the sound of wild cackling. He picked his head up from the pillow which his face had sunken into and rolled onto his back, taking the hotel's blanket with him and sending them tumbling to the floor. Sitting across the room from him, dressed in the classic pine green suit with the peaked lime tipped hair, Gaz browsed through the newspaper. 'Enjoy the party at Timo's last night, Squire?' The Jester said, not taking his eyes off the Peanuts cartoon he was reading. 'What I can remember of it... ' groaned the vampire, noticing his skin was still purple from yesterday's Minion outfit. 'It'll probably end up in the newspaper you're reading anyway.' 'Actually, something happened last night to take note of... some guy was taken to hospital with twisted testicles after a kid dressed as Minion The Imp attacked him. Apparently, Minion jumped him from behind as he did his business in an alley, knocked him out with a plank of wood viciously and then slapped him where it hurts with it...' Gaz read, while Nerezza tried his best to hide a smirk. 'You know something, Gaz... it's amazing what some idiots will do when they dress as someone famous.'
