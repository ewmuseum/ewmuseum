The Transformation: Children of Sanchez Part 1

23 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/23/03 Children of Sanchez The dusty sand blows across the sterling silver chaps of the black leather boots on the tender feet of a man unknown. A cigar is thrown to the ground, with the light being diffused by the rigid and hot air. The boot smothers it into the ground, leaving small particles to remain. The angle leans upward showing the face of El Romantico with his hair shining from the reflection of the sun. El Romantico is in the city of Oaxaca, Mexico. The temperature is hot, and the faces are lifeless. Men walk around in rags, living in houses that wouldn’t be fit for animals. Children are everywhere, but they are not running around or playing filled with energy, but rather lying on the ground coughing. It was three weeks ago where El Romantico made a three hundred thousand dollar donation to these children and this society. El Romantico is back and wants the hard worked income back for himself. Romantico prances around in his high-class designer clothing as he watches hundreds of eyes glued to him. A group of three men come next to him. Romantico smiles, knowing their motives before they ever open their mouths. Sir may we… Romantico feels one man brush across his side and elbow him with full impact to the ground, nearly breaking his nose. Back away you worthless sacks of flesh. You come to take the money of a man who has fed your children. May you burn for your actions, and may your children be poisoned with futile minds you have. Romantico then sticks a lit cigar he’s been smoking in the arm of the man bleeding on the ground. He then digs into his wallet and pulls out a fifty-dollar bill. He throws it on the ground near the body and watches as every run and fight over it. Sick dogs… Romantico continues on his way as he arrives at his destination. A decrepit wood building, that has been barely destroyed by termites. The walls leak with mildew and Romantico doesn’t even want to plant his fingers on it. Where is Sergio Fonsueco? You will have to wait sir. I wait. I do not think so; you see I am a man of importance. I am not like the people in this disease-infested habitat, and by no means am I like you. The man looked in my eyes as I smiled at him and walked right into Sir Fonsueco’s room. The man then sat down and dropped his head. A secretary came over and checked on him to see if he was all right. Senor, are you all right? That mans eyes. They were the eyes of a demon. Something I have never seen before and something I never want to see again. Why is he here? Only the Lord may know. Back in the room of Sir Fonsueco Romantico takes a seat and watches him speak on the phone happily to a donator. He notices Romantico and ends the coversation as soon as he can. Romantico, what a pleasure to see you here! You’ve done so much for all of us. Sir Fonsueco goes over to hug Romantico who backs away and looks at Sergio with utter disgust. Is something wrong? Yes, I want my money back in entirety. I’ve decided to make my investment elsewhere in a more profitable organization. Signor, why? You seemed so dedicated. That was then, this is now. I demand my money. Follow me, and I will show you something that should make you feel as if your donation has done a great deal of help. Sergio brings Romantico into a room where a small boy Julio lives. Romantico looks at the boy, who has a smile on his face and is playing with a new red fire truck. Why did you bring me here? So show you the great things you’ve helped us do. Romantico charges at Sergio and holds him by his collar against the wall. He smiles at him and drops him. He then walks over to Julio who waves at Romantico. Romantico steps on the red fire truck Julio is playing with, crushing it into pieces, he then walks off. My money will be given back. I have no remorse for any of you. To Be Continued………
