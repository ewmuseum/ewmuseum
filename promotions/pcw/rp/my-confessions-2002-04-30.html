My Confessions

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/30/02 *Monologue* Consequences. We all endure them. Nothing is ever fair. For every action we do in this world there is a consequence. Take this for example: Karl LeBlanc does his promo, which is a consequence for us because we watched the damn thing, and it made us fall asleep. Leaving lil’ Billy at soccer practice. Mike Frampton will have to live with the consequence that when he decided to steal the Extreme title he ended a friendship. All for the sake of gold. All because Big Daveylicious is the Extreme champion. Big Daveylicious also made his consequences. He got Frampton into the PCW. He won the Extreme title. He fights his first defense and his opponent no shows. Result: Mike tries to steal gold right from Big D’s nose. All for the following reasons which every wrestler wants fame, fortunate, your name in the lights, women, and the gold. With such prizes it’s hard to resist to temptation. But in the end you give in. Who can blame Frampton for making such a decision? It’s easy gold. Attack somebody in his or her weakest state, which is trusting you. I mean surely a friend won’t double cross you? Would he? AC/DC’s “Who Made Who?“ echoes a question. Who made who? I’ll gladly answer. Your Extreme Champion made Mike Frampton! Big D made Mike into a FSW Rising star champion. Big D made him into a PCW star. Hell, thanks to Big D he’s moved up a division! But does he get thanked for it? Nooooooooo, he gets stabbed in the back just because he had gold. Whatever Mike’s excuses are he’ll get his in the end. He signed his death certificate once he screwed Big D. The grave is just being dug up right now. Remember there may be no “I” in “team” but if you switch the words around there is “me”. (The scene fades inside a church. We notice the detail on the stained glass windows; the icons carved into the columns that support the extravagantly decorated ceiling. There with his hands buried in his face sitting in one of the altar chairs is Big Daveylicious dressed in a black Armani suit and the Extreme title on his shoulder.) Big Daveylicious: Mike’s monologue person is right. I need to ask for forgiveness of all my wrenched sins. I need to be cleansed. How can I be the Extreme champion if I am? (He removes his hands from his face. He puts his hand through his bleached blonde hair and stands up. He walks out of the aisle of the altar chairs, past the front door of the church walking to the confessional booth. He pauses and proceeds to enter the booth. Big D kneels on the board. We hear the shuffling noise of the priest getting into his own compartment next door. A moment later the small, eye-level door slides open. The priest's face is barely visible on the other side of the thick screen, but he's there.) Big Daveylicious: Ok, I’d like a Big Mac and hold the fries. Priest: This is not a place of nonsense. Big Daveylicious: But I thought you guys loved to see your customers smile? What kind of place is this?! I want to talk to your manager. Not Ronald McDonald. Priest: The church is a place of worship. Not a place for a clown with red hair serving hamburgers. (Big D slaps his hand on his forehead and shakes his head.) Big Daveylicious: Forgive me, Father, for I have sinned. Priest: Prepare to receive the Act of Penance. How many sins have you committed since your last confession? (Big D starts counting his fingers.) Big Daveylicious:1…2…3…4…5…6 damn it!… I mean darn it. Too many sins, Father. Priest: What are they my son? Big Daveylicious: I’m sorry. I didn’t flush the toilet when I left or folded my bed or cleaned my house or that I didn’t put the toothpaste in the cabinet. I’m sorry I didn’t… Priest: Please, don’t waste my time. These are not sins. Big Daveylicious: Ok... I beat up my lackey! Priest: What makes you do this? Big Daveylicious: He is…well…kinda gay. Priest: So you beat him up because he‘s happy? Big Daveylicious: No, because he’s kinda like a homosexual. Priest: So you beat him up because of his choice in life? Big Daveylicious: Well...er...uh..kinda...ALRIGHT...YES! I enjoy seeing him squirm like a little sheep! I confess! Priest: If he’s your lackey shouldn’t you be more caring, more loving and more gentle towards him? Big Daveylicious: Are you kidding me?! He may think I float on his boat....I mean I’ll try... Priest: Trying isn’t... Big Daveylicious: FINE! I’ll DO IT! Priest: You are now forgiven, son. Any other sins a burden to your soul? Big Daveylicious:...No. (Big D jumps up.) Big Daveylicious: ALRIGHT! I SLEPT with the hooker from Tijuana for $5! It’s not my fault the Devil made me do it!...OK, I was horny! Priest: Thank you for being honest, child. Big Daveylicious: CHILD?! I’m 30 year old man! Priest: Were all... Big Daveylicious: FINE! I am 23! Happy?! Priest: Yes, I’m very happy that you seen the light. Big Daveylicious: That’s not all, Father. Can I call you Dad? Priest: I’d rather you not. Big Daveylicious: Why not? I mean I’ve never been this close....OK! I’m sorry for being a sexist male pig to the PCW women driving them out! ...Jesu...Jeez I’m sorry for touching there boobs with them not giving me permission! Priest: All is forgiven. You may go home now, child. Big Daveylicious:...I’m sorry, Father. But I have one more sin. I’m sorry for betraying Mike Frampton and costing him his little matches... Priest: Your forgiven. Now, did that feel better to get that off your chest? (Suddenly, a sadistic grin comes across Big D’s face.) Big Daveylicious:.. I lied! I’m not sorry for betraying Frampton! HE CAN GO TO HELL...I MEAN HE CAN GO TO H-E- DOUBLE HOCKEY STICKS FOR ALL I CARE! (The priest shocked is in a loss for words. Our devilish Extreme champion gets out of the confession booth smiling as he walks out of the church as he pulls the churchs wooden door open. He shuts it and jumps to the floor of the stairs leading out to the streets of Miami. He kneels down and lifts his arms outstretched in joy once again.) Big Daveylicious: Whooooooooooooooooooo! I’M FORGIVEN! HOLY POOP....I MEAN CRAP! (Suddenly, lighting strikes our champion. Making him fall to the ground in agony as the scene fades to black.)
