Three Words You Don't Want To Hear: Crow Penis Stimulation

7 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/7/02 The scene opens up in a very scientific looking room. On the tables there are test tubes and vials of strange colored liquids and fizzling substances. On the other side of the room are glass windows, but the camera cannot reach to see what’s behind these glass cases. Sounds of footsteps can be heard and the camera turns to see Rush walking into the room, with Melanie by his side, and the scientist, presumably the Jonas Rafter mentioned last time we saw Rush. Rush is wearing a gray Ecko t-shirt, shiny black jeans, a black and red UNC baseball hat with Chinese characters and a white do rag underneath, and pair of white T-Macs. He’s standing in front of Stacey, holding her and he looks around the room, surprised to see lots of the tubes and scientific stuff. Rush: Jonas, what exactly are you a scientist of? This confirms the fact that the scientist is in fact Jonas Rafter. He’s wearing brown slacks with a long white lab coat, and his glasses rest lazily on his noise, which he has to try and lift every few minutes. Jonas: I’m a deoxyribonucleic acid cardiologist. Melanie looks up from picking at her nails, and she is looking very attractive in a small pink tee-shirt, with tight black jeans and pink Timberland boots. Melanie: You work with heart tissue? Rush: How in the hell did you know that? Melanie: The goddess of crunk has her own secrets, thank you very much. Besides, while you were wrestling, I was in school, actually learning. Rush: Hey, I went to school! Part of being a true god is being intelligent. Jonas: I’d love to continue with this, but frankly I don’t have a whole lot of time. Yes you are correct I work with heart tissue, but recently I’ve started working with gene splicing. Rush: So, you make fake animals and play God basically? Jonas mumbles to himself an answer, and he’s lucky that Rush doesn’t catch all of the scientist’s blasphemy. Jonas: No more than you play at being a God. Rush: What was that, little man? Melanie: Cut it out, Rush. He’s a science nerd like Kannon, he doesn’t believe in real gods, like you. Jonas looks over at Melanie, to which she gives him a fake smile, and he just ignores her. He takes out two small vials, of unidentifiable liquid, and Rush exchanges a confused look with Melanie, as the scientist explains. Jonas: This is the kind of work I do. I deal with DNA and plasma, stuff inside your body. Rush: So, can you tell me what this is all about? You’re not gonna like plasma me or something are you? Melanie: Rush, plasma is like body energy, it’s inside of you. Rush: Gods don’t need plasma, we’ve got godly blood. Why do you keep acting as though your godly “visitor” has come? Melanie: I’m not acting like that, I’m just trying to educate you. If you want people to truly call you a God, then you’ve gotta know what the hell you’re talking about. Jonas: Okay, now I can show you what I called you down here for. Jonas leads the two over to the glass cases. He brings them to the door and opens it with a key from his pocket. They walk up to a cage and Jonas pulls out a…thing. Jonas: I call it: a crunkey Rush: The fuck is that thing? The thing looks like a monkey, except for the fact that it has small wings on the edges of arms and instead of a mouth, there is a beak in its place. The crunkey makes a chirping sound as Jonas begins to pet it. Rush: You named that, that thing after the power that is crunk? You wasted a perfectly good name on a waste of flesh like that thing? Jonas: It’s a long story, just listen up. Well, a few months ago, during this whole September 11th mess, we decided that we would start to do some genetic splicing so that we could release these creations into the wild if nuclear war broke out and they might be able to survive. Rush: ACK! Conspiracy nerd! Melanie: Rush, grow up. But seriously, like ACK! Conspiracy nerd! Rush: Oh good, don’t start that Eleanor Hall shit with me. You know she’s the reason that Kannon probably flipped? All that talking and preppiness, it comes back to bite you in the ass sometimes. Melanie: Jonas, what’s the next part of the story? Jonas: Well, we wanted to make something that was mammal-like but had the power of flight and regular arms for climbing and foraging. We decided to cross-breed a crow and a monkey, for the good of science. Rush: So basically you wanted to get off watching a crow and a monkey have sex? Jonas: Actually- Melanie: RUSH! That’s really not very respectful of Jonas’ work, conspiracy nerd or no. But seriously, is that why you wanted to do it? Jonas: Well- Rush: Well how come it was wrong when I said it? I’m the god around here! Melanie: I’m a goddess. Rush: Strange. I don’t remember Akiyama making you a goddess. Melanie: It’s like a monarchy. I’m your girl, so that automatically makes me the Crunk as Hell Goddess. Rush: That’s not fair! I think there should at least be an obstacle course or something. Maybe some trivia? Pie-eating contest? Oooh… I know, you have to beat me in Jenga! Hey this is fun let’s do- Jonas: As I was saying before I was so rudely interrupted, a crow and a monkey can’t mate directly so we had to use the sperm of a crow, because a monkey’s semen would burst a female crow’s fallopian tubes. Melanie: Rush, do you know what fallopian tubes are? Rush: Of course I do!! What do you take me for, a childish god? The fallopian tubes are the inside of a vacuum cleaner. Duh! Melanie sighs as Jonas Rafter resumes her explaining to Rush and his goddess. Jonas: However, we had to use some tactics to get this sperm, which are rather scientific in their nature. Rush and Melanie: So whaddya do? Jonas: Well, crows do not expel their bodily fluids except during ejaculation so we had to give the crow something. Rush: I don’t want to hear the rest of what you’re gonna say. Melanie: What Rush? You don’t like hearing about crowjobs? Melanie grins at her own retarted joke, and Rush, usually the one grinning at a stupid joke, just looks on a sighs, slapping his forehead. Jonas: We had to provide Crow Penis Stimulation. Rush: AGGGHH! My tainted godly ears! Melanie: Shut up Rush! Rush: You know I’m a god, I don’t have to listen to you when you- Melanie: Shut up or I’ll never have sex with you again. Rush: Point taken. Jonas: Now, we had the semen but there was only one way to get the monkey actually pregnant. Rush: I don’t wanna hear this either. Jonas: Artificial insemination… Rush: Oh myself! Please, if there is an ever higher being of Crunk, strike me deaf so that I cannot hear of this man’s evil evil ways. Melanie sighs and pulls mace out of her bag, quickly spraying some into Rush’s eyes as the Crunk as Hell God cries out. Rush: AGGH! I said deaf not blind. Owww, my fucking eyes kill now! Jonas: By hand. Rush: NOOOOOO! Jonas: So there you have it, now we’ve created a new species and we’d like to name it after you. Rush: I won’t be involved with this terrible place. Besides, my eyes hurt too damn much. Melanie: Oh get up Rush. Your eyes are fine. Rush: Hey, now that you mention it, they are fine. Yeah, whatever we can name it after me. Call it Crunkius Rushius. Melanie: No that’s a stupid name. How about Melinus Crunkius? Rush: Now THAT is a- Our hero sees the look that he is getting from Melanie and makes a quick change of mind that will help him out in the long run. Rush: fantastic idea, my goddess. Melanie smiles at him. Jonas: Okay, that’s all I need. I’ll go now, and the exit is exactly the way that you came in. Jonas leaves the room and Melanie and Rush are left alone in the laboratory. Melanie whispers into Rush’s ear and giggles as Rush’s eyebrows arch in surprise. The last shot of the scene is Melanie pulling Rush into a sideroom, grabbing him by the shirt and Rush grinning like an idiot. This promo was weird as hell, the best thing now is to just fade to black…
