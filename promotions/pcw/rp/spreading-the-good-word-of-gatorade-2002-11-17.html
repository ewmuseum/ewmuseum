Spreading The Good Word of Gatorade!

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17-11-02 The scene opens on a news desk. Just a simple mahogany desk, with a few pieces of paper sitting on it, a large poster pasted up on the wall behind the desk with a familiar looking product on it. The sound of film whirring around the camera breaks the silence, with the sudden sound of footsteps breaking in the distance. Louder and louder they get, until finally a man in a smart black suit, with his long hair tied back in a somewhat presentable ponytail sits down at the desk. Leafing through the papers, the man clears his throat and looks up at the camera. The demented twinkle in his eye only emphasises his character. With a stern look across his face, Skitzo clasps his hands and raises an inquisitive eyebrow. Skitzo: Skitzo: A war is brewing. Many forces have been called to battle one another for superiority over a right all men should have, yet only one can. The battlefield is set my friends, blood will be spilled and names will be taken however it cannot be denied that the heart and soul of every man and woman will be poured into the effort. Skitzo: We have, ourselves, full confidence that if all do their duty… Skitzo: That’s him… Skitzo: Me… Skitzo: And you… Skitzo: What about them? Skitzo: That’s us! Skitzo: Yes………if nothing is neglected, and if the best arrangements are made, as they are being made, we, that’s me, myself and I, shall prove ourselves once again able to defend our name and our claim to the right, to ride out the storm of war, and to outlive the menace of tyranny that is a man governed himself by a pensioner with a dicky hip and family full of cross-dressing butlers and paparazzi fodder…if necessary for years, if necessary alone. All in the name of one thing. Skitzo: That one thing good people is not Peace or Harmony or the welfare of the nation, but of the thirst-quenching Ambrosia of the Gods. War will rear its ugly head once more, and once the violent reaction is complete it shall not be the Dove which flies through the sky signifying peace and carrying the olive branch, no my friends it shall be he refreshing alternative to today’s corporate trash the nation is conned into loving… Skitzo: Even though in the past the prestigious contract has fallen into the grip of the likes of the aforementioned governed, or even the enemy of the Goblin and all the odious apparatus of the Kannon rule, we… Skitzo: That’s still him, you and me! Skitzo: Shall not flag or fail… Skitzo: Ok this is the good bit… Skitzo: We shall go on to the end, we shall fight in the Alamo Dome, we shall fight with pride and unbridled passion for our cause, we shall fight with growing confidence and growing strength in our hearts, we shall defend our soda, whatever the cost may be, we shall fight in the ring, we shall fight on the stage, we shall fight in the crowds and in the backstage area… Skitzo: Fuck me, we’ll even fight in Byron Calver’s boudoir! Skitzo: But we shall never surrender, and even if, which I do not for a moment believe, myself, myself, him, you or I were subjugated and forced into submission, then our great empire beyond…the magnificent and spectacular TEAM BASTARDOS… Skitzo: They would carry on the struggle, until, in God's good time, GATORADE, with all its power and might, steps forth to the rescue and the liberation of the old… Skitzo: Winston Churchill is spinning in his motherfucking grave… The End!
