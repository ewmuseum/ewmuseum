Between Heaven and Hell

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-8-02 :Between heaven and hell: :Imagine yourself laying on a beach. The sand serves as a blanket under your weary bones as you listen to the light sounds of waves crashing onto shore. Seagulls fly over head dipping down into the crystal blue water in hopes of catching something to eat, a helpless fish. It's amazing how beauty can come from death. How death can lead to beauty. When most say beauty and death you call it an oxymoron but in this sense it makes beauty. A whisper could ruin it but it speaks volumes as you sit listening to the sounds of 'Sylence' . Now picture the sky turning blood red, the sea turns to blood, and dead animals wash to shore. Fire reigns from the sky piercing your skin with every touch, it burns as you feel your skin melt from the meat and the meat melt from the bone. It burns but yet you stay to watch as the four horsemen role across the sky's. Fear clutches you, paralyzing you where you stand. You want to run but you can't. Things begin to die all around you and soon your soul is sucked from your body leaving you as nothing but a shell. Still you have thoughts, you see death, you look it in the eyes. Your friends, family, and loved ones all die before you. Your now begging for someone to kill you but still nothing. You dash through the fire looking for death but death doesn't find you. Something taps you on your shoulder, is it death? No, it's just one of the souls dying before you. Anger fills what's left, your so jealous, why can't you die? It hurts inside but your soul is gone. All you want is for this to end. Finally everything goes dark, you see nothing, feel nothing, hear nothing. You look, the fires have died and the earth's floor is covered with the already decaying bodies of the ones you loved. It hurts inside, but you must live with out your soul, you must live in a world alone. Everyone's fear is dying but what if everyone died and you were left alone? Maybe that truly is a fate worse than death. Tonight, someone will face a fate worse than death. Ripper Ryckman will meet the celestial being known as Jay Sylo one on one for his precious little title. Sylo's ready, ready to rip him apart and leave him a blood mess. He wants to hurt Ripper because Ripper seems to think he beat Sylo. He never beat him due to Sylo getting dq'ed before he got his hands on Ryckman. Tonight though Ryckman will wish he had lost, because tonight his reign as champion comes to an end. The talking ends and the action begins as Sylo unleashes himself upon Ryckman. May god have mercy on his soul. We join Sylo after a night of dreaming about winning the match, dreaming about thoughts that ran through his mind. He was changing inside but he didn't know how to describe it but soon enough everything would be revealed. We join him now.: :What a night. He had dreamed about anything and everything on his mind more than once. Over and over his heart beat inside of his chest as beads of sweat dripped from his ice blue hair onto his forehead. His main dream was of the night where he would clinch the Rising Star title and make it his own; That night was tonight. Oh to get his hands on Ripper Ryckman one last time would be a reward greater than death. He would stare him down and slowly rip him apart from the inside, feeding on his fear which would cause this beast's hunger to grow. With a few mighty swipes his opponent would be bleeding, feeding the beast's hunger for blood. Snarls filled the room as Sylo's eyes glowed, destruction filled the mind. He didn't care for Ryckman's well being, he wanted to hurt him and that's what he would do. He would hurt him if he had the chance, he just didn't care anymore. It was time for a new person, a new Sylo, a new monster. He leaped from bed, quick to dress. Soon he found himself walking down the cold streets of some city. The wind blew against his warm skin but it had no effect on his expression. He was the type to mind his own business until someone messed with him, on this day he would do something which would change the name Sylo forever. A man passed bumping into Sylo but instead of saying excuse me he replied with a 'watch it buddy'. This pissed the big man off as he grabbed the helpless man up onto his shoulders and hoisted him down into a dark ally. Was he crazy? He may have very well been.: 'Did Ryckman send you?!' Sylo snarled at the man laying flat on his back. 'W....What? W....who? Please don't hurt me!' :Sylo trudged forward, angry. His eyes began to shimmer as he let out another snarl. The large veins in his large arms began to pump more and more blood throughout enlarging the vein. He reached down grabbing the helpless man by the collar and hoisting him up against a brick wall covered with street slime. The helpless victim covered his face with his hands, begging for mercy as Sylo held him pinned against the wall. Rats scattered underneath Sylo as he kept his eyes locked on the man.: 'Let me tell you something, I am not going to let someone Ryckman sent stop me. I know that's what your doing but you tell him this....Tell him I'm coming for his precious little title TONIGHT!' 'What are you talking about man? I don't know any Ripper!' The man yelled as best he could. :Sylo lowered his head a tad bit, turning long enough to let out a laugh. He once again turned his eyes back to the mans letting out a growl.: 'Then tell your 'boss' I don't need his help and the next time he sends one of his lackey's he will be destroyed!' Sylo snarled again. 'I don't know what your talking about!' The man tried to cover up. :His eyes began to flash as he tried to cover it up. Sylo snarled as the mans eyes finally turned two deep, dark, pools of black. His grip tightened around the neck of this man...well was he a man or was he just another demon? Either way Sylo was beginning to choke him out. Letting out deep gurgling sounds the 'thing' began tapping on the hand of Sylo begging to be let go. He snarled again slowly letting up his grasp around the neck of whatever this thing was. His eyes began to glisten as the 'things' eyes seemed to get darker. His muscles bulged out even more as the 'thing' tried to regain his composure.: 'Fine, you got me! My master sent me to stop you....you denied us and now we will make you pay!' The demon of Satan snarled. :Sylo only laughed as the demon looked down in shock.: 'What's so funny!?' The demon demanded to know. 'SHUT YOUR MOUTH!' Sylo yelled pressing against the demon's neck once more. 'I will do the talking and you will listen. Tell your 'master' that I will never fall to his ways. Soon enough I will be the one on top and there is nothing you nor your master can do about it. Oh yes soon I will be the one you are worshiping you foul demon,' Sylo snarled. :Sylo yelled out tossing the demon head first into a dumpster. A loud 'bang' filled the ally way almost like a gun shot going off. It's body slumped down like a sack of bones. Sylo knew he had broken a few on the demon but didn't care because now he was almost like an animal stalking it's prey.: 'You see,' Sylo said leaning down next to the demon. 'Tonight Ripper Ryckman will be destroyed by the hands of me. The rising star title will be mine whether you, your boss, or Ryckman like it. Nothing can stop me for I am the one you and everyone else fears,' :Sylo laughed a little bit but soon his expression changed as he grabbed the demon by the neck and hoisted it back into the air. He snarled once more staring the demon in the eyes.: 'And you make sure to tell that to your boss!' Sylo whispered. :Like a flash Sylo threw the demon into the cold, green dumpster covered in filth. He slammed the lid down and walked out of the ally way letting out another snarl as his eyes filled with the ice blue liquid once more. He walked past an on coming crowed slowly dissapearing into a sea of people. His words echoed inside the demons head as it laid in the dumpster waiting for Sylo to be gone. He knew he feared him and he knew Sylo wouldn't be stopped, it was just a matter of time before everyone knew this. It was written that tonight Sylo would take what was his, and that was the gods honest truth. And finally we get to see Ripper Ryckman and Jay Sylo clash one on one. For Ripper's sake....may god have mercy on his soul because nothing will stop the beast known as Sylo.:
