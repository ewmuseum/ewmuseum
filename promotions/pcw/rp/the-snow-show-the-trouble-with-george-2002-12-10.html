THE SNOW SHOW: The Trouble With George

Date: December 10, 2002 ﻿************************************THE SNOW SHOWSeason I,Episode 3************************************ “You call that an excuse!?” Shena fumes, staring down at Snow, who’s strapped into theuncomfortable chair by barbed wire and heavy chains. “I just couldn’t take it any more!” Snow argues. “He just wouldn’t fucking shut up! He went onand on... and fucking on!” “You SHOT him in the head, Jason!” barks the mare, her massive head inching closer to Snow. “I’d have given him one in the ballocks, meself,” shrugs the leprechaun, O’Reilly. He sits on thefloor, just behind the horse, peeling a banana. “You know, one fer good measure.” “Typical,” says Shena, turning her back on Snow. “Sometimes I don’t know why I even botherwith you.” “I had no choice!” Snow says in his defense. “No human being could have withstood thatmotherfucker. If I told him to shut up, he probably would have shot me. Besides, I wassupposed to shoot him later anyway - what’s the big deal?” “I’m not saying that I like the fact that you’re shooting anyone for ANY reason,” Shena saysharshly, snapping her head toward Snow in his chair. “But at least if you had waited until yougot to the bridge, you would have been following orders. It still would have been horrendous,but at LEAST you wouldn’t have taken a man’s life over the fact that he TALKED TOOMUCH!” Snow sits silently in the chair as Shena uses the power in her mind to tighten his restrains. Theycut deeply into his skin now, amusing the little leprechaun on the floor behind her. They sliceinto his bones now, cutting further and further, until he’s left in a pile. His various body partsroll around on the floor in an ever growing pool of blood. *** Location: Outskirts of New York, nearing New JerseyDate: November 26, 2002Start of Scene Time: 1:30 pmShot: Roadside Snow ripped the sleeve from George’s jacket and was wiping the blood and bodily fluids fromthe visible parts of the car when Johnny Silver’s Benz appeared in the distance. In what time hehad left, Snow hurried to clean the car. He rolled the broken driver’s side window down, to hidethe blood and brain stains, and then worked to wipe off what had splattered along the top of thedoor. He didn’t realize that he was covered in blood himself. “What the fuck!?” Johnny Silver exclaimed when he got out of his car and saw the scene. “Thatfucking bridge ain’t for another three miles! I told you to wait for the fucking bridge!” Helooked around nervously, checking for witnesses. To their fortune, the road was not one withmuch traffic. In fact, Snow estimated that it had been at least ten minutes since he last saw one,while driving - fat George was still alive then. “What’s the big fucking deal about the bridge!?” Snow burst out, looking down at the silverback-to-front comb over of his new boss, Johnny Rosalini. “Who cares if I did it here or there!?” “Gutsy kid,” Johnny answered, “but stupid as fuck!” He took a deep breath. “The pointof the bridge was that you were to do the job there, so we could just dump the car over the cliffwhen it was over. Now we’re sitting right where anyone can come by and see us, and we’ve gota dead fat ass lying in the fucking car!!” Johnny’s face turned red and his words were exclaimedby the spit flying from his mouth. Snow noticed the gold tooth more than ever. Snow looked toward the road ahead. “Well,” he started with a sigh, “Why don’t we drive to thebridge and finish the job then?” “I’ve got a better idea,” Johnny said with a hint of authority. “Why don’t you drive hisfat ass to the bridge, and I’ll follow - like we was supposed to do in the first fucking place! Youget there, and I’ll help you push the fat fucker over the side.” Snow looked in the back seat at what was left of George’s face. He had imagined himselffeeling uneasy, at least, at the sight of a man killed at his own hands. The Tank had hardenedhim more than he dreamed. ***Location: Outskirts of New York, nearing New JerseyDate: November 26, 2002Start of Scene Time: 1:40Shot: Roadside, near Karhall Bridge Just past the bridge, Snow hopped out of the car and peered down over the cliff and into thewater. It was an impressive drop - at least fifty feet down, with jagged rocks sprouting from thecliff side the whole way down. The water at the bottom moved swiftly beneath the bridge,which was built at the narrowest point between the illusion of two lakes. “Anybody see you?” Johnny Silver asked as he quickly springs from his own car and hurries overto Snow. “A few people passed,” Snow answered with a nod. “But I don’t think any of them noticedanything.” He glanced at the remaining bloodstains surrounding the driver’s side window ofGeorge’s car. “Alright, kid,” Johnny nodded, “lets get this shit over with.” Snow opened the driver’s door and put the car in neutral while Johnny went to the passengerside to prepare to push the car over the cliff. “How deep is the water?” Snow asked as theybegan pulling the car back to line it up with the edge of the bank. “About thirty feet,” Johnny shrugged. “Give or take a few. Ain’t nobody going to find this fatass down there. We’ve been dumping assholes in that lake for a couple of years now, and theFeds ain’t found one of them.” “A couple of years?” Snow sounded surprised as they let stopped the car and prepared for the bigpush over the cliff. “How many bodies are down there?” “How the fuck should I know?” Johnny shrugged. “It’s a well-known spot in the circles. Adozen, probably, maybe a couple dozen. I ain’t got time to do the math. Lets just shove his fatass in there before somebody comes along.” Snow agreed, though he was curious about just how many bodies were below the bridge. Italmost amused him that all of the Government’s Intelligence couldn’t find these bodies. He wasreminded of the old story about the needle in the haystack. With a running start, Snow and Johnny pushed the car toward the cliff, and then released it. Thecar’s momentum sent it flying over the edge of the bank, and it tumbled roughly down the sideof the cliff, with George’s fat body being flung around the interior. With a splash, it slams top-down onto the surface of the water. “Goodbye, ya fat fuck,” Johnny said to the dead man before spitting something else out inItalian. “Johnny...” Snow said quietly, his eyes fixed down on the water. Johnny turned his back to the bank and started back for his own car, ignoring Snow. “Ah,” hesaid, “Georgy had his good side, I guess. He was always good around Thanksgiving - brought ashit load of food over to Ma’s place.” “Johnny...” Snow repeated. He hadn’t moved from the edge of the cliff. “Yeah, I’ll miss him in a way,” Johnny sighed, still ignoring Snow. There were tears forming inhis brown eyes now. “But that’s the business, I guess. The life we chose. George wasn’t sobad... I’ll miss him... I really will.” “JOHNNY!!!” Snow bellowed, finally getting the older man’s attention. He pointed down overthe cliff, prompting Johnny Silver to return to take a look. His face went pale. At the bottom of the cliff, still resting on top of the water, was the capsized car. George’s armwas visible, hanging out the broking back window. “You stupid fuck, George!” Johnny cursed, pointing down at the wreckage. “Can’t you evenfucking die right!? You motherless cunt! I fucking hate your fat ass! Always did, cocksucker! Ihope they starve you in hell!” “Johnny!” Snow said, shaking his companion. “What the fuck are we going to do about this!?” Johnny took a moment to collect himself. “I don’t fucking know!” he finally answered. “Whythe fuck hasn’t it sunk yet? I know why! I’ll bet it’s that fat fucker’s ass that’s keeping the carup!” Snow rolled his eyes. “It’s not floating,” he said. “It’s on top of another car! You guys keeppiling your fucking waste down there - you should have known that one of these days the pilewas going to get so high that it would poke out of the water.” “Alright, alright,” Johnny said, wiping his brow. “Let me think for a second.” *** Location: Outskirts of New York, nearing New JerseyDate: November 26, 2002Start of Scene Time: 1:50Shot: Roadside, near Karhall Bridge “I really think there’s a better way to do this,” Snow frowned as he started down the side of thecliff, with only a rope tied around his waist to support him. At the other end, the rope waswrapped around a tree trunk and then tied to the trailer hitch of the car. “Well, let me know if you think of it when you get down there,” Johnny said with a smile. “Why do I have to go down?” Snow asked, still ascending. “Because I’ve got to stay up here,” Johnny said. “You know, in case any witnesses happen tocome by. Somebody’s got to look like their taking a piss on the side of the road, or people willget suspicious.” Snow reluctantly swallowed his arguments and continued down the side of the cliff, holding therope with both hands for balance. Mob life wasn’t supposed to be like this, he thought. Slowly,but quickly considering the circumstances, Snow ascended down the side of the cliff toward thewrecked car below. He wasn’t sure what to expect, considering the fact that there werecountless dead bodies just below - some of them had no doubt been there for years. He was strangely unaffected by the thought. “What am I supposed to do down there, anyway!?” Snow asked, half yelling - he was nearly halfway down the cliff now, his life literally dangling by a rope tied around his waist... he didn’tunderstand, now, why he let Johnny tie the rope. “Push it off the top of the pile,” said Johnny, nearly out of ear shot. “Then get your ass back uphere so we can get back to the city.” Before long, Snow found himself waist deep in the water, standing on the hood of a car belowthe surface. He was standing on the passenger side of George’s capsized car, and he could seeGeorge’s fat dead carcass on the inside of the car. With all his might, Snow began pushing the car, hoping that it would soon fall off the top of thepile and slide down to the bottom of the lake. It was tougher than he’d hoped. “Would you hurry the fuck up!?” Johnny yelled from the top of the cliff. From the bottom of thecliff, Snow could barely see him in front of the glaring sun. “A car just drove by you know!” Johhny said angrily. Snow wasn’t too concerned. Splashing around in the lake, he finally managed to get a foot hold beneath the water, and the carbegan to move quite slowly. Looking down into the water, Snow realized that his right foot hadlodged itself against the inside edge of a broken sunroof below. It was too late when he realized that the sunroof was broken - he lost his footing and fell beneaththe surface of the water, into the interior of the car below. To his left was a man who’d beendead for at least a couple of months, and although his face was rapidly deteriorating, hissunglasses were still on his face. To his right was another man who had three bullet holes in theside of his face. Snow forgot to breathe. Not that it mattered, considering there was at least eight feet of waterabove his head. On top of the cliff, Johnny Silver’s eyes had grown nearly to the size of baseballs, or at least, hewould have claimed so from the way they felt. Fifty feet below, Snow seemed to have justdisappeared beneath the surface of the water, just after he managed to push George’s car awayand out of sight. “Oh shit!” Johnny exclaimed and began pulling on the rope with all his might, trying to pullSnow out from the water. Little did he know, that he was actually trying to pull Snow throughthe narrow sunroof of a discarded mob-car. Beneath the water, Snow struggled against the rope, trying to slip through the narrow opening. Every time he got close, however, the rope would pull him hard and he would slam against theceiling of the car. Suddenly, he realized the seriousness of the situation - he was running out oftime. Every moment felt more and more like a hammer slamming down into his chest, and hope wasdwindling away, thanks to the random and vigorous tugs on the rope. In his only moment ofclarity, Snow realized that if he was going to survive this situation, he would have to break freeof the rope. Instinctively, he pulled the gun from the inside of his jacket, aimed it at the rope,and pulled the trigger - a stupid idea. Panic began to set in. The pain in his chest was gone now, which he had always heard was asign of drowning. Desperately, he began to search the dead man in sunglasses. In the man’ssock, he found a short-bladed knife strapped to the man’s decaying ankle. Quickly, he began working the rope with the knife, slashing wildly at it. As if in protest, therope pulled back - much more vigorously this time. In fact, it pulled him upward and slammedhim hard into the ceiling of the car. It held him there, firm in its grasp, like a snake choking hislife out at the waist. Instinctively, he opened his mouth to gasp and began choking on the waterhe was engulfed in. On the top of the cliff, Johnny Silver was in the car now, and had the pedal slammed to the floor. It was his desperate attempt to get Snow out of the water. Had the rope not been wrappedaround a tree at the edge of the cliff, it would likely have cut Snow in half fifty feet below. Back in the water, Snow fumbled clumsily with the knife, sawing at the rope that was strainedrock-hard around his waist. His strength was failing him now - he’d been under the water forover a minute, though it felt like an hour to him. As he sawed at the rope, he couldn’t feel theslashes that he was making in his own abdomen. A moment later, he was free. The rope snapped violently, allowing Snow to sink to the bottomof the car, where his head cracked off the steering wheel. He was bleeding from more partsthan he was aware of now. Finally, his rationality slammed back into his head, and he was able to spot the sunroof at the topof his car. Free to move about on his own, he pulled himself up through the narrow opening,well less than half a dozen feet below the surface of the lake. With scampering feet, he was able to push himself onto the roof of the submerged vehicle, andstood straight up. The moment his face burst into the oxygen, his lungs opened and he found himself gasping andchoking on the water - trying desperately to suck air into his deprived chest. He can hear therevving car engine faintly in the distance as he sees the rope slip out of the water and hurry upthe side of the cliff. “Fucking moron,” Snow whispers under his breath. *** Location: New York City StreetsDate: November 26, 2002Start of Scene Time: 2:05Shot: Johnny “Silver” Rosalini’s Car - Interior “How the fuck was I supposed to know you were caught down?” Johnny asked when they got tothe car - Snow was soaking in the passenger seat. “Well, after I didn’t fucking come up when you pulled on the rope, I thought you might take thehint!” Snow wasn’t happy about the situation, though he didn’t quite feel angry about it either. “I thought your ankle might have got stuck,” Johnny shrugged, not sounding terribly concerned. “So I gave a little extra tug.” “You almost ripped the transmission out of the fucking car!” Snow barked. Johnny had no response to that. Some time later, Johnny spoke up again: “Today was a lesson for you, kid,” he said withouttaking his eyes from the rode. They were heading back to New York now, where Johnny woulddrop Snow off at the hotel while he went to report to his superiors. “What happened to Georgetoday... that can be any fucking one of us. That can be you twenty years from now.” Snow looked over at the silver-topped aging man. He knew immediately that Johnny wasattempting to share genuine wisdom, though he couldn’t help but feel the timing was off -considering the fact that he was still drenched from his near-drowning. “This ain’t a fucking game your getting into, kid,” Johnny said. “It ain’t much fun most of thetime neither. People don’t get fired in this business. What you just did to George was as closeto firing as we do. So you’ve got to do some thinking now, kid. You’re already in deep, butpretty soon, considering how fast the Don’s got you moving, there ain’t going to be no gettingout for you. Just like their ain’t no getting out for me. Just like there wasn’t no getting out forGeorge.” Snow knew this all along, though he couldn’t help but feel a chill. “Tomorrow morning, I’m going call your hotel room,” Johnny said, finally looking over at hiscompanion. “Now, if you ain’t there, I’ll assume you flew back to Canada and we won’t callyou or no shit like that. If that’s the case, then I’ll trust that you’ll keep your mouth shut aboutcertain things you know. Then again, if your there in your suite waiting for the phone to ring,I’ll know that the Hattoni family has your loyalty.” Snow stared out his passenger side window. *** Location: ---Date: November 26, 2002Start of Scene Time: 7:25 pmShot: Blackness “Hello,” Raven answered the phone, hundreds of miles away in Canada. “Hey,” Snow said with a sigh. “It’s about time you called,” she said. He knew it was coming. “Listen,” he sighed, “I want you to take Haley and Krista and catch a flight to New York as soonas you can. I think I’m going to take a look around for a little place down here.” He hated NewYork and she knew it. He wasn’t a fan of American life in general. She assumed it was becauseof all the lawsuits. That was part of it, he thought.
