Date: Fuck you Quo A television camera, a chair, and a Skitzo. Let it begin. Skitzo: Ok so things have been quiet for a while. Skitzo: Quiet? That shithead hasn’t come up with any decent material since we fucked him over way back when! Skitzo: True…so it comes down to a war of words. Seems to me that Statuz Quo can’t hang with a white boy, seems to me that Statuz Quo got nothing left in him! There’s one nigga that’s running low on gas, his time has passed, there’s nothing left for Quo, see I don’t even have to try these days to flow! Skitzo: My name is Skitzo. I’m a wrestler. I’m a cracka and I’m one of the bitches Quo claims to own in his conquest of PCW. Well fuck, if taking over PCW means staying in the low card for years then we may as well be calling Bob Vurdo the fucking president. Quo you best be watching this because it’s all going out to you. You’ve ousted yourself from ‘society’ as it were; you’re a fucking disgrace to niggaz everywhere. The next hood you’re walking into is a ’69 Chevy comin’ 100mph towards you with the KKK at the wheel. What? Racism doesn’t affect you, oh yeah I forgot-… Skitzo: Man don’t be hatin’ on a nigga man that shit’s cold! Skitzo: Shut up, that “shit” is the PCW roster spot Quo is taking up. I’ve seen more talent from an NTICWer than this cunt has. Statuz Quo, the biggest baddest blackest muthafucka walking, and the sumbitch can’t even break the tag division never mind out-rap a white boy schizoid. Quo you’ve got nothing left son. You love getting a rise out of people by calling them a ‘cracka’, or saying ‘nigger’, well fuck me break out the handbags we got a protest coming this way! Shit, get on board here; you’re making less than $25 a week you suck so much, and even less when you wrestle in PCW! Skitzo: You’re serious aren’t you? Skitzo: No I’m fucking bored, last week Pete! And I had a good laugh about this little son of a bitch, how he thinks he’s higher than everyone else when really he’s lower than his $2 whore of a mother on a Saturday evening on the Boulevard. Everybody hates him, nobody wants him, and my stock is dropping every day because the cunt has turned something into nothing. I’m stuck here fighting a mouth, and a dry one at that. I’ve heard more controversial shit spewed out the ass of a nun after Father Frilly fucks her four ways from the Church of Christ! Skitzo: ……… Skitzo: Statuz Quo: The man, the myth…the piece of shit that can’t string together two insults without sending the nation to sleep. He’s lost it. His crew don’t have it. Everybody is right, Dante is the only one to have made something out of himself, while Quo is stuck trying to make playground names up for everyone, Khadaffi thinks he’s a porn star when everybody knows his only fuck was a Dyson straight out the store. Duzza, where the hell is he? Playaz Inc. is dead, and Quo’s just another blip coming through the crosshairs. Quo, you’re gonna get picked off, you’ve run out of material, you’re spouting the same old ‘LOL LOL LOL cracka bitch ho I ownz ya ass, repn ATL cracka!’. Your wrestling sucks as well, just go back on welfare and go die in the gutter you call the ghetto. I fucking own you Quo, and I’m Skitzo. You know what that means? Skitzo: It means you’re one cunt hair away from hater’s hell, so get fucking used to it, brutha… The End
