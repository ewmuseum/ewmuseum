The Best Cure for a Loss: PINK! Ice Cream!

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 2, 2002 A hot Chicago sun shone through the PINK! curtains of The Heartbreakers' bedroom. (Yes, they were sleeping in the same room. No you perverts, it was because Jack fell asleep on the futon...yeah, that's it.) Jack turned over in bed, ignoring the sun's call to wake up. Not to be ignored, it shone into a mirror standing on the right side of Valentine's futon, reflecting a bright glare onto his sleepy eyes. Jack Valentine: 'Okay, I'm awake.' He said groggily, sitting up. Dissatisfied with his acknowledgement of the new day, the sun dispersed it's reflection, leaving Jack to rub his stunned eyes. Though he wanted to move the mirror, the glare served as a useful alarm clock, letting him look at his beautiful face. Sitting up in bed, he began fixing his brown hair in the small mirror, talking to the reflection as to a familiar friend. Jack Valentine: 'I can't believe those Pretty Cool idiots tricked me. I lost the match for us.' Johnny Heart moaned and groaned in his bed at the sound of Valentine's voice. Not waiting for a reply, Valentine continued, Jack Valentine: 'At least we got revenge in the end. This Friday's our chance for redemption, this Friday is my chance to make it up to you Johnny.' Johnny's face fell a little, but brightened upon the next thought of becoming #1 contenders to the PCW Tag Team Titles. Johnny Heart: (Moaning) 'It's quite alright Jack...it was my fault to for not being there for you. I should've been there. I'm a bad partner and a bad friend.' Heart added quickly, as if to try to console Jack for being put through the table first. Jack Valentine: 'No, no...it was me who was put through the table. I let down you, I let down our fans, and I let down the PINK! That match was ours for the taking, and I BLEW IT!' Johnny Heart: 'Nobody BLEW anything...it was nobody's fault. Besides, those Pretty Cool idiots put us through a table too.' Jack Valentine: 'Yeah, ever since they decided to get selfish and kick us out of the band, they've been nothing but a stick up my ass.' Jack and Johnny snicker at the reference. Johnny Heart: 'Mine too...I wish I could pull that stick out of my ass and shove it straight up theirs!' Jack Valentine: (Laughing in agreement) 'Ha. Hell yeah!' The picture haunted Jack Valentine all through the night. Although the pair seemed to be over the fact that they 'BLEW,' in their own words, in that match, the thoughts of the loss still plagued their PINK! little minds. Jack ran his finger along it's edges of the futon lovingly, recollecting what exactly happened in that match. The Pretty Cool Guys turn their backs to Heart, already thinking that he’s gone over the top rope. Johnny pulls himself back up to the apron and grabs hold of Moore’s head! He nails Moore with a hard right hand, dropping Moore onto the canvas. Moore then grabs Sherwin, spins him around, and DRIVES HIS FACE ONTO THE ROPES!!! Sherwin turns around and straddles the ropes, trying to clear his head. Duke: This looks like the end of the road for the Pretty Cool Fellers!!! Jack Valentine rushes towards Sherwin as Moore watches from the outside. VALENTINE GOES TO CLOTHESLINE SHERWIN, BUT THE SNEAKY COOL GUY DUCKS THE ATTACK, TURNS AND CLOTHESLINES A CONFUSED VALENTINE OVER THE TOP ROPE AND THROUGH A TABLE!!! CRASH!!! ***THE HEARTBREAKERS HAVE BEEN ELIMINATED!!! Duke: Great Horny Toads!!! Dave: The Previous PCW Tag Team Champs have lost their chance to start the new era of PCW as champions!!! Moore, adding even more insult to the loss, DROPKICKS A STUNNED HEART OFF THE APRON AND ONTO VALENTINE!!! THE TWO HEARTBREAKERS LAY ON THE TABLE BATTERED AND BRUISED!!! Dave: Awww, just like two gay lovers… Jeff and Duke look at Kern strangely. Dave: I mean, Awww, that really fucking sucks!!! Jeff: (Nodding) Score one for the bad guys!!! HA HA HA!!! Johnny and Jack smile together. Jack Valentine: 'Well, at least we had fun while fighting.' Johnny Heart: (Smiling at the ambiguously gay reference, and responding in a day-dream type voice) 'Yeah...' Quietly, Jack put away the futon and placed her mirror on the window's ledge. (No you sick FREAK! They didn't sleep in the same bed...pervert!) Valentine tiptoed to the kitchen, and knelt next to a small stove with one burner. It stood solemnly beside a tiny refrigerator that sat on the tile floor. Unstacking some containers in the corner, he pulled out a pint of ice cream. He grabbed the container and tiptoed back up to the room, with two spoons in hand. He walked in the room with the ice cream and sat down on the bed Indian-style, next to Johnny. Johnny Heart: 'What is this?' Jack Valentine: 'The perfect remedy for a hard fought battle.' Johnny Heart: 'What flavor?' Jack Valentine: 'What else would it be...strawberry!' Johnny Heart: 'Ah...because it's...' Jack Valentine: (Finishing the sentence) 'PINK!' The Heartbreakers engaged in spooning....the ice cream DAMNIT! The ICE CREAM! How many times must I, the poor narrator explain to you...they aren’t gay...just close, um, yeah...that’s it. This Friday, the Heartbreakers had their chance for redemption, against the team that eliminated them, and the team that has been running around making false claims about being better than both The Heartbreakers and Pretty Cool Guys, Cowabunga Connection. In one of the biggest tag team matches PCW has seen in quite some time, these 3 teams will go head to head for the rights to face Chef & Parshin, the newly crowned champs at a later date. Jack Valentine: “I can’t believe those camel-jockeys won the match, how lucky are they?” Johnny Heart: “No need to bring race or color into this...they won because they cheated...after all, that’s the only way anyone can defeat the PINK! anyways...” Jack Valentine: “Ah yes my very good friend...ah yes.” He means very good friend...that’s IT! Come on! Give them a break! Jack Valentine: “Allah? Allah? He is nothing compared to the man who created the color PINK! The man who decided to mix RED and WHITE. The greatest man of all.” Johnny Heart: “What was his name again?” Jack Valentine: (Sighing) “Not important...what is important is that we know that he is better than Allah.” Johnny Heart: “Oh...ok.” Near the end of the match, when it was just Cowabunga Connection, Pretty Cool Guys, Politically Incorrect and Chef & Parshin...The Heartbreakers decided to make their presence felt down at ringside, even after they were eliminated. They took it upon themselves to put Ryan Moore through a table, and exchange punches with Cowabunga Connection. Jack Valentine: “...and Cowabunga Connection...come on. You would think Harry could get up and get back in the ring.” Johnny Heart: “A real champion would have gotten back in the ring.” Jack Valentine: “But hey, that’s not his fault. Again, I blame the Pretty Cool idiots for cheating their way out of that situation. They weren’t even involved in the match. That’s why this Friday, PCG will get a taste of their own medicine...at the hands of The Heartbreakers and Cowabunga Connection!” Johnny Heart: (Confused) “You mean you want to work on...erm...with them?” Jack Valentine: (Smiling) “Exactly.” A simple slip. Don’t call it a Freudian slip either...anyone confuses ‘on’ with ‘with,’ don’t they? Johnny Heart: “But we were brawling with them just yesterday....you want to forgive them that easily.” Jack Valentine: “I’ll forgive anybody as long as they help me get the back of the Pretty Cool Guys.” Jack probably just added the extra ‘the’ by mistake...right? Sure he did. Johnny Heart: “Let’s get back to this ice cream...we’ll worry about the match more later this week.” Jack Valentine: “Good idea...this ice cream is great, because it’s like us...ALL ABOUT THE PINK!” The scene fades as I, the narrator, am at a loss for words. Draw your own conclusions about Jack and Johnny, but I will stick to the fact that they aren’t homosexuals...they are just comfortable with their feminine side. As this is said, Jack and Johnny both dig into the ice cream like 12-Year old girls, laughing and giggling at one another. The scene fades.
