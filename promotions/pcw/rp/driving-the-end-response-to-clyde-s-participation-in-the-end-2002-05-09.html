Driving The End (Response to Clyde's Participation in the Endorsement Match; knock-off of his 'Driver's Ed' RP from a while back)

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/09/02 The seat in the silver Ford Navigator is very comfortable. A man of my height can sink down into it and have plenty of legroom. The A/C’s at medium blast, and the XM radio station is on Urban Adult, with “Lonely Girl” by DJ Rogers, Jr. playing not too loudly. Driving along the road’s a little bumpy. Probably hasn’t been serviced in a while. My driver handles each bump as best he can. Who’s my driver? Bobby Stormbringer. I can only guess what he’s wearing, but I know for sure that it’s his same ring outfit that he wore last time he wrestled in PCW. Knowing him, he’d like to pretend like he’s not on hiatus, and wearing his black trenchcoat, black shirt with the blue storm-globe emblem in the center, and those black pants. “So, why am I here?” Stormbringer asks. I reply sarcastically with, “Everyone asks that question about themselves. You’re so smart, why haven’t you figured it out yet?” Stormbringer gets easily agitated sometimes; this is one of those times. “No,” he bursts, “why am I here in this RP?” What, you were expecting anything less than many breaks of kayfabe with Stormbringer and myself in the same RP, in the same car? Foolish… “Oh, that,” I answer. “Well, truth be told, your handler allowed you to be featured in any RP that my handler does, until your handler makes his return to action.” “What? You’ve gotta be kidding!” “You wouldn’t be here otherwise.” Stormbringer exhales heavily and pounds on the steering wheel with his right fist. I turn the A/C on to cool down the air, and Stormbringer as well. The previous song ends, and Kelli Mack’s “Keep It Real” is played. The track’s got a nice beat, but I’ve heard better singers. Stormbringer doesn’t seem to like this station, and changes the XM radio to Hard Rock. Cold’s “Just Got Wicked” is fading out, and “Inside Out” by Reveille, a band whose lead singer sounds like Mike Shinoda of Linkin Park (except that his lyrics don’t suck), replaces the track. Stormbringer’s breathing is slowed and calmed. Seems he is easiest to deal with when his kind of music is playing. “Still, there’s gotta be a specific reason as to why I’m in this particular RP. What purpose do I serve?” Silly questions. He doesn’t bother to think them through sometimes. Still, I can’t leave him hanging; we’ve got to move along with the RP. “First of all, unless you haven’t noticed, I’m blind, and you’re driving the car. Otherwise, you’re here to give me someone other than the handler to talk to.” Stormbringer continues with the pointless questions. “Then what are they doing here?” Stormbringer asks, referring to The Misfits and Juju the Albino Iguana; Lamar the Leper, Richard the Scruffy-Bearded Man, and Slade the Terminal Sleepwalker sit in the backseats (Drek Redaxe and Juju sit in the trunk to save space). I’d be lying if I said I know what they’re wearing. Let’s just say that they’re clothed, and use your imagination as to what they’re wearing. “They are here because they haven’t been in any of my RPs lately and we don’t want them to be forgotten.” The Misfits break in with their enthusiastic affirmations. “Yeah!” Lamar says. “Richard agrees;” I have to get him out of that habit of referring to himself in the third-person. “Aye!” Cries Drek from the trunk. A low, but still audible, “Wonk!” comes from Juju. Slade mumbles something like, “Ioainfaindpudnc.” I seriously need to use some of my income to pay for speech lessons for him. Or maybe I can just get Stormbringer to do it. That would be material for an RP. Though it’s relevance to anything would be dodgy at best. Nevermind. Stormbringer tosses another speech block into the RP with, “So what’s he doing here?” “You must mean Al Roker in the back by Slade?” I reply with a question of my own. “Yeah” “Well, to be honest with you, I’ve no clue what he’s doing here.” Roker talks, making his time in this RP all the less enjoyable. “My legs hurt.” Slade and Richard open the side passenger door and toss Roker out onto the open road. “So that’s their function in this RP,” says Stormbringer. Oh, this is my time to mention that if anyone’s going to the PWA or just looking for a new gimmick, I will be happy to loan you Richard, Slade, or Lamar to use. Juju would be a manager. Contact me through my information in my bio (the link is at the bottom of the page). To break up my last thought, Stormbringer asks one more question; this one, however, is easily sarcastic. He wants to say it, so I don’t stop him, though from his ninth word I can tell this is going to be a stupid question. “So, I’m driving you around. Is this some twisted Driving Miss Daisy thing where I’m Morgan Freeman and you’re Jessica Tandy?” Stormbringer follows his question with a half-held in snicker, and then a chuckle. The Misfits, though self-declared marks of mine, laugh as well. I give Stormbringer credit for the comparison, not that I fine it all that amusing (it is, but someone has to be the comedic straight-man in the car). “Well, Stormy,” I jokingly address him in response to his ribbing question, “this RP is supposed to lead into the next one, where I’ll be meeting with the Fanta people to discuss becoming their official spokesperson, as well as making fun of Juan Borro; the next RP is meant to be targeted at him. The last one was targeted toward Darkstar.” Stormbringer keeps asking questions. Don’t worry; he’s only got three left before we end this section of the RP. “Toward whom is this RP targeted at?” Stormbringer inquires, perhaps truly interested, but I can’t tell. “Clyde,” is my answer. “He posted an RP some weeks ago that featured him attempting to give Bethany driving lessons. Driver’s Ed was the name of that particular RP.” “But I know how to drive,” is Stormbringer’s response. The radio station changes the track to “Broken” by Bruce Dickinson. Stormbringer must not like this artist; he switches around until we land on Zakk Wylde’s Black Label Society with “Bleed For Me.” I remind him that: “We all know you’re able to operate a motor vehicle, but this is first a response to Clyde’s participation in the Molsson Soft Drink Endorsement Match.” “I heard Clyde’s representing Poison Sobe,” Stormbringer says with a laugh, that Clyde would want to be involved in anything even remotely commercial. “Didn’t you ask to represent sulfuric acid at first?” “Yes, but it’s not considered a drink by any standards. So I chose Fanta, it’s the closest thing to sulfuric acid on the market.” “Ouch…” is all Stormbringer says as he chuckles at my comment. “And secondly, we have to get into an accident. It’ll be happening very soon.” “What?” Stormbringer asks, something I said able to jar him from his personable mood listening to hard rock. “How do you know that?” “Simple,” I say, bracing for an impact. “I read the script for this RP. You might want to hit your brakes now.” Stormbringer exclaims “WHOA!” as the car that shall leads us into Act 2 of this RP rams full speed into the driver’s side of the Navigator. Take a break, and then read on {This commercial is dedicated to Clyde} Bobby Stormbringer sits on a Jennifer black leather sofa in Rooms to Go. Stormbringer has his long red hair tied back behind his head, and wears tattered jeans and an open brown shirt. He faces the camera and speaks to the viewer. Stormbringer:“Hello, my name is Clyde, and I am here to tell you about a fantastic new opportunity. Have you ever wanted lots of money? Fancy cars? A big house? Droves of beautiful women that you have to beat away with a stick? If so, then you must take advantage of my new program. “It’s simple! You stay at home 28 days out of the month, and only do your J-O-B just enough to convince people that you’re still alive. It’s really easy! All you have to do is… lie!” Stormbringer shifts himself forward in the chair. In the background, blurry shapes – people – stand behind the camera watching the infomercial. “I would have you believe that I’m a terrible person that has an 8/9-year-old sex slave, and that I routinely kill people. Actually, I enjoy the works of Dr. Seuss, and nothing could make me feel better than a nice stroll through a meadow of flowers. That’s one type of lie. “Another type of lie would be to say that you’re rich beyond your wildest dreams. I’ve done it, and you can do it too!” The crowd gets bigger. Stormbringer takes off his sunglasses. “I mean, seriously, if you tell someone that you made $10 thousand sitting at home last month, who’s going to know? If you say that you’ve got timeshares in Europe, there’s no way that they can check on that. If you walk up to someone in the mall, point outside to a really expensive car and say it’s yours, how are they going to know it’s not? ”Exactly, they can’t! Using Clyde’s Patented Pretended Life Program, you too can fake your own life in any way possible. You want to be a movie star? Say that you were in independent and foreign films. You want to be a singer? Dub karaoke over your actual tapes. Want to be a politician? Lie about everything; you’re almost there! The noise from the people behind Stormbringer makes him speak a little louder. “You can lie about everything and no one will know! You can do it! Make a difference! Make a change! Lie!” As Stormbringer finishes his last sentence, two burly gray-uniformed security guards with shaved heads walk into the shot from the left, grab Stormbringer, and drag him off camera. “Don’t delay! Start today!” {The preceding was a commercial dedicated to Clyde.} “WHOA!” Stormbringer slams on the breaks and skids away to the right, but it’s too little, too late as the other car crashes into us on Stormbringer’s side. Our car isn’t flipped, but it must have spun 10 times before stopping. The impact trips the other car’s alarm. The distance from the alarm to our car must be a good 20 feet. The smell of the air is thick with smoke. It turns out that the engine in our Navigator is crushed in, and part it penetrated the driver’s dash area. Luckily, Stormbringer wasn’t hurt. The Misfits in the back are shaken, but seem okay. Can’t smell any blood or anything, and though their breathing is panicked, there’s no fear of death after such a crash. I reach over and turn off our engine, just in case. Getting out of our car, I feel the sun coming out from beyond the horizon. I can only imagine what beautiful sunrise I no longer have the pleasure of experiencing with my eyes. That’ll have to wait, though. There’s an accident to attend to. Walking over to the other car, I can hear the young man’s muttered curses. He sounds young, probably not old enough to drink yet. His voice makes him sound like Spiccoli from Fast Times at Ridgemont High. Why don’t we just refer to him as Spiccoli from now on? 'Are you alright, sir?' I ask approaching the car from the front, the smoke getting in my nostrils and making me cough. Spiccoli doesn’t seem to care about his own health, just that his car is ruined. 'What does it matter? Look at my car!” Spiccoli spurts “Damn!” and bangs on the wheel, getting out of the car. From his steps, he moves in front of the car to inspect the damage. I step to the side to allow him space. 'Look what you did to my car!” shouts Spiccoli with fright, most likely about the consequences of his crash. “What are you, blind?' Ah, I hate it when people ask that question. Of course, I can’t lie to them. 'Well, yes.' Obviously, Spiccoli is taken aback by this, as all I can make out from him is, '...?' Spiccoli goes back to whimpering over his car, whining, 'Aw, man, my mom's gonna kill me!' I start playing with Spiccoli’s head in his vulnerable state. 'Hey, dude, chill,” I start off. “I mean, look. We were in a baaad accident. Our cars are totaled. Look at it, we're lucky to be alive!' I can tell my words are getting to him. Spiccoli:'Yeah....' 'So, tell you what. I have some vodka in my trunk. What say we split it?” He’s a little unsure at first, but eventually he comes around to the idea. 'Uh... sure.' So, I go around to my trunk and open it. Before I touch the bottle, I reach into my left pocket and pull out a pair of gloves that cover my hands completely. Checking for the bottle, it is undamaged. Good. I retrieve the vodka and take it over to Spiccoli. 'Lemme go check on my friend,” I say. “You can get started without me. Get some stress off your shoulders.” Spiccoli gets into the bottle, gulping down the potato-based drink trying to get his mind off explaining to his mother about her wrecked car from his mumblings. I go back over to the passenger side of the Navigator, where Stormbringer and The Misfits are sitting at the side of the road. I hear clicking every now and then, so I figure we’re at an intersection. Must be in a part of town that’s not trafficked much at this time of morning. It’s about 7:15 AM; people would be going to work about now. Stormbringer is talking into his cell phone, almost finished with his conversation with whomever it is he’s talking to. “Yeah, we’re at the intersection of Hearst and Cardinal. Yeah, we’re not going anywhere any time soon. Just hurry up and send somebody. Bye.” Stormbringer ends his call with a press of the ‘End’ button and closes up the phone, slipping it into his right lower jacket pocket. “I just got off the phone with the police,” he calls to me as I approach. “They’ll be here in about five minutes.” I point with my right hand back at Spiccoli. “He’s alright. I just got us out of a ticket or anything.” Stormbringer must look over at Spiccoli, because he asks, “What’s that in his hand?” “Seventy ounces of 151-proof vodka. That’s enough to get anyone drunk.” Stormbringer smirks. “Mischievous bastid. Police will do a quick DUI arrest, and if we file a quick report here, do the rest later, and don’t file charges, it won’t take up much time. Wait, if he says that we gave him the bottle, and they give him a polygraph…” I stop Stormbringer there. “No need to worry,” I say. “Polygraphs have a wide enough margin for error that they can’t be considered conclusive evidence. Besides, there’s none spilled in the car, and I don’t have any prints on it.” Another smirk from Stormbringer precedes, “I guess you thought of everything.” Stormbringer takes his cell phone out and opens it, dialing numbers. “I’m gonna call Kira and let her know what happened. She’ll probably hear about this on the news and get worried.” “Fine,” I shoot back before turning my attention to the Misfits. “Are you all alright?” They answer, “Yes,” in unison, though in their own ways. Lamar tugs at my pant leg from his sitting position. “Yes, Lamar?” I address him. He’s a child in mental capacity, so I talk to him as such. Lamar stands up, hopping, and leans in close to my right ear (as much as he can with the eight-inch height difference) and whispers, “I hafta go to the bathroom!” “I told you to go before we left,” I admonish him. “Oh, well. Just go out there in the bushes; I’ll end the RP to take attention away from you.” I turn my speech to Juju. “Go with him, Juju.” Juju responds with “Wonk.” As Juju accompanies Lamar to the bathroom, I walk back over to Spiccoli, who's already downed half the bottle. 'How are you?' I ask. 'Much better;” Spiccoli’s voice is already slightly slurred and relaxed. The drink is doing its work. “This drink helped a lot. I saved a little for you, dude.' I turn down the bottle. By now, my concerned tone changes to a mocking one, as the joke is now over. 'What are you, crazy? I don't drink that stuff. I'm just waiting for the police to show up now. Thanks, dumbass. I can't believe you fell for that.' Spiccoli's not completely drunk yet, but by now he's definitely got a blood alcohol well over 1.0. Realizing his mistake, he goes back to his car with the bottle and drinks the rest of it, knowing he's already in over his head. 'Foolish man. I almost feel sorry for him... Almost....' Sirens in the background... the police approach. I know that this encounter will go well for me. But what about the Molsson Endorsement Match? Meh. I’ll win. I know I will.
