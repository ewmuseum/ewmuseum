Date: not entered “…” The room was silent…dead silent. In each corner of dark room stood a Celestial, looking out at the others with a solemn face. There was nothing the dark room…save a spot of light shining down from above like a fluorescent sun and pinpointing in the middle of the room. Eyes shifted from one another…there was anger afoot. Brooding…silent…rage… Let’s look back to Turmoil… Tomas…Teresa Tomas…some low level wench…decided to put her business where it didn’t belong. She ran out with sledgehammer and dazed Cyclonica long enough to allow a defeated Eddie Mandrake the time to make a pin. She stole the Divine Prophetess’s child…she is responsible for taking what belonged to the Celestial family. She would be the one to pay for it. After all of that went down, backstage wasn’t a pleasant place to be… =©= The room shook with the force crashing into its walls from inside. Past the door reading TRAINER, the Divine Prophetess lay stretched out on the table, her hands folded across her stomach as unconsciousness sung through her body. As the trainer tried to revive her with smelling salts, Anarchy watched with worried eyes. Her father was pacing back and forth across the room, looking more pissed than pleased. No one touches the daughter of Akiyama…no one gets away with it either. The crater left by the god’s fist in the brick walled room left chips on the ground as Akiyama clenched and unclenched his hands, growling lowly trying to contain the anger himself. Anarchy reached over and held Cyclonica’s hand as the trainer leaned back and shrugged his shoulders. “She’s not snapping out of it.” he said hopelessly. “I’m thinking she may be out for a long time.” Anarchy sighed as she looked down at her sister’s sleeping face. Even in slumber, she looked devious, her brow furrowed and her mouth curled down into a frown, but still managed to maintain her cold beauty. Behind her, Akiyama put his foot down on the entire business…through a steel chair. The seat came clear off as he booted it across the room, leaving a dent in the door. The trainer backed off as Anarchy shook her head slowly. “Come on…wake up.” She goaded. But Divine Prophetess wasn’t budging. “Who did this to her anyway?” the trainer asked, holding his phone to call paramedics in to take Cyclonica to the hospital. Anarchy looked up with a response. “Teresa Tomas.” Just then, Cyclonica sat straight up on the table, her eyes flipping open to reveal an icy blue insanity. “B!tch gonna pay.” Rumble and tumble came out of the room, and the door opened abruptly. Anarchy came backing out of the room into the hallway, her frame pushing against someone who was making their way out. Her boots slide across the floor, proving to be not enough to stop Cyclonica from lurking out of the room with her hair tangled around her face, and her eyes glaring outwards in a trance. “What are you gonna do?? Kill Tomas?” Anarchy asked, trying to strike some reason into her sister. “B!tch gonna pay!” Cyclonica bellowed that before throwing Anarchy aside with an overhead Judo throw and stormed off down the hallway. Anarchy looked up from the ground, finding Akiyama standing there in the room with a surprised look on his face. Looks like daddy’s little girl was a chip off the old block when it came to insane rage. Anarchy jumped up and raced after Cyclonica. She rounded the corner and down the next hall from there. Making a left, she found Cal Seaver and Tiffany Jennings standing off to the side with scared looks on their faces. “Have you seen my sister?” she asked them both in a hurry. “You mean the Neo Void Goddess??” Cal said with a sarcastic tone of fear. “She just came by here looking just like her father!” Tiffany nodded. “I asked what happened, and she said--.” “B!tch gonna pay?” Anarchy beat her to the punch line. She nodded with a shrug of her shoulders. “Which way did she do?” Tiffany pointed her towards the end of the hallway. Leaving them, Anarchy turned the corner and found Cyclonica standing in front of a door marked for the person she searched for… T O M A S “CYCLONICA!” her name did nothing to stop her in booting the door in. She stormed forward…but her frame was forced back out as Akiyama stepped through the doorway! Anarchy was surprised, though Cyclonica didn’t look to pleased. The two glared at each other silently…neither one of them moving an inch. Anarchy watched her father and sister, not knowing what was going on. Then the insane look from Cyclonica’s face melted, as a twisted smile came on her face. “Father…that’s just Divine.” Anarchy walked up to the two of them looking confused. “What’s going on?” Cyclonica smiled at her sister, placing an arm around her. “Father is going to get us a new toy to play with…” =©= And that’s exactly what he did… But this toy wasn’t anything to play with…this item was here only to bring the destruction to the mortals that wished to defy their will. This toy, in fact…was no toy at all…but a w e a p o n. Tomas liked weapons…they were the only way she could do anything damaging…so maybe Cyclonica would show her the weapon that her daddy got for her…up close and personal. “Are we going to stand here all night glaring at each other?” Cyclonica shot a hard look at Anarchy quickly. “You’re interrupting business here.” “What are you talking about?” she replied, trying to make sense of everything. “You’re just staring at each other.” “What looks like staring is actually dialogue…between me, our father, and the weapon here.” Anarchy looked at Shin Yagyuu when her sister referred to him as “the weapon”. “I’m not too fond of you guys taking advantage of speaking like that!” she said, pouting and folding her arms. “You know I can’t speak that language.” “Do you want to know what we’re talking about, Ana?” the Divine Prophetess gave her an frustrated look as she put her hands on her hips. Anarchy nodded quickly, her own infuriated look coming upon her face. “I most certainly do!” Cyclonica gave her a slow, evil grin. Anarchy knew that look…it was the look of wickedness brewing…it was the look of violence falling upon someone… It was the look of chaos about to unfold. “Soon…maybe Friday…maybe Turmoil next week…PCW is going to be hit from the darkness…and they wont know what hit them.” What did the Celestials have in store for everyone? Wouldn’t you like to know…
