The New Saga: A Plain Morning.

9 July 2002

Is that what it is to you now. Messiah, Crucifixtion, Desire. False. Prophets. I can't feel my feet. Shake your head Drazail, you are home again I was already home, I was laying in bed with Talena, we were happy. Thats home now. This is your home Drazail, this is your home always. No. Yes. Brilliant arguement. ...shut up. I don't want to be here. You are always here, you know that. There is NOTHING here. There is everything here. Your riddles bore me. This is not my realm, this is neither of my planes. I do not care to stay here any longer. You have no choice. Actually I do. ...shut up. You are such an idiot. Leave me be. Lexian is standing in the light. So? The good are not my problem. Life is my problem. The existence of human beings are my problem. The way they live breathe and talk and worship and believe and... ugh. I hate them all. But Lexian is your adversary I have nothing against him. You need your priorities straight. And you need a fucking light in here. Its your head. Whatever. Come back to us Drazail. No. I'm happy here. Bloodshed and carnage.... I can do all that here. With my love by my side. I don't need insanity again. You are already insane. Am not. Are too Am not See, you talked like one of us No I didn't, you are hearing things. They want to Crucify you. I am not the Messiah. I have never claimed it. Let them bleed Lexans wounds not mine. You need not be the Messiah, you have always been a martyr. I have no cause. It would make it so much easier Drazail No. Then die. ... ...What? This is why I don't like you. My other personalities arent complete jokes. I am not a joke! Sure you are. You don't realize that all this is bigger then a blood lust. This is life. This is the battle of dark vs light. This is what God and Luci and Buddha and all of them have talked about, this is what happens when the side of good mixes with the side of darkness. Especially when darkness is in control for once. But... NO! I came into this without you're help and I will leave without you're help. This is MORE THEN BLOOD. THIS IS MORE THEN CARNAGE. THIS IS LIFE! Then live Drazail, then take your life and roll it into a ball and put it into your big safe pockets and cherish it for as long as you can. I plan on it brain. I hope you are fucking happy. You know. When I look back down to the shining light with the perfect black hair and the slight touch of you peoples insanity, I realize that I am truely happy. Leave me alone. You brought me here. Drazail, this is bigger then blood, yes it is, but it is bigger then you. This is the battle of the White and the Black hats. The False Messiah is in play and he sits poised to use everything he has against you to destroy you. You sit and pretend your happy and that you have what it takes but you don't realize the magnitude of your desicions... You were right Drazail.... This is you're head. And this is your life... Now LIVE.
