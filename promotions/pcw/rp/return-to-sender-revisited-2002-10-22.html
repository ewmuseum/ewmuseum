Return to Sender: Revisited

22 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.22.02 The abandoned Love Box warehouse, South Chicago… -Twisting and turning uncomfortably amid a tangle of bed sheets, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins moans, his mind running through the torturous events of some random dream. Shifting constantly, Rollins sinks deeper into his subconscious, vividly reliving a time not so long ago…- ……… -…A steady stream of hazy sunlight filters in through the cracked skylights, early-morning dust motes floating lazily in their wake. The sounds of the busy city outside seem so far away within the confines of this dwelling, their obnoxious horns and sirens almost inaudible, as if from a dream. Panning slowly, the camera comes down from the half-broken skylight, running along a steel support girder just underneath. The remnants of decades of cobwebs fill every inch, but the sparkling sunlight causes them to glitter with an inner light; the sparkle makes the cold steel look something like the surface of a still pond. Continuing to pan left, the camera passes an old bird’s nest, followed by a pair of black gloved hands, followed by another tangle of cobwebs…- -The camera pauses. Quickly darting back across the cobwebs, two strained gloved fists fill the screen, clutching the edge of the steel girder like an immovable vice. Panning back, the entire body of Rich “The Renegade” Rollins, his bare chest exposed and slick with sweat, is revealed… hanging nearly forty feet off the ground. Clutching the steel support beam, The Renegade pulls himself to chin-level with the hard metal, then lowers. Again he pulls up, the muscles in his arms bulging with intense strain. Again. Again. A look of unfathomable determination has taken hold of Rich Rollins’ face as he continues the push-ups, his legs hanging rigid with a three story drop below them. With one final grunt, Rollins swings one leg up and over the dusty support, hoisting himself to a crouching position on top. The bright beams of sunlight cast no shadows across his face; indeed, no shadow could cover up such a picturesque display of satisfaction. Looking dead-on into the camera lens, Rich Rollins speaks.- Renegade: Some people get goin’ in the morning with a triple espresso, but I say screw it. They’re missin’ all the fuckin’ fun. -Sliding one hand across the webbed surface of the girder, The Renegade crawls across the support beam with a cat-like sense of balance, gently moving around the crossbars and V-supports. Finally reaching a fire escape ladder, he swings down a few rungs until latching on to the sides, his boots making a heavy clunking sound in the silent surroundings of the warehouse. Moving slowly, Rollins makes his way down the ladder rung by rung until his feet hit the hard pavement below. Wiping his hands on his worn blue jeans, he looks up at the rafters with a satisfied smile that spreads from ear to ear on his features. He speaks, his voice laden with enthusiasm.- Renegade: I’ve been called a lot of things in my career, some of them good… most ‘em not so good. Winner, loser, champion, jobber, main-eventer… whatever moniker I’ve carried with me to the ring, the word “quitter” has never once been mentioned. -Ringing through the open space with a startling clarity, a loud electronic bell dings three times, the final sound echoing off the concrete walls like a chorus of distorted chimes. A curious look plants itself upon Rich Rollins’ face as he turns, looking toward the giant loading dock door on the other side of the wide storage area.- Renegade: What the hell...? Unless that’s Lacey… I never get visitors. Unless… -The Renegade’s face suddenly freezes. A pale, fear-stricken look crosses his complexion, but is gone almost instantly as if it were never there. Shaking his head as if in denial, he regains his composure as the electronic buzzing fills the air once more. Moving quickly, Rollins sprints across the storage area and up to the massive loading door, his legs working as if they were weighted with sandbags. Hitting a small red button that hangs from a metal cord along the wall, a screeching blare screams from a warning horn as the door cycles upward, letting in a little more of the early-morning sunlight.- -A gentle breeze stirs The Renegade’s long brown hair as the opening widens, the sounds of the busy city flooding in without restrain. Standing upon the oil-stained concrete landing outside, a brown-clad mailman meets Rich Rollins’ wary glare with a glassy one of his own, the faint smell of cheap cologne exuding from his body. Reaching into his FedEx duffel slung casually over one shoulder, he produces two sealed white letters and extends them into The Renegade’s waiting hands.- Mailman: These are for you. If I could just have you sign here… -Removing a clipboard from a velcro tag on his uniform, the mailman hands it over to Rollins, who absently takes the attached pen and scribbles his name on the dotted line. Nodding in acknowledgment, the mailman turns around and trots off to his FedEx vehicle parked on the opposite side of the security mesh fence, an urgency in his step. Smacking the button to close the huge loading door, Rich Rollins carefully examines the letters as he walks back inside, turning them over in his hands. Tearing open the first envelope, he unfolds the letter within as he walks on, oblivious to everyone and everything around him. As he continues to read, a slow red hue fills his cheeks, a distant look entering his eyes. His fingers grip the paper tighter until it almost tears…- -Reaching an old manager’s office that now serves as his room, The Renegade stands outside the door, holding the second envelope in his hands. All color drains from his face as he stares at the cover, that distant look growing further and further within the vast reaches of his pupils. Without opening it, he lets both letters fall to the ground, and wordlessly enters his room. The door closes softly behind him.- -Sick with curiosity, the camera reaches down and plucks the opened letter off the cold floor, holding so it can be read by the lens: - Richard,I know how much you hate being called Richard. Deal with it. Mother and I received your latest letter, but we wouldn’t open it. We know you’d just try and offer us more money, but we don’t need the charity. We’ve been doing just fine since you ditched us, and are proud that at least we earned everything we have. This is the last time I call you “brother.” You are dead to me. Stop sending us your pity-mail. ~Jason -Setting the letter aside, the cameraman picks up the unopened envelope. Flipping it over, the large, red words “Return to Sender” are stamped plainly, the seal untouched. Tearing it open, the cameraman unfolds the enclosed paper, once again holding it so the lens can plainly make out the words: - Jason,I’m sorry. I’m so sorry. God, I can’t say that enough. I know how much you and mom have suffered… how much we all suffered… but all I ever tried to do was help. Despite how you see it, I wasn’t trying to buy your love. I care for the both of you so deeply that it’s almost too much to stand, too much to bear.So I’m coming home.I’ve accomplished everything I ever dreamed, Jason. I’m a successful wrestler, highly acclaimed and loved by hundreds of thousands of people all over the world. I’m even entered in this huge tournament, and I’m doing really well… but without my family, it isn’t worth it. At your word, I’ll tell my promoter to take me off the active roster and come home to treat you and mom the way you deserve.I’m not offering money. I’m offering myself. With love,~Rich -A quiet, murmured phrase escapes from the cameraman’s lips, so taken with emotion that it comes out a whisper.- Cameraman: Oh, my God… BANG! -The gunshot shatters the interior stillness instantly, the source somewhere within Rich Rollins’ room. Startled, and more than a little shaken, the cameraman frantically calls out to the man behind the door.- Cameraman: Rollins? Jesus Christ, Rich! God, please don’t tell me… Rich?! -Reaching for the doorknob, the cameraman curses as it refuses to turn. Taking a step back, he rams into the old wooden framework with a panic-driven rush of adrenaline, a cry of frustration emitting from his throat. With a horrible snap, the door gives way with an ominous crash, revealing a scene more than a little chilling.- -Standing erect, the still-smoking handgun clutched so tightly in his hand that his knuckles have turned white, Rich “The Renegade” Rollins stares at a poster on the far wall, a single tear falling down his cheek. Falling from eyes that are so distant, they seem dead. Panning over to the opposite wall, the object of The Renegade’s attention is made abundantly clear; a promotional poster for Black Sabbath hangs on the uneven white plaster wall… a bullet hole drilled straight through Ozzy Ozbourne’s forehead. The tone in Rollins’ voice drips with the acid of a man who has lost it all… a man who is already dead.- Renegade: This is all I have… all I have… and you can’t take it away. Wrestling is my life now, and no man… alive or dead… will stand in my way. No one. -As the view begins to become distorted, focused in on Rich Rollins’ face, a visage contorted with an inner anguish that words are unable to capture…- ……… -Jerking up in bed, Rich Rollins sucks in heavy amounts of air, his sweat-slicked face glistening in the pale moonlight that drifts in from a dusty window above his head. Rubbing his eyelids with his thumbs, The Renegade tries to shake the horrible memory from his consciousness, still too fresh to be forgotten. The damned Global Championship Wrestling camera crew had always been a thorn in his side, and the fact they had exposed such a personal event in his life rankled him like none other. His thoughts had wandered back to that fateful day many times in the last few months; it was the day his family disowned him. The day his brother all but told him to go to hell.- -And where was his brother now? Locked up in a federal cell, awaiting trial for multiple homicide under the media-dubbed name, the “Slipknot Assassin.”- Renegade: Forget it. (he sighs) You’ve got PCW now, Rich. Just let it go. -His own words sound insincere even to himself, their dull tone absorbed into the confines of his dark “room” within the abandoned warehouse. Laying back down, he slams a musty pillow over his head to block out the oh-so-bright glare of the moon, ready to shut out the world once more in favor of a peaceful slumber.- -In less than five minutes Rich “The Renegade” Rollins dozes quietly, his subconscious mind focused on Fusion and his first match in a PCW ring.- ~Fin~
