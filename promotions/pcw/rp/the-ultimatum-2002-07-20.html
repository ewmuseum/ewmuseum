The Ultimatum

20 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 20, 2002 Forgive the ignorance, but today has not bee a good day for me. In fact, it’s been down right shitty. I pulled the coat tighter into my body, trying to protect myself from the frigid Seattle weather. Light rain impeded my progress but I continued on my way, trying to get to a small apartment on the upper side of Seattle. It had only a few days removed from Turmoil where I lost the Continental title to DarkStar. Obviously any loss hurts to some degree, but my pain cut deeper than any loss I had ever endured before. It wasn’t only a loss, it was an attack on my very existence. I prided myself on being the champion for the people, but now that I didn’t it around my waist, I felt like a faceless jobber. The apartment complex was four stories high, and the door was already open. I looked about my surroundings, seeing nothing but empty street on both sides. I had received a letter a day following the loss, telling me to come here if I wanted to turn things around for myself in PCW. I’m desperate. There needs to be something to jump start the revolution I’ve been trying to begin. Either it happens now or it never happens. As I walked up the stairs to room 406, the room that I was told to go to, I realized that this could have been a trap. Taking advantage of a man’s psyche was no new trick, however, if it wasn’t, it could have been the key to the downfall of Duke Williams and DarkStar, two men who I’d rather see burn in hell than wear my title belt. The door to the apartment was already slightly ajar, and I pushed it open, walking inside. Sure, it was breaking and entering, but I was invited here and I didn’t feel like waiting. The room inside was dark, with only a few candles lighting a dining table at the far end of the room. It was an eerie atmosphere and I could feel the amulet underneath my shirt. It gave no sensation, meaning there was no danger. Suddenly the light came on, and I found myself face to face with a man who was about as tall as I was. “There you are Mr. Lexian,” He said with a smile. “Isn’t it easier to sneak around a man’s home when you have the light on.” He sat down at the couch, waiting for my response. “You sent the letter?” I asked. “I did.” “So what do you want?” He laughed, slapping his leg in amusement. I was not amused. “Oh, Tom, you’re so funny. They all told me I’d have a ball with you.” “Look, lets cut the charades alright?” I demanded, beginning to lose my temper. “Who is ‘they,’ and why did you ask me to come here?” His face become serious, darkened by another motive that surfaced. “You know why you’re here, Tom. You’re here because you want a way to defeat the powers in PCW that seek to hold you down. It’s actually quite simply, although I thought from my letter that you had already figured it out.” I stared at the man, realizing that I still didn’t even know his name. He had short brown hair, a trim moustache, and well built physique. Perhaps he was a wrestler, but it looked more like he just worked out a lot. “Who are you?” I finally asked. It probably should have been my first question. “Raymond Burris, at your service,” He replied. “I am from New Wave Entertainment, a Seattle based company that produces commercials, movies, and all other sorts of productions. We have recently become quite interested in you.” I snorted. “Me? What exactly do you need from me?” “Well, you’re a big star now, Tom. After your victory in the RPG3 tournament, you all but erased that filthy taste out of our mouths when you first returned to PCW and then left a month later. Everyone’s been raving about you. And that huge case involving your so called son was a huge ratings getter.” “What are you getting at?” He leaned forward. “You have the makings of a star, Tom. A huge star. Quite simply, you have the best ‘upside’ of any entertainer in the last ten years. And I’m not just talking about wrestling, but whole entire slew of other possibilities for you.” I stood there, confused as to what I should say next. He was offering me a chance to make millions of dollars in an industry that I knew absolutely nothing about. I was supposed to buy this? “So that’s why you had me fly up here?” I asked, still skeptical. “You had me come up here so you could sell me on your plans to make me a star?” “I did it for many reasons. Look at yourself right now, Tom. Where are you? The past three weeks you’ve been the tail end of a running joke that has the Dark Age and Duke Williams soaking up the attention that you should be getting. Do you know what that’s doing to your image?” “My image is a hero.” I stated flatly. “And that’s where you’re wrong,” He responded, standing up and grabbing a comic book from the table. It was an early edition of the Amazing Spiderman. “Do you see this? Who is that?” “Spiderman.” “Spiderman, that’s right. That is a hero, portrayed in the way that he can come across as something more than human. Supernatural, even. And that’s the way you want to portray yourself.” “It’s not about portraying myself. My life is out there for everyone to see…” “Bullshit!” He shouted. He ripped up the comic book, leaving the remnants at my feet. “You can make yourself whoever you want to be. Perception is reality. I don’t know about all of these powers you claim to have. I know you seem to have some secrets that no one else understands, but you need to remember who you really are. No one is going to buy this superhero crap, and I know it’s not doing you any good at the moment.” “So what do you suggest that I do?” “Drop the Crusaders, end this tireless masquerade, and listen to my plans.” “Your plans?” I said, perking an eyebrow. “And what stakes do you have in my future?” “More than you think.” And so he told me his plans, the ultimatum that I would deliver to those that would otherwise attempt to bury me. Ambitious and dangerous were two words to describe it. Could I through with this?
