The Makeover- A New Face And A New Man.

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/10/02 Life was going pretty good for Jordan X Shabazz. Not only had he, Thug roC, and Rush Mason make an example out of his one time friend, Duzza, on PCW Tuesday Turmoil. They brutally attacked Duzza, and hit him with a barrage of R. Kelly insults, and then they proceeded to make the Duzzer never forget the name of *Inhales deeply* Pffffffffffffffffffft, ‘The Uprising.’ *Exhales like Goldust*. I had to get that in there. Not only did Jordan and crew DEMOLISH Duzza, but Rush Mason became the first ever PCW BLACK Extreme Champion… that mattered. Karl LeBlanc once held that title, Karl LeBlanc made a mockery of all that was black and talented in the world, now Rush Mason would diligently try to restore some of the lineage of the gold. Perhaps Rush can silence the critics who have plagued his career? Perhaps Rush is a bit unreliable. One night he’s on his game, and the next he’s not. If Rush can get back into pre-March Madness form, watch out whiteys, because you’re in trouble. You’d think that by this point and time in his Civil Rights career, Jordan X Shabazz would have read the autobiography of the man that he claims is his hero, Malcolm X. But Jordan didn’t. Instead, he went onto the Internet and read a short biography about the man who he idolized. But after reading that biography, and looking at the pictures of Malcolm X, Jordan X Shabazz decided that it was time for change in his appearance. Gone was the clean shaven, youthful looking, professional wrestling superstar, and in was the gruffy looking, much older than he really is looking, goatee wearing, professional wrestling superstar/Civil Rights activist. Our scene opens up in a beauty salon… that’s right I said a beauty salon. It’s a small, African American owned place. It’s nice and cozy, today is their slowest day, Tuesday, Jordan X Shabazz opted to choose this day to get his makeover done for the simple reason that it nobody in the area would see his face yet. Inside of the salon is empty, except for Rush Mason and Thug roC. Rush is polishing his newly won PCW Extreme Championship, and Thug roC is reading a magazine called “Black Enterprise” and on the cover of that magazine is none other than Jordan X Shabazz. He is standing in front of his N.I.G.G.A headquarters in the very city that they are in, Dallas, Texas. All three of the men had their minds on other things. Friday Fusion would be their first ever-actual match in which they were teaming with each other. Rush Mason and Jordan X Shabazz had teamed on several occasions, and they were very good friends. They had a good chemistry when they were in the ring together, and they did hold a tag team victory over John Crichton and Andy Murray. Thug roC, Rush Mason, and Jordan X Shabazz as a whole on the other hand, haven’t proved themselves nearly as adequate as a team. While Rush and Thug continuing with their respective things on the outside of the actual place where Jordan is being serviced, so to speak. Now we go to inside of the section of the salon where Jordan is getting his makeover. We hear voices… “Get me a mirror!” says one familiar voice, powerfully and ferociously. We hear some people running towards a cupboard and pulling out a mirror. They return to the man sitting in the sit. He looks into the mirror… and laughs. “HAHAHAHAHHAHA!! I’m MALCOLM X SHABAZZ!! HAHAHAHAHHAHA!” Obviously, the person that was sitting in the seat was Jordan X Shabazz. His makeover was now complete. We see Jordan get out of the chair, grinning from ear to ear. He looks VERY different than we ever saw him before. He has a goatee that looks exactly like Malcolm X’s did, he also has his hair picked out, into some sort of a short Afro, it appears well kept and it is finely groomed. And even more shocking, is Jordan’s clothing. He’s wearing a jet-black suit, with a red tie. On his feet are finely polished shoes that you think you would only see on Jordan if he ever went to church. On Jordan’s face, are rather thick, horn-rimmed glasses, which give off the appearance of a very intelligent man. Jordan opens the door out of the salon and he looks at Thug roC and Rush Mason. Both of their jaws drop. Rush Mason: “J-Jordan…. What the hell happened?” Jordan smiles at Rush and he walks over. Jordan X Shabazz: “It was about damn time for a change man… If I want to portray myself as one of those “intelligent” black business people, I’ve got to look like one. N.I.G.G.A just hit the New York Stock Exchange, and I don’t think that anybody would take me seriously. And I don’t think that they’d want to invest in my company. But now, when they see me, looking like Malcolm X, they’ll say “Hey, did you see that wrestler guy? He’s changed his appearance. I think I’m going to buy stock in his goldmine of a company.’” Jordan smiles at Rush, who obviously isn’t buying it. Thug roC still looks at Jordan in awe. Jordan X Shabazz: “Come on guys… let’s get going…” Thug roC calls up from the seat that he is sitting in. Thug RoC: “Where to?” Jordan smiles. Jordan X Shabazz: “Why of course, N.I.G.G.A headquarters, I’ve got to show off my new look to my workers!” The scene fades into another scene. Outside of the offices of N.I.G.G.A. The place that once looked like a dump. It’s the second of two offices that were built. The other is in Decatur, Georgia. This one is much nicer and better kept, because Jordan lives in the area, he doesn’t allow anybody to slack. We see Jordan X Shabazz, Rush Mason, and Thug roC get out of a rented Cadillac Deville. Jordan X Shabazz walks hurriedly towards the entrances of his office. He owned this place, the place was nearing $20 Million dollars in it’s worth. Each and everyday he moved closer to his goal of becoming one of the most powerful black men in the United States. As Jordan enters the building, two security officers confront him. They grab him by both of his arms. Security Guard #1: “Who are you, and why are you here?” Jordan chuckles, as if it’s some sort of joke. When he looks at their faces, he realizes that it isn’t a joke. Jordan X Shabazz: “Surely you recognize me, I’m your damn boss!” The other security guard chuckles while looking sternly at Jordan. Security Guard #2: “Heh, you are NOT Jordan X Shabazz, let me see your ID card.” Jordan X Shabazz quickly fumbles through his pockets, trying to get a hold on his ID card that he always carries with him. Shabazz finally finds it and he pulls out it, clearly displaying it to the two guards who are holding his arms. Jordan X Shabazz: “There, officers, there it is.” One of the guards snatches the ID card out of the Jordan’s hands and he examines it. Security Guard #2: “How come you look nothing like him?” Before anything else happens, Rush and Thug roC finally decide to step in now and stop Jordan X Shabazz from any further embarrassment. Rush Mason: “Yeah, this is him.” Rush Mason and Thug roC wrest Jordan away from the apologetic security guards, who are looking at Jordan in awe. Security Guard #1: “We’re very sorry, sir. We didn’t…” Jordan cuts him off, understandingly. Jordan X Shabazz: “Ah it’s okay… I’m a new man.” Jordan motions for Thug roC and Rush Mason to follow him up 40 floors to his office, which hasn’t been used in roughly two weeks. *** 5 minutes later, when they reach the top floor. Jordan walks onto his floor with pride, Thug roC and Rush behind him, also smiling. Jordan X Shabazz: “Rush, Thug, we’re the future. You’re Extreme Champion Rush, Thug you’re soon to be Cruiserweight Champion, and I’m soon to be Television Champion. All is right with the world. I have a new look, and a new attitude. No more Mr. Nice Guy. John Crichton doesn’t to give me a shot at his title, only because he’s afraid of what I’ll do to him. He’s afraid that a black man such as myself is rising in power and popularity. He knows that he can’t pin me… I’m number ten… he’s number… a hundred and something… he finished behind… ELLIE~! HAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHHA!!” Jordan, Rush, and Thug RoC all begin laughing, until Jordan stops, turns around, and looks at Rush. Jordan X Shabazz: “Didn’t you job to Ellie~! Once?” Jordan and Thug roC break out into laughter once again as the new PCW Extreme Champion laughs half heartedly as we fade to black.
