Petty, Petty Scuffle

1 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7 - 01 - 03 “Her again… terrific. There seems to be a coalition of walking vaginas against me.” Dorn smirks as he stares at the card layout on paper for Tuesday Turmoil, and more specifically, his match against the ‘Team Green Too’ team of Skylar Green, Gaz Green, and Tessa Windsor-Green. Now normally, Dorn would sweat taking a shit after drinking Sunny Delight than he would knowing he has a match against some green-haired cretin, his whore, and PCW’s blind world champion (by fluke, of course). So what’s so different this time? “So Tessa gets another shot at the Television Title if we lose…” The Astronomer mumbles under his breath and to no one in particular as he continues gawking at the card layout, its reflection manifesting itself on his shades. “She must be sucking VP-S off nightly. That’s the only explanation.” “Sounds good to me.” The PCW Television Champion’s face suddenly curls into a smile and he turns to see his partner, The Pharaoh, walking towards him. Dorn chuckles and shakes his head, “Can you blame him? I’d love to have sex with her lips, too.” Titu nods and stands beside the other third of the Triumvirate, looking down at the card layout with a wondering eye. “We are paired with Dante Jackson… I think you can count on facing Gaz’s harlot again.” “You’re telling me,” Dorn cracks off with another shake of his head, followed by a disappointed sigh. He crumbles the card layout and tosses it to the floor, wanting nothing more to do with it. He steps to the wall, turns, and leans back against it with his coated arms folded across his chest, pulling the TV Title up somewhat from the pulling sensation on his shirt. Pharaoh idly looks about before taking his gaze to the ground. “Heh… my friend, are you thinking what I am?” Dorn shrugs, “I might be.” Pharaoh looks up and nods, “And you’re disappointed too, correct?” “It’s a fucking travesty, so yes,” Dorn spits with a snide, venomous tone. He leans his head forward and whips his ponytail off his shoulder idly, though it probably doesn’t really bother him. He snorts angrily and gives Pharaoh an almost accusing look. “Where were you?” A little shocked by the question, Titu gives him a stumped look and folds his arms across his chest, “What do you mean?” “I mean exactly what I asked.” Slightly offended by this interrogative question, Pharaoh’s face melts into a harsh grimace and the corner of his lips curls up into a snarl. “Who are YOU to ask me where I was at? Where were YOU?” Now it’s Dorn’s turn to be surprised. He looks as though someone just accused him of murdering the President of the United States! The Astronomer does a quick double take and gives Titu a shocked look, wondering how on Earth Pharaoh could ask him such a retarded question. “How on Earth could you ask me such a retarded question?” “WHAT?!” Pharaoh cries, “You asked me where I was, but now it’s a retarded question to ask you where YOU were?? You were probably licking the blasphemous boots of your lizard goddess!” “Take that back, shit bag!!” “NEVER!” At that, the two stablemates, who are supposed to be partners and friend, pounce on each other and fists start to fly. Kicks, punches, and headbutts are all now commonplace in this petty scuffle between stablemates. In the distance, but not far off, stands the third member of the Triumvirate: Violence Jack, with his arms folded across his chest and an annoyed look on his face. “All because I was cheated out of the Continental Title… HOPEFULLY YOU TWO CAN PULL IT TOGETHER BEFORE TURMOIL!” Instantly, the two freeze in motion and stare at Violence Jack with bewildered stares. Dorn pipes up first, “What? Are you kidding? I will be ready, you don’t have to worry!” “So will I!” Pharaoh yells defiantly, before giving Dorn a nasty look. The Astronomer returns the look and the two continue fighting while VJ just watches, shaking his head… [End.]
