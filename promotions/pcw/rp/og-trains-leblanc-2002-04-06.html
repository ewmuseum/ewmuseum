'OG' trains LeBlanc

6 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/6/02 Scene shows a helicopter fly-by of Amaranth Penitentiary, home of PCW Extreme Champion, Karl LeBlanc. Scenes cuts to inside the prison gym. Several inmates are seen lifting weights. Camera zooms across the room to find LeBlanc inside the prison's make-shift wrestling ring. LeBlanc spars with another inmate while 'OG' Orpheus Grant and Warden Riley observe from ringside. Warden Riley: So what do you think Orpheus? OG: Well, the sumbitch definately gots the raw power. I been in tha ring wit him all mornin' and he be tha strongest mutha fucka I ever seen. Thing is... he don't know his own strength and he sho as hell don't know how to use it in tha ring. Warden: Yeah, you're right. He's the strongest guy in our whole prison system. Hell, he's the only inmate we have to actually use The ShockStick on. OG: Tha ShockStick? What tha fuck dat be? Warden: Ohh, you don't want to know. OG: Naw, sheeit biatch tell OG what tha f*ckin' ShockStick is? Warden: (sighs) Well, The ShockStick can be used for either rehabilitation or as a restraining device. It's an instrument that looks somewhat like a cattle prod and delivers the strongest, most intense dose of high-powered voltage that the human body can tolerate without causing any permanent physical or mental damage. OG: Shit! You pullin' my dick man? Warden: No, that's what The ShockStick is used for. It incapacitates the target long enough for us to properly restrain them. OG: Man I need to gets me one of them. Warden: Sorry, they're only available to law enforcement officials. OG: Can I play around wit it sometime? Warden: It's not a toy OG. Let's get back on task here. Is LeBlanc ready for this Six Pack Challenge tomorrow night at PPV or not. OG: Almost. I gots a few more moves I wanna show him first. By tonight he'll be ready. Warden: He better be ready. Swamp can't afford to lose the title again. OG: Aww hell naw... OG will have him ready to tear dem boyz apart. PLUS... don't forget man... OG's gonna be in da ring wit him. I'll clear that ring and let Swamp go in for the kill. Warden: So you're gonna work with him... not against him? OG: Hell... why would I do dat? I been in PCW awhile, now it's Swamp's turn. I feel like his big brutha, man... I'm there to help tha nigga out. Just wait til you see this new move I taught him. Warden: Oh, yeah? You taught Swamp a new move? OG: Dat's what I said, man. Sheeit, I taught him a lot of shit dis past week. Warden: Well, you just guide him through this match at PPV. I know he can hold his own against six other men. Hell, I've seen him clear out the entire cafeteria single-handedly during some of the nastiest prison riots you've ever seen. BUT... as we've found out, these wrestlers are a different breed. They're trained... they know all the tricks of the trade... LeBlanc doesn't know all the tricks yet. OG: Well, that's where I come in. I'm showing him everything I know. And I learnt from da best... Tyler Nelson. He hired da best in the country to train me. Plus, my background as a bodyguard always helps. Trust me Warden... we're gonna get LeBlanc to a level where no mutha fucka in PCW is gonna be able to touch him. Warden: Well, let's just take it slow. It's nice having the Extreme Title belt, but I still think it's all happened too early in LeBlanc's career. Everyone has to remember... this guy is NOT a wrestler. He's a fighter, he's a killer, but he's not a trained ringsman. OG: Ha! Yeah, he ain't trained and he already be a 2-time Extreme Champion! Can you f*ckin imagine what's gonna happen once we actually get him trained?! Camera zooms in on 'The Warden' Jakc Riley. A smile comes across his face after OG's last comment.
