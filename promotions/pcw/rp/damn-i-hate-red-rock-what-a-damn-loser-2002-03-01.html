Damn I hate Red Rock... What a damn loser...

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-1-02 --- The scene opens up to Ganker Ransolver and Ana Lagunez walking along through the park holding hands and laughing and talking amongst one another. Ganker is wearing a yellow RingSide Entertainment t-shirt, black leather jacket, blue jeans, and RSE Cruiserweight Championship draped over his left shoulder, his left arm is helping to support his championship belt so that it doesn't fall off. Ganker and Ana then come to the end of the park and just are entering a nice strip of buildings, one being a small cafe` and another being a bookstore. First off, Ganker and Ana enter the cafe` to put some food and fluids in their stomach, and then they plan to walk on over to the bookstore, more for Ana then Ganker in this case, and for obvious reasons. They take their seats in a booth, on opposite sides, as they take one another's hands and look at one another and begin to converse with one another. Ana: 'So, Ganker, you prove once again why Team Nirvana is such a dominant tag team. I mean that RSE-Vengeance to Carpenter, that was spectacular. You think this Sunday will be your night? Do you think that you, Ganker Ransolver, will walk away from March Madness the #1 Contender for the PCW Cruiserweight Championship?' Ganker: (cocky) 'Pa---lease Ana, yo forget who yo be talkin' to?' (grins) 'I am Ganker Ransolver, a seven or eight tag team champion, and a two or more time Cruiserweight Champion. Is there any doubt in anyone's mind that I will not walk away March Madness the number one contender for teh PCW Cruiserweight Championship? If there is doubt, it's probably amongst my opponents, them being the dumb sh**s they were born and raised to be. I mean let's take Red Rock for example, the man has hated me ever since our days back in the UWF, well actually, it's more NZW for me, but yo know why he hates me?' (Ana shrugs) 'Cause he's jealous, yeah that's right, Red Rock is jealous of I, Ganker Ransolver. He knows he could never compete on the level that I competed on. He knew he'd never be as good as me, he saw the potential I held deep inside of me and knew that when I was actually given the chance that I would shine and be the damn brightest star ever. Red Rock maybe my stiffest competition this Sunday at March Madness, but there ain't nothin' even he can do to stop me from walkin' out the PCW number one contender to the Cruiserweight Championship, and may I add, that PCW Cruiserweight Championship will look damn good arond my waist, cause gold looks great when it's on me. This will only be the beginning for Ganker Ransolver in the PCW, I win the PCW Cruiserweight Championship and then go onto win bigger and better, maybe even badder titles.' Ana: 'Anton the Kid and Kramer Wild Thing are your other two opponents, what about them babe?' Ganker: 'What bout'em? They are a couple of worthless jobbers who aren't even in my damn league. They know they can never be as great as Ganker, they know that there place in this match is jus' to sitback and job like a couple of good jobbers are suppose to do, unfortunately Red Rock was too stupid to grasp that fact and decided to change his jobber ways and go on a winning streak, but we all saw how fast that ended when he lost to Lance Bell and lost his Rising Star Championship. Yo know what I find funny babe?' Ana: 'What?' Ganker: 'Hank and Red Rock both won their first PCW title belts the same damn night, the same damn Pay-Per-View, and they lose them on the exact same night, day, whatever. I think Hank and Red Rock are two of the single most evenly matched competitors and watchin' those two feud back in the NZW and even on UWF tapes, I could see what each of them brought out of one another. Hank never ever competes as hard wit' anyother competitor, Red Rock brings out the beast in that man and I can assure yo that those two will meet face to face in PCW here really soon, whether it be a title match or a non-title match. Those two have a chemistry and I look forward to watchin' their next match, I know that it will be one great one to remember.' Ana: 'That's nice, but I'm only interested in you babe.' Ganker: 'Can't blame yo.' --- They share a laugh as an old lady waitress comes over to their table finally. Waitress: 'What can I get for you two this evening?' Ganker: 'Erm, SOBE~!!!' (pauses) 'No, instead get me a... SURRRRRGGGGEEEE~!!!' Ana: 'A Pepsi if you please ma'am.' Waitress: 'Is that all?' Ganker: 'Yep!' Waitress: 'Alright then, I'll be right back with your drinks.' Ganker: 'Thank yo.' (nods) --- The waitress walks away. Ana: 'Red Rock seemed to have a lot to say about you making RSE look stupid and taking what pride RSE left with and dragging it down with your career. He said that you didn't deserve to be the RSE Cruiserweight Champion.' Ganker: 'Screw him the, the boy is a jealous jerk. Myself and whoever the f**k that jobber was, we teamed up and took out the AKR, in their last tag team match. Red Rock and Duzza, the two biggest jackasses ever to step forth into a ring, but I must give them their dues, they do have heart and do want a great career, and from what I've seen, have had one hell of a career, but for Red Rock, his future is bou teo end, why? Cause he faces Ganker Ransolver and...' (thumb thing) 'Ganker Is God!' --- Ana laughs and nods. Ganker: 'I'll show the world that I maybe small and not the strongest, but I sure as hell can pull my weight, which ain't much by the way.' (laughs) 'Red Rock is a little jackass and from what Hank tells me, he wasn't much better in the UWF when he feuded wit' him as Drummer.' Ana: 'I think Red Rock actually was the newcomer and didn't know much about what to do exactly.' Ganker: 'I was a newcomer in the NZW, my first damn fed and I dominated. I took out the A** Kickin' Rebels, I tore their souls from their bodies and took them NZW Tag Team Championships.' Ana: 'Red Rock's a triple crown UWF champion though.' Ganker: 'So? Who hasn't been? Lord knows I have.' Ana: (laughing) 'True, true.' Ganker: 'Red Rock wants to talk smack bout me, let me show him jus' what I'm made of this Sunday come March Madness. I'll tear Red Rock limb from limb, hit the Screw-Over and then the RSE-Vengeance, why?' (thumb thing) 'Ganker Is God!' Ana: 'What about Fusion?' Ganker: 'Hell, I might not even show-up, I want this number one contendership and my Fusion match means jack sh*t to me, I won't give up my contendership match-up jus' for a stupid Fusion match. Legend Inc better not expect me to be on my best game, cause if he is, he gots somethin' else comin', all my focus is on March Madness.' Ana: 'Why not focus on both matches fully?' Ganker: 'Cause, that's too much for my little peanut of a brain to comprehend, not to mention I don't care bout someone who walked up to PCW corporates and paid them off jus' to have a few matches and maybe even be pushed. Legend Inc does not have my respect, but yo know, if he wants, he can have the damn win over me, I give not a sh*t. Legend Inc is one of those athletes who does not have the proper heart to do this day in and day out. I came back from a major knee injury, Legend Inc probably gets a blood nose and whines about it.' Ana: 'What drove you to wanting to be a wrestling superstar babe?' Ganker: 'Everything, from the first day I saw it on television, Raw is War, Tuesday Torture, Tuesday Turmoil, Friday Fusion, MWF shows, UWA shows. Watchin' what wrestling superstars before me put themselves through jus' to entertain us, jus' to put on one hell of a show, some even to put food in the mouths of their family. All I know it's in my heart and this is my choice, my damn career. Legend Inc, has nothin' to offer me, what's him beatin' me? Jus' another jobber tryin' to prove his lies truthful?' --- The waitress comes back to their table and give Ganker his Surge and Ana her Pepsi. She now leaves the table once more. Ganker: 'Red Rock, another worthless jobber wit' no heart, every damn night I seen the man perform, he's shown me nothin'. Nothin' at all damnit. Red Rock is some little stoned freak-o-zoid. He'll get a taste of my tick doll, speaking of which, I need to reprogram it so it bad mouths Red Rock.' Ana: 'You always were amused easily.' (laughs) Ganker: (laughing) 'I know...' (sips Surge) Ana: 'So, I will be the girlfriend of soon to be Cruiserweight Championship Number One Contender, how exciting.' Ganker: 'Then yo will be the girlfriend of PCW Cruiserweight Champion, why?' (thumb thing) 'Ganker Is God!' Ana: 'I wish I could have seen you in action back in your Neutral Zone Wrestling days, from what you have told me, you were one of the elite in those days.' Ganker: 'Would've been a brighter star, but NZW decided to mold Drummer and turn him into PCW material, hints him droppin' the Drummer gimmick and goin' wit' his natural name, his given name at birth, Hank Gunyon. Notice, PCW molds him into the PCW Extreme Champion. RingSide Entertainment molded me into the RSE Cruiserweight Champion, the last and best Cruiserweight Champion of that damn fed.' Ana: (sips Pepsi) 'So, you almost ready to go to the bookstore with me?' Ganker: 'I ain't into books, but I'd walk to the ends of the damn Eartth wit' yo.' (grins) --- Ganker downs the last of his Surge as Ana puts her Pepsi down and Ganker throws a five dollar bill down and they walkout of the cafe` and right nextdoor to the bookstore. They enter as Ganker walks over to the books about witchcraft, warlocks, druids, black magic, other satanic arts, and whatever else I missed. Ana walks over and starts looking at the magazines. Ganker: 'Now where's that book wit' the spell to turn people into vermin... I think Red Rock would be most greatful to return to his normal form, as a damn rat.' (laughs) 'Now let's see here.' --- Ganker continues to search the shelves as he comes across another book that reads, 'The Dark Arts of VooDoo!' Ganker picks it up and flips through as he grins. Ganker: 'Oh, how fun it would be to make a voodoo doll of Red Rock and have him dance whenever I felt him the need to dance or pick his nose whenever, maybe I'd even rip off his head and watch it roll.' (laughs) 'I hate Red Rock wit' a newfound passion and this is the kinda thing I'd pull off jus' to get that piece of trash outta me life, better yet, I think I might kill Boolie, so Red Rock gets all depressed and doesn't care anymore and quits PCW and admits his jealousy of Ganker Ransolver.' --- Ana comes walk over with a Cosmo magazine on top of her stack of ten magazines. Ana: 'Hey babe, what are you looking at?' Ganker: 'Black magic books babe.' Ana: 'Again?' Ganker: 'What can I say, I like findin' new ways that I might wanna torture Red Rock.' (grins deviously) Ana: 'Well get a few books then, we need to get back to the hotel.' (grins) Ganker: 'Sweet deal.' --- Ganker gathers some books on black magic, white magic, another book on satanic arts, and the voodoo book he was reading before Ana walked over. Ganker takes Ana's magazines and walks over to the counter to pay for them all. Clerk: 'Hello, how are you today?' Ganker: 'Good yo?' Clerk: (beginning to check mags and books) 'I'm fi.... ne...' (looks at magic spell books) 'You're into this sorta thing?' Ganker: 'It's interesting readin', I don't actually cast it, but have been tempted to try some on some of my wrestler competitors, one being Red Rock, another being Duzza, and another being Crow. All three members of the now defunct AKR. These books are jus' for me readin' purposes, I don't have intentions on tryin' to cast the spells inside.' Clerk: 'Whew, that's good to hear. This town is already messed up enough, group of kids who hangout in some cave and read these exact same books. They admit to Satan Worship.' Ganker: 'Not for me, I really don't have religion, it's what's in my heart, that's wrestling. The only god is the god of wrestling.' Clerk: 'You poor lost b...' Ganker: 'End that sentence and I'll be forced to gag yo wit' my sock.' --- The clerk finishes scanning the books and mags. Clerk: ' Your total comes to $175.87.' --- Ganker hands over the cash and takes the bag of books/mags and exits with Ana on his arm, Josh Young walks over after pulling up in Hank's Black Bentley he stole from Duzza back in his NZW days. Josh: 'What's happening my RSE Cruiserweight Champion?' Ganker: 'Hangin' wit' me gal for awhile before my match.' Josh: 'That's nice, but have you seen Hank? I mean after your Turmoil match, he kind of disappeared. Then again, I didn't see him before Turmoil or after Sabotage.' Ganker: 'Must still be pissed bout jobbin' to Karl LeBlanc, but hell, who wouldn't be? LeBlanc is King of the Jobbers.' Josh: 'That's right I know. I wouldn't have hired him in RSE that's for damn sure.' Ganker: 'I'd have been forced to kill yo on the spot.' Josh: 'So, big match this Sunday for March Madness.' Ganker: 'Yup, cruiserweight number one contendership match. I'm psyched.' Josh: 'Should be, I think yo stand a good chance to win it.' Ganker: 'Cause...' (thumb thing) 'Ganker Is God!' Josh: 'I walked right into it didn't I?' (Ana nods as Josh sighs) Ganker: 'Well we're jus' headin' back to the hotel.' Josh: 'So was I, but saw you two, so I stopped and decided to chat it up a bit with you both.' Ganker: 'Thanks, yo shouldn't have.' Josh: 'No problem man.' Ganker: 'No Josh, yo don't understand, yo really SHOULDN'T HAVE!!!' (laughs) 'Fool!!!' Josh: (sighs, slaps forehead, and shakes head) 'Oi... Members of Team Nirvana, why must they always get on my damn nerves all the damn time?' Ganker: 'Cause yo jus' special like that I guess man.' (laughs) --- Ganker and Ana hop in the back of the Bentley as Josh hops in the driver's seat and hits the gas and they drive off back to their hotel. The scene ends and fades out.
