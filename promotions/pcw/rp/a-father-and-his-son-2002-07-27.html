A father and his son

27 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-27-02 There are only a few significant times that I can spend time with my ten month year old son, Toby. This was one of those times. There is never a time in life where you can sit on a couch and look at your son and it just takes your breath away sometimes. Never in my life do I ever get so emotional until I see my son. Growing up on the streets in NYC makes a person tough and strong. But that all fades away until you notice what life you have brought to the world. It amazes me every second. Very rarely do I have a day off from wrestling but this is one of those days. He sits on the couch beside me watching a videotape of “Winnie The Pooh.” He sits there in amazement of the fat orange bear that can talk, I on the other hand sit there looking at my son, smiling as he sits and laughs at the bear. Time passes and he begins to fall asleep on my lap with his bottle in hand. He looks at me at a brief second and looks straight into my eyes and just smiles. He then closes his eyes very slowly and begins to drift off into his own little dream world. My son is everything to me. I remember his birth like it was yesterday. He came out covered in all this shit crying. The doctor put him in my arms and I was overcome with joy. I couldn’t believe that I had a son. All those months leading up to now where amazing with him, watching him grow. I remember my wife and I sitting on the couch watching an old wrestling video of mine. He was sitting on floor playing with his trucks, he peered his head up and looked up at the television and noticed I was on the screen. He started laughing and giggling. He stood straight up and walked over to the television screen and started pointing at me, smiling. My wife looked at me with tears in her eyes awestruck at what just took place. I was in complete shock. He on the other hand just stood there laughing and smiling. He probably stood there for a total of ten seconds before falling down. He tried and tried again to get up but he couldn’t get himself back up like he did before. He went through a brief stage of trying to get up and falling down but now he is walking all the time now. So my Wife has to go out to the local pharmacy and get all these gates and protective seals on plugs and shit. But whatever keeps him safe makes me happy. This day is very special to me. It’s just him and I. My wife is at the mall shopping for some new clothes and I gave her a break of watching him and I decided to spend some quality time with my son. Toby brings a different part of me out every time I’m with him. Usually I’m a bad ass, a PCW wrestler and one of the most world famous casino champions. But when I’m with him he takes me to a different level. He makes Scorpion, Jeff Steiner. He makes me a father. He makes me feel emotion I have never felt in my life until I met him. He lays there fast asleep on my lap with his thumb in his mouth and his favorite blanket in his right hand. I begin to slowly pick his head to put a pillow under it so I could get something to drink and he wakes up, smiling at me. He then picks himself up and sits up looking into my eyes smiling. He then gets off of our couch that he and I were sitting on and heads over to our videotape cabinet. He grabs an old wrestling videotape of mine that I was in. He walks over and puts it on my lap smiling. Scorpion: Do you want to watch this Toby? Toby just smiles, holding his blanket. Scorpion: All right bud, I’ll put it in. I walk over to put in the videotape and he begins to jump up and down laughing waiting for the videotape to start. I put it in and sit back down. He climbs up the couch and sits right next to me. The videotape is a video from a year ago when I broke into the business with a small federation. He sits there in awe because he can’t believe he is seeing me on a television. The door opens up and it’s my wife Stephanie with a bundle of shopping bags. Stephanie: Boys I’m home! Jeff can you please help me? Scorpion: Shore I’ll be right there. I get up from the couch as Toby sits there still watching the television. I hope Stephanie didn’t buy a lot of stuff. I walk into the hallway and there standing in front of me are about seven different bags. Each bag is packed to there limit full of clothes, shoes, appliance, and picture frames, everything there could possibly be in one shopping bag. Once again, I was wrong. She bought the whole mall basically. Well at least the mall can sleep peacefully this night knowing that they have paid off their bills for the rest of the year. As I take the bags up the stairs into our bedroom I begin to hear Stephanie screaming something. I drop the bags in our room and I rush downstairs to Stephanie who is in the kitchen balling. Scorpion: Steph what’s wrong? I didn’t drop anything. Did you forget the credit card again? It’s all right I’ll call the company it’s no big deal. Stephanie: (crying) No! Jeff listen. Scorpion: To what? Stephanie: Come with me to the living room. Scorpion: All right. I walk behind Stephanie into the living room where I see Toby standing straight in front of the television screen jumping up and down and pointing at me. Toby: Daddy! Daddy! Daddy! I don’t believe it, his first words. Stephanie: (crying) He’s talking. Scorpion: Holy shit he is. Daddy is his first word. I stood there for what seemed all day, but it was really only a minute long. I was overwhelmed with joy watching my son grow right before my eyes and seeing him complete a step in his journey through life. I looked at my wife who was crying and smiling at the same time. Toby just looked up at us smiling, he didn’t realize what he completed, probably never will until he has a child of his own. This day was very memorable. I got to spend time with my family and I witnessed my son’s first words. Later that night my wife called every family member in the book. She even videotaped it when we played the tape again so she could send out to everyone. It was truly a day I’ll never forget. You’ll never know how much family means to you until you witness a day like this and my outlook on my family life has changed dramatically and I’m grateful for everything I have gotten in life.
