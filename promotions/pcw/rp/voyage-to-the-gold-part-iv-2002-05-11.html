Voyage to the Gold: Part IV

11 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 11, 2002 Sometimes they wonder yet whether the fans like them or not...the Heartbreakers that is, or even whether anyone really took sufficient interest to care. Their uniform egotistical manner, and way of showing that they care about no one but themselves made it hard to tell. Did fans just cheer the PINK! saps because they hate the Heartbreakers more, or did they really like the Heartbreakers. Did they even care? The whole recollection of that time of their life has such a peculiar quality that the beginning and the end of it are merged in one sensation of profound emotion, continuous and overpowering, containing the extremes of exultation, full of careless joy and of an invincible sadness---like a day-dream. The sense of all this having been gone through as if in one great rush of imagination is all the stronger in the distance of time, because it had something of that quality even then: of fate unprovoked, of events that didn't cast any shadow before. They didn't make a mark in PCW yet, and they had a chance to on Sunday...by capturing the tag team titles. Not that their events at Supremacy would be in the least extraordinary. They were, in truth, commonplace. What to one's backward glance seems startling and a little awful is their punctualness and inevitability. Valentine and Heart brought to the table what they knew. They knew how to wrestle. Despite the fancy clothing, the constant bragging, the overconfidence...they still could wrestle, and that is one thing that Politically Incorrect would have to understand going into the pay-per-view. How could they have avoided The Heartbreakers? To this day one has a shadowy conviction of his inherent distinction of mind and heart, far beyond any two men ever before in PCW. They were unavoidable: and of course Politically Incorrect attempted to their best efforts. They tried to destroy Jack and Johnny in the ring on Sabotage, causing their first loss ever to any team in PCW against Syko & Miyagawa. They attacked the Heartbreakers backstage and even through Jack through his own 'prized' mirror. However, the Heartbreakers are still standing and although they are not at 100% after the events that Politically Incorrect has put them through, they will prepared to battle as they have been on this 'Voyage to the Gold' for so long now. After Jack and Johnny had walked a ways down the street, the camera fades in on the two, both wearing pink sweatpants and sweatshirts, discussed their situation. 'You know, despite the fact that we're the best looking team in PCW, it sure seems very extraordinary that we're in this match on Sunday...almost unreal,' Jack humbly said. 'I will agree with that...on both points,' Johnny laughed. In an almost deadly serious moment, Jack stopped walking down the long road that they were on and began speaking about his career in PCW, forgetting about the looks, the style and everything else in his life. 'You will find everything so simple,' he said. 'So simple that you will be quite able to hold your own. I suppose you know that the world is selfish, I mean the majority of the people in it, often unconsciously I must admit, and especially people with a mission, with a fixed idea, with some fantastic object in view, or even with only some fantastic illusion. That doesn't mean that they have no scruples. And I don't know that at this moment I myself am not one of them.' 'Whoa, whoa, whoa...hold on there Jack. What's gotten into you? You're really weirding me out right now,' Johnny responded. 'I haven't seen a chance at a belt in almost 8 months Johnny...and in comparison with what the belt meant then to me, as to what it is now...it is indescribable. The PINK! is great, but I think that the PINK! with a little bit of GOLD! would be even better,' Jack excitedly explained. Johnny gave him a sidelong look. It met Jack's of the same character and they both smiled without openly looking at each other. At the end of the road the Jack and Johnny paused, and looked back. They looked back at all they were leaving behind...the days of ridicule, the days sitting out pay-per-views, the days of injury...the days of never accomplishing anything, anything at all. 'This isn't going to be easy,' Johnny said metaphorically. 'No, but at least we still look the same,' Jack smiled. The two shared a laugh and began complimenting each other on their looks. It was even worse in a way. It ought to have been more disconcerting. F or, pursuing the image of the tag team championship blundering upon the complications of an unknown scheme of life, it was them, the castaways, who were the savages, the simple innocent children of nature. Politically Incorrect were the favorites, bar none. No one seriously looked at the Heartbreakers as contenders. Naturally...They were still so young! And yet rest assured, that they will lose all sense of inferiority come Supremacy. And why? Of course the carelessness and the ignorance of youth had something to do with that. But there was something else besides. Looking at the gold, their waists wrapped with a new sense of style, with their pink boas draped over their shoulders...they could finally feel a sense of greatness. People of whom had held gold in the past have set down with all the exactness of unfailing memory, that becoming champions would be everlasting, recorded in the books and The Heartbreakers would finally have reached their pedestal; revealed with something peculiarly intimate in the conviction, as if the belt were young exactly in the same way in which they themselves felt young; and that therefore no misunderstanding between them and the belts was possible and there could be nothing more to stand in the way. They have the mindset, they have the strategy, they even have the style...but will they be ready for the team that has been so brutal over the past couple of months? Can they overcome the immense odds of defeating possibly one of the greatest tag teams in PCW History? Only time will tell, because their is still instances where Politically Incorrect can crush their hopes and dreams in a matter of 3 seconds...3 seconds is all it takes to determine a lifetime of wrestling history. Stay tuned for part V...
