A trap is set. But on who?

4 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 4, 2003 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up with Semore Smoke standing about looking at the TV of continuing coverage with Dan Rather of CBS News of the war. Word broke out earlier that day that US Army troops had taken Saddam Airport, or what they have now renamed as Baghdad Airport. Smoke has noticed that his former trooper buddies have been dispatched up north to secure airfields up north and protect the borders of Turkey and such from fleeing Republican Guard members who may try to flee Baghdad and head north. Smoke is sitting in a leather chair in his office that looks more like the room of a child as it’s torn apart with clothes, food, and other such stuff all over the floor, as if he hadn’t left the room for days. Smoke is wearing an old white T-shirt that hasn’t been washed and has a pizza stain on the front of it. And he’s wearing stained blue jeans with a pair of socks with no shoes on. Smoke stands up from the seat and walks over to his desk, which is on the far wall away from the door that exits into the main meeting room. Smoke sits down at the chair behind his desk and hits a few keys on the keyboard of his computer, automatically he’s taken to the PCW website, which features a few new news articles. Smoke scrolls down a bit and clicks the one about the Tag Tournament and stares at the news report for a bit and then shakes his head in shame as if he can’t believe that AJ Trinidad actually teamed the two of them together so he could get his hands on Smoke. Smoke is smiling however as he clicks to view the results for Tuesday Turmoil, he scrolls down the results a bit and finds what he’s looking for, the announcement of first round opponents. Smoke stares at the screen and smiles to himself a menacing grin that only he can understand the true meaning of with the sick plot in his head. The results read that Skylar Green pulled out the pairing of The Schmoopies, Tessa Windsor and Chris Storm to face both Semore Smoke and AJ Trinidad. For sure, if Trinidad and Smoke were to work together in the tournament, then there would definitely be hell to pay for everyone they face, but since The Schmoopies are former PCW Tag Team Champions, Smoke is less likely to take them lying down then AJ Trinidad might. Finally another light bulb clicks in his head and he finds a way to make AJ Trinidad do exactly what he wants him to do. Smoke looks on his desk for something and throws a few pieces of paper astray while looking for something. He then finds what he was looking for, the remote to the TV and he changes the TV channel and it brings up a security camera of Crespo sitting down in the middle of the same cell he was in before, he’s still tied up and looks to be extremely hungry, even though it’s obvious that he’s been fed a steady diet of at least bread and water to keep him alive. Smoke looks at the security camera footage and then back at the listing of the first match in the first round of the tournament and smiles yet again. He then scribbles some information onto a piece of paper and throws it into the top drawer of his desk. He stands up and turns to face a mirror that’s hung on a wall. He is still grinning the menacing grin of his and he begins to talk to himself in a crazed manner… Semore Smoke: Ha! I’ve got you now AJ Trinidad, the best way to get you to work along side with me is to make sure I use my insurance policy yet again. Since Trinidad was unable to win the TV title, maybe if he’s along side yourself Smoke, then he’ll win a tag tournament with you and end up facing you for a bigger prize, the Continental Title! That for sure will make this worth your while Semore! Think about it man! Continental title shot! And all you have to do is force AJ Trinidad to cooperate with you, or risk losing Crespo again forever! It’s a winning situation for you! He thought he had you Smoke! But no! You’ve set a trap for him that he didn’t see coming a mile away! This will surely show who is the best between us both and if we do win, then I will surely stay true to my word of giving Crespo back, and then, a Continental title reign would be almost immanent in my future. {{{{The camera fades on Smoke’s face as his sadistic grin seems to keep becoming wider. Smoke seemingly thinks that he’s got AJ Trinidad where he wants him, or could it be the other way around? Only time will tell…}}}}
