'In-Phuneral Blitz?'

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered 'In memory of the late PCW TAG TEAM DIVISION...' =©= Clouds of gray lie lazily upon the horizon, the air damp with moisture. Rain begins to drizzle down in thin sheets upon the small neatly cropped turf. Gazing down past the tall treetop background, we come upon a small rectangular granite block.=©= =©= A tombstone…=©= =©= It stands before a gaping hole sized for a casket. Panning out a bit more, we find the Celestial couple standing before the open grave with black leather trench coats on over their usual vinyl, mesh, and PVC attire. And even with the dreary skies raining down upon them, they still wore their sunglasses.=©= Cyclonica: Now is a good a time as any… =©= She raises the clipboard in her hands and begins to read from it. =©= Cyclonica: We are gathered here this day to pay our last respects to those foolish men who dared to risk everything in the face of hopelessness…and in the end lost everything. We are here to pay homage to the loss of an entire world destroyed at the hands of the god. We are here…to say our final farewell…to PCWs Tag teams…and to the entire division. =©=The nVg nods his head as she continues on.=©= Cyclonica: Those men…those d@mn dirty apes…they had such high hopes and expectations of themselves. They were stupid to think they could defeat the god…and in the end they did nothing but aid him in the destruction of their dreams for Gold and Glory. They did so much for him that they never truly realized…so we must thank them for their help… =©= With a wrinkled brow, she looks up at the god, who shakes his head at her statement.=©= Cyclonica: The god has granted the tag teams no thanks for helping him win the belts this Sunday…so screw them. But still…we will pay respect to them as this Sunday will be the last night that their tag team division will remain intact. Afterwards, the tag team world will be in chaos after a single individual holds ownership of titles that have only been possessed by two mortals at the same time. They’ll find themselves wishing for the days before Akiyama entered the division alone and made it and all the tag teams within it crumble at his feet. But first…a moment of silence for the soon to be departed. =©= 1…2…3…=©= Cyclonica: All right…I’m sure they’ve made their last respects and all…time to move on to the individual dedications to the departing teams. First…to the Dark Messiahs. =©=Flipping over the page on her clipboard and continuing…=©= Cyclonica: “Dark Messiahs…you made us laugh in so many ways. You were never really dark, just in the dark most of the time…nor were you ever really messiahs of anything besides your own destruction. There was a lot of talk from you boys about destroying the nVg…but after the god crushed one of your windpipes and nearly gave the other flat-scan a concussion…you two really learned not to speak ill of the Neo Void God. This Sunday, Akiyama will take great humor in flinging you from the ring in our path to conquest…making sure to step back and laugh along the way just as you made him laugh during your handicapped match last week. Seeing how you never really were the messiahs of anything in PCW, and delivered nothing but your jobber presence to the tag division we’re about to destroy…we remember your worthlessness with this…” =©=The god holds his arm out, clutching a small teen wearing a red shirt, blue jeans, and a white paper hat. The boy squeals as he dangles above the hole.=©= Cyclonica: “ …the messiah, or deliverer, of pizzas…who in this point in his pathetic little life, holds more importance than your lives. We hope you reflect on this moment as you lie broken outside the ring Sunday.” Akiyama… =©=With a scream, the youth falls into the grave, landing with a sharp thud and a moan. Cyclonica and Akiyama move on like nothing happened.=©= Cyclonica: “Next, we pay tribute to the Suicidal Tendencies. These two…they’ve just recently returned into action, which was bad timing on their parts. They waited until AFTER the god came back to PCW to try for the tag team titles…and that was as dumb as the things they said about him. They absolutely knew nothing of his name…his accomplishments…nor his godly status. They did nothing but put up a crazy tough guy front and talk dumbness. But hey…there’s always room for the retarded in this business…and no one plays retards better than a couple of unknowing suicidal saps like Suicidal Tendencies. So… in memory of your ineptitude…” =©=Akiyama holds out a man in a tank top and shorts. He struggles to no avail to break free.=©= Cyclonica: “ …We give you this suicidal man…” Man: I’m no suicidal!!! Cyclonica: “…Who we snatched up moments before throwing himself before a speeding car…” Man: I was crossing the street!! Cyclonica: “… And we hereby honor Suicidal Tendencies by helping this man commit suicide…because if anyone’s going to die…we want a personal hand in their destruction.” Akiyama? Man: No! I don’t want to dieeeeeeeeeee!!! =©=And down he goes… crashing down straight onto the pizza boy, who lets out another cry as the man lands on him. Akiyama dusts off his hands as Cyclonica flips a page for the next tribute.=©= Cyclonica: And now… “Cowabunga Connection… you two post modern surf slackers have been around here for a bit, yet you haven’t really impressed anyone with your moves. Sure, you talk the talk, but when it comes to walking the walk you always wipeout…well, at least in the big waves. And unless you’re too smacked from pot breaks in between waves, you’d notice that Akiyama’s one big frickin Kahuna…and since you didn’t keep your eyes on the Neo Void Wave coming through…you had no choice but to end up washed out. We hope that in your shameful defeat, we hope you find more fitting employment as beach bums somewhere sunny. So…to remember your sand-caked hair and your sun-cracked skin…” =©= Akiyama holds up a man dressed in black with short cropped black hair and black sunglasses and a trench coat. =©= Cyclonica: “ …We present Keanu Reeves…who looks Celestial in Matrix-garb, but is truly surfer in mind, body, and spirit.” Reeves: Whoa…dude…I’m no surfer. Cyclonica: If you whine like a b!tch, act like a b!tch, and look like a b!tch…you’re a frickin b!tch! Same principle applies to you and surfers. Akiyama…dump him! =©= Akiyama throws him down into the pit, plopping down on the “Suicidal” man and the pizza “messiah”. Reeves coughs from down below, all the dirt and dust cluttering up his lungs used to the ocean air. =©= Reeves: This is bogus, dudes! Where’s Rufus when you need him? Cyclonica: Silence…you’ll spoil the funeral. But speaking of b!tches… “Pretty Cool Guys…we knew ye for yet so little a time. And never once did you bother to impress the Neo Void God in any way. Your little pop routine is yet so old…and also unoriginal, since 3 Count originally ran that gimmick more successfully than you. As you will be sent plummeting from the ring as well as from the music charts…there’s no better memorial than this one.” =©= Another body dangles over the grave. =©= Cyclonica: “Your pop god…Justin Timberlake.” Justin: HEY!! Get him off of my hair! The perm’s gonna come out!! The peroxide!! The peroxide!!! =©= Akiyama roars in his face as he spikes him down into the ground. Sobbing echoes from below as Cyclonica shakes her head. =©= Cyclonica: Pop b!tches… =©= Turning the next page…=©= Cyclonica: “Chef and Alexis Parshin…a Hall of Fame guy and the latest big dumb loser in PCW. We knew just how dedicated you two are on winning those belts and honoring your home countries, but even sending the nVg over the top rope wont bring any glory back to those puissant third world countries. This whole Afghanistan thing is so played out…just like the presence of you two are. It’s time for Boda Junior and Aladdin Bin Disney to realize that all nations fall under one god…the nVg…and he will crush you as well as the spirits of your poor countries. And so…” =©= A turbaned Indian man wearing his native clothing and sandals hangs by his beard from the clutches of the god. He screams out in his native tongue as Akiyama tugs at his lengthy facial hair, letting gravity cause the pain. =©= Cyclonica: “ …This man will pay respects to you in your tag team death…we don’t know his name or where he’s from, but he looks pretty much like Osama Bin Laden, and the tried to rip us off on the cab ride here, so you’ve got to go! May that desert scum Allah have mercy on your ratty souls…” Arab: ALLLLLLLLAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHH!!!! =©= Akiyama finds a handful of hair in his hand as the Arab falls into the grave yelling the name of his weak god. He looks at the hair with a wrinkled brow before tossing it over his shoulder and shrugging. Cyclonica shakes her head with a sigh. =©= Cyclonica: “Now…Politically Incorrect…these two mortal jackasses had the audacity to “warn” the nVg…now that’s just committing suicide right there. Two mortals do not WARN…the NVG! Not even some suited up, yellow striped, gerrymandering, fat intern slob screwing, desk jockey Bill Clinton wannabes! Reginald Lovecraft must be getting senile or something…like being a Southern Baptist is going to impress us. Trying to hype up the fact that you’re Southern Baptist is like trying to hype up politics…they both suck no matter what! It’s like the Dark Messiahs hyping up the fact that they’re human, but they’re still going to fight hard…” =©= She holds her hand to her head and closes her eyes. =©= Cyclonica: “Incoming prophecy…you cannot hype up the fact that you’re all flat-scan mortal jobbers who have no other alternative but to shut your frickin mouths, stop having weak press conferences at crappy movies or whatever, and take your beating like the primate monkeys you are. There’s no warning needed except get out of the f^cking way when the god comes down with a reign of destruction! And without further ado…” =©= Before Akiyama can do anything, Bill Clinton comes dashing past them and leaps into the grave. =©= Bill: WHOOOO DOGGY!!! =©= Thud he goes, and after a few seconds…=©= Bill: HEY! You said there were fine sexy rotund interns in this here hole!! Cyclonica: “Bill Clinton…political stupidity at its finest.” Bill: There’s nothing but a bunch of dirty men down here! This Timberlake guy’s kinda girly though… Justin: Watch the hair!!! You….B!TCH!!! Cyclonica: Quiet down there or I’ll send Akiyama down there to break your necks!!! =©= Silence…=©= Cyclonica: Thought so. =©= And so she turns the pages…=©= Cyclonica: “Misplaced…” =©= The Neo Void God holds up a body bag with someone moving around inside. =©= Cyclonica: “This is Pauleta…who has no point in being here…just like Misplaced.” =©= In the bag goes…and Akiyama cracks the joints in his neck as the Divine Prophetess begins to round the last few pages. =©= Cyclonica: Thank you, Senor Borro. “For the other half of the New Ream Team…AKA, the Heartbreakers…their pinkish antics will not go unforgotten. For they mentioned the color pink at least twice in every sentence they said, and their femininity went so well together with the other half of the New Ream Team, the PCG. Pink boy bands…doesn’t get more homosexual than that. Being that the entire concept of your meaningless existence is not really interesting, and the fact that you were the last champions holding the belt holds absolutely no weight upon whether or not you deserve the belts, you require no further attention.” =©= Akiyama holds out a trash bag dripping with a thick liquid.=©= Cyclonica: “We remember you, Heartbreakers…with broken hearts…fresh from the hospital. It is all the more convenient that it will be your hearts that break when you realize that you shall not be tag champs this time.” =©= The contents of the bag goes pouring out into the grave with a red shower, drawing up massive complaints and vomits from the already grave-bound people. Cyclonica nods as she comes upon the last page.=©= Cyclonica: “Finally…Superior Heroes for Hire…the man named Snow and the one called Rent A Hero…it has been funny how this duo reminds us of the duo of Jeremy Howard and Rent A Hero. Could Snow be nothing more than a lesser replacement of a greater man? Most likely…yes. But these two are the ones that call themselves superior in PCW…where that simply cannot be. There is only one superior being in PCW, and that is the nVg. You two are simply…poseurs…poseurs of being far greater than mortals are. You are nothing but human…just as much human as the simple simians you seek to protect. The greatest blow to humanity has always been the defeat of their best heroes…while we cannot defeat their best heroes; you two will have to do. We remember you two by sending a great hero to his final resting place…just as we will send lesser heroes to their greatest defeat this Sabbath.” =©=Akiyama looks to his side for something, and then turns back to Cyclonica with a shrug. She looks at him with a raised eyebrow.=©= Cyclonica: Where’s the Hurricane? Voice: He had to blow…but I have your hero right here… =©= The Celestials turn around and find, to their surprise, the Black Dragon Master himself standing behind them. Wearing a black trench coat and sunglasses just as they did, his arms were folded over his chest as he bore into them with his eyes. Akiyama ripped off his trench coat and growled loudly at his long time nemesis.=©= Cyclonica: Inphino Blitz!! You have no business here! Inphino: I have no business at all, oracle…but when I find the Divine Prophetess and the Neo Void God hanging around in a cemetery, there’s bound to be some trouble afoot. Cyclonica: You should be on your way before the nVg loses patience with you, mortal. You know his patience was always thin when dealing with rodents like you. Inphino: I’ve been given the task of tracking you two down and making sure you two don’t bring about the destruction of the universe…so I think we’ll be seeing a lot more of each other. Cyclonica: You’re not a Void Element anymore…that’s not your job to do! Run off before you find yourself banished! Inphino: I’ve seen the Void…and existed within it for years…do you honestly think that you can threaten me with it? =©=In a defying move, Inphino removes his sunglasses and glares into Akiyama’s eyes with his equally dark own.=©= Inphino: Well?? Do you?? =©= The god moves forward a bit, but Blitz holds his hand out, pointing two fingers at him and shushing him softly.=©= Inphino: Shhh………Not yet… =©=Going over the limit, Akiyama roars and dives at Blitz. The two of them go at each other in a blaze of fists and feet all around the grave. Cyclonica returns back to the funeral at hand, reading the last bits of the speech.=©= Cyclonica: In conclusion, you will all be remembered after the god has destroyed your meager little tag team title world. Your images will go up into the skies as beacons of victory to the god, and your names will go down in the list of losers that have fallen at the mighty hands of the Neo Void God. SO SAYS THE DIVINE PROPHETESS…AMEN. =©=She tosses the clipboard into the grave and runs off as Inphino nails Akiyama with a superkick to the face. The god steps back at the edge of the grave as Inphino crouches in his martial arts stance.=©= Inphino: One foot in the grave, god…let’s see if you come back to life after three days… =©=Suddenly, a huge horn blares out from behind him. He turns around to find a huge dump truck backing up towards him. He dives out of the way just as a huge payload of soil comes pouring down upon the grave. As he rolls back to his feet, he sees nothing but the huge HAND OF FATE smacking into his face. He flies back into the mound of dirt, becoming semi-submerged as some of the dirt falls onto him. He looks up with a wince of pain as Akiyama grabs onto the edge of the speeding truck. Cyclonica looks back out of the driver’s window at the Black Dragon Master.=©= Cyclonica: See you soon, Black Dragon Master…hope you can stop us before we find the Savage Tranquility and destroy this world! I’ll be dreaming about you!! =©=She blows him a kiss and a wink as they speed off. Inphino pulls himself out of the pile of dirt and dusts himself off. He looks back at the huge mound of dirt and sighs.=©= Inphino: Shikata ga nai… =©=He walks over to the tombstone and picks up a shovel. Then he rolls up his sleeves and cracks his knuckles.=©= Inphino: It’s a dirty job…but someone has to do it. With that said and done…the Black Dragon Master has spoken… =©=As the martial artist digs to save the people buried alive, the camera zooms in on the tombstone behind him.=©= “HERE LIES THE PCW TAG TEAM DIVISION…RIP SUNDAY, JUNE 30, 2002. THE DESTINY OF THEIR WORLD CAME TO AN ABSOLUTE APOCALYPSE AT THE HANDS OF ONE INDIVIDUAL…AKIYAMA, THE NEO VOID GOD.” =©=
