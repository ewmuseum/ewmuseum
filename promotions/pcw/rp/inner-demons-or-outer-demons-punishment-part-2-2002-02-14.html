Inner Demons or Outer Demons? Punishment...Part 2

14 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-14-2002 Fear writ large across his pale face, the giant man runs headlong down a darkened alleyway. He constantly looks over his shoulder, seemingly afraid death itself chases after him. The man crashes headlong into a dumpster and sprawls in the filth that has collected in the dismal alleyway. He tries to crawl but freezes as a shadow suddenly blocks out the moonlight that had illumined his form. He slowly turns over into a sitting position, and his eyes grow even larger with what can only be described as unimaginable terror. The shadow grows larger and then the view suddenly changes. Now we look from the eyes of the hunted, rather than the hunter. In the darkness looms an enormous figure, long hair hangs down from its head, and in its right hand it wields a black bladed sword that glows in the darkness with an even pitcher black. Eyes that burn red peer from beneath the black shock of hair that hangs into its face, and finally, a surprisingly beautiful and melodious booming voice fills the air…. Figure: DEFILER! Thou would try to hide the fact that thou art a defiler of women and children! Thou wouldst hide from the law after thy grizzly murders….DEFILER! Man: Pl….Please, sir…You got the wrong guy!!! Figure: Even as we speak, earthly authorities search for thy worthless carcass….Let me assure you this… The figure draws his sword back and smiles, his teeth a contrasting white to the dark figure he presents…. Figure: Thy carcass is what they shalt find!!! With that, the sword arm drives forward and catches the man across the jaw with the side, knocking him backwards against the wall… Man: YARG!!! The man continues to scream in terror as the dark figure pummels him mercilessly. Soon the large man is a bloody mess, and all that escape his throat now are mindless whimpers. The dark figure becomes annoyed… Figure: Thou gets not the sweet release of unconsciousness, DEVIL! Thou hast one last punishment before mine work is finished… The figure rolls the large man onto his backside and places the tip of the black blade on the large fellows, umm…Rectal area… Figure: You wouldst rape and defile with THY sword….I now do the same with mine… The shot cuts to black and we hear a blood curdling scream….Poor fella… “I had given into the darkness, or was it really the light of truth? It consumed me and mine eyes burned with the power of judgment…The power of Punishment. It felt right; it felt good to dole out punishment upon the wicked.” The shot cuts back once more to the grizzly scene, and now the man is blood soaked from head to toe, while the lurker still hides in the shadow. He kneels and places one hand upon the victim’s neck; if one would call the man, Victim… Figure: Thou livest still. Thy crimes shall be paid for in both an earthly court, and the court of the Angels above. And thy insurrection shall cost thee dearly. I judge thee damned among men…Damned among souls….Damned, and thy eternity awaits, Defiler. The man shakes as he lies upon the ground, and reluctantly he raises his eyes to meet the red orbs of the defiler. In a small, weak voice, the massive man, beaten to submission, and raped how no man wishes to be, questions: Man: what are you? Demon? Devil? Why??? The figure bellows laughter, and the man seems to shrink even more. His eyes are still filled with mortal fear as he tries to look away, but finds his eyes continually drawn back… Figure: Must thou ask? I am the light and the dark. I am the judge as was prophesized. I am the punisher of the wicked, and the saviour of the good. Mine brothers were here before me, as prophets and saints…And now I come. And I will right the wrongs in the world that He has created for all souls. I am the King, and my rule? Final. With a swift kick, the man is knocked unconscious, and the figure laughs again as he walks into the darkness, sirens blaring in the distance…. ****************************************** Pendragon awakes with a start in his massive bed, and he wipes his sweat soaked forehead as he rolls from the bed onto his feet…. Pendragon: AYE!!! But that stone floor is COLD!!! He hops gingerly across the room and pulls out a door from his dresser. He brandishes a pair of fuzzy slippers, drops them on the floor and slips his feet inside. Pendragon: Ahhhh….Much better. He heads for the door, grabs his robe from a hook and goes into the hall…. ****FLASH**** We are now in what appears to be a medieval dining hall. Pendragon sits at the head of a large oak table, and a serving wench brings his breakfast and a morning paper… Wench: Sire…How was thy sleep? Pendragon’s eyes cloud up, but he keeps his cool… Pendragon: Been having very vivid dreams lately, Victoria. I know not the meaning of them, but they haunt the night. Victoria: Thou wilt find thy answer, if indeed there be one, sire… She places his food in front of him, a simple bowl of oatmeal and toast. She hands him the paper, curtsies, and takes her leave. Pendragon grabs a small pitcher and pours some milk onto the oats as he unfolds the paper. Suddenly, his eyes grow large and he drops the pitcher on the floor. It crashes down and shatters, but he pays it no mind. His view is plastered onto the front page of the paper… “Nighttime Redeemer? Front page story chronicles the work of a “Nighttime Redeemer”? What treachery is this?” Pendragon looks warily at the paper a moment before dropping it and heading out the nearest door. The shot zooms in and we see the paper for ourselves….. ‘And the criminals believed nighttime was their time. Last night was not the case. Gregory Stevens, wanted in connection with five rape-murders, was apprehended in a most bizarre fashion last night. Police were called because of screams echoing in an alleyway in North Fairfax, and they were horrified with what they found. “He was just a mess.” According to officer William Bennett. “Not the way I would wanna have my career end” said Don Disbennet. Beaten and sodomized with a very sharp object, Stevens is expected to recover and serve justice for his crimes. When taken in, he repeated the same thing over and over according to officers on the scene… “The King of the Night….The Night King….King of Darkness….King of Light….” Stevens was taken to Memorial Hospital, where he is under suicide watch and constant psychological observance………….. Dreams huh? We shall see……… TO BE CONTINEUD -FINE-
