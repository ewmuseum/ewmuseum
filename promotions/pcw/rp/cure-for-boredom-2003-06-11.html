Cure for Boredom

11 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.11.03 June 11th, only the third day that the PCW Airship blessed the air with the presence of ‘Primetime.’ And it was ALREADY fucking boring. The former PWA ‘Coming Storm’ Champion found himself sitting alone in his room on the third deck of the Cloud Nine airship. He sat in a small padded chair, leaning it on it’s back legs and propping his feet up on the soft, white linens of the bed and staring aimlessly out the window with his hands behind his head. Ugh, he couldn’t stand not being busy. And here he was, wasting away in his room. But what was there for him to do? Ultimately, ‘Cloud Nine’ had been designed to accommodate anyone and everyone. It was designed to offer enough opportunities of fun to last someone the whole trip. But Silvio saw no point in any of them. He could eat but he wasn’t hungry. His stomach still had the odd feelings of anxiousness and nervousness bubbling inside of it. The feeling had been steadily growing, even more so as Fusion approached. He could gamble; his pockets would supply him with enough money to let him pull the handle of the slot machine down for the rest of his life. But what would the point of that be? He could go to the sundeck and watch the single wrestlers and other hopeful guys try and get themselves a hot date and fail miserably! But he had no desire to sit in the hot, sticky air of the room, his nostrils invaded with the aroma of chlorine, only to watch something he’d seen one hundred times over. No, he would not be leaving his room until necessary, he decided. ‘Cloud Nine’ was cheap entertainment, and it was packed with hundreds of people whom he did not like or did not want to be associated with. If boredom was the price to pay, so be it. But he HAD to do something! He looked away from the window and down to his leather briefcase, which sat leaning against the single wooden post supporting the table in front of him. Pulling his feet of the bed, he leaned forward in his chair, letting the front legs hit the carpeted floor with a barely audible ‘thunk’ and grabbed his finely crafted briefcase. Scritch, scritch, scritch… His fingers fumbled over with the rotating dials of numbers, lining up the correction combination. Pop, pop! He lifted off the latches and pushed open the top, setting the briefcase on the tabletop. Sitting inside the briefcase was a manila folder, sprawled open. Its contents had been fifty plus pages of typed material, printed pictures and another miscellaneous pieces of information in a neat stack. Now they sat in a heap, facing every which way. Oh, whatever… Silvio picks up the briefcase and turns it upside-down sending all the contents spilling onto the tabletop, some spilling off onto the black-carpeted floor. He tosses the briefcase onto the bed and picks a random page from the top of the file. Matt Jackson’s official PCW biography… which offered no helpful information, as it only spoke on how ‘not much was known’ of him and it dwelled very little on anything important. Oh well. It had been the most obvious place to start when Silvio had begun to do his homework on ‘The Agent.’ Silvio chuckles and crumples the paper into a tightly compacted ball and tosses it over his left shoulder. It bounces off the tope-painted wall and onto an oaken dresser with an empty top. The people at PCW had been nice enough to furnish his room with a place to store his clothing. Real thoughtful of them, wasn’t it? He picks up another piece of paper from the stack and glances at it. Typed in bold font, centered on the middle of the document “CIA: Can’t Identify Anything! ‘Celebrating’ 50 years of Mishaps and Misgivings!” A quick skimming of the document revealed what had been called the ten biggest intelligence failures of the last fifty years. The article blatantly insulted the agency, claiming the “I” in CIA stood for impotency! How ironic that Matt Jackson would be employed by a government agency so easily made fun of because of their huge intelligence failure. I guess none of the threats just seemed ‘credible enough’. Gee, how IRONIC it is that Matt Jackson works for an agency that has a bad reputation for not taking some things seriously enough and there were often serious consequences to pay. How fucking ironic that Matt Jackson thinks of me as ‘not credible,’ yet all this ‘not credible’ shit on this page turned out to be serious mishaps and stains on the CIA’s record! And how ironic it’s going to be when I, a ‘non-credible threat’ turn out to be an INCREDIBLE threat and come crashing down on his ass like a 747 to the Twin Towers of 9/11! Fuck, this is great! Yeah, we can save this page for later… He sets the page aside, on the bed where he wouldn’t lose it, and grabbed another that faced down on the stack. He flipped it up and found himself staring into the magnificently blue eyes of one Yulia Volkova, half of t.A.t.U. —Some Russian singing duo that Silvio had frankly never heard of and he wasn’t sure if he ever wanted to—and Matt Jackson’s prized guest on ‘Cloud Nine’. Wow, she must get a lot of teenage fans… Horny little bastards looking for a rise in their under shorts, I bet they stare at her for hours and just dream and fantasize about her. Probably already having girlfriends they claim they ‘love’ and all, but sleazing around like they’ve got something to prove. Maybe so, but she was cute. Maybe even beautiful! A ‘prized’ guest indeed, and she was also a weakness. And that made her even more appealing than her doll-faced looks. Matt Jackson had been so overly protective of her. He had been the eagle’s wing over the baby chicks to her. But she was still vulnerable. Ha… Might as well set this one aside too, this could be useful. Rochelli places the sheet of paper on top of the last, also on the bed. He quickly snatches another paper and this time Matt Jackson’s OTHER piece of pussy, Haley Hilton, looks back at him. While clearly not as beautiful as Yulia, it was no secret that ‘The Agent’ had been associated with her over time. …And then it hit him, like a steaming Amtrak train flying 100 miles an hour over the tracks… like a ton of bricks… like a speeding semi-truck creaming some deer memorized by the big rig’s headlights. Suddenly, there was work to do! Rochelli’s mind was working again at a pace that only his could, and he was no longer bored. …June 11th, only two more days until Fusion. Only two more days until Matt Jackson and those other two people, whats-his-face and no-named-bitch. Two more days until the match… and something sinister was brewing in the mind of one Silvio Rochelli.
