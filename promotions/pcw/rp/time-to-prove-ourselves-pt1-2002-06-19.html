Time to prove ourselves -->pt1

19 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06.19.02 :.fade in.: It has been three weeks since me and my brother have been in the ring. What have we been doing for the time off? Well I wouldn't consider it time off. Since coming into PCW we have had two matches. Both which are the top teams in PCW to date. The former champions The Heartbreakers and Cowabunga Connection. The Heartbreakers in our last match showed us why they were the champions, they work good together as a team, they are known in PCW. That doesn't bother me or Ron. I can tell you this right now, we got into the business not for the fans but for ourselves. That goes for the same in PCW. We are going to be apart of a huge Pay Per View on the 30th, is that on our mind...hell yeah it is!! It's for the most prized posession in PCW, and it's for something that me and Ron are no stranger to, the tag team titles. We've held our own, we've felt success in many promotions. So none of them really had the calliber that PCW has....don't get me wrong though, that doesn't mean we are a soft team. Like I said, the pay per view is huge but we do have something before that and that is Turmoil. Coming home from a workout with Ron at the local gym I can see that on the table was the wrestling magazine which I subscribed too. I looked inside and see that we are booked for Turmoil. We are facing a man that has been in the buisness for some time now. But in the headlines it was just his name and knowone else's. Questions arose....who will his partner be? The way me and Ron see it, it doesn't matter who Akiyama's partner is. We have never backed down from a match that had high stakes like this one. 'But it's not for the titles'. Your right, it doesn't have to be for the titles it's all about competing, and competing agaisnt the best. This match could be in our favor, Why? Well Akiyama is not used to wrestling in a tag match, but we won't take him lightly, he has proved himself as a singles competitor and he is damn good, damn good.....but for Akiyama, he may not know who Suicidal Tendencies are, and that doesn't matter, but he shouldn't take us lightly, we are in this for ourselves, we are in it to hurt some people, and we are in it to PROVE OURSELVES. :.fade out - to be continued.:
