Personal Confrontation

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th June 2002 Dreams... Some people believe that dreams are windows into our sub-conscious that they tell us what is really going on inside their heads. Allot of Psychologists have used people's dreams to figure out what sort of person someone is, for example someone good be happy and jolly on the surface but deep inside they could be tormented souls. Indeed dreams tell us a lot about how we are feeling about things. People can go to bed with problems in their thoughts and when they wake up they feel like the world has been lifted off their shoulders. However there is a bad side to dreams, a much more darker and sinister side. This side is where dreams engulf the person's mind and take them deep into their own sub-conscious where their true fears lie. Dreams can be terrifying and that is when they become dangerous... Like everyone else The Dark Messiahs sleep and dream. They dream like everyone else about success and far off lands, but sometimes their dreams go that extra mile. What I mean by this is that when the twisted duo dream sometimes their dreams become doors within their minds that show them the reality of what they really are. However the message of the dream never gets through to Steel and Stone because every time they wake up, they believe all it was, was just a dream. Come with me as we journey into the mind of Steel and Stone, and realise the truth of what they really are... The scene opens up inside an abandoned gym. There is something different about this gym, there seems to be mysterious atmosphere surrounding this gym. There is a cold chill in the air that makes the gym feel uncomfortable to be in. The wood panel floor below creeks with the slightest movement. The gym's walls are peeling away, and the water pipe that runs along the wall leaks causing damp on the walls. It is quite obvious that this is place has not been used for a while. The thing that stands out the most in this gym is the ring in the centre. The ring is the only thing that is truly highlighted by the 1 source of light this gym has, the light bulb hanging over the ring. The light doesn't highlight the entire ring however; it just seems to highlight the centre of the ring in one big circle of light. The corners and the sides of the ring are left in the dark. Soon into the circle of light step Steel and Stone with bewildered looks on their faces... Daniel Steel: (Confused) How on earth did we end up here? Stone just shrugs his shoulder as he can still not speak... Daniel Steel: This place gives me the creeps and it smells funny. I still don't understand why we are here, I mean a minute ago we were at the premiere of the new Star Wars Movie and now we are here. It just doesn't make any... Before Steel can finish a voice similar to Steel's comes from out of the darkness... Voice: Sense? Steel snaps around to see who just said that, but he can't see anyone... Daniel Steel: (Shocked) Who was that? Where are you? Show yourself! Voice: (Evil laugh) You already know who I am, Daniel... As the voice says this 2 cloaked figures step into the light a few centimetres away for Steel and Stone. The smaller cloaked figure speaks, obviously the voice we heard before... Cloaked Figure 1: We are the ones who watch you as you struggle through the PCW. We are the ones who wait in the darkness, waiting for a chance to see the light. Daniel Steel: (Angry and confused) What the hell are you talking about? Who are you? Both cloaked figures laugh evilly and the smaller figure speaks again... Cloaked Figure 1: Why are you so naive Daniel? Isn't it obvious who we are? We are one, we are... Both cloaked figures pull their hoods down to reveal that they are, Steel and Stone?!?! Cloaked Daniel Steel: (Smiling) You! The original Steel and Stone jump back in freight at the sight of themselves standing right in front of them... Daniel Steel: (Shocked) What the... The cloaked Mike Stone steps forward and speaks... Cloaked Mike Stone: (Smiling) Is it so hard to believe? Have you never wondered why some days you wake up and find yourself standing in front of a bloody body? The original Mike Stone steps forward but because of his throat still cannot speak. Steel instead steps forward... Daniel Steel: (Still shocked) This isn't possible, how can you be me, when I’m me? Cloaked Daniel Steel: (Angry) Oh open your eyes Steel! Jesus Christ sometimes I think you refuse to admit the truth. Look at me, I am you. I am the side of you that keeps you going, that makes sure you aren't destroyed by this world's society. You have lost both your matches in the PCW because you refuse to let us out! Daniel Steel: (Confused) What do you mean, 'let you out'? Cloaked Daniel Steel: (Frustrated) You really don't have a clue do you? We are the ones that will not only allow you to win at Fusion but also we will be able to help you get what you grave the most, the PCW Tag Team Championships. Daniel Steel: (Curious) How? Cloaked Mike Stone: (Smiles) We know how much you grave the tag team championships, but the only way you are going to get them is to let us take control. We know the opponents’ weaknesses, we know how to break them, but we won't be able to utilise this knowledge until you allow us to break free. Fusion, (Points to original Stone) you face 3 other men, 3 other men who with their team mates seem to be climbing the ladder of success and leaving you 2 behind. You know why you are being left behind? Because you won't except the fact that we exist. You talk about how you want the tag team championships, but let me ask, how close are you to actually winning the tag team titles? Daniel Steel: (Proudly) We've got a shot at the tag team titles at Gold and Glory, actually. Cloaked Daniel Steel: Yes you may have a tag team title shot but are you the only one's getting that shot? Daniel Steel: (Ashamed) Not exactly no. Cloaked Daniel Steel: Exactly, and if you had accepted our existence straight away at the very beginning it would have been you, and you alone getting the title shot at Gold and Glory. But now you have to go up against at least 5 other teams. Now how are you going to survive in the state you are now? You’re not, plain and simple. You may think you are unstoppable in the state you are now but you win/loss record doesn't seem to show that, now does it? Daniel Steel: (Angry) What makes you so shore that you can get us the tag team championships? Cloaked Daniel Steel: (Laughs) Because quite simply Daniel, we can. We can take things to such extremes that everyone in our path is wiped out. We taste violence! We can feel it pump through our veins every second of every day! We are one with violence! (Grows angry) But day after day we are locked away in the darkness, watching you destroy what we are, and we have had enough of it. You want respect then allow us our freedom and I assure you that once we are free respect will soon follow! Daniel Steel: (Thinking) You know I have stood here for the last few minutes listening to you slag us off, and quite frankly I have had enough. Cloaked Daniel Steel: (Laughs) We are not slagging you off my dear friend, we are trying to make you realise the power and respect that you could hold if you just allowed us to... Before the cloaked Steel could have finished the original Steel had smacked him in the face with a flying dropkick, and the original Stone had smacked the cloaked Stone with a hard right hand. Both cloaked Steel and Stone stagger back holding their faces. The cloaked Steel looks up and smiles evilly... Cloaked Daniel Steel: That is it my friends allow the rage to take over, and soon we will be free! With that both cloaked Steel and Stone disappear leaving 2 empty robes on the mat. Both original Steel and Stone look at each other. As they do the scene changes... We are now facing Steel and Stone in a split screen way, both just waking up from what was just a dream. Steel looks around him... Daniel Steel: (Bewildered) Weird. With the last words of the cloaked Steel echoing inside their heads, both men fall back into their bed and back to sleep as the scene fades to black...
