Y.D: The Khmer Soldier

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th June 2002 As the story of Charlie Loc continued its course, the young Vietnamese Viper found himself in a spot of trouble in a seedy club somewhere in downtown Saigon. Talks and deals went down and certain matters were resolved, but at the same time others were left standing….In fact there was only one thing standing, and that thing happened to be Cambodian and about 6’2'. Perhaps losing his job in PCW had given him a chance to learn a lesson that he needed to learn a long time ago, or maybe it was just another way of Charlie having to prove his worth. What did time have in store for him though? Who or what is 'The Khmer'? Why is it that nights seem to drag on forever in Saigon?Maybe Charlie could finally bring an end to one of his problems before the end of his first….or possibly his last cold night in Saigon, only time could tell…… Young And Dangerous Pt III: The Khmer Soldier Nighttime would seem nothing more than a wake-up call…for the city of Saigon anyway. As it would seem that as the hours of the night wore on and the time grew deeper and deeper into night itself, everything and everyone would wake up to it’s every beckon and call…..with the strip clubs and the karaoke bars all in full swing the clock of Saigon began to slowly tick over near 1am…. WHOOSH~! Night itself wasn’t regarded as being cold, but then again once the clock had ticked over past 12am it was regarded as early morning and with morning, not just cold, but blistering cold was associated as a shrill blast of chilly wind picked dirt off of the ground and carted it into the dark sky over the city. The wind and the dirt wouldn’t seem to phase to many as most night goers would be bundled up in jackets or jumpers with other thick sorts of clothing. The only people that would really have to worry about the dirt and the chilly winds would be the equally as dirty Vietnamese Prostitutes that could be found lurking in dark alleys or wandering the streets looking for someone to satisfy! For 1am the city itself seemed as alive it ever would be with just about every man and his dog out doing something in what would seem to be nothing other than the 'Red Light District' of Saigon. As the breeze began to pick up yet again the huge neon sign of the underground club 'La Singh' stood out like a beacon, spreading its warmth over the street and attracting anyone and everyone to come inside and bask in its glory! Although it was 1am the club was still filled to the brim….either that or the bouncers just couldn’t be bothered letting people enter because the line seemed just as crowded and just as long as it was little more than a few hours ago! The only difference being that half the people standing in queue were now off their head’s and bumping to the music outside the club as it would probably be very early morning before they got in. BOOM~! BOOM~! BOOM~! BANG~! The beats and strobe lights from inside the club flooded out loud and clear onto the pavement sending a wave of excitement through the people lining up in the queue as the huge double doors to the underground joint opened up, letting out possibly one of Saigon’s biggest threats….. As the doors swung open out stepped none other than Charlie Loc who was standing next to his new found friend Sapiet and followed closely behind by the rest of the N.G boys, all bundled up in their huge puffy jackets and baggy pants, some wearing beanies and caps, other just with their hair done…all of them looking like trouble! The women in the line begin to giggle and point as they took their time staring down the group that had just exited the club as none of the leaders nor the rest of the crew seemed to care what was going on. As the noise continued to flood onto the street Sapiet placed his arm around Charlie’ shoulder and waved his hand to the bouncers who nodded and slammed the doors shut, blocking off the noises and lights from inside the club as Charlie and the N.G boys began to disembark from La Singh. It seemed like crime got an early wake up call in Saigon…..as Sapiet, Charlie and the rest of the N.G boys walked down the street looking menacing they seemed to just attract pairs of eyes and attention because just about every head sitting on every balcony or lining up to get into a club swiveled to take a peak. Prostitutes hesitated to reach out and flirt with them as they walked down the cold street of Saigon as the chilly breeze began to blow in the night once more causing the huge group of people to pull their jackets tightly over their shoulders and dig their hands deep into their huge deep pockets! For some reason by looking at the crew walking down the crowded street it’d seem that they had more than just hands tucked away into those deep pockets and more to eye than what they seemed! And for some other strange reason it seemed as though they weren’t just out for a very, very early morning stroll….. Maybe it was just by the look on their faces or maybe it was just by the way that they walked in the large group of about 15 Viets, but either way from the naked eye it would like they were out searching from trouble but if they kept going the way they were….trouble would soon find them! As the group neared the end of the main street, Sapiet wearing a dark jacket and silver jeans nodded his head towards an alley just off the right hand side of the edge of the street as his hair fluttered in the breeze and Charlie stopped dead in his tracks. There was no hint of what was going through his mind as his usual ice-cold expression was covering his literally ice-cold face as it could be seen he was trying hard not to shiver due to the freezing night. Just behind Sapiet could be seen with a sly grin crossing his face as he came up behind Charlie and produced a cigarette. As Sapiet sparked the cigarette the smoke trailed off into the breeze as he watched his 'boys' gather up and then reach into their jackets, producing long and shiny objects before walking off down the alley into the darkness before motioning for him to follow. Something seemed fishy….but there was no way to quiet put a finger on it! Maybe it was the fact that no one spoke, maybe it was the fact that a group of crazy looking Vietnamese were walking down a dark alley carrying huge objects….whatever it was it smelt fishy! As the teenagers walking down the alley motioned for Sapiet and Charlie to follow neither of them hesitated, it seemed that whatever they were doing they were either prepared or preparing themselves! Whatever it may be they didn’t seem to be too happy about it! As they all made their way through the dark alley it seemed like something out of a bedtime story turned wrong! A foul stench carried itself in the enclosed walls and puddles of water could be heard as the N.G group made their way through! The outlines of graffiti could be seen covering just about every inch of the seemingly brick wall as the group continued to trudge through, walking to a 'light' somewhat at the end of the tunnel…alley! Through the sounds of boots splashing in puddles and the occasional dark object being kicked over, rats could be heard and seen as with every step a muffled squeak would soon follow which would then be followed by nothing more than a huge, plump silhouette. No one seemed to really care….it was like they were all in some sort of trance! But after a few minutes of trudging through the dark alley everyone found themselves at the light at the end of the tunnel as they entered a huge circular area with a patch of dirt right in the middle! There was no other way to describe it other than an abandoned, back-street baseball field and it would seem exactly that because swinging on it’s rusty hinges somewhere on a wall on the far side of the field was nothing but a black scoreboard with a white number 6 dangling down being blown around with the breeze.As Sapiet took another draw from his cigarette his boys began to surround the area forming a tight line protecting the alley from which they entered and scouting out the area that they would seemingly be doing 'business' on.! As one of the teenagers moved into the pale moonlight, his hand flashed in front and it became clear that all of them were carrying rather large machetes and if looks could be deceiving then they were also stained with dark blood which put some 'light' so to speak on the situation! Everything began to make sense…or possibly a little as the teenagers surrounded where they entered and began to squat down, removing their jackets and showing huge muscly arms covered with tattoos underneath as a few of them pulled out some cigarettes and began to smoke staring off ahead in the distance. Through the whole ordeal no one spoke….until finally Sapiet took a draw from his cigarette and after exhaling a thick smoke ring off into the frosty air a slight smile crossed over his face as he tapped Charlie Loc on the back of the shoulder…not even causing him to flinch. 'I guess it no backing out now….we finally going to solve this Khmer problem! The problem you started Charlie! I hope you go through with this, because if you don’t….the Khmer aren’t going to be your only problem la!' Sapiet’s voice carries off into the cool wind as Charlie stands motionless with his hands in his pockets. Sapiet takes yet another draw from his cigarette before reaching over Charlie’s shoulder and holding it out as Charlie doesn’t even look, but raises his hand and accepts it, taking silent draws and blowing them off into the night air….sort of relieving some stress! After Sapiet’s words everything returns to silence as the moonlight begins to cast a soft glow over the abandoned playing field somewhere in the back-streets of Saigon. It seems as though it’s just nestled away waiting for a big event like what’s about go down to happen…. Suddenly the peaceful silence is cut as the wind howls and blows a cloud over the moon causing the playing field to be clouded in darkness as the N.G boys who are squatting down can all be seen slowly climbing onto their feet expecting their enemies! Charlie removes his jacket and throws it onto the ground next to him as Sapiet tucks his hands into his jacket and leans over and whispers into Charlie’s ear. 'It on now Charlie…you can’t back down now! Don’t be afraid….' Sapiet can be heard sniggering cutting across the breeze some sort as Charlie’s hair begins to flutter in the wind as the cloud slowly moves away from moon casting it’s light back over the field and revealing a small army of about 20 dark looking teenagers, brandishing machetes standing on the opposite side of the playing field. The N.G Boys show no fear as they are clearly out-numbered but all the same they step up to make the challenge as the crew that lines the other side of the playing field can be seen communicating with each other in a foreign language. Suddenly out of nowhere a huge, built, dark Asian standing at about 6’3' walks out wearing nothing more than a tank-top, heavily tattooed pointing towards Charlie and then at the middle of the playing field as all eyes become focused on Charlie. Being someone who’s used to a lot of attention Charlie doesn’t seem to notice nor care as he removes the tank top he’s wearing to reveal the tattoo’s of the dragon and 'Immortal' on his arms as he takes a deep breath in, pushes his hair back and walks out to face his challenge. At the same time the huge, dark looking teenager makes his way into the center of the field to meet Charlie and settle whatever it is that they had to settle as they both cease walking about 5ft in front of each other as Charlie stares up at the dark looking teenager. A white grin crosses over the dark looking teenagers face as he begins to speak with a very strange accent in the Vietnamese dialect. 'You remember me Charlie? Yeah that right, I the one who say I going to take off your fingers! Don’t worry I not forgotten about that!' A sinister grin crosses over Charlie’s face as he holds his hands up with his fingers spread apart and he snaps back with a smart ass comment. 'Can you count? I see 10….you tell me I not going to have any left? You Nothing…..' Charlie shakes his head as he places his hands back down. The huge dark teenager begins to get angry and his face turns from dark brown to red in an instant as he can clearly be seen revving up in the moonlight as he yells back at Charlie. 'This is it….you think you can fuck with Soldiers?! I GOING TO KILL YOU!' The huge Khmer Teenager roars as he throws a massive fist right at Charlie as both crews begin to advance on the center of the field as the two men begin to fight in the middle. The Khmer proving that he has muscle but no brains as he lugs another punch at Charlie who side-steps and sends a quick snap kick into his mid section but not even causing him to stagger back. The Khmer stops for a second and wipes a bead of sweat away from his eyebrow as he begins chuckle to himself as Charlie drops back into a fighting stance. 'YOU NEVER BEAT ME!' Once again the Khmer lets out a mighty roar as he lashes out with a combination of knees and punches, Charlie having a hard time to block them from the huge boy takes a few and falls backwards before quickly rolling back onto his feet off of the dirt as the Khmer pounces and misses. Charlie wastes no time as he dives on top of him and sends his elbow cracking into the side of the Khmer’s head once….twice…three times as his eyebrow splits and blood begins to draw! Suddenly the Khmer lurches him from on top as the two begin to tussle in the dirt as the crew clearly begin to have tension between each other as they watch their warriors fight. It seems like an olden day battle, the two leaders fighting for their crews honor….only this time the problem was that someone was going to jump in sometime soon as sooner or later someone would get annoyed that the other had the upper-hand and lash out! Finally the two stopped rolling and the dirt began to shake out of Charlie’s hair as the Khmer began to choke him, making Charlie’s eyes bulge out of his head and drain the very life out of his small frame. All this was done with a gleaming white smile on the Khmer’s face! Suddenly out of nowhere, a massive boot flung into the back of the Khmer’s head as he lurched over the top of Charlie and Charlie capitalized sending his boot straight into the back of his head yet again before he locked on a sleeper hold and began to strangle the Khmer in return! Finally one of the Khmer’s couldn’t take it anymore and just as he leaned back with the machete….it was on! Both crews collided in a frenzy of blades that glistened in the moonlight as Charlie and the Khmer leader continued to fight it out in the middle of the fray! Blood began to splatter on the floors as cries rang out the playing field as Charlie and the Khmer leader seemed oblivious to it all as they picked themselves up and began to exchange blows in the very heat of the battle! Just as the upper-hand was swinging in the way of the N.G there was a loud noise of cutting wind as the cool breeze began to pick up again. Looks of fear began to cross over either side’s faces as they dropped their weapons and helped their wounded and disappeared off into the darkness from which they came as a helicopter appeared over the top of the playing field shining its pale blue light directly on Charlie! It seemed Charlie was in a fight of rage because he didn’t seem to notice….instead he picked up a machete dripping with blood and sent it slicing across the Khmer’s upper arm to which he roared and produced his own butterfly knife and swung back before falling to his stomach. For once in his life a look of sheer rage crossed Charlie’s face as he looked at the gash on his arm and reared back with the huge blade ready to swing at the fallen Khmer when red and blue flashing lights began to spin around the walls of the playing field and sirens began to echo…. All of a sudden out of nowhere, police began to rush at Charlie and the Khmer! Charlie frozen holding the machete back ready to strike, directly below the Helicopter with his hair blowing and the spotlight directly on him showing a thick pool of blood surrounding the Khmer at his feet. Life was suddenly bought onto the playing field as from every angle police and ambulance officers rushed in, the paramedics hauling the wounded Khmer away and the police officers dragging off a psychotic looking Charlie…. With hair matted to his face and sweat, blood and dirt trickling over his body Charlie just grinned his sinister grin and let himself be taken away! His job was done but maybe sorting out one of his troubles just lead to another larger problem! The Vietnamese Viper had proven his worth to the N.G and had finally proven why his name was given…..the only question left was 'What Was Going To Happen To Him Now?' The darkness began to envelope around him as the door of the paddy wagon slammed shut leaving Charlie alone in the darkness of the cell, being driven off to face his fate……. To Be Continued….
