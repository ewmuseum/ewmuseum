The Wishmaster --- The Last Hurrah, Part 6 (Retirement Party II)

29 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/29/02 Inside the ballroom, there are people everywhere. Wrestlers and movie stars, recording artists, and just general well-wishers... and otherwise. They're all here mingling or not, making themselves at home. The End's retirement party at the Disney Resorts's Marriott Hotel in Orlando, FL has filled the ballroom, capable of holding 300 people. The End is conversing with some of his guests. Talking with Abner at the moment. But first, the dark-red tuxedo'ed Nerezza walks up to Trigger, who is sitting at the bar that this ballroom isn't supposed to have, wearing a white tank top and jeans. He holds a half-empty bottle of Geikkeikan sake in his hands. Nerezza walks up to him. Nerezza:'You again...' Trigger gives no response. Nerezza shakes his head. Nerezza:'You don't sign on for speaking parts, do you?' It doesn't seem as though Trigger is breathing, he's not blinking eyes half-open in a glazed over look. Certainly not acknowledging Nerezza. The vampire remembers The End's last party celebrating his win of the #1 Contendership to the Extreme Title, where Trigger acted much the same. Figuring that this is another party that's partly a celebration of The End's #1 Contendership to the Continental Title, Nerezza assumes that if he nudges Trigger, that the Infinite Warrior will explode and leave his wallet again. Nerezza: *Tapping Trigger on the shoulder*'3..... 2...... 1!' Trigger spontaneously combusts, then explodes in a small orange mushroom cloud. Nothing is left of Trigger. But his wallet remains on the seat, and the bottle of sake on the bar. Nerezza nods with a smirk and grabs the wallet. *BOOM!* This time Trigger's wallet is set with a failsafe and also explodes. Nerezza doesn't get to steal it this time. Shrugging his shoulders, he reaches for the bottle of sake, feeling parched but not having any Rezz Bull on hand. *POOF!* The bottle's small mushroom cloud sends it into nothingness. Nerezza:'Dammit!' From behind Nerezza, Miami Dolphins Quarterback Jay Fiedler calls to Nerezza. Fiedler:'Hey, Rezz, catch!' Fiedler, clad in full home gear, tosses the ball to Nerezza... and it sails over his head and breaks the window behind him. Michael Vick, who's just wearing a red generic jersey and black slacks, shoves Fiedler aside. Vick:'Move yo' ass ova! Rezz, catch this!' Nerezza once again holds his hands out and Vick bullets a pass straight at his chest. Nerezza takes a step back, the pass that hard. But as soon as he catches the ball-- *Boom!* ... It explodes in his hands. Mani Shigeru, walking up in a long-sleeved denim shirt and jeans, though his mask is over his face, communicates with Nerezza. Mani:'.....' Nerezza:'YOU THINK?! Yes, I really didn't notice that everything I touch just blows up.' Mani:'......' Nerezza:'Yeah, I'll probably leave before I destroy anything else.' Magnum Wolf walks up in a pair of dusty Levi's jeans and an unbuttoned green shirt. Wolf:'Don't you dare. We can become a team, you and I.' Shaking his head, Nerezza declines. Nerezza:'No thanks, this will probably only last until the RP is over. And besides, I've had enough of teams for now.' *And somewhere off in Las Vegas, Gaz Green is probably saying, 'I hate you, too.'* Magnum Wolf shrugs his shoulders. The End walks up with much the same motion reaction to Nerezza's statement. The End:'Fair enough. But could you just work your... stuff on him?' The End nods his head and points his thumb over his shoulder in the direction of Erik Blake. He's stark-naked with a radio sticker on his ass. He wears a cowboy hat, a years-old used condom, a gold necklace, blue scub cap, and there is a tattoo down his right leg that says 'Porn Star'. He is currently chasing after Tempest, left alone while Ivan Stanislav is in the bathroom. Nerezza:'Make him go boom?' The End:'Please.' Looking over at Mani, Nerezza sees the mute one nodding his head. Nerezza:'Very well...' Nerezza walks off to explode Erik Blake. Magnum Wolf holds up his lighter. Wolf:'Hey, End, wanna make the party go up in flames?' The End shakes his head 'no.' The End:'Nah... Sandy spent a lot of time and energy putting it together, and since it's for me I don't really feel like it. But if you see something or someone that I'm not liable for, feel free to go nuts.' Wolf:'Will do!' Mani silently laughs a Magnum Wolf and he proceeds to carry on a short telepathic conversation with The End. Nerezza is trying to get Erik Blakce to stand still, Blake still sexually harassing Tempest. Nerezza:'Hey, Blake! Come here!' Blake:'No, thank you, I'd rather cum on her.' Running past the door to the men's bathroom, the door swings open and Blake is blindsided with a big boot from Ivan Stanislav! Wishing not to touch the mongrel (same as Nerezza, who's given up and walked away), Stanislav merely addresses the crowd with his booming, commanding voice. Stanislav:'Shall anyone else pester the Little Storm?' Everyone silently shakes their heads, 'no' in unison. Stanislav:'Nyet...' Stanislav walks back over to Tempest, who refuses to hug him because he didn't wash his hands. Walking back to the bathroom, his large black boots trail toilet paper, Ivan's head is held high, but his eyes sink to the floor. A laugh from the mass of guests is silenced by a hostile growl from the Big Red Machine. Everyone thinks it was Stanislav, but he already went into the bathroom. No, it was the other Big Red Machine. Except, of course, that his costume is now 10% red, so the nickname really doesn't apply. But mask, ring outfit, single glove and all, the Big Red Machine is in the party. His leather-jacket and jean-wearing partner is also there, his dark face and thick-braided dreads blending in with Duzza and Dante Jackson, Snoop Dogg and Dr. Dre, and the other black people who are sticking to the back of the room because Statuz Quo set the equality movement back by 80 years. Thanks, Quo! The dreadlocked black man nudges the Big Red Machine in the ribs. Booker T:'Shut up, sucka! We suppos'ta be keepin' a low profile!' Kane:'Tell that to Goldust, he's comparing vagina size with Jenna Jameson outside.' Booker T:'TELL ME you didn't just say that!' Kane: 'I wish it wasn't true... Anyway, why are we even here?' Booker T shakes his head. Booker T:'Man...' {FLASHBACK ALERT!} Houston, Texas, the hometown of Dante Jackson. He's holding a house party here for whatever reason. Many people were invited. And though the party is still far from over a 1 AM in the morning at Dante Jackson's main estate, two people have decided to take a break from trying to burn it down, and make a quick detour across the street... Wolf:'Why are we here, again?' The End:'If we're going to burn down Dante's party, it's only fair to let this guy get in on it, as well.' Wolf:'We're getting an accomplice?' The End shakes his head as he pours the gasoline along the outer walls of the Houston, Texas estate of one Booker Huffman, known to people around the world as Booker T. There is little security, there's no real need for it. No one wants to rob him. The End:'No, we're going to burn down this guy's house. And then we'll burn down Dante's house. And when he does that multi-person 80's RP we'll try to burn down something in there, as well.' Wolf:'Huh?' The End:'Never mind.' Upstairs in the bedroom, which has a victorian king-sized bed with the white sheets flung on the floor, with the armoir against the wall by the window. Booker T is naked, sitting on one end of the bed. He's angry. On the other end, his wife Charmice. She's also naked, but her look is one of guilt and apology. Booker T turns to Charmice. Booker T:'I knew you were seein' that Iaukea punk behind my back! Shoulda figured it out when you kep' askin' me to call you Paisley.' Charmice:'It's just the gimmick. I like it.' Booker T shows his anger and disbelief as best he can. Booker T:'My big black ass!' Booker T hears some voices outside and opens the window on his two-story Houston suburban home, looking down. Wolf:'Would you hurry up, I wanna get back to Dante's place.' The End sniffs at the air. The End:'You smell that?' Wolf:'What?' The End:'It smells like attempted sex.' Booker T yells at them. Booker T:'TELL ME YOU DIDN'T JUST SAY THAT!' Magnum Wolf strikes a match and throws it on the gas. Wolf:'Okay, we won't. You might wanna get out of the house, though.' The gasoline lights ablaze and quickly climbs up the walls, to the roof. Booker T goes back into the room. Booker T:'Charmice! The roof-- the roof! The roof is on fire!' Charmice:'Not that I haven't heard that before, but your timing and the appropriateness given our situation is very odd.' Booker T:'Our situation is gonna be extra tasty crispy if we don't get outta here!' Kane, his arms folded, squints his eyes at Booker T. Kane:'That never happened, did it?' Booker T's head goes into his chest. Booker T:'No, I just wanted more lines in the RP.' Kane:'Happy now?' Booker T:'Yeah. Let's go.' So Booker T and Kane leave the party, but there's still much more going on.
