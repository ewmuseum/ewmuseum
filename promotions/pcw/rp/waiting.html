Waiting....

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Just a minute ago. “I am so utterly, freaking BORED!!” The words hang in the stilted air. Spiderman dangles from the huge arched ceiling, listlessly overlooking the ornate and high tech Hall of Justice. Suddenly… the Hot Line rings!!! Spandex throughout the hall comes to life! Spidey unleashes a perfect shot of webbing and snags the phone before any other hero can answer. Spiderman: “Hello?! Erhem! I mean (deep and serious tone) Hall of Justice, Spiderman at your service!!” Tension filled moments! The other superheroes crane their necks, Reed Richards obviously craning his the most, to listen in! Spiderman: “Yes. Yes. Uh… no. (sigh) No, we are very pleased with our current long distance. No, YOU have a nice day.” With the kind of accuracy only possessed by the super human among us, he tosses the phone back onto it’s regular placement with a disinterested flip. The other heroes grumble and gradually file out. Spiderman: “This sucks on toast…” “Forget it. Come on down and play a game or two.” The human arachnid lends his mirrored eye shields down to where the Flash has a small card table set up. A couple of other heroes have gathered, twenties clutched in their mighty fists. Spiderman, intrigued, releases from his perch and gracefully somersaults to the ground, deftly landing on his feet next to the table. The Flash grins and starts flipping away at the cards. Flash: “Okay… find the lady! Find the lady!” Spiderman just sighs and rolls his eyes. He turns to the others, mostly sidekicks, entranced by the dancing of the cards in the Flash’s skilled hands. Spidey: “Three card Monte with the Flash? Why don’t you just take my wallet now?” Robin: (indignant) “Hey! I can follow him! And besides, it’s a one in three shot!” Flash: (smirking) “Sure it is.” Jaina: (of the Wonder Twins) “Yeah! Leave us alone, Spider-jerk! We are old enough to decide for ourselves!” Flash: “Of course you are! Saaaaay. Where is that brother of yours?” Jaina shrugs. Cut to a steamy bathroom, the red, knee-high boots of Wonder Woman alluringly raised out of the water, which has a strange purple-ish tint. Wonder Woman: (breathlessly) “Mmmmm! Form of water, you little cutie!” Back to the card table. Flash: “So, Spidey, you in?” Spidey: “No thanks. Times are tough with the photojournalism gig. I mean, how can I take pics of my battles when we haven’t gotten a decent job in quite sometime?” Flash: “I feel ya. Hey, why do you think I do this? So kids, what’ll it be?” Jaina: “The middle! Definitely the middle one!” Robin: “No way! It’s the right!” Lightning quick hand movements reveal… Flash: “Ooooh! Sorry! Maybe next time kids!” Whoosh! The table is packed and off in a rushing wind, a few scattered cards still fluttering to the ground. The kids hands that had just held twenties are now vacant. And suddenly… ACTION!! The Hot Line rings! The Hall is filled with superheroes and Iceman gets to the phone first. Iceman: “Hall of Justice!! Yes. No. No. Thanks, but we are totally satisfied with our current long distance.” Grumbles of dismay as the heroes again slump off to their corners of the Hall. Spiderman groans and slings webbing between two nearby pieces of equipment and fashions a quick hammock to toss himself into. There, he swings idly… Batman: “What you need is a vacation!” Lazily, Spiderman half-turns his head to the side to see Batman in his forest green cowl and cape, bucket hat fitting nicely over his vulcanized ear extensions. With a fishing pole by his side and fly hooks in the cap, the only thing missing is a string of fish. Spiderman: “Pfft! I can’t afford a vacation right now. Maybe Superman has the right idea…” Batman: (sour look on face) “Supes is a media whore. How many car dealership openings can you attend and still retain your dignity?” Spiderman: (half-grin) “I heard you’re just upset he took the Sav-Rite Supermarket opening on you…” Batman: (yelling) “They called ME, but he overheard it with his super-hearing and torpedoed me! Bastard! (calming down) Well, enough of that. I need to relax. Wanna come? Camping is free, you know, and I have an extra camping utility belt…” Spidey: “Naw. I’m a cosmopolitan. Wait, extra belt? Why don’t you get Robin to go?” Batman: (nervous) “Well, things are different since the restraining order…” Spidey: (holding up hands) “No more, I’ve heard enough. Have fun pitting yourself against the elements, or whatever.” Suddenly, the Hall of Justice springs into action! The Hot Line is ringing! Captain America snags the phone and raises it up. Capt. America: “Hall of Justice!! Yes! Yes! No. No. Sorry, but we are very satisfied with our long distance…” Groans. The captain hangs up angrily, shaking a clenched fist for all to see. Capt. America: “Things just aren’t the same with that Big Alan Steele back!!” Green Lantern: (sidling) “Well, there IS that offer from the UN to join and become a worldwide protector of the down-trodden…” With a furious swing, Captain America lays out Green Lantern with a powerful swing of his shield, sending GL sprawling across the tiled floor. Capt. America: (shrieking) “You fool! Al other countries besides America are EVIL!!” Green Lantern: (up and fuming) “You idiot! The cold war is over!! Russia is…” Capt. America: “Evil!” Green Lantern: (growling) “And the Asian markets are…” Capt. America: “EVIL!!” Green Lantern: “The French…” Capt. America: “EEEEEEEEVVVVVIIIIILLLLLLL!!!” The metallic form of Iron Man steps in between to break it up. Iron Man: “Listen to yourselves! Sure, things are different now that Big Al has come back, but we must not fight amongst ourselves! He, though not among our brood, is a protector of good and a scourge of evil everywhere! We must remember who our true enemies are, the nefarious villains in the Legion of Doom and not Big Alan Steele, and nor EACH OTHER, for that matter! For shame on you both!” Capt. America: “Sounds about right… for something a COMMIE would say!” Crack! The shield is thrust upside the head of Iron Man, who must take a step back from the blow. The heroes are growing in bloodlust as Iron Man points a fist at America. Iron Man: “Bring it on, you red, white and blue fairy!” The fist transforms into a chain gun before the assembled superheroes very eyes and begins firing round after round of lead towards Capt. America, who deflects the deadly hail of gunfire with that trusty shield of his. Chaos explodes as the bullets end up being deflected towards other heroes, who in turn start fighting back. The entire Hall becomes a massive violence orientated orgy of brightly colored spandex and poorly thought out attacks, explosions destroying expensive equipment through the building! RING! The fight rages on as the Hot Line phone rings. A slow green arm extends to pick it up, distanced from the fighting. Hulk: “HULK ANSWER! YOU SPEAK NOW!!” The Hulk clutches the phone to his ear for a moment… Hulk: “YES! HULK INTERESTED IN LONG DISTANCE OFFER!! HULK VERY INTERESTED!!”
