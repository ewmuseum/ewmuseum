VIVA CHEF!!!

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/24/02 The camera fades in… The camera fades in to a small bistro in the heart of the French wine country. The people sit and eat their high priced and not so nourishing food nervously. Back in the kitchen, where the French chefs work their magic each and every day, a group of servers is crowded around a corner, listening to the radio. “And now for the announcement of the first ten members of the new PCW! They huddle and listen carefully as the radio host gives some names. “Jon Kano!” The waiters grit their teeth with a crazed fury. Somewhere in the world a Panda bleeds from its limbs as if it suffered from Stigmata. Iblis laughs maniacally after hearing this wretched revelation. “The Chef!” Suddenly the hatred streaming through the bistro kitchen turns to a relaxed sigh, and then a heartfelt cheer. The Panda bleeds no more. Iblis laughs even more maniacally. The servers run out of the kitchen and shout into the air, “VIVA CHEF!” The patrons respond with the same chant. “VIVA CHEF!” “VIVA LA REVOLUCCION!” The French national anthem plays throughout the streets. In other parts of the world, Islamic clerics, upon hearing the news, preach to the people on the streets of a new prophet. This new prophet will be more powerful than Mohammed himself and will carry Allah’s word and grace with each action he completes throughout his heavenly journey. “SHALALALALALALALALALALALA!!!” The chanting grows louder and louder by the second. As the patrons and servers chant, and the clerics rile up supporters, the camera fades to black. The camera then fades into another image… We see the familiar picture of the Chef and Bertha lounging on their camouflage-colored sofa watching their Toshiba 27 inch Color Television. Bertha mindlessly flips through channel after channel, bypassing both the Trinity Broadcasting Network and Animal Planet for her favorite channel, you guessed it, the Food Network. Bertha: F*cking “East Meets West” with that f*ckingtastic chink mother f*cker is on! The Chef: They prefer to be called “Asian Americans” my gorging goddess. Bertha: F*ck that! I’ll f*cking call those c*cksucker what I f*cking want to, b*tch @$$ mother f*cker! The Chef rolls his eyes. The Chef: Well you might as well get in some TV time now. In just a few days I will resume my career in PCW, and once again we will travel across the globe, sautéing the @$$es of the many infidels we encounter. Bertha: No f*cking sh*t, Holmes… The Chef: And since I’m assuming the date on the Turmoil card page is wrong, my first match back will be against Big Alan Steele, the same piece of rancid pork that defeated me at Spring Fling! Bertha: And he f*cking Gorilla Tossed me, that f*cking @$$hole!!! The Chef: I know it won’t be easy my dear, but I’ve been closer to the PCW Heavyweight Title than I have been in months, and I will not waist my opportunity. I will not let Allah down this time! Bertha: You better not let that f*cking piece of sh*t down! The Chef: I will not, but he has no reason to smite me… I was the one who came out of Supremacy as the official “Soft Drink Spokesperson” for PCW. Since my glorious victory at the last pay per view, the sales for Perrier has risen almost 6.7%! Some people think it’s the gradual rise in temperatures across the Continental United States, but I have a feeling it’s because I’m involved with the product! Praise be to Allah for endorsement deals!!! Bertha: And what about that f*cking pansy @$$ Steele, f*cko? The Chef grabs an ice cold Perrier that was placed on a coaster on his coffee table. He unscrews the top and downs the refreshing liquid, simultaneously quenching his thirst and pleasing his complicated and demanding flavor palette.The Chef: AAHHHH, praise be to Allah for Perrier… Bertha looks at the Chef with an angry gaze, probably still awaiting his answer to her previous question. Bertha: Well f*ckface? The Chef: It is true that Steele pinned us 1….2….3, even in the eyes of Allah. Bertha: F*cking true… The Chef: But I will not blow my second chance with sautéing his @$$ in a skillet with olives, figs, feta cheese, peppers, and serving it between to nice warm and hearty slices of the freshest pita bread this side of the Aegean! Bertha: F*cking good, f*ck daddy! The Chef: Then I’ll take his precious PCW Heavyweight Title that he so recently won, hang it over my shoulder, and admire it’s golden splendor as you jump on him time after time with a few Bertha Big Splashes! Bertha: This sounds pretty f*cking interesting, motha f*cka! The Chef: It will be a heavenly day for us Islamic Militants my dear. I will call up all my terrorist buddies and tell them of the imminent good news! Bertha: How about f*cking Bin Laden? The Chef: I think I’ll avoid talking to him for the moment; I’ve been keeping a low profile ever since I stole his Discover card and you maimed his son Chester. Trust me though, a few of the clerics in high places around the world will get a heads up to celebrate my victory with a tremendous display of suicide bombings! Bertha: And how about f*cking chemical weapons? The Chef winks at Bertha. The Chef: Ok, but just because you’re just a cutie! Bertha giggles. Bertha: AWWW… He-f*cking-He-f*cking-He…you’re a f*cking lothario, f*cko… The Chef: The PCW will not regret their investment in me. I will prove to the world once again that my veteran knowledge, culinary skills, gastronomic weapons, pig-like clone, keen wit, and raw athletic will help me rule the PCW like the French Islamic Cleric I was born to be… Bertha: Go f*cko! Go f*cko! Go f*cko! The Chef sighs. The Chef: Even as rousing, as that chant is my sweet, this task is easier said than done. Bertha: Say it f*cking ain’t so! The Chef: Enough with the one-liners with cuss words attached my portly princess. My victory against Big Alan Steele calls for a serious game plan… The Chef furrows his brow and scratches his chin. Bertha: What’s f*cking up? The Chef: Silence! I’m pontificating on how I should annihilate Steele! Bertha voluntarily disregards the statement and rubs the Chef playfully on the arm. Bertha: OOOHHHH F*cko…I just had a f*cking bath three f*cking ho @$$ days ago… The Chef: Three days!?!?!?!?………………….Compared to the women on the streets of Khandahar, you’re a regular Sharon Stone!!! Bertha: Sharon f*cking Stone? The Chef: Yeah, I heard she likes to take lots of baths, something to do with slowing down the aging process… Bertha nods. The Chef: It was a compliment!!! Bertha’s confused look turns to a smile… Bertha: Hmmm, and I f*cking just ate a few mother f*cking White Life Savers… The Chef: You know my Artificial Aphrodite, if you bite a White Life Saver in the dark, it makes a spark… Bertha winks playfully at the Chef… Bertha: Well f*ckface…we are just going to have to f*cking TURN OUT the c*ck @$$ queer as f*ck lights, f*ck daddy! CLICK!!! We leave the Culinary Couple with a black screen, and their privacy…
