Date: Today :-p OOC: (I was going for something different here, so if some of it gets confusing, it will have an answer in time.) The sun shines honestly as it rises up from the horizon, the clouds shift quickly in the sky and a cool breeze blows. Obviously at early morning, El Diablo and Angel wait in a bus shelter. The Mexican Devil seems to be dressed well, in anticipation for hot weather, which is quite odd considering the dark clouds in the sky and the cool breeze blowing. It is quite apparent that a storm is on its way. No cars or vehicles of any kind pass the road that they are waiting on, only the cold breeze pass them by, not a soul is in sight. '5:30am' Diablo says shaking his head. 'It was your idea to come out here, this early,' states Angel while fiddling around with her long black hair. Angel is dressed quite normal, well normal for her standards anyway. She has on a long black jacket that goes down to her knees, tight leather pants and high leather boots, as well as a revealing crop top underneath the long jacket, but for now she is wrapped up trying to keep warm. What she is wearing is quite a contrast from what her husband is wearing; who just has on a light, black t-shirt and black denim shorts. El Diablo is obviously pre-occupied with Red October to think about anything else, meanwhile Angel puts on her make-up which she couldn't do earlier due to how rushed she was out of the hotel. It is common knowledge to the wrestling world that El Diablo and Snow have been at war the past month or so. So why wouldn't that be on El Diablo's mind? The effects of their war will no doubt come to a conclusion at Red October when the winner advances to the main event. And what is on the line but the most coveted title in wrestling, the PCW World Heavyweight Championship. Just thinking of winning that title can make most people salivate with the desire and yearning for it, and Diablo is no exception. Since before he even entered PCW that title was on his mind, and now for the first time it can become reality. He is nervous and also confident about gaining success, but what he doesn't know are the perils that come with the title- everyone in the federation gunning for you, the amount of publicity events and of course the fear of being a failure as champion, as so many others have been before. The anticipation must be killing El Diablo, the wanting of going to war with Snow and the wanting of PCW's most prized possession. The war with Snow has certainly been brutal, and a new scarier and edgier side of El Diablo has been shown with that electric chair moment. Does he actually want to torture Snow like that? Is El Diablo that sadistic? Who will win at Red October? Those questions will be answered at Red October, there is no doubt on that, but there is one doubt lingering, and that is on whom will be PCW Champion come the end of Red October. The screeching noise of a bus coming to a halt ends El Diablo's path of thought, and makes him leap to his feet right next to Angel, who is already standing. '5:55am' Diablo states the time once more. Angel looks at him now, with her make up on, which makes her look even more beautiful than before. 'But it doesn't leave until 6:00am El,' she says softly. Diablo just nods his head. 'That's ok, we've waited twenty-five, what are five minutes more?' he replies calmly.
