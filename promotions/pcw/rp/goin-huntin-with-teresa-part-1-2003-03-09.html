Goin' Huntin' With Teresa Part 1

9 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-9-03 RP 2 Turmoil 11th and #1 MM Mama Tomas is on a one way trip back to Tennessee. The poor old-fashioned insane old woman finally had enough of Teresa and Scott, that she decided it was best she left. Either it was that Scott is a little younger than Teresa is or the fact that he wasn’t the good ole boy type her baby girl was used to dating. For some reason or another, Mama Tomas did not like him. As always, she disregarded the fact that Teresa is a grown woman now and whom she wishes her see is her business and hers alone. At any rate, the woman is gone and our somewhat of a couple is celebrating. We begin this little story in the driveway of Scott’s New Orleans home. He comes walking out the front door with an armload of different things, dressed in brown hiking boots, camouflage pants, a black tee shirt, and a camo shirt over that. His shoulder length hair is pulled back in a loose ponytail. He grunts and hefts up the stuff as not to drop it, and then walks towards Teresa's truck, and waits near the open tailgate. He really does not want to go on this trip but Teresa insists they go. “ Why are you making me go on this stupid hunting trip?” Teresa is sitting on her orange Honda Rancher four wheeler. There is a metal ramp leaning against the tailgate of the pick up. She turns the key and drives the ATV over the ramps and into the bed of the truck. Teresa then manages to take the different things, a cooler full of cold drinks and food to snack on, a tree stand and a couple of folding chairs, and place them beside the four wheeler inside the bed. After that is finished, she climbs off the ATV and jumps off the tailgate. She answers Scott as she folds the metal ramp and slides it on the other side of the four wheeler. “We can use the break. With my crazy insane mother gone and the boys from that band out of the house, we need to get out too. Did you remember our orange vests?” While Teresa is placing two empty shotguns on the gun rack on the back window inside the cab, Scott climbs into the passenger side of the truck. Soon Teresa climbs into the driver seat and shuts the door. The interior is gray with a bench seat. The windows are tinted and a set of handcuff hangs from the rearview. There is plenty of room to move around inside the cab. In fact, it takes a long leg to reach the clutch pedal and the gearshift is a bit long. An eight ball is screwed on the end of the gearshift as well. Scott adjusts in his seat and watches the road through the windshield. “But why hunting Teresa? I’m not bothered by shooting a deer or whatever, but what is the fun in it. I’m just a city boy from Baton Rouge, not a redneck hillbilly from Tennessee.” Scott shoots Teresa a teasing grin. She just laughs a bit before shifting gears. Her right wrist is placed on the top of the steering wheel while her left hand is resting on her left leg. “If I didn’t like you so much, I’d stop this truck and kick your ass. It’s the thrill of the hunt. Don’t ask me to explain it. Hopefully you’ll understand before the day is over. Don’t worry, I’ll make a real man out of your city boy ass.” Teresa grins at her comeback and soon feels a strong hand on her upper arm and is pushed aside. The two laugh and exchange pushes and a couple punches until Teresa starts to swerve toward the other lane. An oncoming car lays on the horn as it speeds by. “I’ll tell you what Scott. Just cooperate with me today and after we get back and you still don’t like it, I’ll never make you go hunting with me again, ok?” Scott nods in agreement. He’s hopes no one sees him in all this camouflage and is even more glad that Teresa isn’t making him go to the city looking like he just came out of Hickville, USA. “Ok then, deal. I’ll tell you this though. If you get me stuck in some dumb tree doing nothing for six hours waiting on a dumb deer, I will kick your ass.” He looks at her with all seriousness letting her know he means every word. Well, naturally, he wouldn’t intentionally hurt her. She has taken a beating in the ring with the best of them; she could handle a good kick in the ass from him. Teresa merely grins in return saying nothing for a few minutes. The truck makes a turn onto the interstate. Teresa would like to take Scott to a place in Tennessee, but it’s too far to drive. Its called Calf Killer Hunting Club and the land is nearly 600 acres of pure nature, no electricity or even running water. Once must purchase a lease in order to enter the property. To Teresa, the place is one of the most beautiful places on earth. “Hey now, you don’t want to rough me up too much. I have a tag match with my new little spandex buddy and a title match with Walden again on March Madness Sunday.” Scott laughs as if she said something absolutely hilarious. He already knows about Rent A Hero’s attempts to make Teresa his super sidekick sweetheart. “You’re crazy if you decide to team up with that crazy Rent A Hero permanently. That guy is about crazier than that mother of yours.” Without warning, Teresa pulls the truck to the side of the road and stops abruptly causing the truck a fierce jerk. She turns to look at Scott giving him her own stern eye, this time showing him she’s not kidding. “Now listen here. Rent A Hero may have lost a few marbles, but the little feller can’t help it. He wouldn’t hurt an innocent soul and his heart is as big as Texas. If I decide to be his sidekick, so be it. Someone needs to look after the poor thing anyway. It’s not like I’m gonna jump in his suit or anything.” Scott finds himself staring at Teresa, not sure what to say. He reaches for her hand and speaks softly attempting not to ignite her terrible temper she is known for. “Hey, I’m sorry. I was trying to make conversation. Didn’t know you would be so touchy. Let’s just forget I said anything and get going alright?” Teresa sighs deeply. She doesn’t understand why she jumped his case over the whole matter. With a slight nod, she turns the key and moments later; they are back on the road heading somewhere in the middle of nowhere.
