Extreme Jailbait (Part One)

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-28-02 OOC NOTE: I apologize for my last RP. It blew. Due to class, this one's gonna be in pieces. Consider it one RP, or several, I couldn't care less. **Theme Music: 'Teenage Suicide' by Unwritten Law** ----------------------------------------Dear Jenna, Please forgive me for my last letter. It was short, and mildly retarded. I need to learn how to write better if anyone's going to take me seriously. ... Whoops. Well, kayfabe's overrated anyways. At any rate, I'm going to try and do something this weekend to take my mind off things like wrestling, LeBlanc, and my retard nephew. Love,Jason---------------------------------------- [Our scene opens up on the wonder that is dusk on a Saturday evening. Jason Core, the #1 contender to the PCW Extreme Title, and a man without very many friends, is doing sit-ups in his living room. Alone. And watching TV. Alone. Worse yet, it's crap. Elimi-Date. Somebody help this man. He's here instead of getting ready for March Madness, simply because he isn't too comfortable with the other guys backstage yet. But I digress. The door is about to knock.] KNOCK-KNOCK! [How unexpected. You'd think our writer could do better...oh well. Jason is brought out of his fury of health by a sudden rapping at his door. For those of you who aren't familiar, our protagonist lives in Harbid Terrace, which is a primarily 'college living' apartment complex directly off-campus of Florida State University. So when the two people at the door are typical-looking college students, no one is surprised. Least of all, Jason.] Jason: Can I help you with something? [The two young men at the door are relatively good-looking gentlemen, dressed in preppy-type clothing. As a matter of fact, anyone on FSU's campus could identify them for exactly what they were...] Mark: Hey, my name's Mark Cristo. I'm from FSU's chapter of Phi Kappa Psi. [Frat boys.] Joe: What's up, man...I'm Joe Nathan. [Joe extends his hand to shake Jason's, and Jason (albeit hesitantly) shakes their hands.] Jason: Phi Kappa Psi, huh? Come on in. [The younger men accept the invitation and step inside Jason's oh-so-luxurious apartment. They stand around kind of nervously, as Jason plops down on the couch.] Jason: Make yourselves at home, guys. [Both guys take seats, one on the couch, another on Jason's recliner. They trade nervous glances.] Mark: Uh, Jason...do you mind if I call you Jason? Jason: Nope. Mark: Well, uh, we know you're a Phi-Kap from up in Pennsylvania...and we're throwing our biggest party of the year this weekend... [Jason's eyes squint, and expression goes a little dark.] Jason: Ugh...look, I'm not gonna buy beer for you guys. [Mark and Joe trade glances again, this time, with smiles on their faces.] Joe: Well, that's not what we want. We can legally access alcohol, but thanks for asking. [Jason looks a little embarassed, but the boys kind of ignore him.] Mark: We're actually just wondering if you'd like to come to the party. [Jason's eyes narrow again, wondering what they want from him.] Joe: Perfectly harmless, y'know. We were just hoping that a celebrity like you... [Ah. Now J-Core understands.] Jason: ...will make more girls come to the party. [Mark and Joe nod.] Jason: Guys, why would girls come to a party for ME? [They look at each other warily.] Mark: Do you ever hang around on campus? Jason: Nope. [Both breathe a sigh of relief. Our scene cuts temporarily to a HUGE poster with J-Core's handsome profile on it, underneath advertising 'PKP's Fifth Annual Toga Function (featuring PCW Superstar Jason Core!)'] Joe: Oh, well...nevermind, then. [Jason, still wary...looks at both guys, who sit with the falsest angelic expressions in the history of falsities.] ::Dammit, Jase...you might as well go. You've gotta do SOMETHING to take your mind off your match for Madness. Alcohol might work.:: Jason: Why not? Could be fun. [Mark and Joe reach over and hi-five each other.] Mark: Thanks a lot, man. Joe: And if you can, act like you know us REALLY well. Like we're friends of yours. [Jason nods.] ::Big surprise there.:: Jason: No problem guys. [The boys make their way over to the door.] Joe: Be at the Phi-Kap house at 11. With toga. Jason: No problem. [The boys exit...and Mark sticks his head back in quickly.] Mark: Thanks man! Jason: No sweat. [The boys exit, and Jason can't help but kind of smile.] Voice of Joe: Dude! We are SO getting laid tonight!! [Jason laughs a little to himself, and then goes to his room to dig a toga out of his closet. Naturally, he's still got his from PSU...] ::Just like old times.:: [Fast forward a few hours, and it's 11:05, outside of the Phi Kappa Psi house on Pensacola. Fraternity boys in tunics and togas fraternize (hey, it IS what they do) with their fellow members, and drink. A few pledges keep making alcohol runs inside to keep their elder brothers happy. Mingled amidst the fraternity boys, is a relatively large ratio of the chosen target of the evening...women. Most of the ladies in attendance of the party are relatively good-looking sorority types wearing togas or the ever-popular 'togakini.' Togakini girls get the most attention, as always. A few ladies, and fewer guys, are toga-less, wearing dress clothes. J-Core watches all of this for a few brief moments from a distance, before approaching the front yard. But the prevailing thought on his mind is...] ::God, I hope Big Alan Steele doesn't sue me for gimmick infringement.:: [And with this internal battle cry, Jason approaches the party. Most of the partygoers just eye him, either apprehensively (the guys) or longingly (the girls). Jason feels uneasy, as he makes his way to the entrance to the house.] ::God, this is no place for an old guy for me. I'll get some alcohol in me, walk home, and get to St. Louis as soon as I can the next-:: [Jason's internal monologue is interrupted by a friendly tap on the shoulder.] Joe: Jason! [Jason turns to face Joe and Mark from earlier in the day, who are chatting up two lovely young women in togas. Jason smiles at the niceness of recognition. And the two guys beam as their 'prized guest' has made his appearance.] Mark: Girls, we'll be right back. [Mark and Joe approach Jason, and lead him back towards the front lawn.] Jason: What's going on? Joe: We've got to introduce you to the crowd. Here... [All three men stand up on a table out front, as Mark cups his hands around his mouth.] Mark: Attention, everybody! [The collected throng of people all turn their attention towards the three goofy guys standing on the table in their togas. Discretely, a togakini girl peeks up Jason's toga, in order to see what he's packing up there. To her dismay, he's wearing boxers.] Mark: Ahem...on behalf of Phi Kappa Psi, I'd like to introduce the guest of honor for our fifth annual Toga Function...PCW Superstar, Jason Core!! [There's a round of applause, some deep yells from the three or four wrestling fans amongst the crowd, and a more than a few affectionate catcalls from the ladies in residence.] Joe: Now, Jason has agreed to be the first participant in tonight's BACHELOR AUCTION! ::What!?:: [To Be Continued.](Since I can't edit posts anymore.)
