Why Fusion turned out the way it did!

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/8/02 (This is a mix of rp's, I don't like using rp's I use for a tourny, but this has meaning behind the whole Fusion segment.) Time: 22: 36Location: New York CityScene: Brooklyn Bridge “One man’s laugh is another’s cry” Tears shifting, Hearts lifting, Emotions rifting, but everyone thinks it’s a joke. Anger flowing, Sadness showing, Not even knowing why this all hurts me so. Hate starts to rise, Love starts to die, my eyes start to cry, but all I’m left with is this silly hope. Am I man, or am I other? Should I feel this way, because right here my friends is the real hardship I’ll have to live with everyday. Wind is flowing, I am hoping for my destination to be near. I drive my car quicker and quicker as I swoop right through the air. My heart is still not their with me, and that’s not even hard to see, it started a few days ago in a game, from that point on I’ve been put to shame. Portugal had the world all up on their shoulders; I was there-by-there side when their dreams started getting colder. It started with one, then to two, three wasn’t far behind, we tried to make a comeback, but our morale was already fried. When that last bell was slowly whistled my heart crumbled inside, that’s the time where I gathered my things and just wanted to hide. I left that scene and looked to a career that I still called my own, but then I finally realized I’m even more alone. A stupid bet set my future, and now I’m thought of as a loser. Sunshine is the name I get, all because of a bet I regret. I will not stand in cry, but I’ll admit I wished to die. What in life could be so degrading, what I used to have is suddenly fading. Then that night I watched my gold, a cruiserweight title I loved to hold. It was an hour later I was in the ring, my heart was pumping and starting to sing, a soft tune filled the room, but when my eyes were opened I was left in gloom. His name was Wippit Guud and he lied their lifeless, did my anger reach a point where life was no longer priceless. I almost felt like I want to repent, a message my heart never sent. Two days of thinking and listening to criticism, now I’m ready to gain my heroism. Driving quickly up and down. Beep Beep I hear in a faded cloud. I keep going I don’t care, what will they do when I finally get there. There was a place I didn’t know, but when I find it’ll show. It doesn’t have to be special or a place to wrestle, but just a place where I’m not hassled. So I keep driving not even thinking. Sirens blast and I just laugh. Is it the police, what do they want with me; if they call me sunshine I’ll rip out their knees. What am I saying, I could be in trouble. Who the hell cares? Says my side of pure evil. It scary to think I ever made that choice. I put anger before life, and forgot about joy. Couldn’t I go back and change it all, maybe I wouldn’t take such a fall. Can you hear me, I’m crying inside, as if in that moment I’d already died. The police couldn’t catch me, because if they did I’d be an enemy. At that moment I made a choice, I hit the nitrous on my Porsche. A supped up Porsche left all behind, and I found I place I didn’t want to find. A quick turn here and another there, I was suddenly in a hood that brought a scare. Next thing I know it got even worse as the car swerved in circles and left with no more. It’s over I thought now heaven will take me, but at that point I was still healthy. Thirty minutes later I ended up in a dark hall, I saw a man with a red eye who stood seven feet tall. He said “Enter my lair, and I’ll take you to heaven, I took a deep breath and counted to seven. The choice I made may have been my worst, because it was definitely not the first. He showed me a stash and assured me a good time; I’ve seen it before I’ve smoked that rope and line. I didn’t like it then, why would I like it now. Either way I wanted to touch the cloud, A stupid choice I must of made, because at Friday Fusion I began to deflate. Humility hurts so much, it’s like taking the one two punch, but here right now that was all gone, all I could hear was another beautiful song. Sniff Sniff Huff Huff, I know how to puff. Soccer players are good boys; I’m not playing with little toys. Everyone has that breaking point; I hit mine so I’m smoking a joint. Circles come, colors appear, and I haven’t felt like this in years. It’s good it’s hard, but let me get in my car, I’m off to the show and it’s oh so far. The night passed, the day went fast and one day later I actually crashed. I caught a bus and arrived at the stadium, I seemed all right fade umm…. Where am I? Who are you? What do I do? Sunshine who? I went out there and was a fool; don’t expect it against Chuck, because for our next I’ll rule. Don’t think I’m lying because I’m telling the truth, hey Mr. Loc, this all starts with you. Sleep…..Sleep…..Sleep…. Next day. Yesterday night it’s was al so unclear, I felt like my life had just lost a year. Today I’ve awoken and I’m left not knowing a thing, until the moment I watched yesterday’s film. I was so out of it, I couldn’t comprehend, and I said and did things I didn’t intend. Why did I do that? I began to think, suddenly my past knowledge started to shrink. Then out came I man named Loc, I’ve beat him twice before and dropped his stock. All the whining and complaining made me sick, if I could go back in time I’d make sure he didn’t pull the same trick. Once a man I though of respectively, strikes me down and spitted out words excessively. These are not the kinds of comments I take, If I had the chance I’d hang him by a stake. I want sweet revenge, oh so sweet revenge. The footage of me on the stretcher, made my face become reder and reder. Hate filled the air, oh filthy hate, so much anger it could fill a state. Watch out now I’m no longer sane, a stunt like that one adds to the pain. What was I thinking? Why did I do that? Didn’t I know it would turn out this way? What if? How come? Why not? Who did? Why ask questions with no answers, I’d rather ponder about exotic dancers. But I can’t think of that I’m stuck on this, Juan Borro is a strong man that doesn’t take any shit. So they call me Sunshine and laugh around, play with my feelings, and hit me while I’m down, well NAY I SAY, GO AWAY, DON’T EVER COME AGAIN ANOTHER DAY. Are you insane to challenge me, I’ll rip your eyes out and make you scream. So much hate, so many men to fight, who will be the one that makes this feeling all right. Oh poor Mr. Hawke I feel for you, that you have to feel such pain as I do. Loc you got away this time, but that’s because I lost my mind, I don’t blame my own self, I blame the devil down in hell. Why tempt us when were down and lonely, or do you just deal with myself solely? Marijuana you started this, ill release you with a long smooth piss. Now your gone and I won’t give in again, your power is stronger than that of men. All I wanted was to get away, but you didn’t even let me play. Do consequences come from everything, let me sing oh let me sing. You think I’m crazy, I bet you do, but Loc and Hawke, I’ll beat you two. As for now it’s all about me, for you two are no challenges I ever foresee. I’m the one with all the skill, I’m the one that’ll make you shrill, and I’m the one that will fight and kill? Who are you? You are nothing? Now I face an interruption..…interruption…. interruption. Mr. Borro are you all right? Call me Sunshine and I’ll cut off your eye. Excuse me Mr. Borro are you sure your fine? Yes, I’m sure I own a mind. I’m sorry if I offended you I had not intended. But you did, so leave me now for I have been offended. Before I leave you to be, there is one more thing I have to see? What is it that you must see, do I have to go off again and pee. No you don’t those tests are done, why do you act like your going to run? It’s not that I want to run, it’s just I don’t want to be here? Why is that this helpful place always seems so mere? But it isn’t, it almost scares me for what could have been. I’m afraid I don’t really understand, but I must now make my demand. So what is it that you want? I have to put you in rehab for a whole month. Rehab, are you kidding me I smoked it once. The old slogan says, “Once is enough”. The hell with this crap, do you have it in for or me? Not my choice, it was the doctor’s plee. Well I won’t do it I don’t need it, I’m perfectly fine, this life is mine. Let me be and I’ll make my own choices, I’m a big kid all right don’t give me these baby voices. I’m not trying to be mean, I’m telling the truth, if you don’t obey the order, then there is no RPG or PCW for you? That’s pure bull, and I’m going to prove it, rehab is for suckers that take all type of shit. We’ll you’ll have to put up with it if you want to wrestle, you can go now your free to settle. Thank God I hope I never see you again. My fans want my presence and my presence they shall get. So I’m off from here and I’m out of this place, still a bit weary like I’m floating in space. Loc doesn’t scare me! Trevor can’t compare to me! Drugs get in the way for me! And rehab isn’t for me! More answers given to blank questions. Tell me the questions, tell me please, but if you don’t I won’t be shaking by the knees. I’m ready, I’m fit, my mind is legit, from this point on it’s all about Juan. Scene Fades to Black.
