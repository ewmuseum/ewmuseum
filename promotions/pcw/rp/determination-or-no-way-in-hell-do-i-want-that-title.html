Determination (or, no way in hell do I want that title.)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered (The sun sets over the top of THE Multiple Man’s secret lair. Perched on the top of the refurbished water tower, Tmm sits. Raul, shivering in a thin coat, stands nearby.) Tmm: “This proves it. The belt has taken over his tweeny-weeie mind. It’s evil, I tell you, PURE evil.” (Raul grunts, plainly not interested in the least.) Tmm: “How else can he justify his idiotic statements?” (Tmm’s voice rises in a really bad Anton the Kid impersonation.) Tmm: “I’m gonna win! I’m gonna keep my belt!” (Hawwwwk! Spit!) Tmm: “As if. I see through its ploy. It wants to goad me into crushing Anton like the fly that he is, and then, when I’ve won the belt again… BAM! It sucks out my soul!” (Grunt. Raul is still not interested.) Tmm: “But I won’t fall for it! I’ll anti-train night and day, slacking like khakis at every turn! Five hours of television, every day! A dozen reps of donuts! Sleeping in!” (Tmm picks up a handful of last night’s snowfall and crushes it.) Tmm: “It WON’T get me! Though a more difficult than a sumo winning a limbo contest, I WILL lose to Anton! I WON’T win that belt! Or I’ll DIE trying!” (Gesture of defiance as the camera fades out.)
