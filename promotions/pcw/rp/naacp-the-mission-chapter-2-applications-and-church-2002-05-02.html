NAACP- The Mission [Chapter 2. Applications and Church.]

2 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/02/02 As Jordan and Mark’s feud intensifies, more and more things that were once unexpected, happen, and they are no longer considered unexpected. For instance, nobody knew that Jordan would go back to Mark Makonie’s sister. It’s obvious that he doesn’t love her, at all. In fact, many people do not see why she would be with him after all the things she’s been put through. What many people do not know about this feud is that in the NTICW, Jordan made a promo in which he admitted that he wasn’t going out with Jane Makonie because he liked her, apparently he just wanted some PUSSY!! And he wanted to mess with the mind of Mark Makonie. Makonie did make his own little sister pay for her actions though, Mark planted his own sister with a nasty looking reverse DDT in the middle of the ring at the Tuesday Mayhem before Rock On. It didn’t anger Jordan that much, but it did prove that Makonie was willing to sacrifice his own family for the sport of Professional Wrestling. This feud is hot; you best get ready for the violent culmination of the two-month feud. Now that Jordan is here, the honors continue. Jordan is featured in ‘The News’ feud of the week corner, along with his opponent for Jordan’s first PCW Pay Per View, Supremacy. This debut means a lot to Jordan, not only the opportunity to get his hands on his Arch-Nemesis, Mark Makonie, but the chance to become number one contender to the Rising Star Title, which is currently being held by a pretty new PCW wrestler, Kid Krazie. Chances are though, he might not even have the title before Mark Makonie or Jordan X Shabazz get a chance at that title, chances are, that Duzza, Jordan’s long time good friend, might be holding that championship by the time the title shot comes around. Though Jordan values his PCW spot, there’s something else that as of late, has given Jordan reason to be extremely excited when he wakes up early in the morning and gets ready for work… Jordan recently got the job as spokesperson, and head of marketing, for the NAACP. **************************************************************** The scene opens up in a dimly lit office. We hear the sounds of snoring and the sound of a television on way too loud showing the latest in professional sports. It’s a rather nice office with posh and plush amenities lining the room. The walls are covered with large posters showcasing great African American heroes, men such as Jonnie Cochran, Martin Luther King Jr, Malcolm X, Rodney King, those four black guys who attacked that white trucker, and even, perhaps the greatest of all black men, O.J Simpson. As the snoring gets louder, we see a completely drenched in sweat and paperwork, Jordan X Shabazz. His hair is nappy and un brushed. Jordan looks stressed, there are small bags forming under his eyes. He is well dressed, or at least was; his baby blue Armani suit is ruffled. He wears green dress shoes on his feet that are well polished. Jordan’s breathing pattern is rather weird now, he flew to Dallas immediately after Turmoil, where he was brutally beaten, and suffered cracked ribs, at the hands of The Ultimate Authority. KNOCK, KNOCK, KNOCK! Jordan hops to his feet and he turns on his laptop immediately, he turns off the television, and pretends to be as if he was working. “Come in!” Jordan screams to the person at the door. The door slowly opens as a short, beautiful blond lady walks into the door. She is dressed like a hoe… Erm. I meant she’s dressed provocatively. Her blond hair is tied in a neat bow, and she wears an extremely short skirt, and a smile. The top she is wearing barely fits her and shows off an abundance of cleavage. She smiles at Jordan and she places a sheet of paper on Jordan’s desk. She sits in one of the three brown leather chairs that adorn Jordan’s office. Jordan quickly scans the paper. Name: Nancy Martha Smith. Age: 23Experience: I once worked in an office as a secretary, and I enjoyed it, I was fired when they discovered that I had an affair with my boss. I’m willing to have an affair with any of my bosses if it gets me ahead. And so on and so forth. Jordan doesn’t bother to read about her background and her other adventures. He just smiles assuring her. “Okay, I forgot that secretary applications are being taken in today. I apologize for being disorganized,” Jordan says trying his best to sound important and busy at the same time. “I’ll make sure to call you when I make my decision. Are there any other applicants out there?” “Yes, quite a few,” she replied with her most seductive smile. Jordan nodded and smiled back. He couldn’t believe how white hoes were up on his nuts, so to speak. Jordan takes a quick look at her chest and forgets about all his worries. He is quickly brought back to reality when another knock on the door happens. RAPP! RAPP! RAPP! The door opens as a cute looking red headed girl, who is dressed professionally. She is wearing a brown colored business suit, and she wears a smile. Jordan looks down at some papers on his desk as she sits beside Nancy, the blond girl. The red head places a sheet of paper on the desk, and it is obviously her resume. Jordan doesn’t even bother to look at it. He smiles at the redhead. “And what is your name?” “I’m Nicole Jennings, and I want to become your secretary.” “Move over bitch,” says the blond ditz beside Nicole and in front of Jordan. Nicole looks snidely back at Nancy and she smiles, one of those ‘Holier Than Thou’ smiles. She doesn’t speak. Jordan runs his hands through his nappy hair and he tries to force a smile out. “Is that all that came for the job?” Jordan asks the girl with an incredulous look on his face. He obviously couldn’t believe that a single black female didn’t want to work for the NAACP. ‘Something’s wrong with the world today.’ Jordan muttered to himself. “Yes, there’s one more guy. I’m not sure if he’s…” There was an sudden knocking on the door again as a short nerdy looking white man enters the office. Jordan sighs loudly, obviously showing his disappointment. The white man appears to be very smart, his hair is neatly groomed, and he wears some horn-rimmed glasses on his face. In all honesty, he probably has the worst chance at becoming Jordan’s secretary. First off, he’s a man, second off, he’s white, third off, and he’s nerdy… He smiles at Jordan and he places a thick stack of papers on Jordan’s desk. He looks up at Jordan as Jordan takes the papers. “What the hell is this?” Jordan asks the man with anger and unbelievable ness in his voice. “This is my resume, my credentials, all the stuff necessary for this job,” the nerdy looking man begins. “I am good at answering phones, I type 100 words a minute, and I don’t complain… Ever. I’m also willing to work for free…If that helps.” The man finishes. Jordan quickly skims the papers and realizes that this guy is the real deal. Jordan looks back up at him, and then he looks at the two women who are on the verge of snapping at each other. “Okay, I’ve got a compromise,” Jordan says while clearing off the papers on his desk. “First off, Nancy, you’re going to become my ‘personal assistant’ that means, whenever I need a back rub, or some head… Erm… some uh… Some coffee or something, you’ll come into the picture. Understand?” Nancy nodded solemnly as Jordan continued. “Nicole, you can answer phones and write notes, and book all my press conferences,” Jordan said to her as she nodded. “And you, my insecure, nerdy, white friend, you can be my lackey.” Jordan said with the cockiness in his voice. He smelled fear on the man known as Chester Charles, the nerdy looking man. “Lackey?” Chester Charles asks while taking a deep gulp, his Adam’s apple bobs up and down when he does. “Yes, for instance, right now I’m going to Reidsville, North Carolina, and you’re going to drive me there.” Jordan says, knowing that for some strange reason, Chester Charles put a lot of work into his resume, and he is willing to work for free, meaning that he must want the job bad enough to drive Jordan to a church in Reidsville. “And what are we to do?” Nancy asks Jordan. “You guys can go home, I’ll make sure to call you when I need you. I know where to send your paychecks.” The two girls stand up, and walk out of the office. Jordan tosses the keys to his Jeep and says, “It’s the black Ford Jeep, and it’s the only one of its kind. Get it revved up while I clean up around here.” Chester Charles, the highly intelligent, yet insecure lackey, catches the keys and he heads to the parking lot. We see Jordan grab his bible from his desk drawer, it’s says ‘Bible Of The Crunk’ on the front. ****************************************************The scene opens up in sunny Reidsville, North Carolina. It’s around 4:00 PM in the afternoon now. We see a slow moving jeep enter the parking lot of what appears to be a full church. Singing is heard from inside the church. Jordan gets out of his jeep, and he hollers at Chester, “STAY IN THE JEEP UNTIL I COME OUT!” Chester doesn’t say a word, he’s brought a book on depression with him that he will read in the car, and he will be busy. Jordan enters the church as the song and dance stops. There’s a few cameras in the church, just from local news stations trying to show the locals that there is a new religion, sure it isn’t the official PCW religion, but it’s a popular one in North Carolina nonetheless. With all eyes on Jordan, the pastor at the front screams. Rush: OH PRAISE RUSH MASON! Jordan X Shabazz in the hiz house. It’s truly getting Crunk up in this bitch! Jordan slowly walks over to his fellow Malice Crew member, and the two men share a handshake. The crowd erupts in cheers as Rush steps back up to microphone. Rush: Ladies and gentlemen, a true black activist, a man who is truly fighting for us, HE’S KEEPING WHITE PEOPLE OFF THE DANCE FLOORS, and he’s making sure that good people like OJ Simpson and Rodney King are protected. Ladies and gentlemen, our guest reader for today, my good friend, and protégé, Jordan X Shabazz! Jordan raises his hand in appreciation as the crowd continues to cheer the PCW wrestler. Jordan goes into his pocket and he pulls out the thick Bible of the Crunk. Jordan smiles as Rush takes a seat. The entire Congregation is seated and paying attention. Jordan X Shabazz: Okay ladies and gentlemen, I want to tell you a little story. Crowd: YES! Jordan X Shabazz: I see the cameras here, so I just wanted to mention a few words. If you all saw the PCW’s Tuesday Turmoil, I was brutally attacked in a vicious hate crime by a group of cowards. The crowd boos loudly. Some say silent prayers for Jordan X Shabazz who is legitimately hurt. Jordan X Shabazz: That’s right, a brutal hate crime that is up there with the accusation of O.J Simpson, and the beating of Rodney King. The crowd goes nuts. It’s almost riotous, the mere mention of two of the strongest black men in their minds. Two men that fought the system, and won. Fairly. Jordan X Shabazz: They assaulted me and my friend, who is a white man… The congregation begins to boo, some scream at Jordan asking why he is friends with a Caucasian man. Jordan X Shabazz: …. Don’t worry, he was married to a black woman, and he supports our cause. The people begin cheering as Jordan nodded politely and waited for them to cease the cheers. Jordan X Shabazz: But fret not my loyal minions, in the name of Rush Mason, I promise that this Friday I will get my retribution. I face a man known as Tim Walden, he’s in charge of the talent that comes in and out of the PCW, and quite frankly, he’s doing a horrible job… He’s incompetent, and needless to say, he’s white. The crowd sighs in sadness as yet another white man makes a fool of himself. Jordan X Shabazz: He’s one of those lazy ass white people, who fire hard working black people, and he lives off his past reputation as a lackey. Case in point, recently, hard worker, and great technical wrestler, Karl Leblanc, was fired by this man, Timothy Walden, for no apparent reason. There are numerous people who do less than Leblanc and they still keep their jobs, I can’t help but marvel at the racism that still exists in our America. Most of the Churchgoers, not knowing that Karl Leblanc was a stiff, cheer Jordan, thinking that he is fighting for some more black rights. Jordan X Shabazz: That’s right. And another thing, he is ENCOURAGING BLACK ON BLACK VIOLENCE! The Crunk Bible totting people begin to boo Walden, a man whom they do not know, but are growing to hate thanks to Jordan X Shabazz. Jordan shakes his head. Jordan X Shabazz: It’s damn true. The man who is still living off the fact he once teamed with former World Champion Boda… It’s sad, a sad, sad case ladies and gentlemen. Jordan feigns being emotionally distraught, the congregation thinks that he is truly saddened by the fact that Jordan thinks Tim Walden hasn’t done anything of importance since being in Wild Deuces. Or was it Deuces Wild? Jordan X Shabazz: All I ask of you great black fans, is not for violence against white people… Though I do encourage you all to attempt to hurt every white police officer you see… Do it for Rodney King, and O.J, and do it for me. It’d help our cause a lot of the Police Officers became even more afraid of black people then they are now. Jordan flashes his cheesy smile. Jordan X Shabazz: Now I’ll sit down with the congregation as Rush continues our sermon. The scene fades to black with Rush restarting the sermon he didn't finish...
