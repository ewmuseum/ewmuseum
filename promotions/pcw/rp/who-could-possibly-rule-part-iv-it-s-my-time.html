Who Could Possibly Rule? - Part IV - It's My Time.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January Song guide for this promo (listen while reading, if available): 'Points Of Authority' by Linkin Park. (This ends up becoming B.S.'s second PCW entrance/exit theme song!) BOBBY STORMBRINGER Who Can Possibly Rule? - Part IV - It's My Time 'To summarize: it is a well known fact that those people who must want to rule people are, ipso facto, those least suited to do it. To summarize the summary: anyone who is capable of getting themselves made President should on no account be allowed to do the job. To summarize the summary of the summary: people are a problem. And so this is the situation we find: a succession of (Galactic) Presidents who so much enjoy the fun and palaver of being in power that they very rarely notice that they're not. And somewhere in the shadows behind them-who? Who can possibly rule if no one who wants to do it can be allowed to?' -- Douglas Adams, The Restaurant At The End Of The Universe, (c)1980 Then.... STORMBRINGER: Damn! Four thirty! Listen, I'd love to have the 'Reunion of the Immortals' with you guys, but it'll have to wait. Nelson's gonna murderize me if I don't show up soon. CISCO: His office is down this hall to the left at the end. You can't miss it. THE END: What are you talking about? Tyler's office is no where near me right now.. CISCO: Nooo. Not you. Where the hallway ends, I meant. THE END: Ah, duh. Sorry about that, I'm feeling a little scattered today. CISCO: Are you sure taking that chip out didn't have.. other side effects? THE END: Look don't start in on me again, alright? CISCO: Heh, just askin'. B.S. chuckles and leaves Six Finger Cisco and The End to their own wacky devices as he makes his way from the 'intersection' down through the only untreaded hallway to this point. It would only take a minute or two to finally reach his destination. The promo briefly fades to black for the final act of this escapade.... And now.... --1-12-02, Last Saturday, 4:38 pm-- ....and the promo fades back in with B.S. standing in front of a door, with him looking at the name plate on it: Tyler Nelson. Primetime Championship Wrestling Chief Executive Officer, Commissioner, and President. STORMBRINGER: Pfft. Must be nice.. B.S. hesitates no longer and raps his knuckles against the door one, two, three times. After a second or two he cracks the door open and peeks inside. He sees an extremely narrow room with not much more than a nearly empty and very small desk with a cute, petite, brunette lady sitting behind it in her tight, brown mini-skirt and light beige blouse. Also there is an off-teal colored small couch that only has room for two, for the purpose of waiting. You'd need another one and to rip off the arms on each end. If your name was Two Ton that is. As the door opens she hangs up the white company telephone that she was speaking on. Her pleasant, inviting eyes say to B.S. that's it's alright to come all the way in the room that is just barely a step up from a broom closet. There is a large door behind the secretary, and to her left a few feet. B.S. fits himself inside the room and shuts the door behind him, keeping his hands behind him on the closed door for a minute. STORMBRINGER: Hi there. M' name's Robert Stormbringer, I was scheduled for an appointment..? The Showstopper's voice trails off as he looks around for a clock on the wall. He sees a round hands-styled one next to the top of what he presumes is Nelson's door, and it reads four thirty-nine. B.S. silently mouths 'Damn! Argh!' to himself and the hot young secretary doesn't notice. After conferring with some papers on her uncluttered desk, she waves him along towards the door. Tyler Nelson's door. SECRETARY: Alrighty, just head on in sir, he's been waiting for you. Oh boy. 'He's been waiting for you' wasn't the best of signs, as he's now.. the clock clicks to the top again.. he's now ten minutes late. Not a terribly wonderful first impression. STORMBRINGER: Thank you, miss. The secretary stares after certain parts of B.S. as he finally enters Tyler Nelson's office. We can't give you an accurate description of Tyler Nelson's office other than the fact it is decadently large, a polar opposite of the secretary's. We can't because B.S., quite frankly, couldn't care less. The Showstopper's attitude is to simply get the hell out of there as soon as possible before he snaps and goes off on the corrupt son of a bitch for what he's done, especially over the last couple of months. This is an amazing opportunity for Kid Wonder's number one fan to get some payback on Tyler Nelson's bloody, exposed skull, but it would obviously risk getting his contract signed and put him in dangerously scalding hot water while B.S. desires some smooth sailing in the beginning of his PCW career. Through all the knick knacks, and furniture, and paintings, and vases, he barely sees someone behind a desk and its leaning tower of papers. The man who blew the wrestling world away almost exactly one year ago, one Tyler Nelson. And vases.... B.S. takes special notice that on the only clean and neat corner of his rather wide and titanic desk, Tyler is adjusting an unexpectedly butt ugly vase. About two feet tall and a puke shade of green if B.S. ever saw one, the design was random and chaotic. It looks as if the artist or designer has just gone and went psycho about halfway through making it. NELSON: Ah, Stormbringer, I take it? Come right in, sit down. Tyler seems to be in a good mood about the vase and Bobby can't even come close to understanding why. The Showstopper does as he's told and sits in the sturdy, yet creaky chair on the other side of the desk from Nelson. B.S. is only slightly aggravated that he can't even see Nelson from this vantage point. STORMBRINGER: Er, yes, hello. what's the story with that... is it a vase? NELSON: Ha! A vase!? Yes, but this is no ordinary vase, young man. This is from the inner bowels of Chile. It is very expensive. A man of my distinction can accept nothing less. B.S. considers pointing out that Nelson is only a year older than him, but takes a pass on it. STORMBRINGER: ...right. Sorry I'm late, I ran into a few.. old 'friends' that held me up for a few minutes. NELSON: No problem. Luckily for you, I'm not all that busy today. Unlike Sabotage tomorrow, and on Shanghai'ed real soon. Stormbringer can't help but look around for TV cameras, because it feels like Nelson is 'cutting a promo' on him. NELSON: Alright, so lets get to this. Miss Reagan tells me that she's approved of all the details of this contract my secretary has delivered to me? STORMBRINGER: Yessir. She and I approved it over the phone. All it needs is my signature and yours. That's what she told me a week or so ago, anyways. NELSON: Hmm. Now you've had some pretty harsh words for me before... Uh-oh. On top of that worrying statement, B.S. realizes in a flash of insight that Tyler Nelson's two goons he likes to call 'bodyguards' were nowhere to be seen. Where they on assignment somewhere? Did Nelson consider B.S. to be no threat and felt that the bodyguards weren't really needed for this meeting?? Or were they just lurking in the shadows like Inphino Blitz somewhere, and he just hadn't spotted them yet!? NELSON: ...I don't think you'd ever be stupid enough to cross me, though. *yawn* If you do, then I'll just destroy you like I do everyone else, so it hardly matters either way, now does it? You were a good little worker in the PWA, and you didn't screw up your opportunity when we sent you... away, for lack of a better word. So I suppose you'd make a good hand in curtain jerker matches. As Nelson retrieves the contract from his desk drawer and signs it in triplicate, B.S. stifles letting out a sigh and somehow manages to keep himself composed. Only because he anticipated Nelson's crap and nonsense to spew from his hole like that. He hasn't nor will he ever find a way to hide his expressions, his poker face notoriously poor. Good thing these stacked sets of official-like papers are here... Suddenly, Bobby Stormbringer's PCW contract comes flying over the paper piles, accompanying a sinister chuckle from Nelson. B.S. gasps quietly in surprise as he catches the projectile, weighted by a ball point pen clasped to the edge of it. B.S. narrows his eyes at the show of utter disrespect and disregard for B.S.'s eight year career until now, and the fact that he's about to become a member of the PCW community. He messily signs all three copies of the contract. He rips off his copy like he is supposed to, folds it neatly and sticks it in the pocket of his trenchcoat, which he laid on his chair's arm when he sat down. Then, being the bigger man, he gets up and walks around the monstrous desk, shoving the two copies of the contract (one for Nelson, one for Reagan) into the grubby paws of Tyler Nelson. As Stormbringer makes his way back to his seat, he 'accidentally' elbows Tyler's new vase off the corner of the desk. NELSON: No! 'The Greediest Player In The Game' uses his cat-like reflexes. To almost catch the vase before it hits the ground. However, his fingertips slip off the edge of it. SMASH!!! B.S. jumps and turns, acting startled at the sound. Nelson just sits in his plush leather chair, looking befuddled, eyes wide in pure shock. NELSON: What.. what did you just do!? That cost over fifty grand! STORMBRINGER: Damn. Did I just do that? What a freggin' shame. No really. I mean it! B.S. leans forward and yells 'I mean it!' in Nelson's face towards the end of his insincere statement and it makes Tyler Nelson apopolectic in his fury. (Sp?) NELSON: URGH! OUT! GET OUT, GET OUT, GET OUT!! Now! B.S. can't help but snicker aloud about how upset Tyler is. He grabs his trench and rakes his fingers through his hair. STORMBRINGER: What's the deal, Ty? It's a cheap vase anyway. NELSON: Cheap!? You call that cheap? STORMBRINGER: Hmph. You act like you're the only person in this world who's ever had a lot of money. NELSON: Oh yeah? I heard about your father. 'Rob the Drunk'? Like father, like son they say. Stormbringer starts to charge Nelson but stops just short, nose to nose, fangs bared. Nelson just laughs in his face because he knew Stormbringer would and could do nothing. NELSON: Ha hah ha.. don't cross the boss, 'rising star'. Don't cross the boss. I make the matches, as you should know. You'll be facing Jack Valentine next Sunday, the twentieth, on Sabotage in your first match. The following Turmoil? The Rush. Then I got special plans for you on the Pay-Per-View. My Pay-Per-View. Shanghei'ed 2. Get your passport ready or get left behind. B.S., still nose to nose with Nelson, gives him a nasty smirk. STORMBRINGER: Good decision. Everyone wants a piece of The Showstopper. You just worry about your own back and your own ass. Guess that's what you're famous for, right? B.S. walks away calmly and makes his way to the door with his trench over his arm again. Nelson calls out to him: NELSON: I'll make you pay for that vase. I don't need money from the likes of you though, oh no. You'll lose that value out of your hide, and in the ring. Maybe sometimes out. Oh! And you'll need to find a new 'hero' soon after Kid Wonder gets destroyed by my hands! B.S. stops at the door and turns slowly to Nelson, looking at him over his shoulder. He pulls his copy of the new contract out from the trench and holds it up for Tyler to see. STORMBRINGER: My hero? The only person I mark out for in that ring is me! One year ago you went into China competing over the Rising Star title. I'll do the same, and I'll walk out with my name on all of their lips, just like you did. It's my time. Next time I'll see you it'll be in the ring. NELSON: Ha! How many people do you think have said those very words to me!? Who the hell are you, anyway? Whatever, just go be a good little jobber now. STORMBRINGER: I'm sorry. next time you don't see me will be in the ring. You'll regret the day you signed this. And baby... that's no b.s.!! B.S. opens the office door, and in a surprise to anyone, he closes it with a simple click and not with a slam. He looks down at the interested secretary of Tyler Nelson looking at him with intimidated eyes. B.S. leans over the secretary and whispers to her. STORMBRINGER: ...'Power is far in the distance. Corruption is close at hand.'... On that wise note, Bobby takes off his tie, takes off his suit jacket, and slips on his dark silver trenchcoat. STORMBRINGER: Ahhhh. Much better. B.S. carries the tie and suit jacket with him as he quietly slips out of the office and towards the seventh floor, just to find out who's going to speak to the seniors. For your information... it was Jack Valentine! Boy, Cisco got a raw deal, eh? ~END (No, not Skylar Green! Ohhh, never mind..) THIS HAS BEEN A B.S. PRODUCTION. (c) 2002. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED.
