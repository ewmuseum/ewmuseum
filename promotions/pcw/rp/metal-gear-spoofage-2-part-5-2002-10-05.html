Metal Gear Spoofage 2 (part 5)

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/5/02 'Now you die, white boy!' In a lonely chamber inside the massive off shore cleanup facility known as the Big Shell, Raiden sprints from left to right as a mad Asian cackles, firing a rail gun randomly in all directions. Fortune Cookie: I Fortune Cookie! I can't die cause stupid white boy guns suck. Raiden: Gruargh! With a grunt Raiden fires off several bullets from his pistol, but they all veer around Fortune Cookie. Fortune Cookie: Ha ha ha! You silly little cracker. I teach you big lesson! With that the man whips his gun up, taking aim at Jack. Raiden: NO! At the very last second Raiden chucks himself on the ground as a lightning bolt flies over his head, nuking the far wall. Fortune Cookie: Will you hold still!? Muh fucker! Fortune aims up, blasting several lights on the ceiling and causing them to fall. Raiden: Gah!!!! Diving out of the way, Raiden crashes in a heap just as the lights plummet onto his former position. Fortune Cookie: White boy can't even die right. This why you race suck. In desperation Jack picks up a few pieces of rubble and throws them at Cookie. Of course all this succeeds in doing is amusing him as they fly straight up out of view before getting anywhere near him. Fortune Cookie: This the best you do, throw trash. Eat hot death! Fortune aims his gun again, then pauses as a cardboard box with legs creeps out into his field of view. Raiden: Tum de dum, dum de dum. Fortune Cookie: (Shaking his head) You stupid white boy, even for a stupid white boy you stupid. Before Jack can take more than a couple more steps his box is blown straight off his body, leaving him crouching in plain view like an imbecile. Raiden: (Looking around) Nuts. Fortune Cookie: Dumbass! Now die! Raiden cowers in terror, his arms over his head....then the elevator behind Fortune Cookie beeps and lowers. Fortune Cookie: Huh? What bullshit this? The lift hits the ground, opens. Out steps Argy, wearing what appears to be a leather brasier. Argy: I'll take over from here, Queen. Fortune Cookie: Stop calling me Queen, fag. Argy: He couldn't kill you I see. Fortune Cookie: He like you, weirdo crossdressing white boy! Enough to make me sick. Argy: (Licking his lips) Then he's all mine. Raiden: (Gulps) Fortune Cookie: Wear protection. I out of here. Raiden: NO! Raiden suddenly springs up, firing his pistol as fast as he can. The bullets veer around Fortune Cookie....one of them whipping around and striking Argy in the stomach, the other hitting him in the forehead. Argy: That was cruel. With a dazed expression Argy slumps to the ground. Fortune Cookie kneels over him, shaking him. Fortune Cookie: Wake up! This no time for nap! Several more bullets whiz around the Asian, but he pays them no mind. Raiden carefully walks up, his gun still pointed at his head. Fortune Cookie: I thought you could kill me and get me out of stupid story already. But you suck too! As the terrorist shakes his head, Jack pumps several more bullets out. He mashes his trigger as fast as he can, till his gun audibly clicks.....then he throws the empty gun at Fortune. Unfortunately all this succeeds in doing is bouncing the weapon away from FC and thwacking it off his own face. Raiden: Owwwwwww! Tears running from his eyes, Raiden stumbles back onto the elevator and heads up. Meanwhile Fortune Cookie looks on....as Argy blinks. Argy: There's no need for sorrow, Queen. Fortune Cookie: Bitch! I thought you dead! Argy: (Smiling) I died once already....I can't die twice. Fortune Cookie: Crazy white boy! Crazy! Raiden takes the elevator back to the surface, reaching into his boot for a spare gun. He then steps out to find a bomb laying dead center in front of him. Raiden: Oh come on, this is the equivalent of Wile E. Coyote leaving a pile of birdseed for the Road Runner. Nevertheless the timer on the explosive continues to tick down....sighing, Raiden pulls out his voodoo doll, hunches over the bomb and pricks. Nothing happens. Raiden: Huh? Suddenly a whistling fills the air....Jack looks up just in time to see a massive anvil falling towards his head. Raiden: CRAP! Our hero leaps out of the way, just avoiding being squashed by the cartoon killing device. Voice: Darn it. Raiden looks about as a somewhat strained voice whines to him. Voice: So you're the one that the boss keeps talking about. Raiden: Show yourself! From out of nowhere seemingly skates a very, very fat man. The light reflects off his pasty white skin, he audibly farts as he grinds to a stop. Man: I am.... Raiden: I know, you're Bobby Stormbringer. Bobby scratches himself, belching. Bobby: Actually I'm Randy, his brother. Raiden: I don't care, I'm still calling you Bobby. Bobby opens his coat, pulling out a glass of diet Shasta which he sips. Raiden: .....bwahaha! Bobby: What, what's so funny? Raiden: You're drinking diet Shasta....that's such a pussy drink. Bobby: I'm not old enough to buy alcohol, damn you! Raiden: (Points and giggles) Bobby: No one understands my pain. Raiden: You're a giant loser who lived in his parent's basement till he was 32, stalking girls on the fricking internet and drinking.....ha....diet Shasta....ha ha..... Bobby: Leave me alone you bully! Stormbringer skates away, vanishing around the corner. Bobby: This is how it works. I plant a bomb, and you'll have to disable it. That is, if you want to stay in one piece. Raiden: You have way too much time on your hands, don't you. BS looks on....then hangs his head in shame. Bobby: Yes....yes I do. The fat fellow then whips around on his skates, laughing to himself. Bobby: Let's go! Raiden: Let's not and say we did. Bobby: Gruagh! The large yet somehow very pathetic man pulls out a bomb and slaps it to a nearby crate. He then speeds away as Raiden hustles up and produces his voodoo doll. Raiden: (Pricking) Diffuse! Smiling to himself at his victory, Raiden turns around then leaps out of his skin as he finds Bobby standing in front of him again. Bobby: ......wow. BS looks down at the skeleton of Raiden as it stumbles around ineffectually attempting to locate its skin. Raiden's Bones: Not funny. Bobby: Here, lemme help. The fat fellow grabs the pile of Raiden's discarded flesh and hands it to him. The skeleton begins to put it on, then stops and stares as Bobby. Raiden's Bones: Little privacy? Bobby: Oh, sorry. Stormbringer shields his eyes, turning his head. Raiden: Okay, all done. Bobby: Oh good. Now die! BS whips out an automatic pistol, firing blindly at Jack. The commando dives for cover, rolling around on the floor as Bobby does the Curly Shuffle merrily. Bobby: Woo woo woo! Raiden: Must be some way to stop him..... Raiden kneels behind a crate, holding his ear. Raiden: Colonel.... Leah: Jack, do.... Raiden: (Turning off his codec) Ugh! Bobby: What's wrong, little GREYHOUND not up to the task.....uh..... Abruptly BS stops in place, putting a fist on his chest. Raiden: (Hiding) What happened? Bobby: Just....something I ate..... Suddenly what sounds like an explosion erupts, but muffled. Stormbringer opens his mouth, belching a fireball. Raiden: ..... Bobby: ....what? It's not like I got really desperate for a snack and ate my own bomb or anything. Raiden: ..... Bobby: Have some pity.....being a fat bastard is an illness you know. Raiden: ...... Bobby: Oh my head. The large fellow wobbles, wobbles, then falls to the ground with a mighty thud. Raiden trots over to him, pointing his gun to his face. Bobby: (Holding up his hands) I have beautiful hands..... Raiden: Masturbating constantly will do that to you. Bobby: Perhaps. But aren't they lovely? Look.... Raiden: Ew. In disgust Raiden fires off a shot, piercing Stormbringer's paw and causing him to yelp in pain. Bobby: (Holding his hand) Why'd you have to do that? Meanie! Raiden: Are not. Bobby: Are too. Raiden: Are not. Bobby: Are too. Raiden: Are too. Bobby: Are not. Raiden: Ha! Bobby: Grr! The fat bastard attempts to weakly swipe at Raiden, but to no avail. Bobby: Oh well. The biggest bomb in this entire place is still active. Colonel: WHAT!? That wasn't in the script! Raiden: Colonel? Bobby: I'm dead, why would I care about the script any more. Colonel: Good point. But still. Bobby: Heh.....laugh and grow fat. Raiden: Your momma must have had her boobs filled with laughing gas when you were a baby from the looks of things. Bobby: (Staring) Shut up. A final cough, and Bobby Stormbringer slumps to the ground dead. Raiden looks all around frantically. Raiden: Where's the bomb? The soldier searches high and low, under crates and tarps. Nothing. He pulls Bobby to the side, looking underneath him....nothing. Sweat pours from his head as he sits, sucking his thumb. Raiden: Where's the boooooooomb...... To Be Continued
