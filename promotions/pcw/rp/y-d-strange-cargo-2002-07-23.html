Y.D: Strange Cargo

23 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd July 2002 After recent events, things seemed to have taken a turn for the worse as it became official that Charlie Loc opened up a Pandora’s box, managing to get himself exiled from his home country within 24hrs of his arrival!Both his problems seemed to have solved themselves in that night though, as his fight was won and his wrangle with the Vietnamese Police was quickly ended…..but the one thing that remained was how he was going to make it out of his next predicament!Given only 24Hrs to make it out of the walls of Saigon and a further 24Hrs to make it out of his home country of Vietnam, the Viper seemed to have fallen prey to a predator by being snapped up in its trap!Snakes are dangerous and slippery, the Viper is no exception!Is it possible for the Viper to wriggle out of this one? How can someone make it out of a country in 24Hrs without any money? And how much longer does his first night in Saigon want to last for?Well this time, time will really tell because if he hasn’t vanished within 24Hrs then he’s going to vanish! Something must happen soon! Time is indeed against him…… Young And Dangerous Pt V: Strange Cargo WHOOSH~! The chilly breeze that was wind began to pick itself up along with some particles of dust and other bits of garbage that lay dormant on the streets of Saigon. Not only did the wind provide itself as a transport, but it also began to sing a sad and eerie song through its howling. It was the early hours of the morning in the Vietnamese city of Saigon….4am to be precise! And although the night life was very well alive in the city, it would seem that time killed it! Because on the same street that was filled with life and people just hours before, there was nothing more than a few fliers being carted around by howling wind and a lone figure walking down the cracked pavement. The figure was dressed in baggy silver jeans, a white hat and a huge puffy black jacket which it’s hands and neck were buried in tightly so that it may avoid the freezing cold. As the figure began to walk down the pavement of the once alive street in Saigon he became vaguely familiar, by the way he dressed, the way he walked and the way that he kept looking behind him every two or three steps, watching his back for muggers. The wind continued to howl violently in the streets of Saigon as the figure didn’t seem to take much notice of it at all, perhaps he had his mind focused on something, focused so hard that he was oblivious to what was going on around him….. Whatever the case the familiar figure didn’t seem to notice the wind which was blowing violently around him, almost pushing him back….maybe he couldn’t feel it due to the fact he was wearing a jacket which looked big enough to fit four people? Or maybe he was just strong! BOOM~! Out of nowhere a loud banging sound rings throughout the street, sending echoes bouncing off of the now closed clubs and the howling wind sending shivers down the spine. The figure walking along glances back quickly peering through a tiny gap between the brim of his hat and his jacket he squints and manages to see a light pole which has been blown over due to the freak winds which are hailing the city. As the winds continue to blow and garbage continues to float around aimlessly the time begins to pass slowly as the figure begins to pick up its pace. It is as though the figure is being followed….or as though the figure is afraid of something! Small children are afraid of things such as monsters and the dark, why can’t older people be afraid of the same things? After all no one should judge a person on what they’re afraid of! Finally the figure makes his way towards the end of the street, veering sharply to the right he goes down a narrow alley and stops looking behind him and then squatting down and looking towards the end of the alley. Satisfied that no one’s watching and that the wind is being broken by the huge walls of two buildings he removes his neck from his jacket and hands from his pocket, revealing his identity to be none other than the banished, tired and bruised…Charlie Loc. Beneath his hat Charlie looks quiet tired and wide eyed for an Asian but due to recent events something in his mind is telling him to push on as he reaches into his pocket and looks down at his palm as he removes it showing a few notes and some coins in Vietnamese currency. Charlie shakes his head and places them back inside his pocket before pulling up the sleeve of his jacket and looking at the digital 'Sayco' wrist watch he’s wearing as the blue face lights up to show 4:25am as he pulls his sleeve back down and rugs himself up tight again before trudging through the filth to the end of the alley way. It’s almost as though his thoughts can be heard….either that or he talks to himself, or his face tells one hell of a story, because out of nowhere something saying 'Money' flashes around. Finally coming to the end of the alley way Charlie hovers in the entrance or exit whichever it be, depending on which way you look at it as he quickly takes a peak behind him to check if he’s being followed before he walks out into the open. He pale morning light slowly begins to flutter over the Horizon as the grayish light of early morning begins to arise with it. Once again this causes Charlie to glance down at his sleeve showing the time of 4:27am, only a few moments passed since he last checked. Whatever he’s doing or wherever he’s going he seems to be in a hurry….either that or the wind pushes him along as he walks because no matter what the case, each and every step he takes seem to get further and further apart and every slab of concrete he covers seems to whiz by faster and faster underneath his black and white FILA sneakers. Suddenly the wind begins to turn violent again, as dust is thrown into the air as Charlie looks around before quickly darting into a nearby doorway of a club on the street which is parallel to the one which he was just walking on before. Just as he makes it beneath the arch of the doorway a thick cloud of dust is blown down the street as he takes his neck out of his jacket and glances down at his wrist watch yet again. 'Hello' A gruff Asian voice is heard from behind him as Charlie Loc jumps a good 5ft into the air before spinning around to face a seemingly homeless man, sitting in the corner of the rather large doorway with a rug wrapped around him and a tin. Charlie slips back into a fighting stance as the wind continues to howl as the man looks up with his gray straggly hair and goatee as he picks up his tin and begins to shake it. 'Got Any Money For Me? Come on, I just need some money! I not want to hurt you!' The old Vietnamese man’s gruff voice can be heard as he is clearly homeless as Charlie leans back a little before a sinister grin crosses over his tired Asian face. Charlie continues to smile before reaching into his pocket and squatting down to the old mans size before he smiles and receives a smile in return as he removes his hand from his pocket revealing a fist…. CRACK~! With one swift jab his fist comes cracking into the face of the old man, sending blood splattering all over his blanket as he falls back unconscious as Charlie grabs the tin of money, smiles, spits on him and then runs off into the violent wind and down the street. If he’s not running with the wind, he’s running like it because Charlie Loc seemed to be running a million miles per hour down a deserted street carrying some poor old homeless man’s tin of change while trying to get to his destination. Making himself appear like a true psychotic individual Charlie stops around another corner somewhere a few streets down and catches his breath before emptying the contents of the tin into his palm and counting a few notes and a lot of coins. Charlie manages a weak smile and throws them into his pocket with the rest of his change as he looks down at his watch which now reads 4:45am as he shakes his head and begins to run back down the street turning left at the end. The coins jingle in his pocket and his feet pound the pavement as he runs and the wind howls behind him as he finally gets onto a dirt road, stopping for a split second to turn behind him. Behind him lies a city which he has just fled and although he is tired and out of breath, a tear forms in the corner of Charlie’s eye as he shakes his head, wipes a drop of sweat from his brow and begins to run down the dirt road to escape his birth place. A couple of minutes later Charlie runs around a big green looking hill and standing in front of him, hidden by the landscape before is a huge airport, with the runway lights lit and a bustling car park and terminal filled with bicycle taxis, horse carts and even cars. Finally a true smile crosses over Charlie’s face as he runs towards the light of the Airport Terminal as a planes engine can be heard roaring in the background as it takes off. As Charlie enters the Terminal he gets out of the weather and the doors slide open with a CLUNK~! As people begin to hustle and bustle about besides him. Charlie seems to be at home as he looks around at the screens in Vietnamese writing before finding one and then running to the corresponding register. He half jogs there and is looking extremely tired as he sprawls on the counter and a rather attractive young Asian woman working behind the desk with a headset on leans back on her chair and knocks her computer screen as Charlie takes off his baseball cap and lets his hair down. It mats to his face with sweat as the young Flight Assistant begins to talk to him. 'How may I help you at Air Vietnam?' Out of breath but still able to make a sentence Charlie manages to wheeze. 'Next Flight To….New York City' The young Assistant smiles and then begins to touch type on her computer as she runs her finger across the screen before finding it and looking up at him with a smile with gleaming white teeth. 'The next flight to New York City departs in 10minutes….it going to cost you, unless you pay in American Dollars….' The Assistant trails off. Charlie fumbles around in his pocket and then produces a small wad of notes and a lot of loose change as the woman counts it and then taps him on his shoulder pointing down towards it. 'You going to need more than this sir, a lot more!' The Assistant shakes her head and holds her hand out for some more money so that he may pay for his ticket. Charlie reaches into his pocket and fumbles around finding nothing, he tries to beg but only a few words come out of his mouth as he is still out breath. 'Please…..I do….Anything….Beautiful….How Much?' The woman stammers back and then pushes the money towards him before going on with her job typing on the computer as Charlie leaves it on the desk and looks up at which gate its boarding on. He finds it and then spots two people making their way towards it and placing their tickets in the little puncher as he makes a dash for it behind them. 'HEY~! WHAT YOU DOING?!' The attendant can be heard screaming as shrill whistle blasts follow as Charlie leaps down a flight of stairs and onto the runway beneath the giant 747 which is just starting up the engine. Charlie sees bags being loaded into the Cargo hold of the plane and scrambles over onto the conveyor belt and clutches onto it, keeping flat and low and preparing to be loaded on board with the rest of the luggage as he spots seven security officers looking for him. Just as the conveyor belt puts him into the darkness of the Cargo hold he looks down at his watch which is glowing in its blue fluorescent light in the darkness.,,,5:09am He just made it out of the city alive and in time, but the real test was going to be able to see if the Luggage Compartment he was stowing away in was air tight or not. As the plane slowly began to take off, the Cargo door closed and left Charlie in darkness surrounded by luggage as the plane slowly lifted off of the ground and into the early morning sky through the wind leaving the city of Saigon, Vietnam below. The plane slowly traveled farther and farther away as Charlie began to fall into a deep and well deserved sleep. This was nothing compared to what he had faced, it was now time to sleep it all off. As the plane traveled off into the morning sun it would seem that the Vietnamese Viper has finally proved what he’s made of….however the question still remained! How long are the nights in Saigon? Maybe it’d just get longer because by the time he reached New York City, it would be nightfall yet again! The Vietnamese Viper was coming home to New York City, the only question is what was he bringing with him and would his welcome be a warm one, all Charlie seemed to care about was sleep……..
