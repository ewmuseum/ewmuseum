The Life - 4b

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.24.02 Ah, Sunday - that dreamy day of the week, the one you hope for the most. This Sunday is different from any other Sunday going on in my life. Perhaps, its the biggest. For today, is the day of Alliances. I remember one month ago, at Red October and stepping into the ring, suckering Gaz Green into Spike Pilevdriver and giving Playaz Inc the right to call themselves Tag Team Champions by the way and I must say...it made good television. But then, didn't I figure it out towards the mind of Gaz and Nez that the two of them weren't going to make the impact he suggested to his counterparts? Bah, I guess it never amounted for anything, exhibition style match ups re hard to get paid for, and hardly worked. I deserve a vacation, or something to calm the ambition bug that happens to be buried under my skin. Sitting back in a car, chilling down with the bass on my stereo low and the weed moblie just chugging along for the ride - animosity towards people had died down a bit. But that didn't mean I had to survey things out. As an empty lump in my chest when you breath too hard, or get water drained down the wrong tube in your esophagus - this roster, that drink of Cristal` was just that to me. All of them had a philosphy for every single thing that came to their minds, and none of us happened to treat it with respect. These people had busted their assses night in and night out, almost killing themselvesjust for the thrill of a match. But nobody gives them the due respect they deserve. 'Nolite te bastardes carborundorum.' To me, that little saying didn't have much of an impact on my life - something I heard while travelling abroad during New Haven High School's annual field trip to some exotic local in the World. I heard that during my senior year - Paris, France where I saw an old man, down on his luck beating the sense out of a young prostitute at night. Did it matter, to me this girl was going to die whether or not somebody happened to stop this man from killing her. He kept raving in and out of plain French and common English. I didn't care, I sat there on the bench with Tracy gritting my teeth at the entire ordeal. 'Let the authorities deal with it,' I told her with a deep sense of calm in my voice. 'they'll stop it.' They didn't it. And she died, not my problem. So, after we got back from the little trip, I decided to just hang out with Malik (Bishop) and chill out at Club NY. No shootout took place there, but while I ended up getting into it with the bouncer who kept talking and yapping his gums in my face - he gave me the weapon. My weapon of choice. A Calico submachine gun, silver and heavy to hold on towards. I treated this thing as if it were the object of God speaking to me. I holstered it in my jeans, acting all bad ass with it like Clint Eastwood from those 'Dirty Harry' flicks. I never thought I'd have to use it once, just have it around in those, 'In Case Shit Happens' moments. So soon after, I end up getting a call from Reno, claiming to be a hip hop mogul who would put me through the business. Turns out, he was a mob boss, and a dangerous one at that. He had those shifty eyes that everyone happened to look out for, he was big in that Suge Knight kind of way. The type of way that had everyone silent soon as he entered the room and stuff like that. I met him about three weeks before I did my first killing, and the only one I'm ever going to do in my life. Somehow, he and Malik happened to be connected to a local pimp, by the name of Frenchy Davis who happened to owe Reno money. Issued We took my car, and drove out to the docks, on the Jersey shore and waited for Frenchy to show up. 'Bout an hour later, he does with the pink fedora hanging from his head and a jive looking suit. Bishop gets out of the car second, with myself hanging on the outside corner. Frenchy laughed, saying something like 'Ya'll boys gots the money?' Bishop barked at him, 'Money?! Fool, you owe us money!' He reached into that pink coat pocket on his jacket and pulled out his small water gun looking pistol. I hesitated, as the gun happened to be aimed at my head anyway. Pulling out the Calico, I punched the trigger with my index finger. I blasted him straight to hell and fear was no longer in my veins from that night. Bishop looked at me, before I dropped the gun on the ground, fragments of it slapping against puddles of water. Not for a second did I believe I'd be going to Kennedy for Murder 1 (Murder in the first degree) . Bishop told me to be cool, stay alert. That prick...I went to jail earlier last month and he comes back, tryna off me. He flips the script and points it all on me, when it wasn't me in the first place. He gave me the gun, meaning his fingerprints before mine. I went off that Saturday, zooming around New York - looking for him. Nothing came up, 'DAMN!' The Gym Sweat beaded off my face, rising into action as the punching bag swayed left and right. Its the Sabbath, another one of those relaxation days of the week, the proper introduction to my night. It minded me, that Andi and I happened to have a match against one another, and neither one of us were prepared for what I could do to him and vice versa. I've gotten under his skin so deep, its like needing a gun to get me out of his system. This Pay Per View, so many people are looking forward to it - on all fronts, from their personal vendettas to the claim and prize of who will get the victory and attain #1 contendership to not only the World title, but the Continental, Crusierweight, Tag Team, and Extreme titles as well. I got a phone call, admist my own rigorous training schedule and me becoming the athletic freak, thanks to high school bullies. 'Holla?' 'Yo, who dis' be?' the voice rang out like a solemn prayer or vigil to a dead and fallen hero. Only one person I knew had this voice - Bishop. The sneak who almost gave me life without parole, that nigga. I continued punching the bag, thinking of him and his way of doing things. 'So what chu' want? Last time I saw you, you wuz under Reno's arm, like his little bitch n' shit.' 'Oh, so a nigga don't get no love?' Bishop asked me, wanting sympathy. I wouldn't be giving him none, I'm not the punk that sells it to people. He wanted what Gaz Green happened to quesiton for, what the entire roster would want after tonight. 'Fuck love.' He'd push the wrong button when he gave me the gun, and now he was the victim. I was point, and nothing could let it ruin the moment. THWACK!THWACK!THWACK! That punching bag was getting a beating, and looking at my knuckles, I could only see crimson flow from the joints. It didn't matter to me, but Bishop kept calling, wanting me to accept him once again. 'You know, eva' since Reno went on his little 'trip' to ICU, I've been runnin' things. And you know what -' 'I don't want to know shit, you know me Malik. Once you give me things, I don't let go until I change my damn mind,' I told him. The grunts by hitting this two hundred and fifty pound bag just made me continue further and further until I'd collapse on the floor, asthmatic. The phone went dead, he hung up on me - his only non-suck up friend in the world and he shut me out. For some reason, I stopped right then and there, not tending to my wounds. How could I stick my neck out for respect? I'd earn it for the first part - and then take it by the throat. Everything happened to be costly to me, just about everything that is... I laid back, shallowly breathing and blinking up at the lithium filled ceiling. Finally, some rest and relaxation from the little thing in my head called a conscience. Feels good... POW!POW!SHATTER!THUD! Wha? POW!POW!SHATTER!THUD! Son of a... POW!POW!SHATTER!THUD! Revenge Someone wants me dead, and thanks to their idoicy, I live another day, but why? Can't think like that right now, all of the windows inside of this gym have been decimated - shot through with tiny bullet holes serving as reminders. I ducked over as the bullets started coming from the middle window, then coming back as if a car had circled around and fired more shots back behind the couch. I leaped over the couch, before my back smacked against the wall with an affirmative thud, as my spine compressed along it. The question wasn't who wanted me dead, it was who wanted to just feel my wrath so bad that they wanted to kill me? I could give less, but no that's not me - I have something to go for right now, and its not perfect for sure. Anybody can kill somebody, but they must have guts to do so. Like anybody could beat anyone in the ring. Like Gaz Green, like Xavier Kannon, like all the teams in Alliances but my philosophy about things only happens in the ring and that's where people learn either a mistake or an opening. But of course, I have a battle royal to win and it might be my less known victory, but I'm Dante Jackson. And I got revenge on the brain.
