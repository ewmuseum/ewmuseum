Evil Within

12 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-12-02 “I’m a monster…” Don’t get yourself down. The voice popped into my head so suddenly, I wasn’t sure it was there. But as I sat in silence, the voice came again. You have no reason to feel shame. You are the future, Trigger. Love your life. It was undeniable now. The voice was there. But who’s was this? Where did it come from? Could it be the Voice of Reason come back to answer my prayers? To give me redemption? Not quite. No. Even as I thought my thoughts, I knew it wasn’t true. I had destroyed my conscience. I would be soulless forever. So what was this mysterious voice that reassured me so? “Are you the devil?” I wouldn’t go as far to say that. After all, I am inside of you, and for me to be the devil, you would have to be life itself. “So you’re evil?” The purest. “Oh…” My heart sighed. This was not the kind of redemption I was hoping for. What? Nobody has only an angel on their shoulders. I have been here since the beginning. And now that you’re goodness is gone, I am the only one that remains. I am you, Trigger. You are me. It is set in stone. “I don’t want to be bad. I never did. All I wanted was…” I was stumped. What was it that I wanted? What had driven me over the edge, slowly floating towards insanity? All you wanted was a little glory. A little respect. A little pride. “I’m not greedy.” Oh, but you are. Isn’t everyone? For centuries humans have searched for the meaning of life, but what nobody seems to understand is that greed is the meaning of life! Utter contentment will always involve want. And want is just another expression of greed. This wasn’t working well. “I don’t like the way you speak. I hate it.” I’m all in your head, Trigger. To hate me is to hate yourself. Do you hate yourself? There was a long silence. “I think so.” Then what have you got to lose? You have already hit rock bottom. Things can only get better from here. Why not give me a shot? Let me guide you. Let me mold you. Let me use Trigger to his full potential. I remembered the last time a voice had told me to let it guide my life. It had been my conscience, the Voice of Reason. I had then destroyed it. “…What can you do for me?” Inside my head there was a deep, roaring laughter. It’s not what I can do for you… it’s what you can do for yourself. I will guide you. I will show you the correct path. But when the time comes, it will you who stands solely in the spotlight. It will be you who gets the glory. When this had begun, I was unsure. I didn’t want to trust anyone, let alone myself. But as time went on, I realized that this new voice in my head was right. Things could only get better from this point on… and so why not shoot for the stars? Why not accomplish my dreams? After all, evil is only a state of mind. Right?
