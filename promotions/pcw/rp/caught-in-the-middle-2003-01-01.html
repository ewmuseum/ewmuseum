Caught In The Middle

1 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 1st, 2003 Five years may not seem like a long time, but when it’s been that long since you’ve last celebrated a new year’s party with your closest friends from it seems like an eternity. That is why now that I was back home in Massachusetts I was going to take advantage of my time and use it to do what I hadn’t done in five years, spend a new years eve with a group of my closest friends. It had been what seemed like ages since I last saw my group of friends mainly because the small amount of spare time I had I often used to spend time with the children or go out on a date with Chris. I wondered what they would think of me now that I had won fame, respect, and even more money. Would I be the same old Tessa to them, or would I be a complete stranger. Not to mention now I had not one kid, but two kids. They may have not come from my womb, but I still considered them mine. I had bonded with them and I’d hate to lose them. I had cared so much that I had made sure to bring Christa along with me. I would send her up to the guest bedroom inside my friend’s house and put her to sleep while we stayed downstairs and partied till we could no longer stand on our own two feet. 6:55 Five minutes early. Usually I’m the one to be late, but I made sure to be on time for an occasion of this sort. Plus I had to get Christa to bed before everyone arrived and swallowed her alive with compliments and hugs. I walked up the wooden porch reaching out to the doorbell, but before I could reach it I found myself in the arms of my best friend Nicole. “Tessa how are you I haven’t seen you in so long!” She exclaimed, throwing her arms around me trapping me in a hug. I exchanged the greeting wrapping my arms around her back as well and then pulling them back towards my face, swiping a strand of light brown hair back behind my ear. “Hanging in there. I’ve missed you all so much!” I said smiling, feeling like a child once again as my blue eyes lit up with joy. “And this must be Christa.” Nicole said, smiling down at the little girl who looked to be a clone of me with her little blue eyes, tender pink lips, and light brown hair. Christa’s cheeks turned bright red, blushing as she nodded her head in a yes having not the slightest clue who this was complimenting her. “Christa this is my best friend Nicole.” I said as I pointed towards the average sized woman with jet black hair. Nicole smiled, waving to the child as she reached out to the door beside her. “Let’s head inside out of this cold.” She said as I reached out taking Christa by the hand leading her towards the door and out of the bitter cold. Christa simply looked down at the ground not being bothered to be the slightest bit excited. I could tell that deep inside her mind she missed Chris and she missed Alex and she just wanted everything to return to normal once again just like me. As I walked in through the doorway I noticed that everything was just the way I remembered it. It was like I was walking back into the past. Christa took off her jacket, tossing it over the railing. Following her actions I did the same and then led her by the hand up the stairs to the guest bedroom where she would be sleeping. “Tessa did we come all the way back home for this?” She asked innocently, trying to find a logical explanation as to why Chris and I were apart. She knew we hated to be apart so surely she must have known that there was something wrong, but I couldn’t build up the heart to break her precious spirit with news of that sort. “Indeed it is.” I lied through my teeth with a slight smile across my pink lips as I pulled the blankets over her , covering her up as she laid her head on the two pillows at the head of the bed. “But all I’m doing is lying in a bed. Couldn’t I have done that at Chris’ apartment?” She asked, obviously not liking having to be left out and I couldn’t blame her. I would hate to be stuck in a room in a house I had never been in before alone with no one to talk to. I felt guilty lying to her, but it had to be done to keep her heart from shattering into smaller pieces. “Just go to sleep and I promise you that in the morning I will have a special surprise in the morning.” I said, turning off the lights and then walking to the door pulling it shut, leaving her alone in the dark room feeling guilt overcome me. Once again I had messed up, this time dragging the children into a disagreement between Chris and me and then abandoning both of them. Leaving Alex with Chris and Christa in a room to sleep in attempt to erase the horrible image that was surely pasted in her head of the impact the chair had made against my skull. Almost positive that Christa was emotionally damaged from what she saw on Tuesday I couldn’t help but to wonder what Alex was thinking the moment he saw Chris wind up and swing, missing his intended target and nailing me. It must have torn him in two and left him with no choice to side with Chris, perhaps fearing for his safety or just out of angered of being abandoned once again. And although I knew Chris loved me and the children to death and I knew it, I couldn’t help but to wonder was it really a mistake or did that chair have my name written on it all along?
