That's Amore

9 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/9/02 When the moon hits your eye like a big piece of pie that's amore When the world seems to shine like you've had too much wine that's amore The world spins and blurs, rotating around and around again. The clouds move overhead, fading to the night then arising like a phoenix from the ashes. Time flies. Music drifts. Out of nowhere, out of everywhere all at once......Dean Martin. When you dance down the street with a cloud at your feet that's amore Rain falls and splatters on a man's forehead, mercifully cool. The pain wracks him......he remembers. The memories drift like an incoming tide, polluted and green....horrible memories, but beautiful all the same. Haha, you suck! At a bar somewhere in the middle of nowhere, a burned man rocks back and forth on his stool. A glass of pretty poison in his hand, he croons. Clyde: Wheeeen......da........moon hits yer eyeball and I rip it out and......uh........ Clyde turns to his side, where a little girl sits shaking her head. Clyde: Whaat rhymes with eyeball? Bethany: You suck. Clyde shrugs, raises his glass again. Clyde: Whhhhhhhen da.......moon hits yer eyeball an I rip it out and you suck.......that's amore...... The drunken patrons at the bar cheer, clapping drunkenly. Bethany snaps her head around in fury. Bethany: Don't encourage him! drink Clyde hops up onto the stage, commandeering the microphone from a rather shocked female vocalist. Clyde: This is mah party, aahl sing eff I want to.......eff you weel. Bethany: (Slaps her forehead) Clyde: Ahem.....uh......what I sing? A voice in the crowd booms out, echoing through the void. 'STAIRWAY TO HEAVEN!!!!' Clyde: (Staring) I ain't doing no Zepplin till I get another drink. From out of the audience bounds a hula girl, her grass skirt blowing in the air condition breeze. She holds up a fruity concoction for the inebriated burned man.....he takes it, sniffs it. Clyde: This ain't a deenk! Geet me rat poison! Hula Girl: (Giggling) What's a dink? Clyde peers closely at the young lady, squinting through his drunken eyes. Clyde: Hula Boy? Didn't you geet keeled a year ago? The girl looks at the demon wide eyed, then scrambles off the stage. Clyde: Anyway......RAT POISON!!!!! Bethany: (Growling) Be glad to. Up onto the stage hops Bethany, carrying a large bottle clearly marked with a skull and crossbones. Crowd: Take it off! Take it off! Take it off! Take it off! Clyde: Don't meend if ah do! The burned man dances, wriggling out of his overcoat slowly......his evil girlfriend though stops him with a kick to the shin. Bethany: Goddammit drink your poison already! I wanna go home! Halting in mid-dance, Clyde slowly looks to Bethany.....takes the bottle, opens it with a little effort. Clyde: Stooopid childproof poison lids. Finally the demon pours the contents into his margarita glass.....raises both it and the bottle upon high, bellowing. Clyde: I AM THE VIOLATOR!!!!!! Bethany: (Sighs) Clyde raises the glass to his lips......then lowers it and smirks as the drunken crowd chants. Crowd: Chug! Chug! Chug! Chug! Chug! Chug! Chug! Chug! Chug! Clyde: Okie!!!! At that the burned man throws the glass over his head, rears back and drinks directly from the bottle of rat poison. The patrons of the bar gasp as he chugs......chugs.....then finally lowers the empty bottle, opens his mouth and belches mightily. Clyde: BRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRAAAAAAAAAAAAGH! A huge cloud of flame erupts from Clyde's mouth, causing the audience to scatter. The killer looks on......then slowly they begin to clap. Clyde: (Bowing drunkenly) Thaank ye! Taaaank ye! Inside the bar a song starts up.....it echoes through it, piercing the night and all that inhabit it. Crowd: Happy birthday to you......happy birthday to you.......happy birthday to Clyde.......happy birthday to you! Hula Girl: How old are you!? Clyde: ........I don't know. Bethany: Goddammit, this is the last time I ever throw you a birthday party Clyde. Clyde: Fiddle de feckin diddle. Avast ye matey. ARRRRRRRRRRRGH! The demonic serial killer sways back and forth, as the drunken bar members swarm the stage. He dances and grins in spite of himself, the alcohol and the poison working its magic........ When the moon hits your eye..... pie amore moon Rain splatters on a man's forehead. Mercifully cool......Clyde opens his eyes to find himself staring at the night sky. A bass drum of agony blares through his skull.....he groans. How many days had passed? The burned man slowly pulls himself to a sitting position.....retches and vomits. Looking blankly at the mess on his shoes, he shakes his head. Clyde: Birthdays suck. Aggggh...... Clutching his aching skull, the demon crawls......shakes his fist in spite of the pain. Clyde: I'll get you, Dean Martin.....and your little dog too. And as the music drifts and a quite insane and hung over killer crawls into the black of the night, we mercifully fade to black.......
