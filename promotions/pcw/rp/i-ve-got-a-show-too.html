I'VE GOT A SHOW TOO!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1.7.02 (#1 FF) “This is the best idea since the robot idea I had….well, without the whole ending up in total chaos and carnage thing.” Rush Mason’s excited face is the first sight we see as the promo opens. He’s sitting down at a desk, a pencil behind his ear, looking down at the desk in front of him, as he exclaims his ideas out loud. “Hmm…we need a name for it.” Tapping the pencil on his stubble-filled chin, Rush begins to think of a name for whatever zany idea of his this is. However, he’s suddenly interrupted by the door opening to his large apartment. He glances over, then looks back down at his paper, trying to continue thinking hard. Rush: Hmm…I need something catchy, yet not appealing to anyone who I wouldn’t want watching the show. Hmm…exclusive, yet trendy… Sounding somewhat like a fashion designer, Rush continues to think hard, or so it would seem from the bite marks on the pencil that has made its way from perched atop his ear down into his teeth. Barging into the room, none other than Rush’s large manager yells, announcing his arrival with quite a spectacle. “Dawg…a KFC truck spilled on the highway! I was all on that bitch! I got a few 50-piece buckets with some popcorn chicken afore the rest of the crowd got there. You want some?!” Ray Ray seems very pleased with this finding, but Rush barely acknowledges the 300+ plus pounder, just throwing a hand back and giving a very neutral call back towards him. Rush: Sure….sounds great, Ray Ray. Isn’t there some episode of Fresh Prince or a Wesley Snipes movie on TV you could be watching? Surprised by the sight of Rush actually working and thinking on something, Ray Ray steps further into the room, his curiousity piqued. He looks from above Rush’s shoulder, blocking the light source. Ray Ray: Yo….whatcha workin’ on? Rush tries to work through the fact that he has very little light, to ignore his large manager, but it proves impossible. He turns around, bringing his eyes up to Ray Ray, who just continues to look curiously down at the project. Clearing his throat, the man steps out of Rush’s light but doesn’t leave. Ray Ray: Well…? Rush, now working fervently while he scratches away at the paper, planning his project, while vaguely explaining it to his employee. Rush: I just had the best idea in the history of the human race, what else is new? Continuing to pry and not picking up the numerous hints that Rush wishes to be left alone, Ray Ray sits on the other side of the room, being patient for about three seconds before beginning another tirade of questions. Ray Ray: Explain it, then. Realizing that there’s no more work that’s going to get done with Ray Ray in the vicinity, Rush stands up and walks to the middle of the room, pacing a bit as he mouths over his short preliminary planning for whatever project this is. Rush: Now, prepare to be dazzled… Apparently preparing to be dazzled, Ray Ray watches on with interest, hoping that this new project has something to do with food, sleeping, or at best a combination of the two. Rush: My idea….a…TALK SHOW! Ray Ray’s face is blank, continuing to listen, hoping to hear more details, hopefully that it’s a talk show with food, or sleep, or a talk show that provides food so that the guests can then sleep on air. He waits for details, but is unfortunately disappointed. Ray Ray: And? The Voodoo Villain looks shocked at Ray Ray’s blasphemy against such a great plan. He scolds his manager, wagging his finger towards the sitting man. Rush: Whoa, whoa, whoa. I’ve only just begun to think it out….I’ve only had four or so hours to work on it! Even Ray Ray is surprised that four hours of work has yielded one idea, one which will undoubtedly fail anyway. He tries to get Rush to reveal something about this “great plan” which is seemingly less great by the minute. Ray Ray: Great, yo. What’s it gonna be about? Once again, Rush acts as if Ray Ray has announced he’s becoming a Catholic priest. He shakes his head, muttering under his breath about Ray Ray’s lack of intelligence and then responds. Rush: I’m not a damn speed demon! I’ll have to figure something out, but right now, I need to reward myself with a nice sandwich.Very much confused by the conversation that has just gone on, Ray Ray just sits on the stool he has occupied this whole time, his brow furrowed, as Rush leaves the room and the scene begins to fade out. “Uhh…aren’t I s’posed to be the one who’s dumb?”
