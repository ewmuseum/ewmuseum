The Empire Saga Part One: The Universal Understanding of Evil…A new saga begins….

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/7/02 The Empire Saga Part One: The Universal Understanding of Evil…A new saga begins…. And so it began…the new era…the new era of John Crichton’s existence…some would deem this existence as ‘evil’…but what would they know. Prior to contrary beliefs the term known as ‘evil’ wasn’t a minority in this world, it was a part of our being, a part of our conscience…bottom line was evil was a part of every human on the planet…it just isn’t able to get out, to take control. Some are born with this ‘evil’ already in control of their conscience…the serial killers, the crazed lunatics and all kinds of murderers…but the other people…the ‘normal’ people have the ‘evil’ inside them also…but it is bottled up safely deep inside their conscience…they can access or ‘un-bottle’ this ‘evil’ on certain ocassions…when something deeply traumatic has changed their lives…the bottle opens and the ‘evil’ takes control. At least this was Crichton’s ‘new’ opinion on the matter…his theory on how and why he was created within the husk of John Crichton. The ‘new’ Crichton decided that if he was ever going to be free from all of the people who were constantly hunting him down, trying to kill him, or just generally making his life a living hell, he needed power…and lots of it. He decided that he wasn’t just going to bend over and take all the punishment the world had deemed rightfully fit to place upon him…not anymore. If he had power, he could change all of that…he could stop all of the punishment life had thrown at him…essentially he would be taking the easy way out…but his conscience granted him certain leeway nowadays. This would be essential to the process…because gaining the power he needed wouldn’t be easy…a man with any kind of conscience would not be able to do what had to be done…he didn’t have to worry about that. Now, he wasn’t trying to be any kind of stereotypical bond villain…setting out to take over the world…no, he wanted enough power to keep his ass safe from his enemies…he was the new foreman of the body and wanted to have as much fun as possible…live the longest life imaginable…he had to risk everything to BE somebody…he was going to seize power within the criminal underworld. With this power he could BE anybody, he could KILL anyone that tried to make his life more difficult than it had to be…on some levels the ‘old’ Crichton wanted the same things…wanted all of the people off his back…he just didn’t know how to do it…he wouldn’t have the balls to go through with it either…but this wasn’t the old the Crichton any longer…he was the ‘new and improved’ John Crichton….the John Crichton that could get things done…the one who WOULD dominate the criminal underworld…all he had to done was bide his time… ************ John Crichton stood in the alleyway, directly opposite ‘Pastelli’s Warehouse’, his knee long, black leather jacket fastened up and a cigarette sticking out of his mouth. The warehouse was a front….Crichton learned this via various sources…sources that ‘willingly’ give away the information to him…the warehouse was a front for a criminal organisation owned by a crime lord boss known as Mr. Freeze…the word on the street suggested that this Mr. Freeze was no ordinary crime lord…he was ruthless and he some ‘special’ powers. Nobody could inform him exactly what these special powers were… but he decided not underestimate the man…Yeah, okay, after the super human serum he was no longer an ordinary man…but experience told him that if you underestimated your opponent…you’ve already lost the battle. His goal was to gain power via criminal means…he didn’t have time to start from scratch and build up his empire from the bottom up…no he had to seize the power by force…Mr. Freeze’s men would respect the display of strength…they would be too scared to double cross him…an instant Criminal Empire…at his command. He took the remaining butt of the cigarette from his mouth and threw it onto the floor, standing on it to put the orange flame out. He then brushes off a few specs of dust from the front of his leather coat before casually strolling out of the murky alleyway and heading towards the warehouse. Crichton saw the two hefty doormen stood near the ‘public entranceway’…he also noticed the considerably large bulges in their suit coats…obviously the indentation was caused by a gun and an holster…this was apparently the place…two fully armed security guards standing at the entrance of a warehouse…how fuckin’ stupid were they?…just one more thing he would have to remedy when he was in charge. As he went ever closer towards the entranceway of the warehouse the guards twitched a little and began to ready themselves. Crichton walked right up the pathway, leading to the warehouse, only stopping when he was face to face with the security guards. Close up, he realised that both of the guards were more hefty than he first surmised…they had at least three inches on him height-wise and were at least two times the size of him width wise…Crichton couldn’t help the vision of Earthquake and Typhoon from his head…two extremely large wrestlers in their own right…these two guys looked like them exactly…except they were in suits. Security Guard 1: Can I help you sir? Crichton: [looking up to the security guard to the left] I have an appointment with Mr. Freeze… Crichton watched as the two security guards began to anxiously squirm in their suits…he could see it their eyes…they were just itching to go for their guns… Security Guard 2: There is nobody here who goes by that name. So go on your way. Crichton: [looking up to the security guard on his right] Listen…I know he’s in there, because I gave an appointment with him… Security Guard 1: No you don’t sir, so just walk away. [lifting his suit jacket up slightly, showing Crichton his pistol.] Crichton: [A smile developing upon his face] Nice gun there…it’ll look even better when I shove it up your ass! Both Security Guards look at each other breaking out in laughter…they take their suit jackets off, neatly fold them and place them by the side of them… Security Guard 2: That’s it…that’s it now…we tried to warn you…and now we’re going to have to kick y… ************ Mr. Freeze sat on his chair at the back end of the room looking nervously at the security cameras. It was obvious why he was named Mr. Freeze…his white suit perfectly complimented his royal blue hair…also his pale, greyish face…some kind of birth defect that wasn’t fully made aware of…nor could care less about. Mr. Freeze anxiously extracts a rather hefty looking gun from the side of his luxurious, black, leather chair and aims it in vague direction of the door. His hands tremble slightly… …The door bursts open and Mr. Freeze closes his and simply begins to fire his gun aimlessly, not realising that it is one of the Security Guards that Crichton literally threw, through the door. The salvos from the gun are not ordinary bullets, on the contrary, upon impacting the Security Guard, the salvos managed to turn his entire left arm into ice…which smashed into tiny little pieces as he crashed violently to the floor…his left arm now missing of course. Crichton: Impressive…[from outside of the door] Mr. Freeze: What the hell do you want? Then the second Security Guard comes into view, his right arm pushed up painfully behind him, as Crichton takes cover of sorts, behind the mans huge frame. Mr. Freeze glances at the Security Guards obvious broken nose and winces slightly. Crichton: To talk… Mr. Freeze: Not many people are able to take out Luke and Duke here, d…do you want to become part of my crew? Crichton: [popping his head over the mans shoulder, and sarcastically shaking his head] Nah…I’ll think I’ll just take the whole thing… Mr. Freeze: [an angry look developing upon his face] I think not! Crichton: You don’t have a choice in the matter! With that Mr. Freeze lifts his gun once more and attempts to take aim…Crichton swiftly pushes the security guard towards Mr. Freeze, the freeze gun goes off and the security guard falls to the ground…Crichton instantly dives over the body as it hits the floor, taking Mr. Freeze down. The accurately dubbed ‘Freeze Gun’ is forced half way across the floor, as the two of them tussle a little upon the floor. Freeze gains the upper hand and places his knee clumsily upon Crichton’s chest. Freeze: I bet you came here thinking I was going to be some kind of pushover, hey? Well I’m not…my genes have been genetically haltered…I will not give up my empire so easily… Crichton: I’ll be the judge of that… Crichton grabs the top of Freeze’s suit and throws him off, then performs a kip-up, placing him back into a standing position. He waits a couple of seconds while Freeze gets back to his feet.…then goes in for the attack. Freeze throws a punch…Crichton dodges the blow…returning the favour, only hitting him in the kidney’s, the intended target…Freeze stumbles forwards a little…allowing Crichton to follow this up, in one smooth motion, with a vicious upper cut. Freeze falls to the floor, barely conscious. Crichton: Your empire belongs to me now! ************ Crichton sat on his NEW black, leather seat…behind his NEW luxurious mahogany desk…smoking one of his NEWLY acquired expensive cigars…he had basically accomplished what he had went out to do…indeed he had taken control of a criminal organisation…all that he had to do now was convince the others of his supremacy, of his power…basically make his presence felt has their new leader, boss…whatever he was. That was the difficult part in his eyes… His mind shifted from that relatively daunting task and fixed firmly upon his other career…his wrestling career. On that very night he and Jaden would have to go up against the sick individuals known as Clyde and Bethany…the battle wasn’t going to be pretty…it wasn’t going to be full of technical wrestling moves…it was going to be a street fight pure and simple…the kind of fight Crichton loved…fighting a legend like Clyde and winning would make him even more recognised in the wrestling world…he knew he was ready…but was Jaden ready?
