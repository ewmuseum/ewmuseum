Down With Bodaciousness!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.28.02(#3 FF) “That’s right, all you who follow the good word!” The amplified voice of Rush Mason can be heard as the promo opens, as it echoes off somewhere, while we are stuck in utter black, still waiting for a full visual fade in. “We have gathered to stop the evil. We have gathered to protect all that is righteous!” With that, the scene is clear, as we see Rush standing amidst a large crowd, speaking on a podium to the large mass. His voice is quite passionate in the heat of the moment as he speaks to these “followers of the good word.” Rush: Now you all know that I am Voodoo’s most influential warrior, going out there and fighting for our exposure every single night as a I bleed! Fighting for exposure would be better replaced with “whining” or “evilly cheating others,” but these people are Voodoo followers. Rush is the most famous idol they have. Rush: But on Friday, I face a man who is, to say the least, a slap in the face to our beautiful religion! This elicits a small reaction from the crowd of 100 or so, who cheer on their leader at the podium. Rush: This Thomas fellow, who apparently is, “Totally Tubular” makes for the utmost disrespect we can face! Cheering in support of Rush’s words again, there are isolated shouts of hatred against TTT, but the crowd still listens intently as Rush preaches on, defacing his Fusion opponent. Rush: So, today, we have been called by Bon Dieu to slap him in the face. We will destroy his idols! With that, Rush steps down from the podium, looking down on the space around which the crowd stands in, more or less, a large circle. Rush reaches from behind the desk, and one lone man leaps up to the podium. “Take me! Let me lay in Bon Dieu’s arms!” Rush raises and eyebrow and scrunches up his face, not quite sure of what to do in this situation. He tries to whisper to the guy, despite the fact that most eyes are on this man. Rush: Dude, get up. I’m not gonna kill you. The man’s eyes grow large, and his lips begin to tremble. He, in fact, looks as if he’s about to cry, as he pleads with Rush. “Please, sir, give me the honor of slaughter by your hand. I’ve given up my family, money, and everything I have to come today to reach Bon Dieu.” Rush looks over to Ray Ray, who hasn’t been noticeably audible thus far, using his face to motion for some sort of help. The manager, however, has no answer but a shrug. Rush looks out on the crowd, all focused on whether Rush will deliver this man into salvation or not. Rush: Uhh…I have no weapon of proper killing for a man such as yourself. The man, who is seemingly mentally unbalanced, looks up at Rush, with a huge grin across his face. He speaks in a voice of adoration. “Please, sir. Kill me with your hands.” Rush’s look turns to one of disgust as he gulps, looking down at the man. Suddenly, a thought comes across his mind. Rush, out of nowhere, kicks the man in the back of the head softly. Rush: I dare not grant anyone permanent leave with the great God, but you shall have your temporary visit. Now, back to the ceremony! The man’s out cold body tumbles down a few steps, as they turn back to the large circle, and the group begins to throw surfboards, strategically at the protest for no apparent reason. Rush: Throw those heathen’s items to the circle! The pile continues to grow, until finally it is of good height and weight. Rush looks down, grinning evilly. He walks down, standing alone in the circle, as the crowd around averts their gaze to his every move. Rush: Thus…let these boards of… “bodaciousness” and “radicalism” be cast into the depths of the abyss! As he says this, though, Rush realizes that he has no way with which to cast these into the abyss, so he meekly asks the crowd. Rush: Uhh…does anyone have a lighter? On cue, a dozen or so people begin to light the surfboards, and the pile begins to go up in smoke and blazes. The crowd cheers wildly as they watch on, and Ray Ray stands behind Rush, the two talking as the scene fades to black… “Ray Ray…you see how my authority ruled over these people? Like that guy who wanted me to kill him? “Yeah…you mean that guy they’re throwin’ into the fire right now?” “Oh great…”
