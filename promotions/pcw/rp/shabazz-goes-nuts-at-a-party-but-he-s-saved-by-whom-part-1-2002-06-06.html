Shabazz Goes Nuts At A Party... But... He's Saved... By Whom? (Part 1)

6 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/06/02 OOC- Yeah, I know, that title sucks ass, eh? [/OOC] Location: California State mall in Los Angeles, California.Date: 06.06.02Time: 1:23 PMWeather: Clear with a chance of showers. The scene opens up inside of what appears to be a mall. It’s packed with people, people are every which way you look and turn. The mall smelled of the sweat and air conditioning, it was a hot day. Various stores were opened; it was the part of the day when the shopping rush occurred, just around lunchtime. We see our Civil Rights Activist, Jordan X Shabazz, standing with a group of cameras and people behind him. The people are obviously working for Jordan X Shabazz, this is discovered by the type of clothing they were. T-shirts with the acronym ‘N.I.G.G.A.’ on them, and some black or blue sweat pants. Jordan X Shabazz seemed to be telling a long and drawn out tale to the production crew that followed him. When he finished, there was a lot of laughter. Jordan smiled back, proud of his joke, as they kept walking. Jordan was in the mall for a small and short seminar that he was supposed to give to a group of college students, but after pondering it, Jordan has decided to throw a party in the area that was rented off for his speech. It was a good idea, and it was going to provide some serious fun. A lot of people had invited their friends, and drunken college kids were good. Because if they could remember anything, it would be the propaganda that you would spew at them. Jordan had his group of people handing out t-shirts in the mall, and handing out pamphlets. As they neared the area that had been rented for Jordan X Shabazz’s impromptu party, music blared. The song was ‘World War 3’ by The Ruff Ryders, and Snoop Dogg. Immediately upon hearing this music, Jordan X Shabazz broke out into a run. He runs towards the entrance of the party place. When Shabazz gets there, he is shocked to find that a party has already started without him. Tables and tables of food are out, music is blaring, people are dancing, talking, and generally having a good time. You’d think that he would be glad that the party that he had scheduled had already gotten off the ground and people were having a good time. The place was secluded from the rest of the mall so nobody on the outside could see what was happening. Some people were drinking 40 ounces and some people were smoking weed, this is what angered Jordan X Shabazz the most. The area was mainly African American, and so was the college in the area, so Jordan was even angrier seeing this. Heh. Shabazz looked around crazy eyed and screamed. “What the hell is going on in here?” he screamed rather loud, but not loud enough to be heard over the chitchat and the loud music. This angers Shabazz beyond what we have seen before. He immediately looks to where the sound system is based. He runs there and he pulls the plug on the music. The collective groan of anger from College students is heard as they all direct their attention to Jordan X Shabazz, who is now sweating profusely from his head. It is now drenching his brand new suit. “LISTEN UP! THIS IS MY PARTY, AND I WILL NOT HEAR ANY OF THIS RAP GARBAGE THAT YOU ARE PLAYING! THESE MEN DEPICT…” “Shut yo’ white ass up,” one drunk college student to Jordan X Shabazz. Obviously, this angers Jordan X Shabazz. Think about this, you are proud of your race so much, it’s something that you pride you being alive on, your great race, and then somebody has the gall to go ahead and call you the race that you have sworn to fight against? The race that has oppressed you for so long, the people that enslaved your people. So it’s understandable to say that Jordan X Shabazz was angry. So angry that he decided to hop off of the small area where the sound system was (Which is now once again blaring with music due to the fact that somebody decided to plug it back in as Jordan left). Jordan is now in pursuit of this man, but he doesn’t know who said it. Jordan looks around the crowd, which is now back in full party mode. “Who the hell said that?” asks Shabazz angrily. “Just let it go, dog, it’s all gravy, dat nigga was probably kiddin’ wit’ ya.” “No, ‘dog’ I am not going to let it slide,” Jordan says while putting emphasis on the word dog, trying to show his obvious anger. “NOW WHO SAID THAT?!” Sadly for Jordan, nobody is paying attention to him. He looks at some laughing and giggling college students who are obviously drunk and blitzed (High) out of their mind. Jordan, being the paranoid Civil Rights Activist that he is, assumes that these teenagers are laughing at the fact that somebody called him a white boy. Shabazz walks over towards them with intent. “Is there something funny, children?” “You best step off n*gga, we’s just straight chillin’ down hurr,” says one of the blitzed and drunken men. “WHAT DID YOU JUST CALL ME?” Jordan X Shabazz screams. Jordan X Shabazz tackles the man and immediately begins throwing punches, this guy, still drunk and high, fights back, throwing right hands at the face of Jordan X Shabazz. Soon they are broken apart by some intelligent bystanders who realize what could happen if the thing got out of hand and these two men decided to head to guns to solve their differences. Both men are now up, Shabazz with a bloody lip, and the man he had a short brawl with having a bloody nose. Shabazz ruffles his suit and nods. “That’s right, you can’t use the ‘N’ word around me. It’s a disgrace, after all these years of having the white man use that word as a derogatory remark you think that you can just come out here and call me one of those? Give me a break. And that English you used…” Shabazz doesn’t even finish because he’s so angry. Sweat pours down his head, and the smile that had adorned his face when he entered the mall. Shabazz begins to walk back to his group of supporters, muttering words about hip-hop and R&B ruining young African American minds. As Jordan walks to his entourage of fans and supporters, his cell phone rings. He checks the phone box, it reads ‘Jackson, D.’ Jordan picks up the phone. “Hello?” “Yo’ Jordan, what’s crackilackin’ G?” comes a voice. “Cracker lacking? G?! My name starts with a J. Pardon? Whom am I speaking to?” Jordan says angrily into the phone. “Negro please, it’s Dante. Dante Jackson. I was in the XWA, remember?” Ah yes, Jordan remembers. Jordan and Dante Jackson were actually at one time very good friends, both men live around Texas, and Jordan used to spend time frequenting some of the Houston/Dallas nightclubs that Dante frequented. “Ah yes, apprentice,” Jordan says with a smile. “What can I do you for?” “Meet me at Def Jam headquarters in downtown Los Angeles, I’m working a deal right now, come alone. This is important.” CLICK! Shabazz looked at his group of people, Dante WAS a good friend, and he did seem desperate. He was going… ***************************************************************** 30 minutes later…. Jordan X Shabazz had arrived at Def Jam headquarters. Usually Shabazz would be happy to be on the property of Def Jam headquarters, they had produced several of the rap games most popular superstars. Redman, Method Man, Ja Rule, just to name a few. But Jordan had taken over a new kind of being. It was different, he didn’t support the use of Ebonics, and he didn’t like how black people portrayed themselves. So he walked on the property with disdain. As he was walking he was surprised to find guards at the door. Shabazz stood in front of a metal detector with two uniformed police officers in front of him. Shabazz went into his pockets and pulled out everything he had in them, as Shabazz walked through the metal detector, nothing happened. Before he was let through, a question was asked. “Sir, who are you hear to see?” “None of your business, I’m Civil Rights son. I could be hear to see anybody.” The officer, not wanting to anger Jordan, for the possibility that he could be a friend of Russell Simmons, allows Jordan through. As soon as Jordan enters the office he notices the nice smell, the air conditioner booming at full blast, and he notices the smile on the faces of the workers at the Def Jam offices. There were three floors in this building, the second floor was the one occupied by Def Jam South, and the one where Dante was to be in a meeting. Jordan walked up the stairs slowly, they were damn nice, red carpeting covered the stairs, and a nice handle rail. Jordan got to the second floor, he noticed that all the office doors were closed, and that all the workers were hard at work. He walked door to door and looked for the President, who would be in the meeting with Dante Jackson. Shabazz went to each door and opened them just enough to peer through, so far he hadn’t found Dante Jackson. Finally, Jordan walked towards the door that was separated from the rest of the hallway. It’s obviously the room with Dante Jackson in it, because it’s the only stone unturned, so to speak. Shabazz doesn’t even knock on the door, instead, he bursts the door open and he walks in with a big grin on his face. When he enters he sees Def Jam head, Russell Simmons, talking to Dante Jackson and an old white man who appears to be his agent or lawyer. Jordan gives Dante a firm handshake. “Been a while man,” Jordan says to Dante. “Oh, hi.” Shabazz says to Russell Simmons. “We are in the middle of a meeting sir, I’d appreciate if you waited for Dante outside,” the Def Jam head honcho says to Jordan X Shabazz, with a stern voice. “It’s okay, we’re leaving now anyways.” Jordan says while smiling. “You have your nice little stairways, and your nice little office, you make millions and millions of dollars, BUT YOU EXPLOIT US! YOU EXPLOIT YOUR OWN RACE! YOU MAKE MONEY BY…” “Get the fuck out of my office,” Russell says firmly. “Dante, you leave with him, I’ll close things out with your agent.” Dante nods, and shakes Russell Simmons’s hand. Jordan and Dante are almost outside of the building before Jordan speaks. “What was so important that you wanted me here for?” “I’ve been seeing the shiznit you’ve been doin’ on television… Dog, that’s some embarrassing shit, you’re basically a black white guy who hates on white people.” “Pardon me? I can’t believe you are disrespecting me like this, Dante.” Jordan says. Dante shakes his head. “Another prime example of your turning whiteness, if you were normal, you would steal me in my jaw with a right hand and stomp on my face. But it’s all gravy, I’m going to have to BEAT the black back into you, but I’m sure it’ll work.” “But…” 'I mean two months ago, you were apart of a character, a serious damn person whom the black culture looks up to! Now you're just a man who happens to represent 12% of the United States! My god, you were representing 48% last month. You need a forty and a hoe quick!' Jordan looks at Dante a bit oddly. 'And what's that supposed to do to help me gain back my blackness?' “You’ll see Jordan, you’ll see…”
