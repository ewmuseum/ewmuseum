Date: Pre-Fusion For once Turmoil had ensued Friday Fusion, instead of it being the other way around. For in a move that shocked the World, the New Breed reformed with the man long thought deceased and then long thought a complete moron after that, Dustin Downey. But as Downey articulately helped put the boots down with the rest of New Breed he seemed more than mentally capable. To further the confusion, 'Controversial' Corey Williams had cut a promo that had taken the crowd on an emotional roller-coaster ride, never once revealing whether they were to hate him or love him. But what he did proclaim was that he was better than the Ultimate Authority and better than the New Breed, that it was not the talent OR the power that controlled PCW, it was HE who truly owned PCW. For the people controlled PCW, and they were controlled by the controversy, and CCW is the wielder of that controversy. But in a move that no one could understand, Corey completely ignored his prior comments and went ahead and joined the rejuvenated New Breed. He followed the orders of the men whose shadows he had vowed to never live in again. He shook hands with a man who he had often said he hated more than anyone else in the World, a man that seeming lacked any superiority at all. There is but one word on EVERYONE'S lips and mind, why? Or perhaps you just answered your own question...... ::The scene opens up with a shot of a dumpster lying in a dark alleyway. Sticking out of the dumpster is a huge plaque, which, as we can just make out, reads the words 'Williams and Son Inc.' in plain lettering. The camera zooms out and then refocuses, this time on a large building. Above the door is a huge bright-pink neon sign, where we can see the words 'CCW Productions' written in fancy writing. Our camera fades in as we know find ourself inside this very building. We are in a quiet room where a good-looking blonde woman sits at a desk writing on a notepad. Just behind the desk are two large double doors that lead to some place we know must be of some sort of importance. Suddenly a small door at the front of the room opens and sound suddenly flood the room. The camera swings round as 'Controversial' Corey Williams enters the room, Bear fur coat, leather pants, dark sunglasses and a huge grin. On the other side of the door are thousands of reporters, cameras flashing, questions fired left and right. But both Corey and the lady at the desk completely ignore the sounds and sights coming from the other room. Corey turns and closes the door behind them, shutting out the sound. He walks over to the blonde receptionist at the desk and grabs her notepad out of her hand.:: Corey: How are we Sheryl? Sheryl: Not bad Mr Williams. ::Corey inspects the notepad.:: Corey: So, what's on the cards for today. Sheryl: Well, you're kneeing the Prime Minister of France in the balls at 1 o'clock and you have an executive meeting at 2. Corey: Uh-hunh, and at what time will you be giving me a blow-job? Sheryl: That's 3:30 sir. ::Sheryl indicates where it is on the notepad.:: Corey: Great messages? ::Corey puts the notepad back on the desk.:: Sheryl: Mr. Downey rang and wanted to see if you would like to.... ::The receptionist picks up her notes again and reads from them:: Sheryl: ...'hang with the boys in my crib.' ::Corey sighs.:: Corey: ...losers, just because I'm back in New Breed doesn't mean I have to actually talk to them, does it? Sheryl: No, of course not sir. Corey: Thank you Sheryl. ::Corey reaches into his pocket and draws out a hundred dollar bill which he casually tosses onto the desk. Sheryl snatches up the bill greedily whilst Corey begins to head for the large door at the back of the room, which no doubt must contain his office. Sheryl suddenly remembers something and cuts off Corey from reaching the door.:: Sheryl: Uh... Mr. Williams? Corey: What is it? Sheryl: I just remembered, there's a Mr. Wong in your office to see you. ::This changes things, Corey turns a 180 to face Sheryl, no longer smiling.:: Corey: What does he want? Sheryl: He didn't say.... ::Corey ponders the situation, Sheryl looks at Corey cautiously, unsure as to whether or not he's going to lose his temper. Corey shrugs.:: Corey: Oh well, I guess he's just here to ask the question that everyone wants to ask. Cheryl: So...... y-you're not mad? Corey: Of course not, for this is the very moment at which I finally stake my claim of controversy for the whole World to here. Now I have the talent AND the power to back up my beliefs, the World is in for a shock..... And after all, isn't that what they wanna see? Cheryl: Uh....I guess so. ::Corey begins to walk for the door but remembers one thing before he leaves and turns back to face Cheryl.:: Corey: Oh, and Cheryl..... Cheryl: Yes Mr. Williams? Corey: You're fired. ::Corey says it so cheerfully that it stuns Cheryl. Corey turns and walks through the door, smiling to himself.:: Corey: Ahh, Wongy Wongy Wongy, what brings you to my humble abode...... if by humble you mean incredibly mother f*cking huge. ::The camera swings around, where we see the often uni-sexed Sandy Wong sitting on a small chair.:: Wong: CCW! I've been waiting for you f-- Corey: 'Scuse me? 'CCW'!? Lemme ask you a question my squinty-eyed friend, have you ever committed a felony? Wong: Well.... no. Corey: Then it's Mr. Williams to you! ::Corey leans over Wong, whose chair sits rather low, and stares intimidating, Wong shrinks under the strong and powerful Williams. Corey stands upright once again and walks stylishly over to the other side of the desk at which Wong is sitting. Corey sits on an armchair on the other side, which we now notice is comfier and a lot higher than Wong's, furthering the intimidation.:: Wong: ....Mr. Williams, I'm here today to ask you-- Corey: I know what you're here to ask me about. You're here to ask me the question that everyone is dying to know the answer to. You want to ask me the question that is all anyone can think about. The first ever PTC pay-per-view, Rent a Hero losing his virginity, Tyler Nelson regaining control of PCW, Osama Bin freaking Laden declared dead and even Snow winning the title back, ALL pale in comparison to what I did last Friday. And what you wanna know is 'why'. Wong: Well, yeh I guess so. I mean-- Corey: Why did I insult New Breed only moments before joining them, why would I possibly spend half an hour of PCW air time to get the fans to rally behind me, to finally believe what they already knew, why would I do that only to throw it away and join the New Breed. Is that what you wanna ask Wongy? Wong: It's just that-- Corey: Well let me answer your question with a question, Wongy. Let me ask you this, what is controversy? Wong: Controversy? Ummm...it's... it's cha-- Corey: Controversy is chaotic, illogical and sure as hell 'what the people wanna see!' Who ever said that controversy had to make sense? I don't recall that being in the rules of controversy which I created. The fact is that at the end of the night the people were booing more than they had ever before, and it sure as hell wasn't for Dustin Downey! Wong: So.... that's it? Corey: ....That's more than it Wong, that's everything, that's 'what the people wanna see.' Re-joining New Breed had nothing to do with 'brother hood' or 'superiority'.... ::Corey gives a short laugh:: Corey: If it did I would had started a stable with God. No, joining New Breed wasn't about Chase, Downey OR Snow.... hell, it wasn't even about me, it was all about CONTROVERSY. It was about giving the people what they wanna see, it was about giving the junkies their fix. Wong: OK. Well it's be-- Corey: I suppose now you want to talk to me about my return match coming up on Sunday.... Wong: No actually, I was just about to leave. ::Corey completely ignores Wong, only hearing 'what HE wants to hear'.:: Corey: I'm not surprised it took them longer than a week to actually find an opponent to face me; (Corey flexes his right bicep) I'm in the best shape of my life, and I'm also PCW's newest billionaire to boot. Now I've gotta give some credit to Big Alan Steele for standing up to the challenge... but I've also gotta give him credit for being the single most boring, idiotic and blatantly homosexual wrestler in the entire PCW! I mean, for god's sakes, the man claims to be of ancient Greek descent, a civilisation that would still be here today if it weren't for the fact that they were too busy ass-raping each other to bother with the state of their empire! ::Corey smirks to himself, seeing as Wong is barely paying attention now.:: Corey: Now Alan, you do realise that over here is the U.S we wrestle with our clothes ON don't you? If you came for a good time you're probably better off playing nude twister at a high school party.... all boys I'm guessing. ::Corey notices that Wong has fallen asleep, he kicks him in the shins to wake him up, trying not to draw attention to it.:: Corey: Now I know you like to think that the almighty Zeus is you're Father, but guess what 'Bass'......(Corey takes in a deep breath)... I am your Father! ::Corey stares at the camera for about five seconds before breaking into laughter.:: Corey: Well, not really, I just always wanted to say that. But seriously Al, I can see how not having a male role-model in your life has turned you into such a flaming homo, and I can't get you to your Dad, but I'm gonna do the next best thing. This Sunday when you face Corey Williams, not 'just another New Breed member' Corey Williams, but 'CONTROVERSIAL' Corey Williams, when you come up against the single most talked about athlete in the history of this business, the man who has the money AND the talent, you will soon be in the company of MY recently departed Father.... and Bass..... say hi to Dad for me. ::Corey gets up off of his chair and walks towards the door, pushing Wong off of his chair and onto the floor on the way. Corey opens the door and is half-way through before turning back to face Wong, who is getting back onto his feet.:: Corey: Oh and Wong, just in case you wanted to know 'why' again..... it's because: controversy.... is what the PEOPLE, wanna see! ::And with that Corey slams the door shut. Our camera does not cut back to the receptionist's office, but we can hear voices through the door.:: 'Just where in the hell do you think you're going with all that stuff?' 'B-but you told me that I was fired...' 'Fired? Oh yeh, I fired you alright, from your position as receptionist. I DIDN'T, however, fire you from your position as my personal blow-jobber. In fact, I think I have a job for you right now.' ::We hear the dooming sound of Corey unzipping his pants.:: 'That's right bitch, get on your knees and suck my big fat American d*ck!' ::After that, we hear some thought-provoking and most certainly CONTROVERSIAL sound effects coming through the other door. And that's where we leave you until another journey into Corey's World of confusion, Corey and controversy.... hope you enjoyed it, well, to tell you the truth I don't really give a shit, I'm just happy I finally finished this damn rp.:: !The End!
