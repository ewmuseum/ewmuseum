Into the Tomb of the Ancients: Episode 1

Date: May 31 (Excerpted from the memoirs of Bruce Shanahan) The history of the Blackhelm Coalition is pervaded by covert expeditions into buried Egyptian tombs, isolated thickets in forbidden regions and eldritch provinces where the laws of nature are distinctly arcane and bizarrely deviated from that of the common world. Working closely at the side of the veritable Miskatonic University organization, the Coalition brotherhood has undertaken a plethora of fantastic journeys and studied a range of cosmic artifacts with origins beyond the stars and the expanses of human conception. I myself have yet to embark on a field expedition to witness these abnormalities for myself. But that is soon to change. I have been informed that a concentration of heavy CCD activity has been detected in some remote locale regularly monitored by the Coalition as a suspected hotbed of workings perpetrated by those abyssal deities. The ‘mission’ has been labeled as Red Level 3; considered highly dangerous seeing as the exact nature and motives of the perpetrators in question are as of yet still unspecified. As such, a party of standard scientific investigators has been deemed far too risky and the ‘brass’ of the Coalition have decided upon a trained escort. Stryker’s elite force, Delta Wing, has been issued the order to accompany the team into the field. Where do I come in? It seems a collection of my past night visions have centered around the area in question. The great tomb beyond the foreboding moors and through the dense hillside thickets bears the fruit of our scrutiny; they believe the labyrinth I traversed holds a hoard of celestial relics that could prove to be vital to the Coalition’s activities and the research of the Miskatonic scholars. As such, I have been unwillingly ‘recruited’ as a guide of sorts. In honesty, my preference is to retain my talents elsewhere. Tomorrow evening is the awaited ‘School’s Out’ battle for the vacated PCW Extreme title and I’d prefer to fight without this damned expedition hanging over my head for next week. Right now Big Daveylicious remains in my sights for that little debacle he committed last week; leave it to a privileged yuppie punk to steal my f*cking spotlight and virtually bail out that Flaymin Aymin ingrate. Godd*mn, I could almost feel that boy’s neck about to snap under the force of those neckbreakers I wrought on his scrawny ass, one-after-another. Then I turn my back for one second and that hardcore disgrace loses me the f*cking match. Unfortunately the ball is not in my court on the matter of discretion. With that cloud looming on the horizon, I go into battle tomorrow evening against the bloodthirsty forces of Primetime Championship Wrestling and then still have the ‘fortune’ of looking forward to the possibility of facing down the minions of the abyss in whatever God forsaken locale they ship us off to. At any rate, Craven and I arranged a little ‘briefing’ for tonight wherein I hope he’ll shed light on exactly where our little rag-tag guerilla group is being dispatched to and what we will likely be facing. As always, I fear (actually I dread) what he will likely reveal. All revelations I’ve ever received in this matter have had the tendency to be more so disheartening than assuring…. Craven drummed his pencil nervously on his desk, glancing up at his now-disconcerted guest who slumped groggily in his chair. His eyes darted nervously about the ceiling, a semi-blank and woebegone glaze permeating his empty gaze. Craven sighed heavily before pivoting in his chair to face the jet-black safe concealed behind the desk. “You realize how it saddens me to damper your spirits, Bruce,” the wizened minister replies. “But if I gave you false information and you went in unprepared….well, frankly your chances of coming out alive would be slim to none.” “I ain’t a f*ckin’ soldier!” roars Shanahan in response. “This is mad! I can’t go in there and expect to contend with a potential force like that! I don’t experience or training! I’m just an unlucky bastard who happened to be picked at random by some band of slumbering demigods for use as a damned plaything!” “You’ve also been blessed with a gift that can be very instrumental in our cause. Bear that in mind…..” “It ain’t somethin’ I asked for and it’s something I’d sure as hell kick out of my life if I had the opportunity, Randall. I didn’t ask for this supposedly ‘uncanny psychic sensitivity’ as you put it before and all it’s done is f*ck up my life!” Craven tilted back as he spun to again face his irate visitor, Shanahan’s face now taking on a ruby tinge and his eyes blazing with a pristine intensity. “You’ll be fine. Stryker and his team are the elite force in the Blackhelm organization. Besides, you will all be issued the proper deterrents for your descent into the valleys of Leng and thus you shall be virtually invisible to any and all dwellers of that accursed realm.” Bruce pulled himself from the leather couch resting against the far wall, dragging himself somberly to the window to watch as rain trickled down the glass pane. He elevated his eyes to look upon the storm clouds rolling overhead, thunder crashing in the distance as a jagged streak of lighting penetrated the heavens. “For me there truly is no liberation….”
