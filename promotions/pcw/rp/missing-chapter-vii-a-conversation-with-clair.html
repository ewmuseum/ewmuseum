Missing: Chapter VII: A Conversation With Clair

Date: Chapter 7 Still Waiting.... Was I stupid and wasting my time in waiting for this? I was on my AIM name, and had just added Clair Rivence to my list, hoping to get some information. Clair's email showed up perfectly on her database profile I had and I thought it a little odd that someone would put their email adress in a private database. Either way, if it was the real Clair who I had added, I wanted to be the first to talk to her and get information. I only had two people on this private AOL name. On my other one, I had various PCW people who I talked to when I could probably just walk up to them for a chat. So who was the other name I had on this private AOL name? Well, it was the missionary of the Hare Krishna, back home in the USA. I had added him to give him information whenever I got it. The only problem with that, was that I had no real information! Sure, I had figured out three of the five other aliases that Ivan Chistov owned under his belt, and I had figured out that Clair Rivence was a dangerous women. Also, that Henry Claval was the mastermind behind both Clair and Ivan. And plus, even if I had information, the missionary was hardly ever on. I thought to myself. I had always referred to the missionary at the Hare Krishna as a missionary, but he had to have a real name to, right? He had to have some given name, and yet I didn't know it. I had been at the Hare Krishna for about a month now, going and talking to the same person each time, what was that guys name? John? Bill? Kevin? Mark? I could sit here guessing all day. Instead, I made a mental note to ask the question when I got home to America after this was all over with. I looked back at the computer screen on my labtop and realized that I had one person on AIM! The person? Clair Rivence. I brought up the conversation window and punched in the words, 'hello' into the message box. SyN: Hello. Clair: Who are you? How did you get this AIM name? SyN: I just need to know some information. Clair: Who are you? How did you get my AIM name? SyN: I'm an associate of Henry Claval. Clair: How do you know about Claval? SyN: Through Ivan Chistov. Clair: Chistov? Do you currently know where Chistov is? He disappeared about three weeks ago on a scam mission. SyN: I thought he was with you? Clair: He was, disguising himself as a priest again. Then he left for America and I lost contact with him. SyN: Is it possible he is under another name? Clair: Not likely. I checked under all four of his other names and no other activity on any of them. SyN: What are the other four aliases he uses? Clair: You don't know. SyN: I forgot. Clair: Forgot? SyN: I can't remember. What are they? Clair: There is Mystique, Thomas, Chistov, Gordan, and his real name.... SyN: What is his real name? Clair: How can you not know this information. SyN: Claval did not mention in. Clair: This is odd. What are you up to? What is your mission. SyN: I'm on your side. Clair: Ok then, what else has Claval talked to you about? SyN: He didn’t go into detail. Clair: Claval hasn’t even kept in contact with Ivan or me for the past month or so. If he did talk to you, surely you would have some information. Clair: What are you trying to pull? SyN: Believe me or not. What is Chistov’s fifth alias? SyN: Hello? Error: The following message could not be sent: SyN: Hello? SyN: Clair? Error: The following message could not be sent: SyN: Clair? And it was over. She had blocked me.
