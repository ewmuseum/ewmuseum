Hunted Man

15 November 2002

Title: Hunted Man Featuring: Tim Walden Date: November 15, 2002 The city streets were a nice change of pace from the typical days at the office, but I knew it was only a matter of time before I'd have to return..The past week or so had been very up-and-down for me, though the ups were much less concrete than the downs. On the plus side, I knew I could take the Trapezoid alone, he was beatable... And he was the TV Champion. This sucked from the simple perspective of that being a 2-time Television Champion had slipped through my fingers yet again, and that it just wasn't going to have value to be the fourth two-time champion, nobody remembers number 4..I sure as hell didn't enjoy having to be bothered with the title's status yet again if I wasn't the man to hold it, I'd gotten too many failed shots to care.. But however, this also meant that nobody. Not a one person in the match had beaten him, not Tomas, not Pendragon, not Rush Mason.....Nobody had been in the ring while he was counted out, put his shoulders to the mat for a 3 count, made the sorry son of a bitch tap out..... ....Except me. I'm the first person in his PCW run to make that sorry shape tap, and I should have been the Television Champion, but no, the damn referee ignored the submission, let Tomas cheat me out of the win, just because Ms. Saddle figured she could get her to perform that interesting pinning combination privately..Same sort of screwjob that seemed to play into my matches a lot recently, but yet the fans still booed me every time I walked down that entrance ramp. It bothers me that they're willing to ignore the fair and honest victories deserved out of pure spite for my position, but I can always explain it away as an error in judgment for the time being...What mattered here was just that I was always getting cheated, first out of title shots, then out of my freedom, then insult added to injury with yet another TV Title failure..I could play that game too...But I didn't need to tonight. The frustration for gold had led me to this....The Cruiserweight Title tournament...The bracket was just a little tipped in my favour, having to deal with either a fish-out-of-water tag team wrestler or Eddie Mandrake, a pathetically low-caliber worker who didn't even win the title from a real wrestler in the first place in the semifinals...Teresa Tomas, that little Southern bitch to deal with quickly in the quarterfinals, one more night to destroy her physically before she gets the opportunity to use her control to try to do similarly with my mental state, but before I get there, I've got the one opponent that is if anything a little pest to only be bothered with for the possibility of cheating arising on her part.. I don't have the best history with Boca del Inferno, but that's probably a good thing since if I did, I'd probably be a member of the Ream Team by now..We only really met in the ring once, when that nut got all pissy over my accidentally though deservedly knocking Steven Smith unconscious..Heck, I didn't know when the last time he'd really wrestled was, being that last time Boca got into a match it was just Rent-Boy in disguise...But Rent-A-Job wasn't in the tournament at this point, and Boca couldn't just be replaced with him at the last second, we had regulations for this sort of thing to prevent him from slipping through the cracks normally...This warranted a little nervousness, however, still... I wouldn't have the advantage of certainty of my opponent's identity under the mask, and I know that one misstep could be fatal. I could go easy to ensure the safety of the homosexual luchador, but that would be risking mistaken identity and an opponent able to take the advantage of my caution...I have to go all-out anyway, hospital bills for someone who barely wrestles would be better than yet another early exit, and after getting screwed out of my shot at just about every title in PCW by that damn Trapezoid, I think it's high time that I get an advantage here..The only real problem with making a charge through the tournament is that come Alliances, I would be left having to spread out my effort between 4 separate matches. I've already got the Alliances match to work in to win one for the Front Office and rebuild our power structure, not to mention keeping Ellie~! from getting herself killed in the Alamo match while trying to stop the HIT from getting his grubby mitts on that endorsement..And that's not even considering the most important match, the one that has personal aggravation to deal with in the form of those selfish tax evaders, the Tease and the Trapezoid, so happy with themselves since they've gotten on a roll of ill-gotten victories..They think they're so good that they managed to get a tainted pinfall on me 2-on-1 and win that pathetic #1 contendership match in such ridiculous fashion, get my services as a freakin' valet..But I showed them what I was capable of if not kept entirely in check, just needed one spare taste of freedom and I managed to keep the HIT from even showing up to Turmoil... That'll probably come back to haunt me soon, but not as long as I can help it, since there's one important corollary to the rules of valetdom: They can't make you do anything if they can't tell you to do it. ...Ain't it great to have an unlisted cell phone number? Just as long as I keep from being found by them, I'll never have to deal with the consequences..But the problems of having to get my revenge on them as I try to remain out of reach are the main worry. The tag match, the possible quarterfinal against Tomas, and inevitable other confrontations..There's only so much one can do to stay ahead of the game while every public step one takes is an opportunity to get dragged behind...Once you're caught, you can't escape until just the right shot to do so comes along, just as Tuesday came to be the chance I needed to get back my freedom for the time being... But tonight is another day to catch a break or be caught...I need a plan to escape on Fusion, to fail to be found and yet to not only retain the upper hand in my dealings with them and keep my commitments for the Front Office, but also wrestle my match with the abnormal Celestial to a victory on my end... An idea comes to light as I continue along the walk to my hotel, separate from the majority of the roster after the poor luck that brought me to Tuesday's classroom visit last week.. I reach into my pocket, grabbing the cellular phone... 'Hello, Greg?' A pause, and a sigh of relief... 'Could you get me the PCW Production Department?' ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
