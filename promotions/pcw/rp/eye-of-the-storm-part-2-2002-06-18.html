Eye Of The Storm (Part 2)

18 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-18-02 When my hour was up, there was a glimmer in my eye, as I stared back at the newest honorary warrior, formerly known as my attorney. One step of the mountain had been climbed… I no longer had any doubts that this man could represent me. He would know what to say at the right times. He would know when to keep quiet. And he would know how to prove my case without ever uttering a lie. As I slowly walked out of the office, I felt a feeling long forgotten in my warrior body. I felt a ray of happiness! That soon faded however as a guard dressed in blue shackled me from behind, a bitter reminder of my unfortunate predicament. I tried to hold my head high as I was ushered down the steps of the building, and into a squad car, where I was raced to the jail. The jail… what a horrid place of pity it is. The violence, the disrespect, and most of all the feeling of restraintment from the world. It took everything inside of me to block out these distressing factors… but it would take me much, much more to get back into society. Marching down the narrow hallway, with jail cells on each side, I felt like a dog being led to the pound. My actions had only come from nature… yet suddenly I have been attacked and held captive--by the so-called 'good guys.' I was pushed in to a cell of my own, which held only one other inmate; a 6'5 behemoth with tattoos across his arms. The man looked at me, angrily, surely giving me a sign of unwelcomeness. Almost clearly signaling that once the guard had left… I stared back into his eyes, focussing all of the warrior inside of me directly into him. It was as if a lion had leapt from inside of me, grizzly and growling. I would not be bothered by this inmate again. The guard was gone, and I slumped down against a wall, closing my eyes, eventually falling asleep. I was in a jungle… no wait, a storm. The wind was twirling around me, and dangers approached me from every direction. I wanted to yell for help, but I could not be heard. I wanted to run away, but my legs were frozen. …I wanted to cry… Suddenly, a light came down from above, shining into my eyes. Was I dying? No. But I was safe. The dangers were gone, and I was shielded by a spinning wall of wind. It was wondrous… at last I was free! Free from all the dreads I faced day in and day out… free from all the difficulties that I have conquered in the past and must do so again in the future. Free! But then I realized… this was only the eye of the storm. In a few moments… life would return. 'Are you comin' or not?' the angry voice of a guard woke me from my slumber. 'It's time for lunch!' Lunch? I had completely lost track of time. For the first time, I realized that I didn't even know what day of the week it was. It was okay, however, because the only thing I could do right now was take things by steps. Steps that would hopefully lead to the top of the mountain. I sat down at a table in the dining hall, which was covered with dirt and food from fights over the years. Moments earlier, I had gotten my rations: a rotten apple and a lump of what looked like potatoes. I was disgust by the awful sight, but starving inside. I had not eaten in days. It was no sooner that I put the first fork-full of gruel to my mouth that a gang of three prisoners approached me from behind. While the one on my left distracted my attention, the one behind me restrained me and the last swiped the tray of food from under my nose. I turned abruptly around. Did they truly think they could push me around just because they could? Without thinking, I through my power into the largest one, and he tripped back over a table, landing in several piles of food. Instantly, everyone in the room was up, hopeful of a fight. Hopeful of my blood splattering and my body falling to the floor. One of the thugs grabbed my by the neck hard, and the other approached me from the front. Before they could kill me, I threw out my legs and kicked one in the stomach, dropping him like a bug. The one behind me would be harder. I used leverage from the table in front of me to throw back my weight and knock the attacker into the next table. With the grip loosened, I gave a few elbows to the stomach, and then finished it off with a back headbutt right to the man's forehead. Guards rushed in, and I ran backwards, disappearing into the crowd of bystanders. I would not eat today, no, but I had won. I had shown everybody that I could stand up for myself--that I could defend myself! Nobody would be messing with me again as long as I was here. Nobody would doubt my strength. Inside I smiled. I had really won. But the satisfaction soon faded as I realized the truth. This single event might have made things easier for the present time, but in the near future, more danger was lurking. Soon my protective walls of sell-assurance would fade away, and once again I would defenseless to those that could harm me most. I had succeeded in this small encounter, yes… but this was only the eye of the storm.
