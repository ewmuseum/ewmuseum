Twenty Months

16 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/16/2002 *The scene opens up on the beautiful mountain shoreline of Japan. The scene is truly that of nirvana. The sun is setting on the sea on the horizon. The wind is blowing at about fifteen miles an hour coming straight out of the south. Finally, Jon Kano comes into focus standing on top of the mountain looking at the beautiful Japanese sunset. Kano is dressed in a fine red Versace suit.* Kano: Look at that sight. I lived here for two years early in my career. I wrestled for True Japan Wrestling for the better part of two years and I loved every minute of it. I swear to God this has to be one of the most beautiful sights in the world, watching the sun extinguish itself on the sea. I love it. *Kano reaches into his left inside breast pocket and pulls out a fat Cuban cigar. He unwraps it and takes a deep breath of it.* Kano: Ahhhhhhh, Havanna's finest. I don't like Communism, or Cuba, but Castro has got his collective shit together making these little bastards. I try not to smoke or possess these when I am in America, but now that PCW is over here, I can go hog wild on these things. *Kano sticks the cigar in his mouth, and pulls out one of those butane torch lighters that you can buy at just about any gas station in the world. He slowly lights the cigar, puffing like mad on the cigar to keep it lit. When it finally lights, Kano takes a deep drag out of the Havanna cancer stick.* Kano: Oh sweet Jesus, that is good. I don't know why, but whenever I am smoking one of these babies, I feel like getting down to business. Business, but not my business in the sense of Kano Enterprises, but the business I have on Fusion. The Mall Brawl, PCW's annual clusterfuck, the traditional brawl for all of us who just love to get in a place and whoop some ass. Now clusterfucks are what I truly do believe are the most dangerous matches in PCW and I'll tell you why. In clusterfucks, there is no way on telling who is coming from where. One minute you could be on top of the mountain, so to speak, and the next minute you could be laying on your back, not knowing what the hell just happened and why it feels like you just got knocked the f*ck out. The only reason you feel like that is because it happened and your competition in the match is over. You must be in a state of constant awareness in this kind of a match, and there is one advantage that I have over all the other competitors in this match on Friday. I am the only member of PCW to ever compete in all the Mall Brawls. I was there for the first one, I am here now, and I was there in between. Friday's volume in the book of Mall Brawls has high stakes, though. The Continental Title, held by Dukestar, or Duke Williams, or whatever the hell else he wants to call himself these days. I remember when I was Continental Champion. I beat the best. I took Alan Kriegman, PCW hall of famer and evil supergenius, out. Seven seconds in a cage with me and he was no longer the Continental Champion. Seven seconds, EXACTLY SEVEN F*CKING SECONDS!!!!!! And I didn't stop there, I defended that title and gave it prestige the likes of which never seen up to the then and haven't been seen since. I defended the title against Chris Sloboda, Alan Kriegman, Chet King, Stephen Blood, and the Ultimate Warlord. I was the man who set the standard by which all Continental Champions were measured by. But something has been bothering me since then. *Kano takes a Tony Soprano-like drag off of his Cuban.* Kano: When I lost the Continental Championship, it took three people to do it. At that time I was a young blood in PCW. I hadn't learned the game. I listened to the crowd rather than my head. I did what they wanted and it cost me on that night. I was wrestling Dan Hollywood, who I could beat then or now with one hand tied behind my back and both of my eyes closed. Hell, I beat him several months earlier for the Rising Star Championship. He was accompanied that night by his slut of a girlfriend, Buffy Vegas, who has forever soiled the good name of my hometown, and Chet King, the self-proclaimed 'Chat King.' At that time, they were all part of the MEDIA conglomerate and I was still the stupid young buck. I fought off all three of them for as long as I could until I could fight all three of them no longer. I lost my title on that fateful night, but I can deal with that. It is just one thing that I cannot deal with. That thing has been eating a hole in my stomach for the last twenty months since that night. That thing is that I never got my f*cking rematch. NEVER. NEVER ONCE DID I EVER GET MY REMATCH FOR THE TITLE THAT I MADE LEGITMATE IN THE EYES OF MILLIONS. So this Friday, twenty months later, I got my chance. I got the ball and I am running with it and I ain't gonna stop until I can't run anymore. And that my friends, is WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!! *The scene fades to black as Kano continues to smoke his fine Cuban cigar while watching the majestic view of the sunset into the sea.*
