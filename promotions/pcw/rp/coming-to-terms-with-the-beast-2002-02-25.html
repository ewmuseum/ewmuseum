Coming to terms with the Beast

25 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-25-02 Introduction: 'Those who hate are those without love. Without fear you would not have bravery. Without darkness you would not have light. Inside us all lives a beast who eventually feasts on your soul. The fires of hell couldn't even tame this creature, the powers of heaven couldn't destroy it. This power grows within but most just ignore the feeling. Drowning in your own sorrow, dying in your own ignorance, may god have mercy on your soul,' -Jay Sylo We left Jay Sylo last time...he was climbing a mountain which in ways signified his on going battle with the beast inside. It also helped him 'climb' the mountain known as Afwa Kilmanjaro in a way. Now he was at rest in the woods but not before finding out about the upcoming match he would have at the pay-per-view when he would take on three other men for the rising star title. Gold wasn't important but victory was. We now join Jay. He stayed in the woods and set up camp. The animals all watch on but Jay never notices. His pain and conflicts are about to come up and the beast is sure to raise it's ugly head. (The bright red ambers popped up and down in a display of red and orange. The fire did it's memorizing dance as it destroyed the old, soggy wood. Fire....it can be beautiful but yet at the same time can hurt you worse than anything else. In just a spark it can hold so much power, man thinks he can tame it but fire is the one that tames man. An owl sat perched in an old moss covered tree watching all that went on through his old, yellow, eyes which resembled the moon on a clear night. The stars had came out to play by now....dancing to and fro with their flickering motions, millions and millions of stars watching the earth sleep as they have their fun. The moon was slightly covered by darkness but was still there to watch over the world like a mother watching over her child. Three logs surrounded the fire, close enough to behold the fire's beauty but far enough away not to be burned to ashes. A small green tent sat to the side with a stick in the middle holding it up. The flaps blew through a small breeze which would send chills down the small of any mans back but not his. Sylo stood looking out into the wilderness which surrounded him like a fortress, he felt safer here than he did anywhere else. Out in the woods you wouldn't have to put up with pollution, hate, and pain. The woods are calm, all they ask for is your respect. His leg moved over the log, followed by the next. Slowly the individual sat down on the cold, damp log in front of the fire, not to warm but to gaze into it's endless beauty. His white baggy pants with the blue fire around the ankles blew in the light breeze like a flag floating high in the sky promoting it's own logo. His hair stood up, short and spiky, each strand of hair was an ice blue color. The hairs on his chin matched and the ear-ring in his left eyebrow seemed to capture the fire in it's silvery shell. His head felt like he had a thousand little workers digging into his temples, his arms ached along with his legs in a perfect symphony. His eyes were their normal pools of light blue now and his hands trembled a small bit. His nerves were shot, and everything going on in life didn't help the situation. His thoughts ran a race through his brain but it seemed to be an endless race that no one would win. Slowly, moving his hands up to his hair clinching the small rectangles in his hands.) 'It's a pain that I cannot describe....I feel like something is eating me within, something I cannot control. Afwa Kilmanjaro, he is innocent but I do not know if I can control my own actions. I find out today that I have a shot at the rising star title when PCW's March Madness rolls around. I've held gold...yes that isn't what's on my mind....what ways heavy on my mind is the fact I could hurt someone and not even mean to due to me not being able to control my own actions. I don't know...maybe I just need some sleep,' Sylo mumbled to himself. (Slowly he stood from the old, soggy log dusting off his white pants he began to head to the green tent. Twigs and branches snapped under his shoes as he made it to the entrance. Slowly he pulled back the folds and laid down on the old grey sleeping bag which laid open like open arms waiting for a hug. Sylo laid, letting the sleeping bag embrace him and quickly he was off to sleep.) --Start Dream-- (Colors of blue, white, and purple all clashed together in one large, swirling vortex. They struck each other like a hammer hitting a nail, loud bangs followed around as the 6'8' Knight known as Sylo steps through the portal. He looked around, his eyes full of wonder and fear. Taking in every inch of the swirling vortex into his deep pools until an image protruded from the ground. What he saw shocked and horrified him...it angered him....Sylo's face went through an expression of horror followed by one of sever anger. His light blue pools began to cover his whole eyes turning a tad bit darker....Sylo tried to fight it off as he saw the image of a man dressed in all black, in his hand he held a long blade but his face was covered by a black cotton mask. He drove the knife into the back of a sleeping couple who yelled out in pain as they slowly died. This was how his parents had died. Hurt filled his warm, pumping, heart as he fell to his knees on the swirling vortex type ground. Tears rolled from his eyes as he clinched his fists together so tight he could crush boulders with ease. His deigns stuck out pumping the warm, red, liquid called blood all through his body faster and faster as his heart rate increased. He couldn't hold it in his soul any longer as he let out a yell of both pain and relief.) 'I HATE YOU!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!' Was all he yelled (His breathing picked up as he slumped forward. The tears fell from his eyes like a drippy faucet. His head throbbed and his mouth tasted like cotton. He managed to move on foot forward, slowly resting on it as he pushed up his whole body trying to fight off the pain, the hurt, and the beast. He pushed, driving his body past it's limits finally pushing through the barrier which was the beast.) 'You....do not....control me!' Sylo yelled. 'I....control....you!' He yelled once more. (Like a stiff punch to the throat or a stake to the heart Sylo felt pain. The beast was trying to retake it's throne but Sylo fought it....it was an on-going battle inside like all the great wars rolled into one. Another image protruded up, Sylo's eyes began to water once more, his body began to tremble, and his mouth became even more dry than before. The image was of a grave site with a fresh plot dug up. A grey head stone loomed over the top of the hole as people all dressed in black stood around the coffin. A teenager stood putting a white rose on top as the casket lowered into the soft dirt, six feet below. This was the funeral of his grandmother.) 'Why do you do this to me!?' Sylo yelled into the vortex. 'ANSWER ME!' Sylo yelled once more. (Nothing came out....but the next image that came up actually scared Jay. His body trembled, even his eyes. He shook in fear at what he saw, sweat dropped from his head to the vortex disappearing on contact. In front of him stood.......himself. Yes himself except this was Jay....when he was overcome by the 'beast'. His eyes were the blue color from corner to corner and slowly the beast version snarled.) 'What do you want from me?' Sylo asked. 'You think I'm here to harm you or take over your body?' The beast asks. 'I'm not quit sure.....' Sylo replied. 'I am here to help you....I am trying to work with you but you deny me. I can help you if you just allow me to. I will let you control me but you at the same time must fulfill your destiny,' The beast replied. 'And what's that?' Sylo asked. 'I am not the one to say....only you know,' (Sylo bowed his head thinking about things once more. Was he to come to terms with the beast? Would they work together in harmony?) 'Fine...' Sylo finally muttered out. --End Dream-- (Sylo awoke in a cold sweat, his body was plastered to the sleeping bag as he slowly sat up rubbing his temples. He looked forward and could see the beautiful fire giving it's final dance as it slowly faded out. He felt different...almost like he was carrying another being now. He knew he had the beast inside but was the dream not really a dream? He rubbed his light blue eyes as he sat staring into darkness.) 'Could it be that the beast and I are at one? That I can unleash him when needed? If that is so then who is to stop me? Afwa...I will go easy on you because you are a good soul....I will go easy on my opponents in the rising star match....but may god have mercy on the souls of those who confront me, who oppose me, who defy me!' Sylo whispered out. (He laid back down to sleep. His eyes rolled in the back of his head as he slipped back into a coma type sleep. He slept the rest of the night, ready for Tuesday night when he would take on Afwa....and then finally move on to the pay-per-view where he would compete for the Rising Star Title. He prayed to god he really was able to control the beast.)
