Airborne

3 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/3/03 *The scene opens up inside of the first class section of a 747 jetliner. Jon Kano, dressed in a red Armani suit with black pinstripes, is sitting near the window of the plane, with no one within three seats of him. Kano folds down the tray from the back of the chair in front of him and puts a notebook on the tray. He pulls out a golden pen from his breast pocket and begins to write.* Kano (his thoughts as he is not speaking, but writing): Dear Kristin, I am keeping my promise of writing you as often as I can while away. Right now I am in the air above Eastern Iowa on my way to Turmoil. I wonder if anyone is getting my little protest against this new age in PCW. I am not doing what I am famed for. I used to be called a legend for my in ring ability and my locker room leadership. Now I am called a legend for my balls to stand up for what is right. *Kano pauses for a few moments to collect his thoughts.* Kano: I have been in this industry for seven years, honey. I have seen a lot of things. I have watched good things happen, and I have watched the bad. I have seen men actually die in the ring. I have seen others end their careers in one match because of one miscue. But the one thing I have not seen is what I am witnessing right now. A CEO is destroying his own company to prove a sick point. Its f*cking rediculous. What the hell gives him the right to do what he is doing? This company was built on my back while he was wrestling in a high school gymnasium on Sunday afternoons. No one had heard of Xavier Kannon while I was headlining cards in Tokyo or Shanghai. It isn't right. Its not right by a long shot. *A man approaches Kano.* Man: Hey you are Jon Kano, man, you F*CKING ROCK~! *Kano smiles and looks up at the man.* Kano: Thank you. Man: Can I ask you a question, Mr. Kano? Kano: Sure. Man: Are you really pissed at PCW or is it just an angle you are working? Kano: I have never been so serious in my professional life as when I say PCW is going down the shitter and I will be damned if I am going to stand by and watch it happen. Man: You seemed more intense in the ring lately. Kano: Yeah, I am. Last week, I ran through an old friend, Chet King, like he was nothing. I did something I shouldn't of, I wrestled really stiff with him and I shouldn't have done that to him. Its just like I get in the ring and two years of dedication and frustration at what is happening now comes pouring out of me and it doesn't matter if the man across the ring is friend or foe, he is going to get knocked around by me. Man: Is that what you are going to do at Turmoil? Kano: Turmoil is unique. I got three others in the ring with me, all trying to beat each other. Chet is in the match, and I don't know what is going to happen with him. Walden is in the match, and he and I got mounds of history. I taught him everything he knows. I took him under my wing when he was first in PCW and showed him how to roll in the big leagues. I lit that fire that he had in his belly and I put him on track to be a superstar. Then he turned his back on me. Last year, he and I got into the ring in front of 25,000 people. We went back and forth, to and fro, to hell and back, and when all the smoke cleared and all the dust settled, I was standing victorious in the center of the ring over his body. Then we Nerezza. I respect this guy, he is funnier than hell, and has talent. Few months from now, he'll probably be one of those names you hear constantly associated with PCW. Man: Damn. Kano: Now, if you will excuse me, I gotta finish this letter to my wife before I land and go show those three and PCW why I am exactly what I say I am, WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!! *The scene fades to black as Kano resumes writing his letter to his wife and the anonymous man leaves Kano alone.*
