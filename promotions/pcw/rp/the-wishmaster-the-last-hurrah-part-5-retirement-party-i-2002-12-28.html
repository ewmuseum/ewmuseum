The Wishmaster --- The Last Hurrah, Part 5: (Retirement Party I)

28 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/28/02 It's dark, cool out though not too much. After all, The End is holding this retirement party at the Disney Resort's Marriott Inn in the large ballroom suite they have for such occasions. Sandy Wong did alot to put this all together. And it's happening now, the night before The End's planned retirement after his match at Adrenaline. Greetings are done by The End and Sandy, dressed in black tuxedo and hostess white long-sleeved blouse with black mini skirt and stockings, respectively, who take the guests' presents and point them into the door. The End:'It's a nice gesture, doing all this for me. When I said I was retiring, I was thinking more along the lines of just a cake or something.' Sandy hugs her boyfriend, drawing some moans from the jealous people in line. Sandy:'Nothing's too good for you. You deserve it. You've had a long (for PCW) career and if Boda can get a show dedicated to him, you can get two shows and a retirement party.' The End:'Well, I am retiring on my own volition, rather than losing a match for it.' The first to walk up is El Diablo, here from his strike against PCW. He's wearing a tuxedo and his hair is tied back in a ponytail. The facepaint of the Mexican flag underneath his eyes is conspicuously absent, not that The End can see. Diablo's wife, Angela, is attending with him wearing a red dress lined with frills that makes one think she's going salsa dancing. Sandy greets them while The End goes inside. Sandy:'El Diablo, happy you could make it. Hello, Angela.' Angela: 'Hola.' El Diablo presents Sandy with a tape. Sandy:'What's this?' El Diablo:'It's a finished copy of The End. After your boyfriend decided not to go along with the movie, I decided to just green-screen past ring performances of his over the film. He gets pummeled quite a bit in this film. Hopefully he'll watch it, and it will become a favorite of his.' As Diablo and Angela walk into the ballroom, Sandy dumps the tape into the ever-growing 'reject' pile. Next to walk up is Hessian, in his normal ring attire, and Minion the Imp, who is in a witch's outfit save for... the phallus, erect stretching out from his body by a good 20 inches. And he's not even erect. As Minion knocks it against Sandy's leg, Sandy tries to ignore him while kicking it aside, looking up at Hessian. Sandy: *Agitated*'Hi.' Hessian gives Sandy the leash to a Seeing Eye greyhound and a bag of sour (literally) grapes. Hessian:'End was only a semi-dark gimmick, but he was respectable enough. We've come to send him away in style.' Sandy points up at Hessian. Sandy:'No Eternal Damnations?' Hessian:'At least not on anybody important.' With a roll of her eyes, Sandy waves them in and tosses the grapes and the dog named Boris onto the pile. Xavier Kannon in his standard gray business suit and the white-dressed perky Ellie~! walk straight past, simply tossing Sandy a gift for The End, a frame to hang his retirement notice in. Moving into the door, they immediately duck out the side door and go upstairs to the bedrooms. Sandy:'.... Okay.' Once more upon the pile, Sandy greets the next visitor, Cianan. He brushes his hair back, wearing his black jeans and dark red button-up shirt. Sandy:'Hi, thanks for coming.' Cianan:'No problem. Have to pay respects.' Sandy:'He's retiring, not dying.' The RUSH wrestler shrugs his shoulders. Cianan:'Whatever. But he's another lover of Puroresu, so I had to be here. Sure, he's retiring in PCW, but we were in PURE together and that's what I'll remember him more for.' Voice:'There you are you son of a bitch!' Cianan turns around and gets hit in the face with a hard clenched fist. Cianan knocked to the ground, Jesse Ramey (wearing only jeans and cowhide cowboy boots) nods to Sandy while handing her a Sony Walkman and a Snickers bar for The End, then turns back to Cianan. Cianan is getting up, holding his hands out. Cianan:'Look, I know you're upset about that whole death thing, but that was a while back! Can't we put it behind us?' Ramey cocks his head to the side as Cianan offers his hand for a shake. Cianan:'I'll be the bigger man and offer the olive branch. Come on, man, don't do this here.' Alan Kriegman walks up in his black pants, blue shirt, and black tie. His attorney, Mr. Linden Young, is similarly dressed. Except that Kriegman has a Ms. America-esque sash that reads 'OSW Heel of the Year.' In fact, The Rolling Stones' 'Sympathy for the Devil' played him all the way up to the front of the line. Young is holding a pen and contract in his hands. Kriegman is pulling a wagon behind him. Sandy:'I'm surprised you're here.' Kriegman:'Don't be. I'm here to see that The End's retirement goes off nicely. And to reclaim what is mine! MWAHHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!' Kriegman points up into the air, laughing the evil supergenius laugh as Jesse Ramey grabs Cianan's hand and gives it a firm shake. Sandy questions Kriegman, who just stands there laughing. Sandy:'That being?' Kriegman:'Sandy, a piece of advice. Never EVER interrupt an evil supergenius while he is laughing! Now, where was I?' Young:'You had about five more seconds left.' Kriegman:'Ah, yes. HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAH!!!' Jesse Ramey pulls Cianan into a shortarm clothesline! Ramey:'So you still think you're better than me you little punk?!' Cianan on the ground, he rolls away before Ramey can drop the elbow on his throat. Kriegman:'Anyway, I've come to claim legal rights to the name of The End. I've been too busy to do anything about his blatant copyright infringement, but now that he's retiring I'm going to settle this once and for all and give my signature Cradle DDT the proper dignity and tilde that it deserves!' Sandy:'You couldn't have done that before?' Kriegman:'Some morons would have applied the tilde to your putz of a boyfriend, so I thought it safer to wait. Mr. Young here has the contract all ready, it just needs to be signed or X'ed or even if Skylar just licks the page, whatever.' Kriegman scratches his chin. Kriegman:'While we're at it, we'll dig up Michel Krieg and sue him for improper use of part of my last name. Could we sue Big Alan Steele while we at it?' Young:'Hell, with me as your lawyer, we could sue anybody who has your haircut or uses a Palm Pilot.' Kriegman:'Score!' Jesse Ramey tackles the apologetic Cianan and mounts his chest, throwing punches at his jaw. Cianan:'*Punch* Would you *punch* stop pummeling me?! I'm trying *punch* to apologize!' Ramey:'Shut up!' Sandy points to the wagon behind Kriegman. She knows it's there but doesn't know what's in it. Sandy:'What's that? Kriegman:'Oh, another gift.' Stepping off to the side, Alan Kriegman reveals his second gift (End may keep the pen), a headstone. Kriegman:'It's a tombstone for the death of his career. Oh, End, We Wish We Never Knew Ye.' Leaving the headstone but taking the contract inside to find The End, Kriegman and Young invite themselves in. Sandy wheels headstone to the reject pile and while she greets Nerezza, about 18 Skitzos separate Jesse Ramey from Cianan. Well, actually, it's one Skitzo and 17 floating tennis balls representing the other Skitzos. While the main Skitzo is standing by, the floating tennis balls attack Jesse Ramey, just knocking the wind out of him. Sandy is more focused on the fighting tennis balls than Inphino Blitz and Mina de Margo in front of her. Blitz is wearing his trenchcoat, buttoned up that the clothes under it aren't seen. Mina de Margo is dressed in tight black leather pants and a mesh T-shirt with red sports bra underneath. Sandy:'I'm sorry, thanks for coming.' Blitz:'No problem. We wouldn't miss the event. He is probably the only person who could cross the gorge and not start a war. We brought a Blitz-style trench for him.' Mina de Margo hands over the jacket and a black baby tee that reads BLITZ on the front and 'The Black Dragon Master... Has Spoken' on the back. Mina:'Here's something for yourself, too.' Sandy:'Thank you.' Putting the presents in the pile of gifts that is now Tempest's bean bag chair, Mani Shigeru's audio books, and the trench, and Bob Vurdo's box of cigars. Meanwhile, in the line, Nerezza tips his shades down and brushes some lint off of the right shoulder of his dark red tuxedo. Seeing the Tennis Ball Skitzos, he taps Skitzo on the shoulder... Nerezza:'Uh... What's with the tennis balls?' Skitzo looks back at the attacking tennis balls, which more just float stationary in the air, while Jesse Ramey seems to be suspended in the air, and only pretending to get hit. Skitzo:'Ah, the other 17 couldn't really make it tonight. So when this hits the airwaves, we'll have them electronically add the other Skitzos.' Nerezza: *Pretending to find this normal and/or interesting*'Ah...' Nerezza nods his head and then turns around. Vanilla Skillz: 'Sup, pasty cracka' foo'?' On instinct, Nerezza's reaction is to punch Vanilla Skillz in the nose. Skillz, dressed in a black vest and red parachute pants looks a lot like MC Hammer. The Mullet has on jeans and a faded Pantera T-shirt. Few people actually dressed up for this event. The Masked Mullet catches Skillz as he falls back. Mullet:'Hey!' Skillz:'Da fuck dat fo'?' Nerezza shrugs and turns around. Nerezza:'Sorry; thought you were Quo.' Skillz:'Quo stole my gimmick!' Duzza steps out of line in between Ivan Stanislav and Bubba McCoy. His platinum chains, baggy pants and shirt, his cornrowed hair... you'd think he was going to the Source Awards. He points at Vanilla Skillz and makes a comment in defense of Statuz Quo, who couldn't be in attendance. Duzza:'I KNEW it was a gimmick!' Skillz:'Like you got one? Clo'sess thing you had ta gimmick wuz Beat by Jamie Celt.' Duzza:'Can't ya'll stop usin' that insult me?' At this question, the entire line speaks up. Line of wrestlers:'NO!' Duzza shakes his head and tries to get back in front of Ivan Stanislav, but a growl from him makes Duzza step behind him. He tries to get in front of Bubba McCoy, who does much the same, and Duzza moves on. Next down the line, Magnum Wolf merely holds a gas cannister in Duzza's direction while licking his lips and flicking a lighter. Duzza just walks by. And in two minutes, he's at the end of the line. Back up front, Chet King, donned in brown Armani suit, gives Sandy Wong a few books of Burger King coupons. Chet:'He'll be poor soon so these will help to relieve the worry of food.' He then mumbles under his breath. Chet:'They helped me out tremendously during my injury.' Sandy:'What was that?' Coming back to his normal volume and tone, Chet pretends he didn't say anything. Chet:'Nothing, nothing.' Sandy looks at the coupons. Sandy:'Hey... these expired in May.' Chet:'It's not like he'll know.' Slapping her palm with her head, Sandy waves Chet King in. She tosses the coupon books onto the reject pile. Before she lets anybody else in, Sandy goes inside. Coming back out after a short while, she sets down a chair, which she stands on, and talks to the line through a bullhorn. Meanwhile, Minion the Imp is staring up the dress of Chris Matthews's date for the evening. No one knows who she is, not even Matthews. But groupies are easy enough to find. Minion's faces goes from delight to horror as he walks away. Minion:'Damn, that bulge better be hemrrhoids!' Sandy addresses everyone in line. Sandy:'Okay, people! Please listen. How many of you actually got The End a decent present?' All the hands go up. Sandy:'Okay, let's try this. How many of you got him a present that and you were sensitive to the fact that he's blind? How many gifts are not less-than-clever attempts at being pricks?' A few hands go up. Sandy points to the reject pile. Sandy:'Look at all of this. He can't use this because he can't see. Pictures? A slide projector? Binoculars? And the things like walking canes and seeing eye dogs should stop also.' Chris Rock tosses away the seeing-eye midget, a joke from his HBO special 'Bigger and Blacker' that he wanted to put into action. Drek Redaxe stands up, having been thrown away. He speaks in an aside to Chris Rock. Drek Redaxe:'I can't believe I'm out of his RPs for nearly three months only to get a cameo appearance like this...' Chris Rock:'Hey, man, I tried. Anyway, get outta here. I'll just give him the audio tapes of my Bigger and Blacker tour. Dusting off his bearskin tunic, Drek walks away. Angelo Deville's voice comes from the line of people. Deville:'What about dead bats in newspaper?' Sandy:'That's just sick. Please throw it away.' But a man walks by yanking the bat out of his hands. Tommy Dreamer:'I'll take that!' Sprinkling a little salt on the bat, he proceeds to eat it whole. Sandy: *Grimacing*'I really didn't need to see that. Anyway, if you have an honestly thoughtful gift for The End, please step off to the left to line up with the acceptable present pile. If you don't, stay in the reject pile line off to the right, and as you come in, please put...' Meanwhile, Magnum Wolf has snuck up to the front of the line. He pours the gasoline of the reject pile and sets it ablaze. Yes, he even the seeing eye greyhound, Boris. Smiling, he says 'My work here is done.' Then, Wolf walks into the ballroom to find The End. Sandy Wong just stands there for a second, looking at the fire, before going back to speaking to the lines. Sandy:'So if you don't have a good gift, just throw it onto the fire as you walk in. Skitzo, Mullet, Skillz, Pete, Abner, Bubba McCoy; thank you for bringing the proper gifts.' Stepping down, Sandy throws the chair and bullhorn onto the fire and just goes inside while the rest of the people file in. And so, the party gets underway. What happens inside stays inside. But you get to come in too. So join us next time as The End meets with his well-wishers (and otherwise), Rent-A-Hero starts up a Twister Challenge, and a bunch of midgets start a battle royale for the ages!
