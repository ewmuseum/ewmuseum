From the Diary of The HIT

26 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: From the Diary of The HIT Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: 10/26/02 1.......... 2................. 3! The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid has lost due to many things in his career. He has lost after being Red Scared by Ivan Stanislav. He has lost by being DQed after attacking his own partner. Heck, he's even lost after being attacked by Jason Alexander and the power of the ten piece bucket. However, he has NOT lost via crooked officiating. Despite the fact that the HIT makes his living off beating on people, and hitting them with ridiculously violent foreign objects, he has a definite sense of honor, ingrained in him as a teacher. It is evident Bob Charlie does not. As the Trapezoid looks ahead at this week's duties, he finds a stark contrast in officiating. One official shall be fair. And the other? He be all up in Trapezoid's grill, dawg! ---Dear Diary, October 28, 2002- On a flight to Rome, Italy-- It's me, again. I lost to Tim Walden on Friday......God, just saying those words makes me shudder violently. Although, to think about it, wasn't much else I could do. That idiot Bob Charlie counted Walden's shoulders down for the two, and then mine for the three. So, congratulations Timothy! You pinned my shoulders to the mat for a one count! Good for you! ......all joking aside, this is not funny in the least. Guess it's Charlie's payback for me laying him out during the 10/18 thing. Bob must have had the half a brain it took to realize I was the guy who left him laying. Damn Front Office stooge....least I don't have to deal with him for another week. Instead, the officials of my matches tell quite a bit about what's coming up. Buzz Meachem referees my match on Turmoil.....a fourway battle, with the #1 Contendership for the PCW Extreme Title on the line. The one title I've wanted more than anything.....for indeed, Hardcore is in my name. Still, I can't help but feel sad for the demise of Edward...I'd have liked to pay that bastard back for every time he beat me. Regardless of his victories though....he never faced me in an environment I excel at. Primetime Championship Wrestling hardcore is mere child's play compared to what I am used to. Memo to self: Introduce HIT-style hardcore as soon as is possible Anyway....I fight three guys on Tuesday, and a motley assortment they are. Bob Vurdo...leader of a Mafia crime family. Schitzo, the, well, schitzo, and Rich Rollins, a man who made a name for himself in the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. Two newcomers, one a former star here like myself. But, they all must realize that, in an extreme environment, none are better than The HIT. None. Should I win that matchup, I go on to face The End next week on Turmoil, for his title......but that's a whole other can of worms I'm going to open right now. Suffice it to say that Skylar hasn't been the best student a teacher could hope for... Yeah, but even if I do win on Tuesday, getting there's going to be a challenge. That prep bitch Eleanor Hall has me booked in a no holds barred handicap match against Duzza and Ali Khadafi. Duzza I know absolutely nothing about, but Ali, that's a different story. I know him from PURE, and he's one mean son of a bitch. He doesn't care about winning, losing......just hurting people. Physically and emotionally. Perfect hitman for that Scientological prick, Kannon. Kannon......the idiot who Hallmarked my tag team partner off the stage. That's likely a loss for a week or two at least....considering I was in the hospital overnight off a regulation style one. Xavier...if you read the warnings I gave you leading up to 10/18....I'm not after just Timothy. I'm after the entire Front Office. That means you, Duke, Brandi, Walden, hell, we may as well throw Bob Charlie and Jeff Marx on that list. The Front Office is the primary cancer for PCW....as the Red Army used to be. Funny how Marx always leeches his way onto the bandwagon, isn't it? Fact is, without guys like me, Clyde, and RAH to excise the tumor...the Front Office will continue to grow until we're all squished under mountains of taxes and Scientological edicts. But, one must take things a step at a time. Tuesday is of vital importance, and then Friday....is about survival. If I could triumph over the two angry black men, it would be a great feather in my cap...and I'm pretty sure I could despite the odds, but then you throw in the damned official, I.B. Black, and we virtually have a three on one handicap match. Even I can't say I like those odds. But, come on Kannon! You honestly think your punishment matches are going to stop me? You can beat me up....attack me, send people to do it for you......but The HIT will NOT stop coming! The HIT will NOT cease! My mission has been established. To destroy the control of the Front Office is my ultimate goal, and it SHALL be achieved! Tim Walden has a #1 contenders match for the World Title on Tuesday. I wish him all the luck in the world. Because, should he go to Alliances and defeat that hell-demon Clyde....he'll have a smiling Trapezoid knocking on his door for a title shot. And, I know he'll take me up on it. Why? Because....he wants desperately to beat me straight up. He fears me....he knows it, I know it. But for now, dreams of World Title glory shall be replaced by hopes of Extreme Title wars. For Vurdo, Schitzo, Rollins....and even the three African-Americans.....I'M GONNA BEAT YOU DOWN! RIGHT INTO THE GRASS! I'M GONNA SMACK YOU ROUND! I'M GONNA KICK YO' ASS! The HIT smiles, looking around him at the airplane, as he saves the document on his laptop, and shuts it off. Fade to pencils. ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
