Date: December/March Akiyama is a mysterious being… Most men only live one life…but the being known as Akira Akiyama has lived many. The first was the beginning…being born Akira Akiyama to Yoshihiro and Yukina Akiyama. They were two world-class martial artists…and they made sure their son followed suit. Akiyama’s youth was spent mastering Karate. He went to school, graduated with honors, made friends, and graduated from college and graduate school with a PhD in Psychology and Sociology… Just like any other kid. That’s when his first life ended. Despite his vast amount of knowledge, Akiyama could not shake off the fact that he was a physical person. Real hands on… so to speak. Let’s face it; by his graduation he was already 6’5 and 269 pounds of lean muscle. By the time he was finished with school completely he was 6’10, near 339 pounds. He took his knowledge of Karate and his size where he could use it… Bodyguarding. Hey, it was a decent living. Follow people around; make sure they don’t get hurt. Some days you get to shoot people too…that was a dream job. It was almost like the sadistic nature of Akiyama was beginning to brew at this point. He had a range of clients…from the rich and famous to the government to the underworld society as well. And thus his second life ends… There was a job one night…this real rich pair named Alexander James Maxis and Ryoko Godai. They were supposed to be this up and coming Yakuza clan, and they had trouble with both the law and a rival clan. They hired Akira Akiyama for a week’s worth of service, and during that run they’ve never seen such professional violence done. They were so impressed by the work Akiyama did that they give him a position within their company, Maxis Inc.: Director of Elite Forces Fancy word for leader of corporate killers Akiyama wasn’t always the roughneck that he is now…he used to be a corporate machine as well. If Kannon were to step against him at this portion of his life then there’d be nothing left of Hubbardville, let alone Xenu’s son. That’s how he used to do things. How he still does things… He got paired up with Gale and Fantasy, two top-notch guys that got mixed up in the business to make their lives better. Akiyama was just in for the principle, screw everyone else out of happiness, and forget money. That sadistic side grew stronger as the three of them did good work together…everything from wars the Japan Special Forces to rival Yakuza clans like the Void Elements…who Inphino Blitz was at the time, a member. Spent 5 years being a killer and a personal bodyguard and friend to the Maxis daughter, Hana. But then after completing somewhat seemed final then business with Inphino and the Void Elements one night…he found his work…complete. As well as his third life, which lead to the fourth. Unemployed, sitting in his apartment, Akiyama stumbled across a program that caught his eye… “Champ Forum”, a wrestling discussion show in Japan. The federation headlining…Tokyo Wrestling League. But that alone didn’t catch his eye… The fact that the entire Void Elements clan was there…aside from Inphino Blitz. To investigate, Akira enrolled into their training league, TWL Training grounds, where he quickly learned all he could from the various trainers; hardcore, aerial, mat technical wrestling, and advanced techniques. Heading into TWL he made his way to the top, earning belts along the way until finally he cam across the Darkcyde…leader of the Void Elements. It was monster against monster in the final bout, 6’10 339 Akira Akiyama, the battle hungry face against the 7’3 300 Darkcyde…the TWL champion at the time. They fought…and in the end Akiyama came out on top with the original form of The Darkness, Inverted Reverse Full Nelson Jumping Vertical DDT. And Akiyama became the TWL champion… But something happened after that. Akiyama came back after a week off differently. He seemed…colder, more violent. Then one show in a 14 man stable elimination brawl between Akiyama’s assembled team against the Void Elements…tragedy struck. Akiyama betrayed his other 6 allies and joined the Void Elements. The war continued on…and in the end Akiyama and the entire Void Elements were banished from TWL. They had located in Inphino in a top fed…PCW. They failed in all attempts to sway him back into the darkness…so they let Akiyama do what he does best… …eliminate the problem… With Inphino lured back to Japan with false claims of his manager’s death, the Void Elements beat him to near death and threw him in the sea. Efficiently…meanwhile Akiyama was in PCW laying the story on thick for the fools. The training began…and he soon learned the ways… Of being a god… Life #5… This life went well, Akiyama learning to adapt to his superior abilities that seemed to unearth overnight. Void Banishment…Void Trance…resistance to death…electrocution…many abilities were gained. But then he found out that he was merely a pawn in the Element’s game of domination…when he found out he was being drugged. He left behind his godhood and started over with no memory of his life afterwards. He was found by Botan Otonachi, and he and the bounty hunder started a Yakuza clan of their own… The sixth life, the familiar life of crime again. It lasted for about three months, then everything returned to Akiyama. What he really was…his past…his godhood. He regained his fifth life, but it became the seventh. He was the nVg once more…but he was without the Void Elements this time. He was on his own, looking to dominate the world by himself. He established himself as a dark master, banishing his foes to the Void and destroying all who crossed him. this is when the man became them…he became WE…and thought it was never clearly explained why…people just assumed he had mental problems. But then in a flash of light, Akiyama disappeared. He was gone for months…until he returned…but not alone. Akiyama returned mute, at his side his ambassador to humanity, his Divine Prophetess, Cyclonica. She was his voice, as he was now above addressing mortals directly. Akiyama with Cyclonica at his side dominated the tag division alone, and afterwards went on to destroy names like El Diablo, Pharaoh, and Chef. He was a Celestial now…the 8TH life. Superiority was the way of the Celestial…everyone above the weak masses. Akiyama was not alone in his elite ways…Cyclonica and Anarchy were the first, bearing the black blood and being Akiyama’s birth daughters. The End, Skylar Green, was the next, a half-breed of both race and species. They all bonded, and became a family. Together Akiyama managed to force his way back into Japan as a citizen, and destroyed many a foe in their paths. Cyclonica and Anarchy both cruiserweight champions…The End Extreme champ…all with help from their god. But since then…Alliances…things weren’t the same. The betrayal of a father by his own daughter…drugging him and locking him away in a castle like an animal to be forgotten about. But he’s returned... Back from the dead they say... A slave not to pain...not to family...not to company...not to blood... A free man, left to do what he wills upon anyone. Ready to start up a new life… Life…Death… R E B I R T H
