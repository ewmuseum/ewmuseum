Matt Land, Part 9- Fixing Things

17 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/17/03 Matt walks slowly into Haley’s small bedroom, located in her tiny apartment in LA. She is asleep on the queen-sized bed that stands in front of him, wrapped tightly in the white comforter, her head rests on a white pillow and her blonde hair is spread out across it. Next to the bed is an oak nightstand, with a small lamp and a digital alarm clock. Behind him stands a vanity dresser, with many drawers and make-up items that are all tossed around the glass top. Walking past it Matt smiles revealing his white teeth, noticing just how unorganized Haley is, but it doesn’t matter because to him she is perfect. Taking a few steps across the polished wood floor, he inches closer to the bed where she is sleeping. His brown eyes sparkle with a renewed vigor, knowing that he is going to stop Jason Hilton. At the foot of the bed rests an Abercrombie and Fitch, it is empty as the contents of the bag are on Matt’s body. It is a nice change from his usual overdressed state. The blue tee shirt and khaki shorts seem to allow him some comfort that he normally doesn’t have in his suits. Matt continues to walk toward the bed, with his confident swagger and a look of joy on his slender face. Reaching his destination, he stops and goes down to one knee; carefully he places his elbows on the bed and allows his barrel of a chest to come to rest on the side. His arms buckle under the newfound weight of his body, but he doesn’t seem to notice. His mind is focused on the young beauty that is asleep before him, her breathing slowly rising up the silk sheets, only to let them fall back down on her chest once again. Quietly he stands back up to his full 5’8” height and pulls a black cell phone out of his pocket. Flipping up the top he dials a number and waits for the voice on the other end of the phone to pick up. “Hello,” the voice on the other end bellows out. Matt waits a second, running his hands through his brown hair; feeling the gel as the little spikes poke his hands. Pushing the phone more closely to his ear, he rubs his hands together to get the gel off of them and takes a deep breath. “Are you guys doing everything you can to beat this man?” Matt asks. His voice with wrought with anger and the pleasant look that encompassed his face is now gone, giving way to a more stone-faced look. The only thing that remains is the smirk that is across his curled lips. His eyes are still locked on Haley, unmoving and cold. Even she can’t take away his hatred for her father and he is going to make sure that Jason never interferes in his personal business again. “Of course Matt, I think you know just as well as anyone I am doing everything in my power. We have been friends forever and I know how much this means to you. Trust me when I say that we won’t lose,” the voice triumphantly responds back. “The man is a professional Mitch. He is probably in every law book you have in your office. Jason Hil-“ He is quickly cut off. “Jason Hilton is a master of anti-trust law Matt. This isn’t anti-trust and believe me when I say this. Jason Hilton has no case against you guys. Haley is over the legal age and she doesn’t side with him,” Mitch says. Hearing these words brings Matt’s smile back to its full force and slows his methodical pace down. “That is what I needed to hear Mitch. The only other question I have is about my past. How deep is he going to be able to get?” “From everything you have told me it is to hard to touch. Even if he has favours, he still has to go through members on Capitol Hill and I highly doubt they will give him clearance to documents of that nature. Just because he wants to keep his daughter away from you.” “Well then is this all going to be cleared up before Spring Fling?” Matt asks. Knowing the answer before he gets it, but it seems like everything else is going his way. “I pushed as hard as I could to get the court date moved up to this week, but he was able to hold off on it. Don’t worry though, you might as well forget about this whole thing. I am handling everything and you two will never have to see the inside of a courtroom,” Mitch boasts, trying to show what a great lawyer he is and how perfectly he is handling the case.Without another word, Matt flips the phone shut and slides it back into his pocket. “What a god damn con artist,” Matt says aloud. Mitch Blesly is probably the best defense attorney in the nation and he has been special council to the CIA on countless occasions. He would get anyone out of any predicament they were in, no matter how unethical of a road he took and Matt knew this and it was the sole reason he brought him on board. “Who was that?” Haley asks, sitting up and rubbing the sleep out of her pale blue eyes. Pushing the sheets off of her slender body, she stretches causing wrinkles to form all over her silk nightgown. Tilting her head to the side, she gives Matt a smile and falls back on the bed. “It was just Mitch. I was checking up on how the case was going,” he tells her, shooting a big smile back her way. Just seeing her pink lips turned into a smile, makes him smile bigger then he ever has before. Haley is the only person who can turn Matt into a relaxed person, like a sword to its sheath, she holds him back and only allows him to strike when humanly necessary. “We don’t have anything to worry about. My dad knows that and that is why he is trying to force this court case. We are going to bury him, but lets not worry about that because right now we have other things to worry about.” “That is part of the reason I came here Haley,” Matt starts, his voice changing to a more serious note and the smile on his face starts to fade. He walks back over to the bed and sits down on the edge. Taking her hand in his, he grips it tightly and rubs it with his free hand. “I know all about Spring Fling and what we have to do you don’t have to worry,” she tells him. “I know Haley, but I am worried because this is big and over the past few weeks you have just been getting into your groove. Now is the time I need you though, we need to crush those bitches once and for all. I am sick of dealing with them and we are going to prove our point!” “I know, you don’t think I don’t want revenge as much as you? That rich posh beat me with a shotgun and caused a concussion. I am not going to let anything stand in the way of us winning this Matt. Don’t worry.” “That is what I like to hear Haley. I have some business to take care of at the agency, but when I am done with that I will come back.” He gets up and turns on his heels before making his way out the door.
