Wilderness

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27.05.03 There's nothing like the Great Outdoors. The wilderness brings to Abner the touch of sense that whiskey brings to the heart of an Irishmen. Not the drunken, unbreakable addiction of a harmful substance, but a genuine love and primal affliction for such a beautiful, natural thing. Camping out in the depths of the great Vermont countryside, Abner feels more comfortable per se, and more happy with this particular feeling, than even the feeling of “being at home'. He is home. His eyes brighten, his knees tremble and his palms sweat. This is the feeling that nature brings to him. But this is the feeling that it brings to anyone with a taste for themselves, and not merely a 'fad' for rednecks, hicks and hillbilly's alike. As a matter of fact, Abner never really considered himself a hillbilly. For example, as a younger child, sitting on his mother's knee as she sat suing on the family porch, peering out into the bushes, Abner always felt a kind of fear for the woods. Timid. He never really 'loved' the woods as much as say the more useful, practical “redneck” things : taking apart engines, rigging chainsaws and getting his hands greasy. As a very non-conformist 'enviro' type, he despised as much as in protection for what he considered to be a human resource. This view of nature came from an upbringing of fear at home and fear abroad. In fear as a child, it grew to contempt, and bam! Snuck up on him as a teen as in the parties he went to, he'd openly display a burning of rubber tires in front of some of his hippies neighbors, just to get a rise. But that was then, and this is now. As with hicks, pricks and dicks alike (categories in which Abner claims he can fit any individual) a love of nature is not uncommon. And so here he was, out in the middle of nowhere, miles from any form of civilization. And it was great. He loved it. Abner: Hey, Cuzin Ed! Cuzin Ed: Yep? Abner: Throw another log on the fire, will ya? Obediently, the younger redneck nods his head and gets up from his seat. His short, husky frame (not even six-foot, but well over 270 pounds) stands in contras to Abner’s muscular body, and yet, he’s the least lazy of the two, always doing Abner’s work. He tosses a thick, bulky stack of sticks into the fire and then quickly takes his seat. Also in contrast to Abner, Cuzin Ed, though a redneck on the outside, is a softie in heart. Abner: Heh heh heh, Cuz! For no apparent reason, Abner begins to laugh. Abner: Just look at you!!! Cuzin Ed (in a somewhat offended tone): What!? Looking down at the rolls of fat seemingly sliding down the front of his shirt, Cuzin Ed breaks into a smile and him and Abner share a good laugh. Cuzin Ed: Yep, I sure am one fat motherfucker! Abner: That you are ah Cuz, that you are… Abner and Ed: Ahahahahahahaha! Cuzin Ed takes his tired tan-colored hand and wipes the sweat off from the top of his shiny bald head, taking in deep, heavy breaths. He reaches down and rolls up the bottom of his tight blue jeans, and wipes off a thick glob of mud. They’ve had a rough day, hunting and fishing and such. Cuzin Ed (yawning): Well I’m bored, Cuz. Abner (snickering): Heh, we ain’t outta beers yet! Cuzin Ed: Yeah, so? Abner: So shut yer mouth! Cuzin Ed: But... Abner: Eh! (Abner puts up a hand) Shut up and take another swig of this shit…this should get yer ass goin’! Abner pulls out a new bottle of whiskey and uncapping it, tosses it over to Ed. Cuzin Ed: BAH! Abner (equally as forceful): Drink it! Shrugging, Cuzin Ed tips back the bottle and takes a long, purposeful sip. He cocks his head toward Abner, and after careful consideration his mind is made up and a smile of satisfaction comes over his face. Abner: Like it? Cuzin Ed: Yeah. Abner: Good…now shut up and drink!!! Cuzin Ed: Don’t mind if I do… Much obliged, he takes another sip before passing it on to Abner. Abner: What, do I look like a drunk to you? Cuzin Ed (smiling): No...you look like family. After sharing another laugh at their distasteful joke, they move onto more important matters. Abner slowly takes off the old green “John Deere” hat sitting on the top of his head, and carefully sets it on the log behind him. He undoes the bottom few buttons of his hunting shirt, exposing his beer-belly. He folds his hands behind his head and snuggles up against a soft spot in the log. Closing his eyes, he begins to think of the future. Cuzin Ed realizes what’s going on, and follows Abner’s lead. He rips off his blue and black hunting jacket, and uses it as a pillow, folding it up and placing it behind his head. He undoes the top of his laces, and slowly letting the boots slide off his feet and onto the ground, he too leans back and closes his eyes. He tries to comfort his cousin. Cuzin Ed: Well Ab, we all have that a few times in our lives…ya know, shit happens. We all have that... Abner (sitting up straight): What, Mexican janitors? Cuzin Ed: Yeah… Abner cocks his head to the side. Abner (squinting his eyes): Riiiiiiggghhtt....(whispering now) Note to self: keep Cuzin Ed off the whiskey…well, (having second thoughts) go lightly on it, anyway. Cuzin Ed: Well I think you got a chance, Ab. Abner: What, to win the title. Cuzin Ed: No, to become a janitor yerself… Abner: … Cuzin Ed: …Just kidding, just kidding! They share another laugh before Abner takes a more serious tone. Abner: Yeah Ed, you’re probably right…with my luck, Cuz, I’ll be outta work in no time…again…and out on the streets…again. Cuzin Ed: Oh, Ab, it ain’t all that bad! ‘Sides, if you ever did leave PCW…God forbid…I’m sure you could make it just about anywhere you wanted, I mean, a fine young gentlemen like you? No problem. Abner: Seriously though, this whole career…this whole wrastlin’ thing…it’s really startin’ to grow on me. And ya know, with my first match and all bein’ for the hardcore title and all that, feels like them new VP’s they got there put a lot of trust into me. And well, you know that I’m not one to impress…I don’t kiss ass, I don’t ask for no respect…and I certainly ain’t gonna get bossed ‘round by nobody…but these folks, well, I don’t’ want to let them down, see? Cuzin Ed: Yeah, I get it…you’ve gone soft! Abner: Ha! Unlikely, more like…like I’ve gone HARDCORE! Cuzin Ed: Yeah, Hardcore brown-noser! Abner folds his arms across his chest and raises his brows. Cuzin Ed outs his hands up in defense. Cuzin Ed (laughing nervously): Hehehe…I, I was just kiddin’ Ab, don’t get all bent out of shape. I mean, I knows how ya’re…and I know where you stand. And that’s what you do: you stand…you stand like a man. You are a man. Abner: Yeah, I’m a man alright…but I guess I’ll prove that in time. Cuzin Ed: And I’ve no doubt ya will, buddy. Abner: Heh…nor do I. Thing is, it’s kind of hard to claim to be a man if ya get yer ass whipped by a two-foot, two-pound Mexican janitor at yer debut! Cuzin Ed: Which is exactly why it ain’t gonna happen. You’ll beat him, Ab, you’ll beat ‘em.. You’ll whoop him good, I’m sure. Abner: Well, either way…win or lose… He turns around to garb something… Abner: ….I’ll always have you, my love. Abner gives the can of Budweiser a kiss. And so it goes. Two nights after his return to PCW, and one night before his debut, the mighty Abner works it over in his mind; works over his life, in his mind. Churning, turning, winding…and the more it makes sense, the more confusing it gets. He hated the old Front Office, and yet, he likes this one. He’s not a people person, and yet, he loves the fans. He’s not patriotic, but yet, he loves and supports his country’s troops. He’s just a redneck, and yet, he’s much, much more. Why does the Front Office, the VP’s, trust him so much? Why do people have so much faith in him? If he wins the hardcore title from Labor on Turmoil, then what comes next? These questions, all these good, solid questions, must be answered…but how long will it take to answer them, and where? Maybe THAT is the most important question. But Abner, he hasn’t got the motivation that the rest of the roster seems to have. He’s by no means lazy-not your stereotypical country bumpkin-but he has no work-rate, and even fewer work ethics. With the PCW Air Cruise coming up shortly, will Abner go up with the ship, taking himself and his company for a ride, pushing the limits, soaring above the rest, finding new horizons…or will he drop like a brick? Only time can tell. Only time. Or will he lose himself in the wilderness?
