Three to Tango

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30th June 2002 Every seen a film called Three To Tango? Great film. Neve Campbell and Matthew Perry give some good witty performances, and it’s also got that bloke with the beard from the Three Musketeers and Ready to Rumble in it. That bloke gets everywhere it seems. And this does have something to do with this promo... it’s not like I’m bulking this out by putting my top twenty mark out list in the middle or something... We return to what is being a very long interlude from the pope chronicles. Although it’s not exactly a return since after all, we haven’t been to this rather nice restaurant before. You know there is a simple equation that goes all throughout wife... and all you girls had best listen up, because this is true. The more expensive a restaurant a man takes you to, the more he either wants to have sex with you, or the more he is feeling really guilty about something. If he then purchases you flowers and a box of chocolates, then he’s been unfaithful. So keep your eyes peeled for that one. The married couple sit at a table, neither of them really dressed for it... in fact if it wasn’t for Miyagawa being a well known celebrity, they would never have been admitted to what appears to be a suit and tie only restaurant. On the other hand, because he is, he gets away with wearing a loose filling purple shirt and a pair of black trousers, while Natalia (with a much better excuse, being pregnant after all) gets away with well... a dress. Ok, she doesn’t get away with anything since this is the normal thing for women to wear. Although the leather jacket slung over the back of her chair is a little out of place, but no one seems too concerned with it. They both seem to be enjoying their starters which appear to be a couple of bowls of prawn cocktail... well, a couple of bowls for Natalia and she seems to be eating it with Barbeque Sauce as well... Miyagawa suddenly fishes a large lump out of the bowl and waves over a waiter. He hands half an avocado covered with thousand island dressed to the waiter who holds it, wondering what exactly to do with it. Miyagawa continues to eat and then looks back at the waiter, wondering why the chap is still standing there. Miyagawa: Erm... you can take that away now, you know? I don’t particularly like avocado... in fact I asked for no avocado in the prawn cocktail... lettuce would have sufficed... but I won’t complain, it’s not your fault. Waiter: Yes sir. The waiter scurries away, presumably to dispose of the avocado and then to wash his hands completely... and then more than likely to spit on Miyagawa’s steak for making him carry a soggy half avocado through the restaurant. Miyagawa leans slightly across the table and reaches out with his hand. Miyagawa: How’s the food? Natalia ignores him for a few seconds as she wolfs down her half avocado before preparing to start on her second starter. She wipes her mouth with the napkin and pushes the first plate to the side of the table before reaching out herself and holds Miyagawa’s hand wither her own in a gesture of togetherness. Natalia: Not bad... although it won’t be too long until I’m no longer eating for two. The due date is in five weeks, Alex. Have you booked the time off yet? Miyagawa goes quiet for a few seconds and Natalia looks a little annoyed. Natalia: You haven’t have you? How long have I been pregnant now? How long have you know that the baby... our baby is due towards the middle to end of August? Miyagawa: Since the start of the ye... Natalia (cutting Miyagawa off mid-word and looking a little distressed): Too god damned long, that’s how long. You still haven’t asked Tyler Nelson about getting the time off have you? What about asking Duke Williams, I’m sure he’ll ok it. He’s the PCW President, I’m sure he’ll have some sort of pull in the business. Miyagawa (trying to calm her down quickly): You know I have a match with him on Sunday. I’ll ask him when we film Turmoil on Tuesday. Seeing as you’re in state, you might as well come along as well. We can ask him together before the show starts. Ok? Natalia (calming down greatly): Ok. It’s just that there’s not long to go and there’s still things to prepare and well... you know me... I panic and get carried away and try to do everything at once. Miyagawa: I know, but everything will be ok, I promise. I won’t anything bad happen to you, me or our baby. Natalia smirks for a second; which Miyagawa notices. Miyagawa: What? Natalia: You didn’t say our baby boy. Maybe you’re starting to come to terms with the fact that we’re going to have a girl. Miyagawa laughs out loud and waves a finger at Natalia who laughs along with him, and the rather upper crusty other members of the restaurant look at the duo wearing casual clothing laughing happily while in their restaurant. Miyagawa: I’m not getting into that argument with you again, and you know it. I may look stupid, but no one catches me out quite that easily... **looks at Natalia looking in a way as if to say “yeah, right”** Ok. They don’t catch me out twice. Natalia: Just be glad you added that second part there, otherwise I would have had to make some sort of witty comment on it. Miyagawa (smirking): How was the flight anyway, and why didn’t you tell me you were coming? Natalia: If I had told you then it wouldn’t have been a surprise, would it? And the flight was fine. It’s amazing, you become pregnant and suddenly airline food tastes much better. The chicken sandwich I had on the plane actually tasted vaguely of chicken... probably means it was supposed to have tasted of beef. Miyagawa laughs happily. Miyagawa: Well I’m glad you came. We don’t get to spend nearly as much time together as I like... which is why I’ve come to a decision... I know I’ve been attending every taping regardless of if I’m on the card or not, and I think that has to stop. Unless I’m actually booked on the card, I’m going to stay home with you... and our baby... Natalia: That’s just what I wanted to hear... Miyagawa: **cough* Boy *cough** Natalia: Hey! No fair! Natalia flicks a well sized portion of her remaining prawn cocktail with a fork at Alexander, but it flies over his head and splatters right into the back of the neck of some posh dude wearing a tux in the next table over. Natalia’s eyes go wide with surprise but both of them begin to laugh when they see what has happened. Miyagawa: Let’s get out of here. Natalia: I’d like that. And with their Three to Tango style exit, Alexander and Natalia leave the posh nosh restaurant before they get thrown out of it... probably to find somewhere that is a little bit more... “them”.
