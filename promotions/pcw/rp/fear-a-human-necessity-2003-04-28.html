Fear: A Human Necessity

28 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-28-03 Could two men with no remorse almost kill a man and be sorry? But what if the man could not be killed? What if the man that was the victim of this vicious act was none other than “A living Nightmare”? Would you be sorry or would your own fear cloud your remorse? Fear is a human necessity, without fear we would be as great as the Gods. We would fear no man, no beast and no monster. This is not a perfect world and without a doubt we are not Gods and in the end, Fear over clouds all. ”Do you think we should have done that”? A voice echoes in the night like an epidemic spreading throughout a village with a sudden shock. Two men are hunched over breathing heavily, one with a look of ease and deliberation and the other with a look of concern and fright. Many different thoughts run through ones head when they are fleeing for there life, Will I Live? Where will I go? How will it feel? The mix of emotions is enough to drive a man crazy and before you know it, it’s all over. The men from which we speak are John Gargano and Tyler Preston, the team of Entertainment Extreme or E2 which they have come to be known. Fear is a funny thing, it can spread like a dangerous illness, hitting one person then the next then the next and so on and so forth. This illness known as fear has already hit one person, one man that from now on will never be the same. E2 are in the back of what it seems to be a dark and dreadful alley, probably the last place you would want to go if you were hiding from a Vampire, but something draws these men to this spot, whether it is destiny or it is just plain exhaustion, it pulls them in to a place that will change there lives forever. ”Do you think we should have done that”! Tyler responds with a rabid and quick response, almost mocking his tag team partner. John then goes up to the face of Tyler and stares at him with a questioning grin, almost surprised at the response he got from the golden boy who is normally very laid back and care free seeing as he is a comedian. The clouds begin to stir and make a purple and blue haze in the sky on this dark and gloomy night. Both men beginning to get the dangerous illness, the sickness that is feared by all warriors and feared by all men in general, Fear. Could this be a dream? Or is it reality that is consuming your heart. Is this a dark nightmare full of memories and bad decisions, full of your worst fears and your deepest and darkest regrets? Only your heart can tell. - CRASH - A loud crash is heard throughout the alley as both men turn in utter shock of the situation they are currently in and they think about the horror that that noise may contain. Without a word both men dash out of the alley like two cockroaches scattering away from there tormentors, they make a left turn, a right turn and another left but soon they lose there sense of direction, both men with fear in there eyes try to find there way but the dark alley and there fear consumes them and soon both of them are……alone. ”Tyler? ...............Where are you”! A voice slithers from the depths of the empty dark alley, Unknown be its beholder. We’re only led to believe it is John Gargano alone in the dark, standing there, the fear eating away at him like a deadly cancer decaying his organs and consuming his very mind, his very heart, his very soul. Soon footsteps are heard throughout the alley, loud uneasy footsteps which pitter and patter back and forth. What could this be? Is a question that comes to John’s very mind in this situation, Courage, Heart, Strength can not over come this as it is like when you are a very small boy and it’s your bedtime and you just finished watching your first horror movie, you do not know what awaits you, your parents say it’s just a movie but in the end your mind begins to play devious tricks on you and fear overcomes your common sense. John begins to back step feeling around for something to give him comfort, something that will make his heart whole, something that can save himself from his very own mind. The pitter patter of the footsteps grow louder and faster, it is still pitch-black, it is just John and what he hopes to be his mind playing tricks on him. In that moment it becomes silent, the footsteps stop and John is still alone, still full of fear and still wondering, wondering a question that irks the world, was that just my mind or was that something else? ”Hello?” A familiar voice rings out from the darkness, Soon the footsteps start again but this time John is assured to there beholder, John’s only friend in the world at that moment appears from the darkness and he could not be happier to see him. ”Is that you Ty?” John responds in a boyish and hopefully expecting tone, soon from the dusk a figure arouses, John leaps up to see that it is Tyler Preston. Both men with the same look in there eyes stare at each other for a minute a minute that seems like an eternity. Both men without a word turn away from each other and walk into the darkness, overcoming there fear of the Tag Team Champions and accepting there responsibilities for there actions. Fear is a human necessity, everyone has it. Can two men overcome there fear of the dark? Or will it eat them alive? In the end one thing is known and that is only time will tell if it is possible.
