The Hunted Saga Part 1: In The Beginning...

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/3/02 Hunted Saga Part One: In the beginning… The camera opens up in very murky surroundings, in some kind of foggy woodland area, where it is pouring down with rain. The lens of the camera is nothing more than a blur, as the camera pans to the left to see, shiny grey, stainless steel wire mesh. At the top of the wire mesh, barbed wire keeps people in and keeps people out, thereby suggesting that whatever is on other side is both important and dangerous. Beyond the stainless steel wire mesh fencing we can just make out buildings, which do not specifically mark out what exactly is on the other side of the fencing. Then some movement can be see in a distant blur, the camera stays transfixed as the blur moves closer and closer toward the camera. The figure moves towards the fence and begins climb up the stainless steel wire mesh and we can plainly see that it is a man scaling the fence with great ease. The man hesitates slightly as he gets to the barbed wire at the top of the fence, but over comes it by moving about a meter to his left and pulling himself up a dividing post, thereby diverting himself from the probable harm that the barbed wire threatened. The figure elegantly jumps from the dividing post and lands equally elegantly lands on the other side of the fence. The figure looks around first to his left, then to his right and finally back the way he came. The figure seems to stop a little, seemingly to contemplate their next move. The figure then begins to run further and further into the woodland overgrowth. The camera follows the figure as it continues through the undergrowth, it passes a gap in the trees and the moonlight shows us the familiar face of a very square jawed man, with short brown hair. His name Dave J. Constantinople. Now he realised that he would be considered by the US military as an AWOL officer, but this didn’t much bother him. He also realised that the army would try to hunt him down, he didn’t care about that either. All that concerned him now was the need to escape the mighty grasp of the US military. It was only last night that the white coats, the scientists even, had chosen him for the experiment. He was frog marched from his bunker to the science lab on the other side of the military base, where they informed him of the experiment. Dave realised that they wouldn’t be telling him all of the confidential details to the experiment unless they intended him to take part. The scientist laid it out clearly for him, a serum had been developed that had the potential to transform a normal human being into some kind of super soldier. They conducted a probability test throughout the entire US Military and they figured that his DNA stood the best chance of surviving the transformation and when the serum took effect further tests could be completed to find a more consistent serum. Dave didn’t like the idea, but pretended to like it. As soon as the scientists told him the specific details, Dave knew that this place was no longer safe for him. His morals prevented him from accepting this mission, for he knew that if the experiment was successful it would increase the U.S.A’s might, tempting them to cause chaos and start wars. Dave would be in the center of it all and he silently concluded that this experiment would not be conducted upon him. He wouldn’t be used as a weapon to cause mayhem and would not be experimented upon. He silently decided that he would attempt to escape that night. The experimentation mission was but the last straw for Dave, it was a culmination of things that tempted him to AWOL, the army tested his morals too much. Over the last ten years of his service he had been made to do unspeakable things that he dared not recall. It was time to break free from the army and now was the time. Dave suspected that his plan was a good one. He had pretended to be really enthusiastic about the whole idea in front of his superiors and the scientists, thereby giving them no need to put him under any special surveillance. Getting out of his bunker and getting past the patrol guards was no trouble at all and scaling the perimeter fence was even less trouble. He also suspected that his superiors would not realise he was gone until the morning, giving him plenty of time to get a far enough head start to at least put them off his trail for a couple of weeks. By that time he would have set himself up a new identity and a new life. Dave was almost enthusiastic about the thought of his new life, he was looking forward to it. Dave runs past the final signs of foliage into a concrete road. Dave also spotted some car lights in the distance moving ever closer. Dave walked slowly backwards into the bushes. The vehicle was a truck and wasn’t going very fast, Dave took the opportunity and ran after the truck as it went past, diving into the bag. Dave found a blanket and concealed himself beneath it. Dave felt that it was very trying indeed, to stand the smell of the truck. The camera momentarily fades to black and very quickly opens on a busy street in central Chicago, Illinois. It had been over a week since Dave had escaped from the Militaries might and he actually thought that he was doing well for himself. With the little cash that he had, he had booked himself into a run down hotel near the city center. He had also procured a very low paid job cleaning toilets in some damn burger restaurant. It was much but it put food on the table, or at least it would if he had a table. He had even created himself a new identity, he chose the name John Crichton and had changed the style of his hair in a vain attempt to conceal himself from the military. John Crichton walks from around the corner of a building into shot and into the new street. He stops at the fourth building across, opens the door and enters the establishment. The camera cuts to inside the building to see Crichton enter. The camera pans around and the scenery suggests that we are in some kind of cheap tacky ‘60’s dinner. In fact Crichton had become quite accustom to the place, especially with the very attractive waitress that always worked there. Crichton had got her name off the manager, it was Aeryn, Aeryn Sun. Crichton liked her a lot, but he didn’t think that he stood a chance, plus he wasn’t quite sure whether he was willing to bring someone else into his piss poor life. Crichton walked further into the diner and sits on his favourite bar stool. Crichton felt his heart beat faster as Aeryn herself came over to him to take his order. Aeryn looked at the man in interest. She had noticed that he came in here a little often for his own good, hell she’d tasted the food herself. The manager had told her that the man’s name was John Crichton and she figured that he was here for more than the food. Her manager reckoned that it was her he was after, but she didn’t think she stood a chance with him. At every opportunity nevertheless she always was the one who served him. “Hey John, can I take your order?” Aeryn inquired. Crichton blinked in disbelief. Not only did she know his name she used it. Within a couple of seconds of entering the establishment, his hopes had exceeded beyond all expectations. “Erm… yeah I’ll have the toasted cheese sandwich and some tea please.” Crichton replied Aeryn took note of Crichton’s blue eyes. She almost got lost staring at them. She composed herself quickly. “Sure I’ll be right back.” Aeryn said putting her very best flirty smile upon her face. Crichton noticed her smile and couldn’t stop thinking about it. Impossible as it sounds she was three times more attractive when she smiled and Crichton thought that she was quite attractive anyhow. Crichton’s eyes follow her around the restaurant as she tears off the piece of paper, containing his order and handing it over to the cook. She then walks over to a table and begins to clear up some plates. Then the diner door swings open to reveal some guy with a obvious mentally disturbed look upon his face. Crichton eyed his warily as he walks past and sits upon a stool at the other end of the counter. Aeryn walks past the man and grabs her by the arm. “Hey Honey how’s about a little service? Eh?” The man questions. Aeryn swiftly slaps the man’s hand away from her. “I’ll be with you in a minute sir.” Aeryn warns with a stern voice. With that the man rises from his seat and blocks Aeryn’s path. “You will deal with me now for I am CORNOHOLIO, the ruler of the world.” The man states, much to everybody’s disbelief. Crichton rises, feeling that he can take no more of this. He walks over to the man and taps him on the shoulder. “Hey man, why don’t you switch to decaff and leave the lady alone.” Crichton demands with all of the bravado that he can muster. The man slowly turns around to face him. “Oh yeah, you’re a mere mortal, what can you do to me?” The man questions with all seriousness. “Well…”, Crichton begins throwing the man off guard as he follows in with a punch aimed directly at the man’s chin. The blow connects sending the man reeling backwards. The man stops himself and holds himself up on the counter. The man feels around his jaw a little before looking up at Crichton with pure fire in his eyes. The man lashes out with a punch, but Crichton blocks with one hand and follows through with a forearm to the side of the mans face, sending the man ass over head, over the counter. Crichton lets of a giggle at the man’s humorous fall. Crichton turns to Aeryn and all macho like asks “Are you okay?” Aeryn looked up into those deep blue eyes again and decided there and then that she would at least have a go with Crichton, she owed it herself. After all hadn’t Crichton already proved that he liked her, other wise he wouldn’t have stepped in to help. “Erm…Crichton can we have a talk?” Aeryn blurted out in front of everyone. Crichton took a step back and guided her onto one of the stools near the counter. “Yeah sure. What’s up?” Crichton replied concerned. Aeryn took a deep breath and decided to just do it. No regrets. “Wanna come out for a drink tomorrow?” She asked with an hopeful look upon her face. Crichton’s face broke out into a smile, he couldn’t control it anymore. “Hell yeah…” Crichton began “Awww hell I got work tomorrow.” “Really where do you work?” Aeryn questioned. “Cleaning toilets in some grungy fast food joint.” Crichton replied a little ashamed, but he figured she was gonna find out anyhow so what the hell. “That’s bad news. Ya know, I could get you a job with my dad.” Aeryn offered. “Really? What does he do?” Crichton inquired “He’s a wrestling promoter. He runs his own promotion and he has his own gym.” Aeryn answered confidently. “Do you think he’d teach me to wrestle?” Crichton questioned “Hell yeah that’s what I was damn well taking about.” Aeryn replied. “Great. Now I can come for that drink with you tomorrow.” Crichton stated happily. “Yeah. Be at the gym at 9am sharp. Be prepared to get your ass kicked.” Aeryn replied with a smile. “Thanks.” Crichton simply said as he gets up from the stool, kisses Aeryn on the cheek and leaves the eating establishment. Crichton summarised that this wouldn’t be a bad day after all. All’s he had to do now was quit his other job. Crichton couldn’t wait to tell the sixteen year old little pimple faced bastard where to shove his job. The camera fades to black.
