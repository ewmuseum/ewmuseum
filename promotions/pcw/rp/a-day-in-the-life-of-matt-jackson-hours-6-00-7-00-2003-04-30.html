A Day In The Life Of Matt Jackson, Hours 6:00-7:00

30 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/30/03 Turmoil took a lot out of me physically and now I am double booked for the first time ever. Three cards back to back, is surely going to take its toile on my body…break me. Not for the fact that I am not capable of handling the pressure and the men, but more for the fact that I am being held captive…against my will. It doesn’t matter because I am alive and that is all the matters. Staying alive is my only goal at this point…it’s the only thing I care about. This Fusion is one I am looking forward too…giving me a chance to get my hands on Mark Makonie He is partly the cause for my pain over the past week…helping my enemy…helping Jason Hilton. It was his first mistake and quite frankly his last. I won’t be held here forever and when I have free reign again…he is going to suffer. He is going to pay for his mistakes. The only problem is he has help…Shane Robertson. He is just a bystander like SyN…only placed here because they wanted to see how they faired with the PCW “veterans”. Robertson doesn’t scare me…he doesn’t strike fear into me…no one does. Shane is going to purge the PCW of sinners. Funny how it ends up because I am purging it from itself…from the hell hold that it’s in right now. Shane and I have a lot more in common then I thought…we are both on a mission. Yours comes from God where as mine comes from powers higher up…the sinners. “The only difference between the saint and the sinner is that every saint has a past and every sinner has a future.' I can’t help wonder what’s running Stone Burn’s mind though…he failed in his goal to destroy me. Now he is getting his second chance. A “rebirth” so to say…another chance to prove he is a better killer then Christian MacManus. A task that he found wasn’t quite as easy as he originally dreamed…almost impossible. The mistake that he made was challenging me to a battle of skill in murder and I am a cold-blooded killer…a murder. Wielding a blade is good, but not when your opponent is wielding a .9MM. Stone you just aren’t the man you made yourself out to be…you aren’t the killer I am. -Christian Michael MacManus *** Location: CIA Main Floor, Langley VADate: April 30, 2003Time: 6:00 am Walking across the concrete floor of the CIA is Tabby Westerhold. The brunette beauty is wearing a pink dress with white buttons and her black heels click on the floor, as she prances between the massive rows of glass cubicles. Moving with grace and poise past each one, most of the male workers take a double glance at her…the girl with curves in all the right places. She is used to it by now…it happens almost every time she walks by a group of guys. Without stopping…she makes one fluid move into the cubical of Brian Donald. Her petite face is covered in resolve, as her emerald eyes stare at her ex-boyfriend. Brian takes no notice of the woman in front of him…he just continues to type away on his computer. He is dressed in a dark blue button up shirt and his black hair is gelled back per normal. His cold black eyes focused on the screen in front of him…Tabby gets irritated by his ignoring of her. His cubical is one of coziness though…the glass walls decorated so perfectly of pictures of Brian and his family, each being held up in a cold steel frame. In front of Tabby, is his huge oak desk, with his computer in the middle of it and behind him…his the leather office chair he is resting so comfortably in. Clearing her throat Tabby brings his eyes to her own. “Brian I am still worried about Matt…we haven’t heard anything from him in almost a week.” Letting a smile slip across his face, he shakes his head. “You need to cut out this little school girl crush…we all know Matt is fine. He was just on two PCW shows back to back and now he is scheduled for Fusion and Turmoil…they wouldn’t do that if he wasn’t okay.” “We don’t know that for sure…there could be,” she starts off, but not before being quickly cut off by Donald. “Tabby, he won his damn match last night…in convincing fashion I might add. There is nothing wrong with him…other then the fact that he ditched us.” Clinching her fists in anger…she opens her mouth to say something else, but is stopped by the raised hand of Brian…knowing that he is going to continue her verbal assault, she crosses her arms over her busty chest and stands waiting for him to start his rant. “Its simple Tabby…he wasn’t cracking this case as quickly as he wanted too…so he ditched us. He turned with his tail between his legs and left…just to save face and keep his perfect record here at the agency in tact. The man is a genius…put the blame on us and say he did everything he could, but in the end…we were the one’s who messed it up.” “That isn’t true Brian and you know it. He didn’t just leave us…something happened and you are sitting here watching. You could be the cause of his death…why do you have to be such a prick?!” Shaking with anger…he offers: “I am following the book on this one and I request you do the same…this crush is blinding you Ms. Westerhold and I think you better do something to get your mind focused. We have a terrorist to catch and we don’t have a lot of time to do it…now you can either leave Matt as the worthless quitter he is and join me…or you can pine over something you can’t have.” With her eyes glazed over…Tabby looks at Brian in disbelief…coming to the realization that she probably never will get Matt, knowing that she needs to catch the sleeper cell and move past this. Turning on her heels…Tabby almost sprints out of the tiny office, not wanting Brian to see the tears that are falling down her face…smearing her make-up. *** Location: UnknownDate: April 30, 2003Time: 6:30 am Matt hangs silent by tethered hands…letting the sweat trickle down his tan body. Everything is a blur to his bloodshot brown eyes… as he remains quiet, resisting the urge to scream out. With each trickle of sweat another shot of pain runs through his body…sweat into open wounds. He wants to be out of his room…the hunger is burning deep inside. Nothing is coming of it though; he already tried to escape once and it left him with nothing more then taser marks…pain. I am not sure how much longer I can keep this up. My body is in shambles and just going to the PCW venues takes it out of me…with each match I become weaker. Makonie doing Jason Hilton’s dirty work isn’t much helping either…I swear if he touches Haley…or feeds her any of that raciest bullshit I am going to kill him. He shakes his bloody brown hair out of the way and stands to his full 5’8” height…letting the pressure off of his raw wrists. The rope cuts into them each time his legs give out…breaking open the flesh like a knife. Letting out a grunt, he inhales the air…the same air that has been in there for days. The smell of stale vomit still lingers, not giving the operative anything fresh. Suddenly the door on his right swings open and in walks one of the men…the same one that always beats him. Matt turns to face him, letting the door on the floor kick up and fill the small room. Smiling, the man walks over to the agent and stands face to face with him…his hot breath touching Matt’s naked body, making him cringe. The man shows no remorse letting his beady black eyes scan Jackson…pacing the small room, his jeans rub together, shattering the silence. Matt continues to stand quietly watching the man’s every move…watching each of his steps. “You had quite a match on Turmoil Agent Jackson…I am sure your body is still feeling the effects of that one,” the man hisses…stepping back into the agents face. Matt can smell his rancid breath with every puff of air the man takes…exhaling it in his face. “It would have been a lot easier…if you bastards hadn’t beat me before it,” Matt barks out…letting a smile cut his angular face in half. “You can’t blame that on me Jacko…we could easily come to an agreement and this would all be over. Mr. Hilton will pay you for all the time you have lost and all you have to do is keep that stupid bitch away from you.” Matt shakes his head. “You guy’s just don’t get it…this isn’t about money or you winning and me losing. The fact remains I love her and Jason Hilton…Mark Makonie…or you are going to keep me away from her.” The man’s expression turns to one of anger…stepping closer to the bound agent, he hits him with a hard backhand…causing Matt’s swollen lip to split in half and gush out blood like a geyser. He only smiles back at the man, letting the rust coloured substance fill his mouth. It is meaningless by this point…almost becoming an hourly ritual. “Is she really worth all this? All this pain…is she worth it?” the man yells out…hitting Matt again and again, forcing his legs to get weaker and make the rope pull on his arms more and more…cutting into his wrists. Wincing in pain…the agent just looks at him and spits the blood out onto his white dress shirt. Of course she is worth it…are you happy out there in this great wide world? Do you miss me? Do you even wonder if I am alive…these are all things I wish I could ask her…I would give it all up for just a minute of time with her. Just to be free for a minute…to lay with her by my side. Smacking him again. “Well Matt is it…is it worth it?” It has been a long hard road without you by my side…but I am all right. You are starting to become a long lost memory and I am just learning to survive…survive without you my princess. We will be in each other’s arms soon Haley…nothing can keep us apart. 7:00 AM
