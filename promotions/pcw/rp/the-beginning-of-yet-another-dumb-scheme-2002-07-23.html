The Beginning Of Yet Another Dumb Scheme...

23 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-23-2002 ..^../ Our story begins with the bright rays of the moon showering down on the beautiful city below, robust with life as the many who live within it move on with their separate lives. Hundreds of hundreds of twinkling lights, from the flashing billboards to the whizzing of car lights fill the city as we zoom in closer into the city, coming increasingly closer and closer....our focus being a lone rooftop around the 'club' district of the city, where the late night partygoers reside with intentions to party until the sun emerges yet again. As the camera begins to zoom in on the aforementioned rooftop, a dark figure can be seen shaded by the darkness of the roof, hard to make out as the camera comes in closer and closer until finally ..^../ !!~SMACK~!! Voice:'OWW!! NO ONE SAID YOU HAD TO GET THAT CLOSE!!'Off-Screen Cameraman:'Sorry sorry...I'm new at this...' ..^../ The camera unglues itself from the cheek of none other than PCW's evil schemer and vile villain...RENT-A-VILLAIN, who stands perched atop the roof alone, probing his soft blue eyes at the inhabitants below while rubbing his cheek after being rammed in the face with the camera. He fixes his vice-versa red and black mask, which compliment each of his red and black spandex sleeves which cover his arms....the fashion coordination KILLED as the camera probes down to reveal his see-though Venom imprint pants with the ONE item of his Rent-A-Hero innocence intact....his smiley face boxers with his name across the elastic band ..^../Rent-A-Villain:'First you kill my dark intro THEN you wanna probe all down my legs....I'M UP HERE YOU PLUTONIUM PERVERT! Remind me to lock you away in the torture chamber later....geez, all that money I spent on a Rent-Cave just to go back and get a torture chamber.' *sighs* ..^../ The rookie cameraman raises the camera back up to Rent-A-Villain, rubbing the obviously drawn on goatee on his chin, not realizing the cheap magic marker ink is smearing on his thumb as he speaks aloud, with the night sky as his background ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'As it would seem, I have two major objectives at the moment that I must quickly tend to. One, the decimation of this El Diablo. Now I admit, he has no quarrel with me...BUT SINCE WHEN DID A GOOD VILLAIN NEED A REASON TO RAIN DOWN WITH EVIL ON A SUPERHERO!! He is an obstacle standing in the way of my plans to take down a man who may be more EEEEEEEEEEEVIL then me....everyone's favorite prison pen pal, Snow. Well, I hope Snow is paying close attention to my match with El Diablo, FOR WITH MY RENT-A-EVIL POWERS OF EVIL AND STUFF(tilde pending), I SHALL ATTACK HIM UNTIL EITHER HE IS BEATEN BEFORE ME.....OR SO ANNOYED THAT HE JUST GIVES UP ON THE MATCH! Either way, IT'S A VICTORY FOR THE VILE VILLAIN! I'm going to take the beef out of El Diablo's taco for sure....he could never even hope to match my MIGHTY MAGNETO LIKE POWERS! What's he going to do.....blind me with his amazing la cucka racha ability? Throw chalupa's at me? Speak Spanish so fast that it makes my head explode? He is no kind of superhero and he will be forced to realize that harsh reality when I foil any plans he has to defeat me. Then....when I have bested him...it is on to Snow, where I unleash my heinous plans....those plans? Well umm...' ..^../ Rent-A-Villain stands baffled, realizing he never really made up a 'evil plan' for the former 3-time PCW World Heavyweight Champion. Not one to stand lost for words, our villain exclaims with that unusual blind confidence in himself that we've all grown to love about him as he raises a hand in air while striking a sinisterly silly pose ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:''I DON'T KNOW YET!!! BUT I'M SURE IT IS ALONG THE LINES OF GIVING HIM A VILE BOOTY-WHOOPERING WITHIN THE CONFINES OF THAT JAMAICAN UNTIL HE CRIES LIKE A LITTLE GIRL BEING BABYSITTED BY R.KELLY! Snow will learn NEVER to mess with a better villain then him, for he could never hope to compare to my heights of evil. He has no cool costume, no vile catchphrases that turn the stomachs of all the innocent...and he doesn't steal candy from babies...what kind of villain is he? Not a good one. In fact, he's got me so mad I wanna....I wanna....WHAT IN THE NEGABLAZES ARE YOU DOING?!'..^../ The rookie cameraman from before has the camera turned away from what SHOULD be his focus, Rent-A-Villain, and has it fixated on a beautiful woman on the ground with a tight green dress on. She bats some of her blonde hair to the side as the cameraman whistles at her, the girl looking up with an alluring smile and a sweet wink as he exclaims ..^../Cameraman:'HEY GIRL...DON'T YOU KNOW I'M A DIRECTOR TOO!! YEA I MADE 'THERE'S SOMETHING ABOUT MARY'S HEAD'...' ..^../ Off-screen, our villain stamps his foot angrily in place like a haughty teenage girl, appalled by the unprofessionalism of his cameraman as he grabs the camera by the lens and swings it back over towards him, Rent-A-Villain angrily yelling at the cameraman with more of a childish whine then an authoritative scolding. ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'YOUR MESSING UP MY RP CAMERA OPERATOR TYPE PERSON!! YOU'VE GOT ONE MORE TIME BEFORE I DROP YOU IN A VAT OF CREAMY APPLESAUCE.....okay okay....so my tortures are a little green, I'm getting better....right?' *cameraman shakes head with camera following* 'OH WHAT DO YOU KNOW!! Anyways...I can't wait forever to get my hands on Snow....I can't just stand back and wait for the pay-per-view to get a piece of him...it's much too long. There's only ONE way I can get my hands on him early without waiting for that Wrestle Vessel.....GOING TO JAIL!!!! That's it, I'LL GO TO JAIL!! I'll get arrested, end up in jail with Snow, and then I'll unleash my DOC OC TENTACLES O' SLAPPING AND THROW MORE SMACKS AT HIM THEN IKE TURNER AT A WOMEN'S RIGHTS MOVEMENT!!!! Snow shall feel my partially spandex enwrapped wrath even within his jail cell!! Now....I need to find a way to get myself arrested...that shouldn't be too hard seeing as how I'm more heinous then Rosie O'Donald in a g-string....' *shudders at that thought* 'Okay....NEVER that heinous. NOW...AWAY I GO....TO GET ARRESTED....FOR I AM EEEEEEEEVIL....I AM HEINOUS.....I AM....RENT-A-VILLAIN!! GYAHAHAHAHA...' *Cough cough, weez* 'Asthma...as...thma...' ..^../ Rent-A-Villain's sudden coughing fit sends him stumbling back, tripping over the edge of the roof as he goes plummeting over the side and soaring down to the earth below. Gravity is proving Rent-A-Villain right as it sends him tumbling down from high, but our villain looks not to be worried, looking to the camera and exclaiming ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'Never fear heinous followers...some illogical mishap like using an overweight woman as a trampoline or being saved by a giant bald-headed eagle will save me at any minute, this is a Rent-A-Villain RP ya know! It should happen any moment now.....any time now.....get ready for it' ..^../ Ummmm Rent....all your assets are still being transferred to your new name....the illogic for your RP's is yet to be signed over ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'You mean....when I it the ground...I'm gonna...'..^../ Yes ..^../Rent-A-Villain:'Just fade me out now so I can save some sort of dignity good narrator person....and remind me to have a looooong talk with my accountant.' ..^../ With that said, our vile villain angrily folds his arms as he plummets towards the earth, the scene fading as he has made his plan clear....to end up in jail so he can inflict harm upon Jason Snow, his heated arch-nemisis. WILL HE COMPLETE HIS PLANS AND GET ARRESTED?! WILL HE DEFEAT EL DIABLO WITH HIS NEW EVIL POWERS?! WILL I EVER STOP ASKING SO MANY DUMB QUESTIONS AT THE END OF THESE RP'S?! FIND OUT NEXT TIME WHEN RENT-A-VILLAIN DOES THE SAME THING HE DOES EVERY RP.....TRY TO TAKE OVER THE WORLD....one dumb scheme at a time of course.
