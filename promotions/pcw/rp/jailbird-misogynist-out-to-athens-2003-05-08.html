Jailbird Misogynist - Out to Athens

8 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/08/03 (OOC: I am reinputting this here seeing that I made a slight mistake on locations and referred the next Turmoil's location to this weeks Fusion location. Same thing as below just changed title and a word below.) Pondering to assay an escape I grew thirsty, then hungry. I'd only sipped a small portion of water and hadn't even swallowed the piece of bread. I was beginning to swoon with the thought of hunger and thirst but managed to muster some of my consciousness back. My spine was flat on the stone wall, my rear was numb from hours of idle sitting, and I had felt that I'd been immolated for no reason.Yet I was not going to give from the raging harlots who'd imprisoned me. The rodents were running up my legs and in to my thigh, and further on let in by the toga they'd clad me in. I struggled to my feet but stumbled back down in pain as the numbness of my ankles became unbearable and I was in need of motioning them. I'd thought of nothing in the past three hours, and just left my head hanging in the air like the moon. The fear of dying was far from my case but I just didn't want to decay like a cow in this cell, especially when jailed by women. But I swore to myself that I would be liberated from this prison.. but minutes felt like hours as I waited and thought aimlessly to nothing but darkness. The sound of rats scurrying, and chirping filled the air as my mind swooned in to a deeper daze, but I continued to pound myself on the temple, in effort to leave me awoken but it'd failed. Sleeping for hours had brought food and water. In my hand was another single goblet, I thought it was a rat crawling in to it but the ivory shade indicated its presence. An urge for water I drank the entire, semi-filled goblet in a quaff. Turning to my side a bucket had tipped over and another piece of bread came to view in it's brown softness, far from fresh but still quenching my hunger as I swallowed the pieces of bread, this time not specially seasoned like before. Although, it wasn't as tasty it was good enough for now. A siren came from the back of be and I hearkened for a second. Scattered screams reverberated over my cell but stopped after a few popping sounds, ending the frenzy. Minutes after a knock came over the cell door and I barely gave a yell of.. 'I'M IN HERE!' With the counting of some masculine voices, the metal bars once attached to the floors came down along with the door and a burst of light broke in to the room. The rodents fled as I tried to stand up but still had no energy or strength to lift all my weight. A few men, dressed in uniforms, uniforms of police officials came in with their pistols held forward, looking around finally spotting me. One of the Yemeni officers ran down to my attention and it came to a surprise for me as I thought he would rather kill me than leave an American like myself alive. Wrong I was as he assisted me and managed to break open the shackles, which could've unfettered by hand but unfortunately my hands had been occupied also by these shackles. My feet were in extreme pain as I kicked up and down with my foot trying to shake it off. Further on the shackles, tightening the blood in my hands came off as I rubbed them vigorously relieved from having them removed. I smiled in joy even though I was in a horrible amount of pain it was just a thing to dissimulate them. They lead the way to the outside, as I barely strutted forward. And there I saw the women who had kidnapped me being beaten by the officers and I'd seen this many times in America (more on African-American males) but this was just smooth destroying. The officers pounded the women with their semi-automatic guns as they fell unconscious and for the first time I smiled (UN) purposely. If I had any energy stored in me I would've joined the assault but I left off from their remote homestead on my own as the officers split. I limped on in pain, not having the slightest clue of where I was but still relieved of my 'escape'. Continuing on, seeing the bustle and hustle of cars onward I assumed it would be some sort of a city leading me to an airport. I went on seeking some help, as I would have to inform the PCW Front Office officials of my endeavor for financial reasons... Measles and bees went up my toga; rendering kicking hops and homosexual gestures but no one around seemed to mind as they sported the same type of gear. The cuts on my thigh and legs came to no bother, as they seemed like they'd been nursed and rather dissolved from my skin.. I had the sudden urge to go back around to the cell I had been locked in but too lethargic to waste my time for Yemeni harlots. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~Exposition: Stone had continued on further that day, being assisted and almost kidnapped once again. But this time by his own male gender but managing to escape the usual happenings in Yemen. Struggling to get in contact with the PCW he'd finally manage to meet up with a left behind staff worker who'd still been in Yemen on accident. The misogynist was guided to an airport and flew off to Athens, Greece later that night... still in his toga.. and was on the verge of harassing a Yemeni stew artist physically because he swore to avenge the harlots that had kidnapped him, only on someone who hadn't.
