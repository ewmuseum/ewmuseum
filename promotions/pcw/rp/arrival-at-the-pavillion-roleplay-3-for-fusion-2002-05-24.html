Arrival at the Pavillion (Roleplay #3 for Fusion)

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24th May 2002 The fans sit glued to their television sets awaiting the returning show of Primetime Championship Wrestling; Friday Fusion. After the amazing triple threat match just twelve days ago followed by a piece of PCW history in the making, the returning show promises to be one of the world's most memorable shows. After the firing of every single person on the PCW roster (with the exception of Alan Steele), Primetime Championship Wrestling set out a task. To find the best men and women of the wrestling world and place them all in one federation. This is truly a new era in the world of Primetime. Some of the newly hired superstars were previously in Primetime Championship Wrestling. Some of them have came from some of the other big time wrestling organisations. But, more importantly some of the new superstars have made their way from the Primetime Wrestling Academy. No promotions to other federations, no special treatment, just hard work and commitment. Not many people can say they travelled straight from the academy to the world of Primetimne Championship Wretling. Out of the forty superstars and tag teams that were lucky enough to make it to PCW, only a small number travelled from the academy. Jaden, Heaven, The Dark Messiahs...and more importantly, The Price Brothers. This promising young tag team show skilld both in the technical and high flying department than others can only dream about. Chet, the older of the two (23) bases himself upon a great deal of Technical skills. His brother Michael (21) is taller, stronger and ironically more of a flyer. The two young rookies followed the academy for one month working their fingers to the bone every single day in a dream of making it big. Their dream has finally arrived. We set our scene backstage at the PCW Pavillion arena in Atlantic City, New Jersey just hours before the returning show of Primetime Championship Wrestling. The grey walls accompained by lower blue tiles surround the background. A television moniter is built into the wall. This is all based upon light grey coloured contrete flooring. The camera is set facing directly foward. After a couple of seconds, one of the newest tag team additions to the PCW roster appears. The tag team, The Price Brothers. To the right is Michael Price. He is wearing a white shirt covered by a black blazer, a black tie and a pair of black pants. Michael's short brown hair is neatly combed and his chin goatie lingering from ear-to-ear seems slightly trimed. To the left of him is Michael's brother, Chet price. Chet is wearing a loosly worn black T-shirt featuring a small PCW logo on the front and a pair of 'gothic-type' black jeans. His spiked blonde hair seems especially gelled today. The two brothers are both looking directly into the eye of the camera. Michael opens up his first appearence on live Primetime Championship Wrestling television. Michael: 'We, the Price Brothers asked for this specific television time just hours before the show tonight. We want you fans too see us before we go into that ring tonight and show all these PCW stars what we're all about.' Chet interupts his brother. Chet: 'As my brother just stated, we're going to teach the PCW returnies what we're all about. We're tired of being down played just because we came right out of the academy. Well let me tell you all something. It takes a special type of tag team to come straight from a training facility into the big leagues. Not many teams can say that. Most of the teams who are now part of PCW have either been in PCW before or came from one of the other main federations. We, along with a few others were specifically chosen by The Phantom Booker right from the academy.' Michael: 'Now, onto our opponents for tonight. One guy, athletic, youthful, full of life. The other, old and frail. I consider it an insult to be placed in the ring at the same time with a man who's somewhere in his late fifties. He should be retired and not be forced to go against a team of young brothers.' Chet: 'You see, The Price Brothers have one thing that politically Incorrect don't have. Not the will to fight, not the will to survive, but the will to stay side by side like the brothers we are. Politically Incorrect will soon find out what the term 'Brothers' is all about!' The two brothers storm away from the camera sight. The scene draws to a close.
