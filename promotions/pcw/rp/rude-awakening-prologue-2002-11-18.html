Rude Awakening – Prologue

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 18th, 2002 There was always that one person in the group. One that just didn’t seem to fit in…One that was just a little bit off from the rest. It was just something about him that made him different then any other being. Faith… Fear… Hopes… Dreams…. His own destiny… No, it was something more deeply then that. It was almost like he was missing something from his own body. Something not given at birth, but more achieved as life progresses. Yes, he had all his feelings but it was opposite. Day become Night… Night turned to day… And in a blink of an eye he would soon turn back and join them again, in such a way that there would be none the wiser. He could achieve kindness, but in a way he was always questioning the world around him, playing his own friends from ponds, and in a way he was trying to control something he had no control over. To him, life was all one big game, one big competition; one way to show everyone he was superior. But his vision got in the way of his dream, and more importantly his very only beliefs that made the foundation if his life and the way he stood in the universe. To everyone else, he had crossed the line. He went to far, he broke all the rules, and now it was time to get even. It was time for them to proclaim to the heavens that they were not going to be treated like misfits, and would rise up and conquer his competitive spirit. Their vision became impaired, and now it was everyone for them self. He had them right were he needed them to be. The “X” was marked, and soon he would proclaim to the gods he was the very best that could ever be. But that is were he went wrong. He, not only sold his soul to the devil, but worshipped himself to the bone. He would not survive the wrath of the gods and yet he continued on and pushed them the edge. §BANISHMENT§ The lords drew their lines, and quickly he had to flee. Away he went, regretting his choices the rest of his journey. In his thoughts, he could see himself being forgiven for his crimes and his soul rejoined to his body. This outcast was no longer a man, but rather a misfit. He had no identity, no morals, and no destiny to follow. It was all about himself. His fear got the best of him… He sold his soul to win. But truly in the end of things, the fallen angel had won the competition of life by showing everything the victor wanted to hear, as he grasp the soul waiting to make the final trade. Now it was over, no way to turn around and fight back. It was one versus the world. Competition would become the only way to survive. The gods made a pact, never to allow this to happen again. Never to allow a single being to go out on his and try and compete with those who challenge him. They would never lose to the devil himself, and would rather die then to give up another being. There was to be no more free spirits. No more trust in thy self… No more faith in ones beliefs… It was to be just the masters and the slaves, bringing harmony to the universe and peace to the land. Not a single soul would disturb this sacred pack. Everyone would fall into line. Until Now… He was reborn into a new human body that had the form and ability to take on all competition at any single time. He had the power, to do, as he pleased, knowing full well that there would never be an equal, never be anyone greater. Or anyone worse. There would just be groups of different kinds of origin, adding new and impressive prospectives into civilization. There views would be added to his own, their thoughts would become one, their collective mind would thrive on the other helpless forms of life. There was no end in sight. He would only stop once he was accepted for his own personal thoughts. One mind… One idea… One dream… The wind swifted in the trees, the lightning crashed above his head, still standing tall in the middle of an open field he waits, pondering his next move, calculating the accuracy of his timing. He had nothing to lose, nothing to fear, the world was at his knees and all he had to was step forward and take advantage of the possible begginings and endings that were in sight. Picture one man, alone and with no friends to help him. Now picture him with the ability to compete at the highest level possible. Now see him with mystery and the great unknown. Where do you go from here? A man with nothing to lose could very well end what you think is the beginning. Size, strength, speed and agility have nothing to do with winning and competition. Those are just tools in the game. When it really comes down to it only one thing matters throughout this whole game. One question will separate the people who are stars and from a never was. One responds could very well leave you lying in your own deathbed. Nothing to lose, and everything to gain. What will you do to survive?
