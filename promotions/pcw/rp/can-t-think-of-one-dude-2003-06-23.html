Can't think of one dude!

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-23-03 Not to sure whether today is Monday or not, it doesn’t really seem like a Monday to me. Monday’s are usually horrid and gloomy because it’s the beginning of another week that is my life. As I wake up from my slumber in my hotel room bed, I look beside to me to see my girlfriend Sheilla Hisser still asleep. Quietly I get out of bed and walks over to the shower; grabbing a pair of jeans and a black t-shirt to wear. I now walk to the bathroom and grab a towel to use to dry myself when I am finished. After a good fifteen minute shower I am cleansed, as dry as I can be, my hair is the wettest part of my body, being as long as it is, and I am now fully clothed. As I walks to the mirror to further ready myself for the day I stare at myself in the mirror and ask myself, “Is this who you envisioned yourself being at this stage in your life?” I then smile a cocky looking smile as I pick up a brush from the side of the sink slowly and begin to brush my hair so it looks a little better. My opinion on hair is, if it’s pulled back and you brushed it just a little, it will look just fine. So as I finish brushing my hair I pull it back and tie it into a ponytail. I walk over to my girlfriend’s side of the bed and sit beside her as she slowly begins to wake to my close presence smiling down at her. She smiles back up at me, her eye still full of sleep. Sheilla sits up in bed and looks around the room.”You’re up awfully early.” (she says sounding surprised) “What gives, you usually aren’t up for another three hours?”As I think about what she has said I just smile at her beautiful face and beautiful mind. I let out a sigh as I begin to answer after kissing her gently.”You know babe, I jus’ feel I need to start taking life more seriously than I have and start getting things done when they need to be done and if that means getting up earlier than so be it.” (I say with a hesitant voice then sigh afterwards) “Something has to change about my life or else my ultimate Turmoil Winning Streak will not last long.”Sheilla looks at me, her beautiful smile still upon her face. Sheilla takes her left hand and brushes it from her face as she scoots me off the bed and gets up soon after. Sheilla stands up close to me as she grabs me around my waist. I then take my arms and wrap them around her arms and body, smiling down on her. Being five-foot ten-inches, you can only imagine how short she is for me to actually look down on her. I kiss her as she kisses me back then smiles with me afterwards.”So I take it I will see you at the gym?” (I ask with intrigue)Sheilla doesn’t just answer; being her she pulls away from me and then turns away. Sheilla begins to twirl her hair smiling, making me wait for the answer I desire. Sheilla then turns back around and begins to speak, but then walks to her clothes back, being even more of a tease than she usually is. Eventually Sheilla turns around, sits on our hotel bed, and smiles up at me.”Yeah, I’ll be at the gym after I finish getting ready.”“Manuel Labor won’t know what hit him, the new Ganker Ransolver Vengeance!” (laughs) “Although I really don’t know if that’s such a fitting name for my finisher because I really don’t hold a vengeance against anybody babe…””Then change it.” (she says with a hint of laughter behind her voice) “You’re so silly sometimes babe.”(laughing as I sit beside Sheilla) “Yeah, but then I wouldn’t be the man you fell in love wit’.” (I say as I wrap her under my arm and lay my other arm on her leg)(laying her head on my shoulder) “No, you most certainly would not be.”I stand up as I help Sheilla up as well. We hold each other in our arms for quite sometime, but then I eventually grab my hotel room key and whatever else I need for the day, and stuff it into the pocket of my jeans. I leave the room as Sheilla waves with a smile. Sheilla then begins to slowly get ready, enjoying the facto she is alone in the room without me and can be free, or whatever. The Gym Thirty minutes after leaving my hotel I find myself standing in the gym, thinking about how I wish to start my workout. I walk over to the rack of weights and grab a two and a half pound weight. I toss the weight in the air and begin to smile a very sly smile. I now find myself walking over to some guy who’s trying to press 300-pounds with nobody spotting him (idiot). I take the two and a half pound weight and slide it on one of the ends, causing the lifter to feel the bar become unbalance and lose control. The heavier side falls to the ground and all the weights fall off, then the other side falls and all the weights fall off. The lifter sits up and stares at me like he’s going to kill me as I stand there trying to keep from laughing, but look like I’m about to explode. I simply walk on passed him and over to a leg extension machine. The lifter gets up from the bench and walks over to me as he crosses his arms.”You did that! You put on extra weight and made me mess up didn’t you?” (he says with anger in his voice)”Yeah so what if I did? What are you going to do, press me over your head and say you can press at least 160-pounds?” (I say with a hint of sarcasm) “Dude, you so don’t’ intimidate me cause I know I’m so much better than you dude! I’m fighting the likes of Manuel Labor, the extreme champion of PCW in a non-title affair while you’re sitting in this damn gym in this Podunk city of Quito trying to look stronger than you truly are, so back the f-uck off boy before I knock you on you’re ass!”Me saying all of that to him only further pissed him off. By this time I stand up and lean up against the leg extension machine, still playing it cool and flashing, showing off a cocky smile to this lifter, which really pisses him off.”Listen dude, I really need to workout now so if you don’t mind go back to trying to be cool over there.” (I point to a corner of the gym where nobody is) “Cause that’s how many care what you do. So now if you don’t mind I’m going to continue my workout cause I have a huge match to get ready for.””Actually I do mind!””Okay you can mind all you want, but move your ass over there still!” (I point again to the corner of the gym where nobody is) “Cause I’m busy here dude, actually doing something that’ll get me somewhere! Unlike you who will be in this gym for the next several years trying to look cool, you disgust me!””Oh Yeah?”I stare at this lifter like he’s stupid for saying such a chilidish comeback to me. However I find myself getting tired of talking to a man who’s not even listening and decide to make him move, so I kick him square in the balls and watch him topple over. I step over the lifter and walk over to a leg press machine and set the weight to something extremely easy to start off with; 300-pounds. As I sit down to press, I make sure everything is right and I’m going to do the press correctly so I don’t’ strain anything. I get about two reps of twelve done when my girlfriend Sheilla enters and surprises me from behind. I stop lifting and gets up and greet her the proper way a good boyfriend should, or at least so I heard, he, he. I hug and kiss her as she does the same to me. At the Arena I send Sheilla into our locker room before me, but I stay off to do a little strolling before I am confined to a damn locker room for several hours going over some freaking tapes of some wannabe extreme wrestler who just so happens to somehow hold the extreme championship, but then again Hank did hold the Extreme Championship, so I guess anybody can really. Anyway as I walk outside of the arena, I find an old lady feeding the birds. I walk over to this old lady and stand behind her for a second, then hits her across the back of the head, knocking her to the ground, she’s not dead just seriously hurt, I’m not that mean, just cruel. I grab her purse from the bench and rummage through until I find her stash of cash. I take every damn dollar bill she ahs in the purse, yeah I know it’s wrong but I need gas money. After I finish mugging the damn old prune I scram out of there faster than some damn lightning after it hits a damn tree. As I find myself coming back to the arena, I slowly enter and am immediately ambushed by like twenty interviewers… Okay ten… Okay fine one, but he’s like weighs as much as twenty interviewers dude.”No, you can’t know that I hate Manuel Labor and you can’t have a damn interview wit’ your GOD you cheap son of a whore! Dude, you’re fat, ugly, and overweight, and you need to take a shower! So why don’t you go work on those things! Now dude, if you don’t mind I’m going to go see my girlfriend, see you later butt plug.”I shove the interviewer on his ass and walks onward to be with, see my girlfriend, and watch some tapes of Manuel Labor’s past bouts. The scene cuts off to black.
