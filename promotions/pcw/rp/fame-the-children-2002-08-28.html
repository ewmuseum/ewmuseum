Fame: The Children

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-28-02 The scene opens to the view of a hill at sunset. The orange sky silhouettes a figure as it marches upward into view. It's shadow stretches far from the line of vision and slowly this figure comes into view. Now, in the last glistening sparkle of sunlight, we see the face of the Infinite Warrior, Trigger as he continues along his path. He wears his usual black pants and white tank top. No mind that the whether is cold. Now, the sounds of voices. Laughter and conversation fill the silence and turning we can see Trigger's destination. A small restaurant to the side of the road… could he really be heading there? In all time of following the warrior, we have never once seen him go to a place of public gathering, never once seen him act as a normal citizen would. Yet tonight… tonight he does. Outside of the restaurant, little children are playing in the street. They part like a great ocean as he walks through, but their amusement is no more mysterious than the chilliness as the wind ripples by. The children pause for a moment, and the warrior hardly notices them, but soon the kids break out into shouts of joy and excitement. Kid 1: Look guys! It's Trigger! Kid 2: The guy from PCW? Cool! Kid 1: Yeah, the one who got his butt kicked by Akiyama last week! Kid 3: He must be going to Turmoil tonight! Kid 4: Let's get his autograph! The children swarm the Infinite Warrior, pulling out pens and paper from god-knows-where. He does stop, but does not accept the autographs as of yet. Instead he questions their actions, as though only young ones, he accepts a reasonable response. Trigger: Children, what is it you want from me? Kid 1: An autograph! Kid 2: Yeah! Trigger: Why do you want an autograph from me? Kid 3: Well, uh… you're from PCW! I love wrestling! The Infinite Warrior directs his next question right at the third child, who stares blankly back. Trigger: Tell me… had you ever heard my name before I lost to Xavior Kannon two weeks ago? A long silence. Kid 4: Weren't you Rising Star Champion a few months ago??? The children resume their shouting and begging for autographs. Praise from children… the most unworthy reward. Kid 4: Now that I think about it, he has the longest title reign of anyone! Kid 2: No, you idiot, you're thinking of Rent A Hero. Kid 4: No, I'm telling you, Trigger beat his record. Kid 1: Cool! Kid 3: He almost beat Xavior Kannon too! Kid 4: I was hoping he would, I hate Xavior Kannon! Kid 1: Me too! Kid 2: I think he's cool! In their highly engaged conversation, Trigger had somehow slipped away, as he was walking steadily closer to the restaurant. The children spot this, and run quickly to catch up to him. Kid 3: We know who you are, Trigger, man, why can't you give us an autograph? Trigger: I'll tell you what I'll give you, I'll tell you how you can become a warrior. It takes practice, but more than that, devotion. More than devotion, it takes spirit. And more than spirit, it takes soul. You have to be willing to wake up every morning and ask yourself: Are You A Warrior? If the answer is yes, then you will know it is true. If the answer is no, then you will know you have more work ahead of you, but it is achievable, because you are honest. If you wish to be warriors badly enough, I can make it happen… through a long process called the training of the WITs-- Kid 1: Mister! All we wanted was a freakin' autograph! Trigger: An autograph? My signature on a piece of paper? What is that when I have given you so much more? Leaving them there, begging, the Infinite Warrior continues on towards the restaurant. But he knows that if the kids are still here on the way back that he will surely give in to their pleas. A warrior is not heartless, after all. The heart is what makes the warrior.
