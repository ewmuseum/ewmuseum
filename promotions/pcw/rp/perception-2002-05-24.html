perception

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/24/02 -A gritty, deep, tired voice is heard though the darkness.- Voice: It’s been rough and rocky traveling but I’m glad to be standing up right on the ground. After taking several readings I’m surprised to find my mind still fairly sound. They say that PCW is the toughest but I guess they say the same about them all. Getting my education in the rings and streets of the world is what I do. A veteran of in ring combat. An old pro at political maneuvering and mind games. You have to be now a days. It used to be just run your mouth, then cycle through your moves, pin and its over. Not anymore. The secret, is never give them what they think is coming. I hear week in and week out, people calling me a dumb redneck. The perception of idiocy is a gift rather then a nescience. Slight of hand is the greatest tool of the devil. Think about it. You praise God and the world deteriorates. You go to church and they want money. Money the tool of the devil. Wars are fought over God, and lives are lost. Is he really the good guy or is he the devil using perception again you? Perception and public image are everything. Bill Clinton is a criminal, pervert, and con artist. However the fans of the Democratic Party still idolize this man. Why?? James Carville the world’s top spin artist is a good start to finding out why. Public image and your PR staff is what makes you a good guy or a bad guy. You pull your pants down, don’t get caught. You get caught, lie your way out of It. They catch you in a lie, spin baby, spin, you’ll confuse them!! So is Xavior Kannon a good guy trying to spin his way out of his past?? Is he a con artist trying to sucker the Malice Crew into a trap?? Or is he a lost lamb looking for his shepherd. If he is perceived by the fans as a “good guy” does that lower my value in their eyes? Does the market of good become saturated with an over abundance of men fighting against the perceived evil? Or would Kannon be the ultimate ally? In this world of good and evil the only referee and consistent is time. Time is the judge of all battles. Time will resolve these questions. But who the hell has the patience to wait for time? -A flash of light from a match which is used to light a cigar reveals the voice’s face as Duke Williams.- Williams: I sure as sh*t don’t. If Kannon wants to leave the UA, then come Fusion he needs to do so. The more he jerk’s us around the more skeptical we become. If he leaves and still looks at me wrong I won’t waste a second of time to cut his throat and leave his ass for the Ream Team. Kannon you’re a fence riding freelancer. You go with the tide. You feel the shift in the earth’s atmosphere leaning towards the falling of the UA and the uprising of the Malice Crew. Are you weaseling your way out of the war hoping to screw us both when we’re not looking? Or are you looking to profit off of war like the Swiss? Piss on that kid, cus Hoss I won’t allow it. I will track your every move, and document every word you utter. I will figure you out before you know what your doing and if your intentions are……”malicious”…and the perception your putting forth doesn’t accurately portray your motives and agenda. I will stop you. This is not a threat but rather the word of the Malice. It’s my job and the job of the Malice Crew to police and protect the blue collar working man. We are the voice of the people and Kannon the voices have just spoken. -All of a sudden the lights turn on as we see Duke Williams grabbing his sunglasses as his eyes adjust to the light. The room is small and Duke is sitting at a desk against the back wall of the room under a picture of Martin Luther King jr.- Kesha: God Damn baby you ever hear of light. Williams: I was cutting a promo. Kesha: In the dark?? Williams: Yea it sets the mood. Kesha: Mood my ass you can’t see nothing. Williams: Sometimes words are more powerful then details. Kesha: Sometimes people what to see what the f*ck is talking. Williams: Good point. Kesha: You talking about them UA muthafuckers? Williams: Yes I am. Kesha: The match is tonight why don’t you rest. Williams: I have to get one more out. Kesha: Ok baby, just leave one for me. Williams: MALICE STYLE. -The Malice Man winks as Kesha exits the room and turns the light back off so we are just left with Duke’s voice.- Williams: Back to perception. If this was the business world I would be looked down upon and Ty Nelson, even with aids would be idolized. I would be the first victim of corporate downsizing while his salary was upsized. In factories and blue-collar homes across the country I’m a hero and Nelson is the zero. All it comes down to is which side of the fence you stand on. Fortunately for me there are a hell of a lot more people standing on my side of the fence. Nelson that should scare the sh*t out of you. I’m the Red, White, and Blue and your Cuba. Yea you got power but when we get right down too it I can blow your ass out of the water. And the Malice Crew will do just that. Battle one in the restart of the PCW is tonight. No longer are you the ultimate authority but rather just a player in the game. Kano and myself both want to tear you apart and tonight we’ll do just that. We will do it in a style that rocks~! Malice Style. Cry, bitch, whine, lie, or just be yourself I don’t care. Tonight is the first night of the new generation of Malice. New Breed, UA, it don’t matter who the bad guys are, the good guys always ride away in the end. -The door swings open again and the lights flick on.- Kesha: Baby what did you do with the remote? -Duke just sighs.- Kesha: Damn were you still cutting your little promo? Williams: Yes. Kesha: Tell me where the remote is and I’ll leave your ass alone. Williams: I left it on the sofa. Kesha: I looked there and can’t find it. Williams: Check the cushions. Kesha: O…K!! -Kesha shuts the door and Duke goes back to his speech in the dark.- Williams: Mark Mookie and Tim Walden your presence doesn’t concern me. Rush and Shabazz, have more talent in the tip of there nipples then you kids have in your whole bodies. All you kids are doing is taking up space so the UA looks more impressive then it really is. Sad really. In the Malice Crew we’re all equals is that true of you guys?? Timmy you’ve been silent lately has the cat got your tongue, you afraid of the Malice Crew, or doesn’t Nelson let his low level stooges talk?? -A voice is heard through the door.- Kesha: I found it!!! -Duke still in the dark is heard sighing- Williams: I’m glad that mystery has been solved. That brings us to the next guy on the list and that’s Big Alan Steele. What the f*ck is this guy talking about?? I sure as sh*t don’t know but I don’t think that’s a bad thing. If Big Al wants to swing with the way of the people he knows what to do. I can’t change his opinions or what he’s going to do. I have a feeling his intentions will be as noble as he is. I can not change the things I do not control so I will not worry about it. The time for Malice is now. PCW is preparing to be sent WHISKEY BENT AND HELL spread the word and let the perception flow. -The voice stops talking as the promo ends in the darkness.-
