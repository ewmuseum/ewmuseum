The CWS Opens Its Doors (Complete)

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-19-02 **OOC: Completed Form...done in script format after a failed attempt at novel-style. No need to read the last one, it's been redone almost word-for-word.** **Theme Music: 'Pour Some Sugar On Me' by Def Leppard** ----------------------------------------Dear Jenna, It's time. The school's reopening. I've got two students so far, and there're some auditions for people coming in soon. I'm excited...but I don't know if I can afford to be spending my time on this, when I need to practice my in-ring in preparation for OG and LeBlanc...but that's the way the cracker breaks, y'know. I miss you, as always. Love,Jason---------------------------------------- ::On Pensacola street, right off of the Florida State University main campus, there's a warehouse. And in that warehouse, there are years of history. In 1934, it was built, and use to house coal. The smell has never totally left the rafters. In 1961, it's ownership turned over, and the warehouse lay apandoned until the University bought it up in 1965. They used it for storage of old sports gear, and the like...up until the interior renovation of Doak Campbell Stadium in 1998. And since then...it has lain dormant. Until now...now it's mine.:: [The large front doors of the warehouse swing open, in the early morning light, and the figures of three men are shadowed in the entrance.] Mark: This is...just...AWESOME. Joe: God, Jase...this place smells kinda like sulfur. [Mark Farmer and Joe Stephenson, two fraternity members of Phi Kappa Psi's Florida State Chapter, and the first two entrants into the 'new' Core Wrestling School, follow their new mentor, Jason Core, into the warehouse. On the inside, very very simple furnishings. A table. Several folding chairs. One hanging punching bag...and a BRAND NEW PCW ring.] Mark: Whoa, Jason...man, is that a REAL ring? [Mark's eyes bug out in sheer delight at the sight of the majestic ring, completely roped and matted.] Jason: Totally real, boys. Totally real. [A relatively pleased, but serious Jason Core, rests himself on one of the aforementioned folding chairs by the table.] Jason: We've got a big day today. The auditions for Core Wrestling School. You two are in, but I'm gonna need at least eight people, not including you, to make upkeep. Joe, Mark...let's get down to it. Let's get that stuff outta my truck. [With these oh-so-inspiring words, Jason and the boys head out to the car to setup. A clock on the wall of the CWS reads 8:30, and then changes to approximately 9:00. We go back to the inside, where Jason, Mark, and Joe all sit expectantly at the table.] Joe: Dude, this sucks...can we go now? [Mark slaps him upside the head.] Mark: No, man. Are you gonna take this seriously at all? [Mark takes a quick look at Jason, who's gaze is firmly fixed on the unmoving doors. His dark eyes are emotionless.] Jason: They'll come, boys. [Jason turns to Joe, and pulls his keys out of his pocket.] Jason: Do me a favor, Joe...take my car down to Krispy Kreme, and grab two dozen donuts and a gallon of OJ. [Jason tosses the keys negligently to his student.] Joe: Yeah, no prob, boss. [Joe takes the keys, and dashes out of the warehouse, almost running over a sharply-dressed young man in his early twenties.] Man: HEY! Is this the Core Wrestling School!? [He's clad in what appears to be wrestling gear, with high black wrestling boots, leather pants that look designed for in-ring work, and a buttoned up polyester shirt that sparkles blue, black and silver. His eyes are covered by trendy dark sunglasses, and his dye-job-blonde short hair is spiked like a pop star's] Jason: Yeah, I'm Jason Core...and this is my school. It's under renovations. [Jason stands up at the desk, and holds out his hand to shake the youth's hand...but is blown off as the kid just stands looking at the surroundings with his hands on his hips.] Nick: Whatever, man. I can't believe that the PCW Extreme Champion would have a shithole like this be his wrestling school. [Mark jumps to his feet, and tries to defend his friend and mentor.] Mark: Hey, asshole. You try running a wrestling school, okay? [Nick smiles.] Nick: No thanks, man. I'm here to learn, not to teach...although I probably COULD run a better operation than this... [Jason makes a concerted effort to step between the feuding young men.] Jason: Hold up, hold up...what's your name, kid? Nick: I'm 'The NorthStar' Nick North. From Pennsylvania. [Jason's eyes get kind of wide at the mention of his home state.] Jason: Pennsylvania? Nick: Yeah, State College. I just graduated from Penn State, and I was told to come down here and find the best. I didn't believe that a wash-up like you could actually BE the best...but then I see you win that pretty PCW gold, and I think it might not be such a bad idea to come take a look at what you have to offer. Doesn't look like much, but I was told you were the best...are you? [Needless to say, Jason is more than a little thrown by the powerful comments by the headstrong and obnoxious youth.] Jason: Yeah, yeah, I'm damn sure I'm the best at what I do. [Nick seems a bit amused by this remark, judging from the almost-paternal smirk on his cocky face.] Nick: Cool. I'm in. Mark jumps in between the two men, hands high and open. Mark: Whoa, whoa whoa WHOA! Don't we have to screen these guys first, Jase? See if they've got what it takes? Nick: C'mon J-Core...who is this dude, your kid? Check me out... [Nick tosses his glasses into his shirt pocket, and removes his shirt. His body, which looks about 240-250 lbs, is VERY well-built. He flexes his arms, but Jason shows no emotion.] Nick: How about that, Core...lackey? Is that good enough for you two? Or how about this...I already can bump, bleed, slam, work, ref, and do a hundred other things. I've had indy training before, but these guys try to keep ME down, to keep their egos high, and I'm sick of it. I want a place where I can be a bright shining star, and get recognized for it, alright? And if this is gonna be that place, awesome. If it's not, I couldn't care less. Am I in, or out? [Jason looks at Nick one more time, and then turns to Mark for his reaction. Mark slightly shakes his head from side to side as if to say 'No frickin' way' and Jason nods in response, affirmatively.] Jason: North, you're in. [Mark looks shocked, while Nick looks non-plussed.] Nick: F*cking right I am...good choice, J-Man. I'm gonna make you famous... [He goes ahead and puts on his shirt again, and throws the sunglasses atop his head.] Nick: Do you kids have anything to eat, I'm starving here? [Mark opens his mouth to speak...but stutters, and marches off, his mouth muttering words to himself. Things about not being able to believe an asshole like North could be admitted to the school, and how when Joe gets back, he better get the pulp-free OJ. Meanwhile, Nick pulls up two chairs, and sits in one, while resting his feet on the other over by the sign-in table. Jason tosses the kid a handful of paperwork to fill out, from a pile on the table. As Nick looks at it, Jason kicks the chair out from under his legs, startling him.] Jason: Look, you might have raw talent, but I do NOT want you causing problems here, alright kid? You WILL get along with the fellow students, because that is what you do here. I do NOT care if you have arguments between other students. Look at me and The Rush. We have to learn how to team together on Tuesday, even though we’ve never teamed, and are relatively new to the tag team thing, despite Rush’s NTICW Tag Team title win. You and your fellow students will be the same way, or I will force you into a FAR less superior school And you WILL NOT receive preferential treatment due to prior knowledge. I WILL make you relearn everything you know, until you know how to do it right, okay? [Nick's nothing, if not confident. He playfully slaps Jason's shoulder, which elicts a slight wince. It's the same arm that was shot by LeBlanc at March Madness.] Nick: Yeah, whatever, Pops. So who else have you seen today? [Jason spreads his arms to indicate the vast nothingness that exists in the warehouse. Mark and Joe half-heartedly wave.] Nick: Awesome, J-Core...I love private attention. [Jason can do nothing but shake his head, as the cocky new guy playfully smacks Jason's arm again.] Jason: I don't get paid enough for this... [A shot of a clock on the CWS Warehouse wall is shown first at 9:05, then at 9:30-ish. We see Jason sitting contentedly at the registration table, munching on a glazed donut. His students thus far are strewn about the gym like refuse. Mark Farmer, a larger and stocky youth in a CWS T-shirt and sweatpants is doing crunches in the corner. His best friend, Joe Stephenson, is slight, wearing a t-shirt and Umbros, and reading an issue of Maxim and drinking some orange juice on the floor. Nick North, the newest student, is actually balanced precariously atop the ring ropes in the corner of the ring...where he lies in repose. He's the only one of the three dressed in wrestling gear, a pair of tight pants, boots, and a flashy shirt.] Nick: Yo! Boss... [Jason turns his head up to listen.] Nick: If we don't get any more people here, we're f*cked, right? [Jason thinks for a minute, and nods.] Nick: Sh*t. [And with that statement, the obnoxious young man resumes his sleep-like pose.] Female Voice: Hello? Is anyone here? [Mark stops his crunches, Joe stops his reading, and Jase stands up at the table. Through the doorway walks a simply stunning young woman dressed in a white sports bra and tight excercise shorts. He long blonde hair is pulled up atop her head, and she carries an ENORMOUS gym bag.] Sandra: Oh! There you are...my name is Sandra Davis! I'm here for the auditions! [The young men are stunned. Even Nick hops to his feet. Jason merely stands up and nods to her.] Jason: They're tryouts, darlin'...not auditions. [Sandra just smiles warmly, and hurriedly runs up to Jason.] Sandra: Whatever! [She shakes his hand.] Sandra: It's an honor to meet you! [Behind Sandra, Nick makes a jerking off motion with his hand, and Mark throws him a hateful look.] Jason: Sandra...can you tell me a little about yourself? You seem to be in awfully good shape... [Joe nods wordlessly, and Sandra blushes a little.] Sandra: Uh, thank you sir! I just was looking to try out, because I think I have what it takes to be a REALLY good valet! [Jason looks kind of confused.] Jason: You want to be a valet? [Sandra nods.] Sandra: Uh-huh! One of those girls that goes on to pose in Playboy and stuff...they ARE called valets, right? [Joe nearly has a heart attack. Nick calls out to her from the ring.] Nick: Did you know, I'm actually quite a good photographer... [Jason motions for him to knock it off, and Nick does.] Jason: Sorry hon, doesn't look like you're cut out for this school. I only want to train serious wrestlers here. [Sandra's face falls...as does all of the younger men.] Sandra: But... [Mark rushes up to his mentor's side.] Mark: Come on, Jason...give her a chance. [Jason adamantly shakes his head.] Jason: Look...I can't afford to train someone that won't live up to my expectations, or the expectations of this school. [Sandra gets a determined look on her face.] Sandra: Hey! Wait a minute! I'll live up to whatever expectations you can throw at me, Mister! I'm gonna be famous someday...and that TAKES DETERMINATION! [She stamps her lovely, sneaker-clad foot.] Sandra: I don't have very much in this world...I'm not smart, I don't have a lot of money to move out west...all I have is my determination... [She lifts up her hands and cups her breasts a little, for emphasis.] Sandra: ...and these. But they haven't gotten me a job yet! [She smiles a little.] Sandra: So PLEASE, Mr. Cash... [Jason absently corrects.] Jason: Core, actually. [But Sandra is in NO mood to pay attention!] Sandra: ...you HAVE to let me into your school. I need to get my big break somewhere! And I promise I'll work really, really hard, okay? [Jason thinks on it for a moment, as the boys all stand and wait, attentively.] Jason: Fine. You can be in. [Sandra's eyes gleam, and she claps her hands together quickly in excitement.] Sandra: Thank you SOOOO much, sir! [She leans over the table and gives Jason a hug, which he hesitantly returns. Mark, ever dutiful, and obviously taken by the girl, grabs a stack of papers.] Mark: Here...you need to fill these out. [He hands her the papers and she smiles a little.] Sandra: Thanks... [Sandra sits down at the table, next to Jason's chair and starts filling out the paperwork. Mark, Nick, and Joe all grin...hoping to have some sort of chance at the beautiful blonde. The clock changes to 10:18.] Sandra: *yawn* [Sandra sits at the table, filling out a bit of paperwork here and there, while now Jason, Mark, Joe, and Nick are all in the ring, where Jason is lecturing.] Jason: Now...when you fall...you want to take it as flat on your back as possible, and have as much of your back take the impact as you can, alright? [Mark and Joe nod attentively, while Nick scoffs.] Jason: Alright...watch this bump. [Jason bends over slightly, then throws himself backwards, landing on his back.] Jason: I want the three of you to do that. [He gets to his feet, the joints in his knees cracking violently under his braces.] Joe: How many times? Jason: Well, only for about an hour or so. [All three's eyes bug out.] Joe: AN HOUR?! [Jason nods.] Jason: Get to work, now. Become one, with the bump. [And with that...the three men start throwing themselves mercilessly upon the mat, as Jason watches. While all of this is going on, a slight young man enters through the main door. He is wearing a somewhat tight pair of black pants, Converse All-Stars, and a lightweight gray sweater. He's accessorizing with a chain-link bracelet, a barbed-wire choker, an eyebrow stud, and a pair of thick-rimmed black glasses, which frame his mop of dark hair on top of his head. The young man approaches Sandra at the table.] Eddie: Hi...uh, my name is Eddie Russo. I'm here for the tryouts. [Sandra takes one long look at the young man, who can not possibly weigh more than 160 pounds, and is no taller than six feet, and smiles.] Sandra: I thought wrestlers were supposed to...y'know...be big and strong? [Eddie's face falls, and his eyes seem to well up with tears.] Eddie: But...but... [Sandra realizes she's made a mistake, and she tries to soothe the poor boy.] Sandra: No, no...honey, I'm sorry. You can be a wrestler... [She turns towards the ring.] Sandra: MR. CORE! WE HAVE AN ISSUE HERE!! [Jason sees the young man crying at the desk, and Sandra looking quite distressed, and hurries out of the ring, under the ropes.] Jason: What’s going on here? [Eddie’s eyes clear up some, and he looks at Jason with adoration.] Eddie: I…I want to be a professional wrestler. It’s…it’s like…my dream. It’s like being a star in the sky… [Jason slaps his forehead, and shakes his head. Eddie’s eyes begin to well up again, and Sandra pulls Jason over to the side for a moment.] Sandra: Look, this kid’s not, what we’d call “emotionally stable,” right? [Jason looks over at Eddie, who’s drifting between sobbing, and making non-sensical singing noises under his breath. Jason vehemently nods.] Sandra: Let him in, okay? [Jason’s eyes bug out, and he shakes his head, to say “no”.] Sandra: Look, I promise to take care of him, okay? I feel terrible… [Eddie looks over at Sandra, and gets hope-filled eyes.] Sandra: Eddie…you’re IN! [Jason’s eyes bug out even more, while Eddie starts crying tears of joy. Sandra sees Jason’s disapproving expression.] Sandra: What? You want to tell him he can’t stay? [Eddie runs over and hugs Jason.] Eddie: Thank you SO much, sir! It’s…it’s such an…*sobs* [Jason looks over Eddie’s shoulder at Sandra, sighs, and pats Eddie on the back, as Sandra looks on approvingly. The clock changes to 3:22…but no one’s watching.] Sandra: And one…and two… [The entire CWS is doing aerobics. Well, that’s not true. The entire CWS is SUPPOSED to be doing cardiovascular aerobics. Yet, Sandra is leading the aerobics, and facing away from the rest of them, doing windmills. Therefore, Jason, Joe, Mark, Nick, and Eddie are all staring at her hindquarters, while she merrily sways back and forth.] Sandra: …three…four…I don’t hear you moving! [With a quick turn, she turns around, as all the CWS males clumsily try to fake a good windmill. Sandra throws up her hands in half-mocking, half-serious anger.] Sandra: Men! Woman’s Voice: Arrr~! Man’s Voice: Sacre bleu! Madmoiselle Hampton, please do not be zo…uncultured! [Through the door walks two VERY interestingly dressed figures. The woman on the right is wearing a VERY skimpy cut-and-tied shirt that leaves very little cleavage to the imagination, and tight brown, white, and red wrestling tights. Her hair is tied back in a bandana, and her ears are pierced with enormous golden hoops. Her compatriot, is dressed in an absolutely GOOFY old European-style doublet and leggings, that are red, white, and blue. He wears a beret with a pin with the French flag on it, and carries a bottle of cheap merlot. His voice is hideously French accented.] Woman: Arrr~!! Man: Madam Pegleg, please…no more. Ze bus ride waz…unbear-able. Pegleg: Arrr~! Shut yer trap, Jacques. Jacques: Non, that iz NO way for a lady to speak. [Jason turns around and smiles.] Jason: Good to see you two made it. [The other students all reel in a bit of shock. Jason ASKED these people to come?] Jacques: Oui, Monseuir Core. We are ze happiest zat we could be, finally finding work after CCW dumped us like a heap of steenking feesh. [In the back Eddie and Mark exchange brief words.] Eddie: Did he just say “feesh”? What’s a “feesh”? Mark: Knock it off! [Jason turns to address his students.] Jason: Kids, these are two other students here, Jacques Of Arc… [The already irritating Frenchman raises his hand.] Jacques: ‘Allo! Jason: …and “Pegleg” Paula Hampton. [The lovely, tall woman dressed as a pirate raises her hand.] Pegleg: Ahoy there, mateys! [Sandra pushes her way to the front of the crowd.] Sandra: Thank GOD! I was afraid I’d be the only girl! [Jacques’s eyes bug out when he sees the busty, young Sandra.] Jacques (with no trace of an accent): Jesus H. Christ… [The collection of student’s eyes bug out, and Jacques looks around sheepishly.] Jacques (still no accent): My real name is Jack Crevé. Nice to meet you all. [Jason kind of smiles.] Jason: This is living your gimmick, kids. [Eddie, nonchalantly pipes up.] Eddie: What’s your real name, Miss Pirate Lady? [Paula strides forward to Eddie, and lifts him up by the collar of his ‘Poison The Well’ t-shirt, and addresses him angrily.] Pegleg: Arrr~!!!! Do I LOOK like a Miss to you, matey? [Jason rushes over and separates the two nervously.] Jason: Uh…Paula already TOLD you her real name Eddie… [He turns to Paula.] Jason: He must not have heard you, is all. [Paula harrumphs a little, and then goes to turn away. Behind her back, Jason makes a brief finger circle around the side of his head, as if to indicate “she’s crazy.”] Pegleg: Arrr~!!! Arc, let’s go unload the galley and make our way to the mess fer some dinner. [Jacques shrugs, and composes himself.] Jacques: Zee you all very zoon, non? [And with that, the two former CCW superstars leave to unload Jack’s ’92 Camry, or Paula’s galley, whatever it may be. The assembled students are left relatively slack-jawed in amazement, except for poor little Eddie Russo.] Eddie: And you guys thought *I* was weird! [Nick slaps Eddie upside the head, and we’re back to the clock. It is now stretching towards later in the day, 5:19 to be exact.] Joe: So, five large pizzas. Got it. [Strewn about the warehouse like trash are the CWS students. Jason sits at the desk, with Mark and Sandra sitting nearby. Nick is propped up in one corner of the ring, while Eddie is in another, trying to learn how to balance on the top rope. Pegleg Paula is practicing her poses, while Jacques gives her moral support. And Joe is running out the door with the pizza order for our intrepid CWS’ers. We go back over to the table, where Jason is filling out some paperwork as Sandra and Mark look on.] Mark: Hey, Jason? [Jason looks up with tired eyes.] Jason: Yeah? Mark: Uh, so you’ve got a match on Tuesday, right? [Jason nods.] Sandra: Are you, like…worried? [Jason shakes his head.] Mark: You’re that good? [Jason nods.] Sandra and Mark (admiringly): Wow… [The door crashes open, and a sweating, overweight African-American man runs through it.] Man: Am I too late?! I can be too late…that’d be miserable! I CAN’T let that happen!! I’m not late, am I!? [The three people at the table are shocked by the big man’s semi-spastic energy, but not as shocked as Eddie, who falls off the top rope, while no one cares.] Mark: Who are you? Kyle: My name is Kyle Andrews. But you can call me Kyle Velocity! [Every single person does a double-take.] Jason: If you don’t mind, I won’t. [Sandra, ever the blonde, makes a witty comment to Mark.] Sandra: I never heard of the camera TAKING AWAY ten pounds? And even so, how many cameras were ON this guy? [The ridiculously sweaty and overweight Kyle grabs a handful of papers, and starts signing them.] Jason: Whoa, whoa…hold on, son. Why should I let you into the CWS? [Kyle stops signing things.] Kyle: Because I’m big…and big people make good wrestlers. I work hard, and I’ve got great stamina. [The camera briefly shoots to where Kyle’s car is parked outside, directly in front of the building, no more than 30 yards away from the entrance.] Jason: Alright, alright…we’re kind of desperate right now. [Kyle furiously signs the papers, and barely looks up.] Kyle: Frickin’-A~! I’m in? [All the other wrestlers merely shrug, except for Ms. Paula Hampton, who watches with interest.] Paula: Ahoy, Lamen sure has gotten a tan on his voyages, has he not? Jacques (dripping with sarcasm): Frickin’-A~… [All Jason can do is shake his head.] Jason: Oh man, I hope the next few applicants will be a little better. [Almost on cue, the door bangs open, as we hear car tires squeal out of the parking lot.] Female Voice: Thanks, Mom! [Out of the doorway, a very cute little sixteen year old girl dressed in the very Aimz-like attire of baggy pants and a tight little half-shirt walks into the building. Her brown hair even seems to be dyed a bastardized red color. Most of the wrestlers watch her with little or no interest except for two.] Mark and Jason: YOU!? [Bam! Flashback. Previously, when challenging Karl LeBlanc for the PCW Extreme Title, Jason Core went to a keg party. And at said kegger, Mark and Joe sold poor Jason to a “lucky young lady.” Well, that lucky young lady just so HAPPENED to be underage, and obsessed with sleeping with the soon-to-be Extreme Champ. That young lady was named…] Jamie: Hey, sexy. [The young girl winks at Jason, and tries (with some moderate success) to walk sexily to the sign-up table. She bends over, exposing maximum little-girl cleavage (Mark’s just now noticing that she’s not bad for a teenager…) as she signs the papers.] Jason: Whoa, whoa whoa! I am NOT letting you near me for an extended period of time, Missy. [Jamie looks up at Jason with a smoldering look in her eyes.] Jamie: What, you don’t want to see if I’m a natural redhead? [She tugs suggestively at the button to her baggy pants, and Jason averts his eyes as Mark dives over the table to make her put them back on.] Mark: No! [Meanwhile, all Sandra can do is laugh at the boys.] Jason: No. I am NOT letting you in my school, and that’s final. Jamie: Do you mean you’re full? [Jamie pouts her little lips at Jason, who is nonplussed.] Jason: Yes. [Sandra pulls Jason over to her.] Sandra: Mr. Core…we’ve got until seven. That’s all. We need to find TWO MORE people by seven o’clock, otherwise, the wrestling school won’t get started. You better sign her, and then if we get an extra person, you can always kick her ass to the curb.] [Jamie turns around quickly.] Jamie: Did somebody say “ass”…? [She talks to Mark.] Jamie: That little blonde slut better not be fooling around with my man! [Mark stifles a giggle, as Jason groans and rolls his eyes. Sandra’s eyes are hard.] Sandra: Who’re you calling a slut, Miss…Miss… [Jamie poses with her hands on her hips.] Jamie: You can call me Jaimz. [Mark rolls his eyes.] Sandra: Oh, this little tramp is NOT going by that name, is she? [Jason shakes his head.] Sandra: Good, I can’t wait to get her in a ring and throw her around by her hair… [Jamie flips off Sandra.] Jamie: Bring it, bitch! [Sandra gets up from the table, and Mark interposes himself between the girls.] Mark: Ladies, ladies! [Meanwhile, Jason just groans, and falls into his seat at the table. Kyle huffs and puff over to Jamie, and shakes her hand…] Kyle: Nice…to meet you, Jaimz! I’m Kyle Velocity! […and somewhere, the gimmick infringement police are screaming. But that’s not the issue. The issue is the fact that the scene now shows the clock, which reads, quite distinctly, 6:59. And signups close at seven. So, as of now, the Core Wrestling School needs one more student to sign up…] Jason: …and it looks like we’re sh*t outta luck, kids. [All the CWS so far is assembled in the ring, waiting. Outside, thunder seems to crash, and the roar of hard rain is echoing through the roof of the warehouse. The looks on the CWS kids’ faces are of disappointment. A day lost.] Jason: Look, if I ever start up another wrestling school, you’re welcome to come try out. Hopefully we’ll have a better turnout. But for now, it’s seven, I’m out of time, and without another student, at least…I’m out of money. Mark: But… [Jason cuts him off.] Jason: Look, I’m sorry. I’m glad to have met all of you, but… [Nick North jumps out of the ring, angrily.] Nick: But nothing, I’m done wasting my time here, man! [He mumbles to himself as he grabs his stuff.] Nick: I KNEW this was gonna be a waste of time! Thanks for nothing, J-Core! [Nick walks to the door, when an ear-splitting blast of thunder shakes the foundation of the warehouse. Where Nick stands at the door, a loud knock sounds. Nick opens the door slowly…with the door making a loud, audible CREEEEEEEAK as it opens. A barely visible, hulking form is seen in the doorway, framed by a lightning flash.] Nick (frightened): Uh…Jason? Sir? [Nick takes several steps backwards in abject terror, and trips over his own feet.] Nick: *gulp*…I think we have our last student. [The hulking figure takes a couple steps forward, and rain drips off his massive frame. The light is still poor, and Jason turns, and calls out to the hulking man.] Jason: Who ARE you? [Nick scrambles to his feet, and rejoins his CWS compatriots. The dark man opens his mouth, and speaks with a grim voice, and grim purpose.] I AM THE VIOLATOR!!!!! PREPARE FOR TOTAL VIOLATION!!!!!! [The CWS would never be the same again.]
