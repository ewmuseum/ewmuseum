Strip Joints are good for your health, duh.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene fades in to show Stephen Blood’s 1971 Fire Red Dodge Charger nicknamed “The Blood Bullet” pull in front of the most popular strip joint on the suburban side of Milwaukee “Shooters” on a cold snowy night. It is a bitterly cold night, the wind blows sub arctic airs down the streets and into the alleyways, making even the homeless guys seek better shelter than their cardboard boxes. A mixture of snow and rain falls from the sky blanketing the streets with ice. But, this place looks to be exceptionally busy for how bad the weather is, we can hear the loud rock music from the punk band and some hoots and hollers from inside the building. There is even a waiting line of about twenty people outside of the door, a extremely large bouncer, appropriately nicknamed “Crusher” stands at the door, checking Ids and letting people in. He is a well-built man, about 6’5, 290 pounds. HE has a shaved head and sports a long braided goatee nicknamed Charlie. He has been working this joint for years and know the ins and outs of the strip joint and bouncer business. A couple of rowdy kids try getting in the door past Crusher. The huge bouncer picks up the two young men and tosses them out on to the icy street in one swift motion. The two kids slide across the street and slam into the side of a car parked across it. They sit against the car with a dazed and confused look on their faces. They mumble some halfhearted profanities before walking down the street towards their car. They got their skinny college asses kicked…inDEED! The camera cuts back over to The Blood Bullet. The muscle car sits running in front of the strip joint for a few more seconds, before finally shutting off. Some movement can be seen inside of it, before ex-PCW superstar “The New Legend” Stephen Blood steps out of the drivers side door, while current PCW star “The Innovator” Brock Newbludd gets out of the passengers side. The people in line notice Milwaukee’s hometown heroes step out and let out a massive roar. Brock and Stephen acknowledge the crowd as they make their way up to Crusher, who just stares at the two with an unimpressive look. The tag team once known as the Blood Brothers try to proceed through but Crusher steps in their path…) Crusher: Let me check your ID’s. (Brock and Stephen look at each other and smirk, they then walk forward again brushing off Crusher. The large bouncer steps in their way once again, this time putting his finger in Brock’s chest…) Crusher: I said, Let me check your ID’s! (Brock and Stephen’s smirks turn to serious looks as they stare at Crusher…) Brock: You’re serious? Crusher: Yes, let me see them, or else I’ll throw your asses out of here… Stephen: Well, I think we have our ID’s, don’t we Brock? Brock: I believe we do Stephen…. (Brock and Stephen begin digging through the pockets of there coats…) Brock: I didn’t find my ID, but I did find something else… Stephen: Yeah, I found the same thing… Crusher: What? Stephen and Brock: THIS! (With that, Brock and Stephen both deliver a punch straight to the big mans face. The blow sends Crusher reeling back a few feet but still standing. The bouncer shakes off the blows and charges the two men. Stephen ducks down real quick, causing the big man to trip over him and directly into a Brock Newbludd dropkick that sends Crusher down and out. Brock and Stephen stand over the fallen bouncer and high five as the waiting line cheers them on…) Brock: You silly jackass! Stephen: You should know not to f*ck with The Blood Brothers! (Crusher mumbles a few profanities, only to have Brock kick him the jaw. Brock and Stephen high five again, then make there way into the strip club as the scene fades out…) FADE OUT FADE IN (The scene fades in to show the inside of the strip joint that we last saw our ass kicking duo entering. The camera pans around to show the strip joint, it is a very nice place, a large open area with many tables, all with people sitting at them. A stage is set up on the side with a live band playing at it. But, most people’s attention is directed towards the large front stage of the building, pole dancers, strippers and many other beautiful women dance on this stage. A large group of men circle around the stage, stuffing dollar bills into the dancers thong underwear whenever they get the chance. Girls also walk around the table area, giving out lap dances to patrons if they have the money. The camera swings around to show a bar area that is on the side of the stripper’s stage. It is a very large bar, with two bartenders working out. The camera pans over the bar patrons, some drunk, a couple passed out and others talking with their fellow patrons. The camera finally stops on the two men we saw from before, Brock Newbludd and his best friend Stephen Blood. Brock takes a drink out of his beer and lets out a belch…) Brock: What if Stacey and Lindsey caught us here? (Stephen shrugs his shoulders, looks at the strippers and then looks back at Brock, smiling…) Stephen: Who cares dude, strip joints kick-ass! Brock: Agreed. (Brock and Stephen clank their beer bottles together and take drinks. Brock looks around the strip joint, and gets a satisfied look on his face…) Brock: I tell ya bro, this is what life is all about. This is what uptight pricks like Mike Storm loath, and that just makes it even better. (Stephen nods his head in agreement…) Stephen: Dude, you’re fighting the good fight at March Madness. You’re representing all the good things in the world, the things that people love. Brock: Drunken violence? Stephen: Exactly my man. Brock: Ya know, I’ve thought of a theory on why Mike Storm is against my “evil ways”. (Stephen sets down his bottle of beer and leans back on his chair…) Stephen: Go on… (Brock lights up a cigarette and continues…) Brock: I believe that at one time, good ol’ Mikey boy was just like you and me… Stephen: Normal? (Brock nods his head agreeingly…) Brock: Yep, but he was always the scape goat of parties. You know the guy you would get really drunk so you could make fun of him. Or, the guy whose eyebrows you shaved off when he would pass out. Basically the bitch of the party, the dumbass. (Stephen raises a eyebrow…) Stephen: So what’s your theory? Brock: My theory is, Mike Storms trying to get back at all the people who shaved off his eyebrows, treated him like shit. Stephen: A vendetta, huh? Brock: Yep, and who better than me to take a vendetta like that out on, the guys who’s all for beer and violence. (Stephen grows a smile…) Stephen: Maybe he wears a mask because his eyebrows never grew back. Brock: Probably. Well, at any rate, if Mike Storms thinks that by beating me that will cure his problem of being a loser, because quite frankly it won’t. Reasons are simple, he won’t beat me and he will always be a moral piece of sh*t. Mike Storm has no right to judge the way I live my life, because it is my life. The fact is, nobody has ever changed the way I am, and he won’t. Maybe Mikey will finally realize this when I toss him head first off of a ladder at March Madeness. (Brock finishes off his beer and looks into the camera with a intensity in his eyes…) Brock: Mike, I hope you’re ready, I really do. Because when we step into the ring in front of a worldwide PPV audience, you’ll find out that I’m not the drunken pushover that you think I am. Well, let me change that, I am a drunk, but I’m no pushover. Ask anybody who’s faced me before, they’ll tell you that. So, Mike, take your vitamins and say your prayers. Because come Sunday you’ll be kissing your ass goodbye. (Brock puts out his cigarette and smiles a cocky grin at the camera before it fades to black…)
