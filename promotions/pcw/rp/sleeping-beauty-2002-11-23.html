Sleeping Beauty

23 November 2002

Title: Sleeping Beauty Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 23/11/02 The sleeping, pale face of PCW’s Commissioner, Eleanor Hall. Window left ajar and curtains left open, the White nets flap in the light breeze as the low moon peers in through her window like a voyeur. Letting out a breathless sigh she rolls over, reaching for her husband who for once isn’t by her side... Taking a pillow as a replacement and not caring in her rest, hugging it as she fidgets, sending the silk sheets creeping further down off her shoulder. “I’ll win it for her.” The White spotlight hanging in the night sky outside her window bleaches her skin, hair and bedclothes, painting a pale Blue streak across the bedroom. In slumber she is not the same woman PCW knows. A sleeping beauty rather than a preppy bitch. “Just as I’d do anything for her.” She brings out the human side in Xavier, just a shame that nobody can really see it. Popular misconception is that the arrogant Scientologist cares for nobody, only himself. That he values the dollar above life. That his pleasure only comes from revelling in the misfortune of others. True on a few levels, they are wrong on most. He would drop everything for her, he would rather see his fiercest enemy sitting in his CEO’s chair if it would stop one stray blow striking his wife’s face. He’d sign PCW back to Tyler at the drop of a hat if he thought his old mentor could cause her a moment of harm. Equally guiding light, equally Achilles’ heel. “But nothing will ever happen to her, so there’s no problem.” The Green eyes of the CEO are often seen burning in anger or glowing in smugness... During a frenzied beatdown or an expert piece of one-upmanship... But the masks over his eyeballs have slipped in the past. “I won’t let that happen again.” Not even the Scientologist can keep his eyes on an opponent and his wife at the same time, and her petite frame has been bruised... It is then that the human side of Kannon shows through. We see his human emotions such as anger but we rarely see humanity. But when he takes Eleanor in his arms to see if she is okay, to make sure she’s in no pain and to kiss her injury better, there is something there, something that maybe could grow... A glimmer of hope for PCW fans that he isn’t all bad. “I do it all for her.” Away from the cameras, Ellie~! is what she should be. A happy and innocent young woman truly in love and enjoying her life. No more so than now, her face resting on the pillow, sporting an automated smile replacing the sneer. Her sleep is peaceful but animated, hugging the Kannon-substitute pillow tightly while moving in small bursts in pursuit of comfort, each motion keeping the soft sheets heading down her arm while her light hair fans out all around her head. If Xavier can feel like this for one human being, why can’t he have compassion for others. “She’s different.” Again she turns over, leaving the pillow behind and instead pulling the bedclothes back up over her Lilac silk pyjamas, wrapping herself in the sheets as she purrs in contentment beneath the clear starry sky that looms outside the window. A true Wolf in Lamb’s clothing, for when she wakes she will not look the innocent much longer. Just like Xavier, there are times when she too casts off all her negative points, when she is just Eleanor Kannon-Hall, loving wife and not the manipulative and interfering bitch we see so often. If only we could see there good sides all the time. “Stop sickening me.” Her head reclines and her back arches, a yawn is let out before another fidget finds comfort once more. What goes on in Eleanor Hall’s dreams? What does someone dream about when to most she must be living it. She has everything her failed acting career couldn’t bring her, a famous husband, television exposure every week, fortune, residences all over the globe... With none of the auditions and sleazy casting agents. Landing on her feet is the best way to sum it up. “And it’s my job to keep her in the lifestyle to which she’s become accustomed.” It’s not just about the money with Eleanor, although those who see her shallow side may believe so. But even beneath her persona, she feels pride for Xavier’s accomplishments. His rise up the ladder of PCW has taken her from valet to first lady... And she has no intention of going back. To stay there she must keep her man hungry, eager, ever ready to do more. She’s the reason he did it and the reason he must keep doing it. “She’s my reason for everything.” From the window, we pass over the PCW Commissioner, her vacuous pale Blue eyes closed and face devoid of all make-up, to where a bare foot can be seen at the far extremities of the moon’s light pillar. On a chair, watching over his wife sits the figure of Xavier Kannon, standing watch, guarding her as she sleeps. Not that he has anything to worry about as all the sleeping prep has to worry about is waking up. He knows winning Alliances would bring a smile to her face, that small token all her can ask for. “There’s no greater prize.” Ruthless fighter or just a man in love? Whatever the answer, a win on Sunday will please both for the Scientologist is willing to have his body battered and skin shredded just to, come Monday, be in the same bed he watches now as an Alliances winner. He wants Ellie~! to go to bed with him an Alliances winner and he wants for her to wake up to him an Alliances winner. And what he wants, he usual gets. “And so that trend will continue.” The form of Xavier rises out of the chair and perches on the edge of the bed, slowly sliding himself under the covers and to his wife, spooning her as he pulls the loose strands of hair from her face to kiss her illuminated cheek. In love, or in battle... Xavier wins for Eleanor. His sleeping beauty. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
