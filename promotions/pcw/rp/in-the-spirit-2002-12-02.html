Date: 02/12/02 'Honey, aren't you taking this a little bit too serious? [ Grunts and screams are eminating from a upstairs bedroom as we fade into the Jackson household once again as Dante sits back, enjoying some good ole' television in the interior of the home's living room. Of course, we aren't focused upon him yet as the light emiting from the television has caused such a blue hue over the head of him that his wife, upstairs in a different room happen to be seen. Tracy pops her head out of the room, sweat beading down her face like summer rain. ] Tracy: Dante! [ Dante shakes out of his modern stupor.] Dante: Huh, what? Oh look midget bikini wrestling! Gotta check that out, might give Nez and Gaz some ideas. Nope, cut it off right now, alright now what are we watching... Tracy: DANTE! [ The scream rocks the entire household as Dante in tru Skitzo mode, yells in echo, almost as if he were twelve different people in the room. ] Dante: I DIDN'T DO ANYTHING! Tracy: YOU CURSED AROUND THE BABY! [ Raising his eyebrows, Dante looks around, checking to see if his young eight month old daughter Simmone had been stirred or even tampered with by his usage of foul language. He throws up his hands in horror, just about as common to him when he was a black man back in New York. Yes, those were the Rodney King days when the New York City Police Department made it a rule out of finding an African American suspect. He had to have big lips, cursed, smoked weed. Always wore a backwards baseball cap. ] [ And had to have his large penis out screaming obscenities such as: 'WHERE THE WHITE WOMEN SO I CAN RAPE THEM WITH MY BIG BLACK PENIS?!' ] [ Jackson shudders at the moment, slowly peeling himself off of the couch as he raises himself upward from the couch and begins his long trek upwards to the summit of long stairways. He sighs as the steps begin to grow bigger, longer in distance as Dante seems to be climbing a mountain of some sorts. Fire is swelling around him as he dodges a fiery shot from out of nowhere. ] Dante: What the hell? [ Soon, rain coulds form around him as he stops dead in his tracks, marvelling at the site in front of him. A voice booms from the heavens as Dante can only hold his current ground, timid with fear. ] Dante *gulp*: And you are? Voice: Well who the fuck do you think I fucking am? Such a fucking loser, I thought I was going to visit some cool motherfucker with fucking cloud bitches, but NO! Instead, I get stuck with some dumb token black guy who'll only keep telling me things like 'shit', 'damn', and 'cracker'. Dante: You still haven't told me who you are. Voice: Am I supposed to? Another Voice: Oh shut up, you're supposed to be a spiritual guide, not a hovering cloud. God, you've been around Hendrix haven't you? Dante: WILL SOMEBODY TELL ME WHERE THE BLUE HECK I AM?! [ A loud poof is heard as the former cloud happens to change into a short brown man with grayish hair dripping down past his neck. A short, stoppy smile is on his face as another figure appears. His hair tied in a pony tail and a joint sticking out of his mouth. Wearing a valor suit, he grumbles to himself, before punching the first spirit in the arm. ] Voice #1: Okay, I'm Abe and that over there running around like a jackass happens to be River. We happen to be - Dante: NO! YOU AREN'T MY CONSCIENCES! River: Of course NOT! [ Insert awkward stare right here, and right now. ] Dante: Erm, then who are you? River: Abe, explain it to Mr. Misunderstood Rapper here that we are somewhat more than what he thinks. Abe: Basically, we are your spiritual guides, unlike that guy for Robert whom keeps telling him to find underage girls. You know that your wife there, upstairs taking care of your kid happens to be nice, wanting right? Dante: Right. River: *hip* SHUT UPPA YO MOUTH! ANGER AND JEALOUSY WERE JUST TALKING ABOUT YA! ANGER WAS ALL LIKE, 'MAN, I HATE THAT OL' BUBBA FOOTED MUD DUCK ASS! HE'S A F'IN LOSER, A SORRY EXCUSE FOR A F'IN SPIRIT F'IN CRAPPY F'IN SHIT! I'MA GO SMASH ME SOME BUILDINGS F'IN BUDDY ASS GRASS CROCK CRAP THINGS LIKE SOME DARN HAMBURGER STANDS AND WHATEVER!' And then jealously was all *hip* like, 'MAN RIVER, WHY YOU GET ALL THE FAST CARS AND FLY LOOSE WOMEN?!' Abe: My name is Lincoln and I hit people wit sticks. [ Out of nowhere, a large bamboo like stick forms out of thin air and smacks River upside the head. River shrugs it off, before the stick smacks him hard in the face. ] River: Shit, I'm the evil *hip* freakin' spirit! I'm supposed to be hitting with sticks! Abe: I'm the vision of Abraham Lincoln, and you are the vision of River Phoenix - thus I'm cooler. [ Dante clears his voice.] Dante: Okay, so why am *I* here? Abe: You're here because you need a win, and the mayor of this place happened to want some funny out of you, not that usual 'Murder, She Wrote' type of thing as you usually do. River: Meaning, you need to stop all these series and get back to being stupid. Dante: HEY! I AIN'T STUPID! [ Dante pauses for a second, before realizing exactly what he said. ] Dante: I mean, I am not stupid. Jeez, bad grammar, BAD GRAMMAR! Abe: Yeah, most of all black people do, so don't count it against them. You know after your match with that Vampire, you got smacked around - YET AGAIN. River: He DID kick your ass bad. Dante: Alright, so I lost - why do you care? [ River and Abe shrug their shoulders as Dante paces back to the original walkway. Looking down, he sees that there is no walkway anymore, just a cloud of space that is visible only by spirits and creepy ghost people. Dante turns back, looking at River and Abe standing next to one another, not giving off any emotions, before a loud thump is heard overhead. Crash of lightning later and the two spirits and shaking, as the booming voice of MAYOR HOVERS ABOVE THEM! ] Voice of MAYOR: Are you two going to keep aruging or are you going to have a point doing this? I don't want to give the nervous system a self kick in the balls again now do I? Abe: You don't have to kick the ORIGINAL brain, now do you? Voice of MAYOR: Sorry, but you know we CAN'T THINK UP HERE WITH THAT WOMAN RUNNING AROUND! I BET MAYOR NUTS IS HAVING A HECK OF A TIME DOWN THERE! River: How the hell did I *hip* get under YOUR administration anyway? [ The Mayor sighs. ] Voice of MAYOR: The sperm vote, some how they got more than the blood cells. River: Damn you Jackson for having that KID! I could have been partying up in the pelvic area RIGHT NOW and been TEARING IT UP WITH HER FENOMES RIGHT NOW DARN IT! Voice of MAYOR: Well I could send you down there, but Jackson would have to die first. THUS killing you in the process. Abe: Damn, he has a point River. Dante: [ interrupting ] Alright dammit, now somebody tell me where we're going or I'm waking up. Abe, Voice of MAYOR, and River: NOOOOO! WHEN YOU WAKE UP WE END UP ORGASAMING ON COMPUTER PRON! Dante: Pron? I always thought it was Po- Abe: SHHHHH! That word is forbidden in the mind. [ Dante raises his eyebrows, in a 'oooh' kind of way before Abe snaps his fingers quietly causing Dante to grab his ears in obvious pain. River sighs to himself, before kneeing the former PCW Tag Team Champion in the face. Jackson slumps down, clutching his nose and in obvious pain. ] River: Get up, you're supposed to be making a Rush 'Greatest Hits' tape for yourself next Sunday? Dante [holding his nose]: Phy? Ps pit pecause pi pave po pace pim pon Padrenaline pon Punday? Abe: Whatever you said, yeah. River: Oh, and you team with a blind guy. Dante: 'SHIT!' River: See? I told you he'd start that. Dante: 'DAMN!' Abe: Yeah, but its The End, not Mark Amulet or Totally Tubular Tommy. Dante: 'CRAKCERS!' [ Both Abe and River smack their heads with much force as they take Dante by the arms, dragging him along imaginary pavement, skidding along the way. Short sparks of electricity form underneath Dante as he goes along, not saying a single word as Abe and River have a discussion over what would be better, a pussy or a weaking. On the outside however, Dante is sleeping on the couch as Tracy walks down the stairs, checking out if anything is new around the house. Sighing herself, she lays down as the audible snores from Dante can be heard even by his spirits. ] Real Dante: ZZZZZzzzzzz.... Abe: Loud frickin' sleeper isn't he? Tracy: Aw, he's sleepy weepy. I guess the holidays with my parents are going to be good for him then. [ RED BUZZER OF DOOM AND SILLY STUFF POPS UP! ] Abe: Oh dear god no. River: Her parents, even worse than Hank when he's on a winning streak, or Brandi with a workrate. Abe: ITS NOT THAT BAD! River: Have you ever seen her parents? They're worse than a Three Minute and Eight Second person gaining power. [The Xavier Kannon visage in Dante's mind pops up. ] Xavier Kannon's Visage: Somebody call me? Abe: Xavier, go back to owning your Internet Wrestling site. Xaiver Kannon: Wait a second, don't you think we're on the internet now? [ Eerie sounds come from as there is an awkward pause around all as Dante wakes up in the spirit world, shaking his head. ] All: NAH. Abe: Come on, let's go find some of those Rush moments River's been talking about.
