Escape from the Asylum

Date: not entered “Think you can move this piece of scrap metal any faster?” “I have the pedal to the floor! What was all that about??” “By the Void…can’t you humans stop being nosy for one frickin’ second??” The black SUV gunned out of the parking lot of the Rose Garden Arena in Portland, Oregon. They peeled rubber hard as they turned the corner and sped off down the street. Inside the car, Cyclonica sat in the passenger’s seat glaring at the driver…the black/violet haired Dr. Mikado Kisaragi!! “Come on, doc! I don’t have time to deal with those Asylum fools! If they turn this into a high speed chase, I’m going to throw you out the back to slow them down!” Mikado glared at her as she waved through the cars. “D@mn it, Cyclonica…I’m a doctor, not a getaway driver!” “Har dee frickin har, peon.” She turned around and looked down at the unconscious Anarchy, stretched across the back seat. Her arm hung over the side of the chair as her mouth hung open. The Divine Prophetess watched her breathe slowly…deep into a state of sleep. “What was going on back there?” Mikado asked, glancing back at Anarchy. “And who’s she?” “Mind your mortal business!” Cyclonica snapped at her. “You’re just here to check on Akiyama…and that’s all!” The good doctor looked at her with wide eyes. “Oh? So then that means I don’t have to drive then!” she released the wheel, sending the car screeching and swerving all over the road. Cyclonica turned her attention back up front and growled in surprise. “Okay!! All right! Drive the car! Before you kill her!” Mikado took the wheel again, just in time as they began careening into the path of an oncoming Mack truck. “What do you mean “Before you kill her”? I would die too you know!” she muttered under her breath angrily. Cyclonica rolled her eyes, her back pressed against the seat. “You, dear doctor, can be replaced. She…” she pointed back at the slumbering Anarchy, “…cannot. And as for me and the god…it would take more than a simple car crash to send us to the final Void.” “You at least owe me an explanation before I become an accessory to kidnapping.” She asked, turning left on the intersection. “She is a vital role for everything…” Cyclonica said, her voice going distant. “You…me…Akiyama…everything.” She looked back again when they bounced over a speed bump. The loud thud of the god’s head hitting the top of the SUV was sharp to the ears. He sat still in the back of the truck, his back pressed against the back of the backseat. Decked out in the full Void Armor, he was a sight to see as you drove by. Akiyama was always a sight to see…inside and outside of the ring. Inside that ring…he was a machine…a monster…a GOD in his realm where his servants were his opponents…and they praised his name with their cries of pain. They worshiped him by falling out cold on the mat. They served him…by finding defeat at his hands. Truly it was a rejoicing thing to be crushed by the gifted hands of the nVg… almost as if each enemy were the chosen one for the short amount of time they were in the ring. Just then…the car stopped. “What are you doing?” Cyclonica asked angrily, turning around and looking up ahead at the street. Mikado straightened her doctor’s jacket and folded her arms. “Red light.” She said in a matter of fact tone. Cyclonica groaned as she slammed her hands on the dashboard. “You’re stopping for a traffic light?? You’re in the process of doing the GOD’S WILL and you’re abiding to the LAWS OF MAN??” “I’m not getting pulled over for running a light! I don’t care what kind of “god” Akiyama is!” Mikado snapped back at the Divine Prophetess. Cyclonica shook her head as just then…groans began to rise up from the back seat. The two women in the front seat turned around to see the stirring Anarchy starting to sit up straight. “By the Void…she’s coming to!” “By the Void? Can’t you say Son of a b*tch like everyone else??” Anarchy looked up groggily as Cyclonica unfastened her seatbelt. “Wh…where am I?” “Nowhere…now go to sleep!” Cyclonica leaped over the seat into the backseat and started wrestling with Anarchy. Mikado gripped the wheel as the truck began rocking back and forth. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- A black and white squad car came rolling slowly up behind a black SUV. The driver, a hefty looking man with a blonde crew cut peered through the windshield at the back window of the car. “What’s that sitting in the back of that truck, Rollins?” The skinny officer looked ahead and shrugged his shoulders. “Er…looks like some sort of freak in a Halloween costume, sir” “That’s one huge freak, Rollins.” The driving cop stated. Then they sat there and watched as movement shook the car, followed by a series of rocking back and forth, to the left and right of the vehicle. The driving cop looked confused at the car in front of them, his brow wrinkling. “That car’s a rocking, Rollins.” He said, curiosity in his voice. “We had better not go a knocking, sir.” He replied, his nasal voice sounding concerned. “I’ll pull up and see what’s going on.” “Hold still you!” Cyclonica was halfway finished tying Anarchy’s hands together in front of her with a cord of rope. Already a bandana was tied around her mouth, holding her lips apart and gagging her. “Hurry up before someone sees you!” Mikado shouted, her eyes darting to her left and right. The Divine Prophetess straddled across Anarchy’s lap and pressed her shoulders down against the seat while she tied the rope. “Either you stay still or I’ll---!!” Everyone froze as the patrol car came pulling slowly up beside them. Cyclonica stared at them with wide eyes, while Anarchy looked at them upside down in surprise herself. Mikado looked the guiltiest of all, as she looked dead at them with fear. Rollins peered into the car hard. “What’s going on there Rollins?” he asked. He turned back to the driver and scratched his head. “Looks like two pretty girls wrestling in the backseat. And one of them has the other tied up and gagged at the mouth.” “I see…a 169.” He flipped a switch and pulled up in front of the car. “By the Void…” Cyclonica sighed. “Son of a b*tch.” Mikado muttered, shaking her head as she leaned forward against the steering wheel. The driving officer, whom we’ll call Ape because of his distinguishing features; stepped out of the car with Rollins following. They approached the driver’s side compartment and motioned for Mikado to roll the window down. “Yes officer?” she smiled weakly…but she knew she was in trouble. “Are you aware of the law you’re breaking at this moment in time, ma’am?” he asked, sounding professional. “I have some idea, sir.” She admitted ashamed. Ape turned to the backseat area and motioned for them to step out. “Come out there, ladies.” Begrudgingly, Cyclonica climbed off of Anarchy and stepped outside, pulling Anarchy with her. The two of them stood there and looked up at the officers, two black and blue haired women looking nearly identical. Rollins looked back and forth between Anarchy and Cyclonica confused. “Are you two sisters or something?” “What kind of dumbass question is that, Rollins?” Ape said, glaring back at him. “Of course not!” “What’s the trouble here, officer?” Mikado asked, warily. “Step out of the car, ma’am.” He asked. She shook her head and exhaled deeply as she complied. As soon as she stepped out, Ape turned all three of them around and pressed them against the side of the car. “You’re all under arrest. You have the right to remain silent.” “Ow! What are we arrested for?” Mikado asked as Anarchy growled through her bandana gag. Ape smirked. “169…moving violation of the lesbian nature.” “WHAT??” Cyclonica and Anarchy both turned around, their eyes glaring angrily into the tall officer. “Are you stupid or something? That’s not even a crime!!” “It is here in Portland, ma’am!” he replied. Then he turned to Rollins and pointed towards the rear of the vehicle. “Rollins get that freaky looking thing out of the back and see what’s going on. It hasn’t budged since we got here, so it must not be alive.” Anarchy reached up with her bound hands and pulled the bandana from her mouth. “You caught them red handed in kidnapping me and you’re arresting us for alleged homo sex in a moving car???” “Put that gag back in!” Cyclonica snapped. “No! It tastes like axle grease!” Cyclonica put her hands on her hips and waited. “Well excuse me for forgetting the jawbreaker ball gag back at the hotel! I figured you’d keep quiet long enough before we got back and tied you up properly!” “SIR!!” Rollins’ voice wailed out from the back of the van. Ape rushed towards the trunk, where Rollins was hovering in the air as the hand of Akiyama held him by his throat! Ape whipped out his nightstick and raised it to swing, but Akiyama threw Rollins straight into his nightstick! Both men go tumbling to the floor as the nVg rose out from the trunk. Ape stood back up and threw a punch, but Akiyama grabbed him and plunged him back first through the rear window of the SUV!! Mikado held her head in her hands. “Augh…my car!” Rollins climbed to his feet and stared in shock at the monster standing before him. The nVg began turning away from him…but then surged out and backhanded the rookie across the street. Cyclonica shook her head as she rushed to the god’s side. “Get back in the car…we have to get ready for the procedure.” Akiyama slowly turned and stepped into the crushed trunk of the car, pushing officer Ape to the side and closing the glassless trunk door down behind him. Mikado sighed as she climbed back into the driver’s seat and mumbled. “I’m getting too old for this sh*t…” “Quit squawking, doc.” Cyclonica snapped at her as the car started. “Yeah, you’ve been complaining the whole time!” Anarchy said, leaning against the car. “If you’re going to be a part of this kidnapping at least put your heart into it!” She turned then to face Cyclonica, who stood there with her hands on her hips. Anarchy sighed as she slipped the gag back in her mouth and crawled back into the backseat of the car. Cyclonica nodded in satisfaction. “Thought so.” And with that done…the black SUV peeled off into the night…escaping from any purple hearses or police cars that could be on their tail.
