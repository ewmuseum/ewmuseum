XK07 : “All In.”

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/08/02 XK07 : “All In.” Location : The Gaming Table.Date : 18th August, 8pm. Many a man who had it all lost everything by believing in the notion he could obtain just that little more. Many a poor man lost what little he had through attempting a realisation of his dream. Many a hard working man fell into a spiral of debt by using what he had to try and win more. Gambling is human nature. We do it every day. But the stakes are usually low. “Your cards sir.” The face of Xavier Kannon carries it’s usual shroud of seriousness and arrogance... The corners of his mouth and widths of his eyes being the only things that break through the mask.. His smugness protruding. “Thank you.” His right hand presses the cards onto the Green felt of the gambling table.... The small area of lit colour surrounded by a sea of Black where figures can be seen moving, but not seen... Everything, including the focus, is on the table. The cards, with their simple dark Blue pattern remain under the Scientologist’s palm.... They have no value... Their identities aren’t known. “Now, to see what will be.” The bright, piercing Green irises of Kannon’s eyes lose territory in a quickly fought battle with his dilating pupils.... His palm sliding towards him and pulling up the corners of his five cards to see what they hold. A man like Xavier has no poker face... No matter how hard he tries, if he even does, to mask his emotion it flies on his face like a flag... The first thing people see being his feelings... His persona. Smugness. Arrogance. Disdain. Contempt. “Raise you....” A gravely, deep voice speaks from the ether... No body, no direction... Only a voice and the words Xavier wanted to hear. “... The position of CEO.” In front of where Kannon holds his cards there is a clinking. A bunch of keys to the PCW offices land on the Green.... The keys to the CEO’s office and all that is in it. The keys to the engine of PCW. Xavier nods slowly, his head taking an eternity to carry out it’s journey of a few inches.... His eyes never leaving the metallic glare of the keys as his head carries out it’s arc. “I see your CEO position....” The keys are joined by another set.... Landing right on them to give a series of metallic chinks that seem to echo for an eternity. The keyrings overlap and those for the CEO’s office are replaced by the garish Pink with Silver stars pattern of Ellie~!’s keys. Keys that open the Commissioner’s office... The key to match making. “... And raise you 50% of PCW.” Casually the Gold Patron Meritorious leans back, running his hand through his long Red hair while throwing a document onto the table with his unseen hand. A document that carries 2 names and one significance. Xavier Kannon. The Phantom Booker. Half of PCW. “Sure you’re up for these high stakes?” Xavier knows it is a rhetorical question. As he asks the question he raises and eyebrow and chuckles. A gesture to show he is both confident in victory, and a victory that will win him more than is on the table. “I see your 50%....” 2 sides to every story. 2 enemies fighting for it all. 2 Documents now on the table. Over the names of Xavier and the Phantom Booker is Tyler Nelson. 100% ownership of PCW on the table, as close as close can be. The whole of the company together for the first time since it was taken by the creditors. “And raise you.... Your salvation.” Kannon blinks. The stakes may have seemed high already but is this something that Xavier is willing to gamble? While all around the world there are bad people calling out to be saved.... Screaming in the night to be helped.... For their souls to be saved. Kannon is not one of these... As long as there is profit in it then Xavier has nothing to repent for. Dropping on top of the documents and keys is a still beating heart.... The atrioles and ventricles still pumping. The heart is tinted Black. The stakes are high but he is not willing to end the game without a fight.... Or the fight without a game. He has an Ace in the hold. “I call you.” A shadow is cast over the already Blackened heart as a Purple leather strap housing an elegant Gold plate is dropped onto the top of the pile... A beacon that shines bright above the dark heart on which it rests. The PCW World Title. CEO position. Commissioner position. 100% ownership of PCW. Salvation or lack there of. The PCW World Title. The stakes are high and the winner will soon be shown... Both knowing what they have to fight with to win what is before them. “Time to see what you have....” Kannon’s eyes lock on the centre of the Green table... A bright spotlight bleaching the colour from the fabric. A hand reaches into the illuminated circle and places 3 cards face down before it turns the cards over. Darkstar. Syko. Clyde. “Three Dark Knights...” Again the hand reaches in and turns his last 2 cards over to reveal 2 of the very object that sits on the table. “And a pair of World Titles.” The hand slides from the circle of light, only leaving the 5 cards there for Xavier to keep his eyes on. A strong hand. Not much can beat it..... Unless... “Hahahahaha...” The Scientologist chuckles to himself as he places his 5 cards face down opposite those already there and his hand, housing his wedding ring moves over the first and turns it over. “Hallmark.” Then turns over the second. “Cover.” The third. “One...” The fourth. “Two...” The fifth. “Three.” With another chuckle his hand moves towards the prizes and Xavier pulls them towards him, taking the Black heart and casually tossing it off the table. “I do believe that wins.” In Xavier’s hand he tosses the keys about in his hand before scooping up everything and looking across the table into the darkness... Victory painted in his eyes and sculpted on his face. “Been fun playing you.” The biggest gambles sometimes.... Just sometimes.... Pay off.
