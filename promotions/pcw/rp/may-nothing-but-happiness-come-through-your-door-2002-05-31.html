May Nothing But Happiness Come Through Your Door

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31st May After an hellacious streetfight with Flaymin Aymin at the last pay-per view, El Diablo has taken many weeks off to recuperate. It's quite understandable why too, he was dropped 20feet into a dumpster, had his body wrapped in barbed wire and very nearly blinded, but he somehow prevailed which put the Aymin/Diablo saga to rest. During that torrid two and half month spell of bitter hatred between the two men, Diablo lost everything, his wife, his life and even his mind. But now that he is rested and raring to go what is next in the chapter of El Diablo? El Diablo sits silently in the study of what once was his and Angel's home, only now there is an eerie emptiness to it, which would only be filled by her presence. Inside the study are many shelves full of books, a desk with a computer on it, and many old newspapers stored underneath the desk. The purpose of them is unknown, yet somehow reflect upon how El Diablo recent past has passed and now eludes him. The study is very small, but still has a window to look out of. The weather seen from outside the window is that of rain. The raindrops crash against the window hard yet does not receive El Diablo's attention. The study light is on, bringing a brightness to the room that seems unwanted by the figure sitting cross-legged on an old tie-dye rug in the middle of the room. He looks like he would rather crawl into a hole than suffer anymore than he has in his life and especially the last few months. As history will show it, Flaymin Aymin has practically crushed the world that El Diablo was used to and perhaps even killed a part within him. No place to go and see his dead brother anymore, no loving wife to come home to anymore, nothing worth living for anymore. Sure, the victory over Aymin gave him hope and still does, but he will never be the same again. Diablo raises his right hand and touches his face with it, another realisation of what Aymin has taken away, his once most treasured possession is now charred and is unable to be worn by him due to the match stipulations. A dramatic change over two months would be hard for anybody, but of El Diablo's mind frame it is a devastating blow that he will have to try and lift himself up from. El Diablo runs his hand across his forehead and feels the seventeen stitches that were required then runs the same hand through his thick black hair, which is now torn in parts and still all the blood has not come out from it. The eight stitches on his left cheek and five on his right still remain as well as the heavy bandaging on his back and chest. He was willing to put his life on the line to get that victory, and thankfully he did, but now El Diablo must move on and he knows that. However he still remains curled up on the floor, looking a shell of his former self, just wearing a vest and black jogging pants. He looks to be in serious need of a work out. The weather outside slowly intensifies, with large gusts of the window crashing against the window and the rain pouring down heavier. 'What have I done?' asks El Diablo to himself softly, 'I've brought shame upon my brother by losing my link to him,' he finishes. El Diablo slowly makes his way to his feet, and still in question of everything. 'I risk everything to defeat Flaymin Aymin and get fired later on that night, what the hell is up with that?' he shouts loudly, but of course no one is there, he is only in his own presence. Slowly he wanders around the room in thought. Not regular thought, the kind he'll speak out loud, afterall no one else is around, and as the saying goes If a tree falls down in the woods and there is no one around to hear it, does it make a sound? 'If El Diablo thinks aloud, is he not thinking' or something like that. 'I'm a director now,' he mutters to himself, 'What the hell is that?' he sighs, 'At least it's a career I can keep after wrestling,' he comments with little enthusiasm. From nowhere some music starts to play, it takes El Diablo by surprise, who ducks down in case anyone else is around. But, he soon realises the music is coming from his computer. Diablo quickly gets to his feet and walks over to the computer monitor, and looks over it carefully. Mogwai- Tracy It says on the Windows Media Player. But how it started to play is unknown, but El Diablo for once doesn't care as the happy-sad tune hits him. It sets upon a realisation on him, and makes him ponder what is occurring in his life and how it needs fixing. He turns his head and looks at the window, with the rain still dripping down the glass. El Diablo leaves the music playing and walks over to the window ledge and sits down, placing the side of his head against the window. He watches as each raindrop slowly slides down the glass and drips off of it. 'My future is in my own hands,' he whispers, 'The future should begin this Fusion, and the future should begin with an El Diablo direction,' the Mexican Devil speaks louder. The music from the computer remains playing in the background to these spoken thoughts and set the tone of all the emotions going through El Diablo at this turning point in his life. From all the recent lows, he needs to make highs, from all of the heartache he needs to find anew. He is at a crossroad and needs to pick the right road to go upon. 'I've made many mistakes in my life, but now I'm here to rectify those errors,' he states loudly while again standing up from the window ledge he was seated at. He looks all around him in the room, as the music still plays through the speakers of the computer. But behind him, the rain is ceasing and the sun is beginning to shine through. 'I'm going to do something I never thought I would, and I will have it all planned soon enough,' El Diablo promises himself as he half smiles. He takes one step forward. 'That step is how my career will go from now on,' he states with a passion, only equalled by the melody of the song playing in the background from the computer. El Diablo turns around and takes a look out of the window. A rainbow now stands where the rain once dropped. 'Perfect!' is all he can state. The emotion filled music slowly fades away as does the scene of serenity in El Diablo's life.
