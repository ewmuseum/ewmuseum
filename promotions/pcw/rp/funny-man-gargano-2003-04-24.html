Funny Man Gargano

24 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 24, 2003 Relationships are tough to build, but this doesn't seem like a factor for the duo of John Gargano and Tyler Preston. After striking up an agreement to work together to take over the entertainment business, they have found each other to be great company. As Tyler preps John for his upcoming wrestling movie, John still holds fast to his promise of giving Tyler a small part in the movie. This relationship isn't purely business however, for they always find enough time just to hang around. Tyler and his beautiful girlfriend, Kalli, stand outside the trashy comedy club known as the 'Laugh Factory'. Paint chips litter the brick walls and sidewalk around them. Kalli's radiance can still be detected even when she is decked out in jeans and a sweatshirt. Tyler dresses up a little bit with his lime green button down shirt and black dress pants, to continue making a good impression on the comedy club that he has already won over. Tyler taps his silver watch on his right wrist to make sure the time is correct. He sighs a deep breathe, still waiting for John Gargano to arrive and fulfill their previously discussed meeting. He calms down and cracks his neck with his right hand. To pass the time, he makes awkward small talk with his girlfriend, who is still somewhat at odds with him after she informed him that she is on the verge of getting a divorce, nonetheless, very married. “Did you find a lawyer yet? Do yourself a favor though; don’t spend too much on a small divorce case. I’m not a master of law, but that just seems stupid,” Tyler offers as advice to accompany his question. It is a feeble attempt to make small talk while he waits for his good friend. He twiddles his thumbs, nervous to make eye contact during the speaking of this touchy subject. “No, I made calls for two hours straight this afternoon. Apparently lawyers in New York City are too big for small divorce cases,” Kalli replies with not a lot of hope in her voice. She knows that this particular subject makes Tyler feel awkward, and still feels incredible remorse for the pain she has caused him. “Just keep trying, I’m sure if worse comes to worse, I’ll be able to dig up someone. Let’s face it, anyone in the entertainment business has to have a lawyer ready, people are really touchy these days,” Tyler says with a chuckle, thinking back to past instances when his comedy act has gotten a little to racy for a few patrons. He wouldn’t take those occurrences back for anything. “I bet! I’ll keep trying, but Al set the court date for the first of March. That is one of my busier months, but I agreed just because I want to put this whole mess behind me. Although, I can’t help but think that he chose that time on purpose,” Kalli explains, informing Tyler on the date, in hopes that he will come out to the court house a support her despite a few hostile feelings. Tyler’s mind drifts off to Al Sutton, the man that has been occupying his love for all this time. “This guy sounds like a real piece of work. You did the right thing though. The sooner that you are no longer Kalli Sutton, the better,” Tyler says with a smile and a small wink. He is hurt, there is no doubt about that, but he has attempted to adopt a more laid back way of life. After all, he is in the Big Apple. It won’t do him any good to worry about somewhat trivial matters that don’t concern him. “Believe me, I was never truly Kalli Sutton,” Kalli says, solidifying her obvious stance that her previous marriage was just short of a all out disaster. “Come on, John told me that he would be here at eight o’clock and it’s already half past. He better not of stopped for pizza on the way over. The last thing I need, is tubby arriving with greasy fingers and sauce all around his face,” Tyler jokes, consciously trying to get off the subject of Kalli’s marital status. Kalli follows with the flow, and heeds Tyler’s words with a small chuckle. Conveniently right after the bitter joke, John Gargano comes sprinting from right side of the ‘Laugh Factory’. He ends his marathon at Tyler’s feet where he hunches over and breathes deep intakes of air repeatedly. His thick black hair dangles down in bunches, and gathers hints of perspiration. Tyler smiles and slaps John on the back. The weight of Tyler’s hand causes John to hunch down even further. His face comes close to the sidewalk. He finally lifts his head and reveals his glistening face to the duo that he has met with. Kalli looks on as Tyler begins to nudge him in a joking manner. “Where the heck have you been ‘Old Man Gargano’? Jumping rope?” Tyler asks, attempting to hold back the laughter that is bound to make its departure form his mouth. He didn’t instruct Gargano to do any sort of work out. It makes him have an excited, yet fainting idea that John might have taken it into his own hands and started to work out on his own. “No man, but let me tell you, parking on the East Side of this city is a mother,” John says in staccato intervals in between each deep breath. “I had to park about a mile and a half that way, and I was late to begin with so I picked up the pace a little bit,” he continues, beginning to catch his breathes. He wipes his forehead with the back of his hand and stands upright. In the back of his mind he worries about his luxury car that sits unattended a few miles away. “You ARE out of shape my friend. A mile and a half shouldn’t make you feel like you just had open heart surgery without any drugs to put you out,” Tyler continues, keeping the mood light. He tries not to allow John to realize that the whole reason he sprinted over was so Tyler would not be inconvenienced by his tardiness. “I guess we have more work to do on me then we thought,” John joins in the ridiculing of himself by giving a small jab. He knows that he isn’t a perfectly fit specimen, yet he highly doubts that he is a lazy slob like everyone makes him out to be. “Anyway, John, you know Kalli I am assuming?” Tyler asks, trying to not leave Kalli out in the dark. After all, this does count as a date, and he doesn't want Kalli to think that he is losing his romantic touch. It’s just been put on hold for the present moment. “Of course, we met a few weeks ago,” John recalls. John proves that at least his brain if functioning at its fullest. He nods at Kalli with a sort of silent greeting. Kalli nods back and gives off one of her full-teeth, breath-taking smiles. John looks away, acting as if he is looking at something on the ground. He doesn’t want Tyler to think that he is gaining an interest in her. “I’m ready to go in. I ran all the way over here, so this act better be good Tyler,” John warns as a joke on his own behalf. He begins walking to the doors of the ‘Laugh Factory’. Tyler doesn’t follow him, because there is a little detail that he negated to tell his counter part. John pulls the door open and Tyler walks partially in, but then hits Gargano with the news as John stares at him in utter confusion. “Actually John, I’m not doing an act tonight. I guess there has been a change in plans you could say,” Tyler admits, knowing that it isn’t a change in plans, rather just a secret held inside. John looks at him in confusion. He follows Tyler and Kalli into the entryway of the ‘Laugh Factory’. “If you’re not doing an act, then why did I sprint to get here?” Asks John, wondering that himself. He doesn’t exactly enjoy strenuous activity, and he’s starting to regret the fact that he spent his energy in that fashion. “John, welcome to ‘Open Microphone Night’ at the ‘Laugh Factory’. I hope you brought your funny shoes with you, because you are going to try your hand at the comedy business,' Tyler reveals, almost as if it is a surprise that John will find as pleasant. The three individuals stand at the back of the club, behind the tables and chairs that are half full at this point with giggling patrons who sip on drinks. A small African-American man wearing a fancy black suit with a baby blue vest screeches in to the microphone on stage. The sight of this solidifies the fact to John that it is truly ‘Open Microphone Night’. “Wait just a second, you want me to get on stage and tell jokes?” John asks, as if that were not a question with an obvious answer. Kalli chuckles as she sort of ducks behind Tyler’s shoulder. Tyler puts on a serious face and pretends as if he doesn’t know why John would have objections. Although, Tyler knows that John wouldn’t go for such a thing, but he truly believes that the struggling actor should expand his horizons. “Well sure, I came to the movie set for you,” Tyler points out, hoping that John will see the sacrifices that Tyler has made for him. John thinks, and does realize that Tyler has done many things for him. Then the notion hits John that he is not funny in the least bit. He doesn’t think it will work. “Tyler, I can’t tell a joke to save my life. This wouldn’t go by very well, and if Mick heard about this, he would drop me in a second,” John points out. Mick is a very demanding agent, and if John were to make a fool of him in public on a stage, than John’s hypothesis may prove to be true. Tyler shakes off the thought of Mick. Tyler doesn’t see eye-to-eye with the agent. The fact that he could get John to do this act in spite of Mick adds an extra incentive. “Screw Mick, that guy doesn’t give you the time of day anyway,” Tyler points out with a very valid point. John thinks it over and finds that Tyler is somewhat correct, and in some other ways he is not. He turns to Tyler to speak his mind about his agent, but he looks up to find just Kalli standing there with a hidden smile on her face. Her shoulders bob up and down as she keeps the laughter inside. John is very confused as to where Tyler went, and why Kalli is having such a chuckle. His answers come to him when he looks up to find Tyler standing on the stage with a microphone in hand. He smiles and flips back his streaky blonde hair. He adjusts the collar of his red shirt to make himself have more of a stage presence.John slumps down in the corner. He tries to think that Tyler is a bigger man then to embarrass him in public, but deep down in his gut, he knows that Tyler is about to expose him. Tyler shows no remorse as he screams into the microphone to a dull crowd and introduces his plans for tonight. Anyone could tell that Tyler is a fixture at this club, because he speaks with confidence and poise. “Ladies and gentlemen, the action is going to come to a head tonight as I introduce to you one of the club’s biggest prospects. You may have heard of him, and you can catch him in his upcoming movie “To the Ring and Back”. The guy is going to split your sides so give it up for John Gargano,” Tyler says, altering John’s image a little bit to make him sound credible to the club. Tyler points back to John as a spotlight illuminates his face. The patrons sitting at the tables look back at him. John has no choice but to go on the stage. He flips on an almost automatic fake smile and saunters to the stage. He climbs up the stairs and takes the microphone from an almost historical Tyler Preston. Tyler jumps off the stage quickly to leave John all alone. The sweat stains that were beginning to fade start to come back with vengeance and soil his dark blue shirt. He stares blankly at the audience. The bright lights cause him to squint, and make him look paler then he actually is in his nervousness. He fumbles for the right words. He figures that whatever he says, it has to be remotely funny. He digs in the database of jokes that he has learned over the years, but is unsuccessful at finding one. He speaks in a squeaky voice to buy him some time. “Um, hey everyone, how are you doing tonight? Tyler just said, I’m John Gargano, and I’ll be in a movie that will be hitting a little later this year. It’s a wrestling flick, and it should be a good one for wrestling fans and non-wrestling fans alike,” John explains. The entire audience looks at him in a bored and drowsy look. John picks up Tyler who is standing in the back with his arm around Kalli making the motion that he should say something that is at least interesting. It is at this time that Tyler figures this may have been a mistake. “You know, the thing about wrestling is that it’s kind of a homosexual sport. I mean what other sport can you grab a man’s half-naked ass and get away with it? Well, we’re talking American sports, we won’t venture off in to the crazy stuff that the French do,” John says with a nervous smile, uncertain of what the reaction of the audience may be. To his surprise, he scores healthy amounts of laughs, to go with the non-stop laugh coming from the back in Tyler Preston. Even though it’s a joke against wrestling, Tyler still finds humor in John’s attempts. “Seriously, what’s up with French people? They invent a single kiss with tongue and suddenly they think they are God’s gift to the world?” John says with more confidence. He smiles erases when barely anyone in the room laughs. Even he admires his own courage that got him onto the stage, but now he is thinking that he might have bit off more than he can chew. “So what do we do to screw them? We call French Fries, Freedom Fries. Wow ladies and gentlemen, I’ll be surprised if there is one single French citizen that can sleep after us Americans pulled that one on them. Holy cow,” John says in a sarcastic voice, thinking that the words on the French will be found humorous after the most recent controversies in the world. He is correct as quite a few laughs are heard. An evil grin spreads over John’s face as an idea pops in his head. Tyler watches from the back, and a lump gather in his throat, adopting the notion that this idea may involve jokes at his expense. “Really people, I want to thank Tyler Preston for bringing me out here. He’s right there in the back with his lovely girlfriend Kalli. In fact, I have a few pictures of this duo, and it’s in a position that you may find pleasing. I guess it pays to sit in the closet at the Ramada Inn hotel!” John points the duo out, as their cheeks grow rosy with embarrassment. Tyler races to the stage as John fumbles in his wallet to find the pictures in which he spoke of. Tyler acts fast, not wanting to drag Kalli in to this prank war that has seemed to rise up between the two friends. Tyler snatches the microphone from John and pushes him off the stage. John just laughs, finding pleasure in the fact that his bluff got him what he wanted. He winks at Kalli who is almost hiding her face in her sweatshirt. “That’s about enough of John Gargano ladies and gentlemen. The next one up is Kyle Krandel, please give him a hand people,” Tyler says with an awkward smile, attempting to get the new act on and John off as soon as possible. Tyler doesn’t want to look like a fool on his own turf. He walks to the back where John is seated with a smug smile. Tyler chuckles at the mastery of his plan. The next act walks out and begins speaking but his voice is muffled from the distance that is between him and Tyler’s group in the back. Tyler shakes his blonde head at John as to say ‘now it’s on’. Both John and Tyler, even though it may seem as if they are going at each other’s throat trying to one up the other, find enjoyment in their little game. They are starting to feel at home in the big city that they once thought would swallow them alive.
