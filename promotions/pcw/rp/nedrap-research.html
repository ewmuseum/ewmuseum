NedRap: Research!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: By a paralysed monkey. [Slowly our camera fades in, into the beautiful halls, of the Glasgow City library. An unlikely setting for Andy Murray I know, but I’m sure our hero has an explanation, or better yet, a reason. We pan around the shelves of books once, the people here are few and far between, and most appear to be stereotypical library-goers. You know the type, big glasses and all? Our cameras come to rest at a set of tables, all are unoocupied, apart from one. An unknown figure sits, a large atlas covering his face, he appears to be flicking through the pages.] Unknown Man: Hoho! [What a way to give the goose away. The man is of course, Andy Murray, confirmed as he lowers the book down, placing it on the table. Today, our hero is dressed in a nice new blue & white Kappa shell suit … Very nice! As we focus in on the page that he is viewing, we see a map of South America.] Andy Murray: Hoho … They countries have got pure crap names min! [Andy continues to examine the page, when Rab walks into the scene, hands full of books, setting the books down on the table, Rab rests on hand on it, leaning and breathing heavily. Rab is wearing a matching blue and white Kappa shell suit.] Rab: Ho, what you doin’ min? [Andy seems to be paying no attention, as he looks down joyfully at the countries with stupid names. Andy then quickly points one out to Rab.] Andy Murray: Look at this one min … Uragay … That’s pure class by the way. Rab: What sort of a chube wid call there country that? Andy Murray: I don’t ken, but it’s pure brilliant. Rab: Aye. [Ahem, yes … That of course was Uruguay.] Andy Murray: Brazil, that’s pure got BRA in it min! Rab: HOOF MAN! [Andy turns the page, onto a map of Africa, examining carefully for stupid names. However, most of the names he cannot read properly (says a lot for the Scottish school system), so instead, he just cusses.] Andy Murray: What pish … Ho, did ye pure get those books? [Andy looks up at Rab … Of course he has the books.] Rab: Aye. Andy Murray: So have they books pure got stuff about Rap music an’ that in them? Rab: Aye, well, I think so … Should do anyway, if they dinnae we’re pure gonnae hammer that library person by the way. Andy Murray: Aye … Lets get started then, min. [Rab pulls up a seat across from Andy, and takes a book from the top of the pile. Andy also takes a book, an English dictionary. Obviously he has no idea what the hell a dictionary is used for. It looks like Rab has a copy of George Orwell’s Animal Farm … How ironic.] Andy Murray: Hmmm … [Looking at the various words and definitions in the dictionary, Andy flips through, reaching the letter ‘R’. He flips a further page, and looks through it, using his index finger as a guide, until he find the word ‘Rap’ accompanied with definition.] RapFunction: intransitive verbEtymology: perhaps from [^1]rapDate: 1929Inflected Form(s): rapped; rap.ping1 : to talk freely and frankly2 : to perform rap musicSee ‘Gangsta Rap’ for further definition. [Andy scratches his head, he pretty much knows that to Rap, is to perform Rap music, so he quickly consults ‘Gangsta Rap’ in the dictionary.] Gang.sta RapPronunciation: 'ga[ng]-st&-Function: nounDate: 1991: rap music with lyrics explicitly relating to urban gangs, violence, drug use, and the degradation of women [Again, Andy looks a little confused, until eventually he nods his head, and realises what it all means. He turns the book to face Rab, he looks up.] Andy Murray: Read that, min. Rab: A’right. [Using his finger as a guide, Rab slowly reads through it, before a confused look spreads across his face. Andy takes the book back, expecting some sort of reply from Rab, which of course, doesn’t come.] Andy Murray: Well? Rab: What’s that pish? Andy Murray: Och! [Andy shakes his head.] Andy Murray: It pure tells us what we have to dae for this Rap thing. Rab: Oohhh. Andy Murray: Apparently, we have tae perform rap music ‘bout gangs, violence, drugs, drugs, and making fun of birds min! Rab: Hoho! [Rab rubs his hands together in anticipation, and chuckles.] Rab: This is gonna be pure mad, mental min! Andy Murray: Aye! [Rab gets back to reading his book, Andy looks across.] Andy Murray: What’s that pish yer reading? Rab: I’m nae sure … It’s pure crap though. [Rab closes the book, and throws it over his head, it lands on another table with a loud bang. There is a sudden silence throughout the library.] Librarian (In the distance): Quiet please! [Rab turns in his chair, and … ] Rab: GONNAE NO TELL ME TAE SHUT UP!? OR AH’M GONNAE PURE HAMMER YOU, A’RIGHT MATE!? [There is no reply.] Andy Murray: That outta tell tha chube. Rab: Aye. [Idiots, you don’t shout in libraries.] Andy Murray: So when are we gonnae go sing this rap thing? Rab: Nae sure min. Andy Murray: Probably sometime after this Gold & Glory thing that I’ve gotta be in … I’m gonna be in a pure famous match by the way. [Andy looks at Rab, expecting a comment on Rush Mason, obviously Rab has no idea who Andy is facing.] Andy Murray: Ho! I’m pure facing Rush Mason, by the way. Rab: Rush Mason … WHO ARE YE!? Andy Murray: He pure can’t here you, ya chube, he’s o’er in that place called America. Rab: Oh … Andy Murray: But he pure slagged off Scotland … Rab: What a bampot. [Andy nods in agreement, slamming his book shut.] Andy Murray: I ken, I mean, how can that chube make fun ‘o bonnae Scotland, when he’s fae America? America is so crap, that it pure makes Urugay look like a real country. He thinks that ah’m confusing? Well at least I can pure speak proper, ya chube, I bet Rush cannae even say ‘Ah’m gonnae pure hammer you’ proper! Rab: Aye! Andy Murray: And he thinks that he’s gonnae beat me? … BAW BAG! He could’nae beat Kunta Kinte! And who are they Malice Crew people? They’re pure chubes by the way … My mate Dave once told me, that Rush Mason, he pure likes Rap music, well hows he gonnae like it when NedRap hits the scenes!? [Rab nods his head in agreement. There is a pause between the two momentarily.] Rab: Och min, this is pure pish … Lets go down the chippae, then off down to the Barras or sumthin’ Andy Murray: Aye, I pure need some food min. [And … Fade!]
