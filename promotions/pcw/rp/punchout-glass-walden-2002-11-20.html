PunchOut- Glass Walden!

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: PunchOut- Glass Walden! Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: 11/20 Mike Tyson's Punch-Out! One of the greatest games for the Nintendo Entertainment System, many a person sat in the 80s wondering just how the *)^&*^ he was going to beat Mike Tyson. Keep in mind, this is Mike Tyson BEFORE getting raped by Lennox Lewis. Anyway, despite the NES being long dead, it has been rediscovered by one Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid. After using Duck Hunt to defeat Syko the Goblin and advance in the Cruiserweight Title Tournament, he shall use Punch-Out to work his way through several of his key opponents at Alliances. First up? Glass Joe. The Tim Walden of Punch-Out, except, at zero wins, ol' Joe has a better win-loss record. But still...does this mean that The HIT can take Tim Joe.....err Glass Walden....err, you get the idea....can Trapezoid take him lightly? The answer is most likely a resounding 'no.'.. ::The HIT sits in a hotel room, his Nintendo hooked up to his television illegally. He relaxes on his king sized bed, one hand dipping into a bag of Fritos, the other hitting start on his controller rapidly to get him through the start screens.:: The HIT: Well, here goes....my epic quest for Alliances begins here and now. In Mike Tyson's Punch Out lie those with remarkable likenesses to several of my Alliances foes...and I shall learn how to defeat them in real life, by defeating them in the virtual world! And first up is good old Glass Joe, the rough equivalent of Tim Walden. I mean, they have no idea what winning is! Glass Joe even lost to Super Punch Out's Gabby Jay! That's just like Walden losing four times to Flaymin Aymin! PATHETIC!! :Trapezoid smiles, starting up the battle with Glass Joe, as the battle screen appears: Glass Joe: 'This is my last match! I'm too old for fighting!' The HIT chuckles slightly.. The HIT: Come on Walden, bring it. Your damned punches couldn't dent an egg! Also true to life...I mean come on! The man had difficulty defeating BOCA DEL INFERNO, master of the dreaded penis grab! Although, half the reason Walden was having such a hard time was he was probably enjoying it! This is going to be a KO! In short time indeed! :The round begins, as the Trapezoid dodges a quick Joe jab, and fires off a series of jabs to the chin. Joe winds up, however, and catches Little Mac hard in the face with a hook! Trapezoid is stunned, as Glass Joe follows with a rare left jab to the face, but his hook is dodged, as Mac hits more punches...: The HIT: Damn.....this Glass Joe's harder than I remember. Despite being an incredibly easy adversary, I just can't take the bastard for granted! ::Glass Joe steps back, thumping his chest, before stepping in and clocking Mac with a vicious hook! Little Mac totters on the brink of defeat, before stunning jJe with a shot to the gut!: The HIT: That punk bastard almost beat me! Well, not anymore! With 1:30 to go in this round, it's time for a MOTHER-FUCKING KO! ::HIT unleashes Little Mac's Star Uppercut, CATCHING GLASS JOE UNDER THE CHIN! Glass Joe totters, and falls to the mat.:: The HIT: DAMN RIGHT BITCH! YOU GO DOWN AND STAY THERE! JUS' LIKE WALDEN! DON' START NUTTIN, DON' BE NUTTIN! As Trapezoid dances around, GLASS JOE GETS TO HIS FEET AT EIGHT! The HIT: YOU STUPID PRICK! STAY DOWN! WHY THE HELL WON'T YOU STAY DOWN! :A PISSED OFF HIT stuns Joe again, and delivers ANOTHER CRUSHING STAR UPPERCUT! This time, Mario reaches the ten count, as Trapezoid celebrates...:: The HIT: Damn.....he pushed me to the limit. But that's always the way it is with Walden. Despite the fact that he's been mocked and ridiculued for many things, he's a dangerous son of a gun in that ring. He showed on Tuesday he's got a lot of suplexes, and then he has the Change of No Evasion Syndrome Plans, which, yes, I've tapped to before. And now, that we finally meet one on one..even I have to admit I have some doubts about the PCW Cruiserweight Title match.. :Trapezoid drops the controller, as Little Mac continues celebrating on the screen...: The HIT: But Tim......I will be coming at you with everything I have. What started as a grudge against the Front Office has erupted into some serious bad blood between you and I! You hate me....and I hate you. And it looks like that's how its gonna be. For the first time in a damn long while, the PCW Cruiserweight Championship isn't going to be some jobber title two lowcarders are fighting over! It's going to be a match between me......AND ONE LOWCARDER FIGHTING FOR IT! But still....at least it's not Mandrake and Trigger! Walden! And anyone else at Alliances, since I'm only fighting like the entire roster twice.....I'M GONNA BEAT YOU DOWN! RIGHT INTO THE GRASS! I'M GONNA SMACK YOU ROUND! I'M GONNA KICK YO' ASS! :Trapezoid walks out of the room, leaving the still celebrating Mac on screen. Fade to erasers.:: ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
