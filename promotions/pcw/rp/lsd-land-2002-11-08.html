LSD Land?

8 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-11-02 Bob Vurdo hasn't left his room since he got home after Rosanna committed suicide. He hasn't eaten and hasn't been able to sleep so he has just been laying on the bed keeping to himself. He can't stop thinking about Rosanna and what she must have gone through all because of Bob. If Bob wasn't involved in the Mafia none of this would have ever happened. She would be living in Trappani still enjoying life but no Bob had to ruin it all for her. Everyone that Bob has ever been close to has had their lives ruined in one way or another. It comes with the job Bob has to do the job he hates so very much and can't quit. If he was to quit he would be killed within a month without the protection of the rest of his men. A lot of people around the world hate Bob Vurdo for various reasons. Bob just wants the pain to end he has had enough of his life. Bob sits up in his bed and looks at his bedside table which has a phone sitting on the hook. That's the answer Bob thinks as he picks up the phone and dials a number. 'Hello.' 'Hey Docca its Bob.' 'Oh hello Bob what can I do for you?' 'I want to try your crap.' 'You what?' Docca can't believe what he is hearing. 'Just bring me some over.' 'Yeah ok.' Docca hangs up the phone and so does Bob. He then lays back down in bed to wait for the arrival of Docca. *** Bob sits at the table in his room looking out onto the front yard. He then looks down at the table where a white tablet is sitting next to a glass of water. This is the way out of my life thinks Bob as he picks up the tablet and studies it in his hand. Such a little tablet can do so much it holds so much power and yet is extremely small. Bob tilts his head backward and swallows the tablet with a glass of water. *** Bob Vurdo is in a plain white room which is furnished only by a cupboard. Bob looks around confused and then walks up to the cupboard. He opens up the doors as there is no other options. Inside the cupboard is a man who looks like a crazy scientist. His thick gray hair stands up on his head and he is staring right at Bob Vurdo in an emotionless fashion. Suddenly he grins evilly at Bob. Man in the closet: Hello Mr. Vurdo. Bob: Who the fuck are you and where am I? Man in the closet: I am the man in the closet Mr. Vurdo and you are in the magic land of LSD. Bob has obviously escaped his reality through an LSD trip. Bob looks stunned as the man continues. Man in the closet: You must find the treasure of Zurich to survive. Bob Vurdo: Survive? This is only a trip right? It has nothing to do with reality. Man in the closet: Bah... You stupid simpleton you don't realize that if you die in LSD land you can never return to your normal world. Bob Vurdo: Right now I really don't care. Man in the closet: But what about your mother? Bob Vurdo thinks about his mother for a second. Such a beautiful woman who has been put through much but always remained strong. After going through so much she continued to raise Bob. Bob is weak compared to her, he hits a low patch and he runs away from things. That is not what he wanted to do but his willpower just isn't strong enough. Suddenly the room warps into a large pub like area. Bob is sitting at the bar as he turns around and looks at the unusual people in the room. There are short stocky men with large beards sitting together at a table. They seem to be very happy as they talk to each other rather loudly while drinking from large cups. There are a few normal men around the room that seem to be either keeping to themselves or talking to other men. The most unusual thing about the area is the clothes there are men with large green cloaks on and generally olden day style clothes. The other unusual thing about the room is the weapons rack in the corner which bears a few swords and axes along with a few bows. The large cheery looking bartender moves over to Bob and looks down at him in an inquisitive fashion. Bartender: And who might you be? Bob is frightened as the man caught him with his back turned he turns around in his seat and looks up at the large bartender. Bob Vurdo: I'm Bob Vurdo where am I? The bartender laughs. Bartender: I think you might have had a bit to much to drink. The bartender nods his head at Bob and two large men make their way over and grab Bob under the arms. They both pick him up together and take him outside the pub and throw him on the ground. Bob looks back up to see a wooden sign reading alehouse. Bob gets up and dusts himself off before taking a look around. Bob looks up to the sign on the street corner which points to his right reading 'Darjack street' Bob looks at the other which is the street he is on which reads 'Tandrent Square'. Bob takes a look down both streets noticing the olden style houses and the empty cobblestone streets. Voice from the dark: I been waiting for ey. The voice is rather strange the accent is like a mix between an Australians and a Scotsman's. He also speaks in a rather low tone. Bob: Who said that where are you? Bob looks around him but all he can see is darkness. Suddenly a small figure emerges from the darkness but still only the outline of the small but well built man can be seen. The man walks closer to Bob and moves completely into the light. He is a short fat man with a large orange beard. A metal helmet protects his head and appears to be engraved with some sort of pattern. He is wearing a leather vest over a tatty old brown robe. He has leather gloves and boots on and has an axe at his side. Man: I'm Grimgor Ironfist.... I have been awaiting your arrival it is a long story really. You must come with me immediately to the inn where I can explain. Bob doesn't want to go but he knows he has no other choice. He knows nothing of this land and doesn't want to be left alone on these streets. *** Grimgor: Even though us dwarven folk don't like to mess around with that magic stuff the elves do. They predicted your arrival and called you the savior. They also told me that I was the man to be your companion while in this land. Bob Vurdo: How do I get out of here? Grimgor: You don't... Well at least until you have fulfilled your duties here. Bob Vurdo: But I have things I need to do on my planet. Grimgor: Yes that's the catch. This ring will give you the power to get to PCW events from here and only to PCW events. You will arrive at the arena just before the show starts and be brought back here after your match. For some reason the elves see wrestling as important.... Also it is suggested that I become your manager for the time being... Until you complete your tasks here. You must succeed in PCW it is important to your world. Bob Vurdo: I don't need anyone's help and why is it so important. Grimgor: You are the savior to this land and could very well be the savior to your world... Wrestling may have something to do with that. Grimgor reaches into his pocket and pulls out a ring before placing it on the table. Bob studies the shinning gold ring before fitting it onto his finger. Bob Vurdo: I want to go home now I don't want to do this. Grimgor: I knew you would react like this. The elves told me you were a very depressed and deep person. Maybe that's what this land can give you in return if you let us. Bob Vurdo: What exactly is this land? Grimgor: This is LSD land. Bob Vurdo: Why is it called LSD land? Grimgor: We don't know really. Some say it has to do with Landor the Silver Dragon. Bah... That's just a stupid human theory. Bob Vurdo: What if I don't save this land? Grimgor: Then you and all of us would die. Grimgor stares deep into Bobs eyes to show his seriousness. Grimgor: You need to get some sleep as we have a big day tomorrow. Grimgor gets up and walks out as Bob lays back on the uncomfortably hard mattress.
