Because its the day before Turkey Day, and vampires are not out to eat turkey. Vampires and turkey day, who the hell invited them? Damn whitey and his idea for bringing the Natives and he together. We were somewhere around the edge of Barstow, on the edge of the Texan desert, when the buffons began to takeover. Down some road I am, in a drop top 64' with hydro in my mouth and jamming to 'Return of the Tres', performed by Delinquent Habits. Mexican hip hop is just like ours - just harder to get into the United States. Dawn happens to be in the back seat, screaming about a toke over the line..:: 'One toke over the line - SWEET JESUS! I had the wheel, and she had the music for us to jam out towards. By the way this was going, we could have had two or three albums done thanks to her. I wore only baggy black jeans, a white Sean Jean shirt with a platinum chain and pendant around my chest. Dawn: Precisely. ::.She might have been a little drunk, or high or whatever but when she's not on anything she's useful. More useful than a white man whose plan is to run a monopoly. When a Congress tells him that, his response is 'Monopoly is a game; I'm trying to rule the fucking world!' Scary when you have a which white wigger on your side. I remember when I had him singing Fabolous and 'Can't Deny It' Or was that Stephen Hawking? He was timid, afraid of the normal people. Yet this idiot friend of mine, whom noticed the last person we picked up sliced a yellow mouse rat in half. I should have been drinking Cristal, sipping on Bacardi Dark and playing with my daughter. None of that is possible now, as Uncle Scarface happens to try and claim some ground on the charts, he's marketing the hell out of my album. Good plan. 'Well, you seem a bit old enough for it, eh?' He hesitated before saying anything. By the time I gave the mofo the wheel, he laughed hard. Before long, he hopped in the backseat of the car, with the mofo in the driver seat going a full on hundred miles per hour. Right now, I was sweating bullets as a heatwave passes over the state of Texas'. How long could I maintain my game plan, I wondered. How long before one of us begin jabbering and raving at this boy? What will he think then? This same lonely desert was the last known home of the Manson family - and you know how sick those guys and dolls are. For some reason, blood was on the mofo's neck, maybe a bat had got him, maybe not. But whatever it was, it wasn't a bad vibe, rather a known one. Would this boy make the connection when my friend driving starts screaming about evil Vampires coming to takeover the world with Bill Gates as their sidekicks? With Dave brandishing her seven .357's eight .38's, and nine Tech-Nine's? If so, then we'll just have to cut his head off and feed it to Nascosto in some sort of Reservoir Dogs grotesque moment. Because it goes without him saying anything to the proper authorities. He'd report us to some Cracker outlaw police agency and they'd have open season on us..:: Dante[out loud]: Jesus! If I tell him, he might have a better understanding out of journey with the intentions to parade Amarillo half naked, and drunk while we piss on the flag and beat up people wearing sombreros.
