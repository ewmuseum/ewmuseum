The Tank 36 - Mourning After Night

29 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 29, 2002 ﻿*********The Tank __35—********* On the Grounds of Enemies Don’t slit my throat behind my back.I want to see the way your arm moveswhen life runs down my chest. ~Leigh Donovan Saturday, October 26 - Day 115 Snow limps toward his cell at just after 2 a.m., escorted by a pair of guards through the darknessthat has fallen over Unit B. He wasn’t expecting to lose to Duke Williams last night, but inretrospect, his mind was elsewhere. That’s not going to happen this time, he thinks. Thiscoming Tuesday, he’ll get another chance at Duke Williams, along with three others, for achance to fight for the PCW Heavyweight Championship of the World. “In you go, Wrestleboy,” one guard says as he slides Snow’s cell bars open, shoving the bruisedyoung wrestler through inside. Snow is surprised to find that Leigh Donovan is still awake, bentover the side of his lower bunk hard at work on something. “What the fuck are you doing down there?” Snow asks, rubbing his left temple - his eye isbeginning to swell. “None of your fucking business,” Leigh responds. “Go to fucking sleep.” Snow does as he’s told, not because it’s Leigh’s wish, but because he’s dead tired. As he climbsto the top bunk, he catches a glimpse of a reflection in Leigh’s hand - he’s holding a metallicobject. “What the fuck is that!?” Snow asks in a harsh whisper. “You planning to cut my throat tonight,or something!?” Wildly, Leigh looks up at his cellmate. “Fuck no!” he responds in another whisper. “If I wantedto kill you, I’d have strangled you or something you dumb motherfucker! Now keep yourfucking mouth shut before you draw the guards’ attention!” Leigh turns back to his hands wherehe’s attempting to sharpen a hunk of metal. “Who the hell is it for then?” Snow asks, leaning out over the edge of his bunk - he suddenlydoesn’t feel tired any more. “Harvey has a job for me,” Leigh says proudly. “And I’m going to get it done - gladly.” Leighsmiles to himself. He craves the moment that he’s able to plunge the metal blade deep into J-Thug’s massive body. Secretly, he’ll be doing it for himself for all the sexual abuse hewithstood at the hands of the massive leader of the Tank’s black gang. Snow can sense what’s going on - he was well aware of what J-Thug was doing to Leigh. He canstill remember Leigh coming to him, asking for protection. The young Australian was sodifferent then. More importantly, Snow realizes that Harvey has placed the blame of his family’s death on J-Thug and his Thugs, the Tank’s most dominant black gang. As the young bearded lion rolls overin his bunk in an attempt to get some sleep, he can’t decide whether or not these latestdevelopments are good things or just add more gasoline to an already explosive situation. Sunday, October 27 - Day 116 Tim McDonald rubs his swollen nose - still sore from the smash he received from Jason Snowlast week when he realized that young Shane Hood was making Vincent Giovanni’s dangerousphone call to the mafia big wigs. The plan worked beautifully. Still, sleeping comes in small spurts when the man lying just four feet below makes no secret ofthe fact he wants McDonald, and every associate of his, dead. McDonald and Vinny G haven’tspoken to each other in a week, despite spending lonely nights together in a five by nine cell -not since the morning Jason Snow came to the skinny young man’s rescue, when it looked likethe bullish Italian, Giovanni, was going to strangle the very life from him. Now, their chess match has grown silent. Each man moves against the other by day, and thenthey come back and spend their nights silently in the same space. Still, Tim knows that the timeis drawing near that Vincent will attempt to strangle him in his sleep, or kill him some otherway. He puts his weary face in his hands as he sits up in his bunk - Vincent isn’t present in the cell. He must already be trying to plot another way to get word of Hattoni’s murder to the mafia headsin New York. “Fuck,” he curses himself for allowing a sleep session just a few hours after thewake up buzzer. By this time, Snow is in the cafeteria cleaning garbage off the tables. He hasno protection for the time being. Of course, should a situation break out, the Italians that McDonald leads would rush to his aid. But in the aftermath, there would be questions - very dangerous questions. If the Italian gangwho follow McDonald, and previously followed Doug Hattoni, find out about the deadly gameof chess between himself and Giovanni, it will end in an unwanted stalemate - Giovanni will bekilled first, but so many questions would arise for his attack on McDonald that some hothead inthe gang would put the pieces together. Neither Tim nor Vincent wish to lose their life in this - it’s not a battle of honour, after all. ForTim, it’s a battle where his life and his reputation as a dedicated leader of the Italian prisonfaction, despite being of Scottish descent, is at stake. For Vinny G, it’s about punishment. McDonald manipulated Doug Hattoni’s death, using JasonSnow and Harvey Blake as pawns in his grand scheme. Snow was the set up man, Harvey Blakedelivered the kill. Blake’s family has already been dealt with, as Blake himself soon will be. But first, there’s the more difficult matter Jason Snow and Tim McDonald - the ultimateopponent in this deadly tug-of-war. Tim McDonald knows this is Vincent’s position. As he dresses, he tries to find think of somescheme - just one more stroke of genius that would pull him from the fire yet again. His mind wanders to the people involved and he concludes that they’re all being put to their bestuses... but then, there’s a new pawn in this game, he thinks as he pulls his shirt over his head. The young man who made the call for Vinny G - Shane Hood. McDonald heads out of his cell to prowl... Sunday, October 27 - Day 116 It doesn’t take long for Tim McDonald to find Shane Hood, sitting alone at one of the tables onthe main floor and staring hopelessly at the far wall - hard to believe the young man is capable ofattempting to rundown his girlfriend with a car. Tim scans the main floor of Unit B for Vincent Giovanni. When he’s sure the bullish Italian isin another part of the Unit, Tim takes a seat across the table from the dark haired young man. “How’s it going, kid?” asks Tim, ten years the young man’s senior, but feeling an eternity older. “Ok,” Shane shrugs. “Listen, kid,” Tim says in a quiet voice. “I’m not going to beat around the bush with you. Youmade a phone call for Vincent Giovanni the other day, didn’t you?” Hood flushes white but he says nothing. “There’s nothing to hide, kid,” McDonald says. “I know all about it. He gave you a list ofnames to say. I don’t care about that. I just want to know if you still have the number that hetold you to call.” “Yeah,” the young man says in a hushed voice. “I’ve still got it.” “Good,” Tim smiles. “Now, what did he give you to make that call for him. Did he threatenyou?” “No,” Shane says, a little louder. “He gave me money. A lot of money.” “Money, huh?” Tim says while scratching his chin. He then leans in close to Shane andwhispers in serious tones. “Well, you’re going to make a phone call for me now. AndI’m not going to give you money. You know that big blond motherfucker I’m always hangingaround?” Shane nods nervously. “Well, if you don’t make the phone call - in fact, if you don’t follow my directionsexactly,” Tim pauses to gauge the young man’s eyes, “He’s going to fucking kill you.”
