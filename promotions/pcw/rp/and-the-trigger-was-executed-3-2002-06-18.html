And the Trigger was Executed |3|

18 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.18.02 The house was small, but it seemed nice from the outside. Sure, the grass probably hasn’t seen the blade of a machete for years, but the colour of the blue house isn’t very tarnished. The white stripes along the side make it seem like it’s from a “Leave it to Beaver” episode with everything dangling on the edge of precision. The brick walkway could have seen better days, for mud was packed every inch or so, due to the sudden rainfall the days before. Cliff and Jeff walk over the walkway and stroll on by the bushes obscuring the windows. “What are we doing?” Jeff speaks in an undertone voice to Cliff, naive of what’s about to transpire. “Shh…he’s right inside. We don’t want him to hear us,” Cliff informs Jeff, putting his hand in his back jean pocket, making sure that the Beretta was still in place. It seemed to be itching at him to bring it out into the open. Now, the backyard was totally different than the front. It was a wreck. An old swing rest on its side, rusted from the age. A shed sits in the middle of the yard, shattered with fractures in almost every visible place. ‘I don’t know about that Andy.’ Jeff jumps at the sound, before keeping it cool. “The television is on, Jeff. It’s Andy Griffith-now you’re going to have to get a hold of yourself, or this isn’t going to work,” Cliff enlightens Jeff. “I don’t know if we should do this, Cliff. Seriously, if the man is now watching the Andy Griffith show then he has to be a changed man. Rehabilitated…he got what he deserved. Now, come on, let’s get out of here.” “NO…Even if he is a changed man doesn’t mean he can get away with murder. He had no right to do what he did before to my own flesh and blood. Now, you can go on and go play Hide-and-Seek with the other boys, or you can help me get revenge for what this evil man has done to me and my family,” Cliff states clearly to Jeff, with a murmur. “Fine…but, we’re only here to scare him. That’ll be enough,” Jeff notifies Cliff who nods at the remark. The backdoor is held open by a cement block of some sort, obviously to let the air in from the outside, due to no air-conditioning. From the outside looking in, the kitchen is in perceptible sight. Cliff climbs the steps of the back porch and looks inside. He motions for Jeff to follow as they creep indoors. Outlining the walls the entire time, they make it to the edge of the living room. “I think that’s him…,” Jeff whispers to Cliff, who reaches in his back pocket for the Beretta. They stand behind a man sitting in a rocking chair, reading the daily newspaper and watching the television. From behind, his brown hair sticks out, long and past his shoulders. Cliff’s eyes seem to swell up with tears and anger in an instant. With one rapid movement, Cliff pulls the metallic firearm from his pocket. He brings it out in front of him and grabs the barrel of the gun. Jeff gulps as he watches his best friend’s fury unfold. Cliff seems to leap into the air, and comes down with a huge thud and a clank, as the man in the chair falls down helplessly to the floor below. Cliff gets up and gazes at the unconscious bloke. “WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU DOING!?” Jeff shouts at Cliff, nervously moving toward the fallen man. “He murdered my sister, Jeff. He doesn’t get away with bloody murder, god damn it. This whole town thinks that it’s all just a joke…no, it’s not. Not to me, not to my parents…He deserves to pay,” Cliff shouts waving the gun around in the air, showing a crimson blood on the handle. “You said you were only going to scare him!” Jeff yells at Cliff. As he does so, the man on the ground begins to move, slowly rolling over. He stares at the two teenagers with a weary look in his eyes. Click turns the gun around and aims it directly at the man and cocks it, with a look of fury in his swollen eyes. The man runs his finger along the new blood cot on his head, and stares at the barrel of the firearm. “What is the meaning of this? Are you going to shoot me, lad?” The man says with a drained out voice. “What’s your name?” Jeff says to him, making certain that it is who he believes it is. “Kevin…Kevin Whundy. Why do you have me at gu..?” “Does Britney Torstar ring a fucking bell!?” Cliff screams at the man with pure hatred in his eyes. The man just cringes at the name and lowers his head in shame. “YOU KILLED MY SISTER! YOU SEXUALLY ABUSED HER! YOU TOOK HER FROM ME AND MADE MY HOUSE A BROKEN FAMILY!” Cliff bawls out with tears streaming down his face like confetti. “I…,” Kevin begins to speak. “You did it, didn’t you!?” Cliff yells at the man with the gun pointed at his head. “No…I didn’t. I swear to God I didn’t,” Kevin begins to cry with tears rolling down his face. “Don’t lie to me!!!” “Cliff, maybe he’s telling the truth! Let him speak,” I yelled at Cliff, trying to keep away from another crime. “I was innocent, but I paid my dues for knowing about it. It was late that night. She was coming over to a party at my house and…” The voice vanishes as the camera jumps outside on the lone street to show one man walking alone…Kris Burden. He puts his hands in his black PVC jeans and strolls along the street. “No man ever gets to finish his story. They can be somebody important for so long…and then the story ends. What if somebody is innocent? What if they’re telling the truth? What if the law was wrong, putting all odds against one? With one quick movement, the end can be brought. It’s time to end the story for some, and begin one for others. For me…It’s both. I’ve ended the story of being a normal man, knowing that I can no longer be such a thing. But now, I’ve begun another story since then with my friend misery. Who is guilty in this world? Who is innocent? Sometimes it’s hard to tell...but I know it….everybody is guilty.” Burden grins as the camera jumps back indoors. “YOU’RE LYING!” Cliff screamed bringing the gun closer to the man’s head. “Even if he is lying doesn’t mean a thing. He’s already done his time. We can’t take the law into our own hands!” Jeff pleaded with Cliff to let it go. “You’re right……….but I can.” *BOOM!* Kevin Whundy fell dead at one shot. Cliff stares at what he has just done as the gun slips from his hand, falling to the floor. Jeff Donaldson doesn’t move…frightened of what may happen. Kevin Whundy fell dead at one shot. Crimson stained the floor. Cliff stares at what he has just created as the gun slips from his hand, falling to the floor. Jeff Donaldson doesn’t move…frightened of what may happen…afraid of his own best friend. Who is guilty in this world? Who is innocent? Sometimes it’s hard to tell...but I know it….everybody is guilty.” Whatever the case may be…. The trigger was executed.
