The Epic-'Turning It Around'

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-26-02 ::It’s around 12:30 a.m. and James Creon, along with Scott Nolan , are just entering Jacksonville. It’s also about 48 hours after Scott Nolan got the phone call from James Creon in which this whole ride to Jacksonville evolves around. A few days ago James Creon , a 51-year-old wrestling trainer call up Scott Nolan for information that concerned the where bouts of Shawn Mosely. During that time, and this, Scott was able to find out the location where he is . James Creon flew in from Atlanta, Georgia right when he heard the news and now he is in Florida, trying to find the Golden One with Scott Nolan. They both sit there, Scott driving, just glaring out of the car thinking about what to expect with in the next 20-25 minutes as they will hopefully meet Shawn Mosely. Should they expect a cocky and still arrogant man not willing to turn his life around and still make something of his potential. Or a young man still wanting to walk down to the ring and become the ultimate champion in which so many use to believe he could do, now it seems like only 2 people do . Still glaring out the front of the car going about 35 mph down the road which will lead to the “Jaxx Inn” Scott Nolan is the first one to open his mouth since the airport in which he picked James Creon up from:: “Man . I don’t even know what to say” Scott says out of nowhere. James looks at him and just gives a little shake “Don’t worry. I’m the one that got this together . So I’ll do the talking.” James replies. “But . . .I haven’t seen him in a while . I don’t even know what to expect” Scott says. Not really caring about what Scott just said, James gives a little sniffle and whipes his nose with his right hand. With a few moments of silent gone by, James spots the motel in which Shawn Mosely is supposedly staying at . The silence is broken right when James points it out. “There it is” James says in a soft voice as he points at the motel from a distance. “Want me to pull right in the parking lot? . . or what?” Scott replies. James totally ignores Scott’s last comment as he is totally zoomed in on the motel which could contain which he has searched for so many months. Scott see’s that James isn’t listening and nudges on him to get his attention. “Wanna get a drink first” Scott says out of nowhere. Surprisingly, James looks at Scott Nolan and shakes his head “Yes”. Scott noticed a bar right next to the motel, which made him think of getting a drink. Some drinks won’t hurt as they are possibly going to meet the Golden One very soon. Scott parks the car in the motel parking lot, with the bar being about 50 feet from where they parked they walk on over to the old scrubby looking bar. James and Scott both look aware as they walk up to the bar, eye’s definitely willing to sight the Golden One at a glance. As they approach the entrance the focus goes from the motel area onto the entrance door. As all the attention goes onto the bar they make their way in, Scott Nolan leading the way as James gets one last sneak peak outside. The bar barely has 10 people in it, and most of them are hanging out in a group in the back of the bar playing pool. The other people consist of two old men drinking at the bar, and a young kid with pretty long hair a chair seat away from the old men. Scott and James look around and both decide to sit out the bar next to the young man and the two old men. They both glance at each other and sit down without a word spoken the entire time until now. “Place seems kind of weird. Let’s make these drinks fast” Scott says. James Creon, on the other hand, is paying most of his attention on the things going on around him as he barely makes out what Scott had to say. “Uh. .yeah, of course” James replies with no emotion at all. Scott looks at James with a confused face on. “Man, James. Ever since we came here you barely have said a word. What’s going on with you?” Scott asks with a concerned look on his face. James surprisingly looks at Scott and smirks. “You don’t know how bad I want to find this kid. He can be great!” James replies enthusiastic. “Yeah. I’ve heard stories of the great Shawn Mosely” Scott says as he nods his head agreeing with James last statement. As soon as the name “Shawn Mosely” came out of the mouth of Scott Nolan, the young man sitting next to the two old men lifts his head slightly. The young man looks totally out of it and smells like he hasn’t taken a shower since the Ice Age, of course, that’s if the ice age had showers. As the young man glares at Scott, James is the only one that notices the very familiar glare of the young man who just lifted his head for about 4 seconds . Just enough time to give James a clue that the young man just might be the one he is looking for. Scott notices that James is staring right past him looking at something behind him, but he didn’t know exactly what. “Uh . so . .what exactly are you looking at?” Scott says with a puzzling look on his face. *James shakes his head out of the long stare he developed on the young man sitting down* “Um . um . .yes, of course” James replies. “What the hell are you talking about? I asked what you were looking at” Scott says with the same puzzling face on as before.James begins to get up slowly from his chair. Scott still has a puzzled look on his face as he is wondering what the hell this old-timer is doing. But he will soon find out as James seems to be moving closer and closer to the young man he just lifted his head for a split second or two before putting it straight down on the bar again with his hands wrapped around his head for cushion. Scott finally gets the hint of what James is trying to do, which is get a closer look at the young man. James pokes the man in the shoulder. With no response, he pokes it again, a little harder this time. He finally sees some movement and finally says some words. “Uh . uh . .Shawn?” James says softly to the man. More movement comes from the young man. Enough movement for James to actually see a glimpse of his eyes. “Uh. . .Shawn Mosely.” James says one more time, and a bit louder. And then finally it happened. The very thing that James has been waiting for the entire time. The introduction to Shawn Mosely. “What” the man says as he barely gets that word out of his mouth for James to hear. James realizes the man can be the very man he came all this way to find. With out getting too over joyful until he was sure it was who he thought it was. “Y. . You. . are Shawn Mosely?” James asks. And finally the confirmation that James needed, which would be the beginning of something special in PCW. “Yeah. *Pauses.Yawns* That’s me” Shawn replies. “Hey. I’m James Creon. Great to meet you. . .the Golden One” James says. Shawn seems to be out of it in a way, but trying to stay in the short conversation that had going with each other. ::Shawn totally faints right onto the bar. He lays there not moving or saying nothing at all just when James starts to talk to him. Definitely not giving up on himself or Shawn Mosely, James decides that the only way this was going to work was to sit down and talk to the Golden One. James calls Scott over who has been looking on at the whole scene from beginning to end. James and Scott both put one of Shawns arms around one of their shoulders as they lift him up off the stool. With no one paying any attention, Scott and James walk out of the bar dragging Shawn Mosely a long with them to the outside of the bar. James and Scott bring Shawn back to the “Jaxx Inn” in which they will await the waking of Shawn Mosely and begin talking about his future::
