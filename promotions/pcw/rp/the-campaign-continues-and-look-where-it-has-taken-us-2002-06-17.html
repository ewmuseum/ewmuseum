The Campaign Continues.......(And Look where it has taken us)

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th June 2002 Democracy... The dictionary defines the word 'Democracy' as 'a government by the people or their elected representatives. The practice or spirit of social equality.' It sounds good on paper doesn't it? People have gone to war over this word, people have even died over this word, and after it all what does our government do to this word? They twist it and warp it so it is no longer the word 'Democracy' but the word 'Dictatorship.' Yes I am being very political, and should leave the political stuff to Politically Incorrect, but all this talk of democracy is for a good reason... The last time we saw The Dark Messiahs was in a mall of all places. However they were not there to shop. They were in fact campaigning! Yes you heard me right The Dark Messiahs were campaigning. However they were not campaigning for some political reason, they were campaigning for something a lot more important, the tag team titles. Now you may thing this as an absurd thing to do, campaigning for a Tag Team Title shot, but if you think about it, it isn't that absurd. I mean if you were in a similar situation as The Dark Messiahs, where you had just returned to the PCW, and you wanted a Tag Team Title shot but it was obvious the staff wasn’t going to give it you straight away, what would you do? Sure you could go out and try and fight for the shot, but what help will that do? All it will get you is cuts and bruises and no closer to that shot. I mean don't get me wrong The Dark Messiahs love a good fight, but unlike the rest of the tag teams in the PCW, The Dark Messiahs realise that it isn't just what you do in that ring that counts it is also what you do out of it. That is how this whole campaign came around, so will this campaign get The Dark Messiahs their title shot? Or is it all just a waste of time? Well I will tell you this, The Dark Messiahs have one last stop before they call it quits, and let me tell you this is one huge stop... The scene opens up inside the Oval Office in the White House. Yes you heard me, the White House! The Dark Messiahs have actually taken their campaign to the very top of American Hierarchy. Anyway let me set the scene for you, it is a beautiful day in the state of Washington and the light is streaming through the window. The sunlight reflects off the polished wooden presidential desk. On the desk is placed the famous Red Phone, where the President if ever war was immanent would order the Nuclear Strike (doesn't it make you feel so safe that a chimpanzee has the fate of the world's future in his hands?). Along with the phone are a few bits and bobs that you would usually see on an office desk, like a pen and a notepad. The rest of the Oval Office is lined with bookshelves of American History. Also along the walls are photos of the ex Presidents shaking hands with the likes of Elvis and Nelson Mandela. In the centre of the room over the United States government logo is 2 leather sofas and a coffee table. In the far corner is a television set. Suddenly the door on the left swings open and in steps The Dark Messiahs wearing suits?!?! Steel is the first to enter adjusting his tie and trying to make himself look presentable. Soon after Stone enters carrying a whiteboard under his arm. Attached to the board are a few pieces of A3 paper, obviously containing details of their campaign. Stone looks the most uncomfortable in his suit as he pulls at his shirt collar gasping for air. The door shuts behind them and Steel turns to his tag team partner with a smile on his face... Daniel Steel: Oh my god, Mike, we here. We are in the White House. Mike Stone: (Still pulling at his collar and getting disgruntled) Yeah it's great Daniel, but do we have to wear these damn suits, I can hardly breathe. Daniel Steel: (Sighs) You are such a baby sometime you know that Mike. You really think the President would have agreed to speak to us wearing our casuals? Mike Stone: (Disgruntled) No but we........ Daniel Steel: (Interrupting) It doesn't matter now, just put the board over there. (Points to the side of the Presidential desk) Mike Stone: (Putting Sign down) Daniel you don't honestly think that the President is going to take time out of his busy day, to talk to us about our PCW Campaign do you? Daniel Steel: (Nervous laugh) Of course not. Mike Stone: (Suspicious) Daniel? Daniel Steel: (Signs) Alright, alright, I admit it I did think that the President would come and talk to us personally. But you never know. Mike Stone: (Shaking head) Your the eternal optimist aren't you, Daniel? Daniel Steel: (Shrugs shoulders) Well one of us has to be optimistic, and since you always seem to think the worst of things it might as well be me. (Pause) I know why don't we go through our campaign speech before they get here. Mike Stone: (Sighs) Do we have to? I mean all this campaigning is hardly getting us anywhere. Have you seen the Turmoil card? Daniel Steel: (Shakes head) You really are the eternal pessimist aren't you? Yes I have seen the Turmoil card, and we have got a great match set up. A Handicap match, is the best match we can get, that Akiyama doesn't stand a chance. He is going up against 2 guys who don't give a damn about the consequences. Sure he is a big guy, weighing in at 339lbs and a height of 6'10, but he is just one guy. Together you and I are more powerful than any other wrestler in the PCW today. Mike Stone: (Giving in) Yeah I suppose you are right. Akiyama is a bit of a freak, I mean he calls himself 'The Neo Void God.' Is that supposed to scare us or something, I mean you see what this guy used to dress like, he didn't look scary he looked stupid, and he even looks stupid now. However 1 thing I will give him if he even turns up at Turmoil is that he has got balls, because to go up against us is like signing your own death sentence. Sure he may think that he can beat us, and that is a good attitude to go into our match with, but it is the wrong attitude. We are in the PCW for 2 reasons, to get gold and to spread pain and suffering. And without anyone to protect Akiyama he is going to have double the pain and double the suffering. (Laughs) You know for a second I felt sorry for him because at Turmoil he doesn't stand a chance. No matter how big he is, he can't compare with the true power of The Dark Messiahs. Yeah we may sound over confident but looking at the odds you have got to admit it doesn't look good for the 'Neo Void God.' Just as Stone finishes a man wearing a black suit runs into the room grasping a tape in his hand. He looks at The Dark Messiahs with a grin on his face, which disappears, slightly when he sees the size of Stone. He holds the tape up as he speaks... Man: (Smiling) Sorry I’m late. Hi, I’m one of President Bush's assistants, unfortunately he was unable to make it today, but he does want you to know that he is completely behind your campaign and he has sent you this tape. So if you will just take a seat I will show you what our wonderful President has to say. All 3 men move over to the leather sofas. The Dark Messiahs sit down first a bit excited by the prospect of Bush making a video especially for them. The assistant moves the TV set over so Stone and Steel will be able to see it clearly. He smiles again...... Assistant: Okay then I will just pop this tape in and we are ready to role. The assistant quickly pops the tape in and takes a seat on the sofa opposite. Almost immediately President Bush's face turns up on the screen... President Bush: My fellow Americans... As the President continues Steel and Stone grab their heads and scream in pain. The assistant doesn't seem to care that much as he obviously thinks they are screaming with excitement (politicians eh?)...... Assistant: (Smiling but not looking at them) Don't worry he will get to your campaign in a minute. Steel and Stone have stopped screaming now and by the look on their faces it is obvious they have changed. Steel and Stone take a look at the screen and quickly turn to the assistant... Daniel Steel: (Enraged) What the hell is this? Assistant: (Laughs) It's our President, of course. Mike Stone: (Enraged) We can see that you moron. What Daniel and I are talking about is the damn date of the tape! It's 2 damn years old! Indeed the tape is dated 19th June 2000. The assistant looks at the tape and then the enraged faces of Steel and Stone. He gulps and smiles nervously... Assistant: (Terrified) Erm, listen, erm I can explain, you see, erm... Before the assistant can finish Steel has levelled him with a hard right... Daniel Steel: (Shouting) What is it with you damn politicians!?!? Can you never tell us the truth!?!? Mike let's teach this so-called 'democrat' a lesson. Steel brings the assistant whose face is bleeding over to Stone. Steel then places the assistant between Stone's legs. Stone hits the assistant’s back a couple of time before lifting him up for a powerbomb and brings him crashing down right through the coffee table. Daniel turns to Mike... Daniel Steel: (Laughs evilly) That damn freak Akiyama doesn't stand a chance! Both men look down at the assistant and laugh evilly before exiting through the door on the right as the scene fades to black...
