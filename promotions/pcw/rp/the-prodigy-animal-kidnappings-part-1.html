The Prodigy Animal Kidnappings (part 1)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/26-27/02 We hear light breathing but yet we see nothing but total blackness. All of that changes though when a small pen light breaks through giving off a small fraction of illumination. The pen light is waved around as though the person holding it is looking for something. The light stops and rests upon a switch. A big beefy hand with gritty fingernails reaches out and pulls the switch up. Suddenly the room is filled with light revealing two men wearing all black from head to toe. One man is a tall skinny fellow, while the other is short and chubby. Tall Man: Hey Tom. Tom: What Marvin? Marvin: Why are we doing this again? Tom shoots a look towards Marvin with a snarl on his face. Tom: You are such an idiot!! Marvin: Hey what did mom tell you? I'm not an idiot, I'm just special. So come on and tell me what we are here for again. Tom: Cookies. Marvin: Oooooh..I love cookies. Marvin starts salivating and licking his lips at the thought of moist chewy chocolate chip cookies, his favorite. Tom on the other hand stands to the right of Marvin and brings his hand forward in a quick motion slapping Marvin hard in the back of the head. Marvin stumbles forward and rubs the back of his head with a grimace on his face. Tom: I was just joking you moron! We have to get the dog before the old lady gets back. Marvin: Oh right! Now I remember, we have to get the dog before the old lady comes back. Tom: I just said that. Both men go dead silent as a low growl can be heard. Almost at the same time, they turn around and peek down to see a white poodle with an arched back. Its lips are pulled back revealing it's pearly white teeth. The brothers back up slowly, but every time they do this, the dog moves forward. Marvin: What do we do Tom? Tom: There is only one thing to do. RUN!!!!!!! The dog pounces forward and almost nabs Tom, but Tom pushes Marvin into the dog's path. The ravishing poodle latches on to Marvin's ankle and tackles him to the ground. The whole time, Marvin is screaming in pain as the dog is maliciously tearing his clothing and skin apart. Tom: Come here little doggie. Tom makes his way towards the dog slowly. The poodle lets go of Marvin who quickly scurries away. Then it directs its attention towards Tom who is egging the dog on to come get him. As the dog runs at him, Tom with his big black boot, kicks the mutt and sends him sailing across the room. Tom walks over to the small crumpled mass of dog and picks it up around the waist. Marvin limps over next to Tom looking pretty beaten up with his tattered clothes. Marvin: You didn't kill him did you? Marvin looks sympathetically towards the dog who lays motionless. Tom: I just knocked him out. Tom looks at his watch and his eyes grow ten times their regular size. Tom: We better get out of here now. The old broad comes home in a few minutes. Tom and Marvin rush towards a broken window, which is more than likely the way they came in. Tom hands the dog to Marvin as he tries to climb out. He squeezes half way through but gets stuck! Tom starts flailing frantic to get out. Marvin on the other hand starts pushing him which eventually does cause Tom to fall out. It's now Marvin's turn to make the escape. He delicately hands the poodle to his brother and dodges out the window like an arrow. Just as he does this, the front door pops open and in comes Ms. Montgomery, an older lady around the age of fifty. Ms. Montgomery: Trident....come here Trident. She waits but gets no answer. It doesn't bother her though as she figures he is probably sleeping. So she places her jacket on the coat rack and walks into her lavish house which has the finest of just about everything. She takes a right from the hall and comes into her living room. Instantly she goes into a hysterical state as she sees that the window had been broken. Ms. Montgomery runs to the phone and dials 911. As she does this, the scene slowly fades to black. A few seconds later the scene reopens, but it's the next day. And we are not in Ms. Montgomery's house anymore, but Kramer Wild Wing's! Kramer sits on his couch, stretched out as much as possible. For some this is a site not to be seen, because Kramer is only wearing a pair of smiley face boxers and is busy munching down on a huge bowl of Fruit Loops cereal. The grin on his face tells us that he is pretty happy. This could be because of two things. One, the cereal isn't stale, or two he managed to pull out the win on Turmoil despite all of the doubt. A loud thud forces Kramer to stand up and set his bowl of cereal onto the coffee table. He then walks to the door and opens it up. Sitting there on the other side is the Tampa Bay Post which comes out every day. Kramer snatches it up and closes the door, quickly returning to the couch. Before he starts shoveling more cereal into his mouth, he checks out the front page of the newspaper. His eyes rest upon an article entitled, 'Break In Leaves Old Lady Without Prize Dog'. He skims the article and reads bits and pieces out loud. Kramer: Just yesterday, Ms. Mary Montgomery's prized pooch, Trident, was kidnapped. Police say that there was an obvious struggle because patches of blood were found on the carpet. If you have any information please contact your local police immediately. Without really thinking about the article, Kramer tosses the newspaper onto the coffee table and grabs his bowl of cereal and starts munching down. Yet again his breakfast is interrupted, and this time because of Jason Alexander who comes strolling through the front door with his normal dress shirt and black pants. The only thing different about him, is the huge grin across his face. Jason: What a day, what a beautiful day! Kramer looks over at him with a raised eyebrow. Kramer: You seem overly happy. What's up? Jason: Well there are two reasons I am flustered. One, you won yesterday, which I admit was a total shocker. If you win that title then that means more money for us, and you know how I love money. Kramer: Well get ready for the money to flow in when I win two titles in the matter of one week. Jason: Don't get cocky Kramer. Kramer: Why can't I be self assured? I mean on Turmoil everyone thought I didn't have what it took. And as far as the PPV, the majority of people feel that I have no chance in hell to beat all of those men in the trailer brawl. Personally I see history repeating itself. That history is me winning. Jason: I guess this would be a bad time to tell you that I have a grand on Lovecraft retaining the title? As Kramer is still eating his cereal, he almost chokes at what Jason said. Kramer quickly stands to his feet to approach Jason. Kramer: You what? Jason: Anyway the second reason I am smiling is because I got a date. Kramer: Yeah and I bet she is blind and bald. Now why in the world would you bet against me? Jason: First of all there is nothing wrong with a blind and bald woman. Besides I haven't had a date in the longest time so beggars can't be choosers. And I only bet against you because you have never been able to take all of the pressure. I mean it's at Gold and Glory, a pay per view that will be watched by millions. Think about it Kramer. When was the last time you were even scheduled for a pay per view? And on top of that, when was the last time you won at a pay per view event? Kramer looks at him pondering for a moment. Kramer: That's a good point. But I am still going to win. You'll see. Kramer looks to the clock on the VCR. Kramer: Oh crap. I'll be right back. Kramer rushes up his stairs using two steps at a time. Jason takes a seat on the couch and picks up the newspaper browsing through it. Ten minutes later Kramer comes back down fully clothed in a white t-shirt and blue jeans. In his right hand is Grimer who is busy eating his own breakfast in his cage. Kramer: Come on Jason we have to leave now. Jason: What for? Kramer: Remember we start the shoot for the new KFC commercial. Jason: Oh damn, I almost forgot. Jason looks down to Grimer's cage. Jason: Why are you bringing him? Kramer: That's right. I didn't tell you did I? Jason: Tell me what? Kramer: He is going to be starring in the commercial with me. I talked to the Colonel a few days ago and he loved the idea. Jason: I guess the Colonel knows best? With that both men walk outside. As the front door shuts, the scene fades to black.
