Now a Goth God!!! I am Dukestar The Goblin muhahahaha!

12 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Now a Goth God!!! I am Dukestar The Goblin muhahahaha! Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 10/12/02 Dateline: Saturday October 12, 2002Time: 7:00pm CST Place: The secret EVIL GOTH lair of Dukestar The Goblin Event: Plan the demise of Rent-A-Hero by turning into a Goth God -Bob Willis music is being played backwards in dark and gloomy basement in an undisclosed location. A strobe light flickers as the EVIL nasty GOTH one Dukestar The Goblin eats a bat for a snack as he plans his plot to take OVER THE PCW!!!!!!- Dukestar: I hide my face in the darkness to cover the scares of my sin. Hold on one damn minute. -Duke stops talking scary as he walks over and turns off the strobe light in favor of a single beam to his black mascara covered face.- Dukestar: The strobe gives me a headache this should be EVIL and GOTH enough. -Duke goes back to his deep bass scary Malice voice.- Dukestar: Why do you ridicule my pain? The simple thought of Rent-A-Hero with my continental title is freighting………….but don’t be mistaken its not half as scary or GOTH like as me!!!! -Duke hiss at the camera.- Dukestar: I am Dukestar the Goblin the God of the darkside. The king of all that is evil and dark, and GLOOMY!!!. Rent-A-Hero my formidable foe you think you can stop my rays of Malice from blinding the judging of the bookers to keep the gold off my waist? You think you can stop the swing of Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound from paralyzing you in the ring? You think you can put one over one the coalition of evil know as the Front Office? You my tight wearing tyrant are inaccurate. -Duke lets out an EVIL laugh probably the most Goth like laugh you have ever heard.- Dukestar: I made the mistake in your first match in PCW of not killing you. I should have let loose my Malice tantrum to take you out for good. Instead I left you to fight another fight. I’m not some stereotypical evil Goth bad guy like you see on TV. When I get my hands on you I will strangle till your gone. I will not put you in a vat of hot soup only to find out you foiled my plans because of your rent-a-underwear. -Duke looks into the camera with a freighting stare.- Dukestar: No my ridiculed rodent like friend that will not happen. I will take you out with a streak of Malice unlike none unveiled before. A streak so Goth fueled the devil himself with take refuge from me and my powers. I eat small children just to snack and I will rape nuns instead of masturbating. Oh yea my friend Duke Star the Goblin can not be stopped. -Duke turns the light on to unveil a Goth machine that looks like a port-o-potty painted black.- Dukestar: Take a good look at this Rent-a-Dolt take a look at this machine created by the Goth GOD’s at Harvey’s appliance store. This Malicious machine will transform me into an even greater force to be reckoned with. Their will not be any question about it when its all said and done I’ll be a GOD!!!! AN evil Goth GOD!!!! -Duke lets out a loud evil almost Goth God like laugh.- Dukestar: Just a side note that even with out this machine I’m still more Goth then all the Goths, murderers, and Gods already in PCW……combined!!! That’s right kids I’m no Mr. Rogers. Now stand back as I administer the transformation. -A knock is heard at the door, Duke opens it up and its Roseanne Bar. She is smacking he gum with an evil Goth like smack. Her face is covered with powder to whiten it and her eyes are covered with black mascara.- Roseanne: This is great I haven’t had a paying gig in years. -Duke motions with his head that the camera is on and he’s filming.- Roseanne: Oh right. Master, I have arrived to oversee your transformation. Dukestar: Excellent because according to the Goth God handbook once you’re a God your not supposed to talk to the mortals. Roseanne: Really? -Duke hands Roseanne the “Idiots Guide To Goth God Behavior.”- Roseanne: Oh yea it says it right there. OK. I’ll be your mouthpiece then. That shouldn’t be a problem. Dukestar: Excellent. Now let us begin. Rent-A-Hero kiss the continental belt good bye and Clyde you do the same to the world. It’s Malice time….EVIL GOTH MALICE TIME!!!! HOSSSSSSSSSSSSSs -Duke turns the hoss into an evil hiss towards the camera.- Roseanne: OK get in lets get the ball rolling. Dukestar: I walk in a mortal I exit a GOD!! -Duke enters the port-a-potty painted black…..I mean the evil God transformer and exits quickly.- Dukestar: Jesus it stinks in there. Roseanne: I just used it like ten minutes ago. Dukestar: Damn ever hear of a courtesy flush? Roseanne: Sorry. Dukestar: I guess that’s the price you pay becoming an EVIL GOTH God!!!! -Duke reenters the plastic shit house and locks the door till it reads occupied. Roseanne walks over to a side lever glued on and pulls it down. All of a sudden a cheap looking green smoke emerges from the machine.- Roseanne: In a matter of moments Rent-a-geek you will be defeated with out over stepping into the ring. -Roseanne lets out an Evil almost as Goth as Duke laugh. A few moments latter Duke exits the chamber chocking from the green smoke. It looks like he has applied some powder to his face because it is now pale in color.- Dukestar: ……. -Duke clears his throat and stares at Roseanne for a moment.- Roseanne: What? -Duke elbows her.- Roseanne: Owe that hurt…what did you do that for? -Duke points to the “Idiots Guide To Goth God Behavior”- Roseanne: Oh…oh…..oh…right you can’t talk. -Roseanne clears her throat.- Roseanne: Now I’m supposed to trash talk Rent-A-Hero right? -Duke nods.- Roseanne: Rent-A-Hero?? Come on look at you, you’re a nerd. My kids beat up kids like you when they went to school. Who did you blow to get the continental title. You suck…no really you suck big time. Dukestar will destroy in like two seconds. -Duke points to his arm.- Roseanne: Are you ok? -Duke makes a laser beam noise.- Roseanne: Oh yea right….out God Dukestar the Goblin will now use his Malice beam to show off his powers. -Duke points his arm at some empty beer cans sitting on a table. He then focuses and makes a laser noise with his mouth. A second later the cans fall off the table in a Goth like manner.- Roseanne: Oh my God look at his powers. -The camera catches a hand by the table where the cans fell.- Dukestar: God damn it Larry you ruined the shot. -A head emerges from behind the table.- Larry: What I thought I was done…..you told me when you pointed your fist that way to knock the cans off. You never said I had to stay down there. Dukestar: This is terrible. Roseanne: Oh my God you talk!! Dukestar: Yes I’m breaking the code because I’m so Goth I’m above the Goth laws!! -All three Goth’s in the basement bite the head off a gummy bat as the camera fades.- =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
