Flying coach is fun...yeah right...

13 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-13-03 The scene opens to Adam sitting with his eyes closed and a pair of headphones wrapped around the back of his neck in a coach seat of a plane. Jaslene is sitting next to him on the right. She’s reading a magazine. Adam slowly opens his eyes as crying can be head. Suddenly there is squealing as well. “Christ I can still hear them.” Adam says in a hushed voice pulling his head off of the headrest. “Don’t they ever shut up?” Jaslene smacks him on the shoulder. “Adam that is no way to talk about little children.” Jaslene says turning her attention back to the magazine. Adam pulls the headphones down and closes his eyes. He pulls his hand up and begins to rub the bridge of his nose. Jaslene wraps her arm around his neck. She rubs the back of his head. “It’s ok Adam, the flight can’t last that much longer.” Jaslene says with a smirk as the squeals get louder. “Jas do you realize how long it take to get to South Korea?” Adam says opening his eyes and looking into her eyes. “No, not really.” Jaslene answers. “A hell of a long time, let’s just put it that way.” Adam says sticking her tongue out at her. The cries begin to die down a bit. Adam breathes a sigh of relief. He sits his head back into the headrest. “That’s more like it.” Adam says, “The big guy up stairs must be on my side today.” Before he can say anything else, the screaming comes back even louder this time. Adam drops his head and sighs. Jaslene just laughs at him. “It’s not funny Jas.” Adam says noticing that she is laughing at him. She tries to control her laughter but can’t. Jaslene just unexpectedly bursts out into laughter. “HAHAHAHAHAHA!” Jaslene bends over and holds her ribs, “Ahhhhhhhhh…that’s a good one Adam.” “It’s really not funny Jaslene, I need to prepare for the match.” Adam says rubbing his temples, “I need sleep.” “Adam, you have been sleeping for the past week.” Jaslene says sitting back up in her seat. “Yeah but I have been sleeping too much and getting too much rest, do you know the feeling of being over rested?” Adam asks. “Yeah I think I know what you mean but still what else does it take to prepare for this match?” Jaslene responds, “I’m sure you’ll get the chance to take a nap or something either on the way to the arena or before your match.” “I certainly hope so, I need as much rest I can get and certainly doesn’t seem like I’ll be getting any here.” Adam responds. “By the way, do you know what kind of match Dante and I are going to be having?” Jaslene sits back in her seat and scratches her head for a moment. She starts to shake her head from side to side. “Uh…actually no I don’t,” she responds, “I don’t think it was even mentioned which would lead me to believe that it’s whatever kind of normal Extreme match the PCW has.” “Heh, I suppose you are right. I hope Dante is ready for this because I won’t be giving in so easily.” Adam says with confidence. “I know Adam, I know.” Jaslene says picking her chin up and closing her eyes, “I have no doubt that you will kick his ass and take that title from him.” “It’s not about the title Jas, it’s about the show.” Adam responds. “Damnit Adam you had best be changing your attitude if you want to make the most money you can from your endorsement contract.” Jaslene says slamming her hand down onto his thigh. “I’m sorry Jas but I just can’t think that way,” Adam answers, “It’s never been about the money, the glory or the fame, it’s always been about the fans. It will always be about the fans for me. I’ll do what is nessecery to make sure that each and every fan leaves the arena satisfied. Especially now on this USO tour. This is a big thing for me Jas.” “I know Adam you have been like that for a while but you have to realize that things change and you have to change to keep up with the times.” Jaslene says. Adam reaches down and unbuckles his seatbelt. He gets up and walks back through the aisle before stopping in front of the bathrooms. The ‘occupied’ sign is on both of them. Adam turns around and faces back down the aisle. He notices that Jaslene is sitting in his aisle seat looking down at him. She starts making faces at him to which Adam just laughs. A click can be heard from behind Adam. He turns just in time to see a rather large man come out of one of the small bathrooms. The man excuses himself and squeezes past Adam. Adam looks into the bathroom and takes a whiff. He turns around quickly and gasps for air. “Christ…” he says to himself, “Maybe I was wrong, the big man upstairs is definitely not on my side tonight.” Adam steps into the bathroom and closes the door behind him. After a couple of moments the door opens again and Adam steps back out and takes in a few deep breaths. He heads back down the aisle. He passes a very tired looking mother sitting between two twin babies. Adam smiles a weary smile before taking his seat. “Remind me again why we are flying coach Jas.” Adam says buckling his seatbelt again. “Because Adam when you are flying on such short notice, you should be thankful for anything you can get.” Jaslene responds, “Its not like we gave them a decent notice and bought our tickets well in advance.”“Yeah I suppose you’re right.” Adam says resting his head back in the chair. “And besides coach is fun!” Jaslene says with a smile. “Flying coach is fun…yeah right…” Adam says rolling his eyes. “Don’t worry about it Adam, you’ll be fine and we’ll get to South Korea just fine, you’ll get enough rest for your match and you’ll make a huge impression when you win the Extreme Title.” Jaslene says with another smile. “I hope you’re right Jas, I truly do.” Adam says closing his eyes and pulling his headphones back over his ears. “But for now I’m going to try and get some rest.” The scene fades out as Adam tries to ignore the loud squealing coming from behind him.
