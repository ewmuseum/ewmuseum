Title: Judging Celestialogy.. Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: Today. 'Order in the court! Order in the court!' The sound of a bailiff's voice can be heard, his powerful voice echoing through the expanse of space... 'All rise! The honorable Judge Powell presiding!' Judge Powell? Indeed. The Master Debater stands at a bench, which appears to have materialized from nowhere. It seemingly floats in space, nothing to support it, as the justice sits down in his chair. This demonstration purposeless, seemingly. Blackness surrounds Powell. Not the blackness of night, either. A dark blackness. An evil blackness. This, then, must be the Void. But why is William S. Powell in the Void? He was not Void Banished. But, you must admit, it looks quite real.. Which is, of course, the intention. The mock-up of the Void helps to send the message Powell is shooting for. And of course! Now you know the voice of the bailiff! None other than Roger Schultz, the Politically Incorrect campaign manager. A courtroom, set up in the middle of a symbolic representation of the Void? Akiyama will not be pleased. But, then again, Akiyama is not the only person capable of mind games.. ::Sitting in his tall judicial chair, 'The Master Debater' William S. Powell stares straight ahead, gavel poised to pound..:: William S. Powell: ORDER! I demand order. Right now, I declare this court back in session. Before we left the last session, we heard the closing statement on behalf of the Celestials in the case of Celestials vs. Humanity. Now, we shall hear the closing statement on behalf of the prosecution, Humanity. Will the prosecuting attorney please approach the bench? ::A man, dressed immaculately in dark red steps forward to deliver his closing statement. Of course, this man is Reginald V. Lovecraft.. He looks quickly at a clipboard at his side to determine what he will say...:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Ladies and gentlemen of the jury... ::Lovecraft turns to the jurors, a group of faceless, nameless men and women dressed in immaculate suits and/or blouses. He smiles briefly, before continuing.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: In this case, over the past few weeks, you have seen the Celestials state their case as to why they are superior to humanity. I have recieved beatings from the 'Neo Void God' and his so-called Divine Prophetess over this time. I have had my locker room vandalized by those two bastards. They have seemingly destroyed everything I and my partner stand for. Celestial victory? HELL NO! ::The Governor's eyes are flashing mad, as he accentuates his point with an angry fist pounded into his open palm.: Reginald V. Lovecraft: While Akiyama may have abilities beyond an average human being...he is no God. Remember, he is seven feet tall, and three-hundred thirty pounds. This would give anyone incredibly enhanced strength. Sure, he can fly like a cruiserweight, and that whole Void thing is a neat parlor trick... but it is an act. His whole 'I'm silent because I'm better than all of you' thing is just schtick. Essentially, Akiyama is the world's largest magician. And, quite unfortunately for him, his wand is about to fail to produce rabbits from his rather odd hat... ::A quick step towards the jury box is taken by Lovecraft..all theatrics, sure, but sure to impress jurors.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: For you see, the God faces six men at WrestleVessel, aboard a cruise ship. He faces Politically Incorrect, the Pretty Cool Guys, and the Heartbreakers, in a fourway dance for the PCW World Tag Team Titles. Akiyama won those titles in a singles match. He has not defended them since. Some 'God of Tag'. But, now he has to face actual tag teams, who take their art very, very seriously. And so, the God shall be dethroned, and exposed as the mere mortal he is. The prosecution rests. ::Judge Powell turns to the side, staring the jury in the eye.:: William S. Powell: Jurors, you have your instructions....are you ready to deliberate? ::The jurors, nod, leaving without a word. Slowly, the scene swirls around and around, as we fade to on board a cruise ship, the WrestleVessel of PCW.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yeah, that would be cool. But, we both know you can't put a belief on trial.. William S. Powell: It does not matter, Reginald! The trial of Celestiology IS this Sunday on board this cruise ship! Fourway dance for OUR titles. The PCW World TAG Team Titles. TAG TEAM. Meaning TWO people. Not one Akiyama and one hot Divine Prophetess. TWO PEOPLE INVOLVED, WHO KNOW HOW TO WRESTLE IN A UNIFIED AND TAG TEAM ESQUE MANNER! THAT HAVE A TAG TEAM FINISHER! Reginald V. Lovecraft: My partner, in his usual insane manner, is correct. The Neo Void God will be convicted of being a: a lousy tag team, and b: a mere mortal come this pay-per-view event. It will not last, Aki. It cannot. Your reign as tag team champions is about to come to an end. And, when it is all said and done, Politically Incorrect will happily settle back into our office as PCW World Tag Team Champions. William S. Powell: Election Day is at hand, Akiyama. And, it shall be POLITICALLY INCORRECT.......IN A LANDSLIDE! ::Fade to...: Paid for by the friends of Politically Incorrect.The Politicians: Next Tag Team Champs. ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
