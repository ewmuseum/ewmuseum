Getting drunk on milk.....

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [Scene opens at a local nightclub that is buzzing with people, living it up in a vibrant atmosphere. Flaymin Aymin and his brand new valet, Heather, walk into the club. Heather coolly walks across the dancefloor to the bar whilst Flaymin jives and jumps to the beat of ‘It’s Raining Men’. Flaymin high fives a few eager fans and follows Heather to the bar. The Bar Tender leans over and asks them for their drink selection] Bar Tender: What’ll it be? Heather: Rum n’ Coke on the rocks Flaymin: I’ll have milk. [The bar tender raises an eyebrow. Bowing to peer pressure, Flaymin decides to change his drink choice] Flaymin: Ok, ok…. Make it chocolate milk. [The Bar Tender shakes his head in surprise but reaches for the dusty bottle of milk tucked at the back of the fridge. Meanwhile, Heather stares at Flaymin with a puzzled look] Heather: What’s up with that? [Flaymin looks up at the ceiling] Flaymin: I know- the décor in this place is awful.My interior decorator, Pierre, would need triple bypass surgery if he saw this place. Heather: No, idiot- what’s with the milk? Flaymin: Well my doctor told me to stop drinking because it was ‘destroying my social life’. Heather: Uh huh…. Flaymin: He said that I should go out and attempt to stay sober- just to see how much better my life would be. Heather: And you actually listened to him? Flaymin: No of course not. You see, after I destroyed the set of Sunday Sabotage… and ran over a few people with my tractor- the police forced me to take alcohol tests on a fortnightly basis and my next ‘random’ test is in half an hour.If I pass two more tests I won’t have to be on probation anymore. Heather: What if you don’t pass the tests? Flaymin: They haul me off to the slammer for a month. Heather: Oh….Well…. Good luck with that…. [Flaymin leans against the bar and bobs his head to the beat of the music while he waits for the drinks. A moment later, the Bar Tender returns and hands the rum n’ coke to Heather. Embarrassed, he slides the chocolate milk across the bar top towards Flaymin. Flaymin picks up the milk and begins to drink it down. He stops halfway and wipes the milk residue from his lips.He looks at the remnants of the milk and frowns] Heather: What’s the matter? Flaymin: I’m not sure this is milk. Heather: What do you mean? Flaymin: I’m feeling woozy…. [Heather rolls her eyes] Heather: Yeah , the alcohol level in milk is pretty high… [Flaymin grabs a nearby barstool as his eyes begin to drift from side to side. A cold sweat begins to drizzle down his face] Flaymin: I…. I’m losing focus- my sight is getting all bleary! Heather: Oh my god, you’re serious. [Flaymin slumps against the bar top as Heather tries to wake him back up. The Bar Tender walks over to the passed out Flaymin and prods him with the ice scoop] Bar Tender: Geez, maybe we should have served him something not as strong…. Like water! [Heather grabs the Bar Tender by the collar and pulls him towards her] Heather: What the hell did you put in his drink?!? Bar Tender: Nothing.I just pulled it out of the fridge. Heather: How long has it been in the fridge? Bar Tender: Since our last children’s birthday party. Heather: And when was that? Bar Tender: 1997 [Heather’s eyes boggle wide open.She looks at the passed out Flaymin and then back up at the inept Bar Tender] Heather: 1997!?!?! Bar Tender: Yeah, for some reason most parents don’t like holding their kids parties in our nightclubs…. even when we were offering half price strippers- the people still stayed away. Heather: Well how do we wake Flaymin up!?! Bar Tender: I’m trained in alcohol poisoning resuscitation but milk poisoning is a whole other ball game… [Heather frantically looks at her watch] Heather: But he’s got an alcohol test in 15 minutes! [The Bar Tender runs his hands through his hair and then looks at the carcass of Flaymin] Bar Tender: You know, I don’t think he’ll be passing that test in this condition… Heather: GEE, YOU THINK!?!? [Scene fades to black as Heather anxiously attempts to wake an unconscious Flaymin before his ever-approaching alcohol test….] Will Flaymin wake up in time? Or will Heather have to concoct a new plan of action? Tune in next time to find out……
