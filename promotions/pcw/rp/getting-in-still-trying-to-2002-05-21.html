Getting in... Still trying to...

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21-05-02 The Invasion has begun...Well, thats the way I would have liked to begin any normal promo... you know, a nice little line about how something has begun... with a hint of something like Invasion, ya know peoples, just a word to get everybody worried about the immediate take-over that will start in a mere matter of days at PCW Friday Fusion. Yes, its a nice dream but nothing thats going to be given any form of time... because lets be frank for a few seconds here, 'Pretty Cool Guys' are already becoming one of the biggest jokes in all of 'Primetime Championship Wrestling'... thats a tough task for any normal human being... but then you'd just be missing the point about 'PCG'... they're not normal... they're Pretty Cool PoP Icons. And so the day has gone by... and another promotional video has been taped by the first opponents of PCG at Fusion... do the boys care about ths video tape... in some context, they do care about this video... apparently, its the best video tape that I have been able to see in a very long time! However, there comes the time in every mans promo where he has to stick his head down for a few short moments... think about what is going to be said... before opening his mouth rather wide and letting the sounds of the words flow freely... allowing the words to eventually become a phrase and do one of two things... Interest people like they have never been interested before...Or...Bore people to the point where they all pull out 'Shotguns' and showing the living hell out of our famous Duo... Regardless of the motions of people that will be flowing around this very immediate area for this second in time... and every other second that will attempt to follow, its time for me to get down and dirty with the information that should be relayed to you at the beginning of this mater piece... que the 'Dragonball Z' recap music... ** 'Dragonball Z' recap music begins to pound out of some magical speakers into the background. ** Last time... on Pretty Cool G's...Our heros wandered into the magical and mystical confinements of an office... looking around for the two pieces of paper that they need to sign with regards to becoming official members of the 'Primetime Championship Wrestling' roster... they walked into that room feeling all bad ass... and found something a little bigger than they would be able to handle... 'Oh... Rich and Ryan... so you've come to my domain...' As you can see, the monster is already unable to communicate with us in any tone but the one that was relayed just that second ago... and that my friends, is if there is even any tone to the voice of this horrific monster. For at this very second in time... one of the 'Leather Chairs of Doom' remains perched infront of a big wooden desk, seemingly unwilling to turn around... Who is this chair..What is this chair...and more importantly, why the hell did I just bullshit almost everything here?Find out today on 'Dragonba...', ... no, Find out today on 'Pretty Cool G's' | - ^ - ^ - | Chapter 1 : The Beginning of a PoP era...Installment II : The identity... revealed?!... Welcome...Welcome...and Welcome...Hello to the second installment of what should continue to be the most amazing journey through time and hyperspace that people like yourselves, or anybody else for that matter... will never be able to better for another fifteen million years. Unless you were already able to notice... I simply changed a few words from the last piece to make this one seem alot more exciting than its really all cut out to be. In alot of context... its quite genius... don't ya think? No... Rich, this is the most stupid idea that you have ever had in your life... What the hell are you talking about Moore? You always seem to bitch on and on about any idea I've had... and hell, what about those bloody pathetic ideas that you come up with on 'How to pinch a female's bottom without them realizing'? Huh,Huh, Huh... Well... Well.... Well... Exactly... shut up for a few seconds you overgrown ape... and just listen to the story the way that its supposed to be told... and hell, if I even gave you the smallest idea of anything... I informed you that 'Intellectual Individuals are ALWAYS right'... that means, I rule... and you... well, its safe to say that you suck! Thats it... I'm going to tell Sean that you've been very nasty to me... and that you won't let me have the camera for a few seconds... ... Oh My God... Ok, the deal is this... when I finish doing this very important installment of the story... you can make some more of your PoP videos up... send them off to 'MTV' and try to watch as they refuse to play that tape on TRL. You know what people... sometimes I really wish I'd taken Sean Smith along with me... at least then he wouldn't stand in the back and cry about everything that happens. However, regardless of how much I would like to taunt my little friend about not being able to be the 'Beholder of this Camera'... I think its about time I moved over to the body of the promotional video... enjoy! | - ^ - ^ - | The picture begins to open slowly within the second installment of the first chapter... a small tidbit of information that was already being played around a good seventy-thousand times in our minds. Sure, we've heard that this bloody thing is going to get started... but the question is... 'When?'. Well, the picture now decides that its going to open alot more quickly... and we joined both myself and Ryan Moore in the office with that horrible demon chair... and all the other cool stuff that brought back memories of what life was like with my father... all the way back in my original hometown of London, England... I thought you were a native of Georiga, Rich? No Ryan... now run along! Getting back to the point of the story, the picture finally came to its biggest extension while I was standing a mere three paces away from the desk. This installment seemed to pretty much pick up from where we left it last, the chair... slowly beginning begining to turn again in the fashion that had come to shock and rivet us in the installment before! It was horrifying and it wasn't too long until Ryan Moore and myself looked over to it with our eyes... now filled with a extremely large sense of fear. 'STOP!!!', I shouted out in what could quite possibly be, the loudest that any member of any 'Boy Band' has been in their entire career... both myself and my mother believed that I should have won a 'Grammy' for the loudest voice in the world... but it was yet another Title taken away from me. The chair on the otherhand, had stopped in its place. I looked over to the item and could see that the bloody thing had only been able to move about two centimetres... 'What... are you doing... telling me to... STOP?', the chair bellowed, wondering why I had asked for it to stop and come nowhere too near to completing the task of a 180 degree turn. I looked a little white in the face at that moment... it was painfully obvious that I didn't like what I was seeing... and yes, it was indeed painful... I was an intellectual member of the society and now, I was being reduced to nothingness... 'Why... am I telling you to stop... erm, I'm not sure... but Ryan knows... don't you?', I answered in a tone that would make even Chuckie from 'The Rugrats' seem like the most powerful fighter in all of the land. With that sudden closing of my answer... and even a question toward my fellow artist... I took a BIG step backwards and pushed him into view... Rich... you didn't even come close to pushing me into the view of things... hell, you literally threw me over to the table and forced me to give a damned answer... Oh... did that happen to slip my mind... I'm really sorry about that Ryan... so anyway, there I was... throwing Ryan Moore with all the intensity in my body toward the desk. In true fashion, he kinda flopped onto the top and threw everything across the room... showing us a temper-tantrum that could only be matched by 'Mrs Angelica Pickles' from that very same show that Chuckie... Tommy... oh, all the little children that remain four years old for well over ten years of programming. 'Ryan... you can stop scrambling across the table like a crybaby now...', I said... shouting across the room so that everybody within a 10 block radius of me would be able to hear without any problem at all. Ryan pretty much stopped moving about the table like a crazed man and looked into my eyes with a glare that would indicate... 'Eh?'I took a few paces toward my friend and the evil chair... making sure my distance was kept... after all, I'm only 28yrs old and I need to stay alive until at least my 35th birthday. 'Ryan... look dude, that chair is filled some supernatural powers... so if you don't mind, I'd appreciate it if you simply.. stopped messing around... and said something to the chair...', I was slowly beginning to sound serious at this point... but in all fairness, I didn't care about Ryan Moore's life... What do you mean you didn't care about my life... I can't believe it... you told mom that you were going to make sure crazy stuff like that never happened... Mom can worry about that some other time... well, your mom anyway... as for my story people, please ignore the 'Uncool', 'Unhip', 'Unintellectual being' known as Ryan Moore... he just tries to ruin all my good stories. Now... where on earth was I... ah yes, the chair suddenly began to move around again... I was shocked to see something like this happen for the second time in as many promo's... never before had the chair attempted to swing a full distance!Both Ryan and myself were far too scared to try to make any movements... for the front of all chairs that were featured within 'Freaky Talking Chairs Volume VI'... sucked their victims into the apocolypse of doom... 'NNNNNNNOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!' was the only sound that you would have heard around the entire world as the chair came through to face us both. What? Where was the vortex of doom that had been documented of so highly in 'Freaky Talking Chairs Volume VI'? I looked over to where the chair was... it had now been able to complete the 180 degree turn that had only been prevented for the last hour and a half. I breathed a short sigh of relief... and left Ryan to curl up on the table... allowing the poor child to believe that he was going to get sucked into a magical wormhole sometime in the very immediate future. 'Erm... what happened to the wormhole that was supposed to eat me up?', I asked with a large sense of curiosity surrounding my eyes. I cocked my eyebrow like a man who was trying to be cool... and continued to stare at the.... MAN... in the seat. There was a brief moment of silence as Ryan Moore began to realize that there was no such thing as the wormhole... at least, there was no such thing as that insane invention in this room. The man... seated in this impeccable seat... who I must add, was signifcantly smaller than I would have expected anyone to be. The man looked at Ryan on his table... the at the floor... where items from his desk were gathered... before, placing his eyes upon me and smiling that sick smile that somebody gives to somebody else when they say...'Time to Play...' 'Excuse me kind Sir... I was wondering if you could just get those pieces of paper... that say.. 'PCG PCW Contracts?'.. I'd really appreciate it if you'd let my Tag team partner and myself... sign them and be on our way to Fusion.' I stated, trying to sound as business like as possible... after all, I am nothing but an intellectual member of society.. 'Well.... there is one thing that you may be able to do for us... before the papers come out onto the table.', the man said, smirking again as he did so. Ryan Moore suddenly sprung to life and looked at the man with an 'intimidating' glare... I'm using those little ' marks there because... Ryan Moore + Intimidation... ha,ha... thats the greatest joke I've heard in all five months of the 2002. 'What you talkin' about fat man?', he shot at the business character... trying his damnest to act like that little black kid, Arnold from the hit 1970's show... 'Different Strokes'. The Business man continued to gaze at us... laughing manically as the scene slowly... but surely began to fade out deep... into black... Who is this Business Man?What in the hell does he want?What task is required of both Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore... All this and more... in the NEXT Episode... of Pretty Cool G!!!
