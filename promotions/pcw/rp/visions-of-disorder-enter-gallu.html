Visions of Disorder: Enter Gallu

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Promoting sickness and disease is one thing, but inflicting it upon another human is another. That is the plan of the Mexican Devil upon his return to the PCW ring at March Madness following the sickening act that Aymin did to him last Turmoil. El Diablo seems thankful and bitter about it, a conflicting opinion just like the walking conflict of life that he is. Thankful, for the shot made him see reason. The headshot made him have his realisation of Babylonia, and the headshot also gave him the strength and insight in which he desires to be all that he can be. Bitter, however for the bloodshed. Bitter for ending all celebrations of victory and bitter as International Incident had returned to haunt him. A stranger is how El Diablo is being described. He is merely a shadow of his former self, only El Diablo likes his new self, he loves his new beliefs and would not trade them for anything. A stranger to his wife and a stranger to all previous existence of himself, it cannot be a pretty sight for Angel to see, but just like always she will surely stand by his side and follow him as ever. Asag, the first mythical demon of El Diablo's following, a dragon legend from over 7000 years ago. What role should he seek to play in this new temperament of El Diablo? Asag is all about sickness and disease, perhaps they will be the roles that play within El Diablo's mind, that play within El Diablo's heart and soul, if they exist. He still sits reading his book on Babylonia. A look of intrigue is all that is noticeable on the face of the Mexican Devil. Angel still in shock from the earlier occurrence has managed to get partly dressed. A black bra and black thong is what she has placed on so far. She would be dressed further if her husband didn't stop calling her into the room to hear about ancient Babylonia. 'Incredible,' exclaims the seated Mexican. Angel sits down from the standing position she was on, adjusting her thong. 'What is?' she asks slightly worried. He places the book down on his lap as he looks across at Angel full of enthusiasm. 'Gallu is,' he states in glee, 'he would slay all men that encountered him,' comments El Diablo, with a look of bloodlust in his eyes. His wife looks at him with a feigned interest. 'Is that good?' queries Angel to her odd husband. A huge smile fills Diablo's demented face. 'Imagine what power that could hold if I could do it,' he claims with a sick smile on his face, 'with my beliefs I can do that,' he quickly realises, 'I will slay all that get in my way, just like Gallu did,' he finishes happily. Angel stands up. 'Where are you going?' El Diablo quickly asks her. She stops in her tracks and looks a little panicked. 'To finish getting dressed,' she comments with a look of worry on her face. His eyes light up, that same fiery red as before, it causes Angel to sit down once more, and the red from his disappear. 'Not until I finish talking,' states the Mexican while staring at the semi naked body of his wife. Despite these new beliefs he has, he is still a man beneath all that, and keeping his wife dressed like that clearly shows it. He lifts up his book again as his wife remains still with worry, and afraid to move. 'Gallu is just like me,' he states evilly, 'no father, no mother, no brother, no sister, no child. I do however have a wife,' he finishes and looks across at Angel with lust in his eyes. Angel swallows hard. 'But the Gallu tells me that, that is fine,' he comments, much to the relief of Angel, 'although the Gallu had neither the face nor the body of the divine Sukkals that served as emissaries between the gods themselves, they did hold a staff in their hands and carried a weapon on their loins,' he says describing the next demon upon and within him. Angel watches on as El Diablo describes with such passion and enthusiasm. She soon realises how much good these beliefs are for El Diablo and begins to relax a little around him. But being fully relaxed with him around is a definite no, as El Diablo is too much of a stranger for that. 'Interesting,' she comments, still feigning interest. He nods and smiles across at his wife as he continues researching from the book of Babylonia. The book, which he once described as his bible. 'Asag and Gallu will be my protectors and they will serve my purpose well for what I want to do in PCW soon enough,' Diablo says with a look of power in his eyes. He places the book down on the coffee table nearby. One of the rare moments when he puts the book down. Angel watches him happily, knowing she can get dressed soon. 'For once in my life I feel like I matter,' he says with a sick joy, 'for once in my life I feel whole, I feel at one with these beliefs. And this is only the beginning of the new Diablo,' he finishes looking like a man possessed with joy. Angel smiles but still remains speechless as to not anger her husband. 'March Madness I will reveal this change to the world and Aymin will get his sufficient punishment for what he did to me,' speaks El Diablo, dictator like. Angel applauds as she finally begins to understand the change in El Diablo. 'Then I shall practice my beliefs on those in the ring and prove myself once again,' he finishes with the red look in his eyes that no longer scares Angel. Angel stands up smiling, as El Diablo remains smiling from his seat with the red eyes that look to possess him. The strange demonic nature of El Diablo continues as he picks up the book once more to study deeper into the Babylonian past and make his own path in life.
