Good thing shrimp take long to digest!

25 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/25/02 You’re going in circles. You’ve been running for ten minutes, and you just now find out that you’re going in circles. In all of the panic, you didn’t realize that you had been making the same right turns around the block. As you continue to run for your life, you glance backwards behind yourself and notice that your attacker isn’t there. You stop to catch your breath, knowing that he will soon catch up to you. Even though he’s much bigger than you, you’re obviously out of shape. Had you known a psycho would be chasing you, you probably would have gone jogging more. In all of the panic, you’ve lost your train of thought. As you kneel next to a parked Honda Accord to catch your breath, you beat yourself up for being so careless. You stupid fat F*ck! Why did you have to eat the rest of his dinner? As you try and rest, you remember seeing the man get up from his table and head for the bathroom. As he stepped out of the room, you noticed that he still had three of four good pieces of shrimp on his plate. Still being hungry from the horribly small slab of steak you had, you decided that you had to have more. No one would notice right? I mean you would have paid, and would be out of the restaurant before that man came back. And it’s his fault for not finishing his food isn‘t it? As you begin to wipe the sweat from your forehead, you remember snagging the three pieces of shrimp, and stuffing your mouth on the way to the cashier. At the time you thought that no one had noticed. But you were wrong. As you paid the cashier for your horribly overpriced dinner, you notice the large man come back into the room. There, you get a good glimpse of just how big he is. At least seven feet tall and three hundred plus pounds you estimate, as you watch him look for his missing shrimp. Your throat begins to swell up as you see an older man explain to him where his food is. Then the man points directly at you. Before you can curse the bald headed geezer for telling on you, you realize that the larger man is looking directly at you. You lock eyes as the large man snarls at you. Before you could get your brain thinking straight, the man started heading towards you. You wanted to stay and apologize, but the man’s face told you that that you wouldn’t have gotten away with it that easily. Like the coward you are, you dash for the door. As you start to panic, you realize that you are in a part of the city you aren’t familiar with. As you quickly look around, you can’t decide which way to go. You look back at the restaurant, and see that man closing in on you. As you begin to run down the street, you remember that this was the restaurant that your coworker suggested to you, because it was in a safe neighborhood. Yeah, safe. You remember how you had decided earlier in the year that you didn’t need to buy car. You were going to stick with public transportation. You stupidly took the bus here. Of all the times to take the bus, you had to take it today. As you approach the same bus stop in which you arrived, you don’t even hesitate to race right past it. The bus would take too long to get here. You know he would catch you before it arrived. Down the street you run until you spot a shortcut. You notice an alley that leads to another street running horizontal from the one that you’re on. Without a second thought, you dart down the alleyway. You figure it’s almost impossible that the man could know where you went. That is until you see him turn the corner and head down that same alleyway. You gasp and start to run again, still not believing that the man stayed with you. As you race down the street, and make a right, you glance behind you and notice that the man isn’t behind you anymore. Either you ditched him, or he knows something that you don’t. You decide it would be wise if you didn’t wait to find out. You start to run again, taking the first right turn that you saw. What wasn’t known to you at the time was that you were running around the same block. As you peered down the street, you notice that you’re approaching the restaurant where all your troubles began. You stop dead in your tracks and start to run the other way. As you are running out of breath, you notice a Honda, and decide you’ll hide behind it and catch your breath. Hey maybe you’ll get lucky at he’ll give up on you and leave. The strong beating coming from your chest brings you back to reality. As you look down the street towards the restaurant, something grabs you by the hair, and yanks you to your feet.“What do we have here?” As you fight to free your hair from the man’s grasp you sense that it is the same man that has been chasing you. As you turn around and begin to get a glance at him, the man grabs you across the throat and begins choking you. You release your hands from your scalp, and focus whatever energy you have left, on freeing your throat from this man’s grasp. As you desperately try to get air into your lungs, the man releases his grip on you. As you struggle fill your lungs with oxygen, you feel a crushing blow come across your face. You fall backward, landing on the parked Honda behind you. You become disoriented from the attack, but try to get to your feet. Before you can get very far, the man slams you back down to the hood of the car. You try to scream for help, but the man just hits you across the face again. “Shut the f*ck up! I’m not going to hurt you!” You stop screaming, and wonder to yourself why the man hit you if he isn’t going to hurt you? “What’s your name?” You draw a blank, but it quickly comes to you. “G..G..Gary” you mumble. The man then climbs onto the hood of the car, and stands above you. “Well Gary, I want to ask you something.” Not sure if he wants you to apologize or pay for your dinner, you reply “S..Sure anything!” The man draws a knife then asks, “would you object if I told you I was going to get my shrimp back?” As you try to scream, the man jabs the knife into your stomach. Your screaming becomes gasping as you try and fight the burning pain in your gut. The man begins increases the pressure as the slices open your stomach. As you start to black out, you hear him say: “Well what do you know? Them shrimp are still edible!” (The scene fades)
