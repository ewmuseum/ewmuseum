Title: Hallmark of a Hypocrite: Part One Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: not entered Ever since The HIT's return to PCW, something, no, someone has greatly irked The HIT. That someone is Xavier Kannon. In his return match, Mall Brawl, he had disposed of Tim Walden, and was about to do the same to Brandi Wilson, when Kannon came from behind, and Hallmarked him. In his Television Title Match, again, Kannon picked up The HIT after he had taken a Duke Williams powerbomb on the steel steps, and once again Hallmarked him. The HIT may not be a genius outside of math, but he certainly sees a trend developing here. Essentially, Xavier Kannon has a great ability to beat up Trapezoid when he is distracted. But now, The HIT is not distracted. His eyes are wide open. And now, on Tuesday Turmoil, Xavier Kannon will have to march down to the ring with his personal sex toy, Eleanor Hall, and face The Archbishop of Angles one on one in the center of the ring. Is HIT confident? Let's just say he doesn't see much chance of taking a third Hallmark... ::In Brigham Young High School's library sits The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid, poring over several tomes of literature, and with a stack of computer printouts at his side. He smiles wearily, looking as if he has been at his task for several hours.:: The HIT: Finally! After about six weeks back, I finally get my chance to go after the owner of this company! Xavier Kannon.....the original object of my ire. When I threatened the Front Office before arriving, you were at the front of my hitlist. Because you are responsible for them all....Duke, Walden, and Brandi are all your little pets....that you use for whatever you need. You expect this company to lay down at your every whim..pay your taxes mindlessly! Well, it's not going to happen! I refuse to be treated as a second-class citizen here! ::Opening one of the books, he begins to page through it, looking for something specific...:: The HIT: Kannon...for you, it's always been about greed, lust, and power. Greed you inherited from Tyler Nelson, the man who made you everything you are today... :Trapezoid lays down a printed out picture of the man known as 'The Greediest Player in the Game'. The HIT: Lust....lust is from that pitiful excuse for a human being you call a wife. I mean, do you two HAVE to flaunt public decency and fuck each other everywhere you go? I swear, I ought to call the police on you two for indecent exposure. But, I do suppose I have to give Miss Eleanor SOME credit, after all, she made you as much as Nelson did. And then, the last piece of the Kannon equation..... ::A picture of Mrs. Kannon-Hall is laid down besides that of Tyler Nelson.:: The HIT: Power. The ownership of PCW. The Front Office. All of it is controlled by Kannon. With Walden, he can levy and collect taxes. With Ellie, he can make any match he wants. With the Office at his beck and call.....he has more power than anyone else in the wrestling world. Which is good for him, because, without his greed, lust, and power....he's nothing! :Trapezoid smiles, a big, bright smile of sheer bliss.:: The HIT: Throughout his entire career, he has relied on those three things to make him who he is! Strip away Tyler Nelson, Eleanor Hall, and the power his position commands him, and Christian Xavier Kannon would be a very very different man. A man, in fact, that loses battle royales in under four minutes. Ah yes, Xavier was able to accomplish THAT feat without greed, lust, or power. But....indeed, there was a major step in between the loss at March Madness and his rise to the above things. A major step...in the form of an obscure religion known as Scientology, known only to most people by thespians such as Tom Cruise and Nicole Kidman....but used by Kannon to a greater extent than any of its other practicioners. Claiming oppression by a decidedly non-Scientologist PCW, Kannon rode the wave of controversy with Eleanor to hook up with Tyler Nelson and the Ultimate Authority, and the rest is history... :Finally, The HIT flips over the book he was paging through earlier. His grin resurfaces, as he goes on.:: The HIT: So, I had to figure out....how do I discredit this jackass? I mean, his damned Scientology got him to where he is today! PCW officials sat there and sat there and had to buy his bullshit about being oppressed, because no one had the half the BRAIN CELL required to do some research into it. I am sure that I can defeat Kannon, and equally sure that I shall defeat the winner of the Brandi/Dante Jackson match and move onto the Cruiserweight Title Tournament match at Alliances..but I feel it is my duty to you all, the PCW fans, to reveal that Xavier Kannon's machinations were nothing more than an elaborate ruse designed to elevate his power in this promotion. ::Trapezoid nods his head, as if discovering this fact for the first time, unknowing that everyone kind of realizes Kannon abused the whole Scientology thing.:: The HIT: Yes ladies and gentlemen, after hours of meticulous research, careful analysis, and checking of my theories, I have come up with Trapezoid's Fifth Theorem. Now, the statement this theorem makes is powerful, elegant, and shocking at the same time, so if you have a weak stomach, please leave...gone? Good. Trapezoid's Fifth Theorem states simply....Kannon is not equal to Scientologist, or, in layman's terms.... Xavier Kannon is not a Scientologist! You want proof you say? It's coming....oh you'd best believe it's coming.... ::Fade to the book lying on the table, entitled 'Scientology: The Fundamentals of Thought'.....:: ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
