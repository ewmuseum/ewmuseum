'...even in the uncontrollable madness that is New Breed there must be reason'-- Looking past the characters to find reason.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: now So much has changed since we last saw CCW. The last time we saw him was a standoff member of New Breed, explaining to us that his return to New Breed was about controversy, not about any of the ideals which Snow, Chris Chase and Dustin Downey enforce. But either Corey's goals had once again coincided with the rest of New Breeds, as Snow dropped the belt to him on Friday Fusion in order to make sure Big Alan Steele did not get a shot at it. That, and Corey giving the title back to Snow the following Sabotage so as to rob Big Alan Steele of a title shot for the third time, were surely controversial, but was that the motive behind the actions or had Corey finally stepped in line, finally on the same page with the rest of New Breed. ::Our scene opens up at (surprise surprise) a bar, late on a Thursday night. The night before a big match on Fusion that will decide the outcome of the main event of Supremacy, whilst Big Alan Steele-types are busy taking their vitamins and saying their prayers, these four men are out on a drinking binge. Instantly we see the entire New Breed crew all seated together in a booth, the first time since their revival that they have been seen together away from a PCW arena. Each man is positioned on their seats differently, but all are comfortable, holding women either side of them as trophies.:: CCW: Drink up boys, I'm paying. Chase: You? But, you never pay! CCW: Yes, but until now, I was never a billionaire either. From now on when people talk about New Breed, Snow will be the superior one, you will be the arrogant one, I will be the rich, talented and controversial one... ::Chris flashes Corey an annoyed look, but Corey ignores it and continues.:: CCW: And Dustin..... ::CCW eyes Dustin, who is taking Corey up on the drinks offer, swallowing at a quicker rate than Alan Steele's Mother.:: CCW: Dustin's the 'special' one. ::It is a well known fact that CCW has always despised Dustin Downey, whether as a member of High Impact, Wild Deuces or New Breed, as Chase's sidekick or as a corpse, CCW has always hated him. Now Dustin is apparently a changed man, no longer PCW's personal jester, a serious mover and shaker of the business of professional wrestling. But leopards like Corey don't change their spots so easily, and according to him, neither do leopards like Dustin Downey. And be it from alcohol killing his brain cells or from the brain cells not being there in the first place, as Dustin looks up at Corey with a big cheesy grin Corey has to wonder whether Dustin can even spell the word leopard.:: Chris: Yes but if Snow is the superior one, doesn't that completely null out your own attributes. CCW: Err.... umm... I dunno. 'For God's sake man, stop philosophising about matters that are so simple. The simple fact is that we are better than everyone else is... particularly me... and nobody can do a damn thing about it.' ::That's Snow, of course. The man with the belt around his waist and a bottle of whisky clutched to his hand. Two women hang off his shoulder, but he barely even acknowledges their existence, talking to them only to yell at them for doing something wrong. Corey can't help but grin at Snow, the man has been hated for the majority of his life, for so long that he no longer even needs a reason to be hated, that's just the way he is. Snow is Snow, he hasn't ever needed to justify that to anyone, hell, the only time people have ever loved him was when they loved to hate him. Perhaps that simple reason is why that belt is around Snow's waist right now, and not Chris, Corey or even (shudder) Dustin's.:: Corey: I guess you're right... Snow: Of course I'm right! The fact is that New Breed has three World Champions, one of them being the greatest World Champion of all time, making it the most successful tag team of all time. Chris: Yeh, even Dustin's better than the majority of PCW. The only people in PCW who can possibly stop us right now are ourselves, sure as HEEL not some Russel Crowe wannabe. ::Corey smirks at the hidden irony of this statement. For right now he is very conflicted as to whether or not he should stop the New Breed. He joined them to be controversial, but they didn't join him to be controversial. Snow and Chris Chase revived New Breed for entirely different reasons, and God knows why Dustin's there. Why should he fight with and for someone he doesn't believe in. For even in the uncontrollable madness that is New Breed there must be reason, for there is at least some reason behind everything, even controversy.:: CCW: Sure. Steele got a couple of tainted wins over us, but to tell you the truth if I had to beat my opponents through disqualification and interferences I'd shoot myself. ::Corey plays along with the rest of New Breed, ignoring both the conflict and inside and the fact that the only interferences that occurred in his match with BAS were New Breed interferences.:: CCW: And there's no way he can possibly beat all FOUR of us, there's no way he can possibly take the belt away from where it rightfully belongs. Downey: That reminds me, who is the champ anyway? ::Both Chris and Corey's eyes turn to Snow, who is resting back, not at all annoyed by Dustin's alcohol-induced question:: Snow: You are, Dustin. Dustin: Really? That's nice. ::And with that Dustin Downey's turns his neck to the side to vomit onto the floor of the bar.:: Chris: Dude, what the hell have you been eating!? Man, I think I better get you home. Give us the keys to the limo Corey. ::Corey tosses Chris the keys to the famous New Breed limo. Chris grabs Downey and half guides-half carries him out the door. This leaves only Snow and Corey left in the room, Corey is looking down at his empty glass.:: Snow: I guess we'll have to take my car back then. Now where in the name of my superior self did I put my keys.... ::Snow searches his pockets.:: Snow: Ahh, there they are on the ground. ::Snow reaches under the table but can't quite get it.:: Snow: Hey Corey, can you hold my title? ::Corey's eyes light up for a second, but only for a second. He looks up to see Snow holding out the title for him only to realise that Snow was asking Corey to hold the title whilst he reaches down to pick up the keys, not to actually give him the PCW title in the same fashion as he did on Friday Fusion. Corey takes the belt and Snow reaches under the table and pulls out a set of car keys. He hands them to Corey and Corey hands back the belt. Corey's shoulders slump once again and he looks back down to his glass. Silence ensues the room as Corey stares miserably at his empty glass whilst the usually over active Snow lays back and enjoys the company of the women. Finally his eyes move to Corey and he leans forward.:: Jason Snow: What's a matter kid? ::Corey's eyes move up for a moment, catching a glimpse of Snow, not much older than Corey but definitely a lot more experienced. If Snow wasn't such a cocky, young hothead people would call him a veteran, that is if they were intelligent enough to look past the young superior character he portrays for long enough to notice the fact that he never breaks it. And now here is Jason Snow, talking one on one to Corey, man to man, first PWA student to become PCW World Champ to the second PWA student to become PCW World Champ. Just by looking at his eyes Corey can tell that he is serious. Corey looks back down to empty bourbon glass.:: Corey: It's this, New Breed. I mean, why am I here? I don't understand. Why did I join New Breed when they don't even believe in the same cause as me? Jason Snow: By greatness Corey! What is your problem! ::Corey sighs, thinking that Snow has reverted back into character. But instead Snow says something very intelligible, in a very Snow-esque way.:: Jason Snow: Don't you get it, we're not in New Breed because we believe in the same cause, we're not in New Breed because we're friends.... in fact to tell you the truth I think Dustin's an idiot, Chris is one of those homosexuals and you're a loser. We're in New Breed because we ARE the New Breed, we are the only wrestlers worthy of those fans and this belt. When it comes to doing what we do, nobody does it better, no matter what angle you look at it from. You, me, Chris, Dustin, the fans, Big Alan Steele, we all look at New Breed differently, and though I say fuck Big Alan Steele and fuck the fans, even they have to say that we are the best at what we do. ::As the words sink in on Corey, he realises that the opinions of this side of Snow, whilst retaining his bent perception of the World, are definitely right. But that does not make much difference to Corey, because the logic still comes from Snow, to reason with Corey you must use Corey's logic.:: Corey: ......... Jason Snow: C'mon man......... hey I know what will cheer you up... ::Snow grabs one of the women hanging off his arms and backhands her across the face, causing her to fall onto the ground. Snow grabs the woman by the head and runs her face back and forth through the putrid stench that is Dustin Downey's puke. Corey slowly looks up.:: Corey: Woman-beating? Vomit? ::Corey bangs his fits on the table.:: CCW: Now THAT'S controversial! ::CCW breaks into a smile and walks over to where the woman is lying on the ground. He runs up and kicks her right in the gut.:: CCW: Man that hit the spot. I'm feeling better already! Snow: C'mon then, let's go hide in alleyways and scare old people as they pass by. ::The two begin to walk for the door, but Snow stops Corey.:: Snow: Oh and Corey.... If you ever tell anybody about this....... the next time I drop the title to you it will be over the back of your head by greatness! ::Corey nods and he and the killin' villain exit the pub, both renewed in full. The scene fades out.:: !The End!
