The Training - Chapter 4 - Preparing for the dream

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th August 2002 ***OOC Note: The following RPs are from a series I started while I was back in PWA. I shall be carrying on the series here in PCW so I figured it might help to provide you with the beginning segments of the series. Thanks :-)*** Chapter 4 – Preparing for the dream RECAP: Charlotte Lawrence, an illiterate, stereotypical housewife. Normally she slaves over a stove, but aspirations recently started to run high. A couple of weeks ago she gleefully informed boyfriend Joe Nero that she wanted to become a professional wrestler. After consulting the local gym owner, she sent in a videotape to the Primetime Wrestling Academy that demonstrated her personality and communicating prowess. In a move that many would see as a gamble, they accepted her onto one of their weekend training camps. How would she do? It was the eve of the PWA training camp, and for Charlotte, this would be a big barrier to overcome if she was to achieve her goal of becoming a professional wrestler. The personality and hunger for the job is undeniable, but will her physical build be a stumbling block? This is what Charlotte herself was questioning. At eleven o’clock in the evening, Joe was already in the land of nod, but Charlotte was sitting alone in the study. The landing light sifted through cracks in the doorframe, whilst the only light turned on in the room was a lamp. This revealed Charlotte was sitting on a red desk chair, the cotton cushioning supporting her back. Her legs were underneath the old oak desk, which had little scratches on the top from all 50 years of usage, for it was Joe’s Grandfather who purchased the desk in an antiques shop in 1952. When an average person is full to the brim with personal thoughts and emotions, they will often grab a blunt pencil and scribble them onto a piece of paper, otherwise known as a diary. Unfortunately, Charlotte has no such luxury, due to the illiterate condition she has, so she has to record her deepest hopes and fears in another way. Stretching across the desk, she reached out for the portable cassette player, before deftly pushing down the record button with her left forefinger. “Thursday 6th June, 2002,” Charlotte softly whispered, just loud enough for the recorder to trace the sound. There was no need to disturb Joe and his sleep at this hour. “Tomorrow morning I’m leaving for New York and a PWA training camp, my first experience of wrestling. I know I have the heart for the job, but will my body stand up to the rigours of such physical brutality? As Joe kindly informed me this afternoon, at the moment I am but a mere housewife,” reminisced Charlotte, her eyes glazing over slightly as a result of too much ironing. That boring, repetitive motion over the clothes. Back and forth. Back and forth. “This may sound corny, but I’ve never felt so certain about anything in my life. On the face I may by a shy girl, but inside my body is crying out for creativity, and I believe wrestling can satisfy that cry,” carried on Charlotte, her finger tentatively hanging over the ‘Stop’ button. “Anyway, my bags are all packed and loaded in the back of the car, waiting for the journey to New York. It’s going to be a long day tomorrow, no doubt about it. I need some sleep.” With that, Charlotte brought her finger down, like an executioner, and the gentle buzzing the tape player had been making stopped. Rising from the seat, the 22yr old wheeled it underneath the desk. She turned off the light, before silently closing the door, and disappearing into her bedroom. In a blue cuddly bear patterned night dress, she tossed her purple felt slippers across the floor, before swinging her legs into bed, rustling the linen. With a smile on her face, cuddling up to Joe, she closed her eyes and drifted happily to sleep. “Wakey wakey dear!” Charlotte sat up slowly, all her senses overloading. Joe was smiling at her, with sun rays streaming into the room beneath the nylon curtains. The distinct smell of coffee also told her that Joe was waving a mug carefully under her nose. Wiping her eyes, she found the strength to smile warmly back. Carefully, with two hands, she accepted the china mug, and slowly sipped the coffee, the aroma spreading to all parts of her body. Putting the coffee down on her white wooden bedside cabinet, panic washed over Charlotte. Like a bolt, her back straightened up, and the perfectly pampered face tensed. “What time is it?!” asked Charlotte, almost delirious. There was no way anything was going wrong for her today. Joe’s smile did not waver though. He sat down on the warm cotton bed sheet, putting his arms around Charlotte’s shoulders. “Don’t worry Charlie, there’s plenty of time. We don’t need to leave for another half hour,” he comforted, accompanied by a kiss on the cheek. Comforting though these words might be, Charlotte was frantic at the moment, and they did little to calm her down. Jumping out of bed, she hastily put on her slippers and velvet purple dressing grown, which was hung from a plastic hook on the beige walls. Whilst locking the bathroom door behind her, she left Joe with a comparison for him to brew over. “Compare it to you and your horses missing the Kentucky Derby. This is my big day Joe!” shouted Charlotte, simultaneous to rotating the shower dial. For the first time since her abrupt awakening, with the lukewarm water dripping off her face, Charlotte smiled. The journey had started. BEEP BEEP! BEEP BEEP! Charlotte raised her wrist in front of her face, and the watch informed her the time was 3pm. For seemingly hours on end Joe had driven their green painted Ferrari across the USA, speeding so they could arrive at their destination of New York in plenty of time. As Charlotte withdrew her wrist from her field of sight, the couple were soaring across a bridge into New York. The sight couldn’t have been more perfect, with the river being gently rippled by the tip of the insignificant wind. A steam boat was chugging it’s way along, transporting the local residents to positions during their daily routine. “Damn!” exclaimed Joe, slamming on the breaks. Since leaving Prairie Town it had been plain sailing, but the second they had pulled into a city they were confronted by a never ending car park. Joe slapped the leather steering wheel in frustration, before lifting his blue tinted sunglasses, propping them on his scalp. Charlotte merely rolled her eyes, very observant of her boyfriend’s short fuse. “Calm down,” she comforted, patting Joe on the back, “I’ve got plenty of time till I need to be at the training complex. We’ve made really good time today; you’ve done brilliantly!” For his efforts, Joe was rewarded with a peck on the cheek, to which he positively beamed. Perhaps it wasn’t so bad, the building was only five streets away now. Sure enough, the couple made it there on time, pulling up outside the hotel/training complex just after half past three. The front was certainly very lavish, with the hotel’s name (The Epitome) illuminated in flashing lights. Two big, old wooden doors were propped open with a metal wedge, and it seemed like a metaphor: Charlotte’s life was opening up. With a mixture of trepidation, eagerness and dejection, Charlotte nipped out of the car. Wandering around to the boot, she knew she was going to be away from Joe for a weekend, which was not an experience she was anticipating with great enjoyment. She was just about to open the boot herself when it appeared to do a magic act and lift itself up. There was no magic of course, but Joe, willing to please at every opportunity. Grabbing the straps attached to the two bags, he heaved them out the back, dropping them on the concrete. It was indeed true what they said about women, they never travelled light. But that was soon forgotten, as the happy couple stared into each others eyes. A few loving good byes were murmured, and Charlotte even shed a few tears. They had been together for two years now, and this was to be the first occasion when they spent lengthy time apart. With a heavy heart, Joe got back into his seat, preparing for the return trip to Prairie Town. Releasing the hand brake, he accelerated off down the road, and Charlotte waved at him until the green Ferrari turned around a corner and out of sight. Time to get on with the task at hand. Charlotte walked into the lobby, and emerged a few minutes later with a smartly dressed porter at her side. Using his royal-esque white gloves, he moved the bags into the hotel, where Charlotte checked in. “Reservation for Charlotte Lawrence?” enquired Charlotte, smiling as ever. The receptionist was delighted to oblige, and when the question was posed about payment, Charlotte gleefully replied that the Primetime Wrestling Academy would pay for it. She caught the lift up to the third floor, and walked down the expensively fitted corridor. Plants at every corner, spotless red carpets, and artistic lamp shades all added to the aura that she was now mixing with the elite. The next day she would be in for a massive surprise when she mingled with the streetwise wrestling wannabes.
