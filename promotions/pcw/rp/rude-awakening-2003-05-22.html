Rude Awakening

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-22-03 Now it is you who find yourself overpowered in the midnight torpor, staring into the abyss, surrounded by darkness. Ask yourself, I beg of you, there is only one question, to which, there is only one answer. Why do you choose to walk to path that has been laid before you? I lay awake, not suffering from the inability to fall asleep per-say, more over the complete fear of it. I know it sounds childish, as if I dread the figment of my imagination hiding beneath my bed or on the other side of the door to my closet, but it’s not that, it never has been. My four walls reverberate as my eyes twinge losing focus, I’m so tired, so, so tired. The white nothing attached to my cell becomes nothing more than a tattered, dull gray as I begin to feel my brain slowly shut down and my head softly, gently falls back into my feathered pillow. Thrown upon my box-spring mattress lain in the exact center of my diminutive studio apartment, how do I know it’s the exact center? I’ve had too many sleepless nights to count entertaining myself with such trivial chores as measuring, then remeasuring, and measuring again. I begin to wonder if I had actually fallen asleep already, or if this is just another slip into madness. You know the state of consciousness in between totally awake and completely asleep? You snap up in your bed drenched in sweat, trying to convince yourself all the while you had yet to actually cease to be awake, yet for reasons beyond your explanation you’ve just somehow woke up. You thrash about wildly, unsure of where you are, yet you know good and well that you are in your home, in your bed, in the place you are supposed to feel most safe. Anyway, I digress. I’ve counted the squared tiles on my four walls a million times, four thousand three hundred and sixteen tiles upon my walls, another one thousand and sixty-two cover my floor, and one black plate hangs alone on my ceiling. For as long as I can remember, which isn’t much beyond a week and a half now I’ve been subjected to the same room, night after night. I can’t recall why I feel the need to return to this place every night, but the monotony of my day supplies me with little desire for anything else. The nighttime, here in my cell, as I now refer to it, is where my excitement arrives. I close my eyes eventually every third night or so after no longer being able to remain awake and enter a new life, one which I withhold the ability to differentiate from reality, because for me, it’s one and the same. I feel it all slip away, drifting further from veracity and closer to it at the same time. I was overly confused the first few days, it was impossible to make sense of and I was terrified each and every day when I awoke. So genuinely afraid that I fought every night while the entire world was sleeping to stay awake, just to make it another minute, then another second, until the sun would break through my window flooding my sore eyes with more pain, but a pleasurable pain that I oft began to look forward to because it meant that I had survived another twenty four hours. As the days passed my intrigue increased, yet my will power began to squander and I eventually found myself lying here, in this same spot, nodding off. With no fight left inside by body and mind, I succumbed to the sleep and the horror began. I begin walking down a vacant street, the air blows sharply and coldly down the welcomed wind tunnel created by the high rise buildings laden upon the city block. It’s not a familiar city, the streets have no name nor do the shops located on the floor level of each of the buildings. Every block looks like the last, as if though it were an exact replica of the one before it. The horizon in the distance mocks me as I work to near it by seemingly moving away with each step I take towards it. I want to stop and rest, I need to, but I cannot, my body refuses to cooperate with my mind, as if though they are not one in the same, but operate on different levels, controlled by opposing presence. Looking to the window as I walk I notice I’m dressed fully in gray, from head to toe, wrapped in some kind of a uniform, which actually appears to be my exoskeleton as I can’t remove it. My hands are enslaved as well as are my feet by the same dull gray swathe, and when I look up to my face, I’m taken aback by the visage staring back at me. It’s not human, no facial expression at all, completely blank of any color and totally motionless. At first glance it would appear to be a mask, but when I lift my hand in attempt to remove it, I find it two has become a part of me, completely blank with the exception of where my eyes should be, there is a break in the mask from where I can see. I slowly shuffle near my reflection in the window, still not accepting what I am seeing until I am standing almost completely up against the building, staring into my empty face, that’s when I shift to my eyes. They appear to remain; yet I look more closely still seeing nothing. My nose pressed against the glass I make out the shape of two spheres, and slight movement, and to my amazement discover that I now look through two dead black orbs that apparently flow like oil in water, like if you were to look directly at a ominous black cloud from inches away as it swirled about before wreaking havoc on the land below. It wasn’t me! I had become so lost in the reflection of me that I hadn’t noticed that two men had approached me from behind until I strayed my view over my shoulder in rumination. Spinning on my heel to stand face to face with two men dressed in black suits, black top hats, wearing black shoes, and no expression on their faces. They seem to communicate to each other without the use of words and I suddenly found myself in their grasp, being dragged away down the street. I try with all my capacity to scream out for help, but seeing as there was nobody present to answer my call, I too hear nothing. Pushed into a fissure where the buildings suddenly opened like a mouth in front of my eyes I get thrown to the ground and soon there after a heavy metal door slams shut. When I awake, I am back in my home, here in this mattress in the center of my room, every morning at 8:18 am. I remember nothing except what I have told you hear today. Wait; there is something else, injection. My door swings open and my clouded eyes are blinded immediately showing me only the silhouette of three men. They pick me up off the cold concrete floor and remove me from my room where I am taken to another room, which I can’t distinguish. I’m thrown upon a table and strapped in, face down I see nothing but the black boots shuffling around the table beneath the gray concrete floor and me. I hear nothing as I assume the men are communicated without words but I begin to sense what I can only explain is the muffled thoughts of numerous minds. A sharp object punctures the base of my neck and painfully I drift back out of consciousness. That is when I awoke last time, seemingly for the first time, again. The question vibrates painfully in my head, bouncing back and forth between my ears. I can’t shake it. “Why do you choose to walk the path that has been laid before you?” Why? Because I have no other choice…………….
