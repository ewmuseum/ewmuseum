Alliances Promo #1

19 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/19/02 We open with The End standing in front of the viewer, directly addressing the viewer in front of a wooden door, white-painted brick forming the wall. The End:'Let me go ahead and explain what you're about to see. The following is a promo that pretty much makes fun of Ellie, Brandi and Tim Walden as Front Office members. It's about two white women living in the bad part of town.' The End shrugs his shoulders. The End:'Hey, no one's accusing Walden of being a very masculine guy. Well, I'm also fusing Walden and Teresa Tomas into one character because Walden's a woman and Tomas is butch. The white women are big booty white hoes and live in the white ghetto, so this is also a jab at Playaz, Inc. And there is a cameraman and some movie elements involved, so let's just say that it's also taking potshots at El Diablo, assuming he's still a crazed movie producer/director. I really don't pay much attention to him. Anyway, that's what... a potential 9 people I'm insulting in one symbolism promo? While I couldn't insult all of PCW in this one promo, rest assured I will get to all of them. And before I forget, this is one of many promos that I shall be cutting against my opponents in the Alamo Soda Contract Match and the Alliances Main Event. So no one complain about relevance.' There is an impatient knocking at the door. Cyclonica:'Get out of the bathroom, Silverfish!' Releasing a sigh, The End shakes his head at the ground, his hands on his hips. The End:'Anyway, enjoy the promo. And if this is Ellie, Brandi, Walden, Tomas, El Diablo, Playaz, Inc, or the Front Office... screw you.' The camera zips around in a fast pan for no apparent reason, taking us through the interior of a first-floor apartment in the middle of Newark. The moonlight, mixed in with the police lights, creeps in through the dusty Venetian blinds, which hide from the interior view bulletproof glass. Newark after dark on the street? Come on, SOMEONE is getting shot. The shag carpeting absorbs the light and wooden walls reflect it back at the windows. Either way, it's not too bright. Yes, it's the City That Has No Future... Newark, New Jersey. A huge water boiler is haphazardly placed in the middle of the floor, and it obstructs our view of the apartment. *Ten minutes later* We finally move past the water boiler so that we see the focal point, the bullet-ridden door. It is red, boarded up from the inside. If the cameraman didn't drop down through the hole in the ceiling above the door that he quickly pans up to, we would be left to wonder how he got in. Currently the door unlocks and the knob is jiggled left and right, but the boards keep it from opening. From behind the door, we hear two voices. Woman 1:'Step back.' Woman 2:'You got this?' We hear someone on the other side charge the door and hit it full speed. The door doesn't budge and the person on the other side releases a wail of pain. Woman 1: 'Dammit!' Woman 2:'You hurt your head? Should've hit it with your shoulder.' Woman 1:'Shut up!' The cameraman takes it upon himself to step forward and open the door for them. He sets the camera down on the floor and takes out a crowbar hanging from his belt. He pries the boards off and opens the door. From the other side two figures throw the door open and step into the apartment. The first -- hair as red as fire, dressed in a red leather vest, black fishnet shirt, and a pair of white hot pants -- reaches into her pocket and takes out a Zippo lighter and a pack of Winston 500's, taking out a cigarette and lighting it to take a long drag. She tosses a crack pipe, a dime bag, and some money in an open duffel bag on the dirt (this place is REALLY dilapidated) floor, obviously not too concerned about it at the moment. The impact of the bag hitting the floor throws up a cloud of dust through the cramped apartment. The sound is muffled, but still rouses the squatting crackheads before they drift back off into their drug-induced near-coma. Shifting her dark Rayban sunglasses up onto her head, the redhead spits onto the ground with a groan. Brellie`!:'Man, our pimp is spoiling us.' The brown-haired woman charges forward and cluthes the cameraman by the throat, tossing him out. Brellie`! notices the camera on the floor. Brellie`!:'Um... Timesa... I think you kicked out the cameraman.' Timesa:'What? Again?' Brellie`!:'Go get him!' Timesa opens the door and goes to retrieve the cameraman. She's a little like the other woman. Both have the same 100 pound 40 D-Cup frame with huge asses. But Timesa dresses more conservatively, like a Japanese school girl for some reason in a long-sleeved white shirt, red ascot, and navy full-ruffled skirt that reaches down a little past her knobby knees. She was carrying all the other bags, about 10 Jansport backpacks. They couldn't afford any other luggage. Anyway, she dropped everything on the floor in her removal of the cameraman. Meanwhile, Brellie`! looks around the dirty, smelly, urine-soaked apartment. Stepping out into the middle of the apartment, she sees the five crackheads sleeping on the floor. There is no furniture, just the water boiler (which is ice-cold from non-use). Every room is boarded up and probably empty. From the back of her hot pants, Brellie`! pulls a black-handled .22 caliber Beretta. Checking to make sure the clip is full, she points it up at the ceiling above her and shoots off a single round. Cat: *death throes*'MRREOW!' The yellow tabby cat hanging from the light fixture above Brellie`! falls dead on the floor in front of her. Brellie`!:'I don't even want to know how that thing got up there.' The crackheads snap to attention. Brellie`!:'Attention, peons! Kindly get the fuck out! Thank you.' The crackheads mumble as they leave the apartment. Timesa comes back in with the cameraman. She looks around at the apartment. Timesa:'Well, it's better than most places I've slept...' A pause. Timesa:'And cleaner than some people I've slept with...' Brellie`!:'G-Money Pimp Master Funk Homey-Dawg Flex must have paid for our housing with pesos. Damn him.' Timesa:'That's an odd name for a Byrontologist.' Timesa throws her arm over her lover's shoulder. Timesa:'But this'll be great. Nice and rustic. Lots of time to be alone... Just like that one time in Brazil, right?' The cameraman picks the camera back up. Timesa moves over near the east wall and plops down to a seat on the floor. Timesa:'Eww.... it's all wet and smelly here!' Brellie`!:'What are you complaining about, the smell of urine, feces, or semen?' Timesa:'It's not that. I think someone used some Pine-Sol in here.' Brellie`!: 'Crap!' Brellie`! moves over to the opposite wall, where there is a door presumably to the master bedroom by the layout of the apartment. It's boarded up, but the boards are wet and warped and rotting. Not to mention the termites. With her boot, Brellie`! delivers a hard kick, breaking open the door. Immediately she jumps back. Brellie`!: 'OhMyGod, we've got bilge rats!' Timesa gets up and walks over to her lover. Brellie`!:'There's only bilge rats on ships, silly.' But Timesa sees inside the door and is just as shocked. The camera repositions and allows us the same view of the 2-feet-long family of 50 rats piled one on top of the other which is coming out of the wall, nibbling away at some rotting bodies on the floor. Timesa: 'Freaking hell! Close the door!' Brellie`! and Timesa pull the door shut as the rats take notice of them. Timesa looks over at the cameraman. Timesa:'You wouldn't happen to have any napalm, would you?' The camera shakes left-to-right. Brellie`!: 'Damn!' With a shrug, Brellie`! comes back out to the middle of the floor. Brellie`!:'Well, suffice it to say that room is off limits.' Timesa: 'Do we want to explore the other rooms?' Brellie`!: 'No... No we don't.' Timesa: 'Aw, please?' Releasing a sigh, Brellie`! takes out her Beretta, as well as a .45 Magnum (which she's confident enough to hold in one hand). Timesa goes into the duffel bag of money and retrieves two Desert Eagles. Brellie`!: 'Fine.' Brellie`! turns to the cameraman as Timesa takes up a position to the side of the door on the east wall that seems to be banging on the other side. There are strange chewing and growling noises and fetid blood pools under the door, spilling out into the common area. Brellie`!:'You might want to come back later after we've settled in.' The last thing we see is a giant blood-soaked four-digit claw five feet long smashing through the door. Timesa rolls away from the door and Brellie`! takes up position by her. A Hunter from the Resident Evil movie crashes through the door as they begin firing off rounds of ammunition. The cameraman is much too scared too stay and makes a run for the door. The Hunter jumps in front of him and swings a massive claw at the camera. It hits and the feed goes to black, though we still hear voices. Timesa:'Dammit!' Brellie`!: 'Shoot it, shoot it!' *4 Hours Later* And here we are, the camera opening up outside the same rundown crackhouse apartment in Newark, New Jersey about 4 hours after the last time the camera was on. It was dark last time, but only around 9 PM. This part of Newark is particularly decrepit, not a good-looking building in sight. Every apartment complex has been riddled with bullets, one building even looking to have been fire-bombed. All the windows are barred and boarded up on the inside. There are no trees and there is a perpetual cloud of dark smoke that hangs over the town. Brellie`! and Timesa, the two representatives of the Out House who work for the G-Money Pimp Master Funk Homey-Dawg Flex, are dragging a number of trash bags out to the curb. They're wearing the same clothes as last time, but they've got blood (don't worry, it's not theirs) all over their faces. Brellie`!: 'Damn, those rats were harder to kill than the Hunter.' Timesa: 'Too bad the Hunter was trying to eat us. If it could be domesticated I could think of at least one use for it.' That last line said with a mischievous smile as Timesa licks her lips. Brellie`! ignores her and drags out the 14th and last bag of bilge rats to the curb for pickup in the morning. Timesa takes out a pack of cloves and lights one up, taking in a long drag. Turning to the east to face the breeze that is coming in from the coast, she sees the cameraman recording them. Timesa: 'Oh, look who decided to join us!' This cameraman is a little more vocal than the other one. Cameraman: 'What happened to Phil?' Timesa shrugs. She casually swipes her thumb across her throat with her right hand, the clove held between the index and middle fingers. Brellie`! slaps herself in the head with her palm. Brellie`!: 'Crap. Ya know, I think I might've put him in one of the bags.' Brushing off the statement, Timesa takes in another drag of the clove. Timesa: *Exhaling smoke* 'Forget it. Besides, we've still gotta cut that promo against Finale.' Brellie`!: 'Oh, yeah. You go ahead and take care of this one, I'm gonna go take a bath.' Furrowing her brow, Timesa shakes her head at Brellie`!, who is wiping her face off with a discarded blue do-rag. Timesa: 'We don't have running water.' Brellie`! shrugs at her lover. Brellie`!: 'Yeah, but the East River isn't too far from here.' Timesa: 'Actually, a bath sounds good, I'll come too.' Brellie`!: 'What about the promo?' The cameraman chimes in. Cameraman: 'Don't worry, I'll come along and you can do your promo in the river.' Timesa: 'You would, wouldn't you, perv.' Cameraman: 'Look, I gotta get this in before morning, so do you want me to come with or you can just call me when you're ready?' With a shrug and a sigh, Timesa waves the cameraman along. Timesa: 'Screw it. Come on. I'm too tired to think of anything to say to Finale right now. A bath might refresh me.' *Later* Brellie`! and Timesa make a stop off at the only park to Newark. Well, it's not really a park, more of just a single 4x8 patch of grass in front of the Police station. The Out House stand in front of the station and just stare down at the grass. The cameraman gets the grass at first before panning up to Timesa and Brellie`! for a close-up bust shot. Timesa is smoking her third clove for the night. Brellie`! is drinking a bottle of hard Smirnoff in a paper bag. Confused, Timesa looks over at her lover. Timesa:'What are we doing again?' Brellie`!: 'We're cutting a promo against Finale.' There is silence for about ten seconds, Timesa trying to figure out what Brellie`! means. Timesa: 'So... how are we doing that?' Pointing to the grass, the red-haired Out House member swallows her drink. Brellie`!: 'You see that grass?' Timesa: 'For the past ten minutes, yeah.' Brellie`!: 'And isn't it infinitely more interesting than Finale?' Timesa: 'Oh, yeah.' Brellie`!: 'I mean, if G-Money Pimp Master Funk Homey-Dawg Flex showed grass growing instead of Finale promos, there might actually be positive Neilsen ratings for those segments.' Loosing a chuckle, Timesa exhales the smoke through her nostrils. The blood is still dripping off her clothes. Timesa: 'Hey, can we go take that bath now?' Brellie`!: 'Okay, sure. But this time tell me when you're gonna cum. I don't like that in my mouth...' *End*
