Easter...

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-1-02 {BEGIN TRANSMISSION} {The scene opens up with Kevin and Mandy sitting on the edge of their bed early Monday morning. Kevin in his dark blue Wrangler boxer shorts and Mandy in a pair of tight sleeping shorts and a short but tight sleeping shirt that exposes most of her stomach.} {Mandy gently leans over and kisses Kevin on the forehead, greeting him to another beautiful day. As Kevin pushes forward the royal blue bed spread he puts his feet on the warm white carpeting. Panning in on Kevin, he runs his hands through his hair and exhaling deeply. Out of the corner Mandy can be seen taking a white silk robe and walking into the bathroom ready to shower.} Mandy: Kevin, hun what’s wrong? You look so stressed. {Kevin turns and looks at Mandy then smiles.} Kevin: Nada importante. Well it’s sad to say but I’m glad the weekend is over. Mandy: Kevie, baby was it really that bad? Kevin: It was pretty bad and talk about crazy. If I weren’t the bomb I probably wouldn’t’ve been able to survive. {SCENE CHANGES TO EARLY SATURDAY MORNING} {FUZZY FLASHBACK SEQUENCE BEGIN} {Scene opens with Kevin and Mandy in the kitchen kissing and attempting to make breakfast. Then there’s a knocking on the front door. Kevin knowing he can’t cook goes to see whom it is.} Kevin: I’ll get that hun. Just keep up with the breakfast. Mandy: Ok {Kevin walks to the door and opens it. He looks around and he can’t see anyone. When he was about to close the door two little children speak.} Little child #1: Ummmm ummmmm sasasasasasa…. Little child #2: You’re… you’re… Little child #1: Kevin Klaymore Both children simultaneously: WOWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW Kevin: Sorry kids not autographs today… Mandy: (yelling through the house): Who is it Kevin? Kevin: Just a couple a kids. Mandy: Oh crap! {Mandy runs to the door in a hurry.} Mandy: Hey Jon {She gives little Jon, who is wearing a pair of cargo jeans and a Korn T-shirt, a hug.} Mandy: Hi Adam {She picks little Adam up and hugs him. Jon being about five or six looks at Kevin. While Adam being about four, is holding for dear life to Mandy and his little back pack.} Mandy: I’m sorry Kevin, I totally forgot. Kevin: What’s goin’ on are these your kids? Mandy: For heavens sake no. These are Jon’s kids, Little Jon, and Adam. They’re here to spend Easter with us. Kevin: They are? Mandy: Yeah my brother Jon is having a big party and he doesn’t want his kids there so he asked if we would watch them. Kevin: Alright I wish I woulda known about this sooner so I could’ve been prepared. {Little Jon just looks up at Kevin with his jaw to the floor.} Kevin: Ok primos let's get you fixed up. {Mandy closes the door and Kevin takes them up stairs to their room. As they set their back packs down and stare out the window.} Jon: Wow is that a real wrestling ring out there? Kevin: Yup I have it just to train and sometimes have shows in the summer for kids like you. Adam: Can we go out there? Can we can we can we? Jon: Yeah can we? It’ll be so much fun. Kevin: Well why don’t we go into the ring bounce first? Jon: What’s that? Kevin: Have you ever been to ummm… Discovery Zone or somethin like that? Adam: Yeah it was so much fun. Kevin: Well it’s like a big trampoline kinda like they have there. {With that being said Adam and Jon rush down the stairs to get to the ring bounce. Kevin walking down slowly puts his arm around Mandy as they go there to “play around” a little if possible.} {As they exit the house Adam and Jon are already inside wrestling around a little. Kevin and Mandy look at this and smile.} Jon: Aunt Amanda please play with us. Look at me! {He does a little front flip but lands on his face.} Jon: Please come in here and jump with us. It’ll be fun. Kevin: C’mon Mandy let’s go in there and “play a little”. Mandy: Kevin… {Mandy gives Kevin a quick punch to his arm as she drags him in there.} Mandy: If I have to do this then so do you {The four of them jump up and down for a while. Jon trying to do front flips and Adam jumping up and landing on his but doing what he calls the “BANZAI”. Kevin just stops for a moment and watches Mandy jump up and down.} Kevin: Mandy we should do this more often… {Mandy stops and punches Kevin in the arm again.} Mandy: (whispering) don’t talk like that with them here. {The scene forwards to later that night when Jon and Adam are in bed.} Adam: Uncle Kevin wiwl you tell us a stowy? Kevin: Yeah why not? Alright… {Kevin walks over to their beds and sits down. Trying to think of a story, one just pops up.} Kevin: About a year ago, I don’t remember when. There was a nice man, but he lived off his violence. He was a wrestler you see, and he fought to survive. You might know him as Rey Mago, but his real name is Tomás Lobo. One Christmas he was given a chance of a lifetime. He had a chance to fight for the world title with Vampiro the champion. Vampiro said that he was going to fight for the devil and everything evil. Rey on the other hand said he was going to win for God, the children, his family, for everyone. They had their match and after about thirty minutes Vampiro had the upper hand. Rey yelled “Dios ayudame!” Which in English means “God help me”. Some how Rey got the upper hand and pinned Vampiro. He was given the best Christmas gift of all time. He was given his childhood dream. Mandy: That was a sweet story. It kinda reminds me of your story a while ago but with a different ending. {Kevin gets up and cautiously walks to the door and closes it behind him.} Kevin: Mandy what are we going to do about the Easter bunny and all that? I mean we don’t have anything here, except for some candy that Juliet brought over here. Mandy: I’ll go to Wal-Mart and pick them up something. Oh I need about oh $400. {Kevin reaches inside his wallet and hands her the money.} Kevin: Alright I think I have some plastic eggs around here somewhere, so I’ll fill them up with candy and put some candy out. {Mandy grabs a coat and leaves.} {Scene forwards to Kevin in black clothing with a black bag hiding plastic eggs outside. As he slowly creeps around in the front yard Mandy drives past the house and sees someone lurking around.} {Scene changes to the truck} Mandy: Oh my GOD a burglar. {She grabs her cell phone and calls the cops. With in seconds the cop slowly drives up and sees the man lurking around. They shine the spotlight on him and turn their sirens on.} Police: FREEZE {Suddenly Kevin drops the bag and a light comes on inside the house. Jon and Adam look out the window to see what’s going on.} Jon: What’s going on Uncle Kevin? Kevin: We think we found the Easter Bunny just go back to sleep ok. {They turn the lights off as fast as they can and go back to bed pretending to sleep so they can get their Easter Basket. Kevin walks towards the cops as Mandy drives up.} Kevin: What’s goin’ on? I was just puttin’ stuff up for my girlfriend’s nephews for Easter. Mandy: Kevin that was you? Kevin: yeah I was hidin the eggs for them. Cop #1: So this was just a mistake? Mandy: Yeah I thought he was a robber. Cop #2: Let’s get outa here! They have a special at Dunkin’s Donuts right now so let’s hit them quick. Good Night ma’am and have a happy Easter. Mandy: Thank you and do the same. {The cops leave as Mandy brings in a couple of Easter baskets and stuff inside bags.} {Scene changes to the inside of the house.} Mandy: There everything is out except for one thing. Go ahead and go to bed and I’ll be there in a sec. Kevin: You sure? Mandy: Yeah it’ll be ok. {Kevin goes to his room and gets in bed. Soon Mandy walks in after him and gets into bed with him. As they start to kiss and play around a little the sound of two little children giggling and walking down the hall is heard.} Adam: C’mon Jon if the Easter Bunny catches us up he will take everything back. Jon: It’s just a little peak. Then I’ll go back to bed. {They can hear the sound of them getting back to bed and closing the door. So Kevin and Mandy start back up.} {45 MINUTES PASS} {Then a knocking on the door is heard.} Adam and Jon: Aunt Amanda and Uncle Kevin can we go see what we got for Easter now? {Kevin looks over to the clock and it reads 5:07 a.m.} Kevin: It’s Five in the morning shouldn’t you two be sleepin or somethin? Jon: No we want to see what we got for Easter. Kevin: Get back to bed and come back in an hour or so. We want to make sure the Easter Bunny doesn’t see us up right? {Then the children quickly run back to bed.} {50 MINTUES PASS} Adam: Is it a hour yet? Jon: Yeah is it? We want to see what we got for Easter {Mandy looks at Kevin and sighs because they never had enough time to have fun.} Mandy: Yeah it’s close enough let’s go see what you guys got. {The kids run to the living room as Kevin and Mandy gets up. Kevin puts on a pair of jeans and Mandy puts a robe on. They walk to the room where the kids are already going through their stuff.} Jon: WOW I GOT A X-BOX Adam: I GOT A DVD PLAYER Mandy: Kevin you have one too. {Mandy grabs Kevin’s basket and hands it to him. Kevin looks inside and sees a few things that are very helpful for their honeymoon and private time.} Kevin: Thank you babe and there’s one over here for you too. {Mandy goes through it and sees a long velvet box. She opens it to see a diamond necklace.} Mandy: Awwwww Kevin this is so sweet of you. {She leans over to kiss him. Kevin grabs a small velvet box out of his pocket. He opens it and asks the very important question.} Kevin: Mandy will you marry me? {Mandy begins to cry and kisses Kevin.} Kevin: I take that as a yes. {They kiss more and the kids see them.} Jon and Adam: EWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW {Scene forwards to them looking for eggs. Jon has three more than Adam, Kevin has three more than Mandy. Mandy gave her eggs to Jon and Kevin to Adam. As everything is equal there is still one egg missing.} Kevin: There’s one more egg missing. {They all look for it and Adam finds it but a big mean dog is guarding it. Kevin walks over almost to get his leg bit.} Kevin: You stupid dog. {He gets into a pouncing position and grabs the egg quickly. As he walks away the dog begins to chew on his pant leg and Kevin falls down. Eventually Kevin unbuttons his pants and wriggles free. When he looks around Jon, Adam are laughing hard while Mandy has her hand over her mouth lightly chuckling.} Mandy: Let’s get inside and get some breakfast. What do you two want? Jon and Adam: CANDY~ Mandy: How about bacon, eggs, sausage, toast and juice for now and you can have candy later. Kevin: Awwww C’mon Mandy let’em have some candy now Mandy: Alright they can have one piece of candy now then we eat breakfast. {Everyone walks into the kitchen to get some breakfast. As they sit down to eat Kevin tries to feed Mandy some eggs with his fork. Instead of getting it in her mouth it falls down her robe and onto the “oranges”.} Mandy: You jerk, you dropped it on my oranges. {She gives him a quick jab to the stomach and then one to the arm. Then she grabs some eggs and drops it on his head and starts a food fight. Adam throws sausage at Jon who in turn spills juice all over Mandy’s robe. Kevin tries to walk away but his dog jumps on top of him all muddy. Adam then throws bacon to the dog. It lands on Kevin’s crotch and the dog begins to eat the bacon. Mandy looks over covers her mouth and laughs, but looks concerned too.} Mandy: Kevin baby are you ok? Kevin: Stupid dog I swear… get him off hun. {Mandy runs to the dog and drags it to the door and lets him out.} {Kevin gets up and holds his arms up to see himself all muddy with dog drool on him.} Kevin: Dibs on the shower first! Mandy: Go take a shower Kevin while I clean up down here. Adam, Jon go and get your candy. I’ll put it in separate things so you won’t fight over it. {Kevin goes up and showers. As he gets out and gets a towel wrapped around him Adam and Jon run in getting cleaned up. Kevin just ignores it for a while and begins to shave.} {Jon and Adam look at him and begin to mimic him. Mandy walks in to see the three of them shaving. She grabs her camera real quick and takes a picture.} {SCENE FORWARDS TO PRESENT} Mandy: Well yeah it was a rough couple of days but you had fun didn’t you? Kevin: Yeah but watchin’ lil kids is a chore. Mandy: Well one day we’ll have some and we’ll be prepared. {Mandy walks over and gives him a kiss. Scene pans in on Kevin sitting on his bed stretching. The Bomb just got a crash course in parenthood. And it really was a crash course. Let’s see how the Bomb Kevin Klaymore can manage in singles competition. Will he be ready or will he crash and burn like before?} {END TRANSMISSION} {OOC- this event truly happened to me, but was slightly changed to take a Kevin Klaymore format.}
