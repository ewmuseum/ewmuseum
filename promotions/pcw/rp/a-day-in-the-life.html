A Day in the Life....

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-9-02 ~ The scene is in the backseat of a Dark Green Impala. It is just starting to get dark outside. We are in Jersey City, New Jersey. It is a breezy, chilly night. The driver of this Impala is Hoefel's best friend Rob, and in the passenger seat is Hardcore Hoefel. Rob's wearing a white bandana, with a black pair of jeans, and a wife beater. His gold is shining brightly. Hardcore Hoefel is wearing a black Yankees hat tilted to the left, an Avirex sweatshirt, Avirex jeans, and a black and white bandana wrapped around his head. 'Till I Collapse' by Eminem is blasting loudly in the car, and Rob has a blunt in his hand. He pulls a turn onto Kennedy Boulevard. There are a few people on the corner, most likely making a drug deal. As Rob exhales from the blunt, he pulls into the projects he grew up in, known as Curry's Woods. There are young black males all over the perimeter of the projects, and lots of graffiti on the project's walls. Rob pulls into the back of the parking lot and hands Hoefel the blunt. As Rob turns the radio off and raises the windows Hoefel gets a few last hits in, and puts the blunt roach in the ash tray, where about 10 other roaches are visible. Rob and Hoefel step out of the car at the same time,and immediately a group of young men walk up to them. Right away you can see who one of the men is, it's Nick, longtime friend of Hoefel and Rob's. ~ Nick - Whats up niggas? You'se ready to get to some serious ballin'? Hoefel - Hell yeah. ~ Hoefel and Rob obivously had a basketball game planned with these kids, as one of them tosses Rob a ball. He dribbles the ball as he walks over to the basketball court. Wind blows some trash across the empty court. The rims seem crooked,and they have chain nets. ~ Rob - Let's shoot up teams. Nick - Aight let's go. ~ As Rob begins to take a shot, Hardcore Hoefel's cell phone rings. Hoefel pulls out his Nokia flip-phone, and answers it. ~ Hoefel - Hello?.....Who's dis?....Oh, whut up dawg? I ain't heard from you in the longest....Yeah, sure.....how about Barrets Bar?.....Yeah I am right across the street from there now. Aight yo', see ya' in a few. ~ Hardcore Hoefel hangs up the phone. ~ Rob - Whats going on? Hoefel - My boy Joe from back in the day just called me on the celly, he wants to chill. I told him I'd meet him over at Barret's. Rob - Oh, that's tight. Hoefel - Yeah I gotta meet him at Barrets right now...could we do this game tomorrow? Nick - Aight Jay, we'll be here tomorrow. Rob - Hey Jay, mind if I hang around here for a bit while you go meet up wit Joe? I got some shit I wanna catch up on, talk to a few friends, go look at my old apartment, nahmean? Hoefel - Yeah that's cool, I'll be back around here in an hour or so. Rob - Aight Peace out. ~ Rob gives Hardcore Hoefel palm,and Hoefel walks across the street to Barrets Bar. He walks in the front door of the bar. It's fairly empty, as it is only 5:00pm. The bar doesn't usually get packed until around 8:00pm. Once Hoefel enters the bar, he walks straight over to the bar and sits down on a stool. The bartender appears to recognize him, and quickly walks over. ~ Bartender - Hey Jay, how's it goin'? Hoefel - Same ol', same ol'...whut about you Jack? Jack (Bartender) - Same here. What can I get for ya'? Hoefel - Um....I dunno, I guess just get me a Bud for now. Jack - Comin' right up. ~ Jack reaches underneath the counter and pulls out an ice cold glass and fills it with Budweiser from the tap. He then slides it across the bar to him. Hoefel drinks a little bit, and then the two continue their conversation. ~ Jack - So how are things goin' over in PCW? I heard they've placed you in a match against BladeD for your opener. What's up with that? Hoefel - Well for some odd reason they seem to think that me embarassing BladeD would be a good way to start my career in Primetime Championship Wrestling. Jack - You aligning with anyone or plan on making any alliances? Hoefel - Not really, fuck 'em all. I don't need anyone on my side....never did. I know I've pissed off a few people already tho'. Jack - So what's your strategy going into Turmoil? You gonna pull out anything special? Hoefel - Anything special?!? Probly not....why should I?!? BladeD ain't nobody special, he's just another second-rate bitch whose got less wrestling talent than Barry Horowitz. So why should I use anything special on anyone whose not special or hasn't pissed me off to the point where I would want to severely hurt them?!? ~ From across the bar, some guys begin laughing. You can now hear what one of the guys is saying. ~ The Guy - There goes Hoefel....running his mouth again. He ain't got no skills, but he gots to run his mouth anyway. ~ The men all begin laughing again. There are four of them. Hoefel looks up at Jack, and just smirks. He then hops off of the bar stool and walks over towards the table the men are sitting at. Once he's at the table, the guys all grow quiet, and look up at him. ~ One of the Guys - What the hell are you looking at? Hoefel - Well....I was sittin' over there at the bar, drinkin' my beer and talkin' to Jack, when I hear you over here runnin' your mouth like your some kind of a tough guy. So I figured I'd come over here and find out what exactly your problem was. The Guy - My problem is that you and your big mouth are giving me a damn migrane....that's my problem. ~ The guy's friends all begin laughing at that smart ass comment. The guy has obviously drank way too mcuh tonight, and is trying to show off in front of his friends. Hoefel appears to think about what the man has said for a second, and then responds. ~ Hoefel - Well maybe...just maybe....you should prove to your faggot friends over here that you've actually got a set of balls on that scrawny ass body of your's, be a man, and do somethin' to solve ya' problem. ~ The man looks around at all of his friends, who are staring at him and waiting to see if he'll take Hoefel up on his offer. The man doesn't look like he wants a fight, but then again doesn't want to get punked out in front of his friends. So he reluctantly gets up, and looks Hardcore in the face. From the back of the room you can see Rob and Nick walk into the room. They see that the guys are getting set to team up on Hoefel, so they quickly begin to walk over. Before Rob or Nick can get over there, however, Hoefel swings on the guy. The guy falls straight back and lands on his dumb drunken ass. His friends go to try and jump Hoefel, but Rob and Nick are over to make the save. It's now erupted into an all out brawl. Rob, Hoefel, and Nick are beating the crap out of those four guys with just about everything that's not screwed down. They're hitting these guys with beer bottles and bar stools. Hoefel tossed one of the guys through a table. They're just really laying it into these guys. Blood is literally everywhere, but none of it came from Hoefel, Rob, or Nick. The only blood on any of them is the blood of the other guys. It appears as if they are finally finished the thrashing, and are ready to leave. ~ Hoefel - Wait one sec....we can't leave yet. I've got to get something. ~ Hoefel walks back over to the bar. Jack, who you would think would be pissed at them for wrecking the entire bar, is just laughing. ~ Jack - Let me guess.....you need some liquor for the road..... Hoefel - Damn....heh....you know me too well. Let me get three large bottles of Alize, and three large bottles of Hennessey. Jack - Ah....gonna be drinkin' some Thug Passion huh? Hoefel - You damn right. Jack - Okay, I'm gonna have to go into the back to get you your stuff though. I'll be back out in a few minutes. Hoefel - Aight, no problem. ~ Jack turns around, walks across the bar, and disappears into the back. Hoefel, Rob, and Nick start up a conversation while they wait for their liquor. ~ Nick - Do we have good timin' or whut Jay? Hoefel - You sure do.....a couple minutes later and you would've missed all the fun. Rob - A couple minutes later and you would've been gettin' you ass kicked all over this bar by those four drunks. Hoefel - Hell nah, they ain't got shit on me dogg. So....what you'se wanna do tonight? It's only bout 6 o'clock now. Nick - I don't know, I guess drink....smoke....the usual. Hoefel - Yeah but it's mad cold out, I want to find someplace nice and warm to get fucked up. Rob - How bout this, after Jack brings us out our liquor, we head back to my house. We drink, smoke, and chill. And then whatever we decide to do, we do. But at least at my house we'll have someplace to chill if nothing comes up. Hoefel - True dat', maybe we'll go out to a club or somethin later on. Nick - Yeah, I guess we'll just figure it out once we get to your house. Jack - Hey guys..... ~ They turn around, and Jack is there. He's got a huge brown bag sitting on the bar. He evidently found all of the alcohol Hoefel wanted. ~ Hoefel - How much does it come out to? Jack - Ah.....gimme sixty....that should be fine. ~ Hoefel hands Jack a $100 bill. ~ Hoefel - Keep the change.....judging by the looks of this place you could use it. ~ They all laugh about it. ~ Jack - Get outta here ya' smartass. Hoefel - Aight, later Jack. ~ Hoefel, Rob, and Nick exit the bar and head out into the parking lot. They walk over to Rob's car. Rob reaches into his pocket and pulls out his keys. He opens the driver's side door, and then gets in. He then unlocks the other doors so that Hoefel and Nick can get in. Hoefel gets in the front passenger seat, and Nick gets into the back seat of the Impala. Rob sticks the key into the ignition and the engine starts. He waits a few seconds for the engine to warm up, and then pulls out of the parking lot and onto the street. Rob - So whut do you guys wanna listen to? Hoefel - Put on that Mobb Deep shit, bump that song 'Burn'....that shit is hard. Rob - Aight dogg. Yo Nick, my cd's are back there by you. Find the Mobb Deep cd and pass that shit up. ~ There is no answer from Nick. ~ Rob - Hey yo Nick! Nick - One second dogg I'm busy. ~ Hoefel and Rob turn around to see what the hell Nick could possibly be doing. When they see what he's doing, they can only laugh. They should've known....Nick is rolling a phat ass blunt on the case to the Mobb Deep cd. ~ Nick - Right after I roll this shit I'll pass the cd up. Rob - Aight no problem. ~ Nick carefully finishes rolling the blunt, making sure he rolls it well so that it'll burn evenly. After he finishes rolling it, he puts the unlit blunt in his mouth and opens the cd case. He takes out teh cd and passes it up to Hoefel, who pops it into the cd player. Nick then closes the cd case and puts it back underneath the seat where it belongs. ~ Hoefel - Light that shit bitch. Nick - Aight I am, chill for a sec. ~ Nick lights the blunt carefully. Him, Hoefel, and Rob then all puff away with the Mobb Deep's 'Burn' bumpin' through the car speakers as the scene fades to black. ~ ~ When the scene fades back in, the three are already back at Rob's house. Their eyes are all extremely bloodshot, and they've all got cups of Thug Passion in their hands. Hoefel is laid out on the floor, Rob is chillin' on the couch, and Nick is sitting with his back resting against the couch. The light in the room is out, and they are all watching t.v. The movie that's on right now is 'Half-Baked'.....fitting movie for the situation ain't it?!? The movie is just about over, Samson just got busted, and the cops have finally come in to arresst him. Hoefel, Nick, and Rob are all laughing at the movie, even though nothing really funny was said or done. The movie's over now, and they need to find something else to do. ~ Nick - So whut we gonna do now? Rob - I don't know. I don't feel like going out yet though. Let's just see what else is on right now.... ~ Rob begins flipping through the channels with his remote control. He seems to be getting pissed at the remote. ~ Rob - God damn remote.....batteries are almost dead, so the shit only works when it feels like it. I gotta press the damn button ten times sometimes just to get it to work. ~ Rob continues flipping through the channels. ~ Rob - Huh?!? Nick - AH! Turn that shit off! Hoefel - Change it! ~ The camera zooms into the t.v. screen and you can see why they are all so disgusted. IT appears to be some sort of gay porno. Wait a minute.....those two guys in the video look pretty familiar.....it's Playaz Inc. and BladeD! Or at least it looks like it's them! That's sick! ~ Nick - Turn it off! Rob - I'M TRYING! THE DAMN REMOTE AIN'T WORKIN'! ~ Rob is frantically pressing the remote buttons to try to change the channel. He's desperate to get it off, and is just slamming every single button on teh remote now. You can hear from the t.v. BladeD complaining to both members of Playaz Inc., asking why he always has to be the one to get it up his ass. And they're explaining to BladeD that it's just because he's so small that it's impossible to do it the other way around. Hoefel's had enough of this, he dives across the room and yanks the plug for the t.v. straight out of the socket. The t.v. screen goes black. They all breath a sigh of relief, and sit there for a minute....stunned at the disgusting shit they just came across. They all look at each other, and then bust out histerically laughing. ~ Rob - Can you believe that?!? Hoefel - And to think....I'm supposed to feel threatened by the high quality of athletes here in PCW?!? The only kind of hardcore they know about is the shit we just saw.....and I ain't down to compete in that way! Rob - That's fuckin' sick! Nick - Yo, let's go watch t.v. in the otha' room, I know the shit works in there. Besides, the Wrestling Insider's comin' on in like 2 minutes. ~ They all get up and go into the other room to watch tonight's Wrestling Insider show. They put on a pretty good show, and have several interviews guys like Xavier Kannon, Duke Williams, BladeD and many other members of the PCW talent roster. Hoefel appears to be pretty heated by some of the comments that were made, but hasn't said anythin' yet. Then, he breaks his silence. ~ Hoefel - Fuck this shit, c'mon...we goin' for a ride. Rob - Where to? Hoefel - To go smoke another blunt of course. Besides, pretty soon I've got to step into the ring with that fudgepacker BladeD....I need to get my thoughts together. ~ The scene fades out with them all heading out to the car. ~
