::~Eternals Eleven~:: (Part I){ Chicks, Flicks and Pickledicks}

26 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/26/03 Prelude: One down, more to go.. that is Shane’s current PCW status but yet there’s a long road ahead. A life of music and wrestling, time is at the essence and success is a one-way road upwards. No time to sit down and consider a loss, or failure or a mistake, perfection is the keyword. With a brand new album upcoming next month, the Wethers Empire is getting ready for the ‘Eternal-Fest’ rock festival to take place in early June. But first of all Shane needs to create his eleven man security team to watch his back whilst he puts on a show that even else would be proud of. Shane knows full well that due to him being a newbie in the PCW world, he is a liable target for competitors in the rising star division. So now we continue in the conquest of the Eternal Punk, as he begins his search in a little epic we like to call ‘Eternals Eleven!’ Monday mornings, there’s something about that very phrase that makes a weekend seem invaluable. You spend Friday night in a gratified manner whilst drinking so much your brain cells begin to tap dance in your skull. Saturdays are pretty much the same, unless you’ve got a killer hangover and you feel like a character out of Michael Jackson’s ‘Thriller’. Oh, then there’s the ever so precious Sundays where you spend you afternoons in bed, hiding under the covers, hoping the weekend will suddenly be extended whilst jacking off to the page three girls in the local paper. Whichever way you live your weekend, that despondent scent of Monday morning always lurks around the corner. That’s unless you are the lead singer of an infamous punk rock band with hits that make your skin break dance, this is the world of an Eternal Punk. In all his expense, Shane lays back on his comforting king size bed in his mansion based in Central London. The whole room of Shane’s is decked out like a dream room, with a large marble fireplace, a mini-bar in the far left corner, a wall full of golden records and a large four post bed in the middle with purple bedcovers. Giggling and talking comes from under the bed-covers as the faint sound of a car pulling into the pebble driveway of his house can be heard. The sound stops for a moment as the giggling continues. “Knock-knock” All of a sudden Shane’s face pops out of the covers with a surprised expression, his hair is all ruffled like a member of Kiss without the make up. He turns to look at his bedside clock [b]9.15pm [/b] “What the…” Another face pops out of the covers next to him, the face of a petite blonde girl with a smile worth a million-dollars. She sits up, leaning across to Shane who has his back to her, looking at the clock. He slides out of bed as she puts her arm across his chest to try restrain him. “baby..come back to bed” she pleads in her sweet-talking tone. Shane slides on his blue t-shirt with the words ‘Eternal Madness’ in jazzy aluminous green letters. “Let me answer the door and I’ll be right back” Shane replies with a heavy sigh at the end. He picks up his dark blue baggy jeans with a silver chain hanging from the pocket, he pulls them up whilst walking into the landing of his house. He begins to lose balance, swaying forwards a little and then backwards before his legs give way causing him to fall down the steps on his front. “aaaaaaarghhh!” he yells whilst putting his hands in front of him to stop his head hitting the floor.[b]Tumble-tumble-bang! [/b] Shane finally reaches the bottom of the stairs with a dazed expression on his face. He gets slowly to his feet and pulls up his pants, scratching his balls and shaking his head as he makes his way to the door. [b]Knock-knock-knock knock-knock[/b] “Wait a damn minute for crying out loud. If it’s the Avon Lady again I’m gonna shove those cosmetics up your bitch ass” he mumbles as he walks towards the door, gripping the small iron handle, turning it clockwise and opening the door. At the door stands Mikey Mallet Fishmore. [b] Mikey Fishmore[/b] gets the nickname ‘the mallet’ from and old business he used to run with his wife, selling many different kinds of wooden mallets to suit your needs. Needless to say, the business wasn’t exactly the Microsoft of the business world, so things went quickly from bad to worse. He caught his wife in bed with one of the workers, he weren’t best pleased to say the very least, but Mikey being Mikey; he picked up a steel mallet and beat the kid to death with it. It wasn’t a pretty sight neither, with a swift crack to the skull his cranium shattered like a world record. His wife threatened to take him to caught and sue him for everything he had. So he beat her to death too, not exactly a smart thing to do on behalf of his wife. Anyway, Mikey contacted a few friends and did a few dodgy dealings in the car industry, before long he was earning half a million a week. The guy knows absolutely everything there is to know about the crime in London. If there’s a guy you need to do sort out your local taxman or even the guy who’s been screwing your wife behind your back, then Mr Fishmore can sort it for a good price. Mikey stood a medium sized guy with large sunglasses on with a brown lens. The guy is pretty old and is taking drags from a large Cuban cigar; he wears a large musty beige overcoat. He kind of reminds you of Jimmy Saville with an attitude, apart from the ridiculous white hair and distasteful tracksuit. “Where’ve you been Guv?” says Mikey in a concerned voice, “I’ve been pranking your mobi all morning and I got no answer”. Mikeys eyes search Shane in a disturbed manner. “…and why your pants un-done? Tell me you haven’t been whacking off” Shane looks at Mikey with equally disgraced expression “of course not you Muppet, it’s 9 o’clock, what the hell you doing ‘ere?” Shane asks as he begins to walk away from the door rubbing his weary eyes. “Are you insane? It’s Monday morning… time to sort out your security for the Freeze-fest. Otherwise we’ve got a bunch of monkeys at Chester Zoo who will be happy to watch your backs whilst raiding the food cart.” Mikey protests, whilst inviting himself into Shane’s home. He brushes passed Shane and makes his way into the narrow hallway, slowly approaching the steep staircase. The marble floor leaves a creaking sound under the soles of his shoes as he continues to make his way up the staircase as Shane follows in a concerned manner. “Man, what you doing?” he detests in an impatient way. Mikey doesn’t reply, he just continues up the stairs with a determined look upon his long face, he walks straight into Shane’s bedroom. “Mikey… Stop!” Shane yells, but realises that Mike is seriously onto him. “What the hell?”…a yelling comes from Mikey inside the bedroom. Shane walks up the remaining set of stairs and proceeds into his bedroom where he sees Mikey stood looking wide eyed at his girl in bed, stark naked with the velvet covers around her. “Who are you?” the girl asks with fear in her eye, Mikey sees her jeans and blouse on the floor, he picks them up and throws them at her. “I’m your time-keeper, your times up, now fuck off” Mikey declares in an angry and even more impatient tone whilst Shane stands rubbing his eyes trying to make sense of it all. Without hesitation, Shane walks into the bathroom with a smirk on his face as the row continues. Shane puts the small black plug into the plug hole of the wide white sink and fills it with hot water, whilst looking into the mirror at the lines under his bright blue eyes. Who’d have thought that such demonic eyes could look so innocent? He yawns and stretches, whilst sniffing his armpits to check if he still wreaks of sweat from the night after. Shane screws up his face in disgust of the stench and lets go a gut-wrenching belch. He wafts his hand in front of his nose and scowls. “Dang…beer breathe, shame I haven’t got a hang over to go with it” Shane mutters to himself with zest. Shane leans forward and scoops water onto his face once and then twice before looking up in the mirror at his face in the hope that the lines would somehow miraculously disappear. Suddenly the bathroom door opens and as Shane turns around to see Mikey stood there trying to figure out just what the hell Shane is up to. “Just typical, a day with work to do and your knobbing’ some helpless bird.” Mikey complains whilst running his hand across the paintwork on the doorframe. “Aren’t I allowed a little fun now? After all we had a successful gig in Leeds last night. I returned home and put on the show of my life. Doesn’t the saying go like ‘work hard play hard’ ?” Shane quizzes. Mikey chuckles to himself and passes Shane a face towel. “too much play and not enough work me’ lad” he mutters under his breathe. Shane rubs his face down with the towel before handing it back to Mikey. “So what have you done with her?” Shane asks with a smug grin on his face. “Through her out with the rest of the trash” Mikey replies with not an ounce of hesitation in his voice. Shane brushes passed him, walking over to the landing and straight into his bedroom. “You’re two heavy handed with the girls… I was going to get rid of her when the time is right.” Shane says as he slides on a pair of clean Pringles socks. “Anyway, what have you got for me?” he asks, walking back into the hallway. “Ritchie ‘the screamer’ Burrows, he’s excellent with a gun…” Mikey informs Shane, “he used to go pigeon shooting with his father. Rumour has it that his father was very hard on him, anyway one day he snapped and fired the gun at his fathers Mustang, the shot rebound and hit his father square in the forehead. He fires a screamer, hence the nickname.” Shane sighs heavily and looks at Mikey as if he’s insane. “So you got me a hitman who’s as lucky as a lotto winner?” Shane asks in a disappointed tone. “No… that was when he was young, he’s been practicing sharp shooting for fifteen years now” Mikey reassures him. “The geezer can smell trouble a mile off,” “And how much are we looking at?” Shane asks with concern as he pours Mikey and himself a drink of the finest Scotch whisky from the mini-bar. He hands Mikey a glass and perches on the bar corner. “About 3-bob” Mike replies with a slight worry in his voice. He cringes like a school child after a teacher has run her nails down the black board. “Three-hundred quid? Damn, that is cheap… maybe a little TOO cheap” Shane says with a feeling of suspicion. Mikey laughs and walks over to Shane, putting his hand on his shoulder. “Don’t you be worrying kiddo, everything will be explained. Just meet me at the outbound central line in the underground tomorrow at five.” Mikey informs as he lifts his drink in front of Shane with a wink in his eye. “This better be good, otherwise you won’t be the mallet no more, you’ll be the hammered” Shane warns Mikey as the both laugh, clanging their glasses together.
