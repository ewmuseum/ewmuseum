Corprate Bastard

4 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Corprate Bastard Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 10/04/02 The “Malice Man” Duke Williams and his team of buddies are shopping the TV Giant store. Duke has a John Deere ball cap on and a “I’m a corporate bastard” t-shirt proudly tucked into his black wranglers. A sales associate comes over to help Duke find his new boob tube.- Jim: Hello sir how can I help you? Williams: I need a TV. Jim: Ok what price range are you looking at? -Duke laughs with his fat buddy Dave as he points to his shirt.- Williams: I’m a corporate bastard I’ll just write this off as a business expense.- -One of Duke’s friends is Steven “The Corporate Spokesman: from the Dell Commercials.- Steven: Dude your getting a TV!! Williams: On the company. Jim: Well if that’s the case lets take you to the private TV room. We don’t like simple people seeing these TV’s they just smudge on them. -The four men laugh as they walk towards the bathroom. The sign reads “out of order” and the door is locked.- Jim: Welcome to paradise. -Jim looks behind his shoulder to make sure nobody is watching as he unlocks the door and which reveals a giant room full of REAL TV’s.- Dave: Now were talking. Jim: Over here we have the flat TV’s. -Duke looks at the flat screen TV’s. – Williams: What the hell is the deal with that it looks like one of them there damn art paintings. Dave: To hell with that hoss. Steven: Dude it’s flat as Brittany Spears before the boob job. -Duke just stares at the flat screen.- Williams: I already got a picture of them dogs playing poker on the wall. I can stare and laugh at that for hours. HA HA HA HA Jim: So you want a big screen TV? Dave: Them dogs rule!! Williams: I want big. Jim: Over here we have a 52” inch TV. Steven: Dude that is pretty big. Williams: Its almost the size of Miyagawa’s old valets ass. Dave: It ain’t big enough. Williams: I AM A corporate bastard I deserve the biggest. Steven: Dude the biggest!!! -The three men just stare at Steven with annoyance.- Jim: Over here we have a 64 inch TV for the hard working man. Williams: Humm that seems big but I think I deserve better being a former cartoon star and all. Dave: That ain’t dang old big enough man. Steven: It could be bigger….dude. Jim: WE do have projection TV’s. Dave: Like a big ass movie theater? Williams: Now you’re talking hoss. The next champion of the world deserves the biggest. Plus I need something to watch King of Queens on. Dave: You thank Ann Nicole Smith is a fat ass now!! Steven: Dude how about a giant ozzy!! -The four men think for a moment.- Williams: I got it I’ll build a porch out side my back door and I’ll make the wall glass. I’ll have a giant move screen in my back yard to watch TV it will be like a drive in!! Jim: I love rednecks with money!! Dave: Damn right. Steven: Dude that rocks. Jim: OK I’ll write up the order. Duke: Just bill it to PCW. -The sales man walks away with a smile on his face.- Williams: Am I corporate bastard? Your damn right. I’m I the next champion? Yup. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
