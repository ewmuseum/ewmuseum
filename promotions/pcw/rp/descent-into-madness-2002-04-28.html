Descent into Madness

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/28/02 Through the skyways a vessel sails..............wonder of modern technology, bringing humans through the air and crossing distances once thought impossible to traverse in the veritable blink of an eye. 'Thank you for flying Valuejet, and we hope to see you in the future.' The tinny voice of the pilot fades over the intercom...........through the tiny opening in the wall of the Cincinnati airport men and women bustle. A crowd of humanity, eager to escape their confines and go about their way..........and a pair of forms quite divorced from humanity. Clyde: Bah! Through this mass of bodies the two are bumped and shoved...........eventually ending up on the floor in front of the tunnel to the airplane. Bethany: Get........OFF me! The little girl known only as Bethany bites into her lover who had the misfortune of falling atop her............with a yell of equal parts pain and irritation he rolls off and stumbles to his feet. Clyde: Valuejet. That's how far we've fallen in the company kiddo..........they book us on the most cheaply constructed plane known to history. If I didn't know better I'd say they wanted rid of us............. Bethany: That or it's all they can afford.........SNOW is the world champion, that can't be good for buyrates. Clyde: Bah. Dusting himself off, the demon begins making his way through the men and women...........garnering odd stares from every direction. Clyde: What, what in the hell are they looking at? Bethany: Your eyes are flaming dear. Clyde looks on, squinting...........hunches over the counter top of a nearby airport store and notices indeed his pupils are blazing fire. Clyde: Oh, crap. The burned man rubs his eyes on his sleeve...........finally blinking and looking to his young lover. Clyde: Better? Bethany: (Ignoring him) Much. Let's go already. The little girl stomps angrily off..............Clyde begins to follow, then stops and looks down as he notices his arm burning. Clyde: (Shaking his arm) Bah! Bethany: Will you stop messing around? This place smells like Ohio. The killer finally grabs a cup of Coca Cola from the hand of a nearby patron, dumping it over his engulfed arm. With a sizzle his limb extinguishes.........throwing the glass over his shoulder blindly and garnering a scream as it shatters on some unknown's skull he trots after Bethany. Clyde: Where's the damned luggage pickup? Bethany: I don't know, what gate did we come out of? Clyde: I don't know, it should be on our ticket. Bethany: Ticket? Clyde stops short.........looks down upon the evil little girl. Clyde: Yes, a ticket.........you know, the little piece of paper you give to the guys at the gate so they don't boot you out on your ass? You were holding them for us.........our return tickets as well. Bethany: (Shuffling her foot) Mm........... Clyde: (Breaking into a smile) You didn't lose our tickets did you, honey, pookie, love of my life? Bethany: Well............... The sound of bone breaking rings through the airline as a gaggle of stewardesses attempt to save one of their own. She lays on the ground, as a little girl slams her head repeatedly off the flooring. Bethany: I..........SAID........I........WANTED.......JELLO!!!!!!!! As blood begins to coat Bethany's hands, two pieces of paper with the Valuejet logo printed on them slide free..........landing behind her and floating under the seat as she continues her thrashing. Bethany: AND........I.......DON'T........LIKE........THE........INFLIGHT........MOVIE!!!!!! DYAAAAAGH!!!!!!!! Just as quickly Bethany breaks out of her flashback..........glances away from her lover. Bethany: Nope, don't know what happened to it. Clyde: Bah! Angrily the burned man again stomps forth..........looking all around in exasperation. Clyde: Now we're stuck in godforsaken Cincinnati! CINCINNATI I SAY~! Bethany: Well it's okay, you've got a match right here anyway. Clyde: (Staring) Match? You mean I'm not fired already? Bethany: No. Clyde: .............wow PCW is worse off than I thought. Bethany: Well, you are up against Big Daveylicious, Mike Frampton, Badge, the Rush, Kid Krazie, Violence Jack, Totally Tubular Tom, Heavy Metal Harry, Carey J. Nowski, Stephen Cairns, Jack Valentine, Johnny Heart, DarkStar, Akiyama, the Chef, The End, D-Kay Calyx, Duzza, Jay Sylo, Jon Kano, Charlie Loc and the Multiple Man. Clyde: I'm shocked you can remember all that off the top of your head darling. Bethany: (Hiding a cuecard behind her back) Well I'm talented. Clyde: Alright so let's just go find our luggage.............that should help us narrow the field of jobbers. And onward the pair journeys............though the going is rough eventually they stumble across the small cubicle that serves as Valuejet's baggage pickup. Bethany stands on one side of the conveyer, Clyde on the other as they wait. And wait. And wait. Bethany: Uh........... Clyde: It's coming. And they wait. And wait. And wait. Bethany: Clyde. Clyde: Shush. Behind the duo a clock winds round and round.........passing the minutes, the hours. Finally as the sun begins to set Clyde looks to the sky. Clyde: Where the hell is it!? We pan through the tunnel..............all the way through, out into the evening air and up into the atmosphere. Rapidly we zoom across the world, finally landing in a remote island where several small children adorned in grass skirts frolic and play inside an opened suitcase stuffed to the brim with several nasty looking blades, chains and what can only be described as very sharp sexual toys. Little Boy: (with a razor dildo strapped to his face) Me flamingo, ahahaha! Clyde: Bah! Bethany: Well now what the hell do we do? Clyde: Only thing we can do. We sit here in this damned airport and wait for the assorted jobbers to arrive. Maybe by then our luggage will show up............if not we tear them apart with our bare hands. And so our horrible twosome sit on either side of the conveyer belt............throwing each other dirty looks and occasional pieces of trash as they await their victims, and we mercifully fade to black...............
