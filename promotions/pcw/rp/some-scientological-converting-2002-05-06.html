Some Scientological Converting.

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/05/02 Scientology and Hollywood have always been closely linked right back to it’s founding days. The faith has always kept a strong physical presence in Los Angeles and uses it’s current crop of film stars to help spread the words of Hubbard through TV interviews, Newspaper profiles and other promotional appearances. At the forefront of this wave are the likes of Tom Cruise, John Travolta, Kirstey Alley, Juliet Lewis and Mimi Rogers. But it is not just the film industry, celebrity as a whole is used with the likes of Lisa Marie Presley and Courtney Love...Now they also have wrestling to bring the belief to the forefront with Xavier Kannon....And to his disgust...Duke Williams. Now, it is well known that Xavier has his arrogant side....In fact, it’s more than a side, it’s him. So naturally he hasn’t taken to kindly to the intrusion of Duke Williams, his arch nemesis in PCW, but what can he do for Scientology to sway their favour, to nudge Duke out of the spotlight? Well, when it comes to Xavier and Ellie~! nothing is out of the question. “If Duke thinks he can just walk into Scientology then he as another thought coming....” Although we can’t see anything other than shadows, barely moving, we can hear the voice of the PCW Commissioner Xavier Kannon talking softly. “.....I shall show my fellow believers that it is I who am wrestling’s ONLY TRUE Scientologist, and to prove it I shall deliver them what we love more than anything....” As he speaks now he is moving through the shadows slowly and as if he has to navigate things in front of and around him. As he pauses the sound of a live band starting up can be heard, not clearly, just the sound of the bass, drum, and a loud, constant distortion that hints at a guitar. “....Celebrities!” As the Continental Champion chuckles evily to himself we cut away to show Portuguese Canadian Nelly Furtado on stage, the continuance of the music heard before hinting that Xavier was backstage at wherever this concert is taking place. In front of the stage the crowd are packed in tightly as the stunning singer strolls across the stage where her rather generic band play the backing music. Furtado : “Shaky, shaky steps in the middle of the day, a fire in my path and a cool decay....” As she belts out “Party” the camera moves up from her skipping across the stage to the lighting rig above her where we see just a hint of a movement, just enough to give you the feeling someone is there. Furtado : “I’m changing my inflection and how I say the words, I’m hoping it’ll sound like something they’ve never head....” Suddenly the lights go out, and not just that, the whole power looks to have been lost as only her un-amplified voice is heard, barely managing to get above the whispers of the shocked crowd and the naked drum beat. Furtado : “....Oh, the death of the pa....Huh?” For a few seconds there is silence from the stage as the musicians and singer give up, only the mutterings of the crowd can be heard, certain lines standing out from the continual hum of conversation. “What is it?” “The power must have gone!” “Do we get a refund?” “OKAY! WHO GROPED ME?” They are then silenced as the stage lights come back on to show that only the musicians are left on the stage and it takes them a few seconds to realise that the singer is gone, something that is noticed by a collective gasp by the crowd. The musicians turn to each other, none sure what to do. Bass Player : “Er.....Shall we just jam or something?” As the backing band are shown confused on stage we fade to Black and the voice of the singer, not sounding too happy is then heard, her voice sounding like she is struggling to free herself. “Hey....WHAT are you doing, I’m like a bird and these ropes tying my hands together are somewhat preventing me from flying away!” Her voice echoes in the enclosed confines of her as yet undetermined location, but by the way the camera is shaking around and the ambient noises behind her voice the best bet is in the back of a moving van. “Look....This is your last chance to untie me.....I act so tough, tough, tough then you turned off the light and I don’t see any kind of illumination in here!” Once more her quite justified complaints go unanswered as she rocks around in the dark confines. Finally another voice is heard, and it is quite recognisable. “Um, could you, like, um....SO not talk! You really do, like, have such an annoying voice...” A flicker of light appears off camera and this grows into a larger area of illumination that now shows Miss Furtado in all her glory, looking around her at what is indeed the back of a moving van. The camera pans around to show the 1st couple of Scientology sitting opposite her with a paraffin lamp looking at the singer they have captured. Xavier : “So it begins.....My rise back to the top of Scientology shall be secured when I deliver Nelly Furtado to Hubbardville! Has Duke managed to do that? NO...He hasn’t turned up and pulled Alison Krauss out of a van. No, he hasn’t....It is I, Gold Patron Meritorious Xavier Kannon who is the TRUE Scientologist!” Ellie~! nods along with him, staring at Nelly and nodding with a smug grin on her face. Eleanor : “The.....TRUE.....Scientologist!” The camera pans back to Nelly who has a blank look on her face, the fear of her abduction almost being replaced by bemusement for those who have taken her. Furtado : “Chill man.....Are you a Cult or something?” “NO WE ARE NOT A CULT!” Before the camera can even turn to show his irate face, Xavier yells at the singer, loud enough to cause her to screw up her face and in a reflex action try and cover her ears with the hands tied behind her back. Xavier : “We are the World’s PREMIERE RELIGION! Top celebrities such as Jenna Elfman and Isaac Hayes follow us...We are the faster growing faith on the planet....And you call us a CULT?” He shakes his head in despair as Ellie~! sighs and wags her finger at Nelly. Ellie : “Us......A.....CULT?“ Quite justifiably Nelly Furtado is still confused, Xavier’s last words not doing that much to lift the vale of mystery from over her Brown eyes. Furtado : “Then why are you kidnapping me that....That all seems kinda kooky and culty to me?” Even a man as confident in his actions as the Gold Patron Meritorious has to pause for a second to think. Xavier : “Er...I’m kidnapping you....Um...Because Duke Williams hasn’t! He has infiltrated my Religion and I shall stop him any way I can, both in the ring with the use of liberal cheating, and out of it by kidnapping celebrities and converting them just like I am doing with you!” As usual, his valet nods in agreement. Eleanor : “Doing.....With.....You!” As the couple grin we switch to an exterior view of the van speeding past a sign that reads.... “HUBBARDVILLE WELCOMES SCIENTOLOGISTS & CAREFUL DRIVERS” ....And Nelly’s voice, admitting defeat is heard. “Okay....Looks like I’m going to be a Scientologists then....You do this often?” “There are more....” We fade out from the main road into Hubbardville and a man’s voice, very thespian in tone is heard speaking...Every word well pronounced. “Alas POOR Yor-....No, that doesn’t sound right....ALAS POOR YORRICK, I KN-....No, lacks the pizzazz.....ALAS POOR YORRICK I KNEW HIM HOR-...Hmmm, no. Maybe I should understate it...” As we traverse the darkness into bright electric light we see none other than Hollywood actor John Malkovich looking into his bathroom mirror wearing nothing but a Black robe as he runs through the most famous line of Hamlet. Malkovich : “....I can do understated...” The thesp clears his throat. Malkovich : “....Alas poor Yorrick I knew him Horat-....Huh?” Just as he goes to deliver the famous line the bathroom flight flicks off, leaving Malkovich standing all alone in the dark room as what little light there is fades to Black. “Hmm...That isn’t go-..ARGH!”
