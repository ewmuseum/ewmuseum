The druggy Stand-Up Comedian Club!!

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 24, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up on a sunny afternoon in a noisy environment. Taxis and other cars are streaming up and down the vast streets as several people and tourists walk up and down the sidewalks beside the streets on each side. The buildings though seem to be run down though, and the city has to be bad part of town, but what town is it? The camera zeroes in on Semore Smoke who can be seen walking through the crowd on the sidewalk. He seems to actually know his way around, this city isn’t Wilson, its somewhere else. Somewhere bigger. Smoke looks around at the people around him to make sure no one is looking around at him. He then turns down a dark ally. The camera scene changes to a scene from the sidewalk itself. It’s following Semore down the dark ally, which is full of dirty dumpsters and trash all over the place. Semore steps around some old boxes and then steps over a homeless man who’s fallen asleep in the filth. Smoke looks behind himself again and when he doesn’t see anyone he lifts up a dumpster lid and looks in. The camera looks into the dumpster as well and there’s a secret staircase going underground. Semore looks around again and then climbs up onto the dumpster and then into the dumpster. He then heads down the steps and the camera follows him down. He walks down into the cooler underground, which is lighted by assorted torches and flashlights that were left on in holders on the wall. Semore walks down about thirty steps until he encounters a landing in the narrow case and a door is on the other side. He walks up to the door and knocks on it three times. A small window door slides open on the door and a person’s eye is seen through it…}}}} Man: What’s the password? {{{{Semore looks around himself again to make sure no one is there and then he looks at the door and says...}}}} Semore Smoke: Grass. {{{{The eye behind the door closes the small slide in the door and then you hear latches on the door being pulled aside and then the door opens up. As the door opens up Semore looks at the man behind the door and smiles. The man behind the door is none other then the famous druggy stand-up comedian himself, Dennis Leary! Dennis looks at Semore and then has to think for a while for his name…}}}} Dennis Leary: I know you… yet I don’t know you… what’s your name? Semore Smoke: Semore Smoke. I’m a new druggy stand-up comedian plus I wrestle for Primetime Championship Wrestling. {{{{Dennis nods as Smoke says who he is and smiles as he says that he’s a wrestler…}}}} Dennis Leary: Oh yeah, that’s right. You know, I’m a fan of yours right? It’s just that damn weed gets to your short-term memory after awhile. Semore Smoke: I know the feeling. {{{{Dennis looks behind himself and then looks behind Semore and then nods and waves his hand behind him as a welcome and says…}}}} Dennis Leary: Well, don’t stay out there. Come in. Welcome to the New Jersey district of the druggy comedian club. {{{{Semore walks through the doorway and can already smell the reefer smell that he had been so used to now. Dennis closes the door behind him and then latches it all the way shut. They then both walk around the corner together and Smoke sees several of todays and yesterday’s stand-up best sitting down smoking weed, and talking. Semore looks around the room and sees several familiar faces. Jon Stewart is over in the corner talking to Dave Chappell and Jim Brewer, two stars who played in the movie, Half Baked, with Jon Stewart who had a small cameo in the movie. Then seated at another table is the duo of David Spade and Adam Sandler, Smoke never knew that Spade smoked. The final table confuses him though, what seems to be Horatio Sanz, Jimmy Fallon are talking to an old guy… but the old guy looks familiar to Semore. He looks like Saturday Night Live veteran, and thought to be dead comedian, John Belushi! Semore just can’t believe the scene. Dennis puts his arm around Semore as if they were long time buddies and he gives him a tour of the room…}}}} Dennis Leary: Over here we have our bar. You can get weed, anything, from that bar, and the best part it, since you’re a part of the club, you can get it for free! We’ve got a packed house tonight it’s like everyone’s day off. {{{{They walk towards the center of the room and sit down at a table. A waitress with a joint in her mouth brings them both a blunt and leaves two of them a bottle of beer. Smoke opens up the bottle and takes a swig of it. He then puts the blunt into his mouth and lights it up with one of the candles sitting on the table. Dennis pulls out a lighter from his pocket and lights the blunt with it. He then opens up his beer as well but he lets it sit for a few seconds before he drinks it…}}}} Semore Smoke: Wow, I can’t believe I’m actually here with the players club for druggy stand-up comedians! {{{{Dennis takes a drag on his blunt and then chases it with a swig of the beer…}}}} Dennis Leary: Yeah, welcome to the stand-up life, drugs, and woman, everything a grown man needs. So, I hear you’re wrestling tomorrow night at the arena here in New Jersey at the PCW Pay Per View. Who are you facing again and is there anything at stake? {{{{Semore smiles and then swigs on his beer, in fact every time he’s about to speak he takes a swig and then a puff off the blunt…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yeah, I’m wrestling tomorrow night. I’ll be facing some asses named Scorpion, Eddie Mandrake, and Jerome “Blayze” Jordan in a four corners match for the PCW Rising Star Championship. May be the lowest title in PCW, but a start is a start, and I’ve got a dream to live out. {{{{Dennis is doing the same thing as Semore and he takes a swig and puff before his every sentence…}}}} Dennis Leary: Yeah, that’s right. Yeah, there’s always a good place to start… but what’s that dream of yours? {{{{Smoke smiles a huge grin from ear to ear…}}}} Semore Smoke: I want to be one of the very few to ever win all the titles in Primetime Championship Wrestling history. Right now there is only guy who’s ever done that. Dennis Leary: Oh… aren’t you too big to win the Cruiserweight title though? Semore Smoke: Yeah, but that’s when a diet comes in handy. I’ll drop thirty pounds just to win that title if I have to. I will live out that dream. {{{{David Spade gets up and walks towards their table. He sits down beside Dennis Leary and smiles at Smoke. He then turns to Leary and says…}}}} David Spade: Hey, who’s the new guy? Dennis Leary: This is Semore Smoke, he’s new to the business, but he’s been an entertainer for about two years now. David Spade: As what? {{{{Smoke takes a puff off the blunt again and then looks at Spade with his now glazed eyes…}}}} Semore Smoke: I’m a Professional Wrestler. {{{{Spade is high of course and when he hears this he just sits there and laughs uncontrollably, then he stops abruptly and says…}}}} David Spade: You mean you wear tight clothes and run around a squared ring with ropes and run up against other men? Semore Smoke: Uh… yeah I guess so. David Spade: Ha! Cool! I want to do that! {{{{Spade gets up and leaves the table, Smoke turns to Leary and looks at him with a confused look on his face…}}}} Dennis Leary: Don’t mind him, David isn’t an open gay guy, but when he’s high or drunk he’s as open as he could be. As long as you keep everything you see and hear down here, then you’ll be fine. It’s the stand-up code of honor. Everything thinks that John over there is dead ha he’s just been down here managing the place for about thirty years. {{{{Smoke looks behind himself and realizes that he was right about his observation at the beginning when he first entered the bar. He then turns to Dennis and says…}}}} Semore Smoke: How old is he though? He doesn’t look to be much older then sixty, but if he was John Belushi he’d have to be about seventy or so. Dennis Leary: That’s the thing that many people don’t understand, weed doesn’t actually shorten your life. It actually lengthens it by making you look younger feel younger, and even have a longer life. {{{{Smoke never knew this and his mouth just drops. He then gets a question in his head….}}}} Semore Smoke: What about Chris Farley? {{{{Dennis nods and puffs on the blunt, he then smiles as he answers the question…}}}} Dennis Leary: Chris is really gone, he didn’t just smoke weed. The night he died he did about every drug in the world, and it still took him about two days to die off of all those drugs. {{{{Smoke never knew this, he had figured Farley was just the victim of a common overdose, he never knew that he had been doing drugs for about two days straight. He then has another question this one isn’t about a comedian though…}}}} Semore Smoke: What about Elvis? Dennis Leary: Don’t even go there. Not only are we a secret underground club for the drugged up comedians, but also a branch of the Men in Black, which is right down the street from here. Elvis was abducted by aliens and went back home where he came from. The body that was found of him was a look a like of him that we found on the streets. We found the guy drugged up already so we just took him to where Elvis was found and put him in a bathtub, where he slowly drowned. It was a gruesome thing we did, but Elvis couldn’t just disappear. Semore Smoke: Yeah I understand. Well… I’m about to pass out here, so before I do. I wanted to ask if you would go to the match tomorrow. {{{{Leary smiles and nods, then Smoke’s head hits the table hard as he passes out on the table, and the scene goes black…}}}}
