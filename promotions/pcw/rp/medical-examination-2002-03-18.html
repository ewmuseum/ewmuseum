Medical Examination

18 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/3/02 White sterilised tiles adorn the walls of the doctors office. The floor covered with an old grey carpet trampled by thousands of invisible footprints. A small desk with an old computer some medical journals and a half finished cup of coffee stands in the corner, next to that an old metal bed on which sits the newest addition to the PCW roster Ben J. Pailthorpe. Standing next to him is a man wearing an equally sterilised white jacket and holding a clipboard. Doctor: Good afternoon Mr. *looks at clipboard* Pailthorpe. Now you know why you are here. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Vaguely. Doctor: I'm Doctor Phillip Simpson and today I will perfor some test on you for your PCW medical file, do you understand. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Yes, im not stupid you know. Doctor: Good, well I need to check the information GWF sent over, your full name? Ben J. Pailthorpe: Benjamin John Pailthorpe. Doctor: Ok, your age? Ben J. Pailthorpe: Twenty four years old. Doctor: Height and weight? Ben J. Pailthorpe: Six foot tall, Two hundred and fifty pounds. Doctor: Any medical conditions you suffer from? Ben J. Pailthorpe: I had asthma as a kid, thats about it. Doctor: Ok, any history of heart disease in your family. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Yes, on my mothers side. Doctor: Ok *Scribbles Notes* Did your former employers know about this? Ben J. Pailthorpe: No, it never seemed important. Doctor: Aha, any long term injuries? Ben J. Pailthorpe: None whatsoever. Doctor: Glad to hear it, any drug alcohol adictions? Ben J. Pailthorpe: I used to be an alcoholic, heavy drinking from about age eighteen to about November last year. I also used the performance enahncing drug Prolymethene once. Doctor: Hmmm, right. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Anything wrong? Doctor: No, no, its all down here. Now im going to do some tests, first an eye test. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Ok. The doctor walks over to his desk and picks up a white peace of card with black letters on it. Doctor: Mr Pailthorpe, could you read this for me. Ben J. Pailthorpe: D R I N K F E C K A R S E G I R L S *Squinting* G O B S H I T E. Doctor: Oh, sorry thats the promotional St. Patricks Day eye test. Speaking of St. Patrick, had Paddy O'Shea in here the other day, pretty beat up. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Well that would be my handywork, a good old crowbar, sweet as. Doctor: Yes, well look at this one please. He holds up another picture. Ben J. Pailthorpe: R E P Q D S C *Squints* H D L P N A. Doctor: Good, well now I need a few samples. From his inside pocket the Doctor pulls a rather large syringe. Ben J. Pailthorpe: I expect thats for...um...cleaning contact lenses isnt it. Doctor: *With an Evil Grin* No Mr Pailthorpe, its for blood samples. Ben gulps loudly. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Ahem...erm...yeah...ok. Doctor: Hold out your arm Mr. Pailthorpe. Ben slowly extends his arm, rolling up the sleeve of his black hoodie to reveal his bare skin. Doctor: Hold still Mr Pailthorpe. The Doctor slaps the skin untill some veins are exposed, then he slowly pushes the needle into one of the blue veins. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Im cool...im calm...im BJP! With his free hand he points at himself with each letter. By doing it with one arm he avoids all copyright infringement. Doctor: Here comes the blood! Ever so slowly blood fills the syringe and Ben cringes ever so slightly at the sight of his own crimson plasma. The Docotr removes the syringe from Ben's arm and gives Ben a cotton wool swab to hold on the wound. Doctor: Right, now we need a sperm sample. Ben looks startled. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Im sorry? Doctor: A sperm sample. Ben J. Pailthorpe: You cannot be serious? Doctor: Im afraid I am, you see blood tests dont always detect illegal substances. Ben J. Pailthorpe: But, im off that stuff, I dont do it anymore, I spent three months in rehab. Doctor: Im sorry Mr Pailthorpe but its mandatory. Ben J. Pailthorpe: *Reluctantly* Ok. Doctor: Right. *He picks a small cup off of his desk* Here you go. Ben J. Pailthorpe: No thanks, I just had a cup of coffee. Doctor: No you idiot, its for your sample. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Oh, ok. Doctor: Just go through that door there. The Doctor points at a door that looks like a cupboard door. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Whats through there? Doctor: Thats where you give your sample. Ben J. Pailthorpe: You mean now? Doctor: Yes. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Right, I see. Ben gets up and slowly approaches the door, he places his hand on the handle. Ben J. Pailthorpe: In here? Doctor: Yes. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Ok. Ben walks into the room and closes the door behind him, the camera stays outside (of course...) and looks at the doctor. There is a pause and then... Ben J. Pailthorpe: I cant do this. Doctor: Why not? Ben J. Pailthorpe: Because your out there and you know what im doing in here. Doctor: You are giving a sperm sample. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Exactly. Doctor: Oh, your worried because I know your masturbating in there. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Well thats putting it bluntly, but yes. Doctor: Well I have another large needle out here which I could inject into your testicles. Ben J. Pailthorpe: ... Although we cant see his face we can imagine the cringe. Doctor: Masturbating is the easiest way. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Um..couldnt we call it something else. Doctor: What...masturbating? Ben J. Pailthorpe: Yeah. Doctor: Howabout Bashing the Bishop. Ben J. Pailthorpe: No. Doctor: Stroking the salami? Ben J. Pailthorpe: No. Doctor: Petting the dolphin? Ben J. Pailthorpe: NO! Just leave it... Theres another awkward pause. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Couldnt I like take it home with me, wait untill I get a little bored and then...you know... Doctor: Knock one out? Ben J. Pailthorpe: Er...*Sigh*...yeah knock one out. Doctor: Im afraid I need the sample for immediate testing. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Gah... Doctor: Do you need a magazine? Ben J. Pailthorpe: What do you have? Doctor: Readers Digest, Gardners Monthly and Pottery Club. Silence. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Right...i'll do without. Doctor: How is it going? Ben J. Pailthorpe: What? Doctor: The wanking, how is it going? Ben J. Pailthorpe: Very subtle. Doctor: Well, whatever gets you going. Ben J. Pailthorpe: *Agitated* I meant that YOUR very subtle. Doctor: Thank you. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Gah... Doctor: I have a video if you want it. Ben J. Pailthorpe: What is it? Doctor: A video. Ben J. Pailthorpe: I mean whats on the video? Doctor: A sticker. Ben J. Pailthorpe: WHAT FILM IS ON THE VIDEO! Doctor: Oh, Antiques Road Show. Silence. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Your a freak. There is another pause before Ben emerges from the room holding a cup filled with liquid. Doctor: That was quick. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Yeah....right. Whats next? Doctor: Would you wait here while I get these processed quickly. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Sure. The Doctor exits the room and Ben sits back on the bed humming to himself. About five minutes later the doctor returns. Doctor: Mr Pailthorpe... Ben J. Pailthorpe: Yes. Doctor: I have some bad news. Its about your sperm sample. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Yes. Doctor: Well, you see it appears your sperm is...saliva. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Um..yes, well... The scene fades with Ben explaining hmself to the doctor...
