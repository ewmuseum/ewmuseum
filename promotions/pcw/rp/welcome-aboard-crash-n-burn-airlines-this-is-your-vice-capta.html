Welcome aboard crash'n'burn airlines, this is your 'Vice 'Captain speaking...

Date: not entered When we last left off…Flaymin was slowly coming to the realisation that his dreams of having balls of steel had been shattered.Instead, he now has eyeballs of steel. The mission to locate a new testicle continues…. This time across international borders. [Scene opens aboard a Boeing 747 jet slowly descending to the runway below.Seated in First Class and busy trying to figure out how to buckle his seatbelt is none other than Flaymin.He fiddles around with the buckle but is still unable to connect the two pieces] Hirraani: Here, let me do it. [Flaymin’s valet, Hirraani, reaches over and grabs the two sides of the belt.Calmly she slots one buckle into the other until a ‘clicking’ sound is heard] Hirraani: See, that’s all there is to it. Flaymin: I still don’t see the point in wearing these seatbelts.If this plane suddenly crashes, this little belt isn’t going to save my life. [The woman seated opposite Flaymin, looks at him and then suddenly turns pale.Her eyes boggle open and she begins to yell at the top of her voice] Woman: WE’RE GOING TO DIE!!!!!! [The woman frantically tries to unbuckle her seatbelt but is held down by the flight attendants.Meanwhile, the passengers around the woman also begin to panic and start yelling] Hirraani: I wonder what’s up her ass? Flaymin: She must have inside information. Hirraani: What? Flaymin: Haven’t you seen Passenger 57?Die Hard 2?Or that TV show with that cartoon pilot. Hirraani: Roger Ramjet? Flaymin: That’s the one! [The passengers on the plane continue to scream in fear under the assumption that the plane is going to crash.With not enough flight attendants available to calm the passengers, Flaymin takes it upon his responsibility to help quiet the people. He stands up and reaches into the baggage compartment above the seats.He pulls out a video and holds it high above his head] Flaymin: Alright, listen up… this is your um… Vice Captain speaking. Hirraani: Vice Captain? Flaymin: Keep quiet, woman. [Flaymin looks back at his fellow passengers who have all quietened down and are gazing at him in hope] Flaymin: Here in my hand I have a copy of ‘When Jumbo’s Collide’. Passenger in seat 3F: Is that the plane disaster movie? Flaymin: What?No!It’s the porno! If there is anything on earth that can calm the soul, then you can’t go past jumbo jumblies rubbing up against each other. Dildo Digital Magazine gave this movie a rating of 8/10. Passenger is seat 2B: Isn’t that a homosexual magazine? Flaymin: Uh… yeah I accidentally picked it up…. Passenger in seat 2B: And bought it? Flaymin: Yeah that too… Passenger in seat 2B: And read through it? Flaymin: Yea… I mean… that’s none of your business!!What is this?The National Inquisition?? Just sit down and shut the hell up or I won’t play the movie. [The passengers all quietly return to their seats and they buckle up their seatbelts in anticipation of watching the highly acclaimed porno. Flaymin hands the Flight Attendant the movie and then sits back down in his seat] Hirraani: You’re lucky you didn’t tell them you have a subscription to Dildo Digital Magazine. Flaymin: When you live the life of a pornstar, your life is a blur… and the magazines you buy don’t make any sense! [The Flight attendant walks over to the VCR Player in the locked up audio compartment and inserts the porno.She presses ‘Play’ and then a moment later the large TV Screen in the centre of the plane begins to display the movie. A few seconds later, Flaymin frowns when he notices that the movie isn’t a porno afterall] Flaymin: I must have put the wrong movie in the wrong casing…. Hirraani: Well then what movie is this? Flaymin: ‘When Jumbo’s Collide’- The Plane Disaster flick. Hirraani: Uh oh…[Once the opening credits of the movie have finished playing the scene shifts into the sky where a jumbo jet is flying high in the sky] Passenger in seat 3B: This doesn’t look like a porno. Flaymin: Don’t worry, in a minute there’ll be a group orgy in the bathroom… [Hirraani slaps her forehead, knowing full well what will happen next in the movie] Flaymin: Don’t do that, you’ll give it away!![The movie continues to play.The plane in the movie is still flying high in the sky.Suddenly it begins to jerk forward and instantly nose dives at great speeds.The people on board the plane begin flying out of their seats and smashing through the windows, while babies cry uncontrollably and the rest of the passengers yell hysterically] Flaymin: I think we better stop the movie now!! [The Flight Attendant rushes over to the audio compartment and quickly presses the ‘Stop’ Button on the VCR player.But in her sudden attempts to stop the video, she actually presses the Fast Forward button… The passengers surrounding Flaymin all watch in horror as the movie plays at a higher speed.In the movie, the plane dips faster and faster to the ground until there is suddenly a gigantic explosion and bodies fly everywhere.Fire rips throughout the plane as blood gushes everywhere] [But before any more of the movie can progress, the Flight Attendant rips out the cables connecting the VCR to the Power Socket, thus instantly disconnecting the movie. But the damage has been done…The passengers on the plane grow even more hysterical and begin yelling once again. Hirraani shakes her head from side to side with a wide grin on her face] Hirraani: I told you to just sit still, but no- you had to show the passengers a detailed montage of their impending grizzly death] Flaymin: I thought it was the porno! [Before the fellow passengers can grow even more hostile, the Captain begins to speak over the intercom] Captain: This is your Captain speaking.We have just touched down at Moscow International Airport and we thank you for flying ‘Crash’n’burn airlines’.We hope you enjoyed your flight….. because I certainly did considering I’ve never flown a jumbo jet bef… whoops!, I left the intercom on, again!! [The passengers once again come to a sudden halt.No more yelling,The screaming and panic has ceased. Calmness and a fresh sigh of relief sweeps throughout the Boeing 747. Once the plane has come to a complete halt, the passengers unload their luggage from the baggage compartment. They all scowl at Flaymin, who just just shrugs it off and waves back like an idiot] Hirraani: Well here we are. Flaymin: Yeah, do you think I’ll get my video back? Hirraani: Nevermind that.Are you sure The Russian Astronauts can help you?Maybe you should have just asked NASA? Flaymin: NASA???Those morons couldn’t even build a boxcar let alone a rocket. [Scene fades to black as Flaymin and Hirraani pick up their luggage and slowly make their way off the plane] What is Flaymin doing in Moscow?And how on earth will the Russian Astronauts help Flaymin get a new testicle? You’ll have to tune in next time to find out!!!!!!
