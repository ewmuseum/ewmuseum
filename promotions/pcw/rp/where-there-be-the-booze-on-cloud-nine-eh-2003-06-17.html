Where There be the Booze on Cloud Nine, eh?

17 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17.06.03 Stowaway “…Or, for only $6.95, try our new barbeque deluxe! Only at Denny’s.” Waving his hand in disgust, Abner reaches over and grabs the remote. Pointing it in the general directionof the television screen with one hand, and holding a beer in the other, Abner clicks a button and screengoes to black. Abner: Ah, that’s better. Cuzin Ed: Yeah…I mean, you gots to be crazy to like that shit…and uh, who likes the “upin” channelanyway? Abner turns to his cousin; he flashes Ed a dirty look. Abner: It’s pronounced “U-P-N”. Cuzin Ed: Oh… Abner gets off his comfy sofa, and with nothing better to do, …God forbid…prepares to…to exercise! Abner: What has this world come to… Pulling up his pants, those of us reading this roleplay cringe, staring at the rolls of flab on Abner’s hairymidriff, as the jovial redneck tightens his belt. Bending down to tie his boots, we’re treated to anothersurprise... Behind him, Abner’s sofa sits lonely, unattended. It’s big, and red, and ripped in all the “strategic” places,holes next to the reclining lever, cotton foaming at the mouth. On each arm of the chair there are two“beer-holders”...I kid you not, two “beer-holders”...and then there’s another on the actual leg-rest! These obviously are not used to hold sodas… Abne bends down and begins to stretch again, but there’s a loud, grizzled belch, and we turn our attentionin the other direction. Cuzin Ed: No shit! Pulling himself to a sot-up position (and yes, not without a great deal of difficulty...), tubby Cuzin Ed runshis hands across his large, flabby bowl of his “redneck-hood” and comes to attention. Underneath thenearly three-hundred pound frame of the farm boy, a long blue sofa breathes a sigh of relief. Cuzin Ed: Yeah Ab, but is it really worth it? Abner: I reckon. Cuzin Ed: Ya know, leavin’ the room? Abner: Well, we are out of sofa-dispenser “drinks”. Looking down at the couch, one can note that on either side, cut out deep into the arms, are full-scale,massive “pack-holders” specifically designed for the purpose of holding twelve-pack cases of beer. Yes,that’s right…a twelve-pack dispenser built into the couch. But then again, that’s Abner n’ Ed for ya... Cuzin Ed: True. But leaving does require walking… Abner: Point. The yawning, bored-out gets back to his seat. He hasn’t been booked in over a week now, and even aftersuch a victory over such an impressive opponent (a true miralce over one Matt Jackson), he’s time andpastience are running thin...as is his blood-alcohol level. And it’s time for another drink. He begins to rub his chin, thinking hard for a moment. He nods this way and that, looking forward andabout, before finally, a twinkle ligths up in his reddened, burned out acloholic eyes. After a few moremischievous moments of seemingly subversive plotting, Abner makes up his mind. He spins around, andwalks toward the door. He then directs the fattened framboy toward the doorway, as the larger, moremuscular redneck steps out into the hallway. Reluctantly, Cuzin Ed follows. Abner: Eh, what’s the use of mopin’ ‘round this place anyway? There’s a whole ship out there, and allwe’ve done since we got here is sit around, drink beers anyway. Cuzin Ed: Yeah…but Abner….that’s all we do anyway. Abner: Heh , yeah, but this ain’t like old times. I’m a professional now- Cuzin Ed rolls his eyes, cutting him short. Abner (ignoring this): And I’ve got to act like it. You know, go out and there and make notice of myself!Represent my company and all that. PCW’s starting to come back around, and with all these celebritiesaboard, I think it’s time to make a good impression. Anyway, I just won a match against Jackson, one ofthe better wrestlers of today, and it wouldn’t be proper not to celebrate… Abner pops open a can of beer, hidden down in his sleeve. Abner: Properly. Cuzin Ed: I mean, yeah, I guess… Abner: ‘Sides, we can just get some more booze. Cuzin Ed: Yeah, that’s all fine n’ dandy. But what about… “The German”. Abner stops dead in his tracks. Abner: “The German”…Cock-suckin’, rotten… Abner’s mumbling trails off, his mind brought back to reality by a hardy belch of his cousin’s digestivesystem. Abner: I wouldn’t worry about him…seems that he only shows up on event nights. Cuin Ed, showing his cautious, reserved nature. Cuzin Ed: Okay, I’ll go…but I don’t have to like it… Abner shrugs and turns back toward the door... And moments later, side-by-side, the two redneck cousins are walking down the hallway…The larger ofthe two turns and faces the short, fat one. Abner: See, this ain’t so bad. Cuzin Ed: Yeah, well my legs haven’t given out yet…but I can feel them getting tired… Abner: Ed… Cuzin Ed: What? Abner: We’ve been gone for less than three minutes….we’ve probably only walked ‘bout nine-ten yards. Cuzin Ed: Really? Abner: Well yeah, I mean, look up… See…we’ve only been using dialogue for about five or six lines. Cuzin Ed: Oh yeah… Abner: “Oh yeah…” He reaches over, slaps his cousin on the back of his head. Cuzin Ed: What the- He stops dead in his track, looking out in front of him. Abner imitates his cousin, and stops to look andsee what the big deal is. Abner: Ed, I was just foolin’, I mean- All of a sudden, he, too, loses his ability to speak. Abner: Y….yu….you…you’re- Unknown voice: That’s right, I’m Charlie Daniels! Looking up, a looming figure comes into view, bending over and extending an arm toward Abner. He’s abig, burly man…one of the Abner type. He’s got a long, black beard, short, dark hair, and sports a big,friendly country-style smile. His sparkly brown eyes light up, as PCW’s resident redneck, Abner, acceptshis hand and gives it a solid shake. The man is wearing clothing similar to Abner: a plad vest, blue andwhite-with a tee shirt under it-, tight black jeans, a brown leather belt, and, of course, matching barnboots. Abner: I-uh, I... The man shoots up a hand, causing the redneck to go silent. Daniels: “Reckon you’re a big fan of my work”? Abner: Well, no-uh, I- Daniels: Well....cause I am! But Abner, jaw dropped, wad of tobacco hanging out of the side of his mouth, simply doesn’t know whatto say. “Now HOW do you respond or reply to THAT!” The redneck thinks to himself, looking down athis feet now. You see, The Charlie Daniels Band have always been a favorite of his, and now, low and behold, theleader of this griup, a man who Abner has cme to respect and admire greatly, is standing a mere two orthree feet in front of him. He’s shocked. A boyhood idol, a real, in his opinion, living legend...looking,breathing...almost touching him. It’s just too much to take in. Abner takes a step back. I mean, Abner’s not one to “bow down” to the “superiority” of a celebrity...case-in-point, the Bass Masterfishermen...but this, this is simply ridiculous. For once, maybe for the first time in his life, Abner, thepride-spittin’, rough-gruntin’, flag-wavin’ reneck...has been humbled. Completely. Daniels: Well now, don’t ya got somethin’ to say ‘bout that? Huh, hear me boy? Hear? He snaps his fingers in front of Abner’s eyes, causing the star-struck pupils to follow. Daniels: Well, ain’t ya? Cause I tell ya what! That was one bad-ass beatin’ you gave to that there MattJackson...supposedly the “best” of the new guys that there is...reckon? Abner (slowly nodding his head): ...R...rr....reckon... Daniels: Well now good! (pauses) Now c’mon... He takes Abner by the sleeve of his shirt... Daniels: You can buy me a drink! Abner: Duh-duh...uh....okay....hard liquor sound nice? Daniels: Perfect. Turning on his boots, the country music legend scampers on off down the hallway, and in zombie-likefashion, the rednecks match step. Looking down at the feet in front of them, Abner ansd Cuzin Ed see thetrue beauty of this man’s footwear: It’s a long, leather pair of whote and black-striped “rattlesnake” boots,allegedly made by a real, living reptile, killed by the man himself! (Of course, as we all know, reptilesshed their skin...so Daniels had no reason to kill the snake...but anyway, he did. And what, do you thinkAbner knows this? Or, even if he did, he’d care? No, probably not.) So anyway, they continue on. And Abner, fnally getting back his gots, snapping back into reality, begins to make small-talk with theman. Abner: So-uh, you’re the man, huh? Daniels: Uh...well I’m “a” man. Abner: Charlie Daniels...right? Daniels: Yep. Far as I can tell! He lets out a hoarse, elongated laugh. Abner smiles back, shrugging his shoulders. Being Abner and all, itdoens’t take much for the shock to pass.. Because, truth be known, he’s not really himself now...but now,with a bottle of scoth in-hand...he’s slowly, inevitably...turning back to...uh... “normal”. After receivingthe shock treatment and all, he hasn’t exeactly been himself. Two 50mgs of water a day will do that to yawhen you’re a redneck. See, as seen on last weeks airing of Fusion, water has strange effects-very strangeeffects indeed-on the “alcohol-blood level” of a redneck. Sure, he’s had his normal four or five cold onesan hour...but sometimes, he’s cut back to only one...a dangerously, dangerously low level of alcohol, lowlevel of beer for a redneck. Water. It bites. Abner: Whoa! I’m startin’ to feel a bit dizzy here... Cuzin Ed, who, up until now, has remained speechlessed, himself awed by the presence of his rednecklegend, begins to speak. He points down and directions Mr. Daniel’s attention to Abner’s wrist. Sittingthere, rest up against the corner of the hallway, Abner has a long , thin syringe, which he injects somestrange, clear liquid out of, and into his arm. Cuzin Ed: It’s water... Daniels: My God! ... Cuzin Ed just shakes his head, looking down and pitty and sorrow on his older, sickly cousin. Daniels: You mean- Cuzin Ed: Yep. “Germans”... Daniels: That bad? Germans, huh? Cuzin Ed (shaking is head in disbelief): ‘Fraid so. Daniels: Wow... The country music legend drops to his knees, falling to Abner’s side, and begins to comfort him. Takingoff his cowboy hat, he gently places it on the woozy redneck’s head. He takes his hand and whispers... Daniels: Cheer up, son. You see, ever since Abner first encountered the attacks and misdoings/giving by the masked “German”figure, his stress level rocketed sky-high. I mean, if there’s one thing a redneck is, it’s certainly thatthey’re xenophobic. And so when a foreigner, an outsidrer, especially a literal, foreing-born, non-UScitizen...and well, German, man...comes into the picture...things aren’t pretty. And as we also learned onFusion, H20...water, the anti-beer...has an inverse effect on not just the physical being, but the spirit and the soul, of an unsuspecting, weary redneck. Abner is a perfect example. And so as the two make-shift EMT’s, both Cuzin Ed and Charlier Daniels tend to Abner (graciouslyinjecting him with small hits of alcohol), Abner begins to come to. Abner: Uhg! He opens his eyes, looking up at the light, staring into the faces of his saviors. Abner: Sorry ‘bout that, guys... Cuzin Ed: It’s okay. Charlie Daniels: No need to, really. Abner: It’s just that...ugh!...I’m not really feeling too well, and...ugh!...I think I need some more beer... Daniels: Well just what the doctor ordered! He picks up the redneck (meaning, in actuality, merely helping the 250-plus pound redneck to his feet),and begins to drag him down the hallway. Daniels: Come on! Cuzin Ed: Where we going? Daniels: You’ll see...
