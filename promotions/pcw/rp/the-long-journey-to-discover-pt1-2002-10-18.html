The Long Journey to Discover....pt1

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18th Oct Over recent weeks El Diablo has claimed he will rid PCW of the man known as The Pharaoh. But Pharaoh has another agenda where he says he won't be the one leaving, and will in fact fight to stay as he refuses to be pushed out of PCW. Diablo however is fed up of all the filth and vermin in PCW and the widespread corruption, which he claims stems from The Pharaoh. El Diablo is on a mission so to speak, to clear PCW of it's deadweight, it's vermin and anyone that stands in his way on his journey to being the PCW World Heavyweight Champion. The Pharaoh represents all of those factors, and that is why El Diablo is hell bent on getting rid of him. The Pharaoh to El Diablo is just a minor hurdle, someone who stands in his way on his hunt to be champion. The Lord of Mexico is only after one thing, and that is the one thing he desires more than anything else, the PCW Title- hence why The Pharaoh is just a pebble on his pathway to reaching that great achievement. Priority number one is simply to become the pinnacle of PCW and be the champion. ---- A light blue mini van pulls into a car park and comes to a halt once parked. From the vehicle step out Kurt and Leroy, El Diablo's 24/7 security men. They are donning black denim jeans, leather boots, a leather jacket and black sunglasses. If it wasn't for their varying hairstyles they could be mistaken for identical twins. Once they've declared the area safe they signal for El Diablo and his wife Angela to step out of the mini van. 'Thank you! It was getting hot in there,' states Angela as she steps out from the front seat and slams the door shut. Angela is wearing a black tube top, which shows off her tanned shoulders and midriff, as well as tight black pants, and high heeled boots. Meanwhile El Diablo wears a black 'Viva Mexico' vest and baggy black denim jeans as well as some white sneakers. The four of them walk into a familiar building and take a seat at a booth. 'Ah, it's comfy,' comments Diablo while bouncing up and down a little on the cushioned seat. The four look around to see where they can order some food, and right at the front of the room they find it, as several people stand in line waiting to order. El Diablo also sees the people taking the orders. 'I don't like the look of them,' he comments, 'Their faces are greasy and they are wearing ridiculous hats. I think they're here to get me,' he continues warily. Kurt and Leroy look over to where El Diablo was looking. 'El, we're in McDonalds, and they're Japanese teenagers,' Kurt reassures the Mexican Devil. Diablo nods but still looks at them suspiciously. 'Well whatever, just keep them away from me,' Diablo says as he tries to keep calm. Angela also looks around and then back at her husband. 'Are we going to order or what? I'm hungry,' Angela says, looking pretty as always. El Diablo smiles and nods. 'Leroy, you get them in,' he orders him. Leroy stands up and looks back down at them. 'What are you all having?' Leroy asks, as he doesn't know what to order for them. The Director of Dictation sighs and looks up at his hired security. 'Surprise us! Just make sure it tastes good and isn't dog meat. Or is that the Chinese that do that? Well, I don't care, just get us some burgers,' he barks orders at Leroy. Leroy nods and walks away to the front of the room ready to order some food for them all. 'I hope he gets us some good food,' says Angela with grace. As she says that a Japanese couple walk by the booth, but are stopped by the remaining security guard Kurt. The old couple look a little frightened just by seeing the size of Kurt. 'What do you want?' he asks with no emotion in his voice whatsoever. The Japanese couple obviously don't speak English and look bemused at what Kurt just said. Kurt remains in their way and looks down at the smaller two. 'Fine, go ahead,' Kurt states as he goes to sit down. The couple quickly walk by and look back at Kurt with contempt. 'Stupid Japanese,' comments Diablo as he places an arm around Angela. Kurt coughs and looks around to the monitor the area, as he remains seated. 'I don't think anybody will be bothering us today,' Kurt quotas back. The Mexican Devil looks at Kurt with a nasty stare before looking back down at the empty table before them, except for the napkin dispenser, an ashtray and a bottle of ketchup. 'Bother me, you mean,' El Diablo says egotistically. Kurt nods. 'Of course,' he replies sarcastically, but Diablo doesn't notice this as he is too wrapped up in his self-satisfaction. 'How long can it take to get some food?' Angela asks impatiently. Diablo looks to the front of the room and cannot even see Leroy. 'Maybe he's stuck trying to figure out how much it costs in Yen,' El Diablo jokes. Angela and El Diablo laugh and so does Kurt, but with little enthusiasm. 'OK, I'm serious. I'm getting really hungry now,' complains Angela. The Director of Dictation leans down and kisses Angela to try and cheer her up. Of course as El Diablo does this and waits for the food, it is quite obvious that PCW is the only thing on his mind. He sits conjuring up ways to eliminate Pharaoh from PCW and how to win the PCW Title. It's easy to think about, but harder to make into a reality, however El Diablo knows that when he sets his mind to something he can achieve that goal. And therefore he knows that he WILL rid PCW of The Pharaoh and that he WILL become PCW Champion, sooner rather than later. El Diablo trail of thought is cut short as Leroy returns with a tray of food, which consists of four cheeseburgers, four portions of fries and four beverages. 'Ah at last,' Angela comments greedily. Leroy sits down and then they all go to start eating, all except El Diablo who just stares at the food. 'Wow, everything is smaller here in Japan,' he states as he looks at the small burger and fries in front of him. Angela smirks and bits into her food with the force of twelve sharks finding it's first meal in weeks. However, with less enthusiasm El Diablo just doesn't eat. 'What's wrong?' asks Leroy with a mouthful of burger. The Lord of Mexico sighs. 'I just have this feeling that my food is poisoned and that somewhere, someone is watching me,' he states whilst looking around nervously. Angela looks at him in worry. 'Why would you think that honey?' she asks him with concern growing in her eyes. 'Because these Japanese are crazy and I have to be alert at all times, that's why I have you two,' El D says referring to Kurt and Leroy, 'You're my eyes and ears when I can't be awake, you are my protection when I'm too weak to defend,' he continues in a rant. Kurt and Leroy smile but still try to show no emotion to those around them in this Japanese McDonalds. 'This McDonalds is corrupt,' he whispers to his three allies, 'Just like every government on Earth and most importantly, just like PCW! I don't like it here, lets just leave,' El Diablo says quietly, trying not to draw attention to himself. Angela, Kurt and Leroy pick up their remaining food and slowly exit the restaurant as El Diablo walks behind them, looking around cautiously. 'When I have to watch my back all the time, I know something is wrong with society. But first I must start small on my conquest and first on that list is Pharaoh. Goodbye Pharaoh,' The Mexican Devil says to himself as he walks out of the McDonalds. Goodbye
