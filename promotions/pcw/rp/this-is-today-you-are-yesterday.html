This is Today...You are Yesterday

Date: Today, and Yesterday...for the Future 'Time is always short for those who defy you...but for you...you shall have forever to plan their destruction...' ---Savage Tranquility, page undetermined... We return to the aftermath of the assault the Neo Void God made upon the technological compound where the Executives were keeping Cyclonica, the Divine Prophetess. The battle is over...though it wasn't much of a battle at that anyway. Same could be said bout the bouts with Pharaoh, Duke, Chase, Trigger, and El Diablo this week. Nonetheless, silence filled the core of the building deep below the building in Sub-Basement Omega. The room was dark, save the single spotlight flooding down upon the broken capsule that was once the Divine Prophetess's haven. At the edge of the circle of light, a white heel cut into the ring. Traveling up the length of her legs underneath that white silk, meeting at her hips and curving around her torso to her shoulders. Then once the face is visible... There's nothing but blank beauty... perfection... Angelica stared silently at the capsule with her dark eyes. She didn't blink once, even as stray sparks flew off around her from the severed cables. The thoughts running through her head were more than enough to keep her from ignoring such distractions such as those...like Akiyama needed to ignore distractions like the ones on this past Sunday, and this coming Sunday and Tuesday. The atmosphere around the building wasn't that productive to say the least. In Matrix's former lab, Illusion, Spice, and Knight sat around glumly looking over whatever was left from Matrix's data. Illusion sat bored at the desk with his yellow boots propped on the desk. Spice sat on the corner of the table, while Knight stood over near the entrance with his arms folded over his chest. Their faces weren't so bright either. 'This sucks.' Spice muttered glumly. 'Who sucks?' Illusion asked, looking up with a bit of interest. Spice glared back at him, showing him the ruby red nail on her middle finger. Knight sighed as he shook his head at them. 'We doth not have the time to dealeth with such immature tomfoolery. Surely we doth be in peril at the hands of our superiors.' 'Superior-sushmerior,' Illusion yawned, leaning back and tilting his yellow fedora down over his face. 'All I'm concerned with is finding those shower clips of Spice.' Just then, a wave of electricity ran through his body. He let out a surprised shriek of pain as he fell from his seat. Smoke rose from his body as he looked up, finding Reality twirling his electric nightstick in his hand. A displeased look was on his face. 'Whack your wick some other time, Illusion! We need to hurry up and track down Subject #3-9 and 3-6!' Spice giggled at Illusion's embarrassment. The yellow Zoot Suit wearing member of the Executives stood up, straightening out his suit and his hat, and nodded. 'Whatever you say, sir.' mock respect laced his voice. 'By the way, what about our bosses? I'm sure they'd like to know about this little incident that occurred recently.” Reality growled as he looked straight into Illusion’s face. “If we go out there now and search for those two, then there’s no need to report to them now is there??” Spice nodded. “Plus what do you think they would do to us if they found out that we let 3-9 not only get into the compound, but walk out with the Divine Prophetess?” Illusion grimaced at the thought. “Good point, boys and girls. Fine...let’s saddle up those ponies and ride out!!” He stood there, his hand balled up at his side with his arm pointing for the sky. Knight, Spice, and Reality stare at him blankly. The determined look on Illusion slowly faded as he lowered his arms. “That means let’s go!” The rest of them nodded, realizing what he meant as they all headed for the door. The rain poured thick like syrup that night...thick...dark...syrup. Thunder struck here and there, with the occasional lightning flashes. The small condo was lined up next to the others on the sloping street, and the lights inside were not visible. Inside, a faint reflection of light can be seen from underneath the cracks of a bedroom door. In the room, a lamp shed light upon a shapely figure lying on her bed. She was dressed in a pair of black drawstring pants and a yellow tank top. Her black hair was tied back in a ponytail on her head, but her face was shielded from vision by the book she was reading. There was a sound from the front of the house. She stood up, walking to the door of her room on bare feet. Stepping out into the hallway, she reached for a light switch and flipped the lever. A hum died down somewhere within the home, and she knew that the power had gone out. The storm seemed to pick up as she stumbled around the hallway in darkness. Something told her to go to the door. It was like a hunch, but she had that feeling that she needed to go to the front door for something very important. Making her way through the blackout, she reached her front door and swung it open. A scream escaped from her as thunder and lightning struck simultaneously, making the presence of the visitor even more ominous than it would be on a sunny day. “Excuse us, ma’am, but we need a place to hide out for a while.” Standing in her doorway, drenched to the bone, were Matrix, Cyclonica, and looming behind them the Neo Void God. The woman seemed to ignore Matrix’s request and simply stared into Akiyama. The god locked eyes with her, both of them frozen in the moment. Matrix sighed. “This is going to be a lovely evening.”
