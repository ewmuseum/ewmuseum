Settling Into Our New Role Nicely....

9 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/04/02 Ellie~!’s prologue : Okay, I’m...Like...Here to fill you in on stuff...And stuff. Right, so it is, like, Sunday night and Xavier has just SO won the PCW Continental Title...Partly thanks to yours truly...YAY ME!!! Anyway, so he now has that Title, but then he, like, TOTALLY bettered it and he now kinda owns PCW, which is hella cool because he wrestles there as well! So Tyler, Xavi, those Political guys and the one with Tourette’s...That or he just swears a lot...Have bought PCW which SO rules because now Duke doesn’t have it...Because he isn’t a nice man and he calls me slut and stuff...Back on topic, along with the O to the G and...Um...That other guy...Like, SO beat up a load of people and were all “We OWN you!” but the fans were all “Booo!” so we were all “Like, What...EVER!!!”...Anyway, that brings us up to date...Oh, wait...Something I KNOW I have forgotten...Something about a match or something?....... Our shot opens up on the 1st couple of Scientology, Gold Patron Meritorious Xavier Kannon and his preppy valet Eleanor Hall seemingly wandering aimlessly around the corridors of the Arrowhead Pond, basking in their glory. Xavier : “Hmmm...I don’t know what I like best...My PCW Continental Title or my part ownership of Primetime Championship Wrestling....” The Scientologist looks between the Title belt he won, with liberal help from his valet, just a few hours earlier and his copy of the contract handing over ownership to the conglomerate he formed with Tyler Nelson. Xavier : “....Just as I feel I am about to make a decision, I then have to factor in the fact that because I now...Sorta OWN this Federation I can say Scientology again without it being edited out...Listen...Scientology....I don’t think it could sound any better if it had come from the larynx of L Ron Hubbard himself!” Kannon is dressed smartly, wearing a Black jacket and trousers, looking like they were cut by Tyler’s personal tailor himself. Under it he has a v-neck charcoal Grey jumper and his long Auburn hair is neatly tied back, a lot neater than he usually wears it. Eleanor : “I wanna touch it! Let me touch it!!!” The Scientologist raises his eyebrow suggestively only to be met with a Not That! look back at him from his valet before he hands her the PCW Continental Title which she slings over he shoulder, patting the plate on the front and gazing at it in semi-awe. Ellie~! is wearing a White, Purple and Back halter top and a pair of faded flared jeans with Silver stars embroidered down the legs towards the pair of Red and White suede Adidas sneakers on her feet. Unlike Xavier her hair is not tied but still falls neatly into place, and avalanche of Brown with Blond highlights falling down to the small of her back. Eleanor : “Like...WOW.....You get all the pleasure of beating Jeremy Howard Black and Blue...Then Black again...Then Blue...And at the end of it they give you a really cool shiny belt! Only a company you kinda own can be THIS great!” They continue their stroll around the site of their greatest night in PCW. Far eclipsing Xavier’s first Title win on New Years Day where he beat Juan Borro for the Cruiserweight Title, even better that joining Asylum at Shang-Haied and standing toe to toe with the Russian Bear Ivan Stanislav, triumphant over the night he broke Asylum up. Ellie~! then looks away from the Title to the chuffed face of her man with an inquiring look on her face. Eleanor : “So...Like...Who do we actually own?” Her question seems to mildly shock Xavier as it appears out of the Blue, Kannon thinking Ellie~! would be happy with the belt in the same way a baby is with a dummy. Xavier : “PCW doesn’t have Slavery my little Poon Puppy.....Not even an oppressor of Duke William’s magnitude during his tyrannical reign introduced that...We don’t actually OWN anyone....” His news seems to disappoint Ellie~! who’s pale Blue eyes sink and look back to the Title belt which seems to have lost some of it’s sparkle. The corner of Xavier’s mouth then begins to form a smirk as he chuckles to himself on the inside, the faint sound of which alerts his valet and she looks back up to him. Xavier : “...But we do own their contracts, their jobs...And more importantly...Their livelihoods in our hands...It’s like playing God, but only I do it in the name of Hubbard which as you would say is...Like..SO better!” This news brings a new found enthusiasm to her faces as she bounces up and down excitedly, the perks of ownership running rampant in her head. .......Being in charge like...SO RULED....And I was all happy and, like, YAY ME and stuff, so I really wanted to take even more pleasure at someone’s expense by firing them.... Eleanor : “We...Like...SO, as I would say, have to FIRE SOMEONE! Like that man who didn’t open the door for me...Or that canteen woman who didn’t have any Tofu burgers left...Or...I GOT IT!!!...We can fire all the wrestlers so you don’t ever have to fight anyone again! I miss having Fridays of...” Although he feels he really should attempt to curb her desires, Xavier seems to relish the idea himself, an early chance to reap the benefits the money from his Snapple~! endorsement has sewn. Xavier : “Okay....You can fire ONE person, but that is it for tonight...I don’t want to go to work on Wednesday and find there is nobody left to carry my bags or park my car.” As he lays down the ground rules, Ellie~! is already planning out her firing in her admittedly quite vacant head, wondering who it is that she can fire. Xavier : “Oh, and it can’t be a wrestler though...As a Scientologist I know all about Red tape and court room battles...No commentators either...A Ref at the very most.” In her head Ellie~! crosses the names off the mental list she created...Duke, Howard, Mexi....And tries to think of someone else she can sack for the fun of it. As she ponders over who’s livelihood to threaten they continue their walk backstage, almost like a lap of honour celebrating Xavier’s Title win. Eleanor : “Well...I didn’t NEED to fire Howard, you did a pretty good job of beating him up as it is....Tyler can, like, SO easily take care of Mexi....Duke...Well, he is next on the list and look who was on it before him...The elderly of Des Moines and Krayzie...” Suddenly the switch clicks behind her pale Blue eyes and she raises a finger to exclamate it, all that is missing is the light bulb flicking on above her head. Eleanor : “I GOT IT!!! I know who I want to fire...This like, like, TOTALLY be the best firing ever...I am gonna fir-” ......Then as usual, just as me and Xavier were about to have us some harmless fun by adding one to the number of unemployed, along with saving PCW $20,000 a year of whatever....Someone had to get in our way, luckily he didn’t know who he was messing with. because, like, now Xavi owns 20% of PCW...I get to have loads more fun..... As they stroll down the corridor, Ellie~! about to reveal who’s head is on the block and the Scientologist eager to hear who’s misery they will relish in when a crew worker pushes a trolley containing ring ropes and other ring paraphernalia out into their path, causing her to stop abruptly, something she seems none to pleased about as Xavi crosses his arms and grins, having a good idea of what is about to happen. ......And of course, being the visionary he is, and with the all knowing power of Hibbert...Wait, Hubbard behind him, he said he knew what I was going to do even before I did! Wow, if Scientology does that for you I might sign up..... Eleanor : “Er...Like....EXCUSE ME!?!?!?! Would you mind pushing your stupid little trolley...You know...Somewhere NOT in front of me?” The man looks at her, confused at his strange woman who is getting in the way of his job. He looms at her for a second, wiping his hands on his Blue boiler suit before he continues to push the trolley across Xavier and Eleanor, something the prep is even more annoyed about. Eleanor : “Um...Where are you going? Through some little memory lapse you must have forgotten to apologise to me, and act which is best followed up by a minute or so of grovelling before I then inform you on whether or not YOUR tongue needs to shine MY shoes....” The man’s look of confusion looks to one of annoyance as he grunts and carries on across their path, muttering under his breath as he stops to scratch his armpit a foot away from Ellie~!’s face, causing her to turn up her nose as Xavier just watches on, wanting to see if the prediction he has made will come true. Worker : “Okay Princess...How about I do MY job and you do YOURS?” Once she works out him calling her Princess wasn’t a compliment her face sours again before a twinkle happens in her eyes as she hears the line about her doing her job. Eleanor : “Okay...Sorry Mr Worker man...You are right. You get along with your little menial task you got going on there...You know, the one they can train Apes to do! While I’ll get on with mine...” Despite her insult he is just happy to get along with his labours, sighing and tutting to himself as he walks past them and off on his way. The evil grin that Ellie~! has became synonymous with then returns and she turns her head and rasps at him in the most vicious voice she can muster. Eleanor : “Oh...And by the way...YOU’RE SO TOTALLY FIRED!!!” The man replies with a growl of WHAT?!?!?!? and turns to confront Eleanor but Xavier is on hand to slam the doors in his face and drop the bolt down, locking the man in the storage cupboard as he pounds away at the door, yelling at them. “I CAN’T BREATH! I’M CLAUSTROPHOBIC...HELP!!! HAVE A FAMILY!!!” Ellie~! grins and HIGH FIVES! Xavier, both looking pleased at their first firing as 20% owners of Primetime Championship Wrestling as they continue their little stroll. Eleanor : “Wow...I HAVE to recommend owning multi-million dollar companies to my friends, there is like SO much fun to be had by all!” The carry on strolling, happy with themselves and oblivious to the frantic banging on the bolted doors behind them. “SERIOUSLY! I CAN’T BREATHE......SOMEONE.....” They chuckle at the commotion behind them which turns into weak, sedated poundings before reaching silence much to Ellie~!’s pleasure. Xavier : “I think when the banging and complaining stops they are officially fired! Not a bad choice, seeing as you couldn’t fir the Pharaoh, Big Alan Steele of Frisco you made a pretty good firing my little Shag Squirrel!” She hands him back his Continental Title as he puts his arm around her and they walk off into the distance, relishing their final minutes in the arena that will hold fond memories for them as the shot fades to Black. “Are you sure I can’t fire just ONE more person tonight...Like a teeny weenie one...A Midget maybe?.........Huh?”
