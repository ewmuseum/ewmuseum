Learning to Fly Part 1.

15 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 - 15 - 03 Actors have the uncanny ability to be able to transform into any of there new personas they are taking on to accommodate that role. Recently the tag team of John Gargano and Tyler Preston has been taken under the wings of one Nerezza and one Gaz Green. Now to Gaz that expression is just a metaphor but Nerezza, he brings a whole new meaning to that phrase. You see, The P.W.A are not what some people would call a average team, Gaz “The Jester” Green as you can tell by his name is a jokester thru and thru while his counterpart Nerezza is a darker individual, A Vampire to be quite frank. Now don’t get me wrong, Nerezza likes to pull his share of pranks but they are in a whole other league. “The Living Nightmare” enjoys painful, heart stopping pranks full of death and destruction. Nerezza himself can sometimes become an utter nightmare right before your very eyes, even if you’ve known him for as long as Gaz has. Nerezza can strike anytime, anyplace, anyone. Tyler has taken very well to the persona of “The Jester” seeing as he is already a comedian. Some would think that the actor himself John Gargano would probably be the best to mold into “A living Nightmare” but that thought has not come to rest; John just doesn’t seem to be comfortable with the whole Vampire persona and it is very visual. A good student is always important but what is even more important is a good teacher. The young protégé will soon find that statement to be oh so true. Preston: Where are we going squire? Tyler Preston slams the door to Gaz’s newly resurrected baby, his Shelby GT 500. Both Gaz and Nerezza walk away from the car very solemnly leaving John and Tyler standing there waiting for answers which they soon will receive. Green: No worries squire! Just be patient, it will all come together in due time. Tyler shrugs his shoulders unwittingly and races along to try to catch up with his mentors. John Gargano just stands there waiting by the car watching Tyler run along as he silently speaks to himself. Gargano: If I hear that word one more time, I’m going to crack! Preston: Hurry along squire! John looks down at the ground and holds his face as he lightly begins to chuckle as he begins to try to catch up with the others rushing ahead. Green: Here we are boys! As John reaches the other 3 men he looks around to see……..absolutely nothing, the only thing that inhabits the area are a bunch of old run down buildings with broken glass and a little herd of mice occupying the local trash can. Preston: Uh… Gaz? Why are we here? What do we have to learn here or better yet what made you want to come to this hell hole? Gaz looks at the presently silent Nerezza and gives him a little look of assurance to the reply of a oh so sudden snicker from the gruesome mouth of “The living nightmare”. John Gargano and Tyler Preston both just stand there and watch this show of approval with a confused and a somewhat trusting look upon there thoughtful faces. Green: Well my young apprentice, you may be a comedian and all but I myself am the only true man to teach you the real art of the joke. Preston: Yes…But why are you teaching me here? Green: I’m not; as you can see the last place I want to train someone is in a dark abandoned alley! That is why we are not going to be staying here! John and Tyler look at each other at the same exact time with the same exact pondering look upon there face, Gaz and Nerezza just glance at each other and chuckle at the look upon there protégés face. Gargano: Then….where are we going? Green: We are not going anywhere squire! Preston and I are going somewhere, while you are going to be staying here with my good friend Nerezza! John’s jaw begins to drop as he slowly begins to glance over at Nerezza who just stands there and snickers a little. Green: You see, since you and Nerezza are teaming up on Fusion, He thought that maybe you should learn a little something extra for the match, So Tyler go get the car, we going out tonight!! Preston: Yeah! Tyler Preston runs off into the darkness to fetch the car, leaving the three men behind, one of the three his partner standing there almost stunned. As Nerezza begins to inspect the place for whatever he needs for the training, Gaz motions towards John to hunch down. Green: John…I’ve dealt with Rezz for a very long time! Now I have some very important advice for you, don’t make any fast movements, don’t carry meat in your slacks, and most important don’t at anytime, ever………. -Beep Beep- Preston: Gaz…Come on! Green: Oh Ok! Gaz Green runs off into the darkness and hops into the car as it whips off in the pace of a second leaving John Gargano standing there and that is all, just standing there, not believing what he is about to go thru. He has never been alone with Nerezza but there is always a first for everything. Nerezza: Gargano………… John turns around and looks into the cold eyes of “The Living Nightmare”
