The thin line between Demolition and Drugs

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin was revelling in yet another half-assed business ploy: Demolition. With his trusty blowtorch and gas tank in one hand and a whole bunch of TNT and C4 in another, Flaymin went to the scene of his first job. The job?Destroy a Homeless Shelter. Will Flaymin commit the dishonourable sin of demolishing a building filled with poverty-stricken, drunken hobos in exchange for a large amount of cash? [Flaymin stands outside the building with his assistant Jenna and the man (Maxamillion) who wants the building destroyed. Flaymin continues to look at the large amounts of cash and then at the building] Flaymin: Gee, I don’t know if I can do this. Maxamillion: Come on, you cannot back out now! Flaymin: I can get in some serious doo-doo if anyone finds out about this. Maxamillion: Don’t give me that shit.You’ve run over people with a tractor.Crushed the coffin of your archenemy’s brother.Blew up an entire wrestling set. And now…. You develop a conscience?Where the f*ck was your conscience when you did all those other dumbass things? Jenna: He was probably drunk at the time. Maxamillion: That’s no excuse! Flaymin, the money is on the table- Make it happen and the wealth becomes yours. Besides, if you get caught you can just plead insanity or something.You’re already the pinup boy in the Betty Ford Clinic and other rehab centres around the world.You’re set for life! Flaymin: Hmmm…. Yeah alright.I’ll do it! [Outraged, Jenna grabs Flaymin’s ear and pulls it hard] Flaymin: What was that for? Jenna: You’re going to blow up a building filled with homeless people and whores.What are you thinking? Flaymin: You’re right….We better get the whores out of there before the destruction begins. Jenna: The whores AND the homeless people. Flaymin: Sheesh, I better win a humanitarian award for this. [Flaymin rolls up the sleeves of his shirt.He then walks into the homeless shelter. Five minutes later he emerges, carrying 4 homeless bums- one on each arm and two on his back. He dumps them on the floor outside and then goes back into the building to fetch the rest.However the hobo’s follow Flaymin back into the building.Flaymin notices their advancements and tells them to turn around] Flaymin: Get back!! Hobo: I left my teeth in there!! Flaymin: You’ve got no teeth. Hobo: That’s because they’re still inside. Hobo #2: And I forgot my can collection. Flaymin: Forget about that. Hobo #2: But those cans are my children!! Flaymin: How about I help you adopt a milk bottle later on? [The hobo nods his head in approval.But before Flaymin can go back into the building, Maxamillion and Jenna approach Flaymin] Maxamillion: This is taking far too long.Just blow up the building already. Flaymin: But there are more whores and hobos inside! Maxamillion: They’ll get out once they see the fire. [Flaymin walks over to his car and pulls out all the explosives and begins to plant them all around the building. He wires up all the explosives to a central plunger situated a few metres away from the building] Jenna: Are you sure you know what you’re doing? Flaymin: Trust me. I’ve seen this done a million times. Jenna: Where? Flaymin: The Coyote does this to the Road Runner all the time!Duh! Jenna: But haven’t you ever noticed that the Coyote always fails? Flaymin: That’s because he always blows up the wrong things.This should be an easy job. [Flaymin picks up the can of gasoline and walks around the building splashing it everywhere. The thick fumes of gasoline fill the air and Flaymin covers his nose with his mouth to keep the stench at bay. He then walks over to the main plunger connected to the explosives and wiring] Flaymin: It’s Showtime!! Maxamillion: This will be the easiest $2 million dollars you’ve ever made. [Flaymin gently pushes Jenna and Maxamillion backwards] Flaymin: You better stand back, there is going to be flying debris everywhere. [Flaymin then walks over to the plunger once again and looks at the building for the final time.He then puts both hands on the handle of the giant plunger and slowly pushes down, expecting an instant explosion… However, nothing explodes. Flaymin raises an eyebrow and then decides to push down the handle on the plunger one more time. Once again… no explosion] Flaymin: What the hell??? [Maxamillion and Jenna walk back over to Flaymin] Maxamillion: Where is the explosion??You think I’m paying you to screw everything up?? Jenna: That’s what PCW does… Flaymin: Shut up woman. Sorry Max, I don’t know what went wrong. [Flaymin, Jenna and Maxamillion walk over to the building to expect all the explosives in an attempt to figure out why the detonation failed. Maxamillion suddenly notices a large hole in one of the packages of TNT] Maxamillion: Why is there a puncture in that TNT pack? [Flaymin looks at the gap in the TNT and frowns.He picks up the explosive and looks into the hole] Flaymin: Someone has taken out all the explosive powder! Maxamillion: Why would anyone take the explosive powder? [Jenna steps forward and looks at the TNT] Jenna: Can I see that? [Jenna takes the TNT and looks at the hole] Flaymin: Do you know anything about the missing powder? Jenna: I …err….. kinda snorted it….. Flaymin: WHAT???? Maxamillion: You snorted the explosive powder??? Jenna: I thought it was cocaine!! Flaymin: How could you confuse cocaine with explosive powder?? Jenna: They’re both white substances! Flaymin: So is sugar!! Jenna: I’ve never done sugar… but salt was a bitch.It burnt the roof of my nose! [Flaymin shakes his head in disbelief] Maxamillion: Hold on a sec.She only snorted one pack of TNT. The rest of the explosives should have detonated anyway. Jenna: Well, about that…. Flaymin: No!!Don’t tell me you snorted all that too!?!? Jenna: I kinda sold all the other powder to my pornstar friends. Maxamillion: And they bought it?? Jenna: In five seconds flat. Flaymin: Couldn’t they tell the difference? Jenna: Nope. Maxamillion: So all this time I’ve been wasting my time trying to blow up buildings when I could have been selling gunpowder to morons! I’ll see you guys later! Flaymin: What about the building?? Maxamillion: Let the hobos have it.I need to start smuggling gunpowder! [Dejected, Flaymin hangs his head in shame and walks back to his car] Flaymin: Out of all the women in the world, I had to pick the one who snorts explosive powder…. [Scene fades to black]
