Horus' Great Idea...

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/03/02 Horus’ Great Idea… The scene opens up in the kitchen off the Palace, Anaheim, California. Pharaoh, Horus and Isiy all sit around the kitchens huge table and it appears that a vast map is spread over its surface, seemingly covering the table completely. The words of the three cannot be clearly heard at present, but we can see who is speaking, that would be Horus, the mentor and teacher of The Pharaoh. The old man sits on a small wooden stool with a smile on his aged face, making strange hand movements to which Pharaoh looks on sarcastically and Isiy seems to just ignore. Suddenly as we grow closer we can clearly make out the Gods words and soon we understand what he speaks of… Horus: And that’s when we hit them with everything we’ve got! Horus stops, and smiles looking for the reactions of his pupil and Isiy. At length Pharaoh speaks. The Pharaoh: Well…Horus… Pharaoh smiles, but a trait of sarcasm is visible in his expression. The Pharaoh: That’s all good and proper, but how is holding up a small restaurant and then feeding them a cuisine of the best Egyptian food going to make me any popular with the PCW crowd? Horus: Think about it, Titu, by serving up a free meal of the richest and by far the finest food in all of Egypt then surly you’ll get praise! Pharaoh smiles and begins to ponder the possible situations that could follow serving up Egyptian food to a crowd of people being held at gunpoint. The Pharaoh: Well are the guns really necessary? Horus nods his head as if to say, “Yes”. Horus: Of course they are! Suddenly Isiy interrupts. Isiy: But if Titu gets caught holding a large group of people, who are simply minding their own business and eating their lunches at gunpoint and then forcing them to eat food that they have never even seen the likes of before then he could get sent down for a long time! Isiy pauses, thinking over her last words. Isiy: Or even worse, he could get sent to an asylum! A place for the mentally insane! Horus: But surly they wouldn’t do that to a God?! The Pharaoh: Lets get one thing straight, Horus; nobody on earth believes that I’m a God! Horus shrugs. Horus: Then why would they send you to a mental institute? The cameras focus on Pharaoh’s odd clothing, the infamous white silk loincloth, the golden ceremonial bangles and jewellery, and the great blue and gold Egyptian crown sitting on the worktop behind the three. Isiy pulls an ironic face and then looks at Horus with a frown.
