Breaking the Law

31 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/31/02 'No officer I don't think anything's the matter with me.' Off the side of a busy highway, a demon sits behind the wheel of a rusted out Chevrolet. Out his window, a policeman stands and looks at him. 'I do.....I really do,' he states. Clyde looks up, raising an eyebrow to the man. 'I fail to see what the problem is.' 'Look in the mirror,' the officer replies blankly. 'For what, I....' The burned man stops short as he notices his reflection. His eyes, or rather what used to be his eyes are lined with sharp, tiny teeth. Behind them faint bits of flame dance, their source unknown. 'Oh yeah, that.' 'Sir, are you under some kind of influence?', the police officer asks. 'Because frankly I've NEVER in my life seen anything like that.' Clyde blinks his malformed eyes, looking innocent. 'Whatever do you mean?' 'Goddammit if you hadn't been trying to scare that lousy kid this would never have happened!' Both demon and officer look to the passenger side, to see an eight year old girl fuming. 'Little kid?', asks the policeman. 'Oh it's not important,' says Clyde impatiently to the man. 'Just having some fun on Halloween.' 'Most fun on Halloween doesn't involve driving around without eyes apparently in your skull, sir,' he answers tiredly. 'And just how the hell were you able to drive like that anyway?' 'Don't ask silly questions,' shrieks Bethany. 'We're late for Halloween.....the one night of the year when we usually can get AWAY with looking like a bunch of freaks! Except when YOU'RE involved of course!' 'Well, I can't very well let you drive around like THAT, can I?' 'Like what?', asks Clyde. 'I still fail to see what the problem is.' 'You don't have EYES, sir.' 'Dammit!', squeals Bethany while kicking her feet. 'Why didn't you just put your lousy eyes back in Clyde? You just HAD to show off didn't you?' 'Bah!' 'Sir can I see your license?' Mumbling threats of death and destruction, the burned man reaches into his pocket. Producing a small card, he hands it to the cop. 'Clyde.' Slowly the man looks at the driver, then at the card, then at the driver again. 'There's no last name on this card, Mr. Clyde.' 'I don't HAVE a last name!', bellows the demon. 'Why must I be persecuted for this by everyone!?' Bethany buries her head in her hands, sighing deeply as the officer looks on. 'Look I think I'm gonna have to take you in.' 'WHAT!?', yells the little girl. 'But we didn't do anything!' 'I'm pretty sure driving without eyes is some kind of offense,' the man states. 'And besides that I DID find you in the backseat gnawing on a slab of raw meat, miss.' 'So? Is that a crime either?' 'It is when it's still attached to half of a homeless person.' 'Point.' 'Look, just allow us to go,' mumbles the burned man. 'We have places to go, people to slaughter and as the girl said, this is usually the one night of the year we CAN get away with it.' 'People to slaughter?', asks the officer with a raised eyebrow. '......never mind that last part.' And as Bethany slaps her forehead, our police officer reaches to his side. 'Alright....can I ask you both to step out of the car?' Clyde briefly slams his head off the steering wheel, then opens the door. He marches around the cop, who writes into a small pad. 'Alright Mr. Clyde, I'm going to have to write you up for.....driving without eyeballs.' 'That CAN'T be a real crime....' The burned man ceases as another vehicle slowly heads past on the road. From out the window pops a familiar face, that of the boy he had seen previously. 'Ha ha!', chuckles the child as he rolls past. 'Catch you later freakboy!' Clyde's eyes widen in astonishment as he points. However the policeman never looks up from his writing. 'What.' 'There was a 13 year old boy driving a car around!' The man looks up tirely at the demon. 'And you really expect me to believe that.' 'You believe I really have no eyeballs!' 'Well, I'm LOOKING at you and you have no eyeballs sir.' 'True. But still, this is a miscarriage of justice! I demand vengeance!' 'Vengeance, shmengence. We'll talk about it downtown.' 'I'll KILLLLLLLLL YOU!!!!!!!!' Abruptly Bethany leaps through the air, shrieking at the top of her lungs. Just before impact though the cop throws himself on the ground, causing her to sail straight over his head and nail the grass with a thud. 'God DAMN it! That does it, now you're both REALLY coming with me!' Bethany clutches her back in pain, sitting up as Clyde gives her a dirty look. 'You're getting slow in your old age,' he mocks with a slight smirk. 'I've been eight for the past fifty years, old age my ass!' The demon begins to say something else, but the clicking of a gun cuts him off. 'I said you're coming with me,' intones the police officer. Clyde slowly raises his hands, turning his head slightly. He affixes the cop with a dark stare. 'Oh what are you going to do, shoot me? Go right ahead. I DARE you....' A small 'pop' rings out as the gun goes off. A bullet strikes Clyde right above his eyebrow, causing him to hit the dirt. 'Owwwwwwwww!' Moaning, the demon rolls about upon the ground. Then, much to the cop's astonishment he sits up again, staring at him as blood pours into his non-eyes. 'You SHOT me, you little prick!' 'I....I.....,' stammers the officer. Clyde stands, dusting himself off and wiping blood from his vision. 'DIE!!!!!!!' Suddenly from behind leaps Bethany again, tackling the policeman to the ground. She begins bouncing his head off the ground, over and over again as the burned man shakily takes his gun away and throws it into the nearby woods. 'Bethany, can we go?' 'DIEDIEDIEDIEDIEDIEDIE!', the little girl shrieks as she continues to thrash the officer. 'BETHANY!' 'What!?' 'He's dead, let's go! We might yet actually have some FUN this Halloween.' 'Feh.' Leaving the bleeding officer, Bethany stands and climbs back into the car. She is soon followed by Clyde, who looks into the mirror and observes the hole in his head. 'That'll NEVER heal!', groans the monster as he picks at the wound with his finger. 'Not if you keep playing with it! Let's go already!' Shaking his head, our demon stomps the gas pedal and speeds off, leaving the body of an officer to fester in the dirt as we mercifully fade to black.......
