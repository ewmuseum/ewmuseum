Thicker than water...

20 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dec 20, 2002 The bolt on the door turned with an audible click and the door to the apartment swung open freely, revealing a stale scent to the air inside. It'd been a week and a half since he'd seen the two room flat in Pheonix and a fine coating of dust had settled over the contents of the apartment. He'd need to do a quick clean job on it, or hire out a cleaning service soon. His friends would be over the week of New Year, to live it up in the local clubs on New Years Eve, then camp out till the Fiesta Bowl that would be played a few miles away in Tempe. 'Would you move it? These bags are getting heavy, must be all that gold in there.' The voice of his uncle, Joseph Doyle, echoed in the hallway behind him as he stepped quickly into the apartment and dumped the package he'd recieved in the mail on the only decent piece of furniture in the house; a rosewood step tansu that he'd recieved from Alexander Miyagawa when he'd worked in Japan. 'Uncle Joey, that wouldn't be a bit of sarcasm I detect there in your voice, would it?' Kevin Sandusky turned to face his uncle, who was grinning like that cat that ate the canary. Joe set the bags down, being especially careful with the belt bag that contained the RUSH Syndicate Title. 'Nah, I'm actually more interested in who sent you that big of a package from Japan' 'Oh, that..' Kevin shoved his sunglasses up into his head, holding back the sandy blonde hair that had been covering his face as he picked up the parcel and started to tear the tape that sealed it closed. 'I had Atagi-san forward all the mail from my flat in Yokohama.' 'Oh goody, any decent anime-porn?' 'Not likely, the neighbor kids usually steal that stuff from the box in the lobby before Atagi could pick it up.' With one final tug, the package ripped open spilling letters across the floor. Most were covered with kanji, but some were addressed in english. Kevin stooped to pick one of the 'english' ones up. 'Hey, look, Akiko wrote me...' A twinkle flashed in the eyes of Joseph Doyle, but only briefly. But not so brief that Kevin didn't notice. Akiko was a sore subject with Uncle Joey, ever since she had married Cianan and moved back to Japan. Kevin quickly set the letter aside and moved onto another one written in english. A slightly larger envelope this time, with a corporate logo emblazoned across the top. A logo that read 'PURE'. Kevin pointed to it increduously. 'Would you believe that shit. They bury me in favor of that asshole Cianan and that slackass Kensu, then have the nerve to send me promotional materials.' 'Don't burn all your bridges at once kid. I've been there, it ain't worth it.' Uncle Joey had a knowing look in his eyes as he spoke. Years ago he'd make mistakes that had virtually blacklisted him from working in the far east ever again. The blow to his career had been disasterous. 'Why don't you take it? I'll just throw it away.' 'I'll do that, you just might need it later. Especially if your 'boss' keeps double booking you for Pay Per Views like he has. It'd be nice to have options should you have to turn in your walking papers.' Kevin mulled it over for a second, his blue eyes going to the color of grey clouds in a near instant. 'I don't care what they do in the Front Office as long as they let me wrestle my way when the time comes. No more walk-through clusters or gimmick spotfests. Hell, they even gave me a tuner match for the PPV against that Amazonian Pygmy Douchebag on Friday.' 'Ace?' 'No, I guess he's more of a GOTH Amazonian Pygmy Douchebag. Either way, he's a tuner match. Should be a good example for those gold-lusting slugs in the match at Decimation. They wouldn't know an inzugiri from a pescudo if their masks depended on it.' Both keepers of the mask of Ebola chuckled at bit at that thought. The unmasking of those who couldn't uphold the traditions was an endearing long term goal that always lay at the back of their minds. Kevin shifted his weight nervously from foot to foot as each second passed, become more and more tense before breaking the silence with a somewhat forced, yet amiable air. 'Well Uncle Joey, thanks for picking me up at Sky Harbor, but you better get back home so I can start cleaning up around here.' 'You're probably right. Traffic around Arizona Mills is going to be hellish today. Just do me a favor.' 'Anything. Name it.' Joey grinned a bit as he reached for the door to leave. 'Call your mother, she'd never forgive me if I didn't tell you that.' Kevin broke into a wide grin. 'I will Uncle Joey, I will.' And with that said the door closed and Kevin was alone. He waited patiently to the count of 30 before he locked the deadbolt and turned to the package that had been sent by 'Akiko'. This was the real reason he had ushered Uncle Joey away. At any given second he could have recognized the handwriting as not Akiko's, but as that of her husband Cianan. Kevin ripped the envelope open and emptied the contents out into his hand. Two small vials and an index card with a complex molecular equation on it. Anxiously he looked from the vials to the black leather case that held the RUSH Syndicate title and back again as he whispered to himself. 'Just in case...' Slowly he turned over the index card. Embossed on the front in gold relief were two words in large bold text. Noir Redux.....
