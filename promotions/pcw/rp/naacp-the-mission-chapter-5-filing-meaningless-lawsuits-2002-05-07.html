NAACP-The Mission [Chapter 5. Filing Meaningless Lawsuits.

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/07/02 OOC- I've decided to not count the RPs that I did before last Thursday as in regards to the PPV. That's why it's a smaller number than what I've been posting. I posted those too early I think. Anyways, enjoy. 'WHAT THE HELL IS THIS?' The scene opens up in Dallas, Texas, atop the large towers of the NAACP. A shot of Jordan X Shabazz sitting at a table stacked with papers, chocolate bar wrappers, soda cans, old coffee cups, and even a few Playboys. Jordan is reading a magazine, but it isn’t a Playboy, it’s People Magazine. But this isn’t any regular People Magazine, by God; this is the 50 most beautiful People’s magazines. Jordan’s feet are propped up on the table as he quickly, and frantically flips through the pages of the magazine. Jordan X Shabazz: 'Denzel Washington, Halle Berry, Josh Harnett… WHERE IS JORDAN COPELAND?' Suddenly there’s a knock on the door. Jordan X Shabazz: 'Who is it God damn it?' 'It’s me… Chester.' Chester Charles, Jordan’s intelligent, yet lacking in self esteem lackey. Jordan knows that what he’s doing to Chester is a form of slavery, he pays him about $5.00, even though that’s below the standard of Minimum wage, but Jordan knows that Chester won’t rebel. Chester wants and needs this job. The high paced business of computer technology has left him without a job after he failed to crack the code that possibly would have led the CIA and the government of the Untied States, to some suspected terrorists. Jordan marveled at the way he treated Chester, for now he could pay back the crackas that did this to his family over a hundred years ago. Jordan X Shabazz: 'Come in if you must, I hope my coffee is just like I asked you for it.' Chester Charles comes into the office holding two large, scolding hot coffee cups, plus a large box of donuts. One would think Jordan was a cop. Jordan was growing impatient with Chester as Chester neared his desk. Chester was just a few feet from making it to the table when… SHABAMM~! Down goes Chester to the ground. He tripped on a donut that had fallen on the ground. He was on the ground now, scolding hot coffee piercing his skin, jelly donuts and chocolate donuts adorning his white dress shirt and black dress pants. Chester Charles: “AHHHHHHHHHHHHH IT BURNS!” Jordan X Shabazz comes around his desk with a look of anger on his face. Jordan X Shabazz: “YOU IDIOT!” Jordan smacks Chester Charles in the back of the head. Jordan X Shabazz: 'Listen here you stupid white bitch, I want you to get up, and walk your ass back down them stairs, and get ME MY FREAKIN’ COFFEE AND DONUTS DAMN IT! AND YOU BETTER GET JAKE UP HERE TOO!' Jake Jackson, Jordan's professional wrestling advisor and his legal advisor. The two men have quite a storied history, which you can learn about if you read Chapter 3 of this series. Chester got up, still smarting from the heat of the coffee, and very much in pain. He opened the door and began the 21-floor trek to the ground floor. Jordan sat back in his chair feeling like somebody who abuses power like a jackass.... Xavier Kannon, Tyler Nelson.... Jesse Jackson. But he was still angry. Every year People magazine releases their 50 most beautiful list, all the major stars on the Hollywood scene are on there. Hell, last year I believe The Rock was featured... but this year, no JORDAN X Shabazz, what, are they blind? Jordan thought to himself, there's no way they would purposely leave a man of his stature of the list. Hell, Diane Sawyer's on the list... DIANE FREAKIN' SAWYER! Good God... No way. Jordan picks up the magazine and holds it within inches of his face. He can't believe what he sees. Sharon Osborne. Jordan X Shabazz: 'Good RUSH MASON ALMIGHTY! WHAT IS THIS? THIS IS PURE AND UNADULTERATED RACISM! IF I WAS WHITE, WITH MY BEAUTIFUL FACE, I BET YOU I'D BE IN THIS MAGAZINE GOD DAMN IT!' Jordan becomes quiet. He realizes how insane he's acting. He's screaming aloud to himself. Arguing a point to nobody. Jordan laughs aloud to himself, thank Rush Mason nobody heard him right? Wrong. The door busts open as an attractive looking young Caucasian woman comes in. She has shoulder length blond hair and some beautiful blue eyes. She wears a black business suit with a mini skirt. She looks around the room for the phantom person that Jordan was talking to. Woman: 'Mr. Copeland, are you okay?' Jordan X Shabazz: 'Yes damn it, just leave me be, I want to be alone with myself.' Woman: (Picking up a playboy on the Jordan's desk) 'Ohhh... I see. Couldn't wait to get home and do it? It's okay, I won't tell the boss.' Jordan doesn't say anything. He just gives the woman an icy cold stare. He doesn't know who she is, and he doesn't know why she's all up in his business. Jordan X Shabazz: 'You can leave now...' Woman: 'Why should I? I own this place...' 'WHAT?!' The shock on Jordan's face is obvious. He didn't actually know whom the owner of the large downtown Dallas building was, and quite frankly, he didn't care. But now he did, not only had the boss caught him making a complete ass out of himself by screaming aloud in his room, but also he had made the boss think that all he does is jack off and watch television. Jordan X Shabazz: 'Heh, sorry about this small misunderstanding. It's just that I'm a professional wrestler in the Primetime Championship Wrestling organization, and I've got this big Pay Per.... Woman: 'Um... I don't care.' Jordan X Shabazz: 'Good 'cause I was tired of making small talk with you.' Jordan stifles in a small laugh, obviously faked, as the door finally opens. Chester Charles finally makes it back upstairs carrying Jordan's drinks and his donuts. At his side is Jordan X Shabazz's current advisor in the ring and out of it, Jake Jackson. The woman who owns the entire building, Sarah Walker, leaves the room to the three men. The tension is obvious. Jordan shoots Chester a dirty look as he opens up his box of donuts. Jordan X Shabazz: 'You can get the hell out of here now.' Chester Charles, the kid with no self esteem, therefore no guts to stand up to a 5'11, 228 pound professional wrestler, who's been trained in the art of ass kicking. Now alone in the room are Jordan X Shabazz and Jake Jackson. Jordan holds up his copy of People Magazine to Jake Jackson. Jordan X Shabazz: 'Jake, do you know what this is?' Jake Jackson: 'By reading the title I would assume it's People magazine's 50 most beautiful people magazine. But I haven't exactly found the time to look through it yet.' Jordan X Shabazz: 'Yeah, well, Jake, you know I'm a good looking brotha right?' Jake Jackson looks around the room, making sure that nobody else is around, and that nobody can hear what he's about to say. Jake Jackson: 'I really rather not answer this question, but because I'm your friend... I'd see your good looking enough to be featured in this magazine.' Jake Jackson picks up the magazine to show Jordan that he agrees with what Jordan is saying. Jordan gets a serious look in his eyes as Jake Jackson shuffles his Armani suit. Jordan X Shabazz: 'Well, you know how us black people that work in this company love to sue people for things such as this? And even better, you're my lawyer, so I bet you'll support me in this lawsuit.' Jake Jackson gulps, knowing that when you get into something that Jordan thinks involves race, he will stop at nothing until he wins. Jake Jackson: 'Ah Jordan, I'm not sure I want to get embroiled in yet another legal battle, that's why I came here to help you with wrestling. The legal game is getting tiring, putting in 60-hour weeks, sleeping very little. Drinking like a pig... That stuff is what I need to stop. I'm just here to re....' Jordan interrupts his long time friend and advisor. Jordan X Shabazz: 'Great, time to take down some racist crackas.' The scene fades to black as we melt into yet another scene. ************************************************ The scene reopens as the hot Dallas, Texas sun beats down upon the people that walk the streets. Many homeless men are standing outside panhandling, trying to make a buck without working for it.... We now see a rented Toyota Camry pull up to the small courthouse across the street from the not so world famous, 'Shoney's bar and grill.' Out of the brand spanking' new, 2003 edition, of this red Toyota Camry, we see Jake Jackson, and Jordan X Shabazz. Jordan dressed in a baby blue suit, and Jake Jackson dressed in a turd brown suit. Both men walk hastily into the small courthouse. We follow them. Inside the courthouse we see a place that is abuzz with excitement, for some reason it appears as if everybody in this small courthouse has somewhere to go, and people to see. Jordan and Jake walk towards the front of the courthouse, where a receptionist types hurriedly away at her computer. Jake puts some papers on the table as she quickly stops and skims them. She looks up to see a smiling Jake Jackson and Jordan X Shabazz. She immediately runs towards the Judge's tiny chamber where she shows him the case that he might potentially be the judge for. The judge reads the topic of the lawsuit and he stands up. Judge: 'Where are the people that filed this lawsuit?' Receptionist: 'They are waiting at the front desk. One white male and one black male.' Judge: 'Here, go make copies of this, I think I'll read this over tonight, and if it goes to trial, then I'll make sure I'm the Judge.' In case you didn't notice, this Judge has for some reason desperately wants to be involved in this case. Weird huh? The receptionist walks over to the nice photocopier, and she makes copies of the stack of papers that is the lawsuit. In fact, there's not one lawsuit, there's THREE! One against the NHL for not having enough African American hockey players, one against People Magazine for not featuring Jordan in their 50 Most beautiful people list, and last but not least, perhaps the most preposterous of the three lawsuits, Jordan is filing a lawsuit against the state of Texas for their yearly TAKS test. Why are they suing you ask? Because on the TAKS they ask 'Real-Life' questions to connect with the students, and apparently, Jordan didn't think they were using names that were 'Black-Enough' for the children to pass. That's right, you heard me. The receptionist finishes the copies of the thick files and she quickly runs towards the door. She walks out to see Jordan X Shabazz and Jake Jackson waiting impatiently, Jake had called the media to make sure that this lawsuit got some damned publicity, and they had already gathered outside of the courthouse. Photographers, reporters, news reporters, newspaper Journalists, there are all there. After receiving his copy of the lawsuit, Jake Jackson leads Jordan X Shabazz outside and into a sea of craziness. There is media everywhere. Somehow, Jake Jackson grabs a microphone from one of the numerous microphones in the crowd, and he explains the lawsuits, and why they are happening. Jake Jackson: 'My client, professional wrestler, Jordan X Shabazz, has decided to sue the following companies for racism.' The crowd gasps in anticipation of the announcement. Jake Jackson: 'The first one on the list, *pause* the Texas School Board Society. The crowd gasps as a few reporters shout out some questions at Jordan and Jake, but they answer none. Jake Jackson: 'Next on the list, People Magazine for snubbing Jordan in a fit of racism.' Same as before, gasps followed by questions. Jake Jackson: 'And finally, the National Hockey League, for not encouraging blacks into the sport.' Jackson nods to the crowd. Jake Jackson: 'Jury selections begin tomorrow and we should have a trial set by the weekend.' More shouting from the crowd. Jake Jackson waves his hand in the air to the media. Pictures flash and cameras roll, as Jordan X Shabazz and Jake Jackson walk towards their car. Jordan leans in towards his manager Jake Jackson and he whispers. Jordan X Shabazz: 'Wait until Mark Makonie gets a hold of this, he'll be ripe and easy pickings at the Pay Per View. He'll think my mind is completely somewhere else.' Jake Jackson: (Getting in to the car, just like Jordan.) 'Perhaps, but that's in the future Jordan. Right now we've got to concentrate on Makonie, and Politically Incorrect on Friday.' Jordan X Shabazz: 'WHAT?! I'm facing three men be myself?' Jake Jackson starts up the car as they pull away from the madness that is the courtroom. Jake Jackson: 'No, you've got partners.' Jordan hears this news and immediately cheers up. Jordan X Shabazz: 'Really? Who is it? Duke Williams and Rush Mason, Jon Kano and Jason Core?' Jake Jackson's realizes how Jordan is going to react when he finds out he isn't teaming with his fellow Malice Crew members OR a friend of the Malice Crew in Jon Kano. But he tells him anyways. Jake Jackson: 'Even better, you're teaming with the Heartbreakers, future PCW tag team champions.' Jordan screams as the scene fades to black.
