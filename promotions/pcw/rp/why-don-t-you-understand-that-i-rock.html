Why Don't You Understand That I Rock?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: By The Rockingest Referee '...happiness is the highest good, being a realization and perfect practice of virtue, which some can attain, while others have little or none of it...' -Aristotle “This is the happiest day of my life…” This first voice sounds like the voice of a woman, no older than thirty and sounding like she is very convinced that this day is in fact the greatest day that she’s ever been blessed with. “If you’re so damn happy, then why the hell are you always whining?!” This voice unfortunately belongs to a man who is having one of the happier days of his life. However, for this man to be happy, it usually means the unnecessary pain and suffering of another human being. This man is the PCW Extreme Champion, Rush Mason. “Wow…I’ve never thought how truly horrendous it would be to be not me.” Yes, that is the sort of man who represents almost everything that is despised by a normal human being. Rush is sitting on a fairly nice leather couch watching a forty-nine inch TV. He’s got no shirt on, but has a few bandages on his head, which is also covered by a Burberry visor. He’s wearing baby blue North Carolina basketball shorts as he vegges out on the couch. Rush: T, what’re you doing? This refers to Thug roC, whose head pops out of the kitchen and up into the camera’s view as Rush calls for him. The man responds while moving back into the kitchen. Thug: Just hold up, I’m making myself a pre-victory chicken sandwich. Rush shakes his head as he hears Thug respond to him. The man who saved his Extreme Title once again will have a shot to save Rush from facing a man who he deep down fears, Kramer Wild Wing. Rush: Chicken? Do you really want to be eating something that is endorsed by an Injun? T roC pokes his head out with a grin as he responds to the Voodoo Villain. Thug: Hey, that’s good chicken, man. Rush just shakes his head again, and turns back to the TV, which is currently showing some sort of soap opera, which is apparent because of the cheesy music and terribly lit set that is somehow supposed to represent pain and sadness. No, we never really understood it either. Rush: Now T, I understand that these people have hard lives, but is it really that hard to not be as superior and intelligent as me? There is no response from the currently chicken-yearning friend of Rush’s, who just continues to watch the soap opera, as the stars say something about their lost blind son being found on a deserted island. Rush: C’mon, this stuff is written worse than a Mike Frampton promo. It’s cheesy, not entertaining in the least, and the only way it could be worse would be to actually put Frampton’s tiny brain ahead of production. Yet more sappy entertainment pours out of the television, as Rush, no longer able to take the torture, switches the channel, only to find himself stuck on another channel of soap entertainment. Rush: Arrgh, I’ve delivered an albinoization to that pervert Duzza, and this is my reward? Hours of TV that’s more out in space and without sense than Tom Lexian’s rambling?! I would scream, but I might disturb the silent chicken maker *raises his voice* who is obviously up to something in there! This time, Thug pokes his head out again, grinning widely. He pulls his head back in yet again before responding to Rush, who barely notices him moving about in the kitchen. Thug roC: Chill man, I’m just making a sandwich, nothing to worry about. Rush shakes his head again, thinking of plenty of things that T roC could be doing that could be bad. He replies with a heavy tone of sarcasm back to his fellow Uprising member. Rush: Actually, there are about a hundred things that I can worry about, but I’m not going to get up from this absolutely wonderful entertainment to bother and check out what you’re doing. Turning back towards the TV and the “wonderful entertainment,” Rush looks at the screen with very little enthusiasm towards the soap. Rush: These people’s lives are terrible, and all because they’re not me. It’s a sad story, but does it need to be on twelve different stations. Rush’s observation is of course wrong, but he continues to spout random thoughts while paying little attention to the passionate kissing going on between two soap stars. Rush: These people kissing are even making me feel down, but I suppose that’s just because I totally rock. Rush’s ego has reached T roC’s ears in the kitchen, just as the man walks out of the room with a nice looking chicken sandwich on toasted wheat bread. Rush: Well, it took you long enough. During your sandwich escapade, you missed me explaining why I’m better than the common hicks who pay money to watch me. Thug lifts an eyebrow at Rush’s explanation, and responds with his own version of the story. Thug: So you were watching these white people make out and still you felt compelled to talk about how great you are? Rush thinks for a moment, and then nods to the other man. Rush: Yeah, that’s basically it. Although there’s lots of reasons, although I suppose that your amazing chicken sandwich can’t wait while you listen to them. Thug confirms this by not bothering to listen to Rush and chowing down on the chicken sandwich, as Rush just sighs and walks into the kitchen, only to hear a loud obnoxious screeching sound. Rush: What in the name of Bishnu in a can is this shit?! Rush is referring to bones, feathers, and blood that is stained all over the floor. T roC doesn’t even look up from his sandwich and nonchalantly responds. Thug: Oh yeah, I found a chicken in that cage in that shrine thingy, so I made a chicken sandwich. Rush looks over at the sandwich eating Thug roC and shakes his head, while T roC pays no attention. Rush speaks in a low tone as he goes to clean up the mess. Rush: It’s tough enough to find a sacrificial chicken…but now there are predators in my home. Something on the TV mutters something about love and irony, but Rush just mutters under his breath. Rush: Yeah, those common morons think they’ve got it hard. Think about how hard it is to be this much fucking better than them. And thus ends Rush’s egotistical ranting for one day as the scene, with Rush sighing deeply, fades to black…
