The Sage of A Champion: The Interlude Part 5

16 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-16-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!The Interlude Part 5” Chronicles!Chronicles!Chronicles! Chapter 2: Metal Armor? :: The scene picks back up where Commander Fuller and General Powers were in the middle of talking about bribing BladeD into doing this job for the world because they were debating that BladeD wouldn’t want to do the job if he had to risk his life, but he is only 14-year-olds, so the commander said he would be easy to bribe. They started thinking of things to use to bribe him, such as; becoming a hero, money, or something around that area. Anyway, back to the scene. :: Commander Fuller: There are many different ways to bribe a 14-year-old. General Powers: But what about his parents? I mean what if they don’t want him to go into battle? I don’t think there is any amount of money that would equal up to their son. Commander Fuller: You have a point there general, but we are going to just have to find a way to talk his parents into letting him go into war for us. I am not sure yet on the details of it all, but it will have to happen. If we have to take him by force, then we will have to be mean people and take him by force. We can do that because we have the power to do that and I hope it doesn’t come down to that. We are just going to have to explain to his parents how important it is that he goes into war with us and just hope they will understand. If they don’t, then alternate actions will take place. General Powers: You have been attacking very cold hearted ever since we have been learning about Metal Armor and I am not liking this ruthless side of you Commander. Commander Fuller: You may not like, but you are going to have to live with it for the time being because I am not doing it just because I want to general. I am doing it in the matter of keeping everyone alive and well in the U.S. It is for all of our benefits that I go into this “ruthless” state. General Powers: … Read on I have been told that once the wall(s) is knocked out Russia will either tell the U.S. to hand over the money right then and there or they will blow up the U.S. without hesitation. So basically at that point it will be do or die for the U.S. Once Metal Armor is set to go off there is no backing out. There isn’t going to be any kind of off button or anything of that sort. The only way to make Metal Armor stop what it is doing is by destroying it. Point blank. Which it takes a lot to destroy the thing, so I would hate to be picked as the country that would have to make the attempt of destroying Metal Armor. You are a better chance of winning the lottery three times in a row, then stopping Metal Armor. That is what statistics have shown. They compared U.S. statistics when coming up with the chance of stopping Metal Armor dead in its tracks, so that is how you know you have a better chance of winning the lottery three times. I hope that makes sense. Sometimes I am a little bit confusing, but it is of your best luck to just stop Metal Armor while you can and just hand over the money. That is all Russia wants. If you give them your money they will put an end to all of this. If you choose to fight fire with fire you will lose horribly. You will “get owned” by Russia and it’s alliance. That sounds a little harsh, but the truth is like that sometimes. General Powers: ……… Commander Fuller: Yea. I know. Speechless. I am almost speechless myself, but this is an issue that the president and congress are going to have to look over. General Powers: But remember. This person requested we don’t show the letter to the president or anyone for that matter. Commander Fuller: Well, we are just going to have to show them this part of the letter and if they ask for it we will tell them that we don’t have it with us. General Powers: What if they ask for us to go and get it? Commander Fuller: That is a good question and I don’t have a good answer to go with it, but we do need to try and respect his request. If not, we are just going to have to do what we have to do because we can’t let this stop us from saving the world. General Powers: Well, maybe we should just give them the money and not worry about BladeD like this person said. Commander Fuller: That would be a good idea, but the only problem is I wouldn’t trust that theory. They may have been what this person would told, but they would probably take the money and kill us all off anyway. We couldn’t do a contract because a contract wouldn’t stop them from setting off Metal Armor. If they destroy us then the contract would go invalid anyways, so I mean we can’t rely on a contract to save us. Just think about it. General Powers: I guess you are right. They are probably that ruthless and this point. Commander Fuller: But do you blame them? General Powers: I can’t say that I do. Because if I were in their situation I would feel the same way right now, maybe even worse knowing me.Commander Fuller: And see. To defeat the enemy you have to think like the enemy. That is why I wouldn’t try that handing over the cash part. They are probably just going to do that so they can just have to the money, but either way they are going to want to blow us to pieces. If they got the money they could possibly pay back China a bit or just have the money for themselves. See where I am going with all of this? General Powers: Yea. I understand you, but all of this is just happening so fast. It is hard on my head. I mean I am not use to all this pressure. You know I have been under a lot of pressure at times and overcome it, but this is more pressure than I can handle and the more we read on in this letter the harder it gets on me. Commander Fuller: And once you overcome this battle the better of a general you will be because you will be able to say that when you thought you couldn’t handle the pressure anymore you were still able to overcome and that is a great achievement in itself. General Powers: Still. It isn’t going to be an easy task for me. I mean my head feels like it is going to burst already and we haven’t finished the letter yet. Commander Fuller: I know, but you are going to do ok. If you were all alone in this I could see you having a problem, but you have to remember that we are all working together, so you shouldn’t have as much pressure on you. General Powers: I am trying. I am trying. But I can only try so hard. Commander Fuller: I know, but anyway. We are going to approach the president first with this situation and just keep it on the low with just him. See what he thinks about it and also let him talk to BladeD’s parents. He will also talk to BladeD. Together as a group I mean. That way we can talk plans together. General Powers: Maybe the president will do a better job talking BladeD’s parents into having BladeD go in for us, then we would. I don’t think the president would sound as demanding as we would, so his parents wouldn’t feel as threaten, in a sense, toward the president as they may be toward us. Commander Fuller: Good point general. See. Even under pressure you are still able to do your work at your finest. In fact, we may not even have to be in that part of the conversation at all. The president may be able to do it all on his own and we would come into play when making the plans. General Powers: Yea. Commander Fuller: But we are starting to get too far ahead of ourselves. We still don’t even have any plan ideas and like you said we haven’t finished reading the letter yet. General Powers: Should we write a letter to the president or just talk to him face-to-face first?Commander Fuller: I would suggest that we just walk in face-to-face with him because this issue is so big and by the way, right now the president is clueless to what is going on, so we are going to have to explain a lot of this to him once we start talking to him and we won’t be able to let him read the whole letter. General Powers: That is going to be a lot of talking and confusion going on. Commander Fuller: Yes, but it is what we are going to have to do. :: The scene cuts here. Man, this story gets better and better as it goes on so why should I ruin what is ahead with silly questions? I don’t need to recap what has happened so far or give out questions that could ruin what is ahead because of the heat that is building up at the moment. All you can do so just sit and wait for the next part of this interlude series that goes right along with this sage that is amazing enough without this interlude, so I guess you are just going to have to stay tuned in! ::
