Legend of the Sleeping Dragon, as told by Rumiko

15 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/15/02 Rumiko waves to welcome the viewer to the Celestial Realm, to the Oracle's Lookout, from which the oracles keep track of their charges. Her body doesn't quite fit the loose yellow bikini top visible underneath the thin white T-Shirt she has on. At least she was poured into her tight green denim shorts. The viewer returns the wave, catching a glimpse the viewer's own small, light hand. The finger nails are distorted, multi-colored by the ghostly afterimage they leave as the hand trails back over its own back-and-forth path. This seems odd, but the viewer ignores it and makes a point of question to Rumiko... The Legend of the Sleeping Dragon? Oh, you must have been eavesdropping on our conversation. Who? You know, Kenshiro, Sho, and I. Rumiko! You don't even know who I am, and you're asking me about the Legend of the Sleeping Dragon? The viewer nods, though ashamed not to remember Rumiko. After making the trip to the Celestial realm to get information from her, the viewer would at least care to recollect Rumiko's name. Rumiko makes it a point to call the viewer on this point. Shame on you for not paying attention. We are Celestial Oracles. Sho is the highest ranking of us, currently just playing the role of the watcher over myself and Kenshiro, making sure we do our job correctly. I believe Kenshiro is currently keeping watch over The End, making sure that he doesn't go crazy and ravage the Earth. If he were to fully realize his powers in the wrong way, that would happen... The black-haired Japanese man in a grey cloak with khaki pants, black boots, and a green long sleeved tight shirt suddenly materializes. It's like he just phases into this plane of existence. The viewer is taken aback, neve really aware of how one enters the Celestial Realm. Not taking notice of the viewer at first, the man turns to Rumiko and begins talking with her. No, it won't. Kenshiro! You're back, why aren't you watching The End? News... I don't know how to take it... What? Cyclonica... What did she do? Let me talk, geez. She cut it out... His... heart? She killed him? No...She just removed half his heart... The human half... Another older man materializes. He looks like a Shaolin monk. He wears a long red robe that flows around his arms and down covering up his feet. There is no wind, yet the garment seems to blow in a gust. As is his usual mannerism, he strokes his goateed chin. Looking down to the newly formed Orb of Watching that allows the oracles to view the events in the corporeal realm, his hazel eyes reflect the images on the Orb. This is NOT GOOD... Sho! Don't bother with an introduction. Sho turns to the viewer. What are you doing here? Rumiko answers for the viewer, apologetic for the apparent intruder. Anarchy came to me asking about the Legend of the Sleeping Dragon. She said that she heard us talking about The End earlier. She shouldn't know about that, but ignore her. If The End's human half has been removed, then the Earth, indeed, possibly this entire universe, could be in very much danger. How so? Wouldn't this be a good thing? Now he won't have that part of his heart making him even stronger than he would be if he retained the human half... NO, indeed far from it. Cyclonica, in her attempts to search for a quick solution to her hybrid quandry, has in turn made The Sleeping Dragon an even more dangerous creature... His heart is what would have held back his full power... but now... Should I take him out now, as a precautionary measure? Are you mad? His power level is already growing exponentially, he'd rip your head off if he even sensed hostility from you. You are safer keeping close observation. We may have to just run damage control, and keep the innocents out of harm's way. Dang... Anarchy is still perplexed about what is happening, and would ask a question. The two males phase back out of the realm. Anarchy is left with Rumiko, who usually stays up here without a charge. Rumiko crosses her arms, hands clutching her biceps around to the triceps, as though she were cold, even though there is no reall feeling of hot or cold in the air. Looking back to Anarchy, Rumiko ventures to speak with her again. So what was it that you were asking about before we went off on our own discussion? The Legend of the Sleeping Dragon? You really want to know? It's a bit of a folk story, actually. Well, okay then. Get comfortable. That said in jest, Anarchy hasn't really figured out how to freely move about in this realm yet. She just floats in the breathable vacuum, observing Rumiko from a distance of about 10 feet. The Legend dates back a long time... 'Upon the top of a high mountain lived a Sleeping Dragon. The dragon had been sleeping for longer than anyone could remember. Thousands of years.' 'At the foot of the mountain, in a fertile valley that had a river running through it which flooded on a predictable pattern every year. And beside this river there was a village of people who had been living there for a long time. Longer than even the dragon had...' 'They feared not the dragon atop the mountain, for the it was a harmless creature. In fact, the villagers would bring it tribute in the form of food offerings of livestock. The dragon didn't require food in its hibernation, but the villagers brought it anyway. And the dragon, to a small degree remaining conscious in its sleep, did not refuse.' 'While it would never eat a villager, the livestock had to be guided into the dragon's open mouth and this sometimes required the herders going in farther than they would have liked to.' 'The villagers did not mind the offerings, indeed they looked forward to it and preached its importance to the younger generations. The dragon was something of an elder of the village, though not truly of the village. The dragon, in its limited consciousness, was a guide to the people, answering one truly important question per month, as the situation required. In times of drought, the dragon would release a single tear that could water the land for months.' 'In this village, there was no crime, no petty arguments or sin. Everyone shared what they had with everyone else. Leisure time was merrily enjoyed, but when they needed to work, the villagers worked very hard to perform the necessary tasks.' 'The villagers did this becuase of the legend told among them. The Legend of the Sleeping Dragon.' Anarchy shakes her head, Rumiko had only set the scene for it. No, it was a legend within a legend, odd story-telling device that had fallen out of practice until recently. 'Back when the Earth was in the early stages of civilization, there were two tribes, the Ngongo and the Meila. They lived in the shadow of the mountain, which they had never named, existing in hostility for one another. Each tribe wanted to be sole possessor of the land in the valley.' 'The tribes went to a very bloody war over the land, which cost many lives.' 'A dragon came to the mountain, looking for a spot to enter into its millenia-long hibernation period. The mountain was the greatest spot in all the land, and the dragon settled into it, but looking down into the valley, the dragon saw the warring tribes.' 'The dragon had observed the same behavior in other civilizations. Greed. War. Self-destruction from within the communities and warfare destroying them from without. Humanity was wiping itself out, a tiresome repetition of events the dragon saw whenever it move from place to place.' 'The dragon chose to speed the humans along their chosen path of oblivion, and so before its hibernation was to begin, the dragon rained fire down upon the war-torn valley, upon the tribes fighting that war.' 'An interesting revelation... the two tribal armies, realizing the fire was not an act of man, formed a phalanx-umbrella with their shields to protect themselves. The warring tribes joined tgether for the common survival.' 'Intrigued by this behavior, the dragon ceased his attack and descended upon the valley to address the tribes.' 'Speaking with infinite wisdom, the dragon spoke the language of man in his address...' *Humankind seemed to me to be nothing more than a species that wished its own extinction. Warring factions from without and within. Warring dwindled the populations, turning life for you into a constant struggle of vendetta and counter-vendetta. The wants of the individual, much more often than not, outweighed the needs of the group.* *But under the threat of annihilation by what you most likely perceived an act of God, you banded together to survive. That is humankind's most self-neglected, yet most redeeming characteristic.* *I shall soon enter into a deep sleep, lasting many generations. During this time, I leave it to you, the Children of the War, to take that opportunity. I wish for you to cease your fighting, and share the valley in peace.* *For when I awake from my sleep, I shall pass final judgment on humankind, and you shall be the humans upon which I shall base my decision.* *If humans learn to appreciate life, and live in peace and harmony, I shall bestow blessings upon you greater than you could ever imagine...* *But if the human race has not changed its ways... then that will be The End...* Anarchy nods, taking in the story Rumiko has told her. Your fellow wrestler, The End, is the Sleeping Dragon. Something of a 'celestial failsafe' installed into the universal clockwork of the by the original Creator. Humans unfortunately the pinnacle of non-Celestial life in the universe, are the example by which all life will be judged. If it is not a favorable judgment, then that's it. Rumiko stops her point there, Anarchy raises a point of question, asking Rumiko to explain in more detail. The End has the power to destroy reality as we know it, for Celestials and humans alike. He's too powerful to stop, we might have been able to do something about it if we'd have known sooner. None of us could have guessed that Digo the Grigori would produce such offspring. Point of question, who is Digo? Digo? He was what humans would associate with a 'Guardian Angel.' He was a protector of Humans. He defended the realm valiantly when the Psico Realm attempted to invade our own. He entered the war not to protect the Celestials, but the humans he had become so fond of. The End had a human heart, and through his heritage has the same judgmental responsibilities as his Celestial father, Digo. But without that human heart... he may lose that part of his humanity that would have made him spare the universe. Anarchy asks if this is like what the Bible was talking about the second coming of Christ in the Book of Revelations. What? No, not at all. If it is not a favorable judgment then NOTHING will be spared...
