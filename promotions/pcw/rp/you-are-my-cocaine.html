Date: fuck yourself Ali Khadafi- I lie next to you and breathe in the light scent of roses. You lead such a charmed life, with your Mid-Western lifestyle. No struggles, no hatred, no feeling, no emotions. No emotions… You are my cocaine I breathe you in and remember how you told me about your childhood. About the fun you had with your friends shopping in the strip malls. About the time when your daddy got that big raise and took the family to Hawaii. You seem so happy, so enamored, and so fake. So fake… You are my cocaine I drag in the soapy smell from your skin as my lips trace a soft pattern across your taut belly. A belly that has always been full. A belly that doesn’t know the raw cries of hunger. You giggle as I kiss you less than an inch below your navel. Where were you when I had to kill men in Japan? Did you want me then? So dumb, so numb, so fucked. Fucked… You are my cocaine That makes me wonder as your head rests against my chest, how much you know about me. You say you do, but I really don’t think that you can begin to fathom the depth of a mans desperations. How I cling to you like a drowning man clings to a shard of wood. How a junky clings to a needle. Clinging to a needle… You are my cocaine Yet I fear this need. I fear its implications and ramifications. Am I going to change into something that I’m not? Abercrombie and Fitch logos plastered across my finely chiseled pectoral muscles with Gap jeans clinging to my hips? Stylish little duck flip haircut and thick, black horn-rimmed glasses, babe? Or will you change to suite me? Stop being prissy. Stop telling me to wear underwear around the house. Stop telling me to let my hair grow out. Just stop… Let me be… Let me live… Don’t lie to me and tell me that I can do what I want. That’s shit and you know it. You’ve got the collar around my neck and we both understand that. You yank the chain and I come crawling home. You tell me to do this, do that… I’m all pink in the middle. I’m more Mid-West then I know. It’s funny how it happens without a person even knowing that it’s happening to them. That’s why there’s a belt around my neck. That’s why there’s a chair under my feet. That’s why I ju… That’s why you are my cocaine
