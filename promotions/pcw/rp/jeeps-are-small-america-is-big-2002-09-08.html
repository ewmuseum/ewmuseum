Jeeps Are Small & America Is Big

8 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/09/02 “I can’t believe we have to take the BUS!” Our shot opens up on the disgruntled face of PCW’s CEO, Xavier Kannon. His eyes are narrowed in frustration, shadowing the bright Green of his irises. The mane of Strawberry Blonde hair that he sports is tied back behind his head and his thin beard is looking a little overgrown. Xavier : “And in Hubbard’s name... How did you manage to lose a JEEP!” The 1st couple of Scientology are sitting towards the back of a night-bus, heading to Wisconsin where they can catch a connection back to Hubbardville to begin preparations for the City’s hosting of Fusion. Beside Kannon sits Ellie~!, looking as if this isn’t the first time that she has been asked the question on the journey. The prep rolls her vacuous, pale Blue eyes and sighs, exclaiming with her hands. Eleanor : “Um... Like... HELLO?!?!?!?“ She stares right at Kannon’s face with her Bambi-esque eyes. Eleanor : “Jeeps are, like.. SMALL, and America is, well, BIG!“ The Scientologist sighs and shakes his head as he makes another unsuccessful attempt to get comfortable in the cheap bus seats. Eleanor : “So, I don’t think I can be, like, blamed for the size of the Jeep... Or the size of America... So I can’t be blamed at all... YAY ME!“ Despite managing to lose their only mode of transport, the prep seems quite pleased with herself, managing to convince her own preppy mind that it wasn’t her fault. Eleanor : “Anyway, we could have got a Plane but you are all We don’t have enough money...” Kannon whips his head around and growls. Xavier : “BECAUSE MY WALLET WAS IN THE CAR!!!!” The prep just holds her open palm up to her irritated husband’s face. Eleanor : “Like.. What-EVERRRR!... If we’d have just stayed at home this wouldn’t have happened, but you just HAD to drive 100’s of miles for surgery!” Ellie~! again rolls her eyes and shakes her disapproving head at Kannon before looking out the window again where nothing can really be seen due to the bright Orange hue of the streetlights reflecting off the plentiful dust and dirt on the outside of the vehicle. The Scientologist speaks harshly through the snarl on his face, it being one of the rare moments that he is angry with his preppy wife. Xavier : “That’s because my ear-drum nearly had a hole blown through it by being a few feet from Plastic Explosives going off, landing ear first on a C4 board and having Nelly blasted into my head... By YOU may I add!” The Prep turns back to him then pauses, racking her brain for a defence. Eleanor : “Well.. Um...” She pauses for a bit more, then just as Kannon thinks he has won she blurts out something. Eleanor : “Well YOU didn’t waste perfectly good lingerie and the promise of some kinky sex only to be walked in on by some Canadian Virgin!” The CEO has no comeback. Eleanor : “So really, me doing that to you was just a natural defence mechanism for poor, little, traumatised Ellie~!...” She turns her face into one giant frown, looking up to him with her doe-eyes that never fail to melt Kannon, and with one look at her fake, apologetic face he gives in. Xavier : “I know.. I’m sorry my little Fuck Ferret...” Ellie~! grins and bounces on her seat before quickly stopping and looking hard done by again when Xavier looks back to her. Eleanor : “Um.. Bumpy bit of road. Anyway, like, wanna play a game?” Kannon reclines as best as he can in his seat and closes his eyes. Xavier : “Actually, my little Poon Puppy, I’m going to try and get some sleep. That hour long trip to the bathroom we had kind of tired me out.” As he tries to drift off, Ellie~! nudges him.. Again.. And again until he eventually opens his left eye and looks to her. *BEEP* Eleanor : “Er.. You might wanna play because there’s, like, a Bomb on the bus that will now go off if you drop below 5 insults a minute...” Both his Green eyes now spring open as he looks to his wife with caution. Xavier : “Um.. I don’t think they can make them, my little Shag Squirrel.” He closes his eyes again and yawns, fidgeting to get comfortable in the seat as Ellie~! nudges him again. Eleanor : “THEY can’t.. But ELLIE~! could! I made it when I had the residual Evil Super Genius powers from Alan Kreigman, in case we were ever stuck on a boring bus journey and I needed, like, entertainment!” The Scientologist’s jaw slowly lowers as his eyes widen... His face going White. Xavier : “Oh.. My.. Hubbard. You did, didn’t you?” She smiles and nods proudly. Eleanor : “Like... So... Did!” He suddenly springs to life, looking all around him. Xavier : “But you’ll be blown up too.... Phew, you were just kidding. You nearly had me for a minute then my little Poon Puppy.. Nearly had me...” As Kannon chuckles and lets out a sigh of relief, his wife’s lightly freckled face has gone ghostly White, her eyes not blinking. Eleanor : “Ooooooooooooooooooooooops...” Kannon shoots his head around to his valet who has just realised that she too would most likely perish in the explosion and Kannon leaps up out of his seat. Xavier : “THERE’S A BOMB ON THE BUS!!!“ With the Lion-esque CEO’s yelled proclamation the bus flies into a panic.... All the passengers shouting or screaming. “We’re going to DIE!!!!” “It’s Muslims... MUSLIMS I SAY~!” Despite the fact that Kannon stood up and yelled it, all eyes turn to the man towards the front of Indian descent with a thick Black beard and a turban who just looks back at them, a puzzled expression on his face. “Er.. Hello? I’m a SIKH!!!“ With the news it isn’t him, all the passengers just scream and shout again, Ellie~! included. Eleanor : “I’M TOO ELLIE~! TO DIE!!!” She nudges and nudges Kannon. Eleanor : “Insult people.. Or it’s going to blow!” “I have to call my family!” “Let’s all leap off!” “LET US BURN IN THE DEVIL’S ETERNAL HELLFIRE!!!” The bus passengers all turn to the Nihilist at the back and scowl, while Kannon kicks into action. Xavier : “YOU... Where did you buy yourself? Unemployed Bums ‘R’ Us?!?!?” His insult is aimed at the man in a tatty suits across from him who seems shocked that in the circumstances he is being insulted. The Scientologist’s gaze then turns to the woman in the seat next to the scruffy man. Xavier : “And in Hubbard’s name woman, I hope Xenu gave you a receipt for that face and a LONG warranty!” The passengers turn to Kannon and start to yell at him, angry looks on their faces. Xavier : “Please Peons, please believe me... THIS WILL HELP!!!” The driver is panicking and swerving in a blind panic across the road sending the night traffic screeching out of it’s way. Xavier : “Driver! What is wrong with you? You’re going about as straight as Chris Chase and Corey Williams!!!!” As everyone keeps directing their anger on the panicked Scientologist, he turns to Ellie~! and growls, the prep doing her usual reflex of making the Bambi-face. Eleanor : “I’m sorrrrrrrrrrrryyyyyy...” With a few flutters of her eyelids, Kannon sighs and wipes the hostility from his face as we cut to an exterior view of the bus speeding off into the night as the scene fades to Black. “Um.. You have, like, 2 seconds to make 2 insults......1.....Zero..... Hmm, must have forgotten to turn it on.... Silly Ellie~!”
