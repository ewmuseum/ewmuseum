Reasoning

Date: 6-01 ~ Flashback ~ I can’t explain why I do what I do. Upon waking up this morning, the inner voice that speaks to all of us in the background of our thoughts was screaming out so loudly that I had, for the first time ever, considered eating a bullet. It speaks to me in a language foreign to that which I know, making it impossible to decipher and make sense of, yet each and every time I open my eyes after my horrible dreams, the demonic voice crescendos until virtually unbearable, then disappears abruptly as a bizarre warmth overcomes my body, as if though some sort of medicine is taking effect and curing that which ails me. I tend to remember a little more of each and every venture I have in my unconscious mind, from the men dressed in black, to my surroundings, and the constant mumble of inner thought I can’t help but replay over and over again. I feel like a robot, like I’m being controlled and made to prey upon innocent souls for some greater purpose, but that is where my remembrance ceases. These same fucking four walls, that’s all I ever see, that’s all I know anymore. My cube at home, my cube at work my cube of virtual damnation! You didn’t think I just sat inside all the hours of the day did you? Of course not. I’m just another nameless face that dredges through an eight-hour day in anonymity working at a desk, plugging data into a computer, although I can never seem to explain what it is I do beyond that vague description. It seems real enough, but I feel nothing for it, no self appreciation for a job well done, hell, I can’t even remember getting a pay check. But it’s my life, I think. I’m the guy you turn your back to and scoff at when you see me in the lunch line, the guy you refuse to acknowledge is ever real, I’m the person who lays dormant in your eyes and should probably not even exist, but in the same breath, I may be the entity you should fear most. I don’t know what’s happening to me anymore, I am losing control of my thoughts, my actions, my life, is, falling apart, in front of my very eyes. Like a shutter on a camera, my eyes blink heavily, fighting every second to stay open, yet failure is inevitable, failure is evident, and just like so many others, I succumb to failure each and every day now, only to be reawaken to know I’ll return to that place. I can’t live like this. But every time I try to take a knife to my fucking throat a pain arises in my stomach that stops me. My true hell is never being able to stop it, not being able to fight it, and it won’t allow me to just end it all, not now, for whatever purpose it is holding me for. I don’t know. I can’t even begin to guess. I’m stuck here. You don’t know me, hell, I don’t know me, but I see things I can’t explain. I’m forced by some other godly power to react and respond to certain stimuli and I cannot react, I just concede to the power, I have to. I sense another reality with in this realm, but as much as I beg for it to show itself, it continues to taunt me from what seems like light years away. But it’s there. ~ Excerpt from Journal ~ February 17, 2003. This morning I awoke feeling more disembodied than usual. My conscience thwarted my usual morning torpor adding to my usual inability to focus on any of my specific duties related to any given day. I’m not sure why I feel this way, not until remove the comforter from my bed revealing that I am completely in the nude. When I laid to go to bed, I was clothed in flannel pants and a T-shirt, but I awake in the nude, with no idea of where my clothes have gone. I stand and walk noticing how cold the hard wood floor is on the bottom of my feet, in addition to a cold breeze that has managed to intrude into my dwelling through an opened window that I clearly had closed before falling asleep. Standing in front of a mirror in my living room that rises from the floor to be nearly as tall as I, I stare at my naked form, noticing for the first time all morning the damage that has been cast unto my body. A five inch cut running from my naval straight up towards my chest area, two puncture wounds that look to be placed in the exact same place on either side of my sternum, about half way up my torso, and a long jagged cut running along my belt line. I dare not look lower…… But alas, curiosity overcame my will power and my gaze shifts lower. The blood that had run cleanly from my previously mentioned wounds had stained my skin below my belt a pale maroon and beyond that, I step back astonished by a cleanly shaven pubic area. Feeling down around the smooth shaven skin on either side of my manhood I manage to gaze lower in the mirror, but nothing below my mid section seems to be altered or affected. However, an eerie feeling of soft pain brings my attention back to my shorn genitalia region where upon closer inspection I notice redness. Not exactly as maroon as the dried blood located above, but in a more so irritated sense of red, that I also could not explain. Slowly, in an almost worried manor my eyes scavenge back up my body, noticing again the wounds that I wear on my chest and I begin to notice a sense of weariness. My head feels light, I can not place my feet on the ground to walk nor raise my hands at all, I’m paralyzed! I’m floating! Who are you!! ~ End of Journal Entry ~ ~ Fade to Present ~ If I knew then, what I know now, perhaps I wouldn’t have fought it so much. Violence Jack was not taken by me at your Pay Per View for anything he has done for the wrong. No, he was taken because we required him once again and soon he shall return more ready to fulfill his duties. Only now he will be aligned with a likeminded being, created for the soul purpose to aid him in his journey. Fantoche is coming again. Soon.
