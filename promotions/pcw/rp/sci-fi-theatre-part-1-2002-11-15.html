Sci-Fi Theatre(Part 1)

15 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 15, 2002 Announcer: Sci-Fi Masterpiece Theatre…Coming up next… [Soon after the announcer speaks, a eclectic re-mix of the original “Masterpiece Theatre” theme is played over a fly by of a set of sci-fi models, some of which are models of Star Trek, Star Wars, Warhammer 40,000, Battlestar Galactica, and other classic television series, the logo blasts across the screen with laser beams. The title sequence then leads to the room, which happens to look like the old bridge of the U.S.S. Enterprise during the sixties. We then see Eddie Mandrake’s manager, Spider “on the bridge” so to speak, looking to arguing with the person, on the other end of the comm. system.] Spider: Look, I do not care what you can give me right now…I want that fucking drink, understand? [Spider then looks up at the camera and realizes that he is on the air.] Spider: Hi! Welcome to Sci-Fi Masterpiece Theatre. All of you sci-fi nerds are probably thinking…What the hell is this? Did I turn to the wrong channel? I assure you, you haven’t turned to the wrong channel. I didn’t take the old host however…and replaced him with me tonight. So, I am going to present to you a classic within the Sci-Fi genre. [Spider walks over to a glowing reddish-orange vial on one of the near-by red safety rails. He lifts it into his hand, and stares into the glimmering compound.] Spider: Spice….melange…The apparent life-blood and the single most important resource in the distant future. It is a multi-purpose chemical, much like Slurry. It can be used to build materials, heal the old and dying, and double the lifespan of the people that are using it. Some people have an allergic reaction to it, some people take it’s advantages for granted. It is mined from the sand of only one planet in the entire Empire of Humanity. That planet is Arrakis. Dune. [Spider puts the vial down onto a nearby red safety rail.] Spider: During that time, between the years 10,191 and 10,193, there is only one real war chronicled. That war was between the noble House Atreides, and the evil House Harkonnen. This war was fought on a very covert level, and these actions were chronicled with Frank Herbert’s best-selling novel Dune. However, there was one little known battle. One, not officially chronicled. One where a third house, tried to usurp the power from the Harkonnen, during the time where Paul Atreides, a.k.a. Muad’Dib, was consolidating his power in the Desert, even though he was deposed from the official throne of Arrakis, and was thought, in the eyes of the Harkonnen, dead. The house in question was the insidious House Ordos. [Spider walks back to the Captain’s chair, and sits down in it.] Spider: Tonight, on Sci-Fi Masterpiece Theatre, this little known, not officially chronicled battle between the House Ordos, and the House Harkonnen. Starring my client, Eddie Mandrake. This is what is known as “Dune: The Ordos Exposed.” [Spider then clicks a few buttons on the Captain’s chair as the view screens comes on, and quickly floods your television screen. Then a planet appears on the screen. It looks to be white with spots of green…Very small spots of green.] Narrator: Draconis 4…The ice world, and home to the insidious House Ordos. Here, is where the people, although they live in the ice, are the wealthiest of Imperium. They are mercenary. They want for nothing, except for power and profit. They cover all their actions in many different cloaks of secrecy, thus making their motives toward the Landsraad Council, and the Imperium themselves, even more dangerous. This is the story of a person whose alliance with the Ordos, brought their eventual downfall. [We move to the surface of the planet, where there are Ice Plains, Ice Mesas and very few rivers. A hover-tank sails across the plains leading to a large open doorway. The tank almost literally flies into the hanger deck of the capital complex of the Ordos. It comes to a stop almost on a dime in it’s parking space. The hatch pops open, and out stumbles the star of this little series, Eddie Mandrake, wearing some black thermal clothing. He staggers out of the hover-tank onto the floor of the hanger.] Tank Driver: Sorry, sir. I’ll be sure to slow down next time. Eddie: Be sure that you do. God, I have to go to the restroom. That was a long trip from western continent. [Eddie quickly runs to the restroom, we remain outside the door. Some retching can be heard. After about five minutes of constant retching, Eddie walks out of the restroom, and meets up with an attractive woman.] Eddie: Why, Hello. Woman: Spare me your idiotic ramblings. I am the Mentat to the Executrix. You are to come with me. Eddie: Very well. [The Mentat and Eddie walk down a long corridor carved out of Ice, and reinforced with Steel.] Eddie: May I ask you something? Mentat: By all means. Eddie: Since you are from the planet Tleilax…I thought you’d be very ugly looking. Mentat: Yet, I am attractive to you? Eddie: Well, yeah. Mentat: The Ordos paid a lot of Solaris in order to get a mentat that would not fail them. I was the result. Eddie: I see. So, you are a prototype? Mentat: You could say that. Eddie: What is your name? Mentat: Roma Attani. But you will address me as Mentat. [They approach a rather large door, and enter the next room. It is the Throne room of the Executrix, the visage is horrifying. Four beings controlling a single one, each of the beings have numerous tubes sticking out of the vital organs, connecting at the single being’s head.] Being: Mentat, Step Forward. [The Mentat steps forward onto a set of steps leading to the chamber of the Executrix.] Mentat: I have summoned this person, an overseer from the other side of the continent. He is extremely qualified. Being: Overseer, Step Forward. [Eddie walks up the steps and meets the Mentat on the same step.] Being: I am the Executrix’s representative, known as The Speaker. You are being re-located. Eddie: Executrix. It has been my dream to meet you. But may I ask, to where? Speaker: You are being re-located to Arrakis, and you are being promoted to Strategist. If you look to your left, you will see that fear is a great motivator. [They look to the left. Eddie winces as he sees a man, whose head has been altered to fit a piece of machinery. Many different tubes protrude from the head.] Man: Let me die…Please…Let me die…Please… Speaker: This was your predecessor, as the Strategist and Overseer on Arrakis. He lost ALL of our territories during his tenure. Your job is to regain the territories that he lost, and to purge the ruling house of Harkonnen so that the Spice will be ours, and our profits will be increased exponentially. Mentat: Do you understand your duties? Eddie: I do. When do I leave? Mentat: Immediately. The forces will arrive within the hour, and the Heighliners will take you to Arrakis. Don’t fail us. Eddie: I will not fail. Where are the forces going to be gathered? Mentat: Bay 1, that is where you arrived. Eddie: And Promptly blew chunks. I know the way back, thank you. [Eddie quickly leaves the room. The Mentat turns toward the Speaker.] Speaker: What are your hopes with him? Mentat: Very high my Lord. Speaker: I hope that your faith in him is not misplaced. I would rather not have to kill him. [We return to the USS Enterprise, with Spider sitting in the chair.] Spider: Well, Well, Well…This is going to get a little dicey for Eddie. Death for failure. These guys mean business. We’ll be right back with more, after a short commercial break. [Fade to a commercial for Cocoa Puffs.]
