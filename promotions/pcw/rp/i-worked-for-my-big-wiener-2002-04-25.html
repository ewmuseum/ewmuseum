I worked for 'My Big Wiener'

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/25/02 *Monologue* Surprises. Life's full of them. Life is like a hooker. Sure, she can give you a blowjob but you got to give her the $5 first. Which makes life great. But if you screw life over and don't pay for it expect life to bite you in the nuts. This has happened to Big Daveylicious. He's paid his dues for the Extreme title. But to his surprise life has just bit him in the nuts. His surprise his only friend, Mike Frampton tries to steal his title away from him and to worsen everything else that’s going through the man’s mind he gets a 23 man elimination match for his Extreme title. Big D's had two title defenses thus far and this match happens to be the biggest title defense. Not many Extreme champions endure this. Hank~! surprisingly didn't. Nor, Karl LeCrap. Not even Jason Core! It could be Big D's last title defense as the Extreme champion or not. It all matters if he pays the hooker named Life. (The scene fades to a dim lit room. We notice a cheap oak office table. Behind the desk sits a man in a cheap Wal Mart suit. He moves back and forth in his rolling chair. Looking at his fake rolex. Suddenly, a door opens and casts a shadow of a tall figure. The figures shadow falls to its knees, arms streached out holding something that resembles a large belt. Tommy looks over at the shadow smirking. In front of the desk is a metal chair.) Voice: Whooooooooooooooooooo! (We turn to the door to find that the figure is indeed Big Daveylicious in an Aramni suit kneeling on the cold floor with the Extreme title outstretched with joy.) Big Daveylicious: Whooooooooooo! Your Extreme Champion jobbed to Chef! Whoo...wait that's not something to celebrate. (Big D scratches his head, stands himself up, and shakes his head.) Tommy Stillinger: What can I do for ya, champ? Big Daveylicious: I jobbed to Chef. Tommy Stillinger: You jobbed to a chef? Man, what did he beat you with a pan? Big Daveylicious: Women beat me. Not men. Plus, I don't want any man to touch me with his 'pan.' Tommy Stillinger: I meant that as in defeating you. Big Daveylicious: Yeah, I jobbed. He used his fat a$$ wife to beat me! I mean I'm a lover of all women. But I have my limitations. Tommy Stillinger: You like easy 'Paid and get laid' chicks. Big Daveylicious: The fact is I jobbed! I'm the Extreme Champion! I'm not supposed to job! (Big D walks angrily to a chair.) Big Daveylicious: I thought my old job was bad! I remember it clearly. I was 15 years old. (The scene starts to fade to a flash back. It’s a nice sunny summer morning in the streets of Miami, Florida. A younger Big Daveylicious is wearing a hot dog man uniform along with a hot dog stand. He stands in front of a woman's lingerie store. Suddenly, an extremely muscular man walks by. Big D stands in front of him.) Teeny Daveylicious: Excuse me, sir. Would you like to try a “My Big Wiener”? Muscular Man: What?! Teeny Daveylicious: “My Big Wiener” is the best of all wieners, sir. In fact it’s quite big and juicy. A guy like you can use something like that. (The man instantly grabs hold of a the teen up with one arm by the collar and punches the teen boy through the window of the lingerie store. The window shatters as he lands onto the mannequin. He rolls over and stares off in complete blank as a woman whom sees what happened. She notices his hand on the mannequin’s breasts.) Teeny Daveylicious: I was just trying to sell “My Big Wiener”. Woman: You dirty pervert! (She takes mace out of her purse and blinds him in the eyes. As we fade back to the cheap office were Big D is sitting.) Big Daveylicious: All I tried to do was sell a “My Big Wiener”! But nooooooooo I come across as a pervert. Life screwed just because of working for “My Big Wiener”. Tommy Stillinger: Well, I don’t wanna work for you penis, Dave. Big Daveylicious: I was talking about hot dogs. I used to sell hot dogs. Tommy Stillinger: Man, you are pervert. You think dogs are hot. (Big D folds his chair and begins to slam the chair onto the floor yelling.) Big Daveylcious: I WAS TALKING ABOUT THE FOOOOOOOOOOD! Tommy Stillinger: Oh... (Big D drops the chair and folds his arms in anger.) Big Daveylicious: You’ve done nothing to help me and you’re my agent. Tommy Stillinger: Have too! I made you were that druid robe and attack Frampton. Big Daveylicious: Yeah, which sucked. I barely could put up a fight because I couldn’t see in that thing. Worst of all it SMELLED! Tommy Stillinger: Blame the crack hooker I stole it from... Big Daveylicious: Wha... Tommy Stillinger: But that doesn’t matter. I told Britney to be like Madonna. I told Madonna to be Cher. I’m telling you to be like Britney. Big Daveylicious: Be like Britney Spears? (Tommy nods.) Big Daveylicious: Hmmm... (Big D strokes his chin as the scene fades to Big Daveylicious with long blond hair in a schoolgirls outfit. We also notice he gots breast implants!) Big Britneylicious: Oops...I got breast implants! (The scene fades back to Big D without breast implants stroking his chin.) Big Daveylicious: Hmmm....I’d be a mouseketter. Wait a minute that means I’d have fake nude pics of myself downloaded by 13 year-olds. Plus, I’d go out with that fag, Justin! Tommy Stillinger:. You’d also have an autograph from Mickey Mouse. Big Daveylicious: I already have his autograph. (He shakes his head looking at a struggling Tommy.) Tommy Stillinger: Well, why not be like Cher. Big Daveylicious: Too much plastic surgery. Tommy, your my agent and all but your advice sucks. It sucks as much as “My Big Weiner” job does. Tommy Stillinger: Ugh. My advice doesn’t suck much as your blowjobs are. Big Daveylicious: Blow jobs? What are you talking about? I was talking about my old job. Only naked women give blowjobs everybody knows that. Tommy Stillinger: The world needs a heterosexual champion. Find a naked woman! Big Daveylicious: I am a very proud and comfortable heterosexual I love naked women. Tommy Stillinger: Well, go find a “Paid To Get Laid” chick. Big Daveylicious: But what about Madonna? Tommy Stillinger: Hmm... (Tommy scratches his chin as the scene fades to Big D with long curly blond hair, a white cowgirl hat, a golden trench coat sitting in a golden limo.) Big Madonnalicious: Music...makes the people...come...to...what the hell am I singing? Hey, Mister DJ turn the music off this is the end of the promo. (With that the scene fades to black.)
