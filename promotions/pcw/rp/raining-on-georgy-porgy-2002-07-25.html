Raining on Georgy Porgy

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 25 [The scene opens atop a large highrise of apartments in the downtown Los Angeles area. The fifteen story building houses many wealthy business men whom seem to have a knack for young asian women and pretending that they are poor. It just so happens owner of Gringo Industries, which is some kind of bogus dairy factory, lives atop the highest level and is throwing a most bodacious party. George Porgamaker is the CEO of the company and happens to be a big time wrestling fan. He tried his hand in wrestling but in his first match with a fat, large man named 'The Fiery, Flaming Gorilla' he was beaten mercilessly and had to give up all sporting activities after that. But the spirt of wrestling has never truly died within George's soul. He watches PCW religiously and if it's missed he feels he has committed some kind of bogus sin and must redeem himself. Too bad he's missed the past two editions of Fusion, and now he's gone into a frenzy with this. The company may be going under and he has to devote time to keep it afloat. He doesn't want to lose all of his gnarly green he made over the years. George decided to invite two bodacious dudes to his big party. He feels this will redeem himself from missing two PCW shows in a row. So...'Totally Tubular' Tommy and 'Heavy Metal' Harry, THE COWABUNGA CONNECTION, have entered the world of dairy products] [The Los Angeles night sky is clear for once on this night. The stars shining bright and the moon is almost full. The city is lit up for this most radical occasion thrown by the bodacious Georgy Porgy. Harry and Tommy are out of the terrace looking out to the city together. Both men seem distracted as they have been down lately in the way of their careers. They are bodacious. They are righteous. They most certainly are gnarly, but there is just something missing. There is one tiny speculation that is needed to make the Connection a true force to be reckoned with in the PCW tag team division] [Tommy, dressed in a navy blue silk button down shirt, blue silk dress pants with a yellow stripe down the sides. He wears a blue hat with a yellow strip surrounding it. Tommy is certainly stylish for this occasion] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Dude, don't we have a match on Fusion. [Harry stares straight ahead toward a cluster of large skyscrapers just across the street. His eyes seem so focused and relaxed. Unlike Tommy, Harry is dressed in his usual Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. Harry wouldn't have it any other way] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Who is it against? Harry: Misplaced, man. TTT: Oh. We've totally ripped those bogus dudes apart many times already. Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy looks behind him passed the sliding double plated sound proof door. The party has really picked up as many of the 'more important' guests have arrived and everyone has turned their attentions to them. Tommy and Harry are two of the most bodacious wrestlers in PCW and all those people want to do is pass around their bogus business cards. Talk about HEINOUS!] TTT: Harry, did you see any gnarly babes in there that you might take home? [Harry still has that focused and relaxed look in his eyes. Really the look is more like relief than anything] Harry: I saw a few, man. TTT: A few, dude? Are you going to go for some most bodacious threesome action? Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Gnarly. ['Livin' La Vida Loca' by Rick Martin begins playing loudly as all the middle aged business men grab their young asian women and begin trying to dance. The beautiful gowned asian women can move so fluidly and sexy, but with a no rhythm old man trying to lead it makes them take two really bogus steps back. Tommy feels disgusted now that these old dudes have these beautiful and special women because they are powerful and rich. Lets face it, Tommy is powerful and rich as well. He worked his most gnarly behind off to be the greatest surfer of all time, and now he's working his GNARLY behind off to make the Cowabunga Connection the greatest tag team of all time. It's just going to take some time] TTT: It's really heinous in a bogus kind of way that we didn't get to wrestle at Wrestle Vessel, dude. [Harry still has that relaxed and relief on his face. He's standing stiff as a board up against the railing of the terrace. It looks some weird] Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: We've totally laid waste to The Heartbreakers and Pretty Cool Guys so many times that it's no longer gnarly. Yet they get to go after the bodacious tag titles and we have to sit back and watch them. Harry: Misplaced does too, man. TTT: Your right. Maybe we have something in common after all. It would be totally gnarly if we joined forces and totally kicked ass. Harry: No, man. TTT: Yeah, your right again. Those dudes are totally bogus. Harry: Not far out. Not far out at all, man. [A very, very fat man now makes his way on the terrace. Tommy turns to him but Harry stays turned toward the railing not even acknowledging this large man. The fat man has a full beard, bright blue eyes, and he's completely bald. George Porgamaker, owner and CEO of Gringo Industries, shakes Tommy's hand seeming very proud to meet him] George Porgamaker: HI! It is such an honor to meet my favorite tag team! HAR HAR! [Tommy quickly notices that this large man laughs like one of those bogus pirates in the movies. It's kind of scary] TTT: The pleasure is all ours, dude. [George goes to shake hands with Harry, but Harry just nods his head not wanting to offer his hand] George: Ok...HAR HAR! TTT: So, your that Georgy Porgy dude who sells that really cheap milk and cottage cheese. [Georgy Porgy takes large pride in it] Georgy Porgy: That certainly is me. But I think you should know that you guys are one of the highest rated tag teams in PCW.... TTT: Really? Georgy Porgy: Your damn skippy you are...HAR HAR!! I'm in the PCW secret, super dork fan club. We keep track of each match move by move. You and Harry have hit the coolest tag team combinations and exciting moves of any tag team. Your win/loss record is certainly something to be quite boastful about. TTT: Ummm...bodacous? Georgy Porgy: REALLY BODACIOUS...HAR HAR HAR!!! [Harry sighs in absolute relief as he now turns to Georgy Porgy, zipping up his fly with a large smile on his face. Tommy looks at Harry quizzically as Georgy Porgy just grins like some kind of little child who has met his role model] TTT: What were you doing by that rail, dude? [Harry shrugs like it's nothing] Harry: Taking a wiz, man. TTT: On the highest floor of a most high apartment building?!? Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: You could have used the bathroom, dude. Harry: I couldn't make it, man. TTT: You could have ran. Harry: But I would have peed in my pants, man. This is good leather. TTT: BOGUS!! [There seems to be some kind of ruckus in the party now as many people begin jumping to one side of the room, getting out of someone's way. A balding muscle man with a black tank top and black jeans comes storming out to the terrace, soaking wet...and quite smelly] Muscle Man Covered In Pee: WHO PISSED ON ME?!?! [Harry and Tommy quickly point over to Georgy Porgy] Georgy Porgy: HAR HAR HAR!!! I didn't do such a thing, good sir. They are merely ribbing you! HAR HAR!! [The muscle man punches Georgy Porgy in the face and the fat man keels backward into the railing and flips over. He plunges toward the ground to what will be a very messy and splattering death for him, but his pirate like scream stops at half way and there is no loud crash. The Cowabunga Connection looks over the rail to see the fat man hanging by a flag pole, with one of the most brutal wedgies ever witnessed in all of wedgieness] TTT: Totally gnarly!! The flag pole saved his fat ass!! [Harry taps Tommy on the shoulder] Harry: I gotta pee again, man...MOVE!! [Harry goes against the rail again and then large screams are heard from the flag pole as Georgy Porgy is being covered in Harry's urine] TTT: What is wrong with you, dude?!?! Harry: Small bladder, man. TTT: Must be the size of a pea, dude. [The scene then fades to black]
