Date: --- (All wrestler voices are impersonated.) Tyler Nelson:“I made $200 million dollars in my first week on the job!” Pete!: “I lost 50 pounds in a month!” Rush Mason: “I’m not gay!” The black screen opens up, fading in showing the pictures of the poorly impersonated voices of Tyler Nelson and Pete! Rush Mason’s sound alike is dead-on, though. As we cut away from the pictures, we see two figures. One, short, feminine body structure. The second is that of a muscularly built male. The darkened studio is illuminated with the slowly rolled lighting, the kind that fades on and off, coming up to full light. Sandy Wong is wearing a blue Oriental dress with a floral pattern going down the left side. Her hair is full, hanging out and down to her shoulders. She’s normally a slight pale complexion, but her cheeks are blushed and rosy from makeup. Black eye-shadow, apple red lipstick. She doesn’t usually get this dressed up, her significant other being blind and all. Skylar Green wears a black Giorgio Armani suit, double-breasted though the width and breath of the shoulders is not due to the customary padding. His hair is tied back. He wears darkened reading glasses (for aesthetics, obviously). The two are standing in front of the screen that has the pictures of the persons impersonated, covering up their faces. Noticeably, Rush Mason’s face is prominently larger than the others. Skylar begins the scripted on-camera promo with Sandy. Skylar:“Do you know what the these three men have in common?” Sandy gives a quick answer, it is clear that she’s reading cue-cards. Sandy:“They’ve got a stick up their ass?” Skylar merely shrugs. Skylar:“Could be, but try again.” Sandy pretends to think more about the next answer. Sandy:“They’re all afraid to fight their own battles?” This answer gets a nod from Skylar. Skylar:“That’s correct, but not actually the answer I’m looking for.” Sandy turns to her left, facing Skylar instead of the camera. She looks at the pictures more carefully. Somehow (cue-cards) this helps her to divine the right answer. Sandy:“They’re all lying?” A “Yes!” and thumbs up means that’s the right answer. Skylar:“Exactly! Not one of these men is telling the truth.” Sandy:“How do you know?” Skylar:“Simple. Tyler Nelson’s net worth is $200 million, so he certainly can’t make that in a week. Pete gains 50 pounds a week. And Rush Mason has tried to touch my sack so many times that there’s no way that can be true.” Sandy Wong nods, turning back to face the camera. Sandy:“Ah…” Skylar:“But what all these men are doing is they are using a speech device that I like to call Rush Mason Logic.” Sandy Wong covers the sides of her face like Macaulay Culkin in Home Alone, with her eyes wide open looking directly into the camera from the left side of the screen. Sandy:“What’s that?” Skylar:“It’s very simple. Rush Mason Logic is a case of a person blatantly lying, hoping that whomever they are speaking to won’t bother to check into the veracity of any statement that they make.” Sandy scratches her chin. Sandy:“Interesting.” Skylar:“Very. It proves quite useful, except when someone proves them wrong. But it can be fun, I suppose. Why else would they do it?” Sandy:“You try.” A shrug comes from PCW’s #1 Contender to the Continental Title and resident blind man. Skylar:“Hm… I am PCW’s Only Grand-Slam Champion to hold all titles simultaneously! And that includes the Rising Star AND European Titles!” Sandy:“Wow. I’m even more impressed to be your valet now.” Skylar:”I also declare that I have already defeated Rush Mason at the pay-per-view!” Sandy:“What about Gaz Green?” Skylar:“Um… Sure, why not? Him, too.” Skylar nods his satisfaction. Skylar:“Actually, this works well. I already said that I wouldn’t put up a fight against Gaz Green, so to just go ahead and declare myself the winner works out perfectly.” Sandy:“Yay!” Skylar:“So… want to just end the promo right now?” Sandy:“Yes. Yes I do.” *End*
