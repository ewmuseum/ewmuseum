Title: Word up my dark hommies. Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 11/12/02 A man of pure evil only emerges every forty years or so. No matter if its Josef Stalin, Adolf Hitler, Francisco Franco, Benito Mussolini, Mao Tse-Tung, Ossama Bin Laden or Denis Miller. The fact is their impact on history is always felt. A tragic, intense, unforgettable impact that carves and changes the way we perceive the world. Every incarnation of a soul who bares iniquity tarnishes the wholesome outlook we have on our world and our brothers in the human race. From Adam and Eve to Dick and Jane to Justin and Brittany the world has lost it’s purity and innocence. Malice and self-interest is now the law of the land. The mighty Dollar and the Euro rule the world as morals are left with about as much value as the Paso. Pay homage to your new God Dukestar the Goblin….. -Our Goth gangsta God is in the bathroom of his evil Montana mansion. He is brushing his creepy white teeth with strokes of pure Malice. He spits the venomous saliva of a god mixed with toothpaste into the sink as he washed out his mouth with the water from the deadly sink of rage. He yells out to his mistress who is in the other room which probably reeks of Goth Sex.- Dukestar: Honey where is my black mascara I have an appearance in an hour at a Missoula militia seminar for Goths and Guns in Gods Country. I wanna look dope mad in front of my peeps yo. -A voice that can only be describe as nothing less then evil answers back our favorite Goth God and your PCW president Duke Williams- Mistress: Damn dawg yo I used the last of that dope shiznit. Cus the Evil Gates of Valmair whispered satanic voices in my head telling me to look sexy in an evil Beetle Juice kinda way. Dukestar: Couldn’t you save a little for me? Mistress: Cry me the river of hell aren’t you supposed to be a God yo? Can’t you like blink your eyes and shit like that genie bitch on that old dope TV show and make some face goo appear? Dukestar: I’m not a Fucking genie I’m a Goth God. I do not blink my eyes for face goo I wave my finger to create disaster and carnage. -Duke wiggles his fingers with a wicked voodoo glare on his kisser and the devil in his eye.- Mistress: What do them hillbilly Goth’s give a tupac about your face paint. Dukestar: Even as an Evil Goth God I’m still an entertainer first and foremost. I have to put on a show. If ten guys get out of a tiny car we call them dirty Mexicans and shake our head in disgust; if ten guys get out of a tiny car wearing clown makeup we call that funny! Mistress: Yo I gots some brown hommies back in the crip in NC so lay off the Mexican jokes yo. Dukestar: Damn bitch its my house you all getting up in my face in. You best be stepping back before I lay some of my Gothness down on your punk skanky bitch ass dark hoe. Mistress: What ever I’m gonna drink some gin and juice then drop some “E”. Dukestar: You zombie gangasta goth biya-biya-biay-baich!!!! -Duke slams the door on the bathroom and just shakes his head in the mirror. He then speaks to himself and the world while looking in the mirror.- Dukestar: How many four way dance matches have I been in for the continental title? -Duke lets out an evil hiss as he practices some creepy faces.- Dukestar: Always the brides mate never the bride. But who has been in this situation more times then your favorite evil buckaroo?? Nobody. Chef is a pawn in an evil game. Not even a queen or a rook just a fucking pawn…well ok maybe a queen. Anybody who f*cks fat women have something wrong with them in their head. Wacko man. Not stable. Manson like. I’d rather fuck an old skinny whore then a fat fucking model. All them folds man it’s a bitch to figure out. Even though I’m a God I do have to say I can’t even fold a road map let alone figure out where to start on a chubby twat. -Duke cringes as he plucks an evil hair from his nose.- Dukestar: Anyways, even as an Evil Goth God I’m still a president and an American. This white wearing, pastry making, flubber fucking, side switching, dick wad thinks he can take out the Malice Man???? Even if he didn’t say so, my evil Goth God sensors tell me he is thinking that. This guy’s obviously has never tasted my Malice Sauce. No sweetie not that sauce….I’m talking Duke Williams Brand BBQ and Chile. Ummmm damn from Goths to Gods to norms and necrophilia’s everybody loves Duke Williams Malice Brand products. Chef not only I’m I better in the ring, life, and the after life I’m also the better cook. -Duke pulls his hair back in pig tails.- Dukestar: Lets not forget our friend Darkstar. The guy who has been trying since he was just a tiny star to be just like his idol Dukestar the Goblin. He tries so hard its really touching. -Duke wipes a burning sea of salt the Goths call a tear from the corner of his eye.- Dukestar: From the look to the moves in the ring he’s a mini me. What a man. Someday he may be almost a legend. The Steve Young to this Joe Montana. But I must teach my young protégée a lesson with my new move The heaviness Drop. I will lift my opponent in the air as if I’m going to do a Full Nelson Slam, instead, I’ll drop one of the arms that is holding the opponent and slam them down in a Full Nelson Reverse DDT. God Damn I’m a move genius. My Goth God creativity with wrestling moves is amazing. -Duke tugs at his beard with a look of superiority.- Dukestar: Then there is Snow and what can I say about Snow. Hit or miss. Damn I gotta run see you crazy bisnitcha’s on the flip side my goth gangsta hommies and hosses that’s the honky tonk front office goth god gangsta truth. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
