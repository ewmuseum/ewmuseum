The Sage of A Champion: Part 14

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-14-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!Part 14” Chapter 5: Mission Of Violent Ray UnfoldsDay 6: The Insane One, Mir, Begins without BladeD :: The scene is picked up from where we left off. Mir has pretty much left the group for the most part since he took something that was said way out of proportion. He has decided he doesn’t need BladeD or the general’s plans to get through this mission, yet we found out that Mir is in for a dozy. BladeD and General Powers begun to talk about what all is going down and the scene picks up from there. :: BladeD: Now when I go to Sergeant Mills, he is going to answer all my questions right? Meaning, there aren’t any questions regarding Violent Ray, getting to the book, and getting to Violent Ray itself that he couldn’t answer? Correct? General Powers: As far as I know you are correct. I don’t think there are any questions you could ask them that he couldn’t answer. BladeD: All right, I just wanted to make sure. General Powers: Now he may ask you questions about yourself and remember that you can answer any questions he asks. It doesn’t even matter if the questions are classified. They could be as top secret as it gets and it doesn’t matter, so don’t feel uncomfortable about answering any of his questions. BladeD: All right then, if you are sure it is ok, but I doubt he will ask me many questions. General Powers: I guess so, but why are so sure that he won’t ask you many questions? BladeD: Because I don’t have much time to get this book, so when I go to talk to him I am going to be in and out of there as fast as I possibly can. General Powers: Well, you do have a point there, but you have to stay there a little bit. He is going to be a busy man, so he isn’t going to want you to be there long either, but you do need to chat with him as much as possible because the more information you can get out of him the better it will be for the mission because he is going to have a lot of information for you. BladeD: ::sarcasm:: Yea, information I will forget. General Powers: Well, even with that sarcasm of yours you are going to get the chance to remember it all. We are going to have you keep a radio on your belt. The radio must stay online at all times; it has incoming and outgoing capabilities. It is about this big, :: lifts his hand over the desk to show a measurement. :: So it isn’t that big at all. When you press this red button. :: points to the red button :: Everything that is being said comes through to us. :: When that red button is released and this blue button is pushed. :: points at the blue button. :: Then everything we want to say to you, you can hear. That is basically how it works… So when you are talking to people like Sergeant Mills all you need to do is make sure that the red button is pushed down, so everything he says will be recorded and used for future reference. The radio is designed that way, so people think it is incoming only and not outgoing. BladeD: That is a pretty awesome idea… What is more awesome is the fact that it is possible. General Powers: Yea, and it makes you more of a spy like that as well BladeD. BladeD: :: laughs :: Yea it sure does, but the bad part is that even though it is small you are going to have to realize that there is going to be a point of time where I am not going to have this thing on. General Powers: :: saying confusingly :: And what would that reason be? BladeD: Well, when it is time for me to take on Violent Ray I am going to have to take everything out of my pockets and whatnot. The only thing I need on me is my clothes because I can’t have anything slowly me down and it is on my belt. I am going to be rolling around and everything else. General Powers: Yea, well I guess that is a good reason, but other than that. BladeD: Also, what about places with metal detectors and things of that sort. General Powers: Well, you have to realize that you are working for the one kind of people in the world that do these kinds of things better than anyone else in the world. That radio can’t be detected by anything. Nothing known to man will be able to detect your radio. It has been tested with everything it could be tested with. BladeD: That is pretty sweet. General Powers: :: says in a confusing type of voice. :: Sweet…… Yes. BladeD: :: laughs :: Sweet isn’t even really a slang word anymore and you still aren’t use to it. General Powers: I don’t really have time for things of that sort because you have to remember our job is filled with pain and suffering. Our jobs aren’t usually ever “sweet.”BladeD: Except when I do something awesome. Like defeat Metal Armor. General Powers: Yea that could be considered something “sweet.” BladeD: It was for me at least. Because it is was the greatest day of my life. My next greatest day of my life is going to be defeating Violent Ray. Right under that will be getting rid of BattlePowers. General Powers: Which will never happen. BladeD: Oh it will… I will make sure it does. :: smiles :: General Powers: If you say so BladeD. BladeD: Yea, but we will have to talk about that at a different time. Anyways, are there any more people that I will need to talk to that we know of at the moment? General Powers: Not that we know of BladeD. If you find Athens Mir you will need to talk to him, but other than him Mills is the only person we know of that you need to talk to. BladeD: Where is my first destination in Russia? General Powers: Well it isn’t going to be the NMA of Russia. I will tell you that much. The first place is a place called Turkey, Russia. I don’t think you are going to have to talk to anyone there because you are going to need to get into a building called “Ground down under.” It is the weirdest name I have ever heard of for anything that relates to the military or infantry, but you are going to have to get inside that building and find a map of Turkey, Russia. Because right next to Turkey is a place called Aelin, Russia. Aelin is where the main headquarters of NMA of Russia is. We can’t fly you directly to Aelin, so you are going to have to find it on your own. The reason why you are going to have to go to “Ground down under” is because for some reason they don’t have maps or anything of that sort in the airport you are going to be in. I have no clue why they don’t, but that is what I have been told. Also, the reason why is because on civilians maps they don’t show the NMA of Russia. I was told that civilians have no business knowing where those kinds of places are and if they want to know they have to go through other resources to find out. BladeD: Well, the people at “Ground down under” are going to think that I am just a regular civilian and aren’t going to give me access to the maps. General Powers: Actually, they will because we have a military license for you. It isn’t a Russian one, but it will still work. All you have to do is ask for a map. You don’t have to tell them what you are looking for and if they ask you what place you are looking for, and then just tell them that you are just looking around that you haven’t ever been in Russia before and you just want to get a general idea of the place. You have to remember that you have to be friendly. If they start suggesting places for you to go, then take the tip and take it nicely. The friendlier you can be to civilians and people of that sort the better off we all are going to be. BladeD: I will try my best, but you do know that I have that serious combat mood that I get into and the whole time I am in Russia I am going to be in that mood, so I am going to try my best in that mood. General Powers: Well, in that “combat mood” of yours you need to stay cool also. It isn’t going to be the hardest thing in the world. Just act as normal as possible. :: The scene is going to cut here. No real reason at all why it is going to cut here. It is just going to cut here and that is all there is to it, so just get ready for the next part of this series because it is almost time for the action and the adventure of the series. BladeD is almost ready to get going. ::
