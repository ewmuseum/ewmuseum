Last leg or my last stand.

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Last leg or my last stand. Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 03/10/03 -Duke Williams is standing in the front of the magazine rack at his local Wal-Mart Super Store. He is dressed in all black and is checking out FM before moving his glare over to the wrestling magazines all of which have his picture on the cover.- Williams: It took me retiring to get the sweep!! I wonder if I’ll be on next month’s cover with the PCW world title on my shoulder. The Last Stand In Open Country is coming to a close, I have seven more days as a professional wrestler before I kick back in retirement….what the hell??? -Duke starts waving his hand in the air franticly like trying to swat something.- Williams: God damn it what is that a fly??? -Duke keeps on waving away at what ever is bugging him.- Williams: Nope that’s not a fly pestering me it Tim Walden!!! -Duke laughs as he drops the act and picks up a copy of Wrestling Illustrated on which he graces the cover.- Williams: Waldo why do you feel the need to be a pain in MY ass before my big going out bash? Are you trying to get the shine of the spotlight thrusted upon you? -Duke holds up a small black and white picture of Tim Walden from the magazine.- Williams: Timmy you need to face the fact that this is the peek of your career. This black and white photo is the most press you will ever receive. I know you’ve been in main event matches before but this match with me is the biggest of your career. -Duke puts his pointer finger to his temple.- Williams: Think about it Waldo. I’m the number one face in the world and you have a chance to beat me before I go on and kick your ass out of the March Madness rumble! They you can claim to have beaten the champion right before he won the title. But knowing you you’ll blow it like a Spanish whore in Harlem. -Duke stops to ponder his night with a Spanish whore in Harlem. It leaves a smile on his face.- Williams: You see Waldo I have accomplished more in one year of my lengthy career then you have in your whole existence. Does that bother you chump? Does it bother you that your nothing more then a follower and not a take charge leader? -Duke walks over and grabs a Three Musketeer bar from the candy rake as he proceeds to the check out counter.- Williams: It’s Malice Time hoss and there is nothing you can do to stop it except let it run its natural coarse! -Duke smiles as he stands in the 12 and under line.- Williams: Waldo take some pride in knowing your going to be one of the last guys sent Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound!!! -Duke turns his back to the camera then turns around again like he’s forgotten something.- Williams: And hoss that’s the honky tonk truth! =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
