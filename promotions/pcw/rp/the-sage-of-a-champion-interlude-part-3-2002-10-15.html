The Sage of A Champion: Interlude Part 3

15 October 2002

Date: 10-15-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!The Interlude Part 3” Chronicles!Chronicles!Chronicles! Chapter 2: Metal Armor? :: The scene picks back up in the middle of this letter that Commander Fuller and General Powers have been reading and kind of discussing the past far minutes. They are slowly finding out why Russia has decided to attack them. At first the story was completely just a reminder of what happen in the past, but now they are learning new information that was too classified, at the time, for them to know. Plus, it seems like they are getting closer and closer to knowing what Metal Armor is like. :: The angry Russian officers got talked European officials into somehow forming an alliance with Russia to get back at the states. Russian tried China and Japan, but only China expected. So now it is Russian, Europe, and China all in a war together against the United States. You would think that Russian came forward asking money during this alliance, but surprisingly enough they didn’t. General Powers: That is odd… almost too odd. Commander Fuller: Yea. You got that right. They wanted money from us, but not from them… That is too weird. I was thinking to myself. WTF? You don’t want money from them, but you wanted money from the U.S.? Well, I had later found out that China had a…. well let’s just say they had a book. Commander Fuller: WTF, a book that is worth 2.7 billion dollars? General Powers: That is amazing! Well, come to find out this book with entitled “Book of a deadly Sin.” Which was a code name for “Metal Armor.” I am not sure where the inside part of that comes from, but all I know is “Deadly Sin” was the code name for “Metal Armor.” General Powers: MG! China leaded the book to Russia, so that together, the three countries could form this machine. Russia pretty much lost all their troops, Europe didn’t have enough to supply a war, and China didn’t have enough to help. Since they were out of warriors all they had left was this book. Now it said in the book that at anytime if Metal Armor goes wrong… “In the wrong way” it could backfire and turn into suicide for the country(s) that uses it. At this point Russia didn’t even care because they were pretty much defenseless. Any country(s) could attack them at anytime and Russia had no troops to save them. The soldiers they gave up are on contract, so there is no way to get the soldiers back other than fighting the U.S. Which would make no sense, because they would have to kill their troops and wouldn’t get any troops back. Basically Russia was in a suicidal situation anyway, so it didn’t matter what they did at that point. China and Europe thought it was up to Russia to decide since they would lead all the building and everything. Commander Fuller: Building. I am guess there is about to be a money issue involved here. Russia was going to need money for the building. General Powers: Bingo! So a contract was made out for China, Europe and Russia. A rough draft was made by each country and looked over. Once every country looked over all three countries (meaning they looked over they own to make sure everything was in place.), then Russia (since they started this whole thing) made the final contract. There were no small prints or anything. The contract was made out point blank, so there would be no cheating involved by any country. Once the correct people signed this contract it was official. Now in the contract it didn’t say a certain amount of money was going to be involved. In the contract all it said was “until the project is finished.” Which obviously means that an unlimited supply of money would be provided until “Metal Armor” was completed. General Powers: Wow! That is a commitment. Commander Fuller: But China? And Europe? I am still puzzled by the fact that they were able to hide this secret for so long. I mean usually a spy of the U.S. would have stumbled across this by accident without knowing at first, but someone how they gotten away with hiding it really good. Now the plans for Metal Armor and everything that is needed was in a book so huge… Well let’s just say it took a long time to read through the book enough to know exactly what was going on. It was always like going through high school and one of your classes being “Metal Armor” because there was that much to learn about Metal Armor. General Powers: Wow. This thing must be pretty powerful if there is that much information about it. Commander Fuller: Yea no joke. I wonder how many people actually took the time to learn Metal Armor. General Powers: Well, you know people were probably paid to learn the thing and teach others all the information needed. Commander Fuller: Most likely that is what would have happened, but still. That seems like a lot of information to process and remember, but in the end I bet it was worth it money wise. General Powers: Yea, because now they seem to have a very powerful machine on their hands. The only thing I didn’t understand is who came up with this book. The author of this book is “Mr. Unknown” and it is annoying. You would think the person that would come up with such a diabolical machine would at least want to take credit for it, but for some reason the man is unknown. The book was found in an ancient ruin and the book (from what I was told) seemed to be thousands of years old, but there is no possible way. I mean the book was too advanced for even our time. You couldn’t think up technology like this in ancient times unless you were just making up a story, but something this detailed isn’t a story. It is basically a “How to do” book by a person that is very intelligent and possibly too intelligent for his time if you understand where I am coming from. It was like someone came from the future and dropped this book in this unknown ancient ruin. That is how advanced the book is. General Powers: It seems like it would too advanced for its time if the machine were as powerful as it sounds. Commander Fuller: But there is no way that it could just drop out of the sky or someone from the future could just pop up and lay the book down like that. General Powers: Do you think the future was so advanced that people could time travel? Commander Fuller: But we aren’t the future yet. You have to think of it in terms as our time traveling back to past times. General Powers: Or do you? Commander Fuller: I would think so… General Powers: Or the future traveling to us and we not know it. That could very well be the situation because we don’t know what is head in future. Commander Fuller: I understand where you are coming from, but it just seems too unlogical. General Powers: But time travel is unlogical to begin with, so I mean… Commander Fuller: Yea, but still. I mean it is just odd this could be happening. I would just think… I don’t know. I would just think there is a logical explanation for something like that… Thinking in the terms of someone actually coming up with this knowledge on his or her own and writing this book, then selling it to China, and China allowing Russia to use it. General Powers: But the only problem with that is the fact that the book and the machine itself are just too… advanced for our time. Commander Fuller: I know general. I know. :: The scene cuts here. Wow. Amazing what we are learning so far. So is there really someone that came up with this book? Or was time travel from the future used in order for the book to appear? And if someone was smart enough to write this book… are they too advanced for our time? Who knows? I guess we will have to wait till a later time to get this questions answer and one more thing. When are they going to describe what Metal Armor looks like and what it can attack with!!!!! ::BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! Chapter 2: Metal Armor? :: The scene picks up after a lot of information was given to form up a lot of questions that need to be answered. Everything from questions of time travel to a person or persons that are way too advanced for his or her times was brought up last time. Maybe if we are lucky we are going to be able to get some of those answers as we speak, but only time can tell. We still don’t even know who wrote this letter or how much longer it is going to last, but the longer it lasts the better it gets and the more information that is revealed, so hopefully the letter won’t end until every single question we can think of is answered. :: It scares me to think that a person could be this smart because if there is a person alive today that is this smart just think of the possibly of this guy. I mean there is probably no end to the terror he could cause if he has the knowledge that can create a machine that can blow up a country the size of the U.S. By the way, just on a side note, nothing in this letter must be given out to the press. Nothing in this letter can be printed anywhere, so be careful when showing this letter to different people because we can’t be having the wrong people with this letter, but you probably knew that in the first place. Anyway, back to the story. Mr. Unknown had a couple of letters hidden somewhere. If you read the book there are sections of the book that hint places where he has hidden letters. There is no telling if the letters are still in place, but they basically contain secretes he never revealed in his “How to do” book. The “Book of Metal Armor” is 3,470 pages long. You would think that in a book that long everything from the basics to the machines secretes would be revealed, but he has a couple (which hopefully only means two) of letter that is hidden in a couple (once again hopefully only meaning two) places in the world. These letters are described to be about four pages long each with information that are just secretes, so without these two letters you can build and run Metal Armor, but with them you can make the machine two times stronger. I have no clue where to find these letters, but for almost a year now Russia has been on the search for these letters with big money rewards for anyone that finds the letters. And just to make sure that the letter isn’t fake, it has special coding inside of it that basically “marks” it is what Mr. Unknown wrote, and only Russia officers know of these “marks” to look for once someone gives them the letter. I am not sure what the money reward is, but I heard it is so high that you basically won’t ever have money issues again in your life with the money you receive, so Russia is even in worse shape than before right now because everyone and their dog is in search of these letters. The chances of a civilian finding the letters are slim to none because they are hidden that good, but civilians are still taking that slim to none chance to it fullest and I wouldn’t blame them because a chance is a chance and this chance is worth pretty much all the money in the world that they will ever need. I am not going to waste my time searching because as long as I have enough money to survive and a little left over for things here and there I am happy. I don’t like being a greedy person especially with something like this were chances of winning aren’t really even chances if you know what I mean. I wouldn’t suggest anyone go on this “letter hunt,” yet a lot of people have and a lot of people have been asking me why I am not on the hurt. Believe it or not, some business have shutdown for a while in hope that one of their employees can find the letter and get the big bucks for it. It is really the stupidest thing I have ever heard, but Russia doesn’t just want to get back at the U.S., they also want to just put the U.S. out of commission and that is what it looks like is going to happen unless someone stops Metal Armor. The worst thing about Metal Armor is that the thing is so huge and has so many pounds of steel built on it that it would probably take thousands of rockets from rocket launchers to just knock the thing down. I doubt that one nuclear bomb from the U.S. would be enough to destroy the thing. That is how huge and well built it is. Commander Fuller: MG! Why that is insane!! General Powers: That can’t be possible commander! It just can’t be possible! Commander Fuller: I wouldn’t take any chances, but it does sound impossible that Russia could make something that strong. I bet one nuke on top of it would at least knock the thing down and put it out, just not destroy it completely, but that would mean it could still be in use. General Powers: That is just… insane. Point blank. That is just ungodly. We are going to be putting a 14-year-old boy up against something that is so ungodly… I mean… I am not sure about all of this, commander. I am just not sure. It would think that because it is this strong that it would move slow, but it moves at a walking pace which is very surprising and shocking for a machine this heavy and how it is able to do so I am not sure, but in the facility it is built in I have seen it walk around. Commander Fuller: That isn’t good. That means that Russia is probably just waiting for secrete letters and Russia will be ready to attack. I am just going to take a wild guess and just say that they are waiting for the letters, but other than that the machine is ready to go. I am not exactly sure how they are going to get the thing out of the building though. I think that is one of the problems because I think they are going to have to knock out a whole a little bit over two stories high in order to get Metal Armor out of the building. No air plane or helicopter known to man could let Metal Armor out of the building, so taking out a walk and just letting it walk out of the building is their only choice of action for getting Metal Armor out in the open, so I am guessing that is why I am beginning to see the top of the building slowly being taken a part. This process is going to take a while, but before the year is over they will have Metal Armor out and running. They are getting the best guys money could buy to safely, without harming anyone, knocking out two stories worth of this building to get Metal Armor out. It is a fast, but safe job. Most people would take about six months to get this job down and these guys are talking about having the job done in three months. General Powers: So I guess that means, we have three months to stop Metal Armor. That is a good thing. Commander Fuller: Yea. It doesn’t give us too much time for any types of delays, but the time is a lot longer than I expected. I was thinking around six weeks time to get this all done with, but with about twelve weeks, seeing as we have double the time I expected I think we can get this mission accomplished. General Powers: But BladeD sir… Commander Fuller: He is the best man we have and unless you find someone better than BladeD, which I doubt you will, he is going to be the man… I mean boy… kid… whatever… to save the world. General Powers: :: grabs a list of the roster of people in the NMA. :: I could randomly, without looking, point my finger at a name and that person would be a lot better than BladeD. I know that for… :: Commander Fuller stops him. :: Commander Fuller: You know that for nothing. I have been watching these men since they entered and some just entered the NMA. After seeing what BladeD as done at only 14-years-old I know he is tons better than anyone we have right now because I am telling you that, that kid is a one in a million. He is going to be our hero whether you like it or not general. General Powers: If that is way it is going to be, then that is the way it is going to be and I am not going to argue anymore. Commander Fuller: Good. We don’t have time anyways to find anyone else for the job when we know BladeD will do it for sure. General Powers: How do you know that for sure? Commander Fuller: We can offer the kid tons of money or something I don’t know. He is only 14-years-old, so I know we can find a way to bribe him into doing this job for us. It isn’t going to be hard. Even when we tell him that he will be risking his life. We will be able to bribe him no problem. :: Scene cut and man that is wrong. They are going to take advantage of a 14-year-old boy like that, but I guess you have to do whatever it takes when you know the world is at risk, but still. I just don’t like that idea because it just seems wrong. Maybe you guys feel different, but maybe BladeD will want to do it just to become a hero. They may just use that to bribe him and he will want to do it even knowing his life is on the line, he will probably say he doesn’t care and that he just wants to do whatever it takes to be a hero. I guess you will just stay tuned and find out. ::
