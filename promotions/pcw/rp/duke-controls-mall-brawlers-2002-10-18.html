Duke controls Mall Brawlers

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Duke controls Mall Brawlers Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 10/18/02 Dateline: 10/18/02Time: 11:25am CST Place: The evil Goth Lair of the PCW President and Goth Continental Champion Dukestar The Goblin.Mission: Order lunch and suck up the souls of those in the Mall Brawl. -Duke sits back in his plush leather chair made by Norwegian zombie’s in the third realm of Dukedom. Duke is watching everybody’s favorite Goth show lifestyles of the evil and dead. In walks his mortal servant Roseanne Bar.- Dukestar: Ahhh look everybody’s favorite underground Goth and high Satan priest Bill O’Reily is being featured. Roseanne: That’s wonderful you want any lunch?? Dukestar: Oh YEA!!! Get me an eyes of a bible thumper sandwich, which nails, and some diet goats blood. I’m watching the calories you know. -Duke pats his evil Goth God gut.- Dukestar: You know since Gods live forever I’m young again!! Roseanne: Yea…yea…yea….I’m going to get the food. Dukestar: Ok then I will rant methodically into the camera. Roseanne: You a God do what ever you want. -Duke shuts off the TV with the push of a button. Probably an evil black magic remote. Duke turns to talks directly into the camera with an evil glare in his evil GOTH GOD eyes.- Dukestar: Do not be frightened of me. I may be a burnt freak but I’m really an all right guy unless you challenge my superiority. Then I must use my Goth powers to diminish your soul. You know something that bites me in the ass?? I find out I can steal souls, heal cripples, walk on water, drink and not get drunk, but with all those powers I still can’t get DA BEARS to the super bowl and da Cubs….forget about it. -Duke shakes his head in Chicago sports pity.- Dukestar: To take my mind off of that I take control of somebody’s mind and screw up their lives. You’d be surprised how addicting it can be. Now I don’t pick kiddies randomly I just kinda point my finger at the PCW roster and then control one of them. This week its one of my mall brawl opponents the mortal Semore Smoke. Now in order to view Semore and pull up my commands I need to sign on to the SOUL DESTROYER!! -Duke lets out an evil laugh.- Dukestar: Now I’ve been getting some fan mail into the evil secret lair of myself Dukestar The Goblin which is odd since it’s a secret. But anyway a lot of you mortals seem to think my Evil inventions and tools look a lot like common household object’s spray painted black. This is a terrible human mortal misconception. You see us God’s are the one’s who plant the ideas of inventions into your feeble little minds. So yea your stuff probably looks something like our stuff since we give you the idea. Ridicules to think I would spray paint common items and pass them off as tools of Gods. You people thought my chance eliminator was a microwave painted black??? If that’s the case then why do I have the continental title and Rent-A-Hero doesn’t?? Hum?? Because my machine eliminated his chances, DUH. -Duke exits his chair and walks over to the soul destroyer which looks like a PC painted black.- Dukestar: Hold on let me boot it up. That’s God speak for getting it started. This machine is amazing anything a God wants to know about a mortal he can find on this thing I invented called the Gothernet. You just type in what you want to observe and it pulls it up. If you want to look at the human female form it is loaded with accurate scientific photos. -Duke’s desktop is open as he clicks on something labeled Soul Destroyer.- Dukestar: In a matter of moments Semore Smoke will be under my control. I will turn him gay and take his mind off of Mall Brawl this Friday. If your in this match don’t worry I will do this to each on of you’s. -Duke smiles a sinister grin of Malice.- Dukstar: You see that little lad there? That is Semore Smoke in his house. -Duke shows us the computer screen which has a guy looking like Smoke on it in a house. The evil program sure looks a lot like the Sims. As a matter of fact its identical.- Dukestar: You see these meters on the bottem control his needs. Right now he wants to use the bathroom. We will no honor that request. First lets sell off his weightlifting equipment and replace it with and artsy statue. -Semore wets him self as Duke replaces his weight gear with art stuff.- Dukestar: You see the mortal is getting gayer by the second. Now lets replace the wood paneling with pink wallpaper. Ahhh look so much better now. Good here comes the male neighbor now for some kissing fun. How will this man be able to compete with a God at mall brawl when he’s a flaming homosexual? I truly am an Evil Genius and don’t lay that Alan jive down on me he was never a Goth God. -Duke laughs an evil Goth god laugh.- Dukestar: You see once I do this to each and every member of the Mall Brawl roster I will come out victorious. It’s not easy being as smart as I am, as Goth as I am, as evil as I am. But by God somebody has to be. -Another episode of an evil laugh ensues.- Dukestar: This Friday I will keep my title and the evil reign of terror will continue. -In walks Roseanne with the sandwich looks exactly like a meatball sub from subway.- Dukestar: Lunch is here and I’m out. =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
