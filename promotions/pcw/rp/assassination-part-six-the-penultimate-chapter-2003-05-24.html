Assassination Part Six: The Penultimate Chapter...

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/05/03 Upon reaching the desired destination, Blind Rage and Warrior stopped about ten feet away from the warehouse and simply glared at the partially destroyed building. Warrior glanced at the piece of paper, with the address on it, then glanced around the street. “Doesn’t look like much, does it?” Warrior said. “No.” Blind Rage simply replied, as he concentrated on something. “I expected some kind of gothic castle or a massive hi-tech laboratory with various painful-looking devices. This doesn’t stand out at all.” “That’s the idea.” Blind Rage simply replied. “Right here’s the plan.” Warrior visibly deflated. “How come you always get to make the plans? I have a plan and I know it’ll work.” Blind Rage sighed. This happened every time. “I get to make the plans, because I am clever and don’t come up with lame-ass methods of doing relatively easy things ina very difficult way!” “Oh…” Warrior said, nodding his head uncontrollably. “I get it.” “You get what?” “You want to be the big daddy around here…you want to be the leader…” “I am the big daddy…I am the leader!” “So it’s like that, is it?” “Yeah, it is. But lets hear your idea, simply for comic value…” “Right, okay. We build a giant wooden horse. Then we hide INSIDE the horse and wait for Bullseye to wheel us in.” “That could possibly be…” “Wait, I’ve not finished. Then when Bullseye is asleep, we sneak out of the horse and rescue Handbag Warrior and Badger Man and…” Blind Rage holds up his right hand in annoyance. Signalling for his troublesome tag team partner to be quiet. “Just stop right there.” Warrior looked at his almost bemused. “What?” Blind Rage ran his over his mask. “Have you been watching the history channel this week or somethin’?” Warrior shrugged. “So what? The TV was broke. It wouldn’t change channel.” “It worked fine for me before I came out, except the batteries had ran out in the remote and I had turn it over manually.” Warrior looked at Blind in bemusement and wonder. “What…you can actually change channels WITHOUT using the remote control.” “Please. Please tell me you’re not THAT stupid.” “Huh?” “Never mind.” “Since when has this been possible? Is it a new invention or something?” Blind Rage shakes his in disgust. “Just stop talking. Right now.” Warrior grew silent for a second. “So what do you think of my idea?” Blind Rage shot him a decidedly angry look and Warrior seemed to back down somewhat. “That is the stupidest idea that I have EVER encountered.” “Worked for the Trojan’s dude.” “Yeah, but back then it was original.” Warrior nodded, understanding. “So that begs the question; how the hell do we get in?” Blind Rage shrugged a little. “Well, we know there is an obvious trap waiting for us in the warehouse…so why don’t we just walk in.” Warriors eyes visibly widened at the suggested course of action by Blind Rage. “And you called my idea lame!” “Well we know it’s a trap…therefore we have the upper hand. We can expect trouble.” “Rage…that’s suicide! Anyone with half a brain would know that it’s a trap, so it’s not that much of an upper hand!” Blind Rage wore a confused expression. “What do you mean?” Warrior was the one who sighed this time. “I mean, that he knows that we know it’s a trap.” Blind Rage’s confused expression, understandably, didn’t budge. “Huh?” “I’m just saying that we should handle this with a little finesse,” Warrior said. “Think tactically dude. Take every precaution! We can’t just bust in there like John fickin’ Wayne.” “Whoa…whoa…WHOA!” Blind Rage bellowed, pointing his finger at Warrior. “Leave the Duke out of this!” Warrior held his hands up innocently. “Okay…I meant nothing’ by it.” “We can’t stand around here all day tryin’ to come up with a plan, we need to be in there DOING something.” “Well I’m still all for the wooden horse deal…I mean it isn’t original I know…but that’s why it’ll work! Nobody’s tried it for a couple of thousand years…” Blind Rage rolled his eyes around the back of his head. “Shut up with that lame ass idea dickhead!” “Oh…it’s come down to that has it? Berating. You think we’re in the PCW now bum plug? Save it for the three other teams we have to beat on Supremacy.” “We have to go all the way to Iraq for that…” “I know…cool isn’t it?” “Yeah. Now let’s get back to the problem at hand. Bullseye will be prepared for us no matter how we enter the building!” “I know that,” Warrior said. “But if we rush him, unexpected, we can at least get a momentary advantage, perhaps freeing the other two. Then it will be less of a problem.” Blind Rage nodded in agreement. “Yes, that sounds at least a slightly plausible plan of action.” Warrior considered the comments for a little while. “So you’re absolutely sure that nothing can be said for the giant wooden horse plan?” “NNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO” “No need to shout.” Warrior pouted. “Let’s just get on with it, huh?” They ventured towards the warehouse for the final showdown.
