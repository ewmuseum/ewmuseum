Cannonball Run, Part 3.5: 'Suite of Diseases'

13 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/13/02 'The Panzer tank? No, let's go for the Apache helicopter... Can I get it outfitted with high-powered red paintball guns? Sweet...' 'Flight of the Valkyries' plays at 1500 decibels. The music is sent down upon the crowded Interstate 95, rush hour traffic combined with careless drivers equals an accident a little up the road. No fatalities, just damaged cars and hiked insurance rates. The copter comes in high for a diving approach. The guns are set operative, with a few test shots into a nearby sandlot to make sure they're working right. Firing is nominal. The music is so loud it's bringing pain to The End's ears. He has as silent a moment to think as he can, reflecting on the race he's run with Semore Smoke. I assume we aren't going for a stealthy attack. Why an attack is necessary is beyond me. Must be one of those weed-induced ideas. Not that any of them AREN'T. I swear, he's just that screwed up. The weed is probably a placebo. Gathering speed, the Apache helicopter, paint sky blue to blend into the afternoon sky to make the ambush that much easier. Some people below are wondering where the music is coming from, and turning up their own radios to drown out the Apache's music. I've put up with this whole cross-country race for quite a bit. Longer than I normally care to associate with a moron like Smoke. He's loud, can't keep a thought from one moment to another, forgets that the car is moving and tries to get out when we're on a bridge... Actually, that's not so bad... And as the copter approaches the ground faster and faster, it reaches the point to where the G-forces weigh down even more on The End, already more sensitive to tactile pressure being blind. He holds his chest as his heart painfully beats into the ribcage and wall of muscle. All this marijuana is going to my head. It smells terrible. I'm getting tired of humoring Smoke in pretending that I can see. He's lucky I'm aware enough of my surroundings. But next time we get in the car, I'm tempted to just throw it in cruise control at 150 mph and dive out while he's sleeping. And there have already been 200 opportunities to do that. One day, he actually slept for 21 hours. What is he, a mole? The copter begins to pull up ever-so-slightly, but that's only because the guns are fired. The strafing commences. Bernard Colt keeps doing a number of promos, even though he is ill. And I believe it, a legit illness. but he wasn't sick until he started dealing with Semore Smoke. Jared Diamond has a theory he expounds upon in his wonderful book Guns, Germs, and Steel for why certain areas were less susceptible to European incursion and invasion early in the modern historical eras during and following the Renaissance period. The theory rests upon the basis that each area had developed its own 'suite of diseases,' certain pathogens that developed through the animals in the area. And I believe that it holds true to PCW, as well. The paintballs hit a man sitting in the traffic stoppage, driving a gold Porshe Boxster. He looks at his chest and legs and sees welts that ooze red. The person who is perpetrating this is one sick puppy... You see, look at OSW. Everything about it dying aside, it looked like EVERYBODY and their mother wanted to go to OSW, while PCW had the same 30 people month after month. And it's not that OSW was the superior product, it's that there are certain people here who seem to actually drive away any talent that might want to work for PCW. Most recently it is the Front Office. Xavier Kannon has always been a prick, but a prick in a position of power is a terrible thing. But there are other, less direct diseases. Diseases such as Semore Smoke. The music goes up to 2000 decibels, the strafing continuing all the way down I-95 hitting police cars, minivans, school buses, and luxury cars indiscriminately. Let's go back to the Bernard Colt example. A healthy man is rendered nearly incapacitated by a terrible flu that he hadn't had until he came in contact with Semore Smoke. Scorpion, Blayze, Eddie Mandrake... while Mandrake managed to regain some health, the former two are still recovering from the illness I have dubbed 'Smokeosis,' the condition of being exposed to Semore Smoke. I myself am starting to feel ill. I have to get away from Smoke. But I have to wait for him to land. And as soon as the music's off I'll have to ask him what shooting rush hour traffic with a paintball-outfitted Apache helicopter while playing 'Flight of the Valkyries' louder than it should EVER be played has to do with anything... Who knows, he might have a rare moment of clarity.... The End:'So as you now know, Seymour, it wasn't exactly the most pleasurable of experiences being around you. And that's why I would actually take an extra bit of pleasure in beating you at this Turmoil, stripping you of the Extreme Title you fought so hard to earn. And then, once I've taken that belt from you, it will be my pleasure to walk into Black Friday Mall Brawl and, if not win the whole thing, at the very least to keep YOU from winning.' The End, sitting in front of a camera seated on a white stool with a blue wall in the background (normal PCW promo room), shrugs his shoulders. The End:'Well, I'm going to end this here. Because unlike you, I know that no one cares about my exploits in a race against other wrestlers when they'd rather just see me wrestle. You should try that sometime. Just... shut up and wrestle...'
