Transition -- History (Part 4: EW)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered *Three weeks ago, while in FBI detention; dictated to Sandy Wong* From PWA, I went to Eternal Wrestling (they said I was 6'4' because they wanted me to be shorter so as not to outsize the others too much), the place where showing up to work guaranteed a push, and where spilling coffee started 85% of the feuds. It started with Xavier Holmes. He and his cock-sucking manager Prime (actually, I never could figure out who was the bitch in the relationship, but I stopped trying once they disappeared; now that I think about it, Boca Del Inferno and Precious Peter Love remind me of those guys...) thought I was a lock and he put his 7-weeks-defended Primetanium Title up against me. And I beat him. Easily. He dropped off the map after that, his ego crushed. Next was the 6-man tag feud. Myself and Young Tiger teamed with Crimson Flame to take on Dexter Thomas, Gideon, and Ezekial. Thomas was a geek who became a wigger. Pretty much, if people had paid attention to Dexter Thomas, they'd have been prepared for Trigger. But anyway, Thomas feuded with Flame for the World Title that Tiger and I should've been near. We were the most credible contenders to it, but according to President Jackass (aka Cassidy) 'The public isn't ready for a champion or championship contender of Hispanic or black descent.' And thus, the race card was played, and what started as a single match turned into a full-fledged feud controlled by Dexter Thomas and Ezekial, who paid Cassidy to book them to take the World Title off of Crimson Flame; Eddy and myself were relegated to non-factors. We weren't competing for our own careers, just stuck around Flame as his helpers. I hated that, but I made the decision to stick with it, anyway, because I knew that EW was overrated, ranked 3rd in the entire PTC when in terms of the overall quality of its workers and the wrestling product, it was ranked after CCW and Global Wrestling. EW tried to compete with NTICW, but with Xavier Kannon, Hessian, Corg, Pharaoh, Ken Kaze, and others who entered it either at their prime or when they were just learning, their product was too much. Still, I stayed with EW. It was a business decision. If EW, on the whole, sucked, then by my staying there and standing out, it only served to make me look even better by comparison. NTICW, on the other hand, had so much good rising talent that standing out was difficult for them, and only those whose personalities (and wrestling) stood out very much were able to get noticed. The 6-man feud was a joke. Dexter Thomas was largely unreliable in the ring. He couldn't really defend himself. He nearly lost to Duzza. But because white guy workers who appeared at all the shows were a rarity, he was elevated to the World Title. Ezekial positioned himself to take over once Dexter Thomas washed out. Gideon was just there. He was useless, really. His finisher was officially a Shooting Star Press, but he used a chair to finish matches more than anything else, struggling to survive until the point he could get the ref distracted somehow to use the illegal weapon to win. All Gideon ever did was have a camera follow him around (odd, because he was supposed to be keeping a low profile for worry of getting attacked by his former gang) and he would say '(Insert name here) is a fuckin' joke, mahn. He ain't nothin' but shit, yo'.' Around the time that our 6-man issues were going on, Cassidy and Jax (who had left to be a security firm consultant, but ended up running the place) devised a plan to allow them to rule their federation with an iron fist. That was something that their large egoes and small phalluses wouldn't allow them to get past. Cassidy especially. He dedicated more time to himself talking than to the wrestlers, urging them backstage before a match to rush to finish an unscripted performance in under 3 minutes. (And he wondered why our ratings weren't doing well, why the best thing about the federation was the website.) The plan was to have all the wrestlers get inserted with NetBIOS chips that Jax's security firm developed. They acted like a microscopic house arrest bracelet, allowing the firm and Cassidy to monitor where we were at any time, anywhere we were. Despite the fact that this could not legally be done without a court order, (as it violated any number of civil liberties) a court order that Cassidy and Jax did not have nor ever attempt to procure, they went ahead with the plan, counting on the lack of information of the wrestlers. Indeed, aside from personal reasons, no one objected based on a legal defense for why the chips should not be implanted. I objected as well, but I felt I had nothing to hide and decided to get it implanted anyway. It was just supposed to be a simple microchip implanted in the wrist, simple. It sounded like something out of 'Demolition Man.' If it weren't for the fact that no one else saw the movie, I could suggest they got the idea from there, but I digress. I went in for the implant. They said they needed to put me under (gas me to sleep). I wasn't sure why, but I just wanted to get out of there and going along was going to be quicker than raising a fuss. So I get implanted and leave. No big deal, right? And then what happens but I'm suddenly getting attacked at the mall, in line for a movie. Random places, there were Ezekial and Gideon and Dexter Thomas, if not whichever hired thugs that Gideon and Ezekial had sent to try to take me out. This wasn't right. They always knew right where I was. As far as I knew, they weren't supposed to have access to the surveillance information from the chips some of us got implanted, (Young Tiger also got one implanted, but only because he was in a car crash, needed life-saving surgery, and Cassidy would only foot the bill if the implant was included in the package; Bobby Stormbringer got implanted because he knew it was his shot to be upgraded from ass-rapings by Cassidy to a main event push by Cassidy) Cassidy wouldn't have given up or even shared that kind of power. No, someone had given me a different chip, and I think the nagging sting at the base of the C-section of my spinal column had something to do with it. I learned that Ezekial had paid for me to get implanted with a much larger experimental chip connected to my nervous system at the spinal chord. It allowed them to not only track my position, but see what I saw... hear what I heard. So nothing I learned, no appointment I set up, was anything that they didn't know about. Sent the gangland-equivalent of a SWAT team after me. 'I had been a good sport so far. I tried to take it in stride. But when there are guys with guns coming for me, I'm entitled to take any measures I need to in order to protect myself' I thought, leaving a warehouse where I had killed 20 men who were armed with automatic weapons. They killed a few homeless people in their search for me, and no doubt expended much ammunition. I knew that Ezekial and the others saw and heard what I did. I was safe, though. They couldn't go to any police and say 'Look, he's killing our guys with guns that we sent to kill him.' Even if they did, they would have to explain how they can look through my eyes, themselves violating many laws that no lawyer could get them out of because there's not a jury in the country that would have acquitted them of a right to privacy charge. I took the chance of meeting an informant from inside the firm that implanted the chip in my neck at an outdoor restaurant in Chesapeake Bay, MD. It was a good spot, they couldn't storm the place with many people shooting guns without the police and a nearby military base getting involved. At this point, I wasn't hiding anymore, and just came out in the open. He gave me the information I asked for, schematics on the chip, how to take it out... that sort of thing. We were only a few minutes into our meeting when they killed my informant. It was more of a one-person close range sniper effort. I didn't have to kill the shooter, especially in public, merely broke his nose, snapped his arm, and left him for police. I wasn't going to risk being taken out in the middle of an operation, either because Ezekial and the others would tip off the doctors to poison me, or because they'd send in someone to stab or shoot me while I was under the knife. I wasn't going to take that chance. I took a bus back to the motel I rented a room out of that day. I went to 14 in the area and rented out of the 6th, but not go right into the room; while looking, I plugged my ears and ask the price of a room -- at the 6th motel I'd read their lips, and turned around, reaching behind myself to pay so that they couldn't see me doing so. I told them to leave my key for when I returned. The notes about the chip were bad. It was attached directly to my nervous system, especially those chords connected to my optical cortex. Long story short, they mainly saw where I was, 80% of the sensory information came from eyesight that was sent to Ezekial and the others. I weighed the options. On the one hand, suffer the side-effects of removing the chip -- permanent natural blindness (along with other possible side-effects ranging from indeterminate amnesia to death); on the other hand, I could continue to be hunted with guns until the day I slipped up and would get a bullet in the head. Preferring to live, no matter what the inconvenience, I chose the latter. Using a knife and some pliers, I removed the implant myself. It as scary when I crushed it and removed it. As soon as the clamps that attached it to the chord were broken, and I lifted it away, everything just went black. One second, I'm staring down at the sink where the blood was starting to mix with the running water. The next moment, the whole world just blipped out; everything, instantly fading to darkness like when you turn off a bright-screened television. After that, it took a couple of days for me to gain my bearings being blind. Once that happened, I retreated to the forest that I had trained in before making the move to PWA and the PTC. I trained my body and my mind, honed my senses to respond in place of my eyes. Learned to detect my surroundings. With the help of some sparring partner buddies of mine, Marcus Jackson and Julio Garcia (Rhythm & Rhyme, they were in the UWWC shortly as a tag team; actually won the Hardcore Team titles), I learned to sense the kinds of attacks that opponents would throw at me, how to anticipate their actions. It wasn't easy, it still isn't, but I manage well enough. I learned to rely a quite a bit on chain-wrestling, controlling my opponents' movements without letting them get away from me, while always being on the attack. When I had to come to another EW show, my opponent was Duzza. The was never that much of a threat, he was good practice to see if I could put my training to use in a defense of the Primetanium Title. I defended it successfully. Wasn't even that hard either. But then again, it was Duzza and it was EW. After that, there were problems with EW trying to take over PTC and PCW. PCW responded by cutting off EW's funding, and without that they couldn't do anything. EW had to close. I left EW, having enough of being mired in the mid-card when I knew I was better than the people in the World Title scene. *Next session: PCW*
