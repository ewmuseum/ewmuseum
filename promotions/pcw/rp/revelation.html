Title: Revelation Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: not entered Realizations. The HIT's got himself in quite a spot. He faces the Personnel Director of PCW, Brandi. For his Television Title. In a match where it's entirely possible Walden and/or Xavier Kannon will interfere liberally. Add in the fact that Trapezoid's never successfully defended a PCW championship, and you have the recipe for disaster. A disaster impending....but then, a new situation brews on the horizon. A battle for the Continental Title, in Versailles. With Cooking Utensils. Allez cuisine, and let the battle begin! ::Trapezoid sits, shaking nervously, in the arena mere hours before his title defense against the Front Office's Brandi Wilson. He stews, his foot tapping nervously, tossing and turning in his chair. Appearing to be on the verge of physical illness, he stands, PCW Television Title in hand, and a tear in his eye.:: The HIT: Au revoir, Television Championship. Once again....the odds are I will lose my beloved title before I have a chance to defend it successfully. Three on one...it's all too much. I doubt too much. Can I beat Walden? Damnit, I just don't know! Before, I OWNED Walden! I dominated him! I HUMILIATED HIM! Teresa and I owned that son of a bitch. But then, Alliances. He caused myself and Teresa to be eliminated from the tag team match! INCONCIEVABLE! He then BEAT ME for the Cruiserweight Title! INCOMPREHENSIBLE! And then...ALLIANCES! Goddamnit all! Now...Walden owns me. I may as well be stamped 'Front Office, PCW, Tim Walden, all rights reserved! Trapezoid seethes, his body shaking...anger and pain and torment combining themselves in an evil outpouring of hatred for Tim Walden.. The HIT: Brandi Wilson, I know not what will occur in our matchup later this evening. I may not walk out of this building with my title intact, but as you lie in bed later tonight with your new tax dependant, you will know damn well you have been in a fight with the most dangerous man in this company. I may not be able to use dangerous weapons like the whisk, wooden spatula, and the almighty dreaded MELON BALLER OF DEATH! Tim, you don't know what a melon baller can do to you? I assure you, Friday, YOU WILL FIND OUT! And then, THE HOLLANDAISE SAUCE! Boiled Walden with Hollandaise sauce..A RECIPE FOR VIOLENCE! The nearly delusional Trapezoid smiles, pulling the dreaded MELON BALLER out of his pocket! No doubt Walden is quaking in his boots as we speak, as Trapezoid grins sadistically. The HIT: First, I prepare Broiled Brandi with a side of turnbuckle, then I work on the other dishes for Friday. For, in the name of The Chef, I SHALL SAUTE THE ASSES OF ALL WHO OPPOSE ME THIS WEEK! Soon Tim, you shall learn the many ways that a melon baller can be used to hurt you. See, that's why I'm better than you, in the end. You look at a melon baller, and see a tool used to make fruit salad. I look at a melon baller, AND SEE AN IMPLEMENT OF DESTRUCTION! But, the salad I make will not be one of cantaloupes, watermelons, and honeydews. It will be one of Walden heads, Tommy legs, End arms, and Rush backs. A salad of carnage, blood, and evil...damn, I'm reading way too many odd books before bedtime. Trapezoid shakes his head before sheathing the melon baller. The HIT: The time is upon us. Brandi, you and I shall dance the dance of demise in mere moments. And then, with your newly won Television Title, or simply many bumps and bruises, you shall leave the arena, yet another victim of ME! OF ME! THE H! I! T! Trapezoid leaves the room, as we fade to melon removed with a melon baller. ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
