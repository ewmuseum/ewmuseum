Visiting Miya's house, or How to make JJ scream like a bitch.

5 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/05/02 *The shiny yellow and checkered cab comes to a stop. The gruff, but somewhat feminine driver looks back at his occupant.* Cabbie: We’re here. *We are shown a shot of where ‘here’ is. * *The Asylum Dungeon * Cabbie: That’ll be $10.50. Thanks and have a good day. *The cab drives off as the occupant has paid his fare, and exited the vehicle. As the yellow and checkered cab drives off, it runs a stop sign, causing it to get plowed by a tanker truck carrying gasoline. The explosion that ensues is felt for a few miles. The passenger that just exited the cab smiles as the after shock of the explosion dies down.* Boda: Should of gave him a tip... like STOP MEANS STOP. Oh well, time to go in and pay sis a visit *Boda steps forward, up to the door to the ‘Dungeon’. He knocks once, and enters. Inside, he sees the place his brother in law and sister call home. A run down old gym, rebuilt to resemble something of a living area. On the walls, Alexander has placed various pictures of his career, some cut outs of wrestling magazines and newspapers. Entering into the main area, the gym itself, or Dungeon as Alex has named it, Boda sees various equipment, used to exercise. A small, but crude ring is set up to the side. Boda calls out for his sister* NATALIE, you home? *There is no answer. Boda shrugs his shoulders, then walks through the gym, and into one of the many rooms off to the side. He opens the door, and we see a room painted in a hue of light, almost powder blue. Boda steps in, and looks at the crib in the corner. On the walls, borders of little bunnies and kittens. A mobile hangs over the crib. The little planes hanging from it move in with the slight breeze coming from the open window. Boda walks to the crib, and pushes the mobile gently.* Boda: The picture perfect family environment. *Before the former World Champ leaves the soon to be baby room, he takes a bear out of the bag he is carrying, and places it in the crib. The big man leaves the baby room, and walks into the center of the house/gym. He walks to another room, and it just happens to be the kitchen. Boda walks to the refrigerator, looking at a five picture collage, like the ones you get for 5 dollars at the mall, of Miya and Natalie. He takes the pics off the fridge, and holds it up to his face, to get a closer look.* Boda: Look at you, smiling, enjoying yourselves. Well, when next week is over, Natalie will enjoy a lot of time with you Miya. You and Nat and the baby, spending a lot of time together as I will continue my march back to total dominance in PCW. *Boda puts the pics back. Opening the fridge door, he sees a selection of caviar, champagne and bottle water, among other various, normal foods. Boda takes a salami from the meat drawer, and a cold brewski [which ever is Miya’s favourite], from the fridge. Finding a paring knife, Boda makes his way from the kitchen, to the next room over, the living room* Boda: Not a bad set up for a little Japo-American. The yen must be doing good. *Boda is talking about the big screen HDTV in the living room, the 100 gallon salt water fish tank and the all leather couches. Boda walks over, plops his 6’11 frame on the big couch, and plops his size 18 feet on the coffee table. Reaching for the remote, he turns on the tv, and turns the channel to ESPN.* Boda: AWE, the playoffs are on, sweet. *Sure enough, the Lakers vs San Antonio game is on. Boda pops the top on his beer, and cuts a chunk of salami off.* Boda: Man, these Lakers are ruthless. Sorta reminds me of.... me. I mean, Miya is the rest of the NBA WEST, and I am the LA Lakers. Dominate, stronger and a whole hell of a lot better. And, just like the Lakers will eliminate the Spurs from the playoffs, I shall do the same to Alex. Send him home packing, and waiting to see what comes next for him. *Boda sits up, as he hears a door opening up. He decides to wait for the ‘entering’ to ‘find’ him. Who knows, maybe it’s Miya, and this upcoming match can get started early. Alas, there are two voices heard, one female and the other male. Boda gets anxious, hoping it’s Natalie and Alex. As the voices get closer, Boda gets ready. Just as the living room door opens, Boda jumps up, and the screams of a woman are heard. We witness JJ run off, as Natalie stands there, looking at her brother.* Boda: Damn, he’s always been a sissy. *Natalie chuckles out loud. We hear JJ still screaming, as he slams a door behind him somewhere in the house.* Natalie: What are you doing here? You know if Alex finds you here..... Boda: I know... he’ll be angry... well, sis..... good. Besides.... your my sister, I came to check up on you, and see how you are doing. Natalie: Good, now you should go Chris. Boda: What, you don’t want me to visit with you? Besides.... where is Alex? Natalie: He had some endorsement deal to attend. Something about American Football. Boda: Football? *Boda starts to think what exactly a semi-popular pro wrestler would have to do with football. Perhaps it’s a co-sponsorship with a few of the football stars. Or, maybe... maybe he’s looking for a new career, a way to make some cash for him and Nat and my soon to be nephew. Oh well, since he wont be in a wrestling ring anymore, why not. This is America, land of opportunity. Home of the free.* Natalie: Dah, football. Gridiron players. Boda: Actually, it’s gridiron warriors. So, when you expecting him home? Natalie: He will............ AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH *Interrupting Natalie is a screaming JJ, who got very brave, for a second. He decided, in honor of his best friend, Miya, to try and attack the Big Bad Ameri-Czech Bad Ass, only to at the last second remember one thing.... Boda is a huge mother fucker, and well, JJ.... he’s a short little shit. So, again, JJ runs off like the little bitch Miya portrays him as. Boda gets a laugh from the former PCWer.* Boda: Still tries to be more than he is. So.... Natalie, I just wanted to come by, check on my sister, and not cause.... too much trouble. I can’t stay long any ways. I’ve got some personal business here in Anaheim. Got to scout some talent for my fed. Maybe, after Miya loses to me on Sunday..... maybe I’ll give him a shot in NTICW. Nah, on the other hand, I all ready got talented jobbers in the fed. Natalie: Why you talk bad about my husband? He is not bad, you know. Boda: Yeah, whatever..... listen, Nat.... got to run. See you later. *Boda gives his shorter sister a kiss on the forehead. As he leaves, he walks up behind a still frightened JJ. Boda leans down, and whispers something in JJ’s left ear.* Boda: JJ... do me a favour, and give Miya a message for me....... BOOOOOOOOOOOOO *JJ jumps, about 11 feet in the air, and takes off for the baby’s room, locking the door behind him. Boda scoots out the front door, making his way to the streets outside.*
