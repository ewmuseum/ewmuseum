Alliances Victory 101: The Krayzie Folks Cometh...Enter The Asylum

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.16.02(#2 PPV) “This is a waste of time just like that retard was a waste of space.” That would be the joyous and always caring voice of Rush Mason that greets us as the promo begins its fade in from utter black. As is customary in these promos, Rush sounds rather arrogant and much like the asshole that PCW fans know him to be. “Dawg…I think you gotta get a cavity search before you go in. That second voice is that of Ray Ray, Rush’s new manager and partner in crime, despite his obvious lack of immense smarts. The scene begins to fade in and we can see that both Rush and Ray Ray are in a large room in front of a giant steel door. All around them are people who seem to be normal citizens, as well as an abundance of people in white lab coats. Rush: Cavity search? As in, like….getting Boca-fied? Rush’s voice quivers at even the thought of such an act being perpetrated on himself. He looks up at Ray Ray, who nods and then gives a short shrug, indicating that he’s not exactly sure why things are like this. The two men just take a glance at each other, then take a step forward towards the steel door, where they’re greeted by a small white security guard, holding a metal detector. “Sirs, could you please stretch out your arms and put your feet shoulder length apart?” Both men comply, albeit a bit reluctantly. The man feels around their pants and from there goes up, making for a few severely awkward moments. He then speaks into a walkie-talkie and tries to put on a very tough voice. “Okay, we’re all clear.” Rush and Ray Ray step forward, at which point the giant steel door is opened, while the small security guard holds his blackjack in front of him menacingly, as he opens the door for a second to let both men through, then slams the door directly behind them as they enter a long white hallway. Ray Ray: Dawg….Why’d you come with me here? Rush continues to walk for a moment, examining the strange looking hallway and glancing around at his surroundings. He then turns to Ray Ray, and both men desist from walking. Rush: Quite simple, actually. Asylum won Alliances last year, so…. They begin to walk once again, neither saying anything as they reach another door with a buzzer at the front, at the end of the hallway. As Rush hits the buzzer, Ray Ray once again shows his rapier wit with a strange question. Ray Ray: So what? Rush mutters to himself, trying to ignore the question for a moment, but then gives in and goes into detail as to why he would be here. Ray Ray listens intently, trying to figure out exactly what connection last year’s Alliance winners, Asylum, would have with this strange place that the two men are at. Rush: Ray Ray…Asylum is another word for…. Rush looks up at Ray Ray, and realizing that he’s not talking with pure genius, per se, he decides to change his wording. Rush:…a crazy house. Like, this place. Ray Ray’s eyes light up with recognition of a familiar term, and he grins widely, finally getting something that Rush just said. The door then opens, and yet another person in a white lab coat opens the door for them, greeting them kindly. “Ah yes, Mr. Mason, Mr. Mbutka.” Rush turns to Ray Ray quickly, whispering under his breath to the huge man as the doctor walks off for a moment. Rush: Your last name is Mbutka? Ray Ray nods solemnly, looking very serious and speaking in a tone that begins as proud, but eventually fades back into Ray Ray’s slang and mispronounciation of words. Ray Ray: Yeah, that’s an African name….gotta keep it real. With another sigh from Rush, the doctor returns and addresses the two men. “I believe you, Randolph, are here for a visitation, while Rush is here just for your company?” Both men nod, and the doctor smiles at them, but Rush turns to Ray Ray and once again quietly addresses him. Rush: You’re telling me that you’re that big of a retard and a walking steretype, yet your name is Randolph Mbutka? Ray Ray looks around, as if his name is some sort of secret, then hisses back at Rush. Well, hisses isn’t perhaps the word, more like a dull roar that comes back at Rush. Ray Ray: The hell you yellin’ it for? What, you want me to get a cap popped in me? I can’t keep that pussy name, that name ain’t keepin’ it real. Rush lets out a soft guffaw at this, then just turns his head back towards the doctor, who hands each man a clipboard, and gives the reasoning for this very explicitly. “Now, these are liability forms. While our patients are rather mild, by signing these, you recognize every and all statement on that form, and give up your right to use legal action against us in the event of your injury and/or death.” Neither man really pays attention, and just signs their name quickly, then both hand their clipboards back to the doctor, who takes a quick glance at them, surprised by Ray Ray’s disgusting handwriting, if it can be called that even. It says either a blotchy “Ray Ray” or possible “Srav Sroy.” “Very unique handwriting you’ve got there, Mr. Mbutka.” Ray Ray nods and grins like an idiot, thinking that unique is in some way a synonym with good. He replies to the doctor with immense pride in shis voice. Ray Ray: I always used to get ‘C’s in handwriting. Good grades like that made my mama really happy. The doctor puts on a half-smile, and turns, handing off the clipboard to a young nurse who walks by, and he says a short something to her as the two men just wait for the doctor. He finally turns around and addresses the two men again. “Rush, you can come with me, and Ray Ray, this young nurse will show you to your patient.” With that, Rush follows the doctor, who first leads him into a large room with a glass window into a room that holds a large mass of people. These people are seemingly the inhabitants of this mental hospital, and the doctor begins to point out several of the more famous residents. “There’s the guy who thinks he’s L. Ron Hubbard.” Rush looks down at a man who is standing on top of a table, yelling something about Hubbard coming back to reclaim his throne, and Rush rolls his eyes, remarking something sarcastically to the doctor. Rush:Yeah….I know someone kinda like that. The doctor looks shocked, and replies with genuine feeling in his voice, while Rush just continues to stare at the mass of crazy people. “Well, is he getting care somewhere?” Rush’s face goes into somewhat of a sour contortion as his mouth twists sideways and then he turns to the doctor and explains, with a sigh, and as softly as possibly, trying to seem still credible. Rush: Err….he’s kinda my….well….he’s the guy who owns my contract….my boss. The doctors laughs loudly, then looks at Rush, and suddenly a look of shock comes across his face and the scene cuts back to Ray Ray standing with a woman in an open room. Ray Ray: Hey ma, how you doin’? The woman has a crazed look in her eye, and her hair is a mess, not surprisingly. She seems very jittery as she addresses the huge man, who dwarfs her and is apparently her son. “So, ya got the package I asked for, honey?” Ray Ray nods, and pulls out a needle from his pocket, and hands it to his mother. He looks around once, and his mother quickly walks away, but Ray Ray follows her trying to have a conversation. Ray Ray: Ma, how come you ain’t talked to me? I don’t think I was s’posed to give you that either… With that the scene goes back to Rush and the doctor, who seem to be having a friendly conversation, despite Rush’s ego interfering periodically. Rush: So, yeah, after that, I figured I’d wrestle and entertain the nimrods. The doctor nods, looking down at the room of the asylum’s inhabitants and notices a large group of them gathering around the door. “What the? OH MY GOODNESS! THEY’RE ESCAPING!” Rush’s eyes go huge and he looks around, as if already scared that the lunatics might’ve already infested the room. Rush: How? Oh dammit….retard’s in there, I bet he… Before Rush can speak though, the doctor is out in the hall, and spies the marchings crazies coming down the hall. He attempts to stop them but he is plowed over by the stampeding crazy people as Ray Ray comes waddling down the hall, narrowly avoiding the stampede. He reaches Rush and is panting loudly, as Rush looks down at him with an eyebrow raised. Rush: And….? Ray Ray tries to catch his breath, and achieves this as he steps into the room with Rush, letting out the answer in one quick burst. Ray Ray: I gave my mama a needle and she- Rush: You know what? I don’t care. The two begin to walk out, following the huge mass of insane people, but keeping their distance as they leave the asylum. “Dawg, you think they’ll get caught again? “Nah, probably not. You know what they say, “The Krayzie ones forever…”
