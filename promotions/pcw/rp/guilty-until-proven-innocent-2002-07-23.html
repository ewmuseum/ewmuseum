Guilty Until Proven Innocent

23 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd July 2002 Criminal City CourtBrooklyn, NYC23rd Of The 7th 200211:14amCase 106Murder-One The smell of old polished wood rises into the air as a soft, humming murmur quickly accompanies it. As the murmur continues and the smell begins to get stronger a rather annoying clicking sound chimes in with it all as shadows slowly dart across the almost golden brown floorboards beneath. As the humming continues everything begins to seem strangely familiar as the shadows, the ticking, the smell and the murmuring all begin to suddenly fall into place. It seems like something out of a movie yet it’s just one of those movies that can be remembered yet need a name for them to be recognized…. In other words wherever this place was, whatever this place was, it seemed strangely familiar and almost like a Television show rather than a movie. A very posh Television show at that…. BOOM~! BOOM~! BOOM~! Three rather loud and almost amplified strikes ring out and echo in the large hall as a woman’s voice can be heard in concession just as loud. 'Order In The Court!' Suddenly everything becomes clear as the polished wooden floors are that of a court room, the ticking is of a fan overhead and the murmuring is of the people who are witnessing the trial, along with the Jury panel and the Defendant etc. As the murmuring finally comes to a halt the judge who happens to look remarkably like television’s Judge Judy pushes her glasses up her nose and scans around the courtroom at the sea of faces before she adjusts herself in her high seated position and looks directly at the defendant….. Amidst the sea of faces are many of Latin decent, tanned skin, dark hair and dark eyes all, there is also a few whites and the occasional black here and there but majority of the courtroom are of Hispanic decent and all are dressed in either black suits or black dresses. But no matter what kind of background they were in the sea of witnesses all their eyes stared directly to the front of the courtroom, but not at the Judge sitting at the peak of the mountain, or at the bailiff guarding her by the bottom, but at the desk just to the right where two men stood in attention. Both of these men were well presented wearing shiny suits and their hair tied and slicked back with a black hair tie in place, but one of them stood out like a sore thumb….no it wasn’t for the fact that he was wearing a white suit but it was the fact that he was PCW’s own…Charlie Loc. No one in the courtroom seemed to impressed that a PCW Superstar was making a free appearance, perhaps the odd one or two were but no one else. Perhaps the fact they weren’t impressed had something to do with what happened at a certain event? Or maybe it was the fact that Charlie thought he was superior due to the fact he was Asian and wearing a white suit….. Whatever the case no one seemed to be happy with the two men standing at the front of the courtroom, no doubt one man was the lawyer and he was more than likely defending Charlie for some idiotic action he performed! 'Khiem Jung Loc, You are being trilled for the Murder of Juan Borro also known as Sunshine. Please let me remind you that this case is being monitored and everything you say will be taken into consideration!' The female Judge’s high pitched and annoying voice rings out through speakers somewhere in the rafters of the courtroom. As the noise of her whiny voice begins to fade out, footsteps can be heard as a black bailiff walks from his post at the foot of the Judge’s seat towards Charlie holding out a red book and then whispering something to Charlie as the Asian shakes his head before grimacing and placing his hand over its cover. The Bailiff begins to mumble something as Charlie reluctantly mumbles the same back to him as the whiny voice of the Judge cuts in through their chatter. 'You have no sworn on the Oath…Let me remind you that this is Judge Trudy’s Court and any misbehavior of any kind will not be tolerated!' The Lawyer standing next to Charlie who is also of Asian decent leans over and whispers something into his ear as Charlie continues to stare at Judge Trudy in the eye before he smirks and nods his head, agreeing with whatever his lawyer is saying. Finally the Bailiff turns and addresses the courtroom. 'All Rise! Case 106 Is Now Beginning, The Court Is Now In Order! Please Be Seated And Silent Until Told Otherwise' As the Bailiffs voice rings through the Courtroom, there is a sound of wavy clothing as everyone stands in unison before they sit back down. A few of the Hispanic men can be seen conversing through some silent language as they nod their heads and crane their necks to see Charlie at the front of the room. 'Khiem, we have evidence to believe that you are in fact the one who shot at Juan Borro. How do you plead?' The Judge’s voice trails off as she looks down on Charlie with her glasses. The lawyer leans over and whispers something into Charlie’s ear as he smirks again and replies, 'Not Guilty' in his smart-alec Asian tone. A few rather scary looking Hispanic men who are sitting down in the front aisle shake their heads and glare rather viscously towards Charlie at the stands. The Judge doesn’t seem too shocked as she takes a look at the documents in front of her and asks her next question. 'Well then, it would appear that we have some Video Evidence of you in-fact pulling a 9mm Pistol to Mr. Borro’s head, just moments before his death….perhaps we should show you that and ask you the same question again! How does that sound Khiem?' The Judge basically tries to prove Charlie wrong and get things over and done with when his Lawyer raises his hand. 'Your Honor, I am Chow Tran, Master Loc’s attorney and I have studied this Video Evidence and if you play it, you will notice that Master Loc only pull the weapon in self-defence! He also never have his grip tight on the trigger!' The Lawyer tries to sound all posh but has trouble speaking the English language fluently as the Judge pretends to understand. Judge Trudy can be seen nodding her head as the Bailiff pushes a button behind him and a screen opens up on the lecture in front of the Judge’s stand. Familiar footage of the Juan Borro and Charlie Loc confrontation can be seen being played as the Judge pauses it on the blackout. 'I think I have seen enough of this Evidence! Do you think you’re some big shot proving me wrong Mr Tran? You may have been able to prove that, but how is it that the same weapon used in the murder was found to be the one that Khiem is seen clasping in that footage!' The Judge seems to become very annoyed and raises her voice at the end of every sentence. Charlie’s attorney is about to speak when Charlie cuts-in in front of him. 'Because some stupid White Boy try to frame me that why!' A gasp can be heard coming from the witnesses in the courtroom as Judge Trudy begins to go red with rage and Charlie tenses his fists at his side as his Lawyer slaps his forehead, nearly knocking his glasses off of his face. 'THERE WILL BE NO MORE OUTBREAKS LIKE THAT IN MY COURTROOM DO YOU UNDERSTAND?! OR I WILL SEND YOU TO JAIL AND MAKE THEM THROW AWAY THE KEY!' The Judge screams and threatens Charlie who smiles and wipes a drop of sweat from his brow. 'This all because I Asian….isn’t it?' Charlie tilts his head onto the side and grits his teeth whilst looking at the Judge and making his presence felt. Judge Trudy stares back at him and replies in a matter-of-fact tone of voice. 'This Court has nothing to do with any Racial Issues! You think because you’re of a different race you deserve to be treated different? ARE YOU EVEN LEGAL IN THIS COUNTRY?!' Charlie shakes his head as all the eyes in the courtroom dart back and forth as the two continue to argue, the Judge with the power and Loc with the defiance. Charlie opens his mouth and just as the words come out his Lawyer, Chow Tran holds a bunch of papers in front of his face and begins to quickly say. 'I have them here!' Judge Trudy shakes her head as the Bailiff looks up before his feet CLUNK~! On the floorboards as he snatches the documents off of Tran before walking back and handing them up to the Judge above him. She looks down on them with disgust through her spectacles. 'Well it would seem that you do have the legal documents to be in this country Khiem….however that’s just saved you from one fate! Now you’ve got a few more to chose from! Your Criminal record is very impressive Khiem, or do you prefer to be called Charlie?' The Judge speaks like a granny and puts a cooing voice on at the end of the sentence. Just as Charlie’s mouth opens his Lawyer quickly says 'Khiem' as she looks back down at the papers and then looks back up towards Charlie. 'It would also seem you have served your time for these offenses. Car Jacking, Fighting, Armed Robbery, if only you had have got away with these….then I could’ve locked you up for longer than I already am!' The Judge shakes her head as she adjusts her neck and glasses. Charlie grits his teeth and clenches his fists harder at his sides as his Lawyer looks confused and pipes in before Charlie can talk. 'What you mean longer than he already is?' Tran begins to look at Charlie and gets a little hot under the collar as he wipes a drop of sweat away from his brow as he adjusts his tie and Trudy smiles and begins to speak. 'He’s insulted a Judge and he’s Murdered someone, I don’t think it takes a genius to see that!' Her voice begins to trail off. Tran begins to fling through papers on the table in front of him before grasping two and holding them high into the air before yelling. 'He has gun permit! He has permit!' Once again the Bailiff walks over and grabs the documents before the Judge snatches them off of the Bailiff as she reads them and shakes her head in disgust once more. This time the crowd are anxious to see what is happening. 'He may have a license, but it doesn’t excuse the fact that bullets were fired from his gun with his finger prints on the handle and….' Her voice trails off as footsteps can be heard from the back of the courtroom. Everyone turns to see who is walking down the aisle as the Judge shakes her head and chuckles whilst Charlie sees the man and bows his head in shame. His Lawyer simply nods his head in respect as everyone’s eyes follow this man who begins to speak. 'But there is not enough evidence to incriminate Master Loc, therefore bail may be granted and I think sufficient money can be found in this briefcase' The man shakes his head at Charlie before he opens the gate and then steps over the floorboards towards the Judge. The Bailiff reaches down for his baton as the man stops in his tracks holding up the briefcase before opening it and showing her a sea of shiny green money which matches the shiny black suit and slicked back black hair. The reflection of the money gleams in both his glasses and Judge Trudy’s as he smiles. The Bailiff looks up towards the Judge who shakes her head before picking up her gavel and BOOM~! BOOM~! BOOM~! The knocks sound over the courtroom. 'Khiem Jung Loc has been acquitted of any charges against him and been granted bail. I am putting him on a 4month Good Behavior Bond, if this is broken he will be imprisoned….Court dismissed' The Judge’s voice wavers off as she is handed the brief case and she disappears into her chambers behind her bench, clearly throwing away the case for money. The man turns around to face Charlie who is still hanging his head in shame and his Lawyer who nods and bows before packing up his equipment and exiting as the people begin to pile out from the rows, most Hispanics cracking their knuckles and shaking their heads. As Charlie finally looks up the man is revealed to be none other than fellow PCW Superstar 'Legend Inc.' aka Tom Lexian, as he shakes his head in disgust at Charlie. 'I just gave that Judge half a million to keep you out of lock up! You’re mother would murder you if she knew what you get up too! You may be my cousin Charlie, but let me assure you….this is the last time! You owe me big!' Tom finishes his sentence as Charlie nods. 'It ok….I pay you back, you my blood and I owe you a debt. I sorry for doing this but….' Charlie gets cut off by Tom. 'It’s too late for that Charlie, just forget this ever happened and get into line because I mean it when I say that this is the last time!' Lexian glares at Loc before opening the gate as he exits down the aisle towards the outside area. Charlie gets up and adjusts his white suit before his feet begin to slap against the wooden floor boards and he exist closely behind his newly revealed savior and cousin. Everything becomes as it was in the beginning. The ticking of the fan, the shadows being cast around on the floor and the smell of polished wood rises. Somehow the scene looks like a dead Television set as all its life is simply drained out of it. How could one man be acquitted of taking another man’s life? Money is all powerful, or perhaps it’s greed! Either way, a man with money and power is someone to watch out for, as Charlie Loc has somehow proven once again.
