Extreme Shopping w/ J-Core

12 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/12/01 The scene opens up on a dark, back alleyway. The alley isn't particularly disgusting, but it is definitely not a first-rate place to be. Horns, screeching cars, yelling people can all be heard, signs of a big city. The camera pans outwards and we can see a sign in front of a small place in the alley. There is a glowing sign that flickers on and off that says 'Mike's Hardcore Shop.' Seems like a strange place. Certainly not a place that a respectable person would be caught. Well, you'd be surprised where you find respectable people. Onto the screen walk two members of PCW's newest stable, the Malice Crew, Jason Core and The Rush. Alongside Rush is his new girlfriend, Melanie. Jason is wearing a dark blue CWS t-shirt, loose khakis, and a pair of Vans. Rush, meanwhile, is the almost total opposite, wearing a dark fleece Mecca sweater with dark blue baggy jeans and white T-Macs. Melanie is wearing a simple outfit, a tight pink shirt complimenting her chest, tight jeans, and pink Timberland boots. The three walk up to the sign and Rush looks up at the sign marking the downtrodden establishment. Rush: Damn, for a place that has helped thousands of stars, this place sure is a ratass joint. You'd think they could afford a new light. Core: Rush, you sure we need to do this. I mean, I know we're fighting for the Extreme title, but do we really need to check this store out? Rush: J-Core, you think that I wanna be here? The Crunk as Hell God doesn't need to be here, but we are extending our hands to the people, because that's what Malice is all about: the people. Core: I know that, but what does that have to do with me and you being down here? Melanie: He's afraid to say that he can't remember how to fight a hardcore match, since the last time he held a weapon was in Black Attack. Rush: That's not true at all, my crunk goddess! Core: Hehe, Rush, looks like all the other Gods have titles, you want yours? Rush: You two think your very funny don't you? The real reason that we're here is so that I can find a weapon for with to beat the amazing J-Core tonight for that title of yours. Core: No, the belt has been stained enough with LeBlanc and Hank~! holding it, we don't need the Greatest Jobber Alive to hold it too. Core grins widely at Rush, who throws a fake punch at him, which J-Core ducks and walks laughing into the building. Jason opens a large steel door, and slams it shut behind him, after which he hears a the door's lock click. Rush: That's weird. Why would a store want to lock customers out? Core: I don't know. Maybe they get sick of angry black men coming in talking about how crunk they are. Rush swings once again with a fake punch at Core, who ducks again and chuckles to himself. Melanie: Guys, are you sure this place is still in business? The two stop their offbeat shenanigans for a moment and look around the room, to see no one and pretty much nothing in the room, except for some shelves, the contents of which cannot be seen from their current place. Rush: Wow, this place is more dead than Drake Maxwell's parents. Except this place doesn't whine and bitch about it. Jason: Is your jobber rant finished, Rush? We've got to see if you remember how to fight or does being in a stable bring back those horrible memories? Rush: Okay, sure. Make fun of the millitant jobber stable. Go ahead and laugh it up. However, if you saw, that's the same jobber who defeated Xavier Kannon last week. Melanie: *cough* Krayzie*cough* Rush: What's that? Melanie: Oh nothing, I just said this room is hazy. Jason: Hey, there's some stuff on the wall over there. The three walk over to the far wall, and they stop to see that they are, in fact, in the right place. Weapons are mounted on the wall, Rush's eyes growing wide like a child's at the sight of all of them. The first weapon he picks up is a black bull whip. Rush: The hell is this thing? Core: Looks like a bull whip, though I wouldn't really say that it would do a lot of damage in a hardcore match. Rush: I don't think I want that as a weapon of choice. That might be suitable for a woman. Hey, speaking of women, you see that re-decorating that I gave Ty? Core: Hell yes. That man is the reason that I donate to AIDS charities. If AIDS patients turn out like that, then think of the horror. Thousands of Tyler Nelson's walking around in Africa. Rush: Hey, you know what I just noticed? The only weapons on this wall are whips. They're all black leather too. Core: Heh, you'd think that we were gonna be reamed by Steven Smith, dominatrix style. Voice: Not Stevie! A high squeaky voice resounds through the blank room. Rush, cringing, turns around to see the most feared man in wrestling: Boca del Inferno. Rush: BOCA?!?! Core: BOCA?!?! Jeff Marx: HANK~! HANK~! I SAY! Koala: CRUNK~!?!?! Getting back to our scene, now the two men, gulping in fear, and Melanie ignoring them, not scared at all of the horny homosexual, edge towards the door. Boca: C'mon boys, we can have some fun before you go. I know you like it hardcore, J-Core. The two men race out of the store, with Melanie walking behind, but then again, she doesn't have to worry about being violated by Boca. Boca walks over to the whips and begins to examine them as the scene, fortuneatly, fades to black.
