I Really Do Hate A LOT Of People Right Now...

3 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/04/02 “THIS IS NOT FAIR! I AM XAVIER KANNON! I AM THE NEXT CONTINENTAL CHAMPION!!!” Our shot opens up on Xavier Kannon walking out of the America West Arena in Phoenix, the site of Turmoil, where interim President Duke Williams officially banned Scientology after making Crunk the official religion of PCW. Xavier : “AND CRUNK? WHAT THE FUCK IS CRUNK? I THOUGHT EATING DEEP FRIED CHICKEN WAS THE BLACK RELIGION AND THEN I FIND OUT ABOUT CRUNK? WHO ARE THESE CRUNKERS, OR CRUNKETS, OR...WHY AM I EVEN MENTIONING THEM!” He looks up to the sky, breathing deeply and trying to calm himself down after a stressful and physical night. The Scientologist is wearing a Black hooded sweatshirt with “XK” in Purple and Green flames written across the chest and “Gold Patron Meritorious” on the back in Purple. With it he has a pair of Black worker-cut jeans with Purple and Green embroidery down the left leg leading towards his Black, Purple and Grey Skechers. His long Auburn hair is tied back tightly and he carries a Black sports bag. Xavier : “Well Duke can do what he likes, everyone knows that *Religious Reference Deleted* is the official Religion of PCW...NOT CRUNK! How many times will I let the Rush try and drag my career down? First off I carry him to he NTICW Tag Titles and now he uses *Religious Reference Deleted*...MY RELIGION, to try and get CRUNK some exposure!” He makes his way towards Eleanor’s Black Jeep Cherokee, slinging his bag over his shoulder. His Green eyes are burning with fury and his face is contorted into one of pure rage. “Hey...Would you...Like...Wait for me?” From the arena exit trundles his valet Eleanor Hall, herself not too happy with the night's events after being manhandled by Brock Newbludd as she attempted to help out her man. Eleanor : “We should...Like...Quit or something. Or sue them, or go on strike, or kil-” The agitated Xavier snaps at his valet’s preppyness, turning back to her and yelling at the top of his lungs. Xavier : “WOULD YOU SHUT UP WITH YOUR WHINING FOR ONE MINUTE? I JUST FOUND OUT THAT *Religious Reference Deleted* HAS BEEN BANNED! MY FAITH! THE THING THAT MAKES ME SUPERIOR TO THE REST OF THE EARTH’S PATHETIC POPULOUS...BANNED! BY A WHISKY SWILLING, STRAW CHEWING HICK!!!” She drops her head, a tear running down her cheek as she is visibly upset by Xavier’s angry outburst, releasing some of the anger that built up within the Scientologist during Turmoil. She screams back at him through her sobbing, in an even whinier voice than usual. Eleanor : “WELL IT ISN’T MY FAULT YOUR CRAPPY LITTLE RELIGION GOT BANNED SO DON’T YELL AT ME!!!” She stamps her feet and rubs the tears from under her eyes, smearing a Black smudge under each eye where he mascara has run and she sniffs back the tears. Eleanor : “I DIDN’T TELL DUKE TO DO IT! I TRIED TO HELP YOU WIN AND THEN BROCK GRABBED MY HAIR WITH HIS SWEATY HANDS AND I STILL HAVEN’T BEEN ABLE TO WASH IT YET AND THEN YOU GO AND MAKE IT WORSE BY YELLING AT ME!” She struggles to scream through the tears, making Xavier look guilty and the mask of rage slides off his face as he walks over to his upset valet who has dumped her bags on the floor in protest at his harsh words. Xavier : “Ahhhhh...Don’t cry my little Poon Puppy...I’m sorry.” He hugs his sobbing valet who is choking back the tears while trying to speak in a very upset tone of voice. She is wearing a skinny fit Pink Snapple~! t-shirt with Silver lettering across her 32B chest. With his she has a pair of faded Blue hipster jeans and a rainbow stripe fabric belt. Her long, wavy Brown hair with Blond highlights is un-tied yet still falls neatly down her back. Eleanor : “I want you to hit him...HIT HIM......Then I want you to kick him so his stupid Cowboy Hat falls off then jump on his head so he can never call me a whore or a skank or a slut again....Then I want Brock killed for touching me...Then I want you to put Jeremy in hospital again so Jessica gets all upset and then kills herself!” She hugs him tightly as her tears stop during her list of demands that she speaks out spitefully before sniffing back the last of her tears and wiping other puffy Red eyes. As Xavier releases his tight hold and puts his valet back down he grins with glee at Eleanor’s words. Xavier : “Don’t worry....I may not be recognised by PCW but I still *Religious Reference Deleted* behind me, and with the words of L Ron Hubbard ringing true in my ears I shall strike back at all those who oppress us....Jeremy Howard, Jessica, Duke Williams and his pathetic little Malice Crew...I have more than just *Religious Reference Deleted* going for me as they will find out...” He picks up Ellie~! bags along with his own and puts his spare arm around hr shoulder as they walk towards her Black Cherokee that is parked about 50 metres away from the back entrance. Xavier : “I have shown Jeremy what happens when he oppresses me and insults you...He was pulling bits of Snapple~! bottle out of himself for days...After he regained consciousness that was! Then you my little Shag Squirrel, you’ll have your chance to finally get your hands on Jessica an-” His mandate is cut off by Ellie~! spiteful voice, spurred on by her disdain for the mother of Jeremy Howard’s child who has been insulting the preppy valet ever since Xavier turned his back on Jeremy at the Turmoil after March Madness. Eleanor : “AND RIP ALL OF HER BLEACHED BLOND HAIR OUT BEFORE SCRATCHING HER EYES OUT SO SHE CAN’T SEE HER UGLY LITTLE BRAT OF A KID OR HER BRAIN DAMAGED HUSBAND THEN I’LL SCRATCH HER FACE SO THAT HOWARD CAN’T EVEN BARE TO LOOK AT HER!” The Gold Patron Meritorious grins evilly at his valet’s evil intentions towards Jessica as they get to the jeep and the couple hop in, with Xavier’s dumping the backs in the back on his way to the driver’s side. Xavier : “Lets see what Scientology’s number one radio station has to say about this travesty of justice....” He starts the engine and engine and the sounds of the Sci’n’Tolly G show blast out of the speakers, already covering the travesty that has just happened. Sci : “So he decreed that *Religious Reference Deleted* was banned from PCW and rather than *Religious Reference Deleted* being the number one religion in PCW just like *Religious Reference Deleted* is in the world they made Crunk the number one religion, the religion most know for it’s oppression of *Religious Reference Deleted*!” The Gold Patron Meritorious nods along to the broadcast, glad that Scientological radio is picking up on his plight. Eleanor : “Wow, they are saying *Religious Reference Deleted* a lot even for a radio station dedicated to *Religious Reference Deleted*!” Kannon pulls out of his space and navigates the parking lot to exit the arena complex. Tolly G : “This has been a black day for *Religious Reference Deleted* as one of *Religious Reference Deleted* most recognisable members has been banned from even mentioning *Religious Reference Deleted* on television due to the outlawing of *Religious Reference Deleted*.” Sci : “That is true...Duke Williams has screwed *Religious Reference Deleted*“ He drives around the arena and towards the exit but his path is blocked by a rejoicing crowd all chanting in unison as they leap in joy amongst turned over cars and burning trash cans. “CRUNK! CRUNK! CRUNK! CRUNK!” In the car Xavier sighs at the site as he turns the radio off and looks upon the scene of lawlessness in front of him. Ellie~! sneers at the celebrating crowd, looking none too happy at having her path to their hotel room, and the shower blocked. Eleanor : “ARGHHHHHHHH!!!! I WANT A SHOWER!” She opens her door and jumps out, landing awkwardly on her high heel before correcting her walk as she approaches the crowd who turn to look at her in silence. Xavier leans over to her open door and shots out after her. Xavier : “Um..Ellie~! they could be dangerous!” Eleanor : “What-EVER!“ She looks at the various members of the mob who look back at her, then amongst each other before chuckling. Eleanor : “Um...What is like, SO funny? I have had a STRESSFUL night and I would like a SHOWER, look it up, so if you would kindly MOVE then I will be a happy Ellie~!” The leader of the mob, wearing one of Rush’s “Greatest man Alive” t-shirts looks her up and down to her disgust, grinning at he strange girl. Man : “Now why dontcha go runnin’ back to ol’ Whity in yo car Snowflake!” They all laugh at the man words to Ellie’s obvious annoyance and as usual, along with the foot stamping comes the screaming. Eleanor : “FINE! MY BOYFRIEND IS A WRESTLER AND HE COULD KICK ALL OF YOUR ASSES.....AT ONCE.....SO THERE!!!” A few of the men look back at her with raised eyebrows, not believing her hot-headed boasts. Eleanor : “FINE! If you don’t believe me I’ll go and get him!” Man : “Shame, I coulda done wit’ me gettin’ a White ho!” She turns back around and sneers at him. Eleanor : “Crawl into a hole and die!” She hops back into the Jeep and points at the crowd while looking at Xavier with her forceful pale Blue eyes. Eleanor : “I HATE THEM! GO AND KICK THEIR ASSES!!!” Funnily enough the Scientologist doesn’t seem to eager to carry out Eleanor’s demand, especially as a lot in the crowd tower over his 6’2 and far outweigh his 226 pounds. Xavier : “Um...We *Religious Reference Deleted* believe in peaceful protest, if they want to ban *Religious Reference Deleted* and replace it with Crunk then so be it, but I and *Religious Reference Deleted* have nothing to prove to those people.....Who have baseball bats...And are walking towards the Jeep....With the bats....” Eleanor : “With.......The Bats!” He slams it into reverse and screeches backwards into the parking lot before jolting back into drive and driving around the mob, cutting across the flower beds and the sidewalk to dart into the traffic on the main road which swerves to avoid the Cherokee cutting across it. “CRUNK IS VICTORIOUS! THE WHITIES ARE RUNNIN’! SEE YA SNOWFLAKES!” Back in the car the 1st couple of Scientology do not look too happy about the way things are working out in PCW heading into Spring Fling. Xavier : “I HAVE HAD ENOUGH! ENOUGH OF DUKE OPPRESSING *Religious Reference Deleted*! ENOUGH OF JEREMY HOWARD STILL BREATHING! I will not rest, I will get *Religious Reference Deleted* back at PCW’s number one religion before they get petty and start replacing *Religious Reference Deleted* with something like Religious Reference Deleted!” The shot fades out on the Jeep speeding back to Xavi and Ellie~!’s hotel where the couple will begin their fight back against the tyrannical PCW and Jeremy Howard. “Religious.....Reference......Deleted!”
