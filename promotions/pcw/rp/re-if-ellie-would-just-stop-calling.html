Re: If Ellie~! Would Just Stop Calling...

Date: By Someone Who Enjoys Fried Chicken, Gritz, and Watermelon Our scene opens up on the small interior cabin of a very small plane. The whirring outside signals the beginning of the engines firing up and the passengers inside are a limited number, four people: Rush Mason, a young Arab man, an elderly white woman, and an important-looking businessman. The door to the cockpit is of course closed, following the safety rules in our post 9/11 world. Imitating a cockpit voice, Rush breaks the plane’s silence. Rush: Lady and Gentlemen, we will be crashing into the nearby Atlantic Ocean in approximately 27 minutes, so keep those seat belts fastened and face your nice cold death like men. The elderly lady just looks on, mouth stuck in a gasp and look of disapproval, while the businessman nervously twitches his headset, and the younger Arab man just continues to read his book, chuckling a bit at Rush’s mock announcement. Fortunately, there is no stewardess (yes, a stewardess, fuck being politically correct) to actually scold Rush, the only two staffers are the pilot and co-pilot, sitting up in the cockpit, or at least we hope so. Zelda: Young man, what is your problem with life? Why can’t you just enjoy things as they come and not worry so much? Rush looks at the old lady with a raised eyebrow and a twisted mouth, a look of questioning and some disagreement. Rush: Listen lady, if you had to put up with the same shit I have to put up with every day then- The older lady cuts him off, sounding snide in her reply to him. Zelda: And exactly what do you have to put up with every day? Rush thinks in his head, trying to stall anyway, but quickly realizes an idea and blurts out loudly his thoughts to Zelda. Rush: I have to put with amazing crap. That’s part of being famous and being a professional wrestler, you get a whole lot of shit to put up with and you have to deal with it. Zelda: Kind of like the way you have to cope with losing bladder control at old age? Rush blinks his eyes, his face completely devoid of any emotion, trying desperately to keep a straight face and replies in an almost cracking voice. Rush: Umm…welll…errr? Zelda: You still haven’t told me anything that you have to put up with; so far I’ve heard some jive talk about being famous but nothing to back it up. Rush turns away towards the window for a moment and mocks the elderly lady’s tone to himself silently. He then turns back towards her, delivering his reply in a very proud fashion. Rush: First off, there’re the countless calls from Eleanor Hall. She calls me every day, it hurts my ear with every preppy word through the phone. He picks up a mock telephone, made of his last two fingers, and re-enacts one of these phone calls. Rush: Hello? Who’s there? He then changes his voice to a high-sounding squeaky screech, sounding surprisingly like Ms. Hall herself. “Ellie~!”: Like Rush, Xavi is SO off with Tyler again. Rush: Umm…that’s great Ellie~! Is there anything else you need? “Ellie~!”: Rush, can’t you like come over. I’m like SO lonely without my bitch of a boyfriend around. Rush:Bitch..of a…boyfriend. I mean, I guess so, but only this once… Rush then hangs up the mock phone and puts on a visibly insincere smile for Zelda, who just shrugs the look off. Zelda: That’s not that tough. Some little cookie wants in your pants, why’s that so hard? Rush bites his lip quickly, thinking of some other way in which his life is hard, and then puts up a finger as he gets another idea. Rush: See, this Friday I have to fight three of the toughest competitors, okay maybe just Big Daveylicious, Jackie Chan, and El Diablo, but they are competitors. Somewhere, Byron Calver sneaks into Rush’s mind, reminding him that Violence Jack is actually not the same person as Jackie Chan. Rush: For real? Byron: For realzzzzies! Byron’s conscience then slips out of Rush’s mind, and Rush goes back to the matter at hand, arguing with an old lady. Zelda: Why’s that so tough? You have to fake fight against other guys, each one smaller than you? Rush holds up a finger, and thinks while for some reason the Jeopardy music plays as Rush thinks. Finally, he exclaims his reason out loud. Rush: Okay, wrestling is like SO real. I mean, wrestling is real. First, we’ve got Big Daveylicious. Arguably an annoying teenage boy at heart, he still presents…another body in the ring that could very well be used as a weapon, or even pull off a wrestling move, if he ever could boot up the small brain that he has. Zelda: That’s one person, isn’t there two more? Rush snips back at the elderly lady quickly. Rush: Damn, bitch, hold on. I mean, just hold on a second. Then there’s Jackie …umm…Violence Jack. I’ve beaten him before to but his bald head could be used as a reflector of light to burn a whole through my perfectly chiseled abs. Wow, that sounded gayer than I thought. Now, the elderly woman has passed off into sleep, as many of us would if we had to listen to anything about Violence Jack or Big Daveylicious, but Rush continues to speak, albeit a bit softer. Rush: Finally there’s El Taco Bello. Wait a second, why am I bothering with a guy who lowered himself lower than even Xavier Kannon gets on Nelson’s floor? Didn’t he feud with Aymin? He begins to laugh loudly and then his laugh fades into a chuckle, Rush dismissing anyone who would feud with that waste of hair and flesh. He then looks over at the businessman, who is now shaking his hands so hard that his book is vibrating in his hands. Rush: You gonna be okay there buddy? The man looks over, glancing around him and at every part of the plane. He then looks at Rush and yells straight across the room, trying to sound calm. Businessman: THERE’S NOT FUCKING CRACK HIDDEN IN MY ASS OKAY?! Rush looks down, biting his lip in wonderment again. He then looks away, out the window, where somewhere down in the world, lies someone who actually cares about Big Daveylicious. Scary though, huh?
