Behind the Ego of Jason Snow: Family and Relationships, Chapter 1 - Krista

Date: not entered (OOC Note: Again, this RP was originally written for RPG3 but it is major in the development of Snow's character, so I think it's important that those of you, if any of you, who follow Jason Snow should get an opportunity to see it. It's a bit of a stretch from my usual style, if you hadn't noticed.) The room is elegantly laced in pink - fitting for the fragile little girl. She lies sleeping in her crib, her hands almost touching either side of her face, under the watchful eye of her heavy-hearted father. He couldn’t keep up the act. He couldn’t strut around Los Angeles, acting as if everything were fine. He caught a flight to Canada the morning after his battle with Wrestling God 3 favorite, Eli Flair, and now finds himself right where he belongs - staring down at his tiny creation, dressed in her pink cotton sleeping suit. For an instant, he feels lucky - the feeling is soon stripped when he remembers that there is a room next door where the walls are lined with blue. He looks so calm, leaning on the wall of his daughter’s crib, staring down through suddenly gentle, yet heartbroken eyes. He resembles a lion with his blond, ungroomed, beard and matching long hair - keeping watch over his child in case of predators. There are no predators, he realizes - at least, none that he can defend her against. He already learned that this week. “Jason?” a meek voice breaks his concentration and subconscious guilt. She used his first name, so she must be close, he thinks. When he turns around, he finds his twenty year-old sister-in-law, Krista, leaning on the open doorway. “I didn’t realize anyone was here,” Snow replies weakly. “You think my sister’s going to leave you alone with an infant for the night?” Krista giggles, “We all know better than that, Jay.” Jay - he hates when she calls him that, but knows that nothing she says will stop her. “She still at your grandmother’s?” Snow asks, without smiling despite Krista’s attempt. She nods, slowly approaching the lion, unsure of his current emotional state. Snow figured that’s where his wife had gone, despite their recent lack of communication. He knew that she wouldn’t flee to her own parents’ home. “She’s probably going to stay up there for a while,” Krista sighs, “With you being on the road so much - she can’t really be alone right now, Jason.” He knows what she’s trying to do. She’s trying to guilt him into dropping out of Wrestling God 3 so he’ll be able to stay home with his wife. Truth-be-told, his wife does need him right now - he knows. He understands how traumatic the situation has been for her, but he doesn’t think she understands that he’s going through the same thing - the very same trauma. “I know that, Kris,” he sighs, turning back to his sleeping child. “Ok,” Krista says lightly before joining him at the crib, reaching in, and stroking the sleeping child’s cheek. She knows that Snow’s actions are unpredictable - they’ve always been unpredictable. She remembers well the time she visited him in prison after he’d brutally beaten a man who his wife (estranged wife at the time) had dated. She knows that even though Snow realizes how important his support is to his wife, the call of the wrestling ring is often too strong for him to resist. Snow, to the wrestling world, is an egomaniac, constantly looking for things to feed his confidence. Behind the scenes, we see that the character that Snow has built isn’t so far fetched from his real life - he craves glory and the praise of others. It fuels him. Krista knows all of this, and the knowledge leaves her sceptical that her brother-in-law truly will stay for more than a day or two. She doesn’t question his feelings for his daughter or his wife, but she understands that Snow is addicted to the fame. He used to be an alcoholic - now he’s a workaholic. But he doesn’t work for money anymore, he doesn’t need it, now he works for the fame. He loves seeing his face on the front page of magazines, in the newspaper, on television... yet all this time he has carefully protected his wife from the same things that he loves so much - even her name has yet to be leaked in the presence of a reporter, a television camera, or even a microphone. She’s the ghost that drives the “Killin’ Villain,” as Snow usually likes to refer to himself, at least, when he’s not forced to face reality. “You staying the night?” he asks, looking down at her on his right. “Of course,” she replies, still stroking his daughter’s soft cheek, “Someone’s going to have to make breakfast - I can’t have you taking Haley to Taco Bell.” She laughs at her own joke. The truth of the matter is, Snow probably would have ended up buying food for himself and the young girl - he’s horrendous in the kitchen. “Is the guest room ready?” he asks, as if offering to make it ready if it’s not - she knows better. “It will be in a few minutes,” she answers before adding, “Listen, I’m going to take the baby monitor - you need your sleep. I don’t think you’ve slept since...” Her voice trails off. She’s right. Almost right, anyway - there were those few hours on the plane, and a couple of minutes on the sofa while watching Leno. “It’s ok,” Snow says, his voice sounding a little more enthused than it has in days, “I think I’d like to know if she cries.” “I won’t take no for an answer,” his sister-in-law says with serious eyes. A moment later, she frowns, “Jason - look at you. You’re pale, I think you’re losing weight, and God knows you haven’t shaved in weeks - just get some sleep. You’ll feel better.” Snow sighs and nods - she has a point. A couple of hours sleep to be followed by a hot shower would feel pretty good to him right now... Troubled days bring peaceful dreams - too peaceful. Dreams can be so good that when you wake up, you get slapped in the face with the nightmare called reality. Snow wakes up the next morning in that groggy state between dream and reality - wondering if it would be worth it to just slip back into his slumber and spend the rest of the day outside of reality. He’s still wearing his clothes - just black leather pants and a plain white t-shirt; He must have fallen asleep quickly. The first thought that strikes him is Haley - there’s no sound coming from the kitchen, which is just down the hall, so she must still be in her crib. Before another thought can cross his mind, Snow jumps out of his still-made bed and heads for the stairs. He finds the tiny wonder just as he thought he would, sleeping soundly in the early hours of the morning. The sun has just begun to rise, and the light that pours in through the lightly open blinds gently rests itself on the face of the tiny girl. Snow is more thankful for her now than ever. Mark Malloy’s last words, “Just be thankful for the one you have,” are more meaningful now than ever. Still, he can’t shake the thought of the blue room next door. “He’s not up yet, just relax,” a voice suddenly breaks the silence. Snow looks around for the source, but unless his ten month-old daughter has suddenly learned to talk, it remains a mystery. “I think it’s best to be safe,” comes a reply - a male voice. Snow’s eyes narrow as he continues to look for the source of the conversation. “Just relax,” a female voice - Krista’s voice - says, “What can he do, anyway?” Snow finds the source of the conversation - the baby monitor that sits atop his daughter’s miniature white dresser. A moment later, he puts it all together. Krista took the wrong baby monitor to bed - a bed that she’s sharing with a man! Snow bursts out of his daughter’s room, clenching the baby monitor tightly in his right hand, and storms down the vacant hallway to the guest room. “Did you hear something?” asks the male voice on the monitor just a moment before the locked guest room door is booted in by the raging lion. Krista lies half beneath the covers, with her exposed half dressed in a baggy sweatshirt. Her eyes bulge at the fierce intruder, and she becomes overwhelmed with anger. “What the hell are you doing!?” she demands, making sure that the necessary parts of her body are covered by either the sweatshirt or the blankets. “What am I doing!?” Snow rages, “What the hell are you doing!?” He’s always been protective of the young girl. From the moment he met her, his instinct was to protect her. “I think I’d better leave,” says the young man next to her, now sitting up in fear of the monster in the doorway. He’s not a small young man, standing over six feet and weighing nearly two hundred pounds, but in the shadow of Snow, who outweighs him by at least thirty pounds and a beard, he is a boy beneath a man. “Oh,” Snow nods, his wild eyes raging, “You’re leaving alright - you know you’re fucking leaving!” The lion steps in and grabs the young man, who’s wearing only a pair of pajama-pants and a tank top, by his curly reddish brown hair, picking him up from the bed and tossing him forward toward the doorway. The young man stumbles and falls, slamming his chest onto the hardwood floor. “Billy!” Krista shrieks before jumping out of the bed and rushing to her boyfriend’s aid. She glares up at Snow through eyes that simply don’t understand the way the monster thinks, “What are you trying to prove!?” She’s crying now, but Snow doesn’t see her tears. He sees past them. Without a word, the predator steps forward and grabs Billy before he can get to his feet, and proceeds to drag the young man out into the hallway, “What the fuck do you think you’re doing here!?” He screams into his prisoner’s ear, “You want to come into my house and fuck up my family!? You think I’m going to let you get away with that!?” “I wasn’t -” Billy tries to speak while being walked toward the stairs with a fistful of his hair clenched in Snow’s hand. “Jason!” Krista screams, “Let him go! You’re hurting him!” Snow ignores her and continues screaming in Billy’s ear with his low, bellowing, voice, “Did you have a nice time, Billy!?” Billy is terrified now as they near the stairs. He begins trying to pull away from Snow’s firm grasp but succeeds in nothing more than angering his much-stronger captor. “Don’t,” Snow begins as he grips Billy’s throat and slams him against the wall at the opposite side of the stairs, “try to get away from me, you little cocksucker! You can’t get away from me! You think I’m going to fucking let you get away with that? You think I’m going to let you get away with fucking my family?” Billy can’t speak - he clutches Snow’s powerful forearm with both hands as his face begins to turn red. “Jason!” Krista screams again, “You’re going to hurt him!” She’s crying hysterically now, and grabs onto Snow’s waist - he doesn’t even notice. He also doesn’t notice that his beloved child, Haley Snow, has long since been crying in the background - wakened by the screaming and thumping that has been coming from just outside her room. Billy’s blue eyes bulge as he tries to pull the dominant man’s hands away from his throat. He’s a strong young man, but stands no chance against a professional athlete like Snow. “Who the fuck do you think you are?” Snow asks in a voice that is quieter, but laced with the same harsh tones. “Jason!” Krista screams yet again from behind him, still wrapped around his waist in a desperate effort to pull him off her boyfriend. Eventually, Snow does release his grip on the young man’s throat, but in that same moment he grabs a handful of hair and tosses Billy effortlessly down the first flight of stairs. “Oh my God!” Krista says in an absolute screech before finally letting go of her brother-in-law’s waist and rushing down the stairs. She’s able to help her boyfriend to his feet a moment before Snow storms down the stairs and grabs the young man once more. Krista is frantic now. She begins punching and clawing at Snow’s bicep and forearm - Snow doesn’t even notice. He slams Billy into the wall of the first flight landing, prompting the young man to desperately take a vigorous swing at his attacker. The punch lands harmlessly on the side of Snow’s neck, and is responded to by a harsh, loud, slap in the face from the monster. Krista grows more frantic. Her punches and claws have moved up to Snow’s face now - she pulls at his long blond hair and his scruffy beard. All of her efforts are in vain, as the monster tosses her boyfriend down the second flight of stairs and onto the floor in front of the front entrance of the house. “You’re fucking crazy!” Krista shrieks. Snow pays her no mind and angrily stalks down the stairs toward his prey. Billy is fairly incoherent now, after being tossed down two flights of stairs. The only thought that goes through his mind as he waits for Snow’s arrival, is that he’s glad Snow didn’t save time by simply tossing him over the second floor railing. “Get up!” Snow screams at the young man, grabbing him once more by the hair and pulling him to his feet. Billy tries to make a run for the door, but finds himself slamming into it after a vicious pull from Snow. “Come on man,” Billy pleads with blood dripping from his nose, “I just want to leave. I swear, you’ll never see me again.” “You’d better hope I never see you again!” Snow bellows as he pulls Billy away from the door long enough to open it and toss his beaten body outside. As soon as he slams the door shut, he’s attacked by his raging sister-in-law. “You bastard!” she screams, flying into him with claws ripping through the chest of his plain white t-shirt, drawing blood from the skin. “You bastard!” she repeats, “Bastard! Bastard!” Snow doesn’t think - he reacts. He grabs a fistful of Krista’s long brown hair and pulls her down to his waist level. “Is that who you are!?” he screams into her ear as she struggles to get away, “Just some fucking whore!? Is that who you are!? Is it!?” Krista lets out a scream of pain, clawing viciously at the arm that Snow is holding her with. “You just go jumping into bed with that bastard!?” Snow screams again, his voice becoming more and more monsterous, “Is that what those fucking parents of yours taught you!? To be a whore!?” “Jason!” Krista pleads, “You’re hurting me!” Snow responds by tossing her hard into the wall beside the door. Upon hitting the wall, Krista’s face is filled with utter shock. She had always known that Snow was capable of monestrous acts, but never would believe he could stoop this low. Seeing him at his very worst, her face contorts into an expression of mixed hysterical fear and hysterical sadness, as she slowly lets herself fall into a seating position on the floor, shaking her head and never taking her eyes off the attacker. Snow’s hands are visibly shaking. Finally, the monster is burying itself back down into the depths of his stomach - draining itself from his eyes and letting them grow soft. What has he done? A moment ago, he saw the young woman as worthless - giving all of her value away to whoever it was he had just thrown out the door. She was, as he put it, a “whore.” Now, as she sits on the floor, crying and moaning hysterically - never taking her eyes from him - he sees her the way he had always seen her. The innocent child in need of guidance. A friend deeper than any he’s ever had, aside from his wife. And he had hurt her. He never gave much thought to hurting people emotionally - his sharp tongue was never an issue. But now, he had hurt someone who he cared deeply about physically, as well as emotionally. He never thought he’d cross that line. The breath that escapes Snow’s lips is a half sigh, half shutter. He takes a step backward and sits down on the second stair of the staircase, staring across at his sister-in-law. His lips say nothing, but his eyes say “I’m sorry.”
