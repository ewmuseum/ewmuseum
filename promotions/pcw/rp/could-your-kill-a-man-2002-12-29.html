Could Your Kill a Man?

29 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-29-02 Pain and suffering seemed to sufficate me now. The air was getting to heavy with the anger. It was getting hard to breath. Was the room getting smaller or was it all in Chris' mind. Maybe it wasn't the room that was getting smaller. Maybe it was Chris' world that was getting smaller. His relationship with Tessa was falling. His friendship with Jackson Rust was badly damaged. Chris felt like a complete victim here. He can not recall anything he did to reserve this. Fulling the walls close in on him. He had to escape. He had locked himself in his room and he didn't want to leave it. He couldn't face Tessa. The shame he felt at the moment was just too bad. He wasn't sure what the shame was for. Maybe for the way she acted. Or the fact he believes she might leave him. It could have been the shame he felt for not wanting to face her. He did not know. Chris looks around for a way out. He spots it. His chance for survival in this maddening room. The window of escape. Chris opens the window and looks out at the fire escape. It didn't look safe. Years of not being used. Chris hears that voice ring in his ears. 'Running from your problems? You worthless excuse for a solider.' the voice rings. Chris turns his attention to the image Chris. As he does so he hits his head hard on the window frame, nearly breaking it. Chris rubs his head and looks over at the image Storm dressed in the normal army camo gear. The real Chris rubs his head and looks at the image. 'What the hell do you want?' Chris shouts at the image. The army camo Chris just smirks. 'I am just laughing at how pathetic you are. Letting that women and man walk all over you.' the image tells Chris. Chris starts to get angry. He slams his fist down on his desk. 'Chris you have a powerful heart of black. Use it! It will make you stronger then you could image. Yet you push it back when it guides you. Let like weak emotions push you around and weaklings like them order you around.' he tells Chris. Chris continues to get more and more angry with his words. He looks over to the fire escape. Chris makes a dash for it and steps out of the room onto the fire escape. The cool wind blows against him, hitting against his body. Chris jumps down the fire escape, down the different floors of the apartment building. The image jumps out of the window and floats down to the ground, where Chris is already standing. Chris looks around for something. Moving his neck side to side, the echo of his neck popping rings out. The sun slowly falls from the sky casting the world in shadows. Light fades out with each passing second. A chilling breeze dances through the city sending people inside their homes. The wind slams against Chris' body but he doesn't even both to flinch. The vision stands right beside him, watching everything. Chris walks down the alleyway that rest between his apartment building and the other next to it. Only a dim light from one of the windows shines in the alleyway. Chris jumps back as something starts to rustle in the garbage. Chris' body tenses up, ready for whatever it is. Then from the garbage emerges a kitten. Chris relaxes as he sees the dirty kitten. Chris turns away and continues to walk off. The sound of the kitten hissing brings Chris' attention back around. A homeless man crawls out from the darkness. He stumbles into the light casted down from the window. The man has a bottle of burbon in his left hand. His foul breath reeking of the scent. The man gets a good look at who he is facing. 'Hey! Hey I knew who yer are. Yer that wresler guy. Chris Sterm. Yeah yer the one getting pushed around by that chick Tessa.' he slurs. Chris starts to get angry with the mentioning of Tessa's name. 'I saw yer on that tv over at ther repair shop. I can't believe someone like yer is whipped by her.' he laughs. The drunken slurs hit Chris like bullets. Chris' fist ball up. His veins bludge out as his blood starts to boil. 'Yer pussy whipped! That's what it is. Goddamn pussy whipped!' he tells at Chris. The old man laughs as Chris just stands there taking the insults. Leaning over Chris' shoulder, the vision watches on. He looks at Chris. 'You are just going to take that from him? How pathetic. And here I thought you cared about Tessa. Yet you let this drunken fool insult her and yourself. Come on Chris. Tap into your cold, cruel, black heart. This guy is a nobody. You think anyone will miss him? He is just a street mongrel. Worthless to the world. Killing him would a good deed to everyone. One less homeless person begging for food and money.' he tells Chris. Chris' eyes lower as he stares at the drunken fool. His body is shaking with anger. But he holds himself back from doing anything rash. 'Aw what's ther matter? Yer pissy because yer not gettin' enougf at home? Yer tryin' to hit that little girl huh? I see the way yer look at her.' he says as he takes a swing of the burbon. He looks at Chris with a stupid drunken grin. The vision gets in Chris' ear again. 'Chris you are a solider of fortune. Destined to kill without remorse. Don't bottle up your anger. You know you take end his life with ease. So just do it. Kill him!' the vision tells Chris. Chris looks at the drunken man. The bum finishes off the rest of the bourbon and drops the bottle. It rolls over to Chris' feet. Chris just lowers his head in anger. The light casting shadows on his face. 'HahahAHa'.....'haHahAha'....the twisted laugher echos in the dark alleyway. The drunken bum looks at Chris confused. The vision just cast an evil grin. Chris slowly lifts his head. Chris has this weird look on his face. Almost not real. He bends over and picks up the glass bottle. 'I'll make you pay for such talk. No one speaks about my Tessa in such a way. Now get ready to feel pain!' Chris rushes the bum while carrying the glass bottle. Chris swings the bottle back and catches him in the head, dropping him. The bottle shatters to pieces. Chris kicks him in the gut multiple times before finally picking him up and ramming him into the metal dumbster. 'No one is taking my Tessa from me! I care for her too much to let someone like you take her from me! Damn you Jackson Rust!' Chris screams as he pounds on the old bum. Chris pulls his hand back and reaches through the garbage for something. His hand feels a scrap piece of plumbering pipe and pulls it back, crushing it against the homeless man's skull. The sickening sound of bone breaking echos in the dark alley. Chris stands back from the motionless body of the drunk man. The vision comes up right behind Chris and puts his hand on his shoulder. 'Good job. I knew you had it in you. A little messy, but you was releasing your anger. Now didn't that make you feel better?' Chris nods his head. The vision smiles. 'It was fun making him bleed. Feeling him get crushed under your power. It's incredible knowing you are stronger then him. That you control his fate. You want more of this power don't you?' Chris just nods his head again. 'This is what you was breed for. The reason you take breaths every day. You was not mention to be a weak fool living the life you find yourself in now. ' Chris starts to walk off down the alley, back toward his apartment. 'On Tuesday, I will show Jackson Rust that Chris Storm is not a man he wants to mess with. He has choicen to come between Tessa and I. He is a threat. That makes him my enemy. I can not in good conscience allow him to sting Tessa around like he is. In the end all he can do is hurt her.' Chris stops. He looks back over at the vision. 'I am here for her. I breath for her. I live and die for her. I am her guardian angel. Tessa and the children are my life and I will always be there for them to watch over them and protect them. Even if I have to protect them from myself.' with those final words Chris puts his foot on the end of the broken bottle and kicks it up into the air. He catches it in his right hand the quickly throws it at the vision. The bottle goes through it and smashes against the wall, breaking into pieces. The vision looks at Chris surprised. It starts to get angry. 'I know who you really are! You are a cold blooded killer! You think you could have a normal life with her and those brats? You aren't even human! Just an accident no one excepted to happen. Your entire life is all because of an accident that happened. You fool!' the vision yells at Chris. Chris just keeps on walking toward his apartment. The vision fades away cursing out Chris more. Chris turns the corner and rams straight into the metal fire escape. He falls back and lands on the ground hard. His head hits the ground and his eyes close. He can feel a bright light shining on him. He tries to open his eyes but the light is too intense. He has to use his hand to shield his eyes. He finally opens his eyes and looks around. Chris is back in his room in his apartment. He's laying on the ground looking up at the ceiling light. The moon cast a ray of light in the room. Chris sits up and leans on the bed. He looks around. The window is open and there is a little blood on the frame. He touches his forehead. He has a small cut. Chris looks around the room. 'Was it all a dream? Did I even kill that old bum? I must have bumped my head on the window and blacked out.' he stands up and falls backwards onto the bed. 'Man what a crazy dream.' There is a knock at the door. Chris looks over and gets up. Carefully he wants over to the door and unlocks it. There in the doorway is Alex. He appears to just have woken up. Chris bends over and picks him up. They walk over to the couch in the living room. 'What's the matter Alex? What are you doing up at such a late hour?' he ask the little ten year old. Not noticing that Tessa isn't even sleeping on the couch like she normally does. Alex rubs his eyes and looks up at Chris. 'Tessa is gone. She took Christa and left.' he informs Chris. Chris' heart stopped. He just kept blinking. Was this real? Did Tessa really leave him? It didn't make any sense. Why? Why would she leave him? Because of one arguement? They have been through worst together. Maybe she really didn't love him. Chris felt like he was going to throw up. He just could not believe that was happening to him. Alex tugs on Chris' arm to bring him back to the real world. 'Tessa went to gramma's house. Should we go get her?' he ask. Chris forced himself to smile and put his hand on the kid's head. 'No Alex. Tessa needs time herself. We'll let her think and get her feelings out. If she wants to come back, she'll be back. In the meantime, you need to go back to sleep.' Chris tells Alex. He lifts him up from the bed and carries him into the guest room. Chris tucks Alex into the bed and sits down in the chair next to the bed. He watches as Alex peacefully slips to the land of dreams. Chris knew he wouldn't be getting any sleep now. So he was going to do his best and watch over Alex until Tessa got back. If she was ever coming back....
