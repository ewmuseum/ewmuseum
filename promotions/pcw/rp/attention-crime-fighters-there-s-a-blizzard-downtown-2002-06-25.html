Attention crime fighters! There's a blizzard downtown!

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 25, 2002 [The scene opens to the Crusader Mobile barreling down the Golden Gate Bridge at high speeds, dangerously passing up other cars trying to make their ways to San Francisco as well. The Crusader Mobile was about the size comparable to the Ghost Buster mobile, if you remember it. It was incredibly long, with a huge trunk area and shining bright lights on top. On the side of the monstrosity was the Crusaders of Light logo, cOL, with a lightning bolt streaking through it. Night Shard, better known as Robert Yarney, is driving the car, while The Crimson is sharpening his sword using a butter knife in the passenger seat. The constant screeching of the sharp objects filing against one another begins to drive Night Shad crazy.] Night Shard: [screams] Thinh, I know you’re bored, but for the love of god, please knock it off! You’re driving me up the wall with all of that sharpening! The Crimson: Gosh, I’m sorry Robert. But you know how ansy I get when we don’t get to do anything. I mean, why should Tom have all the fun? Night Shard: [points a finger] You know just like I do that Tom is doing important things. I mean, he didn’t get that amulet of Orion or whatever, but he sure as hell did his best. I don’t know what’s going to do. Besides, since when do you talk like this? You’re usually so much more serious and calm. The Crimson: [clears his throat] You’re right. I apologize for my rash demeanor. I will adjust my personality accordingly. Night Shard: [relieved] That’s better. Now you’re the same, boring Crimson that I remember. Good vampire. The Crimson: [looks over menacingly] Don’t patronize me, Robert. I haven’t fed yet tonight. Night Shard: I thought you said you didn’t really need to feed. The Crimson: I don’t. But when I’m upset, it helps to relieve my mind. It’s similar to what you humans call…[thinks for a moment]…oh what is the word? Night Shard: [interjects] Masturbation? The Crimson: [shrugs] Crude, but not too far from the truth, I think. [Robert pulls out a hamburger from the ash tray and begins to munch on it. As mustard begins to drip all over the steering wheel, Thinh catches it with a napkin and cleans it up.] The Crimson: Would you please refrain from eating in the car? I had to clean out your ham sandwich that you were consuming on the ride back to headquarters. There were pickles underneath the car and some sort of red radish stuck in between the seats. Night Shard: Ah, I grew those myself. I call them RASHES. They are actually much hotter and spicier than your average radish, so they give that little kick. The Crimson: [wipes his hands clean] As usual, I am amazed by your ingenious knowledge. But can we return to business for a moment? We’re supposed to be looking for crimes. Night Shard: It’s kind of hard to do that when you’re cruising around in the Crusader Mobile, don’t you think? I mean, why don’t we just advertise that we’re looking to bust some heads? The Crimson: It would not be feasible to use anything else. Night Shard: [outraged] Feasible? Why couldn’t we use the Crusader jet? Oh that’s right, Tom needed to do some repairs on it. Well, that’s just great. Why can’t we just use our powers then? I mean, I could be hiding in the dark, and you can float around in the air sort of like Spiderman, right? [Crimson was a bit annoyed and blew his nose in Night Shard’s direction. Night Shard replied by sticking his tongue out. It was a very mature exchange.] The Crimson: You’re immature response will be ignored. For your information, I do not float at all like a flying squirrel as you imply. I, in fact, soar and am lighter than air when I wish to be. Night Shard: Man, that’s weird. Almost as weird as my ability to move things with my mind. The Crimson: So why don’t you move yourself around in the air? Night Shard: [grabs his beer belly] You see all of this? This is a lot of fat to be moving with my head. I’m too fat to be floating around like you. Besides, I like moving around in the shadows. It makes me feel thinner. [Crimson just shakes his head. He knows that Night Shard is sensitive about his weight, and in real life, it causes him to not take risks. When he is Peter Yarney, he becomes a conservative man who works eight hours a day, six days a week, and goes home alone at night and watches television. The pathetic nature of the life he lives cannot be ignored, but that wasn’t for Crimson to judge. When Robert became Night Shard, confidence suddenly was his best weapon, and his powers unmatched.] The Crimson: Just remember to stick to that strict diet Tom put you on. He just wants what’s best for you. Night Shard: [upset] Did you see that list he gave me? It was horrible. Carrot sticks for breakfast? The Crimson: Carrots are good for the eyes. Night Shard: [exasperated] Need I remind you that I am Night Shard. The whole point of my being is the fact that I can walk around and see well in the dark. I don’t need carrots. The Crimson: At night, yes. But during the day you wear fairly large spectacles, do you not? [Night Shard brought the glasses out of pocket and put them on. Suddenly, his vision was blurry and he couldn’t see anything. He took them off and threw them to the ground.] Night Shard: Fine, so my eyes are horrible during the day. I don’t fight crime during the day, Thinh. And need I remind you, you don’t either. The Crimson: I could if I wanted to. Vampires have been given the unholy reputation of not being able to go outside during the day. I’m not sure where Hollywood has gotten that impression, but it’s not true. Night Shard: So why don’t you ever go outside? I never see you sunbathing. And lord knows you could use it. You’re looking a bit pale. [Thinh always looked pale. He was, after all, a vampire. But sun bathing wouldn’t be able to help that, nor any sort of tan booths or makeup. Makeup was just too messy and tended to run on vampire skin. It’s very sensitive, you know? The only thing that could give color to an otherwise pale looking vampire was fresh, human blood. And we all know where that comes from…] The Crimson: I don’t need to draw attention to myself. Not like some people here. Night Shard: Don’t be jealous because I’m better looking than you, Thinh. I see the way the girls look at you. They’re like…[high pitched voice]…OH MY GOD! OH MY GOD! LOOK AT THAT MONSTER! HE’S SO UGLY! I’D NEVER SLEEP WITH HIM! The Crimson: [not amused] Your attempts at humor have been noted. Night Shard: Oh come on, you know I’m teasing you. [Suddenly, on the view screen dash board, a new report came in. It was a message on the news about an iceman called Blizzard assaulting downtown San Francisco. The lunatic was freezing people in the streets as well as police officers trying to stop him.] The Crimson: Do you see that, Robert? Night Shard: I sure do, Thinh. But how are we ever going to beat Snow and RAH? The Crimson: [confused] What? What in the blazes are you talking about? Night Shard: Our match on Fusion, of course. We have to take on Snow and RAH, two top contenders for the World tag titles, and I don’t know if we can do it. The Crimson: You are a lunatic. There’s an iced combatant in downtown Frisco that we must eliminate or else he’ll turn the entire city into one giant ice cube. [Night Shard pauses for a moment. He looks like he’s in deep thought.] Night Shard: Do you think I would be able to get Frisco’s autograph? I mean, he’s a hall of famer. I would like nothing better than to put his autograph next to my other favorites such as Burt Reynolds and Winona! The Crimson: Stay on task, Robert. First, we’ll deal with this Blizzard fellow and then we’ll worry about Snow and RAH. Besides, how difficult could they be to defeat? Rent-A-Hero is a cheap excuse for a hero with his Spiderman get up, and Snow is nothing more than a lackadaisical element. Night Shard: Didn’t you originally dress up like Spiderman before you picked out your costume? The Crimson: I don’t have to answer that question. Night Shard: [laughs] Yeah, you did. No need to be ashamed. Before I picked out this wonderful ensemble, I had Cher’s outfit. [Crimson merely stares over at Night Shard who has a smile on his face. It looked like a happy smile. Crimson sat back in his seat.] The Crimson: Just drive you imbecile. We have work to take care of. Night Shard: [speeding up the car] Big, bad blizzard man, the Crusaders of Light are coming! What are you going to do about it! [Snow and RAH, the Crusaders are coming for you too. So, soon you’ll have to answer the same question.]
