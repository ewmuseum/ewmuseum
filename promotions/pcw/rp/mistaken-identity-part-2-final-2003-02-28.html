Mistaken Identity (Part 2 final)

28 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/28/03 The more Brianna thought about it she saw that the man looked slightly nervous trying to cover it up with his upset, tough guy routine. He constantly took his thumbs and rubbed them across his fingers as he had them placed on the table. He also had the look of a child who hadn’t studied for a test and wanted desperately to reach for his makeshift notes. He was very suspicious and Brianna didn’t trust him one bit. “The big question I have for you Ms. Emerson is why you did it? Why did you feel the need to rob and then kill the owner of the Western National Bank? What made you decide that you hadn’t had enough of a rush and that you needed continue your immoral ways? Do you not have any pity for anyone’s life?” he hammered. Listening to this flushed away all of Brianna’s worries. What she was looking for had finally happened. The man had just slipped up and Brianna was more than ecstatic to point it out to him. “You said the Western National Bank? That’s quite funny considering the fact that as I was looking through the police reports I saw no mention of that particular bank.” Brianna retorted thinking she had things won. “Those are only some of the reports hunny.” he fires back not at all pleased with her questioning him. Yet again she was stuck, but was dead fast on proving that this man was a total and absolute fake to save her future. Somehow he had managed to recover from his slip up, but next time he wouldn’t be so lucky. “All I have to do is say that I’m guilty and you’ll arrest me and all of those question and answer nonsense will be over?” Brianna asks. “Yes ma’am.” he quickly replies. “Do you mind if I ask you a question before I pledge myself guilty?” “I’m not promising I’ll waste my breath on an answer, but go ahead and ask away.” All of the thoughts accumulated into one section of her brain which processed it into a long chain of events setting up a great defense for Brianna. A smile came across her face as she finally figured out what would show the true color of this liar. “Coming in here it seemed as though everyone, you especially, were certain that I had done all of this and I was who you had claimed I was. You were kind enough to let me see the folder which may have ended up as a big mistake on your part buddy.” This completely catches him off guard and he gets ready to leave not wanting to hear anymore of this. He slides his sleeves down and goes to open the door. Brianna, not expecting this tactic, stands up and rushes over to the door blocking his way. “Get out of the way!” he demands with a face full of fury. “First I tried to figure out why I needed to confess to all of these alleged crimes when you said showed me that all the signs pointed to me. At first I figured this to be a trick to get me to confess when you really didn’t have much on me, like I had seen on several cop shows. But then when I saw the folder I realized that all of that could have sent me straight to jail where I couldn’t pass go and collect two hundred dollars.” The man didn’t find her sense of humor the least bit amusing, and a small bead of sweat fell from his big forehead knowing that the gig was almost up. “And then your whole demeanor….you fidgeted around the whole time and seemed like you were trying not to screw up the lines to this play. Because that’s what it is isn’t it? This was all just a set up.” Brianna spits out growing more and more frustrated with each passing minute. The man’s reaction takes her off guard and he grabs her by the arms and pulls her away from the door and against the wall. “Congratulations inspector, you won! You figured out the big mystery and what do you win for your efforts? I’ll tell ya what. You win a trip to the morgue where you should have been a couple weeks back.” he half yells dominating the conversation now. As if Brianna hasn’t already had her fair share of near death experiences, the guy reaches into his pocket brandishing a knife. He runs the thin metal blade lightly across Brianna’s neck preparing her for what should be pretty painful. “This is where you say your prayers sweetheart because it’s the end of the line for you.” he says getting ready to drive the knife right into her throat. Feeling as though this is it, Brianna slams her eyes shut and clenches her teeth breathing very hard. The knife pricks her throat and all of a sudden a flurry of SWAT team members swarm in and surround the room in their black uniforms. Two of them quickly pull the man away from Brianna and drag him outside. Brianna stands there thankful for her life and yet managing to stay confused. That’s when a figure emerges from the cluster of people which Brianna recognizes instantly. “Kramer!” she says excitedly running over to him and embracing him. Kramer doesn’t really mind the hug but he looks down at Brianna’s neck and sees a line of blood going across which really concerns him causing him to jerk her away. “You’ve been cut.” he says feeling a bit sorry for her. Brianna reaches up and wipes at her neck coming back with two fingers covered in blood. Kramer pulls a handkerchief from his pocket and applies it to her wound looking for concerned for her. “We should have a medic check this out.” “Oh I’ll be fine…just a little ointment and I’ll never know the difference.” she replies. “No we’re seeing a doctor.” “I’m fine!” she protests pulling away. “Ok then, but I still think it would be best.” “Please don’t worry. Instead tell me what in the hell is going on! Now I think two close calls with death merits me an explanation.” Kramer looks into her eyes and sees her seriousness. She really wants to know and probably won’t take no for an answer. Kramer grabs her around the shoulder and escorts her out of the room and walks with her outside where he clears his throat. “The guy as you probably figured out was connected with the men involved in my situation. The two police officers that picked you up, and this bozo, were all working for the mafia. Their plan was to get you to confess to a crime that had been committed by one of their own. Then you would be sentenced to jail and you would no longer be in the way.” “So they pasted my face on those pictures?” “Sure did. As you know the mafia has many connections and so doing stuff like that is as easy as baking a cake. They call in a favor and it gets done.” “But how did you know all of this?” she asked. “That I unfortunately can’t tell you.” he walks her up to a woman and asks if she could take Brianna home. The woman happily obliges. “See you later Brianna.” Kramer says giving her a wink as he walks away leaving Brianna there with the woman. The scene slowly fades to black. Thus another day had gone by in Brianna’s twisted web of a life. What’s another brush with death anyway? Just something that will make Brianna stronger and less fearful. Who knows, this may give her the motivation needed to captivate her first ever wrestling gold.
