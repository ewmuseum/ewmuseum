Don't kill the messengers? Yeah, Right (Part 1)

3 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 3, 2002 ***This promo was taped before the events of the July 31st Turmoil Episode.*** [A muggy night in Toronto is the backdrop to our scene. A night where the smog actually inflicts visibility problems on drivers heading into the city for whatever reason. A night where you can use a chainsaw to cut through the air. We now head to a flat on the waterfront. A neighborhood that isn't hospitable to say the least. Many abandoned buildings are lining the area, most slated for demolition due to the mayor's plans to rebuild the waterfront. A 1989 Pontiac Sunbird drives up to one of the buildings, the car itself doesn't even look safe. Two white, business-type men step out of the car, one with a briefcase, the other just has the feel of importance to him.] Man #1: Are you sure this is the place Jimmy? Jimmy: Definitely, Shawn. He would be perfect. [Both men walk onto the wet sidewalk, and head to the door. Jimmy attempts to open the door, and the door just falls off the hinges. Jimmy looks to Shawn, and Shawn just shrugs as they enter the dark building.] Jimmy: You have the Mag-Lite with you? Shawn: (In a Jamaican Accent) Ya Mon. Jimmy: Man, you're white. Don't speak like a Jamaican. Shawn: Why shouldn't I? Jimmy: You're not even from the region, and it seems unnatural. Oh well, pass me the Mag-Lite. [Jimmy turns on the mag-lite and adjusts it to the widest possible beam. Jimmy winces as he sees a rat cross his path.] Shawn: What does it mean when a rat crosses your path? Jimmy: Shut up, Shawn. That isn't funny. [Jimmy then begins walking up a flight of stairs. The stairs are made of wood, and they creak with every step. Some creak so loud that it seems like that they would collapse at any moment. As they head through the doorway, Jimmy and Shawn brush away some cobwebs. They head down the hall and approach a door. They stop in front of the door.] Jimmy: Apartment number 201. Shawn, you ready? [No response.] Jimmy: Shawn? [Jimmy turns around to see that Shawn is no where near him. Jimmy points the light all over the place, to see nothing except rotted wood, drywall that is full of holes, and cracked concrete.] Jimmy: What a Wimp. [Jimmy Clears his throat and knocks again. This time, the door to the apartment falls off. Jimmy's eyes begin to bug out of his skull. He enters the apartment, flashing the light into several rooms looking for something. All he sees are a mildew covered bathroom with a broken toilet, a bedroom with nothing in it except an old bed frame, and a closet with nothing in it. He then heads into the main room. Flashes the light around, he see a broken television, a couch with a spring sticking out of it, and something on the wall. He puts the light on the wall. There are two words on there. You're mine. Suddenly, the light drops to the floor, turns off and numerous thuds are heard within the dark room. The thuds turn silent after five seconds.] *Darkness* [As the light returns...well, somewhat. Although it is only one twenty-five watt light bulb on a wall, it provides enough light for proper vision. Jimmy is chained to a wall. Some of his clothing has been somewhat torn for reasons unknown. However, those reasons may be revealed shortly. One person can be heard away from the camera.] Man: Wake up, [BEEEEEP]. [Jimmy begins to stir; the usual things come across his mind. Wondering where he is, how many fingers and toes he has, stuff like that.] Man: You better be alive. I have questions for you. Jimmy: Wh...What do you want, man? Man: Funny, I was going to ask you the same question. Jimmy: Who are you? Show yourself! Man: I am the one you seek. Jimmy: Edward Mandrake? Eddie: Bingo. Now, what do you want? [Sweat begins to bead onto Jimmy's forehead.] Jimmy: I...uh. I have something for you, it’s a package containing a wrestling contract. [Eddie then enters the scene right in front of Jimmy's face.] Eddie: Wrestling? You came to all this trouble, setting off my traps, and making yourself my prisoner...for Wrestling. [Jimmy's heart begins to beat faster as he stares into Eddie's eyes, the sweat trickling down his face.] Jimmy: Before I continue...where's my friend? Eddie: Short white guy, same penguin suit you're wearing? Jimmy: Yes. Where is he? Eddie: Part of my decor here now. [Eddie steps out of Jimmy's face, and he sees Shawn on the wall, chained like he was. The face is still and the eyes are rolled into the back of his head. What happened to Shawn is too graphic to be described. Jimmy then begins to shake, almost vomits at the sight of Shawn's lifeless corpse. Jimmy frantically begins to try to free himself. He tries to force the shackles out of the wall, to no avail.] Eddie: I can taste your fear now. I should inform you that he put up good sport by screaming in pain and agony right when he was supposed to. Ah, your friend was quite the play thing. Jimmy: You son of a bitch! If I get out of here I'll... Eddie: You'll what? You won't even get the chance, because you'll be dead! I'll have your intestines strung up like Christmas Decorations! [Jimmy's breathing rate increases.] Eddie: I've read your briefs. You represent a wrestling federation called...PCW was it? [Jimmy doesn't provide a response.] Eddie...Now, that isn't really the smart thing to do. [Eddie picks up a nail gun, plunges it into the left hand of Jimmy, and presses the trigger. Jimmy: GAAAH! AAAAAH! Eddie: Oh yeah! My favorite sounds! The scream of pain, combined with a dash of fear...Now that is an interesting combination. [Eddie puts down the gun] Eddie: Now...you represent PCW? Jimmy: I’m only the messenger man! I work for UPS for [BEEP] sake! [Eddie picks up the briefs, and quickly glances at them.] Eddie: Do you realize how much I make as a Bounty Hunter? I'll mail these in. As for you, you won't make it back in one piece. [Eddie then picks up a giant sword, and begins to walk toward Jimmy. Jimmy's breathing rate has increased dramatically, his pulse pounding, and he's trying to climb up the wall in reverse, but quickly realizes...Painfully I might add that his hand has been nailed to the wall. Eddie winds up for the killing blow and stops just short of Jimmy's neck. Eddie then drops his sword on the ground and clutches his head for a brief second. Jimmy remains somewhat fearful. Eddie then returns to a vertical base and looks at Jimmy.] Eddie: Aw man. I really am sorry for all this. I'm not entirely psychotic; let me get you out of there. [Eddie then heads to a bloody table. Picks up a device that looks like a motorized claw hammer.] Eddie: Now...Hold Still. This is really going to hurt. [Eddie then places the claw portion into the nail and pulls the trigger. The nail comes out with no problem, and Jimmy yelps again.] Eddie: Here, let me help you out of there. [Jimmy is a little apprehensive.] Eddie: Ever read 'Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde'? I'm Jekyll for 8 hours a day, and Hyde the rest of the time. [Jimmy seems rather calmer now. Eddie releases one shackle, and Jimmy takes care of the other one.] Eddie: If I were you, I would leave right now and never come back. Call me at cell phone number, here's my card. [He hands Jimmy a Business Card with all the particulars.] Eddie: Oh, and you better get to hospital, and say it was an industrial accident. GO! [Jimmy grabs his briefcase and hurries out of the room and onto the street. He gets into his car and peels out. Eddie, back in the room peruses the briefs. It contains a PCW Letter-head. He cracks half a smile as he signs the document. The other half of his face now smiles as the light bulb in the room burns out.]
