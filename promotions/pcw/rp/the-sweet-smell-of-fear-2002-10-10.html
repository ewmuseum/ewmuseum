The Sweet Smell Of Fear

10 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10th October 2002 The lights of the technological city Tokyo were flashing around me as I couldn’t help but smile. People of all races and stature were walking past me, all of them not caring who they were just mingling with each other. It disgusted me to see my own people mixing with tourists and the upper class mixing with the scum that can also be found in Tokyo but I was too drunk to care. This my kind of city! I continued to smile as I walked along the clean city streets in Tokyo looking up at a huge fluorescent billboard, even though it wasn’t Vietnam I still felt as though I belonged. I stood and stared up at the billboard, drunk as a skunk as people walked along beside me. All of a sudden someone bumped into me, a dressed up businessman similar to the TokyoPop ones. “Get out of my way Gaijin!” The man grunted before shoving past me, I looked over at him with a drunk smile crossing my face not in any mood to be pushed around, I snapped back, “How you know I not from here?” The man grumbled something in Japanese before disappearing into the crowd that was walking around me. Stupid White Boy… Shaking off the bright light and words off of the billboard which mesmerized me I took another swig from the bottle of expensive champagne that was hanging by my side. The bubbly alcohol trickled down my throat giving me a warm and happy feeling inside my stomach as I shuddered letting it get the better of me. “Alcohol taste so good Huh?!” I put my arm around a passerby, The young good looking but well dressed female got a scared look which crossed over her face before she shoved my arm off of her shoulder and jogged off, hiding in the crowds of people walking around me. I yelled after her, “You not want some of this Vietnamese Pride?” I smiled and then waved signaling I didn’t care as people didn’t seem to notice. All of a sudden as I was walking I spotted a security guard heading my way, brushing my hair out of my face I took another swig from the champagne bottle and hid it behind my back. The Security Guard grabbed from the black baton which was hanging on the right side of his belt before adjusting his cap and pointing it towards me. Here we go again…another one going to get fucked up! I chuckled to myself as I clenched my fists preparing to fight off the oncoming Security Guard. Out of the crowd he appeared in front of me and held the baton to my neck before issuing some command in Japanese as I titled my drunk head to the side dumbfounded. “Don’t you speak Japanese boy?” The Security Guard cringed before pressing the baton tighter to my neck, If I did, I would of knocked you… I mumbled something in Vietnamese as the Security Guard stood back a little before his face lit up. “You’re Vietnamese?” The Security Guard looked impressed, I spat on the ground to my left as people began to scatted, “What it look like to you?” The Security Guard shook his head before leaning up close and looking at my drunk face. I tried to act straight but it wasn’t working so I just began to stare into his eyes. “You’re Jung Loc? The TokyoPop mouth piece?” The Security Guard asked, I stood back a little, he was the first in all of Japan…make that second to actually notice and care who I was as I nodded my head and smiled at him before taking another swig from the bottle of champagne at my side.“That me…..” I beamed with pride, Who else you think I was?! The Security Guard looked shocked as he patted me on my back before he placed the baton back at its place on the side of his belt and reaching for my bottle. “I’m sorry to bother you Jung, but you’re not allowed to drink in public in Tokyo…or all of Japan for that matter! I’m going to have to ask for the bottle before letting you move on your way…” The Officer trailed off as I clenched the bottle tight in my hand, “What you say?” I became aggravated, I put the bottle further behind my back as the Officer reached for it again, “Just hand me the bottle and I’ll let you be on your way! I’ll even take you out for a drink after I get off work…I’m a big fan Master Jung Loc.” I smiled at him, “If you a big fan, you let me go….” “I can’t do that, your breaking the law as it is and I’m being lenient!” The Security Officer stated, Now you fuckin’ asking for it!! I moved the bottle in front of my body and held it up to his head height as he smiled thinking I was going to hand it over. “I not care White Boy….what you going to do if I don’t?” I snapped at him, The Officer reached down for his baton yet again, “I’ll have to arrest you! Please cooperate….” Pfft, fuck you White Boy! I looked around me, seeing a gap in the crowd leading down an alley as I found an escape route. “I sorry Officer…” My voice trailed off, The Officer smiled and then put the baton back down at his belt, “That’s no problem Jung….” “Fuck you White Boy!” I winked at him, The Officer looked confused as I raised the bottle and sent it crashing down on top of his head, sending shards of glass and alcohol flowing over the streets as people in the crowds screamed as I sent a boot into his stomach before running down the alley leaving him bleeding and clutching his head on the pavement. As I ran down the alley I turned behind me to see people trying to help him as I shook my head and spat to my side, Call for back up….it not going to do you any good because you not going to be able to find me! I ran into the darkness leaving the city behind me like light at the end of a tunnel as I ran deeper and deeper down into the alley hoping I wouldn’t get caught…. -=***=- Why people have to fuck around?! Why is it that the law always have to come and stop my fun?! This fuckin’ bull shit! It because I Vietnamese…. If I Japanese none of this happen…. If I Japanese he probably be scared and give me all of his money and buy me some more alcohol before letting me do what I want! I think my reputation already too big in Japan! TokyoPop know who I am, the Yakuza know who I am, the Law know who I am and the people know who I am…. Not a good place to fuck around….but I guess I do what I want! -=***=- I drew my suit jacket around me trying to escape the coldness as I exited the end of the alley and found myself in a beat up part of Tokyo, behind the city streets. Mean looking thugs I was used to seeing were walking by me in groups, eyeing me down wondering why the hell I had moved in on their turf. You have problem?! I could’ve snapped back at them looking at me but I was too drunk to fend off an entire group of Asian thugs so I shook my head and kept walking. The alcohol finally getting to me… I began to sway as the alcohol finally took an effect on me, and it was doing it in the worst place. This not good! Usually I wouldn’t freak out, I’d never get scared or even admit to myself that I was scared but for the first time in my life I was scared and I was actually admitting it. Maybe it was just the alcohol talking but I knew I needed to get out of the cold dark streets before I found myself being jumped and mugged by a group of thugs, I was in no position to be fighting as my stomach began to ache. Man this fuckin’ hurting! I continued to walk onwards through the smog that was arising around me, I think I was just dreaming it but everyone was staring at me as I walked past. “Fortune?” I heard the voice of an old Japanese woman who grabbed at the leg of my pants, I nearly jumped out of skin as I raised my fist and got ready to knock her one as I looked down and noticed she was at least 80 years old and was trying to make a living. To drunk to say anything else and wanting to blend into the crowd I asked, “How much you want?” The wrinkly old lady managed a smile behind her Japanese face as she blew a piece of gray hair from dangling in front of her eyes, “Twenty dollar….” What the fuck… This old woman must’ve seen that I was reasonably well off as she jacked the prices right there on the spot. “Don’t you mean yen?” I gritted my teeth and looked down at her, The woman held out her palm up to me and smiled, “I know who you are Jung Loc…I know you have enough money to last you a life time! Twenty Dollar…” Stupid bitch actually know what she talking about! I didn’t take enough time to think that she’d probably just seen me on a PURE show or during an advert for PCW so I reached into my pocket and produced a green twenty dollar note and placed it in the palm of her hand. The old lady smiled at me and got up grabbing my hand and dragging me to a small run-down tin hut which was behind the curb that she was sitting on. “In here Jung….I tell you your fortune!” The old lady led me away, As she dragged me into the old tin hut I was surprised to see that it was actually quiet cozy inside. It was warm with a small table and two old red cushions sitting on either side of the table. “Sit there!” She pointed to one of the cushions as I sat down, I looked up and noticed a small candle sitting next to a rather large but dirty crystal ball as I saw the dim candle light make shadows dance around the walls of tin. This going to be fake…. I sat down as the old Japanese lady sat down opposite from me and began to gaze into the crystal ball like you see White Boys doing in America. Too drunk to care I noticed that clouds began to swirl around inside the glass ball as the old woman closed her eyes and began to chant in Japanese.“You trying to put curse on me?!” I broke the silence, The old woman glared at me before she clutched her forehead and grabbed the palm of my hand before looking directly into my eyes and speaking. “You are going to run into trouble with the gods Jung….” She trailed off, I glared at her and looked at the palm of my hand as everything became blurry, “You fuckin’ with me….” “I see that you are going to come into contact with many powerful men and soon your soul is going to be taken into a dark direction which will change your future!” She paused, You talk shit… I thought to myself as she continued to read my future by looking into my eyes, the ball and the palm of my hand. “I sense fear in you Jung…you must learn to let it go or it will be your bane!” She spoke in big words much like a wizard would, “I not scared of anyone….” I let my drunk voice waver off, The old lady looked up at me and glared, “You must use your anger to side with the gods and then and only then will you become the master of your fears!” You getting on my nerves…. I looked up at her as all of a sudden I began to feel light headed as I blinked and struggled to keep my eyes open. “What happening to me?!” I snapped at her and tried to move my hand away but I was too weak, The old lady smiled before saying something, “You must give your anger to the gods and then and only then will you become the Viper people say you are…” The old lady continued to repeat the last sentence over and over again as I tried to speak but nothing could come out my mouth as my lips were dry due to the champagne. I could hear her voice trail off and see her serious expression on her wrinkly Asian face as she concentrated and my eyes slowly closed, I passed out and my world plunged into darkness. This not good…
