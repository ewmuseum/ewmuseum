Got to be careful not to piss the fans off

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21 March &#039;03 The scene opens up on the film studio of a futuristic porno with leprechauns getting kinky with some alien gnomes. This film studio is the studio next door to the grey room studio where Chet King has recently been attacking members of PCW. Chet King and Cindy Bi are watching the filming taking place, and as they do Cindy makes a revelation to Chet. Cindy Bi: Chet, I’ve just finished a survey relating to how people are taking your verbal attacks on certain members of PCW. Chet King: You did a survey on it? Why didn’t you tell me. Cindy Bi: A variety of complex reasons. Chet King: Like? Cindy Bi: I couldn’t be bothered. Chet King: Ah ok. So how have people taken to my verbal beatings? Cindy Bi: There’s been both positive and negative responses from the public. Chet King: Negative responses?!? What couldn’t people possibly like about me humiliating fools like Gaz Green or Skylar Green? It appears as Chet has just realized something. Chet King: Hey, wait a minute! Gaz and Skylar both have the last name Green! Cindy rolls her eyes. Cindy Bi: You’re pretty quick on the uptake, aren’t you Chet? Chet ignores Cindy. Chet King: I wonder if Gaz and Skylar are related. If they are it’d make sense as to why they both have pathetic careers that no one cares about. It’d also make sense as to why they both suffer from Makonie/Green Syndrome. Cindy Bi: I suppose it would explain a few things if Gaz and Skylar were related. But lets get back to the results of the survey. Chet King: Yeah, why are people having a negative reaction to me humiliating fools like Skylar and Gaz? Cindy Bi: People don’t have a problem with your attacks on Gaz Green or Mark Makonie, but there has been a mixed reaction to your attacks on Chris Storm, Tessa Windsor and Skylar Green. Chet King: Mixed in what way? Cindy Bi: Some people have agreed with what you’ve had to say and they’ve been pleased by your comments. Some people however feel like you’ve been an asshole to Tessa, Chris and Skylar. Chet King: An asshole? I’ve just been telling things like they are. Cindy Bi: Well you’ve been giving the impression to some people that you’re an asshole, and one thing that you don’t want is to lose any popularity especially since you’re on the verge of being world champion. Chet King: If people don’t like what I have to say then fuck them. I’ve still got many more people on my shit list to put in their place, and I’m going to be saying some things that might upset people. Cindy Bi: That’s fair enough, but try to keep your attacks on people that the fans hate. You’ve got to keep the fans love. Chet King: I’ll be launching my attacks on people I hate. And don’t worry, did you hear the reaction I got at Madness? I’m not going to be losing the fans love any time soon. Cindy Bi: Well considering the fact that people enjoyed what you had to say on Gaz and Makonie I know it’ll take a lot to lower the respect the fans give you, but just be careful. Chet King: Like I said, I’m going to embarrass whoever I feel like embarrassing. And I do plan on embarrassing all those in the world title match at Turmoil, where I do plan on walking out with the gold... Cindy Bi: You’ll do what Duke did at Madness, and you’ll have all your years of hard work finally pay off with getting the biggest prize of them all. Is that what you were about to say? Chet King: Definitely, I can’t wait for Turmoil. First though I’ve got some more people to verbally knock out. Cindy Bi: Would one of these people be El Romantico? Chet King: I’ve been thinking about it, but I’ve also thought to myself that he’s not really even worth wasting my time on. I think I’ll let my talking with El Romantico be done in the ring on Fusion, he’s just not worth wasting my time talking about. Cindy Bi: Speaking of your match with El Romantico, have you seen the ads being played for it? Chet King: No, what do they say? Cindy Bi: They’re advertising it as a technical/high flying bout, which means everyone’s going to be expecting some perfect cruiserweight wrestling from you, no brawling. Chet King: If they want to see cruiserweight action at it’s best, then that’s what I’ll give them. I’ve never been one to displease fans. Cindy Bi: Well you did displease a few fans with some of your attacks. Chet King: Hey, if they were displeased by me, then they’re not really fans! Chet and Cindy stop talking as they watch a rather interesting development which has taken place in the porno filming – twenty naked hobbits (obviously this film isn’t worrying about copyright) have entered the set with bondage equipment. This really doesn’t need to be divulged into any further, so this is where we leave the scene.
