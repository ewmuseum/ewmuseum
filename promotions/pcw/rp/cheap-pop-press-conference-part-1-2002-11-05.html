Cheap Pop Press Conference (part 1)

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05 - 11 - 02 From the electric atmosphere of America's city of sin, Las Vegas Nevada to the grim backdrop, the misery that is London England we return to the temporary residence of Gaz 'The Jester' Green. Opening on the high-profiled superstar himself we intrude on yet another manly moment as the Jester sits before the television set. Almost with a touch of de ja vu, Gaz glares cold-heartedly at what appears to be a ladder match. Titles suspended, we witness two grapplers scaling the ladder in a bid to conclude the contest, a similar fate that lays in wait for the dynamic duo Pranks With Attitude. Sitting in his velvet business attire, Gaz Green watches the footage with interest even though this contest will almost certainly set the pedestal for future championship bouts. 'Why are you still torturing yourself over this Main Event?' murmured the distinctive voice of Natalie Miyagawa who walks into view, showcasing her figure, matched only by that of Eleanor Kannon-Hall. Drawing the attention of her business associate, Gaz looked towards the PCW diva, his mouth ajar, and his response a stuttering mess. 'Err-... Arr-... Wha-...' Natalie merely overlooked the Jester's Gareth Gates moment and sat beside the star struck superstar who almost foamed at the mouth, when suddenly the living quarters door crashed open unveiling Nascosta Ireul Nerezza. Standing as a frightening sight and clad in his casual dress code of the familiar crimson red trench coat, the remaining member of Pranks With Attitude sighed before breaking the silence. 'Gaz, I was thinking. This match at Fusion, do you suppose it somehow came about as a result of our little stunt with Ellie?' As his partner gaped open his mouth to answer, the telephone sounded interrupting the proceedings. The trio peered at one-another, hinting for somebody to pick-up the receiver, a vicious circle if you would but in this case a treacherous triangle. 'For Christ sake-....' groaned the acquired valet. 'Oh! We get beat up so you don't have too, so the least you can do is answer the occasional phone-call. Maybe it's Alex asking for a second chance-... Tell him to hit the road as the conversation is getting a little tiresome, almost to the point that you would want to hit your head against a wall!' sarcastically snarled Nerezza as the behemoth slouched into a chair on the other side of the room. Grasping the receiver reluctantly Natalie made with the pleasantries, her mouth slowly arcing to that of a smile, before becoming a full-blown grin. 'Yeah, no problem - We'll be only to happy to attend - Ok - Thanks again and goodbye!' said the Russian native, her accent long gone, sounding nothing short of American and looking every bit the part. 'AND!?!' questioned the Conniving Comic, raising his eyebrows. Looking rather smug, Natalie's lips remained fastened only spurning the suspense. Keeping the information well guarded, she dangled subtle hints like bait, stringing the devious duo along. 'Guys-... We're going out-... Let's just say there's going to be loads of people including the media and those involved with Primetime Championship Wrestling-...' 'PRESS CONFERENCE IT IS THEN~!' interrupted the Lord Of Laughter with a dose of Extra Sensory Perception (E.S.P) taking the teams mouthpiece by surprise. 'HUH, HOW?' choked Ms. Miyagawa. 'My agent confirmed everything earlier but I was thinking of giving it a miss and leaving some lower card, mediocre talented, under utilised, non-televised, waste of roster space, curtain jerking, beer swilling, tax paying, washed-up, has-been-....' retorted the Emerald Menace in bated breath before an untimely intrusion by P.W.A's towering goliath. 'Whoa-... I never knew you thought so low of Tim Walden?!?' 'Tim Walden? Don't you mean Xavier Kanno-.... Seriously I was actually thinking of Ali Kadafi but most of that covered Statuz Quo, but now I think about it, Playaz Inc. in general' giggled the comedian you would love to hate, again taking cheap swipes at Pranks With Attitude's nemeses. 'So very true my green friend. It also applies to BladeD!!!' replied the Living Nightmare Nascosta Ireul Nerezza. 'LIKE HELLO, NATALIE HAS SOMETHING TO SAY~!' roared the miniscule manager of the current Tag Team Champions. Stopped in her tracks by the open palm of Gaz Green, she sulks further into her chair, looking every bit the spoilt brat. A few jerks of rewind and fast-forward by the video recorder and the footage finally begins on the memorable match involving Commissioner Ellie and the Military Man BladeD, which has been considered by many of the critics as a one-sided affair. 'Poon Puppy Pummel, Shag Squirrel Sharpshooter, Fuck Ferret Flap-Jack and the all important Ellie Bitch-Slap 'O' Doom!' commentated the fan favourite. 'Must make a mental note of these moves as Ellie has a better arsenal than say, me!' continued the cheeky comic his commentary skills surpassing that of Duke Williams-... Err-... Arr-... Dukestar The Commentating Cowboy Midget, or whatever his name is. 'HALLMARK!!!!' hissed the Angel Of Fear as watched the CEO Xavier Kannon spike his wife's opponent, allowing the dizzy diva to pick-up the three count. 'That almost brought a tear to my eye. I can sympathise with BladeD, I've been victimised courtesy of the Hallmark-....' injected Gareth Green, sarcastically rubbing his eyes. Gloating at the fact that his vocal prowess has tabled Kannon's Hallmark by victimising most of the current crop of PCW competitors, when suddenly his jovial attitude was dashed by the witty comment of Nerezza. 'And there's me thinking your sympathy was over his loss to a woman. CHARLOTTE~!' 'That was like so back in the Academy, I mean hello, I'd rip her arms off and beat her with them now. What about me being a five time 'A' scoring, five time 'A' scoring student, huh?!?' retaliated the Jester taking offence to his pitiful past being dragged up. 'Talking about the Academy-... We have that hearing about copyright infringements next week-...' concluded the Spawn Of Evil adjusting his weight in the confines of his seat, a desperate attempt to get comfortable. Watching Ellie roll the beaten carcase of BladeD up for the win, draws a smile from Natalie Miyagawa who seems to be adjusting to the life-style of P.W.A. Gazing down at her watch, it appears time is passing by and the inevitable press conference looms, prompting the petite Russian to rally her troops into action. 'Get your asses into gear - We have a press conference to abuse and with some of the things said today I feel we're going to raise a few eye-brows!' Standing erect in a pose of authority Natalie urges P.W.A to make the effort and attend. The scene finally closing on Natalie's teacher’s stance, her face looking rather devious, piercing eyes accompanied by the sinister grin that usually adorns Gaz Green's face. - To be continued -
