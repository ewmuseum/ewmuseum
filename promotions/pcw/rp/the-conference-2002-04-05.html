The Conference

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/02 *Monologue* Disappointment. Defeated in expectation or hope. Perhaps a man’s expectation has been defeated. This man being Big Daveylicious. It seems unfortunately for him that his PCW career has been nothing but a disappointment. No gold. No glory. Nothing happening. The only thing Big D has left is his history and his tag team. A man without nothing to lose. A man with nothing to lose can be a very dangerous man. What has been going through his head? After another damn interference screwing him out of another title. His frustrations and anger continue to build up. This is very dangerous. It’s not good. He gots to let out this anger before he does something he’ll regret. Like eventually killing LeBlanc. The PCW would be left out of their newly crowned jobber king. Oh, right that’s nothing to regret. But instead of killing LeBlanc what does Big D do? What great act of revenge does he thrust upon? He costs the coward Jason Core the Extreme title to LeBlanc! Sure, losing the title is bad. But losing to LeBlanc is even worse. It’s like your death in wrestling. Not that LeBlanc can really end anybodies career but losing to him does! Why?! Why you ask? It’s simple really. Maybe Big Daveylicious is tired of seeing LeBlanc vs. Jason Core night in and night out with the same results. Maybe he is tired just like you and me seeing Core fight this jobber so he can have a good title regain. LeBlanc vs. Jason Core match’s are on of the reasons this company is in debt. Without the help of Big D implanting Cores head onto that steel chair with a brain buster Core would have had another easy win. Maybe if Core would fight some people that are actually good than this wouldn’t have happened. But what can you do to get a cowards reaction? You prove it. (The scene fades to April 1st inside a white room. In the middle of the room stands a large beat up conference table. Each seat but one is occupied by the doctor whom brought Big Daveylicious to the psychiatric ward, Dr. Nina Hanhorn. Along sitting by her four other psychiatric residents, and their chief, DR. Owen Stamack. The door of the white room opens, out walks the man whom costed Core the Extreme title, Big Daveylicious. He’s dressed in the same khaki pants, shirt, and robe we saw him in before. He is followed behind an orderly. He sits in the one empty chair facing the doctors. The agitated Big D speaks.) Big Daveylicious: This is a place for crazy people. I’m not crazy. I just love naked women. Resident: We don’t use the term “crazy.” Big Daveylicious: Oh, really? What do you use? Insane, psychos, idiots, loons?! Resident: We actually use the term “mentally ill“. Big Daveylicious: I’m not ill. I got skill to thrill, kill, and do death-defying spills. Dr. Nina Hanhorn: Kill? Big Daveylicious: Well, I haven’t killed anybody. But I have killed their careers. Dr. Owen Stamack: Mr. Daveylicious, please. Enough of this nonsense. Big Daveylicious: Who are you to boss the likes of me? Dr. Owen Stamack: My name is Dr. Owen Stamack. Big Daveylicious: Stomach? Dr. Owen Stamack: No, St-am-ack. Big Daveylicious: What kind of name is Stamack? Dr. Owen Stamack: What kind of name is Big Daveylicious? Big Daveylicious: Who writes this shit? (As Stamack is about to answer that Hanhorn interupts the Doctor by standing up to get the attention of Big D. Which it does.) Dr. Nina Hanhorn: Mr. Daveylicious...you told me... (She looks into a file.) Dr. Nina Hanhorn: and I quote “I’ll be your crazy little patient”. Big Daveylicious: You will? (He winks at her smiling devilishly. She rolls her eyes disgusted.) Dr. Nina Hanhorn: You said that. Why did you say that? Big Daveylicious: Because I wanted to have sex with you. (The doctors all look at each other with glances.) Dr. Owen Stamack: Do you want to have sex with us? Big Daveylicious: No, I don’t swing that way. (They all look at each other again.) Dr. Owen Stamack: Why would you want to have sex with her than us? Big Daveylicious: Because I’m not gay. Dr. Owen Stamack: You’re not happy?(Big D has a very pissed disgruntled look on his face. He jumps out of his chair.) Big Daveylicious: You damn right I’M NOT HAPPY! Dr.Nina Hanhorn: Why’s that? Big Daveylicious: You really want to know why? You tricked me with sex! Damn you women! You used your sex appeal into tricking me into this mental hospital! Dr. Owen Stamack: So if you were happy you would have sex with us? (Big D hits his hand smack down on the table.) Big Daveylicious: NO! I’m not a HOMOSEXUAL! (The orderly lunges forward for Big D but is restrained by a hand signal by Dr.Hanhorn.) Big Daveylicicious: I’m not gonna hurt anyone. Do I honestly look like somebody who would hurt somebody? Dr. Nina Hanhorn: You are a Professional Wrestler. Big Daveylicious: So? Dr.Nina Hanhorn: You’re paid to hurt people. Big Daveylicious: Stop changing the subject. You manipulated me into this hospital by using your sex appeal and because my partner, Mike has multiple personalities. Dr. Owen Stamack: I thought you said you weren’t a homosexual. Big Daveylicious: TAG TEAM PARTNER! Dr. Nina Hanhorn: Calm down, Dave. Take deep breathes. (Big D takes a deep breath and releases it.) Big Daveylicious: Mike Frampton is my tag team partner. Dr. Nina Hanhorn: Why do you think we should let you go out of our facility? Big Daveylicious: Because I was falsely accused of being insane. I’m just... Resident: Sorry but we don’t use that term...”insane.” (Big D takes a look at him and pounds the table again.)Big Daveylicious: SICK OF THESE DAMN INTERRUPTIONS! How’d you like it if I’d interrupt you? Deep down inside I am a sensitive human being. It just tears me up when somebody thinks I am bad. You think I am insane. That’s even worse. Why we got guys in the PCW who think they are an Egyptian God, a Superhero, and even a man who thinks he is gladiator in Ancient Rome! AND YOU GOT ME! I DON’T WANT A DAMN SPONGE BATH AGAIN! Dr.Owen Stamack: Well Mr.Daveylicious, your gonna continue to get a sponge bath by our orderlies due to the fact YOUR NOT LEAVING... (Big D falls to his knees and looks straight up screaming.) Big Daveylicious: Whhhhhhhhhhhhhyyyyyyyyy?! Dr. Owen Stamack: April Fools. (The orderly next to him taps him on his shoulder. Big D stops screaming as he turns his shoulder to notices the orderly.) Big Daveylicious: I’M NOT TAKING A SPONGE BATH! Orderly: Say it don’t spray it. Sir you are free to go. (Big D looks at the doctors whom are laughing.) Big Daveylicious: I can go. Dr. Nina Hanhorn: Yes, you can go. (A smile comes over with Big D as the scene fades to black.)
