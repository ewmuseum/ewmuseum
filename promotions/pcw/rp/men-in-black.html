Men In Black

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July, 3ed 2002 (Duke Williams walks into a theater surrounded by press. He is dressed in all black from his boots to his shades. His hair is back in a pony and his cloths are neatly pressed. Its is the world premiere of Men In Black Two where Duke Williams makes his second cameo reprising his role as the alien wise guy. He is stopped on the red carpet by an entertainment tonight reporter.) ET: You excited about Men IN Black two? Williams: Johnny Cash is here? ET: The movie….the one your in? Williams: If I’m in it your damn right I’m excited. You don’t see kids who call themselves legends in movies do you. ET: Ummm no? Williams: They may win big tournaments but damn it hossette they ain’t in movies!! ET: Right!! So have you seen Will Smith tonight? Williams: The colored guy? The fresh prince? No why? Did he steal something? If its one thing you shouldn’t trust its rappers and Mexican devils. There not honest people like thee old Malice Man. ET: O…k? Williams: Have you seen Tommy Lee? Tommy Lee and me used to pick up stripers in Tijuana after drinking the town dry, I’m afraid I may have impregnated one of them salsa bangs many years ago cus some rassler thinks he’s the devil. Everybody knows I’m the real red reaper. ET: So did you enjoy making the movie? Williams: They put a lot of damn makeup on me. I know I’m good looking but damn they made me look like a monster. ET: You were an alien. Williams: No young lady I was born in America!! El D is the one with out the papers. Shove his ass on a boat and say adios! ET: Who is El D? Williams: Jesus Christ I can tell your not a tennis fan are you? ET: Huh? Williams: I wish I were stoned right about now. ET: You smoke dope? Williams: Oh yea Tommy Lee, Willie S, and Duke Dubya got high and happy on the set of this here movie. ET: Tommy Lee Jones and Will Smith? Williams: No Tommy Lee Thomas and Will Smith Bodine. They were extras. ET: Ok…..ahhhh….thanks. Williams: Anything sweet cheeks. Would you like my number? ET: That’s ok. Williams: Playing hard to get that’s kind of sexy. ET: What ever. (Duke walks into the theater were he runs into Tommy Lee Jones who’s talking to some rich people.) Tommy: DW how are you? Williams: All right hoss! Tommy: This guy here is an animal!! Him and I went to the same high school. Wild as the west. (The rich folks stare at Duke) Williams: So you ready to get rowdy with the new movie? Tommy: You know it Mr. Williams. Is that a PCW camera crew behind you? Williams: Yes sir they wanted to run a special for the camera’s. Tommy: Say can I join you in hyping your next match? I’ve always wanted to do that. Williams: I’d be honored. Tommy: Get Will over here. (Somebody grabs big Willy) Willy: What’s up Tommy? Big Daddy Duke! How the hell are you. Tommy: Duke is going to hype up his next match and is going to let us in on it. Willy: Damn right we let him in our damn movie!! Lets hype baby…HYPE!! Williams: Lets rock!! (The three men stand side by side with Duke in the middle. The Men in Black our in there suits and Duke is in all black.) Williams: This Friday on Fusion El Diablo…the Mexican Devil will be destroyed by a streak of Malice so mighty no body can stop it. (In his wise southern accent) Tommy: Now listen to my for a moment here would you? Look El D you’re a star but your not super. Duke is super and is a super star. Now Batman is cool but he’s not super you understand? Will: What the Hell ARE YOU TALKING ABOUT? Taco D you’re a joke. Look at you with that Ultimate Visanta Fox paint job. Your going to get whack when my boy Dukie come and kick you ass! Williams: Straight up!! Holmes your ass is mine!! Then Legend INC is mine with his phony nice guy persona. He has no respect for the old. Tommy: None of these kids do. Will: Wait a minute. Tommy: Shut up Will! These kids need to be taught the traditions of the past and relish the future. It’s time hoss its Malice Time. Williams: El D and Legend Inc Get ready to go Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound….hoss. Will: and kids that’s the honky tonk truth! (The camera fades out)
