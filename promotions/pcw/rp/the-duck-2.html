The Duck

undated

Date: Part 2 Welcome to GOBLIN HQ, the secret, high tech facility designed specifically for the world dominating needs of any Goblin super villain bent on world domination. “GETITAWAYGETITAWAYGETITAWAY!” And here’s just the little sprite himself, starting an ordinary day within his humble abode. Joining him within the corridors of his HQ as he sprints for his life, we watch Syko the Goblin dash by in a rash kind of way. Waddling at his heels, that Mallard Duck pursues with a powerful yearning… Duck: “(Foaming at the bill.) QUACK!” …and he’s followed by Syko’s goons, a bandaged LAB and Esther who trail with a limp, each holding a net. LAB: “Heeereee Ducky, Ducky, Ducky!” Just when we think we have a lock on things, directions reverse; the Goblin, duck, goons, nets, ect all parade by again… STG: “(Arms flailing.) AHHHHHHHH!” Duck: “QUACK!” LAB: “VAIT! COME BACK DUCKY!” (CRASH) Catching up to the chaotic scene already in progress, we find the whole gang in struggle within the tech lab. Amongst scattered tools and a tipped lab bench on the floor, Syko is pinned down by the webbed feet of the rabid Duck. It flaps, and hisses ferociously at the pair with nets, cautiously closing in. Looks like it’ll be a long busy day! --- Three bloody, agonizing, and bone shattering hours later. “An enigma, zat iz ze only explantion!” The innards of the lab lie in cluttered ruin, as if someone had gutted the place, then shoved it all back in. Shattered viles and beakers, disassembled creations of mechanized form, broken equipment and tools, chunks of foundation… “Ze specimen seems to have extraordinary attributes uv like no other!” “QUACK!” Residing beneath a flickering light that survived the chaos, using equipment salvaged from the wreckage, Syko and LAB survey the destructive Duck that is strapped to a propped up operating table. Several reading devices are hooked up to the duck’s body, monitoring its vital stats. STG: “BRILLIANT OBSERVATION, ARISTOTLE!” Something is twisted about this scene. LAB: “Hmph! Zere is no explaination to zis enigma” The mad scientist is scoured with gauze on the head, the wrap slanted to serve as an eye patch, his arm rests stiffly in a sling, and he supports his weight on one foot and a crutch. Yet, Syko is unscathed. STG: “BAH! This bastard creature is bent on crushing me, and you dare compliment it?!” LAB: “Actually, zat is incorrect.” STG: “What do you mean, ‘Incorrect?’ Like… I’m wrong or something?!” The old man touches his nose, with an obvious gesture intended. STG: “(Indignant.) PREPOSTUROUS!!” From the corner of his eye, Syko watches the duck struggle a bit against its binds, trying eagerly to get as close as possible to the Goblin, even if only a few centimeters. STG: “(Calmly.) It chased me home, and destroyed my domicile in pursuit of me… IF NOT TO ANNIHILATE ME, THEN FOR WHAT?!” The old man grins. LAB: “To become ze von and only soul mate for you.” Duck: “(Agreeably, nodding.) QUACK! QUACK!” Shocked by the statement, the Goblin tenses, clenching his fist, and flaring with anger. STG: “YOU’RE OUT OF LINE, INFIDEL! DARE YOU HONESTLY TEMPT THE KILLING UPON THE AQUATIC BEASTS UNSHACKLING?!” LAB: “Oh, Zire, believe me. I don’t doubt zat ze bird vould rampage me if I vere to accidentally look at him ze wrong way. But, at least here my theory out. Vile I am beaten badly, you are unharmed. Ze bird…” Slowly inching his good hand towards the duck in demonstration, LAB changes the fowl’s mood almost instantaneously. The operating table begins to violently shake in turn from a ferocious attempt to lash out from the duck. Syko steps back, but LAB continues to move his hand ever closer to the snapping bill of the Duck. Duck: “GRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR!” The tip of his finger is barely within biting distance… LAB pulls his hand back just as the bound duck tries to chomp it off! LAB: “…It is not normal. No… It iz, shall ve say… A mishap uv nature? Zis creature hates all living zings, and vould like nothing more zan to kill zem all.” The hostile duck tugs at the belts, muscles bulging in an unearthly manner, as it howls out with a primal ferocity. STG: “…AND YOU’RE POINT?!” LAB: “Do you hate ze bird?” STG: “I hate everything, Grandpa!” LAB: “(Chuckling.) Exactly ze point! You and zis bird are von and alike! You are ze only person it vould ever understand!” STG: “BLASPHE-GHAR!” Without thinking, Syko snaps his chompers at LAB’s fingers! The old man, having baited the Goblin without warning draws his hand back quickly, cackling with delight! LAB: “WHOO! Almost got me there!” STG: “(Flustered.) THIS PROVES NOTHING! THAT BIRD CAN ROT IN HELL! I WANT IT OUT, NOW!!!” The shattered Old man begins to hobble out, laughing hysterically. LAB: “Oh, zen shall I summon ze national guard?! Bwahahah! Face ze facts… ZAT DUCK IZ NEVER LEAVING!” Duck: “QUAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAACK!” (SNAPSNAPSNAPSNAP!) The binds finally give to the unnatural might of the abnormal Duck, allowing it to break into a fluttering flight straight for Syko… STG: “NOT AGAIN!” Once again, the duck tackles the Goblin, only to smother him with an affectionate tongue bath. LAB: “Zere, now, ze? He loves you! Bwahaha!” Damn bird better not screw up my plans for Pendragon… DAMNATION! I’M SCREWED! To be continued
