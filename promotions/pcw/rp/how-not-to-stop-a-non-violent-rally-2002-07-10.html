How NOT To Stop A Non-Violent Rally

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07.10.02 Date: 07/10/02Time: 3:32 PMLocation: Oxford, Mississippi. Great news. The great news has hit Jordan X Shabazz and the rest of the Uprising. Not only would Rush get his first defense of his coveted Extreme Title. But Jordan X Shabazz would get a shot at the title that he longed for. A title that he believed was his. A title that John Crichton SNATCHED out of his hands: The PCW Television Championship. The last match Jordan X Shabazz had with John Crichton was marred by outside interference. Jordan had the match won, when Jaden stepped in and ruined it all. But the chances of that happening this time are very unlikely. For Jordan had gotten backup, before he had backup, but it wasn’t real. It wasn’t what he has with Thug roC and Rush Mason gave Jordan X Shabazz. Call me racist for saying this, but when black people have your back, they HAVE your back. When white people say, “I have your back” it doesn’t mean anything. You can’t trust white people. The scene opens up in Mississippi. This is the site of the upcoming PCW Tuesday Turmoil. Though Jordan, Rush, and Thug roC have to be in Louisiana for PCW Friday Fusion, in which they will take on John Crichton, Andy Murray, and Charlie Loc, Jordan X Shabazz has begged and pleaded with Rush and Thug roC to follow him to Mississippi. Why you ask? Because the KKK is holding their annual monthly rally, and Jordan X Shabazz, Rush Mason, and Thug roC are going to try and gather as many black people as they can, to stop this rally from going on. Anyways, back to our scene. The scorching Mississippi heat is beating down upon the HOT blocks and the HOT Mississippians walking the streets. We see Jordan X Shabazz, Rush Mason, Thug roC, and a group of angry black folks standing in front of a courthouse. They are screaming, and a whole lot of white people are walking hurriedly by, because they know… Lesson About Black People Number 2: When you see a group of angry black people screaming…RUN THE F*CK AWAY!! We hear some of the chants they are screaming. “F*CK THE KLAN! F*CK THE KLAN! F*CK THE KLAN!” is what is heard. Jordan X Shabazz holds a megaphone, ala the great Jimmy Hart. He is smiling. He had gathered all these people around; it was hard, because some black people are lazy ass bitches. So Jordan, Rush, and Thug roC went door to door, knocking on them, promising the people inside a barbecue within a few days. And you know how us black people love our barbecue… So now, there are about 30 people out there with Rush, Thug roC, and Jordan X Shabazz. Jordan raises the megaphone close to his lips. Jordan X Shabazz: “HELL NO, WE WON’T GO!!” As Shabazz screams into the megaphone, the crowd gets rowdier and rowdier (Insert K-Krush jokes here.) Finally, Rush thinks they’re rowdy enough to start the riot that they wanted, so his whispers in Jordan’s ear. Jordan relays the message to the crowd. Jordan X Shabazz: “IT’S TIME TO GO F*CK THEM WHITE DEVILS UP!!” The crowd cheers loudly as Jordan hops off of the small chair that he stood on to scream to the masses. Jordan started walking, with Rush and Thug roC beside him, walking towards the KKK rally… Meanwhile at the KKK rally… We see a group of people in white hoods. A HUGE screen that was obviously loaned to them by a Drive in theater plays a video of the LAPD punching a defenseless young teenager in the face, and smashing his head on the hood of their car. There are no sounds coming from the screen, instead, beside it stands a man in a white hood, smiling proudly on a podium with a microphone in front of it. Klan Leader: “Ladies and gentlemen, slowly but surely, the WHITE MAN is rising back to power! We have people on the force that are putting these dirty, no good, disgusting, car thievin’…” “SHUT YO’ WHITE ASS UP!!” One of Jordan X Shabazz’s trademark phrases is heard. The entire Klanvern of KKK members in their white robes and sheets turn around. They were sitting in plastic chairs, watching the big screen and hearing the speech beside it. The riot police are already standing by, knowing that the Klan gatherings are usually clean fun, but the possibility of a mob of angry black people rolling up was always in the back of the mind of everybody, so the police were necessary. The officers that were dozing off and now see Jordan X Shabazz, Rush Mason, Thug roC, and a group of angry people, standing in front of a Klanvern of the KKK, the most violent part too. Jordan X Shabazz waves at the KKK members, who are seething mad. Their peaceful gathering had been interrupted by a group of people who wanted nothing but violence, or so they thought. Jordan X Shabazz: “This state has always been known to be one of hate…” As Jordan X Shabazz begins his speech, the police officers, all ten of them, complete in riot gear, run in front of the group of people. Making a wall between them and the stirring Klanvern. Jordan X Shabazz: “But like a great man once said, ENOUGH IS ENOUGH AND IT’S TIME FOR A CHANGE!!” The mob behind Jordan cheer loudly, before anything else happens, the leader of this Klanvern speaks up. Klan Leader: “Why don’t you go back to Africa, you monkeys? Or better yet, move to LA where assaults like this are common.” This statement incenses Jordan X Shabazz, yet he doesn’t know how to reply. Now, when you think about, Jordan’s plan is chock full of holes. There are 30 of them… 30! Going up against about 50 hooded KKK members. The people in Jordan’s mob are mainly in their teens. They’re young, tired, and sweaty. They’re also thinking about the barbecue that Jordan has promised them. Slowly, starting with one kid, they all start leaving. Jordan doesn’t realize this for he is trying his best to keep cool in the hot Mississippi sun. Wearing a dark suit in this kind of weather isn’t exactly recommended. Jordan’s glasses are tight on his face, showing a look of intelligence. As the officers realize the thing is fizzling out because Jordan’s group of people have gone back to their homes. Now stand only Rush, Thug roC, and Jordan X Shabazz. When the KKK members realize that the cops are back to dozing off to sleep. The KKK members begin walking towards Jordan, Rush, and Thug roC; with menacing looks on their faces… this is ‘The Uprising’s’ cue. Jordan, Rush, and Thug roC all break out into a sprint in the opposite direction. Knowing that they’re black, and youthful, and white people are chasing them, they got away after running for about 30 seconds. They failed in their attempt to stop a hateful, yet non-violent gathering. But can Jordan bring gold back to the group that supports him? Can he follow in the footsteps of Rush Mason? A man that has been a bigger brother figure of sorts to him? Can Shabazz get revenge on John Crichton for screwing him out of a title that he so rightfully deserved? We’ll have to wait and see…
