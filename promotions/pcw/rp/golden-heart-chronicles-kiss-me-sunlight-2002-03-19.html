Golden Heart Chronicles: Kiss Me Sunlight

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/19/02 Time: 8: 15 A.M Location: New YorkScene: Chef’s Place “Kiss Me Sunlight.” Golden Heart Chronicles The scene opens with Juan Borro sitting down in Chef’s place. Chef, Bertha, and Juan are all present as Juan is sitting back and relaxing. Chef and Juan are going over some International Incident activities before they reveal it to the rest of the group. Juan: You know it’s hard to do things now days with all the politics floating around. You never know what is acceptable and what is not. Chef: You most definitely have that one right. Juan: Just look around in this country, there are so many laws and restrictions and people still go wild and make it one of the highest crime rate countries in the world. Does that make any sense? Chef: In a way it does. It shows people need space and can’t be restricted. If people were to not know of all of these things then they wouldn’t do them, but no the United States is run by a savage race of apes that think if they set a law people won’t rebel against it. Bertha comes into the room, and takes a seat near Chef. Bertha: Chef don’t you think you should go pick Elian up from the ballpark. He’s been there quite awhile. Chef: Okay, I’ll do it in just a second. Juan look on a little confused, almost as if he hasn’t just heard what he thinks he had heard. Juan: Did you just say Elian Gonzalez? If you did, I hope you mean you’re going to Cuba to pick him up from a call park. Chef: No he’s over here, we brought him over. The kid needs us, not that dirty ingrate he calls his father. Juan: Oh man! I knew you guys had something to do with Elian, but I didn’t think you would steal him. Chef: We didn’t steal him. Steal is such a strong word with side affects behind it. I like to use the word saved. We saved him from a world of being poor and not taking any show for the rest of his life. Plus, how did you know we had something to do with Elian? Juan: How easily do you forget things Chef? Kevin and I went to Cuba to gain Castro’s support and we actually did. He said how you seemed like a strong man when you tried to help Elian, but when he finds out you took him from his homeland he’s going to completely freak out. Chef: And how do you expect him to actually come into this country. Juan: He’ll be here in two days because I invited him to be come and see my title match. I planned the whole thing to get him over here, so the United States government is helpless. What are we going to do? Chef: I don’t know, but Castro’s support is a huge part of this campaign, the boy will have to go back. Bertha then comes charging in and lies down on Chef’s lap. Bertha: Oh Chef, please don’t do that. Elian like it here he looks up to me as if I was a mother, how can you make him go back. Chef: No. I don’t are what he thinks, the child is not worth enough for us to be punished from Fidel Castro. Bertha makes a moping face and responds. Bertha: He even said he wanted you to be him Culinary Conquistador? He wishes he could be like you when he grows up. Chef: He did. A tear comes from the eye of Chef in pure sentiment. Juan laughs, but then notices Chef is taking her seriously. Chef: You’re right Elian will stay. Juan: Chef are you kidding me, the kid probably didn’t say any of that. Bertha is lying to you and you know it. Chef: Shut up, and get in the car. Juan follows Chef out to the car, as Bertha shows up from behind Juan and hits him in the head with a frying pan. Juan: Owwwwwww!!!!! Bertha: Ah Juan, you know how much I love you and your golden heart, but if you ever try and take my Elian away, I’ll take you nuts and do the ritual pull, squeeze, and twist routine on them. Juan becomes frightened and takes a big gulp, as he goes running to follow Chef in his car. When Juan gets in the car, Chef sets off for the ballpark. Juan: Don’t you think someone might notice a Spanish-speaking boy named Elian in a ballpark? Chef: It’s New York, Juan; I’ll guarantee there are thousand of Spanish speaking kids named Elian. Juan: Hey I had a few other things to run by you that might be of some concern to you and the rest of the team. Chef: What is it, and make it quick, Elian doesn’t like to wait. Juan: Well I’ve been hearing you bailed that sack of crap Charlie Loc out of jail. What do we want with that disgrace of his nation? Chef: It’s a little plan I stirred up, don’t worry it won’t affect us too much. Just have to play with his mind a little bit and see what I can get out of it. Juan: Okay, fair enough. What should we do about this Malice Crew, with Frisco aboard I could just imagine there even more pissed. Chef: I really don’t care about those uncultured barbaric animals. We try to bring grace and pride to the ring, and then those dirty sleaze bags come in to bring us down. If they want a fight, they can have one, but they are just a pebble thrown at us in our fight to take over the industry. Juan: I agree, now I see this Legend Inc figure wants revenge on Frisco. They’re booked fro the upcoming Pay-Per View, as is Flaymin Aymin against that ungrateful demon El Diablo. Reeco Suave has a taste of gold coming at the Pay Per View, to add to the list. Chef: Frisco is a Legend, and Legend Inc. should take a few pointers. I expect Frisco to make short due of him. Flaymin Aymin seems like he’s in a world on his own, but knowing him he’d rather kill than loose his hair. We’ll make sure somebody like El Diablo doesn’t get one over on an International Incident member. Lastly Reeco should definitely win. He needs to shape up a little and set down his priorities, but the Pay-Per View should be a good night for International Incident. Juan: I agree most of us are yet to be booked also. I plan to get the double double sometime next week. Two Time Cruiserweight Champ and 2 Time Tag Champ. Chef: Either way it will work for us. International Incident has the premiere talent in this corporation; all we have to do is let It out. Chef turns into the parking lot of the baseball field Elian is playing on. As Chef leaves the car to get Elian, Juan notices the sunlight coming out. The sun is so colored and bright, and it reflecting off of the young and happy Elian. It’s almost as if the sun is kissing him. Juan sees happiness in the young boy, something he didn’t have much of as a young child. He sees the child and thinks of him as a younger him. When Juan was young his mother also died. Elian was lucky to have a caring father, which is something Juan never seemed to have. In a way Juan thinks what Chef is doing is wrong, but then he thinks of the world Chef opened to him, and it’s more of a great deed than something wrong. Elian enters the car and waves to Juan. Elian: Hola! Juan nods and smiles at the young child who is playing is throwing his ball up in the air and catching it. Juan: How long until we can stick this kid on the baseball field and make some money off of him. Everyone laughs and smiles, as Chef drives off. Juan knows what’s going on may stir up some commotion, but for the first time in a long time Juan doesn’t care. Juan is doing what he feels is right. Sure Juan has had evil thoughts and sensations, but a child is a child and no child should ever be harmed. With this Juan clears his mind, and waits for the problems to come to him. Until then: Together We Stand,Divided We Fall. Scene Fades to Black. All rights are reserved. Copyrighted by the Golden Chronicles 2002. Featuring Chef, Elian Gonzalez, and Bertha.
