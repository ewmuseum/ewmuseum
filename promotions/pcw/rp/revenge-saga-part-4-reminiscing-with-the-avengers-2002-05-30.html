Revenge Saga Part 4: Reminiscing with the Avengers…

30 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/5/02 Previously… The pupils back Crichton off against the nearest wall, all relentlessly beating him with the sticks. In one last-ditch effort, Crichton grabs out in the vain hope of grasping onto something, and connects with one of the wooden sticks. With all his might Crichton yanks the stick away, causing the pupil it was attached to, to impact against the wall at a ferocious speed indeed. And so the battle continued, Crichton’s ferocious rage taking over as he instinctively fights… Five minutes later, there was no longer a fight in progress, nor were any of the pupils still conscious. This had been a handy little exercise and it had taught him two lessons. The first was to never underestimate an opponent of any stature and the second was that when it came down to it, if he relied on his instincts he could win any battle. This was not battle tested enough for him to use it as a viable tactic though, something he had to remedy before exercising revenge upon the rich industrialist Miss Zemoff or for the extreme title match on Friday Fusion. He needed to be tested more and that’s when fate reared it’s ugly head. In the form of the Avengers! Crichton stood in the middle of the karate hall, the classes pupils surrounding him, unconscious and bloody upon the floor, with two wooden sticks in his hand. The battle had indeed tested his abilities to an extent, but it was far from satisfactory for his liking. He won the battle far too easy, something that he once was thankful for, but now he was doing the hunting rather than being hunted. The Avengers had responded to the commotion when one of their members, Scarlet Witch, was passing by and saw Crichton enter the establishment via the roof. She instantly recognised Crichton from previous encounters and after hearing what happened last time some of the Avengers ran in with Crichton, she thought it apt that she called them in to deal with situation. Within a couple of minutes of making contact with the Avengers mansion, with her communi-card, the Avengers were on the scene. The Avengers team on this day consisted of Captain America, Scarlet Witch, The Black Panther and Iron Man, three of the members that were responsible for Aeryn’s death in Crichton’s opinion and in this matter only his opinion counted. Crichton remembered the event like it was yesterday. Then without warning the door to the room, in which he was being held slowly opens and Crichton tenses himself up for confrontation with the damned scientists. The door opens agonisingly slow, but Crichton is relieved to discover Aeryn’s head creeping around the side of the door. Crichton’s face lights up with delight, as Aeryn steps into the room quietly closing the door behind her. “What took you so long?” Crichton questions sarcastically. Aeryn tuts slightly. “Now let me think, should I break you out or not? I’m going to be a while thinking ‘bout this one.” Aeryn jokes in response Crichton goes into a bout of fake laughing. “Your Funny! NOT! Now stop arsing around and get me out of thi…” Crichton’s words are cut off by the almost deafening noise of a gun blast. Aeryn simply stares on wide eyed, Crichton follows her gaze down to her chest to see the crimson red patch appearing in the middle. Aeryn looks back up again and simply slumps to the floor, revealing the shooter of the weapon, one Mr. Fleming. “Damn! I’ve always wanted to do that!” Fleming states insolently. “Damn you Fleming, if I ever get out of this I WILL kill you!” Crichton states voice full of anger. “That isn’t very likely now is it? For one your strapped up and two your going to be dead soon!” Fleming states fitting the evil villain stereotype perfectly. “Why are you doing this Fleming? How can this possibly benefit you!” Crichton screams. Fleming sighs. “You really are that stupid aren’t you! Because the Thunderbolts captured you and I didn’t I became the laughing stock of my agency, and since I no longer benefit from you being alive I now join Lyons campaign of wanting you dead. Except of course I will be the one who’s going to kill you” Crichton felt his anger reach maximum, he knew he had a temper, but hadn’t really had chance to explore it, his army life had taught him to become cool in a crisis, but this was different. Aeryn lay on the floor dying and he could feel something that hadn’t been there before he was captured. His muscles flexed and his anger grew to an almost unbearable level and without warning the metal shackles across Crichton’s wrists broke and his hands were now free. Crichton was as shocked as Fleming. Then the scientists run into the room, waving their hands about. “No! Your ruining our experiment, oh…” The first scientist trails off at the sight of the now free John Crichton. “I see our experiment has gone far better than we ever expected. He should only have just above human strength, now he’s got at least three times that!” The second scientist observes. Crichton didn’t register his comments at the moment and simply slowly paced forward intent on ripping Fleming’s throat out through his ass. Fleming snaps out of the shock and attempts to point the gun, however, Crichton quickly knocks the gun out of his hand and lifts him up in the air effortlessly. A couple of wild punches have no effect on Crichton whatsoever and he simply tosses Fleming, however, Fleming flies through the door and is knocked unconscious against the hard concrete wall. The scientists sensing danger attempt to run, but Crichton swiftly grabs them by their collars and lifts them up into the air. “What experiment?” He questions already knowing the answer. The superhuman experiment! We injected you with the serum as soon as you arrived.” The first scientist declares. Crichton simply grunts in anger as the scientists also find themselves flying towards the same concrete wall. Crichton turns back to Aeryn and lifts her up and drapes her over his shoulder he then goes in search of the armoury. Fleming was indeed the one who shot the woman he loved in the back, but the Avengers were ultimately to blame, for putting her in that situation in the first place. Both parties vowed revenge soon after the event and it looked like things were going to come to ahead right here in the Karate gym. The epic confrontation was still firmly fixed within Crichton’s memory. “Do you think that Aeryn is fairing well Captain America?” The Black Panther questions. “I don’t think so at all look!” Captain America states pointing to Crichton as he exits the building with Aeryn draped over his shoulder. “What happened Crichton?” The Black Panther asks. “She was shot! And I would begin to run if I was you!” He states bluntly, running towards the exit with considerable speed. Cap simply shrugs. “Come on Avengers lets get our asses out of here!” Cap states making his way toward the exit. When they left the military compound they never received much resistance and nobody was following them. Cap suspected that the just wanted rid of them. As they reached the Quntijet, a huge deafening explosion rocked the very ground they stood on. Cap looked up at Crichton to see him wearing a very sadistic smile indeed. “What the hell was that?” Cap questioned walking over to Crichton getting face to face with him. “Justice!” Crichton simply replies. “Innocent soldiers were at that base and they were killed because of YOU!” Cap stated beginning to lose his temper. “That’s what a soldier signs up for when joining the army, you know that!” Crichton replies. “How can you possibly justify that!” Cap enquired. “I wanted to destroy all remnants of that serum destroyed before it ruins anybody else’s life! And the same way you can justify sending an unarmed woman into a highly guarded military compound to rescue me!” Crichton replied with venom in his voice. Cap just stepped back and walked away. “It was her choice Crichton! Her choice!” Iron Man stated. “That don’t matter none. She is still dead and for that I will never forgive you! Let me tell you that this ain’t over yet!” Crichton stated and with that he simply walked away. The costumed clad Avengers entered the Karate gym and found Crichton with unconscious bodies surrounding him, with his head down, obviously contemplating something. Almost as if he senses their presence of the four superheroes, he raises his head to look them in the eyes. A broad smile fills his face, as he throws the wooden stick to the ground and steps over the unconscious bodies, lying upon the floor. The Scarlet Witch lets out a little shocked sound at the carnage before her. “Crichton, you’ve gone too far this time! I told you that if we ever saw you again, we would have to take you into custody, whether we were in fact responsible for her death! And this proves my point entirely.” Captain America states. Crichton cocks his head to one side. “Well it isn’t the ‘Mighty Avengers’! You come for a little bit of a workout too then?” He declares, sarcastically. “Come quietly Crichton! We’re taking you back and we are going to take a few tests.” Iron Man states bluntly. “Tests? What kind of tests?” Crichton questions. “We need to test the super serum the army implemented you with and map out the serums development through your system.” Black Panther answers. “There really is no need! I feel fine!” Crichton declares. “I’m afraid you do not have a choice in the matter Crichton, we will take you in and the only choice you have is whether you come quietly or whether we take you in by FORCE!” Iron Man replies, even more bluntly than before. “Oh I see! So you really think that you can take me down, eh? Well I need a workout, care to join me?” Crichton questions assuming a fighting stance. It doesn’t take anything more to provoke the might heroes and they begin to charge at him with murder in they’re eyes. And so the battle began…The most extreme challenge Crichton had come across so far in preparation for his extreme title match on Fusion. To be continued…
