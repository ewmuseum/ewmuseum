Date: not entered We gaze upon the plush confines of a waiting room, bathed in rich, Corinthian leather. The room speaks of dignity and intelligence, a large bookcase against the far wall and a dark and thickly lacquered mahogany coffee table snuggled between the waiting chars and a bored token receptionist buffing her fingernails at the desk guarding the office door. A placard on the door reads… Theodore Kaos, PHD. The door opens, and a woman, screaming hysterically, spins out the door, clutching at her head. Woman: “MY MIND!! What have you done… what have you done to me?!?! The voices!! They push me forward… edge me on!” A suit steps out. The statement is apt because it is essentially an Armani suit stretched over the frame of a hulking armor, the helmet glinting with red steel, a long yellow and glowing single oblong eyepiece where the eyes should be and a myriad of tubes connected to the back of eth head and a long single one to the mouth. A voice, clear as day, is transmitted through a the mouthpiece. Kaos: (waving a consoling hand) “No, no, no. You are well on the way to recovery, my dear. We’ve made a big breakthrough today. Regular time next week! Ha ha ha!” The woman crashes through the exit door, weeping and crying. Dr. Kaos seems to ponder for a minute, his finger held up to where his mouth should be. He leans in to his receptionist. Kaos: “Better get THAT one’s waiver-release cued up…” A booming voice: “CHAOS!!” We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he stares in wide-eyed anger at the doctor standing before him, as he steps through the rubble that was once the front door. Kaos (eyepiece red): “STEELE!! DIE DIE DIE DIE!!” A huge ray-gun appears in Kaos’ arms and he fires a fireball directly at the doorway, but Steele has already jumped out of the way. Kaos laughs maniacally as he repeatedly pulls at the trigger as Big Al leaps and rolls across the waiting room. Steele shoots across the room and slides across the receptionist’s desk and he ducks behind the furniture, as a huge flame scorches the desktop. BAS: “Hi-Keeba!!” Steele pops up from behind the desk and throws a paperweight with precision, and the desk ornament cracks into the gun, popping it back in Kaos’ hands. Big Alan Steele then crashes across the desk, and tackles the villain, and the two start wrestling on the waiting room floor. “Doctor! Doctor! He’s you next patient!” The receptionist bravely jumps on top of the wrestling pair, Steele on top with his hands wrapped around the neck portion of the armor. The eyepiece goes from red to yellow again, as the doctor kicks a leg up and flips Big Al off him, sending him flying into the bookshelf. Kaos: (happily) “Well, why didn’t you say so!” Dr. Kaos gets up off the floor and straightens his tie while smoothing his suit down. Steele picks himself up to a crouch, his eyes glaring with anger. Kaos: “Sorry about that, sport! I had forgotten your (trusted) manager (Sue Saksnussum) had set this little chat up. The old reflexes are still there, you know? I hope I haven’t damaged our doctor-patient relationship…” BAS: (standing up warily) “Nothing that cannot be repaired…” Kaos: “Excellent! Nancy, hold all my calls, please. Now, this way, if you will…” Steele fixes his own accoutrements and puts on a stony face, as he follows the doctor inside his office. Within the inner sanctum is more of the same. Tasteful opulence and a maximum of comfort. A long day bed, ala a couch, rests in the center of the room, along with a chair next to it for the doctor. His desk rests against the far war, and a catalogue of impressive-looking literature adorns the other wall. The skyline of San Jose peeks in through the picture windows of the corner office. Dr. Kaos clomps through the room, his titanium alloyed frame resounding off the wooden floor with each step. He unbuttons the bottom of his blazer and gestures to Big Al to lie down. Steele eyes the surroundings warily. Kaos: “Please, make yourself comfortable. After all, it’s you money paying for this hour.” BAS: (haughtily) “So, we are Kaos now, PHD?” Kaos: “Yeas, please. I’ve changed my ways after our last little tussle. I have since changed my name from CHAOS to Kaos, got an accredited degree, all legal, to compliment my massive brainery and now there is no mental dysfunction that can withstand my evil-genius might! BWAHAHAHA…Uh, sorry...I promised myself I wouldn't posture during office hours...I'm trying to work through my issues too, you know.” BAS: “Old habits are hard to break, eh Dr. Kaos? And perhaps we could also break the habit where you aim a focusing particle beam cannon at your prey as they lie helpless to your charms?” Dr. Kaos rubs the area where his chin should be, chuckling. He slowly makes his way to the bookcase and flicks a candleholder and the books flip upwards, revealing a powered-up beam cannon, glowing power flux cables sending energy to the cannon proper. Kaos stabs at a button, and the machine’s lights blink off. The lights of the office, however, grow stringer and more bright. Kaos: “We know each other so well, Steele, from our past skirmishes. So now we start anew: no tricks and no secrets. Now, please, make yourself comfortable. You have my honor as a doctor that you are safe.” BAS: (pfft!) “I knew I was safe from our last battle! You cannot defeat me!” Kaos: (dark shadows growing around him) “I would have if I had accounted for your idiotic reasoning… who can predict your imbecility?!” BAS: “Tis not easy being in my rut! Hi-Keeba!” Kaos: (growling) “Yes, Hi-Keeba, ha ha ha. Lay down, now.” Steele slowly eases himself onto the long couch, Dr. Kaos rubbing his hands in glee. Kaos: “Now, try to relax, as I know that the situation must be rather uncomfortable for you…” BAS: “ZzzzzzzzzzzzZ.” Steele’s tongue is lolling out of his mouth, a puddle of drool forming on his shoulder. The eyepiece on Kaos glows crimson, as he roars and kicks at the couch, jolting Big Al back into consciousness. Kaos: “Not TOO comfortable! Now… where shall we begin?” A long, pregnant pause as Kaos waits somewhat impatiently as Steele starts to test the cushioning of the couch. Kaos: “WELL?!” BAS: “Alas, I do not know where to begin, nor why am was scheduled here today. Ms. Sue fears for my sanity on a daily basis, yet she has never forced such as your ilk upon my person.” Kaos: (sighing) “Okay, perhaps I shall get the ball rolling here.” BAS: “I would appreciate it!” Kaos: “Okay… have you been feeling unloved lately? Possibly unwanted?” BAS: (thinking hard) “Not so much unloved as universally hated.” Kaos: “Good, good! Go with that!” BAS: “Tis true! Take my most recent booking! The venomous New Breed circles the ring like sharks with a taste for human blood while taking on Tyler Nelson, a vile, evil being able to wield men’s minds as controlled weapons and as devious as Hades himself! Everyone is out to get me, Big Alan Steele, son of Zeus!” Kaos: “Aha! A breakthrough already! Your mind has chosen to manifest your ambitions for the PCW championship title into paranoia due to… um… you know. That thing that does that.” BAS: (dubious) “FULLY accredited?” Kaos: (indignant) “Hollywood Upstairs Psychiatric Kollege! It’s very, very exclusive!” BEEP! The intercom on the desk rings loudly, the receptionist speaking loudly. Receptionist: “Doctor Doom on Line one. I’ll patch him through on speaker phone…” Kaos: (screaming) “NO! I said no calls!” Doom: (on speakerphone) “Hey bud? Find out Big Alan Stooge’s weaknesses yet?” SMASH! Kaos splinters the intercom with one of his metal fists. Kaos: “Ha ha ha… er, yeah. Um, so… you were saying?” BAS: (Narrowed eyes) “Yessssss… as I was saying, sometimes I feel the entire weight of the PCW upon my broadened shoulders, not unlike Lord Atlas! Though I am capable of such extremes, I have to admit it tears at me so.” Kaos: “Interesting. Perhaps you are fearful…” BAS: (roaring) “Big Alan Steele fears NO man! Nothing can shake the immovable object of nigh invincibility!” Kaos: “Nigh invincibility? What is it that can… strip you to your core, Big Al? What is it that makes you fearful?” BAS: (gulping hard) “Clowns.” Kaos: (writing down feverishly) “Ah, clowns. It is either hilarity or horrors. When confronted at a young age with force we cannot understand, it can imprint terrible things into our psyche.” BAS: (shaking fist angrily) “My psyche is impeccable!! How dare you besmirch my powerful psyche!” Kaos: (holding hands up to calm) “Okay, okay. So the son of Zeus isn’t challenged by his id. But here’s what you need to do, Big Al. You need to go out there, and face your paranoia head on! No more running from it! If you feel these people have a problem with you, then confront them! Once you take the proverbial bull by the horns, you’ll find your problems to be mere figments of your imagination!” BAS: (confused) “But… but that is what I shall be forced to do anyways!” Kaos: “Really? Well, then perfect! Mental health awaits you, my boy! Go! Go and chase away your demons!!” BAS: (standing, bewildered) “Yes… chase the demons.” Kaos: “And I want you to send me FRONT ROW tickets, so I can view your progress against your problems.” BAS: (scratching head) “Free comps for you…” Kaos: “Yes! Now go, and allow me to get my clown-warriors prepared! Oh, by the way… here’s your bill.” Big Al absent-mindedly grabs the bill and shuffles towards the exit door. Kaos waves, his helmeted head almost smiling… BAS: (astounded) “One thousand and sixty-one dollars?!?! Tis criminal!” Kaos: “Well, all my services ARE listed there…” BAS: “HI-Keeba!!” Steele turns, his face turning red, when CLANG!! Kaos shoves Big Al to the outside just as a three-foot thick steel-reinforced door drops down over the original doorway. Steele bangs pounds his fists on the door, shouting heroic epithets the entire time. BAS: “By the gods! You shall pay for such treachery and deceit! Come out from behind your cowardly tricks and face me man to man-god!!” Kaos: (overheard inside, to his secretary) “Ha ha ha ha ha! You know, I don’t know how other psyches get along without that little wonder… so? Who’s next?” BAS: “DAMN YOU TO HADES!!! HI-KEEBA!!”
