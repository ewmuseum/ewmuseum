Coming of age

19 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/05/03 'Aren't you Manuel Labor?' they used to ask. I'd merely nod my head and ask how they were doing. I didn't really think much of it. Being a well-known wrestler at PCW isn't a big deal. So what... people knew who I was. It's hard to miss me, considering my 'situation'. Most of the people who called me by name were girls-- most of the guys would just glare at me. 'You're amazing,' they'd say as their boyfriends stared me down. 'Give me a call sometime?' If you're lost, then you haven't been paying attention to me. It's okay. Neither has anyone else. THESE CLOUDS AROUND YOU BREAK YOUR FALL AS YOU CAME CRASHING TO THE GROUND DID YOU LEARN ANYTHING AT ALL? YOU CLIMB BACK UP TO COME BACK DOWN But where am I going? There's a sort of no-man's land that I've become stuck in, mired in the mid-card. Every single fan sees me as better than three quarters of the roster, but not near as good as the rest. I'm better than everyone below me and yet I stand no chance against those above me. As far as most of them are concerned, I'm just a warm-up match for The End or Dorn Tracer. I'm just another Mexican for them to mock before the main event. EVERYBODY KNOWS YOUR NAME BUT THEY DON'T KNOW WHO YOU ARE BUT TO THEM IT'S JUST THE SAME YEAH, YOU'RE JUST ANOTHER NAME Fuck you all. I've beaten Flaymin Aymin, even when no one thought I could do it. El Diablo, he's another one that lost to me. I'm nauseated by my image -- I have no image. Ask around to the fans, and half of them will get me confused with Edwards or Jackson. I'm just so close to making a mark that will make me memorable, and yet I'm so far away from where I want to be. See, that's the goal. The prize, the thing my eye is always set on. People coming up to me in a restaurant, asking for signatures. Despite the fact I'm a Cruiserweight, I want people to think I'm good enough that whether they like me or hate me, they want my autograph. I want women to dream about me at night. Fan clubs. Little boys who bounce from Fred Durst to Blink 182, they should be emulating me. I want to see second grade boys bouncing on the trampolines at gym, hitting the rise to legend screaming about how they hit him with the finishing move, 'just like Manuel Labor!' SHE SAID FAME WILL BRING YOU DOWN AT LEAST THAT'S WHAT SHE USED TO SAY THEN I HANDED HER ANOTHER CROWN SHE SAID IT WOULD NEVER BE THIS WAY JUST ANOTHER NAME I am Manuel Labor, but I'm so much more than that. Or maybe I'm so much less than that. Is wrestling a piece of me, or am I a piece of wrestling? EVERYBODY KNOWS YOUR NAME BUT THEY DON'T KNOW WHO YOU ARE BUT TO THEM IT'S JUST THE SAME YEAH, YOU'RE JUST ANOTHER NAME JUST ANOTHER NAME YESTERDAY SHE WAS JUST A LITTLE GIRL PRETENDING SHE WAS QUEEN SHE DIDN'T KNOW IT WOULD CHANGE HER WORLD DIDN'T KNOW WHAT THIS WOULD MEAN I want more than anything to succeed, become larger than I could have ever been in the same little life that my parents bought for me. It's not that I'm ungrateful or that it wasn't good. It was safe and yet it hurt me more than you could imagine. At least now, when I get hurt, I can say that I made the choices that caused it. Manuel Labor comes of age this week. Pinning Adam Stryker would do nicely, but all I want is to be remembered. The fans shall stand and chant my name, or pour down boos, or scream in anticipation... but whatever they do, they'll walk out of that arena with my face emblazoned in their memory. They'll tell their kids one day about the time they saw this brilliant cruiserweight beat up this guy, and how he was so amazing, and that he went by the name of Manuel Labor. The Prologue is over and I'm moving on to Chapter One. This weekend, the Lucha Legend is born. WHICH MASK WILL YOU WEAR TODAY? HOW ABOUT THE ONE WITH THE PRETTY SMILE? TO YOU IT'S JUST ANOTHER DAY IN A LIFE YOU HAVEN'T LIVED IN QUITE AWHILE EVERYBODY KNOWS YOUR NAME BUT THEY DON'T KNOW WHO YOU ARE TO THEM IT'S JUST A GAME AND I THINK IT'S GONE TOO FAR
