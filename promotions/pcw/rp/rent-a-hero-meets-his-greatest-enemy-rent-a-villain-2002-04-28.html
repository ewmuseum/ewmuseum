Rent-A-Hero Meets His Greatest Enemy....RENT-A-VILLAIN!

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Rent-A-Hero Meets His Greatest Enemy....RENT-A-VILLAIN! Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 4-28-2002 ..::/::..Floating....floating lifelessly within a giant tube of an unknown liquid is where we find none other then PCW's only superhero, Rent-A-Hero. The room about him is dark, the only light being emitted being from the aforementioned tube housing our hero. The liquid is a strange indigo color, our hero floating within it as he looks to have been stripped from his usual spandex replica superhero costumes in favor of tubes and thin wires within his skin, a pair of tight white shorts, and the remnants of his Spiderman 2099 costume, being his red and black shimmering metallic mask. Eyes closed, no life, and seemingly dead within the tube, our hero is suspended within its confines after being captured and taken by C.L.I.T....a trip to confront the group about their harassing turning into a revelation of Rent-A-Hero's origin...born from a test tube and meant to be a weapon of destruction who turned about to be a failure and would soon became the hero we know him to be and we've grown to love. The same man who's energy flowed constantly and his love for life made obvious...just floats in peace, not a disturbance in the world as it seems. All this would soon end as from nowhere, a large steel wrench rips through the air and slams against the large glass tube, shattering the glass as an intricate web of cracks overtakes the tube, the weight of the water pressing down as minuscule glass chips begin to tear away from the tube, tiny spouts of water flowing free and without restriction out of the tube. Shortly, another mighty impact from the steel wrench hits the tube, the glass shattering violently all over the surrounding area, a wave of water and broken shards of glass rushing out...leaving our hero suspended in the air by the tubes mentioned before, water trickliing off his ripped and well-built body. Lights begin to cut on set by set from the ceiling, the room filled with illumination as the light begins to shower our hero's closed eyes, which begin to slightly crack open, rapidly fluttering from opened to close as the light continues to bombard his eyes. For the first time in an unknown time span...he is able tos ee the world, now noticing his surroundings inside the sophisticated laboratory, looking down at himself and shocked to find the tubes and wires attached to him. While looking about...he sees a familiar face in front of him...the lovely face of Miss Clarise Honeypanties, the lovely C.L.I.T. secertary from before who helped our hero to escape....and in a turn of the unlikely, it is Miss Honeypanties who has the steel wrench in her hand, now confirming who Rent-A-Hero's savior was. Her long black dress is now soaked and damp, coming formt he excess of water hitting her from before as she presses a series of buttons on a tiny control panel next to what used to be the glass observation tube. In mere seconds, the wires and tubes retract from Rent-A-Hero's body, his buoyancy now gone as he falls fromhis elevated base and lands on the metal floor of the tube with a loud !!!THUD!!! Rent-A-Hero catches his breath and pushes himself up with his hands, getting up to one knee before exclaiming..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'HOLY UNDERWATER SNORKLING ADVENTURES...THAT WAS NOT WIZZER!!' *Cough Cough* 'Clarise...might I ask what your doing here? It seemed as if you'd be as unlikely to save me as Vanilla Ice is unlikely to be leader of the NAACP...' ..::/::..The lovely and pure Clarise walks up the small steps and helps our hero to his feet slowly, using all her strength to get him up to a standing position as she explains in her usual nice and heartfelt tone..::/::..Clarise:'I noticed you didn't come out of Miss Hennessey's office and I got suspicious...I did some snooping around and I come to find this place and I saw you within the tube. I had NO idea this was what C.L.I.T. was all about...I feel so betrayed Rent-A-Hero. I believed in this cause and wanted to see it succeed, then I come to find it's just a ploy to lure you and much worse....play God. I read the archives and Rent-A-Hero...I'm sorry...I'm so sor...' Rent-A-Hero:'No need for apologies Miss Honeypanties....you were unaware, I can't help what I now know I am.' Clarise:'Are you ready to escape?'Rent-A-Hero:'I'm afraid that's not an option Clarise...I won't leave this cursed placed until it is DESTROYED and I find out who it was that created me...so I can BIZNITCH slap him across these walls. I won't let this genetic engineering take place ANY longer. It's a crime to play with lives and they will pay for that injustice...just as Chef shall pay for his injustices against the United States! With Juan Borro by my side, me and him will WALLOP this supervillain for his many injustices to the PCW and the good ol' U. S. of A. he has commited...him and his filthy clone of that wretched Bertha too! Rent-A-Hero ain't playing games, unless their PS2 quality of course, anymore and it's time that both these villains be slayed NOW!! I've got work to do! Clarise, I suggest you make your way out before things get as ugly as Rosie O Donald's backside in this facility..'Clarise:'No...I came to find you, now I'll help uou destroy this God forsaken place...'..::/::..Our hero flashes a warm smile to the brave Clarise as she helps our hero off the platform where the tube used to sit, Rent-A-Hero gaining more strength in his legs as they continue traversing foward, leaving the room where our hero was left in by himself...upon exiting the room our hero is in awe of the pleathora of advanced technological equipment littering the floor below, computers of all types fillignt he level below, and in a lighted circle in the very middle of the below level are dozens of tubes much like the one our hero was just encased in...what's within them unclear from the level Clarise and Rent-A-Hero are at..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'My God...this place is unbelieveable...' Clarise:'Tell me about it, this place has been under here all this time and I had no idea Rent-A-Hero...but I don't think you want to see what's down there.' Rent-A-Hero:'I fear nothing Clarise...I must see it.' ..::/::..Clarise nods hesitantly, helping Rent-A-Hero over to the spiraling steel staircase that leads downward, both taking small careful steps down as loud clanking sounds accompany their every step until finally, the duo reaches the lower level with the many consoles, monitoring equipment, and monitors set up all seeming to have something to do with the tubes. Our hero's focus immediately turns towards the dormant tubes, our hero breaking away from Clarise as he slowly limps his way over to the nearest one within that illuminated circle. Clarise turns away, afraid of the reaction of Rent-A-Hero as she braces herself for whatever he may say. Our hero approaches the tube slowly and with extreme caution, finally right next to it as he cups his hands around his eyes and takes a look within....shortly jumping back in utter anger as he looks to Clarise, pointing back at the tube while exclaiming..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'IS THIS SOME KIND OF SICK JOKE?! THAT'S NOT WHAT I THINK IT IS!! IT CAN'T BE REAL...IT'S NOT REAL!!'Clarise:'But it is Rent-A-Hero....no matter how much you may choose to deny it..it is.'..::/::..The camera slowly pans to behind Rent-A-Hero ominously, taking a close-up only to find something completely within the water inside that tube...an EXACT dead on CLONE of RENT-A-HERO!! Everything is down to a T, from the body build to the soft blue eyes except for TWO major differences...the dark jet black hair and a tiny tattoo branded upon the right shoulder with '089' upon it. Our hero is swept up in a cocktail of anger and shock, noticing each tube houses a clone of our hero, each with it's own number tattooed upon it and floating steadily in the water..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'HOW SICK IS THIS?! WHO WOULD DARE DO SUCH A THING?! WHO WOULD DARE CLONE ME?!' ..::/::..Before Clarise can even fathom trying to answer Rent-A-Hero, who has his hands pressed against the tube, still staring at the clone of within....the dark voice from before can be heard from the upper platform the two just came from, speaking ominously as he answers our hero's question..::/::.. Evil Voice:'It's not evil to want to create something Rent-A-Hero...my little failure.' ..::/::..Clarise and our hero's eyes shoot up to the top platform, a set of lights suddenly flickering on as an elderly man in a lab coat stands on the edge of the railing. His eyes look filled with a deominc evil, staring down at our hero as he stares back with thoughts of revenge overtaking him. This man begins to calmly walk along the rail, still looking downward as he runs his fingers through his sheeny grey hair, speaking aloud as both Clarise and Rent-A-Hero keep their eyes locked upon him..::/::..Evil Man:'Yes...the little failure who went on to actually prove my experiment a success...proving he could have DRIVE, GOAL, PURPOSE....it's so good to have you back with us Rent-A-Hero. Ahhh yes, Clarise....you seem to have found my little 'after hours' project...you will die after this you realize that?'Clarise:'I don't care about all that, just tell me what the he** is going on here!' Rent-A-Hero:'We want answers....now.' Evil Man:'Hehe...answers...the same thing I wanted when my son died. If you don't know, I am Dr.Harkins, genetics specialist. I used to work for the government and I was a key mind during the cloning experiments...but after my son Carl died, I had no lust for my work, especially after not getting help from the government in terms of his medical bills...so I set out for revenge. 22 long years ago...I created YOU Rent-A-Hero, in the PERFECT image of my son...you were meant to be a killing machine, a thing of destruction....a harbinger of death...but something was wrong with your genetic encoding and you came out...I don't know...'caring'. I disposed of you, but I had no expectations that you'd falsify memories for yourself...find love...even learn to care about the feelings of others. I was simply amazed by your progress...that's why this C.L.I.T. ploy was created...to pin-point your girlfriend to the point where you'd find us...then we'd have you to study. My God Rent-A-Hero...you truly are unique and if I could take that drive you use for good, and implant that in those dozens of clones I have behind you...except for hatred...there will be no stopping the destruction and chaos I could bring on the Earth.' ..::/::..A tear begins to trickle down the eye of Rent-A-Hero as he stares a whole through the elderly man, his fists clenching as Clarise nears our hero for protection, the man beginning to take slight steps down to their level as our hero speak with a deep flurry of emotion in his voice..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'So that's it...I'm a puppet of your dead son...a son who would look down on you and cy for the actions you want to bring upon the earth. DO YOU FEEL BIG DR.HARKINS?!!?! DO YOU FEEL LIKE A SUPER-SAIEN...does it make you feel GREAT that I'm such a SUCCESS after all?! I AM NOT SOME PLAY TOY FOR YOUR AMUSEMENT....I AM NOT A MODEL ON AN ASSEMBLY LINE....YOU ARE NOT GOD!! I AM A FRIGGING PERSON YOU ANIMAL!!! I HAVE DREAMS, THOUGHTS, FEELINGS, HOPES, AND DESIRES AND THE FACT THAT YOU'D PLAY OFF OF THAT SICKENS ME!! I feel cheap...like this is a life I'm not entitled to! I swear...I will make you PAY for putting me through this pain I'm forced to deal with now! I will decimate you....' Dr.Harkins:'Haha...surely you jest Rent-A-Hero...you don't have what it takes. No matter, you can whine and cry all you'd like but no one shall hear your pleas because you shall soon die Rent-A-Hero...the failure wll finally be incinerated like he should have been long ago! I have waht I want from you Rent-A-Hero...those long hours you spent in that tube were to find what it took to give 'desire' to my weapons and now I have it....enjoy these last few moments of your worthless life for they all come to an end...now.' Rent-A-Hero:'WHAT IN BLUE SMURFBERRY BLAZES ARE YOU TALKING ABOUT?!' ..::/::..Dr.Harkins laughs hysterically as the sound of a door opening can be heard from upstairs, all three looking up, all shocked except for Dr.Harkins as standing over them is a man in a full black body suit, cracking his knuckles and stretching his arms as he stares directly towards Rent-A-Hero, our hero looking back at this man who has the SAME mirror image body and face as him...yet with eyes of slight red and his hair dark black, as if our hero was looking at the exact opposite of all he stands for. The clone laughs along with Dr.Harkins, leaning over the rail and after catching his breath, he stares longingly into the eyes of our hero and says..::/::..RAH Clone:'Hahaha....hello 'brother'. As much as I'd love to torture you and rip every genetic imperfection from your body...the good doctor has ordered me to make this quick. So my brother, I will make your death quick and tasteful...the girl too. But before I kill you, you should be entitled to my name. I figure since you have dubbed yourself 'Rent-A-Hero' it's only fair that since I hate and dispise all you satnd for...I shall now dub myself...RENT-A-VILLAIN...enough of the introductions brother...IT'S TIME TO PAINT THE WALLS WITH YOUR BLOOD!!'..::/::..With that said, the newly dubbed clone 'Rent-A-Villain', leaps from the rail, jetting down towards Rent-A-Hero as he pulls Clarise behind him, standing tall as a battle of pure good versus henious evil shall take place. WILL RENT-A-HERO GAIN REVENGE UPON HIS CREATOR?! WILL HE SURVIVE THIS BATTLE WITH HIS 'BROTHER'?! WHAT WILL BECOME OF THE OTHER CLONES?! FIND OUT SAME RENT-A-TIME...SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!!! ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
