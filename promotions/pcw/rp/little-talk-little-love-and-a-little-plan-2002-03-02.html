Little talk, little love, and a little plan

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-2-02 {The scene opens up with Kevin, Juan, and Mandy at the hotel awaiting a phone call from his Imperial friend from China. Mandy slowly massaging Kevin’s shoulder’s in her baggy white windbreakers and tight white shirt that exposes her stomach and neon green belly button ring. As she continues to rub, Kevin and Mandy try to comfort Juan as he begins pacing around the room,} Juan: C’mon ring, ring, RING! {Juan keeps looking at the phone as nothing is happening.} Kevin: Yo dude calm down a little ya know. Trust me I have everything under control. Mandy: Yeah Kevin has everything taken care of. Juan: But I’m worried about going back to jail. It took every ounce of planning and heart to escape from that hellhole and now I have to go back. I’M NOT GONNA HAVE IT! {Juan getting scared paces around even more. Walking a hole through the floor, Kevin gets up to let Juan in on a little secret.} Kevin: Juan snap outa it! {Kevin shakes Juan a little hoping it’ll clear his mind.} Kevin: Look I have something hooked up where the worst you’ll get is probation. {Juan looks a bit confused and answers.} Juan: What do ya mean Kev? And continue I wanna hear what you got planned. {Kevin motions Juan to sit on the bed. As Juan does Mandy begins to massage Juan’s shoulders hoping he’d loosen up.} Mandy: Juan you are so tense just relax a little. Juan: I am; now Kev please continue. {Juan looks on giving Kevin his full attention, and seeming quite interested in what his friend is doing for him.} Kevin: Alright remember when I said I lived in Japan for a while? Juan: Yeah but it’s the CHINESE that’s after me not the JAPANESE. {Kevin smirks a little and gives Juan the “hold on” motion} Kevin: Well I was at an Imperial Guard Training Facility and I met this one dude. Juan: Go on… {Kevin takes a drink of water before he continues one.} {Mandy then takes Juan’s shirt off as she continues to massage his back and shoulders.} Kevin: Well he was a member of the Imperial Family and his dad was the Emperor. Juan: Like I said bra. He’s Japanese not Chinese. Kevin: Hey dude I’m not done yet. Juan: Then continue… Kevin: Well at the last day, kinda like our test, I showed him up in front of his family. So being showed up by an American his family kinda disowned him or something. And at the party there was this girl all over. You know me Juan and I don’t like Japanese women. So I told her to talk to PY. Well the story ended up that PY is the Ambassador to China and married that girl. Juan: So what good can he do? {Kevin laughs a little and explains.} Kevin: Well he owes me one. Juan: And what is goin on? Kevin: Well this is hush hush but he says he can pull a few strings and the worst you’ll get is probation. Juan: So you tellin me Kev that you are getting me off the hook? Kevin: It might be just that bud-dy. {Juan begins to straighten his faded goatee as he ponders. Is this possible a good plan by Kevin? Or like many times before will this be an attempt that won’t really work. As Juan runs his hands through his hair as he tries to think there’s a knocking at the door. Mandy gets up to answer the door.} {The sound of her pants rubbing across each other fills the dead silence. As she opens the door a familiar figure walks in. Juan looks on in disbelief as the female waives.} Juan: Juliet it’s you. Wha… what are you doing here? Juliet: I’m here to talk to Mandy, we have some things to discuss. Juan: Ok cool… {Juan keeps on staring in disbelief as Mandy and Juliet grabs Kevin’s keys and leave. Juan can not believe his eyes. His love, Juliet, is back in a different kind of way.} {Or is she? What exactly are those things Mandy and Juliet needed to talk about? And will Kevin’s plan hold water?} {The scene picks up at Juan rubbing the back of his neck and then running both hands through his hair as he tries to think. The scene quickly pans in on Juan and fades to a close. In the words of that one Star Wars movie. Juan “may the force be with you!”} {Scene goes to black.} {END TRANSMISSION}
