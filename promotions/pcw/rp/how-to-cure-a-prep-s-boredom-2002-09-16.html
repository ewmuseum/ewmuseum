How To Cure A Prep's Boredom

16 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/09/02 “Urgh... I’m, like, SO bored....” Our shot opens up on PCW’s preppy little Commissioner, Eleanor Hall, as she stands quite lazily, her body language giving away her aforementioned boredom. Eleanor : “Owning everything you want SO totally sucks...” In each of her petite hands are 3 or 4 shopping bags, the contents probably costing well into thousands of dollars, not that Ellie~! seems to be caring that much right now. Eleanor : “And then the stuff I, like, DO want doesn’t even exist... Realty is, like, WAY overrated and stuff!” Standing beside her, his hands carrying even more bags than his wife... All being hers... Stands the PCW CEO and Champion Xavier Kannon... A slightly concerned look on his face as he listens to Ellie~!’s words. Xavier : “Don’t say that, my little Poon Puppy...” His fingers are White where the heavy bags are cutting off the circulation to the extremities, Ellie~! seemingly haven taken the lightest bags by far. Xavier : “It threatens my sense of wealth... Saying my money is useless is like calling me impotent....” Her pale Blue eyes look at him oddly. Xavier : “And being rich is far more of an aspiration than being... Well, you know...” Obviously she doesn’t as her row furrows and her head tilts. Xavier : “Any peon can have an idiotic child, it takes a certain type to be rich... Luckily I fall into the latter!” Although not quite getting his point the prep acknowledges it... Even if it is by palming off two more bags onto him, the Scientologists starting to strain under their weight. Eleanor : “Ooooooooooookay... Then buy me something I’d want!” The Gold Patron Meritorious looks around him at all the boutiques his wife has already been in and bought from, all the furniture stores she has furnished their numerous residences from.... Xavier : “Hmmmmm.” ... Then he sees something she’s like. Xavier : “Want to see that fat man their crawl on the floor and oink like a pig?” His wife’s pale Blue eyes look across the Mall and upon a rather obese male walking out of a Burger King, her little face looking disgusted by his very existence, even more fun to be gained by Xavi’s proposal. Eleanor : “That would, like, SO RULE!!!” With a glimmer of evil in their eyes, the couple make their way across to intercept the man who notices the two of them out of the corner of his eye. Xavier : “You, peon...” The fat man has beads of sweat forming on his forehead from hauling his gargantuan frame about the Mall. His squinty eyes look at Kannon, puzzled. Xavier : “Sell your meal to me for $20!” Looking from his Brown paper Burger King bag to the Scientologist’s face he looks a little confused before shrugging and nervously handing the meal to the Scientologist who places a $20 note into the man’s clammy palm, gesturing for him to stay put as the Scientologist looks through the contents. Xavier : “No need for these...” As Ellie~! peers at the food in disgust, Kannon pulls out a greasy bag of fries and drops them on the floor before lifting out a drink. He looks to Ellie~!, offering the drink while questioning the obese man. Xavier : “This diet?” The man shakes his head and Ellie~! holds her palm up to the beverage, rejecting it’s sugary contents. Xavier : “Didn’t think so.” Ellie~! runs her vacuous Blue eyes over the large man... His stomach falling over the top of his tight jeans and stretching his shirt.. The buttons looking ready to pop at any minute and beads of sweat make their way down his Red face. Eleanor : “Didn’t..... Think.... So!” Finally, before dropping the bag right next to a “DO NOT LITTER” sign, Kannon pulls out a burger carton and opens it to reveal the squashed, anaemic and tasteless fayre that usually passes for a fast food burger. A cringe forms across his face as with a fingernail he flicks the top off the burger and places several large denomination notes inside, before carefully flicking the top back on... In one second increasing the value of the burger by about $10,000. Xavier : “Want it back?” Ellie~! twigs what her husband is doing and starts to look enthused. Eleanor : “Want.... It.... Back!?” His deep set eyes widen as he looks at the corners of the notes emerging from the burger.. Salivating over their the greasy food item or it’s contents. Xavier : “Want to eat this expensive Pig Swill.... Oink like one!” As the man contemplates it, Ellie~! bounces on the spot, excited. Eleanor : “Yeah! Oink... Like... One!” After a few seconds... The moist obese man lets out a quiet oink, looking embarrassed and passers by watch him with peculiar looks.. The 1st Couple of PCW relishing the moment. Eleanor : “He’s, like, SO sounding like a Pig.. That totally rules!” The uncaring prep points and laughs at the man as he continues to degrade himself for Kannon’s money, a smug grin of power showing on the Scientologist’s face as the oinking and snorting continues. Xavier : “Let’s say we up the stakes....” His wife is now fully into it, loving every second f the sweaty obese man humiliating himself for their money and pleasure. Eleanor : “Up... The.... Stakes!” With another stack of notes, Kannon tips the lid off the rapidly cooling burger, it’s grease starting to solidify. At least another $5,000 is added to the bread, meat, cash and ketchup as the CEO smirks to the curious fat man. Xavier : “Now.... Crawl on the floor like the Pig you are!” Ellie~!’s eyes light up and she bounces on the spot again, Kannon calmly enjoying the fun while keeping a cautious eye on the shopping bags at his feet. Eleanor : “Yeah, like.. On... The ... Floor!” The obese, sticky man has no shame and slowly lowers his large frame to the Mall’s dirty floor, his ass nearly busting out of his pants as he snorts and crawls around.. Ellie~! doubling over in laughter. Eleanor : “I, like, SO take it back... Money is, like the WAY greatest thing ever!” The giggly prep continues to crack up as Kannon watches the almost mesmerising site of the large man crawling around on the grubby slabs, seemingly pretending to sniff for truffles now in his pursuit of money. Xavier : “It takes a very strange person to enjoy this...” Kannon drops the burger down onto the floor where the man drags his bulk over to pull it apart.. His large, ketchup and grease covered fingers clawing at the money. Xavier : “... But it takes a rich person to make it happen.” The CEO lets out a little chuckle as he picks up his wife’s bags again, and with Ellie~! by his side and craning her neck to see the obese man try and pull his lardy body off the floor, the couple make their happy way off as our shot fades out. “Make... It... Happen!”
