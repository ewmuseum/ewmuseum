Facing one's Fears..

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Facing one's Fears.. Featuring: Politically Incorrect Fear. Fear may just be the emotion that is inspired by the widest variety of things. People can be afraid of anything, be it little tiny mice or big huge alligators, a real threat, or a movie monster. Why is fear so widespread? The answer is simple. The media. The media glorifies violence. It glorifies the things that people fear most. And, in many ways, on Friday, Politically Incorrect comes face to face with two stereotypical objects of fear. I'm sure our politicians are up to the task though.. ::A movie theater, no different than hundreds of other megaplexes throughout the country. Not the place where one usually finds politicians, but then again, Politically Incorrect are not your normal politicians. Both men stand at the ticket booth, trying to decide what fine film to see.:: William S. Powell: Man, it's been eight years since I've been to a movie theater. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Same here. What shall we see? William S. Powell: Well, E.T.'s back out.....but forget that. I've seen that damn movie eighty times in my life...damn children's movie day at the Des Moines Public Library! Damnit all to hell!! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Why do you keep funding it and attending then? William S. Powell: Because, the head librarian that asks me for funding is young, cute, and she gives excellent fellatio. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Ah, that's always an excellent reason. William S. Powell: Indeed. So, what shall we see? Reginald V. Lovecraft: My vote for the picture we should be seeing on this fine day is Resident Evil. William S. Powell: Why the hell would you want to see Resident Evil? I hate those damned games. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Because, my friend, from reading reviews of the picture, it seems to be a rather scary movie. William S. Powell: Scary? Pah! You want scary? Go watch a movie about Greenpeace executives. Now THAT'S scary! Those damn environmentalists executives never give you a moment's peace! Always screaming about saving the whales or some nonsense... Reginald V. Lovecraft: Um.....William? William S. Powell: Yes Reginald? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Why would Des Moines environmental executives want you to save the whales? There's no oceans within 1000 miles of Des Moines! William S. Powell: Well.....THAT'S WHY I HATE EVIRONMENT LOBBYISTS!! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Can we get back to me explaining why we should be seeing Resident Evil today? William S. Powell: FINE! HAVE IT YOUR WAY! I WAS DAMN HAPPY BITCHING ABOUT ENVIRONMENTALISTS! GO AHEAD AND TAKE AWAY ALL OF MY FUN! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Thank you kindly. Now, anyway, Resident Evil is scary. And what do scary things do? William S. Powell: Make you pee your pants? ::Lovecraft looks extremely exasperated with his young colleague.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: No! Scary things make you afraid. Scary things inspire FEAR! William S. Powell: Oh! I knew that! But why are we talking about fear? Reginald V. Lovecraft: How did you become a politician anyway? William S. Powell: Local Republican party nominated me. Let's just say some....err....unusual tactics were employed. Reginald V. Lovecraft: As I thought. Anyway, one of the teams we face in the PCW Tag Team Title Gauntlet match on Friday is a team known as Fear Factory. And, Fear Factory have to be the most stereotypical representation of fear I have ever seen. William S. Powell: Why do you say that? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Well....we have Rudolph Von Hackermann. With a name like that, he could ONLY be an evil psychotic mad scientist plotting to take over the world. Geez, you'd think that if he had any real evil scary scientific powers, he'd be running a small country right now instead of trying to get a wrestling federation's tag team titles. William S. Powell: I really hate mad scientists. I actually hate science in general..because science is what those GODDAMN ENVIRONMENTALISTS STUDY!! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Powell, I seriously doubt that Rudolph is an evironmentalist.. William S. Powell: AH-HA! HE HAS YOU FOOLED TOO! Listen Reginald, I know my environmentalists! RVH is INDEED one! Look at the shrewd voice...the shifty eyes.....THE ANALLY-RETENTIVE WHITE LABORATORY COAT! HE IS AN EVIRONMENTALIST! HE IS ONE I TELL YOU!! ::Reginald falls silent, undoubtedly speechless at how much of a true and utter idiot his partner is.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Fine then, he is an environmentalist.....whatever you say....but, like all mad scientists, he has his tag partner.....his Frankenstein's monster if you will, in Mason Creeper. William S. Powell: What is a Mason Creeper? Reginald V. Lovecraft: It's Rudolph's tag team partner/Evil Creation. William S. Powell: Oh, why are we seeing Resident Evil again? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Shut up. We're going to go stare fear in the eye now. William S. Powell: Will Sunday be Election Day? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Of course, William. William S. Powell: Yay! That means we will stand triumphant. ::Reginald shakes his head, as we fade to the usual.:: Paid for by the Friends of Politically Incorrect!Your NEXT PCW Tag Team Champions!
