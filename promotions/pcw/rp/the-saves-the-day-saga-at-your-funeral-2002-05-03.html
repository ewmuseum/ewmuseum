The Saves the Day Saga: At Your Funeral...

3 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-3-02 During the time DarkStar was remembering what happened before his little run with the Gods. He realized that things werent getting better. You see, Death controlled one of the most important functions on earth. Death came to you in whatever form you wanted to see. But at heart, Death was a beautiful girl, who doesnt look a day over 25, Death controlled well.... death. Without death, there is no lifeforce to cycle through the world, theres no thinning of the population. Now that death has dissapeared. Things are different. All because she seemed to think that winning at chess was more important to the universe. ----------------------------- Darkness. Nothing but. Neverending light killer. But yet... Was this how it was supposed to be. It was assumed that he was quite dead. I mean, no vital signs, no breathing, no heart beat, no blood, embalmed, dead. Right? Erik reached over and pinched himself. He could feel it, his skin felt cold, but he could feel it. Hmm. Thats... odd.... You see. Erik was terminally ill. He died, a few days ago, and sat in a freezer for a while. And now, his funeral. He thinks he slept through the viewing. Hmm. Shouldn't there be deadness? I can still move and stuff. Weird. Wow... is that my mom giving the eulogy. She's doing a really shitty job. What the hell? I never played Little League... Dude! I was not a nice little kid! Do you remember Suzie Watson! I fingered that whore in your car! God. What the hell is she talking about... Thats it, Im standing up, they will be happy to see me... --------------------------- DarkStar turned on the TV, the news flickered on. War. Bombing. Suicide.... News Caster: In breaking news, apparently the Dead have Risen in a local funeral home, more info at 11. '....' 'Shit.' He stood, turned his TV off, and grabbed his coat. This was gonna be a long night.
