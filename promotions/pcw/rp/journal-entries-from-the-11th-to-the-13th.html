Journal entries from the 11th to the 13th…

Date: Just now... From the journal of Titu Xansiv: entry dated Tuesday the 11th of February 2003: Clyde is already halfway there now. The plagues are imminently foreseen upon the horizon and I can do little to stop them. Why is it that this federation allows such disgraceful acts to carry on without any assistance from personnel whatsoever? It discourages me when I look about the environment I’m expected to call a second home and see shying faces unwilling to help me out when I‘m in dire need of it! I feel now that I can’t really do anything to stop what’s happening, other than perhaps battle it out with the demon until he decides he’s had enough. But then again, this is Clyde we’re talking about, not some lowly idiot with nothing better to do with his time than bother me. Then again, that really does sound a lot like Clyde. He’s a demon who has been living for centuries with nothing to do and now he’s growing bored, so he’s decided to bring about the apocalypse for a laugh…or something along those lines, anyway.I can’t allow him to bring about all the plagues, I truly can’t! It’s up to me to stop him, and me only. The tenth plague portrays a terrifying fate where the first born of each Egyptian family dies…I can’t allow that to take place…not in my lifetime. If I were to fail in my task and the tenth plague did indeed take place then it would probably turn me mad! I’d feel wholly responsible, and frankly I would be. If it weren’t for my big mouth, Clyde would quite possibly have never thought to himself, ‘Hmm…what’s a good way to make an Egyptian suffer? Ah! I’ll bring about the ten plagues of Moses and let him watch as his following of loyal fans deteriorates gradually, just like his mind!’ I attempted to speak with Ra himself about the whole thing, but he left The Catacombs at The Palace before I had the chance to talk to him. Ha! And they say the Gods are ever-listening. It matters not now anyway, all that really matters at the moment is that I defeat Bob Vurdo later tonight and confront the demon in some way. Whatever happens though, I do indeed need to have a few words with him…but what if he finds me first? Perhaps he has the same agenda with me as I do with him…maybe he’ll be on the lookout for a passing Egyptian? Whatever tonight brings, I can only pray the Gods are with me, watching over my personage with stern and careful eyes… From the journal of Titu Xansiv: entry dated Wednesday the 12th of February 2003: It all happened too fast, damn it! It all happen too damned fast! I didn’t even know how to react to it. My body just charged up and took over. When he told me to attack him I did, blindly. …How foolish I was. Now he has the advantage. He knows too well that he’s annoying me, aggravating me, driving me mad with anger and frustration! I could scream blue murder given the chance! He has greatly besmirched me tonight, and I’ll be damned if I’m going to let him away with it! On Friday Snow and I will take those tag team titles from P.W.A and destroy Clyde and DarkStar in the process! I don’t care whether Duke Williams is the referee in the match or not, I’m out for revenge and if the ‘Malice Man’ even dares to touch me I’ll make sure he pays come next Tuesday! Jennings didn’t make matters any better, either. The silly woman downed my hopes and aspirations for the night in a few fell swoops! Damn her to Duat along with Clyde, DarkStar and even Violence Jack! That idiotic false idol-worshipping schizophrenic is really beginning to churn my stomach! His strange Gods, The Great Old Ones, are shrouding my beliefs in PCW as the new religion of the federation. Though he acts the typically evil heel in the company, he is beginning to build quite the fan base. And it’s all because of that damned octopus deity…Ktulu, or something. What am I saying?! I’m not even involved with the man, it’s just Clyde: he’s annoyed me so much today it’s quite unbelievable! At least I beat Vurdo I suppose. I’m in the March Madness match now, a bout I wasn’t involved in because of a niggling injury last year.With the great Goddess of war Sekh-met looking over me I shall make a huge impact on that battle for the title and hopefully win it… From the journal of Titu Xansiv: entry dated Thursday the 13th of February 2003: Month of Malice? Pfft… what is this ignoramus thinking? The idiot really believes the fans care? Is that it?Ha! What a joke… The fans couldn’t give a damn about your little campaign Duke and let it be known that the Gods of Egypt don’t either! So, Duke Williams, the president of Primetime Championship Wrestling wants to chalk up another W on his tally just because it’s his last month here in Primetime, eh? Well not this time, you ignorant fool.You see, the Gods are with me and through them I cannot lose!Not unless an illegal object gets involved.But let’s face it Duke, you’re the good guy, the nice guy, the fan favourite just like me and you wouldn’t dare hit me over the head with a chair to pick up a cheap win, right?Duke really thinks he’s great, doesn’t he? What with his little sit-down promos and his inferior ‘trash talk’, why his in-ring abilities are second to none, right? How much further from the truth could you possibly be, my friends? I can see their faces now as Duke, proud and bold, strides into the ring from his commentary chair at ringside, a glint of hope in his eyes only to have his smile wiped square off his face as a sharp superkick is delivered first class into his jaw line. What will they do? They have a choice, you see. My dear fans can either cheer a man who has put his life on the line for years; a man who many consider a future legend in this sport; a man who has held titles in PCW and devoted his countless defences to them, the fans……or of course they can support Duke Williams, the has-been. You see, over the past few months, Duke hasn’t truthfully praised me. Sure, he might say things on air about how much of a decent fellow I am, and how great an athlete I am, but when he runs his mouth, he can’t help but spew filth all over my name.Well it stops on Tuesday, Duke. You’ve got a few wins recorded over me Malice Man but this Turmoil things change. DarkStar might be in the match too but I’ve already beaten him, haven’t I? Ra himself showed me through that bout, and now Ra shall show me through our match, and it’ll be I who stands victorious above your broken body. Let it be known now that not all faces get along, some hate each other as much as the next person, and between me and these pages of papyrus I can’t stand Duke. So that’s why I’ll take extra pleasure in crushing his frame and pinning him for the three count. Of course first I have to concentrate on winning the tag team titles with Jason.Over the past couple of week he and I have become quite good friends and it’s going to be hard trying to decide whether I’d rather have the title he processes around my waist or a friend in him when we meet at March Madness. Much must be contemplated, including that sickening Clyde and how I can possibly stop him from bringing about more of the plagues. So for now I’m calling it a day.
