Conflict

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-22 Inside the large, cluttered office Brandi moves around quickly, not bothering to sort through the papers thrown across her desk as she drops them into a cardboard box and scoots it into a small closet. Just as the closet door is closed, loud grunts and groans come from the adjacent room. Brandi walks over to the wall and places her head against it. She listens carefully and giggles quietly. Then Brandi grabs a hold of the double doors separating the two offices, and yanks them open quickly. Brandi does some sort of leap into the room and looks around with a grin. Brandi- I thought you said that woman from the escort service left? Tim Walden emerges from the hallway, struggling with a large, apparently heavy box. He grunts as he pulls it across the floor towards Brandi and leaves it next to an empty desk before standing up and wiping his forehead. Walden- Did you say something? Brandi looks around and shakes her head. Brandi- Nevermind. The click of a door shutting is heard from the other room and both Brandi and Tim turn around. Nathan walks towards them and stops at the double doors. He leans one arm up against the doorframe and cocks an eyebrow, flashing his best smile. He nods in Tim’s direction and winks at Brandi. Nathan- You ready, babe? Brandi turns to Tim and rolls her eyes. Then she walks back into her office, smacking Nathan on the stomach lightly as she passes. Brandi- I’m not your babe, and I’m not ready. Nathan quickly closes the door behind Brandi and the smile fades away from his face. He jumps into the leather chair behind Brandi’s desk and spins around quickly. Brandi- Uh, would you mind not acting so childish? Nathan stops spinning and looks at Brandi angrily. Nathan- What’s your problem? Brandi- Excuse me? I’m the one that should be asking you what your problem is. Nathan leans back in the chair, putting his feet on the desk and his hands behind his head. Nathan- What my problem is? Brandi- Yeah, that’s what I said. You just walk right in here like you own the damn place. Nathan- Oh, you get mad at me, but not that little prick Walden? Brandi- That’d be Mr. Walden to you, and he’s not only a partner of mine, but a damn good friend as well. Since when did you start having a problem with him, anyways? Nathan- Ever since he’s been trying to move in on you! Brandi- Move in on me? I’m letting him have an office nearby and suddenly he’s moving in on me? I don’t know how you figure that, but you sure as hell need to rethink what you just said. And keep your feet off my desk. I just cleaned it. Nathan moves his feet off the desk slowly and stands up, staring at Brandi. He makes his way towards her, never taking his eyes off of hers. Nathan- You think that just because you’re the personnel director that I’m gonna have to jump as soon as you bark a command? You really think that I’m like all those pussies that don’t speak their mind because they’re afraid of what you might do? Brandi- Damn straight. You’re in my office, and you’ll do as I tell you to do or you can get your ass out and start begging someone else for a job. Nathan turns his back towards Brandi and breathes in and out slowly. He looks at the ground and shakes his head back and forth a few times before turning back around. Nathan- Look, let’s just forget about it, alright? Brandi- Are you done insulting good friends of mine? Nathan- Sure. We’ll go out tonight and then get ready for our trip to Alliances. Brandi- Our trip? You’re going now? Nathan- Of course. Brandi- I don’t think so. I have two plane tickets. One is for me, the other is for Tim. That means that there’s none left for you. Nathan looks offended and he casts a glare at Walden through the wall. Nathan- Why does he get to go and I don’t? Brandi- Hmmm….let me think about that one for a minute. Could it be because we both are wrestling at Alliances? Or possibly that we have a tag team match that night as well?! No, that must not be it… Nathan- God damnit! I want to go with you. Brandi- So to prove how much you want to go with me, you’ve resorted to throwing temper tantrums like a child. What’s next? Are you going to fall to the floor, kicking and screaming? Nathan- No. I just want to be there when ya walk away victorious, that’s all. Brandi- Well, I’ll order it on Pay Per View for you and you can watch it then. Now, are we still going to try and catch a movie tonight, or are we going to stand here and argue over Alliances? Nathan- Nah, I don’t wanna argue anymore. Brandi- Alright. I’m going to go take a shower real quick and get ready, then we can go. Why don’t you go help Tim move in? I think he could use some help. Nathan nods and Brandi goes off into a small room, shutting the door behind her. Nathan pauses for a few moments, waiting until he hears the sound of running water drift from underneath the door. Then he opens the door to Tim’s office very quietly and looks around. Walden has his back to Nathan and is attempting to hook up his phone. A sadistic grin stretches across Nathan’s face and he creeps into the room. Nathan balls his hands into fists, before shaking his head and looking around the room. Boxes still scatter the room, but Walden’s desk seems to have been set up already. A stapler rests in one corner and a large marble paperweight in the other. There are actually a few forms resting on the desk, with a cup of pens next to it. Nathan’s eyes scan the desk before coming to a rest on the paperweight. Nathan- Asshole!! Nathan charges towards the desk and grabs the paperweight. As soon as Walden turns around, he’s met by the marble hitting him just above his right eye. He crumples to the floor and Nathan drops the paperweight. He leans against the wall for leverage, and kicks at Tim. Walden manages to crawl away and uses the desk to pull him up. Just as Nathan runs towards him, Tim stretches his leg out and catches Nathan a bit below the belt. Nathan doubles up and Tim puts his hand to his head, feeling the blood as it trickles down. Nathan struggles to his feet and gets down in a football stance. He runs at Tim, full speed and connects, sending him hurtling into the far wall. Tim’s head bounces off of the plaster and he slumps down to the floor, unmoving. Nathan stands up and stares down at him. The sadistic grin returns to his face. He quickly hurries back into Brandi’s room and makes sure that the door to Walden’s office is closed. Brandi- Did you guys get everything unpacked? Nathan- Yeah, we sure did. Everything’s unloaded and Tim just left for the night. Brandi- Damn. I had something I needed to talk to him about. Guess it’ll just have to wait. Are you ready to go? Nathan- Always. Nathan wraps his arm around Brandi’s shoulder and guides her into the hallway. As they pass by the door to Tim Walden’s office, he can’t seem to take his eyes off of it and watches it until the two round a corner.
