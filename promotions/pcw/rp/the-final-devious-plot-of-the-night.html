The Final Devious Plot Of The Night

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.24.02 (#9 PPV) “Dawg…these plans ain’t workin’.” The voice of Ray Ray, the new manager of Rush Mason, comes across in the blackness of the screen as the promo begins. There is another sigh in the background before we hear another voice reply. “We try to find evil, but we ain’t really found nothin’ but trouble.” We can now see the extent of the scene although there is not much of an extent to see. Rush and his plump manager are walking outside in a large complex of what seems to be a rented storage space. Rush: Trouble?! We didn’t even cause a miniscule menace! We’ve committed less evil than a teenager who eggs houses! Just shrugging, as is usual for him, Ray Ray continues to walk, making sure to keep a few feet between him and Rush. Upsetting his employer in his current foul mood mightn’t be the best plan of action and Ray Ray realizes that. Rush: But, of course, me being the genius that I am, I’ve got a few back-up plans. Stopping on a dime, so to speak, Ray Ray calls out to Rush, thinking of some of the last ideas that were had by the group, Rush in particular. The manager’s voice wavers as he asks Rush the intent of these back-up plans. Ray Ray: We ain’t like blowin’ nothin’ up, right? Rush turns around and looks back at the 300 plus pound man, then just shakes his head, more at Ray Ray in general than to actually give a negative answer to the question. Ray Ray, thinking this a rather good answer, considering what ideas Rush has been known to come up, and follows behind as they continue to walk. Rush: Speaking of which, why don’t you want to do anything illegal? You certainly can’t be afraid of jail, because everyone is exactly like you except with a felony on their record and a few less brain cells. As he continues to walk, Ray Ray replies in a loud tone, announcing his reply to the whole world and more. Ray Ray: Dawg, I’ma be a mayor some day. This brings Rush to a halt, and he turns, looking back at his manager, and then just begins to walk again, shaking his head and chuckling to himself as Ray Ray continues to follow. Rush: Okay, it should be around here some where…Pulling a key out of his pocket, Rush looks around at the large storage doors, trying to find the one matching his key. He walks over to one side and at the second door he inspects, finds the one that he’s looking for. Rush: Give me a hand here. If I get a hernia, I’ll be exponentially less formidable to all do-gooders who oppose me. Before he helps out Rush, the fleshy manager continues as to what “do-gooders” would want to oppose the Voodoo Villain. Ray Ray: You mean, like, Xavier Kannon and Akiyama? Rush rolls his eyes, and then looks up at Ray Ray, who shrugs. He replies in an annoyed tone, still looking for some help lifting the large storage door. Rush: No, they’re not do-gooders, they’re just pricks. Let’s just say I’ve become somewhat of a loner due to certain circumstances. However, Ray Ray still doesn’t immediately spring to Rush’s side to help, as he seemingly wants to know more about Mason’s “loner” qualities and the reasons for such an outlook. Ray Ray: What circumstances? Oh, you mean like Black Attack? This quickly makes Rush turn his head, looking back at his manager once again, and now replying angrily, trying to avoid the topic of the eloquent militant black stable that graced PCW. Rush: Maybe. Now, will you just help me with this damn door? Finally satisfied by the answers, Ray Ray walks over and lifts the huge door from the ground up. Inside, there seem to be a dozen or so very large plastic containers, looking like huge tupper ware. Rush: And here, is the birthplace is the most devious plan to ever hit PCW and not involve Judas Steele! BWAHAHAHA! Flashing a smile first, Ray Ray then turns, making the sort of face you would if you find out that your employer might be partly psychotic. He then looks at the huge containers, wondering exactly what makes them so evil. Ray Ray: Rush, yo. Whassup with those boxes? They got smallpox in ‘em or something? Rush looks shocked at such a suggestion, and then opens one of them, revealing what look like small shiny legumes, from a distance, and Rush grins widely as he spies them inside the containers. Rush: There we have it, Randolph. This surprises Ray Ray because 1) Rush has never called him Randolph, his birth name, before and 2) Beans don’t seem that evil to Ray Ray, although he’s not a typical genius. Ray Ray: Rush, them’s beans. Before his manager can continue any further, Rush cuts in, taking the lids off several of the large containers, and looking at them as if they contained solid gold. Rush finally turns to Ray Ray, answering in a very strange way. Rush: These….are no ordinary beans. These are the most repulsive tasting jelly beans known to man. They shall help me win Alliances! Ray Ray pretends like that makes sense at all, nodding. Then he continues to ask Rush exactly how these beans are supposed to accomplish the tall task of winning Alliances. Ray Ray: How? The question interrupts Rush’s basking amongst his beautiful new plan and he turns to Ray Ray, sighing as he walks over and puts an arm on Ray Ray’s shoulder. Rush: Randolph, we shall distribute the foul-tasting jelly beans into the food of all of PCW. Afterwards, they will have the taste of Pubic Hair or Rotting Corpse stuck in their mouth all night as they vomit away! Wincing at the thought of such flavors, Ray Ray finally partly understands the plan a little more, but asks one last question. Ray Ray: Uhh…Rush, Alliances is in five and a half hours. How we gonna get the beans in the food? Suddenly realizing a glitch in his plan, Rush scrunches his face up, looking back at Ray Ray. He looks down at the beans, and then up at Ray Ray and replies strangely. Rush: Meh, I dunno. You’re the muscle, I’m the brains. As Ray Ray turns, he winces and the scene begins to fade to black…
