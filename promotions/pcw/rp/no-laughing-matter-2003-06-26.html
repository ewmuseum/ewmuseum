No Laughing Matter

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 26, 2003 It has been some time since the Order of Society and Logic has broadcasted from a location other than Turmoil or Fusion. SOCIETY has been busy indeed, capturing the PCW Cruiserweight Title, well, in a manner of speaking anyway. But, really, isn't that what the Order of Society and Logic does best? They spin doctor things to suit their own sense of reality. They have been fined twice by PCW, for taking out two officials. Their actions are controversial at best, evil at worst. But you've got to give it to them: they've been successful.And they are being rewarded for their success. A shot at the PCW World Tag Team Championships lies in the balance, as Krikorian and SOCIETY prepare to take on Entertainment Extreme. And for SOCIETY, this is no laughing matter. 'SOCIETY, you have a chance to become a double champion! Very few athletes have ever had the chance to accomplish this!' Chang smiles, his tongue firmly in cheek with the statement. He knows as well as anyone that SOCIETY is not the rightful champion...although, even PCW seems to be coming around in that regard. 'Yes, I know Mr. Chang. And, as important as that match is, I am also looking forward to defending my Cruiserweight Championship on Turmoil against Mr. Jackson...a man who I owe a beating from my earliest days in Primetime Championship Wrestling.' Ah yes. Chang remembered. PCW had finally officially made one of SOCIETY's matches for the PCW Cruiserweight Title. He laughed to himself. Finally, the Order had forced the company to conform. 'You may worry about that match when Krikorian and yourself have tag team championships around your waists!' SOCIETY nodded. As much as he was looking forward to the chance to punish Jackson...more gold would further cement the Order's place in PCW. 'Then I shall. How is Krikorian doing?' 'He has had some..troubles, but he assures me that he shall be ready in time for our bout against Entertainment Extreme.' 'Entertainment Extreme. E2. The former disciples of the Pranks With Attitude, one of this company's finest tandems. Broke from them after becoming PCW World Tag Team Champions.' SOCIETY's words were concise, as if he had read them from a report. Chang knew that he most likely had; one of the Experiment's greatest strengths was that he had a strong head on his shoulders to compliment his massive physique. 'Yes, indeed,' Chang said. 'How do you plan to defeat them?' A small smirk formed itself on SOCIETY's face. 'I foresee no difficulties. Now that Sivasian and I are on the same page once more, a cohesive Order will simply destroy the inferior tandem we face, and raise the tag team titles high in the name of CONFORMITY!' SOCIETY was confident. Perhaps too confident, Michael Chang thought. But, when you were the size of SOCIETY, it was difficult not to be overconfident. 'Then,' Michael said, 'I reccommend we get in touch with our colleague, Mr. Krikorian. That way, we can iron out any discrepancies in gameplan that we may have, so that you two are as cohesively bound as possible.' SOCIETY responded with his usual head nod. Still, from a man his size, the gesture carried considerable weight. 'So, we'll meet him at the usual,' SOCIETY inquired. 'Exactly. Let's go.' The two Order members turned off of the street that they had been walking down, striding off purposely to meet with Krikorian.
