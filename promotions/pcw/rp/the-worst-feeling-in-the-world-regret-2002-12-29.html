The Worst Feeling in the World, Regret.

29 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 29, 2002 Regret It was a word I had grown to relate to far too much lately. First regret about not keeping a close eye on Christa and Alex, and now regret for the decisions I had made in life. Maybe I should have never gotten involved with a man who as a former porn star. Sure I loved him to death, but maybe I just never knew what love is. I never had the chance to learn about love growing up. My parents were far from the loving kind. And my brother Brandon, sure I admired him but he was nothing more than a carbon copy of my parents. I loved Alex and Christa, but who couldn’t love children that looked up to you like you were god? Maybe all along I was just seeking comfort and protection. I wanted to feel safe and loved and I knew I would be just that if I was with Chris Storm, or so I thought. If Chris really loved me back then why would he shun me the way he did. Why would he bring home a woman from his past, and why would he keep records of the past. I thought he loved me, but maybe I wasn’t what he wanted. Or maybe he didn’t want me at all. Perhaps it was my innocence that he wanted to take. Maybe this was all one sick and twisted mind game he was trying to play with me. If he had no problem tossing me through a table in the past, and burying my head in a cake then why should he have a problem with breaking my heart? Regardless of the facts that stood before me though, I refused to believe that Chris would do something so devious. Maybe all Chris needed was space. Maybe that was why he had thrown me out of his room and locked me out of this room. Well if he wanted space that was just what I would give him. I slipped silently into the guest bedroom and turned on the light, waking up the kids. The sun had not risen yet, but I wanted to get out of there as soon as possible even if it had meant waking up the kids from a peaceful slumber. Surely waking them off and lying to them telling them everything was fine was better than putting them through the hell of having to pick side between Chris and I once they woke up in the morning. Pulling Christa into my arms I walked over to Alex’s bed and shook him softly, until his eyes finally opened slightly. “Tessa the suns not even up yet.” He whined, rolling over on his side and pulling his pillow over his head. “Alex we have to go.” I said making sure not to move too much and awaken the sleeping Christa. “Where are we going?” He asked as a yawn followed the question, the pillow still on top of his head, somewhat muffling his voice. “We are going to visits grandma. She wants us there early.” I lied through my teeth. If anything my mother would shoot me for bringing the children over at this early of an hour but I needed time to myself. The lectures were just a cost of keeping the children out of the argument. “Can I stay here?” He said, pulling the covers over the pillow that sat atop his head obviously trying to keep the light from stinging his eyes which were obviously well adjusted to the dark by now after sleeping several hours. “But what about your grandma? I am sure she misses you a ton.” I gave him a guilt trip, hoping that somehow that would convince him to come along with me. Too bad it would be me feeling guilty later on when he found out I only said this to him to get him out of the house and away from Chris. “Liar, you just want to go back home to get away from Chris.” He pouted back, totally sending me into shock. How did he know what was going on? Was he awake last night when all of this occurred? I was screwed now and there was no way I was going to be able to get him out of this bed if he knew once he left it he might never see Chris again unless it was in the ring with me, fighting out our problems. “That’s not…fine! You can stay.” I yelled, giving up. First I seem to be losing the person I care about the most, and now the child I had cared for and bonded with. The world couldn’t get any worse, but if that was the way it had to be then so be it. I stomped out of the room and out of the apartment with Christa in my arms. Walking down the hall towards the elevator I began to wonder if I was doing the right thing. Should I really just walk out and leave like this? What if Chris really did care and this was all just a big misunderstanding? Surely he would be heartbroken if he woke up and I wasn’t there. But how could this be some big misunderstanding? He knew exactly what he was doing when he yelled at me, taking the side of that two sided bitch, Ashea. I couldn’t stay around any longer if it was going to be like that. I couldn’t stay around to see the man I had loved so dearly suddenly stop caring for me, all because of some girl who had to get her attention through spreading her legs. Walking into the elevator I felt the tears sting my eyes. How could this happen? Why did all this shit have to happen to me? It seemed that as of late life was picking on me. First I lose my grandfather to old age, my brother to a crash in the night, then my father nearly dies because of a man named Erik’s jealous rage, then my angels nearly get killed by a mute and his companion, and now I was going to lose the person that meant the most to me just because a girl named Ashea had walked out of a porno back into his life. Why did all of this have to happen to me? Why couldn’t something happen to somebody else for a change? Luck was certainly not on my side. -Ring- Reaching the bottom floor I began to feel all the regrets pile up on top of me as I exited the elevator. It was like I sunk into hell and now there was no escape. I simply continued on my way out of the apartment complex and towards my car. Leaving a trail of footprints engraved in the snow, I loaded Christa into the back of the car, leaving her stretched out and fast asleep, ignorant to the situation that had formed around her. Shutting the back door and walking to the front seat I promised myself two things, the first being that I would keep the kids out of this. The second thing being that I would get Chris back into my arms, no matter what the cost was. I couldn’t let go of the best thing I ever had and I wouldn’t. Ashea would pay. So help me god she would pay dearly.
