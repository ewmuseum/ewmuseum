Wham Bamn Thank You Slam!

16 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Wham Bamn Thank You Slam! Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 16/10/02 “Will you, like, SO GET UP, ALREADY!!!!” With the shrill yelling of PCW Commissioner, Eleanor Kannon-Hall, our shot opens up once more in the hotel bedroom of PCW CEO Xavier Kannon. The Scientologist remains in bed suffering from burn-out while Ellie~! looks aggravated. Eleanor : “Like, COME ON! It’s been aaaaaaaaaaaaaages since Red October, you can’t STILL be burned out?” From under the covers a groan is heard as Kannon rearranges them and pulls a pillow over his head. Xavier : “Sleeppppp....” The little prep stamps her feet and storms off through the door. Eleanor : “Fine, then I’m going to watch TV and order TONNES of Room Service which YOU’LL have to pay for.... HA! Ellie~!, like, SO out smarts you!” As Kannon just groans again, Ellie~! walks over to the Entertainment Centre and looks around. Eleanor : “Hmmmmm....” After a little rummaging, she pulls out a tape marked “OSW Slam!”. Being the naive girl she is, Ellie~! decides it could be fun to watch and after a minute or so of trying to get the tape into the machine, it finally goes in with a whirr and a click before she hunts for the remote control. Eleanor : “Come onnnnnnnnnnnn... Where are you, I wanna see my Slam!“ Her whiny tone is enough to make anyone with ears cringe before she finally finds the remote and skips over to the sofa, leaping up then down across the furniture. Eleanor : “YAY REMOTE!“ Excitedly she presses play and the OSW intro sequence flashes up... And keep playing.... A bit more... And more.... Still playing.... Eleanor : “Wow, they REALLY like their graphics don’t they!” Finally the sequence ends and the show begins, the little Prep’s eyes lock onto the screen, the show reflecting in her wide, vacuous eyes. Eleanor : “Wow, Ellie~! feels tired....” As the show continues, the Commissioner’s eyes blink more to stave off the sleepiness but it fails.... Slowly her volumous eyelashes lead her eyelids together and the prep drifts off.... .... But Slam! still plays and in her preppy slumber, her brain takes in the show’s content. And no, it doesn’t melt and run out of her ears. “Huh? Wha? I, like, SO wasn’t asleep or anything....” Rubbing her eyes, the prep darts out of her sleep and takes a quick look around. Finally her eyes settle back on Slam! and the much hyped Main Event between Howard Porter and Propaganda. Eleanor : “Like, PHEW! I didn’t miss the Main Event!” She excitedly hops her legs onto the sofa and sits cross-legged, hugging a cushion and leaning forward in anticipation of the Main Event. Eleanor : “Like, SO HUH?!?!?!?!” Suddenly the pictures stop and are replaced by static. Eleanor : “Whoever is coming out now has a WAY annoying entrance video...” She remains seated, pulling her hair from her eyes as they continues staring at the flashing Black, Grey and White static. Eleanor : “And it’s TOTALLY too long as well!” In her own little way, Ellie~! just sits there.. Her Bambi-esque pale Blue eyes just remaining fixated on the static that has replaced the Slam! main event. Eleanor : “Maybe one of them, like, has this move where he can release an electromagnetic pulse that knocks out all the electric stuff and his opponents alarm clock so he doesn’t wake up and misses the match so he wins?” Another minute passes, another minute of Ellie~!’s eyes staring at the static. Eleanor : “Maybe not.” Her eyes haven’t blinked since the static kicked in... The constant flashing filtering down her optic nerve and into her brain where the sounds of Slam! seeped to through her ears... The flashing beginning to trigger things off in her preppy brain. You get the story. “My little Poon Puppy, you still awake in there?” We suddenly cut back to Xavier’s bedroom where the CEO is standing in front of the mirror, still looking sedated and worn out. He has pulled on a pair of Black sweat pants and is tying his long mane of Strawberry Blonde hair back, waiting for an answer. Xavier : “Ellie~!?” After splashing a handful of cold water into his face to help wake him up, Kannon makes his way to the living room. “I look at myself in a mirror and it isn’t Ellie~!.... So, um, I must be a Vampire and someone is standing next to me or something...” Xavier’s brow furrows as he hears the strange words spouting from Ellie~!’s mouth when suddenly he sees a flash of Silver out of the corner of his eye. Xavier : “Ya wha?!?!?!?” His preppy little wife jumps on his back with a Garlic Press in her hands, trying very unsuccessfully to crush Xavier’s head in the small kitchen implement. She holds on tight as he tries to gently pull her off. Eleanor : “Like, SO hold still!” Xavier: “WHAT ARE YOU DOING?!?!?!?“ Still the little prep holds on tight, trying in vain to crush her husband’s head. Eleanor : “I couldn’t find one of those Trash Compacting things so I’m going to crush your head in this!” Xavier stops struggling as Ellie~! continues to try and fit the press around his head... In vain, of course. Xavier : “May I ask WHY?!?!?!?!?“ Eleanor : “Eeeeeeeeeeeeeep!” He backs to the sofa and thrusts back, pushing Ellie~! off so she lands in the cushions. Eleanor : “Because I’m Bipolar, dummy!” With a look of peculiarity crossing his face, the Scientologist looks down at his little wife who’s sitting on the sofa cross legged again, pushing the Garlic Press down and down and making Squish noises. Xavier : “Bipolar I or Bipolar II? I was reading up yesterday... Bipolar Disorders, also known as manic depression, are characterised by alternating episodes of mania or hypomania, and depression. Bipolar disorder is often not recognised by the patient, relatives, friends, or even physicians. In its early stages, bipolar disorder may masquerade as a problem other than mental illness. For example, it may first appear as alcohol or drug abuse, or poor school or work performance.” On the sofa, Ellie~! looks back at him, blankly as she plunges the Garlic Press down again. Xavier : “If left untreated, bipolar disorder tends to worsen, and the person experiences episodes of full-fledged mania and clinical depression. An early sign of manic-depressive illness may be hypomania...A state in which the person shows a high level of energy, excessive moodiness or irritability, and impulsive or reckless behaviour. Hypomania may feel good to the person who experiences it. Thus, even when family and friends learn to recognise the mood swings, the individual often will deny that anything is wrong.” Ellie~! wields the Garlic Press along with one of her hairpins and goes the charge Kannon who just puts a hand up and holds her forehead back as she tries to squash or stab his head. Xavier : “There are four types of mood episodes that a person can experience as part of Bipolar Disorder. These mood episodes include major depressive episode, manic episode, mixed episode, and hypomanic episode. A major depressive episode is not a disorder in itself, but is a part of another disorder, most often major depressive disorder or bipolar disorder.....” After 30 seconds or so of trying to injure her husband the prep gives up and drops her weapons, stomping her feet and crossing her arms in a huff again. Eleanor : “Being Bipolar is, like, SO boring!” Relieved his wife is no longer trying to gruesomely maim him, Kannon finally notices her clothing... And more importantly the pair of Red suspenders she is now sporting. Looking to the TV’s static and seeing a tape missing, it finally clicks in his Scientological brain. Xavier : “My little Shag Squirrel, have you been watching OSW again?” The little prep looks innocently back at him and adjusts her suspenders. Eleanor : “Like... Nyet.” Shaking his head, Kannon puts an arm around his petite wife and sighs. Xavier : “I’ve told you, OSW before bedtime will give you nightmares.” Ellie~! then perks up and holds a finger skywards. Eleanor : “I know what we need!” She quickly darts out from under her man’s arm and scurries to the kitchen where the sound of her rummaging and muttering to herself can be heard before a waft of Fire bursts out through the door and the little prep is seen standing with a bottle. Eleanor : “TEQILLA~!“ The CEO sighs and shakes his head. Eleanor : “Don’t swallow the worm!” Kannon mutters under his breath. Xavier : “You don’t swallow a lot of things...” His wife rushes over to him where he quickly confiscates the bottle much to her annoyance and throws it down on the sofa before ushering his wife towards the bedroom. Xavier : “I think you need to lay down....” The little prep struggles and protests. Eleanor : “Okay, but after I do one more thing... One thing that, like, SO rules and will be, like, the best thing in the world EVER!!! PLEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEASE!?!?!? It TOTALLY rules!” She turns and looks at him with her best puppy dog eyes which never fail and Kannon melts within seconds. Xavier : “Okay, you’ve hyped it up... What is it....” Just as Ellie~! opens her mouth the feed is lost and we see only static before fading to Black. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
