The Search for the Friends of Juan Borro (Part 1) featuring El Diablo

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/17/02 The Search for the Friends of Juan {Fade} It’s a cool November night with silence in the air. The leaves are shriveling and falling as nature is at it’s finest. The woman and children are sleeping and the men gather together for a night, a night of mischief. El Romantico is present with Alliances teammate El Diablo as they look over at the city from a distant hilltop. “Are we going down there with those disgraces.” El Diablo asks as he puffs a cigar Romantico had given him. “Someone has to save this world from sin, in which man engulfs himself in.” Romantico responds as he puts out his cigar in his own flesh. “Sure someone may save the world from sin, but it isn’t going to be me, I’ll laugh as they creep into the arms of hell.” Diablo responds letting out a belt of horrific laughter. “Well I’m going, you can do whatever you want.” Romantico says as he graces his way into his Cadillac. “You Spanish are all the same, so stubborn and unreeling, easy to persuade.” Diablo says as he follows Romantico into the veihicle. “Say what you want, but man needs to evolve.” Romantico replies angrily. “Before we go anywhere my friend, you’ll need to get out of that mockery you call clothing. I for one look like a force to be reckoned with. You well, you look like some type of gay pride correspondent.” Diablo says with a smirk. Romantico just nods and hits the gas of the car, leaving nothing but dust in his path. Diablo gives Romantico a black bandana and a white wife beater. Later on that night, the men arrive in town. Music is blasting and men are seen everywhere, fighting, having sex, and making mayhem. Romantico isn’t here for there already lost souls, and neither is El Diablo. The two men are here for a young man named Raul. Raul used to be a great friend of Romantico’s brother, Juan Borro. The two were in Shanghaied prison together and escaped together. El Diablo knew him as well, when him and Borro were in the same wrestling stable. “I hope you know what you’re getting yourself into, rookie. This isn’t the nicest place in the world.” Says Diablo whispering to Romantico. “I know.” Replies Romantico as he throws a drunken bum in the air because he had grabbed him in the arm. As the two make there way to the bar they’ve been told Raul is fighting at, they engulf in a little conversation. “You know your brother was a strong fighter.” El Diablo says with seriousness on his face not knowing if he should have brought up the conversation. “I know he was.” Romantico shortly replies as he looks up at the road. “He was always so happy it would make me sick, but I think he had something eating away at him on the inside.” Diablo says knowing Romantico might not want to hear it. “Why are you telling me this?” Romantico replies. “Because I see the same thing eating away at you. Your young Romantico, don’t make his mistakes.” Diablo proclaims as he goes back to not caring. Romantico doesn’t reply but rather just takes a moment to think of what Diablo had told him. “I wish you well against Clyde, I know you must have worked hard to get to the spot your at now.” Romantico says trying to bring up a little conversation again. “I believe I’ll win, but even if I don’t we’ll meet again one day in hell and finish what we began.” Diablo replies as he spits the gum in his mouth out to his right. “Why do you think these men continue these paths of destruction, knowing nothing will come of it.” Romantico asks as he looks as the disgraces around him. “Some souls are already lost, and there’s no way to find them again. I know, because it’s happened to me.” Diablo responds as Romantico interrupts him. “There it is, the Machismo.” Romantico points out. “What a fitting name, for a bunch of vain men.” Diablo says as he makes his way to his entrance. “Hey…hey…. hey, where do you two think your going.” The guard at the front door barks out. The guard pushes Diablo back and Diablo grabs the guard by his nose ring and throws him against the wall. “We have reservations.” The two men make there way into the bar and see men gathered in a circle. They push to the crowd and see two men fighting. The men are brutally going at one another until one man gets the upper hand. He grabs his opponent and smashes his head into the hard cement floor, until there in nothing left to smash. After his opponent’s stops breathing, he goes into the pockets of his pants and takes out the man wallet. Another group of men come and sweep his body to the side. As the next match gets ready to begin they spy Raul getting ready. “Go talk to him, I’ll stay here and make sure nothing gets out of hand.” Diablo says to Romantico as he holds his position. Romantico goes over to Raul and taps him on the shoulder. “Who the hell are you.” Raul says as he turns around. “I’m the brother of Juan Borro, I heard you were a great friend of his.” Romantico whispers. “Yea, he was a good guy. If you don’t mind I have to go and fight now.” Raul says as he pushes his way to the ring. “Juan was killed.” Romantico screams out as Raul turns around. Raul’s face shows his sadness, but he still goes on and gets ready for his match. “Don’t do this Raul. Don’t be like Juan, keep your life.” Romantico begs. “This is my life. I need money, and this is the way I get it. My mother died, my brother was killed back in the prison. I have nothing, nothing to lose, just to gain. And I’m going to gain.” Raul responds as he cracks his knuckles. “I’m not going to let you do this.” Romantico says as he steps in the path of Raul. “Come on, get your boyfriend out of here and let’s fight.” The opponent of Raul screams out from his corner. “Get out of my way.” Raul asks. “No.” Romantico responds firmly. Raul looks Romantico in the eye and then punches him in the face. Romantico still doesn’t move and hold his ground. “Get out of here.” Raul shouts. Romantico still stays there and Raul punches him again and knees him in the gut. Diablo comes over trying to aid Romantico, but Romantico waives him off, still not moving. “What are you stupid, I have a fight to fight. Do you want to get killed.” Raul says violently. “I want you to turn around and leave.” Romantico responds with what little breath he has left in his. Raul doesn’t stop and punches Romantico twice in the face followed by a knee to the head. Romatico finally falls as blood drips from his head. “I’ve tried.” Romantico finally gasps out as El Diablo comes over and drags Romantico away. Raul’s face shows his displeasure in himself, and later on that night with Romantico looking on Raul died in the ring. His body was swept away like the rest and he just lied there in a corner almost like he meant nothing to anybody. That night Diablo and Romantico left not saying a word. Both knew they did everything they could and some people just couldn’t be saved. RIP RAUL ROSA DIAZ!
