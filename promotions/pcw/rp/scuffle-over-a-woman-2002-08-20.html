Scuffle Over a Woman

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/20/02 Ever since Kramer had laid eyes on Nancy, his father's nurse, he can't stop thinking about her. That elegant beauty, well fit body, and shoulder length strawberry blond wavy hair, is all he can ever seem to think about anymore. In his thirty-three years of life he has never felt this way about a woman (or a guy either for that matter!). Sometimes if he spotted a pretty woman she would catch his eyes for a mere few seconds, but never his heart. And at this point and time Nancy has stolen Kramer's heart..... The scene opens up inside of a dimly lit section of an undisclosed restaurant. Sitting down on the big plush seats are Kramer and his father who sits right across from him. It seems as though they have just arrived because there is absolutely no food on the table and the waiter is just now taking their drink orders. Kramer orders a glass of water while Walter orders a cold beer. The waiter walks away after he hands both gentlemen a menu. Kramer opens it up and starts looking at all of the dishes, but Walter just sits there staring at Kramer. Walter: You still haven't told me why you brought me here. I mean its not like you to bring your old man out to a restaurant and offer to pay. Kramer: Why do I have to have a reason? I just thought it would be nice for you and me to catch up on things. Remember you can order anything you want so don't worry about the price. Kramer adds while staring back down at the menu. Before Walter can prod Kramer with more questions, the waiter comes back with the drinks and pulls out his pad an pen ready to take their orders. Kramer: I would like the eight ounce steak with a full garnished baked potato and Caesar salad. Waiter: And you sir? Walter: Ah...give me the sixteen ounce steak everything else that buffoon over there said. The waiter walks away to process the orders which gives Walter the time to get the real reason as to why he was brought here. Walter: Come on Kramer, give me some credit. I know something must be up. Realizing that he has been figured out, Kramer blushes not sure how to say what he is about to say. Walter: You're not dying are you? Kramer: NO!...no I'm not dying. Walter: Well come on with it then. Kramer: I....I was wondering if it would be possible for you to ask...um Nancy if she would like to come see me wrestle on Turmoil. Walter: Oh she loves wrestling! I will tell her to tune in tomorrow-special request by you. Realizing that it's hopeless explaining that he wants Nancy to be there in person, he just stays quiet sipping his water. Walter looks over at him again and notices the look on Kramer's face which gives him the impression that there is more to it. Walter: That wasn't it was it? I mean taking me out to dinner just to tell Nancy to tune in to a wrestling show doesn't make much sense. Kramer: To be truthful, I want you to ask her out for me. Walter: WHAT!? Kramer: I think she is a very beautiful woman, and I just don't have the courage to ask her myself. Walter: Now son I know I tell you all the time to try to find a nice woman but Nancy is mine. Kramer:You mean you two are going out? Walter: Not at the moment, but I plan on making my move very soon. Kramer: But your an old man! I mean you are old enough to be her grandfather for crying out loud! Walter: Age is nothing but a few numbers. Kramer: How do you plan on even keeping up with such an energetic woman? Walter: That's no business of yours. Besides you have no chance in hell. Kramer: No chance in hell? I don't see why she wouldn't like a decent looking young guy with a steady income, as opposed to a wrinkled old fool like you. Walter: Though you may be younger, you lack experience my boy. I can't even remember the last time you went on a date. Now Nancy seems like a woman who wants a man with experience when it comes to dating, and thus I will prevail the victor. Kramer: Oh we'll see! Before their arguing can continue on, their food arrives. They eat not speaking a single word to one another the rest of the evening. The scene slowly fades to black. Could it be possible that Kramer will be at a higher performance on Turmoil to show off to Nancy? But then how would she feel seeing Kramer hit a woman seeing as how he has to face Jaden and Brandi. Thinking about her watching makes Kramer even more nervous, afraid of what she may think. Then again, the little word scuffle he had with his father may prevent Walter from telling Nancy to watch Turmoil so Kramer may be safe after all.
