MTV's Diary [1]

28 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28.12.02 Yo, I'm Dante Jackson, you know - the 'Lyrical Messiah' and I wrestle for the mecca of entertainment, Primetime Championship Wrestling. You'll be going through a week with me, yes five days of how I live, how I act, and how the hell you can deal with a 9x platinum star on national television. You think you know? Brotha please, you have no IDEA! This is the diary of Dante Jackson; life, liberty, and the pursuit of victory. Friday, December 27th, 2002. The scene opens up towards the vage and desolate Marcy Project grounds in Brooklyn, paper trails and various bags of shopping dispensary hang around along with bullet holes, small tablets of crack cocaine, otherwise known as 'rocks', and the various wide eyed whinos and gangsters. Intersped at the same time happens to be Dante Jackson sitting down in a chair with his eyes focused upon the camera. 'To me, this is home - where I was born and bred man. My pops, he was a wrestler, and he tried to keep me out of these spots, but still when it came to being on the microphone, not a lyrical typhoon of anybody could take me away from here. This is where hip hop moved into full swing, and I think for a certain thing, not many people understand it. You've got people that can seriously try and take the mic from you when they really can't. That's what its about, hunger to be on top.' The camera pans around as the sight of Dante comes into view, he looks around on the block, checking for people he's seen before, reminders of his past as a bald headed man in black, wave cap and black leather coat acessorized upon him. 'Bishop,' Dante whispered. Turning around, the man beared sort of a resemblence to Tupac Shakur, with slanted midnight eyes, short but shown mustache and a small crevace of his nose opened by a hole were a gold nose ring would comfortably fit. The two men twirl around, hugging a bit before tossing their first on to the air and slamming them down upon one another in a vertical fashion, commenly refered to as 'dap'. 'This my dog right here!' Dante shouts in laughter as Bishop and he sit back, enjoying the scene around him. As the two men walk towards a hang out spot of theirs, 'King Corner', Bishop takes a seat on one of the benches, enjoying marijuana smoke before hiding it as a New York Police Department Squard Car patrols the neighborhood. 'Can't believe you'd come back here, Mr. Hollywood.' Bishop puffs hollow smoke as Dante stands, nodding his head. 'See you on magazines, television, shit is real isn't it?' Dante nods once more, 'Yeah, realer than any rhymer on this block. But I hear there's some cats down here wanting my spot.' 'Damn right, it ain't hard to tell man.' Dante leans forward into the camera, 'You see this, everything around here I breathed it, going to this spot here in Kings Corner, I murdered so many pretenders. Its just like wrestling man.' He looks back at Bishop, before keeping his vision controlled on the man instead of the camera, as the words light up upon the screen as the shot returns to Dante sitting in the chair present time. 'Nobody has the heart to stop me.' A smile grows upon his face as Dante looks ypon a couple of whinos sitting down in front of a corner store, looking away from the Bed-Sty housing projects a few blocks away. 'Who's the hottest cat out here right now?' Bishop turns his head to face Dante. 'Some dude named Juice, but he has nadda on you.' 'Just like Blake did back then?' Dante asks. Bishop shakes his head a bit, before crackinghis neck. He sits the blunt in between his lips, short puffs as white smoke passes away from his lips and nostrils as he holds the blunt up towards Dante. 'You wanna hit it?' 'Naw, Kannon got us mandatory piss testin' whenever we do something.' 'Ah.' Bishop puffs on the blunt once more as he puts it back near his lips. 'Look man, I gotta go, do this MTV thing a'ght?' 'Cool, kid. Stay up.' Bishop tells him as the two clasp hands once more as Dante ventures off. As he begins to get into his ride, a chrome silver Jaguar S-type with twenty four inch wheels with Sprewell rims fitted to them, he speeds off as the camera steady follows him. He looks around, but finding himself directly in Manhattan in less than no time. Opening the door to the ride, he gets out as soon as fans catch sight of him, they been to swirl, almost crying at the sight of him as he steps into the record store. Looking into the rap section, he picks up a copy of his own second disc, 'Tha 2nd Comin.' 'This is classic right here,' he points to the camera, shilling his own CD as he puts it back down, since he really does have the master copy at his home back in Houston. 'I have alot of distaste with the industry right now, both of them actually. In the wrestling industry, you have people hating on you because you're better than them. Its like I'm the truth, and they're the lesser. They know they aren't even close to how good I am, so they begin hating. You have your Chet King's, your End's, your Rush Mason's, and your Totally Tubular Tommy's all wanting a piece of me. Tommy, I'm looking for him personally. He's got something I want, and something I'll take. Its only natural. Music, you've got everybody wanting to go pop, like you just want sells and fame, but you should be wanting the chance to showcase your skills. I sell more albums than the next man because I have talent, more than any other person in the damn nation.' Dante chuckles, 'Maybe I should pick up X-Tina's new album. I hear its good, but then so is Chet King...' He walks up towards the check otu desk, looking at the various people around, waving to some of them before placing a CD down on the table, Justin Timberlake's 'Justified'. The black clerk looks at Dante suspiciously, before asking him. 'You DO know what you're buying right?' 'Yeah, J Tim a problem?' The clerk looks around, before leaning in on Dante. 'Justin Timberlake isn't occasionally popping up in a black man's stereo if you know what I mean. Yeah, the dude can sing, but....' 'But what?' 'HE'S WHITE! A WHITEBOY ON SOME BLACK MUSIC MAN!' the clerk screams out exciting some customers whom have somehow have overheard the conversation between Dante and the clerk as the man leans back. Dante raises an eyebrow. 'And Eminem isn't on some black music? I don't what the hell that Benzino guy is doing, afterall, he's a Vanilla Ice if I've ever heard of it. The man has bastardized The Source man, and all because somebody from half his race is selling more than every other artist. Not to mention that he wasn't the top seller in 2001!' 'So, this like a remix of how Chet King has better ratings because he's white but you've taken over his show and become like Public Enemy #1 with him?' the clerk questions. Dante nods, 'Just like that.' 'Cool, cool.' the clerk relaxes a bit as he scans the Justin Timberlake CD for Dante before wrapping it up in a bag. 'That'll be $13.31.' 'Nice!' Dante seems confident with the transcation, he didn't have to spend more than a twenty on a white man CD. He lays down the exact change, thirteen dollars composed of a ten dollar bill and three ones along with thirty one cents, three dimes and a single penny. The clerk registers the sale in the register before wishing Dante, 'Have a good day, and thank you for getting the white man's music.' Walking out of the store, he looks at the camera, 'See, even a black man can get white service, except when it comes to taxi cabs...' 'YO TAXI!' The Lyrical Messiah gets no response whatsoever from the yellow cars as he sighs to himself, before hopping into his car, burning away at the speed of light. You think you know? Playa, you don't have any idea. This is the Diary of Dante Jackson - pimpin'. End Part 1
