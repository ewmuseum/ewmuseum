Contemplation

3 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03-04-03 48 days had passed and one more was on the horizon when Skitzo would again have to defend his title, this time against another woman he knew nothing of…and quite frankly didn’t care. Lacuna Debris wasn’t the main concern of Skitzo right now; he had other fishy fish to fry. Brianna Washington was making things clear now; her objective was to destroy Skitzo bit by bit. First she took his sexuality, now she was after his title. His title that he had held close and kept for 48 days! The bitch. Even dragging her little homo poodle around trying to punk out Skitzo, her intent was obvious and her methods were worse. Gadgets ahoy. Skitzo had gadgets too dammit, ok so maybe they were illegal in 13 states and would make her scream for a different reason, but they were gadgets all the same! It didn’t matter really; he had already gone through Brianna, Clyde, and the Young Tiger not to mention the great gaggle of ganks to win the thing. Skitzo was reduced to a curmudgeon now due to Brianna, but revenge would be his soon…oh yes. Blows had been traded, but still Skitzo had managed to keep his title and his dignity-…ok well his title. Now more people were being thrown at him, Lacuna debris next up for one HeadFuck. Although he was confident he didn’t underestimate any of his opponents, the last thing he wanted was to brag about something and be defeated, why bother when he can brag honourably and be defeated with his dignity intact? Brianna he would make an exception for… On the night of his victory, and the beginning of a new record, Skitzo celebrated. And so it came to be that after 23hrs, 67 friends, 15 prostitutes, 6 farm animals, 3 tonnes of tequila and 1 penis in a fridge for Joe, Skitzo had racked up $1,743 in damages, and $2,333 on his tab. Who says the church isn’t fun? Two days later Skitzo was back on his feet again and training, readying himself for Lacuna and Brianna if needbe. Gynaeoco Joe meanwhile watched on, contemplating life and Skitzo’s antics, but never judging The Mental One. Who’d have thought a vagina could be more complex mentally than physically? On this day, as he continued his training and his preparation as always, like every good wrestler should do and did, Skitzo became drawn to a large picture on the wall before him. Ceasing his exercises he proceeded to check out the picture, and was surprised to find that the man before the camera was not like other men. A small memorial plaque hung below the picture, along with a poem inscribed into the brass that read: His tears trickled, twinkling like diamonds down his face,Though he could not wipe them, brush them away,His life he led with love and grace,His memory we can’t replace,A prayer sent to the heavens high,With the earth forever his body shall lie,The sweetest embrace he could not hold, His loving kiss, a story told,May angels take him… Skitzo pulls out his Swiss army knife and begins hacking away at the last row of letters, masterfully scrubbing out the final part so that it reads… May angels take him to the second hand shop, limbless BASTARD! The man who had obviously lost both his arms from a car accident had been commemorated, and now desecrated as Skitzo laughed at the picture and left. While he was content with his vandalism, he couldn’t help but think of the poem. The story of a man disadvantaged such as he was, couldn’t even wipe his tears from his face or hug someone, while Skitzo was having difficulty getting some and getting enough. All disadvantaged people had their difficulties, obviously…but from what Skitzo had read, they were remembered the most. And so he would be remembered. How many other manbians held the PCW Extreme Title for a record 48 days? How many schizophrenics can say they represent PCW’s official soda – Gatorade! How many world-renowned wrestlers can say they own a 20” black rubber dildo and a vagina in a jar? How many men can say that after they beat Lacuna Debris on Fusion they will surpass their target record date of 49 days and go on to whoop the tar out of Brianna Washington!?!?!? SKITZO CAN, MUTHAFUCKA!!!!!!!! The End
