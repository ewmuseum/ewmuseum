That's Not Me (Featuring Chris Storm)

3 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 3rd, 2003 As the noon hour struck and the clock sounded off I waited outside the front door of my Chatham home with a paper bag of groceries in one arm and my free arm knocking at the door waiting for someone to answer. Becoming tired of waiting I placed the bag of groceries aside and turned the knob, pushing the door open. I reached over to the groceries and took them into my arms once again as I walked into the house which was unusually quiet for this time of day. I walked into the kitchen and placed the groceries on the table and walked over to Chris who was sitting on the couch, examining a package that had come to him in the mail. She is teasing me!' I thought silently to myself. A devilish smile crossed my face. I looked back at Tessa and gave her a big fat smile. 'Oh this, it's nothing. I guess this must be the information about Evan's adoption or about the park.' I lied. Tessa smiled back and walked into the kitchen to put away the groceries. The second I saw her leave the room I instantly ripped open the package and dumped the contents onto the coffee table. Pictures came pouring out like rain. My eyes lit up like a kid at Christmas time. Shattered on the table were pictures of Tessa dressed very unusual for her. She was dressed in pink underwear with lacy frills and a stunned pair of fishnet stockings that were also pink. She was in very risky poses also, something very unnatural of Tessa. But I will admit, the second my eyes laid on those pictures, I became very excited. I wanted to jump out of my seat and grab Tessa then and there and throw her onto the bed. I was about to throw the package down when I discovered something else inside. I pulled it out to reveal one of the stockings. My heart skipped a beat. Feelings inside me started to stir that I had not felt in a long time. Like my animal instincts were starting to kick in again. I held the stocking up to my nose and smelled it. It was the same perfume. Looking back at the package, the address was sent to some 'Clint Steel'. I never heard of any Clint Steel before. In fact I do not know is Tessa has either. It was stamped 'Return to Sender' 'Insufficient Postage' under it. I looked at the stamps and sure enough there was only one postage stamp on it. I started to think about all of this. 'It's rather strange that Tessa would forget postage. Not to mention, why would she send this stuff to a Clint Steel?' I just could not piece it together. Maybe it was a fan. That still does not explain why she would send him this type of pictures of herself? Could she be cheating on me? 'No...' I muttered to myself. She would not do something like that. Then it hit me like a ton of bricks. 'Clint Steel, CS. Chris Storm, CS!' She must have been to shy to give me the pictures herself. So she mailed it off, knowing good and well it would be returned. That clever little fox. 'Steel, oh boy! That give's me some ideas. Oh I never knew Tessa should have such a wild side to her.' I told myself. A broad smile sat on my face as dirty thoughts ran through my head. I heard her footsteps coming and I scooped up everything and placed them back into the package before Tessa could see them. I walked back into the living room and sat down beside Chris as he fumbled with the package, stuffing whatever it was that was inside there back into it. I looked over at him as he looked up at me with a giant grin across his face. 'So what was in the package?' I asked as I laid my head on his shoulder. Chris' grin simply grew wider as he sat there staring at me as I looked into his eyes trying to catch his attention in any way I could. 'Chris?' I tapped the side of his cheek softly with the palm of my hand. 'Helloooo Chrissss.' Not getting a response I decided to check out the situation for myself and reached out for the opened box but before I could reach it Chris suddenly came back to himself and jumped out for my hand. I grabbed her hand and cupped it with both of mine. I looked into her eyes as she had a confused look on her face. I leaned forward and kissed her sweet strawberry lips. I leaned in more, adding pressure to the kiss. Tessa started to lean back onto the couch. I soon felt a hand against my chest, pushing me back. I sat back upright and looked at her. 'What are you doing Chris?' She asked, a little upset in her tone. I could not stop smiling though. The image of those pictures kept flashing in my head. I could almost smell those stockings again. I was ready to leap on her again, but I restrained myself from doing so. 'Thank you so much my Tessa Bear. That package was incredible. But how about next time you just give me the stuff straight forward. Although these little games did turn me on.' I told her, a soft coo in my words. I looked over at Chris totally lost as to what he was talking about. My eyes narrowed in confusion as my face wrinkled and I asked, 'What on earth are you talking about, Chris?' 'The package, what else would I be talking about silly.' He chuckled as he wrapped his hand around my arm and gently pulled me closer. 'The package?' I questioned 'I did not send you the package.' 'Oh Tessa Bear I know you probably feel ashamed about it, but really it's nothing to be ashamed about. Everyone wants to be a little seductive at some time or another.' He smiled as he wrapped his arms around my shoulder, pulling me closer. 'Chris I swear I did not send you that package. I do not even know what is in it.' I responded once more while reaching out to the package to see just what he was talking about that was leaving me totally stumped. I got behind Tessa and started to rub on her shoulders as she picked up the package. I still cannot believe she did this for me. Tessa's hand flipped open the package. My heart was racing as I watched her delicate hands at work. I could smell her now as I rubbed my hands into her shoulders. I leaned in to kiss her neck softly but the sound of the package dropping onto the coffee table snapped me out of it. Tessa's body was shaking now. I leaned back from her to try and see what the matter was. Out of the corner of my eye I could see some of the pictures now shattered on the table. As the contents of the package scattered across the floor I felt my heart sink as my head dropped and fell into the palms of my hands. The tears were so strong I could not hold them back. The only thing I could do was wonder who on earth would do something that low. Was this Chris' idea of a joke, or was someone else behind it? I stood up from my seat on the couch and fell beside one of the pictures. Taking the picture in my hand I stared at it and tried to figure out how this could happen. It looked just like me. The skin tone was the same as mine as was the figure and everything else about it. But I knew I had never taken a picture like this, so how could something like this come back to me? What did I do to deserve this? The moment I saw her fall to one of the pictures my heart sank. Something was not right here. I wrapped my arms around her and hugged her softly. Using a free hand, I pulled the picture away from her and moved them away to the side of the table. I pulled my hand back and placed it on the side of her head. I pulled her in to let her cry on my shoulder. 'Tessa, don't be upset. I love the pictures. I don't think any less of you either. How could I? I use to take pictures like that all the time. Only mine where much worse. You just wanted to give me a gift you knew I would love. And I do, and I love you for it.' I pulled her face to look at mine, our noses touching. 'You are my Tessa Bear. You are now, you was then, you always will be. Now let's dry up those tears and get these pictures out of here before the kids see them.' 'Chris you do not understand.' I pouted, burying my head further into his shoulder as I continued to cry, with no sign of tears ever coming to stop, like a never ending stream. 'Tessa of course I understand.' He whispered into my eyes softly, rubbing the back of my neck with his hand as a source of comfort. 'I used to take pictures like that myself. I'm not ashamed of you I promise.' 'No Chris, you really don't understand.' I protested back as my voice got softer with each word, becoming lost in the shirt that covered Chris' upper body. 'What makes you think I do not understand?' Chris questioned as he positioned his hand under my chin and lifted my face up so that my eyes were starring into his. 'Chris those are not my pictures.' I said as my eyes dropped, unable to maintain eye contact with him because I was sure he would not believe me. How would he be able to believe it was not me? The person in the picture looked so much like me they could have easily been my twin. But they were not and I knew it, so the only thing I could do was hope in the bottom of my heart that he would believe the words that dropped my mouth. I just left my arms around her, holding her close. I was trying to take this all in. How could it not be her? The person in the picture looks just like her. Tessa has never lied to me before though. Why would she lie to me now? I started to laugh. Tessa lifted her head up and looked at me confused. I touched her nose. 'If you did not take them pictures, then how about you take some now?' I told her. Tessa leaned back, shocked I would say something, however I kept laughing. I weakly smiled at Chris' joke as my mind continued to wander back to the pictures. Who would do such a thing? I laid my head on Chris' shoulder once more as he scooped me up into his arms and sat down on the couch, positioning me on his lap as the answer to my question hit me like a brick. If anyone were responsible for a joke of this sort it would be the two to go by three simple letters. P.W.A
