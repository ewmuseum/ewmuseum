Date: not entered So Jason Snow and Rent-a-Hero have become companions - friends, even. They’ve been seen together on a regular basis lately, and are booked to team up this coming Tuesday against Politically Incorrect, one of the top tag teams in the history of PCW. Our scene opens as we watch the former three time PCW Champion sitting on the bed of his luxurious hotel suite in Los Angeles where he awaits his match against James Sexton of FWF in the first round of the Wrestling God 3 tournament. At the moment though, his mind can’t be further from the tournament... Snow: “Well this doesn’t make much sense...” Snow, amazingly enough, is flipping through a comic book. There are more comic books scattered across the bed... Snow: “According to this, all I need to do to use my Superior powers for GOOD is grow an enormous pair of breasts and have them bounce all over!” Snow tosses the comic book aside and collapses back down onto the bed... Snow: “How can I use my Superiority for the purpose of good? How, damn it!? HOW!?!?” Snow slams his fists down on either side of him, crinkling comic books in the process. A moment later, he leaps to his feet and exclaims... Snow: “I’ve got it!” With that, Snow rushes out of the bedroom of his suite, and then out of the suite altogether. What does Snow have in mind? As we ponder this question to ourselves, the scene fades to black... Moments Later... Black Screen... Snow: “Damn it, wench! Would you just go across the damn street!” The scene fades in as Snow scolds an elderly lady on the busy Los Angeles sidewalk. The elderly lady, who’s walking with the support of a walker, glares at the former PCW Champion, who must be at least fifty years her junior... Old Lady: “But I don’t want to cross the street!” Snow: “Damn it! I said cross the street! I’m here to help you, wench!” Snow grabs the old lady by the arm and prepares to physically drag her across the street. The old lady, however, will have none of it. She begins hitting the wanna-be-good-guy Killin’ Villain in the arm with her purse, pushing him away from her... Old Lady: “I already told you! I’m walking to the bus stop! And it’s on THIS side of the street!” Snow: “I don’t believe that I asked you, wench! Now, it is a good deed to help an old lady cross the street, and damn it, I am a GOOD Killin’ Villain! On with it now!” Old Lady: “But I have to catch the bus!” Snow: “Well maybe there will be a bus over there!” The old lady begins her slow motion toward the bus stop, which is some hundred feet away. In a fit of frustration, the young PCW Superstar stomps his Snake Skin clad foot down on the sidewalk... Snow: “Damn it, wench! Don’t make me use force!” The old lady ignores Snow, frustrating him even more. She might be a little afraid of him. Snow is, of course, an intimidating looking character when not in the wrestling ring. He’s well above the size of an average man, and his jet black leather pants and Snake Skin boots usually don’t go over well with strangers. Still, Snow is persistent... Snow: “Come on! You’re crossing the damn street, wench!” Snow grabs the old ladies arms and begins dragging her across the street... Old Lady: “Somebody! HEEEELP!!!” Snow: “I AM helping you! What the hell do you want!? A piggy back ride!?” While Snow is pulling her across the street, she drops her walker and it is run over by an angry motorist within seconds. Traffic is forced to stop, and honking ensues. Snow glares at a few drivers... Snow: “Damn it! Can you not see that I’m performing a good deed here! Silence! The lot of you!” Snow continues to drag the old lady across the street. Out of no where, a pair of hoodlums flash by - stealing the old lady’s purse on their way. Snow, who’s staring at the destination, doesn’t notice... Snow: “By Greatness, have you been slacking with the prunes? You weigh more than most wrestlers I’ve demolished!” At that moment, the old lady’s hair - a wig - falls to the ground revealing that she only has patches of natural hair on her head. Snow continues to drag her across the street... everyone in sight is watching now... but this is LA, so no one does anything about it... Snow: “There!” Snow drops the old lady on the opposite sidewalk and dusts off his hands. He scratches his chin and speaks his thoughts aloud... Snow: “Well... I don’t FEEL more good than before... I know what I need! More good deeds! I wonder if there are any cats who need rescuing from trees?” With that, the Killin’ Villain begins walking away, leaving the camera to focus on the fallen old lady... bald... purseless... the victim of Jason Snow’s new “nice streak”... the scene fades to black...
