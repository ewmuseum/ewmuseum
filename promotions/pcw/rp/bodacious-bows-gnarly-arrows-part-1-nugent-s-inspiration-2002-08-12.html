Bodacious Bows, Gnarly Arrows - Part 1 - Nugent's Inspiration

12 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 12 [Politics are totally bogus. Lets face it, dudes. You've got these government compadres who pass all these rules and talk about what is better for the American people. I thought that we were supposed to be free and gnarly?! I guess we're just on a most short leash here in the States. Talk about bogus] [The Cowabunga Connection couldn't get the job done of Friday Fusion, they once again came up short of winning the most bodacious prize in tag team competition...the PCW Tag Team Titles. The Heartbreakers fought a most vicious battle and came out on top, but barely. If a view had just seen the match then they would have never guessed what happened 20 minutes before their match started. Politically Incorrect happened. Those TOTALLY BOGUS politicians stuck their heinous noses where they didn't belong. Maybe their bogus beef was with The Heartbreakers, but a compadre just can't stand in the locker room and watch their opponents get beat on. It wouldn't be a straight up bodacious victory if The Heartbreakers were broken and battered. It would seem that the Connection would have been in totally bogus cahoots with the evil politicians. The most heinous Incorrect ones nailed the Connection with the ring bell, which in turn rang their bells. Both teams were out of it during competition which leaves the door wide open to who really could have won that title match. Talk about HEINOUS!!] [The PCW booking committee has taken it's bodacious liberties of booking a most bodacious match of Fusion. A triple threat Tag Team Tornado match. AWESOME, DUDES!! Not only will The Tubular One and the Heavy Metal One get their hands on Politically Incorrect, but they get another crack at the dudes in Pink as well. Things could be more bogus in the lives of the Tubular One and Harry's, but keeping a positive outlook on everything manages to help the Connection out in many stick situations. That winning this match can propel them to the top of the tag team spectrum, making sure that the reign in PCW is MOST BODACIOUS!] [Shooting an arrow about 50 feet to a dot the size of a golf ball seems like a most hard task. For rock star Ted Nugent it's a totally easy thing to do. Every since Harry saw a live show from Ted he's wanted to be able to shoot a bow and arrow just like in Medieval times. Tommy knows that Harry really just wants to find Randy Raggamuffin, and then shoot him in the ass with an arrow. Tommy smiles brightly to himself, his pearly white teeth glistening in the sunlight, and believes that is a most bodacious idea. After all, Raggamuffin is the totally bogus dude who stole Shailyn from him] [Bobby's Bow and Arrow Range is the most gnarly place to go if you want to learn how to shoot like a pro. It's about 500 square feet of open grass land for all sorts of bow and arrow activities. Just shooting at that totally overused bullseye target in the shooting range, hit gnarly moving targets to increase your accuracy, or to go on a fake safari and shoot up some cardboard deer. As Tommy, dressed in his PCW T-shirt and blue swim trunks, looks around he feels like he's under a safe environment. Suddenly an arrow flies past his face and his smile turns into absolute horror] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Not Bodacious... [Harry giggles to himself with his bow in hand and a bundle of arrows attached to his back. He proudly wears his Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. The uniform of a true 80's headbanger!! He checks out the shooting range and nods. Then he checks out the moving targets and nods. Then he sees cardboard deer popping up and he has a large smile on his face with that] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Do you think that they'll let me shoot cardboard cut outs of Fred Durst, man? [Tommy shrugs] TTT: I don't know, compadre. But I have to say, THAT IS A TOTALLY GNARLY IDEA!! [Harry smiles and nods. The two men begin making their way over toward the safari zone. Arrows wiz behind them as if it is some sort of Elven-like war going on in the country area of California. Both men are absolutely oblivious to the large amount of arrows almost striking them] Harry: I really wish we could nail those politicians, man. TTT: Totally, dude. I think if it weren't for them that we'd be wearing those tag team straps right now! It's such a TOTAL BUMMER! [Harry nods as he begins playing with his bow, stretching the string back and pretending that there is an arrow in it, and then letting go as if he'd just nailed a gnarly target. His face turns to full concentration and he goes into celebration mode, killing the imaginary game. Harry has bagged his first kill!!!] TTT: Maybe we need to stop being so bogus about this. I mean we always find ways to blame it on these other bogus dudes, but maybe it's really us that can't get the job done. [Harry is totally out in his own zone as he has the string pulled back looking all around him like a vicious predator. His face is as mean as a pitbull, he's got ice in his veins, and his sense of smell is like a wolverine. He can feel the prey shaking underneath his feet. He knows that their going to die a not so far out death, but that's because they're lower in the food chain. That kind of stuff just happens, man] TTT: We do have that tornado tag team match to totally redeem ourselves on those bogus politicians. That Powell and Lovecraft have been like two heinous thorns in our sides since day one, dude. They are like a bogus cold that just won't go away, always giving you those heinous sniffles and stuffed up nose all the time. Ugh, I hate that!! [They come to the safari entrance as the guy running it smiles brightly to the Connection. The man has pretty short brown hair that is parted on the side. He doesn't look that old in his brown dress shirt and khaki shorts. His steel toed boots and socks pulled up to his knees really don't do it for his image. He looks rather bogus in a most heinous kind of way if you catch the drift] Safari Owner: HI THERE!! What can I do for you? [The man's voice seems rather high pitched with a very odd lisp. Harry and Tommy both look to one another, really trying not to laugh in the man's face] Harry: Do you happen to like Pink, man? [The Safari Owner looks at Harry with a large amount of confusion on his face] Safari Owner: I'm not a big pink fan, but there is nothing wrong with it. Just a color, I suppose!! [Harry laughs hysterically and points at the odd looking, odd sounding Safari Owner] Harry: HAHA!! Nothing wrong with Pink, man!!! Talk about not far out, man! TTT: He's a bit excited about shooting some arrows, dude. [The Safari Man smiles brightly and nods, showing that he understands] Safari Owner: Of course!! Though, you have to understand that archery is to be taken quite seriously, guys. You can't just go around thinking that your great at it. It takes years of practice to be a real professional. There are a lot of sacrifices to be made when trying archery. When you just want to go out to the club and have fun, you have to forget that and go SHOOT SOME ARROWS!!! [Harry looks at the man as if he wants to rip his head off] Harry: I just want to shoot an arrow like Ted Nugent, man. [The Safari Owner looks at Harry with confusion again] Safari Owner: Who's Ted Nugent??? [Harry is shocked. He puts his hand on his heart as if this Pink loving dude gave him a heart attack] Harry: WHAT?!?!....man. [The Safari Owner looks a tad worried now, looking up to Tommy for some kind of explanation. All Tommy can do is shrug his shoulders. A dude deserves to get a most heinous tongue lashing for not knowing who Ted Nugent is. Cat Scratch Fever is only one of the most gnarly songs of all time, coming close to derailing the Beach Boys, but that's a different story at this point] Safari Owner: Well...listen. You guys just GO WILD and have fun here. [Harry nods with a very stone cold look and walks through the main safari gate. Tommy follows him in trying not to laugh hysterically] Safari Owner: HAVE FUN!! [The two bodacious compadres walk into the forest for a safari that they will soon not forget. With Harry armed with very sharp arrows, Ted Nugent playing in his mind, and the hatred of one thousand suns burning for Politically Incorrect, he is a very dangerous man. God pray for those cardboard deer.....]
