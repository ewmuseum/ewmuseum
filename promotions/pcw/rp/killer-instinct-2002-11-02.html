Killer Instinct

2 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 2nd, 2002 It was a night like normal, as normal could be. The brisk breeze filled the air and the night sky rained down onto the land. Thousands of people, millions if that where being affected all at the same time. It wasn’t a cool day, but a little warmer then usual. Vilas headed out in his nightly walks and slowly pressed on keeping his head down making sure not to make eye contact with anyone that pass. He made his way through what appeared to be thousands of people but really was only the wind picking up and pushing the leaves around on the pavement. Vilas stopped right in his tacks and with him so did the leaves and wind. He lifted his head and looked around. Nothing their but the shadows of the towering buildings the stretched out across the streets. The leaves where gone and the wind seemed to be a never existing push. Vilas continued on his way and sure enough the wind began to blow again lifting the dead leaves off the cement and up into his face. He closed his eyes and continued on his way. I stop and the world stops. I move and the world moves with me. What is really going on here and why am I being a key to this world? What makes me so special after all? Vilas stopped again and looked back into the street behind him. He peered into the night and could just make out a small figure that was looking at him. The small gray figure started to move closer towards Vilas and come into more of a clear view. The figure was all covers in black and had a dark blue mask pulled over his face as he crept closer and closer to Vilas. Who is this man? Wouldn’t somebody notice this man walking the streets in a mask and think he is up to no good? He could have followed someone else but yet and follows me. So many questions but what little time I have left won’t care what the answers are. Vilas looked around trying to maybe find someone else around him, just in case things went for the worst. He slowly tilted his head to the right and noticed no one was there. A city of millions and everyone had vanished like they were just picked up into think air. Vilas started to get nervous as the figure stepped closer and closer. Vilas backed up and tried to stay a few steps ahead of the figure but it wasn’t doing him any good. He could no see the whites of the figure eyes as they peered at each other. Vilas skin grazed the bricks on a wall that seemed to just appear from the wind. He pressed up against it and stopped moving. The figure stopped as well. A sigh of relief came over Vilas face as watched the figure put out his hand in what appeared to be friendship. Vilas smiled and also stretched out his hand and meet with the figure. Their hands locked and they stood still with no moment. Vilas smile faded and he wondered what was going to happen. Finally the two shook hands and Vilas gave another sigh of relief. He tried to pull away but the figure trip was too much to get away. Vilas started to pull hard to get free but couldn’t get out of the grip. The figure pulled Vilas towards him, and looks him straight in the eyes. In the figures other hand he holds a large knife. Vilas stares down at the knife and as a chill runs up his spin and freezes his body still in his place. The figure brings the knife in front of Vilas eyes and turns it around so he can get a good look. The figures grip of the knife loosens and Vilas watches’ hoping it doesn’t slip and pierce his hand. The figure snaps his grip and Vilas gives another sigh of relief but the figure still moves down and slowly cuts the hand of Vilas bring blood from the surface. Why is this happening? I have done nothing to this guy and yet he wants to hurt me. Is this some kind of test or joke that I am not in on, or is there something I am not getting? Is there a hidden meaning that is just out of my reach? Vilas looks into the figure eyes and pulls his hand away. A sadistic laugh comes out of the covered mouth as Vilas tries to stop the bleed. He looks up into the eyes of the figure and strikes a look that could kill. The blood trickles down his hand and dripps into a pool of blood on the cement. He gives the figure a swift backhand to stop the laughter. The figure shoots his head back at Vilas as the two stand face to face. The figure lifts his knife into the air again and thrusts at Vilas. Vilas ducks and takes off running. I’m not going to wait for this wacko to get up and take my life. I think it’s safe to say that this is fear at it’s best. Still the question remains is why is this happening? Vilas starts to sprint down the sidewalk looking back to find where that figure went. Sure enough fallowing right behind is the masked figure. Vilas cuts into an alley and pills boxes and creates that are scattered in the alley up to slow down the shadower. Vilas waits for the figure to pop his head threw and continue the attack. Nothing comes from either side of the alley. Where did he go? He wouldn’t give up this easy would he? I know I would continue the chase and make sure I have the eliment of surprise. Of course I have no people to worry about if I end something. I have already been dimissed. Vilas slowly walks out into the street and looks around. Emptyness as far as the eye can see. No people on the street, cars left in the middle of the street with any driver and papers blowing in the wind and wrapping around polls. Vilas walks out into the street and checks a few cars and looks for a trace of human life. He looks in a few mirros and windows but nothing is found. This has to be some kind of dream. No way everyone just got up and left like this. It’s impossible for a whole city to relocate in a matter of minutes. Vilas walks into the middle of the street and looks around trying to find an answer somewhere in the headlights of the cars that surround him like a cage. A sudden chill rushes up his body as an object brushes against a car roof. Vilas stops and turns around and finds the mask figure in front of him. He wipes the blood off the blade and again walks towards Vilas. The masked figure makes a dash at Vilas but Vilas manages to get out of the way and trip his nemises up. He kicks him in the back to knock him down and takes off again running over top of the cars and into the city streets. He looks back only to see the figure pick up his knife and run off in a different direction. Vilas doesn’t wait and he quickly looks for another exit out of this line of fire. He heads towards a large hotel complex. Maybe if I can see him I will be able to go after him and become the hunter. Isn’t that always how we like things in this world? Vilas heads towards one of the doors of glass. He pulls the door and can’t gain entry so he breakes the glass with his foot. The glass falls to the floor and spreads on the ground into a thousand pieces. Vilas checks back at the door and continues to walk to the elivator door. He presses the button then waits as the starts its count down to the ground level. Vilas looked at the numbers tick down and waited for the door to open. He looked back at the shattered glass making sure he was not being followed by the figure that had death in his eyes. He motioned with his hand for the door to quickly come open. If felt like an hour before the doors swung open. Vilas took a stop and looked up. Standing in front of him was none other then the masked figure. Didn’t I just leave him back their about five minutes ago? Smart man is this figure. Vilas started to back track but stopped dead in his tracks. He peered across the floor at the figure that was still coming closer and closer. Vilas smiled which starttled the figure in his tracks. Vilas calmly walked by the figure that didn’t do anything. He headed toward the elivator doors and stepped in. The figure know what was happening as Vilas tried to escape. The figure jumped but only got a face full of door, as they slammed shut. Vilas pressed the roof button and up the elivator went. It was clear to me now who he was. It was so odvious and I can’t believe I didn’t figure it out sooner. It was just a matter of time before he would be unmasked. The elivator stopped at the roof and the doors open. Sure enough standing on the other side of the roof was the masked figure. Vilas stepped out of the elivator and onto the roof. Only the two men and the elivtor sgaft were on the rooftop. The figure took off running at Vilas again, but Vilas just stood still. Their eyes locked together as the knife was raised high into the air. Down with forst came the knife, slashing a whole into Vilas skin. The blood started to flow again but Vilas smiled at the sight of his own inners. The masked figure came for another swipe but Vilas caught the knife in his hand. The two struggled back and forth trying to get control of the knife. Vilas looked on the figure hand and noticed blood flowing from a cut. He was never cut, so where is that blood coming from? The struggle continues on as they roll on the ground in an attempt to get control. Vilas finally gets control after head butting the figure and sending him into a daze. Vilas grabs the knife and swipes it with his fingers to clear off the blood from before. The masked figure looks at Vilas and runs at him again. Vilas waits for the figure to get close enough and then sticks the knife into the figure heart. Blood poors out of the wound and gets on Vilas hand. The masked figure struggles to keep his balance but falls to the ground. Vilas walks over and pulls the knife from the suffering mans chest. He looks down at the mask that covers the figure face. He reaches out and pulls it off. I knew it was he all this time. How else could he have been every where that I was and knew everything I did? He was tricky but of course I can always beat my self in any battle.Vilas looked down at a face he knew all to well. It had all made sense now. This was the reason he was able to be in the same place as Vilas and why he always knew were he ways. Vilas tood face to face with himself. Vilas took the mask and threw it on the ground next to the knife as he watched himself die. Truly this was all a lesson. Would I even kill my own self to survive and achieve my goals. I think we all know what I will do in the future. But now the one question remains that still has not be answer. Who really is the killer? Vilas smirks and picks up the knife and places the mask on his head. He heads back to the elivator shaft and opens the doors. He looks back at the dead body that is himself and gives a sadistic laugh. The doors swing close and the elivator heads back down to the lobby.
