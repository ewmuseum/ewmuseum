PI finds nothing 'tubular' about the CC..

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: PI finds nothing 'tubular' about the CC.. Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: March 29th, 2002 Confusion. Normally, the statement here is made by myself, a hired announcer and narrator for Politically Incorrect, in a confident tone, because my employers are confident. After all, I do all of the work for them for their campaign commercials. I am used to delivering a firm strong message. But the message my employers have for me today is something akin to this.. WHAT THE F*CKING HELL?! Lovecraft and Powell both seem somewhat shocked at looking at one of the teams who will be opposing them. Powell is a 26 year old man, and Lovecraft is, well, considerably older. But neither thought they were THIS far out of touch. What has made these two men feel extremely old? The answer can probably be summed up in two words: COWABUNGA, DUDES! Yes, the Cowabunga Connection have been anything but silent in regards to the PCW Tag Team Title Gauntlet match that is being held on Friday Fusion. However, our powerful politicians have had some problems formulating a reply. Why? Because, they can't figure out what the hell the Cowabungas are saying! ::Venice Beach, California. A well known stretch of sand to most in the world. Known for hard bodies, great tans, and excellent surfing. Keep 'known to most' in mind. Because, we can now see two men who do NOT embody ANY of the above three sentiments, Politically Incorrect, sitting in lounge chairs under a nice big beach umbrella. Both men are clad in bathing suits, Powell being the better looking of the two for any ladies out there. They sigh, enjoying a nice relaxing day, while pondering what they are going to do later on during Fusion.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: Now, this is the life, is it not, Councilman Powell? William S. Powell: Ah, it is Mr. Lovecraft! JUST LOOK AT ALL THE WOMEN AROUND US! Wearing revealing things, at that! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Sorry William. I'm happily taken by Katherine. William S. Powell: Ah yes, your intern....you mean, YOU'RE ACTUALLY FAITHFUL TO YOUR INTERN?! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yes, Mr.-- William S. Powell: YOUR INTERN?! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Why do you sound so surprised? William S. Powell: THE ENTIRE POINT OF HAVING AN INTERN IS SO YOU CAN HAVE SEX WITH SOMEONE OTHER THAN YOUR WIFE! Reginald V. Lovecraft: I have no wife, Mr. Powell. Katherine is my one and only. We truly love each other. ::Lovecraft looks annoyedly at Powell's continued laughing. Seeing this, Powell determines this to be a good time to bring up another subject:: William S. Powell: It seems that more of our opponents have been speaking...although, there is a problem. I cannot understand them for the life of me! Reginald V. Lovecraft: The Cowabunga Connection, you mean? I know.....it is like they are speaking some foreign language! William S. Powell: I know! I mean, it can't have been THAT long since I was a teenager.....and I used words that my parents never understood....but those two are ridiculous! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Apparently, a new language has been formed using words such as 'cowabunga', 'bogus', and 'tubular'. Man, I remember the last time this happened in my native Georgia. All the African-Americans began speaking the ebonics! William S. Powell: The ebonics are at least understandable, foo'! Don't be dissin' the ebonics or I'm gonna bust a cap all up in yo' ass, yo! Reginald V. Lovecraft: William, NEVER EVER do that again! Ebonics are BAD BAD THINGS! BAD!! William S. Powell: I know....I know.. ::Powell reaches into his cooler, and pulls out a nice refreshing Snapple, and begins imbibing.:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: What's with the Snapple kick all of a sudden? William S. Powell: Xavier Kannon recommended it to me the other day while finalizing plans for the Des Moines Church of Scientology...it's no Cabernet Sauvignon, but its not bad for something in a can. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Has to be better than that champagne in a can we tried last night. ::Both members of Politically Incorrect shudder:: William S. Powell: Regardless, it is clear that we both have work to do. Despite their use of language Lovecraft and I cannot understand, the Cowabunga Connection certainly poses a threat in the Gauntlet match, and indeed later on if they make it to Spring Fling and the Fourway dance. They are, by nature, fun-loving and laid back, but they definitely possess ability in the ring. Reginald V. Lovecraft: 'Totally Tubular' Tom's been in NTICW for a while....this is his first shot at the big time, like us. He and his partner are undoubtedly going to approach this with all of their might and energy. Yet, they will fail....as have most of our opponents. Why? William S. Powell: Because....along with our political power, we have the power of Scientology on our side! And, while we may both be baptized Catholics, we are not bigots! We see the necessity for religious tolerance in Primetime Championship Wrestling! And, Xavier Kannon pays good money, so he is a friend. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yet I assure you, he will NOT be at ringside. You see, unlike Golden Game...we do not need an army at our back to be victorious. We do not need to have a man come in and help us win. We may be Politically Incorrect, but damnit, we do things on our own. And, for Gold Patron Meritorious Xavier Kannon, his valet Eleanor Hall, and for the entire PCW....this Friday shall be Election Day! William S. Powell: And....on Election Day, Politically Incorrect ALWAYS triumphs! ::Both men toast to a can of Snapple, as we fade to....:: Paid for by the Friends of Politically IncorrectThe Next PCW Tag Team Champions ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
