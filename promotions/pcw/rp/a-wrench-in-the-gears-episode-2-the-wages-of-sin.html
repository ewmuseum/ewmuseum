A Wrench in the Gears: Episode 2 -- The Wages of Sin

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 27 of 2002 A.D. Bruce Shanahan was miles away. Though wholly present physically, with eyes glued unblinking on the ceiling and ankles crossed at the foot of his bed, his mind drifted and wandered amongst an array of scenarios played time and again like a broken record in his brain. Ever since his boarding of the Wrestle Vessel, only two things had crossed his mind, each shifting sporadically with the ticking of the clock: the Extreme title match on Sunday, and the warning he had received only about 18 hours prior via his brother-in-arms, Marcus Stryker, and the Delta Fox unit. The former he was undoubtedly worried about; several times he had been granted this very opportunity and come up on the short end of the stick in each battle. His focus had waned, his attacks not as crisp and brutal as was essential, his defense weak and ineffectual. He had vowed it would not occur again. Yet another defeat at the hands of that wretched Rush Mason was intolerable, and elimination at the hands of either that trendy wannabe Wild Wing or the testicularly-crippled Aymin would be even more deplorable. For this, he would be ready. He would stake his shoddy slum-based apartment on it, as little as that was worth. But God….the latter he was helpless. Not because he was hesitant to break his word. Given a window of opportunity he’d betray that devious Chaos in a heartbeat; Nyarlathotep had no honor and Shanahan had no qualms about returning the favor. But it wasn’t dissimilar from the vampiric myth of countless ages. “Do not invite them into your home, and they cannot harm you within its walls. But the moment you break the seal and utter invitation, all barriers are broken and you will assuredly pay in blood.” Alas he had broken that golden rule. Not into his home, per se, in terms of plaster and thresholds, but his soul resided in the vile clutches of his greatest foe. And no exorcism of any Earthly priest could purge the eternal evil inhabiting his body. Not of one so powerful and blasphemous as the Crawling Chaos. And it went without saying the villainous fiend would likely not surrender the hold He had on the body of His mortal vessel….. Stiff upper lip, Bruce. Surely I’m not that bad of a guest…..heheh….” His eyes narrowed into fine slits, lips pursed and teeth clenched. “You lied to me, Old One. Ya gave my word of honor….” “Honor! HAHAHA!! Bah! Honor is a human principle, one founded on a basis of lies and foolishness. It is not one myself and my kindred find particularly appealing nor necessary. A mortal virtue, a divine folly. Don’t kid yourself. Your avarice, your thirst for glory and blood….it was your own flaws that led you to this decision. As you humans say…..it’s time to pay the piper.” Regardless, Shanahan cracked a smug grin. “Do what ya will with me, you’ll never get to Derek. He was always smarter than me. He’ll never listen to ya, ya hellish bastard.” “Oh contraire, my dear mortal. It’s merely a matter of appealing to the desires of the heart, and in the right fashion. But…heheh….sometimes I truly do wonder what it’s like to be constrained by such ignorance. Apparently the cosmic wisdom my kind enjoys comes with time. So it’s by no fault of your own that your species make such brash insinuations. Such imprudence comes with the fleeting existence that afflicts mankind.” That all too familiar pang of helplessness gripped the tormented Shanahan again. The beast was right; if he hadn’t let his insatiable longing for the Extreme title override his reasoning, he wouldn’t be in this mess. Having made his own bed, it was his time to now lay in it. He couldn’t alter the ship’s course without fear of reparation, both on himself and his still-missing brother, and focus entirely on his reason for embarking on this God-forsaken voyage: the task of taking out Aymin, Mason and Wild Wing to restore dignity to the most devalued trophy in the company. His only hope was that Stryker and his crew would be able to come through with their mission and send the multi-million dollar liner around the threat without incident. Bruce cringed at the bitter irony the situation unveiled so clearly: in most missions his involvement was pretty unwarranted, almost like a broken wheel or a wrench in the gears of a well-oiled machine. Yet the one time his association was crucial, his intervention was forbidden by his own damned recklessness. Nyarlathotep had won this round. Devoid of options, Bruce merely squeezed his eyes together and swept all thoughts of Sunday from his mind. A single tear streamed down his cheek, cascading down his chin to settle in a bead upon his goatee. “BWAHAHAHA!! You insolent fool, you know as well as I do your cries will go unheard. This is your purgatory, the atonement for all your wicked deeds. Don’t try to escape you fate! Your God has condemned you! No one will lament your exile and you know that fact as well as I do! Save your prayers for your dying breath, mortal.” As usual, the barbs of truth stung more than any flesh wound. (Excerpted from the memoirs of Bruce Shanahan) Yet more regurgitated bullsh*t. How many f*cking times must I be subjected to Aymin’s pitiful sob stories about his missing testicle? They’re not much different from his in-ring work: monotonous and empty. Maybe he needs a hobby. Stamp collecting? Maybe buying a book on magic and mystifying captivated audiences with his wondrous illusions. Well, scratch that. He already does well enough in that area showcasing his waste of a career. And regretfully, has garnered a fairly strong following in light of it. Blow up one weekly program set and instantaneously get promoted to messiah. So they want destruction? Some real demolition to accompany their bloodlust? Ask and ye shall receive. Oh, I have plans in the all-too-near future for Aymin, the cancerous tumor festering in the general populace of PCW; his purpose minimal and achievements negligible, yet all overshadowed by a celebrated presence unwarranted. Already my brain floods with an excess of sinister and wholly wicked schemes to finally rid myself of that incarnated disease of a man. Though I suppose much of the plans now seeping into my thoughts are forged by the insidious essence that has taken hold of my body…. Of course that’s not to say my focus is not identical in scope on the key component in the match; hell, if not for Rush Mason’s participation then it would be just another fight. Unfortunately with accepting this Pay Per View shore-side of the Wrestle Vessel, his debacle of a title reign is drawing to a close. Shame the sorry bastard doesn’t know it yet. However, I feel no sympathy for the ingrate. He confirmed his own collapse when he inked his signature on the contract only two weeks ago in the offices of PCW’s headquarters. And with his dethroning comes my coronation. I have come to believe it’s my birthright and innate destiny to hold the Extreme championship, as potentially the only truly worthy individual still standing tall in the total wretched organization. An accolade virtually disgraced by mockery-laden battles in a plethora of tasteless ‘gimmick’ matches and intermittently seized by the tarnished hands of suburbia’s most prestigious sons, it’s only fitting it goes back to the slums of Boston to regain merit. From the hands of prestige to bastard….. What better channel to prove my capabilities and ruthlessness before pursuing bigger and better things, eh? Wild Wing is no issue….his inclusion in my division is a sham anyhow, and his annihilation will be nothing but collateral damage when I sweep him up in the foray of Mason and Aymin’s impending obliteration. He’s a scourge and it almost brings a tear to my eye when I recollect that he once held the belt now in the clutches of The Rush. Kramer Wild Wing is a malignancy on the face of Primetime Championship Wrestling, even more vile and repulsive than second-rate (yes, I said second-rate) Rush Mason or that teary-eyed whiner Flaymin Aymin. It’s in the stars. If I’ve learned nothing else from that abhorrent Nyarlathotep, it is my preordained fate. In scope, I trust my purpose in life right now exceeds that of Sunday’s inevitability. Lives are at stake, and with failure comes genocide. Lord, bestow your favor upon me. Unless you’ve already appointed another who waits in the wings even as I speak, I don’t know if you have much choice right now. Shatter all limitations and waive this judgment hanging over my head. Grant me the power to defeat thy enemies, and overcome that which currently afflicts my being. Bless this life of sin.
