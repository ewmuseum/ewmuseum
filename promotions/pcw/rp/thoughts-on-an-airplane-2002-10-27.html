Thoughts On An Airplane

27 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Thoughts On An Airplane Featuring: Tim Walden Date: October 27, 2002 The match had been decided, the outcome another victory to add onto our impressive record since formation...In fact, I had gotten the victory over the Trapezoid himself, a decent measure of revenge...But it wasn't enough for me. I wanted victory, and I got it..In a mildly tainted form, but I at least felt that I could have won if it had gone on. However, I just wasn't satisfied. This was the man who had terrorized the Front Office for weeks on end, had the gall to attack a defenseless intern and get his PCW contract by force, cheating me out of the Mall Brawl and then rubbing it in our faces by managing to evade taxes with the hideously self-centered terms of the new contract..It's bad enough to attack my colleagues and use trickery and non-moves to gain your victory, but if there's one thing that I could not bear, it is the fact that this...this alleged man would have so much contempt for his own federation as to make a deliberate ploy to evade his taxes! I have to find a way to defeat him..Decisively..But I shall settle for merely breaking his spirit at this point, for I have bigger fish to proverbially fry. Turmoil...An atmosphere that I haven't wrestled in for a good while, but on this occasion circumstances make it beneficial to change my schedule, as I get my first big opportunity to reach for the World Title...All I have to do to make this opportunity pay off is to defeat three other men, one of whom is the President himself, and I have little reason to consider this beyond my reach yet, for I have yet to lose a match on my own merit thus far since my return...Once disqualified for the fact of men unrelated to me running in against my opponent, and once defeated by the treachery of the Trapezoid, but never beaten by someone I could honestly say had earned the distinction by outwrestling me. This could change, however, Tuesday, as I step into the ring against three very capable opponents, all of whom had competed in the Red October World Title qualifying matches the month before whilst I was eradicating the scourge of PURE from our ranks, and were more used to fighting men of their caliber than I have been thus far...My hopes, however, do not rest on the difficulty of beating three other men, but moreso upon the match's structure in itself, as I have the advantage of an opponent whose agenda is on the same page of my own. Really, this match would be better described as El Diablo versus Snow versus The Front Office, for while our opponents are both quite determined to win the shot for themselves, and in fact have a history of rage between themselves that will put both of them at odds from the get-go, Duke and I have unity as a pairing. We are not merely fighting just for the glamour of personal gain here, though a World Title shot is the sort of thing that can take a wrestler into the spotlight, but to bring the title back to stability and away from the madness of Clyde, to avenge the undeserved victories of the burned man last month, and to bring the Front Office into possession of the remaining power once again, thus further increasing our dominant force in PCW..The only real worry is that of the interlopers in our recent battles. The Trapezoid may take this as a perfect opportunity to continue to frustrate my efforts and thus those of the collective group, and knowing the complexity of his plan thus far, he might just have something up his sleeve that even my allies cannot prevent..But that possibility is negligible for the time being, and I would best be concerned with more important matters and focus on the match...I cannot help but be distracted by the location at the moment. Florence, Italy, a beautiful city according to the in-flight magazine, though I wouldn't know at this point yet, still on the airplane that takes me from the Israeli stopover of the latest world tour...I wonder how far it is to Italy..The Mediterranean Sea outside my window looks nearly endless, a pool of blue that stretches to the horizon...It lets my mind float gently away from my worries for a moment, thinking less as a wrestler than just a tourist, ignoring my plights in favour of the joys of the simple life...I could stare across the water for almost an hour, pondering over the events of the trip to come.... 'Would you like a drink, sir?' The flight attendant walks down the aisle with a cart of beverages in tow..First-class is still the only way to travel, as executive transportation goes..Well, that or a private jet, but Lockheed's been on a shortage lately. I think I'd be best off taking something non-alcoholic, so I can keep my head straight after I've landed, but it's gotta quench my thirst..'I'll have an iced tea.' 'Heeeere you go!' Well, can't argue with that..The sweet refreshment is a great relief to my parched lips, and I rest it to the side..Maybe it's time to read up. I take out the copy of Pro Wrestling Weekly in my carry-on, and lay it onto my lap, thumbing through in preliminary fashion..Heard about it, don't care, jobbers...Gah! 'Trapezoid HITs the Front Office'?! ...This is a totally biased article! 'The Trapezoid's skillful attacks culminated in a brilliant revelation of his identity to Front Office member Tim Walden, before showcasing his incredible technical skills in a dominant performance to eliminate him from the Black Friday Mall Brawl, before being ambushed by Xavier Kannon in a sneak attack soon thereafter.' Dominant? Dominant is beating up two people at once, not getting pummeled and fluking out a roll-up! Since when was a schoolboy technical or dominant?! And it doesn't take skill to use hunks of wood wrapped in barbed wire to beat people up! This is obviously written by someone who lets his fanhood get in the way of being impartial, a regular Dave Kern of sports columns...Memo to self: Anti-Front Office Commentary Tax for Kern..I have enough aggravation as is, I should give this guy a piece of my mind....Where's that official Front Office stationery now? From the desk of Tim WaldenPCW Financial Reclamation Executive Dear Editors, It has come to my attention that in your October 20th issue, your contributor Jay Roberts's article 'Trapezoid HITs the Front Office' contains several factual errors which throw the accuracy and impartiality of his reporting into question. Firstly, I find his analysis of the build-up to his debut lacking from a standpoint of truth, as his two attacks were both quite cowardly. He invaded an office, then attacked a referee, as well as a defenseless intern and brutally pummeled a woman, causing severe blood loss. Your message in publishing this is that these attacks are justified by what you consider poor behaviour on our part, when in reality the attacks against mostly-innocent bystanders as opposed to, for instance, the actual wrestlers involved in the Front Office are unjustifiable as anything more than sadistic behaviour on the Trapezoid's part. The article then goes on to declare his Mall Brawl performance 'dominant', where in reality his pinfall over myself was a fluke in the midst of a deserved thrashing. I sincerely hope that you keep your facts straight in future issues, or I may cancel my subscription. And if the Trapezoid is reading this, I'd like to point out that though he may have beaten me once, what you neglected to point out is that he also lost to me at least thrice at this point in one place or another. Sincerely, Tim Walden Sheesh..Still no sign of land...How long can this damn plane take? Bah, I might as well finish my drink, then take a nap..Oughta get some sleep now to be in top condition for Turmoil, Trapezoid or no Trapezoid... ...Too bad that it means that sinking back from living the dream, to dreaming it once again. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
