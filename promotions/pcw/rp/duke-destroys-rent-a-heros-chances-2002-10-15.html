Duke destroys Rent-A-Heros chances.

15 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Duke destroys Rent-A-Heros chances. Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 10/15/02 Date: October 15, 2002Time: 11:03am CSTPlace: Evil GOTH lair of the Malice Man Duke Williams. -Dukestar The Goblin sits in his very Goth looking leather chair. He is wearing all black and his face is painted white with black mascara around his eyes. His fingernails are polished black and he strokes an evil GOTH black cat sitting in his EVIL lap. He is watching his Goth produced television set that he bought from the kingdom of corruption know as Wal-Mart. On the screen is the latest incarnation of a Rent-A-Hero rambling.- Dukestar: Reflection seems to be an appropriate behavior for a mortal like yourself. Turning back the grains of time to a place much warmer then your current state. I wish I could turn back the clocks to the time when I was a normal mortal instead of a burnt freak Goth God. -Duke holds up his pinky finger which has the tiny burn he got from cooking a pizza the other night.- Dukestar: Look at me I’m hideous. That is why I walk on the dark side. I tell you Rent-A-Mortal its not easy being the MOST GOTH person in the WOLRD!!! -Duke lets out an evil Goth laugh.- Dukestar: AS you sit there in reflection remember your first match in PCW. Reminisce your first loss in PCW. Recollect who beat you? It was an earlier version of me. I beat you when I was a mortal and a norm. Now I am a God. Do you really think you can handle a God? Mr. Hero don’t be foolish. You need not worry about the man who put you in the match with me for he is not the issue. You should not sit their and talk about defending the continental title at Mall Brawl for their will be no defense for you. -Duke waves his spooky Goth pointer finger.- Dukestar: One day at a time Mr. Hero. First you must get through me. You couldn’t beat me as a mortal now your think you can beat me as a God? Not a chance my suburban Brady bunch friend. A can swear on the soul of Marilyn Mansion that will not happen. -Duke strokes the EVIL back of his kitty cat.- Dukestar: The fact is nobody can foil my EVIL plans to concur the Continental title then the WOLRD!!!! -There is a dramatic pause for effect then Duke hisses into the camera. The cat stares at him like he’s crazy.- Dukestar: Face it my comic loving rodent foe you can’t stop my Malice Beam. -Duke pushes down on his fish net glove on his hand and a beam of light shoots out of his hand. It looks like he has a flashlight tied to his wrist.- Dukestar: I must turn off the Malice beam before I accidentally destroy the world. If I did that I would have nothing to rule now would I. No I must keep the world intact so that I have slaves and workers to clean all my Gold GOTH belts. -Duke lets out another evil GOTH God laugh.- Dukestar: I was on a talk show the other morning and they asked me what its like being so Evil and I told them that’s an easy answer. It’s like being really good and going to church and stuff….only the opposite. -Duke glares into the camera as the kitty lets out a loud meow.- Dukestar: In a few short days you can cut another one of them their promo things where you can reflect on how you lost the Continental title too this good looking burnt Goth God freak. You can talk about how you should have worried about me and not the Mall Brawl. Such a pity really. You see after I beat you I will have to defend my EVIL Goth continental title in the Mall Brawl. Which is next to perfect since Malls harbor most Goth fans. God knows all them freaks are behind the God of the Freaks….DUKESTAR THE GOBLIN!! -Duke lets out another hardy evil laughs as the lair door opens and in walks Duke’s mortal mouth piece Roseanne Bar.- Roseanne: I got us a case of beer. Dukestar: I don’t drink your mortal flavors of bliss. -Roseanne looks at the camera rolling film.- Roseanne: Oh right, I didn’t realize we were recording. Dukestar: IS the on air light on? Roseanne: I guess. Dukestar: Then what does that mean? Roseanne: Your recording an evil promo. Dukestar: Yes so lets try that again shall we? -Roseanne exits through the door then reenters, Duke acts surprised.- Roseanne: Oh great Goth God I have arrived with the goats and priest blood. Dukestar: Good work my mortal servant is it the light kind? The regular blood leaves me feeling heavy? Roseanne: Yes master. Dukestar: Evilly excellent! -Duke lets out a hardy laugh as Roseanne sits down on the Goth coach.- Roseanne: What are you doing? Dukestar: Telling the mortals why I will rule the PCW. Roseanne: You can’t do that with a cat in you lap. Dukestar: Yes I can. Roseanne: No you can’t. Dukestar: I saw some Evil genius do it on inspector gadget. You know the cartoon. Roseanne: They do a lot on cartoons that isn’t true. Look here. -Roseanne picks up the “Idiot’s guide To Goth God Behavior” which is sitting on the EVIL coffee table. She turns to the edict chapter and reads out loud.- Roseanne: Thus shall not be seen with any form of feline unless it’s in the coarse of eating it or intercourse. Dukestar: Intercourse? Roseanne: It’s a Goth thing. -Duke stares down at the kitty who looks up at Duke like he knows what was said. The kitty lets out a loud “meow” and runs off.- Dukestar: Memo to self no more pussy on camera. Roseanne: So did you unveil the chance debilitateor yet? Dukestar: No my mortal servant but that’s a great idea. -Duke and Roseanne walk over to a creepy looking boz. Well really it looks like a microwave spray painted black with cotton spider webs placed around it.- Dukestar: This box was built by the souls of sweatshop slaves. It is made with the Evilest parts in the world from teachers nipples to the metal from concentration camps. It was made by GOTH MAGIC AND EVIL!!!!! -Duke quickly covers up the Sears logo with some spider web.- Roseanne: Tell them what it does!! Dukestar: I can use this EVIL GOTH machine to eliminate the chances of you beating me Mr. Rent-A-Hero. Lets get started. -Roseanne takes a cut out of Rent-A-Hero’s image from PCW’s magazine. She places it in the chance debilitateor and sets the clock at twenty five.- Dukestar: Unless you can stop the count down Rent-A-Splooge your chance will be eliminated. So if I were you I would run out of the Rent-A-Center and hop into your car from Budget rent-a-car and head to my EVIL Goth Lair. -Duke hits start as the machine starts working counting down backwards.- Dukestar: Are you going to try to stop me? No? -Duke and Roseanne stare at the entrance waiting for Rent-A-Hero to try and stop them.- Dukestar: Funny They usually bust through about now on the TV shows. Roseanne: That’s because heroes aren’t real! Dukestar: Ahh but Goth Gods are!! 10 Dukestar: Is anybody going to stop me. 9 Dukestar: Evil is unstoppable in the hands of Malice. 8 Dukestar: First the continental title. 7 Dukestar: Then the World!!!! 6 Dukestar: Five seconds Rent-A-Moron. 5 Dukestar: Looks like victory is mine!!! 4 Dukestar: Is that a burrito in there? 3 Dukestar: ….. 2 Dukestar: His chances are gone 1 Dukestar: You might as well just crown me the gold hoss. -The microwave/chance debilitateor blinks end as Duke lets out an evil laugh and Roseanne pulls a cooked burrito out of the machine.- Dukestar: That is a burrito isn’t it?? My evil powers not only eliminated his chances but also turned his photo into a burrito!! Roseanne: Your powers are truly amazing. Dukestar: And I will show them off first against Rent-A-Hero then again at Mall Brawl when I send the roster Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound…….GOTH Front Office Malice EVIL GOD STYLE!!!!! -The camera fades on Duke laughing.- =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
