::~Eternals Eleven~:: (Part II)

28 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/28/03 Prelude: Last week Shane put the first step into forming his security team at his infamous Rock Festival in June, known as the E-Fest. Shane has been recruiting eleven members to a security team known as ‘Eternals Eleven’. These eleven men or women will play a huge part in keeping things in order at the event, which will have tickets on sale this Friday. Wethers has a lot of things on his mind with the stakes in the rising star division getting even more intense, but with the help of a loyal agent and long time friend, Mikey Fishmore is going to help Shane find the Eleven men.Today we meet the first recruit to see if he really is the real deal, or just another fish in the pond of failure. In a lifetime known as reality lays a rough capital city with enough smoke fumes to choke a dragon and enough noise of car engines to make a monk go crazy. But this is the gentle place we call home, after all this is London, England. The classy yet stupid people drive around in fast cars, complaining about how the counter-flow system introduced by the government, has caused more problems that its worth, whilst the smarter people pay the small fee to take advantage of the London Underground, otherwise known as the ‘tube’. A dark blurry shadow covers a small patch of a stonewall. A quiet, calm and yet cocky voice begins to speak “Now call me stupid but I have fond memories of growing up here with my family, not exactly as fun as it may sound, especially when your family was the most wanted family in the south. It’s just lucid atmosphere and the outstanding stench of body odour from people rushing around like headless chickens that stand out in my memory… along with that, my goals and ambitions as a child.” The rumbling rail noise in the distance can be heard as footsteps approach, followed by a mass of voices from the travelling people within the city. Diseased rodents cover vast areas of the rail joinery, trailing across dirty rotting litter. “I won’t spend anymore time taking you through my life story, not yet anyway. In fact, a lot of your fragile little minds may not be able to handle it and before you know it you’ll be overgrown with jealousy about how a normal everyday guy like myself could ever become one of the most profound musicians in world today. I recently read on the PCW newswire, a little article about my rumoured entrance, seems the paparazzi were sticking there noses where it didn’t belong, but I’ll deal with them later. But more to the point, I took clear notice of the part, which questioned my success of a multi-platinum album. The stench of envy is brewing from the very doors of PCW, why can’t people recognise a hard-working man like myself? I’ll tell you why, it’s the f^cking media and the press! Bunch of nobodies, writing disrespectful articles about people they wish they were, no wonder they seem to love Britney Spears, the little bitch is probably complimenting those low-lives with sexual favours, hence the song ‘hit me baby one more time’, I’d love to hit the bitch.” The rattling and rumbling of the railway increases with a loud piercing screech and the train approaches at a high speed, causing a deafening roar for a moment or two. “After being the front man of a band with growing success at its feet, I chose this time to join the PCW. Now I know what’s going to happen, I’ve seen it many times before and it still happens a lot now. I’ll walk into PCW with all my glory and there will be the usual amount of sorry pickledicks that will need to throw insults at me like they’ve got nothing better to do. Well just so you are all aware I am ready for it, Cos it’ll will take more than your lame half-wit comments to pull this train off of it’s tracks. There’s rising stars… and then there’s the risen stars, I’m the risen star off the music world and I once was a risen star in the world of professional wrestling, but now I find myself at the bottom of the ladder scratchin’ my balls and wondering what the hell just happened. I’m now ready to climb once again and I will get there eventually, I don’t care who I beat or who I don’t beat… it all comes down to one thing, ‘who is the better man’ and I always am. The big time wannabes can come help me pick the sweet corn out of this. Excuse my harsh words of reality, It’s hard to be humble when you’re so damn big.” “A large majority of pro-wrestlers enter the wrestling league in search of a few matches with a small chance of stardom in Hollywood, I mean look at the Scorpion King, what the hell is with Dwayne Johnson, does he have a death warrant with his name on it? I already have it all; I don’t need wrestling as a launch pad for fame and riches. I’ve been successful all my life; it’s been a distinct feature in the family genes.Now its time to put those genes to use once again and own The PCW just like I own England. No one complains about my leadership, after all everyone appreciates the Eternal Punk. I’m the one who put the ‘Brit’ in celebrity.” People board the train carriage in a rush, as if it was a bank in the middle of a clear out sale. The unsettled expressions of people on the train make you wonder if it is really worth it. The voice echoes off the speakers… Please stand clear of the doors…Please stand clear of the doors As the train moves away, a figure comes into the light and Shane Wethers is stood with the local paper in on hand, screwed up as if he’s getting ready to beat someone with it. “Alright Guv!” Shane turns to see Mikey stood behind him with a guy who stands at approximately six-foot-six. Shane, whom stands at six-foot-three, has to look upwards to face the guy. He is wearing a leather jacket and dirty blue jeans, his beer belly hangs out over his jeans and almost touches his knees. “Shane, I’d like you to meet ‘Ritchie Burrows’, known to many as the ‘screamer’” Mikey announces in a straight forward introduction. “Guhhhhh…G’day boss” mumbles Burrows like a shy child. “Burrows is educated with a gun, he’ll go well as your security at the Eternal-Fest” Mikey declares. “What’s your shot accuracy level?” Shane quizzes whilst running his hand along the bristles of his chin. “97.9” replies the bizarre hit man “not bad” Shane says with a grin. He turns to Mikey “So what’s the…” Shane goes to ask… “Ah..anal prodding rhino stuffer!” screams Burrows like a strange child. Shane turns and looks at Burrows with a surprised yet confused expression. “That’s what I was about to tell you,” Mikey adds, “he’s got..er…turrets syndrome” . The big guy hunches over in embarrassment as Shane turns to face Mikey with a blank expression on his face, as if he’s tryin’ to believe what has just happened. “Ahhhhhhh Shit licker!…. um sorry” Burrows yells, still going red with embarrassment. Shane looks over his shoulder before looking back at Mikey “Cool” Shane declares as a grin appears on Mikeys face. “Really?” Mikey asks with surprise. “Yeah” replies Shane, “This parties gonna rock, and it’s about time we had a security guard with an attitude” Shane smirks and Shakes the big hand of Burrows “Welcome aboard kiddo” Shane welcomes Burrows, “Thhhh….Bollocks!…aaank you” Burrows yells in Shane’s face, Burrows then takes out a handkerchief from his back pocket, going to wipe Shane’s where he spat all over him. Shane resists. “Its alright mate, I’ll get Mikey to contact you later about the time you need to be at Eternal Fest” Shane informs. “See ya later” Burrows says farewell with a respectful nod of the head before turning to walk away. Shane turns to Mikey, walking straight passed him. “I don’t know how you do it… but you got away with this” Shane says as he continues walking “now come on… one down, ten to go!”
