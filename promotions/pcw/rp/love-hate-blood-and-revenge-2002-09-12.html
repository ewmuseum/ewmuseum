Love Hate Blood and Revenge

12 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-12-02 RP 1 for Turmoil 17 Jack Slayter, you’re fired. Teresa says these words smoothly as if they had no meaning. She reaches for her coffee and sits back in her booth seat. Her and Jack are at a McDonald’s in Wisconsin making an attempt to enjoy a hot breakfast. Teresa has an Egg McMuffin while the hungry Jack consumes a complete breakfast platter. As soon as Teresa’s four words register in his brain, he drops his plastic fork in his sausage gravy. Fired? Fired! Couldn’t you have waited till after breakfast? Now I’ve lost my appetite for my biscuits. Teresa shrugs while taking a bite of her Egg McMuffin. She shows no pity or remorse for Jack. He has been part of her wrestling career since the beginning in one way or the other. It was always either him or Scott at her side. Who’s next? I’m sure you’ll manage, but you will not be managing me. Jack picks up his black Stetson and places it on his head. He hasn’t taken his eyes off Teresa the whole time since she said ‘You’re fired.’ You need me Teresa. Who’s gonna keep you company at night? Who’s gonna get drunk with you after the shows? Huh? I’m all you have left Teresa Tomas. You told me that. I know what I said Ace. At the time I meant every word. I actually thought you had changed, but as of late, I’ve been reminded how much of an ass you are. I’m tired of it and I’m tired of you. By now Jack has had enough of Teresa’s little games. Without a word, he grabs Teresa by the arm and pulls her out of her sat. Once she is on her feet, Jack picks her up and slings her over his shoulder like a sack of potatoes. The McDonald’s employees all stop and watch as Jack carries Teresa to the men’s restroom. At the same time, our PCW Diva is cursing her usual profanity and calling Jack every name she can think of. There is no one in the restroom besides our favorite uncouple. Jack slides Teresa off his shoulder and pins her to the wall with a huge handed chokehold. Listen Bitch. No one has loved you more than I have. NO ONE! I’ve been there for you this whole time. I even drove the Teaser for you. This is what I get in return? SMACK Jack gives Teresa a hard slap to the face. The force of the slap forces her head violently to the side. Teresa’s hands grab his large arm trying to pry his hand off her neck. I don’t think so Teresa. You think you have a temper? Just let me show you what a temper is. Since you like your men rough, you may enjoy this. Jack smacks Teresa’s other cheek and drops her to the floor. She falls to her knees gasping for breath. Jack then pulls his leather belt off his waist and grabs Teresa by the hair. He wraps her wrists with the belts and ties it tightly. Teresa manages to rise to her feet, but Jack gives her a punch to the gut and grabs a hand full of her hair once more. Teresa groans in pain and Jack forces her head into one of the ceramic sinks. After two hard blows to the head, Teresa is out cold. This is for beating the hell out of me in Canada. Is revenge as sweet to you as is for me? The unconscious Teresa obviously says nothing. Jack laughs to himself and lays Teresa on her back. Her wrists are still tied with the leather belt. Jack gets on his knees beside her and rips open her shirt revealing a white lace bra. The bra fastens in the front so it doesn’t take much effort to pull the bra apart also exposing her now bare chest. With her own blood from her busted open head, Jack writes the words ‘Fuck Me’ across her breasts with is finger. He then stands and stares at her for a moment viewing his handiwork. Whistling to himself, he washes his hands and steps out of the restroom, out the door and into Teresa’s midnight purple step side Chevrolet pick up truck back to Texas.
