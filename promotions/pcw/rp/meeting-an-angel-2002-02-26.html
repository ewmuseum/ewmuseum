Meeting An Angel

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/2/02 Alone. All alone. Nowhere to go. Nothing to do. Alone. All alone. Ben J. Pailthorpe has spent 6 years of his adult life in the United States of America away from his friends and family. All his old friends he has left behind in England, all his newer friends he left behind in GWF, now he is in PCW with John Crichton and Andy Murray but in a way he is still alone. Andy Murray is a Satanist, this immediately drives a wedge between him and Ben with Ben of course being a Christian. Andy is also Scottish so there is the old England/Scotland divide coming into things, yet suprisingly they are together still. John Crichton is a pretty secretive guy, Ben doesnt know much of him, he knows a few things about his past but nothing more then that. It could be argued that the Forgotten Ones are not the strongest of groups because of the difference between the members. All they have in common is that for a long time they were over looked by the ranks of PCW, untill now. Ben J. Pailthorpe has been more involved then anyone out of the Forgotten Ones, on Fusion he was involved in a match for the number one contendership to the Television title, he lost in the end due to interference from Television Champion Akiyama. Also on Fusion he was brutally attacked at the hands of Charlie Loc which resulted in him having to recieve medical attention. On Turmoil Ben goes up against Mike Frampton and Charlie Loc. That is of course is Charlie Loc makes it after he was attacked on Sabotage. The scene opens on a crowded street, everything in the street is black and white, the cars, the shops the people all except one piece of colour, slowly making its way up the street. The colour starts taking a shape, a black jumper, blue jeans, long black and red hair, the colour is Ben J. Pailthorpe. He walks slowly his head bowed his feet stumbled along the sidewalk. He lifts his head up and flicks the hair out of his eyes, the camera zooms in on his pale green eyes which are wide and watery. The young Englishman bows is head again and continues walking through the sea of black and white. Narrator: 'This is the symbolism of Ben's loneliness, as he sees it he is the only colour in a watercolor of grey. His thoughts, his flaws are all exposed by the prying eyes and coldness around him and all he wants is a little love, some compassion, a friend who is like him.' Morning dew in your eyesTears on the flowersSay it straight say it trueFor we all feel the same Ben runs his hands through his long black hair and he looks up to the sky for a moment asking the question, why? He's been unhappy since he stopped drinking, but the drink was killing him, he had more friends when he was drinking, but the drink was killing him. Memories of parties in the GWF, the hospitality of the Youngblood Club and the free tab provided by good friend Johnny Cruse. Take my handTake my handTake my handTake my handFor we all need someone to live forWe all need someone Ben slowly approaches a small chapel, outside a large poster saying 'It Is Holy Week' hangs on the wall. Above the door is the famous scene of the crucifixion, with Jesus nailed to a cross. Ben looks up at the cross and stops dead in his tracks. Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Your my only friend...' He sits heavily on a nearby bench and sighs loudly, the look on his face says it all, despair. IF the human heart is nothing but muscle why does it long to be loved so painfully? Dont say he doesnt answer prayer... A young lady, about 22 or 23, emerges from the chapel carrying a bouqet of flowers, shes has an aura about her of happiness and joy, its almost as if she glows. She is about 5'4 and she has long golden brown hair, she looks over at the sullen figure of Pailthorpe and looks at him with concern, Ben looks up feeling a presence and she smiles at him warmly, he forces a small smile back and she walks airily over to him and sits down next to him. Girl: 'Hi!' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Hello.' Girl: 'What brings you here?' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Just thinking, y'know sorting out a few things.' Girl: 'Its a good place to do it.' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Yeah.' There is an awkward silence. Girl: 'So whats your name' She says this with a smile that could melt butter. Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Im Ben, you are?' Girl: 'Stefanie, but my friends call me Stef.' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Mind if I call you Stef?' Stef: 'Not at all.' Ben smiles for the first time in a while. Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Do you work here?' He says this looking up at the chapel. Stef: 'I help out alot.' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Cool.' Stef: 'What do you do?' Ben hesitates. 'Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Im a wrestler.' Stef: 'Wow, cool.' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Yeah it is. Pretty lonely business though.' Stef: 'Dont women like throw themselves at you?' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Nope not at all.' Stef: 'Wow.' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'They go for some of the guys, the world champs etc but not guys like me, overlooked you could say.' Stef: 'So whats it like?' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'What?' Stef: 'Being a wrestler.' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Its fun, incredibly panful, pretty well paid though.' Stef: 'Cool.' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Yeah.' Stef: 'So where are you from?' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'London.' Stef: 'I knew it, I knew that was a London accent.' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'And yourself?' Stef: 'I was born in Newcastle but I moved to Australia when I was young and then to here.' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Oh cool, you havent got a Geordie accent.' Stef: 'I know, I was so young when I moved that I picked up a kind of Australian/American mix.' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Cool, I would have guessed that you were American.' Stef: 'And you would have been wrong.' She laughs and smiles and her aura of happiness seems to surround Ben's cold emptiness. Stef: 'So...you look unhappy sitting here.' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'I am, kinda...' Stef: 'Why? Its good to talk about your problems.' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Im kinda lonely, I dont feel that I have any real friends, no family over here...' Stef: 'What did you used to do when you felt lonely?' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'I used to turn to the bottle, but I gave that up and now ive turned to Jesus, but I still feel lonely.' Stef: 'I know what you mean, I have alot of friends in Australia who I miss, and they are on the opposite side of the world.' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'How do you cope?' Stef: 'I get along, I have good friends over here, I have this place.' She says smiling as she looks up at the old chapel. Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Nice old place.' Stef: 'Yeah, its really nice being on this street, surrounded by all the hustle and bustle, just a little bit of serenity.' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Its nice to get away from it all, America is such a busy place, its like no one rests.' Stef: 'True, you should come along here more often.' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'I think I will.' Stef: 'Cool.' She smiles and takes a small white rose from the bouquet, she puts it in Ben's hand. Stef: 'You are never alone, remember that...' She smiles at him again and gets up and walks back up towards the chapel. Stef: 'Will you be along on sunday?' Ben J. Pailthorpe: 'Yes...um, I mean ok sure.' Stef: 'See you then.' She disapears inside the chapel and Ben looks at the little white rose, a guardian angel had visited him, in the disguise of an angel...
