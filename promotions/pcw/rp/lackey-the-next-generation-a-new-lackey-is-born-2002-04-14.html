Lackey : The Next Generation - A New Lackey Is Born

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/04/02 Ellie~!’s Prologue : Like....Wow! I never thought Xavi had SO much power! At Fusion he was all, like, I want to book my own show and Duke and Dave were all He can’t! but Jeff and me were like, He..Um..SO can....Then he did! He is like, a really cool Superhero with the megapower of making matches! If he has that much power, imagine what power I have as Commissioneress! Although I don’t want to make matches or anything, I’ll see if I can trade it in for flying or something...Anyways, where was I...Oh yeah, Xavi did NOT run away from his match at Fusion...He was...Um....Helping rescue a kitten from a tree in the crowd...Yeah, and you’ll see on Sunday and Tuesday that he isn’t scared of anything...Apart from when retards talk to you and you don’t know where to look...But I don’t think Frisco is a retard.....Who’s Frisco?...... “What do you mean you are sending me someone?.......So what if he wants into PCW, so does everybody, why do I have to jobber-sit?..... The shot opens up in the grand and lavishly decorated hall of Xavier Kannon’s Beverly Hills residence. The Commissioner is pacing around talking on the speaker phone to PCW’s Personal Director Tim Walden, Kannon not looking too happy at being involved in the conversation. “Look, I am the fucking PERSONNEL MOTHER FUCKING DIRECTOR and I told that little shit to get his bastard ass over to your place to be your fucking bitch and beg fore a tryout...” The Scientologist is looking more casually dressed than in recent days, enjoying the day of rest at home after a busy week asserting the Ultimate Authority’s order over PCW. The Continental Champion is wearing a Black hooded sweatshirt with “XK” written across the front in blazing Purple and Green. Along with this he wears a pair of Black jogging bottoms and house socks, looking far from the corporate figure he has become. Xavier : “But...TECHNICALLY....This is a hiring, so it is your department. Say what you want about the Christians, you know...Catholic Priests and 13 year old boys and all....But whoever decided that Sundays off thing was a genius. I am totally refreshed for my match tonight...” Kannon continues to pace around his hall, over the large rug with an image of L Ron Hubbard woven into it to greet visitors. He rolls his Green eyes up as Tim shouts back. “I fucking said the kid wanted a fucking tryout match. Now forgive me if I have shit for brains but I thought making the fucking matches was located somewhere around your fucking side of the mother fucking court?” He realises that Walden has him and that his attempts to palm off the PCW prospect back onto Tim have not worked and he will now have to spend the day with some rookie riding his coat tails. He sighs at the realisation of it all and concedes to the Personnel Director. Xavier : “Okay...I suppose my pool could do with being cleaned....When is IT getting here?” ......DING.........DONG...... The doorbell is heard and Xavier turns around to see the form of a well built man showing through the glass. He drops his head and shakes it, bracing himself for the day afternoon ahead. “Guess who the shit headed bastard at the mother fucking door is....” Xavier is now faced with the prospect of spending time with one of the single most annoying forms of animal on the planet....Only just behind the Mosquito and the Farmer...The prospective PCW wrestler. Xavier: “Okay....I’m coming...Ring that bell again and I’ll release the hounds...” He walks over to the door as unenthusiastically as possible, dragging his heels in protest at the interruption of his relaxing day and he flings the door open to show none other than NTICW Extreme Champion...Jordan X Shabazz....Not that the Scientologist knows this. Jordan : “Xavier Kannon! Wow! I didn’t think I would ever be able to say I stood this close to you...I mean, I am only in NTICW because you were the Champion there...My whole career is based on trying to emulate the supreme talent that you have!” The unadulterated praise that is being lavished upon Xavier wipes a little of the resentment off his face, but the mask of confusion is still adorned as he looks the man up and down. Jordan is about 3 inches shorter than Xavier and a lot more bulky. He is dressed to impress by wearing an Ultimate Authority t-shirt, paired with a simple pair of Blue Nike pants. He stands out on the doorstep, not even risking an insult to Xavier by stepping into his home. Xavier : “Um....Who in Hubbard’s name are you?” Shabazz doesn’t look deterred by his idol’s lack of knowledge and interest in him and carries on, determined to sell himself to the Commissioner in hope of a match. ......Okay, so there is like this wrestler guy...That or he is just fat....Wants to be in that Fed Xavier owns and I’m Commissioneress of. It must have been watching Fusion that did it...Especially when I cancelled that other Krazie guy...Not the one Xavier used to hit alot, but another guy and the one with no face....See, with me as Commissioneress applications are, like, WAY up....... Jordan : “I’m Jordan X Shabazz, I watch PCW every single show...And man, you, Nelson, Ellie~!, Politically Incorrect and Tim Walden....What you did at Spring Fling was great...You beat those other guys up and then took over....And on Turmoil where you sorted out who was who and even on Fusion when you booked the best card in PCW history....” As the rookie reels off a list of superlatives at the cocky Continental Champion, Xavi’s hostility seems to be slipping and the thought of having a walking ego boost follow him around appeals to his arrogant side. Jordan : “......And I would do anything......I mean anything to be in PCW! I’d get down on my knees and beg, I’ll lick your shoes, I’ll give foot rubs, massages, go collect take out, park your car, carry your bags....” Jordan’s pleading to get in Xavier’s good graces continues until the rather excited voice of PCW’s Commissioneress, Xavier’s preppy valet Eleanor is head from the stairs behind him. “Wow......We, like, SO have to let him do all that stuff!!!” Relishing the prospect of having what basically equates to a slave, Eleanor hurried descends the stairs and looks the man up and down. Jordan’s eyes grow at being able to see in person the site that is Ellie~!. ......This like, rules! Most servants always want paying, and minimum wage and decent working hours...Living conditions and awkward things like that. Maybe we should have thought about getting a pet jobber earlier..... The prep is wearing her Pink skinny fit t-shirt with Commissioneress written across her 32B chest in Silver glitter. With this she wears a pair of Blue sandblasted jeans with sequinned stars running down the legs. With these jeans there is a rainbow patterned fabric belt and around her wrists are numerous bands and bracelets. Her long, wavy Brown hair, subtly highlighted with Blond is loose and falls down her back and over her shoulders. Jordan : “Eleanor Hall? Ellie~!? Oh my Go-...Sorry...Hubbard....I can’t believe that is you, you are, like...SO the best valet in PCW history!” While Xavier was mildly appreciative of the praise levelled at him by Jordan, Ellie~! is relishing in it. Eleanor : “I....Like....SO know! Yay me!....Can we keep him? Pleeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeease?” With her whiny pleading the Gold Patron Meritorious immediately melts as his Commissioneress pulls out the puppy eyes, giving him a full shot with her pale Blue eyes and long eyelashes. Xavier : “Okay my little Poon Puppy....I’ll give him this week to show he is worthy of a tryout match. After the insults PCW signed under the William’s era....I mean, Avalanche? Damion Xaviers?....I need to know that Jordan here will want to prove himself in PCW...” Despite Xavier’s rather harsh decision Jordan seems ecstatic as Xavier steps aside and gestures for Shabazz to enter his grandiose home. As soon as Jordan steps through the door he sees the image of L Ron Hubbard woven into the rug and launches into another of his praise barrages to show how worthy he is of a PCW berth. Jordan : “Wow...L Ron Hubbard right? Maybe I should become a Scientologist too, I mean, if you are one then it has to be the single greatest religion on the planet...Wait, what do I mean must be...IT IS the greatest belief system ever invent-..Um...proven!” The Scientologist turns to his valet, almost as if he is warming to having the PCW hopeful with him...But not warming enough as to pass across the opportunity for exploitation. Xavier : “Right...Jordan. Are you ready for your first task, if you complete them all then you will have proven yourself worthy of a chance to be in PCW. Fail it, and then maybe your heart just isn’t in it enough...” .......Now what can he do?....Maybe I can get him to hit Duke Williams so he’ll be nice to me...Wait, Xavi will do that....Um....e could rob a back for us! Hmmm, no, I think we may get in trouble for that...Wait...I have it! Again ignoring the downside of Kannon’s words Jordan nods along eagerly as Xavier runs through in his head what he can suggest the young wrestler too. Xavier : “Okay, I think I have it.....You ha-” Just as the Commissioner is about to reveal the much anticipated first task, Ellie~! decides that she wants the first labour to benefit her and jumps right in. Eleanor : “I want you to go and find me ONE can of Tab Clear....” As she excitedly stands there having laid out her demand, both Xavier and Jordan look confused. Kannon’s confusion is mixed with mild annoyance at Ellie~! ruining the fun he was about to have by getting Jordan to perform an outlandish or unfeasible task while Jordan’s confusion looks to be combined with willingness for enlightenment on the subject. Eleanor: “...You know, like, Tab Clear? It was a Cola, but it was SO not Black, it was clear...Hence the Clear, and the Tab was for....Um.....The tabs?” While Xavier is still in the dark about the soda matter, his brow furrowing with mild agitation at Ellie~!’s shunning of Snapple~! in favour of the failed drinks venture of the early 90’s. Jordan : “Oh...I remember that stuff....I haven’t seen that for years! Is there a shop in town that sells it? If there is I’m there right now buying some for you Miss Hall!” Ellie~! looks to him with her innocent expression she carries around in times like these. Eleanor : “Bulgaria......They have it in Bulgaria.....” Finally, despite the unbridled enthusiasm shown by the young wrestler in search of his dream, Ellie~! has managed to knock some of the steam out of him in a way only she can. Eleanor : “....It was on the Internet, I was, like, I SO haven’t had Tab Clear for ages so I typed it in and it like the national drink of Bulgaria...You know, like Snapple~! is to PCW...” Shabazz steadies him self for the task ahead and nods assuredly. Jordan : “Okay Miss Hall, my passport is back at my motel room so I’ll run back there and catch the first flight to Bulgaria I can...How much do you want me to bring back? I can leave my stuff over there so I don’t exceed my baggage weight limit...” She things about it, pursing her lips before shaking her head. Eleanor : “Hmmm....No, just one can...” Jordan : “Okay....I’ll be back on the first flight I can get, I’ll try and buy a cool box at the airport so it will be nice and chilled for you when I get back...Better still, I’ll buy a bag of ice on my way here...Then it’ll really be cold...Anyways, I’d better be getting off...Thanks for this chance Commissioner Kannon sir, and you Miss Hall, it is an honour to travel to Europe to buy you a Soda....” He hurriedly says his goodbyes and heads out through the door, eager and ready for the task that lays ahead of him. In the hall Xavier turns to his valet, almost in disbelief. Xavier : “He’s actually doing it? I don’t believe it! We have a great week in store my little Poon Puppy, having out own lackey to order around at our beck and call.....Sucker...” Ellie~! smirks, happy in the knowledge that as in soon as 24 hours time she will have a lovely chilled glass of Tab Clear. Eleanor : “That is like So right.....Make me a Caesar Salad will you...Pleeeeeeese...” Without a seconds hesitation Xavier heads to the kitchen leaving Ellie~! standing alone in the hall as the shot fades to Black. “What a mother fucking lackey fucker that dumb shit bastard is!” Eleanor turns curiously to the phone as Walden’s spiteful taunt is heard, the Personnel Director still on the line and having listened to the whole thing and the shot hits Black as Ellie~! inquisitive preppy voice is heard one last time. “Yeah...Jordan too......”
