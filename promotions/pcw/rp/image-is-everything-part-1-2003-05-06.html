Image Is Everything: Part 1

6 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 6, 2003 The careers of two, once struggling entertainers have seemed to take a turn for the best. While John Gargano enjoys the success in the PCW as well as in movies, Tyler also wallows in great success in the comedy circuit. However, there is a thought implanted in the minds of everyone that says 'you can do better'. This causes men to strive for changes and continue pursuing perfection. The scene fades in to the interior hall of a middle class apartment complex. The crimson red carpet looks soft under the feet of John Gargano who is sporting casual attire consisting of a light blue T-shirt with a pair of jeans. He stands outside of a white door with gold letters that read ‘547’ on it. This is his own apartment room, and he stands outside of it waiting for his good friend. From around the corner of a gray colored wall walks Tyler Preston with about the same formality of clothes on. He walks at a brisk pace as if he is in a hurry to get somewhere. He makes eye contact with John and approaches him with a smile. They shake hands. Tyler looks over John’s shoulder which hints that his hurriedness may have something to do with John’s apartment. “What’s up John? I hustled over here just about as fast as I could. Now are you going to tell me what this little surprise is?” Asks Tyler with total wonder in his bright blue eyes. It’s not usual that John will play something off as a surprise, which makes Tyler really think that this could be something very beneficial. “Not so fast their Tyler. Man, you just have to know everything the minute you walk in the door,” says John with a smile on his face. He does this to buy some time because he knows that the secret is eating away at Tyler. He figures that the longer the secret is held from Tyler, the longer he holds Tyler in the palm of his hand. “You mentioned on the phone that you had something that would boost my career in your words. I just don’t really have any idea what you could have that would boost my career,” Tyler says, still in wonder. His mind shuffles through a myriad of possibilities, but can’t settle on one that he wants to serve as a prediction. He is truly clueless as to what kind of help John can lend him to help out his career. “You know me Tyler, I never go back on a promise. There is something behind this door that will change your career forever. In fact, it could almost make you a whole new person,” John says with a chuckle, knowing the inside joke on the matter but leaving Tyler in the dust of confusion. Tyler’s eyebrows furl up in wonder. Tyler reaches for the door but John moves the frame husky body in front of it so that Tyler doesn’t have access to it. “Come on John, stop playing like this. Let me just see what it is,” Tyler pleads, starting to get annoyed that John knows something that he doesn’t. Tyler doesn’t take this to well in any case and it proves no different in this one. He gives John a little shove, but John plays it off as a joke and lets out a small laugh. Tyler lets go of a small smile, but doesn’t want John to think that this is a laughing matter. “Have patience my good friend. Let me just say that I am really happy you decided to take me on and train me for the movie, and also for being a great partner in the PCW. I was just contemplating on that fact just the other night when it came to me. I need to do something so special that will thank him more than words could ever. I think that I have it, so come on in and see what I have for you,” John says, leading up to this very special moment. He turns the brass doorknob and flings the door to his apartment wide open. The sunlight that shines through the dirty windows almost blinds Tyler as he peers in to see what is waiting for him. His hopes are dashed when the only sight that reaches his eyes is John’s agent Mick Varelli parked on John’s crusty tan sofa, sorting through papers that lay over his red, leather brief case. Mick looks up from his papers just to give Tyler a quick nod. Tyler’s smile that he wore so proudly upon entering the room has erased to a straight and still confused face. Tyler doesn’t realize how this could possibly be a career changing thing. John knows that Tyler and Mick don’t always get along, and Tyler has been trying to convince John for months to drop him as his agent. “Ok, it’s Mick Varelli hanging out at your apartment. Where’s the big surprise? Is Mick sitting on it?” Asks Tyler, somewhat blaming Mick for this anti-climatic ending to what seemed was a great surprise. Mick just shakes his head and continues to get his business done. Tyler glares at him. John looks a little disappointed that Tyler was not jumping for joy. After all, John had worked hard to bring Tyler this surprise, and was hoping for a better reaction. John decides that he should explain the whole reasoning behind it, and then Tyler will see how momentous this surprise really is. “Tyler, you have no idea how busy Mick is during this time of year. I am really glad that he could find the time to come and visit us. We can have a little conference and get a few things straightened out with your career. Trust me man, this guy can put you on the map like he did with me,” John boasts, hoping that Tyler will get the idea and finally be happy. This is not the case. Tyler understands that Mick is a somewhat important man in the field of entertainment, but his dislike for the man heavily outweighs Mick’s credibility in the business. Mick looks up with a smile after John’s small rant to stroke his ego. “What are you talking about? How is your agent going fix my career? By the way, I don’t really think there is anything wrong with my comedy career. Come out to the ‘Laugh Factory’ sometime and you’ll see that things are going quite well,” Tyler asks, and also says in defense. He doesn’t want to give out the idea that he needs Mick to succeed. He is proud of his career in comedy, and takes this as a small blow to his pride. “The ‘Laugh Factory’ huh? That’s pretty good for beginners! After I get done with you, you will be headlining Carnegie Hall,” Mick finally breaks in after taking a break from his paper scanning. Mick rises to his feet and straightens out the gray dress paints to his suit. He readjusts his royal blue tie and extends a hand to Tyler. Tyler hesitantly accepts the hand with his own and they exchange a hearty handshake. “I wouldn’t exactly call my stuff beginner. I’ve been doing this for quite sometime and I think that I have a hold on some of the key concepts,” Tyler fends off. He wants to make sure that he is gaining and maintaining the respect of the two men in the room. Nothing would be worse then to let people think he is insecure about his career. “Tyler, don’t fight it. The man knows what he is doing. He’s guided a lot of the main comedian acts out on the stage these days,” John adds, still blatantly brown nosing to Mick. Mick smiles a full teeth smile and John lets out a small chuckle. John attempts to get on Mick’s good side so that it could help him out to score roles in future movies. “So I understand that you want to develop some sort of a gimmick that sets you apart from all the other comedians, is that correct?” Mick asks in attempts to make clear what his mission for today is. Tyler just looks at him blankly, but decides that perhaps he can be helpful in the least bit. Tyler humors him with a response in a flat tone of voice. “I guess I might need a little something to set me apart from the rest of the comedians. I’m not sure if I’m the type of guy that would want to hide behind a gimmick to get popular,” Tyler admits, dealing with his shortcomings but not willing to compromise his morals just to get ahead in the business. “I’m not telling you to go run and hide behind a gimmick. I’m just simply suggesting that there you should adopt a trait to your character that makes you unique,” Mick explains. Mick is trying his best to get Tyler Preston to the top, but for selfish reasons only. While Tyler thinks that Mick may have had a change in heart, Mick still is selfishly grounded in the fact that if Tyler prospers, then he may get a little bit of the money also. “Remember Mick, Tyler is going to be in the wrestling flick with me. That will gain him pretty good publicity won’t it?” Asks John, makings sure that Mick hasn’t forgotten his promise of including Tyler in the upcoming movie, ‘To the Ring and Back’. Mick nods his head recalling the moment that he made that promise. “That will definitely help. However, a single cameo in a movie isn’t going to launch him to the top,” Mick explains, conveying the idea that Tyler is a shoe-in to be in the movie. At the same time, he wants Tyler to be led on to think that he still needs him, even though he is enjoying mediocre success at the moment. “Do you have any ideas on how we can do that?” Tyler asks with wide eyes, actually becoming engrossed in this conversation. It is at this time that he realizes that Mick might have something to offer him. “I have a number of gimmicks that you can chose from. The only thing that you have to do is pick one that suits you. That way the you are not compromising your originality in the process,” Mick explains, showing that he is not attempting to get Tyler Preston to become a whole new person. Tyler respects this and replies to it with a nod of his head “Everyone needs a little sprucing up in their career, now it is your turn,” John says in encouragement to get Tyler to become putty in the hands of Mick Varelli. Tyler smiles at this comment, knowing that John set himself up for a burner. John looks at Tyler with a serious face, trying to keep the mood in check. “Yea, kind of like you have to knock off twenty pounds of else they won’t let you be in the wrestling movie?” Tyler asks as a joke. This scores a big laugh out of Mick, but John’s face turns a rosy red color. He is very insecure about his weight, and finds this as a low blow. Tyler pats him on the back to signify it was all a joke and he meant no harm by it. John perks up and accepts the joke like a man. “That is exactly what I’m talking about. Just think of the things you could accomplish with a whole new outlook on your career. You could be big Tyler,” Mick encourages in attempts to seal the deal. Tyler’s mind wanders off to the many gigs that he could have the potential of doing. He envisions great things that could come out of this. “Well, ok, I think I’ll go along with it,” Tyler finally replies, unsure at the moment if this is a good idea or not. Mick smiles and claps his hands together a single time as it echoes through the entire apartment. “I knew that you would see it my way. Let’s make you in to the star that you have had the potential to be your entire life,” Mick says in an energetic voice ready to get down to business. He pulls out his brief case and begins to pour out papers. Tyler and John both smile simultaneously knowing that this could mean good things for both of them. The view on the trio begins to distance itself. The conversation amongst them becomes inaudible. Mick remains seated on the couch with his suit and tie and begins explaining things to Tyler, who replies every so often with a nod. Finally, the scene fades out.
