Things Change

25 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-25-02 Less than twenty-four hours ago, things were fine. He was the Infinite Warrior, and nobody could take that away from him. He was had three Warriors In Training, almost at the point of entering the next realm of their training. Last of all, he had Minion; a former enemy turned close friend. Strange, yes, but not nearly so as the events that followed. Sometime in the morning, Minion had come unannounced to the home of the WITs and taken them away, with excuses Trigger did not know. They had disappeared, and when the Infinite Warrior returned, there was nothing to do but look for them. He had to retrieve his WITs with help from nobody, and no clues at all. All he had was his mind, and that being tuned to the mind of Minion he was able to track down where they had previously been. PCW local offices it was, and the destruction The Insane One Minion had caused was unbearably brutal. He had to find Minion and the WITs immediately. He had to figure out what to do that would make things right again. But this time he wasn't going to spare anyone. Minion's insanity had hit, and now it was time for extreme measures. The Infinite Warrior turned and sprung out the door of the building. He kept running, right into the street, unaware of the high-speed car coming right at him, unable to stop. Trigger turned, just in time, but there was no way to escape… When Trigger awoke, all he saw was white. Bright lights shined into his eyes, blinding him momentarily. He struggled to sit up. Where was he? It took him several moments, but he finally realized that he was in a hospital bed. 'A hospital?' he wondered. 'How did I get here?' It was then that the Infinite Warrior remembered crash outside the PCW local offices. How the car had come barreling down on him, and there was no time to dodge. He remembered seeing the driver out of the corner of his eye. In that split second, he knew that the driver had seen him. Why was it then that he didn't stop? Trigger struggled to recall more. What did the driver look like? He couldn't think that closely, maybe it would come to him later. Suddenly, Trigger felt a terrible urge to know. What did the driver look like? He knew it was crazy, but in some way he felt that he had recognized the person, whoever it was. He was trying to relive the scene when a nurse entered the room, carrying a clipboard and a pen. When she saw him sitting upright, she rushed to his side. 'Lie back down,' she said in a voice that was stern but comforting at the same time. 'You hit by a car, and you're at the hospital. Do you know who you are?' For a moment Trigger truly wasn't sure if he did. 'Yes,' he replied, in a voice that didn't seem to be his. The nurse grabbed a wet towel from a pain beside the bed and placed it upon the Infinite Warrior's forehead. 'Rest,' she told him. 'You only have minor injuries, and you will be able to return home anywhere between one and three days from now. But if you want to get better, you need to sleep.' Moments later she had left the room and closed the lights. Trigger did not want to sleep; he needed to think, but in turn his body was too weak and his eyes yearned to shut. In the end, the Infinite Warrior gave in, sinking back into the comfortable pillow and falling into slumber. Trigger did not sleep well, however, as evil thoughts began to brew in his mind. First it was just a bundle of fear, but then the irritant exposed itself as a dream. Trigger had never liked to sleep because of this very factor. When someone falls asleep, they are unprotected from dreams; dreams that can make peace of one's life, or terrorize their soul. The latter tended to occur when Trigger slept, and it was apparent now that he would awake in fright. In his nightmare, he was walking along the road on a sunny day. The empty sky matched the empty sidewalk, and it seemed as if he was alone in the world. Suddenly, dark mist enveloped the heavens, and all at once it started to rain. Thunder boomed in the background and lightning cracked. Now people started to flood the sidewalks, and cars the roads. Trigger was being suffocated by the crowds, and quickened his pace. He looked up and gasped in horror as he saw a car speeding directly at him, with no intention of halting. The Infinite Warrior tried to move, but stumbled and fell. Amazingly, the car did stop, only inches away from the helpless Trigger. With a click, the door opened, and the driver got out, but for some reason the Infinite Warrior could not see his face. The mysterious man walked over to Trigger, and stooped down before his face close enough so that the Infinite Warrior could look into his eyes, yet he still did not know whom he was facing. Then, as the mysterious man opened his mouth and uttered three words, his face became picture clear. He was face to face with The Insane One Lance Minion! 'Hell awaits you,' said the man, as he stared deep into Trigger's soul and everything went dark. As Trigger opened his eyes, he wanted to scream, for his eyes were locked right into those of Minion, the mysterious man from his dream. 'M-M-Minion…' the Infinite Warrior creaked. Minion backed away a bit, and upon further examination Trigger realized that the WITs were also there, in the hospital room. Throwing his personal health out of his mind, the Infinite Warrior practically jumped out of the bed. 'Where have you been?' Trigger demanded of The Insane One. 'Trigger, relax,' he replied. 'We came to check on you… I got a call saying you'd been hit by a car. Are you okay?' The Infinite Warrior ignored what Minion said, continuing with his barrage of questions. 'I said where have you been?' Trigger growled. 'Earlier today! Where were you? Where did you take the WITs?' Minion looked innocent. 'What are you talking about? We were at the abandoned shop the whole day. Maybe you took a hit to the head… really, Trigger, are you okay?' Hate filled his heart as Minion's lies filled his ears. Despicable. How could he not have seen this coming? How could he have not known? It was not about the past though, it was about right now, and Trigger had had enough. The Infinite Warrior's head began to throb, and he needed to lie back down. WIT 2 came and stood over him. 'Honest, Trigger, it's true. We were at the shop all day, waiting for you.' This made the Infinite Warrior's stomach cringe. Of course Minion would lie… after all, it was The Insane One. But for one of his very own Warriors In Training to look him in the eyes and lie? Further thoughts were interrupted as a nurse opened the door and upon seeing Trigger's condition, forced Minion and the WITs to leave. Without saying a word she closed the light and shut the door. 'Betrayal is the worst form of humiliations,' thought Trigger as he faded off to sleep, 'but Minion has not won yet. Whatever game he is trying to pull, I will play as well, and when he is ready to strike I will be ready to defend my honor. This is not over… This is not over.'
