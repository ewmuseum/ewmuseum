The Midnight Opus: Part Two – The Whole Opium-Puppet Thing

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th August The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book Three: Deus Ex Toaster…… RP#1 of 4 for Turmoil (6th August 2002)… Chapter Three: The Midnight Opus: Part Two – The Whole Opium-Puppet Thing… {Note – the symbols * or # denote Footnotes…refer to index at bottom of Roleplay for more information…} “The Vacation” Whatever happened to the Bravado Kid after he vanished from the very limelight that he himself embodies? The Vacation# – the vacating of oneself for some purpose, some underlying goal that smacks you in the face with the strength comparable to several million tons of TNT. You know not what to do, yet feel strangely in balance with the big picture. That doesn’t happen often, it doesn’t happen at all with just plain ordinary people. Those with a purpose in life take vacations. Others tag along for the mere fact that they can get away from their lives and the stress for weeks or months, just so they can suffer more when they return to the life they left behind. Those that had a purpose returned to life, but didn’t find it exactly how they remembered it. Misplaced, hidden from them like some twisted game of “Hide and Seek”…except the outcome is inevitable…something must be done. Indeed, the Midnight Opus goes on…it goes on continuously, evolving, changing from one form to another. Indeed, it will be a composition remembered for all eternity…shall we recap up until so far? The Midnight Opus## has barely begun, a prologue and barely anything but a minor ditty to kick off the story of the rejuvenation and realization of the Bravado Kid, the Savior-Fiend. It wasn’t the true beginning of the Midnight Opus, just merely a wedge driven into the crack of difference – splitting that ever hard rock of cliché-ism. Like so many eyeballs glued to the remains of some unlucky idiot who bargained with death and lost his shirt – we knew that they’d find their way back here… We’re too damn addictive after all…this story’s that pink pill that you’ve been longing to swallow… Indeed, the Midnight Opus goes on… ::.~(Musings of Chris Chase)~ Stuck between an alley and a particularly irritating place – this is where you find me. Directionless, without a rudder to steer my way, and without a crew to launch an assault – I find myself stranded on the rocks once more. Great, that helps a whole bunch. I never was able to get the greatest of assistance. They all thought I was too much of an asshole and passed me off as a soon-to-be convict. How wrong could they be? Up a creek without a paddle and with only a cameraman to keep me company in these dark hours around the Midnight zone. It’s a dark time in my world. There’s no celebrations, no parades, no running around like an utter lunatic with his ass ablaze. Just something I’ve been cooking for a long time. You’ve been wanting to know my story since I came back, the truth’s never been revealed until lately. You’ve just never bothered to ask me why, why I disappeared, why I vanished off the face of the earth. I’m my own hero for that…if only Kannon would do us all a favor and follow suit….:: “Before time, there was me…the indomitable young brilliant idol. I hang on the very words, the very thoughts of the nations of the world that I’m bound to. I’m no triumphant Savior; I’ve been co-opted into doing the dirty work of GOD! “And do you have any proof of this, Mister Chase?” “Proof? Who said anything about Proof? How can I prove that I’ve been indirectly been co-opted into doing God’s dirty work?!” “Yes, and how does that make you feel?” ::. I’m surprised it took me this long to see a shrink, maybe I was just too stubborn and bullheaded – that and the fact that I’m not all too fond of nosy people. On my scale they stand alongside the likes of Communists and Scientologists, except maybe to a lesser extent than to my main pet hates. Throughout my troubled career so far, I’ve always thought that one day I’d be recounting all those experiences that have left me awash on the sidelines, being subjected to countless numbers of idiotic jobbers and loud mouth old men who can’t honestly get their facts straight or keep away from incessant clichés and pointless anecdotes about ‘Young Punks’. Therapy isn’t my thing at all…it’s the eternal babbling of idiots who failed in Society that really riles me about the whole thing. It’s even more pedestrian than the blasts that I somehow received from Duke Williams over the past few days. You’d think that these people would actually let me speak before they go off on a complete tangent… And that somewhat foolish entourage meant that I was forced off my orbit entirely…well, it helped it a little. It makes you wonder why he hasn’t compared me to Uranus yet… *cough* It’s been nearly a week now since I failed once again in a PCW Ring. Great, I guess that means that I can apply for entrance into the…wait, no I can’t. It’s been desperately mind numbing for me since my return, the utter confusion of the past Turmoil or Fusion has given me nothing to grasp onto. Nothing to latch onto and be dragged by, screaming for my dear life. I suppose this is why I get thrown in a match with Duke Williams, I don’t know for sure. You see, this is why I’m taking therapy. The last scene I remember was nothing more than an ill-placed nightmare –the whole incident with the owner of a French restaurant was non-existent, if only to save my own embarrassment – if only to save me from the shock-rendering horror of Dustin Downey making a mockery out of the former Tag Team known as High Impact, which is undoubtedly saying something. But the Midnight Opus continues to register a blip on the existence scale, regardless of whether that whole charade was worth it or not….:: “You don’t think I could…” “Absolutely not, Mister Chase! I’m sorry, but the ramifications of your actions could reap vast quantities of wanton destruction upon this very room…not to mention the havoc that would ensue if the Koalas escape…” “I tell you what, why don’t you go pander to the needs of the Koalas whilst I go get my deposit back and leave the delusion to those less fortunate than I? Heck, I’m willing to give it all away just as long as it gets me “Humanitarian of the Year”. Jesus Christ!” 'Is Blasphemy really needed here, Mister Chase? After all, you don't want an uprising amongst the more religiously inclined Koalas now, would you?' 'Oh sure, I'll make sure I don't make the same mistake again...' 'Now then, don't you even attempt to break through that damned door!' {Insert loud noise synonym here…} 'Oh lookee here, now it's unlocked! I think I'll pass on future therapy sessions here, thank you...' 'Come back here Mister Chase!' {Sound of more crashing, banging and general loud noises...} “Gyah! The damned client has escaped, and that’s the third one in the past hour. It’s a slow day today, don’t you think? Miss Mirage…send in the next hopeless individual for a deluded grilling of epically minor proportions…” ::.It’s Monday morning, again. I mean, heck, it only happens once in seven, so therefore it is worthy of some note, no? It’s little more than ten o’clock, not necessarily the early hours of the morning, but the nagging feeling that I could use some rest that I’m not exactly accustomed to in this tumultuous and rather stricken times. The Institute for Psychiatric Well Being, a meaningless and perhaps mistakenly named building by any means, being a home to lowlifes and idiots, it’d be better off calling it ‘Bob’ than anything that requires an attention span of more than a few seconds just to comprehend it. It’s those kind of things that are fucking useless. Speaking for myself, I’m a sorry sight to say the least. Unshaven, unclean, and generally dejected…the only seemingly active personal trait of mine seems to be the sarcastic stare that I put on whenever I feel wholesomely dubious about anything… Which usually happens to be quite a lot of things… It’s quite easy to see the exact state of mental depression that I’ve been lowered into, kicking and screaming like a child forced to eat the entrails of a rabid dog…or listen to Hank Williams records. Let’s face it, the files in my C-Drive have been messed up for too long now, and the Chris Chase mainframe has been long overdue an overhaul. Like a sharp kick to the nether regions, the scene stuns the audience with much aplomb…that, or I’ve spent too much time dealing with the likes of shrinks. Outside the institute, I slump onto a chair, or perhaps even a bench…not that I care so much for it anyhow. A tumbleweed blows by, and I come to terms with the fact that the building behind me is the only building on a one-way street leading into a visual cacophony of lights, homeless thugs and the occasional road-rage intoxicated traffic jam. The fact is, I’m a day early…most of my recent women have told me that that’s a particular habit of mine. A cold wind picks up on this Summer’s morning…and for the first time since I left Canada almost three weeks ago, I shiver….:: “Why if it isn’t Mister Chris Chase, boy, I haven’t seen you around these parts lately my boy. How’s the latest shoe-in for PCW World Champion holding up, eh? I tell you, just one match against that Xavier Kannon fella, and you’re sure to beat that weirdo’s ass, hoss…” “Try telling that one to whoever decided that a Chris Chase versus Duke Williams match would actually work. After all, I’ve got no beef with Duke…and all he seems to be worried about is his retirement home arrangements…” “That and the fact that his promo tapes taste like chicken.” “Excuse me?” ::. It’s become rather disconcerting all of the sudden. Or rather, my own presence in a world that has the Dark Ages as saviors. To say that I’ve been blatantly swept out of the way by a bunch of light bulb fearing goth types is perhaps beyond my own perception at this time – perhaps even beyond that of the immortal puppet masters that run this infernal world. I wish I could be like them…they have porno on for twenty-four hours a day – that, and making someone dance for the hell of it seems a lot more fun than being made to dance yourself. Finding myself is one thing, finding myself next to a blithering Texan whilst seemingly stuck to a wooden bench at 10 am is almost entirely another. Swinging my head around, I look at the perhaps all too blithering Texan, who sways to and fro on the bench in time with the soft, yet cold breeze that had made its presence known to me a second or two ago. Here’s hoping that he goes and bothers the people in the institute, they could use some tedious aversion therapy….:: Texan: I said they taste like chicken, you freaking moron. I hope you haven’t been learning from the State idiot###…I tell you, the guy’s trouble. Even saying his name could bring about a catastrophe that could ruin Texas forever… Chris: If that’s the case, then I’ll willingly oblige and not mention that person. After all, he’s caused enough accidents for the time being. After that whole incident with the Chili cook-off and several cardboard cutouts*, I’d rather not be held responsible for anything that could damage my chances of getting heat again… Texan: I’d use a radiator if I were you…all manner of heat from just a simple boiler type thing. That or a portable fireplace, you can plug it in anywhere, for instant heat… ::. Now I know where Downey gets it from…they must put something in the water around these parts… Entertaining the down and homely folk is an ever-occurring kick in the gonads for me. It seems to happen more often than Snow has attitude adjustments, which is something impressive in terms of statistics at least. In real terms, the statistics lie about the whole purpose of keeping people entertained whilst rummaging through the rucksack of my brain to find an adequate escape plan. I think I must have dropped it in the Institute, and there’s no way that I’m going to be dragged in there. I wipe a bead of sweat off my brow, flicking it off without thinking twice about it…or at all for that matter. Twitching with a vicious shudder, the Texan tries to pull himself off the bench…and fails miserably. It’s a start at least. I’m beginning to hope that a part of the bench will fall away, and said Texan Mastermind will fall through a trapdoor like a thousand Texans before…but it looks like my luck’s not holding out…at least for today anyway… The PCW never did tolerate a cynical hero though. Questioning your own actions was never all that much fun, it was better to go blindly into battle…well, it was at least more entertaining and random. Duke and I have a lot in common…did I just say that out loud? We both despise a certain former NBA player turned former wrestler, and we both can’t stand the likes of the current Main event entourage. However, I can’t say that I can even tolerate Country and Western music – I have a feeling that I’ll be recalling the whole incident with Billy Ray Cyrus in some future therapy session…and the fact stands that I’m nearly twenty years the Junior of Mister Williams. Great, I guess in his terms that makes me half the man he is…that is, if the math ever worked itself out. But would that ever be truly the case? Absolutely not. I’d rather be mutilated with a swordfish than settle for being half the man that Duke Williams is. He might not rank at all on my list of enemies, thankfully enough for his sake, but Chris Chase doesn’t settle for less than one hundred percent… Are you calling me a lazy bastard again?.:: Chris: ~(Rolls eyes)~ I don’t think that lugging around a radiator is going to help me at all…unless used as some form of projectile weaponry – hitting Xavier Kannon from half a mile away with a flying Radiator would be some feat indeed… ::.If only my fondest dreams came true…I wouldn’t have to worry about getting my ‘Heat’ back….:: Chris: Getting it airborne would probably be the hardest part of the whole operation... Texan: But wouldn’t that mean that you’d be cold almost soon afterwards? ::. And I chuckle at the trivial nature of this very query, as the one-way street begins to pick up – in terms of traffic. There’s a tiny hint of lens flare as the camera pans out over the likes of myself and the Texan, who seems to be more amused with this than before….:: Chris: ~(Laughs)~ It’s a small price to pay…but it’ll all be worth it in the end. To see the look on his face, or lack of it thereof, after being hit with the damned thing would be absolutely priceless. But let’s face it though, I’m hardly likely to face Kannon, unless I beat Duke Williams, and subsequently half of the PCW roster…What am I letting myself in for? At this rate, I’d been signed up as another ineffectual lackey of Nelson’s… Texan: That Duke Williams is one tough hoss, hoss…you’d find it quite hard to overcome his experience. Xavier Kannon on the other hand, is a Scientolog-whatdyacallit…nothing more than an actor passing himself off as a wrestler. Duke can’t friggin’ well act for crying out loud… Chris: Well…true. And I’m a Professional Wrestler passing myself off as a humanitarian, a minion of god, a firm believer in the Gilligan’s Island Theory** and currently, a Saint. These people would believe anything if you told them so, right now, they’re actually believing that Duke might just edge out the likes of myself, and that I’m nothing more than a Young Punk trying to make his mark in the annuls of PCW History. The funny thing is, I never listened to Punk Rock***…not once… ::. Relevancy was never my strong point. I guess there’s always room to break a habit or two. There’s been that twitch that I’ve been wanting to get rid off since the Dark Ages crucified me, and my abuse of irrelevancy can also go out of that opaque window. It wouldn’t see it coming – I don’t think the person below the window would either. Ouch. That’s what’s kept me from achieving anything in the past three weeks, my inability to keep things from going wholesomely random – unlike the times when everything was randomly relevant to the topic. I guess I must have read the fine print wrong, maybe I need glasses…I wonder if Duke wears bifocals these days – maybe he could recommend a brand to me. Need I say any more? The Midnight Opus is beginning to weave it’s magical web of cloth around the big picture…and I still don’t know what the Midnight Opus is. There’s nothing less than a smile on my face as I begin to joke to myself among my thoughts. Jokes that could change the world as we know it, and your opinions on little old me – That Crazy Canadian-British Wrestler….:: Chris: Well, my slightly helpful local pal…it’s time for me to bid you adieu. Just in case though, I wouldn’t try the shrimp in these parts…not really worth of merit, at all! Now then, if you’ll excuse me…I’ll just try and – ::.Yep…it’s the old stuck to the chair routine again. Catch me next time, to see whether or not I managed to escape from this somewhat minor predicament…And you can Justify…that!.:: {Chase’s Footnotes: - # The Vacation – The widely scrutinized disappearance of Chris Chase after handing over the World Title to Jason Snow. Rumored to be either an Injury or Mental Health issue. ## The Midnight Opus – Story still in formative stages, keep reading Chris Chase promos to find out the outcome. ### Come on, you know who this guy is already. Don’t ask! * Another Dustin Downey Promo – I don’t want to talk about it… **Gilligan’s Island Theory (GIT) – The acceptance that we’re not exactly sure of anything… ***Chase’s Denial of any connection with Punk Rock – A blatant lie, High Impact, that loveable PCW2/PCW Tag Team encompassing Chris Chase and Dustin Downey (With added Mark Estep and Shawn Prodigy), were connected with the teenage cliché of Punk Rock about a year ago now…}
