The black slong theory....Malice Style!

17 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/17/02 -You know you’re in Gods country when the camera opens up and all you see is a giant moon. The light from the moon reflexes off of Malice creek, which is on the back wood estates of a one Duke Williams. A shadow of a man dressed in all black is seen on the flat. His black trench coat flaps loudly in the wind as he just stands and stares. His voice is deep and gravely reminiscent of old Lee Marvin- Williams: Reflection in the pale of the Montana moon light. A man could find his inner soul in the strength of the wilderness sucking on the breasts of Mother Nature. Survival of the fittest is the way of the land….always has been always will be. -A gust of wind almost throws Dukes Stetson off his head.- Williams: I've got a long list of real good reasons, for all the things I've done. I always carry my head up high and don’t look back. Unlike the children in Asylum I am rational and mature. Unlike the slow wit of our audience I think before I act. -Duke freezes as a wild Bear is seen as it walks near by. Duke has his hand on a knife that sits on his belt till the bear passes.- Williams: I've got a picture in the back of my mind, Of what I've lost and what I've won. I can’t say every battle has fallen in the direction of Malice but every war has. Look at those who have attempted to stop me. From Vampir to Nelson where are they now? When the final chapter of PCW is written will anybody remember the antics of PWA or the rants of Miya?? Probably not. They will however remember who was the president in that golden age of ratings. They will remember that good looking, funny, intelligent stud calling the action from the color position. They will never forget my legacy. Does that make me a living legend? A legacy that is only budding with the new found relationship with the best thing wrestling has to offer in Xavier Kannon. -Duke pauses to stare into the moon.- I've survived every situation, knowing when to freeze and when to run. Like when I saw ICWA taking a dive. Jew offered me a contract loaded with incentives while PCW offered me nothing but a job. I had to start over again in PCW while I could live like a king with the jews. When something is handed with you respect is not part of the package. I’ve always been a bit of a maverick that likes doing things the hard way so I went with PCW. I earned my respect. Right now I’m standing back freezing like a rabbit in danger. Holding my cards waiting with my poker face. I know what PWA and Miya has done to me and revenge will be paid. To debunk the rumors I am not Jesus Christ and I do NOT forget. I am the Man in Black, The Missoula Mad Man, The Father Of Malice, and quite frankly your worst nightmare. You thank that old ladies flabby, wrinkled up, liver spotted, arm that serves you coffee at the truck stop is ugly….then you’ve never seen me mad hoss. Cus I make that arm look like a virgin, eighteen year old, high school cheer leaders, steamy, wet, vagina, on a sultry Alabama night. -Duke lips his lips.- Williams: I’m sorry I played the show for the fans. That did nothing for me except slow me down. Those people are all diseased. Ask your self what kind of culture ignores a war hero like John McCain or a literary icon such as Twain but yet glorifies and iodizes men like Jerry Springer and Dr. Phil. Give me a break hoss. It’s like the asylum has taken over. What happened to normal, blue collar, hard working, honest, sincere, individuals like Xavier Kannon. Ellie, Timmy, Brandi, or myself? We our the leaders of the morality, respectability, honesty, individuality, and the pursuit of happiness. You can join us or be left behind. Noah didn’t take those with out tickets and his little boat booked up pretty fast. As a matter of fact we OUR the Noah of wrestling and PCW is the great ark. We built it, we patched it up, and now we’re writing up the tickets to board cus baby the rain is coming. Sink or swim hoss cus you mess with Malice you mess with the Front Office and we run the boat. Sure I regret letting the fans like me but regret is just a memory, written on my brow. And there's nothing I can do about it now! -Duke spits and watches as the wild Montana wind carries a few yards.- Williams: I've got a wild and a restless spirit. I've held my price through every deal. So when I say the Front Office is hear to stay I mean it. I’ve pledged my loyalty to Kannon and he has returned that favor. God is a wicked man. He made black men’s slongs to lay triple the size of the white mans. Sure we find ourselves jealous but we deal with it. We our man and man can be rational. We accept the things we cannot change and move on. What God intended to do was split the races. He ENJOYS watching war. What fun is watching a movie where everybody gets along? He did all he could to create ciaos and the slong theory is only a small example….or maybe a long one? I don’t know but the point is Miya tried to tear me away from the Front Office by me gaining the number one contender slot. My friends it just shows how strong the bond of the Front Office is when I passed on that. My will cannot be contended with. You see Ty Ne*BEEEEEEEEEEEP* created great army’s based on greed. That never works. The Front Office is bonded by respect and trust. Its forever. Malice is Forever and so is Duke Williams. -The wind blows cold as Duke walks away.-=w=
