Revenge Saga Part 24: A SHORT encounter with Wolverine...pt4

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/7/02 Revenge Saga Part 24: A SHORT encounter with Wolverine…pt4 Fleming squirmed in his uncomfortable plain wooden chair again, still in shock about the comments of the tyrant who sat opposite him. Fleming studied the man closely as Baron Helmut Zemo sat back in his chair, his left hand stroking his chin, with an extremely cocky look etched upon his face. Fleming knew that he was being called here because they wanted something from him…hell he even suspected that they wanted weapons technology and money from the Zemoff Corporation, but he didn’t expect them to be so bold about it. On reflection, if knew that it was in fact Zemo in charge, he would have suspected it and he would have turned to plan B…which was to run like a bastard and hoped that they never found him. He was in a sticky situation, he didn’t want to stray this far off the straight an narrow, but it didn’t seem that he had much of a choice in the matter. At least he would have Crichton in custody soon enough and once he was dealt with, Fleming would have to find a way to get out of the hole he dug for himself. That was if he survived this meeting…He was fairly sure that they wouldn’t kill him…yet. He still could serve a purpose to them, plus they needed his weapons tech and his money and he very much doubted that they would go to the trouble of flying all the way back to New York simply to catch Crichton…for him. ************ Wolverine stopped outside the establishment that Crichton’s scent was emanating from, of course the two old men and a pub landlord still, wearing his apron, running for their lives also helped him find the place. His hyper-sense of smell picked up Crichton’s scent as strong as ever, it was the other three scents that troubled him the most. Every civilian had exited the establishment, so that meant that some Crichton’s buddies were in there. Wolverine really didn’t like going into to a battle unprepared…but it wasn’t a necessity. He cracks his knuckles and opened the door. ************ Crichton edged further back against the wall as the three Thunderbolts assembled before him bad intentions blatant. Why did this shit constantly happen to him? What had he done to deserve such hatred? the answer was simple. He had done nothing to these people, they were here on business, and that business obviously included kicking seven shits outta him. It was SO important to get job satisfaction these days! Ah…the word satisfaction, meaning content…happy even. He couldn’t really remember the last time he was happy…the last time he felt happy was when Aeryn was alive…the words bore no meaning for him…not anymore. He would say he forgot what it was to be happy…but he couldn’t really say, he wasn’t familiar with the feeling. Mach III: We have come for you Crichton… Crichton: [clapping his hands together] No shit sherlock! Mach III: …like I was saying, we have orders to take you back with us… Jolt: [stepping forward] We can either do this the easy way or…well you know! Crichton: [sighing a smile developing upon his face.] Let me tell you guys something…believe it or not this ain’t the first time this kinda thing has happened to me! So I suggest that you get your asses in line with the rest of them…if you don’t decided to heed my words, that’ll just mean having to kick your asses now…[cracking his knuckles] rather than later! Mach III: Really, well I…[stops as a coughing sound in the background his heard] The three Thunderbolts turn around, to see none other than Wolverine leant against the pub’s doorframe. Mach III: Listen up Wolverine…we don’t want any trouble we’re just here for Crichton… Wolverine: Well bub…that’s who I’m here for too, so step aside and I won’t have to kick your asses as well! Crichton watched as the three Thunderbolts and the X-man known as Wolverine began to argue over him. If this had been four girls arguing over him, he would have been flattered, after all he was the greatest PCW Television Champion…of July 2002…but this just made him sick. The four of them were arguing about who was going to kick his ass first. Crichton hoped that both parties would get into a rumble and simply beat the living hell out of each other, allowing him to slip out through the door. He knew for a fact that he would never get THAT lucky. Mach III: Well if you want to play it the hard way well… With that his wings extend and the other two Thunderbolts took up fighting positions. Wolverine simply grinned at the sight and within a blink of an eye lunged forward at Mach III, who dodged the advance by taking off from the ground and provocatively hovered above the action, as Wolverine slammed into the unfortunate jolt. Crichton watched on with anticipation as the battle ensued. Crichton was in two minds about the whole thing…should he just cut bait and run? Or should he stay and fight? He had special feelings for Wolverine…to be fair he couldn’t give two shits whether Wolverine died right before his eyes…but he had vowed to take revenge upon all those who had previously wronged both he and Aeryn…the Thunderbolts effectively fit into that criteria. If he ran now he might not get the opportunity to fight them again…and if he did, it would be because they were hunting him down. If he ran and they hunted him down they might actually succeed in whatever they were trying to do. This distraction by Wolverine might be the only chance he’ll ever have to extract revenge on these individuals. His made was made up, the die was cast, he would fight. With one leap Crichton jumped upon the bar, drawing the attention of, the still hovering, Mach III…but it was too late. Before Mach III could move out of the way Crichton pounced. He expected that the sheer velocity of his pounce would bring them back to the ground…he was wrong, VERY wrong. Seconds after the pounce, Mach III’s stabilisers accounted for the extra weight as Crichton hung on to Mach III’s waist for dear life. Mach III brought down the point of his elbow down hard upon Crichton’s forehead, but still he hung on. Mach III repeated the action a couple of times and realising that this tactic would not work he headed towards the front door. Crichton realised that the only advantage he had over Mach III was the enclosed space of the battlefied…if they went outside he gave this advantage up. He had to act and he had to act fast. The opportunity presented itself as one of Jolt’s electric blasts hit Wolverine square in the chest, propelling him half way across the room. Crichton let go of Mach III’s legs. The loss of weight made Mach III go faster…he was unable to stop himself impacting upon the wall opposite the bar, as Crichton fell with a thud upon Jolt, knocking her clean out cold. Within a couple of seconds of fighting he was no longer outnumbered…his confidence grew as he realised that he might actually pull this one off, as he turned to face Songbird…the master of sound. Her enhanced vocal cords could make hard sound…amongst other things. She simply smiled at him, he couldn’t doubt her beauty, but he sure as hell could doubt her intentions. The first blow came within the blink of an eye…even testing Crichton’s enhanced reflexes to the limit as he moved to the left of a particularly vicious looking demolition ball of pink sound. Crichton took the opening in her defence and leap forward with a vicious punch to the jaw sending her flying over the bar and smashing into all the glass bottles containing liquor, similar to old western movies. Crichton stood there all of the Thunderbolts were down…it seemed almost too easy for Crichton…this kinda went against the grain for him because nothing he ever did was easy. He simply put it down to aggression, the thought of revenge and training his ass off so he could kick Shabazz’ ass for a third…and FINAL time. Crichton grabbed Jolt by the throat and hoisted her lifeless body up into the air. This was his time for revenge, but the question was, could he do it? Could he murder this woman in cold blood? If he did do it he would be what every super hero thought him to be already…a murderer…if he didn’t do it his vow of revenge would become meaningless. He also knew that the one act of murder could be the very thing the dark pulses residing him would need to turn him completely…or had the dark impulses already taken control? The very thought of murdering somebody in cold blood would suggest so. He didn’t get much time to debate the situation. Wolverine: Hey Bub, whatever these people have down to you it don’t warrant cold blooded murder… Crichton: I know…Wolverine: [Looking slightly bemused] You…know? Crichton: Yeah, I know! Let me ask you Wolverine, what would you do if the woman you loved were murdered? Wolverine: I would do anything I could to pay the bastard back. Crichton: Even if it ruined your reputation? Even if it changed you into something you don’t wanna be? Wolverine: Yeah…I guess… Crichton: Well now you know MY situation… Wolverine: Yeah…I do. You’d better go before the police get here… Crichton: I thought you came here looking for me? Wolverine: [Walking out of the door] Like you said…I understand your situation…
