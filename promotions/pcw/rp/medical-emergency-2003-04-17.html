Medical Emergency!

17 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/04/03 “So tell me one more time… why am I here?” muttered a displeased Nerezza, fiddling with the Velcro strap of one of his gloves. The buzzing noise that seemed to come from the clock above his head had irritated him for long enough. Waiting is something that should come naturally for a predator, but when there’s the thrill of the hunt and prey at the end instead of… whatever was waiting for him… things tend to slow down. “Routine check-up on health, Squire. They’re just cleaning up from me and then it’s your turn.” answered Gaz Green, sporting his ever-present smile that could be glued to his face. “After all, PCW requires all it’s employees be looked over at least twice a year and we’re well overdue for an appointment.” “Hmph.” retorted the vampire. “I hate hospitals. My brother wanted to be a doctor or paramedic or something stupid. My ex-girlfriend became a nurse. What next, Fluffy going in for a medical degree?” “Never underestimate the Macarena-dancing Chupacabra.” Gaz smirked. The pair of wrestlers stuck out against the pale blue walls of the waiting room, surrounded by out-of-date random magazines and crossword puzzle books that, upon Nerezza’s inspection, had already been done. The only company other than his partner was a fat, black haired receptionist who didn’t quite fit into her profession, looking more like a test subject for inner-tube inflation devices. “Guh… I already know my health is perfect… well… perfectly lacking. But then again, I guess corpses can’t catch disease.” Nerezza mused to himself. “Nascosto Nerzera, please.” Came an announcement over the PA system. “Grr… it’s NEREZZA.” grumbled the behemoth as he heaved himself out of the seat. “Have fun!” Gaz grinned. “Bollocks to that.” Nerezza answered with a glare. Briskly stomping down a corridor, he placed a palm against the door of “Examination Room 3”, or so said the plate. Standing in front of the vampire was an aged looking man of eastern-asian looking descent, perhaps India or Pakistan. His nametag, Dr. Patel soon answered that. His wire-like grey hair puffed out from the side of his head into two tufts that shook when he turned his head towards the entering vampire. “Ahh, Mister Nerzera?” he said, producing a pair of glasses from his white coat and perching them on his nose. “Nerezza. And yeah, it’s me.” Nerezza said, half-hearted as though he was guiltily admitting to a crime. The room had a black leather examination table and a long series of blue paper towels covering it. Scattered about the room were various pregnancy tests, heart monitoring devices and other medical testing equipment pieces. “Okay, would you like to lie down for a minute and roll up your sleeve?” Dr. Patel asked. Nerezza removed his ankle-length red jacket and chest armour, placing them on a chair nearby while the doctor unwrapped a Velcro band and struggled to hook the entire thing around Nerezza’s bicep area, barely managing to hold it in place. Connecting the band to a wire that fed into a machine, the band inflated suddenly around Nerezza’s arm, squeezing as though it was one of Gaz’s arm-bars. For a few seconds more, it tightened like a Velcro anaconda, until it finally deflated back to normal, releasing the pressure. “Ehh… it would appear the sphigomometer is broken.” The doctor resigned. “Huh?” the vampire grunted. “This machine measures your blood pressure but it is reading that you have none at all whatsoever! Please wait while I go find a replacement. In the meantime, I require you to remove any clothing that will obstruct access to your torso, feet and hands.” he instructed. “Yeah, yeah, hold on.” Nerezza sighed, waiting until the doctor had disappeared before pulling off the black anime T-Shirt and gloves, then his boots and socks. Left with only his jeans and doing his best to hide his wings, Nerezza shuffled back onto the table and rested his head, waiting for the doctor to return. Before he could finish humming a tune to himself, Dr. Patel reappeared, wheeling in another machine on a trolley. “Okay, now I am going to hook you up to this machine to test your heart rate. Have you ever been on an ECG before?” he enquired. “No… is that thing electrical?” Nerezza asked with a slight nervous quiver in his voice. “It is, but do not worry, it is not the defibrillator, this cannot send out any shock, sir.” answered the doctor in a reassuring tone, before pulling open a drawer on the trolley and taking out around a dozen small pads and pressing them onto Nerezza’s body on the chest, wrists and feet. Attaching a series of wires to each of them, he then switched the machine on as a graph of some kind was slowly pushed out on a piece of paper, representing Nerezza’s heartbeat. The doctor looked on in amazement as a perfectly flat line came out. “Mr. Nerezza… I think there is a severe equipment malfunction here!” he gasped. “Oh, really…?” Nerezza smirked in mock surprise. The doctor responded by quickly pressing an ice-cold stethoscope to the vampire’s chest and inserting it into his ears. “There… there is no heartbeat!! WE MUST GET YOU TO THE EMERGENCY ROOM RIGHT AWAY!” shrieked the doctor, slapping a panic button, prompting a team of doctors and nurses to storm into the room, armed with a stretcher and various life-support tools. Yanking Nerezza off the table and onto the stretcher, the medical staff quickly hook up a blood pack to his arm and a breathing mask over his face, rushing him through the halls of the hospital. Nerezza contently watched the trail of lights as he looked up, enjoying the free meal being injected into his arm. “Mmm… coppery.” he thought to himself as a pair of rubbery doors burst open ahead of him, where more hospital staff lay in wait with electrodes in hand. “CLEAR!” Nerezza’s eyes opened wide as he recognized the line. “Wai- ARGH!” he screamed as a jolt of electricity streaked through his body. “WHAT THE BLOODY HELL DO YOU THINK YOU’RE DOING?” he yelled, sitting up. “Sir, his heart is still not beating!” shouted a masked nurse. “But he’s sitting up and talking!” another snapped. “OF COURSE I’M SODDING TALKING!” roared the vampire. “Are you alive, sir?” a female nurse asked politely. “Well… no, technically not, but-” “CLEAR!” “AAAAAAARGH! IT’S LIKE A TEXAS DEATH SENTENCE IN HERE!” “Don’t worry… I got something to kick-start his heart” a familiar voice spoke. “…Gaz…?” Nerezza gingerly whimpered. “Yes Squire, hold still, setting buzzer to full power!” the Emerald Menace turned a dial on the right of his hand buzzer and with a loud click, he placed a palm on the defibrillator, sending a massive shock into Nerezza’s body that caused the vampire to leap clean off the stretcher and even making the lights in the room dim for a moment. Finally, the flatline turned into a rapid series of beeps. “Success!” Gaz applauded himself. “You moron! I’m full of dead blood with decayed internal organs and now you’ve made me live again, I’m going to… urk… my… stomach… uhhh…” Nerezza gasped as he fell flat again, like his heart monitor. The vampire’s eyes forced themselves closed, as the last thing he heard was a monotone beeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeep… … … … … … … “GAAAAAAAAAAH!” Nerezza woke up, jerking into a sitting position before smacking his head on something and falling back down, bumping the back of his skull on whatever he was laying on. His eyes fluttered open again slowly into darkness. Cold air swept all over his body, which after a second of speculation, turned out to be naked except for some sort of sheet over him. “I’m in… the morgue? Aw, bollocks, I’ve already done this dying bit, my deathday’s on May 29th! Hmm… that reminds me, gonna have to tell people to buy me presents or something… okay Rezz, one step at a time. Quit talking to yourself and get the hell out of here.” With that, Nerezza’s arm quickly extended and shot out like a bullet and pierced the weak door that held his “corpse” in place. His skeletal fingers danced around the edge of the puncture, eventually congregating on the handle that secured it and yanking it down, freeing him. The vampire withdrew his hand and heaved himself out of the temporary resting place. “Brrrrr….” he shivered, wrapping the sheet around himself. “Looks like I’ve been born again… and it’s just as crap as the first time!” Nerezza looked around the place. Behind him was what looked like a giant filing cabinet, except each of the drawers held a deceased person, awaiting their autopsy, burial or whatever they had chosen for the fate of their empty shells. The vampire had heard stories of nerves in corpses making them sit up, or the escaping gas from their carcasses producing nerve-shattering noises. He chuckled for an instant as he thought of the irony – some corpses move, talk or break wind when dead. This one trash-talked, played pranks and grappled after death. Back in the waiting room, a guilty Gaz Green sat down, a puzzled look on his face as he asked himself over and over for a solution to break his partner out of the hospital… if he survived. His concentration was suddenly disrupted as alarm bells began ringing and as if in an instant, burly orderlies sprung out of nowhere and ran down the corridor with shout of “The morgue!” “Hmm… the Vamp-Squire!” he thought, and quickly joined in the marathon as they pelted towards the direction of the morgue. Upon arrival, both the orderlies and Gaz were greeted by the same sight… a smashed window, the word “BYE!” scratched into the curtains and Nerezza lying on the floor with the sheet wrapped around himself like a towel wrapped around someone who just left the shower and a massive lump forming on his forehead. “He must have not noticed the bars on the window…” Gaz commented as the orderlies began to drag the vampire out of the room… ~ Hours later… ~ “Wheeeeeeere am I…” Nerezza groaned as his eyes blurrily opened for the second time that day. “It’s okay, you’re here with meeeeeeee….” answered Gaz, partially mocking him. “Aw, BOLLOCKS.” Nerezza cursed. He was now in a regular ward, with only the blood pack hooked up to his arm and in bed, dressed in a mint green apron and bib. “I wish I was unconscious again now!” SMACK! “ARGH! What the bloody hell was that for?” he squealed. “Erk, not enough power.” The Jester deduced, before standing up and folding up his chair… “WAIT!” SMACK! … ~ MORE hours later… ~ “Uuuuuuuuuuurgh…” “Nurse! Lurch has come round!” Gaz joked. “Kisama…” Nerezza growled. He knew Gaz didn’t know what he spoke about when he was talking Japanese, but by the tone of Nerezza’s voice, the meaning could easily be guessed. “You want to tell me why the hell everything that happened today happened?” “Oh, my fault entirely, Squire.” Chirped the Prince of Pranks. “Y’see, upon booking this appointment, I was in such a rush I neglected to inform them of your special condition. I should have told them about the abnormally low heart readings being expected and all, but it didn’t occur to me to say until you’d attempted the great escape straight out of the window and into some steel bars. That’s when I told Dr. Patel that all you needed was a blood pack hooked up to you and you’d be fine in a while. So… yeah. Sorry!” Gaz and Nerezza’s mouth went in opposite directions as Gaz’s face lit up into a smile and Nerezza’s formed a deathly frown, only unseen to Gaz because his eyes closed as he scratched the back of his head apologetically. “Oh, no worries. You’ve had Vampirism to be labeled as an official condition, so you even have blood prescribed. No more killing people, you can simply take a bag from the pharmacy like any other medicine. Just imagine, if I’d told the doctors this before, NONE of today’s antics would have happened!” Laughed Gaz. Nerezza then squinted his eyes shut and grasped his chest, groaning loudly. “Grrr…. nggghh…” he grunted. “Something I can get you squire?” Gaz suddenly squeaked with concern. “Sedative… and… a clean… needle…” Nerezza gasped. Gaz quickly grabbed the item off the table next to Nerezza and handed it to him. Nerezza pulled the protective cover off and squirted a small amount to make sure it was working, then twirled his finger around. “Turn around, Gaz… you don’t want to see where this is going.” Nerezza warned. The Jester turned on his heels and look in the opposite direction. “Feeling in pain, are you, Squire?” Gaz smiled to himself, amused by the situation. “Nope… the vampire grinned as he drew his arm back and aimed the needle for Gaz’s rear. “You are…” EEEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARGH!!!
