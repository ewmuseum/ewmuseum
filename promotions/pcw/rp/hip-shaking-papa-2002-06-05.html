Hip Shaking Papa

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/5/02 -“The Malice Man” Duke Williams sits in a barbers chair on the North West side of Chicago. IL. All we see is Duke’s head and the barber standing behind him. The barber is an older man of about mid-sixties and looks to be old school Italian. They talk politics, religion, and wrestling. Lucky for the PCW fans the camera just happen to pick up one a wrestling chat.- Williams: A bunch of f*cking clowns if you ask me. Tony: Who des masked hump again? Williams: That turd is the Phantom Booker. Tony: What is he a fruit or something wearing pantyhose on his head? -Duke smiles- Williams: No. The fruit is Nelson he likes little boys. Tony: That sick twit should be shot in the balls. -Duke laughs out loud.- Williams: That or leave him locked in the room with a bunch of Catholic priests. -The barber smacks the back of Duke head.- Tony: A watch a your mouth a…..I’m a proud Catholic. Williams: You have the scissors I’ll shut up. Tony: Good boy. -Tony trims up the back of Duke long brown hair.- Tony: So who else is in this match? Williams: Some other pud named Powell. This guy has the ball not to mention me in his speeches? F*ck him I hope he underestimates the hell out of me. Cus I’ll rip his toe nails off just to say I did. Tony: That doesn’t sound very pleasant. Williams: About as pleasant as a Phantom Booker promo. Tony: I assume they’re not pleasant? Williams: No, they’re just none existent. -Duke smiles at his bad joke.- Tony: I thought you already beat this joker Ty Nelson. Williams: I kicked his ass from Mobile to Memphis all the way up to Minneapolis. Tony: That’s quite the trip. Williams: He rode first class whisky bent and hell bound. Tony: Now didn’t this Booker kid own PCW at one point? Williams: Yea but he’s a half ass businessman. Who in their right mind would let a company with the power of PCW slip away from you? How can you let Ty Nelson of all people outsmart you? This guy is a blithering boob. I’ve had the chance to speak with him a few times on PTC MALICE on fancast.com at 11est every Saturday night. -Duke smiles at his cheap plug.- Williams: You know what he offers to the show?? Tony: Inside information? Williams: You would think! But no, he gives us the death of the ratings every weekend. Tony: Sounds like a real yokel. Williams: Yea and the funny thing is, he’s the guy I like best in this match. Tony: The others must be real pieces of work. Williams: You can say that again. Tony: What’s the deal with that little lady your with? Williams: That’s Kesha and she’s a hot piece of ass. Tony: Is it true what they say? Williams: Hoss!!! Once you go black you don’t go back! -Tony finishes up Duke’s hair and the camera cuts away. We rejoin Duke in his famous leather chair in his private room. He has a cigar in his mouth and talks directly to the camera.- Williams: My hairy ass hillbillies I’m telling you this is a dream come true!! I get to step in the ring with Ty Nelson and the deck stacked against him? The Phantom Booker hates Nelson. Willie Powell thinks Nelson is about as cool as fubu pants on a white guy. The Malice Man knows Nelson is an aids infected, corporate criminal, with a mind about as sharp as Roseanne Bars ass. This guy is a proven failure who fails time and time again. PCW’s cockroach, the insect you can’t get rid of. Nelson I can’t wait to get in the ring with you three on one. -Duke takes a drag of punch cigar.- Williams: You know what I think of the Ultimate Authority? I think they f*cking blow and they’re your worst stable ever. Ya hear me Nelson? Huh?? Did you hear me tell you like it is?? They f*cking blow. -Duke reaches under his chair and pulls out a megaphone and speaks into it for the whole world to hear.- Williams: THEY F*CKING BLOW!!! WORST NELSON STABLE EVER!!!! -Duke puts the megaphone down and laughs as he’s obviously a tad high.- Williams: Nelson you know what your problem is?? Besides liking boys…..besides your animal fetish…besides the fact your ugly….besides your best ally is Kannon…..no, not that either. Your problem is you don’t talk enough. Come on you filthy f*ck say something. -Duke yells at the camera like its Nelson.- Williams: What’s the deal with this Powell kid??? Why do you brag about getting p*ssy in Iowa? Hoss, that’s about the equivalent of being the most educated in Alabama. It just ain’t something you want others to know. SO shut up and maybe everybody will forget. Christ you kids don’t know sh*t these days. -Duke spits into a can.- Williams: Then there is The Booker. Can’t wait to get into the ring with this f*cking mute. If only the Phantom Phive were half as cool as the Jackson Five. But they’re not. Booker you get in my way and your way will be Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound….and hoss that’s the honky tonk truth. -The camera fades thankfully ending this pathetic promo….sorry.-
