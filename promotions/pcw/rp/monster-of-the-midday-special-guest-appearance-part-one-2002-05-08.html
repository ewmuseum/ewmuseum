Monster of the Midday. Special Guest Appearance. Part One.

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/08/2002 *We open in an unusual place. Well, at least as far as a promo goes. There sitting on a couch, leather and brown, is former 2 time World Champion, Chris Sloboda, known as simply Boda. He sits there, listening to the talk show that is on the air.* Host: So, we are going to go to a break, and when we return....... The last man to bring any time of World Championship to Orlando is here, in the studio. Co-host: Yeah, and I tell you what.... me and him, we go back a few years. Woman Host: D.J, you lie through your rotten green tooth. Host: Okay, lets get to that commercial. *Some commercial for a local Orlando business airs. The door to the room that Boda is in opens, and in walks a 6’0 blonde headed young man, known to the thousands of listeners to the show as Tuddle. Now Tuddle, is sort of Miyagawa-esq. A man, small in stature, small in mind. Thinks he is the one, a go getter, who for reasons unreasonable to the rest of mankind, is not afraid to back down. That is his downfall, as Tuddle’s a wimp, a tiny Tim if you must. There for, like Miya, Tuddle often runs his mouth, and gets himself in a sticky situation.* Tuddle: Uhm, hello. I’m Tuddle. I’m an executive intern, meaning I got more stroke than Soul Brotha Kevin. I am the man. No one is better than me. Boda: Yeah, save it boy. Time for me to go on? Tuddle: Uh-huh. *Boda follows the blonde headed little pip-squeak out of the Green Room, and into the studio. Sitting around the table, hiding almost behind all the radio equipment is the host, Russ Rollins. Russ is a rather large man, coming in at about 240 pounds. His nickname is MC Sandwich, because he loves his sandwiches. Sitting across the way, the producer and co-host, Dirty Jim. DJ is Russ’s brother in law, and claims to know every celebrity well enough to call them by some made up nickname or their first name. Also in the room, three other members of this talk show. A tall, hot blonde, with recently done boob job, named Savannah. Bubba Whoop Ass Wilson, the RedNeck Messiah sits near Russ. Bubba is a musical genius, writing and producing most of the show’s music. And then there is Bubba’s long time childhood friend, Jeff ‘The Bad Ass of DownTown’ Howell. Now this man, is a talented singer. More talented than Ricky Martin, and if you are ever in Orlando, go to Cricketer’s Arms on a Friday night, and witness that which is Jeff Howell. Finally, the three remaining members of the show, Daniel Denis and BlackBean. Daniel, about 4 foot 7, a punk little man, who runs the boards, and BlackBean, the Cuban refugee, who works for black beans. That, my friends is the make up of the hottest talk show in the south....... THE MONSTERS OF THE MIDDAY!!* Russ: Wow, he gets bigger every time he’s on the show. Sit down. Get this man a pair of headphones. *Boda sits at a chair, web cam in his face. DJ hands him a pair of headphones. Boda shakes hands with the members, gathered around the table. The intro music into the show ends, and Russ starts the segment with former World Champ Boda.* Russ: Welcome back, that of course was the Monster Song by Grumpy. They are currently recording a new album here in Orlando. But, enough about them, we have us a HUGE guest. DJ: Huge ain't the word. This man is by far the biggest person ever in this studio. Russ: Please, all welcome from the PCW, Orlando’s own son of wrestling...... Boda. *Some canned applause, typical for radio shows, is played. * Boda: Wass up all. Damn, it’s good to be back on the show. Savannah, you look fine as always. Savannah: Thank you. How are you doing? Boda: Good. Things are good in Boda’s world. Got a few possible movie roles. The recent Orlando riots missed my house, and of course..... this weekend, I end one mans wrestling career. Bubba: I used to sing in a group called Clutch, ever hear of them? Boda: No Bubba.... and you still can’t whoop my damn ass. Bubba: damn. Boda: So, Russ..... how’s it been man. Seems you guys get Carrot Top on more than me, what up with that? Russ: Hey.... you’ve always got an open invite when you are in town. Boda: I know... just giving you some [censored] DJ: Hey there potty mouth. You can’t say that on radio. Savannah: Boda, look at me. Do you think I’m pretty? Russ: Ok, Boda... just ignore the blonde retard over there. Boda: Hey, listening to her is like listening to Alex Miyagawa. It makes no sense, the words spewed from their mouths. *Laughs pour from the members, sans Savannah who gets upset. Boda apologies, saying it was all ‘in character’.* Russ: Okay, we are getting off the subject a bit. Let’s talk about the possibility that this weekend, might be your last wrestling match in PCW. Boda: Yeah.... it could possibly happen, and then again..... The Devil Rays might be World Champions too. DJ: Ouch. Boda: You see, this match on Sunday... it’s alot more than just a loser leaves PCW match. It’s got a bigger story behind it. There are two names in PCW that have been foundations for the company for it’s almost 2 year existence. The first one.... Alexander Miyagawa. The second.... your looking at him. This match represents the last of the ‘Old Schoolers’. This match is about respect. This match.... is about family. So, you see.... there is a lot more on the line than just a career. Bubba: So, how big is this Miyawhatever? Boda: About 5’10, about 230 pounds. DJ: So, you outweigh him by about what? 100 pounds? Russ: Actually, looking at the tales of the tape here..... Alexander Miyagawa 5’10, 230 pounds. Chris ‘Boda’ Sloboda 6’11, 350 pounds. You are a monster compared to this man. So, there isn’t any reason to think you’d lose to him? Boda: If I lose to him... I deserve to retire. Daniel: So, this is a one on one, standard match? Boda: Well, loser leaves PCW too. BlackBean: That’s boring. Why not spice it up? Boda: Like what? A hardcore match? Been there, got the scars from that. DJ: What about a bull rope match? Boda: Nah... to WWF. Russ: How bout a sandwich platter match? *Dead Air, as the host Russ drools thinking about a huge ass sandwich.* Boda: Ok.... tell you what.... how bout this.... would you all get the PPV if I wrestled Miya in a ‘Submission Finisher Match, Loser leaves PCW’? *The show erupts. They love the thought of that very stipulation. Blackbean reminds Russ that in order to pay the bills, they must go to commercial and play a sponsor plug.* Russ: Allright... Boda, you got time to hang right? Boda: Sure. Russ: We need to go to commercial. But when we return... more from Boda, plus your phone calls at [detail withheld], or [detail withheld], and *1041 on your Bell South phone. *The show goes to commercial. This one about the upcoming PPV for PCW. Then onto a few more commercials.* [to be continued]
