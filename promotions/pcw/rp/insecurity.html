Date: I&#039;m going die It is labeled by those who know of its existence as “The Most Dangerous Place on Earth.” A brisk gust rushes over the face of the jagged “island,” although the Warden is undaunted by the bumps that surface all over his body. Cool waves of sea breeze are the last thing on his mind right now. Nerves racked to the point of agony, he bites his lip, and twiddles anxious fingers deep within his trench coat pockets. Forming a barricade in front of their superior, heavily armored guards stand shoulder to shoulder, ready to perform the duty at hand under any means necessary. There are about 15 of them with a seemingly endless resource of tools to enhance their chances, all at a holsters distance away, yet they’re still shaking at the knees. Tasers, blunt striking objects, knifes, tear gas grenades, tranquilizer rifles, and should the danger get too out of hand, a .45 Caliber tucked into hiding beneath the bulletproof breast plate. A small spec at first approaching the massive rock at sea turns out to be the helicopter they had dreadfully expected with a special “delivery.” Given the nod by the Warden, a lone guard paces to the center of the heli-pad, points a flare straight towards the sky, and fires. Minutes later, the double bladed cargo chopper levitates above the helipad, preparing to descend… “This is going to be hell.” Thinks the Warden, his heart pounding a million miles per hour. Ten+ feet above the pad, the carrier is almost within contact of the smooth landing point. A safe landing seems definite until a violent ruckus is heard within the airborne vehicle, causing the whole thing to tilt back and forth rigorously. Scattering, the entire lot of guards dash in random directions to avoid the now uncontrollably spinning chopper, hitting the deck just before it crashes upside down. Looking up from his position on the pad, the Warden watches as jagged shrapnel just misses him overhead, and thanks his own God that he was belly down. The twisted propeller blades throw dust and debris about into the form of blinding clouds, and it’s hard to make out the condition of the Helicopter. Petrified, the men stare in silence for a while, waiting for the chaos to settle. The wreckage seems to be devoid of any survivors, as they soon see it for themselves, approaching with caution. Mutilated into a mangled hunk of steel, the crash landed transport shows damage of all sorts: Large indentations in the sides from impact, windows shattered into grains that crunch under the guard’s boots, and fuel leaks as a trickle down the sides of its rear flank. Getting up to one knee the Warden watches from a distance, his face blackened by smoke and his temple drips a stream of blood. This isn’t what he had expected, but was also exactly what he had dreaded all at once. Now the men have surrounded the downed craft, and are closing in when suddenly it shifts, a hair-raising clatter from within. Jolted into shifting its weight around aimlessly like a dying beast, the craft is rocked around by an unknown force inside, and a monstrous bellow echoes through the sky. The sliding side hatch dislodges from its threshold following a loud bang, and drops freely to the concrete below with a clang. A blood red glow emits from the gaping wound in the helicopters side, and tumbling out a cast iron cage that has the persona of a wild beast. Whatever is inside is having a fit, a shill roar testifying to that fact, and the momentous fury inside the cage is set on severing the shackles of imprisonment. The Warden watches in horror as, one by one, the misfortunes transpire: Foolishly a handful of his men rush the cage with their Tasers, driving them through the thin slots only to be jolted themselves, and hurled clear off the helipad. Others run, leaving 3 to try and stop the mayhem, alone. The 3 reveal the sheathed .45’s, strictly forbidden before hand by the Warden, and aim at the metallic crate, planning to take action into their own hands. Driven by fear they fire with futile effect, as taking aim at a moving, thinly slotted set of air holes would be. Bullets ricocheting, they fail to see what the Warden does… The bolt that is giving way, which is the only thing holding the hinged door of this cage shut. He wants to scream “No!” Warn the guards somehow. But, nothing comes out. As the cage begins to expand, the Warden somehow finds the strength to reach into his trench coat, and pull out a walkie-talkie calling for more back up… If they aren’t on the way already. …A shrieking, inhuman clap of cackling, deafening to the eardrum laughs at the Warden.One week later… Welcome to the Camelot Maximum Security Containment Facility! Population: Expanding, but never ever decreasing! You won’t know how to get here, and you definitely wont know how to get out! The UN founded C.M.S.C.F. in recent years, shall we say 20XX? It was designed by the alliance of governments to hold the most devious and diabolical of criminals across the globe in secret captivity. Essentially, the facility itself finds its foundation upon a man made “rock” located somewhere remotely in the Pacific (Ocean). There’s a single way to get to it, and that’s by condoned aircraft arrival to the helipad perched high atop the prison. Not too deep into the bowels of Camelot’s holding area, Level 1 to be exact, there’s a little commotion going on inside a closet. Struggling against the rope that tightly binds his wrists and ankles together behind his back (Hog-tied); a guard of Camelot tries to blurt hysterical calls for help. Unfortunately for him, his strong voice is muffled by a thick piece of duct tape. The perpetrators are doing a likewise job on his unconscious partner close beside him. It isn’t until the two mysterious men are done that he sees the most bizarre and unsettling sights in his life: A mirror image of himself and his downed ally. They flick off the light switch of the janitor’s closet, and shut the door behind them leaving the baffled guard in the darkness. I yearn to find a being… Curiously, these imposters begin to roam the Level 1 holding area. I have heard tales of a “Witch.” Some say she can foresee the future, and others say she can change it. Four stories high, this sector of the C.M.S.C.F. is a grand hallway with walls patterned with prison cell wallpaper, the standard vertical bars, holding one bed, and one toilet per bad guy. The only difference about Camelot’s prisoners is the fused shock collar leashed around their neck. Should one of these monstrous criminals actually escape from his/her cell, then somehow make it out of the indoor portion of the facility, an invisible fence on the perimeter coast (Of the rock) would trigger the one time use collar to release 400 kHz… I need both, as a matter of fact. Flickering from cell to cell the pair of undetected intruder’s scan the Level 1 holding area, searching for someone. Or something… I do know that somewhere in this prison, she lies in wait, longing for freedom. And, I plan to find her, after I sift through these pathetic losers. Passing a cell, one of the suspiciously roaming guards catches a glimpse of a peculiar captive inside of it. This prisoner is a deformity, one side of his face disfigured and the other fine, both evenly split down the middle by straight defined line. His more human eye focuses on a coin lying (Scratched) face up in the center of his palm, while the bulging yellow eye, rippling with spidery, blue veins wanders about, watchfully. Two Face: “(In a tone that shows much civility.) So, what do you think my most trusted advisor? Should we enjoy the time far from crime, here in this nice cushy cot… (Now, suddenly his voice sounds inhuman, as if there were a mutant frog lodged in it.) …Or ShOuLd We BuSt OuT fRoM tHiS fReAk HoLe, AnD gEt AwAy FrOm ThEsE nOsEy BaStArDs?!” One of the voices orders the other to “call it,” and soon the coin pelts the stone floor with a ping. As the guard listens to the reaction in the cell, far behind him, he sneers with disgust. “WhAt Do YoU mEaN fIgUrE iT oUt My SeLf?! YoU’rE fIrEd!” Pathetic… Having not given any thought to their suspicious frolics, it never crosses the pair’s mind that they might look a little conspicuous. Having a little bit too much fun, one of the imposters immaturely taunts the likes of a ferocious, saber-toothed man beast that swipes angrily at his face. Just out of the beast’s long clawed reach, the goof lets his tongue hang out, and prances about in a mocking fashion. Little does he suspect the approaching guard whom, among others has been watching closely, until it’s too late. Swung roughly around by his shoulder, and lifted by his collar to his tipi-toes, this obnoxious little fiend finds himself eye to eye with an uptight, clean-cut guard. Guard: “What the hell is wrong with you, Gary?! You’ve been acting like this is some kind of amusement park all goddamn morning! Sabertooth is a dangerous murderer… He’d shred you to bits dammit!” Undaunted at first, “Gary” turns his head back to look over his shoulder at Sabertooth who still reaches for his through the bars, flailing without success. Sabertooth: “(*GRRRRRRRRR!*)” Then he returns his view, indignantly furious, to the big guard that holds him tightly by the collar. Who does this foolish, infidel think he is, speaking down upon me? My co-worker? Bah, I say, as there will be no “Co”-anyone to take away from my supreme rule when I should take my throne! He cannot stop me, for I am… Wait. He bought the disguise. Well then, I must be inconspicuous, for there is no trap I can’t escape. No mind I can’t outsmart. No good doer I can’t defeat. I and I alone am the greatest mastermind of evil the world has ever… Guard: “Ahh! (Clenching teeth, letting a heinous hiss emerge from his mouth.) You little… Someone stop him! He’s gone insane!” Hopping in agonized pain on one foot, clutching his shin, the guard sounds off the rest of the prison before collapsing under his own weight to the grate floor. Now the security, as well as most of the prisoners, have turned their attention to the obscurely out of normal guard who is making a mad dash for… Well, anywhere to prolong his charade. Why am I endangering my own evil freedom so carelessly? Pressing their faces as far as they possibly can through the cold metal bars, all the prisoners are trying to see the action for themselves, and there is just one word on all their insane, homicidally evil minds. “JAILBREAK!!” Unable to suppress a small grin upon hearing all these freaks cheer him on, he continues to run, heading towards the end of Level 1’s corridor, witch is marked with a massive gate. 3 feet thick, the titanium gate is labeled “LEVEL 2,” and is overseen by a single armed guard. All of a sudden, blindly caught in the heat of the moment, this loose cannon realizes his partner in crime is nowhere to be found. He can’t stop, not with all the guards on his tail, and the guard at the gate is bound to think something of the pack pursuing this oncoming threat. The answer is very simple, really. Unsheathing an Uzi from its resting position, the guard at the Level 2 gate takes aim. Guard at Gate: “(At the top of his lungs.) Halt! Halt, now!” Prisoners: “(In unison.) Jailbreak! Jailbreak! Jailbreak!” An easy target, dashing right in line with his scope along the grate floor, the renegade guard doesn’t stop, forcing the Uzi bearer to regretfully tighten his squeeze on the trigger… “No, I believe that is quite enough for now.” The barrel of his rapid-fire projectile is suddenly weighted down, allowing the startled guard only a few shots that misfire, and ricochet off the grated floor. Releasing the firearm, he allows the hand clasped on the barrel to pull it away, and drop into to the floor with a clang. I desperately desire an answer, myself. The Level 1 sector, wall to wall, not one person says a word. Total silence and awe ruminate, filling the area well over the brim. Standing there, just as rough-cut, and grimy looking as ever, the Warden stares down the Guard, his eyes ablaze with fire. Anger, one of the Warden’s trademarks along with the usual scruffy 5 o’clock shadow, the rugged “flat top” crew cut, his dirty old trench coat, and of course, a stubby cigar clamped tightly between his back teeth. Guard: “W-w-w-Warden?! I thought…” Warden: “(In a jagged Texan voice.) Can’t you see that this man is in a hurry to help me down in Level 3?!” The Warden points back to the strangely out-of-whack guard who now watches from within a circle of other guards, barring him in with clubs. Gate Keeper: “(Gulps hard, slightly confused like everyone else.) …Him?” Warden: “OPEN THE GATE!” Moments later, the Warden and his guard assistant are stepping through a wide open door, and into Level 2. Nudging the Warden, “Gary” murmurs something… “Gary”: “(Softly.) Voice distortion?” The Warden nods. Level 1 is silent still, even in their absence, but not for long. Disappointed by the “Jailbreak,” the prisoners need something else to entertain them… “FOOD FIGHT!” Level 2 is much different. No more collars, but now a custom cell for each and every prisoner, meant to hold him or her in by suppressing their different… Abilities. The answer I desire is more so a prediction, than anything else. You see, lately a lot of critics have been choosing a man named Stryfe over me. Why? There aren’t as many cells here, nor guards. But, as the fakes pass through there is still a sense of extreme confusion. Not for them, but coming from everyone else. Maybe because he was a former world champ. Not that that has any credibility anymore… They pass a vaulted cell door where a bluish flicker illuminates the cell inside. No limit to what he might do just to have a moment of purpose again, the bolt throwing Electro releases massive wattage amounts of voltage to try and crush the walls that contain him, in vain. Rubber prison… But, I hath defeated this fool once… I can most utterly crush him again! Two reptilian creatures, Killer Croc and The Lizard lay in their cell, completely drained of any will to stay conscious. Their cold blood can’t help them in a frosted over fridge. Yes, Stryfe… I will find this Witch, and she will tell me what I must know. Another cell, water sprays down from the ceiling like rain constantly, leaving any chance for the fire builder, Pyro to cause any trouble totally washed up. He sits in the corner, knees tucked to his chest, miserable now, but dreaming of the day that he might wield the blade of fire again. Why must I know? Distracted by an endless pursuit of a pork chop, the incredibly unmovable, incredibly unshakable… oh, lets just say the fattest ass around. Anyway, he spends his days in his cell on a treadmill pointlessly chasing a pork chop strung up in front of his face. I don’t know… Stupid civilians have me believing their predictions! And in another, Mr. Freeze sits in his boxers; the cold temp in his cell is all that keeps him alive. To leave would be fatal. But, call me insecure, and I’ll wipe you off the face of the Earth. My Earth, soon. Clayface: Giant play-doh set. Sandman: Air tight cell, no cracks. Hydro-man: Same thing. Scarecrow: Fire prison. Finally, they make it to Level 3. The guard had given them a hard time, just because he had seemed so utterly bewildered by the Warden’s presence. Warden: “What are you waiting for? Let us in!” Guard: “I thought… I thought you had decided to retire, sir!” Warden: “When? Why? (Just as baffled.)” Guard: “Last week… You said it wasn’t worth it with freaks like that witch coming in, sir! You got scared out of your job!” Warden: “(Gulps, nervous now.) Maybe… we shouldn’t go… heh.” But, his partner in crime “persuaded” him, and now they are finally at the end of the road. Level 3, of C.M.S.C.F. Level three isn’t much to look at. Actually, here there are only 5 cells. A massive titanium vault door, extremely thick, and extremely impenetrable shields each one. TBC
