An Eruption of Flavor

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/10/02 Thud. Thud. The sound of a body hitting the floor.........but not in your usual fashion. Thud. Thud. Again. Again. Through a poorly lit corridor she journeys...........finally reaching her destination. Bethany peeks her head out to behold the masses..........the slovenly and excited. Packed tightly like sardines, waiting to be devoured and defecated out............yes, this should prove fitting. Bethany: Wake up lazy ass! The bitch goddess gazes down upon her lover, a burned and scarred demon known only as Clyde. Currently he has fallen under a bizarre spell..........rather than really do, well, anything it behooves him to sleep instead. Bethany: Graaagh! Ripping at her own hair, Bethany looks to him...........resting on a cot with blankets pulled tightly around his body. At the foot of it stands the rope with which she was using to drag his carcass to and fro........now which she uses to strangle him as she straddles his chest. Bethany: WAKE UP OR I'LL KILL YOU DEAD!!!!!!!! Clyde: (Choking) Gaaaak! The burned man throws his arms madly, eyes bulging as his supply of air is cut off.........finally a wild thwack sends his eight year old lover flying across the room, tumbling through the curtain which separates the backstage area from prime time. Ricardo Montalban: Yes Tattoo, call in now and you too may enjoy my special Montalban lubrication. For the next twenty minutes I will be offering double servingsAH!!!!!!! The sexually deviant old man is cut off as a small form barrels into him, knocking him over. His jar of product flies from his grasp, landing on a young man sitting on the front row and drenching his face in an odd white liquid. Bethany: (Pulling herself up) Oops. Bethany looks to the fellow in the audience, as the white goop spills down and obscures the letters on his t-shirt. The camera pans to it, closing in on the words 'H* My Na*e Is By**o* beneath the product of Ricardo Montalban's love. Bethany: Hey I know that guy. Suddenly out of nowhere charges a fellow in a black trenchcoat, stringy black hair billowing behind him and wearing an 'I Love Verizon' shirt. He gets up in the drenched young man's face, snapping a picture, sneering and vanishing just as quickly as he came. Bethany: Uh, yeah. Anyway.......... Dusting herself off, Bethany looks to her right and then her left. With an expression of exasperation she stares.........then marches off stage through the curtains again, out of camera range. Bethany: (Muffled) I told you to wake the hell up! Clyde: (Muffled) Meatloaf again? But we just had that yesterday.......... Bethany: (Muffled) GAAAAAH!!!!!!! Another series of thuds..........and finally the terrible twosome emerges, Bethany dragging the cot containing the lazy killer known only as Clyde behind her. With a grunt of effort she props it up behind the podium in the center of the stage as the crowd cheers. Crowd: Clyde! Clyde! Clyde! Clyde! Clyde! Clyde: (Waving sleepily) Thank you, for my next number I'll be doing While the City Sleeps on a banjo........... Bethany: Goddammit! With a mighty slap to the back of his head Bethany awakens the dozing psychopath at last.........blinking several times he smiles out at the crowd. Clyde: ...........Bethany where the hell am I? Bethany: At an infomercial taping on Channel 983. We're commandeering it since you were too lazy to actually shill your soft drink and since your........actually to hell with that, MY career is resting on your winning. Clyde: (Yawning) You know good and well neither of us have a career. Let me sleep. Bethany: (Pulling his hair) NO! Clyde: AGGGH! Ignoring the burned and sleepy man, Bethany climbs over the podium and grins. Bethany: Heya everybody. We're here today to introduce you..........yes all of you to an exciting new product! But what is it I hear you asking? Ricardo Montalban: Breakaway assless chaps? Clyde: (Drooling) Poison Sleepytime Lemonade? Bethany: (Staring at Clyde) No, it's better than that. It's......... The little girl crouches beneath the podium briefly.........then reemerges, holding a bottle by her cheek and grinning cheesily. Bethany: Sobe! Bethany holds her pose, and is met..........by complete silence. For nearly a minute she stands there...........the audible sounds of crickets chirping ring forth. Bethany: ..........bastards. Thinking quickly, Ricardo Montalban leans close to the microphone and speaks. Ricardo Montalban: Yes Tattoo, but is is not just Sobe. It is an exciting new flavor! Bethany: That's right Ricardo! This new blend will soon become the official soft drink of Primetime Championship Wrestling! Clyde: (Mutters and mumbles) Bethany: (Kicking him) Wake up! Clyde: (Blinking) Wha? Oh yes. Shaking his head, our highly lazy friend takes the bottle from his short sex kitten and displays it for all to see. Clyde: (Yawning) But don't take my word for it folks..........my good friend the Violator is passing out free samples as we speak. Bethany: New Vesuvius Surprise Sobe is SO tasty we guarantee you'll enjoy it..........and if you don't we personally will pay for a lifetime supply of it! Clyde: But if they don't like it why the hell would they want.......... Bethany: (Kicking) Shush plothole boy! Clyde: Ow! We zoom over to witness the huge man known as the Violator strolling up and down the aisles, wearing a large Sobe sash over his chest and handing out small cups filled with the delicious drink. Finally with all of them handed out he gives a thumbs up to Clyde, grins. Violator: Prepare for total violation! Clyde: (Mumbling) Yeah..........hunan chicken and rice.........blah......... Bethany: WAKE THE HELL UP!!!!!!!!! Full of rage, the little girl breaks the glass bottle she had been displaying over the burned man's head............which oddly enough begins to smoke and fizzle. Ricardo Montalban: Tattoo I believe that was not a wise course of action. Bethany: Shut it Ricardo! A series of shrieks and screams cut off the goings on upon the stage..........the camera pans over to the captive audience. Their sample drinks downed, they now clutch their heads and wave in unison, a bizarre dance. Clyde: (Snoring) Dvon, get the umbrella. Bethany: BITCH! The little girl yanks Clyde down to the floor, as one after another the audience members become aware of the true 'surprise' of Vesuvius Surprise. Their heads pop like watermelons at a Gallagher show, one after another..........in a neat little line, sending pus and skull fragments in every direction. A rain of gore, cascading and spiraling downward............pop, pop, pop. Again, again again............at last our hapless cameraman, enjoying a sample of his own glances over. Shaking his head he lowers his lens.........then screams, clutching his temple as his skull explodes like a mighty volcano and his camera falls to the floor, our image fading to static and leaving only sound. Bethany: Goddammit you lazy son of a bitch, you did it again! Clyde: (Snores) Violator: Prepare for total violation! Bethany: AAAAAAAARRRRGGGGHH!!!!!!!!!!
