Date: TODAY (Get working, match writer. Good luck Ross, I gave it my all. :)) Fredrick Thomas? Another name popped into my head. Who was this Fredrick Thomas? I stopped writing in my hournal for a second. How did I ever end up finding out about my brother, Sven? All these names at the time made no sense and yet, they all fit together in the end. As I look back on it now, I scold myself for not being able to figure out all the facts sooner. I possibly could've figured out the mission and would have had PCW to look foreward to. But no, I had to play the dumb one and go through ALL the facts. I stared down at my journal. Almost everybody in the whole entire world does not know of what peril this Project 84 could've done. I basically saved thousands of people and nobody knows it. When will I get recognized in this world? I know when. When I win the PCW Rising Star title. Not until then will I get the fame that I thoroughly deserve. Anyway, back to the recap. With Henry Claval's name, I now had another name - 'Fredrick Thomas'. Name: Fredrick Thomas Age: ??? Other Aliases: Ivan Chistov, Fred Mystique, ???, ???, ??? Occupation: ??? Other Information: Fredrick Thomas resides currently in Germany where he is a priest at a local church. Fredrick was framed by a Henry Claval a few years back and vowed to get revenge. Fredrick lost connection with Claval in 2001 and went to his home town to become a priest and change his ways. No information on whether Thomas is still in Germany. Now, I was confused as ever. I had gotten another one of Ivan Chistov's aliases, which made three or half of the names I needed. I really didn't know what to do, until it hit me in the head. I was to goto Germany and find the house where Fredrick Thomas was supposed to be staying. There, I was hoping I could find yet another lead. And another lead, I did find. At the house, I didn't find Ivan Chistov, but instead the 'caretaker', Clair Rivence. Clair seemed like a nice lady and I had nothing against her. I had nothing on her, that is until I entered her name into the database: Name: Clair Rivence Age: 36 Occupation: Secretary, Undercover Spy Email Adress: [detail withheld] Other Information: Clair was more of a teacher's pet in school but when both of her parents died in a house fire, she found the wrong crowd. On a new path of hatred and evil, Clair found a spy, Henry Claval. Claval, who is considered armed and dangerous, brought Rivence under his wing when he was looking for some new workers. Clair and Henry's other top student, Ivan Chistov teamed up together on a Nazi mission but their plan was foiled when the CIA found out. Ever since then, Clair has resided in a small town in Germany, acting as an innocent, poor church women who takes care of the priest's house. Recently, when Ivan returned to Germany, the two were in suspicion of plotting another plan but since then, Ivan has disappeared. No other information available at this time. That's right, the nice, innocent Clair Rivence was in with Chistov and Claval. She was just another part in the game. Besides Clair's profile, I still had no lead. I didn't know any more of Chistov's aliases and to be frank, I was sort of scared. My PCW career had hit a peak at the time and I needed to consentrate on that, before anything else. The next clue came when I signed onto my AIM. Since I had figured out Clair's email adress, I hoped that their would be a chance that I could talk to Clair. I did talk to her. In the 20 minute talk with her, I had figured out the next aliases last name. Gordan. Now all I needed was a first name. Through some extensive research, I knew Chistov's fourth alias, Terry Gordan. Gordan was in fact the fourth alias of Ivan Chistov. Now, all I needed was Chistov's fifth alias. To figure this out, I went all the way back to Germany to the same house I was at where I had met Clair. At the house, I didn't find anything. Dissapointed, I went to the back of the house where a door was open in the back of the church. As quiet as I could, I listened into the conversation between the two of whome I recognized as Clair Rivence and Henry Claval. The two were talking about 'Project 84', the next big thing. It was a bomb that was very deadly and dangerous. When the two went out of the secret room they had been in, I sneeked in and took the papers for 'Project 84'. After reading a very lengthly thing on the history of Project 84, I was scared but excited. I was scared because the bomb that was called Project 84 could wipe out New York in a second. I didn't know what to do. On the other hand, I was shocked to say the least. I had figured out Chistov's fifth alias. Sven Neirlinger. My own brother who had once tried to kill me was in on this plan all along. I wouldn't let my own brother take advantage of me and I needed to stop him. I had read in the booklet for Project 84 that I had stolen that in order for the bomb to go off correctly, fuel gas was needed to 'power up' the bomb. Where else would there be lots of fuel, than at Cloud Nine, the PCW airplane that I would be boarding. The ship needed tons of fuel and I knew that was where I needed to go. I quickly got into my rental car and drove to where Cloud Nine was being fueled. When I was there, who else should I run into than my own brother. Sven was perched up on the top of the semi and with a rifle in hand. I had spotted Henry and Clair in the distance, putting gas into a container. The bomb was beside them. Running away from Sven almost got me killed. He fired at me a bunch of shots that I managed to avoid. In the distance, I shot at Henry and Clair to let them know I met business. After just a short number of shots with my gun, Clair went down. She was dead. I still regret killing Clair but business is business. Henry quickly fled the scene and tried to take the bomb with him. I wouldn't have it and shot at the bomb, sending a small explosion through the scene and making Henry run for his life. Henry Claval and my brother, Sven fled in a helicopter and I had stopped them. Picking up the bomb, I was still being shot at. A bullet hit the box of the bomb and sent an explosion through my ears... And here I am now, in this hospital. I'll be fine for the Rising Star title match and I hope that Edwards knows I mean business. Will I win the Rising Star title? Can I defeat James Edwards? I'll just have to wait and see...
