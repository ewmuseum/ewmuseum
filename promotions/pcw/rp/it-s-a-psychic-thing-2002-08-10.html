It's A Psychic Thing

10 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/10/02 Fast asleep in confinement is little Grimer, whom we haven't heard much from or about for quite a while. He's grown a few inches since we last saw him and he still looks healthy and happy which is always good to see. He only moves once to scratch behind his ear the whole time the camera is on him. Suddenly the attention is taken away and is now pointed towards Kramer who has just run into the room. Kramer's eyes are bulging out of his sockets as he peers into Grimer's cage. His baggy blue jeans catch a snag on Grimer's cage, which causes it to shake a bit waking Grimer up. Seeing Grimer move, Kramer seems relieved as he lets out a huge sigh. Kramer: You're alive little buddy!! Kramer digs into the cage and snatches up the tired mass and hugs him. He then lays him back down and lets Grimer get back to his slumber as he leaves the room. Kramer: That psychic was full of more stuff than a piñata. There has been absolutely no danger towards someone or something close to me. Even when I asked her what my future looked like for PCW she said it was bright and that I would be on top of the business. Now we all know that ain't happening. Kramer starts laughing hysterically at the thought as he makes his way downstairs. He grabs his car keys off of the coffee table in the living room and heads outside, of course locking the door on his way out. He opens the driver side door of his black truck and hops in. Before he turns on the car he just sits there with both hands on the steering wheel thinking about the psychic. Kramer: Can anyone become a psychic? I mean how hard is it to say that someone has riches coming there way, or will fall in love? That's all I ever get when I call and neither has come my way. Maybe I'm in the wrong business.....I mean wrestling is great but sitting at home in my underwear telling someone over the phone that they will meet the man or woman of their dreams in the near future, seems a heck of a lot easier. He kind of shrugs it off as he revs up the car remembering why he had gotten into the car in the first place. The scene fades to black shortly and reopens half an hour later in a rest home where Walter (Kramer's father) now resides. Walter moved in about a month ago after he figured out how easy he would have it there. His favorite thought of course was having a young nurse scrub him down from head to toe. He may be an old pervert but whatever makes the old man happy right? Kramer sits in the old creaky rocking chair by the window as his father is sprawled out on the bed with his hands behind his head looking up at the ceiling. Walter: You haven't been doing too bad for yourself in PCW. Kramer: I have my days, but more often than not they suck. I'm just surprised that I am still around after so long. I mean I am sure there are other guys dying to get into PCW who have a lot more to offer. Walter: But how many of them will be Indian, and get pumped up by buckets of chicken? Kramer: You never know with people today. Walter: Well you shouldn't say stuff like that or your will find your ass without a job. I mean what would happen if someone heard you say that? Kramer: Good point. Kramer looks straight at the camera and starts talking. Kramer: Umm.....forget what I said. I am the best in PCW, and I have held belts, and uh represent the company well? Kramer shoots a questioning glance trying to prove that he is still worthy of a job and that he doesn't want to get fired. Although he is far from the best in PCW, he hopes it will help him out a bit. A few seconds of pure silence pass by and he turns back to his dad resuming their conversation as though nothing out of the ordinary had happened. Walter: Everyone else seems to be giving their opinions, so what do you think about Trigger getting a shot at Kannon for the biggest title in PCW? Kramer: Trigger is a decent guy I suppose but no way in the hell will he beat Kannon! Well let's look at it this way. Tomorrow night I take on Trigger and if he can't even beat me, how can he expect to beat someone of Kannon's caliber? Walter: What if Trigger does beat you? Kramer: He still doesn't have a chance no matter how you slice it. I mean Kannon has years of experienced stacked up and Trigger is bound to make a lot of rookie mistakes. The pressure will just be too much. Walter: Enough talk about Trigger......let's talk about my new nurse Nancy. Isn't she a sight? Kramer: I must say I was shocked when I first saw her. Walter: The woman is a miracle! Kramer: So what happened to Helga the old nurse you had? Walter: I paid her off. I told her if she got me a nice young dame then she could have a date with you. Kramer jumps up to his feet with anger and surprise. Kramer: You told her what? Walter: Oh right, she's picking you up next Wednesday at eight. She said to wear your wrestling outfit because it makes her tingly inside if you know what I mean. Kramer: Did you ever stop to think about how I would feel? I mean the woman is twice my age! Walter: But look at Nancy. Wouldn't you have done the same thing? The young and flawless nurse walks into the room in her white uniform, which fits nicely along her slim body. All Kramer can do is gawk at her as he is simply blown away by how pretty she is. Kramer: Hell yeah I would have done it! Nancy: Done what? Walter: Oh we were just having a little dispute. Nancy: I just came in to tell you it's time for your sponge bath Walter. Walter: It's that time already? Go start filling up the bathtub toots, I'll be there in a few minutes. Nancy walks away and both Kramer and Walter's eyes follow her into the bathroom. Walter: See what did I tell you? This place is amazing! Kramer: Well I guess I should be going....sorry I couldn't stay around longer. Walter: Feel free to visit any time son. Kramer: Remember don't do anything I wouldn't do. Walter: Son that's why you're still a virgin! Kramer's face goes red in embarrassment as he hurries out of the room. The scene slowly fades to black as he heads on down the hall. The part of the conversation we didn't catch was Walter's thoughts on psychics and how he had been one for a short time. Kramer and Walter then discussed which kind of psychic was better, the ones you call, or the one's at carnivals. They decided that the carnival psychics were more believable, besides the crystal balls are cool. Another day in the life of Kramer and like always some parts just didn't make sense. Until next time.....
