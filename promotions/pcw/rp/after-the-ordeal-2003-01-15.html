...After The Ordeal

15 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15 - 01 - 03 The last time we saw Gaz Green alias 'The Jester' he had catalogued the previous six months of his life, be it the end of an era, NTICW and his emergence alongside Nerezza at 'Third Dawning'. The debut of Pranks With Attitude on Primetime Championship Wrestling television saw them invade the PCW Tag Team title match under the Asylum banner. With so much to catalogue, so many twists and turns the real story lay with the teams demise as Gaz 'The Jester' Green sold out to The Front Office temptation causing a rift between himself and former friend Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. The last six months seem so insignificant to the seven days that have just passed! Opening on the familiar setting of Gaz Green's Las Vegas apartment, a dwelling situated in the heart of the 'City Of Sin'. Surrounded by the asset's of this buzzing City, be it casinos, hotels, the bright lights could not spurn happiness for the residents of this pad as they reflect over Nerezza's seven deadly sins he inflicted on the Conniving Collective over a course of seven eventful days. The beautiful furnishings that litter the apartment are present and correct as are the two occupants, in this case Gaz Green who sits in a trance like state at his workstation and Natalia Miyagawa who continues to relive her ordeal. 'Why me....' murmurs the Soviet Scarlet solemnly. The Jester's valet sits staring into space, her eye shadow running along her once rosy red cheeks creating a cascade of colours. Her misery filters further as her eyes flood with tears, trickling she tries desperately to hold back any full blown surge of emotion but as the seconds pass the tug-o-war begins to swing the other way. Suddenly the falling of tears is accompanied by the trembling of her lips and finally she bursts into an emotional melt down. 'WHY ME~!' roars the Russian beauty. As the flood gates open Gaz Green gazes up with a sympathetic look and standing erect he strolls over, no cocky strut, no showcase of arrogance, the Jester merely went over, draping his arm over her shoulders in an act of comfort. Cradling Natalia, Gaz comforted his valet in her hour of need, after all, her husband Alexander Miyagawa was banished from her life and ironically it was Gaz Green who exiled him from PCW with a heinous attack. 'Nerezza has gone to far this time...' hissed the Emerald Menace. Nurturing the sobbing Natalia, Gaz Green hissed his statement with an evil glint in his eye; obviously this long-standing feud was counting down to its pinnacle moment of a return match, scheduled at Shanghaied. As the emotion levels raised a shallow murmur filtered from another room, the sound of a baby crying. 'Do you want me to go?' asked the Front Office Entertainment Manager with a smile. Natalie retorted with a wave of her head and went to investigate the noise as Gaz Green retraced his steps, taking a seat back at his work station he opened the red leather book marked 'Journal' and began making his entry. The previous page, scribbled in the Jester's handwriting was a reflection of the previous six months and the conclusion of this entry stalled on the heading 'Shanghaied' obviously a good starting point to focus on. Lifting a pencil aloft, the nib finally dropped onto the blank sheet and work began... During my last entry I reviewed the previous six months and finally admitted the oppression of Nerezza who made me his own personal shadow... His own reflection so to speak. It is common knowledge that vampires don't have reflections and maybe this is why Nerezza tried to mould me in his own image but Gaz Green wasn't intended as a puppet and maybe this is why he resents me so much... There were good times granted, capturing the PCW Tag Team Championships was the light at the end of the tunnel, finally the NTICW nametag would vanish with the awful memories of that pitiful promotion. There were some fun times too, I mean we got to give Xavier Kannon some sleepless nights, we managed to get Ellie to pose in lingerie and the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtle episode was enjoyable but who masterminded every last detail... That’s right, Gaz 'The Jester' Green. Not bad for a supporting player, huh?!? When the names disappeared and the voids remained unfilled I sold out to The Front Office so that Nerezza may have a singles career. I silenced the critics, those who said we dominated a 'Dead Division' and gave the fans what they wanted, Nerezza, the vampire without the deadweight Jester and how was I repaid? I had my teeth knocked out at Decimation and my only retort was to wash Nerezza in green liquid, which spurned his ranting, no requesting, at Shanghaied he wanted the vampire to face the Jester once more. I maybe fortunate enough to lock myself behind an office door or hide behind legal red tape but that didn't stop the blood thirsty brute finding a legal loop-hole. If this hatred for me is based on jealousy then really I'm speechless.... If he resents me because Xavier Kannon wanted my services over his then, why? I haven't been issued any star treatment, title opportunities have been few and far between, and instead I can look forward to the usual array of roster oddities. 'The Has-Been Man' Duke Williams 'The Stevie Wonder Inspired' Skylar Green 'The Man Of A Thousand Fo's' Statuz Quo Skitzo... Skitzo... Skitzo... Skitzo... Skitzo... Skitzo... Skitzo... Skitzo... Skitzo... Really if you want to face this level of competition then all you had to do was say, 'Hey Jester how about letting me face them mooks!?!' Instead you continue to lust after me. I blatantly told you that I was not interested in facing you at Shanghaied but firstly you try to force the match by getting a bunch of 'Furby's' singing in unison. Then you inflict revenge by kidnapping the only thing I hold dear... No not the PCW Tag Team Championship... You took Natalia from me, against her will. Shanghaied You played the only possible trump card available to you and I commend you for making a gutsy move. I commend you further after you successfully defeated Xavier Kannon for his PCW Tag Team title and reforming the Pranks With Attitude banner but after this bout the P.W.A revival concludes. It was clever to write a clause stating that this match will be 'First Blood' but once I'm through with the pale vampire you can expect me to add a little colour to those cheeks, something Natalia is finding hard to do after you... As Gaz presses the pencil down harder in frustration the nib snaps. The Jester appears to be reflecting over his words, his perception of how these last seven days have played out when suddenly his eyes roam that of his desk and he spots a letter marked 'Gaz Green'. First inspection would deem the envelope as rather formal, hand-written, baring a Wrestle Tattler insignia in the corner, prompting the Jester to question its origin with Natalia Miyagawa. 'NATALIA....' Waltzing into the next room, Natalia and the baby are cradled asleep on the bed. A soft smile by Gaz shows a more human side to his personality and standing in the midst of the doorway he opens the letter and examines, his eyes lighting up as he recites the opening line. 'It is a pleasure to inform you that Gaz 'The Jester' Green has been inducted into the list of 'Graduates' with immediate effect by the ruling of The Phantom Booker....' Like a child on Christmas day his face turns to that of a jovial expression, mouth ajar in a smile, eyes gleaming and full of colour, his face radiant as he continues to clutch the letter tightly. His eyes roamed further, swaying side to side as though they were fixated in a pendulum motion he concludes by reading the latter half of the letter, which refers to a ceremony for the inductee's. 'Further more those inductee's will receive their rewards during a special ceremony orchestrated by the governing body of Primetime Wrestling Academy (PWA) to be held this weekend....' Finally something to relish, as Gaz Green looks forward to his moment in the Wrestle Tattler spot-light, blocking any thought of his 'First Blood' match with Nerezza in the process. With a cheeky smile adorning his face and dropping into a somewhat dream-like state, the episode finally draws to a close, another chapter in this ongoing saga done and dusted. - The End -
