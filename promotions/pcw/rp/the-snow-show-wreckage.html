Date: Season II, Episode 3 ﻿************************************The Snow ShowSeason II, Episode 3“Wreckage”************************************ Location: Marty Verrelli’s Apartment, New York CityDate: February 4, 2003Time: 3:15 am Marty Verrelli was surprised to wake up - he had thought Snow was about to kill him when hepast out earlier. Of course, it took him a moment or two to recall what had happened, but fromthe moment he woke, he felt a sense of surprise at the peace that he was resting in. It wasn’tuntil his mind sent him a flash of Jason Snow’s hating eyes that he remembered what happened. When he sat up on the floor, he realized that his left eye refused to open. Gingerly, he touchedthe skin around it and found that the swelling had long since numbed the cuts and bruises aroundit. He could feel the dry blood crack under his finger tips and he watched it flake off and flutterto the floor. When he looked around his apartment, he realized that Snow didn’t save any of his aggressionwhen he decided not to kill Marty. The apartment was a disaster; The sofa was overturned, thefront window was broken, the television and stereo were in pieces on the floor... Everything,even the dishes in the kitchen, was destroyed. He put his hands on his beaten face, wincing at the pain from his broken nose, and layed hisback down on the floor. Everything was ruined, he thought. *** Location: Snow Residence, New York City SuburbsDate: February 4, 2003Time: 12:20 pm Everything was ruined, she thought. Krista hadn’t left her room since yesterday, other than a night time trip to the bathroom. Eventhat was made after she was sure her brother-in-law was in bed. From her bedroom, she heard the fireworks explode when Raven came home from the mallyesterday. Obviously, her sister wasn’t happy when she found that every telephone in the househad been smashed to prevent Krista from calling Marty in advance. Despite Snow’s explanation,Raven failed to see this as a good reason. Krista could only hear muffled voices, however, so she never did get an idea of what her sisterthought of Snow heading over to Marty’s apartment with the intention of hurting him, or worse. She hoped Marty was still alright, but even her most optimistic side couldn’t convince her of it. Every time she closed her eyes, her brother-in-law’s face would flash in front of her, painted inits menacing hatred. She didn’t notice that her hands had been resting on her stomach for hours. Suddenly, there came a knock at the door. Krista didn’t move to answer it - it might be Jason,she thought, and she wanted nothing less than to see him right now. Raven peaked her head in around the door and gave a faint smile. She had always been morelike a mother than a sister to Krista, and this was one of those times that Krista needed a mother. More than ever, in fact, because she was now faced with becoming a mother herself. She hopedRaven would be more supportive than her husband. “Hey,” Raven said, her small features twinkling in the darkness of the room. “You ok?” “What do you think?” Krista tried to be as stubborn as possible. She wanted nothing more thanto convey her hurt to her sister. Raven might understand, she thought. “You haven’t come out of your room all day...” “It wasn’t worth it,” Krista answered. Raven sighed. “He loves you, Krista,” she said as she took a seat next to her younger sibling onthe bed. “He just doesn’t know how to show it... well, not properly anyway. You’re like adaughter to him, you know? The only real difference is that he didn’t get eased into these years,like most parents do. Instead of having a baby, he had a teenager with you. He doesn’t knowhow to handle that sometimes.” “Stop making excuses for him!” Krista said sharply, though her voice remained quiet. “I’m not...” Raven started to say, but Krista was right and she knew it. “He cares so much,Krista.” “If he cared so damn much then why does he always hurt us? You and me both! Why did he goto prison, if he cares so much about us?” Krista began to cry. The truth was, she loved Snow asmuch as she had ever loved her real father, she just couldn’t handle the complications of havinga parent figure being little more than a half decade older than she was. “How many things canwe forgive him for?” “As many as we have to, I guess,” Raven sighed. “He’s... complicated, Krista. But that doesn’tmean he’s a bad person.” “He’s a good person that does bad things,” Krista sulked. “What’s the difference?” “Krista,” Raven tried to reason with her, “try putting yourself in his shoes. Since he and I havebeen together, he’s tried his best to look out for you. It sort of became second nature to him. And all the while, he’s been trying to keep you out of a situation just like the one you’re in. Hehas a right to be upset, Krista.” Krista’s brown eyes burned a hole in her sister. “So it’s ok that he smashed up all the phones!?” Krista erupted. “It’s ok that he went over toMarty’s and did God knows what!?” Suddenly, she began to cry too hard to speak. “I didn’t say that,” Raven answered as she put her small hand against her sister’s cheek. “It’s not like it helps things to go crazy!” Krista said through her manic sobbing. “I know,” Raven agreed as she put her arm around her young sibling. “You’re right. Evenhe knows that.” “Then why is he... like he is?” Krista was almost pleading for an answer now. As much as sheloved her brotehr-in-law, he was becoming unbearable as she grew older. “Look at his family, Krista,” Raven sighed. “He comes from a long line of violence. It’s all heknows. He doesn’t think about situations - he reacts. But Krista, that doesn’t mean he doesn’tcare about you.” Krista never questioned the fact that Snow cared for her, but that wasn’t her point. Still, itseemed to be the only thing that Raven could see in this situation. Krista didn’t feel like arguingany further. *** Location: Hattoni Mansion, New York CityDate: February 5, 2003Time: 2:30 pm “We got problems, chief,” Petey the Vice said with a sigh as he entered the spacious office ofJustin Hattoni. The son of the family’s founder sat behind his massive desk looking as stressedas ever. Johnny Silver was sitting in a chair to the far right of the desk flipping through a stackof papers on his lap, trying to make himself look busy. “What now?” Hattoni asked with a sigh. He rested his head on his hands and shook his head. Business in the family was thriving, but it was more stressful than ever now, it seemed. Ofcourse, he had to cover up the murder of John Mossilano, the head of the Mossilano family. Then again, John’s own successors were doing most of the covering. And of course, it would only be a matter of time before the Feds started sniffing around aboutthe murders of the three men were recently suing Jason Snow. “It’s the Irish kid,” Petey answered his boss as he stepped over to the desk. “Rumour has it hewent fucking crazy on Marty Verrelli a few days back. Beat the kid to a fucking pulp.” “Verrelli?” Hattoni’s eyebrow perked. “Isn’t that The Dragon’s kid?” Petey nodded. “Jesus,” sighed Justin as he put his face in his hands. “Does The Dragon know yet?” “Your guess is as good as mine,” answered Petey. “The kid called my nephew after it happenedto take him to the hospital.” “How bad?” Justin asked. “Pretty fucking bad,” Petey said seriously. “I mean, the kid’s going to be alright in the long run,but for now he’s got a couple of black eyes, a nose that’s broken in three places, and his face isall cut to fuck.” “Christ,” Johnny Silver piped up. “I told you guys Snow had a fucking temper on him.” Justin ignored his odd-looking companion. “Do you know why he did it?” asked the Don. “Yeah,” Petey answered with a sigh. “Here’s where this shit gets tricky, boss. Apparently, and Idon’t know if this is true, Marty Verrelli knocked up Snow’s kid sister.” “Snow doesn’t have a kid sister,” responded Johnny. “He’s an only child.” “Whatever the fuck,” Petey said, silencing Johnny. “His wife’s sister. Somebody’s fuckingsister. Verrelli got her pregnant, and Snow’s not exactly happy about that.” Justin winced. “How old is she?” Justin asked. “I don’t know,” Petey answered. “Nineteen, maybe twenty. She’s not very old, that’s all Iknow, and Snow watches over the girl like bear watches over its fucking cub. I’m stumped onthis one, boss. That’s why I’m coming to you. I don’t know what the fuck to do about it.” “It’s obvious,” Johnny Silver spoke again. “Snow fucked up here. You can’t just go aroundsnapping on people. Especially not the son of a legend in the family.” “Snow’s a made guy too,” Justin said as he turned to Johnny. “Chief,” Johnny said seriously, “with all do respect - Snow may be a made guy, but he ain’tfucking blood. The choice here isn’t between a made guy and some dumb fucking kid - thechoice is between a mick and some dumb fucking kid who just so happens to be the sonof a family legend.” Justin’s eyes hardened on Johnny Silver. “When I brought Jason Snow into this family, Ibrought him in completely - blood be damned. And he’s to be treated as so. And youwill respect him as so.” Johnny raised his hands defensively and leaned back in his chair. His silver comb over gleamedin the dim light. “It’s a touchy situation,” Petey said after a long awkward moment, trying to bring theconversation back around to the situation at hand, and not the fact that Snow wasn’t Italian. “The kid fucked up, that’s for sure - you don’t fuck with a man’s family... But Snow beat himpretty bad, Justin.” “How close is Snow to this girl?” asked the young Don of the family. “Very,” answered Petey. “He’s pretty much raised her, from what I can tell.” “She lives with him,” Johnny added. “Hot little broad, if I do say so myself.” Petey and Justinboth shot fierce looks toward their companion - not only was the comment ill-timed, but Johnnywas nearly fourty years Krista’s senior, as he was well into his fifties. “He treats her like a daughter,” Petey got things back on topic. “She’s pregnant?” Justin asked though he already knew the answer. “A little over a month along,” said Petey. “I don’t know how long she’s been with Verrelli, butSnow just moved here a couple of months ago, so it couldn’t have been long.” “Hot and easy,” Johnny said. He was quickly silenced by the sharp glares of hiscompanions. “There will be no punishment,” Justin said with a tone of finality. “Maybe he went a little toofar with things, I know, but I’ve got kids - I can understand where he’s coming from on this.” “So that’s it?” Johnny protested. “You’re just going to let him off with this? Come on, Justin -Verrelli may be a dumb punk, but his father’s a fucking legend.” “I don’t give a fuck who his father is!” Justin snapped at Johnny. “But for yourinformation, no, that’s not it. We’ll have a sit-down with Snow. Maybe somewheredown the road we’ll sit down with both him and the Verrelli kid. Maybe the Dragon too. Buthe’s not to be punished. Understand?” Petey nodded quickly - he never liked Marty Verrelli anyway. Johnny Silver, on the other hand,only stared at the young Don.
