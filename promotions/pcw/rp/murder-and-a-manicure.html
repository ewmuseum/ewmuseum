Murder and a Manicure

Date: not entered [Scene opens at 'Julia's Body Conditioning Salon'. It's a large salon, filled with rows upon rows of hair dryers and styling chairs. Filtered throughout the salon are leafy green plants and soft music that emit an aura of serenity and tranquility. Alexander Pryce walks in through the front entrance and waves at all the employees who seem to know him by name. Large Bulgarian Woman: Alexander, nice to see you. Alexander: Hello Henrietta. Henrietta: Are you here for the sauna? Alexander: No, my skin is feeling quite replenished, thanks. Alexander continues to walk through the salon, where he next encounters a sexy brunette with a name tag that reads 'Alicia'. Alicia: Hi Alex, did you book a facial for today? Alexander: No, but i'll be sure to give you one later on. Alexander winks at Alicia and walks right past her as a smirk appears on her face. Alexander makes his way over to the manicurist who is sitting behind a table with a nail file in one hand and nail polish in the other. Manicurist: You're late. Alexander: My dear Esmerelda, don't you know its fashionable to be late? Esmerelda: Well i charge by the minute, so you can be as late as you want. Alexander sits down at the table opposite Esmerelda and stretches his hands out towards her. Esmerelda inspects his hands and nods her head in approval. Esmerelda: Aloe Vera? Alexander: Yes, The Body Shop has a new range. Esmerelda: The feel nice and smooth. I need to get me some of that. Esmerelda begins to file Alexander's finger nails. In the meantime, Alexander casually looks around the shop. Esmerelda puts on some glasses so as to get a better view of Alexander's nails and produce a closer shave. Esmerelda: What colour varnish did you want to apply? Alexander: I was thinking mauve or lillac? Esmerelda: I like mauve. It goes with anything. Alexander: Then mauve it is. Esmerelda: Did you want to get a peticure today as well? Alexander: No, i've got an appointment for a massage in an hour so i won't have the time. Esmerelda: That's a shame, you have such pretty toes. Alexander: I was told they were good enough to qualify me as a foot model. Esmerelda: Wow, there's big money in foot modelling. Alexander: Yeah and i've heard quite alot about this 'Camel Toe' sensation which sounds interesting. Esmerelda looks at Alexander with a puzzled look on her face. She shakes her head and continues the manicure. 'CRACK' Having briefly taken her eyes of Alexander's nails, she accidentally slices the nail on his index finger. Alexander: WHAT THE HELL DID YOU DO!?!?!?! Esmerelda: I...it...and.... Alexander: YOU BROKE MY NAIL!! How the hell am i supposed to go out in public looking like some deformed freak with a sliced nail? Esmerelda: Maybe we can cover it up? Alexander: With what? Alexander waves his hands in the face of Esmerelda. Alexander: Take a good look at these hands, woman! Their gorgeous and now you've just murdered my nail!! Esmerelda: How about a fake nail? Alexander: You can't stick a fake among nine other natural beauties and not expect people to notice. A look of saddness crosses Esmerelda's face. Esmerelda: Well the only other option would be to cut them all to even them out. Alexander looks at his nails, knowing full well that there is no other option. Dejected, he reluctantly sticks his hands out for Esmerelda to cut the rest of the nails to even them all out. One by one, Esmerelda begins to clip the well manicured, and immaculately conditioned nails on Alexander's hands. The clipped nails begin to flutter on to the table below. After all the nails have been cut, Alexander looks at his hands and shakes his head in misery. Esmerelda: Don't worry, they'll grow back. Alexander: Don't talk to me. You've done enough damage for one day. As Alexander gets up to leave, he see's Alicia approaching. Alicia: What was all that noise? Alexander looks at Esmerelda and scowls. Alexander: Edward Scissorhands over here cut the nail on my index finger..... so i had to sacrifice the nine other lovelies. Alicia looks at Alexander's fingers and frowns. Alicia: Why didn't you just wear gloves until the nail grew back? Alexander's eyes boggle open and he remains in a frozen state as he ponders why the hell that idea never crossed his mind earlier.... ....Scene fades out....
