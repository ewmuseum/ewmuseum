Other Religions!?!? BAH!!!

29 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/29/02 The camera fades in… The Chef sits on a fine leather chair, smoking a thick cigar and twiddling his thumbs as if he had nothing better to do. The room looks eerily similar to the set of Masterpiece Theatre, or perhaps Tyler Nelson’s library. Rows upon rows of books are scattered throughout the room, a millions upon millions of porno magazines are locked behind a 10-inch thick steel safe. The Chef furls his dark fluffy beard, and puffs out a thick gray chunk of cancer-causing smoke. He leans back in his leather chair and begins what will be undoubtedly another mind-numbingly stupid promo… The Chef: Greetings fellow gastronomers!!! I am the Chef and I will be giving you a small presentation tonight on why I think my partner Alexis Parshin and I are destined for tag team glory. The Chef takes another puff from the cigar and continues. The Chef: It’s simple really, we are the only men in the PCW right now that are truly under the safe care and watchful eye of the good master himself…Allah!!! Allah is omnipresent, omnipotent, and don’t forget infallible, but he’s also a really swell God to worship, and Parshin and I enjoy prostrating before him. With Allah guiding us, smacking us in the face to keep us in line, and providing us with multitudes of virginal women we are truly going to dominate the PCW like no other tag team. Our Religion, the only true one I might add, translates into our Success!!! Pete, the chalkboard!!! The Chef claps his hands as if he was ordering someone to do his bidding. A small fragile old man creeps into the view of the camera carrying a chalkboard with some text etched on it. “OUR RELIGION=OUR SUCCESS” The Chef quickly points to the words on the chalkboard, but can’t quite reach that far off his chair. He claps his hands again, and Ol’ Pete does the pointing for him. The Chef: But what about other religions you say? Yes there are other ones, completely false and full of nothing but contradictions I might add. I’m here tonight to present the other religions of the world in a heavily biased and ignorant manner that could only be done by an Islamic Militant of my caliber. PETE!!! The old man quickly erases the text on religion and success. Ol’ Pete grabs a piece of chalk and writes “Buddhism” on the board. The Chef: Thanks you miserable little infidel. Our first religion of the night is Buddhism. What is Buddhism? Rubbish? Clap Trap? Hearsay? Mumbo Jumbo? Pretty much all of these things and some ill-conceived ideas on “peacefulness”. My Allah, if you want to be peaceful, join the frickin’ Quakers and make frickin’ oatmeal!!! The Chef puffs his cigar violently. The Chef: Peace, bah, who needs peace? I sure don’t. If everything was peaceful, we wouldn’t have the PCW, NRA, KKK, or the Black Panthers? I can’t imagine a world without these fine groups, with the exception of the PCW…Bah!!! Clap Trap I say!!! … … … The Chef: Pete!!! The old man uses his hand to erase “Buddhism” and writes on the chalkboard, “Judaism”. The Chef: Well Shalom my infidel brothers, next up is good old fashioned Judaism. There are lots of problems with this religion. One, you can’t eat bacon…BACON!!! Pete: Well what about turkey bacon? The Chef scowls at the old man. The Chef: Two, you have to sacrifice food to some god… Sheesh, Allah doesn’t want food you morons… He created the stuff to begin with, why does he need you to get it for him, HUH!?!? Finally, Judaism has it’s Sabbath, or day or rest. Do you think if I rested one day a week I would have been a PCW champion!?!? No!!! I’d be called, “lazy”, a “sloth”, or “just another fat French guy”… Nay Nay infidels, I’m no lazy boy! I like sautéing @$$ and do it 24/7………..PETE!!! The old man, who was snoring during the Chef’s utterly dull speech, is startled and begins frantically writing down the next religion on the Chef’s list, “Hinduism”… The Chef: (Doing his best Apu impression) Oh yes, the next religion is Hinduism. Millions and millions of morons around the world through themselves at the earth and proclaim it’s a divine manifestation of God.... (Changing his accent) Well I’ve got news for them. Allah might be omnipresent, but he prefers the heavens, ok!?!? He likes a room with a view, and likes to see to it that his peeps can get a similar view. All they have to do is declare him as the one true god, and it’s theres. These pieces of rancid chicken rings don’t however, and they’ll be sent straight to Iblis once the croak, and that’s something I can GUAROOOOOONNNNNTEEE!!! They worship Shiva, the great destroyer, well I’ve know some more news for them! The only great destroyer is the god that sent the floods, the locusts, those frogs, and the millions of renowned suicide bombers, it’s gotta be Allah, not Shiva…bah!!!….Ahem… The Chef looks at Ol’ Pete with a stern gaze… The Chef: Let’s go old man, I’m not paying you in crushed sweet corn to sleep!!! The old man erases “Hinduism” and writes in “Christianity” The Chef: Ah!!! The final stop on my tour of the World’s Worst Religions!!! Christianity has to be the worst religion of all time, anywhere!!! First of all, this doctrine of the Trinity…who does this “god” think he really is!?!? Ok he can walk through a door, but people can’t see him, but he can provide fish, but his followers don’t recognize him, my Allah what nonsense… They also have this cannibalistic ritual where they believe they are eating the flesh and blood of some dead guy!?!? SICK!!! And they persecute those fun-loving Goths? They are the real freaks… Pete: Mr. Chef sir, I’m a Methodist… The Chef: That’s great you old buzzard, but I don’t give a flying frappicino!!! Pete: But I believe in the resurrection of Christ, and I believe he died for our sins… The Chef: (Sighing) You’re right about one thing, people do sin. But some bi-polar Rabbi didn’t save us… We have to save ourselves!!! How you might ask? By allowing the grace of Allah to come into our lives and change us, his followers can gain eternal life… It’s just like the Beatles song…”All you need is Allah…” Pete: I think it was, “All you need is Love”… The Chef: Love, schmove!!! All you need is Allah, infidel!!! Pete hangs his head as the Chef shows the man a look of disgust. The Chef: Get out of my sight before I make a quiche out of you… The old man walks out of the picture. The Chef: And guess what, for that little rude, crude, and unacceptable interruption, you get…NO CRUSHED CORN!!! The man nods, quietly sobbing and walking away from the Chef, murmuring, “Turn the other cheek Pete; don’t let the boy get to you…” The Chef rocks back and forth on his chair and puffs his cigar once again. The Chef: I’ve hoped you infidels has learned a valuable lesson from me tonight! Let’s recap shall we? 1) Allah is the one true god… 2) All the other religions are false and stink like rancid sausage… 3) You all are stupid… 4) I’m one sexy sexy Chef! The Chef continues… The Chef: If you feel you haven’t learned anything from this promo, please read it again and count it as my 5th promo for the PPV, my handler is a pretty lazy guy… Remember infidels, Allah loves you all, and some more than others, but you all can gain eternal life not through worship of false deities, indulgences, good deeds, or even getting the “Joan Rivers” combo that the neighborhood plastic surgeon. The only way you can get eternal life is through SUICIDE BOMBINGS!!! Or perhaps maybe some non violent worship of Allah, although I’m skeptical about that… Adios enemigos!!! The Chef puffs out a huge cloud of smoke, which covers the lens of the camera as the camera mercifully fades to black…
