Two can play at that game, Skylar

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27 Dec &#039;02 The scene opens up outside a building which reads ‘FUCKS of America’ which Chet has no doubt come to following his meeting with Dr Roundstone (See last promo). Obviously he’s here to do what Dr Roundstone asked (Once again, see last promo) But as Chet is about to enter the building, he stops as he notices the camera crew running down the street trying to film his every move in order to make a promo out of this event. Chet walks over to the crew and starts talking to them. Chet King: Hey, you guys are here to make a promo out of me going to this wrestling addicts thing, aren’t you? Sound Technician: We’ve actually been told to make two or three promos out of it. Chet King: Fair enough, but I was wondering if I could actually take this time to cut a good old fashion trash talking promo on The End. Cameraman: I don’t know man, we’ve been told that trash talk promos aren’t quality enough to be shown on PCW TV. Chet King: Come on, The End got to attempt to write trash about me, and his writings were shown to the world, or at least the four or five people who care what he has to say plus me. Cameraman: I guess we can film this man, but we’ll have to run it through management before it gets aired. Chet King: I’m sure they’ll be pleased to see a promo which goes back to the basics, rather then a set of fifty sandbagging promos that just seem to have no point…like The End’s. Cameraman: Well okay man, we’ll let you do this, uh, what you want to say? Chet looks into the barrel of the camera and starts to talk. Chet King: Skylar, let me tell you just how glad I am that you had an attempt, a poor one, but an attempt nonetheless at talking smack about me, because the most fun I’ve had in this business is when I get to go toe to toe with guys with the best mouths in the business, like Duke Williams. Unfortunately I know your never going to be anywhere near as talented as Duke in talking or in wrestling, but I’ll try to have some fun with you anyway. Skylar, you sounded so proud when you were talking about how you rose to the top by coming third in the Mall Brawl this year. Guess what Skylar, I’d been there and done that a year before you but coming third in the mall brawl doesn’t come near to the top of my accomplishments in wrestling like it does for you, I guess that’s just because I have so many, and well you have, ah, that and being the tallest cruiserweight in PCW history. Oh, I forgot, you also have coming third in Alliances. You seem so proud of that little accomplishment as well. But guess what? I never was eliminated at last years alliances, which technically means that I was the winner since Jeremy Howard left the ring. But once again, being the technical winner of Alliances doesn’t even come close to the top of my list of accomplishments, like a mere third place, a mere tied third place, does for you. As for your victory over me, I do remember that match somewhat, but you’ll have to excuse me for not caring about it then or now. I guess it’s just because I was wrestling in big matches, main events, while that match was probably at that point in time the crowning highlight of your career. Come to think of it, it’s still probably the highlight of your career. Isn’t it interesting that your criticizing me for coming back, saying, and I quote, “you’re a loser; one of the 'good-ole-boys' who came back into spotlight they didn't deserve, to relive long-past glory days.” I think I sense jealousy. I mean even if my glory days are past me like you claim, which I can assure you they’re not as you’ll find out on Sunday, at least I had glory days. I think you’re just jealous of the fact that I had glory, while you had your third place in Alliances. Oh, well I guess you also had some accomplishments in other federations like HWF, where you became a hardcore and tag team champion was it? Wow! That’s fantastic! You couldn’t even win their world title, which no doubt they were whoring away. I bet everyone and their dog, cat, brother, sister and mailman held that title…except you. Maybe the reason you didn’t get it was because of politics, like when you were in the UWF? Maybe they were all saying The End is too good, we can’t give him the world, it would destroy the chance of anyone else getting it. We can’t give it to The End because he’s the greatest wrestler the world has ever seen. Heh, not likely. I think what happened in the UWF was that you pulled an Avalanche. You were losing every match you were in, but yet you thought that you should be given the world title didn’t you? I bet you jobbed to Hardcore Crazy Fire Undertaker Austin didn’t you? And then you went and said to management over there ‘I’m The End, I’m the bestest wrestler ever! Give me the world title or I’ll quit!’ And they just laughed at you, which caused you to quit and blame ‘politics’ for not getting given their world title. I know that winning the Cruiser Weight and Extreme Title, three times and two times respectively, is nothing to be proud of as well as holding them at the same time…but hey, at least I wasn’t just handed those titles like you were for you second cruiser weight reign. It’s just so pathetic that you couldn’t even win the CW title, a title which you just admitted isn’t worth anything, you had to be given the title. How sad. As for my comments about winning the Continental Title in my non title match with TTT on Friday, what I meant was, uh, umm, that’d he’d give me the Continental Title out of respect, because he’ll realize that I deserve it once I beat him. (This causes the sound technician in the background to roll his eyes.) Chet King: On to my first reign as Continental Champion. It was uneventful you say? I think people remember my one reign as Continental Champion more then they remember your dull career. I did it all during my reign from making Mexi feel pain to being the longest reigning champion of any sort in PCW at that time. Like I said, people remember my reign as Continental Champion more then they remember your career since I had some interesting stuff happen to me…oh wait, I forgot, you’ve had something interesting happen to you, you came tied third at Alliances. How could I forget that huge achievement? Heh. There is one thing I’d like to ask of you Skylar, and that’s that you don’t retire. You see, you claim that now that I’ve come back I’m going to ruin my legacy, well the opposite goes for you. If you retire now, then you won’t have a legacy for people to remember. Why don’t you stay on and try to achieve something worthwhile, and with a bit of luck people might remember you doing something, even if it is becoming the leader of the next incarnation of the Ream Team. Heck, on second thought, you probably shouldn’t even try, the Ream Team wouldn’t even hire you, your making the right choice Skylar. Just give up. There’s so much more I could talk about. From telling you what a moron you are for trying to knock my achievements, to knocking yours…oops, you don’t have any to knock. I’ll just leave you with this Skylar, on Sunday I’ll show you who is washed up, I’ll let you know that you’ve made the right choice in retiring. And a quick message to all the others in the match on Sunday. You all better be ready to run and hide, not just because I plan on winning the match, but because you no doubt will all have to go through the same fate I have, and have The End give a pitiful attempt at trash talking you all. (We leave the scene with Chet King turning into the FUCKS of America building)
