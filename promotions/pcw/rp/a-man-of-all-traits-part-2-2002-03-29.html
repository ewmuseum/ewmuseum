A Man Of All Traits Part 2

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 29th, 2002 (Mike and Graves sit together at a comedy night club. Mike seriously considering Graves' suggestion has begun to obsess over stand-up comedy. Graves picks up a glass of Vodka mixed with coke and chugs it. He smiles and falls down on the table. A new comedian walks out onto the stage. He is fairly plump with rosy red cheeks. He holds a brown paper bag in one hand.) MC Billy Buffer: Ladies and gentlemen please welcome Kris Kringle! Kris Kringle: So what's the deal with lamp shades? If you have a lamp, why do you need shade? (Mike turns to Graves.) Mike Frampton: He stole that from Seinfeld. Michael Graves: Who cares, it sucked anyways. (Kringle takes a drink out of his bag.) Kris Kringle: So I was walking down the street the other day and this guy dumps his coffee on me. So you know what I did? Crowd: What? Kris Kringle: I smashed a beer bottle over his head. (Mike stands up.) Mike Frampton: *In a loud voice* Man you suck! (Kringle stares blankly at Mike.) Kris Kringle: Ladies and gentlemen, I give you Dingus LeRoux! Mike Frampton: What is that supposed to mean? Kris Kringle: Whatever the hell you want it to mean! Mike Frampton: You saying you want a piece of me? Kris Kringle: Trust me you don't want any of this. Mike Frampton: I am gonna make you wish you weren't born just like your momma! (The crowd gives a round of cheers and laughs.) Mike Frampton: Who do you think you are Kringle? Santa Clause? Believe me the only thing you have in common with Santa is you both love children. Unfortunately you love them in a different way, you sick freak! (The crowd bellows with laughs as Kringle's face turns sour.) Mike Frampton: And what the hell is with that paper bag? What do you have in there? A -(beep)-ldo? Kris Kringle: No, I have a... Mike Frampton: Sick man I don't want know what you have in that bag, for all I know it could be moose piss. Get off the stage amateur.(Kringle stumbles backstage as Frampton receives cheers. Frampton snickers and jumps up on the stage.) Mike Frampton: So what's the deal with N'Sync? They claim they are men but why the hell are they always together and always seemingly touching each other. (The crowd ponders this sentence. Frampton receives a couple of boos.) Mike Frampton: So what's the deal with Communism? Michael Graves: BOO! Mike Frampton: Umm. Michael Graves: BOO! Mike Frampton: One moment please. (Mike jumps off the stage and walks to Graves. He picks him up by the hair.) Mike Frampton: Ladies and gentlemen, it's time to take out the trash. (Graves elbows Mike and throws him out the door.) Michael Graves: And the trash has been put out. (Frampton tries to charge back in but Graves locks the door. Frampton begins to scream through the door.) Mike Frampton: I'M GONNA KILL YOU! You're gonna be my -(beep)-! Michael Graves: GOODY! Mike Frampton: On second thought. (The scene fades to white. A moment later the scene fades back in to Frampton and Graves.) Michael Graves: Maybe you could try acting! (Fade to black.)
