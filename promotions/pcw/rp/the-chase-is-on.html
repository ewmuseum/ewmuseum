Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin’s emergency penile operation was interrupted by inept surgeons, trainee surgeons and a whole bunch of Japanese tourists. Keen to escape the eager crowd of people, Flaymin made a run for dear life. And now the search for Flaymin intensifies…. A man with a sabotaged penis running around a hospital….Being chased by a bunch of crazed ‘Damaged Penis Fans’…. Will Flaymin get out of the hospital in time?Or will the hunters pounce on their prey? [Scene opens in the hospital foyer on the fourth floor.Flaymin is hiding in the fire escape, peering out every so often to make sure that he isn’t being followed. He looks down at his savaged penis and wipes all the blood on the wall with a look of anguish emblazoned right across his face. Suddenly, Flaymin notices that the area around him begins to flash every so often. He looks up expecting a faulty light only to witness a group of crazed Japanese tourists snapping away. The tourists stop shooting and begin to discuss something with each other.One of the Japanese men nods his head before reaching into his bag and whipping out an extra large zoom lens and attaching it to his camera. Flaymin’s eyes boggle open and without waiting another second he makes a dash for the nearest exit.The Japanese Paparazzi contingent begin yelling at one another as they attempt to lug their heavy camera bags around the hospital in order to chase Flaymin. Meanwhile, Flaymin has escaped the wrath of the intrusive Japanese photographers….He dashes down the corridor leaving behind a trail of blood and damaged penile skin. However Flaymin’s escape reaches a dead end when he arrives face to face with the elevator- a.k.a. the only source of escape.He frantically looks around for an alternative getaway but decides against jumping out of a window on the fourth floor….. for obvious reasons! As Flaymin awaits the elevator, a nurse comes around the corner wheeling a bed.She smiles at Flaymin and waits besides the elevator. A curious look crosses the nurses’ face as she hears a sound getting louder and louder. She momentarily leaves the bed to see what all the commotion is about. Seeing this as a golden opportunity, Flaymin lifts up the blanket on the bed and hops in underneath it before once again raising the blanket over his head to cover himself. In the meantime, the nurse inspects the loud noise only to realise that it is only the sound of the trampling shoes of all the Japanese tourists running around. One of the tourists reaches into her pocket and pulls out a translation book.She flips through the pages and then approaches the nurse] Japanese Tourist: Escoosa me, yoo see man? [The nurse frowns] Nurse: Excuse me? [The Japanese Tourist rifles through her translation book once again] Japanese Tourist: Man wis za big err… co’ock in blood? Nurse: Oh right!The cafeteria is on the second floor but I’m not really sure whether they cook stuff with blood or not. [The Japanese tourist translates the misinterpreted information to her comrades before smiling and thanking the nurse. While all this is going on, Flaymin is still underneath the covers on the bed.The whole time, he feels some object digging into his spine. Carefully, he reaches his hands down behind his back and feels something cold.He runs his hands up and down the cold object yet is still unable to figure out what this object could be. Cautiously, he slightly tilts his head to the side so as to see what this object could be.In doing so he comes face to face with…. Another face…. A dead face. Flaymin gulps and chills run down his spine as he realises he’s lounged on top of a corpse on its way to the morgue. While Flaymin comes to terms with this shocking realisation, the nurse wheels the bed onto the elevator curious as to why the bed is now that much harder to wheel around… Also on the elevator are the Japanese tourists, standing only inches from the covered Flaymin.Flaymin remains as quiet as humanly possible in order to not attract any attention.But one thing he can’t control is his bleeding penis… Slowly the blood becomes thicker and thicker that it starts to make a small red mark on the blanket.Gradually the puddle of blood on the blanket begins to spread…. Upon exiting the elevator, the nurse looks down at the strange pool of blood imprinted in the blanket and scratches her head in confusion. The nurse begins to wheel the bed down the corridor, all the while looking down at the pool of blood trying to figure out how it got there. With the blood stains distracting the nurse, she fails to notice an oncoming bed being wheeled by a doctor.Suddenly the two beds collide and uncontrollably spin around in circles. The nurse and the doctor run after the beds to stop them from crashing into the walls and all the hospital equipment lining the corridor. Having brought the beds to a halt, the nurse and doctor stop to work out which bed is which. Doctor: Which bed is yours? [The nurse shrugs her shoulders] Nurse: I’m not sure.I was just wheeling a dead guy to the morgue. Doctor: Dammit, I was transporting this deceased guy to the operational theatre because he’s a donor and they’ll be pulling out his heart and other useful body parts. Nurse: Why don’t we just roll back the blanket to see who’s who? Doctor: In the middle of a corridor with people watching?!?!? Nurse: You’re right…. I’ll take this one and we’ll call it even? Doctor: Done. [The nurse wheels away one of the deceased carcasses, while the doctor wheels away the other bed- the one containing Flaymin….. on its way to the operational theatre for a heart transplant and kidney removal….] [Scene fades to black] In the next instalment….Is Flaymin about to have his heart literally ripped out of his body? More importantly, is the dead body underneath him starting to arouse Flaymin in the most unsuspecting way???? Tune in for a truly necrophiliac experience!!!!
