Babysitting The Princess

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Before The Vampire Escapade “Like, where is Xavi and stuff?” The preptastic voice of Eleanor Kannon-Hall opens this promo, seeming like some sort of an error, this being a promo for Rush Mason. The likes of the infamous Ellie~! haven’t been seen associating with Rush since the days of the Greatest Team Alive, former holders of the NTICW tag titles. “Oh dear Bon Dieu, what have I done to deserve this?” We can now see that Rush Mason is sitting in some sort of office, while Eleanor Kannon-Hall is pacing around, looking as concerned as a twentysomething prep ever looks. While everything else in the room is a dull gray or brown, Eleanor’s pink mini-skirt and tank top combo provides a vibrant contradiction. Rush: I already told you. Xavier is doing some business in Europe. After the favor he did for me with the whole Sky match, I figured I’d pay him back by babysitt…er…keeping you company for a while. Ellie~! looks down at the Voodoo Villain, who is wearing an Ecko sweater with dark blue baggy jeans and navy Timberland boots, with a sneer on her face. Thinking Rush might be some sort of impostor, she inspects him carefully…as her little mind can fathom. Ellie~!: How do I know you’re not the, like, doppelganger of Rush Mason? You could be, like, all imposternating him and stuff. Ignoring Ellie~!’s butchering of the English language, Rush looks up at the clock that is mounted above the CEO’s own leather chair. Dissapointed with the amount of time that is left in his baby-sitting gig, he gives in and tries to show Ellie~! logic. Rush: A perfect doppelganger is an impossibility for someone not born as a twin. Of course, logic isn’t always the best way to fight against someone who doesn’t know common sense from Nordstrom’s. Ellie~!’s counterargument is not exactly what you’d expect. Ellie~!: Wow, you’re that guy that like SO used to tag with my Xavi! Sighing in realization that he could have a more intelligent conversation with a toddler, Rush just decides to go along once again with Ellie~!’s non-logical flow of conversation, trying to keep a civil tongue. Rush: Yeah, that’s me, Rush Mason. Remember, you, me, Xavier, and Derrick Diamond were gonna take over NTICW? Scratching her un-blemished face for a second and squinting hard, Ellie~! tries to recall any past interactions with this man, but her prep memory only spans as far as her last shopping spree, so Rush is lost from her thoughts. Ellie~!: Errr, like, no? Taking the opportunity to remind her, Rush begins into a schpiel of the olden times of the Greatest Team Alive. Rush: Well, Blain Bishop wanted both me and Kannon out of the picture as singles champs, but then we were paired together in the Lethal Lottery tournament and we won the tag titles, thus earning the greatness which our name implied. Some of the more confusing words in this string, like “tournament” and “implied” have the little princess quite confused, but she plays it off, treating them as very small, unimportant details. Ellie~!: Well then, like, where was Ellie~! and stuff? I, like, SO only remember that pizza guy. Surprised that she remembers anything, Rush elaborates on Ellie~!’s statement, although it is at least partly true. Rush: Pizza guy….that was Alvieri, my first partner in crime. You were just a little less anno…a little less exposed back then. The word exposed brings back some bad thoughts for the pink-clad diva, and she immediately begins to interrogate Rush, weary of more pranks being played upon her, like those famous Ellie~! shots that we all wanted to see. Ellie~!: Like, you better not be one of those Pranks With, like, Attitude, because I’m like WAY ready for anymore tricks. I’ll just give the Prep Slam O’ Doom~! And win and be, like, YAY ME AND STUFF! Rush nods, acknowledging Ellie~! but trying to wait out the rest of the time, while Ellie~! tries out her Prep Slap O’ Doom~! in case anyone is in need of a nice fresh one right across the kisser. Rush: Hmm…has anyone actually ever been harmed by your so-called doom-inflicting slap, Eleanor? Turning and facing Rush, Ellie~! seems to have a fire in her eyes as she limply brings back her arm and issues the deadly slap onto Rush’s arm…barely even making him twitch, as Ellie~! turns back to practicing again, thinking herself the victor of this little argument. Ellie~!: See! Ellie~! like WAY told you! Rush looks down at his arm, and then back up at Ellie~!, whose back is turned as she practices her limp-wristed slaps. He mutters to himself, although the words are audible to Ellie~! Rush: Bon Dieu, XK better get here soon… Spinning on a dime, Ellie~! calls back to Rush, once again acting like her sweet self, although Rush knows if he tells her the truth, he will have a just over 100 pound problem on his hands for the rest of the night, so he thinks fast, as the prep asks of him to repeat. Ellie~!: Huh? I was all occupationed by my awesome slapping and I SO didn’t have time to listen to you. Rush: Ehh…I said XK better get some poon… With a sigh of relief, the scene begins to fade to black, but as we hear Ellie~!’s voice as the the last sound, we can hear the sigh turn into a sigh of desperation… “Yeah, you should, like, SO see us when me and Xavi are going hard…”
