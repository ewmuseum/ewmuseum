Exercizing Civillian Authority

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/11/02 On Friday Fusion, we saw a very impressive in-ring debut by Alexis Parshin. Fighting with the Chef, he easily defeated one of the Phantom Booker’s associates, BloodAngel. Now, the infiltration and the path towards dominance in the United States has fully begun. Now…Annihilation is inevitable. Moronic capitlist can not actually find this sport entertaining, can they?!” As usual, the booming voice of Alexis Parshin welcomes us to another mishap/adventure in his journey. As the scene opens up, we can see that Alexis and Raghab Karszat are sitting in what appears to be a study, with a lacquered mahogany desk in the middle and the television sitting in an open cabinet across the room. Alexis is sitting back in a leather office chair, while Raghab sits on a nearby leather upholstered stool. Both are turned towards the television, which has a cricket game on at the moment. Alexis is wearing a long-sleeve camoflouge shirt and matching pants, of course his feet being covered by his normal combat boots. Raghab is wearing a hideous brown colored t-shirt, mismatching perfectly with his blue corduroy pants. “No skill is involved, therefore it is the perfect sport for pathetic Americans and Brits.” Raghab sounds strange giving such a proclamation, his round belly bouncing up and down with every word that he speaks. “Well Americans detest every sport they are not good at, and must use their power to keep countries like Uzbekistan out of the World Cup!” Alexis still holds more of a grudge because Uzbekistan isn’t competing in the World Cup. “Konstantin, does Uzbekistan have a soccer team?” Alexis scratches his chin for a moment, then replies with power in his voice, “That’s not the point! We have fought for too long to be just shut down by the capitalist insects!” Raghab looks at his boss puzzlingly while Alexis continues to watch the cricket game. “Raghab, who has Tyler Nelson instructed me to demolish next?” Raghab curls up his lips, looking pensive, though actually thinking more about today’s lunch. “You will be teaming once again with Chef, and you take on a team known as Misplaced.” Alexis thinks for a moment, then asks Raghab again, “And they are both Americans?” Raghab, for some reason, knows the answer to this question as well. “Yes, both men are from America, and one of them is pathetic enough to find some sort of entertainment in this moronic sport.” The two men continue to watch cricket, until Alexis becomes fed up and clicks the TV off, only to be almost deafened by a sudden noise from the other room. Both men cover their ears as the sound stays at the same volume. Bellowing over the noise, Alexis can still barely be heard, “Raghab, do you have any idea what that immense racket is?” Raghab shouts back, but his voice isn’t even audible above the sound. Fuming, Alexis stands up and throws the door open as he reaches it, almost sliding it off the hinges with his hands. Raghab waddles behind, holding his ears, which are already covered by a black winter hat. Out in the hall, Alexis spies where the noise is coming from: the adjacent apartment. He stomps over and smashes his knuckle into the rotting door, making a loud rapping noise on the door. “Open this door, and I will let you continue to breathe!” Alexis cries loudly over the music of some sort, which thankfully is shut off as footsteps are heard coming towards the door. Alexis tries to put on his meanest face, and is VERY surprised as he sees who opens the door… It is a beautiful woman, her skin tanned to a degree much like Alexis’, looking of Arab descent and holding a tanned small baby. Alexis’ face partly shows his surprise, it shows a blank face, one eyebrow raised as if to question the reality of this situation. Meanwhile, Raghab has just uncovered his ears, and has spied the beautiful woman, his tongue hanging out of his mouth in a very untasteful manner. “May I uhh…” The woman becomes speechless as she spies the 6’10 man outside of her door, but even strange is Alexis’ relative speechlessness. Suddenly, he regains his composure, “Uhh….ma’am we were just uh…” Raghab quickly butts in with relative snap in his voice, “We came over here for you to shut off that utterly horrific noise!” Alexis tries to quiet Raghab, but he continues to rip into the woman, “How could you make such noise and not think that neighbors would care? You make all these pathetic Americans think of us as just more dumb Arabs, is that what you want?!” Alexis’ head goes down ashamed but too…shy? Is that the word? Too….shy, too scared to reply. The woman just replies softly, “Sir, I’m sorry about the noise, that’s my two sons playing their video games, but could you keep your voice down, the baby must sleep!” Alexis has now built up the courage to talk to the woman, but Raghab is already ahead of him, “Ma’am, you must teach your children who is in charge, or someone on the streets will! Now keep those disgusting mongrels in line!” With these words, Alexis puts a hand up to his head, exasperated, and the woman’s face turns into a cold glare. She says nothing and Raghab just walks off. Alexis lifts up his head to meet the woman’s face for one moment, only to see the door closing loudly, slamming into his face. He just walks off after Raghab, wondering… Back in the apartment, Alexis has resumed his normal egotistical ways. “Raghab, I must talk to Chef once again, would you contact him for me?” Raghab nods and dials on the phone, as Alexis lays back in a large loveseat. Raghab hands him the cordless phone and waits as a voice on the other line picks up. “Who the f*ck is this?” Hearing the familiar voice, Alexis replies warmly, “Ah Bertha, how lovely to hear your voice, its sweeter than the milk of a cow during Ramadan.” Bertha replies back in a strange fashion for her voice, “Aw shut up f*cko.” However the words have a warm resonance to them. “Is Chef there at the moment?” Bertha seems to think for a second, then reply, “F*ck no, that f*cko is out drinking f*cking Perrier or something!” Alexis just shakes his head and grins, “Well it’s always a pleasure Bertha.” Bertha mutters some curse-ridden greeting as Alexis hangs up the phone. “Raghab, this week the reign truly begins. This week, not only do I take down Misplaced, but the Ultimate Authority will blast off; igntion will be complete. This week we will truly prove our dominance over all those who do not respect our authority.” For some reason, no Eric Cartman joke is made. “Tyler Nelson has plans for the Malice Crew, the Political Machine, and the Phantom Phive. Plans that will get them Annihilated…”
