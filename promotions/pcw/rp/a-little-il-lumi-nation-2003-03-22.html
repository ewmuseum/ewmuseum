A little il-Lumi-nation

22 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/03/03 “…precisely.” A familiar, upper-class English accent echoes down a tunnel. The rhythmic thumping of a pair of boots accompanied the voice, laying a drumbeat to the vocals. Following was the sight of the duet in question – the high pitched and smooth tone of Gaz “The Jester” Green and his raspy, loud companion, Nerezza. Scraping a claw along the graffiti-covered walls, Nerezza ripped a chunk out of the tiles and snarls. “What do you mean?” Gaz chirped, thinking of adding his own message to the walls. “I mean…” Nerezza growls. “… that our new boss seems to have taken a personal dislike to all members of the former Front Office, regardless of their position. That means that we’ve got to alter our schedules slightly and instead of reclaiming those belts, which are our property, we have to become a fodder match and face people we have no differences with. Unless you count the fact that they’re simply human.” Gaz doodled on one of the tiles with a marker pen, making a fairly accurate representation of Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor, then adding an arrow through their heads. “Less of the anti-human talk, Squire, the only one who’ll support you got flung off the Jumbotron by Hessian.” Gaz said defensively. “Besides, Mr. Howard has no control over how our opponents act. Especially when provoked! Don’t worry, Rezz. I have a plan. First of all, we need to announce to the Schmoopies that they can marry freely without the threat of us attacking. Secondly…” The Jester’s words began to fade out to Nerezza as he turned around, feeling something behind him like a chilling wind brushing all around him. When the swung back to face the direction he was facing, he saw someone standing dead middle at the end of the tunnel. The outline of the silhouette was feminine in shape and not too intimidating, but Nerezza felt an undeniable concern deep in his stomach. Although at that distance Nerezza could not physically see her eyes, it was as if the two had made eye contact with hooked harpoons. Gaz’s words began to fade back in as he finished off a picture of Tessa and Chris being hanged by the same noose. “… thus meaning they were lying all along about their popularity and fooled the general public. See?” Nerezza didn’t answer, still locking eyes with the girl at the end of the tunnel. Gaz stopped his artwork to see what was distracting his partner and spotted the girl too. “Gaz…” Nerezza hissed a little, like a startled cat. “I need some private time. No audiences. If asked, I was playing with Fluffy all night.” “Why the sudden need for secrecy? Normally it’s over in…” “GO!” Nerezza interrupted, showing his face full of teeth. Gaz turned tail and quickly with a slight yelp. “Rezz doesn’t mind me watching while he feeds normally!” The Jester thought as his feet carried him away from the scene as fast as they could. “I don’t even want to guess why he doesn’t want me to see what he’s doing…” As Gaz fled away from the glowing streetlamps and towards the safety of the hotel, Nerezza stood frozen, intimidated by the girl ahead. She began to pace forward slowly, her shadow engulfing him. While the power she gave off wasn’t as much as his, any human with that level of energy was something to worry about. She grew closer, with the flickering overhead lights finally offering her features to Nerezza’s eyes clearly. Her hair, like his, was purple, cut to just lightly brushing her shoulders. It fell neatly down in straight rows, with one part cascading down over her right eye. Underneath the hair was a set of wraparound sunglasses, violet in colour and fading to translucent at the bottom. She was petite and almost cute if it wasn’t for the strange aura she was emanating. A pair of dark grey fingerless gloves covered most of her arms, ending well past her elbows, just below the armpit. On each wrist and just below the end of each glove, there seemed to be a metal bracelet, from which hung a chain connecting one to the other. On her neck was a similar collar, leather with a ring on the front. She continued to walk towards Nerezza as though she were helplessly magnetized to him. The vampire began to back away slightly as she drew even nearer. An extremely baggy pair of khaki cargo pants dropped down to halfway down her shin, where a simple pair of Doctor Marten style clumpy boots covered her feet. On her chest was an oddly designed piece of clothing – it looked almost like a handkerchief top except for the plunging neckline and off bottom part, for the midriff was bare except for the sides, which were cut out as though resembling two fangs on either side of her belly. On the lower section of her breasts were two red triangles that looked like eyes. It was like a monster’s face – like Nerezza’s own. The girl was so close now that Nerezza could make out her eyes to be blue and even see the frosty pink lip gloss she had on. Before she could get any closer, Nerezza’s hands began to change into claws, revealing has talons. Then she stopped, still with the look on her face of a mindless doll. “You won’t know me, but we’ve met before.” she said in a husky voice. Nerezza’s eyes narrowed in confusion. “I don’t remember you. What’s your name? How did we meet? Where did we meet? How did you know where to find me? Why are you- ” Nerezza growled. “You don’t recognize me?” She whimpered as though rejected. “Then let me show you.” With that said, she dropped to the ground, unconscious. Nerezza flinched. The girl herself was still alive and the power he felt was still around, just not inside her anymore. “…” Nerezza paused. For the first time in his unlife, something was spooking him. Slowly, the girl rose into the air as though a puppeteer had picked up her strings. Her head hung loosely to one side with a blank, empty look in her eyes. Then something began to appear next to her. Like the air being bent and distorted when extreme heat in a desert or above a fire changes it, slowly, a humanoid figure appeared in the air next to her. Devoid of any real shape, four limbs, a body and a head could be made out. Then, like a solid shadow, two eyes appeared next to her head. “Hmph…” Nerezza grunted in distaste. “So, That Man has come back to haunt me.” “…don’t you like his new body?” said the girl, being controlled by him as he sunk his fingers literally into her neck, being a creature that lacked any real physical presence. “He likes it. He thinks you like it too.” “It’s a nice touch with the chains, but I don’t really like that dreadful accessory with it.” Nerezza smirked, sheathing his claws and relaxing. That Man was the name he had given to a ghost who had sought his help, but rubbed him the wrong way. The ghost has such little sense of self-identity that it accepted the name. It even talked about itself in third person perspective. “The chains are his statement. It is to show that her body belongs to him now. She is like a pet to him. She will help him find out who he was. She will carry him when he needs to be carried.” continued the girl. She floated down, her feet finally touching the ground while the flickering air around her detached itself, gently propping her up against the wall first. It then floated in front of Nerezza, narrowing his shadow-like eyes. “But The Man is here for a reason.” Nerezza looked around to make sure nobody could see him. The tunnel was dimly lit and at the time of the morning that it was, only a few homeless people and those who deserved to die in his opinion would roam. Them, and anyone who was probably not a human being. “And what reason is that?” Nerezza asked, crossing his arms over his chest. The ghost turned away. His eyes completely disappeared as though the back of his head were solid, yet still Nerezza could see the crude drawing of Chris and Tessa through him. “The reason is for you to decide a name.” The Man sighed. His attention was captured as suddenly, Nerezza burst out laughing. His hand was clapped over his eyes and his head was tilted back, nose lifted into the air and pointed towards the roof of the tunnel. His mouth had split at the edges and opened fully, showing off every tooth in his mouth. The laugh itself was a true gesture of humour rather than spite, even causing tears to flow down the face of the vampire as his cackling continued. When the shrieks finally subsided and his face was wiped on his arm, his giggles receded as he cleared his throat. “You want me to give you an identity? Have you forgotten what happened the last time you begged a favour from me? Or shall I ram my claws into your temporary home and you again?” Nerezza threatened, bringing a hand to eye level and extending one solitary claw. The Man shook his head and looked towards the girl. “He knows you will not kill her as you did his last body. And he knows that you are amused by his presence. But he asks not a name for himself. He asks one for HER…” the ghost said, gesturing what could only be a representation of an arm towards the slumped girl. Nerezza took a look at her then suspiciously at the ghost. “Why me?” Nerezza hissed. “Why did That Man bring this girl to me, dress her up according to what he believes my tastes are and ask me to name her?” The vampire looked down on her. For a moment, he almost felt pity for the way she looked. Slumped and unconscious, not only bound by the ghost’s grip in her but now literally with the chains and collar he made her wear as a symbol of his control over her. Then again, it may have been petty jealousy on his part that someone other than him was causing possible misery to a human being, especially in such a creative way. He crouched, inches away from her face. She had a very childlike look to her, just an ounce of baby fat on her cheeks and when they were open, bright blue eyes. This close, he could even see a trace of freckles behind her visor. “I didn’t know That Man could read minds.” Nerezza said to The Man, not taking his eyes off the girl. “He can’t.” The ghost replied. “Time for him passes differently in the other side. It moves much slower there. All he may do is think and ponder. From the images he remembers of your home and how you spoke, he began to construct his thoughts about you.” “He built a psychological profile around me from those few minutes I allowed him into my home? To the extent that he managed to seek out a girl and customize her to my taste? …Impressive. But then, wouldn’t he be able to decide what to call her?” Nerezza said with a nasal tone, challenging the ghost. The Man’s eyes sunk in defeat. “He’s not that good.” he sighed. Nerezza stood up and yanked the collars of his coat. “I’ve decided.” he said triumphantly. “So what will you call my his body?” The Man asked anxiously, as though something depended on it. Nerezza cleared his throat. “First, I want you to promise me something. Agree, or she goes nameless.” grinned the Living Nightmare. The ghost let out a tortured moan. “Why do you vampires always have to play games?” it groaned. “Because it’s in our predatory nature. Besides, I haven’t tormented anyone in such a long time and tormented spirits seem to be a fairly common stereotype. So Yes or No?” Nerezza giggled, splitting his mouth into a smile again. “Yes, yes, YES!” the ghost impatiently croaked. It’s eyes dipped into an angry frown. “Keep your ectoplasm on… my condition is if that girl ever asks you for a name, or offers you one, you’ll take it. I’m fed up of talking about you like you’re not here. That Man is hardly appropriate. Are we agreed?” Nerezza arched an eyebrow. The ghost’s eyes moved up and down as though nodding. Looking back to the girl, Nerezza gestured towards her as though presenting a work of art. “Seeing that she is like me in some ways and in others she is my polar opposite… I’ll name her Lumi.” “…Lumi…?” The Man asked. “She looks as though she could be my own sister with that hair. That top she’s wearing is also a parody of the mask I have hanging in my home. You tried to make a female, living, human Nerezza. And seeing I am a dead, vampire male who’s name means ‘Darkness’ in another language, then I name her Lumi. Short for ‘illumination’, she’s like me, but my opposite.” Nerezza announced. “Strange… vampires can be so poetic yet so savage. But Lumi it is. And he shall take the name she gives him. I’ll take her now and cloud her mind from the truth. Thankyou…” the ghost replied, dissolving slowly and moving through the air. It spread all over her body like water as though gravity had altered, then merged into her, inside her. Then her eyes fluttered open. The first thing she saw was Nerezza looking down on her. She rose to her feet but fell down quickly, landing on her backside and once more resting on the tunnel wall. “Where am I?” she slurred as though drunken and half-awake. Nerezza quickly thought of a simple excuse, obviously not wanting to explain that a ghost had possessed her body. “You fainted in front of me.” Nerezza said. “I caught you and propped you up against the wall until you came around.” “Really? I don’t feel bad or anything” Lumi said, standing and brushing herself off. “But thanks!” she squeaked. Nerezza couldn’t help but see a slight reflection of Tempest in her manner. Although at this stage, if he received the same kind of hug from Lumi as he would from Tempest, he’d see That Man’s gaze behind Lumi’s eyes. “You’re not suffering from any pain, are you? You know where you live, right?” Nerezza asked, still slightly out of concern. “Yeah, I live at… hey, I’m not telling some stranger my address!” She quickly yelped. The vampire adapted quickly. “Heh, looks like you know that old trick. Oh well. If you feel worse, see a doctor or something. Take care…” Nerezza waved, turning his back and walking back in the direction Gaz went earlier. Lumi, under That Man’s influence, headed off in the opposite direction. As he walked away, Nerezza began to ponder. “Like Enigma, That Man now hides behind a mask. A very, very pretty mask. Also like Enigma, he has secrets that shall one day become known. I wonder though… will That Man take off his mask first… or will I have to force people to remove Enigma’s mask for an appropriate name in the obituaries?”
