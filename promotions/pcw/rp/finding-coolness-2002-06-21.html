Finding Coolness?

21 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21th June Surfs up...Surfs up?!Did I just use that term... for like, the first ever time in my existance on this planet? Wow, I guess it is true... looking at information about the enemy really does make you start to slide into their realm a little more. Not a day passes me by... when I think about the kinds of carnage and mayhem I could be causing... instead of only being able to get this damned job at the Narrator of one of the biggest loser bands in the history of the world.. meh, this duo aren't even a band for that matter... they're a couple of kids... some people like Ant and Dec... a couple of boys who want to believe that everything around them is cooler than life itself... but alas, I guess some things are just that little bit cooler than life itself. So, what happened to our heros in the last part? Should I be bothered to sit here and go through all the details of the event.. just to please you people who couldn't afford to place five minutes of your lives... engrossed in another fantastic episode of a seriously fantastic installment? I mean... you people, you always have the time to watch... Eastenders... Home & Away... or something totally lame on those lines... but then, you just don't have the time to curl up, flick the channel a few times and say, 'Hey... you know what I should do now... I should look back at what the 'Pretty Cool Guys' got up to the first installment of their story... because lets face it, I think its becomming just a little bit of a drag for that narrator guy to keep on saying the same spanktastic stuff... over and over again...' In the previous episode of..Previously on...Last time when we were with...The many different tones that suggest one is looking back on the past, there are just too many of them for me to bother about at this very moment in Christian time. So, for all of you that are like... 'Hey, lets take a look at the latest tape that the boys of 'Pretty Cool Guys' managed to pull out... if you had any thoughts about reading over the aftermath and the effects of what happened last time... well, you're going to find yourself getting the very minor of detailing that I... or any of my brother-like Narrator companions would even dare to spare you! .. And so, there they were... both Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore... running off into the distance, the distance that would lead them to one of the biggest beach parties in the history of the term. Our heros had decided to do something that nobody else would have dared to do... that nobody else in their history would have dared to do... even if it depended on the ending to their ever to insignificant lives. True, this is not going to be as shocking as Madonna accepting £1 to go topless... and true, this is not going to be as shocking as the very first moment that George Michael finally realized everyone knew he was a little batty boy... however, that doesn't go to say that nothing you're about to see in this fantastic installment is going to exciting! I mean... how can it no get exciting from here on through? I mean... we have a beach as the setting... we have loads of hot chicks walking around in all their fantasy glory... and best of all, we have the dudes that the 'Pretty Cool Guys' will be trying to mix with... trying to prove their coolness to as they struggle to make the cut of men... as they struggle to make the unbearable transition from PoP Icon... to rude boy surfer like their new found icons for today... 'Heavy Metal' Harry and 'Totally Tubular' Tommy of the Cowabunga Connection!!! So... now, without any more delay... and without anymore of the explanation for what I will be expecting these two fellows to do within the installment of the lifetime... I bring forth... I bring to you... I bring to... everyone in their homes... the NEXT installment... of the 'Pretty Cool' Story... Hello... and welcome back to yet another installment by the story that is not only gripping America... thats not only gripping all the poor people in Europe, but generally, gripping people by the throats all around the world... and by God, I really mean it... this current situation, its getting its big, sweaty, dirty, ugly man=hands around the throat of whatever person is standing around... its taking that person by their head, looking it square in the eye and shouting, 'OI... YOU... THIS... IS THE DAMNEST MOST SHOCKING THING IN THE WORLD.... ITS GRIPPING DAMN IT... SO LET IT GRIP YOU!!!' Jesus, that gets me all the time... the intensity... the emotion and of course, the dramatic course of action that went along with this decision to change... for one day... and one day only of course. The decision that we made... was to dress up like 'dudes'... to meet the stereotypical value of the dude and walk into a beach party... riding high and hanging cool with all the people who define the term... cool! We join both myself and Ryan Moore as we walk along the beach on a calm and very hot afternoon. Of course, I could go over what we were wearing again... but that would just be waste of both my time and your time... hmm, wasting your time though, its not something that I would go out of my way to let pass now is it? Ha... and there we were, walking across the beach wearing out very lightly colored shorts... mine, beaming bright Orange while my assosciate had decided to purchase the pair that came along in a very nice shade of Blue. As the time passed us by... while walking over the beach that is, I decided to open up a little bit of conversation with my friend... just to keep the mood lively, and get us into that zone of 'Coolness' and... 'Dudeness' for when we arrived at the location of this totally cool party. 'Hey Ryan... are you feeling cool at the moment?', I asked, looking over toward him, while continuing to kick at the sand with every step. He stopped for a few seconds and after taking a two pace lead, I stopped and turned around again to face him. He looked to be a little shocked about something... a little worried about something else... but regardless of how minor these events may have... or may not have been, I felt it was within the best interests of the group to follow through with a question or two, trying to find out what could be up with our little buddy here. 'Rich.. do you think that we're going to be able to pull off something of this scale... something this big... I mean, this is going to be bigger than the moment that Snake sneaked into that complex in Shadow Moses... its going to be bigger than the moment that Raiden was able to get into the Big Shell within Metal Gear 2... and hell, there is a chance that this is going to be bigger than that girl who had those totally massive...', he tries to finish the end of the line... but I was quick to raise my hand and cut him off from finishing the end of the line... I know the age of the people that are looking on at this feature.. and I don't want to expose them to the knowledge of things that I saw unless they have the full consent of their parents. Ryan looked a little taken back by the way my hand had shot up and cut him off.. it was almost as though he assumed my reaction had been with hostile intent, although I was quick to remind the fellow singer, that I was doing this for his benefit... and not his disadvantage. 'Ryan.. I know what you're thinking about right now... two very big things that could eat the whole entire planet if anyone was to find out exactly what they were... but its too damn bad because I know what those problems happen to be. The first, you're wondering why I raised my hand up... dude, I don't need to be reminded about how big they were... and second, this mission... it is going to be something that not even James Bond was able to do in Goldeneye... its going to be something that Indiana Jones was unable to do in Raiders of the Lost Ark... its going to be... what Ethan Hunt was unable to do... in Mission Impossible... my friend, we are going to pose as dudes... and we are going to become two of the coolest dudes that have ever come to exist in this pathetic little world... and you know why we're going to become two of the coolest dudes?', I asked, starting to sound a little more intensified with every passing second. Ryan could only look ahead at me while I stood in the sand... he shook his head from side to side, not even being able to understand what could make us that cool. 'We're going to become two of the coolest dudes that this beach has ever come to see... because we have the one thing that nobody else in the world has been able to harness... true, people like Xavier Kannon and Miyagawa have been able to ultilize the value of 'Star Pull'... but my friend, we have been able to harness the powers of something alot greater... and of alot more value to people in this situation... we have been able to obtain the 'PoP Pull'... a power thats very, very similar to the 'Star Pull'... but at the same time... its very, very different.', I ended the line by placing alot of emphasis down on the 'different' part, turning away from Ryan and looking over toward the ocean, imagining myself as being something of a rogue... something of a cool thief that was living his life as a lone-ranger... talking about how this single power was going to become the greatest power in the history of mankind... and I was on top of the moon... ontop of the world... until... un... til... he asked the dumbest question in the history of all things human! 'What... in the red, white, yellow, blue, green, indigo and violet hells are you talking about Richie?', he asked, sounding really serious about the question being posed. If I was as nasty as alot of people seem to think I am... I could have... and hell, if he was anyone else, I would have laughed my PoP Star ass off about his question.. but instead, I was able to dig deep... and hide the sniggers that were coming out of every pocket... I was able to chill for a few seconds before turning around, looking Ryan Moore in the eye... and giving him a few answers to set his little childish mind at ease. 'Ryan.... just a few things I'm going to tell you know... with an answer to your question... and a small pointer about the question itself. OK, I'm going to start with the question... I gotta hand it to you, you decided to use a little bit of innovation and give me something that was not only interesting... but damned colorful... with all the red... yellow... and blues... yes, it was colorful... but like, the answer to your question... 'PoP Pull' is something that you have inside of you... like Yoda said to everyone that could be bothered to hear his wrinkly ass voice... it is all around us... and we are one with it... its the Force for all those who dabble in the world of 'PoP'... Britney Spears being one of the only girls who can't use such powers..', I stated, brushing my arm across my nose and looking down to the sand for a few moments. Ryan Moore caught onto what I was saying about Britney and began to laugh a little... it seemed as though the compound in her boobies was preventing the powers from ever being used. Ryan Moore walked over and stood next to me, I patted the guy on his back a few times, before looking off into the distance and continuing to walk forth, Ryan Moore tagging along a few metres or so behind. We continued to walk at a normal pace... something that hadn't been achieved for a long time... but it was something we had been able to perfect now... Ten Minutes Later... Yes... ten minutes later, we had been able to arrive at the very scene of the party... the beach... although there was one small problem with the size of the party... and maybe even the location that I had thought about so much over the last ten minutes or so... wow, uncannily, that was the duration of the journey over here. Both myself and Ryan Moore walked into the small ruled off area where I assumed the party of all time was going to take place... the only problem now was the fact that... nothing happened to be around for this party... hell, it was almost like we'd missed it or something. Ryan stroked his chin a few times... copying the way that a man with a goatee would do so. He began to pace around in a circle for a few moments, before stopping... and giving forth his diagnosis of the problem... 'Hmmm.... Mr.Sherwin...', he began... putting on a voice that made him sound like some kind of lame inspector. I didn't say anything to him at the time... ya know, since I was so nice and everything. Instead, I just decided to play along with his attempts to be 'Inspector' like... and looked around the immediate area as he continued with, '.... it would seem as though the party has finished... and that we are actually something in the region of hours or so late...' I froze on the beach at that moment, the information slowly beginning to process through my mind... through my body... and then coming back out as something of a reaction to the information that had been digested. I stomped in the sand for a few moments, throwing something of the biggest temper tandrum that could ever been seen by somebody... yes, it was a moment in my life that will forever make me look like a little child... but hey, at least it did me a little good... and I even lost about two hundred calories or so during the entire process! 'Well Ryan... I guess, we'll just have to be cool... another way..' That was the last line I muttered in this failed adventure... but alas, good things come to those who wait... at least, thats what I'm hoping for anyway... Will Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore be able to prove themselves as being 'Cool' before they take on the Cowabunga Connection on Fusion? Nobody knows for sure... but to find out any more answers... STAY TUNED!!!
