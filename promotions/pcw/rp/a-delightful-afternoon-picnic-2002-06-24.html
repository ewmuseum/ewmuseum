A Delightful Afternoon Picnic

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/24/02 The camera fades in... We see the shadows group of people walking up a hill in a grass-covered and scenic park. The sun shines down on the earth with an incredible light, and the group of people are slowly unveiled as the camera peers upwards towards the sky. The sound of the wind breezing across the landscape and birds chirping in the tress brings incredible peace to the group of friends, who continue to walk towards their destination. “How long do we f*cking have to take mother f*cking c*ck sucker!?!?” Bertha stampedes up the hill, carrying a large basket with her. “If you f*cking snooze, you f*cking lose, b*tch @$$es!!!” Behind her walk the Chef and his new tag team partner, Alexis Parshin. The Chef: Are you sure you want to go with the “Terrorists R’ Us” name Alexis? I mean, why not something catchier like “Allah’s Boys”…eh? Parshin: Well Chef, to me that sounds like we are insinuating that Allah has testicles and we are his testicles… The Chef: I see, so no testicles? Parshin: No Chef, no testicles… Allah created the world through his word, the most powerful and awe-inspiring force imaginable. The Chef: Yeah Yeah, uninspiring, powderful…I hear ya! Behind the Chef and Parshin trudges Raghab, carrying what looks to be a heavy backpack full of dining utensils such as plates, napkins, spoons, forks, oyster mallets, lemon balers, citrus zesters, wine glasses, soup bowls, salad forks, water glasses, and the almighty ladle… Raghab: I am not cut out to do such work! The Chef: Qualified or not Raghab, you need to lose some weight! Raghab: But I am happy with my figure! The Chef: No you aren’t… Raghab: But I am! Allah has given me this form, and I shall stay that way throughout my earthly life. My only hope is I inherit a skinny body on my return to heaven with Allah… The Chef: Listen pal, I had to go on the Richard Hatch Liposuction Plan for almost eight trials before I could lower my weight down to a brisk 284 poundaroonies… Parshin: (Snickering) And Raghab needs the exercise anyways… Raghab: Bah! Bertha: F*cking stop you @$$ lickers!!! Bertha holds the group up at the top of a hill shaded under a magnificently huge tree. Bertha: We f*cking eat her, f*cking now!!! The rest of the group hurries to whip out the picnic blanket and the utensils. Bertha begins pulling sandwiches out of the basket and downing them in one gulp. Bertha: F*cking hurry up, and I’ll save a (gulps) f*cking sandwich for ya mother f*ckers!!! Raghab rolls the blanket out and The Chef and Parshin lay the utensils on the blanket in the correct fashion. Bertha tips the basket over and empties out the rest of the food onto the blanket. Luckily all side dishes were enclosed in Tupperware containers. The Chef: Praise be to Allah for Tupperware!!! Parshin: Have you ever tried GladWare, Chef? The Chef: Nah, Tupperware is just alright with me… Parshin: But GladWare is just as strong as Tupperware, and is microwavable, all for the fraction of what you would pay for a Tupperware container!!! The Chef: You’ve been watching that commercial with George Costanza’s mom haven’t you? Parshin: (Lowering his head) Yes… Bertha: Well f*ckos! Let’s f*cking dig in! Raghab: I’m with you Bertha!!! The Chef: WHOOOOOOOOAAAA!!! Hold on one second! We haven’t said a prayer to Allah… Parshin: Yes, we must thank Allah for everything! The Chef: Even when we get an erection? Parshin: Yes… The Chef: But how about if I take Viagra? Parshin: Well, it certainly was Allah’s will that Viagra would be made and that you would be able to purchase it… The Chef: (Nods) Praise be to Allah!!! Everyone except Bertha bows their heads. Parshin: Bertha, since you are so ready to eat, why don’t you lead the prayer? The Chef: I wouldn’t… But before the Chef can finish, Bertha begins her prayer… Bertha: Oh mother f*cker, you’re so f*cking fine! You’re so f*cking fine, you f*cking blow my mother f*cking mind, f*ck you Allah!!! Now let’s f*cking eat!!! Bertha then grabs a bowl full of Seven Layer Salad and downs the entire quantity of food, including the dressing bottle. The Chef shrugs and digs in as well, piling some potato salad onto his plate. Raghab nitpicks through a fruit salad under the watchful eyes of the Chef. Parshin slowly unwraps a roast beef sandwich. The Chef: So Alexis, what do you think about our chances this weekend at Gold and Glory? Alexis: I don’t know Chef; you are the veteran, why don’t you give us your input? Bertha: Yeah c*ck s*cking mother f*cker!!! The Chef: Ok, I believe we will triumph over all our adversaries on Sunday and will be crowned Tag Team Champions. We simply have too much raw talent, too much power, too much experience, and we are allied with the one true god, Allah… Parshin: We surely have many good things going for us, praise be to Allah for them… The Chef: But tonight we have to help Mr. Nelson and company win a big battle over some Malice Crew infidels and that piece of rancid pork, Miyagawa… Parshin: Yes, and with the help of the religiously confused yet very talented Xavier Kannon, we will surely triumph over our adversaries with relative ease… The Chef: The behavior of the Malice Crew has been rude, crude, and unacceptable, and I believe we have Allah-given rights to exterminate them pronto!!! Bertha: Where’s my f*cking roast beef sammich b*otch!?!? Parshin does a Vince McMahon-esque Adam’s apple gulp and hands a half eaten sandwich to Bertha. The Chef: Now Bertha, you can’t eat every sandwich here, we have to be generous and provide for all the followers of Allah, just like it says in the Holy Koran. Parshin snatches the sandwich from Bertha, nods arrogantly and finishes the rest of the sandwich in one gulp. Big B is infuriated… Bertha: Holy f*cking sh*tty as f*ck mother f*cking c*ck sucking @$$ humping piece of c*ck @$$ed shit mother ho @$$ f*cking all night for a d*ck @$$ed mother f*cking nickel b*tch @$$ed f*cking skank ho fairy @$$ed piece of f*cking shit @$$ed Mongolian Sh*t Bird, finger f*cking son of a b*tch @$$ed mother f*cking mother f*cking the mother I’m f*cking f*cking to f*cking with pansy @$$ loser @$$ed piece of f*cking cow sh*t for brains f*cking crack whore!!! Raghab falls instantly in love. Raghab: My Allah… Bertha lunges at Parshin, tipping over various Tupperware bowls, glasses, and containers. She sticks her hand right down the throat of Parshin, trying to get him to regurgitate her roast beef sandwich. Bertha: F*cking throw that piece of f*cking sh*t back up mother f*cker! Parshin: EEEHHHHHAHHHHAARRRGGGHHHHH!!! The Chef: My Tupperware! Don’t step on my Tupperware!!! The Chef dives onto the blanket and rummages up the rest of his unscathed plastic bowls. He notices a small bowl in his arms labeled “Bertha’s tranquilizers”. He pulls out a tipped tranquilizer dart and STICKS IT IN THE BACK OF BERTHA’S NECK!!! Bertha slumps over… Bertha: …………….. Parshin begins to gag and wretch, trying to breath normally… The Chef: ……….. Raghab: ………………. Bertha: ………………F*CKING C*CK SUCKER!!! Bertha suddenly wakes up from the tranquilizer and goes right back to cramming her hand down the throat of Alexis Parshin!!! The Chef just shrugs and gives up. The Chef: That’s my Bertha!!! The camera fades out on the Culinary Couple and their guests as Bertha continues to struggle to free her lunch from the windpipe of Parshin… Fade to black…
