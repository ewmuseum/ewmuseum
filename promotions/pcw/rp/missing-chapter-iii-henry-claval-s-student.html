Missing: Chapter III: Henry Claval's Student

undated

Date: TODAY Name: Henry Claval Age: 38 Other Aliases: None Known at this time Occupation: Con Artist, Chief Detective, Former CIA Agent Other Information: Henry Claval's location has been unknown for almost eight years and is believed to be hiding in the arctic or middle east. Henry is considered very armed and dangerous and only a few selected people know where Claval is. Henry Claval's top student, Fredrick Thomas is believed to be the only real person who has been keeping in touch with Claval. Henry taught Thomas in 1994 the trick of the trade and the secrets of being a con artist. The last time police got in touch with Fredrick Thomas was in 1995 when he was picked up for minor theft in New York. After Thomas was released from jail, the police realized who they were dealing with and sent out a look-out team for Thomas to no avail. Soon after, Fredrick Thomas' name was totally forgotten, as he was believed to go under another alias. No other information at this time. This was amazing! I knew that Ivan Chistov was in contact with Claval but I didn't know how dangerous this Henry Claval could be. I looked over the information in the database program that was on my computer. If I were to find Ivan, you could be sure that I could find Henry not far behind. I heard some breathing behind me suddenly on the airplane that would take me to PCW's next venue and turned around to see a man, in about his forties or so grinning at me. Man: What do you have their? Sounds interesting. Anger filled inside my chest. Couldn't I do anything in private anymore. I replied as lightly as I could. SyN: Do you mind? The man smiled at me and nodded. Man: Sorry sir. The man slumped back down into his airplane seat and I shifted my spot. Making sure the guy behind me couldn't see what I was doing, I continued my work. I still had no real information on Ivan. I read over Claval's biography one more time and the name, 'Fredrick Thomas' sprung out to me. I nodded to myself and typed in Fredrick's name into the database. I waited patiently for the information I needed to pop up. Name: Fredrick Thomas Age: ??? Other Aliases: Ivan Chistov, Fred Mystique, ???, ???, ??? Occupation: ??? Other Information: Fredrick Thomas resides currently in Germany where he is a priest at a local church. Fredrick was framed by a Henry Claval a few years back and vowed to get revenge. Fredrick lost connection with Claval in 2001 and went to his home town to become a priest and change his ways. No information on whether Thomas is still in Germany. A priest at a local church? That does not sound right from some one who was trained by a former Con Artist. I looked at the past aliases and realized what was happening. Wait a second! Fredrick Thomas is Fred Mystique and Ivan Chistov! If they are all the same person, then what is going on? Fred Mystique was a former name Ivan used in his fortune telling scam so I don't need to worry about that. Ivan Chistov was just in New York at the Hare Krishna before he escaped with four million dollars. If Fredrick Thomas is in Germany as a local priest, then what about Ivan? Did Ivan goto Germany after robbing the Krishna? If he did, then the computer database I was working with was extremely up to date! Why would Ivan just go and become a priest? I needed to find out the other three aliases that Ivan had and then maybe I could figure out where, and who he was. I wasn't too sure anymore. The only way I could find out for sure would be to go into Germany, find out where Thomas is and see if he really is the priest that it says in his biography. All the pieces fit together and yet, I had no information! I would be in Germany anyways with PCW so I might as well give it a shot!
