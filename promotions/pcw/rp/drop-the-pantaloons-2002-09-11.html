DROP THE PANTALOONS!

11 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/11/02 Last time we left Rush, we saw that he was screaming about something in his bedroom as he looked in, while ceasing from grumbling about the security that was ordered to protect him, but he still doesn’t know why. “My…BEDROOM! The only time this many men were in a bedroom….Flaymin Aymin and some farm animals were involved! That would be the sort of wonderful introduction we have grown accustomed to from Rush Mason, as usual while we begin to fade in while Rush starts spouting some random things that would make sense…well…at least be in better context if we could see what was happening. “Seriously….what is this? My fucking first-rate bedroom looks like the set of a low-budget porno! Well, maybe it’s better that we’ve haven’t faded, but now as the scene clears…it’s time for the kids to go to bed…because this sadly is not a show about a purple dinosaur… Rush: Is one of you Neanderthal morons comprehending what I’m saying? Because I don’t speak grunting unfortunately. The scene shows Rush still standing his doorway, tapping his foot impatiently while quite a few security guards sort through his things and others stand guard, and yet again Rush is frisked, and begins to yell while the guard checks him for weapons. Rush: Why am I being frisked in my own bedroom?! Wouldn’t the point be to protect me? What, do you think I’m gonna pull some sort of faggy Goth kid suicide? The frisker ignores him, and then steps back, and lets Rush step fully into his bedroom, and he looks around with a skeptical look at the men, shaking his head, as one of the men responds, looking down at the floor. “Unfortunately sir, we can’t discount the possibility of the terrorists impersonating you to get to the real you. We have gone to Security Level Tangerine, you know? Rush hits himself on the forehead and covers his face in disgust with his hands, trying to understand this logic. Rush: So…have I already walked into my room, geniuses? Or is security level Papaya coming? The men look sheepishly up and shake their heads to indicate a negative answer. Rush goes over to sit down on the bed, but as he sits down, a whooping alarm is set off, and the walls turn in, shocking Rush as men with pistols begin pointing them in the direction of Rush on the bed. “Stop, Intruder!” “Secure the premises, we’ve got one of the terrorists!” Rush: IT’S ME YOU NIMRODS!! I’m Rush Mason…you know the Future of PCW…the first-rate guy who lived here- alone I might add- until you ass bandits made your way in here! The men who came from within look around, still not convince, and the man closest to Rush points his gun at him while he addresses him. “Sir…we’re gonna have to ask you to drop your pants..” Well, we thought Rush’s jaw dropped when he stepped into the doorway of his room, but this time his jaw drops approximately to his knees when he hears this, and screams back at the security guard, his yell shaking the room Rush: THIS IS MY HOUSE!! THERE IS NO WAY IN THE NAME OF BON DIEU THAT I AM DROPPING MY PANTS!! 5 Minutes Later… “Thank you for co-operating with our cavity search, Mr. Mason, it’s all in the name of your own safety, you know. Rush sits shivering on the bed, apparently suffering some sort of trauma from the cavity search. He finally snaps out of it as the men begin to walk back into where they came from on the other side of the wall. Rush: No way! This is my house and nobody goes into secret rooms without me! Besides, you second-rate piss-poor excuses for security guards would probably fuck up my house. The men pay little attention to Rush as he follows behind, walking into what is a much smaller room with a lot of computers and cameras and other electronic gadgets and Rush looks around confused, because he sees no other people amongst the machines. Rush: How the hell has this room been here…right under my first-rate nose?! A voice chimes in from somewhere behind one of the machines with a short squeak. “You should check out our interior decorater…he’s a little fruity…but very good for his thrifty price.” Rush looks around, stunned, and rubs his eyes, but looks around yet again and sees nothing and raises an eyebrow, confused with himself. One of the security guards is about to explain something, but Rush calls out to the room. Rush: Is this the Lights of Loa….Has Bon Dieu blessed me by sending me amongst the loa?! Suddenly, as the voice chimes in again, a small person steps out from behind the machine, notable shorter than the machine itself. “You didn’t suspect you had little people working for you?” Once again, Rush’s jaw drops as he begins to celebrate for some reason, dancing around pumping his fist. Rush: I HAVE MINI-ME’S!!! THE END DOESN’T HAVE MINI-ME’S!!! THE FRONT OFFICE DOESN’T EVEN HAVE MINI-ME’S, BUT I FUCKING DO!!! Everyone in the room looks at Rush strangely as he tries to straighten himself up, and keep composure. Rush: Err…nice to know that there are people working behind the scenes…but seriously….how the hell is there a security system in my house that I don’t know about? The little person nods as Rush leans his ear down to hear the little guy, and the voice chimes into his ear. “We gotta save something for future RP’s…” Rush gives him a thumbs-up and then begins to walk back towards his bed room, and we hear the alarm go off yet again, but Rush just whispers to himself as the scene begins to fade… Rush: Fuckin’ Little People….oh man, this is so much better than being blind… Now the scene goes entirely to black as we hear the alarm still going off, but we hear voices as the scene fades away “Who goes there?!” “It’s me again you dipshits!” “Sir…we’re gonna have to ask you to drop ‘em…”
