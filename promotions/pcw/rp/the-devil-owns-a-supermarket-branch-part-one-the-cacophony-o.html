The Devil Owns A Supermarket Branch - Part One: The Cacophony of Bleeps, Idiots, and Assaulted Vendors.

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16th July 2002 - #1 for Turmoil FORTY-FOUR CANS OF CANNED SPROUTS. (Quick OOC Comment – I apologize once more for a distinct lack of roleplays over the past week, which is mainly down to school and work. Next week, the former won’t exist for a month or so, so feel free to book me as much as you want.) The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book Three – Deus Ex Toaster…… RP#1 for Tuesday Turmoil, 16th July 2002… Location: The Oxford, Mississippi branch of Wal-Mart, tucked between a run-down bowling alley and a row of pawn shops. (No, Pawn, not Porn…Perverts!). Conditions: A typically balmy Summer evening…Sunday. Chapter One: The Devil Owns a Supermarket Branch – Part One: The Cacophony of Bleeps, Idiots, and Assaulted Vendors. -=- “OOOOOOOOH! CANNED SPROUTS! I tell you Chris, we seriously can’t have too many of these, I was raised on these bad boys, don’t ya know? Of course you don’t, you blew your chance with the PCW World Title, that’s why you’re dumber than I am! …Don’t ignore me when I’m trying to berate you…and fill you in on the awesome intricacies of the canned Sprout…” “Could you do that in some other manner rather than buying forty-four cans of that junk? Perhaps by illustrating the positive merits of some downright disgusting item of canned produce in some spontaneous way at least? Look Dustin, don’t get me wrong here…but heck, Canned Sprouts aren’t exactly going to remedy the problems at stake…after all, what’s that possibly going to do to avoid this judder?” “Well…come to think of it, Sprouts are notoriously effective in removing the after-effects of Electro-therapy, notably after about 5 days or so, post-trauma…” “Do you have any idea of what you’re rambling on about again, Dustin? Jesus Christ almighty…can’t you stop making such random comments and get to your point, rather than leading us off on a skewed route exploring the joys of slapping a moron with a frozen fish?” “You’d really do that? Seriously?” “I did punk out the Dark Age, Dustin…” ::.~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ Retail. The persistent selling of sundry items to cater for the needs of your own self. How typically, and rightfully pedestrian. The deception is easy, the perception less so. Idiot customers walk in day by day, claiming that they ‘need’ such an item or whatnot. But being the eternal cretins that they might be, Dustin Downey or otherwise, they’re being silently duped into lining the pockets of the greedy, pirate-esque habits of the average retailer. I mean, seriously, human beings have survived for a long time without retailers…in fact, we pretty much managed to survive for millennia on a diet of picked berries and carcasses of wild animals…for the wonderful price of nothing. Yep, it costs nothing more than a few hours labor to retrieve this food…but humans like you or me are lazy. Lazy enough to even consider going to a retail outlet nearby…of this little incident, it’s Wal-Mart. Wal-Mart have sales in excess of $100 Billion per year, the more impressive of outlets around the world. Yet, the ever cynical eye of ‘Saint’ Chris Chase is nonetheless, unimpressed…what? I’m not that opinionated! A Saint. A Saint is what I just may be…according to general opinion. But what right has general opinion have to pigeonhole me like that? Maybe I should just be grateful that they’ve managed to simultaneously solve the ever-recurring problem of the fact that ‘The Messiah’ was ever so slightly pedestrian. Society as a whole needs a more, should I say, saintly figure for it to produce a pragmatic savior for all its faults and idiocies. And that’s barely piercing the surface tension of a 100-meter deep lagoon. You see, mankind needs something, or maybe somebody, to usher away the evils…the typical and overly clichéd battle between good and evil. And well, the vile acts of the Dark Age have prompted me to just sit back, and receive whatever futile attack they may throw at me next, at the crusading drive behind PCW’s rehabilitation. And while Duke and Lexian squabble over a paltry PCW Continental Title, I’ll again be left to clean up the pieces, then conquer the federation on my lonesome, AGAIN. Let’s face it, I’m easily co-opted into these things. Why do you think I dropped the World Title? Dustin Downey wears a referee shirt. Yep, they were THAT desperate. By they, I mean those that run PCW from behind the scenes, you know – the ones that don’t necessarily have lives. Oh, now I don’t necessarily have a job…fine. Ahem, blatant mockery of the PCW staff aside, it’s Monday evening (Yep, I’m cutting a promo a little late again, yes, my “other” job sucks). Monday’s have never been kind to me, exactly. The beginning of the week is a tentative time, prior to Tuesday’s event, and very much a pointless day altogether. The time, well, it’s bordering on ten post meridian – the later hours of a already eventful evening. Well, If you call Dustin attempting to take a lamppost ‘Downtown’ after initial ‘threats’ from the post eventful, of course. Wal-Mart isn’t quite as impressive as it makes out to be. There’s relatively little staff other than the vendors at their checkouts. The weather’s hot… Balmy even…as I sulk with bored overtones, sighing at any given moment….:: Chris: ~(sighs)~ You know Dustin, this is even more positively INANE than the time we exhibited PCW-based puppets in an ice-rink. Come to think of it, it’s not…but it’s pedestrian all the same – we walk into a retailer outlet of all places, we pay for forty-four cans of Canned Sprouts – being forced to pay through our noses as a consequence. You KNEW my budget had practically been blown after the steps taken so that I could resist the attacks of those irritating Satanic Jobbers… Dustin: Huh? I’m a PCW employee now for your sake! And on the subject of those pesky Satanic Jobbers, do you like anyone, Chris? Wait…that’s a stupid question. Now then, for a question with a purpose…how much of these bad boys should I give to my ever growing hamster horde? ::. The sound of palm meeting forehead can be heard reverberating around the room. A cry for help no less, with Dustin being the forever dumb-witted, hamster-loving moron that’s he’s always managed to be. Not that that’s unsurprising, or even unexpected – the likelihood of Dustin forming some kind of relevant and intelligent thought in his head is as likely as the three-man tag team match ever becoming more than just a quick fix rumble. Who invented the Three-Man Tag? I’d like to know. Why? I’d like to play and thoroughly thrash the bastard at tiddly-winks and teach him just what it feels like to be in an utterly pointless match. Call me an ego or whatever, but I need no help to take on the Dark Ages…they’re past it. Let’s face facts, Satanists had a superficial ‘coolness’ to them, and a perceived IQ of above genius level. Crucifying people was in fashion…black was the new black, and about half-a-million Goth chicks turned up in droves just to jump out of human-flavored cakes…Yum. Wal-Mart, isn’t so bad after all. Sure, there’s the faint smell of spit propagating for the shelves, and the managers are playing mini-golf with dancing girls, but it isn’t SO bad. Dustin prods me in the ribs with a stick. I hate it when he does that. I’d break the stick over his head if I didn’t have to keep the violence to a minimum. Thanks to a little known American federation, we have to be child friendly. Sure, Clyde provided a perfect example of a child-friendly man, but that simply wasn’t enough for the heads of the industry. They all wanted us to be ‘friendly’ to children, which would normally mean ten to twenty in a medium security prison and being subjected to continual prodding from a man named Trixie. The lights flicker on and off in the store, probably a power surge no doubt. Maybe Doug Troy’s in town, I don’t need to guess. Dustin again, pokes me, grabbing my attention by the balls and forcing me to comply without laughing my ass off at my own stupid analogies. Grabbing a packet of Corn cereal off the nearest shelf (I’ve heard that it comes with a free mysterious artifact of mass-destruction this month) and shoving it straight into the trolley, I finally bring myself to Dustin’s blank stare….:: Dustin: Jeez…you so-called ‘savior’ types should really learn to respect the space of the usual moron. Come on, am I really going to let you place a minimalist packet of cereal in my trolley, if only to replace one of the forty-four cans of Canned Sprouts? ::.I sigh….:: Chris: Dustin, there’s room; you did manage to pick up the Trolley-Grande after all. It’s not like it’s one of those freaking economy trolleys that wobble all over the place, forcing you to force an aisle 12 clean-up without doing so much as push the damn thing. It’s downright foolish to think that forty-four cans of Canned Sprouts can ruin our chances of fitting anything else in there? Dustin: But what about the intervention of a strolling minstrel and a couple dozen Satanists? Chris: We can always take that into consideration… ::.I snort with the tiniest hint of arrogance at this. Consider it? Hardly…I wouldn’t touch it with forty-four cans of Canned Sprouts and a packet of cereal. It’s like making the word ‘BOOM’ your own official catchphrase, to think of it takes less brain energy than it does to flick a switch…and to carry it out takes less effort than taking a stroll down to a food retail outlet. Which effectively brings me back to the scene at hand. Cracked eggs litter the floor of the adjacent aisle. Obviously some freckled intern hit it lucky with one of the female vendors and spent half an hour trying to futilely reach the Promised Land. Either that or a horde of rampaging midgets managed to once again have an influence on the proceedings in a Chris Chase promotion. The pair of us bypass the aisle, not exactly rocket-science, but there’s reason enough to explain why. No-Egg-On-Feet. Got it? Good. Now that you’ve done questioning the actions of your supposed savior and Patron Saint, let’s consider two options. A – Continue to collect inane and never-seen-again items until the cows come home and Jordan dies of over-marking…or B – Find some other subject matter to ponder to cleverly disguise a change in the plot. Or, for Communists and people who consider 2-Answer questions to be restricting… C – I can do fuck all about it and let the spontaneity continue en masse. A great man once said to me: ‘If in Doubt, Pick C…’, of course, his options included a bath in human excrement, Jobbing to Tim Walden, or hitting it off with a foreign supermodel. Lucky Bastard. So, what’s it going to be Chase? Well…do you think I’m really going to tell you? Am I?.:: Voice: Can I help you sirs? ::. The Checkouts are never far away, in fact, you can’t leave an aisle without running into one of the thirty or so on offer. It’s like appearing at a wrestling federation only to find a dozen cocky guys – inescapable by every single means. The checkouts at this particular Wal-Mart are typical of every retail store, they’re generic. They’re the Xavier Kannons of the checkout world, used by the spotty and snooty employees of the company, the kids that usually end up looking like they’ve been dragged through a couple thousand bushes and getting increasingly tired of it. Taking a moment to glance at my watch, I grab the trolley and fling it in the general direction of an occupied till, where the black-haired vendor lives. Yep, apparently they don’t even let the poor guy out after the store closes… To cut a long story short, Dustin and I silently packed the forty-four cans of Canned Sprouts and the packet of cereal onto the conveyor belt, without so much as a word, a sharing of communication between two former Tag Team partners, and now Wrestler and one of his federation’s referees. The vendor glances at us, with somewhat of a menacing grin on his face. I’ve always known not to trust that kind of person – due to personal experience with a trio of Satanists, but that’s another story….:: BLEEP! ::. The sound of one item being scanned can be heard over the silence of the store. Seemingly disturbed by this, Dustin flinches….:: BLEEP! ::. And again. Sighing in a typical manner befitting of the person that I am – an impatient, misunderstood genius who has a few claims to fame to his name – I smack Dustin with a swift backhand….:: OW! BLEEP! BLEEP! BLEEP! ::.A cacophony of bleeps picks up as the two of us stand there, watching the rather evil looking vendor scan through the items of our choosing….:: Dustin: Why in the blue blazes did you have to hit me? ::.Dustin’s question goes unanswered, like many before it. Indeed, the ratio of questions asked to questions answered for Dustin in particular is pretty minimal to say the least. It’s like a cry for help, only in a rather muted sense that no one truly pays attention to. It’s the perfect analogy for Satanists…as Dustin is about to find out, as the forty-fifth and final item passes through the checkout. The Vendor looks down at us from his position on the checkout, smiles, then brings up the bane of all shoppers throughout the world. The Price….:: Vendor: Err…that’ll be thirty-four dollars and twenty cents please. Do you want to pay via cash, check, card…or…your lives? BWAHAHAHAHAHAHA! *coughs* Sorry, I didn’t mean that… Dustin: I’ll pay with my card…what possible harm could it do? ::. Now, as our mind extends to include that of Tuesday’s imminent match…Dustin passes his credit card over, only to be subsequently passed a slip for him to sign. He duly signs, before looking dazed for quite some time, muttering to invisible objects standing on the shoulders of the vendor…then….::\n WHAM! SMACK! PUNCH! MAUL! ::.Dustin does the unthinkable….:: To be Continued… Next time: Dustin assaults his own personal demons, Chris displays another fluent performance of blatant self-promotion, and this narrator learns how to speak fluent moron. Only here…on the Chris Chase network.
