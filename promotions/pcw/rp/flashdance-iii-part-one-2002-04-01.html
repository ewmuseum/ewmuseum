Flashdance III - Part One

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 1st 2002 OOC – Apologies for my piss poor Fusion performance…school work etc hampered my ability to Roleplay. This roleplay’s major influence comes from “Flashdance II” by Matthew Good Band…I suggest that you download that song…NOW! The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book One: In the Council of the Immortals…… RP#1 for Turmoil and #1 for Spring Fling… Location: Manhattan Island, New York City. Chapter Four: Flashdance III – Part One – Barely Living, But I’m living large… “~(Reading from a Magazine Clipping)~ ‘Thanks to the likes of Alexander Miyagawa and Chris Sloboda, the PCW lockerroom has apparently been milling with rumours and questions concerning the sexuality of one Chris Chase, Number One Contender for Sloboda’s World Title. According to such rumours…he’s gay…’ ~(Sarcastically)~ Oh whoopee-do…they finally figured out my secret. I suppose this means that they’ll send me into the jobberific ranks of the Ream Team, eh? Ah…they’ll make anything up if they’re that jealous of yours truly…” “~(Coughs)~ People jealous of Chris Chase? Oh, when did that happen? Was that when you were dating that Russian Mad woman? It’s no wonder that Miya-jobber decided that he wanted one of those too…” “Jeez…did you really have to bring up Uma at this time of day? I’m still having trouble trying to block out the mental images of the daily torture sessions over the Christmas period. Besides Serenity, I thought you whores have a strictly no-touch policy…what’s with THAT?” “Pardon me Chris, your cousin had me escort you…and it’s my duty to keep the future of PCW away from any detrimental influences that could harm your chances of realising your year long dream of capturing the PCW World Title…” “When since did a stripper have a wide range of vocabulary?” ::.~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ One-offs happen. Like the World Title reign of one Stewart Hix, like Doug Troy jobbing to HANK~! Yes, they happen from time to time, popping up like the nuances in the eternal law of the universe that they are. So much for paradigms set in the year 2001, though, how can I apply this theory to this year, to this timezone. Submitting to Miyagawa, a Japo-American gigalow with a poor taste in insults and a former tendency to run around like a complete goggle wearing lunatic. After injuring my own tag partner inadvertently, I was caught unaware by the swift and deft hand of a scrawny little nimrod, and thus after failing to save myself, having to submit to a move that basically sums up Miyagawa’s own homosexual urges, the urges that his own wife wouldn’t be able to put up with because he somehow got her pregnant the first time that he actually made love to someone. And I originally thought that incontinence was the only embarrassing flaw that he had to display… After all, all he’s been doing of late has been playing swallow the leader with current PCW World Champion Boda-Boy. As soon as he catches wind of a lame, and highly unoriginal insult conjured by the excuse for a jackass…he makes it his own, sucking it down to the very depths of despair and suckitude…leeching off the current “Top Dog” of the federation simply because he’d attempt to leap frog yours truly in the pecking order than even bother to reclaim my number one contendership by beating me. The cowardly cretin. A long and varied week stretches out into the horizon before me…first, with absolutely nothing on the line other than the pride of a former leader and his former lackey…as I go up against the pleasantly deranged individual of Big Alan Steele…the guy who thinks himself of something of a demi-god. Then, I have the pleasure of watching my opponents for Spring Fling and another six halfwits beat the hell out of each other as I referee my first match in the PCW… And then, in no less than seven days time, ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase gets his first REAL shot at PCW World Heavyweight Gold, in what could be a milestone match for the PCW. It’s hard to believe for you people isn’t it? That a young, impressive superstar such as myself is receiving his first shot at a prestigious title, after only just coming off the back of an ultra impressive Cruiserweight Title reign. Being only the third former Cruiserweight Champion to hold the most important title in this federation is something…being only the second wrestler from PCW2 that didn’t hold the PCW2 World Title to win the PCW World Title is even more impressive. However, if one thing’s going to knock me off my game…then it’s definitely these rumours of a homosexual Chris Chase….:: Stripper: Meh…half the schmucks that walk into here, desolate and poor beings, they don’t seem to realise that behind some strippers, some of us animals that there’s the intelligent, sophisticated side that’s occasionally missing from the bitter depths of society. I’ve got a Theology degree, and I’m making money through this…say, have you ever watched Dogma? Chris: About as many times as I’ve made jibes at Boda…it’s a great movie. Why’d you ask? Stripper: Well, I don’t know whether you’ve noticed or not Chris…it deals with much more than the pondering of religion in a modern sense. Why do you think that the schmuck of a heroine, a Catholic, works in an abortion clinic? To make money…sometimes we have to do things that go against our moral standing to get ahead in this world, Chris. ::. It’s a Sunday evening, the Sabbath day…a day that isn’t exactly held faithful by yours truly. I mean, if you had to work on a Sunday night, you’d lose faith in religion wouldn’t you? And thus, after careful deliberation with the powers that be, I’ve taken a day away from the trials of the PCW contingent to focus myself. Diligent Chase-fans around the world would remember my exploits with the Lesser New York Mafia, the Di Chase family…my distant cousins. Luigi Di Chase had an interesting proposition for me, which partly explains why I’m sitting in a strip joint with a woman somewhat resembling Salma Hayek. Fact is, I’m no homosexual…there’s no proof that I am. Come on, is it realistic for me to play for the other team after dating a Russian Mad Woman by the name of Uma ever since the incident with Borischev until early February…then dating other random women up to this very point? Whatever unoriginal jibes my respective opponents throw at me no longer matter…they can accuse me of such sins on the radio, on their promotions…but much like their careers…it’ll only be shortlived. I smirk to myself, as that fact finally catches up with me, as I sit next to a varnished bar and an unusually well-dressed employee of this joint. The inforced riffs of Matthew Good’s “Flashdance II” reverberate around the room, as Serenity…the woman that just happens to be accompanying me in this wonderous place chatters about the issues of being an intelligent stripper. Serenity betrays the definition of the word that is her name. Loud, obnoxious and a keen patron of sarcasm…with her latin accent, she represents some of the finer qualities of yours truly. In what must be strange for those used to Chris Chase Promotions of this ilk, Dustin Downey is absent from this exploit…off on official business. Instead, the joint is blessed with the installation of a cardboard cutout of Dustin Downey, that stands next to me with that same blissfully ignorant expression on its face….:: Dustin Cutout: WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! I AM JESUS!! WHOOOOOOOOOOOOO! ::. Of course, it’d be my pleasure to kill the nimrod who conjured this wonderous idea…which results in the annoyance of patrons in said strip bar and the bruised forehead of one Chris Chase, after line upon line of moronic catchphrases and witticisms. Strangely enough, surrounding the cutout are a crowd of dimunitive Japanese men….:: Japanese Man #1: Ah Dustin Downey-san, why it is an honour to finally meet you in person, after all…we’ve heard so much about your heroism in the Borischev incident of November 2001. Why, you saved America and the world from yet another Communist maniac… ::. In pure exasperation, I slam my fist squarely onto the bar, nearly knocking over a small cluster of drinks. The barman glares at me with a piercing stare, as I grumble frustratedly at the lack of true facts in that last sentence….:: Chris: ~(Growls)~ Grrr…I saved the World from Borischev and his motley crew of Communist Maniacs…not Dustin Downey. Heck, that was back when Dustin had been canned…all he had been doing then was sipping drinks back home in Texas. Ah well, it’s not like the spotlight that shines on me at every waking moment is ever going to peeter out, is it? With these minor embarrassments out of the way, tossed to the side like yesteryear’s Boda…it’s undoubted that Chris Chase, PCW World Heavyweight Champion, is going to herald a new age of wrestlers…a new era of talented superstars. And with me leading the way, who’s likely to have anything to say against it? Ha! That’ll prove the critics wrong… Serenity: Critics? They’re nothing more than schmucks. If you’re not good at anything in particular, than you can become a critic. Chris! What do these ‘critics’ have against you? ::. I shrug my shoulders ignorantly, smirking at the my awesomely illustrated exploits against the likes of critics…for you see, critics are the bane of Professional wrestling as a whole…they influence the trends of the public, they fool ordinary folk into thinking less desirable things about the heroes and saviours of the industry…in short, they’re more tiresome than the usual Boda promo…which in any case would be a key factor in the induction of a comatose state upon the populus….:: Chris: Jeez…what do critics have against life in general? That’s the second most important question in the flow of life at this point in time. What’s ultimately more important is the question concerning the PCW World Title, a certain PCW Pay-Per-View…and a certain, brilliant, awesomely talented wrestler by the name of Chris Chase. And now, for your viewing pleasure ladies and gentlemen, ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase is going to detail you in on the finer points of my triumphant victory at Spring Fling --- Serenity: ~(Cutting in)~ Wait Chris, aren’t you forgeting the beating of a poor schmuck by the name of Big Alan Steele? HI-KEE --- ::. Forget? How in the blue hell of Zanzibar land could I forget about that match? Why…it’d be wholly stupid of me not to remember the clash with Big Alan Steele, a man who also was once dubbed ‘Gay’ by his opponents and the media. With his name it’s not hard to see why. The dirty riffs of the aforementioned Matt Good song ring loudly in my brain, shaking me to the very depths of…whatever. You realise that coming up with this deep and downright shameless drivel is beginning to impede on the cocky soul that I am? Well then, why should I be forced to constantly force myself into speaking in this damn way…this damn over-sophisticated shiz that really shoots me into the oceans of snobbery… (Cough) Ahem…ah…mumblings aside, there’s far much more to the scene than meets the eye…you guys really don’t pay attention do you. Buxom and rightfully hot women fling themselves onto poles around the bar, entertaining the dirty old men that come to watch these women. Serenity flicks her thin, dark pony-tail of hair back over her shoulders, pulling the corners of her leather jacket ever closer together. Sipping on the straw of a cocktail drink, she looks up at yours truly with the glittering eyes of a teenager, of what a teenage girl would do when faced with probably the greatest PCW2 wrestler to only ever hold one PCW Championship belt…probably the greatest Cruiserweight Champion of all time…and Xavier Kannon’s own personal annoyance. Like I said, these places are meccas for dirty old men…like Boda. It’s no wonder that his wife has moved onto other man meat, when he keeps stalking the wife of an enemy….:: Chris: By Zeus Serenity! Why that be copyright infringement! HI-KEEBA! --- Jeez…how can anyone actually talk like that for the vast majority of the time, without becoming highly annoying ala my cardboard assistant here? ::.Please say it isn’t time for another pirate joke….:: Dustin: ARRRRR MATEY!! Shiver me timbers and blow the man down…I’m Dustin Downey – Hardcore and Mighty Pirate…ARRRRRRRRR!! ::. I smack the cardboard cutout with a fierce backhand, sending it to bounce off of the bar, before whacking one of the group of Japanese groupies around the forehead. Sighing to myself, I turn back to my female accomplice, who looks at me with a cynical stare….:: Serenity: Used to smacking your own managers, eh Chris? Ha! I can see why these schmucks have been calling you a ‘Closet Homosexual’…you get kicks out of beating up the harmless folk… Chris: I hardly think so…besides, beating the living crap out of Chris Sloboda is something that I’ve been doing well for the last year or so…so he’s harmless, he damn well deserves it! Especially for making those friggin’ allegations about me…I tell you, if that idiot was here right now…let’s just say that the vast majority of this joint’s furniture would be in a similar state to that Nimrod’s own career…dis-frickin’-jointed! Serenity: ~(sarcastically)~ Alrighty then…Mister Big Shot Superstar wrestler! Would that be because you’d have just been thrown through them? ::. Of all the idiotic notions…why, who’d of heard of something so outrageously preposterous?.:: Chris: Boda’s a damn slacker, he wouldn’t be bothered to take that liberty, even if he could for MY sake! And besides, if Miyaga-low the Gigalow was here, he’d be just a tiny bit easier to clean the bar with, you’d think? ::. To be continued…Fade to White!.::
