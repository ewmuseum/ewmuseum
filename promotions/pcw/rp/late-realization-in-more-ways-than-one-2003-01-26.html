Late Realization (In more ways than one)

Title: Late Realization (In more ways than one) Featuring: Tim Walden Date: Jan. 26, 2003 The sun beams through the window, across the inside of the hotel room all the way to where I'm eating my Chinese take-out...I can't stand it. The sun, that is. It's like being under interrogation as I eat, how can I enjoy the food in peace? I close the blinds, the feeling of borderline insomnia still against me for the moment...I couldn't sleep if I wanted to. Granted, it's only about 6 P.M., but the stress is starting to weigh on me again...Winning this match would just go to proving that I could beat Romantico at his own petty games....But somewhere along the line, he let up. Where was the attack? Where was his competitive spirit gone, that he would just avoid confrontation? This wasn't my usual sort of opponent, this was an underhanded little bitch who would be all-too-willing to take a few cheapshots to win the match...I wasn't in a mood to deal with him now, but maybe that was his entire plan...But what a sad plan. Defeat through irritation? Who the hell did he think he was, El Janito? Ugh...This guy was the polar opposite of the Trapezoid. Where the HIT had fought with at least shreds of honour, valiant in his hopes to displace me but unable to get the job done, Romantico has practiced evasion...Not of his taxes, but of me myself. It's as if the man was not even concerned about dealing with me before Shanghaied, he just wanted his title shot and I was in his way...Just like the fact that he couldn't outwrestle opponents whom I'd had only mild trouble with to get a contendership, just like the fact that when he DID get a shot he couldn't manage to keep me down for three, just like every other fact, he thought there was a way around me...And either to his credit or my fault, his way around managed to use my emotions as a distraction from the task at hand, delaying the subsequent confrontation sufficiently to make it aware enough that it wasn't his main focus, so that I would have no anger left to bring to the surface now... He was more clever than I had originally thought to give him credit for. The mental game was half of the battle, and keeping me on a runaround had caused me enough difficulty on that side that I could only now get straight my perceptions of his motives...His efforts had finally come to fruition, it seemed. I hadn't worked for this one as hard as I may have, too concerned with the mental stresses....If he'd merely kept me angry, he could've maintained my focus, but I couldn't make myself believe it was his strategy though and now I was denied my concentration.. I would have to resort to my plan for fighting him the first time...I could outwrestle him, I knew I could, but that was when I was at my peak condition. I'd gotten lax, now I was going to have more difficulty against him than before...But maybe I could get back onto the road to peak condition....I put down the chop suey and picked up the phone. 'Hello?' The surprise in his voice wasn't much of a shock, Jerry didn't get many calls on Saturday nights in his profession.. 'Hey Jerry, it's Tim..I was wonderin', could you keep the place open a little longer tonight? I've gotta get some late training done before my match tomorrow..' I knew he wasn't one to typically go past the normal closing time, but I had a good feeling that he'd make an exception in my case... 'Well, it's sort of unusual, but sure, I guess you could come in for a couple hours.' Just as I figured and hoped...Maybe there might be hope for me to best Romantico yet, thanks to my sensible pre-planning.. It may be tough to keep a fitness center open an extra night, but it's not quite so difficult as filling out Chinese tax forms. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
