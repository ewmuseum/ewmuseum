Date: 10.10.02 [1981]Back in the Day (~It is the year of 1981 and down in the dirty south of Atlanta Georgia are family of five sleep uncomfortably on their hard cheap out beds they were lucky to find near a dumpster. The somewhat apartment lies at the far end of the ghetto away from heavy population and nice houses. It is about 9:34pm, the sun is down and the dark sky is filled with millions of bright shining stars surrounding a huge elegant moon. The roof of the house barely supports rain and other things that may fall from the sky. All the tiles are gone only leaving the wet cement and wood beams that could collapse any minute. Drops of water pure down from the ceiling with huge bowls and cups catching pretty much everyone. The foul smell of dirty cloths and over flooding trash reeks throughout the apartment crashing fresh air. There is only one bedroom where the family sleeps or can afford to get seeming as the mother has no job and the father is barely supporting with his low wage job. The dirty wood floor is cracked and broken with dust and cloths spread about carelessly. A picture is nailed against the almond brown wall that was once white that says, 'God bless this mess', in a funny manner but it really is funny by chose seeming how they didn't choose to live this way. In the room they all sleep on this huge pee stained mattress, well at least that's what is smells like seeming how they rarely take a bath and really don't have good running water. The mattress is a nasty yellow-brown color with the rusted springs near the ends jaggedly sticking out. To the right of the bed a few feet away there is one of the two dirty smudged windows. You can hear the tree just outside screeching against the windowpane making another unsettling noise along with the gunshots and police sirens heard slightly far away. The three little African-American children lay shoulder to shoulder uncomfortably sound asleep as the also black mother lays in a sloppy manner on her stomach with slobber coming out her mouth and all. She has her arm resting on the husband, as he lies there wide-awake staring at the ceiling with a thousand of thoughts racing through his head. His dark black skin makes his light brown eyes stick out unusually along with his big pink lips. His nappy black hair is cut low and his nose fairly fits his face along with his ears. He lays with one hand behind his head seeming how he can't get his other hand free of his wife who is lying on it. The dad lays there pondering a million of thoughts wearing a dirty dark red short sleeve shirt that is kind of tight on him along with blue jeans that have dirty oil spots on them. He takes a long gaze at his wife and children who lay there like ghetto angels. The moonlight barely piercing through the dirty smudged window upon them faintly revealing the dust floating about in the air. As he watches his family lying there in this torn down, ghetto, smelly, and unstable apartment a tear comes to his eye and he knows he can no longer keep this up working low wage jobs to support them and buy them the things needed. The tear rolls down his check and he leans forward and lays a gentle loving kiss on his wife's check. Then in a sneaking manner not to wake any of them up he slides one leg out the bed. As he lies there stiff and scared out his mind not to wake any of them up he removes his wife's arm from over him and slides the other leg off the bed. He lies there with both legs off the bed hoping no one wakes up and then with smooth motions he slides himself off the bed landing on the dirty cracked wood floor. The bed makes a screech as the springs pop back up seeming how there is no weight to weight them down and the wife stops snoring. The father's heart stops. He waits for one of them to wake up and ask what is he doing but he hears nothing. Then finally his soul is put at rest as his wife begins to snore once more. Darnel, the father, tip toes over the piles of trash and dirty cloths that lay about with the ground making slight screeching noises, Darnel walks out the bed room and to the front door where his shoes sit. He puts on his shoes and heads back through the darkness to the closet where his already packed suitcase that has the outer cotton cover ripped in certain areas hanging there looking bad with the handle broken off and the left corner half way gone leaving a sort of big hole there. Darnel looks back at the room where his helpless family sleeps. Tears roll down his checks as he tries to be a man and see through them. He walks over to the small refrigerator where a note pad magnet sticks onto it with a thin black marker attached to the side. Darnel removes the marker and erases all the other notes written there and begins to write his farewell.~) *Note: I don't know how to put this so I'm just going to put it like this. We have been together for ten years. Everything that has came our way we have dealt with it together and got through hard times. I love you with all my heart but I can't stand this anymore. I've been supporting this family since day one but I can't do it anymore. I can't stand to watch you all suffer and your disappointed faces when I come home with a small amount of money or none at all. I'm sorry but I just can't take this anymore. So it pains me to say this but I'm leaving you all... (~Darnel not being able to write no more drops the marker. Another tear rolls down his check as he stares at what he wrote then turns himself away from it and heads for the door. He stops and looks at his family one more time then back at the note with the moonlight shining on it through the other window that sits to the left of it. Darnel then grabs the golden colored door knob with brown rust spots on it, turns it slowly not to make a noise, and pulls it open gently. The rest of the projects are seen before him all beat up and looking like garbage, which is what is also spread about the ground everywhere. Darnel doesn't dare to take one look back as he steps outside and shuts the door behind him. As the door shuts a few minutes later one of the children wake up and sees one of the cups beside him is over flooding with the dripping water from the ceiling. His skin is of a light brown color as his eyes are of the same color. His ears are very small along with his nose and his dark brown hair is corn rolled back. He wears these slightly tight blue nightclothes that have space ships and stars all over but the color is red around the wrists and collar. AJ, the little boy, picks up the glass jar and dumps the water in a big plastic bucket in the corner they use for showers and washing cloths then sits it back to catch more water dripping from the ceiling. As the boy grabs the thin sheet of green cotton blankets that is only covering him and his brothers, he notices his father is gone. AJ rubs his eyes back and forth in a sloppy manner to straighten his vision from his state of sleep that he just came out of then piers forward to see his dad is really gone.~) 'Mom, dad is gone.' (~AJ sits there shaking his mom's shoulder while rubbing his left eye with his other arm.~) 'Mom, wake up. Dad's not here.' (~Mom slowly opens one eye then closes it again and in a tired voice she responds to her son.~) 'Oh honey, Dad probably went to work or find another job. Don't worry, he would never leave us...' (~She falls asleep as AJ hops into bed and pulls the blankets over him then falls asleep too. Night turns to day as the sun shines through the window. The mother now has taken over the dad's spot on the mattress and the boys now have inches of space between them. The three kids lay there still caught up in their dreams as the mother is just opening up her eyes. Joy overwhelms her as she is so happy to see the sun shining through the window and her and her kids are alive. She whips the slobber away from his mouth and check then sits up. A bird chirps it's sweet little song just outside the window and it warms her heart. The day is now Sunday. It is around 10:56am in the morning and all she remembers she has to feed her kids real quick and head to church, which around here starts at 11:47am. It would start at earlier but the preacher always comes late and no one apparently knows what he does at night. Barbara Jenkins, the mother, climbs out the dirty mattress and yawns while stretching her arms out above her head. She stands to her feet and pulls down the purple silk gown that is a bit to small for her seeing as how her husband bought it for her at a lower size seeing how he could not pay for one that fit her weight. As she pulls it down the side rips and she is filled with remorse knowing that her husband would be heart broken knowing she ripped it. But her knowing the kind of man her great husband is she looks at the thin in width silver ring that has a heart on top and a very small diamond in the middle and is overwhelmed with love as she knows her man would still love her. The cracked wood floor begins to creek as she walks her beautiful 'Big Momma' body to the kitchen where she walks right past the refrigerator and opens the smudged window. You can hear the creaking and screeching as she lifts it up letting the sweet sun light shine bright through her window. The light reveals the dust and dirty flying about in the air then it all breaks away as she walks through it to the green small refrigerator that has no handle and it's so old the paint is falling off. Barbara disregards the letter that summons great pain upon her life and opens the freezer. A gust of slightly cold air spills out of the freezer hitting Barbara in the face cooler her sweat from the awfully damp and hot apartment. She pulls out the only thing that sits there, bacon, and shuts the freezer down. As soon as she shuts the freezer door the letter is knocked away from the freezer and makes a rustling noise as it hits the ground. Barbara with her short, stringy, black, and gray hair tied back and secured in a ponytail by a purple hair band, her sweet brown eyes lock onto the letter. Barbara sets the bacon on the counter and picks up the letter but all she can make out is, 'Dear Barbara.' She pulls her glasses from out her pocket and breathes onto the lens before wiping them with her gown. Then she makes out the rest of the horrific, heart-breaking letter. ~) *Note: I don't know how to put this so I'm just going to put it like this. We have been together for ten years. Everything that has came our way we have dealt with it together and got through hard times. I love you with all my heart but I can't stand this anymore. I've been supporting this family since day one but I can't do it anymore. I can't stand to watch you all suffer and your disappointed faces when I come home with a small amount of money or none at all. I'm sorry but I just can't take this anymore. So it pains me to say this but I'm leaving you all... (~She puts her open hand to her chest in shock then drops the letter. Barbara begins to panic only will the words burned into her mind. She crouches down and covers her mouth as tears begin to pure from here eyes. The glasses fall to the floor and crack as she just sits there looking at the letter crying. What once started out as a good blessing day, suddenly turned into a damned horrible day. The tears begin to roll of her check and wet her gown, as she cannot control it. Then a faint sleepy voice is heard approaching the kitchen.~) 'Mommy, where are you.' (~Barbara tries to reply but only silence comes out as her word are replaced with tears or pain. The voice is heard louder and louder as the little boy approaches the kitchen in search for his mom. Then turning the corner, the boy finds his mother heart break and in tears. He runs to her and kneels at her side trying to comfort her in her time of need.~) 'Mommy, what's wrong? Why are you crying? Please mommy stop, you're making me sad too.' (~A tear comes to the boy's eye not being able to take watching his mother in pain anymore. He wraps his arms around his mother and they both begin sharing their pain. Just a few seconds later the two other boys in the bed wake up rubbing their eyes from sleepiness. They both throw the thin green cotton blankets off them and hop out of bed thinking it's another exciting day to play. With big grins on their faces one would question why they are so happy seeing has how they have nothing. AJ, the smallest wearing these fuzzy gray cotton night cloths pants with no shirt and the other Trez, the middle child, wearing just plan white underwear. Both with smooth chocolate brown skin that matches their perfect little light brown eyes that light up the room everywhere they go. Seeing as how Trez and AJ got along the best, they were too alike meaning they often get into brother-to-brother fights, which does happen. But as Trez and AJ play carelessly they began to hear cries coming from the kitchen, which is just around the corner since their apartment is so small. Then they stop playing and look towards the kitchen.~) 'Who's crying?' The six-year-old Trez says puzzled 'I don't know. Lets go see.' (~The bowlegged five year old AJ says as he makes his way to the kitchen with Trez following right behind. But when they turn the corner a huge tall black figure to them, turns the corner shaken up with her eyes red from crying. She looks down on AJ and Trez then kneels down before them. Her hands are shaking and her face is drained from all happiness. AJ and Trez look at one another scared thinking they are in trouble then their mother breaks the heartbreaking news to them.~) 'Now, I don't know how to tell you this but your father walked out on us. He left us and he ain't coming back.' (~Trez and AJ confused at first finally get it and look at their mother who is kneeling their emotionally hurt.~) 'Momma, where did here go? Is he coming back?' (~The mother replies stern.~) 'I told you he isn't coming back you hear! He's gone and he isn't coming back any more! He left us with nothing! I should have known when he was gone in the middle of the night but I guess I was to blind to see.' (~Barbara look at her children who stand there not knowing what to do then looks up at the destroyed ceiling and begins to cry while shouting out here pain.~) 'Please lord! No! How could this have happen to my babies and me? Why must I be the one to live such a live? I don't deserve this! Lord, please help me! Bless me with you good will, please Lord help me!' [1987]Time Pasted, Years Pasted ~Over the years Barbara and her kids struggled to get back, barely making it at times. They had no food, no water, and damn near no cloths. Everyday they all worked small pride taking jobs to get back but it seemed like they were never going to get out the hole they were buried so deep in. In 1983 AJ's and Trez's oldest brother died of food poisoning. Sometime when Barbara had brought the food home from standing in long lines at food drive the military people were having near there neighborhood, she must have brought home a rotten fruit. See Barbara would do anything for her children and even if it meant standing in pride taking military food drives for half rotten food, she would be there. It destroyed her heart as she brought home food day after day seeing her little boy's faces like up with joy and hopes as they rip through the pile of food looking for something fresh. But after the eldest, Jay, died of food poisoning from one of the half rotten fruits she brought home, she was devastated. Since they had no money for a nice burial service, his mother and brothers had no chose but to bury in a field not to far from the projects. Time progressed and they matured, got older, and begin get through hard times. Unfortunately Trez got tired of watching everyone suffer and joined a gang and AJ seeing how much money it was making joined after his older brother. There AJ got the name of lil' Thug, for being the smallest one in the group. Then later on when they saw how much money AJ was making off selling drugs they gave him a tattoo on his abs that says, Bling Bling. Their mother never really like the chose they made but she couldn't or didn't say much seeing has how they were financially helping out. They finally moved to a nicer apartment in the projects. It had running water and working stove. It was a bit bigger than the last one and smelled a lot better too. Then things started to get a bit more serious and the problems came back. Trez began using drugs, killing, and stealing. AJ was a bit wiser and didn't get to deep in all of it but slowly watched his elder brother waste away. Several times he tried to get him under control but it didn't work. He just became addicted and before AJ could do anything, it was to late.
