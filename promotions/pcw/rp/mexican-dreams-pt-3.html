Date: not entered 'Yes, I require the full works, get it all in, for a reasonable price of course,' states the Mexican Devil. El Diablo is on the phone to somebody but for what purpose? 'I'm sure. Yes I will be going ahead with it, thanks a lot, bye,' finishes El Diablo. The phone slams down onto the receiver as El Diablo ends the phone call he was having. 'My dream is nearly true, Angel,' shouts El Diablo to Angel who is located somewhere nearby. There is no response from her but El Diablo isn't worried at all. The Mexican Devil has a certainly busy week, as Fusion he has a chance to reach the Main Event for March Madness by defeating Anton The Kid, on Sabotage a chance to compete against the other members of International Incident and on Turmoil another match against Anton The Kid, and some other matters that he has to deal with. The room El Diablo is in looks rather nice, the walls are painted a nice white color, the carpeting is a dark blue and in the small room is a big brown desk that El Diablo is seated behind, and behind him there is a bookshelf that is full of many Mexican books. On Diablo's desk are several white papers and files, a black telephone and a tub of pens, not to mention the lamp that he has on so he can see what he is writing and reading. In either corner of the door, which is facing El Diablo are two more seats and that completes the room set-up. Diablo is wearing his black leather mask, a white T-shirt and black sweat pants, it seems obvious that he is inside of his home, somewhere in Chicago, Michigan. Which just so happens to be Angel's home state. 'This is perfect, the plans for this are perfect, I cannot wait for this to eventually happen,' El Diablo says aloud to himself. El Diablo must be speaking of his plans to build a Mexican restaurant to add culture the United States, but doesn't he realise their is a Taco Bell? Or will he just make it different? Whichever it is, El Diablo just has to make it a success. Despite all these business plans El Diablo has, he must concentrate on other things of importance to himself too, and that is his upcoming matches. A shot at March Madness is up for grabs in an unlikely final of his group, which looked set to be Vampir Nosferatu against Big Alan Steele, but instead it is The Mexican Devil against Anton The Kid and it's quite ironic how the two of them will face each other again on the following Turmoil. 'Angel,' shouts El Diablo, 'Can you come in here?' he finishes. Some footsteps are heard as Angel comes through the door and sits herself down in the left hand corner of the room. Angel is wearing just a black sports bra and tight black shorts that only reach part way down her thighs, it seems obvious she had been working out as her sweat is glistening from all over body. And of course she has on her black cosmetics even while she works out. A very strange lady indeed, and it's either lucky or unlucky for El Diablo to be married to her. 'Yes?' she responds politely and completely unlike her. El Diablo remains seated where he is and smiles across at her. 'These plans are going fantastically,' says a jubilant El Diablo. 'That's great,' replies Angel, uninterested. Her uninterest discomforts El Diablo. 'Don't you want this to be a success?' questions El Diablo. Angel sighs, 'Of course I do. I just think there are more important things than a gimmick Mexican restaurant,' El Diablo stands up, and Angel lets out a little snicker. 'What?' asks El Diablo, confused. Angel just points at El Diablo's crotch; El Diablo looks down and finds that his zipper is open. Angel quits giggling as El Diablo turns around and a sharp zip up sound is heard. 'What can be more important than this?' remarks El Diablo. Angel wipes the sweat from her forehead. 'Us, PCW etc,' she responds calmly and like a typical woman. He walks over to Angel and crouches down next to the chair she is seated on. 'I know what's important, but once this is completed, everything else can get back on track,' El Diablo reassures her. She looks down at her husband, much like morally. 'If PCW takes a back seat, you could end up defeated and not go to another Pay-Per View. Are you willing to risk that?' asks Angel. El Diablo smiles. 'OK, you're right. But this is just something I have to do,' states the Mexican Devil. 'I understand that, but just remember I'm right, and get your priorities straightened out,' Angel says again. El Diablo pulls Angel up off from the chair and sits down it himself and indicates for Angel to sit on his lap, well more a less lie across him. 'I promise you that I will focus on my matches, you already know what's in store for International Incident if Akiyama continues his current attitude,' El Diablo reassures Angel. Angel smiles and looks up at her husband, who speaks perfect English. 'Yes I know, I just want everything finalised that's all,' says Angel while starting to pout. El Diablo simply smiles at her and continues speaking, 'I understand and soon it will be, and then we will even have a family business too,' Angel wraps her arms around El Diablo's neck and plants a kiss right on him. 'I know everything will be fine, I just want you to be successful in your career and especially with the changes you are willing to make,' she says to him. 'I will be fine, you will be fine, we will be fine, it's just a few adjustments here and there that need completing and finalising and even detonating that can start this,' he reassures her once again. Angel puts her legs down and stands up once more. 'Just make sure you complete everything soon,' she says to El Diablo as she exits the room getting the last word. El Diablo stands up as well, and walks towards the door, but instead of leaving the room, he closes the door and heads back to his desk and sits behind it. 'Of course I'm prepared for wrestling, it's my life,' says El Diablo to himself, 'Once this restaurant is built, I can get further and wealthier, and really make myself the living American dream,' he finishes as he speaks under his breath so that Angel definitely cannot hear him. He is just like any man really, needing his privacy. He picks up the phone and presses in a few numbers. 'Hello, so you can start building it on the 26th?' asks Angel to the other end of the phone. He writes on a piece of paper. 'That's excellent, I'm looking forward to it. See you then Jose,' states El Diablo as he hangs up the phone once more. El Diablo though, sits there with a concerned look on his face, he looks down at one of the papers and suddenly picks it up and tears it in half, and places it back down where it was and lets out a smile. El Diablo stands up and proceeds to exit the room. On the torn paper is the symbol of International Incident.
