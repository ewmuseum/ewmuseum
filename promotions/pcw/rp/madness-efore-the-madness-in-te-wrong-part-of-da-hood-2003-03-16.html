MADNESS EFORE THE MADNESS-In Te Wrong Part Of Da Hood

16 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: MADNESS EFORE THE MADNESS-In Te Wrong Part Of Da Hood Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 3-16-2003 ..::/::..When we last left our hero, he was struggling to make his exit from the local Blockbuster Video, his routine trip to the store to get a copy of 'Unchained Hearts' cut short by a trio of terrifying ninja's attempting to assassinate our hero in an effort to keep him from getting to March Madness. Having survived the battle intact, our hero is now striding confidently down the dimly lit streets of The City, holding his copy of Unchained Hearts in his hand with pride as his chest pokes out with a sense of self-victory. Fixing the Spiderman 2099 mask on his face, our hero says in his broad voice..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'HA! HA AGAIN I SAY! Those ninja's thought they could stop me from making a beeline STRAIGHT to March Madness, well HA to them again. It's gonna take a lot more then some Shinobi wannabes to stop the SPANDEX FORCE O' SEXINESS THAT IS RENT-A-HERO!! No one can stop me from realizing my destiny…and that's putting away 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, Dante Jackson, AND VenPen to once again become the Continental champion…not to mention becoming the FIRST ever CRUSIER-NENTAL champion…. -He rubs the Crusierweight title still about his waist- … NOT to mention winning the March Madness battle royal to become the PCW WORLD Heavyweight champion! No FIENDISH FIEND OF FILOINDERING can hope to stop me! I'd like to see them try…' ..::/::..As our hero continues down the street, having just finished his heroic spiel to an audience that's nowhere to be seen in the dark, dingy streets, our hero keeps up his big steps with his head held high. Unaware to our hero however….a group of shadows appear behind him, our hero too in to his whistling rendition of 'Sky Surfers' to pay them any attention. The shadows continues to keep a good pace behind our hero, our hero not noticing until he hears a group of footsteps that are not his. He paces begins to slow, the group of shadows continuing to follow as our hero begins to clench his free hand tight into a fist. Looking to the camera, he says..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'I know what your thinking….a group of EEEEEEEEEEEEVIL Super Saiyan Samurai Pizza Cats are looming behind me and are waiting to tear me to shreds right? So now I'm supposed to LEAP into action, slaying them all within a 2-4 page time frame so YOU, the viewer, can leave the monitor to take part of real-life actions and such….WELL IF THAT'S WHAT YOUR THINKING…YOUR DEAD WRONG, I'M OUTTA HERE!!..::/::..Our hero clutches his 'Unchained Hearts' tight to his arm, bolting at the speed of sound from the shadowy figures as he races to any kind of safety he can get. However, that plan is cut short as an arm bolts out from nowhere and CLOTHESLINES our hero down to the concrete street, Rent-A-Hero on his back as he holds his head..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'HOLY CLOTHESLINES OF CATATONICA…CAN'T I JUST RENT A MOVIE IN PIECE?!'..::/::..Our hero happens to have landed near a metal street light, it's dim light flickering on and off at various intervals as our hero's mysterious assailant and the shadowy figures behind him unveil themselves in the light. They are revealed to be a group of four black men, all dressed in the latest of Sean John and Fubu clothing. All four men look menacing and muscular, shooting menacing stares at our fallen hero. One of them steps forward, his hair in corn rows and his mouth filled with platinum fronts (mouth jewelry), his teeth glistening in the light as he grins at his fallen prey, which would be our trapped hero. He scratches his chin before saying..::/::..Man:'Looks like your in the wrong part of the hood eh homey?'Rent-A-Hero:'Excuse me sir….but I believe it's 'neighborhood'! It's not like The City is a giant sweatshirt and this area happens to be the 'hood' of it mister!'..::/::..The four thugs look dumbfounded by Rent-A-Hero's over-analyzation of their ghetto slang . The man with the platinum teeth merely shakes his head then says..::/::.. Man:'Look dawg….I think you know the stillo fo' sho. Just give up your N's and that big ass gold belt 'round your waist…'One of the Thugs:'And that 'Unchained Hearts' tape..' ..::/::..The sound of a record stopping overtakes the scene, the other three thugs looking to the bald thug who just made that request. He looks embarrassed, holding his head down in shame as he says..::/::.. Bald Thug:'Man damn…ya'll act like a thug don't have a soft side yo'. -almost comes to tears-Lead Thug:'I need me a new crew….anyways, GIVE IT UP SPANDEX BOI'!' ..::/::..Our hero rises to his feet, looking to all of the thugs around him as he sucks in the fear, exchanging it for all the bravery he can muster. Clutching his 'Unchained Hearts' tape tightly, he addresses the street thugs as if they were little children as he just out his index finger while giving his commonplace heroic speech….which means you all can get about five minutes of sleep, I know I will..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'NOW YOU LISTEN HERE…YOU…YOU…RUFFIANS!! Rent-A-Hero refuses to go down to a GAGLE OF GANGERS LIKE YOU!! I am not only PCW's ONLY superhero….but I'm also the man who will attain the first ever WORLD CRUSIER-NENTAL TITLE IN PCW HISTORY!! BUT I CAN'T DO THAT WHEN FIENDS LIKE YOU KEEP IMPEDING MY PROGRESS TO MARCH MADNES….AND I CAN'T EVEN WATCH MY DARNED MOVIE!! SO IF YOU WANT TO BATTLE…LET'S BATTLE RUFFIANS….BUT I WARN YOU….MY SPANDEX BIZNITCH SLAPS O'DOOM SHALL STING!!! Prepare to battle the OTHER last son of Krypton…RIGHT NOW, COME ON!!'..::/::..Rent-A-Hero closes his eyes, swinging his hands wide and open with intent to slap one of his four assailants. The thugs let him miss blindly as he huff's and puff's while in action, the four having a discussion amongst themselves..::/::..Lead Thug:'Did this fool just say…BATTLE?' Bald Thug:'That's what he said my boy…' Other Thug:'Fo' sho' man….I say we give him that one hitorquitter (pronounced 'one hit or quitter', a devastating one shot punch) and take him down to the Basement playa…' Other OTHER Thug:'For reals man.' Bald Thug:'Yea…then we take his 'Unchained Hearts' tape and bootleg that shit ya'll.'..::/::..The bald thug once again draws shameful stares, making him promptly shut up and lower his head as the discussion continues..::/::.. Lead Thug:'Aight…let's do this fool and be out.'..::/::..Preferring not to waste anymore time…the lead thug waltzes over to our hero, who is still swinging wildly, and catches him in the face with a crushing right hand. Our hero topples over, out cold on the ground as he manages to irk out..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero: -in a daze- 'No mommy….I don't want to wear cotton underwear…'..::/::..The world goes dark for Rent-A-Hero, the scene fading as the four thugs drag him off into the darkness to take him to this mysterious 'Basement'…our hero's road to March Madness in serious question..::/::.. WHAT WILL THE THUGS DO TO RENT-A-HERO?! WILL OUR HERO SURVIVE TO MAKE IT TO THE MADNESS?! WHY ARE ALL THESE STRANGE EVENTS HAPPENING?! FIND OUT….SAME RENT-A-TIME…SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!!! ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
