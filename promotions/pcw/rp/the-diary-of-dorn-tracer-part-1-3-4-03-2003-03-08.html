The Diary of Dorn Tracer - Part 1: 3-4-03

8 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-8-03 Written: 3-4-03 I haven’t written in this thing for so long, I figure I probably should. There’s quite a bit to tell about, but where to start? I won the PCW Rising Star Title on the twenty-first of February; defeating some guy named Jackson Rust in what I think was a sub-par match. Nothing personal against him, but he proved to be a little less than challenging. And due to that, I think maybe the crowd had the same idea about me. Not that I care for them anyway, though. It’s the principle of the thing, though. Know what I mean? There I was, Dorn Christopher Tracer III, in front of jeering, booing crowd of green-tongued animals and fat demonic marks holding up the coveted PCW Rising Star Title. Sweating and battered, I felt proud of myself amidst the rain of hate that night. I guess they really can’t appreciate someone who’s different than they are. I can go out there and throw myself around for an hour at minimum, and they’d still bite my head off just because I’m different than they are. I suppose it’s human nature, but it breaks my heart. I just can’t seem to please anyone. And it’s been that way my whole life. I won’t into details, journal, just because you’ve heard it all before. And therefore, you know exactly what I’m talking about. They don’t appreciate my break-neck efforts at achieving greatness just because I’m not what they are. If I’m not some fat, beer-swilling slob who works at McDonald’s, then I’m not the hero. Well that’s BULLSHIT, journal! I won the PCW Rising Star Title, so I know CONTROL the entire tier of newbies and babyfaces. You think they like that? Of course not. And just to rub it in their face, I prayed to En’Flarim to perform the Cleansing on the Title, officially converting the trophy into something fit for the Ace Of Astrology. It’s the least anyone can do. With a fighting spirit and a soul fueled by solar fire, I’ll be sure to charge through whatever management sends at me, giving all I have to those in attendance. What will I get in return, you ask? Absolute shit. They’ll still boo me. They’ll still hate me. If I don’t eat wafers and allow my balls to be fondled by a priest while I’m the SHOOTING Star Champion, then I’m not good enough for those fuckers. Typical. They’re just like my fucking father. Nothing is ever good enough. Nothing ever stems their ever-present greed and want for the norm. They want some fucking Christian athlete out there holding the title and kissing their ass. Sorry boys, but I Dorn Tracer doesn’t blaze that trail. Since society has all but excommunicated me for being who I am, I’ll just be a ruthless outcast who could give less of a shit about those around me. All I need is my Rising Star Title and the stars above my head as a guide to greatness. A number of blasphemers have been dealt that same reality already, falling to my star-guided hand. They should feel as lucky, for it could have been much worse. One in particular, a very irritating being at best, goes by the name of Eric Wolfson. A green-tongue with a car jack stuck under his nose, he’s decided to get in my way. Like his venomous kin, he has looked down upon me as a suitable holder of the Shooting Star Title. And who is he to say such things? No one. A complete and utter nobody looking to make a name for himself by committing ritual suicide by challenging me. Hopefully he realizes no one will come to his funeral OR read his tombstone when I’m done with him. By the great En’Flarim’s decree, the blasphemer will fall before me. One way or another, he will fall before me. But the time to do it is nigh. Almost right over the horizon. This Friday, I must face him and a wretched pair of titted dogs to prove I’m worthy of holding the Rising Star Title. That’s fine with me, a fair price to pay for holding coveted piece such as this. We’ll see, journal. Oh yes, we will see who is worthy. But alas, the results will have to wait. I’m tired, and my hand muscles are beginning to cramp. I shall write again in a day or so, telling you of my plunders. Good night, journal. [End.]
