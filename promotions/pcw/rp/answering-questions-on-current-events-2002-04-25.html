Answering Questions on Current Events

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 25 PCW has changed over the last year and a half - some for the better, and some for the worst. On the better side of things, athletes are now more athletic than ever and PCW has a good mix of youth and experience. On the worse side, the PCW Championship is being degraded more by the day. Jason Snow, a young man from a small village called Backwater in NB Canada, is at the core of both. Does he care at all about the PCW Championship? We can’t be sure. We do know that Jason Snow sees the title as a weapon - and he’s never been one to shy away from weapons. He uses the weapon against none other than Big Alan Steele, who should probably be the champion right now. But by way of deceit and cheating, Jason Snow finds himself as a three time PCW Champion while Big Alan Steele has yet to wear the companies highest gold... ...But it’s not so high now. Jason Snow and New Breed have brought shame to the greatest prize in all of wrestling, lowering it in the eyes of most wrestling fans. And all for his own selfish purposes... Snow has never been too high on morality... Snow: “I hate that damned Willie Nelson...” Snow scowls, thinking of his last encounter with the country music legend. The meeting ended with Snow lying unconscious on the floor after having Willie’s famed guitar, “Ole Trigger,” smashed over his head... Snow: “I’m convinced that he’s the paternal maker of that peasant, Tyler Nelson!” The scene opens in the latest of Snow’s luxurious hotel rooms. The Killin’ Villain, looking much the same as always (hair tied back into a pony tail, leather pants and snake skin boots), stalks around the room. Mark Malloy is present at the moment - he and Snow always end up crossing paths. Over the last year Snow has used Malloy as a slave more often than not... Malloy: “Snow, you don’t have any proof of that.” Snow: “This isn’t court! This is wrestling! Who the hell needs proof!?” Malloy rolls his eyes... Malloy: “Well, I don’t see what your problem is anyway. The way I see it, Tyler and the guys in Ultimate Authority have been more than generous with you and the rest of New Breed. I mean, even you have to admit that...” Snow looks confused... Snow: “Why, by Greatness, would I admit any kind of generosity has been shown to us by the Ultimate Peasants?” Malloy: “Well, they did help you keep the Big Alan Steele from getting a title shot.” Snow gasps... Snow: “By Greatness, I did no such thing! I’ve given Big Alan Steele ample opportunity to win the PCW Heavyweight Championship of the World!” Malloy: “Oh please, Snow! Every time he gets a shot you guys hand the title off to someone else, leaving Big Alan Steele with nothing!” There is a long moment of silence while the Killin’ Villain glares at Malloy. Malloy, who usually keeps these sort of complaints to himself, can’t help but feel the urge to lash out at Snow. Malloy is PCW through and through - seeing the championship degraded leaves him with as bitter a taste in his stomach as anyone. He never thought he would see such a sad day in PCW... and the day was brought on by none other than the Killin’ Villain himself... Snow: “Big Alan Steele?” Snow scowls... Snow: “He’s had all the opportunity he needs. Had he not interfered in the epic battle between Corey Williams and I on Sabotage, I may have given him a title shot. But really, why does everyone consider that lowly peasant such a great contender, anyway? Is he so important that Corey should have denied me my rightful, and mandatory, rematch for the PCW Heavyweight Championship?” Malloy: “You’re making excuses!” Snow’s eyes narrow on Malloy and there is a cold moment of silence... Snow: “And you, peasant... are treading on ever-thinning, Superior, ice...” Reluctantly, Malloy holds his tongue... Snow: “Besides, peasant, Steele is getting a chance to prove himself on Fusion...” Malloy’s eyes narrow but he says nothing. What he wishes he could say is that if BAS wins, he’ll still only be granted a shot at the championship, not the title itself. Not only that, but Big Alan Steele has to go through every member of New Breed to get it. Although, considering Big Alan Steele’s talent... anything could happen... Malloy: “Ok... fine... whatever you say, Snow.” Malloy sighs and sits down on the white leather sofa that always seems to be present in Snow’s hotel rooms. With Snow’s ever-angering eyes on him, Malloy turns away to a window and looks out over the city. Quickly, he tries to change the subject... Malloy: “So what’s this vendetta with Tyler about? I mean, from what I’ve seen, he’s done nothing but help you. If it weren’t for him, you wouldn’t be half as wealthy and -” Malloy has gone too far and he knows it... Snow: “Nelson had nothing to do with my riches! It was my Greatness and Superiority that made me millions! Have you no notion who you’re talking to? I’m the Killin’ Villain, damn it! I’m the PCW Heavyweight Champion of the World!” Malloy: 'So?' Snow: 'So, peasant, Tyler Nelson has dubiously won himself a chance to win my championship!' Malloy rolls his eyes. Snow didn’t earn that championship, which is something that he could at least say about his first reign, despite the finish to the match against Jeremy Howard. His last two reigns, however, came at such controversial circumstances that it’s hard to even refer to Snow as the Champion. Still, the Killin’ Villain is the man with the title, and it appears he has no intentions of letting go... Malloy looks around the room... Malloy: “Where is the title, anyway? Usually you keep it at arms reach.” Snow scowls... Snow: “These damned hotels! I paid thousands for this damn place, and STILL they can’t give me a television stand that doesn’t wobble!” Malloy: “What does that have to do with the -” Malloy stops and his face goes pale... Malloy: “Oh Snow...” He can’t bare to look in the direction of the television... Malloy: “You... You didn’t...” Snow: “What in the hinges of hell are you babbling about now, plebe?” Malloy’s nightmares come true. He looks to the television where the PCW Heavyweight Championship in the World - the highest prize in all of wrestling - is being used to prop up Jason Snow’s hotel television... Malloy: “Damn it, Snow! You can’t do that!” Snow: “I can’t do what? Do you not know that I’m the Killin’ Villain!? Thus, I can do whatever the hell I want!” Malloy: “Snow!” Malloy leaps into action, hurdling the coffee table, flying by Snow... and tripping over his own two feet... he crashes a few steps away from the television... Snow: “What the hell has gotten into you, peasant!? Are you trying to stain the carpet with your blood!? If you do, it’ll be your maid cleaning it up, you know!” Malloy, lying on his stomach and wincing in pain, looks back at Snow... Malloy: “I don’t have a maid, Snow...” Snow scowls... Snow: “Well send your butler then!” Malloy rolls his eyes... Malloy: “Snow... I don’t have a butler either!” There is a short moment of silence while Snow considers the information that Malloy has just passed on to him... Snow: “Do you...” He squints with curiousity... Snow: “...live in a cave, or something?” Malloy glares at Snow - he’s getting braver by the minute, and crawls up to the television. Carefully, he pulls the PCW World Heavyweight Championship out from under the leg of the television stand. Snow objects... Snow: “What are you doing, peasant! What if while I’m watching the hockey game tonight, the puck starts sliding down one side of the ice because of you!” At times, we can’t help but wonder if Snow is truly as clueless as he sometimes appears. It’s as if he thinks the entire world really revolves around him - he thinks a hockey game is about to be played in his television. Malloy stands up, staring down at the PCW Championship, saddened by how the Killin’ Villain is treating the prize... Malloy: “Snow... don’t you understand that you’re the PCW Champion? You have something to live up to! Why are you disrespecting everything this company stands for?” Snow steps over to Malloy and takes the championship from him. After staring at the title for a few minutes, he carelessly tosses it onto the sofa... Snow: “The Championship doesn’t make me Superior, Malloy - my GREATNESS makes me Superior. What good can the championship possibly do for me?” Malloy can’t believe his ears... Malloy: “Snow! You’re the man who couldn’t stop talking about this belt a few months back! All I ever heard from you was how you were going to ‘the top of the wrestling ladder’! Well you’re there now! And you know what, you don’t f(CENSORED)king deserve it!” With that, Malloy stomps out of the hotel room. He’s never stood up to Snow before and he knows that if he had stayed for another moment he would probably find himself in a world of hurt right now. Snow, inside the empty room, is shocked - he can’t believe Malloy’s words. Malloy has never said anything like that to him in the past... Snow: “I really have to find him a woman...” Snow drops down on the sofa, accidentally sitting on the PCW Championship, and grabs a remote control - the game is about to start. Shaking his head, still in shock about Malloy’s lash-out, Snow scratches his chest while the scene fades to black...
