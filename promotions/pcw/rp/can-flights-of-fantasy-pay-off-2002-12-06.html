Can Flights of Fantasy Pay Off?

6 December 2002

Title: Can Flights of Fantasy Pay Off? Featuring: Tim Walden Date: December 6, 2002 I sat back contented, the in-flight dinner just completed with my side salad's remains still fresh on the plate..I was moving away from my compatriots at several hundred miles every hour, and I couldn't change things in my favour now, what had been booked wouldn't just unbook itself..I would be on the negative side of things alliance-wise, if any alliances formed, but then again, I would probably be better off should they be more concerned with mangling each other than with teaming up to attack me. They knew that my efforts would probably end up distracted with the Trapezoid, it left 3 men to take a smaller brunt of the attack amongst themselves, they would end up inevitably less damaged than whoever survived of us.. The disadvantage was rather not a positive consideration for me, maybe even to the point where I couldn't win if things didn't go my way, but I still held hope. I would do it all for her, I'd decided it with myself before, my own little secret source of anger when I needed it..I knew she'd smile if she could see me standing over the broken, beaten shape, I drew energy from the thought of her, beaming with happiness at the two golden belts slung over my shoulders as I came back, she was my worl-- I panicked. She wasn't my world, what was I even thinking..The delusions had found a footing in me. This couldn't help one bit, I had to stop the thoughts...But if I let them go...Would I be letting the Continental Title go as well? Was that really worth my paranoia that I wouldn't be able to let go of the fantasy that I'd created for my business of vengeance? Was I really that weak-willed that I had to worry about it in the first place? This was my own imagination, I had to keep ahold of it.. This was my way to victory, my pure animosity was peaking whenever I let myself slip into it, I would not let the opportunity pass me by just because of my weak will...But was I only imagining victory to be passing me by? Could I possibly beat him without her on my mind? It wasn't too late to pull out of the downward spiral that attachment was almost certainly going to lead to, not yet, I had my share of anger already..His violent temperament had already found a fairly decent match when we met in simple wrestling matches, but this was the moment that I had to dread for now..If I didn't shut myself off from compassion entirely, he might yet be able to take advantage and render me irrelevant before Trick or Treat IV even came around... Wouldn't that be something, to go from the Cruiserweight Title to premature retirement. I didn't want it to end that way. It shouldn't. It couldn't. I had to be sure...Didn't I? But certainty on one front would just render my personal life unstable. It was like Heisenberg's Principle in relation to business relationships, one side would have to give way to the threat of the other. I had to decide what would be worse...What could happen to me with an infatuation threatening the stability of my best friendship and a psycho on my case, should I fail to keep control on that side, or on the other hand, what could happen to me with a title that could have been mine and myself at the mercy of 4 men, some of whom were probably even more psychotic, bearing large sharp tools of systematic anatomic destruction.....When I put it that way, my feelings on the matter took a sudden shift. Things were always easier if you could express them in black and white. One could always find a new best friend...A Continental Title would not be so easy to replace... But what if I were to let the Trapezoid win? I would be able to fight him for it later then...No. That would require letting him get the edge on me, I could not give him the satisfaction of victory...I just wanted my shot at the Continental Title, I hadn't hoped to let anyone get what I'd earned in advance, but I was predestined to fight for this one...It was out of my hands, but the belt wasn't. I could win it. Nobody would take it away from me, I had too much pride to let it go. If I was vicious enough, I could be victorious...The two went hand-in-hand in this type of match, they even sounded alike...All I had to do was take the step away from reality that would bring me the killer instinct to win..This was my chance. My opportunity to attain what I wanted, an indelible spot in the history books right next to the one that I'd gain from Trick or Treat, another crucial win to give me the fullness of momentum against the Trapezoid, the gold that I'd never had hope to reach with Duke in my way, that I had never thought I would get a chance to reach.... To use my mind's partial sexual fantasy as a means of attaining my personal professional fantasy, it seemed so sordid...So wrong....So abusive of my own desires and ignorant of my restraining emotions... And yet so irresistible. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
