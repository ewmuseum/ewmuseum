A Letter to a Brother

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/22/02 Romantico has chosen to fly straight to his father’s destination. El Diablo did not follow his this time as he insisted to go alone. For the next six hours El Romantico will be alone in his plane trip to Lisbon. El Romantico sips a bit of coffee and then takes out a pen and paper from his duffel bag. The first words he right on it are the hardest, as his fingers shake while he puts his few feelings and emotions into words. Dear Juan, You’re a brother, a friend, a mentor, and a mystery. You were all of these to me, but I never questioned you as one of my own. You may of thought my persona displayed me as a violent and heartless man, but I hope you don’t truly believe that. I just wish you could be here today so I could tell you how much I’ve changed as a person. You’d be proud of me, and even though I know you don’t think I would ever want your approval, I do. If you could just be here today, to see how many people were affected by your death. In the last few days I’ve seen more tears and pain then I’d like to see in a lifetime. You affected millions of fans, and even though you may have not affected them deeply, you still changed their lives. But the people you really affected were the people who knew you best. Marco, Raul, Pedro, and Rui were your best men when you rotted there away in prison. Those men saw you as a hero. You were there light in a dark tunnel. You led them to success and made their lives better to live. Till this day every one of them are grateful to you and will never forget it. Kevin Klaymore, your partner and best friends. You both had struggles, but you worked your way through it. Kevin was lost without you and you were lost without him. He needed you to lean on and it was almost like he had lost one of his own when he found out about your death. Then there was Juliet, oh sweet Juliet. Her eyes were all I needed to look at to see her heartbreak. She still loved you as much as the first day she met you. You changed her life completely and you’ll always be a part of it. She was the one you were meant to be with and she knows it. I hope you realize what a great thing you had going for you. What I’m trying to say is that even though you died not thinking you were loved, you were in fact loved by many. No one will ever forget your contributions, especially me. Till this day when I step in the ring it is for you, and when I win it will be for you. In my dark and dull world your what keeps me going. My goal is to succeed what you couldn’t and present you with these gifts in heaven. My life? My life is over. It’s almost like it never even began. Trying to make this life better is impossible, trying to make you realize how great yours was isn’t. So Juan I leave you with this poem. My heart, my soul, and my energy is indulged in every word I speak. JuanA name of a man where perfection is keyYour lifeWas so great, it was a shame you couldn’t seeThat you flourishedIn every possible thing that you triedYou’re worth itAnd even though you didn’t feel it it’s trueI mean itI can’t express the love I have for youSo fewAre how many people I gave part in my lifeCaringThe one benefactor that kept me aliveMy painWas something I couldn’t brush away?It stayedEven though I begged for it to go it’s separate wayIn the endIt was you who saved me from the pillYea youThe man in my dreams who haunts me stillBut I knowThat I thank you for everything I possessYou knowI would never think of you as any lessFinallyWere together in a place called heavenThank GodIt’s so close I can almost smell itBut I’m notThere yet, I have to last it out a little longerMore timeBefore I finally grow any strongerWhen I doI will be a dominant forceAnd I will winWhat you call the primary sourceThe goldThat you came so close to holdingBut you and me will finally be rollingAwayFrom this world and up to a better one placeWhere we meet face to face Love, your brotherEl Romantico The scene then fades ahead eight hours as El Romantico stands above his brother’s grave and drops the letter on top of the soil, which lays above his body. He then tears a pedal from his white rose and puts it together with the letter. One Day…One Day…
