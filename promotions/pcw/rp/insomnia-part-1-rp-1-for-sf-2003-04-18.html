Insomnia Part 1 (RP#1 for SF)

18 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-18-03 A full moon hangs high in the sky surrounded by stars trying to shine brightly. There are no noises everything is quiet. The streetlights of Los Angeles glows in the distance. A cool breeze begins to move through the trees causing some to sway back and forth. The moon’s light is reflecting off of a glass. “Not long now,” a silent voice mutters, “Spring Fling is just mere days away.” The glass is lifted from its spot. The figure veiled in darkness holds the glass up and hesitates to make a motion with it. “My first true test…” the voice trails off, “Spring Fling will bring about a change. Many will see it but few will believe it.” A snicker escapes the darkness. After rolling the glass around for a few moments, it’s finally pulled back and emptied of its contents. “The fools…” The glass is returned to its former position. A shuffling is heard as Adam Stryker appears from the darkness. The moon’s light hits his face causing his pupils to dilate for a split second. He rests his elbows on his knees and drops his head into his hands. A smile comes to his face, a twisted looking smile. “Spring Fling will bear witness to the birth of a new man. Gone will be the carefree, easy going outer layer.” He says to himself, “Only my true self will remain. AJ is the only one with a clue. He’s seen my core before.” Adam starts to shake his head. “But what if it doesn’t work?” he questions himself, “Will it be worth it? I could lose it all…my friends…my career…and most importantly my Jaslene…” He looks back to the darkness but grits his teeth. “It will work! I refuse to be a laughing stock anymore. PCW should be and will be my stomping grounds!” Adam says clinching his fists together against his temples. He looks up to the moon and just stares at it for a moment. Slowly, little by little, he begins to release his fists. “Maybe I rushed into this…maybe I wasn’t ready yet…PCW had been the goal since that first interview. Then, when that didn’t work out it was onto the DWO and Callisto-fucking-Bane. Maybe I should have spent more time there.” Adam says running his hands through his hair. He stands up and walks to the railing at the edge of the deck. He places his hands down onto the cool wood. “That’s nothing but loser talk! I spent almost nine months there. My goal was PCW by September and I failed!” He drops his head and sighs. “Then maybe I should just face facts…I am a loser. I am a failure. Nothing is going to change that.” He says to himself. A sound can be heard behind him. He turns to look as Jaslene appears from the darkness. She is wearing an old DWO shirt. The white letters glow in the moonlight. “Adam?” What are you still doing up?” she asks through a yawn. “It’s almost three in the morning.” “I-I-” Adam stutters for a moment trying to find the right words. “I don’t know Jas. I couldn’t sleep. I couldn’t stop thinking about Spring Fling.” She stands next to him with her back to the moon. She leans up against the rail. “That’s understandable Adam,” she says yawning again, “It’s your first big event since coming back to the PCW…well first big event anyway, not to even mention your opponents. Two former DWO Champions and a former PCW Champion? Hell I’m not shocked to find you out here to be honest with you.” Adam looks down to her and just studies her face. The way the light reflects off of her tanned cheeks strikes Adam. “Well I’m not worried so much about my opponents.” He says looking away. “Oh? And why is that?” Jaslene asks trying hard to stay awake. “I have beaten Rust before. He is nothing compared to me. I’ve also beaten Trinidad to within an inch of his life.” Adam says just trailing off. “And what about Smoke?” Jaslene finally says after about a minute of silence. “What about him?” Adam fires back looking down to Jaslene. “Smoke is nothing as well. I’m going to be the most talented man in the ring come Sunday. That much is clear.” “But wait a minute,” Jaslene says but suddenly stops. Adam notices that Jaslene’s eyes have closed. He looks at her for a moment before giving her a nudge. She gets her eyes to open a bit. “Huh? What?” she says, “What’s going on?” “You were about to say something and I guess you fell asleep standing out here.” Adam says with a chuckle. “What was I going to say?” Jaslene asks rubbing her eyes and moving her head from side to side. “How should I know?” Adam responds, “Do I look like I mind reader to you?” “Well what were we talking about before I dosed off?” Jaslene says walking over to the deck chair and taking a seat. “Spring Fling.” Comes the reply. “Huh? Oh yeah, I think I remember know.” She says sitting back in the deck chair. “You say that Rust and Trinidad are nothing but don’t both of them hold victories over you? Something that you cannot say about Trinidad?” “Details Jas and minor ones at that.” Adam says turning to face her. “Besides, I beat Jackson once and I can do it again. I had AJ beaten but I allowed emotion and personal issues cloud my judgement. It won’t happen again.” “How can you say something like that?” Jaslene says from the darkness. “After all you and AJ have been through together? After the battle tested friendship where you actually took a chair to the feared Callisto Bane? I have a hard time believing that it will all just disappear because you say it will.” Adam smirks and rubs his chin. “Don’t worry about that Jas. Drive and the will to succeed have impact in everything we do in life.” Adam says, “I can imagine that Trinidad wouldn’t expect any less than my best. And you know me, I hate to disappoint people.” There is no response from Jaslene but Adam doesn’t really notice and continues on. “Rust I could really care less for. He’s in and out and all over the place. I don’t know what is going on with him but his mind is clearly not on wrestling. That will bring him down. Smoke…Smoke is another story though.” Adam turns and looks back to the moon. The breeze once again picks up. “A former PCW Champion is the volatile ingredient to this match. I know I haven’t made him an ally and I know that Trinidad probably wants to rip the guy’s head off. This is actually making for some interesting scenarios. Rust in fact seems to be the oddball in this match. He’s really got no connection to Crespo.” Adam laughs for a moment before turning back around and leaning up against the rail. “The only reason this match is even taking place is so that Trinidad can try to find Crespo before Smoke does anything too rash. I don’t know how this match is going to play out but I know it will somehow end up being in my favor.” Adam says with a smile. “I may not win but I certainly won’t lose this one.” He finally realizes that Jaslene has been silent for the past few minutes. “Jas?” he says softly. There’s no reply until she mumbles something and changes her position on the deck chair. “Heh, that’s a first. I’ve never put her to sleep before.” Adam walks closer to the chair and looks down on a sleeping Jaslene. “Well let’s get you back inside and into bed.” Adam reaches down and scoops her up. She instinctively wraps her arms his neck and nestles her head into his shoulder. He walks to the door but stops and turns back to look at the moon one last time. “AJ I hope you get over this ‘illness’ of yours soon. I don’t want any excuses from you when I win the match at Spring Fling.” Adam turns and walks back into the house. He closes the door behind him.
