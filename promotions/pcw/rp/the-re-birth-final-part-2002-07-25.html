The Re-Birth!!(Final Part)

25 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 25, 2002 OOC: I planned on more RPs in this series, but people are getting bored, as well as I am, so I decided to end it off here and just go back to present time and use Semore Smoke's name, instead of a past handle... hope it's good htough... {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up on a shot of the sun fading beyond the horizon as he sets upon the city of Wilson, North Carolina. Smokie Tang is still seen walking down the sidewalk as he’s on his way to his friend and whore’s house, Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey. Smokie seems to be severely shaken up from the walk alone, and he knows that around any corner danger could be lurking. He’s wearing one of his finest pimp suits, but it doesn’t make much of a difference now, since he’s forced to go legit by the corporate neighborhood. Smokie is thinking of a possible plan, but first he needed to talk it over with a friend, Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey was that person. Smokie stops in front of a small house, which sits in an odd place, right between two stores, the front yard of the house is neatly cut, as if a normal east coast family lived there. Smokie sticks out on the setting as he walks up the front lawn towards the front door. He steps up the neat porch and reaches the front door. By now the first of man street lamps are starting to light up on the dark and dismal night. Smokie knocks on the door, hoping that the lights that he sees in the house are those welcoming someone, instead of those warning others away. After knocking on the door for what seemed to be an eternity, but maybe was only a few seconds, Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey opened the door and to her surprise saw not what she expected… Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey: What the hell are you doing here!? {{{{Smokie looks down at his shoes with a look of despair on his face, and then he looks back up and answers…}}}} Smokie Tang: Well, I heard that you hadn’t left me after all that’s happened, and I needed a friend to talk to about a plan of mine to get out of this. {{{{Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey’s eyes just widen from what she just heard…}}}} Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey: I’m so sorry. I didn’t mean to come on strong, it’s just that I’m expecting someone, but I can talk until he gets here. Smokie Tang: Still making money I see. {{{{Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey blushes as she looks at Smokie…}}}} Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey: Actually, I like this guy a lot. We’ve been dating for a good month, but he doesn’t know that I’m a hooker, so it would be best if you weren’t here for long, and you went out the back way when he arrived. {{{{Smokie looks embarrassed as Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey lets him into her house. The interior of the house is as surprising as the outside of it. It’s so clean and neat, but then again a little too neat. Maybe Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey had done all the cleaning for this man who she loved, but it’s very odd. Smokie sits down on the couch, which sits over in a corner, and there are also two other LAY-Z-BOY chairs on the wall. She sits down on the space next to Smokie, who looks to be on the verge of crying as he realizes what he must do…}}}} Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey: What’s your plan, Smokie? Smokie Tang: Well, I need to get rid of the Smokie Tang persona. I need to kill Smokie Tang… {{{{Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey gets an alarmed look on her face as she hears Smokie Tang utter the words…}}}} Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey: You’re going to kill yourself? {{{{Smokie Tang manages to get a grin out and says…}}}} Smokie Tang: Not literally. I’m going to fake my own death, and then change my name to something catchy, something like Semore Smoke. How does that sound? {{{{Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey smiles as she answers him…}}}} Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey: That’s actually a great name right there. I understand your troubles, and your plan seems to be full proof, so what can I do to help? Smokie Tang: Well, you can get together a few boys who wouldn’t mind faking a knock off of Smokie Tang. They’ll become the heroes of the neighborhood. {{{{Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey thinks for a few seconds and then answers…}}}} Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey: Well, there is that Trench Coat Mafia that’s always hanging around outside the arcade down the road. They seem to be a nice bunch of guys, even if they are a Mafia. {{{{Smokie thinks for a minute and then he grabs Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey’s hand and kisses it…}}}} Smokie Tang: You’re a lifesaver, I’ll go to them and tell them that I want to be “killed” and see what they can do. I might join their gang after that as this Semore Smoke person. Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey: Ok, good luck… {{{{She’s cut off by a knock at the front door…}}}} Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey: That’s him! Get out of here! {{{{Smokie gets up and thanks Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey by nodding his head towards her. He then runs through the kitchen, which is right next door to the living room and then out through the backdoor. He jumps over the fence in her backyard and then runs down the ally way. He turns the corner and runs right into a huge brick wall… or it felt like a brick wall at least. Smokie looks up after falling on the ground to see a huge man wearing a huge black trench coat. He’s looking down at Smokie and offering his hand to help him back to his feet. Smokie takes the offer and gets back on his feet. He looks at the huge man and says…}}}} Smokie Tang: James, you know I was just looking for you. I need a favor from you and your gang. {{{{As soon as Smokie stands up he realizes that James’s whole gang is behind him backing the leader up as always…}}}} James: What type of help do you need Smokie? Smokie Tang: I need to fake my death, and I thought what better way to do it then by having you guys do it. Right now my reputation is dwindling down to nothing and you guys would be considered the top gang around after taking me out. And I’d then change my name and my look and join your gang as someone named Semore Smoke. {{{{James looks behind him at his crew and they’re all nodding and smiling at the idea…}}}} James: Well, I can’t let my crew down. We’ll do it, but how are we going to pull off such a stint like this? Smokie Tang: By “kidnapping” me, then saying that you murdered me and threw me into some lake. Your reputation will make you so big that the cops won’t even be able to touch you without some serious heat on their hands, so you get away free. James: Ok, sounds simple enough, we’ll do it…. {{{{The scene fades to black and then reopens with a shot of a news report. Dan Rather is on the news with the CBS Nightly News…}}}} Dan Rather: Also, in other news, local hero, Smokie Tang, was said to be kidnapped by the Trench Coat Mafia last night in Wilson, North Carolina. His whereabouts are unknown, and the Mafia has already said that they killed him and dumped his body into the Great Lakes in Northern United States earlier today. Many people are happy about the disappearance of Smokie Tang, and we’ll have more on it as it comes in. {{{{The scene then fades as the following words fade in on a black background in white…}}}} Smokie Tang died on August 5, 1993 {{{{They fade to black and then these words fade in…}}}} That same very day, Semore Smoke was born…. {{{{The entire scene fades to black…}}}}
