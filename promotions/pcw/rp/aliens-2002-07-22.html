Aliens?

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-22-02 Brandi lays on a lawn chair out on the wooden deck of a log cabin. She’s working on her tan, wearing her bikini top and cut off jean shorts. Behind her dark sunglasses, Brandi’s eyes are closed. When a friend of Brandi’s offered her the use of her cabin for a week, Brandi quickly said yes, and she’s been glad she did ever since. With no bookings from PCW she had nothing better to do. Now she’s quite rested and very relaxed. Brandi- Yeah, I needed this. Just some time to be alone and concentrate on myself for awhile. No worries about fans or matches. No strange happenings. Almost as if on cue, a loud crash is heard coming from somewhere out in the woods. Brandi gets out of her chair, and pulls her sunglasses off. She throws the glasses onto the lawn chair, and walks over to the railing on the edge of the deck. She leans over the edge of the railing and stares off into the woods, trying to discover what made the loud sound she heard moments earlier. Brandi- Hmmm…I must have been imagining things. Just as Brandi turns around, another crash is heard. This one smaller than the first, but still loud. Brandi turns around again. She stares off into the woods once more. When she doesn’t see anything out of the ordinary, she shrugs. Brandi quickly walks down the steps leading up to the deck, then changes her mind and runs back up, going into the cabin. When she comes back out, she’s wearing some black sandals. She walks back down the stairs again and starts off into the woods. Brandi- I guess I should go find out what that was. Small twigs and braches crunch underneath Brandi’s feet. Although the trees above provide plenty of shade, it’s still quite hot outside. Brandi stops underneath one of the large trees. She rests a hand on the tree, and looks out into the direction the crash had come from. Still, she can see nothing. A small tingle creeps up Brandi’s arm. She giggles slightly at the tickle, then looks to her arm. Large, black ants have made their way from the tree, up Brandi’s arm. Brandi quickly pulls her arm off the tree and gives it a few hard shakes. Most of the ants come off, but a few stubborn ones sink their teeth in and Brandi has to pull them off. Brandi- Damn bugs. Brandi walks around the tree, making sure to not step in one of the many ant hills. She keeps walking towards where she heard the sound. Something looks like its glowing behind a small tree, off in the distance. Brandi stops and stares in that direction, trying to make sense of what the strange object is. Brandi- What the… Brandi inches forward, and stops once again. Tree braches snap somewhere behind her. She turns around and peers through the trees. Then, the branches snap once again. A dark figure runs behind some trees. Brandi walks in that direction, curious. The figure darts in front of her and runs off. Brandi quickly runs after it. Brandi- Hey! Hey, wait up! This seems to have given the mysterious person even more speed, and Brandi struggles to keep up. She ducks underneath a large branch, but trips over a log. One of her shoes slides off as Brandi flies face first to the ground. She lands on a slope, and her momentum keeps her rolling down the small hill. She bounces over rocks and twigs, before finally coming to a rest at the bottom of the hill, in front of a boulder. Brandi moans, but manages to sit up against the large rock. Her arms and legs are cut, bruised, and dirty. She frowns. Brandi looks up the hill, at the place she fell. The figure she had been chasing is standing there, looking down at her. He starts down the hill, as if coming to help her, then he thinks better of it and runs out of view. Brandi kicks off her other sandal, and struggles to get to her feet. She manages to painfully climb up the boulder, and look off in the direction of the figure. Once again, it has vanished. Brandi sighs, and starts climbing down the rock when she hears something behind her. She turns around and slips on the rock, lading in a pile of leaves. Brandi looks up to find someone looking back down at her. It’s a man with long, stingy hair. He has a bushy beard that looks like it once had a black color to it. Now it’s mostly silver. His clothes are smeared with dirt and his pants have holes in the knees. Man- You ok? Brandi- Yeah, I think so. Man- Good. Can you stand up by yourself? There is a throbbing pain in Brandi’s ankle. She tries to stand up, and fails. She winces with pain, and shakes her head. The man isn’t watching her though, he’s staring up at the hill. Brandi tries to stand up again, and this time succeeds. It’s quite obvious that it’s painful for her to walk, to even stand, but the man pays no attention to this. He just stands there staring for a bit longer, before walking off again. Brandi limps after him, struggling to keep the man within eye distance. The man shows no signs of slowing down. He talks to Brandi as though she were walking right next to him. Man- You heard the crash. Then you went looking for them. They found you before you could find them. You’ve seen them too, haven’t you? If the man had been facing Brandi, he could have seen the look of confusion on her face. Brandi- Seen what? At this, the man stops and waits for Brandi to catch up with him. He looks her up at down, grimacing at the cuts covering her body. One of the cuts on her arm is bleeding badly. He stops and tears off a small section of his shirt. Then he ties it tightly to Brandi’s arm. Brandi- Hey! That hurt! The man ignores Brandi’s protests and finishes with the makeshift bandage. Then he looks up into the bright sky. Man- Them. They crashed out here earlier. That’s what you heard. They came looking for something. They wanted to land, but something went wrong and they went down. Now they’re out here somewhere. They’ve seen you. They know you’ve seen them. Soon, you will see. You were hunting for them. It will become obvious to you. The hunter will become the hunted. The man starts walking again. Brandi is caught off guard by the man’s remarks. She stands there, staring at the space where he just stood. It takes her awhile to realize the man has taken off again. She limps after him as quickly as she can. He’s slowed down quite a bit from the first time, so she soon catches up with him and manages to keep an even stride. Brandi- What’d you mean by that? The man doesn’t bother to look Brandi’s direction when he talks to her. She realizes he doesn’t watch where he walks, either. He just keeps staring at one thing. A spot in the sky off in the distance. Man- Soon, you’ll see. Have you ever had anyone hunt you down like prey? Have you ever gone face to face with someone that you just know you could never trust? How about laying there helpless as someone hovers over you, certainly meaning to give you get pain? Brandi has to stop and think about this one. Finally she nods. She’s decided that there’s one answer to all those questions. That answer is Bethany. Brandi- Yeah. Well, I haven’t exactly stood face to face with them, but yes. The man quickens his pace. Brandi tries to keep up with him, but her throbbing ankle won’t allow it. Brandi- Wait! The man stops, and turns around. Brandi- What exactly are you trying to tell me? Man- Their spaceship crashed. They’re here. Brandi now looks at the man like he’s crazy. Brandi- Their spaceship? Are you trying to tell me that I should be running from little green men? Man- Oh, no. I assure you that they aren’t green, or little. This comment brings a laugh to Brandi. The man’s look doesn’t change, however. He is very serious. Brandi quickly stops laughing. Man- I’ve seen them many times. Their leader has failed to get me. They’ve hunted me all these years, but never once have caught me. I suggest you run, and you run fast. They’re gonna get you. I don’t want to be around when they do. The man turns around and runs off, leaving Brandi standing in the middle of the woods, confused. She isn’t quite sure what to make of the whole situation. Brandi- Well, I sure hope nothing is trying to hunt me. I have to be able to wrestle Friday night. Brandi forces a laugh, but cuts it short. She’s all alone in the middle of nowhere, and the sun is starting to go down. Then she hears something fall behind her. She runs in the opposite direction, not stopping to look back. She keeps running straight ahead, not knowing where she’s going. Brandi just hopes this thing isn’t following her. After quite awhile she stops, resting her hands on her knees and breathing heavily. A little ways ahead is the main highway. Brandi grins, and starts the walk towards it. Brandi- Looks like I’ll make it after all. I wonder who that guy was. Well, hopefully he gets out ok. When I get back to town, I’ll have someone go look for him. Soon after she says this, a man’s scream is floats across the woods. Brandi stops immediately, and turns towards the woods. Another scream is heard. This one longer than the first, and filled with more pain. A few more screams drift through the air. It sounds as though someone is being tortured. Brandi runs towards the road. She doesn’t stop or look back until she gets into town.
