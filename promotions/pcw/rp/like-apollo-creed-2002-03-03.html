Like Apollo Creed

3 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-3-02 Scene opens on the helicopter pad outside Amaranth Penitentiary. Several armed prison guards escort 'Swamp Dawg' Karl LeBlanc to the chopper. Following closely behind is 'The Warden' Jack Riley. The crew is preparing to leave for St. Louis, where Monday night Extreme Champ, Karl LeBlanc, will make his PCW Pay-per-view debut. The 26-year old phenom shuffles to the helicopter. He is shackled and wearing his Extreme title belt proudly around his waist. While the guards load LeBlanc into the chopper, two reporters approach Warden Riley. Reporter #1: Mr. Riley, tomorrow's the big PPV... we haven't heard much out of you guys the last couple days... Warden: Well, that's because we've been training. This is Swamp's first PPV, we want to send a strong message to everyone in PCW. Reporter #2: This is also the PPV debut of LeBlanc's opponent, Jason Core. Warden: Yes, but there's a big difference. LeBlanc is young, LeBlanc is ferocious, and most importantly... LeBlanc is the champion. It took this kid only 5 professional matches to claim gold. He's only 26, which means he hasn't come close to his peak. Meanwhile, Jason Core is over-the-hill, a 34-year old rookie for Chrissakes! Reporter #1: Is LeBlanc ready for this match? Core has been unleashing the sneak attacks on both of you guys lately... Warden: And that sonofabitch will pay. After we pull Swamp off Core's crippled ass, then I'm gonna get me some too. No one attacks the Warden from behind and gets away with it. Core is gonna get the beating of his life Monday night. Reporter #2: Do you think Jason Core is ready to face an animal as powerful as the Swamp Dawg? Warden: Not a chance in hell. Do you remember in Rocky IV when the old, washed up Apollo Creed faced the young Russian beast Ivan Drago? Reporter #2: Yes. Warden: Do you remember what happened to Apollo when he didn't take the Russian seriously? Reporter #2: Yes, he died. Warden: Well, I'm not saying that LeBlanc will literally kill Jason Core, BUT... the old bastard better leave his toga at home and bring his A-game because LeBlanc is out for blood! Reporter #1 (gulp): Any predictions? Warden: LeBlanc will retain his Extreme title, make a VERY impressive showing, raise the eyebrows of some important people, and take his first step towards stardom. As for Jason Core, he may very well leave St. Louis in a pine box. I'd hate for that to happen, but the dumb bastard asked for it. Nobody picks a fight with LeBlanc. Monday night, J-Core will learn his lesson the hard way. Warden Riley ducks into the helicopter, slaps LeBlanc on the back, smiles, and signals the pilot to take off. Scene fades as the chopper flies off out of view. Destination: St. Louis, Missouri.
