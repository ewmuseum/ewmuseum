Date: Whenever *We open with an audio prologue from The End.* End:'At the request of Sandy, though she didn't write what I'm about to say, I've decided to, for one match only, make the following alterations to my ego --' Instead of normally being humble and merely wishing my opponent a good match, I instead declare that I shall defeat Totally Tubular Tommy. ..................................... Correction, in her honor I shall thrash Tommy. Not a bit of him will be intact. Note to the jantors that they'll need vacuums to get him off of the mat. I don't mind ending his career. Because it's for her. Not that she asked me to, but because I shall. *We open in the studio where End cuts his promos. This must be a portion that Sandy has scripted. Indeed, we see that End is holding a sheet of paper in his hands. We don't know how he's reading it. Well, seems Sandy has a typewriter that puts her words into Braille. So The End can read what she writes for him. There's nothing wrong with that. It's not required that a wrestler's words be his own, just that he says them. Even the President has a panel of professional speechwriters. And he still manages to talk like an idiot. Some things professionals just can't change. Shame, isn't it?* *The End is in his standard ring attire. Man, he needs to get new clothes. Maybe that's a good reason for winning the Continental Title: the payroll bonus. Wait, no, he still protests Front Office taxation by waiving his pay. Dang, he doesn't make sense in anything. I so want to kick his handler's ass right now. That's a common feeling around the WT. Come on, I know where he lives! Let's get him! IT'S CLOBBERIN' TIME!* *Sorry, narrational tangent. But you know that I just had to go--* Undertaker: Old School! *Quiet, you.* Undertaker: *Sulks and sinks his head into his shoulders, walking off crying.* *Anyway, The End has a promo he'd like to get to. He apologizes for these shoot promos, sure they're short and pointless compared to storyline RPs but his handler has been busy with the holidays and writing the Decimation Main Event, and in a pinch these are easier to do. It can be forgiven. Right? Right?* *Oh, God, End's gonna lose by default. Well, there goes the kegger I had planned. Then again, maybe I should just try to convince him to hold another early party. It's worked for him in the past.* *Man, I talk to much. Take it, End.* End:'But seriously, Tommy? A surfer? Maybe he's under the sea somewhere after all, how do I know he even exists? Maybe Rush Mason hits me with a chair. He's always been jealous of me. Maybe it was Chet King. He needs attention. Dante Jackson? Nah, he's not the guts for it. Snow? Possible. He'll attack anything that moves.' 'Seriously, do we even know if Totally Tubular Tommy is real? Did some 22-year-old wannabe professional wrestler with a couple of minutes to spare fill out an application and patch together a video of some guy and pass it off as him? {Wink wink}' 'At this point, I might as well keep cutting promos against former roster members and low-carders. It worked well enough for me and I had a little fun doing it.' *At this point, End tears up the paper, which goes off on another point, and decides to do his own thing.* End:'I have a poem I made up. I'd like to read it to you all, if you don't mind.' *The End takes in a deep breath and starts up the poem.* Yes, ladies and gentlemen. Check the garbage, you'll definitely have more courage. Look in the outgoing ships and trains and buses and planes. Look in fair weather, snow, wind, and rain. Go high and low, to and fro. Check a house even if the woman's visited by Aunt Flo. Totally Tubular Tommy is trying to run, but he can't hide. He's a punk, to keep from being too snide. In our match together, you'll be amazed, the way my kicks will leave poor Tommy dazed. A powerbomb here, a jawbreaker there; to save face, he'll start hitting himself and I'll just be sitting in a chair. He can't touch me, not a single punch lands, at this point he's nervous, making demands. 'Let me hit you,' he'll cry with a tear, but I'll be too busy laughing to hear. His impending loss clear from the beginning, all the people betting on me thinking merrily about all their winnings. Odds will be 0:1000 on Tommy, the faker, but there still won't be that many takers. I'll beat him so hard he'll be absolutely astounded, I'll slam him so much he'll think he's surrounded. And he'll decide, in a single action so bright, to stop his poor attempts at trying to fight. At the end of the night, he'll be seeing white; starting straight up at the arena lights. Yes, indeed, it will be the end, for our dear surfer Tommy, our friend. But like all my stories, there's a single ray of hope. The roster will be cleared of another useless dope.... *Off-camera, Sandy claps at End's rhyme, and unfortunately, we're out of time...* *End*
