'Bob's Story Time'...Sometimes, even God makes mistakes, part 4

29 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-29-2002 The scene opens once more into the office of God, or Bob as he prefers. He is dressed in a pure white robe today, and his legs are pale albeit well built…After all, would God be out of shape? I think not. He lounges on the white leather couch in his office today, and he holds the leather bound book on his lap. He smiles warmly as the camera zooms in and he pats the book with his hand… Bob: Greetings once again, Children. I trust that you have been waiting eagerly for this next installment of what I like to call “Bob’s Story Time”. Where were we? Ahh yes. I remember. The great Venneman Pendragon had just experienced his first reincarnation. Sometimes you should watch what you wish for…You may just get it. Let’s see…. Bob opens the book and the shot zooms in on the scene that we left last; a beautiful blonde woman in golden armor, checking herself out in the polished window of a hospital delivery room. A confused look is set upon the face of Vennewoman, and she let’s out a distressed womanly scream. With that done, she races from the room and the scene cuts to show the newly curvaceous Vennewoman walking down the street. The male passersby give looks of obvious approval and Vennewoman grows more red with anger at each look of lust. Finally, he ducks into the gloom of a bar. He heads to a corner and sits down, trying to keep to him/herself. “Christ! I never realized being an attractive wench was so difficult in today’s society. Were it times past, men would not be so wanton in their looks. Respect ruled the day and people normally got their due. Now though. Good Christ! I do not think I can handle this. Though I am strong, being a queen is not near the same as being a King…” Almost as if on que, a group of five thuggish looking men walk into the bar. They strut up to the bar and order various drinks, paying no attention to the stunning blonde in the back of the bar… “Hmm…Maybe this could be my ticket back upstairs. Those foolish men may be able to help me to right my…Problem.” Vennewoman stands and saunters up to the bar. He/she gives the fellas a “come hither” look and smiles… Vennewoman: Any of you lads have the sac to order me a drink? The five men fall all over one another to try and get his/her attention and finally Vennewoman gets a beer. He/she takes a sip and frowns… Vennewoman: Hmm…Poor taste. Looks like you boys won’t be having fun after all… With that, he/she turns to go and the five men get angry. One of them grabs Vennewoman and spins her/him around, pressing his body close…. Thug 1: Aww….Don’t be like that honey! We just wanna have a lil fun! Another moves up behind him/her and gives his/her ass a hard pinch…. Thug 2: Yeah baby….No need to be like that…. Vennewoman’s face grows more red by the second… “I must escape from this body. I guess if I am going to make a move, it might as well be now….” Vennewoman: I bet thy members would not satisfy one of thy own asses, let alone a real woman such as I. Thug 3: Huh? What language you talkin anyhow? Thug 1: Talkin da shit about our peckers, boys… Vennewoman: unfortunately, size does matter, and you boys, size ain’t. With that, Vennewoman draws Excalibur and hacks one thug down the middle of his shirt. He screams and falls down, grabbing his bleeding chest. Vennewoman smiles and backs into a defensive stance… Vennewoman: Tough love, boys…Anyone else in the mood tonight? The four men who are still standing gasp at the hot chick’s violence. They look at one another, nod, and each one pulls a handgun… Thug 1: Sometimes da bitches just gots to get put in check boys….. BLAM! BLAM! BLAM! BLAM! BLAM! BLAM! BLAM! BLAM! Vennewoman spins around like a marionette as she/he is filled with hot lead from the bullets of the murdering thugs. They continue to shoot until the body is a bloody mess, driven to madness by the fact that she smiles the whole time they are shooting… “Finally….RELEASE!” The shot abruptly switches back to the waiting room, and Peter sits behind his desk, a look of obvious annoyance writ large upon his face. He shakes his head as Venneman, now back to his manly stature and clad in white armor, stands smugly before him. Before Peter can speak, Venneman reaches across the desk and pulls Peter across by his coat’s lapels. He lifts him into the air and gazes up at the frightened cleric… Venneman: What treachery thought you to enact upon thy Lord King? Art thou insane? Thou wouldst send me back as a WENCH! I would rather be a dumb animal than a wench! What is the problem up here? Can thou do nothing right? Peter, visibly shaken, tries to respond in a confident tone, but his shaky voice betrays his true state…Fear… Peter: Uhh….We made no mistake. We sent you to the same time and general area as when you first left the earthly realm. What more did you ask? Venneman: THOU SENT ME HOME AS A WENCH! I am a warrior. I am a MAN! Women are no warriors! What kind of life would that have been for one such as I? I need to be a man! Women do not rule men! I DO! He drops Peter to the floor with a thud. Peter picks himself up from the cold white tiles, dusts himself off and sighs…. Peter: Ok….We will try to fix the situation. Same time, same place….Male. No problem…. Venneman nods curtly as Peter leads him from the room to the RESEARCH facility. He enters the reincarnation chamber and the men in white coats go about their business. Peter smiles as they press the GO button and send Venneman once more on his merry way. One fellow looks at Peter, a tad unnerved… Man: Uhoh…. Peter: What? Tell me you didn’t fuck it up again… Man: Umm….I didn’t fuck it up again? Peter: Now tell me the truth…. Man: Just come look at the screen…. The shot zooms in and we see Venneman Pendragon push the baby to safety. The car screams in and hits him full on, once again. He flies through the air and the scene zooms in on a squirrel that just happens to be crossing the road… “Hmm…Why does everything look so big? Oh SHIT!” The squirrel looks up just in time to see the body of Venneman Pendragon falling right at it. Pendragon lands with a sickening thud and the squirrel is squashed with a sickening >>SQUISH
