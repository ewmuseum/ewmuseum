Bens World - I Am Driving In My Car

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Tuesday April 9th Oh my, Ben Pailthorpe is in a very silly mood, that must mean Ben J. Pailthorpe will also be in a very silly mood, now that kafaybe breaking is over lets get on with it... ...it being the roleplay. The scene opens in a brightly lit room, yes a brighlty lit room, how original. Sitting in chairs *gasp* are Ben J. Pailthorpe and GW superstar Fazz McLovely. 'Reindeers are everything to these people...' remarks Ben as we take a closer look at the chairs. Red leather, ooooh, cushions, suits your sir, foot rest, pwhooooaaarrr, looking very comfortable, do you want it sir? do you? Ben is wearing clothes, who would of thought it? A nice friendly t-shirt with a hand and the word 'Love' on it, blue jeans and a pair of Airwalk trainers. Fazz McLovely is wearing a red thong. 'Dude, put some clothes on.' Ben says while averting his eyes to the ceiling. 'But I like the freedom.' says Fazz. 'Just do it!' says Ben, sternly. What clothes should Fazz wear? 'Have him in blue jeans cut off at the knee white trainers, his blonde hair tied back wearing cool sungalsses with an 'I Love Lucy' T-shirt on' says Fazz's handler. You got it! I feel like wearing blue jeans cut off at the knee, white trainers, my blonde hair tied back while wearing cool sunglasses and an 'I Love Lucy' T-shirt'' says Fazz smiling. Fazz disapears off into an ajoining room and reappears fully clothed in, you guessed it, blue jeans cut off at the knee, white trainers, his blonde hair is tied back and he is wearing cool sunglasses with an 'I Love Lucy' T-shirt on. 'Wasnt that weird.' remarks Ben. 'What?' asks Fazz. 'That whole thing that just happened.' says Ben. 'Yeah.' says Fazz. Right...' says Ben. '...I want to buy a car!' 'Cool!' says Fazz. 'Totally, I still got 500,000 left over from whooping that team of professional footballers a few weeks back.' says Ben. 'Sweeeeeeeeeet.' says Fazz. 'Yeah.' says Ben. 'What you gonna get?' asks Fazz. 'A car.' says Ben 'What car?' asks Fazz 'Dunno.' says Ben. With that our scene fades out....and then back in again (wow!) and we are at a place that sells cars...yeah. The dodgy flickering neon sign says Dodgy Daves Dangerous Drives and it buzzing very loudly. BJP and Fazz walk under the sign into the lot outside the shop where all the cars are parked with little price tags in the windows. BJP and the Fazzman are approached by a weasly looking guy in a cheap suit wearing a bad toupe. 'Hi.' the man says. 'Im Dodgy Dave.' 'You sure are!' says Fazz. Ben hits Fazz. 'Hey, i'd like to buy a car.' says Ben. 'Then youve come to the right place!' says Dodgy Dave. 'Great, Ben wanted to go to Ikea.' says Fazz. Ben hits Fazz again. 'How much do you want to spend?' asks Dodgy Dave. 'Well, I have about 500 grand with me.' says Ben. Dodgy Dave's eyes light up. 'Excellent...' says Dodgy Dave. Dave leads Ben up to a Ferrari 355 F1 Spider. 'What do you think of this?' says Dodgy Dave. 'Well...' says Ben. 'He'll take it!' says Fazz. Ben hits Fazz. 'No thanks.' says Ben. 'Ok...' says Dodgy Dave. He takes them over to a Jaguar XK 180. 'Wow.' says Fazz. 'Nope.' says Ben. He leads them to a McClaren f1 GT. 'Woah...' says Fazz. 'Top of the line.' says Dodgy Dave. 'Na...' says Ben. Ben spots an old beat up Volkswagen camper van over the back. 'How much for the VW?' asks Ben. 'That?' asks Dodgy Dave, pointing at the van. 'Yeah.' says Ben. '$350.' says Dodgy Dave. 'I'll take it.' says Ben with a smile. 'Noooo.' says Fazz. Ben hits Fazz. 'Are...you...sure?' asks Dodgy Dave. 'Yes.' says Ben happily. He walks over to Dave's office. A moment later he emerges with the keys and a smile, Dodgy Dave shakes his hand and he calls Fazz over into the van. 'This could be the start of a wonderful friendship...' he says as he pulls the van coughing and spluttering out of the lot and off into the sunset.
