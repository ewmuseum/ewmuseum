The Institute.

Date: not entered “Hello and welcome to the Institute of Wrestling Sciences. Here at the IWS, or the Institute, as we like to call it, we are making your safety our number one priority!” A very technology-filled setting greets the eye as we follow a pin-headed nerd of a scientist throughout a vast laboratory filled with computers and wrestling paraphernalia. The scientist pushes up his glasses by the tape holding the bridge together and gestures grandly at the expansive outlay. “As you can see, our top men are working around the clock to keep not only our wrestlers as safe as possible, but our ringside fans, and in the extreme case, even our viewers.” The scientist walks over to containment cube with a bust with a bright orange(?) colored wig plastered on top. A team of men work a robotic arm from behind a great Plexiglas, each movement of the arm painstakingly calculated. Sweat pours down their faces as the arm runs through the hair, unnatural colors refracted from the wig. The original scientist places a hand on the shoulder of one of the men working the arm, “So, how go the experiments regarding Xavier Kannon’s hair?” “Well, we haven’t been able to isolate the color yet, and we may be dealing with a whole NEW color entirely!” “Fascinating!” Another man stands, grinning, “We’d like to call it ‘Musk,’ if tests hold up.” “And the tests regarding the epileptic seizures?” A small man in a lab coat frown, pointing inside the room to a small cage of mice, twitching and convulsing on their backs. “Unfortunately, whenever the hair is tossed thusly, they still go into seizure, meaning we’ll have to keep up with the topical treatments on Kannon himself. The color is so unnatural, in fact not found in any other capacity in the world, that minds cannot deal with it, thus the seizing. The real bitch was finding wallets small enough to fit into the little rodents mouths…” The first scientist rubs his chin, thoughtfully, “Hmmm… that is a problem. Well, keep trying to isolate the colors within… perhaps one day we can permanently treat his hair to a more natural color…” “Yes sir!” “Yes, here at the Institute, we work hard to keep you, our high paying, wrestling consumers, as protected as possible. Hey Jeff! How’s the work on Aimz coming?” A man set aside in a special sterilized isolation room looks up from his work, head to toe in a containment suit. “I’ve found 273 STDs to date with no end in sight, including NEW ones I can name after myself!!… or Aimz. Probably name them after Aimz…” “Well, keep up the hard work as our customers demand only the safest in sports-entertainment action! Over here is our ‘Reanimation Lab’, specifically designed to figure out EXACTLY how certain wrestlers have a tendency to rise up from the dead.” Enter a wing of the lab filled with Frankenstein-esque equipment, boiling beakers with long running tubes and multi-colored liquids piping through. A voodoo shaman walks over, a grim expression. “Hey Steve,” says the shaman, his pleasant tone belying his appearance, “Well, I’ve managed to figure out a FEW things here and there in regards to Darkstar, Kriegman, in a round-about way the Pharaoh’s 4000 year existence, etc., but nothing concrete. It’s a mystery even to me.” “But is the public SAFE?” “Well, unless they go chowing down on their brains and they turn out to actually be just zombies, then the men of wrestling who recently were post-humus seem to pose no significant threat.” “Excellent!” “Later Steve,” waves the shaman, shaking a maraca and a necklace of chicken bones. “Over here we have the Lord Crom Griffin D’agoth Research Study where our men are trying to determine the exact effects of… a…” Steve walks by a large table, where a slew of men in lab coats are snoring noisily, drooling on the marble top. A D’agoth promo prattles on the television in the middle. Steve reaches into his coat and produce a shiny, red button. He hits it, and a crack security team fitted with blinders and ear plugs rushes the table, dragging the poor men to safety. Steve sighs heavily as he faces the camera, “Poor guys. That’s the ninth brave team we’ve lost. But, after a short coma, subsequent occupational therapy, and keeping them away from sharp objects and long ropes for a month or so and they’ll be alright. Sad, really… but a risk we take here at the Institute to keep you, the viewer, as warm and safe and cozy as Alistair Cooke, sipping cocoa by the fireplace and growing fat on ring-dings.” “You should have seen the Shawn Prodigy experiment…” A violent, cold shudder. His face grows dark with remembrances “Some of us are just now learning how to love again…” The nerdy Steve shakes it off, smiling bravely into the camera. “But that is what we do here at the Institute for Wrestling Sciences! No risk is too great, no challenge too daunting for our undertaking as long as the fans are the ones to benefit! To my left, you’ll see our research into the astounding secret behind Duke William’s success!” A man looks up from a binocular microscope, punching upwards in the air for affect. “BOOZE!” “Behind me is the testing grounds of THE Multiple Man’s workrate, on the electron microscope…” A huge, dazzling machine, towering up into the air. A team of unhappy scientist’s look up from their work, a man in a beard stepping forward. “Still haven’t found it,” he grumbles. “Over here,” says Steve, waving excitedly, “We see JUST how much Byron Calver values his virginity! A chart here detailing the ‘Russian PUSH’ theory! Vegita! The lasting and deeply scarring psychological effects of Flaymin Aymin’s lost testicle! How much goblin IS Syko, anyways?! Is the SWERVE physically possible?! All these questions needing answers! BWAHAHAHAHAHA!!!” The lab stops, all looking at Steve and his maniacal laughter. Gradually, he becomes aware of their stares and coughs, straightening himself up and calming down. “But over here is our most important work to date. You see, based on several metaphysical theories, matter and anti-matter cannot come into contact. To do so would create cataclysmic consequences on a universal scale! But unfortunately, such a situation may exist with the recent Friday Fusion card in PCW.” Steve places a welding helmet over his head and steps through a steel-reinforced door. There, the room is sectioned off in the middle by a thick, lead wall. Men dressed in safety gear head to toe bustle and hustle, prepping the room for the oncoming experiment. Steve walks over to the head of the experiment, a skittish man with haunted eyes. “So,” queries Steve, “How’s it going?” “It’s not good, not good at all,” the man nervously replies, eyes darting every which way, “The two samples have been reacting violently with every test.” “Hmmm…” “I just don’t see the point!! It’s not safe!” Steve turns to the camera, gravely. “This room houses samples of the essence of our World Champion, Big Alan Steele, and his most recent arch-nemesis, Frisco.” “We sampled their essence at a routine physical. Standard procedure,” the squirrelly man chimes in. “Indeed. But we have, unfortunately, run into serious problems here at the Institute. What was once thought to be a cute little gimmick match may in fact have DIRE consequences to all involved!” “Right Steve. You see, when we get the samples anywhere near each other, they become highly unstable substances, more sensitive and explosive than even tripolium! But when we tried to tell the essences that they had to work together for a common goal…” “Wait a minute… ‘you TOLD the essences?’ That doesn’t sound very scientific.” “Well, I admit to being a tad glib. We injected the essences into lab mice and forced them to work together in order to be fed.” “And?” The scientist frowns and pints across the room to a scorched area, a gaping hole carved into the steel wall. “It didn’t go well at all.” “Egad!! What of the safety of the fans?!” “I’ve already sent a memo to the brass in PCW and they replied saying, I quote, ‘Duh, that’s what we want’, end quote.” “Hmmm… add in Kannon’s hair and Pharaoh’s ‘longevity’… this could get ugly…” “It already has!” Even between the lead partition, the small vials of essence, barely 5 feet apart, begin to smoke and bubble violently. Steve shakes his head sadly, before continuing on with the tour. “In the next room, we have taken steps to find out exactly what Ed’s ‘IT’ really is… and our scientists have reason to believe it is a creamy layer of nougat…”
