Jogging With A Golf Cart

28 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 28, 2002 [When a man gets out of the habit of doing one thing by jumping into something similar, but with an entirely different element...that man begins getting used to that way of his life. He begins to realize the ease and the togetherness that comes with this new line of his career. It's the same profession, but with a partner at his side. When the man has dedicated his life to this new part of his career he is excited and can only keep his mind of that one thing...that one goal. Suddenly, by the flash of fate it's taken away from him. The entire dream, the entire goal is wiped from existence because of one thing. That's all it seems to take really, just one little mistake. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy now knows about this situation because he's living it at this very moment. His brother, and now former tag team partner, suffered a career ending injury...a broken back. It was just a bad fall, one wrong turn and it's all finished. The Cowabunga Connection is dead...buried...rest in peace. Tommy had such high hopes for the team, that they would dominate until the day they called it quits, on their own terms...together. Tommy can't live with that fact that he's a singles wrestler once again. He was quite a damn fine one in NTICW, going as far as being named one of the most influencial and legendary men to ever grace the federation...but now he's in a totally new element...he's in Primetime Championship Wrestling...as a singles star. Nine months of being a perfect tag team, a unit...a man can get used to that. Tommy can't fathom Harry standing in his corner once again, just a spectator like in the NTICW days. Watching Harry walk over to the announcers chair to do commentary...to make sure that Tommy gets the correct calls on his moves. Not that Harry ever gets it right either, but at least he tries and that's what counts. The Tubular One must accept his new destiny, or else he could face true failure and damnation...or end up crippling his brother, whichever happens first] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Harry...don't...doubt...yourself...dude.... [The tanned man jogs down the boardwalk of the popular line of Los Angeles beaches. The sun is just peaking over the horizon for another early morning start. Tommy's blonde locks bouncing up and down as he takes each stride like a true champion. His bright blue eyes have focus in them, but yet heartbreak at the same time. His black wifebeater is soaked with sweat, the Cowabunga COnnection logo fading from use. The irony is bittersweet. His blue and yellow Bike shorts cling tightly to his muscular thighs giving him the optimal flexibility when going for his warm up jog] TTT: You...shouldn't just give up on everything else...dude. [The scene zooms out to show Harry beside Tommy...in a golf cart. Harry has his long brown hair tied back in a tight pony tail today. There is a faint smile on his face, and no it's not the refer, but it's the feeling of a new beginning. The golf cart used to be white, but with a far out spray painting job it now sports an all black exterior, with a Twisted Sister emblem on each side. It's the Twisted Mobile, man. Pretty far out, huh? The golf cart rides along side The Tubular One, keeping up with his very fast paced jogging. The small, black tires rumble against the wooden planks of the boardwalk. The faint sounds of Twisted Sister's 'See No Evil' drowns out that sound though...that could also explain the smile on Harry's face. Who knows really] Harry: Stop the talking, man. Run faster! [Tommy gives Harry a hard look and then looks forward] TTT: What the hell is that all about, compadre? Harry: Motivation, man. TTT: Dude, you never were like that. Harry: I know, but I want to be like that guy from Rocky, man. TTT: Har, just stop before you get heinously ahead of yourself. [Tommy chugs on, already running around the large circular boardwalk twelve times for a grand total of 15 miles. He has to keep his endurance up to an all time high if he's going to be stepping back into the squared circle as a totally gnarly singles competitor. Hopefully it won't be too long as a loner inthe ring, just as long as Harry gets his bogus butt back into gear. By the looks of his face in that righteous golf cart, he has absolutely no signs of wanting to climb back into that ring. It's ashame because Harry had such excellent potential to be a great wrestler] TTT: DUDE, WATCH OUT!!! [Tommy was too deep in thought to not notice Harry slamming into another jogger with his golf cart. The poor man, who looks to be in his forties, rolls onto his back. The man shakes his fist in anger mumbling about every curse word that he can muster] TTT: You just nailed that dude. [Harry looks over at Tommy with an evil smile] Harry: This is our boardwalk, man. [Tommy just shakes his head and keeps on keepin' on. Every stride is a step closer to higher endurance, much more stamina. He's a lean, mean, gnarly wrestling machine. He's the master of his bodacious domain. He controls his future now. The wind blowing in his blonde locks, the morning air rushing in and out of his lungs, and the righteous sun beginning to gleam on his tanned skin] TTT: HARRY!! [A twenty-something jogger hops to the side to avoid Harry, but the retired wrestler swerves the golf cart toward the man and side swipes him over the railing, into a pile of sand. Harry chuckles looking back at his far out conquest. He chuckles toward Tommy who looks at Harry as if he's a lunatic...which he is] TTT: You just purposely ran into that compadre. Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Why?! He moved out of your way. He did nothing bogus. Harry: Do I need to reason to run over people in the Twisted Mobile, man? TTT: Not if you want to spend some heinous time in the slammer, dude. Harry: No way, man. [Tommy sighs, wondering what exactly goes through that dudes mind sometimes. One minute he can be so normal and so human, and then the next he's the oddest oddball that God ever put on this Planet called Earth. It's kind of tough to believe that they are brothers...actuallly, they are half brothers. Different fathers, but born to the same mother. They are one year apart, The Tubular One being the oldest, so he wins when it comes to the wisdom department. They started out best friends from the days in diapers. They flung poo at the walls together. They then worked their way to crawling, walking, talking, and being freakin' clueless! Inseparable is the word that first comes to mind. Two different personalities, two different bodies. Both of them almost on a different spectrum of personality yet they bond so well. A perfect duo. When one is missing the other loses a little bit of that fire in their eyes. When they found out they were brothers it was pretty shocking, but not entirely. They already considered themselves brothers, it's just now that it was legally binding. They were blood. They were tag team partners, lighting PCW up with such greatness, hightening the bar for future tag team stars to grace the ring. Giving PCW fans something different, something that many can relate to, and something for the ladies...at least Tommy anyway. They conquered so many things as a tag team, except what seemed to matter the most...the PCW Tag Team Titles. Now with Harry's broken back and early retirement, it seems the Cowabunga Connection will never achieve that dream of becoming tag champs. Tommy can't cope, but at least Harry is still by his side] TTT: Rush isn't going to know what hit him, Har. My first singles match in so long, a journey into a new bodacious realm. It's going to be a most gnarly thing to make Rush my first victim in a long line of heinous dudes that will fall by my radical hand. [Harry is still listening, but uses his golf cart to clip a roller blader. The inline skaters does a front flip straight onto his back. Luckily the man wore elbow pads and they absorb the crushing fall. Harry laughs like a madman, his bladder constricting in and out. He feels like he could pee himself, but that would TOTALLY not be far out. He holds it, but continues his maddening laughter] Harry: Your going to have no problem with Rush, man. The only reason he's got the spot he has is because he rode Xavier Kannon's coat tails in those NTICW days. TTT: You were stoned crazy during those times, dude. Harry: That's true, man. But I still can remember that...I mean who couldn't. TTT: You. Harry: Yeah...your right, man. But your going to win, man....it's that simple. Your doing this jogging thing while Rush screams to the God's of why he lost at Alliances. You'll have the edge because...I'm your manager, man!! NOW RUN FASTER YOU PUSSY!! [Tommy gives Harry another hard look] TTT: Your really cruisin' for a most heinous beating, dude. [Harry laughs like a crazy man and smashes into another roller blader, but this guy ends up on the hood of the golf cart, hanging on for dear life] Roller Blader: AHHH!! Your insane! Harry: HAHAHA, MAN!!!! Roller Blader: MOMMY! [The roller blader rolls of the side of the golf cart, rolling like a tumbleweed across the boardwalk. Harry laughs banging his head to Twisted Sister] Harry: FEAR THE ALMIGHTY TWISTED MOBILE, MAN! TTT: God help us all, dudes. [Fade to black]
