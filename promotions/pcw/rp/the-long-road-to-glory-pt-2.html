Date: 04/09/04 With more alcohol on tap than a USC frat house, the Hilton Plaza in Memphis, Tennessee is the site of a pre-Turmoil star gala. Many high-profile guests are in attendance, including Hollywood stars and some of sport’s most prolific athletes. It just so happens, by a matter of coincidence, that one “Untouchable” Shane Crockett finds himself smack in the middle of the madness, drinking the only beer he’d ever touch, a Corona. He saunters through the crowd, hoping to get drunk as Hell on the last night of his non-wrestling life. And to avoid interviews, which he’d grown excessively sick of. As he walks, a tall, husky man brushes past him. Shane turns back, and is startled to be staring straight at the Governor of California himself.Shane Crockett: What in the Bloody Hell? Arnold Schwarzenegger? Dude, you’re like, the coolest, yo.Arnold Schwarzenegger: Thank you, and thanks for the invitation, Buzz… right?Shane Crockett: No, Shane Crockett. Buzz is getting shit-faced somewhere over in the corner.Shane does a double take, making sure he really just saw Arnold Schwarzenegger walk by. After a few more drinks, he’d be seeing Halle Berry in her birthday suit. A rough looking fellow wearing a faded denim cap broke that thought off relatively quickly. Shane could tell by the pad in his hand that he had another fucking interview on the way.Shane Crockett: Well, the fun continues.Rolling Stone Interviewer: Mr. Crockett? Sorry, this will just take a second. I’m Greg Anthony, with Rolling Stone?Shane Crockett: Yeah, I’ve got time for an interview.Rolling Stone Interviewer: Well, actually, I wanted to get your opinion on a few things, since you seem to be the talk of the town.Shane Crockett: Hey, if you dressed like Ashton Kutcher and rode in a pimped out ride like my GTO, you’d be worshiped too, man.Rolling Stone Interviewer: I thought you drove a Celica that was, quote, “A piece of shit”?Shane Crockett: Oh, that car. Yeah, I like to-Rolling Stone Interviewer: Can you tell me how you’re feeling about tomorrow night’s big match against Jimmy Diablo? Shane Crockett: Jimmy Diablo is about twenty four hours away from a Major League ass kicking, Crockett style. And there sure as Hell ain’t gonna be no Alamo references, or no butter references. He thinks he’s good, maybe even my equal? I’m Scarface, baby; he’s a Star Wars Prequel. Rolling Stone Interviewer: Uh huh, and you’re not worried at all about losing in your PCW debut in front of a nationwide audience?Shane Crockett: As far as I’m concerned, this match is already over with. You know, I might have been worried for about 5.2 seconds in between Tucson and Omaha, but after that, I’m like Jackie Chan, baby. A little Kung Fu Power in the tower for Memphis.Rolling Stone Interviewer: I thought it was the Pyramid?Shane Crockett: Right. Whatever. A pair of stunning young women walk by, drawing Shane’s attention away from the interviewer. He quickly jumps away from the befuddled reporter, running to catch up along side the two buxom beauties. Shane Crockett: Why, my God in Heaven! I didn’t know we had angels in the room tonight? Tell me, how would you like to skip this little get-together and re-enact Creation at my hotel room?Blonde Woman: No thanks. Re-enact Creation? Is that your pickup line, honey, because if it is, I think you need to head out around the corner where those street warmers are giving pleasure for twenty bucks. Redheaded Woman: Let’s go, I need a drink. Shane Crockett: You need a Stair Master, with so much junk in that trunk! Damn, there’s people trying to walk around here without getting knocked through the wall by your sick, bulbous ass, you carpet munching freak!Blonde Woman: Ugh, whatever!Shane Crockett: Fucking lesbians.Shane gigs them behind their backs, wishing intently that he’d used a better line on two pricks like them. Still, the thought would’ve been nice, and they certainly looked hotter than Carmen Electra and Anna Kournikova in swimsuits. With a swig of his brew, Shane spins back to find the reporter… and runs smack dab into Cal Seaver, Tiffany Jennings, and several camera men baring the PCW logo on their shirts.Cal Seaver: Shane! Can we get a word with you?Shane Crockett: Jesus H. Christ…Cal Seaver: I’m sorry, but it will just take a moment.Shane Crockett: No, no, Hell fucking no. I’ve already talked to three or four different interviewers already, and I’m about out of material already! I’m not Eminem for goodness sake! I can’t pull shit out of my ass on command after a week of nothing but interview after interview!Tiffany Jennings: How about an interview with me?Shane Crockett: I’d be delighted to grant you an exclusive interview.Tiffany Jennings: Great! Alright guys, we’re shooting for one take here. Let’s not fuck this up too badly.Shane Crockett: You heard the woman, guys. Don’t fuck this up, or I’ll have to beat your ass like ghosts on Pac-Man.Shane straightens his shirt, tossing the empty beer bottle aside as Tiffany takes a microphone from a mountain of a man standing nearby. She fluffs her hair up as Crockett suddenly has perverse thoughts about putting his arm around her shoulders. A loud cackle from a production manager over a headset ends that thought rather quickly, as a crowd starts to swarm towards the make-shift interview segment. Tiffany gets the green light from a camera man, and begins the conversation.Tiffany Jennings: On the eve of the largest Turmoil card in years, we’re here at the Hilton Plaza in Memphis, Tennessee, where I’m standing beside one of PCW’s newest superstars, Shane Crockett.Shane Crockett: Thanks for doing me, I mean having me.Tiffany Jennings: Okay, then. Shane, you have a match against Jimmy Diablo for the #1 Seed in the Face to Face tournament tomorrow night. Although you’ve been anything but shy in your verbal challenges to the man, Diablo has strangely remained silent for the most part over the past week. How has this affected your game plan going into tomorrow night?Shane Crockett: I’ll tell you just like I told another reporter a few minutes ago; Diablo must be pissing his pants right now over the thought of having to get his ass whooped on FOX tomorrow night! I mean, I really feel for the guy, getting knocked out by a rookie in his first match. God, that’s got to suck. But you see, Jimmy, you’re destined to lose. People like you are destined to lose in the end, because the Good Book says so. Now I’m not a bible-thumping maniac like some people in this federation are, and I don’t even pretend to be. If I can fuck my neighbor’s wife, steal his car, and piss on his lawn and get famous over night, then I’m there. But until then, I’ll hold stock in the fact that God said you’re getting your ass whopped tomorrow night in Memphis!Tiffany Jennings: Well, looking past tomorrow night, who else are you looking forward to meeting inside the ring?Shane Crockett: Ha, funny you should ask, because there’s a ton of guys that are just lining up, one by one by one to get their ass kicked by greatness. I mean, it’s almost downright comical how many people are bending over already, just because I talk a big game!Tiffany Jennings: But you do talk a big game?Shane Crockett: Second only to my bedroom game, sweet thing.Tiffany Jennings: But-Shane Crockett: Yes, I like your ass, now let’s get back to the topic at hand. There’s plenty of fools out there that I can make asses of, most notably a man by the name of Propaganda. Now Props, bro, no offense, but you got beat by Jimmy fucking Diablo, the guy that represents everything you hate. Again, no offense, but are you that damn pathetic? A fucking Teletubby could beat Jimmy Diablo, and you still lost? I’ve seen Barry Horowitz wrestle better matches!Tiffany Jennings: With all due respect, Propaganda is one of many superstars in PCW that have given everything they’ve had in every match. That just goes to show you how tough the competition here in PCW is!Shane Crockett: No, that just goes to show you that Propaganda sucks, PCW is full of scrawny faced, dim-witted stoners that think Mary Jane is their cousin from West Virginia, and two-bit sluts definitely don’t need to be intelligent to give blowjobs like the bottom feeding whores they are!Tiffany Jennings: Excuse me?Shane Crockett: You heard me, bottom feeding whores! Those two jezebels over there are just completely classless at a function like this. And to think that you’re eyes have to be subjected to that kind of filth, good God! Tiffany Jennings: Oh, for a second there. Ha ha, for a second I thought you were talking about me!Shane Crockett: Why would I say that about you when I desperately want to just carry you off to bed right now and ride you like a pony?Tiffany Jennings: What the Hell did you just say?Shane Crockett: Just kidding! Good God, you’re as gullible as Jimmy Diablo! Take a fucking chill pill already!Tiffany Jennings: I think we’ve got it-Shane Crockett: No, you don’t have it, you don’t got it, take a shot, smoke some pot, whoopee fucking do! The hour’s drawing closer, Dibbles! I’m going to expose you for the Beebop loving, deep throat shoving prick you are! One match, one bus to glory for me to catch. You better get your ass in gear and show up tomorrow night, honky, because I’ll fuck you up worse than R. Kelly at a Michael Jackson sleep over! Tiffany Jennings: Well, there you have it, Shane Crockett everyone. Shane Crockett: Tiff, baby, the evening is still young! Listen, maybe you can do me a favor.Tiffany Jennings: Stop rolling, guys. What the Hell do you want?Shane Crockett: Well, you see, I’m feeling kind of sorry for Jimmy Diablo. I mean, everyone and their uncle knows he’s gonna get beat tomorrow night like the prison bitch he is, but I still want to find some way to make it up to him. So I tell you what; Jimmy needs a friend after the match is over, and since the only woman he could get inside of is the Statue of Liberty, maybe it’s time he got that lovin’ feeling, you know?Tiffany Jennings: Go to Hell!Shane Crockett: I’m already in Hell, baby, watching you saunter around in a dress I just want to rip off! Diablo wouldn’t touch you with a ten foot pole! I’m the party baby, any time you want. Ric Flair might be Space Mountain, but I’m Disney World, baby! I’ve got balls the size of Epcot!Tiffany Jennings thinks about slapping the taste out of Crockett’s mouth, but Shane ducks away before she has the chance. Whistling to himself, Shane starts towards the exit of the building, wanting to catch a few hours of sleep before his workout in the morning. Whether he needed much energy to beat Diablo was one thing, but he could definitely use a nice, long session in the weight room after slacking off. Shane pushes through the doors into the warm, serene night air… and suddenly realizes just how important this match would be. He could make all the tasteless remarks he wanted about Jimmy Diablo, and while most of it was warranted, he still couldn’t deny the fact that his life could change in a big way on Saturday night. With his confidence wavering somewhat, Shane starts the long, dreary walk back towards the Pyramid downtown, thinking about the moment that he’d be able to maim Diablo and get the match over with.Crockett: Soon, you little prick, I’ll get you soon enough. Sleep now, Jimmy Diablo, because it’s the last night you have of being number one! Sleep well, my friend. You’re gonna need all the Help you can get!Por Fin.
