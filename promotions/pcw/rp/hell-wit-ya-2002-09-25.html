He wipes the sweat away from his smooth face and continues to pass by sweeping up hair. There is a line of black leather seats lined against the left side of the wall. Mirrors are screwed into the white wall in back of every seat. Between the seats and mirrors there is a forest green marble sink with hair, scissors, and hair clippers. He is wearing no shirt exposing his oiled down rippling muscles. He has an earring in each nipple, which makes Buuday’s heart stop. The first lady is in her mid 50's with the wrinkles to prove it. Her eyes are of a blue color and you can tell in her facial features she is Korean. Sheeca notices how perfect her nails are and knows that she is in for something great. The Korean lady has her hair pulled back and tied in a ponytail with one braid hair hanging in front of her face. She wears a beautiful sky blue dress with red flowers on it. The Korean lady leans to the other one who is doing someone else toenails and says something to her in her native tongue. Sheeca looks at them as they talk to one another and glance at Sheeca everyone once in a while.] | 'Hold up, hold up. I know ya'll are talking about me! See why ya'll have to clown on me? Sheeca walks out giving that lady death threats with her eyes as the Korean lady sits her back in the seat and takes off her shoes.] | 'Why I got to get the devil lady today?' | [The Korean lady pulls out red nail polish and begins to start on her nails. As she is polishing you see the door fly open to the rub down room and Buuday is trying to get out but she is pulled right back in and the doors shut once more. Sheeca just shakes her head and thanks the lord that that is not her. The Korean lady pants toe after toe after toe carefully then grabs Sheeca's hands and places them on the table.] | 'Damn woman, jerk me around one more time and I'm straight whoop yo ass!' | [The Korean lady also pants Sheeca's long nails red one by one. Tyrone keeps yelling over her screams that it is part of the rub down. Back out in the spa, Sheeca is at the edge of her seat ready to break out running when her hair dryer cuts off and the Korean lady turns around the corner. She grabs Sheeca's arm and takes her back to the front where she curls her hair with a curling iron while the other Korean lady is putting on her make-up. For what seemed like an eternity but were only a couple of minutes the Korean ladies back away and bring Sheeca the mirror. The mirror reflects the smooth caramel skin of Sheeca with blood red lipstick. She has light blue eyeliner that gradates from dark to light. Her eyebrows are perfectly plucked and her hair is like none others with elegant curls of a god making her look that a movie-star.
