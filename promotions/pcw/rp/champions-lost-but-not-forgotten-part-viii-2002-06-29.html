Champions: Lost but not Forgotten [Part VIII]

29 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 29, 2002 Well, well, well...what an exciting Fusion. Just two days remain before the biggest match in PCW Tag Team history takes place at one of the biggest pay-per-views of the year. In yet another man on man screwing...Jack Valentine was pinned, on the bottom, on Fusion. To recap, Akiyama made his presence felt by interfering against all of the men who were not involved in the match. This created an all out brawl on the outskirts of the ring. During this, Pretty Cool Guys took it among themselves to gangbang Jack Valentine...they pulled him out of the brawl and into the ring, giving him the Pretty Cool Jam, and allowing Rich Sherwin to pick up the victory. There you have it, Jack Valentine was literally f*cked in the ass by Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore. Not that he minds or anything, that match meant nothing to him...his singles career is over...he prefers being side by side with another man in the ring now. We also saw chaos later on in the evening as all hell broke loose after the Akiyama/Mark Makonie match. All teams involved in the battle royal on Sunday made their presence felt as the battle royal nearly took place in Nashville, Tennessee. It took 4 teams, yes 4 teams to eliminate Akiyama from the ring. This left us pondering...what if Akiyama remains with only 1 other team....or even 2 or 3 other teams. Would they be able to eliminate the new monster of PCW from the ring? However, The Heartbreakers proved just why their belts were just lost and not forgotten because they were one of the final teams left in the ring. They lasted longer than Akiyama, and this Sunday will be the same story. The Heartbreakers will prove their greatness yet again as the PINK! will reign supreme over the nVg...the crude PoP stars that wouldn’t let Jack and Johnny into the band...the surfers who luckily defeated Jack and Johnny a few weeks back, the politicians who were destroyed by Jack and Johnny at Supremacy...and all the rest of the teams that want to claim victory in this match. It’s the end of June. The peak of summer is arising, and the peak of The Heartbreakers’ career is approaching as well. Both Jack and Johnny were beginning to believe and think what it would be like to capture those belts once again. Jack Valentine: “Guess what Johnny?” Johnny Heart: “You bought a new PINK! suit?” Jack Valentine: “Close, but no cigar. I was just thinking about our match this Sunday...do you realize that if we win this match, we will be the undisputed tag team champions. No team can claim the right to being the greatest team in PCW, because we would have beaten them all.” Johnny Heart: “Undisputed, huh?” Jack Valentine: (Sighing) “Undisputed.” Johnny Heart: “That’d really show those damn Pretty Cool Guys who’s boss. Nothing like Undisputed tag team champions leading a band. We would’ve made them millions, but they were to selfish to realize our talent.” Jack Valentine: “Maybe someday we’ll start a band of our own...long after our wrestling careers are over.” Johnny Heart: “Yeah, like the YMCA but with a Hall & Oates twist...we would be legends.” Jack Valentine: “Indeed...we would wear custom PINK! spandex, with PINK! t-shirts and PINK! guitars. We would call ourselves PINK!” Johnny Heart: “Isn’t there already an artist named Pink?” Jack Valentine: “Oh yeah...but they’ll kick her out of the biz when we come in...” Johnny Heart: “Why is that?” Jack Valentine: “Because we really know how to get a party started.” The pair laughs at the amazingly corny joke. By any means, the excitement for this tag team extravaganza on Sunday was building. It left the Heartbreakers feeling healthy, in high spirits, and in great shape. They have fully prepared for this, as it is definitely the biggest match of their tag team careeer, and maybe even their single’s career. Bigger than Vampir, bigger than Ed...even bigger than Trashcan Man. This match will go down as an epic in PCW history. The evening, was still and cloudy; the Kentucky air was at its heaviest; the distant hum of the street-traffic was at its faintest; the small pulse of the life within them, and the great heart of the city around them, seemed to be beating faster and faster in unison, excitedly and more excited, with the sinking sun. Much of the town was eagerly anticipated the pay-per-view, as much of them were going to attend. Many were talking about the Mount Olympus match, others the Cage match, a few about the Last Man Standing Match...but most...well, most were looking forward to the battle royal. After all, battle royals are the most exciting match to watch, and they are the most action-packed with the most superstars competing at the same time. The quiet twilight was still trembling on the topmost ridges of the heath; and the view of Kentucky below me them sunk into a black gulf in the shadow of the cloudy night, when they stood before the gate of the hotel. They still pondered what it would be like...the glory they would have from winning the battle royal. A young bell-boy sat in a chair on the outside, smoking a cigarette, with an enormously evil grin on his face as he stared at The Heartbreakers...almost like he was hoping for them to show their homosexuality so he could be the first to prove that the pair was gay. Although Jack and Johnny have claimed not to be homosexuals, and just in touch with their feminine side, rumors are still running rampant. The Heartbreakers spot the boy and walk over to him. His evil grin turns into a nervous smile as Jack and Johnny tower over the smaller figure. Jack Valentine: “Hello young man.” Bell Boy: (Sarcastically) “Hello...EX-Champs!” Johnny Heart: “Do I sense some hostility Jack?” Jack Valentine: “Well I don’t know about you, but I certainly do...” Johnny Heart: “You know what this boy needs?” The boy looks on in trepidation. Jack Valentine: “I know exactly what the boy needs.” The boys looks at Jack and Johnny, sliding down in his chair in fear. Jack & Johnny: “A spanking.” Bell Boy: (Screaming in interruption) “NO! I’m sorry...actually, no I’m not. You faggots suck!” Jack and Johnny smirk. Bell Boy: “You probably swallow too!” Jack and Johnny then look on as if they don’t know what the boy is talking about. Jack Valentine: “We do swallow...” Bell Boy: (With a look of disgust, yet eagerness on his face) “I knew it!” Jack Valentine: “We will swallow up our competiton this ‘coming’ Sunday.” Johnny Heart: “You said...” Jack Valentine: (Not letting Johnny finish) “You need to understand the PINK! young man. Here is a lollypop.” Jack hands the kid a PINK! sucker and the kid just tosses it aside. Bell Boy: (The kid’s face turns red out of anger) “You guys really suck! I hope Akiyama or Pretty Cool Guys lay waste to your faggot asses this Sunday because you stand no chance in hell!” Johnny Heart: (Patting the kid on the head) “It’s alright to live in denial...but one day you are going to need to accept what we want to give to you.” Bell Boy: “Ewww...Sick!” Jack Valentine: “We will give you the respect for ourselves and the PINK! that the majority of PCW fans have...with a few minor exceptions...” Johnny Heart: “...like Jeff Marx.” Jack Valentine: “And this Sunday, we will show all of our fans...” Johnny Heart: “...all of the higher ups...” Jack Valentine: “...and the other 8 teams just why it’s all about...” Johnny Heart: “THE PINK!” Jack and Johnny pick up the sucker and hand it back to the boy before they walk away, heading back to their room at the hotel near the Rupp Arena. The biggest match is only 24 hours away...better rest up. ...TO BE CONTINUED...
