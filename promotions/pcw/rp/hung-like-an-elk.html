Hung Like An Elk

Date: 18.12.02 (#2 FF) “I’ve been runnin’ down the road tryin’ to loosen my load, I got seven women on my mind….” Abner blasts up The Eagle’s “Take It Easy” on his radio. Speeding down the highway in his old, beat-up pickup, Abner’s thankful to get away for a while. Away from the city it is. After leaving Mexico, and for a short while, taking a break in a quite area of Texas, Abner began to feel less “homesick”. Quite content with his new life, and pleased with his career thus far in Primetime Championship Wrestling, he has no complaints. There’s no reason to go home, and he’s probably better off on the road anyway. “Hot Damn!” Abnner swerves to and fro across the open lanes of the deserted highway. Somewhere between the rolling hills of Montana, and the snow-covered peaks of Canada, the 1986, red Ford F-150 is as free as it has ever been. So taking his time on his way from the Southern hills of Georgia to North York, Ontario, Canada, Abner has no reason to be in rush. Why his last contact with any formal acquaintances was Duke Williams and Teresa Tomas at her Christmas party earlier on this week. But now, that’s over. And so with his hangover-induced headache gone, and his four-wheel drive locked into place, Abner hits the open road. “Yee-haw!” Only stopping to rest (and for the occasional drink of course), he’s covered quite a few miles in so little time. With the perfect timing, he should arrive at the Amesbury Arena just six or seven hours before his match begins. Yes, PCW would pay for the plane ticket. Yes, Holiday Inn would provide him with free boarding. And yes, The End would certainly buy him a few more twelve packs. But as independent as he is, this ol’ redneck just wants some time alone, and to tend to his own wounds. “ Yep,” Abner begins to mumble to himself, “Duzza and Khadiffi in a handicap match. Ye sir, come Friday Fusion I’d best have mah shit together. Ain’t too long ‘fore I be getting’ my ass whupped! See, friendly as the bottle be, ain’t too good during the match.” And then he smiles. “But before and after…..well, that’s a whole notha story…..” “What the!?.....” Slamming on his breaks, Abner swerves the ol’ bird to the left, narrowly avoiding the object in his path. “Well I’ll be…...” Abner slows the truck down, puts on the e-brake, and takes the keys from the ignition, shutting down the Ford’s worn engine. As soon as it comes to halt, Abner (seatbeltless of course) throws open the door and hops out onto the road. “Jesus Christ!” And there, standing in front of him is the massive figure of a fully-grown, fat male Elk. “Horseshit-in-a-can! Well whack my pecker and pepper mah pickle……” Abner’s super-charged hick senses take control, and immediately, he goes into action. “If that ain’t the biggest Goddamn buck I ever seen…..then……” He reaches up and scratches his head. “Ah fuck it! Where’s mah gun?” Abner rushes back to his truck and hops into the back, frantically searching for his hunting rifle, and all the while keeping a steady eye on the seemingly un-intimidated Elk.“Shit!” And then he realizes that it’s not the early ‘90’s, he’s not in the green woods of Vermont, and Hell, he hasn’t even hunted in over five years! And that’s not including the minor detail that due to his felony, all privileges to bare firearms were stripped from him. “Son-va-whore!” Denting the side of his truck, Abner bangs his fists in disgust. “Ah well, guess I’ll have to buy mah venison this year…..” Shrugging, he hops out of the truck and heads back up toward the beast. Standing only a few mere feet away, Abner is bewildered by the balls of this beast……no really, he is. You see, it’s the rut. Mating season you know, and this Elk, well, it’s hung like a horse….er…..um, an Elk to be exact. But needless to say, it’s quite scary. A the bulging figure of an Elk, and of an Elk’s penis, is not what Abner wants to see. So slowly backing away, Abner decides that it’s best to just continue on down the road. But there’s a slight problem, you see, the Elk is kind of in his way. The large brown figure, stretching lengthwise across the road, allows little room for the bulking figure of his big, beat-up pickup. So turning back towards his rig, he decides to try and give it scare. “Goddamn freakin Elk……I’ll show him who’s the fuckin Boss of the Hoss! He won’t move, well, I reckon I’ll move ‘em mahself then…..” But suddenly, just as he turns, the Elk takes note of this peculiar little thing’s actions. Raising up his tail, and gently scraping his hooves, the Bull slowly advances toward the hick. Lowering his head, the Elk shows the massive two-foot span of his thick, spiky antlers. Prancing with confidence toward the foreign object, Abner doesn’t quite know what to do. “Uh…..hey, you!” The Elk lets out a snort. “Yeah, you!” And then twisting it’s head in indecision, the Elk comes to stop. “Yeah, that’s right! Git! Yep, get on home you little ol’ reindeer. Don’t make me come ova there and make your face just as red as ol’ Rudolph’s nose!” But it’s not the Elk’s nose that’s red. Suddenly, to Abner’s dismay, the full erectile length of the creatures privates comes into view. Filling the redneck’s view, this Elk’s enormous sex organ seems to engulf his “safety zone”. “Shit!” Abner shouts out in dismay, “Haven’t been in this situation since prison!” “BAHH!~” The Elk shrieks out in delight. “Uh-oh!” And so quickly trying to escape, Abner turns his backside and sprints away from the threat. But this is just what it wants. Quickly out maneuvering the man, the mammal mounts the redneck just feet short of his truck. And so with his means of escape so close, and yet so far away, Abner succumbs to his fate. “BAHH!~” Thrust after thrust of love jabbing by the Elk, Abner is forced to fall to his knees. “Holy hippies n’ horseshit!” And only but a moment or two later, the Elk explodes. Ounce after ounce of icky, slimy Elk sperm oozes down Abner’s back. Covering his shirt and engulfing his beltline, the thick white liquid serves as a symbolic mark of the Elk’s defiance. And then it’s over. Before Abner knows it, he slowly opens his eyes, first the left, and the cautiously the right, and then solemnly gets to his feet. Taking his time, Abner painfully drags his two-hundred pound carcass, and what’s left of his bruised ego, back to the truck. His shirt stained, and his pride washed in the wetness of Elk-libido product, the sad hick starts the truck. The Elk, nowhere to be found, must’ve have run off into the woods. “Goddamn”, Abner thinks to himself, “I know Khadiffi and Duzza are a little weird…..but if they ride me like that……I mean, a man can only take so much….” He lets out a sigh. “….But ah well, I am from Vermont…..”
