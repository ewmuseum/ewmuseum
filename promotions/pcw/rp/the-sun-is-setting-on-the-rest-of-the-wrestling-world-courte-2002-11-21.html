The Sun is Setting on the Rest of the Wrestling World, Courtest of Mob Rule

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/21/02 Tyler Nelson – 2 time former PCW World Champion; former PCW Television Champion; 2 time former PCW Rising Star Champion; former CEO, Commissioner, President, and owner of PCW Angelo Deville – reigning FSW Primetime Champion; reigning WT Universal Champion Timo Bolamba – current FSW World Champion; former PCW Television Champion; former PCW Rising Star Champion; prior Alliances winner Mikey Marvelous – former FSW World Champion; former FSW Intercontinental Champion; former FSW Tag Champion; former FSW Primetime Champion; former PCW Television Champion; FSW Hall of Famer Separately those credentials are enough to inspire awe in any wrestling fan or enthusiast in the world. Lump them all together into one team and you have the makings for something almost God-like. Four of the greatest wrestlers to ever lace up a pair of boots all gathered on the same team under a common banner. If only they got along. Four men, two different philosophies, one common goal…..win Alliances. A feat much easier said than done. Nelson and Deville hate Bolamba and Marvelous. The feeling is mutual going back the other way. These four would much rather tear each other limb from limb than fight alongside each other, except for one thing. Pride. To Nelson and Deville, Alliances is a chance for them to prove the dominance and sheer might of Mob Rule. They plan to have the wrestling world bow before them and surrender to their will. Alliances will give them the opportunity to make Mob Rule the all encompassing symbol of power in the world of professional wrestling. Pride translates into ego, and their ego’s are larger than life. On the other hand there are Bolamba and Marvelous, two men who have been the best of friends and the most bitter of enemies. Currently fighting on the same side, these two have a differing idea of pride. They fight in Alliances to represent their federation, Five Star Wrestling. They do battle with the best in the world to prove their medal against the stiffest of competition. They fight the good fight, trying to raise up the name of their home above all others. From pride springs respect, but for Nelson and Deville they have none. Therein lies the quandary…..with agenda’s so different how can they possibly work together long enough to achieve the one goal? We will find out in four short days. The picture fades in with an overhead view of a remote highway somewhere in Texas. The roadside and surrounding area is desolate, with more dust and dirt than grass to be found. As we skim over the blacktop pavement, zooming along at a reasonably high rate of speed, we come upon a black stretch limousine. The angle of the camera lowers, allowing us to see the beautiful sunset that the limo is driving into. The mystery as to who is inside the vehicle is quickly solved as the Texas plates read only a single word…. GREED. As Tyler Nelson travels inside the limo, along with Orpheus Grant and Mr. Simmons, he gazes out the window and ponders the days ahead of him. Mob Rule is becoming more and more dominant by the day. They have the opportunity to gain control of Cassius Deamont’s contract this coming Glory, effectively removing him from the opposition force spearheaded by his Alliances teammates Timo Bolamba and Mike Marvelous. Tyler is beating Timo at the mental game of chess, nearly having him in check-mate. As The Only Six Star Wrestler in Five Star Wrestling as he likes to call himself, Tyler Nelson is making yet another name for himself. But what of the past? That’s what Tyler thinks about as he watches the barren land pass by on the other side of the heavily tinted glass. He thinks about all the things he accomplished in Primetime Championship Wrestling. He thinks about the days of ultimate control, of world championships, and complete dominance. He remembers the battles with enemies such as Ivan Stanislav, Xavier Kannon, Kid Wonder, Reverend Jon Hull, and The White Mexican. Those memories which had been pushed to the back of his mind Tyler now recalls, the impending date with that past close at hand. He mumbles to himself as his forehead presses against the cold glass window. Tyler: Would we get in trouble if we did actually kill them all with a gatling gun? This is Texas after all. The question goes unanswered as the highway continues to be a blur underneath the limo. Time passes by slowly, with the inhabitants of the limo keeping a eerie silence. Nelson stares out the window, Orpheus sits in the seat across from DA BOSS~! and bobs his head to the beat coming from his headphones, and Mr. Simmons just sits there staring straight ahead. His eyes are hidden behind his standard issue security guard sunglasses. Suddenly the trance-like atmosphere is disrupted as the limo begins to decelerate. Tyler perks his head up and sits upright in his seat. Tyler: What the hell’s going on? We can’t possibly be there yet. Nelson reaches over and presses down a button on the limo’s door. The tinted glass partition separating the back of the limo from the driver buzzes to life and slides down. Tyler cranes his head forward and shouts at the driver. Tyler: What the fuck do you think you are doing, Cal? We’re out in the middle of nowhere! The driver, PCW veteran reporter Cal Seaver, twists in the driver’s seat and makes eye contact with Tyler. Cal: Well, we have a small problem. Tyler points a finger at Cal and motions for him to turn around. Tyler: Yeah we do, don’t ever look me in the eyes again! I’ll drag you through that partition and beat the living shit out of you. Cal quickly turns back around and continues to navigate the limo as he talks. Cal: I have to take a piss. Orpheus slides the headphones from over his ears and gives a puzzled look to Tyler. Orpheus: Did that muthafucka jus’ say we was pullin’ over so he could piss? Nelson glares forward at Cal and barks an irritated command, ignoring OG’s question. Tyler: Get back on the fucking road! We’re behind schedule enough as it is without having to stop every five fucking miles for you to drain your baby-bladder. Cal: But- Tyler: GODDAMNIT CAL!! Grant butts in, a sympathetic tone in his voice. Orpheus: Damn, Ty…why don’tcha jus’ let tha lil’ cracka take a leak? Tyler spins his head around and glares at OG with a bit of shock present on his face. Tyler: What? Grant speaks up again, his deep voice seeming somewhat tame compared to it’s normal gruffness. Orpheus: Let ‘em piss, man. Ya don’ want tha muthafucka ta piss all over ya ride, do ya? Tyler: (miffed) That’s a moot point, because if he pissed in the limo he’d be washing it out with his tongue. Up front Cal shivers at the thought of having to spit polish his own urine from the front seat of the limo. Orpheus is steadfast in his defense of Cal, however. Orpheus: C’mon dog, let whitey up there pull over for a sec. It can’t take long wit his pencil dick anyhow. Tyler leers at Orpheus out of the corner of his eye, seeing that Grant is doing his best to feign concern and hide his discomfort. Nelson finally makes the connection. Tyler: Enough already with the bullshit. You have to piss, too, don’t you? Grant sighs and smirks. Orpheus: Like a fuckin’ racehorse. Tyler: Very well then. We’ll stop but you had better make it fast. We don’t have time to be wasting out here in the middle of nowhere. There are two gorgeous blondes warming my bed up as we speak and I would hate to keep them waiting because two grown men have no bladder control. Grant chuckles a bit and places the headphones back over his ears. Cal grins nervously as he spots a gas station up the road a bit. He guides the limo to the station and slowly pulls into the dirt paved lot. The limo comes to a sliding halt as Cal throws it into park. The car is barely stopped before Cal flings the driver’s door open and leaps out of the front seat. He makes a bee line for the side of the station, looking for the men’s room. Cal has a decent head start before Orpheus Grant steps out from inside the limo and casually takes off in the direction Cal went. We switch back inside the limo where Tyler and Mr. Simmons still sit. Nelson looks over at his security chief and speaks with annoyance in his voice. Tyler: Simmons….do you have to take a piss, too? If so you better go now or else you’re going to have to hold it the rest of the way back to Dallas. Mr. Simmons just sits there, facing straight ahead and unresponsive. Tyler gets a little perturbed and shifts in his seat to face Simmons a little more. Tyler: Hey! I’m talking to you! Do you have to piss? Still no reply from Simmons, which totally ticks Tyler off. He reaches over and snatches the sunglasses off of the security chief’s face. Tyler: I ASKED YOU A QUES- The sentence is halted as Nelson realizes that Mr. Simmons is sound asleep. A frustrated growl precedes Tyler throwing the sunglasses into the opposite seat and climbs out of the limo. A soft squish is heard as he plants his foot down on the dirt. Tyler: Son of a bitch! Nelson looks down and sees that he’s stepped right in a pile of shit. He curls his nose and quickly looks away from the disgusting scene. Up at the station, we can see the massive form of Orpheus Grant disappear around the side of the building where Cal went. It’s only a matter of seconds before Cal Seaver comes flying back into view, pants around his ankles, courtesy of O.G. Cal: AAAAAAAAAAHHHHHH……..OOMPH!!!!! He lands with an unceremonious thud, then quickly scrambles to pull his pants up. Back by the limo, Tyler scowls as he searches for ways to clean the shit from his shoes. Tyler: I can’t fucking believe this! We stop for those two jackasses to go and spank it in the bathroom and I step in a pile of shit! He holds his foot up off the ground, hopping around on the other as he does so. He spins around in a circle, still looking for a solution to his problem. Suddenly he gets an idea and sticks his foot inside the limousine door. He shimmies and shakes his foot in several different directions, wiping the fecal matter from his shoes and onto the sleeping Mr. Simmons’ pant legs. Tyler: About time the sorry son of a bitch made himself useful. I’d fire him again but I need an outlet for my excess anger. He finishes wiping the shit from his shoe and proudly admires the job he did. He then looks around the desolate area and then up at the service station. He sees Orpheus Grant heading back toward the limo and Cal re-entering the bathroom. He then looks over at the setting sun, just about to disappear behind the horizon. Tyler: Rather symbolic….the sun setting on another day, bringing darkness along with it. That’s exactly what will happen at Alliances when the Mob Rules. Tyler Nelson and Angelo Deville will reign supreme, eliminating all others en route to becoming the number one contenders for PCW titles. Hell, after we take care of Timo and Marvelous we’ll just arrange for Ming and Chase to get their title shots. After all, they would have rightfully been fighting alongside us had it not been for Timo and Mike fixing the drawing. The time has come to right the wrongs, to slaughter the weak, and to stake Mob Rule’s place in history as the single most dominant force the wrestling world has ever seen. The time has come for Tyler Nelson to return to the place he was so grievously exiled from and show the world why he is the Greediest Player in the Game. A sinister smirk comes across the lips of Nelson as O.G. arrives back at the limo. Tyler turns to him and points inside the vehicle. Tyler: Take that smelly bastard and throw him in the trunk. Orpheus: Ya sure ‘bout that, Ty? Nelson looks at his bodyguard with an expressionless face. Tyler: Did I stutter? Grant just shakes his head and does as he was told, scooping Simmons up and dumping him in the trunk of the limousine. Those two pile in the back as Cal runs full speed back to the limo. Tyler: Get the fuck in the car and let’s go! Mother fucker… Cal doesn’t say a word, but nearly dives into the drivers seat of the limo and turns the key over. He slams his door and the gas pedal at the same time, spinning the tires in the dirt and creating a enormous cloud behind the vehicle. The tires squeal a bit as they reach the pavement, but quickly grab the road and speed the limo off into the distance.
