The Fall of the CEO

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/01/02 What happens to you when you feel threatened? What happens when fear starts to take over your way of thinking? What happens when someone else tries to take what belongs to you? You do whatever it takes to protect yourself and what’s yours. Whatever it takes. You fight, claw, scratch....or even kill....to protect what you value most. To Tyler Nelson, greed is the most important thing in his life. It is what he values almost more than life itself. Greed is what makes him the man that he is, and greed is what gives him strength. He built an empire on greed, and even named said empire after it...Greed, INC. Greed is what fuels Tyler Nelson day in and day out, and now someone was trying to take that away from him. The White Mexican...perhaps unbeknownst to him, perhaps not....has started a chain of events that is snowballing out of control. It all started with his lawsuit claiming unjust termination from PCW. Once the investigation started into this, some people in the Department of Justice saw this as their opportunity to make a move on the greediest player in the game. Tyler Nelson had been on the top of many lists in the Department of Justice for some time now, as his business practices were widely known to be on the shady side of the street. The only thing was that they couldn’t get enough evidence to pin a parking ticket on him, let alone put together a federal case. No matter how hard they tried it seemed that the CEO of Greed, INC was always one step ahead. There were accusations of bribery, extortion, and blackmail of judges, governors, senators, representatives, and a bevy of other dignitaries from all parts of the political landscape. None of it, not one red cent, could ever be proven. For years this went on, the Department of Justice continued to go to bat against Tyler Nelson....and each time he struck them out without them even getting a swing at the ball. When Nelson bought Primetime Championship Wrestling in January of 2001 the D.o.J. thought they would catch their break. PCW was a high profile acquisition, and surely Nelson would slip up with the eyes of the world on him. For over a year they waited with their fingers crossed and breath held while Tyler made a mockery of them time after time. As Tyler’s empire grew by leaps and bounds, the Department of Justice’s hopes of stopping him shrunk to near nothing. That is until Mexican Moe Momar came walking into their life. A man scorned by the tyrannical CEO of PCW, White Mexican decided he was going to stick it to Tyler Nelson for humiliating him the way he did, firing him on national television. Once the Department of Justice got wind of this lawsuit they pounced on the opportunity it presented. This would give them unprecedented access to Tyler Nelson’s records due to the nature of the case. They would use the guise of researching past personnel records to delve into the financial records of both Greed, INC and PCW. Kind of an underhanded trick, but considering who they were dealing with it seemed fair game. While Mexi battled Tyler Nelson in the courtroom for his job back, the officers from the Department of Justice built a case against the CEO from the information they had gathered. Mexi won his case against Tyler and got his job back, but that was the least of Tyler’s worries. The CEO got wind of the investigation and tried to fend off the attack, but it was too late. Tyler tried in vain to destroy any records that may incriminate him, but they had already been copied by the Department of Justice as they conducted their secret investigation. In Tyler’s mind the blame for this whole debacle lies solely on the shoulders of one Mexican Moe Momar. If he didn’t file that lawsuit then there would have been no investigation, thus no D.o.J. involvement. This brings us to this weeks edition of Friday Fusion, where Tyler will face off against Mexi. Fusion, where Tyler will try to exact some semblance of revenge for what Mexi has set into motion. Fusion, where the CEO will show all why he is and always will be the greediest player in the game. Fusion, where someone may just have to die to make things right in the mind of the CEO. So I ask you again, what happens to you when you feel threatened? What happens when fear starts to take over your way of thinking? What happens when someone else tries to take what belongs to you? You do whatever it takes to protect yourself and what’s yours. Whatever it takes.
