Jackass?! Watch and Learn...

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3rd June Jackass...Hmm... I remember watching a Television show going by that name on the channel of MTV... a show where people would do really crazy assed things inorder to win over the masses of people and entertain all the viewers... bringing up their numbers in the Nielsens ratings. Now, why would somebody like myself begin to talk about this very show for? After all... the heros within this lattitude of time and space at PoP Icons... Boy Band to the max... the bad ass of bad asses... at least, they're that last thing when you compare them to people like... Justin Timberlake, Nick Carter and Bradley from 'S Club 7'. Well, for the show that will follow... the subject of Jackass has been brought to the world in the form of two very different people... Two very different people that walk this planet under the names of Michael and Chet Price... one would feel sorry for people that contract diseases... its not very nice... all those germs floating around your body... and to think, that our stunningly stunning superheros have to face them... TOMORROW night!? When somebody has a 'disease' that could be passed on through the mere touch of another person... you have to be worry about all of the consequences that could follow within time. You need to be aware... of all the pain that one could suffer... all the horror! THE HORROR GOD DAMN IT.... but alas, I'm ranting on about something that is merely mental... and not the 'T-Virus' that claimed so many lives in the dreaded 'Resident Evil' video games... The Pretty Cool Guys... are looking to win their second Tag Team match in 'Primetime Championship Wrestling'... and with that very idealistic aim under their belt... will they be able to defeat a team that spends their spare time.. looking around for crazy assed things to do? Once again, there is no more need for the sounds of the 'Dragonball Z' recap music... oh no, there is no worry for that entertainment... for in the words of a man that I met in China... 'Chi Yow Pong... Cha Ching Shing Ya Pow... Ey Cho Po...'... and in the words of the man seated next to him.. 'Ignore the words this crazy fuckhead is saying... he's not even quarter Chinese...' And now... let the show... BEGIN!!! | - ^ - ^ - | Chapter 3 : How much is that Title... in the window? Woof-Woof?!Installment II : The 'Jackass' parody... Tonight... we see the real deal in entertainment! Tonight... we see the stuff that has been able to captivate the lives of millions.. making them dribbling wrecks at the sight of it! Tonight... we see the powerful duo of Rich Sherwin and Ryan Moore... run through fire, water, brimstone... and anything else that can be used to emphasis pain! Some people would say that its not so nice to mock people for what they do... that its not essentially nice for people to run around taking the... piss... out of people so to speak because the only thing they'll become good for is throwing themselves from the top of buildings... or jumping infront of two ton trucks and getting up afterwards.... waving their arms around the air screaming.. 'Hey mom... I'm alive... I'M ALIVE!' before passing out and being taken to the nearest medical rehab centre. The Price Brothers... they are unfortunate enough to only be able to entertain the fans in this very method.. for one reason or another... they're only able to jump infront of cars to make the world go 'wow'... as for the boys, they only have to open their mouths... and purify the air with waves of sound that would bring tears to the eyes of every man for a three million mile radius. However, I'm pretty sure that as narrator of the piece, I have rambled on about this tragic dealing of information for far too long... henceforth, I will now stand back and allow the show to commence... 'Hey... my name is Rich Sherwin... and I'm going to be parodying Jackass...' The scene slowly began to open up in the confinements of a basement somewhere around the United States. It opens up at a remarkably slow pace due to the cheapest of quality here... although, it would probably cost more to make it seem as though the picture is opening in a cheap... degraded manner. Either way, the picture is finally able to open in the confinements of that same basement, Rich Sherwin standing alone in the middle of the shot... dressed up in some old clothes that make him look rather stupid.The whole setup was there at this point... just to show the world that he'd be doing something that required not only the absolute concentration... and mentality... and strength.. and artistic flare that followed with athletes like himself. Sherwin looked into the screen, seemingly at everyone watching the segment at home and decided to be shrewd, pulling off a few poses to show his muscles and what-not... bascially, showing off to whatever young chicks were sitting at home watching this shit... 'As you can see,' he began, looking all the more smug with how the show was starting to fall out after a efw moments infront of the camera. He looked from left to right before staring back into the screen and continuing with, '... I'm kitted out to show those Price Brothers the real meaning of this 'Jackass' stuff... you see, they've been diagnoised with some disease... some horrible illness that actually forces them to do crazy stuff. I intend... to not only show them off at their own game... but give myself a sense of immunisation for the task ahead at Turmoil...' Sherwin begins to pose again for another few moments before he turned around to face a large staircase leading upwards, probably to the ground level of whatever complex that we may be in at this moment in time. He began to walk ahead, beckoning the camera forth with his right hand. Nobody knew what kinds of lengths that Rich would go to when it came to preventing himself from gaining a disease of all sorts... especially one that there is no common cure to... or even a common data logging of it. He slowly began to walk up each of the steps... taking every one as if it could be his last. The scene begins to follow closely... getting a little closer to Sherwin each passing second. 'Now... I bet you're wondering why I'm climbing this staircase in such dramatic fashion...', Sherwin said out aloud while continuing up the staircase in something of a panic. The camera finally knocked Sherwin in the back, sending the elite Tag Team wrestler forwards, falling onto the steps in something of a heap. The rickity steps they were standing on shock violently... from side to side like a ladder bridge that was hanging across a burning pit of lava... or not. Sherwin turned over onto his back and looked into the screen with all the facial expression of a 'mad man'. 'You see that Price Brothers... that stunt there... was so intenese... so insane, that I feel myself... beginning to become immune to the ways of your stupidity.. I feel myself falling away from that feel of being unable to control myself... being unable to realize the rights and wrongs of this immature acting...', he informs the viewing audience before looking off into the darkness of the basement and raising his right hand into the air... 'I feel... a song coming along... yes, this moment of madness has brought forth a song to further make me immune ahead of this weeks wrestling contest... baby.. ohhoah... even in my heart, I see.. you're not being true to me.. deep within my soul, I feel... nothings like it used to be... you hear the sounds of the 'Backstreet Boys'... its awesome power beginning to.. Show me the meaning... of being lonely... is this the feeling, I need to walk with..', he continued... before turning back to the camera... and trying to keep his composure as a sane member of this world.. and not one that should be carted off by the men in white coats.A few seconds passed us by with ease, nothing seemed to be happening... and then the door to the basement at the top of these... rickity stairs swung open and a familiar face looked around from behind it. He stared down to the camera and then reverted his attention to Sherwin, who was still looking off into the distance of a wall... holding his right hand into the air... almost as if a scene from a famous play was being recreated. 'Sherwin...,' the voice began... coming down the narrow space in an amazingly loud tone. Rich snapped out of his apparent trance and looked up the stairs, the camera now following the moment of Rich and doing the same thing. The voice seemed to vanish for a few seconds... but came back strong a little while later with, '... I think you should come out onto the balcony and do that crazy stuff now... I've got people from all over the place... wanting to not only see 'Pretty Cool Guys' show their worth out of the ring against 'The Price Brothers'... but wanting to see exactly what kind of escapade we can pull off...' Now, this may have given you the buzz of hope at this moment of time... given you the feeling of light that would suggest our pathetic heros would actually attempt to jump infront of a bus... die.. and then never haunt your television sets ever again.... HOWEVER, would you really think that would be the case for a couple of geeks that simply adore the attention given to them from screaming 16yr old girls... even screaming 13yr old girls for that... do you really think they would do something as whacked as that? Well... lets just put it into real easy terms... Boy Band members are general pussies.... so, Boy Band members... doing crazy stuff thats really worthwhile... is a Big 'No-No'. However you choose to look at this small scenario thats beginning to unfold in the very safe quarters of the 'Pretty Cool Complex'... one thing is for sure... nothing drastic will happen... not even if you were to offer the lads a sum in the region of a couple of hundred thousand... or million dollars. As we get back to the scene at hand, Rich jumps up from his position on the floor and begins to sprint up the stairs at the understanding of news. The recording camera sees no other alternative but to allow the picture to fade out into the darkness of the night... Merely... SECONDS... later... The scene reopens outside the home of these two heros... outside in the front yard of their place of residence. Gathered in a small area... are people from all over the world... news station people from all over America... and of course, some of the very same 13yr old Girls that had turned up to see the seventy-five second performance of PCG at the mall... just a few short days ago or whatnot. Ryan Moore was standing infront of a small pile of crash mats... the cameras from the media all focused upon him. The younger member of the boy looks into all of the little eyes and begins to announce the main attraction of the day. 'People... People... People... my name is Ryan Moore... and what you're about to see... is something that no other man around the world would dare to attempt... you're all about to witness something that nobody within their minds would be able to do... and you're about to see something that only PoP Icons like Rich Sherwin and myself are able to attempt.... however, before we roll along with the show... I will be taking some... quick questions from the media...', Ryan called out in his usually cool voice. He looked around at all the members of the media with a smug look on his face, expecting them all to ask questions about the dates of the 'Pretty Cool Guys : Summer Tour' and the current status of their positions within 'Primetime Championship Wrestling... however, nothing of that kind happened from the media... instead, they all looked up to the roof of the building, patiently antipicating the 'Crazy Assed' thing that Rich Sherwin was about to do... However, with this locked in mind... the small girls that were waiting on the lawn with these media people... stepped forth and raised their hands... buzzing with preppiness and desire to ask the star a few questions about things... although, we could all pretty much guess what 13yr old girls would be asking somebody in the position of Ryan Moore. Moore scanned the girl fans and nodded at one... they took a step forwards and looked onwards before asking their desirable. 'Ryan... my mom says that girls like me need to find boyfriends... and she said that you'd be an excellent choice... will you go out with me?' she asked, in all seriousness that could be placed with the word. Ryan Moore stood still in one place and looked at the girl with absolute shock. He couldn't believe that somebody so tiny was trying to hit on him... and even try to get him to go out with her. Ryan looks worried.. but doesn't want to make the girl feel really bad... after all, a lost fan is a lost record.... and lost records mean that you lose money. 'Well... little girl... why I'd like to say that you look good in whatever you call clothes... I must confess, that you're just a little bit too young for me at the moment... maybe when you are a little older... say, about ten years or so...' Ryan had tried to let this little girl down slowly... but had been unable to really do so... she jumped in the air and started to scream... getting all excited about something thats never going to happen... ever, despite the fact that ten years was mentioned. Moore sighed loudly... and then remembered to press the secret button for the event to actually begin. 'OK Ladies and Gents!!!', he hollared from the sidelines before looking into the house and pressing the secret button... on a secret control... in a secret place. The media all looked up to the roof... expecting somebody in the form of Rich Sherwin to jump off and onto the crash mats below. They tremble with fear... expecting something big to happen... and something does happen... its just not as big as they would have hoped. Sherwin runs through the interior of the house and leaps through an open window on the first floor, crashing down onto the mats with ease.... the cameras, still looking up at the roof... expecting something to happen.Rich jumps up from his position from the floor and looks over to all of the cameras... smiling and flashing the 'Peace' sign as a symbol of supreme victory. All of the little girls run over to him and begin to hug the superstar... unable to believe the showing of bravery that came from such an icon of the PoP industry. 'NOW THAT... MY FRIENDS... IS HOW YOU REALLY DO THIS STUFF!', Sherwin yelled out at the top of his voice, catching the attention of all of the media people and making them point the cameras down toward him. The older member of PCG begins to laugh in the way that Mr Satan from Dragonball Z would do so. The media people lower their cameras and look at Sherwin with big goggle eyes... 'Where the hell did you come from?', one of the reporters exclaimed, unable to believe that Sherwin had made it down to the floor without actually being seen leaping from the roof of the household. Rich took a step back to his 'Partner in Crime'... with all the girls around him of course and whispered a question. 'You did tell... them the real focal point of this stunt didn't you?' 'Erm.... no..', was all that Ryan Moore could give as an answer at this very moment in time. The girls remained clinging to Sherwins legs as the media people all looked over to him. Sherwin smiles again and tries to force out another one of his now trademark poses... but nothing happens. Instead, the camera people start to pack up everything and walk off into the distance. The little girls realize the shamble that was the show and kick Sherwin in the shins before running off to their respective parents. 'Ryan...', Rich said... sounding a little more than just annoyed at his Tag team partner. Ryan looked over to Rich and tried to bring up a little smile that would have normally read...'Eh... Eh... sorry?' but the one being projected was sheepish... and very... scared. The scene began to fade into black as Ryan Moore ran off down the streets... Sherwin chasing him down the very road that they'd come upon... Will the boys be able to defeat 'The Price Brothers' on Turmoil and move forth in the Tag Team Round Robin Tournament? Will they be able to defeat everyone that gets in their way... and quite possibly, the biggest question known to the history of man... with the house of the 'Pretty Cool Guys' unlocked and free.... how much of their stuff will actually be in the same place when they get back?!?!?!? All this and more... NEXT TIME!!
