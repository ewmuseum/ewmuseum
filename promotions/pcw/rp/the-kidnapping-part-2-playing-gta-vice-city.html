Date: I haven&#039;t had a date yet, because I&#039;m a slob The sun caressed all the clouds up with a dim yellow light as morning broke through Dallas, Texas. Birds began to squeak in the sky and perched themselves among power lines and trees for a morning chatter. Of course, in the silence then, it was pretty evident this promo would be by Young Tiger, or Eddy simply because his recognizable voice mumbled something incoherently in the background and a cough was heard before he was visible wearing a black long sleeved shirt and blue jeans. His hair was never changed, a normal long hanged black hair and he looked merely different then regular. He was leaning on a brick wall of a house and examining his knuckles as if he was bored and had nothing truly important to do. Suddenly, a police car pulled in front of Eddy with a large white police men holding a box of donuts walking out from the driver’s side and another white man, not as big and not as short, but a little overweight himself. The 'larger' police man lifted a chocolate donut from the box and began to bite away. Eddy approached him with a blank look on his face. 'Well?', said Eddy. The donut holding police man swallowed his current donut bite and began. 'Nothing.' Eddy looked confused over this. 'Nothing at all?', Eddy said. The same police man nodded. 'Not a single thing, except this…' From his pocket, the large police man lifted a pipe, a small bag of dope and a few cigarette butts. Eddy sighed and turned slightly away. 'Well, I already KNEW she was on drugs.' 'Well, yeah, but it’s all we found', said the fat police man, munching away on his donut. Eddy turned as the same his the police man’s name tag and glimmered, showing 'Fred Baker' in engraved letters. Fred ate his donut, licked his fingers and looked very unprofessional as a police man. The other police man had rarely been visible since he was behind the other side of the car. He finally appeared and looked confused. The other police man had a big thick brown beard and brown hair that was neatly trimmed and was pretty short, unlike his beard. Eddy never caught an eye of his name tag simply because his never stood straightforward to Eddy but always sideways, turned, or had his arms crossed across his chest where you couldn’t see. Eddy sighed. 'Listen, my sister is out there, somewhere. Probably high on drugs and heck, she’s probably being beaten or bruised to. I need to find her, she’s somewhere, and I’m hoping you guys have the duty to find her', Eddy said. But neither one of the cops seemed to even care and were opening the doors and about to drive off. Eddy wanted to say something else, but decided not to and let them off. Eddy turned and kicked a stone into the grass, with a audible thump. The police car started and drove off. Eddy almost was sure the cops would do nothing about this and he would probably have to take matters in his own hands. Eddy perched a seat on the door step wondering exactly what he was going to do. Then Eddy had a thought. 'Well, I should relax a little, take a break from this hardship and do something fun for a change…Maybe I should go down to Blockbuster and rent a movie or a game? Hmm, sounds good….', said Eddy. He begin to walk to his truck glimmering red in the sunlight and stepped in. The put in the keys, turned the ignition, but the car never really got started because it was completely empty on gas. 'Argh! No gas! I guess I have to take a bus…I should just walk. Probably need to get ready for my upcoming match against Juan Borro, er, I mean El Romantico…' Eddy started to stroll down the road. It was a pretty nice, decent day. A little on the chilly side, but not too uncomfortable. Blockbuster was a ways out but Eddy didn’t mind, he just decided to go along with it. A little while later… 'Hmm, what should I get…', mumbled Eddy looking through the games section. 'What is this? Britney Spears Dance Beat? No thanks', said Eddy scanning the aisle. 'Hmm, Madden 2003. Maybe that will be cool?', said Eddy lifting it to his face. 'Yeah, this…' Eddy picked up the game and walked up the front counter with the game in his left hand. The clerk was a passed out 20 year out long brown hair kid with drool going down his lip. He was leaned in his seat asleep as if he was drunk off his ass. Eddy tried to be nice. 'Sir, are you awake?', Eddy said softly but the boy still asleep. Eddy started off louder. 'Sir!' The boy however was still asleep. 'Sigh…', said Eddy, as he lifted cash from his pocket and sat it in front of him, then he got a video game box opener and opened off the protecting plastic shield, then scanned the box and walked out, hoping he did do what was right. He put the game in his brown backpack and started walking back to his home… Later… 'This isn’t Madden 2003! This is…' Eddy pressed the X button on his PS2 and up came 'Grand Theft Auto: Vice City.' 'What?!!! These are the kind of games I fight against for there indecent public exposure to the youth!' Of course, Eddy has to eat his words because a second later he was tapping away frantically on the buttons as if tomorrow didn’t exist. He was on the streets as his character running around. 'I wonder…What does the O button do?' Eddy pressed it and it sent a punch flying right into the jaw of a cop standing in front of him. Eddy had the 'Whoops' look on his face. 'Uh oh…Why is there a star lighted up at the top of the screen?' The police pulled a gun and pointed it at Eddy’s character, which sent him laying on his back, which made 'Busted' come up. 'What???? It was an accident Mr. Police officer! I apologize…Oh no, I’ve lost the game!' However, he didn’t. Eddy was back at the police station steps. He sighed, then started walking off. 'What does triangle button do?' Eddy stood next to a car and pressed it, when suddenly the door popped in and the character jumped in. 'I stole a car! That’s not right, and very unlawful! Hey cool, I can drive off without getting caught…' Eddy zoomed forward with his car on full speed, crashing into cars and running red lights. Then, he ran over an old lady with a squish sound. 'Oh no! I ran over someone!' Eddy made the character get out of his car and walk over to the squished old lady which was a pile of blood. Eddy wanted to take this old lady to the hospital, so he pressed O to tried to pick her up. However, he kicked her. 'How awful! How disrespectful! I’d do anything to take this back ma’am, I’m sorry!', screamed Eddy at the screen wildly not knowing what to do. He pressed O again and kept on kicking her, which suddenly he saw a some money come out. 'What?' Eddy walked through the money and he got the money. He froze. 'I took a dead old lady’s money! This horrible! Hey look, an ambulance!', said Eddy noticing an ambulance on the road. He walked over to it, pressed triangle and jumped in, and started zooming over to the old lady. 'Don’t worry ma’am! I’ll help you!' Eddy zoomed over and ran over the old lady again, before getting out. Suddenly, the body was gone and all that remained was blood. Eddy almost cried over his pain of killing the lady and taking her money. He ran off from the scene like a pussy over to another red, small car and stole it. 'Raining Blood' by Slayer was on the radio. RAINING BLOOD! FROM A LACERATE (OOC: I’m not sure if it’s lacerate or something else, so please don’t rape me to death…) IN THE SKY! Eddy started to drive off and tried to be careful about his driving. Yet he couldn’t do well as he crashed into walls, other cars, people, poles, everything imaginable. Suddenly, his car was on fire. 'MY CAR’S ON FIRE?!!! What should I do?' Eddy was confused, then suddenly the car blew up into a million pieces in front of Eddy. 'Wasted' came up on the screen. 'Oh no! I died!' Eddy stared confused at the screen not sure exactly how to feel. Then he appeared in front of a hospital completely healed. Eddy sighed. 'I’m not sure how you car undie, but OK….' Eddy decided to get off from the game awhile and go eat somewhere. He got out, then forgot he still didn’t have no gas. 'Well, no gas again…' Eddy walked outside the cool air and decided to walk to Subway. On his way, he thought for a moment about his match he had heard for the Cruiserweight title against El Romantico. 'Well, it’s seems I’m against Juan Borro, er, El Romantico. The Cruiserweight Title too! I’m so excited, it’ll be my first title if I win! I hope I do, it would be great to be an influence on the youth of America and many other countries, and winning the title will make everybody have good thoughts of me. I just hope I can get around El Romantico, it would be an honor to be a title holder of PCW', said Eddy, walking along. 'I’m at Subway now, I guess I’m going to eat…' Eddy opened the door, with one of those little ringing bells as he opened as the camera man turned away, but fell down as the camera broke down the middle, then the screen faded…. Into static. 'Dammit, I broke the fucking camera!' was heard in the static. 'I’m going to lose my job!'
