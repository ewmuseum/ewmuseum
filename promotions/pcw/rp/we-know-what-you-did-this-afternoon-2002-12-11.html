We Know What You Did This Afternoon

11 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-11-02 RP 2 Fusion 13th Twenty-eight, twenty-nine, thirty. Teresa sits up. Her arms crossed over her chest. The setting is an at-home gym. The time is 10pm and she is alone. She stands from the padded bench and grabs a towel. The past two hours has been spent working out and now she’s going to take a break before finishing up. You about done Teresa? I don’t want to rush you, but 5a.m. comes early and I have to be leaving first thing in the morning. A young man stands at the doorway. He stands a few inches taller than she and wears his shoulder-length hair down and wet. A thick towel is wrapped around his torso, evidence he just stepped out of the shower. The towel is black with the letters ‘NSW’ sewn down the side. He has been watching her for the past few minutes or so wishing he didn’t have to leave. The past week she has spent with him in his Louisiana home. Together, they made more memories to take with them as they did both return to their jobs and away from each other. When their paths will cross again isn’t certain. When the time returns that they will meet, they will continue their relationship they have kept secret all these months. Teresa whipes perspiration from her face and meets Scott at the door. Suddenly, the doorbell rings. Who could be coming at this time of night? As Scott rushes upstairs to dress, Teresa heads to the door. When she opens however, no one is there. Draping the white towel over her shoulder, she bends down to pick up a white envelope. In black ink are written two simple words. WE KNOW What the hell? Teresa mumbles these words to herself and slowly shuts the door. Carefully, she rips open the top and finds a folded piece of paper. The paper is white with no markings on the outside. She unfolds it to reveal a picture. HOLY SHIT! SCOTT ADAM TAYLOR! GET YOUR ASS DOWN HERE NOW!! Seconds later, Scott runs down the staircase wearing black and silver pants. He meets her at the door and grabs the picture out of her hand. He holds a picture of him and Teresa when they went to Burger King for lunch. They were holding hands over the table and sharing a passionate moment. Slowly, he refolds the picture and hands it back to Teresa. It’ll be ok Teresa. Nobody of importance really cares anyway. Think of it this way, if that picture gets printed, it’d only give our fed more hype. Scott gives Teresa and reassuring smile and pulls her to him for a hug. She rests her head against his chest and takes in a deep breath as she controls the urge to punch a hole in the wall. Instead, the folded picture is crumbled into a wad and dropped on the floor. We are grown adults. This has been going off and on since I started PCW. We shouldn’t have to hide it. Teresa steps back and picks up the wadded picture. Without a word, she walks past Scott and tosses the paper in the fireplace. There is a small fire still burning and the paper quickly turns to ashes. She feels two strong arms wrap around her and his lips softly kissing her neck. Scott then whispers. Let’s go to bed. We can make each other forget about all this. We have something special between us and no one is going to take it away. The scene fades with our favorite couple walking upstairs and smoking ashes of the photo left burning in the fireplace.
