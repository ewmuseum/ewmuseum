Date: 12.26.02 | Any booboisie of white people; absent around here. Not an Eminem remake or a Nerezza: Dracula wannabe. This was the real dark streets. Lyrical healing blessing the kids, while the white had to be in by sun-down. Any Martin Luther King road, Martin Luther King himself wouldn't feel safe to live on, but Atlanta's; it was the mother land. The subfusc color inside the underground union known as Planet-Rap gave off an rich delicious feeling. Ancient graffiti, cracked and crumbled on it's texture by age. But that was later to come, all that was put on pause while Dante and I chilled across the street in my drop top red Cadillac. | 'U ready playa?' I continued, 'I mean those catz in there take no prisoners. If you don't come ya fullest, they'll eat yo ass alive.' I spoke meticulous to Dante about what he was in store for. 'I don't know... My style is wack as hell playa.' It was like Dante to castigate his own work when it came to flowing. I tried again to cogent Dante to the realization he might be good enough to battle these cats, 'You got da skillz. All you need to do iz kno' u do. Jus' go out there and show those wack MCs' they need to stop tryin', go home, and work on it. U feelin' where I'm comin' from?' 'Yea, you're right. Those MCs' couldn't hold a candle to my ass! Let's go do the shit.' Almost like a palindrome, which ever way you read it, it all meant Dante was ready. | As if Dante has doffed himself of nervousness that lead to fear and put a huge encumber on it; he opened his door and climbed out with much smooth vim. Slightly grinning successfully, I lowered my hydraulics, leveling my car to the ground, and followed across the slick wet street to the entrance of Planet-Rap. As we inclosed on the structure, the music got more and more orotund. Before we got all the way there, we stopped to let a car go back, then continued our journy. | 'Don't you have to train for that match with Nerezza this Sunday-' Badinage in tone, knowing Nerezza wasn't a threat to the least. The name Nerezza was a cacophony to my hears, 'Don't mention his name again, I feel nauseous and depressed jus' thinkin' about havin' to whoop his motha fuckin' pale ass.' | Dante laughed as the propinquity between us and Planet-Rap increased to hundred percent. At the door, we flashed our passes, passed the word, and like a verb, the bouncer's action was to let us in. We exchanged a pound(dap) with our people at the door, then headed to the back. As soon as we entered, nothing but my second oxygen (weed smoke) engulfed us and attacked our lungs. One would cough at first, but I inhaled it like fresh air and let it ooze from my mouth. My eyes quickly turned blood shot, as Dante walked off to get ready. In the mean time, I soon went to go spot the competition. | 'IN THIS CORNER, WE GOT THE CHALLENGER, THE DON!!!' The announcer, and rap-ref, yelled over the ballistic crowd with much vim in his tone. Then segued, 'AND IN THE OTHER CORNER, WE GOT THE CHAMPION, Bar-Buryin!!!' Speaking more on the level to the two battlers. 'Ok, ya'll more got two minutes to bring all you got to the table.' This time to the DJ, 'Spin da beat playa.' *DJ plays beat as The Don steps up on the mic* 'Yo...' AKJDKLSJFSLDKJFSLFKJ' Going on and it, it all seem to mold together into a bunch of jibberish.
