Back to Basics Pt II: Preminition of Death

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05th April 2003 And so the time had passed. Jade had taken it unto herself to take back what is hers rightfully and entirely illegally. Her life was plagued by the misery she calls Mani. Family perhaps, but who ever said that you can choose your family? It is a damned shame too because he would not even be on the same planet as her if she had the choice; Alas she did not and so she was lumbered with ridding his sorry ass from every bodies lives. On the way she would enjoy the ride a whole lot better if she got the opportunity to beat the snot out of the slime ball she hired who goes by the name of Damien, that two faced rat was going to pay for what he did without a doubt. The rain splashes down from the heavens by the bucket load. The night was as dark as ever and there is a creepy amount of mist lying at the bottom of the alley; it lies interrupted by heavy footsteps created by a pair of loose modifies black army boots which trace up to the thin Hispanic coloured knees. The rest of the feminine legs are slapped gently by the bottom of a leather over coat. With the female body is hugged tightly together by a white suede sleeveless cat suit cut off at the thighs. The body belongs to Jade of course; worry fills her eyes as the rain bounces off her now dishevelled brown and white hair. “Home sweet home.” Her voice sounds discouraged as the toe of her black army boot allows her foot to swing at an angle. The club is back to the way it was. Lacking the fanciness on the exterior at least, without all the fixtures that Jade had spent literally thousands paying for. Jade was furious but then there was nothing fury will get her but a beaten ass. Jade walks over to the door and gently places a key in it. “I guess it is worth a try!” Jade places the golden effect key into the door and tries to turn the key. Jade tries to open the door but there is a noise from behind it causing her to hurtle into the other side of the alley. The muffled sounds Jade had heard from behind the door did in fact belong to two of the men supposed to be on post guarding the building from such attackers as our dear Jade. They each have a cigarette in their hand as they chatter amongst them selves. The last thing on their mind would be an attack from any outsider; after all who would be stupid enough to try that? Jade hovers above a puddle with a concentrated look upon her slender face. Jade is just about to scramble towards the men at the front door when a bright light shines right down the alleyway. Jade scuttles back into the corner and pulls a black bin liner over her to stop anyone seeing her. There is an army-esque jeep that has just pulled into the side street behind the club. Four identically dressed security men walk up to the men at the door and nod to each other. The four official looking men then make their way inside the building as Jade looks on. “Shit!” She curses under her breath, her legs raise slightly as she bends over behind a trash can. Her sight is partially blocked but enough is there to see the door. Jade jumps out of her skin as a rat screeches down the alleyway; one of the men pulls out a sawn off shot gun and blows the remains of the rat against the wall. Jade flinches and looks around, constantly keeping an eye on her surroundings. Jade was assessing her situation, running it over and over in her mind. If she was gonna stand a hope in hell of getting anywhere with this she would need a rough estimate of how many people were in the building. All she knew right now was that there were two ‘guards’ standing between her and the doorway, that and there were four men now in the club. All Jade was here for was that son of a bitch Mani, the one with the similar bloodline; the one who was infesting her club with low lives and sucking the energy she put in it right away from her. The disappointment was boiling inside of her creating something much more sinister and intense: Rage. The heavy metal door swung back on its hinges again and slammed against the steel posts. There, stood in the doorway, was Mani. Her apparently much richer cousin stood in the doorway cockily; the baldness in his head reflected the light from the porch and created a most Maniacal picture in her mind, like a beast he stepped into the alleyway without a care in the world. Jade had to restrain herself to prevent herself from running into the alleyway and ripping his head off before he even got into the jeep. She gritted her teeth and forced her chin into her chest, the absolute strain it put on her to not run out there was immense, and the temptation to spill his blood over his ice cream white suit was something she could no longer resist. Her white eye almost seemed to glow in the dark as her body rose. In her hand was a steel bar, once leading into the gutter and filthy with decomposing trash and excreted faeces. That was the last thing which was on her mind was the shit melting away in her hand. Her eye began to glow like the sun as the man; her target turned his head and was met with a boot to the face, blood spilt from his mouth as she wasted no time in following up with a harsh taste of the bar around his oral cavity, blood spilling from his mouth as the men which surrounded him unload a full barrage of bullets into Jade, Mani was now on the floor trying to avoid the mess which was surrounding him. The blood from his cousin stained his clothes as he was left vomiting on the floor. The rattling from the bar echoed in the night as Jade fell to the ground…dead. -[Fade]- “Here lies a great woman.” The scene opens in a small graveyard. A large Oak coffin sits in the middle of a grave hoisted, almost floating waiting for the service to end. “Who lived and thought for better things, for excitement, she died on the search for this excitement, and I doubt she actually cared that she did it while searching.” Lacuna stood clad in a black outfit, a large black hat and a book in her black sleeved hand. Lacuna was comforted by the smaller Carys as they look down on the grave. Tears clouding their eyes as they look down. Both drop a white and red rose respectively and begin to walk away. Jade looks on in the scene the graveyard looking slightly transparent. Her hair was white, her eyes were white and she looks at the scene surrounding her. Her legs trudge along as she catches a sight of a large bald creature walking subtly behind Lacuna and Carys. A knife is brandished in his hand as it plummets into Carys. It is Mani, that bastard Jade runs and dives at Mani her body going straight through his torso. Jade looks up and tries to kick him in the head before the knife pierces into Lacuna. “Join your friends you filthy little bitches!” Mani cursed as Jade screams looking at her hands, before flashing back to the inscription on the tombstone. ‘Jade’ -[To Be Continued]-
