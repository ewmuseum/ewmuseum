The Diary of Dorn Tracer - Part 4: 4-12-03

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Dorn Himself Written 4-12-03, Quite a spectactular string of events has taken place, journal. In fact, they could very well change the foundation of PCW itself. A triumvirate of immense power has been formed; a group of three men taking charge of all that they see. Despite our differences, we have banded together for the common good and now we'll be able to take down any blasphemous force that stands against us! We hold many more cards than they believe, as well, for we are all champions. Titu is Cruiserweight Champion, Bruce is the Co-Continental Champion, and I am the Shooting Star Champion. We hold just one under half of ALL of PCW's active titles. That's a ton of territory covered, but that suits me just fine. But something about our alliance makes me uneasy, journal. Bruce and I got into a heated argument over who should be the ruling body of the triumvirate. He though he should be, while I suggested myself. We couldn't agree on anything besides the fact we didn't think Pharaoh could be either. A small void has already formed in our team, and I fear it may grow larger. Now don't get me wrong, journal, I have tons of faith in us. But even a small ripple can make a giant wave sooner or later. If these problems keep arising, an even bigger one may make itself apparent. We HAVE to set our differences aside, or they shall tear us apart. Even though I think Chtulu to be foolish and the concept of Ra to be LONG dead, their respective advocates are my allies; I cannot turn my back on them just because they're infidels. At least, not yet. But on the much brighter side of the moon, there's a lot in store for us. We're the only group in PCW right now, which gives us free reign to do as much damage as we want. Oh sure, they can call security or have us suspended... but that'd be suspending THREE champions, and you know how bad for business that would be? To put it bluntly, we're untouchable. And with that in mind, it's time to discuss possible matters for our enemies. Pharaoh seems to have Chet King well in order, if not totally dominated. Not much has been seen of that blabbermouth blasphemer, so one has to think that Pharaoh has things perfectly in control. Bruce and Skylar Green are still going at each other's throats constantly, with it coming to head at Spring Fling. And it's to crown the sole Continental Champion. I think Bruce has it won, but should things turn ugly... he can count on us to get involved. Jonathan Drachen is no longer of any concern to me. He seems to be hiding in a rabbit hole somewhere, doing his best to avoid me. That's perfectly fine, but I have a feeling he will show at Spring Fling. To ultimately meet his doom in our 'I Quit' match. I have conjured up quite a punishment for him... and if he survives it, then he will know EXACTLY what Dorn Tracer is all about. Other than that, journal, I can tell you nothing. Our plans are secretive, and I cannot garuntee you'll keep your mouth shut. So until then... Keep it real.
