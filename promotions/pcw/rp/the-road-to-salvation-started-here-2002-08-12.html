The Road To Salvation Started Here...

12 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/12/02 I am your salvation, Xavier. Give yourself unto me and I shall set you free. Things have certainly changed, haven’t they? Not so long ago Tyler Nelson was the most hated man in professional wrestling. A man not to be trusted. A man so power hungry that he was capable of doing anything at any time to get what he wanted. The wrecker of many a wrestling career, the CEO of PCW had no regrets for the wrongs he had done. Then it happened. Like a bolt of lightning it struck him….from out of nowhere and totally unexpected. In a flash of light his whole life was shown to him, from growing up in the middle class household in Dallas to the creation of Greed, INC to the destructive force that was Media and then on to the formation of the Ultimate Authority. All of the evil things he had done were bombarded upon him in a single instant, overloading him and flooring him in his office at PCW Headquarters. He lay there on the floor dazed for what seemed like an eternity. His eyes bugged open wide, unblinking and dried out. His mouth slightly agape he simply lay there, actually realizing what he had been doing his whole life was wrong. Could it be? As quick as it came the light was gone. The epiphany had begun. As he struggled to get to his feet, dizzy from his experience, thoughts rushed into his brain which had never even been whispered in its darkest corners before. Feelings barged into his heart which had never been allowed to exist. Remorse. Compassion. Regret. Sorrow for things he had done. It was all so sudden, this influx of feelings. Tyler had to sit down. He needed to regroup. He needed to think. He needed to get these thoughts and feelings out of his body. They didn’t belong there. They weren’t supposed to be there. He didn’t want them there. Or did he? As much as he tried he couldn’t drive them from inside himself. Emotion, which had been suppressed and exiled for the better part of 27 years, came pouring out all at once. He began to cry. The big and bad, uncaring, unfeeling, Greediest Player in the Game cried. What on Earth could make Tyler Nelson feel this way? He couldn’t explain it. Nor could he deny it. In the blink of an eye he had changed. So there the search began for a reason. The quest for the answer to the ever present question of why? Tyler gathered himself up and left the office. He was still in an enlightened daze as evidenced by him leaving his briefcase on the desk and all the lights on in the office. He wandered down the hall to the elevator and pressed in the ‘down’ button. His mind wandered to a million places in those short few minutes while he waited for the elevator to reach his floor. The ding nearly startled him as the doors slowly opened. “This is insane,” Tyler muttered to himself as he shuffled onto the elevator. Was it? Was a man so cold-hearted and cold-blooded as Tyler Nelson finally feeling remorse and regret for his actions insane? Or was it so totally real that it was unbelievable? As the elevator came to rest in the lobby the doors opened once again, allowing Tyler to step off and walk through the lavish lobby and out the front doors into the New Jersey night. It was so unmistakably different this time, though. He didn’t feel disgusted as he stepped into the New Jersey atmosphere. His stomach didn’t turn over and over at the smell of the Jersey air. He didn’t have the urge to vomit at the sight of the vagrants sleeping on the front steps of PCW Headquarters. As the CEO walked toward those steps, toward those vagrants, he found himself involuntarily digging in his front pocket and producing his money clip. He snapped the clip off the wad of bills and thumbed through them, all hundreds. Tyler looked down at the man laying at his feet, and elderly man of about 70 years old, wearing rags for clothes and dirt as a mask on his face. He was sleeping, newspapers acting as a blanket to shield him from the chilly New Jersey night. Without even thinking, Tyler knelt down and stuffed a bunch of money in the old vagrant’s shirt. The old man didn’t awaken, only rustled in his slumber. Tyler smiled as he stood back up, peering down at the homeless man. “That felt good. Strange, but good,” said Tyler as he stepped around the man and walked down the stairs. His limo waited for him, the driver standing at attention with the rear door held open, but the CEO ignored him and took off on the sidewalk. The clear night sky welcomed Tyler as he headed off to nowhere in particular. The blur in his mind continued to revolve at breakneck pace, shuffling his thoughts and feelings into one giant jumble. As he walked down the street he got a strange feeling in his gut, urging him to turn down a dark alley. He followed the urge, making his way into the darkness. The smell was foul. Garbage lined the alley which didn’t get much air circulation. The stench didn’t phase the CEO, however. He walked like he was looking for something, but didn’t know what it was. His eyes squinted as they adjusted to the lack of light. Suddenly Tyler was frozen in his tracks. His muscles were unresponsive as he ordered them to move. His body was not his anymore. Against his will the CEO of PCW was forced to his knees, head bowed in servitude. His teeth gritted together as he struggled against the force which inhibited his being. “What….is…..happening….to….me?” the CEO struggled to say. From out of the darkness came a cloaked figure, slowly making it’s way toward the kneeling Tyler. As much as he fought and struggled the CEO was helpless against the advancing figure. It stopped short of him, seeming to look down upon Tyler. It’s face could not be made out, in fact it looked to not have one at all. “Who….are…..you?” Tyler demanded wearily. His struggles to be free of what inhabited him had exhausted the CEO greatly. “You have been chosen,” boomed a loud voice seemingly from all around Tyler. “Chosen…for…what?” asked the weary CEO. “You have been chosen to be the one to save the sinners.” said the voice again. Tyler let that statement soak in for a few seconds. “Me……save sinners?” “The prophesy states one who has sinned much will be chosen to save all other sinners from themselves. You are the one chosen.” replied the figure. “I….I don’t…..understand….,” stammered Tyler, confused and still dazed from the happenings of earlier. “You will be provided an army of Dark Knights, sinners themselves, to aid you on your mission. In order to save all of the sinners you must save the biggest sinner of them all.” said the figure cryptically. “An army? Biggest sinner? Who is it?” wondered Tyler. “You will know.” And with that the cloaked figure retreated back into the darkness of the alley. As soon as he was gone Tyler’s body was freed if it’s invisible bonds and he collapsed to the ground. His exhausted body laid there for a long while, on the ground in the dark and disgusting alley. While his body was tired, his mind was still swimming from everything he had just endured. What would this night of paradox mean? With enlightenment came intrigue. With answers came more questions. With salvation came a mission to save. And so it began.
