Return of the Skitzo: Finding Fusion Part IIII - Fucking Queues and Self Harming!

15 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15-10-02 This place is not human… Skitzo walked along the platform of the station, trying to decipher the Japanese scrawled across the many signs and posts dotted here and there. The sky was beginning to darken, clouds floating high overhead showing signs of rainfall on the way. Skitzo had to think. Firstly, everything that had just happened was not real…but it felt like it had happened, not just in a dream. Skitzo realised his illness was capable of worse happenings than a simple personality change…things were happening that he couldn’t remember taking part in yet knew somehow…he had. The first few droplets of rain tapped against his face, and zipping up his coat, Skitzo left the hustle and bustle of the station, carrying the essentials for a week’s stay in Japan packed nicely in a backpack. Pushing past a group of students laughing and playing, Skitzo made for shelter as the rain began beating down hard, yet he barely acknowledged it as thoughts began flying through his warped mind, thoughts of his future, his career, how life would work for now on. Thinking then on his new career in PCW, Skitzo grinned at the prospects of being the sidekick for the almighty Goblin himself. A year had passed, Syko had gone places, and Skitzo had gone insane. The two had been reunited and together they had beaten down and pissed off Syko’s new foe – the Chef. Skitzo had spent 343 days in solitary confinement and now he was thrust into a battle with one of PCW’s best. The prospect of being sliced diced and served to Bertha sent Skitzo’s stomach churning with a sickening feel, and the feeling of being lost and alone only added to the sickness. Taking a seat on a common old bench, Skitzo glanced over to the Timetable as it showed the times between journeys. For a man who had learned many languages during his tenure in the pokey, Japanese had never appealed to him for reasons best left to themselves. The names in Japanese meant nothing, though the times did…and Skitzo started hunting for ‘Destination: Tokyo’ with the earliest possible time. His eyes lit up as at once he found a train, the right destination, and the place where he was! Skitzo: What the-…where the hell is Liyama?!?! Skitzo: The fucking train left ten minutes ago anyway! Skitzo: Next one isn’t for two hours… Skitzo: Il mio odio per questo posto è forte! Skitzo slumps back into the bench and sighs heavily. People walk past trying to ignore the bummed out wrestler, looking ever so slightly like a homeless man with his backpack of supplies sitting solemnly by his side. As he yawns, gazing at a clock hanging overhead reading 5:41pm, an old woman walks past, and places a coin on Skitzo’s lap, petting him on the head and walking on, happy having done her deed for the day. Skitzo: I don’t want’cha damned money, li’l ol’ whore! The woman turns back around to Skitzo, and waves at him with a nice little smile on her face, having no clue what he just said. Skitzo: Yeah I fucked yer maw too ya stupid old biddy! The woman replies with some inaudible phrase before taking off for a newly arriving train. Skitzo sighs and gets back up, heading for the ticket office. Struggling to push past all of the people, commuting, travelling, exploring, Skitzo barely manages to keep his head about him as he finally reaches the ticket office, and a massive queue. Skitzo: If Richard Branson ran these trains the queues would be so damn smaller… The few English people that are in the queue turn around and curse the American under their breath, while Skitzo smiles to himself. Standing in the queue for a few minutes seems like ages though, as each person buys their ticket and takes off for their train. Skitzo however becomes increasingly agitated as children and idiotic adults push past and generally get on his nerves. A schizophrenic in a queue full of frustration and anxiety isn’t a good thing, as shown by the rather large vein about to burst on Skitzo’s forehead. Skitzo: Gotta get out of this place… Skitzo: Must leave… Skitzo: Need ticket… Skitzo: Only one thing for it! … … … Skitzo: THERE’S A BOMB IN THE QUEUE!!! Skitzo closed his eyes and put his hands over his head, trying to listen for the thousands of people running away in terror…when he did open his eyes, about six people were looking at him in disgust. Skitzo: This is Japan, no one understands ‘BOMB’ here…idiot… Skitzo: Fuck you… Skitzo: I still maintain what I said…IDIOT… Skitzo: I’ll give you an idiot, YOU LITTLE-… Suddenly, Skitzo punches himself in the face and forces himself onto the ground. Grabbing his own neck, Skitzo throws himself up into the air, hitting the ground and punching himself in the face. The crowd around him filter out of the queue and surround him as he punches himself in the face again and sits up, slamming an elbow into his groin. Skitzo: I’LL FUCKING KILL YOOOUUU!!! Skitzo: Bring it pussy, you hit like a fucking democrat! Skitzo again pulls himself up, this time by the hair on the back of his head, and rams himself into a pole. Recoiling in pain, Skitzo grabs his nose, and then suddenly drives his left leg into his right and trips himself up, slamming his head into the ground. The crowd watching are getting panicky as Skitzo tries to punch himself in the face, only to successfully block it each time. Skitzo: A worthy opponent I see… Skitzo: I fuck your mother… Skitzo: My mother is your mother… Skitzo: I’LL KILL YOU FOR SAYING THAT!!!!!! DIIIIEEE!!! Skitzo suddenly grabs hold of his testicles and squeezes. The throbbing dull pain pulsates through his body as his hand squeezes harder. The people watching begin to shy away, running as the scene becomes too graphic for them. Skitzo tries with the other hand to release the grip, only to have it thrown away, and start punching his groin continuously. Skitzo: DO...YA...LIKE...THAT...HUH! HUH! DO YA!!! Skitzo: NO! Skitzo: Do ya give in?! Skitzo: NO! Skitzo: Then…TRY THIS ON FOR SIZE!!! Skitzo suddenly begins sliding across the floor, one hand pulling at his hair while he kicks and yells out, being dragged across the floor. The people watching have almost all left for another ticket office, and as Skitzo’s head sickeningly crunches against the ticket office he was waiting to get to…he suddenly stops fighting with himself and sighs. Skitzo: Well, it took a bit of convincing, but the fucking queue is gone…now to get my ticket! Skitzo: Indeed, sorry about the groin there old fellow! Skitzo: No problem, I had it tucked away just in case! Skitzo: That’s m’boy! Skitzo picks himself up and dusts himself off. Giggling evilly, he waltzes to the front desk and clears his throat. Skitzo: I would like a Single, One Way Ticket to Tokyo, please… The man behind the counter takes what seems like ten minutes to interpret the question, before coughing and spluttering mucus across the glass shield in front of his stall, then replying. Ticket man: You want ticket…he’e you’ ticket… The ticket man produces one ticket from the ticket box, and takes the correct change from the waiting hand of Skitzo. Taking his ticket, Skitzo turns to leave, but looks back at the man and winks mischievously. Skitzo: Sayonara, Skippy! With that, Skitzo sits back down on the aforementioned bench, one ticket up, 24¥ down, and in a whole lot of pain from his little stunt. Now all he had to do was wait the Hour and a Half left until the train arrived to keep himself busy…he had time to think about PWC, the situation with Syko and Chef, and the Friday Mall Brawl… what could a Schizophrenic do in a Mall Brawl? Oh the possibilities of taking so many names in one go. Skitzo is an army of one, whether he rises or falls though is yet to be seen…for now he just had to pass the time! To Be Continued now, y’hyear!
