Scarlet Letters...Finale

Date: Later... The lights dimmed as the soothing sopranos and the wavering tenors sounded from the speakers. The CEO dimmed the lights. The entire company that he ruled was built from the ground up by his hand alone. Like the hand of a god, he guided society down the path to technological prosperity. His angels, elite forces, would guard him throughout the night. There were so many that would wish to do him harm. There was one that outshined his forces once...he was his own personal guardian deity. How unstoppable he was... He was Akiyama... He sat at his desk, typing up memos and organizing information when he heard a loud thump at the door, interrupting the wave of violins weaved with the baritones. Thump. Tack, tack. Bang. The CEO looked up. The bodyguards would take care of it. Shouts were heard from outside of the door. 'Oh my god!!!' 'Run for your life!' The CEO looked up, face in a frown. The door hissed open. The CEO saw a familiar face. At first, he was astonished, but then he broke into a smile. 'How nice it is for you to....' His voice faltered. He saw the eyes, filled with rage and hatred. 'We have come for you,' the newcomer said in a smooth, clear voice that was just above a whisper. The sound of seven clarion trumpets played from the speakers in a dramatic cresendo. And CEO Misawa screamed. ~~~Akiyama 9:99 - Breaking of the Seal~~~ 'When the Lamb Broke Open the Seventh Seal, there was Silence in Heaven for about half an hour.'-Revelations 8:2- 'Injunction' The Angel that presided o'er our birthSaid, 'Little creature, formed of joy and mirth,Go, love, without the help of anything on earth.'-William Blake- Dear Yuu, We can see nothing, and feel nothing, except for one thing: the slippery, warm blood that is all over our hands. As we write this, our pen is shaking. The page is not white, but stained red. It is horrible. And we are scared. We thought we was scared a few weeks back, scared of humanity and the city, scared of the Paradise for the Cowards of the human race. But that was nothing compared to the heart-striking terror we feel now. Cyclonica tells us that we have no soul, but when we feel this pain inside our emotions, threatening every cell in our body, overwhelming all of our nerves, stabbing our heart with a pain not physical, but purely emotional, we know we have a soul. Only a soul can be vulnerable to such intense emotion. Cyclonica tells us that the soul is the weakness of the human race. The CEO of Digital Technologies, Tetsuo Misawa, is dead. When we approached his desk, surrouned by seven lampposts, the sword, our precious Saishukessen Ougii, left the sheath, parted the air, and slit open the chest of the man. He died just as we heard the clarion call of seven trumpets cry from the speakers above. We destroyed the first of society's god. God made no move as blood, his red, flowing blood, sprayed forth from the two halves of his body. The liquid that gives us life shot forth from the severed arteries and the slit heart. The blood flowed over our sword and dripped between our fingers. But neither the blood, nor the use of our sword, was the source of the fear. The fear comes from within. Inside, where Cyclonica inhabits. She believed herself to be in control, believed herself the only one inside, inside the self that we know as us. Within our heart, the beating heart of Akiyama, our conscience, our feelings, and our thoughts still lie, unable to be kept down. When we killed Misawa, our feelings and our conscience were not there. Only after the man died, and we escaped, did our feelings rush back to fill us. And our mind did not like what it saw. We do not know how we can live with ourselves. We thought we despised the CEO. We thought we would be doing good for the people. We thought we were going to be accepted. But it wasn't us doing the thinking. How could this possibly lead to good? Digital Tech may have done some things to harm society...and we used to one be his protector, but we did not even let him speak. We charged him and destroyed him. He was an unarmed man. He used to be our master. We so desperately wanted to be a good man! We wanted to love, and to hope, and to be like anyone else on the world! We wanted to return to society! We wanted to share our experiences with others, like the people in the city do. The city is a paradise for cowards who dare not live unprotected, but yet, we ran away even from there. It is no longer possible for us to return. Our hands are stained. Cyclonica stained our hands for us. We cannot forgive ourselves. Even though it is Cyclonica who tried, it was us who did the killing. It was us who were unable to resist the temptations she offered. We cannot forgive ourselves. We do not expect others to forgive us either. You can forgive us? No, you cannot. Revenge? Yes, we have received our revenge. Revenge is bitter. It is not sweet. We cry. We do not deserve to live. Our tears are mixed with the blood of the CEO. The clear water mixes with the crimson on our page. Our letter is bloodstained. We want to destroy ourselves. Our experiences are stained with the consequence of our actions. The world is not for us. And we cannot live. Our sword rises. We cannot do it. We are so weak! We cannot stand the thought of the pain. We thought we was strong. We were wrong. If we were strong, we would be able to tolerate ourselves, just like Grace tolerates us. We want to do good. But it's too late. So we should give up? We've worked so hard! We've killed the CEO. Yes, we know where it has gotten us. It has sent us nowhere. We are a nervous wreck. We cannot speak about it. We wanted to do goodness, but instead, we only feel the warm blood on our hands. We cannot get rid of it. We shake our head. It is hopeless. We want to die, but we cannot. We want to hide, but we cannot. We want to escape, but we cannot. We hear a voice. She tells us to remember. We have done all we can. It is not our business to change, for we have done everything we could. It is the business of others to change. It is the business that we send out our message through violence...Madness will be the perfect opportunity... Cyclonica is right. We have tried our hardest. Others must change, not us. We cannot forget the blood. But perhaps we can still try to do good. We want to make others change. We want to open the minds of humans. We want to be accepted. Our bloody sword is in its sheath. We must try to clean the weapon. The blood still scares us. But the blood on the letter will be there forever. How appropriate. The blood will forever stain our letter, just like darkness and death will forever stain our life. ** Akiyama **
