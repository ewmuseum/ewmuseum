A Sage Of A Champion Series Part 3

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-29-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion'The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!'Part 3 Chapter 2: The Battle BeginsDay 2: Part 1 of the battle against BattlePowers :: Scene from 2:: BladeD: Hello General. General: Well, Hello BladeD… Next time try knocking first. BladeD: Do I ever knock first? General: (Grins) That is the problem. BladeD: Anyway, We need to talk about BattlePowers for real this time. I am very serious when I say that I don’t like the idea of it. General: Well BladeD. I have told you time and time again that it is a good system that helps. BladeD: It gets people hurt for real and I don’t like that… It would be a better system if fake bullets or something of that sort were used. General: But we want the simulation to run in complete real time. Which even means that all the traps and bullets must be real. BladeD: Screw the real time General. It is getting REAL people hurt and I don’t like that idea. General: Well BladeD. I don’t know what else to tell you other than it is working right now and as long as no one dies BattlePowers will stay fully functional until farther notice. (The scene backs up from the conversation it was left up on.) BladeD: Well General. I am not going to get any killed in BattlePowers, but I have to find a way to get this thing shutdown. General: BladeD. I just don’t understand what your big deal is with BattlePowers. BladeD: You still don’t understand what my problem is? BattlePowers is a diabolical machine that is going to get people killed… That is what my problem is. It hasn’t gotten anyone killed yet, but it has gotten plenty of people hurt and I have watched a couple of BattlePowers. I know there is stuff done that people are going to get killed for there. There are areas of BattlePowers that some people can’t handle and once that happens they will die because they don’t have the skills yet to be able to avoid those dangers. General: I just think you are in denial BladeD because everything is running fine and you act like we are trying to kill the guys. We are just trying to make the simulation as real as possible. BladeD: Well I don’t want my little brother apart of this, because I don’t want him to have to take the risk of death. I don’t want him to be in the position I was in a year ago, because there is no chance that he could survive. He doesn’t have half the skills he needed to avoid death like I did. General: BladeD come on! Will you ever get over that? I mean it isn’t like you died. You are alive and that is all that matters. BladeD: How can you say that general? Have you lost your mind? I mean… I almost died out there and you want me to just let to leave my mind? I could never do something like that. That is the closest I have ever come to death and was in an environment that your men and you created… That is what makes me so mad and that is what makes me want to work so hard to make sure that the thing is put to rest. General: BladeD. I think you should stop worrying about it and just let us handle BattlePowers like we always have. Everything will turn out a lot more safe and better if you allow us to do so. BladeD: I would if I didn’t know the real dangers of the thing. General: BladeD. Give it up!!! It was one little mistake!! That was the first time and only time anything like that has happened in BattlePowers. BladeD: Well I feel it was setup. I feel your men set that up without telling you. They wanted to get rid of me and make it look like an accident. General: BladeD you are really losing your mind because my men would never do something like that to you. Bladed: Well General I think I haven’t lost my mind and I am going to find a way to prove that my close call to death was, in fact, setup for me. I am going to prove this, so I can get BattlePowers closed and not have to worry about my little brother going through what I had to go through. General: Well BladeD. You know what? You can go ahead and do whatever you want because at this point I really don’t care… You go ahead and you do whatever you can do prove that they “set you up,” but you aren’t going to get much information anyway, because you know we keep most of the information classified. BladeD: Well general. There are ways around everything, so I will make sure to get access to all the information that I need, so I am not going to worry about what information is “classified” and what information isn’t. Now if you would excuse me general I am going to leave you be and if you confront your men about… well call me and let me know what happens. General: BladeD I will excuse, but before you go I need to tell you this. Even if I did confront my men they wouldn’t tell me the truth until they were proven of what they have done. Until proven guilty they would act like nothing happen because that is the kind of men they are. BladeD: Well if those are the kind of men that you work with… How do you what they say and do is the truth? How can you believe every word they say if you don’t even know if they are telling you the truth or not? I mean. What has happens to you general? It is almost like you have completely changed as a person. A year ago you would have never had trouble telling me the truth or getting to the truth and you definitely would be working with men that you couldn’t trust half the time. Times have definitely changed in a year. That is for sure general. (BladeD gets out of his big black leather chair and walks toward the door in front of home. Before he opens the door he looks back and he sees the general with his head down writing something. BladeD proceeds out of the door and go down the hall-way taking two rights and gets on the elevator, then takes that elevator to the bottom floor. Once in the bottom floor BladeD walks out of the building and gets into that big yellow car that everyone loves… the taxi! From the taxi BladeD gets home. While on the road BladeD watches all the streetlights and different cars past by while thinking of everything that has gone on wishing it was easier than it is. This could be BladeD’s biggest challenge yet. BladeD has been through some tough challenges that the normal person could never survive, but this is probably the biggest challenge…. We leave you with that scene.)
