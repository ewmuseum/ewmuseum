N.I.G.G.A Meets Wallstreet

7 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/07/02 “Complete and utter bullshit.” We hear the angry, yet solemn voice of the PCW’s Civil Rights Hero, Jordan X Shabazz. The scene opens up as we are outside in what appears to be the streets of New York. Jordan X Shabazz is seen, dressed to the nines, wearing a dark black suit, and a black top hat. The camera pans back as we see Thug roC and Rush Mason with Jordan, also both dressed in their finest. They are waiting for a taxi in the smoldering New York heat. The fact that people are rushing to work and hurrying around the streets doesn’t help them. We see Jordan raises his arm out, desperately trying to hail a cab, but he finds that most cabs are already occupied by people going to work, or wherever New Yorkers go in the mornings. Jordan is obviously getting fed up, Rush Mason is off in a different world reading about some Voodoo God. Thug roC is writing in a notepad, taking notes on what he should have on roC TV. Jordan sweats and looks out into the see of humanity running around. Jordan X Shabazz: “Typical, corporate white America.” Rush looks up at his angry friend. Rush Mason: “Jordan, what the hell are you talking about? We’re going to the heart of corporate white America today, and on by your request.” Rush was right, for once. Jordan X Shabazz had invited Thug roC and Rush Mason to be his guests at the unveiling of N.I.G.G.A on the New York Stock Exchange. They were going to go there and open up the trading day. Jordan had been invited to ring the opening bell. So naturally, he brought his two buddies, Rush Mason and Thug roC with him. He apparently was very excited about getting to be on such a mainstream attention. He tried to take his mind off of his loss to John Crichton on the last Sunday. He was so close to the Television Championship. He was literally 1 second away from holding the championship and becoming the first Black Canadian citizen to hold the championship in history. Jordan felt as if he had been screwed. Screwed by John Crichton. Screwed by Jaden. Screwed by the PCW. And screwed by the white man. First off, John Crichton, the last Television Champion of the PCW, a man who Jordan knew he could defeat. Jordan X Shabazz, number ten out of all WT federations. What does that mean? In terms of the Internet “Smarks”, Jordan X Shabazz was “over” with the fans. John Crichton on the other hand… 134th. What does that mean? In the terms of the Internet “Smarks”, it means that Crichton isn’t over. Plain and simple. He’s not over, his character is not over, the Television Title is not over. John Crichton holding the Television Title diminishes the division, or at least in Jordan’s mind. Next on the list, was Jaden. Jaden, a woman that Jordan thought was on his side, a woman that Jordan wanted to save from the evil clutches of a racist white man with a dog named “N***er.” Jordan didn’t have a lick of respect for Crichton. But Jordan, did respect Jaden… as hard as that is to do. But Jaden turned on the Shabazzonator. Just as Shabazz had the opportunity to become the Television Champion of the world, a title that eh had worked so hard to get a shot at, a title that everybody watching the PCW at home thought he had earned, and would get, but she screwed him. And now, her name, along with Crichton, remains on Jordan X Shabazz’s list for destruction. Next on the list of SCREWAGE~! Was the PCW. The very company that was paying him. The very company that saved him from the sinking ship that was the NTICW. Sure you can say that there was nothing the PCW could do because the referee’s call is final. Sure you can say that the referee didn’t see a damned thing, therefore nothing should happen. But when you live in Jordan’s world, the world in which everything is in black and white, the world in which conspiracy is the root of everything that happens to him. So, in Jordan’s mind, PCW screwed him. And finally, the white man screwed him. This happens in Jordan’s mind to him everyday. “Hey, that guy cut in front of me in line. DAMN WHITEY!” “Why is my credit card bill so high? DAMN WHITEY!” “Hey, why does that guy have nicer teeth than me?” DAMN WHITEY!!” That’s Jordan’s life. Everything is blamed on Whitey. But do you blame him? Years of being stopped by police officers, years of being accused of crimes, and years of being passed by by cabbies who were afraid of being robbed, had trained Jordan to think this way. So there Jordan stood, staring at Rush, dumbfounded, thinking about wrestling, his match that was to happen on the following Friday night against John Crichton, Andy Murray, and Charlie Loc. Rush was defeated by Andy Murray at the Pay Per View, and Jordan X Shabazz was defeated by John Crichton at the pay per view. Thug roC wants to regain the cruiserweight title that he once held. So each man in this match on the side of the Uprising has something to gain, and pretty much nothing to lose. The Uprising, a brand new stable built up by Rush, Thug roC, and himself. The ignorant white Smarks all around the country we’re saying it as soon as the men got together. They were whispering “Heh, Parent Advisory 3!! And Black Attack two!!” But the difference is, Thug roC, Rush Mason, and Jordan X Shabazz all bring something different to the table. While as before we the prior stable, all of the men in it were essentially the same person. Jordan X Shabazz snaps out of his trance like state as Rush has finally hailed a cab. Jordan gets in the front seat, Rush and Thug get in the back. Jordan X Shabazz: “To wall street.” The taxi starts off to wall street, while Rush, Thug RoC, and Jordan X Shabazz have ushered a brand new era into the PCW. This isn’t Black Attack, this isn’t Parental Advisory, this is three black men, rebelling against the racism, and rebelling against the glass ceiling that a few “White devils” decided to cast over them…Thug roC, Jordan X Shabazz, and Rush Mason were going to make the white man pay for his sins…. In blood.
