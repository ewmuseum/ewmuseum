Tell me what you see. Fuck that stupid philosophical shit. I’ll tell you what you’ll see. You’ll see blood veins skeining my fucking eyeballs. Sleep depravation. I fuck some ho all damn night and then I catch the early flight out of the city. Sleep depravation blows. You fucking RUSH sell-outs blow. And your fucking Sell-Out attitude, afraid of fighting the fucking fights, so you suck Xavior Kannon's cock. But fuck fighting the good fight, because my people are too busy trying to survive. The real blacks in the projects; the ones being treated like second-class citizens by their social service agents. Not the blacks who drive those nice cars and wear those nice, comfy suits. Sure, they got their fucking education and they read, write, divide, and subtract how much taxes that are paying for my food, and my clothes is being taken out of their paychecks. Welfare is great. It makes fighting the fight obsolete. Why do you need any motivation to better yourself when you’ve got the man buying your food? You don’t need to go out and work, just walk on down to the corner and snag your check. Shoot the shit with the boys down there, maybe cop a joint, and then go on down to the store. Buy some toilet paper, a magazine, maybe a pound or two of hamburger, and a case of Bud. Fuck, you’re set for life. But, then you don’t realize the oppression that’s going on around you. You don’t fucking recognize that this is just what the government wants. They want your ass sitting in front of that television, a beer in your left hand, a cigarette in the other; working you way to high-blood pressure and heart disease. Feel the oppression. Feel the hate. You bastards and your fucking playstations. You bastards and your fucking steaks. You bastards and your fucking titles. You bastards and your fucking women. Oh wait. I’ve got one of those too, but she’s a skank, so I don’t give a fuck. You bastards and your fucking marijuana; you can actually pay for bud, while I’ve got to pay for shakes and stems and roots and fucking SEEDS. I can’t even get high any more, so fuck you guys with your weed. You bastards. Bastards.
