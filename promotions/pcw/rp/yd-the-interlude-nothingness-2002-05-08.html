YD: The Interlude (Nothingness)

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8th May 2002 As every great story is told it must be broken up in some way or another to keep someone interested. The story of Charlie Loc is still going strong but is nearing the point where it needs to be cut up the most. Strange and violent best describe this story, as the young hero is being put to the test. He has less than 24 hours to escape his home land or be killed, he is unsure of his future in America and everything else is looking down for him! Who knows what could unfold?With Charlie Loc nothing is ever as it seems……. Young And Dangerous Pt V: The Interlude (Nothingness) As far as the eye can see, there is not a trace of light! Not a single drop of color…all around is darkness! Complete and utter darkness! Darkness that is darker than the darkest night and blacker than the blackest cat, it’s as though it’s nothing…. If anything else could describe the setting any better it would’ve been said but there is nothing else other than 'Nothingness'. It seemed to be what people pictured the end of the world to be, or what it was like where you went when you die, it was like the scene that everyone can see in the back of their minds but can never put an accurate description onto it except for….nothing! All around everything was pitch black! The darkness seemed to stretch on forever, that’s if it even stretched out at all! With everything being pitch black it was hard to tell where the horizon was or exactly where the ground was but there was one thing for sure, this 'Nothingness' was indeed a place! Where ever it was, what ever it was called, it was a place….but the strange thing was that there was absolutely no sounds….as well as complete darkness there was complete silence, which tested the imagination even more! Once again questions began to rise, 'Where Is This Place?' 'What Is The Place?' 'What The Hell Is Going On?!' all of which seemed to have no possible answer! It would seem that every great mystery in the Universe has no possible answer, this 'Nothingness' place would appear to be no exception! With the thought of that yet another question clambers up and pops itself into existence 'How Can A Question Without An Answer Exist?' All the questions, all the descriptions, everything around seemed to just make everything else seem more confusing than it already was! No matter which way it was looked at, this 'Nothingness' place was perhaps the biggest mystery in the World or even the Universe! But for a mystery a detective was hired….in this case there was none which changed the light on the surroundings just a little, perhaps instead of this 'Nothingness' being a place of mystery, it was just one big riddle! This leads to even more unanswered questions! Even some riddles don’t have answers, the old Chinese myth of 'What Is The Sound of One Hand Clapping?' has never been solved because it is truly impossible or maybe just not humanly possible to clap with one hand! Maybe that was the same case with this 'Nothingness' the riddle was figuring out what exactly this place was….. BOOM~! Suddenly out of nowhere there is a truly blinding flash of light! It lights everything up covering the last inch of darkness with it’s bright white purity, it is like the darkness has just been touched by the hand of God himself…or some god at that! The only problem with this blinding white light is that it indeed was blinding! Usually people just use the word because they want over-exaggerate the intensity, but in this case the light was truly blinding! The darkness itself had just disappeared from sight and left nothing…no trace whatsoever of its existence…which just added even more confusion to the Riddle/Mystery! If the un-answered Riddle/Mystery was beginning to boil blood then it would soon have a competitor because the flash of light seemed to illuminate the 'Nothingness' and turn it into a simple, in fact very simple version of 'Heaven' which made matters even more confusing! Perhaps the darkness of the 'Nothingness' was the very pits of hell, maybe that’s what it’d be like being stuck in eternal damnation! In complete darkness with not a soul in the world to talk to, wondering if you even existed….Then again if that was hell, heaven itself was pretty dull too! Yet another question raised itself from nowhere, 'Why the hell is heaven like hell?!' It was like the questions had minds of their own! People would rather be stuck in the middle of a World War than spend the rest of eternity thinking about crazy thoughts in the 'Nothingness' 'Uhhhhh….' The deep groan of someone who has just been hurt echoes around the walls of the 'Nothingness' as the blinding light begins to fade at a rapid rate, slowly illuminating a figure sprawled over on a patch of white light. Just as another million and one un-answerable questions are about to spring out of nowhere and accompany the others the light quickly fades and draws into sort of a spotlight…illuminating a small looking Oriental man laying sweaty and tensed on the floor with the darkness surrounding him. Suddenly it all became clear….if there could be a birds eye view, that’s if it was even possible! Perhaps it could for the 'Yin Yang' or in other words this young Asian man would be stuck in the circle of good surrounded by bad…. 'For Everything Good There Is Always Something Evil, For Everything Evil There Is Always Something Good!' No matter which way the quote was looked at it made complete sense and began to make everything around seem a lot clearer! 'Welcome Khiem!' BOOM~! There is a loud rumbling sound as a voice that can be nothing more than that of a god shakes everything, or seemingly shakes everything as the man sprawled in the center of the patch of light bolts upright revealing himself to be Charlie Loc! 'It CHAR-LIE….What the hell you want?!' As though it’s an instinct the young and sweaty Charlie Loc looks around with an angry looking red face snapping back at the voice of the god. 'The real question is….what do you want?' The voice causes the rumbling sound to start yet again as it becomes deafening! As the sound echoes on the walls around Charlie falls into the center of the circle clasping his hands over his ears as he waits for the rumbling to finally cease. It doesn’t take too long as Charlie opens his eyes and looks around before taking his hands off of his ears…standing up and looking around to check who saw him! It would seem that everything is about image to young Loc. As he turns around he begins to snap back with comments in a menacing tone, 'I ask you question, you answer! Then you ask me question and then I answer!' There is no response from the voice as Charlie smirks thinking he’s just won a childish argument. He looks around and shakes his head before leaning forward to step out of the circle of light as the voice Booms once again causing him to fall on his ass dead in the center of it! 'If you leave the Circle Khiem….you have already lost! Listen to what I have to say and then will your questions be answered, and then will you be able to leave! If you step foot outside that circle not only will you turn your back on your faith….you will turn your back on yourself!' The voice suddenly stops booming as it becomes gentle speaking almost like a quote. The small Vietnamese eyes on Charlie’s face grow wide as he brushes his long hair out of his face and looks around him before nodding his head in agreement and then shifting onto his knees and hanging his head down low…realizing who is speaking to him! 'I see you’ve found out who I am Khiem! Do you see now? Anger does not solve all of your problems, if you be calm the answers will find themselves to you instead of you having to find them!' The gentle voice becomes clear as it changes into a fluent English speaking voice of an Old Oriental man. Charlie looks up from his seemingly 'praying' position and nods his head before examining everything around him and asking a question in a rather nice sounding tone, something that has never been displayed before by Loc. 'I sorry to have disgraced you Buddha….I not know who you be! Sometime I get crazy dreams and when I tell the spirit to go away it get scared and leave! Where am I? I asking now…' Charlie speaks almost like a little kid telling his mother that the other children are scared of him. 'You are in the 'Yin Yang' Khiem. The darkness surrounding you is everything bad, the circle of light your sitting in is everything PURE. I have bough you here to discuss some urgent matters my young Leing Sei!' The voice talks back to him like a tolerant parent, which is strange because people usually don’t show much patience with the hot-headed Charlie…. 'What they be?' Charlie looks confused but knowing and trying to hide as he looks upwards towards the light before shielding his face and rubbing his eyes, proving that the light truly is blinding. 'I’m not here to play games with you young Khiem…it would seem that you are in quiet the predicament and you wish to be respected by your country and the rest of the world! In other words you wish to be like me! Is that true?' The voice asks him almost coaxing him to give the reply he wants to hear. 'I not know what you talking about….' Charlie blatantly lies and there is a whooshing sound as the circle of light draws tighter around him. 'I’ll ask you again, do you wish to be high, powerful and respected by the world?' The voice becomes more menacing, which is scary for a god to become angry, especially Buddha! 'Maybe…' Charlie doesn’t exactly lie but he doesn’t exactly tell the truth as he hangs his head trying to cover his guilty face. WHOOSH~! The 'Ying' draws itself closer around Charlie as he draws himself in tight and begins to look quiet alarmed as the god speaks once more. 'This is your last chance Leing Sei, do you wish to be like me?' The god asks and the rumbling begins again. Charlie doesn’t reply instead he just hangs his head and nods as the Circle remains the same and the rumbling and echoing begins to fade out into the distance. 'Very well Khiem…you shall learn the hard way to speak the truth! You are lucky that I have for-seen good tidings in your life that may be of some use to me Khiem, otherwise you would have been laying in evil for the rest of your life! Do you wish to hear my instructions?' The god becomes a little happier as he asks Charlie a question. Charlie sits motionless with his head hung low as the voice booms and rumbles once again. 'Do you?' Finally Charlie nods his head and looks up towards the light as the talking of the god Buddha begins to continue as the darkness around the light seems to keep itself at bay. 'To become like me…you must prove you are everything! You must show you can overcome any boundary, any hurdle, you must prove you will fight to the death. Do you understand me?' The god speaks out towards him again. 'Yeah I understand, I already know all that why you telling me again?' Charlie almost whines back. 'Perhaps because you know it and you never seem to do it! Khiem you are a prized fighter and you have the courage, the speed and the movements to become like me but you lack one thing….' The god’s voice trails off into the distance as Charlie’s face grows red with anger. 'What I lack?!' Charlie snaps back almost immediately! 'You have just proven my point, you lack discipline! In order to get anywhere you must prove you have discipline Khiem! I cannot help you the whole way, I can only advise! In order for me to advise you must listen and absorb my advice!' The god states in a matter of fact type voice. Charlie mumbles something underneath his breath as the god chuckles and speaks back again. 'I can hear every word you utter Khiem…I watch you every step you take, every breath you take! It’s your choice to listen to me as time is short!' Charlie sulks his shoulders and shakes his head as a million and one evil thoughts fly through his head. This can be noticed as the Ying draws itself tighter and tighter around him until he realizes what’s going on and stops with a scared look on his young Vietnamese face. 'Remember Leing Sei, I can see and hear everything you do! This is your final chance, listen to me and gain some discipline in order to become one step closer to becoming like me! It is up to you….but in order to do that, you must overcome your first obstacle….' The god states his intentions again. A deep breath can be heard as Charlie gets up and stands tall and tenses all the muscles on his small 5’8' frame. If Buddha’s face could be seen, it would be seen smiling as Charlie steps up to take the challenging heeding his advice. 'What my obstacle?' Charlie whispers in a low tone as his voice echoes and trails off into the 'Nothingness' around him. 'Your obstacle is to make your way back to America in one piece….' The god states as Charlie hangs his head and begins to flex his arm muscles. 'How that give me discipline?' Charlie whispers yet again as the familiar confused, lop-sided look crosses over his face. 'Remember what I said Charlie discipline and you will succeed! Overcome the obstacle and then will I return to assist you more Leing Sei! Do not fail me…I will not be impressed!' The god’s voice trails off into the distance as the light around Charlie slowly fades, shadows slowly casting across his face. 'But I not know what you mean….' 'You will soon see Leing Sei….you will soon see!' The voice of the god begins to fade into the distance from where it came. The confused look on Charlie’s face is the last thing to be seen as he slowly disappears as the darkness envelopes and covers over him causing him to disappear from where he came! Usually desolation can be described as having a wind blowing with barren waste lands and not a soul in site, but after experiencing this, desolation can only be described as Nothingness....
