The Week Begins.

10 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Week Begins. Featuring: Tim Walden Date: March 10, 2002 Seething, yet again...The opportunity for a clear victory had been taken from my grasp once more, barely even to my knowledge that something had happened...he couldn't have caught my knee like that, I had him down and out, not unless he 'Heroed up' or something else like that... He didn't catch my knee like that. Once more, my hopes for some much-needed momentum were foiled by HER...Every time, every good opportunity I'd had seemed to get cost me by her involvement, and every time the crowd just cheered it further. It boggled the mind to a point..I was going out there, every night, putting my heart and soul into the matches and always keeping it competitive, and I couldn't buy the crowd's applause, no matter what she did they still loved her. I had an idea why...she was everything they liked, energetic, down-home and cute, an all-American girl if ever there was one. They'd never turn their backs on her for the same reason they would always be behind Duke while he fought on 'their side', and to them, it just meant they had to hate me....I'd had to live with that fact for a while now, but I wasn't going to let the bother of being disliked get me down by any length. There was always a way to take the best side from something like this, and the coming week was no different. Duke...Kano...Teresa. Three southern characters who would more than likely have the crowds on their side, three matches where I would more than likely be subject to their shenanigans, she would want to cost me the opportunities, except where she was the opportunity... The chance to become Television Champion once more, to spite the ignorant masses by beating their heroes, to prove my self-worth as they fell, just as when I'd shown my superiority over her and the shape months before...Hindered by the officiating. It was a given that Sally would show favoritism, as she had previously when I had last had my opportunity. I'd made sure she got the memo the next week as to how I frowned upon favoritism when tax time comes around, so perhaps maybe this would be fair at least...then again, Rent-Boy would be a threat. Or not..Much as the guy was smitten, he didn't have the guts to show up if I kept it clean, it would be against his supercode or something. Shame Teresa'd never go for something like that, or things would be easier on me..Instead, I seemed to be running an emotional gauntlet over these two weeks. Every match, a reminder of past aggravations, each one greater than the last, each one subject to the involvement of my foe..She was walking a fine line through her efforts. Each loss I took at her hands would hurt my momentum, and with it my pride...It'd already taken damage from yet another failure to beat Rent-A-Roni, and it was only beginning. But where pride may suffer...The desire for vengeance builds. The rage, that would grow deeper and deeper with each defeat, would all come back to her in time. That was what this was about for now...It was just a matter of time. So why would I waste it now? It would be of no effect to my mindset to be beaten fairly by either man, much as the chances of such weren't necessarily so good, and so I would have to maintain just a little focus to them for now...It was a good thing these two were different from her in one regard. She at least knew well enough not to run her mouth constantly in public. Every night of a show, Duke was always at the commentary desk without fail, ranting about how we were ruining PCW with our management style and how we were holding down the hosses, and every time he got hold of a microphone away from the table, he was always giving the willing masses their fill of the Book of Malice or whatever he liked. The fans ate up whatever he had to say, never mind that he only left the Office because he wasn't satisfied with his share...Greedy little son-of-a-bitch would never have let me get a chance at the Continental while he was on with our side, left Brandi and I to fight for whatever title scraps were out there...Maybe it was stubbornness, maybe it was just a discouragement. One thing I did know, was that much as he'd been plowing through opposition in his Month of Malice, he wasn't invulnerable. If he was, he'd have won the title at Red October. He wasn't out of my range to beat..I'd beaten him in the Alliances match, even if by the Trapezoid's own effort, I was just as talented as he'd ever been. And he couldn't be too confident in his ability to beat me...There would be something, in the back of his mind, even if he let the world know of his confidence, he couldn't shake the possibility of defeat...It harkened back to the days when he let the world know differently, when we had the match for a World Title shot, and while we were supposed to be fighting for the good of the team, he made it clear that I was not to win the match under any circumstances...He proved to me how heavily his ego weighed on his decisions at that moment. Maybe things had changed... But for me, it was just going to stay the same. It was healthier for me to believe in his ego at this point, where there was no mystery about that of Kano. But all the ego had done there was cause pain for me, every time that delusional case gloated over his victories...Tainted if ever they'd been, firstly by Flaymin Aymin's blatant involvement, then again, in humiliating fashion. I never even saw the West Hollywood Brigade coming, and their ridiculous 'weapon shots'...What purpose did they serve, that their so-called efforts could not keep Kano down? I didn't believe it, the little bitch had probably hired them out just to make him look good..It didn't hurt to shock me into a state where I wouldn't be able to resist, hell I'd honestly believed the crap before finally managing to get a hint from DiCaprio's publicist under duress. ...But that was probably neither here nor there. The fact of the matter remained. Kano had two wins on his account over me, clean or not. I wasn't going to let him get a third while I was still conscious, not while he still had the gall to pretend that titles won for lack of competition could ever stand up to my Cruiserweight reign of terror, to a TV Title won from a true legend, to technical skill that stretched beyond anything he could ever have taught me in 'the weekend in the woods' that he always talked of as if it was real... He was on his last legs, not much better than his first ones had been..And I was going to cut them out from under him, use them to trip him around the ring, then snap them like the twigs that someone of his lack of conditioning would wrestle on. I hold no mercy for the ignorant. And no matter what the outcome, one thing holds firm...There would be no evasion for anyone. Not Kano, not Teresa, not anybody else who would get in my way or challenge my superiority.... It would all have to start with Duke. It would be difficult to take care of business against someone with such competitive momentum lately, and I know the pitfalls that lay ahead either way...But I know one more thing as well. When the opportunity comes tomorrow, when his 'Honky Tonk Heroes' hits and the crowd comes to life, the Month of Malice will have been over for more than a week... Now, it's my turn to steal back the spotlight he's shined upon himself...My turn to keep people watching, whether they want to or not... ...My turn is just about to begin. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
