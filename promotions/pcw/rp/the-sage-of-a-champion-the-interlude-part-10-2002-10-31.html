The Sage of A Champion: The Interlude Part 10

31 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-31-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!The Interlude Part 10” Chronicles!Chronicles!Chronicles! Chapter 3: The Mission Begins :: Last time’s scene was BladeD in his room talking to his brother about everything that is going down. Once he leaves him he won’t return until the mission is over, so he is taking every second he has to just sit back and chill with his family while taking time to think of what all he is going to do when he goes out into the war against Metal Armor. The scene opens with BladeD in his room. The room is small, dark, and almost depressing at the moment because the one lamp light on and that is it. There is just enough light to see. It is dark, cold, and sort of damp outside. The weather isn’t good enough to be outside and with the privacy he has he is going over final plans. BladeD is sitting at pretty big, very round, height wise it is normal size, table. Scattered across this table our millions of papers. These papers range anywhere from reports, special documents, letters, blueprints, and basically just any type of paper you could think of is scattered across this table. BladeD even has scratch pieces of paper everywhere from right in front of him to on the floor that he is writing on for notes and all that good stuff. :: BladeD: So the main building I am going to be at is 4500 x 2400 feet. That is pretty big, but for some reason that just doesn’t seem big enough for a main building. It is only four stories tall. I have a feeling this isn’t the main building. Maybe it is just a branch of some sort because the main building would have to bigger. :: BladeD begins to look at a blueprint of the first floor that shows where all the entrances, exits, doors, ect, are. :: BladeD: I will mark this entrance on the left side as entrance 1, the one on the bottom number 2, and the one of the right as number 3… But it doesn’t show an entrance for the top… Maybe it is an exit only type of thing and it isn’t marked on here, but this blueprint doesn’t even show an exit for the top either. That is strange. Oh well. If I go through entrance number 1, I will have three hallways straight ahead of me to the left, right, and forward. That seems simple enough. :: BladeD looks over the hallways and begins to read off some of the rooms that are surrounding him. :: BladeD: There is a waiting room over there. :: points to the left :: :: Stops to think. :: Waiting room? There are that many people that come to the building, so that they have to have a waiting room? That doesn’t seem right. Man the farther along I go with this the stranger it becomes. It almost like this place isn’t even a headquarters for military or infantry services. This place is beginning to just seem like a regular government building of some sort. I have never heard of a military or infantry building of any kind big or small have a waiting room. Maybe that is some kind of slang term for something else over seas, but I doubt it. :: BladeD gets distracted and skims over the blueprint some more. :: Bladed: Well over there. :: In the middle right-hand corner. :: Is a storage room of some sort. From the looks of this name that I can’t read I am going to take a wild guess and say that this storage room holds guns of some kind. If this were true then this place would have to be an infantry place of some kind, but a waiting room? That is still throwing me off. It is probably meant to through me off… :: It is getting late like 11pm. which for a 14 year old BladeD. That is late. He is getting to the point where he can’t think straight and now that he has been thrown off track like this, he decides it is time for him to go to bed and does so. :: :: While BladeD is in bed he has a nightmare about this whole thing. He has a dream that he is in the building and there are actual people in this waiting room that have nothing to do with the military or infantry, but they all have shotguns in their hands and they are ready to kill BladeD. He is running in every direction he can to get away and finds his way to that storage room, but once he gets there all the guns, armor, and everything is gone. The only thing in there our people with shotguns ready to kill him. The next thing he knows he is running as fast as he can in directions he doesn’t even know trying franticly to get ahead from all these normal civilians with shotguns trying to kill him and before anything serious happens he wakes up from the nightmare. :: BladeD: OMG!!! OMG!!! OMG!!! :: BladeD’s heart is pounding a million miles an hour right now as he has just been scared almost to death. Lucky he manages not to wake anyone up. :: BladeD: OMG!! Now that was a nightmare. I got way too stressed over that whole waiting room thing. Man, I don’t even want to go back to sleep now because that was so scary. I haven’t had a dream that scary since I was like ten years old or something. I need to go back to sleep, but man. That was just crazy with all of those normal people with shotguns and no weapons to be found. I wonder if that was some kind of sign from God telling me that the storage room really doesn’t contain weapons. :: BladeD lays his head back down and tries to go back to sleep, but he keeps thinking about the nightmare he just woke up from and doesn’t want to go back to sleep because of it. His body gets so tired that he finally just falls a sleep on his own. :: :: This time it is one of those types of deals where you go to sleep, but you sleep and don’t really dream of anything. You wake up and just know you didn’t dream of anything. That is basically what happened this time and BladeD just wakes up normally at around nine o’clock. :: BladeD: :: BladeD yawns. :: Man that was a crazy nightmare last night. I am still not over that. I am surprised that I was even able to go back to sleep because I really didn’t want to. I was too scared I would relive that nightmare again. At least I didn’t because, then I would have fa sho not have gone back to sleep after that. :: BladeD crawls out of bed and that is last of what we see from him for now. ::
