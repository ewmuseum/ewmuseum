Date: not entered Welcome Sci-Fi Freaks! A camera zooms in on a space shuttle set where a tall man in a brown suit is seated. The set is a couch placed in front of a huge space shuttle in the background on a rainy day. The man is placed in the center of the blue leather couch, and he is reading a Star Trek Comic Book. As he gets a signal he drops the book and stares into the screen. Brown Suit Man: “Welcome to the Sci-Fi channels newest show! I’m your nerdy host if you may say so, Jerry Mufti! Serious with Sci-Fi is making it’s television debut, and we hope to please all of you nerds, geeks, fans, and fanatics out there. We have a jam-packed show with the PCW tag team of Misplaced, and the very of David Duchovny! It should be a whopping good time. We’ll get started right after this advertisement break.” Serious Sci-Fi is brought to you by: X-Files—We now suck! Serious Sci-Fi is placed in the middle of a television screen, and it then fades into Jerry Mufti. He slouches back in his seat and prepares for the show. Jerry Mufti: “Ladies and gentlemen, I’m proud to introduce the PCW tag team of Misplaced! Misplaced isn’t well known around PCW yet, but they’re trying hard. In that team we have Carey J. Nowski who is as big of a Sci-Fi fan that you’ll find. So lets give a warm Sci-Fitting welcome to Misplaced!” The crowd is mixed with a few cheers and some silence. Carey comes out first with his Fox Mulder doll in his hand and following him is Stephen who carries a disgusted look. Jerry Mufti gets out of his seat to greet Misplaced. Carey gives a warm handshake but Stephen ignores the Sci-Fi nerd host. Stephen Cairns: -Muttering-…I hate you so much Carey. I hate you so much Carey. I hate you so much Carey. Carey J. Nowski: Excuse me? Did you say something? Stephen Cairns: No. No I didn’t. I sure didn’t. Not me. No. Of course not. No. Carey gives a sigh and takes a seat on the couch next to Jerry. Stephen asks for a separate chair away from the two, and he gets his wish. Jerry tugs on his suit and prepares to chat with the two esteemed tag team partners. Jerry Mufti: “Welcome to the show guys! We’re honored to have you here as our first guests on Serious Sci-Fi! How are you both today?” Carey J. Nowski: “Just great Jerry. I’m excited to be here, and I can’t wait to meet David. Carey holds up his Mulder doll with pride, and the crowd cheers. Stephen shakes his head. Jerry Mufti: “And how are you over their big fellow?” Stephen Cairns: “Leave me alone.” Jerry Mufti: “Well we certainly have a cranky pants here today.” Stephen Cairns: “Leave me alone.” Stephen turns his chair away from the host who seems to be terribly annoying Stephen. Carey gives a slight sigh at his big headed partner. Carey J. Nowski: “Excuse him, he’s not having a good day. At least it sure doesn’t seem like it.” Jerry gives a smile to Stephen and chuckles. Stephen turns his chair around further, and the hosts smile drops. Jerry Mufti: “Wow, rough start to the show. Anyways Carey, how do you like the new show?” Carey J. Nowski: “How do I like it? I love it! There are not enough shows out there for us so called -snort- nerds! Plus I get a chance to meet the one and only David Duchovny! It’s great!” Jerry Mufti: “I’m glad you’re pleased. Meeting David should be exciting, but for being our special guests we have something for you. Meeto, come on out!” A person dressed in an alien suit comes out with a box in outer space wrapping paper. He has a seat next to Carey and puts the present on his lap. Meeto: “This…is…for…you!” The alien Meeto sounds as if he has a voice box on, either that or he’s just extremely strange. Carey picks up the box and shakes it around. A big grin comes on his face when he hears the rattle of a gift just for him. He tears off the paper to reveal a bright blue box that is taped around the edges. Carey tears off the tape and pulls the cover off of the box. He gets a huge grin as he peers into the box. Inside there is a tape recorder with a tape in it. Carey looks at Meeto. Carey J. Nowski: “A tape recorder? What is this for?” Meeto: “Play…the…tape…and…you…will…find…out…” Carey clicks play on the tape, and Meeto’s voice can be heard on the tape. Carey listens in. Carey…I…am…Meeto. You…don’t…know…me…or…at…least…you…don’t…think…you…do. You…are…really…a…big…fan…of…me. If…you…guess…who…I…am…you’ll…receive…a…prize. Waaaaaa… Meeto’s strange soft voice puts Carey in a dazed mood. He sits puzzled trying to figure out who Meeto could be. Carey J. Nowski: “You sound just like my mother when she was always drunk. Mother? M…Mother?” Carey places his guess for Meeto to be his mother. Meeto pulls off his hat to reveal none other than…his mother! We had met Carey’s parents in an earlier episode of Misplaced’s life. Carey’s jaw drops at the site of his mother. Carey’s Mother: “Hello Son! I am Meeto, or you know me better as mother! Would you like to know what you won?” Carey J. Nowski: “I…I guess so.” Carey’s Mother: “You’ve won a brand new scooter!” Men in gold jumpsuits run out with a motor scooter for Carey. Stephen looks at the men and bows his head down. The expression on his face is the usual. Meanwhile Carey is as excited as…well…he’s excited. Carey J. Nowski: “This is awesome! A motor scooter! Wow!” Jerry Mufti gives one of his big grins at Carey’s satisfaction. Jerry Mufti: “We aim to please! That was our first ever celebrity surprise game, and you are a lucky winner! Anyways, lets get on with the show. It’s time to go through the crowd and see what questions our guests have.” Jerry Mufti gets up off of the couch and walks over to a bleacher full of an excited audience. They all have their hand up in hope to get to ask Misplaced a question. Jerry randomly picks a person from the crowd. Jerry Mufti: “You in the black! What questions do you have for Misplaced?” Man In Black: “Wowed! I get to ask celebrities a question! Super! Misplaced, why are one of you so nice and the other is a dick monkey?” This question draws Stephen’s attention quickly. He gets up off of the chair and takes a seat next to Carey so he may answer the questions. Carey J. Nowski: “Well, you see…” Stephen nudges Carey’s shoulder and interrupts his sentence. Stephen Cairns: “…there is no answer to that. Die. That’s the answer.” Carey gives an upset look at Stephens simple smart-ass remark. The man in the stands quickly takes his seat, and Jerry exits the bleachers. Jerry Mufti: “You know what, maybe the question session should be saved for David. So lets waste no time and bring out your Fox Mulder, David Duchovny!” The crowd gives a loud pop as David enters the stage. He walks to the front of the stage towards the bleachers and gives a bow. The crowd gives another loud pop. Stephen laughs as the crowd idolizes what to him is a washed up actor. David takes a seat next to Stephen on the couch. Stephen Cairns: “Why are you sitting next to me? Get your own damn couch you bum.” The crowd boos at Stephen for insulting their god David Duchovny. Stephen once again simply laughs at the crowd. Jerry tries to break up the crowd booing by bringing up a subject. Jerry Mufti: “David, it’s great to have you on the show! We apologize for the not so warm greeting. Stephen Cairns: “I really do hate you Carey. I’m going backstage. You girls have a nice freakazoid show.” Stephen exits the seat and the set which leaves just Jerry, Carey, and David. Carey gets up and leaves the set also. He pops his head back on the set and says goodbye, but he once again exits the set. He wanders around a while until he spots Stephen getting a cup of water, so he approaches him. Carey J. Nowski: “Why did you do that? You’re ruining my opportunity as usual! I’ve had just about enough of this! We’re supposed to be a team, remember?” Stephen Cairns: “If we’re a team then why do you control us? Why don’t we ever go out to play cricket, or do anything that I like. We just go to stupid ass conventions and stupid ass television shows. I’ve had enough of that!” Stephen and Carey both pull up a chair to chat. A look of depression comes across both of there faces. Carey J. Nowski: “How about we start acting like a team? I’ll do what you want sometimes, and you’ll do what I want sometimes. That seems like a fair deal.” Stephen Cairns: “That’s fine. Good. What about our wrestling though? What do we do with that?” Carey J. Nowski: “That’s where teamwork comes in again. We need to act like a team. Our career has gone rough in PCW so far, but we can change that. Lets win that match and get us a name amongst the wrestlers. I’m starting to get sick of losing. It’s partially my fault though. I’ve been doing a lot of work with conspiracies around the world. It’s tied me up.” Stephen shakes his head. He picks up a magazine out of Carey’s pocket, which is a sci-fi magazine. He holds it up and looks at Carey. Stephen Cairns: “You need to drop this stupid stuff. We’re wrestlers not X-File agents.” Carey J. Nowski: “Damnit Stephen! You don’t understand! Nobody understands! I’m the only one who realizes the truth right now. People don’t believe my theories, but one day they’ll all come true. People will see my true power and wisdom. My day will come! When we face The Pretty Cool Guys they will see my true knowledge! The whole world will come to idolize me!” Stephen Cairns: “That’s why you need a life. Whatever you say Carey. Maybe one day your theories will come true, but until that time people will think your insane. Carey takes a cup of water and sips at it for a while. He sits back with his Mulder doll for several minutes staying silent. They both are sitting there with a strange atmosphere around them. Finally he finishes the water, and he continues to look at Stephen. Carey J. Nowski: “You don’t believe me now. Nobody does. Damnit though, you all will. PCW will fear my knowledge.” You can tell that Stephen is holding back a laugh. He gets out of his seat and he shakes his head at Carey with a smile on his face. Stephen walks away and leaves Carey sitting alone in this empty room. Carey slouches in his seat and shuts his eyes.
