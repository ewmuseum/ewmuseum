FOOOOORRRREEE!!!

22 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/22/02 “FOOOOOOOOOOOOOORRRRE!!!” shouts the CEO of PCW Tyler Nelson as we fade in. We find ourselves on a breathtakingly landscaped golf course situated on the private and elite Dallas Country Club. Huge trees line the perfectly manicured bentgrass fairways on this beautiful plot of land somewhere in the middle of nowhere. There is little in the way of sounds….birds chirping and singing, squirrels scampering about from tree to tree, the wind blowing through the limbs of the trees, and Tyler Nelson cursing up a storm. “Goddamn mother fucker,” grumbles the CEO gruffly. “I’m getting sick and tired of this fucking slice! No matter what I do I always slice my Goddamn drives.” Tyler stands on the tee box for the third hole, a magnificent four hundred eighty yard par five with a dogleg slightly to the left. He wears a white polo shirt with ‘UA’ embroidered on the front, a pair of tan khaki pants, and some black Nike golf shoes. He uses his hand to shield his eyes from the late morning sun as he tries to ascertain the location of his errant ball. Back several yards, sitting in the custom made black Cadillac Escalade golf cart, is Orpheus Grant. The massive bodyguard for the CEO wears a red Nike golf polo, matching red Nike visor, and black khaki shorts with black Nike shoes. OG shakes his head in amusement as he watches Tyler. “Where in the hell did that little bastard go,” wonders Tyler aloud to himself. He scans from left to right several times before shrugging his shoulders and finally giving up. “Fuck it.” Tyler turns and heads back to the cart. “You ain’t find ya ball, Ty,” asks Orpheus, knowing full well the answer. “That DAMN wind got a hold of that thing and pushed it way right,” lies Tyler. “I couldn’t believe it. That baby was heading right down the sprinkler line, too.” Tyler grins sheepishly as he digs around in his Calloway Golf bag, retrieving another ball. Orpheus looks at Tyler with a feigned scowl. “What’chu think you gonna do with that?” “Hit a provisional,” says Tyler matter-of-factly. Orhpeus can only laugh as Tyler tees the ball up into the ground. The CEO takes several steps back and measures up his shot, then walks up to address the ball. Tyler sets up and waggles the head of the driver behind the ball, getting comfortable with his positioning. Finally Tyler slowly brings the club back, then swings through with all his might, making solid contact with the ball. The golf ball explodes off the face of the driver at one hundred fifty miles an hour, and immediately starts to snap right. “YOU FUCKER!!!” yells Tyler. The ball veers further right and heads right for another group of golfers that are on the parallel fairway. “FOOOORRRRE AGAAAAAAIIIIN!!” warns the CEO. As he watches the ball speed through the air it reaches the group, then one of the players goes down like a sack of potatoes. All of the other people in the group huddle around the downed person with a look of panick about them. “Shit!” exclaims Tyler frantically. “We gotta get down there NOW!! Step on it, OG!” Tyler hustles over and hops in the passenger seat of the Cadillac Escalade golf cart and Orpheus almost punches the gas pedal through the floor. The cart spins it’s tires and OG steers the vehicle toward the huddled group of people, driving directly through the tee box. The cart speeds down a hill and finally comes upon the group of golfers, three men standing around the fourth man. They are older gentleman, more than likely retired, and wearing the typical plaid polyester pants and loud shirts that are frequently associated with men of that age and type. One of the men comes waddling toward the cart as it skids to a stop in the plush grass, dredging a path as it does so. “Are you a doctor, sonny?” wheezes the old man. “Hell no! I’m the CEO!” proclaims Tyler. “Do you have one of them cell phone thingies we can use to call a doctor or an ambulance for my friend here?” asks the old man with concern draped over his words. Tyler gets up from the seat of the golf cart and pushes past the old man. “What in the hell do you need an ambulance for?” “He was hit on the head with a golf ball,” explains the old man. “He was knocked out cold for a few minutes and now he can’t remember who he is.” “Probably for the best,” Tyler mutters to himself. “Huh?” asks the old man. “My hearing ain’t so good these days.” “I SAAAIIIIIIDD TTHHHAAAAAAT IIIIITTTT’SSS PPRROOOOOOBBBBB—aww, never mind. Lemme take a look at him,” says Tyler. The CEO walks over and pushes the other two old men out of the way. “Gimme some room here. I NEED SOME GODDAMN ROOM!” The men back away hesitantly as Tyler stares down at the old man. He notices right away that the man is probably concussed and his eyes are glazed over. The CEO looks over the man and the area surrounding him with a puzzled look on his face. “Hmmmm…..” “What is it?” asks one of the other old men. “Is he gonna be okay?” “Hell if I know,” quips the CEO sarcastically. “I’m not a friggin doctor.” Tyler kneels down and rolls the old man over. The other men think about protesting the idea but they all three take notice of the massive black man that gets out of the drivers seat of the Cadillac and think it wise to just shut up. As Tyler unceremoniously shoves the old man onto his stomach his eyes light up like a child that had just found it’s lost puppy. “A-ha! There you are,” exclaims the CEO. “I knew I would find you sooner or later.” Tyler reaches down and picks up his golf ball. He frowns as he notices a small red spot on the ball. “That won’t do.” Tyler looks around for a few seconds, then decides to grab a hold of the old man’s shirt and polishes the golf ball up. The other three men are looking on in shocked disbelief. “Good as new,” gloats Tyler. He stands to his feet and starts to head off toward the golf cart. One of the old men behind him calls out as he walks away. “Hey!”, shouts the old man. “Is our friend gonna be alright?” Tyler half turns to respond so he can keep walking. “I don’t know, don’t care. I think if he wouldn’t have stolen my golf ball then he wouldn’t be in the predicament that he’s in.” The three old men look at each other, dumbfounded. Tyler slides into the golf cart and orders Orpheus to go. The cart once again speeds on it’s way, leaving the three men to continue their vigil over their fallen friend. In the cart Tyler looks ahead without remorse. “That old bastard tried to steal my golf ball, but I got it back. That bastard Porter stole by PCW World Championship, and now I’m going to get it back.” “Preach on, nigga,” encourages Orpheus. “Oh I will…..I’ll preach the Gospel of Greed to Frisco, Legend, INC, and Boda,” continues Tyler. Then he pauses with a sour look on his face. “What’s wrong, Ty?” asks OG. “Boda? BODA? How in the HELL does Boda even deserve to be associated with the PCW World Championship. Look at the depths he’s taken it to already. Jobbers are NOT supposed to hold the World Title. Then there’s Frisco, a washed up shell of his former self that is trying desperately to grab the spotlight for one last time. He should have quit when I kicked his ass for the belt the first time. And then we have Legend, INC, a man that dares to utter my name and strives to emanate me. He and his Jobber Crew mates may not make it to Turmoil in one piece anyway, so he may not be a factor. Come to think of it,” says Tyler. “Neither one of those three losers even deserves to be in the same arena as me, the CEO, let alone have me kick their ass in a match. I think that concessions or valets are better jobs for those assholes than wrestling. It’s a total waste of my time to have to suit up, get all ready, and then go out there and kick their asses in a matter of minutes.” “It’s somthin’ ya gotta do, Ty,” explains OG. “Cause if ya don’t them muthafucka are gon keep runnin around PCW like they is somebody. Ya need ta show em that they ain’t shit.” A confident smile crosses Tyler’s lips. “You’re right. I’m gonna go to Milwaukee and kick some fucking ass. Unfortunately they can’t stop me, because I’m the CEO.” We move outside the cart as it approaches the green. OG veers the cart off to the left and suddenly a ball comes shooting out and rolls onto the green. The cart stops and Tyler climbs out. “Would you look at that,” oozes Tyler. “My ball is on the green! What a magnificent shot by yours truly if I may say so myself.” From the cart OG offers a golf clap as Tyler takes a mock bow. He walks over and grabs his putter from his golf bag as the camera fades to black.
