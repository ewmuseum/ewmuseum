The Sage of A Champion: The Interlude Part 11

5 November 2002

Date: 11-5-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!The Interlude Part 11” Chronicles!Chronicles!Chronicles! Chapter 3: The Mission Begins :: The last time we saw BladeD he had just woken up. Well, it was his second time waking up because the first time he woke from a nightmare. He had fallen a sleep after doing a lot of research on the area he is going to be in while on the mission. The scene leaps off into BladeD back at the same table gathering together information. :: BladeD: Man there is so much information. This is crazy, but on top of that I still don’t understand why exactly there is a waiting room. I wonder if that storage area stores something different other than weapons. It is almost think I am looking at the wrong blueprint of the building. :: BladeD continues to look farther into this to solve the problem and farther understand this up. :: BladeD: I GOT IT! Man I can’t believe I didn’t think of this before. The waiting room is for new recruits. They just have it in the same building. We, in the U.S., have a waiting room of sorts for new recruits in a different building, but they keep theirs in the same building, so that is what it has to be. :: BladeD continues to cover the map. :: BladeD: Three restrooms. That is good. The more the merrier is the way I see it because it is a good place to hide people. :: Laughs :: I am going to be doing a lot of that or at least I would suppose so, since it is the safest form of “being sneaky” that being taking out people and hiding them (the evidence). :: BladeD looks over beside the restrooms and sees a hallway. This hallway leads down to an area that says classrooms. :: BladeD: :: in a confused voice :: Classrooms? …. :: BladeD stares in wonder while he thinks about this. :: Maybe it is far something different. Instead of classrooms that you would be taught in maybe that is their form of meeting rooms for planning proceeds of attack and things related to that. :: BladeD pauses for a minute and thinks. ::BladeD: This is really weird. Everything is just so structured differently that it is confusing and hard to understand. This is really putting me through a lot of stress. It is getting to the point where it is too much for me to handle, but oh well. :: BladeD looks over toward the left corner of the map. It is one of the few places left that he hasn’t searched around in. :: BladeD: Well, there isn’t much in this part of the building. It looks like a janitorial area. That could be a very useful place for me. I could really be sneaky with a place like that in the building. So far this place is turning out to be the easiest, but it only looks simple. That is the downfall because I doubt this mission is going to be as easy as it is looking right now. With something this important there has to be a greater challenge to it, then all of this stuff. :: BladeD gets on the phone with the commander. :: Commander Fuller: Hello. BladeD: Hello commander. This is BladeD. Commander Fuller: Hello BladeD. What gives you the need to call me? BladeD: Well, I have been looking over this map and it is strange. I mean it looks so easy. There is a waiting room that I don’t fully understand why it would be there, there are three bathrooms, there is one area of the building for janitorial stuff, and it looks so easy. Commander Fuller: Don’t let something like that fool you BladeD. It looks like that to make the place look easy, but you have forgotten about all the people that surround that place, all the cameras, and all the other forms of security that are going to keep this mission hard.
