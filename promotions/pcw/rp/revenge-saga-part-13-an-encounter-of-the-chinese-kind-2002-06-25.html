Revenge Saga Part 13: An encounter of the Chinese kind...

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/6/02 Revenge Saga Part 13: An encounter of the Chinese kind… Fleming had established contacted with the Russian Military, via secure channels, over a week ago and it was nearly time for the fateful meeting. It had initially bothered him that he would have to betray his own country in order to gain some level of revenge, but the more he thought about it, the less it bothered him. Before even thinking about contacting the Russian’s, he went to his superiors within the United States Military and suggested that the super human experiment should be terminated. He obviously wanted to kill Crichton for murdering his lover, Ms. Zemoff, but it was obvious to many people that the experiment was lost. They hadn’t been able to re-capture Crichton since he escaped, just after he was injected with the super soldier serum, and even if he was re-captured it was highly debatable that they could hold him for long. After all he had escaped from government control twice already. Still, when Fleming approached the military officials they were still adamant on capturing Crichton, rather than simply eliminating him. They had decided that since the military had put so much money into the project, results were needed to justify the project. ‘On the front line’, so to speak, Fleming knew that this goal was unattainable and decided to look elsewhere for help. The one conclusion he gained from this, was that he had to betray his country, for he couldn’t let Zemoff’s murder go unpunished, he just needed money and backup if need be. He needed enough capital in order to continue the use of the Thunderbolts, he needed enough money to make the necessary weapons to kill Crichton and he needed the money with no questions asked. Enter the Russians. Truth be told, Fleming wasn’t the stereotypical American Nationalist, but he didn’t want to be thought of as a traitor, and in essence he wasn’t. His deal with the Russians wasn’t going to jeopardise national security or anything like that, but he knew that the Military would brand him as a traitor. The Russian’s only wanted the fruitful weapon designs that the Zemoff Corporation would inevitably create for the right price. Essentially his deal with the Russians didn’t involve John Crichton, but it did involve selling advanced weapon systems to Russian. Weapon systems that could essentially be used against the USA in the future. Fleming didn’t live for the future, he lived for now. If World War Three kicked off in the near future using the weapon systems that the Zemoff Corporation had created…well…that was a different problem. Besides, the Zemoff Corporation was still producing weapons systems to the US Military. The US would have more weapon systems that the Russians and therefore would out number the Russians – on weapon systems at least. Fleming’s conscience was clear, as far as he was concerned he wasn’t betraying his country at all. Fleming had been having the same argument in his head for the better part of a hour, while he was waiting for the Russians to arrive at the dock. Fleming was sat in his car, eyes fixed on the location where the meeting should be taking place. They were late. He hated it, when people were late; especially when it was such an important and dangerous meeting. He was beginning to become paranoid. What if the US Military have somehow found out about the meeting? What if the Military were already on their way here? Fleming managed to force the paranoid thoughts from his head and began to concentrate on the task at hand. If the Russians were not here within the next twenty minutes, he would have to assume that he and this whole meeting, was rumbled. ************ Crichton was still reeling from the events of the previous night. Jaden had set fire to nearly all of Crichton’s photos. His photos of Aeryn. The argument was a simple one, an argument Crichton didn’t think mattered too much. But it did matter, it mattered to Jaden and as a result nearly all of his photographs of Aeryn were destroyed. Jaden hadn’t got to the photograph in his wallet. He knew Jaden had problems the day she called him for help. He knew about the pills and the suicide attempt, but still was so attracted to her that he chose to ignore the problems. He could ignore the problems no longer. She had hurt him in a way she could never comprehend. He really had no clue what he would do next. Should he go back and forgive her for her actions? Or should he forget the whole thing and sever all ties with the woman, before he was really hurt? It was a difficult decision to say the least, not one he could make easily. And so he found himself upon the same roof he always went, when difficult decisions had to be made. Granted, usually something or someone interrupted him, but in the end his decision was made. He contributed this to the fact that he thought well under a little pressure, even though he hated the conflict, which was another by-product. People knew that he liked it up here, hell last time, both Kimlar! The Master of Time! And Dan Lyons found him the last time he was up here, so he didn’t doubt that someone else would confront him now. Crichton absently thought about who it could be The Avengers maybe? The Thunderbolts? Hell! It could even be the damn X-Men! He silently contemplated, making his way over to the edge of the building. He half hoped that The Thunderbolts did show up, after all he did have a bone to pick with them. They were involved in the series of events that had led to Aeryn’s death. He had always blamed himself for Aeryn’s death, but since his last confrontation with The Thunderbolts, at the military base, led him to the conclusion that the Thunderbolts were to blame. They were cold-blooded killers and therefore deserved to be punished. As Crichton watched the people below him, scurrying around in they’re seemingly pointless existences, the inevitable began to happen. He heard footsteps, step from the fire escape, up onto the roof and were beginning to close in on him. Crichton simply sighed. Crichton: Well, it looks like I’m gonna get some answers today! Crichton turns and looks at three people, attempting to sneak up on him. He breaks into an uncontrollable laughter. Crichton: Oh Great! The Three Stooges! I haven’t seen you three in a long time! The three Chinese Triads simple look at Crichton, trying to figure out why he wasn’t cowering in fear. If only they knew. Well! I sure as hell ain’t gonna tell ‘em anything! He silently declares, developing a fighting stance. ************ Fleming sat, silently staring at the filthy looking steel wall and sighed, not for the first time in the last hour. The Russians had shepherded him onto their very own frigate and then led him to a confined room, deep within the bowls of the frigate. He was currently waiting for the leader of this little operation. Two very sturdy looking soldiers had been assigned to guard the door and he had to admit that they were intimidating him a little. It was as if he was waiting to be interrogated. Fleming had heard old war stories about Russian interrogations and he hoped that wasn’t the case. Even if it was, he had assured himself backup, by making sure that a couple of The Thunderbolts were ready to storm in if anything were to go sour. Efficient to the last detail. His thoughts were disrupted, as the steel door opened and Colonel Kolof enters the room, signalling for the huge guards to exit the room, as he takes a seat directly opposite Fleming. Colonel Kolof: [Looking up from the papers placed before him upon the table] Sorry for the wait, Mr. Fleming I had some other business to take care of. [His thick Russian accent abundant within his speech] Fleming: [Smiling] I understand Colonel, it happens to me quite a lot. Colonel Kolof: [Returning the smile] Yes, I imagine. Fleming: Right! Now that the small talk is over, let’s get down to business. Colonel Kolof: [Wearing a very different smile now] Agreed! ************ The three Chinese Triads edged closer to Crichton upon the roof of the abandoned warehouse, but Crichton hadn’t moved an inch. He had never been accused of having a good memory, but he recognised the faces of these three, from his last encounter with the Chinese triads. Last time, before he was ever injected with the super soldier serum, he and Aeryn managed to kick all three of they’re asses. So, he had to wonder Did they even know about what happened to him? Were they aware of his new powers? He had assume that they were not aware, otherwise they would have brought more men and a shit load more weapons. Triad 1: Well, if it isn’t our old friend John Crichton! Triad 2: We haven’t seen you for a long while. Crichton: [Nodding with a smile on his face.] Yeah and as I remember the last time we met, I kick all three of your asses. Triad 3: [Smiling] Yes, you and that little bitch of yours! Where is Aeryn? Crichton: [Smile evaporating from his face] No…she died. Triad 1: [His grin growing wider] It couldn’t have happened to a nicer person! That’s when Crichton lost his temper. To Be Continued…
