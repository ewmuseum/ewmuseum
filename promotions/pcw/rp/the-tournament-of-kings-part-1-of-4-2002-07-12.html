The Tournament of Kings...(Part 1 of 4)

12 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-12-2002 (The scene opens into that would seem to be medieval times. People are clad in archaic dress and are gathered in rudimentary bleachers that look upon a grassy field split by a long fence. Different banners fly here and there....One has a lion in gold upon red, others hold other animals in different colors...The camera comes to rest on one that depicts a golden dragon in a crown on a blue background...Where are we, I can hear people asking? Well look around...Looks like a tournament. You've got the fans rooting for their favorite competitors; you have the stadium like atmosphere. From the dress of the spectators, we can tell that it is not a baseball game, if the lack of a diamond didn't tip you off already. We are at a good old-fashioned jousting tournament. Why is there one going on now? Well, we are not going to get into that one...Let's just say that a certain someone has a certain amount of influence and money; not to mention a taste for grander times than we live in now. A man walks down the aisles of the bleachers waving papers. The camera zooms in on the man and he holds one of them up...'The Pendragon Tournament of Kings.'So what all does the tournament entail? Jousting, some swordplay, archery and horsemanship. Not a bad spread for the 21st century. The camera shot switches and we are met with a scene that looks like it is right out of a different era. We have men dressed in armor of all kinds and colors.... Mail, steel, leather. Behind the men in armor trot a few people who we will assume are retainers such as announcers, or shouters, squires, and minstrels. To the right, two men practice the sword with one another in front of a huge row of tents. The tents are all in a perfect line and each one sports a banner much like the ones we have already seen in the grandstand area. At a glance the number of tents appears to be upwards of fifty. Fifty competitors have the sack and the resources to compete in this tourney. The camera slides down the row of tents, passing men who shine armor and weapons, others who are brushing horses, and some practice the sword without a partner. The camera comes to rest on a tent that bears the Golden Crowned Dragon on blue of the family Pendragon. The sponsor of this grand affair, he is also a competitor....After all, that is the only reason he holds the tourney at all...So he can gain glory the old way. The camera moves through the open doorway of the tent. The tent is large, but the contents are not so many...A bed and a table, an oil lamp and some books occupy the room along with an ornate saddle and a golden helmet. The camera pans through the tent as it passes through to the open back flap. Once outside, we see a group of four men. A huge blonde haired man wearing a suit of golden chainmail armor dominates the group...Venneman Pendragon. Another man stands beside him wearing a flowing white shirt and dark blue pants. In one hand, he holds a Lyre...AN archaic stringed instrument. He is the Minstrel of the group. On the other side stands a man in a golden silk shirt, black breeches and high leather boots; Pendragon's announcer. They all watch as a third man tries to draw an immense bow to shoot an arrow at a target that looks to be about one hundred yards away. He wears common brown pants and a drab brown shirt; the Hostler, or horse-handler. He finally gives up and shakes his head. He hands the bow back to Pendragon. Pendragon steps in front of the other two and picks up an arrow. He easily draws the bow and looses the arrow. The shot switches instantly to one that appears to be the target looking back. The arrow point grows larger as it approaches until finally it thuds home. The shot switches and we see that the arrow has hit a perfect bulls-eye. Pendragon smiles and the three other men give him a round of applause. He takes a bow as the minstrel starts to strum the Lyre...) Minstrel: 'Alas the hostler could not draw, the bow of iron wood.....But surely we have seen the might, for Lord Pendragon could....' Hostler: Hey! Minstrel: 'Lord Pendragon of the mighty arm, the mind of wit and charm!!! Muscles formed just like a rock, also a giant....' (Pendragon cuts him off abruptly, though his mood is light. The strange insane light that almost always seems to be in the back of Pendragon's eyes is hardly visible. He looks truly happy...) Venneman: Enough, good Edward...There may be children who see this...You know a camera is always around. Anyhow, I think I like the idea of having someone around to sing of my deeds and prowess. I am glad I thought to hire a minstrel this year. You seem to lighten everyone's mood. Well, except for maybe Peter, my hostler. Come now good Peter...There is not anyone who can draw my bow but me. I know you are strong, but I am always stronger. It is not your fault; it is merely the way that it is. Peter: Aye...I know 'tis. I just canna let myself not try, my lord. I do not wish to disappoint. (Pendragon smiles and puts an arm around Peter...) Venneman: You just make sure that my stallion, Xerxes, is the best looking horse in the tournament....it would not do to allow the king to sit on anything less than the best, would it? After all this is my tournament, and I do plan on winning. Peter: Aye...'Twould not do to have you on a shaggy animal....I'll see to me work, liege. (With that, Peter heads to a small tent off to the side, evidently Venneman's stable tent. Pendragon picks up a few more arrows and shoots them one after another into the target. He points towards what appears to be a small pile of rudimentary Frisbees....Round leather discs that look to be able to fly for some distance. The announcer picks them up and begins launching them into the distance. Pendragon picks them all off after they get out so far...he doesn't want this to be easy. The announcer throws two at once and Pendragon pulls a feather from one arrow and shoots two at the same time. Each arrow lands true. Three discs are then thrown at once. Pendragon pulls a feather from two arrows and shoots three at the same time...Two find the mark and the third sails off into nowhere...) Announcer: You missed one, my liege. (Venneman chuckles a bit as he replies...) Venneman: Ahh Benjamin...Archery has never been my strong suit...The sword and the lance. That is where the action is....That is where you have to get up close and personal with the enemy to win. That is where the true glory is. Anyone can take aim from afar and take down a foe...It takes a warrior to look them in the eye before one strikes the last blow. Glory and honor are never gleaned by bow wielding assassins; strong willed warriors earn them...Knights. (The minstrel starts up again as Venneman finishes his thought...) Edward: 'The shouter with his lightning tongue, tried to make a lark; but like Pendragon's arrow did, the quip did miss the mark...''Lord Pendragon fights the enemy, and bravely stands his ground; while Benjamin the shouter now, doth seem just like the hound...Lord Pendragon!!! He is Lord Pendragon. The BRAVE!!! The Strong...Now hear, my song.......' (Pendragon and Benjamin start laughing as the minstrel sings his song. Pendragon puts the bow down and checks his sword in its scabbard.) Venneman: Let's go have a look at Xerxes, shall we? (The trio heads over to the hostler's tent and pull back the flap. Inside, Peter the Hostler is brushing down an enormous, snow-white stallion. The horse's eyes are fierce and wild, not so much unlike Pendragon's own. Once he spots Pendragon in the group, he calms down and puts his nose toward the floor. Pendragon reaches out and pets the beautiful animal's muzzle...) Venneman: You're ready, aren't you Xerxes? I know...You wanna run off some of that energy...Well, I need to practice the horseman course, so you can have your chance now. Saddle him up and meet me at the course, Peter. Peter: Aye, me liege.... (The shot switches to show Pendragon upon the massive mount, resplendent in golden armor, his blonde hair brushed back from his face, Excalibur in his right hand. With his left, he holds the reigns to the energetic war-horse. The shot pans over the course, which is set up as follows....The course opens with a jump over a brick wall, followed immediately with a sharp turn to the right. After a short straightaway, the course takes a left and on the right hand side is a watermelon on a pole. The object of this obstacle being to cut said watermelon in half. Almost right after this is a jump over a stream that cuts through the course. Ahead on the left is another watermelon, this one a little lower than the first one. A sharp right brings us to yet another jump, this one over a log. A final straightaway passes six watermelons, three to a side. The first on the right, head level...First on the left, leg level...Second on the right, leg level, second on the left, eye level. The third on the right is high and the last one on the left is on the ground. The object is to pick this one up, not chop it. And then, the finish line.) Benjamin: Looks like the course is extra tough this year, my lord...Especially that last obstacle. Venneman: Aye, it is. Last year was not enough of a challenge. Hmmm....I think I will just run Xerxes through the course a couple of times to let him get a feel for it...No since using my sword for his benefit... (With that, Venneman sheathes the sword and takes off through the course. Xerxes performs flawlessly over the jumps and is lightning fast through the turns and straight-aways. Pendragon's hair flies back in the wind and his eyes are bright blue orbs. It almost looks as if he has found a little bit of heaven on earth...Ahh, the glorious days of old. If only they would stay around forever...The Minstrel picks up again as the scene begins to fade...) Edward: 'The mighty Lord Pendragon he, the Knight out of the past; mightiest in the world today, his rule will ever last................' >>>>>>>>>>>>>TO BE CONTINUED
