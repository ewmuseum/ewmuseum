Movie Pre-Premier Party

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/11/02 11:26 PM A house party is being held at a Boston Market in Lake Worth, FL. In fact, a pre-premier party for the new movie The End. Seinfeld actor Wayne Knight (I don't care what KWW thinks, NEWMAN MADE SEINFELD!) joins The End, who can't seem to wear anything other than his black ring attire. The use of Boston Market for the party would seem to indicate that this is in a way a jab at Kramer WildWing. Many extras are on hand for the event. The only person of any noteworthy importance is Anna Keyson, in her usual black leather suit, with purple make-up all over. She sits over at the counter which serves as the bar for the party, drinking a margarita. Keyson snaps fingers and points to her empty glass, and the white apron-clad bartender (Tom Cruise, preparing for Cocktail, Part 2) refills her glass while smiling at her and winking. Keyson returns the flirting with by flipping him off. Also sitting at the bar is Rent-A-Hero, who is getting yelled at by Naomi Campbell (wearing a blue two-piece bathing suit for no apparent reason, not that we're complaining) from Coyote Ugly. Campbell:'I don't care how much you beg, we don't have virgin drinks!' RAH:'Well then, Model-Woman, may I at least have a glass of water?' Naomi Campbell jumps up on the counter with a fire hose in hand. Campbell: *shouting as a general address*'Hey! Do we serve water at this bar?' Wayne Knight stands up from his seat playing X-Box Dead Or Alive 3 with Jordan X Shabazz (all decked out in a regular rental tuxedo, though his doesn't have barbeque stains on it). Knight:'Actually, we do. County regulations.' Knight has his finger pointed up in the air to bring this to Campbell's attention. He gets doused with the house for his troubles. The model hops down off the bar, muttering curses under her breath, and flings a bottle of Zephyrhills water at RAH, which he manages to catch. RAH turns back around, and speaks with Anna Keyson sitting next to him. RAH:'So, Purple One, it appears we both have matches on Turmoil. I, the Rent-A-Hero, shall team with former PCW champion and fellow good sport Jason Snow to face Geriatric Giant and Wet-Behind-The-Ears Boy... a contest as lopsided as Tonya Harding versus Paula Jones... They want to call themselves Politically Incorrect. Ha! what they fail to understand is that the world is steadily moving away from such notions as Political Incorrectness as fast as it is moving away from its belief that they can't believe it's not butter, or that Diet Dr. Pepper does taste like regular Dr. Pepper. Both of them, old and young, are obsolete, slowly dying out as the entire world society turns their back on them like Paul McCartney shuns hot dogs. Yet they continue to disturb the peace with their raucous antics. They form their stable, their Injustice League, to prevent other mild-mannered and good-natured workers from enjoying a pleasant environment in which to perform. Which is why your friendly neighborhood Kryptonian Super-Saiyan must fight them off with strength, speed, skill, my Kamehameha Wave of Destruction, my Yo-Yo of Mild Discomfort, and Star Shield... wait, I don't have a star shield... and my Biznitch Slap O' DOOM~! to end PI's reign of terror as quickly as all sensible comic book fans ended the Chyna collection's reign of terror, only in my match with PI, no fire or harsh acids shall be involved.' Anna Keyson reaches into her boot and pulls out a sharp pick-blade. She crosses it over her throat, and then points it menacingly at Rent-A-Hero. Keyson:'You have three seconds left to live.' Rent-A-Hero looks over to his left, and then back at Keyson nervously. He looks back over to the left and speaks in an aside more to himself so as not to further upset Keyson. RAH:'Uh... Look! Is that an X-Box?' RAH hurries away. Phillip Michael Thomas comes up (dressing in his old trendy gray suit with blue shirt that he wore in his Miami Vice days) and starts trying to talk with Anna Keyson. Thomas:'Hello. Are you in the movie, too? What part do you play? I'm lonely.' Keyson punches Thomas hard in the nose, breaking it and knocking him out. Thomas falls limp onto the floor. Keyson stands on the body and continues drinking. Syn and Duzza are arm-wrestling over one of the tables. Syn wears some jeans and a black X-Men T-shirt. Duzza has on a tuxedo, but it's two sizes too small. Ricky Thunder and Jesse Ramey betting on who will win. Dante Jackson is coaching Duzza to make sure he'll win. Dante:'Put your wrist into it!' Duzza:'Shut up, n'gga! I'm trying to beat 'dis cracka foo'!' Syn gains the advantage over Duzza and pushes his hand closer to the table. Thunder:'I told you, Ramey, Syn is the better man.' Ramey:'Duzza's managed to survive in whatever feds by waiting until he's about to lose, then making a big comeback.' Thunder:'Or by waiting until he's about to lose, then lose in terrible fashion.' Ramey:'... Shut up.' Duzza's hand moves ever closer to the table. Dante Jackson continues to attempt to stir up Duzza. Dante:'Come on, man! You gonna lose to this wigga? What would R. Kelly say?' Duzza looks up to the ceiling and thinks about what his idol, R. Kelly, would say about Duzza's predicament. In Duzza's mind, R. Kelly appears to his right, standing tall in a New York Knicks jersey and some Fubu black denim shorts. Duzza:'It's you!' R. Kelly:'Yeah. You need my help.' Duzza:'Yeah, I'm about to lose to this big cracka over here.' With his free hand, Duzza points to Syn while looking up at the imaginary R. Kelly. R. Kelly:'I tell you, Malek, don't sweat this wigga. Nah, look over there at that shortie.' Duzza turns his attention to his rear left, where a Puerto Rican woman in tight skirt and see-through shirt is dancing to Nelly's 'Hot in Here.' R. Kelly:'See that hot ass?' Duzza nods his head and licks his lips. Duzza:'That is one fine ass.' R. Kelly:'Go tap that ass, my brotha. You want that ass?' Duzza:'I want that ass.' R. Kelly:'Say it again.' Duzza:'I want that ass.' R. Kelly:'Say it loud, brotha! Say it with conviction!' Duzza stands up, his arm still locked with Syn's, and turns in the direction of the woman, shouting at the top of his lungs. Duzza:'I WANT THAT ASS!' The party stops, everyone turning to Duzza. Dante:'Duzz, man, whatchu talkin' bout?' All in the party are so shocked that even Syn has stopped, staring wide-eyed though blankly at Duzza. Duzza realizes that he has been talking to an imaginary figure. He quickly sits back down and slams Syn's hand to the table while he ponders in vain Duzza's cathartic release. Duzza walks away. After a few more moments, Kid Rock (who cares what he's wearing, he's Kid Rock!) starts the music back up. With everyone eating rotisserie chicken and drinking Fanta (bring it, Chef, Kannon; Borro's alright, since he realizes its awesomeness), The End calls Wayne Knight (his penguin suit having a few spills of buffalo wing barbecue sauce on left breat pocket, and more spills on the shirt lapel) outside in the cool, moist night air. Knight:'What's up? You seem down.' End paces back and forth on the sidewalk with his head at the ground and his hands in his pockets. Seeing a stone by his foot, he kicks it. End:'I just wish I had people care enough to come to these parties that I throw.' End gets a pat on the shoulder from his short, chubby sidekick. Knight:'What are you talking about? There are a ton of people in there! It's a packed house!' End looks at Knight with a serious face. End:'I mean, I wish people would come to parties and I don't have to hire them to fill the room.' Knight:'Oh. That. You know, you really shouldn't worry about those things. No one's ever liked you, and it's hard to believe that's going to change anytime soon. Just live your sham of a life while you still have a job to support it.' Knight coaxes a smile out of End. End:'Yeah, you're right.' Knight and End pull on the door handle to rejoin the party, but it won't budge. End:'They locked us out!' Wayne shrugs, as if he's not surprised. Knight:'People were ignoring us all night, and it's not like you hired them to spend to with you, just to come to your parties.' Knight and End sit down on the sidewalk, putting their hands under the chins. There they sit, silent, not doing anything. After a while, End pulls some jacks and a superball out of his pocket, and starts playing with them. End:'Wanna try?' Knight says nothing, only slaps End on the back of the head. 2:55 AM End:'Wow, they're making this an all-nighter.' Knight:'You think we should leave?' A pause. End:'... No.'
