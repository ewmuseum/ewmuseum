Date: not entered The night was fair…brisk and chilly… Much like the heart of evil that plagued this fair town… It’s tough to be a hero…you have no idea. Night after night, day after day…it’s a full time job. Heroism may be a dangerous line of work, but none is more fulfilling. Seeing the smiles on those people’s faces, hearing their thanks and gratitude for a job well done…vanquishing the foe that threatens their lives… Heroism… it’s not much different than wrestling. It’s my position as a wrestler to go to the ring and do my job, night after night, day after day… it’s a full time job, though technically I work only two nights a week. Seeing the smiles on the people’s faces…hearing their cheers of appreciation… gratitude for a job well done…vanquishing the foes that threaten their entertainment and joy. And it would be a never-ending job… one that I could never quit, or be fired from. Because the people deserve to be safe…the people deserve to be entertained… And Miss Moonlight is the hero for the job. ------=------- “All right! One! Two! Three! GO!” Naomi threw out a right, followed by a left hook and a right uppercut before she spun around with a blurred spin kick. The heavy sandbag hanging in the corner of her bedroom swung from the impact her powerful leg made, nearly thudding into the wall. Naomi came out with a few more sidekicks to the side of the bag before nailing the top of the bag with a hard straight punch. The ceiling shuddered from impact, the girl backing away before she continued. Naomi was a piece of work, no doubt about it. 5’10 and 200 pounds (or 177.8 centimeters and 75 kilograms, given Japan’s metric system) of tough, sexy woman. Most men were intimidated by an athletic woman, but Naomi wasn’t one of those macho chicks who’s testosterone levels were off the charts. Naomi had feminine graces, not to mention she was beautiful. Her body was in proportion entirely, slim hips, ample high C chest, long legs… toned to perfection. And it was that athleticism that helped her in her activities outside of college. She bobbed on her toes, wearing a pair of tight black short shorts and a black sports bra with black tape on her hands. Her long black hair, tied up behind her in a ponytail, bobbed as well like a pom-pom. Her chest rose and fell steadily, her stamina showing through as she kept her eyes on the bag. “Keep focused…” she muttered to herself in a near whisper. “You get distracted for one second, and it’s all over. There’s no room for error in this business.” She thought of two things as she concentrated… The first was her destiny… as Miss Moonlight, protector of Tokyo. The fair city was too beautiful, and too grand to stand unprotected from the scum and vile villainy that plagues the land. The police? They didn’t have the resources to take care of things promptly. All they did was paperwork, some jail time, and some fines, and the criminal element would be back out on the street. There’s no lesson learned whatsoever… Miss Moonlight was going to change that. If she had to beat each and every thug, brute, rapist, and thief down out there on the streets, then so be it. She had the skills…she had the means…she had the determination to do so. So why not don a mask and costume and protect the citizens of a city you love so much? If you’re passionate about doing something…do it, and do it until you get it done. That brought us to the next thing Naomi thought of: Wrestling. As Miss Moonlight, protector of Primetime Championship Wrestling, it was her job to make sure that the people were represented by a hero… a champion… a person of honor and justice that knew how to take care of the evildoers and heels that tried to take control of their fair company. She was determined to do her best to represent the people… fight for the people… win for the people. She had her debut match coming up soon… a battle royal for the Cruiserweight championship. Big names all over the place, people who’ve been in PCW a lot longer than she has…people who’re getting their first chance at a belt that deserves to be represented well. Miss Moonlight was a shadow in this match… no one expected her to fare well at all. She was a rookie…a newbie… someone who had to pay her dues in this company before earning something as great as the PCW Cruiserweight championship. Well… she had a thing or two about dues… She’s trained for years in amateur wrestling, trained for years in professional wrestling. She started out when she was fourteen training, working small jobs in the Tokyo Wrestling League dojo until she was prepared to fight her first match. Then from there on she moved up into better things… becoming a female puroresu star overnight. She’s not a rookie…not by a long shot… she’s proven herself in Japan…and she’d prove herself in America in PCW by winning the Cruiserweight belt and her debut match over everyone else. Naomi took a deep breath, centering herself before lashing out with a flying spinning back roundhouse kick to the sandbag. The bag swung violently to the side and crashed into the wall, a huge boom resonating throughout the entire apartment building. She landed in a crouching position and looked up…watching the bag swing back and forth with a steady gaze. Then there was a knock at the door. She sighed, knowing exactly who it was by the force and repetition of the knocks. Grabbing a towel to wipe the sweat off her body, she walked from her bedroom through the living room to answer the front door. She looked out with a smile… “Yes?” Standing there was an older man, wearing a black suit with a white shirt and black tie. He looked at Naomi with a tired look in his eyes. “Hi… I was wondering if maybe you could…um…stop all the noise that’s going on here?” Naomi nodded, her smile still on her face. “Sure thing…I was just working out. I didn’t think I was bothering anyone.” The man smoothed his hair back and nodded, rather nervous at the sight of the large and attractive woman. “Uh yeah…you are. I’ve been working all day and I’m trying to review some papers, but I just can’t seem to get things done with your working out here. I’m sorry.” He began walking away, Naomi watching him with a confused look on her face. She shook her head, a smile on her face before closing the door behind her. “Maybe I should find my training elsewhere…” ------=------ The man in the black suit sat at his desk studying his papers from his office intently, the streetlights shining through the window in front of him. The look in his eyes told you that he was drowsy… but as soon as he began to drift off something dark and shadowy landed in front of his window. “AAAAH!!” He flew back, hitting the floor hard in his chair. Miss Moonlight turned around and peeked into the room by cupping her hands around her eyes. “Sorry!!” she said, waving to the man before running off. He looked up, a flustered look on his face and sighed hard. “I gotta move to a new apartment.” ------TO=BE=CONTINUED------
