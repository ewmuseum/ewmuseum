Kidnapped?!-Part 2: Consequences for the Divine

27 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/27/02 The shot goes to the same blackness as the last promo that we saw Rush in. In fact, that is exactly what we need to talk about to get a few things straight. Last time we saw Rush, he was busy minding his own godly business, when he was kidnapped by two followers of a man known only as master. Now he is unconscious and in their hold, things aren’t looking good. This master seems to want to teach Rush a thing or two about acting godly. The scene opens up on a dark amphitheater like circle, filled with stone seats all around, the smallest noise emanating all around. And that’s what can be heard right now. The smallest sound. The labored breathing, not wheezing, but soft breaths of air against the cold stone. Rush has finally awoken from his tantalizing painful sleep. However, having sustained two shots from a lead pipe already in the day, getting up may be more a task then usual, even after a brutal match. Rush continued to try and get up, pushing up off the ground, but it is too much at the moment for Rush, and he begins to cough up deep red blood onto the ground, clumps coming out and staining the gray stone. As he lifts his head a small amount from the ground, he sees a pair of feet right near his head. He angles his head as far back as possible, trying to see the face of his captor. However, it is the master, and his black hood hangs over his face, shrouding it into darkness. Master: So, I see you have awakened from your sleep. However, you have not come into any better care then the terrifying dreams that I know haunt you. For at a time, they haunted me as well. Rush stares right at the face of the man, or where he believes the man’s face to be. For a long time, there is silence in the circle of stone. The only sound are the two different breaths, but the master’s breathing almost makes a hissing as he inhales. Rush: I’m not like you. You see, I’m a god, not a mere mortal anymore. Even as the Greatest Man Alive, I was never as powerful as I am now. A god will never be defeated, except by deceit and trickery. In fact, that’s the only way that I’m going to lose the March Madness match. The master, his two sleeves put together, parts the two sleeves, to reveal a very strange sight. His hands are not normal; in fact, they’re far from it. They have a strange glow in the darkness, like a reflection where there is no light. However, that’s just the beginning. His hands are a scaly texture, scratching together to make a horrendous sound. Finally, where a normal human would have fingernails, there are long, hook-like claws. Rush sees this and gulps, trying not to show fear if he can avoid it. Master: So you see, you and I are not far alike. You will see that I am just as powerful, and more powerful that you in many ways. You have just begun to explore your vast, endless potential, but I can show you how to tap that energy to become a true warrior. Confused by these words, Rush tries once again to get up, maybe to be ready to defend himself. However, the result is the same, and he ends up coughing up more blood, his wrists still to stiff and sore to support a human body’s weight. The master, no emotion on the void of a face of his, extends a disgusting freakish hand to Rush. Rush just stares into the abyss of the cloak, as he balances himself enough to sit up. Finally, he takes the hand within his own, and is pulled up to his feet, which are wobbly as he first rises, but he quickly is able to support himself. Master: Come, we have much to talk about. I seeked you across the world, far and wide. How strange to think that I could’ve just come here to this town you call home and waited for you to come right to me. Rush: Come to you?! I was kidnapped and brought here against my will. I’m not sure how that slipped your mind to apologize for either, but in no way did I come to you. You arranged all this stuff, not me. Master: Of course, how could I be so clumsy with words? What I meant is that fate has brought us together. You have come to be taught, and I waited for an apprentice worthy of my teachings. Fate, delivered for us both. Rush thinks about this for a moment, and analyzing this statement, finds it completely false. However, he’s not going to get into a heated argument with this man who has claws and many followers. Master: You see, Rush, if you and I were to work together, we could be more then just mortals. You could be god-like. Rush: For the last time, I’m already a god. I don’t need to be your apprentice. A guy already made me a god, and his name is Akiyama. If you wanna fuck with him, be my guest but he’s not in the best of moods and I don’t think you wanna spend the rest of your life in the void. The Crunk as Hell *God * doesn’t need this blasphemy from a criminal like yourself, so I’ll be going now. Rush turns to walk away from the man. He starts up the stone seats to the edge, but suddenly he keels over and starts to write on the stone in pain. The master laughs evilly on the other side and watches on as Rush, trying to grab himself to stop the shaking, is tortured nearby. Master: I’m not asking you anymore, I’m not trying to make you my apprentice, I’m Telling you that you will be taught that only those who are truly powerful can be called gods. You are a little bug, pestering me on my quest to rule over all those who have turned to their God for support. Oh, and that little sting you’re feeling all over your body, that’s the power of a Real God. The shaking of Rush abruptly stops, but he can only hold his ribs, nowhere near close to getting up. The master adds a kick into his already injured ribs to make his point even more crystal clear. He walks away, leaving Rush rolling in pain, holding his injured ribs as two men walk over and pick up Rush, who blacks out from the pain… The next day is a beautiful one, as the sun is rising slowly towards its pinnacle high above the stone building. The flag outside blows softly, in the light wind that is coming from the east. In the day, it can be seen that the building is disguised as a large mansion, but its real intentions are already partly known from the previous night’s events. The shot switches to an office, which is rather plain, with two sets of shelves in the back of the room and some papers and magazines scattered over the desk, which also has a flat screen monitor and a keyboard that is rolled out on a lower level of the desk. On the shelves are manila folders containing many papers. We can also see the man who talked to Rush in the car is standing in front of a large tall-backed office chair that is placed near a desk. The man standing in front of the desk is wearing a navy blue coat over a collared blue-striped shirt, tucked into his pleated khakis, which go down and cover part of his shiny brown dress shoes. The man is visibly scared by the figure that sits in the chair across the room from him; His knees and arms are shaking with every breath he takes. That is the only sound in the room until the man behind the chair breaks the silence with his deep, mysterious, yet familiar sounding voice. Man in Chair: I see you’ve come to bother me, Damien. What is it that you want from your God? You saw what I did to that blasphemous pig last night; I don’t think you want to suffer the same fate. Damien: N-n-no, sir. I just came to tell you tha-th-t-that- Man in Chair: Stop stuttering and get to it! The man’s voice boom throughout the room, causing vibrations on the desk and the shelves. Damien is now even more scared and he begins to hold him arm to stop it from shaking but nothing can hide his quivering knees. Damien replies, talking into the ground. Damien: He, he escaped, Master. Master: What, I can’t hear you son, speak up when addressing an immortal, especially one of such power! The man seems ready to break down and start sobbing. His whole body quakes now with every breath. He takes out some of his nervousness by smoothing out his shirt, but the Master still needs his answer. Damien: I’m sorry! We just found the room empty today and another of your servants dead! He’s nowhere to be found, we searched everywhere! The man behind the chair, who we know realize is the same individual who handed out Rush’s punishment the night before, starts to breath heavily, each breath becoming louder and louder. His hands now grip the chair harder and harder. However, his voice keeps its same level of placidity. Master: Do you mean to say that the blasphemous one has escaped, due to the fault of you and your bumbling idiot men? You know what, just find him. Damien: What if we can’t? Master: You will. You will not be alive to find out the consequences if you don’t find him. Go! The man in the chair spins around but the shot is already fading to the next one and the face is blurred. The shot opens up next, on a lonely highway. The only car on the highway is black BMW M5. The rims on the car, at least 19’s from what can be seen, whirr and hum as they go around and around as the car gains speed. The shot goes to the inside of the car, and it’s Rush, with a wide grin on his face, one hand on the steering wheel and the other out the window. But he doesn’t know that he hasn’t escaped yet from Master Back outside the car, there is another car pulling up towards him. He looks back to see a large black Hummer trailing him. He sees that more cars of the same type have formed a blockade further up the road. Cursing at himself, he looks around the road for any options, but none seem to be there. Heavy forest surrounds Rush on both sides, and there is almost no space to navigate around the roadblock, as the car behind him gains speed. Seeing that he has no options, he makes a very strange decision. Rush: I know what I gotta do. I guess we’ll find out if I really am a god now. I pray to me that I make it out of this shit alive. Well, it’s now or never. Rush, at the wheel, suddenly turns the car, trying to turn around, but the speed of the car and the sharpness of the turn are perpendicular to each other, each one trying to cancel the other out. With this, Rush’s BMW goes into a swerve, but he’s still holding onto the gas. He tries to switch into reverse, and hits the gas as he does, but this sends his car barreling right through the guardrail and plummeting through the forest. The car narrowly misses a lake but goes right off the edge of a steep rock face. Rush frantically tries to hit the brakes, but the car continues its slide down the rocky slope. It also continues to pick up speed. There is nothing he can do, but watch in horror as the car smashes into the rocks at the bottom with a sickening crash. Up above, the Master’s minions are watching on as this turn of events unfolds. Damien looks but then looks away and cross his arms. His eyes now seem to have a strange glow to them. Damien: Go check the crash site. He may not be a god, but he’s a resilient bastard. I want any evidence of this crash burned and the body burned. If he’s not dead, then you know what to do… A short, pudgy, man tries to argue with Damien, afraid of what could happen if they broke the law at such a magnitude. Man: But sir, that’s murder. Damien: I know damn well what it is. Now go do it. I will not fail the Master, and I suggest you do the same. The men start walking off through the forest, as Damien watches them go. As they leave, a smile breaks his face, but for what reason, it’s not clear yet. He begins to chuckle to himself and gets into the car. As he drives off, somewhere down below, a beating heart stops but nothing can be seen, as we fade to black…
