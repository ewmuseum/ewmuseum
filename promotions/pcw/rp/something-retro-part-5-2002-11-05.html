Something Retro (Part 5)(

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 5, 2002 [We return from the commercial break for “The Alabama Bat Fetcher 500” and we return to Gladiator Arena, to see that the floor has been vacated except for four raised and numbered sections. In the northern part of the arena, section one is situated. In the south, marker number four. On either side of the arena markers two and three, and lastly on that floor, four large steel spheres.] Larry: Welcome back, and up next is our “Rage in the Cage”…Atlasphere! Mike: In this event, our two contenders will run around in these 450 pounds spheres, trying to go over the scoring markers on the floor here, they have to do this as much as they can within sixty seconds. However, guarding these scoring markers is two of our gladiators. For each marker that the contenders score, they score two points. Tonight we have Gemini and Laser as the guardians of the scoring pods. Larry is down with Laser right now. [We go down from the action shots to the floor where we see Larry with Laser just outside of his sphere.] Larry: Laser…Earlier tonight you set out a challenge to one of our finalists, in Eddie Mandrake. What will be your goal for this event? Laser: I will ram him so hard, I will force him to land on his head…I will paralyze this asshole, just like he did Hawk! He will pay! This I will guarantee! Larry: Uh…Okay, Laser. Good luck. [Larry walks off, as Laser loads himself up in the sphere. We return to the action cameras with Mike’s commentary.] Mike: r against the gladiators all day. I sure hope that his brutal style can handle that large sphere. I guess we’ll see right now because we are ready to go. Larry #2: Contenders ready! Gladiators Ready! (WHISTLE!) Larry: And off we go…Laser and Eddie are making a bee-line right for each other, while Rico and Gemini as pairing off. Mike: This is exciting, who will veer off first from this imminent collision between Eddie and Laser… CLOOOOONG! HISSSSSSS! Larry: Rico scored a point! Mike: While Eddie was sent flying by that collision! MY GOD! Larry: While Rico is making Gemini look slow and sluggish. HISSSSSSS! Larry: And he scored another point! Mike: Forty-Five seconds remaining, Laser is still keeping Eddie at bay at the center, while Rico is off to the races to scoring pod number four. HISSSSSSS! Larry: And another two points for Rico! Mike: Eddie is finally moving again after Laser kept him at bay, he’s heading for pod number one. Will he get there? HISSSSSS! HISSSSSS! Larry: He does and Rico gets another as well… KLOOOOONG! Mike: OH SHIT! Larry: EDDIE WENT FLYING INSIDE THAT STEEL BALL! Mike: Damn, I have never seen anyone fly like that since…well….EVER! Larry: Thirty seconds left in the event, and Rico is on a major roll! Mike: And Eddie has ground to a dead stop, and he is not moving too quickly. Laser is poised to strike again. Larry: Man, Gemini isn’t what he used to be. Mike: Laser is still the same. Rico heading to pod number three… HISSSSSS! Larry: Rico scores again! Mike: We don’t here that very often. Larry: We do when he’s with men… Mike: GAH! HISSSSSS! Mike: Fifteen seconds left and Rico has done and given himself another two points! Larry: Damn this is turning into a major blow out. Mike: STOP WITH THE INNUENDO! Eddie is still pinned against the wall near pod number one. Rico is still rolling along looking to get more points here. Larry: Somehow I don’t see Eddie getting any more points here. It seems that Laser has embedded Eddie against the wall, while Gemini is being made a fool of here…AGAIN! Crowd: 5, 4, 3, 2, 1… (WHISTLE!) Mike: And that’s it…Eddie just popped out of his sphere…Uh-oh, he just ripped the hatch off of Laser’s sphere…OH SHIT! Larry: FIGHT~! Mike: Eddie is raining down punches to Laser, and the Gladiators are trying to get in so they can separate the two! Looks like Larry Thompson has managed to get in there to try to get them out…All the male gladiators are out there…and they are pushing the sphere over to try to empty them out of it... Larry: It works. They are out. Mike: In all seriousness now, we are going to try to gain some comments from Eddie Mandrake and Laser. Human Cannonball is slated to be next, so stay tuned, you don’t want to miss what’s coming up next.
