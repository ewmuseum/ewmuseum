Brawl In A Mall (Gaz Green vs. Natalia Miyagawa Round 2)

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27 November 'Isn't that Gaz Green!?!' 'Whoa-... PCW's Gaz 'The Jester' Green!' These were the murmurs of the surrounding public, our setting, a simple shopping mall set somewhere in the heart of Las Vegas, away from the lighting spectacle people picture when regarding the City Of Sin. Well this outing in the ongoing saga of Pranks With Attitude saw the Jester in a reverse role, from throwing his weight around in the squared circle to throwing his money in a spending bonanza. 'THE JESTER~!' Another cry from the densely populated shopping mall bellows over all other activity, to Gaz however it is this showering of interest that authenticates just how far he has come from being a mere student in Primetime Academy to the household name he has became in his short tenure with Primetime Championship Wrestling. Standing covered in shopping bags, basking in the public eye and enjoying his time in the spot light, Gaz Green oozed with arrogance, not so much egotistical, but this fussing has came from blood, sweat and tears. It has been well documented that this lavish life-style has indeed came from hard work and solid graft, to Gaz though, these days almost feel like a huge pay off. 'I certainly turn heads-....' 'But which way, huh?' injected a sweet and distinctive voice. Obscured by the 226 lbs. frame of Gaz 'The Jester' Green, it is clear who owns the voice. Now in full-blown view, Natalia stands holding a catalogue pose, showcasing a slender Russian physique and rolling her eyes, she knows first hand about attracting interest. 'Oh, so you’re done spending my money!' replied Natalia's client waving his hand through his dyed green hair, almost competing with his valet for attention. 'Not quite!' whispered the separated wife of former PCW performer Alexander Miyagawa through her soft crimson red lips. With the hoard of bags swaying violently, bulging with items, expensive items mind you. From clothing, jewellery, colon, items that would make any hard working person drool with envy, surfacing their inner green-eyed monster and almost teasing the hopefully watching Tim Walden into a tax frenzy. The Front Office's Financial Reclamation Officer has certainly lived up to his job description, taxing the very wealth of the roster and installing enough resources for his stable mate CEO Xavier Kannon to buy out the competing RUSH brand. 'More shopping?' sighed the Emerald Menace, struggling to keep the dead weight of the bags from hitting the tiled floor of the shopping mall, his battle shown in the stress of his face. 'This is nothing compared to what Alex and me bought during our shopping trips!' continued the petite Russian glancing over to her meal ticket. 'Talking of Alex, heard anything of interest from him?' asked the inquisitive Jester trying to jog the conversation along, not really caring greatly for his former stable mate but making the effort nonetheless for Natalia's sake. Chatting as they waltzed happily over to another set of stores, Natalia responded to Gaz Green's question. 'Not really. Everything at Alliances was new to me, everything from having his cranium encased in metal, making him the obvious choice for the lead role if producers ever decide to resurrect Robocop to signing with Ohmygodthatfederationreally Sucksat Wrestling, OSW!' Giving the Alexander Miyagawa conversation the cold shoulder treatment, both parties wanted to shop in two different stores, meaning Natalia wandered off unattended whilst her client Gaz Green opted to checkout men’s clothing giants 'Top Man'. Pushing open the large glass-panel doors and entering an utopia of designer labelled clothing, Gaz stood speechless, clinging to his bags he stormed forward. 'Err-... Arr-... Excuse me sir, excuse me!' said a voice from behind. Pivoting on the spot and standing before a beautiful blonde, her assets level par to that of PCW's ditzy diva Eleanor Kannon-Hall, the Jester twitched his eyes for a second opinion, mouth dropping, he turned to a stuttering mess. 'Err-... Arr-... Well-... Hmm-... Hey there!' 'Sir, upstairs is the women’s section, the section for men has been relocated down stairs!' retorted the shop clerk with a smile; arm out stretched as she pointed to a flight of stairs. 'Well who's to say that I wasn't going to buy my girlfriend lingerie, huh?!?' proclaimed the Lord Of Laughter, counting the clerks early judgement, a little cocky for a man who was impersonating Gareth Gates only seconds earlier. 'OH!!!' 'You know what they say sweet cheeks, Two's company and-...' said Gaz Green, reciting the parable and hinting for the assistant to finish the latter half of the sentence. 'And three's a crowd!' 'Two's company and three's my fantasy!' injected the Jester, with a display of human flirting, winking he set hotfooted down the stairs and into a room filled to the brim with various clothes racks, catering for all tastes. Jerking about, still laden with bags, making a welcome change from being overwhelmed with 50 lbs. of gold, namely his PCW Tag Team Championship, our party host browsed through rack after rack, his eyes frantically swaying side to side. Gaz manhandling garments, continued to search for this elusive piece of clothing he desires when suddenly he stood triumph, a green business jacket held aloft. 'FOUND IT~!' roared the man labelled the Prince Of Pranks. Gleaming at his prize and drawing some odd stares from fellow shoppers, he almost leapt out of his loafer shoes as a hand crashed down on his broad shoulder. 'WHAT THE FU-....' Spinning on the spot, a picture perfect re-enactment of pop idol Michael Jackson, Gaz threw himself at his assailant, who was to be unveiled as the long-suffering Natalia Miyagawa. The Russian beauty stared disgustingly at the dangling jacket, something she has seen her colleague wear a thousand times before, well maybe not this particular one but not a great deal different neither. 'Urgh - Have you no sense of style!' bellowed the Pranks With Attitude saviour, the women who restored the gold around the waists of Gaz Green and Noscosto Ireul Nerezza. Taking the not so subtle hint, the item returned to the rack. Looking almost solemn, Gaz's face began to glow like a child on Christmas Day as he spotted something in the corner of his eye, racing over he snatched and accelerated to the cashier desk with a box, it's content still a mystery. 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO~!' hissed Natalia. Parting with a wad of notes, Gaz returned to the faithful side of his valet. The box now concealed within a bag that accompanies the hoard of other bags he currently sports, he subtly grinned and concluded his charade. 'Before you ask - It's for Nerezza, a gift, I'm a giving guy, if you don't believe me then ask my endless list of conquests-....' said Gaz before an untimely interruption from Ms. Miyagawa who shook her head in a pendulum motion. 'Your seriously warped, demented even, hell Skitzo has more sense than, YOU!!!' 'Now that's below the belt. Also it was a little vague, after all there is twelve Skitzo's, didn't you watch Alliances2K2?' questioned the prankster to his irate manager who stormed off, her feet crashing down on the stairs like blasts of thunder. The camera bobbing about like something out of the Blair Witch Project pursued the squabbling couple, collective, couple being a little strong in view of the circumstances. Anyway with a bickering session well underway, Gaz tried desperately to resolve the issue, his kneecaps beaten harshly by the constant thud of the bags. With a concluding yelp from Gaz 'The Jester' Green the scene slowly drew to a close, leaving nothing but a snowy picture of static. 'LET'S TALK ABOUT THIS OVER A BITE TO EAT, HOW DOES THAT SOUND SQUIRE?!?' 'And I'm not your damn 'SQUIRE' neither, hmph!' - To be continued -
