Role Models?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Happy Fourth to the USA. We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he and his ever faithful crime fighting partner, the Masochist, return home to Stately Steele Manor, giddy from the violence of a long night’s training/policing. Big Alan Steele: (posturing) “Ha ha ha!! Huzzah!! Another night of well-executed maneuvers that are sure to increase my stamina and endurance within the gladiatorial ring! Hi-Keeba!” The Masochist: “Yeah… that group of robotic ninja assassins was particularly difficult.” BAS: “InDEED!” tM: “But that part with the monkey was really funny!!” BAS: “Again, I concur!! Now, a repast to sate our appetites and head blissfully into the waiting arms of Morpheus! A good night’s rest is most definitely in order…” “Hey, uh, Big Al? Got a minute?” The voice of trusted manager calls out from the other room, both men compelled to turn and stare. In the living room, Sue sits calmly, a cup of java resting in her hand, a cigarette smoldering in an ashtray on the coffee table. Both Big Alan Steele and the Masochist’s blood are chilled to the bone. tM: (whispering) “I don’t like the looks of this!” BAS: (also whispering) “Nor I… tis far too late for her to usually remain awake.” Sue: (very evenly) “This will only take a minute, guys.” The two men shiver. tM: “Well, what do we have to lose? Maybe it’s nothing…” BAS: “We go in tandem, I shall take the front and you cover the rear…” tM: “Gotcha.” Big Al and the Masochist slowly, warily make their way inside the room, eyeing the surroundings carefully. As they get nearer and nearer, they can see the beautiful and blonde Graces already inside, a red flush to their cheeks. As the men cross the threshold completely, the Graces circle around them, cutting off the exit. Steele eyes open wide in panic, as the Masochist grips his toga in unadulterated fear. tM: “Game over man!! We’re trapped!!” BAS: (grim) “This does not bode well for us, Lord of Pain!” Sue: (calm, collected) “Relax guys. All I want to do is ask you two one simple, easy, honest question.” Our two male heroes tense up, sweat beading on their foreheads with anticipation… Sue: “What’s the deal with you GUYS?” Nervous looks are exchanged between Steele and the Masochist as an impertinent Sue looks on in impatience. Sue: “Well?!” tM: “Er… us? Me and bossman here?” Sue: (snapping) “No!” BAS: (soothingly) “Perhaps this evening falls upon one of those most feminine of feminine days. Perhaps if we made a quick trip to a local toxicologist…” Then it happens. Sue explodes with rage, filling the air with enough epithets to make a drill sergeant cry in embarrassment. The Graces begin yelling in unison, shaking lithe fists at the two in anger, as Big Al and the Masochist cringe within the center of the maelstrom of anger. Sue: “See?! See what I mean?! I ask a simple, honest question and you have to drag it down to PMS. Must be that darn PMS. Better get Sue some Midol because she’s pre-menstruating again. NO!! While I wasn’t NECESSARILY referring to you two Neanderthal buffoon, you CERTAINLY have proven a very important point for me!!” Sue seethes. The Graces snarl and circle. BAS and the Masochist pray to their favorite gods. Finally, Sue regains a modicum of control, managing to revisit the question. Sue: “Now… what I MEANT was… what is the deal with men in wrestling?” Big Al takes a small step back, trying to shield himself with the Masochist. BAS: “Men in wrestling? Well, what else would you expect in such a battlefield!” Sue: “I guess what I’m saying is… why are all the major PLAYERS guys? Where are the strong, intelligent women? Where are the role-models for the females? We comprise of 52% of this god-forsaken planet and yet we only really manage to be eye-candy for the drooling, slack-jawed troglodytes at ringside?” BAS: “Uh…” Sue: “And the wrestlers? Let’s NOT EVEN go there! In PCW we have Jaden, Heaven Rayne and Brandi. Jaden – ex-cheerleader. Brandi – may not last the week, from her track record. Heaven – long, blonde hair and she’s an angel! Not too mention Tyler nelson won’t let them NEAR a ring before they have enough silicone to re-caulk the upstairs AND downstairs shower!!” tM: “Well…” BAS: “Just because Brandi may not last, doesn’t mean she’s not masculine in many important ways…” Sue: (anger rising) “Shut up! OSW you say? Drug addicts, whores, and diseases… and that’s only covering Aimz! The rest are negligible, and the only thing of consequence has been Aimz’ rape. Great message for the kiddies. Gather the family around the ole PPV to see Aimz get raped to shit by some psycho maniac, because her male protection was available!” BAS: “I see, but…” Sue: “Oh, the managers, eh? Bimbos and floozies. Take Xavier Kannon, prime example. What, EXACTLY, was Ellie's job?” BAS: “Hmm…” Sue: “Sex. She was a little fuckrag for the idiot, her only job to attempt to distract other testosterone-infused wrestlers into noticing her jiggling her ass on the apron of the ring. Nice. Anyways, she’s a little brainless tramp with little to no redeeming qualities. And what happens?” TM: “………” Sue: “Right! She gets a TITLE PUSH!!! A title. That her loving husband actually delivers into her useless hands. She couldn’t figure out how pop tarts work. She can’t even talk using complete sentences. Yet because she has a big chest and flaunts her goodies to a paying MALE public, she gets widely recognized.” The Graces chime in whit various ‘yeahs!’ and ‘you go girl!’ BAS: “But tis why I have you! You are a sublimely unique female, more capable than…” Sue: “You HYPOCRITE!! Look around you! Personally, I see eight gorgeous blonde women, each incapable of taking care of themselves in the outside world! Idiots who are the butt of your jokes, whose only real job is your dance team!! A dance team, fer cryin’ out loud!! And when was the last time they danced?” BAS: “Guh…” Sue: “Uh huh. Exactly. Maybe two years ago, right? Pathetic!” One of the graces pipes up, shaking a fist in anger. Steffi: “Yeah! We suck… non-sexually speaking, of course…” Big Al cringes as Sue rises from her seat, somehow seeming to loom above him. Sue: “Well?” BAS: (meekly) “Well what?” Sue: (roaring) “Well what are you going to do about the state of wrestling!! You’re the champion!! Set and example!!” Big Al straightens up to his full, impressive height. BAS: “Huzzah!! You are correct, good Sue! No longer shall I oppress and subjugate the good women of PCW, or anywhere else!! Good Hera shall be pleased with my actions!” He turns to the girls. BAS: “You are all fired.” Sue: “WHAT?!” Graces in unison: “WHAT?!” BAS: “Tis with a heavy heart that I do this…” Sue: “You IDIOT!! You CAN’T FIRE them without cause!!” Candi: “I’m suing.” Sue: “Now, now, calm down girls. We’ll work this out here…” BAS: (shrugging) “What? I did as you bode, merely eliminating an offensive element to my entourage!” Sue whirls on Big Al, fire blazing in her eyes. Sue: “NO no no no no!! You have to make them better!! You have to INCLUDE them more in your promos, allowing them to assume REAL and important roles!” tM: (grinning) “Well, I suppose I can take one or two for the team, heh heh heh…” Sue: “Nope. None of that usual ‘secondary love interest’ stuff. Real, meaty parts.” Tammi: “Yeah! We want meat! Non-sexually speaking, of course…” Sue: “Right. We want… no, we DEMAND major comedic antagonist roles for each and every blonde!!” BAS: “You mean as in a coconut cream pie in the face?” Jodi: (shocked) “That’s sexual harassment!!” BAS: “But.. but tis above the neck!! And what could be funnier than a coconut cream pie in the face, properly delivered, of course.” Candi: (huffy) “I’ll sue!” Kelli: “I’m calling the agency for Platinum Blonde Empowerment!!” tM: “You can’t even SPELL empowerment!” Kelli: “So?” Sue: “That’s a sexist comment!!” tM: “But it’s true!” Uproar!! Mayhem!! The women are in a lather as they advance upon the two defenseless men. Suddenly, Big Al’s eyes light up with an idea… BAS: “Perhaps… if we were to PAY you all for a major comedic antagonist role, WHILE decreasing your workload… perhaps that would solve this foamy problem?” Sue: “WHAT?! Are you implying that all women are is money-grubbing gold diggers that only think about…” Steffi: (curious) “Wait a minute, Sue. Let’s hear this out.” BAS: (breathing sigh of relief) “Standard scale for a major comedic antagonist role. Lessened work around the house. No more dancing. But the major promotive formulae remains the same.” The blondes furrow their brows in deep thought as smoke fills the room. Sue: (acidly) “You know, that last sentence is a perfect example of what I’m talking about!” BAS: “Kayfabe!” Sue: “ Screw your precious kayfabe! I love your solution here, Al! Throw money at the women and they’ll…” Steffi: “DEAL!!” Sue’s head slumps in defeat as the blonde whoop and holler, celebrating their ‘victory.’ Sue sinks into her chair as Big Al comes up behind her, putting a hand on her shoulder. Sue: “It’s not right, Al. The state of wrestling is not right. When will the pejorative stereotypes end? I’m tired of the sex-kitten archetypes!” BAS: “I agree, but we simple don’t do enough promos to allow access into the Graces everyday lives that consist of drudgery. And do not doubt that you HAVE changed the way people view managers on your own… as the woman at the wheels of the juggernaught known as Big Alan Steele!!” tM: “Easily the most powerful and feared woman in the wrestling industry, aside from Jane Reagan.” Sue: (hissing) “The slut.” BAS: “Hi-Keeba!”
