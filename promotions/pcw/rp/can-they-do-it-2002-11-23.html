Can They Do It?

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 23, 2002 [Fear not dudes and dudettes, the Cowabunga Connection is alive and well. The bonds of friendship, of brothership, will never take away the greatness the Cowabunga Connection has together. Pranksters With Attitude are the tag team champions, but do they truly have the unity that it takes to be what they are? No, they don't, dudes. Does Playaz Inc. really have that unity to be a breakout tag team the likes of the Cowabunga Connection? No a chance, compadres. The heavy hitters of the tag team division do not have the true unity, the true drive to be the champions they should be. It's ashame too, dudes and dudettes. Our gnarly heroes have to sit on the sidelines and watch all these bogus teams do battle in the tag team title match, watch as the titles, that should have been there's for such a long time, slip further and further away from them. The agility, speed, and crisp move architecture or 'Totallly Tubular' Tommy. The heavy hitting, powerful, painful moves of 'Heavy Metal' Harry. It all comes together like one of those totally bodacious 1000 piece puzzles that takes like months to put together. Yeah, it's like that, compadres. Are the two men angered by being left out? No angered, but they have their mixed feelings about the entire situation] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: It's totally bogus. PCW is the number one federation, and they want to stay that way don't they? Well, what do they do? They do the most bogus thing that any federation in the world can do...they don't put the Cowabunga Connection in the tag team title match at the Alliances Pay Per View, compadres. It's the most heinous thing that they have ever done to us. Lets not even talk about the totally bogus times that front office has cost us the tag team titles. Countless number one contenders matches taken from us just because they feel like doing it. It's HEINOUS I TELL YOU!!! [Tommy said this at a small restaurant just outside of Los Angeles. In his Hawaiian button down shirt and cargo khaki shorts he seemed to be quite an angry young surfer. Bitter, really. I'm sure any man would feel some bitterness if he was recovering from an infected testicle. While he waited in line at the small restaurant to order his gnarly garlic omelet, Tommy had some other angry words about the Alliances Pay Per View this Sunday] TTT: Then what else do they do? They stick us with Redemption Song for the huge Alliances match. Now, tell me how bogus that is, dudes. Those compadres have been a thorn in our side ever since their heinous jealousy led them to insult the Tubular Tykes Foundation. They hurt one of the little compadres, and they hurt me. I was able to steal their valet, who turned out to be a totally heinous bitch...but it happened. Then we end up making those dudes leave for a short time, just like we do with all the tag teams! They can't handle the Cowabunga Connection, they can't compare to the unbelievable bodaciousness that we radiate. Now we have to be a team with those totally bogus compadres?! NO WAY, DUDE!! They'll just bring us down just like they bring down everything that they do, dudes. [Tommy quickly grabbed his omelet and stormed out of the little restaurant named 'Julie's'. Many of the customers wondered what the commotion was all about, but quickly dismissed it when they learned it was 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. The bitter, recovering surfer man. The man who's thought the Cowabunga Connection should be held on the highest pedastal in wrestling. The man who has always believed that his skills as a wrestler were better than any other man's, that they were all truly jealous of the talent he could craft. They were all jealous of the true power he could weild with his gnarly people skills. That Tommy and Harry could weave their way to the top of everything that they've ever done. Is it some kind of horrible delusion caused from dominating the world of surfing? Some say Yes, but others do believe in the talent of the Cowabunga Connection. They believe they can make the waves necessary for that gnarly ride. For instance, Gregory Besterman, a fellow professional surfer] Gregory: Man, Tommy is the FREAKIN' man!! He took those waves like he was God, dudes. I swear to you he got struck by a lightning bolt and it gave him these freakin' powers, man....well, that could have been the six drops of acid...but I SAW IT!! I WAS THERE, MAN! Everytime I watch him in the wrestling ring I'm amazed at how he can fly around there the way he does. He's the freakin' man and should be up there. Harry's not bad either, he does his part. They go together like Batman and Robin. Siskel and Ebert. Butch Cassidy and The Sundance Kid. Marijuana and A bong. It's just freakin' awesome. [Gregory sits in his green lounge chair, just outside of his one million dollar mansion. His smile stupid and burnt out. His brown hair slightly sprouting out of his once shaved head. Gregory looks closely at the camera with a confused look] Gregory: So when do I get my cash for saying nice things about the Cowabunga Connection? [Gregory is quite a greedy man, but his heart is usually in the right place. He and Tommy go pretty far back. They once fought over a bodacious babe back in high school. Tommy gave Gregory two black eyes and a broken nose. Needless to say, the babe ended up with Gregory...and they got married. They have two kids. So, Tommy did him a favor and one that Gregory owes him for quite a long time. Gregory is just one of the many supporters of the Cowabunga Connection. One of the dudes who knows that they have the vital tools to be the best. They just have to shovel their way through the jealousy of everybody else] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: I got this new bong, man. ['Heavy Metal' Harry, Tommy's trustworthy brother, walks through what he calls 'The Museum Of Bongs'. It's just oe of the rooms in Tommy's house where Harry stores all the bongs. They are cluttered all over the floor and underneath each other. Not really much of a museum, but more of a storage facility. Some bongs are very creative like the Vagina Bong that is shaped like Chasey Lain's vagina. It took Harry two months to finally finish that bad boy, and the smoking pleasure was most far out. Of course in a glass case there is the ultimate bong name JEROME BONGUS. Pittsburgh Steeler running back who donated one of his helmets to charity, actually that's what he thought, until Harry got a hold of it and turned it into one of his most masterful creations of all time!] Harry: This new bong is like freakin' far out, man. It might seem pretty freaky at first but once you try out the special feature there will be no turning back, man. [Harry holds up what looks to be a glass replica of The Pilsbury Dough Boy. Just the design is very interesting. Where you place the refer is where his testicles would be, and the inhaling device is the dough boys mouth. It seems rather fruity at first but as Harry takes a hit from it and the smoke fills the glass dough boy, it's quite an amazing site] Dough Boy: HE HE!!! [It's brilliant. Absolutely brilliant. The dough boy laughs everytime you take a far out puff of it. Now that is pretty damn cool. Harry giggles to himself as the smoke exhales from his mouth and nostrils. He looks rather proud of the invention with a smile of an 8 year old boy] Harry: It's really not that bitchin' that we don't have that tag team title match. That's all right with me though because I don't want to wrestle twice in one night. Isn't once enough, man? Seriously. Tommy pretty steamed about it, but then I showed his The Pilsbury BongBoy and he got pretty brightened up. I think we have to team up with...ummm...Redemption Song. Dude, I haven't even heard of that song, man. If Twisted Sister didn't do it, then I sure didn't hear of it. Redemption Song probably songs anyway. Probably one of those wuss guys like Michael Bolton or Kenny G did it. Doesn't matter, man...we'll probably win. Tommy will do something pretty far out, man. [Harry can only take another hit from his bong to clarify his statement] Pilsbury DongBoy: HE HE!! [Amazing. But when Alliances rolls around the corner, which it already has, who will be truly ready for this insanity? So many teams going into one place at one time. Anything can happen. Underdogs, people who you wouldn't think of winning, could just come up with the big win. Teams not even from PCW could somehow create a huge rift within the wrestling world. Can an unlikely team like the Cowabunga Connection and Redemption Song pull something like this off? Two teams that have been forgotten by the tag team division, two teams that seem to not have much of a future because of the way they have been treated, two teams that have absolutely nothing in common. Can something like them pull it off? Can they take Alliances and shove it down the throats of all the non-believers, all the ones that have held down the Connection...made sure that they couldn't be good enough. Only at Alliances will these things be proven and all four men are more than ready to do this] TTT: ARGH!! We are bodacious, we'll win! Harry: Isn't this bong far out, man? Gregory: DAMNIT, WHERE'S MY MONEY?!?! [Fade to black]
