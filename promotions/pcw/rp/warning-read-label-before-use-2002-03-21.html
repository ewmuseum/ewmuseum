Date: 21st March 2002 I stand here in complete silence, in total abandonment of those who love me for who I am. I stand here alone, unaided, and unwanted by most and yet I still stand. I do not fall. I shall not fall. I must not falter or else the one person who feels true love for me WILL fall. I cannot let that happen... I must not let that happen. Hitting the punching bag tends to relieve my nerves sometimes, but not today... I’ve been doing it for the last hour or so and the only thing it’s caused is a rather nasty cramp in my shoulder. I face in a days time someone else who already lost the person that he loved the most... that of course being the Chef, and the person that he loved... his wife, Bertha. I can appreciate what he must have felt now, I couldn’t at the time. The fact that you want to help, you would do anything to help but you are completely powerless and must let fate take it’s course. Chef broke that however. He defied fate and managed to make a clone of Bertha. The question is simple, if I lost Natalia would I go crazy and sign with the devil of religion and ultimately realise that I cannot live without my wife and do anything to reclaim that love that we once shared? Chef won the PCW Title for Bertha... but Natalia was hurt because I won the PCW Title. They’re two very different kettles of fish. Chef won the title and devoted it to Bertha... I however blame that very same title for what happened to her. He can look back on his PCW triumph and tell the clone that he won it all for her, but I just look back on that title with contempt... even if I do manage to win it in the future I don’t know what I would do with it... just to look at that hunk of metallic crap around Boda’s waist makes me feel anger... but I must not. Boda will not win... I will not hate him, because if I do then I am no better than Boda is himself. I slam my fist into the punching bag again, and make a dent in the red rubber exterior of the kinda squashed oval of the bag itself. Mohammed Ali used to ignore Joe Frasier when he was training before they fought because Frasier would knock three tonnes out of this bag in a forty minute session. I can’t say I blame him. In much the same way Chef and I are like Mohammed Ali and Joe Frasier. We’ve been in this game for a long time... Chef was in PCW before I was and I’ve been in it now for eighteen months... comparatively to others who haven’t even been here twelve months that makes us the old boys of this federation. Chef is currently targeting Big Alan Steele, a man who hasn’t been here even ten months yet... and I’m facing Corey Williams a man whose one claim to fame is that he beat Boda once. Beating Boda isn’t a claim to fame. Beating someone important might be, but Boda isn’t that. I have to face Boda at Spring Fling. It’s the only way I can beat him into giving Natalia the blood... it’s the only thing I can do... and to do that I must do something I would contemplate doing otherwise... I must kill Corey Williams. I don’t mean stand up on a stage and screaming something as lame as “I’M GOING TO KILL YOU COREY!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!”. That might be old school screaming, but old school isn’t necessarily correct in all manners. Old School isn’t good school. Thump with my fist into the punchbag, it wobbles slightly against the pressure. Chef is Old School. I am Old School. Boda is Old School. I suppose Chris Chase and Corey Williams are border-line between new and old, considering they were in PCW2 together with Boda. Actually what am I saying... Chris Chase might not matter more than watering down a gallon of beer with a eye dropper full of water... but why on earth does Corey Williams have to copy him all the time? It’s pathetic, it really is. Thump goes the fist again. Maybe they’re doing some sort of alternative lifestyle. Maybe they’re in love. I’ve got nothing against gay wrestlers, just as long as they don’t want to share a locker room with me. That does explain it... maybe I should get them a present or something... after all if they are dating or live in lovers or something then it’s nearly a year since PCW2 started, so it could be nearly a year since they got engaged... or whatever gay couples do. Do they even get married? Maybe they’re actually Chris and Corey Chase-Williams. Maybe. Well at least thinking of Chris and Corey the C Minuses as being gay took my mind off my own problems for a few seconds. I shouldn’t feel so damned down... maybe I should take those anti-depressants that my doctor prescribed. That might make me feel better. I fish around in the pocket of my jeans and pull out a small bottle. Thump goes my fist... mainly for good measure. I open the bottle slowly, first of all failing to open the child safety cap and then remember to press down on the bottle and opening. I remove a pill, it’s actually one of those caplets and I swallow it down quickly. I take another couple and then read the label... “Take one every 24 hours”. Oh no. *******************************
