Here let me take that monkey off your back!

24 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-24-03 “Here let me take that monkey off your back!” Jaslene cries out with excitement. She jumps onto Adam’s back. Due to the surprise of it all and the sudden influx of weight on his back, Adam falls flat onto his face. Jaslene explodes into laughter as she rolls off of him. “AHHHHH! HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!” she starts laughing so hard that tears start to roll down her cheek. That is until she looks at Adam and notices that he isn’t laughing. Adam sits up and sits back against a wall. He starts to slowly knock his head back into the wall. “What’s wrong Adam?” Jaslene asks extending her hand, “You should be happy, you won your match with Hank~!, in quite a fantastic showing if I do say so myself, and you are the number one contender to the Extreme Title!” “It was Hank~!…f*cking Hank~!…” Adam sighs as he drops his head. “SO?” Jaslene says not quite understanding. “Jas, Hank~! is the JOBBER KING of the PCW.” Adam says half heartily, “Anyone could beat him…I could have done it in a wheel chair.” “Still a win is a win Adam. You shouldn’t sell yourself short just because it was Hank~!…” Jaslene responds, “You are the number one contender to the Extreme Title, Brianna Washington isn’t going to know what hit here when you step into the ring with her.” “Yeah…” Adam says looking away. “ADAM!” Jaslene yells out. Suddenly Adam opens his eyes and looks around. He is sitting on a plane. Adam lifts his head up and looks around. Jaslene is sitting to his left and another woman is bending over him with a clipboard in her hand. “Huh? Wha?” Adam looks around groggily, “What’s going on?” “What would you like to eat sir?” the woman bending over in front of him asks with a smile. The wrinkles in her forehead become apparent as she giggles at Adam who is still looking around like he is lost. Adam’s eyes dart everywhere until they finally land on the woman’s nametag. It reads, “Katlyn”. “Ohhh…” Adam groans still trying to clear his head, “Uh…what are my choices?” “Well sir we have a chicken meal and a beef meal.” Katlyn says standing back up, “That is unless you are a vegetarian. Are you a vegetarian sir? I wouldn’t think so looking at the way you fill that shirt out.” She winks at him. Jaslene scowls. Adam still doesn’t know what is going on. He’s looking around, going back and forth from Katlyn’s smile to Jaslene’s scowl. “Well? What will it be?” Katlyn asks once again. “He’ll have the chicken.” Jaslene cuts in. Katlyn makes a notch on her on her clipboard, “And you miss? What will you be having?” Jaslene wraps her arm through Adam’s, “Chicken as well.” Jaslene smiles as Katlyn makes a notch and walks to the next aisle. As soon as she disappears through a curtain to the back, Jaslene slaps Adam in the arm. “OW!” Adam says finally waking up. “What the hell was that for?” “That stewardess was hitting on you!” Jaslene exclaims getting closer to him so as not to be too loud or draw too much attention to them. “What stewardess?” Adam asks rubbing his eyes with his hands, “I don’t have the slightest clue as to what you are talking about Jas.” “Uh huh, so then what was with that wink she gave you? And then the smile? And then the attitude she gave to me?” Jaslene rapidly fires out. “I really don’t know what’s going on, I was sleeping…” Adam says moving his hands to the back of his neck, “You know jumping me in the locker room like you did wasn’t the smartest move.” “Well I didn’t think you’d tip over so easily!” Jaslene says sitting back in her seat, “I told you I was sorry!” “When?” Adam asks her pulling his arms down and unbuckling the seat belt. “Uh…” Jaslene hesitates, “You know, that time…uh…” Adam just gives her a look. “I just did so nyah!” Jaslene sticks her tongue out at him. Quick as a cat, Adam grabs it. Jaslene squeals as Adam squeezes it laughing. “Lep Gh F mb tong!” Jaslene muffles. Adam laughs as he lets go her tongue, this prompts another and harder slap from Jaslene. The smack reverberates throughout the front portion of the plane prompting the other passengers to look at Jaslene and Adam. “Look what you have done Jaslene.” Adam says looking around and smiling a nervous smile. “I didn’t do anything.” Jaslene says, “You were the one holding onto my tongue!” Adam just chuckles for a moment. Jaslene crosses her arms in front of her chest and looks straight ahead. “Calm down Jas. You are making a scene.” Adam says stretching his arm out and placing it down around her. “What were you dreaming about?” she asks sitting back and relaxing a bit. “Actually I just had a dream of the time we spent in the locker room after my match with Hank~!” Adam says. “Oh? Is that what brought about the question about my apology?” Jaslene asks looking at him. “Don’t you mean lack of an apology?” Adam responds with a grin. “Apology, lack of an apology…what’s the difference?” Jaslene says returning the same grin. “Did I miss anything while I was out?” Adam asks getting serious for a moment, “I know I’ve got a match tomorrow for Fusion against Rust and Smoke with Trinidad but was there any news regarding next Turmoil.” “Yeah there is actually, you going toe to toe with Smoke on Turmoil.” Jaslene says, “Mano-a-Mano, you and Smoke, in the ring, no one else-” “OK!” Adam says cutting her off, “I get the picture Jas. Me and Smoke, one on one at the next Turmoil. But God I’m starting to feel like Trinidad, a type cast. It seems I’m only to fit to wrestle Rust or Smoke or Hank~!. I want some real competition.” “Well Brianna is going to be some good competition. That’s for sure, she won’t give up the title she worked her ass off to get so easily.” Jaslene nods. “That’s most certainly true Jas, which makes me thing, when is this title match going to take place?” Adam asks. “I would have thought this coming Turmoil but that got ruined with this match with Smoke.” Jaslene responds, “So I dunno, maybe next Fusion?” “Oh God I certainly hope not.” Adam sighs. “Why do you say that?” Jaslene asks sitting up and looking at him. “You feeling all right?” “Just a little burnt out I suppose.” Adam says running his hand through his hair, “I mean week after week, doing both Fusion and Turmoil will wear anyone down. I know Trinidad is feeling the grind so I’m not the only one.” “Yeah but as soon as you can establish yourself, you won’t have to worry about doing two shows a week since you’ll be a star.” Jaslene points out. “Yeah but that could be worse, if I do become a big time star, won’t the people want me to perform on a more regular basis?” Adam retorts. “Ohhhh…good point, I guess I didn’t take that into consideration.” Jaslene replies, “But still when you manage to work your way up the card, at least you’ll have more of a say in your career. You’ll be able to request more time off. Now you just need to focus on pleasing the fans and the higher ups of the PCW. I’m sure you will succeed sooner or later.” “I certainly hope so, I left a good thing in the DWO to be here in the PCW.” Adam says sitting back in his seat. “Good thing? Haven’t you been saying that the DWO was a waste of your time and talent?” she asks him. “Aye, but that doesn’t mean what you think it to mean. I could be the DWO World Champion right now, someone bound for the DWO Hall of Fame, that’s assuming Callisto ever decides to make one, I could have been on top of the world. But I still would have been a big fish in a small pond.” Adam says resting his head back and laying his arms down on the armrest, “Instead of the small fish in the big pond I am now. The PCW is going to help me prove to myself that I deserved everything that was handed to me in the DWO. If I can make it here than I know I made it in the DWO. If I don’t make it in the PCW, then I know the DWO was a wash.” Jaslene rests her head back as well. She gently places her hand down on top of his. “You made it all by yourself in the DWO. I was there, Gina was there and AJ was there. We all helped you and you helped us. The DWO was a family to an extent but like all families it had…er…has it’s problems.” Jaslene says, “But life’s too short to be dwelling on problems in the past. It’s time to look to the future. And that future is you in the PCW.” “You’re right, even if I have to face Smoke for the rest of my days, the PCW is my home.” Adam says with a content smile on his face.
