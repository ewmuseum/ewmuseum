What Lies Beneath...Yuu...

undated

Date: ... “The mind is the greatest pinpoint…targeted by all forms of existence. From north, south, east, and west…up, down, left, and right…from the outside…and from within. Foes will become worthless, and your friends will become your worst nightmare…” ~Savage Tranquility, page undetermined A silent memorial for a lost friend… …Fallen in the fight for universal harmony… …Taken by the HAND OF FATE…wielded by the Neo Void God. Our memories of this lighthearted girl are vague…she was never one to stand out. She first appeared on the night Akiyama was banished form Japan. She appeared to him as, and to quote the banished: “A Gift…from the gods.” She never explained what that was supposed to mean. All she ever did was care for the Neo Void God…gave him love and kindness that the world never desired to give him or never did. Her devotion to this man was truly admirable, considering that he was guilty of actions that she could never have thought of committing. But now…she was gone… …Banished, without provocation. Now normally, if the god is to banish someone, there’s never a second thought about it. But this time things were different. First, her banishment was accidental…Portalis had maneuvered himself so that she would be banished, and not him. Second, she was someone that really didn’t deserve to be banished…no matter the circumstances were. He sat there in the darkness… not the darkness of the Void, but the darkness of his unlit bedroom. His large frame was stretched out across the velvety comforter atop of the mattress, his head staring straight up at the ceiling. Outside, the moonlight slowly flashed light in and out of the room through the balcony window as the clouds floated before it. Sorrow was thick in the air that night, choking and throttling at the mental and emotional breath of the NVG. His chest rose and fell slowly without a sound, almost as if he were as dead as she whom he banished. {~That is odd. That girl was banished quite differently than all the others.~} The Divine Prophetess…Cyclonica…the woman who has been invading the mind of Akira Akiyama for the past few months now. The god knew nothing about her, save that she was the Divine Prophetess, and that she was Cyclonica. Her motives were never clear as to if she was aiding or yielding the NVG’s progress…but she had never been wrong in her words, so Akiyama never really questioned her. Not that she was giving the orders…she merely acted as a guide…just another voice in the multitude of madness that resides in Aki’s head. {~Have you ever seen someone disintegrate before your eyes during a Void Banishment? It is very odd indeed.~} Akiyama made no response to her. He just stared up with his deep almond eyes. Cyclonica made a sound that sounded like she was sighing in a frustrated manner. {~Are you even listening to me? I’m saying something important here. There may be something up with that girl you’re mourning for that should draw your attention.~} “We saw her…” murmured the god in slow syllables. His demanding voice was barely above a whisper, but of course audibility was not a factor when talking to a voice in your head. {~You did not see that girl.~} “We saw her in the eyes of Isiy…that sweet moment when she looked over to us in fear…once we had made her bleed for us…that ecstatic face she held with a dirty, ashamed pleasure. We looked past her existence to and recognized all that keeps her with her husband. That jealous ingrate…that filthy stink child…she sticks with him despite his obvious flaws. And it is all those things…that Yuu used to have for us.” {~That girl is not important. She is gone, and despite all your searching and your efforts, you will not find her. Now…forget about her and begin your planning for combat.~} He closed his eyes and exhaled deeply. “And now you are the one to order us around? We thought we had an arrangement between us.” Cyclonica was getting a bit frustrated with the Neo Void God about this point. Surprising, considering her overwhelming mental fortitude. {~Akiyama…if you want to sit here and wither away in depression, drop your belt, become a wizen worthless waste of a god, and give into the likes of Pharaoh…then go on and exist as you are now. When you are ready to get back into the Neo Void God business…give me a call. You know how to reach me.~} And with that said, silence returned. There was nothing left for him to do at this point. So he reached over to the bedside table, his hand returning with a cigarette. With a flick of a match, he lit it and exhaled deeply. His eyes looked into the smoke, and for a second, he thought he saw her… …Yuu… But a sudden gust blew away the image he saw…a breeze similar to the one that would blow away all hopes and dreams of defeating Akiyama in the minds and hearts of his foes. But that was when Akiyama was in a Celestial state of mind. As of right now…Akiyama wasn’t too sure if he was much of anything anymore. The black sheer curtains blew softly in the breeze flowing in from the open balcony window. The air teased at the embers of the cigarette, the glow growing brighter. Akiyama dropped his arm to his side, the burning cigarette wedged taut between his fingers. “It is all over…nothing left for us here in this realm.” And with one last puff, Aki dropped his arm back down, and released the cigarette. It fell slowly towards the ground, looking for a place to land and burn the room to ashes. A fiery death…how fitting for a god who’s caused so much hell for everyone…a god who otherwise would make each of his foes on Fusion taste a burning hot HAND OF FATE just for thinking that they stood any chance against him…but no, this time the fire would cleanse his despair and sorrow. Then…a hand caught the cigarette before it could hit the black shag carpet. Akiyama flipped his eyes open to stare at the person by his side. It was her…just as he saw her last…just as he saw her when she was banished. Silver high heels, silver knee-length slip dress, her light brown hair…and her face was the clincher over everything else. “Yuu?” he questioned in disbelief. And just like before…she flashed him that smile that could brighten up the darkest of rooms. “Hiya, Aki!” “By the Void, Yuu…what are you doing here?” he stood up before her and placed his hands on her shoulders. Was the NVG…happy? Maybe more content with finding her alive rather than happy…that’s a little extreme for the Celestial to feel. Surprise was more what he felt. “Where have you been?” She just smiled at him, reaching up and taking his hands into hers. “Don’t worry about that now, Aki. Just be glad that I’m back.” Akiyama let out a huge laugh, shaking the room with sound. “BWAHAHA!! Now, this is just Celestial! The NVG has his sidekick back, and now there’s going to be nothing but Divine trouble for those bastards on Friday!” “Shh, Aki. Sit down…let’s talk.” She pushed him gently back onto the edge of the bet and plopped down next to him. She wrapped her arms around him, leaning her head against his shoulder. The god’s mind was racing now that he had is little buddy back…looks like the fight for Fusion was back on. “What do you need to talk about? We have a lot of training to catch up on now that you’re back. Make this quick.” “But don’t you want to know how I got back here?” she asked, playing on the sympathy role with a tease in her voice. Aki sighed and nodded. “Very well, but make it quick. We’re in the mood to dish out some Astral @sskickings now.” She bounced up on the mattress quickly in a happy fashion. “Great! Well, you banished me…” “And? Get to the point where we don’t know what happened.” Yep…just like old times… “Okay. Well, I was floating around in the Void there, and I didn’t know what was going on. Then I found myself back here. Someone had pulled me out of the Void.” “Someone pulled you out?” he asked angrily. “Who would dare to step into our realm and pluck banished beings without our permission?” She giggled a bit. “A fine group of people. They called themselves the Void Elements, and they said that they from the Void too…and that they wanted to help you out in your quest.” The god stared at her blankly. “Who…saved…you?” “The Void Elements.” He turned his head forward, his blank look remaining. “Son of a frickin’ b!tch.” “What? They seemed just like you. And they were talking about how they liked what you were doing in PCW with the banishments and such. They said they were really interested in helping you!” “You don’t know anything…Yuu.” It could be said about Fusion’s batch of losers to be defeated as well. “Whom did you talk to? Darkcyde’!? Shinnosuke? Or how about the Daughter of Darkness herself? Did you talk to Hinageshi, Yuu? Well? What nonsense did they fill your head with? Please tell us it’s something more reasonable than all those flat-scans’ ideas about defeating us.” She looked up at him with her wide eyes, full of hope and optimism. That’s how Isiy looked when she talked about her husband…but it’s a shame that hers was so poorly placed in one destined to play second fiddle to the NVG. Yuu was so naïve though…you could screw her brains out by force and tell her it wasn’t rape…and she’d go along with it. Akiyama knew that, and he sighed because he knew it. “All they said was that they wanted you to talk with them about some things. Discuss some plans that you may like to go through with in order to accomplish your PCW domination. That’s all they told me.” Akiyama lowered his head, exhaling deeply in contemplation over her response. He didn’t look at her, but he could feel her weight leaving the side of the bed near him. Her weight shifted to behind him, where her hands gripped into his shoulders and massaged. “Don’t you worry about it now, Aki. All you need to do now is relax. You have a big TV title match coming up, and you’re going to have to be at your best to defeat your opponents.” There was sensuality in her voice…something a bit strange coming from her. Akiyama shook his head, the moonlight fading away from his face under the dark clouds. “Yuu, you don’t know anythi-.” She suddenly pulled him back onto the bed, her body pinning his down against the mattress. Akiyama glared up surprised at the woman on top of him. She had a dark look in her eyes, and a smile that could win toothpaste commercials. But the god…for once the NVG was in control…and not the NVP. “Yuu…get the freak off of us. We don’t have time for your games.” “This is no game, Aki. This is for real.” She ran her hands over his wrists and pinned them back against the headboard. The NVG was growing agitated with her. “Hey! What’s gotten into you?” The moonlight was fading fast from the room behind the veil of clouds again. He looked up one last time at Yuu smiling naughtily at him before the darkness cloaked the room again. There was movement in the darkness before words were whispered. “That naïve fool is finished, Akiyama.” Akiyama heard her words, as if they echoed off the walls. “Who?” he asked. A small chuckle came from Yuu…resonating almost like it was unnatural. “Yuu.” 'Me?' {~Move, Dumb@ss!!~} He only needed to be told once. With a shove and a roll, Akiyama dived off the bed into the middle of the room. He stood there staring forward into the darkness as that tinkling laughter echoed around him. He stood poised as the moonlight began to slowly come back into the room. Yuu… was no longer on the bed. “Behind you, Aki, honey!” the NVG swirled around and found the light haired girl reaching for him. He grabbed her by her arm and tossed her across the room, her back crashing into the wall and leaving a crater. He stood watching with his arm stretched out from throwing her, a shocked look of anger on his face. “What the freak is this?” anger coated his voice like coating on a candied apple. {~That isn’t Yuu, Akiyama. That’s someone else…someone with powers like my own, if not equal.~} He watched as Yuu…or whoever she was…began to stand back up slowly. Her laughter filled the room like surround sound. He stared into her laughing face, looking exactly like the Yuu he knew. “Oh, do you not recognize me, Aki, honey? Here, let me help you out!” she twitched her nose, and her appearance changed. Gone was the silver dress and light brown hair, and in its place were a black skirt, yellow heels, and a yellow v-neck silk spaghetti strap shirt, and black and light brown hair…though the face of the woman never changed. Akiyama’s eyes looked as if they would pop out of his head. “Sonata?” he sounded glad to see this woman named Sontata…but something from within told him otherwise… …Actually, Cyclonica told him…but the origin of the answer still holds… {~Do not believe her lies! It is another façade!~} “Listen to the Divine Prophetess, Neo Void God!” the woman who looked like Sonata…who also looked like Yuu…laughed out at him. “I’m still surprised that you haven’t realized anything yet! You make combat less of a challenge for me, as well as those idiots you fight on Friday!” Her appearance changed yet again. This time she wore tight black jeans, boots, and a yellow tank top underneath a black mesh long sleeved shirt. Her hair turned completely black, but the face that had belonged to Yuu and Sonata still remained the same. “You…” was all the god could mutter when he recognized her. She laughed yet again, the sound of her giggle becoming arrogant and annoying. “No, I’m not Yuu, I’m SYMPHONY! But there is a reason why Yuu looked like me…and there’s a reason why I look like you precious Sonata that you grew up with.” “Then you had better start squawking before we don’t care anymore and beat you into paste before banishing your remains.” The growl from within him came out almost feral like, the words he said barely comprehensible. Symphony stopped laughing finally and smiled at the god mischievously. “You see…Yuu never existed to begin with. She was a Psionic Entity that I created to have you drop your guard long enough for you to fail in your quest and return to us to complete the mission we have for you!” “The Void Elements.” He knew the “we” that she meant…the Void Elements that used to keep him under wraps for their bidding back before and during the beginning of his PCW career. “You’re telling us that Yuu was never real? She was just some frickin’ illusion you made up to have us lose focus and give into your prissy demands?” “Indeed she was, Aki, honey! And you gave into her hook…line…and sinker. I would have gotten away from it if not for that meddling Cyclonica!” “What do you know of Cyclonica??” he snapped out at her. “And why do you look like Sonata??” “Oh, I know a lot about Cyclonica…and Sonata as well. I know where both of them are…and if you’re smart, you’ll play your cards right and do as Hinageshi says. Once you’re finished serving the Daughter of Darkness, maybe we’ll tell you where they are!” Akiyama was growing more and more impatient with this girl. And that would not be good for those he faced on Fusion…angering the NVG, as it has been said before, is like f^cking for virginity…a hopeless endeavor, and at this point, doing hopeless things like defy Akiyama are most likely to get your mouth jaw broken by the HAND OF FATE. “How ignorant to you think we are? Do you think we’re as stupid as Pharaoh is? You’re holding hostages against us like we’re holding hostages against him! But Isiy is no hostage…she’s a prisoner of war! And despite any war, there will always be casualties…Carmoon was first…Isiy will be second…Pharaoh will be next…but for tonight…if you choose to keep your mouth shut and not tell us what we need to know…you’re going to be tonight’s casualty!” “And what are you going to do to me? Banish me to the Void?? Please, Akiyama! That stuff doesn’t work on me!” she laughed at him again. “Oh, as a matter of fact…here…I’ll banish myself!!” Akiyama roared out at her as the darkness swirled about Symphony’s body. He dived at her, but by the time he reached her, she had already jumped into the Void. He sat there on his knees and pounded his fists into the floor, shaking the room like an earthquake. {~Akiyama…get a grip on yourself! You have more important issues to deal with! You want to defeat Pharaoh, don’t you? You want to make him pay for betraying you, right?~} “We want to make him bleed…” he hissed through clenched teeth. “We want him to bleed from every orifice that exists on him. Just as Moses made the Nile River into blood…we shall turn the Nile crimson with Pharaoh’s own blood!! Anyone stepping in our way…gets an all expense paid trip to the Void…courtesy of Aki-frickin’-yama!!” {~Akiyama! You’re not listening! They’re playing the same game you’re playing with Pharaoh!~} The NVG snarled out at the voice in his head. “Well…if this is all just a game to play on some computer for sh!ts and giggles…then there’s no frickin way we’re going to play by the rules!” He jumped up and ran out onto the balcony. Inside his head, the voice of the Divine Prophetess echoed warnings to him, but he ignored them…just as he would ignore the attacks and efforts of his opponents on Fusion…Cylde…Badge…Pharaoh…they would be useless to stop him now…the god vaulted over the railing of the balcony and disappeared, leaving the bedroom ravished of violence. Fusion and Spring Fling…Pharaoh on both nights…looks like the God Wars would be starting early. Pharaoh wanted to make something noble about all this…but there was nothing noble about the reason for fighting Akiyama he began with. It was all tainted…jealousy, envy; being the second man behind the scenes…he couldn’t take being nothing behind Akiyama’s rampage. It ate at him inside all that time…and now he’s trying to make some crusade out of it to justify his own self-inadequacies. And what of Akiyama’s deeds? Banishing Carmoon and taking Isiy? He was simply stooping down to Pharaoh’s level that caused him to betray him…it seemed like yet again another being was going to attempt to do evil against he who rules over the collective of universal evil…and that is simply ignorant to even attempt. But what else can you expect from Pharaoh Punk@ss anyway? Fusion…Akiyama…Pharaoh screaming in The Darkness… Pharaoh would find out what he lies beneath very soon...the boot of the Neo Void God... All of it would be nothing more than…Celestial…
