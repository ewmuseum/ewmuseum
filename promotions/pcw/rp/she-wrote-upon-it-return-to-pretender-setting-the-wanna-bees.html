(she wrote upon it) Return to Pretender; Setting the wanna-bees in their place first

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: right here, right now. ::Our scene starts with a shot of a limo, long, sleek and black. Moving gracefully and powerfully simultaneously, like a panther. We see it move along the highway as the camera struggles to keep up with it. Then the camera fades into the interior of the limo, where we CCW 'Controversial' Corey Williams, the camera is no longer moving at a fast speed trying to keep up, in fact it seems perfectly still. If you hadn't have seen the shot of the highway you would barely know the limo was moving. CCW leans back on the plush leather chair, grinning at his own self-fulfilment. For right now, Corey is on top of the world. It has been going for a month now, about the time his good friend Jason Snow became World Champ. That's the difference between New Breed and Management, when you're with Management you see a lot of scowling and such, even though Tyler Nelson is CEO of PCW, even though he is a rich motherf*cker, he is still greedy, he still wants more. New Breed ain't greedy, for what can one wish for when one has everything? What more can one want when one is the most controversial athlete in the history of this business. New Breed are different to management because they are better, New Breed are different to EVERYONE because they're better. Egos? No way man! We just simply happen to know that we are better than everyone and will constantly remind those who forget, and those who CHOOSE to forget. Coz after all, the truth hurts. So as CCW sits back in that limo, sipping on champagne, grinning his ass off, his dark sunglasses making his big pearly white teeth stick out, there is only one thing on CCW's mind; CCW. Sure, from time to time CCW will talk about other people, but that's not because they have caught his eye, turned his head or surprised him, no one can catch the eye of this walking piece of eye-candy, no one can turn the head of the man who's been turning heads since day one, no one can surprise the master of the swerve. Damnit, CCW is THE most controversial man to ever grace PCW, to him, others peoples attempts to be controversial are just lame. CCW is talking into a phone, we hear a muffled voice coming from the speaker.:: Muffled Voice (through phone): Yes Corey, the house should be ready by the time the Pay-Per-View is over. CCW: That's great news Pauline, that's some great construction workers you have there over in Australia, that's twice as fast as it would take to build a normal house over here in the States, let alone a MANSION. Pauline: Yes, well that's because I didn't hire any Blacks, Nips or Abbos. ::That's Pauline Hanson for you inferiors, you know, the most controversial politician ever.:: CCW: Ahhh, so that's your secret. Hey, you should use that at your next campaign speech. Pauline: Yes, I'm having my speechwriters work it in between 'please explain' and 'I don't like it.' CCW: Genius, just pure genius. So how is the Eugene's Mom of Australian politics going in her bid to be the first woman Prime Minister of Australia? Pauline: All right I guess, but there's one problem, apparently I'm not popular enough with ethnics. CCW: Gee, I wonder why.... Pauline: Yes I know, can you believe it? I don't see why their votes should count the same as white people anyway, it's our country for Chase's sake! CCW: I'm sorry, did you just say for Chase's sake? Pauline: Yes, sorry, I've been watching your promos, that's some good stuff Corey, keep up the good work. CCW: You too, seeya. ::And with that, CCW puts the phone back on the hook. So like I said, other people's attempts to be controversial are just lame, for it takes a certain degree of understanding to be controversial. But at the moment, it's not the lack of quality of the people trying to be controversial, but the overwhelming quantity of people trying to be controversial. It seems that lately, everybody wants to be like Corey, and upon inspection of Corey, it doesn't come as much as a surprise. And that is where our promo begins.....:: CCW: I heard that Juan Borro is getting into politics, good for him. The only problem I have with it is that just a few minutes ago I happened to be on the line with the POLITICIAN Pauline Hanson, whose One Nation Party I've been involved with for over a month now! CCW: Is this the same Juan Borro who tried to become more eye-catching by wearing a bear fur coat? Something that I've been wearing to become more CONTROVERSIAL for the last 6 months. ::CCW doesn't get into any more detail than that, he lets us work it out for ourselves. Seeing as you people probably ain't too smart, let me tell you that the answer to the question in 'yes.':: CCW: I couldn't help but notice good ole Bobby Stormbringer having a row with his narrator, now correct me if I'm wrong, but isn't Bobby Stormbringer a huge 'mark' of a certain someone that is well-known for firing his narrators? ::Of course he is Corey, oh incredibly handsome and controversial one (figure that one out for yourselves):: CCW: Of course B.S is a member of Asylum, that try-hard New Breed stable. Why didn't they just call it Jew Heed, B.S could've changed his name to Snorey Silliams, Xavier Cannon could've changed his name to Priss Face and Jeremy Howard could have become Basin Ho! ::CCW takes another sip of his champagne, something that most people recommend doing after trashing the shit out of Asylum. Well at least we can guess that's what they recommend, we wouldn't know, there ain't too many people with the balls to trash the shit out of Asylum.:: CCW: You know, I'm sick of all these wanna-bees. All these guys who are trying to be me. Trying to be like the most envious stable in the history of wrestling, the New Breed. Trying to be.... CONTROVERSIAL. This Sunday you will all realise the error of your ways. For by striving to be like me, you can never be BETTER than me. To beat me, to beat the New Breed, you have to strive to be better than the New Breed. And whilst you're doing that, you might as well strive to become a bigger asshole than Boda, you might as well strive to become more inferior than Miyagawa, you might as well strive for the impossible, because you CAN'T become better than the New Breed. The New Breed are at the top of the game, and I don't mean THEIR game, like said, THE game! ::Corey's face becomes all shocked and surprised.:: CCW: I'm the fourth man in!?!?!?!?!................ So? ::After a few moments of that shocked face turns back to that confident grin, and we realise that that comment was layered in sarcasm.:: CCW: You think I care? You think I'm not better than The End, Miyagawa, Chet King, Bobby Stormbringer, Jon Kano, The Chef, El Diablo, Flaymin Aymin, Boda, Darkstar, Xavier Kannon, Juan Borro, The Rush and Tyler Nelson? Well I think that YOU'RE wrong! I think that I'M RIGHT! And you know what, in thinking that, I AM RIGHT! Why? Because I am better than The End, Miyagawa, Chet King, Bobby Stormbringer, Jon Kano, The Chef, El Diablo, Flaymin Aymin, Boda, Darkstar, Xavier Kannon, Juan Borro, The Rush and Tyler Nelson AND YOU! And therefore I have better judgement skills. Kinda confusing, ain't it? But let me assure you, it's a loophole that will work in any court in America, I can tell you that from experience. 'Excuse me Mr. Williams.' ::The camera zooms out to allow space for the Chauffeur to fit on screen. That's when we remember that this is a limo that we're in, a moving limo.:: CCW: What is it? Chauffeur: Where is it that you want to go anyway? ::CCW is disgusted by the question.:: CCW: What!? You mean I have to tell you AGAIN!? Chauffeur: Actually sir, you never told me. CCW: Excuses, excuses, excuses! It's time you faced facts and took responsibilities for your own actions! Now take me to the airport! ::CCW turns back to the camera.:: CCW: And the same thing goes to the participants of the March Madness Battle Royal. This Sunday the wanna-bees will be distinguished from the real thing, the pretenders will be show for what they truly are, and the men will be separated from the GREAT men. This Sunday will be a reality check to a lot of people......... ::CCW takes a sip on his champagne, raises the dividing window between the Chauffeur and himself, and pushes his sunglasses up his nose.:: CCW: But NOT New Breed, because reality cannot touch New Breed…….. ::CCW grins his famous Hollywood grin.:: Corey: …..for we are above reality..... beyond reality. The only time New Breed use the word real is when we reassure all those envious bastards that 'yes, we ARE for real!' And come Sunday, as the fans look on in disbelief, the New Breed will be all TOO real to some seventeen people! ::Our scene fades out of the limo, as we see it from the exterior, moving along the highway...... The scene fades out.:: !The End!
