Like The Movies

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/03 Have you ever had one of those days where you just want to fall back in a chair and bawl your eyes out until you can’t cry anymore? I sure have. For me my past few weeks have been like this. It seems like nothing has been going right and even worse it seems that everyone has lost faith in me. But who could blame them, who could have faith in someone who manages to lose the love of her life and the two children she once had custody over in less than a week. I sure wouldn’t, perhaps that why I don’t have faith in myself. Tuesday is coming sooner than you can imagine and that places me against El Diablo, Mark Makonie, and Jade. That probably gives me another chance to lose another singles match the way I have been as of late and get my ass handed to me. A Southern Comfort from Teresa that nearly cripples me, a Class Difference from Lacuna that cost me another singles match, and a chair shot from Gaz Green that knocked me out totally. It’s not wonder everyone considered me the weaker link of Chris and I. Being the small and more vulnerable one it was obvious that people would think that, in fact as of late I have been thinking that myself. After all perhaps if I put up more of a fight with Gaz inside the car that day, perhaps I would have never had to be known as Mrs. Tessa Green. Looking back I realize that even then from day one it was obvious I was going to be a failure. It was no wonder my parents had no faith in me. Perhaps they knew that I would be the one that ruins the family name. Perhaps they knew ahead of time the tribulations I would have to face, and that is why they wanted nothing to do with me once I moved out on my own. I don’t blame them, I wouldn’t want to be associated with failure either, but I am. I’m married to a man who I despise, I lose the children I care for as if they were my own, I lose the one man I care for because of a mistake I could have prevented if I tried a bit harder, and now it seemed that all chances of ever redeeming myself have faded away. How could I possibly beat two well respected people in PCW along with the tag partner of a person who destroyed me in singles competition, it didn’t seem at all possible but somewhere in my heart there was a flicker of hope, maybe I wouldn’t be a failure, maybe I could pull this off….if I was the luckiest person on earth which with the way things were going now didn’t seem possible. As I took a seat on the living room couch I felt dizziness set in. I had become such an emotional wreck that this had gradually become a feeling I was used to. It no longer bothered me the way it used to. I simply lay down and let it take its course. Maybe I’d become so dizzy that I’d pass out and wake up from the nightmare, but I couldn’t because this was a nightmare I was living. There was no way out of it but the coward’s way, and no matter how depressed I got at times, I refused to lower myself to that level. Turning on the television I noticed a group of happy couples and friends. I wondered why my life couldn’t be like this. How perfect would it be if life was like the movies and every day finished off with a happy ending? It wasn’t possible. I hit the remote once more turning it off, too depressed to watch something that I knew was so far from the truth. If everything had a happy ending then Chris and I wouldn’t be fighting right now and I could go into Tuesday knowing that I would walk out of the match number one contender. I closed my eyes and sighed as only one thing came to my mind, that thought being… Why can’t life be more like the movies?
