A Lead To My Fame

4 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4th October 2002 I had to put up with Clyde and his stupid White Boy antics for hours…. I not know why I put myself through this shit! I put myself through hell just so I can actually get money, so people actually know who I am! So people look at me and see me as the Vietnamese Viper that I truly am, so they see I not to be fucked with!! Finally I back in Japan, how long can I hide my face for? If anyone see me it just mean that I getting publicity, but TokyoPop can go get stuffed because I not going to be looking after there company once this deal is over! I taking my money and leaving, that right I not going to be no carry over Champion…. I going to be Jung Loc….the man with money coming out of his ass!! -=***=- “I’m finally home!!” Udai’s face lit up as we entered the crowded mall somewhere in Tokyo Japan. I not believe this…. As I look up around me I see a million and one pictures of PCW Superstars and logo’s, everything is hyped up to the event that is going to put Tokyo on the map. PCW’s Red October….but I seriously not see what all the fuss is about!! Every month a Pay Per View is held in America, you never see it get this much recognition…then again Japanese love White Boy’s so it all make sense, I guess they have to celebrate them when they come so they hope they come back….. Either way I not like this place one little bit!! I continue to look around until I spot something and my eyes become glued, I cannot move. Udai begins to tug at my arm, trying to get me to follow his lead through the chaos of the mall and onto a hotel so I can “talk business”, Pfft, there time for business later! Right now there only one thing on my mind and it flashing in front of my eyes. “What are you looking at Master Loc? Come on before someone spots you!” Udai continued to tug at my arm but I just pushed him off with one of my hands and begin to walk over to what I staring at. In front of me, past the million and one Japanese shoppers scurrying about is an arcade…and shining like a beacon from the front of it is a brand new Tekken 4 game in English!! Heh, they even make it so White Boy’s can come here and play it…Nice!! As my eyes continue to stick to the arcade machine I begin to push shoppers out of my way as the hood of my jacket falls off of my head, exposing my face to the people who not seem to notice or care who I am. I smell like seaweed, I look like shit because I not had a change of clothes for a few days but I not care because I about to play the newest video game in Japan, Tekken 4. I not know what came over me, I began to shove people out of my way left right and center, making old grandmothers hit the floor and mothers drop there shopping, creating attention and chaos as I moved towards the arcade but I not care about all that. “It’s a picture of you, who really cares Master Loc?!” I heard Udai state behind me in an angry voice, I flipped him the finger but he kept on talking. “Can we please get out of here?! I can get you this game on Playstation 2 back in the hotel! Please Master Loc, if you’re spotted I….” I not able to take it anymore, I turned around and let my right fist fly straight into Udai’s nose, sending blood and a loud cry up into the air as the whole mall turned to pay there attention to him. Fuck him… I didn’t really care what I just did or how I probably just sent shards of glass into his face and shattered his nose…all I cared about was playing Tekken 4. No one respect Japanese games in the States!! The game lit up like a beacon in front of me as I finally made my way into the arcade. People began to swarm behind me crowding around Fat Man as I heard people talking in Japanese…You think they speak English pffft, Not caring too much about what repercussions I might receive in the near future I reached into my jacket pocket and produced a quarter. I knew that American currency would have absolutely no worth in Japan but I couldn’t be bothered exchanging money. The machine made a sound as I inserted a quarter into the slot and a faint smile crossed over my weary face as the character selection screen came up and I began to fiddle around for a character. “Why they not have any Vietnamese fighters? HUH?!” I think I must’ve said it a little too loud as a small Japanese kid popped his head up in front of the screen and replied, His voice annoyed the shit out of me but he spoke relatively fluent English, “Because there are no good fighters from Vietnam….” I raised one of my eyebrows as the character selection timer began to count down as I struggled to see over the young kid’s head, “Get out my way Du Mah!” I hoped the kid could understand Vietnamese, Now who’s smart White Boy?! “You shouldn’t swear at people Charlie!” the kid replied with a frown on his face. All of a sudden I realized what the kid had said and I looked down at him as a snarl formed over my face and I reached out and grabbed him with one of my sea wrinkled hands around the neck, lifting him up from in front of the screen and bringing him to my eye level. I heard the poor kid gulp and quiver as he began to sweat as I pulled him close and whispered to him, “If you tell anyone I here in this mall….I going to break your little face! Understand White Boy?!” The kid began to nod furiously as my snarl changed into a menacing grin and I held him up in the air for a little while longer while enjoying the moment. “There he is!” I heard an angry female shout from behind me as I turned around to see two uniformed security guards with batons in there hands advancing on me. I dropped the kid out of my hands and stepped back into a fighting stance waiting for the security to pounce on me, Bring it on muh-fuckers…I not in the mood for any Japanese bullshit! “You want trouble….” I cracked my knuckles as I spoke, “You got trouble!” I shook my head and reared my fist back ready to knock the closest security guard when another voice could be heard yelling from the background. It was Udai, “Stop! That’s Master Jung Loc! PURE Jnr. Heavy Weight Champion! Don’t arrest him!” He just got himself into big trouble… Before I knew it there were camera’s flashing around my face and all the people in the mall were rushing to see me as I sighed and watched the two security guards and the bleeding Udai disappear underneath the crowd. All of a sudden members of the media began to appear asking questions in fluent English as I just glared as a camera poked up from above a crowd member. “Jung, how long are you planning to stay in Tokyo?” One of the people holding a notepad in the crowd asked. Before I had time to answer yet another question was flung at me from another direction, “Is it true that TokyoPop is paying you to secure a PURE win at PCW Red October?” I gulped because I knew shit was about to hit the fan, Fuckin’ White Boys!! Just as I opened my mouth everyone swarmed in around me…I didn’t know what to do. People began to yell and scream, girls began to reach out to touch me and member of the media began to ask questions, there was nothing more I could do than just swing blindly. I collected two people at the front of the crowd, knocking them down as the advancing crowd trampled on them, I tucked my knees in and left up onto the Tekken 4 podium before screaming at the top of my lungs. “Fuckin’ leave me alone Du Mah Mai!!” the crowd hushed and began to fall back stunned by myoutburst. Finally they get the fuckin’ picture! I stepped down from the podium as I began to heard a shrill whistle blast through the noise of the crowd. A path was suddenly cleared and several armed security officers ran up to me as a smile of relief crossed over my face. “Finally you come to give me some security…how much I owe you?” I hesitated with the last part of my sentence not wanting to fork out and money. A security officer smiled from behind his helmet and sunglasses as he reached out and grabbed a-hold of my hand as I looked down in disbelief, “That’s right, Jung Loc…you’re coming with us!” -=***=- I not believe this…. I in a country for little over five fuckin’ minutes and already I broke one White Boy’s nose, threatened some little kid, knocked two reporters and got dragged into custordy…what the fuck up this is?! They think they can touch me?! They think they can drag me up here?! I show them…. -=***=- “Thank you for your cooperation men, you will be paid well for this!” a familiar looking Businessman spoke to the row of security officers who were lined on the wall behind me. As if robot controlled they all nodded in unison before leaving the room in single file, leaving me in a huge executive office with Udai holding ice on his nose on another leather chair next to me. The big familiar looking Businessman shook his head before speaking, “You are making a mockery of the company! I cannot believe I assigned you to look after Jung here Udai…you are a disgrace!” Udai hung his head in shame next to me as I took a look down at my knuckles which had begun to grow red and swell up, Must’ve hit that White Boy in the tooth…stupid White Boys! “As for you Master Loc…” the man trailed off as I looked away from my knuckles and up at him, “Yeah, what you want?” i shot back. The man continued to shake his head before he advanced on me and bared his teeth in my face speaking in almost a whisper, “If you pull a stunt like that again….I’m going to make sure that you get paid for none of your work and that TokyoPop makes an example out of you!” I sat back in my seat stunned, This guy have really bad breath!! “Just because you hold a PURE Title and you work for TokyoPop doesn’t mean you can do as you please when you please! This is the last time and your final warning…for the both of you!” the man trailed on once more as Udai looked up and over at him. “If you two so much as hit a mediocre article in a daily newspaper I will make sure you both get ‘paid’ accordingly! TokyoPop doesn’t like attention, there is enough rumors going around already! We do not need you two making them seem real!” the man leant off from over me. “This is the last time Jung…” “Last time for what?!” I snapped back in a smart ass tone, ”For you to screw things up for the company!”
