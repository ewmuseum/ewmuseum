A Chat With Allah!?!?

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/09/02 The camera fades in… The Chef is shown in his dressing room, preparing for a match against Boda. He laces up his dark black military boots, buttons his desert camouflage jacket, and zips up his Islamic Militant pants. He brushes his thick black beard, removing all of the pests and small objects that find their way in there. The Chef: Allah said there’d be days like this, there’d be days like this my Allah said! (Then in a girlish Motown voice) ALLAH SAID! Allah said there’d be days like this, there’d be days like this my Allah said! (Then again in the girlish Motown voice) ALLAH SAID, WHA-OOP DEE-OOP!!! The dressing room door suddenly bursts open, and in steps the Mastodon we call Bertha. Bertha: What the f*ck are you doing? The Chef answers smugly. The Chef: What does it look like I’m doing…? Bertha: Pulling f*cking popcorn shrimp out of your b*tch @$$ beard? The Chef: Yes…and I believe that will be the last time we go to the Long John Silver’s with the cook that has turret’s syndrome! Bertha: At you least you didn’t f*cking hushpuppies up the mother f*cking sh*tty as f*ck nose, f*cko! The Chef: Yeah, well you deserved them after stealing that girl’s Chicken Plank! Bertha: I was f*cking hungry, and she wasn’t going to f*cking eat the mother f*cker! The Chef: Still that’s no reason to steal from an infidel child! The Chef tries to calm himself down. The Chef: Ok, enough of that. What I’m really doing is preparing for my match against Boda… Bertha: Why so f*cking early!?!? The Chef then realizes he’s a day early for the match and smacks himself in the forehead. The Chef: You know, sometimes I think that if Allah was ever going to give the world an enema, he’s stick the tube right here… The Chef points to his posterior. Bertha: That f*cking reminds me! Bertha reaches into the backside of her black sweatpants, and pulls out a buttered corn on the cob. She then takes a huge bite out of it, spraying some of the watery butter in the face of the Chef. He wipes the butter away. The Chef: Why did you place that in your pants? Bertha: To f*cking keep it warm! It fits nicely in between my mother f*cki… The Chef: I’ve heard enough! The Chef waves Bertha away. Bertha exits the room. Bertha: (Her voice gradually reduces volume as she waddles away) Ok, but you f*cking asked for it, and I f*cking told you, so stop f*cking getting b*tch @$$ f*cking grossed out by motha f*ckas like me or getting pissy sh*t angry with me, ho skank @$$ b*tch! The Chef sighs and sits on his chair and stares into the mirror. The Chef: Allah…said…there’d…be…days…like…this… The Chef slowly begins to fall into a phase of slumber. His eyes gradually begin to close, and he yawns every few seconds. His head begins to nod and he closes his eyes completely when… “CHEEEEEEFFFFFF!!!” The Chef: Wha? Huh? Wha? Juh? Bah? Kah? “CHEEEEEEFFFFFF!!!” The Chef: Bertha, is that you again? I’m tired of you pretending to be Allah… “Nope, it’s really me this time…” The Chef: ALLAH!?!? “Yep.” The Chef: Really!?!? “Yeppers.” The Chef: No kidding!?!? “Ceautinly! Knuk Knuk…” The Chef: Ok, then answer this, whom did I lose my virginity to? “You lost your virginity to…Bebette the webbed toed whore! The Chef: Wow, it really is you!!! Where are you? “You can’t see me, but I’m here…” The Chef: That doesn’t make sense… “I’m not tangible you dope! Now prostrate before me!” The Chef: Can do sir! The Chef prostrates on the ground and chants some sort of Islamic gibberish. “Excellent! Since I see that you are humble and penitent before me, I want to reveal to you something that only a select few men have seen!!!” The Chef: Don’t tell me, you’ve got that Porno of Britney Spears!?!? “Ye…I mean NOOOOOOO! I’m talking about the meaning of life!” The Chef: Why would I want something like that? “Fine, just waste my time and your opportunity to see me!” The Chef: Ok Ok, just let me prostrate some more, I don’t think I’m humble enough… “Stop that, It’s only fun when I order you to do it! Now, let’s get down to business…” The Chef: Sure, ok, what’s the meaning of life?” “Hold on, first I must ask thee these questions three…” The Chef: This sounds very complicated… These hopefully aren’t questions about swallows are they? “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!” The Chef: (Quietly) Ok… “If you prove to be both penitent and wise, I shall reveal to you the meaning of life!” The Chef: Ok, but I’m not giving out my credit card number… If Allah had a forehead, he’d slap it right now. “Question numero uno, what is my favorite song?” The Chef: What kind of question is that!?!? “Ahem, first of all, I’m Allah, so I make the rules! Second, I’m asking you to look into my mind and imagine the world through my eyes. Then and only then will you be able to see the truth being the world’s mysteries!” The Chef: That was very eloquently put, I’ll guess…hmmm… The Chef thinks for a moment. If he were Allah, what song would he like? The Chef: ……………………… “ENGHHH!!! Time’s up, I need an answer!” The Chef: Ummm, “It’s Raining Men”? “Damn… Ok you got one right…” The Chef: I did? Yay me! HUZZAH!!! Praise you!!! “Ok, enough with the praising already, let’s head to Question dos, Where’s the Beef?” The Chef: Hmmm, isn’t this a rhetorical question? “Um, what?” The Chef: Rhetorical!?!? Don’t tell me the master of the universe doesn’t know the Earth’s vocabulary!!! “HeMan doesn’t know?” The Chef: Wait a second…did you just squeak? “No, no I didn’t” The Chef: I noticed a very high-pitched voice there for a moment, almost as if it was… The Chef pulls the mirror out from the wall and sees a small eyehole sticking through the wall. The Chef: AH HA!!! Just as I expected, NO GOOD TEENS!!! The Chef picks up his Koran as some funky 70s music starts to play in the background. The Chef: It’s time to go to work. The camera goes to slow motion. Out from the room opposite the Chef’s dressing room, the Pimply Faced Intern Kid, and some of his pimply-faced friends burst out from the doorway and race down the hall. The Chef comes flying out of his doorway, carrying the Koran, and puts the peddle to the metal, quickly catching up with the kids. PFIK: (Breathing heavily) Damn, I…need…to…cut…down…on…the…Nintendo…and…fast…food….UGH… The Pimply Faced Intern Kid drops onto the floor, exhausted. PFIK: Guys…wait…up… The Chef catches up with PFIK, and quickly grabs him by the collar, interrogating him. The Chef: So, you think you’re real radical imitating my god, huh? PFIK: Not really, my friend just dared me… The Chef: Well, I don’t who should be more ashamed… Me for believing that you were Allah, or You for liking “It’s Raining Men”!!!??? PFIK: Probably you… The Chef holds PFIK closer, tightening his grip. The Chef: Wise guy eh? Well, I’ll show you what a true believer in the power of Allah can do! The Chef tosses the Koran onto the ground. He quickly grabs PFIK by the hair and PLANTS HIS FACE AGAINST THE KORAN ON THE FLOOR WITH A CONVERSION!!! The Chef stands back up and speaks to PFIK. The Chef: Praise be to Allah for punks like you, because who else would be ignorant enough to mess with Allah's Champion?!?! PFIK: Ugh... The Chef: Enjoy that book Mr. Intern Kid; perhaps if you read it, you might be able to become a better person like yours truly. Adios Enemigo! The Chef walks away from the battered and bloodied PFIK, but then stops after realizing one important factor. The Chef: How did he know about Bebette!?!? DUN! DUN! DUNNNNNNNNNN! The Chef continues to ponder the mystery as the camera fades to black.
