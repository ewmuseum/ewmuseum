The Aftermath...

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/2/03 “Matt…” Sitting on his leather couch, deep in thought, Jackson ignores the female voice that just came off from the left of him…he doesn’t want to look Haley in the eyes, he hasn’t been able to ever since, he killed Stephan. The feelings running through his body are some he has never felt before…the feeling of remorse. Taking a human life never had an effect on the Stampede, until now, suddenly, he wishes that the situation could have been handled differently. Possibly Haley had rubbed off on him, more then he knew. She’s the one who wants him to stop killing and not vice-versa. “Matt!” she screams, walking over the white carpet, getting closer to the young agent. Her blonde hair swaying, in unison with her denim jeans and purple tee shirt, her slender face, one of anger, as she marches toward Matt. He pays no notice to her, as he sits on the couch, his feet kicked up on the large oak coffee table in front of him…his head bowed in mourning. She doesn’t care…she just keeps walking towards him, dodging the two leather recliners off to Matt’s left. Taking a seat next to him, the beautiful blonde, wraps her arms around his neck and pulls him closer to her…he still doesn’t take his green eyes off of the table in front of him, where his pistol sits, they haven’t moved ever since they got back to the small Boston apartment, just staying locked onto the killing device. He doesn’t understand, why these feelings are going through him, but they are and it’s something he knows he can’t figure out right now. His mind is too far lost in it’s chaotic thoughts, to consider anything realistic right now, which is strange to him, being that he was calm and cool, when that trigger was pulled, but now he’s a wreck. He doesn’t like the feeling. Even in PCW, when the chips seem to be down, he finds a way out of every situation, but this time, there isn’t anyway out of the feelings that he’s having…it was the only way out, the only way to protect Haley, but he still can’t help feel, that maybe he did something wrong. He took a man’s life, no matter how justified it was, he still took another humans life, it wasn’t the first time and it probably won’t be the last time, but none the less…it was a man’s life that he took, a man who probably has a family, friends and others in his life, but he will never see them again…they will mourn him and wonder why his life was taken. Stop it! His mind yells out, cutting through all the other thoughts that are filling his head. You had to kill him, there was no other way…this is what you were trained to do. There’s no way around it, you are a killer and that’s all you will ever be…you can’t beat the Hitokiri inside of you; it’s the only thing you know…it’s the only skills that you have. Suck it up, and just go on like you always do. Haley continues to wrap her arms tighter around his neck, paying no notice to the look of distain that’s crossed his angular face. She doesn’t understand, what’s going through his head right now, she just believes him to be uncaring about the man’s life he took, just a few short hours before, but he does care…there’s no way around it, he cares even though he doesn’t want too. Without a word, he throws Haley’s arms off of his neck and stands up to his full five foot eight inches of height…he runs his hands through his large spiky blonde hair and reaches down to the table and grabs his Berretta and sticks it in the shoulder holster and walks away, towards the door of the apartment, his black boots making huge indents in the carpet. “Matt…” The door shuts behind him and Haley is left, alone. The tears begin to fall, from her pale blue eyes, wondering why exactly the Stampede is handling himself like this…why he is pushing himself away from her, when she only wants to help. ~>~…… Standing outside in the cool Boston air, Matt pulls his long blood red trench coat tighter, trying to keep himself a little warmer. It’s been a while since he wore this trench coat, but it’s reservation was for the Stampede and now that he’s back, there’s no reason for him not place it back on…it’s the one thing everyone knows the famed killer by and that’s what he is once again, there’s no way around it, remorse or not, once he pulled that trigger he was back. Killing to protect once again, it’s his life. Walking down the parking deck, Matt listens silently, as his boots thump against the cement ground under his feet, careful not to get lost in his thoughts and bump into the pillars that are on the right and left of him, each one just a few feet apart, from the one in front or behind it. Hiding the rows of cars, which rest behind them, just hidden from Jackson’s vision except for the few that stand between the pillars, none to distinct from the one next to it. None of it would make an impression on him anyways, as his thoughts are still stuck on that room…the death of another human. He is too pure now, not the hardened man he was so long ago, but it’s not a bad thing, because he will still kill. The words of his instructor still play through his mind: Have you killed? There’s nothing pretty about this…it’s murder. In order to create a new world, one must tear down the old. It’s a repulsive job, but it must be done. For the new world…can you kill? To protect this country and make it a new can you kill? All he could do at that moment, was nod yes…it had been what he was training for, what the CIA had been making him. People might have found it hard to believe he had been trained since he had been able to walk, but it was what they did…to make him into the best, the only killer that could do what they needed and wanted, to protect the country, that needed protection…to protect the people inside it. Saving those who were too weak to save themselves, that’s why he is pure. For some reason, he can’t get it into his head this time, he knows he did what he did to save Haley, but he still can’t help feel bad for the man he killed…he doesn’t want to, but it’s burned itself into it’s brain that he must. His mind continues into anarchy, as he walks towards his car, the trench coat flapping in the wind, to reveal his clothing below; a full black jump suit, with black gloves and hard black knee pads and elbow pads. Everything is ready to do what he must, though he’s not entirely sure what that is. Maybe it’s to protect those weaker then me? Too defend all the good in PCW, but I can’t say that for sure. His questions and troubles, begin to probe his mind looking for an answer, but come up empty handed…getting nothing even from the deep dark recesses for his mind, everything is too much of a blur for him to think right now, to figure out his purpose. Over the past few hours, PCW hasn’t even entered into his thoughts…he wants to think about his upcoming matches and prepare for them, but it’s been far to hard, as his mind is to far gone on Stephan. He knows that Robertson will be a good foe and Abner will be even better, but it’s not what he cares about right now. The three people he is facing over the next week are nothing more then names, who stand a chance to beat him…just like Edwards did, he is no long invincible, it’s been shown that he can be beat and it’s been shown that just about any run of the mill person can do it. He wasn’t meant to be a wrestler in the beginning, but now it’s one of the only things he has left to hold on too…when Kannon brought him in, it was to destroy the PCW, but he doesn’t want to do that anymore, he wants to protect it from the men…the men he answers too. The only way he can do that is too fight…to survive. This week is just another test of his character, being double booked on both shows. Facing a man who made his return to the PCW, just a few weeks ago…he knows Abner will be hungry to prove that he still has it, but Matt isn’t going to let that get to him. He’s not going to just lay down for the man…he’s going to welcome him to the new era of PCW, the era where he is slowly becoming king. Only one man on the roster can say he’s beaten Jackson, and his win is even questionable, but that doesn’t matter to Abner…all he cares about is living in his shabby hick home, wishing that he could be Matt Jackson. You want to be me…your not good enough, his mind sneers, through the constant thoughts that are flooding it. Everything I stand for, makes me different, makes me better then some back water hick…this isn’t just another Friday on the farm, he chuckles at his last comment to himself and continues to walk…not paying any mind to the rather dark garage. Abner can’t be of main concern though…I have to get past Robertson first, he tells himself…knowing that’s going to be his biggest foe of the week. The man who was probably going to give him his hardest time when obtaining the Raising Star Title, but alas, he was hurt by injuries and now he’s just coming back. Hungry to get that first match out of the way, hoping that he can pick up a win and prove, that he still has the fire inside of him, but the only problem is, he couldn’t have picked a worse week to come back…to face Matt Jackson. After, losing to Edwards, he knows he has a lot more to prove and he’s not going to get beaten by a man, who simply believes in the same things as him. That’s the problem with you Robertson, his mind bellows out. We share similar ideals, but in reality, you don’t have the faith to follow through with them…when it comes time, you just aren’t ready to go, as far as I. You have to ask yourself how far are you going to take this…the question is not how far. The question is do you possess the constitution, the depth of faith to go as far as is needed? I know I do… Getting closer to his car, Matt stops for a moment and takes a deep breath, causing his barrel of a chest to heave up and make contact with the wooden rosary, which is placed around his neck. It brings a tiny smile to his lips, knowing at least when he did kill Stephan, it was morally correct in some ones eyes…the only eyes that matter when final judgement is passed. Which could be at any time in Jackson’s hellish life…that’s the reason the rosary brings him a bit of happiness. ~><>…<><~ Reaching his car, the young agent pulls out a set of keys, from his trench coat pocket and turns off the alarm of the mid-night blue Viper. He goes to open the door, but he stops himself, as he hears tires squealing and a vehicle moving, in his direction very quickly…spinning around, he sees an unmarked white van speeding down the narrow row, which separates the two lines of cars from each other. Jackson quickly takes a step back and unbuttons the top button of his coat. The van keeps coming, gathering speed, as it cuts down the fifteen feet between Matt’s car and it’s current position. Matt grips the rosary tight and tucks it inside of his coat…preparing for the worst, but he isn’t scared or worried, he knew this day would probably come…sooner than later. Its just part of the life of being a Hitokiri…an assassin. He’s known this was going to happen, almost since he could form his own thoughts, killing as many people as he has, is bound to anger someone, or some government, who would want his head. Suddenly the van slams on its breaks, stopping in front of Jackson’s car in the narrow path way. Matt’s bright green eyes, light up like the forth of July, not believing that they didn’t just run him over…keeping his eyes glued to the van, the Stampede, stands unmoving, except for the flaps of his trench coat, which sways in the wind, causing him no concern, as his mind is locked into full out defense mode, getting ready for whatever is about to happen. In one quick movement, what is about to happen turns into what is happening, as the door of the van swings open and out steps a rather large man, compared to the smaller, more built Jackson. He stands at about six foot three inches and he is decked out in a white cowboy hat, a flannel shirt, and jeans, which are held up by a large belt buckle. Locked into place on the belt are two holsters, each containing a six-shooter, looking like that came out of a bad Western. Great the man they sent to kill me is a John Wayne wantabe…this is fucking ridiculous, his mind sneers…his face also sneers, showing his dislike for this new situation, but his will never weavers. He continues to stare into the dark eyes of the hick standing before him. The hick makes no motion, except to take a few steps with his long legs and step closer to Matt. Matt makes no motion to move, proving that he doesn’t fear the much taller man, who isn’t that much taller then the five foot eight inch agent, because his long spiky blonde hair adds about a foot to him and his boots add another six inches. Even with all that, the man is still gigantic compared to Jackson, just in his body weight alone, but the Chameleon, has never backed down before and he doesn’t plan on doing it now…it’s not what he was trained to do. “I’m sure y’all be reckon who in the moonshine hell I am,” the hick begins. “Well I am going to tell y’all right now Agent Jackson. I am Abnear; I believe you met my brother Stephan a few hours ago…you can’t believe I was just going to let you get out of that alive can you. I don’t totally agree with what he was doing hoss, but you killed him in cold blood and now it’s your turn to pay with your life.” This man actually believes I am going to take him seriously? He’s just lucky I am stopping with just his brother…this isn’t some kind of sport, he yells out in his mind. I just hope this guy is smart enough to turn around and get back in his van and leave…then everything will be fine. “Y’all know your pretty silent hoss, for being a famed CIA Agent and all…yes I know all about you, my brother had folders and papers all over about you. I believe the thing they called you was the Humanoid Typhoon…destroying people in your wake, never leaving survivors, even women and children if necessary…whole thing made me sick,” Abnear yells out at Jackson, who is still standing unmoving, just keeping his eyes locked in place. That was all in my past…I’m not like that anymore, he thinks, wondering why he did do the things he did, but there’s no way to change the past…at the time he believed he was doing the right thing and if he had to do it all over again, he probably would…in fact he knows he would, because it was to protect the freedoms of this country, his country. “ANSWER ME!” Abnear screams, his stone face almost turning bright red with anger…anger at the agent for showing almost no emotion, as he just looks at the hick, with a glazed over look in his eyes…knowing what’s going to happen, when it’s all said and done. “Desiderate una risposta? Il vostro fratello mi ha preso qualcosa, basicamente provando ad avvitarlo da qualcosa che non appartenesse a lui ed ha pagato esso, con la cosa la maggior parte del caro... la sua vita,” Matt tells him in Italian, not wanting to anger the man anymore and further push the situation, but wanting to get his feelings out all the same. “So, y’all think it’s funny to reply in a different tongue…well no matter, I didn’t wear my six-shooters for nothing, you spiky haired freak, I wore them to end your life and that’s exactly what I plan on doing hoss,” he tells Jackson, reaching to the holstered pistols to his sides. Do it Matt…take this man’s life, who cares if he hasn’t done anything yet…it’s you or him. Murder this man. His mind screams out, but his body is motionless, as he just stands and watches the man, pull out the two silver pistols, with the ivory handle, that he’s now gripping so tightly. His eyes, there’s something about them…you can see his fear. Fearful that he might die today, but uncaring all along, not worrying, his eyes are so much like mine used to be…like mine still can be. Not wasting another second, Abnear places his chubby finger on the trigger of both pistols, he points them at the Stampede and cocks back the hammers on both of them…making them ready to slay the agent. Matt only squints his eyes a bit, and places his left foot a little behind his right, as so to keep his balance. Without a word, Abnear pulls the trigger on the pistol in his left hand…the bullet just grazes by Jackson, missing him completely, but right after the gun in his right hand goes off, as Matt jumps out of the way behind his car…feeling a sharp pain in his left leg. Collapsing behind the Viper, his eyes quickly dart down to the source of the pain, noticing the blood flowing out of it. Hearing the bullets zing over his head…he quickly pulls the belt from around his waist and ties the wound off…gritting his white teeth in pain, all the while. He got lucky the bullet just grazed his thigh and didn’t go all the way through, but it’s still painful nonetheless. He sits quietly against the front end of his car, listening to Abnear fire off the last few bullets in his guns. Reaching into his trench coat, Matt pulls out the silenced Beretta and waits, finally hearing the clank of Abnear emptying his shells onto the ground and trying to quickly reload his gun…the Chameleon jumps up to his feet, wincing in pain, as he applies some pressure to the left leg. Abnear stumbles back in surprise, but wastes no time getting back to trying to reload his guns. Jackson limps forward a bit, trying to purge the pain from his face…he holds the gun up to Abnear and pulls the trigger, sending a bullet into the hicks heart…the recoil of the pistol almost sends Matt falling back, but he braces himself against his car and scans over the area to make sure no one saw. He pulls the rosary out from his jacket and lets it hang down once more. “…proceed to heaven.” Oh shit the blood! I have to get the blood up, he thinks, as he limps back to his trunk and pops it open, grabbing a clear plastic bottle of ammonia. He pushes himself over to the area where he got shot and sprays the bloodstains, making sure they are well covered, before placing the bottle into his jacket pocket. He doesn’t care that this could be looked at, as self-defense, because Stephan can’t be looked at like that. His knee is no longer flowing like a river, so he stops for a minute…to catch his breath. Looking down at the body of the hick, he knows he has too remove it. Walking over to the body, he pulls the van keys out from the man’s jeans pocket and pops open the sliding door of the van. Taking a few painful steps back to the corpse, he grabs the hick’s arms and starts pulling him towards the side of the vehicle, trying not to put any pressure on his leg. After a few moments, the body is next to the sliding door and bends over, using all his strength to lift up the much heavier man…almost screaming out, as he does so because of the pain that’s surging through him. Matt slams the door shut and goes over to the driver’s side door and pops it open. He starts up and van and backs it up…letting him park it in the spot next to his, knowing that he’s going to have to get rid of the van and the contents inside soon, if he doesn’t want to get in trouble. He doesn’t exit right away though, as he steps to the back and crosses the man’s arms over his chest and puts pennies over his eyes. He bows his head in prayer. his didn’t need to happen again, but I had no choice…this is my life.
