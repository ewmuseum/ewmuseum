The Jade Effect (Part One)

6 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/03/03 Things were looking up for the national boundary transcending Lacuna Firenze “Debris” Devries as her personal plane touched down gently on a small airstrip on the outskirts of her English grounds . Rubber burnt as the speeding wheels met with the dirty tarmac of the landing strip, screeching to a near halt easily as the small plane began to reach the end. Inside, Lacuna was twiddling idly with the edge of her safety belt that held her tightly to the soft, comfortable fabric of the chair. She stretched out her long legs and smoothed out her smart trousers. There was always a plus side to flying privately, especially when the pilot was a member of your staff. Things could be arranged to suit you and would work for you in whatever way you wanted. Plus, legroom was never an issue. Owning the plane was just a nice little touch. It would only take her from her English home to its French counterpart due to its limited fuel capacity, so she had to fly commercially when crossing the Atlantic. Sometimes she wondered why exactly it was that she did not invest in a larger jet, maybe a 747 or the like, then she would never have the legroom problems that you get, even on the best airlines in the best sections. There were always problems, even the reverse had a reverse. “Thank you for flying Devries Airlines” beamed the pilot, Williamson. He had a nice look to him, warm and comfortable with his rugged good looks and medium length fair hair. “Always a pleasure when you pay for it yourself” she replied while releasing herself from the harness and climbing out the door Williamson opened for her. ”It was a good flight, you really are worth my attention. Care to drive me back to the house” “Like I have a choice, I would love to have the honour of driving you, Ma’am” He chuckled lightly with his BBC English accent, Lacuna returning the sentiment with a laugh of her own. Her smart shoes landed light as a cat on the wet tarmac of the ‘U’ shaped runway. She wore a smart (and ludicrously expensive) three-piece suit, her hair was combed neatly back into a ponytail and a pair of reading glasses were perched precariously on the end of her nose. Her right hand held a tan leather briefcase and the look of smug contention on her face would have typed her with successful female businesswomen. In a way she was, but she hadn’t been for a while. She had made herself from next to nothing and she smiled as the thought crossed her mind. To her right, two gold buggies were parked under a neat white canopy to shelter them from the near-constant rain. She placed the briefcase in the back of the buggy marked with a large purple ‘A’ and climbed in, Williamson followed her into the buggy, taking the drivers seat as they began to accelerate along the muddy but neatly mowed grass lawns. She did not intend to talk much today, she was already tired enough as it stands. She had been in France sorting out some stupid mistakes regarding some bills, which her loyal workforce had somehow ‘misplaced’. Needless to say, some shins had been well and truly bruised for weeks to come and the error had been sorted. By now, the house was looming large in her view and they were skirting the water gardens. The endless fountains and beautiful culturing that surrounded them was a sight to remove the breath from any set of lungs around the world. It had cost hundreds of thousands to design, but it was so worth it. One fountain in particular, the centre piece of the area, was a magnificent piece of architecture and would spray a huge mist of water around ten feet into the air, letting it settle gently as minute droplets rather than one giant jet of water. When she had first had it built, she would spend whole days around it, but the busy lifestyle did nothing but cut deeply into the time allocated for relaxation, especially as she worked in an entirely different continent. She would have to spend much more time in America now, especially as she had Jade boarding there while her ‘high quality’ strip club was closed. She couldn’t help but like the girl, even if she was a shit stirring, ill-mannered, half-witted, lower class fool. The problem was, she knew as that as soon as she got back to her Washington home that she would be telling people to clean up after her or having to completely re-order the décor of the whole bloody place. She wasn’t a bad person at heart, Jade that is, she is just the complete opposite of Lacuna. That’s what Lacuna told herself anyway, but recently she had been feeling a little more affiliation with the girl. Just recently she had turned up to help beat off her evil cousins cronies for no apparent reason, other than boredom. For Lacuna, boredom was less of an emotion and more of a way of life, and so she had to try and find as many ways as possible to fill it. This one time it happened to be by helping Jade. The thing that scared her was that she enjoyed it. They made a good team and there was no use in even attempting to deny that simple fact. They would have won the tag team competition in Primetime Central if Jade hadn’t become lazy in her training. Maybe she was at fault for this as well, it was probably a well-founded reason, but Lacuna didn’t like to take the flak for anyone. Maybe she was being hard on the girl, maybe she didn’t deserve all the criticism. She had had a hard life up until now, and didn’t need the extra criticism. And then it slowly started to dawn on poor little Lacuna… she was starting to like Jade. It was just too hard not to. The house was directly in front of them now and the buggy crunched to a halt on the yellow coloured stones, letting Lacuna jump out and stretch out her long arms and legs with a sigh. The huge stained oak door was heaved open by two of Lacuna’s black clad work force and she stepped inside the house. She nodded towards the door openers and they closed it again. She kicked her right leg violently, which sent one of her smart shoes scuttling down the polished mahogany flooring, quickly followed by the other as she repeated the process with her left leg. “ I will get them for you Ma’am” Spoke one of the black clothed door openers as he began to scoot down the hall “Damn right you bloody well will” She smiled, if she couldn’t be mean to the people she paid then the world would be a very lonely place for Lacuna. She glanced around and began to make her way up the impeccably clean stairwell. She was going to visit her second upstairs bedroom today, as it had the biggest bed and was the cosiest, what with it being one of the smallest in the house. She sped down one of the cream walled red carpeted hallways and took the second oak door on the right, turning into a large room (small by her standards) decorated entirely in black and purple, with most of the furnishing being made from beautiful black leather. She began to settle onto the four-poster bed and closed her eyes. The day had been a long one and she deserved a little bit of a rest, well, until teatime anyway. Slowly, consciousness began to slip away, that great feeling when everything begins to slip away… “Lacuna” there was a strong knock on the door to follow the bellow “Brook is on the phone, he says that he is going to require one hell of a pay rise if he is to keep looking after Jade” “Urgh!” She turned over violently, trying desperately to open her eyes “Bring me the phone” The door opened and in came a different black clad man, with thinning grey hair and a bit of a beer belly protruding from his flattish upper body. He handed Lacuna the phone and she grunted in way of saying thank you “What’s this about a pay rise?” She listens patiently for a few seconds before… “SHE’S DOING WHAT?!?!?!”
