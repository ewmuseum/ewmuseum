Pendragon in New York...Travels, part 1 of ?

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-22-2002 (The scene opens to show our resident Knight, Venneman Pendragon, clad in black breeches tucked into high leather boots, shirtless, walking down a long, stone corridor. There are doors lining the walls of what can only be a castle...What better place for a Knight to reside. Anyhow, torches provide the illumination in this windowless environment, and the light is imperfect to say the least; the shadows almost seem to move. After what seems like an eternity in the corridor, Venneman opens a door on his right, and we follow him in. The room is huge, with high ceilings and more torches. Along one wall rests a large canopy bed; along another is an external closet. Pendragon walks over to a desk that looks out through a large circular window and picks up a few envelopes that are strewn on the desk. His mail. Yeah, I guess even people who try to live the knightly life get mail. He plucks through them and finally drops them disgustedly on the table. He sighs and sits down with a shake of his head. Suddenly, the phone that occupies a table next to his bed rings. I guess Knights have phones as well. Pendragon sits listless and lets it ring. After three rings, it stops. A knock on the door brings him back to reality...) Venneman: Enter... (The door opens and a pretty young woman walks through. She is clad in the flowing skirts of a medieval serving girl, and she speaks timidly, as was the custom...) Girl: Lord Pendragon...I have good news. Pendragon: What is it, Ariel? Ariel: I am pleased to inform you, my Lord, that you are invited to participate in two affairs dubbed, 'The Wrestle Vessel, and Tuesday Turmoil.' They said the news would make you happy...You have seemed so down as of late. (At the words 'Wrestle Vessel', Pendragon instantly comes to life. His strange eyes that so often totter on the brink of insanity clear up and he smiles. His enthusiasm is evident in his voice...) Venneman: BATTLE!! Oh I have longed for more action than I have been getting. It has been hard for me to sit idle around the house and wait. But now, not one battle, but TWO!!!TWO BATTLES!! It would appear that I have a little travelling to do...And I guess I should visit a couple of places before I arrive at the first battlefield, a place called 'Jacksonville'....What would be more incentive to shell out the bucks to see the glorious event than to see I, Venneman Pendragon, PCW's Rising Star Champion, on his way to the battlefield? Nothing! (Pendragon picks up the phone and presses the number 9. He waits a moment and then begins to speak into the receiver...) Venneman: Bradley...Get the jet ready. We are going to New York! (He slams the phone down and whirls around to face Ariel. He grabs her hands and dances a couple of steps with her before she blushes and pulls away....) Ariel: Ahh Lord Pendragon...Why do you fly to New York? Your matches are in a place called Jamaica...And Jacksonville. (Instantly Pendragon's animation stops. His eyes grow cold and he stares, dumbfounded, at the wench....) Venneman: Do ye question me, wench? Is it any concern of yours where your Lord decides to go? (Ariel backs away from the large man and stutters some unintelligible sounds. Pendragon shakes his head...) Venneman: Never you mind...Anyhow, pack my things. I have a long week ahead...Oh, and I plan to dress casually today, so lay out my clothes accordingly. (Ariel curtsies deeply but Pendragon waves her off with a motion of his hand. *****FLASH******* The scene changes to show Venneman Pendragon boarding a Lear jet that sits in what appears to be the rear of a full size castle. He now wears a shining silver chain-mail shirt over his breeches and boots. He also wears the PCW Rising Star belt upon his waist. I guess casual means that the golden armor stays inside. Two towers fly the Pendragon sigil, and the place looks straight out of a fairy tale, except for the plane that is. Pendragon shares some final words with a commonly dressed fellow we'll assume is Bradley before he enters the jet...) Venneman: And Xerxes has plenty of food in his compartment? It is not a long flight, but I do respect the noble animal... Bradley: Aye Lord, he does. He should be a happy horse...And you know he does enjoy flying. If that is all, I bid you farewell and good luck. (With a bow, Bradley backs away and Pendragon enters the jet and closes the hatch. In a moment, the plane heads down the runway and takes flight. The scene fades as the plane flies into the distance... ******FLASH******* We now find ourselves on a busy street in what appears to be New York City...That's where he said he was heading, anyhow. The busy New Yorkers hurry this way and that, and traffic is at a standstill, as is the norm for the big city. A few people are now getting out of their cars and stare at something. What is it? Hell, we can't see; it is to the shot's right. Eventually, Venneman Pendragon comes into view. He rides his huge white war-horse and stares crazy eyed at the multitudes. Strapped to his back is the sword Excalibur, and over his right shoulder rests the Rising Star Title. Each man that he looks at shudders a little and involuntarily backs away. A voiceover soon begins...) 'Why is it, every time I come to New York, I have a bad experience? Are the people here so bad? Is the karma associated with the city so suffocating? It all started at the airport. There I was, minding my own business, just like everyone else....' (The scene switches to show a small terminal at LaGuardia International Airport. A glance at the computer screen above tells us this is Pendragon's arrival port. Suddenly, out walks Pendragon. He has his sword strapped to his waist and he leads his giant war-horse behind him. He actually makes it a little way before he is surrounded by three security officers....) Venneman: Ahh my good men of the law...If it is the horse you are in a twist about, I have his papers here in my pocket... (Venneman reaches into his pants pocket and the three instantly draw their weapons. Pendragon puts his hands in the air and gives the trio a quizzical stare...) Officer 1: Horse? I think you need to remain still...You have been stopped due to our increased security in New York. Because we are now able to search based upon racial profiling, we have stopped you as a potential threat. So...What country are you from Mohamed? (Pendragon's eyes burn with their strange insane light. Pendragon looks at the cops, lowers his hands and begins to laugh. Slowly at first, but soon he is bellowing madness to the entire airport. The three security officers keep their guns trained on the massive man in the Silver chain armor...) Venneman: HAHAHA!!! Oh my...You boys had me there for a second...I am from America my lads. Officer 2: A likely story Mohamed...Prolly went to college over here, learned how to make bombs and are plannin on using them right in this airport. Look...You even have a horse... (Pendragon's mirth instantly fades from his voice as he continues his explanation to the ignorant security guards...) Venneman: Now look here...In case the blonde hair and blue eyes failed to tip you off, I have my credentials in my back pocket. And as for the horse, I can assure you that I am not a Trojan. (The three men cock their heads at his reference to the mythological Trojan Horse that proved to be the downfall of fabled Troy...) Venneman: Never mind...Here. (He quickly reaches into his pocket and pulls out his wallet; a leather sack tied with a leather cord. He tosses the sack to the third security guard who drops his gun in an attempt to catch it. Upon impact, the gun goes off, and the three officers jump for cover. Both Pendragon and Xerxes, the horse, remain calm throughout. Pendragon looks down upon the second officer, who appears to have been shot in the foot. He rolls around in great pain...) Officer 2: Oh GOD!!! MY FOOT!! I'M SHOT!! THE LAST ROUNDUP!! (Pendragon shakes his head and retrieves his pouch as he looks disdainfully down on the three 'officers'.) Venneman: Next time, do your job and I am sure everyone will be fine...And now, you shall let my horse and I pass...Your Lord Pendragon commands it. (The two unscathed officers look at each other and mouth 'What the fuck?' as Pendragon hops onto his horse and gallops from the airport, the hoof-beats echoing into the distance... *******FLASH******* Back on the street, a crowd has gathered around the angered King. They follow the enigmatic superstar through the crowded streets of New York as he rides his horse. Where are the real cops I wonder?) 'I suppose there is a bright side to everything...At least they didn't lose my luggage; of course, I left it on MY plane, or I am sure it would be gone. It must be the poor energy of this town...its aura clouds even the purest of intentions. A warrior comes to town in prelude to great battles, and all these blathering idiots can do is follow him around slack jawed like a bunch of damned sheep. What is the glory in this?' (Pendragon reins up his horse and stares at the crowd, who in turn stare back at him. In a city full of pierced freaks, spike haired punks and two-dollar whores, how anyone can stare at the great man upon the horse is beyond me. Pendragon draws his sword and rests it upon his left shoulder as he rises in his saddle and begins to speak in a voice that seems perfectly suited to command...Or rule...) Venneman: MY PEOPLE!! I have journeyed long to bring to thee a message! Lord Pendragon, your King...I shall soon be waged in a battle with evil that may decide the fate of my kingdom...The fate of you, my loyal subjects. I must defend my dominion from the evil that would consume the hearts of good men such as thee! In a place called Jacksonville waits a being who makes a fit adversary for a Knight...A goblin, and the most evil of the race that remains upon earth...Thought vanquished long ago, a remnant of a remnant has remained...It is up to me, Lord King Pendragon, to rid the world of this plague that would covet your King's gold... (He taps the belt draped across his shoulder...) Venneman: From there, I will wage the battle for freedom onto the sea...I do battle with four men who I have already bested for my gold, the same goblin that I go to meet in 'Jacksonville' and a man unworthy of residing in my realm at all...It will be a glorious day, and I command you, my loyal subjects, to send what you can to your King on his momentous battles...You may very well help to decide the fate of MY entire free world...Be sure to tune in on PPV...But most of all, my children... (Pendragon lifts his sword towards the heavens and Xerxes rears up on his hind legs...Crazily, Pendragon bellows...) Venneman: PREPARE FOR THE PAIN!!! ONWARD SOLDIERS!! WE RIDE!! (Venneman takes off in the direction that he came from, Xerxes at a full gallop. The few people who stayed to hear Pendragon speak look at each other and shrug...I guess New Yorkers have finally seen it all. Or have they? After all; it IS New York!) >>>>>>TO BE CONTINUED
