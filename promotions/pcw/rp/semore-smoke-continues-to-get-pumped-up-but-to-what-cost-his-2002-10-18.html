Semore Smoke continues to get pumped up, but to what cost? His sanity?

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 18, 2002 OOC: If the last two didn't make much sense, then they're not supposed to. It's just Smoke being depressed and going nuts after his lost to The End, and how determined he is to win the Continental title.{{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up where it last left off, with Semore Smoke nodding back and forth chanting the same thing over and over again. This time you see several bills and coins in front of him as people keep dropping him money in pity as they either don't know who he is and believe that he needs money for help, or just think that since he's on the ground that he's some loser who needs money and they're just trying to be charitable. Smoke looks around with the same half-cocked grin on his face and stands up. He bends over and rakes the money that he's gotten into his hand and puts it into his pocket. He then looks straight with the same crazed/high looking face. He walks forward and passes the toy store he had seen before, he continues to walk forward and passes several stores, some of which have American backgrounds. Like a Radio Shack, then a Foot Locker, and Foot Locker Jr. next to it. A few jewelry stores are up next, and then a small restaurant by the name of 'K&W' a chain that has already become famous in the United States, seems that Japan is catching up on American food. He passes a sushi shop now that is jam packed full of different people, one of which looks like Skitzo from PCW, but Semore just continues on, without even thinking that it could be him. Smoke begins to talk to himself again as he walks towards the arcade… Semore Smoke: Well Smokey, there's the Arcade, do you go in and play the games? Well, do you? No! You go down that way, yes to the left and buy some food from the food court to get ready for your match, you need food, and the other wrestlers will be here soon, yep, soon. {{{{People are now staring at Smoke as he walks away from them. Smoke now starts to make sense as he passes the arcade and turns to the left towards the food court part of the mall…}}}} Semore Smoke: You lost your Extreme title, no big deal, but tonight, you will win the Continental, if not, you will definitely flip. Yes, you think you're crazy now Mr. Smoke, you haven't seen anything yet! You'll need psychiatric help after tonight for sure! Whatever you say, you demon! You're the result of The End! And The End will pay later on, tonight, my aggression will be taken out on the entire Primetime Championship Wrestling roster! {{{{Smoke walks up to the first food set-up he sees, which is a pizzeria. Smoke looks up at the menu and sees exactly what he wants for the evening before he goes out to fight for the title that he's got his eyes set up for good…}}}} Semore Smoke: He wants a large pepperoni pizza! {{{{The man behind the counter luckily speaks English, and he looks behind Smoke and then says with a confused look on his face…}}}} Man: He? He who? {{{{Smoke points to himself this time and says once again…}}}} Semore Smoke: He wants a large pepperoni pizza! Man: You mean you want a large pepperoni pizza. Semore Smoke: Yes! My friend in my head here and I want a pizza! Fix it now! {{{{The man just shrugs his shoulders and then walks over towards the oversized oven that all pizzerias use to make their pizzas. He opens it up and then grabs a large tool that holds a pizza from beside the oven. He turns around and opens up a small freezer and puts the tool into it and grabs a large pepperoni pizza from inside the freezer and pulls it out. He then turns around and sticks the pizza into the oven and closes the door to it. He puts the tool back where he got it and turns the knob of the oven on high. Smoke starts to get impatient after just a minute of waiting…}}}} Semore Smoke: Tell him to hurry up and get that pizza ready! Ok! Hold your horses! Hey… excuse me sir, but is there anyway you can move a little faster? That's a wussy's way of asking! You wuss! You won't win shit in PCW if you keep acting like that! {{{{The man is starting to look frightened at the now schizophrenic Semore Smoke. He then just answer's Smoke's question…}}}} Man: Yes, I can make it go a little faster. {{{{The man reaches behind the oven and turns another knob, as he turns it, flames come rolling out from the oven door, and then settle down. He turns the knob off again, and then goes back to the front and turns the main knob off. He had just increased the gas and caused the pizza to be heated automatically. He opens the oven door, but shakes his hands after opening it as it's extremely hot and burned his hands. He then takes the pizza tool he had before and sticks it into the oven and brings out the cooked pepperoni pizza. He places it into an already open box sitting on the counter and then pushes it towards Smoke. He then walks to the register and hits a button and waits for a price to pop up. When it does, he turns to Smoke and asks him…}}}} Man: So, you're American. Will you be paying in American dollars? {{{{Smoke just nods as he walks towards the register with the pizza in his hand. He puts it back down on the counter and then reaches back into his back pocket, when he realizes that there is nothing there he then yells at himself…}}}} Semore Smoke: You idiot! It's in the other pocket! {{{{Smoke just smirks, he's lost the crooked smile, but is still acting way off. He reaches in the other pocket and pulls out his wallet, he pulls out fifteen dollars, which is what is on the cash register. He hands the money to the man behind the counter who puts it in the cash register and smiles at Smoke. Smoke grabs the pizza and walks away with it. He walks towards a table that is in the food court and places the pizza on the table. He sits down at the chair there and opens up the box again to look at the pizza. He looks at the pizza and then realizes that it's been burn to a crisp!}}} Semore Smoke: Why that no good! You ought to go kick his ass! No I won't, let's forget about eating, the match will start in about thirty minutes, lets just get back to the escalators where we'll get ready for our part of the match. Ok then. {{{{Skitzo, who was originally in the sushi restaurant walks by Smoke and is arguing with himself as well…}}}} Skitzo: You have to win this! You will win this match! You will win! No, I don't think I have much of a chance, no, now you're thinking like Semore Smoke… Hey you! {{{{Smoke stopped his arguing as well as Skitzo did, and Skitzo begins to yell at Semore…}}}} Skitzo: Could the two of you shut up!? We're having a conversation here! Semore Smoke: Whatever you two, we're just getting ready for our match… {{{{Skitzo just turns away and walks away as Smoke gets up, Skitzo gets somewhat out of the scene as Semore Smoke says out of the blue…}}}} Semore Smoke: SQUAWK!!! {{{{Skitzo turns around and just smiles, he then walks away and continues to argue with himself as he was before. Semore just continues to walk away, leaving the burnt pizza behind on the table as he goes. He is still arguing with himself, how will this effect him in the match tonight? Who knows!?}}}}
