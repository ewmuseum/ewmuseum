Preparing to pay the price..

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Preparing to pay the price.. Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: May 24, 2002 Power. Like money, it is the object of many a person's desire. Many lust for it, many crave it, yet few recieve it. Perhaps that is why money begets power. And perhaps that is why absolute power corrupts absolutely. Tyler Nelson fired the 20 percent owners, Politically Incorrect. In doing so, Nelson essentially stripped Politically Incorrect of their leadership of the tag team division. Needless to say, PI won't be defending against teams like Misplaced and the deceased Hank and Ganker. No, Nelson and The Phantom Booker have decided to fire up tag competition. A tournament. A round robin tournament. Eight matches must be had before Politically Incorrect will reign on high as champions. Eight matches...more straight tag matches than Politically Incorrect has had all year. The playing field in the PCW Tag Team Division has been evened. And there is nothing that politicians hate more than a fair fight.. ::The Politically Incorrect limousine is visible outside of Bally's casino in Atlantic City, New Jersey. The two men seem entirely prepared for the rebirth of Primetime Championship Wrestling. The two politicians seem also to be prepared for more than just fighting, as Roger Schultz is with them, carrying a large briefcase, presumeably with PI's bankroll for some gambling.:: William S. Powell: Thank God! Atlantic City may be a hellhole, but at least we have some casinos! I haven't been gambling in ages! Reginald V. Lovecraft: In some ways, it is good, because in the coming weeks, we have a politician's road of problems to deal with. William S. Powell: What do you mean? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Well, for one, now that all tag teams are on an even keel, every team we fucked over is coming after us. Misplaced'll be pissed, Heartbreakers will be pissed, the Cowabunga Connection will DAMN SURE be pissed, not to mention all the new teams that want to make a reputation off defeating Politically Incorrect. ::For the first time in quite a while, Roger Schultz speaks.:: Roger Schultz: That's what happens when you pushed your advantage too far. Sure, you guys cheated like bastards to win the PCW Tag Team Titles, but you did your own cheating for them. The Ultimate Authority thing took you off of your game...you guys aren't used to being on your own. William S. Powell: Yeah...I know....we'll get Nelson back for that someday. But, until then, we own 20 percent of the company. For that reason, I hope Nelson and the Phantom Booker can get their asses in gear, because Xavier Kannon, Tim Walden, and ourselves will reap half the benefits. Roger Schultz: Don't worry, your assets are being well taken care of. I'm working on a deal to get you that second limo you wanted. William S. Powell: Nice...our garage will include that, Politically Incorrect One, and the Judicator. Roger Schultz: The Judicator? ::Lovecraft interrupts:: Reginald V. Lovecraft: It is what we have named the black limousine we arrived here in. Roger Schultz: Ah..very well. Your PCW stock is fluctuating, but we have Powell's share in his family hog business, and Lovecraft's slush fund to keep us going while we wait for Primetime Championship Wrestling to get back on its feet. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Excellent. As long as we do not have to worry about our money situation, we can focus exclusively on two things: Vengeance, and this round robin tournament. William S. Powell: Indeed...we must regain our tag team titles..and the road to do so goes straight through every tag team in PCW.....starting off with The Price Brothers. Roger Schultz: Prices, rest assured that Mr. Lovecraft and Mr. Powell have never found a Price that they could not pay. William S. Powell: That is one of the advantages of being wealthy, one does not have to worry about paying the price. We can easily afford to do so, both monetarily, and inside the ring. Lovecraft and I can take anything you two can throw at us. Reginald V. Lovecraft: It's a simple matter of the aristocracy versus the peasants. The Prices are the peasants of course, and we are the aristocracy. Unfortunately for them, this is not France. The French Revolution, nor the Reign of Terror shall be precedent for what will occur in the ring later today. William S. Powell: The city we are in now was once a beautiful place...a place of happiness and frolic. Now, it has been reduced to the junior Sin City of the United States. The righteous decays...the good turns to bad...it is the way of the world. The way the world is, and the way the world always shall be. And thus, the noble effort of the Prices shall be ended....trapped....in the Halls of Justice. Roger Schultz: Beware, Prices, your time draws near. Your courtroom date has been set for this evening, at Friday Fusion. And, Politically Incorrect has already determined your sentence. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Indeed. We have found you guilty for trying to combat the single greatest tag team entity in Primetime Championship Wrestling. The verdict is 3-0. In other words, the verdict is unaninimous. William S. Powell: Your sentence....hard time in the Halls of Justice. And, once you are trapped within the Hall's iron bars...you will realize that indeed, this day, and ALL days, are ELECTION DAY! Roger Schultz: And, on Election Day, Politically Incorrect ALWAYS TRIUMPHS! ::The three men walk into Bally's, as we fade out to..:: Paid for By The Friends Of Politically Incorrect!Friend or Foe of Tyler Nelson.. ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
