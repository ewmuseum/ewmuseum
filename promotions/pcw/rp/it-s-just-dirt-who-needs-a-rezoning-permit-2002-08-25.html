It's Just Dirt, Who Needs a Rezoning Permit?

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-25-02 (Sand...miles and miles of sand. We are now away from the urban confines of Cairo and only miles away from the ruins of the long dead ancient Egyptian civilization. The hot wind whips around sand dunes, creating a haunting whistle. And the warm North African sun beats down on the sand, making sure there is no place to hide from the heat.) (Out of the natural sounds of the desert come the unnatural sounds of vehicles and men approaching from the Nile. A huge line of vehicles slowly rises from the other side of a towering dune, showing the irresistible force of presidential candidate Frisco's El Partido de la Pantera de Argentina's militia. At least 4 tanks, a dozen humvees, and 200 footsoldiers come marching through the desert determined to destroy something or someone. Of the many vehicles, one silver humvee adorned with fluttering Argentine flags leads the way. The burly gunner on top spins around in his turret, looking for any potential targets.) (We are drawn closer to this vehicle and are finally pulled inside where we see Francisco Valerius looking like a clone of 'Stormin' Norman Schwarzkopf--the famous American general who won the Persian Gulf War. El Presidente wears his desert camouflage gear decorated with his 5 silver stars and countless medals and ribbons. The dictator slips on a pair of dark sunglasses and takes a peak at the rolling dunes outside as he pours himself a glass of STOLICHNAYA VODKA~!) Frisco: 'Look at this boring place. Who in their right mind would want to build a community here? It's in the middle of fucking nowhere! Now that I think about it, it makes sense that this is where Xansiv is from. It's boring, dry, and lacks anything remotely interesting.' (Frisco's new Secretary of Presidential thoughts, Governor Mendoza nods.) Mendoza: 'Si, señor.' (Frisco nods and takes a sip of his vodka.) Frisco: 'However, luckily there is something here somewhat important to destroy. Well, important in the sense of what it will be after it is destroyed. We will destroy that blister on Egypt's ass--Giza and then will replace it with...Argentinatown.' Mendoza: 'What is that, Presidente?' Frisco: 'Well, Mendoza, the way I see it, there are too many countries lacking Argentine communities nowadays. As a matter of fact, there are about none. We should start colonizing the rest of the world to enrich this planet's culture. I have a dream...' (Frisco inhales and smiles.) Frisco: 'That one day every country on this fair planet will have its own mini-Argentina. You see places like San Francisco and New York that have Chinatowns, and China has a billion people! Greedy commie fucks. Argentina has a lot less citizens and only a handful of people who are aware of its beauty and class.' Mendoza: 'Oh...' Frisco: 'Can you believe that 99% of Americans don't know who the great Maradona is?' (Mendoza gasps.) Frisco: 'If you say Maradona, one of those fat, lazy assholes will think you're talking about Madonna and will congratulate themselves on knowing our culture...which they think only consists of a crappy musical motion picture involving that whore.' Mendoza: 'Que malo!' (El Presidente picks up his glass of fine vodka and takes another sip.) Frisco: 'Hmm, we're almost at our targets. I hope Busmuchacho and Ignatio are keeping up...' *SCENE CHANGE* (The sounds of heavy machinery is now much fainter as we see El Magnífico Busmuchacho de Mysterio and El Burro Ignatio de los Galvantes de Santo Maranito by themselves, struggling through the vast Egyptian desert. The previously assaulted Magnífico, still covered in Arabic graffiti, squints his eyes through his mask as he peers ahead at the militia convoy dipping below the horizon.) El Magnífico: 'Dios mio, Ignatio!' (The matted donkey snorts and sluggishly picks up his hooves to carry the Guatemalan luchador across the hot sands.) El Magnífico: '¡El convoy está allá!' (The Guatemalan sighs.) El Magnífico: '¿Por qué no podían tomarnos en un vehículo?' (Ignatio makes an angry grunt.) El Magnífico: 'No me queje. Vaya más rápidamente! (Ignatio quickly bucks his rider to the ground and watches the Guatemalan take a roll in the burning sand.) El Magnífico: 'Ay!' (As Busmuchacho begins to rise, Ignatio runs and drives his mask horns into Busmuchacho's head, knocking him back down. The luchador looks up at his rebelling donkey and shakes his fist.) El Magnífico: 'Que estas haciendo!? Pinche burro!' (Ignatio gives his owner a 'Go fuck yourself' look and begins to walk on without him. El Magnífico reels back in shock.) El Magnífico: 'Ven aca! Ven aca!' (The angry Spanish donkey continues to walk away from his owner. Magnífico dusts himself off and begins to run after him.) El Magnífico: 'Por favor, Ignatio! Te necesito!' (The magnificent one tries to mount his donkey, but is once again knocked to the sand. The rejected high-flyer gets on his knees and pleads to his offended transportation.) El Magnífico: 'No vaya! Por favor!' (Ignatio briefly stops to look back at his pathetic owner whose lip quivers slightly.) El Magnífico: 'Te amo!' (Ignatio finally gives up and jerks his head to his pleading jockey. El Magnífico sprints towards Ignatio, leaping onto his back and kissing the donkey on his forehead.) El Magnífico: '¡Vamos a ayuda a Frisco!' (Ignatio snorts loudly and picks up speed towards Frisco and his militia.) *SCENE CHANGE* (Meanwhile, back at the convoy, the ancient city of Giza is surrounded by Frisco's tanks and armed personnel. Tourists are being rounded up by soldiers and the once calm desert is now a point of conflict.) '¡Capture a las turistas! ¡Capture a las turistas!' '¿Son los explosivos listos!? '¡Traiga la munición!' '¡Apunte sus armas a las pirámides!' (Out of the crazy scene, General Frisco appears with his cigar in his mouth and an officer's sword in his hand. The dictator stomps towards his troops, yelling directions and movements. The brave Argentinean even tackles a tourist who tries to make a run for it.) Frisco: 'Aha! You fucking worm! Trying to get away from us, are you?' (A young man in an Egypt shirt and fanny pack squirms in the dirt and speaks up in a British accent.) Tourist: 'Please, sir! Don't hurt me! I'm just here to see the pyramids with my girlfriend! That's all!' Frisco: 'Why don't you go somewhere nice, like Argentina?' (The tourist frowns in confusion.) Tourist: 'Well...it's a 3rd world country, its economy is shite, and there's nothing to see there.' (Frisco shakes his head and points his sword into the tourist's neck.) Tourist: 'Ahh!' Frisco: 'British swine. For that insult, you will not be allowed to return home. You will remain a prisoner of war until I am elected president, our country finally conquers the United Kingdom, and you are sold to the highest bidding citizen.' (Frisco picks the tourist up by the collar and shoves him to one of his soldiers.) Frisco: 'Che! ¡Puesto le en el carro!' (The soldier nods and jerks the Brit over to an armored transport truck. Frisco continues to walk around with the utmost confidence, directing his army.) Frisco: 'Colonel Juaquez, tell them to move those tanks over 8 kilometers!' (The soldier nods to Frisco and salutes his order.) Colonel Juaquez: 'Si, Señor Presidente!' (The colonel rushes over to the tanks, screaming out...) Colonel Juaquez: '¡Muevan los tanques, ocho kilómetros!' (Frisco remembers something crucial and quickly paces back to his humvee as his soldiers continue to rush around, subduing tourists and commandeering vehicles.) Frisco: 'Damnit!' (Frisco quickly opens the door of his humvee and yells loudly.) Frisco: 'FUCK!' (The object of Frisco's distress happens to be his glass of vodka, which has unfortunately vaporized in the hot Egyptian climate.) Frisco: 'You will go to hell now, Egypt!' (Frisco throws his glass into the sand and marches toward his troops once again with his sword raised.) Frisco: 'Estan listos!?' (Frisco looks around at the whole town of Giza subdued by his fairly small militia and blows a kiss to the famous Sphinx, which unfortunately just had its nose job.) Frisco: '¡Punterían!' (The loading and clicking of weaponry echoes from around the ancient structures.) Frisco: 'Goodbye, Giza, you decrepit old fuck. You may thank your precious Pharaoh for your total destruction.' (Frisco takes one last drag of his Cuban and blows out a cloud of smoke as he flings his cigar towards the base of one of the great pyramids.) Frisco: '¡FUEGO!' (Frisco drops his sword and the already bright landscape becomes even brighter with the explosions of RPGs, tank shells, and grenades. ) BANG! BANG! BANG! BOOOOOOOOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BA-BOOM! BA-BOOM! (After a barrage of tank and small arms fire, the ancient ruins come crashing down to earth in chunks of debris and dust. The troops hit the deck and Frisco quickly hops into his humvee as a huge cloud of dust sweeps over the area...) '¡El Magnífico está viniendo!' (From the comfy interior of the Presidential humvee, Frisco smirks and shakes his head as he sees his Guatemalan servant arriving at the wrong time.) El Magnífico: 'AHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!' Frisco: 'You stupid fuck.' (Frisco waves through the window at Magnífico who is blasted back by the powerful cloud of dust and debris. Both Ignatio and his rider are knocked to the sandy floor and are shunted away many feet, cutting through the hot sands.) El Magnífico: 'AYUDAME! AYUDAME!' (After a few minutes of letting the dirt calm down, Frisco finally emerges to survey the area. El Presidente takes out his sword and begins sifting around the debris as his troops cheer loudly at their accomplishment.) Frisco: 'Excellent.' 'WHOOOOOOOOO!' 'WE GOT IT ALL!' 'FELIZIDADES!' 'BARBARO!' (The brutal destroyer of history looks up at the once glorious Sphinx--which is now decapitated and severely damaged.) Frisco: 'Hey, asshole. I've got a riddle for you...' (Frisco briefly scratches the side of his face with his sword.) Frisco: 'What poses as a warrior but has no brain, no balls, or a heart?' (Frisco cups his ear and approaches the destroyed piece of beloved culture.) Frisco: 'What? What was that?' (Frisco shakes his head.) Frisco: 'I'm sorry, you're wrong. The correct answer was 'The Pharaoh' Titu Xansiv. Tricky, huh? But I assure you it's the right answer. That boy lacks everything that is needed to succeed against me, and now just as I have brought you to a crumbling death, he will be next to join you in oblivion.' (Frisco tips his cap and takes one last look at the horrible scene. He then begins his retreat to his transportation, but El Magnífico finally gets back on his feet and in front of Frisco, covered in dust and the previous graffiti from Cairo.) Frisco: 'Why are you looking so disgraceful? I can't be seen with you now!' El Magnífico: 'Pero, yo...' (Frisco holds his sword up.) Frisco: 'Shush. You find a shower and clean yourself off. Maybe then someone will allow you to travel with them. You are certainly not travelling with me. You've already pissed me off by lagging behind and not helping with our mission.' El Magnífico: 'Pero, pero...' (Frisco shakes his head.) Frisco: 'If you want to do something, go take down what's left of the pyramids.' (Frisco picks up a large rock and tosses it to the dust-covered luchador.) Frisco: 'Have fun.' (El Presidente nods to his lackey and enters his Presidential humvee. El Magnífico frowns as his master is whisked away from the scene.) El Magnífico: 'Arrrrrggggghhhhhhh!' (The angry man leads Ignatio over to the remains of a pyramid and flings the rock at it, but the rock unfortunately bounces off the tough adobe and hits him in the head.) El Magnífico: 'Ay!' (The unlucky luchador grabs at his head and falls back into the sand. Ignatio looks down at his master, and then up to a camel which was previously used for a tour. Ignatio looks down once more, only to shake himself off and head towards the sexy camel.) (The scene fades out from the ruined ruins of Giza and the militia convoy which remains behind, preparing for additional clearance for Egypt's upcoming Argentinatown.)
