The Jealousy

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/24/02 After Turmoil Jaden would not talk to John. He was a plague to her. She did not want anything to do with John and she hated Aeryn although she never met her except through her pictures, holding hands and kissing John and just smiling back at her. These pictures brought tears to Jaden’s eyes. Jaden does not know where she and John stand now or where they messed up. Was he like the rest of her boyfriends, only using her for sex or showing everyone he has a fine woman by his side? Does he truly love her, no one knows but the one she calls her love. After Turmoil… “…What was that John? What is wrong with you? Why are you treating me as if I am a child and wont commit to me anymore,” Jaden says, as hurtful tears run down the sides of her cheek. “No, tell me what that stunt you pulled out there with Jordan! You know what Jaden; I really do not even want to know. I do not want to argue with you and just do not let it happen again. I got to take a shower.” “What…wait,” Jaden says grabbing him by his arm as John turns around. “No, say what’s on your mine, because we are not leaving her until you tell me what is your problem. After you got back from your little trip revenging Aeryn’s death, you make me feel as if she is more to you than me. You have not shown me any kind of affection. When is the last time we went out to eat or spent some quality time together?” “That’s what this is all about! Look, you know I really do not like opening up to people. And, I hope you know I love you and if you don’t I wish you knew. How about after I take a shower, we go out to eat. Meet me at “Palace” in an hour, I have to make a stop before going to the restaurant,” John says as their lips connect, just showing Jaden he still cares for her. Jaden goes to the motel where John and she stay grooming herself to look nice for John and to look presentable because the Palace is a Five-Star Restaurant. As Jaden begins to get herself ready, she just sees Aeryn pictures everywhere. Is this a shrine because oh her death? Jaden does not know what to think. “Maybe I am making to much of the subject.” She cannot help to get annoyed by the pictures because they seem inhabited by the undead, possessed as the eyes of Aeryn begin to follow her around the room. Meanwhile, John is ordering long stem red roses, hoping that this may cheer Jaden up but he does not know what the future will bring. He had the roses sent to his locker room so he would not have to waste time going to get them. Knock! Knock! Knock! “Just a minute,” John says, wiping the stem from the shower and telling the person at the door to give him some time. John quickly grabs a towel and rushes to the door. As he opens the door, he finds Jordan holding his roses. “This was so sudden. You cannot just surprise me with roses like this. I don’t know what to say.” “Jordan give me my fucking roses and get the hell out of my face. I beat you once, once is enough.” The Palace… Jaden sits and waits for John’s arrival. She is getting tired and frustrated. He is more than an hour late. As she gets ready to leave the restaurant John walks in. “I am sorry I took so long I had to take care of some business, he says, as he hands her the beaten roses.” “Thanks for the stems, they will look really nice in the trash!” “I am sorry about that too. I had an unexpected vistor” “Who Aeryn, she mumbles!” “What did you say?” John is getting annoyed of her nagging about Aeryn. “I said, Oh the AIR IN HERE, its cold! Look, can we just eat.” “Yes, let’s eat. Jaden, I wish you could have met Aeryn she was a nice person. I think you would have like her. She was very caring, smart. You know what I mean.” “No, I don’t enlighten me, she says, folding her arms, raising an eyebrow, and rolling her eyes.” “Whoa, is it getting hot in here,” he says, as he loosens he collar. Jaden then snaps her finger… “Yes miss, may I help you.” “Yes, turn on the air over here for this gentleman. He said he is getting hot!” “Ok, miss!” “Ok, you can finish….” “Well-umm…she is nothing like you, totally different!” “WHAT!” Jaden stands up and slaps John across the face before she throws the water in his face. “Wait, Jaden I didn’t mean it like that,” he says wiping the water from his face and chasing Jaden out the high-class restaurant. “Wait, I am sorry!” “I am tired of hearing you say you are sorry! Oh, and I found pictures of me. Do you know where I found them. Under some, stale Twinkies in you dresser drawers. It shows how much you carry about me.” Jaden and John continue to bicker as they walk to the hotel building. It is about a block away. “Can we just finish this at home? You are causing a scene.” “Am I really. Yea, we can finish this at home I have a special surprise for you.” “Jaden what did you do?” John begins to run down the block to the hotel to see what mischief Jaden caused to his room. As John opens the door and begins to see what Jaden has done, he falls to his knees in tears as he watches Aeryn’s face ratter away in the fireplace.
