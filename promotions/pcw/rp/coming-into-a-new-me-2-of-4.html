Date: not entered The soft lyrical vibrations from a small harp resounded through the small bedroom in which Cozette lay. As she opened her eyes, nothing was immediately familiar to her. The yellow paint of the ceiling, along with the brown ceiling fan looked odd her. She took a deep breath as she tried to sit up. Barely propped up on her elbows the room starting spinning as she realized she had the worst hangover in her life and something was wrong with her ribs. Her raven-haired full head hit the pillow quickly knowing that lying down stopped the dizziness. As let out a small groan as she turned her head to the left which was the direction the harp music was coming from, so she thought. On her left she saw a dark wooden high back chair, which must have been old do the age in which the wood looked. It sat next to an almost matching nightstand, on which a basin full of water sat. Cozette tried to remember the last thing that happened to her. All she got was fog, which on confused her more. She glanced back over toward the right seeing a set large wooden double doors, with gleaming brass handles, which was reflecting a decent amount of sunlight straight into her eyes. She closed them so a second feeling momentarily blinded. She groaned a little bit louder, with that the melodious sound of the harp stopped. Cozette bit her lip almost scared. She heard heavy footsteps coming from outside the door. Cozette, like a little child pulled the blankets up to her nose, feeling it would hide her from any danger, which might be coming. Peeking back at the door handles she saw them move as someone was opening the door. She hastily pulled the rest of the covers over her face as the door opened. Sweat started forming on her brow as the heavy steps come closer to the bed. She didn’t know where she was.. She didn’t know who the footsteps where from.. As she thought a little more.. She couldn’t remember her own name… No she did.. She was Cozette dú Pres, a twenty-year-old French woman, who now lived in America. She was about to get marrie… And that was that.. Her fear of the person that was on the other side of the blanket was gone. It was replace with that of pain and suffering, despair and hopelessness, and the will to just want to die. The warm salty tears returned to her eyes as she visualized the Death Valley Driver that took her lover’s life. It replayed over and over. It was the last image she saw and the blackness overtook her again. Tanya looked down at Cozette’s unconscious form the ramshackle apartment. It smelled of puke and alcohol. Everywhere she looked, broken bottles, empty bottles, and just random trash laid all about the white bur-bur carpet. The last time she was here it was a beautiful place. Cozette was always a great housekeeper; the way the place looked now was something she thought she’d never see from her. Cozette was not a person Tanya considered a friend. She was more of an acquaintance, her friend’s girlfriend if you will. Tanya really didn’t feel like taking care of Cozette at the moment. She didn’t feel it was responsibility. Yes she felt empathy for her.. But, she was somewhat happy Cozette wouldn’t be with her friend. Her friend was way too good for a piece of street trash like Cozette. Tanya shook her head. “Je vraiment ne devrais pas penser comme ceci. Après tout le Cozette juste perdu son amour. Et j'ai perdu le cher ami,” she said aloud talking to herself. She walked towards the kitchen noticing a large picture broken on the floor. She bent down and picked it up being careful of the broken glass. She smiled gently as she recognized the picture. It was the one she took of him and Cozette at the little Italian restaurant around the corner. It was the night he proposed to Cozette… She then dropped the picture. “Chienne! Vous l'avez volé de moi.. Je ne vous pardonnerai jamais!” She screamed at Cozette who she knew couldn’t hear her. She glanced back over at Cozette’s motionless body with a scowl. With a full speed run, as much as she could in four-inch high heels, she kicked Cozette on the right side of her ribcage. She repeatedly kicked the comatose woman until she was out of breath. Tanya bent over panting lightly. All of the sudden she heard a soft clapping. Startled she spun around looking for the source. By the door she saw a medium height woman with a short-cropped black hair. Her dark eyes stared at Tanya; they seemed to penetrate her soul. The woman crossed her arms and leaned against the wall. “You must be awful proud of yourself. You beat up a helpless woman. What kind of person are you?” Tanya stood up straight and smile. “A person just leaving. I have taken care of my business.” Tanya stepped over Cozette, taking one last look at her. “Je vous déteste putain. Je vous espère matrice!” As she neared the door, the short-haired female stepped in her way. “That was uncalled for, I think you need to apologize,” she said with a twinge of rage in her voice. She stared at Tanya directly in the eyes. Tanya looked uneasy at her. Now that she was up close to the strange she could tell she was of Asian decent, Chinese most likely. “I will do no such thing. Get out of my way.” With a fluid dead on strike the Chinese woman punch Tanya in the stomach knocking all the air out of her. Tanya doubled over and fell to her knees. The dark-eyed lady removed the bright pink bomber jacket she had been wearing, revealing a solid blue Billabong crop top. More interesting was the intricate tattoo of cobra that wrapped around the length her left arm with its hooded head ending on her left breast. The woman knelt down next Tanya placing her mouth near her ear. “Now.. What is it said you aren’t going to do?” Tanya tried catching her breath to reply. In a gasping voice she replied, “Allez à l'enfer!” Tanya tried spitting on her but missed. The woman shook her head as if she was disappointed. “Hell? I’ve already seen it.” She whipped her head to the side striking Tanya in the temple. Tanya fell to the ground unmoving. The woman sighed as she got to feet picking up her jacket. She walked over to Cozette’s side and placed a hand on her neck feeling for a pulse. “You really did it this time.. Didn’t you Emerald?” She rolled Cozette onto back, and gently picked her up. “Don’t worry.. Van is here.”
