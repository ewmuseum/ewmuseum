The Odd Couple

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/20/02 Mason Creeper and Rudolph von Hackenmann were defeated… But, despite their bad luck in the ring, Rudolph is having some good luck himself. His castle was finally repaired, and he moved out of the shoe factory for good. But now, Rudolph is going to fall into some more horrible luck… He has a visitor…) [The bright sun is hanging in a deep blue sky, shinning down on a serene, winding road. Birds chip and sing as the roadway cuts in and out of the hills. The gravel drive goes on for about two miles before coming to rest on a large stone castle. Here, shrouded behind tall pine and spruce tress, the castle is set in shadow. Peeking into a window and wandering down the hallway, we come to a door that is slightly ajar. We’re able to make our way down a spiral staircase, countless steps turning, turning, and turning. The sound of electricity and a metallic taste fills the atmosphere in this basement laboratory. Rudolph von Hackenmann can be seen hunched over a workbench, stray wires and contraptions strewn about. He is humming something to himself, pausing now and then to scratch his head or complain about something out of place. Reaching down deep, he growls madly, and then…] “Success!” he screams. [RVH wields a small red tablet. He walks over to a large, complicated-looking microwave, pops it in, turns a knob, and DING!] [He opens the door and pulls out a steaming large pepperoni pizza.] “Mmm, smells divine! Hackenmann, you’ve outdone yourself this time!” [Just as he’s about to bite into a slice of pizza, the doorbell rings.] “What? Who the **** could that be at this hour? Hold on!” he dashes to the stairs and takes them two at a time. DING! DING! “Just a second! I’m almost…damn, how many stairs did they put in this place?” DING! DING! DING! “Blast! I’m coming! I’m…ooh, my pulse is racing!” [RVH is walking to the door, holding two fingers to his neck and checking the heart rate on his watch.] “Nice!” he mumbles to himself. “Now who could be…”? [He peeks through the peephole, only to be confronted with a grin most foul.] “AUGH!” Rudolph angrily yanks open the door. “Creeper, what are you doing here? How did you get here? Why are you here?” [Mason Creeper stands before him, his dark green army jacket hanging loosely on his frame. He’s dirty, sweating, and scratching at his cheek, absent eyes taking in nothing.] “I was just walking and thinking and thought that perhaps what was really needed for us…” “Us?” “…was to maybe sit down and…get to know one another.” [He finishes his sentence by chomping his teeth together and shuffling in.] “Hey, whoa wait! You just can’t come unannounced! I was working hard and you interrupted my…!” “Smells like pizza.” [RVH turns in his rant to see Mason walking down the steps to his laboratory.] “Mason! Damn! What the hell do you think you are doing, you disease infested rodent?” [Mason snaps around angrily, but emotion is void from his eyes. However, he talks with a clenched jaw.] “What would you know of my condition? What would you know of being shunned from all that was once warm and welcoming in this world? How would you feel when you’re very existence depends upon an all-consuming fever that…” “Yes, it is pizza. Pepperoni.” “…? Pepperoni you say?” “But let me get it for you, my lab is spotless, impeccably clean! I can’t have some moronic Zombie from the Center for Disease Control poking his scabby fingers—just you wait here!” [Mason simply nods and shuffles out of the large foyer. He finds a divan in a dark corner and sits, kicking his muddy shoes off. Minutes later, Rudolph comes back wearing a towel around his neck and sipping spring water, the pizza tucked in his arm.] “…Do that a couple of times a day for conditioning! Hey Creep-fest, here’s the pi—Aw, look at what you’ve done to my floor! There’s mud everywhere!” [Mr. Creepy stands and grabs a few slices of pizza and heads off down the hall. Rudolph stares aghast at a large sweat stain left by Mason, visible even in the shadows.] “SON OF A ****! You sick, twisted…? Where’re you going?” A gravelly voice replies, “Got anything to quench this thirst? I’m always thirsty, you know.” “That’s the library! ****! Next door on the right is the pantry.” [RVH scampers down the hallway after setting down his pizza. Walking into the expansive kitchen, he sees Mason leaning against the counter, drinking wine from the bottle.] “Not the Chateau Lafayette! Please don’t be…“ “1860? This has waited long enough.” “Oh good lord!” [RVH stumbles back and thumps against the wall.] “I’m not a man, Rudolph.” “You’re telling me…” “Not a man like you. I’m a wanderer…I was kidnapped into a world of unending anger and torment, a writing pain burrowed deep into my skull. I am a Disciple of the Lost, and I must keep moving, not knowing peace, and bringing to those who stand in my way a taste of Hell, much worse than my own. I will be staying with you only periodically, at times when I must conserve what kinetic, Fevered fuel I have to unleash upon the next man…“ “*sigh* If you’re staying, there’s a spare room on the second, no, fifth floor. It’s uh, a little cluttered. Okay, it’s a storeroom! You can stay there, just don’t TOUCH anything! The bathroom is right around the corner from there. Mason, I…“ Rudolph turns to find nothing but a swinging door. Mr. Creepy is gone. LATER Rudolph awakes with gentle sunlight streaming into his room. Lying atop his plush, four-posted bed, he sleepily climbs out from under thick blankets and mountainous pillows. He stretches and yawns, pulling on some morning clothes. RVH shuffles off to his master bathroom adjoined to his room and stops in horror. His large porcelain tub is caked with filth. The once white, pristine tiles are now streaked with blacks and grays. “No…” Glancing at the deep sink, he sees his toothbrush, bristles flattened and toothpaste all over the mirror. “No no no…” A gurgling noise interrupts him, and he turns to see his toilet rapidly filling to the brim. “AUGH! No! NONONO!” Snatching a plunger, he dives at the toilet, slipping on the water and cracking his shin on the lip of the john. Water pours out onto this feet and he screams, plunging frantically at the overflowing water. Finally, the water recedes. Wet and severely pissed, Hackenmann strides down the many halls and corridors and stomps down to the kitchen. Kicking open the door, Mason regards him blankly while forking a frozen waffle into his mouth. The kitchen is in shambles, eggs and milk in a puddle on the floor. The coffee maker is bubbling and steaming, and a knife is jammed into the toaster, the blackened outlet having shorted out. “No…” Mason is wearing a tightly stretched cardigan with RVH stitched in the upper right hand side. A black of khaki slacks have been slashed along the sides with the cuffs at Mason’s shins. He dribbles some sticky syrup down onto his fingers and licks it up from his raw hands. To top things off, his muddy prints have walked crazy circles all over the shiny floor. Rudolph’s face is positively red, going on purple. His hands are clenched so tightly, his nails are beginning to cut into his palms. He’s shaking as finally, the dam bursts “You. YOU! I’m going to kill you!” Mason almost seems to shrug as he shovels another waffle into his mouth and grinds away at it. “I’m Lost.” This Sunday, this strange team Fear Factory takes on the team of Metallyx in the middle of the ring. Will they be able to last until then without killing each other?
