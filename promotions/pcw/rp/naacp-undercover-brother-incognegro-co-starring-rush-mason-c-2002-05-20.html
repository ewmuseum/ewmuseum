NAACP- Undercover Brother, IncogNEGRO [Co-Starring Rush Mason] [Chapter 1. The start.]

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/20/02 Praise be to the God of Crunk. It has happened. The five months of blood, sweat, tears, and more blood, has finally paid off for Jordan X Shabazz. May 12th, 2002, Jordan X Shabazz, along with a whole lot of wrestlers, got fired, brutally, and publicly. Jordan was worried. He had a part time job with the NAACP as a spokesperson, but that wasn't paying him what he needed to survive. He somehow had to find another job. And being an outspoken Civil Rights Extremist, it was hard for Jordan to find the right job. If he worked at a McDonald's he would complain that Ronald McDonald wasn't black. If he worked at Burger King, he'd say he's worth much more then the minimum wage he was earning. If he was working KFC, he would complain that because he's black, he was obliged to eat the greasy chicken. But somehow Jordan HAD to make a buck. So he went back to his roots... Pushing Weight.... For all the white people reading this, that means selling drugs. Jordan started working the streets of Dallas, like a common drug dealer. Once he was a professional wrestling giant, making mad cash, making big waves throughout the wrestling world, including being featured in 'The News' Feud of the week, with Mark Makonie. Then, while he sat at his office in Dallas, Texas, Jordan heard his cellular phone ring. He immediately checked the caller screen to find out who could be calling him. The name said 'Jackson J', which meant Jordan's long time lawyer, and manager, Jake Jackson. The phone conversation went like this. Jordan X Shabazz: 'Jake?' Jake: 'Yeah. Good news man.' Jordan took a deep breath to prepare for what possibly Jake could have deemed as good news. Jordan X Shabazz: 'What is it?' Jake Jackson: 'On Saturday they're going to announce the first few PCW guys and girls, that they've signed so far. Make sure to tune in.' Jordan X Shabazz: 'Sure, whatever.' CLICK! Jordan hung up. He acted like he couldn't give a damn on the phone, but inside, Jordan was more excited than anything. He was jacked, ready, and waiting for his name to be called. Everybody he talked to that was unemployed because of the PCW, told him that he would for sure be one of the people that was rehired. But Jordan didn't know what to think. Saturday night came and went, with Jordan not being one of the ten people that were announced as PCW signees. He was depressed. If he wasn't going to be rehired, where in the world could he find a job as a wrestler? But wait, he had until Sunday to see if he had to find a new job. Sunday came.... And went... With Jordan being announced as one of the talented and lucky few that made the cut. Needless to say, along with the other resigned guys and new signees, Jordan was very happy. His hard work wasn't to waste.... But then, the very same day it was announced that he was signed by the PCW, Jordan got another phone call. This time from Debbie Barker, the NAACP woman in charge of assignments, such as lawsuits, paperwork, complaints, and criminal defense fundraisers. She seemed serious over the phone to Jordan, Jordan didn't know what to think. He walked into her office with a smile no his face, knowing that 500 wrestlers applied, and he was one of the lucky few to be considered talented enough to be signed. He felt elite, superior, if you will. He walked into the office, smiling, full of emotionally happiness. The office was beautiful; she held a high position in the NAACP. Leather chairs, shelves full of law books, and books on Black History. She had numerous plaques lining the walls. Her desk was nice, brown, and large. Her nameplate was made of solid gold, and showed her importance. Jordan wasn't intimidated. He sat down in one of the leather chairs, and quickly noted her beauty. She wears little makeup. Her brownish, reddish, hair was tied back in a bun. She wore a nice looking short skirt, with a business suit like top. She smiled at Jordan, and spoke. 'Mr. Shabazz, as of late you have been proving your worth here at NAACP Dallas. The higher ups have been pressing me to give you a large assignment that they believed only you could do.' Jordan got excited. Anytime he was given a compliment as he just was, he was very happy. This could mean a pay rise! Jordan nodded solemnly at Debbie, and waited for the huge assignment that the higher ups of the NAACP wanted him to do. 'Well, we want you to do some undercover work for us,' she began with a reassuring smile on her face. 'Which basically consists of you trying to infiltrate several Ku Klux Klan groups, and several Neo Nazi groups. Also a few businesses that we presume to be racist.' Jordan takes a deep breath. He smiles weakly. This was a huge assignment. How the hell would he infiltrate the KKK and some Neo Nazi groups, among other things, if he was black himself? 'Okay...' Jordan said unsurely. 'I guess I can do it.' Debbie smiled. 'That's the attitude I want to hear,' she said. 'Your first assignment will be sent straight to your 2 way pager tomorrow morning. Be ready.' And that was the end of that. Our scene opens up in a small apartment in Dallas, Texas. It appears to be kind of cozy. Small, nice place with a small 12 inch TV in one corner of the room, a small couch, a bean bag seat, a small coffee table, small light bulbs that light the room, a PlayStation 2, a small microwave, a small fridge, and several stacks of professional wrestling magazines. Jordan is seated on the small couch reading one of the numerous magazines, this one appears to be Pro Wrestling Illustrated or some extremely white name like that. Suddenly, Jordan's two-way pager begins to buzz. Jordan dropped the magazine to his lap and he picked up the two ways. He checked the text message window, it read Mr. Shabazz, your first mission is to be done with a partner. Not a sexual partner, a male, physically in shape, partner of yours. Perhaps one of your professional wrestling buddies will be good. Your mission is to drive from Dallas to Norma, Oklahoma, and find the congregation of St. Joseph's Baptist church. It's an all white, KKK sponsored church, your job, with your friend, is to somehow infiltrate the church, and make sure you steal all of their funds, which is hidden somewhere in the church. Sorry that I am short on details, the whole situation is sketchy, it'll be hard for you to do alone, so pick the right partner to go in on this with. Mr. Shabazz, we have faith in you, good luck. Debbie Barker, NAACP. Jordan went into his room and packed the few clothes and food he would need for the trip. He would have to either sleep in his car, or pay for a cheap motel, PURE checks hadn't arrived yet, and since he was just resigned by the PCW, they had yet to send him any money. Jordan went into the pocket of his baggy khaki Phat Farm shorts and he pulled out his cell phone. He pressed one of the four numbers on his speed dial. Dialing... 'Hello darkie?' Came the voice from the other line. Fans of the PCW would know this man as PCW wrestler, Rush Mason. 'Hey, Rush, it's your dog, Jordan.' Shabazz tells Rush. 'Ah, the young apprentice, what can I do you for?' 'You're going to have to be my second in command for this... this thing the NAACP wants me to do. It involves spying and stealing from white people.' 'OH HELL YEAH! I'm in! We're going to be like... Mission Impossible!' 'Sure. Just meet me in Tulsa, Oklahoma okay? Take Greyhound, we'll need to drive to a small hick town after that.' 'Sure dog, thanks for calling me up for this.' 'No problem, just be ready dog, it's going to get Crunk up in that bitch.' 'Lates.' CLICK! He packed three pairs of heavily starched jeans, three Phat Farm T-shirts, two pairs of Lugz Boots, an Ecko visor, and his trusty Timex watch. He went to the kitchen and packed three bottles of Kool Aid, one bottle of Perrier, a can of Crunkola~! This was going to be a long trip. ******************************************************** The scene reopens up in the small Greyhound bus station of Tulsa, Oklahoma. It's Tuesday, May 22nd. It's about 5:00 Eastern time. Jordan X Shabazz just spent a few hours driving from Dallas to Tulsa. It was a good trip, which rendered no traffic stops, and no constant following by the police. They've learned to leave the Negroes along from Dallas to Oklahoma, Jordan's constant bitching, and marches on city hall HAVE worked apparently. The place is abuzz with business. Countless people hurry to their bus terminals, and countless people run to their buses and whatnot. There's a large staircase where most of the travelers walk down to get to their destination. The place is hot, muggy, and musty. It doesn't smell too good on the account of a small Arab owned shop. It sells food, drinks, magazines, and cigarettes. Jordan needed food and cigarettes. He hadn't smoked since high school. This was an omen. Turning over a new leaf. In a completely new company. With a bunch of new people... There's only three black wrestlers... How will Jordan survive? Two of his buddies, Legend. Inc and Jason Core were not resigned by the PCW. He had Jon Kano, Rush Mason, and Duke Williams, the new Malice Crew, for partners on PCW’s first show back with the new roster… Time to let the black man loose.
