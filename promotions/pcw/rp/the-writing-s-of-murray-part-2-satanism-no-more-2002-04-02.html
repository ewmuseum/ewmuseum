The Writing's Of Murray Part 2: Satanism No More

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2nd April OOC: Quick note, I know this RP is very short, but it's all I had time for I'm afraid. Expect a longer one up by the end of the week. Entry Date: 2nd April, 2002 It’s been a few weeks since I last made myself known to the public, but over those few short days several dramatic changes have taken place in my life. After over 10 long years of serving the dark side, over 10 years of doing Satan’s bidding, I have decided to call it quits … I am severing my ties with the dark prince. Why? Well, I have many reasons, some which I will not go into detail. I’m just feeling a whole host of new feelings that I have not felt for a long, long time. Ever since I came to this country I have lived an outcast, a social nobody. Nobody in this society knows who I am, that is because I have spent all these years locked indoor preaching the ways of Beelzebub. Satanism has been a drain to me; a drain that has sucked all the life that was once inside me down through the dregs of existence, but now all that must come to an end. Besides, what has Satanism ever done to help me? Absolutely nothing, that’s what and I am not going to let the bitter emptiness of the dark prince drag me down any longer. Only now have I realised that I was a poor misguided fool to fall for that crap in the first place. Where I once saw enlightenment and bliss, now, like most others, I see nothing but a pit o suffering, despair and brainwashing. And trust me, I have had my own fair share of suffering & despair during my life, so there is no need to continue. I have finally woken up to reality; I see what the world truly is all about. No longer will I look upon people as just things for me to hurt and punish, no longer will I look upon religion as a curse spreading across the globe, for I know now that Satanism is the curse. Religion, however, still means nothing to me. I see no need to worship a false idol that people do not even know existed. I am my own man; I need not a “God” to rule over me like I have done in the past. People who worship Gods truly are foolish indeed, can they not see that one’s life should not be ruled over by anyone or anything? That is what I have come to realise over the past few days. So what triggered this sudden change of heart? Well, to be perfectly honest with you, I simply do not know. Perhaps it was the sudden realisation that my brother and parents are gone, and are never going to come back. When I was at the side of Satan, I always thought that if I had kept searching, I would see them again someday, but now I realise that they are gone for good. I thought perhaps when I left this life for the afterlife, that we would meet up in heaven or hell somewhere along the line, but now, like religion, afterlife is nothing to me. Or perhaps the realisation that I am alone in this world, with nobody to turn to caused this sudden change of heart. I am a lone wolf, a loner if you will, for all that I have left in this world is an aunt, uncle and cousin that I never see, and my only 2 friends left in this world, Ben J. Pailthorpe and John Crichton. For most of my life I have accomplished everything by myself, and I intend to keep things that way. I do not need to do the dirty work of a dark entity such as Satan anymore, his guidance is no longer important to me. Another thing that could have triggered this is a tragic event that I witnessed from the window of my apartment the other day. A car containing a young family had swerved off the road, and had crashed into the side of the local cafeteria. The car erupted into a ball of flames, and all 4 members of the family had indeed died. Not only that, but 3 passers by had also been killed in this horrific accident, and over 15 people had been injured. Satan is supposed to be the root of all evil in this world, and if that is true, then he must have been responsible for this event. How can anyone take away 7 innocent lives like that? Life is a gift, not a burden; it should be cherished, not destroyed. So what’s next for me? Well, to be honest, I do not know. I am at a crossroads in my life, but I am blind to the direction of which each road leads to. Maybe I could get a job like everyone else and try to fit into society, but I guess it is to late for that. Besides, the people of this world are blind to the grim reality that covers our existence. And I do not know if I want to be just like everyone else either, for they are all just cogs in a very big machine, a machine called life, and who wants to be just a cog in a machine? I do not, for one. Another possibility for me is that I could follow in the footsteps of my friend Ben J. Pailthrope, and turn to Christianity, but like I said, Christianity and religion is pointless and meaningless to me. I could also try and track down what little family of mine remains on this planet, but that just seems pointless, for I know that it would take a near eternity, and when we parted, I know that it would just hurt me more down inside. Or maybe I could live this city that I dwell in, in search for a new life, but again, that seems pointless. For what would I do after I moved? It all leads back to the crossroads of life I am now at. I have absolutely no idea what direction my life is heading in at the moment, for all I know, I could end up a bum by next week. But one thing is for sure, if I was a PCW wrestler, I wouldn’t want to get involved in a match with me any time soon. For all these emotions that are locked inside my head right now are going to be released in the ring, no matter whom they are. Shawn Mosley is the first who shall taste defeat at the hands of me, for he is another poor misguided fool. He feels to see who I really am, and he shall be punished for it, along with the so-called Asian Gangsta, Charlie Loc. Loc is another fool, he fellows the ways of the streets, or the “ghetto” as he likes to call it. No doubt Loc is an impressive athlete, but he shall fall by the wayside just like everyone else. I shall show him that hiding behind a false character like he does is not the way to success, not by a long shot. The way to success is to be who you are, to show your true self, and that is exactly what I am doing right now. I do not hide behind false images of myself, for I know what success in life is all about. Goodbye Loc, goodbye Mosley … Goodbye Satan. Entry Date: 2nd April, 2002 It is rare occurrence for me to come back and log my thoughts twice in one day, but I feel that there are a few things that I have failed to mention. There was a time; about 10 years ago, when the pieces of my life all fit together perfectly, everything was great, but then I slowly watched those pieces slowly fall away. When my brother disappeared, when my parents died, when I left for America, when I turned to Satan, those were the defining moments in my life, and I feel as if I am on the verge of another defining moment in my life. I cannot quite put my finger on it at the moment, but I can nearly taste my life turning around. The pieces will fit together perfectly again sometime soon, just watch. One might think that during my lost Satanic years, that my education went by the wayside, and that I had turned into an uneducated fool. Well, you would be wrong for making that assumption, for I have an intelligence level that engulfs most. I may sound a little over confident or even cocky, but trust me; I have a near genius intelligence level. I put part of that down to my love of literature and reading. Literature enriches the mind, body and soul … There is nothing more enjoyable than to spend your time reading the many writings of the likes of Edgar Allan Poe or Mark Twain. Literacy is a gift that should be treasured, many people fail to realise this. If it were not for literature, the world would be a much, much worse place. The people of this world are to caught up with their televisions and radios to realise where the real entertainment lies. Of course, the average absent-minded fool will never realise this, but the few smart people left on this planet will ALWAYS know this. Well, I guess that’s all I have got to say for just now. Goodbye people.
