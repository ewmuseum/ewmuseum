Armageddon.

Date: not entered “Well, this is just lovely. Those rations were supposed to last us 2 years!!” We rejoin our ruthlessly efficient and trusted manager to Big Alan Steele, Sue Saksnussum, as she issues a trademarked scowl at one of her henchwomen, the beautiful Grace Steffi. The two are working their way from a parking lot to SupR-SavR Supermarket pushing a shopping cart in front of them on this beautiful San Jose afternoon. “I had set those rations aside for just such an emergency!” seethes Sue, eyes bulging in anger, “So how is it that all those vacuum-sealed K-rat packets are empty?!” “Well, we’ve just been sitting around doing nothing since Tuesday, and you know how all of us Graces eat when we are bored…” “But TWO YEARS worth?!” screams Sue, choking on her bile, “We needed to hole up to get through this!! There’s no telling what will happen now…” Steffi gives an indignant pout. “Well, maybe if Big Al didn’t go and get himself kidnapped…” Sue knocks the cart over in a rush as she clamps a hand over the fearful blonde’s mouth, her nervous eyes darting every which way. “Don’t you EVER say that aloud again!! Should the wrong ears hear that…” The words drip like acid from Sue’s tongue, slowing sinking in on the Grace. Sue slowly releases Steffi, a waving a warning finger in front of her nose. The cart is returned to its upright position and they continue on in stilted, pregnant silence. “Two years worth of rations.” “We said we were sorry!” “Let’s just make this trip as quick and as painless as possible. You take half the list and I’ll take the other half. Just grab’em and go, and we’ll meet up in the front. Just keep your fool mouth shut, agreed?” Steffi sighs, “Agreed.” “Good. Then we’ll head on over to he gun store for shot gun shells.” The two women enter the store, nod in silent determination before racing through the store, setting all sorts of land-speed records for female shopping. In a matter of minutes the two have achieved their goal, pushing their two separate carts together at the only remaining check-out line that is open. Steffi grins and gives Sue a big thumbs-up, but Sue groans smacking her forehead in disgust. “Ah, Ms. Saksnussum. Pleasure to meet your acquaintance again…” A huge, hulking figure stands in line before them, his purple cape flowing unnaturally in the wind-less market. Occasionally scraps of metal fly out and attach themselves to his shimmering skin, forcing him to pull them off in exasperation. His alloyed armor gleams under the incandescent bulbs of the supermarket. Smiling warmly, Magneto puts down his copy of the National Enquirer and positively beams as he extends a handshake to our heroine Sue. Sue looks up to the ceiling and mouths the words, ‘Why me?’ “When was the last time we met?” Magneto ponders, rubbing his chin, “Could it have been the Plot to Subvert your charge, Big Alan Steele, to the dark side?” “Actually, I think it was that last time Big Al raided the Joker Poker machine at the Legion of Doom…” Sue counters. “Ah, yes. How careless of me to forget. “When Big Al crushed your head with two of them together, like a Joker Poker sandwich…” “Yes,” growls Magneto through clenched teeth, “I remember now…” “So… how did the recovery go?” “Oh, you know how it is. A few spins on the ole turbines and Magneto is recharged and refreshed!!” “I’m sorry to hear hat,” Sue says dryly, “What brings you uptown?” Magneto smiles, and pats a huge, industrial-sized bag of Fresh Step Kitty Litter. “I have to take care of my team! Sabertooth ran out a while ago and, frankly, the neighbors are beginning to complain.” “Serves them right for living next to the Legion of Doom.” “Well,” Magneto states, thinking aloud, “Rents ARE reasonable. And besides, we never bring our work HOME. Most of the times we bring pestilence and plague to OTHER parts of the city.” Sue glances down the line, noticing the checkout girl. Just under ninety, she s-l-o-w-l-y waves a bag of frozen peas across the lasers, failing to register a price for the umpteenth time. Nervously, she steals a look at Steffi, who, to her relief, is engaged in another hard-hitting issue of Tiger Beat Magazine. Forced to continue her small talk, she gives Magneto a thin smile that doesn’t reach her eyes. “You know, sometimes I wish superheroes worked in super markets. The Flash would be a good fit for the check-out line, right about now.” “Ha ha ha!” laughs Magneto, “Yes, how droll you are! But perhaps all we need is, dare I suggest, a super… villain?” Magneto punctuates this with a wink, to which Sue raises a curious eyebrow. Stealing a quick look back at Steffi (engaged in the Weekly World News), she gestures grandly towards Magneto. “Be my guest.” “Now, you do understand, I don’t make a habit of this… but you do seem to be somewhat preoccupied and in a hurry, and I do hate to see a lady of your caliber suffer so…” “Think nothing of it.” Visible waves begin to radiate from the diabolical Magneto, which flow outwards in concentric circles from his body. The people in line before him all get nervous looks as their shopping carts begin to rattle and shake. Then, with motion of his arm, the carts fly through the air, propelled with great force, impacting the walls and exploding through them with fantastic speed!! People begin running and screaming as Magneto cackles maniacally. He turns back to Sue, a normal face quickly returning to his visage. “Unfortunately, I do have an image to maintain,” he says with a sigh, “it’s so vexing to force the laughter and the motions.” “Well,” Sue replies, “Broom Hilda didn’t seem to mind.” The wizened cashier smiles wanly at Magneto though Hubble-thick glasses. He grimaces back and then does her the favor of running the litter through, bagging it and then slapping his money on the pay counter. Accepting his change, he bows deeply to Sue in respect. “It was truly a pleasure to see you again, Ms. Saksnussum. If ever you need a genocide or other unmentionable act, please, do not hesitate to call upon me.” “If it’s unmentionable, how will I tell you?” “Ah! Again that trademark wit! How I do enjoy it so… well, ta-ta for now, and please, give Big Al my best wishes, as well as my intentions for… a rematch, perchance?” Sue opens her mouth to speak, and hears words that aren’t in her voice. “Oh, that could be awhile, since Big Al is kidnapped and all…” Sue whirls around and slaps a hand over Steffi's mouth. A brief instant of emotion flashes across the face of Magneto, but he covers it up almost just as fast. His eyes dart back and forth as his breath becomes ragged… “Uh… nice seeing you again. Gotta go…” He walks as fast as he can for the exit, quickly gaining speed. Soon, he’s all out sprinting and laughing, tossing aside the kitty litter as he races through the electric doors. Sue trembles, tempted to wrench Steffi’s neck. Slowly, she lets go. “Okay… let’s not panic here. Maybe its not too bad.” “It’s never as bad as you think, Sue.” “You’re right. I’m sure he just kinda glossed over it.” “He seemed like he didn’t take the news to heart,” adds Steffi. “Yeah. I’m sure it’s no big thing. Right?” “Right.” Sue nods in agreement. “Right.” Beep. Beep. Beep. The old crone rings up the bill, each item beeping in turn. Beep. Beep. Sue sniffs at the air. Beep. “Do you smell smoke?” “You’re total is…” Sue and Steffi share a look and rush outside… San Jose is in flames. Utter chaos reigns as super villains race up and down the streets, like the madmen they are, burning and looting, some carrying the unconscious forms of the members of the Hall of Justice with them. A red haze hangs in the air, as the screams of innocent bystanders languish overhead. Armageddon. Sue sighs a sigh that originates from her very soul while Steffi begins chewing on her fingernails. “Ooops.” “Yeah… ooops,” snarls Sue. Deep laughter forces the girls to turn their attention to the street, where a gleeful Shawn Prodigy drags Superman. Prodigy woots through the boulevard in triumph, Kryptonite strapped to Supe’s chest. “MOMMY!! HELP ME, GOD!! OH, PLEASE!! WAAAHHHHH!” as Superman scrabbles like a ferret in an aquarium. Sue frowns. “I think we had better hire someone to find Big Al, but quick!!”
