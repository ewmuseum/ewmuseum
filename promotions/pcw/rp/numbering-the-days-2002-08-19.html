Numbering the days.

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/19/02 Count the days left in your sinners life, Xavier, because they are drawing to a close. Your salvation awaits. The day of reckoning draws closer, and as the CEO of PCW Tyler Nelson walks through downtown Oklahoma City, the daylight draws to a close. The sun slowly disappears below the distant horizon, bringing nightfall as it does. Tyler walks alone, dressed in black leather pants and a long black leather trench coat. The cool summer night is a welcome relief from the heatwave that has plagued the Midwest for most of the summer. The people he passes on the street seem oblivious to his presence, ignoring the CEO of PCW as he continues his stroll through the downtown area. “How did things go so wrong in this world?” he wonders aloud to himself. Just as the words disappear into the night air the CEO comes upon the site he was walking to find. “How did human nature take such a wrong turn?” The memorial site of the Alfred P. Murrah federal building, destroyed back in 1995. Two men who had lost their way in life decided to make a statement against their government in the most heinous of ways. “Why do people resort to violence and hatred to try and solve their problems? Why did those two people kill all those innocents and destroy so many others’ lives?” says Tyler as he gazes upon the memorial built upon the grounds where the federal building once stood. He shakes his head sadly, bowing it in homage. “Their lack of faith in the salvation that is God is what made them do it,” says Tyler. “They lost their way down the path of life and went astray of hope. In the end one paid the ultimate price for his sin, having his life taken as penance.” The CEO raises his head again, a stone serious look on his face. Gone is the cockiness and greed that once infected him. Gone is the cold-heartedness that once plagued his soul. “Those two men lost sight of the vision….the vision of salvation. They could have saved themselves, but chose not to,” Tyler says somberly. “They made the wrong choice. They needed someone to help them along the way, but they were failed by their family and friends. Such a shame.” Tyler begins to walk through the park that is the Oklahoma City Bombing Memorial. It is so peaceful and serene, a distinct difference from the panic, chaos, and destruction from so many years ago. A small rectangular pond stands near the center of the park, motionless and looking almost like a mirror laying on the ground. “Xavier Kannon is on that same path….the same path I was on until I was saved. He is on the road to ruin, led by ruthlessness, greed, hatred, and the lack of a real God to seek guidance from.” Tyler pauses his walk, taking a moment to look around and smell the fresh night air. “His wife is failing to help him, out of sheer stupidity. She can’t see that he needs help because he can’t tell her that he needs help. Ellie’s lack of intelligence is her downfall and only adds to Xavier’s feelings of dominance and his erroneous self confidence.” Tyler sighs before he continues. “Then there is the Phantom Booker, a man without scruples or regard for anyone but himself. He sees Xavier as a meal ticket, pushing the young man to the ends of evil to make himself more and more money. Once the cash cow is dry Booker will move on, leaving Kannon in his wake. In my renewed faith it is my duty to help Xavier and save him from himself. He must repent his sins and give himself to me for his own good. Salvation rests only in me, and it will come this Sunday.” Tyler sees a group of people standing with candles, mourning lost loved ones. It almost brings a tear to his eye. “I was once Xavier Kannon. I was once on the road to peril. But now I have seen the error of my ways….the sins that I have committed and the impact it has had on others. I have ruined lives and careers. I have performed unspeakable acts of evil. Now I must make things right. My task is to save Xavier Kannon before he destroys himself and all around him. In order to cleanse my soul of all sins, I must save another. I will do whatever it takes to accomplish my agenda.” Tyler stiffens up and takes in a deep breath, fighting back the tears. Not so long ago it would have seemed unthinkable for the CEO to be brought to tears by such a sight. These days are different, however, and the Tyler Nelson is renewed by a newfound faith. A faith in a God that forgives sins. A faith in a God that has paid the ultimate price for him. A faith that he will impose upon Xavier Kannon in order to save the PCW World Champion. Everything is on the line. Ownership of PCW. CEO’ship of PCW. Commissionership of PCW. The PCW World Championship. “I must succeed in order to completely save myself. I must be the one to save Xavier Kannon. For if not, no one can.” The CEO walks off into the Oklahoma City night as the scene fades to black.
