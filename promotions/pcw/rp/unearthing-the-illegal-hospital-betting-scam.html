Unearthing the Illegal Hospital Betting Scam!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin had successfully managed to cut off Red Rock’s Balls of Steel and escape the wrath of Red Rock’s sister- Ruby Rock (with boobs of steel). Now Flaymin is armed with balls of steel and ready to have them planted into his nutsack.Surgery awaits.This great accomplishment is only a surgeon and a scalpel away…. Or is it?? [Scene opens in the pre-Surgery ward at Parkes Hospital. Lying on a bed in the waiting room, wearing a blue hospital gown is Flaymin Aymin.His gypsy assistant, Hirraani, stands at his side warmly holding his hand. Flaymin slowly looks around the room at the other patients awaiting surgery. He gawks at a man with hoses running all throughout his body.A moment later his attention shifts to a woman with a broken leg.] Flaymin: Hirraani, did you fetch my wallet? Hirraani: Huh? Flaymin: $100 bucks says that geriatric with the hoses running all over the joint doesn’t make it through surgery. Heck, $250 bucks says that pee’s his pants before he even makes it into the surgery room. [Hirraani stares at Flaymin in disgust] Hirraani: That’s awful, I can’t believe your betting on the mortality of a helpless old man. Flaymin: Hey its not my fault he has such great odds stacked against him. [A young nurse walks over to Flaymin with a chart in her hand] Nurse: So what will it be? Flaymin: Put me down for $100 that the old dude dies.And another $250 that he wets the bed within 15 minutes. [The nurse quietly begins to scribble some words into her chart. Meanwhile, Hirraani looks at the nurse in amazement, unable to digest the shallow gambling taking place before her very eyes] Hirraani: I can’t believe you’re in on this, too!!Have you no morals? Nurse: Easy there, lady.We take bets here all the time. See those progress charts hanging on the end of all the patients’ beds? Hirraani: What about them? Nurse: Well, they’re not progress charts.Instead they contain the latest odds on the survival rate of each individual and other important gambling tips. Why do you think doctors are so rich?They’re always in here checking those charts and getting the latest odds.And if they don’t like the way things are going they can always fix the final score with the flick of a single switch. Flaymin: How do they do that? Nurse: By simply turning off the Life Support machine.Doctor Wells made a $4000 profit on Mrs Winters just this morning. Flaymin: The old hag croaked???Whooo!!!I win $600!! Nurse: Yep, Dr Wells was betting that she’d expire within 10 hours of surgery.And things were going terrible because we thought she was going to make it afterall. Flaymin: So how did she die? Nurse: Dr Wells was getting desperate so he ‘accidentally’ tripped over the life support plug and dislodged it from the wall socket. Flaymin: Wow, that’s one smart man.So who is currently the hot favourite to drop dead? Nurse: Well insiders are betting big that Mr Roberts doesn’t make it out of his Triple Bypass Heart Surgery scheduled for later this afternoon. Flaymin: Oh man!I’d love to see that. Nurse: No problem, I’ll tell the receptionist to hook you up for the Pay Per View. Hirraani: You broadcast these atrocities on TV? Flaymin: Of course they do!Sick people dying= Ratings. [Hirraani looks around the room at all the other sick patients who are unaware that their lives all depend on the latest gambling odds. Her eyes filled with sorrow, she shakes her head and storms out of the room. Flaymin looks up at the nurse and shrugs his shoulders in confusion] Flaymin: Wow, she seems pretty angry. Nurse: We can always put her on the market.With one phone call I can arrange for an ambulance to knock her down. She’d be within the emergency ward in15 minutes and on the betting market within 20 minutes. [Flaymin strokes his chin at the possibilities of having a hit and run attack aimed at his valet. A few seconds later he speaks up] Flaymin: Nah we don’t want to do that, because then I’ll have no one to drive me home after the operation. Nurse: Suit yourself, she’d have some great odds. Flaymin: Can you answer a serious question for me? Nurse: Sure. Flaymin: What are the odds that I’ll make it through the operation? [The nurse flips through the pages of her ‘progress chart’.A minute later she locates the odds associated with Flaymin’s operation] Nurse: Hmmm, well to be brutally honest… your odds suck major donkey balls.It seems your operation isn’t life threatening at all. Flaymin: I don’t know whether I should be happy or not. Nurse: Well if you were betting against yourself, you’d be wishing you were dying.We can um… arrange tha…. Flaymin: NO!!I mean, I think I’d like to live. [Suddenly the Nurse’s pager begins to beep.She takes it off her belt clip and looks at it] Nurse: Oh no, I need to go- it’s an emergency. Flaymin: What is it? Nurse: A person who broke their back just arrived in the emergency ward. I need to work out the odds of whether they’ll be a paraplegic for life or not.And if their not, I’ll have to break out the fire extinguisher and crack some backs to help raise the odds… if you catch my drift. [The nurse winks at Flaymin and then quickly runs out of the pre-surgery room. As soon as she leaves a doctor walks over to Flaymin] Doctor: Sir, its time to insert those Balls of Steel.Good luck with the operation. [Flaymin reclines back into the bed and closes his eyes, while the doctor wheels the bed into the operation theatre] How will the operation go?Will the betting odds on Flaymin improve?Tune in next time to find out!!! -To place any bets, contact me :)
