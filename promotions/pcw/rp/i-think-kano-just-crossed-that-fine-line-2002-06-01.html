I think Kano just crossed that fine line

1 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/1/2002 ~Retribution. Revenge. What ever you want to call it. It is a bitch. Jon Kano is the master of revenge. Tim Walden stabbed Kano in the back, Kano beat him so viciously and embarassed him so badly, that Walden was fired. Nelson screwed over Kano when they were both part of the MEDIA conglomerate, Kano has since done his best to make Nelson realize why Kano is a legend. On this past week's edition of Turmoil, Kano went head to head with a man who thinks he is 'Controversial.' The two men fought to and fro, back and forth, to hell and back. Kano, seemingly out of of nowhere, speared Corey Williams into next week and covered the 'Controversial' one. Only to have his own shoulder pinned to the mat, with Mr. Williams having a handful of Kano's tights, giving Mr. Williams the advantage. Now Corey has pissed Kano off. On that same edition of Turmoil, a man who praises Allah felt the wrath of Jon Kano. He tried his damnedest to get away, put felt all of the air rush out of his body when Kano cut him off with a Kanospear to end all Kanospears. And what did this Allah-praising man known as The Chef do to deserve such a devastating move? He dared to assault Jon Kano's good friend and confidant, Duke Williams. And if that was not bad enough, he let the scum known as Xavier Kannon join in the assault of Duke Williams. Jon Kano was merely defending his friend and stablemate. Then we have a man seemingly thrown into the match just for the hell of it, Mark Makonie. Who? Well Mark Makonie is a man who has an on going feud with Jon Kano's stablemate and brother in arms, Jordan Shabazz, and is a member of the Ultimate Authority. So what does this all mean? Well you have four competitors in a match. None of them like Jon Kano, and all of them would love to put Kano out. So does Jon Kano have to win? No. Does anyone expect him to win? No. But what exactly does he have to do? Survive so he can fight another day. He must survive so he can continue to fight for what is right, to fight Ultimate Authority to restore power to where it belongs. But the odds are not looking good for Mr. Jon Kano on Turmoil.~ *The scene opens up in front of the historic Liberty Bell in the beautiful cradle of American history known as Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Jon Kano is standing in front of the Liberty Bell admiring the the bell. Jon Kano is dressed in a blue t-shirt with PCW's next Pay Per View, Gold and Glory,'s logo across the chest, blue jeans, and black Doc Martin boots. He is also sporting golden-framed Armani sunglasses.* Kano: Look at this bell. It has been here for over two hundred years. It has seen the ups and downs of this country. I guess you could make an analogy that the Liberty Bell is to America what I am to PCW. I have been in PCW for what seems like two hundred years and I have seen the ups and downs of PCW. I look upon the empire I helped build and I see the changes coming. I was here when Nelson bought PCW. I was part of MEDIA. I helped put Nelson into the position he is now, and by God, I will be the one who takes him down. I don't care if I have to bring myself down in the process, I will take his ass OUT, if it is the last thing I do. I see The Phantom Booker returning to the motherland. I see he has come back to accomplish the goal of getting Nelson out of the company that TPB and me laid the groundwork for. I don't know where exactly TPB stands in regard to the Malice Crew, but what I see is what I have seen on the History Channel about World War 2. I see UA as the German Nazis, I see the Malice Crew as the US forces, and I see TPB's group as the Soviets. Now if you know anything about WWII, you know that the Soviets and the US forces fought into Germany and finally the Soviets took Berlin. Oh God, I can see it now. The only thing is that we both will take PCW and restore the power to where it belongs. And I pray to God himself that Nelson, who in my mind, is nearing Hitler's status as a bastard, does what Hitler did as we close in on UA. I think everyone knows what I am talking about. *Kano begins to walk away from the Liberty Bell and begins to walk down the streets of Philadelphia.* Kano: And that leads me to Mark Makonie. Well I guess not exactly to Mark Makonie, seems he is no longer aligned with Tyler Nelson. Well good for him, but Makonie has a problem with my brother in arms, Jordan Shabazz. If you have a problem with one of my running buddies, then you have a problem with me, and if you have a problem with me, then you are pretty well f*cked. Look at all of the people who have come up to me in my illustrious PCW career and had a problem with me. Chuck Manson, he was the first, and he left. PHD, he was the second, and he is nothing but a memory now. Ivan left. And the list goes on and on. Mark Makonie needs to look at himself hard in the mirror and ask himself if he really wants to continue to have a problem with Mr. Shabazz. Because if he has a problem, I have a problem solver, and its name is a Kanoplex. *Kano stops in his tracks. He rolls his eyes back in his head, and crosses his arms across his chest.* Kano: Guess who I am. I am the Chef's wife. Now I know you are all saying, 'Did Kano just make fun of another man's deceased wife?' Why yes, yes he did. Why? Because the Chef deserves it. He turned his back on everyone when he joined last night with Tyler Nelson. But that is not even the worse part about The Chef. The worse part is that he worships a man who stole and murdered. Yes, Mohammed stole and murdered. Now I am not saying Islam is a bad religion. I think all religions have become too much about business. Hell, I was raised Catholic, and I now reject nearly everything about the Catholic church. But, The Chef walks around like he is better than us all because he believes his religion is the right way to live your life. Why do we as a whole species have to get in a pissing contest about whose beliefs are right and whose are wrong? I just don't see the point. The only thing I am really pissed off at The Chef about right now is the fact that he is one of those people who made PCW and now he is supporting a man who could destroy PCW. Did I say could? I meant will destroy PCW. Tyler Nelson is out for one thing and one thing only, his own personal interests. Yet The Chef joined with this man? Where the hell is The Chef's head at? What the hell has Tyler ever done to help PCW? What has Allah ever done for anyone? I am more about doing things for myself or for others to serve the common good. Novel idea, huh? *Kano resumes his walk down the street after making a mockery of The Chef's deceased wife.* Kano: And finally, Corey Williams. Corey f*cking Williams. This is the guy I have a huge problem with. You know I have done a lot of things in my career. I have screwed over people in and out of the ring. I am an asshole, I admit it. But I would never ever pull the tights on a man who is of the legendary status like I am in the ring. Corey crossed that fine line last Tuesday when he did that to me to get the win. Why did he have to do that? Was it because he couldn't beat me otherwise? Or was it because he has an anal fixation and wanted to see my ass? Either way it was wrong on so many different levels and on this week's Turmoil, Corey Williams will pay dearly. And why am I going to make these three men redefine their own defintion of pain on Turmoil, well simply put, it is because I am what REAL LEGEND ARE MADE OF. And another thing, how f*cking cool is this shirt? I mean look, I am even on it. GREATEST T-SHIRT EVER!!!!!! *The scene fades to black as Kano continues his walk through the beautiful city of brothery love.* ~Revenge is Kano's. What a bitch for the other three.~
