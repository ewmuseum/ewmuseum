Date: not entered [Scene opens at Flaymin’s hotel room. He’s sitting in the lounge room, watching TV with his new pet Zebra Fernando and it’s care-taker, Pablo. The Zebra quietly laps up some beer in a bucket until he makes a queasy sound. Pablo looks at Fernando the Zebra and then taps Flaymin on the shoulder] Pablo: I think he needs to go. [Still watching the TV, Flaymin answers in a gruff voice] Flaymin: Go where? Pablo: You know… Flaymin: Fine, tell him to get some bread and milk on the way back. [Pablo looks at Flaymin confusion] Pablo: I don’t think you understand… Flaymin: Yeah, yeah, the Visa Card is in my wallet.He knows how to forge signatures, right? Pablo: Uh…. I think he just needs to go to the toilet. Flaymin: Alright, just make sure he doesn’t forget to flush.And if I see Zebra piss all over the toilet seat, he better start running. [Pablo shakes his head in bewilderment] Pablo: I think I’ll take him out to the park across the road. Flaymin: Eh, your call. [Suddenly, the doorbell rings. Flaymin looks at the door and yells] Flaymin: Come in! [A moment later, the door opens and Flaymin’s valet, Heather, walks into the room. She walks over to Flaymin and looks at the Zebra with a bemused look on her face] Heather: I think we need to talk. Flaymin: Sure. [Once again she looks at the zebra and Pablo and raises an eyebrow. Suddenly, a noise is heard in one of the bedrooms] Heather: I think there’s someone in the bedroom. Flaymin: Yep. Heather: Do you know who it is? Flaymin: Yep. Heather: Well who the hell is it!?!? [El Diablo’s wife, Angela, suddenly appears from out of the bedroom, wearing a tight, white shirt and long black pants. Heather looks at Angela and frowns.Meanwhile, Angela looks back at Heather with a touch of nervousness] Heather: Flaymin, I think you’ve got a lot of explaining to do. [Flaymin sighs.He then gets up and turns the TV off. Flaymin looks at Pablo, Fernando the Zebra, Angela and Heather] Flaymin: Wow, I’ve got one hell of a posse now, don’t I!But I don’t think you’ve all been introduced to each other, so this would be the perfect time to get to know one another. [After a long pause, Flaymin then sits back down on the couch and switches the TV back on] Heather: Uh… are you going to introduce us all? Flaymin: Geez, I might as well buy some freakin’ name tags and serve cold sandwiches while I’m at it. [Flaymin gets back up off the couch.He walks over to Pablo and pats him on the head] Flaymin: This here, is Pablo. [Flaymin looks at Pablo] Flaymin: He’s… short and…. he likes Zebra’s. Pablo: I look after Fernando. Angela: Fernando? Flaymin: Oh right, moving right along. [Flaymin walks over to Fernando the Zebra and strokes his long mane] Flaymin: This is Fernando the Zebra…. My new pet.He’s not quite white and he he’s not quite black… he’s ‘whack’…. and he sort of looks like a donkey… but isn’t quite a horse. Fernando, I’d like you to meet Heather and Angela. [The two women look at the little Zebra] Heather: Why do you need a pet Zebra? Flaymin: I don’t know.I guess I’m going through a ‘phase’ or something. [Angela eyes Heather.In return, Heather coldly stares at Angela] Flaymin: I’m sensing a little tension in the air. [Flaymin sniffs the air] Flaymin: No wait, that’s just a Zebra fart.Dammit Pablo, how much baked beans did you feed that damn thing!?! Pablo: Only two cans! [Flaymin shakes his head in disgust] Flaymin: Anyways, where was I?Oh yes, Angela this is Heather. Heather, this is Angela. Angela: You know Aymin, I don’t know if this is going to work out. Flaymin: Sure it will work- two female valet’s will work like a charm. Heather: What makes you so sure? Flaymin: Well, you’re a woman…And she’s a woman…I have a porno video in the bedroom and…. Well I think you can see where I’m going with this. Angela: You’re joking, right? Flaymin: Nope.The only way you two are going to get on is if you both actually do GET IT ON!!! Heather: That’s not going to happen. Flaymin: Fine, I understand…… but what if I throw the Zebra into the mix? Heather: Ugh! [Furious, Heather storms out of the hotel room.Meanwhile, Angela goes back to her own bedroom and slams the door] Pablo: What’s going on? Flaymin: Beats the hell out of me, but all of a sudden I’m feeling like baked beans! [Scene fades to black as Flaymin, Fernando and Pablo walk over to the nearby cupboard and pull out some cans of Beans] Will the Flaymin-Angela-Heather alliance ever work?Or is it doomed for failure?
