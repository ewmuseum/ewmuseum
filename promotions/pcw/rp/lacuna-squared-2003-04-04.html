Lacuna Squared

4 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/04/03 Lacuna smiled; she had been here before; it was comfortable, safe territory and she was no longer at risk. Beneath her feet, the detritus was nourishing trees, worms were feasting and things were happening as they should. The certainty of the situation consoled her rough, sharp emotions as they grated against her brain; sticking the plaster over the wounds she had caused herself through her hours of thought. She was more upset than she had been used to, because everything had been going so well. She had been doing so much recently that her thoughts were being slowly plagued without her realising. The doubt had been creeping back in; the pain was slowly rising from the depths of her long-term memory. Was she coping? She had asked herself this question so many times that it seemed almost routine to her now, as if it was just another little part of her daily schedule. How much this depressed her. How could any normal person map out their life like this? Her breath rose in front of her eyes, dancing and twirling into oblivion as the cold air surrounding her absorbed it. She was fascinated by the patterns that the breath she expelled created for her, how could such a simple little task look so stunning in the cold half-light? Yeah, she had been here before; it had been so long since this last happened though. At least this time she would know what was going to happen to her. The confrontation really took it out of her last time, mentally and physically. Nothing had changed in the time it had been since her last unavoidable visit. The full moon hung in the air like some silvery glowing button, perched up above to strike the country with its beautiful rays. The dew on the grass had risen into a mist that was hovering around ankle level, hiding the ground from view with its semi-transparent haze. She could feel the wet grass waving in the breeze, lapping gently against her bare ankles in the freezing cold of the night. It was always the same time, always the same conditions. “You here?” she called into the darkness, with nothing replying, as expected. It was scary that even her thoughts were taking the same direction as her last few visits to this desolate place. She shivered slightly as she realised what she was going to say next. “I haven’t got bloody all night, you know” She knew that next the mental rambling would commence again, and she would contemplate the theory of the abyss, and what would happen if she screamed into it. It was all too familiar. She would begin with a simple theory and expand upon it until there was nothing else to think, or until the thing she was waiting for actually arrived. Its hard to imagine what would happen if you could remember everything that was about to happen to you, but Lacuna was quite taken with it. She had remembered thinking this last time as well, but she enjoyed it now, because it was vaguely trivial compared to the ordeal she is soon to face. “It’s been a while” came a voice from the shadows. It was Lacuna’s voice. “Always a pleasure to speak with me” responded Lacuna, her voice oozing sarcasm. Just this once, she thought, just this once I would love to see me, to see me hiding in those shadows. Thing is, she cant actually move. Her legs seemed bolted to the floor by some invisible shackles. “You know why this is happening, I know you that well at least.” came the voice of the shadowed Lacuna. It was somewhat German, full of ruthless efficiency and used no more emotion than it required to get across the message. “Hilton?” “You know the answer to that question. What are you doing about the answer?” she was indeed full of order; no niceties involved at all. Straight to the point, just as Lacuna wanted to be. “I have been thinking in isolation, just as you told me last time” now the original Lacuna took the emotionless approach, her voice seeming to hold back a little on the venom she wanted to pelt her with. “Excellent. I trust you are not speaking to anyone?” “Just the essentials, nothing more or less” Again, she attempted to move, yet her legs were still bolted to the spot, hard as concrete. “You will find your answer.” “You mean your answer? I still wish to do this on my own, I cannot even prove that you exist, like some God or something” “Warmer, your getting warmer. Do what I say and you will be fine, your answer will appear. Just don’t try your own way, or it will all start again. Mark my words. Goodbye.” And then she opened her eyes. Somehow, although she remembered everything else, she never remembered it was a dream.
