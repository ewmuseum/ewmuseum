Taking Care of Business

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/19/02 Time: 10: 34 P.M Location: New YorkScene: Ice Cream Parlor “We be Taking Care of Business, it’s all good.” 1st Title Defense The scene opens with Juan Borro, Kevin Klaymore, Chef, and Elian Gonzalez eating out of a big bowl of ice cream after a straining day of playing sports, going to arcades, and learning a little bit of cultural knowledge. The ice cream parlor is dark and there are not many people in there. The four men are more or less bonding. The climate is cool, but the men are all sweating from the action packed day. With smiles open wide and emotions running clear, the four men sit down and enjoy their ice cream. Elian: Thanks Uncle Chef, this sure was a great day. I can’t wait until we do it again. Can we do this everyday? Chef cracks his back, which he practically broke when he tried to skateboard earlier in the day and then responds. Chef: No problem kid. I don’t think we’ll be able to do this tomorrow, but I can go and show you the wonders of Allah at a museum tomorrow? Elian: Ummm………. Juan: Sorry Chef, I already planned to have him meet some of the old soccer guys tomorrow, maybe another day. Chef shrugs and when he turns around, Elian pokes Juan on the shoulder and holds his hands together as if he was saying “Thank You” Kevin: I don’t know about you guys, but I was really intimidating when we were playing baseball today. Did you see all those kids run away in fear? Juan: That’s because they thought you were going to hit them with the bat. You had you’re initials KKK on your shirt and was swinging a bat in a minority area. I wonder why they all ran away. Kevin: Why? Juan just nods his head in disapproval, as Kevin shrugs not knowing what he had done wrong. Chef: Elian, are you still sure you don’t want to go to that restaurant I showed you on the way hear? Juan: Chef, it had a squid on its menu, I think the kid would rather kill himself than eat that stuff. Kevin: What’s wrong with squid, he’s like the coolest character in Rocket Power, other than Twister of course? Juan: How many times do I have to tell you to stop watching this American television Kevin? It just warps your mind and tries to make you stupid like the other zombie Americans who religiously watch television. Chef: I’d have to agree with that one Kevin. Elian then jumps up on his seat and gives Kevin a huge high five. Elian: Rocket Power rules Uncle Kevin. It’s ummm….knarly, but my favorite character is Reggie. A tear comes from the eye of Kevin in sentiment. Kevin: He just said knarly. Juan rolls his eyes and responds. Juan: You must be so proud. Kevin: He’s quite the player also. I bet I can figure out a few reasons why he likes that Reggie chick. Elian blushes. Juan: It’s a children’s cartoon Kevin you’re scaring me. Kevin shrugs. The employee of the ice cream shop comes carrying a huge platter of Ice Cream for the four guys. It’s filled with chocolate, vanilla, caramel, nuts, cocoa, candy, sprinkles, strawberry, cool whip, with a bright red cherry on top. Everyone is about to dig in when Chef stops everyone and speaks. Chef: Before you guys do anything, let’s have Senor Gonzalez over here get the cherry. He is now a proud member of International Incident. Golden Game: Hoorah! Elian takes the cherry and eats it, but the second he finishes all of the other three men become barbaric and eats through the whole platter of ice cream in under ten seconds flat. They then look over at Elian who is just starring at the three men without blinking. Ice cream begins to fall form the men’s faces. Kevin: Oh did you want some? Elian just shrugs and suddenly a rock comes flying right through the window, just missing Elian. Chef looks out the window and sees a group of young white American college students who are most likely drunk. Juan and Kevin notice the fumes coming from the nostrils of Chef and try to contain him, the ice cream owner is about to go out and do something, but Chef offers his assistance to the problem. Chef: Oh please, let me take care of it. Juan: No way, Chef you aren’t going out there. You have to let Elian over here look up to you. You can’t go taking people out. Chef doesn’t respond while still enrages. Kevin: Don’t worry Chef, Juan and I will go take care of it. Elian: Uncle Chef, what’s going on? Chef: Nothing, let’s just go get some more ice cream. Meanwhile, Juan and Kevin go outside to try and talk some sense into the college student’s, if that’s what you want to call it. They are met with a barrage of curses and slurs as they get closer and closer. Juan: Do you two realize, you almost hit a little kid in there? Cocky College Student: Who cares? Juan: I care. Other College Student: I’ve seen you two before you’re in that fake wrestling thing and come from the Iberian Pissninsula. The college student’s laugh, but Juan and Kevin don’t seem to be phased. Kevin: That was almost funny, but it’s not as funny as a group of college students found beaten half to death in front of an ice cream shop. Juan and Kevin get angered and go straight after the four college students. It seems like they’re in complete control of the fight, until four other pop up from a car and go over to defend their friends. They quickly outnumber Kevin and Juan, until they beat them to the ground. Chef notices what’s going on and quickly drops everything and runs outside. Elian: What’s going on? Elian follows Chef, who runs up to the pack of men and scares them all away accept one of them. The one last man continues to kick at Juan, but back off a bit when he sees Chef coming. Elian then comes out and the college student notices who it is. College Student: Holy Shit that’s Elian Gonzalez? Chef: No, it isn’t now why don’t you get the hell out of here. College Student: I’m not stupid, that’s him. I’m going to tell the police that you freaks have him. The college student runs off, but Juan regains his strength and sprint after the student. He quickly catches up and leg sweeps him to the floor. Chef grabs Elian so he doesn’t have to witness the violence. Kevin approaches the student and grabs him by the head, then throws him into a brick wall. While down, Juan gives him some quick kicks to the head, until he completely stops moving. Juan: Take that you bastard. Kevin goes down to check his pulse. He’s still alive but seems unconscious. Kevin picks him up and throws him in a near bye dumpster, they then wipe there hands off and go back to Chef. Chef: Good job guys, thanks for taking care of that. Juan: No problem. He’s happy I didn’t kill him, when he was kicking me that stuff started hurting. Chef: Well it’s all over let’s go. Chef takes his hands away from Elian’s eyes and ears. They then start walking back to they’re car. Elian: What’s going on? Chef: Nothing important let’s just go home. Scene Fades to Black.
