Gulag: Castle of Hell

1 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1st May After several hours questioning it was deemed that El Diablo was not one of the bank robbers and he was let off with a stern warning. So much for his directing career- or so would be thought. The time is close to midnight and El Diablo is at a castle somewhere. The night is dark and all the stars in the sky are visible. The castle is very old and is practically wrecked on the inside and is falling apart, but for some strange reason this doesn't faze The Mexican Devil. El Diablo however is not alone, with him is his estranged wife Angel. Angel is tied up with rope around her ankles and wrists. She is wearing a short black skirt and a tight white top with her red bra showing through. Despite her state, she is clean and looks ok, with no harm come to her. Diablo is wearing a black t-shirt and black pants to go with his black sneakers. Of course he has his Mexican flag face paint on and his long black hair is flowing behind him. El Diablo and his wife are upstairs in the dangerous castle at an opening. Obviously there are no windows but Diablo and his wife are right by an opening looking out into the night. The only reason Angel is there anyway is because of El Diablo kidnapping her on Turmoil. But why did he do it? And what purpose does it have taking her everywhere with him? The Mexican Devil sits near the opening on the upper floor of the castle, at least 20ft high. Angel is further back away from the danger. Diablo looks out at the stars, as his wife remains silent thinking of why she is even in this predicament. 'This is a beautiful site,' says El Diablo, 'Now, if only I had my video camera,' he complains to himself. Angel just sighs. 'Isn't it captivating? The stars, the planets, the beauty of a silent night,' Diablo speaks once more with a passion, 'Just a pity I cannot record it,' finishes Diablo with regret. His questions were obviously to his wife but she just chooses to ignore him and lay and wait until her husband decides when they leave the castle. Diablo turns away from the opening and looks at his wife. 'You know they're after me,' he says with concern for his well being. Angel simply shakes her head in disbelief. 'Who are 'they' Raul?' Angel asks with little interest. The Metaphor of Magic looks around steadily and then looks back at his wife. 'Everybody,' he whispers. Angel smirks. 'Am I after you?' she jokingly asks. Why she is teasing her husband whilst in this situation is hard to know, but she is doing it without much care. Diablo looks at her. 'Are you?' Diablo asks with concern in his eyes, and gritting his teeth, praying she isn't. His estranged wife shakes her head to indicate no. 'I knew you weren't Angel,' he says as he stands up and hugs his wife. Angel looks disgusted but just goes with the moment. 'Are you going to let me go now?' she asks Diablo once he stops hugging her. Diablo leers back and looks at her in shock. 'You'd like that wouldn't you?' El Diablo says angrily, 'You'd like to just go back to Flaymin Aymin wouldn't you?' he sneers at her. He then shakes his head in disgust and to indicate no. 'Well it isn't going to happen, you're stuck with me,' he yells at her. Angel though is still unfazed by what is happening and remains tied up, just more frustrated now. 'What are you going to do with me?' Angel asks him. Diablo sits back down near the opening and smiles; this gets Angel's attention. 'What?' she asks. He remains silent, not answering his wife. 'What is it?' Angel pleads. The Director of Dictation smiles again and looks back at her. 'I'm going to win you back,' he says to Angel's shock. She remains still and looks up, not knowing what to think. 'And if that doesn't work, I don't know what,' finishes El Diablo with a sick grin on his face. The grin frightens Angel slightly, not knowing how her future will turn out. El Diablo again looks back at her, he notices the rope seems to be getting loose. He walks over to his wife, but she kicks him away. Diablo stumbles backwards and falls out through the open. He yells for help but none appears. Suddenly El Diablo blinks, and he is just staring at his wife again, she is still tied up, and the rope isn't loose. He looks scared and is sweating heavily on his face. He looks down at his hands, and his palms are sweating. Diablo starts to breathe heavily and look around in fear. 'Are you ok?' asks Angel with genuine concern for her estranged husband. El Diablo looks up and stares at her. 'It's you!' he gasps at her, 'You're doing this,' finishes Diablo in a panicked tone. Angel looks both concerned for his well being and shocked that he would even think that about her. 'Doing what?' she shouts back. 'You're one of them,' he shrieks. Diablo holds his hands out and watches the sweat drip from his head onto them. The sweat drop likes rain, this obviously worries El Diablo. 'One of who?' asks Angel. 'You're after me too,' he shrieks. He stands up and looks out of the opening, looking out into the sky. 'SOMEBODY HELP ME!!!!!' he screams at the top of his lungs. He turns his head to look back at his wife who is watching him in panic. 'Goodbye,' he says. To Angel's amazement he leaps out of the window. Diablo blinks and once more he is sat staring at his beautiful wife. 'What's going on?' Diablo asks her in confusion. She looks back at him strangely. 'What do you mean?' responds Angel. El Diablo begins to breathe heavily again and slowly everything around him keeps spinning until he simply drops onto the hard stone floor of the castle, passed out. Angel remains tied up staring on in shock. What can El Diablo to escape his personal hell? Is he really in a castle or is he just trapped inside his mind? Due to how screwed up his life has been recently. Is there an escape from his Gulag?
