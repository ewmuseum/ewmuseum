Forgiveness

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12th March 2002 Tuesday the 12th of March 2002. We saw the debut of an aspiring individual, a man who hailed from none other than Vietnam in Asia. Not only did this one small man attempt to take on a giant in the ring, but he put everything he had into it…only to have it thrown back into his face! Not only was the man gaining the upper hand, but a certain team named 'International Incident' decided they would make themselves a factor and show up. The men from this so-called crew not only disgraced Charlie’s home country of Vietnam but they made a complete fool and a mockery out of everything the man had worked so hard for…the young man will not give up. It is not in his nature….he will not give up until he has what he wants….Redemption! The sweet scent of body odor can be smelt, as it usually can be, in the once clean but now dirty locker room of a PCW Superstar. Clothes and wrestling attire litter the floor and hang over benches and lockers, making the room look somewhat like a bomb blast had hit it! The people who work as cleaners in these kind of places would be expected to be getting a lot of money, for the work they do must be of a tremendous amount and not only would it be slave labor to pay them for less than there job is worth, but it would be an insult to their career! As the clothes that litter the floor and the lockers that hang open with Superstars can stretch as fire as the naked eye can see, smoke begins to rise and slowly fill the gap between the tops of the lockers and the freshly painted white roof! The smoke looks somewhat like a cigarette but it gets thicker as it continues to flow from wherever it is that it’s coming from, making it seem as though there could possibly be a fire somewhere inside the confined room! Although the smoke can be seen, it doesn’t smell like usual smoke…it smells somewhat sweet, it even overpowers the smell of the B.O that inhabited the locker room before! The thought of a fire in the PCW locker room is horrible but just as the sense of alarm begins to fill, it slowly fades as the soft mumbling of someone 'chanting' can be heard coming from the same direction that the smoke is flowing from… 'Forgive Me….' The voice is nearly a whisper and is about as soft as the smoke is thick. The voice can only be recognized as that of a disgraced Charlie Loc. As the mumbling continues on below a whisper, Charlie Loc can be seen behind the lockers facing a white wall, with somewhat of a shrine sitting in front of him. It is like something out of a book, a rather large and shiny looking statue of none other than Buddha with his legs crossed and his arms on them, with the familiar smile crossing his face. On either side of the statue are two small cups filled with what seems to be dirt with a small stick poking it from either one in opposite directions…. The sticks can only be what everyone uses to pray and that is Incense. The smoke can be seen flowing off of the two ends of the Incense sticks and floating into the air around Charlie, making it look like something you’d only ever see in a Buddhist temple. Sitting in front of this and mumbling something in a low whisper, possibly in Vietnamese is Charlie Loc, in a similar position as the statue of Buddha is. His legs are tucked underneath his body and his hands are clasped together with his head hung low above them, his long hair covering his face. He seems to be kneeling on a pure white, silky cushion. His back is covered with red scratches that could’ve only come from his recent rumble with Reeco Suave. For some strange reason and the muscles on Charlie’s back are flared and even though he seems to be aggravated, he seems to be in a sense of deep meditation that can only be referred to as being 'In Sync' with your soul.All of a sudden the mumbling stops and everything seems to grow silent in the locker room…. The smoke continues to flow from both sticks of Incense as Charlie seems to be motionless. Out of nowhere his head begins to tilt back and his hair falls back into place as he leans back on his feet. He lets out a long loud sigh before clearing his throat and speaking somewhat to the statue that is facing directly in front of him. 'Tonight I disgrace my country and I disgrace my religion! I come to you now to ask you for forgiveness…I hoping that you may understand!' Charlie speaks in a soft and calm manner that’s not usually like him at all. Charlie pauses for a second and stares at the statue before tilting his head and speaking solemnly to the statue yet again. 'We all make mistake Buddha, I only human! I not mean for this to happen! I come here now to pray to you and ask if you can grant me redemption and help me to win back the honor of my country! It all I ask of you Buddha…' Once again Charlie pauses. As Charlie opens his mouth, a cracking sound can be heard coming from somewhere back behind the lockers…Charlie doesn’t seem to notice nor care as he looks up at the statue before tilting his head down yet again and praying to his god. 'Please listen to me….' Charlie begins to beg to the statue. Instead of his usual routine of rising his head to look at the shrine, he keeps it hung low and squeezes his eyes shut tightly. As Charlie keeps himself in a state of serenity there is a buzzing sound before the lights begin to flicker in the locker room! Strangely enough there is no sound of someone flicking the light switch and once again the locker room seems dead, with only Charlie in there. This time though, Charlie notices the flickering lights and opens his eyes and looks up towards the statue, a glimmer of hope in his eyes. 'You have heard my calls….' Charlie speaks a lot lower than what he was speaking before and his voice can be barely be heard in the room. He continues to stare at the shrine with his hands clasped as he awaits an answer from the presumable 'Buddha' that just gave him the sign of his presence. 'Your calls have been heard Mortal!' A voice boom’s over the locker room shaking the statue on the ground and causing the two cups with the dirt and Incense to rattle and shake, but strangely enough everything else in the room still seems peaceful and silent. 'I not mean to disgrace my country tonight! Please Buddha, I ask you for one more chance….please?' Charlie speaks a little louder this time but still his voice can barely be heard. 'As you said before…you are a Mortal but only Mortal Fool’s make a mistake! It is just your luck that you have pleased me Charlie…I can see much wealth in your future!' The voice booms loud and clear throughout the locker room once more, this time causing the lights to flicker yet again. As the lights stop flickering and the rumbling of the shrine begins to cease, the room is once again left in silence as Charlie sits, kneeling in front of the shrine with his eyes closed and his head hung low. 'But….I feel you need to repay me! Everything comes with a price Charlie, do not think that you will be let off with a favor!' The voice booms and rumbling begins to pick up yet again. Charlie opens one of his eyes and looks up at the shrine before speaking yet again. 'But all I ask for Is forgiveness…why I need to repay for that?' Charlie’s voice goes to it’s normal tone and he opens both his eyes, staring in a weird way at the statue of Buddha in front of him…which has miraculously change facial expression!Not only has the Buddha’s grin widened but his eye’s are opened fully and they are staring directly into Charlie’s…surprisingly enough Charlie doesn’t seem at all phased by this, he probably hasn’t even noticed! 'Are you questioning me MORTAL FOOL?!' The voice booms and the shaking and flickering comes stronger than ever as the lockers begin to rattle and the two cups with incense fall over with a loud CRASH~! Shattering on the locker room floor! 'But I not know what I ask for that cost me price…' Charlie speaks almost in a challenging tone to the voice of the supposed 'god'. 'You asked for redemption! You have been given Redemption!' The voice booms once more, only this time nothing shakes…not even the statue! Charlie’s eye’s light up as he leans back on his legs and asks yet another question to the almighty god. 'I have redemption? I owe you big time Buddha, thank you….but who I get my redemption from?' 'You will get a chance to prove your worth and gain your forgiveness by facing Anton The Kid! If you fail me not only will I make your life a living hell, but I will make sure that demons plague you through the after life!' The voice continues to bellow with an almost almighty tone. 'I not fail you again…' Charlie breaks out of the concentration that he was in and let’s go of a huge grin in front of the statue before the voice replies once more. 'You had better not Mortal…for your sake! I have given up my time to tend to your matters, now you must prove your worth and repay me by gaining Redemption! Then and only then will your forgiveness be complete and your conscious clean!' The voice slowly fades off into the distance as the rumbling begins to stop. The grin on Charlie’s face can be seen turning into a sinister one as a soft snigger can be heard from outside the door of the locker room. As Charlie wipes the smile off of his face, he lowers his head and continues to pray, thanking Buddha for all that he has just done… From behind the wall and next to the door of the locker room, standing with his hand covering his mouth is none other than the Egyptian god himself…The Pharaoh! Trying hard to contain himself from doubling over in laughter one hand holds his mouth while the other clutches his stomach as his body guard turns around to see this and takes it as a sign of Charlie harming his master. An angry look crosses over his face as walks in front of the doorway to the locker room and looks back at his master, tilting his head to signal his will to attack. The Pharaoh wipes a tear from his eye before shaking his head and regaining his composure as the two leave Charlie praying to his god in the locker room while both the Pharaoh and Isiy walk down the long hallway of the backstage area of the PCW arena…. A cruel trick has just been played on the man who was humiliated in front of thousands just moments before…his hopes have now risen and he looks set to gain his Redemption against Anton The Kid! Is the Pharaoh doing this for Charlie’s own good or is he doing it as a cruel joke, who knows! But one thing is for sure, Charlie is back on his warpath and he’s looking directly down the line at none other than Anton…
