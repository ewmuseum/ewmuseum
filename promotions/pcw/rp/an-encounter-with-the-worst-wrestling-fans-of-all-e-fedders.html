An encounter with the worst wrestling fans of all: E-FEDDERS!!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered [Scene opens in the lobby of the Sheraton Hotel in Dallas, Texas just hours before Friday Fusion is set to go on the air. Some eager fans hang around in the lobby hoping to get the autograph of their favourite PCW stars. Meanwhile, coming down in an elevator from his 14th floor hotel room is none other than Flaymin Aymin.Inside the elevator, he checks his bag to make sure that he hasn’t forgotten any of the necessities needed for tonight’s match against Violence Jack] Flaymin: Boots…. check.Testicle Guarding Compartment…. check.‘Fro Comb…. check.Blowtorch…. check. Yep, that’s everything! [Still in the elevator (hey it’s a long trip to the bottom!), Flaymin looks across at a posh woman who’s also been staying in the hotel.Decked out in a fancy dress and expensive jewellery, the woman looks back at Flaymin before turning her head away in disgust] Flaymin: Hi, I’m Flaymin, nice to meet ya! [The woman frowns at Flaymin before answering in a tense voice] Posh woman: The pleasure is all mine. Flaymin: I don’t think you should be pleasuring yourself in the elevator…. Especially considering this damn thing is see-through. Posh woman: Hmmph!Brute! Flaymin: So where are you staying? Posh woman: The penthouse on the 19th floor. Flaymin: Penthouse huh?I prefer Playboy actually. [The elevator finally reaches the ground floor and the ‘ding’ sound is heard before the elevator doors open up. The woman quickly exits the elevator in an effort to get as far away from Flaymin as humanly possible. However, before Flaymin can get into a waiting Taxi he is mobbed the hoards of enthusiastic autograph seekers who have been camping out in the lobby for the last few hours] Flaymin: Aw crap!Can’t you people go bother someone else?I hear Mr Nelson is giving away free money…. [The fans ignore Flaymin’s plea and instead shove their memorabilia in his face waiting for The Flaymin One to sign it all] Flaymin: Get this trash out of my face before I smack someone upside the head! [Suddenly, without warning Flaymin dashes out of the way and tries to make it out of the hotel.With the fans in hot pursuit, he manages to dodge a few bellhops and a variety of suitcases scattered everywhere. Finally out of the hotel he looks for a taxi.His head urgently swings to the right and then back to the left. But on both occasions he fails to find a waiting taxi.With the angry fans closing in, Flaymin begins to panic and attempts to find a temporary hiding place. But before he can successfully jam his body into a nearby garbage bin, a beeping horn of a blue sedan rings out. Not willing to stand around and consider his options, Flaymin runs towards the car, opens the door and jumps into the backseat. Inside the car, Flaymin looks around at the two other people in the car] Flaymin: Do I know you? Jeff: Hi, I’m Jeff and I’m a huge PCW fan! Stan: Hi, my name is Stan- can you autograph my shirt? Flaymin: Eh, at least two fans are better than 40 or so morons. [Flaymin takes Stan’s shirt and begins to autograph it] Flaymin: So guys like wrestling, huh? Jeff: Yep, in our spare time we do a lot of e-fedding. Flaymin: You feed what? Stan: No, it’s an electronic wrestling federation!Basically we create wrestlers and write about them. [Flaymin looks at Stan with a confused look on his face] Flaymin: You what? Stan: Yeah!We spend our time sitting in front of a computer typing out or failed hopes and dreams of what would have happened had we become professional wrestlers! I’m a World champion in my fed and Jeff is the World champ in another fed. Flaymin: Whoa, hold on a sec!How can you both be World Champions?Last I checked we only had one world. Jeff: But it’s all made up so it doesn’t matter! Flaymin: You mean you both compete on different planets or something, kinda like Star Wars? Is there like a Universal Championship that connects all these different worlds together?You know like how the planeteers would combine their rings to summon Captain Planet! Stan: Uh… no. Flaymin: Well then how can you call yourself a world champ?For all I know, there’s probably a whiz in Barbados who e-feds better than you- so shouldn’t he be world champ? Jeff: Dude, its just a game! Flaymin: No its not a game!!Life isn’t a game, son.If you’re going to claim to be World Champion, you have to be sure.You can’t just create some phoney baloney computer game which all of a sudden entitles you create your own rules in life. [The two fans look at each other in bewilderment] Jeff: You’re totally missing the poi… Flaymin: NO, you’re missing the point.I feel sorry for you, actually.The fact that you sit behind a computer and type up crap which won’t get you anywhere in life is a sad, sad tragedy. Stan: It did get me somewhere in life!I’m now a Newspaper Editor! Flaymin: No one reads newspapers- people just use them to wipe their ass when they run out of toilet paper. Jeff: I don’t think its fair if you disrespect our hobby without even seeing what its all about?Writing matches is time consuming, ya know! Flaymin: Ha!You write matches, too? I’ll bet you create little pictures of yourselves too so you can be proud of your imaginary creations? Stan: Yep, there is a program just for that! Flaymin: Must be a pretty crappy TV show. Stan: Its…. Not that kind of program. Flaymin: Who are you to say what’s right or wrong??You’re out here parading with your imaginary world title and your cartoon pictures- you’ve got no right! Stan: No right to what? [Flaymin shrugs] Flaymin: I don’t know- I kinda ran out of ideas mid-way through the sentence. But the matter of the fact is simply that I refuse to allow this sport to be tarnished by geeky assholes who sit around typing stories based on real wrestling. Jeff: Yeah!?!?And what are you going to do about it? Flaymin: You just wait and see….. [Flaymin rubs his hands together and grins as the scene fades to black] What does Flaymin have up his sleeve? Whatever it is, when its all said and done….e-fedding will be dead and buried!!!!
