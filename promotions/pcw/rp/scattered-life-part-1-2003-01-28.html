Scattered Life (part 1)

28 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-28-03 The scene opens up to a very alarming news report about a fire ripping a two story house apart in several minutes, trapping a little three year old boy in his room. The lady covering the story alerts everyone that the boy has been found and taken out, but he isn't breathing. The newsman changes the angle of his camera getting a shot of paramedics standing over a little boy trying desperately to resuscitate him, as his parents look on in fear, crying their hearts out, expecting the worst. Several minutes pass and the paramedic leans back in his kneeling position and looks up to the parents signaling that it's just no use. This of course sends the mother into a frenzy as she starts flailing around balling her eyes out. The camera slowly pulls away until the people around the boy are as small as ants. It then cuts off for a mere second, before reopening. It now focuses on an up close shot of an eye with a stream of water flowing from it. The camera slowly pulls back and reveals the face of Brianna who stares blankly at the television screen, overcome with emotions. Her white short sleeve v-cut shirt flattens out from it’s crinkles as she sits back onto the couch stretching her legs out in front of her. The wipes the tears away as she stands up to answer the door as a 'ding-dong' can be heard. She quickly pulls her hair back into a loose ponytail before opening the door. Standing there is Thomas wearing a blue button down shirt and brown khakis. Brianna looks extremely shocked to see him as she normally does..... 'What are you doing here Thomas?' 'The question is what are you doing here? This isn't your house.' Thomas replies while walking right in. 'Tell me what you want and then leave.' she says standing in front of him, preventing him from advancing further into the house. Thomas sees what's she's doing so he just stands there looking around. 'So you never answered my question sweet pea...what are you doing here?' 'I don't believe any of that is your business, and stop calling me anything but my name!' 'Ok, I see it's that time of month again so I will just let you be.' He pushes past her and walks into the living room taking a seat as Brianna stares on looking appalled. 'Will you get out! I don't recall inviting you in.' 'Who said I was here for you?' Before Brianna can make a reply, her current boyfriend, Raphael comes strolling in acknowledging Brianna by giving her a peck on the cheek. Then looks over towards the couch. 'Thomas, glad you could make it.' He walks over and shakes hands with Thomas which makes Brianna's jaw drop. 'Listen we will get going as soon as I change.' Raphael walks away in his dirt and grass stained outfit and Thomas quickly diverts his attention back to Brianna. 'Raphael never told me he had a girlfriend, and never for the life of me would I have guessed stubborn ol' Brianna. So how long have you two been dating?' Thomas blurts out as he waits for an answer. 'I met him week or two ago at a bar and we have been seeing each other ever since.' she reluctantly replies as she takes a seat in a chair next to the couch, flipping through the latest issue of Cosmo. 'So you went to the bar after one of your victories, got plastered, and met up with Raphael?' Thomas asks slyly knowing that it will get under Brianna's skin, which it does. She slams the magazine shut and looks up in his direction with hate in her eyes. 'You know there really is no point in me talking you. Besides I regret ever telling you anything because it's none of your business in the first place!' she tosses the magazine onto the coffee table, and as soon as she does Raphael comes back in. 'Are you ready for an rousing game of golf Tom?' Thomas stands up and walks out the door never looking Brianna once in the eye. Raphael follows right behind giving Brianna a peck on the cheek as he walks out with a golf bag over his shoulder. Though they have only been an item for a couple of weeks, Brianna feels very connected with Raphael Cravis. Ever since she met him that day in the bar, she knew that something was special about him. Not only is he an outgoing, caring, handsome, and intelligent man, but he is very interested in Brianna’s line of work and her ambitions. Now knowing that Raphael is friends with Thomas, Brianna is upset and scared that Thomas will mess it up for her. Having those thoughts running non stop, she walks into the kitchen area, and sits down at the round table boasting a beautiful bouquet of flowers. She pulls something out of her pocket and sets it on the table. It looks like a two inch thick miniature envelope. She presses a button on the side and it starts to unfold into a circular shape. Before it does anymore, Brianna presses the button a second time and then tosses it into the open trash can a few feet from her. 'All of these gadgets, and not one could save a life.....' a tear comes to her eye as she recalls the newstory she heard earlier that day. Seeing a kid die that young really gets to you, and not in a good way. It tears your heart apart....and in Brianna's case images from her past flash in her mind linking to the story. All of that washes away as soon as a knock is heard on the door. Brianna shakes her head and snaps out of her daze as she gets up for her trip to the door. Another knock. 'I'm coming.' She dashes to the door not liking to leave people waiting. Standing there is a bigger woman, wearing a red shirt and black skirt, who looks shocked to see Brianna answer the door. 'Is Raphael home?' She asks while giving Brianna a quick look over. 'No he isn't, but he should be back in a few hours.' Brianna replies not sure what this is all about. 'You must be the twig he's banging now right? Look I know he's in there, and his chicken ass just saw me so he told you to get it for him. Well tell that idiot that he needs to start visiting his son, because I didn't lay in the bed myself, and I am sure as hell not raising Lawrence all by myself.' The woman walks away rolling her eyes at Brianna as she stands there speechless.... The scene slowly fades out as we get a close up of Brianna's face. Everything crams together in her head as the scene switches from the news report, to Thomas arriving, to Brianna not understanding why she can never make a life saving invention, to the lady bearing unthinkable news, and finally back to Brianna's expressionless face....TO BE CONTINUED!
