Blockbuster Bastards

15 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15th March When you're afroless what else can you do but buy a wig? And that is precisely what Flaymin Aymin had to do after El Diablo shaved his ridiculous afro hair off. But all things comedic have a hint of seriousness to them and this would be no exception, only things could become very serious this time. But why? And what happened? Sure, El Diablo had some fun shaving off Aymin's afro and sure he laughed a few times about it, but one person that won't be laughing is Aymin himself, and he must be out for vengeance somehow, but when will he strike? And what exactly will this Australian native do? What was once comedic will soon transpire into something deadly. El Diablo sits on a black leather chair in his home in Chicago, Illinois. Well Angel's home really, as it was her who convinced him to buy a house there. He hasn't travelled for Fusion yet and doesn't look set to either, it looks like the Mexican Devil may not be showing up for Friday Fusion, which could be for his own protection or maybe just due to his laziness. He is wearing a black top and black denim pants, which match his black and charred mask, which became charred at Diablo's and Aymin's confrontation on Sabotage, which resulted in El Diablo claiming a victory over the afroless Aymin. Surrounding the black chair is a large room, a nice navy blue carpet on the floor and clear white walls, which is the complete opposite color of El Diablo's soul. In that room is a large television set, coffee table in the centre of the room, a large black leather couch and several cupboards for storage as well as a bookshelf for El Diablo's books of Babylon and evil. El Diablo seems to be doing nothing at all, but being seated in his chair and staring into nothingness. The front door slams shut as Angel walks in wearing only a black dressing gown, which fails to cover her body properly and clearly shows her legs. Her hair is a mess from the wind outside and she tries to style it with her free hand as she holds the mail in her other. 'Mail's here,' she says to El Diablo aloof. El Diablo suddenly blinks and looks up at her as she stands right by his chair, and hands him the mail. She continues to style hair as she walks to the couch and sits down still playing with her long black hair. Diablo flicks through the mail, while making snide comments as he flicks past one letter to another. 'Bill,' he says annoyed. 'Bill,' he says again as he discards them both to the floor. He continues to flick through them. 'Do I want a sex change? NO!' he speaks monotone like Al Gore while hurling the letter at the waste paper basket some distance away in the kitchen and obviously failing. 'Pantene free shampoo sample, hmmm,' he ponders while touching his long dark hair. He places the sample in a pocket of his jeans. 'Blockbuster,' he says confused. He proceeds to open the letter and reads it out loud to himself. 'Dear Customer, you have failed to return 'Anal Blue' on time and will therefore be made to pay a customary fine of $2 a day for lateness and your membership of Blockbuster will be suspended until the video has been returned. Many thanks Blockbuster,' he finishes reading with a confused look on his face. El Diablo looks across at Angel whom is still playing with her hair, trying to get it just right. 'Angel,' he calls, 'Did you rent out a movie called Anal Blue?' he asks in relative ease. Still playing with her hair, 'No honey, why?' she replies with question. El Diablo looks more confused than ever now. 'Oh, no reason,' he responds even sounding confused. He looks again at the letter, and speaks under his breath to himself. 'Could I have?' he interrogates himself, 'No, I haven't been drunk lately,' he answers himself like he is playing cop and robber. He sits and thinks some more. '$2 a day?' he exclaims in shock, 'I'm not paying that,' the cheapskate states horrified. El Diablo gets up to his feet and places the Blockbuster letter back into its envelope and then folds the letter up and places it into one of his jeans' pockets. 'I'm going out,' he says to his wife as he walks out into the hall to get a jacket. Angel is still playing with her hair. 'OK sweetie, but watch out, it's very windy,' she says with lack of concern in her voice. El Diablo heads towards the front door putting his jacket on and whispering under his breath about the situation. 'Damned Blockbuster, I don't even rent movies. And if it wasn't Angel then it must be a mistake,' he curses. He opens the front door and walks out slamming it shut behind him. His hair blows in the big gusts of wind as he walks along heading for his local Blockbuster video shop. 'I'll soon sort this mess out,' he whispers to himself as he walks along oblivious to the world around him. He soon reaches the Blockbuster video shop and walks in, heading straight for the counter. 'Yes may I help you,' says the geeky teenager at the counter. El Diablo just stares at the boy with greasy black hair and acne filled face. 'I received a letter today saying I rented a video out and have not returned it,' he states in an angered fashion, 'When in reality I have not even rented a video from here,' he finishes angrily. 'May I see the letter?' squeaks the teenager. El Diablo nods and goes into his jean pockets and then slams the contents onto the counter. 'Shampoo?' the teenager asks confused. El Diablo swiftly grabs the shampoo packet and puts it back into his jeans pocket. 'Wrong pocket,' he states in minor embarrassment. He goes into his other pocket and hands the letter over the teenager. The kid opens it and reads the letter while nodding. 'I'll check this out on the computer,' the kid says as he walks into the back room. El Diablo waits at the counter impatiently for a few minutes until the teenager returns with the letter still in his hand. 'Everything is in order, sir,' the teen says to El Diablo while handing him back the letter. El Diablo sighs and looks angry. 'How is this so?' Diablo asks in annoyance. 'Well we have the records of a man described as you renting it out,' the boy replies. Diablo puts the letter away in his pocket. 'Well I wasn't here,' El Diablo finishes as he walks away from the counter in anger. He browses the store a while until he finds the Adult section and find another copy of the video he supposedly hadn't brought back. 'Well, well, well,' he says as he sees that Flaymin Aymin is on the front cover of it with his current valet Jenna Jameson. El Diablo picks the video up and then runs out of the store as fast as he can. 'Hey,' shouts the teenager as he tries to jump over the counter to make chase but falls over seriously hurting himself. El Diablo remains running until he reaches his home. He opens the door quickly and steps in hurriedly slamming the door shut. He stops and breathes heavily after the long run. 'Home already?' shouts Angel from the living room. El Diablo catches his breath, 'Yeah,' he replies. He then takes off his jacket and walks back into the living room, where Angel is still playing around with her hair. El Diablo walks towards the Television and VCR and switches them on as he opens the video box and puts in the stolen movie 'Anal Blue' He then sits back down as Angel looks at him in shock. **TIME ELAPSES** Angel is no longer in the room and El Diablo is about half way through the porn film. 'That bastard,' he exclaims as he notices something. He immediately stops the tape and rewinds it, then plays that part again. 'It's bigger than mine,' he finishes in an angry tone. **MORE TIME ELAPSES** The porn film is near the end now and El Diablo is just staring on in anger at something. 'I can't believe this,' he says to himself as he stands up. He stops the tape and rewinds it again. He watches it over and over again, rewinding each time. And every time he does he gets angrier and angrier, until the throws the remote down onto the floor and stamps on it. 'He won't get away with this,' he shouts lividly to no one except himself, 'Aymin has just changed everything and now this is serious,' he yells as he storms out from the living room. The television is still on and on there is a tractor, until the tape finishes and rewinds itself. And again, something that was once comedic seems destined to transpire into something deadly serious.
