Hi. Yes I do exist.

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-31-2002 Hiro: I’m facing…Spiderman? [Or some other superhero from the Marvel Universe. Contrary to the confusion in his voice Hiro really isn’t surprised. Sure, he is only twenty-three years old and he has been in the business for only a few years but in those few years he has experienced some mind numbing highs and some earth shattering lows.] [Facing a man who wrestles in superhero outfits is nothing new. Hiro has wrestled a man who believes he is Napoleon Bonaparte, he has wrestled a hermaphrodite, a hardcore Jesus freak, a mobster boss, a professional golfer, a homosexual who had a crush on him and a man who talks to magical appliances. This man’s best friend is aptly named, ‘Violent Jesus.’] Hiro: Or Captain America… [Hiro sits on his forest green couch leafing through a folder full of pictures and reports. Pictures of Spiderman and Captain America wrestling. Much like how Hiro is a mystery to Rent-a-Hero, Hero is just as much of a mystery to Hiro. He takes out one of the reports and scans over it over as he leans back on the couch while propping his feet up on the coffee table.] Hiro: Former cruiserweight champion… [His warm hazel eyes intently run over the paper. Absorbing all the knowledge he can about his first opponent in the PCW. There is no day off for a professional fighter. You study, train, study then train again. Obviously this is the study part of the day for Hiro. A pair of black mesh sorts cover his thighs and a orange ‘Quicksilver’ tee shirt covers his torso.] Hiro: [Looking directly into the camera] I honestly have nothing about you, [looking back at the paper] Rent a Hero. Well besides the info I got from your bio and such. But I do have some questions for you… [Hiro is that kind of guy, full of questions. You know that kid who was always asking questions in fourth grade? That was Hiro.] Hiro: Why do you dress up…dress up the way you do? I mean why don’t you just be who you are? I have nothing against what you do and it obviously works for you but…why dress up? Why wear a mask? [He glances at the paper again then places it back on the table.] Hiro: I mean if you want to make a difference and be a good guy then why don’t you just…go out…and do like good things? It works for me. Just keep true to yourself and do the right thing…I don’t know… [Saotome shrugs then reaches over the side of the couch and pulls up a simple blue shoebox. Placing it on his lap Hiro smiles awkwardly and removes the lid.] Hiro: Now uhh, this is something I’ve never done before but from what I understand there are a lot of questions surrounding me and the PTC. [Looking into the shoe box] I’ve only been here a few days and already the mail has started to pour in…so I will take some time to answer some questions. [Giving a polite nod to the camera Hiro takes the first envelope out of the box and looks at the face then slides his index finger inside the sealed flap. Roughly tearing the top of the letter open Hiro smiles…] Hiro: Brad Reed of Worland, Wyoming writes… Dear Hiro, I’ve followed your career ever since you started out in MSW and I was really stoked to find out that you signed with PURE…what happened man? You never showed up and that like totally sucked. I really wanted to see you and Miyagawa square off again and I was really hoping to see Kensuke and you team up. That would have been such a bad ass team! So yeah, what happened Hiro? Oh yeah, is it true you used to participate in events such as “Toughman” and “Pride” in Japan? Thanks for reading and I hope you show up in PCW, you could kick some serious ass! Brad [The trademark Saotome smile has arrived. Hiro has never been one to take a compliment well, he blushes and squirms like a school girl in heat. This smile is a telltale trait for young Hiro. When he is nervous it appears, when he is anxious it appears and when you embarrass or, even worse in his opinion, flatter him the smile will appear.] Hiro: Thanks a lot Brad. You asked some great questions… [He neatly folds the back up and places it back in the torn envelope] Hiro: First let me say thanks for the kind words, I appreciate it. PURE didn’t pan out for a few reasons. I had some contractual problems with the fed I was working in at the time and when they found out I signed with another fed I apparently broke my contact with them. I didn’t want to risk a law suit and I sure didn’t want to be put on probation making me ineligible for possible matches. There was only one route of action I could take at the time and that was to just wait my contact out. And yes, I have participated in the ‘Pride’ fighting tournament in Japan. I have also competed in various boxing tournaments but I’ve never participated in ‘Toughman.’ Who knows, maybe I will? [Hiro again flashes his smile and pulls out another letter, this one was previously opened. He removes the letter…] Hiro: This was a letter from a friend of mine. Wilde Tanke. I won’t bore you with all the personal stuff but… I so can't wait to get to wrestling in Memphis. But before I go, I wanted to hear if you really skipped out on RUSH. I know that’s not your style, what happened?. So is it true or is someone bs'ing us all? Between you and me, do you think I'd have a shot in hell at signing a fed, Hiro? Hiro: Heh, this question has been asked to me many times...maybe I can clarify. Technically I am still in RUSH but only for a short time. I will be working a few shows but my focus and attention will be here in the PCW. [The smile vanishes for a moment as Hiro’s eyes stare off into nothingness, his brow furrows slightly. It looks like he is debating something, like he is deciding if he should go into more detail about this question or not.] Hiro: I couldn’t get into the place in all honesty. I didn’t feel wanted and I just wasn’t comfortable there. I have nothing against the wrestlers there, maybe I didn’t give it enough of a chance but…when I heard there was a chance I could get on the PTC flagship, the PCW, I couldn’t pass it up. [He blinks, glances into the camera, smiles then goes back to the letter…] …Nah, I think I'm happy with what I got. Did you ever hook up with that girl I told you about down in South Cali? Talkin' red hot babe there. [See the smile? He is a little embarrassed] Hiro: Heh…sorry Wilde, I didn’t… Oh, Shelly said she heard Jeremy Thomas is off to be some kind of actor, or writer, or whatever. Not true, is it? [Hiro’s smile slowly fades away…] Hiro: Yeah…Jer and I had a falling out. Yeah he joined the UME…then he…retired… Um, that's it, I'll tell you about Amanda more next letter. Gotta go. Stuff to do, people to con, all that good stuff. Sin,'WA' Wilde T Hiro: Let’s see what the other letters say… Hey Hiro-chan! You are like totally hot and I have all your posters! You are so nice and cool to people, you gave me your autograph in Hawaii and I like almost died! Oh yeah! Did I mention that you are like WAY HOT?! Why did you go to the PCW? I will miss seeing you in Hawaii! I hope Edward is doing good, he is such a cool guy and I heard that he is in PCW with you. Keep cool QT! L8trSk8tr! Kayla! [Letters like this bring a smile to Hiro’s face. What we can’t see or smell for that matter are the small pink hearts drawn all over the paper and the light perfume smell emitted from the envelope.] [Hiro smiles brightly then gives a quick bow to the camera.] Hiro: Thanks a lot Kayla, I really appreciate it. I hope I continue to live up to your expectations. I signed with PCW because of Edward. I found out that he was coming here I gave him a call and he said that he was already talking to the proper people about me coming in. Thanks for the letter Kayla… [Hiro folds the letter back up and slips it back into the envelope.] Hiro: I am sending you an autographed poster some other stuff. Hope you like them. Make sure you tune into Turmoil this week So yeah…uhh…that’s about it from here…bye? [And on that note we fade to black]
