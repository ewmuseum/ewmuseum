The Parents You Never Want To Meet

1 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/1/03 Tesssaaaaaa A voice crept out of my dreams and into my ears, but yet it was too early to listen to get up so I blocked it out of my mind and went back to my dreams. Tessssaaa It cried out once more. I looked up in this foggy state of mind and realized that I was back in my dreams, but was this a dream it would it turn horribly wrong somewhere and turn into a nightmare? The only way to find out was to get up and explore my surroundings and find out just who that voice belonged to. Knowing that I would much rather find out what this dream was all about than wake up and face another day of a Chris who wanted nothing to do with me I rose myself out of the bed of mine is this image of mine and began to walk through this house that was unfamiliar to me. “There you are!” The voice that was calling my name cried out. I looked up and saw a woman I didn’t recognize walking over to me to embrace me in a hug. I was not one to turn away a hug of any sort but at the time I wanted to know just who in the hell this woman was and why on earth she had her arms around my like she had known me all my life, or at least a vast majority of it. “Who…” I started to stutter out as I slowly pried myself away from the woman and studied her carefully. “Oh I’m sorry. I haven’t introduced myself yet have I?” The woman said with a slight tinge of guilt in her voice perhaps feeling guilty for not introducing herself earlier. “No you haven’t.” I answered hoping that this would be my answer. “I’m Mrs. Green….” She started off with her sentence but before she could say anymore my mind began to run wild. Mrs. Green? Could this be who I thought it was? “You mean….” I started off nervously “You’re thinking right. I’m Gaz’s mother. It’s quite enchanting to have you of all people as my daughter in law.” She chuckled before unleashing a bright smile. GAZ HAS PARENTS? My mind screamed out in question. I knew this was a dream and that anything could happen but was this true in real life and would it be Gaz’s idea for a good prank one day to bring in his either of his parents for display and call me his wife. GAH! Just the thought of Gaz calling me his wife disgusted me. Me a Green? It was absolutely the worst thought ever. Beside why couldn’t he just become a Windsor? “Why don’t you come downstairs to meet my husband and Gaz’s father?” She continued on. Okay now it’s time to wake up!!! I forced my eyes opened and faced the morning sun. Today brought a new day and the challenge of Teresa Tomas and even if it were yet another day where I would have to put up with P.W.A. antics and Chris refusing to speak to me then I would much rather put up with that than the thought of ever having to be called Tessa Green again, or ever having to face the parents of a man I absolutely despised and have them call me their daughter and law.
