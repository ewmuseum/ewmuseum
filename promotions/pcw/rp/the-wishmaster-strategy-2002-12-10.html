The Wishmaster --- Strategy...

10 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/10/02 Open with Sandy Wong wearing a red dress, pacing around The End. The End, in ring attire, sits on a metal black stool. The room is empty, there seem to be no walls. There's just a single spotlight on The End with Sandy circling him. This is what would be a 'Strategy session.' Sandy:'Who is your opponent?' End: 'Dante Jackson.' Sandy:'Yes. What are his dimensions?' End:'Six feet, four inches. Two hundreds twenty pounds. Tall and slender.' A file headshot picture of Dante Jackson is projected on the back wall behind The End, not that he could see it anyway. The camera zooms out to show everything. Sandy:'What is his attack?' End:'Dante Jackson works a style based on speed. He moves fast, attacks fast. His offense is based on aerial moves. He is capable of submissions, but doesn't use them as much. He may also try some lifting, but nothing that is based solely on arm strength.' Sandy:'Very good.' The picture shifts to an old RUSH stillshot of Dante Jackson performing his experimental finishing maneuver (at the time), a top rope spinning tombstone piledriver, at a house show to an unknown opponent. Sandy:'What is your strategy for attack?' End:'Attack the legs, take out his vertical base. He cannot most quickly if he cannot stand.' Sandy:'Good. What else?' End:'Injure his back. His leg and back strength are what support 80% of his offense. He cannot lift me or attack with his legs and back injured. Dante Jackson was a champion in RUSH, but that will not matter. I am bigger, stronger, faster, more skilled than Dante Jackson. Dante Jackson has been primarily a tag team wrestler since coming to PCW, I've mainly done things on my own.' The picture of Dante Jackson is replaced with a picture of The End, standing victorious in the ring. Sandy:'What will you do?' End: 'I will win. I will win at Turmoil, and I will win at Fusion, triumphing over Dante Jackson twice, on my way to reclaiming the Continental Championship as my own.' Sandy:'Good. Now close out the promo with some random nonsense.' End:'This is a poem dedicated to Dante Jackson's childhood, when his parents tried to give their brain damaged little boy a glimpse of a nice life when they sign him up in the ghetto little leagues full of other equally hopeless children...' Our team and the other team were tied 0-0 with a minute left to play.Jared made a sloppy hand-off to Carlos; I knew I should’ve been quarterback. Carlos ran with the ball for about ten seconds before three big guys tackled him.He really ought to lose some weight if he wants to run faster. When Carlos got hit, Sammy from the other team got it and ran and ran and ran.He’s good; too bad his parents took him to that other team down on Pine Street. Ty ran after Sammy, but he couldn’t reach; but he tripped and Sammy fell, too.I told the coach that his clumsiness would come in handy. Sammy tripped and let go of the ball. I was at the end of the field.I slipped when we hiked the ball and I kinda got up slow. I ran and I got the ball. Louis tried to get it but I got there before him.I guess it didn’t really matter who got it ‘cause we’re on the same team. I picked up the ball and held it real tight and ran to the goal.Then I had to run back the other way because I almost ran to our team’s goal. The guys that tried to tackle me but they slipped because of the muddy ground.It rained an hour before the game and our cleats didn’t come yet. I ran to the other team’s goal and I got past this last guy.He was the only one on his team with gloves on. I came into the goal and the crowd was cheering!My team came over and picked me up and carried me on their shoulders. We went in the back and the coach had pizza, ice cream and soda for all of us!I ate until I got an ice cream headache from all the Rocky Road I had. The other team came over and we all shook hands.The parents brought their cars around and picked up the kids. Mommy talked to me on the way home and said something really weird.Daddy, what’s soccer? Sandy:'Good.'
