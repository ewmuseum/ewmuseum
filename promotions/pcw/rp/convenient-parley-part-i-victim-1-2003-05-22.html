Convenient Parley - Part I - Victim # 1

22 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/22/03 (OOC: I decided to take out my other starting part named 'Back to Bainbridge' with this.. this RP isn't about my match but others parts will be relevant to literally, speaking about it.) Peering through the side window panes was the Misogynist himself, behind a shady gray night as the moon was yet to appear and the past frigid temperatures swoon in to a rather warm state all found in the bowels of the rural-like county of Bainbridge. Stone continuously pushed and pounded the door bell, profusely shifting his head from left to right to the sides of him, where the cube shaped windows remain clear and without streaks, glowing with the back light. Finally, the door before Stone opened, coming after it was a well-tailored male, in his mid-30's, goatee grown, slick (back) and sleek brown hair.. he stepped to his side, offering Stone his entry.. 'Hello Stone..' he curtly introduced as Stone nodded, stepping in, finding himself in the alpha of a spacious passageway.. the homestead seemed professionally embellished, dull yet sophisticated.. Frames over the refined and rippleless white walls.. a tandem of closet and room doors.. and lastly the usual yet clever idea of a labyrinth shaping. The man leading Stone through the monotonous scenery went by the name of Elliot Costa... he himself wasn't the usual person, maybe not as fiend as the Misogynist would be but still rather uncanny... Known as the women cognoscente not related to Stone but of a past stowed acquaintance. Coming through the all white and monochromatic walkways they came to a single, door less room, legion of sofas and tables. Elliot advanced forward giving Stone a tutorial of his seeing's first with the gesturing and motioning of his hands.. Stone strolled forward, plopping his rear on to an elaborately designed sofa, not for consistent sitting but just at betimes of coffee klatch. Elliot sat divided on a similar sofa, carelessly throwing his legs on to a glass-made table to the front of him. Sitting at silence, the ticking of multiple ancient Grandfather clocks resounded throughout the commodious house bringing Elliot to break the deadness.. 'So.. What’d you like to know?' he pertly started, reaching to his side on a wee wooden table, marbling the littered paper on it senselessly. 'You should already know..' he tautly retorted as Elliot nodded, reaching even further to his side extracting a sealed manila folder.. and sending over to the other side with a stiff toss to Stone, catching it, examining the front and back, then opening it.. Bringing out a few missive-like papers, he riffled and read through them, but looking at his expression, it was all seemingly too scant. 'This doesn't look like much.. There’s not even any direction or guidelines to how I can pull of these crimes and murders.. Plus how it's going to interfere with my full-time job.' he added as he placed the papers on to the table, gaining a shrug from both of Elliot's shoulders.. 'It's nothing too difficult or too easy.. and wait.. you have a full-time job? Since when?' he injected.. 'Don't worry about my job.. just worry about this.' he commanded as Elliot gently nodded, sliding the papers back over to Stone's side, while standing up, having his shadow silhouette over Stone.. 'When you think about these things just remember they are feeble-minded and weak physically.. well at least the majority of them.. but coax them, as they are the number one gender that is easily wheedled.. and from there you'll dominate your killings with ease.' he explained, referring towards women in general and the women actively on his list. Stone went forward, disregarding the advice sliding through the papers and lists coming through his mind.. as the list of soon to come contrition's went on and on.. Elliot perched on the arm handle of the sofa, waiting aimlessly as Stone leafed through papers. Finishing his look through examination Elliot pointed his pointer finger to the side of him, leading down towards the exit.. Stone looked up as Elliot ended.. 'You may leave now.. and after every murder you have pulled off report to me.. CLEAR? If not.. I'll have to teach you how because this can get me in a LOT of trouble.' Stone confusingly looked on, stood up with a single paper, and came on over Elliot, facing him in a nose-to-nose position.. 'Don't you tell me what to do.' Stone piped but being salved by the threat of an semi-automatic pistol handled by Elliot who smiled devilishly, even more diabolically then Stone, as he spread his lips and let out his straight teeth in a more than (needed) wide grin. Stone threw his hands up in innocence as Elliot pointed to the exit, and hastily through went Stone, strutting out with a gait but watched by Elliot pointing the pistol.. almost getting himself lost through the maze-like pad he made his way out through the place, turning the door knob opening the door and heading out, slamming it and dashing forward on to the street pavement. He ran a bit more until' he stopped through the darkened and shadowy night, moonless and starless without the production of much needed light.. and once again Stone read through the paper naming, labeling and stating: VICTIM # 1: Name: Anna Marie TorresHeight/Weight: 5'6'/121 poundsHair Color/Eye Color: Light brown.Address: 136 Bainbridge County, Georgia.. to the side of Jake's Mini Market.Race: Minority - Latin/Spanish Other irrelevant things ran down as Stone continued on, officially embarking his newly supported misogyny campaign.
