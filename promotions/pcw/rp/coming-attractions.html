Title: Coming Attractions... Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: Whatever today is. ::Instead of the usual speech that would normally go here, Politically Incorrect stand at the head of the red carpet that leads into the movie theater at which 'Corruption Man' will be shown. Only Lovecraft, Powell, and Schultz are present. Myriad press figures and other assorted minor celebrities are awaiting the signal to enter, but Politically Incorrect must have their moment...:: William S. Powell: This is amazing, Reginald! We should have entered the movie business a long time ago! Reginald V. Lovecraft: I know...I'm excited. We've poured lots of dollars and manpower into this... I cannot wait to see the final result, the fruits of our labor.. ::The politicians both look at Schultz. Roger has a worried look on his face somewhat...:: William S. Powell: What's wrong, Roger? You look like you've just lost a couple grand on the stock market. ::Schultz does not crack a smile.:: Roger Schultz: The pundits appear to be quite sour on your chances this Sunday at Gold and Glory. Reginald V. Lovecraft: We cannot expect the pundits to always support us. After all, there are a fair number of sources that will not accept our bribes, for whatever reason. It does not matter anyway. We are Politically Incorrect. The leaders of the Political Machine. And, as such, we are the greatest tag team in this company, no matter how many tandems there are now. William S. Powell: It will not be easy. There are many teams in that ring. This is one of those 'clusterfucks', I think they're called. My partner knows how to survive in a clusterfuck. Ask Alexander Miyagawa or Xavier Kannon. Reginald V. Lovecraft: With the exception of that false God Akira Akiyama, I am the largest man in that ring on Sunday. Which means, simply, I'm not easy to throw over the top rope. I have battled the best in Primetime Championship Wrestling. To take on a few tag teams shall be no problem. Roger Schultz: Um, Reginald, you do realize that you're going through AN EXTREME TITLE MATCH before this thing, right? You may not think highly of THE Multiple Man, Blood Angel, Violence Jack, or Kramer Wild Wing....but it doesn't take much skill to use foreign objects! You'll be bruised and bloody by the time you get there! Remember, if one of you is eliminated, both go! Reginald V. Lovecraft: I can be battered.....I can be bruised......it does not matter! For, with Extreme Title or not, I will go into the tag team title match standing proud and tall! It will take more than a fight in some Kentucky trailer trash trailer to put REGINALD V. LOVECRAFT out of commission! Do not worry!! Now, it is time for us to go inside, and introduce our film to the masses! ::Lovecraft gestures to Powell and Schultz, who begin escorting the celebrities and journalists into the movie theater. The theater has somewhere around 250 seats, which quickly fill up. Three remain in the front, these three of course for Politically Incorrect.:: William S. Powell: Wow....this is it. Our first feature film. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Little bit more than feature I'd say....five hours! ::Both chuckle. The lights slowly dim.....there are no previews in this presentation. Powell has but one thing to say....:: William S. Powell: It begins.. ::The following scrolls down the screen, for a movie presentation....:: 'HERE HE COMES TO SAVE THE DAY!' The infamous announcement of Mighty Mouse's arrival. Now, Mighty Mouse was never the most threatening of superheroes. But, it does not matter. Why not? You see, Politically Incorrect, despite breaking off from The Ultimate Authority, are not the nicest of people. They are, of course, corrupt politicians, and, in the eyes of some, evil. What is a superhero's job? To conquer evil. Now, Politically Incorrect have no desire to be conquered. They want the world to realize that indeed, super villainy is a noble pursuit! And so, when the sun goes down, and when their wrestling matches are over, mild-mannered William S. Powell and Reginald V. Lovecraft......change. Yes, in a top-secret chamber in the Des Moines town hall, they become.... CORRUPTION MAN! YES, CORRUPTION MAN! With his extra-special sidekick, FILIBUSTER BOY! These two goodness haters fight niceities, despise kindness, and strive for the proliferation of evil! ::Finally, in big bold letters, the movie title and credits roll across the screen.: CORRUPTION MAN!Act I: OriginsSTARRING: (in order of appearance)Random Elderly Woman as Grandma in DistressByron Calver as Thoughtful Boy ScoutReginald V. Lovecraft as Corruption ManWilliam S. Powell as Filibuster BoyRoger Schultz as Corruption Man's ButlerKatherine Willis as Intern GirlLouis Brown as William S. Powell's uncle.Tobey McGwire as Rent-A-HeroKatie Cann as Sexual Innuendo GirlRon Jeremy as Trojan Man Our story begins, when we return......
