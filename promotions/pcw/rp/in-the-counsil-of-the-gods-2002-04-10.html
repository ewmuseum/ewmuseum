In The Counsil Of The Gods

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/04/02 In The Council Of The Gods… ‘Something’s wrong here!’ says a familiar voice [A lengthy pause follows] ‘Something’s very wrong’ it repeats [The scene opens up on the face of Titu Xansiv. From the looks of things Pharaoh is staring deeply towards something, or someone, even. A look of deep concern grows about the Jewel’s handsome mug and then he suddenly grins, revealing his pearly white teeth. As the cameras pan out we find that Titu rests on a white leather couch in front of his wife, Isiy. The Egyptian beauty sits blankly looking at Pharaoh without an expression upon her brow or about her gorgeous lips. Pharaoh’s smile suddenly drops and the concerned look from earlier on grows, once again, about his handsome visage. The cameras pan out a little more and we find Master Horus to be sitting next to Pharaoh. The three immortals are surrounded by what looks to be the lounge of Pharaoh’s new home in Cairo, Egypt. Pharaoh looks in the direction of Horus and Horus looks back, he too bears a troubled look on his aged face.] The Pharaoh: There’s defiantly something very, very wrong here! What’s up with her? She was fine after I rescued her; and now look! Not even an expression! Akiyama’s evil has played a great part in this, I’ll bet! “What the hell was up with her? After I’d rescued her she’d been perfectly fine! I couldn’t understand this at all! Akiyama had laid down some sort of curse upon her beautiful head, the scum! He’d promised to return her safely, and as if witnessing her been tortured alive wasn’t enough he had now seemingly muted her permanently. She wouldn’t speak, she would move without being forced and her expression was ever blank and unchanging.” Horus: Hmm…are you sure that she was acting her normal self after the match? I mean, was she like this only when we arrived back here? [Pharaoh nods.] The Pharaoh: Only when we got here, mentor! [Horus strokes his wrinkled chin and then sighs.] Horus: Let me take a closer look! [Horus leans forward and examines Isiy with his wise old eyes. At length he retracts himself away from the young beauty and sighs deeply to himself, not out aloud for Titu to hear.] Horus: I have never seen anything like this before. She has no reactions to anything I do! The Pharaoh: Yes… [Horus leans forward again and waves a hand in front of Isiy’s face. Her watch upon the world around her doesn’t change and she seems not to see the old Gods wrinkly hand before her. After a moment or two Horus slumps himself back down on the comfy couch and breaths a deep breath of confusion.] Horus: I don’t know what to recommend, Titu, but I know one thing! We are scheduled to be setting off to the council of the Gods any moment…are you ready? “Was I ready? Ready for what? To lose my godhood? Ha! What a question. Of course I wasn’t ready, but I was braver and better than Akiyama, I would keep up to my side of our bargain and not cheat my way out of things like he had done!” [Pharaoh sighs slowly.] The Pharaoh: Not really, master…this will be something highly unusual to me, having my powers taken from me will be like losing my TV championship to Tim Walden…completely embarrassing… Horus: But you went out there and did what you aimed to do! You defended your Title successfully and you rescued Isiy… The Pharaoh: It doesn’t seem that way to me! [Pharaoh looks over towards Isiy again; her expression still unchanged.] Horus: Do not worry; if this is something of Akiyama’s doing then we shall report it to the council of the Gods. You are keeping up to your side of the bargain; Akira obviously hasn’t or isn’t keeping up to his! Akiyama said he’d return Isiy safe and sound. And look now! She doesn’t do anything! He’s made her a mindless drone, the cretin of a vermin! “If we don’t get things stored out then I’m going to flip! Just wait Akiyama, if this is of your doing then you shall pay! I promise!” [Pharaoh gets up off the couch and walks towards Isiy. He picks her up over his shoulder and heads towards the lounges door.] The Pharaoh: Come, mentor…the Gods await… [Pharaoh opens the rooms exit and walks out into the hallway, a long, winding place of great beauty. Many doors of immense craftsmanship line the walls of this elegantly designed hallway and rows upon rows of tiny objects, themselves sitting upon small sparkling tables, can also been viewed. Pharaoh walks towards the very end of the hallway closely followed by Horus.] The Pharaoh: I hope the heavenly doorway has been fitted well… Horus: I made sure it was; don’t worry, everything’s going to be fine… The Pharaoh: Like the fact that the two books are still back in California? You should have told me that you were thinking about buying a new home! Horus: Well it was a surprise, wasn’t it! How was I supposed to know you’d found the book of life and the book of the dead and hidden them somewhere in the old palace? [Pharaoh grunts unhappily over his mentor’s foolishness in not talking with him about purchasing the new Palace in Cairo.] “Don’t get me wrong, I respected the old God as much as I respected Zeus, the king of the Gods, but he had been foolish in his decision to buy this place without my consent, especially when he sold the old palace in the same stupid move! The two books are out there and I know have to go and reclaim them, but when? My schedule is so busy these days. Woe is I!” [At the end of the strenuous passage a distant door can be spotted. Pharaoh, carrying Isiy still, and Horus trailing slowly behind the former Egyptian king head towards it with great speed. Eventually they reach the entry and just from looking upon its strangely designed exterior it highly predictable that this must be the ‘Heavenly doorway’ Pharaoh spoke of moments ago.] Horus: Well…this is the last time we’ll be moving through this passage… [Pharaoh sighs.] The Pharaoh: Yes, it truly is a shame that my Godhood must come to such a simplistic end… [Pharaoh sighs again and hangs his head in sorrow. A moment passes and soon Pharaoh raises his head proudly before, and with a stern look upon his handsome brow mind you, he places his hand upon the doorknob and twists. The door flies open and suddenly a blinding light hits our eyes. Pharaoh shields himself from its utter brightness with his arm yet Master Horus doesn’t seem to be affected by its sightless colour at all.] Horus: Here we go, Titu. [Horus strictly looks out into the white distance and then, with a smile, he turns back to Titu.] Horus: Let us enter… [The Pharaoh watches on in awe as Horus walks through he door, disappearing simultaneously into the blinding light.] “Well this was it, the last time I’d ever get a chance to enter the heavenly realm…and a sadness filled my heart suddenly as I looked on into the distance. The blinding light was awe-inspiring and that was more than enough to describe its purely marvellous appearance. I gulped and then took a step forward…” [Pharaoh slowly walks into the light too. The Light suddenly falls from around us and we stand in what appears to be a park. The birds can be heard singing in the trees about us, marvellous trees of great beauty they are, and long streams lined with small rocks and stones can be found too, their glimmering waters shimmering in the hot afternoon sun of the heavenly realm. In the distance, already half way towards the Godly council’s temple Master Horus waves towards Pharaoh.] Horus: Come, quickly, we are quite late as it is! The Pharaoh: Well you don’t have to carry Isiy on your back do you? [Pharaoh, now struggling to walk up a small ravine in the ground because of the additional weight of his wife, is laughed and mocked by Horus.] Horus: Come on, Titu…we haven’t got all day you know! [Horus chuckles.] The Pharaoh: Yes, yes, as you keep saying…but what you fail to realize is that you’re not the one who for one thing is carrying your wife over your shoulder up a small hill, and is secondly about to lose his Godhood! [Horus sympathetically smiles at Pharaoh.] Horus: I’m sorry, my pupil…I guess this is a big thing for you… “A big thing? Ha! Horus, this was the biggest ‘thing’ to happen in my entire immortal life! Even bigger than getting a chance to once again defeat Xavier Kannon on this weeks up and coming Tuesday turmoil! The Gods know how I despised that man! The PCW Continental champion! Ha, what a joke! What the world seems to have forgotten is the interesting fact that Xavier has been defeated by my former best friend and bodyguard, Carmoon, and me! I’d make sure Kannon knew all about The Pharaoh once again after I beat him on Turmoil!” The Pharaoh: It’s quite all right, Master Horus… [Pharaoh reaches the top of the small hill and there directly before the three currently godly being, and I mean currently because Pharaoh is about to have his Godhood taken away from him, is the temple of the godly council. A huge white place of prayer it is, and home to all the most significant Gods. Even Master Horus once held a position there, but that was a long time ago…the Pharaoh, carrying the still unmoved Isiy, and Horus walk swiftly towards the giant-sized temple, a look of deep thought on Pharaoh’s face…] The Pharaoh: What happens to me after I’ve been dethroned as a God? Horus: You will simply be banished from entering these plains again, my young friend… The Pharaoh: I see. What about you? And what about Isiy? Does she have her Goddess-hood taken from her? And are you allowed to stay on earth and help with me in my training? [Horus sighs.] Horus: Only the Gods can decide that I’m afraid… [The gates of the temple are finally reached and without further stalling Horus pushes them open with ease. Their golden hinges make no sound as the golden gates are opened up before the three immortals. Pharaoh takes a gasp as the interior of the temple is given away to those who stand before it. A place of splendid magnificence; a white and grey, and black marble floor lines the ground, no walls are to be found here, only colossal pillars and somewhat one hundred metres away from them sits the council.] “What greatness this place filled my heart with. It was a place of brilliance; and beauty flowed from its very interior…what a shadow it cast down upon my palace back in Cairo…” ‘Won’t you join us?’ bellows one of them in a thunderous voice… [Pharaoh and Horus enter cautiously as the scene fades to black.] To be continued…
