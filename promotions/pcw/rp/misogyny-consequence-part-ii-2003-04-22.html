Misogyny Consequence (Part II)

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/22/03 One of many new additions to the PCW cluster naps silently, inhaling and exhaling peacefully but within his mind actively runs a bedlam of thoughts. In HIS MindMisogynist versus Misogyny Annihilator Stone Burns AKA. The Misogynist is flat on his back against a wooden shelf, in a James Bond-like tux and a bowie knife while being in a colloquy standoff with the Misogyny Annihilator AKA. Shane Wethers, clad in ivory colored armor gear and in firm grip of a semi-automatic pistol, readying to depress it. The depth of their division was very close as the Misogynist hesitated to take a step, as it might be the last one. Finally, he valorously stepped forward only to be deterred by a single round from the semi-automatic pistol reluctantly fired by the Annihilator. 'You're going to die one way or another, you might as well surrender.' the confident Wethers shouted as Stone stood there, his pair of legs trembling as he knew the Misogyny Annihilator was not a dilettante marksman and would definitely take him out with his accuracy. But being the far from frightful, blade wielding maniac, he educed his bowie knife before him and bellowed.. 'You're wrong!' Dividing them were a line of dull shelves, Stone backed up on one as Shane stuck on to one. The only source of light came from bulbs on the verge of dying out and darkening the room more and more. The room seemed to be caved in as that area was the only area that was fit for movement. Stone went on forward unseen and Shane did the same but adding words with It.. 'I am getting closer and closer.. And the ends of your days are getting closer and closer.' He presumptuously added as Stone just pondered longer and tried to listen for audible footsteps. The Misogynist Annihilator effused a few more rounds from his weapon, trying to fright Stone but failing. He advanced once again unseen, and now near his adversary, just separated by three shelves. Stone pondered as Shane advanced, now only two shelves away but Stone leaped up, and forcefully shoved his sheltering shelve, toppling and hitting the other in front of it creating a domino effect, and it reached to where Wethers was positioned and buried him in between the collapse. Stone proximately scrambled two shelves onward with a cocky gait, cosseting his bowie knife and grinning widely. He stood to where the Misogynist Annihilator was placed but.. Nothing. Shane wasn't there; he kneeled down and looked much more thoroughly through the formed hole but nothing. He stood up and looked in to the depthless room but not another human figure in sight. But behind him stood Shane, semi-automatic pistol cocked firmly between the grip of both his palms. He called out lightly.. 'OH.. STONE.' Stone slowly emerged to the back as Shane fired but missed. The Misogynist brought his blade/knife forward but was unsuccessful but all it took was another shot to put Stone to the ground. The bullet ripped through his chest, instantly spewing blood. Shane came over Stone aimed his pistol and ended.. 'You've just reached your Turmoil!' And one stray bullet came out in to his head, accompanied with an uproarious sound, instantaneously killing him. Out of HIS Mind Stone abruptly sat up, perspiration running down the far side of his cheeks. He shook his head, getting out of the daze. He looked to the side of him where the sheath sheltering his blade rested and he smirked sadistically and nodded, continuing his quiescent nap just before his debut on PCW's event, Tuesday Turmoil up against his opposition, Shane Wethers.
