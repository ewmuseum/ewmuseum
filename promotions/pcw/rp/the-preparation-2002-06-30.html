The preparation

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/30/2002 ~The moment of truth is upon us, my friends. After all of the hype, all of the build up, all of the trash talking, all of the sneak attacks, and everything else is over. Tonight is the night when everything stops, and the time to put up or shut up is here. Tonight, two great competitors will enter the ring and only one will walk out. One man is a man who aligns himself with hooligans hired by an evil CEO of PCW. This same man prides himself on being able to create controversy. This man will stop at nothing to win. This man is 'Controversial' Corey Williams. The other man is the best of the OLD SCHOOL PCW. He is man who's back PCW was built upon. Unlike other so-called PCW OLD SCHOOLERS, this man stayed with PCW through thick and thin, gradually establishing himself at the top of PCW and gaining respect from any one who calls himself a PCW fan. This man is Jon Kano. These two will bleed. These two will sweat. These two will nearly kill each other. The only way to win is to incapacitate your opponent to the point where he can't get up. The only question is who is going to prevail in this match pitting New School versus Old School?~ *The scene opens up in the Malice Crew locker room. The only thing is that the only two members of the Malice Crew in the room are Duke Williams and Jon Kano. Duke is dressed in his trademark Texas-western attire, complete with ten gallon cowboy hat and comboy boots. Jon Kano is half dressed in his ring attire. Both men are facing each other on stools as Kano starts to lace up his boots.* Duke: Why are you getting dressed already? You usually don't get dressed until about half way down the aisle. Kano: Because I have to be a step ahead of Corey. Duke: Even getting dressed? Kano: Every little bit helps. I am more ready for this match tonight than I have been for any other match in a long time. I have studied his move set and I think I have a counter for just about everything in his play book. If he is going to win this match, he is going to have to pull something out of his ass that no one expects. Duke: I still think you are going overboard. Kano: I don't. Duke for once, I don't think you understand me. Duke: Say what? Kano: I am old school. Duke: So am I. Kano: Yeah, but I am in the really old school. When I first came here, everyone said I was just some big goofy bastard that would never amount to anything in PCW. I proved them wrong by setting PCW on fire. Now I got people saying I am over the hill and that I lost what I had that made me great. Do you know what I am going to do tonight? Duke: What? Kano: I am going to prove them wrong again. I must make an example out of CCW. I must ROCK his world. He will stop at nothing, and I am the same way. Rumor has it that whoever wins this match will be in line for a Continental Title shot. Hmmmm, I'd like to get my hands on that title again. I will do whatever it takes to beat Corey tonight, my career depends on it. Tonight, will be two things. First, I will reclaim my greatness. Second, it will be the beginning of a new era of Kano dominance in PCW. And here is the best thing, Duke. Tyler can't do anything to stop me. Not Tyler, not Kannon, not TPB, not even God himself can stop me once I start on this reign of terror over PCW. Duke: Jon, I know I shouldn't say this as a fellow member of the Malice Crew and a friend of yours, but I think you are taking this a little too seriously. Kano: You just proved my point. I am sitting here, 26 years old, being told that I am over the hill. I hear them say that and I begin to think about my career in PCW. I have beaten the best. I beat Kriegman twice, Nelson twice, Boda, I ended Walden's career, I gave PCW to TPB after I kidnapped PHD. But I have also lost to people I shouldn't have ever lost to. Duke: We all do. Kano: I jobbed to the Sexual Harassment Panda!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Duke: You had two cracked vertabrae in your neck. Kano: But I still shouldn't have lost. But that is all in the past, and I live in the present. The present is tonight. And after tonight, my future is bright. I must capitalize on this opportunity. If I don't, then maybe I am over the hill. Duke: Nah. Jon, regardless of what happens tonight, you still are my friend and a living legend in PCW. But quickly, lets go over a little strategy for tonight, it will put you at ease. Kano: Maybe you're right. Duke: Go over the match. Kano: First, I'll walk down the aisle, cheers and chants coming from the crowd, nearly blowing the roof off the arena. We'll lock up and proceed to kick the hell out of each other. He'll hurt me, I'll hurt him. He'll make me bleed, I'll make him bleed. And then he'll make the mistake. I'll hook him into position and Kanoplex him to hell and back. He'll lay in the center of the ring, flat on his back, nearly unconscience. I'll be standing over him as the ref counts the ten. And then and only then will Corey learn that I AM WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *The scene fades to black as Kano and Duke nod at each other, agreeing to the scenario Kano just laid out.* ~Who's gonna win? Williams or Kano? Tune in PCW Gold and Glory this Sunday to find out.~
