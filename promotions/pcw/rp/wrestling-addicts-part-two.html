Wrestling Addicts (Part Two)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12 Jan &#039;02 Last time we saw Chet King in a promo which was for Chet, he was in the FUCKS of America building, attending a wrestling addicts meeting, in the hopes of curing some peoples addictions to wrestling. This is where we rejoin Chet, in the wrestling addicts meeting, with the meetings leader Dr Roundstone. Previously he was sitting around with the entire group, this time he’s sitting around with just four people while Dr Roundstone is attending to the rest of the group over the other side of the room. Chet King: Dr Roundstone has told me that I may be able to help you guys cure your addiction if I get to know you personally. How about you tell me about yourself, and how you became addicted to wrestling? Chet points at a male teenager who looks about 15. He’s wearing a PCW t-shirt, wears glasses and is a pretty skinny build. 15yr Old: Hi Chet, can I just say it’s an honor to be talking to you today. Chet King: Yeah well I can imagine that you wrestling addicts have wet dreams about a wrestler like myself coming to talk to you, but lets just talk about you. What’s your name? 15yr Old: My name’s Bobby, Bobby Brown. Chet King: Alright BB, when’d you first watch wrestling? Bobby Brown: Did you just call me BB? I have wet dreams about wrestlers calling me by a nickname! Chet King: Heh, well that’s what I said befo…wait a minute…eww! Bobby Brown: My doctor says having wet dreams is normal. Chet King: Not when it comes to dreaming about me. I’ll try to help you, just don’t get too excited while I’m here, BB…wait, I shouldn’t call you that, I’ll just call you Bobby! Bobby Brown: Ok, but I don’t mind if you call me BB. Chet King: I’ll be fine thanks. Why don’t you just tell us about when you first saw wrestling and how you became addicted. Bobby Brown: The first time I saw wrestling would have been around August 2001. I was just flicking through channels, and then I went onto a PCW show and do you know what I saw? I saw Tim Walden! He was wearing his wrestling gear, and from that minute on I knew what I wanted to do with my life. I wanted to become Tim Walden in every way, from his manly good looks to his superb intelligence. Chet King: You’re not just obsessed with wrestling, you’re obsessed with Walden, aren’t you? Bobby Brown: If covering my walls with posters of him, having hundreds of action figures of him, spending my free time drawing pictures of him and making toys that looks like him, taking photographs of my TV every time he comes on it and dressing like him, then yes I am! Chet King: Man, you’ve got issues. Are you obsessed with Walden because you want to be like him, or because you’re attracted to him? Either way you’ve got serious problems. Bobby Brown: Because I want to be like him. But I do have the occasional wet dream about him, but like I said before, I do that over most wrestlers especially if they call me by nicknames! Chet seems to be pretty scared. Chet King: Look, I’ll tell you what. I’ll cure you’re obsession with Walden on Turmoil when I meet him in a four on four tag match. I’ll beat him so badly that you won’t want to look like him any more. As for your wet dream problem, go talk to Val Venis about how to get some ‘Choppy, Choppy!’, because that’s the only way I can see you curing that problem. Bobby Brown: Thanks Chet! Chet King: No problem, I’m just doing what I can to help you weirdo’s. Chet points to a large twenty something year old man sitting next to Bobby Brown. Chet King: What’s your story? 20Something Yr Old: My name’s Rick Comboy. I started watching wrestling back in the late 80’s when there was classic matches such as generic masked jobber #55 versus Hulk Hogan and generic masked jobber #112 versus Ultimate Warrior. Since then I’ve become obsessed with wrestling, never going a week without watching at least fifteen hours of it whether it be live shows or tapes of generic masked jobber #272 versus Randy Savage. Over the last two years I’ve turned my love of wrestling into a love of PCW with it being the main wrestling show which I watch. I don’t think I’ve missed a single show in the history of PCW. Chet King: Really? So you’ve seen every one of my matches? Rick Comboy: I guess I have. Chet King: What do you think of me, as a wrestler that is? Rick Comboy: You have talent, although lately you’ve been on and off. You did well in defeating TTT in a non title match, but the next week you blew it by losing to Statuz Quo of all people! Chet King: Yeah, that was a pretty poor effort on my behalf, but I don’t think I’ll ever be doing a job of that magnitude again. Rick Comboy: Let’s hope not. Chet King: You’re not addicted to anyone in particular like this kid next to you, are you? You’re just addicted to wrestling in general? Rick Comboy: Pretty much. I just watch way too much wrestling. I watch all PCW shows, plus the replays of the shows. Then I watch tapes of old PCW shows and pay per views. Then I watch OSW as well and I also watch tapes of 80’s wrestling classics such as generic masked jobber #398 versus Ricky Steamboat. Chet King: The only way I can see you curing this is by meeting up with someone like Totally Tubular Tommy. Have a chat with him for about an hour or so, and after listening to him and his stupidity you’ll probably never want to look at another wrestler again, for fear that they may insult your intelligence like TTT. Rick Comboy: You think that will work? Chet King: I know it will. Rick Comboy: How do I meet up with TTT. Chet King: Just tell him you’ve got a ounce of weed, and he can have as much as he wants for free. Rick Comboy: Thanks Chet! Chet King: No problem. Now I’m sorry to the other two of you, but you’ll have to wait until the next time I do a promo to have me solve your problems, as you see I’m only doing these meetings when I do promos. Not only does this mean that you two will have to wait, but my other opponents for this week, Dante, Gaz and RAH will also have to wait until next time for some insults thrown their way. Heck, Dante should just watch the replay of Turmoil if he wants his dose of insults. As Chet continues to ramble to himself, we leave the scene, but of course we’ll be back next time.
