What Do You Have Planned?

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 26th, 2003 Skylar Green sits on the sundeck, sunbathing as it flies through the bright sky toward its next destination, Friday Fusion. He has in his brown synthetic eyes, and his muscular body shows as he has on a pair of dark blue Polo swim trunks, resting his head on a yellow towel. Tania steps out of the pool and sits next to him on a lounge chair, drying herself off. She has on a pink two-piece bathing suit which looks darker from the water, but is rather thick and doesn't shrink around her figure for being wet. She smiles as she thinks about her sisters. 'How far along do you think they are now?' Tania refers to Rush Mason and Isiy Xansiv, who have left to return to the United States to put all the necessary documentation in for the return of Rafaela and Ines Garcia to Skylar. His arms folded behind his head, Skylar is in a peaceful resting moment though still awake. His eyes are closed and they stay that way as he answers Tania's question. 'I'd say that they're in Florida now. They should at least have an attorney by now. They really can't lose, someone will represent them. All they need is to submit the contract and the appropriate change of legal guardianship forms that I had Isiy attach to the contract that Titu signed, and by Saturday, they should be back with us.' Tania nods her head. 'Will you... we still have to deal with this Pharaoh?' 'Most likely,' Skylar says. 'By Tuesday, the revelation of what he signed will dawn on him and he'll come knocking at the door looking for payback.' Tania takes out some generic SPF-45 waterproof sunscreen and rubs it on her arms and legs. 'The contract that you had Pharaoh sign; he says that it was for you to lose the PCW World Title to him on July 1st. That's what he says, anyway. Are you going to do that?' Skylar smirks. 'I doubt I'll have that chance, but I'm a man of my word. If July 1st comes around and I have the PCW World Title in my possession, I shall willingly and purposefully lose it to him.' 'But, what about your match tomorrow? Against... Dorn Tracer? You say that you doubt that you'll have the chance to lose the PCW World Title to Pharaoh. Are you losing it Friday?' Skylar shakes his head with a smile. 'No, no, no. At least not intentionally. Dorn's my opponent, so very well. I'll do my best to keep it.' 'Then why don't you think that you'll have the World Title by Tuesday?' Skylar's smile leads to a laugh. Tobey McGuire and Ben Affleck run through the pool, making sounds as though they were Spiderman and Daredevil, respectively. Samuel L. Jackson cannot help but to laugh at them. 'Who said anything about Tuesday?' Skylar scratches the right side of his nose as he chuckles. 'That contract said that I'd lose to The Pharaoh if I had the PCW World Title in my possession by July 1st.' Tania tilts her head to the left, furrowing her brow. 'July 1st is the first Tuesday of next month. It's the date of Turmoil. You do know that, don't you?' Skylar quickly sits up in place, slapping himself in the forehead with his left palm. 'Oh, crap, I forgot!' 'What? What's wrong?' Tania worries about the plan that Skylar and his associates have thrown together, and whether or not it will fall apart. It's gone well so far, to have it fail now is unacceptable to Tania. Skylar waves his right hand and casually lies back on the blue-banded lounge chair, laughing. 'Nothing. Don't worry, everything's planned out. I took the date into consideration. Trust me, I know what I'm doing.' 'I hope so.' Tania then adopts an inquisitive look. 'What did you do?' Skylar shakes his head. 'Nope, I can't tell you. You've been involved in this enough, to let you know anymore would make you a target, and I don't want that to happen.' Tania sighs. 'Okay. Just get them back, okay?' 'I will,' Skylar turns his head toward her and nods, giving her his vow to return the smaller children and reunite the family. 'I'm going to get dressed, then get something to eat. Are you alright here?' Tania looks over to the west corner of the sundeck, checking out some 14 and 15-year-old boys. 'I'm good, yes.' Recognizing the slight change of inflection in Tania's voice when she spots cute boys, Skylar stands up and turns away, saying, 'Don't do anything you wouldn't want your sisters doing.' knowing that it always works. 'Alright,' she says, defeated, as Skylar leaves her to return to his room.
