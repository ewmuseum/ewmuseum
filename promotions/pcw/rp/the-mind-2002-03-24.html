The Mind

24 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/24/02 *Monologue* The human mind. An element or complex of elements in an individual that feels, perceives, thinks, wills, and reasons. Big Daveylicious has his reasons to think he’s great. He’s won the FSW World Heavyweight champion. That boosted that ego that made him realize he was actually good. That made him calm and relaxed. But he hasn’t been truly relaxed the past 5 or 6 months of not winning any PCW gold. He’s been frustrated after being screwed every damn time fighting for gold. Finally, this Sunday he gets a shot at the Extreme title against Jason Core. Jason Core. A man that won the title after being here a month from a man or if you can call him that. But he is known as Karl LeBlanc. Who beaten Core before. Has Jason Core beaten anybody worthy? No. He only fights Karl LeBlanc on a daily basis to stack wins on his record. If Karl LeBlanc were actually good than I’d actually call Jason Core a true champion. But the fact is LeBlanc should go to another federation that suits his “unique talent”. Ah, hell with it. He sucks! Core puts the “or” in jobbing whore for LeBlanc. I mean it’s lackluster already. But finally, Big Daveylicious gets his shot at the title. A worthy competitor to fight the likes of Jason Core. A man that Core has faced and that’s the kinda man that is actually GOOD. Core may think he gots this in the bag but the keyword there is “think”. The human mind thinks. Doesn’t know all. So how can Core actually think he is going to beat Big Daveylicious when he only is thinking? (The scene fades into a pair of closed eyes. A light passes the closed eyes and the eyes of this person open. We zoom back to find only the body of Big Daveylicious in one of the thirty beds in the dormitory room of the pysch ward. We can hear the breathing, occasional moans, and the snores just like the crowd during a Jason Core vs. Karl LeBlanc match the millionth time. We see where the light came from when we see the orderly carrying a flashlight with a nurse making their rounds, checking each bed. Big D‘s eyes are just adjusting to the dark. He just waits until they exit. He turns to see a patch of moonlight coming from a barred window. He takes a quick glance at the other patients in their beds. He quietly sits himself up in his bed and climbs out. He scratches his head.) Big Daveylicious: Where am I? (He moves closer to the barred windows. He looks out to see the moon. He stares wondering.) Big Daveylicious: Am I in prison again? Damn, that south of the border prostitute from Tijuana! Damn her to... Voice: Nope, sonny this isn’t prison. (Big D startled turns his head to find the voice of an old man in a bed next to him.)Old Man: You can’t remove the grill. It’s welded, sonny. (Big D ignores the old man and checks the grill anyway. The old man was right. Big D turns to the old man.) Big Daveylicious: Who in the hell are you? Old Man: I’m Saint Nick. Big Daveylicious: Santa Claus? (The old man nods and sits up in his bed. Big D looks at the man and he is bald skinny old wrinkly man.) Big Daveylicious: But isn’t Jolly Ol’ Saint Nick supposed to be a jolly ol’ fat man? Old Man: I used to be fat but the damn orderlies kept raping me each time I wanted a glass of milk and chocolate chip cookies. All I wanted was some chocolate chip cookies but nooooooo I get it up the... Big Daveylicious: Ugh. Orderlies raped you? Old Man: Yes, each time painful. All they said if I didn’t say anything I’d get the cookies. But instead they gave me unwanted nookie. You know damn sure they’re getting lumps of coal in their stockings for Christmas. Big Daveylicious: I need to get home. Lord only knows what’s going on. Graves gots no life as it is. (The scene quickly fades into Big D’s living room. People are partying in his house. Various beer bottles are on the ground. So are the drunks. Big D’s lackey, Michael Graves can be seen jumping up and down gleefully.) Michael Graves: PARTY! (The scene fades back to the dormitory with Big D on looking the moon.) Big Daveylicious: Graves, gots to be helpless and misses me. Poor guy stuck in a world without me. Old Man: Why don’t you sit on my lap? (Big D looks at the old man odd.) Big Daveylicious: I’d rather not.Old Man: Why don’t you sit on Santa’s lap like a good little boy and tell me what you want for Christmas. Big Daveylicious: I’ll sit next to you. (Big D awkwardly walks to the old man and sits next to him on the old man’s bed.) Old Man: Tell Santa what you want for Christmas. Big Daveylicious: I don’t want to wait ‘till Christmas. I want it now! That is the Extreme title. Old Man: Damn, you youngin’s always wanting. Never giving back. I give these orderlies presents when they were children and what do I get back? I get it up the chimney. Big Daveylicious: That’s not totally true. Old Man: It’s not true?! You want to see my bleeding bum hole?! Big Daveylicious: No thanks. Old Man: WANNA SEE MY BUM HOLE! (The old man keeps reaching out to pull his pants down. Big D is getting disgusted with this and jumps to his bed.) Big Daveylicious: Shut up. I got to get out of here. Old Man: That’s no way to talk to your elders, sonny. But instead of putting a lump of coal in your stocking, you’ll get a lump of SH*T IN YOUR STOCKING! Big Daveylicious: SHUT UPPPPPPPPP! (Other patients are waking up as the NURSE and two ORDERLIES burst into thedorm and head straight for Big D who's struggling to calm himself.) Big Daveylicious: I’m sorry but I got a bit agitated after he told me that you raped him. Orderly: Him? Big Daveylicious: YOUR QUESTIONING WHO YOU RAPED?! YOU RAPED SANTA CLAUS! (The orderlies grab him out of his bed But Big D struggles to fight them off.But they finally get a hold of him.) Big Daveylicious: WHAT ARE YOU IDIOTS DOING?! I’m a MENTAL PATIENT! I’m supposed to act up! (The nurse takes out a needle and pierces the needle into the skin of Big Daveylicicous. A bit wheezy he struggles enough to say one more thing.) Big Daveylicious: AWWWWWWWWW.... that some good sh*t. (With that the scene fades to black.)
