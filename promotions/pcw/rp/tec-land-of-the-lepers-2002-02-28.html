TEC: Land of the Lepers

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/28/02 The forest is dark and dense and dry. Captain Planet put the fire out recently, but there is still a smell of smoke and ash and embers. The canopy has some holes in it where the fire reached, but overall it is still very dark because of the late hour. Richard has recovered enough from Smokey the Bear’s brief but vicious mauling, and is walking between Slade and Drek. Drek, who used to be holding a solar-powered flashlight that didn’t work because there was no solar energy stored in it, tossed away the useless plastic a few miles back. The Misfits are following some slow, methodic vibrations in the ground, which coincide with Smokey the Bear’s directions. The Misfits are close to their destination: the Land of the Lepers. Handler:“It’s taken us a while to get here; it’s taken the Misfits a while to get here. I’d like to go back and do a quick recap of what happened leading up to this point. “It started out with Lamar getting deathly ill after The End left the house for the weekend to deal with his hectic work schedule; he had a match on Friday Fusion against THE Multiple Man to get into the Main Event Rumble at March Madness, a match that The End won with some help from Drake Maxwell. “Next, The End had a match early Saturday morning against Nick Extreme of the WWA to advance to the second of the Global Title Tournament, a match won by the mere fact that Nick Extreme was so afraid of facing The End that he chose to no-show the scheduled match-up (he was on vacation from his own fed, so he didn’t have anything else to attend to). “Last, but not least, The End had another match Sunday teaming with Drake Maxwell to face Xavier Kannon and Bobby Stormbringer in a No-Holds Barred tag match. The End’s team won the match after a technical masterpiece of a match. “Three wins in three days, and as a reward from PCW, rather than fighting a replacement challenger for the retired Asa Fountain (by the way, this RP is dedicated to Asa Fountain, my former opponent for Friday Fusion). The End has spent his time in seclusion, training for his match at the PPV. Of course, The End must face Drake Maxwell in the second round of the GTT. “I mention this about The End because he kept questioning me as a handler. I’d be going into the Misfits’ story, and he’d whine and complain about not being mentioned or used. YOU HAPPY, YOU TALL, BLIND FREAK?” The End:“Yes. Very much so.” Handler:“Geez.” Handler:“Anyway, back to the Misfits. They must go to the Land of the Lepers to get medicine to cure Lamar’s illness. To do that, they needed money, so all of the Misfits got jobs. Richard worked as an ice cream man and made $50. Drek worked as a photographer and lost $50 (in addition to his photo shoot resulting in a giant millipede eating two babies and the man that was supposed to buy the pictures Drek was taking). Slade worked as an auctioneer, and earned $300, though one of the $100 bills was a counterfeit bill. “The Misfits mailed themselves via UPS to the Land of the Lepers. The pilots, drunk of their asses, dumped the box without landing, as well as throwing out the adorable Bobo the Albino Iguana. The Misfits landed in the forest, and soon Richard started a fire. Captain Planet put out the fire, and an irate Smokey the Bear took them back to his cabin, where he yelled at the Misfits and mauled Richard. Soon, though, the Misfits were back in the forest, walking toward the Land of the Lepers. “Well, that concludes the recap, so I’m not going to talk directly to you anymore, and get back to the normal format of this RP.” The End:“Me too.” Handler:“SHUT UP!” Drek:“Man, the handler’s making us walk a good distance. Are we out of shape in his mind or something?” Richard:“We got quite a bit further along the path with the handler making that long, pointless spiel about The End.” Slade:“Um… wait, the attention’s back on us now, isn’t it?” Drek:“Freggin’ A….” Slade:“This might be a good time to get back in character.” Richard:“I agree.” {Commercial: This RP is brought to you by rebellious dumbass characters. Let them get out of line, and there’s almost no salvaging the story… okay, they’re in character now. We, or rather I, return you to the regularly scheduled RP, already in progress.} Finally, the Misfits come to a clearing, where there is torchlight and music. Some chanting/singing of tribal sound is coming from the clearing. The Misfits move beyond the edge of the trees, and come upon the Land of the Lepers. It is a very large village of small straw huts, and some larger wooden buildings shaped like Native American longhouses. The starry night and full moon shines some light upon the village, but most of the light comes from the illuminating glow of a large bonfire that rises above the tallest buildings in the village, focused in the center of the city. The houses themselves are run-down, terribly maintained sties. Holes ununiformly dot the architecture of these straw, wood, and adobe homes. There is an almost overbearing smell of urine and feces. Scraps of food lie around on the ground. All the animals for livestock wallow in their own filth in a makeshift pen built to the right of the city. The Misfits emerge completely from the brush and survey the Land of the Lepers. “This place is a sty,” Drek exclaims. “How they manage to survive here is beyond me.” Richard, blood still dripping from his cuts received during the mauling by Smokey the Bear, speaks despite his woozy condition. “Richard agrees,” he says, “Richard has never seen a worse sight.” Slade nods in agreement, saying, “Quixl pix tjekncs.” “Well put, Slade,” Richard says with a friendly pat on Slade’s shoulder. “Well said.” The Misfits move into the village. Through the holes, they peep into the huts. All empty. At the center of the city, there is much noise. All the people must be there. The Misfits begin searching the huts, looking for something that could resemble medicine to give to Lamar. After a 30-min search, nothing is uncovered and the Misfits are no closer to completing their mission. The Misfits, having no other option (I made it so; I CONTROL THEIR FATE!), go to the ruckus at the center of the city. The streets are wide, and the Misfits have no problem navigating them. Must be so lepers won’t bump into each other and knock off each other’s limbs. In the center of the city, there are tribal musicians beating drums with their hands. Everyone is passing around wooden goblets of some mysterious purple substance and passing it around. The Misfits slip into the festivities. The goblet is passed, and they all take big swigs of the viscous liquid. Blood, ugh. Everything is going well for the Misfits, who begin searching for a doctor and learn that the Lepers can understand Slade, until the ceremony changes to a point where everyone tears off a limb and passes it to their neighbor. The Misfits just pretend to try to pull of limbs. Slade pulls hard on the arms of Drek and Richard. They get pulled off balance, and fall to the ground. Slade points at his two friends. Slade “Auskjn oasdh oadun asd aweu hzoxc vnzkjrn!”(Translation: They are imposters! They’re not lepers! Get them!” The lepers all eye Richard and Drek with something of a bloodlust. They raise their eyes to the sky and yell loudly. They begin to tear off arms and legs, and literally throw punches and kicks at Richard and Drek. The two Misfits, betrayed by Slade, can only get pummeled to unconsciousness… {Time Compression: 1 hour later} Richard and Drek awake locked inside of a cage made of extremely hard, dense wood. They are inside a small hut with a contained fire burning in the corner to provide light. Drek is sitting Indian-style on the floor by the time Richard gets up. He rubs his head from the beating he received earlier. “Richard can still feel the hits,” he says in a little painful manner. “Yeah,” Drek affirms without looking back at Richard. “They can throw punches with the best of ‘em.” Richard glances around the hut. “They did not get Slade?” he asks. “Nah,” Drek says, “it doesn’t look like it. Everything is alright, though.” “If by that, you mean that it was our intention to come and get captured by a band of rogues and put in a cage so we can’t get Lamar’s medicine, then Richard agrees, everything is just peachy.” Drek turns back to Richard, a little agitated by the negative tone taken by the scruffy-bearded Misfit. “Slade’s still out there,” says Drek. “He’ll get us out.” Richard remarks snidely, “Wasn’t Slade the one that yelled, ‘GET THEM!’ rather out of the blue?” “I’m sure there was a reason for that. Slade had some plan. I hope.” “You are not filling Richard with confidence.” Handler:“On this note, we’ll fade out. Join us next time for the conclusion to the Misfits RP Series – Lamar’s Sick!”
