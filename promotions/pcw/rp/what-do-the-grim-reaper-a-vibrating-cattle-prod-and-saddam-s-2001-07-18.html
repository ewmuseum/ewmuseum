What do the Grim Reaper, A vibrating Cattle prod, and Saddam's Weapon Shack have in common?!?!

18 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/7/01 When we last left off…..Flaymin had just been injected by the surgeons with a lethal amount of tranquilliser.While he drifted off to sleep- his spirit rose.The soul of Flaymin was encountered by the Grim Reaper who took Flaymin’s hand and showed him what life would be like if Flaymin was never born- waaaay better! But the stumbling, fumbling Grim Reaper was unable to complete his task and rip Flaymin from the womb of the world and into the afterlife.Having failed miserably, the Grim Reaper broke down and began to cry.His evilness apparently sucks. But never fear- Flaymin is gonna help The Grim Reaper get his groove back…But how?And at what price? [Scene opens where we last left off….. [The Grim Reaper breaks down and begins to cry] Grim Reaper: *Sob* I try to be intimidating.I’ve seen all of the movies in the Freddy Kruger series, but nothing seems to work.I’m hopeless at being a bad guy.I’m this close from being on the dole! [The Grim Reaper continues to cry and wipe the tears away from his eyes. Flaymin begins to feel uncomfortable.He reaches over and hesitantly pats the Grim Reaper on the back] Flaymin: There, there.Its not the end of the world.Life goes on…. No wait, you’re supposed to put a halt to that, too. Grim Reaper: And I can’t even do that right!!! [Flaymin strokes his chin in an attempt to come up with an idea] Flaymin: Hmmm…. I think I may be able to help you. [The Grim Reaper looks up at Flaymin with a glint of hope in his eyes] Grim Reaper: How? Flaymin: Never mind that.But you have to do something for me in exchange for my help. Grim Reaper: You want my Skeletor costume? Flaymin: No I don’t…. wait actually…. Yeah ok that sounds good.But there is also something else I ask for. Grim Reaper: Name it. Flaymin: If I help you get back into shape, than you must release me and allow me to continue my journey through the world. Grim Reaper: In other words, you want me to release you from your looming death? Flaymin: Exactly.The afterlife can wait.My duties on Earth aren’t complete. [The Grim Reaper nods his head in approval] Grim Reaper: Ok, that sounds like a fair deal. Flaymin: But how do I know I can trust you?You’re the Grim Reaper for crying out loud! Grim Reaper: Its not like your dealing with the devil…I’m just the next best thing! Flaymin: How… reassuring. Grim Reaper: Trust me.I swear on your life that nothing will happen. Flaymin: That’s better! Right, now how shall I transform The Grim Reaper into a mean, ass-kicking, life-taking, mighty machine? [Flaymin strokes his chin as he considers the several different options available.Suddenly he clicks his fingers together] Flaymin: Got it! Grim Reaper: Is it the costume?I hate this piece of crap…. especially because it has to be hand washed all the time. [Flaymin gently strokes the black, hooded cloak of the Grim Reaper] Flaymin: Is this polyester? Grim Reaper: Cotton. Flaymin: Ugh, that’s even worse.Tell me something- what do Superman, Batman and Spiderman all have in common? Grim Reaper: Extraordinarily unoriginal names? Flaymin: Uhhh… besides that. Grim Reaper: They’re dressed like homo’s? Flaymin: Close enough.The answer is they all wear spandex with a hint of lycra! Grim Reaper: Once again- they look like homo’s. Flaymin: You’ve got it all wrong.That tight, bright coloured, shiny stuff… actually… does…. make them look like homo’s….Hmmm, that ain’t good. Grim Reaper: I’ve got a respectable job here.I can’t walk around dressed like a poof and expect people to fear me! Flaymin: Hey, it works for The Phantom Booker!He’s got the whole pantyhose thing going for him. Grim Reaper: Now that you mention it, I do kind of have nice legs. [Flaymin reaches down and feels the legs of The Grim Reaper] Flaymin: Wow, they’re smooth too.What kind of wax do you use? Grim Reaper: None.I just get soulless fools to lick it off. Flaymin: Wow, I need to get me one of those. Um… but back to the costume, I think we should just keep this one for now.Maybe we can get Versace to design something for you. Grim Reaper: Nice. Flaymin: The most crucial aspect of your moniker that lets you down is your weapon of choice. Grim Reaper: The Scythe!?!?!?I’ve had that for an eternity. Flaymin: Which is why we need something new. I mean, just look at that stupid thing!Are you here to take people to the afterlife or to cut their wheat? Grim Reaper: Can’t I do both? Flaymin: NO!You look a hillbilly farmer with that stupid scythe. And even farmers don’t use scythes to cut the wheat anymore.Rocket Scientists invented lawn mowers years ago! Grim Reaper: But… Flaymin: No buts about it.We need to get you a new weapon. Do you know of any Weapon Shopping malls? Grim Reaper: Sure do.I’m a shareholder at ‘Saddam’s Weapon Shack’. [The Grim Reaper grabs Flaymin’s hand.He then taps his toes together three times and… WHOOSHKA!! Flaymin and The Grim Reaper disappear momentarily only to reappear with a sudden flash a second later in front of the revolving door of ‘Saddam’s Weapon Shack’. Flaymin and The Grim Reaper walk into the store and begin browsing the aisles] Grim Reaper: Do you think I need a shopping basket? Flaymin: Nah, you’re better off getting a trolley. [The Grim Reaper walks over to the trolley checkout and picks one out.He then rolls the trolley down the first aisle to where Flaymin is browsing the shelves] Grim Reaper: See anything interesting? Flaymin: I like this Cattle Prod.You could just walk around zapping people into the abyss. [Flaymin turns on the cattle prod and begins playing around with it] Zap! Zap! Zap! Flaymin: How cool is this!?!?It would be like zapping bugs. Grim Reaper: Does it do anything else? [Flaymin holds up the Cattle Prod and begins to slowly move it around with his fingers in an attempt to find another use for the weapon] Flaymin: Actually, this thing is about the size of a dildo. Grim Reaper: Which means…. Flaymin: Which means, in your spare time you could just shove it up your ass and zap yourself- like a vibrator! This thing charges a thousand bolts per zap, so you’ll either get the orgasm of your life… or a burnt asshole. Grim Reaper: Wow, I never looked at it like that. Flaymin: Oh sure, my ex-valet, Jenna used to use these things all the time.Except she used to shove it through her mouth and get this gigantic tingle in her throat. She’d be buzzing for hours like a broken walkie-talkie. Grim Reaper: A cattle prod that doubles as a high-powered vibrator….I’ll take it!! [The Grim Reaper takes the cattle prod and puts it in the trolley. Flaymin and the Grim Reaper then walk down the next aisle casually browsing at the different weapons on offer] Grim Reaper: Hey look at this!‘Five Machetes for $10’ Flaymin: Nah, we don’t need to cut anything open. Grim Reaper: How about this AK-47? [The Grim Reaper holds up the rifle and puts his finger on the trigger to check out its aim] Grim Reaper: It looks pretty dandy. Flaymin: Pffft. Now this is something you need- a bazooka! [Flaymin struggles to pick up the bazooka but eventually musters enough strength to put it on his shoulder] Grim Reaper: What would I do with that? Flaymin: Cannonball people to hell. It’d be great. Just like a human cannon. Grim Reaper: All these fancy weapons…I still prefer my good ol’ scythe. Flaymin: Listen here Jebus, you’re not a hillbilly.You need a man’s weapon! Grim Reaper: Like the vibrating cattle prod? Flaymin: Exactly! [Suddenly a sound begins to ring loudly] Flaymin: What’s that? Grim Reaper: My Beeper.Excuse me for a second. [The Grim Reaper takes the Beeper off his belt and looks at it] Grim Reaper: Well it seems your Balls of Steel Operation is almost complete.We have to get your soul back into your body, pronto! [Flaymin breathes a sigh of relief] Flaymin: You mean, you’re going to let me live? Grim Reaper: Yeah… I’m really starting to like this cattle prod! [Once again, The Grim Reaper grabs a hold of Flaymin’s hand.He taps his toes three times and then WHOOSHKA!!!The two men disappear. A moment later, they both reappear in the operating theatre where the body of Flaymin is currently being operated on] Grim Reaper: Well I guess this is it. Flaymin: Yep, good luck with the whole killing thing. [The Grim Reaper releases his hold of Flaymin’s hand.Like a falling leaf, Flaymin’s soul ever so slowly flutters down to his limp body. A second later, the journey is complete- Flaymin’s soul is back in his body and the Grim Reaper has disappeared. Steadily, Flaymin begins to open his eyes.He blinks a few times until finally he is awake. Flaymin looks around in dizziness only to see the Surgeon staring back at him] Surgeon: Are you ok? Flaymin: Uh… yeah.I was just on a magical journey with the Grim Reaper! [The Surgeon looks at his assistant and frowns] Surgeon: This patient doesn’t also require a brain transplant, does he? [The assistant flips through the progress chart and then shakes her head] Assistant: Nope, just an Eyeballs of steel operation. [Flaymin’s eyes suddenly boggle open and the hairs on his arm creepily stand on end] Flaymin: Eyeballs of steel???? Surgeon: Yep! Flaymin: BUT I F*CKING WANTED BALLS OF STEEL!!!! Surgeon: Uh oh… [Scene fades to black] Yet again, another terrible twist in Flaymin’s endeavour to have a new testicle inserted! But hey, eyeballs of steel are cool too…. Aren’t they?? TUNE IN NEXT TIME TO FIND OUT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
