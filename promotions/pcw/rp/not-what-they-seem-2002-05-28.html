Not what they seem

28 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/28/02 *The scene opens as Ricky and his uncle are leaving the restaurant. They get outside to the parking lot and Ricky stops his uncle* Ricky: Tony. I just wanted to say I had been thinking. While you were eating I decided something. Tony: And what was that you decided Ricky? Ricky: Well thought about what you said and I decided you were right. There is no need no mull over the past. What is done is done. Shane was a great athlete and was repected for it. Now it is my turn to shine as I will be leading the new wave of talent in the PCW. I mean hell, The Phantom Booker personally selected me to be in his new Phantom Phive. The PCW is where I let go of Shane's legacy and start leaving my own. Friday Fusion, I am going to show up for that Schools Out Match, and the only thing that will be out, is me. Out of the building and off the grounds that is. Tony: Ahh, that a boy Ricky. I knew you would come to. Hey I gotta get back to work, let's go. *The duo steps around the side of the building and notices the two thugs that were eyeballing them when they arrived, rummaging through the truck. Tony shouts out at them as one of them takes off running while the other stands there frozen in fear. Ricky and Tony rush to the Blazer as the young man stands there silently. Ricky lunges at the guy as Tony intercepts him, and asks him to wait by the building. Ricky growls at the guy as he takes a step backwards before retreating to the building. Tony reaches out and places his hands on the guys shoulders. As he begins speaking to him the kid remains motionless and silent, only nodding occassionally to acknowledge what is being said. The young mans face is paralyzed in fear, his eyes locked on the man in front of him. Suddenly he jerks backwards and steps away from Tony, there is a quick flash of steel and a loud bang as Tony hits the ground. Ricky's eyes widen as the guy bolts from the scene scaling the chain link fence, and disappearing into the night. Ricky rushes to his uncles side who is lying on the ground clutching his stomach. The red blood is flowing through his fingers as Ricky kneels beside his uncle* Ricky: I'm sorry Tony, you shoulda let me kill him when I started too. Tony: I'll be ok. It's just a flesh wound. Besides, if this had been you laying here, how would you win that match on Fusion? Ricky: This is no time for jokes Tony. I'll be right back, I am going to run inside and call an ambulance. You going to be ok, long enough for me to? Tony: Well, I am going to lie out here and bleed to death if you don't, so I guess I will have to be. *A small smile comes across the face of Tony as Ricky jumps to his feet and rushes inside.* Ricky: Hey Lady!!! I need to use your phone!! My uncles been shot! Waitress: Oh my! Is he ok? Ricky: The phone!!! *The waitres hands him a portable phone, and stands near as he makes the call to 911* Ricky: Yeah, uhmm, My uncle's just been shot...we are at Denny's, on Haddon Avenue. He is around back..hurry up! I don't knowe what the guy looked like..and he had better be damn glad! Now, please hurry up! *Ricky tosses the phone back to the waitress and rushes back out the doors. As he rounds the backside of the building he notices the first guy that had fled the scene had returned. He was crouched down near his uncle, but suddenly stands straight up. Ricky can see that he is holding his wallet in his hand, the guy looks down at Tony then fires 3 more shots into him. He drops the pistol on him and says* Man: I guess you were right Tony, all bets were off. Looks like you lose this time. *Ricky screams out as he rushes towards the guy who then follows his friends lead and disappears over the fence and into the night. Ricky rushes back over to the scene when three cop cars followed by an ambulance pull into the parking lot. The police shine the spotlight on and can see the gun on his uncles chest. They all immediately surround him, weapons drawn demanding he step away from the scene and ie face down on the concrete* Ricky: THIS IS MY UNCLE. I'M THE ONE WHO CALLED YOU! Police: ON THE GROUND NOW! *Ricky slides away and lies face down on the concrete with his hands interlaced behind his head. The cops immediately rush in and cuff him as the EMT's make their way onto the scene to tend to Tony. The cops put Ricky in the back of the car, and grabs a clipboard from the front. He then makes his way back to the backdoor and standing in front of Ricky begins asking him a some questions* Police: What is your name? Ricky: Ricky Thunder. Am I under arrest? Police: Not yet. We just needed to secure the area before we could allow the EMT's in here. So tell me what happened. Ricky: I just got in town. I was doing a show in Atlantic City, for PCW. I decided to come into town to visit my uncle. I went to his office we talked for a few, we decided we was gonna grab lunch, and the rest is history. Police: Would you mind coming downtown with us in order to fill out a report? Besides there a few things I would like you to see. Ricky: I guess I don't really have an option. Sure why not? How is Tony? Is he.... Police: I don't know son, I can't comment on that until we are certain. Ricky: In other words, yes. *The officer removes the handcuffs and slowly shuts the door. Ricky leans his head back against the seat and the officer gets in and heads back out onto Haddon avenue. Ricky focuses intently on the actions. The men, his uncle, the loud shots. All of which continue to repeat that dreaded nightmare. Suddenly the car comes to a halt and the officer hops out, opening the back door. He leads Ricky inside of the station past all of the booking, and holding cells, to a small office where he offers him a chair. They sit down opposite each other as the officer begins to speak* Officer: By the way, my name is Officer O'Malley. The reason I asked you to come down here, was well, to clarify some things. Your uncle Tony, wasn't everything you thought him to be. Ricky: What are you saying? O'Malley: Your uncle's job, his persona, everything. It was a cover up. We have been tracking one, Tony Pricana, for about 2 years now. He has had strong lines linked to the underground mafia he in New Jersey. The DEA, FBI, local bboys, everyone. We have all been watching him. I am surprised he didn't know. The truth of the matter is... Ricky: Wait a minute. You're telling me my uncle was a mob boss? get off of it. Tony was no mobster, he was the nicest guy you could ever meet. He didn't deserve what happened to him tonight. Nobody deserves that. It is just bullshit that it went down that way. The guys who did this will pay fo rit, I will assure you that. O'Malley: Let me show you some video footage we had confiscated from surveilance. *O'Malley gets up and goes over to the small tv/vcr combo sitting on top of the steel file cabinet. He hits play on the panel and the tape jerks to life. A soundless black and white video begins to play as it shows Tony, socializing with a group of well known local gangsters. They stand about talking for a bit when the door busts in. There is a large guy standing there holding a slim man, wearing a pinstripe suit and glasses, forcing him into the room. A few guys run up and take their punches. Then they all step back and one guys approaches Tony, speaking to him. He then points to the guy as Tony shakes his head, but the man initiates another conversation and hands Tony a .45 automatic. Tony puts the gun to the small, professional looking man's temple. He wiggles his fingers a bit a takes a deep breath as suddenly, the big guy, pulls a gun out and blasts the buisness man in the back of the head.* O'Malley: You see. That man that was killed. Gregory A. Studegar. He was a well known bookie. He had no job, no family to speak of, and absolutely no background. We tried for a long time to bring him in, but we had nothing on him, howver, his obvious ties with the mob finally did him in. He took one bet too many. Did you happen to see the guys who were responsible for his shooting? Ricky: Only ...for a second... *Ricky's voice trails off as he is still staring at the blank tv screen. He doesn't know what to make of what he just saw. He galnces around the room then back at the officer* Ricky: I think I need to go. O'Malley: Well I wan't to show you a few pictures real quck. See if this is one of the guys who were responsible. *The officer shows him a few different pictures before Ricky pinpoints one* Ricky: That's him. I'm sure of it. Who is he? O'Malley: Reggie K. Griffen. Hired hitman for the mob. I woulfdn't take any chances, I would get out here as soon as possible Ricky. These aren't the kind of guys you play around with. Ricky: I believe you. I got to get to the airport anyhow, I have to get ready to head out for fusion. About Tony... O'Malley: You know as well as I do.... * A small tear drops from the eye of Ricky Thunder as he gets up and makes his way from the office and back down the corridor leading outside. He exits the police station and hails a cab, as he instructs him to the airport.*
