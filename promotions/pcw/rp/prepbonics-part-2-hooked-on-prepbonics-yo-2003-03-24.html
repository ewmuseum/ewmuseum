Prepbonics Part 2 'Hooked on Prepbonics, Yo!'

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/24/03 With two hours nearly past since the arrival of Jackson Rust at Tessa Windsor’s Chatham home, Tessa cannot help but wonder where Jackson had disappeared to. She knew he was still there, his car was still parked in the front driveway, yet she had not heard a word from him which was quite unusual for Jackson. She began to worry until her fears and worries were confirmed to be false from a screeching voice coming from upstairs. Jackson: Yes, I’ve got it! The first few phrases of our new language are right here on this paper! Running down the stairs Jackson nearly trips over his own feet but quickly regains his balance as he jumps off the third step and over to Tessa who is waiting at the bottom of the steps curious to see what these sayings are. Tessa: So let me see them. Jackson: Well they err uhhh sort of smudged….but okay! Tessa grabs the sheet of neatly torn notebook paper from Jackson’s hand and begins to read the little sayings scribbled on the sheet in a dark blue ink. Tessa: Like hollah back girlie. Jackson: Wooo wooo! Tessa: Huh? Jackson: It’s just a song dear, keep reading. Tessa: We be like all thugged out in our like bling bling and super kewlie gap t-shirts. Jackson: You tell them sistah! Tessa: Uhh? Jackson: Just keep reading! Tessa: I’ma go chillin wit muh homiez and gurliez. Jackson: Woo, I can already see it. Jackson Rust and Tessa Windsor, proud inventors of the collaborated prep and Ebonics language. Tessa: I don’t know if I should be proud of this, or totally ashamed. Jackson: You know you love it. Keep reading! Tessa: I am like so totally styling in my bling bling. Jackson: Tessa, do you realize what we have created! Tessa: An utterly ridiculous language that no one not even ourselves understands? Jackson: Close, but even better! Tessa: And what’s that? Jackson: A language that both Ellie and Statuz Quo can understand! Tessa: They are probably the only ones. I mean Jacko, just what do all these sayings mean? Jackson shrugs looking over at Tessa as clueless as a child when his or her favorite toy disappears to seemingly non existence. Jackson: I don’t know, I just tossed random words from each language in there. Tessa rolls her eyes but forgets that Jackson has achieved his goal, not of achieving a new goal but in getting Tessa’s to smile and nearly crack up into a series of giggles. Jackson: See I knew I could get you to crack! Tessa: Yeah you win… Jackson: Gee that was an easy win. I hope Lacuna and Jade don’t defeat you that easily come tomorrow night. Tessa: Them beat us? Ha that’s about as much a joke as your so called new prepbonics language. Jackson: Hey, you know you like it! Tessa: Actually I have to admit, it’s addictively funny. I bet I can think of one of my own. Jackson: Give it a shot. Jackson takes a seat on the stairs as Tessa paces around in a circle ending in the same location she started before her head finally pops up, ready to blurt out a saying of her own. Tessa: Come like tomorrow yo, I am like so going to bust a cap in Jade and Cuna’s ass. Hollah if you girlies hear me! Jackson smiles trying not to crack up in laughter of his own. Instead he pats Tessa on the back before heading to the door. Jackson: That’s the spirit champ. You go get them! I’ll be cheering you on! Jackson flashes Tessa a smile and then heads towards the door, waving goodbye to Tessa to let her know that he must be on his way. Tessa merely waves back before laughing to herself as the door shuts behind him. Tessa: Prepbonics eh? It may be a nonsense language but one things for sure, come tomorrow I’m so going to do whatever I said to Jackson about Jade and Lacuna. As she says this she heads up off the stairs and goes off in the direction of her room, obviously wanting to catch some sleep before her big match tomorrow.
