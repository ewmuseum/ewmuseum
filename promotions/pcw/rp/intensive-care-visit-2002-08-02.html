Intensive Care Visit

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 2nd, 2002 [A brother has been disowned, an old lifestyle has been adapted and a newly found friend has been hospitalized. Blayze can consider himself two for one on this occasion with Rush Mason in the hospital as the downfall. Cocky, conceded and of course a bit concerned for his teammate, Blayze decided to miss his current flight to Texas and stay Thursday in Haiti to visit Rush and keep him company before Blayze fights for both himself and Rush on Fusion. We take you to the always-crowded Sacré-Coeur Hospital still in Port-au-Prince, Haiti. The waiting area right when you walk in, is like a early morning Los Angeles traffic jam, you must need a miracle to be able to find a seat. Patients awaiting treatment with broken elbows, pink eye, busted eardrums and the occasional groin pull. The medical staff is scurrying all over the place, pleeing for the patients to take a seat and wait for their numbers to be called, sadly, everyone is nervous, restless and aggravated. Dressed in Baby Blue Jeans, white sleeveless shirt, baby blue track suit type blazer and his baby blue Bandana, Jerome “Blayze” Jordan is flabbergasted by the amount of lunatic people just begging for a simple dose of medication or a little needle prick in the shoulder blade area. As he pushes himself through crying kids and a wheelchair bound 40 year-old female, Blayze manages to force his way to the reception counter] Blayze: AYO! [Blayze shouts at one of the nurses who ignores his aggressive yelling. She looks up at him once, and returns flipping through her patient’s files.] Blayze: Oh so that’s how it’s gonna be? You just going to ignore a brother cause I aint got no damn Medicare card on me? I am just here to visit a fuckin’ friend! [Before he can continue to mouth off at the staff, a young, quite beautiful Latino nurse walks up to the counter and pulls out a document folder] Nurse: Shhh Sir, it is bad enough we have a hoard of patients just giving us headaches every minute with their complaints and impatience. So seeing as you are just here to visit, what is the name of the patient you are here to visit? Blayze: Rush Mason [The nurse begins to search through the folder and within a second, she pulls out Rush’s folder] Nurse: I see, possible corrective surgery on his weak larynx, a bit of blood lost and other than that, no other serious injuries. Blayze: Will he make it out alright? Nurse: It will be a while before his speech is hundred percent, he is also a bit weak so he might have trouble moving certain limbs. Blayze: What room is he in? Nurse: Actually he is in intensive care area of the building but it just means he is being well treated so we can do a number of tests daily to see if surgery is needed. To be specific, he is in room B3 Blayze: Aight thx Nurse: Have a nice day [Thanks to some friendly help from a sweet nurse, Blayze takes no time in waiting around the reception area. He bolts around the corner, almost tackling the janitor and rushes to press the elevator button. Shifting side by side as he awaits the elevator, once it opens he notices a bunch of people crammed inside. Impatient as always, Blayze pulls a patient out of the elevator and shoves himself in, the others around him just eyeball Blayze. Quickly pressing the button marked “IC”, he stands in the crowded space and hopes it just skips all the other floors]. A few minutes later, once arriving at the Intensive Care area, Blayze runs down the hall but is stopped by a thick glasses wearing doctor] MD: Sorry sir but there is no running in this area. Staff members are all around and we do not want and collision injuries Blayze: The fuck? MD: We do not want to have to send you back down to the waiting room Blayze: If you dare do that, you will have that fancy clipboard in your hands indented into your forehead, you dig? MD: Sir.. [Blayze pulls on the collar of his shirt] Blayze: Ask me if I was kidding… [The doctor shakes off Blayze’s hands from his collar] Blayze: Just tell me where room B3 is MD: Down the hall, turn to your right [Blayze pushes him aside and runs down the hall, curving down the first right he sees. He stops, making sure he does not pass the room, he checks each number above the doors. A2, A5, C3…There! B3.] Blayze: Damn… [Blayze just pops his head into the room and suddenly a depressing sensation begins to flow through his body. There lies Rush, lying there with his eyes shut like a soul-less corpse, attached with respiratory wires, a few tubes and two other tubes shoved into his nostrils. Blayze turns away for a moment, looks back with his head down and just steps into the room.] Blayze: Ah damn brother [He pulls a chair that was stuck near the bathroom door and places it near the side of Rush’s bed. As he sits, he grabs Rush’s hand and holds it tight in his.] Blayze: I have seen to many brothers that have been hospitalized, now I have to be forced seeing another.[Rush must have vividly hear Blayze’s voice as he opens his eyes and tilts his head towards Blayze, sporting a crooked smile] Blayze: Rush? RM: Bl..Blay…. [Blayze lifts his index finger to his lips and signals for Rush to be silent] Blayze: Do not force yourself to speak, keep your breath my brother. RM: All I want to know if you are feeling fine. If you can move a few limbs and at least get some speech back, give me a sign. [Rush smiles again then gives a “thumbs up” motion, Blayze can now finally relax] Blayze: Shit man, Clyde really tried to crush your hopes and your body. That burnt-to-crisps blood-sucking bastard will soon understand what it means not to push a thug to his limits. I have only been here about 4 days and already some demonic dick is playing mind games with me by trying to send you to the Emergency Room! [Blayze punches the hospital night table and the telephone almost topples to the floor] RM: Fus…Match… [Rush tries to spit out the words “Fusion” and “Match” but is failing at each occasion] Blayze: Don’t worry about that, if I have to face that cracker jack pale white nazi and the Glenn Lewis Look-a-Like by my self, then I will do just that. A win over them for me, is a win for both of us. You want to see how it feels to have tubes jammed in their noses then I will have them both be taken out on stretches attached with neck braces and upper body support. I will not let my brother down, you want me to rise the black fist for black power and show the world why we are the future on Fusion? Your wish is my command [Rush raises his thumb and slowly tries to nod in agreement] Blayze: Another thing, they need to hurry up and get your ass healed. Why they got you stuck in this dump of a room with no TV or radio when the white dude next door… [Blayze leans back and notices that across the room, a white male is laying up in his bed, changing channels on his ceiling level TV.] Blayze:… gets to act like a fuckin first class citizen with his 36 channel TV, massive medication and colorful blinds on his window. You need some respect and better treatment, hold on! [Blayze gets up from his chair and moves to the exit of the room. He glances at a doctor on the far left posting medical results on a bulletin board, interrupting the M.D. in his job, Blayze shouts some vulgar words at the man] Blayze: Yo Doc! I need some damn aid for here, can ya white jumpsuit wearing ass getting over here![A bit insulted by the comments, the doctor walks over to Blayze and is pushed into the room by Blayze] Doctor.: Excuse me sir but… Blayze: What is this?? Do you treat some patients more special then others? Do you have any prejudice thoughts in that miniature mind of yours? Why you give my friend this garbage of a room with no TV, no clean bathroom and no blinds on the windows when the cracker next door gets a TV with remote, sun blocking shades, new bed covers and a nurse at his side at this moment? Explain that Doctor: Your friend was rushed into the hospital at a busy time. We knew he needed to be examined quickly and put in intensive care so we booked him in the first room that was free. This was the first one that was free Blayze: Is it a coincidence that it is the poorest, ghetto looking room in the building? Doctor: Would you rather he was left on a waiting list and possibly never be examined? Blayze: I’d rather you give him better treatment [The doctor tips his glasses off his eyes then puts his hand on Blayze’s shoulder] Doctor: I can do one of two things: Pull your friend out of this room and try to find him a better one which erases his name from the list of aid and all medical attention or leave him here and continue the day-to-day checking on him. Your choice sir? [Blayze shrugs off the doctor’s hand then looks over at Rush] Blayze: Brother, I have to get going and fly down to Texas. I will live up to your expectations and send two punks to their deathbed on Fusion, take care and I will pray you heal. As for you doc, keep an eye on him and if I get one word that you do one simple fuck up, you will be going through constructive surgery on that pretty little face of yours. I am sure you know what is good, so do your damn job! [Blayze murmurs a “peace” to Rush, eyeballs the doctor then leaves the room, heading down to the exit of the hospital.]
