Date: 03/11/02 The scene opens inside of a long hall, longer than conceivable by the human brain. The hall contains countless books that written on every imaginable subject, shelved and organized in standard alphabetical order, but each book, each subject, being so large and thoroughly researched that reading but one book would take an entire human lifetime. A bright cascading bluish-white light radiates down from an unknown source about the hall. The hall itself has no walls, no floor, no ceiling, just emptiness with the exception of the books and the shelves. We, or rather I, return you to the regularly scheduled RP, already in progress.} Finally, the Misfits come to a clearing, where there is torchlight and music. Some chanting/singing of tribal sound is coming from the clearing. The Misfits move beyond the edge of the trees, and come upon the Land of the Lepers. It is a very large village of small straw huts, and some larger wooden buildings shaped like Native American longhouses. The starry night and full moon shines some light upon the village, but most of the light comes from the illuminating glow of a large bonfire that rises above the tallest buildings in the village, focused in the center of the city. The houses themselves are run-down, terribly maintained sties. Random holes dot the architecture of these straw, wood, and adobe homes. There is an almost overbearing smell of urine and feces. Scraps of food lie around on the ground. All the animals for livestock wallow in their own filth in a makeshift pen built to the right of the city. The Misfits emerge completely from the brush and survey the Land of the Lepers. “This place is a sty,” Drek exclaims. “How they manage to survive here is beyond me.” Richard, blood still dripping from his cuts received during the mauling by Smokey the Bear, speaks despite his woozy condition. “Richard agrees,” he says, “Richard has never seen a worse sight.” Slade nods in agreement, saying, “Quixl pix tjekncs.” “Well put, Slade,” Richard says with a friendly pat on Slade’s shoulder. “Well said.” The Misfits move into the village. Through the holes, they peep into the huts. All empty. At the center of the city, there is much noise. All the people must be there. The Misfits begin searching the huts, looking for something that could resemble medicine to give to Lamar. After a 30-min search, nothing is uncovered and the Misfits are no closer to completing their mission. The Misfits, having no other option (I made it so; I CONTROL THEIR FATE!), go to the ruckus at the center of the city. The streets are wide, and the Misfits have no problem navigating them. Must be so lepers won’t bump into each other and knock off each other’s limbs. In the center of the city, there are tribal musicians beating drums with their hands. Everyone is passing around wooden goblets of some mysterious purple substance and passing it around. The Misfits slip into the festivities. The goblet is passed, and they all take big swigs of the viscous liquid. Blood, ugh. Everything is going well for the Misfits, who begin searching for a doctor and learn that the lepers can understand Slade, until the ceremony changes to a point where everyone tears off a limb and passes it to their neighbor. The Misfits just pretend to try to pull of limbs. Slade pulls hard on the arms of Drek and Richard. They get pulled off balance, and fall to the ground. Slade points at his two friends. Slade “Auskjn oasdh oadun asd aweu hzoxc vnzkjrn!”(Translation: They are imposters! They’re not lepers! Get them!} The lepers all eye Richard and Drek with something of a bloodlust. They raise their eyes to the sky and yell loudly. They begin to tear off arms and legs, and literally throw punches and kicks at Richard and Drek. The two Misfits, betrayed by Slade, can only get pummeled to unconsciousness… {Time Compression: 1 hour later} Richard and Drek awake locked inside of a cage made of extremely hard, dense wood. He rubs his head from the beating he received earlier. “Richard can still feel the hits,” he says in a little painful manner. “Yeah,” Drek affirms without looking back at Richard. “They can throw punches with the best of ‘em.” Richard glances around the hut. “They did not get Slade?” he asks. “Nah,” Drek says, “it doesn’t look like it. Everything is alright, though.” “If by that, you mean that it was our intention to come and get captured by a band of lepers and put in a cage so we can’t get Lamar’s medicine, then Richard agrees, everything is just peachy.” Drek turns back to Richard, a little agitated by the negative tone taken by the scruffy-bearded Misfit. “Slade’s still out there,” says Drek. “He’ll get us out.” Richard remarks snidely, “Wasn’t Slade the one that yelled, ‘GET THEM!’ rather out of the blue?” “I’m sure there was a reason for that.
