Hunted Saga Part Five:Memory Pt2...

29 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/3/02 The story so far… The hunted man known to the army as David J. Constantinople and known to the world as John Crichton, found himself lying in a piss stained alleyway, with no memory of who he is and more importantly what he is. In the last instalment he had discovered that his name was John Crichton and discovered that a company ‘Zemoff Industries’ was apparently to blame for his memory loss. The story continues… The camera opens back up in the drizzly and incredibly murky alleyway at the heart of the great city known as Chicago, Illinois. A figure can be seen standing in the shadows of this murky surrounding, the camera slowly moves forward and we can clearly make out the very familiar features of John Crichton. He looks at his watch and then contiues to observe the hotel, in which the homeless man claimed to have seen Crichton enter earlier in the morning. Crichton was very wary, both about the current situation he now found himself in and of the patchy information given by the barely trust worthy homeless man. Crichton had already decided to take everything the man said with a pinch of salt as soon as the guy opened his mouth. However, the only two leads he had, was this hotel and ‘Zemoff Industries’. Before he tried anything with that company he would need a shower and a little rest. He thought back to the picture and began to speculate about his relationship with woman in the picture. Crichton had felt romantically linked to the woman upon first sight of the picture, and Crichton sure, as hell wanted this to be the case. Crichton also began to speculate about the reason why she was not with him currently, but Crichton decided it was better to deal with the situation at hand and speculate when he knew more about what was going on. Crichton slowly, but very confidently started to make his way toward his hotel, the camera follows him as he soon becomes submerged in the sea of people on the busy streets of Chicago. Crichton swiftly barges past a few people to make his way to the road. He crosses and then enters the hotel building. The camera follows him inside of the building and we see that the interior of the place wasn’t up to much. Crichton walks up to the guy on reception. “Hey man. Can I have the keys to my room please?” Crichton requests. The guy behind the receptionist simply looks up. “Sure what room dude.” The guy replies. “Erm…I don’t remember. I’ve been staying in a lot of hotels recently and all the room numbers are the same to me.” Crichton replied with as much confidence a person could have with the stains of blood down one side of his face. “Yeah, I understand. What’s you name?” The guy and reception questioned. “My name is John Crichton.” Crichton instantly replied.The man takes a look in his books, flips a few pages then looks back up at Crichton. He then turns around and extracts the key to room 123 off of the hook and holds it out in front of Crichton. “Your in room 123 Mr. Crichton.” The guy stated almost unnecessarily. Crichton takes the keys out of the guys hand. “Thanks man, I appreciate it.” Crichton thanked, before heading up the maroon carpeted stairs, in the vague direction of his room. The camera cuts to the hallways on the upper level of the hotel, as Crichton moves from door to door, each time looking at the brass numbers, which appear at the top of the doorframe. Crichton finally comes up to the room marked with brass numbers of ‘123’ and puts the key in the door. He unlocks the door with one turn then opens it. The camera follows Crichton as he enters the room, he looks around for a while before being confronted by a man sat, rather too cockily in an arm chair at the center of the room. Crichton then feels himself, being taken roughly by the arms. He looks up to see two massive guys, wearing suits, almost dragging him over to the guy sat in the armchair. It was only now that Crichton suspected that the homeless guy could have been speaking at least some truth. If his description of the guys who offered the homeless guy money to point Crichton’s location to them, was correct, then the inclusion of ‘Zemoff Industries’ to the whole situation. The man in the armchair leans forward, in a James Bond Villain manner. “John Crichton you are very predictable indeed.” The man stated almost patronisingly. “What makes you say that…” Crichton trails off. “It disturbs me how you have forgotten my name so readily John Crichton. As you will surely recall I am Zemoff’s right hand man, Mr. Baxter. We’ve been looking for you all morning John, ever since you escaped our grasp. You Crichton are so predictable because you came back ton the very place, in which we captured you.” Mr Baxter explained. Crichton takes in the information like a sponge absorbing water. “That explains a lot.” Crichton whispered to himself. “Does it indeed Crichton. Well let me level with you.” Mr. Baxter began, picking a phone up off of the table. “We only need you as a bargaining tool, the woman we found you with, Aeryn Sun, is expendable and for all the trouble you have placed upon us I will be making that phone call right now!” Crichton felt his stomach churn, not for the first time today, and again he was vaguely reminded of some feelings for the strange girl from the picture in his wallet. He felt the adrenaline pump through his body, as his anger reached maximum level. Crichton had no reason to distrust this maniac’s word and he knew full well he couldn’t let the guy make the call. Crichton then swiftly swung his leg backwards with as much force as possible, keeping the accuracy up to 100% as the back of Crichton’s leg impacts with the guy holding Crichton’s left arm. The guy lets go and consequently keels over on the floor. Crichton then instantly uses his free left hand and swiftly turns letting his fist connect with the guy’s face. The guy stumbles backwards and falls over the other guy, who is still clutching his testicles for dear life. Crichton then tackles Mr. Baxter to the floor, making sure the phone his knocked out of his grasp and more importantly out of reach. Crichton lands a punch squarely on Baxter’s jaw, knocking him perfectly unconscious. Crichton feels around his victim, extracting a piece of paper, with Zemoff Industries street address on it and then extracting a perfectly sheikh looking black pistol. Crichton, momentarily bedazzled by the black pistol, was taken by surprise by the fist gut, whom Crichton low-blowed not a minute before grabs him in a bear hug type position. Crichton took note of the man’s over sized bulging muscles as he felt the life being squeezed out of him. Crichton doubted that he could hit another low blow on this guy, he would of course be expecting it. Crichton tried to head butt the guy, but the man had that one pegged too. As each breath was being squeezed out of him Crichton became more and more desperate. He could now only think of one option as he remembered the mesmerising black pistol. It was a long shot and extremely dangerous, but Crichton realised it was the only option applicable to the situation. Crichton aimed the gun downwards, but at an angle, to avoid shooting himself and pulled the trigger. The noise from the gun was almost deafening, as the bullet exited the black pistol and entered the over sized man’s foot. The guy let go and instantly fell on the floor clutching his destroyed foot. Crichton then shot the other over sized body guard in the leg and just wanted to get out of there as fast as he could, but he told himself that, that was unacceptable. The man named Baxter could regain consciousness at any moment and what would stop him from making the phone call? Crichton dragged Baxter and the two guys and dumped them in the maintenance cupboard and locked the room from the outside. Crichton sat down in the armchair, which Mr. Baxter once occupied and tried to further digest the information that he discovered from this little confrontation. He began to wonder what kind of man would leave the woman he loved to escape a mad mans grasp, leaving her behind like that. This worried him, but he figured that some kind of plan must have been in mind for him to do that. Crichton summed up the events from the last couple of minutes and quietly devised an answer. Although it had been a bit of a struggle he was able to take out the guys without an awful amount of trouble. He figured that the only reason that they were able to deter him before was because of Aeryn. Therefore it was perfectly reasonable to assume that both of them were not able to escape within the same moment, so whoever escaped had to go back and free the other. Crichton felt that this sounded almost like it was in his character, but he still didn’t like the sound of it and suspected he didn’t like the sound of it at the start of the plan. Either way there was one more thing he needed to do and he suspected that it wasn’t going to be easy. Crichton held the miniature piece of paper, containing the Zemoff Industry address and placed it in his pants pocket, he then placed the black pistol in the inside of his coat pocket. The camera cuts to the outside of the hotel building as Crichton emerges donning his ‘procured’ long leather coat and we also notice the blood down the side of his face is in fact gone. Crichton hails a cab and one pulls up to the curb almost immediately. Crichton gets in and the cab moves on immediately. The camera cuts to outside a massive twenty story building, the camera is placed at the bottom of the building as it pans upwards. The end of the building cannot be seen at such an angle. Then the camera revolves 180 degrees to see the same cab carrying Crichton pull up to the curb just by the building. Crichton paused before attempting to enter the building. He realised that some sort of plan needed to be made, otherwise he was just gonna get his ass caught fairly quickly. He sure as hell knew that they weren’t just gonna let him stroll into the establishment. He racked his brains and came up with nothing. Then he noticed a truck pulling up at the curb near the building and a brain wave hit him. The camera focuses in on the truck, which as a logo saying ‘Planet Picture’. Crichton just shrugs. “What the hell! It works in all of the movies.” Crichton comments walking over to the van. The guy driving the van gets out and walks to the rear of the van. The guy opens the back doors of the truck and messes about with the pizzas in the back. Crichton walks up slowly behind the man, takes a quick look around to check if anyone is watching, then pushes the man. Crichton jumps in too and shuts the door behind him. After a couple of minutes Crichton emerges from the van, donning the pizza man’s work clothes and a large deep pan pepperoni pizza. He swiftly strolls up the steps leading to the building and opens the massive glass door – with a struggle – then enters the establishment. The camera cuts to inside the reception area, Crichton makes note of the classy marble finish, to the massive pillars holding up the ceiling, as he nears reception. Crichton walks right up to the receptionist. “Excuse me sir, anyone order a Pizza.” Crichton stated bluntly to guy at reception. The man at reception looks at a piece of paper placed before him and then looks back up at Crichton. “Yeah Okay man. A security guard will escort you up.” The receptionist said nodding over to a particularly big looking man in a blue uniform. Crichton began toward the set of elevators, closely accompanied by the security guard. This was better than Crichton had expected. He thought that they would try to take the pizza off him themselves and take to whom ever ordered it. Crichton could now take out the security guard, lock him up somewhere then go and hunt for Aeryn. Crichton followed the security guard to the nearest lift and waited, as the guard presses the button, summoning the elevator ton their position. The elevator doors open and the security guard ushers Crichton into the metal compartment. Two other people followed them in and Crichton kicked himself. It would have been far better and easier for Crichton to take out the guard in such an enclosed space. The security guard presses the button for level 20, while the other two people press the button for level 12. Within a couple of seconds the elevator came to a stop and the two other people exited. Meanwhile Crichton had managed to edge slightly behind the security guard so that he could catch the security guard unawares. As soon as the elevator doors close, Crichton taps the security guard on the shoulder, the guard briefly turns round to catch Crichton’s fist coming at him. The blow connects perfectly, but the security guard simply stumbles backwards and props himself up, upon the back wall of the elevator. The security guard takes his hat off and undoes the buttons upon his jacket, before showing off with a variety of ninja styled moves. Crichton realised that this guy was a trained fighter, once the demonstration had finished the guy simply strikes a martial arts style pose. Oh great! The one thing Crichton didn’t expect just had to happen. Crichton decided that another tactic would be needed to defeat this foe, he knew full well that he couldn’t rely solely upon his own fighting skills. Crichton needed more space to deal with this situation and as soon as the elevator door ‘dinged’ open, Crichton turned and ran. “Aw…hell karate man!” Crichton exclaimed whilst placing distance between him and the security guard.
