Golden Heart Chronicles: GOOAALL~!!!! (Chapter 4 Part 2)

12 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/12/02 Golden Heart Chronicles Chapter 4 Part 3: The first two chapters of the Golden Heart chronicles were the insight to the life of Juan Borro. Through these chronicles we were able to experience what Juan went through to lead to how he turned out now. The first two chapters in the Golden Heart Chronicles are where something sparks “flashbacks” during Juan’s present day life to take us back to his childhood. In chapter three and it’s following parts will focus on Juan and his relationship with being a fighter. He puts other things behind him and works on becoming a determined fighter. In chapter 4 Juan focuses on his International match up for the PTC I-PPV. It is composed of his huge Borro (GOAL~!) Bash. Everyone Read and enjoy the final part to chapter three. Time: 1:53 P.M Location: Lisbon, Portugal Scene: Streets (GOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOAAAAAAAAALLLL~!!!!!!!!!!!) Part 2 The scene opens with the beautiful block party type bash Juan set up fully organized and ready to start. The DJ’s are up top working the turntables, people are arriving by the hundreds, and Juan is being the usual social butterfly. Juan looks around and never thought that his party would become such a festive event. He knew throwing something like this would get the stress off of his back before the big match tomorrow night. This day will not just be one other day, but it will be the biggest in your face, wild, unrestricted, do anything, go anywhere, bash in this side of Europe. Everyone from Portuguese politicians, to famous athletes, to convicted felons, to computer dorks, to strippers are here. Of course the biggest line in the whole place is the one to the bathroom, with the line to the bar being a close second. But, then again one leads to the other. The day is sunny, and there are so many things going on Juan can’t even keep track of it. That’s why Juan got a custom-made hand held video programmer to keep track of everything that’s going on in HIS (GOAL~!) Bash. Juan starts off by having a few drinks with people in high places. One of which being Nelly Furtado and the other is Meredith Vierra. Both of them are seen in America, but of Portuguese ancestry. Juan makes his way to the two ladies and starts chatting away. Juan: Ahh Ladies it’s great to see you can make it here on such short notice. The people here love you two, do you know that. Meredith: Yes Juan, we’d love to have you on The View one day. We can talk about you’re thought on American Politics, Abortions, and your sexual preference. Doesn’t that sound like fun. Juan just stares at Meredith nodding and draws his attention to Nelly. Juan: Hey Nelly how are things going with the family. I remember our ancestors going way back all the way to Azores. Nelly: Beep Beep! Juan: Excuse me. Nelly then starts going off and making weird noises, as a bunch of monkey that follow Budha follows her. Juan then draws his attention back to Meredith who is standing there and taking notes on the native people as they walk by. Meredith: Note to self. People in Portugal wear these things that support your breast. They call them bras, but I don’t know what they’re talking about seeing as I have never used one. Juan stares down to see if Meredith’s comment is indeed a fact, but then quickly regains his normal pose as she turns around. Juan: So….that Nelly over there. She sure is weird, sometimes you think shes even Indian in her videos, right? Before she can respond Barbara Walters jumps out of a bush, and karate chops Meredith in the back of the neck. She then leaps off the ground and dropkicks Meredith. While Meredith is out cold she takes out a bag and throws her in it, fleeing the scene immediately. Juan shrugs and checks his ultra high tech video programmer thingy to see what else is going on. He notices people arriving through the PTC entrance area, and quickly rushes over. He is then met by his first guest Blayze from CCW wearing some fine ghetto gear which Juan likes to refer to it as, accompanied by his brother Darren Jordan. Blayze: Hey Juan what’s chillin? This is my brotha Darren. The two shake hands, as Juan does some ultra long handshake trying to make like he’s ghetto. Juan: I see my homies, I got brothas back in my hiz house too. Blayze: No he’s really my brother. Juan: I hear ya gangsta, you boys are brothas from different mothas, thas coo thas coo, I hear ya. Blayze: No we have the same mother. Juan is then stumped and in a feeble attempt to not look like a complete moron he does the only respectful thing he can do. Juan: Where is my present? Blayze takes out the official Portuguese jersey signed by Figo. Juan looks at it for a few seconds and laugh. Juan: It’s nice, but seeing as I was on the team, I have about fifty of them at home. And since you guys have connections with Figo and all let me call him over. Juan whistles and Figo comes over. For some reason Blayze and his brother, are nervous and sweat starts to drip from their foreheads. Figo arrives and looks at the shirt with pure confusion. Figo: That in ne wey es me signature. With that said Blayze starts shaking, but using his brain to get him out of the jam. Blayze: Look over there its Shakira. Blayze says this while running away with his brother as Juan and Figo turn around. Odd thing is, over to the left of Juan is Shakira. It seems her and Nelly Furtado are getting in a little bit of a fight. Nelly: BEEP BEEP! Shakira: Lela la le la le, le lala le la lela le. Juan draws his attention back to the area where the PTC superstars are entering. In come Kwik FX, sporting a red headband and Tom Mason, wearing a nice Armani suit. Juan, who cuts to the chase and demands his presents, greets the two men. Juan: Come on hand over the presents, I don’t have all day. Kwik FX hands Juan a platinum chin with a golden flag at the end, and Mason hands Juan a bag of shiny new soccer balls with his name engraved in it. As Juan looks at the presents, a tear rolls down his eye. To show his gratitude Juan gives the men entry into the time of their lives. Juan: Boys, go over to that tent, inside you will see hundred of strippers around a pool. Tell them Juan sent you, and for them to do whatever they want to you guys. Kwik FX: WOOHOO!!!!! Mason: Sounds good, Renee want to come. Renee who is with Mason and dressed in dirty rags then starts sniffing Juan as Juan back away, and Mason attaches her back to her leash. Coming in next is Calvin Rilley looking all buff and strong. Juan goes up to his and shakes his hand, as Juan gets thrown up and down by the big mans handshake. Juan ignores it and grabs the present out of his hand. He rips it open and sees a t-shirt signed by Pele and Mia Hamm. Juan then throws it aside and looks back at Calvin. Juan: What is this, how dare you insult me with that dirty American women’s name and that Brazilian we broke the legs of autographs. Just for that I order you to go inside and get shit faced drunk. Calvin shrugs and goes in. Juan starts getting tired and takes a seat in the fishermen section. Of course it smells like tuna fish and sardines, but that’s what it means to be Portuguese. Juan has a little update on his friends, as in sector 1 Barbara Walters is starting an argument with Portuguese Politicians on why she should be cloned. In Sector 2, Shakira and Nelly Furtado and still going at it. But down at Sector three Juan notices a little bit of a fight going on between. When Juan gets to the scene he sees a big crowd surrounding Blayze and Charlie Loc who must have sneaked in. It’s the battle of the Crip walk. And things have heated up. Juan: Hey fellas, wait until the dance competition. You’ll be able to battle it out there. Loc: No way it’s getting settled right here. The people wanna see Loc vs. Blayze in the battle of the Crip Walk. When I say What you say Holla! WHAT? Portuguese Citizens: HOOLA! Loc: See Juan even though they can’t pronounce Holla they still wanna see Loc vs. Blayze. Juan: Fine, but first you better hand over your present. Charlie not knowing what to give takes some fine Hennessey and throws it to Juan. Like any respected Portuguese man, Juan grabs it and chugs it, like a real man. Juan: Ahh that was good, now I have to go back off to the PTC entrance, I see a few more people arriving. Juan goes over and sees Chris Mathews wearing a nice watch and golden shirt. He acts arrogant and doesn’t acknowledge Juan’s existence. He just hands him a cactus, which one take one look at before jiving it back in Mathews head. He then rips of Matthews’s watch and shirt, as security throws him with the wild chicken. Mathews notices all the crazy chickens around him and tries to make a run for it, but then attack him, with feathers flying in the air and a large scream heard all throughout the bash. Back by the entrance Juan notices some man in the distance walking around, consistently hitting walls and poles. Juan quickly finds out it’s The End, but not feeling like being a nice and helpful gentlemen. Juan just goes up behind him and grabs his present from his hand, as he runs away opening the cool new memoirs of Prince Henry the Navigator he received. After seeing the present Juan felt bad about how he treated The End so badly. When he goes over to assist him, he sees him by a table with six ladies around him, playing poker and taking everyone’s money. Juan: What the hell is going on? Next up Juan notices the new school and old school PCW boys arriving. First being Suburban Uprising with Weezer, a group of strippers, and there dog Rover. Juan: Are you guys here for the bash or are you just looking for the animal porn service. That’s down the road. Teddy: It is, sweet. Juan: No I was joking. Put your strippers in the tent, your dog with Mason’s wife, and Weezer I think there are a few kids getting wasted in the back, you can join them. The strippers go into the tent as they notice the already coma toast Kwik FX and Tom Mason who have just been experiencing the time of their lives. Next in comes the cruiserweight portion of the guys, Chris Chase wearing his world title with Dustin Downey and his wife Serenity. Behind him is Xavier Kannon with Ellie~! and his five million scientologists. Juan receives a video of Shanghaied 2 and a Montoya jock strap from the two wrestlers. Juan adores his two present, or at least acts like he does. Before Juan can leave with the tape Kannon steals it and gives it to give science cronies who digitally alter it to show Kannon winning in the end. Meanwhile Chase and Kannon fight, while Juan grabs their World and Continental titles fleeing the scene. Trying to look cool Juan walks around sporting the two belts. He then glances at his watch and notices that it’s time for the dance competition to begin. Juan gets up and stage and begins. Juan: Excuse me ladies and gentlemen, but I’d like to introduce you all to the (GOAL~!) Borro Bash. Right now we are all going to be entertained by witnessing the PTC (Goal~!) competition. The rules are simple. There will be two sets of five minute heats where four of these PTC wrestlers will take the floor and strut their stuff. Our talented panel of Nelly Furtado’s monkeys will judge them. Meanwhile, the crowd notices the monkey’s wearing clothing and with pens in their hands, getting a huge “AWWWW!” from the people. Juan is about introducing the teams, but from out of nowhere comes a group of gangs trying to ruin the bash. Juan wonders who is behind all of this, and jumps off of stage. He sees a huge circle of four different gangs of Portuguese citizens led by PTC athletes. To his left he sees OSW’ s Aimz, with a bunch of mad and fed up Portuguese wives with weapons. To his right he sees Da Westsidaz, with a bunch of young Portuguese men wearing baggy pants and designer shirt, singing along to Run DMC songs. Right when Juan can’t see it getting any worse he sees Xavier Kannon and Tim Walden with nobody on their side accept the good for nothing scientists, because of course nobody likes Kannon and Walden. The last two groups in front of Juan are a group of surfing Portuguese men with bleached hair led by Cowabunga Connection and a bunch of snotty Brits who are angry at the world led by Pailthorpe. Juan doesn’t know why any of these men are here, or what they are doing with all of these people on their sides. We’ll find out more of what went on and why things turned out this way in the Westside story fashioned last part. Will PTC take over Portugal. Only time can tell. Scene Fades to Black.
