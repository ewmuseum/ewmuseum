Days Of The Week Ahead

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 20th Thursday, the week draws nearer to it's end. The days grow short and the weather becomes sweet. People get ready and make their plans. I sit alone at home waiting for the next day to come and go. Thursday I watch the sky roll on and the clouds blow north. Oh Thursday is the day that nobody keeps but I, only I keep Thursday dear. Friday, the day of the week that everyone loves, everyone but I. I see young teenagers walk down the street in groups smiling and laughing. I see the twenty something crowd enter the neighbourhood pub for a round of beers. I see young grade school children play in the streets imitating their favourite sport heroes. How I loath Friday, the day of the week that brings you joy brings I but sorrow. When Friday ends I come out and have my day of fun. Saturday, oh Saturday, the day that brings me fun. Turn on the television flip through the channels and land on channel one. Static. The static on the television emulates the static in my life. I feel like the television. Empty and turning. Screaching and loud. I am the static you see on the television, the show you watch is my heart and soul. Sunday, the day of the lord. Sunday I am normal. Sunday I am like any other person who practices the Catholic religion. I go to my church and say my prayers. I listen to what the Priest has to say and try to apply it to my life. Ten minutes after church I am as evil as I was before, honking and cursing as I try to get home through the traffic. My rage is unleashed ten minutes after salvation. Monday, I look forward to the next day and wish that this would end. Tommorrow is the day I receive my chance, tommorrow is the day I get a shot. My career will be made or broken. With one win I receive my chance. With the win after the next I can finally leave. I will leave only on top, once I capture gold I am gone as quickly as I came. Tuesday, all my training comes down to this. Three men stand in my way of glory. My history means nothing. My life and future lies ahead. As I step between the ropes I see the cameras flashing. This is it, I know, I feel, that this is my night. This is my night and nobody can take this away, the future is mine and the night is golden. Wednesday, the day of celebration. I have captured what I wished. My life is now closer to my fate. With that win I have my shot, my shot at pure redemption. I know my critics say I am done but I will prove them wrong. With the people who push me away and taunt me in the back. 'Go to the FSW now, you do not belong here.' To them I say you are right, why should I stay with people like you. That is why I must now win, my life is on the line. Now you all can cheer for me because you can finally see me leave.
