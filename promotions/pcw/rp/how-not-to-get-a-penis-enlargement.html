Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin was coming to terms with the realisation that his infamous ‘pornstar-esque’ penis was shrinking. How do you counteract this shrinkage problem?Via an ancient method known as jelquing….. [Scene opens in the lounge room of Flaymin’s house. Flaymin walks into the room clutching a giant mirror.He places the mirror against the wall and then walks over to the coffee table and picks up the ‘Jelquing Instruction Manual’. He opens up to the first page and begins to read the instructions] Flaymin: “Welcome to the powerful art form known as jelquing.Over the next few weeks your penis will miraculously stretch in places you never thought imaginable”. [Flaymin skims through the rest of the introduction and then flips to the instructions on page 2] Flaymin: “Warm up your anatomy by stretching it with your hands.If applicable, try mashing it with your toes”My toes?? [Unable to complete the second step for obvious reasons, Flaymin begins to stretch his donger as if playing with clay. After about thirty seconds he pulls out a mini-ruler from behind his ear and starts to measure if the size of his penis has changed] Flaymin: Damn it!It’s still exactly the same size! [Flaymin turns the page in the manual to see what the next step is] Flaymin: “A critical component required for jelquing to succeed is visualisation.Envision your penis is growing 3 inches.Transform your mindset into a position where you can feel the size increasing.Imagine it…. Believe it…. Achieve it”. [Flaymin closes his eyes and begins to hum as if in some sort of trance. The humming continues…. Except he now seems to be bopping his head back and forth, also. After a minute and a couple of nifty dance moves later, he realises that he’d been humming the Simpsons Theme Song all along] Flaymin: Damn Simpsons!They’ve thrown me off my train of thought! [Flaymin closes his eyes again and begins to mutter to himself….] Flaymin: Come on, come on- bigger willy, bigger, come on, woooo, el grande! [Flaymin opens his left eye and looks down at his penis to see if there has been any progress. Disappointed by the same, shrivelled penis, Flaymin slams his mini-ruler onto the floor. He takes a few deep breaths to make sure that he remains composed and then walks back over to the manual. He flips to the next page and begins reading the instructions] Flaymin: “When undertaking the next step we advise with great caution that you do not burn yourself”. [Flaymin momentarily raises an eyebrow before continuing to read] Flaymin: “This next step is of vital importance since it is responsible for loosening up the skin and cells in your penis, thus allowing it to grow in size. To do this, get a small towel and put it in a pot filled with cold water. Then take the pot and put it on the stove and start boiling the water. After approximately 5 minutes, the water should reach a warm state.At this moment in time, turn off the stove and using tongs take the towel out of the warm water.Then, CAREFULLY wrap the towel around your penis for a maximum of THREE minutes”. [Flaymin nods his head in agreement. He walks into the kitchen, fills up a pot with water and places it on the stove.He then picks up a nearby tea towel and places it in the pot. [Flaymin looks at his watch and begins to count down the time. A couple of minutes later, Flaymin walks back over to the pot on the stove and looks at the water.He dips his finger into the water and realises that the water is exactly the right temperature- warm. Flaymin begins to stroke his chin as he considers an alternate plan] Flaymin: Hmmm…. Let me get this straight.If I take the towel out when the water is warm, then my donger will probably only grow an inch or two. Buuuuut, according to my mathematical know-how, if I leave the towel in there until the water reaches boiling point and THEN apply it to my penis, then I should probably grow at least 4 inches. [Flaymin nods his head reassuringly. He decides to leave the water on the stove and wait until it begins to boil. A few minutes later, he returns to the pot and notices that the water is really boiling to the extent that some of the water is beginning to spill over the side of the pot and on to the stove] Flaymin: Its showtime!! [Flaymin picks up the nearby tongs and reaches into the pot of boiling water and pulls out the steaming hot tea towel. With the heat of the towel beginning to burn his hands, he quickly folds the towel in half and then slaps it onto his penis. And then it happens…… Flaymin’s left eye begins to twitch….. His right eye boggles wide open….. His jaw drops….. And then….. Flaymin: AAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Flaymin rips off the hot towel and looks down at the remains of his penis- a burnt crisp little willy with blood beginning to trickle out from the side] Flaymin: AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Scene fades to black] Tune in next time when Flaymin visits the Intensive Care Ward at the hospital and discovers the likely future of ‘Flaymin Junior’!!!
