David Arquette Syndrome - Part 2 - Heather vs. Courtney

8 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 8 [The David Arquette Syndrome, AKA DAS, is something that must be taken seriously. Your loved one or someone you know could be infected by this and surely become sucked into it's web of insanity. The screeching voice and the horribly placed jokes will come on like a speeding car. Changing from character to character, easily able to drive family and friends to a quick insanity themselves. It hasn't bee proven to have spread, but being surrounded by someone with DAS could rub off. One must be careful when trying to treat a DAS patient] [Psychologist Hans Peterson has explained this epidemic to be caused by unnatrual brain patterns in the right lobe of the brain. That is usually where the 'David Arquette blows' comments are stored. When something tragic or something mentally demanding happens that part of the right lobe is disabled to make you forget the David Arquette blows. You'll suddenly begin believeing that he's the greatest actor in the entire world. Comparing him to the greatest silent actor Buster Keeton. You don't even know it. It just happens. It usually begins with the yelling. The unpredicatable Arquette-like outbursts and then suddenly you begin making horrible jokes. Soon after the carrier will form a oneness with the actor and begin emulating his characters from his films. Once it gets that far it is extremely hard to cure. Eventually that person will become David Arquette and go to Hollywood to try and get major movie roles. Claim that he is a great comedic genius and then get shot down. Evenutally it will lead to heavy, heavy drinking. Then long, lonely nights in your boxer shorts thinking about your lost wife, Courtney Cox. Then the worst happens...suicide. Most DAS patients are locked up in cells that accomodate their state of Arquetteness. Scream sets. Ready To Rumble sets. Usually the patients will adapt and live in the world over and over again] ['Heavy Metal' Harry, the stoned, 80's hair band loving man has come down with DAS. Caused from passing up a night of love with a beautiful naked woman and being called 'gay' because of it. His right lobe was triggered and he began his rampage. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, his half brother and tag team partner, has made it his God given right to do anything to find the cure to this evil epidemic. Yes, he knows they have a tag team title shot at Friday Fusion against The Heartbreakers but he wants his brother back. He wants him stoned more than ever. He wants Harry headbanging to Twisted Sister, Bon Jovi, and Quiet Riot once again. Tommy can no longer take the insanity of having to hear the Scream series word for word. The only thing that Tommy wants from Scream is Neve Campbell, naked] [After taking The Therapists advice to visit a Shaman Healer, whom liked to act Mexican and dress like a Luchadore. The visit did not go well at all with Harry turning into Gordie, from Ready To Rumble. He speared the Shaman Healer, leaving him unconcious in his cavern. They fled the scene left with no sign of curing from the Healer] [Tommy has tried many ancient remedies to hopefully cure Harry of his illness....] [On the beach Harry/David sits staring out to the calm tide. The evening night blaring down on him with beautiful stars and light from the three-quarters moon. He smiles brightly with a different twang in his smile. It's no longer stoned out and happy go lucky. It's almost like he's possessed by some sort of David Arquette demon] Harry/David: I'm a great actor.... [Suddenly....] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: OOGA BOOGA!!!!!!! [Tommy leaps up from behind Harry/David in an attempt to frighten the illness out of him] Harry/Chris McCormack from Eight Legged Freaks: GET AWAY FROM ME.....YOU EIGHT LEGGED FREAKS!!!!! RAAHHHHH!!! [Harry/Chris points his index at Tommy trying to shoot him with a shotgun, but obviously failing because it's his finger. Tommy looks depressed] TTT: That only works for hiccups...bogus..... [Then Tommy came up with a brilliant plan. Harry's fantasy is to have Heather Locklear come into his room completely naked, except for a Twisted Sister T-shirt, and do with him as she wished. Once he saw Heather things would be back to normal!] [Harry/David sits on th leather couch parked in fron of the 52' television that has HDTV. The soothing movie of 'See Spot Run' is playing and Harry/David has a huge grin across his face. Tommy sits down beside him with a folder on his lap. Tommy has a smile on his face, knowing full well that this is going to work] TTT: Dude, want to see something totally bodacious in a far out way? Harry/David: Down, Spot. [Tommy rolls his eyes] TTT: HEATHER LOCKLEAR!!! [Tommy begins stuffing the picture of Heather LockLear in sexy white lingerie in Harry/David's face] TTT: You remember, dude. Heather Locklear in only a Twisted Sister T-Shirt. She wants you, compadre. Heather is calling your name. She needs some HEAVY METAL in her life!!! [Harry/David looks at the picture and begins shaking wildly, like some kind of metamorphisis is taking place. Tommy smiles brightly, knowing that Harry can break this thing] Harry/David: COURTNEY!!!!! [Tommy's jaw drops in total shock] TTT: HEATHER!!! Harry/David: COURTNEY!!!! TTT: HEATHER!!! Harry/David: COURTNEY!!!! [Harry/David grabs Tommy by his neck and tosses him outside on the deck and then closes the sliding door] Harry/David: BAD SPOT!!! [Tommy shakes his fist at the sky] [Harry/David sits on the deck, just outside Tommy's totally bodacious bedroom. He is soaking up some rays so that he can hopefully star in a new movie where he can have a nice frontal nudity shot. He needs to give the ladies what they've all been missing and only what Courtney can get] Harry/David: Hollywood, here I COOOOOOMMMMEEE!! [Suddenly Tommy descends from the roof with a wiring connected to the harness he is wearing. He holds the picture of Heather LockLear in his hand, sticking it directly in Harry/David's face again] TTT: HEATHER LOCKLEAR, DUDE!!! Harry/David: COURTNEY!!!!!!! TTT: HEATHER!!! Harry/David: COURTNEY!!!!!!! [Before Harry/David can show force against the evil 'Ghost Face Killer' Randy Raggamuffin comes running onto the deck with a tomato in his hand. He fires it at Harry/David and laughs like a madman] Raggamuffin: YOUR GAY!!!! Harry/David: I'M NOT GAY!!!! TTT: Your not helping, dude! Your blowing it. [Raggamuffin is onto the beach laughing wildly] Raggamuffin: Good! Because Harry has gone absolutely insane. Now you two will never have a chance to become tag team champions. You'll be losers, just like fate had made out for you. Lets face it boys, It's All About The Pink in PCW these days. Like it would even be logical for a surfer and a stoner to carry prestigious titles in a major wrestling company. PUHHLEEASE!!!! Harry/Gordie: WRESTLING'S NOT FAKE!!!!!!! [Harry/Gordie runs out like a bolt of lightning and spears the hell out of Raggamuffin, leaving him unconcious for yet a second time in a week. Tommy just smiles brightly] TTT: Well, it came into use for something. [The cord snaps and Tommy slams onto the deck] TTT: Totally could have done without that, dudes....ouch.... [Now the two men sit in the living room as Harry/David enjoys Scream 3, hooting and hollering about a great accomplishment or some half assed line that he thinks was the greatest thing ever said in a movie. Tommy's off in space and can no longer take this. It's starting to get a little frustrating, almost like he is turning into David Arquette. He's finding himself screaming a lot more lately. Noticing that yes...Scream 3 is a REALLY REALLY good movie. It is actually the best he's seen in a long time. NO!! WAIT!! This can't be true. He must fight it] TTT: DUDE!! Your Harry! 'Heavy Metal' Harry! Your my brother and your my tag team partner. We've got a big tag team title match on Friday against The Heartbreakers!! Those guys are always preaching about PINK!! PINK!! You love Twisted Sister and you love BLACK!! Black rules!! Harry rules!! COME OUT HARRY!! [Harry/David just looks at Tommy as if he were a two bit nothing actor, nothing up to his standards] Harry/David: What are you talking about? I'm David Arquette, the greatest actor that has ever lived. TTT: NO!!! Your Harry! You smoke pot all the time. Harry/David: No, I don't. TTT: YES YOU DO!!! Harry/David: NO I DON'T!!! [Tommy jumps on top of the couch and begins screaming like some sort of madman, beating his chest like he was King Kong] TTT: I WILL RULE YOOOOOOOUUUUUUU!!!!!!!! Harry/Gordie: CROWN ME!!!!!! [Tommy jumps off of the couch and nails Harry/Gordie with a double ax hammer. He is unconcious. Tommy is breathing quite heavily not really sure what the hell just happened. He doesn't know whether it was bogus or bodacious, maybe it's a little bit of both. He sits down on the couch and changes the channel on the television] TTT: Gnarly...Six Feet Under. [Tommy then uses Harry/David's unconcious body as a foot rest] TTT: We'll figure something out tomorrow, dude. [Tommy sees the red headed girl from Six Feet Under and suddenly comes to a realization] TTT: Dude....that's it!!! Harry had a most mental experience with that babe from the party!!! He just needs to totally get with her and prove that he isn't gay!! TOTALLY RIGHTEOUS!! TO BE CONCLUDED
