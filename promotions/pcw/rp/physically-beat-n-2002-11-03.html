Date: 11.3.02 | Lil' Thug looks at Buuday uneasy. Sheeca sits there with at the edge of her seat waiting for a reply. The light turns green and the limo takes off down the street once more. Gray clouds in the sky start to slowly become darker as the sun's rays become thinner and thinner. As the limo rides by the projects on Martin Luther King street you can see people turn there head and watch the long stretched platinum silver coated limo fly down the road blinding with the light reflecting off of it. There are red brick apartments that make up the projects on the left side of the road. The sight of cloths hanging out on wires and big bags of cans on the front pouch remind lil' Thug of when he was little and had to live like that. Lil' Thug sits there looking out the window as Sheeca sits beside him waiting for an answer. The limo hits a slight hole in the lose gravel road and jumps as Sheeca is thrown back busting her head against the window on the other side. Buuday covers her mouth and laughs as lil' Thug not knowing what happened turns around and looks at Sheeca. | 'Don't hit da window cuz u mad I haven't answered u yet! Damn, if you want to go that bad we'll go. Shit, don't fuck up my car cause yo ass want something! I swear yo ass is so fuckin' childish!' | Sheeca looks at lil' Thug shocked and speechless as she was just wrongly accused of doing something she wasn't. Well not purposely doing. Lil' Thug turns and looks at the window as Buuday is still laughing. Sheeca pushes Buuday signaling for her to shut up when lil' Thug picks up his cell phone and calls the driver. | 'Wuz up lil' Thug, you want something?' 'Yeah, my home gurl Sheeca needs to go to da spa because she ain't gonna embarrass me walkin' on the show lookin' like who did it and why!' | Buuday hears spa and pops up. | 'Lil' Thug, drop me off there too!' 'Aight.' | With the limo driver knowing his way around and the spa already close by, he soon drops Buuday and Sheeca off as lil' Thug takes off to go get ready. Buuday and Sheeca stand right in front of the glass doors of the spa. On the glass 'We do you right' is stained on the glass in white. Buuday and Sheeca look at one another and walk into the spa. A gust of wind hits them as they open the doors and proceed in where the aroma of hair spray and lotions take over their senses. Different types of colors of hair lay all scattered about the floor as a young boy about sixteen years old comes by with a brown. His brown hair is cut shirt as his skin is of a dark brown color. With his brown eyes locked on the floor he continues to sweep up hair wearing his ridiculous blue apron that hangs around his neck and ties at the lower back. He whips the sweat away from his smooth face and continues to pass by sweeping up hair. There is a line of black leather seats lined against the left side of the wall. Mirrors are screwed into the white wall in back of every seat. Between the seats and mirrors there is a forest green marble sink with hair, scissors, and hair clippers. The scissors lie on the sink counter while the hair clippers hang from hooks underneath the edges of the sink counter. Buuday and Sheeca look around admiring the ghetto spa as they approach the front desk where a black lady sits talking on the phone. Her jet-black hair is in perfect curls and her lips are of the finest red color. She lies back in her seat having a good conversation on the phone with someone they assume is her best friend because they are going wild with every piece of gossip. She has her legs cross while wrapping the curly phone cord with her right index finger. You can hear the tapping of her long nails that have an elegant design on them. At first Buuday and Sheeca are to busy looking around wondering what they are going to do first then right when they turn to ask the lady at the front desk a fine black male comes from the back with a cut of piece of cactus in his hand. Buuday turns her head and checks out his cute butt as he walks away. Sheeca turns to the lady at the front desk and asks for assistance. | 'Can we getz some assistance up in this biatch?' | The lady keeps on talking on the phone ignoring every word Sheeca just said. Sheeca looks at Buuday like she is going to whoop that ladies ass then turns back to the lady at the front desk. | 'We need some assistance bitch!' | The lady turns her chair around and continues her conversation. Sheeca about to jump over the counter and beat her ass, Buuday turns in time to stop her. | 'Sheeca, what the hell are you doing? I know you weren't about to jump over this counter and beat her ass! Let me handle this aight.' | Buuday pushes Sheeca aside and yanks the phone cord out the phone. The lady turns the chair around quickly and sees Buuday with the dull pink looking phone cord in her hand. | 'What the hell you do that fo'? I wuz talkin' on the damn phone bitch!' 'Well if you would have gotten yo chicken head ass off da phone then we wouldn't be going through this shit! Now look ho, we need some damn assistance! I want my muscles to be less tense and Sheeca wants a make-over.' | The lady looks at Sheeca. | 'Damn, she does need a fucking make-over!' | Sheeca launches for her head as Buuday holds her back. The lady at the first desk is up out of her seat demanding Sheeca to come over and get some. Sheeca is calling the girl all out her name with every curse word in the book; flicking her off and trying to grab her. Buuday finally gets Sheeca to calm down as they take a seat and wait to be taken next. After a few minutes a tall dark skin brother walks up to Sheeca and Buuday whose eyes run up and down his body. He is wearing no shirt exposing her oiled down rippling muscles. He has an earring in each nipple, which makes Buuday's heart stop. The smile on his face melts Sheeca's heart as he stares down on them with his nice brown eyes. | 'Hi, my name is Tyrone and I will be doing your rub down.' | After Tyrone strokes his short braided hair back he extends his hand to Buuday. Buuday smoothly shakes his hand and stands up trying to turn him on. | 'Right this way lovely.' | Tyrone directs her to the back leaving Sheeca sitting there all alone. Sheeca then gets up and proceeds to the make-up area pass the front ladies desk. Sheeca gives her a dirty look then sits down in front of a two short Korean ladies. The first lady is in her mid 50's with the wrinkles to prove it. Her eyes are of a blue color and you can tell in her facial features she is Korean. Sheeca notices how perfect her nails are and knows that she is in for something great. The Korean lady has her hair pulled back and tied in a ponytail with one braid hair hanging in front of her face. She wears a beautiful sky blue dress with red flowers on it. The Korean lady leans to the other one who is doing someone else toenails and says something to her in her native tongue. Sheeca looks at them as they talk to one another and glance at Sheeca everyone once in a while. | 'Hold up, hold up. I know ya'll are talking about me! See why ya'll have to clown on me? I just came in her for a simple makeover and ya'll gotta clown on me! Bitch don't make me get on yo ass!' | The two Korean ladies give Sheeca a dirty look as Sheeca leans back. | 'What bitch? We gon do something?' | Sheeca backs out her seat and puts up her fist ready for the two Korean ladies to attack her. But instead they welcome Sheeca back with a grin. At first Sheeca doubts going back but then caves and seats back in the seat. | 'So what you want?' 'I want my nails did, my huuair did, and my toe nails did so I can sport my new sandals.' 'Alright then, lay your head back.' 'Wait how much this gon cost my ass?' 'Two-hundred dollar.' 'What? Bitch you don lost yo mind! What ever happened to the one dolla business ya'll use to be saying?' 'You pay or leave.' | Sheeca looks at her deeply before handing her two hundred dollars. The Korean lady puts it in her pocket and pushes Sheeca's head back into the sink. | 'Damn, you ain't got to get rough wit it!' | The Korean lady immediately begins to work her magic putting in shampoos, conditions, hair die, and all the works. She runs her fingers through her hair making it all soak in. Sheeca is screaming trying to get the Korean lady off her head as it feels like she is trying to rip the hair out her head. Before long it is over and the Korean lady pulls up Sheeca's head and wraps a white towel around her head. | 'I'll be back.' 'No the hell you won't! Yo ass is crazy!' | The Korean lady gets up and leaves. Sheeca sits there trying to recover when she hears screams and slaps. Sheeca directs her attention to the rub down room as more screams and slaps are heard. The slaps die out as Tyrone comes out the room with a cactus leaf. Sheeca stands up and backs up as Tyrone approaches her with the cactus in hand and all. | 'Look foo, you fine and everything but you ain't beating my ass today!' 'It's cactus treatment. It sooths your body.' 'Youz a got damn lie! Look you ain't touching me with no cactus!' 'Alright, have it your way.' | Sheeca slowly puts down her fist as Tyrone grabs a paddle and proceeds back into the rub down room. Sheeca shakes her head then the she sees the Korean lady walking up. At first Sheeca tries to hide but the other Korean lady points her out. Sheeca walks out giving that lady death threats with her eyes as the Korean lady sits her back in the seat and takes off her shoes. | 'Why I got to get the devil lady today?' | The Korean lady pulls out red nail polish and begins to start on her nails. As she is polishing you see the door fly open to the rub down room and Buuday is trying to get out but she is pulled right back in and the doors shut once more. Sheeca just shakes her head and thanks the lord that that is not she. The Korean lady pants toe after toe after toe carefully then grabs Sheeca's hands and places them on the table. | 'Damn woman, jerk me around one more time and I'm straight whooping yo ass!' | The Korean lady also pants Sheeca's long nails red one by one. With Sheeca's hair in curls and a towel wrapped around her head the Korean lady quickly finishes and takes Sheeca all the way to the back where the hair dryers are. She quickly unwrapped the towel from her head and throws her under it and cuts it on. Sheeca sits there in a daze amazed at how fast that was and feeling a little uneasy. Afraid when the psycho lady is coming back. Sheeca looks to the side of her and sees a lady that has just came from the 'rub down' and getting her hair washed she is laying over to the side looking paralyzed with slobber coming out her mouth. Sheeca gulps and looks around with her eyes open wide. Inside the rub down room Buuday is on the table screaming in pain as Tyrone has her in the figure four-leg lock. Tyrone keeps yelling over her screams that it is part of the rub down. Back out in the spa, Sheeca is at the edge of her seat ready to break out running when her hair dryer cuts off and the Korean lady turns around the turner. She grabs Sheeca's arm and takes her back to the front where she curls her hair with a curling iron while the other Korean lady is putting on her make-up. For what seemed like an eternity but were only a couple of minutes the Korean ladies back away and bring Sheeca the mirror. Sheeca pulls the mirror forth and she's how beautiful she looks while the other Korean ladies try to whip the make-up powder dust out the air. The mirror reflects the smooth caramel skin of Sheeca with blood red lipstick. She has light blue eyeliner that gradates for dark to light. Her eyebrows are perfectly plucked her hair is like none others with elegant curls of a god making her look that a movie star. Sheeca is amazed at how well she looks at as a 6'4ft brown skin brother walks in the spa. He has ten platinum silver chains that hang down to his balls and his silver colored hair is back in zigzag corn rolls. You can tell he's a good dresser by the way he wore his black Fubu jacket with his red Fubu jeans that are two times to big for him. Wearing a Phat Albert shirt underneath that jacket and black Lugs boots, lil' Thug looks down at the woman who is hugging him and wonders who is she. Sheeca backs away and looks at lil' Thug. | 'It's me, Sheeca!' | Lil' Thug backs up in amazement. | 'Damn! Now that's what I call southern hospitality! Where's Buuday?' | Just then the doors to the rub down room open and Buuday is wheeled out in a wheel chair. Her head is tilted to the side as slobber falls from her mouth. She looks paralyzed as Sheeca stares at her. Lil' Thug looks at Buuday and back at Tyrone who wheeled her out. | 'Ya'll were pose to give her a rub down! Instead ya'll took her back there and whooped her ass!' 'She will be alright in a couple of minutes. Our rub down will make her feel great.' | Buuday tries to say something but Tyrone talks over her and pats her on the head. Lil' Thug looks at Sheeca. | 'Sheeca you see Buuday?' 'Yeah.' 'Remember this site because Thug iz going to look exactly like this when I'm done with hiz ass.' | Sheeca pushes lil' Thug. | 'How you going to joke at a time like this?' | Sheeca takes another look at Buuday and a slight laugh comes over her. | 'Ok, that's not funny' | She says to her self as she covers her mouth and walks out the spa. |
