Observation of a Fall

14 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/14/03 'I am laughing now. Yes I am. The demon.....by the 'Gods', ho ho, this foul creature as you put it, with his head struck asunder. Yes, while his skull may have been split wide open, rest assured what lies beneath in the recesses of his soul is not fit for the vision of one such as you.' The scene fades in from the black to show the eternally dank abode of Clyde. He sits upon the cold floor, reflecting....we zoom in on his eyes. In the midst of them, in their horrible glow an image dances. That of an Egyptian, rapidly losing his mind. Clyde: I watch you as you fall, Titu. I watch you as you speak to your 'favored weapon', the cold metal with which you so temporarily cracked my bones open with....I watch as this moment passes. The image of the Pharaoh sprawls out before us, along his bed as he rolls beside his blood covered sledgehammer. Clyde: (Contemplating) You desire now.....what do you desire now? To lay with your weapon, to procreate with it.....you worry about whether police officers shall take you away screaming into the night. You blame me for your plight, but in fact it is YOUR doing, Titu.....that is the sad truth of the matter. I am revealing nothing that was not already buried within your own pitiful little mind. In this vision in Clyde's mind, Titu moves away from the sledgehammer and looks on....blank, expressionless, utterly lost. Clyde: (Chuckling) I believe you are right in what you say. As you sit in the darkness that is your own soul, it has all gotten to you. But not me, and certainly not a bunch of silly plagues. Oh no, what has gotten to you is your own mortality. The burden that all such as you suffer.....the fact that you are LIMITED! That you are CREATED that way! And that there is NO escape from this curse! In the demon's eye, Titu stands. He walks across his bedroom and gazes into his mirror, a gold lined reflection of complete and utter despair. Clyde: In this moment you even fail to see yourself. What would you do if you could, Titu? If you could TRULY gaze upon the man you have become, just how far you have fallen? You are a specter, an undead wraith, nothing more than a phantasm....you are something LESS than human. Would you scream? Would you scream for me, Titu? A moment of terrible silence sets in as the Pharaoh continues to look upon himself yet not truly seeing what is there. He turns to the left and to the right, struggling.....and he hangs his head. Clyde: (Laughing softly) And my answer is forthcoming. You scream for me so well.....you scream 'Why? Why have I done this to you? Look at you, look at you!' The monster's laughter rises, growing hysterical as he watches Titu Xansiv erupt into a rage. He laughs, laughs as he slaps various ornaments of ancient Egypt to the floor, and then joins them as he falls to his knees, weeping. Clyde: And you ask, you DARE to ask me what I have done? I? As your tears flow, you STILL deny.....you place the blame on me. When will you accept, Titu? When will you accept that I haven't done ANYTHING to you? Your pain is SELF inflicted! The Pharaoh stands and runs in the direction of his sledgehammer in the view of Clyde's vision. He points at it and bellows almost drunkenly. Clyde: Oh, now what? You stoop to accusing your WEAPON of the crimes you have committed? Honestly, the sheer DEPTH of the denial you have placed yourself in SICKENS me! You are no different than the common cattle I have butchered for AGES....no, you're worse. You disgust me! Titu then picks up the hammer and throws it as hard as he can, denting the door where it strikes through the sheer force of the blow. Clyde: Oh I think you are wrong. I think you do need me, Titu.....you need me as the junkie needs just one more hit of precious heroin to make it through the day. You need the suffering I allow you to bestow upon yourself. The once proud Egyptian tries to put on a brave face, but ultimately sinks his head once again. Clyde: What have you become, you ask? I have your answer. You have become nothing, Xansiv.....you haven't changed a bit. You're the same broken shell of a man you always have been....I have merely removed a few layers of petulance. A few layers of self-denial.....a few layers of lies. It does wonders for the soul, you know. Again the demon laughs at Titu's expense, as he watches him rant and rave about upcoming trials and tribulations to come. Clyde: An unnatural being, you call me? Able to continue this barbaric treatment even after the destruction of my skull? I tell you....I think your hammer speaks the truth. Titu begins biting his nails, his eyes darting to the left and to the right while the dark laughter of Clyde surrounds him. Clyde: And then at last, through the endless parade of drivel spewing forth from your mouth, we begin to reach the root of what you are. A greedy little man, no different than all the rest....you desire gold. You would murder your so called friends for it.....you would kill everyone for it, wouldn't you? The demon smiles, the Pharaoh still wandering inside his pupil. Clyde: Finally you catch on, in your own roundabout way. You should take what you want out of life.....you shouldn't attempt to bury your innermost desires until they consume you and drive you insane. For one brief and terrible instant, the grin on Clyde's face and the grin on Titu's face match. And then the Egyptian moves again, into the hallway of his palace where several of his guards mysteriously lie. He gazes upon them, again not really seeing them at all. Clyde: Madness. Madness indeed, Titu.....madness approaching, like a speeding train while you stand oblivious on the tracks. Your madness has not yet arrived in full, my friend. Clyde smiles once again, his sharp teeth glinting as his eyes slowly fill with unearthly fire. The blaze rises to meet the image of the Pharaoh as he talks to himself, losing his grip ever so rapidly, unknowing and unseeing. Clyde: But I can assure you.....it will. The fire fills the demon's eyes, engulfing a lost soul as we mercifully fade to black......
