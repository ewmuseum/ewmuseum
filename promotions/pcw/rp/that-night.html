That Night...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered (In the residence of Skylar Green; Wednesday, February 5, 8 pm EST) Open in a sparsely furnished room, a bedroom which has been converted into a reading study. The room is split half-by-half symmetrically. On the east side of the room, a great quantity of books on all subjects, written in English. On the west side, the same books in braille form, or books on tape or CD-ROM. In the middle of the room is a lounging couch and a loveseat. A coffee table is next to the loveseat with a simple gold-colored lamp with an off-white lampshade over it. The light flickers on. Sandy Wong sits down at the loveseat with her bottom in the seat, reclining against the right arm of the red upholstery with her legs up over the left arm. She wears a light blue houserobe with white roses and violets forming a figure eight pattern over the vines. She has a 30 pound book in her hands. It's a random selection by HP Lovecraft. The name is rubbed off. A secondhand book, Sandy likes to buy literature from garage sales. She opens to the first page of the first chapter and begins to read. The air, thin with rainy moisture, the cold air breezing through the dark leaves. Thunder claps roll along the hills, producing a sound like a strike against aluminum siding with a hammer or other hard object. The lightning flashes bright, illuminating the prarie for miles around. The mountains in the distance meet the sky in a brilliant contrast of purple-white and gray-brown for but a few fleeting moments before their colors disintegrate and the two separate horizon markers of the heavens and the earth sink into the same empty darkness that made them one. The wind sweeps the ground, stirring up mud to ruin the purity of the cleasing rain that falls, the tears of the gods corrupted by the essentially dirty nature of the corporeal world. All the voices of nature, gone. The birds are quiet, huddled in their nests to weather the tempest. The rabbits have retreated into their holes, safe from the loud storm above that sounds as though the gods themselves were clashing in heated battle. Prarie dogs take shelter under rock formations, hiding from the noise and bright lights that the climate of the ecosystem would consider a rarity. The coyotes and mountain lions stick to their cave habitats and dens in the mountains, biding their time until the storm passes that they might rule supreme over the land, free from the frightening tyranny of the intrusive thunderstorm. The rain gets harder, faster; it's almost sexual. Down it pounds into the ground, dropping from indeterminably high heights above. Each drop producing the rhythmic beating of a drum that could lull the most alert animal to sleep... *Five Hours Later* Sandy:'OKAY! IT WAS DARK AND GLOOMY! FUCK!' A few awkward seconds of silence pass before Skylar pokes his head in through the door, the light meeting his questioning face and brow. His eyes are even whiter with the light shining on him, and his black hair seems to reflect the lamplight. Sky:'Is..... everything alright?' Sandy closes the book with a quiet snicker, closing her eyes and slightly shaking her head from side to side. Sandy:'I'm alright. I didn't wake the kids, did I?' Entering the room, Skylar has on his old University of Miami sweats, an orange sweatshirt and green pants to go with it. He wears some white wool slippers over his size18 feet. Sky:'No, they're sound sleepers. You could yell bloody murder in their ears and they'd probably stay asleep. You... aren't going to try that, are you?' Sandy exhales with a smirk, looking up at Skylar with an equally mocking expression. Sandy:'No, but I did need to bang some pots and pans in the hallway. How're you holding up? That was a rather big match that you were cheated out of back at Fusion.' Skylar sits down across from Sandy, leaning forward on his knees. He sighs and shrugs his shoulders at Sandy's question in reference to Skylar's loss to Totally Tubular Tommy at Fusion when Tommy held onto the ropes to pin him, which the referee wouldn't have stopped because he doesn't believe that Skylar Green is 'black enough' to be treated equally in the enforcement of the rules. He's got a point, Skylar is half-Japanese. But still, rules are rules. As it is, there are maybe 2 or 3 active referees in PCW that would call a match fairly for Skylar. Sky:'It is disappointing, but look at it this way; I can't remember the last time someone's beaten me cleanly. It's like it just doesn't happen anymore. If I could just cut down on these dirty losses now, then hey.' Skylar does a short thumbs up motion. Sky:'That doesn't bother me so much, I can get other shots at the Continental Title. What is starting to bother me is Violence Jack. He's always been a bit of a wackjob, but I don't appreciate how he continues to pester me. I'm trying to get on with my career post-Mason and he decides now, of all times, to get in my way. I don't like how every time I try to get to something like the Continental or World Title, there are guys I've never even dealt with before coming out of the woodwork to get in my way. Jack and I have got no connection whatsoever, despite whatever the little voices inside of his head have to say.' Sandy:'Yeah, that is creepy.' Skylar shakes his head and reclines in the chair, folding his hands behind his head. Skylar:'But Jack will lose interest and there'll be other shots at the Continental Title. And even then, the March Madness tournament has been declared. I beat 2 people to get in last time. This time I need defeat only one opponent, Adam Stryker.' Sandy:'He's from DWO. Apparently he just left them a week ago. Was their World Champion, if I'm not mistaken.' Sky:'Well, considering that I summarily crushed the last DWO World Champion that I had to face, I think history is on my side in this case. History always repeats itself. Rush Mason is an unfortunate example of that. Not that I like effectively retiring anyone, but he proposed the match and I wasn't going to stand idly by and let him beat me. Hey, who was the last DWO guy I had to face?' Sandy Wong scratches her head, a habit she has when trying to remember something. Sandy:'Umm...... I think it was Rich Rollins.' Skylar shakes his head and rubs his neck, something more from memory than anything else. Sky:'No... he was the guy that stalked me after I beat him in the Global Title Tournament (the event which somehow crowned a Universal Champion).' Sandy:'But it was the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament.' Skylar brushes off the reply with a roll of his wrists. Sky:'Meh, new name, same thing. They just decided to change the name since Hessian killed Vampir and couldn't get a finals match with Akiyama for the GTT2 crown. Both belts are rusting in a closet somewhere.' Sandy:'That's it. Jackson Rust.' Sky:'The DWO guy?' Nodding her head, Sandy affirms. Sandy:'Yes. You beat him before Rich Rollins to win your bracket in the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. Stryker's a different animal, comparable to you in size and technique. But you would focus more on high-flying and martial arts maneuvers than he would against you. That might give you the edge in beating him.' Skylar nods his head. Sky:'I'll give Stryker the benefit of the doubt in considering him a viable threat in a match. It'll be up to him not to prove me wrong.' Sandy yawns. Sky:'You should sleep...' Sandy shakes her head and loosens the cloth belt on her robe, opening it up slightly. Her pale skin is made lighter by the lamp. She wears nothing under the robe save for a white bra & panties ensemble. Sandy:'I'm fine. You're the one who has to get up in the morning and train for your 8-man tag in two days. And your match with Adam Stryker. I can stay up til 9am if I want to. I wouldn't mind some sleep, though.' Sandy stands up and completely opens her robe. Her body is full, not chubby, just retaining some baby fat. She's 5'9' and weighs 140 pounds. She steps over to Skylar and puts her hands on his face, gently pushing him back into the couch, mounting him over his legs. Sandy:'Why don't you help me with that?' *End*
