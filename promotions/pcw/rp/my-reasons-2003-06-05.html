My Reasons

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/06/03 Tuesday Turmoil Tuesday, June 10th, 2003 Live on your TV They call it the biggest show on Primetime. The largest spectacle in this business. The biggest night of the week. This is it… You would think that’s exactly what I’d be thinking about right now… I wish it were that simple… -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The last few months have brought me to this point. Five years of hard work, determination and overcoming the odds have made me the man I am today. Hell to be honest… the last twenty-two years… every day of my life… has brought me to where I stand today. To make it this far… and to be among the greats that headline such an event… is an accomplishment in itself. To stand in the spot I currently occupy is a dream even I thought would never come true. Yet here I am. They kicked me when I was down. They threw cheap shots every chance they had. They used every weapon within reach. They did all one could possibly imagine. They used politics against me. They tried to rid the wrestling world of Manuel Labor. They tried to hold me down. What they didn’t realize… what nobody seemed to be able to grasp… is quite a simple concept… You can’t hold me down. You can’t hold me back. You can’t stop fate. You CAN knock me down. But I WILL get back up! Some would say that I rise to the occasion. Throw adversity my way, and I’ll be sure to overcome. To some… that’s more than a belief. It’s a harsh and bitter reality. But it’s reality none the less. There are many individuals whose names may or may not be familiar to the PCW roster and executive… who hear the name of Manuel Laboe and nod their head in acceptance… and in respect. “One of a kind” is an expression I’ve been lucky to hear over the past few years. There are men and women who have walked by my side for nearly four years now… who would look me dead in the eyes and tell me… I’ve done it all. I’ve done it all, they’d say. Maybe they’re right. Maybe I have done it all. But no… they’re wrong. You see… as far as I’m concerned, there is ALWAYS something more to accomplish! THIS is the big league! This is where Manuel Labor has come into his own and proved to the world that he is beyond a shadow of a doubt one of the greatest symbols this industry has ever seen! Respect? Check. I’d like to believe I’ve accomplished that task. To me, this is just important as any other goal. But what about… the PCW Extreme Title? Standing not just AMONG the very best… but BEING the very best, and reaching the top of that proverbial ladder? Legendary status within PCW ? PCW Hall of fame? It seems that I haven’t really done it all. But will I? Who knows... I’ll give it everything I have… and I’d like to believe I'll do it all… or I’ll die trying… -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- This is what I should be thinking about right now. These are the thoughts that should be running through my mind right now. They need to be. Green. Windsor-Green. I need to be thinking about them. Don’t get me wrong, I’ve got confidence. I know my time is coming. But one makes a mistake when he becomes too over confident. An ego… an ego is a good thing. You need an ego to make it anywhere in this business. But there’s a distinct difference between having an ego… and being a primedona! This is the area where people like Tessa Windsor and I can stand proudly and go about our business with integrity. Gaz… well… The Jester is an ego freak. But that’s just him. It doesn’t change the fact that he’s a multi time Tag champion and Current Continental Champion, the second most powerful man in this federation. A prediction? Not really. Ego? Nah. Confidence? No, I wouldn’t even say that. In reality… it’s all we’ll accept. I’m here for the same reason I always dreamed of making it this far. It’s the same reason I sat in the bosses office eight months ago, not giving a damn how much I’d be paid, or if I’d be paid at all. It’s the same reason I signed that contract and haven’t looked back since. It’s the same reason I walked into the PCW nearly 2 months ago. It’s the same reason I broke into this business as a teenager. It’s what I do. It's what I love... I’m hungry. I sat long enough. Without this… life is incomplete. This is what I live for. I’ve got something to prove to the world. I’ve got something to prove to myself. That may not make me the most dangerous man in the PCW. But it gives me something to fight for. And I won’t give it up.
