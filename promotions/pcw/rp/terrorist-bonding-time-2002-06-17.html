Terrorist Bonding Time

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/17/02 The camera fades in… “I WANT DIS ONE!” “I WANT DAT ONE!” “OH OH DIS ONE IS GOOD!!!” The Chef takes his adopted son Manuel Hung through the aisles of a Toys R’ Us store in downtown Washington. The two tactical terrorists glance at plastic packages full of dolls, action figures, video games, remote control vehicles, and sports equipment. The Chef: Now Manuel, Allah doesn’t provide me with all the money in the world… The Chef looks up at the ceiling as if he were daring Allah to shower money on him at that very moment. Manuel: I know Chef, but I’ve don’t have no toys… The Chef: What about your gym bag? Manuel: That not toy daddy… The Chef: Yeah, but every once and a while I crave some up that hip zipper music! Manuel just rolls his eyes at his “uncool” father. The Chef: Geez, my kid is two and I’m already not “with it”… Manuel: Just give me a few minutes ok daddy? The Chef: OK Manuel, but please hurry! Parshin and I have a match tonight and then on Friday night against those corrupt fat cats of Politically Incorrect… Manuel: Wow daddy, are you actually mentioning your opponent’s names in your promo? The Chef: Yeah I know, I rarely do it, I guess my handler just likes the comedic aspect of my promos better…go figure… I’m just very keen on getting those Tag Team Titles back for a third time and becoming the greatest PCW Tag Team wrestler of all time! Manuel: Can I go now daddy? The Chef: Sure, knock yourself out… The Chef lets go of Manuel’s hand and lets the little tyke roam the store on his own. The Chef then decides to head back to the front of the store to await Manuel’s decision. The Chef: Easy Bake Ovens…Basketballs…Diva Girls!?!? Bah!!! Give me violent destructive toys any day!!! Suddenly as the Chef glances into one aisle, he spots the toy of his dreams… The Chef: But of course!!! It is you!!! The Chef frolics over to the aisle like a young lad skipping to school. He picks up a box with a plastic covering and looks at the colorful text printing on the front. “Timmy Terrorist” The Chef: I can’t believe it! They have a Timmy Terrorist doll! Praise be to Allah! The doll is similar to the Buddy Lee doll, only garbed in camouflage gear with the usual military supplies. The doll also sports a wired TNT pack, ready to detonate upon the owner’s command for a successful suicide bombing operation. The Chef turns the box around and reads the information on the backside. “All proceeds from the purchase of this doll go directly to the Al Qaida network”. The Chef holds up Osama Bin Laden’s Discover Card, and nods indifferently. The Chef: Well, he’d at least be getting his money back! The Chef notices that there is a cord attached to a string sticking out of the back of the box. The Chef: Oh my! I bet even Mohammed himself is salivating over this!!! The Chef pulls the cord and listens… “Hating America can be cute!” The Chef pulls the cord again… “I hate you…” The Chef pulls the cord again… “Make bunker time fun: read a bomb-making book!” The Chef is overcome with excitement, he looks around the store, hoping to find Manuel and give him his new toy. The Chef: Manuel!!! Manuel!!! Come quickly I’ve found your… The Chef stops in front of an aisle and notices Manuel sitting on the ground playing with something. He slowly steps forward towards Manuel, ready to surprise him with the Timmy Terrorist doll… The Chef: Manuel, look what Allah has blessed us with! Manuel suddenly turns around holding the worst thing a militant Islamic father could find his son playing with… The Chef: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! Manuel holds up the toy… DUN! DUN! DUNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNN!!! Manuel: Do you like my Colin Powell doll? The Chef instantly faints upon hearing Manuel’s question. ………………. ………………. Wake up Daddy! ………………. ……………… What? ……………… Wake up! ……………… The Chef: Ok Ok, I’m up I’m up… The Chef opens his eyes and sees Manuel playing with a plush Colin Powell doll. The Secretary of State doll is clad in an authentic important-looking suit and holds United Nations papers in one hand and pen in the other. Manuel: Isn’t it cool how he regulates and dispenses justice throughout the world dad??? The Chef: (Frowning) I guess so…but Allah dispenses justice too! Manuel: Yeah, but Powell isn’t a figment of someone’s imagination… UH OH!!! The Chef: Ok, Ok, I’ll ignore the fact that you are talking like an educated adult just for the purpose of not straining my handler’s wrists from the constant correcting and backspacing he must do, but I cannot ignore your blasphemy! That’s rude, crude, and unacceptable!!! Manuel turns to his father, and a tear falls from his eye. Manuel: I just wanted to make you proud daddy… The Chef suddenly realizes he’s gone over the edge. The Chef: I’m sorry son; I didn’t mean to shout at you like that…far be it from me to judge a man by his religious beliefs… Manuel: But didn’t Juan Borro leave I2 because he was a… The Chef: (Interrupting) I said, “Far be it” Manuel… Manuel: OK daddy… The Chef runs to Manuel and embraces his adopted son with all the love of a natural father. The Chef: Ok Manuel, let’s go purchase you’re doll… Manuel: I didn’t want to buy it daddy… The Chef suddenly is intrigued by Manuel’s tone. The Chef: Then what were you going to…OHHHHHH!!! The Chef hands Manuel the Timmy Terrorist doll. Manuel rips open the box and removes the TNT strapped to Timmy. He then places the TNT on the Colin Powell doll. The Chef: Someday son, you’ll be a great terrorist… Manuel: Daddy, that day is now… Manuel places the Colin Powell dolls inside a giant Lego Castle on display in the Lego Aisle. He picks up the detonation controller and prepares literally bring the house down… Manuel: Ready daddy? The Chef: I’m ready son… The Chef hides the controller inside his military garb and holds Manuel’s hand as they both exit out the building. They both stop in front of the parking lot and turn around to face the store. The Chef pulls the controller out of his outfit and hands it to Manuel, who eagerly begins the count. Manuel: 3……..2……. The Chef: Wait!!! Let’s say it together! The Chef puts Manuel on his shoulder and the duo shouts in unison… 3!!! 2!!! 1!!! The Chef and Manuel smile at each other as the tyke presses the button on the detonation controller. The camera fades to black…
