The Refrain of SOCIETY

2 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 2, 2003 Begin transmission. (For the first time, SOCIETY is alone, without his confidante, Michael Chang. The picture of conformity is seated, looking at a television set, the channel focused on MTV. Music videos of all shapes, sounds, and sizes play, as SOCIETY’s usually impassive face begins to smile…) “You know, I don’t know if I entirely agree with President Chang sometimes.” (SOCIETY…with independent thought? This doesn’t seem to compute.) “I have been taught that music is evil, that it is a reflection upon society. But, music in and of itself cannot be evil, yet, it, like all things, is created by imperfect human beings, and therefore, it must be purged, along with the imperfect people who create it. Yet…it is beautiful, in its own way.” (He turns his head to one side, reaching for a remote at his hand, as he reaches over his right shoulder, turning on a stereo, as Beethoven’s Ninth begins to play. The big man looks to be at peace with himself, as he reclines back in his chair.) “The sounds of mankind’s greatest. Not perfect, by any means, but as close as most mortals will ever get to that magical pinnacle of perfection. Which is why it pains me deeply to see this Shane Wethers trying to put on his own musical performance. He is not perfect! On the contrary, he is as nearly imperfect as any I have yet laid eyes upon!” (He shakes his head, banishing Beethoven from the room with a simple press of a button. The TV is banished as well, as SOCIETY sits alone in the dimly lit room.) “My theme music is imperfect as well. It is not even a tune I like especially. But, it was selected by President Chang for the words. “Die..for my philosophy.” Something, I have been told, that the Order of Society and Logic has had to do many times in its history. Many brave members have sacrificed themselves in the noble quest for perfection. It is only now that two have attained it. Chang, and myself.” (The creaking of the chair under SOCIETY’s weight bothers the perfectionist, to the point where he stands, lashing out, kicking the chair with a massive force, sending the chair toppling backwards. The seating device crashes into the ground, and is then silent.) “As that chair works, so too does the silencing of the abnormal. The loudest scream always comes before conviction, as that final act of defiance is the most flagrant. Why would it not be? If you are convicted to die, and your fate is inexorable, why NOT flout your nonconformity? In essence, that is what my three opponents are doing this week. Demonstrating their non-conformity, for what will be the last time.” (SOCIETY grins cruelly, his deep blue eyes gazing straight, unflinching. He flexes his right arm out, and then his left, stretching both arms back and pushing out his massive, tattooed chest.) “Once again, Krikorian returns to challenge me. Thus far, he is the only man to escape the wrath of SOCIETY. The Ahtumoth likely lies broken in a hospital in Lexington. Rich Cannon has been fired from this company after I destroyed his non-conforming body with a well placed steel chair. Ukranian, you must ask yourself if you think you can survive SOCIETY a second time. If so, please, come with everything you have… last time was such a bore!” (Wanting to sit, but realizing he has no chair, SOCIETY sits down across the chair he broke earlier, adjusting his position.) “And then, Shane Wethers.. A waste. A man who preaches his musical talents, and yet, does not seem to possess any. And worse, a man who has been arrested. By being arrested by police, you demonstrate that you have no respect for the rules of the society that you live in. You are thus, a nonconformist. And one who cannot even conform to the simplistic rules common society lays upon him CERTAINLY will not stand up to the scrutiny of the society of SOCIETY! Nor, of course, will he be able to stand to my clubbing blows, and raw power. Although you were victorious once, as I have been, do not underestimate my abilities, Shane. You see, the power of righteousness flows through my veins. You have two choices. You may conform to my ideas, or you may attempt to force your own upon me. For, that is ultimately what any clash is. A battle of ideologies. But I warn you, you would be much better off to conform to what I believe. Any who attempt to make me conform to non-conformity will ultimately be forced to CONFORM! (Standing, SOCIETY glowers down upon the imaginary forms of his opponents, lying broken and ruined in the ring..) “TO!” (The gigantic man makes a fist with one hand, and rams it into his open palm. He repeats the action, and for the final impact, strikes with a hammer fist into his hand..) “SOCIETY!” (Heading for the door, SOCIETY grabs hold of the handle, and delicately opens it, headed for the outside.) End Transmission.
