Divina Natura :: Divine Nature

8 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 8th, 2002 Let's review. In two matches, both of them on the same card, Playaz Inc. have come either close to victory, or actually achieving it, whether some people may doubt it our not. I think to many, Playaz Inc. may be viewed as various pushovers, only there in the winners circle because The Rush has placed them there in the first place. Both men have talent, that's for certain. Although the Syndicate title in RUSH is considered a Rising Star title here in PCW, Quo held it the longest, defending it almost on a weekly basis, and becoming not the speculated 'paper' champion that many thought he would be. And then, you have Dante Jackson, the most successful crossover in entertainment today. You can't put a face upon greatness, only have an incarnation of it. Jackson came to RUSH, from the XWA, from RSW, from PWA to make it to the big time. Jackson even was naturally the show in RUSH, the epitome of being 'the shit', other than Statuz Quo of course. Playaz Inc. dominated in the place, and then they come to PCW - winning their second match and already earning a #1 Contenders shot for the PCW Tag Team titles. And you thought they wouldn't? 'Where is that Quo?' That question is on his mind at the current moment as the scene happens to open up in the seedy and dingiest regionsof Atlanta Georgia, or ATL in short. We can see the various pimps, rappers, and throwback jerseys abundant in the vibrant place. It makes sense that it would be the exact place you could hear Dante Jackson calling out for Statuz Quo, outside of Quo's block, or respective project tenant. Jackson is still himself, even after suffering a few bumps and bruises from Friday night's match. He wears a backwards Nu Era Atlanta Braves jersey, color crimson red, along with a red Burberry T-shirt and matching black jeans. The moonlight over him abundantly can be seen as his shadow glows somewhat on the sidewalk. Upon closer inspection, you can see by the grin on his face that he's having the time of his life right now, trying to holler for his best friend to come down from his building and join him on a little Sunday morning escapade. But right now, he isn't answering. 'Come on Quo, I know you're up there!' The radiant stereo sounds continue throughout the neighborhood as Jackson pushes through the graffiti tagged door. He looks around, finding the main desk pestered with vermin and other types of ill things that shouldn't be mentioned. Jackson shakes his head, before moving upward and going past a few flights of stairs, looking on a small piece of torn notebook paper. '3 1 4' A room number given to him some few months back, when he and Quo first met. He kept it, knowing it would come in handy one day. Looking upon the empty third floor, Dante sighed, before taking a simple step on the floor and a crackhead brushing along side of him, before heading downstairs. 'Hey, get out of here cracky!' Jackson mused. 'Now where is this 314 at anyway?' He questioned, before moving down the hallway and finding the door, most of its gold numbering rubbed off, or taken off by stray bullet holes, during many Crip and Blood gangfights throughout the neighborhood. Knocking twice, Jackson jingles the door handle, before pushing open. There, he happens to find his partner in crime, loading up for the night. 'And just who in the hell are you out to cap tonight?' the Lyrical Messiah questions, bringing a hand to his chin. Quo laughs, 'Whut do it look like boy? Bout to cap sum dem Crip bitches fo fuckin' up my transactions las' night!' 'Right, and they did what?' 'U mean whut dem hoez didn't do! They basically fucked all my shit up!' Quo shouted, clipping up his Uzi and keeping it on the safety. 'One second,' Dante muttered, before pulling something out of his pants. 'Boy, whut the fuck is dat?' Dante sighed, 'Its something for people to understand. Look, we're facing two pasty creepy white dudes for the #1 Contendership Friday, and we don't need to be looking foolish in their eyes. They're fucking jesters! THEY KNOW COMEDY!' 'And?' 'And?' Dante mocked. 'They have more power over you and me!' Quo clicked his teeth, before buckling the gun under his waist. 'Let me get this shit nice an fuckin' tidy. Yo ass wants to fucken let me not speak any kind of muthafuckin' ebonics to dem bitch ass cracka's? Whut fo'?' 'I have a plan.' Dante nodded, before revealing open the small brochure that happens to be entitled, 'Hooked On Not Speaking Ebonics.' Quo eyes the small pamphlet before raising his gun up towards it, causing Dante's eyes to water. 'Guy...put the piece down.' 'Naw boy, fuck dat! U tryna fuck me wit one of dem white teachin' methods!' Quo eyed the pamphlet, leaning the gun towards it. Dante gives a deliberate oh sigh, before nodding to Quo. 'All right look, you want to be heard right?' 'Yeah.' 'And you want to be noticed and not mocked from your words right?' 'Of fuckin course, but I don't give a shit bout dat.' Quo berated, chuckling at the comment. Dante fell with his eyes heavy, shaking them off quickly. 'Look, this will help us against those comedic guys anytime of the week. Understand?' 'Naw.' Quo told him. Dante sighed, 'Well, you can go win a damn Tag title by yourself, if you're going to act like that and shit. All you had to do was to talk proper for only a few goddamn sentences, and you couldn't even do that shit! What the fuck does it take in this bitch for you to act like you fucking care about your fucking job!?' he emphasised on the last bit, his voice getting louder and louder. 'Boy, would you shut up? My hoes second cousin, first husbands, mothers, brothers, niece is in the room right now!' Quo stopped him quickly in his tracks. 'And fine, I'll do it, ONLY FOR THIS WEEK AND THIS WEEK ONLY!' Dante let out a sigh of relief, before picking the pamphlet back up in his hands and flipping it open, beginning to read the pamphlet. Quo snatches it out of his hand however. 'What the hell?' Quo looks up and downward at Jackson, 'I don't need this, and so don't you! We live in the house because that we just don't care where we live.' Dante begins to look around at the apartment Quo happens to be staying in and begins to reels back in complete and utter fear. In one closet happens to be nothing but flossy platinum chains and in the background, an entire army surplus of weaponry at his disposal. Dante's mouth drops to the floor, in sheer shock. 'Damn son, LOOK AT YO CLOSET!' 'Oh, I've been meaning to get that fixed.' Quo wasn't talking about all of the flossy chains, or the guns stocked into the closet, rather a dent of the background he customized into the back of the closet. A small, microscopic crack is found in the base, causing Quo to rush over and fill it in, before moving back to Dante. Dante moves over towards the kitchen, looking down into the window and starring completely down at the concrete. 'Quo, come here!' he motions over to Statuz, who happens to oblige, moving over towards the window himself. 'Damn that hoe got a ass!' he quipped at Jackson, who turns his head. 'Hoe? I wasn't talking about - DAMN LOOK AT THEM TITTIES!' he remarked, focusing his attention towards a girl, standing on the corner a few feet away from the project building, wearing a PVC top with low rising red pants, a small and skimpy black thong appearing. Jackson ogles at the woman, before Quo slaps him out of it. 'Now, what were you talking about again?' Statuz asked him. 'Oh,' he points down as the camera goes down with him as he isn't even in the picture. 'there's a fucking baby standing on the corner!' Quo looks down, before looking at a white person crossing the street, smiling and whistling the tune of 'Working On The Railroad.' Quo yells down at the man, 'BOO!' 'The hell is wrong with you?' the man asked, in his calm, yet seriously high voice. 'Those days are over. Go on, get on with your life!' Dante chuckles, 'Atleast the police aren't barging in blaring out that we robbed the place and stayed here. Hanging pictues of our families everywhere. Saying that we're on PCP and calling it an open and shut case. Then they'd sprinkle crack on us and get the fuck out there.' 'They'd get capped on the spot!' Quo joked, before looking back down at the child on the corner. 'Think we should help lil' man?' 'What? Fuck no! We can't come near him!' Too late, Quo had already stuck his head out of the window and began to yell at the small baby, who didn't even look scared. He had the look of an annoyed person, somewhat like Nezerra when you piss him off, yeah that kind of look. 'Hey baby, baby go home man. It's three o'clock in the morning, what the fuck are you doing up?' 'I'm selling weed biatch!' 'Oh shit!' Quo yelled, before moving out of the window all together. Dante looks at him, before moving his head outside of the window. 'Hey baby, stop selling weed, you've got your whole life ahead of you.' The baby shot both of them the middle finger, 'Fuck you niggas, I got kids to feed.' 'Gawddamn!' the two of them shouted, by that time a police car had come down on the street, finding a crackhead under their wheels, apparently of a sudden hit and run by the police. Not leaving everyone to worry, they sprinkled some crack upon him and he got back up. 'I'll know the next time I'm coming here, I'll expect some crazy shit.' Dante mutters as he closes the window. Its all in logic, as both men prepare for their third PCW matchup, against the already known #1 contenders to Primetime Championship Wrestling's Tag Team Championship belts. Both men know that they have to prove something not only to themselves, but the people that think they happen to get victory by cheating. Friday night, its their biggest test to date as they face P.W.A - Pranks With Attitude. From one step, the pranking they need to move on if they want to win the Tag titles, hopefully. Even if the ghetto or another showing of the Makonie house kills them first.
