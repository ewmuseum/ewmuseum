Tania's Thoughts

13 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/13/03 Tania stares out of the sundeck window-wall, where the ship is soon to take off again for its Friday Fusion destination. You were supposed to stop him... The pool is open. Mark Walhberg and Charlize Theron are chicken-fighting with Seann William Scott and Eliza Dushku. The younger team loses. Theron: Ha! In your face, 'Wrong Turn!' I told you that you couldn't beat the supreme female of 'The Italian Job!' Mark: Once again I prove that I am better than Donnie! Everyone stares at Mark for his pointless comment. After an awkward pause, they all shove him down and go about their business. You're the World Champion. You're supposed to be strong, fast, and skilled enough to keep him from splitting us up. Over at the bar, Matt Damon and Nelly are in a heated debate. They're both already drunk for the early hour. Damon: I say that Roger Moore was the best James Bond! He perfected the Bond wit! Eight movies, more than any other actor. Remember 'Octopussy?' That's right, everyone does! Nelly: Nah, son. Sean Connery is without a doubt the best. Everyone knows it. 'Diamonds are Forever', 'Goldfinger', 'Never Say Never Again'... can't go wrong with him. And it fit, a Scotsman playing a Scottish guy. Damon: James Bond is NOT Scottish! Why did you just stand there and let him take them away? Samuel L. Jackson approaches in Mace Windu outfit, wielding a purple lightsaber. Jackson: Actually, Bond is of Scottish descent. Damon: Who cares what you think? The closest thing you got to a secret agent movie was that 2 hor long Mountain Dew commercial 'XXX'. At least I had 'The Borne Identity' and 'The Truth About Charlie'. Jackson: Shut you ass up, foo cracka! Nelly: Aw, shit. He's goin' 'Shaft' on your no good pasty white motha trucka behind! Screw the legal document crap. Just kick his ass. Pierce Brosnan joins the fray. Brosnan: Personally, I find myself to be a very good bond. The Bond for the new millenium. It doesn't get any better than me. Damon & Nelly: Oh yes it does! If I were as strong as you, I'd have beaten him so hard that he'd apologize for even thinking about breaking us apart. The handlebar-mustached bartender cleans out a glass and makes a suggestion. Bartender: I've always been partial to Timothy Dalton. Brosnan: Are you kidding? He nearly killed the Bond franchise. Nelly: David Niven wasn't exactly the best at it, but Dalton is definitely worse. Bartender: Even worse than George Lazenby? Damon: Dear God, yes. A single tear falls down Timothy Dalton's face. Dalton serves as the bartender. Why didn't you? Justin Timberlake and Eminem arm-wrestle at a table for the juice bar. Eminem: I got you, I got you. Lil' Kim comes up and flashes them. Eminem: Ain't nothin' we haven't seen before. Justin Timberlake prematurely ejaculates, and subsequently loses the arm-wrestling match. Xhibit: So that's how he gets those high notes in those videos. What am I missing? He wants us apart, he has to be gotten rid of. You don't have to kill him, just beat him into submission. What's keeping you from doing that? Is there something else? Something underneath the surface? Tell me what I'm not getting. We could take them back right now. What's wrong? Why aren't you? A steady humming sound. The engines are hit. The airship is about to take off. There, we're going up into the sky now. He can't escape. Just take them back. Please.
