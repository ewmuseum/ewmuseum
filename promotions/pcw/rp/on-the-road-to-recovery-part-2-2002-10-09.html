On the road to recovery (Part 2)

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 9, 2002 The next day, the transition is not seamless from night into day. From a clear night, the clouds rolled in, as did the cold, and precipitation is coming down heavily. When the sun rose on the morn…Gray was the colour of the day. At a nearby public health club, many people are on the machines today, as the weather doesn’t permit much of anything else. Spider, Eddie’s manager, is making small conversation with the woman behind the desk, trying to impress them with his muscles, and his brain power. Spider flexes raising a nineteen inch bicep. The women aren’t impressed. Eddie Mandrake, wearing all black, walks into the club. Spider notices him immediately. “Well, it’s about time you showed up. I’ve been waiting here for the last hour.” “Sorry, man. I kind of slept through my alarm” Eddie excused. “Uh-huh, always making excuses.” Spider said sarcastically. “I’m serious.” “So am I. Come on, let’s start it off. Go get warmed up…Just go with what you normally do with that” Spider instructs. Eddie walks off into the warm-up room. Spider turns back to the women he’s trying to hit on and continues to try to get somewhere. Meanwhile, in the warm up room, Eddie gets into his workout clothing, and begins some stretching, mainly of the legs, and the arms. He contorts himself into numerous different positions, over a span of five minutes, after which he gets up and walks back out onto the main workout area. He sees Spider trying to impress the staff. He walks right up to him… “Hey man, I thought you had a girlfriend?” Eddie Inquires. The woman’s eyes widen, and she slaps him across the face! Spider gets spun around 180 degrees and he holds his jaw. Eddie cracks a small smile, and chuckles. Spider’s eyes widen as he turns to Eddie. “Come on you little bastard, we’re starting with cardio” Spider says with some fury in his voice. He pulls Eddie toward the treadmill. It seems like an ominous structure. “What the hell is this thing? It seems like something that a rat would use” Eddie says. “Of course, since you are a rat, I’d figured this would be perfect. You simply run on this thing, and it will tell you how far you’ve gone, and how long you’ve been on it, okay” Spider instructs. “Please, there is nothing like stealth training during the day. How is thing going to work?” Eddie inquires. “Well…This will accelerate your heart rate. That’s why it’s called…” “OH! I get it now. It’s like me running from the cops after a botched sniper deal.” Eddie Interrupts. “Uh…I guess, just pretend the cops are chasing you. That’ll work.” Spider instructs. “Okay. Get this bitch started!” Eddie jumps onto the machine. Spider hits a few buttons, and the treadmill starts up…Eddie flies right off of it, into another machine. “OW! THAT MOTHER-“ “EASY EDDIE! Get on there and start running…NOW!” Eddie jumps up onto the machine, and begins to run. The setting is on ‘light jog’ and Eddie is coping rather easily. “Spider, what kind of trickery is this? I thought you said that I’d be at the speed where I run from cops” Eddie complains. “I thought you would get used to it first.” “Why?” “Because if you are going to get used to it, then you can either run right off the treadmill, or fly off it into another machine or person.” “Just do it Spider. I don’t care about them.” “You’ll be sorry, but okay.” Spider concludes as he sets the speed higher. The settings now increase from ‘Light Jog’ to ‘Heavy Jog’ to ‘Sprint’, and Eddie keeps pace with the increase of speed. Sweat begins to bead on Eddie’s head as he runs at a sprinting pace. Beep! “What does that mean?” Eddie asks. “You’ve finished your first kilometre.” “Great! (Panting) How many more do I have to go?” “Another nineteen kilometres to go. I’ll come by later to see how you’re doing.” Spider says as he leaves to return to the juice bar. Eddie just stammers as Spider leaves his side to get something. Eddie continues to run on the treadmill, with an increasing frown on his face. A few minutes later Spider returns to Eddie’s treadmill and he is still running, but faster than he was before Spider left to get some juice. Spider looks at the control console. He sees…’100 metre dash’. “HOLY SHIT, EDDIE!” “(Panting)…What?” “Are you fucking crazy? Do you want to kill yourself?” “(Panting)…I Got used…To the settings…” Eddie says exhaustingly “You are almost done…” Beep! Beep! Beep! “Correction, you are done…just stay on for the cool-down period, it’ll only take a minute or two.” Spider instructs. As the cool-down period begins, Eddie’s signs of exhaustion are apparent. Spider looks are the treadmill slows down, and as Eddie slows along with it. Then, the tread moves to ‘soft walk’ mode, and Eddie jumps off. “I haven’t run like that since I blew up that car on Highway 402!” Eddie exclaims. “Eddie, we aren’t finished. Weights are next…Let’s go.” Spider says. “Aw, man!” To be continued…
