And now, there’s nothing left to do but be alone. His hair is stained with blood that still trickles from the wounds broken in the back of his head -somewhere beyond this tiny cell, there are three Correctional Officers clean his blood from theirnight sticks. The left side of his blond beard is growing annoyingly stiff as the blood that soaksit begins to dry. He wonders what happened to Jim Geraldson, the ogre of a man that he beat mercilessly notthirty minutes ago. He remembers little of the incident now - he doesn’t know when theCorrectional Officers came on the scene. In the hole, a minute seems like an hour. An hour seems like a day. Being aware of this,however, can remove time completely from the hole. One enters the hole through the front door- the birth canal. And then, when leaving, the same door opens, letting in a large blast of whitelight that one might enter on his final day on the planet. In between, a lifetime passes... Monday, September 23 - Day 81 “Writing fucking poetry?” Tim McDonald snickers as he enters his cell to find his cellmate,Vincent Giovanni, writing in a notebook. “I thought only faggots and that crazy cock-bitingfreak were into that shit.” “Just passing the time,” smiles Giovanni as he slides the notebook under his bunk. “For fuck’s sake,” chuckles Tim as he climbs up onto his upper bunk. “Built like a fucking bulland you spend your time ‘toiling with the arts.’ You should put that body to good use.” “What do you mean?” “Oh, come on,” Tim says, leaning over his upper bunk to peer down at his husky cellmate. “Don’t tell me you haven’t busted a couple of heads in your day. Tim rolls back over and stares up at the dirty ceiling, happy to have some time to relax after themorning’s ‘business’. Running the prison mob isn’t as easy as he had thought it would be, butstill, it’s a welcome responsibility. Dreams of running Hattoni’s real family on theoutside fill his head as he closes his eyes. “So tell me, kid,” Giovanni interrupts Tim’s day dreaming, “what happened to that biker thatyou had beaten yesterday?” “Infirmary,” shrugs Tim. “He’ll learn to pay for his weed from now on. Fat cocksucker.” “Who was the guy that beat him up?” “Guy named Jason Snow,” answers Tim. “Fucking celebrity on the outside. They let him outtwice a week for wrestling shows.” “Jesus,” Vincent gasps, “you’ve got a fucking pro wrestler working for you.” “He doesn’t work for me,” says Tim. “Not any more. I mean, you’re Scottish bydescent, but I thought you were the only exception.” “Hattoni was a dumb-fuck at times,” shrugs Tim. “Not like all the legends say he was. Hescrewed up his fair share of times. That’s why he needed Snow, really. For the last coupleweeks of the fat fucker’s life, Jason Snow was the only thing keeping those Nazi motherfuckersfrom slicing his throat.” “So Snow was like a body guard?” “Yeah,” Tim nods, “but like I said, he’s fucking unpredictable. If you cross him, he’ll breakyour God damn neck. Hattoni knew that, but he hired him anyway.” “Are you saying...” Vincent hesitates. “Jason Snow killed Doug Hattoni?” Tim McDonald is silent for a moment before he rolls over to glare down at Vincent’s curiousface. “I didn’t say fuck all about who killed Doug Hattoni,” he snaps. “And what’s with all yourfucking questions any way?” “Just curious,” shrugs Giovanni. “I mean, I don’t think there’s an Italian alive who hasn’t heardof Doug Hattoni. It’s just curiosity.” “Yeah,” Tim says sharply. “Well, curiosity killed the cat. Hard to say what it’ll do to fatfucking guineas.” Wednesday, September 25 - Day 83 “Where’s your fucking psychotic cellmate?” asks one-eyed Nazi leader, Harvey Blake, as hetakes a seat on Leigh Donovan’s bottom bunk of the cell he shares with Jason Snow. “They brought him out of the hole yesterday morning to take him to the wrestling show,”answers Leigh in his thick Australian accent. “He ain’t back yet. What the fuck do you guyswant?” Harvey glances to his left at his right hand man, Jake Hooper. Hooper stands as large as ever,with his ever-growing arms folded over his developed chest. Aryan tattoos cover his youthfulbody. “Got quite the spirit, doesn’t he?” Harvey snickers to Jake, who returns a broad smile. Harveyturns his attention back to Leigh. “We heard what you did to that nigger, J-Thug.” He’sreferring to the day, a week or so back, when Leigh viciously bit the penis of the massive leaderof the Tank’s black gang, the Thugs. “So?” snickers Leigh. “You looking for a bit of the same treatment, mate?” “Easy, son,” says Harvey Blake, extending a calming hand. “We come in peace.
