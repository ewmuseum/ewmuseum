Rolling Cigars on a Virgin’s Thigh

16 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/16/02 Time: 12: 15 P.M Location: CubaScene: Pool “ Rolling cigars on a virgin’s thigh.” The scene opens with Juan Borro at the hotels pool. Him and Kevin have been they’re staying in Cuba for the entire week. Kevin went out to go work out before their big match on Sunday. Juan, unlike his tag partner has little worries. He knows the only thing he has to rely on is his mind set. Juan’s mind has been wandering lately and not for the good. Juan’s act has usually been a class act. He is a nice guy with a big heart, who would do anything for his country. People in the wrestling world referred to Juan as a heel, but he was never really a bad guys, he just fought for what he loved. Two days ago when Juan saw Fidel Castro taking charge and killing that man, he felt a feeling he had never felt before when seeing somebody hurt near him and that was the feeling of satisfaction. It may be an odd feeling, or something that doesn’t make sense right now, but Juan knows his outlook in life has changed. Juan isn’t thinking of love and treating people right, but he is thinking of success. Juan has had success, but he’s never felt God like, something Juan envies to become. The day is early and Juan is taking some sun. Juan is feeling strong and powerful today, almost as if he’s on top of the world like Fidel. Juan spots a man across the pool rolling up a cigar. Juan can smell the fumes, which draw him, closer to the man who rolled the initial cigar. Juan finds himself right in front of the man staring him straight in the eyes. The man looks back at Juan and puffs smoke in his face, as if Juan means nothing to him. Juan smiles, and out of nowhere Juan finds a knife in his pocket. The knife is quite the beauty, shining all throughout the sunny sky. It’s craving Juan’s fingers. Wanting to be held so badly, that Juan’s hands begin to twitch until it finally it up. Juan takes the knife which guides it’s way right to the man’s neck. The knife is right at the man’s neck when Juan spots. The cigar falls from the man’s mouth in slow motion, until it bounces twice against the ground. Juan puts one finger up to his mouth for the man to not speak. The man stays quiet and Juan smiles. The once rude and inappropriate man now wants pure mercy from Juan. Juan loves the feeling and wants more of it. Juan knows he can have one sack of crap on the earth eliminated with a twitch of a knife. Sweat begins to drip from the head of the nervous man. His eyes show his fear. Juan thinks about how the man was as a young boy, but only laughs come to him. Juan knows he is not a killer, so he takes the knife away. What Juan does know is that he takes things he wants. Juan grabs a cigar from the man’s lap and smiles as if the two men were good friends sharing cigar. Man: Please, don’t hurt me. All I wanted to do was enjoy some cigars. I didn’t want to get into any trouble. Juan takes in the words and remembers the way the frantic man said them to Castro just two days previously. Juan: Why should I have mercy on you? Juan sees himself asking the same question that Fidel asked the frantic man. Juan’s expression and feelings are all the same. Man: I didn’t do anything to you, I swear, I don’t know why you’re acting so crazy to me. Juan smiles once again, as he remembers the words vividly with the frantic mans plea from mercy. Juan: Why must you lie? Juan thinks back to when the man puffed smoke in his face. It may have been just the smallest offence, but Juan needs to set an example. Man: I’m not lying. Juan: Was it not you, who just puffed smoked in the face of a world-class soccer player and God like warrior. Man: I’m sorry I didn’t know. Juan: It’s too bad you didn’t know. Is it really my fault that you didn’t know? Please save me the time. Juan looks at the man and smiles. He grabs the cigar smokes it and puffs the smoke right back in the face of the ungrateful man. Man: Okay, I guess we’re even now. Juan: I don’t thing were even. Juan stares at the man and then looks back at knife he placed on the ground. Juan looks at it, and the man makes a move. He does it quickly and grabs the knife. Juan reacts even quicker by grabbing his cigar and stabbing it in the leg of the man. The man screams in pain, as the hot air is rising from his leg. He drops the knife, and holds his burn mark. He scream loudly, but no one around him seems to hear these screams. Juan takes the cigar and throws it at the man. He then grabs the pack and walks back to his seat. When Juan is walking back, he notices a beautiful young woman sitting in his seat. The woman is probably seventeen to nineteen years of age, but Juan doesn’t seem to mind. Juan approaches the woman until he is right by her side. He looks at her, but she is yet to realize he is there as she is taking in the sun. Juan looks at her beautiful and precise curves. He notices the slightest possibilities of her body and stares them up and down. The woman looks up and finally notices Juan. Woman: Oh excuse me do you want something? Juan: From you yes, but for now no. The woman blushes, as Juan continues. Juan: You do seem to be sitting in my seat though. Woman: Oh, I’m sorry I didn’t know. The woman is about to get up, when Juan steps in the way, insisting for her to sit back down in the seat. Juan: Oh please, get comfortable. Juan smiles at the girl who sits down. She then bends over to grab her drink as Juan checks out her other assets. Woman: So what’s your name? Juan: I’m Juan, but that doesn’t matter right now. Juan sits down near her lap, and she smiles. She looks over and notices the cigars in Juan’s hand. Woman: Ooh I love the taste of cigars. She then licks her lips and the two make eye contact. Juan grabs a cigar and puts it on the woman’s leg. He then rolls it up, going higher and higher up to ladies legs. She takes a puff of the cigar, and Juan bends over her. She then lifts her top when the SCENE TURNS BLACK. ****************************************************** Juan then finds himself waking up from a dream in a cold sweat. He thinks about the dream he just had and wonders why all of these thoughts were in his head. For a second he is scared. For that other second he feels a sick feeling of happiness. Juan thinks about the weeks ahead, the tag title, the cruiserweight title, and everything between. Juan thinks about dominance. Dominance he never felt before. Not just to win, but to brutalize. Scene Fades to Black.
