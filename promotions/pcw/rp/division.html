Title: Division Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: -- Mark Antony and Julius Caesar. Adolf Hitler and Joseph Stalin. What do these two pairs of men have in common? They were partners at one time, and, with a greater prize on the line, they split...creating great conflict. Antony and Caesar split over Rome...leading Mark to ally with Cleopatra of Egypt..and of course, their asp-erations were cut short, indeed. Hitler double-crossed Stalin out of greed, a desire to hold all of Europe..a desire Stalin knew well with his theories on the necesary spread of Communist teachings. Still, there is one business in which an object of desire can seperate friends even faster than in global politics. Yes, you got it right. Professional Wrestling. One half of Politically Incorrect, Reginald P. Lovecraft, pulled the shock of his life when he defeated Gold Patron Meritorious Xavier Kannon for the Primetime Championship Wrestling Extreme Title The battered and bruised 49 year old is ready, looking forward to his match-up teaming with Powell against Snow and Rent a Hero. However, his partner is looking forward to Friday, when he, by Tyler Nelson's edict, gets a shot at the Extreme Title. William S. Powell wants glory. He wants fame. He wants power. HE WANTS THE EXTREME TITLE! But first, he must cooperate with his partner. Allies one night, adversaries the next! Can the Politicians survive the strain? William S. Powell: I'M GOING TO MURDER YOU!!! :A briefcase goes flying across the room. Reginald P. Lovecraft: BRING IT ON! ::Braving the yelling and screaming, Roger Schultz charges into the middle of the fray. He stands, trying to calm the two politicians. The two men continue throwing objects at one another., as Schultz screams loudly.:: Roger Schultz: STOP IT, YOU TWO! THIS IS RIDICULOUS! YOU TWO ARE TAG TEAM PARTNERS! William S. Powell: DAMNIT MAN! I HAVE A SHOT AT THE EXTREME TITLE! THIS TAG MATCH MEANS NOTHING! WE'RE FIGHTING THE KILLIN' VILLAIN AND THE HIRO JOBBING HERO! Reginald V. Lovecraft: Powell's right! We're facing two confused morons. Our victory is assured. Now, I must deal with my partner here! William S. Powell: RIGHT! Roger Schultz: DAMNIT! YOU TWO ARE GIVING INTO THE ULTIMATE AUTHORITY'S PLOY! THEY WANT TO DIVIDE AND CONQUER POLITICALLY INCORRECT, AND YOU TWO ARE GOING TO ALLOW IT TO HAPPEN! William S. Powell: We don't care.....for one night only, we need to tear into each other...to give it our all. It is only then can we go on teaming, I have to know, right now, once and for all, if I am better than him, or if he is better than me. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Snow, Rent-A-Hero, consider yourselves lucky you face a divided Politically Incorrect. For, us divided is the only way you can defeat the flagship of The Political Machine. Consider it a blessing, a gift... ::Lovecraft smiles....Powell steps forward to attack, but Schultz holds him back, as we abruptly fade out.:: ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
