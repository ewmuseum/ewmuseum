Legend of the Groundskeeper.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Later than it should have been. He had the greenest thumb in all the land, able to transform barren patches of dirt into lush, beautifully landscaped lawns of refined taste and elegance… But a mishap involving various lawn chemicals, a hedge trimmer and a freak explosion, resulting in a wound to that thumb, allowing the chemicals unfettered access to the bloodstream… Lying in a rotting compost heap for days, unconscious from the concussive force of the blast, the toxins slowly dissolved into his tissues… The thumb went bad, and spread its now evil green throughout his being… The sun’s rays revived him, finding strength in its warmth. With renewed vigor, he vowed revenge on the man that had caused this to happen. He cursed the day he answered his call and now seeks to destroy the one person that made him… become… become… We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, inside the empty foyer of Stately Steele Manor. He and his noble sidekick, the Sado-Masochist, are busily readying themselves for an afternoon of grueling yard work. Big Al has all manner of garden weaponry strategically attached to his denim overall-style toga, while S&M follows suit, even including a string of bug bombs across his chest. Steele grins and expertly spins a garden hoe into the air and steps into its path, allowing it to sink into a sheath placed across his back. A thick pair of gloves completes the look. Trusted Manager Sue Saksnussum now enters, ear connected to her cell phone. Angrily, she snaps it shut. Sue: “I have been trying for the past three days to get a hold of that lawn jockey you hired, but no one answers his phone! Who runs a business like that?!” Al gets a faraway look, taking in a deep breath. Big Alan Steele: (dark tones) “He cannot answer the phone, for he is already here… I can feel his presence…” S&M: “Man, I hate weeding…” BAS: (booming) “Come come Lord of Pain! Tis not merely a simple day in the sun pulling at invasive and detrimental foliage, but a true battle against the malignant forces of nature that have come calling literally to our front door! No test of courage and strength can be greater than the test of mettle of Man versus Nature’s fury! Hi-Keeba!” The ‘Hi-Keeba’ is punctuated with a lightning fast swing of the garden hoe, reaping a nearby (and browning) Philodendron in twain. Sue frowns at the mess and turns to Big Al. Sue: “Yeah, well keep your battles outside! And try and get this done today, okay? The furniture company won’t deliver here with the lawn so bad and we’ve lost no less than three Chinese delivery boys out there. If I don’t get the smell of Shawn Prodigy out of this house ASAP, I’ll just light the place on fire and move. At least then I’ll get some smoke…” BAS: (growling) “No cigarettes! You know the decree! Your habit has already sucked too many years from your fragile life strand! Now stand aside, as there is much work to be done!” Sue: “Okay, okay. Here. This is a map to the garage. If you get there, you’ll be able to get at the lawn tractor, which should really turn the tide and make things easier for you guys. However, aerial reconnaissance reports that there is no clear path anymore, as it’s now overrun by plant life. But if you follow this route, it’s your best bet.” S&M: “Aerial reconnaissance?” Sue: “Of sorts. I sent Tammi up to the roof. She’s still up there with a walkie talkie in case you guys need anything.” BAS: (thoughtfully) “Perhaps I should instead train for my match against the noted footballer, Juan Borro…” Slight strained pause before the three burst out in laughter. Sue gives Big Al a mirthful shove towards the door. Sue: “I see that vacation loosened you up a bit. Now get out there and mow that lawn.” BAS: (smiling) “Hi-Keeba!” The front door is opened, and the two exit into the jungle before them. A strange silence ensues, with a ragged breathing heard over the foliage. The forest pulses with a strange and malevolent power… S&M: “Big Al!! Look!” The Sado-Masochist points backwards to where the just emerged, but all visibility of the house is gone. The jungle has swarmed behind them and put up a wall of thick-vined thorns dripping with poison. Big Alan Steele stands tall and slowly turns about, his face a mask of grim countenance. BAS: “Great Caesar’s Ghost! One cannot even begin to find their bearings in such a ever changing environ!” S&M: (clutching at a weed whacker) “I don’t like the looks of this Big guy… I mean, the forest looks like it’s MOVING…” BAS: (raising a steadying hand) “Fear not, oh Pained One! We shall not fail in our divine mission to restore this yard to its once eye-pleasing origins, and forever disperse this land value detracting vegetation to the four winds! Lock and load!” Big Al whips out his garden hoe and twirls it around in a manner that would make a ninja tear with delight. S&M pulls back hard on his whacker, the single-stroke engine roaring to life, billowing out acrid blue smoke. He spins it around and poses dramatically, the spinning edge at the forefront. BAS: “HI-KEEBA~!” With that declaration, Big Al slices into the forestation, ripping out huge chunks of green and splattering himself with chlorophyll with each powerful swing of the hoe. The Sado-Masochist lowers a pair of safety goggles (for safety) and guns the engine, the enhanced edge of the weed whacker effectively cutting large swaths of vegetation. The atmosphere of the jungle changes, as small screams fill the air and an acidic scent begins filtering through the leaves. S&M: (yelling above engine) “Hey Big Al, what’s going on here?!?!” BAS: (grunting) “Cease and desist!” The two stop their hacking and look ahead. Miraculously, the evil forest is mending itself right before their very eyes. The ripped up plants behind them are rotting away as if in a time-elapsed photo. The screams continue, and the sky is turning crimson. BAS: (confused) “What manner of depravity be this?! What druid magic could cause these plants to behave in such a manner?” S&M: (rubbing arms nervously) “I don’t know, man. Something just isn’t right out here…” BAS: “The oppressive yoke of evil pervades this garden of sin. We must not believe to take on the forest itself, but find its source of power and destroy yon generator. Therefore, moving quickly as we slash and burn through the forest before it can catch up to our persons.” S&M: “Uh, okay. You first, heh.” Steele gives his partner a patronizing look before stepping to the front. Taking a deep breath, he rears back and begins spinning his garden hoe in concurrent vicious arcs, making figure-eight motions that sweep a path through the jungle plant life. Quickly the two hurry forward through the path that opens up then swallowing them whole into the belly of the jungle… High over the ceiling of the jungle, perched atop a single towering tree rising above the jungle of a lawn below, man with a flowing shock of orange hair. Lightning streaks down from the blood sky, revealing green skin beneath tattered, tan clothing and a patch reading ‘Chem Lawn’ on the shoulder. Hands balled into fists slowly relax, lengthening talons. A spiked and toothy grin crossed his face as he makes a motion towards the jungle below… … and a clearing opens up to a surprised Big Alan Steele, who continues with a follow through of the hoe. The small field is surprisingly quiet, an inviting birdbath situated in the middle. S&M steps in behind him, wrinkling his nose in disgust. S&M: (holding nose) “Sweet monkey Jesus! What on God’s green Earth is that horrible stench?!” Big Al holds up a hand for silence, his eyes narrowed and slowly scanning the surrounding area. Deliberately, he puts a straight arm in front of the Sado-Masochist and slowly backs him up towards the birdbath, circling around, backs to each other. When they get to the concrete structure, Steele tenses, balancing the garden hoe in his hands. S&M, used to seeing this sort of reaction from his boss, also tenses and fires up the weed whacker, a line of sweat running down the side of his face. BAS: “The smell of death.” The man above cackles, unheard by our heroes, and clenches his fists in anticipation… Steele cuts the hoe upward, slicing something in half as the sides of the clearing burst forward with life! The Sado-Masochist jambs the whacker into the midsection of something huge and made of rotting vegetation, but it merely gets bogged down in its midsection. A pseudopodia of compost swings around to take off his head, when the garden hoe of Steele rips it from its body. More and more explode from the jungle into the clearing, leaping towards our heroes. Steele: (swinging and yelling) “Switch to a more cutting weapon! Their composition can absorb straight blows!” S&M rolls to the side and reaches back swinging forward a hedge trimmer. With a malicious gleam in his eye, he swings wildly at the approaching horde, sending large chunks of unmentionable material flying. Hacking and slashing, the two, manage to dice and slice the would-be rotted vegetable assassins, strewing their material across the field. The components still twitch and flop on the ground, as our heroes take a moment to wash the slimy ichor from their bodies in the birdbath. Steele cups his hands and splashes water onto his face, rubbing the grime away, when S&M eagerly tugs at his toga. S&M: (in horrified tones) “Uh… Al? We’ve got a slight problem here…” Big Al turns and his eyes open wide at the sight before him. The various mounds of plant matter have begun to reassemble, but this time into a few larger creatures. The rotted plants slither across the ground and are absorbed into huge monstrosities of decaying compost, three in all which rise up and blot out the sun, huge maws opened up in silent screams of rage. Steele backs up, spinning the hoe into a more defensive position. S&M also backs up, notably behind the Big guy. S&M: (panicking) “What are we gonna do; WHAT ARE WE GONNA DO BIG AL?!?!” BAS: (turning head slightly to S&M) “By the gods, be calm yourself! I ask you, what is the major aspect of these towering automatons of garden decay?! And answer quickly before they descend upon us!!” S&M: (stuttering) “They look like huge compost heaps, so I guess a lot of rotted vegetables and… ugh… shit.” BAS: “Correct. And what do children do with small bags of fecal material on the steps on enfeebled and incontinent old men for pleasure?” A light goes on over the head of the Sado-Masochist and he allows himself a small smile. S&M: “I gotcha, Big guy! But, how do we burn them up?” Big Al merely reaches back and awkwardly taps at one of the bug bombs strapped to S&M’s chest. S&M turns it over and reads the back label: CAUTION! Keep away from flame – contents extremely flammable. And with that, Big Alan Steele swings his garden hoe forward and lets loose with his battle cry, Hi-Keeba, and charges into battle, shucking and ducking the three towering piles of vegetation, occasionally ripping a gash from a ‘leg’ or an ‘arm.’ S&M: (calling after BAS) “But… but how do I use this stuff?!” BAS: (in the midst of battle) “Damn your hide to Athena!! MAKE IT WORK!!” The Sado-Masochist frowns and busies himself with the canisters and his Zippo, trying to figure out a serviceable plan. Meanwhile, Big Al miscalculates a swing and the blade end of the hoe gets lodged in the side of a rotting veggie-monster. Big Al tugs, but it refuses to dislodge and the monster swings downward, snapping the long handle like a twig. Steele grimaces and gets crushed with a blow from one of the other monsters, sending him spinning through the air and sliding across the grass, face first. Quickly, he rolls forward, narrowly missing a downward slam from the ‘fist’ of one of the creatures. Reaching down into his toga, he produces a pair of small spades, holding them out like a knife fighter. S&M: “I think I got it Al!” BAS: “Then get at it post haste!!” A creature swings at Steele, who rolls into the creature and jumps up, digging the spades into its façade. Using the spades as leverage, he flips himself upwards, avoiding the monsters pseudopodia and perches on its head area. He then leaps, just as another veggie sends a screaming fist into the head of the last, the powerful blow exploding a huge crater where the top part once was. The wriggling pieces of the monster fall and flop on the floor of the field as Big Al hustles away to plan a new attack. The Sado-Masochist has wrapped a rag around a stick and holds out his bug bomb at arm’s length along with his lighted Zippo. Flicking his thumb, he pulls the pin on the bomb… S&M: “AAAAAAAAAIEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!” A huge ball of flame engulfs S&M and he runs screaming around the clearing, the upper half of his body a bright red-orange fireball. BAS: (wide grin) “Tis the spirit! Hi-Keeba!!” Steele dives at S&M, grabbing him by the ankles and whips him hard into an advancing monster. With a whoosh of flame, the beast is instantly engulfed in fire, wildly whipping around its limbs in a desperate attempt to put out the flames. Steele then quickly ignites the other two veggie piles as they try to escape. Three huge bonfires are set off into the sky as the screaming Sado-Masochist is dropped to the ground and doused with the water in the birdbath as he rolls with a sizzle. Thick black smoke billows into the air from the compost heaps as Steele slumps to the ground in an attempt to catch his breath while S&M groans in pain, his leather still bubbling from the intense heat. S&M: (whining) “I absolutely HATE yard work.” BAS: (grin) “True, but at least we shant have to rake up afterwards!” S&M: “What, are ya gonna hire someone? Because that worked out soooooo well last time…” BAS: (not paying attention) “Hush.” Big Al stands, his head cocked to an angle. With a decisive motion, he looks towards the Sado-Masochist. BAS: “Come. Much work is to be done.” S&M: “But… but I haven’t even stopped broiling yet! My skin is extra crispy here!” BAS: (haughtily) “I thought twas you that enjoyed the nocioceptic endorphins associated with painful stimuli.” S&M: (under breath) “It’s not the pain, it’s the ever nearing hand of death I don’t enjoy…” BAS: “Come NOW, for we must be prepared when they arrive!” S&M: “They? Who?” The green man in the great tree throws back his head and howls in anger. Making a grand gesture indicating ‘swarm’, he wildly gesticulates towards the jungle, wherein Big Alan Steele is now hurriedly cutting a wide swath. Steele has taken the hedge trimmers and swings them with pinpoint precision, dragging along a complaining S&M behind him. Every once in a while, Big Al stops and looks behind him, listening to the jungle; tasting the air. A small path opens up before them, huge pod-like plants with pink leaves underneath lining the sides. A tight smile indicates Big Al was ready for this. S&M: “What is going on? What the Hell are they?! Who the heck is following us?!? C’mon Al! Let me in on it!!” BAS: (indicating to be quiet, whispering) “Do you not hear it? Can you not feel the vibrations? Huge, lumbering beasts, about 100 meters behind…” S&M: (yelping) “Yikes!” A few of the closer plants rumble a bit, large, gaping maws slightly opening at the intrusion, sticky ooze running down jagged ‘teeth.’ The Sado-Masochist clamps a hand over his blistered lips as a frown from Big Al admonishes him. BAS: (finger to lips) “Shhhh! Silence is paramount here! These plants… (gestures to the pods) can devour a man whole, quickly delivering him to the Elysian Fields forever hence! But they can only react to sound and vibrations, so make not a sound, and tread most softly! Understand?” S&M nods quickly, hand still clamped over his mouth. S&M: (garbled) “Mphgfgah!” Big Alan Steele mutters an oath to Zeus before ripping the hand of the Sado-Masochist from his own mouth. S&M: “How do you know all this?” BAS: (guiltily) “Well, we had ordered Chinese before, and witnessed the demise of their delivery man. I could do nothing to save him!” S&M: “How long ago was that?” BAS: “Approximately an hour ago.” S&M: “Damn! Then the plants’ll be hungry again. (deep breath) Okay, I’m ready. You go first.” Big Al makes an imaginary line down the path and tightropes his way down, tiptoeing past the sleeping plants. The Sado-Masochist follows close behind, both careful not to step on a twig and to avoid anything which could possibly trip them up. Steele keeps looking behind him nervously as a low rumble can be felt. The man eating Venus traps are slowly becoming aroused. BAS: (hissing behind him) “We must go quicker! Though they are slow, I fear they gain on us in this slowed state!” S&M: (whispering) “You never told me who before…” They have reached just about halfway when the huge, lumbering forms make their appearance at the entrance to the path. BAS: (yelling) “By the gods! Them!” Steele picks up S&M and hurls him to the other side of the pathway into a stinging picker bush. At the opposite end, a cluster of evil and moving trees groan and shudder, shaking the earth as they tread. Vines shoot out towards Steele who leaps high to avoid being caught… and is enveloped by a man-eating Venus trap! S&M: “Big Al! Nooooooooooooooooooooo!!!” S&M starts to move forward, but leaps back to avoid himself being entrapped in a snapping plant. Face tear-streaked with grief, his head turns this way and that looking for a way to save his employer and crime fighting partner. The pod that has captured Big Al looks like it swallows, a large bulge in the lower portions of the plant. Moments seem like hours. Suddenly, the unmistakable shearing sound of a hedge trimmer in action as the blade protrudes from the belly of the Venus pod. BAS: “Hi-Keeba!!” With a 360 degree spin, the entire stem of the plant is severed, the top heavy portion careening into the center of the path. Sticky with plant juices, a weary Big Al leaps aside as another plants snaps at his location, taking a huge chuck out of the remaining stem. Meanwhile, the trees have continued their methodical advance towards Al, who is now moving and spinning and shucking and jiving just to remain outside the belly of yet another Venus trap. S&M is growing impatient and increasingly more desperate as he sees Big Al narrowly miss the jaws of the plants each time. Finally, he decides to help, when Steele yells over. BAS: (huffing) “Do not, Lord of Pain! Tis under control!!” As he falls backwards to avoid another barrage of shooting vines, and rolling as a pod grabs a face full of dirt for its dining pleasure. The Sado-Masochist growls in frustration, as the trees have surrounded Big Al. Steele howls and slams a shoulder into a living tree, knocking it from its roots. He runs up the truck, careful to avoid the grasping branches and dives away just as a Venus trap clamps down on the trunk. The plant, obviously single minded, tries to lift the heavy oak and, in doing so, topples over, its own roots becoming upended. This sets off a chain reaction as the trees begin falling over the prone Venus trap and tree and more Venus traps sensing the vibrations and latching onto the trunks of the trees. When the dust clears, the flailing braches of the tress wave harmless at Big Al and every man-eating Venus flytrap lies on top, each having ripped themselves up in their greedy attempts at sustenance. Steele rejoins S&M at the other side, sliding his hands on his bohunkular arms in a feeble attempt at ridding himself of the plant slime. The Sado-Masochist gives the Big guy a big man-hug. S&M: “I thought you were gone, bro.” BAS: (pushing him away in disgust) “Indeed, the situation was most dire, but I was able to call upon the powers of Olympia and extricate myself from the icy cold grip of the Reaper.” S&M: “Metaphorically represented by my hedge trimmers.” BAS: “Yes. Now come. I feel we are close to our goal…” The hedge trimmers roar to life as Steele hacks a new path into the jungle… Above, the groundskeeper’s eyes burn with anger as he watches the path hacked By Big Al grow ever closer to the garage. He scratches at his chin with a talon before a wicked smile graces his lips. With finality, he leaps off the tree and free falls, the trees below catching him and sucking him down into the forest proper. Big Al and partner fight their way through the forest when the Greek demi-god stops and grins. S&M looks at him, puzzled, until Steele points towards his sandals. Blacktop! Though covered in vines, the unmistakable blacktop burns up like a beacon in the night sky. S&M: “Awesome! Now all we have to do is follow it to the garage and get that lawn tractor!” BAS: (looking skyward) “Indeed, the gods have shone favorably on us!” A wry look from the Sado-masochist causes Big Al to shrug. BAS: “Well, for the most part. By Mercury’s wings, are our hearts not still beating, coursing the fire of battle through our veins?!” S&M: “But at least YOU haven’t been pan-fried to a nice golden brown.” BAS: (indignant) “Well, I did not see you swallowed into the gastric compartment of a great plant-beast!” Interrupting the evolving argument is a crackle on the walkie-talkie from the long-forgotten Tammi on the roof. Tammi: “Come in Big Al! Come in Big Al, over!” Steele fumbles at the talkie, confused at its technology until the Sado-Masochist snatched it from his hand and hits the red button. S&M: “This is S&M, I read you. Over.” Tammi: “From the north, a group of… somethings. And the east! There’s another one! And two! MY GOD! They’re everywhere!!” S&M: “Who? What?! You’re breaking up! Over!” Tammi: “RUUUUUUN! Over.” S&M: (tucking away the talkie) “That wasn’t very ambiguous, was it?” BAS: (pointing) “Look! The jungle recedes before our very eyes!!” And it’s true. The jungle magically pulls back, revealing the garage. And, also revealed, pouring out the jungle depths, a phalanx of grinning evil plaster death! S&M: “Omigod! Lawn gnomes!” BAS: (scratching head) “Hi-Keeba?” Hundreds of ceramic lawn gnomes launch themselves at our heroes, bouncing off them with audible pings and tinks! Big Al and his partner flail wildly, but the heavy little lawn ornaments are quick and plentiful, leaping into the bodies with devastating accuracy and effect. S&M staggers forward, close to succumbing to unconsciousness as Steele fights his way towards the garage… …and a grinning, green man, who laughs maniacally. BAS: (groggy) “You! Carl! A final gnome leaps directly into the face of Big Al, knocking him off his feet and onto the ground. From there, its leap after leap as the vicious gnomes pile on, each somersaulting and driving their pointed hats into his temples. Steele puts up his hands to try and fend them off, but their assault continues. Big Al looks over at a bloodied and prostrate Sado-Masochist. Suddenly, the plaster pounding ceases, and a taloned hand reaches down, lifting the 264 pound Big Alan Steele effortlessly into the air. Groundskeeper: “Carl no longer exists… and I suppose I have you to thank, for you made me into the beautiful creature that I am today! Now I obtain my energy from the sun through photosynthesis! Now I can command the plant world at will! And now I can restore this world to its prehistoric goodness that was robbed when you ANIMALS took over!” BAS: (seeing triple) “I… I created you? Untrue, for I should never hope…” The groundskeeper snarls and swings a lawn gnome in a wicked arc, connecting with Steele’s head and sending him flying into the blacktop. He skids harshly to a stop on his face, close to the Sado-Masochist, who is now coming to. A boot is stomped onto the back of Big Al’s neck, grinding his face into the pavement. Drool drips from the groundskeeper’s mouth as he speaks. Groundskeeper: “It was YOU that made me this way! I was a happy groundskeeper until YOU did this!! And now you shall pay and soon, I shall RULE THE WORLD!!! BWAHAHAHAHAHA!!!” BAS: (muffled by pavement) “I did no such thing!” Groundskeeper: (snarling) “Don’t you remember?! As you left for vacation?!” Flashback scene: The Winnebago is loaded to the roof with luggage as Big Al, S&M, Sue and the Graces prepare to leave for the airport. Steele is the last to enter and stops before he does. Looking over, he sees the groundskeeper, Carl (noticeably without the greenish hue), working in the front hedges. A big canister of lawn chemicals is strapped to his back and he’s working diligently trimming the bushes. BAS: (grinning) “Take care, Lord Carl, and keep the grounds fit for a god! I never know when company may drop by in the form of member of Mount Olympus!” Carl: (a bit confused but congenial) “Of course. Take care and have fun on vacation!!” BAS: (correcting) “Merely a change in crime fighting locations! May the gods of Olympus watch over you! Hi-Keeba!” Carl: (mumbling) “Yeah, Hi-whatever you too.” The big vehicle rumbles to life as Sue expertly backs the barge-like Bago down the driveway and into the street. There, she lights up a cigarette. Steele frowns at her. BAS: “By Zeus, woman! Must you consume the brimstone of Hades but every moment of the day?! Be gone with that fire stick post haste!!” Sue: (whining) “But it’s nearly a fifteen minute ride to LAX!! How am I supposed to make it?!” BAS: (growling) “Dispose of it at once! It makes my chariot smell of embers!” Scowling mightily, Sue takes a final drag on the cigarette and rolls down the window. With a final scornful look at the impassive Big Al, she flicks it out the window… A great whoosh of flame and a loud, shrill scream of pain. Sue calmly rolls up the window. BAS: (straining to hear) “Did you hear something?” Sue: (sighing) “NO! Now, c’mon! We have a plane to catch!” Back to the present, where the gnomes have circled around the groundskeeper, tears rolling down their ceramic cheeks as they hear his woeful tale for the umpteenth time. BAS: (grumbling) “I knew twas wise to force Sue to quit her habit…” Steele has used the respite wisely, nudging at the now syncoptic S&M, who secretly passes an object to Al… Groundskeeper: “YOU left me to die! YOU left me to rot like so much compost! But instead I became what you see today!! And now, I shall have my ULTIMATE REVENGE!!” The plaster hats of the gnomes bristle in anger as their beady little eyes turn to the duo lying on the ground. If their smiling faces could move, they would have contorted into evil looks of unbridled hate. Groundskeeper: (hysterical) “Now, my pretties! FINISH THEM OFF!!” Time slows down. Just as the gnomes leap in unison, Steele gets to his feet and rears back his arm. Steel glints in the sunlight as he wings a machete towards the Groundskeeper. BAS: “HI-KEEBA~!” The blade spins wickedly in the air and implants itself in the groundskeeper’s chest, throwing him back and pinning him to the garage door as he screams in pain. The gnomes fly at Steele, but he ducks and scoops up the Sado-masochist by the legs and swings the protesting sidekick like a baseball bat. Lawn ornaments fly off into the sky as they crack into the form of S&M. With more battle cries, Steele begins swinging the screaming S&M wildly, smashing his bodily form down hard on the advancing gnomish groups, splintering them into shards of plaster, many of which imbedded in the howling Sado-Masochist. The pounding of S&M seems to go on for days until the final gnome is crushed under his skull. Victory at hand, Big Al drops the bleeding S&M to the ground and slumps to his knees. He looks over to the garage and the groundskeeper, eyes closed, machete through his chest and into the wood of the door. He kneels down and checks on the Sado-Masochist. BAS: “Good friend, are you okay?” The eyes of the groundskeeper fly open! Groundskeeper: “He won’t be okay once I get through with you two!!” With horror, Steele stands up and watches the green man struggle to regain his balance. Then, with a great shout, he lunges forward, dragging the still stuck machete through his body with a great SLUP! Staggering to his feet, his eyes burn with cruelty as he flexes his muscles. The gaping wound on his chest, flowing green, slowly begins to mend itself together. Groundskeeper: “Very impressive!! It looks as if I underestimated your own devious devices, for I never would have imagined you would harm one of your best friends so! But now it’s just you and me!! Bring it on!!” The green man races forward and attacks, sending powerful punches at Big Al, who is on the defensive. BAS ducks, and backs away, waiting for his moment… and reaches forward, grabs the grounds keepers head and cracks a knee into his midsection! The two remain locked like that for a moment, before Steele hears something he did not expect – laughter! Groundskeeper: “Fool! Did you actually think you could hurt me?! My cell walls have been fortified with cellulose, giving me a tough outer skin akin to tree bark! You can’t penetrate my defenses!” And with that, the groundskeeper reaches down and grabs Steele’s knee, swinging him around like a rag doll and into a tree with crushing force. The wind rushes from Steele lungs as he slumps down the bark. Quickly, the green man is atop him, flailing away with blow after blow, punishing Al into the tree. Grabbing Steele’s head, he hurls him across the blacktop, where Big Al lands awkwardly. Steele clutches at his back, writhing in pain, as the groundskeeper slowly advances, cracking his knuckles. Groundskeeper: “Yes, I underestimated your depravity in hurting your friend for your own benefit, but now it ends!” The green man leans over Big Al and rears back a clawed hand, preparing to deliver a final blow, when he instead throws back his head and howls in pain! The Sado-Masochist runs by, shards of plaster jutting from nearly every part of his body, green ichor running down the shards on his right forearm. He grins maliciously. S&M: “Big Al didn’t hurt me… he only made me more dangerous!! Mmmm! Feel those endorphins!!” Groundskeeper: “AUGH! Nooooo!” BAS: (leaping to feet and running to S&M) “Lord of Self-Inflicted Pain!! Form Blazing Maso-Sword!!” The Sado-masochist grins and sets his body rigid. Steele picks him up for the second time of the day and raises him up, the jagged pieces of plaster somehow glinting in the noonday sun! Groundskeeper: “NOOOOO!! I’LL KILLL YOUUU!!!” BAS: “HI-KEEBA~!” Big Alan Steele rushes at the Groundskeeper, who rushes at Al in return. The two leap into the air and strike at each other… Steele lands on the ground, facing the opposite direction, holding the Sado-Masochist out in front of him dramatically. The groundskeeper lands behind him, and turns, a look of unadulterated murder on his face. He runs back towards Steele… and falls into two neatly carved slices. Plant juices spill out of the body halves as the mouth opens in a silent scream. Like a scene out of a horror movie, the hands grapple for each other, trying to reattach themselves and make use of his regenerative powers. Steele sets S&M upright and the two look on. BAS: “QUICKLY! The hose!” S&M: “The hose? But why…” But Big Al has already reached the garage and has whipped out the garden hose. The water turns on and he generously begins watering the halves of the groundskeeper. A horrified S&M runs over and makes a vain attempt to wrest the hose from Big Al. S&M: (screaming) “What are you doing?!?! He’s getting bigger!!” And indeed he is! The halves begin feeding on the water, growing larger. But the expression on the faces, as they are, are ones of fright, not exaltation. Soon enough, the growing stops, and the eyes close, as the halves begin to turn brown. The sky changes from blood red back to blue on a gorgeous San Jose afternoon. BAS: (smugly) “Tis simple. I knew that should I OVER water him, he would die a watery death! Hi-Keeba!” S&M: “Too much of a good thing, huh? Kinda like how Sue keeps ruining the Philodendron in the front foyer.” BAS: “Indeed!!” Both share a hearty laugh of mirth and relief. Slumping against the wall they relax for a moment. Finally, Steele pops up. BAS: “Well then! I suppose we should get around to mowing that lawn!” S&M: (groaning) “Man, I hate yard work!” BAS: (thumbs up) “Hi-Keeba!”
