Sainthood (Part two)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/33/23 Her dancing figure filled his mind. Dancing, dancing, on her tip toes in the night air.....eternally dancing. Eternally his. He held her close, then danced with her.....danced the night away. Looking deep into her eyes, he realized how incredibly in love he was.....and how nice a thing that was. So very nice. Just as quickly though she faded......faded out from his sight. He looked around for her, but only the blackness filled his eyes....until a piercing scream shattered all. He raced towards the source of it, but his legs moved pitifully slow. Why, why would they not move? In anger he struck himself, edging ever onward......until finally he found her. He ran to her side, cradling his love. Her bones creaked, shattered and protruding from her skin though she lived on. He wept, wept......for the first time in forever he wept. 'Hold me,' she whispered. He tried to tell her to hang on, though he knew in his heart it was too late.......far too late. And as she died, the rage bubbled up inside him, erupting like the fiercest volcano. 'I'LL KILL YOU!!!!!' And in response to this outcry, a form did appear in the darkness. Barely visible, but it was enough......a twisted visage. His hair hung in clumps, at his side a knife glint in what remained of his hand. He chuckled.....a childlike, insane chuckle. This was simply too much. Abandoning his lost love, he rushed.....his eyes blazed with the fires of hell as he leapt at the killer..... John awoke suddenly in a cold sweat. His heart pounded in his chest, echoing up into his skull......pain flashed through his shoulder, though he paid it no mind. All he could think of was the rage.....which gradually subsided as he realized it was a dream. John: Mary. Breathing deeply, John Morgan looked all around.......and in his state of mind, he swore he could see faces. Faces laughing, grinning.....that childlike laugh....... The aging police officer snapped on the light which rested on the stand next to his bed......the darkness shattered, leaving him quite alone. Eternally alone. Morgan reached to the stand beside him, taking a small photograph encased in a frame. He looked upon it......at the girl within. Remembered......remembered when he took it. The wind blowing over the edge of the lake......his Mary smiling. Smiling and alive. The rage filled him again. Blinding rage. The picture shook beneath his grasp, the wooden frame cracking. 'Police continue to be baffled by the string of disappearances. Five women, vanished from the same.......' John: Turn it off, Bev. Bev Carter, for all her years as John's assistant had never seen him like this. So......weary, was the only way she could describe it. Weary. Bev: John......I know this...... John: No Bev......no, you don't. Morgan rose, walking to the television and turning it off manually. He then marched back to his chair, plopping down and rummaging through a series of papers. Bev: John, you've gotta tell me. You put off your own retirement for this.......you've been on this case for three months. Why? Tell me......why? John looked up at her, and what the middle aged woman saw in his eyes filled her with despair. An overwhelming despair.....never had she seen such tiredness. Such sadness. John: It doesn't matter. Nothing matters at this point. With that Morgan, staring with blank eyes at the door of his office. In that second his secretary of so many years swore she saw something she never had before...... Tears? Just as quickly though the old man shook his head.......he looked at Bev. John: I'm going out there, Bev. And I may not be coming back this time. But if I don't......do me a favor. The cop reached into his coat pocket......produced a tiny locket, affixed to a chain. He held it out for Carter......after a brief moment she took it, looking at him. John: Find Cassie.......give it to her. It was......her mother's. I want her to have it. Bev: Your daughter? I..... But before another word could be uttered John walked to the door. He took one look back......scanned his office and smiled. John: I'm gonna miss this place. And with that John Morgan left, never to return. The corpses had piled up One after another.......they rotted. Rotted and festered, always there, always staring at him......whenever he'd awaken, there'd be a corpse on top of him. Death. Death. Death wherever he went. Powerless to stop it. Always powerless. ALWAYS. Always. He remembered......remembered the times. Good times, weren't they........a lovely family situation. His father, beating him into the cold, cold ground. His mother, such a lovely vision......bloody as well, doing little or nothing to stop what was going on. Did she simply not care? Was her brain too scrambled to care? WAS IT? He screamed again, in this eternal darkness. Another girl was in his arms, but it simply didn't matter any more. Her lovely hair, brunette.....her soft skin, soon to putrefy. The boy ran his disfigured hand along her soft cheek......his two fingers, all he had left. His fingers. His very fingers taken from him. Why, he asked? Why were they taken......was he not good enough at the piano? Did he not put in enough effort......is this why? Why? The question asked again and again. But he knew in his heart it was nearly over. Though he didn't know why at the time, he knew this......it was certain. Someone was coming for him. Dad? He giggled hysterically at the thought. Dad was coming......coming back. It was never enough......never enough for him. More punishment. More. More. More. What did it take to acquire sainthood? As the eternal melody played in his head.......the tinkling of the piano, his mother's piano, he remembered her words. Miracles were required for sainthood, she told him. A pure soul, a good life. Can I be a saint, a little boy asked innocently. And what did she say in reply to him? He honestly couldn't remember. Footsteps were falling, now. Outside this little shed.......the little boy broken looked to his left, and to his right. Sainthood. Could I gain sainthood? Peace. Peace to a broken world. One broken soul at a time. The door burst open......the light blinded him. He shielded his eyes temporarily, then ran for the more comforting darkness. John stormed the shack, his gun drawn. The light of day penetrated, and what he saw shocked him. Crosses. Crosses erected in this run down house. Bodies affixed to the crosses. Very carefully the old man crept up to them.....the smell gave it away, but he took a pulse regardless. Quite dead. Bags covered their heads, black as the night. Morgan looked closely......then something struck him. Something......a feeling, though he never would know what it was. The cop pulled the bag off the head of the leftmost corpse. His stomach leaped into his throat, as the maggots growing in her sockets gave the illusion of living eyes.....after a long moment he regained his composure. John looked at her......shook his head, then moved to the next. Dad. Is that you dad? In a similar state of decay, but this wasn't what he was looking for. What WAS he looking for? Even John could not answer that. Nonetheless he moved.......another, then another. Two remained........ Dad. Why, dad? Morgan removed the hood of the next to last......then the final, and though her face had long since lost it's flesh, he knew in an instant. It struck him like thunder. John: ........Mary. Her clothes were the same......the same as that night...... But what had happened that night? You killed her. The voice warbled at John from the black.......he drew his gun and fired. Crack, crack went the tiny explosions, but no scream.......only that awful, tittering laughter. Why dad? Why? Morgan carefully looked all around, his eyes wide. The voice seemed to come from everywhere, all at once......it warbled at him, mocking him. Why did you kill her, dad? John: You killed her. And I don't have any sons, you freak. Once again John shot into the black.......again only laughter greeted him. At least take responsibility for your actions. Violence can be forgiven.......ignorance can't. Isn't that what you always told me, dad? John circled......his leg flared with pain, though he ignored it. His gun aimed in all directions......then a crack caught his attention. The officer spun just in time to see a large figure dive upon him from the shadows. John: Agh! Morgan tumbled about, the glint of a knife shining through in the darkness. The figure assailed him, screaming. Why? Why? WHY? John threw the man off him, scrambling to his feet as fast as he could. Again the figure rushed him......tackled him to the ground. Its fetid breath seeped into his nostrils as he wrestled with it, trying to stay alive. Tell me why. John: You.......won't.......WIN again! With a final burst of fury Morgan bucked the killer off of him, pinning him to the ground. He pounded its already mangled face with the butt of his gun.......again, again he whipped him. The creature, pitiful and pathetic finally moaned. Dad. Pain wrenched through John's chest and shoulder......he clutched his arm, sitting beside the fallen monster. Squinting, he looked up with tear filled eyes at the cross bearing his late wife......and for the first time in years, he cried. John: God Mary......why.....? Tell me why. Sobbing, Morgan struck the ground.......finally after an eternal moment, he looked up. The creature before him, the 'saint'......twisted, mangled. The old cop grabbed him by the tattered hood he wore for a mask, the same hood his victims wore.......yanked it off. At the sight of him John's heart stopped in his chest. The color drained from his face.......his jaw fell open, hanging slackly. A young girl fought with all her might, to no avail. He held her fast.......she bit him, though he paid no mind. As she tried to run he caught her foot......tittering, he pulled her in for the last time. He dipped two of his fingers into her blood, wiping it on his cheek.......laughed. Laughed. Laughed forever. Another, and another......their screams filled his mind. And it made him happy.......it blocked out the memory. The knife was hot in his hand, or rather his fingers, for he held the index and pinky of each close at all times. Dad. Was it fun, dad, for you to beat me? Dad. Memory. Memory. She lay before him, sliced open.......she cried, though his mind was made up, his course of action set. His knife struck her locket, which she always wore around her neck......roughly, he ripped it from her. Saint. Saint. Mom. He remembered as he slashed........he cried as he slashed, cried along with her. Mary. He tried to forget over time, and he succeeded. But it never escaped him.......never escaped him. Never. Can I be a saint, a little boy asked innocently? No, his mother replied. Sainthood isn't for those of this earth. John stared down, at the broken form......his own face stared back up at him. Slowly he rose......looked to his wife, reduced to bones and the dust of the earth. He hung his head, cried, very much alone in this little shed......put his gun to his temple, and fired.
