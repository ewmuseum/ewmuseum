Boarding The Wrestle Vessel...Travels, Part 5 of 6.

27 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-27-2002 (The scene opens onto a sunny day to show a long wharf in Jacksonville Florida. A huge white ocean liner tagged 'Wrestle Vessel' sits in dock and a stream of people flood aboard by way of the ramp. The mammoth vessel overshadows everything else at the pier, including a broken down tugboat...Who would ride that piece of junk? The cheerful voyagers give their tickets to the friendly attendants and the line moves briskly. The shot pans around to the street leading to the wharf, and we are met with this sight: Venneman Pendragon, dressed in a silver chain-mail shirt, wearing the PCW Rising Star Belt, carrying a sword. Over his back is what appears to be a large blue beach towel emblazoned with the golden dragon sigil, worn like a cape. He rides his war horse Xerxes, and he holds the leash to what appears to be a pack-mule. Evidently he has never heard of a suitcase. The mule is quite loaded with supplies, and is topped off with a lifejacket and what would appear to be an unopened pack of inflatable armbands...) 'I've never been a big fan of the water, at least, being on it. I came prepared though...I had my extra life jacket and my arm band flotation devices. I just hoped I would not need them...' (He canters his mount over towards the large ship and stops short of the entrance ramp. He hops off of the horse and looks as if he savors the solid ground one last time. A passerby looks at the large nervous man and tries to spark up a conversation...) Passerby: Aww...Don't be nervous, friend. It is only a ship, and Jamaica is not far. You should have no trouble on the trip. No reason at all to be afraid... (Pendragon looks at the feminine acting young man, and his eyes burn with the deep fires of wretched insanity...) Venneman: Afraid? ME? I am Lord King Venneman Pendragon, and I fear nothing! Do ye hear??? Nothing...It's just that were man supposed to be seaworthy, we would have gills, not noses. Who are you to question your lord, whelp? (The man shrugs and takes off. Pendragon shakes his head and watches him go. He then looks at the ramp, and at the boat and back again. He takes a deep breath, reaches into his pocket for a ticket and starts up, overcoming his obvious disdain for sailing. He reaches the attendant and smiles nervously. He hands her his ticket and she smiles...) Attendant: Ahhh...Rising Jobber, I mean Rising Star Champion Venneman Pendragon. We have been waiting. Venneman: I had some business to attend to last night. Made for a long couple of days. Attendant: No matter. You're here now. Do you have your luggage in order? (Pendragon points to the mule and his horse. He smiles weakly at the attendant who gasps in surprise...) Attendant: You never hear of a suitcase? My you wrestlers are quirky! One fellow wanted a tractor, the other wanted a mule...What next? Venneman: Umm...I actually have passage booked for three...Xerxes, my horse, and Elisha, my pack-mule. They are a little nervous around the water though, you know how animals can be. (The attendant looks at the pack mule and sees the armbands. She grins knowingly at Venneman before she replies...) Attendant: Well, they won't fit up the ramp, so if you can find a way to get 'em on the boat, that's fine with me...Why me? Tractor, horse and mules...Damnit.... (Pendragon shrugs and strokes his chin, thinking. He then brightens up as he exclaims...) Venneman: I have an idea...I can get them onto the boat...No problem, I'll be right back. (Venneman runs down the road on foot, leaving his two animals. The shot follows and we see Pendragon heading for a shipping company. He goes through the gate and the shot stops abruptly, waiting for him to come out. In a moment, a large crane equipped with a flexible winch drives out of the yard; at the controls? Venneman Pendragon...) 'You never knew when a good horse would come in handy, and I was not about to leave my luggage on the pier, so I had to improvise. You can do that when you possess a superior intellect such as mine.' (Pendragon dries the crane back to the wrestle vessel and maneuvers it up next to his horse and his mule. He climbs down and walks over to Xerxes. He pats him on the head and then grabs the winch, which he unclasps and runs underneath the horse. He snaps the harness tight and gives it a tug. He nods and climbs back into the crane. He pushes a lever and Xerxes is lifted into the air. He pushes the crane forward and sets Xerxes down gently on the deck of the Wrestle Vessel. One down, one to go. Pendragon climbs down and walks to the ramp that he navigates cautiously to the deck of the ship. He walks over and unclasps the harness and gives the nervous looking Xerxes a reassuring pat. He then heads back down to the crane. He maneuvers the winch down so he can lift the mule and climbs AGAIN from the crane. He snaps the mule in, runs back to the crane, and lifts him towards the wrestle vessel. A small crowd has gathered on deck to watch the show, and everything seems to be going well until..... *****SNAP****** The cable breaks and the mule falls toward the deck. The people scatter; all except one unlucky fellow who just watches as the mule comes closer and closer. *****THUD****** The mule lands on the unfortunate man who hollers in pain and surprise. The mule bellows a little itself, disoriented from the fall. Pendragon's eyes become two orbs of insanity as he takes in the scene...) Venneman: HEY!!! YOU!!! WHAT DO YOU THINK YOU ARE DOIN TO MY MULE???!!! I KNOW FLORIDA IS IN THE SOUTH, BUT I HAD HOPED IT WOULD BE DIFFRENT HERE, THAN SAY, BOONEVILLE!!! KEEP IT IN YER PANTS, WILL YA???!!! (Pendragon leaps from the crane and runs up the ramp, throwing three tickets at the attendant. He runs over and starts talking to the mule...) Venneman: You Ok, Elisha? Hmm...Nothing seems broken. Sorry about that... Man: What about me? You dropped a fricken' mule on me, ya bastard!! (Pendragon glares at the man who pulls himself from beneath the mule and runs off. Pendragon shakes his head and his eyes clear a little...) Venneman: All right, boys...Let's find our rooms. I got us deck level so you wouldn't have to worry about stairs... (Pendragon leads the horse and the mule off in search of rooms. He looks at his ticket stubs and finally finds what he is looking for...) Venneman: Ok, Xerxes...This is your room. He ties Elisha to a railing and walks Xerxes through the door. He looks around and nods at the posh surroundings before he straps an oat bag to Xerxes' head. All right boy...If ya get bored, watch some TV or something. (Venneman chuckles as he leaves and locks the door behind him. Next room over, he opens and walks Elisha in. He straps a bag to his face as well and grabs the large bundle from off of Elisha's back. He leaves and hits the NEXT door over which is his room. He enters and flicks on the light. He nods at the enormous room before dumping his bundle on the bed. He unrolls it and proceeds to hang up his clothes, which appears to consist mostly of different styles of chain-mail shirts, a couple pairs of pants and one pair of blue swimming trunks emblazoned on the rear with the Pendragon sigil. He places the armbands and the life-jacket beside the door and covers them with his 'Cape' before he sits down on the bed. With a sigh, he flips on the TV. The channel? PCW, ALL THE TIME. He shakes his head and flips the TV off. He lies back on the bed and closes his eyes...) 'Well, this boat wasn't so bad. As long as I was in here, it would feel like a hotel room in any city, rather than a floating death trap on the ocean. I figured I would catch a few Z's before I went out and did any exploring on this tug. It was a long night, what with Syko trying to cheat me of a victory that should have been a cakewalk. At least the PCW powers that be didn't let it go down. In the end, it worked out better, because I was able to dismantle not on, but TWO pieces of filth. I guess even garbagemen can get some satisfaction from their work. Sunday night though. That is where the supposed 'real' test awaits. I am actually starting to lose hope in this match though. How can I give the fans what they want to see when all PCW gives me are six whelps who have no business fighting for MY title? It will be a hell of a thing for the opening match of the PPV to be a wash. We've seen what Syko is capable of. Trigger is too busy knowing he is a failure to cause a problem. The man who thought he would be a warrior...It's sad really. But I doubt I lose any sleep over breaking him one more time. Should be fun. Jaden? Well, Jaden is a woman in a man's world. Enough said there. Thunder? Hell, I don't know if Ricky is even gonna make it down here in time for the match; especially having to rely on some washed up freak who couldn't cut it in the big time. Though Ricky is a resilient bastard, I'll give him that. He should probably do well in the Cruiserweight Division, but the Rising Star Division? MY division? He is just a little man with no hope. The days are only gonna get worse Ricky. I'm warning you cause of the boys against me in this farce, you are who I would say is closest to being a man. Keep working Ricky. It'll come; just not yet. Who does that leave me? Burden? Hmm...I doubt it. That leaves us Scorpion. I remember him from my CCW days. Full of ideas that he could never back up. Kinda reminded me of one of those talking dolls where you pull the string and it talks, except he pulled his own string...Same thing, over and over and over. But here, he is the 'King of Casinos'...Well, I got news for everybody involved....Pendragon is not only the king of CASTLES, but king of all that he sees. The Rising Star Title is MY title. And I plan on bringing it back to respectability. When I am done, I'll move on to bigger things in PCW...Who could stop me? Nobody in this match-up. Take me outta the match and you have a cluster@#$% straight outta Jobberville USA. At least I bring some credibility to the sad affair. I guess it will be a good watch though...If you enjoy seeing six unworthy competitors being broken by the one champion in the match...Me. Lord Pendragon. Prepare for the pain, kiddies....Prepare for the pain.' (Pendragon sits up and looks around a little nervously. He retrieves the arm bands and opens the package. He looks around again before sitting on the bed and proceeding to blow them up...Wonder what he is planning on doing?) >>>>TO BE CONTINUED
