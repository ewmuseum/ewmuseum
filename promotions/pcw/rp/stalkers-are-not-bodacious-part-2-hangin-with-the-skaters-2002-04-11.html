Stalkers are not Bodacious Part 2: Hangin' With The Skaters

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 11 [The scene opens on a group of younger kids, around twelve to thirteen, skateboarding at a local skate park. Many pop mediocre ollies off the funboxes, the above average are doing boardslides and 50-50's on the grind rails, and the amazing are working their magic on the vert ramps. It's all an amazing sight to see these young adults fly through the air on a piece of wood with wheels underneath. The pioneers of the extreme sport of skateboarding would be proud to see just how far that it's come] [One man who is proud is a shadowed figure of a man. The dark stubble on his cheeks gives away the familiarness of this man, and that's the trenchcoat man. He stands in the shadows, watching the skateboarders nail their trick. He feels a sense of satisfaction as he watches these young adults, as if he is on a board himself doing the tricks] Man in Shadows: Skate on, my sons. [The man's voice is pretty raspy and undistingishable, whatever the hell that means. His shoulders move up and down, showing that his breathing is deep and heavy. His mind fixed on the two men that he hates the most in this world, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy and 'Heavy Metal' Harry, the Cowabunga Connection. He despises surfing and everything that goes along with it, especially Tommy. A man with that small of a brain should not be allowed to walk God's green earth, he should be put to sleep like a pathetic dog] Man in Shadows: It's only a matter of time. [A matter of time? What does that mean? Well, this man in the shadows feels that he is the one that can put a stop to the driving force that is the Cowabunga Connection. They've had their fun, and now it's time to end all of the fun. End the wrestling, end the surfing, and most of all...end the women!] Man in Shadows: The Cowabunga Connection is history! [Then across from this shadowed man, his hated enemies appear at the entrance of the skatepark. Tommy, in his PCW T-shirt, blue and yellow swim trunks, and black sandals, looks extremely observant as he enters the skatepark. Harry, with his long brown hair hanging down in his face, wearing his Twisted Sister T-shirt, and black leather pants with black cowboy boots, looks completely lost as he looks up to the sky. Maybe he sees a spaceship, hopefully it'll take him away to help make the man in the shadows job a lot easier] [What are these cretins doing here? Wait...it makes sense. They are facing Suburban Uprising Friday and Sunday, it could be they are trying to get into their minds. Obviously this was Tommy's idea seeing that Harry would never even have an inkling to try and study his opponents. These two are absolutely in the wrong zone if their trying to study their skateboarding opponents. The man in the shadows knows for a fact that his skateboarding army will immediately take down these two imbeciles, simply because their surfers. Surfers need to die!] [It's just then that the crowd of skaters immediately stop and run over to Harry and Tommy. It seems inevitable that their about to get a huge skater beatdown...but...what's this?! The skaters are shaking the hands of the Cowabunga Connection?! TRAITORS!! How dare those skaters shake the hands of the enemy. Damn them! Damn them to hell and back. CRETINS!] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Not all at once, compadres! I'm still a litte sore from Spring Fling. Skater # 1: You guys are MAD funny! [Harry smiles] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. Skater # 2: Your finisher, Bodacious Bombs, is freakin' SICK! [Harry smiles even wider] Harry: Yeah, man. Skater # 3: Yeah. Tommy, I've seen you surf a couple times, it's bad ass! I don't know how you ride some of those monster waves. [Now Tommy smiles wide, showing off his bright, pearly white teeth] TTT: Just comes with time, dudes. Skater # 4: Cool. Why are you guys here at the skatepark? [Tommy's smile fades as he begins to think about the upcoming match with Suburban Uprising, the bogus dudes that attacked him and Harry from behind after that most awesome tag match at Spring Fling] TTT: It really has to do with our most awesome match coming up on Friday... [Harry interjects, still wearing his goofy smile] Harry: Don't forget Sunday, man. [Tommy then smiles and gives Harry a thumbs up for remembering] TTT: Good work, my friend. [Tommy then turns back to the group of skaters] TTT: And Sunday as well. These dudes are skateboarders and after attacking us they seem to have some kind of problem with us. Harry and myself were thinking most bodaciously last night of what could push them to attack gnarly dudes like us. Then it hit us...I'M A SURFER!! [Harry leans forward, as if trying to one up his best friend and tag team partner] Harry: I went boogie boarding once, man. [Everyone stops and just stares at Harry with confusion and bemusement. Then Tommy decides to go on] TTT: There has always been this inner war between skaters and surfers. For some reason they have always had a problem with each other...nobody really knows what it is...but something very strange AND BOGUS! So, if that's the most heinous case then it's only fair that we show who is better. Skater # 2: So, your saying that surfers are better than skaters? [The group of skaters take a step forward, with boards in hand. It seems that tempers may flare after all] [The man in the shadows breathes harder now, feeling the fight coming on. Tommy messed up, he always messes up. When there are things on the line, when there is something important, he's always dropping the ball. Even as a surfer he would drop the ball and he was the best in the world! Talk about being a complete dumbass] [Tommy smiles] TTT: No way, dudes!! I was just making an anaology between us, the Cowabunga Connection, and those bogus dudes, Suburban Uprising. All Skaters: OH!!! [Harry looks a bit frustrated and kind of taps one of the skaters on the shoulder] Harry: YEAH, MAN! Skater # 1: I've actually hung out with Suburban Uprising before. They seemed like pretty cool guys, they could shred like no other. They were a bit on the weird side though. TTT: Weird side? Skater # 3: Yeah. They would just flip out and start breaking things for no reason. The one guy grabbed this little kids skateboard and just started smashing it off one of the grind rails. It was pretty disturbing if you ask me. TTT: BOGUS!! I hate when dudes try to push around little kids! Skater # 4: I hear ya. [Tommy nods, now having a sense of what their up against. As he begins to reflect on this, he notices a dark figure in the corner of the skatepark. No way, dude. His mind is just playing bogus tricks on him. He's just paranoid from the other day with that heinous dude in the trenchcoat. Ever since that day Tommy has been on his toes, trying to keep an eye out for any strange characters. Hmmm...that figure in the corner is kind of strange] [Meanwhile...as Tommy is going over what he should do about the figure in the corner, Harry is showing the skaters his brand new bong invention, The Bongification Proclamation. The skaters seem taken back by it and almost worship the craftsmanship that went into the bong construction. Harry then looks over to the corner and sees something odd in the corner as well. What is that, man? Maybe it's two pigeons making woopy, man. Naw...pigeons aren't that big. Well, they could be mutant pigeons from planet Pigeonus trying to take over earth. Who cares, man. This bong RULES!!] [The man in the shadows seems to be a bit frightened now. Tommy has seen him and so did Harry. Fortunately, Harry is too stoned to even notice anything, but Tommy must have been reading a lot lately because he is way too curious. Damn THEM!] TTT: Dudes, if you'll excuse me I'm going to go check something out. [Tommy then begins to make his way over toward the corner. He surely knows something is there now, he can make out a man. He almost looks familiar as Tommy gets closer. Like lightning the figure jumps over the fence that he was leaning against and begins running down the small cement hill. Tommy knows it's that BOGUS dude from the other day!! He's stalking them!!] TTT: GET BACK HERE BOGUS, DUDE!! [Tommy leaps over the fence like an action movie star would, no not Chuck Norris or Jean Claude Van Damme style, but JACKIE CHAN style!! Jackie Chan is TOTALLY GNARLY! Anyway... Tommy runs down the cement hill and is making a little progress on this mystery man. He has dark hair, that he can tell from a distance. His clothing is all black, and very baggy, so it's hard to tell how big or small this man is] TTT: Why are you stalking us, compadre! That's totally against the laws of California! [The man doesn't answer at all and just keeps running. They make it to a busy street in a suburban area of California. Cars race through a busy intersection not ready for the mystery man running into the road. A maroon Chevorley Cavalier bumps the man and sends him to the ground] Mystery Man: ARGH! [A blonde haired woman in a very sexy business suit gets out of the car and begins screaming obscenities at this mysterious figure. Tommy is going to catch up, he can feel it!! As he does the man takes matters into high gear. He throws the woman aside like a rag doll and she falls to the ground in a heap] Woman: AHHH! [The figure leaps into the drivers seat and takes off in the car. Tommy runs alongside the car as if he were Will Smith in Bad Boys, but he can't get a good look because the windows are tinted very dark. He can just see a dark figure, and then he sees the figure extend it's hand and give him a figured middle finger. BOGUS! Tommy can't keep up and just stops running. Behind him the group of skaters pull up on their boards as Harry hitches a ride on one of their backs] Harry: What happened, man? TTT: It was that stalker dude again. I'm starting to get worried about that, compadre. Harry: Did he try to go after another little boy's shovel, man? TTT: No, he's gone into the car theft business. Talk about heinous. [Everyone shakes their heads in disappointment. As they do the woman in the business attire approaches Tommy, very upset] Woman: Thank you for trying to get my car back, I really appreciate it. TTT: It was no problem, bodacious babe. [She then flashes Tommy a sexy smile and bites her lower lip] Woman: How can I ever repay you? [Tommy smiles and gives the skaters a thumbs up. Harry seems a bit upset about this but them looks at his newest bong creation...then all his sorrows are washed away] TTT: There is ONE way...YAHTZEE!!! [The scene then fades to black]
