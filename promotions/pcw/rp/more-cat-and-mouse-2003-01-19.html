More Cat and Mouse

19 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19-1-03 The last time we saw Bob was when his intern drove off from outside the council H.Q. With a tape that had enough evidence on it to put Bob away for the rest of his life. She starts up the car and speeds off as Bob and Tony near the blue car. 'Now we have a problem.' Bob says in an exhausted voice. As it turns out the events that followed where very unusual. *** 13-1-03 8.30pm Receptionist's Home The receptionist unlocks the front door of her small suburban home in a nervous fashion as she looks over her shoulder to make sure Bob isn't following her. She opens the door and slides into the small lounge room before locking the door in three different ways behind her. The room is rather dark as the curtains are drawn and there are no lights on. She runs over to the couch which fills up most of the room. She picks up the phone and presses the speed dial button which has 'police' written above it. *** Meanwhile in Bob Vurdo's car Bob and Tony sit in Bob's black Mercedes' in the car park outside the council H.Q. 'What are we going to do?' Bob is obviously nervous about the position he's in. 'I've got a man in charge of finding her place and a few men ready to take her and the evidence out.' Bob sighs in a half relieved fashion. 'Lets hope we get there before she does anything.' 'Just relax Bob it will all work out fine.' Tony smiles and pats Bob in the shoulder. 'Now lets get going.' 'Where we going?' 'Have you forgotten already.' Bob frowns as he tries to remember about the meeting. 'Earless Andy wants to have a speak to us about a business proposition.... He has come all the way from New York so we have to make sure he feels welcome.' 'Oh that's right. Sorry I have just been worried about the issue with the receptionist.' Tony nods as Bob starts up the car and drives out of the car park. *** 8.20pm The Receptionist's home The police car pulls over outside the house of the receptionist. Two large policemen get out of the car and walk to the front door. One of the police officers knocks on the wooden door and waits for a few seconds. He then knocks again and after a few seconds when there is no reply he takes a few steps back. 'Kick it in.' The police officer then runs toward the door and goes to kick it down but the door doesn't budge and he falls over before clutching at his leg. 'My fucking leg!' The police officer rolls around clutching his leg as the other officer cringes in pain. 'You all right?' The police officer on the ground continues to roll around clutching his leg. 'Do I look all right?!' The standing police officer backs down. 'I'm going to go in myself.' 'all right.' The police officer on the ground stops rolling and just lays on the concrete path in the front yard. The standing officer pulls out his gun and presses it against the door handle before firing his gun and knocking off the door handle. The police officer then kicks the door open and enters the lounge room where he is surprised to see the receptionist laying on the couch with a meat cleaver stuck in her forehead. The officer immediately pulls a walky talky out of his pocket. 'I have a 187 here and am requesting backup.' The officer puts the walky talked back on his belt and reaches into his holster before pulling out his handgun. He then walks back out of the house and onto the front yard where he carefully turns to see what his partner is doing. The other policeman is laying on the ground groaning about his leg. 'She's fucking dead!' The police officer on the ground looks up at his partner in surprise. 'Get backup.' 'Already have.' 'I think I broke my leg.' The officer on the ground then puts his head back flat on the grass of the front lawn in order to reduce the pain his leg is giving him. Three more police cars arrive at the scene and the officers immediately get out of their cars and approach the two officers in the front yard. 'We'll go in and sort it all out.... Is he okay.' The man that is talking to the two original officers is dressed in a gray trench coat which suggests he is a higher rank than the other police officers. 'He thinks he has broken his leg trying to kick the door down.' The officer in the gray trench coat laughs before turning toward the house and walking toward the open front door. Four other policemen follow closely behind him. Two other policemen seal off the house with the yellow 'Crime Scene' tape. *** Meanwhile Mat's car We then see a large pale man sitting in the driver's seat of a mid sized red car. Beside him is a rather tall, scruffy looking man who seems to be rather bored as he stares out of the car at the moon in the sky which is half covered by clouds. 'Do you think the moon is god?' The large white guy looks over at the man sitting next to him staring at the moon. 'I don't believe in any of that superstitious crap Lukey you know that.' Luke turns to look at the large man who's attire is mostly black leather which isn't a good thing on a fat man. 'Where the fuck did you get that suit anyway Mat?' Mat looks over at Lukey with a rather angry expression. 'You aint looking that flash yourself.' Mat looks over Lukey who is wearing a pair off faded brown pants and a green and blue checkered shirt which also looks fairly old. Mat is then interrupted by the ringing off his cell phone. He pulls it out of the pocket of his black leather jacket. 'Yeah?' 'Have some manners this is Bob your speaking to.' Mat has never spoken to Bob but he has heard a lot of stories about him. 'Sorry about that.' 'Tony has faith in you which means we expect a lot from you and you better deliver otherwise there could be harsh repercussions.... If you do well you will be on easy street from this point on so make the most of this opportunity.' Bob doesn't like leaving important things to amateurs but the Vurdo family needs some more full time workers to get things done more efficiently and Tony seems to like Mat and Luke. 'all right.' 'Now we have found out where she lives and I want you to go there and kill her quickly and quietly all right?' 'Okay.' Mat looks over at his partner and puts his thumb up. 'Okay she lives at Fourteen Darlington Road in the suburbs.' 'I know the place.' Mat smiles in satisfaction with himself. 'Don't fuck up.' Bob then hangs up the phone. *** Just after the phone call. The back of the ol' Casino. Bob sits at the table next to Tony as he hangs up the phone and places it down on the table. 'Who is this guy?' 'That was 'Fat' Mat who is new to Amber Hill but not this sort of thing. His friend 'Lanky' Luke is always with Mat but not in a gay way. They have been the best of friends since primary school.' 'Mat is very innovative in the way he works and is also a great driver. In two days he memorized the position of every road in Amber Hill and claims he is capable of mapping it out from his memory.' 'Luke is the dirty worker of the two and he is quite viscous when he wants to be. Rumor has it he once stapled a man's eyelids to his eyebrows and also put a staple in his ball bag. Needless to say he was able to get his way on the issue and the other man paid up on the spot.' Bob laughs as he pictures the situation in his head. 'Sounds good.' 'They are when they work together but without each other they are practically useless as each member serves an important purpose to the team. Mat is the thinker and Luke is the thug.' 'When's Andy going to get here?' 'Very soon.... Very soon.' To be continued
