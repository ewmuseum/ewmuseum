Keeping THAT Tape Away From Eleanor

24 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/04/02 Ellie~!’s prologue : Okay, so like some freak of nature I wasn’t killed when Duke Williams decided he was a woman hater and hit me with a chair. Maybe the God’s of prep-dom interfered on my behalf and decided that if I die there would be nobody to remind people when they are poor, badly dressed or just not as good as me. Anyway, come Friday when Xavi and Mark put Duke in hospital, and it won’t be a Hubbardville hospital like I had, then we will be the ones standing triumphant and not his little jobber crew friends...Eugh....I HATE Duke Williams.... “So there I was, stood toe to toe with Duke Williams who was about to...Um..kill me, yeah...But I was all...Hey Duke, I am not scared of you....Then he just hit me with the chair for no reason at all....” Our shot opens up in the Grease-esque Benny’s Milkshake Parlour in the west-end of Hubbardville near the junction with Germanycanoppressusalltheylikebutwearestillareligion Drive. In it Eleanor Hall, the valet of wrestling’s only Scientologist is telling anyone that will listen her variation of events at Sabotage. Eleanor : “So he, like, broke my neck and laughed, saying that he hated women everywhere and that now I’d be able to do the washing up in a neckbrace....” The prep is drinking a huge banana milkshake will all the extras through a straw as she addresses the crowd who don’t realise her recollections of the event are pretty far from what happened. She is wearing a skinny fit pink t-shirt with PREPPYNESS IS A VIRTUE written across her 32B chest in sparkly gold. With this she has a pair of sandblasted hipster jeans and her usual assortment of bands and bracelets on both wrists. ........Wow, I’m glad to know that I am not the only person to hate....And I mean HATE Duke Williams, all these people hate him now that they think he tried to kill me...As do the people at the bus station when I told the that he buses his nephew and throws rocks at old people.... All those around her gasp in horror at what they are told the President of PCW did. That’s disgusting! Typical man! He should be fired! Chauvinist pig! You poor little thing! Now I see why you hate him! As she basks in their sympathy we see Xavier Kannon walking over to her table, bottle of Snapple~! Apple in hand. The crowd around Ellie~! say their goodbyes and give her their sympathies as Xavier takes a seat in their booth opposite her. Xavier : “Were you telling them the story about how you got hit on the head by a biological weapon trying to free Iraq were you?” She shakes her head as she takes a long sip through the straw, looking almost proud at the fact she toned down her outlandish portrayal of events. Eleanor : “No....I’ve stopped exaggerating it now...But hey, I like, SO could have been stuck in a neckbrace which are really unfashionable by the way so forgive me if I milk it a bit....The one thing worse than being hit with the chair was being close enough to smell his breath...Did something crawl down his throat and DIE?” Her face contorts as she thinks about the site of the Malice Man getting closer to her, spit and his last meal stuck I his beard and his dirty whiskey breath in her face. Meanwhile Xavier takes a sip from his Snapple~!, his face not showing a look of disgust but more of worry as he talks about Duke. .......I can still se his ugly face now....I think I might need counselling or something, you think they have therapy groups for preps attacked by rednecks?....... Xavier : “Yeah...Um...Don’t worry about Duke. I have Makonie to keep him aw-...I mean, HELP me fight him at Fusion....I will get revenge for that hideous act he carried out upon you...Deliberate act may I add....Which I could do NOTHING about....Yeah...” His naive valet looks at him curiously, wondering why he is protesting so much as she continues to drink her shake, her long, way brown hair tied back out of her face. Eleanor : “Um....Okay...” She continues to look at him in a peculiar fashion before something behind her pale blue eyes clicks and jolts her memory. Eleanor : “Did you remember to get a copy of Sabotage from the Tom Cruise-arama Video Reprographics Emporium just off Dianetic’s Drive....You know...Near the Battle Earth Field?” Kannon seems to squirm in his seat, wondering how long he can avoid Ellie~! seeing the tape where Xavier saves his won skin at the expense of his valet...Or her head at least. His eyes shoot around the Milkshake Parlour, along the walls mounted with photos of famous Scientologists, including him, and then outside of the window where a good view of the L Ron Hubbard memorial statue can be seen clearly, anything to avoid eye contact with Eleanor. .......It’s kinda strange that I haven’t seen the tape yet.....I mean, I was actually THERE, and so was Xavi, and people are talking about it, but I can never seem to get a copy of it....It’s almost as if someone is trying to keep it from me..... Xavier : “Um.....It got lost....Yeah, I went to the place and they said they had lost it so I told them they were a disgrace to the name of Tom Cruise then....Um...Then I left and came here...That’s it...” The Commissioneress of PCW looks none to pleased about this fact and scowls, an expression that would look more menacing if she didn’t have banana milkshake over her top lip. Eleanor : “Well, I like, say we SO go down there now and demand that they look for another copy or you’ll have them thrown out of Hubbardville because...Um...because you, like SO totally can!” Eleanor goes to get up but Kannon springs up, nearly knocking his bottle of Snapple~! over in the process as his thighs jolt the table and he tries to dissuade her. Xavier : “Um..Wait...There’s no need for that my little Poon Puppy..Um, I forgot, they DID get it, I..Er...Sent Giovanni Ribisi to pick it up and he lost it...I don’t know what I was thinking, the shop loosing it...So, Ribisi lost it...” .......This thing is, like, SO turning into the Tommy and Pamela Lee tape...Everyone seems to have it apart from the one horny little geek boy who wants it...... She slumps back into her seat with her arms crossed, looking almost upset that she couldn’t go and threaten the staff at the Tom Cruise-arama Video Reprographics Emporium. Eleanor : “So where is he now? We could always go and throw him out of Hubbardville then stand just inside and point at him...And laugh...While Ribisi cries because we won’t let him come in and he has to stand in Wisconsin and eat cheese all day...So, where is he?” Again, Xavier looks to deflect the question, wondering when his valet will cease enquiring about the tape before he incriminates himself. Xavier : “He’s in Guatemala.....Yeah, Guatemala...It’s a country somewhere....And it’s also where Giovanni Ribisi is too funnily enough...Because he’s there and everything...” She looks back at him, her face even more blank than usual and her pale blue eyes have a certain blankness to them as she digests the facts which cut through even her naievity. Eleanor : “Why is he in Guatemala....Wasn’t he in Hubbardville about an hour ago?” Xavier : “Er...You know Scientologybucks? The coffee shop down on Isaac Hayes Boulevard? They sent him there on a special mission to find...Um....BEANS! Yah, he is their bean finder....But I’m sure he’ll be back in time to see me finally avenge you against Duke Williams, who by the way I do NOT fear in any way.....” She still looks at him, not in mistrust but in confusion as she finishes off her milkshake, causing the little amount left to make that all familiar noise as she sucks the last of it through the straw. Eleanor : “Um....Okaaaaay....Can we go now, my head is getting a major brain freeze from the ice cream in this thing....Then you can et some raining done ready to beat up Duke Williams!” Xavier : “But I was just going to get Makonie to do al-....I mean, yes my little Fuck Ferret.....I want to be in top shape for when I finally get my hands on that damn Duke Williams....” The couple get up and leave, Xavier looking none too enthusiastic about his upcoming encounter with both the Malice Man and his former Lackey Jordan X Shabazz as the shot fades to black.
