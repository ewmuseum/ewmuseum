5 Days Of Dating Heck-Day 2....Blood-Sucking Beauty

28 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-28-2002 Day 2 ..^../ Our scene opens with a passed day since the nightmare that was the 'Two Of Hearts' dating game show. But bound by villainous honor, and a heft contract might I add, our hero must complete a gauntlet of three dates with the aforementioned girls introduced to him at the show which begins today. Of all the places where you could start a date, this seems to be one of the most unusual as our scene opens to a dark and dreary night, thunder booming in the distance with dark clouds swarming the night sky, the moon barely visible as it shines down upon a desolate wasteland below...filled with malnourished trees, barren earth, and filthy expanses of water unfit for drinking...all of this laying before a towering brick castle in the background, with each strike of thunder, the light illuminating it's ghoulish exterior ominously. A winding dirt road leads to what appears to be an old 15th century castle...the eerie silence the kind that scary movies dream to have. Just like all scary movies though...you always need a dumb traveler looking to wind up facing whatever evil lies within that structure....and who more dumber then RENT-A-VILLAIN, who just so happens to traversing the winding road in his brand new Rent-A-Car V8 EEEEEEVIL Edition, a 2000 Mustang with candy black paint and numerous villain decals strewn all over and of course to top off the evil look....a pair of fuzzy dice hanging from the rearview mirror. He bobs his head from side to side happily, the red dirt kicking up behind him as he winds the trail and his light blue eyes lighting up with joy behind his orange and black mask as he sings with joy along to what appears to be 'Born To Be Wild' thundering from his sound system ..^../Rent-A-Villain:'BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOORN TO BE EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEVIL, BOOOOOOOOOOOOOORN TO BE EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEVIL!!!!!!!!' ..^../ Thrashing his head to the old school stylings of that rock classic, our hero finally makes the last turn which leads through the decrepit gates of the castle, our hero pulling into the gray earth which sits before the large wooden front door with an old-fashioned door knocker embedded upon it as he puts his his car in park, shutting it off as all goes quiet again. Stepping out of the car, our villain closes the door and takes a moment to primp his jolted black and blonde hair in the mirror, also looking at his attire...which includes no shirt, his well sculpted chest and arms covered in an elaborate body art of a phoenix rising from the ashes, and the memorable combination of clear baggy pants, smiley face boxers, and black combat boots. Looking a bit troubled by his already unique, to say the least, dress sense, our malevolent minion rubs his chin in question as he asks himself ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'This is a DATE....I have to look presentable....I KNOW!!' ..^../Our villain dives body first into the Rent-A-Car V8EE, his legs sticking out as he reaches out for something in the backseat. Shortly, he emerges holding a black tuxedo bow tie in hand as he rounds it about his neck, clasping it together via the hook as he looks at himself yet again in the mirror with the addition of the bow tie as he smiles with approval, saying to himself ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'SEXY VILLAINS OF VALARIA, what damsels could dare turn down a villain with the....BOW TIE OF DELICIOUSNESS!!?! With this bow tie around my neck, I will be as delightful and delicious to look at to a woman just as a little 8 year old girl is as delicious to R.Kelly! AWAY I GO!'..^../ Our villain fixes his bow tie so it sits straight upon his neck, feeling a little more confident with his Pee-Wee Herman inspired wardrobe addition as he walks with that wide stepping stride towards the door of the decrepit castle, clearing his throat and brandishing a a bright smile before knocking on the door with giddy expectation. He idly rocks back and forth on his heels, awaiting an answer when suddenly....the large wooden door creeks open slowly, our villain slightly startled by this act as he slowly approaches the opening, stepping through with care as he looks about cautiously. Upon entering the castle, the door slams itself shut and with that, the castle is illuminated by an army of candles, all flickering on at one simultaneous time as it gives off an ominous glow about the castle, littered with olden paintings, beautiful tapestries, gothic furniture and sculptures, and reeking of ancient antiquity as our villain slowly takes steps through the castle, exclaiming in an inquisitive tone ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'HELLO....ANYONE HOME?!' ..^../ As our villain looks about for an answer, he feels a soft tap against his shoulder, that silky caress spinning Rent-A-Villain about as he turns to find a seductively evil woman standing in front of him...the kind you know you should stay away from yet are drawn to. She places a hand on her hip, smirking at our hero as he eyes her pale skin covered in a sleek black dress, tight to her boddess as she draws a wanting eye to our villain. A hint of red liquid is strewn on the side of her lip, our villain saying to this beautiful vixen ..^../Rent-A-Villain:'Ummm...hello, Velina is it? Nice to meet you your house is quite...umm.....DEAD.' Velina:'Oh why thank you...I had the undertaker decorate himself....of course no need asking what happened to him after.' *smiles sweetly* Rent-A-Villain:'HOLY RED KOOL-AID MIX UNDISTRESSED DAMSEL, you have some strange red substance on your full lips, might I ask what it is?' Velina:'Oh oh, I'm sorry...I ate just a while ago, I'm sorry. It's not like me to spit and yet not swallow...' Rent-A-Villain:'Yes, that tightens my spandex as well.'..^../ Missing the sexual and non-sexual undertones of that statement, Velina softly slides her tongue around to the right side of her lip, licking off the red liquid and swallowing it happily. She then makes slow strides over to our villain, taking his hand in hers as she prepares to lead him into the next room, the gothic vision of gorgeousness saying ..^../Velina:'It's been quite a long time since someone has taken me out on a date...usually they just come here and they never want to leave. So...your a wrestler I've heard. This means you should have some really larger veins...I mean TITLE REIGNS...correct?' ..^../ All the while as Rent-A-Villain breaks into a monologue in reply to the question just asked, Velina is quietly eyeing our hero, mapping out each and every pulsating vein with her eyes as he is speaking. ..^../Rent-A-Villain:'Well...I did happen to have quite a nice Crusierweight title reign and a long Rising Star title reign...but titles are irrelevant this Sunday at Wrestle Vessel when I face up against that villain who tries to be more vile then me, Snow! I can't believe he could dare even lie to himself and think he could best me in a match of physical strength and intensity. His mental games and physical assaults are about as EEEEEVIL as a 5th grade bully, and it's gonna take a lot more than that to foil my scheme to bring him down to his knees and bow down to the TRUE villain of PCW...myself of course. In that Jamaican jail cell...no mercy. He will receive a beating unlike any beating he has experienced within that ring or within the confines of jail. He can't break me....for my superpowers are more than enough to bring him down and end him before he ends me. I mean for King Koopa's sake....where's his snappy evil phrases?! Where are his little henchmen?! Does he even have ANY kind of super gadgets for world domination? I THINK NOT!! I made villainy cool and when we square off, no superhero will be there to aid him...defeat is the only thing he can be certain of my gothic date for the evening. I refuse to be bested in a battle of the VILLAIN'S!! Velina...Velina?'..^../ Our villain turns around, noticing Velina has a brass candlelabra in hand raised above her head in a manner that would crush in our malevolent minion's skull. With that usual dumb inquisitiveness, our villain asks..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'Ummm...what you intend on doing with that Velina? I mean all the places we're gonna go have light. You weren't intending to HIT me with that in an attempt to drain my mortal lifeforce or something were you?' Velina: *being obviously sarcastic, hoping he catches on even* 'Why OF COURSE NOT Rentie....the thought never came across my mind.' Rent-A-Villain:'Alrighty...I'm just gonna turn around and not expect a swift strike to the head with that candlestick then. Here I go...' ..^../ Our villain turns about, Velina amazed by the utter stupidity of Rent-A-Villain before CRASHING the candlelabra over his head, Rent-A-Villain making a girly scream before falling unconcious to the floor. Velina drops the candlelabra, saying to herself followed by a sigh ..^../Velina:'Is it me or do my victim's get dumber and dumber every century.' Later..... ..^../ Our villain awakens, his eyes opening slowly as he finds himself in an ancient looking dungeon, the bricks stained with the flesh and blood of past victims and some of them still chained to the wall, their dried bones still bonded in servitude to Velina. Looking about, our hero notices his hands bonded to a large wooden rack, stripped of his clear pants and boots as he now finds himself in just his smiley face boxers, his feet also bonded by tight leather straps as well against the rack. With a sigh, our villain says ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'Well..can't be any worse then what Aimz had me doing....'I'm not gonna tie you up too tight' my behind!! Now how sould I attempt escape from this...hmm...maybe a James Bond-esque precision laser that will cut through these straps? Or...I CAN WRESTLE AROUND LIKE A LITTLE GIRL WITH THESE BODNS TO NO AVAIL!! Wrestling sounds cheaper...' ..^../As suggested, our vile villain wrestles around with the bonds, unable to free himself as he makes girly struggling noises. Shortly, he is not left alone with his foolish escape antics as the evil Velina opens the steel door in fornt of him, entering the dungeon with a bright smile with two new additions....RAZOR SHARP FANGS on the left and right side. She approaches our bonded bandit of badness, running a finger over his bare chest as he giggles ..^../ Rent-A-Villain:'Hey...that tickles.'Velina:'It's almost a shame I have to kill you Rent-A-Villain...but I need to replenish my lifeforce soon or I will rot away into the Netherworld, never to hold onto ym everlasting youth and beauty...' Rent-A-Villain:'AND NOT TO MENTION BOOTY, WOO, your gifted for a vampress of evil!' ..^../ Velina happens to take a slight glance at her ample behind, her pale face actually managing a blush as she replies..^../ Velina:'Well...fresh blood does keep your rump plump I guess.' *smiles* 'So...any last requests before I drain you of your very being.' Rent-A-Villain:'Well yea....can we fade out and continue this non-sensically funny story in the next RP...I got a pay-per-view series to do ya know.' *winks at the camera* Velina:'Now that's certainlya new one...' ..^../ With that said, the scene begins to fade, the fate of our villain to be decided in the next exciting installment. WILL RENT-A-VILLAIN BE DRAINED OF HIS LIFE FORCE?! WILL HE EVEN LIVE TO FACE SNOW AT THE WRESTLE VESSEL?! IS BLOOD THE NEW SECRET FOR A FIRM BOOTY?! FIND OUT NEXT TIME WHEN RENT-A-VILLAIN DOES THE SAME THING HE DOES EVERY EPISODE.... TRY TO TAKE OVER THE WORLD....ONE DUMB SCHEME AT A TIME!!
