The Greatest Story Ever Told Part 3

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 25th, 2002 *Monologue* What has this company come too? Hiring Nazis, men who think they are Egyptian kings and superhero impersonator. Personally I think it is great that these guys have work but come on, no self respecting man could ever pay a Nazi sympathizer let alone a Nazi. For what it's worth I rather not be paid by this company. I rather just get on television and not take the dirty money earned by Nazis. *End Monologue* Alone in a foreign country. The world seemed to be a large and scary place. Whoever said it's a small world after all didn't know shit. Surviving all I had this should not have been so hard. At the age of three I was living on my own. Soon I was discovered and brought to a Toronto city half way home. I stayed there for almost a full year. Then my saviors arrived. Some were saved by Christ, I was saved by a man and a woman with an endless sack of cash. The next few years weren't hard on myself but rather my adopted parents. They seemed content on saving me from hell. No matter what I did they never gave up on me. Some people kill their biological parents. One thing I wish I could do but can't. My true parents are the ones who showed me love. The drunken fool with a dick who is my dad is only my dad. In the words of a famous man, it takes more than a penis to be a father. My real father is the one who tried to put me on the path of good and pure. The world was a constantly changing place. As a teenager I was a hell raiser. Fights were my thing and I was the ultimate fighter. I thought I was invincible but I soon realized I was anything but when my world came crashing down all around me. My once enemy but now best friend was murdered. The reason behind the murder was me! I killed my best friend. I may not have been the one who drove the knife into him but I am the reason that he was there. I have lived with this guilt and nothing can relieve me of it. Soon after my best friends death I became involved in a world of music and drugs. Ozzy was my new god and I worshiped him. My parents saw my changes as I listened to his wonderful music. In their attempt to free me from his grasp a guitar was purchased for me. I loved it. I soon would be the greatest musician of all time. I would be the next Ozzy Osbourne and that would lead me to my future career. The career that has taken me around the world. Wrestling!
