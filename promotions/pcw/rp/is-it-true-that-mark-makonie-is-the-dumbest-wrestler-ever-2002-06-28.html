Is it true that Mark Makonie is the dumbest wrestler ever?

28 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-28-02 ::Introduction:: What in the hell is Streak talking about? He said that Mark Makonie was trying to act like he was African American in his speech for the National Socialist Party Of America. If Mark is a Neo-Nazi, how can he act like he is African American? Hmm…that boggles my mind because I don’t see how that can be. The National Socialist Party Of America is made up of a lot of different parties. Who said that Mark was running for the leader? No one exactly! Mark was just trying to get on the party to try and add another element to his gimmick and his outside lifestyle away from wrestling. Now Ryan “Streak” Stephens is the guy who came into PCW without a gimmick. How can a guy be in wrestling without a gimmick? It boggles my mind a lot. Another thing, the records do not matter in wrestling, it is how you perform night in and night out, so if you want to make fun of my record all that you want then go right ahead Streak because that doesn’t bother me. ::End Introduction:: The scene opens up to inside a brick building with gray cement to fill in the cracks. The roof is black with shingles and has a steam pipe coming right out of the roof. The door on the building is a brown wooden one with a brass door handle to get in. There is a window that has doesn’t have a curtain pulled so that you can see inside. Inside of the building there is a desk and there are lounge chairs inside that are dark red colored. Mark is seen inside of the building standing in front of the brown colored counter. He is wearing black shorts and has a yellow shirt on that reads on the back: A PCW Superstar. He has on white Adidas Superstar 2G shoes that have black stripes on the side of them. “Could I have your name and where you live?” asks the lady who is behind the counter, dressed in a white button down shirt and a black mini skirt. “Mark Makonie and I am from Pasadena, California.” Mark says as he looks into the lady’s dark brown eyes. “Now could I have your birth date and your martial status?” asks the lady looking up at Mark and seeing that he is staring right at her. “7-12-82 and I am a single man and will stay a single man for awhile.” Mark chuckles with that last statement as he knows he doesn’t have any love feelings for girls and he never has. “Well now if you just have a seat there we will call you back when we have a room available.” The lady says just looking at her computer and then looking up at Mark with a smile on her face.\n Mark sits down and he is the only one who is in the business building right now. Mark is thinking about Fusion and his match with Akiyama, the Neo Void God himself. In the past recent weeks we have seen Akiyama take apart the tag team division single handily. In Mark’s eyes Akiyama is a very good wrestler and he knows that it is going to be a tough match on Fusion to handle this beast himself. But if Mark walks out of Fusion with the victory over the Neo Void God himself than that would set a good tone for Gold and Glory. This match is a tone setter for Mark for the Gold and Glory pay per view. Mark is not saying that he is looking past this match because just like any other match, no one knows what is going to happen in any match. So what was Stephens trying to prove when he said that Mark was looking past his match with Akiyama? I don’t know because Mark stated in a recent date that this was going to be one of the hardest matches that he has ever had and will ever had in the PCW. “How was I trying to act like I was an African American when I was giving a speech for the National Socialist Party of America?” Mark asks himself as he remembers that point of being brought up in Stephens’s promo. “My statement in the speech that I made: “Now I think that the African American race is not as good as us white race…” is when Stephens thought I was trying to act like I was part of the African American culture. When I said “us” I was talking about the white race since I was giving my speech in front of a whole white race audience. You know maybe it was my problem for making myself not clear enough since Stephens thought I was trying to act African American. Wow and he calls me the stupidest wrestler ever? Well we will find that out today.” “Mark Makonie please come back with me.” A lady’s voice calls out as Mark looks up and he sees a different lady standing there with a clipboard in hand. Mark gets up from the red lounge chair that he was sitting in. The lady then turns her back and she tells him to follower her back to the one of the rooms. They go down a hallway and Mark just looks at all of the pictures that are present in this business area. There are pictures of Albert Einstein and a guy who is in a wheelchair. Mark stops right in front of the picture of the guy in a wheelchair. “Who is this?” Mark asks as he is eyeing the picture of the man and then the lady turns around to walk back to where Mark is standing. “That is a man who has a really high score on the IQ chart.” The lady says as she stands next to Mark who is still looking at the picture, as it is a piece of art. “He is really fascinating because this guy was a guy who couldn’t even talk, but yet he score high on the chart. “Well he is white so he must be pretty smart.” Mark says as he is looking over at the lady now instead of the picture. The lady just gives Mark a weird look and she proceeds down the hallway with about four to five rooms on each side. The lady goes all the way down to the end and she goes in the last room. Mark just looks around the hallways as they have info on the brain and how it works and also some more pictures of some famous people known for their IQ. Mark proceeds into the room that the lady went into and there is a table in the room with a cushioned seat. “Please be seated.” The lady responds as Mark gets into the room. Mark goes over to the brown-topped table with a darker brown color for the bars that hold up the table. Mark pulls out the chair and he takes a seat. The lady gives Mark a sharpened number 2 pencil with a tip that is as sharp as a push pin. “Now you will be timed on this test so take it quickly, but answer the questions to your ability.” The lady says as she is standing right in front of Mark who is sitting down with the number 2 pencil in his right hand. The lady gives Mark a booklet test, which consists of a couple of pages. The lady goes to the back of the room so that she can time Mark and so he cannot cheat. Mark takes the number 2 pencil and with his left hand he turns the booklet to page one. Mark looks at the fist question. Question 1: What is 3 + 10?Mark’s Answer: 13 Question 2: What is 49 + 35?Mark’s Answer: 85 Question 3: How many total legs are there on three chickens and eight cows? Hint: think delicious!Mark’s Answer: 38 This is what the IQ test is like and Mark is turning the pages when he gets done answering each question as clearly as possible. On the last section there are some trick questions. Like this is the last question that there was Question 35: p::/w..eees:par/kcosmht.pey.ztx.xyzsp:t, What is your answer?Mark’s Answer: p::/w.eees:par/kcosmht.pey.ztx/xyzsp:t That concluded the test and Mark puts his pencil down on the desk. He scoots his seat out from the table and he heads over to the assistant lady who is standing next to the wall, having her arm lean on it. The lady stops the stopwatch that she has in her hands and she takes Mark’s test that he was just taking. “Please follow me.” The lady says as she takes both the test and stopwatch in her hands as she heads over to the door. Mark does as she says and he makes his way over to the door just like the lady is doing. She leads Mark back to the waiting room at the front of the building and the lady behind the counter is still there typing away on her computer again. “Please have a seat in the chair over there as we calculate these results.” The assistant says as she points over to the section of red lounge chairs. Mark heads over to the chairs once again and he takes a seat like he was told. The lady disappears through one of the back rooms straight back and Mark starts to think again about Gold and Glory, but also about Ryan “Streak” Stephens and his latest promo. “The fact is that I am not worried about you Streak.” Mark says as he is talking to himself once again because he wants to get something off his chest. “I am worried about the fact of my pride can be lost at Gold and Glory if I do not perform the way that my abilities will let me. I will perform like I do every night and that is go out there and fight every fight like it is your last. It is that you came into PCW not having to work for anything because down in PWA you had “D” grades before you were brought up. I came up through the hard way going through PWA for a couple of months and then to NTICW where I made a name for myself. You wonder why I call you a low carder, it is not all about records it is how you perform and I have seen you do not perform that well. You probably just go out there and care about your damn rumors too much to perform. I am just going to tell you Streak that I am going to bring everything I have at you and we will find out who the real low carder is.” After Mark finishes that up the assistant comes out just right in time. She has a piece of paper with her and she starts to go over to Mark. She is walking smoothly with her high heels against the carpet and she then gets to where Mark is sitting. She takes a seat right next to Mark and she holds out the paper so both of them can see it. “You scored pretty high on your IQ test.” The assistant starts to say as she is looking at the paper she is holding. “On this sheet of paper there is all of the questions and what ones that you got right and wrong. It tells your time here at the bottom and finally your IQ score of 75, in this case being low is good.” With that Mark has just proved to Streak that he has a good IQ and that is probably the average of what people get in America anyways. So Mark has just proved that Ryan “Streak” Stephens made everyone dumber by cutting that promo on who the hell knows what. So who is the dumbest wrestler ever now?
