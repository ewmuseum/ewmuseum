Preparing for THE ESCAPE! Part 1

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/22/02 Time: 11: 24 P.MLocation: Shanghaied, ChinaScene: Prison Part 1 “Don’t give in, without a fight.” The scene opens with Juan Borro sitting in his cell, and making like he’s reading the paper. What Juan is really doing, is making a checklist of things he is getting ready for the escape tonight. He is disguising is by pasting it onto an old newspaper. Juan is wearing the same prison suit he always wears, but under his covers he has five Chinese officer uniforms, that he paid for an unknown source. Juan glances at the clock at the end of the hall, and notices it is just thirty minutes until the plan goes into full affect. Juan’s cellmate then jumps up into Juan’s bunk, and just sits there, staring at Juan for a few moments. Juan becomes nervous, and quickly hides the plans not wanting his cellmate to find out that Juan is not sticking by the promise he made to his cellmate a week ago, when they were discussing what would happen if Juan were to plan an escape. Cellmate- I’m probably going to get some sleep now. Juan- Okay. His cellmate is about to just go back into his bunk, just has to get something off of his chest before he does. Cellmate- You know I never really told you how much I admire you. Don’t think I’m fruity or anything, but when I call you rookie and say you’ve seen nothing, I don’t really mean it. I heard the story of how you were put in this place, and how your childhood was, and it’s pretty inspirational. Juan- Thanks man, I appreciate that coming from you. I know how hard you’ve had it in here also. You’ve probably had nightmares in this cell, and I don’t think there is anything you can do unless raping a group of people or being a cereal killer, to make anyone deserve the treatment you were given. Cellmate- Well life goes on and things are forgotten. As the years go on, your mind has to grow, and I’ve had a lot of time to grow in here. Juan- I guess you’re right about that. But I still can’t imagine people being so brutal and heartless. Cellmate- It’s nothing new, it goes on everyday. In a way this place brings you down to reality. When your young you’re so high in yourself and you think you can do anything you want without consequences. This place shows that there are consequences. They let you learn what you did wrong by the best possible way you can, torment. Juan- Ya know, sometimes I don’t even think the owner of the jail, is the one that makes it so bad. It’s mostly the prisoners themselves. Don’t they know it would be so much easier if they were just to cooperate? Cellmate- talking to the men in here is like talking to a brick wall, because they listen to absolutely nothing you say. Juan- It’s a shame. Cellmate- It sure is. The two men go silent for a few moments, and Juan sits back in his pillow. He takes in the vibes from the prison, and realizes how negative things are around here. How there is nothing but sorrow in the air. He thinks about the poor free men like himself, that have to stay in such an awful place without even doing anything. He thinks about the families of the men, or anybody who raised them. Like, who would ever picture their child growing up as a prisoner? Just from that observation it shows most of the guys in here had troubled childhoods, dropped out of school, and had a dedicated like in violence. Juan also wonders about the Scared Straight PTC special he organized. Now that he’s seen a real life prison, he realizes how much of a joke to whole thing was. It would never have to take all that effort to have them stop. All they’d have to do is come here for ten minutes, and that would be reason enough, for them to stop. Juan thought about a lot of things, and didn’t even realize that in twenty minutes he would be taking the biggest risk in his life. Juan knows the consequence, but is trying to block the whole thing from his mind. Juan’s cellmate than comes up from his bunk once again, to say one more thing to Juan, before he goes off to sleep. Cellmate- You know man, I’m glad you took my advice, and decided not to do the whole escape plan. It shows me you actually took something I said to heart. Juan- Juan, well ya know. When answering, Juan makes one of those faces you make when you’re feeling guilty about something. Cellmate- Well just wanted to tell you that. Good night man. Juan’s cellmate now went to sleep, and Juan is surpassed with a huge amount of guilt. Juan just came off as a complete liar, and he feels awful about it, but he knows he’ll feel even more awful if he stays in this place any longer. Juan then lies back down once again, but is quickly interrupted by a whistling Jose, who is in the cell across from him. Jose- Hey man, do you have everything ready and set. The rest of us have all of our parts finished. Juan- Good, I have my part complete, now it’s just a matter of timing. I made sure I recorded the exact second the alarm sounded, that way we’d be right on time for today. I have every single detail down to the bone, not much could go wrong. Jose- I hope your right man. I just still can’t believe that in about 15 minutes I may be on my way to freedom. All I’ve been able to thinks about is that big step I’ll be taking into freedom. Juan- All I can think about is my friends, family, and my profession. It may sound corny, but those are the things that bring me the most enjoyment. Ever since I was a little kid, all I wanted to do was be average, and like everyone else. Right now, I’m closer to that then I’ve ever been, and this got in the way. So I just can’t wait until this is no longer in my way. Jose- Want to go over the plan, one last time, to make sure everything is crystal clear before it goes into affect. Juan- Okay, well let’s go down the list: 1) In about five minutes I’ll take my officer suit and put it on. 2) Second thing in the beginning of the day, I snuck into the main tower, and planted a recording of the open cell recording. That will play in about ten minutes and the cells should all be opens. 3) From there we’ll leave the cage, and then I’ll round everyone up, meaning you four, and give you your suits. From there we’ll leave with the rest of the officers. I even have stuff, we can use to disguise our faces with bruises, so they think we would have been in a scuffle trying to get the men back in their cages. 4) We’ll then stay toward the back of the line, and instead of going back into the may office, we’ll take the security staircase, all the way down to the sewage lines, from their we’ll take a little hike, and even though it might not be pretty it’s the only way out. 5) If timed perfect we’ll big our way out right by the side of the tower, that way the officers are off angle, and can’t see what’s directly under them. Then we’ll go for a run, and begin screaming, someone has escaped in Chinese. We’ll still be in our uniforms, and won’t be close enough to see our faces so they should fall for it. From then on, we’ll leave the area, and take our trip to the water. And from their were pretty much free. Jose-Sounds good. Juan- In five minutes, we’ll be on our way out of this place. Jose- I can’t wait. And with that the two men waited anxiously, as in just a few moments they would be taking an all or nothing risk. Juan knows the consequences at hand, but he doesn’t care, because there is only one thing on his mind, and that’s being FREE. Scene Fades to Black. TO BE CONTINUED…………..
