Some Questions Answered

22 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/22/03 The wind whips the trees vigorously causing them to swish around as the sound of crunching leaves can be heard. In one instant the serenity of the woods is shattered with a single gun shot, which ripples into the air causing birds to fly hastily from their nests and squirrels to peak out of their burrows with much interest. “Are you sure this is necessary?” a sore and tired voice complains in the distance. “Stop your complaining and keep your eyes open.” a familiar voice retaliates. “We have been out here all day and I am sure the hunters will go away once they kill a few deer. What’s a few deer anyway? NOTHING! I don’t really think it’s necessary to come out here and bother them.” “That’s not the point. They are out here in a protected zone which means they are breaking the law. I for one am not going to let a situation like this just slip under my nose. Besides the mere thought of those poor little deer being killed for mere game appalls me in every possible way.” The voices edge closer and closer, and the two males finally come into view. Standing there in matching beige colored shirts and black pants are Kramer Rinc (aka Kramer Wild Wing), and his former manager, and actor, Jason Alexander. Jason was the one doing all the complaining which is evident by his sour mug. Why I didn’t just stay home I’ll never know. Jason thinks to himself as he and Kramer travel deeper and deeper into the woods rounding closer to the men showing their pride in killing animals. As they make it past a clearing with a dead tree sprawled out on the ground, they see a group of four men wearing bright orange vests and matching hats. Judging by the odor roaming in the air, they have had quite a lot to drink. The scattered beer cans on the ground also give it away. “Then I told her, listen shut the hell up before I skin ya right here right now. She was like, I was just trying to make talk…..” a standard sized man, with black a mustache says as he swings a can of beer in one hand, and a shotgun in the other. “Excuse me, but I do believe that what you gentleman are doing out here is illegal, and I will call the cops if necessary.” Kramer pipes up while keeping his distance, puffing out his chest to seem tough. Their presence is obviously a shock as the four men stand in a curved line looking at Kramer and Jason with derisive faces. The man on the end steps out and takes a few steps towards them, which causes Kramer and Jason to back up in alarm. “How long have you two been here?” the man asks softly trying not to scare them anymore. “Uh..we…we have been here for a while!” Kramer says decisively. “What all did you see?” another man asks, stepping forward as well. “We didn’t really see anything per say but we do know that you have been shooting things all morning, and I would really like to know what it is.” Kramer replies. “Kramer look over there.” Jason whispers nodding his head towards the side of the men. Kramer looks and what he sees causes his eyes to widen. Poorly covered up by fallen leaves is a hand which lays palm up and bloody. Kramer gulps loudly as a bead of sweat forms on his forehead. The men haven’t noticed that they have seen anything as they continue to stare at the two men who are on the verge of soiling their pants no doubt. Suddenly from the right of the woods comes another man with a big blood stain on his light gray shirt. “Mr. Small was a bitch to bury.” he says without looking up. As he does he sees Kramer and Jason standing there and instantly shuts his mouth. “You…you’ve been murdering people out here!” Kramer exclaims too wound up to keep it locked inside any longer. “I said let’s just leave it alone, and you forced me to come out here! Can’t we just pretend that you were killing deer and you let us go?” Jason pleads as the men have their guns pointed at them. “If only things could be that simple.” one of the men says as he steps forward with his gun pointed right at Kramer’s head. Before we can see anything further, the image becomes fuzzy like how it would be on a television screen. Then slowly a camera backs up showing that it is in fact a television. The person who had been watching this was Brianna Washington who looks very very surprised. She looks down at the letter in her hand and begins the process of rereading it. I said that I would inform you on my predicament as soon as I could, and here it is. Freaky isn’t it? You must be wondering to yourself how we got all of this on tape? I can’t explain that part of it to you right now, but Jason and myself are now fleeing for our lives. These weren’t any ordinary murderers. They have a whole clique and the five men you saw are now in jail, and their connections are now looking to kill us. I still have so much more to tell, but I am not at liberty to say. All you need to know from this point is that Grimer is very important in shutting this unit of mafia men down. If they get a hold of him, Jason, or myself, then the whole case is blown to bits and they continue doing the bad things they do. Kramer “I didn’t know things were that extreme. I just hope that Kramer and Jason manage to stay safe. He doesn’t have to worry about Grimer, because I have things well under control.” Brianna says to herself as she pulls the tape from the VCR and puts it back into the jacket it came in. She then peers up to the clock which reads 6:49pm. “Milo!” She jumps up and runs straight to the door throwing on a light pink jacket. Before she rushes out the door, she grabs a cage which was sitting on the coffee table. Sitting inside is Grimer who is fast asleep. “Can’t leave you here all by yourself.” Brianna whispers to him as she tries to keep the cage level while hurrying out the door. The scene fades to black for a mere second, before reopening inside a car with Brianna at the wheel. She drives moderately fast while following all the signs along the way. Strapped in by a seatbelt beside her is Grimer who is still peacefully sleeping inside of his cage. She pulls up in front of a movie theater highlighting “Chicago” in big flashing lights. Standing underneath the vibrant sign is a very sullen looking Milo who walks slowly to the car pulling the passenger side door open. “You know, there is nothing you should fear more than a homosexual male left outside for nearly half an hour because his friend obviously doesn’t own a watch.” he looks at Brianna who has an “I’m sorry” look posted on her face. Milo goes to sit down but sees the cage and starts screaming like a little school girl. “You brought…brought that animal?!” he says stepping away from the car. “Oh come on Milo, he isn’t a harm at all. Just get in the backseat.” “Say what you want but those rats…” “He’s a mouse.” “Mouse, rat, they all carry diseases.” “Do you want a ride or not? Look I’m sorry that I was late, but if you don’t get in here in five seconds you can find another way home.” “Alright, but only because my feet are killing me from STANDING so long.” he fusses as he gets into the backseat and slams the door. “Are you ever going to explain why you are keeping it anyway? Why not have it exterminated or give me a bat and I’ll beat it to smithereens.” “I really wish I could tell you but I can’t.” “Well then when can I move back into the house? Is my wall still being fixed due to that damn old lady crashing into it with her bent up Cadillac?” “Uh yeah. They said it will probably take another couple of weeks.” Brianna revs the engine and drives off down the street as the scene fades to black. She really wants to explain to Milo that the real reason he can’t come home is because things just aren’t safe. Brianna doesn’t want to run the risk of dragging him into everything because it’s hard enough watching after herself, and Grimer…..
