A very strong odor tickles his nostrils, as Abner peers out onto the lawn. And there, still lying face down, head buried in the snow, is the stiff, cold body of the still-sleeping cameraman. “What in da hell is some gay doin’ on my lawn?” And then finally coming to, the cameraman’s mind kicks in, and he comes back to life….. “Why…..just you wait ‘til I get my hands on you…..” ---------- Miles down the road, on their way to the local hospital….. “Crickey!” “Steve, what is it?” “Jarred……” THE END
