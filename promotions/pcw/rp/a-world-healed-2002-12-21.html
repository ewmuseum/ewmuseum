A world healed

21 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/12/02 Let us imagine that at some point in the future, the ambitions of the planet's true masters was fulfilled. 'The sky has become blotted out. Clouds starve the earth of it's precious sunlight because of continued abuse of humanity's devices. In retalitation, or rather, as thanks, Vampires were allowed to march freely upon the parched soil. The planet, in a state of cataclysm, has lost almost all of it's natural resources. We had waited aeons. We were the planet's rightful masters, for the reason that we required no more than a balance. We only took blood, something that regenerates. We didn't require technology. We were CONTENT. Then at some point, you came along. You and your ideas... the wheel. Fire. Gunpowder. Electricity. Computers. NUCLEAR WEAPONS. You greedy bastards. You slaughtered almost all the vampires in existence. The only ones you left alone were the ones you couldn't tell apart from yourselves. You were paranoid. The entire human race purged us like Stalin. You came as friends and we allowed your number to grow. We agreed that in return for hosts for us, you would be allowed to be our 'special' species. You would tip the balance if it would benefit us. And you betrayed us, until the only vampires that remained were those we had taken. We are their descendants. We are the survivors. We are those that nowhave taken our place. Our numbers grew among you. And you thought that you could be rid of us with your 'deterrent' of nuclear weapons. And that is why the ground births no grass. Why no rain falls and why the sky is red. You created your own Hiroshima. You created your own Nagasaki. Then finally, you created Moscow, Washington, Beijing and London, in the push of a mistaken button. Maybe 50 or so years passed... and you are driven into lead-filled cities to shield yourself from your own fists. Then WE came. We ripped off those doors and took these vast arks as our own. We renamed them to our own designs. Where was your technology then? Where were your guns and your swords and your sticks? Albert Einstein once said 'I do not know which weapons will be used for World War 3. But World War 4 will be fought with sticks and stones.' And wasn't he right? Soon, in accordance with you wiping out almost all other species on your planet... sorry... OUR planet... your numbers wre once again... BALANCED. Balanced to the number of remaining species. I once saw a mouse cry because it couldn't find a mate. Loneliness penetrates all armour and transcends all other fears. Death is so cold because it us loneliness. We are without our friends. We are without our allies. Even without enemies to acknowledge our presence. And unless someone tells us we are so... then we are not. That is why I did this... in what was once known as Las Vegas, Nevada, now renamed in MY very own honour for leading the assault here... Ireulia, I saved you for last. Your grandchildren, your children, your wife... I won't even drain their corpses. You loved her so much after you stole her. And your children, such wonderful parents they made... if I were capable, I'd cry for taking the lives of those little cherubs, but they couldn't walk on two legs. So, in human logic, they are lesser beasts. Cattle. FOOD. And as you stand there, quivering, you gaze down at your wife... how many years was it? I don't care at all... for those years, I had gone back with the proposition... the proposition to finally fulfil the writings in the book of Arrabus. I started the final revolution. I led the first and the last charge on the human retreats. And now, Gareth Green... it gives me unequalled pride and pleasure... to drain the blood of the final human being left on the Earth.' We all have our fantasies. Our perfect scenarios. Our ideal roles. But I can see this. I see myself pressing a traitor against the wall by his throat. I see myself gazing into his yellowing, tired eyes and sinking my talons into his wrinkled, spotted skin. And me, as I was when I was turned, with my long violet hair, skin of milky youth and glorious in my divine perfection. My breath is silent in my lungs save to speak. His weary, worn out lungs strain to take in what they can through a shrunken wind pipe. But most of all... I see the tears waiting to emerge. After all... what would it be like to be the last man alive? Had Gaz not turned his back on me, I might not have turned my back on the species I thought deserved a second chance. But then again, nobody wants to be a martyr. Greed led Gaz into this as it has led mankind for millenia. And for his beliefs in greed... he died. Of course, this is just a daydream. But if one believes hard enough... they can make their dreams come true. Meh, then again, I'll just slaughter the mook tomorrow in front of everyone in Canada. Saves time and mess.
