I Wish Upon a Shooting Star!

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/20/02 Time: 8: 24 P.M Location: New YorkScene: Roof of Apartment “ Can I still make a Wish Upon a Star?” 1st Title Defense The scene opens with Juan Borro sitting down on the top of the roof of his apartment building. He’s been up there quite awhile and seems to be a bit confused. Juan knows that he promised to himself that he wouldn’t think about things as much, but he still needs some time to get away and relax. The day is nice with warm weather and clear sky. Juan looks down below at all of the people in a rush to get home from work. All of the children chasing their buses, and getting nervous over finding rides. Juan wonders why people put so much stress on themselves. Stress leads to absolutely nothing. It’s hard to control, but once you can take of it, it’s not anything that’s too hard to control. Juan stares at the sky thinking about what’s up there. They’re probably aren’t any jobs or stress up there. It’s all good and everyone just gets to relax and be completely free. The only problem is, what’s the fun of just relaxing all day. There has to be something more up there to keep us occupied. Juan thinks about when he was a young kid and always used to go to church. Juan knows Chef and some other fellow stable mates don’t agree with his religious background of Christianity, but Juan stands strong behind it. He knows God is up there looking after him, and has guided him this far in life. Juan went to Catholic school until he could no longer afford it, but Juan felt no satisfaction of a catholic school. It was just strict rules, beginning for money, and lots of unreleased anger. For a school that it supposed to be dedicated to God, it didn’t seem like that nice of a place. Juan preferred going to the church and just spending days in there. It was always cool and he knew all the priests well. It was a nice place to be and Juan knew he’d always be safe there. As Juan looks upon the star he is about to say something, but interrupted by Juliet who comes walking up the steps. Juliet: Hey Juan, what’s going on up here? Juliet grabs a coat and puts it on, as the climate gets cooler. She then walks outside and sits by Juan. Juan: It’s been a tough last two months. I’m happy all of this is finally coming to an end and I have time to just relax. Juliet: Yea, I must admit it sure does feel good to have some free time on our hands. We actually get to spend time together also. Juan: Yea, I was just thinking about myself the last few weeks, and thought about how much I have grown in the last two months. I’ve went from power trips to the complete bottom to depression to hardships and struggles, and now I’m at a point that’s kind of hard to explain. Juliet: In a way I actually realize how you feel. I think I’ve kind of grown with you. I mean come on Juan I used to be the normal college girl who than worked at a library. You opened me to a whole new world outside of books. A new culture a new lifestyle, a new outlook on certain matter, and a new everything. Juan: Well it’s nice to know I’m able to change peoples lives. I’ve doubted my own strength before, and I just hope I can one day become a dominant force that can influence people. Is that so much to ask? Juliet: No. Just make sure you know where to draw the line. Just think of all of the people you’ve already influenced in your life. I’m sure you write a book just on that. Juan and Juliet are about to kiss when they are interrupted by a door slam. When they turn around they see Chef with Bertha and Elian behind him. Juan: Oh woah, what are you guys doing here? Chef: We just thought we’d invite ourselves over and go out to dinner. I mean if that’s all right with you. Juan: Yea, it’s fine we were just talking. Juliet notices Elian and waves to him. Juliet Aww!!!! So that’s the little giant over there. I’ve heard a lot about that little one, and it is all-good. Elian blushes and hides behind Bertha’s leg. Bertha: Don’t mind him he’s a f*cking cr*ck head loser. It’s a sign of f*cking love, do you know what the f*ck I’m talking about b*tch. Juliet: Excuse me? Juan taps Juliet on the shoulder. Juan: Don’t mind her, she tends to curse a lot. What can I say clones can’t be perfect now can they? Juliet: Umm…..Okay. Chef: Juan, I’m sorry I forgot to congratulate you on standing up for the International politics against those dirt bag ingrates called Politically Incorrect. It’s a shame people still believe in such a money hungry and dishonest system. Juan: It sure is a shame, but what can you do? It should be a both respectable and good match, but expect Kevin and I to come out on top. Chef: Where is Kevin anyway? Juan: I don’t know, he was supposed to be here an hour ago. Scene fades to busy streets where Kevin Klaymore is sweating and screaming as he is stuck in a load of traffic. Kevin: Oh my God I hate this country and this state, and all of these goddamn scumbags here. Mandy: Calm down Kevin, it isn’t that bad we’ll be there in no time. Kevin: I was supposed to meet Juan an hour ago to go and train for our match. He’s going to be pissed off. Mandy: Oh please Kevin, you guys always train, do you think one little time will really hurt. You’re fighting an old man and a snob, why are you so worried about physical characteristics. Kevin: I guess you’re right on that one. Mandy: Of course I am. Mandy then slowly lifts her skirt up until she shows a lot more of her ASSets. Kevin quickly notices and gets excited as, he jumps in the back and starts making out with Mandy. Kevin opens the door as the two go rolling out of the car into grass field, which is off by the side of the road. The traffic finally end, but Kevin’s car is still in the way and the drivers are becoming mad and impatient. Driver: Move you’re f*cking car. All we see in a middle finger pop up form the grass field until it is yanked back down. Kevin and Mandy continue to make out as the scene shifts back to Juan. Chef: Well I think you guys should be able to win, if anyone can bring that tag division to it’s peak, you are the guys. Juan: Thanks. Bertha: Let’s go, the f*cking rat a$$ is hungry over here. Chef: Well I’ll be in our car, were going to get going. Chef, Bertha and Juliet go on there way until Juliet stops. Juliet: Juan are you coming? Juan: I’ll be there in a second. The scene then goes to Kevin and Mandy who have finished doing their business. Mandy then gets up and adjust her self. She makes her way to Kevin’s car and calls out to him from there. Mandy: Are you coming? Kevin: I’ll be there in a second. The screen then splits as it shows both Juan and Kevin looking at the sky at the precise time. They see a star shoot through the sky, and when they see it they both make their wishes at the same time. Juan and Kevin: I wish to succeed. With that said both men go to where they have to be. You may think that Golden Game is all ready at where they have to be, but some people believe to the contrary. Golden Game knows they can do more, and are willing to use every team they face as just another stepping stone to their success. Scene Fades to Black.
