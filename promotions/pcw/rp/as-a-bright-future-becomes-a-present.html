As a bright future becomes a present.......

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Hmmm, is that EST or over here? Foreword written (in third person) by Corey Williams CCW, 'Controversial' Corey Williams.......... The man who on Turmoil destroyed Orpheus Grant to make his way into the main event of the March Madness, finally proving that the white man IS superior. For not only can he move better than a black man, but his wits easily outweigh a black man's dumb strength. Corey, having little doubt that he would win in the first place, now moves onto bigger and better things. Well.... I guess that's not exactly an accurate statement, seeing as OG is one of the largest wrestlers and PCW, and a member of Management could easily take out some punkass nigga like Timo Bolamba or Afwa Kilmanjaro for that matter. And as for Boda, that guy is at least a little better than Jeremy Howard, and maybe he'll pose some sort of threat on Friday, that is as long as that don't make this 6-man tag match for a belt, seeing as Boda has so much trouble capturing them. The Wild Deuces? At least when New Breed broke away from them we had enough respect for them to kick their asses a few times, this supposed new rendition is nothing more than a jobber stable. Red Rock? Was that guy even in PCW2? If he was he sure didn't hold a light to me. Tim Walden? To quote Blink182, 'say it ain't so, I will not go, turn the lights off, CARRY me home.' New Breed has surely proved its dominance in PCW, don't believe me? Ask The Rush as he goes around calling himself the 'greatest man alive.' Ask Darkstar, calling himself a 'Dark Messiah.' Ask every goddamn 'good guy' in the business who can't seem to understand how we can be hated by the fans AND be good wrestlers. Hell, it's never been done by anyone else coz we ain't anyone else. We're the New Breed, the most innovative stable ever. Breaking all the rules and not giving a shit what anyone thinks......... oh and this time, we mean the fans too! ::The scene opens up in a dim-lit room. The camera focuses in on 'Controversial' Corey Williams, leaning against a wall, looking at something. The camera focuses more to reveal that what Corey is looking at is a replica of the PCW Rising Star Title, Corey cautiously touches it with his hand, he moves his fingers up and down the belt, stroking it like one would stroke a pet.:: Corey: You see Jeremy, I have a Rising Star Title too..... ::Corey turns to face the camera, his face surprisingly calm.:: Corey: And no, I didn't go buy this at Shopzone.com, I EARNT this, much like you earnt your Rising Star Title before me. Both of our careers were born in the same fashion, which is strange when you look at us now. I am a member of the most elite group in PCW, a former Extreme Champion and the most controversial man in PCW. You, on the other hand, what have you achieved? Nothing, leader of the jobberest jobby jobber stable to ever job and.... well.... I guess that's it. ::Corey smirks as that face that can go instantly from a confident grin to a vicious snarl begins to get into gear. The fact that Corey is so utterly indulged in himself and New Breed allows him to 'overlook' Jeremy Howard’s other achievements, the peak of which is being the World Heavyweight Champ.:: Corey: Now that is strange, but not to strange to someone with my intellect, for you see, I have something that you lack, that being courage. You are a wuss Howard, you have been from the moment you were born, THAT is why you ran away from your Friday meeting with New Breed. Hell, I don't blame you, facing the three most athletic, well conditioned and all-round supreme beings to ever walk the face of PCW, that's a scary thought for even me! ::Corey looks back to his RS belt replica.:: Corey: You know, that belt may not be real Howard, but the history of it is. And history says that I EARNT that replica behind me Jeremy. Don't believe me ? Just ask your ex-wife, Timo....... ::Corey turns about 45 degrees to the right, as if turning to talk to a different person. The camera mysteriously changes accordingly, so that Corey is facing the camera once again.:: Corey: Ahh, there's no place like Russia, Timo, ship-jumper Ivan Stanislav can attest to that. The sights, the sounds, the smells. The sights? None better than seeing your body sprawled across the mat, just barely conscious enough to see me crawl on top of your body. The sounds? How about hearing the referee slam his hand against the mat....1.....2......3! The smells? How about the smell of defeat, Timo? You sure smelt a lot of that in Russia. ::Corey grows more confident by the minute as he insults Timo through the camera, no fear of the consequences. For even those damn Samoans with all there Voodoo magic can't reach through the camera and grab him.:: Corey: How does it feel, Timo? How does it feel to know that someone is better than you? That THAT someone will always, ALWAYS be better than you. Seriously Timo, I'm not just asking the question for sh*ts and giggles, I'm honestly curious........it's never happened to me before. ::Corey's grin grows to a climax.:: Corey: I bet it feels terrible, terrible to know that no matter what you accomplish from this moment on in your life, that person will always have one over you. But I guess you've gotten used to it, after all you have to feel a lot of that Timo. Because despite the fact that I constantly make fun of Afwa's intellect, the fact is that of the two, you are the dumbest Timo. Coz only a man of true stupidity would get himself kicked out of PCW's premier stable. Only an IDIOT would choose love over superiority. Because that's what it is, isn't it Timo? You can't hang around with a buff Samoan guy who can barely put together a sentence, let alone an intelligent conversation and calls you 'precious' and then tell me that there's no homosexual tension going on there. You can't hang around with some black dude named Afwa who wants to 'eat' Jason Snow and seems to have a weird pineapple fetish and then tell me that there's not a little summin' summin' going on. You're a dirty man Timo, and I'm not just talking about the colour of your skin...... ::Corey gives a short laugh.:: Corey: Heh, I guess this just proves the old saying, the more things change the more they stay the same, because on Sunday, all your accomplishments, all your titles and all the support from the fans won't mean d*ck. Because on Sunday, we go back to Russia, and once again I prove to everyone that I am better than you! ...But you should be proud Timo, no one else can say that they've been involved in two milestone matches in my career, and you will. Because after I beat you on Sunday, it's only gonna keep gettin' better and better. I've got a bright future ahead of me, pity I can't say the same for you. But just remember one thing Timo, one thing as we grow apart as I move to the top whilst you sink to the bottom, as I live out my destiny and that bright future becomes the bright present..... ::Corey turns around to look back at the RS Title, the camera zooms out to see another podium, on which an Extreme Title replica sits, the camera zooms out further to reveal five more podiums, each with placards on them. Cruiserweight, TV, Continental, Tag Team.....and World. Corey looks back to the camera with a smartassed grin as the camera zooms back in on his face.:: Corey: ......We'll always have Russia. ::The camera sits on Corey's face for a few moments, then it zooms out to reveal the podiums again before fading to black. As the scene fades out, we can hear faint, but unmistakeable, evil, bone-chilling laughter.:: !The End!
