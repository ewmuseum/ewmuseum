Date: not entered The waterfront loading docks of San Jose. Salt air lingers in the drawing dusk, helping to somewhat mask the filth and rot of the shipyard. “Lord of Pain… can you be sure this is the proper locale?” We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he and his partner in crime (fighting), The Masochist, pick their way through the rows of warehouses in the older, run down section of the San Jose International Commerce Zone. Steele looks at what appears to be a very average building from his vantage point behind rusted out barrels, the door slightly ajar with only inky darkness to be seen inside. The Masochist shrugs as he creeps up behind Big Al. “This is the place the tipster said. Something about strange, bizarre rituals and a sacrificial virgin…” Big Al’s face contorts into a scowl of unbridled anger, his hands balled into fists and shaking in anger. “By the gods! Demonic sacrifices to unholy deities… the very thought of it causes the bile to intermingle in the blood!! I cannot allow such an innocent and virginal woman to be torn asunder by the Hades-spawned devils intent on appeasing the underworld in a grab for unholy powers!!” “Now, get a grip, Big Guy,” the Masochist cautions, “I’m getting a baaaaaad feeling about all this. the phone call couldn’t be traced, and what are the odds of there being 21 year old virgins left in San Jose?” The last sentence is abruptly cut off, as a towering Big Alan Steele grips the Masochist by the neck, rising him into the air. He snarls into his face uncharacteristically… “Do NOT besmirch the good and virtuous women of San Jose, as I know at least eight saintly Graces back home worthy of such respects!!” “Okay, okay!!” chokes the Masochist, “Lemmee down!!” Steele seems to regain his control and drops him, a nervous look crossing his heroic visage. “I apologize, Lord of Pain,” Steele begins, stammering, “I… I have to admit to being somewhat…” “Wound tighter than the rubber tubing around Robert Downey Jr.’s arm,” the Masochist finishes, rubbing his neck, “Hey, I understand. Frisco’s got a hold on you…” The fists get tighter, a flush overtakes Big Al’s cheekbones as he growls low and menacingly. “And come Sunday, when he is thusly delivered into my eager hands…” The Masochist raises his hands in caution, “Whoa, whoa, whoa! Easy, man! See, that’s what I’m talking about. It’s rare to have someone get inside your head like this. Which is why I think this may be a trap.” “A trap?! You suspect treachery?! Zounds!! It had not even occurred to me!!” “Hell, it’s not like he hasn’t already tried to catch you like that. And plus with all the suspicious elements of this one…” Suddenly, an ear-piercing scream cuts through the air, both Big Alan Steele and the Masochist straightening at the sound. “SWEET MOTHER HERA!! Yon damsel is in distress!! Hi-Keeba!!” Steele leaps up, preparing to bolt for the warehouse, but the Masochist places a gentle hand on his shoulder, holding him back. He smiles emotionally. “Just be careful, Big Man.” Steele grins, placing a gruff hand on the Masochist’s shoulder. “You always DID become emotive during duress, but fear not, gentle Lord of Pain!! The son of Zeus shall prevail!! Now, quickly! Put forth into action our normal procedures and by the GODS we shall give them what for!!” The Masochist salutes and runs off as Big Al turns, his face menacing as he cracks his knuckles. Soon, he is running towards the warehouse and the door that is slightly ajar. With one, smooth motion, he pulls the heavy, metal door back on its whining hinges, the little light left not able to penetrate the darkness inside. His eyes narrowing in a futile attempt to see inside, he pauses at the entrance, hoping to catch a glimpse of what lay beyond the doors… The scream shakes him from his thoughts and, without thinking twice, he plunges headlong into the shadows of the huge warehouse. BOOM!! As soon as he crosses completely inside, the doors slam shut behind him. Cursing silently, he turns back to them in an attempt to push them back open. The steel does not give. He turns back to the emptiness, seeing no other alternative now. His sandals echo oddly against the metallic floor, as he slowly makes his way forward. His eyes are adjusted to the darkness, but can provide no other information as to what lies before him. Steele begins to grow angry. He strikes a suitably heroic pose and shouts. “Cowards!! Verminous scum!! Show yourselves and face the righteous retribution of Big Alan Steele!! Hi-Keeba!!” His words rebound off the walls, along with a new sound. Titters. Giggles. Barely constrained laughter above him. And around him. All around him, from every direction… The lights snap on, forcing Big Al to raise his arms to the painful intrusion. He drops to a knee, hiding his eyes from the searing lights as the laughter begins. The massive warehouse has been converted into a sort of arena. Big Al crouches in the exact center of the pit, the high, thirty-foot high metal wall lined with all manner of villainous scum. The Moa, his blackened, shining stone Easter Island face grinning wickedly down on Big Al. The Vulture, in his full costumed regalia, guffawing underneath his monstrous nose. The notorious Big Tobacco, downsizing all in his desperate bid to allow California its cigarettes. Chronos. The Succubus. Disgruntled Pete. The Baker. Broken Felix. We-6. Kool Aid Man. Big Duce. Experiment Number 581. Black Sampson… and many more. All have turned out to witness the spectacle of Big Alan Steele in gladiatorial combat to his death. And standing on a dais like Caesar in days of old, grins the ringmaster of it all. His huge, engorged cranium tilts back as he laughs at Big Al’s predicament, his great, bald skull braced by his comely female arm candy so he can re-lift it into position. The Head. “Bravo, Big Alan Steele!! I must admit, you do not disappoint in your predictability. I sincerely thank you for being so gullible, so naïve, so that all these paying customers shall be allowed to watch the show of the century… the demise of Big Alan Steele!!” “The cerebral assassin!!” gasps Big Al. “The same!! BWAHAHAHAHA!!” The villains follow suit and begin to hoot and holler, as the Head takes a sip from his vodka martini in an attempt to look cool. His giant head bobs on his little body, threatening to tip him, but his ever-present female attendant, Propsy McUphold steadies his massive noggin. Steele shakes an angry fist towards the Head, shouting for all to hear. “Your entertainment, such as it is, shall have to come from elsewhere as I, Big Alan Steele, shall not be defeated so easily!! I’ll see you and you ilk in Hades before tonight is through!!” More jeers from the partisan crowd, as the Head smirks. “Such bravado from what amounts to a condemned man! Now, I’m not saying you won’t survive for a WHILE, but eventually, you shall lie dead at my feat, as I doubt you can survive all that I have in store for you…” The crowd cheers as the circular, metal floor begins to slowly rotate clockwise, as well as tilting. Big Al adopts a wide stance to retain balance as the floor sways underneath him. The Head grins as he begins to hit buttons on his control panel with unabashed glee. Eight separate doorways surrounding the rotating platform open up, each revealing a robotic assassin with a different implement of destruction. On with a flame thrower, another with a whip sizzling with electricity, still another spinning blades. The robots sit in their stalls with unfeeling eyes, glowing softly with green light. Big Al turns slowly with the platform, taking it all in as the Head cackles with delight. “You shall find the walls of your arena crackling with energy, making any attempt to reach us above a rather ‘shocking’ achievement. And I can read your face, Big Alan Steele. You think a mere eight robots cannot stop you, well I’m telling you I may agree with you. So fear not, son of Zeus, as more of them await, each with a more deadly weapon and each programmed to respond to your aggression!!” Steele’s face grows dark as the Head continues. “You cannot win the day, Alan! So at least die like a true warrior, in the process providing us with the greatest spectacle ever known to Villain-kind!!” Cheers and applause, as the mechanical monsters begin to slowly clank towards Steele. We now re-rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, at Stately Steele Manor, along with the Graces, trusted manager Sue Saksnussum, and the Masochist in this roleplay already in progress… Big Al takes a long draught from his mug of hot chocolate, with marshmallows, before continuing. The girls, especially, are desperate in their anticipation. “So?” Tammi asks. Steele plays coy, a wry smile upon his lips, “So indeed…” “WHAT HAPPENED!!” “Twas then when it hit me, like a bolt straight from the arm of Zeus himself!! I was, indeed, trapped in a situation where extrication seemed quite impossible. Not only my fate was at stake, but also the entire metropolis of San Jose as well, for surely these villains would run rampant across the sunny city without regard for fellow man! I could not win by their game…” Sue’s face lights up, as he blows out the smoke of a cigarette. “Big Al!” she exclaims, “Did you come to a revelation today?” BAS nods, his grin barely contained by his face, “Indeed I did!!” The Graces are understandably puzzled. Steffi raises her hand questioningly. “Ummm… I don’t get it. What did he get?” “It’s simple,” Sue explains, “When someone is playing a game you can’t win by their rules, you have only one of two options… first is change the rules. And since ‘trickery’ is right out for Big Al…” “I decided to NOT play the game!! Hi-Keeba!!” Big Al says this triumphantly, as Sue nods knowingly towards him. The room remains silent for a moment or two before another lithe hand stretches up from the blonde masses. “Ummm… huh? If I’m hearing this right, you beat them by… not doing anything?” Big Al crosses his beefy arms across his chest as he smiles. “Indeed!” Silence. Another hand rises. “Well, I’ll bite,” says Candi, throwing her hands up in disgust, “Exactly HOW?” “They had anticipated a violent reaction from my own, aggression most extreme in a battle to save my own life! The mechanoids that threatened me were programmed as such, made to respond to my attempts of battle in order to provide maximum entertainment value…” “So when he didn’t fight,” chimes in Sue, “The robots couldn’t attack!” “So, what? You just stood there for, like, ever?” “Correction!!” booms a jubilant Big Al, “I SAT there!!” “Well, that hardly seems the most heroic course of action. How’d you win again?” says Jodi, rather snidely. But Big Al merely wags a finger, still smiling, “But you see how your comprehension fails you! You have yet to take into account the scads of ne’er-do-wells lining the upper balcony!! The sight of your Olympic demi-god squatting in the center circle drove them into a fury!! Soon enough, they began to fight amongst themselves, and thusly, the automatons took THEIR aggression as a threat!!” “The watercourse way… when the path is blocked, the river shall merely flow around the obstacle, gently wearing it down through time…” says Sue in heavy, mystical tones, as she reaches for a coffee. “You had to see it,” adds the Masochist, “The robots were sizzling against the walls trying to get at the fighting villains… what a scene, man.” “Excuse me,” cuts in Suzi, “What exactly did YOU have to do with anything?” “Standard procedure,” states the Masochist, “I go around back and wait. If there’s no fighting, I hang out and see what I can do. Once I saw all Hell break loose, I knew it was only a matter of time before Big Al would get involved. So I went and cut the power line.” “And thusly I stacked robotic corpses, climbed the sheer wall and engaged the remaining villainous curs in hand to hand combat!! Hi-Keeba!” “And.. I learned an important lesson,” he continues, “I learned to once again let the action of a situation come to myself. As Frisco mounts his forces in assault, I must allow myself to be content with reaction, instead of constant anticipation. When his meager obstruction rears its ugly head, I shall not be stopped. Surly he shall rise, and I must be ready, but I cannot be… be…” “Keyed up like ‘It’s Raining Men’ at a San Francisco discotheque?” Debbi quips. Big Al smiles, sagely, adding a thumbs-up. “Hi-Keeba!!”
