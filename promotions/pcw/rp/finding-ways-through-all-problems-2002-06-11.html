Finding Ways Through All Problems

11 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/11/02 The scene opens up on a pretty active street. Many cars of different calibers and colors whiz by hoping to get to their destinations as soon as possible. The camera turns a bit and we see the dark blue house which we know is owned by Kramer Wild Wing. And just as soon as the camera turns, Kramer himself walks out wearing a black silk shirt and dark blue baggy jeans which hang loosely around his waist. He scampers down the few steps that lead to his recently car washed black truck. He peeks into the mirror checking his appearence shifting a few hands of hair back with a quick motion. Jason: No matter what you do your still ugly. Kramer turns a full one hundred and eighty degrees to see Jason smirking in his black suit jacket which sits over a plain white shirt, as well as black pants. In Jason's grasp is a KFC bucket containing sixteen peices of that crispy tender chicken that the world seems to love so much. Kramer: I would take ugly over short, bald, and pudgy any day. Jason: Mocking my class 'A' figure? Jason sets the bucket in the back of the truck so he can strike a pose flexing his muscles. Kramer looks at him shaking his head snickering. He then opens up the car door and gets in buckling himself up in case of an emergency. The passenger side door opens as well and Jason gets in with the bucket in hand. He buckles his seat belt as well and Kramer starts the engine. Jason: So why didn't you bring Grimer along? I thought you were set against leaving him home by himself? Kramer: After watching The End's disturbing promo I have decided that it just isn't safe. I can't risk Grimer getting eaten by an iguana named Juju. They would be a terrible way to go. Jason: Speaking of the The End....have you seen the trailer for it? Kramer: For what? After turning the key several times, the engine finally starts up, and Kramer takes off down the street on his way to Baltimore, Maryland. He flips on the AC and almost instantly the car fills up with nice cool air which battles the hotness emitting from the sun. Jason: The End. Kramer: Oh yeah. It looks quite interesting. Although they could have used a real star. Jason: Such as? Kramer: Well me of course. Jason looks towards Kramer with a raised eyebrow. Then, almost in unison, they both start to laugh. When they reach a red light the laughter starts to fade away. Jason: You never did answer me before. Did you leave Grimer at home, or did you find a suitable babysitter? Kramer: I called my dad and asked him if he could watch him. He said he wasn't doing anything anyway and could use the company. Jason: So you won't be worrying about Grimer, which means you will focus more on your four corners match, right? Kramer: Right on the money. Yet again it grows quiet in the truck. Kramer mumbles along with the radio as Jason stares restlessly out the window checking out the young ladies walking along the sidewalks. The scene switches to the inside of an apartment which is pretty well furnished. Sitting on a firm brown couch is a man we haven't seen for a very long time. It's Walter, Kramer's dad. Walters snowflake white hair, wrinkles, and brown Dockers accent his age. Sitting next to him fast asleep is the adorable white mouse, Grimer. Walter: This babysitting gig is quite easy. And to think Kramer is paying me a hundred dollars to boot. I can't wait to see what he pays me to watch his kids. That is of course if he ever has any. The scene quickly switches back to the truck as Walter goes into deep thought. Kramer and Jason are still cruising along as quiet as before. The music has been turned off at this point, and so the noise level is close to zero in the truck. But all of that changes as a huge pop can be heard. Kramer looks into his side mirror and sees that his back left tire is blown. Not wanting to risk a wreck, he pulls into vacant parking lot. Jason: What happened? Kramer: We blew a tire. Jason: Hurry up and fix it. Kramer: I would only...... Jason: Only what? Kramer: I don't have a spare. Kramer looks over to Jason with an 'I am sorry' look plastered on his face. Jason's look in return is an angry one. His eyebrows point down and his face turns red. Jason: You don't have a spare!? Kramer: You saw the back of the truck. You saw that I didn't have a tire so you should already know. Jason: Well I figured maybe you kept it somewhere else besides the back. Kramer: Pull out your cell phone then so we can call someone who might be able to help. This time Jason gives the 'I am sorry look.' Kramer bangs the steering wheel in frustration. Kramer: That's great. Just great! The one time you don't bring your cell phone and we really need it. Jason: Hey I'm not the one who doesn't keep a spare. Kramer: Do you happen to have any money on you? Kramer looks to Jason already knowing the answer. He watches as his head shakes from side to side. Kramer steps out of the car and looks around. Suddenly his eyes bulge as an idea clicks. Jason steps out as well wondering what Kramer is looking at. Kramer stretches out his arm and points to a couple of bicycles across the street. Jason: You can't be serious! Kramer: What other choices do we have? Besides you can burn some of that fat. Kramer starts to walk and Jason reluctantly follow. They cross the street and walk up to the red and yellow bikes which are ironically the right size for both Kramer and Jason. They turn to the store that the bikes are located in front of and look to see if anyone is coming out. Kramer: Ok on my signal grab the bike and ride as fast as you can. Kramer picks up the red bike and rides off. Jason doesn't notice at first and two teenagers walk out and Jason takes off as well but slowly. The boys chase him throwing rocks which Jason manages to dodge. The boys constantly shout that they want their bikes back. Kramer slows down and waits for Jason who speeds right on past as he hits a huge hill. Jason starts screaming because the brakes don't seem to work. Kramer yells at him to wait up and the scene slowly fades to black. You may be wondering how they will make it in time for Turmoil, but believe me they will. Maybe not in the best condition but they will make it. Second, you may be wondering about the bucket of chicken that Jason left in the truck. Won't Kramer need it? Just so you know, Jason always has a few fresh buckets of KFC chicken put in Kramer's locker room before every match, so he always comes prepared. Although Kramer may be a little tired after the long bike ride, he is going to give it his all in the number one contenders match. And so far it seems that the only real competition is in The End, who has had a lot to say about Kramer as well as Saotome and Krazie. Both Kramer and The End have expressed much interest in winning and getting the chance to face Borro for the Cruiserweight title. But Kramer has held the belt before, and as far as I know The End hasn't. So you would figure there would be more drive for Kramer to regain. All in all the main reason Kramer may pull out with the win is because he is looking for revenge against Juan Borro for winning the title from him on November 27, 2001. Some things you just never forget.....
