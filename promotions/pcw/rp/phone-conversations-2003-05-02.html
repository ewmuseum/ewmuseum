Phone Conversations

2 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 2nd, 2003 The calm mood in the air was sweet, a temperate of a splash orange with a faded gray for cloud cover. All in all, the quiet night would seem to be that way for quite sometime. In his hotel room, the phone is like a huge African elephant. It’s unmoving, slouched and powerful, and all three qualities that he hated in inanimate objects. For quite sometime right now, he sat there just thinking about anything that would ever come to his mind. Ranging from powerful topics to skeptic criticism of his peers, whatever it could be. Then, the phone rang, and he looked at it as if it were a dead person, frightened. A ghastly speck of air unbridled its way towards him as he breathed in, hopefully taking a smile to his face before picking it up. “Hello?” “Good to hear from ya, old chap!” He shuddered as it a violent storm had just smacked him directly in the face. “Reno Enders, we speak again.” “Yeah, yeah. Phone conversations aren’t always the best policy with people, you know that don’t cha Dante?” he snickered, the New York crime boss could be heard smoking his cigar and coughing a few times as he could hear it. Then the tide shifted over on Dante’s side of things, completely unaware of knowing things that Enders knew. But as always, Dante would have a plan to make Enders life worst as it already is. Enders, being the slow moving, calculator was always going to be playing defense. Atleast, that’s how Dante thought of it. Dante nodded, “Never was that Reno. You know that you and I aren’t even sincerely on speaking terms.” Reno chuckled. “Are we? Because I have a job for you to do. And this one, it has to be clean, swift, and painless.” Before Reno could even finish his statements, Dante rang out a resounding, “No.” “Excuse me?” “I’m out that business, because you put me BACK in this one. Don’t try and mix up your cards now.” He snarled with a furious passion before Reno eased up. “Okay, okay! You don’t need to use that type of tone with me, because I own you. And you’ll come by Saturday morning to pick up the procedures. Meanwhile, I’ll be in touch.” Click. The dead tone caused Dante to raise the phone back up, and once again proving that there was nothing to stop what Enders did. After all, the three words Dante was going to remember Friday night were said by him. “I Own You.”
