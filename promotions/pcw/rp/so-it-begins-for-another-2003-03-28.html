So It Begins For Another

28 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: So It Begins For Another Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 28/03/03 When I saw what was awaiting me at Turmoil, I didn’t even look at who I’d be fighting. In fact, I probably couldn’t even tell you now. They’re not insignificant, well not entirely, just not what caught my eye. I only noticed who I’d be teaming with. Totally Tubular Tommy. I envy you, Tommy. You’ve that arrogant innocence to you that I wish I was still capable of carrying. They haven’t got to you yet, they haven’t worn you down like they have me. You’re the fresh new Champion. The next lamb to the slaughter. Soon it will start. The old-time fans saying how you’ll never be as good as Jon Hull. They’ll say that no matter how many wins you knock up, you’ll never be as great as Ed. They will watch, and no matter how impressive, you’ll never replace Meanstreak in their hearts. Being Champion of PCW is a thankless task, Tommy. And as much as I envy you now, I know that soon you’ll follow the route I have taken. In case you haven’t seen the map, it leads to bitterness. I’m a fan of yours, Tom. I see a lot of myself in you. That’s your let-down. They don’t want people like us, no matter what we put our bodies through. No, they’ll only care after we quit and go to the opposition, that’s the trend it follows. We were a year too late to be stars in their eyes, we weren’t there at the beginning. No, we ride the coat tails of those they adore, we cash in on their legacy. We didn’t “Build” PCW. So Triple T, when we walk out on Tuesday to face... DAMN! I still can’t tell you who. When we go out there, we’ll sweat, maybe bleed, maybe knock a few weeks off our career, give all we have until we’re heaving for air... It won’t matter. We might as well go out there and do 2 minutes of punching and kicking for all they care. Why bother? Whatever we do it won’t have that “Myth” to it, that aura that PCW events used to have. I don’t have the heart to tell them that it wasn’t an “Aura”, rather just grainy production.Why shatter their little bubble. 15 minutes of brilliance, 30 minutes of astounding wrestling, a whole hour of back and forth, psychological warfare... It would make no difference. Some 5 minute match 2nd from the bottom at March Madness 2001 will be seen as better. I don’t mean to deter you, Tommy. Carry on doing your thing. You’ve got that swagger about you, the way you hold your head up high, the way you move in the ring. Keep going no matter what they say. You’re Totally Tubular Tommy, the PCW Champion. The longest reigning Continental Champion. So what if you weren’t in Media? So what if you weren’t in the 1st 64 man tournament? So what if you didn’t fizzle out of PCW and go to the opposition... You have that belt. As long as you wear that Title around your waist, as long as it’s draped over your shoulder, as long as Clay says “PCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION” when he announces you... As long as you have those, they’re not a patch on you. Do any of those they love have that Title? No, you do. Why? Because you’re actually in Primetime Championship Wrestling, that always helps. I want you to hold that belt for as long... Or even longer... Than you held the Continental Title. I want you to go out there show after show after show and shove it down their throats. Fuck the past. Admire the present. Look forward to the future. You’re the future, Tommy. Your name will be in lights outside the arenas we go to. Your name will headline PCW PPVs. Your name will be jeered. Your name will be announced as Champion. You can never win over the fans, you can never convince some people that PCW is better than it was. All you can do is keep it up, keep climbing the mountain and looking down upon the peons below. It will be an honour teaming with you on Tuesday, Tommy. Not many things actually excite me anymore, but I’m looking forward to the match. I don’t want to team with Kano, or Jon Hull, or Ed, or Da Westsidaz, or Kremmen. I want to fight alongside the future, not dwell in the past. Sometimes I feel amnesia is the only thing that will make PCW fans tolerable. The ball is now yours, Tommy. Run with it. Run hard and run fast. Run right through your doubters and run over those who look down on you. The future is bright, the future is you. My footsteps are there for you to follow, and go beyond. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
