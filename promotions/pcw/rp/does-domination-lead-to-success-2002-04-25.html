Does Domination Lead to Success?

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 25, 2002 OOC Note: I tried something a little different in this promo. The language and usage of words is a little far-fetched and sometimes hard to comprehend. If you pay attention and study the words carefully though, you will get the picture. I guess this is just another effort of mine to turn a roleplay into a piece of literature. :) 'He that would learn to succeed must be brought up to the constant practice of it from his youth, trying first only to use his wings as a tame goose will do, so by degrees learning to rise higher till he attain unto skill and confidence.' -J.M. Bacon It was a Wednesday night of the year 2002, in the town of Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Two talented men, Jack Valentine & Johnny Heart, collectively known as The Heartbreakers, were sitting together over a fireplace. While watching the smoke curling up the chimney Jack propounded an idea by way of a sudden inspiration. 'Watching this smoke makes me think of one thing,” Jack said. “What’s that?” Johnny replied. “I am so damn beautiful it is sickening,” Jack cockily responded. “Rightly so my partner…and we have become the most dominating tag team in PCW since Da Westsidaz,” Johnny stated. “Yeah, forget about Political Losers, forget about the Surfer Idiots, forget about the Terrible Camel jockeys, all of our fans know that The Heartbreakers are the team to beat…even if we don’t hold the titles,” Jack sarcastically uttered. The world was waiting for this utterance, which, it would seem, was on the tip of the tongue with many others. At least the arrogant Heartbreakers would like to think that this many people care about them, when in fact, they only get a few cheap pops here and here. Jack had already discovered what he designated 'success,' though no one had as yet told Jack that he had never succeeded. Moreover, in treating of this success—The entire PCW organization, as much as eight or nine months before the date we have now arrived at, had suggested that Jack would never succeed in PCW. With a shade more of good fortune, or maybe with a modicum more of leisure, Jack could’ve made it as a superstar, however injury and cockiness kept him down. Heart came almost nearer, and actually putting the same idea into practice, had succeeded as a headmaster in PWA, the same organization that Jack Valentine graduated from to make it into PCW. Some say the only reason Jack made it over Johnny was because of conspiracy between the two wrestlers. Some even say that it should’ve been Johnny who move out of PWA and not Jack. But he had accomplished no more when, as related, in the autumn of the same year, Johnny resigned from PWA never to be heard from again. This was the crude idea, the application of which in their hands was soon marked with notable success. Their own trade supplied ready and suitable materials for success as a pair, and, making an effort to cooperate with one another has further enhanced each one of their abilities to succeed further on down the road. As cocky pair humorously debated over success and the other tag teams in PCW, they failed to realize that it was their ability to get rid of their egos and cooperate that has allowed them to completely dominate the whole tag team division thus far in PCW. “How come you never got your hands on PCW gold?” Johnny said, as he stuck a marshmallow on a rod into the fireplace. Jack sneered at Johnny and tried to play it off as if he still was the over confident, egotistical man that he was a few months ago. “I didn’t try that hard. The level of competition just wasn’t there for me to give it my best effort. Hell, the competition will never be there as long as I…we’re in this federation,” he eagerly replied. “Funny how it’s going to be me that carries you to your first title here in PCW, it’s a shame you couldn’t do it on your own pretty ass,” he snickered. “As long as ‘Jack Valentine’ is on a team, it is he who is carrying the team. Besides, you wouldn’t be here if I hadn’t kissed ass to get the head honchos to sign you to a contract,” Jack said. “Kissed ass? Are you sure that’s all you did?” Johnny inferred evilly. After more comical bickering from the pair, they finally decided to settle down and agree upon something. They always seem to get in these little scuffles, but some common ground constantly pulls them together. “Ok, ok, I agree we’re a great team, the best looking team, the most talented team, the sexiest mofos ever to step foot in PCW,” Jack screamed. “Did you just say mofo?” Johnny questioned. “I think I did…mofo, mofo, mofo!” Jack stated. “So what’s the deal with Pailthorpe and Crichton? Where the hell do they get off wanting to face us, the number one contenders to the tag team titles…the next tag team champions, the future greatest tag team ever to step foot in PCW,” Johnny asserted. “I think that’s exactly why they want to face us. Because we are the greatest thing to hit PCW since, well, sliced bread!” Jack said. The pair high-fived after Jack’s amazingly corny statement. Enough; the time was already ripe for a public demonstration of the new domination coming from The Heartbreakers, and accordingly the 5th of the following May witnessed the ascent of the hope for a championship with due ceremony and advertisement. From this time the tide of events in the tag team world rolls on in full flood, almost every half-year marking a fresh epic, until a new team rolls along with the desire to become the best ever. It had been erroneously supposed that the ascent of Goovil and Da Westsidaz had been due, not to the rarefaction of the talent within it--which was its true cause--but to the evolution the new and the fans craving for new young and fresh teams. It followed, therefore, almost as a matter of course, that the new teams were already acquainted with so-called 'success,” or greatness, grasped the fact that their team would serve better than any other for the purposes of PCW. The task of The Heartbreakers becoming a successful tag team was one of considerable difficulty, owing partly to the necessity of procuring some effort which would prevent the risk of injury and therefore making it more difficult to prepare for their upcoming matches. However, even with the lesser effort, The Heartbreakers managed to dominate through the ladder of the tag teams and make their way up to the number one contenders. Only one task now stands in their way and that is the task of defeating the front office in Politically Incorrect. The preparations have been completed, one last and all-important match remains in the future for The Heartbreakers. That match would be the one for the championship. The only championship that matters for the pair. The question remains, will they get their shot with Politically Incorrect running the show. They book all of the matches and control the tag team division. Why on Earth would they want to give someone as talented and good-looking as The Heartbreakers a title shot? Still it lingers that the pair will continue their domination of the division until Politically Incorrect are man enough to step up to the challenge of the PINK!
