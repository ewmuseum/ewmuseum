The Dating Game Finale

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/5/03 Director: And we are filming in 5 4 3 2 1 The overly cheerful music for the show titled ‘the dating game’ began to feed through the speakers in the studio, the sound was horribly tinny and would no doubt be sorted out during the sound dub later on. The music played for a few moments before the shows presenter Richard Nobleman stepped out in the smarmy fashion that he had always done, at the top of the stairs as he stepped down onto each individual step they illuminated giving the indication that he was some higher power or at least was special, when really he wasn’t he was a low life scum of a human being who had smarmed his way onto television. Upon reaching the bottom of the stairwell he waited for a reaction from the crowd, a few in the back started clapping and wolf whistling, a smile formed on Richards face as he heard this, his eyes glancing the audience trying to figure out where exactly the cheering was coming from, the smile was soon wiped off his face as his eyes made contact with a pair of eighty year old women, their greying hair and croaky voices calling out to Richard Women: We love you Richard we want to take you home and look after you oh please won’t you come back with us please Richard!!! Richard visibly shuddered at the thought of being ‘pampered’ by the old women who simply wanted to mother him, a thought which made Richard wretch, realising he was still being filmed and not wanting to harm his credibility Richard let out a trademark smile showing off his pearly white teeth and winking at the two women causing them to scream even louder, until they were kindly asked to leave the building due to something about insurance. Walking over to his high stool, Richard sat down and stared directly down into the camera lens, a whirring audible as it panned backwards in order to focus on his face. Richard: Hello and welcome to the second part of the celebrity special of ‘the dating game’ where we will be meeting up with Michelle our beautiful contestant from the last show and the celebrity that she chose the rodeo rider James Edwards, lets have a look at what made Michelle pick James in the first place. A video plasma screen suddenly projects into life just above Richard’s head and displays a still image of James sat next to David Schwimmer and Henry Winkler before it almost instantaneously jumped to movement showing feedback from the previous episode. James: Hi Michelle, I’d have an espresso so that when my lunar module hit your black hole…you’d see the Milky Way. The video continued to play as we go backstage the occasional cheer being heard from the studio audience as the smut of the predetermined lines was repeated on the video screen. James Edwards was leaning against a wall waiting for his call to come out on stage and get it over and done with. Unlike his previous forays into the world of television James wanted to get out straight away, the only reason that he was here was that she deserved an explanation for what he was about to say, he couldn’t just stop calling, he had to tell her face to face because he was not a coward for from it. The fact was that he cared for her too much to do that and that’s why this show was going to hurt. James sighed slightly only to stop himself as he winced in pain, he looked down at his chest which was expanding and contracting at an alarmingly quick rate, the breaths short and clearly not supplying him with the oxygen that he required. It was at that time that Michelle wondered round the corner fresh from the make-up department, once again she looked stunning her body being demonstrated wonderfully by a low cut black dress which hung delicately off the shoulder. Upon noticing James, Michelle darted forwards flinging her arms around him to an audible crack, which in normal circumstances would have made James, cry out in pain as his rib buckled under the pressure that had been exerted by Michelle. It had been just a few days since Supremacy and he was still feeling the effects of the brutal four way corners match, a match which he had lost to the same man that he always seemed to lose to. Matt Jackson the new Rising Star Champion. Michelle: My god, what was that, what has happened to you are you ok? Michelle pulled back and saw James’ bruised face clearly now, she obviously had not before the upper left of his lip was slightly swollen and he ached all over. Michelle glanced down and saw James clutching his ribs tightly with his right hand, she could see the bruising clearly through his plain white tee shirt and took a few steps backwards in horror. Michelle: Who did this to you? Did you tell the police? Did I hurt you? What happened? James what happened? The flurry of questions flew at James one after the other without being given time to give an answer that would be deemed appropriate. James glanced up at Michelle her large brown eyes almost looking into his soul, deep down James thought Michelle already knew that things weren’t going to be able to work, if he was going to be coming home from work this battered and bruised he could not stand to see Michelle face like this night after night, the pain and suffering that was being caused mentally, a rather nice juxtaposition to James physical pain. James: If I remember correctly, my head is still a little woozy it was Matt Jackson, SyN and Stone Burns, I didn’t tell the police as it’s my job remember. No matter how many times that moron out there says I am a rodeo clown I’m night. I’m a wrestler this is what I do. The stakes were high on Sunday so the beating was more intense than usual but hey you should have seen the other guys. James lets out a nervous laugh which turned into a cough causing immense pain to his cracked ribs, the burning sensation almost becoming too much as he drops to one knee trying to clutch at the lilac carpeted floor but to no avail, Michelle looked on in horror not knowing what to do in the situation that she had been forced into James: Sorry to say this but yeah you did hurt me when you gave me that hug, I know that you didn’t mean to but I think you may have just recracked my rib but hey that’s no big deal it will just mean I have a bit more trouble breathing and as for what happened well, to put it simply I lost, I had my chance at winning a title and I lost Michelle. I had neglected my training and I was not up to par and this was taken advantage of. James’ breathing returned to something a bit more normal as he raised to his feet once more and moved towards Michelle slowly still clutching his side tightly, Michelle seeing this stepped backwards at the same rate as James was moving forwards causing James to stop in his tracks. James: What’s wrong Michelle? Michelle: I…I don’t want to hurt you again. James laughed slightly and moved towards Michelle who once again began to move backwards completely unaware that she had just moved into sight of the cameras and was about to be stepping down the stairs which Richard the presenter of the show had just stepped down. Turning around sharply Richard notices Michelle and snaps into presenter mode. Richard: And here she is now the wonderful and beautiful Michelle The crowd breaks into a frantic cheer as Michelle turns around swiftly and glares at the studio audience immediately stopping them in their tracks. Richard: Now now Michelle no need to be so hostile to the crowd. Michelle: Hostile? Hostile? You want to hear hostile? James get out here now. The crowd murmurs between itself not wanting to be glared at again because if looks could kill they would be having dinner with Satan just about now. Slowly and unsurely James stepped out, the bruising and swelling looking even worse under the bright lights of the studio exaggerating every single bruise and every single cut. James: Michelle please don’t do this. Richard: Whoa some bull really landed on you bad this weekend man, I hope you didn’t take Michelle along to get hurt are you ok honey did the bull hurt you to? Richard had turned from sarcastic to sleazebag in a matter of seconds, his arm was now wrapped around Michelle’s waist with James glaring from the top of the stares slowly making his way down the stairs. Michelle notices Richard’s fingers rubbing the top of her thigh lightly causing her to push Richard away and move towards James slightly, not because she had forgiven him for the impending speech but simply because Richard was being too much of a pervert. Michelle: James just blamed me for losing this weekend he said that he hadn’t been able to train as much this week which basically is blaming me, he’s saying it’s my fault because I distracted him so he wasn’t able to be at his peak performance. James: Of course I was distracted I couldn’t get you out of my mind! Michelle was stopped mid rant, her eyes meeting James’ and realising that what he was saying was in fact true. She hadn’t taken into account the idea that maybe just maybe James would feel something for her, after all he was a celebrity and she was just a normal girl, he had not asked her any questions about herself during the champagne picnic. Michelle: What? James: Have you ever looked at yourself, you are a stunningly beautiful woman. Richard: damn right. Both James and Michelle shoot a glare at the presenter Richard who throws down his clipboard in a blatant strop something, which a five year old would do before starting a rant of his own Richard: Now look here dammit this is my show ok, MY SHOW, I made it you are just some two bit hussy who was put on the show because she has a reasonable sized rack and you, why the fuck would a rodeo rider be put on a celebrity dating game name me one famous rodeo rider James looked blankly at Richard momentarily before moving slowly towards him Richard not moving an inch squaring up to James mocking him by poking himself in the ribs and pretending to be in a serious amount of pain. Richard: Oh look at me I’ve fallen off a bull and I can’t get up wah wah wah. James continued to walk towards Richard until he was finally toe to toe with him and in a flash connected with a solid punch to his jaw, a disconcerting grinding noise was heard throughout the studio as Richard’s jaw became dislocated and he began rolling around on the floor in pain James standing over him looking down with fury in his eyes. James: I’m a fucking wrestler ok and that’s why it hurts me so much to say this… James’ face became less harsh a look or sorrow now filled his face as he turned to stare at Michelle. James: We aren’t going to work. People need to be able to accept what I do and face the consequences, I saw it in your eyes backstage when you saw how much pain I’m in. You could not cope with it, you were like a very attractive deer in the headlights. I wouldn’t want to be responsible for seeing you in pain or worried about me, I care for you too much to do that to you, which is why after this show I’m just going to go. Michelle did not say anything, she didn’t need to James had looked deeper in her than anyone else before and as a result she knew that what he was saying was right she stepped towards James tentatively he dress, shimmering with her movement leaned forward and kisses James lightly on the lips tears welling up in both of their eyes. Director: AND CUT! Great show people James the punch was amazing you almost made it look real this is going to get me an award for sure. James and Michelle remained locked in their embrace for they knew that once they broke apart it would be the end…
