Title: Our feature presentation! Act II: THE UNITED LEAGUE OF DO-GOODERS! Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: 27th CORRUPTION MANAct II: The United League of Do-Gooders!Starring: All From Before, PLUS:Two Guys from California as The Wonder HeartsSecret Service Man as The God of GoodnessThe San Diego Chicken as Fried Chicken Kelly Slater as Surfer Dude #1 and #2 ::The narrator's voice booms.:: Even as Rent-A-Hero and Trojan Man head out to rid the city of our villains, they have made their way back to the TOWERS OF CORRUPTION. To most, these buildings are the headquarters for Des Moines Insurance, a major firm in the city. However, little do most know that deep in the bowls of this building lies the HALL of Corruption, the hideout of the most devious villians in history. CORRUPTION MAN......AND FILIBUSTER BOY!! However, in the Towers of Corruption, they are little more than two businessmen and politicians...Reginald V. Lovecraft, and William S. Powell. As men with careers...they sit, discussing their latest plans, not for business, or for politics, but for their WRESTLING career. At Gold and Glory, an event in the near future, they battle many tag teams, and Lovecraft has the additional burden of a defense of his Extreme championship. All hell is about to break loose. But the politicians remain calm... ::The two men sit at a table in the mythic Towers of Corruption. Looking at a bunch of papers, Powell and Lovecraft frown. Clearly, their hearts and minds are not in insurance now, as is evidenced by the giant 'Gold & Glory' poster. They throw the papers to the side, and begin to speak.: Reginald V. Lovecraft: This is not going to be easy, Mr. Powell.....every tag team in this company has designs on capturing the tag team titles... William S. Powell: A boy band...a pair of misplaced men...two Islamic militants.. a pair of suicidal men...the pink men...surfer dudes.....heroes for hire...a false God, and last, but not least, a pair of politicians. It's odd, really...all of us, with different motivations, from all different walks of life, we come together in a wrestling ring to beat the living shit out of one another....for the glory of PCW Tag Team gold. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Our focus, though, remains on the Neo Void God, Akira Akiyama.. You disparage, well, Cyclonica disparages our profession. Being a politician may not be tasteful...we may indeed be among the worst sort of people in the world. But THAT, my Celestial friend, is why we are dangerous. We are afraid of nothing save impeachment. We will do anything, say anything to get what we desire. We do not fear you. You may feel speaking to us 'mere mortals' to be below you, but come the Glory, your mouth shall open once more, and it shall be screaming at the pain we inflict upon you! William S. Powell: You know something Reginald? Reginald V. Lovecraft: Yes, Mr. Powell? William S. Powell: I've come to an important conclusion. Reginald V. Lovecraft: And that is? William S. Powell: Cyclonica's hot. ::Lovecraft smacks himself in the forehead.:: William S. Powell: I think I'm going to go introduce myself. Reginald V. Lovecraft: Um, ARE YOU OUT OF YOUR FUCKING MIND?! The woman's trained in Brazilian martial arts, drunken boxing, and Lord only knows what else Akiyama managed to teach her. She'd kill you! William S. Powell: But how do you know she doesn't need loving? I thought Gods were asexual.. Reginald V. Lovecraft: THE GREEK GODS FUCKED EVERYTHING THAT WALKED! STUDY HISTORY, DAMNIT! William S. Powell: I[m still going to say hello to her sometime. ::Lovecraft shakes his head. Just then, Roger Schultz walks into the room, dressed in butler attire.:: Roger Schultz: The Corruption Symbol is blazing in the sky, sir! It appears that the United League of Do-Gooders has returned to our unfair city! Reginald V. Lovecraft: QUICK WILLIAM! TO THE CORRUPTAVATORS! ::The two men charge to a pair of elevators, and enter them, pushing the 'down' button. As they lower, their real clothes somehow change into their CORRUPTION MAN! and FILLIBUSTER BOY! outfits. As they step off the Corruptavators, they instantly notice one of their instruments in the Hall of Corruption is blinking off the charts.:: Corruption Man: ZOUNDS FILIBUSTER BOY! This bleeping can only mean one thing! ::Both supervillains look at each other, and exclaim at the same time.:: Both: THE UNITED LEAGUE OF DO-GOODERS!! TO THE CORRUPTACADILLAC! ::Both men hop into the Corruptacadillac, as on the other end of town, Trojan Man and Rent-A-Hero sit at a table, surrounded by various other men. Two, dressed all in pink, have W's on their chests. A large imposing man is dressed up in all white. Kelly Slater is divided in half by a black line down his surfer outfit. The left side of his mouth is curved up in a smile, the right holds a bong in between his lips.:: Rent-A-Hero. We're all here....except for one... Where is that Fried Chicken? ::Suddenly, a man busts through the door, dressed as a fried chicken drumstick, and carrying a bucket of fried chicken. He takes his seat at the table.:: Rent-A-Hero: ROLL CALL! God of Goodness! :The man dressed in all white raises his hand: Rent-A-Hero: Wonder Hearts! :The Wonder Hearts smile, and nod goofily.:: Rent-A-Hero: Fried Chicken! ::He raises the bucket over head, causing lightning to crash, and thunder to boom.: Rent-A-Hero: Surfer Dude #1 and #2! ::The sole man raises his hand:: Rent-A-Hero: And Trojan Man and I are here! THAT MAKES THE ENTIRE UNITED LEAGUE OF DO-GOODERS! And, now...to tell you all our mission... ::Suddenly, glass shatters, as a shadow is visible above the men at the table, cast down upon the table's surface.:: Wonder Heart #1! HELP! HOLD ME! ::He jumps into the arms of his fellow Wonder Heart. As they shake, Trojan Man retains control of his faculties.:: Trojan Man: BATTLE STATIONS, EVERYONE!! Who is the shadow waiting to pounce on the League?Can Corruption Man and Fillibuster Boy destroy pure goodness?FIND OUT NEXT TIME, IN SCENE II OF THIS EXCITING ACT! ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
