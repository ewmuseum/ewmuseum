Interview with the Devil: The Awakening (Part 1 of a series done in another fed, DO NOT count toward match)

25 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-25-03 “It feels good to be alive”, a quote from a song by Demon Hunter titled “My Throat Is An Open Grave”, and what a quote that really is. Life is good, unless you are Shane Robertson, then life is hectic and very tragic. Thoughts billow through Shane’s brain as he tries to comprehend why in the world he has been placed in a situation such as this, not knowing the stipulations, he wasn’t trained for this sort of thing to happen this early on in his career of ridding professional wrestling of evil and wickedness. Yet, he finds himself having to try and deal with all of these problems as they hit him head on, much like that of two big Mac trucks coming to a collision. Not knowing what to do, Shane does the one thing he does best, he talks to God about his problems, trying to come to a decision on what to do, surely the Lord of all Lords would be able to answer his questions. Panning in from darkness, we find ourselves located in a small, darkened area of a rather normal looking hotel room. The lighting looks to be in proper condition, just merely turned down for some reason, which is unusual as it is closing in on night fall and most reasonable people would leave at least one light on fully. The sound of footsteps are heard in the background as we find Shane Robertson making his way to the side of the bed and kneeling down to both knees. Shane brings his hands to his face and closes his eyes. “Lord,” Shane says with a bit of remorse in his voice, “I’m still unsure of this whole thing. Why would you place me into a situation where I was forced to fight?” Shane begins to shake his head a little bit, “I’m sorry for questioning your motives, Lord, but I really am clueless on what to do now. I’ve won many victories in your name and I didn’t have to do anything wrestling related, yet you’ve taken me through so many trainings and now I’m here.” Shane pauses for a moment, as if he is meditating, “I know, Lord, and I’m sorry once again for questioning you. I know deep inside that you wouldn’t let anything bad happen to me, and even if something bad did happen I would know that was merely Satan battling me off and trying to keep me from doing the work you have placed before me.” Shane pauses for a moment and then begins to shake a little bit, “No, Father, I’m not ready to do anything along those lines just yet. I know you have a divine plan for me, but I don’t feel as though I am ready to take it to that level just yet.” Shane pauses once again as his face begins to grow scarce and pale, but his eyes remain shut, “I’m not questioning you, Lord. Nor am I trying to say that you are wrong. I understand you have this set in my path to be done, but can’t it wait just some time? I understand I must take a sacrifice in your name, but I need some more time. It’s too early to be doing anything like that.” Shane lowers his head a little bit. “Yes, I know this would be the perfect time and I understand it could look as though it were merely an accident, but Lord, I cannot do it. I am not ready to take another man’s life just yet and I’m certainly sure the Vatican wouldn’t approve of this plan.” Shane’s face begins to grow even more pale as a small tear begins to stream down his face, “No, I’m certain they know nothing of your plans my Lord and I’m also certain that they wouldn’t want me to follow through with them this early on.” Shane begins to shake his head a bit as another tear begins to spout down the side of his cheek, “I’m not even sure I’m talking to my Savior now; the Lord I know as my own would never ask me to take another’s life. All He would ask of me to do is try to convert those from their wicked ways.” A small poof is heard in the background as Shane flinches a bit, “What’s that noise? Who is there?” Shane continues to leave his eyes closed as he reaches around for his Bible, which he finds placed on the nightstand. He lifts it up high and turns around sitting on the bed, as a pair of red beading eyes are seen glowing through the darkness. The scene slowly begins to fade into the darkness as the eyes begin to emerge a little more. ~Series To Continue~
