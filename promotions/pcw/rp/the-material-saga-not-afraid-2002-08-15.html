The Material Saga: Not Afraid.

15 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 15, 2002 The sky stretched out as far as anyone could possibly see. It was that beautiful blend of pink, red, and orange that you generally see only when you stare at a light and close your eyes to fall assleep. The clouds moved across at an odd pace, almost as if it was time lapse photography. It almost didn't look real. Don’t leave me alone Then again, look who you are dealing with. His entire existence doesn't seem real, everything he stands for revolves around worlds of fantasy and myth. Its the easiest way to live though, you have to use a large stretch of the imagination to believe anything that DarkStar says at all. Yet its all true... Every last disturbing and fantastic word of it has happened. I’m feeling so numb Alot had happened recently. He had changed. It depended on who you asked to know if he changed for good... or if it was just his new found cockyness getting to him. It was true though, he wasnt the disturbed goth that he once was. He isnt going around murdering people for revenge or to cure his depression, he isnt paying attention to his book of stories... now he is a shrewd, psychopathic, goth business man. He realizes that he himself is marketable. He himself and the image that has been his for years now became something everyone wanted to have in a box for 24.99. But one thing was missing... I need you to be here, I need you to call She left on a trip today, a PR trip. PR... Public Relations. What had this become. They were spokespeople now, spokespeople for a line of clothing, spokespeople for the federation that less then a month ago they were set to destroy and make crumble and adhere to their rules. It had all just changed though. The league did adhere. These freaks became what everyone wanted put on their TVs, they called out for more and more TV time, they wanted to breathe in the escapades of DarkStar and his lover, the heartbreak and the pain to the happiness and the joy. They wanted to see Tyler Nelson on his cleansing of PCW, they wanted to see Syko and his attempts to take out Pendragon, they wanted to see Clydes sadistic face grace their television sets everynight. And everything’s wrong But it wasnt... things felt right this time. He didnt know how to deal with this, his split personalities were just too much to deal with. Had it really only been a month since the clinical agreement that he had a personality disorder? It seemed like alot more. Things had changed alot, thats why. A beautiful shame ... He knew what would make this all better. He picked up the phone and dialed the numbers to Talenas cell phone... It ringed... and it ringed... and it ringed again... The voice mail picked up and the bubbly voice of his lover spoke about leaving a message, he ignored it and hung the phone up. He needed to hear her perfect voice, he yearned for it like it had been the only reason he existed. Truthfully, it had been. It wasnt like there was anything else to keep him on this plane of existence before. Before he became as okay with himself as he was now, it was all about her. Were they growing apart? Had this all changed so much that they couldnt be the same around eachother that they were before? It couldnt have been that different. So what, DarkStar accepted himself, he just believes that the true 'himself' is this arrogant, self serving gothic prick that is better then everyone around him. Talena doesnt mind, if she did she wouldnt have left her doll behind, she changed too. They werent just deitic gods, they were worshiped both on earth and off of it, because of who they were... They didn't sell out... They bought in... She wouldn't answer her phone, but this time he realized she was probably just on the computer. He sighed and walked into the other room, clicking on the computer. He needed to talk to her, he didnt want five years of the only real relationships hes ever known to end because they were suddenly in the spotlight. He would give it all up for her, no matter how much fun it was. Im not Afraid..
