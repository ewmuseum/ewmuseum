Press Conference...

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.22.02 [fade in] Me and Ron grabbed our bags and we were out the door of our house. We throw our bags in the back and jump into the Escalade. Ron on the passenger side and me behind the wheel. I backout of the driveway. Reverse....then drive. We get onto the street as Ron and I begin talking. 'Hey Ron? Are you feeling good about this bro.' 'I do Mike. I really do. It will be our chance to shine in PCW and start of our careers right in PCW.' 'Yeah I know what you mean. I mean our first match in PCW is a match against those, if I can say, 'tubular' guys Cowabunga Connection, in a round robin tourney to crown the Tag Team Champions.' 'It's going to be one hell of a night on friday Mike no doubt about that, but I have a question for you..' 'Shoot bro..' 'Are we headed to PCW or somewhere else?' 'Well Ron, we will be headed to PCW sometime, but I believe we are going to a press conference to tell the people what we plan to do in PCW.' 'Okay. Sounds good. Hey wake me up when we get there aight.' 'Sure Ron.' Ron puts his chair back to take a little nap. I continue driving thinking to myself that we are lucky. Not lucky that we have been so successfull in our wrestling careers, but lucky that we get a chance to go for more gold. I guess we were at the right place at the right time, but I know that our match against the Cowabunga Connection will not be a walk in the park. It may be the best challenge that we've been up against in a long time. All I know is that this friday night, we gotta bring our a game and show the fans of PCW that we are for real and will be a tag team that will not go down lightly and will not back down from a challenge. We are only a few days away before we make our debuts in PCW. I can't wait. We pull up to the building where the press conference is being held. 'Hey Ron , wake up bro. We are here.' 'Aight. Let's go in and do this Mike and let's hurry up, I'm getting hungry.' 'Man your always hungry,' I replied. 'Hey, when my stomach is growling, that means it's time to eat.' 'But it's like growling twenty-four-seven.' 'Man shut up and let's do this damn press conference.' I drive up to the parking lot and park up close to the building. I put the Escalade in park as Ron and myself get out. We walk into the building and are directed to the press area. We enter the room, with quite a few of media people there. A guy approaches me. 'Hello Mike and Ron? I am here representing PCW, and I just like to say thank you two for coming today.' 'That's no problem. It's our pleasure,' Ron said. 'So what you two will do is one of you, whoever you choose, will go up to the podium and introduce yourselves and tell everyone what you want them to know about you guys.' I looked at the guy and responded,'That doesn't sound to bad. When do we start?' 'You can walk up right now and begin.' 'Okay we will do that. Let's go Ron.' Me and Ron walk up to the podium. I appoint Ron to be the speaker. I stand behind him as he begins. 'We are going to get this thing started real quick. How are you guys doin today.' [the media all in unasin] 'good' 'Well that's good to hear. I would like to welcome you guys to our press conference. I am 'The Truth' Ron Jacobs and this is my brother 'The Real Deal' Mike Andrews and we form the tag team Suicidal Tendencies. We are here in PCW, to start a new and become the first Tag Team Champions of this new restructuring. I know that we will have a big match against us on Friday Night, but it's nothing that we can't defeat. We arrived here in PCW to do many things and one of them is to entertain the fans, but most of all is becoming tag champs. We both know that it will be a long road and the tag division here looks to be one of the best that we have been involved in in quite sometime. We are just happy to be here and you guys will not be disappointed in us. Is there any questions?' 'Yes I have one,' says a gentlemen in the front row. 'Kevin Williams from Wrestling Mag. I notice that on the Fusion card that was faxed out to the media, that the name you guys are under is The New Iconz, and you guys are out here calling yourselves Suicidal Tendencies. Can you clear that up for us?' 'Yes I sure can Kevin. In alot of peoples eyes I guess you can say we are or will become The New Iconz of PCW, but our tag names have and forever will be Suicidal Tendencies. I think it was a little miscommunication between us and the front office. Yes you..' 'Michelle Johnson from twenty-four seven wrestling, now you guys have a chance that many tag teams wish they had here in PCW and that is become tag champs on a short amount of time, how do you feel about that?' 'We are very thrilled to be apart of PCW once again, and we are thrilled to have a chance to become tag champs, but we both know that our match this friday against CC will not be an easy one. There are alot of great tag teams here, and we hope to be the ones that they come after. Anymore questions...no...ok. Well that was a short q and a but it's okay, hopefully we will have another one of these, but we will have some gold with us. Thanks for your time.' Ron leaves the podium and I follow right behind him. We talk to the gentlemen that stopped me earlier. 'Well that's it man, thanks for having us here.' 'Thank you two, Ron and Mike, for taking time out of your schedule to do this.' 'No problem. Ron you ready to go?' 'You know I'm hungry,' Ron stated. 'Well let's go then.' Me and Ron shake the guys hand and leave the building. We get back into the car and drive off. 'What in the hell was all that in there Ron?' 'What are you talking about?' 'Man bro you never talked like that before.' 'So what are you saying?' 'Man that was good, and well put. You should do the speaking for us from now on.' Ron begins to laugh as I do too. 'Shut up and just drive somewhere so we can get some food.' 'Alright.' [fade out]
