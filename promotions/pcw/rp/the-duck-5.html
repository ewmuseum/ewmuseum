Date: A preview of sorts... Watching as the falling sun crashed into the horizon, the rugged ‘butch’ of a man stayed on a steady course with the wheel, guiding the semi down the long and lonesome road before him. Heavy eyes met the red glow of sunset with utter disgust, the boredom and loneliness of it all driving him into a zombie like state of mind. At least the harsh crackle of the engine is drown out my the calm, country tune relayed to him from his radio. Endless road… Another loooooong night. As the sky darkened, he flipped on the headlights automatically, a loyal habit. … … Suddenly, the reach of the lights caught something on the side of the road. He jumps, awake… Armadillo? Turtle? Skunk? Hitchhiker. His heel spikes the brake, bringing the rig to a smoking, sudden halt, and leaving a fresh coat of rubber behind. He waits for a moment, then slides over to the passenger side, unlatching the door, and kicking it open. The sound of footsteps upon the gravel along the side of the road faintly fills his ears, getting closer and closer. Finally, an old man holding a sign appears in front of the doorway… Trucker: “…Well?” The old man seems pale. The trucks pilot looks at him for a moment. It’s hard to make him out in the dark… Trucker: “What are y’all waiting for? Common’ get in.” The old man drops to the grave, limp as a brick. Trucker: “Oh Jesus!” Scrabbling to the other side again, the Trucker hangs his head out the door to check on the old… CHIK-CHIK …Look down the twin barrels of a shotgun. Swallowing hard, he shifts slowly back to the drivers seat, allowing the hijacker entrance… Webbed feet land upon the worn leather, a creature getting in, and closing the door behind it. Glaring at the trucker, the intruder maintains its aim. Trucker: “…..a….a DUCK?!” Scowling, the Duck scoffs at such a greeting. The Trucker knows what to do. Drive. --- The Goblin sat alone, admiring his Continental Title, and over all good fortune… Fortune? BAH! HE HAS NOT BEEN AWARDED!! He earned it with his great power! That’s the ticket. Syko the Goblin: “Note to self: I’m the best.” Well, well, well… …Not a trace of the bird in his memory, which might be for the better. --- The mallard sighed, shakily covering the trigger. Where is it going? What is its plan? Origin? WELL?! TBC
