Date: 2-17-=2 We were standing but a mere few inches from each other. Our eyes gazing into each others. Our breath meeting in the small space between us. Him the Ripper and me… I’ve been calling myself by his name so long, I’m not sure who I am anymore. Not even sure that we were different people anymore. Not sure that one couldn’t exist without the other. Not sure that we could no longer co-exist any longer as well. Him a vengeful twisted madman. But I wasn’t like him. I couldn’t do the things he does. I couldn’t even begin to think the thoughts he thought. I do know one thing. I’m not as sick and demented as he is, nobody can be… nobody. It was as if I were standing in front of a mirror. My reflection looking back at me. But it wasn’t a mirror, it wasn’t a dream, it was something I perceived as real. As real as it can be, to a man losing his mind at least. Am I loosing my mind? Did I already lose my mind? If not losing my mind, then what? As I stood there looking into eyes… eyes that would have been my own. My thoughts were betrayed… or he already knew what I was thinking. Is that possible? I am not a dream… Not a dream? Then I really am insane. To some people you probably are… That’s weird it’s as if he’s reading my mind. Is that possible? Can he really be standing here in front of me and still know what I think? I may stand here before you. (Pointing at my head) But I am still here… How can that be? Your standing here before me? Yes, I am… but no I am not… Ok, I’m confused. Are you or are you not standing here before me? Do or do you not hear my thoughts? How can this be? We are the same, you and I are one… This thought sent a shiver up my spine. Then a thought crossed my mind. NO! EMILY! We can’t be the same. I would never have done what you did, NEVER. I couldn’t take it anymore, I was loosing my mind. I had to make him stop. I closed my eyes and put my hands over my ears. I began to yell at the top of my lungs. “GET THE F(CENSORED)K OUT OF MY HEAD!!!” I held my breath, kept my eyes closed tight, and my hands stayed on my ears. I was hoping it worked. I was hoping that when I opened my eyes, uncovered my ears, and took in a deep breath that he would be gone. That he would no longer be around. That he would have disappeared just as my words had faded off into nothingness. I then took my hands off my ears and slowly opened my eyes. To find… nothing or better yet nobody standing before me. A grin came over my face. I’m free. He’s gone. I win and he loses. He wasn’t as strong as I had made him out to be. Now maybe I can go on and live a normal life… whatever that may consist of. As I sat there thinking of what may had happened so long ago if I had done what I just did. Would things have went different? Would it have prevented what had happened? Would Emily still be around? Would we still be together? Would we both be happy? So many questions, questions without answers. As I were running thoughts through my mind. I felt a warmth on the back of my neck. Warmth that reminded me of something, a warm breath. Someone was breathing on the back of my neck. One of my opponents at the pay-per-view perhaps. Johnson, Sylo, maybe even the Rising Star Champion himself Lance Bell. As I stood there staring at the far wall. The only nose in the small gym built in my basement came from the lights over head, which let out a low hum. My senses where on full alert and I was expecting at any moment a blow to the back of my head. A blow that would send me to the floor in a heap. After waiting for what seemed like an eternity nothing happened. No blow to the back of the head. No blow to the small of my back. Nothing, nothing at all happened. Nothing but warm breath coming off the back of my neck. If it were one of my opponents wouldn’t one of them have done something by now? Wouldn’t one of them try to act all macho? Say some stupid remark about how they had me where they wanted me? Yes, if it were one of them then they would have said something or would have done something by now. One of them isn’t stupid enough to allow me to turn and face them. As I stood there a low toned voice could be heard. Your nothing without me… My skin began to clam up, the wind in my lungs was forced out violently, and my knees just about buckled. I had thought he was gone. I had thought I had gotten rid of him, for good. I had thought I defeated him. But yet here he was, standing behind me. His warm calm breath bearing down on the back of my neck. His voice inside my head… STILL! I slowly turned to face him. To face the psychotic freak. To face the monster. To face the beast. To face… myself. As I turned and faced him again his face was covered with a devilish grin. His eyes looked as though a fierce fire were burning within them. And his thoughts, were still inside my head. We are one… No, we can’t be I’m here and you, you stand before me. We are one… Stop saying that. We are not one. We are not the same. We are not… Why do you deny me… Deny you? Why don’t you just leave me the h*ll alone? You can not exist without me… What? Your telling me that if you die, then I as well will die? That without you I can not live? We are one… Would you stop saying that. We are not one. We co-exist… We are one… I don’t believe you. You are weak now get the f(censored)k out of my head. If you truly wanted me gone. Then I wouldn’t be here. But I am here. All because you are weak… Weak? Am I really weak? Is he stronger than me? Is that why I always do as he tells me too? You are weak. And soon I will be the one in full control… NO! I will never let that happen. You will never have control of me. I closed my eyes and held them shut as tight as possible. As I did so I kept repeating. “Get the f(censored)k out of my head. Get the f(censored)k out of my head. Get the f(censored)k out of my head.”When I finally opened my eyes again he was gone. I quickly turned and look to each side and behind me. No where could I find him. He was gone. Gone for good I had hoped. Out of my eye sight and out of my head. I had to keep him suppressed deep within. I couldn’t let him out again. I couldn’t let what happened to Emily happen again. Not to somebody else. We are one… The voice was in my head. He had gone but he still exist in my head. I looked down at my hands and let out a terrifying scream for his blood stained hands were my own. We are one…
