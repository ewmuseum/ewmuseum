Analyzing the Opponent: There work!

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/21/02 From the bottom of my broken heart….” ZZZZZICH! After that disturbing noise, we see Carey J. Nowski and Stephen Cairns, better known as Misplaced. It appears that they have an accompaniment with them. They’re all gathered around a CD player in what appears to be a music studio. CD’s of N’Sync, The Backstreet Boys, and various other boy bands are scattered around on a table next to them. A man in a suit bursts through a door that is behind the guys, and he is none other than Jason the studio manager. Jason: Guys! What’s up fellows? Glad to see ya’ll checkin’ out my pad down here. Dig it? The teams accompaniment, Jackson “Hoe” Pallish, chuckles for a moment at the way he’s speaking. Jackson: Man, dawg…you sound like dem’ f*ckin’ koons’ when I be shootin’ dem’ n*gga’s up from my back yard. You just get b*tch slapped by a rooster or somethin’ dawg? Carey and Stephen just chuckle while Jason looks on in amazement. Jason: My god! It’s a Billy Bob, Jay-Z mix! This is decent! Lets hook up… Jackson: Man n*gga, you be rippin’ on me like that one more time I’ll be beatin’ yo’ white a*s with a pitchfork! Jason: What the…. Finally, Stephen decides to jump into this before it becomes a studio brawl. Stephen: Hey! One more bloody word and I’ll have you all head first into that glass! Jackson, back off. Jason, shut up. Now, can we please get to work? Thank You. They both back off at the threatening look in Stephen’s eyes. Jason finally makes a move by starting…conversation in the hostile room. Jason: All right, dudes…what can I do for you today? Come to buy some records? Stephen: Ah…no, we’re recording. You see, we have something that we need to take care of. We need to sing some songs that involve…boy bands. Jason: Boy Bands? Well, you wouldn’t seem like the bunch I’d be expecting to do this, but I’m sure I can hook you up. Are you all three singing? Carey and Stephen both agree, but Jackson once again has some comments… Jackson: Hold up! Ain’t no way I be singin’ the boy band stuff. I gots to get home to mama’ to feed her and our kids. Jason: Ah…that’s nice. Taking care of your mom? Where’s your wife though? Jackson: Didn’t you her me? Me and mama’ gotta’ go take care of our kids! Our anniversary is coming up soon. Jason: You married your… Before Jason could finish, Carey kicked him in his shin which knocks the words back inside of his mouth. Carey: **whispering** Don’t bring up a sore subject, eh? Jason: All right, lets get started then. I’ll put you guys in booth 5, which is off da’ hook! Just as they’re going in, Jackson comes to a halt. Jackson: I swear, you be talkin’ like one of us one mo’ time, I’ll be rippin’ your leg off and using it for dinner. Been a while since we’ve had fresh leg! Jason: Yes Jimbo…ah…Jackson. Jackson just rolls his eyes, and the three make there way into the small booth. All around there is sound equipment, and hi-tech machinery. Jackson stares in awe, as he is used to seeing a pitchfork and a pig attached to the bottom. There are three microphones set up in the room, and steel chairs by each of them. Jason: All right guys, how are we starting today? I can hook you up with a Backstreet Boys slow tune, or maybe we want an N’Sync jumpy tune? Maybe something like “Bye, Bye, Bye”? I’ll tell you what, you look like some active guys so I’ll hook up a lively beat. Stephen: Whatever… Jason slowly turn and trots out of the room. After a few minutes of waiting, the guys hear a beat starting to play. It sounds exactly like N’Sync, but it is not. After a few moments of the guys just standing there, Jason bursts through the door. Jason: What’s going on here? Aren’t you supposed to be singing along? You’re wasting my time and your money! Stephen: I…I just can’t do it. It’s to god awfully horrible! What is this, anyways? Jason: Oh, this? It’s a song made by The Pretty Cool Guys. They’re a good band, plus they’re wrestlers. Pretty talented guys if you ask me! Stephen: That’s there song? That? I’ve heard so much hype over them, and I come here to find out that is there song! Why that’s just damned ridiculous! I’ve had enough. I can’t do this. I just can’t. No way. No How. No! Carey: Calm down! We don’t really need this now. Lets just go, we can’t do anything more here. We have already learned the crap our opponent sings; now lets just head out. They all agree to the plan, and they make there way out of the booth. They exit the room, and step into a long narrow hallway with records hung up on the walls. They continue to go down the hall until they reach an exit sign, but they notice something on the wall. There is a poster of “The Pretty Cool Guys” hung up on the wall, with a picture of them holding a CD case. “Tour Now” is written underneath the picture, so Stephen quickly turns around out of curiosity. Stephen: We have a chance to see what crap out opponent does, and at the same time meet some of those weird boy band people. Why not? Each of them reluctantly nods there head, and they all make there way back down the shiny hall. They finally come upon a room that has “Tour Sign-ups” written on the outside of the door. They all shrug their shoulders, but they burst through the door in a ruckus of noise. Carey, in his X-Files shirt and his white robe, Stephen, in his all white tight suit, and Jackson in his “Thug 4 Life” shirt with suspenders covering it all make an interesting picture as they make there way into the room. Inside it is a rather large room, but it is bare. A desk with a computer and supplies lies in the corner, and various cabinets are placed in the room. Posters of many boy bands are hung up on the walls, which creates a rather unpleasant atmosphere. A young female attendant is sitting at the desk sifting through a pile of papers. Finally, she shifts her attention to the boys, and her name tag reads “Happy People Inc.; Tours; “Amber”. Amber: Welcome to the Happy People Inc. Tours! The next tour to view the process of the boy band will be coming through in about five minutes. Would you like me to add you three…gentlemen to the list? Carey: Please do so. Thank you. Amber: You’re quite welcome. Now please relax and have a seat while you wait for your tour guide to pick you up… In the middle of there conversation, Jackson rudely interrupts. Jackson: Dizzamn! You be lookin’ fine girl! You wanna’ come home with me to help me an’ momma raise the chickens? You can help with da’ kids if ya’ want! Stephen gives Jackson another shot in the knee to shut him up once again. Amber: I’ll pass. Your tour should be here any min… Before Amber can finish the door started thumping. Carey politely saved Amber a trip by opening the door to reveal a much less pleasant picture. In the frame of the doorway, literally, is a very large male who appears to be rather sloppy. He also has a nametag on that reads “Tour Instructor: Miquel”. He motions the three out of the room, and back into that narrow hallway. Miguel: Good day. I’ll be your tour instructor today. We’ll be visiting such features as the recording rooms, the publishing rooms, and you may even have a chance to meet some stars. So, lets get started! We’re coming up on the publishing room where a speaker should be there. Miguel knocks on the door, and allows the gentlemen to enter the room. Inside is the man from earlier, Jason… Jason: Yo’ guys! It’s me again! Glad to see that you all have decided to drop by again… Stephen: **whispering** For Christ sake, we have to see you again. Jason: Excuse me? Did you say something? I could’ve sworn I heard one of you dudes shouting some totally bogus language out. Stephen: Nope, none of us. Jason just rolls his eyes, and continues to blab about all of the stars that have recorded here and all of the stars that he has met. After so long, none of them are very awake. Jackson: I swear, you be the most annoyin’ person I ever met, and that be a lot comin’ from me! You deserve to be stabbed wit’ somethin’ yo’! I dunno’ what, but I’ll be figurin’ something out! Jason: Excuse me? I’m doing a tour here, and if you don’t like it you better get out now! They all pause for a second, shrug their shoulders, and they turn out the door. Jason doesn’t seem pleased Jason: Guys! Wait! I was just joking…come back! They all kindly ignore him, and they continue down that hall. They come to a room with “Publishing” on the outside. Miguel once again taps on the door, and another young female attendant is standing at the door. Her name tag reads, “Michelle”. Michelle: Good day Gentlemen! Welcome to the publishing room! I’m glad to see you here. Well, this is the publishing room, where famous bands such as N’Sync, The Backstreet Boys, and The Pretty Cool Guys have had their CD published! The most famous of course is N’Sync, The Backstreet Boys, but you can’t count out the young talent of The Pretty Cool Guys! Not only can they play a good tune, but also they can kick some butt in the ring! Right over here you’ll see the…. Michelle continues to speak, but the three men don’t really pay any attention. They just talk between themselves softly. Jackson: Man…these white peeps gets mo’ hype than snake day back down home. Stephen: Yeah man, I’ve never even heard of them before today! Did you know that we have to wrestle against them on Friday? Jackson and Carey both chuckle, but quickly get a serious face. Meanwhile, Michelle is still blabbing about The Pretty Cool Guys. Michelle: …They’re not the most talented band yet, but they have potential! Carey: Excuse me, ma’am. Can these guys wrestle well? I mean…do they have any talent? Michelle: Of course! They jumped from the PWA straight to the PCW! They’re apparently pretty decent. They’re the group of tomorrow! That’s all that we have in the publishing room for you! Good day, gentlemen! They once again exit the room to reveal that same old hallway. Miguel: Guys! This is the fun of the tour! We have a chance for YOU to sing, and N’Sync is signing autographs! Lets hurry! The three look at each other in dismay, but they all follow Miguel to a room that has, “Private: Do Not Enter” on the outside. Miguel ignores that though, and he bursts through the door in joy. There are three microphone, and the band N’Sync sitting in the corner in steel chairs! Justin Timberlake stands up to greet the guys. Justin: Hey fellows! Glad to see some more tourists. Having a good time so far today? Stephen: Not…really…. Justin: Well, that’ll change! We’re going to give you an opportunity to sing just like us! You guys ready? Jackson sighs, Carey yawns, and Stephen just stands there. You can see the excitement in there eyes… Jackson: Man beeyo*ch, do we have to sing that crap ya’ll be singin’? I be hearin’ some hurt cows soundin’ better than dis’ crap! Justin: Well aren’t you a grumpy Gus today!! Don’t worry, this’ll be FAAABULOUS! Carey: Richard? Richard Simmons? Is that you? Holy good lord…it’s amazing. Justin Timberlake is gay. Justin: Silly Billy, who here is gay? The only thing that has a taste of gay is you guys not singing! Justin’s gay voice, and the way he is talking attracts a laugh from even Stephen. Stephen: And you had Brittany Spears? Why is Jennifer Lopez not on my lap then? This is just insane. Justin: Guys, stop being crazy! I’m ready to sing my heart out! How about you? Without saying anything else, the three men step up to the microphones. Justin gives a big grin, and signals for Joey to hit the music. Before they know it, “Bye, Bye, Bye” is blasting through the small room! Justin: This is one of my favorites! Can’t you guys sing? Lets go! Jackson steps up first to sing to the music, but he gets mixed up. Jackson: Uhh And you don't stop…Rockafella y'all …Clueminati. Who got the Gangsta Gangsta S*it? We got the Gangsta Gangsta S*it… Justin: This is “Bye, Bye, Bye”, not some stupid woopid rap stuff! Just “wrap” that up and send it away! Justin laughs at his own joke, but Jackson is far from amused. Jackson: You be dissin’ my music country boy? Mama’ will beat yo’ a*s if you keep dissin’ our style! You wanna’ have a pitchfork shoved up yo’… Stephen: Easy boy, easy! Look, I don’t really think we want to sing, alright? Now we’ll just be on our way. The three make there way to the door, but Justin tries to stop them. Justin: Wait guys! We can bond! Maybe we can cook some smore’s! Don’t you at least want our autograph? All three stop at the door way. Carey, Stephen, and Jackson: NO! Finally, they exit the room. They find themselves in that hallway for the last time. They see the exit down at the far end, and they slowly walk side by side until they push through the silver double doors. Outside is the city of Seattle on a cloudy day. People are walking through the streets to do some shopping, and many are walking their dogs. Right outside of the building is a bench, where the three guys all sit down. Stephen: Well, we know our opponents can’t sing, but can they wrestle? Carey: I guess only time will tell. If they act anything like N’Sync does, this should be a piece of cake. Those pop singers have never had a tough guy reputation. Stephen: Plus, we’ve analyzed our opponent! Anyone who has a voice like that can’t be better than us… They all sit back and relax. The wind is blowing, and a storm is coming into town. Jackson: I dunno’ bout’ you guys but I’m gonna’ be bouncin’ out! I gots to go help mama’ feed dem’ chickens! The kids if they’re lucky… Stephen: Well, it’s looking like a storm is coming. Lets head home. Jackson, we might just join you. Carey: Yeah… The guys get up off of the bench and start there way down the sidewalk. It’s about a mile walk to there apartment, and a storm is on its way. There’s not much more to see…
