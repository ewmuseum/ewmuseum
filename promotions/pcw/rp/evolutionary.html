Evolutionary

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered (Here, deep within the secret lair of THE Multiple Man, resident entourage techie Valerie Dietrich painstakingly maneuvers a tiny screw into an equally tiny hole on a device with no discernable purpose whatsoever. Small droplets of sweat form on her forehead as she nudges the bit of metal just a little bit more, just a little… the screw flips off the weakly magnetized surface of the screwdriver and rolls underneath a thick patch of wiring.) Val: “Aww… crud, not again.” (Outside, a shrilly monotonous beeping penetrates the thick walls of the lair, but this is dismissed as irrelevant as Val taps the side of the device’s casing in an attempt to shake the screw into a reachable position.) Val: “C’mon, you little…” (Tap, tap, tap, WHUMPH! The ground shudders under a harsh impact… and the screw bounces into place.) Val: “Gotcha!” (Swiftly, she stabs at the mischievous fastener with the sharp point of a Phillips-head! KA-THUMP! And the screw bounces right back out again. Val shrieks a long, keening wail as she flings the screwdriver at the nearest wall, where it clatters most satisfactorily. WHUMP! The screw jumps back in the hole like it was laser guided.) Val: “Yes!” (Hastily, she digs through a pocket and withdraws a small Swiss army-style knife. Flicking open the screwdriver, she reaches in… THUD! The Swiss army knife joins the screwdriver across the room. THOOM! The watertower shakes again.) Val: (with finality) “No, I’m not playing your little game any more. So there!” (She deliberately turns her back and marches to the ladder leading up to the rooftop, which she climbs without a backward glance.) “That looks good boys. Whoops! It’s starting to…” (WHUMP!) “… tip.” (Looking down from the roof, Valerie sees THE Multiple Man looking glumly at a massive slab of black rock carved into a rectangular shape. The weary figures of Raul and HV hurry to reattach lines to the top of the monolith in preparation for raising it yet again.) Val: “Are you QUITE finished with that?” (Tmm looks up, the grim focus on his face slipping into his usual sloppy grin.) Tmm: “Hiya Val! Check it out! I just got me the COOLEST lawn ornament EVER from Target! Let the neighbors envy ME now!” (He makes a rude gesture at the small hills blocking the direct line of sight to the nearby houses.) Tmm: “Lawn Gnomes! Bah! Pink Flamingos! Feh! This baby’s a certified one of a kind and full of stars to boot!” (Groaning and straining, HV and Raul struggle with the massive pulley system in another attempt to lever the sable slab upright. Ropes and men creak with effort, but the monolith does rise, inch by ponderous inch.) Tmm: “Another… WAIT! There! Hold it! Hold it! Now let it go!” (It wobbles briefly before settling solidly upon its base. Immediately a weird, almost buzzing music fills the air. Tmm pats the stone, almost as if it was a living thing.) Tmm: “Perfect! Now to get my sleeping bag!” (Tmm runs full out, chuckling all the while.) Val: “Uh-huh.” (She shrugs, dismissing the whole incident from mind as she descends the ladder again.) Val: (mildly surprised) “Huh.” (Strangely, the screw is now snugly bolted in place.) ---THE NEXT MORNING --- (The loose gravel of the dirt road that leads to the lair crunches underneath the sure tires of a dark green Honda Accolade as it makes its way up the low hill. Slowly the car stops, halted by the clustered figures of Raul, HV, and Steve deeply engaged in discussion. MEEP! The Accolade’s horn honk causes all three men to jump in surprise. The driver’s side window rolls down and Val pokes her head out.) Val: “What’s the holdup? I got stuff to do and I wanna park this thing!” (More deep discussion between the men.) Steve: “Umm… could you come and look at this?” Val: “Look at what?” (An expression much like painful gas crosses over Steve’s face.) Steve: “It’s better if you just look, I think.” (Curious, Val kills the engine and climbs out of the car. As she walks up, the men part, giving her a clear view of what’s ahead. Two strong impressions impress themselves upon Val’s mind in quick succession. The first: “The Monolith is gone.” And: “There is a floating ball of light looking at me.”) Val: “Duh?” (Cool thoughts flow over her consciousness like a refreshing drink on a hot summer’s day.) “Do not be afraid, you are in no danger. It is me, I have evolved.” Val: “Huh?” (The glowing ball of light quivers briefly and changes into… a glowing ball of light, with the ‘Tmm’ symbol upon it.) Tmm: “See?” Val: “Umm… yeah. So you’ve evolved…” (The sphere bounces up and down happily.) Tmm: “That is correct. I have shed my earthly form and taken this one composed of pure energy. I have moved beyond all material things and become one with the cosmos. It’s pretty cool actually, I’m really diggin’ it.” (The men cluster around Val and murmur.) Steve: “What do you think?” Val: “I’m really not sure what to think.” Steve: “Aww, c’mon! You can at least GUESS.” Val: “I’d have to do a whole battery of tests, but I would GUESS that they’d just confirm what the sphere said. The boss has turned into pure energy as previously stated.” Raul: (quietly) “[Something in Spanish.]?” (Light laughter from the sphere.) Tmm: ”Hahahaha! My friends, I have gone beyond such trivialities as wrestling! What appeal does physical violence hold when the mysteries of the multi-dimensional leyline matrix beckons? Steve: “WHAT?!” Tmm: “Silly mortal! Come, join me in perfect bliss. It is easier than could be imagined. Think of it! We shall travel the planes like gods forevermore!” (A blue nimbus of light starts forming around the sphere.) Steve: (whispering out of the side of his mouth) “I have a plan, play along!” (Steve clears his throat.) Steve: “Umm… there’s something I have to get first. In my room. Something I really, REALLY need.” (The nimbus of energy fades.) Tmm: “What? You’re going to be beyond such trivial matters, you won’t need anything at all!” Steve: “It’ll make me feel better. How about the rest of you?” Val: (confused) “Well, there are a couple of tools that would come in handy I could pick up for my lab…” Raul: “[Something in Spanish.]!” (HV simply nods meaningfully.) Tmm: “All of you? Sheesh. Oh well, if it’s that important to the lot of you, I suppose a few minutes more won’t matter when compared to eternity.” (Steve charges into the watertower with the others in tow. The sphere drifts along agreeably.) Steve: “This might take a little while, you might as well make yourself comfortable in the living room.” Tmm: (cheerfully) “Whatever you like.” (The ball wanders into the living room.) Steve: “I hope this works…” (He reaches under his shirt and pulls out the remote controller to the TV.) Raul: “[Something in Spanish.]!!!” (Steve grips the controller even more tightly.) Steve: “Hiding it is the only way I ever get the damn thing, and be glad for it! It just might save us all!” (Taking careful aim, he clicks on the TV. With the same motion, he slams the door to the living room closed.) Steve: “Now we wait.” Val: ‘Wait for…?” Steve: “My genius to save the day.” (Steve withers under the scornful looks thrown his way.) Steve: (defensively) “Well, it WILL! An hour should do it.” ---ONE HOUR LATER --- (The door to the living room creaks open slowly. From the small gap between the door and the frame, Steve’s arm pokes through and blindly fires the remote controller, turning off the television. This done, the door opens more fully.) Steve: “Hello?” (An animal-like grunt responds. Steve grins broadly.) Steve: “Like I said, my genius! Consider yourselves saved!” (There sprawled on the couch, is a very hairy Neanderthal-looking man staring blankly at the TV, an open can of Coors Light in its hand. The Ape-creature swivels its head and regards the Entourage from beneath sloping brows.) Ape-Tmm: “That Jimmy Spencer’s one fine racer, I reckon!” (Val turns to Steve.) Val: “NASCAR?!” (Steve’s grin somehow gets wider.) Steve: “Yup! Best way I could think of to devolve him, and it worked, too!” Ape-Tmm: “But that Ricky Rudd… WhooooEEEE!” Val: “A little too well, I’d say.” (Steve waves aside the concern nonchalantly.) Steve: “Get a little Tolstoy in him and he’ll be good as new. Not too much though, only a page or two I think…” Ape-Tmm: ”Turn that there picture box on, son! Monster Truck finals are comin’ up!” (…And the camera fades out.)
