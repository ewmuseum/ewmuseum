Date: Not too Long Ago... Out of His Mouth Came a Sharp Sword 'Self-Analysis' The tumult of our fretted mindGives us expression of a kindBut is is faulty, hars, not plain-My work has the incompetence of pain. we am consumed with a slow fire,For righteousness is our desire;Towards that good goal we cannot whip our will,we am a tired horse that jibs upon a hill. we desire Virtue, though we love her not-we have no faith in her when she is got:we fear that she will bind and make us slaveAnd send us songless to the sullen grave. we am like a man who fears to take a wife,And frets his soul with wantons all his life.With rich, unholy foods we stuf our maw;When we am sick, then we believe in law. we fear the whiteness of straight ways-we think there is no colour in unsillied days.My silly sins we take for our heart's ease,And know our beauty in the end's disease. Of old there were great heroes, strong in fight,Who, tense and sinless, kept a fire alight:God of our hope, in their great name,Give us the straight and ordered flame! -Anna Wickham- Dear Yuu, We clutch on to the last tatters of our heart. Constantly, our mind is gnawing at our emotions, telling us that our intuition is wrong. But our reason is flawed; our mind is corrupt and evil. A calamity from the skies. A falling disaster. She has called herself 'Cyclonica'. Our heart longs for goodness, but now, we fear that it is now impossible. Were we ever good? Did we ever do right? All we were doing was deluding ourselves. Telling ourselves that we were a good man when we were not. Telling ourselves we could change when nothing of the sort would happen. And yet, we hear ourselves saying, 'Resist, resist.' Yes, we am weary, tired, of trying to build ourselves up. Trying to impress other people, and tell them that we are worthy of being their friends. Trying to get others to tolerate us. But Grace tolerated us. She liked us for who we were. Why were we not afraid then? Why are we so afraid now? Because we know we are right. Grace is an exception, a beautiful light in the dark sea of woes. She is one of those rare people who can accept all she sees, and makes a judgment for herself. Had it been anyone else, they would have reported us to PCW and turned us in for crimes. In the afterlife, we learned that the Neo Void God's image was feared. The sword, Saishukessen Ougii, was the sharpest, and one of the finest weapons ever built, passed on from generation to generation. The skill of Akiyama was beyond comprehension. Godhood was his friend. His sword danced in his hand. But he was also cold-hearted and ice-blooded, caring nothing for anyone around him, not even himself. When we returned, we found that we were still Akiyama, but only in body, and not in mind. When we heard the serene voices in the afterlife, they told us of all the horrible things we had done in our life. They told us about Cyclonica, PCW, and our birth. When we rose again, how we did not know, but when we rose again, we vowed not to be like our old self. But it's such a hard journey. And, when we think about it, it can't entirely be up to us. We attempt to be good, to be noble, and to uphold honor. But we will never be recognized as a good man. We have done all we have done. Now, it's up to the minds of others to change. The puny minds of human beings are small and closed. When they see someone, they make a stereotype. A man with dark skin, they imagine, must be violent due to his large muscles. A man who cannot speak must be without intelligence, because he cannot say anything to anyone else. The white man is superior to all others because he has managed to enslave so many other men… Nonsense… Humans cannot learn how to tolerate others. That forever will be their weakness. They will be limited, not by their frail bodies, not by the need to breathe oxygen, and not by the need to digest food, but by their minds, which refuse to open up to accept the impossible, which refuse to open up to see people for who they really are. And that is why the path is so hard. We do not know how much farther we can go. We are sad all of the time, now. No one is going to accept us for who we are because when they see us, they will associate our image with one of an evil being. And we cry at the thought. No matter how hard we try, they will see us as an evil being. What if we destroyed all of those who are the true evils? If we took out Nelson, then the people would like us. They will like us if we help them, and we know the Nelson along with Chef and Wonder are evil men. He has hurt hundreds of people by his orders. He has harmed hundreds more because of those who work under him. The strength of the man pales in comparison to ours. Will people like us if we defeat the wrong? If we defeat them all, then we will be killing two birds with one stone. The people will like us, and we will also get our revenge against the man who wanted to be our god. They say that a god is invincible, indestructible. We will prove them wrong. The petty humans, with their closed minds, will open up when their leader, their evil, tyrannical leader, is destroyed. They will hail us as a man who wishes to do good. They will accept us for who we are, and not who we used to be. Right? We are not sure. But the defeat of the CEO feels so right. It is so proper for it to happen. If we do it, it is the good thing to do. Cyclonica agrees with us. She is happy that we are doing something with our life, instead of hiding out in a cave. We believe we cannot develop any further. Our skills are at their best. Our sword is straight. It flies true. It does not waver in our hand. Our mind is clear. As clear as it can be. Cyclonica helps us now. She encourages us. We will destroy those who are feared and hated. We will gain our revenge. We will earn the respect of the people. We will grab our sword, the Saishukessen Ougii. It is sharp, and it is honed. It will serve us after a delay of almost five years. All who commits injustice… prepare for Akiyama. ** Akiyama **
