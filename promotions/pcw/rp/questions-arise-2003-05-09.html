Questions Arise

9 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/9/03 I never thought a talk with Thomas could revolutionize my life the way it has. The fact that he thought to depart with all of his ill feelings towards me, to help me out in a rough time, meant a lot to me. Possibly more than he will ever know. Of course if I did mention it to him his ego would set in, bringing us back to the bickering and arguing that we are so accustomed to. He just boosted my confidence level by fifty percent, and now it’s at a healthy 85%. That man is just a major mystery. I will never understand how he can go from drowning me out and barreling me with insults and hate, to pulling a Dr. Phil and helping me with my problems. I guess I just don’t understand the male mind. Grimer squirmed in Brianna’s hand as she tried feverishly to give the little white mouse a bath. This is the only thing she dreaded as it always served as nothing but a hassle. You would think it would be over in a mere five minutes or so, but it always escalates to half hour with a couple of bite marks here and there. “Come on…just let me put a little soap on you and your done.” Brianna pleaded with Grimer as he was sitting in the bowl of nice cool water, squirming around violently. She managed to get a little on him and began to rub it gently around his little body with him clawing away at her hands trying to free himself. She then takes a little capful of water and pours it over him, washing away some of the soap. She repeats this process two more times and he latches onto her hand with his two front teeth causing her to pull away. “I have really got to invent something that’s gonna make this easier. You don’t like it, I don’t like it, but it has to be done if you want to keep your perty fur.” She pulls him out of the bowl and Grimer’s attitude quickly changes as she sets him down on a plush red towel and dries him off. Giving little Grimer a bath is always a struggle, but it’s just something that needs to be done. It’s kind of like wrestling for me. I struggle to fight the critics, and try to become victorious over my opponents. Sure it doesn’t always go as planned, but I just keep at it. Grimer is the least of my worries now. I have to go on tonight to take on one of the toughest newcomers in PCW to date. Miss Moonlight is definitely no push over, so this is going to be a major challenge. I’m just going to have to give it all I got, and if it doesn’t work out, so be it. The important thing is that I made an attempt. Besides I'm not sure I'm ready to handle another title. I have a lot to work on personally if I hope to be a successful title holder. Her mind stops momentarily as she places Grimer in her arms and carries him back to his cage on top of her dresser. As soon as he hits the wood chippings, he darts over to his bowl full of pellets and pigs out. Brianna feels her stomach grumble and decides she should take after Grimer and get something to eat, so she races down the steps and into the kitchen. “What to eat….what to eat…hmmm.” She says rummaging through the refrigerator like a raccoon through a pile of trash. She rests her brown eyes upon a small container of berry yogurt. She greedily pulls it out and is about to close the door when Milo pops up behind her. “Step away from the yogurt!” he yells as though he were a police officer about ready to arrest a suspect. “Come on have some heart. I’m hungry!” “Fine have it. I have a date tonight with Danny anywhere and we’re going to that new restaurant down the street.” He says while inspecting his smile on a well shined spoon. “You change men like you do your clothes. Everyday it’s something new.” “I’m just not the kind of guy to tie down. Don’t get me wrong, I like to get tied down, but not the way your talking about.” He says giving Brianna a wink as she cringes at the mere thought. “You’re disgusting sometimes you know that?” “You need to take after me and get yourself a man girl. Maybe then you wouldn’t be uptight all the time.” “I’m not uptight!” Brianna says defensively as she places her hand on her hip ready to square off. “That right there only proves it. Go out and have fun! Go to the bar, get drunk, and all your troubles will be put behind you.” “When do I have time to do that? I especially can’t tonight, I have a big match that I should be getting ready for. Besides I’m going to be out of the country remember? All the way in Greece.” “Damn that’s far! Ok then, what about Friday?” “Definitely not going to happen. We’ll be in Korea and there is no way I’m not going into work. I’m in the main event! Do you realize how long I’ve waited for that kind of opportunity?” “Yeah, yeah I know. Who knows, you may find yourself a little Korean man who wants to shack up with ya.” He says laughing as he exits the kitchen. “Whatever.” Brianna says after him, digging the spoon into the yogurt, piling it into her mouth. Am I really uptight? Do I really need a man? The scene slowly fades to black as she ponders that thought.
