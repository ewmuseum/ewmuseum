ID? I Don't Need No Stinkin' ID!

1 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4 - 1 - 03 A steady stream of golden, skin cancer-causing light pours forth through the slick, transparent windshield of the black Jeep Rubicon and casts its shining aura on the shades of Dorn Tracer, who pilots the vehicle. Wearing a grim look of irritation on his handsome face, Tracer steers his new Jeep with one hand at the bottom of the wheel and cradles his other arm across the console sitting in between him and the passenger seat. ‘Son Of X-51’ by Powerman 5000 plays at a low, but still speaker-pounding volume within the confines of the 4x4 Jeep, sending a slight thumping through the disturbed head of the Shooting Star Champion. The headache-like feeling of the song’s bass in his head irritates the Astronomer further, but yet, he does nothing about it. Something obviously disturbs him… even to the point where he speaks aloud to no one in particular. Tracer, to himself: The Continental Title tournament… quite a prospect. Hopefully that fool Drachen can hold out, both mentally and physically. It’d be a shame to lose because he suddenly goes insane and has a stroke in the middle of the ring… Tracer, still to himself: But then again, it’s to be expected from such a weakling. I bet he’d shit his pants tenfold if someone with silver hair walked by and said hello… The Shooting Star Champion and Ace Of Astrology begins to laugh to himself, a convincing laugh of satisfaction and humor. Lately, he’s been trying to get within Drachen’s head and find out what causes him pain. Any kind of pain. The lowly pizza boy seems slightly resistant to physical pain, though… perhaps his martial arts training aided in that. Tracer: He can throw kicks, sure enough… but yet, his concentration is so easily broken. It puzzles me… Dorn branches off from his train of thought and slowly presses down on the brake as he comes up to a stop sign. Breezing through the empty countryside helps him clear his thoughts; he doesn’t have to worry about other drivers, other wrestlers, other people at all. He can just aimlessly wander the maze of amber waves of grain, just thinking to himself. No one else out there. It allows him to contemplate his endeavors… which he’s focusing on right now. Dorn lets loose of the brake and continues on down the narrow stretch of road, still having a conversation with himself. Tracer: Ah, but yet, he’s fueled by the fire of his past failures. Against Deamont, against Mathews… just failure in general. He’s so tired of being branded a failing nobody, that’ll pick ANY fight, no matter how impossible, and try to regain respect. Admirable, but his pride… or lack of… will be his undoing. What Dorn fails to realize amidst his sinister thoughts of victory is that he has begun to pick up speed that scales beyond the limit. The limit is 55, and Dorn doesn’t realize he’s going 75. Tracer: He’s already tasted my wrath, but yet, he persists. Must be awfully desperate. I don’t see how he’s missing the fact that he cannot stop me, one way or another… with the backing of the Tiamats and very stars above my head, NO ONE can stop me… HAHAHAHA! His sinister, glee-sown laugh breaks out like a scene out of a movie, and disrupts him just the same. Dorn fails to notice that a pair of tell-tale lights are suddenly whirling around inside his vehicle, reflecting off his mirrors and appearing in the windshield. The Astronomer just continues laughing for a moment before the horrifying sounds of police sirens cut him off. Almost like his face froze up, Dorn stops laughing and gazes into his rear-view mirror. Sure enough, there’s a cop trailing him. Tracer, angrily: FUCK!! Damn pig… Dorn turns the wheel to the right with a look of extreme apathy on his face and slams down on the brake as his Jeep enters the shoulder of the road. He shifts the Jeep into park and angrily boots the underside of the dash, disgusting by his thread of luck. Tracer: This is all I need… stars damn this uniformed donut slammer… The cop Buick pulls up about 10 feet behind Dorn and comes to a halt, but the lights remain. The sirens dull out and the car’s engine sits at a low rumble, showing it to be in neutral. Dorn continues to scout the law enforcement vehicle through his rear-view mirror, wearing a look of disgust upon his face. It curdles into something awful when a tubby police officer steps out from within the vehicle. His black suit stretches against his unholy girth as his long, handlebar mustache waivers in the wind. The officer grabs his belt and tries to suck his gut in, obviously an attempt to look important. Like a penguin with a badge, he waddles over to the driver’s seat of Dorn’s vehicle. A rather agitated Dorn rolls down his window and puts on the best polite look he can muster through his internal thunderstorm of anger and disgust. Tracer: Hello… officer… Officer: Well now boy, you, uh, know what I, uh, pulled ya over fer? Tracer: No idea, sir… In all reality, he had a pretty good idea. But he wasn’t about to admit anything to this slob, who still smelled of chocolate éclairs. Officer: Well uh, you were, uh, going pretty fast… ‘bout 70, I’d say. Tracer, feigning guilt: I’m SOO sorry, officer. How irresponsible of me. Luckily, the pedestrians of this open countryside had you to protect them from me! I’ll try not to do it again. Officer, falling for it: No problem, sir. But, uh, I need to uh, see your identification. Dorn hadn’t though that this donut-eating behemoth would actually ask for his ID after that little facade. His face begins to slowly sink into confusion and dismay, for there is another problem… He had forgotten his ID. He had forgotten his wallet in general. Dorn left it back at the motel because he only planned to drive around in the countryside for awhile. Now that this pepper spray-armed pig was after his ID, what was Dorn going to do? His eyes dart around for a moment and he reaches back around to his right back pocket… but ultimately comes up empty. He gives the cop a puzzled look and shrugs. Tracer: I don’t think I have it with me. Officer: Driving without a license, eh?! Tracer: Well… uh… Suddenly, an idea springs to Dorn’s crafty mind. He shoots the officer a grin and reaches into the backseat of the jeep. The officer, somewhat panicked by this sudden motion, backs up and places a quivering hand on the butt of his pistol. But when the Astronomer returns with a large, golden looking belt, the officer sighs and steps back up the Jeep. Officer: What is that? Tracer: Take a good look at it, officer. It’s my identification. The fat cop takes a good, long look at the belt and doesn’t realize that he’s staring into the glittering face of the PCW Shooting Star Title. Dorn beams proudly as he holds the beautiful title up for the officer to see, its shimmering face displaying its brilliance. The officer, though impressed by the spectacle, fails to see how it passes as ID. Officer: But uh… that isn’t identification. Tracer: Sure it is! Read the nameplate. The fat, uniformed authority figure gone donut eater stares at the golden name plate below the center of the face and cocks an eyebrow. Officer: Dorn…Tracer? Tracer, with great pride: The one and only! Now, officer, that should be enough. So if you don’t mind, I’ll just be- Officer: I still need your ID. The tan color on the Astronomer’s face begins to slowly drain, like shit through the bottom of the toilet bowl. He presses the Shooting Star Title forward, like a last ditch effort to escape. Tracer: Right here! Officer: Uh, son… that’s not an ID. That’s a huge belt. Tracer: IT’S NOT JUST A HUGE BELT! Don’t you watch PCW? Officer: Can’t say I do… Tracer: Well listen here, you fucking Fief wannabe! I’m Dorn Tracer, the fucking PCW SHOOTING STAR CHAMPION! I’ve stomped foes left and right! I’ve worn this title with pride and dignity! The company has placed an entire division on my shoulders. I have to face some fat slob in the form of the Overweighter this Friday. Be sure and watch, so you can see who you fucking stopped for something as trivial as speeding in the country!! I’ll have you chocolate-stained badge, you slovenly waste of cellulite! The officer just stares at Dorn blankly for a moment, who is steaming with rage. After a moment of pseudo-silence, the officer squints at Dorn angrily. Officer: You’re coming with me. Tracer: WHAT?! I have to prepare for Friday!! Weren’t you listening?! Officer: I, uh, don’t care. Let’s go, sir. Right now. Tracer: NEVER, OPPRESIVE PIG! YOU’LL NEVER TAKE ME ALIVE!!!!!!! Dorn flings the driver’s side door open, smashing it into the cop’s stomach and sending him sprawling backwards into the street. The Astronomer leaps out of his vehicle and sprints towards the cop car, Shooting Star Title in hand. The dumbass donut eater has left the door open, so Dorn leaps into the car and slams the driver’s door shut. The cop has already recovered and darts over to his vehicle as fast as his little legs will carry him, but it’s too late. The Shooting Star Champion locks the door and slams his foot on the gas, turning the car just in time before it slams into his jeep. The Barney Fief wannabe staggers back and lands on the concrete ass first, a puzzled and shocked look on his face. As the stolen police car speeds away, the driver’s side window rolls down and Dorn’s middle finger, hand and arm with it, appear and face the cop. In the stolen vehicle, Dorn quickly grabs the CB radio that broadcasts like a megaphone. Tracer: FUCK THE SYSTEM! [End.]
