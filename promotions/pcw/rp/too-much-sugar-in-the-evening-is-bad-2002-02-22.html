Too Much Sugar In The Evening Is Bad...

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/02/02 The quest to find Justin Howard is well underway, using the Super Genius power of Alan Kreigman that she inherited through the Snapple~! endorsement deal given to her by Xavier, Ellie~! (That’s right, Frisco gave her the “~!”) has constructed a machine, that using a DNA sample from one of Justin Howard’s hairs can locate the brother of Xavier’s Asylum Stablemate Jeremy “Krayzie” Howard and transport him into her secret underground laboratory, removing him from the clutches of Drake Maxwell and the End. This task was set nearly 3 weeks ago, after Jeremy Howard snapped and obliterated Clyde and Akiyama, his moment was soiled by the arrival of Drake Maxwell, the man who turned Jeremy’s brother against him appeared and made a revelation. The man who Howard thought had played Cane to his Abel was in fact the End, who had teamed with Drake Maxwell to make a name for himself, and how better to do so then go after one of PCW’s top guys. However Jeremy didn’t give them the satisfaction and instead dispatched Kannon and Bobby Stormbringer onto them, leading to the creation of the aforementioned machine. Ellie~! put the hair in and completed her DNA location, finding a match and making the transportation. However, it looks like something went awry with the machine as what came out didn’t look like anything the End could have played convincingly, and will things get worse when she introduces the creature to Snapple~! “Here Justin, Justin, Justin!”. We hear the voice of Xavier Kannon’s valet Eleanor Hall as we fade in from Black, she is holding out a bottle of Snapple~! Apple to the small Brown and White creature that came from her machine, which was meant to transport Justin Howard from wherever the End and Drake Maxwell have him hidden. As earlier, Ellie~! is wearing her White lab coat with Snapple~! written on the breast pocket and under it she has a Purple roll-neck sweater, Tan coloured Aztec patterned fabric belt holding up a pair of faded Blue jeans with Red embroidery down the sides of the flared legs. She tilts the bottle towards the small, curious creature and it leans in slowly, seemingly smelling the official soft drink of PCW. “EEEEEERRRKK!” it squeaks as it hops on the spot, still checking out the bottle. The creature is just shy of 3 foot tall, and if anything it resembles a Furbie but with sharp teeth inside it’s mouth. “EEEEEEEEEEEERRK!” it squeaks again, hopping right up to the mouth of the bottle. “There, drink the Snapple~! Apple...” says Ellie~!, tilting it even more as the little animal tries to get it’s mouth to the liquid. “Just time to have a quick drink, then I think I might have too send you back....” “AIIIIK!” it then says, almost as a question to Ellie’s last words, showing a sense of understanding. Ellie looks back at it, wondering if it actually knew what she just told it. “Because,” she begins to answer, taking it’s squeak as a question as to why it is to be sent back, “I am looking for Justin Howard, and although I’ve never actually seen a picture of him...I don’t think you are him....Are you?” “NEEEEEEEEEK!” it squawks back, lowering it’s head and eyes, as if it is admitting not being the brother of Asylum’s leader. “Then I’m afraid you have to go back, and you wan to know who to blame?” she asks it. The creature then lifts it’s head up and cocks it’s head to the side, letting out almost a purr, signalling to Ellie~! to explain why. “Okay, there is this guy called Drake Maxwell, who Xavier....Oh? You don’t know Xavier...Ah well, I’ll fill you in on him later. Anyway, back to Drake, he was like, all....” Ellie~! then pulls the most miserable and disinteresting face she can, pulling her bottom eye lids down and frowning, “....Then there was the end, and he was like, all.....” She then rolls her eyes up so that only the Whites are showing before prodding around in front of her as if carrying a cane, “....So together they were all SO freaky and miserable, but only one ripped off Jeff Hardy for his look!” As Ellie~! explains the story, complete with facial expressions the creature nods along, appearing to follow her every word and understand it. “Then one day, the freaky one that could see asks the extra from Eyes Wide Shut to dress up for him, but, you know, in a hetro way...Well, I sincerely hope, that thought would SO not be good for me right now,” The Preppy Evil Super Genius screws her face up at the thought of their relationship being anything more than just business, “Then the freaky one with the funny hair that could see and acts miserable decided to kidnap Jeremy’s brother, because, lets face it, Krayzie was kicking his ass around every arena we went to, so the freaky one who looks like Jeff Hardy needed something to at least make it near level.” Seeing that the creature is now comfortably crouching down listening to her and not going for the Snapple~!, Ellie~! chooses to take a sip instead to refresh her mouth for the continuation of the story. “Where was I....Oh yeah, Hard-...Sorry, Maxwell had kidnapped Justin and then got the End to dress up as him...Man, that would have made for some sick threesomes...” as she thinks about what she has said, her face screws up as if she had just swallowed every sour ball in the world, or taken her earphones out when she listened to Drake Maxwell promos, “EWWWWWWW! Alright already with those images please brain!” She says, slapping herself on the side of the head. As the creature stares on bemused, Ellie~! gives her head a vigorous shake to get that thought out before continuing, “So then he got the freaky one who couldn’t see to turn on Jeremy, whilst staying under a hood, and not being able to see.....Wait, how the hell does that work?” she asks, the creature shrugs, “He’s Blind, yet he, like, does normal stuff. Now I know he will probably say it’s some heightened sense thing, well if that is true...TRY HEIGHTENING THE ONE YOU DON’T HAVE AND THEN YOU MIGHT BE ABLE TO SEE!“ she rants before breathing out and calming herself down. “Okay....Calm...” she says as the Brown and White animal in front of her sighs as well at the ending of her raised voice, “So I guess it must be blind as in he wears a blindfold so is, like, blind...But then he looks under it so he isn’t, y’know...Blind!” muses the ex-Cruiserweight Champion’s valet, “At least that’s how I guess it will work, how did he get blind anyway....What could have possibly been rammed into his head to make hi-.....EWWWW!” Ellie~! cringes again and shakes her hands at another unwelcome image in her head, “I SO have to stop DOING THAT!” once again the Preppy valet attempts to empty her head, the little creature in front of her wondering what kind of dimension he ended up in. “Right, the freaky one that can’t see, well, he can’t see...We’ll leave it at that. So on Sunday they face Xavier, the guy I told you about earlier, and Bobby Stormbringer....But I don’t really know who he is but his name is Bobby, and I think he is a weatherman or something, anyway, that isn’t important as all he will do is beat up the blind one...What is important is that Xavier is SO totally going to punch a smile onto Drake’s face so that it cracks and falls off....Hmmm, maybe that should be a metaphor...Which ones are metaphors again?” she asks the creature, watching it shrug it’s shoulders before continuing. “Well I think that should have brought you up o date on what’s going on,” she tells it, “So that is why you can’t stay, I have to search for this Justin person for Xavier because he lets me stay here and stuff for no money....That and I want to have something for him when this secret cave collapses his house above it!” she adds, looking around her secret space under the Scientologist’s Beverly Hills mansion. The small animal nods it’s little head, understanding why it must return. It then gestures to the Snapple~! with one of it’s little arms and Ellie~! and she extends the bottle out, tipping it just in front of the little animal. It moves it’s head slowly forward and eases it’s mouth into that of the bottle and takes a sip. “There, isn’t Snapple~! Apple SO the best flavour?” she asks it, however, the creature is not resounding and instead is standing upright, shivering, “Hehe, someone’s got a sugar high!” she giggles before the animal lets out a huge roar. “AAAAAAAAIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIGGGGGGGGGGGAAAAAAAARRRR!” it bawls, causing Ellie~!’s face to whiten before she falls backwards off her seat and rubs the back of her head. “Like....OW!” is the whine emitted as the Prep gets back up to her feet and looks around the lab, trying to see where the animal; is. “Hey, get over here and apologise!” she wails, upset at what happened, “Hey, I brought you here and I can send you back too.....If I can find you that is, so that’s why you need to get here!” A crash is then head over the other side of the lab and when she looks over she sees the Robotic Young Tiger strewn on the floor, it’s wiring pulled out and raining sparks all over the concrete floor. “Hey!” she screams, “That’s my Super Jobbertron 89-70 model! I don’t have any spares of that you little furball!”. As she walks over to it another sound is heard, this time a large explosion over in the corner which sends bits of bench and scrap metal flying across the lab, “No! Not my exploding elbow pads!” she runs over but again the creature is up to mischief and the sound of hammering distracts her again as she sees a slightly larger Green scaly creature smashing the device she brought it into this reality with. “Nooooo,” she whines, stomping her feet in annoyance and jumping on the spot in anger, “Talk about being ungrateful you skanky little hairball....Hmmm, maybe you are Drake Maxwell’s valet?” she stands and sulks as the small Green creature wreaks havoc in her lab despite her protests. Suddenly guitar music is heard. “Eh?” Ellie~! exclaims, “Nerf Herder?” as Ellie mentions it the theme from Buffy the Vampire Slayer (The Season 4 version, which despite being maybe the worst series had the best edit of the theme) starts up and a Blond woman is seen leaping down from the fireman’s poll connection the lab to the house above’s living room with a flying kick that sends the Green creature flying into a wall before it scurries off under the tables. “Hey,” says the Blond woman who looks strangely like Sarah Michelle Gellar, “You didn’t feed that thing anything sugary did you after the sun went down?” she asks Ellie~! who gives it some thought. “Um, it had some Snapple~! at just gone 7.” the Prep answers. “That explains it,” replies the girl, “These creatures have been looking for any small tear in the fabric of time so they can crawl in, looking all cute and furry then showing their true colours and reeking havoc....Anyways, what were you doing tearing through space and time?” she asks. Ellie Um’s and Ah’s, trying to think of an excuse, “Er...” the valet says stalling, “Isn’t that it over there?” she says, pointing over to the corner and causing the Slayer to leap up in a front somersault onto the bench in front of her before cartwheeling down it and flying off, pulling her stake out and driving it into the Green creature causing it to go limp. “Yeah!” yells Ellie~!, “That’ll teach it for messing up my lifelike robot, explosives and time fabric tearing DNA locator device...” she blurts out before realising what she said, “Er....Or the TOASTER as some people call it!” The Slayer tosses the dead creature into the bin in the corner and says her goodbyes, “Okay, just try not to set your toaster to the wrong dimensional path oscillating frequency, try 7 next time...Bye.” As the Slayer effortlessly shimmies up the poll Ellie~! looks around the smoking, broken remains of her lab. “Damn,” she whines, crossing her arms in a huff and stamping her feet, “Now it’s going to take me a whole 3 minutes to design an automated debris cleaning until...Do they think I am made of time or something?” As Ellie~! gets to starting the rebuilding process Xavier slides down the pole and lands, looking around the lab with curiosity showing in his piercing Green eyes. “Did I hear Nerf Herder? I thought you said they were a freaky metal kid’s band...” he then takes in the full extent of the damage, “What happened here!” he exclaims, opening his arms out wide to gesture to the whole lab. The camera zooms in on Ellie’s pale Blue eyes, trying to look as innocent as possible, “Um....” she says, “Nothing?” the Prep adds as the shot fades to Black.
