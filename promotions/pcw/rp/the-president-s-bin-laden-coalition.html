Date: not entered The Oval Office… “Let’s move out people! This isn’t a drill! This is the real deal, by George!” Officials ran down the hallways, their big mission at hand… “Come on!! This is a matter of the utmost urgency!!' The orders were given…and now they must be executed to the fullest, lest they put the fate of their lives into total anarchy… 'If I have to go and do it myself I will! I’ll make sure that they pay for what they did!' Our leader…strong and bold…his head clear and concise in this time of crisis. He sits at his desk with the back of the chair facing us as his orders are executed by his commanding troops. =©= Outside… the metal clicking of sharp heels are heard across the pavement, accompanied by heavier steps behind them. =©= 'I want a status report!' A suit in shades stands before him, a large black man with a shaved head and a mean glare. 'Our target is heading straight for us, sir! What do you want us to do?' The chair swivels a bit, and the top of our leader’s head can be seen, the gray hairs looking down before nodding. 'Let him in' A look of surprise came over the Secret Service agent. 'But sir! Letting him in may be a serious breach of security! If he gets in, then who knows what kind of chaos may unfold!' 'I SAID… let…him…in.' A sigh came from the guard’s lips as his head lowered. 'Yes, sir.' =©= Outside…the visage of a mountain of a man stands on the front lawn of the White House. Next to the huge shadow was a smaller one, ladylike in appearance. =©= 'Sir! The “messiah” has just entered the building and is now heading your way!' We could see the back of the man’s head nod in anticipation. Everything was going according to plan, or so it seemed… 'Inform me of when he about to enter the Office. I want to be ready for anything.' =©= Eyes turned towards their direction as they stalked towards the pinpoint of operations. The two of them, above humanity…as proven so before in PCW feats of inhumanity. As if you didn’t recognize the two storming America’s castle at this very moment… Those vinyl pants…those black taped forearms…those black nailed hands…black mesh shirts…her long black and blue hair…his black and silver braided ponytail… Akiyama and Cyclonica… The Neo Void God and the Divine Prophetess were going after the President of the United States… …So much for being faces… =©= The five guards in the Oval Office were ready, their hands inches away from their guns. The black agent from before pressed the ear piece closer into his ear. He nodded and looked over to the chair, the back still facing away from us. 'He’ll be here in thirty seconds!' Just then, the doors flew open with a crash. The nVg walked in with the Divine Prophetess close by his side. They both ignored the drawn weapons of the Secret Service and approached the desk of the nation’s president. Cyclonica leaned on the desk with both hands, her nails digging into the wood and leaving indentations. ' So…you dare think you can get away with this, you filthy swine?' The chair turned around, revealing George W Bush, dressed in a gray suit and that grimace on his face. 'My thoughts exactly…' He stood up and stepped around his desk. Then with a bold statement of courage, or more or less foolishness, he stood before Akiyama and stared him straight in his black eyes. 'Pardon me.' The god stepped aside slowly, allowing him to lay his beady eyes upon the youth behind him dressed in a red shirt and jeans. Bush looked sternly at him. 'Son…I don’t know what you thought you were trying to pull, but around here, we don’t take too kindly to acts of terrorism by they who think they can get away with doing this country wrong. Now…I believe there’s something you owe me.' The youth lowered his head, reaching into his pocket and pulling out a ten-dollar bill. He placed it in the President’s hand reluctantly. 'Is that it?' Bush asked the youth. 'Sorry…Mr. President.” 'Good…now agents…get this goon out of my White House.' Two agents hefted the kid by his arms and led him out of the Oval Office, closing the doors behind him. Bush pocketed the bill and looked back to Akiyama and Cyclonica, who were staring at him with irritated eyes. 'What…the FREAK…was that all about?' 'I tell that kid time and time again not to put anchovies on my pizza, but the snot nosed punk never listens! So this time, I took back my tip!' Silence…and glaring… 'You demand the presence of the Neo Void God to this pig’s sty of political corruption and filth…and then you waste our time by setting us aside for the pizza youth?' Cyclonica hissed at him, clenching her nails deeper into the wood desk. 'You mortals are fools…and it seems like the political mortals are more stupid than the standard ones.' Bush chuckles. 'You PCW people are a riot! Now come on over here and have some pizza with the others!' He walked over to the side room and opened the door, revealing a banquet room with about six people inside munching away at the contents of pizza boxes on the table in the center of the lavish room. A huge sigh came rushing out of the Divine Prophetess as her head lowered. 'Humanity…is lost…to ignorance.' They walk in, ignoring the pizza, and obtaining a good brooding position in the back of the room. The other guests turn their attention to Bush at the front of the room. 'I’d like to first and foremost thank you all for attending this very important meeting. I’m United States President George W Bush…how y’all doing?' The Celestials cringed at the cheerful response from the other people. Bush smiled and nodded. 'That’s good to hear. Now, I’d like to go around the room and announce everyone that’s here, so we can get to know who’s who. I’ve gathered the world’s top intelligence operatives to help me out on this case.” Bush points over towards a plump Jamaican woman in a multicolor dashiki and a wrap matching around her head. She was busy filling up with bread sticks on her plate. “Ms. Cleo…world renowned psychic.” She looks up with her wide toothed grin, particles of parmesan bread stuck between her teeth. “I AM MISS CLEO!! CALL ME NOW!” Bush shrugs. “If you say so…everyone…Ms. Now…world renowned psychic.” Cyclonica dropped her head, looking down at the ground. “By the Void…” “Next…” Bush went on, trying hard to be the perfect host here for this event. “…Is Guy Noir, first class private eye.” Some worn out gumshoe in a brown crumpled suit and brown fedora was hunched over a sausage pizza, picking off anchovies. He looked up and rubbed the stubble on his chin. “The name’s Noir…Guy Noir. I tell you, the tension in the room was so thick you could cut it, put it on plates, and pass it out in servings like birthday layer cake. Nobody knew the reason why the President of the United States had gathered us all into that small, cramped, pizza-scented room but he and he alone…but I was determined to get to the bottom of it…or my name wasn’t Guy Noir…Private Eye.” With that said, the detective goes back to inhaling the anchovies on his pizza. “Third, there’s Trent Lockhart, expert global hunter of all kinds.” Bush motions towards a man wearing a pair of Zebra skin pants, a Lion skin jacket, a white tank top, and a cowboy hat lined with Crocodile teeth. He sniffs at the pizza before tossing his blonde ponytail aside. “If it moves, I can sniff it out.” He takes in a big whiff through his nostrils. “Ah, yes…there’s air in here…” And this is the point where the Celestials get restless… “Fourth, there are two special guests, Akiyama and Cyclonica, of PCW fame.” Bush announced. The three people looked back at them with raised eyebrows. A slow growl from the god sent them looking back to the prez. “Why are we here?” Cyclonica asked impatiently. “What do we have to do with what you’re looking for?” “Oh, I figured if there was anyone that could help me find whom I’m looking for, it would be you two! I just love all that kooky banishment stuff you do on TV!” chuckles rise up from the nation’s leader. Cyclonica rolls her eyes, folding her arms and leaning against the corner she lurked in. “This is going to be one of those times when I wish I was still back in the isolation tank at the Department of Divine Examination in Japan.” =©= Japan…the Department of Divine Examination… Around a round table sat the Executives, Reality, Knight, Illusion, and Spice. They all held a hand of playing cards, and eyed each other warily in that darkened bare room. “When do you think we’ll get clearance to chase after Akiyama and Cyclonica?” Reality asked them, putting down a two of diamonds before scratching at his blood red hair. The yellow haired Illusion scooped it up and chuckled to himself softly. “Whenever Angelica gets off her perfect ass and puts more effort into it.” Down goes a five of spades. “Which, going by her record of promptness, was about a few months ago.” Spice looked down at her hand carefully, which was positioned above her revealing cleavage of her red mini dress. “Hopefully we can get out of this building and get some action in. We’ve been here since Akiyama broke the Divine Prophetess out. It’s not doing a thing for my complexion.” She snatched her hand away when she saw Illusion leering over at her. “Stop peeking!” “I doth not think that Sir Illusion was looking at thou hand, Lady Spice.” The larger Knight watched drop down a seven of clubs, which he picked up and laid to the side. “Anyway, we still haveth the matter of looking for Squire Matrix. He hath been missing since that entire fiasco.” Reality yawned as he picked up another card, dropping the final two upon the pile. “Well more or less, there had better be some action going on around here soon…or I’m liable to blow a gasket. And that’s the truth, my compadres.” The door suddenly flew open, and a shapely figure stood in the doorway. “What’s going on here? Why are the lights out?” Angelica flipped on the lights, causing the other four Executives to hiss at the light. She rolled her eyes as she stepped into the completely white and bare room, save the table. “Get off your Executive asses and get your briefcases. We have a job to do.” Illusion jumped up, slamming his cards onto the table. “Do we have clearance to chase down Subject 3-9?” She shook her head, her big black eyes peering into them. “No…but we have something just as good…just as dark…” =©= 'You are trying our patience, old man.' Cyclonica didn’t care if he was the President or not…she called it like it is. If PI and UAE were nothing more than flat-scan weaklings, who are you to say she’s wrong? 'Why are we here??' Bush’s beady eyes widened a bit at her question, still coming out squinty. 'You’re all here to help me find Osama Bin Laden. Understand now?' The other three guys all let out an 'Oh' of clarification, but the Celestials weren’t too impressed. Akiyama let out a low growl as he crouched in the corner and cracked his joints. 'We have nothing to do with this.' Cyclonica announced. 'You two are national heroes for disbanding the United Allah Enthusiasts on national television! You two will surely help!' Cyclonica glared harder, wishing that she had heat vision at this point. 'We did that because they were in our way…not because we like America. You’re wasting our time. We have more pressing matters to deal with.' 'Like bringing about Universal Harmony?' Bush asked eagerly. She nodded slowly, her brow furrowed. 'Yes…like bringing about Universal Harmony' 'I’m all for that!' He replied with a smile. The others agreed with him, giving their nod or quip. “CALL ME NOW!' Ms. Cleo yelled out suddenly before stuffing her big mouth with a slice of pizza. Cyclonica cut her eyes at them all. “Um…I don’t think you all understand OUR concept of Universal Harmony. You see…' 'I almost forgot!!' Bush exclaimed, cutting off the Divine Prophetess in mid speech. She sighed and crouched down next to the god as Bush ran towards a door off to the side. 'Fine…they don’t want to know about our concept of Universal Harmony…they’ll regret it when it falls upon them.' Bush walks back into the room with a man on his arm dressed in a pair of blue jeans, sneakers, a T-shirt that reads 'USA ALL THE WAY' over a picture of the American Flag. The man had a thick and knotty beard, and a red, white, and blue turban on his head. 'Friends, this is---' Cyclonica jumped up and glared at them. 'Isn’t THAT Osama Bin Laden?' Everyone murmured in confusion. Cleo shoved another slice into her mouth and chuckled. 'I AM MISS CLEO!' she yelled out in realization of the man’s identity. Bush shook his head as the Arab man looked cautiously around. 'No! No! This is NOT Osama Bin Laden. This is one of the Arabs that have switched sides to make right the wrong that his people have done! People…meet Nedal Nib Amaso!' Noir, Lockhart, and Cleo all nodded their heads in agreement. 'CALL ME NOW!' she squawked at Cyclonica, looking at her like she was stupid. Cyclonica bore optical daggers into her. 'You…Violator…gimmick infringement…Enough said.' Her head lowered, a sad tone going to her voice. 'I am Miss Cleo…' she sniffed, keeping back tears. 'What is wrong with this species where they cannot even bother to realize a ploy before their eyes?' Cyclonica sighed. Nedal looked at Cyclonica with a nervous glance. 'I know nothing of what you talking about, young woman. I merely Afghanistan refugee here in USA to aid President in finding evil Osama Bin Laden! Yes, yes, yes!!' A fold of the Divine Prophetess’s arms told him that she wasn’t buying it. He was getting desperate. 'You not believe? I show! I show!' he started flexing around, humming the beat to “Real American”. 'I-AM-REAL-AMERICAN! FIGHT-FOR-RIGHT-AMERICAN! I-AM-REAL-AMERICAN! FIGHT-FOR-RIGHT! FIGHT-FOR-RIGHT!' He started cupping his hand at his ear, waving towards the other three people. Cleo was gyrating to the humming beat. 'I-AM-MISS-CLEO! CALL-ME-NOW! CALL-ME-NOW!' 'SILENCE!' Cyclonica snapped, shutting them all up at once. Nedal was in the midst of ripping his shirt, but stopped and slumped over against the wall, grabbing a nearby can of soda. Noir sighed as he straightened out his suit jacket. 'I tell you, that dame was one loony bird. If there was a cage to cage up this wacky canary, it would have to be made out of padded bars. Yeah, she was a real nutcase, walnut style. But it was my job to deal with the crazies and the Krayzies and the Krazies…because I’m Guy Noir…Private Eye.' Lockhart scoffed at her snootily. 'I can smell Osama Bin Laden nine blocks away, and I don’t smell him here at all!' Noir looked at him. 'What’s he smell like?' 'Sweat, feet, and spoiled falafel. It’s not a pleasant smell.' Cleo nodded in agreement. 'Call me now!' Just then, a folded steel chair flew out from the back of the room, striking Bush straight in the face! “EAGLE’S DOWN!!!” Secret Service swarmed into the room as he collapses, diving on top of him to protect him from more projectile furniture. Looking back, we find the arm of the god stretched out, as if he had just recently thrown something. Cyclonica folded her arms across her chest, a satisfied look on her face. “Good…Leader of the Free World is dead, now we can move onto Universal Harmony.” But to her dismay, Bush stood back to his feet with the help of the Secret Service. He dusted himself off and nodded to his men. “I’m all right!” One of the men looked at the chair, and noticed a metal dart sticking out of the steel back. Bush examined it carefully, his squinty eyes closing more and more. “Hmm…poisonous dart…” Cyclonica and Akiyama immediately turned towards “Nedal Nib Amaso”. He was sitting there with his straw pointed up and towards Bush’s general area. He looked back at the Celestials and slipped the straw back in his drink in a panic. She snapped her fingers, and the nVg rose to his feet menacingly. “Nedal” shrieked before dashing out of the room. “Akiyama!” Cyclonica gave the word, and the god was off. He disappeared out of the room. Roars erupted…screams rang out…lots of bodies hitting the floor…just like it would be on Turmoil… And just like Turmoil…the god walked out dusting his hands off from a job well done. Noir’s mouth dropped open. “It was like nothing I’ve ever seen in my life…that monster standing there before me. He was like death personified, with darkness oozing out of him like crème oozed out of a glazed chocolate éclair. He may have very well been a god…if not the only god I would ever look upon. I feel bad for the sons of b!tches that cross his path…or my name isn’t Guy Noir…” He snuck in a bite of pizza, chewed quickly, and swallowed. “…Private Eye.” Cyclonica pushed past him suddenly. “Silence, dick. I think this problem is solved…and we have more pressing matters to attend to. If you will excuse us…” Bush watched as they walked for the exit of the room. “Where are you going?” She snapped her eyes back at him. “We have a meeting in Oxford…with a few gerrymandering bastards and a few Allah wimps. Farewell…” They walked out, leaving the room in silence. Ms. Cleo shook her head as she closed the lid on her pizza box. “Call me…now.” She said solemnly. Just then, Cyclonica walked back into the room and backhanded the portly Jamaican. She bounced to the floor and held her cheek, looking up at the Divine Prophetess with a hurt expression. “So says the Divine Prophetess...you faking b!tch.” And out she goes again…leaving the room in more turmoil…play in words…Bush lowered his head, disappointment sinking in like a fat politician’s filthy teeth sinks into the plush folds of the American economy. Lockhart sighed as well as he picked up another box marked as ham and pineapple pizza. “There’s a Hawaiian pig in here…” he sniffed out. Bush turned to his agents shortly, a new look of determination. “Don’t just stand there…go help Mr. Nedal Nib Amaso to his feet! Make sure he’s all right! We have work to do!” They jumped at his word, rushing to the next room to help out “his Arab associate”. Bush straightened the lapels on his suit and smiled. “It’s good to be the Prez….ah, yes.” … Arabs and Politicians… …Collective Retards… … Collectively doomed to failure at the hands of the Neo Void God… =©=
