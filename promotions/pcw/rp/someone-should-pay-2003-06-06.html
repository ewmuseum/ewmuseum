Someone should pay..

6 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/06/03 Uncomfortably switching sides on his cramped and rented motel bed came the misogyny manipulator tux tailored, as always and the blade to the side draw along with his Louisiana itinerary. Beside both objects came the hampering sound from a digital alarm clock, reading the time of '7:00 AM', precisely being the beginnings of Stone's morning. Wincing from the sound, Stone finally slid his hand over towards the far side of the drawer and floored it with the sweep of his hand. 'Damn..' he whispered, wiping the remaining sleep over his eyes, picking out the crusts pestering him, and holding his body up in to a sitting position abed. He pulled the itinerary over to him, riffling through it for a few moments and placing it back down whining inaudibly. The room, scant of light drove Stone over towards the window as he jerked the drapes aside, giving off the wee light coming from the blue sky even though the intermittent sun hadn't been shining at this time. Still it gave off enough light for view. He seemed to be prematurely leaving for Fusion as it just was many hours away and giving him enough time to wriggle in to lively activity. Pulling his already loaded duffel bag, and shouldering it he turned the knob in to his motel room and stepped out, only to retreat back in shock, and his jaw literally dropping in surprise. Before him was the girl he' thought had been dead. He was so thunderstruck he just left his mouth open as he saw.. His daughter... Emily Burns. Emily didn't seem to be the dainty and thin, blonde-streaked girl.. She seemingly transformed in to a hideous, adipose, black colored woman. But Stone did still recognize her from the style of her hair. Emily didn't seem to be mad nor' happy, just straight in expression but as she roared out these words, her hair swayed to the side, and her face turned completely crimson. 'YOU TRIED TO KILL ME!' Stone drew back in to his apartment pad in effort to close the door but to no avail as she had way more power from her obesity. Shoving Stone to the floor she came in and slammed the door shut, bolting it with the double locks. Scrambling to grab his blade, Stone was thrown back to the floor by Emily, approaching the blade herself. Emily grabbed it from the drawer, and petted the glossy object coming towards Stone, crawling in a frontward position, backward. But ceasing his movement was the incoming wall, ending his attempted escape from mutilation. Her stalwart body seized Stone, in one hand, while holding the blade in the other. Bringing back the blade in a final wind, she brought it down between Stone's groin, sticking to the floor giving Stone the opportunity to attack but instead he kept panicking, furthering his unsteady maneuvering and also bringing Emily more time to recapture the blade and Stone himself. He booted Emily square in the chin, staggering her back, as he pulled the blade out of the floor only to be re-approached by the charging, gluttonous figure, looking very unusual while dashing onward. Stone stabbed at her arm, as she cringed in pain, only giving Stone another shot to her organs, this time sinking the blade straight in to her neck. Blood instantly poured out of her mouth, as she frantically flailed her arms, and then falling rattling and rumbling the entire motel room. Stone devilishly grinned and chuckled, then dabbing at his eye, kneeling down and tearing the blade ruthlessly out of her neck. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Jumping up from his dream, Stone sat, like before abed, his blade to the side of him, not imbrued by the blood coming from the gluttony behemoth of his dream daughter and it was seemingly all a comedic joke. The alarm continuously rang, but instead of entitling '7:00 AM' instead it was '2:00 PM'.. His laugh turned in to a frantic dash up from his bed as he'd been certainly late for his flight to Baton Rouge, Louisiana, and out from the sheets. He shouldered his duffel bag, and ran out the door after grabbing his Baton Rouge itinerary and pocketing his blade.. Then lastly saying.. 'Imagine a fat daughter.. Ughh.. Well, someone should pay for what I dreamed for. (Matt Stryker, maybe?)'
