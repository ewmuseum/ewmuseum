Superiority and Greatness Festival (my best in quite some time)

27 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 27 For two weeks, New Breed has been disgracing the PCW Championship at every opportunity. They’ve passed it around, made fun of it, Snow has even used it to clean coffee spills and steady a television stand. ...But this is Jason Snow... the Killin’ Villain... the man who fought like his life was on the line for nearly a year to capture the PCW Championship? Has it really lost all meaning to him? Is it really just another tool to damage his enemies with? If so, it’s working well. But not only have New Breed gained the hatred of PCW’s favorite hero, Big Alan Steele, they’ve also gained resentment from the rest of PCW. But what does Snow really think of the PCW Championship? We know that at one time he valued it more than any other prize in wrestling... The scene opens up to a shot of the PCW Championship, resting on a mantle in yet another of Jason Snow’s luxurious hotels - that’s where it should be. As the shot widens, we catch a glimpse of the Killin’ Villain himself staring at the championship across the room. Snow looks so much better now than when he took time off to recover from knee surgery two months ago. His face looks fresh - maybe it’s the fact that he’s back together with his wife... Snow: “...It’s been a long ride...” And it has been now. Snow has been in some part of the PCW family for nearly a year now. He’s accomplished things that most legends can only dream of... but still, he has that fire... that desire to be the very best... so why did he take such a cheap way to achieve further success. ... ... The Killin’ Villain leans his head back on the sofa, never taking his eyes off the championship. What does it mean to him? We don’t know... Maybe we never will... Suddenly, the phone rings, breaking a silence which had crept in... Snow: “By Greatness!? Who in the hinges of hell has the nerve to disturb me!?” The phone rings again. Snow glares at it for a moment before reaching over and picking it up - it is sitting next to the sofa on small glass table. A stern look comes over his face as he answers... Snow: “Who in the hell is this and how did you get this number?” There’s a moment of silence... Snow: “REALLY!?!?” Snow’s eyes show excitement for what his hearing and he leaps up off the sofa... Snow: “There’s a Superiority and Greatness festival right next door!!?!?” Snow waits for a response and then happily fires... Snow: “I’ll be right there!” Snow slams the phone down and hurries to the window. After looking out for a moment, he turns to the camera... Snow: “There it is! By Greatness, it is true!” When we get a look out the window, we spot a sign over a building across the street. The sign has been constructed out of paper and in clumsy, bold black letters it reads: Superiority and Greatness Festival... Snow: “What luck! By Greatness, my Superiority must have been working overtime when I chose this hotel!” Quickly, Snow checks over himself to make sure he’s ready to go... Snow: “...Superior leather pants of Greatness...?” Snow looks down at his pants, which are of course black leather and gleam under the bright light of his hotel room... Snow: “Check!” Next item on the list... Snow: “...Hair tied perfectly back into a pony tail to show off my particularly Superior side...?” Snow reaches up and lightly pats his hair, turning slightly to the left and giving a pout to the camera... When he’s finished, his face becomes all business again... Snow: “Check.!” Next item on the list... Snow: “...Genuine Snake Skin boots that have occasionally had to be surgically removed from peasant’s asses...?” Snow glances down to his feet - indeed, he’s wearing his Snake Skin boots. Then again, it’s been nearly a year since we’ve seen him without them... Snow: “Check!” Next item on the list... Snow: “Adorable face that the mere SIGHT of has been KNOWN to cause ORGASMS (!!) ...?” Snow reaches up and pats over his face... Snow: “Check!” Quickly, Snow rushes toward the door, his pony tail flapping in the wind he’s creating. Quickly, our PCW camera man follows him. Snow hops down the stairs to till he reaches the bottom floor, sprints out the door and heads across the street - paying little attention to the honking traffic. When he gets to the building where the “Greatness and Superiority Festival” is being held, he stops to gaze at the sign... Snow: “Finally! A gathering of truly Superior Individuals! And I, of course, will be seen as the most Superior of them all. I am the Killin’ Villain, after all - and I’ve yet to see another quite so Superior as I.” Just then, Snow looks at the doors... glass doors... glass doors that are the only thing between the MARRIED PCW Superstar and what seems like a numberless pack of screaming female fans... Snow: “They don’t... look Superior...” Just then, their true intentions are revealed. The women nearly break down the doors as they pour out of the building toward their prey - Jason Snow. Snow’s eyes flash wide with fear and he immediately runs the other way - he is married, after all. Snow sprints into the street when... Snow: “OH MY GREATNESS!” ’95 Pontiac Sunbird... Anonymous Voice: “Oh God! That guy just got hit by a car!” Indeed, the statement is true. PCW’s World Champion is lying on the pavement clutching his leg... All of the stampeding women stop in their tracks and watch with horror. The driver of the sunbird rushes out of his car to see what he can do to help... he is frantic... Driver: “Oh my God! I swear, I didn’t see him! He just came out in front of me!” Snow: “Damn you, peasant!” Snow cusses at him from the pavement. The driver, a tall dark haired man in his mid-twenties, is horrified. Snow scowls... Snow: “Have you no sense, peasant!? Didn’t anyone ever to tell you to watch out for Superior Individuals while your racing along in your inferior automobile!” Driver: “Look man, I’m real sorry. Money? Do you want some money?” Snow scowls... Snow: “I don’t need your pennies, plebe!” Driver: “What can I do, man!? I’ll do anything! Anything!” Suddenly, Snow turns pale and he looks up at the young driver in horror... Snow: “You’re not one of the homosexuals, are you!?” The young man gives Snow an odd look... Driver: “N-no...” The color returns to Snow’s face and he looks down at his body - everything seems to be ok... Still, Snow’s going to milk this for all it’s worth... Driver: “A-are you ok, man?” Snow slams the palm of his right hand down onto the pavement... Snow: “Am I ok!? Of COURSE I’m ok! I’m the Killin’ Villain, damn it! It would take a lot more than a mere brush with death at the hands of a peasant like YOU to hold me down! By Greatness, I... er... I...” Snow’s voice trails off as an idea strikes him... Snow: “Er... I mean... OOOOOHHHH!! The pain of it all!” Snow clutches his chest and lies down flat on the pavement. We have no idea what he’s doing, but he seems to be intent on having us think he’s been injured... Snow: “Damn it all! Damn... it all... to HELLLLLLL...!!!” Most people who have stopped to look are now watching in disbelief as Snow fakes an injury. The women don’t know whether to comfort the fallen PCW Champion or to keep their distance. For the moment, they don’t approach... Snow: “Have you no notion who I am, peasant!?” The driver looks at Snow for a moment... Driver: “Well... you do look familiar...” Snow: “I’m the Killin’ Villain, damn it! And now, because of you, I’ll won’t be able to defend my PCW Championship against Big Alan Steele this week! It’s all your fault, peasant! YOUR fault!” Driver: “I had no idea, man, I mean...” Snow interrupts... Snow: “Peasants never do! Now I demand you call me an ambulance! The pain that’s rushing through my Superior body is damn near overwhelming!” Something in a woman’s nature will always prompt her to comfort. A mob of women aren’t much different, only they’re driven by lust and are star struck at the moment. One woman speaks up... Woman: “Lets comfort him!” To Snow’s horror, the mob of star struck women rush up to help the fallen PCW Champion. Snow, realizing that if his wife were to turn on the television in time to see him being fondled by several lust-filled young ladies she would no doubt leave him for a third time, quickly jumps to his feet and stops them with an outstretched hand... Snow: “Hold it right there, wenches!” The mob stops, surprised at Snow’s “miraculous recovery”... Snow: “I’ll have no part in your sexual deviance! I know that you all want and crave me, but I’m a happily married man, damn it! I can no longer randomly hand out the thrill of a lifetime to whatever young wench I please!” The women all begin exchanging glances. The driver is relieved that Snow is standing up and looking healthy... Snow: “And another thing!” Everyone who’s watched Snow since he began wrestling back in 1998 knows what’s coming next... Snow: “I’ll have you know that you CAN NOT stare at me with those large, lust-filled, eyes...” Here it comes... Snow: “Because the mere SIGHT of me has been KNOWN to cause ORGASMS!!!” None of the women break their gaze. One speaks up... Woman: “Well, I’ve got no problem with that.” Snow’s eyes grow large at her response and he scowls, stomping his right foot down hard on the pavement... Snow: “You think it’s a joke!? A joke!? How would you like to wake up tomorrow morning in the hospital, reading a newspaper headline that reads: Multiple Orgasms Casualties Found Foaming at the Mouth With Their Skirts on Fire!” Some of the women giggle at the thought of this. To Snow however, it’s no joke... Snow: “Oh! You laugh now! Suuuuure... But I, the Killin’ Villain, have seen it happen before! Why, by Greatness, there was the flooding of Indiana back in ‘99! The tragic explosion of a coffee house waitress just a few months back! And don’t even MAKE me bring up the incident at Madison Square Garden last year! By Greatness, I’m STILL paying for the damaged seats!” The driver doesn’t see much point in any of this. He quickly steps toward his car and opens up the door. Snow, who’s still glaring at the mob of young women, doesn’t notice until the Driver waves and speaks... Driver: “Well, if you’re ok, I’ll just be on my way. Sorry about... you know... hitting you with my car, and all...” Snow: “Wait! You can’t leave! I’m an injured Superior Individual here!” The driver waves again, as if he didn’t hear the Killin’ Villain’s words, and drives off. Snow scowls... Snow: “Peasants have no respect these days!” Seeing that the women are ready to make their move again, Snow quickly rushes over to a Taxi that’s parked on the other side of the street. He jumps into the back and tosses some money at the cabbie... Snow: “DRIVE, PEASANT, DRIVE!” The women chase after him, but it’s no use. Snow is long gone in a speeding Taxi. Frustrated, the women head back to their building. One woman notices the camera man... Woman: “Hey! The camera guy’s still here! Maybe we can have some fun with him!” We can tell by the way our view shakes that the camera man has been excited by the words... Another woman responds... Woman 2: “The Camera Guy? What are you? Some kind of slut?” And with that, all the camera man’s dreams are shattered. The women file back into their building leaving our camera man alone on the sidewalk... silence... the scene fades to black...
