Hunted Saga Part Three: Revelation...

25 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/3/02 The camera opens up inside Hank Sun’s gym at the heart of Chicago, Illinois, the camera pans around from a height, looking down upon all of the people working out on the various exercise machines, before turning and entering the office of one Hank Sun. Hank his sat behind his desk busy looking through his mail, as John Crichton and Aeryn Sun sit in, two rather old looking chairs, opposite Hank. Hank simply looks up from his mail, as soon as they sit down, and removes his reading glasses, which were balanced precariously upon his nose. He folds them up and puts them in his breast pocket. In Crichton’s whole three weeks at Hankn Sun’s gym, he had only been in Hank’s office the once. At the start when Aeryn persuaded her father to let Crichton compete in his independent federation. He hadn’t taken Crichton long to learn the majority of the moves, since he had a background in hand to hand combat, which he hadn’t divulged with anyone yet, not even Aeryn. Him and Aeryn had got ‘close’ over the three weeks and Crichton actually believed that she was the one, for him at least. “So Hank, what did you want to talk to me about?” Crichton questioned rather impatiently. Hank nervously taps the wooden desk before speaking up, “Crichton I have some very good news.” Hank replied. Crichton was taken back by this, he really didn’t like the sound of this. He was very wary about this and his instincts told him something bad was coming along. “Really? What might that be?” Crichton said warily. “Crichton, Primetime Championship Wrestling have contacted me and have shown their interest in you. They would like you to be a part of their company.” Hank stated as straight forward as he could. Crichton felt himself be physically winded by this revelation. The PCW wanted him? He did think that he was that good, but they obviously did. “Oh hell. That is good news.” Crichton replied still in shock. Then the realisation of what this meant to him hit, and hit hard. The PCW attracted coverage from a worldwide audience, millions upon millions of people would be watching. If he went to the PCW, he would undoubtedly be on national TV, the army would recognise him and they would come and get him. In way this was the best thing that had ever happened to him, but if he went through with it, it could also be the worse thing that ever happened to him. “But I may have to turn the offer down.” Crichton added with a disappointed look upon his face. Crichton noticed the smiles of both Hank and Aeryn Sun, slowly evaporated away at his latest comment, as he rose from his seat and headed toward the office door. Aeryn rose from her seat too and follows Crichton as he walks down the steps to the ground level of the gym. The camera follows them both as they wade through all of the sweating bodies, leading ultimately to the locker room. Crichton walks up to his locker, removes his key from his pocket and unlocks the thing. He then slumps down upon then pine bench about a meter away from the locker. He places his head in his hands and rests there a while, dwelling on the news he had only just received. Crichton raises his head from his hands once more as a beefy looking man, Crichton knew as Buzz Cagney and the independent wrestling scene knew as Goliath walked past and stopped before his very own locker. He wasn’t named Goliath for nothing, as he was twice the side of Crichton. “Hey John, I heard ‘bout your offer from PCW. Personally I don’t think you deserve it…” Buzz began. “Really Buzz, that doesn’t surprise me at all. Crichton interrupted. “…I should have been drafted. I’ve put all that I can into this company for Hank and the PCW choose some guy who’s still training to be a wrestling superstar. I can’t understand it. I bet Hank pointed them toward you, why won’t he point them toward me. And what does a nice girl like Aeryn see in you any way?” Buzz ranted Crichton sighed. Buzz had been giving Crichton a hard time for a while now, mainly because both Hank and Aeryn had both taken to him. He couldn’t help it, it was beyond his control and lately he’d been getting quite pissed off with him of late. “Don’t worry Buzz I’m planning on turning it down.” Crichton simply stated. Buzz walked over to him and grabbed him the collar, forcing Crichton to his feet. “You think your so good don’t you? Your so cocky your gonna turn down a contract from the greatest wrestling federation this planet has to offer. Your just staying here to piss me off!” Buzz shouted Crichton swiftly breaks the hold Buzz has on him and pushes him away. Buzz stumbled backwards and slammed hard into the locker. “Buzz get it through your narcissistic skull that everything is always ‘bout you!” Crichton exclaimed with fury in his voice. Buzz simply composes himself as a decoy then instantly throws a punch, Crichton moves his head left of the blow and Buzz’s hand impacts upon the metal locker. Pain was etched in Buzz’s face, as Crichton knees him in the stomach and throws him head first into the lockers once more. The imprint left behind in the metal locker almost frightened Crichton, as Buzz slowly gets back to his feet and exits the locker room, with blood pouring down from his brow. Crichton simply sighs once more and sits back down upon the pine bench and gets a far away look in his eyes as his mind wanders to the subject on the PCW. Crichton’s concentration is broken once again, as the locker room door opens and Aeryn Sun cautiously enters the locker room and sits beside Crichton upon the bench. “Buzz as just come out of here saying that you just attacked him.” Aeryn simply said, to break the ice. “Yeah he was being a pain in the ass again. That guy has a complex I’m sure of it.” Crichton replied attempting to put Aeryn at ease. “So why aren’t you gonna go to PCW? It’s what you’ve been waiting for, for quite a while.” Aeryn probed. “I can’t tell you Aeryn, I just can’t.” Crichton replied with a firm tone in his voice. “Why Crichton? Why can’t you tell me?” Aeryn pleaded. “Because it would change everything between us, it would put our relationship at great risk… And if I appeared on PCW Tv the likely hood is, you would never see me again.” Crichton answered reluctantly. “Crichton nothing and I mean nothing could change the way I feel about you.” Aeryn stated with a reassuring smile upon her face. “Maybe, but it would change things between us, it is that big.” Crichton said. A worried look etched itself upon Aeryn’s beautiful brow. “You have said to much now Crichton. You had better tell me whatever, it is your hesitating upon.” Aeryn firmly commanded. Crichton’s face instantly changed from being worried to an ear to ear smile. “I love it when you command, but your right I need to tell you the bare bones of why I can’t go to PCW. Three weeks ago, just a couple of days before I met you and your father… I escaped from the army.” Crichton revealed, pausing to catch Aeryn’s reaction. Aeryn’s face didn’t know what to do, first it was bemused, then pleased and finally concerned. “Crichton what are you talking about?…” Aeryn began, only then realising she knew next to nothing about Crichton’s past. Crichton didn’t like the look of Aeryn’s face after his first revelation. “Aeryn, please let me finish. I joined the army when I was seventeen. At twenty-five I was assigned to the Special Forces section of the army. Then last month the white coat scientists decided that I was the perfect subject for a little experiment they had conducted. Apparently my they predicted that their experiment had the best chance of success using my D.N.A, Aeryn the experiment was to create a superhuman soldier that would revolutionise the armed forces. I knew as soon as they mentioned it to me, that this experiment, would be used for nothing more than carnage. If the Russians found out about the experiment it would lead to nothing less than war and I could not let that happen. I had to escape and I did. So that’s about it, I am AWOL from the army and if they saw me on the TV, they would come and they would get me. I cannot allow that Aeryn, at all costs I must avoid this.” Aeryn remains silent for a while, in a vain attempt to absorb all of the data Crichton had just revealed to her. She wasn’t angry, yet she wasn’t happy, she couldn’t decide how she felt about this. There was simply too much for her to take in. However, she now saw why Crichton thought that this would come between them. How could they expect to lead a normal life together if Crichton was always on the run? But she also thought about how Crichton must have been feeling about this. Knowing that one day, he didn’t know when or where, they would come for him and she knew that not even Crichton could from something this big forever. “John, you realise that the army will catch up with you one day don’t you?” Aeryn stated rather too bluntly for her liking. Crichton looked up at her. She was stepping on territory now, in which Crichton definitely did not want to go. “I realise that I suppose, but I try not to think about it.” Crichton replied almost as bluntly. “Then why did you trick me into loving you!” Aeryn exclaimed, with tears rolling down her cheeks. Crichton was taken back by her outburst. She loved him? He didn’t realise that much and he didn’t trick her into loving him. “Aeryn honey, I didn’t trick you into anything, I just fell for you the moment I saw you, and I couldn’t help myself.” Crichton replied moving closer and putting his arm around her shoulders. Aeryn was bedazzled by this, she thought that Crichton was using her to help himself stay undetected from the army, but Crichton was here telling her that he was in love with her too. “Really?” Aeryn questioned. “Hell yeah. Aeryn I will understand if you want me as far away from your father and from you as humanly possible if you so desire. The last thing I want to do is place you in trouble.” Crichton commented painfully. Aeryn cleared the tears away from her eyes and composed herself a little. “Now why in the hell would I want you to do that? Your all mine now John Crichton, whether you like it or not.” Aeryn replied with confidence. Crichton eyed her up strangely for a second then blurted out, “You plannin’ oln making me your bitch?” Aeryn giggled a little, but could not say that this thought hadn’t entered her mind before now. “No John, I’m saying that we’re in this together now, you’re no longer alone.” Aeryn stated. Crichton looked down onto the locker room floor once more. “Aeryn I can’t let…” Crichton began, before Aeryn launched herself at his lips. Aeryn reluctantly broke off from the kiss and added, “No Crichton, you don’t understand WE ARE TOGETHER ON THIS, no matter what you think.” said Aeryn as boldly as possible. “I can’t risk it! You’re the only think that I care about, in this world at least.” Crichton replied, knowing that this would have no effect on Aeryn. “Crichton you cannot protect me from this any longer. I suppose I should be mad that you’ve never told me about this before, but I guess I will have to understand. One thing you pointed out that does make sense though is, we have to get away from here. I don’t want my father to be involved in any of this.” Aeryn demanded. “At last something we both agree on!” Crichton remarked. “The bottom line is though Crichton, we would be harder targets if we didn’t stay in the same place all the time. This is where being on the road comes in handy.” Aeryn stated. “Yeah, but the people from the army would recognise me no trouble.” Crichton commented. “Not if you cut your hair shorter and lose your moustache! Anyway, how many army officials that know ‘bout you and this experiment watch wrestling anyhow? But your name is a problem, you’ll have to change it…” said Aeryn deep in thought. “I already did change it.” Crichton replied Aeryn turned around with a bemused look on her face. “What?” Aeryn simply asked. “Yeah, my name was David J. Constantinople.” Crichton commented. Aeryn began to laugh. “I think I prefer, John Crichton don’t you?” Aeryn replied before lunging at Crichton for another kiss. The camera pans to the left like in all infuriating softcore porn films to the locker room door. The door is ajar and we can see Buzz’s sticking through as the camera fades to black.
