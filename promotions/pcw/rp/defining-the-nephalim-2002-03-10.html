Defining the Nephalim

10 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/10/02 Well, let me warn you that this is probably going to suck. With that, let's get to it. Here is The End, giving his thoughts IC about exactly what he is. So, I'm a Nephalim? Sylo's convinced of it. But what exactly is a Nephalim? I've done much research on this. Apparently, it's quite an odd creature. The story of the Nephalim starts with the Grigori. The Grigori, also known as The Watchers, are the mysterious eighth order of heavenly angels. The gentle Grigori were created by God to be Earthly shepherds of the first humans. The Grigori were both physically and spiritually gigantic, at least by the standards of the people who later wrote about them. They served early humanity as vast reservoirs of information concerning the finer points of civilization, and their selflessness was beyond compare. They were also called the Watchers, as it was their job to observe humanity, lending a helping hand when necessary but not interfering in the course of human development. Grigori were what we would conceptualize to be Superman. In every way, they were better than humans. And yet, their position made them subservient to humans, God's mortal creations being his most loved creation out of everything he made. As anyone could guess, angels -- though filled with God's divine grace -- are eminently corruptible in the corporeal world and, perhaps more importantly, when in the presence of humans. The Grigori, designed as the type of angels farthest from divine, proved to be so much more corruptible that it wasn't long before their enormous zest for life tripped them up, spiritually speaking. They took husbands and wives among the humans and led greatly debauched lives, neglecting their heavenly duties. These creatures, better than humans in every way, soon became no better than humans in the two most important aspects: intelligence and spirituality. Legends tell us that the children the Grigori begot were hideous, misshapen monsters, now known in demonic circles as the Nephalim, and that a group of angels were quickly dispatched to destroy the half-breeds, after which the Grigori were damned to Earth for the remainder of their long lives. This is mostly true. The Grigori, both for the sin of lust and for neglecting their duties, were banned from Heavenly society, doomed to spend the remainder of their days roaming the Earth. The Seraphim Council believes that even speaking the name of the Grigori disturbs the Symphony -- they refer to them as the Watchers, since that was their original duty. A quick word about The Symphony: The Symphony is everything. The lives we live and the reality we feel are just hints of its ultimate complexity. The pattern of the Symphony is inescapable, written in every blade of grass and every star in the sky. It holds all knowable secrets, from the certainty of the present to the promise of tomorrow. In writing the Symphony, God created the heavens and the earth. And as long as God was making a universe, He took advantage of the opportunity to create Himself as well. What few angels know, even today, is that besides their more famous gigantic spawn, the malevolent Nephalim, the Grigori did successfully engender half-breed children, part-angel and part-human, that we shall not go into here. Angels are strictly forbidden to associate with the Children of the Grigori or, God forbid, any actual remaining Grigori they might run across in the course of a mission. Now, leaving the story of the Grigori open, to be finished later, we now shift to the story of the Nephalim, the Whisperers.The Nephalim are the twisted offspring that are often created from the union of a human and a Grigori. Angels hunted the majority of the Nephalim shortly after the Judgment of the Grigori, but rumors persist of such monsters that can be found in some of the darkest recesses of the Earth. Some say these obscenities can even be found in the badlands of Hell, but if so, no one has seen them in recent memory.What exactly would this monster be? I’ve heard stories of it, and read many accounts of the creatures. Note that there are two planes of existence in this known universe: corporeal and ethereal (heavenly). Each angel, or angel/human hybrid, has an appearance wavelength in the corporeal and ethereal realms. In the corporeal realm, it’s standard practice to assume human form. Ethereal forms vary, however; for instance, Seraphims are winged multi-eyed ophidians in the ethereal realm, whereas Ofanim are wheels of fire. Kyriotates are multi-colored clouds of random limbs, mouths, and eyes that each pulse to no particular beat.And so, each angel, and angel offspring, has two forms.An original Nephalite’s appearance remains the same regardless of the plane of existence, though no two Nephalim are exactly alike. Some Nephalim appear to be normal to a cursory examination, although their eyes often display a greenish luminescence, or are completely black. A more detailed examination shows their skin to have an almost rubbery texture to it, with no bone structure at all. Tentacle-like growths have been seen on some of them, as well as an ability to stretch, twist and elongate their bodies in a decidedly unnatural fashion. In the more typical of the breed, the skin turns a glistening black color, while the arms seem to atrophy and lose cohesiveness, becoming ropelike tentacles.Perhaps you’ve seen contortionists? They are more of a more mixed breed of Nephalim, having little celestial blood. True Nephalims would be closer to giant squids, although even that would be a trivialization of its true form. Nephalims are… think of Shuma Gorath from Marvel v. Capcom. Then you’d be getting somewhere to conceptualizing its true form.Applying this to myself: I’ve always been tall and extremely flexible. I’ve never had any broken bones, and apparently, (according to the information that Sylo dug up), I’ve died several times in the past. However, for a Nephalim, or any angel or Child of the Grigori, to be killed in its corporeal form, the heart must be removed.The Nephalim are not strictly Celestials, and so do not have a Resonance or Dissonance Condition per se, however there are certain curious effects that have been noted.According to accounts and research of captured nephalites, quantifying the average Nephalim is a difficult task due to the way that the Nephalim that have been captured seem to have a very different type of mind than most Corporeals or Celestials. They do not appear to speak any languages, even though it's often clear that they understand what is being said. One such as myself apparently can speak normally, though at times I seem to just frighten people. Their behavior patterns swing from being completely unresponsive to outside stimuli to watching and observing their captors for hours on end. The public consensus is that they are dangerous animals that should be put down.Should I be a nephalim, I consider myself not an animal, but a human being. Nothing more, nothing less.Apparently, the reason that Archangels continue to lead people to believe this hostile view is that listening to a Nephalite's jabbering and mumbling has an effect on the minds of the listeners. Placed in a room with several different celestials, all of them heard the Nephalite speaking to them individually about different subjects, all of them extremely personal and deeply upsetting to the listeners.It appears that the whispering of the Nephalim causes the listener to hear the Nephalite speaking about his deepest fears or concerns. It's not believed that the Nephalite actually knows what that fear is, but once a subject starts speaking to them, they will start carrying on an intelligent conversation, complete with philosophical details, secrets, etc. In truth, the Nephalite is still simply whispering its nonsense, but the listener will find it almost impossible to believe that.A Seraph applying its Resonance (its very being and way of existing; in this case, Seraphim search for truth and do their best to uphold it without revealing anything unnecessary) to a Nephalite will find some disturbing information - their Resonance will read the entire conversation as a lie, even though the Seraph knows that certain parts of what the Nephalite said were true. In actuality, since the Nephalite is not actually saying anything cohesive, the fact that the conversation is taking place is a lie, and all the supposed 'Truths' are coming from the Seraph's own mind. In addition, a Seraph who acquires the 'higher truth' from his Resonance will find that the Nephalite is trying to drive him insane - any further inquiries are inconclusive.Prolonged exposure to a Nephalite's whispering will slowly start to cause slips of disturbance to the listener. Although the damage is slight, it builds over time until the person reaches their breaking point and starts to lose his mind. The insanity will only be noticeable when commented on by an outside party.This happened to me once, I believe. I was just talking normally to a priest in a confessional booth about what sins I’d committed. During the conversation, he went crazy, and I had no choice but to kill him. An earlier conversation with my former employer, Cecil Cassidy of EW, netted somewhat the same effect; casual speech with him evoked fear in his eyes, and I sent him away. No one knows precisely how many Nephalim are left on the Corporeal plane, but strangely the number seems to be going up slowly. The few Grigori that have been found and asked about the matter seem as mystified at this latest turn of events as everyone else.Where have I found all this information? Akiyama has introduced me to the Celestial Halls of Knowledge, what knowledge I have access to derived of the Symphony itself. It appears Nephalites are even in the King James version of the Bible. However, it’s only a passing mention in Genesis 6:1-4 and Numbers 13:33. Even more, it’s spelled Nephilim, and is translated to mean “giants,” though true, original Hebrew would use a different word altogether. Now… I’m not really sure what I am anymore than when I believed that I was human. What am I? Some half-breed between a human and a Grigori? An octopus-monster with no eyes that drives people insane merely by conceptualizing a conversation with those people? I preferred it more when I was but a 6’8” cruiserweight with a possible handicap of being blind. One thing that I do know for sure is that my father… the one that was killed on July 4, 1981, is not my biological father. He died of head trauma, which is not a plausible means of death for any meta-human “celestial” creature, even the lowly Nephalites. Akiyama is a celestial… Jay Sylo is a Celestial (now anyway, though he had the blood in him from birth Akiyama tells me). Apparently, I am the spawn of a celestial being. This could explain my awkward body type, my agility and strength beyond my human capacities. Drake Maxwell… he claims to be a Nephalim as well, calling himself the “Fallen Angel.” However, Nephalim were never in a state of grace, and therefore cannot fall from grace. He may be a different creature, but I find it hard to believe that he is a nephalim. Maybe I’ll ask him next time I’m bashing his head in. Bobby Stormbringer… I don’t know why I’ve taken an affinity to bludgeoning the man. My head hurts… I need rest… at least my corporeal form does….
