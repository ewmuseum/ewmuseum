Never Looking Back...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Days after &#039;Televised Legends&#039; RP 'Their souls are lost Because they could never find What's this life for' ~Creed 'What's This Life For' It's fucking cold. We can feel it radiating from where our hand presses against the asphalt, seeping into the thin bones of our fingers so that the tips feel practically numb. We can feel it through the fabric of our pants, chilling our knee, freezing our toes... Hell, if there isn't a place on our body that doesn't sting despite the layer of clothes, we haven't found it yet. But we'll put up with this shit because it's separates us from the rest of those apathetic deities in the heavens. Sure, the first thing we said when we crawled out of bed this morning was that freezing our balls of on this night sounded like a whole lot of fun. Yeah, well, if certain vital parts start going numb, we're saying screw this. We give up a lot for this job, but some things are just sacred. Reaching into the pocket of our overcoat with stiff fingers, we pull out a stray match. Holding it firmly, we strike it on the ground, watching as the head catches and flares to life. Some days, we think this shithole of a city would be better off burning to the ground. But hey, it’s home, so we don't suppose we should say that. We mean, without the good ole PCW, we'd be out of a job. How fucking sad. We’d probably turn into a barhopping, womanizing drunken martial artist like our old man. Some days, that reality is all that stands between self-destruction and us. Pulling the match higher, we shield it with our hand to keep it alive. This is our last match, we're cold, and we want a cigarette. If this goes out, we're not going to be too happy. Not that we need to worry much. How much fresh air do you think the slums get? You're right. Not a whole hell of a lot. If you want to get away from the stale atmosphere that reeks of sweat, garbage, and sewage you need money. Since money is pretty damn hard to come by in this town, your chances aren't too great. You either need to be born with a silver spoon in your mouth, or do what we did. Become the darkness... and play by its rules… To do that, you'd have to be willing to slit the throat of your mother. Us, we've done worse. We beat the life out of the woman we used to love, dumped her body in the middle of our hotel room, stepped over her, and never looked back. Botan Otonachi… Heartless? She was a traitor. She was trying to kill us at the same time that she was trying to fuck us. So let us ask you, which do you think we was going to choose? Love, or life? We’ve seen what love is. Love is our washed out shadow of a mother who stayed with our father through the beatings, the booze, and the other women. Life is having no fucking boundaries, having no one stop you, and totally pummeling and banishing anyone who even considers doing so. we'd do whatever we had to keep that. Even kill those closest to us. It's a dog fucking eat dog world. You either bite first, or die. So we chose life over love. And if we've ever regretted it, if we've ever hated the scarred, pale face staring back at us in the mirror, well then, haven't we all? Don't we all have something we wish we could take back? Don't we all hate ourselves in some way? That's part of life. We don't care what they brochures say. It isn't beautiful, and it sure as hell isn't fair. We like where we are, so we’re going to do everything to make certain we stay here. Get in our way, and we'll pound you into the ground. The planet has screwed us over many times, and the only person we watch out for is ourselves. Even above our fellow Celestials. We’d forsake them all if it was in our best interests. So welcome to the world of Akira Akiyama, the Neo Void God. If you don't like what you see, that's too damn bad. All exits were sealed the minute you stepped in. We shake the match out, and inhale deeply, letting the nicotine work its way into our blood, wishing it was alcohol. A nice shot of Blood would warm us right up. But see, we only have one moral. We don't drink in the heat of battle. You can thank our dear old dad for that. We’re a murderer, a monster, and an all around bastard, but we’re not a worthless, drunken piece of shit. No, but like him, we have a taste for it. The difference between us is that we control it… it doesn't control us. We’re not going to live our life by the bottle. That's a pathetic existence. We live our life by death. And we somehow doubt that's much better. Narrowing our eyes against the smoke, we stare across the glowing tip of our cigarette and into the alleyway across the street. Nothing. The only activity we've seen all night is the rats scouring through the overflowing waste bins. We’re not just talking about the rodent variety, either. To a homeless man, an unattended alley is a buffet line. It's sickening to watch. But it doesn't turn our stomach. We’ve carved the eyeballs out of a man while he was still breathing. So watching another stuff his mouth with moldy pizza is mild in comparison. But we don't do anything about it. We just watch, superior in the knowledge that we don't have to stoop so low, and ashamed for the person, because someone was witness to the depths of their deprivation. Yeah, it wouldn't kill us to give him a wad of bills and bless his life with an act from the god. But you save one, then you have to save them all. We’re not the world's savior...not in material context. We weren't put on this Planet to help the weak or the poor...only to stomp over them in our goal of total harmony. It's hard enough to survive yourself, without worrying about what other people are doing. So we let the weak rot. We’re the Neo Void God. The Darkness. We bring death with us where we go, not life. We’re feared, we’re hated, and we’re respected. People move out of our way when we walk down the street, leave us alone when we enter a bar, and shy away when we raise our fist. We should feed off this. Our ego should be bigger than the whole goddamed city. But like the guy digging in the trash, our reputation sickens us. We wanted to be as different from our father as we could, and boy did we ever make it. We’re a fucking god. We could twist your neck and banish your carcass while you’re giving a speech and everyone would look the other way. Yeah, we made it to the big time. We pull to our feet on stiff legs, settling our back against the brick of a nearby building. Something runs across our foot, but we don't bother to look. It was probably just a rat. You get used to their company when you're on surveillance. Maybe even prefer it. They don't talk back, and they don't occupy much space. Besides, We’re a god, we should feel right at home. We may be covered in clean clothing, smell faintly of cigarette smoke and soap, and have some place warm and dry to go back to, but beneath it all, we’re covered in the same filth. Clothes and skin are only the outer layers. Inside, we’re as black and cold as the night. When we was younger, we used to question our reason for being. What was the point of going to school, of coming home and training endlessly without rest, of listening to the sickening snap of flesh on flesh? We always felt that we would be better off dead. But suicide is the cowardly way out, and there was no way in hell life was turning us into a weakling. So, instead, we found our purpose. We would never be like our father. Our entire life would revolve around being something he was not. Yeah, something better. So ditch the human race and become a god. Work your way through as a grunt, dishing shit out as well as you could take it. Then, when the opportunity approaches you, gives you a order, tell you it'd get you out of society, you take it. Yeah, and when you go home, look down on your father, you'll listen to him tell you that you're no better than him. Lower, even. Because he may be a drunk, but at least he isn't an assassin, a man that profits from other people's deaths. You won't beat your wife, dwindle your money away, or sleep with women you pick up in a bar. You'll just kill strangers, ruin countless lives, and become a household name of fear. But you'll never go hungry. You won't have to live amongst the filth, or be on the same level as any of those non-enlightened humans ever again. You can stand tall, and walk proud, because you're gained a better existence, and you know you look damn good in all black. And somewhere, along the way, you can lose your soul. Because if you don't, then you lose yourself. Flicking ashes onto the ground, we watch them roll across the surface and disappear into the shadows. We don't mind telling you we’re getting tired of standing out here. Not that it changes much. We’ve got a task to do, and we never leave a task half-finished. It doesn't matter what we have to do to end it, we'll find a way. We have a reputation to keep, after all. We’re known as a strange god who would rather be kicking back in a bar than working. And no, before you ask, that isn't the reputation we’re talking about. That's just the one people see. Funny, how what you see isn't always what you get, isn't it? We’re ruthless, we’re thorough, and we’re patient enough to wait an entire day in the same spot for our target if we have to. And very few realize this. Good old Champion Miyagawa knows what we are, though. He knows, that despite our complete lack of respect for him, our oftentimes-unique way of doing things, and our easy smiles, that we would just as soon snap his neck as look at him. We’ll smile into his face while we do it too, while his blood runs down our hands, warm, until the chill of the air touches it. Smiling is an easy mask to project, after all. So is laziness. We only have to slouch a little, wear our clothing askew, and rarely act like we give a crap. We can be everything we’re not to you, and you'll never be any wiser for it. Not until those last moments, when the gap between life and death is closed. Some days, when we’re walking the PCW halls, and we hear hopeful wrestlers talking about what life would be like if they made it to our status, we want to beat the shit out of them. They're so stupid, so naive. They talk as if what we do is some grand thing, as if life couldn't possibly get much better than this. We know some of these rookies get that way. They come into this thinking only of the glory, and after coming against us, going to come out of it, thinking only of the death. This way of life is going to kill their innocence, smother their optimism, and make them hate the Planet, and all the people on it. Because here, all you see is the ugliness. And when you're faced with so much, you begin to struggle to see the beauty, until you realize there isn't any left. You can't tell them that. You can't tell any of them that. They can only see it for themselves, and that's sad. But we'll spare our pity, and save our worry for something else. They went into it of their own accord, and there isn't a damn thing you can do to stop them. We're all going to grind ourselves into ruin, until there's nothing left, and nothing is going to be able to save us. Not that we give a flying fuck. We don't need to be saved. We’re right where we want to be…we’re the only one who can save ourselves… The voice once again echoes through our head, and we drop the cigarette to the ground, grinding it beneath our heel. 'He's heading your way. He should enter the alley for the package in less than a minute,' a calm, apathetic voice tells us. 'We understand,' we answer, sharp eyes cutting to the alley again. We don't have to wait long. A figure, dressed in black shuffles slowly past, looking over his shoulder every so often. His nervousness is apparent, but his caution isn't needed. He's already dead. He just doesn't know it yet. We step away from the wall, our size not giving away our position. If things go as we want them to, we probably won't need brute force. But that all depends on our little rat here. Our feet are silent as we cross the street, pausing only once to watch him with a half-smirk. There are many stupid things you can do. One of them is crossing then NVG. We aren’t kind to challengers. No, we send go after them. Through our HAND OF FATE, we enact our dominance to discourage things like this from happening again. Yes, while we stain our hands with blood, we grow closer and closer towards our final goal. Sliding into the alleyway just behind him, we catch a flicker of a movement down the sidewalk. Sylo. He doesn't leave us with the dirty work when we're here to accomplish something. We do it together. But we don't thank him for that. He doesn't want our gratitude anymore than we want his. We fall right behind him, our breath a brief cloud in the air. He rummages through the dumpster, still unaware of our presence. The stench already filling the alley covers whatever new smells we might bring. Otherwise, he probably would have smelled our cigarettes. Or at the very least, our cologne. But no, he remains oblivious, right up until our hand falls onto his shoulder. He jerks hard, trying to spin away. We tighten our grip so that we know it has to be painful. 'Hold still,' we inform him quietly, as Sylo falls on the other side of him, blocking off all signs of an exit. We catch a glimpse of his face. Wide-eyed and frightened, he knows who it is that has him now. He probably also knows he's going to die. The risks we take for money... It's sad, really. 'What are you going to do with me?' He stutters. The muscles in his shoulder are tight beneath our hand. We smile, our teeth a flash in the dark. 'We have a better question. What's in the dumpster?' we ask, our voice pleasant enough. 'I-I don't know what you're talking about,' he answers quickly. We look to Sylo. He nods. 'That's the wrong answer.' One hand on his shoulder still, we reach down and grab his hand, settling it along the top of the dumpster so that his fingers trail over the side. 'What are you doing?' He asks frantically, head jerking back and forth between us. 'What's in the dumpster?' we ask instead. 'I told you,' he insists, his hand cold beneath mine, 'I don't know what you're talking about.' 'See, now that's strike two.' And, before he can react, Sylo reaches out and snaps his index finger. He cries out in pain, trying to pull away from us, but we’re stronger, and holding him down isn't much effort. 'We wouldn't recommend lying. Our friend here has no problem with breaking every little bone in your body until we get the answer we want.' He's crying now. 'Why don't you just look for yourselves?' We punch our thumb down on the snapped finger. He cries out again. 'Because we want you to tell us. It's much more fun that way.' 'If I tell you, will you let me live?' We shrug. But living wasn't on the list of options. Cyclonica told us she wants him dead, and so that's what he's going to be. 'Maybe,' we respond, noncommittal. He takes a shuddering breath. 'It's Maxis, Inc. programs in techno-drug developments. I’m going to sell them.' 'Oh, yeah? Nice hiding place,' we say conversationally. Sylo stands by, a silent, threatening presence. We wonder if he ever gets tired of being the 'strong arm'. 'You'll... you'll let me live now?' He pleads, and the hope in his voice is sickening. 'Well,' we begin, moving behind him quickly so that our arms come up around his neck, 'we could be a nice guy and let you live. But we’re not.' And we twist, the brutal snap of bones telling us what we need to know. Then, we let him fall, not watching as he hits the pavement. 'Get the materials. He slipped them into his coat just before we grabbed him,' we tell Sylo, as we step over the body. Whatever we once was is bottled up. All that remains is emptiness. We’re a walking ghost. Dead the day we was born, dead until the day we die. So if you see us on the street, don't be fooled by our easy smile. Look the other way and pass on by. Because we won't hesitate when you're the one we’re ordered to kill. We’ll let you fall, we'll step over your body, and we'll walk away. Miyagawa should head straight towards us with all of his championship abilities ready…because we know just how much of a championship chump he will be once we make him submit to the Akiyama Lock. We’ll take his TV title and become the god of Television… And we won't ever, look back.
