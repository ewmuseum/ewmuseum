Switching Places

5 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-5-02 -=[The scene opens with Thug roC, now going by Thug roC, sitting on his couch in his D.C. home. He’s watching the evening news when his son Michael arrives home. He walks by Thug roC and says hi. Thug roC waves his hand and Michael walks upstairs to his room. RoC’s wife, Shannon, has seen this and she walks into the room]=- Shannon: Terrel, you don’t show that boy any attention do you? Thug roC: Sure I do, I waved didn’t I? Shannon: When was the last time you had a day out with your son? Thug roC: Ha, got you there. I took him out on his Birthday Shannon: It was his 10th birthday! That was 7 years ago! Thug roC: I still took him out. Shannon: That boy is deprived of seeing his father enough as it if, and even when you’re here at home you don’t share any of your time. Thug roC: Who are we kidding Shannon, we both know he doesn’t want to spend time with me and vise versa, so why waste my time? I’m a busy man you know. Shannon: Yea, I know. You were so busy you couldn’t even retain that piece of tin you call a title. Thug roC: I don’t see you doing any better. All you have to do is sit on your ass and watch the kids. Shannon: That’s hard work Terrel! Thug roC: Bullshit, all you do is wipe off a couple counters and fix a few bowls of cereal. I on the other hand, must beat the crap out of these odd looking wannabes. And now you want me to spend time with Michael, you don’t even want to do that! Shannon: Fine then Terrel, I’ll make you a deal. You watch Clare and Lisa for a day, and I’ll take Michael out. Thug roC: That’s all? You have yourself a deal. Shannon: That’s all? Then fine, I’ll even do some of that training you so. Thug roC: Wait a minute; you mean you’ll get into a ring to prove a point? Shannon: That’s right. Starting tomorrow, you will realize just how hard I have it. Thug roC: If you say so. Tomorrow the girls will be all ready for a good long day with dad while you are suffering trying to keep up with Michael and Machine in the ring. -=[Shannon leaves the room and Thug roC laughs it off pretty well. He laughs so much that he rolls off the couch while holding his stomach. The day goes by and a new day is upon the Roc family. The day opens with Thug roC and Shannon in the bed still sleeping. Suddenly, Thug roC’s youngest daughter, Lisa, runs in and jumps into the bed. Thug roC jumps up along with Shannon. Lisa, who is 6 years old, starts jumping on the bed over and over again]=- Lisa: Hungry! Hungry! Hungry! Hungry! Thug roC: Shannon, fix this girl some good so she can calm down. Shannon: [Still Tired]. Oh no you don’t. This is your day to take on the girls; I don’t have to get up for a couple hours. Thug roC: Damnit… Lisa: Ooooo, Daddy said a bad word! -=[Thug roC gets up from bed and grabs Lisa and throws her over his shoulder. Lisa is still yelling at the top of the lungs as Thug roC takes her downstairs. Shannon simply rolls back over and falls back to sleep. A couple hours later, Lisa has already been fed. She is watching Scooby Doo in the living room while roC’s other daughter, Clare, is just now eating breakfast. Clare is the oldest at the age of 16 and she has that ‘I’m better then you’ mentality. Wonder where she got that from? Thug roC is sitting down at the breakfast table with a plate for himself as Shannon comes down for her workout. She has some sweat pants and a Starplosion shirt on ready to get into the ring]=- Thug roC: I hope you’re ready for a beating. Shannon: Watch me Terrel, I will go into that ring and do what you do 10 times better then you ever did. Thug roC: Yea, sure you will. Machine should be here soon to pick you up. Good luck. Shannon: You make it sound so hard. Thug roC: It’s a lot harder then you think. Shannon: Watch how I win this one easily. -=[A horn is heard outside the house and it’s obviously Machine in a SUV. Shannon smiles and picks up her bag. She runs out the door and closes it behind her. Thug roC has a smile on his face as well. He continues on his day, as it has only just begun]=- -=[Commercials: roC-TV is brought to you by the PCW, by Pepsi, and by the Orlando Magic.]=- Time: 11:00 Am.-=[We come back from commercial and Shannon has arrived to PCW’s training facility in D.C. She arrives with Machine and practically skips to the entranceway. Machine has that tired look on his face as he makes his way behind her. He swipes his ID card and the door opens for them.]=- -=[Meanwhile, Thug roC is at home with his daughters as Lisa is running from Michael as he wants his Baby Ruth candy bar back. Thug roC simply sits on the couch, as he sees nothing wrong with this.]=- -=[Back at the training facility, Shannon is in the ring with Machine as they are ready to start]=- Machine: Alright Shannon, seeing this is your first time in the ring, we are going to start of slow. Lets start by teaching you how to take a bump. Shannon: Bump? I’ve seen those millions of times, lets move on. Machine: Are you sure? Shannon: Of course I’m sure; I am the best woman in the business… Machine: Oh my gawd. Shannon: Lets have a full-length match! Machine: You must be nuts. Shannon: No I’m not nuts; I just want to show the world what kind of wrestler I really am. Machine: Let me get this straight. You are cocky, egotistical, and you claim to be the best woman in this business. Shannon: Damn right Machine: I always wondered how roC got married; now I know. Time: 11:30 AM-=[We go back to the roC household as Thug roC still sits on his couch and the family is still going wild behind him. We find that now Michael and Clare are fighting over the Baby Ruth bar as Lisa has been hung by her shirt on the closet door. This all is happening behind Thug roC’s back as he is watching Pardon The Interruption on ESPN. We see Clare wrestle Michael to the ground right behind the couch. We can’t see them until Michael pops up with Clare on his shoulders performing a roCing Horse! A crash is heard as Michael lands the move.]=- Thug roC: Could you guys keep it down, I’m watching TV. -=[Thug roC still hasn’t noticed the fight and Michael gets up from behind the couch raising the Baby Ruth bar in one hand. He turns around and Lisa has released herself from the hook. She leaps up and takes Michael down with a cross body. We see Lisa get up and jump back down with an elbow drop. Now Lisa is up raising the Baby Ruth bar as if it’s a title or something. Thug roC yawns, as he is now getting tired and starts dozing off. Clare gets back up and picks little Lisa up and hangs her back on the coat hook. Lisa simply kicks Clare in the mouth and Clare drops to the ground. Lisa somehow, someway, unhooks herself again. She literally climbs to the top of the closet door and perches herself there for a while.]=- Thug roC: I wonder why it’s so quiet… -=[Both Michael and Clare gets up at the same time and Lisa jumps off the door crashing on both of them with a suicide dive! CRASH!]=- Thug roC: That’s more like it. -=[ We now continue these series of events picking up at the PCW training facility where Machine is about to go at it with Shannon]=- Time: 11:45 AMMachine: Are you sure you are ready? Shannon: Of course I am ready. I’ve watched millions of wrestling matches, it’s just acting. Machine: Ok, if you say so. -=[Machine and Shannon lock up in the center of the ring. The loud echo of the canvas is heard as Machine then shoves Shannon straight on her ass]=- Shannon: Shit Machine! That hurt! Machine: Your point? Shannon: Is that the way you treat a lady? Machine: This is the ring, I don’t give a damn if you’re a lady or not. Now lets try this again… Shannon: Fine. -=[The two lock up again, this time, Machine pulls off an arm bar]=- Shannon: What the hell? Let go! -=[Machine lets go]=- Machine: What’s wrong now? Shannon: That’s not how you do it! You’re suppose to only make it look like it hurts! Machine: Shannon, this is how we do it, now either you can do it the right way, or go home. Shannon: Aight, one more time. -=[For the third time now, the two lock up. Machine whips Shannon to the ropes. Shannon kinda trips on the way there, but when she turns around, she sees Machine clinch his fist. She continues running, and when Machine reels back, Shannon drops to the ground and covers her head]=- Machine: What is wrong now? Shannon: You were going to hit me! Machine: I repeat, what is wrong now? Shannon: You’re not supposed to hit a female! Machine: This is going to be one long day. -=[We go back to the roC household where the Baby Ruth Battle Royal is still going on. We find Thug roC sitting on his couch just like we left him as little Lisa is seen sprinting across the room. Michael carrying a chair follows her. The doorbell rings and Thug roC gets up to answer it. It turns out to be a newsperson. Thug roC has his back turned to the kids once again as Clare has snuck up behind Michael and delivered a low blow!]=- Newsperson: Mr. RoC, what are your feelings about the latest comments from Shaq? Thug roC: Shaq? What did he have to say? Newsperson: You mean you haven’t heard his comments. They’ve been all over the news and papers… Thug roC: Well spill it, what did he say? Newsperson: Well, I can’t quote him right now, but he pretty much said that his knee has healed and you are your next opponent -=[In the background we see Michael on the ground clutching his groin as Lisa smacks Clare with the heavily padded chair. Clare is stunned, but isn’t effected by the 6 year old’s shot. Clare picks Lisa up by her armpits and goes for the coat hook again. Lisa starts kicking wildly and knocks over a candle. The candle falls to the ground and a fire has now been made. Thug roC has yet to notice any of this]=- Thug roC: The whole media has blown this thing between me and Shaq fully out of proportion. I don’t have anything against the guy really. Not many people know that Shaq and I are long time friends… -=[Meanwhile inside the house, Michael has gained his senses and notices the fire. He rushes out of the room as the girls are all screaming. He runs back in with a giant sized picture of Thug roC. He throws the picture on top of the fire in an attempt to put it out. The fire merely goes right through the picture. Michael panics and runs out of the room once again]=- Newsperson: You mean to tell me that you personally know Shaq? Thug roC: Of course I know him. When we were little, we use to fish together. But we didn’t use fishing poles or anything, he’d take off his shoe, dunk it into the lake, and when he picked it out of the lake, we’d have an easy 4-5 fish. Newsperson: Are you kidding me? Thug roC: How do you think I became so strong? When we were kids, I use to ride my bike with Shaq on the handlebars. Newsperson: Even though you say you’re his friend, he still has a personal threat out towards you. Thug roC: I am not afraid of Shaq in no way, shape, or form. Newsperson: You sure did look scared at that Piston game a couple weeks ago. Thug roC: That was me acting like I was scared. I was really scared. Shaq may be big, but I have faced bigger. He may be strong, but I have faced stronger… -=[In the background we see Michael rush back into the room with a hand full of shirts. The shirts are mainly of Thug roC when he was with PCW. Michael throws the piles of shirt onto the fire and they burn up with in seconds. Lisa runs out of the room and returns with a replica of the JeW Cruiserweight title. She throws it into the fire and watches the gold melt. Clare and Michael are both shocked at what little Lisa did but the fire soon enough goes out]=- Thug roC: I mean, why be afraid of Shaq? Lets say he wants face me in the wrestling ring. I am the best damn cruiserweight in the business. He would have to chance against me. Or lets say he wants to face me in a boxing ring. All I have to do is dress up like a free throw. That way he can’t hit me! Newsperson: Well, that’s all the time we have for this conversation, thank you very much Thug roC. Thug roC: No problem…tell me something. Do you smell smoke? -=[Thug roC turns around and sees the puff of smoke coming from the side of the couch. He rushes over there and sees what’s left of his shirts and JeW title. He has a look of anger on his face as he glares at the kids and they all scatter in different directions as if they were roaches with the lights on. Thug roC starts twitching as we switch back to Shannon in the ring]=- Time: Noon-=[Shannon is seen being placed on a stretcher on the side of the ring. Machine has his hand on his forehead in disbelief. They wrap up her right leg, left arm, her head, and put her in a neck brace]=- Machine: I swear Shannon I didn’t mean to kick you in the face. I didn’t know you were tying your shoe at the time. Shannon: I told you Machine it’s ok. Machine: And I also apologize for dropping repeated elbows on you while you were down. And for picking you up and giving you a piledriver, move that is almost illegal worldwide. Shannon: It’s ok Machine. Machine: And, I’m sorry for the Hollywood leg drop, the falling headbut and for well…[mumbling]…looking at your ass… Shannon: [Jumps up from the stretcher]...YOU WHAT!? -=[The paramedics are forced to hold Shannon back down onto the stretcher as she tries to get up and choke Machine. Machine backs up a good amount and they finally strap Shannon down. We then see Shannon being loaded onto the ambulance shaking her fist in anger]=- Time: 12:45 PM-=[Back at the roC residence, Thug roC is pacing back and forth looking at the big dark spot in his carpet. Some of the carpet has been burnt straight down to the hardwood floor while some of it is just plain burnt. The kids are all standing at attention as Thug roC has a worried look on his face. The phone rings and Thug roC goes to answer it]=- Thug roC: Hello…has she been taken care of? Machine: Yea, I made sure she doesn’t want to wrestle anymore for the rest of her life. Thug roC: Good, at least that’s one thing I don’t have to worry about. Machine: Why, what’s wrong? Thug roC: These damn kids burnt a hole in the carpet. Machine: You know you’re going to hear Shannon’s mouth right? Thug roC: Don’t worry I’ll fix this. -=[Thug roC hangs up the phone and turns around towards the kids and they are gone. Suddenly you hear a female scream and Thug roC sprints up the stairs. We then see Michael applying a half boston crab to Clare in the hallway]=- Michael: Don’t worry, I’ve seen dad do this all the time. It doesn’t hurt at all trust me on this one. Clare: Ouch Michael this hurts! -=[In the background we see Lisa eating the Baby Ruth candy bar]=- Thug roC: Boy get off of her! -=[We then see Lisa drop the candy bar on the ground. She panics and runs off out of sight]=- Thug roC: Don’t you know only professionals can do stuff like that. -=[Lisa comes back with a bottle of liquid cleaner. She starts spraying it on the chocolate stain but sees no results. She then decides to unscrew the top and dump it all out onto the stain. She fails to realize that this wasn’t any kind of cleaner; this was a bottle of hair spray. She then panics again and runs back of screen]=- Thug roC: No Michael go to your room. And Clare go in the basement and see if we have anymore of those carpet samples. We have to see what we can do about your little self-made fireplace. -=[Michael and Clare walk off the screen. Thug roC can smell something. After a few sniffs, he turns around and looks on the ground to see this discolored carpet. Thug roC does a 180-degree spin only to find nobody around. All the kids have ran off already. He looks around for Lisa only to find that she has already disappeared. The scene fades as we look at the bluish brownish stain on the carpet]=- Time: 3 PM -=[We go outside of the Dewitt hospital in DC where we see Shannon exiting. Machine is there in his SUV ready to pick her up. Shannon has a neck brace on along with multiple bandages and slings. Shannon gets into the SUV and stares straight ahead not looking at Machine at all]=- Machine: So I take it you’re not talking to me. Shannon: … Machine: And I take that as a yes. -=[Machine takes Shannon back home as it finally dawns on Machine that Shannon can’t go into the house just yet, for if she does then she will realize the chaos that is going on inside]=- Machine: So, would you like to get a bite to eat? Shannon: You take me all the way to the house, and now you want to get something to eat? What the hell are you thinking? Machine: I just realized that you haven’t eaten anything and you’re probably hungry. Shannon: Whatever Machine, I’m going inside. -=[Shannon exits the SUV and makes her way towards the front door, Machine jumps out of the car to intercept]=- Machine: Are you sure you’re not hungry? Shannon: Move Machine or I’ll tell Terrel you were looking at my ass… -=[Machine moves out of the way quickly and Shannon opens the front door. It’s like walking into a Real World house, as the place is sparkle clean. Furniture is polished, and the floor has been vacuumed. Shannon drops her bag in shock as this seems to good to be true. Thug roC comes around the corner with a dust broom in his hand. He posts it against the wall as he sees Shannon walk in]=- Thug roC: So Shannon, how do like the house? Shannon: Did you clean up? Thug roC: Of course I did. Shannon: Oh my gawd…I can’t believe this. Thug roC: Well that just shows how much you underestimate me. Shannon: Did you…move the couch and entertainment center? Thug roC: Um…yea…well…I hate to vacuum under them somehow, and after I moved them, I figured to leave them the way they were. Shannon: Well I don’t know what to say. Thug roC: Wait, that’s not all…there’s more! Remember that MLK statue that you saw a couple months ago… Shannon: Yea… Thug roC: Well, I went out and bought it. Shannon: You did?! Thug roC: Yea, it’s upstairs in the hallway. Shannon: Thank you Terrel! We should put it in the living room! Thug roC: Um…you know what, I think it looks good just where it is. I mean that thing is heavy; I might pull something trying to pick it up again. Shannon: Then we’ll just have Machine or Carl help you with it. Thug roC: Um…that’s ok. We don’t want to bother them. You know they are busy nowadays. We shouldn’t bother them with our little troubles. Shannon: Maybe you’re right. But we’re going to have to move it sooner or later. Thug roC: Maybe more later then sooner… Shannon: What was that? Thug roC: Oh, nothing. Don’t you have something else to do today? Like take Michael out? Shannon: Matter of fact I do. And watch as I be the good parent. Thug roC: I would love to see this, but I will sit back and watch the girls while you try to stand a day with the boy. Shannon: Tell Michael to get ready so we can go to the movies. Thug roC: Fine, he’ll be ready. -=[Shannon staggers to get upstairs as Thug roC walks into the living room where Michael is. Thug roC hands Michael a hand full of money and Michael slips it in his pocket]=- Thug roC: Now boy, I don’t need your mom to brag about how she’s a better parent. So be sure to give her a hard time Michael: No problem, if there’s one thing I know how to do, is giving mom a hard time. -=[The scene fades out and back in at the movies. Shannon and Michael are just walking into the packed lobby. You see hundreds of people all standing around talking. Michael and Shannon continue to walk until they get in line. The line seems not to be moving as an obese man is at the front of the line buying everything he wants]=- Michael: Mom, can’t we just get into another line? Shannon: Why? Michael: That man will take forever. He might not leave anything left for all of us to buy Shannon: Michael, stop making rude comments. Those comments are not necessary. Michael: But mom… Shannon: But nothing…hush your mouth. -=[Both of them wait in line for another two minutes as the fat guy is still ordering more stuff. Suddenly the man’s beeper goes off. Michael has a burst of energy as he turns around and yells…]=- Michael: WATCH OUT! HE’S BACKING UP! -=[The lobby erupts in laughter as the fat guy turns around and seems like he’s about to cry. The fat guy then runs out of line making the ground shake with every step. Multiple people fall over from the floor’s vibration as an earthquake alarm goes off. The fat guy then trips and falls. He finally just rolls to his car]=- Shannon: Michael! Why’d you say that? Michael: I really thought he was about to back up… -=[The two go through the line and get some popcorn and drinks. They give their tickets to the usher and proceed down the hallway to their theater. Michael is walking much faster then his mom. Shannon finds herself trying to catch up with him with every step. Michael turns the corner and Shannon is still trying to catch up. When she turns the corner sharply, she runs smack into him. The soda pores down Shannon’s shirt making her soaking wet! Michael cant do anything but laugh as he looks at his embarrassed mother]=- Michael: Wow, I have to tell dad how I made you wet! -=[Shannon all of a sudden takes her look of embarrassment into a look of disgust as she grabs Michael by his ear and they make their way out of the movie theater]=- -=[The scene comes back with Shannon and Michael arriving at the house where Thug roC is watching TV in the living room. Thug roC takes one look at Shannon and starts cracking up laughing]=- Thug roC: Nobody told me it was raining… Shannon: That boy of yours drenched me in Coca-Cola. Thug roC: Oh really? I must’ve forgotten to tell you to bring your raincoat. Just be lucky yours was Coca-cola. When I took him it was red fruit punch. Shannon: That’s it, you keep doing what you’re doing. I can’t handle your work. First Machine tries to kill me, then Michael wants to bathe me. I just can’t take it anymore! You be the father. Thug roC: So lets get this right, I win, and you lose. Shannon: You’re the worst winner I know… -=[Scene fades to black]=-
