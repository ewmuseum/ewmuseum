Flushing the shit down the drain, CEO style.

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/22/02 The Executive Washroom is a place that most young businessmen only dream of being able to visit. The gold trimmed fittings and solid marble construction are worth more than most of them will make in their entire lifetime. The stalls are made with all of the comforts of home, from mini television sets to magazine racks to small refrigerators stocked with small bottles of champagne and caviar. To them, the young businessmen just getting their feet wet in the real world, the Executive Washroom is a symbol of success. To them, it means that they have made it. That’s them. To the CEO of Primetime Championship Wrestling, the Executive Washroom is just a shitter. Most of the time it serves as a second office and a place where one can think in private. To Tyler Nelson, the Executive Washroom is a necessity, not a privilege. One of the CEO’s status has to have a nice Executive Washroom for those times when peace, tranquility, and a nice herbal scent are a must. With that being said, we are taken to the Executive Washroom off of the CEO’s office inside PCW Headquarters in Atlantic City, New Jersey. Even though it smells like rotten ass outside, inside the Executive Washroom it smells something akin to a bed of roses. The grey marble stretches from wall to wall on the floor, and up those same walls to the ceiling. The soft lighting is dimmed, except for a reading light inside of the Executive stall. It’s rather quiet in the washroom, with only the sound of flipping pages to be heard. The silence is broken, however, by the CEO himself. “I can’t believe what that son of a bitchin Phantom Booker has gone and done,” says Tyler in disbelief. “Look at some of the names on this roster!” More pages are flipped, with intermittent sighs heard as well. “Kano? Snow? Frampton? Big Daveyfuckinlicious?” reviews the CEO. “I thought I told him to get rid of losers like that! Instead, he brings them right back and now we’re in the same Goddamn boat we were in before! Just goes to show that when you need something done you have to do it yourself.” Anger and frustration lace the CEO’s voice as he continues to flip through page after page inside the black marble Executive Washroom stall. “I’m not sure how much more I can stand of the likes of Kid Krayzie, Kramer Wild Wing, and….Trigger?” The CEO pauses out of pure astonishment. “Trigger? Trigger? TRIGGER?! HE HIRED A FUCKING GUY NAMED TRIGGER?! TRIGGER IS A FUCKING DEAD HORSE FOR CHRIST’S SAKE!!” The echo from the CEO’s angered voice reverberates throughout the Washroom, shaking the stall door somewhat. The echo’s subside and quiet reigns once again in the Executive Washroom. Light breathing from the CEO can be heard as yet more pages are turned. “Friday Fusion, Malice Crew versus The Ultimate Authority with Big Alan Steele as the Special Guest Referee,” sneers Tyler. “That just reeks of that pantyhose headed freaks stench. If I would have known that things were going to go down like this I would have left the bastard lying in the gutter I found him in. Try to do something nice for someone and they always repay you in shit. Maybe that’s why I don’t do nice shit for anyone. Speaking of shit….” We can see the CEO’s polished leather dress shoes shuffle around as his suit pants hang around his ankles. His weight shifts from one side to another, growing somewhat frantic in their motion. “What the…,” mutters the CEO. “No toilet paper? WHAT THE FUCK?!” Tyler pounds on the side of the Executive Washroom stall and it makes a loud thud as his fist hits the marble. “OUCH!” A faint kiss is heard as Tyler tries to soothe the pain felt in his fist. “Whoever forgot to stock this stall with toilet paper is FIRED!!” seethes the CEO. “Man I wish it was Booker so I could FIRE his fucking ass! Or maybe Big Alan Steele! Or even Duke fucking Williams!” The prospects of being able to fire those three men are extremely appealing to the CEO, but unrealistic at the present time. First things first, he needs some toilet paper. “Let’s see here…..what can I use?” wonders Tyler. “This tie is too DAMN expensive, and besides that would be something Duke would do.” Suddenly above the stall a light bulb appears and lights up brightly. “That’s it!” exclaims the CEO. Soon we hear the shuffling of papers. “There he is….Duke Williams.” A wiping sound is heard, followed up by the crumpling of paper. “And there’s The Rush.” More wiping. More crumpling. “And Jon Kano.” Wipe. Crumple. “Ahh…Jordan X. Shabazz.” One last wipe. One last crumple. Again Tyler’s feet shuffle and we see a pair of hands reach down and pull his pants up. A belt is fastened and suit adjusted. Then the toilet is flushed. “Just like what will happen on Fusion,” says Tyler arrogantly. “The shit is going to get flushed down the toilet for good. Then I’ll be rid of the whole lot of those Malice Crew assholes.” As the water finishes flushing the shit and papers down into the sewer the CEO emerges from the stall, refreshed and relieved. “Much better,” Tyler sighs satisfactorily. He smirks a cocky grin as he makes his way over to the lavatory and washes his hands. He finishes and grabs the monogrammed towel from the rack, drying his hands thoroughly. As he does he glances in the mirror and shoots himself a self absorbed wink. “You are the man,” coos the CEO to himself. He tosses the towel down on the black marble counter and exits the room. The scene fades to black.
