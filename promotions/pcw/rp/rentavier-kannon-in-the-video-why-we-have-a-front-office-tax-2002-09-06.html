RENTAVIER KANNON In The Video...'WHY WE HAVE A FRONT OFFICE TAX!' w/ Smellie!~

6 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-6-2002 ..::/::..Our story begins on a gorgeous sunny day, the yellowish orange jewel known as the sun shining down on the small town below....the people off the streets and homes empty and bare as it seems everyone is at the arena, which happens to be host to a hot PCW house show that has been sold out for weeks. Their anticpation for stars such as Darkstar, Xavier Kannon, Rent-A-Villain, Clyde, Pharaoh, and countless other staples of PCW talent has grown to a fever pitch as the sound of the fans thunderous roars from within can be heard on the outside of the arena, breaking the peace outside with their excited cheering. Our focus however, is not on the hot untelevised action within the ring, but the backstage area of the arena. Walking the halls calmly are two young wrestlers, one with short green tights, spiked black hair, and an eagle tattoo on his right shoulder....the other in a full piece brightly yellow set of tights and brandishing honey blonde hair with a matching goatee. The two appear to be young PCW development workers, conversing about seemingly anything as they make their way to the tiny locker room assigned to them for this show. The blonde wrestler is carrying a single black VHS tape in hand, holding it up to his friend as he says..::/::.. Worker#1:'Hey man, did you hear...even the development workers gotta pay this 'Front Office Tax' stuff man.' Worker#2:'Total bummer man, that Xavier guy seems like a slave driver. But if we want any chance of hitting it big, we better learn to pay these taxes....or try for OSW.' Worker#1:'Wrong answer man, you didn't hear? To get in OSW now you have to have been kicked out of CWL, Asylum, PIW, UTA, fWo, a no-name fed, at least ONE fed with an 'X' in it, AND have to have jobbed to LLB at least twice!' Worker#2:'D**n standards and practicies...' ..::/::..Slightly peeved by their options in the wrestling world, the two workers step into their locker room, finding it meagerly furnished and at the bare minimum, due to their status and all. Eyeing the room, they find only two tall steel lockers, two metal folding chairs, a TV, and a VCR, not finding any of the other luxuries like a fruit bowl, changing screen...or in some cases....a hot young girl of the evening. Nevertheless, the two young workers plop down in the already readied folding chairs, the blonde worker sliding the tape within the unfed slot of the VCR as it devours the tape within it humbly. Leaning back in their chairs, awaiting the tape to start, the dark-haired worker comments..::/::..Worker#2:'Sure hope this tax isn't too much or it looks like we're gonna work up our fed count for OSW.'Worker#1:'Shhh, it's starting.'..::/::..The tape transfers from a blank, desolate black screen to what appears to be gritty, home video-esque style footage as the tape begins within a makeshift office, almost an exact replica of Xavier Kannon's regal PCW office...if it weren't for the cardboard cut out desk and the 'PCW' insignia spray-painted upon it. The desk is covered with assorted bottles of lotion, ranging from tiny tubes of Victoria's Secret scented lotions to the over the counter staple, Vaseline. The only thing not akin to lotion on the 'desk' is a nameplate sitting tall and proud on the edge of the desk, the camera getting a close-up of the plate as it reads..::/::.. Rentavier Kannon-PCW CEO & Gold Patron Masterbate-orious ..::/::..The two young workers wince at each other, wondering what is going on pertaining to the silly nature of the tape, the two too intrigued by what is going on to turn it off, opting instead to see where this 'informative' tape goes. It starts to sink to a much more comedic level as the camera pans up, where we find behind the desk a large black revolving chair, the back currently turned to the camera as it jerks violently, squeaking like an army of tortured mice as this rocking motion continues. Finally, the rocking stops, the chair spinning around to reveal....RENT-A-VILLAIN?!..::/::..Worker#2:'Hey, it's that Rent-A-Villain guy ain't it?' Worker#1:'Yea, he's so cool....not like I'm just saying that because the writer of this FABULOUS RP is making me say this or anything...I mean, he's just so da** cool.' *winks*Worker#2:'Why is he dressed up like that?' ..::/::..The worker refers to the unusual nature of dress our vile villain has donned....Rent-A-Villain wearing a a mop on his head, which has been dyed blonde so blonde, it looks as if the makeshift wig had been beaten up by a bottle of peroxide. Rent-A-Villain also compliments this with a drawn on goatee, done with the brightest of yellow highlighters, and to finish off this silly spoof of PCW's current CEO, our vile villain is wearing a light gray suit, his right hand strangely absent from camera as it remains unseen below the desk. With a smile, our spoofing supervillain begins his silly tirade, executing his best Xavier Kannon voice with full intention to embarass..::/::.. Rent-A-Villa....I mean RENTAVIER KANNON:'Greetings you mindless peons, Hubbardville's Bastard Son and your PCW CEO, XAVIER KANNON HERE! As I was jerking....I mean choking...I mean pumping...I mean jacking...I MEAN THINKING in my office...there was something on my mind. Recently, I had released this video explaining how to fill out the 'Front Office Tax' that has now been imposed on the PCW roster. But you know what was missing....a tape as to WHY the tax was being imposed in the first place? I mean, I already am a piss-poor PCW President who jerks off his own ego when well...I'm not jerking off. And I already cut ANNOYING promo's about some dumb town nobody cares about. And I am responsible for bringing in the DUMBEST belief system ever to PCW....Jackoff-erology...or Cheapwhore-erology....OH OH YEA, SCIENTOLOGY, some dumb religion that I can't even spell but I say because it's more than 4 letters and makes me sound smart! So I figure after all those things, why not GIVE you an explanation as to WHY I need this 'Front Office Tax' and to help me, is my lovely Comissioneress and the woman that makes Anna Nicole Smith seem like friggin Madam Curie....SMELLIE!~' ..::/::..The two PCW workers giggle like giddy school children, obviously enjoying what they see as they continue to watch this 'edited' feature by Rent-A-Villain himself, the sound of a door opening in the background as the camera pans to the side. Entering the 'office' is a buxom, well-endowed, bleach-blonde model. She enters the office in a gray business suit type dress, curling the front strand of her with her index finger while rudely opening her mouth wide open and chomping down again on her thick piece of strawberry bubblegum. 'Rentavier' glows with excitement as he looks to Smellie!~, turning the chair to his right as he exclaims..::/::.. Rentavier Kannon:'WHY HELLO SMELLIE!~ I see you have come just in time to help me explain my reasonings for this inane tax of mine! I'm sure you have a few words of bleach blonde wisdom for me before I begin.' Smellie!~:'Like...the halls in the mall....are like....TOTALLY TOTALLY TALL! YEAH!' Rentavier Kannon:'See PCW, how can PCW go under when we have a bleach blonde bimbo for a comissioner who only got her spot because her breasts stick out like rusty cannons!' Smellie!~:'NAH UH! I like sooooooooo didn't totally get this job because of my bre....bre....GRRR, I hate words with more than five letters....BREASTS, YEAH! I got this job because YOU said I had a mouth like a Hoover.' *She nods with justification, believing she just defended her dignity and reasoning for her job*Rentavier Kannon:'Here Smellie!~, while I list my reasons for the tax...how about you do some important work...like play with this keyring!' ..::/::..Rentavier hands Smellie!~ a small keyring, the blonde bimbo taking it within hand delightfully as she looks at it, turning it over....looking at it...then turning it over again. This silly cycle continues, the dumb blonde giddily smiling and jumping up and down through the cycle as the camera focuses back on Rentavier, who begins his list of reasons for the 'Front Office Tax'..::/::.. Rentavier:'Well right now...it's time for me to give the top three reasons why the 'Front Office Tax' has been implemented in the first place. Lets begin with number one you peons...which is...'#1....'XAVIER NEEDS A SET OF BALLS!!' Rentavier Kannon:'That's right, apparently...when your a follower of Scientology, you seem to be missing a set of balls. This explains the reason why I hide behind guys like Frisco and other minions of mine....because I'm missing the normal available set of balls required to actually have that thing called...um...what was it...oh yeah, GUTS! That's it!'Smellie!~:'Did you know that if you turn this keyring over...it says the SAME thing on the other side...and if you turn it over again....it says the SAME thing! LIKE, I'M SO TOTALLY EARNING MY KEEP IN THE OFFICE!' Rentavier Kannon:'With a comissioneress like Smellie!~, who needs those silly things like ORDER, JUSTICE, FAIRNESS, AND INTELLIGENCE! Anyways, back to my testicle deficiency situation...it's not like you can buy a set of testicles at the nearest Wal-Mart, and if they did, not like I'd buy them from there seeing as how they'd be the cheap kind, not like I want any 'Rolled Back' testicles anyways in the sales bin. Maybe if I had a set of those wonders, I'd show more guts and fight people head on, stop being such a coward, and ACTUALLY work up the nerve to do the right thing for a change! So pay your taxes so Xavier can have the balls he needs! Number two reason you ask?'..::/::..By this time, the two developmental workers are in tears from laughing, completely enthralled by the blatant spoofing of Xavier Kannon as they let the tape continue to roll..::/::.. #2....'XAVIER NEEDS LOTION!!' Rentavier Kannon:'What do you expect me to do all day being the CEO of PCW? Give back to the workers? Make the shows exciting and competitive? Quit whoring out airtime to myself? Sign hot talent and build up the best talent from within? WHY OF COURSE NOT, being a CEO is ALL about whoring yourself out and JERKING OFF IN YOUR SPARE TIME! Your tax dollars make sure that I have the widest assortment of lotions from Jergens to Oil of Olay so that your CEO can jerk with absolute comfort and softness! I mean, why spend that money on better facilities for you guys? Or to donate to charity? Or even use to better improve our product? That money needs to go to a good cause...and that's my hand...so that I can place that hand on my 9 centimeters of Scientologist meat and give myself a classic 'HUBBARDVILLE HANDJOB' in the comfort of my own office!' Smellie!~:'Like....did you know that like has like, four letters in it! OH MY GAWD, that is like the greatest thing! FOUR LETTERS!'Rentavier Kannon:'GOOD WORK SMELLIE!~! Now later, you can put those Hoover skills to work! You have to earn your keep you know!'Smellie!~:'LIKE I'LL SO TOOOOOTALLY GET THE MAGNIFYING GLASS, THE TWEEZERS, AND THE LOTION!'..::/::..The two workers laugh out loud, their breath almost gone from what they are viewing as the blonde worker looks over to his dark-haired friend, saying..::/::.. Worker#1:'I think I feel just a little bit better about paying that tax!' Worker#2:'I mean, we do want that money going to a good cause.'..::/::..Finally, Rentavier straightens up, clearing his throat so he can list the third and final reason for the 'Front Office Tax' on this tape, Smellie!~ jumping about inanely for no real reason with the keyring tight in her grasp. Finally, Rentavier composes himself and says..::/::.. Rentavier Kannon:'And the third and final reason...' #3....'TO BUY A PLANE TICKET AS FAR AWAY FROM RENT-A-VILLAIN AS POSSIBLE!!' ..::/::..At that instant, the spoofing stops, as 'Rentavier' dies right before the camera, giving way to Rent-A-Villain as he flips the desk over, tossing the chair to the ground violently as he begins tearing and shredding at the suit. Smellie!~ runs off-screen, screaming abruptly as Rent-A-Villain continues ripping at the suit, now ripped and loose, he strips it from his body, revealing his usual costume we've come to know and love, also throwing off the bleach blonde wig as well as he stares deep into the camera, his eyes filled with fury as he become an intense ball of fiery anger...geared straight for Xavier Kannon..::/::.. Rent-A-Villain:'No more jokes...no more games...last Fusion, you started something you can't finish! You wanna screw with the roster....you wanna screw with what I care about....you wanna screw with ME, I'll show you just how intense I'm gonna get. You don't realize how many lives you screwed over and changed last week. You have NO IDEA the amounts of pain you've made people suffer through you sick piece of crap! But that's just fine...every dog has his day XK and you'll have yours by my hand soon enough. It starts with Frisco....and it ends, with YOU. Tick tock XK...tick friggin tock....your time is running out...and when it's reached zero, you'll know. See ya Friday, Kannon...and don't worry...I'll have my taxes ready by Saturday...' ..::/::..Rent-A-Villain smirks devilishly at the camera, his eyes piercing through the screen with a real hate as the footage abruptly ending as black and white static overtakes the screen, the tape ending as the two workers look at each other with smirks, the dark-haired worker pressing the rewind button the VCR as the sound of a speedy whir can be heard from the innards of the VCR, the blonde saying to him as it rewinds..::/::.. Worker#1:'I think we better stick around for a little while...I wanna see where this goes...' Worker#2:'Cool...but until then, LETS WATCH THAT AGAIN!!'..::/::..With that said, the two workers await the VCR to end it's rewind so they may yet again witness our vile villain's satire of the current PCW CEO....the urge for revenge ever brewing within the soul of Rent-A-Villain.
