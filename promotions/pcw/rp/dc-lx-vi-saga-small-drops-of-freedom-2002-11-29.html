DC/LX/VI Saga: Small drops of freedom

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/11/02 He didn't know nor care what Webster's dictionary defined freedom as, but by God he knew that he was feeling it now. It felt good, it felt right, for the first time since he could remember he felt alive, truly alive, with a purpose to want to wake up tomorrow, with a purpose to inhale another breath. Even if this period of freedom he had was only small, he would stretch it and make it last as long as he could. Since the dark duo of Gideon Poe and Jeremiah O'Barr reached the hotel they were staying at after this weeks Adrenaline, where Gideon had suffered the horrendous beating from Steph O'Connor, Jeremiah had refused to let EMT's touch or attempt to heal Gideon, instead he would apply his own medical treatment. Once back at the hotel Gideon found out just what that was, something foul of smelling and stinging like a thousand hot vengeful bees in sensation when applied to his wounds. Gideon didn't want to know what it was, but it had numbed the pain in his forehead incredibly for at least 3 straight hours, he almost felt doped out and high from the healing potion, but that was soon passing. It was whilst Jeremiah decided to take a brisk walk and explore the gardens of the hotel, or so he told Gideon, but Gideon suspected he had more of a hidden agenda, that he received a telephone call in the hotel room. Upon answering it, a female voice greeted him. * * * * * * * * * 'Yes?' 'Gideon Poe?!' '.......... Yes?' 'Thank God!' 'Who is...' 'No time for explaining, your in danger. Meet me, Cooper's beach, tonight, 10pm. There is a Dodge Viper outside in the parking lot of the hotel, the keys for it are on the front left tyre, be sure that to get there. I'll be waiting for you, and for the love of God come alone, don't let Jeremiah know where your going!' 'What..... how do you know my name, how do you know Jeremiah?' '...... Cooper's beach, 10pm, come alone.' * * * * * * * * * * * Gideon was taken back to say the least, and it wasn't often that happened to him anymore. The young girls voice was full of panic and anxiousness, that, and just like the hotel room, reeked of something more than popery, her voice reeked of fear, he could almost smell it down the end of the receiver. Gideon's head was telling him to stay here, let the girl suffer. the last thing he needed was to worry about some girl, he had the committee to worry about. That was when the sudden cold realisation hit him. This girl must know something about the committee, this DC/LX/VI. She had to know and be willing to share information that Jeremiah would never even dream of sharing with him. His head told him to stay out of it, leave whatever it is be. His heart told him to go, find out about whatever information this girl seemed to know about, for all he knew his meaningless worthless life may depend on it. Then again, he welcomed death everyday, it was the last true escape he had from the miserable life he now led. Nevertheless......... By 9:30, Gideon Daray Poe was ready to leave. * * * * * * * * * * * * 'Where are you going?' croaked the familiar voice. 'Out'. Gideon tried to lie to Jeremiah as little as possible, as it was a waste of breath, somehow the elderly evil knew Gideon was lying, and right now was one of those times Gideon was going to lie as little as possible. 'Where?' 'Out' 'Dammit Gideon Poe, tell me where you are going this INSTANT!' Gideon ignored his squealing bark, and continued to put on his long black leather trench coat. 'Do you want this committee to kill you!? Lord knows Steph O'Connor almost did tonight' Gideon turned sharply at the question, and stared down at his petit unwanted companion. 'Do you really want me to answer that?' There was another predictable hate filled moment between the two, before Gideon stormed past the elderly evil, leaving him alone in the hotel room while Gideon went in search of some answers he had been probing for, for what seemed like an eternity. * * * * * * * * * * * * So here we are now, with Gideon experiencing his first slices of real freedom in years. Driving the Dodge Viper through the vast empty streets, the radio on as loud as possible, playing some old Guns n Roses classic with Axl Rose blaring down the microphone. He couldn't decipher all the words because his mind was too focused on being free once again, it wasn't until Rose's voice reached the chorus that Gideon realised it was 'Welcome To The Jungle'. Why the woman had prepared a Dodge Viper for Gideon to drive was beyond his knowledge, he thought the car attracted too much attention, but right now he was in no mood to complain. There was no worries in his mind, not even about how scared and anxious the girl sounded on the telephone, instead he was excited to hear what she had to say, not worried. Most of all though, there was no Jeremiah burdening him or lurking over his shoulder or sticking to his side. As Gideon continued his positive emotional ride down the empty streets, the sound of a phone ringing broke his trail of thought. He quickly turned down the radio. 'Sorry Axl' he said as he did. He then looked around the car and quickly opened the glove compartment. As he did a small cell phone fell into the palm of his hand. He looked at it confused for a few moments, before slamming the glove compartment shut and answering the call. 'Hell-' 'Where are you?' inquired the voice. 'Well..........' Gideon looked out of the car window, suddenly realising he had no idea where he was. 'Jesus Christ how hard is it for you to find a beach?!' snapped the voice. ' Listen lady, I don't who the fuc-' 'Look, just wind your windows down and listen out for music, then follow it, now hurry.' Before Gideon could ask anymore questions the line went dead. The clock on the dashboard read 9:55. Gideon quickly rolled down the windows of the car and turned the radio off completely. As if on some warped sadistic cue, the faint but instantly recognisable sound of furious guitar riffs and frantic drum beats invaded his ears. He quickly turned the car 180 degrees on the empty desolate road, and accelerated towards where the music was coming from. * * * * * * * * * * * * * * The beach was filled this night with around 200 or so teenagers, roughly between 15 -19. A small bandstand had been created near the shore where a small band local band were playing, that had been the music the mystery woman had told Gideon to look out for. Among the hormonally misguided teens were various empty trashcans with hot steaming flames shimmering from the brim, which provided a warm calm temperature and atmosphere, while the sound of the crashing waves added as an extra drumbeat to the performing band. Gideon walked casually amongst the various people, for once he seemed to fit in and not stand out. He felt at home with these people which surprised him even more, if he wasn't under the current burdens and circumstances he was at this present time, he might actually go as far as to say he was slightly enjoying himself. One miracle at a time though, eh... The Shade looked through the crowd, looking for anyone who just by appearance he could match to the voice he heard on the phone. Eventually however, he spotted a rather tall blonde girl, standing near one of the flaming trashcans looking directly at him. Gideon met her eyes and waited a moment, before the girl eventually nodded and gestured with her index finger to follow him, to which Gideon quickly responded. He followed her from about 20 yards away as she led him down further to the sea and away from the crowd, and hoped this was the actual mystery girl and not some uncontrollable horny vixen. 'Um, are you.....' Gideon suddenly found himself without a name to address the girl with as they stopped near the sea. 'Shelly'. The girl answered in the same identical voice as she did on the phone, and Gideon breathed a small sigh of relief. 'Why here?' was the first question that came into Gideon's head and the first full question that came out of his mouth. 'Why'd you think. If this committee somehow finds out where you are, our even your keeper Jeremiah, they're hardly going to do anything drastic with all these people, all these witnesses.' 'Good point. But why did you bring me here, what do you know about the committee?' 'Ha, what don't I know would probably be a better question' chuckled Shelly. 'Well for God's sakes tell me'. Shelly looked up at Gideon with a little look of hesitation, before nodding slowly. 'OK. The committee, the precious committee. Otherwise known as DC/LX/VI. I'm guessing Jeremiah has told you something about them, when they were formed and all that bullshit.. 'Yeah, yeah he did' 'Good, saves me having to explain it. Anyway, this committee has been around longer than both me and you could imagine The so called legend states that the members of this committee formed just after the first world war by a number of strong Satanists . They predicted that the end of the world would be triggered by the world war, but they were wrong. They somehow thought that they had brought Satan dishonour, and decided they would try and path things up with the old guy by trying to bring about the apocalypse themselves. Bold idea huh?' Shelly had the typical attitude of the average angst ridden girl next door, that was clear enough to spot just by the way she told the story, yet Gideon already knew 90% of what Shelly had told him, but it was reassuring to hear that he was right in his assumption. 'So the is a bunch of string Satanists who thought they brought shame on him by falsely predicting the end of the world..' 'Got it in one Colombo.' The Avatar Of Death merely nodded. Truth be told all this made a lot of sense to him, but after the life he had lived since having to burden Jeremiah O'Barr nothing much did take him by surprise anymore. 'How do you know so much about this?' Shelly's usual rebellious young teen persona dropped once again as she took a deep breath and searched for the right words. 'My father is the current leader of the committee' '....Sweet Jesus!' Shelly merely sighed at the shocked outburst from Gideon and shrugged it off quickly, almost pretending it never happened or that it didn't matter, as if she heard it all the time. 'Yeah, tell me about it, now look. The only way I traced you was because I know that their onto you already. They probably sussed out Jeremiah would tell you something and that you'd go searching for them.' Gideon looked at Shelly awkwardly before responding. The tone she spoke the information to him was at a pace that made his head hurt when taking it in, she was so calm and sure of what she was saying, that made things even more frightful. 'So their on the hunt for me already' 'Got it in one again Colombo! I don't know what you know about them, but it sure as hell must be more than they want you to know cause they aint best pleased with the progress you've been making on their little scam' Before Shelly could finish her powerful yet enthralling rant, a vibrant flash of blue hit her eyes, causing her to blink and take a step back, at the same time a bright red flash hit Gideon's eyes, causing him to do the same. Soon the flashing colours of the sirens stopped, and the police cars pulled over just near the bandstand where the band was performing. 'Shit!' 'What is it?' asked Gideon, obviously not alarmed by the sight of the authorities. 'Cops supposedly, but I'm willing to bet my Alanis Morisette CD collection that they aren't run by the legal authorities.?' 'What?!' 'Jesus, haven't you listened to anything that I've told you? Wake up! This committee, they have members in the police, government, hell, they even got Jerry Springer.' 'So this is just one big conspiracy?' 'To an extent, now quick, come on. They know it's us!' 'What?... How?!' The scene then slowly begins to fade as Shelly drags Gideon's arm as the police officers break through the crowd of misguided and stride towards their direction.
