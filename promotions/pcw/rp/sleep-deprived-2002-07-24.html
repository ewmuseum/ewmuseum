Sleep Deprived

24 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/24/02 It's the middle of the night and Kramer can't help but stir in his sleep. He moves from one side of the bed, gets uncomfortable, and moves again. He does this for a few more minutes until he sits upright and throws the covers off of his waist with a quick flick of the wrists. He rubs at his forehead while letting out a huge sigh and then slides out of his bed revealing only a pair of black boxers. After stumbling a bit he makes it out of his room and downstairs where he slumps himself onto his couch with a thud. Kramer: I have a screw job match tomorrow and I can't get any sleep! I don't want to lose again although there isn't much of a plus side to winning. I mean even if I become the number one contendor, I still don't get a one on one shot at Mason like I had wanted. No, some dumbass had to set up a match at the PPV where I have to go against a bunch of other unworthy fools. So now it won't really matter to Mason if he cheats me yet again! Damn this world can be pretty unforgiving!!! He dramatically tilts his head back and rests the back of his palm on his forehead. Then he just starts to laugh as he regains his composure. Kramer: Since this match isn't as important as once thought, I guess I am going to have to play a few cards in my favor. Muhahahahaha!! Rush steps out of line and his black ass is mine along with his homedawg roC!! Muhahahahaha!! He continues laughing until he gives himself a good old smack. Kramer: Ouch! Now I know how much pain Jeff goes through eveytime Duke slaps the taste out of his mouth! I have to be slap happy from not getting enough sleep. Either that or my mind is slowly deteriorating causing me to act like I have no sense. He continues to ramble on making very little sense. To Kramer the time continues to slowly go by which causes him to get extremely bored. He races back upstairs to see if Grimer is up. He peaks into Grimer's cage and to his dismay Grimer is fast asleep. Kramer walks out of the room and goes back downstairs to the couch. Kramer: What in the world am I suppose to do? He takes a few seconds to think of a fun activity he can do all by himself, yet nothing really exciting comes to mind. After failed attempts at wasting time, he decides to head out of the house. He puts some clothes on and gets into his truck and just drives around. A few minutes pass and he decides to drive into a parking lot. Kramer gets out of the car and looks up at the sign which reads 'Pool Hall'. The enormous motorcycles make him a bit weary, because where there are bikes, there are bikers. With that thought in his mind he still decides to go in because he doesn't want to go home. He steps in and slides off to a vacant pool table. Kramer: I'll play a few games and hopefully will get tired enough to go to sleep when I get home. He racks up the balls and is ready to break when all of a sudden a big black guy comes over and pushes the much smaller Kramer to the side. Then he and his buddies take over the table and start to play. Kramer stands there a few seconds dumbfounded until he gets the courage to confront the man. Kramer: Didn't you see I was playing? Man: This is our table. Kramer: Listen just let me play one game and then you can have it. Man: You see it just doesn't work like that. We get the table now! Kramer: Ahhhhhhh!!! Kramer slams his fists on the pool table and stares at the man with anger in his face. Kramer: Yet again a black man is cheating me!! First my title, then my ice cream, now this pool table! What in the hell is wrong with you people? Don't you have any moral bone in your body? Kramer bit off a bit more than he can chew, as the gang of men crowd around him. The scene fades to black momentarily and the comes back on. It's a few hours later and we are at Kramer's house again. In through the door comes Jason who is dressed and ready to go for Turmoil. Jason: Kramer I was thinking maybe we should get there early.... He notices Kramer's slumped head and walks around to the front of the couch. There Kramer lays, with a bit of drool hanging from his mouth, and a big black eye. He looks content as he finally managed to get some rest. The scene fades to black.
