Dorn Tracer vs. The Greek Gods: Special Edition

10 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 - 10 - 03 The field looks as though the Third Great War has struck it. Smoldering craters of burning shrapnel and dead bodies litter the landscape like foam on the sea. Trees are burnt to their trunks, smoking like factory smoke stacks and falling to the ash-covered terrain below. There’s an eerie quiet settling over the land as a hungry mist consumes the remaining pieces of sanity floating about the ghostly hills. The stench of death wafting through the charred blades of grass causes the clouds to cringe and part, casting strangle gray beams of sunlight to the ground below. What could this ghastly landscape be? What happened here? What flaming stampede of elephants came stomping through? While it’s not elephants, it’s something awfully close judging by the rumbling sound that now begins to resonate over the horizon. The rumbling noise starts off slowly and quietly, almost like a minor tremor an hour after an earthquake… but it begins to rise. Bits of earth cracked off from the ground begin to bounce about like anxious insects from the quaking tremor. The shaking begins to get more violent and is heralded by a bright orange glow growing forth from the horizon. Rising like the mighty sun itself, the glow becomes larger and larger, illuminating the land and air around it. The rumbling gets louder and more enraged as the aura grows, until… HUZZZZAAAAHH!!!!! Bursting forth from the horizon like a horizontal lightning bolt is a stampede of 6 black stallions, blowing fire from their nose and racing across the landscape with unimaginable fluidity. An intimidating chariot races behind them, attached to the ropes harnessing the horses together. The cart of doom sports steel wheel spokes with razor sharp spikes and set of saw blades sticking out from both sides of the chariot. But perhaps the damnedest thing of the entire set is the rider: Wearing a black Grecian-style helmet and matching obsidian plate armor, the figure raises a spear above his head and whips his horses into a frenzy, calling out things furiously in another language. As if that wasn’t enough, another comes racing over the horizon; only it’s a brunette-haired vixen behind the ropes. ‘CHARGE, MY STEEDS!’ She cries in a mystical accent, her gray eyes shining beneath her golden helmet. Her chariot parallels with the man’s and the two race down a large, charred hill to the dipping valley below. At the apex of the hill from whence they came stands a tall, strong man with a white, flowing beard and shining blue eyes. What appears to be a lightning bolt caught in stasis sits in his hand and a dark aura covers his majestic eyes. The old, yet strong looking figure gazes down at the racing chariot’s below and frowns, ‘I fear for their safety. Apollo and Poseidon were crushed. What makes you think Aries and Pallas Windsor can succeed?’ A beautiful women wearing a long, flowing robe of gold and a matching crown steps up beside him and shakes her head, ‘You’re such a paranoid God, darling. Your brother was a pussy anyway, and Apollo was doped up before the battle, you need not worry about Aries and Pallas Windsor, though.’ ‘What makes you say that?’ The ‘god’ booms, his voice rumbling like the earth beneath the hooves of the mighty steeds below. ‘Poseidon was a mighty warrior! He was lulled into thinking that our opposition was weak through false messages and illusions! It was trickery!’ With a scoff, the woman shakes her head and stares at the ground, ‘It was a damn sly trick is what it was. And your brother believed every bit of it. That man was quite a master at disguising that mammoth machine. But since we know of it now, Pallas Windsor and Aries have this one in the bag.’ ‘I hope you’re right…’ The burly, thunderous god says, staying out at the opposite horizon. As his words drift off with the wind and the clouds of dust from the racing horses begin to dissipate, another rumbling noise begins to rise forth from the other side of the land. Both the god and goddess bring their gaze up and stare out over the horizon… winded, almost FRIGHTENED words escaping their lips in dark harmony… He’s coming… As Pallas Windsor and Aries race towards their ill-fated destination, a giant, metallic creature with a HUGE nose and mechanized tracks comes rolling over the tip of the opposite hill, stopping at the top with a whistling sigh and a release of steam. The gargantuan machine of death moves what appears to be a stout cannon about and angles it down towards the charging chariots. The black and silver tank-like machine suddenly begins to hum, starting quietly then becoming louder with each passing second. A pink light begins to emerge from the tip of the cannon, and the chariots come to a grinding halt below. ‘What in the name of Zeus is that??’ Aries cries to Pallas Windsor with a hint of panic in his voice. His female companion turns to him and shrugs. ‘I haven’t a clue, but-‘ She doesn’t get the chance to finish however, as her words are interrupted by the shrieking ray of piercing pink light as it soars from the cannon towards the two warriors. Pallas Windsor dives from her chariot as the destructive laser comes crashing down, but Aries isn’t so lucky… ‘AAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGGG!!’ ‘ARIES!!!’ The burly bearded God of Thunder cries from atop the hill, his eyes bugging out with horror. He reaches out, as if to whisk his warrior away from the impending death via massive laser, but not even his powers can pull away the God of War. The woman beside Zeus holds him back and watches in horror as the chariots explode, the horses are turned to black giblets, and Aries is totally disintegrated. Pallas Windsor pulls herself up from the charred ground and stares in horror at the carnage that has ensued. In the distance, she can hear the cries of Zeus as he grieves the loss of Aries. ‘I must avenge him…’ She mumbles to herself, her voice filled with hatred and venomous intention. She stares up at the Lunar Tank as it lies unmoving upon the hilltop, as if asserting its dominance over everything it sees. Pallas Windsor draws her mighty blade from her jeweled scabbard and begins to quickly ascend the hill towards the Astrological war machine. In that same tank sits ‘The Astronomer’ Dorn Tracer, wearing a black and gray army helmet and smoking a big Cuban cigar. One hand is firmly grasped on one of the acceleration levels, the other hovering just above the ‘Fire’ button. Wearing a huge, maniacal grin on his face, Dorn laughs through his cigar and shakes his head, ‘Damn infidels just never learn. Looks like one of them wants to play one on one. I suppose I should go out there, play fairly, face her man-to-man… but who in the name of En’Flarim told them I’d EVER play fair?’ With that, Dorn lets out another sinister chuckle and slams his palm down on the fire button. The tank gives a low hum that quickly increases in volume before conjuring up a pink aura at the end of the cannon. ‘NOOOOOOOOOO!! We must save her!’ Cries the Thunder God in the distance, witnessing the charging up of the Lunar Tank’s cannon. The woman, presumably Hera, sighs and shakes her head in dismissal, ‘No Zeus, we can’t. We must let Pallas Windsor do this on her own. She’s quite capable of destroying the Astronomer and his machine. Zeus turns his frantic gaze towards his wife and says, ‘But what if she fails??’ Hera merely shakes her head again. *************************************** Climbing the hill with unbelievable zest and blind rage, Pallas Windsor has only HEARD the powering up of the Lunar Tank’s main cannon. But that doesn’t deter her a bit. Her determination and lust for revenge is a driving force mightier than that of the Astronomer’s war machine, or even a hundred of them. This is her chance to prove that she can do it on her own, without the help of Zeus or Aries or anyone. ‘I can do it… this bastard is mine!’ She hisses to herself, completely ready for the challenge ahead. The Lunar Tank is only about twenty more feet up, but the cannon is angled enough that it could very well explode her mid-way… The Goddess of War and Wisdom continues to climb until the humming sound from the Lunar Tank turns into an ear-splitting shriek. Pallas Windsor only has a moment to duck as that ominous pink laser blasts forth from the barrel and soars towards her at blinding speed… … only to pass right over her and obliterate the surface of the hill about 10 feet below her. Her body is tossed and rumbled from the impact of the cannon’s laser, but other than that, she’s holding on steadfast. ‘You can’t get rid of me that easily!!’ Pallas Windsor cries in righteous fury, hoping Dorn Tracer heard her. She scrambles up the rest of the hill quickly and stands face to face with the Lunar Tank. A feeling of anxiety, fear, and adrenaline courses through her body as she prepares to face the mighty weapon. Inside the tank, Dorn Tracer is quite aware that Pallas Windsor has made it to the top, but isn’t overly concerned. ‘It’s not she can get in here anyw-‘ He’s interrupted by the sound of a sharp metallic THUMP noise above him. The Astronomer turns his gaze to the steel roof above him and drops his jaw in confusion. ‘What in the name of Desima was that?!’ He turns his gaze back to the radar and gasps when he sees that the little blip representing Pallas Windsor is right on top of the Lunar Tank. But as he remembers that she probably won’t find her way in, he leans back in his chair and smiles. ‘Come and get me…’ *************************************** ‘SHOW YOURSELF, COWARD!’ Pallas Windsor cries, looking about the tank for a way in. She realizes he probably won’t respond, so she sets out to find the entrance to the tank. Before she can even budge, however, four square hatches open, one on each corner of the tank, and sprout guns! ‘Uh oh…’ The guns are whip around and point towards her, aiming down a little as not to shoot each other, and begin to unload. Pat-pat-pat-pat-pat-pat-pat-pat-pat-pat-pat-pat! The machine guns spit lead like crazy, causing Pallas Windsor to flip and jump about to various parts of the tank, trying to avoid their line of fire. Like bees to honey, the guns follow her with their aim, even when she hides behind large offshoots on the surface of the tank. ‘This is insane!!’ She gasps to herself. ‘I’ve got to find a way to get rid of those things!!’ She back-flips onto the lower tier of the tank, but finds that the guns closest to her can still angle themselves down towards her. A feeling of defeat begins to form in her head… until… ‘Woah, what’s that?!’ She gasps upon seeing the large cylinder object firmly attached to the tank. It has a triangular Danger sign on it, and when she sees that, a long grin crosses Pallas Windsor’s face. ‘Perfect!’ As the guns finish reloading and resume their fire, Pallas Windsor dives to the bottom tier of the tank and raises her sword into the air. A bolt of lightning suddenly soars from the sky and strikes her sword as she brings it down on the cylinder object, embedding it deep into the metal. The guns stop firing and the machine gives an elongated sigh. What happened? Pallas Windsor wonders, looking around. The tank doesn’t move and the guns sit dormant, their barrels smoking from the rapid-fire sessions. Suddenly, the entire tank begins to rumble violently. In a panic, Pallas Windsor dives off the machine and rolls across the ground. She turns around to look at the tank and smiles as a hatch on the top flies open and a dark figure steps out. The figure sprints down the top tier of the tank and leaps off with all the force he can muster just as the tank goes open in a cataclysmic explosion. The Goddess of Wisdom covers her head and braces herself as bits and pieces of flaming steel fly pour from the sky like hellish rain. When she no longer feels the paining bite of the debris, she stands up and stares at the flaming wreckage of the Lunar Tank, smoke bellowing into the air. An exhausted grin crosses her face as she looks on at her handiwork, ‘I did it! All by myself…’ ‘Too bad you won’t be able to enjoy that fluke victory.’ Pallas Windsor turns around and her smile dips into an enraged frown as she gazes upon the vision of ‘The Astronomer’ himself, his arms folded across his face and a pair of broken shades on his ash-covered face. The scowl on his face could curdle milk as he returns her angry gaze, ‘Leave it to a woman to find the easy way out by stabbing the fuel tank. But no matter, your little string of achievement ends HERE, Pallas Windsor.’ Pallas Windsor stands tall against the Astronomer and steps forward towards, prepared to meet Dorn Tracer in a one-on-one, dragged out affair with no outside influence. The Astronomer also steps forward, ready to meet the Goddess head on. Pallas Windsor drops her sword and steps forward. Dorn Tracer throws his broken shades down and steps forward to her. The battle begins… … and ends this Tuesday. [End.]
