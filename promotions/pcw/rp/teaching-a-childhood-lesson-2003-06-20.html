Teaching a Childhood Lesson

20 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/20/03 Ever since I boarded the PCW airship on the evening of the seventh of June there had been one thing I had been meaning to do but I had never gotten around to, spending time with family. I had brought along my adopted son Evan, and my eight year old niece, Christa to get away from the stress created by my mother, yet now I felt a certain guilt in bringing them aboard the ship each and every time I was with them simply because I had unknowingly put them in a worse situation that being with my mom, now we were stuck aboard a ship sharing a room with the man I was regretfully married to, Gaz Green. It was because of that I hadn’t gotten to spend the time with the children I would have liked to. Instead the majority of the time they spent without me inside the twenty four hour daycare service just so that I could keep them away from Gaz. The last thing I had wanted was for the children to meet Gaz, especially Evan who would be convinced that just because Gaz and I were married Gaz was his father now. The idea of just that happening disgusted me even more than the thought the pizza boy suggesting to Sivasian that I would be the perfect woman for him. The only place aboard the ship where I could escape from it all was a place where I knew neither of them would dare to go, the twenty four hour daycare center where I had left the two children I had legal custody of. Not only would I be taking care of the Gaz and Sivasian problem, but I’d also be able to spend time with the children as well, it was perfect, but of course I’d have to be in the company of others children which wasn’t always the greatest thing since they all seemed to only want one thing, attention. “Good morning Ms. Windsor.” Greeted the woman who ran the center as I walked through the door leading to the center with a smile hoping that today would be one of those rare good days. “Nice to see you.” I replied to her with a friendly smile and a wave before walking past her to the room where Christa and Evan were both playing together with two other girls, one who looked to be Christa’s age and one who looked to be Evan’s age. “Tessa!” Cried Evan who spotted me first, perhaps because he wasn’t as engrossed in playing with Barbie’s as Christa was simply because he was raised around his father telling him that playing with Barbie’s was wrong because he was a boy, and it was because of that to this day the four year old was still hesitant to get near anything that you dressed up. “Hey Evan.” I said as I embraced him with a hug before walking over to Christa and sitting down beside her on the carpeted floor as she ignored my presence and continued to play along with her friends. Evan not wanting to get involved in the game any longer and now having a reason to not want to play decided that my lap would be the place to stay so without another word the four year old plunked himself onto my lap and watched on with a yawn to signify his displeasure for this game. “Evan, will you be Ken for us?” Christa asked as she picked up the only male doll of the bunch and handed it towards Evan who stared down at the doll winkling his eyes and then looked up at Christa trying to answer as sincerely as possible. “No, I don’t want to play anymore.” He said as he took the doll from Christa and then placed it beside the other bunch of dolls that were not being used at the moment. “I’ll be Ken.” Proclaimed the high pitched voice which sounded more like a squeak. The voice belonged to the little red headed child who looked to be around Evan’s age who seemed eager to play as Ken. The girl jumped up from her seat on the carpet and dashed over to the pile of dolls beside Evan grabbing the Ken doll as a smile crept across her face, before the interruption of Christa. “Megan you can’t be Ken!” Christa announced like a parent enforcing the rules of the household as she snatched the doll from the little girls grasp. “Why not?” Cried Megan, her little blue eyes almost in tears from the theft of her Ken doll. “Because you’re a girl and Ken is a guy!” Snarled Christa as if Megan had broken the ultimate law of childhood, never play with a toy meant for or of the opposite gender. “Now Christa she can be Ken if she likes, there is nothing wrong with that.” I jumped in with gentle words like a parent or a caregiver would at a time like this. “But Tessa, Ken is a boy!” Christa argued back wanting her point to be seen, and not wanting to lose a debate that she started and had insisted she was right about. “So? Let me tell you something Christa. Professional wrestling used to be and is still considered a sport that only guys should participate in, but do you think that stopped me from become a wrestler which I had always wanted to be?” I laid the guilt on her feeling rather guilty that I was picking on an eight year old but at the same time I felt that this would be the only way she would learn that people should be allowed to do and be whatever they want to be. “But Tessa you are a good wrestler which is why you have the Television Championship. That is why they signed you.” Christa argued back in hopes that she could save her point of view from being demolished. “But I wasn’t Television Champion before they signed me now was I?” I questioned knowing that the little girl could not argue with that. “Now let Megan be Ken.” I said hoping that this would convince Christa to allow the little red head to use the Ken doll, and sure enough it did and their play continued on. “Girls.” Evan signed, trying to be a little comedian. I just laughed at his remarks and nodded my head as my lesson came back to strike me and made me realize that on Fusion I along with Miss Moonlight and Lacuna Debris would try to teach SOCIETY and Sivasian that same lesson, the only question that remained inside my mind now was if I would be as successful teaching two grown men that same lesson, or would I fail and continue on my losing streak?
