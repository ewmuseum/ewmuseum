The Flashbacks [ChapterTwo] - Escape The Sanatorium

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th of June, 2002 With an innocent expression upon face, Isiy edged around another corner in the Hospitals interior. The place was unimaginably huge and it seemed as though she’d never find her required destination, that being the nurse’s locker room. A tall man wearing a white lab coat pushed a long silver medical trolley past her, so suddenly that she almost jumped, a fatal move that could have given her reason for being here away. She sighed a breath of relief as the man passed her without a flinch of his face and without any questioning of her secret struggle to find the nurses cloakroom. “Where the hell is it!” she said, loud enough for only her own ears to pick up. She peered around the corner again to her own dismay. She couldn’t find it anywhere! And she’d been looking for nearly an hour now. Titu would be wondering where the heck she was, he was an impatient man after all and time was of the essence here! Isiy stood, her ears and eyes being her ideal tools for this particular job, and gazed. Her ears, however, were beginning to let her down for she was quite unexpectedly startled by the booming, yet womanish voice of someone, or something behind her. “Can I help you at all?” said the voice, deeply gruff, but with a womanly charm. She turned quickly with a jump; her heart pounding like a drum beat…and smiled. Isiy gawked at the person in front of her and stared up and up and up and down again in shock. A hoarse laugh followed. “No, no. I’m perfectly fine, thanks!” The nurse eyed her up and down suspiciously. “Aren’t you…the wife… of that wrestler who was brought in about two and a half months ago?” The nurse was a huge woman. A mountain of bluish grey, carefully topped at its peak with a small tuft of brown curls. Isiy seemed inferior in size to the woman, and we call her a woman only because that is what one would automatically believe her to be, considering she’s wearing a nurses uniform, and is attempting to be of some vague assistance within the confines of the hospital. “Erm…well, yes…” said Isiy beginning to blush slightly. She didn’t want to be asked these sorts of questions, her Plan for Titu’s escape was something she has no intention of giving out, but nevertheless she was a coy woman and blushing was part of her persona; she couldn’t help it. The simplistic humanly flaw in her character didn’t seem to intrigue the mountain of a woman at all, and she simply nodded in response. “Well, if you don’t mind me asking Mrs-?” “Xansiv,” Isiy informed “Xansiv,” the nurse repeated, “what are you doing all this way from your husbands room?” Isiy had to think fast; if she gave a stupid answer to a simple question like this then suspicions would be aroused. “Oh…I was…err, just looking for a coffee machine that actually worked, I can’t seem to find any in my husbands ward.” She said, adding a shy laugh to give her answer a more believable feel. “Ah,” began the monstrous nurse “well if you turn a left here you should find one towards the end of the corridor.” Isiy thanked her with a beaming smile and then continued her search for the nurse’s locker room. That was a close shave, she thought. It was a good job she had remembered the sign on the broken coffee machine to the left of the hospitals entrance, though it had been almost two weeks since she’d seen it for the first time. She smiled as she turned another corner and to her surprise and utter joy she found a door featuring a gold pate entitled ‘Nurse’s - Personnel Only’. Obviously she had found what she was looking for. The Pharaoh sat nursing his still aching head. He couldn’t believe how long it had taken for him to get to this stage of his rehabilitation. His head had hurt a lot worse when he was first admitted, although some of the doctors had began to say that it was worse nowadays and although the pain had slowly subsided he still had the throbbing aching headache that had plagued him for weeks now. He looked up towards the door. Isiy certainly had been a while. Where could she be? Many questions flooded through his mind… to his annoyance and pain. Thinking made his head hurt more, and the doctor’s had recommended that he did little with his brain on the intellectual side for a few weeks. Pharaoh wasn’t built like that. He enjoyed reading, and loved expanding his mind and so being the stubborn human he was he simply ignored the doctors orders. When you come to think about it a lot more people than one would first expect ignored the ‘docs’ these days, they had been down-classed to a form of annoyance that everyone seemed to rather do without. Then again they did have their up sides - like the red lollies they used to give you as a child, now that was when they came in handy! Unfortunately that side of them wears off quite quickly…and then they become irritating. Ever since Spring Fling things hadn’t been going Pharaoh’s way. He had hoped to retain his TV title and go on to become one of the greatest TV champions in PCW history, but alas things had not gone as planned - he’d taken a bad, bad bump to the head, lost his title and had been out of action for a long time. Things in PCW had changed too. Isiy had told him…but he didn’t believe her, or didn’t want to. Apparently The Phantom Booker had returned to his ‘throne’ and many new stars had been signed, shaking the title divisions considerably. How would he be able to fit in again? He’d been out of action so long; half the guy’s he’d known before the new roster had been announced were gone… He sighed quietly to himself. “I need to get back there! I need to make my name known again…” he said, almost silently. Pharaoh was an ambitious man, anyone could tell that… It was the sort of -in lack of a better word- ‘vibe’ he had about him. He was electrically charged for immediate action, and was one to always compete in anything that looked like he might have a chance of winning. The matches he had wrestled so far in PCW gave that away. His first ever TV title defence was one for the books. He defeated Akira Akiyama, one of his most ferocious and hated enemies, in what could be called a battle of epically proportioned pride. He didn’t like this hospital. He knew that. It was keeping him back. He wanted back in PCW and the sooner he got out of this ‘sanctuary’ the sooner he’d be back on the road to wrestling glory! Now that he had lost the TV title he knew he’d have to make some sort of huge impact upon his return, and he had a brilliant plan to make sure he did just that. His return to PCW would be, to say the least, ‘enough’ to get his career back on the ‘right’ track… He only needed to be booked and then he could get his plan into full swing. And what a plan it was… He had gone over it hundreds of time, and upon his return he would finally get a chance to reveal a certain ‘difference maker’ to the world of professional wrestling that he was sure would help him to rise to fame… He sat thinking…and it hurt, badly…but he didn’t care. No pain, no game, as they said. He laughed manically for a second, which seemed to bring interest from outside the room inside. There stood a doctor. “Is everything alight, Mr Xansiv?” Pharaoh broodingly looked up, the same thoughtful smile that had painted itself upon his handsome visage so many times before had been expertly brushed on once again. “Yes, Doctor… I’m more than fine…” The man of medical genius looked the Egyptian wrestler up and down in curiosity… “And how…is your head…feeling?” he said thoughtfully, stroking his chin. A sharp pain shot through Pharaohs skull, just above his eyebrow…He yelped in distress… ‘Absolutely perfect timing’ he thought, ironically. “I see,” said the doctor whilst turning to a group of nurses outside The Pharaoh’s room’s door. “Erm, I believe Mr Xansiv might need some more medication for his head aches…perhaps we could up his dosage?” The Doctor turned back to The Jewel. “Do not worry Mr Xansiv, this should help the aches and pains you’re suffering…” The Doctor motioned for a nurse to enter. It was the monstrously huge one that Isiy had encountered earlier on in our tale. In her bulky hands she held a tiny bottle of pills, which shook wildly as she walked, making a loud crackling sound as each pill bounced up and hit the side of its container. Pharaoh gulped as she moved towards him, with a sickening smile. “Now, if you’d just put this to the back of your tongue-” “Yes, yes I know the routine!” sighed Pharaoh in annoyance. He picked up the glass of water on his bedside table and looked on waiting for Isiy to arrive. Isiy heard a faint clank outside the door. She froze in sudden contemplation and quickly thought of a plan. Suddenly the huge wooden entrance to the nurse’s cloakroom creaked open and in walked a pair of women, both of which were dressed in nurse’s uniforms. Unknowing of Isiy’s presence they talking amongst themselves. Isiy immediately ran towards the far end of the room looking around frantically for what she needed. Conveniently she found a locker filled with a nurse’s uniform. Just what she wanted! She had to get dressed quickly though, and quietly mind you, and all before the two nurses spotted her… Suspicions would be aroused if she was caught and that would certainly be bad if she wished to help her husband’s plan of escape to come true. Thankfully they hadn’t noticed her yet; it was, after all, a huge cloakroom. They chatted until they heard the sound of feet shuffling across the floor. They turned and stared into the dull light at the other side of the room. They slowly walked towards the shape of Isiy getting dressed. They moved like assassins, as quietly as a cat, as Isiy’s breathing got heavier. Time was running out for the Egyptian enchantress. Pharaoh was peaceful now… The pain had seemingly gone… The upping of his medication had sorted out those pesky headaches! That was for sure! And now all he felt like doing was falling asleep. He rested his head down on the cushiony pillow beneath his cranium and sighed a cheerful breath of relief. It felt good to be calm and at peace again. The headaches were annoying and he didn’t like them one bit…who would, apart from Mick Foley, perhaps? Finally he shut his eyes and drifted off, as one does when you haven’t been able to sleep in a while because of a sudden pain that feels like your skull has been ripped open and a thousand shards of razor sharp glass have been dropped, from a great height mind you, into your unsuspecting brain below. He was content now, or so it seemed… He saw a man, the very same man he had done in the dream he had had a day ago. He stood with a smile looking down upon a small boy, much bigger than the one he had previously seen in his dream, but a small boy nevertheless. The lad was around the age of ten years. He was scrawny but cheerful looking and a glitter was in his eyes. “Here you go, son,” said the man with an eager smile. Out of nowhere the man seemed to bring forth a parcel tightly wrapped in a red and gold paper. “I hope you like it!” The boy playfully unwrapped the present in his hands with a joyful grin. The box that had been concealed within its red and gold boundaries earlier was now fully exposed. The letters were dull and Titu couldn’t quite make them out but the boy took a small chisel out of the box and a microscope soon followed. “Oh, dad! This is great! Thank you!” The boy ran up to the man, yet he seemed to run forever and then he was alone… He looked around and nobody was there. The man, who, from the boy’s words, we’ll presume was his father, was nowhere to be seen. He stood in a land that appeared to be…Egypt? Yes, Titu knew this place… it was the Valley of the Kings! Xansiv rolled over in his sleep and thought he could remember something, just on the brink of his mind…but alas…he awoke from his dream, the second of its kind… “So how long have you been working here?” said the voice of woman. Isiy had been discovered, but with better consequences than she had first imagined. “Well around two weeks to be honest, I’m kinda new.” She laughed. The two nurses joined in on the friendly chuckle. “Well we’ve been here for nearly two years. In our time here we’ve seen some terrible things! Once a man came in with a-” Isiy interrupted. “If you don’t mind I really should be going, I have to get down to-” She paused, then she remembered the number of Titu’s ward. “- Ward six…this wrestler got brought in with concussion weeks ago.” She said confidently. She looked down at the watch tightly strapped to her wrist and nodded before looking up at the two nurses stood in front of her. To them she was just another nurse, nothing special, and certainly not someone who had simply stolen a nurses uniform to ‘rescue’ her husband from the dungeon-like hospital- no, that thought never crossed their minds. “Yep, I defiantly have to get going…” she conceded. “Well alright then… Isabella? Right?” “Yeah, Isabella.” Isiy verified. “Well, nice to meet you…” said the second nurse. Isiy turned back towards them as she left the room. “And you too!” The Charm shut the door behind her and breathed a deep pocket of relief-filled air - now to get Titu out of here. Back in the cloakroom the two other nurses chatted. “Nice girl, that…” “Yeah…yeah, she was.” “Funny though,” The second nurse’s brow wrinkled. “What is?” “Well, I ain’t never seen her ‘till now…” The second nurse wordlessly concurred. “Ah well, let’s get back to what we ‘really’ came here to do.” “Yeah!” The first nurse, a fairly well built woman, prised open a random locker. “Bloody hell! Cynthia’s been hiding a secret supply of hot chocolate from us all this time!” It was clear from her booming voice that she was outraged. “Well!” she began. “The absolute nastiness of it! And to think…all the ‘nice’ things we’ve done for her!” She carefully closed the locker again, and sighed. “Just shows you can’t trust ‘anyone’ these days!” The other nurse nodded as they both left the cloakroom. “Outrage!” she sniffed in disgust. Titu looked up towards the door in front of him. The clock on the small table next to his bed ticked almost silently in his ear. Isiy had been gone for an hour or so now. And now he was nervous- nervous about the strange dreams. What did they mean? Why had he started to have them? So many questions now flowed smoothly through his head and he was so divulged in deep thought that he didn’t notice the pain from the concussion, which had plagued him for weeks now, had not returned as it had done many times before. And then it happened… The room’s door slowly creaked open and the head of his gorgeous wife, Isiy, popped around the corner. “At last…” he murmured. “Well I got caught up with a few nurses in their cloakroom, Titu, please forgive me, won’t you, for trying to help you?” she said sarcastically. Pharaoh went a little red and then slowly dragged himself into an upright position on the bed. “I’m…sorry, my love. Now help me to get dressed.” Isiy walked across the room towards a small black bag in its left hand corner. She unzipped it quickly and took out a few items of clothing for The Pharaoh. They were fairly normal everyday casual clothing, which was unusual considering that Titu usually wore a long silk loincloth and a crown made from the purest gold. “Excellent. All you have to do now is simply escort me out of the building as though you were simply being a helpful nurse!” he said with a brooding grin. “Are you sure this plan will work?” “Of course I am, Isiy!” Pharaoh snapped. “I have the utmost confidence in this escape! Now come along, we have to be out of here before the nurses arrive with …today’s… meal…” He shuddered. “Hospital…food…” he murmured faintly, with a gulp. Pharaoh and Isiy stepped outside Titu’s room and peered cautiously into the winding halls of the hospital. Pharaoh’s head, since he had arrived at the hospital, had never felt as good as it did right now, and it was thanks to the upping of his dosage of medicine. But deep in the mysterious recesses of his soul Pharaoh knew that the migraines would be back - he just had a feeling… “My head feels fine,” he stated with joy. “And everything looks clear up ahead, my love, now lets get the hell out of this- this-” He tried to think of an effectively useful word to describe the ordinary hospital in a moment like this. “-Place.” He finished, lamely. How incredibly poor was that for an attempt at describing something that he really hated? ‘Very’ would be the ideal word to answer a question like that… Isiy nodded her head. She slowly crept half way down the turquoise corridors in front of them and then turned to Titu, gave him a hand motion to follow her and then continued walking. Reluctantly Titu followed his wife’s movements. When they got out of here Pharaoh knew exactly what he was going to do, he was going to ring PCW headquarters straight away! He was going back to work as soon as possible! He actually felt the ‘need’ to wrestle again. He knew this time he could be more successful than ever before, and he had a new ambition in mind… He followed Isiy along the hallways for five minutes and then eventually the pair came to the hospitals reception area. A pair of shifty-looking nurses stood in the background of the reception whilst a beautiful woman, obviously the receptionist, sat upon a small black chair behind the desk, which seemed to house more than the recommended amount of papers and forms, and ink spillages for that matter. Her glasses dropped down the bridge of her nose in a highly classic seductive-receptionist way. She looked up from her work, a flustered expression painted upon her visage. “Nurse? Where are you taking that man?” Isiy turned and smiled calmly. She had to act naturally or else all hell could break loose. “He got lost, I was just showing him the way out.” said Isiy. Silently, and to herself, she breathed a sigh of relief. “Alright then-“ The receptionist looked back down at the notes on her desk and sighed, her pen had leaked yet again… “-just make sure he knows the way out next time.” Pharaoh laughed, as did Isiy, as did the receptionist, but that isn’t to say that Pharaoh was amused, in fact…he wasn’t… ‘Nurse’ Isiy led Pharaoh out of the hospitals electric double-doors, but not without getting her foot stuck in the easily exited machine, and smiled at him as they walked through the parking lot, with a slight limp. “Ah!” said Pharaoh whilst taking in a deep pocket of fresh, and I say fresh meaning fume filled and polluted just like most cities these days, air. “Finally…we have escaped the sanctuary!” Isiy looked back on her not-so-fond memories of the hospital. “Yes,” she began. “Finally.”
