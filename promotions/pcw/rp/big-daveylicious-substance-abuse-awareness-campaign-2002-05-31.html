Big Daveylicious Substance Abuse Awareness Campaign

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/31/02 *Monologue*Unemployed. No gold, no money, no women, and no name in the lights. The dreams of Big Daveylicious quickly ended after being fired by the PCW but to no surprise he was rehired. But among that he has comeback with no gold. The Extreme title was his first taste of PCW gold. Having not tasted gold in 7 months made him starving. He feasts for 36 days and than what does the PCW do? Soon after he defeated his ex-friend, Mike Frampton to determine who is the Best FSWer in the PCW he was fired like the rest of the PCW roster at the Supremacy. Costing him the chance to beat Dan Hollywood's 41 Extreme title reign. His dreams faded. Dreams to reappear when your rehired. Unfortunately, with this they created a hungry, Big D. They left him in the cold with no gold to hold. He did as he was told. But now he wants to get his gold and be bold. This Friday on Fusion he gots the opportunity to win back his Extreme title. The title that broke his friendship with Mike Frampton. The title that he beat Chef, Clyde, Akiyama, and Jay Sylo. The same title he kept for 36 days. Where do they fight? Inside of a school. How appropriate. Just think of what a wonderful education of ass kicking does Mr. Daveylicious display for the classroom. Oh, I don't want to know what his detention is! Quite possible the greatest punishment of them all making us watch a reruns of Karl LeBlanc promos! Didn't expect that? I didn’t think so. (The scene fades into a control room of a studio. In the control room sits Michael Graves and the producer is looking through the sound proof glass at a man on a stool looking towards the ground in a wrinkly Armani suit, a black beard, and bleached blonde hair. He holds a paper in one hand and a beer bottle in the other a microphone towards him. Precisely this drunk is our Extreme champion, Big Daveylicious or maybe I should say was.) Producer: Okay, Big D. Were gonna try it once more. Just remember introduce yourself and read what's on the paper in front of you. Still got it? Big Daveylicious: Yeah, yeah, yeah, I got it. Producer:Okay, Big Daveylicious Substance Abuse Awareness Campaign. This is take nine. Big Daveylicious: Hi, this is Big Daveylicious of Miami, Florida. Kids, when you drink and drive... Producer: No, Dave. Cut, cut. It's not when you drink and drive. Big Daveylicious: Well, let me do it again. Producer: Yeah, good idea. Big Daveylicious Substance Abuse Awareness Campaign. This is take ten. Big Daveylicious: Hi, this is Big Daveylicious. When I drink and drive I.... Producer: Cut, Dave, cut! This spot is supposed to be against driving intoxicated. Big Daveylicious: ...What? Producer: Nevermind. Uh...we'll do it one more time. Now, remember say 'Don't do drugs and if you must drink this holiday weekend don't drive. Got it? Big Daveylicious: I told you I got it. Producer: Big Daveylicious Substance Abuse Awareness. This is what?...Take eleven? Big Daveylicious: Hi, I'm Big Daveylicious telling you if you do drugs this weekend don't drive. Producer: Wrong again, Dave! It's don't do drugs! DON'T DO DRUGS! Big Daveylicious: That's what I said. Producer:No, it's not. That's not even close to what you said. Big Daveylicious: You come in this booth and I'll show you how close it was, Sherlock! Producer: Were gonna do it again, Dave. Say 'Don't do drugs. If you must drink this holiday weekend don't drive.' Big Daveylicious: This is Big Daveylicious. Holidays are for drunks. Do drugs. Producer:Uh...thanks, Dave. I think with a little editing it should be just perfect. Big Daveylicious: Editing?! It’s perfect if I do say Mister Wizard of Oz. Can you take me home back to Auntie Em and give my friend a brain. (The producer looks at a snoring Graves lips on the window of the control room. We than zoom into Graves as into his white skin fading to white and than black and blue and maybe even poop.)
