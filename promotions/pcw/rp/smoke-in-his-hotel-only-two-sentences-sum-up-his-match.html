Smoke in his hotel, only two sentences sum up his match...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Previously Recorded: October 14, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up within what appears to be a hotel room. The lights on the room are cut on and you can view the entire room from the position of the camera. There are several Japanese style paintings on the wall, but the hotel looks mainly like that of an American hotel room. There is a single bed up against the wall and a pretty good-sized TV with a VCR attachment to it. The TV is on and is cut on the hotel Pay Per View section someone was obviously searching through the movies to see what they might want to watch. The shower is heard in the other room where someone must be taking a shower, of course. The camera turns to face the bathroom door, which you can now see a shining light coming out of the cracks around the edges of the door. The showering sound stops and you can now hear the coughing of a man coming from the other side of the door as they must have a cold, or have been smoking something. A few seconds go by and then the door opens up and steam mixed with green smoke emerges through the door first. Then a man dressed in what appears to be night clothes walks through the door, in his mouth is a huge blunt, which is lit and emitting a green smoke, like that of what rolled out through the bathroom door with the steam. The man is none other then Semore Smoke as you can now tell as the steam starts to settle away from his face. He walks forward and turns to head towards the bed. He sits down and takes a huge puff from the blunt. He lets the smoke swirl through his lungs and then he takes the blunt and lays it in an ashtray that is seated on the table next to the bed. Smoke lets the smoke out through his mouth and then grabs the remote to the TV and turns the channel from the hotel set up. He passes a few nothing channels that are local Japanese news channels and other such. He finds a channel that interests him though. On the channel is an episode of Dragonball Z, in fact, it's an episode he's never seen yet. But then again, he knows that they view at least a season of Dragonball Z's ahead of Americans. On the TV, you see Goku fighting what appears to be another Buu type creature, except this one if obviously blind. As Smoke watches the TV, he then imagines that the two people who are fighting, blood and all, are himself and The End. Skylar Green, also known as The End, is the blind Martian, and Semore Smoke is the good guy of the show, the one that every Japanese boy and most American boys idolize to be like, Goku. After analyzing the show for a few seconds he realizes that it wouldn't be worth watching if he couldn't understand a word of it, so he changes the channel. He goes just one channel up and sees something that really interests him. It appears that Skylar has taped yet another promo to prep for their match later the next night, and this one was going live! Skylar is speaking of Semore Smoke with a bunch of ninjas, Smoke had never realized that The End was a trainer for ninjas so he finds this interesting. One of the ninjas appears to be training for a stint in Primetime Championship Wrestling, in fact, he even asks about killing Semore after The End 'beats' him. Smoke just chuckles a little as he watches the promo on TV. He can't believe that Skylar has sunken to the low of bringing in some of his ninja pals into their match. It's just a one-time deal, for his title, and he'll come out the victor as he has the biggest advantage in the match. But with the Extreme title, he can't be disqualified, but with the small edge of experience as the champion, he can surely take care of The End easily. Especially when the man is blind, he'll never see it coming! Smoke just stares off into space as he watches the promo. He then cuts the TV off with the remote and throws it across the room with a hint of aggression, the remote almost shatters against the wall, but the batteries just fly out of it. He grabs the blunt from the ashtray and sticks it in his mouth again, he takes a puff to keep it lit, and then another puff to let the smoke fill his lungs again. Smoke can't believe that everyone around the area are all-big into Opium, they're not as big into pot or marijuana as he is. Everywhere he goes, he finds Opium dealers, but not a single weed dealer. He can wing it though with what he's got and live through his trip. Smoke exhales the smoke and it's green as all the other puffs that he's taken since the scene began. Smoke places the blunt back in the ashtray and then continues to stare at the ceiling. He then utters a sentence that no other words could bring to you more straight forward… Semore Smoke: Skylar Green, tomorrow is your last day in PCW. Your career, will be over, your life will be over, and my life, would have just begun… {{{{The scene fades to black on the insane looking face of Semore Smoke who begins to break out into hysterical laughter…}}}}
