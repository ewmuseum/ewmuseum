Brawl In A Mall (Aftermath)

27 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27 November Prologue: After digesting the revelation that CEO Xavier Kannon had bought out the competitive federation RUSH, which had been brought to light during Sunday's Alliances Pay-Per-View, Primetime Championship Wrestling's very own Jester Gaz Green decided to unwind. Celebrating back-to-back, successful title defences in odds that certainly favoured those other than the Champions themselves, Gaz opted to take a moment from his hectic order of life. With an adrenalin packed afternoon of shopping with Natalia Miyagawa in company, the trip turned sour last time the cameras rolled leading us into the next chapter of what could only be described as 'Brawl In A Mall'. The tension appeared to ease; the storm has looked to have past and the signs look positive as Gaz Green cockily strutted from a fast food joint immersed in conversation with Natalia Miyagawa. You could almost foresee the headlines of the media as the married wife of Alexander Miyagawa happily shopped with her business associate. 'Wrestle Tattler Love Triangle!' 'PCW's Jester Courting Married Colleague!' 'Primetime's Cheating Wife!' Wiping his greasy face with a tissue, Gaz Green languished behind, his departure spurning an influx of loyal wrestling fans that pressed their grotesque faces against the panel of glass windows. Waving and screaming, causing a ruckus Gaz Green simply smiled at the attention he drew and vanished into the populated street. 'PHEW-....' bellowed the Jester in bated breath, drawing up to his recently acquired valet who simply smiled, the first real indication that any rift had been bridged. 'So where are we going?!?' asked Gaz Green, his breathing now returned to his normal rhythmical pattern. Still tied down by baggage, a productive day by all accounts, Gaz continued to slither forward. Looking every bit the bellboy, I mean they have a corporate image and Gaz dresses in nothing but green business suits, a trifle difference, however on this occasion they have similar duties. 'Keep up!' yelled Natalia. With such a tone of authority we can clearly see who wears the trousers in this relationship, business relationship mind you. Still cluttered and weighed down by the excess baggage, Gaz yelped in pain as the corner of a box purchased earlier in the day violently crashed against his leg. Grinding his teeth to absorb the impact the box swung in a pendulum motion once more with another impact, and another, no longer could the PCW comic hold back his cries. 'AAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHHH~!' 'THAT'S IT!!! I demand to know where we're going?' hissed the Emerald Menace. Natalia simply tilted her head and peered back as she laid her eyes over the spoilt little boy pose Gaz held, his annoyance scrolled across his tainted face, puffing and wheezing he stood motionless. 'We're going to get you a car. How can a Tag Team Champion turn up, week in and week out in his partners purple hearse-... I mean purple is so yesterday-... Hearse and corpses, hello, dead people and stuff!' retorted Natalia with her own impersonation of the ditzy diva, Commissioner Ellie. Any PCW fan will tell you there has been no love loss between the Conniving Collective and the Backstabbing Bitch, not a real term for the preppy cheerleader but the private joke of Gaz Green and Noscosto Ireul Nerezza. Anyhow after a short chuckle, the Jester trekked forward and into the dealership where he stood in awe, surrounded by some vintage automobiles and newer models with bigger engines and certainly bigger price tags. 'Can I help you sir!?!' asked a voice from behind. Standing in a pressed ironed suit, nose tilted into the air and what little hair brushed over to one side the dealership's assistant offered his services by extending his hand with a welcoming gesture. 'OH!!!' Gaz leant forward and both parties shook hands. The assistant with the nametag of 'Colin' tried to release from the tight grasp of his potential customer when suddenly, ZZZZZZZZZZZZZZAAAAAAAAAAAAAAPPPPPPPPPPPPPP 'MOST ELECTRIFYING MOVE IN PRIMETIME~!' The sales clerk suddenly began to spasm and his hair rose into what appeared as a side-spike, jerking violently in a seizure he flopped to the ground with a splat. Looking around for eyewitnesses, Gaz Green cheekily smiled to his valet who tried desperately to hold back the tears of laughter, trying desperately to keep a stiff upper lip. 'Err-... Arr-... Doesn't this constitute as A.B.H?' 'Isn't that a bank?' replied the prankster. 'No that's T.S.B, the bank that likes to say yes, I said A.B.H' 'Oh-... A sexually transmitted disease-...' 'ATTEMPTED BODILY HARM YOU TWIT!!!' hissed the Russian beauty with an eager interruption, her eyes swaying left and right like a hand puppet. Lifting the limp body of the clerk, the Jester tossed the carcass over the bonnet of a Shelby GT 500. With a thunderous thud from the offices, another sales assistant homed in on the devious duo, with little time to spare, the moment required some quick thinking and this is a department where Gaz Green is dangerous. 'What are we going to do?' gulped the unofficial third member of Pranks With Attitude, frantically pacing back and forth as the minutes to this unveiling counted down. Dipping his head into the bags individually, the Lord Of Laughter was fighting a losing battle as he rummaged. Almost shredding the bags like a wild animal, he finally emerged, bottle in hand, holding it aloft in triumph before nestling it into the clerk’s hand. Removing the screw off cap, the duo looked over their conquest, ready to hatch their caper. BANG~! 'May I help you-....' said the second sales assistant, his hand clamping to the broad shoulder of the PCW asset Gaz Green. Both Gaz and Natalia parted like the red sea, revealing the incapacitated dealership worker, shaking their heads in disgust, voicing their views, they spurned a verbal frenzy. 'Err-... Arr-... Well-... Hmm-... Argh-...' stuttered the general manager in his strong English accent, his dumbfounded facial expression told a thousand stories. 'Well squire, I don't mind people having a drink at dinnertime but don't you think this chap here as had one too many, huh?!?' injected the Prince Of Pranks continuing his charade. With Gaz rallying his outrage, he was further backed up by that of Natalia who returned to preppy Ellie mode, full-blown Eleanor Kannon-Hall. 'Like drunk people are so bad for business, eeeewwww, his breath could do with a mint and stuff-... I mean personal hygiene, hello-... I'm so not happy here!' 'Ma' m I'll get to the bottom of this. I can only apologise, this has never happened before, and I guarantee that it sha’n’t happen again, COLIN YOUR FIRED~!' bellowed the manager, striking his finger towards the poor victim of Gaz Green's hand buzzer, unbeknownst that his job has been axed, contract terminated, there's the door. With an irate manager going off on a tangent, he stormed to his offices allowing the Trickster Team's Gaz Green and Natalia Miyagawa to head for home. With the image slowly closing, we witness the duet making their urgent exit and hightailing it from the crime scene. 'So how do you suppose we're going to get home?' questioned the separated wife of retired superstar Alexander Miyagawa, retired, I meant ship jumping as he now resides in OSW. 'Err-... Taxi-...' - The End -
