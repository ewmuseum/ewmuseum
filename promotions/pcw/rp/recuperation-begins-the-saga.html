Recuperation Begins The Saga

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.25.02 (#1 TT, FF) “Ugh…worthless turncoats.” The constant beep of a heart monitor and the cold voice of Rush Mason open this promo, the first after Alliances for Rush. Alliances was a crushing blow to the head, and perhaps ego of the Voodoo Villain, but more on that later. “Those meddling idiots put me in here. Just couldn’t let their old boss succeed while they rotted away in mediocrity.” With that, the scene fades in from black and we can see Rush Mason, lying in a hospital bed with the front of his head bandaged, most likely from the vicious chair shot sustained last night at Alliances. Rush: On the night of my glory…The night that I was going to win the #1 contendership, those buffoons had to show up. The treatment for the head wound has apparently not made Rush any more chipper, though. He sits in the bed, a sneer on his face as he re-calls the events of Alliances 2K2 and the “tragic” story of how he was viciously attacked. Rush: I did exactly what I said, and carried my team of damn hooligans. Spitting as he recalls his joke of a team, Rush continues to speak with disgust in his tone and on his mind as he thinks of his former employees attack on him, as well as the tough card that he was dealt, forced to team with Aymin, Vurdo, and Rollins. Rush: Minding my own business, winning Alliances, when…WHAM! Those two-timing whores jump ME! Me, of all people? What have I ever done to deserve such a vicious assault? The list could go on for months with explanations, but as there is no one with a whole lot of self knowledge, or knowledge of the fact that Rush is a hypocrite, in the room, the fact goes totally unnoticed as Mason continues to plead his case to the hospital room. Rush: I mean, I could see attacking Akiyama and The End. They did need to be informed that the Void is an illusion of their crack binges. However, he digresses. Rush quickly gets back on his case of demeaning the members of RUSH who, in his mind at least, cost him what was going to become the biggest victory of his life last night. Rush: First, a head injury? Then, Kannon, just as much a two-timing whore, books me in the main event for Turmoil, and the semi for Fusion. Mason shakes his head as he thinks about the “evil” of the CEO, when in fact, the man sitting in the hospital bed is only slightly paled in comparison when we consider dastardly deeds. Rush: Of course I deserve to be main eventing, and even a man with delusions of grandeur like Kannon can realize that. Grinning like an idiot as he thinks of how fantastic he is, Rush is not a pretty sight. The bandage on his head might make one take him not as seriously, but then again, most people not within the realm of PCW will take a 6’4, 260 pound black man quite seriously (even if he is a regular pussy cat like a certain bed-ridden Voodoo follower). Rush: Snow? The End? Surfer Prototype A? Not exactly a list of Herculean defeats for me, but they will only continue to prove that I not only deserved my win at Alliances, but it was only wrenched from within my grasp by unwarranted attacks perpetrated by certain former employees of mine. As Rush begins to shake his head again, continuing his hypocritical spout of nonsense, he is interrupted by a knock at the door, and he yells out to whoever wants to come in. Rush: Go away. My lavish Loa-infused body needs rest, something that you nimrods won’t grant me. However, despite Rush’s orders, the knob turns and in walks Ray Ray, his manager, who wears a large smile on his face, which sends Rush’s mood even deeper into the foul depths at which it is already pawing at. Ray Ray: Dawg…I watched Alliances. Mason looks up at him, his face an absolute picture of pouting. However, Ray Ray’s smile persists and the huge man looks down at Rush with a look of….adoration?! Rush: The fuck do you want? Yeah, I lost. I was cheated by those two bit prostitutes and their orange-haired dumbfuck of a ringleader. Ray Ray shakes his head, his grin fading a bit, but still very much apparent on his face as he addresses Rush, the adoration now very much audible as he addresses his employer. Ray Ray: Nah, dawg, you came in 3rd. You was cheated, but that means you beat out 56 other homies! However, optimism has never been a strong point of the Voodoo Villain, and his face stays foul-looking and his bad mood is still noticeable as he replie, pouting. Rush: I suppose so. Well, fatso, this just means another chapter begins in the epic legend of Rush Mason. The story will be told by the common dumbfuck for years to come as they think of the man who brought Bon Dieu and the Loa Agwe into their lives. Ray Ray, not knowing what half of those words mean, just nods as Rush scratches his chin and the scene fades to black… Alliances dieVillains are foreverLet the new chapter of the villain’s saga beginHowever ruthless it may beAnd so, indeed, it does begin...
