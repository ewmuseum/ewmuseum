The Assassination Series Part Three: First Attempt

19 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/5/03 The street looked like the lord and master of all that is chaos had come over and took a dump slap bang in the middle of the road. However, this was typically not the cause of such chaos. The cause and the perpetrator of the heinous crime was a mystery. A mystery Justice Inc would soon discover; much to their dismay… Warrior pushed himself up from the ground in an uneasy manner, and held the back of his head. A brief twinge of pain rippled through his visage as he stood upright, using the wall of a building as leverage to prop him upright. He waited a couple of seconds, to regain his senses, before looking for his partner, Blind Rage. Warrior subconsciously realised that the massive 7’3” frame of Blind Rage had protected him from the majority of the blast, but at the same time realised that the explosion must have hit the mammoth hard. Warrior realised that Blind Rage’s frame was near indestructible, but he did take the majority of the blast, which would have undoubtedly killed a normal human being. Warrior quickly rushed to the side of his old friend, and knelt beside his apparently lifeless frame. However, as he draws closer, Blind Rage bolts upright, out of the blue, startling Warrior somewhat, forcing him to fall backwards. Blind Rage tenderly held the side of his head in agony, before he pushed himself up from the pavement. He then extends his arm to Warrior, who takes it and consequently his effortlessly pulled to his feet. “What the hell was that?” Warrior asked, brushing some of debris from his already ripped costume. “Don’t know, but I suspect it’s deliberate.” He replied. “Deliberate? Who? Why?” “I suspect that Bullseye was not the only mercenary contacted by the mysterious Owl-Man. I suspected he has placed some kind of bounty on us…” “That means that every super-powered goon from the underbelly of society will be on the hunt for us…” Blind Rage nodded gravely. “It seems that way.” “What the hell are we going to do? If we don’t get to the warehouse in time, Bullseye will kill Handbag Warrior and Badger Man without a second thought.” “He intends to kill them anyway…he just wants us there so he can take out Justice Inc in one go. This could be tricky.” “So who do you think planted the bomb?” “The bomb wasn’t planted it was thrown.” Blind Rage began, eyeing up the debris of what was once the Justice Incorporated van. “It looks like it was thrown from a height too.” Both he and Warrior look up into the sky. Nothing but a blue sky and some white clouds. “Dang.” “That’s not the worst of it…” “Go on.” “He’ll strike again at any minute…a mercenary has to confirm that he has made the kill. Therefore whoever did it will be lurking somewhere ready to strike.” “Do we just wait for him?” “No. We don’t have time we have to get to the Warehouse. Just be on your guard.” “As ever…” Blind Rage simply glanced at Warrior with dismay and instantly disregarded the comments. He then set off in a brisk jog, for now it was the only way to get to their intended destination. Warrior soundly took up pace behind Blind Rage, constantly looking around him for intended threats. The first sign of trouble was signalled by a maniacal laugh, which sent a chill down Warrior’s spine. Blind Rage briefly turned to Warrior and gave him a grimaced look. The attack would come soon. The pumpkin bounced off the building wall and landed about ten feet away from Blind Rage’s position. Rage recognised the threat immediately and halted. Warrior, not looking where he was going, ran into the back of Blind Rage and consequently fell on his ass. Blind then quickly dived to the ground as the explosion of the pumpkin bomb rocked the entire street. Warrior managed to squirm free of Blind Rage’s ample exterior and noticed the goblin creature flying on a sled type device. Warrior recognised the villain from TV reports. It was the Hobgoblin. His orange cowl swept behind him in the wind. Warrior watched as the villain started another attack vector, whilst also extracting another pumpkin bomb from the satchel of weapons, hanging down from shoulder. Warrior quickly extracted the laser axe, attached to his left thigh, and pressed the engage button. Consequently, a green, laser axe shape formed at the far end of his steel staff. He took aim, and then launched the axe into the air, with ferocious force. The axe soundly indented itself into the front of the flying sled, causing the sled to lose power and begin to plummet towards the ground. As a resultant of this the Hobgoblin lost his footing, forcing him to briefly juggle the pumpkin bomb. However, upon finally gaining control of the bomb, it was too late. The bomb exploded in his face, sending flying from his sled and into the wall. Hard. Warrior retrieved his laser axe from the wreckage of the glider, just as Blind Rage regained consciousness again. The mammoth looked around him in shock. “What the hell happened?” Blind Rage murmured, as he realised the threat was over. “I kicked his ass!” Warrior declared with gusto. Blind Rage sighed. “No…tell me what REALLY happened?” Warrior pointed over to the crumpled up frame of the Hobgoblin, who was soundly embedded within the wall of a building, and smiled, thinly. “I kicked his…ASS!” “What’s that now…4 for you and 3567 for me, huh?” “Don’t spoil the moment Rage.” “We’d better get going…it doesn’t look like the Hobgoblin is going anyway soon.” Warrior nodded with a smile. Blind Rage couldn’t stand him like this and so wordlessly, he began to jog once again. There wasn’t much time left and it looked like it was going to be one hell of a tough day.
