'The Searching' - Part VII - Untold Truths - FINALE?

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-12-02 NOTE: 15K on notepad! I love rambling. :) This deals with B.S.'s thoughts as of right now.. we'll flashback a week to March Madness and finish this off next time. (epilogue) .Song listening guide: 'The Way You Like It' by Adema (These guys freggin' rock!) BOBBY STORMBRINGER 'The Searching' - Part VII - Untold Truths - FINALE? Then.... The only semi-concious Stormbringer gets his popular catch phrase cut off with a vicious chair shot right to the top of his skull!! Bobby's eyes roll up in the back of his head and the promising athlete falls over like a ton of bricks, all the way over this time. At least he doesn't have that semi-concious thing to worry about anymore. Sinister snickering is heard and the camera pans up and to its left to show.. the same man? No it can't be. The only difference is the length of their hair, but it is obvious who has perpetrated this unwarranted travesty. Randy Stormbringer, the man born from the same womb as Bobby some six minutes later on, now drops the now thrashed and dented chair right next to B.S. with a clang. Randy now laughs low in his throat as he grasps his twin by his dark silver trenchcoat and looks for a decent grip. This explains why Bobby Stormbringer couldn't help Jeremy Howard after Kid Wonder's unforgiveable actions against Krayzie. If Randy wants B.S. healthy enough to show up for the NTICW Pay-Per-View in their home of Orlando so badly, then why would Randy pull a stunt like this now!? Much has happened in the week after March Madness and Bobby will address the *ahem* 'turmoil' that has arisen in his professional life as of late. For now it is more than personal. This is family. But things are going to get more personal than he ever could of imagined. {edited} The Searching is over. Let the Stalking begin. However, B.S. will be the one who is stalked. Until then we are left with the image of Randy Stormbringer dragging his brother's near lifeless body to an unknown location. An Unknown fate. Once the two twin brothers are out of our range of sight we cut right to blackness. And now.... * * * 'Every search for a hero must begin with something every hero requires. A villian.' -from Mission Impossible: 2 --Sometime in March..?---- Going backwards through time, starting with the future... This Tuesday night on PCW Turmoil Bobby 'The Showstopper' Stormbringer will go face-to-burned-face with a man that he fears like no other. Ever. That would be because this man is the only person on Earth Mr. Prime Time has ever feared at all. Not Stanislav. Not Boda. Not Meanstreak. No, not even Steven Smith. Clyde. The 8-year-old pest by the name of Bethany (is she still eight after all this time??) can be easily dealt with and disposed of with careful precision. But even then she serves as a deadly distraction for her.. 'beau'..? *narrator violently throws up* Ugggghh. Okay... I think I'm okay. Two days before this on Sabotage, B.S. laid waste to The End while his stable leader.. or former stable leader once B.S. officially proclaims it.. Jeremy Howard delivered a stunning beatdown about the head and neck of one Xavier Kannon. Something B.S. wishes to do before too long. Perhaps in April? We'll get back to Jeremy Howard in a minute. Let's not lose sight of some facts. Hank~! was once Extreme Champion, sadly enough. B.S. has defeated him. Karl LeBlanc took the championship from him in one of the biggest travesties in the history of PCW.. and trust me, there have been some big travesties! B.S. has defeated him in a four way by making Anton the Kid tap. After Miyagawa stepped on Stormbringer's toes and 'saved the Television Division' B.S. looked towards qualifying into March Madness like everyone expected him to. Naturally. Brock Newbludd, despite Akiyama's win, is the number one contender to the TV title. By defeating 'Unamazing' Mike Storm. Whatevah... In his first of two matches to qualify for the main event at March Madness, B.S. was struck with a nasty, nasty flu strain that made him incredibly weak and constantly running to the bathroom to err... 'release some fluids', shall we say? Despite all of this... B.S. has defeated Brock Newbludd as well. Surprise! (With help from Mike Storm, but B.S. didn't bring this Storm. It was unwanted and Mike is still on a standing warning for that sh!t) So for three. Count them. Three different reasons that B.S. deserves a PCW midcard title shot. Extreme or Television. B.S. has also been reminded that you can NEVER trust your tag team partner. Jason Core or Akiyama. A flip of the coin and having it landing on the wrong side could mean the end of one of their little title reigns. Considering the wacky junk that Aki has already done to B.S. and his friends. (stuck him inside a video game, sent him back in time to the eighties, ya know. Real minor stuff.) B.S. always speaks his mind. Microphone in the guy's hand? All hell could break loose. This is what transpired last Friday on Fusion. B.S. thrashed Darkstar verbally for interfering in Miya's World title match, as it goes fully against B.S.'s honor code. He even has to force himself to attack someone during their match, his feelings are truly that strong about it. Before or after? That is the 'proper' time for such things, The Showstopper believes. That lil' whiney bitch Xavier Kannon acted three times worse than D.S., however, at Madness. He aired Asylum's dirty laundry in public, number one. He wouldn't admit responsiblity to the fact that no one in Asylum f*cked up worse than he did, not even Darkstar. B.S. did the best he could to help Miya through, despite the fact that he was hurt from his failed interference in the prior match. Gee, Krayzie only rescued his brother and kept the Continental belt home in Asylum where it belongs. Don't feel that Miya and Jeremy lay blameless, however. Did B.S. hear one damn shread of thanks from the wrestler he's admired for so long in Miyagawa about the incredible sacrifice he made for only Asylum's benefit, and mostly for Miyagawa himself? No. Did B.S. hear one damn shread of thanks from Jeremy for stopping Maxwell from getting to Jeremy's brother at one point in his match? No. You want to know what Bobby Stormbringer has to hear on a daily basis instead? Besides for complete silence from Asylum's two so-called leaders?? MINDLESS DRONE #1: Were you ever REALLY in Asylum, Bobby? MINDLESS DRONE #2: You're in Asylum? Don't misunderstand Bobby Stormbringer. B.S. always understood that there was a pecking order built on how long you've been in PCW. Being unceremoniously 'ranked' behind Xavier Kannon is and was annoying, but B.S. knows the truth. That he is technically superior to the geek and more creative in the ring than he could ever be. Compassion, love, anger... whatevah. All you need is truth in your life and the rest will fall into place. No really. I mean it. Bobby had several misconceptions. That there would be some sort of common insights within the group, for one. He should of figured out that something was fishy from the start. To even get into Asylum he had to 'prove himself' by taking down Drake Maxwell and The End. With Xavier Kannon..? BS THOUGHTS BACK THEN: Instead Jer, how about Skylar and I beat the sh*t outta Xavier and Drake!? That's what B.S. really wanted to say to Krayzie. Eternal Wrestling versus the old Yoder NTICW. It may of only lasted a few months, but the fued between the wrestlers involved will never die. Don't believe me? Just ask B.S. why he threw Anton the Kid down a sewer at Shang-haied. Just ask The Showstopper why he didn't defeat Anton with The Perfect Storm in that four way match, oh no. Oh no that's too easy. B.S. had to make the former ego-driven NTICWer squeal and tap. Just an exclamation mark to further E-Dub dominance! But getting his hands on Drake Maxwell, the last of the Yoder-era NTICW Champions... punking him out in that hallway on that fateful Sabotage with his trusty sledgehammer... That was worth it all. That was payment enough. Only because of the heinous things Drake has done in the past, like how he colors himself as some sort of a hero for his twisted views? Puhlease! If given half a real chance now he would snap Ellie~!'s neck as freggin' quickly as Kannon's. Don't like it? Too bad. Get over it. B.S. believes in total equality for women in wrestling, remember? That's in positive ways like success. But hey. They better be able to take the punishment like men usually do in return. Because B.S. for one is an equal opportunity offender. B.S. is no hypocrite though, so don't try to play that card on him either. He would only treat people this way if he feels that they deserve it. Drake Maxwell.. The End.. Xavier Kannon.. Darkstar.. Clyde.. Akiyama.. they all have sins to repent, karma that needs to be leveled for the sake of 'The Wheel Of Fate'. For the sake of the mind-nubbing balance that pure chaos brings to our lives. But the chaos must be even, level. None should escape the full fury of its wrath. If they don't do these things for themselves and for the sake of their spirit.. their very own souls.. then they must pay the penalty. Strange. They always choose the 'penalty' route. B.S. doesn't have to be the administor of necessary justice to these people. Or anyone. But it's just easier this way. Unfortunately? Miyagawa and Jeremy Howard are fast approaching a high level of ignorance. Of laziness. Of perhaps the worst thing of all... Being repetitive. Miya's 'crimes' have been minor and forgiveable. While Jeremy? Well, Krayzie's are beginning to stack up on him, quite frankly. Jeremy interfered in Drake Maxwell's TV title match against Miyagawa a few weeks back. So how can these two f*ckers be so hypocritical of Darkstar? Are they better? Do they rise above the laws they set for the rest of us? Who are they to judge anyone if they act like this... if they act like New Breed or worse.... Management? And no B.S. doesn't care what Drake Maxwell's done. Beat his head in before the match, or better yet? After. After Miya has retained the title with no stains, no tainted victory. Too much to ask? Apparently. Bobby and Jeremy are extremely alike as has been alluded to before. Both are seeking family and in different ways they have both found it recently. Jeremy has released his brother.. with help from The End and Bobby Stormbringer, even though he would like you to forget that. Randy Stormbringer has shocked the world, revealing himself, B.S., and Aim to all be related. Not just loosely related. Siblings. Randy and Bobby's father is still alive, not dead in a long forgotten plane crash some eight years ago. Their father is Aimz' father. Also Jeremy has gotten his son back home. Finally. It had only been two years. It had only been four years since his brother was stuck in jail. But the PCW World Heavyweight title was much more important, you see. Bobby is four years older than Jeremy. Bobby has been in this business for eight long years, off and on from injuries and once for alcohol abuse. B.S. has made every other mistake there is to make. B.S. doesn't agree that Jeremy is a golddigger. But he does feel that Jeremy is still young and that The Krayzie One needs to get his priorities in order. Need an example?? BS RECENT THOUGHTS: How about fixing Asylum and reloading Asylum for the war to come!? How about concentrating on your stable instead of your petty revenge for once. Forget Kannon for now! Look at the bigger picture. A bigger picture than a title sweep, for Gawd's sakes. This isn't like Maxwell. Xavier didn't rob you of your family, this is just business. Namely Xavier being jealous of you. Another friend that stabbed you in the back? Timo, Snow, now Xavier. I know the damn sob story. But enough is enough. It's time to grow up and be the bigger man. No one has ever said that B.S. holds back. Family, friends, enemies? All human. All with responsibilites to not only themselves but others as well. All deserving of the full force of The Wheel Of Fate. All deserving of something simple. The truth. B.S. still considers Miya and Jeremy to be friends. Just friends that are badly screwing up right now when it comes to anything else but Championship titles. The Showstopper couldn't care less if he's been in PCW for two months to the day come March eleventh or not. He's still been on this dirtball a lot longer than most other PCW superstars. What good is experience if you don't put it to good use?? Despite all this, B.S. feels no need to share his feelings and thoughts with the two good friends. What would it change? But none of that could of concerned Mr. Prime Time last week during Madness. Not even Clyde could of, considering Bobby wouldn't learn of the intimidating match some two days later. And it certainly didn't concern Randy Stormbringer. Because B.S. was too busy being knocked out and drug around the St. Louis Savvis Center at the hands of the forementioned Randy last week during the Pay-Per-View. What did Bobby think when he saw Snow's injury? When Miya didn't get much more support to win the World title? When Boda finally became champion? When Mexi sacrificed himself just like B.S. did for Miya!? Randy tortures with his teasing mental games in the aftermath of The Searching. This should be good. And baby? That is no R.S.! ~END..?
