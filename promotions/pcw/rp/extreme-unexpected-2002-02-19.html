Extreme & Unexpected

19 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-19-02 **Theme Music: ‘Gone Sour’ by River City High** ----------------------------------------Dear Jenna, Again, I’ve got good news and bad news. The bad news is that in a surprising turn of events, I had a real opponent for Sabotage. The good news is, I threw out the match of my life, and the kids in the front office decided to give me a hack at the PCW Extreme Title. On Sunday, I go one-on-one-on-one with Karl LeBlanc and the legendary HANK~!. My first real shot for success, and it came so early… …damn, where did my bags go? Love,Jason---------------------------------------- **Our scene opens in a rental car carefully navigating the streets of downtown Portland, Oregon. The car’s nothing spectacular, some late-model import Toyonda or something, in a deep green. The windows are pulled up, but on the inside, a casual observer might be able to see a large man belting out song lyrics…** Jason (singing): When did everything…go…so…sour? **Huh…never knew J-Core could hold a tune…he’s no frickin’ Kyle Velocity, though…** Jason (singing): When did the thought of your face…bring a sick taste to my mOWth? **Ouch. No more high notes for you, mister.** Jason (singing): We’ve heard this once before… **The camera pans to the backseat, where we see Jason’s carry-on gear bag…and that’s it. Hrmmm…** Jason (singing): …and this is how it is, and I can’t take it anymore-HEY! **We see Jason’s car slam on it’s brakes in front of an American Eagle outlet. After a bit of creative parallel parking, the rental car is firmly wedged between a Range Rover and a Miata. As J-Core steps out of the car, the sky cracks with thunder, and JC enters the outlet as the rain begins to come down.** Jason: Whaddaya know? My timing isn’t that bad… **An obnoxiously smiling young greeter near the front of the door (think ‘Brian’ from Office Space), nods towards the large Jason, who barely acknowledges his presence.** Greeter: Welcome to the American Eagle Outlet! We’ve got a couple of great- **Jason smiles kindly at the man, and walks past. The greeter, undeterred, begins to dog Jason’s steps.** Greeter: …really great deals today. All cargo shorts are 20% off, and all of our men’s button-down short-sleeve… **Jason turns around and the greeter, obviously having succeeded in his pestering, smiles triumphantly and addresses the PCW star.** Greeter: …well, what can I do for you, sir? **Jason kind of rolls his eyes.** Jason: Okay. Over the past few days, my bags were lost at the airport. I need new clothes now. My first rental car blew a tire, and to top it off…on Sunday…I jobbed to a corpse. Could you do me a HUGE favor, and go away? I’m not in a very good mood right now… **The greeter’s face falls, and he is unsure as to how he should react. Jason turns away, and starts pawing through a rack of plaid shirts…** Greeter: Maybe I can help you find what you’re looking for? **Very…very…slowly, Jason Core turns around to look at this greeter. J-Core stands almost a foot taller, and easily weighs about half again as much as the greeter, but all that extra weight is in muscle. Jason rolls his neck, inflates his chest a little, and cracks his knuckles…** Jason: Look, I’m as much for second chances as anyone…but if you try approaching me again, I’ll break you in half. Nothing personal, you know… **The greeter swallows hard, and his voice is lowered a little.** Greeter: Look, if I don’t sell you some freakin’ clothes, I don’t get my commission. So you’ll have to excuse me for bothering you, but I NEED to sell you some clothes. If I can do that better by going away, I will. If I have to keep following you around, I will. But either way, I’m gonna make you walk out of here having spent some money. **Jason’s eyes are wide, as this guy who’s no more than 170 lbs, just stood up to him, a man nearly twice his size, and built for physical combat.** Greeter: Sir…is there anything I can help you with? **Jason looks at the greeter thoughtfully for a moment. He smiles a little.** Jason: Yeah, actually. I need some clothes. What would you recommend? **The greeter smiles, and walks over to pull some XXL’s off of the cargo pants rack. Jason mutters a little something to himself.** Jason: What do ya know…that guy’s got guts. Most normal guys wouldn’t stand up to someone my size…he kinda reminds me of HANK~! in that way. I mean, not a lot of kids have the sack to step in a ring with a huge convicted felon and another big guy, and be willing to put their gold on the line. **The greeter comes back, with several pairs of khaki cargo-style pants, and a few pairs of jeans.** Greeter: We’ve got a dressing room ready for you. Jason: Wonderful. **The greeter leads Jason to a dressing room, which Jason enters, carrying armloads of pants. As he changes into a pair of long khaki cargos, he reflects…** ::So, I’m officially 0-1 in my PCW career. I tell you, I didn’t expect for that kid to come back from the dead…much less wrestle so well. I mean, I think I should be more disappointed, but hey, I can’t knock another guy’s comeback, right?:: **The khakis are a little short around the ankles, so J-Core goes ahead and tries on a slightly longer pair in the same style.** ::S’a shame that since he came back, that DarkStar kid’s playing at being a dark messiah. He doesn’t need some goofball background in the supernatural to be a real force in this business, he just needs to go out and wrestle like he did on Sunday, and he’ll be a real big deal someday. There’s still a few things I can teach him, but he’s doing okay…:: **Nice. This pair of khakis fits perfectly, and Jason moves on to a pair of jeans…** ::So, management decided to give this old warhorse a shot at the PCW Extreme Title. Honestly, I can’t figure out if they wanna try and cripple me, or if they want to push me forward. I mean, LeBlanc’s got a history of beating on guys…he IS a killer…but neither he or HANK~! are top-quality wrestling talents. If they really wanted to help those two, they would’ve booked ‘em in a regular contest with me, and let me show ‘em a few moves you don’t learn on the streets or by swinging a chair.:: **Another score! The jeans are a fit! The small victories in life, I swear, they make it all worthwhile. Jason fumbles through the rest of the clothes, looking for something that he might like the appearance of…** ::At any rate, I can’t afford to lose this match on Sunday. If do, LeBlanc may keep beatin’ the hell out of guys for no reason. If I do, then HANK~! will never improve as a wrestler, and the PCW Extreme Title’ll keep losing credibility. But most of all, if I lose this one, I may never get another chance like this. Hell, I keep jobbing out, and the brass may fire me. Come on, with the OSW fiasco, each talent has to be at top form.:: **No such luck, and Jason puts his original sweatpants back on…** ::Yup, on Sabotage, LeBlanc and HANK~! will learn about a new style of Extreme…my style. A style that isn’t so much about brawling and weapons, a style that’s about never saying die. A style that’s about knowing exactly what to do, no matter WHAT your situation is. Hell, in PCW, I could end up defending this belt on top of a moving train, in India, with a goat as the special guest referee. The PCW Extreme Title is going to be about performing under Extreme pressure, against Extreme competitors, in Extreme situations.:: **Jason flashes a smile at his reflection in the mirror. A first, if you think about it.** ::It’s the start of something good, you could think. All he has to do is defeat a felon, and avoid being like Doug Troy. One’s not so tough, the other is. I wonder if Riley’ll try anything. I wonder when he was the NCAA Champ, I’d love to toss that sumbitch around the ring…some people shouldn’t be allowed to have certain positions in the world…and I’m sure Riley’s using LeBlanc for his own benefits.:: **J-Core exits the dressing room, carrying three pairs of pants that would be worth owning. The greeter is waiting by the register to check him out.** Greeter: That’ll be it? Jason: No doubt. Greeter: Hey, one of the other guys said you’re a pro wrestler, is that true? Jason: Yep. **The greeter looks up for a minute, as if fascinated, then looks back down at his register.** Greeter: Cool. Jason: Yep. Greeter: Well, my manager wanted to let you know that if you start winning some matches, and get some exposure, we need someone to do promotional spots for AE, if you’re interested. **Jason’s kind of surprised.** Jason: Seriously? **The greeter nods a little.** Jason: Alright then…I guess I’ll be in touch. Greeter: That’s seventy-one eighty-two. **Jason pulls a wad of bills out of his pocket, after hitting the bank after his bags were lost…and pulls a hundred. (Note: It’s the biggest bill in the wad, easily)** Jason: Here you go, I’m in a hurry. I have a dark match to work at Turmoil… **He just hands over the hundred, and starts walking out.** Greeter: Oh! Well, see ya later, sir. **And Jason is back outside, into the Portland rain, and ducking under awnings to get back to his car…** ::Well…that was unexpected. I guess, as the PCW Extreme Champion, I’ll have to be ready for the unexpected. My entire life’s been one series of unexpected events after another. On Sunday, HANK~!, LeBlanc…all of PCW…they’ll experience a little unexpected event of their own. I’ll win the PCW Extreme Title…and for anyone to take it from me, they’ll have to go back…to…school!:: **Catchphrase out, J-Core’s ready to hit a Chik-Fil-A, and the gym…he’s got a lot of work ahead of him, as our scene fades to black.**
