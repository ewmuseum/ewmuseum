Lord of the Allies

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 22, 2002 &#039;Now that we all agree I&#039;m in charge,&#039; Xamine Herr Kanyon calls in a lordly voice, still overseeing the assembled wrestlers from high atop his rock. Bellie~! and The Virgin Byron Calver stand at his side, trying to imitate his condescending air but failing miserably. The waves crashing against the shore provide the only sound as they wait on his word, completely ignorant of the significance it will bear on all their lives. Kanyon revels in their attendance for another moment, then spreads his arms wide like a father returning home to his children. &#039;Let&#039;s implement the most integral part to running a successful society...&#039; he pauses again, a greedy smile spreading across his face, and when he speaks it&#039;s as if the word floats off his tongue, too magnificent to be merely uttered. &#039;Taxes.&#039; The crowd is instantly buzzing with scandal and outrage, and half-assed shouts of protest are shouted at random. The negative reaction is immediate and unanimous, uniting all of them against a common enemy. The ghetto gangstas from Homeless Inc. finally find the high-class gangsters of Mob Rule listening to their strife, and everywhere former enemies complain good-naturedly to one another about how they&#039;re getting screwed. &#039;Like everything else in your life, Herr Kanyon,&#039; Tyger Smells&#039;Em snarls over the rumble of the gathered, and they immediately quiet down. &#039;Your plan sucks.&#039; Tyger stops abruptly and grins, crossing his arms over his chest and leaning back skeptically. &#039;If you think I&#039;m going to give you money just because we&#039;re on some island, you&#039;re more air-headed than your walking sock puppet there, and more naive than The Virgin.&#039; Herr Kanyon just nods smugly, ignoring the distrustful looks cast at him from all angles. &#039;I never said anything about money, Tyger,&#039; he trails off mysteriously, surveying the spectators to make sure he has all of their attention. &#039;Money would do us absolutely no good right now. Something we will need lots of, however, is food. Which is why everyone must gather pineapples every day and bring them back here... I can see an orchard just over there along the edge of the forest, and if everyone ca-&#039; &#039;WHAT?!&#039; A wondering scream steals the words from Kanyon&#039;s mouth, and another flock of birds screeches up above the trees in irritation. The source of the voice, a wide-eyed Timid Bologna, stampedes to the front of the crowd and grabs onto the rock like a drowning man onto a boat. His eye twitches as he cranes his neck closer to the newly elected leader, a dreamy cast about his look and movement. &#039;What did you just say?&#039; Timid demands of Kanyon in a far-away voice. Weirded out, Kanyon narrows his eyes and looks to either side for help, then cocks an eyebrow at the crazy Samoan. &#039;I said you owe me some pineapples,&#039; he begins in a cautious way, &#039;which you&#039;re to collect over there, from the orchar-&#039; Herr Kanyon abruptly stops again, this time due to a reason other than a noisy interruption. At the word &#039;there&#039;, Bologna tore off like Wile E. after the roadrunner, and is presently halfway to the pineapple orchard. Everyone looks after him in mystified awe, wondering what the hell he could possibly do with a pineapple that would make it such a prized possession. Kanyon gives the crowd a cock-eyed, questioning look, and as one they nod wearily, not needing to put the sentiment into words. &#039;There is no minimum amount of pineapples you must bring each day,&#039; Herr Kanyon says with a parting worried glance after Bologna. &#039;Keep in mind, however, that we don&#039;t know how long we&#039;re going to be stranded here, and the value of your contribution determines how seriously we take you when we assemble. If you don&#039;t put up anything, don&#039;t expect us to pay you any mind in return.&#039; A new wave of murmurs kicks up among the assembled, but they halt quickly when Bellie~! ambles to the front of the rock and calls in her high-pitched, whiny voice. &#039;And remember to, like, only give us solid, quality pineapples. We &lt;i&gt;so&lt;/i&gt; don&#039;t want any ghetto fruit.&#039; Herr Kanyon nods approvingly, and Bellie~! beams. &#039;You hear that, Homeless Inc.?&#039; Grass &#039;The Burning&#039; Green jests. &#039;Not only are they not accepting your food stamps,&#039; he trails off, looking expectantly to his side at Nerezzula. &#039;But you&#039;re not even allowed to touch the pineapples,&#039; the giant vampire finishes the statement for him, and most of the crowd enjoys a good chuckle. &#039;LOL LOL LOL LOL LOL!!!!!&#039; One of the members of Homeless Inc. brays incoherently, obviously confused over where he is and what he&#039;s doing. &#039;Shut yo mouth cracka, u ain&#039;t swash shit to no one! Why u alwayz be hatin&#039; on us? I tell ya why, cracka, it&#039;s cuz you envy!&#039; During this tirade the speaker has invented several hundred new words in sign language, flailing his hands around before his body like his friends from the special white bus. &#039;Please don&#039;t talk, Ho,&#039; Herr Kanyon pleads in an exasperated tone, beckoning Bellie~! over to massage his temples. &#039;Ever. And stay away from the crack,&#039; he puts the last in as an after-thought, pointing to the gaping pit in the sand before them. Many would have sworn it wasn&#039;t there a minute ago, and if you were to ask any of Homeless Inc. about it even now, they&#039;d all play dumb and pretend they&#039;ve never even seen anything like it. &#039;Who u clownin&#039;, Xaminer?&#039; Spazzing Ho retorts, then sucks on his teeth as if on a lemon, apparently intending some form of disrespect. &#039;U ain&#039;t swash shit either... None of u crackaz iz. U all just envy! LOL LOL LOL LOoooooooooooooooooooooo...&#039; The last incomprehensible word stretches on and fades into nothingness as Ho plummets down the gaping expanse of the crack, the burning prints of Cruel Devil&#039;s hands still stinging into his back. Devil stands where ho was, satisfactorily wiping his hands in front of him. The crowd heaves a collective sigh of relief, each silently thanking whatever it is they worship that the senseless jabber is finally over. On every face lies a contented grin. &#039;Yo! Dat was my dawg, yo!&#039; Well... Almost every face. Dudza and the two other members of Homeless Inc. are bouncing up and down where they stand, contorting their faces to try and scare Devil by looking retarded - or, as they call it: the screwface. &#039;Me and him waz tite like me and R. Kelly, yo!&#039; Dudza continues his barrage, pressing himself right up to Devil&#039;s chest but stopping there and doing nothing except whip his head around. &#039;Bullshit,&#039; Devil says simply and smugly, trying to focus on the man&#039;s pogo-stick head but failing miserably. &#039;WHAT?! Whatchu say, cracka?!&#039; Dudza screams louder, still not actually doing anything but whipping his head around slightly faster. &#039;You&#039;re like twenty years too old to hang around with R. Kelly...&#039; Devil responds callously, enjoying the look of horror that crosses Dudza&#039;s face. Ali @%#&amp;dafi takes a menacing step forward, shouting at least twenty swear words per second, and goes to push Devil with one finger. Before he can make contact, however, a big right hand from Tyger sends him hurtling backwards and down into the crack. A ceaseless stream of swears comes up and out of the gap, and continues coming, not seeming to fade like it naturally should. &#039;Enough!&#039; Kanyon yells, the temple massage apparently not doing its job of keeping the headaches at bay. Dudza visibly struggles with himself, wanting to avenge his friends - his dawgs - but at the same time not wanting to join them. With the look of every &#039;dawg&#039; forced to take it dry, he hangs his head in abject shame and tries to pretend it never happened. &#039;Yes, enough,&#039; Spikey Funktacular chimes in from the centre of the crowd, and all heads turn an appraising eye on him. &#039;I don&#039;t see what the big deal is, anyhow. Is there anyone here who can honestly pretend they didn&#039;t know the crack would eventually kill those guys?&#039; He quips, and... TING~! Hundreds of billions of years from now, on some distant planet in an unknown galaxy, alien lifeforms will see a blinding flash in the sky and mistake it for divine presence. Sacrifices and prayers will be raised to the light from that smile. In the here and now, however, it has a much more physically devastating effect. Everyone but Funktacular is on the ground, having been thrown by the sudden burst of energy, and they sit cowering with their hands clamped over their eyes. &#039;I&#039;m blind!&#039; Mr. Hyde, the PCW World Champion, screams at the top of his lungs. He throws his demonic arms into the air and howls with unholy might. Cords burst on his neck as he whips his head back and cries for mercy. &#039;No! This can&#039;t be happening!&#039; His sightless eyes suddenly widen with some startling revelation. When his voice is heard again it is fully saturated with the most pitiful sorrow in the history of time. &#039;He&#039;s turned me in to The End! &lt;i&gt;The End&lt;/i&gt;! I&#039;m a jobber now!&#039; The screams degenerate into muffled sobbing as Hyde slumps to the ground, and everyone else climbs to their feet slowly, warily eyeing Funktacular, who pays them no mind. &#039;Hubbard!&#039; Kanyon whispers as he straightens his shirt, then glares down at Spikey. &#039;What the hell is with you? Keep those things buried in Aimz, where they can&#039;t hurt anyone!&#039; Agitated, he runs a hand through his hair and scans the crowd, then erupts. &#039;Why are you all just standing here, staring at me?&#039; &#039;Yeah,&#039; Bellie~! joins in, then stutters as she tries to think of something to elaborate with. &#039;Like... Yeah! ...&lt;i&gt;So&lt;/i&gt; yeah!&#039; What she lacks in eloquence she makes up for in glaring. People begin milling about aimlessly, casting disparaging glances towards their leader and his concubine. A joyous shout sails through the air from the orchard, a cry of purest glee, proclaiming the everlasting grandness and sweetness of pineapples. Many people cast furtive glances towards the noise, but some just ignore it, learning already that the madness is contagious if you let it into your mind. &#039;Take all your pineapples to Pornstar! He knows just what to do with them,&#039; Herr Kanyon grins and looks around for his pal, &#039;right Pornst-?&#039; He cuts off with a jerk when he finally spies the man off to the left, a thick foam spread across his chest. &#039;What the hell are you doing?!&#039; &#039;Relax, hoss,&#039; Pornstar the Gobbler says, expertly running the bic down across his pink chest. With the wild hair atop his head and his tendency to use words like &#039;hoss,&#039; being smooth like a woman doesn&#039;t really suit the redneck, but he doesn&#039;t know or care. &#039;Gotta keep everything nice and smooth for the ladies&#039; lapping tongues, if ya know what I&#039;m saying... For the guys, too,&#039; he says as an after-thought, then winks knowingly at Kanyon. &#039;Uh... Right,&#039; Herr Kanyon mumbles nervously. &#039;Malice style, hoss... Right in yer eye.&#039; There is a moment of profound silence as everyone stares at the gothic redneck shaving his chest, whistling the tune to some tasteless country song as he works. The madness of the island begins to take a toll on its guest, working over the easiest first... &#039;Hey there, good lookin&#039;,&#039; Shitzo says in a conversational tone, clicking his tongue against the top of his mouth. All those around him take a couple of big steps back, but it isn&#039;t to them to whom he speaks. &#039;Hello yourself, studmuffin,&#039; Shitzo replies in a slightly different voice, his face suddenly awash with amusement. &#039;Wanna head over to my place?&#039; The first voice pitches, the words rich with suggestion. &#039;Oooh... Can I come?&#039; A new voice comes into play. &#039;We can make it a threesome!&#039; &#039;Or an orgy,&#039; another voice recommends, and Shitzo follows it up with a series of affirmative grunting noises. &#039;Let&#039;s head over here, then,&#039; he says, turning and walking towards the forest. &#039;I&#039;ve got a little something to show you freaks,&#039; he chuckles in the first voice again, moving off in his own little bubble of satisfied madness. &#039;Alright,&#039; Grass suddenly shouts in a scolding tone. &#039;Who told Shitzo to go fuck himself?!&#039; Rather than responding, Boris Stained&#039;da&#039;Slav steps up to the rock and grabs Kanyon&#039;s ankle, making sure to get his attention. &#039;I see you are already into da greed, yet do not even know if it is necessary!&#039; He shouts, only revealing his fury midway through the sentence. &#039;We do not even know if this is an island! We could be anywhere!&#039; All those within earshot suddenly stop and turn back, intrigued by this rare display of sense. For a moment Herr Kanyon can only stare at the massive Russian, astounded, but then he comes to and shakes his leg away with a scowl. &#039;When the hell did you learn to use your brain, Boris? You&#039;re absolutely right...&#039; &#039;Dah,&#039; Boris grunts, &#039;so what do we do, great leader?&#039; He struggles to keep a straight face after giving Kanyon that title, but his laughter isn&#039;t easily resisted. &#039;DYAHAHAHAHA!&#039; Herr Kanyon seems to ponder this for a moment, tapping his chin thoughtfully. &#039;Alright... Hey Team FSW! You guys are in charge of finding out where we are,&#039; Kanyon shouts, drawing the attention of Devil, Tyger, and Funktacular. &#039;Two of you go that way, two go the other... Don&#039;t come back until you find civilization or meet on the other side!&#039; The three FSW members share a look, then shrug their shoulders. Tyger and Devil begin to walk off in the opposite direction of the pineapple orchard, leaving Spikey to go find Bologna. A large man, black and bald, steps directly in their path and turns his shoulder to them, facing Xaminer. &#039;Why the hell should we send these guys? I mean, Smells&#039;Em may be able to get the job done, but Funktacular? Devil?&#039; The man smirks to show what he thinks of those names, then continues. &#039;Devil thinks he&#039;s bad because he&#039;s the Universal Champion, but what the hell does that mean? Any one of us could have won that thing if we had tried!&#039; &#039;Uh... N-Dub?&#039; Xavier prompts, arching an eyebrow. &#039;You did try, remember? You and like 15 other PCWers let me down in that thing.&#039; &#039;That was crap!&#039; N-Dub Mason shouts angrily, balling his fists together at his sides, on the brink of a tantrum. &#039;I got cheated! I was, uh... sick! Yeah, sick! That&#039;s what happened!&#039; &#039;That&#039;s why you got knocked out of the first round by a PTC fedhead?&#039; Devil snickers, nonchalantly pushing Mason out of the way. He and Tyger swagger past, not bothering to give him another glance, though Devil does call back over his shoulder. &#039;You just stay here and let us real men handle this... You&#039;d probably get around this first bend and find yourself owned by Jason Chae.&#039; And so the assembly breaks up completely, with people moving every which way with any number of things on their minds. Before long they should know exactly how bad their physical predicament is, but there is no way for them to discover or gauge the depths to which their minds will plunge. Blissfully unaware of what is to come, they scurry happily away, trying to lay a foundation for order where it cannot exist.
