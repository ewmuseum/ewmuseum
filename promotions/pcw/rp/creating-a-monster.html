Creating a Monster.

Date: not entered Big Alan Steele: “Diabolical!!” We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, deep within the bowels of Stately Steele Manor… the deepest, darkest, portion of the cellars. The parts of the Manor that few people tread. Some of the blondes have whispered about dark, lonely things crying out for the warmth of humans in the corners of this dank crawlspace, and at the moment, one can surmise why. The settling of the house provides innumerable creaks and groans. The low water table creates moisture that runs down the walls, constantly drip-drip-dripping against the hard packed dirt floor, covered with decades of dust. And the steel-reinforced door which currently has our hero transfixed, a look of horror in his eyes as he slowly rubs his chin in worry. A couple of the Graces poke their heads out from behind his massive carriage, clinging to his toga in fright. Steffi: “This is INHUMAN!! How could we let her do this to herself!!” Candi: “She made us promise! We can’t give her anything or open the door for any reason, no matter WHAT, until we have to go to her match. Did you call the U-Haul people, Terri?” Terri: “Yeah, but they said we couldn’t rent from them with her in this… (gulp!)… condition!!” BOOM!! Those gathered, Steele included, jump back at the forceful pounding on the door, shaking the hinges with it’s force. BOOM!! BOOM!! A craggy, hate-filled vocal follows, sounded somewhat like that of trusted manager Sue Saksnussum but only… different… Sue: (guttural growling) “I’m hungry!! Go get them!!” One of the Graces, Tammi, bursts into tears and runs screaming from the cellar up the rickety stairs, off to fetch food. The pounding stops, and the only sound Big Al and his girls can hear are the drips of water and their own heartbeats rushing in their ears. Sue: (pretty voice) “Saaaaaay… why don’t you guys let me out of here? Hmmm? Then we can all go upstairs and watch ‘Fried Green Tomatoes’ and have a good cry. Whaddya say?” Steffi: “Well, I guess that would be alright…” Steffi reaches out for the door, but the meaty hand of Big Al slaps over it, his head shaking ‘no’ in a sad manner. BAS: “No. She has come too far to deny herself now. Her fate lies in Athena’s capable hands now, and to release her of her self-imposed bonds would warrant swift and instant retribution from her… in approximately a week or so.” Crash! Bang! Boom! The door rattles from intense force, blows from the inside as Sue’s voice turns into a high pitched wail, instilling fear in even the most hearty of souls. Sue: “YOU BASTARDS!! How can you do this to me?! I work and I slave to make your lives better and this is how you repay me?! You lock me up in a padded room with only a waste bucket for company?! INGRATES!! I made you! I made you all! And I can destroy you if… if you just open this door.” BAS: “Not one of her better arguments…” The door at the top of the stairs opens and the wailing of Tammi can be heard. A shiny red box is tossed haphazardly down. It lands with a thud on the floor. Sue: (wired) “Is that them? I know that sound! Give them to me! Give them to me!!” Concern on his face, Big Al (with the Graces still clutching at his finery) walks over and grabs the box, wiping his palm across the top to clear it of most of the dirt. He then goes back over and slowly opens the long slot at the bottom of the door, for serving meals… And a slash of wickedly sharp talons nearly takes his hand clean off! The hands continue to scrabble through the slot, eager to get a hold of something. Steele gingerly holds the heart-shaped box a good body length away from him, before dropping it down towards the slashing hands of Sue Saksnussum. The box is snatched before it hits the ground, and instantly disappears. Ripping, shredding, tearing, munching sounds echo inside causing all outside to blanche in disgust. BAS: (clucking sadly) “Ripping apart a heart shaped box of bon-bons – if tis not a metaphor for all that is not right in the world, then I do not know of one.” BURP! Steffi: “That’s all she’s eaten for the last two days! Do you think she’ll be alright?” Candi: (muttering) “Never stopped her before…” Sue: (roaring) “I HEARD THAT! So what are you saying? That I’m fat?! Is that it?! DO YOU THINK I’M FAT?!?!” Candi: (tears) “No, I meant…” Sue: (now crying) “All these years, I’ve protected you, kept the books, ruled with an iron fist… I guess they mean nothing to you all! I hate you hate you hate you hate you hate you all!” BAS: (hissing) “Stop it Candi! You know she is on an emotional and hormonal roller coaster!! The slightest misstep in words shall have her attacking you as if the enemy!!” Sue: “OH, so Big freakin’ Alan goddamned Steele has to put in his two cents, huh? Are you saying I’m sensitive!” BAS: (yelling at door) “By the gods, woman, yes! Tis only natural!!” Sue: “You’re damn right its natural! You treat me like an ANIMAL, and you expect me to not be upset?! What have I done to deserve this?” Steffi: “B-B-B-But you told us to do this!” Sue: “Shut up! Didn’t you realize what I REALLY meant?! You unfeeling BASTARDS!! I’LL KILL YOU ALL!!” Terrible grating and scratching noises at the steel door make the Graces squeal in fright and our decidedly at the moment less-Bold hero jump back in alarm. Horrible, horrible screams and wailings and gnashing of teeth that bring up ‘Exorcist’ comparisons. BAS: (sweating) “Great Zeus! What have we done!” Candi: (crying) “All this for a stupid, stupid match against a fat cow! I miss the old Sue! I miss how she used to order us around and make us do things and yell at us…” Steffi: “Dude, that wasn’t cool…” Sue: (demonic) “I will swallow your souls! BWAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!” Candi: (hysterical) “It was better than that! I LIKE my soul! It’s fun!” BAS: (breathing deeply) “I believe we must all calm down – this was Lady Saksnussum’s plan. We cannot be faulted for the destiny of another as tis not our responsibility. She chose her path and now she must walk it.” Sue: (sweetly) “Hey guys? How about letting me out, huh? I’ll be good. What do you say we all go out to Olive Garden… my treat!” BAS: “Quiet thee, foul demonic temptress! We know that your menstruation shall remain for another few days!” Sue: (demonic) “SWALLOW YOUR SOULS!!! BWAHAHAHAHAHA!!!” Steffi: (meekly) “Can’t I just give her a little bit of Midol?” BAS: “NO! You’ll ruin everything!” Sue: “Coffee! Cigarettes! Midol! Why must you take away all that is good in life?!?! I’m retaining water like a sponge! My nic-fits are everlasting! My caffeine intake is dangerously low!! And the CRAMPS! My god, the CRAMPS!! I lied! Deal is off! Time to let me out!!” BAS: (quietly) “You can have all that, and MORE, once the Chef’s significant replicant is beaten into submission by your own hands.” Sue: (normal, breathing hard) “And… and cigarettes too?” Steele leans back, thinking hard. Candi: “C’mon, Al! After all this, she’s earned them!” Steffi: “Yeah, and mostly because we can’t take her spitting tobacco habit anymore – she leaves spitters everywhere!” BAS: (relenting a bit) “You shall be allowed a singular pack AFTER completion of your holy mission.” Sue: (crying) “You are the best, Big Guy! I swear it! The best!!” BAS: (smiling) “I know, I know…” Candi: “Wow! With incentive like that, Sue can’t lose!” Sue: (howling) “What is THAT supposed to mean, huh?! You’re dead, you hear me? DEAD!!” BAS: (frowning) “At this rate, I just hope the main supports of the house hold up under her barrage.” Sue: (wailing) “A fate worse than death, by my own FRIENDS!!” Steffi: “Still, Bertha’s finished. That has to give you some good feelings.” BAS: “This PMS is a terrible fiend, indeed, to unleash upon the populace! I just worry about our transportation of her to the arena, we may not make it in one piece!!” Sue: (evil) “Not if I can help it…” BAS: (weak thumbs up) “Hi-Keeba?”
