Date: 5 - 7 - 03 Shortly after the chaotic and somewhat embarrassing events of Turmoil, the participants in the main event have sunk back into their little locker rooms to pack their things and continue on to the next foreign venue… except one man, that is. One man is still seething from his loss, trying to devise maniacal ways to get back at those who did him wrong… That man is Dorn Tracer. The Astronomer hurriedly stuffs his possessions into his duffle bag, his arms jerking in an enraged manner. His hair is still unkempt, his forehead is cut and bleeding from the chair shot he received from his so-called stablemate, and the rest of his body is sore from the fight with The Druid. But what hurt worst of all… was his pride. ‘Fucking moron!’ Dorn roars, still stuffing the sports bag. ‘Not only is he an infidel, he’s BLIND! I bet Skylar fucking Green could swing a chair better than that sand-sucking ass pirate! I CAN’T BELIEVE HE HIT ME!’ With that fleeting cry, Tracer sweeps the bag off the bench and sends it flying across the room, with the contents scattering in the breeze. The Astronomer falls to the bench and buries his face in his hands, trying to contemplate what happened. Dorn knew he could not just blame it on Pharaoh… it was the conjoined efforts of The Druid and him both. But why the fuck did he feel the need to hit ME? Tommy was right there! The thought runs through his head again and again… Maybe it was a mistake, or maybe his aim was just a little off. After all, he IS blinded by his blasphemous ways…, but none of them make any sense to Dorn. Why would his own partner hit him? If it was a mistake, it was a damn stupid mistake. En’Flarim would never allow such dribble, so why would Ra or Osiris or King Tut or whatever allow it? ‘BECAUSE THEY DON’T EXIST!’ The Astronomer jumps to his feet, proclaiming to the heavens above. He turns around and slams his foot into the bench, flinching a little from the pain, then turns and stomps towards his duffle bag. The Astronomer picks up the bag, making sure the TV Title is secure inside, and stops off… headed to the airport where the next venue, and probably screw job, is: Athens, Greece.
