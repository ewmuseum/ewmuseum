ROCK the Vote!

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-7-02 (It's a beautiful day in Orange County as we are introduced to the skyline of Anaheim, California--site of PCW's 2nd annual Spring Fling. The warm Southern California sun shines brightly on the land below, bathing it in a warm glow. The criss-crossing highways bustle with cars going about their daily business and a couple of rollerbladers glide along the sidewalks near a lush park. Zooming into a cluster of buildings, we notice an elementary school packed with cars and news station vans. The reason for this is evident by Frisco's huge campaign bus. The bus is roped off and guarded by men in brown overcoats and automatic weapons. The gunner at the top of the bus slowly turns his turret around and searches for any troublemakers among the loud children who swarm the bus. We enter the brick building known as Running Springs Elementary school where we're immediately brought to the cafeteria where hundreds of small children sit at their benches. The benches circle a leather recliner from Frisco's bus, and on it is the man himself--the future dictator of Argentina and PCW Title Sweeper, Francisco Valerius. Frisco smiles brightly, straightens his red velvet FRISCACEMANI~! suit and puffs on his Cuban cigar. The principal of the school, a tall plump woman walks up to the microphone near Frisco and begins to speak.) Principal: 'Welcome students!' (The chattering begins to quiet down.) Principal: 'Now, today is a very special day for us all. Mr. Francisco Valerius, PCW wrestler and candidate for the presidency of Argentina is here to talk to us about politics and answer your questions about voting and electing presidents.' (Some of the kids clap. Frisco nods and puffs on his cigar.) Principal: 'It is my understanding that you've all been given a number and told to come up with questions for Mr. Valerius in your classes, so whenever your number is called, you may stand up and ask one of you questions. But if you don't want to, that is okay.' (Frisco looks on with boredom and annoyance as he rubs his temples.) Principal: 'You have been told to be very quiet and listen to what this nice man is saying. In many of your classes, you will be tested on this presentation, so pay attention!' (A few scattered groans are heard from the students.) Principal: 'And here he is, the next president of Argentina...Frisco!' (The kids clap and cheer. Frisco's head jerks up and sighs. He then stands up and waves to the children as he shakes the principal's hand. Frisco then grabs the mic and paces around with his smoking cigar in his hand.) Frisco: 'Good afternoon, students! As your principal has informed you, I am Frisco. Some of you may know me as a PCW superstar, but I am much more than that.' (Frisco paces around with his cigar leaving a trail of fine Cuban tobacco smoke.) Frisco: 'Pro wrestling is a fine career and anyone who is a semi-talented athlete with vocal cords has the ability to make a good living from it...just ask my opponent for Spring Fling, Legend Inc.' (A kid from the audience begins to cheer.) Student #1: 'Legend Inc. ruuuuuuuuullllllllleeeeesssssssssss!!!!!!!!!' (Frisco frowns and motions over to a Hispanic man in sunglasses and a black suit. The guard quickly runs over to the child and grabs him by the arm.) Student #1: 'Hey! Leave me alone! Where are you taking m--...' (The strange man muffles the child's mouth and drags him out of the cafeteria.) Frisco: 'Now, as I was saying, pro wrestling is a respectable career...as is police officer, doctor, lawyer or just about anything else. However for outstanding individuals, the career for you is a leader of people!' (Frisco smiles warmly and peers at the children. His smile turns into a semi-frown as he sees most of the kids not paying attention.) Frisco: 'I realize that many of you...well, all of you will never be able to amount to as much as I have amounted to, but you can always dream. You need to shoot as high as possible when you're looking for a goal to accomplish. Never set your dreams too low, and never be modest when it comes to what you want to do.' (The principal and the other teachers in the room clap, as do the children.) Frisco: 'I am living your dream, kids. I am living the people's dream. And through me, the people of Argentina will accomplish their dreams!' (Frisco looks down and mutters slightly.) Frisco: 'Unless their dream is to overthrow my government and take my place, in which they will be captured, tortured, and executed....' (Frisco realizes he spoke out loud and changes the subject with enthusiasm.) Frisco: 'Well, kids, I won't keep you from participation, so I will now accept questions!' (Frisco motions to the principal, who then grabs a box filled with slips of paper. She pulls out a piece of paper, unfolds it, and speaks into Frisco's microphone.) Principal: 'Number 234...' (A small boy in a red shirt stands and raises his hand.) Frisco: 'Yes, you there!' (The boy picks up a piece of paper and reads a question.) Student #2: 'Yeah, um, I was just gonna...' Frisco: 'Speak up, son!' (The kids laugh and the boy begins to get nervous.) Frisco: 'Go ahead...' Student #2: 'Well, in class we were supposed to like...' Frisco: 'I didn't ask for your life story. Just questions, please.' (The small boy begins to sniffle.) Student #2: 'D-do do you like being the president?' (Frisco rolls his eyes.) Frisco: 'Have you been listening, child? I'm not the president yet, I'm a presidential candidate! And by that, I mean I will soon be president. And to answer your inaccurate question, I enjoy this very much.' (Frisco puffs on his Cuban, which is almost burned to the end.) Frisco: 'I enjoy this because I get to travel all around the world, spreading the world about how I will help people. Of course, I would much rather be the president right now, but some inexperienced idiot was appointed before I was, and he hasn't resigned yet. However, I am happy to be campaigning for this upcoming democratic election that I will win. This is the chance for the people to see the ecstasy that will come when I am elevated to guider of lives, savior of people, and ruler of all that I survey!' (Frisco looks at all the confused students.) Frisco: 'Well, to put it in simpler terms, being president will be fun because I'll get to make everything how I want it. Nobody will tell me what to do. Who likes it when their parents tell them what to do? Anyone?' Students: 'Booooooooooooo!' Frisco: 'Exactly. I will get to do anything I want to do and I will get to tell everyone what to do. I am just like your parents, except I didn't make any horrible mistakes like creating a child.' (Frisco chuckles, as do the teachers. The students look on in confusion.) Frisco: 'Next question?' (The principal picks out another piece of paper and reads it.) Principal: 'Number 42, are you here?' (A small girl with blonde pigtails stands up and smiles at Frisco.) Frisco: 'Hello, sweetie, what is your question?' Student #3: 'I've seen your commercials and they're cool, but what are they for?' (Frisco nods and takes a drag of his cigar one more time before tossing it onto the floor.) Frisco: 'Good question, little girl. Those are campaign ads. I do them because it spreads the word of what I will do for my people when I become president. They also bash my political opponents so people will not fall for their bullshit and vote for them.' (The children giggle.) Frisco: 'They are similar to my promos I do for my wrestling matches. Do you watch the PCW Promo Channel?' Student #3: 'No, but my brother does.' Frisco: 'Yes, well your brother should know that some of them are better than others, and the ones that are better get more people liking them.' (Frisco smiles.) Frisco: 'And what did I tell you about Legend Inc.'s promos when I showed them to you in the bus on the way over?' (The students giggle and speak in unison.) Students: 'THIS IS WRESTLING, NOT A FUCKING ROBERT FROST POEM!' Frisco: 'Very good!' (The children bashfully cover their mouths and giggle at what they just said. Frisco chuckles and rustles the hair of a young boy a few feet away.) Frisco: 'Now just as wrestlers do our little skits, presidential candidates have to do them too. My friends have worked very hard on making ads that will show what I will do for the people and at the same time telling people what the other person will not do. See what I'm saying?' (A few scattered 'yeah's can be heard.) Frisco: 'Now, next qu--...' (An associate appears out of nowhere to tap Frisco on the shoulder and whisper in his ear. Frisco's eyes bulge.) Frisco: 'Uh oh, there's some business that I have to attend to, so I'm going to have to cut this real short. You all may go home now!' (The kids cheer for being freed from school on a Sunday.) Frisco: 'I'll come back later and talk to you again, but for now, be sure to ROCK the vote!' (Frisco waves and quickly exits the cafeteria as the children clap and the teachers begin to guide the kids out. The scene switches out to Frisco hopping into the bus and the bus speeding away and fades to the blue sky.)
