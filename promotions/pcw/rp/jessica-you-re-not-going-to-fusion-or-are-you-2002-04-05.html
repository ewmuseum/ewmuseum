Jessica, you're not going to Fusion!.... Or, are you?

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-5-02 It's the night of Fusion. The last show before Spring Fling. It's the last time either man can make a statemen to the other before they meet for the Continental title at the pay per view. On recent shows, its been the Gold Patron Meritorious making the statements, by either putting Krayzie through a Snapple machine or beating him repeatedly with a chair. Will Fusion be the same news, or will a new one be written? With the way this fued has gone, neither man might not make it out of Fusion. Beforehand though, Jeremy has a loose end to tie up. Because, it seems a certain desirable female thinks she's going to Fusion to get her hands on Eleanor Hall. In the mind of Krayzie, such is not the case. The Krazed Lunatic can't risk Jessica's health now. Not this late in the game. So, as the scene fades in from black to the master bedroom of Jeremy's home in Elkhart, Indiana, it's understandable that the mood is dark. Jessica: What do you mean I can't go? I go EVERYWHERE with you! Jessica, wearing only a flowery purple night gown, stands beside the couple's king size bed, not in the loving mood fans are used to seeing her in. Jeremy stands inside of the walk-in closet, packing clothes into his wrestling bag. Jeremy: Because, I can't risk letting you get hurt before the match. Jessica: I'm old enough to take care of myself! Jeremy sighs, trying to ignore his lady love's pleas. Recently, Eleanor Hall has made it widely known that she intends to bring harm on Jessica if she comes face to face with the bleach blonde beauty. Something of which the Krayzie One plans to not let happen. Jeremy: I've already said all I'm going to say. I have to get my things ready so I can go. Jessica storms over to the doorway of the walk-in closet, continuing her protest. Jessica: But I just want to get her back for what she did to you on Sabotage with the set up and everything. Jeremy: I have enough to worry about with executing my plan. I don't have time to be worrying about you. Jessica: You don't have to. I can take care of myself. Jeremy turns his head toward her, with a not so pleasant look on his face. Jeremy: Correct me if I'm wrong, but didn't you almost get attacked back at the school by those three men when you tried to save that girl? Jessica knods her head, 'yes'. She knows that if Jeremy hadn't been there, there's no telling what might have happened to her. For her sake and the petite Puerto Rican girls, Jeremy was there. Jessica: This is different. It's girl on girl. Not three men and two women. Jeremy: Save the girl on girl action for a lesbian video. No! Unhappy at her progress, Jessica stomps her feet away from the walk-in closet, over toward the window of her bedroom. From her view, she can see her entire backyard. In her backyard, she can see Jeremy's seventeen year old brother, the infamous Justin, playing basketball on the Krayzie One's custom built basketball court. Jessica: Will you at least take Justin for you own safety? Jeremy shakes his head, getting angry at her constant persistence that he needs help. Jeremy: Do I look like some kind of weakling? I can handle myself just fine. Jessica: Like you did on Sabotage when I wasn't there? Jeremy doesn't answer. Rather, he grabs his wrestling bag and walks out of the walk-in closet. Jessica turns away from the window, toward Jeremy. She notices his displeasure in his last statement. Jessica: I'm sorry. I just want to be there for you. Jeremy: Then stay home! Jessica: No! I don't want too! Slam! The door shuts on Jeremy's bedroom. Jessica in the bedroom, Jeremy outside of it. Jessica stares at the door, feeling the urge to cry. She stands there, a state of shock coming over her. Jessica: He wouldn't leave.... Would he? Jessica moves over to the door, reaching out for the handle. As she listens, she can hear Jeremy's black 2002 Cadillac Escalade start up. She turns the handle, opening the door. The Escalade begins to move out of the garage. Jessica rushes out of the bedroom, pushing right by Troy playing in the den, and out toward where the Escalade would be. It's gone! Jessica: Jeremy! Come back! The only Jessica can see is the rear end of the Escalade and the 'Krayzie' license plate on the back, leaving the street in which Jeremy's residence resides on. Jessica's spirit seemingly falls. Her feelings hurt. Jessica: No way is he just going to leave me and go to Fusion alone. Instead of agonizing over it, Jessica decides to act on something. She rushes away from the garage and to the backdoor. Justin continues to play basketball on the court. Jessica: Justin! I need you quickly! Justin, being the supportive brother/brother-in-law that he can be, drops the basketball and rushes as quickly as he can run over to where Jessica stands, still in her nightgown, in the backdoor. Justin: What is it?!? Jessica: I need you to watch Troy. I have to get to Fusion before Jeremy executes his so-called plan. Justin: I thought he said he wanted you to stay home. Jessica sighs, giving Justin that 'I know, but I'm going to defy it anyways' look. Jessica: He did. But the last time he went to a show alone, he got put through a damn Snapple machine and spent two days in a hospital. I can't let it happen again. Justin shrugs his shoulders. Justin: Alright. I have fun with the little guy anyways..... Just be careful so he doesn't ring both our necks. Jessica: I'm going to keep him safe. He should appreciate that..... Sorry, I gotta get ready to go. Justin doesn't get a chance to reply as Jessica bolts out of the dining room. She races into her bedroom and into the walk-in closet. A censor covers half of the screen, but it's obvious that Jessica is changing. It takes her all of seconds before she runs out of the walk-in closet, now wearing tight fitting blue jeans and the wildly popular black 'Krayzie Till I Die' t-shirt. Jessica gets to the dining room, grabbing a set of keys. Jessica: Justin, make sure Troy doesn't mess up the Playstation again. And, remember, he likes to watch cartoons right before he goes to bed. Justin: I know. I know. Me and him are like best buddies. We know each other inside and out. Jessica smiles. Jessica: You're a life saver. Justin: So you don't mind that I stay here? Jessica jerks her head back, kind of shocked at the question. Jessica: Why would I be? If it makes Jeremy happy that you're here, then I'm happy. Besides, it's nice having someone here that I can trust to take care of Troy. Justin: Thanks. That means alot. Jessica: Just take care of my little boy. I have to go take care of his father. Justin grins, finding humor in Jessica's words. She gives her brother-in-law a quick hug, then she races through the house and back to the garage. With the number of vehicles Jeremy has in his garage, which one does she take? Her first car and dream car. Jessica rushes over to the red 1998 Ferrari 355 F1 Spider. Jessica gets into the Ferrari and peels out of the garage, heading toward Fusion. As the scene begins fading out, the question is, is she going to help or hurt Jeremy's plan...... to black!
