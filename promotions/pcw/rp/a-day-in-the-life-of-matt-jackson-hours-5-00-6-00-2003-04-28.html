A Day In The Life Of Matt Jackson, Hours 5:00-6:00

28 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/28/03 Dreams are something everyone has…some people just have too many of them. All this week, three men have been having dreams about me and dreams about their success in PCW. First Stone Burns…he had a dream of being able to kill me. He believed that he was going to be able to take out the famous agent with just a knife and a gun…kill me? In all my time in the CIA…people have tried to kill me. None have been successful…they all failed. Yet Stone Burns thought he could do it in a mere matter of minutes…he thought that he could do something else that no else had done before him…he was going to be the first. Too bad it was nothing more then a fantasy because I am still being held hostage…no one kills Matt Jackson. Then Sivasian Krikorian the famed Sniper dreamed he had a chance to strike out against me. He wanted to have a chess game with the killer that is Christian MacManus…me. Even in his dream he underestimated the man he knew nothing about…it cost him. At Turmoil he won’t have to pay with his life for underestimating me…he will only have to pay with a loss. The stakes aren’t as high for him inside the PCW…he won’t have to pay with his life. James Edwards has a dream of his own and that is to become a professional wrestler. His ambitions are much different than mine…he wants to make the PCW…I want to destroy the PCW. The only problem with Mr. Edwards dream is that it is unrealistic…much like Stone Burns and Sivasian Krikorian. It is just too bad that dreams are easy to crush…that they aren’t real. I dream that I am going to be able to escape from this prison I am…getting back to the woman that I love. The only problem with dreaming it is that…I know it isn’t going to come true. Dreams never come true no matter how badly you want them. The only way to get things done is to stand up and do them yourself. I will escape…I will be free…I will get back to Haley. -Christian Michael MacManus *** Location: Matt Jackson’s Apartment, Washington, DCDate: April 22, 2003Time: 5:00 am Matt Jackson’s apartment isn’t that great in size…it is only one bed and one bath, but it is still cozy. The living room is the most commonly used room; with it’s faded grey walls and old furnished hardwood floors. All around the walls are pictures of Matt with his adopted family. Along the back of the room sits a leather couch; his pillows in disarray and the blanket that sits on it normally is sprawled out across the floor. In front of the couch is an oak table, large in size, but it fits comfortably into the small room and just past the table along the front wall are two green recliners. Seated on the couch is Haley Hilton, the girlfriend and manger of Matt Jackson. Across from her in one of the recliners is her sister Laura Hilton; her elder…by five years. She is always here when Haley needs her and now is a time that the beautiful blonde needs help. Her boyfriend is missing and worse off it’s at the hands of her father…Jason Hilton. “Why do you think he is doing this to us Laura?” Haley asks. Her voice is full of anger and rage. Her slender face tells a different story…it tells that she is just a scared little girl, worried about the man that has been protecting her…the man she loves. Shaking her head…tossing her shoulder length black hair out of the way…Laura just looks at Haley and smiles. “Dad is protective Haley and he got scared something was going to happen to his little princess.” Scrunching her face…Haley grits her teeth. “I am not a little girl anymore…I don’t need dad to look over me at my every turn. There was no need for him to stick his nose into my business and ruin my life. H-he just had no right!” “I am totally agreeing with you Haley. He went over the deep end this time and he had no right doing what he did to Matt,” she humbly offers, trying to make her sister feel good as always. Haley puts her shaking petite hands up to her pale blue eyes, as tears begin to fall from them; like an angle crying for a sinner…she blames this on herself. Thinking maybe there could have been a way to stop her father from taking Matt…from keeping him from her. She wants to stand up for herself, but the strength isn’t there…nothing is there, other then fear and sadness. Standing up…Laura glides across the wooden floor, her jeans swishing back and forth, as she moves. Taking a seat next to Haley, she puts her arm around her shoulder and lets her little sister cry into her green American Eagle tee shirt…smiling all the while. Noticing that her sister is almost exactly like her, they have the same personalities, dress the same…such as today and they are always there for one another. Patting her back, Laura offers: “Don’t worry Haley…dad isn’t going to do anything that will kill Matt. He is a lot smarter than that and he doesn’t want to spend the rest of his life in jail…plus Matt is trained for things like this. You told me yourself…everything is going to be fine.” Looking up into Laura’s emerald eyes…Haley wipes the tears away from her face, smearing her make-up a bit in the process. This only causes a smile to curl on her power-pink lips…letting Laura know that she is glad for her being there. “W-what if the people dad hired do kill him though?” Haley probes…wanting to make sure that Laura has all bases covered and is hopefully going to give her a little piece of mind…to let her know that everything is going to be safe. “Then we go to the police and we send our own father to jail. He is in the wrong her Haley not you,” Laura replies with a gentle voice. One that puts her sister at ease…stamping it down for the final time that everything is all right. With her arm still around the youngest Hilton…the sisters gradually drift into sleep. Resting for the first time in many hours…resting, hoping that the man they care for is going to be fine and hope that the man they lost respect for pays. *** Location: UnknownDate: April 22, 2003Time: 5:30 am I need to get out of here…I need to get Haley back…I need to fulfill my dreams. No matter how impossible and worthless dreams are I want to take the step and make it happen. I don’t want them to be fantasies anymore…I want them to be reality. Matt remains firmly in place…tied in the small room he is starting to call home. His whole 5’8” frame…still screaming out in pain, from the constant abuse he is receiving here and not to mention the beating he took from Makonie on Fusion. Everything about his body explains what is going on in his life…the mass of cuts and bruises on his barrel of a chest…the cuts that plague his face and worst of all the fact he is away from his princess. Makonie has her…he has Haley and I am not going to stand for it. I want her back…I want to protect her and keep her away from that bastard father of hers. He is the reason I am in this situation…he is the one keeping me from my goal and my career. He dreams I will disappear from his daughter’s life and just like Burns, Edwards, and Sivasian Krikorian… his dreams are going to amount to nothing. Bloody, beaten and broken…Matt continues to hang from the rope in the tiny room. The stench of blood and vomit lingers in the air…Matt’s blood and vomit. Snapping the fog out of his head, Matt lets his legs support most of his weight…the rope was beginning to cut into his raw flesh once more and he couldn’t keep the pressure on it. Suddenly the door on his right swings open and in walks the man…the man that has been taking him to PCW shows…the man who has been delivering quite a beating to him. Matt stares at the man through bloodshot brown eyes…the man only stares at his naked beaten body and smiles, walking forward, letting his grey suit sway like a sail in the wind. “Matt…you tried to escape after Fusion and that worries me,” he says in a scruffy voice. “I thought you were starting to learn and figure out that if you don’t follow our rules…punishment ensues. Just leave her Matt…it will be so much easier.” Tossing his head back…Matt removes the bits of bloody brown hair that hang down in his face. He cracks a small smile, as the pain continues to shoot through his body…the sweat in the open wounds is like nothing he has ever felt before, but there is no comfort in the hot room. “I gave you my answer and it is final. I am not going to let Jason Hilton win…he won’t beat me,” Matt tells the man, licking the blood from his lips. He is going to get another beating and he knows it, but he is ready for it. “He will beat you…he has the best on his side,” the man quickly replies…making his point known with a stinging backhand. “I am the best…he can’t beat me. This is my element and you guys are just rookies in it…welcome to my game.” His words send the man into a fit of rage…making him give Matt back hand after back hand…punch after punch. Not stopping even with his hand in pain…the man treks on making sure that Matt understands his point and Matt does, but he isn’t going to fall to it. The man finally stops when Matt is a bloody unconscious mess. My dream was once again destroyed…but I will get out of this mess, I will get back to you Haley…I will make your dreams come true. 6:00 AM
