Criticism

21 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/21/02 *Monologue* Critics. Throughout the world there are many of them. They poke at Eminem and Elton Johns relationship. They tell us that Striptease was a bad movie. They also criticize how Britney Spears dresses because she influences the youth of girls in America. If that’s the case I hope she becomes a nudist. What do critics really do? It’s just a mans opinion publicly. Some jealous a$$hole that didn’t make it anywhere but since they didn’t they do what everybody else does. B*tch. Except publicly. Sure, America gave the freedom of speech. But damn I want to take my freedom of not listening to them. Because frankly nobodies listening and nobody cares. We’re all critics. Just we don’t do it publicly. Don’t tell me you never criticized how stupid some kid is or how in the hell Karl LeBlanc got into the PCW. Big Daveylicious is no stranger of criticism. When he beat up the women of the PCW. Woman's rights protesters called him a pervert and that his actions were absence. When in fact they looked past the fact that maybe. Just maybe he was giving the women roster a chance in the PCW. But nooooooo he’s a pervert. Even as Extreme Champion Big D hasn’t been forgotten by critics. Calling him a filler champion. They look past of what he accomplishes. They look past the good things. They just go to what? The bad things. They ignore his good things he‘s accomplished and focus on the bad things. Because they want to b*tch about somebody else than themselves. Believe it or not I’m being a critic right now just mentioning them. So I guess that makes me an a$$hole too. (The scene fades to the White Mexicans face. As we move back we are not only looking at Mexi’s face. But were looking at Mexi’s face from the cover of PCW’s April Magazine. We back away to see the magazine in the face of the man. He sits on a leather reclining chair. He’s clad in khaki shorts and a black Vans Hawaiian shirt. Suddenly, the magazine is pulled away onto his lap reveling it is none other than the face of Big Daveylicious. He shakes his head. Seems a little disappointed even with his Extreme title around his waist he still seems disappointed.) Big Daveylicious: Graves, get in here! (Suddenly, we back away again to revel it is the living room of Big D’s beach house. His lackey, Michael Graves hurries over to Big D clad in black and face paint.) Big Daveylicious: I thought you said naked women were in this issue? Michael Graves: That’s Play Boy. Big Daveylicious: Oh...am I in this magazine? Michael Graves: You are in two columns. Divisional Dissecting and The B@stard Speaks #5. (Big D grabs onto the magazine like a child in joy awaiting a surprise. He scurries through the pages ‘till he stops at a page. He reads it out loud.) Big Daveylicious: “DIVISIONAL DISSECTING with the Dissector” Who writes this stuff? Skip, skip, skip. Ah, here I am. “BIG D AS CHAMPION YOU SAY? Now, if he had won it of Brandi instead of Hank~! I could have bought it. But this toned down Big D is suffering the same as his supposed tag partner in that nobody really cares. “ How dare he speaks of his Extreme Champion this way?! Nobody cares about me winning the Extreme title?! My mom did...My dad did...and most importantly I did! What does he mean bought it? I won the damn gold at the pay-per-view defeating Core in a Figure Four. Michael Graves: Big D, you know wrestling's fake, right? Big Daveylicious: No, it’s not! Michael Graves: You’ve been wrestling a good two years now and you didn’t know it was fake. Big Daveylicious: It’s not fake. It’s real. I’m the Extreme Champion! Michael Graves: Core sold that finish to you. Big Daveylicious: You paid him to lose to me?! Michael Graves: No...forget it. Big Daveylicious: “It may have gotten him some heat but Big D as a woman hater worked, it was right...Who is Big D now? Well, apart from the guy with the Extreme Title I couldn't tell you anything else.” When did I become a woman hater? I like women. But love naked women! Why do people take it you hate women because you beat them up? I just don’t get it. You know how much it hurts. He broke my little heart. Most of those women on the roster were b*tches. Not women. Michael Graves: I knew it you didn’t have sex with them! Big Daveylicious: Who do you think those three naked women were at the pay-per-view? Michael Graves: They have cooties and Momma told me to never touch their private parts. It’s the devil! Big Daveylicious: Graves, I thought you came from the Third sector of Hell? Don’t you praise him? Michael Graves: Just because I am from the third sector of Hell! Doesn’t mean I believe in the Devil! (Michael puts out a little tear.) Big Daveylicious: Well, whomever the Dissector is? I hated science too, Little Dissector. But you got to quit packing your fits of frustration in to your little bundle of crap that you call a column. But I call an opinion. If I were you I’d dissect myself my “I’m afraid to let you know who I am” pen name and go with that. But than again you probably got your balls dissected. Michael Graves: Yeah, he didn’t even talk about your personal life. What kind of critic is he?! Big Daveylicious: Very well. Now, off to “The Bastard Speaks” (He turns a few pages and stops at the page. Once again he reads it out loud.) Big Daveylicious: LeBlanc sucks, yada, yada, yada... Oh here I am! My names mentioned twice! “As for people who I don't think are worth while keeping... here's a selection. Mike Frampton... he's been Big Daveylicious' tag team partner... erm... that's his claim to fame... and he was in FSW as well I believe... but I don't think that is something to boast about. “ What’s his problem? I mean Framptons lucky I considered him as a tag partner. I mean he had the Extreme Champion as his tag partner. Now, what about me? “Big Daveylicious... was in a feud with some women and then finally won the Extreme title in a huge shock... and since Mike Extreme is a one time Extreme Title holder, I guess that belt must mean a great deal... not... “ It does mean a great deal! I’m the Extreme Champion, damn it! Not a guy who hides behind a pen name in his mothers basement with his comic book collection. But than again you’re a dog? Well, Sparky fetch! Fetch your damn balls and don’t hide behind a pen name. Because nobody cares! Michael Graves: But I... Big Daveylicious: Shut up! (Big D stops reading and tears the magazine in half. He folds his hands calmly as the pieces of paper from the magazine go everywhere on the room.) Michael Graves: Now, was the really necessary? Big Daveylicious: I’m the Extreme champion! Michael Graves: Now, I gotta to clean up that mess. Aren’t you gonna help? Big Daveylicious: No. (Mike stomps his foot as he picks up the pieces of the magazine.) Big Daveylicious: Maybe I should relax. (He turns on his television with his remote in his hand. We spot an old woman talking.) Old Women: I don’t like that, Big Daveylicious. He doesn’t kiss babies or dress like James Dean or even acts like a gentleman. He’s a pervert. He has blond hair and if anything why does he wear those sunglasses all the time?Why doesn‘t he dress up like that nice boy, Thompson from Cooper street..... (An angered Big D turns off his television with the remote.) Big Daveylicious: Does everybody hate me? (The scene fades to a 32-year-old nerdy man inside his parent’s basement. Typing on his computer just after seeing Big D’s promo. ) Nerdy Man: Ohhhhhhhhh, I hate that Big Daveylicious! He’s evil. I’m gonna e-mail him in big red bold letters. That should show him for telling me to get some balls. (With that he types on as the scene fades to utter voices of other critics complaining about Britney Spears boobs.)
