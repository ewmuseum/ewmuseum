Can We All Just Get Along?

10 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10 - 10 - 02 The PCW camera-crew swamps the backstage of the company's training complex like a swarm of bees as they try to unearth the next big story. With Red October almost a week ago, there remains many un-answered questions waiting to be unveiled to the world, the next question however who will be the first to shred some light on the happenings of Primetime Championship Wrestling? The camera sways back and forth as its bearer steps hotfooted through corridors and abandoned locker rooms like the filming work of 'The Blair Witch Project'. Suddenly this entire scene halts affixed at a wall with a small but clear note that read, 'Alexander Miyagawa and Anarchy, We being Pranks With Attitude have called an urgent meeting in the Conference Room; all details have been left on your respected answering machines... We would advise you to attend.' Basking momentarily on the brief message, the scene slowly draws to a close. Almost instantly the scene re-emerges within the confines of the secluded conference room, lavishing in formal arrangements. A podium has been pre-constructed with table draped in a crimson red covering that looks down at the congregation of seats below from an acute angle. The camera sits at the back, away from prying eyes awaiting the 'BIG' unveiling, nothing is concrete as this could be a prep talk, a general bonding of Asylum or something to rock the very foundations of this four-man collective. 'Well these guys are always unpredictable so this should be interesting!' said the distinctive voice of Alexander Miyagawa as he stepped into the room, followed closely by another member of his Asylum flock in Anarchy. The two sit parallel to the built up stage, in the midst of chairs, fixated in conversation, piecing together the reasoning behind this unexpected gathering. 'Maybe Gaz needs perking up, I mean he hit the bottle hard....' smiled Anarchy, her face alight with happiness. A real u-turn to her more recent showing of emotion, well after all, she parted company with the Cruiserweight Championship and found out the truth behind her identity. 'Duzza hit that chair shot harder don't you think!' interrupted the man once regarded as Miya-Jobber. 'Well the event was a charade - Between Ellie's personal vendetta for the team seeping through and the odds tabled against them, I admire their valiant effort!' replied the mysterious Anarchy, the essence of her troubled past a real talking point. 'Yeah, but they dropped the ball at Red October....' said the luchadore before being intruded upon by none-other than Gaz 'The Jester' Green, appearing almost from thin air or possibly the Neo Void. 'Well Miya old boy... Call us a collective of losers, because if my memory serves me right Red October was a shambles!!!' 'Oh and thanks for the help - Gaz was almost crippled courtesy of the loser Duzza and I sustained a nasty headache....' rambled Nerezza with a hiss, his face personifying evil. 'Did offer the big man a tablet but he insisted on a virgin!' injected the Conniving Comic, subtly winking at his female comrade. 'AND DO YOU KNOW HOW HARD THEY ARE TO FIND!!!' groaned the goliath, ushering his Pranks With Attitude (P.W.A) partner to the crafted stage, not realising this charade will soon become common knowledge. The devious duo slouch into the seating, watched on by both their peers and the press. The once jovial face of Gaz Green cowers beneath a snarling frown, a similar expression adorned on his trench coat wearing colleague Noscosta Ireul Nerezza. 'To business... Simple really, Friday Night Fusion we have a return match for the PCW Tag Team Championships and would like a little participation this week!' snarled the titanic Nerezza, his eyes cold heartedly locked on those of Alexander Miyagawa. 'And that was the polite way of asking...' continued Gaz Green rolling his eyes sarcastically. 'Now what’s the harsh version?!?' questioned the former number one contender to the Heavyweight crown Alexander Miyagawa. 'Err-... Arr-... Hmm-... Please Mr. Miyagawa would you, PLEASE GET OFF YOUR ASS AND SHOW US A LITTLE SUPPORT, HUH!!!' roared the Emerald Menace, it's recipient a little taken back and his long suffering friend Nerezza impressed, congratulating him further with a high five. 'Now you guys wait a god damn minute....' cut-in the Asylum leader, prompting a comment intrusion from the PCW vampire Nerezza. 'NO YOU WAIT A GOD DAMN MINUTE. WE HAVE GIVEN HUNDRED PERCENT EVERY WEEK, WE'VE AIDED YOU EVERY STEP OF THE WAY AND WHEN PRANKS WITH ATTITUDE NEEDED A LITTLE ASSISTANCE WE FIND OURSELVES THROWN TO THE LIONS!!!' 'Anarchy how about assisting me now huh?!?' asked the master of the five elements frog-splash, almost pleading with his incoherent partner. 'What-... Did you say something?' murmured Anarchy, her mind and soul traveling north and south as she deals with her own personal troubles. The group falls into an intense silence as Asylum tries to overcome its biggest obstacle, themselves. From being the thorn in the side of the Front Office they have now been reduced to squabbling children, none willing to take the bull by its horns. 'I say to you 'What Asylum?' Anarchy is having loyalty issues and you Miyagawa are looking out for number one!' groaned the Living Nightmare, almost pleading for a response. 'Friday Night Fusion will either gel the Asylum or cripple us as a unit....' continued the behemoth. 'Don't you think your being a little harsh?' retorted the former Cruiserweight Champion. 'Oh welcome back to reality, and how is the Neo Void this time of year?' joked the Angel Of Fear in a sarcastic manner, Anarchy's mouth dropping ajar at the poor attempts of comedy by one of PCW's more serious combatants. '...................................................' 'Nerezza lighten up a little, give her a chance!' proclaimed the Lord Of Laughter in defence of Anarchy. 'Ok - Where was you when Akiyama kidnapped me, huh?!? Thought you may have overlooked a few minor details, no wonder you were crushed by two skinny Negro’s when Akiyama himself dismantled both you as you fought with weapons!' 'Daddy has his coming!' said the Spawn Of Evil, grinding his teeth, almost enraged by the comments made by Anarchy. 'Before we take down daddy, it's usually accustomed to ask a girls father for his permission to take out his daughter so what do you think of my chances sweet cheeks?' questioned the inquisitive Jester with a wink and smile combination to Anarchy. The trio gazes up at the PCW Jester with star struck expressions, taken back by his timing and choice of questions. Anarchy can only blush, but Nerezza uses her reaction against her as his sinister side continues to seep through, an evil glint in his piercing eyes. 'I know exactly what you’re thinking.... It'll be like a lamb to the slaughter! She'll entice you in Gaz and get her b*stard father to re-enact her own ordeal' 'I WOULDN'T!!!' screamed Anarchy, her and Nerezza generating a tense rivalry, still yet to manifest into a physical contest they create a verbal slugfest. 'I think you guys should wait and see what happens at Fusion, don't you?' asked the Asylum leader Alexander Miyagawa. 'He's right squire - And try to be nice to the little missy - Anyways I think we got what we needed to say off our chests, so any more questions?' Anarchy and Miyagawa look at one another and raise their arms aloft. Gaz's eyes continue to roam, peering further back to the empty seats, simply avoiding the obvious, his mouth parts again in preparation for his next vocal outing. 'QUESTIONS! Anybody....' Same thing happens again, a moment of de ja vu. 'Guess not, so that concludes the meeting! All donations for the church room-... Err-... I mean any donations for Juniors Of Komedy Era to be paid into my account pronto!' gloated the former NTICW comedian. 'And what exactly is J.O.K.E?' said his dumbfounded co-worker as the Trickster Team upped and left via the double door entrance of the conference room. The two other Asylum members looked on at the P.W.A shenanigans, taking their idol threats loosely and soon follow suit. In unison Anarchy and Miyagawa stood erect ushering towards the doors, unbeknownst to them that the chairs had be laden in paint that coincidentally been smeared over their clothing. As Miyagawa steps through the congregation of chairs, his colleague gazes at the handy work of Gaz Green. 'Hmm, Alex, you've been pranked!' The PCW star turning his top around to see the thick smearing of forest green. 'GGGGGGGGGAAAAAAAAAAZZZZZZZZZZZZ!!!!!!!!!!!!' cried the upset Asylum founder, making haste from the room. Anarchy shadows behind as Miyagawa vanished through the door, disappearing from sight, the camera captures the exact same prank had been pulled twice with the former Cruiserweight Champions ridden in green paint. - Scene eventually closing -
