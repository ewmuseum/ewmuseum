A History Lesson - Saving an Innocent

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-22-02 Introduction: The past can explain a lot about a man, it can show strengths and weaknesses, it can show desires, and planes which quickly died. The past can be like a safe in ways, storing the memories of hurt and pain, which can either be unlocked by you or cracked by another. Memories are as precious as gold, thought not valuable to anyone else they can mean the world to you. Jay Sylo, a man which is very complicated in more ways than one. His past remains like a book you can't keep your hands off of, filled with it's ups and downs but mostly it's downs. Still, Sylo has managed to keep his mind clear and his life straight; somehow. Last time we left Sylo he had just escaped from someone that had kidnapped him. Who did it? No one knows yet but a strange feeling fills the air. One that is sure to be revealed in the days to come. During his escape he ran into a warrior which tried to attack him due to the fact the man figured Sylo was a threat. Sylo quickly disposed of him but helped him in the end. Once the battle was done the man took Sylo back to his village to heal his wounds. Once there he met a woman. She healed his wounds and also offered Jay some information which would help him with his up-coming battle with Afwa Kilmanjaro. Sylo took this information as valuable as he left the village for the city. He had quit a journey ahead. History Lesson – Saving an innocent No one quit knew what lead Jay Sylo to becoming a warrior for good. We all know that he came from along line of knights which soul purpose was to protect the innocent, but in this day and age who is really innocent? --Flash Back-- (Thunder popped through the night sky as lighting followed close behind like an electric whip which cracked open the sky allowing rain to fall down to the earth. It was spring time, a time when storms usually occur the most but this storm seemed more violent than the rest. Most people were safe inside their houses with loved ones so most of the streets were like a ghost town. It was about ten thirty that night, nothing special really but something did stand out like a soar thumb. A young man in his early twenties could be seen walking down the street decked out in normal street clothes (blue jeans, t-shirt, and a hat), but each article of clothing was soaked like a wet rag. Why in gods name would someone be walking in weather like this? This late at night? Why? The shadows of buildings loomed down on the young man almost like they were reaching down to grasp him and hold him close. The young man was very tall, handsome, and muscular but still he held no look on his face. He had no expressions, no signs of feelings, nothing. He passed by buildings, but one in particular stood out. An old warehouse which had the windows boarded up, the glass you could see was broken, the pain was chipping, and the door appeared to be locked, most people would have walked past just thinking it was an old run down building but this young man, also known as Jay Sylo, noticed the lock wasn't locked. As he approached the old rusted lock he could hear sounds coming from inside the building. The scream of what sounded to be a young girl and the voice of men to follow. Sylo stood with his ear near the old, rusted door for a few moments listening.) 'Yeah this is a good en where did you find er?' One voice rang out. 'She was on the playground near the school. All alone, silly girl should have known not to take a ride from strangers!' Another voice said laughing afterwards. (His face began to scrunch up a small bit and a look of concern moved over his face. In this building were two pedophiles who had apparently kidnapped a child. Sylo knew he had to do something. Maybe get the police? He thought until he started to hear the girl sob.) 'Quit yer crying! No one can help you...the three of us are gonna have some fun!' The first voice boomed out in a sick tone. 'What are ya waitin fer? Take her clothes off!' The other voice boomed out. (Just then Sylo kicked the door in and stared at the two men who quickly turned their attention to Sylo. He held his fist together as he stared at the two. Both men with-drew weapons quickly as the little girl stared at Sylo pleading for help.) 'Who are you!' One man yelled. (Sylo said nothing.) 'Boy I asked you a question!' The man yelled again. (Still nothing from Sylo.) 'Didn't you hear him boy? Are you one of those retarded kids?' The other man asked. (Sylo lifted his head a small bit clinching his fist tight. His eyes seemed to change to a solid ice blue as he stared at the two men.) 'I am an justice, I am what is right, I am the law, and I am the executioner! You have violated what is right and now it is my job to destroy you both!' Sylo yelled. (Both men laughed as one swung a chain while the other approached with a knife. Sylo stood still, waiting for both men to reach him. In a flash the man with the chain threw it out trying to whip Sylo, quickly Jay threw his arm out letting it wrap around his huge forearm. Once fully wrapped Sylo pulled the man forward and closed lined him down onto the old cement floor of the warehouse. The next man approached with the knife. Sylo popped his neck and motioned for him to come on. He charged at Sylo only to be tripped. The man then landed on his own knife but it only stuck into his shoulder, not a fatal blow.) 'You dare question my authority and now you both will pay!' (Something inside clicked...a beast began to grow inside of Sylo. His eyes became a solid blue and his pupils were gone completely. He picked up the man who at one time had the chain and threw him into a pile of old wooden chairs. He walked toward the man, a look of anger on his face as he picked up the man once more. Sylo raised him above his head in a display of almost super-natural strength and threw him through a near by wall. Sylo snorted, almost like a bull as he walked over to the man on the floor with the knife in his shoulder. Sylo lifted him by the throat and held him in the air with one hand. The man couldn't breath because Sylo was slowly squeezing at his throat. His feet dangled, he didn't have the strength to move them.) 'Do you think it is ok to take an innocent child and turn her into your play thing?' Sylo asked staring at the man. (The man couldn't answer due to Sylo squeezing his throat. With one mighty thrust Sylo sent the man sprawling into a post near the wall. He then hovered over him, ready to kill it seemed.) 'Now answer me!' Sylo yelled. 'N....No. P....Please I'm a very wealthy business man and I can get you money!' The man pleaded bleeding from the nose and shoulder. 'I don't want your dirty money! I want you dead you pile of scum!' Sylo yelled into the man's face. (He coward in fear as Sylo drew back to deliver the final blow but just then the same switch that had turned him into a wrecking machine switched back off. Sylo lowered his fist but still picked the man up and placed him by his shirt on an old hook. He shook his head as his eyes turned back to normal. Walking toward the girl Sylo could see how scared she really was. He untied her arms and legs and helped her out of the warehouse. The two walked over to the pay-phone where Sylo dialed 911. It wasn't to long before the police arrived but once they were there all they found was the man still laying in the wall, the man on the hook dripping blood, and the small girl standing safe. She tried to explain to the police about the kind man who saved her but there was no reward for a shadow of the night.) --End Flash Back— (He stood over looking the city, his hands placed loosely by his side. Sylo stared down at people going to and from place to place. He let out a long sigh as he shook his head.) 'Two years ago the beast was revealed....I vowed to never show that side of me again but something's changed....almost like I can't control it now,' (A look of concern was on his face, one of the only emotions he knew. He closed his eyes and squeezed his fist one good time before letting go and looking back out over the city.) 'Tuesday night I fight Afwa Kilmanjaro, I do not want to hurt him but something inside is telling me that something is about to be unleashed. Something not even I can control, but who did this to me? Who is the one responsible for unleashing this beast?' Sylo looked to the sky. 'Please do not let me hurt Afwa....for he is innocent but may god have mercy on the souls of those who oppose me...' (He squeezed his fist once more as he let out a long sigh. Sylo then began to walk toward the city. His mind was full of thoughts, He thought about how to win against Afwa with-out hurting him, the thought of his ordeal when he was kidnapped, but most of all he thought of the unleashing of this 'beast'. Things would be revealed but time was needed...and for now we are out of time.)
