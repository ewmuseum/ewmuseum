The Road to Kentucky

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/06/02 “ARGH DUZZA” That is a phrase that often comes out of peoples mouths when they are around Duzza. Whether it’s his homies on the stoop back in Harlem or in the middle of a wrestling ring, people seem to often have a reason to holler “ARGH DUZZA.” The reason for it being shouted out now is unknown at the moment, but what is known is that it came from the passenger in a car with Duzza. The two African American men are in a big black low rider, one of them is Duzza and the other is a long time friend of Duzza’s called Jay. Duzza is dressed in a pair of baggy white pants, a black wife beater and has a black bandanna covering his cornrows, he also has a big platinum chain around his neck, Jay is wearing more or less the same, just minus the platinum. Duzza is set to participate in a Kentucky brawl at the next PCW, Gold and Glory brawl, and since he failed to receive his plane ticket from PCW, Duzza decided to pack up some clothes, half of his CD collection and grab a homie and head out on the road and drive from New York to Kentucky. Now a clever man, hell a man with half a brain cell, wouldn’t even think about doing this unless he knew where he was going. But Duzz, well Duzz isn’t exactly the brightest fish in the sea, and thoughts of getting lost in the middle of white trash county didn’t even cross his mind. Well not until he did get lost… They are driving down a long, deserted country road, the sun is shining, the sky is blue and it is very hot. Duzza and Jay have been driving all night and it is about 10 am in the morning now. Jay: “ ARGH DUZZA” Duzza: “ Bro!? What’s wrong man?” Jay: “ We have been driving for ages man, I done sat in this chair for so long I can’t even feel my ass anymore” Duzza looks at Jay with a look of disgust Duzza: “ Why the hell, do you want to feel yo ass anyway man? What’s wrong with you?” Jay: “ Duzz, man I was just, err It’s just a figure of speech? Anyway forget about my ass, what I wanna know is where the hell are we going bro?” Duzza: “ Man, you’re a fool, I already done told you we was going to Kentucky, man I told you that when we past Chicago, gees” Jay: “ Well, I’m sorry for not having the memory of a genius. But why the hell we going to Kentucky? And ain’t you ever heard of flying?” Duzza: “ ‘Course I done heard of flying, just thought a road trip with my homie Jay would be cooler than sitting next to some cracker on a place for 7 hours” Jay: “Uh huh….so what’s the truth?” Duzza: “The office seemed to have forgotten to send my plane ticket to me. You belive that! Me! Duzz! Having to drive” Jay: “ Hmph… man, where the hell are we? I ain’t seen a signpost since we got past Phoenix, hell I ain’t seen nothing buy damn fields for as long as I can remember. And what ever happened to the radio?” Duzza turns on the radio, and after having to tune the radio a bit, a station finally comes on. A loud man, with a strong Southern accent comes on Radio presenter: “ Hey y’all, thanks for joining me on GMKD, “Good Morning Kentucky Daily,” were glad to announce that the weather is hot and the sun is shining, just like always” Duzza and Jay, their mouths wide open stare at each other in shock Jay: “ KENTUCKY?! Kentucky?! Man, why the hell are we in Kentucky? I thought you was joking!” Duzza: “ Fool! I wasn’t joking! Heh, I wonder why it was so easy to persuade you to come” Jay: “ Oh really? ARGH DUZZA, I have a business meeting for 4 o’clock east cost time! You son of a…” Duzza: “ Man, I’m sorry a'ight? But player, this is the year 2002, we ain’t gonna run into no trouble, believe that” Jay: “ Man, how am I gonna believe that? Last time you said we weren’t going to run into no trouble I ended up doing a night in jail, that cost me my job fool” Duzza: “ Dude, listen I done told you a million times, I’m sorry about that man, but trust me, it’s a Sunday and it’s 10am, we’ll be fine” Jay: “ ARGH, it’s a tuesday damnit” Duzza looks shocked Duzza: “ Tuesday? What? Aww man, I’m either gonna be days early for the ppv or I’m a couple of days late” They continue to continue driving for about another 30 minutes without any trouble and on the horizon they can see what appears to be a small town. They can see about 10 or 15 houses, none of them in very good condition, but there not falling down either Jay: “ Wow! Houses! Man, starring at a blue sky and never ending rows or crops get’s old after the first 10 minutes” Duzza: “ Same here man, but bro, I don’t think we should stop here, I mean it’s all good knowing were near civilization but man, I don’t wanna stop in no town in Kentucky man.” Jay: “ Yeah you right there boi, ain’t no way I’m stopping in this town” Suddenly with out warning a small cat runs across the path of Duzza’s low rider, Duzza notices it and swerves to miss it, he manages to miss it, but has to pull of the road, now only a few yards from the small Kentucky town, while of the road he runs over a big rock, all of a sudden the car just stops dead in it’s tracks Duzza: “ What the hell?! Aww man” Jay: “ Duzza...what did you do?” Duzza: “ What? Hey it ain’t my fault, I just didn’t want to hit that cat” Jay: “ Duzz, you must be the weakest thug since Puff Daddy” Duzza: “ Yeah, your so funny, now stop joking and let’s go see what’s wrong with my car a’ight?” Duzza and Jay both jump out of the car and take a look at the low rider. There is a big slit down one of the front tires and hitting the big rock knocked the tire off a bit. There is no real damage and just replacing the tire will be all that needs doing to get it back on the road Jay: “ Aww, it ain’t that bad man, fix it no problem” Duzza: “ Yeah, man I fix it in no time” Duzza walks around to the trunk of the low rider, he grabs a tire iron and then goes to pick up his spare wheel, Duzza struggles to life the wheel as Jay looks at him with a mixture of laughter and shock across his face Jay: “ You need some help there Duzz?” Duzza, now a bead of sweat running down his forehead, takes a quick break from struggling with the wheel to holler back at Jay Duzza: “ Nah dawg, I got it all under wraps man, just a lil’ bit heavier than I expected, that’s all bro” After about 5 minutes of struggling Duzza finally manages to get the wheel from the trunk to the front of the car, then after a quick rest he attacks the other wheel with the tire iron, he has no luck with it Duzza: “ Hey, you wanna help a brother out?” Jay: “ Well, to be honest, it’s more fun watching you struggle, a’ight let me help” Both Jay and Duzza try together to get the tire of but it won’t budge, they need some extra help Duzza: “ Argh, we almost had it that time, listen man, I think we should just go knock on someone’s door and ask for help, what’s the worse that could happen?” Jay: “ What’s the worse that could happen? How about us hanging from some big ass tree? How about that? Damn” Duzza: “ Listen bro, ain’t no one gonna mess with us man, c’mon” Duzza picks up the tire iron and followed by Jay, slowly walk up to the first house in the town. The house is pretty well kept for a house in the middle of nowhere, with two small steps, before the front door. Duzza knocks on the front door, wiping the sweat of his forehead with one hand and the tire iron in his other hand. Jay is standing nervously behind the 6’0, 252lb frame of Duzza. A small, bald white man appears the door, when he opened the door, he was smiling probably waiting to see who was visiting little ol’ him, but as he saw Duzza and the tire iron, or in his own eyes, two big black men with weapons his look changes from a grin to one of fear White man: “ Oh my it’s one of them, please don’t hurt me, I have nothing of value” Duzza: “ What the f*ck? Man, listen my car broke down back there” At this point, Duzza swung the tire iron to point in the direction of the car, this though almost gave the old guy a heart attack and without warning he starts hollering White man: “ POLICE POLICE! HELP! Someone!” Duzza: “ Dude, we ain’t gonna hurt you, we just need help” White man: “ HELP SOMEONE” Jay: “ Duzz, let’s run” Quickly before anyone came looking Duzza and Jay take of down the steps and run across the only street, which runs through the entire town, they run behind a small house Jay: “ ARGH DUZZ! I should of done the talking man, that fool thought you was gonna kill him” Duzza: “ Hey is it my fault he thought that I was carrying a tire iron to beat him with?” Jay: “ Of course it’s your fault, who else’s fault could it possible be? Geez, man f*ck this, let’s just go back to the car, we’ll try harder and we’ll just get the damn tire on ok?” Duzza: “ Yeah man, let’s do that, ‘cos we can’t stay here man” Duzza and Jay come out from behind the house, they give a quick look to see if it’s safe and then start walking up to where their car is, they walk about six feet before someone behind them calls them. Behind them is a small, pudgy white guy in a police officer. Jay: “ FOOL, you said it was clear?” Duzza: “ Well sorry for not noticing a white guy in a police uniform” Police officer: “ Now you too, I am going to need a favor from you to, is that ok?” Duzza: “ Err…ok” Officer: “ Now, I’m afraid the years have not been good to me, and well, my eyesight isn’t quite as good as it used to be, hell I’m not going to lie to you, I’m as blind as a bat, but I am the only thing that stops this town from being over run by criminals, now I have had a report saying that there are two suspicious looking men around these parts, have you seen them?” Duzza: “ Err, what they be lookin’ like dawg?” Officer: “ Well, there is two of them, they’re apparently black or Negro, carrying baseball bats and they are said to talk ‘funny” Duzza and Jay both have a slightly worried expression on their face, knowing that the officer is probably talking about them Duzza: “ Err afraid not mister police officer, we haven’t seen anyone like that…” Officer: “ Hmmm…ok then, carry on” Duzza and Jay slowly walk up to where their car is parker, when Duzza makes a shocking observation Duzza: “ Hey Jay, I just noticed, this town ain’t even got like a cement road or anything. This entire street is just dirt…nothing but dirt. Man, glad were not wearing white” Jay: “ Nothing get’s past you does it Duzz?” Duzza: “ Nope, nothing at all” Jay: “ Err Duzz, obviously you haven’t noticed but we haven’t actually asphalt on a road since like 11pm last night. Hell, we’ve been driving on dirt longer than we’ve passed crop fields” Duzza and Jay turn the last corner before the straight road back to the car, and Duzza bumps into a tall guy. Dressed from head to toe in white and with piercing blue eyes Man: “ You had best watch yourself boy?” Duzza: “ Hey, sorry bro, didn’t mean to bump in to ya then, my bad” Man: “ Your bad indeed, you know we don’t want your type round these parts” Duzza: “ What you mean by that man?” Jay: “ Err Duzz, perhaps we should just get back to the car” Duzza: “ Nah man, I want to know what this fool meant” Man: “ I mean, you don’t want to mess with me boy, your not welcome here, so leave, or else” Duzza: “ Or else what fool? I’m the one with the crowbar?” Man: “ Or else, you’ll find yourself hanging from that tree yonder” Duzza and Jay both look around and see a very large tree, with branches coming of it at all directions Jay: “ Now I did not notice that tree when we walked through here earlier” Duzza: “ Nah, me neither man, well, it looks like we done taken up enough of yo time” Duzza and Jay walk away, but the man continues to watch them before he walks into the nearest house, with a sick grin on his face Duzza and Jay make it back to the low rider, the car intact as they again try to get the wheel of the car, but with little success. As they stop for a rest, Duzza once again makes a shocking observation Duzza: “ Yo Jay, you notice how that dude was dressed all in white? I mean, that dude must get dirt all over his clothes, so why he be wearing white? Damn cracker fool” Jay: “ Man, let’s pray he just likes to wear white. And man what is that sound? Sounds like a army of freakin’ horses or something?!” Duzza and Jay both look for the source of the noise, and then they spot it. On the horizon, heading from where they have just be, a small heard of horses can be seen galloping towards the car. But that isn’t the problem; on top of these horses are men. Every single one of them dressed from head to toe in white, each with a white hood Duzza and Jay stare open mouthed and what seems to be a gang of kkk members heading in their direction Duzza: “ Oh Sh*t” Using strength they never knew they had and speed faster than any they have had before, Duzza and Jay quickly replaced the broken wheel of the low rider for a good wheel and quickly jump into the car, once in Duzza says a quick prayer Duzza: “ Lord, I promise I will do good for all my time left on this earth, just please let us get out of here alive” Duzza then starts the ignition, which fires up, he quickly thanks God before speeding away, past the kkk members and hopefully onto a city. Once out of range of the crazy white folk, Jay asks Duzza a important question Jay: “Err Duzz. You know, you got the ppv match on Sunday yeah?” Duzza: “Yeah dawg” Jay: “Well, don’t you have a match on like Turmoil wit’ the Egyptian brother?” Duzza: “Nah you must be…what..wait…umm…awww shiznit…” Jay: “You did didn’t you! You’re a fool” Duzza: “Shit, and I think Turmoil was in Mississippi… how the fuck are we gonna get to Mississippi dawg!” Jay: “This mess, your gonna get out of on your own, I’m getting in the back seat, and going to sleep and when I wake up we had best not be getting beaten up and or arrested… aight?” Duzza: “Aight man…aight” The camera starts to fade as the black low rider speeds a long the country road, with Duzza, no doubt trying to work out how to get to Mississippi for his match against The Pharaoh…
