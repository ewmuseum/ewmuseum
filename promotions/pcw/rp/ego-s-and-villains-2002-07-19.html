Ego's and Villains

19 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th July El Diablo is more often than not a complex a man, not because he is genius enough to make himself complex, but because he is so unstable that he comes across as complex to anyone that encounters him. So would this be any different today? Or would El Diablo manage to be stable for one day in his ever so miserable life. The scene opens up in a parking lot of some sort where near enough all parking spaces are taken up. The parking lot is just a multicolored playground of cars, ranging from blue to red and white to black. The only problem in this perfect scene is a red car and a black car head to head in the middle of parked cars, one car trying to exit the parking lot, while the other tries to get in to gain a parking spot for himself. The sound of car horns beeping and blasting loudly is largely audible, and what would you know, none other than El Diablo himself is behind the steering wheel in the red car trying to get in. A few moments pass, and slowly but surely El Diablo switches off his car engine, removes his seat belt, opens his door and steps out of his vehicle. As usual El Diablo has his Mexican flag face paint on, and his long black hair loose, as well as wearing a black jacket and black denim jeans as well as his black sneakers. Diablo slams his car door shut and waits outside of his car, as the opposite car continues to beep at him for a few minutes, before finally realising that El Diablo won't move and turns his engine off. The man then steps out of his car and looks very angry at what has transpired, but for only a few seconds, then the angered look on his face turns into a look of awe. The man is at least six feet tall, and has shaved blonde hair. He is wearing some dark sunglasses, a white vest and blue khaki shorts with white socks and white sneakers. El Diablo stares at the man, until the taller man approaches El Diablo, removes his sunglasses, revealing his light blue eyes and looks ready to talk. 'You're that wrestler right?' asks the man. Diablo looks to his sides and then behind him, points at himself, and then slowly nods his head. 'Haha, I knew it!' exclaims the man. Diablo simply nods. 'What gave it away? The face paint by any chance?' responds Diablo in a very sarcastic manner. The man however, doesn't pick up on his tone. 'Yeah, man. But you know what?' he asks. Diablo feigns interest in what the man has to say, 'No, what?' 'I'm a huuuuuuuuuuge Snow and Rent A Villain!' he says with happiness clearly in his tone. Diablo sighs. 'And why should I care?' asks Diablo obviously quite annoyed in talking to this man. The man scratches his head and looks at El Diablo in a certain degree of shock. 'Well, in case you weren't aware dude, you wrestle those two guys very soon,' he exclaims in his Californian accent. Diablo leaps back, almost as if he is in shock. However El Diablo made it quite apparent that he was feigning his shock. 'I know who I am wrestling, and just why am I talking to you anyway? MOVE YOUR DAMN CAR!!!!' yells El Diablo at the man. The once angry man though, remains as cool as a cat and stands directly in front of El Diablo still. 'Well dude, you're going to lose those matches and you're talking to me because I like totally rule, and besides it's your car that is soooooo going in the wrong direction,' responds the man calmly. El Diablo takes a moment to look behind him, and sure enough there is a 'One Way' sign staring back at him, which only annoys our temperamental Mexican Devil some more. 'You know if I had my video camera I could turn this situation into a film,' states Diablo with a little smirk on his face. The man shakes his head while standing his ground against El Diablo, 'If I had your video camera I'd send it to the cops and get you arrested for going the wrong way in a one way zone,' El Diablo seethes and literally goes red in the face. 'Whoa dude mellow out before you blow,' states the man to him, 'But wait, you already do blow....in the ring,' laughs the man. The Mexican Devil regains his composure and shakes off the comments of this rowdy PCW fan. 'Well you have opinions, fair enough. You like the bad guys and that's fair enough too, but there is no need to insult,' comments El Diablo calmly, and so unlike his usual self. Diablo then walks closer to his car and sits on the front of it as the man fakes a pout and nods his head. 'What's the matter dude, I thought part of being a wrestler was being able to take the ribs,' he comments without a clue. El Diablo though, soon snaps back, 'I thought part of being a fan was being able to take a wrestlers heat,' For once this cool and calm Californian man doesn't have a response, which pleases El Diablo slightly. Are these signs of a new El Diablo? Or is this just another characteristic of himself? Or perhaps El Diablo is just high on mescaline again, and isn't talking to anyone but himself in his hallucinatory state. What? It could happen. Still both men remain with their cars facing one another and stand talking after what seems like an eternity of arguments and general talking. 'Why do I even need to wrestle that self oriented, egotistical bastard, Snow anyway?' asks El Diablo rhetorically to the man. The man however doesn't answer as Diablo gets interrupted by a tap on the shoulder from behind, which causes him to leap up off of the front of his car and turn around in the defensive. 'Excuse me can I have an autograph?' asks another man. Diablo looks at him and then back at the other man he was talking to. 'Do you mind? 'I' was talking about 'MY' matches, and 'I' was talking about 'ME' Do you have no manners you selfish man?' responds El Diablo in a self-oriented and egotistical kind of way. The second man blushes and walks away slowly. Suddenly El Diablo hears laughing and turns around to see the first man laughing at what happened. 'That....was....brilliant....the...way....you......shunned...him,' he comments while laughing and taken breaths in between. El Diablo stands tall and stares at the man. 'What are you on about? Move your damn car, fool!!!!' shouts Diablo to the man in the same disrespectful way that the man once spoke to him. So what now is there to expect from this Mexican Devil? And could he win another two in a row? Well, El Diablo certainly hopes so.
