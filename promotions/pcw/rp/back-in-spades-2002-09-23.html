Back In Spades

23 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd September 2002 Heh, I guess I survive yet again…I come out with hardly a scratch but still no one recognize what I did. It like I nothing….. Oh well people soon going to find out what I like, people soon going to find out that you not get rid of Jung by cutting off a limb, it going to take way more than that. Only way you going to get rid of me is if you kill me…but I not think that going to happen anytime soon, nothing can kill me….it only make me stronger! Nothing can stop me, because I…am…Jung -=***=- I felt a soft rumbling in the leather chair I was sitting in which snap me back into reality. I really not in any mood to talk business, all I in the mood for is sleep and relaxation. But they say business need to be dealt with…I getting paid for this so I not complaining! “Jung it’s a pleasure to have you back with us in Tokyo, but we do not have time for greetings, since you have been away urgent business needs to be addressed!” I not like that guy… I looked up and across to see a familiar plump face of a happy Japanese businessman. Maybe it his annoying voice, or maybe it just the fact he never stop smiling or talking about “the company” what ever it is, he shit me off…or maybe it just the fact he Japanese. “Jung could you please pay attention! This meeting is costing the Company valuable money!” I shook my head and glanced to my left, yet another “happy” Japanese Business Executive sat smiling and nodding his head at me, they all shit me off in some way. “Excuse Tomoki please Jung, but since you have been gone, the Company has been in Turmoil. That Black man Ali Khadafi has caused our stock to plunge…we need….” HehI silently smirked as his voice trailed off…I knew that they needed me, I knew that TokyoPop couldn’t stand for five minute alone without me, knowing this made me feel that little better. I nodded my head so that they Businessmen would get the impression I was paying attention, little did they know that I don’t care what happen to TokyoPop, I only in this for one person….me!! “…Of course the price would be high, as you are one of us we are happy to pay for all expenses!” the plump man’s words chimed out loud. I lifted myself up a little in my seat as the mention of “price” and “high” were mentioned, “Price” meant that I was going to get paid and “High” meant that I was going to get paid well, suddenly it no longer bother me that I was talking to two stupid Japanese men. Out of the corner of my eye I caught the second businessman nodding his head extending out a piece of gold paper, for a minute I stopped to think…. “Well what are you waiting for Jung?” I shot one of my ice-cold glares at him as he seemed un-phased. “If you need a pen just remember…” I glanced down at the document that was being waved in my face before I looked towards the first fat businessman and tilted my head…Japanese are like the Asian version of White Boy’s!! “What you think this is?” The question came out of my mouth like a bullet coming out a gun, if it had been to anyone else they would’ve had to stop and think…but not these men. I could’ve stabbed him right then in his chair…”It’s a contract.” “I not fucking stupid, look at it!” I not know what came over me but I lurched up and grabbed the plump Japanese man around the throat and dragged his red face towards the paper, once again the smart ass had something “stupid” to reply with. “There’s nothing wrong with it!!” He began to gasp for air and clutch at my hands as my knuckles turned white, surprisingly the other businessmen didn’t seem to care. I dragged the man close to my face and whispered, “What I look like to you?! HUH?!” Stupid White Boy… I never met anyone so dumb in all my life. Although the thought of killing him was flowing through my head I never once smiled to let him know I was playing….I like playing with prey, it make everything more interesting or fun…either one. The man began to gurgle as the rage began to flow through my veins. I not know what was coming over me, I even forgot why I was torturing the poor fat man. “Ohh, it’s in Japanese!! So Sorry Jung ! Allow me to read it for you!” the other businessman chimed in from across the seat. It was like all the tension was suddenly released with that one comment. All the rage drifted out of me like steam coming from my ears as I released the plump man and slumped back down in my leather chair, Poor guy probably wishing he not dumb now! The Businessman cleared his throat as he prepared to read “Ahem” He began to talk in his annoying Japanese voice as I blocked everything out yet again. I glanced over at the now breathing Japanese Businessman gasping for dear life and adjusting his suit as I managed yet another chuckle and smirk before I took a look around at the glass window behind me. It was night fall and the pretty lights of the Japanese city of Tokyo lit up the darkness like a Christmas Tree…I let out a sigh as I tried to think of the last time I enjoyed Christmas. …. I never think I actually experienced Christmas, as that thought hit me the rage began to flow back into my veins as I turned back to face the talking businessman, my eyes locking onto his. “You better not be trying to fuck with me….” “I assure you I…we’re not trying “fuck” with you Jung,” “I warning you….” I threatened the businessman with the evilest look I could conjure, it seemed to work as he pulled out his collar and adjusted his tie, clearly folding under the pressure. I find that I have that effect on quiet a few people so I guess it not really any accomplishment making some poor Japanese man heat up under pressure. Either way I not like being fucked with….I think the look I giving him was making sure that he got that impression!! Suddenly out of nowhere footsteps could be heard slapping against the tiled floor of the lounge. “What he’s trying to say is that if you do as we ask, you will be rewarded!” The voice, which belonged to the feet, boomed. How many people want to fucking talk to me?! it seemed that the whole company wanted to have a word with me, it not easy being Vietnamese…But I guess someone have to work for them. “Jung all we’re asking is that you help TokyoPop out and we’ll help you! No questions asked…..” I sat up a little in my chair as the man continued to speak. “All we need is for you to merely keep PCW occupied while we tend to the Blitz problem, that man has cost the PURE company it’s weight in gold.” The man became irritated. As he became irritated so did I, at the mere mention of Inphino Blitz it was like I was fighting in the ring all over again. Because of that idiot taxes on PURE wrestlers were raised in PCW, because of that man PCW was turned against me…a return was out of the question.Fucker!! “I listening…..” Something needed to be said, although I not looking to forward to tackling Blitz I was looking forward to receiving the pay check TokyoPop was willing to give me, I shook my head as I tried to shake the thought of actually working on my own free will. “We have the Inphino Blitz problem being dealt with, The Pharaoh, The End and Alexander Miyagawa all seem to be holding their own ground, all we need is you to divert attention.” The man confuse me, this had turned from boredom into James Bond. “And how you plan on me doing that?” I knew something was I not like was going to be said, “You’re going back to PCW” “What….” I bolted up-right in my chair thinking that I had mis-heard what the man was saying, it didn’t bother me too much but dramatizing was a specialty of mine. “You back to PCW, you cause some commotion, we handle Blitz and you receive a nice…large….pay check!” The words rolled off the mans tongue. I Only doing this for me!! I thought to myself as I glared at the huge man standing across the room, I only in this for me…and the money of course. “How much we talking?” I snatched the contract out of the businessman’s hands and the pen along with it. “We’re talking a 7 figure sum here, if the job is done correctly and you do as we say” I began to count in my head $1, 000, 000 that was 7 figures alright, I guess I could use the extra money with the tax and all, so I shrugged my shoulders and put pen to paper. “You’ve made a wise choice Jung, you’re plane leave for New York in 10 minutes. Your luggage is already on board as we anticipated your signing…” WHAT?! the mans voice trailed off as I realized I had been set up by my own company. I looked around as the two Japanese businessman began clasp their hands together and bow towards me….i’d wipe the smiles off their faces as soon as I was paid. “Thank you Jung…We’ll catch up in New York” I began to shake my head as the man strolled out of the lounge and the businessmen got up out of their seat and pointed behind me towards a rather large window. I slipped out of my seat and glanced at a rather large plane sitting in the distance with flashing lights illuminating the night sky, I had a feeling this was going to be a long night. Yeah…wave, we see who going to be smiling in the future, we going to see! The businessmen began to wave to me as I made my way to a door off to the edge of the lounge room. -=***=- I glanced out a window to my left as the rumbling began to vibrate through my body once more. I couldn’t believe what was going on… Somehow I going back to America, somehow I going back to PCW, somehow I going back Home and for once it not what I want…. “Folks, we should be arriving in New York City in little less than 9 hours, please sit back and enjoy the flight…for some of you this could be the only chance you get to relax for a looooong time!!”
