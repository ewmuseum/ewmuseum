Open, Closed, Open, Closed part II

3 March 2002

Date: 03/03/02 The room was filled with the ghastly scent of expensive musk and death. Not a good mixture by anyone’s standards. The lights were out; there was absolutely nothing Jade could do about that. However, there were torches tracing the room in hope of finding Jade herself. One thing Jade knew was that she was not ready to play the martyr after being betrayed by her supposed ‘friend.’ She was going to make every single person in here rot in a pit of flames. At least that was the plan. She knew almost picture perfectly where everybody was in the room and that gave her a good advantage. Well at least she knew where these ‘innocent’ people were to say the least. Blood was strewn all over the walls and floors, what little light in the room which did not come from the torches, these bastards possessed, came from the lights beneath the spirits counter. Screams filled the air like a cat being strangled, a difficult sound to be dealing with rest assured. Jade was ready to gag and vomit in her place if she did not know that her life and probably the club too depended on her cousin not finding out exactly where she was sat. The taste from the bodies which was left on her tongue made her only want to throw up even more and she lay against the bar in a foetal position trying to deal with these strong urges to hurl. The bare wood against her respectable suit made splinters slowly crawl into her skin. Her back ached and arched as she tried to stretch the pain right out of her body. She struggled to come up with a plan which would be any kind of help to this situation. She softly and slowly let her breath linger out of her lungs, throat and mouth so not to be detected by the people which surrounded her. All this time she could only think about making it out alive for her family and friends. To whom she had become an almost vital pillar for them to lean against. This is just plain typical. As soon as things begin to go right for a hard working girl like me, it is almost guaranteed for them to disintegrate. At all costs that perverted egotistical bastard will not get this club back. If only I could make a call to someone to help me deal with this. I finally got to get a long with Lacuna again after a rough couple of months and knowing my luck she will soon be getting the news of me lying in a hospital bed with those horrible tubes up her nose. Carys still needs help to become a more accomplished fighter and it will another few months trying to keep a float after this. Life is such a pain in the ass, you can -never- have things working for you. It is like the first and most basic rule in being Jade. “I think we found her boss!” The light shone brightly near Jade. Though the light was not directly at her. Jade stumbled to her feet ready to pounce and rip his throat out, the torch moved however and focused more on a black haired young stripper. There was a thud as what can be presumed was Mani sent the back of his arm flying against this dumbasses head. “What are you ingrates? Does this look like the photo fit I gave to you all? This is how mistakes like Friday’s are made you obtuse dismal excuses of second class ‘fixers.’ Jade is not dead! She is far too intelligent for that. Intelligence is exactly what you lot miss out on! Who knows? Maybe she had your share of that. Now get searching before I beat the hell out of the lot of you! Damien could you come this way please? I have a small accolade I wish to present to yourself before things are dealt with here once and for all. I am very pleased with your perfidy on this situation. It will get you far in my eyes.” Mani placed his arm around the smaller, yet not small shoulder’s of the filth who handed Jade on a plate. Jade wanted to beat the crap out of the pair of them and for some reason she was far to wary of them. Something about Mani had changed since last time. Something which she was not about to experience again in a hurry if she could at all avoid such an occurrence happening. The door creaked into the hall. Jade knew from the creak, of course Mani knew the codes into the house. She flinched in the dark hearing her expensive furniture being shattered as someone walked up the stairs. Jade knew the door was still open, her head flung up as she rolled through the darkness; stealthily she banged into the back door. If doing that is at all possible. There was no noise created and now she made her way up to the stairs. “Psst.” Jade made the noise and the man suddenly turned. He wasn’t able to mutter before Jade broke his nose. She was silent as ever. She crept up the stairs with the unconscious man over her shoulder. “Hold it right there miss!” Jade turned around and rolled her eyes. She was nearly back out of the building and then this happens. As she rolled her eyes she realised that it wasn’t her who was in trouble. Jade grinned and continued to walk to the top of the stairs. She dumped the man on the top of the stairs, about to make a progression into the bathroom when she heard a grunt and a crash. Jade stamped her feet quietly and whined. They were smashing up her property and only she was allowed to do that! Jade headed down the stairs and into the hall once again. She pressed her head against the now closed door. She heard a familiar voice. “My bloody arse! Hi Mani! Healing is a bloody bitch eh?” It was Lacuna. Jade screwed up her face. She mouthed a simple ‘what the?’ Now she thought she was going to have to save her again. She needed a plan, a great fast working plan which would do the trick. Jade sat on the bottom of her stairs and looked at the new crumbled antiques. Jade laughed as she thought why the hell not? “Jason, this is Mani! Is the whore upstairs?” He asked three times. Jade laughed again and grabbed the two way radio. Jade first grabbed a floor board from the stair and made sure to have a nail in there. She still ‘loved’ Lacuna. She was still as much of a friend as ever. However much she knew Lacuna would deny it she needed help from Jade. Jade knew what needed to be done. “Pay back is a bitch. Mani, remember me? The bitch, the whore, well I have a name cousin. Jade, I really can not believe that you’re already standing, how does THAT work? I mean you only got shot in the leg the other day? Don’t you have my jewels with you as well?” Mani was already on his way into the hall. Jade waited on the stairs and swung the plank at his head. The nail stuck ion and he barely flinched. “Jesus Mani what the hell kind of wiz are you on these days?” Jade could not pull out the nail from his head. Jade hit a standing kick into Mani’s head, which was quickly followed by a knee to the gut. Meanwhile… Lacuna smiles as she kicks a small guy hard under the chin, swinging her leg around and flooring a second in one swift move. This was why she liked Jade; the excitement, she thought to herself as the first man made it to his feet and received a crafty knee to the stomach and is flung head first into the groin of the second man. 'Oh come on guys, are you paid to fail this bad?' She searches the floor, settling her hands on the black bag that had previously adjourned her shoulders, removing none other than Steve, her trusty shotgun. The short guy backs off a little, but the taller man has no sight of the weapon and is knocked to the floor as the handle wraps itself around his head. Smiling still, she jumps on to a table and pounces on the small guy, sending him to the floor with a neat axe handle, the barrel of the gun cannoning off his forehead. He collapses to the floor unconscious. 'Bah, hired goons these days' “Hey Lacuna if you’re finished in there honey…” She is cut off when Mani drives his head into the face of Jade. Jade flies back against the stairs. “Some help in her would be appreciated.” Jade flexes her body upwards and dives at Mani’s head, her feet are pressed on his shoulders and she tries to flip him backwards into the stairs. Mani grips one of his tree trunk arms into her chest and slammed her into the stairs again. Jade went a little wheezy and she rolled onto her stomach. Mani drove another of his arms into her back. It was so hard that bile form the pit of her stomach flies onto the stairs. Oh this pain was real. More real than perhaps any other emotion in the whole of the world. The pain was exhilarating her to a limit like some S & M freak she lifted her head and tried to pull herself to a vertical stance once again. “Is that really all you have whore?” Jade giggled and turned on her feet driving the back of her fist into his eye. Lacuna crashes the door open and rams her foot into the back of his shin. Se grins and winks at Jade and Jade tries to smile, the blood pours through a crack in her lip. To be continued
