GOD and GLORY

Date: not entered The rain fell outside their window as if the world was dying; slowly coming to it send in a rage of madness. Lightning colored the small, dark room each time it struck, and the thunder rolled in the distance like the screams of the damned. The rain hit the window pain, each little hit like the hands of the clock, each driving closer towards insanity. Inside the room, there was a small round table and a chair set up in the middle of the floor. Scattered across it were various documents with information about PCW wrestlers, photos included. As the lightning struck, it gave momentary view of some of the faces photographed. The Chef… Alexis Parshin… Jason Snow… Rent A Hero… Among other tag team waste… The room faded back to darkness yet again as thunder rumbled in the horizon. The rain seemed like it would go on endlessly at its rapid tempo. Silence fell thick upon the room like the dead was sleeping in the next room. There would be a lot of silence left in the tag world once the nVg won those belts… one man winning the tag team titles alone?? Impossible…it cannot happen! And that statement is correct… One man most certainly cannot hold team gold alone successfully… That’s why the god is taking on the challenge. Man most certainly cannot handle the pressure nor possess the skills needed for such a test of fortitude. That’s why it’s only a task for the gods to take. The corner next to the window was especially dark; light had hard time reaching that crevice. Lightning comes snapping through again…and as the room lights momentarily, the corner becomes visible. … As does Akiyama… He sits in that dark corner, his silver and black hair undone and hanging messily in front of his face. Nothing but shadow can be seen from under his locks. All he does is staring…forward…it’s the only direction he can go now… …Forward… Another flash of lighting…another glimpse of the god… Those moments of light were like snapshots of a black and white camera, each one recording the image of the nVg in a gray photogenic reality. That’s basically how he exists now…in the gray of this reality. He is hated…and he is cheered…people despise him, but they still respect him and fear him. Neutrality is his only method to his madness…but those officials have dubbed him “heel”. “Heel”… The bottom of one’s foot… The Neo Void God will in no way be associated with being described, as something commonly called the bottom of some mortal’s foot. The masses cheer for the Banishment…yet hate his motives…there is no other alternative other than to show him as the god keeping the border between light and darkness solid. Yet he is the monster…now and forever more…the monster to be feared and hated… A light comes from across the room, at first thin and then widening as a door came fully open. The light shuts off, and standing there was the shadow of the future… the beautiful dreamer of tomorrow… Lightning flashes…and Cyclonica is there…standing in the doorway staring at the god. Her attire is the same, tight black vinyl clinging at her waist down her thighs and the rest of her legs, knee high leather silver buckle strapped high heel boots with the razor sharp stiletto, her ice blue PVC halter top tight against her skin underneath the equally tight black see through mesh long sleeved shirt. Her waist long ice blue and black hair hangs over her shoulders and her face as she bores a hole into the nVg. He makes no notice of her presence as he sat there in the corner, staring ahead at nothing. A deep sigh comes rolling up from within her. “He’s not the same as he used to be…mentally speaking. Physically he’s stronger than ever, but his mind’s not as it was before. Still…that wont stop him from ripping the opposition to shreds. His mind automatically goes into battle mode, because that’s how he’s lived his entire life to think. No…what’s wrong here is his psyche.” Her heels click over towards him, and she stands before him looking down at the top of his head. He did not move, his eyes still locked on staring, even if it was straight into Cyclonica’s pelvis. She reaches down and tilts his head up so that she can meet his eyes. Their pools of solid darkness met, and each of them stare into emptiness. “They say the eyes are the portals to the soul…we must be soulless then. With the acts that we are going to commit, there would be no mercy for our souls. Akiyama’s eyes…they stay fierce…but they’re still hollow…” Her ice blue nails trail up from his chin to his lips, which were pressed together tightly as he blankly glared up at her. “He has yet to speak since I’ve found him after Supremacy. There’s something that’s inside his head…something that he experienced when he disappeared in that huge flash of light right after he banished Jay Sylo. I have yet to find the answer in my dreams, but that is another matter to deal with. First we deal with Makonie, and then we deal with destroying the tag team world.” The Divine One takes the hand of the god, motioning for him to stand. He complies, and like a mother guiding a sleepy child, she guides him slowly over to the chair in the middle of the room. He sits as Cyclonica stands behind him. She runs her fingers through his hair, drawing it from his eyes like a wedding veil pulled back from the face of a bride. Then she tilts his head back, taking another glance at the nVg’s face. “You have a long battle ahead of you, Akiyama… you are marked for death in this world…this tag team world… almost twenty men looking to throw you to your doom. You’re a threat to their existence…they fear you…” She takes a closer look at the scratch near the god’s left eyebrow. Her thumb glides over the mark slowly. “This is the proof that they fear you. It took six of them to slow you down with their weak maneuvers. Even a god at half speed is enough to fling trash to the ground. And then one of those Suicidal Boys…those heathens…they dared to touch me. And for their sin, you struck the fear of the god into them. They tried fleeing, but you destroyed them for touching the Divine Prophetess, and just as you were going to put them out of their misery those other flat-scans came running down to stop you. They had to collaborate against you…enemies fighting for the same goal aligning just to stop the biggest threat they have ever seen. And during their petty assault one of those boys bled upon you after you had crushed them, and now people think you have been cut apart by those mortals.” Cyclonica presses the tip of her nail into the scratch, looking to see the reaction on the god’s face. He does nothing but close his eyes as a soft exhale comes rising through his nose. “Gods do not bleed...and even if they do, it’s not the same red piss that mortals bleed. Our blood is black…black as oil. Rich and pure… not filthy like the liquid flowing through the rest of these mortals… That swill in their veins is what fuels their body to be the weak machines that they are. It’s that blood that keeps them from being anything else than pathetic human mortals. Those men fear you…that’s why they hope that they can eliminate you from battle before it happens…but they cannot. But you will eliminate their existence from your path to championship gold as they fly from the ring.” She pulls her hands back and smoothes his black and silver locks back behind his head. Then she slowly begins to braid his hair into a ponytail. “Yes…Makonie’s first. He should know that we would not forget about this man before such an event. He’s a member of the Political Machine…with those hypocritical Politically Incorrect men. He who lies with the dogs shall be beaten like one. There’s nothing personal between this boy and the god…this will be the first time he comes in contact with him. Therefore we shall make sure that he leaves with a lasting impression of what the true master race of this world is…the Celestial Race. Nothing personal, Makonie…you’re just in the way…much like everyone else that comes across the nVg.” Those locks…twisting and intertwining together into one chain of hair…halfway finished… “After the bastard son of Hitler is disposed of because of his foolish ties to foolish men, there’s the domination of the tag team world. It’s easy to see that those political punks are trying to single the god out. It’s a wise…but foolish move. And that insect Powell…thinks I’m hot? That’s not wise of him…last person to make sexual references to me was Duke Williams, and he met his Absolute Destiny Apocalypse to the cold hard ground from over the top rope. With the amount of fools disrespecting me lately, I’m sure Akiyama would not be pleased with his words. Besides…he looks like he have no upper lip, or he just has a fat bottom lip. Lovecraft isn’t any better himself…looks like he lost his upper set of dentures or something. No matter how you tally the votes, the polls are stacked against them for this battle…they have no chance of winning here. All of their little theater theatrics will prove to be nothing but a fool’s play against the drama of the loss they will be handed.” She closes her eyes and sighs…the ponytail three fourths complete. Her arms go to her chest, folding below her bodice and clinging tightly to her fair frame. “These mortals…these swine…all of their eyes are molesting my body, giving their crude little underdeveloped minds ideas that they will never get the chance to try. That’s their hormones that control their minds…their libido that confuses them. Testosterone…it clouds strategy, and allows blind adrenaline to rush in. Fools…rush in. Akiyama’s battle is strategic…not just any musclehead can walk in here and do this. Just as men’s minds err in the sight of my body, their minds err in the sight of the god. His mere presence brings fear…anger…and anything that’s clouding their minds will be nothing more than the diversion Akiyama needs to remove you from the equation. But…if one more of these mortals touch me again I think I’ll go insane…there isn’t enough alcohol to sterilize their stench from my pure skin.” Enough of the Amy Campbell female bonding session…she returns to braiding the last bit of Akiyama’s hair. “All of those other fools…Heartbreakers…Pretty Cool Guys…Cowabunga Connection…Misplaced…Dark Messiahs…Suicidal Tendencies…those men will pay for the disrespect they shown the god recently. Their efforts are nothing…their simple little gimmick plays for the masses trying to make themselves look like something to fear. As we have been speaking the truth, they’ve done nothing but accept the truth and make unrealistic aspirations of obtaining victory through their accepted flaws. That entire concept is just stupid of them. Their will is gone…they cannot even defy the god’s words. They only accept it with promises of fighting past their flaws…reaching for the stars is a fair goal to achieve, but completely impossible if the stars are not in the sky. Hmph… if it’s the stars they want to reach for, then the Neo Void God can send them hurtling for them so they can get a handful of the shiny b!tches.” The tip of the ponytail is left open, the strands of the hair curling around her fingers slightly. She wraps a strand around her finger and tugs at it. “Swearing…is there a purpose for it at all? They’re used as society’s words to enforce a point, but society has come to the point where it’s standard vocabulary. They’re crude words, yes… but it does have its advantages. They have effect… but when people chronically abuse them, it’s just plain low classed of them. I guess it’s one of those mortal things that I shouldn’t concern myself with.” On the table, her eyes trail to the bandana…that yellow and black speckled pattern. It was the only article upon the god that wasn’t black or silver. Her fingers wrapped around it… …And her head felt like melting… { A forest…clearing…a man and a woman…she gives him something…yellow and black…emotions are stirring…love…the woman is gone…and despair is everywhere…} “…” Cyclonica stands there, her jaw slack and her eyes fluttering from underneath her lids. She opens them with a sigh and looks down at bandana. It’s nothing but material in her hands…but with it carries a past of pain… “That hero seems like he’s not the only one that can lose love…your past before you became the god has had its human little ups and downs. This bandana…it’s one of the reminders that you used to be human…but not anymore. Humanity is an ever burdening reminder of how far you’ve come in your existence, and how you’ve risen from the gutter of evolution into the pinnacle of complete life. That’s one of the reasons that these humans must go…so much of a waste of life they are when they don’t even have the desire to evolve into greater beings. But…one world at a time…they’ll all fall down…” With that said, she wraps the bandana around the loose end of the braid, tying it in a knot and tucking the folds underneath. “Why we were even cursed to exist in this realm is beyond my divine comprehension. We’re to be here…and implode the world from within. Ultimate suicide…ultimate harmony…” Finished…the tight woven strands finally chained into one solid mass…that’s how those mortals would have to operate if they wished to defeat the god. But they could not do that…their own selfish desires will keep them from operating in that manner. They will bicker and quarrel amongst each other…ultimately drawing into a situation where there’s no one left but one team…and the god…and with those odds…there’s no hope for salvation. Cyclonica walks around the table and looks at Akiyama. His eyes glare forward, twitching slightly as his muscles jump. She nods, placing both of her palms against his face and looking right into his eyes. “Be patient…you’ll get what you deserve…you’ll get the splendor and the honor that belongs to you. This is your time…your era! You are the monster of PCW…the monster of this world. And you will be the one that completely strikes the fear of god into the hearts of men. Your name will be with the legends after this…above the likes of that pathetic Chef…you will be the only person in history to hold doubles’ gold. You and you alone hold that honor…no one else.” And he knows this too…his head slowly nods at her words. A small, barely noticeable smirk inches onto her lips as she nods with him. “You…are the GOD…of GOLD AND GLORY…So says the Divine…Prophetess…” =©=
