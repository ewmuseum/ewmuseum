Date: January 17th, 2003. The scene struggles its way to open up, as the damn editors are messing everything up! The scene quickly reverts to JacksonCO’s promo studio, where several men and women frantically try to fix the beginning of this promo. Editor #1: Hurry up, guyth! My lithp won’t lath too muth longer! Editor #2: Why does that matter? Editor #1: Becauth I won’t get any airtime if I don’t have my lithp. Editor #3: You don’t get airtime anyway! Now hurry up and fix this promo! The editor with the lisp sits down in his JacksonCO swivel chair, and crosses his arms, pouting. Editor #1: There ith no God. More frantic editors and producers, and generic staff members one through twelve hurriedly push buttons, pull levers, and do other technical and machine like operations, before the scene dives into static and ‘snow’. Muffled voices are heard from the background, as more mumbling from the lisp-baring editor about “there ith no God” or something of the sort, as the promo swiftly dives back into the normal scene. Editor #1: Ladieth and gentlemen…I’ve come to the concluthion that there ith no God! Otherwithe, I’d get actual airtime on televithion. Generic Producer: What the hell? Generic Producer: Did I just step into an episode of Buffy, or are we gonna show this promo?! Just then, a beautiful woman walks up behind the producer, and taps him on the shoulder. On instinct, he turns around. Sarah Michelle Gellar: Yeah, excuse me…but you’re gonna have to let us have our equipment back. So yeah…umm…piss off? Generic Producer: Right away, Ms. Gellar. Sarah Michelle Gellar: Thank you so much, you adorable little wanker! Jackson Rust: My promo…my…beautiful, beautiful was ruined! Al: SMG is hot! Jackson sighs. Jackson Rust: If you’re going to complement my ex, Sarah Michelle Gellar on her looks, at least say her name, and not her initials, you ass. Al: SMG! SMG! SMG! Booming Voice: FEEL MY WRATH! *SMACK* *SMACK-AN-IDIOT* *SMACK-A-QUEER* Narrator: NEVER, and the Narrator means NEVER! EVER! Do that...A-GAIN! Al: Ouch…that really hurt, man. *SMACK* Narrator: Suck it up you pussy. Al: Jesus, dude…stop hitting me! *SMACK* Jackson stands back and watches as Al gets physically abused, smirking to himself. Narrator: Complain again, and I’ll knock you into next week! Al: But— *SMACK SMACK SMACK SMACK SMACK* Narrator: Bitch. Al collapses to the ground, and to make matters worse, Jackson accidentally chucks the water bottle that was placed into his hand at Al’s head. Jackson Rust: *looks around* Ooops. I dropped my water bottle. Kimberly: You threw it at his head! Jackson smirks. Jackson Rust: I know. Steve: O-oh. Kimberly: He looks out anyway. Narrator: Little pussy. Kimberly: Stop insulting him! Jackson Rust: No! I insist…continue. Narrator: Alright. Kimberly: Wait! Do you ever listen to Jackson? I’m the brains here! Narrator: Yeah, but I really wanna insult Al. Kimberly: Oh. Jackson Rust: Let’s what his dreams! Narrator: Can we do that? Jackson Rust: Here at the Jackson Rust Promos, ANYTHING, BY GOD, CAN HAPPEN! Steve: Sh-shut up. Kimberly: Please do. Jackson Rust: Anyway…it’s my God damned promo, and I say we look into his dreams. Narrator: Okay. Kimberly: Wait! Shouldn’t you be training for your match? Jackson sighs and turns around. Jackson Rust: Shouldn’t YOU be filing a lawsuit against the kids that destroyed Al’s new car. Kimberly: That’s his shit, not mine. Jackson Rust: Touché. The scene suddenly starts getting all wobbly like, almost the setting for a flashback. Jackson Rust: NO FLASHBACKS! The scene reverts back to normal pretty damn quick. Jackson Rust: We need a “going into Al’s dream” type of effect. A gay pride pole dance is shown on screen. Jackson Rust: Okay then…how about, we just go to the dream? The scene reverts back to normal even faster than the first, in attempt to cause at least less trauma. Narrator: Yeah. The scene quickly dives into Al’s dream, which is surprisingly a setting on the beach; the sun is shining upon the clear, crystal-like ocean, and it’s all warm and stuff. Moaning can be heard from a distance, as the camera in Al’s dreams, pans around to see under a beach umbrella, Al snuggling, and eating grapes with a semi-naked, damn good looking in a swimsuit, Kimberly. Al: Oh yeah, baby…eat my, I mean…those grapes. Kimberly: Give me more, Ally…I want more of your grapes. She moans as Al feeds her another grape, as screaming can be heard from a distance. The camera pans away for a mere three seconds to see in the water, several killer Great White sharks gnawing at Steve, and tossing his disembodied limps around like a football, before panning directly back to Al and Kimberly. Kimberly: Oh yeah…Al…you’re so manly. Al: I know, baby…I know. And you’re so hot. Kimberly: Ah…God…look at me again, and I might orgasm! Al looks at her again…oh yes he did. Kimberly: AH! AH! OH YES! LOOK AT ME MORE! Al continues to look…oh yes he is. Kimberly: JESUS CHRIST! AH! AH! Al: My eyes are so orgasmic…oh yeah! Al does a poorly executed double pelvic thrust. Al: I own. Kimberly: Yes baby…you do. As she calms down from her dream climax, a dream Jackson Rust walks out onto the scene and stares down at Al and Kimberly. They look back up at Rust. Jackson Rust: What the hell? You…her? Al: Hey…you had your chance in real life, and dreams. I got here first. Jackson Rust: Where did the confidence come from? Al: It’s a dream; remember? Jackson Rust: Oh yeah…anyway, can I have the grapes? Al tosses him the grapes, as he gropes Kimberly. Al: Knock your socks off, man. Jackson catches the bag, and walks to a beach chair, sitting down. Jackson Rust: They’re already off. He grapes the bunch of grapes, and bites into them, smiling as Al continues to grope Kimberly, and Steve continues to be gnawed at in the ocean. The scene slowly fades out and reverts back to Jackson, Steve, Kimberly, and the unconscious Al. Kimberly and Steve are shocked, as Jackson adorns his regular expression of confusion and uncaring emotions. Kimberly: The perv! Steve: H-he a-ate me! Jackson Rust: I got grapes! Narrator: I wasn’t involved! The glove opens Al’s leg, and then grabs Rust’s leg, and throws it forward into Al’s crotch! Al: … Kimberly: Jesus Christ! Kimberly whines, as tears roll down her cheek. Kimberly: If I saw him naked, I’d be clinically dead for an hour and a half! Narrator: Longer than that. Jackson Rust: Yare, yare. The scene abruptly fades out, and goes right back into the production stage, where infamous “Editor #1” gnaws on a dead Sarah Michelle Gellar’s neck. Editor #1: The’th mine! All mine! Generic Producer: Didn’t he die?! Editor #1: Nothing killth me! I’m infamouth Editor #1 for Chrith’th thake! Generic Producer: Oh, well…then would you kindly piss off of the JacksonCO property? Editor #1: Thure. Generic Producer: Great! The scene NOW abruptly fades out…for good.
