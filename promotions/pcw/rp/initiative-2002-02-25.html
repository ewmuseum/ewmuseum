Date: 2/25/02 OOC Note - AIM me at [detail withheld] and MSN me at [detail withheld]. HELLO NEW YORK~! Erm... i dont really know what to say... this is the role-play for round 1 of the Global Title tourny... hope ya enjoy... Initiative... Saturday, Febuary Reuniting, one of the greatest feelings in the world. To see someone that you have missed for so long, and have dreamt about seeing for so many years. There is nothing better than reuniting with a loved one, or someone really close to you. But, like all great theorys, there is a contradictory side of the story. With every family that gets along, there are 5 that dont. It just so happens that the Esteps do not. They have a history of arguing and bickering on a daily basis. People come and go in the family, but Mark has been in his family for 24 years, and he believes that it is what made him what he is today...The craziest bastard in professional wrestling... even 'Krayzier' than Jeremy Howard... so he likes to think... Gentle... quiet sound of morning. Nothing out of place, or misordered in any way. Then, the peaceful sound of a bird chirping outside of a window is heard. Then, suddenly the peacefulness and quietness is disrupted by gunshots and screaming, and the reality of Chicago surfaces whenever Mark Estep is woke in his bed by a ring from a telephone, sitting next to him on the night stand. He reaches over and answers it... Mark Estep:Hello Voice: Hi Mark, how you doing..? Mark Estep: Im fine... who is this? Voice: Its me, your uncle. Mark Estep: My uncle? Voice:Jason... Jason Core... Mark Estep: Oh, what do you want? Jason Core:Cant i call my favorite nephew every once in a while? Mark Estep:No... and im not your favorite nephew... or you would have contacted me in the last 4 years.... Jason Core: See, i would have, but i saw how much success you were having in NTICW, then PCW, so i didnt want to leave any weight on your mind. Mark Estep: Oh, i see... Jason Core:Yeah, i haven't talked to you in a while, huh? Mark Estep: Yeah, since our falling out... Jason Core:That was terrible Mark... i just wanted to apologize about that... Mark Estep:It's a little bit late for that, but apology accepted i guess... I want to apologize too... Jason Core:Don't worry about it, its in the past... Mark Estep:Oh, i don't worry about it at all.. trust me... Jason Core: I didnt think you would... but what's been up since the reunion? Mark Estep:Nothing really, i went to NTICW and won the Extreme title there... Jason Core:Yeah, i know about that... congradulations by the way.... Mark Estep:Thanks... Jason Core:Are you going after Hank~!'s Extreme title? Well, what will be my title after Sunday... Mark Estep: I dont really know, im not currently employed by PCW, but someday ill be back, and i might have to go after him... Jason Core:Or after me... Mark Estep:Nah, i wouldn't go after you old man, you might break a hip... Jason Core:I didn't break a hip when i beat the crap out of you at that family reunion now did I? Mark Estep:I told you, i had Ham Salad in my eye... it wasn't fair... Jason Core:Sure sure... you learned your lesson then didnt ya... Mark Estep:Yeah, ill never pick a fight at a picnic.... Jason Core:Uh huh... Mark Estep:So what do you want anyway? Jason Core:Well, you have that big Global Title Tournament coming up and... Mark Estep:And you want me to come to your school and work out, right? Jason Core:Well... yes and no... Mark Estep:What? Jason Core:I want you to come down to Tallahassee and work out, but not at my school... Mark Estep:Why not, that place was awesome... Jason Core:Yeah, but it was burnt down two years ago... Mark Estep:WHAT!?! Serioulsy!? Jason Core:Yup, sucks huh? Mark Estep:Yeah it does... was anyone hurt? Jason Core:Nope, nobody was there... thank god... Mark Estep:Yes, he does work in mysterious ways.. But um, why do i have to come down to Tallahassee? Jason Core:Because thats where i live... Duh... Mark Estep:Is that where you are calling me from right now? Jason Core:Yeah... Mark Estep:LONG DISTANCE!?! Jason Core:Haha, sure am... but dont worry, i used 1-800-Call-ATT With a chuckle we both began to remember the past. After almost half an hour of regrouping and talking about the past, and what had happened in the last four months... Jason Core:Well, are you gonna come down to Tallahassee? Mark Estep:Well... what time is it? Jason Core:Six-Thirty... Mark Estep: In the morning?!? Jason Core:Well, are you usually asleep at 6:30 at night? Mark Estep:Good point... smart ass.... Jason Core:Hey! Watch your language... Mark Estep:Sorry, i dont really want to come all the way down to Tallahassee though... Just then, the phone beeps from call waiting on the other line... Mark Estep:Hang on a minute Jason... I have a beep... Jason Core:Alright, but remember, this IS long distance.... Mark Estep:Yeah, thanks... I switch over to the other line to hear the voice of D-Kay Calyx. D-Kay Calyx: Yo Mark... Mark Estep:Hey man, how's it goin? D-Kay Calyx:Cool, you doin anything today? Mark Estep:Well, i was plannin on going to Tallahassee later tonight... D-Kay Calyx:Where's that at? Mark Estep:Florida. D-Kay Calyx:Oh cool, how long are you staying? Mark Estep:Until Sunday night... D-Kay Calyx:Rock on... Mark Estep:Why do you wanna know? D-Kay Calyx:Because, you know that Calvin Rilley guy? Mark Estep:Doesn't ring a bell... D-Kay Calyx: Oh, you know, Boda's class champion in PWA.. Mark Estep:Still... doesnt strike me.... D-Kay Calyx:You know the annoying PWAer!?! Mark Estep:The Music Man? D-Kay Calyx:YES! Mark Estep:Oh yeah, i know him... What about him? D-Kay Calyx:He has an extra ticket for the Daytona 500 tommorrow at Daytona Beach... you wanna go? Mark Estep:Sure... D-Kay Calyx:K, be at my house at 6... bring something to drive 6 other guys to Daytona Beach dude... Mark Estep:Wait... 6 people? I have a Ford Mustang for crying out loud!! D-Kay Calyx:Later dude!! He hung up, and i was stuck. How was i gonna find a vehicle that could take 6 people? Wait a minute... Jason Core is on the other line...... LONG DISTANCE!!! Mark Estep:Jason, you there!?!? Jason Core:Yeah, im still here.... Mark Estep:Damn, i was hoping that you might have hung up or something.... Jason Core:Gee, thanks Mark... Mark Estep:No, i didnt mean it like that, i meant if you would have hung up, i wouldn't have to pay for them 10 minutes that i was just on the other line.... Jason Core:Oh, well i have to go anyway... Mark Estep:Alright... Ill be there sometime soon.. alright? Jason Core:Yeah, see you when you get here... Mark Estep:See ya... Ol' Man... Jason Core:Later kid... We both hang up and i lay my head back down on my pillow. Just as soon as my head hits the fluff of the pillow, the door bell rings. Mark Estep:Ahhh crap! Is everyone in this world awake at 6 in the morning today!??! Amber: (Just waking up next to me) No, shut up, im trying to sleep!! Mark Estep:Oh, sorry babe... So i get out of bed and begin my path down the steps and into the living room. The door bell keeps ringing... Mark Estep:Hold on, IM Comin!! Ding Dong.... Ding Dong..... Ding Dong...... Mark Estep: (Opening the door) What?!? Delivery Guy:Sign here... Mark Estep: (While signing) What am i signing for? Delivery Guy: (Taking the board from me) This truck... Mark Estep: Truck!?! The delivery guy nods and walks away and gets in a pick-up truck with his partner, as they drive off. When they drive off, the camera and my eyes both lock on to a giant Semi truck with a huge 48 foot trailor with the 'Gatorade' logo on it. My jaw drops Mark Estep:Hpmh... I guess six people could fit in there... The scene closes out slowly as my jaw dangles from the rest of my face.
