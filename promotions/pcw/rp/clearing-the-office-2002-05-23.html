Clearing The Office.

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/05/02 Since Spring Fling, Xavier Kannon and Tyler Nelson had been inseparable. Between them they own 70% of PCW, something revealed right at the end of the PPV when Tyler announced he owned 50% and Kannon, using money from his Snapple~! endorsement owned 20%. From there they spearheaded the Ultimate Authority’s reign of terror over PCW, with Tyler as CEO and Xavier naming himself Commissioner they had all the power they needed to shape PCW in their image, making the matches they wanted and guiding the company in their direction. But now, their relationship seems to have dissolved in the wake of Supremacy. Nelson lost his rag and fired Kannon, and Kannon, not being the most flexible and forgiving people in the world seems to want nothing to do with the CEO... Not good news as they will have to team on Friday Fusion to face the Malice Crew. So it seems the Nelson/Kannon alliance is over, as is Xavier’s role in PCW’s “Front Office”... So there can only be one thing left to do... “Time to finally sever the ties...... He wants to fire me so I lose the Commissionership then fine.... I’ll pack my stuff and leave.... Lets see who books him in title matches now!” Our shot opens up in an elevator in the PCW Building in New Jersey, host city of the return Fusion where Xavier will be forced to fight alongside the CEO, the man who fired him less than 2 weeks ago was they do battle with the Malice Crew. Xavier : “I gave him his shot and..... Surprise, surprise he LOST! Did I lose? NO, I won... So therefore I had no reason to BE jealous and fire everyone!” In the elevator stand none other than Xavier Kannon, almost looking like he relishes the task of clearing out his old office. By his side, as ever, is his valet Eleanor Hall, who has been subject to rumours linking her to a wrestling contract with PCW. Xavier : “Look at it this way.... Shanghaied II he loses to Duke... Supremacy I beat Duke. Supremacy he loses to Big Alan Steele, I BEAT BIG ALAN STEELE!!! Even with Jeremy Howard trying to kill me I pinned BAS.... Anyone else starting to wonder if the wrong person got into the position of power?” *DING* Now from the outside we see the doors open and out walk the couple, both of them screwing up their faces and holding their noses. Eleanor : “Eugh.... For those seconds in the elevator I forgot this place was in Jersey!” Kannon goes to reply but is harking up his lungs in disgust, nodding in agreement with his preppy valet and he looks around the open plan reception area. Xavier : “The stench of Atlantic City, coupled with the hideous odour of jealousy left in Tyler Nelson’s office is just too overbearing to the senses....” Xavi and Eleanor walk through the reception towards his old Commissioner’s office where such matches as Big Alan Steele v Snow v Tyler Nelson were made when Kannon was playing the role of puppet to Tyler Nelson. Xavier : “I hate Atlantic City... The Mayor should look at Hubbardville and take some hints... What does he expect when you cram a cess pool full of Casinos... Money always attracts those who don’t have it, and who in Hubbard’s name wants poor people living in their City?” While Kannon rants on the economic class of people who frequent Atlantic City they reach his door, which still has... “Commissioner Gold Patron Meritorious Xavier Kannon’s Office” ...On the front. He looks at the door and sees his reflection in it, the bright Green eyes that lurk within the curtains of Auburn hair noticeable. Xavier : “I am not bitter..... But unlike a certain CEO...” At the mention of Tyler’s name, his valet again sneers, running through her reasons to hate him in her head. Eleanor : “C.........E.........O...EUGH!!!” Xavier : “...I can look into this plaque an see myself staring back and I see a winner.... I see a man who WON at Supremacy.... I see a man who DIDN’T lose the Presidency to Duke Williams... Wait, make that DIDN’T LOSE FULL STOP to Duke Williams!” He pulls his set of keys from the pocket of his Black leather trousers and unlocks the door, letting it fall open before him and he slowly walks in and surveys his old stomping ground. Xavier : “Time to clear out the shit.... Oh, that’s right.... Tyler KEPT himself on...” Ellie~! joins him in walking into the room and stands just inside the door with her hands on the hips of her sandblasted Blue flared jeans with Silver sequinned stars running down the legs. Eleanor : “Kept...... Himself........On!” Kannon goes to his desk and begins to open the draws, pulling out old contracts and requests for matches from before Supremacy. Xavier : “Oh.... The memories.... What do we have here....” He pulls a random sheet from the pile and reads it over, smirking at it’s contents. Xavier : “From Mike Frampton, to Xavier Kannon.... EUGH... I’m not even going to read that, I can’t believe I got THAT drunk that I put Frampton on a PPV... If Tyler would have fired me for that I would have understood!” He goes to toss them all in the trash when he sees one sheet that takes his eye, and as the others drop down into the refuse, one contract is left held between his fingers and he scowls at the text printed onto it. Ellie~! looks at him with raised eyebrows, muttering to herself. Eleanor : “Wow... He must be, like, SO pissed off.... He’s angry with paper now!” The Scientologist looks down from the paper to the trash and back up to the paper, shaking his head. Xavier : “The trash is too good for this!” As he holds it up we see it is a contract we have previously seen him with, the contract he and Nelson concocted.... Entering Tyler into the World Title match at Supremacy with Snow and Big Alan Steele. Xavier : “What more did he want me to do for him... WRESTLE?” He then starts to mock Nelson’s voice... Xavier : “Hey Kannon, put me in a World Title # 1 Contenders match will you.... Hey Kannon, book me for a Title show will you... Hey Kannon, sorry I blew it so I’m just going to have to fire you and the whole of the PCW roster to make up for my own inadequacies...” As he mocks Nelson, his valet just stares at him, wide eyed with her hands on her hips. Eleanor : “Wow.... He’s, like, taking this hard!” As Ellie~! sighs, the Gold Patron Meritorious heads over to the shredder and his stern face melts as fond memories come flooding back, and he just looks down at it before wiping the beginnings of a tear from his eye. Xavier : “Ah.... Beloved shredder.... I remember the good times we had.... How I used to give all Duke’s match requests to you.... All the extortionate personal expenses receipts... All shredded..... The outgoings I wanted hidden from Duke.... All shredded.... Ah well, here’s one for old times...” With glee he holds the contract to the shredder and the motor kicks in and within seconds the Supremacy Main Event contract is left in fine strips. He dusts his hands off and goes to the cupboard and looks at his corporate wear, expensive shirts and ties, with matching jackets from a tailor recommended by Tyler. Xavier stares at them for a second before closing the door and sneering. Xavier : “Let whatever flunky Nelson and the Phantom Booker decide on next wear them.... Maybe they can add some extra padding for when the knife goes for their back...” “Can we...Like, you know.... GO now or something?” A rather bored looking Eleanor whines from across the room and Xavier closes the cupboard with one last sigh and walks back to the door, swiping a paperweight off the desk on his way before taking Ellie~!’s arm. Xavier : “Wow... I hate Nelson, I hate Booker, I hate the wrestlers AND I hate the fans....” The door closes shut behind them and the shot fades out with only Ellie~!’s voice being heard. “Yeah.... Hating people is like, SO much better than, you know, like.. Liking them!”
