The Thriller Speaks Out

7 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/7/04 ‘So what. So I lost to you Boda. Big deal. I blame Jon Kano for it anyhow.’Our scene opens with Nathaniel Oscar Seller standing outside a motel, near a busy highway.‘Yeah, Kano made you really mad this past week, what with torching that pathetic jersey of yours. And what do you do? You take it out on me, like I’m the one that lit the match.’The sound of tires screeching interrupts N.O Seller. A car door is heard being slammed, and suddenly a large man is standing in front of N.O Seller.Large Man: ‘Yo…. You’re that little punk that thought you could beat Boda. HA. You got taken to school by The Big Bad Boda Daddy. You looked more like The Boda Bitch. HA.’N.O Seller laughs with the man, throwing the man off guard.‘You’re right, puke. I did lose. At least I tried. But dammit, I won’t be held down for long. This coming week, is the last open challenge, and god dammit…. The Thriller will thrill all, and I’ll hit my patented 450 splash, and pin my next opponent. And in the Elite Eight, The Final Four…. I don’t care when or where, I will meet Boda again, and I will show him why I am The Giant Killer.’The large man starts to laugh. He looks N.O Seller in the face.Large Man: ‘Come on, who the hell you think you can beat? I mean, I’ve been a fan of PCW for a long time. And they let you into the open challenge tournament. You need a year or two in the PWA, and then maybe you’ll be a good jobber. But, to come in to the top of the mountain, and proclaim for two weeks now that you’re going to be the next World Champion……….. HA, HA I say. And besides, who you gonna challenge this week? Skitzo? Blitz? Boda again? Come on, who?’‘The one that calls himself ‘The Buzz’.The large man looses it. He hugs on N.O Seller, laughing so hard his face turns bright red.Large Man: ‘That’s rich. You’ve challenged The Buzz, for his seed? HAHAHA’N.O Seller seems to be angry at this point in time. He pushes the man off him, and then delivers a well placed kick to the nuts. The big man drops to a knee, where N.O Seller is able to move in, and plant a stiff roundhouse to the large man. Out like a light he goes down.‘Who the fuck are you, to put your hands on me? I am untouchable, you understand you fat pig. And you, Buzz, you better take me as serious as a heart attack, because, punk… The Thriller is going to fly high this Saturday night. I’m going to knock The Buzz down a peg or two, and then win my way into the Elite Eight. From there…. There is no stopping me. Come this Saturday night, I’m a be more prepared, and The Buzz, you won’t see it, but you’ll feel it. I’m talking about me beating your ass. The time for games is over…. I’ll see you in Memphis.’N.O Seller kicks the large man in the head, then turns and walks into a motel room behind him as the scene fades out.
