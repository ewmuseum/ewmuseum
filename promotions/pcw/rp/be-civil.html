Be Civil.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered A cool breeze blows across the craggy cliff overlooking San Jose as we rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, along with trusted manager Sue Saksnussum and partner in crime (fighting) The Masochist. The Masochist shivers and looks upwards unto the gothic castle looming before them, an omnipresence of evil oppressing the spirit. Sue checks the invitation, making sure everything is in order. “Well, this is the place. Can’t imagine there being another stone and mortar castle in the hills near San Jose and the beach.” “Man,” he grumbles, “Do the bad guys ALWAYS have to go with a style that absorbs all warmth and light? I swear it’s darker up here than in the city… just once I’d like to see a villain holed up in a charming bed and breakfast, or maybe a nice condo over on Ventura.” Big Al leans in close, whispering menacingly, “Rumor has it that this used to be a lab for strange experiments that went against the very fabric of humanity! Godless creatures of lore roamed the halls thirsting for the blood of the righteous upon their lips; an undead cacophony of the hoary denizens of nightmares, their very existence mocking the gods of Olympia!!” Sue smoothes down the folds of her business suit as she shoots a scornful look at the two. “Shut up, the both of you. This retreat used to be Stephen Baldwin’s place…” “Evil! EEEEVIL!!!” “Keep on task, Al,” replies his manager curtly, “For all we know we may be walking straight into a trap. It’s not often one of the criminal elite sends us an invitation for tea and crumpets.” Steele snaps into a suitably heroic pose, “Fear not, Ms. Sue, for I, Big Alan Steele, shall not let any harm come to thee on this day, be it simple date for tea or cunning death trap!! This I swear! Hi-Keeba!” “What are you saying? I didn’t expect you to let anything happen to me! In fact, I’d stake your life on it…” Big Al adjusts the collar of his toga. “Ah, what I merely wished to convey…” “Shhhh!” hisses The Masochist, “I think we’ve been made!” The rusty sound of turning gears grates overhead as the two huge, heavy double doors are slowly pulled back with the aid of ancient mechanics. The three peer into the murk inside, slightly startled as a wizened and enfeebled butler shuffles his way to the doors and makes a stunted gesture to follow. Big Alan Steele’s eyes narrow, and he leans in close to Sue. “We must be wary of that one… as tis a common fact that all butlers are inherently evil…” Sue dismisses Steele with a scornful look and strolls inside. The Masochist shrugs and also enters, Steele looking around warily before also allowing himself to be swallowed up by the darkness inside. Just as their eyes adjust to the dark, they enter into light, a warm British accent welcoming them. “Ah! It is truly a PLEASURE to finally meet with all of you!! I daresay, I was a trifle concerned that you would find my offer not to your liking, but then again, I should have known you would not refuse such without proper advance regrets. Please, have a seat!” The trio have found themselves ushered into a rather pleasing living room, devoid of the dank and murk normally associated with castles. A kind of elegance that only the British seem to be able to capture pervades the setting, delicately ornate furniture surrounding a quaint table setting near a fireplace. The man greets them warmly, taking each by the hand to show his gratitude for their arrival, and earning only suspicious looks for his troubles. “Oh dear, but where are my manners! I apologize, but I have been so consumed with your arrival, I seem to find myself a tad flushed…” The man bends at the waist in a graceful bow, keeping his eyed fixed on the group. His face lined with years, yet still handsome, match well with his salt-and-peppered hair. His suit speaks of wealth and privilege. The only distinguishing feature being a long jagged scar running along his jaw bone on the right side and extending upwards, just nicking the corner of his right eye. “I am Mr. Civilized, and I do so appreciate your coming! Please, be seated, as I promise you, no death traps await you on this day! To do so would be… uncivilized.” Sue sits down in a Louie Cans, the briefest hint of a smile tugging at her pursed lips. The Masochist settles himself into a love seat, obviously enjoying its comforts. Big Al narrows his eyes, arms crossed across his chest. “I do not associate with villains, on any level! Let us begin this…” Mr. Civilized frowns, but a frown of true sadness. “I do wish that today could be one of discovery and pleasantries, as I am sure we shall soon enough be in the throes of battle. Can we not behave in a civilized manner and indulge our human sides for just a simple hour or two?” The dashing man snaps his fingers and a maid bustles into the scene, discreetly placing a silver tray on the coffee table. Mr. Civilized smiles warmly. “I have done my homework, yes?” Steele looks down at the Rice Krispie treats, then back up to the villain. Then to the treats. Then Mr. Civilized. The treats… Slowly reaching down, Big Alan Steele hesitantly picks up a treat, his eyes never leaving his host. He bites, slowly chewing, before he slowly sinks into a plush chair, unable to contain his grin. Mr. Civilized can barely contain his glee. “Excellent! Earl Grey, anyone?” “Please,” replies The Masochist, “But maybe we should get started on this little tete au tete…” “But there will be plenty of time for that…” “I am afraid there will not be, for time constraints of my heroic exploits and wrestling workload pressure this tea party into a strict timetable,” counters Big Al, “Hospitality aside, I’m afraid.” Mr. Civilized sighs heavily. “You Americans, always bustling this way and that, never taking the time to enjoy the finer things in life, such as good company and properly served tea. So be it; on to the business at hand, eh? Where shall we begin?” “Well, for one, we can start with your invitation,” says Sue, after neatly sipping at her cup. “Yes? And what of it?” “Well, it says ‘You are cordially invited to partake in tea and company of one Mr. Civilized, blah, blah, blah, … and spirited discussion regarding my current plans to destroy San Jose, send Big Alan Steele to heaven, en route to conquering the world.’ See anything odd about that?” Mr. Civilized leans back in his chair, pursed lips, thinking hard. “I believe it states my intentions properly.” Steele swallows a mouthful of Rice Krispie Treat. “Though I am pleased of your wishes of me in divine company, I must say I am not as of yet ready to join the gods of Olympia in the afterlife, and nor shall I be willing to stand by and allow your schemes of global dominance to go unchecked!” “But I don’t WANT you to allow my plans to go unchecked! I fully endorse your ministrations to counter my plans, for it would be unsporting to assume otherwise.” Big Al gapes. “Wha-…?!” Mr. Civilized grins and stands up grandly. “But that is what today is about!! I want you to know exactly what you’ll be up against, including the who and the when! That is what gamesmanship is all about. Anything less would be…” “Yeah, yeah, we get it,” snarls Sue, “So you actually think you have a chance against my boy Big Al here? No one has been able to yet.” “Mostly because they went about their work incorrectly. Trickery and deception can only lead to a grim demise. I feel that I am well-equipped to take on such a Herculean-task, pun intended. Ha!” Steele flashes a grin to Sue, along with a thumbs up. “I like the cut of his jib!” Sue stands, her face flashing crimson, as she grabs a fistful of toga, pulling Steele’s face in close with hers. “That’s just it, you clod! He’s TRYING to soften you up so maybe you’ll pull your punches later!!” “No, no, no! That’s not it at all,” implores Mr. Civilized, “I am merely wishing to allow the playing field to be seen. I expect no quarter once the game is afoot.” “And therein lies your nefarious nature!!” exclaims Big Al, pointing accusingly at his host, “You think this a game, when you plan the demise of thousands of lives and the enslavement of the planet!! Tis no game I know of… only the pure, unadulterated stench of true evil!” Mr. Civilized sniffs at the air, wrinkling his nose. “No, I’m afraid that is the stench of the Baldwins, as no matter what remedies I implement, whatever they did here before I purchased this lair liners to this day…” “Do not play coy with me, Lord of Forked Tongues!” Sue rubs her chin in contemplation. “You know… All we’ve really heard is what he wants to do, but not how he plans to do it. Nor what he has to make it come true. Maybe he’s bluffing…” “Bluffing?!” Mr. Civilized nearly chokes on the word, “Maybe in YOUR uncivilized culture such crude tactics are deemed acceptable, but not in mine, milady!!” “Oh yeah? Then what have you got, hmmm? What do you have that will pose a threat to Big Al here?” Mr. Civilized trembles in anger, his fists tense as he shakes them in fury. “Oh, you shall find out! In fact, I invite you to tour my vast army of destructive power that I house right here, in this very castle! I have stormtroopers, armored and equipped with the finest weaponry! I have robotic suits piloted by men, the highest technologies invested! Missiles with ballistic capabilities! Warhawk helicopters bought on the black market! And, the coup de grace, a genuine nuclear warhead, hidden from this locale, to hold nations of the world hostage!! Personally, I am trained in 6 different martial arts and have military training regarding troop movements and strategies!! I am well financed and cold-blooded enough to keep the human element of emotion out of my decisions! I shall be unstoppable!! BWAHAHAHAHAHA!!” “Uh-huh,” Sue says, incredulously, “And this is all where?” “Oh, forgive my lack of manners. Through that door and down the hall.” Steele growls. “Lord of Pain, go yonder and check it out, to see if all is as they say.” “Thanks for making me relevant…” grumbles the Masochist as he does as told. Big Al eyes up Mr. Civilized, the madness of the rant now gone, replaced by the well-mannered man they first encountered. “Tell me then, my soon-to-be worthy adversary, why is it that you reveal all to me? Do you not fear my reactions to your diabolical plots?” Mr. Civilized grins, rubbing his hands together in glee. “But of course, but as I stated before, I have done my homework. You will not harm me this day.” “Your arrogance shall be your downfall…” “But it is not arrogance, Mr. Steele!! As it is, I have done nothing yet! All that I have admitted to you is technically not illegal in the U.S., God bless America! Save for the nuclear weaponry, of course, yet you shall not find it here, and therefore its existence is merely hearsay. No court could convict me and your tightly woven swears to Lady Justice force you to react to my plans, instead of taking a proactive approach. Heroes are so predictable…” The Masochist bursts back into the room, breathless with excitement, his eyes wide in alarm. “It’s all there… it’s all true!! He got, like, a whole army of stuff!!” Big Al tenses in alarm. “By the Gods!!” Mr. Civilized throws his head back in laughter. “But of course it is all true! I am not a liar! Soon, I shall crush you and your precious city, as I have seen through of all the angles… all the possibilities! And you are powerless to stop me!! BWAHAHAHA-…” CLANG!! The eyes in Mr. Civilized head roll back in his head and he pitches forward, slumping to the ground, unconscious. Just behind where he once stood is Sue, the small, iron shovel from the fireplace wielded in her hands like a baseball bat, the follow through evident. She smiles her own evil smile. “Betcha he didn’t see THAT one coming!” Big Al steps across and check on his foe, whom is out cold. “Sue!!! Such tricks go against the very definition of a hero!! We shall have no proof of any wrongdoings of his part!!” “Right, but I’m not a hero. And we will once we show the proper authorities this invitation. It’s like a signed confession! And it will be once I get his fingerprints on this thing… now, lift him onto the chair. That’s a good boy.” Big Al plops the Englishman on a sofa, as Sue slaps his hand over the card. “Okay, now Masochist, you tie him up, and make the ropes as tight as you like. I’ll be, uh, confiscating evidence.” “I doubt those golden candlesticks shall be used against him in a court of law,” growls Big Al. “Listen, this guy is going down. We have justifiable cause and, heck, he invited us search the premises. Chances are they’ll just take everything and sell it off at police auction, for a fraction on the worth. I’m just going to make sure some of his finer things are put to better use. He would want it this way. I’ll give his personal affects a better home that some police warehouse.” The Masochist looks up while tying off a few ropes. “You know, you scare me sometimes, Sue.” “It’s the only civilized thing to do.” Big Al throws up a hand in victory. “Another villain defeated by our righteous hands!! And his stuff will be given a better home! The gods of Olympus shall be very pleased, indeed!” “You know,” says Sue casually, “This is the third roleplay that we haven’t mentioned our opponents.” Big Al grins, “Well, I would hate to break a streak, especially now that a great battle looms near! And make sure you grab that plate of Krispie treats… tis like ambrosia for my soul!!” “Sure,” shrugs Sue, “I think the server is antique silver.” “Hi-Keeba!!”
