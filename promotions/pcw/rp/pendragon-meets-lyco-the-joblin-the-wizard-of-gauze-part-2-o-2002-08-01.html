Pendragon Meets 'Lyco the Joblin'...The Wizard of Gauze, part 2 of ?)

1 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-1-2002 (When last we left our hero, Venneman Pendragon, he was in the land of Gauze, supposedly as a result of the horrific attack at the hands of Syko the Goblin on the Wrestle Vessel Pay Per View. He is still in the land of gauze, still dressed in his golden armor, still wearing his championship belt, only now, he holds Excalibur in his right hand as he and his guide, Psycho, make their way down the yellow brick road. Why has he drawn his weapon? When last we were here we encountered a very happy and beautiful landscape. That has changed. The trees through which the road now winds are twisted and maimed. A slight mist rises from the ground and envelopes the lower trunks of these hideous trees, yet fails to cover the yellow brick road. Pendragon looks confident, as ever, yet Psycho seems a little nervous, as his metal clinks together as he shivers slightly. Damn. I frightened robot. Pendragon looks up the road a ways and the camera turns to take in what he sees. Sitting in the middle of the road is a tiny, child-shaped creature. Covered in a brown robe, the figure sits cross-legged in the middle of the road. A cowl covers its face, but by shape we would judge it to be a man. Psycho hides behind Xerxes and whimpers, but Pendragon stares at the figure, coldly calculating the situation. He rides until he is nearly on top of the squatting figure before he is stopped by a rasping voice from beneath the cowl...) Figure: And who dares to ride down my road...Any that do must pay me a sacrifice...What do you offer, stranger? (Venneman stares at the figure, unbelieving. He then shakes his head and answers coldly, the insane light in his eyes growing more noticeable with each word...) Venneman: I offer nothing, and I give nothing except a question...Who are you to order the Lord king Venneman Pendragon? I answer to no man, least of all one who hides his ugliness in a cowl, and barely reaches my waist in height. That just doesn't happen... (A jolt goes through the figure, and he bounds to his feet. Still raspy, yet excited, he speaks again...) Figure: So....The rumors are true indeed. A hero has come to The Land of Gauze. Oh what an event. The master wants to have words with you, and who better than me to show you the way? You must be tired of trekking aimlessly... (Pendragon chuckles. Seems like everyone is happy to see him. He shakes his head at the figure.) Venneman: I already have a guide. He is taking me to see the Wizard. I appreciate the offer, but I think I s will keep the company of Psycho. He doesn't seem like much, but at least he is no longer a variable to me. So, thanks, but no thanks. (Pendragon turns to point out Psycho, who is now huddled behind Xerxes, shivering. Pendragon looks from him to the figure, and the 'What the hell?' look crosses his face. The figure rises and walks back to the cowering metal man and looks down upon him.) Figure: So...You found him first. I had hoped I had taken care of you last time, but sometimes mistakes can be made. Say, Pendragon; I bet this guy has been tellin you you are dreaming. That you got hit kinda hard and BOOM!!! Here you are, except you are really safe in your bed. Let me be the first to assure you that he is wrong. (Psycho jumps up, shaking off his fear for a moment of defiance...) Psycho: Why would I mislead him? He saved me from the fate that you sought to leave to me. He helped me move when I could not help myself. I do not lie; he is dreaming! Figure: Ahh my poor, poor, Psycho. You never learn, do you? Do you really think the Master wants to have a half-wit like you leading this important man around the Land of Gauze? DO you? I guess I need to help dispel that notion right now... (Psycho backs away a couple of steps as the Figure pulls what appears to be a rather large Supersoaker water gun from beneath the robe. Psycho lets out a holler as he turns to flee and gets hosed down with water from the gun. As the water touches him, his knees and hips seize up with instant rust and he cannot move. He turns to Pendragon to speak, but gets a mouthful of water instead. The Figure proceeds to coat him with water so that soon, he is as unmoving as he was when Pendragon found him. Only this time, there is no oil can handy.) Figure: MWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAAAAAA!!! Foiled again, Psycho! You were never a match for me anyway...You are too weak, too frail. Venneman: Why, you vile creature. What was that about? Why did you do that to poor Psycho??? All he did was fear you. You need not have done that. Figure: He was in my way. I am supposed to lead you to the master. He knows and I know that he would have failed. Hell, he tried to tell you this was a dream. What a fool...Ahh well. He will have plenty of time to think it over..Come on...We must get going. (The Figure takes off but Pendragon merely sits his horse and crosses his hands on the saddle. He stares haughtily down at the smallish figure and speaks coldly, his eyes a pair of insane orbs...) Venneman: I will do no such thing. First off, you need to explain to me who and what you are. Second, you can explain just who the 'master' is and what he wants with me. If he is so powerful, why can he not come to me? Get talking or get yourself gone....I have spoken. (The Figure stops and turns around. His shoulders sag a little as he walks slowly back to the sword bearing King. He shakes his head and begins to speak in that annoying rasp...) Figure: If it is the only way to get you to follow, I shall speak. My name is Lyco the Joblin. I was sent by the master, who shall remain nameless, to fetch you to him. It is your destiny to meet this man, and he has great plans for you. You are a man of great power and intellect and the Master believes you will help him accomplish great things here in Gauze. You are merely to follow me and then speak to him. Any questions? (Pendragon's face has become more and more confused as 'Lyco?' talks. He finally speaks, a tad quieter than before...) Venneman: Lyco? Your name is Lyco? Will I never escape from the taint of that neo Goth Syko the Goblin? Lyco: Why, his name sounds almost like mine! Venneman: Aye, it does...And do not interrupt. I am not finished. I can see that you two are connected in some way...Look at how small you are. I bet if you take off that hood that you are a scary looking monster who at any moment might try to eat me, am I right? (Lyco shakes his head. He pulls the hood back and we are met with the face of a small child. Pendragon chuckles a little that the raspy voice came from such an innocent looking being. Lyco looks angry, but Pendragon doesn't notice....) Venneman: The similarities are never ENDING!!! And, just like the real Syko, you fellows just keep coming! I have crushed the fellow twice this past week, and the bookers in PCW feel that I have to do it again on Friday! Just like him, you keep coming! First, Psycho meets up with me...Now, you do, and you look almost just like Syko! Is there no justice in the world? (Pendragon breaks out in laughter that starts small, but soon grows quite large. Tears stream down his face he laughs so hard. And we all though Syko was psycho. Lyco stares uneasily at the big man before risking a couple of words...) Lyco: Ummm...Venneman? (Venneman breaks from his laugh at the suddenly out of place rasp of Lyco...) Venneman: And just what is a Joblin, if you would be so kind? (Lyco looks kinda sheepish before he answers. Evidently, he is not proud of being a 'Joblin'. Lyco: A Joblin is a mix of a Goblin, a ferocious beast in almost any circle, and also, a...a....*sigh* What you might consider a 'Jobber' in your world. A no count being who is only there by mere luck? (Pendragon starts laughing again, this time, so hard that he falls from his horse. He lies back on the ground and keeps laughing. He tries to speak, but it is tough to understand what he is trying to get at...) Venneman: The...Similarities...HAHAHA!!!! Go Even...FURTHER!!! HAHAHAHAHAHA!!! A Joblin...That's Syko if ever anything describes him. Lord yes.... Lyco: Are you quite finished with your revelry...My Looooord? We have many miles to cover, and the way is not easy, regardless of what that pathetic fool psycho tried to tell you... Venneman: Give me a minute. I have to take all this in....HAHAHHAHAHA!!!! 'As I lay there on the bricks in a Land called Gauze, I couldn't help but think again what I was doing here...Was it a dream? It could be. I know I have seen enough of Syko of late to insert him into these strange fantasies. I don't get it. How many times must I prove my superiority over the midget? The only chance he has had against me is when he gets his over-pierced friends in on the act, and even then I prevail. That is the way that it is. I am a champion, and he is a Joblin. Need I say more? No. I'll just have to reassert my superiority come Friday...Say...Is that a Duck coming down the road????' (Venneman sits up as he and Lyco stare quizzically at the duck, and a look of what can only be called desire passes over the face of the childlike Lyco. The scene fades with Pendragon scratching his head, and Lyco licking his lips....) >>>>>>TO BE CONTINUED
