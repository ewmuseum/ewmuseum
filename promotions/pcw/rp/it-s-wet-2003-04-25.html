It's wet.

25 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/04/03 There’s something calming about water. Sure, when you get hit by it, your clothes get wet, cold, clingy and unpleasant. But when you’re completely surrounded by it, immersed in it, you feel… relaxed. Except humans can only enjoy this sensation for so long before their deprived lungs begin to scream for air and they have to break the surface and leave the wonderful cradle. And the other option comes at the price of heavy equipment and a mouthpiece stuffed in your mouth, silencing you, or a huge tank on your back. Quite simply, the feeling may come from a more primal instinct. Remember floating around inside your mother, surrounded on all sides by that amniotic fluid, cushioned and tiny? No, of course you don’t, but you must have seen it on television or in school. You know how it feels. It’s comforting, reassuring, safe. BUT I DON’T HAVE THAT. I’m trapped inside this metal coffin, entombed at the bottom of some body of water. I’ve not fed in days, and I can smell my skin rotting all around me. Waterlogged, suffocated, starving, alone, denied any form of pleasure or content. I imagine how things are going on the surface. PCW is still holding events. Gaz Green is still wrestling. Eating. Breathing. Tessa is probably nursing her little Chris back to health. Snuggling. Hugging. Being close. And here I am… rotting… decaying… being hungry. But there are two kinds of hungers I feel right now. One is the way my stomach feels as though it’s going to shrink and tear itself from the sides of my torso, snapping all sorts of muscles. I guess that what these stabbing pains are. It’s like an omnidirectional vacuum, pulling inwards from all directions. I can’t burst through this metal shell in this human body and I don’t have the blood to transform. I can’t even keep my human disguise up any longer. When occasionally a small streak of sunlight penetrates the water this far down and I see it through a crack, I can see that my skin has turned white. It’s even coming off in patches. I’m decomposing. This body is slowly wasting away. And then there’s the other kind of hunger. I’d trade everything I own right now – my house in Japan, everything in it, the PCW Tag Title around my waist that damned me to this watery grave, my clothes, even my abilities as a vampire, ANYTHING to be released from this place and float up to the surface, empty the water from my dead lungs and find the suicidal morons who did this to me. That’s hunger for revenge. Some people say I have a temper on me… I’m always a little edgey, they say. What do you expect from someone who used to be able to transform into a monster when they got slightly annoyed? And the thing I hate most about down here…. it’s the boredom. I’ve been curled up in an almost fetal position for… well, how do you measure time when you can’t even see the sun? I’m immersed in total darkness. Thinking to myself. Occasionally seeing a tiny crack of light, but not enough to tell me if it’s been a day or a week. Sleep is something I can never do, because this car keeps moving. Banging on rocks, hitting things, flipping around. All I can hear all around is the permanent sound of water, broken up by a few loud THUMP noises. I guess I’ll end up truly dying here. But who could ask for more than a release from this hell? Just a few hours in this is enough to make a person long for their death. once I run out of blood entirely, my body will rot completely. I’ll be little more than a skeleton, by which time the metal will have rusted and the fish will strip the flesh from these bones and the smaller animals will have taken my brain to pieces. I’ll be nothing. But there’s one hope that I have, one dream, one little fantasy I can hold onto even long after I finally am no more… when they… or rather, if they pull this steel casket from the water and open the trunk to find a pile of bones, they’ll see one thing around the bottom of his spinal column… the Primetime Championship Wrestling Tag Team Championship belt. I can promise myself one other thing. If I make it out of here intact, with my memory and body still working properly… … Everyone will suffer.
