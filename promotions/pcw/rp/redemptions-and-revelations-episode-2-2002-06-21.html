Redemptions and Revelations: Episode 2

21 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 21 (Excerpted from the memoirs of Bruce ‘Violence Jack’ Shanahan) Hiro Saotome has nothing on me. Truly, what is there to be scared of in a reject like this chump? He’s a blight, a festering tumor on the otherwise healthy body of Primetime Championship Wrestling. Several weeks ago he had the nerve to show his face in the main event of Fusion (or was it Turmoil? Bah, does it matter?….) with a paramount fanfare as part of the ‘wonderful’ new blood merging into the roster. In spite of that elaborate charade, he has failed to live up to the publicity surrounding his arrival. He’s an abject disease to all I hold true, and tonight I terminate that cancerous growth and cleanse the wrestling business of yet another overhyped wannabe. I’m sick of having to fight with veritable tooth-and-nail just for a shred of recognition while lazy, unethical propaganda clowns are paraded around as future cornerstones. In all fact, I’ve garnered myself some rather favorable opportunities in the near future. Hell, not only do I have the honor of annihilating that punk-ass bush leaguer this evening (and for all who disagree, I’ll book them a ringside seat personally and hope some of the blood splatters on them) but the upcoming Pay Per View finds me as a contender in the Extreme championship match-up. I’m putting Wild Wing, Lovecraft, and whatever other second-rate ‘hardcore’ talent this company can dish out on notice after I make an example of Saotome. And then whatever pain I inflict on his decrepit carcass I will extend sevenfold at Gold and Glory. June 21st’s edition of Fusion breeds more than just the simple prospect of disposing of the Japanese import trash and making my intentions known for down the road. As has been the case for months, I enter into the possibility of furthering my own retribution. Defeat at the hands of Jason Snow was a difficult pill to swallow, regardless of his accolades, and with the departure of Big Daveylicious also signaled the disappearance of Derek Shanahan. Following that horrid vision of my kin tied to that dangling cross above the boiling mass of fetid tissue and nuclear chaos called Azathoth, I awoke to find his whereabouts unknown. I’ve plunged into a rigorous search and my pursuit has even carried me into the Dreamlands several times. Thus far, no luck. All leads have proved unreliable. Can Saotome and this match-up at the Pay Per View help ring in a new beginning for the white trash street warrior? Seeing as my stay in the promotion has been lackluster at best (mostly of my own doing), I hope the demolishment of Hiro and whatever comes of the 30th changes the proverbial landscape for my PCW career. Mercifully, my duties alongside the Blackhelm Coalition have leveled out and our ‘missions’ have severely declined in light of the tragic excursion into nethermost Leng, where we pursued a set of fabled relics buried beneath the infernal tombs of Nyarlathotep himself. Our perilous, and now disastrous, expedition was planned under the impression their discovery would prompt even greater breakthroughs in Miskatonic and Blackhelm activities. Alas, thus was not so. I shudder to even evoke the memory of what happened only days ago….. * * * Bruce stared into the murky darkness before them, adjusting his eyes to the blackness as a plethora of iridescent shapes began to shuffle silently about. He cast his eyes momentarily to Stryker and then back again, the veteran officer catching wind of his distress and looking back ahead. A muffled curse escaped his mouth, followed by a series of frenzied orders. “Vanessa! Jago!” he shrieked as his rifle barrel cruised left to right. “Take left! Christian and Ryder, fan out right! Dammit!!….” A silent array of shapes cascaded from the trees above, landing amongst the confused battalion whom slipped about in a bewildered turmoil. As they sought to regain their bearings, the spectral shapes swept in from all sides, including amongst the befuddled Delta Fox, and a great thrashing resonated throughout the forest as a massive looming form came crashing through the undergrowth in a slaughter-induced fury. “What the f*ck?!…. The hulking Slade cast eyes despondently north as trees plunged to the ground in a torrent of raging fury the likes of Bruce had never seen. The shadowy fiends slithered to and fro within the confused ranks of Stryker’s Delta Fox unit as the soaring monstrosity arose on two bulky hind legs, hurling its front legs skyward as razor-edged talons glimmered in the night and coiled tusks silhouetted like spear-headed snakes against the unnatural night sky. Stryker and his compatriots took up positions behind trees as the giant thrashed its colossal mass against its battered surroundings and a throng of horrid tentacles flayed like whips from its shoulders. The nightgaunts continued to strike disorder throughout Delta Fox, though they seemed content to do no more then perplex and disorganize the terrestrial intruders. Marcus Stryker reared back with his carbine rifle and fought off a pair of the shadow fiends. “Fire! Don’t let up until it hits the ground!” Shanahan, still fumbling with his weapon, abruptly hits the dirt as bullets whiz by his skull and the darkness-flooded forest is suddenly enveloped in flashing light. Norton dives to the ground beside Bruce with an ‘oomph’. “Sorry about this, Bruce,” breathes the war-hardened soldier. “Hell of an introduction for you into a real combat situation…..” His words were drowned out in the Boston native’s frenzied panic as he groped at his weapon, feebly trying to unjam it. Norton hurriedly snatched his comrade’s weapon, mended the mistake within the chamber, and tossed it back. At that point, more terrible crashing resonated several hundred yards off from both flanks and the initial gargantuan tore through the ranks of Delta Fox. * * * Father craven paced nervously about the room, garbed in a drab set of casual clothes as he watched the beeping monitors. A long row of beds lined the wall before him, all full and composed of Bruce Shanahan, Marcus Stryker, Stryker’s elite unit, and the dozen military volunteers recruited for the mission with Delta Fox. All were fast asleep and being monitored by Blackhelm technicians for irregular brainwaves and disturbing sleep patterns as they traversed the known dreamscapes of Kadath and Leng. The priest dropped his head and bit his lip until blood coursed as one monitor abruptly flatlined. “Corporal Hearse,” muttered one of the technicians as medical doctors rushed to his side, his body twitching unnaturally. Two more near the far wall did likewise to the chagrin of Craven. His face contorted in dismay. “Private Richards and Sergeant Brooks,” informed a different technician. “What is going on?!” demanded Craven. “What’s happening in that wretched dreamscape?!” His subordinates could only shake their heads. “No way of knowing,” declared the first with a powerless sigh. “Obviously they’ve hit a tough spot and something picked up on them. We can attempt to bring them from the trance, but the results could be catastrophic. Considering the effects that serum has on the brain, abrupt awakening could lead to a surge of hormones that would possibly damage their minds permanently, if not cause outright death.” Craven pivoted on his left foot and trounced angrily toward the wall. He hated decisions like this, realized either one would almost inevitably result in further losses to the team. What was the price he was willing to pay for those artifacts? If he took a risk and surmised they could handle the dilemma, it was possible they could find their bounty, and as such, the deposit of dream relics would correspond to their real-time location on Earth which could help their cause immensely. But if they were truly engaged with the forces of Leng, and that despicable Crawling Chaos Nyarlathotep himself, there would be no victory. Not even Delta Fox could defeat a god…. His decision was almost instantaneous as both sets of consequences dawned on him, two more life signals falling to zero simultaneously. “Bring them out of it,” he commanded the medical team. “It’s over….” * * * Bruce rolled to the side as another of the recruits collapsed at his side, one of the beast’s flailing tentacles lashing out and nearly severing his mangled body with a single swipe. It was a slaughterhouse. None of the actual Delta Fox members had fallen yet, but he knew at this rate it was only a matter of time. Two more of the looming quadpedals had burst from the recesses of the forest and the nightgaunts continued to rove about, sowing further confusion amongst them while causing no actual damage themselves. Sensaki lay quietly behind a fallen tree, clasping his sidearm to his chest as his gnarled weapon lay crushed in a slab of twisted metal beneath the fallen hardwood. Bruce crouched low and took his side, the Japanese clutching a fresh wound on his left bicep. “Damned monsters,” he snarls. “They knew….somehow they knew…..it was hopeless all along….” He lowered his eyes on the fierce-eyed Shanahan. “You were their pawn, Bruce. They used you. They used you to lure us into their trap.” The jaded street tough growled as another of the recruited soldiers fell with a horrified shriek as he was gored by the coiled tusks of another of the rampaging things. “There’s no way out of this one I fear, Bruce,” remarked Sensaki, struggling to display a reluctant grin. “I guess that Snow fellow won’t have the chance….” * * * Shanahan’s eyes shot open as wide as half-dollars, sitting bolt upright and gasping for breath as his allies did likewise. Doctors withdrew the numerous electrodes and wires from his body as he watched bags covering the bodies of those who had not been so fortunate. Instantly, his eyes found the distressed form of Father Craven in the corner. Shanahan’s lips parted into a sneer as he locked eyes with the teary-eyed Catholic priest. “How did they know?!” he roared. “How the f*ck did they know?!” “Forgive me, Bruce….” came the meek reply. “Forgive me…..”
