In walks LOVE

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today (The scene picks up with Kid Krazie walking around in the Randall’s Flagship grocery store parking lot. He doesn’t know who he is, where he is or what he is doing after taking that nasty spill. He looks like a confused child looking for his mother as he walks towards the back of the grocery store.) Krazie: “WOW!” (As Krazie walks around the corner and makes his way to the back of the grocery store he is amazed at what he sees. There is a dumpster area with used and broken shopping carts nearby, the loading dock where shipments are delivered, and emergency exits strategically positioned along the back. Krazie walks towards the dumpster area and as he gets there his eyes pop wide like a kid at Christmas morning.) Krazie: “WOW!” (Krazie sees some of the most remarkable things ever. As he makes his way to the open dumpster the saying: Another person’s garbage is another person’s treasure. He begins to gaze at the wonder people call trash. As he moves some trash to the side to get a better view of the things at the bottom he is shocked.) Krazie: “Gotcha!” (Krazie jumps into the dumpster digging to the bottom for something that caught his eye. He climbs out of the dumpster with some garbage hanging out of his khaki colored cargo shorts and some stains from the garbage on his sleeveless t-shirt.) Krazie: “WOW!” (Krazie pulls out the treasure he sought after and again his eye pop wide with glee.) Krazie: “What a slingshot!” (Kid Krazie stands with a rotten, used, stain covered off white thong in his hands. He tests it out but as he tries to fire it off his glee is erased.) Krazie: “This must be one of those newer models that require three people to work it! Oh well, I’ll save it for later!” (Krazie stuffs the nasty thing in his back pocket and turns around making his way away from the dumpster.) Voice: “Hey sexy!” (Krazie turns around and looks for the person who said that. He looks around at the dumpster area but doesn’t see anyone. In fact all he sees is the garbage filled dumpster, some broken down shopping carts, a lone rusty shopping cart off to the side away from all the others. Krazie just scratches his head and turns back around. But again as he makes his way from the dumpster he is stopped by a mysteriously sweet voice.) Voice: “You’re just going to ignore me are you?” (Krazie wheels around and again doesn’t see anyone standing. But he does notice that the lone shopping cart is not in the same position as before! This time the shopping cart is just three feet behind him.) Voice: “What? You never seen a talking shopping cart before?” (Krazie shakes his head side to side never taking his eyes off the cart. He stands there with fear in his eyes as the shopping cart rolls forward a bit.) Voice: “Why do people freak out when they see a shopping cart talk? I mean here ya’ll see talking frogs, talking turtles, and what’s the deal with these talking animals getting their own television shows?” Krazie: “… I… don’t... know…” (The shopping cart turns and begins roll like it’s pacing back and forth. Krazie just stands there watching in amazement. All of a sudden the shopping cart stops pacing and bolts forward towards Krazie causing the deranged man to jump back screaming out in shock like a little sissy girl.) Voice: “Oh how rude of me! I haven’t introduced myself! My name is Luna Luvedick. But all my friends call me Lu-Lu. (Krazie just nods his head sticking out his hand. He soon notices though that the shopping cart has no hand to shake his with so he retracts his hand and tries to play it off by scratching his head.) Lu-Lu: “Well? Aren’t you going to introduce yourself?” (Krazie shakes his head trying to clear his thoughts and then coughs to clear his throat.) Krazie: “My name… That’s a good question!” (Krazie scratches his head yet again. He then turns his upper body so he can check his tag on his underwear.) Krazie: “Well these say Joe Boxer… Hi! I’m Joe Boxer, you can call me Joe, Boxer, or Joe Boxer.” Lu-Lu: “It’s nice to make your acquaintance Joe.” (Krazie smiles and nods his head.) Lu-Lu: “Can we get out of here? Seeing all of my brothers and sisters sitting over there dead is kinda freaky!” (Krazie nods his head and turns around making his way out of the dumpster area and towards the way he came with Lu-Lu rolling along right beside him.) Lu-Lu: “So what do you do for a living Joe?” Krazie: “That’s a good question Lu-Lu.” Lu-Lu: “What do you mean? What you don’t know what you do for a living?” (Krazie shakes his head and then begins scratching his head again in confusion.) Krazie: “Nope! No earthly idea… Hold on let me see…” (Krazie looks for his wallet but notices that it is not in his back pocket. He then shrugs his shoulders and begins walking with Lu-Lu rolling along.) Lu-Lu: “So Joe, where do you live?” Krazie: “I don’t know…” Lu-Lu: “You don’t know much do you Joe?” Krazie: “I don’t know…” (As Krazie and Lu-Lu make there way across the parking lot of Randall’s they are stopped as an old obese woman comes waddling her fat ass towards them. Lu-Lu stops moving as the woman approaches causing Krazie to stop and look at the cart in confusion.) Lu-Lu: (whispering) “Don’t let anyone know I can talk! They’ll try to take me and make some money off it!” (Krazie nods his head as the old fat woman comes to a halt just a few feet away. She doubles over trying to catch her breath. Krazie looks on in confusion as the woman just stands there with raspy breathing.) Woman: “I… think…. you… drop… ped… this…” (The woman pulls out a brown leather wallet and hands it to Krazie who looks at it with much confusion. He smiles and nods his appreciation to the old fifty year old woman. But as he turns around to walk away the old woman who grabs him by the elbow stops him.) Woman: “So… what are you doing later tonight?” (Krazie shrugs his shoulders.) Krazie: “I don’t know…” Woman: “Well, here take this with you…” (The woman hands Krazie her phone number and winks at him as he turns around and walks away pushing Lu-Lu in front of him.) Krazie: “Lu-Lu?” (As they walk a little further Lu-Lu speaks up and begins to roll along with out the help of Krazie.) Lu-Lu: “Dang she was FAT!” Krazie: “Big women need luv’n too!” (Krazie walks on and as he finishes that statement Lu-Lu just stops in her tracks. Krazie notices he is walking alone a few steps ahead and turns around to see Lu-Lu just sitting there.) Krazie: “What?” Lu-Lu: “ ‘Big women need luv’n too?’ What’s this?” (Krazie shrugs his shoulders and continues walking along with Lu-Lu a few rolling feet behind. The scene fades as the two continue to walk.) *****Part Two****** (The scene re-opens at a local neighborhood park just five minutes from Krazie’s home that he doesn’t remember. Krazie is sitting in Lu-Lu with his legs hanging off to the side dangling in midair. The two are enjoying the wonderful afternoon as kids play football, girls swinging on the swing set, and little toddlers playing in the sand.) Lu-Lu: “So Joe…” Krazie: “Yeah Lu?” (Out of nowhere a football flies and rebounds off the back of Krazie’s head. A kid comes running over to retrieve the clumsy throw. He is wearing a PCW Rent-A-Hero t-shirt along with some blue jeans that contains a white handkerchief and some black nike shoes that are not tied.) Kid: “Hey you! Can you give me my ball back?” (Krazie looks to the ball on the ground near the basket Lu-Lu and then back up at the kid. He shrugs his shoulders as he gets out of the basket and picks up the football. He tosses it underhand to the awaiting hands of the nine-year-old kid. He nods his appreciation and then turns around to join up with his friends who are all waiting impatiently.) Krazie: “…. Wait…” (Krazie stands there with a mix of determination and a bit of confusion displayed on his face. He turns around and climbs back into the Lu-Lu and continues to think to himself.) Lu-Lu: “So Joe? What’s going on?” Krazie: “Sssssuuuussssshhhhh!” (Krazie puts his index finger to his pursed lips as a light bulb is switched on above his head. He jumps out of the basket Lu-Lu and raises his arm with his index finger pointing to the sky. He has a grin ear to ear as he catches a lot of the kids attention.) Krazie: “I’m Kid Krazie from Primetime Championship Wrestling!!!” (Krazie stands there for a few seconds as the kids all turn back to whatever they were doing before completely ignoring Kid Krazie and his basket.) Lu-Lu: “So your name isn’t Joe?” Krazie: “I have a match this Sunday against Stryfe!” (Krazie changes his pose as he now stands there with his hand on his hips with his muscles flexing. The smile is replaced by a scowl as he stands there beginning to growl. Again kids look over in his direction but after seeing it’s just the same lunatic they turn back around.) Lu-Lu: “So you are a wrassler?” (Krazie puts his arms down, stops growling and takes the scowl off his face. He climbs back into the basket Lu-Lu and dangles his feet off to the side like before.) Krazie: “Yep…” Lu-Lu: “So? Stryfe right? You got a plan?” (Krazie’s face drops as reality slaps him in the face. He may have remembered what his name is and what his occupation is but he has no clue as to how to wrestle!) Krazie: “No…” Lu-Lu: “Well then I guess it’s a good thing that we met!” (Krazie looks down at the shopping cart he is sitting in.) Krazie: “Why is that!?” Lu-Lu: “You need a manager and here I am!” (So it looks like Krazie and Luna Luvedick have formed a friendship, but have also decided to have a work relationship as Lu-Lu takes charge and Krazie follows her every order! Tune in next time as Krazie goes to the gym with a SHOPPING CART!)
