Tears... And Blood... Of A Clown

9 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/09/02 “Are you ready to meet Ronald, kiddies?” “YEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!” Our shot opens up inside a McDonald’s, the camera showing a section that is roped off and labelled Private Party. Seated are a group of young kids with a few parents dotted around, while before them stands an overly enthusiastic McD’s employee. “Well... HERE HE COMES!” From behind a door marked Staff Only walks the jovial face of McDonald’s... Ronald McDonald himself.. Dressed in his usual attire of Red, White and Yellow with smiley painted face and big, bright Red wig. He starts to hand out balloons with the corporate M logo on to the children. “YAAAAAAAAAYYYYYYYYYYYY!!!!!” The party children all cheer, wearing their party hats and broad smiles as the remnants of their Happy Meals lay scattered amongst the tables. A balloon is pulled from the Clown’s pocket and in a very animated fashion, Ronald blows it up and begins work on a balloon animal before something interrupts him. *UMMMMPPPPPPPPPHHHHHHHH* A clenched fist, decorated with a vicious knuckle-duster connects with the Clown’s chin, breaking the skin open and sending the brightly dressed figure dropping to the floor to screams from the children and their parents. “NOOOOOOOOO!!!! MUMMY... HE’S KILLING HIM!!!!” 2 men dressed in dark Grey suits set about the misfortunate Clown, one holding his head up by the big Red wig while the other sends the loaded fist into the clowns now bloody face... His eyes closed and his lip scarlet. With the Clown suitably beaten, the 2 men rise back up and dust their hands off, looking down at the clown with glee as the children sob. “Heeeeeeeee killlllllled Ronaaaaaaaaaaaald!!!” Hearing the sobbing, the 2 men turn to the kids and shrug. “Just doing our job...” They nod to each other and hop over the Private Party sign, the man who threw the punches dropping the knuckle-duster into his pocket as he starts to whistle “Always Look On The Bright Side Of Life”. “Heeeeeeeeeeee’s deeeeaaaaaaaaaaaaad!!!” The children all flock around the unconscious Clown, his face already swollen and bruised from the punches to his face while blood trickles from his mouth and the shot fades to Black. “I fucking hate Mime...” With the muttering of a passer by, our shot re-opens on a city street where a thin, waif-like man is standing on the sidewalk in a Black and White striped outfit and a White face, a small Black tear under his right eye. “.................” He holds his hands out, palms parallel to him and pretends he is in a box, pressing his hands against an invisible wall in an effort to get out. “........” By his feet is a Black beret filled with change which the odd passer-by, entertained by his act, sends coins crashing into, maybe to a thumbs up by the Mime artist. “.............” He crouches, looking all around him puzzled before looking up and right ahead.. His left arm raising to pull on an imaginary rope.. His right one follows and with a climbing motion from his upper body and returns to a standing position to a mild applause from those watching. “................” Kneeling down, he pretends to take something from a case then springs back up and mimics bouncing a ball on the concrete before Miming a tennis serve, and all the following shots with his imaginary racket before he misses one and pretends to throw his racket down in frustration to a few laughs from his audience. “........ ARGHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!” From out of nowhere a Lead pipe swings towards him, the Mime artists just managing to block it with his forearm to the sickening crunch of bone breaking. *AAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!* Those watching the street theatre gasp in shock as a man in a dark Grey suit, similar to those warn by the 2 men in McDonald’s, continues to bludgeon the hapless Mime with the lead pipe, each shot drawing a yelp from the beaten man and a disgusting crack of bone buckling. After about 15 hits, the man tucks the pipe inside his belt and looks down at the convulsing Mime artist who is rolling into the foetal position. “Sorry mate, just doing my job!” With a final gesture he throws the pipe down onto the Mime’s skull before walking off and removing his Black gloves... Behind him, a crowd rushing to help the broken man is shown as our shot fades to Black. “I just look to entertain.. But at the same time test my own limits as a human being...” With the dry monotone voice of TV Illusionist David Blaine, we fade into a sunny Los Angeles back street where the understated star is seemingly giving an interview. “I mean, something as simple as a card trick can entertain people just as much as me encasing myself in a block of ice.... Here, pick a card.” The illusionist fans out a deck of cards and holds them out to the interviewer, who’s hand protrudes into the shot to pull one out. “Now, show it to the camera.” The hand turns over and holds the Jack of Hearts up to the camera. “Here’s 2 slices of Salami and a Pickle... Make a sandwich with the card and eat it.” The pair of hands at the bottom of the picture bend the card in 2 and take the deli items from Blaine, tucking them inside the card and pulling the makeshift food item off camera where munching sounds are heard. “Now, I can tell you that your card wa-” *SREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEECCCCCCHHHHHHHH!!!* Just as the Illusionist is about to reveal the card, he is send flying up into the air by a dark Grey car ramming right through his legs. “AAAAAAAARRRRRGGGGGGGHHHHHH!!!!!!“ He crashes down onto the sidewalk in a broken heap, holding both legs that dangle limp... His expressionless eyes then suddenly widen and light up as a White light from the car is reflected in them. “NOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!” Desperately trying to levitate, Blaine struggles and struggles but can’t get out of the way as the car reverses back over his legs to another scream from the TV star before it screeches off and the interviewer runs over to Blaine. “Were you going to say Jack of Hearts.... David.. Were you?” He slaps the stubbled face of the Illusionist as the shot fades to Black on the continued attempt to revive David Blaine. “Another well devised plan well executed...” With an all too familiar voice, the picture fades in from Black on the grinning, thinly bearded face of PCW CEO Xavier Kannon as he watches 3 monitors before him that show a different 1st person image on each. The left showing a fist slamming into Ronald McDonald. The middle showing a hand gripping a Lead pipe crashing down onto a figure in Black and White. The right showing the view through a windshield as the vehicle ploughs into a human being. Xavier : “Clowns..... Mimes.... Illusionists...” The Scientologist’s bright Green eyes don’t even blink as they watch the footage continue, his only action being to run his hand through his long, Strawberry Blonde hair. Xavier : “Only the Jester, the Vampire, the Freak and the Goggle Wearing Moron to go.” The only light in the darkened room comes from the 3 monitors that reflect of Kannon’s face.. Then, satisfied with what he has seen, the PCW Champion flicks them off one by one... Each one darkening the room until the right hand’s button being pressed plunges us into Black.
