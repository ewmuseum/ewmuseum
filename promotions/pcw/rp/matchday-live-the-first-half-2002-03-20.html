Matchday Live: The First Half

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/3/02 Football, its a funny old game. Twenty two men, two nets, two sets of white posts, a ball and a grass pitch are the elements that make up the sport that is watched avidly by millions of supporters worldwide. So why are these eleven wrestlers playing football against a team of pros? It started with football players, pundits and fans making snide remarks about professional wrestlings legitimacy as a professional sport. These remarks were taken personally by Ben J. Pailthorpe and he set out to do something. He contacted the man who he knew could help him, Kid Wonder. After initial diffculties in explaining who he was Ben managed to explain what he wanted to do and why he wanted to do it. Kid Wonder jumped on the bandwagon and became the team coach of the team made up of PTC stars, Team PTC! Uknown to Kid Wonder and the rest of team PTC Ben had put a wager on the game, $500,000 was the princely sum placed on the outcome of the game, should Team PTC lose the match then Ben will have ALOT of explaining to do, if he wants to avoid broken knees. The first Team PTC training session took place with a minimal turn out. Aimz, Robert Renfrew, Fazz McLovely and Mr. UK turned up in support of Team PTC, five players when you include Ben. Ben took the first training session and noted the skills of a certain individual named Aimz, once the snickering about her being female ended she showed that she had talent on the football pitch. When Ben was feeling a little disheartened he decided to silently send up a prayer, for more players he prayed, for more time. His prayer was answered by an unlikely bunch, DarkStar, Jayson Frost, Shawn Ryan, Chris Chase, Jay Sylo, Kay-Nyne and The End! They now had eleven players and a blind referee. Juan Borro came to Ben's aid in the coaching area, the former professional footballer helped Ben to choose his players and tactics. Then Ben and Kid Wonder chose their team, eleven wrestlers now become eleven footballers, the pride of professional wrestling on their shoulders and in Ben's case $500,000. Then Ben took the members of Team PTC out for a Last Supper, of sorts. The waiter turned out to be Kirk Morris who was a traitor to Team PTC and he tried to poison Kid Wonder with a radioactive Budweiser. Now we are here, the day of truth, MATCHDAY! 'The 80,000 capacity Silverdome in Michigan is playing host to the biggest match in footballing history. Fans from both sides of professional sports wrestling and football will converge on Pontiac for the biggest match ever.' The television is switched off and the camera swings round to see Ben J. Pailthorpe sitting alone in a white tiled changing room. All around him hanging on the walls are eleven football shirts with individual players names on them. Ben puts the television remote down and buries his head in his hands. He lets out a deep sigh. Ben J. Pailthorpe: What...what if we lose? At that moment in walks the other ten emmbers of Team PTC and mabager Kid Wonder. Kid Wonder: Coem on people, sit down shut up its time to talk. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Er...yeah. The team assembles, each member sits infront of his or her shirt. Kid Wonder: Right, today is the day we have been preparing for, its the big match. You have had no alcohol, you have had no bad food, you are all finely conditioned and tuned into this match and... The door opens. Boy: Pizza for Mr Wonder. Kid Wonder: Ahem...could you guys wait a second. Kid Wonder walks over to the Pizza Boy and pays him, while Ben takes over the talk. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Right, we have all worked hard for this, we all have other things to think about, e all have our own careers but today all are differences are put aside, we unite under the banner of professional wrestling! Kid Wonder appears back with a slice of pizza in his hand. Kid Wonder: Yeah, to the victor goes the spoils, to the loser goes defeat. Are we gonna be the losers? Team PTC: NO! Kid Wonder: Are we gonna be defeated? Team PTC: NO! Kid Wonder: Is this good pizza or what? Team PTC: OR WHAT! Ben J. Pailthorpe: Yeah, thats enough of the pep talk. Kid Wonder: Right, out onto the pitch for warm up! Kid Wonder picks up his pizza box and walks out with it, he is followed by ten other members of Team PTC. Ben stands there for a minute before sitting down and bowing his head. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Lord, I thank you for what you have done for me, I thank you for everything that has happened to me. Lord, I pray that today you will be with each and every member of this team, whether they acknowledge you or not let them feel your presence, guide them to victory. Amen. Ben slowly gets to his feet and walks out of the changing room. When he gets outside the door he bumps into a police officer. Officer: Mr. Pailthorpe? Ben J. Pailthorpe: Yes. Officer: I have some bad news. [Scene Ends] Kid Wonder: Run....Jump...Stop Team PTC are being ordered about the pitch by manager Kid Wonder. Ben J. Pailthorpe makes his way slowly out of the tunnel, he calls the team to him. Ben J. Pailthorpe: I just spoke with a police officer, I have some bad news. The team are subdued. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Fazz McLovely was in an accident, he passed away in the early hours this morning (I have permission.). There is a stunned silence. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Fazz may not have been the smartest, or the most sensible, but he was loyal and a good friend, the match will be played in his honour and we WILL win in his honour! The team clap quietly before returning to training all a little subdued by the news they have recieved. [Scene Ends] The stadium slowly fills, one half wrestling fans, with signs and hug foam fingers one half football fans with flasks and sandwhiches. Let battle commence. The theme music from the European Champions League plays and from the tunnel emerge the Allstar team of the worlds best soccer players. P.A Announcer: Lets here it for the Allstar Team, manager Alex Ferguson, Peter Schmeichel, Roberto Carlos, Gary Neville, Palo Maldini, Tony Adams, David Beckham, Robert Pires, Zinedine Zidane, Patrick Vieira, Ronaldo and representing Porto...JUAN BORRO. The wrestling world is stunned, PCW Superstar Juan Borro is a traitor? He emerges from the tunnel smiling smugly wearing a number eleven shirt. You sold out chants ring from the stands occupied by wresting fans. Duke Williams: And now, representing Professional wrestling and Primetime Central, TEAM PTC! Manager Kid Wonder, Goalkeeper: Robert Renfrew, Scotland, GWF. Right Back: Ben J. Pailthorpe, England, PCW. Center Back: DarkStar, USA, PCW. Center Back: Frost, USA, NTICW. Left Back: Kay-Nyne, USA, DRWF. Right Midfield: Andy Murray, Scotland, GWF. Center Midfield: Jay Sylo, USA, PCW. Center Midfield: Chris Chase, UK/Canada, PCW. Left Midfield: Shawn Ryan, USA, NTICW. Striker: Mr. UK, England, Unknown. Striker: Aimz, USA, NTICW. There is a huge cheer from the wrestling fans as the team appear from the tunnel. They are wearing an all black kit with red trim, the PTC logo on their badge. Ben J. Pailthorpe and Patrick Vieira stand face to chin in the center circle. The End who is refereeing flips a coin and Team PTC choose to kick off. Duke Willaims: Folks, welcome to the Pontiac Silverdome for todays match, im Duke Williams and im here alone to commentate on this huge event! Mr. UK and Aimz start off and they pass the ball back to Andy Murray, he tries to run forward but he is dispossesed by Patrick Veira who plays it through to Juan Borro. Duke Williams: That treacherous son of a bitch better watch himself. Juan is scythed down by a tackle from Jay Sylo and The End has no choice but to award a free kick. Juan steps up and floats over a cross, Zidane jumps u and headers the ball but Raboert Renfrew makes a comfortable save. Duke Williams: Close one there. Renfrew throws the ball out to DarkStar, he in turn plays it to Kay-Nyne who punts it upfield, Chris Chase takes control and skips past Zidane and Vieira, he dummies past Adams and cracks a shot at goal... Duke Williams: Chase with a good shot...oh, just over. Chase spins around and looks disapointed. Peter Schmeichel restarts the match and Roberto Carlos plays a cross field ball to Robert Pires. Pires tries to skip past Sylo but he is tackled cleanly, Sylo gives it to Pailthorpe who plays a through ball to Aimz, she starts running towards goal but she is badly hacked down by Vieira. Duke Williams: FOUL! Who does he think he is. Ben J. Pailthorpe runs up to Vieira who is much taller then him and he grabs his head and hits the Morning View! Duke Williams: Oh My! Morning View! This is degenerating into a streetfight! The All Star team's players crowd around the ref. Juan Borro: DIDNT YOU SEE THAT?!?! The End: No, im blind. Free kick to Team PTC. Chris Chase places the ball down and looks up, he runs at the ball and unleashes a cracker of a shot, it soars higher and high its heading straight for the... Duke Williams: TOP CORNER! TOP CORNER! The net bulges as the ball soars past Peter Schmeichel's flailing arms. Duke Williams: 1-0!!! 1-0!!! The wrestling fans erupt and the team celebrates on the pitch. Duke Williams: Twenty minutes into the first half and Chris Chase scores for team PTC! P.A Announcer: The first goal, scored for Team PTC by CHRIS CHAAASSSEEEE! Duke Williams: TAKE THAT SOCCER! The team and the fans slowly calm down and head back to their own half. The All Star team kick off and go on with a new prupose. They surge forward and launch attacks on the PTC goal. Duke Williams: Come on Team PTC! PTC hold out untill The End blows the half time whistle. Duke Williams: At half time its 1-0 to team PTC! [Scene Ends] Alex Ferguson: What the f*censored*k was that? The Allstar Team locker room is silent, the eleven players have their heads bowed. Alex Ferguson: They are a bunch of f*censored*king wrestlers. This is YOUR sport. Juan Borro stands up. Juan Borro: They are playing a simple 442 formation, anything more then that would confuse them. Alex Ferguson: In the second half...DESTROY THEM! [Scene Ends] Ben J. Pailthorpe: Well done. The Team PTC changing room is jubilant. Kid Wonder: You did PTC proud in the first half, now more of the same in the second half. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Chris, you did well, carry on like that. Aimz watch out for them fouling you, they are gonna try it again. Kid Wonder: Remember whats at stake here. [Scene Ends] Ben is walking alone through the halls of the stadium when he is approached by a man in a suit. Man: I am the CEO of Fifpro, I have an offer for you. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Huh? Man: If you lose your going to have to pay us $500,000. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Yeah... Man: Howabout we change it a bit, if you lose we pay you $500,000. Ben J. Pailthorpe: A bribe? Man: No, no, an incentive. Ben thinks for a moment. Man: Think what you could use the money for. There is an awkward moment. Ben J. Pailthorpe: No, I will not sell out wrestling, I will not sell out my friends. Man: Then so be it, I look forward to taking your money. The scene ends as the man walks briskly away from Ben.
