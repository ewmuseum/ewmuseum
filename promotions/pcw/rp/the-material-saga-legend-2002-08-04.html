The Material Saga: Legend.

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 4, 2002 DarkStar nursed his wounds as he sat on the deck of the Wrestle Vessel, the PPV was over and he, along with the rest of the Dark Age, had come out on top. Just like it was predicted. They were unstoppable, BAS couldnt do it, Kannon cant do it, and... obviously Tom Lexian cant do it. The sun was setting, in a couple of minutes they would be back in the US after the many battles that occured on the god forsaken boat in the middle of the ocean. The clear cerulean water in all its beauty was home for the last few days, it would be nice to be back on real solid ground again. Things were different now, obviously, different then they were when DarkStar came to this boat. He set foot as the bad guy, what everyone stood against, he caught alot of cheers for some reason that he could only attribute to his own personal greatness, but he didnt revel in it. Now he was a Dark Knight in charge of keeping PCW's throne. Legend Inc ran away from PCW with his tail between his legs, he ran because of a conspiracy theory, DarkStar scoffed. He knew the real reason why Legend Inc left PCW. It was because he knew he would NEVER be able to beat DarkStar. Legend Inc, the supposed savior of PCW who was to keep the darkness out and rain light in, he was supposed to carry PCW over the rivals two fold. He was given every perk he needed and every oppurtunity that existed, he had gold and push pumping through his veins, everything he possibly needed to go over DarkStar and become the most successful wrestler in the fed. What does he do? Chickens out. He couldn't beat DarkStar. Never would. So he ran. I dont even think he cleaned out his locker. DarkStar snarled, it was loud and abrasive, the port was just past the rising sun. Talena walked out, she stretched like a cat, extending her perfect body towards the air. DarkStar glanced over and pushed his face back into its non-angry state. He gave a pat to the empty spot next to him, Talena walked over and sat down. 'What?' DarkStar just smiled and kissed her on the cheek, his lipstick lingered longer then his mouth did, she giggled. 'You are in a good mood today.' He nodded and looked back out to the rapidly approaching shoreline. The sun hung in the air painting the sky elaborate colors, they streaked across like they were getting away from something. 'Its not that I'm in a good mood. It's just that I realized something.' He stood up and walked forward, leaning out and looking down on the water below him, it broke and chopped and the waves licked the bottom of the huge ship. 'What did you realize?' He had a real thought, he really did, something important and exciting. Some sort of great last line, he forgot it though. 'I realized that I have no clue what they are going to do with this boat after this, I mean... It was just for the PPV...' he scratched his blue dyed head, 'Kind of seems like a waste of money to me.' Talena just looked at him funny. He tried not to acknowledge her. He could picture them unloading his Porsche from the cargo hold, it was going to be great to be home, especially since he wa-... Thats it! 'I remember now!' He seemed way to elated, it was early though, and he was hungover, so what do you expect. 'This is a new day...' He gestured towards the coast as the boat slowed to the dock, 'This is what a thrive for, existing on this plane and realizing that I...' He quickly glanced over towards the humanity that toiled and worked everywhere. 'That I... am better then everyone here, everyone in PCW, everyone in OSW, I am beyond them, I am a champion, the Lord of Dreams and the Prince of Nightmares, and most of all... a Legend' Talena grinned and stood, she held onto him as the camera panned away and out of sight.
