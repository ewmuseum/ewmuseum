Fo shizzin it and nizzin it on the black side

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17-11-02 Grimgor's life will be on the line. If he stuffs up he's a dead dwarf but if he succeeds he will have earned Bob's respect. Grimgor has spent the night studying movies that are based on black people such as Friday. Grimgor is fairly confident in his ability to act as a black man and infiltrate the gang of black men who are taking over Amber Hill. Grimgor sits in the car waiting for Bob to get in and drive him to the black side of town. Grimgor is looking younger without his thick orange beard. He looks quite ridiculous with his black gangster attire on. Bob walks up to the car and opens the door coolly before sitting down on the seat and looking over at his dwarven wrestling manager. 'You ready for this?' Grimgor grunts and turns his head to look out the window and away from Bob. He has never felt so ridiculous without his beard and now wearing all these stupid clothes. 'You'll be right.' Bob puts the keys in the ignition and starts up the car before reversing out of the driveway and driving off. 'Shouldn't we get some KFC first.... All black men eat KFC.' Grimgor has obviously done his research. 'Put your hat on.' Grimgor picks up the Nike hat and places it sideways on his head. He is not used to head wear like this in LSD land he used to wear metal helmets. Bob pulls the car up on the street where the black men are known to live and 'chill'. Grimgor reluctantly steps out of the black Mercedes and looks at the black men he can see in the distance. Grimgor takes a deep breath in then prepares himself for the mission ahead. Grimgor begins walking toward the black men at a fast pace so his San Diego baseball jersey stretches out behind him. Grimgor approaches the black men who all look down at him in a weird fashion. As he gets closer to the men he realizes how big and scary these men really are but he doesn't head back. Grimgor walks up to the black men who are sitting on the steps of a house and turns around to face the black men from the footpath. 'Fro Shizzin it nizzin it on the west side.' Grimgor sounds stupid saying these words in his strong dwarven accent which is like a mixture between a Scottish and an Australian accent. The black men look down at the little dwarf as if insulted. 'Yo Fo don't be so moe on this doe.... Ima doin it and suin it like you a tottin it.' One of the black men gets up and walks out onto the footpath. He stares down at the dwarf with flared eyes. 'Who the fuck are you?' 'I'm a F-J and I'm new in town dawg.... I was looking to join your gang here in Amber Hill.' Grimgor realizes he forgot to act like a black man and gets scarred. 'But you a white boy.' 'Na Nigga I'm a nigga through and through.... Just Albino that's all.' The black man smiles and extends his arm in a gesture to shake. Grimgor knows what this is from the movies he watched last night. Grimgor does that special shake of the black mans hand before pulling him in and staring him in the eye. 'You can hang out with us dawg.' Grimgor has been accepted and is quite pleased by this. 'Come on up here and sit on these steps.' The biggest of the four black men gestures for Grimgor to sit beside him. Grimgor moves up the stairs and takes a seat beside him. 'So wassup man?' 'Nummuch Brodah.... Just chillin on the west side willin dillin to stop frillin my sillin.' The other black men nod in approval to whatever Grimgor just said. 'So true brother.' The large black man smiles at Grimgor. 'So we ganna finish off these guinnie fucks?' The men are now talking to each other which is a big relief to Grimgor. 'Yeah.... Those fucking bastards are as good as dead anyway.' The man who approached Grimgor at first appears to be the leader of the gang. The leader turns around to F-J 'Yo nigger.... Sorry I forgot my manners I am G-Dawg.' Grimgor nods at G-Dawg and then looks at the other black men who are waiting to introduce themselves. 'I'm D-Dangerouse.... Ya have a beef with anyone you see me aight?' The big man extends his arm and Grimgor gives him the special handshake. 'I'm Rahor pleased to meet you F-J.' Grimgor nods at the lanky man with a black goatee. 'I'm Q-T.' The other relatively plain black man holds his fist out and Grimgor bumps his against it. 'What we're talking about is these fat fucking talians think they own dis place and we own dis place.' G-Dawg pulls a gun out of his pocket without even looking around to see if there is any police. 'We're ganna finish them off tonight.' 'Yeah cool like a clock on the docks with a spot of a parking lot rottin away at the apple core man.' The other men laugh at what F-J is saying and it become apparent that most black men make up what they say and pretend to know what others are talking about. 'Well.... I gotta skip it nigga's I'll see ya's all tonight.' Grimgor gets up from his spot on the steps and walks off. 'Later Nigger,' the other men say simultaneously.
