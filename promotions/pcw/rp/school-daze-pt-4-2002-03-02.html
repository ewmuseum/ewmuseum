Date: 02.03.02 'In consequence of the views now adopted by most naturalists, and which will ultimately, as in every other case, be followed by others who are not scientific, I have been led to put together my notes, so as to see how far the general conclusions arrived at in my former works were applicable to man. This seemed all the more desirable, as I had never deliberately applied these views to a species taken singly. When we confine our attention to any one form, we are deprived of the weighty arguments derived from the nature of the affinities which connect together whole groups of organisms- their geographical distribution in past and present times, and their geological succession. The homological structure, embryological development, and rudimentary organs of a species remain to be considered, whether it be man or any other animal, to which our attention may be directed; but these great classes of facts afford, as it appears to me, ample and conclusive evidence in favour of the principle of gradual evolution. The strong support derived from the other arguments should, however, always be kept before the mind - Charles Darwin CXI: Wooing Ms. Falan ( continued) It was like walking on air the next fall day, as the swaying leaves passed my face with an omission of falling to the ground. Outside the sun hadn't come up yet and I was stuck inside of my bed, with nothing except white boxers and a undershirt. As my eyes opened I could only see the dank light from outside and walking to my cabinet pulling out a bowl and a box of Lucky Charms. Sleep was good last night, although it took no time to actually go to sleep. Picking up a spoon, I dived right into the Lucky Charms stuffing my face with them as I sat down watching television. Soon I got bored from it all, and placed on a pair of blue shorts, a black Metallica T-shirt, and a pair of the unreleased Michael Jordan Space Jam basketball shoes to match. In the past I even heard of Jeremy Howard at some point or another back when he was wrestling for the IWA back then. This was 1998 and soon he would start getting into the mess that very well revolves around our controversy that boils over Sunday at March Madness. I shook my head and placed on a classic Yankee's ball cap and walked out of my dorm, the bowl of cereal replenished through my stomach. Flyers were posted around school about father-son trips off campus, but I know and the people of today know that I lost him and well that's a story I even did a book upon. For now though I ripped the paper off the the trunk of a tree it was placed on and started to read it for myself seeing people peach about Marxism in the United States in front of the library. It what makes wars start-propaganda, including title belts. I found myself directly inside of the library looking up books and trying to relate things for a history project our professor wanted us to do. Something about the voyage and the reason why we shouldn't have participated in Vietnam. Sending our African American brothers to the front lines just to die for a country that hates them. I remember Muhammad Ali words on Vietnam before he went to jail for draft dodging 'No Vietcong ever called me a nigger.' Somehow, she slipped out of the guard dog eye of Gavin and the rest of his clan, looking at The Red Baron for literary taste. I crept up on her with the simplest of motives - trying to get to know her better. Her eyes moved back and forth from the words spawned onto the book and I continued to look at them with the kindest of heart. She tried leaning backwards in her chair, her hands swinging to catch her balance before I stopped her. She tried catching her breath before speaking, 'You...saved my life.' I nodded my head as if I were some deaf mute trying to make sense of everything around me. Her screams caught the attention of everyone inside of the room, including myself. 'Saved? Not really, just her in the nick of time.'I told her, with a smile on my face trying to atleast be friendly with her. 'Well still, why did you save me? I mean you may be a little big shot and all but why me?' she asked me. I couldn't tell why I should or shouldn't but it was just under my skin itching to come out the words of the truth. 'No reason, just trying to get your attention without those block head goons around you and all. Are they your older brothers or something because they look overprotective and some X-Files shit to go along with it.' I told her, she laughed at the last comment and I smiled, I love making people laugh. 'Yeah, that's Gavin for you trying to be the hero all of the time when he needs to just limit himself to just one thing at a time. He'll burn out like that.' she retorted. I took a book off of the shelf and placed it down on the table. I first met her and Gavin inside of the same class, History so yeah I knew she was doing the project as well. 'Forrest Gump?' she asked me about the estrange book that has turned into Tom Hank's original Oscar heat. 'Yeah, I mean you know the guy went through some of the oddest mess in history since 1946. He went through-' 'JFK, LBJ, Watergate, Vietnam, Ford, Regan, Nixon and they were all seen out of the eyes by a man with the IQ of 75. And then he marries-' 'This chick named Jenny whose father touched her and kissed on her( rapist) and she got to live with her grandmother and just dogged Forrest out when she-' 'Left 4 times during every decade he had seen. Then she got AIDS and died leaving Forrest to-' 'Raise little Forrest by himself.' We looked at each other in amazement as if we had seen the movie countless times before and analyzed it for everything it would be counted as. I had gotten her respect, and she had gotten mine. 'Woah.' I told her with the slightest of things before everything Soon I would take her off of Gavin's hands secretly before we, you know. CXII: Back to Dreams In The Future So here I am, forty eight hours before March Madness and I figure myself out to be pretty much in some hot water and a place to find my spot finally in PCW. If I hated Jeremy Howard, then we would have been swapping people that we found kin to us and then, everything could have been different. But no, we chose this path and now we live it for the sake of entertainment or our own personal gain over one another. Whatever the case is, I figure it out to be something special like a flower on the midnight moon, glowing in beauty. As I laid my head along the soft, but stable sides of the pillow looking up at the stars of the night, I continued contemplating what to do with my life. It's not that I am one of the dark sick people on the face of the Earth, but what you are in life is determined by your thoughts. It was one of the things my mom told me before the incident occurred. I looked next to me, finding magazine clippings of what my career is talked about on and find a smile on my face. At one magazine from the RING's January 2002 issue, I saw a spotlight on myself and in there it was something that described me to the very brim of reality. 'The Fallen Angel' is a man on a mission, being described at a hybrid mix of Sid's raw intensity and Bret Hart's technical sound to the sport. He lives for the sport, because he grew up on it. And his sadism, brought on by years in conditions that were horrible, brings him to the level of madness that we will see come out of him. The passion he has creates his obsession about overcoming whatever is put in his way. He'll fight to the bitter, sometimes unsavory and most often unbelievable, end. I could smile at it for endless moments in time, but I needed some sleep to get off the pressure placed on me by trying to go out and face Jeremy Howard. Dreaming, St. Louis Dreaming. **** I could picture myself inside of the ring, absolute darkness circling me from all corners as I found only chanting voices and people sitting at a desk outside of the ring watching from monitors. They had the best view as I could see nothing but my own body waiting for someone, something to crawl out from the darkness. I heard the pithier patter of feet along our enshrined walkway, a large structure placed over us like glass over the body cutting inward to fit itself. I found myself only stuck on edge's end, trying to let the emotion of fear run its course, before I take strife in my battles and wage war against my enemies. I could see the person, long black hair spayed with shine watching me with a course of trying to let my eyes deceit me. Was this Jeremy Howard? Or was this a shadow waiting to strike me in the most opportune of ways? The man with sunglasses lunged forward catching me off balance trying to knock me to the ground forcing his weight onto my body, I quickly moved out of the way as his cat like quickness tried to defeat what I had bestowed in my body. I looked above at the top of the cell in which we were caged like animals and saw a man sitting in a chair waiting for release from the black prison he had been engulfed in. My hand beckon the warrior in shades to come closer, and he responded with a series of hands to my face, tight and hard with fury. I could see him releasing all of the anger I had placed in front of him since I had done the deed. I blocked one and got in a hand of my own as he started reeling backwards and into a corner. I climbed onto ropes suspended at equal height but on different corners and kicked my feet into the face of the man with shades the so called Krazed Lunatic who had been running rampant. I grunted as he tried coming back to a standing stance of fight before I took off, as fast as the Eagle flew into the skies I ran like the cheetah on land, catching the man in shades off balance with a full fledge football tackle to the stomach almost knocking the wind out of him, and a rise out of the viewers. I thought I had victory out of it, but not realizing, the man in shades capitalized on the moment and placed his right arm around my neck bending me downward before dragging up upward into the air, I could feel the swaying wind touching my feet before he brought me crashing down onto the mat. I was gasping for air at this point, trying to get back up. but he pulled me by my hair and placed my head right in between a circular space he had made between his armpit and his waist before slightly lifting me in the air and dropping me face first onto the mat, my nose shifting out of its correct placement and myself, limp without movement. My eyes were closed, I could feel his body on top of mine just waiting for the beating of three hands along the mat. Beat Beat Beat It was over, the captivity of his retainer and now I was left, once again, a loser. Mist all of the cheering, I could feel another body draped over me, the cell was gone and instead was a shadow amid blinking lights and more cheering of viewers. He had just hit me with another move from the top rope, I could feel my head throbbing profusely. Beat Beat Beat And it was over just like that. I knew what has just happened and now...it cannot happen.
