Becoming... A Schmoopie (Conclusion)

4 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04 March 2003 Prologue: We have already seen both members of the ‘Conniving Collective’ preparing themselves for their dinner with two different objectives. Firstly the Jester has brandished a ring indicating he may propose and Natalia has the gut instinct that she will be subtly made unemployed but being this uncanny pair nothing is certain, so we catch up on the action as the duo lock eyes within the hotel reception… I peered straight ahead and almost lost my breath at the picture of beauty that was standing opposite. I could sense the eyes of the male population evaluating my date but never once felt threatened, I mean I saw off the challenge of her ex-husband with relevant ease so this should seem like a walk in the park yet my nerves were apparent. I practiced my opening line under my breath as I slowly strolled towards the Soviet Scarlet, “Hey sweet cheeks…” The latter seemed so common compared to the other lines but I wasn’t about to approach her in a smug manner, maybe a simple smile would be enough for now. Standing here on my lonesome makes me a target for the singles in attendance; their stares made me feel uncomfortable yet I peered forward with tunnel vision towards my white knight that appeared to be muttering under his breath. The twitching of his mouth was a tell-tale sign that he is rehearsing a speech, maybe it’s his farewell, I can see it now, it’ll play to the tune of, “Natalia it’s been a fun ride but even roller-coasters come to an end so as from this moment I’m cutting our relationship, business relationship that is, short!” Natalia seemed uneasy as I stood leaning over wetting my lips to kiss her. As I moved in for the kill she had scouted this approach and tilted her head forcing me to peck her cheek, which made me evaluate the night, was my valet about to unearth a few truths or was this the beginning of the end… I always wanted her to voice her concern about the reforming of Pranks With Attitude but she sat silently and maybe now she feels it is the appropriate time to take action. Was I about to bare witness to the opening of the floodgates, firstly she’ll rant her disgust towards the vampire then snap about our arrangement with the Front Office and possibly conclude with a typical women’s hair-pull and scram tactic towards Eleanor Kannon-Hall. Gaz being the charmer tried to kiss me but I retorted, after all why should I cave in. Equal rights. If he’s going to show me a P45 then I’m not prepared to accept his futile attempts of softening the blow, I was so anxious to hear his articulate speech that I stormed into the restaurant area as Gaz Green conversed with a penguin tailored waiter over our seating arrangement. Even before I could usher the Russian beauty into the restaurant she had set hotfooted through the glass-panel doors as I murmured to a waiter, who by all accounts still hasn’t grasped the full use of the English language, “A table for two, the booking was under a Mr. Chris Storm squire!” Did I hear that waiter call the Jester Chris Storm? I was like a ticking time bomb, even I would admit to imagining such a statement. Hastily I walked away, suddenly being surrounded by strangers sitting at their respected tables made me freeze and wait for my partner who continued to converse with the hired help. Did Natalia hear the words ‘Chris Storm?’ I placed my hands together in prayer hoping that she missed that, already the wheels were in motion for my next caper. Unsatisfied whether or not she had heard I ventured over to test the water and politely began, “What a nice restaurant, the schmoopies would be in their element?” I paused, firstly I could swear that the name Chris Storm had been mentioned and secondly my client uses their collective name in a question he expects me to retort. Could this be coincidence? Already Gaz had lost two strikes, a third and the game was up, he was fighting for his teams survival but I decided to play along after all I was more concerned about our business relationship. “I guess so… But there again Chris Storm knows how to treat his woman!” I detected the sarcasm immediately and replied as I pulled back her seat in a gentlemanly fashion. “His women? His bitch you mean, those being the very words he used!” We sat opposite one-another, a rift seemed in place as the silence filtered over. Seconds felt like minutes and, well time was fading fast when suddenly a waiter appeared in a fashion that even David Copperfield would applaud. I fingered through the menu and strung a few words together to make my order, as did my beautiful valet who occasionally had the urge to stare with quick glances hoping I wouldn’t notice. The Jester made his order in no time, me on the other hand struggled but finally settled on something, and however I couldn’t help but stare with a warm fuzzy feeling. Funny, one minute I loathe him and the next I desire… That was it; I had to speak, “What is this dinner all about?” I gulped hard at the question Natalia asked me, “What is this dinner all about?” Did she have any clue as to the reason? Anyway I rummaged within my pocket and removed the small jewellery box unveiling its content of a diamond ring. A few sparkles and Natalia looked in awe, her mouth ajar. My question was quickly answered by the showing of a ring and not just any, it was unbelievable, and I stuttered a response, “Err… Arr… Well…” Then my hopes were crushed by a single sentence. “It’s my mothers birthday, neat huh?!?” And there’s me thinking the Emerald Menace was proposing, I should’ve seen the swerve but through my disappointment I found the courage to challenge his motives of the nights proceedings. “Yeah neat. So all you wanted me to do was assess the ring? Now you can answer my question, are you doing away with my services, can I expect to be employed come Monday or will I be demoted to PURE?” Whoa, Natalia was well impressed with my birthday gift but somehow I’m sure she thought I was about going to drop to one knee and recite some half baked speech. Her reaction was straight to the point, unlike the usual timid responses so I decided to lay her mind at rest, “Easy tiger! I wouldn’t want to demean you by sending you to PURE. I mean I can’t see you accompanying some overweight Japanese Sumo who can’t even string a English sentence together without having to refer to his Janet and John book…” Saved. Our meal surfaced as Natalia brandished a half-hearted smile, but now I planned to take the next step in tarnishing the good names of Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor. I gazed down at my food, which wouldn’t even feed an Ethiopian and complained in an enraged façade. “What do you call this tripe?” The was merely the beginning. “I know the staff here are half-baked but that shouldn’t account for the food to?” I continued portraying the irate customer evilly. Gaz was getting worked up over nothing, complaining about the food, he hadn’t even touched his meal, something didn’t add up, even with the aid of prank-e-matics. “Look his food is under cooked… And that dish, it looks like bread crumbs in soup, a meal that would wipe a mans credit card in this establishment!” Oh everything was to plan as I peered over to the ominous looking gentleman holding aloft a camcorder capturing the staff’s futile attempts at silencing my displeasure. Trying not to giggle that man was my personal pawn Nascosto Ireul Nerezza who under strict orders was filming the footage of the staff as they battle Gaz “The Jester” Gre-… Chris Storm “Why are those waiters calling the Jester, Mr. Storm?” My mind finally twigged as Gaz continued to set-up the schmoopies knowing full well that the complaint would be forwarded to Primetime Championship Wrestling. That was it; grinding my teeth I contributed to the chaos by launching my dish at my date, which accidentally spurned a cluster of food to hurtle from opposite sides of the room. “YOU CAN STICK YOUR DINNER GAZ!!!!” I bellowed as I hastily departed, trying desperately to hold back the tears of embarrassment and vanished through the glass-panel doors a second time. I felt for Natalia but business before pleasure even though I was getting a kick out of the shenanigans of the restaurant. Surrounded by chaos I soon followed satisfied that the video footage via some editing would see both Storm and Windsor facing the ultimate authority, Xavier Kannon and being a Front Office stable mate my two cents worth would be greatly valid. Ushering from the crime scene I took one look back to gloat at my conquest, noticing Nerezza had also followed suit I left under the bombardment of jeers, “MR. STORM YOUR EMPLOYER WILL HEAR OF THIS!!!!” “PRIMETIME CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING WILL BE NOTIFIED AND BILLED!!!!” “CCCHHHRRRIIISSS SSSTTTOOORRRMMM!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” - The End -
