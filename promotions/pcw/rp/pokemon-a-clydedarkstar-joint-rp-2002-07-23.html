Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-23-02 The Quest for Snotball An oddly yellow sky lights the horizon as a pair of figures traverse the countryside. Though their long journey they have remained together......bad times and good passing. But onward they walk....... 'How much longer is it asswipe?' To the right trots a little girl, no older than 8. Her hair a shock of fire read, she tugs on her blue overalls and complains to the man at her side. Bethany: My feet hurt! I wanna lay down! I wanna kill a squirrel! Clyde: Shut up. To the left, a tall man with a burned face. His clothes are old, ragged.......on his head a small red baseball cap, seeming very out of place. He removes this and wipes his brow under the midday sun. Clyde: God it's hot. Violator: I am the Violator! On Clyde's back rests a small yellow rat. It's tail juts at a very strange angle, zig zagging back and forth.....it claws into the burned man's neck, drawing blood but he pays no notice. Clyde: Yes I know you're tired too Violator, but try to hold it together. Violator: I am the Violator. The little rodent known as the Violator hops from Clyde's shoulders to his head, it's claws illiciting a yelp of pain. It then jumps to the ground, running forward. Bethany: Why the hell did you pick that stupid thing up anyway? Clyde: I don't know, I don't know anything anymore. Leave me alone. Irritated, the demon puts his red cap back on and stomps ahead........Bethany trots after him. As she rounds a hilltop though she runs right into the back of her suddenly stopped lover, falling flat on her ass. Bethany: What the hell!? Clyde: Look over there. Clyde points, to a spot in the distance. Bethany strains her eyes to see a large green form at the base of a tree, growling and snarling. Above in a branch rests the Violator, proclaiming he is the Violator in terror. Bethany: What the hell is it? Clyde: Lemme check the pokedex. Bethany: The WHAT? The burned man flips open a small palmpilot looking device........hits a few buttons, causing a quaint voice to rise from it's speakers. Jesus: Pokemon type: Snotball. Snotball: A rare pokemon, it lives in it's parent's basement in northern Maryland well past the state of adulthood. At this point it mutates into a twisted and bitter form, oozing green slime and complaining about minor league baseball while using its powers to hold those better than it firmly down. Clyde: Sounds like quite a nice fellow. Let's catch it. Bethany: Can we not and say we did? Ignoring the little girl, Clyde springs forward.......both Snotiball and Violator glance at him as he leaps into action. Violator: I am the Violator! Clyde: You're coming with me, pokemon. The burned man spins his cap around backwards, reaches into his pocket and pulls out a small globe. It opens, a beam of light flying out. Clyde: Hosshoss I choose you! From out of the ball materializes a large form........it takes shape, revealing what looks to be a big drunken cowboy. It lays flat on its back, a half filled bottle of Jim Beam resting in his hand. Hosshoss: Hosshoss......hiccup.......hosshoss...... Bethany: Dammit his only power is to suck! The demon kicks Hosshoss absent mindedly, cursing his luck. Clyde: We'll never capture the elusive Snotball this way. Bethany: I say we just leave Violator up in the tree to listen to the giant booger's stories of baseball till he croaks. Violator: (Angrily) I am the Violator! Clyde: There must be SOME way....... Clyde holds his chin.......then almost as if on cue another form walks over the hills, holding a backpack over his shoulder. Bethany: Broc......uh, I mean Darkstar! Darkstar: Hey Bethany. Clyde: What are you doing here? Darkstar: Well it's a funny story, I was off having lunch when suddenly 7 girls ran up to me and just started stripping. I asked them what they were doing that for, they told me it was because I was Darkstar, the world famous continental champion. Then the one girl told me her pipes at her home weren't working, so I followed her back.......when I looked at them nothing was wrong. Then she told me it wasn't THOSE pipes that needed fixing......so then she spread her legs......then the pizza girl showed up......and she had whip cream...... Bethany: We get it already, your life's a porno. Shut up. Darkstar: Blah. Our spiky haired friend walks up to the large mound of puss, which turns and growls viciously at him. Clyde: I don't think Snotball likes you. Darkstar: Eh. The blue haired man walks around the living pile of goo......attempts to touch it. Just as he does though a voice rings out. 'Drop that booger.' Our three heros and Violator look over, to see a man and a woman standing on the hilltop smirking. Bethany: You're....... Girl: Prepare for trouble. Boy: Make it double. Girl: To annoy you all into devastation. Boy: To bust all yall crackers in this nation. Girl: Shoving down your throats our tales of lust. Boy: Our relationship sucks but what the fuck. Girl: Amy. Boy: Jamar. Amy: Team Suckit blasting off at the speed of light. Jamar: Wonder how many times we'll break up tonight. Darkstar: ........that was gay. Team Suckit leaps to the attack, as between them a small cat races. It growls viciously, baring its fangs. Bethany: What are you? Slowly the tiny beast opens its mouth.......finally......... Cat: (In a squeaky voice) Byronzzies, thats rightzzies!! Out of nowhere laughter erupts.......the little cat turns. Darkstar cracks up, along with Bethany and Clyde......the Violator as well, followed by Snotball and Team Rocket. Over the hills the laughter rolls, until eventually it becomes deafening. Cat: ......./pointzzies. DarkStar looks down and wipes tears from his pale eyes, he slowly calms his laughter. DarkStar: You... you must be...... HAHAHAHAHAHA More laughter as Clyde doubles over. DarkStar points his pokedex in the general direction of the Pussy. Jesus: Pokemon type: Byronzzies. Byronzzies: A pussy like pokemon, it preaches its wholesome, although slightly homoerotic, living to know end. Is constantly mocked for his assumed devotion towards the pokemon 'Skankadon', usually a lackey. Beep. Im Jesus. Byronzzies: Quitzzies your laughing! We are here to destroy you and take this rare Snotballzzies. DarkStar blinks at him. DarkStar: Wow... Amy: What? DarkStar: I didn't know anything could possibly make catching small animals in magic balls of light any gayer. Clyde grows slightly angrier. Clyde: Enough of this, lets just beat them and take that blasted Snotball. DarkStar agrees, and the extremly colorful and trippy landscape swirls around as horribly tiny music begins to play. Amy: Uhhhh..... I did LSD........... lots of it.................................. thats why this is happening........................ yup I love LS-.. Everyone: SHUT UP!!!!!!! DarkStar grabs a simple rock from the ground and throws it at Amy. DarkStar: Fucking Rock! I choose you! It barely misses her head. Jamar: Thats against the rules! DarkStar sighs and kicks at the ground, he mutters something about wishful thinking. He then reaches into his bag and pull out a burlap sack. He throws it and it hits the ground with a thump. DarkStar: Extremapod! I choose you! The sack opens and a shelled, yellow thing with ugly sting like face paint pops out. Extremapod: Poop. Clyde stares it it. Clyde: Uh... didnt you... DS glances upwards and shakes his fist. Then back down at Extremapod. DarkStar: Extremapod! Hit them with your Incoherent Rambling attack! Extremapod launches himself at Byronzzies. Extremapod: Poop!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Jonny Knoxville!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! MTV!!!!!!!!!!!! PUSH ME!!!!!!! POOP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! GRANDMAS WITH SOCKS!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Byronzzies doesnt know how to react, he freezes as a small amount of cat piss rolls down his unanatomicaly correct cartoon body. Jamar thinks quickly. Jamar: GO IWOTTO!!!!! Out of nowhere pops a Pigeon wearing a IWO T-shirt and waving IWO patented triangle shaped flags (which IWO did before anyone else). IWOtto: IWO... IWO! The words '//roll1sides20' appear over his head. 11 Extremapod explodes, but only mildly. DarkStar: Wow... that was different. Jamar and Amy do the Team Suckit pose in a sexually suggestive manner. Jamar: To continue fighting us is just insane! Amy: Let us win! I want Cocaine! Clyde waves DarkStar back. Clyde: Violatorchu, just get them. Violator leaps down from the tree. Violator: I am the Violator! Violator shoots electricity at IWOtto, but IWOtto uses a grounding wire (which IWO was the first to ever use) to make sure nothing happens. Violator stands stupified. Violator: I am the Violator? DarkStar pulls another burlap sack from his bag of tricks, this one is a tad bit bloodstained but he flings it anyway. DarkStar: Mentaldisorderqueen! I choose YOU! The bag lumbers in mid air as a nearly dead pokemon flies out, IWOtto dodges the bag but doesnt notice the corpse. It lands on him with a sickening crunch, weird swirly things go over their eyes, signifying unconciousness. Mentaldisorderqueen: No BS! No BS! Clyde walks over and kicks it in the face, knocking it out. Clyde: Thank god. Amy and Jamar stare dumbfounded. Jamar: Im thinking IWOtto was a little overzealous. Amy: Well I call Renonat to make you jealous!!!!!! Jesus: Pokemon type: Renonat. Renonat: Really nothing to say, the writers of this story have nothing against the guy, come on, its funny. Even Jamar is laughing at this point. Beep. Im Jesus. Jamar glares at the plantlike pokemon. Jesus: Or not. Beep. Im Jesus. DarkStar and Clyde slowly back away. DarkStar: Thats not going to go over well. A big puff of smoke where fighting is happening appears. Jamar: Brandido! I choose yo-... Amy: NO YOU DONT!!!!!! Clyde and DarkStar put the large burlap sack over Snotball and run away screaming into the night. The scene fades out, and it seems the show is over as Amy and Jamar fight to the death about their love for the pokemon. Or does it! The scene opens back up, on a large suspension bridge in Baltimore.Clyde and DarkStar stand over the water, holding a large burlap sack. Inside the sack, the voice of Snotball...... the rare pokemon. DarkStar: Well, we finally captured K-... Snotball. Clyde nods. Clyde: Yep........ now what do we do? DarkStar looks around. Clyde: Noones looking... DarkStar thinks for a second. He smiles and grabs the big sack of snot. He throws it over the side of the bridge, and about halfway down he yells... DarkStar: Snotball! I choose you!!!!!! Clyde: (under his breath) ..to drown horribly. DarkStar: Nice... now lets go find the big sack of Stryfeizard and we can call it a day. fin
