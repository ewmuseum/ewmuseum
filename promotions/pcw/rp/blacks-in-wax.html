Blacks In Wax?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/14/02? I don&#039;t know the date. OOC- I would like to thank Miya for this idea, I didn't know such a place existed. They have a website which is rather educational, http://www.greatblacksinwax.org/ [/OOC] “What the fuck is this, man?” He was angry. Jordan X Shabazz was angry. He had received the booking sheet for the following week's Tuesday Turmoil and read that he wasn't booked. The money was needed, his business at N.I.G.G.A wasn't going as good as he thought it had the potential to go. Money was going down the drain quickly, and he was thinking that he had to fire some people to move on with his company. Another thing that irked Shabazz, was the fact that his match on Turmoil was ruined by his former friend, Duzza, when Duzza got pinned by John Crichton as the two men used some double team tatics. The camera opens up to show Jordan X Shabazz sitting in front of his black laptop Dell computer. Rush Mason drives a rented black Cadillac Deville, the Baltimore Sun beat down on the vehicle, Rush didn't even know where they were going. They were going to drive to Delaware where they would meet some of Rush' family members, but Jordan didn't want that. Jordan X Shabazz : “Say, Andrew, what’s this Blacks In Wax museum here?” After realizing that he isn’t booked, Jordan decided to just surf the web. He doesn’t want to dwell on something too long. The website he’s on is the Baltimore city tour guide, giving links and addresses to landmarks and tourists attractions. Drew Hostyle turns around while driving the car. Drew Hostyle : “What the hell did you just say?” Jordan, who is in the backseat typing away at the computer, turns the screen around to show Drew Hostyle a shot of the museum, Drew swerves on the road and immediately turns back around as Jordan laughs. Jordan X Shabazz : “We’re actually almost there, just turn off this highway here and drive for about five minutes, we’ll be there.” Drew nods from the front seat as they near the museum. Later… Drew Hostyle pulls up to the large parking, it‘s rather full. Cars of all shapes and sizes are seen, most of them appear to be owned by rich people. But what the hell would rich people be doing at a museum like this one? Jordan gets out of the backseat and it’s obvious what he is wearing, a large black Malcolm X t-shirt, some Nike track pants, and some white Nike Cross Trainers. His head is covered by a full Houston Astros baseball cap. Drew Hostyle is dressed in the clothing that Jordan usually would have denounced, baggy blue Phat Farm Jeans, a large khaki FUBU t-shirt, a black doo rag, and some brown Lugz boots. Hostyle had a scowl on his face, like usual. Jordan and Drew enter the museum and are both immediately over taken by the smell. Anybody who grew up where Jordan and Drew did would know that smell, it’s the smell of marijuana. Drew and Jordan are immediately met by a short, African American female. She looks to be in her early twenties, and is very beautiful. She wears typical tour guide clothing. Her name tag reads “Angela”, her shoulder length hair is tied back in a hair bun. She looks at Drew and Jordan. Angela : “Hello, I’m Angela, the admission fee is $5.00.” Jordan X Shabazz immediately get outraged at this. Jordan X Shabazz : “FIVE DOLLARS?! FIVE DOLLARS? YOU ARE CHARGING US, HARD WORKING BLACK MEN, FIVE DOLLARS TO GET INTO THIS MUSEUM? WHEN YOU KNOW DAMN WELL THAT THIS MUSEUM IS MADE FOR US AND BY US! I’M BLACK, WHY CAN’T I GET IN FOR FREE?! DAMN CRIMINALS!” Jordan begrudgingly goes into his pocket and pulls out a ten dollar bill that pays for him and his older brother, Drew Hostyle. The woman looks at Shabazz as if he’s crazy, what he just did was a little over the edge, but nothing too out of character for Jordan X Shabazz. Angela flashes the fakest smile that you’ll ever see. Angela : “Okay, I’m going to be your tour guide for the day. Currently representatives from FUBU are here and are looking around with a few television channels, so we’ll have to try and stay out of their way.” Jordan is obviously angered by this. Fubu is ‘For Us By Us’ but it still angered them, the entire group. Jordan, on more than one occasion, had seen white people wearing their brand of clothing, and it angered him, a lot. He had even seen white people in ads that Fubu has placed out. Shabazz is about to go off on another tirade, but he realizes that he really doesn’t want to embarrass his brother, Drew, because he knows that Drew would smack the hell out of him. The two Copeland’s are lead through the halls by Angela. They see several different African statues, and Shabazz looks is awe. While his older brother isn’t really impressed with all that he’s seeing. Jordan X Shabazz : “Say, Drew, ever notice how Black History month is in February, which coincidentally just happens to be THE SHORTEST FUCKING MONTH!! They have Black History Month, Hispanic Heritage Week, we should get a freakin’ year, like the Leap Year, and Hispanics should get our month of February! Think about how great that wou…” Jordan X Shabazz is cut off in the middle of his tirade as the representatives from Fubu come. It’s not the executives, it’s Fubu Spokesperson, LL Cool J, and much to Jordan’s surprise, a bunch of annoying white reporters and cameramen. LL Cool J sees Jordan X Shabazz and he offers his hand out for a handshake, Shabazz immediately scoffs at this, Shabazz looks LL Cool J in his eyes. It is also now obvious where the smell of marijuana is coming from. LL Cool J must have had 10 pounds of marijuana on him. Jordan X Shabazz : “A disgrace. A disgrace to the entire black race. I can’t believe you, you walk around with your shaved head, and your baggy jeans, talk about smoking marijuana, you make me sick.” Jordan X Shabazz spits in LL Cool J’s face. Some thugs grab Jordan and muscle him towards the door. Drew Hostyle just walks towards the entrance shaking his head. The cameras and light bulb’s flash as LL Cool J goes into a rage and tries to attack Jordan X Shabazz, who is now on the outside of the museum. Drew and Jordan enter the Caddy and the scene fades to black, LL Cool J cutting a promo on Jordan X Shabazz in front of the camera's inside the museum and all.
