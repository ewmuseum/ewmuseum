Date: not entered [Scene opens in the lobby of the Hilton Hotel- just another stage in the eternal journey of a professional wrestler. Flaymin walks through the lobby sipping on a beer.He walks over to a nearby lounge and sits down. Flaymin then picks up a newspaper and begins to read through it.He flicks through the pages but finds nothing of interest. Enigma suddenly appears and walks over to Flaymin. Flaymin looks up over the newspaper and smiles] Enigma: You made it. Flaymin: Yep, we have a few issues to iron out. Enigma: Sure. [Enigma takes a seat near Flaymin on the lounge] Enigma: So what’s up? Flaymin: Well…A couple of weeks back you burst onto the scene here at PCW claiming to be an old friend of mine. Enigma: Uh huh. Flaymin: And to be brutally honest, I couldn’t remember you. I thought about it long and hard and your name still didn’t ring a bell. Enigma: That’s awfully strange, because I remember you very clearly. Flaymin: So I went through my photo albums in hope that I’d find something which would evoke a memory of you. [Enigma looks at Flaymin in surprise] Enigma: You have a photo album? Flaymin: Sure, I ripped open an empty carton of Fosters Beer and glued pictures on the inside of it. Enigma: Oh. Flaymin: But the great thing is that upon sifting through my photo album, I came across a photograph…. Of you. Enigma: Really? Flaymin: Yeah.You were wearing a wolf mask and decked out in blue overalls. [Enigma raises an eyebrow and scratches his head] Enigma: Are you sure that was me? Flaymin: Oh yeah, it was definitely you.It was at Joe’s 84th birthday. Enigma: Joe? Flaymin: Joe!The local cow inseminator! [Enigma rolls his eyes] Enigma: Oh…. right……. Joe….. Flaymin: Those were happy days weren’t they! [Enigma looks around the lobby trying to divert attention from the fact that he doesn’t remember Joe the Cow Inseminator] Enigma: Look Flaymin, I just want to help you get El Diablo.That shrieking little asshole irritates me. Flaymin: But his wife sure is hot. Enigma: Angela? Flaymin: Yep.And I think she still has a thing for me. [Enigma sits up and looks at Flaymin with interest] Enigma: What are you planning? Flaymin: Well…I’ve got a little plan up my sleeve.The way I see it, El Diablo doesn’t have much left in his life. Angela is his foundation.Remove the foundation and the Mexican midget will crumble. Enigma: You do remember what happened the last time you tried something like this. Flaymin: I know, I know- I lost a testicle.But I’ve still got one left and that’s really all I need. Enigma: So how would you like me to help in unleashing destruction upon El Diablo? Flaymin: Decoy.Just stand there and flap your arms or… do the moonwalk…. or something and distract El Diablo.Then I’ll pop up from behind and BAM! I’ll clock him over the head with a chair. Enigma: That’s your plan? Flaymin: You don’t like it? Enigma: It’s just…. so basic. [Flaymin shrugs his shoulders] Flaymin: Look, you can go out and create some masterplan and I’ll just go home and bone some pornstars.Because at the end of the day, a chair across the back of the head is as effective as anything…. Enigma: You’re the boss. Flaymin: Exactly.And you’re just the decoy. [Enigma slumps down in his chair] Enigma: Decoy….. right. Flaymin: Good boy. [Flaymin gets up and pats Enigma on the head before walking away. Meanwhile, Enigma remains in the lounge shaking his head in dismay and disapproval as the scene fades to black]
