The Sage Of A Champion Part 5

6 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-6-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!Part 5” Chapter 3: The Battle To Destroy BattlePowers BeginsDay 4: Part 3 of the battle against BattlePowers {The scene picks up in BladeD’s bedroom once again as we are listening to his thoughts} ^BladeD’s Thoughts^ I am going to take over NMIAAF and make sure that BattlePowers is shutdown. I just have to figure out how I am going to do it. I mean, if I take the offer made by Banks and become President/Co-CEO I have to drop the subject of BattlePowers, but there is no way I could be President/Co-CEO. I couldn’t just sit there and watch BattlePowers go on with all the things that is happening while people on in there, but at the same time it is going to be hard to find a way around this whole mess. {BladeD stops daydreaming and walks into the living room where his brother Saber is watching wrestling} Saber: Sup? BladeD: Nothing much. {There is a dead silence for a little over a minute} Saber: WTF? BladeD: … … … Saber: Now WTF? BladeD: … … … Saber: There is something seriously wrong with you B. You are never silent like this. BladeD: I am thinking…. Saber: About? BladeD: About the whole BattlePowers thing. Saber: Why are you wasting your time? You need to just let it go. BladeD: I told you time and time again how dangerous it is. I wish I could take you to the control center one time, so you could see the danger for yourself. Saber: If it were as bad as it sounds it wouldn’t be allowed to go on. BladeD: That is what you don’t understand. It is as bad as it sounds and the NMIAAF controls all this kind of stuff… That is why there are all the strict rules of you not being able to tell people and whatnot. Saber: I have kind of always wondered about that… But I mean. There isn’t anything you can do, but just… I don’t know… just live with it I guess. BladeD: Nope, that anit happening. Saber: What you need to do…. Today…. Is go to Mr. Banks’ office and talk to the guy… There as to be something you guys can work out… I am not sure what that might be, but made instead of shutting the place down, you can get it toned down a bit… You know…. Where it is still around, but less dangerous. BladeD: I doubt it. Saber: Why do you doubt it? BladeD: All that danger is “part of the simulation,” so it wouldn’t be a full “simulation” if the realism was toned down any. Saber: Well that anit right. BladeD: Tell me something else new. Saber: ::LOL:: Shut up. BladeD: I need to go to Banks, but that is what I have been trying to think of…. That is…. what I am going to say and do. Saber: Well, you need to think of a way to talk him down or something. I wish I could help you B, but the only problem is that I don’t really have any experience in this stuff… That is what you are here for. BladeD: Yea, I know. I am the kind of guy that isn’t suppose to need help in these kinds of situations and for the most part I don’t need any help, but for some reason it just seems weird. I mean. It seems like I need help. I don’t know really. It has just been on my mind a lot. Saber: Well you need to go somewhere and get your mind off this stuff…. You have plenty of time to take a break from all of this. You should go bowling or something. BladeD: Yea, I guess you are right, but that isn’t the easiest thing in the world…. This is something that means the difference between peoples’ lives. How am I supposed to just sit around with this knowledge stuck in my head? I mean, how am I supposed to just try getting it off my mind, when I know that every time I try getting it off my mind I know that I could be costing someone his or her life? Saber: I don’t know. I mean, I understand where you are coming from and all…. I just think you should…. You know… just get this stuff off your mind for a little bit. I am not saying weeks. I mean, just for a day or two… Just to give you sometime to relax and not worry about anything… you never know…. It could do you a world of good. BladeD: Yea, well I don’t really want to get it off my mind because it is such a big thing. Saber: Well I am sick of hearing what you want to do about it…. You either need to just get it off your mind and go talk to Banks…. Or you just need to go talk to Banks… Either way the next time I hear something about that subject come out of your mouth it better be good… Because all this worrying is making me sick, so you better have something like… I am going to get this fixed by doing blah, blah, and blah…. Instead of all this… I am going to fix all this once I find out how. BladeD: All right! All right! I am kind of getting tired of it myself. I mean I want it… {Saber stops B in the middle of the sentence.} Saber: Stop! Don’t repeat yourself like that! Now continue! BladeD: Well do you want to go bowling with me? Saber: No I would, but 1) you would kick my booty like always and 2) I have other stuff I want to do just hanging around here, so you need to just go to the bowling alley without me… I will be fine here and you will be fine alone, so don’t worry about it…. Try to find a hot chick or something while you there and {BladeD stops Saber} BladeD: Find a hot chick? Ha… Funny. Saber: You have seen me do it before now haven’t you? BladeD: Yea, I have, but the only problem is that, that is a once in a lifetime type of thing. Saber: Actually, it isn’t the only thing is that you just pick the wrong times to go… ::laughs::. BladeD: Yea, while I don’t think today would be a day I would find a hot chick, but I am going to go the bowling alley anyway, so I can go bowl. Saber: All right then… I hope you have fun and remember how hard you have to concentrate, so you will forget about every thing else. BladeD: ::laughs:: Yea I guess you are right. {BladeD gets out of his seat and slaps hands with Saber, then says later. He walks out the door and jumps in his 2002 Silver Trailblazer and cranks up “Air Force Ones” by Nelly…… … And heads to the bowling alley.}
