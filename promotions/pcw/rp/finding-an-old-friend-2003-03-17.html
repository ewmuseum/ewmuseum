Finding an old Friend

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-17-03 The floor creaked with each heavy footstep. “I know it’s around here someplace. I left it here.” A voice rang out. In the darkness a figure could be seen moving around. It moves toward the wall. CLICK Light filled the room. “Much better” he said. Chris Storm looked around the room, standing next to the light switch. Chris walks around the room, getting a feel for it. He let out a pleasant sigh, glad to be back in his old apartment. It was a few hour trip but it was well worth it to Chris. He came looking for something here. Chris flops down on the couch and looks around the room. “Now where did I put it?” he asked himself out loud. His eyes turn toward the door that led to his old room. Chris smiled and pushed himself off the couch. “Ah yes, now I remember where I hide it. Perfect, this is just what I need for my match with Tim Walden. Just the thing I need to help drive my point into his thick skull.” He says. Chris opens the door and walks into the room. He looks back over to his closet and walks into it. The closet is long empty, all of his stuff either being thrown away or moved to Tessa’s house. Chris turns around in the closet so that he is facing out to the room. He stands up on his toes and looks at the wall inside the closet. There appears to be a large section of it cut out. It’s about 3 feet by 1 foot long. Chris removes the cut out section of the wall and places it by his side. “There you are. I have been looking for you. It’s time I introduce you to some new friends of mine. They have been bad and deserve to be taught a lesson.” He says as he pulls out a black case. Chris puts the cover back over the hole in the wall and walks over to the bed. He places the case on the bed and opens it. “Still in perfect condition, just like I left you. But soon you’ll be red with the blood of my enemies. Together we will start over in the PCW and this time do it right. No more being walked all over, no more abuse, no more lack of respect. And you my sweet little darling are going to help me accomplish that.” Removing the object from its case, Chris holds up a black steel chair. He turns it around and looks at the back of it. Storm is written in light blue letters. A yellow lightning bolt streaks around it. Chris holds the chair up and looks at it. “It’s been too long since I last used you. Do you remember when I use to hold you tight and bring you down upon some poor victim’s head? Well all that is about to start again very soon. In fact, we will start with Tim Walden come Turmoil. He has been saying some very mean things toward me and my sweet Tessa and he must be punished for it. So what do you say, are you up for the task?” Chris asks the steel chair. Chris puts the steel chair back into its case and locks the case. He picks up the case and starts to leave the apartment. He stops at the doorway for his room. He looks back over his shoulder at what use to be his room. “We’ve had some fun times haven’t we? I’ll miss them.” Chris says. His hand reaches down into his pocket and pulls out a small black box. Using his thumb, he opens the box and reveals a small golden band that Tessa was going to give him on their wedding day rest inside the small box. They had decided since the wedding was ruined by P.W.A. that they would still exchange rings as a promise that one day they will officially get married. However neither would wear the ring, only keep it in a safe spot. Chris had chosen to keep it on him inside the small black jewelry box. Putting the ring back away, Chris exits out of the apartment with the case containing the black steel chair. Stepping out of the apartment building, Chris takes in a deep breath of New York air. “Wednesday can not come soon enough for me. Although I am sure it will come too soon for Tim Walden.” Chris says with a slight laugh. “Defeating the newly crowned TV champion will be a nice little boost for me. Crushing the newly crowned TV champ and leaving him in a pool of his own blood will be an even bigger boost for me.” Chris hails a taxi cab. As he climbs into it, a figure from the shadows looks on at him. As the taxi cab speeds off, Ein steps out from the shadows. He has an angry look on his face. “Chris, you finally understand how to get ahead in wrestling. Now on Turmoil is your real test. I want you to draw the blood of Tim Walden. Show him no mercy and crush him into the mat. Prove that you really are sick and tired of being walked all over. Show the entire PCW that you are more then another falling Storm. Make them pay for ever underestimating you. I will be watching you…” his words trail off as Ein walks back into the shadows. The cab speeds its way back to the home of Tessa Windsor and Chris Storm. In the backstage Chris sits with the case resting on his lap. Chris has a smirk on his face as he looks out of the window. The scenes of the upcoming Turmoil play themselves through his head. Already he can see the things he plans to do. What will he do? Tune in Wednesday to Turmoil to find out for yourself.
