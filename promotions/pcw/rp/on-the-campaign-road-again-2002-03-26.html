On the Campaign Road Again

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-26-02 (We are introduced with an overhead view of the vast tan lands of the Southern New Mexico desert and the black path that is Interstate-10 stretching from the sister cities of El Paso, Texas and Juarez, Mexico. As the view from a thousand feet up in the air absorbs the white and gray clusters that are the respective cities and the winding blue waters of the Rio Grande which cut through the tan earth, we are moved closer to the highway and the passing cars exiting and entering the state of New Mexico. Among the cars is the slow moving convoy of a silver limo and a dozen black luxury cars that has just passed over the Texas-New Mexico border. We are drawn closer to the convoy which is obviously the one of the former PCW World, Two-time Continental, Television, Extreme, Cruiserweight, Rising Star, and Tag Team Champion Hall of Famer, the first Title Sweeper in the history of Primetime Championship Wrestling--Frisco. Our future dictator's limo cruises slowly through the warm, dry desert air as the Spring sun beats down on it, reflecting light of its beautifully painted hood and through the fairly transparent Argentinean flags that are adorned on the special, custom-made vehicle. The 4 flags on the vehicle wave proudly as the machine gun turrets that are built into the bullet-proof car slowly turn to spot any potential trouble-maker. The black Lexuses that surround the limo tightly close up any space between the rest of the traffic and the important presidential ride. Although the dozen car convoy clogs up the road and backs up traffic for about 5 miles, a few cars still try to get around it, by means of the dirt medium. One such disrespectful human being in a red truck tries this maneuver and is subsequently blocked off by one of the cars. A door of the stopped truck opens up and a frustrated man emerges from his vehicle, angrily shouting at the convoy member, but is quickly jumped from behind by two suit-wearing men and dragged away out of sight. We are brought into the presidential limo of our title sweeping hero and see the man himself sipping on a glass of STOLICHNAYA~! and going over a speech. 4 members of his staff are also present and working on all aspects of Frisco's campaign. Frisco sighs deeply.) Frisco: 'How long will it take for us to get to this shithole?' (One of the party members--identified as Manuel Reyes, Secretary of Time, Transportation, and Roast Beef Sandwiches speaks up.) Reyes: 'Cuarenta y cinco minutos, Señor Presidente.' (Frisco shakes his head and lies down his sheet of paper.) Frisco: 'I have no idea in fuck why I'm going to some little town to make a 10 minute speech for people who aren't even citizens of the country I'm going to rule. What's the point?' (A fair-skinned party member to the left of Frisco--Jeffrey Vizcaya the Secretary of Scheduling, Condoms, and Filling Buckets with Ice at Hotels looks over to Frisco.) Vizcaya: 'Sir, many of your fans from Buenos Aires will be in Las Cruces to watch your match. They will also be attending the rally. It's a good idea, trust me.' (Frisco sips his drink and nods.) Frisco: 'Oh, right. I have a match. Pfff! I can't believe I'm still in PCW. What the hell am I thinking?' Vizcaya: 'Well, si--...' (Frisco quickly chops the man in the throat, making him cough loudly.) Frisco: 'I don't need you to tell me what I'm thinking, you dolt!' (Vizcaya resumes coughing and grabbing at his throat.) Frisco: 'I have Señor Panchito for that.' (Frisco looks over to another one of his secretaries, this Panchito Pacheco, Secretary of Thoughts, Aspirations, and Masturbatory Material.) Frisco: 'You! What was I thinking?' Pacheco: 'Ehhhh...' (Frisco slumps in disappointment.) Frisco: 'You suck at your job, you know that?' (Frisco holds his hand up.) Frisco: 'Wait, don't answer that. You probably suck at thinking on your own too.' Pacheco: 'No, no! Ju were thinking that ju want to make more maney off merchandise for ju campaign!' (Frisco looks up at the ceiling and points at the man with an unamused look on his face.) Frisco: 'You know....you're right!' (Frisco chuckles and Pacheco joins in with an uneasy laugh.) Frisco: 'Why, I was all swept up in this title sweeping thing, I forgot what my true calling was--selling out! Especially this whole International Incident thing. That was legitimate thinking on my part! Genius, pure genius. Not only will this stable promote my presidential race and my country, but it can also supply me with fresh new fans out of all those smarks who like the bad guys and hate the faces. This is sure to generate a load of cash for the progression of my campaign! Absolutely beautiful! Entirely smart thinking on my part.' Pacheco: 'Actually, jur agent thought of that.' (Frisco reaches out to smack Pacheco but coils back to his original position and strokes his goatee.) Frisco: 'Yes, I know this, but it was my idea to agree with him.' Vizcaya: 'Actually, that was your Secretary of Agreements' idea.' Frisco: 'Oh, right. Where is the old chap? I'd like to give him a raise.' Vizcaya: 'You fired him last week and gave his job to Armando.' (Frisco thinks for a minute.) Frisco: 'I see. Who's Armando?' (A flamboyant looking Latino in a multi-colored shirt across from Frisco raises his hand.) Armando: 'I am, silly!' Vizcaya: 'He's your Secretary of Presidential Hair, Beard, and Agreements.' (Frisco smacks his forehead.) Frisco: 'Oh, right. The hairdresser.' (Frisco finishes off his vodka and lights up a Cuban. He clicks his phone on, which rings immediately, but Frisco declines to answer and just presses a few buttons.) Frisco: 'Ah, Armando, Boca called. He wants you to meet him at Dick's Cafe when we get to the city.' Armando: 'Dick's Cafe?' Reyes: 'Si, es un restaurante in Las Cruces.' Frisco: 'I think that's some sort of innuendo, dear follower of mine.' Armando: 'Boca wants some of this chorizo, does he?' (Frisco shudders slightly.) Frisco: 'Yes, I'm sure he does, but watch him carefully. He might be trying to usurp your power to usurp mine. You can't trust anyone nowadays.' Armando: 'Even sexy Boca?' (Frisco pounds his fist on his seat and snarls.) Frisco: 'Especially sexy Boca!' (Armando frowns. Frisco puffs happily on his cigar and looks over his speech again.) Frisco: 'Where is this event again?' Reyes: 'En la universidad, jefe.' Frisco: 'Oh, great. Another one of those on-campus speeches. I hate all these smart-ass college students. They're nothing but idiot hippies who care nothing about important issues, but stupid piddling crap like clean air and the fucking ozone layer...' (Frisco knocks on the separating window where his gunners sit behind their turrets. The window rolls down.) Gunner #1: 'Si, El Mero Mero?' Frisco: 'Si hay algunos hippies, usted tiene mi permiso de matarles. (The gunners smile and salute.) Gunner #1: 'Bueno!' Gunner #2: 'Gracias, Señor Presidente!' (Frisco nods as the window goes back up.) Frisco: 'At any rate, there should be some sexy dames there, ripe for the picking.' (Frisco puffs out a cloud of smoke.) Frisco: 'I just hope they had their shots. This place is awfully close to the border.' (Reyes hangs his head in shame.) Frisco: 'Oh, lo siento, Reyes. I forgot you were a filthy Mexican.' (Frisco reaches out his hand to pat Reyes on his head before quickly wiping his hand on the man's suit.) Frisco: 'But don't fret, you will gain your citizenship upon my appointment. You will then be able to look at yourself in the mirror and know you are not a common greasy wetback, but a refined Argentinean politician.' (Frisco straightens his party member's suit and smacks his face.) Reyes: 'Gracias.' Frisco: 'De nada.' (Frisco sucks on his cigar once again.) Frisco: 'Now, someone please fill me in on this week's events. I'm in the dark here. My fucking agent is worthless at these types of things.' Reyes: 'Bueno, primera...' (Frisco sighs.) Frisco: 'En ingles, por favor.' Reyes: 'Jes, sir, Meester Presidente.' (Frisco nods.) Frisco: 'Proceed.' Reyes: 'Tonight ju have the match with jur partner, Chef, against Legend Inc. and Big Alan Steele.' Frisco: 'Oh, yes, of course, dear Chef. May Allah guide us to victory this evening.' (Frisco thinks for a second and cracks a smile.) Frisco: 'And I think the deal is just about sealed for us with Chef's connections. Allah will be happy to give us a win over that fucking pagan Alan Steele. As for Legend Inc....well, I think you've all seen what I've done to him. I used him up like a box of condoms at a strip club to complete my title sweep.' Reyes: 'Box of condoms at a strip club?' (Frisco sighs.) Frisco: 'My apologies. The jet lag has assured a lack of creativity this afternoon.' (Frisco shrugs helplessly.) Frisco: 'At any rate, this one's in the bag. What next?' Reyes: 'Well, ju got a match with Jason Core at Sabotage...' Frisco: 'Oh, deary me! I thought I'd never have to do more of these breaking in of lower-tier fucktards again. What is happening to my career?' Reyes: 'Is ending?' (Frisco shrugs.) Frisco: 'Why, you're certainly right. By my choice, of course. God knows I wouldn't stick around to wrestle when I have a country to run and the lives of millions of people to enhance.' Reyes: 'And ju have jur match with Legend Inc. at the PPV...' Frisco: 'Yes, I was aware of that one. This should be quite fun.' (Frisco sniffs the air and frowns.) Frisco: 'What the hell is that smell?' Vizcaya: 'Cows, sir. There's a dairy farm to your left.' (Frisco looks out the window.) Frisco: 'Ah, so there is. Disgusting area we're in. You'd think all of America would be able to get milk without filthy, smelly cows, yet they lack so far behind the rest of the civilized world.' (Frisco smacks his lips.) Frisco: 'Speaking of milk, someone make me a White Russian.' Armando: 'I'll do it!' (Armando reaches for the small fridge to take out the ingredients.) Frisco: 'Oh, and someone cancel my rally. I'd much rather do it tomorrow.' Vizcaya: 'Yes, sir.' (Frisco smiles and props his feet up on the opposite seat as he blows smoke out of his mouth and sucks it back in through his nose. Vizcaya picks up his phone and begins to dial the phone. The view departs from the interior and zooms out as the convoy approaches the Southern New Mexican city for what should be an interesting trip to follow.)
