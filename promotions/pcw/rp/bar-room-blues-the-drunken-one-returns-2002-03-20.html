Bar Room Blues: The Drunken one returns?!

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/20/2002 Life could be such a lonely existence at times. He never had anyone to love, anyone to love him, nor did he have any family left. He had grown accustom to being alone and it never really bothered him. He had more important things to worry about than the constant nagging of loneliness for you see he was the only man who could destroy the sin of the world. Now he had the job of once again destroying Lance Bell. Bell, the same ignorant jack ass which fell to the feet of the Sylencer once before. He needed a drink. He couldn't believe the bookers would make him defend his Celestial star title against this sorry sack of skin but he loved to fight, he loved to destroy, and he loved to shed the blood of those who opposed him. Sadistic? Maybe but he never thought about it, it was almost like when Akiyama gave him these Celestial powers it unleashed some other power which had laid waiting to be unleashed inside. The day before Jay had decided to get a few more tattoo's, why he did he doesn't even know. But now his right arm is covered in black, fire looking tattoo's and he now has an additional piercing. He thought if he changed his appearance that it would drive away those who sought to get close, those who wanted to harm his soul, those who were out to hurt him. 'The Spinal Tap,' Jay's voice rang out. His eyes were locked upon a neon sign with the words 'The Spinal Tap' on them. He shrugged and walked in but there wasn't very many people in during this time. The room smelled like alcohol mixed in with cheap cologne. To the far part of the place were about four pool tables with men standing at two of them, most likely betting. This wasn't his kind of place and if he hadn't needed a drink so bad he would have never came to this god forsaken place. He made his way over to a dark red seat with metal bars that supported the cushion. A portly man with a receding hair line walked over to Sylo who sat with his head down. 'Can I help ya Mack?' The bartender asked cleaning out a few glasses with an old white cloth. 'Give me the hardest thing you have,' Sylo replied never raising his head. The bartender tapped the bar and walked over to the side pulling out an unmarked bottle. He placed it in front of Sylo along with a crystal shot glass. Sylo didn't care what it was, he needed alcohol and he needed it now. This was the first time he really had the chance to think about how alone he really was. It sort of got to him. He never had anyone to go home too, never anyone to be with, never the companion of the opposite sex. It hurt, yes, but what woman in their right mind would want someone with the type of demons he had? He downed a few more shots and turned his head. He was shocked; sitting next to him with puffy red eyes, obviously from crying, was a brown headed woman. She was beautiful and through the redness he could see she had crystal blue eyes. Her body was well built and she had luscious ruby red lips. She lightly sobbed as Sylo continued to look over. 'Ma'am is something wrong?' Sylo asked trying to look concerned. 'It...It's nothing...' The woman replied. 'Can I maybe buy you a drink?' Sylo asked still holding the look. This was a side of him never seen. A compassionate side. The side of himself he tried to hide from the world and until this point he had done a great job of it. 'Please, I'm not in the mood to be hit on,' The woman said dabbing at her eyes with a tissue. 'Oh, no Ma'am I'm not hitting on you....You just seem like you need a drink,' Sylo replied. 'Your sweet,' She replied. 'Make it a vodka,' Him sweet? He hadn't heard those words in quit some time. He looked at the lady then back to the bartender. 'Gimme a vodka,' 'So what's your name?' She asked Sylo. 'Names Jay,' He replied. 'Oh, I'm Laura,' Laura, she had the name of an angel. He couldn't believe in a matter of moments he had already let his defenses down. Soon her vodka came and she downed it in no time. The two sat talking for a few hours and then finally the question came. 'So what do you do for a living?' Laura asked with a smile. 'I'm a...I'm a professional wrestler,' Sylo replied. 'Oh, I used to watch wrestling as a little girl,' She said keeping the smile. 'Really? I have a match against someone I have already beat before, he goes by Lance Bell and I'm really sick of fighting this no name prick so I suppose i'll just destroy him once and for...' he caught himself. 'I'll just beat him,' He looked down trying to hide his eyes. He noticed he hadn't let her look into them the whole time they talked. 'Thank you,' Laura said looking at Sylo. 'For?' He looked up and for once she finally saw his eyes. 'Oh, god I'm sorry,' 'NO! Don't be...their beautiful,' She said smiling. Did she mean it or was she just being nice? He so hopped she meant it, he actually felt wanted for once in quit a long time. He tapped his large fingers on the bar turning back to Laura. 'So why were you crying earlier?' Sylo asked point blank. 'It's just, I caught my boyfriend in bed with another woman. I mean we had been together for two years so I thought I would surprise him, that's when I found them,' All the memories of when he was younger rushed through his head. His only love, he had found her in bed with another man and it burned. It had burned a hole in his soul, he didn't think he would ever get over it but he did. Now it was back and the same lump started to form in his throat as he fought to hold back the warm salty tears swelling up in his eyes. 'Did I say something wrong?' Laura asked looking concerned. 'No...It's just a long time ago I had a love, I thought she was the one but the same thing happened to me,' He said taking another shot of whatever it was he was drinking. 'Oh I'm so sorry,' She placed her soft hand on his large, tattooed arm. Her touch was as soft as silk, did she pity him or did she really care? He didn't know, so much emotion was running through him. He had just met this woman and already she had broke down his defenses. 'Listen, I have a few things I need to do so I can get ready for my match....If it's ok with you I would like your phone number and to keep in touch,' Sylo said looking Laura dead in the eyes. 'Of course!' She boomed and quickly wrote it down on a cocktail napkin. Sylo did the same as he paid for the drinks. He stood up and this was the first time she really got a glimpse at how big he actually was. 'Good luck with your match thing,' She said with an innocent smile. 'Thank you, and I will be in touch,' 'You better' She said grinning. Sylo turned and walked out of The Spinal Tap. For the first time he was actually smiling, it had been so long that this feeling was a new one. For once he felt no pain, for once he was happy. But soon thoughts of destroying Lance Bell took over. He tucked the cocktail napkin in his pants pocket for safety and walked toward his car. Just then from out of no where he was startled. 'SQUEAKY!' A shrilled voice boomed. 'Rolf?!'
