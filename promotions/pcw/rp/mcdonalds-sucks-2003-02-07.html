McDonalds.....SUCKS!!!

7 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/07/03 *The scene opens up outside of an American favorite, McDonalds. A 2003 Lincoln Navigator, jet black, comes screeching into the drive-thru lane. As the Navigator halts to place an order for drive thru, you can make out the license plate which says, 'RL LGND1.' The scene then fades out and back inside of the Navigator, with PCW's very own Jon Kano driving to the window of the McDonald's. Kano has his long, blonde hair tied back in a ponytail and is wearing jeans, a 'Kano ROCKS~!' t-shirt, and platinum Armani sunglasses. Finally, Kano creeps to the pay window.* Clerk: $6.12, sir, oh my God, you are Jon Kano! You are like my favorite wrestler. *Kano smiles at the young female clerk.* Kano: That is good to hear. You coming to the show tonight? Clerk: Mr. Kano, I got tickets in the upper bowl of the arena. Kano: Nah, thats no good. *Kano reaches into his back pocket and pulls out two tickets.* Kano: Here ya go, front row. Clerk: OH MY GOD!!! Thank you!!! Kano: Here is a fifty, keep the change. *After saying this, Kano creeps up to the pick up window.* Pick up Clerk: Just a minute, sir. If you would just pull up over to that curb, it will be just a few minutes and I'll bring it to you. Kano: Alright. *Kano obliges and pulls up and over to the curb. After Kano throws his trademark Navigator in park, he reaches into his center console and pulls out a cigar and lights it.* Kano: Nothing like a fine cigar, my friends. Its a sign that life is good. I have had some ups and downs over the last few months. I went flying off the stage and never thought I would wrestle again, I came back and retired the vermin known as 'Controversial' Corey Williams. *Faintly, in the distance, you can hear cheers.* Kano: Damn, I guess people do appreciate that. My wife, whom I love more than anything in this world, and I were going to have a child. That changed last month, eight months pregnant and she miscarried. It was almost a medical first. It happened and she and I didn't know what to do. But like a Phoenix rising from the ashes, she and I got through it. She and I have been through a lot, but we always get through it. *Kano takes a deep puff off of his cigar.* Kano: Tonight, I am at a crossroads. If I turn one way, I go to March Madness but run the risk of alienating one of my best friends, Duke Williams. If I turn the other, I don't go to March Madness and I may never get another shot at the PCW World Heavyweight Title. Quite a predicament have I found. Duke knows me well enough to know that I don't go easy on anyone and he is the same damn way. He and I are going to get in that ring tonight, shake hands, and have at it. The fans will cheer when he hits a move, they will cheer when I hit a move, and when it is all said and done, PCW will learn what being professional is all about because the two of us will walk out of that ring together and sit at the bar and get shitfaced like the days of old. And tonight, I will show Duke, Kannon, Syko, and anyone else who watches the match why I am exactly what I say I am, I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!! *Kano sticks his head out of his window.* Kano: Where the hell is my food?!?!?!?!?! *The scene fades to black as Kano finishes his scream at the fast-food establishment.*
