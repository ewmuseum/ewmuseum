The Eternal Minute

8 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Eternal Minute Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 08/03/03 *BEEP-BEEP* *BEEP-BEEP* *BEEP-BEEP* The raw and shrill beeping of the electronic alarm clock cuts through the still air of the Kannon-Hall residence’s bedroom. Through the curtains no morning sunlight penetrates, only the white circular hue of the moon still hanging in the night sky. “Ughhhhhhhhhh...” A throaty groan resonates from under the covers as the beshevelled head of Xavier Kannon emerges, his sleepy eyes narrowed and chin stubbled. Getting his bearings in the first few seconds after being torn from his slumber, his eyes work side-by-side with his ears as the Scientologist’s senses hunt down the alarm clock. Xavier : “What the....?” His thin fingers rub his tired eyes, the hardened secretions that lurk in the crevices of his skin and sit in eyelashes like pollen in bee fur being removed. As his pupils adjust to the light, the stark contrast between dark room and bright red read-out makes it easy to see the hour he has arisen. 3 : 08 Xavier : “This can NOT be happening...” 3 : 08 - The numbers that haunt the Scientologist to this very day, and around the time of March Madness they were always going to play on his mind. He blinks, eyelids washing over green irises to hopefully change what they send to his brain, but his optic nerve is not lying. It’s just after 3 in the morning. “Mmmmmmmhhhhhhhh...” A moan is heard beside him as his wife finally wakes up too, wondering what is going on. Her movement doesn’t distract her husband however, Kannon’s face now pale and eyes wide as he stares at the alarm clock. Xavier : “Did YOU do this, my little Poon Puppy?” Much slower to reach an alert state than her man, Ellie~!’s lightly freckled face emerges from under the top of the sheet, her blonde and pink streaked hair frizzy from her sleep. Eleanor : “Whaaaa?” Eyes arching, she looks bemused as to what the question is regarding, yawning long and loud before deciding she doesn’t want to bother finding out what topic of discussion is and collapsing back down into her mound of pillows. Eleanor : “Ellie~!’s too tired to.... Anything...” In the darkness, his skin bleached by the white moonlight, Kannon cannot look away from the clock, why won’t the minute end? How long can it be 3 : 08 for? Rarely does this look cross Kannon’s face... He’s spooked. Xavier : “If this is him, I’ll.... I’ll....” He doesn’t finish the sentence, each repetition hopefully prompting 9 to flick up, but it isn’t happening. Could it be Akiyama messing with him? Surely not, it isn’t his style... Putting Xavier in hospital is. Xavier : “You didn’t touch the alarm clock... Did you, my little Fuck Ferret?” His worried and hopeful question is not answered by Ellie~! who’s now burrowing deep under the covers, trying to get back to sleep again to leave her man alone in his stake of nervous awake. Desperate for an answer, Kannon asks again, maybe she’ll say yes just to shut him up... But at least it would be a yes. Xavier : “...Did you?” Looking down at the bump under the sheets that is his wife, Eleanor, Kannon then snaps his head back around, the thoughtful emerald eyes hoping to see 3 : 09 looking back at him but all he sees is 3 : 08 standing firm in defiant electric red. “HELLO?!?!? Like... SO asleep and stuff!” Her high pitched, whiny voice dictates to him through the bed clothes, leaving Xavier alone in his concentration, willing the clock to tick forward with his stare. Xavier : “It won’t happen this year... No oppression, no early elimination... This year 3 : 08 will be the time I’m in the ring before I win.” Talking through gritted teeth towards the alarm clock’s digital face, Xavier sounds as if he’s trying to convince himself as much as anyone else... Fearing a repeat of a year ago more than almost anything. As embarrassing as it was then, it would be nothing compared to if it came to be in 2003. Xavier : “Last year opened my eyes, this year... I use them to win...” He’ll have no excuses if it happens again. He played the religion card, the oppression card, the newbie card... Now he holds all the cards. Eyebrows creep down closer to eyelashes, wrinkles forming from his brow down to the bridge of his nose... If Xavier was capable of telekinesis, the display would be racing forward so quickly it’d show 23 : 47 by now.... But still, 3 : 09 doesn’t come. Xavier : “3 : 08 was last year, but take the first 2 digits and you have the reason why it won’t happen THIS year.” Despite his confident and prophetic words, his eyes don’t shimmer with that air of confidence they usually do... Sitting in bed at gone 3 in the morning, talking to an alarm clock isn’t the best preparation for what lays before him this week. How long has this minute lasted? The inner demons that are housed in the Scientologist’s psyche slow down his perception of time. 60 seconds drags out to seem like forever trough his eyes, so distorted is his account of time right now that he doesn’t even realise it’s only been 55 seconds. “Oh, I kinda, like, forgot...” Finally his eyes break away once more as attention switches back to Ellie~!, popping her head out from the sheets once more with a mischievous grin on her make-up free face. Eleanor : “I set the alarm for, like, 3 : 08! Seeing as, you know, you WAY got eliminated last ye-” Before she can even finish her cheek explanation, Kannon grates a husky growl her way. Eleanor : “...Hey, it was, like, SO funny and stuff!” His chiselled face looks un-moved by her finding it humorous, Ellie~! sulking and delving back down into the warm and snug bed. “Some people have, like, SO no sense of humour...” With Eleanor moaning and whining to herself, Kannon’s head slowly turns back to the clock, eyelids closing in on one another while his view pans. Head aimed at the clock, mind ready, the eyelids slowly move apart once more, view slowly gaining clarity until he sees it, burning through the blur. 3 : 09 ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
