Autograph Session

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-26-03 PrologueI can’t believe I lost, not to a man like Manuel Labor! What the hell is wrong with me to lose to such a pathetic miscreant like that pompous ass! I just don’t understand, I’m the supreme talent yet I’m being held back, or am I? I’ve been given the chance to perform where Hank~! Only got the new talent and jobbed the good job. There’s no man like me in this industry, why am I worried about one mealy loss? So Manuel Labor got a cheap victory he shouldn’t have gotten, big deal, like it will happen again. However time to redeem myself will come and it will come a week from a cheap win, I Ganker Ransolver will have the chance to redeem my name and become the PCW Extreme Champion! All I have to do is beat four other men, Manuel Labor being one of the four. The other three are; Abner, Matt Hunt, and Flaymin Aymin. Now I may have my work cutout for me with Flaymin Aymin in this match, I mean he’s held countless titles, countless numbers of time! However I am Ganker Ransolver and that statement was me jesting at the fact I actually sweat Flaymin Aymin for a damn second. A man of my style and flair doesn’t sweat a man like Flaymin Aymin, the worthless pile of cow dung is all that boy is to me. As for the rest, Manuel got his lucky cheap win over me, but it won’t be repeated as I will walkout the new PCW Extreme Champion, proving once again that I am ten times the man that my former tag team partner Hank Gunyon was! The Extreme Championship is only to get my foot into the door for bigger and better things though I can really see my future shaping here in the PCW as the next big cruiserweight champion! I far surpass everything Kid Wonder has done. PCW has never seen a force like Ganker Ransolver and quite frankly because Ganker Ransolver is ONE OF A KIND! I rant on and off in third person every now and then.The scene opens up to Ganker Ransolver walking down the street to and autograph session he’s been scheduled for in Lima, Peru. Though Ganker doesn’t like the fact of signing autographs for his so called ‘unworthy wretched fans’, it’s the cash that brings him this way. Ganker stops by a bum who is waving his tin cup looking for pity, but mainly tips. Ganker hears the rattle of the tin cup as his eyes become wide and bright like a child on Christmas Day. Ganker looks down at the bum then kicks him in the nuts then continues to kick him several times after. Ganker grabs the tin cup and grabs the entire five dollars and fifteen cents from the cup. Ganker stuffs it in his pocket then throws the cup on the bums head. Ganker continues down the sidewalk as he finally comes to the record store he’s been booked to sign autographs at. Ganker enters and takes a seat behind a table set up for him. The store manager approaches him.(typical gay sounding voice) ”Mr. Ransolver you’re late!””Dude I’m going to make you some money today dude, so you better be glad I even showed up. It’s bad enough I have to sit here for hours listening to fans express how much they love me and have looked up to me. I’ll tell you one thing I hate more than my tag team partner right now, is obnoxious fans who can’t control themselves when presented wit’ greatness such as myself. Kind of makes me wonder how big the mobs will be after I become the PCW Extreme Champion.”(typical gay sounding voice) ”That’s not now though Mr. Ransolver! What is now is a lot of fans waiting to sign your autograph and buy records in the process.”The store manager opens the doors the a row of fans storm in and up to Ganker’s table. The first in line has an old replica of the RSE Cruiserweight Championship. Ganker smiles as he grabs a special pen to write on the black leather.”And who do I make this out to?””Laura Islea and can you put to my adoring fan?”(smiles up at her) “Yeah, and might I say you’re kinda cute.””Thanks, I’m a huge fan.” (in shock of seeing Ganker)”Yeah I have a lot of those.” (laughs)”I’m fifteen by the way.””I’m twenty-two, it wouldn’t work.”Laura walks over to look at the records after picking up her RSE Cruiserweight Championship. The next person in line drops an issue of PCW Magazine where Ganker graces the cover; he is standing behind his former tag team partner Hank Gunyon. Ganker sigh as he reluctantly signs.”Had to bring up bad memories didn’t you kid?””Man Hank was so much cooler than you! I got his autograph when he came here too and he was so much cooler! He actually looked up at his fans when they talked to him, not comment on merchandise he didn’t like! You’re just jealous because Hank was better than you could ever be!””Listen kid, if I don’t look at you it’s cause you’re fat and ugly and I don’t wish to go blind As for my commenting on the merchandise, it’s me simply muttering to myself, you need not pay attention kid. Although I can understand why, I am GOD after all.””I’m gong to sell it on EBAY when I get home!”“What’s your name kid?””Mike Perez.”Ganker signs the magazine and personalizes it to really piss him off so he can’t sell it on EBAY like he planned because like you know a personalized autograph doesn’t get near as much as one with just the signature of the wrestler. Ganker hands the magazine back as Mike reads it then turns red.(staring at Ganker then reads what Ganker wrote on the magazine back to Ganker) “Sell this on EBAY chump?” (turns red) “Why you…”(laughs) “I know what I wrote and that’s what you’re ass gets for trying to use me to make a few bucks. I hate being exploited boy and you no suffer for trying to do so to me.”Mike walks over to look at some records as another guy walks up, he puts down an NZW tag team title, one of the actual titles used. Ganker slowly looks up to notice it’s his tag team partner Hank Gunyon. Ganker stands up and the two men embrace. Ganker then sits back down as he signs the belt.”I heard you were signing autographs, but I didn’t expect such a large line man.””Yeah well it’s what you get when you’re better than the PCW Jobber and come into the business actually winning matches, although I am two and two.””I won matches while I was contracted, but I dunno PCW jus’ wasn’t my cup of tea. Anyway I heard you had a shot at the Extreme Championship this week, congratulations on that man. Hopefully your career is longer and better than mine was.””Well seeing as I was the man behind our tag team and was the real talent, that won’t be a problem. I’ve already proven it when I first arrived that you don’t screw wit’ Ganker Ransolver or you will feel the ‘Vengeance’.” (he says with a bit of pent of aggression behind his voice)”Relax dude, we’re friends man.””Yeah well this friend is finally out of your damn shadow and I don’t want to go back to being in it! You were never the better of the two, I was! I’m in PCW to prove something to not only myself, but the entire world! However since you’re a man of the people, why don’t you takeover here, I’m sure Lima, Peru wants your autograph more than mine anyway.”Ganker stands up and exits the record store as Hank shrugs and takes his place signing autographs. Ganker looks in the window looking at Hank just ignoring anything Ganker said. Ganker begins to walk down the street as a young lady comes up from behind Ganker and tugs on his arm; she is about five-foot four-inches with light brown hair.”There’s a real superstar in there who actually gives a damn about the fans, why aren’t you standing in line?””Because I’m not a Hank Gunyon fan, I’m one of the few who knew you were going to breakout on your own. You should see my apartment; it drives a lot of men away because of how much of your stuff I have of yours.”(hearing this brings a smile to Ganker’s face) “I might have to break my hate the fans policy.” (smiles as does this girl) “So do you have a name?” (she nods) “And it would be?”(coming off a bit shy, being around her favorite superstar and all) “Arlene Hilton.””Well it’s nice to meet you, Arlene.””You wouldn’t happen to want to get a cup of coffee would you? I know this little café down the street. I know you have a girlfriend, but it’d just…”(smiles as he places his index and middle finger over her lips) “Yeah I’d love to grab a cup of coffee with you.”Ganker and Arlene walk around the block and into a small café and find a booth. The waitress recognizes Arlene and brings out two cups of cappuccino for the two of them.”You know Sheilla was a fan of mine before we got together, as a matter a fact that’s how we met. She was the interviewer for some company and we hooked up.”“Yeah I know, I’ve followed your career ever since it started.””Yeah you’ll definitely have to meet Sheilla, you two definitely have a lot in common.”“Yeah that’d be cool; I’d like to meet the woman who’s captivated the heart of the greatest wrestler and my favorite.””Maybe you’ll get to sooner than you think.””Where are you staying?””Why you going to stalk me?” (in a joking tone)(in a joking voice) “Maybe.”(laughing with Arlene) “The Holiday Inn.””That’s cool, I’ll have to come by and see you sometime, maybe even meet Sheilla while I’m there.” (smiles) “Maybe we could get a bite to eat, the three of us.””Uh yeah sure.”“So tell me about this match you have for the extreme championship, a match that I know you’re doing to so totally win Ganker!””Yeah I am going to win, that’s a give, but it’s because I am GOD!” (Arlene laughs) “Manuel Labor is the champion because he’s never defended against a GOD like me! Manuel Labor will lose the title to the better man, and as for the other fruit loops? It doesn’t matter bout any of them, I’m going to drop them wit’ the ‘Vengeance’ and be done wit’ them!”Ganker and Arlene continue chatting up as the scene cuts off to black.The night went on and I really enjoyed spending time with Arlene Hilton. I never thought I could enjoy spending time with a fan so much, well other than my girlfriend Sheilla Hisser, but that’s why we’re together. After the two of us finished our cappuccinos we took a walk outside under the stars together. She explained how she was into astrology like me and we just talked up a storm. Together we ran into Sheilla Hisser, my girlfriend, and talked up and then I felt the need to go lie down after hearing those two exchange stories and have a grand time. Needless to say Sheilla later made it up to me the only way she knows how and thank the Heavens for it too! All and all the night wasn’t that bad after the autograph signing in the record store, which still wasn’t that bad considering I got to humiliate an idiot and meet two true Ganker Ransolver fans. So the night was good and now I’m asleep so leave me THE HELL ALONE YOU JACKASSES!
