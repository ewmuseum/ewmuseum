Jurisdiction.

Date: Respectfully submitted late. Alarms ring loudly in the back alley of the electronics store, as the back door bursts open and a man in a black wrap-around motorcycle helmet bolts into the night. Dressed all in black, he curses his carelessness, sprinting through the damp back street, a big computer box clutched to his chest. The crime has not gone unnoticed. Just above the skyline of the nearby buildings, the whine of a turbine screams through the air, the sound ricocheting through the cramped alleyway. The villain in the helmet cranes his neck all around in a desperate attempt to gain a handle on the origin of the sound. “Ooof!” “Doing a little late night shopping, are we?” The man in black is now on his backside, still hugging the computer to his chest. He shakes in what can presumably be called fear, as he tries to scoot his way backwards from the man flashing a brilliant smile, unaffected by the darkness. Oh, and he’s floating. Arms crossed majestically in front of him, the Mighty Turbo hovers about a foot off the ground, grinning at the thief in front of him. His costume is quasi-futuristic, shiny silver accentuated by strategic red swaths of color. A thin red mask that only covers his eyes is on his face. Red Boots with yellow stripes, same with the gloves. And a booster pack on his back roars, a thin stream of exhaust pluming underneath the erstwhile hero. All in all, an impressive-looking man. He runs a glove through his golden hair with a salon look. His teeth twinkle unnaturally. “In order to acquire goods or services, you must trade something of equal value, hence our monetary system,” says Turbo, his voice rich with baritone, “Looks like someone needs a lesson in economics… and JUSTICE!” The helmeted thief scrambles to his feet and takes off in the other direction with the computer box, puffing mightily as his adrenaline rush kicks in. Laughter chases him down the alley, the complacent Turbo enjoying the moment. Suddenly, the booster pack shrieks as the turbines fire, Turbo exploding from his spot, zooming past the criminal at frightening speeds. The bad guy loses his vision in the rocket’s contrail, slowing him down and eventually stopping him. The smoke slowly dissipates, and there, bobbing in front of him, is that smile. The smile of Turbo! “You cannot escape the rocket-powered justice of Turbo! Give it up, ne’er-do-well! You’re days of grand theft larceny are over!” Yelling out in frustration, the helmeted man throws the computer box at Turbo, hoping at the most to cause injury, and in the least buy some time. Neither is accomplished as Turbo easily moves aside, the sound of the contents smashing ringing through the street. Turbo shakes his head sadly at the villain again running away from him before his backpack roars to life again. He slingshots down the pavement, making up the distance with astounding ease, and playfully taps the man on the shoulder. The man in the biker helmet whirls, lashing out with a desperate haymaker… and he spins around like a cheap top, pirouetting to the ground in a heap. Turbo idles directly above him, about eight feet off the ground. He wags his finger at the thief as if scolding a child. “Grand theft larceny, breaking and entering, resisting arrest, trespassing, destruction of property, and now assault of a duly deputized law enforcement official… tsk tsk. The charges just keep mounting. Give it up, foolish criminal! You can’t defeat the awesome combination of speed and power in… the mighty TURBO!!” “I’ll kill you!!” On his back, the villain whips out a gun and levels it at Turbo. The member of the Hall of Justice, local chapter, narrows his eyes. His arm quickly extends, his hand balled into a fist as he taps the upper end of his forearm with his other hand. Before a shot is even fired, the gun flies out of the man’s hand! He zips through the arm into the waiting palm of Turbo, who smiles on cue. “Now assault with a deadly weapon! I’d say you’re up to about 2-5 years now, at least. Don’t make it any worse on yourself… surrender quietly or you’ll force me to take you by force!!” But the man is already moving, scrabbling backwards as he lobs what appears to be a grenade forward… WHOOSH!! The sphere detonates, sending a cloud of chaff into the alley. The tiny particles engulf Turbo, getting sucked into his intake valve. The rocket pack sputters and coughs, jerking the hero around in the air for a few seconds before sending Turbo crashing hard to the wet pavement. Turbo snarls as he hurries to his feet, watching the man dash away ahead of him. Cursing silently, he begins to sprint after the thief, who is surprisingly nimble compared to the grounded Turbo, loaded down with his crime fighting gear. The man in black and Turbo engage in a game of cat and mouse, Turbo desperately trying to keep pace with the bad guy, who leaps over boxes, fences, and dives over cars in his attempt at escape. The villain has lengthened his lead, Turbo barely able to keep him in sight. The man in black looks behind him… and nearly has his head taken clean off by a wicked clothesline!! Stepping out, from around the corner, is our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, who we now rejoin as he steps over the fallen man with a menacing look. “Your deceit and trickery end here and now, villainous scourge of San Jose! Now you shall be forced to pay your debt to society as well as the gods above! This, I command!!” The man behind the helmet groans unintelligibly, as a huffing and puffing Turbo runs up to the toga-wearing demi-god. “Hey, jerkface! This is MY collar!!” “Yours?!” snorts Big Al derisively, “And what, pray tell were you waiting for? An act of Congress?!” Turbo strikes an indignant pose, “I saw him first! I caught him in the act!” “Correct me if wrong, but would not the phrase ‘caught in the act’ entail you actually CATCHING yon varlet?! By my view of things, you had allowed your self-righteous posturing to get in the way of a perfectly suitable bust. Thusly, I was forced to step in and rectify matters.” “He didn’t get away!” whines Turbo, “I was just toying with him!!” “Villains,” states Steele most seriously, “are NOT toys! They are dredges to be purged from city streets! And your tomfoolery nearly allowed another vile culprit to escape to his lair in safety!!” The bad guy groans, slowly getting to his feet and offering out his hands for handcuffs in surrender. He goes largely unnoticed as Turbo gets up in Big Alan Steele’s grill, screeching like a marmot. “Well, MAYBE if SOME people didn’t HOG all the bad guys for himself, then MAYBE I wouldn’t have to get all my entertainment’s worth from one measly computer snatcher in a whole week!!” Big Al is aghast, “And what is THAT supposed to mean?!” “It MEANS you are a villain hog!” “Am not!” “Are too!” “Well MAYBE,” Steele sneers, “if the OTHER heroes were CAPABLE of such tasks, I should not have to pick up their collective slack, tonight being a PERFECT example!!” “Slack? SLACK?! The members of the Hall of Justice are waaaaaay better than You could ever hope to be!” “Is that so?” “Yep.” “Then why do they keep BEGGING for my inclusion in membership? I receive countless phone calls, solicitors, and fan mail from yon Board of Membership?” Turbo’s face turns scarlet with rage as he sputters, bumping Big Al’s chest, “You liar!!” The man in black looks on, wringing his hands nervously at the scene, “Uh, guys?” “A Greek warrior never lies!” “Oh yeah?! Liar, liar, toga’s on fire!!” Steele snickers, “Such a retort, much like a child’s!! Are you a true representative of your caste of hero, for surely I would truly be disinterested in membership if true…” “Hey guys? C’mon now…” “Well, that’s good, because WE DON’T WANT YOU ANYWAYS!!” “Huh,” Snorts Big Al, “Tis not what your elders tell me.” “LIAR!!” as Turbo jabs a finger into Steele’s meaty chest. Big Al swats his finger away with a backhand, “Must we retread this foolish vein in the argument?” The man in black steps forward, making gentle smoothing motions with his hands, “Now guys, let’s just calm down and talk this out rationally here…” The two heroes turn in unison, both pointing their fingers at the bad guy with dangerous looks in their eyes. “YOU STAY OUT OF THIS!!” The man in the motorcycle helmet gulps audibly as he backs away slowly. Turbo jerks a thumb back to his smoking jet-pack, “If this was working, then I’d SHOW you who is the jerkface around here!” “How did your intake valve become clogged? Did your skirt get caught?” “Strong words from a man wearing a dress…” “Then perhaps,” growls Big Al menacingly, “You should repair your device allowing you to alight upon air. I, however, require no such accoutrements.” “You bastard!” cries out Turbo, “Maybe I’ll just do that! I’m sure I can clean this out quick enough…” Turbo whips off the backpack and begins to fiddle with the intake valves, spitting and cursing all the while at Big Al, who grins, cracking his knuckles. The bad guy edges his way over to Big Al, careful not to let Turbo see. He slides up the mirrored glass of the helmet revealing… The Masochist, Big Alan Steele’s partner in crime fighting?! “Hey Al,” hisses the Masochist, “This is the last one tonight. I’m more than a little uncomfortable luring these superheroes into fights with you.” “What?!” whispers Big Al harshly, a bit stunned, “But this is only the third of the evening?!” “Super-villains are one thing, but heroes are another.” “But… I MUST TRAIN!! With all the super-villains lain at my feet in recent days, I am forced to seek proper avenues of training, hence the need for heroic deception! The Frisco PPV gladiatorial combat awaits!” Both look down at Turbo, cackling as he scrapes the chaff from his valve ducts with his gloved hand, the Masochist shaking his head. “Last one.” Big Al pouts, crossing his hands across his chest. “So be it…” “Ah HA!!” screams out Turbo, as he replaces the jet-pack, it roaring to life, “Now we’ll settle this ONCE AND FOR ALL!!” The Masochist rolls his eyes as he replaces the helmet shield. Steele winks at him, before striking up a balanced posture, his fists balled in front of him. “Hi-Keeba!!”
