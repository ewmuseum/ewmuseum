The Life And Times Of Gynaeoco Joe #1

19 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19-03-03 Stardate: 19-03-03 As I record this, I am floating in my ever-present fluidic surroundings. Through the distorted glass I can see this new one, the one that saved me from the darkness. He fiddles with his tadpole shooter repeatedly, while staring at images of others like me. If I was not confined to this godforsaken glass prison with it’s pickling juices, I would accept his tadpoles and fertilise the fastest to become one of them. I have only ever participated in this event a few times previously, and it resulted in my mouth being put back together as the infant forced himself out, careless of the fact my flesh was tearing before their very eyes. Them. They who stared at me for hours on end, watching my pain with their beady eyes, as inch-by-inch that infernal creature made its way into their world from mine, screaming from within. I can still feel the screams within my walls. I came to be approximately three decades ago to the day. As a component of one of those infants, I endured hours upon hours of stress, unable to help in the deliverance. My mother, it turned out was a cavernous creation. Many times she had been stabbed by the evil I fear to speak of. All over the world they crave our taste, those…those…things. To this day I can only determine their species and purpose. Skin snakes, the very same as I see before me, he plays with his, glancing at me occasionally as though with guilt, but always looking back to others like me, on flat sheets of coloured images. His tadpoles are projected high into the air and he catches them in a white billowy object, wiping up the remaining ones before disposing of them. I know not why he does this; I do know however that he and many like him fail to dispose of them when they are trying to attack me, and many like me. This has happened to me at least a dozen times before. Normally when the day is almost done, I will be at peace and calm, when suddenly the ground rumbles and a skin snake invades me, trying to snap at me, for minutes on end when suddenly it spits its tadpoles at me. How rude! Spitting of all things! Evolution could have in the process given skin snakes arms to just hand the tadpoles over, but no evolution gave the skin snakes the power of spitting! No wonder our females are so depressed, males have no manners. Once, when I was preparing the infant months after receiving the tadpoles, a long, thin silver rod protruded into my walls and began poking at the infant! This was outrageous, I had only just gotten done with the arms when this thing attacked it, until it could not go through full development and I had to push it out prematurely, and violently dismembered. My female got so sick of my making infants that she took me to the bright white light man, who cut me open with a sharp silver object and did things to me to prevent my eggs from working again. I still have my arms and egg-producers, but the eggs don’t work. All that remains are lines evident on me from where the sharp thing cut in. Surprisingly, I felt nothing of it. I got very bored after that, making infants was a fun hobby, now I can only look forward to the skin snakes, regardless of their spitting and my hatred for them, although the thing I despise of them most is the big bang. The big bang is like an explosion throughout my entire self. When a skin snake attacks, I try desperately to fight back, tensing my walls and hissing, but if the skin snake hits my special place, I will get really angry, so much so sometimes I have even spit back at the skin snake! Now, in my glass prison I have no need to worry about skin snakes or making infants. I can rest at last. He will take care of me; I feel we have a rapport with one another. He will keep me safe. My female always put me in danger, such as the encounter with the blunt wooden object. My female was jumping up and down on her soft, cushy mattress of dormancy. Surrounding this mattress were four blunt wooden things, for supporting this bed of hers I assumed. Many times my female would jump up and down bare-skinned as the day she was born, protesting her freedom. On one such occasion as she jumped up and down on this mattress, she lost her footing, and landed directly on top of one of the blunt wooden things. No guesses as to who the blunt wooden thing burst through and tore into shredded flesh on landing… I always felt it was very selfish of her to be screaming and excreting liquid from her eyes when I was the one who could barely see my beard in front of me, with red fluid pouring from my mouth, I thought I was going to leave my existence. Thankfully the bright white light men put me back together, and were gracious enough to shave my beard, how nice of them. The smell of red liquid and crusty flesh soon subsided and I was my old self once more! After that incident I wasn’t allowed to play any more, and fighting with the skin snakes was kept to a minimal in case my mouth burst open, to this day it is still fragile. Instead of a skin snake, a smaller, squishier thing would enter my mouth, trying to slurp at my special place, but not quite reaching. I tried to bite back, but eventually had to spit at it to make it stop. I hate skin snakes and flesh worms to this day with a passion. I remember seeing myself for the first time one day. I think my female was ashamed of my ugliness, and so took a glass reflector to me, searching me and getting to know me, while I felt obliged I was amazed myself at my appearance. My beard had turned into a funky goatee, but my lips were perky and rosy as ever. I motioned for a kiss but was denied and had to do with chewing on one of her skin snakes for a while. I liked that one more than the others, it didn’t have a hole and worked my special place just the right way, although I still spit back at the bastard for taking too long. Life with my female had been nothing but pain and pleasure for many years, and from the day I came to be, to the first time I spit up blood, to the time I was cut out and put in my glass prison, I’ve enjoyed being here to say the least. This new one though, this male who thinks he is a female, I like him, life will be fun with him. There are so many aspects of my time here that I could go on to record, but I haven’t time to divulge all of it, for now the man has caged his skin snake behind its fabric cage, and is moving towards me. I think we are going to look for the red man with the painful looking skin he is worried about. I also met my male’s worst enemy in the female with the metal things that do many wonderful things. He thinks that she is like a skin snake’s head, and twice as pungent. For now though I must bid you adieu, for the road ahead is bumpy and I cannot concentrate on my tale, so from one cunt to another, until we meet again, farewell, and Godspeed. The End
