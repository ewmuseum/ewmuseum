Chet King 'The Rap King'

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19th Dec &#039;02 - - - - - - - - - - The scene opens up at Madison Square Garden, New York, where 20,000 screaming fans are waiting in excitement for their favorite rap/hip-hop star to come out onto stage. There is a huge buzz around the arena as the lights dim and running out onto center stage is the most popular MC in music today…Chet King ‘The Rap King’! Chet has come out in a pair of baggy black pants, a white t-shirt and has gold jewelry all over his body. The crowd is in a frenzy over their hip-hop idol. Chet pulls a microphone out of the pockets of his pants and starts to talk to the delight of the crowd. Chet King: Yo, MSG, what’s happening in dis mudda fuckin’ house?!? The crowd pops big for this. Chet King: So who here wants me to bust out some rhymes?- - - - - - - - - - The scene then changes to a hospital, where in a hospital bed with green sheets, we see Chet King ‘The Chat King’ lying with a big white bandage around his head due to the sledge hammer attack given to him by Dante Jackson at the end of Turmoil. Next to Chet is a nurse who’s checking on him. Suddenly Chet starts to mumble to himself much to the shock of the nurse. Chet King: Yo, MSG, what’s happening in dis mudda fuckin’ house?!? The nurse then yells out to a doctor who’s walking past. Nurse: The patients talking, I think he’s waking up. The doctor walks over to Chet to investigate as Chet King continues to mumble. Chet King: So who here wants me to bust out some rhymes? Doctor: Hmm, he hasn’t come too yet. He’s unconsciously talking to himself. - - - - - - - - - - We go back to MSG where Chet King ‘The Rap King’ has been performing some of his most famous hits. In fact we join him half way through his recent hit single, “Bitch named Dante” Chet King: This bitch named Dante thinks he’s a DJBut he’s so shit he don’t even get payHe thinks that he’s some big starBut he’s not, so he might as well give up rap and play guitar The crowd is hanging on Chet’s every word as he continues to rap. Chet King: This bitch named Dante thinks he can fightBut he can’t beat anyone even if he tries with his entire miteHe thinks he’s going to win Continental goldBut he won’t be able to do that when he can’t even perform a fucking wrestling hold- - - - - - - - - - In the hospital we now see that the nurse, and the doctor have pulled up chairs next to Chet’s hospital bed, and are laughing at him as he unconsciously mumbles. Chet King: This bitch named Dante thinks he’s a DJ Doctor: Hahaha! I love it when people make idiots of themselves while they’re unconscious! It’s fantastic! The nurse looks a bit shocked at this comment. Nurse: You shouldn’t be saying that about patients. Doctor: Oh come on Nurse, you know it’s funny. The nurse looks at Chet King. Chet King: But he’s not, so he might as well give up rap and play guitar Nurse: Hahaha, it is pretty funny actually. Chet King: He thinks he’s going to win Continental gold A hospital janitor then walks past Chet King’s hospital bed and hear’s Chet rapping unconsciously to himself. Janitor: What’s going on? Doctor: This fellow was knocked out with a sledge hammer, and now he’s unconsciously turning into the next Justin Timberland. Janitor: A white hip hopper with no skills? Doctor: Yep! - - - - - - - - - - In MSG, Chet has started his next rap “The countdown” Chet King: The clock is tickingIt’s counting downDante Jackson better get ready‘Cos I’m going to make his ass look like a clown The End, Rush, TTT and SnowBetter all be ready to have their asses kicked as well, yo! In the front row screaming girls are shouting out “We love you Chet!” Chet King: Yo dawgs, I love you too!- - - - - - - - - - In the hospital, we now have a crowd of five watching Chet rapping to himself. The crowd includes the doctor, the janitor, the original nurse and another two nurses. Chet King: The clock is ticking Doctor: Hahaha, this is why I became a doctor. I just love seeing patients who have been knocked out come into the hospital and start unconsciously talking to themselves and humiliating themselves in the process. Chet King: Dante Jackson better get ready Janitor: This guy is the worst rapper I’ve ever heard, it’s hilarious! Doctor: I wish I had a video camera so I could play this at the Christmas party. - - - - - - - - - - As the MSG crowd continues to go wild, Chet King enters into one of his classic rhymes. Chet King: Come Sunday Night I’ll be the champWhen I win the match and walk up the rampI’ll be the best the worlds ever seenEven if means having to be mean Because why? Crowd: Because your Chet! Chet King: I can’t hear you dawgs! Crowd: Because your Chet! Chet King: Yo, dats mudda fuckin’ right! - - - - - - - - - - The crowd of five has now grown to a crowd of twenty including nurses, doctors, janitors and patients. Everyone is laughing at Chet as he horribly raps unconsciously to himself. Chet King: Come Sunday Night I’ll be the champ A patient mocks Chet’s lack of skills. Patient: This guy is one of the best the rap game’s ever seen. He’s right up there with 2 Pac, Biggie Smalls, Jam Master Jay, Dr Dre and Dante Jackson! This causes laughter to come from the room. Chet King: I’ll be the best the worlds ever seen Janitor: I’m just waiting for him to give us some “Ice, Ice Baby” Chet King: Yo, dats mudda fuckin’ right! Janitor: Of course I am! This causes more laughter from the room. Chet King suddenly starts to roll around and stops rapping to himself. Doctor: Agh. I think he’s coming around. Sorry folks, shows over. This causes a groan from the people in the room as Chet King stops rolling around and opens his eyes wide. Chet King: Wh…Wh…Where am I? Doctor: You were knocked out by a sledgehammer. You were rushed here as soon as possible, and have been lying here ever since. A look of anger shoots into the eyes. Chet King: Dante will pay for this. Janitor: Not as much as I’d pay to hear you rap some more. Chet King looks genuinely puzzled by this. Chet King: What? Doctor: Don’t worry about him, he just wishes that he could have been at Madison Square Garden with the rest of you adoring fans. The crowd of people laugh at the unknowing Chet King. Chet King: Uh, what? Was I supposed to be wrestling at Madison Square Garden while I’ve been out? Or was I supposed to hold a big chat show there? Because if so, I’ve forgotten all about it. Doctor: No, no. Don’t worry. Just rest now and get over your injury. I’ll explain it all to you later. (Talking to the crowd of people) Come on people, let’s give him some space. The crowd starts to leave the room. Doctor: Oh Chet, I believe there was a request for you to give us a rendition of “Ice, Ice Baby”, so if you could perform that for us later, that’d be fantastic! Chet: What?!? The crowd of people laugh as they leave the room, leaving Chet to wonder what happened while he was knocked out thanks to the sledgehammer shot courtesy of Dante Jackson.
