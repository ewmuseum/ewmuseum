Title: NO, NO, NO FUCK YOU by the most overrated wrestler in PCW. Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 1/24/03 An old oak tree sits in a forest in the middle of Des Plaines, Illinois. The tree is large and has with stood the test of time. You can tell this by the broken bark and snapped branches that linger on its large frame. Inside the old oak tree, live over a million parasites, making the tree their home and meal. The outdoor tempter is frigid as a cold front is sweeping through the Midwest. The actual air tempter is around five degrees, but with the wind chill, it feels like negative ten. The wind is a large factor blowing out of the southeast at thirty miles per hour. The time is seven o’clock PM central standard time. On the TV is a rerun of Law and Order but the tree doesn’t watch TV. It has no eyes nor does it own one. IF the tree were to be cut down it could be used to make a home with a tv in it but it still wouldn’t be able to watch. With this tree’s luck it would probably end up a piece of paper. Paper sort of rivals TV since you can print books on paper and if your reading chances are you not watching TV. Tree’s don’t really think about this sort of thing since they lack a mind. Millions of army ants live beneath the tree and do have brains. But like the tree they can’t watch TV. The picture is just too large for their tiny eyes. As a matter of fact the only thing ants think about is eating and reproducing. Which when you think about, its really kind of sad they don’t watch tv. What good honest American wouldn’t want to eat, reproduce, and watch tv. Ants have three factors of that dream life, too bad their just so damn tiny and can be crushed by the remote. The sky high above the tree is dark. If this were summer it would be alive with light, and birds, but in the Midwest in the winter time the sun goes down by five thirty pm. It’s a cloudy night so the moon really isn’t shining at all lending zero visibility in the dark forest. “What the hell does this have to do with my promo?” Ponders the Malice Man who was recently voted the second most over rated wrestler in the WT. The Malice Man sits in a hotel room in Shanghai, China. The chair he sits upon at the cheap hotel desk actually came from wood from the forest the tree lives in. Duke doesn’t know this and that’s why he sits confused in nothing but white underwear. He is drinking whiskey that was produced in Lynchburg, Tennessee home of Jack Daniels whiskey. The taste is bitter sweet as it was filtered through coal. The carpet in the hotel room is dirty green with zero shag to it. You can tell its been vacuumed more times then Anna Nichol Smith has been fucked. The whole hotel, along with Duke’s room, reeks of Chinese’s food. The funk is unpleasant to the nostrils of Duke. Duke’s nostrils are full of hair, as is most older mans. They say its purpose is to filter out the dust. The body is smart about stuff like that unlike most peoples voting skills. “Fuck” exclaims the Malice Man who hates his promos buried in details. Details are the current trend in the WT and if one doesn’t partake they risk being voted one of the most over rated wrestlers around. With the PPV tomorrow and time for only one, maybe two promos Duke is in a bit of a jam. There is no way his overrated body can produce enough promos to pull a win off. You see in the business wins general come to the person who produces the most details. When Duke started in this little hobby over seven years ago. The feds where made up of wrestling fans and people who liked to trash talk. Over the years things have changed and the feds have filled up, but not with wrestling fans, rather geeks who like to write. Duke’s writing skill are sub par and since nobody engages in debate, insults, or any kind of interactive behavior he often fails. Lucky for him his opponent this Sunday is Xavier Kannon who still interacts in his promos. One of the few good guys left. “Don’t complement that dirty fuck. The guy shit in my soup and now he’s going to pay.” Yells the repetitive, uncreative, foul-mouthed, character. It no wonder he was voted the second most over rated wrestler in the WT. Duke gives me the writer the finger as he sits there red faced. Thoughts of his big match tomorrow, retirement, pussy, and Rolaids fill his mind. Can he accomplish what none of the assholes who filled out the prediction thread for shanghaied say he can’t and beat Kannon yet again? The thought of a hard thought fight actually puts butterflies in his stomach as he scratches his balls through his cotton briefs. “Ah fuck it no matter what anybody says I’m going to send his ass whiskey bent and hell bound.” Blabbers the overrated, has been, Duke.
