Origin - Primetime Wrestling Academy

15 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15th April 2003 From the cesspool that was the now defunct Dark Wrestling Alliance the Jester in making has finally stepped from the shadows and prepares to apply his trade within the Primetime Wrestling Academy. The first steps on the yellow brick road, but unlike that infamous cobbled path this road doesn’t lead to some Emerald City but another myth in Primetime Championship Wrestling. The Academy has attracted some of the biggest names in the industry and is the catalyst for those clawing their way to the top. In life nothing is free and neither is Primetime Wrestling Academy as those lucky enough to be inducted are expected to show both commitment and determination while attending under the microscope of ‘The Powers That Be’. PWA as it is affectionately known has made and broken some of the most high-profiled names to ever grace the circuit and it was a case of past history being nothing more than a memory. New Blood would merely be a couplet of two words rather than the man who once adorned both the Dark Wrestling Alliance’s Hardcore and Light Heavyweight Championships. From the moment the tuition started there was no New Blood, Gareth Green was a clean slate and like many before him the time had come to apply a gimmick to his arsenal of tricks and tricks being the operative word. And so the era of the Jester was born. Enough talk of the little fish gracing the predatory pond for the Jester’s importance back then was microscopic and his name adorning his class roster was just that, a name. Tutors had come and gone but the fundamentals of the Academy always have and always will remain the same and that was for people to gain an insight into the business from their own experiences. The Academy offered the opportunity to fine-tune a person’s character to a point where development was no longer necessary and tenures change from one person to the next. It was a case of adapting. A tried and tested formula for the greats had once walked this very same path, individuals like Primetime Championship Wrestling’s alpha male Xavier Kannon and his Old School Entertainment counterpart Ivan Stanislav. With a persona taken onboard and his egotistical attitude quenched the Jester was ready to be schooled by the best of the best and was shipped into a class overlooked by RUSH World Champion Cassius Deamonte. The World Champion had picked up the reigns from his predecessor and the Jester was inducted into his flock. Deamonte the Shepard however wasn’t there long but long enough to bare witness to the Emerald Menaces first taste of in-ring competition as the Jester duelled in a Triple Threat Match. Not as memorable as his first taste of Primetime Championship Wrestling gold for the verdict was that of a loss; in truth the Jester had been out-classed by fellow student Charlotte, a name that has since vanished into obscurity. The parable, ‘If at first you don’t succeed, try, try again’ really applied here and not feeling disheartened the Original Prankster continued to shape and mould under his tutor. Not much in the way of significance really happened in the weeks that followed. Grades were the personified ticket out and they began to tally but deep down his desire to jump class had began to swamp his body like a plague for Hessian was surely tutoring the next hot prospects to infect the Wrestle Online community. Old School Wrestling’s goliath Hessian was busy grooming talent like the mobster Angelo Deville, the astronomer Dorn Tracer and the vampire Nascosto Ireul Nerezza who plays an important role in the career of the fledgling Jester. The Crimson Angel Angelo Deville was the man making all the headlines and he alongside the equally talented Dorn Tracer was invited to participate in the resurrection of Five Star Wrestling (FSW). Now was the time to strike, with the departure of Hessian’s hand groomed protégés an opening had materialised and the Jester was only too willing to fill the void. But it wasn’t to be. However another loop hole had came to light offering the Jester a chance to limbo under the legal red tape and make the desired transition from Deamonte’s class to that of Hessians. An announcement was made that Old School Wrestling (Now Entertainment) would be staging a sixteen tag team event with the winner being offered a luxurious contract with the company. Originality was being highly looked upon and the offer indeed extend to Academy students so the only remaining snag was that of finding a suitable partner. Lady Luck was shining down on the Lord Of Laughter during this part of his miniscule career for adorning Hessian’s class was the behemoth Nascosto Ireul Nerezza, the man once known as Double MJ. The Mercenary Of Satan then went to the leading authority of the Academy El Diablo and arranged the transfer of Gaz Green thus placing together the Jester and Vampire collective together. Like the birth of Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green, the Academy inadvertently opened the Pandora’s box that is Pranks With Attitude. The initial creation of what would be the most dominant team of Primetime Championship Wrestling’s Third Age. One would assume that the team of Pranks With Attitude would waltz into Old School Wrestling’s sixteen-team tournament but assumption supposedly is the mother of all fuck ups, it certainly applied in this case. P.W.A, named so in tribute to the Academy could only warrant a brief mention on the live radio broadcast of Malice before having their hopes shattered as the teams were announced. Fellow student’s Redemption Song had shone enough to earn a placing but not the Jester and Vampire collective and as a goodbye gesture the two parties were booked against one another in what would be both teams final matches as Primetime Wrestling Academy students. Not really a benchmark but Pranks With Attitude left the Academy with their heads held high as the Conniving Collective took their biggest scalp to date. P.W.A had defeated the brotherly Indian and Cowboy combination before accepting their fate that had been left in the hands of the community’s ultimate power, The Phantom Booker. Like Angelo Deville and Dorn Tracer, the Jester and his Vampire accomplice were shipped off to another resurrecting promotion in New True Internet Championship Wrestling, a name that would send a shiver down the spine of the sturdiest upper-lipped Brit. Another story for another time… - To be continued -
