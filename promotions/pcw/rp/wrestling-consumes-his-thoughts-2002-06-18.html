Wrestling Consumes His Thoughts

18 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-18-02 [Fade In] [Hotel door, room 112 swings open and there stands ‘Scandalous’ Ryan ‘Streak’ Stephens of PCW notoriety. He’s wearing mesh shorts and WWE sponsored Tough Enough 2 T-shirt. His hair his uncombed and he’s wearing no socks with his Adidas running shoes. Stephens has always been one to run to clear his mind, and relax. He’s loved running since his high school days when he ran cross-country and admired Steve Prefontaine. It always comes down to the self-satisfaction you get from it Stephens once said.] [As he opens the door a good aroma flows through the air in the room straight to his nose. The shot leaves Stephens and goes to the inside of the room where his wife stands over the stove in the room. Stephens can see from his spot in the doorway that his wife has cooked him lunch. The bar connected to the kitchen is set nicely with hotel plates and silverware. Stephens steps into the hotel room and shuts the door behind him. His wife Dana looks up and him and smiles.] Dana: How was your run baby? Ryan: Refreshing. [He intended on coming home and writing some ideas he thought about while running down so that he could put them on his web site, but he can see that Dana has gone through a lot of trouble making him dinner. There are pea hulls in the trashcan along with aluminum foil used to help the baked potatoes sitting out cook. There’s a wrapper that once housed chicken fried steak she fried; his favorite food by the way. Cooking utensils are strewn about the counter top, and some have already been tossed in the sink.] Ryan: Baby, this all looks so nice. What’s the occasion? Dana: Well, you’ve just been working with your new site so much, that I fill like I haven’t got to really talk to you lately. I figured I’d cook us a nice lunch and we could eat it, and talk about anything you want except wrestling, and that new web site of yours. Ryan: Sounds like a very fair deal baby. Should I shower up first? [She looks him over. He’s fairly sweaty from his morning run.] Dana: Nah, it’s okay baby. I already have all the food ready and there’s no sense in letting it get cold. Let’s just eat and you can shower up later. [He nods and goes over behind the counter and reaches his right hand up partially on his wife’s neck and partly on her cheek with his fingers straddling her ear, and kisses her gently on the lips. She stares into his eyes, and smiles at him; she loves him something awful. When they dated back in high school she loved him so much that he tolerated her, very nicely though, instead of dating all the girls he wanted to.] Ryan: This looks great baby, let’s eat it! [Stephens walks over to the pitcher of tea his wife has prepared and pours both he and she a glass of iced tea. He then sits the glass next to the plates, and picks up his wife’s plate and hands it to her. She takes the plate from him and puts on it one piece of chicken fried steak, a small helping of peas, half of a baked potato, half of a corn on the cob, and a thin slice of cornbread. Stephens gets much larger helpings of everything and they sit down at the bard and begin eating. As Ryan bites into a piece of chicken fried steak Dana smiles her cute little smile at him and says.] Dana: Well baby, does it even compare to your mama’s? Ryan: [with a mouthful so that it’s somewhat muffled] Yes, it’s very good! Thank you so much for cooking such a fine meal. [She nods in order to accept his thank you and laughs and his muffled talk. She then puts her fork down and looks at him. She has that look on her face that she wants to say something, and yet she doesn’t know if it’s the right time. She decides to say it anyhow.] Dana: Baby, I’ve been thinking. [she hesitates a little as Ryan listens carefully] I think it’s time we get out of this hotel and find a house, especially now that you’re earning the extra cash. [They haven’t talked about buying a house lately, and she doesn’t know what Ryan’s reaction will be. He stops shoving the food down is mouth long enough to reply.] Ryan: I couldn’t agree with you more baby. Perhaps this weekend we can fly down to Texas and begin looking for a nice house. [A huge smile lights up Dana’s face and she jumps out of her seat and hugs her husband tightly around the neck and plants a big one on his cheek.] Dana: Thank you Ryan! I was hoping you were ready to get out of this hotel also. Ryan: I am, it will be nice to get settled in a nice house, especially when I have weeks, or even a couple of days off. Dana: I couldn’t agree with you more. Oh it’s going to be so great. We can find a nice country home on top of a hill with lots of land. Ooh, and I want a big bathroom with a whirlpool. Ryan: We’ll find something nice that suits the both of us, but you know I’m not going to purchase a massive house that will be too much room for the both of us. It’s better to save our money in case I ever get injured or something. Dana: Don’t even say that baby! And I know you don’t want a massive house, just something nice with a few luxuries is all I’m saying. Ryan: Well of course Dana! [Ryan feels like the situation has been settled and picks up his corn on the cob, flowing with butter, and sprinkled with pepper, and begins to knaw on the cob. Dana neatly scoops some peas upon her fork with her cornbread and puts them in her mouth. Everything she does is so proper.] [Time passes as the two enjoy their meal and talk about things such as the new house they plan to get, how their families are doing back in Huntsville, old times, and every other thing besides wrestling. As Stephens talks about those things though, there is a tiny wheel turning near the back of his head thinking about his match tonight.] [He’s prepared himself very well, and he talked to Thunder who hasn’t cut a promo since last Wednesday, but claims he’s prepared as well. The Supreme Machine, being the beast he is should be ready, but Stephens is unsure. Part of him feels that the Phantom Phive is struggling, and though he isn’t ready to admit it, he can feel it within. What is he to do? A win against the Political Machine tonight would be excellent, but what if Thunder and SuMa aren’t prepared, he won’t have it in him to defeat three men by himself. Not even Big Alan Steele is that good.] [He continues on talking to his wife, but now Makonie lingers in his mind. Whose is Ryan Stephens he thinks? Stephens laughs within, he doesn’t want his wife to know he’s thinking about wrestling, but she knows him well she can probably tell, but will refrain from saying anything because he’ll merely deny it, and they might argue, something they rarely do. Stephens thinks to himself, if Makonie wants to think I’m some lower card nobody then so be it, it will make him look a lot stupider if I prevail on Fusion. And what’s with this trial thing? It’s as if it was just recently resurrected or something. You’d think we would’ve heard about this trial way back during Makonie’s NITCW days had it been very important. I mean he was accused of killing this man a year ago, and just now, just now, a year later when the trial is coming to he’s going to start worrying about it. That’s just kind of bizarre to me, Stephens thinks.] [Finally after cleaning both their plates Ryan leans over and kisses his wife on the cheek.] Ryan: Thank you so much for lunch. It was great to have a good home cooked meal. I’ll help you clean up before I jump in the shower. Dana: Nonsense baby, I got it. You go ahead and jump in the shower. You should take a nap or something to relax before your match. Ryan: You went through all this trouble to cook such a lovely meal, I insist that you let me help you clean all this up. [She nods, and the two begin clearing the dishes, and carrying them over to the hotel sink as we fade to black.]
