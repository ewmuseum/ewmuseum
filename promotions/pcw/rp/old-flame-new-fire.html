Old Flame New Fire

Date: 8-2302 RP1T Last week of August not only means the beginning of the last days of summer. The last week of August for the small town of Crossville Tennessee means the arrival of the State Fair. Thousands possible millions of dollars are wasted on gate admission, games, food, and expensive ride tickets. Teresa remembers as a girl, she would visit her grandparents during this week before school started. They would take her to the fair, buy her a candied apple, and she would ride the rides till her twenty dollars on tickets would run out.Tonight though, Teresa is at the Cumberland County Fair alone. Both her grandparents died when she was thirteen. The smell of funnel cakes, sounds of the games being played in the booths and the warm air on her face reminds her of these times. This isn’t a flashback, but yet a new chapter in Teresa’s life and a fresh start of a new future. This future begins with a part of her past, Jack Slayter. When Teresa first started the wrestling industry, she began in an indy fed as a manager. That’s where she met Jack. He was a tall man, larger than most, with bedroom blue eyes and shoulder length blonde hair with a tint of red. He was the first she managed and Teresa helped him to championship titles more times than one. With time, their professional relationship grew to a personal one. Like another redneck we all know, Jack went home to Texas without notice and never returned to the business. Now it’s Teresa’s turn to be in the spotlight. She has made it to one of the biggest feds around and now it is her turn to show the world just whom the hell Teresa Tomas really is. Tonight, the State Fair is hosting a Monster Mania monster truck rally. All the top ranking trucks of the local television show are performing a two-hour free style event. And you can bet your bottom dollar that everyone and their uncle in Crossville are crammed in the stands like a can of tuna. Some of the younger fans are holding models of their favorite truck. The older fans are praising the performance of their favorite trucks. Inside the outdoor stadium, about 8 trucks take their turns to strut their stuff and sling mud everywhere. Teresa walks to the small stadium just as the drivers and crew are cleaning up and getting ready to leave for the night. Mud is speckled all over ringside and empty cups and candy wrappers are scattered throughout the bleachers. Wearing baggy pair of overalls and elbow length camouflage shirt, Teresa appears to be one of the locals by first glance. The employees cleaning the mess doesn’t seem to take much heed to where she is going. Once she reaches the end of the first row of bleacher, she jumps the guardrail away and walk toward the back parking lot where the truck will be loaded for hauling. Jack is leaning against the nose of a one of the semis smoking a cigarette while four men work to clean Teaser . He was last to go into the large mud pit and just parked her for washing. Jack is wearing the typical attire a Nascar driver would wear though he is a monster truck driver. He is wearing a yellow outfit with the Teaser logo along with Monster Mania. An Ace of Spades card covers his back. The card resembles his nickname, ‘Ace’. Jack notices Teresa approaching him before taking one last puff of the cancer stick, then dropping it to the pavement and killing the fire with the toe of his snake skin boots. “Teresa, What a surprise. How the hell are you?” “I’m doing quite well actually. Jack, we need to talk. Let’s find a quieter place. Teresa holds the same seriousness in her face as is heard in her voice. She motions Jack to follow then leads him under a large tree not far from the parking area. She leans her back against the tree and slides her hands in her front pockets. Jack stops in front of her resting the palm of his hand over her head. “This is business Jack, so don’t go jumping the gun.” Teresa steps out from under Jack’s arm and he lowers it then crosses both arms over his chest watching her with a semi confused, semi curious look. Teresa continues. “I’ve been promoted again Jack. I need someone to travel with me, help me with my luggage and just keep me company on the long trips between shows. How would you like to accompany me? As these words slip from her lips, Teresa wonders what she is doing. It is still up in the air whether or not her and Scott Taylor are still together. She knows he would be furious when he finds out that she has another man traveling with her that isn’t an employee of PCW, especially his long time rival, Jack ‘The Ace’ Slayter. Since Scott is in OSW and Teresa is in another fed, the distance and constant travel has put a huge strain on their relationship. In fact, the last her and Scott spoke, it was the last time she heard or seen of him. His confused and scared expression on his face left its mark in her memory after she attacked him that night. Jack raises an eyebrow a bit surprised at Teresa’s suggestion. There is the question of who will drive Teaser but there are alternates which can take his place behind the wheel when he isn’t able. He looks down at Teresa almost feeling sorry for the poor girl. She has worked so hard to get where she’s at just to be left near the top with no one. Deep inside, he never really stopped caring for her, but he is too proud to admit it. Jack releases a deep breath and slips his black Stetson off his head. He uses his other hand to scratch his head before placing the cowboy hat back on. “Tell you what Teresa. I’ll take you on your offer, not because I’m not taking advantage of the situation to get closer to you, but because you don’t need to be alone. I’ve taken care of you before, I can do it again.” A warm breeze passes over the two brushing Teresa’s bangs across her forehead as she looks up to the 6’1 245lb man standing before her. A smile sneaks across her face before the seriousness expression returns. Instead of giving him a hug and thanking him like any other woman does, Teresa simply nods before walking past him. “Thank you Jack. It’s appreciated. Meet me at the Ramada Inn early Friday morning and we’ll depart from there. Have a good one.
