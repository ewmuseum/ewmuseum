What IS an alliance?

21 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/11/02 Alliances. I don't know what that means to you. Alliance, to me, means a temporary co-existence for mutual gain. An alliance is not a friendship. Once the whole dance is over, you're back at each other's throats. Since I entered PCW, I've forged a few Alliances of my own. Considering my original plan was eradication of these lesser beings, I must say that I've become attached to some. Then again, there are others who are simply... alliances. The first one is that woman Gaz likes... the wife of that little slug, Alexander Miyagawa. I trust her about as far as I can be thrown BY her. She's all over Gaz, forever trying to manipulate him. I don't see anything in her at all. She's not a looker at all and her dress sense is... well, less than to my taste. We don't see eye to eye at all but rather than deal with her, I'll let Gaz keep her. She's proven useful once, but only once. Maybe we'll hold onto her until we lose those titles again. At least I'll have an excuse to get rid of her then. Skitzo... he's another one I call an ally. But I like him... he's rather chaotic. It's nice to know that I'm not the only person in two minds about things. He's got that little... SPARK... the one like Gaz has. That desire to cause confusion and sow so much misery has been quite a meal for me. I've tried to look into his head, but it's like trying to read the license plate of a car while being in the middle of the road. So much going on in there, I can't pick out a single thought. Vilas, on the other hand, is the dark horse of Team Bastardos. I can see inside his head as clear as a window. He doesn't care for the team. All he cares about is that El Romantico person... a pity, really. Perhaps if he kept his eyes looking around instead of narrowing his vision to only El Romantico, he'd see what else is coming. He distances himself from the rest of the team, he drinks neither Gatorade nor Rezz Bull. He refuses to join us when we address the audience most of the time. The definition and personification of 'ally' if there ever was one. And like most allies, once the battle is over... Of course, there are those I'd prefer to get closer to and become allies with. The quartet of other non-humans, the Celestials. They fascinate me. How blind was I to not even notice that one of them was among us... and the goal that they could have brought me was right underneath my nose the whole time. The End... despite our only collision being at a house show where I took a move from him, I named it after him out of respect more than to rub salt in the wounds. Cyclonica and I met the one time where she mistook me for that other one... I felt both insulted and underappreciated when she addressed me as 'Vampir'. If nothing else, I wish to redeem myself and prove to her that I'm so much more than that piece of ash. And the man himself, Akiyama... the man who symbolizes an entire universe of negativity. I must confess, once, I wish that I might raise my hand to him to shake his, rather than to block a punch. And also, there are those I NEVER want to be allied with. The vile, selfish and downright disgusting Duke Williams. The man who calls himself a goth and even classes me as one... I HATE goths. Hypocrites who say they dislike conformity yet try to get as many facial piercings as they can to keep up with the trend... the ones who force their music and depressing ways on everyone else like some dark missionaries. The ones who adhere to a dress code of black and makeup... how moronic. They merely re-enforce their own stereotypes and alienate themselves. How I despise them. And how I despise their avatar in the form of Duke Williams. Even that man, though, has a superior. Higher in command and lower in my eyes, that blasphemer, Kannon. Rambling about his Hubbard and pretending that some form of holiness has been endowed on him, preaching his 'Scientology' and using it as a cheap excuse for righteousness... how disgusting. At least, as far as I know, Vampires have no organized religion. No rules to follow, no ideals... freedom. Perhaps, one day, I might get close enough. Past the bodyguards, past the security, past the Front Office's door, past his own fist... and then, in one instant, show him what life would be like from MY point of view as I inject my doctrine through his neck and into his very heart and veins... what a grand moment it would be. Maybe, in days to come, I'll change my views on these 'Alliances'. Who knows, I may end up pinning someone under Kannon's command. But one thing is for sure. As much as I hate to admit it, Gaz is no ally. And just like each year, Alliances will come and go... just like each day, allies will come and go.I'll make sure that if the apocalypse ever happens under my terms and the earth is filled with vampires, Gaz will remain the last human alive alongside my throne.
