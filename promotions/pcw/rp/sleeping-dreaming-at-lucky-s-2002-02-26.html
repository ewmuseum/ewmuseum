...sleeping, dreaming, at Lucky's...

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26.02.02 'Time is essential coming down to the final seconds, and when it's done, you know you're a champion when you're arm is rasied.' CII: All The Fair Games Lucky's SupermarketMilwaukee, Wisconsin2:30 PM Tired and fatigued wasn't my thing from a hectic flight from New York to Milwaukee for my Television title match against Isawa Miyagawa tonight. Barely could I sleep on the plane realizing just what my destiny is: to win a championship. Not just a championship, but many championships. Start building up Trojan~! franchises across the globe and even becoming head of Trojan~! all together. In order to become the head of the company, I have to defeat a sound opponent in Trojan~! Man. I've heard from some reliable sources, that he is maybe better than Isawa Miyagawa, but that's only rumor after all. Our rented car, a 99' smoke gray colored Lincoln Town Car drove though the thick fog of the Milwaukee air as we crossed through a bridge which became almost unseen thanks to the thick fog, I believe our driver barely could keep the car steady and almost swerve off of the bridge and into the ocean. But somehow, he kept it steady while I slept, keeping it out of my mind. We stopped at a nearby Supermarket for supplies since the meal on the flight was not even close to adequate. Risk everything you have over good food and then you can come talk to me about plane service. Lucky's was a fabulous market, stacked rows of different products, its on cheese section being that Wisconsin is the leading producer of cheese and a built in arcade complex to enjoy kids. As the car pulled up into the supermarket parking lot, I couldn't shake it from finish. The dream you idiot, what you thought I was still on some old shit? I'm not Miyagawa and guess what when it's done tonight, I am leaving with the Television title, and there isn't a thing Miya can do about it. I can see the two sides of myself, the good and the evil looking over my shoulder each in appropriate costume of heaven and hell. Each one has a innervision of what your future is Miya and really, that isn't my job to picture to understand if you do Alexander. Each one wants you to believe something and now right now heaven wants you to give the belt to someone else, just to be a nice little jobber, which is your actual day job ain't it Miya? Yeah, and that little guy with the horns on the other side of you, he's telling you just about the sane thing and that is beat Maxwell within an inch of his life. In this case, unlike others...he's wrong. Not that I doubt Miya, or Jeremy Howard but really should they be a benefactor towards what I do inside of that ring? Back to the story at hand though, don't want to be another Kano or Chase. As the doors came open, I could hear an overhead voice stating something. The intercom here was pretty placed over a bullhorn because someone came in a few weeks ago and blew out the speakers with the song Defy You by the Offspring. As they would say on the Simpson's DOH~! As we walked, Amy looked around and tapped me on my shoulder ganaring my attention while I kept my eye on finding somewhere conspicuous so they wouldn't find me asleep. Jesus, sometimes like I stated way back when I first faced Miyagawa that sleep can either come from three things: boredom, exhaustion, Miyagawa himself. Amy continued her look for pamphlets on child birth, while the limo driver continued his looking at dirty magazines in the reader's section. He pulls out the pull out poster and gawks at it as if it were pure gold. I'm not going to say who was in the picture, but all I can say is, he was looking pretty deep into Crack Whore Magazine... Soon I crashed out, sleep right inside of the empty aisles in which there were no products to shill and just emptiness inside. It felt like a desert, remote and complete in isolation from everyone else- same with Miyagawa's childhood. CIII: Dream Sequence It was dark, and I was sitting inside of a moving vehicle something with a loud V6 engine and a silent driver. Maybe it was because I barely could even make out why I was even here in the first place but maybe it was more needed than usual to dream before a big match. No bigger than the Superbowl or anything else, but I was exhausted from everything- competition, the new baby on the way. Way too much pressure to be putting on my mind and that's where I found myself on the backseat with my legs sprawled out and my feet kicking the door. 'Where in the hell are we?' I asked the driver of the vehicle. But he responded in complete silence, causing me to shiver a little bit. I asked him again, 'Dude, answer me.' He turned his head in some sick fashion coming straight out of Evil Dead and his face, was just his eye balls and a red shadowed over his face, something I've seen before as in the demons inside of me but it wasn't them. 'Look's like I finally have you.' He told me with a evil German kick at the end. I could see a swastika tattoo on his right bicep and on his deep green uniform. His teeth showed, clean white. Maybe I was inside another dream, or this was the Red Skull from those Captian America comics. 'Have me where?' I asked him slightly, I could feel a tight pull against my jaw as I stretched it out just to prove I could talk. 'Inside of a car you America idiot!' I usually would give a no shit Sherlock answer to this type of question, but in history books I never realized how stupid, yet brillant the Germans where and Red Skull was no different. 'Are you plain enough to see that I am going to get out of this situation?' I told him, using more bravado than a Terminator movie. That guy is bad ass. 'Silence American infindel. For I shall string you up and the blood of America shall be dashed and the history books shall rewrite us as the victor's!' He gave an evil cartoon villian grin afterwards, he was another Osama Yo Momma Bin Laden, and once again America had to defeat Germany. 'This is SO lame.' I muttered under my breath. 'What? Are you calling Germany...lame?' He asked me as I chuckled, I knew he was reeling on the hook from this moment on. 'Do you know a Anaheim wrestler by the name of Alexander Isawa Miyagawa?' I asked him, a repeat preformance of double question. Kinda of something you would usually find in cartoon moments. 'Yes...and I regret ever believing in him.' he told me in a low voice afterwards. I couldn't believe he even believed in Miyagawa in the first place as my eyes looked in the aware, and big fashion, like I was a little child in the candy store. 'Why did you believe in the first place?' I asked him, something was not right for a strong German warrior in this man to break down over a Japo American wrestler whose spit more lies then Ken Lay at a Enron meeting. 'Once, he was something.' 'And now he's nothing so you gotta let that fade away from your memories man. I had that once, and look where it got me, jobbing to The Rush.' I chuckled, he chuckled to for we were sharring a common bond. Until...he brought up Captian America. I could see the man, ripped muscles, with the colors of our freedom on his chest, he bleeds America and I'm not going to doubt it for a second. 'QUACK!' A duck popped out of nowhere and landed on the windshield of the car. Red Skull swerved around slightly and shook the bird off before looking back at me with fear in his eyes. 'I fear them too.' he said. 'Who?' I asked him, thinking it was Captian America but a much larger group at stake. He sighed as he clutched the steering wheel with his palms and it happened without a plea with his words and tone of manner. 'Wildlife Foundations for environmental crimes.' 'ENVIRONMENTAL CRIMES?! You're the Red Skull for crying out loud and you're scared of a freaking wildlife foundation? Isn't this for believing in Miyagawa?' He cries out in shame. 'Yes.' I just looked at him with shame, more shame than what you would usually find looking at Miyagawa or Jeremy Howard. Really if you're Captian America right now, you're telling yourself that when Red Skull gets back to his sensess then the battle between us will be easier. 'Ok, any place where I can just leave and get away from just a pathetic loser like yourself? Oh wait, here comes Captian America.' Just as soon as I said his name, the driver side window shattered within the impact of a foot going through it. One I saw the red, white and blue, I knew who it was. Red Skull stopped the car as he felt a tight grip along his throat and was suddenly jerked away from the car and thrown onto the slippery bridge. America looked at him sternly as he brought up his fist and begin smashing into the face of Red Skull. His cries for help were suddenly cut off as I continued everything my boyhood reading brought. America stood atleast 3 inches taller than myself and his muscles looked strengthed by every blow he landed on the body of the German mercenary. I wondered if Red Skull would even put up a fight as authorities soon came after, the wail of the poilce siren became a mainstay in my life and I guess it's no different from childhood to adulthood. As the cops dragged Red Skull into the patrol car, I could hear him whimpering for all his emotions could hold. He continued his muttering of words, like Mojo Jojo from the Powerpuff Girls, he repeat himself over and over again not saying the same thing, but meaning it. 'Miyagawa ruined my life. His lameness has caused the catastrophe to occur. I loss my childhood thanks to him. Miyagawa has dammed me.' That was more whinning than many of a Jeremy Howard or Jessica when they get angry or perturbed at something that anyone of their enemies had done. It was one of their lack of integrity towards somethings and if it wasn't for Kid Wonder then Jeremy would be, just a liar and maybe own impotence caused Snow to turn on him, Hinkle to take away a child, and his life to be so screwed up. America walked over to me, I could see he wanted to me the man who was talking to Red Skull, who wasn't on his team and survived. I could tell by the look on his face, nothing but pure determination and no trust for myself. 'So you're the man who survived Red Skull huh?' He told me, a smile showed on his stone chin as everything became more relxaed after that. 'Yeah, that's me.' I told him, with a cocky grin. America was smiling from ear to ear and I couldn't believe I was meeting Captain America in the flesh, not really carring if it was a dream or not. 'And you're facing Isawa Miyagawa and Jeremy Howard on Tuesday and Sunday night right?' 'Yeah Steve, and right now if you understand it...I'll defeat both of those liars and fakes and become champion on two separate nights. So guess what? They're screwed and I'm living in glory.' 'Victory; how's that working for ya?' 'Great Steve, and now I must head back, for a have that match with Miyagawa to take care of. Just remember one thing my friend.' I told him. 'What?' he asked me. 'Red Skull is a little snub and whenever you mention the word of Miyagawa to him, he cracks.' 'Thanks, I'll keep that in mind.'he said before walking off and back to his co-horts in the Justice League who also analyzed the crime scene. Yes, these were the secret heroes of the country and the world, and one evil dooer off of the streets is good enough for me. Maybe I can clean up crime from wrestlers like Miyagawa and Jeremy Howard for I don't think they represent truth, dignity, and the American way! I mean, they don't wrap it up and I bet you they are part of the ugly children cause. I am not letting Miyagawa parade my television with his lameoisty and his unproper use of condoms! You don't eat them Miya! I guess I'll once again prove to Howard that when you call a bluff from yourself, you tend to not show up at all and when I'm a double champion...I'll be the best! (Insert Evil Laugh) Well after waking up, I find myself right now getting ready for Miyagawa and Jeremy Howard Sunday night at March Madness. Seems like with them, you only get old and dry in the talent pool. Time to go get me a Television title my friends. 41 days ain't coming.
