The Tank 30 - Answers and Answers

4 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 4, 2002 ﻿*********The Tank —30—********* Answers and Answers Budding like a flowerWilting like decay ~Leigh Donovan Monday, September 30 - Day 88 “So nigger,” says Darryl Johnson cautiously as the Tank’s most dangerous man enters his cell -to stay, “how’s your... you know...” “Go fuck yourself, motherfucker!” snaps J-Thug, glaring at his new cellmate. He then gesturesacross to the other side of the cell balcony where Leigh Donovan is standing calmly, lookingdown over the main floor of the Unit. “Do I see what I think I see on that motherfucker?” Darryl Jarvis squints as he peers across and notices the swastika tattoo’ed on the youngAustralian. “Yeah,” he nods, “the motherfucker’s in league with the Nazis now. One of HarveyBlake’s boys.” J-Thug scowls. “Things are getting out of hand!” he says. “Motherfucking Nazis are gettingmore powerful every fucking minute. Little bitches like Leigh Donovan joining up with him andacting like a bad motherfucker? Naw! Fuck that! That ain’t gonna ride with me.” “Don’t forget,” Jarvis interrupts, “he started the Unit E riot too. We lost a shitload ofmotherfuckers up in that.” “Don’t worry,” J-Thug smiles evilly, “I haven’t forgotten shit.” Wednesday, October 2 - Day 90 “Where the fuck do you keep that notebook?” Tim McDonald mutters to himself, though hiswords are directed at his bullish Italian cellmate, Vincent Giovanni. “What aren’t you tellingme, you fat fucker?” The Scottish descendent leader of the Tank’s Italian mafia gang continues to rumble throughVincent Giovanni’s personal belongings in search of the small notebook that the large Italianseemed so adamant about keeping private. All of McDonald’s instincts, which have a fantastictrack record for their accuracy, are saying that the bull he has for a cellmate is much moredangerous than he would have the prisoners of Unit B believe. And of course, McDonald is still haunted by the notion that he recognizes Vincent Giovannifrom somewhere on the outside. If only he could place the bulldog face and the oxen body. Quickly, Tim rummages through the drawer below Giovanni’s bunk - nothing. He checks beneath the sheets - nothing. “Fuck!” he exclaims, collapsing on Giovanni’s lower bunk. It’s hopeless, he decides. Giovanniprobably tossed the notebook when Tim walked in on him writing in it a few nights back... ormaybe... he just found a more clever way to keep his notes - Tim McDonald spots a copy ofHomer’s *** jutting awkwardly out of Giovanni’s open drawer. “So Vincent,” McDonald smiles ti himself, “lets see if you’re really into this Greek shit or if youhave other motives.” Tim flips through the book, searching for any suspicious markings Giovanni may have made. He finds nothing until he comes to the story of Polyphemus. There, taped to the opening page ofthe story, is a small square sheet of paper. In hen-scratch handwriting, it reads... Harvey Blake —> Trigger man, Likely co-ordinator Jason Snow ----> Hired guard. Sell out. Possibly co-trigger man. Tim McDonald ----> ??? Tim stares at the sheet of paper for a long moment, not quite sure of what it means. “What thefuck are you up to, Vinny?” he says quietly. And then, in that short moment, something clicks. “Vinny...” the name has a booming meaning inside Tim McDonald’s busy brain. “Holy shit!” Wednesday, October 2 - Day 90 Every push up brings him closer to the PCW Championship. But after a pair of strenuous hoursof an exhausting work out, every push up also brings him closer to regurgitation. But if that’s what it takes, that’s what Jason Snow will do. Since coming to the Tank, physical dominance has been Snow’s primary objective. He’s gainedthirteen pounds in the last three months, and now enters the ring at a bulky two hundred and fourty-five pounds. And now, just days away from possibly making history at Red October, Jason Snowis focussed on staying disciplined. Every thought has turned toward winning a fourth PCW Championship - a record amount. As hepushes himself up off the floor of his cell, his face contorted and strained, he can’t see the wallsthick concrete walls in front of him. Jason Snow sees thousands of screaming fans, a ring, and aslew of opponents, including his most hated El Diablo, who he plans to show no mercy on. “Snow,” a voice breaks his concentration as he pushes himself up one final time, his arms andshirtless back rippling under the exhaustive strain. He allows himself to collapse on his chest, wherehe pulls his long sweaty hair aside to see who’s speaking to him. “What the fuck do you want?” he asks, seeing that the other man in his cell is the bleached blondTim McDonald. “We’ve got to talk,” McDonald says. “Can’t talk,” Snow responds as he rolls onto his back. “Red October’s coming this weekend. I’vegot to stay focussed.” “Fuck that!” snaps Tim McDonald. “You don’t fucking understand. We need to talk,motherfucker. And we’re going to talk if I have to send a team of goombas in here to tapeyour ass to that wall.” Snow sighs, “What do you want, McDonald?” “Where’s that little pansy?” McDonald asks without acknowledging Snow’s question. He peersoutside the cell to see if Leigh Donovan, Snow’s cellmate, is anywhere near. “We have to talkalone. I can’t have that little cockbiting motherfucker coming in at the wrong moment. This isserious shit, man.” “Leigh’s out with his new ‘friends’,” Snow says, rolling his menacing blue eyes. “Giving praise toHitler or something - I don’t fucking know.” “So he’ll be gone for a while?” “What the fuck is this, McDonald?” Snow asks, his voice growing louder. “Quiet the fuck down!” Tim barks in a whisper. “I can’t have anyone knowing I’m here, you stupid fuck!” “If you don’t hurry the hell up and tell me what’s so fucking urgent, you won’t be here longanyway,” Snow says coldly. “Quite frankly, I don’t like you, and I’m about three seconds away fromtossing your weasel ass out over that fucking balcony. Your ‘mafia’ be damned.” “Ok,” McDonald says, holding a pair of calming hands out to Snow. “Listen, you know that new cellmate of mine? Older guy, kind of a fat fuck?” “What about him?” “What would you say if I told you that was Vinny ‘S&D’ G!” McDonald says, his brown eyesgrowing wild. “I’d say that was a stupid fucking nickname,” Snow shrugs. “Get to the point.” McDonald steps closer to Snow, cautiously, and with pleading eyes. “Vincent Giovanni,”McDonald whispers, “is known on the outside and Vinny G - Vinny ‘S&D’ G! Mr. Search and De-fucking-stroy!” “Why should I care what his friends call him?” “I’m not talking about his friends, you stupid fuck!” barks McDonald. “That motherfucker’s a madeguy!” Snow narrows his eyes. “Sorry,” he says, “I’m not up on current mafia slang. What the fuck are youtalking about, McDonald?” Tim sighs and puts his head in his hands. It’s not often that he gets so nervous that he finds himselflosing his usual cool exterior. “Snow,” he finally says calmly, “Vinny G was one of Doug Hattoni’smain hitmen. They call him ‘Search and Destroy’ because that’s exactly what he’s best at -searching out his target, and taking it the fuck out.” Snow considers the information for a second. “So?” he finally shrugs. “Another mafia goon gotcaught and canned. It happens everyday.” McDonald grabs his hair in frustration - he’s not used to talking to people who are either not on thesame page as he is, or not up to date in current mafia, specifically Hattoni family, affairs. “Use yourfucking head!” Tim exclaims, his eyes wildly glaring at Snow. “For fuck sakes, Snow, think aboutit! When was the last time you heard of a big-time mob motherfucker serving six fucking monthson a God damn assault rap! Jesus fucking Christ, Snow!” Suddenly, things start sinking in for Snow and he understands why Tim McDonald is so hysterical. “It starting to sink in now, Snow?” Tim asks sarcastically. “It’s the way it fucking works, Snow -they took our phonecalls and visitation privileges away, so the Hattoni family put a guy right in herewith us. A fucking undercover mafia hitman! Do you understand now, Snow? Do you!? Has iffinally sunk in that this motherfucker wants our blood for killing Doug Hattoni!” “I had nothing to do with Doug Hattoni’s death,” Snow says shakily. Deep down, something hastold him all along that Tim McDonald knew of Snow’s involvement. But Snow knew at the timeof Hattoni’s death, McDonald was looking to get the old mob Don out of the picture anyway. “Don’t act fucking dumb with me, Snow!” scowls Tim McDonald. “You handed that fat fuckerover to the Harvey Blake and his band of merry Nazis, and Vinny fucking G is here to make you payfor it!” Snow slows the situation down by turning his back to McDonald and taking a few deep breaths. “Alright,” he finally sighs. “You got me. That sick fuck had a God damn rapist dealing drugs tomy sister-in-law! You’re fucking right I sold him out to Harvey Blake! But what I want to knowis what you had to with it...” Snow turns around to glare at Tim McDonald. “What do you mean?” McDonald gulps. “I didn’t have anything to do with it.” “Then what the fuck was all of this ‘us’ shit, Timmy?” Snow says quietly and coldly. “And whatare you doing here? Don’t play fucking good Samaritan with you, you fucking weasel. If this prickdidn’t have some dirty shit on you, you’d let him cut my fucking throat.” Snow’s burning eyes rip holes in Tim McDonald’s handsome young face. Finally, McDonaldrealizes that there’s no lie believable enough to keep Snow on his side in this time of crisis. Theonly way to get out of this ordeal alive is level with the ferocious blond lion. “Alright,” McDonaldadmits finally. “I was in on it. Are you fucking happy?” “Harvey Blake smothered Doug Hattoni,” Snow says with narrow eyes. “What, exactly, wasyour part?” “Jesus Christ, Snow!” McDonald says while rolling his brown eyes. “Didn’t you ever even questionhow the whole fucking thing went down so smoothly? Didn’t you ever ask yourself why no guardswere anywhere near the scene? Why none of them saw you, or Harvey Blake, near Hattoni’scell? Look at you - all fucking proud of how you orchestrated Hattoni’s murder. You didn’t do jackshit, Snow! You and Blake were both just pawns. I was the one controlling the fucking game,Snow. For fuck’s sakes, I was even the one who put you on the right track to find out that Hattoniwas partly responsible for your sister-in-law’s rape!” Snow stands in silent shock. His first thought is Tim McDonald is a criminal genius. His secondthought is that Tim McDonald can’t be trusted. “Alright,” Snow says quietly. “So how much does he know?” “Way too fucking much,” Tim replies. “He knows Blake’s part. He knows your part. And heknows I had something to do with it.” “I guess we’ll have to watch our backs then,” Snow says while stroking his beard, not willing toadmit just how concerned he really is - showing true feelings to Tim McDonald is like tying his ownnoose. Instead, Snow tries to disguise his true feelings and sheming. His disguise, however, doesn’t suit McDonald well. “Like fuck!” he exclaims, a little too loudly for his own comfort. “Don’t you fucking understand,Snow!? These motherfuckers don’t kid around! The only reason why either of us and our entirefucking families are alive is because phone and visitation privileges are revoked. Once they comeback and Vinny gets word to his buddies on the outside what happened to the Don of the Hattonifamily, that sweet little wife and baby you have up in Canada are going to become victims of Mobrevenge against you!” Snow’s face turns pale. “Yeah Snow,” McDonald says coldly. “It’s that fucking serious. We have to get rid of this guy, andwe have to do it fucking quick. If not, me and my future, and you and your family, are going to besmeared on the bottom of an expensive Italian loafer.”
