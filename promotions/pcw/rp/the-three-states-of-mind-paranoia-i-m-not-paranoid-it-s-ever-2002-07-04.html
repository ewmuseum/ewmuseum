The Three States of Mind: Paranoia- 'I'm not paranoid it's everyone else!'

4 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4th July We've seen states one and two of El Diablo's mind, so now we go to state three. The paranoia that has consumed El Diablo ever since his rivalry with Flaymin Aymin. The moment his wife left him was the moment this uncontrollable paranoia started within him. Is there anything that can stop it? Or will it grow and grow to insurmountable levels? That is the question we expect to be answered very soon. In sight is a large city centre located in Mississippi, the state that will host the latest instalment of PCW Friday Fusion. The city centre is very busy currently, and outside the many shops and malls are literally thousands of people. No one is really noticeable from the crowd, except for this transvestite walking just like a man, yet wearing a skirt and push-up bra, an eerie site to say the least. The sound of all these people talking and walking around is almost deafened by that of an airplane coming in to land at the nearby airport. Suddenly, El Diablo is seen running into the large group of people while looking up at the sky and as he's not looking where he's going he bumps into an old lady carrying some groceries in a bag and knocks her over as well as himself. The groceries amazingly remain in the bag, but the old lady has taken quite a bump. El Diablo gets up quickly and still keeps a watchful eye at the sky as he begins to help up the old lady he knocked down. 'Are you blind you foolish young man?' asks the old lady with malice in her voice. Diablo quits looking at the sky a second and now looks at the lady. 'That airplane was monitoring my activities, they're all monitoring my activities, you gotta help me, you gotta hide me,' rambles El Diablo in panic. The little old lady looks at him strangely. 'What are you babbling on about you...you, HOOLIGAN!' she shouts at him, somehow relating to the face paint he's wearing. Diablo, meanwhile, keeps one eye on the sky and one on the old lady. 'I'm sorry I knocked you down, you just gotta help me,' pleads El Diablo, who is now looking very nervous in panic. He grabs hold of the woman by her left wrist and starts to nervously bob up and down. 'Get off of me and leave me alone you odd young man,' she screams at him as she shrugs his hands off of her and walks fastly away, cautiously looking back at him to make sure he doesn't follow her. Diablo looks around at the crowd of people walking with their shopping and then takes a look back up at the sky. 'Oh shit, they're fucking after me,' mutters Diablo as he tries to find a place to hide. Diablo soon wanders into a shop. **TEN MINUTES LATER** An odd looking man wanders out of the shop casually. He is wearing a black top hat, and has a strange moustache on his odd, very pale face, as well as wearing a black suit with a black bow tie and shiny black shoes. He carries a plastic bag with him in his right hand and walks slowly mixing in with the crowd. If I didn't know better I could swear it was Charlie Chaplin. He walks on a little bit until a small bald man with a walking stick interrupts him. 'It's you isn't it?' asks the strange bald man. Charlie Chaplin looks nervous and just nods to agree with the man. 'It's Charlie Chaplin!' the man finally remarks, 'Here, take my stick and do a funny walk with it,' says the bald man offering the stick. Chaplin takes the stick, places it down on the ground and starts slowly walking around it, bobbing up and down several times and looking oddly around him in strange poses, he then leaps over the stick and holds it out for the man to take. The bald man applauds him as he takes back his stick. 'Excellent, thank you,' he says and gets going on his way. Chaplin takes a bow to no applause, but just to suit the end of his act. He carries on walking until a large man bumps into him, and suddenly the black moustache drops from his face. As he walks further, someone has stood on his pants and the thread is now ripping away at the suits pants, revealing black shorts. A gust of wind blows heavily and off blows the top hat revealing a headfull of long black hair. It's El Diablo. 'Dammit, is the whole world out to get me?' shouts El Diablo tearing off the rest of the disguise and looking around anxiously. He casually walks on, looking on nervously at the people around him and silently impressed with the walk he did earlier. What a strange man this El Diablo is, like everyone in the world is out to get him......right? Diablo tries to continue walking, but is stopped a Cop out in the city centre. 'Just what are you dressed as?' asks the Cop, amazed at El Diablo's appearance. El Diablo bows his head in horror at been stopped by a Cop. 'I'm a man being monitored by airplanes that fly in the sky,' remarks El Diablo, trying to gain the Cop's belief. The large cop, dressed in typical blue looks at El Diablo and simply laughs at him. 'You mean...' he chuckles away, '...that the whole world...' he continues laughing, '...is against you,' he finally finishes while continuing to laugh. El Diablo looks at the Cop, annoyed and still anxious for his safety. 'Exactly, donut belly!' Diablo responds, 'Now can you help me?' he pleads for the mans help. The Cop looks angry at the last remark about donuts. 'Go on, get out of here you loon,' motions the Cop. Diablo stands still out of fear for his personal safety and anger at the cop. 'I'M NOT PARANOID, IT'S JUST EVERYONE IS OUT TO GET ME!!!!!!!' yells Diablo gaining a lot of the shoppers attention. The Cop looks at him and pulls out his handcuffs. 'That's it, you're under arrest,' states the Cop. He cuffs Diablo's hands behind his back and starts to pull him along in the direction of the Police Station. 'SEE!' shouts the Mexican Devil, 'He's out to get me, you're all out to get me,' screams the Mexican as he is dragged away. The shoppers that have noticed what is occurring just look at El Diablo strangely and carry on walking away. Did his paranoia reach insurmountable levels in there? Or was El Diablo right? And will this hinder his chances at defeating Duke Williams? What will be next for El Diablo?
