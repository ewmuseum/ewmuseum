013 - The Mall [2]

14 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10.14.02 On the other side of the mall, as many shoppers are tending to get their Christmas done in early, a boy happens to sit by himself, pushing down on the controls of an Playstation 2 console. His eyes reflect that of the game as he continues to force himself upon the game, now playing it for what looks to be the fourth straight hour. His palms are sweaty, barely able to lift the controller in his hands, but he finds a way. His brown spiked haircut, cropped down into a buzzcut his been left for the side, carefully knocked over by sweat and perspiration. Walking up towards the boy happens to be one of the men featured in Friday's Black Friday Mall Brawl, Dante Jackson. 'Stupid Pac-Man, if there were a black game on the market, everybody would play it.' he mumbled to himself. Looking behind him, he could see the crowd forming once more around Dante and the Pac-Man machine. 'These ASSHOLES!' He bursted the last sentiment loudly, enough for the many people in the mall to here. The boy who was playing PS2 has now stopped, unfit to continue. Sobs begin to murk from him as he sniffles a bit, before coming down in complete tears. The store owner steps outside, looking dead at Quo for stopping one of his customers from enjoying themselves. 'Boy! Do you know what you've done?' Jackson backpedaled, before bouncing back into a group of small midgets, clutching away at his legs. 'GET OFF ME!' he screams, before returning a swift mule kick to one of them, sending the lad back into a group of posters. 'BOYYY!' The Game Store owner screams, obviously a little ticked off from Jackson's actions as he stomps his foot down in an anticipate maneuver or something. Dante looks at the man, moving into somewhat of a trance, before seeing the man drops his pants and shake his ugly glutted ass into the face of Statuz. 'NOO!' he tries to fight it, but the midgets have a tight grip upon him, not wanting to let go. He struggles to move his feet, inch by inch as it may be, but still he fights away, his courage not to have his beautiful almond skin in contact with the Game Store Owner's ugly backside. 'Coma hera! You deserve it! You owe me threy fiddy!' Jackson looks at his opponents, before jerking away. 'Man, I don't have no problems with you, so first off step the hell off me. Second, get your tired stereotypin' ass BACK IN YOUR STORE!' 'Youri komma domo?' 'Say what?' 'Youri komma domo!' 'Are you high on some weed I haven't smoked before?' 'YOURI KOMMA DOMO!' the evident infaction of the words are enough to send the man into a rage, falling to his knees and watching his clothes shred. He begins to change colors right before Quo's eyes as his puny body is now rippling with muscles. His short black hair has now turned bright green, along with the rest of his body. 'YOURI KOMMA DOMO!' You're going to get fucked up. Jackson screams as if he has never screamed before, as the large green store manager begins to take his first steps, each one rippling the floor of the mall like an earthquake. Using the bare minimal of his strength, Jackson flings the midgets into the face of the large man and happen to knock him off of his feet! An innocent bystander, stands on the side of Jackson, his arms packed with goods from the mall and he looks over, speaking clear English. 'How did you do that?' 'Psh, they're midgets! You think this playa is going to lose to a bunch of screwed up in the head midgets?!' Jackson did have a point. A 6'4' and 220 pounds, its not hard for Quo to present that type of figure around anybody. He is pratically a black Adonis, without the Adrian part in front of it. He walks away from the scene, before knowing that he has beaten a Green thrice, and looks for a third on Fusion. Meanwhile... * * * A seemingly buzzing crowd is shown around the console that a man happens to be hovering around as the echoing sounds of the Pac Man beat happens to be playing. The easily heard death causes many people to groan within, waiting and screaming, 'WHO THE HELL IS GOING TO GET THE HIGH SCORE?!' Jackson, by this time has now made his way through the commotion and has pushed his way through, making sure that he isn't the one not caught up. Looking at Jackson, his attention focuses towards the screen, with another backed into a corner. The small sprite maneuvers over towards the left, but is caught in-between two ghouls. Dante tries to go up, but the red ghoul makes his way downward, soon as the blue ghoul makes his way left. The man smiles, before shifting the joystick and picks another power pellet, but hitting a super pellet and now on the attack. 'Your ass is STILL playing this game?' Jackson questions, before looking over the crowd. Some of these people were teens, anxiously awaiting to get their hands upon Tekken 4 for the PlayStation 2, others were waiting to go in and play Dead to Rights. With Dante Jackson wanting to hog the Pac-Man console stationed at the very front of the arcade, nobody's getting in. He starts up, and before long, he makes it farther that the previous two happened ,as Dante is now past the Baby Stage. The existing high score happens to be a bit close to not being out of his reach, but in his grasp. The cheering continues as he twist the joystick, still stuck on two lives left, and six hundred eighty four thousand points set and they keep ascending. Dante shrieks as he is stuck in between a piece of the puzzle, down like a sewer rat trying to come up into surface. 'What's wrong?' one patron asks. 'I'm stuck,' he replies. A massive count of groans appear, as Dante tries to manuever his way out the sharp maze that has him blocked in. He cuts around the side, picking up more power pellets before grabbing the large power pellet to make him the hunter instead of the hunted. He gobbles up four hundred, eight hundred, one thousand two hundred, and then one thousand six hundred points. His eyes water up, before looking upward at the high score, the previous one is no more, as the one is compliment to Dante's score at the moment. He smiles for a second, being able to relax, until a loud clink is heard, shutting off the game console. 'NOOOOOOO!' A loud ridiculous scream is heard from the lungs of Dante Jackson as the scene fades.
