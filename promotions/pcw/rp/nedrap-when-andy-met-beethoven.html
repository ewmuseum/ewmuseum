NedRap: When Andy Met Beethoven!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Ah&#039;m nae sure min. “Are ye sayin’ ah’m stupit!?” [Slowly, but surely, our scene fades into a small room, cream material is beautifull draped along the walls, making the room look something from Swan Lake or something. The room is beautifully illuminated by server lights, which really highlight the material draped across the walls, making them look quite splendid … God I’m starting to sound like a toff now aren’t I? Ok, I’ll stop. A beautiful white grand piano is the centrepiece of the room, at it sits a young Ludwig van Beethoven, one of the world’s greatest classical composers. The now deceased composer wears classical attire, and sits playing one of his finer (In the views of some, anyway) pieces of music. The young composer sits there, playing away, when suddenly there is a slight change in ton, as Andy Murray, Glesga Superstar, steps into view, wearing a blue Adidas shell suit with white stripes, and matching baseball cap. With a confused look on his face, Andy looks around the room.] Andy Murray: This is pure pish min … Am I in London or somethin’? [Andy paces around the room, before latching his eyes on the composer, who hasn’t noticed our hero yet. Andy jumps back when he sees Beethoven, and chuckles to himself at his appearance.] Andy Murray: Hoho! What a toff min! [Beethoven continues to play, as Andy takes a look at the composer from several different angles. Of course, being from Glasgow and all, Andy wouldn’t get to see much of this kind of person, especially not with Andy’s upbringing. Andy manages to stop chuckling for a moment, and says something to Beethoven.] Andy Murray: Ho! You! [The composer either pays no attention, or cannot hear Andy due to the volume of the music he is playing. A confused look spreads across the face of Murray, before he shouts at Beethoven … ] Andy Murray: HO! BAW BAG! [Whilst continuing to play his music, Beethoven turns around, and notices Andy. He quickly stops playing, and turns to face Andy.] Beethoven: Ahhh yes … Andrew, you have made it. [Beethoven speaks English? Err? And isn’t he dead anyway?] Andy Murray: Dinna call me Andrew, or ah’m gonnae malkie ye! Beethoven: I’m sorry, I am not quite familiar with your choice of vocabulary. Andy Murray: Dinna give me that pish, or ye’ll end up siz feet under? YE GOT ME!?! And what’s all this pish anyway? Beethoven: Allow me to introduce myself then … [Beethoven rises from his stool, and offers a handshake to Andy.] Beethoven: My name is Ludwig van Beethoven, full-time composer, how do you do? [Beethoven grins, Andy just looks at him with that confused look we have all come to know and love. Andy notices Beethoven’s outstretched hand, and immediately thinks of one thing … ] Andy Murray: Ah’m no given ye any money ya chube, I need that later for when me an’ Rab go down the chippae … Beethoven: I’m sorry? Andy Murray: Are ye trying tae make me look stupit? Beethoven: No. Andy Murray: Then what do ye want!? [Beethoven retracts his hand.] Beethoven: Andy, it has come to my intention that you are currently producing a piece of music, is this correct? Andy Murray: Spick English ya dobber. Beethoven: Yourself and ‘Rab’ are recording a rap record? [Andy scratches his head.] Andy Murray: Oh, aye. Beethoven: Well Andrew, er, Andy … I’ve seen you prepare for your recording, and believe me, you are going the wrong way about it. Andy Murray: How? Beethoven: Well, for a start, the key to a great piece of music is not in the library, or in the dictionary for that matter. You see, a piece of music has to come from the heart, to truly be considered a classic … [Again, Andy scratches his head.] Andy Murray: Och min, classical music is pure pish. Beethoven: No no, I am not making referrals towards the classical genre, I am merely saying that if you want your piece of music to be remembered, you must think it up from the heart, here is an example. [Leaving Andy with a confused look, Beethoven turns, and starts to play another tune on the old piano. Andy begins to yell above the sounds of the piano.] Andy Murray: HO! Listen tae me ya chube, I did’nae want tae hear from some toff with a pish haircut, how tae make a rap song, awlright? Me and Rab are gonnae make a pure briwliant tune, so shut it, before I hammer ye! [Beethoven of course cannot hear a word of what Andy is saying. When Andy realises this, he shakes his head, and pulls Beethoven up by his shoulders.] Andy Murray: Listen ya chube, if he dinna listen to me, ah’m gonnae tell the Big Man … Beethoven: Excuse me? [Andy shakes his head, before nailing Beethoven right between the eyes with a Glasgow Kiss, or a head-butt to you and me. Beethoven falls to the ground in a heap.] Andy Murray: Hoho! Ye fannie! FLASH! [Suddenly, we cut to the bedroom of Andy Murray, Murray suddenly bolts upright in his bed, a familiar voice can be heard from beyond the door.] Rab: Ho! Andy! Fitba’s on min! It had all been a dream …
