The Crusader Code Cannot be Broken

4 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 4, 2002 Heads will roll. That’s right, heads will roll. Because on the night that one man stands, another will fall to his knees begging for mercy. I stumbled down the steps of the arena, clutching my head which had been busted wide open by Rent-A-Villain. The pain was so great that I thought I might pass out right on the steps here, waiting for someone to save me. Ironic though, isn’t it? The hero waiting to be saved? Poetic justice would have me lying there for the rest of my existence, while half the world wanted me to just disappear. How do I know this? Because I do. Because half of that arena was cheering for Duke when he slammed that god awful bottle into my head and when RAV laid me out. How could they be so unforgiving? How could they do this to me? I was supposed to be their hero! You can’t be a hero. You’re just a wrestler. Another piece of meat they want to see paraded in front of them like a circus animal. Just fight and make it good. “No,” I whispered, closing my eyes to keep the voices out of my head. “I won’t give up. I can’t be a hypocrite. Not now, when everything is on the line. It wouldn’t be fair.” Who were these voices that haunted my mind and my dreams? It sounded like one person, but they came so often now, I didn’t know if it was a group of people or just an obsessive subconscious eating away at me. I still remembered the last promo done by RAV, and how he completely defaced my entire existence. Legend is nothing but a judge....never seeing the wrong within him yet somehow finding the wrong in others. Perhaps I do, but it’s only because I had hope. I had hope that the hero that everyone in PCW knew before the Crusaders of Light would realize his true calling. Be the man that we all know you are and stand side by side with us, not across from us. All of this tension, all of this anger…it’s all for nothing. Then men like Kannon and Duke Williams get their way and laugh all the way to the bank as they watch the good guys fighting amongst themselves. I sat on the lower steps of the stair case and felt along the side of my head. The cut was fairly deep, but nothing a few stitches couldn’t fix. Legend Inc. holds himself on a higher standard then everyone else and he is FIRMLY entrenched in his belief that he is a hero and a legend... Bullshit. Utter bullshit. I hold myself to the only standard that I can see, and that’s someone who is moral and right. Maybe you’re wrong, RAV. Maybe instead of holding everyone else, they’re all holding themselves down? Have you ever thought of that? Maybe you’re so low that you don’t even realize that you’re no better than the scum of the earth that made you a hero in the first place. The pain in my head throbbed more and more. I gritted my teeth under the pressure and put my head against the guard rail. The cold steel seemed to numb the knee shattering pain. To you, he's just short man speaking really loudly on a box.... But maybe that box was one that you vacated. A vacant box which no one else has the courage to stand up on and announce that they are ready to fight. To fight for the masses and all of the good people, because god knows there aren’t enough of us. It’s so easy to do wrong, and so much harder to do right, yet you’ve chosen the path easiest. Your head is full of false dreams and promises. The pain you feel right now will never be satiated with the utter relinquish of your former life. “Why doesn’t he let me help him?” I murmured under my breath, puffs of cold air coming out of my mouth. The chill in the night was enough to make the railings as cold as ice, yet I didn’t notice it. The middle of summer and the whole world felt cold. Or was it just me? “Tom?” Thinh Alexander hurried down the steps and placed a blanket around my shoulders. He was still wearing his wrestling gear even though he didn’t wrestle a match tonight. “Thinh, what the hell are you doing out here?” I asked despondently, feeling very sleepy all of a sudden. “We’ve been looking for you,” He answered, trying to get me back to my feet. “We lost you after your match with RAV. Look, we got to get you to the ambulance with Robert.” I stared at him in his wrestling tights. “Aren’t you cold? It’s freezing out here.” “It’s almost eighty degrees out here. You’re cold because you’re going into shock. You lost a lot of blood tonight.” It might have been the first thing I actually understood the entire night. My body was sending me warnings that I needed help, yet I ignored them. Maybe the questions in my head were enough to override my common sense. “Duke Williams…where is he?” “Gone. Don’t worry, we’ll make sure he doesn’t try anything else tonight.” He bit his lower lip. “He should be the least of our worries. Nelson and that Dark Age…they are the real threat now. Williams got blasted with us today.” I didn’t understand how Duke could do this to me. TO ME. Of all the people in PCW, I thought we had an understanding. But he couldn’t see one foot in front of his own jealousy, and now I was left with a dangerous Hoss who saw red whenever he took a glimpse at me. But he didn’t come into that ring tonight to save me. He just hated Nelson and the Dark Age, and along with us, he fell to a darker power. How do you fight an enemy that can’t stand you? Not just a hatred of an opponent, but hatred for being myself. It was a question of character. A man of no real importance who tries to make himself seem big when he's really not. “I am a man of importance,” I stated boldly as Thinh lead my back up the stairs. I stared him in the eye and nodded. “I am.” Thinh glazed over me and shook his head. “We’ve got to get you to a hospital. You’re not making any sense.” “Of course I am!” I shouted, trying to loosen his grip on me. “Don’t you remember what RAV said about me? He said I was a nobody. He said I was trying to make myself someone who I wasn’t, but I am someone. I can make this world a better place. I can change people.” He didn’t even answer me. He just led me on towards the ambulance that waited in the docking bay to take me to the hospital. I can’t say that I blamed him, but after all of this, I was on my own. No sidekicks to root me on, or a classic friend who could offer me advice. It wasn’t about the match anymore. He just wanted to get me to the ambulance so they could attend to me and make me right once more. Perhaps he thought that’s what should have been done, but let’s not kid ourselves – I might have been the most clear headed than I’ve ever been. He had a bandage on his neck that resulted from being bled in the ring. I was shocked he was even walking on his own power, but then again, Thinh was a vampire. A little blood loss here and there was a minor delay. As it seems on Turmoil, it's not exactly a classic battle of good and evil, where the villain is painted in shades of black...and the hero shades of yellow and white... It seems more like shades of gray all around.... Shades of gray are all that surround us, and maybe the heroes aren’t so clear cut anymore. I’ll give that to you. But I am not a villain, and I am not evil. Get that through your thick skull, RAV. I am not the bad guy. There was a time that I could say the same about you… Now look at us both. We’re at the whim of a force that wants to envelope the entire PCW into a whirlwind of chaos and blood shed. Whose side are you going to join? Ours or theirs? The ambulance personnel attended to me quickly as we approached them. One man took my arm over his shoulder and helped me into the back of the ambulance. As I sat down, he stuck a needle in me and murmured, “For the pain.” “That shit isn’t going to work on me,” I replied solemnly. “It’s not in the body. It’s in the heart.” I stared over to my right, and there lay a man’s body on a stretcher. He was unconscious, but alive. It was Robert Yarney. I could feel a sting of hurt pierce through me as I looked over at him. He had tried to protect me, to tell me to watch out for myself, but he himself got caught in the crossfire. Fuck the Dark Age. Fuck them to hell. This isn’t how it’s supposed to be. The good guys always prevail… Rob’s hand dangled from the stretcher like a lonely flower caught in the wind. I placed it back on his chest and squeezed it. He was going to be alright…or so I told myself over and over again. There would have to be a response to this. There would have to be… “Never again,” I said defiantly. “Tyler Nelson is going to pay for this, and by god I will do it with my own two hands.” The EMT looked at me like I was delusional, and out of the corner of my eye I could see Thinh just shrugging his shoulders at the other ambulance attendant. He was telling him that I was hurt pretty bad and that I didn’t know what I was talking about. Why is it when you’re finally talking from the heart, no one wants to listen? But when I’m the cocky, arrogant, son of a bitch that I can be, everyone can’t help but eavesdrop? Before they could close up the back of the ambulance and hall me away, I shouted, “I am a hero! You can’t take that away from me yet! Not ever!” No one answered me. Maybe I was a hero, but I sure as hell didn’t look like one right now.
