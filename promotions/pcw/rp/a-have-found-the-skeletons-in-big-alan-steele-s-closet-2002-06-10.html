A Have Found the Skeletons in Big Alan Steele's Closet!

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/10/02 What tangled webs we weave, eh? The big and bold hero may not be exactly what he seems. All of the weak minded fools who cheer him on like zombies don’t know the real story behind Big Alan Steele, do they? They don’t know the horrors he has committed. They don’t know the suffering he has caused. And I’m not just talking about him in the wrestling ring, either. He’s done things that the CEO wouldn’t even think of…….well, maybe as a last resort when bribery, extortion, and blackmail didn’t work. So what exactly did Big Alan Steele, the favorite of all the mindless fans around the world, do that was so dastardly, so evil, so vile, so megalomaniacal, so…so….bad? Well, the truth must be told and the CEO is the one to spill the beans. The CEO is the only ones with the monetary means and the will power to start this investigation and see it through to it’s conclusion. When all is said and done, Big Alan Steele will be looking forward to life imprisonment, death by electrocution, death by lethal injection, or even death by public stoning. The crimes are heinous. The deeds dirty, and done dirt cheap. This is the REAL story behind Big Alan Steele. As we fade into view we find the CEO of PCW, Tyler Nelson, walking up to the door of a run-down shack of a house somewhere in the ghetto if Los Angeles. The dirty white paint flakes off badly, exposing the warped wood underneath. The screen door hangs on the bottom hinge and the front door is ajar a good six to eight inches. Tyler climbs up the front steps, dressed to the nines in a tailor made dark blue Armani suit and black leather Bruno Magli shoes. His face prunes in disgust as he looks at the front door knob. He delicately reaches up and takes a kerchief from his inside breast pocket, draping it over the knob and entering the dwelling. He peeks around the front door as he calls out into the home. “Hello?” the CEO yells. “Is there a crackhead in the house by the name of Creon?” There is silence for several seconds as Tyler takes a few more steps into the disgusting and ransacked house. Empty cans of pork and beans are scattered about all around the living room, accompanied with a solid layer of wrinkled and crumpled newspaper on the floor. The smell of ammonia can be smelled coming from the overflowing cat litter box in the far corner of the room. A small oscillating fan mixes the stale and stagnant air which nearly forces the CEO to lose his filet mignon lunch. “For the love of money get your ass out here!” begs the CEO as he chokes down vomit. A voice comes floating out from one of the back rooms. “Hold on a Goddamn minute! I’m takin a shit.” “Lovely,” sighs Tyler. “I’ve walked into a literal shithole.” As the CEO looks around he curls his lip in disgust, scanning for a place to sit but finding nothing suitable. He resigns himself to stand, tapping his foot on the newspaper floor, creating a rustling sound with each beat. He pops his arm out and checks the time on his platinum Rolex watch. “I’m going to be late for my manicure if this old coot doesn’t hurry the hell up,” Tyler seethes. A few more seconds pass before a shuffling of feet can be heard coming toward us. As Tyler spins around we see an old man, wearing a dirt stained toga and a pair of house shoes, come struggling into the room. Trailing the man is a long strand of toilet paper, still connected to the roll on the bathroom. The CEO is speechless. “Sorry about that,” apologizes the old man in a raspy, wheezy voice. He reaches out in an effort to shake Tyler’s hand. “Are you fucking crazy?” the CEO asks in shock. “You didn’t flush the toilet, you didn’t wash your hands, you have a roll of toilet paper trailing behind you, your house is as disgusting a living area as I have ever seen, your kitty litter box is overflowing with piss and shit, you are a dirty old man, and you smell. Why in the WORLD would I shake your hand?” The old man looks down at his extended hand, a small brown stain running down his palm. “Suit yourself, junior. What can I do for you?” The old man plops down in the garbage covered arm chair and the toga accidentally positions itself in the exact wrong position, revealing the old mans privates. The CEO gasps in horror. “My GOD man, cover yourself!” Luckily for all of us the repulsive image is blurred out of view. “Huh? Oh…,” the old man says as he scratches his groin area. “I’ll make this as quick and painless as possible,” the CEO quickly says. “I need you to tell me about what Big Alan Steele did to you and your daughter.” We can see that the mention of Big Alan Steele’s name stokes a fire in the old man’s eyes. “Did you just say Big Alan Steele?” sternly asks Creon, gritting his teeth angrily. “Did I stutter?” responds the CEO rhetorically. “That son of a bitch, and she was a bitch, killed my daughter!” exclaims Creon. “And the grandchildren, too!” The CEO feigns surprise. The old man continues. “That mother fucker then went on to kill a whole bunch of other people, too. Kept tryin to be forgiven for all the slaughters, but then he’d turn right around and kill some more folks the next chance he got. The fuckin police could never catch up to the bastard because he never stayed in the same town.” “So you are telling me that Big Alan Steele, the big and bold hero of all the morons of the world, is actually a cold blooded killer of both women and children?” recaps the CEO. “You damn right,” Creon says, nearly in tears. “He’s a fuckin murderer above all murderers. Worse than Dahmer. Worse than Bundy. Worse than Vegita. Hell, even worse than Clyde.” “This is certainly a startling revelation for all those people out there who worship the dumb son of a bitch, wouldn’t you say?” retorts the CEO with a smirk on his face. Creon looks up at Tyler with a puzzled expression on his face. “Huh?” “I wouldn’t expect a simpleton like you to understand,” snorts the CEO. “Highly educated individuals like myself understand the implications that this revelation holds for the world of professional wrestling. The whole career of Big Alan Steele has been a lie, a farce, a sham, a charade, a scam, a rip-off, a cheat, a swindle, an all out….lie!” The CEO is defiant in his accusations, sternly driving each point home with a clenched fist into his open hand. Creon just sits in the chair, still not getting with the CEO’s program. “Thank you for this information,” Tyler says vindictively. “Now the people will know that they are truly idiots for being duped by a big goof in a sheet. This Friday on Fusion, I will restore the prestige and respect that the PCW World Title has lost in the grubby, thieving, murdering, dirty hands of Big Alan Steele. Justice will be done. I will have what’s mine once again, and Big Alan Steele will be banished to the lower card where he belongs. I will not be denied!” Tyler spins around and hastily makes his way out of the shambles of a house, leaving Creon dumbfounded in the arm chair. The scene fades to black.
