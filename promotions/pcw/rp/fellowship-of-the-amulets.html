Fellowship of the Amulets

undated

Date: ....... It was time. Whether I liked to admit it or not, it was time to leave and bring back the amulet. I was still in my hotel room, but not alone. Beside me was AJ “Pippin” Palmer. AJ was a working at the kings’s castle. He helped a lot with cooking feud and preparing stuff for guests or me. When I had last talked to Gandalf, he told me that he would try to get Pippin to come with me and after talking to Pippin he had of course agreed. He would come along with me to find the amulet. Along with Pippin, I had some other back up. Sam, the care keeper at the castle was also coming with me. With the three of us, I knew that we could find the amulet. When Gandalf was here, he gave me some useful information. The information was that an evil man named Sauron and his main guard, Hessian had the amulet is a protective box to hide away from the world until Sauron had enough information to unleash the evil power inside the amulet. “When is Sam gonna be here?” I looked at my watch for the fifth time this afternoon. Sam Ramey was supposed to be here almost a half hour ago but there was no sign of him. I hope he shows up soon or we will be late. “And what about this house that you supposedly have?” Pippin did ask a lot of questions and some were at the most inappropriate time but now, since we were waiting and just letting the time pass by, I could answer some of his questions. “Well, it’s in a town called Brickethollow, somewhere down the road. Sauron and his forced probably know about me already coming towards him by the way that news travels so fast so the word will get along that I’m just taking a trip. Which I think I am, actually.” I wanted to tell Pippin and Sam that I was gonna stop at Brickethollow for a while, a couple of months or so but the truth was, it was all a decoy so that I could leave quickly on my own. Travelling alone was more dangerous but I’d be less likely to be seen. “Cool. And how about...” Pippin was cut off because a knock at the door stopped her. She stopped abruptly as I went to answer the door of the hotel room. I opened the door to the vision of Sam Ramey, pack on his back, ready to go. “Hey man!” Sam walked straight in, nodding at AJ Palmer. He sat on the bed, looking as if he was catching his breath. When he was done, he stood up as to say that he was ready. “Let’s go. We’re leaving by foot and pretty soon it’ll be the hottest time of the day.” I didn’t want to leave by foot at first and has debated with Gandalf, Sam, and Pippin about it but all three and the king at the castle had insisted to go by foot because it is old style and I needed to know old style methods to destroy the amulet and also, Gandalf had joked that I needed to lose some weight. I mean, I WAS only 238 pounds! 3 Three Hours Later We had been walking for almost three hours and the terrain that we past wasn’t that exciting. We had walked on the main room, nonstop, watching the trees pass us by. Every so often, we would pass a ditch and would have to either cross by a small bridge or through the ditch itself. The packs we had were pretty heavy and would only last us to Brickethollow, no longer for sure. I was the first to bring up what had been on all three of our minds. “Shall we stop for a snack?” Pippin and Sam looks at me with ‘thank GOD’ look, each nodding at me. We sat in a nearby clearing, putting out a small blanket for sandwiches. When we were about half done our sandwiches and had some food in our system, conversation broke out. “How far is Brickethollow from the hotel where you were staying at?” was a question that Sam had asked me right after he had finished wolfing down his food. “Walking, it should take no longer than 2 days or so, depending on the weather and other conditions.” If we would have taken a car, it would’ve taken about twelve hours but instead, we were forced up to 48 hours of walking, plus lunch, supper, and sleep breaks. “Is your house that your settling in for a few months nice?” Pippin asked me, her sandwich was now down her mouth and she could finally talk. This was also too perfect. I would have thought that Pippin and Sam would have found out my plan by now but obviously they hadn’t and everything was going perfectly to plan. “I heard it was pretty nice. Makonie had told me that someone else from the castle was going to keep it warm and live in it, waiting for us.” “I heard about that,” said Sam, continuing, “Meridoc Kaldorn, one of the servants who worked in the kitchen with me I think was who Makonie contacted to keep the house warm. I never heard if she accepted the offer, heard she was having some personal problems or something but if it isn’t her, I’m as clueless as her.” Pippin nodded, starting to put the leftover ham and bread back into her pack while I finished my last bite with the others and dusted off some crumbs that had fallen. Pipping took a small break to talk. “I heard about that to, pretty sure she accepted the offer cause I saw her talking to the head chef about a trip of some sort and I also saw her putting away some clothes and things like that.” “Oh.” Me and Sam got off the blanket while Pipping wrapped it up neatly and put it in Sam’s pack where it had originally come from. When the whole place was cleared up, we walked back to the main road. We walked on the main road, continuing along the curves of the road. We passed through ditches when Sam stopped. “Do you hear that?” “No, what do you hear?” Pippin was the first one to reply. We stopped dead and listened for a sound. All I could hear was a small thumping in the distance which I thought was my heart. “Those are the hooves of some horses. By the sounds of it, the horses are big. That is trouble!” “Who rides a horse any more?” Pippin asked, shooing away a mosquito that was by her. “Black Rides! I have heard of them and they are not freely. Some say that they are followers of Sauron!” came the words out of my mouth. I wasn’t exactly sure of where I had gotten the piece of information that I had just blurted out but it sounded right. “If they are related to Sauron some how, then they will be after us. Quickly get into the bushes!” said Pippin, quietly but loud enough. We all nodded to each other and dived into the bushes. We took off our packs and tried not to make a sound. We got literally underneath the branches as a big, black horse stopped right in front of us! From what I could see, the horse was all black and big. On the horses back was a person that was dressed in black clothing. You couldn’t see his face but he didn’t look happy. The horse bent down and picked up some grass with his teeth, sniffing right beside me. After sniffing for what seemed to be forever, the horse got up and turned around, going down the same road that we were on and into the distance. TO BE CONTINUED
