A lesson learned

12 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/12/02 The scene opens backstage inside of El Diablo’s locker room. Inside of the locker room stand El Diablo, Ricky Thunder, Rent A Hero, Jordan X Shabazz, Duzza and PWA student Charlotte. Jeff: Oh no, not this again. Dave: I think it’s film time. Duke: But where is The End? El Diablo stands at the front of the room, being the typical director. El Diablo: OK people, get ready to read your lines and test them in this rehearsal. Charlotte raises her hand! El Diablo: Yes, you, what is it? Charlotte: Errr, um, I can’t read! El Diablo: Well dammit, just improvise!!!! She shrugs it off and tries to find a way to do her bit. El Diablo: Whoa, whoa, whoa!!! Hold up, just who are you? He points at Ricky Thunder. Ricky Thunder: I’m Ricky Thunder; I’m in PCW and this movie. Diablo gives him a blank look. El Diablo: OK, whatever you say. If I really “did” cast you in this move, what role did I give you? Ricky Thunder: Well it wa.... El Diablo: Whoa, hold up, I remember it was that homosexual guy! Carry on! Thunder looks bewildered and walks away from El Diablo shaking his head. El Diablo: Wow, this is going better than expected. Diablo goes to try walk around the room to hear everyone’s line when Rent A Hero jumps out in front of him. RAH: Holy smokes citizen! I believe you made a mistake with my role. El Diablo: What? How? I don’t make mistakes. RAH: It’s the entire script, oh good Mexican. It lacks my Superhero prowess! Your hero should be in every scene! El Diablo: You’re a what now? RAH: Nevermind my Mexican friend, I’m sure you will rectify the script to suit your hero.With that Rent A Hero quickly exits the scene, leaving El Diablo standing in the centre of the room, confused. El Diablo: What was that all about? Diablo walks further in his locker room and notices something. El Diablo: Hey, who are you? He taps Ricky Thunder on the shoulder. Ricky Thunder: Excuse me? El Diablo: I don’t have time for this, if you’re that desperate for a role you can play one of the extra cyborgs. Ricky Thunder: Huh? El Diablo: Excellent! *SuMa begins to laugh as Ricky relayed the story to him from backstage at Turmoil. Ricky shakes his head in disgust a bit as SuMa continues laughing.* Ricky: Extras, Coffee fetchers, HOMOSEXUALS!!! What the hell is Diablo trying to prove? I mean they approached me about a role in this movies. I am Nathan DeFalco. I am the storyline. I am the means of this movie. I created th efirst ever cyborg, and now I am being treated like this? This is bull man. I should just quit, and tell em to find another cyborg doctor. SuMa: Creator. Ricky: What? SuMa: Cyborg creator. You created them you aren't a doctor for them. Ricky: Whatever SuMa. Anyhow, back to this movie thing. If it weren't for me there wouldn't even be any cyborgs. Ahh to hell with it. SuMa: Dude, you are taking this way to seriously. You are not The Rock. You are a wrestler. Forget about it. It is only one chessy movie, that is being produced by some half talent jack-ass. To hell with El Diablo. I mean come on dude, why sweat it? Ricky: Yeah I guess your right. Anyhow, on to more important things. Friday. Did you see that shit!? Chricton and Murray? Dude, this is not going to have a pretty outcome. I mean I tried to mind my own business and fo9llow the ways of the company, but NO! They want to come down and interfere in my frickiin matches. I tell you what. This is just the start man. Phantom Phive, or Thunder One, they will fall. This Chrichton fella thinks he can just walk in and put his hands on me without any repurcussions. It ain't gonna happen. What is gonna happen though, I am gonna.... SuMa: Ricky, why bother? Your worse than the American government. Ricky: Wha..... SuMa: Dude, why tell everyone what is coming? Why warn someone before you launch an attack on them? What is the point? This gives them the advantage to hide and counter. Ricky: Hide. Yeah, that sounds a bit like Chrichton. But it's cool, I don't care if he hides, but counter he will not. You see, what I have planned for him is simple. I am sure, he will have his title match of some sorts coming up sometime soon. When he does, I'm not saying I will interfere, but what I think I am trying to say, is that he will know I was there. SuMa: Well all I am saying is watch yourself. You know every since we joined PCW, we were labled superstars. We were the CCW man. Now look, everyone in PCW is taking cheapshots at us trying to get to the top faster. I guess signing with The Booker's little group didn't help us from sticking out either. Ricky: you know, honestly I don't care about sticking out, I don't care about being a target, because in my to matches in PCW so far, I have proved myself. I have shown I have what it takes to succeed. I outlasted 20 other people in that schoolyard match, then I beat Makonie last week. SuMa: You didn't really win that match. Ricky: Bull! You know as well as I do, that had Murray not pulled me off of him, he was a good as pinned. Why do you think Murray saved him? Think he would have bothered if he could have kicked out? No. I beat Makonie. Not the technical way I wanted but a win none the less. So far, I am undefeated in singles competition. SuMa: 1-0? Wow, that's impressive numbers. Ricky: I know. Some of us got it like that and some of us don't. Anyhow, Makonie, Chrichton, Murray, those three, will be my next three wins. Ooops. I already beat Makonie, that nazi bastard, so I guess that just leaves those other two jackasses. Unless, we could show them the power of The Supreme Team. What do you.... SuMa: I don't know Ricky. I don't know. I mean, if you look at it, the tag team division is pretty sewn up. Why even bother? I say we just go on about our way and let them do their thing, and we continue doing ours. Ricky: SuMa. I am not asking for a shot at tag team gold. All I am asking is for one match against Chrichton and Murray. We were unstoppable in CCW. What makes PCW any different? We are already headliners, why not make it mean something? SuMa: I will think on it. I will let you know soon enough. In the meantime, watch your back against these two ass clowns. Ricky: Bro those two are the least of my worries. The main of which is where to find a cold drink! You up for one? SuMa: Definately. *The two of them exit out of SuMa's new apartment, and make thier way back out on to the busy Manhatten streets*
