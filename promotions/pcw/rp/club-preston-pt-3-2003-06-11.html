Club Preston (Pt. 3)

11 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.11.03 It had been a long time with many set backs, but finally, the supplies to transform Tyler’s Cloud Nine room into ‘Club Preston’ have finally arrived. With the thoughts of Matt Jackson, Silvio Rochelli, and Warrior nagging at his mind virtually every second, Tyler tries to busy himself with his new project. Tyler opens the light steel door slowly with anticipation. It creaks as the white light from the halls floods in the room. He finally opens it all the way to reveal a bell hop with his arms full. Next to him sits a large steel crate with numerous metal folding chairs stacked on them strategically in a row. Tyler’s eye brows arch over his dark brown eyes. He flashes his off-white colored smiled and greats the Cloud Nine employee with a bright look in his eyes and friendly tone in his voice. The bell hop looks a little agitated as he finally drops all the objects that he was carrying. His scarlet red uniform remains wrinkled in the location that the objects were pressing against his body. “You guys are really kings of service. This is truly better than any hotel could ever offer me. I hate to be a whiner but I need to make sure everything is here. It’s very important for my project,” Tyler says as he gets to a knee and sorts through the junk that was thrown on the ground. The bell hop joins him in attempts to be as overly helpful as he can to possibly gain some sort of a tip. “I believe everything is here Mr. Preston. It was a tough chore to locate the fog machine, but since you are such a valued passenger on the vessel, one was located for you,” the bell hop says in an obviously fake friendly voice. He flashes a forced smile to go on top of it. The muscles in his smooth face tense up in attempts to make it look at realistic as possible. “Wow, I must say, you guys did a great job. How many folding chairs are on that crate, do you know?” Tyler asks as he stands to his feet again and counts the chairs by pointing a stubby finger at each of them. The bell hop turns around and eyes the same rack. “That rack right there is suppose to accommodate around seventy-five chairs. I am not to sure how many were taken before I grabbed that rack, but my estimate would be somewhere around sixty chairs,” the bell hop says after doing some quick math on his hand that harbors a snow white glove. Tyler smiles after hearing this piece of good new. “That’s great. It looks like we’ll be having a maximum attendance of about sixty people or so. You’ll have to stop by young man; it could be a special treat for your time off,” Tyler says as he pats the bell hop on his back. As he does, a few particles of dust fly off from the jacket. “If you don’t mind me asking Mr. Preston, what exactly do you have in mind? It’s not the most usual thing for a passenger to request all these odds and ends,” the bell hop asks as he looks over Tyler’s left shoulder in attempts to see if Tyler has anything out of the ordinary set up in his room. Tyler puts his large arm against the door frame and smiles an innocent smile at the curious bell hop. “I’m glad you asked young man. Let me reply to your question with another question. Do you like a good stand up comedy act?” Tyler asks with a smile, allowing the bell hop to think over his answer to this loaded question. “I never really thought about it, but I don’t really mind it. I watch it every now and then on Comedy Central,” the bell hop replies, deep in confusion. Tyler begins shoveling the objects on the floor in to his room as he continues to speak. “Then you will want to come back for this. I figured that the Cloud Nine needed a little extra something. That’s why I’m creating one of the world’s smallest comedy clubs right here in my room. I’ll use those pieces of wood to transform the bed in to the stage,” Tyler explains as he raises his eye brows up and down to get the bell hop excited about the idea. The bell hop doesn’t look pleased about this information he has just learned. He shakes his head in disapproval. “I would run this by the management. I don’t think something like that would be allowed on the residential deck. There will be people try to sleep up here,” the bell hop says, taking on the mature role of the management. Tyler looks at him with squinted eyes as if he is speaking crazy. “Oh yea, I wouldn’t want to wake up guys like Silvio Rochelli and Matt Jackson. They are too important to be bugged. Is that what you are saying sir? Rochelli and Jackson are more important than I?” Tyler asks, knowing that the bell hop didn’t even suggest this idea. Tyler is a little one ends, disappointed over the fact that Rochelli and Jackson enjoy the limelight of the singles scene virtually every week, while Tyler is a newcomer to this whole deal. “Mr. Preston, I didn’t mean anything like that. I just didn’t want to see the management get cross with you. They have had a reputation of kicking people off the ship on account of unruly behavior,” the bell hop warns as he ducks in close to Tyler’s ear as if it is a secret. Tyler just pushes his head away with a smile. “Don’t worry about me kid, I have everything under control. If anyone has a problem with it, then I’ll just give them front row tickets and they laugh until they forgot there was ever a problem. Now, here is the tip I promised, don’t spend it all in one place. Thanks a lot kid,” Tyler finishes as he wheels the rack of folding chairs in to his room and tosses a limp, one dollar bill at the bell hop. The bell hop sneers, but his cusses do not reach Tyler’s ears as he slams the door shut and surveys where he is going to place all the supplies. ‘I have a lot of work to do before this old piece of crap turns in to the magic of a comedy club. I can’t wait until all the PCW guys try flooding in to hang with all the honeys. I’m sure they’re all mocking me right now. Hell, Matt Jackson is probably trying to dispatch his entire unit on me. I don’t care though, because just like so many other times, I will have the last laugh with this one,’ his mind tells him as he begins by lying the wood down on the bed to construct a small make shirt stage. He begins to work tirelessly; plagued by the future thoughts of his match that is mere days away. -=15 Minutes In=- The bed has been completed and totally transformed in to a stage. Where he is going to sleep, that is another question. It is an issue that he failed to notice before, but now he will just have to deal with it. ‘I’m sure John will let me crash in his place whenever I want. That’s what good friends are for. To bad he can’t be with me in the ring at Fusion. Rochelli and Jackson are really quick to shoot of their mouths when E2 isn’t at their best, but I would like to see them say the same things when the entire E2 team is bearing down on them in the ring. Sure, I have to learn to be independent some time, but everyone knows E2, when together, is the most powerful force around these parts. I would dare that skunk Rochelli to get a tag team partner and deal with E2, then we’d see what smart ass words the wannabe mobster would have to say,’ his mind shuffles through as he concentrates half-mindedly on his construction project. -=45 Minutes In=- The progress continues to grow as Tyler sets up the lights and the fog machine in the corner. He turns off the cabin lights to the room to test out the system. The multiple light bulbs glow so brightly that Tyler must shade his eyes from the glow. The fog machine sporadically spews out a thick, white blanket of fog. Tyler laughs a booming chuckle at the sight of things working. ‘I wish everyone in the PCW was this creative; perhaps it would be a better place to wrestle. Unfortunately we are stuck with clichéd mobsters like that punk Rochelli. I think he has seen ‘Good Fellas’ to many times. And of course there is secret Agent Matt Jackson. Who does that guy think he is? I don’t know if he got the memo, but the role for ‘Bourne Identity’ was already taken by Matt Damon. It’s a good thing we have refreshing gusts of wind with men like Warrior. Sure, he probably deserves to be in a mental institution, but he’s still more original then the rest of those goofs,’ his mind mocks as produces a half smile. He bites the bottom of his lip to keep concentration. -=65 Minutes In=- ‘Club Preston’ comes to completion. Tyler backs up with a smile on his face after assembling all the folding chairs around the stage. The lights are set at a nice brightness to give the room a healthy glow. A live microphone lays on the stage, and is attached to one large speaker. Tyler jumps on the bed, slash, stage and grasps the microphone in his hands. He clenches his knuckles around it tightly. “Club Preston is now open for business. Trust me, there will be a lot of good times in here,” Tyler screams in to the microphone as the sound is amplified by the speaker and bounces off the bare walls of the room. He laughs, noticing that he speaking no one, but still can’t help to think of the future moments that he will spend in his new establishment.
