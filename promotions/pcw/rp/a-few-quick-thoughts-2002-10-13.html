A few quick thoughts

13 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-13-02 Nathan quickly runs down the corporate hallways, bumping into people rudely but not stopping to mutter an apology before continuing his frantic run. As he rounds a corner he realizes that he’s gone a bit too far and manages to skid to a stop before running into a wall. He smoothes out his wrinkled white t-shirt and tries to walk back around the corner nonchalantly. He lowers his head, blinks a couple of times, and gives the large door in front of him a few solid knocks before opening it and hurrying inside. The door shuts with a loud click, and he looks around the empty office. Papers are strewn across the desk, with a few resting on the floor. The large leather chair is facing an open door on the left hand side of the room. Nathan- Hello? He walks over to the desk and looks down at the unfinished paperwork. Nathan shrugs to himself before taking a seat in the chair and propping his feet up on top of the desk. He looks out to the two empty chairs before the desk and grins. Nathan- So this is what it feels like. A noise from the other room quickly wipes the smug grin off of Nathan’s face and he jumps out of the chair, almost knocking it over in the process. He manages to catch it before it falls, but then realizes that he’s left a smudge of mud on a few of the papers laying on the desk. He frantically rubs his hand across them, managing nothing but smearing them worse. Brandi- Is someone here? Nathan looks around and spits into a cupped hand before giving the papers one final rub. He looks at them and frowns at the very visible spot. Nathan- Yeah, I’m in here. Brandi- Oh! Hey Nathan. You can come on in. I’m just changing real fast. Nathan- Uh, it’s alright. I’ll just wait for you in here. Brandi- Don’t be silly. Come on in and grab that shirt off the chair on your way. Nathan notices the black v-neck shirt sitting on one of the chairs in front of Brandi’s desk for the first time. He grabs it and walks over to the room, holding his hand out and looking the other way. Brandi- Well, are you going to bring it here or what? Nathan walks into the room and sheepishly stares at the ceiling. Brandi is standing a few feet away from him, buttoning a pair of her low-cut jeans. She watches Nathan and giggles. Quietly, she walks over to where he is standing and takes the shirt from his grasp, pulling it over her lacy black bra. Nathan- You dressed now? Brandi- Yep. Nathan seems glad to be able to look at Brandi again. She grins and gives him a little wave before having a seat and slipping on a pair of boots. Nathan stands, staring down at her for a few moments before realizing what he came for. His shyness seems to disappear instantly and he quickly drops down to the floor. Nathan- I had to come let you know that you have someone on their way to see you! Brandi- Someone? Nathan- Yeah. I uh…can’t remember her name…Miss something or other. Anyways, she’s coming here to talk to you. Get an interview or something. Didn’t really say. Just said that she was on her way. Brandi- And who gave her permission? Nathan- I did. Brandi- And did you talk to me about it beforehand? Nathan- Not exactly. Brandi- Not exactly, or no? Nathan- No, I didn’t. I didn’t think you’d mind. I mean, she’s coming here to talk to you! To ask you some questions and get it on TV and everything. Brandi- Uh…Nathan, I don’t need her to get on TV. If I have something to say, I make sure that it gets said. I don’t sit here and wait for some reporter to call me up on the phone so they can do a half ass report and screw it up somehow. Understand? Nathan stands up and nods. He tries to hide his disappointment, but he isn’t very good at it. Brandi- Now, you go get rid of her for me, would you? Nathan- Of course I will. He takes Brandi’s hand and helps her up gently, bringing her close to his chest. She looks up into his eyes and leans towards him, decides against it and quickly turns and walks away. She sits down at her desk and watches as Nathan leaves. Brandi- Now why didn’t I… Brandi is interrupted by a knock on her door. Brandi- Come in Nathan. The door opens and a sharply dressed woman walks in and takes a seat in front of Brandi. Woman- I’m not Nathan, but I hope you’ll let me stay anyway. The name’s Marissa Nicks. Marissa holds her hand out across the desk, offering a handshake to Brandi. Instead, Brandi leans back in her chair and stares coldly at the intruder. Marissa shrugs off the rejection and sets a briefcase on top of Brandi’s desk, promptly opening it and pulling out a tape recorder. She places this on top of the briefcase and presses record. Brandi- You need a briefcase to carry that around? Oh, and you’re on my paperwork. Brandi looks past the woman and notices that she’s left her office door wide open. Brandi- God woman. Didn’t anyone ever teach you any respect? You just barge right in here, leave my door wide open, and try to take over my office? After that, you expect me to give you an interview so that I can help get your name out there in newspapers and on TV? Ha! The woman looks shocked, not quite knowing what to say. She just sits there, staring at Brandi. Brandi- Jesus. Don’t stare. I’m correct when I say you want an interview, right? Marissa- Yes. Brandi- How’d you manage to get in here, anyways? Marissa- Some nice blonde woman at the front desk directed me to your office. Brandi- Tina…probably payback for the dog incident. Marissa- Dog incident? Brandi- Nevermind. Just tell me what you want to know and I’ll consider talking about it. I don’t exactly appreciate your just barging in here. I have to be going soon so make it quick. Marissa- What are your thoughts on the upcoming Mall Brawl? As I understand, you’ll be a participant along with fellow Front Office members Tim Walden and Duke Williams. Any sort of strategy? Brandi- No, I don’t have a strategy. I haven’t even given it much thought. It’s just another match. That’s how I’m going to treat it. I am very confident that a member of the Front Office will come out with the victory. Marissa- How can you be so sure of this? Does CEO Kannon have something up his sleeve? Brandi- I’m sure because that’s what’s going to happen. Anyone that can beat three members of the Front Office would surely deserve the belt. All I’m saying is, that’s not very likely to happen. Marissa- Aren’t you being a bit cocky about this whole thing? Brandi- Look, woman! I don’t owe anything to you. I could have you thrown out of this building a hell of a lot quicker than you got in, so stop pushing me. Got it? Marissa nods. Behind her, Nathan peaks his head in nervously. Brandi welcomes the interruption and motions for him to come in. He looks shocked to find the reporter sitting in the chair, and mouths “I’m sorry” to Brandi. Brandi- It’s quite alright Nathan. Why don’t you join the two of us? Nathan takes a seat in the chair next to Marissa. His entrance has seemed to alert something inside the reporter’s head, and she constantly looks back and forth from Nathan to Brandi. Marissa- The world wants to know what’s going on between the two of you. Brandi winks at Nathan, and flashes Marissa her famous grin. Brandi- The world, huh? Well, for some reason I seriously doubt that so much as half the world could give a shit, but for those who do I have one question. What difference does it make? If we were friends, or if we were dating, would anything be different? No. Marissa- I’m afraid I can’t accept that answer, Brandi. Are you avoiding the question? Brandi- Well, Marissa, first of all there was never a question. Secondly, I thought I already warned you about getting on my nerves? I don’t give a shit about what you’ll accept as an answer. You’ll accept what I give you, and you’ll be thankful for it. Marissa- Ok…well, what about your project in Los Angeles? How is the building of its newest mall coming along? Brandi- I have nothing to announce on that matter, and I think it’s time you left. Marissa- I have a few more questions for you. Brandi- No, you don’t. Now, you can either leave on your own accord, or I can send for a nice little escort for you. Marissa pushes stop on her recorder and places it gently back in her briefcase before heading for the door. Before she steps out, she turns around and faces Brandi. Marissa- You’re a bitch. Brandi- Funny how you didn’t get that on tape. Brandi grins once more and waves before Marissa storms out of the building. She stands up and turns her attention towards Nathan. Nathan- I’m so sorry about that. I didn’t realize… Brandi- Nah, don’t worry about it. It’s not a problem. Now…I was on my way out to grab something for dinner. Care to join me? Nathan- Sure. He stands up, and Brandi throws her arm across his shoulder. Brandi- Don’t worry about the papers, either. A little mud never hurt anything. Brandi winks at Nathan, and he bursts into laughter as the two head for the elevator.
