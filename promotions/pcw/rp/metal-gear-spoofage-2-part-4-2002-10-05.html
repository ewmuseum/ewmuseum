Metal Gear Spoofage 2 (part 4)

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/5/02 Sitting in a dark chamber with dead bodies in every direction, a tired man rests. Exerted from his recent struggle with a flock of dancing zombies, he looks to a blond haired boy standing before him. Raiden: What's your name? Man: My name's Sna.....uh....er.....Plisken. Yeah, that's the ticket. Raiden: Nice to meet you Snauherplisken. I'm Raiden. Plisken: Raiden....that's a stupid name. Raiden: Hey! Plisken: Well it is. It's pretty much the most stupid name I've ever heard in my life. Raiden: (Tears well in his eyes) Plisken: Oh, you gonna cry now? You gonna cry? Huh? Plisken stands up, mussing up Raiden's hair with his hand. Raiden: Stop it you big bully! Plisken: Gonna cry? Cry? Huh? Wawawa? Agh! Suddenly a bolt of pain shoots through the man called Plisken, causing him to double over and clutch his balls in pain. Raiden: (Wiping his eye) What happened? Plisken: Uh....old war injury. Raiden: To your testicles? Plisken: Yeah. Raiden: If you say so. Plisken: Don't judge me, kid. Raiden: I don't. Looking away, Jack mutters to himself. Raiden: He's probably got the clap. Plisken: I heard that! Raiden: Wasn't me, it was the Navy captain! Plisken: ...... Raiden: Well, guess I'd better keep moving. Plisken: (Motions with his hand) The oddly familiar man falls quickly asleep, as Raiden moves on. Through another door he travells, again stopping short as he hears screaming. Raiden: Not again. Sighing, Jack takes a hiding position and looks out to a crossway connecting two struts of the Big Shell. Crowded around this walkway, several Navy Seals wait....firing their guns at a willowy figure standing on the far end. Raiden: Huh? Though the bullets go near this person, they never actually hit.....instead veering off, every last one straying off course. The young man, holding in his hands a massive rail gun merely shakes his head. Fortune Cookie: Why I no fucking die? More bullets careen past him, to no avail. Then grenades, merely falling as duds at his feet. Fortune Cookie: You stupid white boys no learn! I teach you lesson! The Asian man hefts up his gun, firing at the crowd of Seals. What seems to be a bolt of lightning erupts from the weapon, hitting the commandos and sending them flying in every direction. Fortune Cookie: I Fortune Cookie, lucky in killing white boys and nothing else. And I no die, so I just kill you stupid ass instead. Again, again Fortune shoots, blasting the hell out of anything that moves. Fortune Cookie: I got BIG fucking gun! I shoot seagulls too, see!? The man aims up, shooting bolts of electric death at the gulls circling about. They explode in a shower of guts, as he laughs hysterically. Fortune Cookie: I da fucking man, you no ever forget! Finally from behind FC strolls Argy, who taps him on the shoulder. Fortune Cookie: What you want? Argy says something to the psychopath, hidden underneath the low roar of agonized and dying men though. Fortune Cookie: Damn white boy conspiracy! I go see president of cracker country. Fortune Cookie stomps away, dragging his rail gun behind him. Argy begins to follow....then stops, turns and looks up at Raiden. Raiden: Eep. A kiss is blown....Argy winks at the hiding voyeur, then vanishes. Raiden: Come in Colonel. Leah: Oh, hi. Do you know what day it is tomorrow? Raiden: ......Colonel, are you there? Colonel: Maybe. Raiden: What do I do now? Colonel: Stop relying on others to solve your problems? Raiden: Hey YOU got me into this mission. Colonel: Blah blah blah. Why don't you go try and meet up with the bomb disposal expert that came in with the Seal team? Make yourself useful and get rid of the explosives on the Shell. Raiden: Alright. But I'm not doing it for you. I'm doing it for.... Raiden pauses for a second. Raiden: Ah hell I don't know why I'm doing it. Raiden out. Pouting, our brave hero makes his way across the shattered remnants of the bridge. Into the next chamber he goes, carefully searching....then across the room he spies a man, hunched over with his back to him. Raiden: Stay where you are! Man: Hold on, I'm not a terrorist. The man slowly reaches to his right....to what seems to be a stack of voodoo dolls. Raiden: I said don't move! Voice: Settle down. Into the chamber walks Pliskin, looking irritated. Pliskin: Don't point that thing everywhere, kid. Looking on, Raiden abruptly pulls the muzzle of his gun out of his nose. Raiden: So....uh.....what the hell are you? The man on the floor looks up to reveal what seems to be a bone skull mask. Vampir: What? What is it? Raiden: You're....the bomb disposal expert? Vampir: Uh, yes. I'm Vampir, pleased to meet you. The strange man extends a paw, which Raiden merely looks at. Raiden: Uh..... Vampir: What? Pliskin: (Nudging Raiden) Be polite. Raiden: Um....nice....books....down there. Vampir: Thanks, I wrote them all myself. Raiden: ....huh? But your name isn't on..... Pliskin: (Nudging Raiden harder) Mr. Vampir, we're here to ask your advice. We need to dispose of the bombs in this plant. Vampir: Ah.....yes. They were planted by my student, Bobby. Pliskin: .....wait a second here. Vampir: He was born the son of a clockmaker. He didn't have any friends, so he lived in his mother's basement till he was 32. Pliskin: But Bobby..... Vampir: I thought I'd found a son in him. But that boy was such a screwup that I realized if he was my son I'd be better off castrated. Raiden: (Snickers) Vampir: I taught him well, but I didn't teach him the most important thing. Sometimes you just have to learn when you're being annoying and to go the hell away. Pliskin: Well....we'd better find those bombs. Vampir: Take this. Vampir hands a small voodoo doll to Raiden, who looks at the front and the back of it in confusion. Raiden: Huh? Vampir: It's symbolic, son. It's symbolic of the spirit of all the bombs....find one, prick the doll and the bomb'll be defused. Didn't anyone ever teach you about symbolism? Raiden: ...... Snake: (Nudges) Go along. Raiden: Uh....yeah! Symbolism! Right..... Vampir: Anyway, I'll be hiding here in the liquor cabinet if you need me. Pliskin: I'll be wandering around the Shell in a cardboard box. Raiden: ...... Pliskin: What? Raiden: Nothing. Pliskin: Semper Fi. Who dares wins. Be all you can be. Shama lama ding dong. Sayonara. The man gives a salute, puts his fist in front of his nose, opens his hand, wiggles his fingers. He then turns to leave, as Vampir shakes his head. Vampir: He's no Marine. And I have no idea what the hell 'shama lama ding dong' is supposed to mean. Raiden: Me either. Vampir: Well, good luck. With that said and done, the three men head in their opposite directions.....Raiden journeys to the women's bathroom. Then, his ear rings. Leah: Hi Jack, do you know what day it..... The young lady pauses. Leah: .....why are you in the women's bathroom with a voodoo doll? Raiden: Looking for bombs. Leah: Oh, okay. So Jack, do you know what day it is tomorrow? Raiden looks on....then produces a pin and jabs it in the eyes of the doll. Raiden: Come on, there must be some way to convert this thing to Leah mode. Leah: You're so silly, Jack! Raiden: Argh! A beeping sound emerges from above Raiden, as his jabbing deactivates the bomb. Raiden: The hell, it actually DID work. Raiden phones in on his codec, calling up Vampir. Raiden: Vampir, your plan actually.....what are you doing? On the monitor, the bomb disposal expert shifts through several volumes of literature. Smiling, he speaks to himself. Vampir: These should hold me for the next six months.....mwaha..... Suddenly he looks up. Vampir: (Putting the book behind his back) Oh, hi Raiden. I was just....uh....writing some more theories on bomb disposal! Yeah....not the number one guy in the field of explosives for nothing. Raiden: ...... Vampir: What? Raiden: .....anyway. Any more bombs around here? Vampir: Well, there should be one on the roof. Raiden: Why would he set bombs on the roof? That wouldn't really harm the shell at all, it's.... Pliskin: Don't....QUESTION, kid. Just go do it. Raiden: Argh! And so our hero very begrudgingly makes his way up to the rooftop.....however, all he sees is a massive harrier jet. Raiden: ......how the hell did they get THAT? Shaking his head, Jack looks underneath the plane to find a bomb strapped to the bottom of it. Raiden: .....that makes even LESS sense, Why would they blow up their own plane? Colonel: Raiden, we have to ask that you don't think so much. You're dragging the plot down. Raiden: Huh? Colonel: I mean, uh....yes, dispose of that bomb. Raiden: ...... Jack does as asked, getting on his belly and crawling beneath the Harrier. He then produces his voodoo doll, begins to prick it. Leah: Hey Jack! Raiden: (Slamming his head off the plane) OW!!!! Leah: (Giggling) Jack, you're so silly. Do you know what day it is tomorrow? Raiden: Urge to kill.....rising..... Leah: I love you too pookie! As Leah waves, Raiden cuts off the monitor angrily and finishes prodding his odd bomb diffusing device. He then crawls back out, holding his head and muttering. Raiden: Now what do I do. Suddenly Jack stops short as he sees a female soldier speaking on a walkie talkie. He takes cover as she communicates with a mysterious someone. Brandi: I saw a man dressed up like a Ninja just now. Voice: What? Brandi: Yes. Voice: You're sure you haven't just been indulging in the shrooms again, you know I told you to stop that while you're on a mission. Brandi: I am not hallucinating! I know what I saw. And I saw a man hiding under a cardboard box too. Voice: Hm. Brandi: You believe me this time? Voice: Only one man I know could possibly be stupid enough to hide under a box. We'll set a nice little trap for him. Brandi: Over and out then! Brandi shuts off her radio, then pauses as a voice behind her yells. Raiden: Freeze! Slowly, the female soldier turns to find Jack pointing a gun very close to her face. Brandi: ......no. Out of nowhere Brandi swings her foot up, booting Raiden squarely in the balls. She then backflips off the railing, spinning through the air and vanishing out of sight. Leah: Hey Jack, do you know what day it is tomorrow? Raiden: (Holding his crotch) Ooooooooh. Leah: So silly! Raiden: Ooooooh......now what do I do...... Colonel: Well, you have around 400 seconds to diffuse the bomb that was just lit. Raiden: WHAT!? Colonel: Yeah....sorry. I sort of called Bobby while you were messing around.... Raiden: Why? Colonel: I was bored, I needed someone to make fun of besides you. Raiden: Argh! Colonel: Yeah. He lit the bomb at the entrance to the shell. We're all going kablooey in 400. Raiden: What do I dooooo!? Colonel: I'd suggest you haul ass. Raiden: Agh! Sprinting as fast as his legs can carry him, Raiden heads back down the stairs....through the chamber where he met Argy, where a lone soldier inspects the bodies. Guard: I should have listened to mother and become a paleontologistAGH!!!! A scream escapes his lips as Jack barrels straight over him, stepping on his face. Then around the corner to an elevator, which he takes down. As he does, his ear buzzes. Vampir: Hey Raiden....uh....I have something to tell you. Raiden: What? Vampir: I fell for it. Raiden: Huh? Vampir: Bobby has my number. Bobby: (Popping up on codec) I also have a rocket in my pocket. Vampir and Raiden: ...... Bobby: Sorry. Vampir: Anyway.....I....accidentally set off more bombs. Raiden: How many more bombs....? Vampir: Well, let's just say with the collective impact we're all about to feel, we'll be whistling dixie on Pluto. Raiden: Damn. But can't you defuse them? You're the best bomb disposal expert ever, you wrote all those books. Vampir: ......I didn't write those books. Raiden: Huh? Vampir: I....looked at some books and copied them down. Raiden: ....... Vampir: And I don't really have a peg leg. Raiden: ......I didn't know you had one to begin with. Vampir: And my name's not Vampir, it's actually Bob Stephens. Raiden: ..... Vampir: I work in a record store. Raiden: ...... Vampir: I can't help it, I'm a pathological liar! You'd have done the same thing if......AAAAAGH!!!!!! Suddenly a huge explosion rips behind Va.....Bob. It reduces him to ribbons, as the codec cuts off. Raiden: Ulp. Down, down the elevator rumbles.....eventually hitting the ground floor. As fast as he can Jack rushes out, searching all around frantically for the bomb. Raiden: Where IS it!? The seconds tick away.....Raiden counts down with it. Raiden: 10 seconds left for me to live. And I had so much I wanted to do....like live. And not die. And stuff. Whimpering, the commando slinks to the ground.....then looks down curiously to find he is sitting on a bomb. Raiden: ? Standing up quickly, Jack fumbles in his pocket.....as the time limit reaches 6.....5....4....3...2....and he jabs the voodoo doll as hard as he can. Raiden: (Closing his eyes) .......huh? Shakily, he opens one to find he is not dead. Raiden: I'm not dead. The soldier slowly laughs, hopping up and down. Raiden: I'M NOT DEAD! MWAHAHA! EAT THAT EVERYONE! NOT DEAD NOT DEAD HA HA HA HA HA!!!!! HAOOF!!!!!! A bolt of lightning seemingly shoots past Raiden's head, exploding the wall and knocking him to the ground. He wobbly sits up, looking on as a figure stands on the far side of the room. Fortune Cookie: You dead! You so dead the dead wish they less dead than you! Raiden: Ah.....crap!!!!! To Be Continued
