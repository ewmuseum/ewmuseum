Back In Effect

8 October 2002

Date: 10-8-02 Its the days after Red October, and you would think that both Dante Jackson and Statuz Quo are living the lights as Tag Team champs right? Wrong. When you're given an amount of time to work, you're suspected to pull through and do your job. Pranks With Attitude did horrible with this, and now, currently they are slated to get another shot at the tag team titles. The only difference? A Steel Cage match. To Playaz Inc. its another time to prove to the entire world just why that happened to win those Tag Team titles on Sunday. Its not like they're the main reason why the tag team title scene is blazing as of this moment. They are the tag team in the division. They are the one and only P.W.A Ass Kickers, and do they think they will take the tag team titles off Quo and Jackson? 'No.' Tapping his foot along a porch, he sits for above him shadows the Shady Hills Apartment Complex in New Jersey. Of course his color scheme happens to be not trend setting, but none the less one of those which causes admiration. A black Oakland Raiders jersey and black sweat pants as nothing covered his head, just the black hair and the earrings two, one in each ear. Looking past people walking through, he could not understand just why did Proctor did that towards him, but he knew it had reasonable doubt in order for a match between them to be set up. One thing happens to be different from his usual attire.The gold plate, signaling that he was one half of the PCW Tag Team Champions, one of five new champions crowned at Red October. A cell phone was right in his hear, no high pitch from a cheap disruption of static. He blinked in and out, second after second he heared new things, old from his past and now new, there aren't many people that would call him. He meticulously continues to nod and doze off from the questions, nothing happened to fufill his happiness. 'W-What do you want me to do, act like I don't care about it?' Dante stammered before finishing on his statements. A husky voice came from the phone, all but known to Dante as one of Reno's right handed men. The right handed man on the other end happened to be short and chubby with a crew cut hairstyle and a toothpick hanging out of his mouth. He had long blue puppy dog eyes which made you kind of feel sorry for him when you looked at him, but Dante never would pay attention to sucker details. He said himself, looking like a tough guy while you're a pussy isn't cool. He paced around, a drip of sweat falling from his brow and a huff of his breath showing his fear. Looking behind his back, red background walls with yellow painted doors and workers gait in manner towards getting where they belong. He hears the voice of Reno booming from the outer door from last door in the hallway. The launching of a table hitting the wall happens to eat up the wall, causing a hole between the two different rooms. The right hand man sighs to himself, 'Shit is chaotic.' 'How so?' 'Look at it from this perspective, Reno's pissed off from you winning and him not getting his cut.' 'He wants a cut? Damn, I got to deal with not only that person from my past, but another from creepy old England's past. Ain't that a bitch?!' Dante told him, chuckling after his last comment. The right hand man reassured him from his previous confidence, 'I wouldn't be the one saying that, afterall he had you and Bishop in his palm.' 'And I worked my way out of the palm, in fact that ingrate should be thanking me. I'm the one pulling in more than any other worker. Tell him that!' Dante told him firmly, closing the door on the phone drawing a buzzing tone on the other end. Whispering into the phone, the right hand man curses at Dante, but no answer is given back to him. Then, the voice of Reno calls out from his office. 'Walt! Get in here! NOW!' The voice of Reno, loud and powerful causes Walt's body to start its walk down towards the man, and in essence proving that a man without a mind of his own, is weak towards others. Time for Dante to slyly walk up from the steps of the porch and catch a bite to eat, before the phone rings once more. Dante tries to act impatient, standing on the sidewalk before a daily crossed intersection, Filmore and Pissless. He flips the phone open and in one strange occurrence, the name strikes an odd chord in him. Statuz Quo. Yeah, him the other half of Playaz Inc. but as Dante presses the send button on his phone, Dante can hear the loud, yet arousing sounds of what seems to be sex through the phone. 'QUO! WHAT IN THE JEDI KNIGHT FUCK ARE YOU WATCHING?!' 'PRANKS WITH ATTITUDE: GAY PORNO JEDI STYLE!' Laughter ensues as Quo shuts off the televison found in his hotel room, back in Japan of course. The larger man of the pair happens to have a red bandanna tied upside down in a Tupac fashion with his shirt off and a pair of black Iceberg pants. Remember, Iceberg pants are better than Prankster pants because they're SPOOFAGE PROOF! 'You know we got a match on Friday, thanks to those bitches the fans.' Dante mused to Quo, who suddenly cut the tape back on, as the two men can hear the screams of what seems to be Gaz Green. 'OH! OH! OH! UH! UH! UH! BITE ME LITTLE PUPPY! BITE ME!' '....' '...the hell?' '...' 'Stop.' '...' 'Cut the bullshit out.' '...' Quo barks at Jackson, 'SPEAK YOU WORDED CAPSULATED JOKE OF A BLACK MAN WHO'S NAME IS MOCKED WITH ANOTHER BAD MOTHERFUCKA!' Dante wakes up for a second, 'Oh, I thought the porno was still going on.' Quo slaps his head as he begins to recline back in a executive chair, puffing away on a lighted blunt. Jackson sighs, 'Alright, so you're saying that those Pranksters get another shot? After we beat them on Sunday?' 'Apparently, the fans didn't see it that way.' 'So?' Jackson quips. 'If I wanted to be a lazy bum who begged for everything instead of working hard for it and then lying my way into a Tag Team title shot then I'd be wrong?' 'Stop telling our secret plans!' 'Sorry,' Dante muses. 'Guess they're wanting to be Playaz.' 'Hold that thought...' Quo told him, before hanging up the phone. Jackson eyes the phone, much as he would his hand in the power that lies inside of it. 'Ain't that a bitch?' Dante slyly walks up from the steps of the porch and catch a bite to eat, before the phone rings AGAIN. Dante tries to act impatient, standing on the sidewalk before a daily crossed intersection, Filmore and Pissless. He flips the phone open and in one strange occurrence, the name strikes an odd chord in him. Mommy Dearest: Bunnykins Luvs U! Now Dante wasn't a momma's boy to say the very least as well he did respect his mother like the Bible told him to do and he even made things to promise to his mother when he blew up in the land of music and television and glitz and fame. His eyes didn't bug out in a instance as he read the three letters, but it was close towards that. His mom, calling him like he was a cheap prostitute you regularly called. 'Hi mom.' 'Why didn't you tell me you were on television Sunday? How come you don't tell me anything now a days? Did your father tell you not to speak to me or something?' 'Mom, chill out nothing like that can happen. Besides Dad is worried about even trying to keep up with wrestling! You know he's still working his job as aide to Colin Powell and will for two more years hopefully, so don't even try that.' Dante stated. 'Still, how you doing precious?' Dante sighed, he hated being called precious from his mom, it always made her seem like a grandmother instead of a mother. He walked down the street still with a smile on his face as he began to tell his mother of the times in which his first day as a champion, including the Tag Team Cage Match against P.W.A, also known as Pussy Whipped Assholes Who Want Our Titles. She congratulates him for the victory, even though he did most of the clean up duty. She asks him with a perpetual question that most people over thirty five in the new generation of living happen to ask about music, 'What's the difference between rap and poetry.' 'You don't call my writing poetry?' Dante as in a derivative fashion, easily and confident that he might here the answer he was looking for. Fat chance though, as his mother strikes down upon it with a furious blow. 'It isn't poetry if it doesn't have anything deep to tell.' 'I have something deep to tell, everyone other than me may or may not, but you've heared it haven't you? I mean I played a few songs from the demo and you said you liked it.' He stops before going on, thinking that maybe motherly affection might have caused her to nod her head to it. The background to it wasn't part of the computerized day, more like 'Old School' instrumentals, like Ice Cube's 'Today Was A Good Day', P-Diddy's 'Can't Hold Me Down', and Bob Marley's 'I Shot The Sherrif'. All classics in their day, yet Dante felt that his mind was made up, no matter what his mother told him. 'Or were you just saying that to get my hopes up?' 'No - I happened to enjoy it. Really' She stammered. Dante knew a liar when they happened to stutter. He clicked his tongue and said his goodbye, before hanging up the phone. The impossible stood before him, cloaked in doubters blood and liars words. His eyes lit up like fire in the distance, a smile on his face without a curl but a straight one. Sunlight captivated people into thinking that inner beauty could be shown either way, from one side towards another, but Dante couldn't feel that inner beauty for he had been betrayed, by Proctor, by his mother. No worries though, it was all in his palm, and now was the time to get everything out of the way and let the end come. Nah, the end's coming for Pranks With Attitude as he gets strung up way back for flashback memoirs and played as the laughing stock. Dante puts himself through the doors of the Airport Terminal, re-location hadn't been a strong point in his life, although he needed to get out of the City, both of them in order to try and get music done. 'What flight will you be staying on sir?' The flight correspondent asked of Dante as he had his head turned, looking directly at a buxom woman walking out of the airport, with a slight curved figure and pretty face. 'Huh?' He turned around to face the correspondent with that stupid look on his face, couldn't blame the guy though. 'The flight?' Dante studdered out, 'Oh yeah, the flight! Book me for one ticket round way to Osaka.' She began tapping computer keys with her fingers, rapid and quiet incessant in noise. It was kind of like that of a pair of feet, wooden shoes clacking against a surface. He didn't pay attention though, as he rolled his eyes in the back of his head before being reawakened by the voice of the correspondent. 'Okay sir, your flight happens to be at Gate 9 at 8:35.' Looking down at his watch, he could easily tell that he was a little late trying to get from New Jersey to Osaka Japan, plus the hour time difference. His watch ending up reading with the short hand on the eighth dial and the long hand on the sixth dial. 'Crap!' he exclaimed, 'I'm late!' Like a hopped up junkie, Dante rushes out of the doors and ends out on Gate 9 Terminal, his eyes catching the plane out of the corner of his eye. He said nothing towards his other passengers and rushed up those stairs, his shoes clacking along the metal. Rushing down the cornflower blue asile, he ended up on a window seat, right next to a black midnight sky and not a single ounce of cloud cover. Just the blazing stars and the gleaming moon to look at for just an hour, or two. A slight smile came across his face as he looked at the other passengers passing him as they gait their way to the appropriate seating that they were given on the ticket stub currently stuffed betweeen pocket lent and wallet containments. 'I wonder what the hell Reno wanted with me anyway,' Dante stated as he began to slip on a pair of headphones connect to the headphone jack on his portable CD player. 'one reason why you never get involved with dumbshits like him. Stupid pranksters.' The plane engine whooshed along as the plane picked speed on the runway, with its wings now in the air. Dante could catch some shut eye, for only a few moments or so.
