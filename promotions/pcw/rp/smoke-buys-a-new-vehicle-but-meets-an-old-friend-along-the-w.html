Smoke buys a new vehicle... but meets an old friend along the way...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Previously Recorded: October 4, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up into a stormy day. The clouds in the sky are extremely dark and off in the distance you can see and hear the strikes of lightening and the booming thunder. The scene pans down to show you the entire scene instead of the sky. It's that of a car dealership store where you can now see Semore Smoke climbing out of a cab in front of. The scene switches down and now you're right beside Semore Smoke. Smoke is wearing the same clothes that he was wearing at the end of the Cannonball Run race, a now dirty white T-shirt, a pair of stained black jeans, and a pair of Timberlands, that are pretty clean, which shows that Smoke must have been trying to take care of them as they're hundred and twenty-five dollar boots. Smoke has his wallet out and is paying the cab driver, the cab driver is wearing a dirty white shirt of his own, and a pair of brown slacks, he's roughly built and has some rough hairs on his face as he looks to have not shaved for weeks. Smoke pulls out about ten twenties from his wallet and then throws them to the cab driver, the cab driver catches all twenties in mid-air and smiles at Smoke. As Smoke tossed the money to him, he stated to the cab driver… Semore Smoke: That should cover the expenses from Los Angeles, California to Wilson, North Carolina, considering I also paid for gas along the way. {{{{The cab driver sticks the money into his pocket and smiles back at Smoke…}}}} Cab Driver: It sure did Mr. Smoke. Good luck with your wrestling, or whatever it is you do. Semore Smoke: Thank you. And good luck to you with your… driving… Cab Driver: Thanks. {{{{The driver then puts the car into drive and pulls away as Smoke takes a few steps back away from the car. As the driver drives off the scene splits into a split screen. Both men who had been smiling lose their smiles and then say at the same time in the split screen…}}}} Cab Driver and Semore Smoke: Jerk! {{{{Since Smoke had lost his car in LA after a devastating crash at the end of the Cannonball Run, he had no vehicle to get him back to Wilson, and with a fear of flying that goes back to his days in the military, he had no other alternative then to pay a taxi to drive him across the country. The long taxi ride had reminded him of those he took frequently when he first became a wrestler, a little over a year ago. He would take taxis to all the Universities that the Primetime Wrestling Academy wrestlers would visit and entertain at frequently. When he was partnered up with Kya 'Oracle' Brady in the PWA tag tournament that he participated in, he didn't take a taxi so much and just road shotgun with Kya to the events. Kya would later go on to PCW where Smoke is right now ,she made a name for herself and then left on somewhat good and bad terms with the company. Smoke sometimes wishes that he could have told Kya goodbye just one last time before she left, as he was the first real partner and friend he made in the Primetime Wrestling circle. Semore Smoke turns around in the parking lot and looks out at the wide variety of vehicles to choose from. His last car, the blue 2000 Dodge Viper, had cost him up to nearly forty thousand dollars. He couldn't afford another one of those, so he instead asked to be dropped off at a Ford dealership where the prices seem to be lower. Smoke is looking out at the parking lot and to his left is a row of Ford F-150's that are ready to be rolled out onto the street. Smoke never was a big fan of trucks, but if he were to be forced to buy one, then he would buy it. In the center right in front of Smoke is a line of Ford Escorts, these small cars and station wagons look to also be ready to pull out. But, Smoke would only buy an Escort if he had to, just like the trucks. To the right you see a line of Ford vans, now Ford isn't known for it's vans as much as the other vehicles, but this line of vans aren't your normal vans. They're full sized vans, the size that Smoke hadn't seen since he was a little hippy in the late seventies and early eighties. He would love to have a full sized van that he might be able to fit a bed into. He could then go on road trips by himself and not worry about having to pull over at a Super 8 or Holiday Inn and paying for a fifty-dollar room for one night. Smoke begins to walk forward towards the vans as that is definitely what he wants. As he walks towards the vans he looks sharply to the right as he thought he saw a familiar face. When no one is there he just continues forward toward the vans. He walks up to the first one in line, which is a red van with chrome hubcaps and Firestone tires. Since Firestone has a local plant right here in Wilson, all manufacturers in Wilson use Firestone tires, if you didn't then you would probably be shot by the locals who work at Firestone. Smoke begins to walk around the van, admiring all the craftsmanship gone into the new wonder. The brand of the van had never been seen before, it must be some rare feature from Ford that they're trying out. But it says that it's a Ford V-150, much like the Ford F-150 name. Smoke keeps walking around the van, and as he makes his way back to where he started from, a woman speaks up behind him saying…}}}} Woman: Excuse me sir, but may I help you? {{{{Smoke turns around, and as soon as he turns around both people get shocked expressions on their faces. Smoke's mouth drops and so does the woman's. They stare at each other for several seconds without saying a single word. Smoke then speaks up, with a stutter at first…}}}} Semore Smoke: You… you… you're Kya Brady right? {{{{Kya smiles and nods her head…}}}} Kya Brady: And you're Semore Smoke! Long time no see! {{{{Smoke grins a smile from ear to ear and nods his head. The two walk closer to each other and hug a friendly hug and let go. Smoke looks down at her and smiles again…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yeah it's me. Wow… so you work here in Wilson selling cars now? {{{{Kya's smile turns to a frown as she hangs her head down low…}}}} Kya Brady: Yeah… it doesn't bring in the big bucks like working over at PCW did. But it gets the job done, I don't need much money anyways. Semore Smoke: Hey, don't you fret. Look, I understand how hard it is to find a job that really suits you. Took me years to realize that my calling was to be a wrestler, you'll find what yours is soon enough. This is just one of those roadblocks along the road of life that you need to go around. {{{{Kya looks up and the smile reappears on her face. She doesn't look too bad after the year or so that Smoke had last seen her. She's about five foot seven or so, has shortly cut brown hair, her skin complexion is that of a dark Native American look, and she's wearing one of her old trademark necklaces around her neck. She is also wearing a Ford dealership T-shirt that says 'Bill Murray Ford's' written across it, she also wears a pair of dark dress pants, as it goes along with her uniform, and then a pair of brown work shoes or boots to match the dark pants. Kya is staring at Smoke with the look of amazement still in her eyes…}}}} Kya Brady: So… are you married? Or anything… Semore Smoke: Well… nope. Not at the time at least. How about you? Kya Brady: Nah… I never got married, I've got a kid though… not sure who's it is… I think it's some guy named Byron Calver's kid… {{{{Smoke chuckles out loud, but tries to just keep it at a chuckle…}}}} Semore Smoke: Byron Calver was the name of a WrestlingTattler.com reporter. But I don't think the child could be his, considering he's one of the gayest people I had ever seen… or at least I believe he's gay. Let's just say he looks and acts gayer then Gary and Ace from the Ambiguously Gay Duo cartoon on Saturday Night Live. Kya Brady: Ha! Well… it doesn't matter, I don't know who's he is… but it's my son, and I've got to care for him just the same. {{{{Smoke just nods his head as he understands her situation…}}}} Semore Smoke: Well… do you guys get any commission here for selling cars to people? Kya Brady: Yeah, Ha! I saw you looking at this van, do you like it? {{{{Semore turns to set his eyes on the van again…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yeah, it looks roomy inside. You know what, don't give me the standard speech, I'll just take it. You can get the commission on it as well. {{{{Kya just grins bigger, but if her grin gets any bigger, it might get stuck on her face…}}}} Kya Brady: Ok Semore. Thanks, I haven't had a sell in about two weeks now, and business is extremely slow. Just come inside, we'll get the paper work subsided today, so you can drive this beauty home. {{{{Smoke turns around and smiles and follows Kya as she leads him towards the building to get the paper work done for the van that he had just purchased. Kya continues to talk to him as they walk towards the building…}}}} Kya Brady: Will you be paying in cash or credit today? Semore Smoke: Cash, I'll be making payments though, I'll start with the down payment of a thousand today and then just work on monthly payments. Kya Brady: You've got that much money to throw around? Ha! Well, lets just get this baby rolled out for you. {{{{Kya opens the door for Smoke and lets him enter the entrance of the store first. Kya then enters behind him and shuts the door. As soon as she shuts the door, the clouds seem to give up their struggle to hold rain, and the rain starts to pour down in huge droplets. The scene fades as the camera watches Semore and Kya enter an office and sit down to go over the paper work…}}}}
