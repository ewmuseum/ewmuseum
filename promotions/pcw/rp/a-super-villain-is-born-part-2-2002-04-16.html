A Super Villain Is Born Part 2

16 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 16th, 2002 (Mike Frampton is seen in front of a very large Cathedral. People walk by blessing themselves. Mike takes a seat on the front steps of a church. A man sits to his right. He wears a black cowboy hat, a black shirt and black pants. The man all in black turns to Mike. His name is Sean.) Sean: So when do we break the news? Mike Frampton: In due time Sean, in due time. Sean: You say Andy is alive? Mike Frampton: Indeed. In fact, he is somewhat of a quote star unquote. Sean: What do you mean? Mike Frampton: He is sort of, shall we say, wrestler. Sean: Like you? Mike Frampton: Indeed, however I am also a super villain. Sean: What? Mike Frampton: My name is Lex Luther. My partner is the Penguin. Sean: Excuse me? Mike Frampton: Penguin, come here a moment. (The Penguin waddles onto the screen eating fish. He slowly sits down beside the man known as 'Sean'.) The Penguin: Mmm, fish. Mike Frampton: Yes Penguin, we all like fish. (A sound of a drum hitting it's symbol in succession is played.) The Penguin: Who is this young bucco. (Mike turns to whisper in The Penguin's ear.) Mike Frampton: This some moron I found on the street. I convinced him he is Andy Murray's long lost brother. The Penguin: Why? Mike Frampton: It's for my hobby. Lately I have found myself wrestling. The Penguin: So this guy thinks he is some shmos brother? Mike Frampton: Indeed. (Mike Frampton and The Penguin turn back to Sean. Sean turns towards The Penguin with a queer look on his face.) The Penguin: Fish? Sean: Sorry I don't eat fish.The Penguin: I suppose if I had a hotdog you would take it then? (A sound of a drum hitting it's symbol in succession is played.) Sean: When do I see my brother. Mike Frampton: Eventually. Sean: I want to see him now! The Penguin: Calm down Liberache. Sean: Shut up you fat fish eating slob. *BANG * (The man known as Sean falls to the bottom of the church.) Mike Frampton: Damn it Penguin stop killing everyone. The Penguin: That Liberache loving sicko deserved it. Mike Frampton: Now how are we going to get to Murray. The Penguin: Fish? Mike Frampton: That's it, we are through as a team. The Penguin: Nooo, please Lex, I can change. (Mike gets up and walks off.) The Penguin: Oh well. *BANG* (The scene fades out as the Penguin rolls down the stairs on top of the man known as Sean.)
