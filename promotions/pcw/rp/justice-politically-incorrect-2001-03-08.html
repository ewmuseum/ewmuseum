Justice: Politically Incorrect!

8 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Justice: Politically Incorrect! Featuring: Politically Incorrect Date: March 8, 2001 Anger. Pure, unmitigated loathing. Anger is a rather odd emotion. Why? Because it is one all posses...... ......yet few do anything about. Well, there are two PCW superstars who are about to do something about it.. Last time these two men were seen, their promo time was cut by a network affiliate in Des Moines. William S. Powell OWNS Des Moines, Iowa. And he was embarassed, in front of his new tag team partner. William S. Powell DOES NOT get embarrassed. More importantly, he was humiliated in front of all related to PCW: it's workers, its fans, its owners, everyone. See above. William S. Powell DOES NOT get humiliated. And so now, while Lovecraft and his lovely intern sit in Powell's mansion enjoying the day, City Councilman Powell has come to the local network headquarters for a, settling of accounts, shall we say. May God have mercy on the network... ::WDMI, in Des Moines, Iowa. A quiet building, completely unaware of the furor it has unleashed. The most upset man in Des Moines, William S. Powell, is en route to the building in the back of his long black limousine. Unsuccessfully attempting to calm him is, as usual, campaign manager Roger Schultz.:: Roger Schultz: Should I call ahead to the network and tell them of your arrival? William S. Powell: No. I'd rather they be surprised. Roger Schultz: It COULD just be an innocent mistake you know.. William S. Powell: Damnit Roger! It doesn't matter if it was an innocent mistake or not! The fact of the matter is that I was cut off in the middle of my promo! Whoever made that decision is sure as hell going to pay for it! Roger Schultz: Think of how this will look to the voters... William S. Powell: I OWN HALF THE VOTERS! You know that! You know how much help I give the labor unions, the Knights of Columbus, and all other special interest groups in this town! They pay me money and vote for me, and I reciprocate with government funds and key votes! The voters will have no problem with me going in and issuing a wholesale housecleaning of ANYONE WHO CUT ME OFF! ::Schultz has a panic stricken look on his face as the limo comes to a stop in front of local network headquarters. Powell emerges in his usual black suit, looking severely and utterly ticked off. He marches into the building, his eyes staring daggers at the cowering secretary.:: William S. Powell: GET ME AN APPOINTMENT WITH THE HEAD OF THIS NETWORK RIGHT NOW! ::The woman immediately pulls her phone out and prepares to dial. She reaches the other end, and unfortunately, recieves an answer she KNOWS will not be to his liking.: Secretary: Mr. Powell.....he's......he's.... William S. Powell: OUT WITH IT! Secretary: In a meeting. ::Powell looks for a few seconds as if he is going to explode. However, he calms, and turns, a touch of steel still in his voice.:: William S. Powell: Well then, inform him that I wish to see him when he is finished. ::The secretary does as she is told, shocked at how easy she got off. Powell takes a seat in the waiting area, ready to discuss his other annoyances.:: William S. Powell: It now appears that thanks to the inappropriate action of some LeBlanc fellow, that I will have my first solo contest here in Primetime Championship Wrestling on Sunday Sabotage, against a fellow named Ganker. My question of course is, WHO THE HECK IS GANKER? But, thanks to my fine research team, I can answer that question. ::He smiles, reaching into his everpresent briefcase. He emerges with a document entitled 'Ganker', inside of a manila folder.:: William S. Powell: Ganker. Tag Team Partner of Hank. #3 contender to the PCW Extreme Title. And, he, still sucks. What a shock. I mean, he's related to HANK~! He HAS to suck. But still, that doesn't mean I can take the man lightly....We're both tag team specialists..yet I have the advantage of actual TALENT and CLASS. I have been in this business seven months, and yet have more ability than you and that worthless HANK creature have put together! ::Powell has tired of waiting. Rising quickly, he paces like a caged animal to the elevator.: William S. Powell: Next stop....office of a soon to be arrested idiot.... ::The elevator button for floor ten is pushed. Forty-nine seconds later, William S. Powell arrives at his destination. Pulling a cellular phone out of his pocket, he dials..:: William S. Powell: Des Moines police? This is Councilman Powell. We have a problem at 5347 Main Street. Meet me on Floor 10. ::Powell puts away the phone.:: William S. Powell: ALRIGHT! I'M GOING IN!! ::Powell moves to the Executive's door, and tries to open it, but it is locked from the inside.:: William S. Powell: DAMNIT! The bastard locked himself in! Oh well....it's only a matter of time. ::Even as he speaks, the policemen are on their way up the elevator. As I'm sure you guessed, like everything else in this town, they are owned by one man. William S. Powell. Powell cordially turns to the lead officer.:: William S. Powell: Break this door down. Now. ::The officer takes a fire axe from his side, and hacks away at the solid oak door. After creating a hole, the officer reaches inside, unlocks the door, and calls on his squad to enter.:: Officer: FREEZE! ::The network executive sighs, and raises his hands, realizing he has nowhere to go.:: William S. Powell: Take care of him....I have a call to make. ::As the officers cuff their unlucky victim, Powell places a call to his associate, Lovecraft.:: William S. Powell: Yes Reginald, we got him. The situation's taken care of. Mr. Network Executive will be up the creek for five years on, oh....what's a good charge? Income tax evasion? Reginald V. Lovecraft: That'll work. Hey, listen..I'm pulling up some info on Stormbringer and DarkStar. I'm trying to analyze these two for weaknesses.Remember man, DON'T MISS YOUR FLIGHT!! I know you have that to deal with, but you have to get out to Texas for our match! William S. Powell: I know, I know... don't worry! ::Powell smiles as he sees the former networj executive being led off.:: William S. Powell: Now, if you excuse me, I-- err, the network, has to choose a new exec... ::Councilman Powell rubs his hands together as we fade away....:: ---- View Politically Incorrect's Biography
