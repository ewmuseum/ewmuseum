The Dream Becoming Realistic

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-23-02 Prelude After realizing what had just happened to Mark on Sunday he has noticed that his dream has become a reality. That is what everyone thinks when they enter the PCW that they have made it to the biggest fed possible. This was not even thought of to Mark when he entered the wrestling business because he was just in to take out his violence on someone where it was legal. Working from the PWA to the NTICW was just some other feds for Mark. He did not have a dream to be in PCW from his first impression on wrestling. When NTICW announced that they were a feeder fed for PCW and OSW Mark then started to make a dream of where he is right now. Hopefully in PCW Mark will get noticed and feel what other people feel when they are champions.End Prelude Sitting in the grass of his parent’s house and looking out to the lake Mark is thinking about what he is going to do now in wrestling. He now knows that he has a match up on Friday night and also a pay per view match on May 5th with Jordan X Shabazz, Mark’s famous NTICW feud. Mark knows that he is going to have to be at the top of his game now that he is in PCW and that there cannot be any flaws in anything that he does. Mark has worked hard to get to this point and there is nothing that will stop him to get that much closer to be a champion. “Mark, dinner is ready!” says Mark’s mom yelling from the porch screened in door that is letting the cool breeze in from the lake. “I will be there in a second mom.” says Mark turning his head and yelling back at his mom. Mark picks out a piece of grass from the ground and he starts to fiddle with the piece in his hands. As he looks out to the lake some more and he thinks about Shabazz not kissing his ass on Sunday night because of the feud they had in NTICW. But better yet because Shabazz is a civil rights leader for the African American. Mark then sees a wake boarder behind a Ski Nautique with a black tower on the boat, blaring some rap music as an African American man goes by on his wakeboard. “That is all the African American people can do is show off what kind of people they are through their music.” says Mark to himself because there is not anyone else to talk to. “They talk about how they are the best people here in the USA because of their sports skills and because of Martin Luther King. They think just because he made some famous speech that they are in control of the USA.” Mark takes a deep breath for a second, cooling himself off from his blow up to himself. He then stands up from off of the ground and starts to make his way up the hill to his house. “I am going to teach Shabazz that not every time will he win against me.” says Mark getting more steam off from the thought of Shabazz. “I am going to team up with Kannon the PCW commissioner against Duke Williams and Shabazz. How can’t a Neo-Nazi with a scientologist beat a lame ass announcer and a black civil rights speaker? Well the can always beat those two because unlike Shabazz and Duke who are probably out on the town now getting drunk and not thinking about things to come. We are thinking about our match and how much we hate our opponents.” Mark gets to the porch and he heads up the basic three stairs up to the main part of the porch. He then goes to the screen door and he puts his right hand on the door, pushing it open to get inside. Mark gets inside the door and he puts his bare feet on the tile of his parent’s home kitchen. The screen door slams behind Mark, making a big racquet throughout the house and scaring the family cat under the stairs. Mark goes over to the sink and he turns on the hot water, placing his hands under to get them clean from the grass. Mark then takes a white flowered towel and he wipes off his hands. Then he goes over to the wooden round table and he pulls out his seat, sitting down in front of his plate. Mark has his own plate and he picks it up looking at the bottom at the engravement that he made when he was a little kid still playing with the YMCA youth soccer league. He then places the plate back down and he then reaches for the basket with the biscuits under the towel. His mom slaps his hand away from the biscuits and Mark gives her a grinning look. “What was that for?” asks Mark in a curious way because she had never done that before. “Have you ever in this house reached for your food without saying grace?” asks his mom looking deeply into his eyes, along with the stern look his dad is giving him across the table. “No maim.” says Mark looking down at his lap, hiding his face away. “Well then it looks as though you will be saying the grace for tonight.” says both of his parents at the same time, them thinking the same thing. ”Dear Heavenly Father, We thank you for this food that you let us put on the table and I thank you for what abilities that you have given me and my family to make our lives successful. I hope you get us through the rough times of our lives and cheer along with us as we celebrate the happy days of our lives. I am wishing that you will be with me on Friday night and take care of me in the ring against my opponents.Amen” “Amen.” Is said by the rest of the family together. With the grace said Mark then reaches for the biscuits and all of the rest of the food his mom cooked. His mom takes the silver lid off the pot of food and out comes the steam from the food. She takes the spoon and she puts it in the pot, scooping out a spoonful of corn for all of the plates on the table. Then Mark’s dad takes the big napkin off of the meat he has prepared and he takes a big fork to put into the meat. He sticks it in and he then puts a piece of meat on everyone’s plate for them to eat. Mark then passes around the biscuits to everyone, coming back to him after everyone is done getting what they want. “So tell me more about this match on Friday Mark.” his dad says sounding more interested in it because he knows Mark is at the peak of his career. “It is just my debut match into PCW.” says Mark making it sound like it is no big deal. “Who are you wrestling or fighting as you like to call it, darling?” says his mom to find out more about Mark’s lifestyle in wrestling. “Duke Williams and Jordan X Shabazz.” Says Mark once again making it sound like it is not that big of a deal. “Remember dad when I told you that I would be feuding with the wrestling from NTICW? That is the guy.” “Feuding? What is feuding?” asks Mark’s mom not knowing anything about wrestling.“Well why do they have you feuding with this guy?” asks Mark’s dad having Mark getting hammered by questions. “First of all they have me feuding with this guy because he is a Civil Rights speaker for the African American race.” Trying to get his parents understand his character. “Remember that I am a Neo-Nazi inside and outside of the ring, no matter what because I have been screwed over by some many African American people that one day they will find out what it is like to be screwed over like they did with me. Remember me coming home from high school on those days because of what the African American people did to me dad?” “Well son it has been so long that I don’t remember.” says his dad in complete innocent way. “Well I do!” Mark says raising his voice to his father. “I remember exactly coming through that door and going up to my room crying because I got made fun of at school! Because I did not fit in with the rest of the crowd there! Because you guys told me it was alright with having the friends I did! Well to come and find out they were only using me to do my homework. I remember getting wedgies at school and then getting put up by my underwear onto the pegs in the locker room!” says Mark getting everything off of his chest that he had going. His father just looks at Mark, speechless in the manor that he was just spoken in by Mark. He then looks down at his food realizing that Mark had gone through a lot in high school and even in college. He remembers Mark telling him about dropping out of college because he was teased by all of the African American guys. His father also realizes that Mark has never ever felt any feelings for any girls and that he never supported Mark in anything that he did. That he just sat back and watched Mark grow up instead of interacting with his own son. “I am sorry son for all of the things that I have done to you and your life.” says his dad in a sincere way towards Mark. “I know that I never supported you in anything that you did because you never did anything that had any interest to me. I didn’t help you grow up, only just sat back and watched your whole life develop in front of you.” Mark does not say anything only just putting a spoonful of corn on his spoon and the placing the spoon in to his own mouth. A sound of the front door closes and Mark puts his own spoon down and finishes chewing up his corn into little pieces, swallowing it down.“That must be my little girl, coming home for supper.” Mark’s mom says putting her hands together hoping that her daughter brings someone with her. Mark’s little sister who is eighteen years old, one year younger than Mark, comes into the kitchen and looks around the table. Then she goes over to the table and sits down at her seat, across from her mother. “Well where is he?” asks the mother of Mark, wanting to meet her boyfriend. “Come on in Tyrone.” says Jane, looking at the doorway to the kitchen. Mark’s eyes get big as he hears the name Tyrone. No he doesn’t know who he is but just the sound of the name sounds like he is going to be African American. Tyrone comes walking through the doorway and Mark turns his head over that way to see the black man walk through the door dressed in his Fubu clothing with a black do rag on the top of his head. “What in the fuck is he doing here?” asks Mark getting up from his seat and walking towards Tyrone. “Jane you know how much I hate African Americans because what they have done to me. They are filthy dirty and all they are good for are to pick cotton and to do our own work. Why did you do this Jane?” “Mark I didn’t know.” says Jane in a sweet and innocent voice. “You know what, I am sick and tired of this family not supporting me and doing things to make me mad and angry.” Mark says heading over to the doorway. “Why don’t you just give Tyrone my spot in the family because I am out of here and hope to see none of you for a while.” With that said Mark walks out of the kitchen and he goes over to the front door. He latches on to the brass door handle and he turns it as fast as he can in the right direction, pulling the door open. He makes his way out the door and slams the door, sending a picture of Mark in high school from the wall smashing the glass onto the floor of the house. Mark makes his way down the steps of his parent’s house and out on the street in all. “Every time I come it is something new with them.” says Mark to himself trying not to cry emotionally, only to get angrier. “Friday night is the night that I will prove to everyone in the world that I am alive in the world. That what I believe in is right and I will take this as far as it can go. So come Friday night, my debut match, I am going to go out and perform the best that I have ever performed before. I am going to fly a little bit higher and work a little bit harder to get a statement out to PCW that I am here and I am not going away.”
