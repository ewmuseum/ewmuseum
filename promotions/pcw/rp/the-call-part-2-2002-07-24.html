Date: 7/24/02 The phones have been ringing off the chart all day at the Crichton residence. However, I doubt there were for John Crichton but his girlfriend Jaden. Every time he would answer the phone the call would always hang up as if they were hiding something or wait for him to leave. The phone begins to ring again for the fourth time. “…WHO THE HELL IS THIS,” John says with a fieriest tone, so strong, so firm. He wants to get to the bottom of this nonsense. “Clicked,” replied the phone, followed by more and more clicks. As John was hanging up the phone, he noticed the doorknob moving that must have meant that Jaden was get ready to make her grand entrance. John hopped out of the couch, from his up right position and rushed to the door. As he flung Jaden inside and began to interrogate her with the five W’s. Who is keep calling the house JADENWhat are you hiding from me?When do you think I might be able to trust you again?Where are they, I know it is a NIG-! (SMACK) He was unable to get the rest out while Jaden’s hand collided with his face knock out the rest of the words, as he slowly wiped his face, fixed his jaw and sat Jaden next to the phone. “I am sorry for that one but someone has been calling her for the past hour or so and every time I say hello they hang up! Do you know anything about this?” Yea I do, that was running in Jaden’s head. “No, no John I don’t. I am not sure who has been calling.” Jaden looked at John with her dark brown eyes and made a face towards him as if I don’t know shit! Yea I bet you don’t, he thought to himself but he must have said what he was thinking out loud because he wasn’t through him her. “Ok, you know what we will do? We will sit and wait for this NIG—I mean person to call back and you will answer the phone.” John knew that the call was for Jaden but who was calling he wanted to know. As he expected, the phone rang again. “Answer the phone!” Jaden was so nerves she knew the game was over and her time was up. “Hell-hell-Hello!” “Hey, what’s up baby. You gonna be ready for tonight, I wanna take you out!” The man at the other end of the phone said. John began to get fidgety. “What are they saying. Put the damn phone on speaker!” As Jaden slowly put the phone on speaker, he heard the person call Jaden Ebony. “Ebony so what’s up? I know you wanna be with me now instead of that hot headed bitch ass motherfucker you are with now. Why you go with him when you had this Onyx right here girl?” John face turned blood shot red. He was not going to let no one disrespect him or try to get with his woman. John began to shout out at the man on the other end of the phone. “Look, I don’t know who the fuck you are but don’t say shit about me unless you are talking to me, don’t call my house or mess with my woman!” The guy just sat there and laughed, listening what John had to say. He knew John could not stop him from getting what he wanted. He always had a way for getting what he wanted. “Yo, E-baby, holla at yo’ boy later. I am staying at Rasun house, aight!” “Click,” he hung up the phone saying what he had to say. Jaden and John sat face to face. Was their relationship over, who knows! To be continued…
