Bad Date (Part: 2)

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 24, 2003 Last time we were with Tyler, he was out on the town with his girlfriend Kalli. They planned to have a romantic evening out which included dining. Those romantic expectations were dashed when a young woman interrupted their meal to take a picture with Tyler. Kalli, feeling a little left out, starts to wonder if Tyler has any more time with her. Tyler assures her that the rest of the night belongs to her, but that thesis is also proven wrong as John Gargano busts through the door. John decked out in his suit and tie, prances over to Tyler’s table with a smile. Tyler shakes his head already, knowing that this will turn in to an ordeal. Judging by the way things have gone already, Tyler doesn’t think this is the best thing to happen. He hides behind his crystal water glass as he takes a huge sip of water. John approaches him and slaps him on the back with a loud pop. Kalli sits with no amusement and watches the events unfold. Hey buddy, fancy meeting you here. It's just weird how we bump in to each other so randomly like that. Just imagine that,' John says with sarcasm. He had full knowledge that they would be present at this location, but he plays it off as if it is some freak accident. Tyler does not find it amusing, and grimaces at John. 'What are you talking about? I told you just last night that I was going to be here with Kalli tonight. There's no coincidence about it at all moron,' Tyler says in an irritated tone of voice. He remained patient in the presence of the woman, but now his patience is growing thin from these interruptions. 'Oh yea, well, I guess it wasn't as amazing as I thought it was. While I'm here, I better just get something to eat. I'm starving,' John says, breaking away from the sarcasm. He rubs his stomach from hunger and grabs a menu from the table. Tyler snatches the leather menu out of his hands. 'No, I don't think that's such a good idea. Maybe you should go for a job instead if you know what I'm saying,' Tyler says as he nods his blonde head in the direction of the door to the restaurant. John smiles and pretends that he doesn't comprehend Tyler's meaning. 'Why?' John asks, as if he didn't have a single clue. Tyler just sighs and rubs his forehead with the tip of his fingers. He looks over at Kalli who is red in the face, angered over the interruptions of what was supposed to be the perfect night. 'Number one, you are getting a belly on you, and you need to be in shape for 'To the Ring and Back'. Number two, we're kind of in the middle of a date,' Tyler says. He figures that he is insults him enough, he may just leave on his own on account of embarrassment. John gets a goofy look on his face and talks like a baby to the couple. 'Awww, that is so sweet. Kalli and Tyler, starring in to each other's eyes deeply, telling each other how much they love each other. Love is so sweet Tyler,' John says in attempts to be funny. His attempts fail as laughter is the last thing that is found at the now hostile table. 'John, stick to acting, because your comedy skills are a little lacking,' Tyler insults again. He knows it's not the worse of insults, but it would do for the moment. 'What are you talking about? You know that when I did that show at the 'Laugh Factory' I tore the house down. Didn't I Kalli?' John replies, counter attacking the insult from Tyler. He looks over at Kalli who is attempting to ignore him. Yet, he gets right up in her rosy red face and waits for an answer. 'Yea, I guess,' Kalli says through clenched teeth. She tries to agree with everything he says so that he will be more likely to leave. 'All right John; I'll just catch you later. We havn't even ordered yet so we just need a few moments of peace,' Tyler says as he slams a huge paw down on the padded table. The silverware jingles and John jumps back, startled at the sudden outburst. 'Ok, I got you. I might pick up a doggy back on the way out. Is there anything that you recommend from this menu? It just looks like a lot of fat,' John says as he thumbs around with a menu once again. Tyler stands up and pushes John towards the exit. John just laughs and straightens his suit. He heads over to the 'to-go' counter to pick up something to fill his oversized belly. 'GOODBYE JOHN,' Tyler yells to accompany his strong shove. John gives him a goofy wave and goes about his business. Tyler straightens the creases in his gray pants and sits down once again. 'I'm so sorry about this Kalli. I don't know why the guy would track me down here and find it funny to barge in. He's a moron,' Tyler apologizes. This is more of a plea so that Kalli won't get angry at him and ruin the entire night and quite possibly the entire week for Tyler. 'No Tyler, I'm a moron, for ever thinking that you have time for me. I can tell just by tonight that you are way to busy for me,' Kalli snaps back as she picks up her purse. Tyler acts quickly to avoid her leaving prematurely. 'No Kalli that is not the case. I have time for you, and I want to be with you,” Tyler pleads as he gets to his feet to physically keep Kalli present at the place of dining. “Tyler, just leave me alone for a while. You need to get things straightened out in your life because quite frankly, I don’t like riding in the back seat,” Kalli yells. She is done being patient and is demanding results from Tyler. “Kalli, I can’t lose you at this point. You are the only source of motivation that keeps me going. You are the reason that I do what I do,” Tyler says as a short testimony. This beautiful sentiment pulls at Kalli’s heart, but not strong enough. “You’re not losing me Tyler; you just have to get some things figured out. I’m always going to be here for you. I guess the only question is when,” Kalli finishes as she makes her way to the door. She holds the bottom of her black skirt up so it doesn’t drag against the ground. “Wait just a minute Kalli. I love you,” Tyler says as a last resort. He knows that this statement holds a soft spot in Kalli’s heart. “I love you to Tyler, and that is why things are going to start to slow down. I don’t want to see any of us get hurt. The way that see it, I will get hurt because you don’t have enough time for me, or you will get hurt because I get angry at you. This is just the best thing for the moment,” Kalli further explains, stopping in her tracks. “When are you going to give me another chance?” Tyler inquires, already not enjoying the concept of being with out Kalli. A wave of sad emotions his Tyler which is foreign to the funny man. “You’ll always have a chance Tyler. You know my number, give me a call when you decide you want to continue with this,” Kalli finalizes. Just as she finishes, she walks out the clear glass door at a decent pace. She leaves Tyler standing in between their table and the door with a look of disappointment on his face. Tyler goes back to the table and plops down in the plush, white chair. He throws his arms out on the table and lays his head down. His face is buried in to the table cloth as he thinks about Kalli and all the memories that have made in a short period of time. Tears well up in his eyes, but he holds them back for the sake of the public that surrounds him. He tries to keep his head up and maintain a good attitude. He walks himself to the door, takes one more look around, and exits, biting his bottom lip firmly.
