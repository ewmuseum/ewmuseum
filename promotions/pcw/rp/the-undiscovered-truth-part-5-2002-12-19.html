The Undiscovered Truth (Part 5)

19 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/19/02 The Undiscovered Truth Part 5 I will not be a toy. I will not let people have their way with my body. I will not sacrifice myself for a scientific cause. With God as my witness I will fight until the end and not let them taint God’s creation. So when are we executing this plan of yours? You’ll see, just be patient. Things will happen and nobody will be hurt. Everything will just go back to the way they were supposed to be. I hope you don’t mind me asking, but why don’t you just let us have our tests. We’ll make sure your safe and it’ll be a win for everyone. Do you realize the money you could make from these tests? I don’t need money, all I need in this life is my dignity and love. Money is just a delight that we’re granted. If I were to let tests be subjected to me, I’d be going against everything I believe in, not to mention my life would be at risk. We’d make sure your life is treated with ultimate care. Don’t feed me that garbage; you know exactly how I’d be treated. I’ve seen these things go down before. I mean for God sake look what you’ve subjected me to thus far. How dare you even lie right to my face? I’m sorry okay, but this isn’t easy on me either. I have a family at home, and I need this job. If I screw this one up I’ll be fired and my family will be poor. I can’t just let this go away, it’ll affect my entire life. I’m usually a kind man, but in this situation I could really care less. This not only might affect my entire life, this is my entire life. Maybe you should realize the value of a human body before you take jobs like this one. I just don’t know what to do anymore? I’ll agree with you on that one. Can I ask you a straightforward question? Sure as long as it doesn’t have to do with me feeling sympathy for you and your hard life, because I’ll tell you right now, there is now way I’m going through that testing. I just want to know WHAT you are? What do you mean, what I am, I’m just like any other guy out there. I just happen to have a higher energy flow then other. That’s the only minor detail that differentiates me from the normal man. That’s not just a minor detail; you do realize that inside of you is something that is unknown to man. In other words, your one of a kind, your DNA is probably like no one other on this planet. Well you’ll never know because I’m not subjecting myself to that. I can tell you’re scared? Scared, you think I’m scared. Well where in the world would you get that idea from? Do you know what it’s like to not feel emotions, and to always rethink every decision you’ve ever made? I can’t just do think and forget about them like everyone else, they come back and haunt me night after night. Sometimes it’s like I’m living on hell on earth. Sure I have these qualities that woman love and that make me a strong fighter, but God I’d trade them in a second for a normal life. I don’t even know my own name? Do you have a family? I did, my father has built a new beginning, and my mother died. How could your father just leave you? My mother was a prostitute, I can’t even verify if it’s even my father. It could have been a number of people with the same blood type. That must be hard on you to live with. You could only imagine. I can’t say I know exactly what you’re going through, but I have the same past that haunts me. You see my daughter has cancer, and the cancer cells developed because I had a drug habit that I wasn’t able to get rid of. I made love to this random girl, ended up having to marry her, and then because of my foolishness the girl had to pay. I was lucky to let the hospital hire me as patient attendant. You never know how life is going to turn out; it’s best to just forget. If it was only as easy as it sounds. Sometimes I think that in another lifetime we learn from the mistake in this one. That’s why God always reminds us of our mistakes, so that even though we can’t go back and change it, we can prevent it from happening in our new life. That actually makes a lot of sense. I guess, I don’t know what makes sense anymore. I wish I could just make sense out of who I am and why God chose this life for me. Both men sit silently and then the door creaks open and voices are heard as two men enter the room. Both the attendant and Romantico draw their attention to the men quickly as the scene fades away.
