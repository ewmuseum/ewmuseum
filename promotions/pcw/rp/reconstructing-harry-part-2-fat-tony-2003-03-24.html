Reconstructing Harry - Part 2 - Fat Tony

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 24, 2003 [The last time Tommy was in New York City he nearly froze to death. Well, not really, but it sure felt like he was freezing to death with that bitter cold. It had to have been fifty below zero out there. Well, it wasn't fifty below zero. In fact, it was in the mid-thirties which isn't too bad for winter time in New York. Tommy just never really felt the bitter cold too often and it really got to him. He suddenly then remembers his limo ride to the office building where his meeting was held. The way his puffy coat got stuck in the limo just seemed mind boggling and the heinous limo driver flying through the streets like a madman. The car ride may have only been two minutes and just a few short blocks down the road, but it was the scariest moment of Tommy's life. One thing he was upset about when they were leaving was that he NEVER got a chance to be with any bodacious babes. He heard that New York babes were always the best to have in the sack and Harry demanded that they leave immediately to get on with Tommy's training. How bogus was that?! The old Harry would have been down with a little partying and then some hot long night with a bodacious New York babe. Unfortunately, it was the new Harry that was making the decisions at that time. He's cool and all, but he just seem to be that old Harry. Happy go lucky, laid back, and mostly of all...HIGH!! Stoned completely out of his mind! This is why Tommy needs to do this. He needs to get Harry to taste that bodacious marijuana once again!] [As Tommy makes it to the back exit of the airport he looks back toward the security checkpoint with a little distain on his face. It seems that the New York airport likes to treat their customers exactly like the Los Angeles airport. A rough search, going through his things, and a tiny bit of violence thrown in there. His clothes are COMPLETELY wrinkled to hell and back. He hopes that Harry has an iron at his new pad because those wrinkles are going to be MOST unacceptable. Tommy walks outside into the sixty degree spring-like weather and feels a little bit at home. It is warmer back in LA but this isn't too bad. He hails a cab and it just passes him up, picking up a red head with her long legs showing. Tommy sees another yellow cab come around the bend and he waves his hands in the air like an anxious student in class. The cab just passes him up picking up a balding man with a briefcase at his side. Tommy puts his hands on his hips in anger now. Its one thing if a horny cab driver picks up a babe over him, but not some balding, ugly dude! Once again a cab rears around the corner slowing down in front of Tommy. The Tubular One gets his bags ready to put in the trunk and then the cab just peels out right in front of him. The skinny driver hangs out of the driver side window with his middle finger extended] Cab Driver: HAHA! How about you suck on that, fag surfer!! [Tommy drops his bags on the sidewalk in shock] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Now that was just totally uncalled for!! [Tommy walks over to a pay phone and gives Gino's Limo Service a call. Just as he is about to get through a black limo pulls up to the sidewalk. Tommy stares at the limo, then looks at the phone, and then once again back toward the limo] Tommy: That was fast. [Tommy walks up to the passenger side door and the tinted window slowly rolls down. Tommy then sees a grin that he thought that he wouldn't set eyes on ever again. The same heinous limo driver that drug him in the limo last time, Fat Tony. His double chins folding backward as his grin grows wider and his beard stretching from all the heinous fat on his face. Tony lets out a large, deep chuckle like he's some sort of evil limo driver. Tommy just thinks he looks like a really bogus John Candy. Tommy gets in the passenger seat and he stares over a Tony with a 'Well, what are you waiting for...' glance. Tony just sits there and gives Tommy a 'What the hell do you think I am, your slave...' glance] Tommy: Aren't you going to grab my bags, dude? [Tony just lets out another large chuckle] Tony: No. You can take care of your own bags, surfer boy. Tommy: Your the limo driver, you HAVE to put my bags in the car! Tony: This is a favor that I'm doing. I was paid just to drive your from Point A to Point B. I'm not going to be your bell boy, got it? Tommy: See, I can already tell your just being totally bogus. This ISN'T Point A that we're at right now. This is the airport, dude. And Harry's new place is a loft in the city, it's not Point B. I don't know WHO told you to come here and be all heinous, but they just wasted their money. In fact, whoever the compadre is was just better off flushing the money down the toilet. [The limo driver just stares blankly at Tommy, wondering just how he gets by in life] Tony: Step one. Get out of the car. Step 2. Grab your bags. Step 3. Put them in the back seat. Step four. Get back in the car. It'll take you less than thirty seconds, guarentee. [Tommy just grunts and gets out of the car. He then grabs the bags. He places them in the back seat and then, finally, he gets back into the car. He gives Tony a very evil glance, sort of like the one that a child gives his parents for making him do something he doesn't want to do. Tony just shrugs it off and smiles as he throws the limo into gear] Tony: Harry tells me that you lost that title you were carrying around with you. That really sucks ass if you ask me. I once held the title for being one of the best limo drivers in the city, that was for five years. Then some young punk rolls in here with his hair all slicked back and he takes my title away from me. I know how you feel. Tommy: You don't know how I feel, dude. You lost a limo title, that doesn't even COUNT! I lost a championship belt in a wrestling match. I actually broke a sweat when I lost my match. What did you do, eat a giant ham sandwich while you lost yours? Tony: It was corn beef, by the way, and I DID break a sweat. Tommy: Eating the sandwich?! Tony: Yes... Tommy: Good Lord your fat! Tony: Tell me something I don't know, Einstein. Tommy: My last name is Gordo, not Einstein. [Tony just gives Tommy a confused look and then shakes his head. He then looks over at Tommy who is staring out of the window at all of the beautiful New York sites] Tony: I guess you couldn't really enjoy these sites last time as you were crying like a little baby in the back seat. Tommy: I was NOT crying! Tony: You were too. Like a newborn. Tommy: It was the cold. It makes me sound like I'm crying when I scream. Tony: Pft...whatever. So, for a guy who lost that title you really don't look like your too down about it. Tommy: What makes you think that? Tony: Well, the fact you have that shit eating grin on your face and the fact that you have a notebook sitting in front of you that has says, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, PCW World Heavyweight Champion' over and over again. Tommy: It's for an art class I'm taking. Tony: That's not art, that's just words. Tommy: Words can be art. Tony: If it's written as a book or somethin'. Not the same thing repeated over and over again. Tommy: Fat Tony, your really putting a damper on my thoughts about becoming World Champion. I need my most gnarly concentration on that thought. Tony: Who's the retard that gave you a shot at the World Title? You didn't even deserve the Continental Title from where I stand. Tommy: I didn't deserve it?! Do you know the compadres who I beat to get that title and all of the compadres I defeated while defending it? They were heavy hitters in PCW, thank you very much!! Tony: Sure...whatever you say. Keep trying to convince yourself that you deserved everything that you got. Keep trying to convince yourself that your the best wrestler on the roster. Because everybody else who lives in this little place called reality thinks that you suck. Tommy: Well here in Tommy's bodacious fantasy world I'm the most righteous dude in the land and I'm PCW World Champ. My world is much more bodacious. Tony: Yeah...whatever. If I were going up against nine other guys in a no holds barred match I'd be just a little bit concerned about it. [Tommy has a bit of confusion on his face] Tommy: How'd you know about the match? Tony: Everybody knows about that match! It's huge news all over the wrestling world. Ten of the best and biggest PCW stars stepping into one ring to crown a brand new PCW Champion. Why would anybody want to keep something like that a secret?! They'd be retarded to do that. Tommy: Right... [Tommy thinks to how everytime he saw the PCW Front Office number he'd just turn off his phone and go about his business like it didn't matter. He never would have thought they would be calling him up to be in a World Title match like that. Maybe in a Continental Title rematch or something, but never something this big. Some would say it's a big honor to step into a ring with all those great stars, but Tommy just sees it as them having the honor of being in competition with the greatest. The best of the best. The cream of the crop. 'Totally Tubular' Tommy WILL come out victorious in this match, there is really no other option] Tony: Here we go, dumbass. Tommy: Don't call me dumbass. Tony: What are you gonna do, hurt me? You suck! Tommy: Meh. Bite me. [Tommy gets out of the car and looks up at the old German style building in front of him. It looks really nice and kind of reminds him of the apartment building fromt the movie Big. Tom Hanks had a bodacious pad in that movie equipped with a gnarly trampoline even!! Maybe Harry got a hold of a trampoline or something. That will a bodacious way to get him to smoke weed too, but Tommy knows it's not going to be that easy. His one attempt a week ago only showed him that this new Harry is REALLY smart. It's going to be tough] Tony: When your going up there don't just barge in there or anything. Harry got himself a chick living with him and they're probably...you know. Tommy: Jumping on a trampoline? Tony: Yeah...exactly. Jumping on the trampoline....retard. Fade to black
