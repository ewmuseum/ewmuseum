History, a great teacher

22 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/22/2002 *The scene opens up in Jon Kano's plush hotel room, littered with empty liquor bottles and cigarette packs. But he who rocks is nowhere to be found. The scene keeps shifting in an effort to find the REAL legend. Finally, in the master bedroom of this luxurious suite and in front of a full-wall mirror, Jon Kano stands. He stands there, wearing nothing but a pair of jeans. Kano looks like hell, his hair messy, about a three day growth on his face, but just looks into the mirror.* Kano: 'I see the Devil in the mirror'- Dr. Dre, 1994. I feel like this right now. I look into the mirror, and I think back on my career here in Primetime Championship Wrestling. Everyone says that I got nothing left, that I am over the hill. Well son of a bitch, I'll let y'all know when I am over the hill. I am only 26 years old, I just have this thing that most people in this business don't get until they are well into their 30s, it's called respect. I respect this business. I respect those who made this business possible, and I respect those who keep this business going. Tyler Nelson is supposed to be the devil in the flesh, but I beg to differ. If you think he is sadistic, wait until the perverbial shit hits the fan on Tuesday when I step into the ring with a man who refuses to respect this business, Semore Smoke. *Kano begins to pose in front of the mirror, exhibiting his fantastic body that he has worked so hard at the gym for.* Kano: Semore is such a little bitch, when it comes right down to it. Hell his friends don't even respect his ass, so I guess it's about time I checked his ass. I once had a feeling of almost friendship towards him when as I was handcuffed to the ropes by Corey Williams, so Corey could win the last man standing match, he assaulted Corey. I liked him. Hell he '...could come over to my house and f*ck my sister' -Full Metal Jacket, 1987. Wait, I don't have a sister. But anyway, Smoke seemed to find his way in the path of one of my thunderous right hands and down he went. He got back up and decided to make a name for himself by getting over on me. Well, f*ck that. The last person to do that was Tim Walden and look where he is now. Semore can try all he wants to get himself over at my expense but when Tuesday rolls around, it will be I standing over him as he lays on his back on the canvas, barely conscience. Same deal at Wrestle Vessel. Corey and Semore and I one more time. *Kano stops facing the mirror, so he can see his muscular back.* Kano: The three of us will enter the ring on Sunday and only one of us will leave victorious. If history is any teacher, then I will be the one walking out with my hands raised in victory. It wasn't that long ago that the three of us did what we will do on Sunday and I dominated. And history always repeats itself, my friends. You know usually I give my opponents respect, but these two jackasses deserve a swift kick in the ass. I blame their parents. They probably were the type of the parents that would never discipline their child, like the parents of that John Walker Lindh guy. They probably babied Semore, and Corey's parents babied him, despite what he says, and now that caused these two to become whiny, snot-nosed, skinny little shits that don't know their asses from their foreheads. When I did something that was wrong, my father kicked my ass. Every day I thank him for that. He made me what I am today....TOUGH, RESPECTFUL, AND HARD-WORKING. Look at my body, I have sculpted this body like a sculptor chiseling granite. I worked damn hard for ten years to get my body in the phenomenal shape that you see here today. I worked damn hard to start Kano Enterprises, and now it is a Fortune 500 company on the New York Stock Exchange. I am still working hard at as CEO, and own nearly 75 percent of the stock. I have conquered it all in my 26 years. I reign supreme in the wrestling world. I have captured gold on six continents. I am an universally recognized celebrity. I am the head of a major corporation in Las Vegas, the city I love. I am the new-age Howard Hughes. I have a beautiful wife that I love more than life itself. She is my crutch and the glue that holds me together. These two have so far to go to make it to my level. But they are like two little pieces of gum stuck on the bottom of your shoe that just won't get off. I kick around these two for weeks on end and they just don't go away, so I guess on Tuesday, I'll make Semore go away. And on Sunday, I'll make both of them go away...FOR GOOD, FOR ON SUNDAY, I'LL SHOW THE WHOLE DAMN WORLD ONE MORE TIME WHY I AM WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *There is a knock at Kano's hotel room door.* Kano: What? Voice: It's Duke, ready to go drinking, buddy? Kano: Yeah, let me throw a shirt on and we'll go kick it...MALICE STYLE. *The scene fades to black.*
