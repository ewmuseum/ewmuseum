Date: not entered Miss Moonlight is the main event. For the second time in my PCW career, I am placed in the main event. And I plan on keeping my main event record as pure as the moonlit sky. 2 and 0. With the allies I have backing me, there’s no way I can lose this match. Brianna Washington, Tessa Windsor… the Mistress of the Moon couldn’t ask for anything more. Stryker and Trinidad… former DWO wrestlers. They knew the power that women could possess. They worked for the Olden Beast, Callisto Bane, after all. They knew that if they stepped out of line, A WOMAN would beat them back in line. Callisto demanded respect, as do I. I demand respect from PCW, as well as the villainy of Tokyo. They’ll learn that when they step out of place, Miss Moonlight is there to knock some sense into them. When it came to justice in PCW and Tokyo… justice wore a skirt. And eyeshadow… and perfume… panties… but enough about details. You’ll learn the folly of your errors when you’re face up on the mat staring at the magnificent moon shine down on you. But no, even though Stryker, Trinidad, and Labor are the foes on Turmoil, Chet King is the mastermind behind it all. He wants to show the dominance of manhood? He’ll have to prove that he’s a man first by facing me one on one for his honor. And in my eyes, as well as before the moon… the pain and grief he caused his beloved Cindy Bi means he has no honor… Oh crap… I lost track. I’m sitting here on this fire escape keeping watch on the busy streets of my beloved Tokyo. The numbers moving en masse… a harmonic human flow. But something in the middle of that crowd caught my eye. A local goon… goes by the name of Shun. Tall, muscular, sloping forehead and brooding face; the average strong arm. He walked along in jeans and a tank top, licking an ice cream cone, scratching his head and sprinkling his dessert with dandruff flakes unintentionally. “Aha, one of the local rapscallions,” I muttered under my breath. “No doubt up to some nefarious scheme to get over the citizens of Tokyo.” Everyone wanted to get over someone at another’s expense. You couldn’t think it wrong, it was the way society was. I as the keeper of Tokyo wanted to help those who couldn’t get over… by stopping those who could. Wait, what’s this? That fiend… he’ll pay for the crime he committed under the light of the moon. -=-=-=- “Ah, pistachio nut ice cream.” Shun licked the cone, exhaling deeply as he walked along the street. “Nothing can beat it… well, except maybe beating a guy in the alley until he bleeds out his mouth for his wallet. Those were the days…” “The Moon watches over us all…” Shun rolled his eyes. “And on the opposite end of the scale…” He turned around, seeing Miss Moonlight standing tall in the crowd. People backed away from them as they stared each other off, people wary of the new heroine in town… or the new masked nutjob. Either one… “Nice to see you again, Miss Bitchlight.” M/M rolled her eyes, “Clever, but my gestures are cleverer STILL! I witnessed your foul deed!” Shun licked his cone again. “Say what?” Miss Moonlight took Shun by the hand, pulling him through the crowds and crossing the street to approach the youth standing next to the ice cream vending cart. Still rubbing away tears in his eyes, he looked up as Miss Moonlight stood tall over him. “Return that ice cream cone to this poor little kid! He bought it with his rightly earned money!” Shun stood there, and licked his cone again. “No.” Our hero’s eyes widen before she snatched the cone out of his hand. “How can you steal from one so young and untouched?? Is your heart so dark that a LUNAR ECLIPSE is a 100 watt light bulb in comparison??” “Why don’t you make like a banana and split?” Shun said, snatching his cone back with an annoyed face. “What?!” Miss Moonlight snatched the cone back just as quickly. “And let you continue down the ROCKY ROAD of crime?? Why, I’d have to be a CHUNKY MONKEY to let that happen!!” Shun exhaled deeply, rolling his eyes at the weak puns of the hero. Then the boy in the visor stopped rubbing his eyes for a second. “Hey miss. If you want some ice cream so badly why don’t you just buy a cone of your own?” Miss Moonlight’s brow raised confused. “B-but I saw him--- didn’t --- why are you crying if he didn’t take your ice cream?” He thumbed towards the traffic passing by. “Exhaust fumes.” Then he pointed to the front of the ice cream card, the sign reading ICE CREAM FOR A GOOD CAUSE. “I gave him the ice cream because he paid for it.” “He PAID for it??” “Good job there, ace crime fighter.” Shun smacked her in the back of her head and chuckled. She glared back at him, and the mere look caused him to break off down the street. Miss Moonlight scratched her head, trying to figure out what just happened as the kid leaned against the cart. “It’s for a good cause… after all.” On the side of the cart, another message read: PLEASE CONTRIBUTE to the Young Hoodlum Association.
