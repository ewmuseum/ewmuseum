The Documentary pt.1: Selling The Product

29 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29th Oct con·ceit·ed (kn-std)adj. Holding or characterized by an unduly high opinion of oneself; vain. To be conceited is near narcissistic, and that is never a good thing, so they say. However it can do wonders for those suffering from a lack of self worth. It's arguable on how it is right to judge ones self, and just because common society say being conceited is wrong, does not make it so. If society said driving a car was wrong, would it be? That's questionable, but a point is being made, just because society say something is wrong or bad, it does not make that so- it is just the general opinion. Some will be thinking that if you take that theory, then killing a human being shouldn't have to be seen as wrong- Have some common sense. If being so damn conceited is a bad thing, then why do those people never feel down? They just feel pride in their own worth, and not one person can get them down by any insult- isn't that a dream? Most, when insulted feel mentally bruised for a while, and eventually cope. But, wouldn't it feel better to never feel that slump in self worth? Being conceited works!------------------- 'Mr. Diablo, you and your associate can now enter,' the polite secretary speaks over an intercom system. El Diablo and Kurt are inside ABC studios, for what reason has yet to be fathomed, but they are there nonetheless. Both are dressed extremely smart, like they are going into a crucially important meeting. Kurt is in a tuxedo and for some reason is carrying a video camera around with him, and is actually recording the going ons. He turns the digital video camera to face him. '1:58pm, after an hour of waiting we are finally going into our meeting,' he says with a cheesy smile. Meanwhile, El Diablo is walking behind Kurt, one half of his security. Diablo is wearing a blue shirt with a red tie, and also has on some nice black pants and polished shoes. His long black hair is tied up in a ponytail. It's definitely an important meeting of some sort. Walking into the office is definitely a marvelling site, the office is large with a long wooden conference table stretching down the middle of the room. A great window view oversees a beautiful garden down below, and along the walls are many framed pictures of this man and many celebrities, including Andy Kaufman, Danny DeVito and Tony Danza. There are also several plants situated around the room and a large filing cabinet with stationary placed on top of it. This is definitely no office, it's a negotiation room, or conference room. So what is El Diablo's purpose there? 'WOW! A large room, for our meeting,' comments Kurt as he studies this large room, with wooden walls and a red-carpeted floor. As Kurt remains filming, El Diablo steps in front of the camera and at the end of the conference table, a small, balding man is sitting with a glass of water by his side and pen and paper directly adjacent of him. The man stands up and walks over to where El Diablo is. The man is wearing a completely blue suit, bar the black tie and black shoes, and from close up the man wears rather small spectacles. 'Hello Mr. Diablo,' he says while offering his hand to shake, 'I know you've wanted this meeting for a long time so here it is. My name is Jeff Ruebens, as you will already know,' Diablo looks at his hand and chooses not to shake it, perhaps a fear of capturing germs from him was the case, so Jeff just retracts his hand and walks back to his seat, with El Diablo and Kurt following closely behind him and sitting just two seats away from him. Jeff sorts through his papers for a moment, before finally drawing his attention back to El Diablo. 'So, you got this meeting at your request, what is it that you require?' Jeff asks, as the wrinkles on his fifty-three year old forehead become visible. The Mexican Devil takes a large cough, much like anyone does when they have prepared a long speech. 'It's something untested, innovative and is bound to bring ratings to your station,' he remarks with a certain glee. Jeff remains sitting there, but has this look on his face telling El Diablo to continue. 'Picture it, El Diablo on your station! Once a week, and a two-hour special during holidays. You will have El Diablo exclusive to ABC,' Diablo says this whilst rubbing his chin. Kurt suddenly stops filming. 'CUT!' he yells, 'El, just go over that last part again, only put in more emotion,' Kurt continues as he points the camera back at El Diablo. Diablo puts his right hand thumb and finger over his eyes and breathes in for a moment, as Mr. Ruebens the ABC chief watches on bizarrely. 'OK, ACTION,' Kurt shouts again. Diablo now has tears in his eyes and gulps. 'Picture it, ME on ABC! Weekly and specials at the holidays. I will be exclusive to your station,' Diablo practically repeats what he said earlier. Jeff Ruebens looks on in amazement at this debacle occurring right in front of his eyes, as Kurt gives El Diablo a thumbs up for a great performance. 'So, what exactly is it you want?' Ruebens gets directly to the matter at hand while swabbing his forehead with a handkerchief to wipe away his perspiration. El Diablo turns his head to the camera and Kurt zooms in for a close up. 'A documentary,' El Diablo exclaims with honor. Jeff Ruebens looks at the conceited El Diablo and suddenly bursts into hysterical laughter. Kurt directs the camera onto Ruebens. 'Man dies laughing,' Kurt remarks as they wait for Ruebens to stop laughing. The Lord of Mexico looks at Ruebens, baffled. The ABC chief, composes himself by taking a drink of water from the glass next to him. 'You seriously want a documentary of yourself to be aired on ABC?' Ruebens asks again to clarify the situation. Diablo nods, 'Yes,' Ruebens places his right hand over his face and takes a deep breath. 'Are you completely out of your mind? No one wants to see that!' he practically yells at El Diablo. The Mexican Devil stands up, and towers over the seated Jeff Ruebens. 'MTV show The Osbournes, and they're crazy. They're all idiots too, and I know I'm better to watch than a drugged up has-been, his annoying wife and two bratty children,' he speaks softly and slowly in anger, 'I am damn more entertaining and therefore DESERVE my own documentary,' he places extreme emphasis on the word 'deserve.' Ruebens stands up, and walks over to the door of the conference room. 'Thank you for wasting my time and yours,' he says while opening the door. Of course El Diablo is irate by now. 'I flew in from Italy to get this treatment from vermin like you?' El Diablo questions him. Kurt now stands up and walks to the back of the room to get a full camera shot of everything happening. 'GET OUT OF MY OFFICE,' Jeff shrieks loudly. Kurt zooms in closely on the red with anger, face of El Diablo. 'So you're not going to give me my documentary?' Diablo asks in a mortified tone. 'SECURITYYYYYYYYYYYYYYYY!!!!' Ruebens shouts loudly. Sure enough three security guards rush in, all wearing matching black suits. 'Get this man out of my office,' Ruebens orders them. The triad of security walks over to El Diablo and start dragging out of the room by his arms. Kurt, meanwhile walks casually behind them still filming. 'YOU'RE ALL CORRUPT. I'LL SEE THAT THIS GETS AIRED ON CBS, FOX OR EVEN COMEDY CENTRAL! I WILL GET MY DOCUMENTARY, JUST YOU WAIT AND SEE!' El Diablo screams at them, until his face is an unhealthy red. He continues to get dragged out of the building and is thrown onto his ass on the sidewalk outside of ABC Studios. Kurt turns the camera to face himself, again, 'I think that went well.'
