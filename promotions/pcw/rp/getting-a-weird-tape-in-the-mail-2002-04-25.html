Getting a weird tape in the mail

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-25-02 With Fusion just a couple of days away now Mark is not thinking about his family confrontation that happened yesterday at their house. He just does not understand why she goes for the African Americans that try to show off their baggy jeans and thier pants half way down their pants. Makonie only believes that African Americans should just be slaves for all of the white people who own a farm or who just want to boss around an African American like what Mark wants to do. The scene opens up to the apartment building of Mark Makonie and it only has a bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, and living room. It is in a decent shape but the carpet is all brown and has a couple of stains in it from other people before Mark. Mark is in the kitchen looking around for something to eat and there are three Papa John pizza boxes scattered all over the kitchen counter with a coupld of bottles of Kahlua Iced Coffee laying around on the ground. Mark has his shirt off and he has on some black sweat pants that are perfectly up to his waist so that he won't be like an African American man with his pants halfway down his body. 'Lets see what is in the fridge.' Mark says despertly trying to find something to eat in the morning, opening the fridge door. 'Lets see there is butter, pickles, mustard, cheese, and Kahlua Iced Coffee.' Mark shakes his head as he takes out one on the Kahlua bottles that will be represented in the PCW pay per view coming up on May 5th. He lifts the blue top off of the bottle and he takes a big gulp of the dring. His body shakes and then he opens his eyes as it gets him going in the morning. He then goes over to one of the pizza boxes and he lifts up the lid of one of them. He takes out a cold piece of supreme pizza that looks as it has been sitting out for about three days or so. Mark just lets the top of the box down and he grabs his Kahlua and goes over to his white leather couch to sit down. 'Damn I swear that pizza and Kahlua Iced Coffee is the best thing to have in the morning to get you going good.' says Mark trying to send out a promotion for the Kahlua company. Mark picks up the remote to the T.V. and he pushes the red power button as he puts the cold pizza in his mouth, taking another bite. On the television there is an African American preacher on the God channel preaching about the Church of Crunk. 'The Church of Crunk?' Mark says in a surprising voice. 'That is the church that was founded by Rush Mason and that is what Jordan X Shabazz believes in. That is another event that African Americans like is preaching about the Gods from above that will come down and make a difference someday. You know what I have to say about your preaching? Go pick some cotton!' Mark screams into the T.V. leaning forward as he takes another bite of his pizza. Mark pushes the yellow channel up button on the controller and he goes past the cartoons and the sports channels. Mark goes past the channel of Mtv and VH1 along with CMT the country music station. 'CMT is probably what Duke Williams listens to because he sure looks like a hillbilly with his cowboy hat and he definatly talks like one.' says Mark making himself laugh as he keeps on flipping through the channels. 'Then there was one radio comedian in PCW and his name is the Malice Man himself Duke Williams. Now I tell you that he has horrible radio skills because all he preaches about is getting pussy from his old grandma and his sister. Then he said that he is the biggest, meanest, and baddest of all of the big boys in PCW. Now come one Duke you have got to be joking me because if you wrestle like your mic skills show then you are going to get wasted by Kannon and I on Friday night. Wait you might just show up wasted you whiskey drinking motherf*cker. What are you going to do now Duke since I opened up my mouth? Going to go and get my mom and stick your oil spilled dick in her mouth. You and Shabazz would probably go over to my house right now with Tyrone and Jane getting it on and you and Shabazz tag teaming my mom. That is all your tag team is good for is my mom.' says Mark getting everything he needed to say about Duke off his chest. A knock is heard on the door and Mark puts the crust of his pizza in his mouth and then sets his bottle of Kahlua Iced Coffee on the table in front of the sofa. Mark sets the remote down on top of the table as well as he leaves the channel on a Hitler documentry show. Mark goes over to his white based door and he turns his sterling silver door handle with a pull on the door to get it open. Outside there is the post man dressed in his blue shorts and his blue shirt that has the post office bird labled on. 'I have a package here for Mark Makonie.' says the post office man looking down at the brown envelope. 'All you need to do is sign this form here and you get the package.' the post office man saying as he gives the clipboard over to Mark. Mark looks at the white sheet of paper that is on the clipboard and then he takes the blue pen and scribbles his name down on the sheet of paper. Mark gives the clipboard back to the man and the man gives his the brown package in return. 'Thank you.' says the post office man only to get the door slammed back in to his face as Mark enters his apartment. Mark goes over to his kitchen counter and sets the brown envelop on the counter. He then goes around to inside the kitchen where in a drawer he finds some cutters to cut open the envelope. Mark heads back around and he gets to the brown envelope that was sent directly to him. Mark picks up the package and looks in the top left hand corner to find a name but does not see one. He then turns the envelope around and he takes the scissors to cut into the envelope. Cutting along the edge, the flap on the envelope starts to come loose and starting to make an opening to the item in the envelope. Mark gets done cutting and he places the scissors down on the counter and putting his hand in to the envelope. Mark fells around and he pulls out some bubble wrap at first. Then he grabs an item, pulling it out to reveal a tape that is marked on the front, What a Good Night. 'Who in the hell is sending me a tape about a good night?' Mark wonders as he throws down the envelope and heads over to his VCR in the living room. 'It is bad enough when I get people writing to me about how racist is bad, I just want to know who would make a tape and send it to me.' Mark gets to the VCR and he squats down so that he can put the video tape inside the VCR. Mark then goes back over to the couch and he picks up his Kahlua Iced Coffee drink. He picks up the remote as well and he sits his butt down on the giving leather of the white couch. Mark sets the T.V. to channel three and then he hits the play button that brings the T.V. screen black. 'What the hell is this?' Mark is asking to himself as he sees two people getting it on in a bedroom. 'What kind of sick f*ck would send me a porno of people getting it on.' Mark then sees the cowboy hat at the end of the bed and then he sees the lucious blonde hair of his mom on the bottom of the two people. He sees the head of the man turn and he recongnizes that face with the thick brown beard from a distance. 'Hi y'all.' says Duke Williams into the camera as he is going up and down, riding Makonie's mother. 'He did tell me that we could get my mom if I talked about him one more time.' says Mark can't believing his eyes. 'I am glad that he can get my mom because I don't really care about her or anybody else from my family. Right now Jordan is getting my sister probably and I don't reall care because she is a dirty hoe.' Mark grabs for the remote and then pushes the stop button on the bottom half of the remote. He then throws the remote control down on the couch and getting up and grabbing his Kahlua Iced Coffee. He goes and he heads to his room where he has all of his Nazi items hanging on the wall, from a Confederate flag to a Nazi painting of Hitler directing all of the Jews around the concentration camps. Then he has a Confederate flag blanket set up on his bed with a bookshelf full of Hitler books. 'Jordan X Shabazz, you talk about going into PWA and nobody knowing who you were.' says Mark, while he looks over the Hitle painting on the wall. 'Well of course no one is going to know you when you are in a wrestling academy. Then you talk about how Walden gave you a contract, blah blah blah, why do we care how you feel about your contract. You are an African American and we whites don't even care about what you guys think. Do you see this picture here Shabazz?' Mark asks as he points into the picture of Hitler directing all of the Jews around. 'Well this should be you African American people because you don't do anything to help out America. The white people are the ones who do all of the work to help out all of the America.' Mark takes a couple of deep breathes as he closes his eyes and starts to lay on his bed. He starts to get images of the Malice Man himself doing his mom right in his mom's own bed. He then gets an image of Duke going all around Chicago, the worst city their is because of all their trash hanging around everywhere, telling people about how he is going to beat Kannon and himself on Friday.
