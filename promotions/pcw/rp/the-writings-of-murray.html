Date: 19th March 2K2 Entry Date: 18th March, 2002 It’s been awhile since I have felt like this. I can’t remember feeling this way since the day that my parents died, or maybe it was the day that my brother disappeared, either way, I am feeling the same feelings right now that I felt all those years ago … But the question still remains, why do I feel this way? And I hear you ask, “What way to I feel?”. Well, it’s simple really. Right now, I feel the need to hurt someone. I feel the urge to maim someone, scar him or her for life … Kill them. All this rage that is stacked up inside my head must be unleashed on the world in some shape or form, and right now just seems to be the perfect time to do so. Maybe I am doing it for myself, maybe I am doing it for my parents, or maybe I am doing it for Satan himself, but one thing is for sure, Primetime Championship Wrestling is about to enter it’s darkest era in a long, long time. An era full of nightmares, an era of pain, of punishment, of carnage and destruction. An era where nobody remains safe, not even me. An era that will show the world who I really am, and what I am all about. PCW is about to enter the era of Andy Murray. I am not here to mess around, I am not here to show off, I am here for one reason, and one reason only … Vengeance. I am here to avenge all the torment that this world has put me through during my life, I am here to avenge the death of my parents, and I am here to gain payback on the wrestling world that has overlooked and held me back for so long. No longer will I be oppressed by the wrestling world, people will no longer look at me as a second rate superstars, because when PCW sees the impact of this new nightmare, they will wake up to reality and realise that myself, John Crichton and Ben J. Pailthorpe are the REAL superstars in PTC. Crichton and Pailthorpe … 2 of only 4 men I still trust in this world. We are The Forgotten Ones, we are the oppressed, and we are the overlooked. We are the ones who were bushed aside by our superiors, and we are the ones who were branded “No hopers.” But look at us now, we have hit the big time, we ARE the future of PCW. We have proved all our doubters wrong, as we will continue to do for as long as we are here. Many thought that by making us wrestle in crap-holes like the GWF and PWA, that we would no longer by a throne in anyone’s side, and we would quite happily wrestle out of the spotlight, but guess what? They were wrong. Now that The Forgotten Ones have entered PCW, people are going to start wishing that we had just been accepted into PCW in the first place, but now PCW and it’s superstars are just going to have to learn what happens when you overlook Andy Murray, the hard way. It started on Sunday night with the destruction of the pathetic peon known as Paddy O’Shea. The Forgotten Ones took Paddy O’Shea, and made an example out of him, we crushed him, we crushed one of the many men keeping us out of the spotlight. Paddy O’Shea was the first man to fall victim to our wrath, to MY wrath, and believe me when I say this; many more will follow. Men such as Red Rock and Charlie Loc, the men who were noticed when we were not, will soon learn that they are nothing compared to Andy Murray. PCW has no idea who I am, nobody here knows what I am like and what I can be like in the ring, but let me assure you, the ignorant slobs that work around here are about to find out who I am, what I am. With the power of darkness behind me, I will be the spear through the heart of PCW, I will destroy everything in my path, and I wont stop until I finally get the respect that I truly deserve. My life has been a misery for 10 long years now, and it is time for everyone else to get a taste of the misery for themselves. It shall start on Friday Fusion, when John Crichton and myself shall take your beloved Jobbers Inc. and physically dismantle them. Hank and Ganker, two of the most pathetic morons to ever set foot inside a wrestling ring, will be the first to realise that I am The New Nightmare and that I am the root of all bad things to happen in PCW. I shall fear no one, for those who are not true to the devil are only dangerous to what is weak. Jobbers Inc. shall be the first out of many to fall to the might of the dark side. Entry Date: 19th March, 2002 I was thinking last night. Thinking of how the people of this world could be so ignorant, so pathetic. Specifically, the people around Primetime Central. Why did they ever ignore individuals such as myself, John Crichton and Ben J. Pailthorpe in the first place? And how could they favour the pathetic excuses for superstars such as Paddy O’Shea over us!? It’s preposterous. But I will no longer be overlooked, for my actions will soon have superstars all over PCW begging for mercy. Another thing that I have been thinking about, is the day may parents died, the saddest day of my life. Even today when I think of it, the black heart that is locked away inside me cries. My family is the only thing left on the face of this planet that I still cherish. I can still remember the day that they left forever like it was yesterday. I knew that my father had had a problem with alcoholism since the day my brother, Sean, disappeared, but I didn’t think it would ever come to anything like it did. I don’t know what happened out there, I know my Mother wouldn’t have let him drive if he had drunk too much, and I know that my Mother couldn’t drive. Maybe they were forced off the road by a psychotic mad man? Maybe my Dad just suffered a lapse of concentration, which cost him dearly? I doubt that I will ever know the truth, but I do know that that fateful night cost me the two most valuable people in my life. I was silent for a long time after those events unfolded, because I was trying to fight off the demons that now dwelled inside my head. Demons of unhappiness, sorrow, dread and pain … I knew that I couldn’t escape their shackles. My life was a mess, I was a mess, it was horrible. But thankfully for me, there was one man who I could still turn to, one man who I knew would make everything alright, and who was this man I hear you ask? Why, Satan of course. I looked guidance from the dark prince himself, and he welcomed me with open arms. He showed me the real side of the world; the twisted reality that we all live and breath in … He showed me life. When I travelled through the bowels of hell and back, I transformed into what I am today, I transformed from an emotional wreck, into a satanic minion, a messenger of Satan. I no longer look upon the world as a meadow of opportunities, I now look upon it as a gold mine. A gold mine not for mining riches, but a gold mine for my personal gain. What sort of personal gain? To satisfy my lust for punishment, my lust for pain, and my lust for suffering. There is nothing more I love than putting a beating on an innocent, or breaking the bones of a nobody, it just feels so gooooood. Call me sick, call me twisted, call me what you will, because you know what? You are all right. I am everything you say I am. I am the sick bastard you label me as, I am the anarchist who shouldn’t be allowed anywhere near humanity, but whatever you call me, do NOT call me a heartless bastard, for I do have a heard, be it a very black one. I care only for 2 people on this planet, who for the moment shall remain nameless. I will never be just another face in the crowd, I will never fit in with others, I will never follow trends, I will never do what I am told, because I am m own person, nobody else’s. Independence is a thing I value highly in life, and I am glad that I have the independence to destroy anyone I want without anyone being in my way to stop me. So what if I get suspended? So what if I get arrested? So what if I get fined? Just as long as I get to beat the hell out of as many people as possible, I am happy. I am happy … How can that be justified? That is a good question, because after all, are any of us truly happy? Or are we all living in a pit of sorrow? Are we pretending, or are we truly happy? How do we ever know how we are truly happy? Well, for me, the answer is simple. See, we are all living on the foundations of the world that the dark prince, Satan, created for us, we are just too ignorant to realise it. But, unlike others, I HAVE realised this, like a few others have. Knowing things like this truly enlightens me, it reminds me that I am one of a select few who have seen the light and turned to the darker side of life. As you can see, I am very philosophical. Contrary to many people’s beliefs, philosophy is not the enemy, and neither is literature. Literature truly enlightens one’s soul, particularly the writing and teachings of those who follow the dark prince. Literature has played a big part in my turn to the dark side, and without it, I may still be the emotional wreck that I was years ago. I believe that I also have a match at the upcoming PCW PPV, Spring Fling. An eight man over the top battle royal, for the PCW rising star championship, my first shot at PCW championship glory. Jay Sylo, Lance Bell, Mike Frampton, Charlie Loc, Reeco Suave, and more significantly, John Crichton & Ben J. Pailthrope will be the men standing between myself and victory. I know that the others will not be a challenge to me at all, and I know that John and Ben will be no pushover. It seems like PCW is trying to wedge The Forgotten Ones apart, but trust me when I say that they will FAIL! We have more unity than anyone could ever imagine, and that’s bad, bad news for a lot of people around here. Jay Sylo, current rising star champion, and the self proclaimed Celestial Knight. Sylo would be a challenge to your average superstar, but I will brush him aside like the fool that he is. He is foolish to oppose the ways of Satan, and I Will be doing a deed for the dark prince himself when I destroy Sylo. Lance Bell, a former PCW rising star champion who has seemingly disappeared since the last time we saw him. Like Sylo, I do not see Bell as a serious challenge for me to overcome, because quite frankly his ability leaves a lot to be desired. Mike Frampton, a newcomer to PCW. I do not know much of this young superstar, but I have been lead to believe that he is one of the man who will bring PCW into the future. I, however, be to differ. How can a man that teams with Big Daveylicious be one of PCW’s future legends? Frampton may be an indy legend, but when crunch time arrives, I shall throw him over the top rope like the other 7 guys in the match. Then there is Charlie Loc, one of the many superstars that were holding The Forgotten Ones out of the spotlight. I feel a lot of hate and anger towards this man, for he has token the fame and fortune that rightfully belongs to ME! Loc and his Asian gangster ass better be ready for Spring Fling, because I will show him who the one that should’ve been overlooked really is. Reeco Sauve is another I know little of, but he, like Frampton, is also new to PCW. I have heard his name mentioned a few times before, and what I have heard of him does not worry me in the slightest. As I have said, Crichton and Ben will be me only real challenges, but the unity of The Forgotten Ones will bind us together and I know that one of us will be walking out with The Rising Star championship to our name. I, however, still believe that I am the superior athlete in PCW, and that I will win the battle royal, but we shall just have to wait and see. Hank and Ganker, the task at hand will be a nice warm up, a nice easy victory to help my career get started. Their peculiar antics will get them nowhere, for they have a date with the dark side, and that is something that you cannot survive, no matter who you are. I feel quite offended that I would even be considered to be put in a match with the lowest of low in PCW, but I guess that’s just the way things go. Friday night, I will walk down to the ring alongside John Crichton, and we shall dismantle Jobbers Inc. I shall do it for my fallen father, for my fallen mother, for my disappeared brother, and for Beelzebub himself. I will show the world that The New Nightmare is upon us, and that The Forgotten Ones is where the talent really is! Welcome to my nightmare, I know you’re going to like it.
