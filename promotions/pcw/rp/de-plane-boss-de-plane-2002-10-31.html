De plane, boss, de plane

31 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/31/2002 ~Last Turmoil, he came back. He was resurrected from the ashes of what was known of him. He was reborn to his old ways. He is BACK. The original OLD SCHOOL BASTARD IS BACK. He is no longer the man people love, or more recently the man some people like and some people don't. He is the one, the only 'Man who Rocketh the Mosteth,' and the 'Real Legend.' He is Jon Kano. Last Turmoil, Kano took out the trash as he has so many times before. This time, though, the trash was new and green. The trash was in the form of Mark Amulet and Rich Rollins. These two young rookies made rookie mistakes by trusting the man who when he first came to PCW no one trusted and no one would ever make the mistake of trusting. Typical rookie mistakes, that if they have as many brain cells as a third world crack baby, they will never make again. This Friday, Kano will hold school like he has so many times before when someone has disrespected him or the tradition of PCW. Think about the names who Kano has taken to school, Tim Walden comes to mind, as does Corey Williams, as well as Tyler Nelson. The three men just mentioned had more talent in their left hand than Amulet and Rollins have in their whole body.~ *The scene opens up inside of the first class section of a Boeing 747 heading towards Zurich, Switzerland, the location of this Friday's edition of Fusion. Seated by himself, with no one within two rows of him, is the legendary Jon Kano, dressed in a dark blue Versace suit with a black silk tie and wearing golden framed Armani sunglasses. Kano is feverishly typing away at his laptop computer.* Kano: Jesus, I can't believe how well my book is doing right now. Three weeks from now and a world-wide launch of my book is scheduled. No one thought this book would sell well, or at all. 39,000 copies sold in the first week, and a grand total of 82,000 books sold. But anyway, I have business to take care of this Friday. *With saying that, Kano folds down the screen of his laptop computer.* Kano: I wonder how Mark Amulet and Rich Rollins feel right now. I wonder how their bodies feel after Amulet going through a table courtesy of the most devastating move in the business today, the Kanoplex, and Rollins after landing with a sickening thud on the concrete floor. Most people would call me a coward, but I am anything but. I had all the intention in the world of just having a nice, heart-to-heart chat with each of them. *Kano smiles that smart-ass little sly smile of his.* Kano: I never planned on doing what I did to them, but they disrespected me and that is one thing I don't tolerate. If they were any kind of a wrestler, they would have done their homework and learned not to disrespect me or PCW. They made their mistakes and they paid dearly for them. They are like pieces of trash, just like Corey Williams was, is, and always will be. Not to steal another man's gimmick, especially a fellow old school legend, but I feel like a trash can man. The one thing I have always been good at is taking out the trash and on Friday, I will take these two brand new, green pieces of trash OUT. When both of them are looking up at the lights, with their backs flat on the canvas, and wondering why they are experiencing a pain radiating throughout every square inch of their bodies like cancer. Then it will dawn on them that they have just been the latest victims to the deadly duo of the KANOSPEAR AND KANOPLEX. And that will be how I show them WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!! *The scene fades to black as Kano flips his laptop screen back up and begins to type away again.* ~Christ have mercy on us all if Kano is truly back.~
