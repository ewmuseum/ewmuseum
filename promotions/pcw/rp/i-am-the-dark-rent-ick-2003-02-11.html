I AM THE Dark Rent-Ick!!

11 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: I AM THE Dark Rent-Ick!! Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 2-11-2003 Rent-A-Hero:'INSTRUMENTS OF DESTRUCTIOOOOOOOOOOOOON!!! TOOLS OF CAAAAAAAAAAAARNAAAAGE!!!' ..::/::..Our story begins on the streets of The City, filled with all sorts of loose activity as cars whiz by, various citzens commute about their daily lives, all while the sun showers down its glorious rays upon all those fortunate enough to be in it's area. All the while, one man surely stands out amongst the crowd of random citizens....and that man is our very own PCW superhero, RENT-A-HERO!! It's not as if it wasn't hard to notice our hero with his black and white, fullbody Venom costume on with matching mask, but the fact that he brandishes a pair of headphones and a CD player in hand while listening to 'Transformers: The Movie Soundtrack' sure adds to the extra attention. As our hero traverses down the street, he stops at a large metal pole with a button built within it, a crosswalk for the many citizens looking to traverse across the street to other activities. Our hero presses the button, taking off his headphones and resting them around his neck as he looks to the camera and says to the audience, in his usual happy-go-lucky tone..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'Greetings Rent-Head's with cable capabilities....I have no real idea why it is I'm here at this crosswalk OR why I'm about to deliver a long-winded monologue with no clear cut purpose, but what can I say, the narrator tells me what to do.' ..::/::..Our hero shrugs, continuing to talk...all the while, the bright sky surrounding our hero turns grey with gloom, a cold win breezing through the streets. All the while in an alleyway not even a few feet away from our hero, a set of crimson red eyes can be seen as they gaze upon our spandex-enwrapped superhero with intent. The eyes are by no means warming, filled with an evil unlike that of this world....not a good sign as it says in a low, menacing tone..::/::.. Evil Voice:'1:00 p.m....check. At the crosswalk....check. No reason why he's there while doing a long-winded monologue...check. Pick up mom's laundry.... *sigh* check. Big ass bust that comes out of nowhere and completely mutilates one Rent-A-Hero......'Rent-A-Hero:'As I was....whoa!!!!'..::/::..Some strange fit of fate sends our hero stumbling over the sidewalk, the red eyes staring on at the brutal events that follow as our hero doesn't even get the chance to hit the ground....his body caught in mid fall by a Greyhound bus which comes HURTLING at him, smashing his face and body into the huge grill of the bus. The random citizens scream in horror as our hero goes soaring through the air with no grace whatsoever, slamming against the paved street and laying limp...blood slowly seeping from out of him as a few people check to see if he's okay, car's stopping abruptly at the sight of the fallen Rentable One. With the carnage completed and the fallen...and dead body of Rent-A-Hero laid out upon the street, the man who is the owner of those eerie red eyes says..::/::.. Evil Voice:'Check. Man...I got more hangtime off the last mark though. Oh well, I've got all eternity to break my record.'Shortly... Rent-A-Hero:'Whoa...whoa...'..::/::..The world around our hero is dark, total darkness to be exact with only a single spotlight shining on him. His body is how he remembers it, still adorned in a Venom replica costume and mask...but it seems he has NO idea how he came from laying dead in the street to in this total plane of darkness. Shaking his head and collecting his bearings, Rent-A-Hero stands up slightly groggy as eerie, uncomfortable music starts to play..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Okay citizen...this isn't funny! Someone cut on the lights now before I get reeaaaaaaaaaaaaaally mad and slap the SHIZZNITTLEBAM out of someone!'Evil Voice:'WELCOME TO LIMBO...THE LAND OF THE DEAD...'Rent-A-Hero: *spinning about* 'WHO WAS THAT?!' Evil Voice:'IT IS I...THE KEEPER OF ALL THAT IS DEAD...THE MASTER OF LOST SOULS....I AM....sagffgffffetFVFF.....ERR?! Damn sound syst...fsapsfnifcifdydsis....ARRRRRRRRRRGH!! Screw it!' ..::/::..The hulking, evil voice fizzes on and off with the sound of annoying feedback accompanying it from time to time. Shortly, the sound of a mic being unplugged canbe heard as our hero stands confused by all that is taking place. All is revealed however as a short skeleton in a red robe comes out from the darkness with a Maxim and a PSM magazine under his arm. His bony face is obviously peeved about the faulty sound system for his voice. He meets our hero in the center of the darkness as our hero merely stares down at him, the robed skeleton barely coming up to his waist..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Umm....in all my year's of superheroing....'Robed Skeleton:'WHAT?! WHAT?! HUH?! You thought I'd be TALLER, HUH, TALLER?! Well what you see is what you get pal, so shove it. It doesn't help that I've got a Napolean complex to begin with....now I'm getting picked on by Captain America himself. What you wanna give me fashion tips now huh? Your gonna tell me this robe is out of style?! HUH, HUH?!'..::/::..The robed skeleton is obviously a surly sort, which prompt our hero to step back cautiously from the vertically challenged being. Our hero addresses him in a polite manner..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Excuse me. Forgive me, but I'm confused? What is this place? I mean...if my Kryptonian memory banks serve me correctly, I remember being SMITED with the front of a Greyhound bus and left for dead in the City street. How'd I get here?'Robed Skeleton:'Easy you...YOUR DEAD!! YOUR TAKING THE BIG DIRTNAP!! YOUR PUSHING DAISIES!! YOUR LAYING ON YOUR BACK FOREVER! It was your time spandex boy, and now your here with me! And we've been real busy lately.....damn Dark Gimmick and those other boys of mine, they kill more people then I'm able to handle. No matter, let's see if your on the list. By the way, I'm Death....but you can call me Dave. Sounds less 'intimidating'..::/::..'Dave' snaps his boney fingers which causes a parchment paper list to appear within his dead hands. As Dave reads through the list, our hero awaits to hear his name t know it was truly his time...::/::..Dave:'Ren Jared....Renna Maples....nope...no 'Rent-A-Hero'. Pffft, 'Rent-A-Hero'...who named you kid. DAMNITY DAMN DAMN DAMN IT!!!! I can't ever have ONE century without some major screw up by my boys!! First they kill way too many people....NO THEY KILL THE WRONG GUY! NOW I GOTTA FIX IT UP AND STUFF, ERRR!! Mental note....kill Michael Jackson, yeah...that'll make me feel better.' Rent-A-Hero:'Death'll be a lot better then what he gets when he goes to court anyways my boney-fingered compatriot. But with this knowledge....HOW DO I GET BACK TO LIVING AGAIN?!'..::/::..Dave sighs heartily, his skeleton frame showing so much expression for saying he has no muscle or skin. He scratches his chin while pacing back and forth, Rent-A-Hero's arms folded angrily as he waits for a solution to his problem. Dave snaps his fingers and a lightbulb appears over his head as he says..::/::..Dave:' I GOT IT KID! Since Dark Gimmick and those other idiots have messed up my work....you'll be the one who fixes it! Tell me, do you like wearing robes?' ..::/::..Dave motions our hero over into the darkness with him, Rent-A-Hero reluctantly following as it seems Dave has a plan for getting our hero back to the world of the living..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'By the way, it seems pretty quiet up here for saying this is the 'Land of Lost Souls' and all.'Dave:'Yea I know...damn healthy foods and medicine. Slowing business down here. But things'll really pick up once we go to war....II'm gonna have so much business I'm gonna need my clients to pull numbers! I love this job...sometimes.'..::/::..With that, Dave and Rent-A-Hero fade into darkness with Dave's plan yet to be said...but whatever it is, it is our hero's ONLY hope for returning to the world of the living. WILL RENT-A-HERO RETURN TO THE WORLD OF THE LIVING?! WHAT IS DAVE'S PLAN?! WHY IS DEATH SO GRUMPY?! FIND OUT...SAME RENT-A-TIME....SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!!! ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
