Malice Style: The Karma Sutra Of The South

20 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/20/02 -Duke Williams sits in a chair in front of a card table with a red cloth covering it in front of Borders Books in South Carolina. Duke is wearing a PCW t-shirt, black jeans, chrome shades, and a green John Deere hat. He has a red fine tip maker in his hand as he signs books. There is a long line of people waiting to pick up Duke’s latest book. “Malice Style: The Karma Sutra Of The South.” He smiles as the fans place the book down and Duke signs it. The process has been going on for the last half hour and will probably continue for another hour or two.- Williams: What’s your name? Fan: Timmy. Williams: Timmy you look a little young to be reading this book. Timmy: Malice Style Till The Day I Die!!! -Duke just smiles and signs Timmy’s book.- Williams: Can’t argue with that hoss!!! Thanks buddy. -A heavy set mark wearing a vintage ICWA Duke Williams T-shirt is up next.- VintageFan: Hey Duke I’ve been following you for years!! I’m so glad you got rehired!! YOU DA MAN!! Williams: Alright thanks hoss!! What’s your name. VintageFan: Tony. Williams: I like the shirt there Tony. Tony: Thanks Duke!! So have you heard Kannon’s latest rambling. Williams: Rambling is giving him to much credit. This kid cry’s more then the big haired lady with all that make-up on the holy channel. This prick said him being fired was a joke?? The only joke is this kids life. Tony: He sure is rubbing it in that you lost to him. -Duke strikes a glare at the man through his shades.- Williams: You can win a match and still lose the war. Yes Kannon beat me, but what was the out come?? Does he have the Continental Title? Is his ass still raped by Nelson every night. Did a chick with genitals warts have to help him to win? My balls are still intact, but the outcome proved him noting more then the weasel he really is. F*ck Kannon. -Duke signs the book and the man exits. A young lady of about twenty two with long blonde hair walks up.- Williams: God damn baby I didn’t know Miss America was in town. -The young lady giggles- Williams: So what’s the Dillio? Lady: Can you sign page twenty-four. -Duke flips to page twenty four.- Williams: Ummmm the Malice Buffet. All you can eat baby!! Lady: It’s my favorite chapter but I don’t have anybody to come over to dinner. Williams: Honey if you stand there any longer I’m gonna eat your drawers off of you. -The girl laughs as she hands Duke a card with her info on it. Duke signs the book.- Williams: To the hottest piece of ass in South Caroline….Love Duke. Lady: That’s sweet. Williams: Not as sweet as you. -The young lady exits as a black kid of about eighteen enters.- Williams: How ya doing hoss? Kid: What’s up Duke?? Williams: Not much what’s your name buckaroo? Kid: Kenny. I just want to say I think you and the rest of the Malice Crew kick ass. I think Rush is the greatest wrestler in PCW and Shabazz is pretty damn sweet too!! Williams: That’s why there my brothers in the crew. There is no race in the crew just righteousness. -Duke poses for a picture with the young lad.- Williams: Hi what’s your name? Man: Jim. Williams: Well Jim how the hell are you? Jim: Doing good Duke. I’m glad to see you and Kano on the same side nothing’s going to stop you guys. I think you will do what nobody else has been able to do and that’s stomp out Tyler Nelson. Williams: We will not rest till Nelson is no longer. I don’t care if I have to kill him, it my life long mission. Jim: Kick his ass Duke!! I mean it’s really Kannon and Nelson then two other scrubs and the PI. The crew is a hell of a lot more talented and tighter. Williams: YOU said it hoss, thanks for your support. -Another young lady of about twenty five approaches Duke.- Williams: Hi how are ya you young fawn. -The lady who is wearing an Ellie T-shirt picks up the water on the table and throws it in the face of Duke.- Lady: YOU PIG!!! This book is nothing but filth. -The lady rips up the book as Duke smiles on.- Williams: I bet your not a big Ron Jeremy fan are you?? Look sweetie nobody forced you to read it. Lady: You promote this filth to kids. Williams: You promote that ugly face for the world to look at. Lady: You a sexist. Williams: Yes and your ugly. You know what I think you need, a good p*ssy f*cking. I think your so tense and hard up you p*ssy is holding a protest right now to stick something in it!! Go home and play with yourself honey you need it. Lady: I can’t wait to watch the UA beat you back into retirement you old pervert. Williams: Honey the odds of that happening are about as good as you getting laid in the next year. You dick hating c*nt, I hope you get into a car accident on your way home and f*cking die. -The crowd goes wild as security escorts the women away as Duke wipes the water off of his shirt.- Williams: Damn right. -Duke stands on top of the table as the crowd chant’s speech.- Williams: Oh hell yes!!!! The Malice Man is back in the saddle again!! My hairy ass hillbillies if you can dig that give me a hoooooooooooooooooooosssssssssssssssssss! -The crowd goes wild.- Williams: If you work nine to five, love to drink, love your country, hate your ex-wife, love your kids, love to drive fast, and LOVE TO F*CK then the Malice Crew wants you!!! -Crowd goes wild.- Williams: Oh hell yes!! The UA ain’t got sh*t on me and you. Yea Kannon beat me. -The crowd boo’s.- Williams: But I beat Nelson. -Crowd goes wild.- Williams: and I’ll do it again, like this Friday. Kano is gonna rock it, Shabazz is gonna darken it, and Rush is going to Crunk it. Somebody’s going Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound!! -The crowd goes wild.- Williams: The best part is that hoss Big Alan Steel will be in the ring taking names and laying down the order. He is the world champion and I’ll give credit were credit is do. -The crowd goes wild for BAS.- Williams: I don’t trust anybody but if I have to trust one man his name is Steel. PCW is going to be set on fire Malice Style starting this Friday!! Now lets get some books signed baby!! -Duke hops down and goes back to signing his book as the camera fades.-
