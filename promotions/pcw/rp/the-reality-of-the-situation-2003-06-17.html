The reality of the situation

17 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/06/03 Lacuna, her head draped low, sat down on a small bed. Her tanned face was hidden bestride a cascade of her raven black locks dangling down towards the ground as she closed her hazel eyes and wondered what exactly she had let herself in for this time. For someone who had lived her life off either junk food or stuff prepared for her, she was finding it understandably difficult to exactly manage to cope with living on her own. It wasn’t that she couldn’t do it, she had reassured herself on that matter many times before and during her trip on the PCW airship, but she was sure her inadequacy was stemming from the fact that she could plain not remember how to do anything she was not expected to do. “It’ll be fun, Lacuna! Have a break Lacuna! It’ll do you good to get away Lacuna!” she muttered bitterly as she lifted her head upright and glanced around the spacious room of the VIP section of the airship as it hovered off on its trip around the world. It wasn’t that relaxing really, and she was pretty sure that it wasn’t doing much good for her either, as she had rarely felt that she couldn’t cope on her own before. If anything, it was opening her up to what kind of world she was exactly living in, that people could generally cook for themselves without burning and/or destroying the food. She was so used to having people to do things for her, and not being able to order pizza was quite upsetting as well. “Great bloody break this is. I swear this is the last time I ever listen to people I pay. Bloody morons.” She spat out angrily, her face contorting into a scowl, which suddenly brightened into a beaming smile. “However…” She jumped to her feet excitedly and scanned her deep brown eyes around the cabin eagerly before reaching out with her thin right hand and grabbing a pillow from her bed. She drew the hand back and tossed the pillow across the room, letting it hit the wall with a soft thud. Her smile brightened, as she glanced around nervously. She had never purposely caused a mess before without it being cleaned up a few seconds after by a scurrying tirade of her black clothed henchmen. She had never quite realised quite how fun it could be. Her hand reached out again, this time ripping the duvet from the large bed effortlessly, while spinning it around and tossing it to the floor. “Stop acting like a child Lacuna, you’re a woman of class remember?” she chided herself, although completely ignoring her own words as she looked towards the bed, its covers thrown mercilessly around the room, and launched herself on top of it with a mighty leap and bouncing around happily, flailing her arms wildly as she did so. “Balls to the rules!” she screamed at the top of her voice, not caring whom exactly heard her yells. She felt so oddly elated at doing the most simplistic acts. It was like being young again, carefree and burden less. Gone were the shackles of highly civilised class sorted life and surrounding her was… nothing. She was on her own, on a holiday, and she felt like a small child let loose with a fiver in a sweet shop. By now, her room was more of a mess than her houses had ever become in well over a year of inhabitance. She had never felt seen such a calamity of colours and items thrown together on the floor of the large cabin of the airship. It was quite breathtaking as she stopped bouncing on the highly sprung bed and took in the sheer mess of the floor. “Meh” she shrugged her nimble shoulders and eyed the door with her deep brown orbs “Time for a walk I suppose” So she stepped gently and nimbly down to the trashed floor and made her way to the door, sliding out and locking it in one neatly practiced movement. She stood in the hallway and looked around, the adrenaline surge beginning to drop down to a more acceptable level now. She swept back her raven black hair with the rear of her right hand as she panted heavily, earning a highly dubious look from the passing figure of James Hetfield. James Hetfield. It suddenly dawned on her that the guy giving her such a strange look was actually the front man of Metallica. It was quite a strange feeling really, and it took her aback even more than her earlier feelings of elation. “Been having fun in there?” he spoke, suddenly catching the little woman off her guard, and she jumped in herself visibly. “Oh” she stuttered “Its not what it looks like, honestly” She had suddenly realised quite what it –did- look like, as she was sweating profusely and panting loudly, her hair thrown around in such a wild manner that it did indeed appear she had been dragged through a hedge backwards, as the popular phrase goes. James winked. “Sure, darling. So, you’re a wrestler here then?” “It’s a way to pass the time, you know, getting a chair wrapped around your head and the like” she flushed, it was quite usual for Lacuna to speak to people with money, and she had done many acts alongside famous people, but it was probably the first time she had began to hold down a conversation with someone of his social stature. She held her tongue as soon as starting the ‘what do you do?’ question, realising she already knew the answer. “Enjoy it then?” he probed again, removing a neat pair of black sunglasses that adorned his round-ish face. His bright eyes appeared to look right into the flushed Lacuna, who could do little to return his gaze, as she was still in a state of shock. “Well, when I am not getting killed. Its fun, but at times its not, and then it is again, but I am really babbling now, you can tell me to stop at any time you know? You do? Good…” “Lacuna…” “Its so very exciting to meet someone famous, I’m not sure I have ever been this close to such a talented musician as you, its just so hugely bloody exciting! “Lacuna…” She finally babbling randomly at her infuriatingly fast pace as the words of James Hetfield finally got into her thick little head. “Yes?” she replied earnestly, trying to hide her obvious embarrassment at the situation. “I have to go now… erm, goodbye…” he spat out, before turning and walking at a frightening pace down the corridor and away from lacuna, who sighed loudly. She had never had much of a way with men.
