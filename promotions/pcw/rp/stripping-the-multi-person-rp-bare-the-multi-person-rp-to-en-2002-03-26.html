Stripping The Multi-Person RP Bare....The Multi-Person RP To End All Multi-Person RP's...I Can Only Wish....

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/03/02 The shot opens up on a busy street, just an average street with average shops in a city, or big town, it isn’t really that important as you’ll find out. “Why do I get the feeling that this isn’t going to be a standard Promo...I mean, we have no clue where we are?” With this the 1st couple of Scientology, Eleanor Hall and Xavier Kannon appear, walking through the crowds, away from the areas of higher congestion. Eleanor : “Okay, we don’t know where we are, or what we are doing...This could be pretty bad!” Eleanor is wearing a Pink skinny fit t-shirt with Snapple in White lettering across her chest. With it she has a pair of tight Blue jeans with Silver sequinned stars down the legs. He long, wavy Brown hair with Blond highlights is un-tied and bounces with natural volume with each step. Xavier : “Fear not my little Shag Squirrel, for it may appear that the setting is not important, or that there doesn’t appear to be a set story-line yet, but there is something that will save us...An Ace in the hole, something up the sleeve as it where....” Kannon is wearing a full length Brown leather coat with an open Black silk shirt under it, falling open to reveal a Black t-shirt with “Gold Patron Meritorious” written across it in Purple. With it he has a pair of his Black jeans with Green stitching and Purple embroidery snaking down the left leg to his Black, Purple and Grey Skechers. The Scientologist grins gleefully as he prepares to share the secret to how a seemingly pointless promo can be salvaged. Xavier : “I shall simply draft in ten or twenty other people to do all the speaking!” This concept seems to ring a bell with Ellie~! as a vague look of recognition crosses her face. Eleanor : “Ahh....You mean a Many people Roleplay?” Xavier : “Multi-Person Roleplay my little Poon Puppy, Multi-Person...Whoa....” To fit in with the promo mandate, Xavier says his WHOA in an un-characteristic tone...That and he would never normally say WHOA and point of camera in a promo. Xavier : “Look over there....Isn’t it....WINSTON CHURCHILL AND ADOLF HITLER!?!?!?!?!” The camera pans away to show for dead Prime Minister of England and the late Fuhrer of the Third Reich Adolf Hitler playing dominoes on a sun-drenched street. Ellie~!’s voice is then heard in response to Xavier. “So it is.....” Despite minor differences in the past such as World War 2 the couple seem to be playing amicably, concentrating only on their came. Adolf studies the table then places a domino down to a suspicious look from Winston. Winston : “You wouldn’t be trying to cheat now would you Adolf? I still remember what you did to Chamberlain with Czechoslovakia!” Hitler shakes his head, slamming his fists down on the table that shakes the dominoes. Adolf : “NEIN!!!!” Winston : “That’s okay then....” The portly Englishman puts his last domino down and grins a Hitler in his victory. Winston : “That makes it 3-2 to me I believe...Add the Battle of Britain and World War 2 it’s 5-2!” The pair then chuckle over Churchill’s joke as Hitler shakes his hand, being a good loser. Adolf : “Gut....Gut...” Winston : “Glad to see you are becoming a better sport, I don’t want you putting a bullet in your head everytime you lose now do I....Stalin doesn’t like dominoes so I’d have nobody to play!” The pair of dead mid-20th century rulers laugh heartily as Xavier and Eleanor walk past them, the valet looking confused. Eleanor : “I don’t get it...I know history was like, SO boring but I at least listened enough to know that hey didn’t really get on, I think they had a little fight once or something. As she speaks Xavier grins broadly at his valet’s lack of understanding. Xavier : “That’s the wonder of a Multi-Person RP my little Fuck Ferret, sworn blood enemies who can beat the hell out of each other week in and week out can get along, share a drink, buy cheese together or go on a road trip or something!” The preppy valet still seems puzzled by the concept, thinking it over thoroughly and still finding flaws with it. Eleanor : “Wait, why are we doing this again? Are you Kid Wonder and it’s your birthday or are you Rent-a-Hero making your comeback after being fired, you know, something that would warrant one?” Kannon shakes his head. Xavier : “Nope....I just wanted to post a big sign-up thread on the Fan Board and get lots of names associated with it even though they rarely if ever match the wrestler’s personality!” Eleanor : “But you didn’t post a thread on the Fan Board?” Xavier : “Why would I need to...I JUST HAD ADOLF HITLER AND WINSTON CHURCHILL! Those 2 are A LIST! The couple continue walking on, Ellie~! starting to understand. Eleanor : “So are you saying that it really doesn’t matter what wrestlers are in it, or even if they are in it at all as you already have a set role for them whether they are Meanstreak or Syko the Goblin?” Xavier : “Well, I wouldn’t go as far as saying they have a “Role” as such, they generally walk in and say “Hi”, pretending to know me and then walking off.” Eleanor : “So it’s about reputation by association?” Xavier : “Yes...Otherwise known as Heat Seeking.” They turn the corner, leaving the site of Hitler and Winston sharing a hot-dog behind and suddenly Jerry the famous cartoon mouse runs past them.... Eleanor : “Wow, Tom must be after hi-” As she speaks, Jerry’s arch nemesis, Tom the Cat comes sprinting past them and chases Jerry into an alley. Eleanor : “Either he’s chasing the mouse or Spike is after h-” Again she is cut of as Spike the dog from the Tom & Jerry cartoons nearly knocks her flying in pursuit of the cat. Xavier : “They are like, SO going to have a TOTALLY huge fight!!!” She runs to the entrance to the alley and looks disappointed by what she sees. Eleanor : “Why aren’t they fighting!!!!” Kannon walks over to join her and puts his arm around her shoulder. Xavier : “The same reason as Hitler and Winston didn’t....Otherwise multi-person RP’s would be one huge fight.....This creativity is the one thing that drags them above an in ring Roleplay.” Looking down the alley it is seen hat the trio have a Football and are throwing it around between them, enjoying themselves. Eleanor : “I suppose...But this has now ruined all those cartoon for me...” She turns away, followed by Xavier and they continue past the entrance of the alley and down the street.” Eleanor : “...let’s carry on to wherever w were going...” Suddenly there attention is distracted by a high pitched male voice bellowing. “YOU WANNA FUCK ME? WELL COME ON THEN...I’LL FUCK YOU UP! I’LL KNOCK YOU DOWN AND TURN YOU OVER AND I WILL FUCK YOU! I’LL FUCK YOU RIGHT UP THE ARSE AND YOU’LL LOVE IT...I’LL FUCK YOU!!!!” They look across the street to see Mike Tyson and Lennox Lewis, separated by the respective entourages, trying to get to each other. Lennox : “Please refrain from biting me...” Eleanor : “Um...Something has gone wrong...Why are they trying to fight if this is a Multi-Person RP? I thought that feuds that have taken weeks to build up were thrown away just so they can appear in someone else’s RP even though it won’t count towards their match?” Kannon sits her down on a bench and points to the on going altercation in front of them. Xavier : “Sometimes when a feud actually has some heat with it, then the writer will try to justify making them friends by an even more outlandish idea then simply having 20 people who’s job it is to beat each other into unconsciousness get on...” Ellie~! listens attentively, keen to know the trick that is used. Xavier : “They do this....” The Scientologist clicks his fingers and the shouts are replaced by an appealing cockney accent. “CORBLIMEY GUVNA, UP THE APPLES AND PEARS, EVENING STANDARD! EVENING STANDARD!” Looking ahead of them it is no seen that Lennox is dressed up as Dick Van Dyke in Mary Poppins and Mike has drawn the assignment of being Miss Poppins herself. Lennox : “Me good lady, would you do me the honour of becomin’ me old trouble and strife?” Tyson then bursts out into song as he starts to fly. Tyson : “JUST A SPOONFUL OF SUGAR HELPS THE MEDICINE GO DOWN, THE MEDICINE GO DOWN, THE MEDICINE GO DOWN....” Eleanor looks on horrified at the sight before her eyes, her jaw dropping as low as it can and her eyes wide open as Lennox van Dyke and Mikey Poppins fly off over the skyline. Eleanor : “What.........Was..........That!” Xavier : “Sometimes in a multi-Person, a movie or TV show will attempt to be replicated and the fact that enemies get along is replaced by the even more stupid notion of wrestlers playing characters from the said TV show or Film, as you saw in this case, Mary Poppins!” She still sits in disbelief. Eleanor :: “Why would someone DO that?!?!?!?!?!?!?” Xavier : “Because then they get to post a sign-up saying that they are going to do a Mary Poppin’s RP and people that they have never heard of before can sign up to play 3rd man from the right...Only 3rd man from the right is then replaced by the said person’s name!” The blank face of Miss Hall turns and looks at Ellie~! Eleanor : “Huh?” Xavier : “I don’t understand it myself my little Poon Puppy, but it happens, so therefore it has to be in the Roleplay! Hey....Isn’t that God and the Devil playing Skeeba-” The Scientologist is cut off from his sentence and we quickly switch to a plain White room where the couple are standing confused at their sudden change in surroundings, Lawrence Fishburne walking around them in a long Black trenchcoat and Silver glasses. Lawrence : “I have high hopes for you...I believe that you could be the 2 that were prophesied, now we can leave this matrix loading room and return to my ship where 20 other wrestlers are waiting...Maybe you can eat cake together?” Eleanor and Xavier : “ARGHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!!!!” Fade to Black.
