Date: By A Man Of Immense Greatness The scene opens up on the large apartment of Rush Mason, which normally would be cluttered, but with womanly influence, it seems to be at least partly clean. The first thing we see on scene is Melanie storming into the living room. Melanie: Rush, you’re so childish, and all you ever do is complain, cut the bullshit now okay! Rush emerges, in a wife beater and basketball shorts from the bedroom, looking at her in confusion, a popular look for Rush. Rush: What’re you talking about? Melanie: I can’t stand to be around you anymore! You may be a great guy and I’ll forever be in your debt that you saved me from gorilla man, but you call me when you grow up! Rush just watches on as she walks out the door, blinking several times. She slams the door while Rush rubs sleep from his eyes. Rush: Damn, well I guess that means I’ll just go hang out with the guys. He looks around and scratches his chin for a second, then asks himself the age-old question. Rush: Wait, did that sound too gay? Well, apparently this break-up hasn’t exactly killed Rush’s emotions and he walks over to the sink. He reaches above into a cupboard and pulls out a bowl. He then reaches across his body to the other cupboard above the counter and pulls out some Special K with Red Berries cereal and pours it nonchalantly into the bowl. Cracking his neck, he reaches into the refrigerator and pours some milk into the bowl. Rush then takes his bowl of cereal and spoon and sits down at his computer chair. Rush: Wow, it’s been a while since I used this thing, I’ve been so worried about getting my own job back. I might as well go see what the smarks have to say. He moves the mouse over to the Internet Explorer Icon and clicks, then types in an address for a PCW chat site. He scratches his chin for a second when considering his username. Rush: Hmm…what’s something that defines me? Rush thinks for a moment, and then types in his Screen Name, which happens to be “LikeSOdidNOTjobtoEllie~!” However, Rush thinks one again out loud. Rush: Okay, now I know that’s gay. Rush deletes that SN and decides to type in “KannonBobsForCock.” He enters the chat and here is what his computer shows: KannonBobsForCock has entered the room PCW Fan Chat 22Authority4Life:KBFC, you’re gay.Streak>Meanstreak:I LOVE RYAN STEPHENS!KannonBobsForCock: What’re the smarks up to at this hour?PurpleMonkeyLove: If anyone wants nude pictures of Anna Keyson, hit 69KannonBobsForCock: What is wrong with you people?Authority4Life: What makes you so much better?KannonBobsForCock: I’m not a loser who wastes their whole life on people like Ryan Stephens and Xavier KannonILUVSTORMBRINGER has entered the roomILUVSTORMBRINGER: Heyzzzies! At this point, the camera shows Rush, staring blankly at his computer, but he continues to monitor the chat. Streak>Meanstreak: Ryan Stephens is going to annihilate Rich Mason on Turmoil!Authority4Life: Definitely.ILUVSTORMBRINGER: U guys r cr8zy. BS way outr8’s them!KannonBobsForCock: Rich Mason?DarkAgeRulez has entered the roomStreak>Meanstreak: Yeah, Rich Mason, that black guyKannonBobsForCock: THAT black guy? Is that all he is to you?DarkAgeRulez: No way, The Rush is making a dark gimmick turn!KannonBobsForCock: Rush Mason, you mean.Streak>Meanstreak: Rich Mason is just another dumb black gimmick like Duzza and Statuz QuoKannonBobsForCock: Whatever, enjoy your fantasy world morons. Rush then closes out the chat window and thinks out loud to himself again. Rush: Are they right? Am I really just another black gimmick? I guess I am kinda regular… He then sends a personal message to Streak>Meanstreak and a chat ensues: KannonBobsForCock: Why do you think that about Rush Mason?Streak>Meanstreak: Because that’s what he is, a dumb stereotypical black guyKannonBobsForCock: So you think he’s not original at all?Streak>Meanstreak: Not really, he’s just boring most of the time with his white people jokes and crappy feuds.KannonBobsForCock:Boring? He’s exciting if nothing!Streak>Meanstreak: Well, he used to be, but now he’s just…mehKannonBobsForCock: I’ve turned into meh?!KannonBobsForCock: I mean he’s turned into meh?!Streak>Meanstreak: Pretty much.KannonBobsForCock: But Ryan Stephens doesn’t even have a gimmick!Streak>Meanstreak: g2g With that, the chat is closed and Rush shuts down his computer. He stares at the black screen and continues to think out loud. Rush: What if I am just another black jobber? That’ll never get me over with the fans…I guess I need to change, not just as a wrestling gimmick, but as a person. Wait a second, where did I put that number…? Rush frantically goes over to a dresser and begins to throw things to and fro from the drawer, tossing things every which way. Finally he holds up a small business card and reads it out loud. Rush: Shango Obatala, Vaudan Chieftain and Spiritual Guide, Jacmel, Haiti572-9091I guess I might as well give this guy a call. Rush picks up the cordless phone from the kitchen counter and taps the numbers in, waiting for a reply from the other end. There is no reply, so Rush walks over to a large loveseat and promptly closes his eyes and falls asleep. However, the camera, for the second time in two promos, cuts to a split screen of a dark mysterious figure watching Rush on one side, and Rush peacefully sleeping with no knowledge on the other. This division is strange, but it will be solved somehow…
