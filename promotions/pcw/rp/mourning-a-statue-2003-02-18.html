Mourning a Statue

18 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 18, 2003 [Some totally bogus dude once said, 'Keep your friends close, but keep your enemies closer'. Whoever said that NEVER went through the gnarly world of Primetime Championship Wrestling. One 'Totally Tubular' Tommy has been battling a heinous compadre named Rent-A-Hero for the past month and a half. Did it stem from jealousy? Was it just Tommy's lust for money and power? Or was it because one of them is scared. That one of them has so much fear deep in themselves that they'll do anything, at any cost, to try and push the fear down deeper. It's easy to say that Rent-A-Hero is NOT that man, but his greatest foe is the man with the fear. The PCW Continental Champion is afraid and has absolutely NO IDEA what to do. See, on Tuesday they are teamed together in a match made by someone who is OBVIOUSLY bogus. Two men who have been at each others throats for over a month have to find a way to co-exist. How can they? After the beating Tommy sustained after his sit down interview with Tiffany Jennings, how can these two men even be close to each other without tearing one another apart. Rent-A-Hero will probably be able to handle it, do what any good guy who do, and slide the past away long enough to get through the match. Not Tommy. NO way, dudes and dudettes. His bright blue eyes will be ravaged with anger and fear. Not even he knows what he'll do, but he just knows that Rent-A-Hero CANNOT leave that ring with the upper hand. Tommy has to get the vengeance from the sit down interview. It has to be done in the name of bodaciousness] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Just let it sit, man. [Tommy's brother stands on the back deck, overlooking the beautiful Pacific Ocean, and tries to calm the Tubular One. Tommy has sadness in his eyes, as if a loved one had been taken from him. Harry places his hand on Tommy's muscular, tanned shoulder for emotional support. Tommy lets out a sigh] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: I can't believe that Rent-A-Jerkface did this. He blocks me from bringing Tiffany back to my bodacious pad, and then he DOES THIS!! [Tommy motions in front of him, to the naked lady statue. It's the same statue that was behind him during the sit down interview. It WAS Tommy's pride and joy. A beautiful piece of artwork that he purchased from some gnarly rich dude for fifty thousand dollars. He's had it for years and has always used it for good luck. Even as Tommy battled with the pain in his head from that german suplex, he watched the statue shatter into a thousand pieces. He can still feel Rent-A-Hero's pleasure in watching the statue shatter. How the breasts immediately broke off and scattered toward the camera man. The woman's head bouncing toward that fat guy holding the microphone. The legs and arms shooting in opposite directions, somehow defying the laws of physics. The pain in his head went away and immediately shot down to his heart. It broke the second that the statue broke] Harry: The glue is going to dry, man. TTT: IT WILL! BUT IT DOESN'T LOOK THE SAME, DUDE!! [The statue sits on the patio table, with large amounts of super glue oozing from the cracks. It took them days to piece the statue together and hours to get the glue into the tiny crevaces. Tommy let a few tears drop as he glued the breasts back on. The once gleaming statue looks absolutely pathetic underneath the setting sun of the California coast] TTT: I still can't believe that I have to team up with that heinous dude. How could they do that to me?! Everyone saw what he did to me and WHAT HE DID TO THIS STATUE!! He destroyed something that I truly loved! That I loved more than life itself!! TALK ABOUT BOGUS!! BOGUS BOGUS BOGUS!! [Harry shrugs his shoulders, trying not to look at the statue] Harry: At least he left me alone, man. [Tommy just looks at his brother with an odd look of disgust] TTT: It would have been BETTER if he beat you up! [Harry seems a little shocked that Tommy would say such a thing. It was just a statue that some guy carved out of a rock. If anything Harry can have some carving guy come and make Tommy an exact replica. Hell, Harry can find the chick that the statue was made after and Tommy can keep her in special room to look at her. But what if Rent-A-Hero had gone after Harry? It could have paralyzed him, man. Harry's back is completely messed up and will NEVER be the same after that brutal move he received from The End and Akiyama. It ended the Cowabunga Connection once and for all, and it shot Tommy's single career into an upward spiral. Tommy was saddened by his brother's retirement, but once he started winning it became an after thought...just something he refers back to when he wants to say something mean about The End. Everyone is bogus...except for bodacious babes and Tommy himself. Harry begins to wonder why he puts up with it sometimes] Harry: You can just kick his ass on Tuesday, man. TTT: We're partners though, Har. PCW will do something HEINOUS to me if I would do something like that. As much as I want to tear that bogus superhero limb from limb I think that I HAVE to hold it back! That I have to just pretend that he's someone else, someone who didn't break my naked lady statue. Harry: You can always pretend that he's me, man. That the Cowabunga Connection is back together for a reunion show...sort of like The Beatles, man. TTT: Harry.... Harry: ... TTT: ...THAT'S A BODACIOUS IDEA!! [Harry smiles proudly] TTT: If I can just use my MOST EXCELLENT imagination and pretend that Rent-A-Hero is YOU, then everything will be fine! I won't want to shove a ring post down his throat, nor will I want to kick him in the bal-- Harry: No man should go through that, man. TTT: Harry, he BROKE my naked lady statue! He deserves it. Harry: Ok, man. [Tommy stares back at his statue, remembering what it once was. Full of life, just like he used to be. He remembers that before jumping on the plane to go to a show, rubbing the breasts for good luck. It was that night that he went out for the performance of his career, defeating The End, Dante Jackson, and Rush to obtain the gnarly Continental Title. He remembers the crowd, even though they HATED him, giving him the respect that he deserved. He could see in their eyes that they remembered the Cowabunga Connection and the fun they always brought the crowd. That they never forgot the person that Tommy was at one time. Everytime Tommy looks at the statue he sees a rebirth. He sees a new man in the midst of the beach. No longer is there a fun loving guy who just did things to please his dead father, there is a man who is out there for HIMSELF now! Besides, Radical Randy could never gotten the job done and now his son IS getting things done. He's etching his name into wrestling history, just as he did in the surfing world] TTT: Beautiful statue. [Tommy goes to hold the statue, his love, and it crumbles back into a thousand pieces] TTT: NO!! [They scatter all over the table top and to the stained wood of the deck. Harry looks on with wide eyes, backing away from it. Tommy's eyes well up with tears as he watches his hopes shatter along with the statue] TTT: Bogus... Harry: We need stronger glue, man. TTT: No... [Tommy begins gathering up the pieces, putting them into a small pile. He looks back to his brother with tears running down his cheeks] TTT: We'll give it the respect it deserves, dude. Harry: I'll get the shovel, man. [Tommy nods] TTT: I just need to be alone with it for a few moments, dude. Harry: All right, man. [Harry goes back into the house as Tommy's body collapses over the broken statue. His anger, fear, and sadness wells out into the tears. His large body convulses with every cry he lets out] TTT: I will avenge you, babe!! [Fade to black]
