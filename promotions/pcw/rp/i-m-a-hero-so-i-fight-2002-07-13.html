I'm a hero, so I fight

13 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 13, 2002 Remember the Alamo? It was an intrepid battle where the U.S. held their own against the Mexican armies and showed a fortitude and courage that generations later would be enamored with until the story was told again. Now, I’m not sure if I should be comparing that amazing event in United States history to my battle now, but it looks oddly familiar. The Incredible Hulk versus the very “lonely” Legend Inc. The bets are in, and the Hulk has the odds on his side. Lucky me? The Hulk had a man in his grasp, a poor soul locked in a suit who was obviously on his way to some sort of business meeting. But now he found himself the next victim of the Hulk who was going insane himself. He would have to pray to whatever God he believed in to take care of his family, forgive his sins, and hope that he makes it to that proverbial heaven that everyone’s trying to get into. Ack. Stop praying, you’re not dead yet, bro. I’m still here. Tom Lexian: [shouting loudly] Hulk! Stop right now! I’m talking to you! Hulk looked over into my direction, which made me stop in my tracks. I wanted to get his attention, which I had apparently succeeded in, but now what? I took a step backwards and evaluated the situation. There were almost no people around him so he couldn’t take anymore innocent lives, but there was plenty of furniture for him to toss around. I had the advantage of speed. Shit, is that even an advantage? The Hulk: [angrily] Who are you? Why you talk to Hulk like that? Tom Lexian: [taking baby steps forward] I’m not here to fight you, Hulk. I’m a friend. I’m a good guy. But I can’t let you hurt that man. The Hulk: [stares into the man’s face, who looks like he’s about to pass out] Man dies now. He got in Hulk’s way. HULK ANGRY~! Yeah, well, what the fuck is new? You’re always angry you big, green piece of shit. Now let the man go before I have to take his place. Man: [shakes his head] Please, let me go. I’m sorry I tried to cut you in line. I’m so sorry… The Hulk: Too late for sorry! You make Hulk angry! Tom Lexian: [grabs a chair] Hulk, listen to me. I don’t want to fight you, but if you don’t put him down, I’m going to hurt you. Now I’ll give you to the count to three… The Hulk: [screaming] YOU NOT HURT HULK! HULK HURT YOU! He was right. How could I ever beat the big lug? I had a chair that he could snap into two pieces in half a second, and then pick his teeth with the legs. Tom Lexian: [warning tone] One… Hulk tightened his squeeze on the man, whose face began to get very pale. If I didn’t do something soon, he would pass out. There would be less chance to save him if that happened. Shit, time to be a hero… Tom Lexian: [rolls his eyes] …TWO…THREE! I heaved the chair at the Hulk, who batted it away with his free hand. As I rushed him, he threw the man to the other side of the room, turning his full attention to me. I barreled into his stomach, and I fell back dizzy. It was like running into a brick wall at full speed with your head down. I had nearly knocked myself unconscious. Tom Lexian: [seeing stars] Holy fuck, that hurt… Hulk screamed like a banshee, with destroyed my ear drums, and then grabbed me by the collar, lifting me into the air and throwing me behind the ticket booth. I ricocheted off of the wall, landing next to another airplane ticket employee who was scared and huddled in a ball. Employee: Oh my god, are you alright? Tom Lexian: [rubbing his head] Sort of, I guess. You’ve got to get out of here, or else he’s going to come after you. Employee: I’m not fast enough. He’ll catch me when I try to run. I looked at the bottom of her desk, grabbing a small bottle of water and taking it in my hand. As I uncapped the bottle, I nodded my head at her. Tom Lexian: I’ll distract him. When he grabs me, I want you to take off running as fast as you can. Do you understand me? She nodded fearfully. I stood up, immediately seeing a green hand grab me by the throat. As I was lifted into the air again, I kicked my legs in the air, as a signal to the woman to get going. She didn’t need any more inspiration as she took off like a fox running from a predator. Hulk noticed her running away and roared in furious anger. The Hulk: NO! HULK NOT HAPPY! Tom Lexian: [coughing] Well, what else is new? I threw the water in his face, and it stunned him enough to let go of me. As I stumbled away, I realized that he was already coming back for me. Shit, water doesn’t hurt him. What was I thinking? Does he look like the Wicked Witch of the West? Tom Lexian: Hulk, listen to me! You’ve got to stop this! The Hulk: HULK NOT STOP! HULK HURT! As he reached down for me again, I grabbed his hand, turning it at an awkward angle and twisting it to the side. I stood up holding his wrist and belted him with a punch to his midsection. The Hulk: RUUUUUUUUUUAH! Tom Lexian: [favoring his hand] God damn that hurt. The Hulk let loose with a backhanded punch of his own, one that knocked me clear across the counter again. He ripped the counter apart, not allowing me anymore hiding places and took one piece of it, using like a baseball bat. The Hulk: Now Hulk finish you! Tom Lexian: [stunned] Ugh…this can’t be good… As he tried to slam the counter on top of me, I moved out of the way, somersaulting to his left, using my free leg to trip him. He toppled over and I jumped onto his chest, hammering him with hard rights and lefts. It didn’t work – after all that pounding, he was still one ugly ass mutha fucker. Tom Lexian: [punching away] Take that…and that…and THAT! My last punch didn’t make it, as he caught it, and tossed me back against the wall with an incredible force. I didn’t bounce back, as I found myself entrenched in the wall. As I saw him lumber towards me, I hesitated. I’m helpless… WAIT! STOP RIGHT THERE! I noticed two policemen pointing guns at the Hulk. The Hulk noticed too, because his face twisted in an angry rage again and he began to run towards them. They were minced meat now. Tom Lexian: [getting out of the wall] You fucking idiots. Get out of here! He’ll kill you! One of the officers tried to run, while the other one fired shots right into Hulk’s chest. They hit alright, but they weren’t stopping the Hulk. The Hulk grabbed him by his little pencil neck and was a second away from breaking his neck. The Hulk: NOW HULK MAKE YOU PAY! Officer: [gasping for air] ….No… SMACK! The Hulk fell to one knee as I cracked his cranium with the steel pole again. The officer crawled away to safety as I swung again, this time catching him at the bridge of his brow. The Hulk was only stunned, and soon this would only get him angry again. I did what any self respecting man would do if they were fighting someone stronger than him… GROIN KICK TO THE MUTHA FUNKEN GROIN! The Hulk’s eyes crossed each other and he fell over unconscious. I dropped the steel pole and fell onto my back, exhausted from the battle. He had no weak spots, but he was STILL a man, let’s no forget. And if there was weakness every man had, it was their groin. The bigger you are, the more it hurts. As I laid there, bruised and battered, I could hear the adoring applause from people in the airport that watched my victory. I wish I could acknowledge them, but I couldn’t. The pain was too much. Then a blur or red, white, and blue came into my view. Captain America: Good job, Executioner. You took out the Hulk. Tom Lexian: [smirks] He’ll be okay, right? Captain America: [stares over at him] Yeah, he’ll be fine. When he’s Bruce Banner, he’ll be a little embarrassed, but happy that someone stopped him. He’ll probably be glad it wasn’t Wonder Girl. I had to agree. That would have been more embarrassing.
