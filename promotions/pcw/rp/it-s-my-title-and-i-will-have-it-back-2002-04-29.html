It's MY title....and I WILL have it back!!

29 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/29/02 “It’s good to be the King.” Words to live by, no doubt. When you are the King, you can have anything you want, anytime you want, anywhere you want it. The King is the Ultimate ruler of the land…the be all, end all of law and order. The King is the one who makes the rules and punishes those who choose to disobey them. One could almost be as bold to say that a King is about as close to God as one can get without being God himself. Tyler Nelson is the King of the wrestling world. Being the CEO of Primetime Championship Wrestling makes him the most powerful man in the business. Being the two time PCW World Champion makes him a legend in the sport. Being the man who will restore the credibility of the PCW World Heavyweight Championship makes him the greatest wrestler to walk the face of God’s green Earth. Too many unworthy people have groped, caressed, touched, buffed, grasped, clutched, held, and otherwise violated HIS World Title. Low life losers such as Stewart Hix, Boda, Trashcan Man, Miyagawa, and Ivan Stanislav have had their names illegally etched onto the gold belt that belongs to the CEO. But that’s just the tip of the proverbial iceberg. As of late there has been a band of vandals that have trashed the CEO’s title. Jason Snow, Chris Chase, and Corey Williams have taken it upon themselves to play hot potato with Tyler’s coveted title belt, soiling it nearly beyond recovery. The CEO doesn’t appreciate that. Then there is the rogue challenger….Big Alan Steele. The toga wearing buffoon is giving chase to the thing that the CEO covets most. Steele seeks to possess what the CEO should already have in his vise-like grip. BAS wants the PCW World Title. The CEO, the King of Wrestling, doesn’t want him to have it. And we all know that the King has the final say. And what about the Malice Crew? What of the group of mid-card pseudo talent that has banded together to try and dethrone, to overthrow, to unseat, to otherwise defeat The Ultimate Authority. Come this Turmoil, the Malice Crew may be lacking it’s leader. The Mal-Ass Crew may be missing a whiskey swilling, foul smelling, distasteful dressing, foul mouthed, child molesting, drug taking, anal intruding, salad tossing, piece of shit named Duke Williams. How do you kill a snake? You cut off it’s head. The Malice Crew is like a little snake in the grass that is about to get ran over by the Ultimate Lawnmower. Soon they will be no more, and the world will be a better place. So where does that leave us? Well, it leaves us at PCW Headquarters in raunchy, stinky, smelly New Jersey. The place is just disgusting…who in their right mind could live in a place like this? It’s a good thing that the CEO only has to go there every once in a while. As our picture comes into view, we get an outside shot of the Headquarters, a towering building of all mirrored glass that would almost be majestic if it weren’t in New Jersey. The smog in the air makes it seem cloudy even though there isn’t a cloud in the sky. In front of the building are several flights of stairs, some of which have vagrants camping out on them. Several security guards swarm out from inside of the building and begin to shoo away the riff-raff. As the homeless losers filter down the concrete stairs we focus in on the face of one of them….a rather disheveled looking man of about thirty years of age. He looks hauntingly familiar to Robert Downey, Jr. and smirks as he passes by the camera, snacking on a package of Reese’s Peanut Butter Cups. As the security guard jabs him in the kidney with a nightstick he hurries out of sight, and we turn just in time to see an arriving black Cadillac Escalade limousine, stretched nearly a block long. It pulls to a smooth stop and the driver comes flying out of the drivers seat, scurrying around to the rear passenger door. He yanks the door open and backs away as the security guards block off the sidewalk. Out from inside of the limo comes a bevy of beautiful women…blondes, brunettes, tall ones, short ones, busty ones, petite ones…ten in total. They are dressed in various skimpy outfits, varying from black leather mini skirts with matching tops to skin tight one piece outfits that show off every curve perfectly. They talk amongst themselves like all women do, forming two rows on either side of the door to the back of the limo. After a few moments he appears….the man himself, Tyler Nelson. Stunningly dressed in an immaculate navy blue Armani suit with black leather Bruno Magli shoes, the CEO of PCW scans his surrounding with a disgusted look on his cleanly shaven face. His dark brown hair gently flutters in the light breeze and a platinum Rolex, barely visible underneath his cuff, glistens in the hazy sunlight. He straightens out his tie and pops his cuffs, then begins to walk toward the steps leading to the building. As soon as he takes a step, he snaps his fingers and the women file in behind him, giggling and cooing the whole way. The entourage enters the building behind the escort of the security officers. Moving through the halls of PCW headquarters like a head of state, Tyler briskly makes his way to the private elevator reserved for The Ultimate Authority. The CEO and the ladies pile inside and the doors slide shut. All we see is the mirrored outside of the elevator for a few seconds, then the doors start to open once again. The sounds of erotic giggling and moans of pleasure can be heard and as the doors open completely all we see is a huddle of the beautiful women. “Okay, okay….,” begs Tyler. “We have to finish this later. I have some work to do.” Groans of disappointment are heard as the women peel off one at a time and leave the elevator until all that remains is a ravaged CEO of PCW. He suit is in disarray and his hair is mussed, but the usually present cocky smirk is prominent as ever on his lipstick covered face. He straightens himself out as best he can, wiping the lipstick off with a handkerchief and slicking his hair back with his hands. He bounds off the elevator and starts down the hall, the women in tow once again. The same giggling and chattering is heard from the women that was heard before as Tyler comes to an office near the end of the hall. He turns around to the harem that is following him closely. “Go ahead on to my office,” orders Tyler soothingly. “I’ll be there shortly and we can continue what we started in the elevator.” A wink from the CEO sends the women into a frenzy of lustful cooing and high pitched giggling. They make their way to the end of the hall and the huge hand carved office door. They disappear inside the office and we pan back to Tyler, looking over his shoulder. The nameplate on the door in front of him reads “Gold Patron Meritorious Commissioner Xavier Kannon, PCW Continental Champion and Official Spokesman for the Official Soft Drink of PCW, Snapple~!”. Nearly out of breath in his mind from reading all of that, Tyler raps on the door several times. “Who is it?” comes Xavier’s voice from inside. “Who….is…it?” follows the Commissioneress Ellie~! Tyler frowns as he replies. “It’s Tyler…open up!” Sounds of scurrying about are heard as a frantic Xavier stalls. “Ummm….hold on a second….the uhhh….the door is stuck!” “Door….is…..stuck!” shouts an obviously frazzled Ellie~! Tyler buries his face in his hand and shakes his head back and forth. After several minutes there is some fidgeting with the door knob and it’s easily pulled open. “I thought the door was stuck,” says Tyler warily. Xavier looks at the door suspiciously. “Hmmm…it was about an hour ago.” Kannon shrugs his shoulders and steps back so Tyler can enter the office. We follow the CEO into the office and we can immediately see that it is an absolute disaster area. Lamps are knocked over, sheets are strewn all over the black leather couch, and a few articles of clothing are left on the floor at the foot of the couch. Nelson surveys the room, then looks over at Xavier, who looks back at him and grins. Tyler chuckles to himself and moves over to where Xavier’s desk is. “Sorry to interrupt,” apologizes Tyler sarcastically. “Interrupt?” says Xavier, feigning surprise. “You weren’t interrupting anything Mr. Nelson. Right my little poon princess?” A shrill voice yells out from the bathroom. “Not….interrupting….anything.” Tyler sits down at one of the chairs opposite Xavier’s desk as the Continental Champion sits behind it in a high back leather chair. The large mahogany desk is covered with paperwork, most with PCW letterhead on the top. “Sorry for the messy desk,” says Xavier. “I’ve got all these contracts that I need to sort through. I tell you, that Duke Williams sure had things all screwed up. It’s a DAMN good thing that we got control of the company when we did or else there would have been nothing to be the Ultimate Authority over.” Tyler’s eyes light up. “Contracts? Did you say….contracts?” A smirk crosses the lips of Kannon as he begins to shuffle through the mound of paperwork on top of the desk. He rifles through and finally retrieves a single contract. “Is that it?” asks Tyler like a child on Christmas morning. Kannon dangles it in front of Tyler’s face by two fingers, swinging it slowly in front of his face like a hypnotist. Tyler’s eyes follow the contract back and forth attentively, and we move in for a closer look. The contract says “PCW World Title Match” at the top. Below that is “Supremacy May 12th, 2002”. Still below that is “Triple Threat Match”. The last line we can see, in bold type, is “Jason Snow vs Big Alan Steele vs Tyler Nelson”. “Beautiful……” coos Tyler, his voice fading off as he gazes at the contract. “All it needs is your signature to be official,” Kannon informs Tyler. With a swift move Tyler snatches the contract out of a surprised Kannon’s fingers, then flips through to the last page. He nearly drools as he grabs a pen from Kannon’s desk, then quickly signs on the dotted line. “Get this to them as quickly as possible,” orders the CEO. “I want this thing signed, sealed, and delivered back to me pronto! I don’t want either of those two chicken shits weaseling out of this at the last minute for fear of their lives.” “Got it, Mr. Nelson,” affirms Kannon. “Would you care for a Snapple~!?” Xavier pulls open a refrigerator and reveals it stocked full of the Official Soft Drink of PCW. “Ummm….no thanks,” politely refuses Tyler. “I have to get to my office to umm….take care of some business. Make sure that contract gets signed by those two clowns.” “Not a problem,” assures Kannon. “Not….a….problem,” reiterates Ellie~! from the other room. Tyler gets a strange look on his face as he rises from his chair. “See you on Turmoil for the extermination of Duke Williams?” asks Tyler. Kannon smirks. “Wouldn’t miss it for the world.” Nelson glances at the contract once again as he heads toward the door to the office. As he leaves Nelson mutters to himself, “It’s good to be the King.” The CEO of PCW leaves the Commissioners office and heads toward his own office where the harem of beautiful ladies awaits him. He whistles a happy tune as he reaches his doorway. The scene fades to black.
