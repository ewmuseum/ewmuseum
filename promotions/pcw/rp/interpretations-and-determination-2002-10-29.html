Interpretations and Determination

29 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Interpretations and Determination Featuring: Tim Walden Date: October 29, 2002 A black limousine pulls up alongside the curb of a street, occupant or occupants obscured beyond recognition by the tint of the windows, before stopping, as the window lowers just enough to make out a pair of eyes, peering out across the road..The eyes disappear from view as the window slides shut, and is followed shortly by the opening of the door, from which Tim Walden emerges, looking over the surrounding scenery before walking deftly around the back of the limo, briefcase in hand as he steps onto the sidewalk and begins trekking down what appears to be a typical city street of an Italian town, briefcase in hand as he turns suddenly, opening a wooden door in a stone wall to come into a building...He stops in the doorway, confused. 'Er..Does anybody speak English here?' Walden's voice is a little cautious, as he looks in on whatever is happening within the building... 'I do..'A small Italian voice comes out of the building, faintly, and Tim immediately begins to step through into the room...The camera follows, to reveal a moderately sized bistro, the patron who answered standing up and walking toward Tim himself..He has a distinct Roberto Benigni look to him, though not so much as to cause confusion..'The name is Phil Gariola, I'm an Italian-American bookstore owner on a trip back to see the fa--' 'I just needed the Reader's Digest condensed version, not 'The Big Book Of Phil's Life'...However, I think you could serve a helpful purpose, if you're willing to come outside for just a moment..Say, do you watch much wrestling?' Tim looks at the man quizzically.. 'Well, not really, why do you ask?' Phil seems rather confused at this point, and one couldn't quite blame him with this sort of sudden behaviour on Walden's part, unknowing what was to come from this as he walks out the door... Tim spots the uncertainty on Phil's face, and decides to tread carefully with his next words. 'Well, I'm with the PCW, and I need somebody to act as an interpreter for the next few days, to save me some time in preparation for my match Tuesday...' Phil looks rather stunned, both by the suddenness of the offer and the odd nature..'Well, I'm a little interested, but I want to know how you're going to make up for taking a good chunk of my vacation t--' His query is interrupted by a soft ka-chunk sound, as the briefcase opens to reveal...A bunch of tax papers and some recording equipment...'Damnit, I could swear I put the money in here!'...Tim scrounges about, pulling up the papers to grab a handful of twenties, holding them out before the 30-something man's eyes..'Here, you can have this as a down payment if you take the job.' Tim looks anxiously at Phil, who is thinking the deal over...On one hand, it detracts from his vacation greatly, but on the other hand, he could be paid very handsomely for this...'All right, you've got a deal.' Phil reaches out his hand, which Tim shakes heartily.. 'Okay, then hop into the limo and we'll be off.' Phil perks up at the realization that the limousine is to his right, and he walks quickly back into the building..'Wait, where are you going?!' Phil turns briefly back...'I still have to pay my bill! I did give my name in that place, you know.' Tim sighs. 'Oh, all right...Just don't be long, okay?' Tim walks back to the limo, as the scene fades to black. It then proceeds to fade back in with 'Twenty Minutes Later' in white block letters on the bottom of the screen, before cutting to Tim in the back of the limo, looking mildly peeved...The door opens, and Phil steps in.. Tim gets rather annoyed, and immediately questions Phil. 'What took you so long? And why is there froth on your upper lip?!' Phil shrugs, and holds the drink in his right hand..'Well, you know that down payment? I couldn't resist trying one more latte...And that led to a mochacino...And that led to an iced cappucino....' Tim growls slightly..'Well, let's get started already..I could've replaced you by now, you know.' Phil holds the drink out to Tim, as if it were a gesture of apology..'I figured you might be a little mad, so I got you an iced cappucino too.' Tim takes the drink grudgingly, before grabbing the intercom speakerpiece and holding the button...'Well, don't just sit there, Jasper, drive us out to Florence!', he says with a little less anger, before turning back to his interpreter. 'So....Phil...' The smaller man recoils slightly, nervous from the prompting to speak..'Er..I didn't catch your name actually.' 'The name's Tim Walden, PCW Financial Reclamation Executive and hopefully the next name in the list of PCW World Champions...'Tim leans back in a slightly arrogant fashion, sipping gently from his cool drink... 'Isn't that a little much to say? And what the heck is a Financial whatever-you-said?' The look on Phil's face shows both a confused skepticism and a mild intrigue, as Walden turns his head briefly away in annoyance.. He looks back over...'You know, I haven't really had to explain things much to people who don't watch much of PCW, so this might be a little confusing...' Phil nods in response. 'But it's like this..When I said I'm the Financial Reclamation Executive, it means that I basically take care of bringing in PCW's tax money from the wrestlers so that the cash flow of the federation remains maximized. You're a businessman, you understand that, don't you?' Phil glances over, still intent on understanding in spite of his lack of knowledge..'Yeah, gotta keep the overhead down and increase profits, okay..But tax in a wrestling federation? Isn't that a bit odd?' Tim shrugs. 'Eh, a little, but at this point, there's still a lot of people who're lickin' their lips at the thought of being here, so most of the people don't complain too much for the moment out of fear..There's always at least a couple of tax evaders though, and that's where I've gotta use a little muscle for the job beyond just writing up new taxes...It's worked out really well so far for the most part, few people've tried to attack me out of anger, but my co-workers in the Front Office help keep that under control typically..Just hoping they manage to keep that up on Tuesday Turmoil.' 'What've you got on Turmoil?' Phil's interest was a little piqued by the lead-up, and Tim was quick to sip the cappucino, but slow to finish doing so, as he set his feet up on the seat across from him. He exhales in satisfaction with the drink, before finally speaking once more..'Well, it just happens to be a match for a World Title shot at the next Pay-Per-View, Alliances..All I've gotta do is beat three other guys to get the shot, but they aren't pushovers...First, you've got that guy El Diablo, 'The Mexican Devil' he likes to call himself, the guy's been getting an ego lately and it's starting to affect his wrestling. I swear, he doesn't think of anybody but himself, and judging from Fusion possibly some other guys, though it's not relevant...The fact is that the guy's just gotten ahead of himself trying to challenge the World Champ, a sadistic freak named Clyde, when he hasn't even earned his shot at the title yet. I don't know what the heck is wrong with him there, but I'll be nice and make sure to put in a good word to Ellie~!, our commissioner, to get him a fight with Clyde some time after he's lost the title...I mean, he could get lucky, everyone has a chance to win, but make no mistake, his chance is a lot smaller than mine.' Phil looks back out the rear window of the limo, watching the road fly by before turning back..'Who else is involved?' 'Well, there's Jason Snow, former 3-time champ, though two of those times were just his group funneling the belt to him, so he's really only a 1-time champ in most people's books...' Tim thinks for a moment, then has a slight gleam in his eye, as if something just came to him. 'Hey, did you hear in the news the whole thing a couple months ago with the wrestler murdering that guy?' Phil racks his brain, looking for the memory..'Oh yeah, I think I heard about that..Isn't he in prison?' Tim nods in acknowledgement..'Yeah, he's in prison now, but he was in the main-events, so we managed to work out a deal with the prison to get him out for the shows weekly..You know what the darnedest thing is? The crowds love him. The guy is a MURDERER, and they don't seem to mind that! It's just ridiculous, they have the gall to boo me for doing my job, and they like to cheer for a freakin' killer! Sometimes I just don't understand the fans..Well, I'm still pretty pissed at Snow for double-crossing a group I was in last year and disgracing the title later, but I think he'll be too preoccupied with avenging the beating he got from his guards and El Diablo to really have much of a shot of winning this, I mean he's obviously pretty hard to keep down, judging from how much of El D's offense he got up from on the latest big Pay-Per-View..Though that could be partly about Diablo not being very effective in general, but I can't afford to underestimate him right now...It's not going to be a walk in the park, but I'm confident that if anything, at least we can win..' 'Wait...We? Don't you mean you?' Phil was a little taken aback by the choice of phrasing, his head tilting slightly to the side to emphasize his confusion.. 'No, I mean we, as in the Front Office..That's because the other guy in the match is my associate, the PCW President Duke Williams..He hands down fines generally, which are more one-time things than taxes, and he does commentary on the side...I figure if we work together, we can easily take out the other two guys, but then you get into the whole sticky 'I want the shot! No, I want the shot!' situation...I figure we've got the easiest chance if we just agree not to break up each other's pins and the better man wins, but obviously we both want to win this thing..We've never won the title either of us, and he's been fighting in the main-event scene for a while now, so he's a little more determined to win, and that's got me just a little anxious as to the result..I figure if it comes down to us and things get out of hand, I have a decent shot at outwrestling him, but I've never had to deal with a situation like that yet since coming here....Maybe it'd be best to just let him get the win, but then I'd be forgetting my dreams for someone else, and I'm not too happy with the thought...It's so much to give up so early, even if for the sake of management unity, but with Alliances down the road I don't know if it's worth the risk of breaking up the group over a title shot..You know how it is, eh?' Phil just shifts in his seat, nodding..The limousine stops, and the shot switches to an outside shot of it in front of a lush botanical garden. 'All right, we're in Florence now Mr. Walden..' 'Hmm..Where to go first? May as well turn off the cameras, there's nothing left to see anyway.' On that note, the scene finally fades to black. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
