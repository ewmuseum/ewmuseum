Lost in the Woods (RP #1 for SS)

13 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 13th [The scene opens on the large round, shining sun. The large rays beam through the tops of large oak trees, giving some illumination to this large area of forest. There is a very large dirt path turning and winding through the very thick wooded area. Two large figures are way ahead, walking very slowly as if they can't seem to go on any further. The scene begins closing in, walking past the foilage on the ground and the odd looking weeds that grow from the ground. The man on the left wears a large black, leather backpack with matching leather pants. He turns around breathing heavily as it reveals he is wearing a Twisted Sister T-shirt. His long brown hair hangs in his face, weeving in and out with each large breath he takes. The other man doesn't even look winded at all. The bright, blonde haired man, with the perfect tan, the perfect smile, and a perfect body. He wears a T-shirt that reads 'PCW' on the front and when he turns around the back says 'I'm Tubular and your not!'. The T-shirt is accompanied by blue swim trunks with yellow trim at the waist. He also wears a backpack and his matches the swim trunks. Thats right. The first promo of the...COWABUNGA CONNECTION. Consisting of 'Totally Tubular' Tommy and 'Heavy Metal' Harry. Tommy smiles to the beautiful sky and throws on a pair of Oakley sunglasses, happier than ever] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: We did it, dude. We made it to the most bodacious big time. [Harry still pants a little bit] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah...man... [Tommy then looks around him, realizing the beauty that lies within this forest. Many berry bushes, oak trees, shrubs, and other various forest like objects. Tommy then approaches a bush that looks as if tiny flowers are growing from them. He breathes deeply taking in the scent, he then coughs a little as the smell isn't sweet but bitter. Tommy coughs it off and smiles again] TTT: Nature is gnarly. We are surrounded by trees, and where they come from is a most odd mystery. The smell is also a righteous thing too, Harry. We are so used to the most HEINOUS city that we forget about nature, dude. [Harry gives Tommy a very strange look, as if Tommy is just BSing] Harry: You hate nature, man. [Tommy's smile fades as he begins staring a hole through Harry, then looks to the sky thinking about Harry's comment] TTT: Hmmm, I don't really remember liking nature before. I guess you just discover something new everyday, compadre! Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy then begins looking around again, trying to to take in the scenery. He enjoys the colorful sights of nature but there is one thing that is boggling his tubular mind...how did they end up in the forest? Tommy's face drops down into complete and utter confusion, trying to find out exactly how the two men ended up in this forest. Tommy taps Harry on his broad, stocky shoulders] TTT: Dude, how exactly did we get into the woods? Do you remember? [Harry begins laughing hysterically, holding his stomach because the laughter hurts so hard. With bloodshot, teary eyes, Harry looks up giving Tommy the answer that explains Harry's state of mind] Harry: Your asking me where we're at, man? TTT: Yeah, dude. Who else would you think I'm asking, a most bogus bear? [Harry's face turns from an upbeat smile to a face of fright. He leaps into Tommy's arms like a child would his mother] Harry: Bear? Did you say bear, man? TTT: A most bogus bear. [Harry yelps loudly, burying his face into Tommy's chest. Tommy seems a bit weirded out, but all he really wants to know is how they ended up in this forest] TTT: There is no bear, compadre. I was just making a gnarly joke, but you took it much too seriously. [Harry then takes his face out of Tommy's chest looking at the large wet marks he left behind from drool, snot, and tears. Harry quickly wipes it all around and back off from his best from and tag team partner] TTT: TOTALLY BOGUS!! You got snot all over me, dude. Harry: Sorry, man. [Suddenly a red blotch of paint explodes against a tree right above Harry's head. Harry doesn't realize what happened as he goes about his business, but Tommy realizes. Its a paintball! He now remembers why they ended up in the woods. They were being chased! Then a very large man wearing a long sleeved, red shirt that says 'Who Flung Poo?' and tons of padding comes over a small hillside. The large man holds up a paint ball gun and begins to scream in a very high pitched voice] Large man with paint ball gun: There they go! Get those fart sniffers! [Tommy then grabs Harry by his Twisted Sister shirt and the two men begin to run through the large collection of high weeds and bushes. More paintball men come wobbling up the hillside firing their paintballs in the direction of Harry and Tommy. The newest PCW tag team runs through the high weeds as paintballs fly past their heads, almost splattering all over their bodies. In the distance you can hear a bunch of high pitched voices screaming about how their going to get those turdeaters and how their going to bust their kiddymakers. Tommy begins looking to Harry with a confused look as the two men still run] TTT: What is a kiddymaker, dude? [Harry jogs a little bit behind Tommy, panting heavily] Harry: I don't know, man. [The two keep running through the weeds finally making it to a large opening. There are no trees, no weeds, just a large dirt circle with the sun shining down right on it. Harry is panting as he walks into the middle of the circle looking up at the bright sky. Tommy has a look of fear not trusting this sort of thing. He's read the articles and seen the shows on TV about things like this. Those bogus crop things that always happen in places like this. Harry is going to be the next victim!] TTT: Dude, don't look into the light!! [Tommy dives at Harry driving him into a large pile of weeds. The two men begin to plummet down a hole and smack to the bottom. It seems that they have fallen into a pit of some sort. Just as Harry goes to begin swearing at Tommy many footsteps are heard just above the ditch. Tommy covers Harry's mouth as he begins spitting out muffled curse words. The footsteps then begin to get farther away. Tommy then releases his hand from Harry's mouth] Harry: ...then you can sit and rotate on it, man! [Tommy then looks at Harry angry and confused. Tommy just shrugs it off getting to the point at hand] TTT: Dude! Remember earlier today? Harry: No, man. TTT: Well, I do. TUBULAR FLASHBACK ----------------- [The scene shows the inside of a very redneck like hunting store. Many bear and deer heads line the walls, with a few stuffed fish here and there. The place is filled with guns all over the walls where there aren't animal heads. The large glass counter which a very old, ugly man stands behind, holds many of the paintball guns and equipment. The old, ugly man wears a torn up 'Bass Huntin'' cap with a dirty flannel shirt and blue jeans. His face looks like he's taken a few beatings in his life and he has about a grand total of four teeth in his mouth. He spits out his tobacco juice and looks to the door which is covered in stickers. Harry and Tommy walk through the door with their backpacks on, smiling the whole time. Tommy looks all around a bit frightened of the sight inside the place] TTT: This place seems most bogus, dude. [Harry has the same look of fright on his face as he spots the redneck behind the counter] Harry: Yeah, man. [The two men approach the counter, looking through the glass at the paintball guns. Tommy smiles and gives Harry a thumbs up as if signifying that he was right about them being here] TTT: This is going to be a bodacious promo, dude. Harry: Yeah, man. [The man behind the counter just stares at them like they are the freaky looking ones] Redneck store owner: What the HEEEL are you boys doin' here? TTT: We're going to use paintball guns to shoot pictures of our bogus opponents at Sunday Sabatoge. Its going to be a GNARLY promo, dude. [The redneck looks at Tommy as if he has dancing boogers coming out of his nose] Redneck store owner: Are you high, boy? What the hell is a Sunday Sabatoge and what in the BLASTED HEEEL is a promo? [Tommy looks at Harry and shakes his head, disappointed that this redneck doesn't know. Harry just shrugs his shoulders with a stoned smile] TTT: Sunday Sabatoge is a most gnarly show put on by the most BODACIOUS wrestling company, PCW...which is Primetime Championsip Wrestling. Harry... [Tommy points to the stoned man with the Twisted Sister T-shirt] TTT: ...and me, TOTALLY TUBULAR TOMMY...also known as THE COWABUNGA CONNECTION, are going to be in a most triumphant contest on that show against Rudolph von Hackenmann and Mister Creeper, Fear Factory. Now that is a most bogus name for a tag team, compadre. Harry: Yeah, man. Redneck store owner: Your a couple of fruity wrestlers. I see what you do with each other, all feeling each others crotches and BEEEEhinds!! I hope you all go to HEEEEL!!! [Tommy drops his smile to a bit of a frown and points down to the glass case] TTT: All I want is a paintball gun, dude. [Then the front door opens up as three very large men wearing different colored long sleeve shirts walk in. They are all decked out in extra thick padding. The man in the blue shirt, his says 'I like Pie.'. The man in the green shirt, his says 'I kill fags'. The last man in the red shirt, his says 'Who Flung Poo?'. The three men immediately approach Harry who is looking at a poster of a half naked woman. Tommy is across the room playing with a fishing rod, actually a bit confused at how this rod works. The man in the 'Who Flung Poo' shirt goes to speak with a very deep, man like voice] Man in 'Who Flung Poo' shirt: What kind of fag wears a TWISTED SISTER shirt! Man in 'I like pie' shirt: Yeah!! Man in 'I kill fags' shirt: Your some kind of loser! [The three men then laugh to each other as Harry still pays no attention to them. Tommy doesn't even notice because he's pretending like he's casting out the fishing rod for a large mouthed bass] Man in 'Who Flung Poo?' shirt: Yeah, son. WHAT DO YOU WANNA DO WITH YOUR LIFE!! [The three men laugh loudly to each other as the Redneck store owner now joins in on the laughing. Once again Harry doesn't hear the Twisted Sister bashing, and Tommy doesn't see the three large bullies messing with his tag partner] Man in 'I kill fags' shirt: Yeah, what a freakin' fa-- [Just then Tommy catches the man in the crotch with the fishing pole, doubling the man over. His finger accidentally hits the trigger on his paintball gun, shooting a vicious paintball shot at the 'I like pie' guy hit him directly in the crotch. The man stumbles backwards into a display case of guns, knocking that display case into another, then into another, and then finally into a large, wooden gun rack. The rack topples and falls just short of the crotch of the 'Who Flung Poo?' guy. He looks to the sky in thanks...] Harry: Lets go, man. [Harry turns around stepping on the man's crotch, leaving all three men hunched over in pain, squealing in high pitched voices. The Redneck owner just stands in amazement and bewilderment] END OF TUBULAR FLASHBACK ------------------------
