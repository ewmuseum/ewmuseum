Search for Pure Adrenaline

27 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27-09-02 Justice is not always performed by humanity just to be a ‘respectable citizen’ there is often much more to it then that. So do it because it makes them feel good. Some do it to impress others. Though… some do it for the greatest reason of all… pure adrenaline. A green BMW convertible vehicle came racing down the streets of Ames, Iowa. Three men occupied the seats. Those three men were easily identified. The driver was a tall, extremely week built man, with curly brown hair and a light complexion. He continuously made fast sharp turns. The man beside him was of course Jamie Celt. His hair was spiked as usual, and he had a green jacket and blue jeans as his attire. In the back sat Jamie’s best friend in the world, Gerald, his cousin. Gerald wore a Boston Celtics shirt and black jeans. He was looking at every street and every building he possibly could. The car continually searched the streets. Gerald and Jamie’s eyes were like eagles, searching for their prey. Jamie looked all around him. He looked through shop windows, down ally-ways, everywhere he possibly could. Why where they doing this? The Celtic gang was looking for crime, crime to eliminate. They were ’spreading their empire’ so to speak, beyond the clutch of Boston City. It was Jamie’s idea, and he partly did it for his own self-interest, so his friends could accompany him on his PCW travels, though he thought ’Why not kill to birds with one stone?’ and why not indeed. They were in Ames this week for probably Jamie’s biggest match in his PCW career thus far. It was for the PCW Cruiserweight Title. A title Jamie had dreamed about winning since he was a child. He loved watching the cruiserweights in action, and now he had the chance to entertain the fans the way older wrestlers once entertained him. Though his task of winning the title would be no easy one. He would have probably the toughest opponents to date to go through first. Anarchy, past Rising Star Champion current Cruiserweight Champion, probably the best female in the business. Jamie did not feel intimidated by her, though he treated her like a huge threat. Then, there was Charlie Loc. Many consider him to be the greatest PCW Cruiserweight since Kid Wonder of Miyagawa. He was up for this one. He was probably favourite tow in the match due to his extensive record. Though there was no way Jamie was lying down to him. Jamie had something to prove. He wanted to prove that he could hang with such greats as Charlie Loc. And he intended on doing that at Fusion. Loc was indeed great, though that was just the chance Jamie needed to shine. Finally, there was Teresa Tomas. The obsessive female who, on several occasions, had attacked Jamie. What reason did she have for these assaults? None of course, she was like a plague on Jamie that would not go away. He just wanted to know why she continually attacked him. Jamie wanted to get his hands on her, and not in the sick perverted way many thought. He wanted her in the ring, in a match. He knew she was good, but he believed he was better. The Front Office had kindly given Jamie this match. Where, at Red October Jamie would take on Teresa Tomas, one on one. The reward was a shot at the PCW Cruiserweight title, that is, if neither wins the title on Fusion. Jamie had been given a lot of chances in PCW. He did not want to waste them. He had already received the right to have an Extreme Title shot after Red October. Which, again, he did not want to waste. PCW was treating him right, and he wanted to do the same to the fans. Jamie was soon woken from his deep thought when he saw something going on in a small shop across a town road. Jamie shouted at Peter, the driver… “Stop!” Peter braked hard. They were beside the curb on the opposite side of the street as the incident was taking place. Jamie raised his head, trying to get a better view of what was going on. It seemed as if to large males in black coats with balaclavas and guns were holding up an old store clerk. “Okay guys, look likes something’s goin’ down.” Jamie shuts while quickly getting out of the car without the robbers noticing. He goes to the back seat and pulls out a small box. Out of it he takes a M-54 handgun. “Peter, stay here and call for backup, get Dave, Daniel and Steve down here as soon as possible!” Jamie orders. “Got it Jay!” Peter said while beginning radio transmission. “Gerald, let’s go!” Jamie said. Gerald does not respond, he simply loads his own gun and gets out of the car. Jamie and Gerald run up the street and cross the road at a safe distance so the robbers cannot see them. They then sneak up along the street. Few people roam the street at this time, and the one’s that do strangely pay little attention to Jamie and Gerald. When the two come up along side the shop. Jamie halts Gerald. “On three I’ll dive in and you cover me, try not to let them see you at first.” Jamie tells him quietly. “A’ight.” Gerald replies in a quite laid back tone, showing the two’s experience in such situations before. Jamie then makes one huge leap and dives into the shop. He fires one warned bullet that hits the ceiling. “Everybody freeze!” Jamie shouts. The three men look at him, with stunned expressions of their face. “What the hell are you doing?” The clerk asks Jamie. Jamie looks confused as Gerald stands in the doorway with a huge smile on his face. “Uh… I was trying to save your life!” Jamie replies, sarcastically. “Why in the hell would I need saving.” The clerk asks intimidating. “Because these to guys are holding guns to you!” Jamie replies loudly. “Yeah! You usually hold guns in a god damn gun shop!” The clerk replies equally as loud. Jamie’s face goes pale. “Eh… Gun Shop?” “Yeah! Gun Shop!” The Clerk says once more. “Ah…I see.” Jamie replies embarrassed. Gerald stands in the doorway, laughing as hard as he can. Of course he knew what was going on, though found the whole thing to funny to let Jamie know. It was obvious Jamie missed the rush of Celtic life, and was so desperate to get back init. He needed the pure adrenaline he got from it, and since it did not look like he would be getting it from anywhere else. It looked like he would have to settle for Fusion, where he knew his chances of winning gold in PCW where at it’s highest point.
