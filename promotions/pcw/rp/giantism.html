Date: late, god forsake it In my hangar I wait impatiently, while the final preparations of my ultimate tool for revenge wind down to completion. The cold, indestructible, metallic frame of my exo-skeletal machine reflects light off it’s shiny chrome exterior, the glimmer illuminating. An exclusively, private weapon, comfortably snug to only my size of fitting. A customized glove, or gauntlet rather, meant only to be worn by the likes of an unrelenting, ceaseless, invincible iron fist. And it will most certainly be my steel-clad strike upon my eternal rivals, revenge satisfied to the fullest capacity, that will utterly terminate their very being. But, there is less of a desire for vengeance, as there is lust for what they have acquired by natural selection, never actually realizing the gift bestowed upon their ungrateful existences. I envy the one thing that simultaneously handicaps me permanently, yet advances their odds against my evil will constantly, in fact almost always. Size. Bastards tower over me… Swine out weigh me… Even those in between cast an inescapable shadow over my territory. They all crush my pride, defying the laws cast deeply into the tablets of time. It has long since been a day where survival of the fittest still had any relevance. Now, it is survival of the biggest, fattest, and the strongest. And, these days, it seems everyone knows some secret that I do not. The shell that I must wear now is ready for my destructive being to take the helm. Brought into the realm of reality by my hands, it mass capable of vanquishing me with like a bug. But, only I control it. Before me sits my greatest invention. Synthetic size molded by the wonders of mastered technology. Don’t dwell upon the matter like a loser accusing one of cheating. The hand of revenge is never played fairly, under any circumstances. Besides, I had no choice. I had no other alternatives. You all forced me to the edge, demanding growth in exchange for success, and this, you persistent fools, is my substitute for the solution! Alas… It tears my inner conscience into the shreds of a frustrated downfall. I cannot bear the jealousy I have for the overgrown. I tried so hard to grow myself… To grow as big as you, Boda. Earlier, before the vengeful plot against all large mankind, the Goblin was facing certain… “Self-esteem” issues, after having several dreams and flashbacks from past experiences with Boda. We are greeted by the image of a loathing creature, Syko the Goblin, as he leans against an aluminum fence, his fingers entangled tightly around the wiry frame. Breathing hard, or hissing to describe it more like it sounds, he gently taps his forehead upon the fence, in a deranged manor. But, all the while, as he rocks to and fro allowing the fence to carry his weight, he never removes his glare off the ironic display before him. Caged within the fencing walls, the elementary school playground can be a cruel and dangerous place. Cut Billy for a sooner turn on the slide and say goodbye to the light of day. How sweet of little Mary to offer you the swing set spot, even after you misplaced her crayon. But, how unfortunate that the chain should break, sending you to an untimely… The particular predicament taking place now is that as preformed between a bully, and a squid. A scrawny, mop haired runt, short for his age even, cowers beneath the looming height and build of a freakishly oversized boy. A sacrificial lamb laid out for a leviathan; he shields his face, and awaits a hopefully quick fate. But, “Butch” would rather intake the orgasmic fantasy from pain, perhaps foreplay of intimidation. He starts of with a small shove that nearly topples the small prey; meanwhile Syko lingers by the outskirts of the playground, mysteriously cloaked in a trench coat, clinging to the fence. Through the eyes of the Goblin, one would see Boda stepping all over Syko, rather than the obvious reality. A single jab, nothing more than a minor thrust thrown by a fist fighter, sends Syko rolling like a tumbleweed backwards, unable to control his own momentum. Arms extended with inescapable reach, the brute captures the weakling with a tight grip on the collar. A bead of sweat surfaces upon Syko’s forehead as he beholds another illusion of himself in the clutches of the Czech monster. This time he helplessly beholds himself struggling high above the ground, held there by the claw like grip of Boda’s exaggeratedly large hands. The sickly child hits the ground hard, and Syko struggles to stay on his feet, the mental shock therapy intense in itself. Watching in horror, the Goblin grits his teeth for a final deathblow, the heavy-set boy advancing forward to lunge himself upon the much smaller… Syko balls up as Boda, massive as a skyscraper now, prepares to squish an insignificant bug beneath the weight of his boot… Pulling away from the fence, the frustrated Goblin growls angrily, heaving heavily. Syko the Goblin: “LIES! This was all a conspiracy to fool me!” Turning from the atrocities on the playground, Syko digs his hand roughly into the trench coat pocket, and resurfaces holding a book. Syko the Goblin: “Learn ways to grow fast, my ass!” Flipping through the pages, he scans vigorously, until stopping halfway through. His eyes following his finger over the words, he reads examining the content for something he may have missed. Syko the Goblin: “(Reading from the book.) The three easy steps to getting bigger, in a hurry. Step one: Gaining weight. To gain weight, one must beat his metabolism in a race of consumption…” Getting Fat A local diner, always packed with hungry commoners, serves its food fresh to its customers at a bar like counter. Today is no exception, except for one slightly out of the ordinary change of things. The uneasy, middle aged, skanky make-up wearing waitress stares uneasily the new visitor at the far end of the counter. Making beastly grunts, Syko drives his face ravenously into a large plate of… Well, something that used to be solid matter. A glint of horror in her eyes, her sagging bottom lip quivering as Syko licks the sloppy gravy like remains from his plate with the speed of a humming bird. Waitress: “C-c-can I get you something else…?” Snapping his head up from the cleaned dish, he licks his face, bloodshot eyes darting about, realizing everyone in the diner is watching him. Syko the Goblin: “Yes… (Snarl.) …No, wait… NO TIME! MY MATABOLISM IS GAINING UPON ME!” With that, Syko hops onto the counter top, an inhuman ferocity in his eyes. One by one, he pounces upon the unfinished meals of the other consumers, making his way down the counter top. They don’t bother trying to stop him, surprised more than angry, and a mother breaks for the door dragging her child along. Waitress: “Now hold on, if you want more food…” Syko the Goblin: “BRING MORE, WOMAN! THE VOID DEMANDS MORE… NO, WAIT! ONLY THE FLESH OF AN UN-WED VIRGIN WILL SUFICE ITS CRAVING, BUT YOU WILL DO!” Syko leaps off the counter at the horrified waitress behind it, roaring ferociously.
