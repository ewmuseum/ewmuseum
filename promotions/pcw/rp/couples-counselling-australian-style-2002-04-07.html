Couples Counselling- Australian Style

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/4/02 ‘The aftermath’. The story so far: On Fusion, Flaymin’s ‘assistant’, Pornstar Jenna Jameson was caught in the middle of a sexual rendezvous with Angel, the wife of El Diablo. Shocked, distraught and infuriated was Flaymin Aymin upon discovering he had no more beer in the frid….err I mean once he figured out his woman was cheating…. With another woman. A last-ditch solution?Couples Counselling. Australian Style. [Scene opens in an old, dusty Australian town.There are only a few dozen houses that line this remote little town out in the middle of woop woop. A run down truck rumbles towards the town, kicking up gusts of dirt, creating a mini-whirlpool of dust. As the truck arrives near the town, it comes to a halt.The passenger door opens and out steps Flaymin Aymin followed by PCW’s very own Porn Queen- Jenna Jameson. They both wave goodbye to the truck driver as the truck drives off once again into the distance. Jenna is wearing a tight, short chequered dress with a matching cowboy hat while Flaymin is decked out in a pair of faded jeans and a black ‘WHAM!’ shirt. Jenna looks at Flaymin’s shirt and shakes her head] Jenna: I told you that shirt was gay. Flaymin: Hey if it weren’t for this shirt the truck driver wouldn’t have pulled over and given us a lift. Jenna: He also wouldn’t have stroked the back of your head while he was driving. Flaymin: He said he was looking for the seatbelt. Jenna: He was already wearing a seatbelt!! Flaymin: Well then maybe he wanted another one for extra protection. Jenna: If he were looking for protection, it would have been the small, circular, rubbery type. Flaymin: Small?? Jenna: You know what I mean. Flaymin: No I don’t know what you mean.I don’t know anything about you anymore! Jenna: Well hopefully this Counselling session will save our relationship. [Flaymin approvingly nods his head. He and Jenna then begin to walk through the small town. They stroll through the ‘main’ street and come to a stop outside a tiny, run-down shack with broken windows and a door hanging off a single hinge.Hanging above the shack is an empty carton of Fosters beer with the word ‘Fosters’ crossed out and replaced by a badly handwritten title which reads ‘Robbo’s Sheila-Trouble Solving Joint’] Jenna: Um… what is a sheila? Flaymin: Australian term for ‘woman’. Jenna: This place doesn’t look professional.Wouldn’t we better off discussing our troubles with someone slightly more qualified? Flaymin: Robbo is qualified!He can down a keg of beer in 10 minutes flat! [Jenna sighs before she walks into the smelly, rotten shack. Inside the shack is a broken fridge, placed on its side in the middle of the room. On one side of the broken fridge a.k.a the ‘table’, are two broken toilet seats.On the other side of the broken fridge is a stool made out of empty wine bottles. Flaymin and Jenna walk around the shack looking at all the oddities that are in the room. Suddenly the fridge on the floor begins to move and the fridge door flings open. Out steps a hairy, naked man with foul body odour. He looks at Flaymin and Jenna and smiles] Robbo: G’day fella’s, my name is Robbo.Ya’all must ‘ave an appointment or sumfin’, eh? Jenna: Yeah we do. Robbo: You rippa!I’ll just check my appointment book. [Robbo (still naked) walks over to the side of the room and picks up a beer carton.He opens up the flaps of the beer carton and sticks his hands inside.After ruffling his hands around for a minute, he pulls out a piece of paper and looks at it] Robbo: Yep…… yep…. You ‘ave an appointment now. Why don’t you both take a seat. Flaymin: On what? Robbo: The flamin’ dunny! [Flaymin looks at the toilet and shakes his head] Flaymin: They don’t look to clean. Robbo: Crickey!Are you ‘ere for sheila counselling or are ya a part of the ‘Toilet inspecting commission’ thingamajig? [Flaymin shrugs his shoulders.He and Jenna then sit down on the dirty toilet seats. Robbo sits on the empty wine bottles and then puts his arms on the lopsided fridge. He looks at Flaymin’s shirt and raises an eyebrow] Robbo: WHAM?What’s all that about? Flaymin: Long story. Robbo: We got time, I’m billing by the minute! Jenna: Can we just get on with this? Robbo: Alright luv, no need to get your knickers in a twist. Tell Uncle Robbo how the whole commotion began? [Flaymin looks at Jenna.He then looks at Robbo and takes a deep breath before he begins to talk] Flaymin: To get straight to the point, I caught Jenna getting ‘freaky’ with another woman. [Robbo’s eyes light up.A moment later he stumbles off his seat and hits the floor with a thud] Robbo: Sweet mother of mouldy mongrels!!This sheila was boning another woman?? You wouldn’t happen to ‘ave any surveillance footage would ya? Flaymin: Uh…. no. Robbo: Jenna, what is your take on this? Jenna: Simple- I like to drink from ‘The furry cup’. Robbo: What? Flaymin: She likes muff… Robbo: Wooooooooooooo!!!!! Hey ‘WHAM’ boy, what are you complaining about?You should be over the f’n moon! Flaymin: This isn’t what I expected.I expected pornstars to have a higher level of commitment. Robbo: She’s a pornstar!?!?!?!?!This keeps getting better!!! [Flaymin quickly moves his head away and covers his eyes] Flaymin: Hey Robbo, you’re stiffening up downstairs. [Robbo looks down at his exposed genitalia and promptly sits back down] Robbo: Sometimes I forget I’m naked.Besides, I thought that kind of thing would turn you on, ‘WHAM boy’? Flaymin: Please.My thumb is longer then that. [Robbo begins to blush in embarrassment] Robbo: Errr… lets get back to the point now. Jenna, is this the first time you’ve been unfaithful? Jenna: What?I’m a pornstar! Robbo: So I guess that’s a ‘no’? Flaymin: For crying out loud…. Robbo: Hmmm, well this is a very strange situation. I think that maybe a child would help bring you both together. Flaymin: I ain’t kidnapping no one.I learnt my lesson the fourth time around. Robbo: No mate, why don’t you make a baby! Jenna, do you have any children? Jenna: Not that I know of. Robbo: Do you think you’d make a good mother? Jenna: Well I’m good at spanking and I’m an expert in the whole ‘Breast Feeding’ department. Flaymin: You could feed an army of babies with those bazookas. Jenna: I’m also not afraid to be vomited on by babies’ either- I’ve had my fair share of white substances being spat at me Robbo: Flaymin, its your turn now.Do you have any children? [Flaymin raises his head closes his eyes as his lips quiver quietly. He then begins to count on his fingers while Robbo and Jenna wait by patiently. A minute later he opens up his eyes and looks at Robbo] Flaymin: You wouldn’t happen to have a calculator on you? Robbo: That many children huh? Flaymin: Enough to fill an entire Kindergarten class. Robbo: As an ‘experienced’ father, how would you feel about poppin’ out another kid? Flaymin: Another one? UGH!I’m paying enough child support as it is. Robbo: It really would help bring you and Jenna closer together. [Flaymin quietly ponders the option of having another child. He looks at Jenna, who gazes back affectionately, anticipating Flaymin’s response] Flaymin: Well…….. [Scene fades to black] What will Flaymin and Jenna do?Are PCW’s Premier Porno Couple going to have a baby? And where on earth did Flaymin get that damn WHAM! Shirt anyway? STAY TUNED to find out~!!
