Torture

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-10-02 Can you hear my voice? It echo's through your every being. It sends sensations of shock to the very tips of your nerves causing them to tingle with fear. It make your movements stiff like a long nights sleep. It makes your eyes burn with pain of fire. It makes your heart jump through your chest beating and jumping like a rabbit running from it's killer.------- Can you feel my grip? It squeezes around your skull like an anaconda starting to squeeze the life out of it's dinner. Does it hurt? Do you feel no relief from my grip? It grows stronger with your whimpers for it to stop.----- Do you feel it? That feeling of insecurity? That feeling of intensity? That feeling of warm breath on the back of your neck making the hairs rise to attention. Do you feel scared? Of course you do. Do you have that feeling that you want it to go away, all of it? Smile for me..... So I can knock out your teeth. Bleed for me...... Shed tears for me.... Do you feel safe and alone Akiyama? .....Your not. Torture: The limp sack of bones, blood, and organs hung limp like a scarecrow on a post. The calming yet eerie sound of blood splattering onto a smooth concrete surface was all you would hear. 'Splat' It began to pick up in speed over and over becoming louder with each drip. 'Splat, Splat, splat, SPLAT!' A fresh business suit now turned the color of a fine wine, the colors of blood ran into the sweat causing a sweet, bitter smell. The lips tried to quiver but it was no use, they were to weak with pain. Was he dying? No. This was only the beginning. 'Are you awake?' A strong voice boomed. He tried to raise his groggy head but it was like trying to pick an elephant up with a toothpick. His eyes were glazed over and even if he managed to raise his head the clotty, thick blood would make everything a massive blur; like his college days. 'John Michele' The voice boomed like thunder once more, 'The god on high demands you to look,' The head slowly began to rise almost as if something was lifting it.... nothing was. He could see perfectly like a fresh water had washed away the blood from his eyes.... It hadn't. He made out the form of a massive figure; man or beast he couldn't tell. It had an eerie blue glow to it's eyes and seemed to have blue spikes coming from it's skull. The face slowly moved into the light, half still shrouded by darkness. It was a man, a very large man with a very unhappy look on his face. 'Ah that's more like it.....now as I was saying; John Michele, business man, graduated from Harvard, murdered 5,' The voice turned sharp as a knife. 'H....How do you know? Who are you?' His groggy voice asked. 'SNAP!' Went his Jaw as a massive fist slammed into it like a hammer. 'I ask the questions you answer. Understood?' The voice boomed once more with a snarl. He only moved his head up and down. 'Good,' The massive footsteps slammed against the cement in a rhythmic sound. This figure meant business. 'Why did you murder them?' 'what?' 'SLAM!' the fist of the figure was drove into the man's stomach. Vomit began to come and spew on the floor. Blood dripped into it soon covering it. 'Ok....Ok.....My wife wanted a divorce.....she was going to take the kids. I couldn't pay the child support. Hell I didn't care for the little bastards....it was all for the money. She had moved in with her mother....so one night when everyone had went to sleep I killed them all? Ok!? I KILLED THEM!' His voice boomed out. 'Now I will allow you one question,' 'W....Who are you?' His voice muttered. 'Me? I am nothing more than an image produced by your mind; or am I? I care not for you only for what the one true god commands me to do. I torture you for your sins and I have full control on what happens....It's funny isn't it? Your mind could produce such pain or did it? You will walk away from this either in a body back or which ever way I please....now....my turn. ARE YOU SORRY?!' The voice boomed. 'Y....Yes!' 'LIAR!' The fist slammed into the other side of his jaw with a violent crack. 'DO YOU ASK FOR FORGIVENESS?!' 'Y....Yes!' 'LIAR!' The fist slammed into his ribs cracking a few. The sound was deafening. 'Do you want to die?' The voice asked. 'Y....Yes!' 'To bad,' From the murky windows flashed the red and blue lights of police cars. Their sirens whined and cried through the night sky looking for a long lost lover. Everything went into slow motion as the police kicked in the old plywood door. Blood covered the ground and laying there was this scum with a note attached to his forehead. It told of everything he did; no questions were asked. And as the figure stepped into the night sky with the stars being his only witness except for the darkness he looked up. 'Tyrel your business is complete, power me for the hell I must step into next,' One star twinkled like Tyrel had said thank you.... and with that.... Sylo sat up in bed; his hands covered in blood but not his own.
