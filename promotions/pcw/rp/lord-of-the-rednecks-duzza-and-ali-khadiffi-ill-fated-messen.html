Lord of the Rednecks: Duzza and Ali Khadiffi; Ill-Fated Messengers

Date: 18.12.02 (#3 FF) “You will refer to me as ‘Your Royal Abnesty’, is that understood?” “Yes, master.” Abner, pointing his royal staff, gives his subordinates a sharp look. “Excuse me?” “We mean…..yes, Your Abnesty…..” The two men bow before their king. Taking a step back, Duzza and Ali Khadiffi fall to their knees. Abner nods his head smiling down upon his loyal subjects. Abner, seen as he never has been before, proudly wears the golden crown of a king. A shiny, yellow helmet on his head, decorated with the finest jewels of the land, this handsome young ruler is dressed appropriately for his position. Covered in the finest garnets and robes of all royalty, his attire is capped off by a luxurious cloak of thick, soft red silk with white lacing. Though obviously only a young man, perhaps still retaining some of his boyish features, His Abnesty sports a thick white beard, and sees with a pair of deep lordly eyes filled with the kingly virtue of wisdom. For once not red (due to the intoxicative elements), they pierce through the hearts of those below him. Duzza, head shaven, and obediently bowed, sits trembling on the floor below his superior. Dressed in ragged gray clothing, and dirty straw shoes, this dismal young wrestler seems lost in the void of poverty. But unlike the fellow sitting to his left, he still has his dignity. For also paying tribute to His Abnesty, sitting cross-legged on the cold, hard stone floor of the castle, is the gutter-bug of all slovenly fellows. Lice ridden, and matted hair has grown down the sides of this poor man’s face; he himself, on the bottom of the pile of even the most wretched of society. Wrapped in a single piece cloak of cheap, brown wool, he fails in comparison to the majestic beauty of the redneck. Ali Khadiffi, dirt-covered and partially inebriated, brings a frown to His Abnesty’s face. Huh. Well, if it weren’t for the dark pigmentation of his shell, the grime and street-scum would be quite noticeable……pitiful, yes, yes indeed. “Now what is it?” Acting as if they possess confidence, the two rise together. “H-hey, me first!” Shoving his partner aside, Duzza rushes forward. “Hey!” Quickly retaliating, Ali is on his feet. Grabbing Duzza by the bottom of his cloak, he viciously rips at the poor man’s rags. “Yo, you mother…..” Turning, Duzza charges forward and the two embrace in a common grip of friendly warfare. Letting out a sigh, His Abnesty’s has obviously had just about enough. “Alright! Alright! Alright now boys, that’s quite enough.” Together, they get to their feet. Quickly apologizing, “Sorry Your Abnesty!” they shamefully replying in unison. Snorting in disgust, Abner tries to remain cool. Regaining his pose, “So boys, back to the issue at hand.....” “Yes, well…..” “Hey!” Before Ali can finish, Duzza shoves him out of the way. “Boys!” Pretending to get to his feet, the two peasants quiver under the mental might of their king. Taking advantage of the situation, the hickish-monarch takes his seat, satisfied that order has been restored. “Alright then, now Duzza, please continue…..” “But…..” “Ah!” Raising his hand, Abner motions Kahadiffi to be silent. “You shall have your turn.” “B-But…..” “SILENCE! Capital S-I-…..lents……SILENCE!” Shifting from foot to foot, the subdued peasant bows his head. “Yes…….Your abnesty…..” Abner lets out a deep sigh, and this time with great impatience, he snaps out at Duzza. “Now what do you have to say for yourself. I want a full report……I want all the details, and please, for the love of Elizabeth, make it quick!” Duzza kneels down in front of his master. Wiping the sweat from his head, the frightened servant shudders. Slowly taking his master’s hands, he presses his lips upon Abner’s ring. “Alright, already…..enough with the formalities!” Shaking it off, Duzza gets to his feet and begins to make his report. Feebly voiced: “Your Abnesty, as I, your humble servant, and Mr. “Fuck-Rag” here made our way to the edge of the clearing, we were in for quite a surprise. You see, not more than twenty of Your Abnesty’s Royal Foot-Measures away, stood the Dreaded Dragon of……. GASP “SNAPPY!”GASP “…..The Fire-Breathing Heathen of Snapple……the TRUE official drink of PC-Dub……” A random assortment of the PCW roster, dressed in appropriate attire for the Middle Ages comes out: claps. They exit. The king, taken aback by this statement, stumbles out of his chair. Crudely fixing his crown, “Wh-wh-what!?” “Yes sire, it’s true…..” Khadiffi steps forward, “SNAPPY does indeed exist…..” “Yes master”, Duzza steps up again, “We’ve seen it with our own ‘you’re going to get our asses at Fusion’ eyes!” Scratching his royal chin, “Hmm…..I see…..” He seems to be in deep thought. And then, a moment later, he replies. “Well then, as lord of these lands…..as overseer of my people, I have no other choice…..” They both gasp in anticipation. Rising on their heels, Duzza and Khadiffi wait for their king’s reply. “You see, as powerful as I am, and hung like a horse……pleasing all the ladies day and night……makin’ yo momma scream……the Royal PIMP of yo moma’s ‘Middle Earth’…..” Duzza and Ali blink. “Oh…..sorry…..anyway, where were we…..Ah, yes!” Clearing his throat, “As powerful as I am, alone, I cannot fight the great Snappilicious one…..yes, yes indeed, mighty powerful is SNAPPY, fed by the power of Snapple, liquid heave, made from the Best stuff on earth…..Uh…..anyway, and so I cannot, will not face it alone.” The two are in shock….. “B-bbb-bbb………..but that means…..” GASP “We have to…….tttt…………to face it!?” “Yes, I’m afraid so my loyal servants”, adding, “And yes, the black guys always die first…..” And with that….before they know it… POOF They’re gone, and led out of the palace by His Abnesty’s royal guards, their fate awaits them……------------------------------------ Stay tuned next time, for another episode of “Lord of the Redneck”…..
