Back To Work (Part 3)

18 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-18-02 'All rise,' shouted the bailiff. 'The court is now in session. Honorable Judge Snyder residing.' Where was I right now? What was I doing in the court of law? Why was the PCW Rising Star Champion being held in the view of others as no more than common criminal? There are questions that seek answers… but answers that stay in the shadows. Answers that are not willing to emerge. As I sat down into the bench of the Defense table, I zoned out, letting the trial go on without my mental participation. I thought back to how this all had begun. You see about a month ago, things were going my way. This was an unusual occurrence for me, but nevertheless I accepted it and held this moment of time close to my heart. First of all, my life's duty had been called into order, as I had taken three young men under my wing, teaching them the ways of the warrior. They were my students, and I was their teacher. I had begun the process by stripping all three of their identities, renaming them only WIT (Warrior in Training). Under constant pressure and endless work, they began to mold the way I had planned… they began to see things that they could not before, to hear things not possibly heard, and most of all to sense what was barely reality. They were coming along well, and in no time at all I would have accomplished my goal. I would have made them into warriors, and my legend would have lasted for all eternity. Not only was my personal life going the way I wanted it, but my career had once again sparked, bringing me to the place I had always wanted to be. Bringing me to Primetime Championship Wrestling. I had worked long and hard to get there… I had graduated the wrestling academy, and I had spent time in another feeder federation. Once I was in PCW, though, I surprised even myself at the energy I put into making my stay successful. In only my second match, I had become Rising Star Champion! Not only was it an honor… it was a gift. A gift from life I believe, for throwing one too many roadblocks in my path. Even in my greatest moments, however, I knew that something would happen. I knew that I would end up down in the gutter once again, taken by a fatal mistake of some kind. And that is exactly what happened. I made a mistake--I punished my WITs too severely. I got caught. 'The defendant is accused with assault, a deadly crime, and one not to be taken lightly,' began the prosecuting attorney. 'There is no doubt that the defendant committed this crime, and therefore there should be no doubt in the minds of you, ladies and gentlemen of the jury, that the defendant is guilty!' I droned out again. Not that I needed to hear this--I knew I was guilty. I knew what I had done… after all I had done what I knew how to do. I knew how to punish… and this time I had taken the punishment too far. Warrior In Training #3, or in my mind The Defiant One, had been acting up much more than I expected. He had been questioning my every statement; countering my every question. He resisted my leadership so much… that I needed to do something big. I locked him in the trunk of my car. I stepped on the gas pedal, and I jumped out. I drove WIT 3 off of a bridge. I thought that was the end of it… WIT 3 eventually came back to me, vowing to diligently work towards becoming a warrior. Training continued, and once again things were going great. But to my surprise, just under a month ago, I was caught. Either I was foolish, or the police were lucky. Either way I ended up here. '…Today I will prove that the prosecution does not have enough evidence to find my client guilty of the charges, and therefore cannot assign him to the full sentence, fifteen years in prison.' Fifteen years? Was that what I was in for? For the first time, I began to actually worry. In most cases I waive the troubles of my life, and let them take their tolls without struggling or fighting back. But this… this was my life! Just to think… what would happen if I had to leave the world for fifteen years? What would it be like when I returned? I knew that if I got convicted, there would be nothing left for me in fifteen years. The WITs would already be fully introduced to the ways of the warrior… and I would be much too old to continue my stay in PCW. I would have no reason to live. And if I was not found guilty? Then things would remain the same. I would train my WITs to be the best that they can, to make them warriors of the past and the future… to look upon them with pride and respect, knowing that they hold the key to my life. I would continue to fight in PCW, defending my Rising Star Title in every chance I get. Suddenly I remembered. Didn't I have a match tonight? Defending the very Rising Star Championship that I loved so much? Kris Burden-- that was his name. Truly a rising star in the wrestling industry… truly someone that had a chance against me. I would need all of my might to defeat him, and even using everything I had it was not a certain victory. This would surely be my greatest challenge yet in the PCW. But I could do it. That is… if I made it out into the world tonight. The case was wrapping up, and I was beginning to fret. It had gone by very quickly… was that good? Or was it bad? Would I enter the PCW arena tonight as a champion; a hero? Or would I be sleeping on the hard cement floor of the local prison? I glanced at my attorney as the jury went to deliberate, but he would not meet my eyes. He was worried. I was too… I really was. I assumed for the first time that the saying must be right, you don't realize how much you like something until it is gone. Despite my anger… despite my trauma… I loved my life. I wanted to live and prosper, to bring joy to others as well as teach lessons and learn new ones. I didn't want to go--I wouldn't go! The jury returned fast, and the judge beckoned them to announce my fate. 'Foreperson, have you reached a verdict?' 'Yes we have, your honor. We find the defendant…' I cringed as he said my name. Blood rushed to my head, and sweat pumped down from my face like rain. This was it. The moment of truth. The moment of justice… '…Guilty.' I closed my eyes. No… no this could not be happening. Everything I had worked for… everything that I deserved… everything that I prayed for… The judge began to put the exclamation on the ruling. 'Due to lack of evidence…' Lack of evidence? I beacon of hope arose inside me. '…the defendant is fined fifty-two hundred dollars, and sentenced to ten hours of community service.' I wasn't going to jail? I wasn't going to jail! Yes! Things had finally worked out for me… it was the greatest feeling ever. I no longer needed to fear capture. I would go on training my WITs, bringing them to the top of their game, sharing my experiences and tragedies with them. I would continue to wrestle in PCW, among the brightest stars in the world. I would continue to live! My attorney turned to me, smiling. 'Congratulations. You've been through a lot; now you can go home and relax.' I too smiled, a thing I find myself forgetting to do much too often. 'No, no I won't. It's time I go back to work.'
