Wrestlers are athletes too, right?

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 26, 2002 Talk about your bad dreams. But this wasn't a bad dream. It was reality, unfortunately, and one none too sweet for Tom Lexian. Oh, he could complain, kick furniture, or just brood about it for the next week...or he could do something much more productive - like watch ESPN. The show where the best athletes in the world were showcased on Cable television and envied by millions of couch potating males across the country. Suffice it to say, Tom was one of those males. He loved the competition, especially his own, but watching it sure was entertaining. While Tom was watching the show that single handedly bonded all men 15 - 45 yrs of age together, he saw a note being slipped under the door. Hmm, very interesting - could it be Natalie? Only one way to find out, slick.... Tom threw himself out from the couch and headed over to the door and picked up the note. It was scribbled on a napkin which had the 'Crispy Cream Doughnuts' logo on it and was written in green ink. It read: Lexian, Meet me in the back of the Crispy Creem Doughnot store. I'll be there waiting whering a brown trench coat and green pants. Don't be late. Nine o'clock. - The Joker: Tom scratched his head at the note. Nine o'clock in the back of the Crispy Creem Doughnot store? Wait, he mispelled 'cream' and 'doughnut.' Blah, forget it. So this guy, this mystery Joker fellow, wanted to meet Legend Inc:. - but for what purpose? He wouldn't be hard to find considering he would be whering a brown trench coat and green pants. Wait, he mispelled 'wearing.' What a dork. Tom sighed and thought to himself for a second. 'What could he want? I just had the worst dream in the history of all eternity, plus I had a match to be training for. This can’t be good.” Tom thinks for a second... ....Tom grabbed his coat and left. * * * Back Alley of 'Crispy Cream Doughnuts' - 8:58pm Legend Inc: parked his car right outside of the back alley and stepped out into the cold San Francisco air. He buried his hands into the warm pockets of his six year old leather jacket and stumbled through the alley with his two year old Reeboks. Did he own any new clothes? Anyways, Legend Inc. looked up and around the alley which resembled a war zone out of Saving Private Ryan or something. There was assorted debris everywhere including old boxes, garbage cans turned over, and left over clothes most likely from the local riff raff. To make matters worse the ground was soaking wet and there were puddles everywhere - and it had been up to 80 degrees during the day. It hadn't rained for weeks. Go figure, - I suppose alleys are automatically rain soaked just for that alley appeal. Legend Inc. stopped abruptly as he noticed a figure standing out in the distance staring directly at him. He was tall and well built wearing an Al Capone top hat and an evil grin - wait, is that a trench coat and green pants? For the love of god - it was no one important, just The Joker. * * * Legend Inc: [shakes his head] So are you going to just stare at me all night or did you actually come here to tell me something useful? The Joker: [begins to walk forward] With that attitude it's no wonder you couldn't take care of business with Xavier Kannon. Legend Inc: Oh c'mon, you didn't call me out here just to rub it in, did you? Because if you did I have better things to do with my time then to listen to some broken down old timer. We haven’t even had the match yet… The Joker: I didn't come here to rub it in, although I do want to discuss why you’re going to lose… Legend Inc: Well, I don't, so let's just drop the topic. The Joker: [smiles] No. Legend Inc: No? No? I hope you're not trying to be cute. If I say that I don't want to talk about it then the topic is officially closed, got it? The Joker: You have a lot to learn, Lexian. First off, you don't give me orders. I give instructions and you follow them. Secondly, I want to talk about the match so you will discuss it and like it. Legend Inc: And I'm going to do this so I can get your expert opinions, right? The Joker: You made a few faux pas' out there. For starters, the way you come out to the ring in your introduction. Legend Inc: [looks confused] I came out, I raise my arms in the air, and I get into the ring. What did I leave out? The Joker: You didn't make a comment to the camera. Legend Inc: [laughs sarcastically] Oh, ok! I get it! I didn't say a few choice words for the adoring fans out there. Is that what was bothering you? Ok, next time out I'll flip everyone off and just tell the camera to 'Fuck off.' Is that good? The Joker: No, I don't mean curse at the damn camera. You came out there too relaxed, like you didn't even care about the match. You have to go out there with confidence, like you already know you've won, and you have to say it into the camera. Legend Inc: Why? So I can replay the tape back and see how confident I was or something? The Joker: No, so your opponent can see it. You have to realize that a lot of wrestlers watch these intros from the back. If they see you're confident, then they'll worry about you. They'll think that maybe there is something to that guy. And that's how you win matches without even wrestling them. Legend Inc: How does that affect me when I'm actually wrestling? The Joker: It doesn't. All of that's up to you, and obviously you didn't get the job done on that. Legend Inc: Oh, here come the cheap shots. The Joker: It's not a cheap shot, it's a fact. You went off your game plan, Lexian, and you know it. You're not a brawler. You're a wrestler. You use holds and power moves, not steel chairs and barbed wire. Legend Inc: Hey, I could be as crazy as Xavier Kannon if I wanted to. The Joker: Yeah, but you wouldn't be half as good at it. Face it, Lexian, you need to stick to what you're good at. You don’t use tildes and you sure as hell don’t have a woman like Ellie. Legend Inc: Don’t talk about Natalie that way. The Joker: That fatal four way match with Miyagawa, Duke, and Kannon comes to mind now. Don’t tell me you’ve forgotten already? Legend Inc: [sarcastic] Wait, how do you know this? I haven't even heard about it yet and I'm in the match. The Joker: You can joke around all you want. But when the chips are down, I know this match is on your mind. It’s got to be. It could make or break you in PCW. Legend Inc: [puts his hands up] Alright, alright. You're the fucking Joker, what else do I have to lose? So, is that all you had to tell me? The Joker: [angrily] Do you think that was important enough for me to drag myself out here in the middle of the night in the back of a doughnut shop to tell you? Of course there's more. I have a special assignment for you, and it has to do with the promos you're going to do for the match. Legend Inc: Wait, you're going to tell me what promos I'm going to do for the match? Shouldn't I be the one to decide that, not some overgrown, sorry excuse for a mystery man in mismatching clothes? The Joker: You watch your mouth. First off, you really screwed up your last promos so I expect you to be begging me for advice on your next series. Legend Inc: Screwed up? What are you talking about? Those were some quality moments in... The Joker: [interrupting] ...in what? That weird ass dream you had where you were chasing an amulet that really wasn’t there? No one was impressed by that. They all thought it was stupid. Get it straight in your head, Lexian, you have to do things more main stream. All this looney bin nonsense has been done before and is outdated by the likes of idiots such as Mick Foley. You have to be original, Lexian, and a bit more mainstream. Legend Inc: Oh, like I’m the weirdo? Miyagawa is so obsessed with this best man business with Kannon that he can’t even see three feet in front of him. The Joker: You don’t worry about him. He’s a former World champion. What have you done? Legend Inc.: Don’t give me that crap. You know like I do that if you’re head’s not in the game, you might as well get out. And Duke…my god, he’s having conversations with puppets. PUPPETS! At least dragons have a personality. The Joker: [crosses his arms] Again, you’re not listening. Legend Inc: [crosses his arms] Alright hotshot, what's your big plan? The Joker: ESPN. Legend Inc: What about it? The Joker: I want you to get on the ESPN Sportscenter Showcase and do an interview from there. Legend Inc: [bewildered by The Joker:'s idea and shakes his head in confusion] What....?!? The Joker: You know, the portion of the show when they focus on a particular game that viewers vote for on the ESPN.com website... Legend Inc: [snaps back] I know what it is, thank you Joker. I mean, how the hell am I supposed to get on there? That's a nationally recognized show, not some public access channel. Why would they let me on? The Joker: [sighs] Please tell me you're not as naive as you're sounding like right now, because if you are I'm ready to leave your sorry ass and hook up with the Juan Borro. Legend Inc: [sarcastically] Oh please, holy Joker! Please don't leave me for the Borro! I'll listen to whatever you have to say! Please, don’t you know that Borro already retired? The Joker: That's cute, Lexian. Real cute. Why don't you make more jokes? Maybe one day you'll look out your window and find it not under surveillance. Legend Inc: Oh, that would be devastating. The Joker: Alright, just shut up. Here's what you're not realizing, my naive overbound-muscle-head friend: Do you honestly think that the viewers choose the Sportscenter Showcase? Legend Inc: Well, then what's the point of having the poll on the website? The Joker: What's the point of having these knuckle head citizens vote for the president when it's the electoral college votes that get the gray haired dork in? There is no point. It's reassuring. It's to have the people think that they're making a difference. Legend Inc: So who does choose the Showcase then? The Joker: Whoever is the highest bidder. Legend Inc: [pauses] You're telling me that whoever has the most money to spend on their own showcase will have it aired? The Joker: Boy, you're quick aren't you? Well you should be since you went to USF right? Legend Inc: Cut the sarcasm already. Just tell me, how exactly am I supposed to be the highest bidder for the Sportscenter Showcase? The Joker: Well first you need some money. The minimum bids start around two million dollars.... Legend Inc: [shocked] Two...two...million dollars? Are you fucking crazy? Where the hell are we supposed to get that kind of million? The Joker: Correction - where the hell are you supposed to get that kind of money? Legend Inc: Great plan, oh mighty Joker. A plan in which I have no means of carrying out. The Joker: Shut up, Lexian. Your mouth is starting to annoy me. Do you think I thought up this plan without a way to actually get the money? Legend Inc: [feeling a bit stupid] Oh, so you do have a plan... The Joker: [hands Tom a fake ID and a loan contract] We can't have anyone figuring out that you are the one who is actually behind the money transfers so I set up a fake ID to do all of the exchanges. He's your pseudo manager...Tee Howard. Legend Inc: [looks at the ID and the name] Tee Howard? Is that supposed to be some lame joke? I mean, I used to be friends with Jeremy Howard and Tee…well, I don’t know anyone named Tee. The Joker: I thought it would be oddly ironic. Legend Inc: I think it's oddly lame. Not that I mind being lame. The Joker: Good, I didn't think you'd mind. Now, there's also a loan contract in those documents I handed you. Everything's set up, just go down to SF National Bank and finalize the details and sign on the dotted line. Legend Inc: That's all I have to do? The Joker: Well, as long as you don't screw anything up. You might have to answer a few questions, but since you're such a great actor and all, I don't think you're going to have that much trouble making up a story. Legend Inc: [gives in] Right.... The Joker: Now, after the loan goes through you're going to have to call Jimmy the Fish. Legend Inc: Jimmy the Fish? Who's that? The Joker: A bookie I know. I want you to take out the money that was loaned to you by the bank and use it to bet on the USA-Germany world cup game. He'll give you 2:1 odds on whoever you place the bet on. Legend Inc: Wait, why am I placing a bet? Don't we already have the money we need from the bank? And plus, that game happened a few days ago! The Joker: [angry] Imbecile! Who's going to pay back the loan? I'm certainly not. You get the loan, make the bet and win it, and you automatically double your money. Money to both repay the bank without interest and money to get the Sportscenter Showcase. And it doesn’t matter that the game has already ended. This bookie works backwards and he’ll mark your bet so that it was made before the match was played. Now, did you get all of that? Legend Inc: [puts the documents in his jacket pocket] I'm set. The Joker: [begins to walk away] Now don't lose any of those documents - I don't have any extras. And if you get caught, you never knew me. You tell anybody about me and our relationship is terminated. Legend Inc: [watches The Joker: walk off] Hey, has anyone told you that your car is on the other side of the alley? The Joker: [stops and pauses] What car? Legend Inc: You own that broken down ol' Datsun? The Joker: Yeah that's mine. Legend Inc: [points to the other end of the alley] It's on that side, genius. The Joker: [curses to himself and walks to the other end] Son of a....always forgetting where I leave my damn car...[stops again and thinks]....how did you know that was my car? Legend Inc: [shakes his head] You misspelled, 'The Juker' on the license plate. It's supposed to be an 'o' not a 'u.' * * * Well, ok. That was the kind of relationship Tom and the mysterious Joker had together. It was built on individual selfishness and was a venerable tug of war struggle. This time The Joker: had won out. His plan made sense...well, kind of...but he had the evidence to back it up. There were just too many questions swirling in Tom's mind... Why did Jimmy the Fish always give 2:1 odds? Well, maybe he's overly confident that he can win his bets. Maybe he has enough money to be able to support that kind of guarantee. Besides, if he wins then he gets a lot of money per bet. Makes sense. Where does The Joker: get these fake I.D.'s and loan contracts? In his own words, 'I'm the fucking Joker.' I suppose these mystery guys always have other contacts in higher places that can do this 'dirty' work for them. What team was he supposed to pick – USA or Germany? A tough choice. Not one that Tom wanted to make in a back alley in the middle of the night when he was tired. This was a Tuesday morning kind of decision. Well, first Tom had to get the money to make the bet in the first place. Bah. What are you thinking? Germany won already, duh. Would Tom be able to 'not' screw up the loan deal? Tom is a decent actor - he can do it. Uhm, maybe for that day he could refrain from the rather obtuse jokes... Why was The Joker: such a horrible speller? Some people just quit after the 2nd grade. It's so sad... Tom began to walk back to his car and pondered the many questions that he had. There was no doubt in his mind that he was going to go through with the plan - that much was certain. But even if he did make it on to the SportsCenter Showcase, what would he do on there? The show was basically a very detailed analysis of a game or a something to that effect - what would they be analyzing for the Showcase? As Tom opened the door to his car he just tossed the idea on the back burner. First thing's first, and that was getting the money to bet on the game. We're sure that won't be hard, aren't we....
