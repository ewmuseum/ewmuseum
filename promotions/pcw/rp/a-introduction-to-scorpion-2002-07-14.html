A introduction to Scorpion

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-14-02 Scorpion, the name came from the tables in casinos around the world. That is where the name came from. I was given the name because at anytime I could strike my opponent like a Scorpion and end the game, taking all there money. People say I’m the best ever casino player in the world, they are right. My strength’s are the poker and blackjack tables. I don’t recall every losing to a man and if they said they beat me then they are probably wrong. I have the best luck in the world; the casino always loses their money when I walk in. From slots, to craps I win them all. I’m the best dam hustler in all the world and Las Vegas in my middle name. People say I’m beatable, but do they really know? I have been to hell and back, from rags to riches and they can’t beat me. The casino world knows my name like the alphabet, soon the wrestling world and the PCW will as well for my wrestling abilities. Some say my gambling hobby is an addiction; I call those people sore losers. Those you try to face me, lose everything they have every owned. People have bet their houses, their life insurance, there life savings against me, and they think there brave, but I call them stupid. The people that did that lost everything they put on the line. Stupidity runs wild in the world of gambling, you need to out think your opponent before he or she out thinks you. I have traveled the world and the seven seas and yet no one can beat me. Those who try fail and those who don’t are smart. The minute someone puts money in the pot it’s like signing your life away to the devil because you know your never ever going to get it back. I’m the world’s greatest gambler in the world. I’m the Muhammad Ali, the Michael Jordan and the Babe Ruth of the casino world and soon my status in casino across the globe shall continue in the world of wrestling. From sea to shining sea from pole to pole, I will be the greatest wrestler in the world. Now I try my luck in PCW the granddaddy of all wrestling organizations in the world. Now when we talk about luck, we know I can’t be beat. Luck for me equals success and those who oppose me shall feel what I say. In the casino world, I am, the best in the west and the beast in the east, I cover all countries around the globe. People wanted to know why I was called Scorpion and now you see why. When you think you got me, WHAM! I sting you out of know where with venom stabbing you in the back just like a Scorpion and once I see you are paralyzed, I go right for the win. In gambling I take all the money in the pot, in wrestling I pin your ass and I get a win. I started out in one of the smallest federations in the world, then I went to a higher level and I kept climbing and climbing the ladder until I reached CCW, one of the top organizations in all of the wrestling world and when that door closed another one opened another door for Scorpion. The door that I stepped into was PCW, the biggest wrestling organization in the entire world right now. I have been waiting and watching for this moment and now that I have been granted a contract in PCW everyone that’s anyone that knows me, from casino’s, from friends, to other wrestlers, the know that I will not let myself down, it will take a long process but I will climb that ladder rung by rung, step by step. Like an ace in a hand, I can’t be beat. Primetime is my time, I’m ready for it and I won’t let myself down. I have worked very hard to get where I am today and I won’t let all the hard work slip away. Now that you know me, you will never forget me. The name is Scorpion and I’m here to stay, place your bet, try your luck, cause from now on I call the shots.
