Come and knock on my door

11 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 11, 2002 I sighed, still feeling the pain in his leg. Why did the officer had to go and crack that damn door against my leg like that? Ruuuuah! I popped in a few aspirin, which the police officer took from him instantly, thinking it was something else. After realizing that it really was aspirin, the officer shouted at me to hurry up. Well, it wasn’t quite that nice. It was something along the lines of, “Hurry the hell up you sloth looking ingrate!” Nice, huh? I think that the officer pocketed a few of the aspirin for himself. I got the bottle back half empty. Tom Lexian: [finishes searching his wallet, where he usually leaves his license, but he can’t find it. He turns back to the officer, giving him a sympathetic face.] Look, I can’t find my license. Police Officer: [sarcastic] Awww…that’s too bad, isn’t it? We’ll just have to forget about this then…back at the police station. C’mon, get out of the car…. Tom Lexian: [shocked] The police station? I can’t go to the police station….I’m an important guy. Way too important to be spending the night at the county jail for something as stupid as this. Hell, this is my car, I guarantee you. You can ask Officer Lee… Police Officer: Who? Tom Lexian: [hurrying, trying to confuse the officer] Duh! Officer Lee, short asian guy, long black hair, carries a badge…anyways, he can testify to the fact that I have a license. He was right behind me in line at the DMV when I was picking it up. You see, I had made the fatal mistake of not re-registering my license when I was supposed to. I figured I would just have to pay a fine or something. But those bastards wanted me to come down there myself and retake the written test….do you know how hard that was? Shit, I barely passed it when I was 16…it’s not like that knowledge sunk in after ten years. Well, after a couple of failures and a close test where I only missed out in passing by 5 or 6 questions, I finally passed, after copying this guy next to me. He had this huge bifocals on, so he couldn’t tell whether or not I was copying his test or checking him out. He was just too scared to say anything about it. Anyways, Officer Lee was right behind me in line when I was getting my license, so he’s proof. Plus, he can testify to the fact that I have insurance as well. I kind of….sort-of…ran into his new 2001 BMW after I had gotten my new license. He was pretty nice about it though. After he cursed me out and threatened to shot my “bloody brains out,” he calmed down and accepted my insurance card with no problem. Police Officer: I don’t know what you’re smoking, but I intend to find out. Let’s get a few things straight here – I don’t know an Officer Lee, I don’t care about your driving skills, and I could care less if you almost got yourself killed. I want to see a license and a drivers license now, or we’re going downtown. Tom Lexian: [begins to fumble through the glove box] Look, calm down. They’ll be no need for that….I’m sure it’s in here somewhere….I got it! I pulled out a small bag that contains his license and registration for the car. I remembered that I placed it there so I wouldn’t forget it, in the case that I ever got pulled over. Wow, I actually did something right for a change. This isn’t in the script. Things aren’t supposed to be going well for ME! Oh wait, they’re not. Just as I pulled out the bag, a magazine falls out of the glove box as well. Tom Lexian: [gives the officer his license proudly] Here you go, officer. I’m sure that’s what you were looking for the entire time. I told you I had it, didn’t I? Police Officer: Yes, yes…stop gloating. Now…[notices the magazine]…Uhm, what is that magazine say on it? It looks awfully familiar…. Tom Lexian: [looks confused] What magazine? Police Officer: What do you mean, ‘What magazine?’ Don’t act stupid, it’s sitting there right on your passenger street. I looked over at the passenger seat, and does indeed see a magazine sitting there. I gasp in horror as he sees the title of the magazine, which reads, “Men Are Beautiful Too” magazine, only $2.45 as the regular newsstand price. Not only was it a magazine about men, but it was also cheap. How embarrassing Tom Lexian: [tries to hide the magazine] I don’t know what you’re talking about….I don’t see anything… Police Officer: [shines his flashlight into the car] Don’t be an idit….[sees the magazine]…Oh my god….oh my god…..OH MY GOD! Tom Lexian: [grabs the magazine, shoving it into the officer’s face] Please, I swear to the lord almighty, this magazine is not mine! I never bought this…hell, I would never buy something like this. Please, I’m not some weird sicko that you would usually find on the streets. A friend must have placed this in my car. I have a lot of immature friends, trust me. There’s this one guy I know, his name’s Duke Williams, and he’s the lamest…he probably put this here! Police Officer: [backing away from the car] Get it away from me…I mean it! Tom Lexian: [flips through the pages] Don’t be alarmed! I swear, it’s not mine. Look, I bet there’s a note in here from one my friends, telling me what an idiot I am for falling for this trick… I continue to flip through the magazine, but I suddenly come to a place in the magazine where I stop. The male model looks oddly familiar. Now, I’m not the kind of person that would enjoy looking at a magazine of this type of…content. But this really caught my eye. No you perverts, not THAT! I look at the model’s name, and then recognized the face. I looked at the officer’s badge…. Tom Lexian: OH MY GOD! THIS IS YOU!? Police Officer: [stammering] …..da…ba….err….la….po…. The police officer runs to his patrol car, gets in, and drives off quickly. I throw the magazine out of my window, backs the car up, and runs it over. I wiped my eyes harshly, trying to get the picture out of his head. I mean c’mon…it was a terrible picture! This was the kind of thing that could scar a person for life. Speaking of scars, that officer had a scar right above his left thigh….ARGH! Tom Lexian: I can’t believe this. The night just keeps getting worse! First I run into the living blow doll, Britney Spears, then Marvin Gaye disses me and runs off when the police come knocking, and then I find out the police are posing in magazines like, “Men are beautiful too.” I need a break from all of this… I turn on the car radio. It begins to play some soothing classical music. I begin to get relaxed, and rock my head gently to the beat. That’s when the radio cuts off, and the DJ’s voice can be heard. Radio DJ: “Hello all you cool cats. That was a way fly jam by that homie, Beethoven. Did you like that? Were you down with that jam?” Tom Lexian: Amen brother! I was down! Radio DJ: Gooooood. Now, before I play some more cool jams for all of you, I have some news. Now, as you’ve all known, we’ve been holding surveys and other polls to find out what you, the listeners, want to hear more of. Now, one of the requests was more news, so we became more specific, and we wanted to know what sort of news you wanted to hear. Was it world news? National news? Bad news? I mean, what was it? Well, the results are in, and the news we’ll be playing for you is….Wrestling News! Tom Lexian: [exclaiming] Wrestling news!? Radio DJ: That’s right, this is now KJAM, the best in classical music, as well as professional wrestling. And kitty cat, we are the best. Do you know why?….BECAUSE STONE COLD SAID SO! Tom Lexian: [gets ready to turn the radio off] Yuck. You know someone sucks when they quote Stone Cold Steve Austin. Hell, whoever quotes a wrestler really sucks. And if anyone has a problem with that, I’ll kick their Roody Poo Candy Ass! Haha! Oh, oops. I quoted the Rock again. Oh well. [shrugs] Radio DJ: “Now, we have some late breaking news concerning the wrestling promotion, PCW.” Tom Lexian: [happily] Hey! I know them! Radio DJ: “Yes, the PCW recently had a show, Tuesday Turmoil, and in the main event, Clyde and Darkstar absolutely beat the living hell out of Legend Inc., Tom Lexian. After the match, he had to be saved by his arch rival, Duke Williams, who assaulted them with a bat. Suffice it to say, Lexian owes him his ass.” Tom Lexian: [gets depressed] Ouch. Those are some pretty harsh comments. Good thing this broadcast is only in the Bay Area. Radio DJ: “In other news, KJAM is proud to announce that we’ve been expanding our broadcasts beyond the Bay Area. We’ll be broadcasting all over the world!….Starting five minutes ago!” Tom Lexian: [Howling] Noooooooo!
