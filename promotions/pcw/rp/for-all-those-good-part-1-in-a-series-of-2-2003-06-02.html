For All Those Good...(Part 1 in a Series of 2)

2 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-2-03 A drive. Something that burns inside of a person. The ability to do the things that he wishes to do in life. It’s like a train wreck ready to happen. Growing more and more each day. Can’t this stop? Evil intentions built from an outside place. The good that lurks within. Which can survive? Which will prevail? The scene begins to slowly open up on the inside of a large passenger train, we found ourselves on the inside of one of the sleeping cars; a man sits on the edge of his bed as the light in the room flickers on and off, shining across his face for a millisecond each time it passes by. A book rests in his hand and the thought that his eyes are closed is evident. The camera begins to pan in closer to the man, as the door is heard opening and the light shoots on in the room. The man on the edge of the bed is revealed to be Shane Robertson, but the person walking in is nothing more than another passenger on the train, who is obviously rooming with Shane until their next stop. The passenger stops, shocked, in his place as Shane opens his eyes and begins to glare at him. Passenger: (Panicking) I’m sorry, did I disturb you? Would you like for me to leave? Shane continues to stare at the man until a smile comes across his face. Shane: Of course not, this is your room as much as it is mine and it is always nice to be able to speak with someone, come in. The passenger proceeds to walk into the room and takes a seat on his bed; a sigh of relief is heard coming from his mouth as he just lies back. Shane: So, tell me, what brings you on your voyage? Passenger: (Chuckling) I wouldn’t really call it a voyage, but I’m just searching. Shane: (Sitting up straight) Oh, really? What is it that you are searching for? Passenger: (Letting out another sigh) It’s really a hard thing to answer, I mean, you probably wouldn’t want to hear it anyway. Shane: (Chuckling a bit) Well, of course I would love to hear your story. It’s not like we don’t have time or anything, the next stop isn’t for a while. Be welcome to start and I’ll humbly sit back and listen, friend. Passenger: (Smiling) All right, my name is Timothy Radcliff, I’m from Los Angeles, California, and I’m twenty-seven years old. I’ve been lost in this world for so long now and I’m just searching for what my purpose is. I was the top of my class in high school and I graduated from UCLA in the top five percent of the class. If you really think about it, I should be one of the happiest people in the world, but I’m not. (Another sigh is heard) I’m not happy in the least. I’ve got nothing in life to live for and I’m searching for something to live for. Shane: (Raising a brow) Shouldn’t you live for your family? I mean, forgive me for asking, but do you not have a faith? Timothy: What do you mean by a faith? Shane: (Pulling his Bible out) As in a religion. Do you believe in anything? Timothy: (Scratching his head) I’ve never really thought about it. My grandparents were Christians, but my family never pressed anything on me, I never attended church services, and I had many friends in college that were either Atheist or something else. When I really think about it, religion is another big thing I’m confused about. Shane: (Shaking his head) I’m honestly sorry to hear that, but religion isn’t something you should really be all that confused about. There isn’t much to it, and I’m not going to go off on any other religions. (Chuckling) That’s what you would expect from a Catholic, but I’m not your average Catholic. I believe a different way, or at least now I do. Timothy: I’m interested in what you have to say, please tell me. Shane: (Holding his Bible out) This book has all of the answers in the world. I’ve lived by it for so long that I can’t remember when the last time I had a major problem in life was. Timothy: Do you believe in helping others? Shane: (Raising his brow once again) Of course I do, it’s the way things were meant to be. Jesus lived his life a way that every person in this world should strive to, but most people are so blinded by Satan that they cannot see the light. I live to help people, although my mission to begin with was far different. Timothy: What do you mean, your mission? Shane: (Sighing) It’s a long story, but I can make it short. I was trained for several years to become a professional wrestler, a program that was funded by the Vatican in Roman. I was sent into the profession to take out any and all people of bad faith and religion, but I guess you could say I have gone rouge on them. Timothy: Why? Shane: Because God told me what they were doing was wrong. I was told to not be the way they wanted me to be, for Jesus would have never been like that. God told me that he would be happy with me just spreading the Gospel to those who are not believers and convert all that I can. Timothy: Convert? That sounds kind of cult like. Shane: Oh, of course not. I don’t convert people. Timothy: Who does then? Shane: (Pointing up) The Lord Almighty does. When you believe in your heart that God has saved you then you are converted. Jesus touches people in miraculous ways, all you have to do is have a willing heart to let him into your life and someone can pray with you over it or you can be alone and just let God work in your life. There is honestly nothing to it, but the road ahead of a Christian isn’t easy. Timothy: The way you make it sound though, it seems so easy. Shane: Trust me, it isn’t an easy life, but the outcome of it all will be worth more than any challenge Satan can throw in front of you. Timothy: What is the outcome? Shane: An ever-lasting life in Heaven with the Lord. No pain and no sorrow ever again, a life that we all wish we could have here on Earth, but that will never be because Satan has a control over people on Earth. Timothy: If God is such a mighty being then why does he let Satan temp people the way he does? Shane: If the Lord could have it His way, then I’m sure nothing would happen to His people. It does though because Satan is so evil in his ways and is given free reign over what he may take on the Earth. Though God is a jealous being, he still allows Satan to do the things he does, as a test towards His people. Timothy: Ah, I believe I can understand what you are saying. Shane: It seems as if you may need this. Shane extends his Bible to the young man. Timothy: (In awe) I can’t accept this, this is yours. Shane: (Smiling) Of course you can accept it, but I ask that you read it and come to a decision on your own about the Lord and if you ever feel compelled to give your life to the Lord I’m sure you’ll easily find the right place to do it. Whether you are alone, or you attend a church services and ask someone to help you in your walk. Shane stands up and begins to make his way out of the room. Timothy: (Standing) Wait! Shane: (Turning around) Yes? Timothy: (Extending his hand) Thank you so much. Shane: (Smiling and taking Timothy’s hand) You’re very welcome. Timothy: (A tear begins to run down the side of his eye) You have no clue how much your talk meant to me. I was on the verge of a mental breakdown and was really ready to commit suicide, that’s the reason I was really here. As soon as we got a little further out, I was going to jump, and kill myself. Now, I believe I have something in life to live for though, and I thank you dearly for that. Shane: (Pulling Timothy in for a hug) I thank you for sharing that with me. I am glad God could use me to touch your life. (Letting go and stepping back) I am on my way to go get something to eat, if you’d like to join, you are more than welcome to. Timothy: (Shaking his head and wiping the tears from his face) No, I’m going to stay here and read what you have given to me. Thank you once again. Shane smiles as he turns and begins to make his way out of the room. ~Scene Fades to Black~
