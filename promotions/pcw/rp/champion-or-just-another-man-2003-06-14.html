Champion or just another man?

14 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/06/03 The clinking of metal echoed down the corridor followed by a brisk sweeping sound as James lay silently on his bed staring at the cold metal ceiling above him, James’ door had been left open intentionally, at first on the airship James had enjoyed the privacy, a time for recollection and a time for personal training, now however things had changed and the solitude was slowly driving James crazy, that’s why the door was open on its hinges, just for the chance of seeing a friendly face, hell it didn’t even have to be friendly just seeing another human being would have been adequate for James. As the sweeping approached James turned his head slightly and looked out of his door to see the figure of Manuel Labor hunched over a broom whistling happily to himself not seeming to have a care in the world. James turned around fully and glanced at Manuel who nodded his direction and gave a little smile before realising he was not being paid to nod but to sweep and continued down the corridor taking away the noise of boots on metal and the constant sweeping faded into the distance. James sighed deeply to himself and let himself fall backwards on the bed once more, hitting the back of his head on the solid metal walls as he did so. James: fucking room. James kicked out randomly sending a small lamp flying across the room before shattering into a number a small pieces upon connection with the wall, James glanced momentarily at the lamp before shrugging and lying down slowly this time taking precautions not to repeat hitting his head. He didn’t understand how Manuel always seemed so carefree any of the champions for that matter. If you took a look at them individually none of them seemed to be worried about the pressures and responsibilities of being a champion. The green team each in their own way did not show the pressures and strains of being a champion. Skylar and Tessa firm favourites with the crowd, knowing that they will always be cheered for never fearing to lose their title. Gaz being so cocky and arrogant the pressures never seemed to get to him, the same applied to Gaz’ one time protégés E2 so confident of their own abilities and unable to think about their own weaknesses yet it was their confidence that made them prevail and Miss Moonlight was in a class of her own, not fearing or worrying about anything always putting others before herself. Then there was James Edwards. The rising star champion. The sound of clinking once more made it’s way down the metal decking as the sound of footsteps became progressively louder making their way towards James’ room as the figure of Silvio Rochelli’s figure blocked out some of the light that was making it’s way through the doorway into James’ room. Glancing in Rochelli looks at James Edwards who is still lying on his bed seemingly a million miles away. Rochelli: Yo punk you seen Jackson. James sighed deeply turning away slightly from Rochelli much to his disgust at a mere man daring to turn his back on him. James: I’m guessing you mean Matt Jackson. Rochelli:Yeah you seen him? James shook his head which received a snort of disapproval from Rochelli who removed himself from being an imposing presence in James’ doorway and made his way down the corridor stomping intentionally causing the sound to echo and reverberate all the way down the spacious corridor causing James to wince slightly as the sound penetrated his ear drums. Jackson, there was the reason that I did not feel like a champion. There was the reason that I could not sleep at nights, the other champions knew that they were the top of their respective levels and that they had earned the right to be called champions but what had I done? Lost to Jackson on three different occasions before finally getting a fluke win when it counted and at the end of the day, that’s what it was a fluke. Or so people had been telling me. I had been told that I shouldn’t have been in the match in the first place. I had been told that Jackson was not focused on the match and that’s why I won, because of something that Jackson did wrong James rolled over on his bed as a glint of metal caught his eye, the rising star title hanging in the far corner of the room the light from outside making it glisten as though it were a star. James raised to his feet his knees clicking worryingly loudly as he did and walked over to the title and clasped it in both hands staring down at it. The fact of the matter was though that I was a champion because of things I had done right, I had trained hard and I had worked hard I had defended my belt once against SyN, I had fought upper card members and whilst I may not have always defeated them, I had always given it my all and in the process people were starting to take notice. I had to beat this curse that was being inflicted upon myself. I had to defy the critics once more and beat Jackson once again; after all he is only another wrestler. James’ thoughts were interrupted by a loud rumbling noise, which caused James to look down and rub his stomach lightly over his dark blue t-shirt realising that he had not eaten in quite some time. Hanging the rising star title carefully up on the hook that he had taken it down from James exited his room and moved into the spacious and empty corridor closing the door behind him, he locks the door and drops his key into the pocket of his jeans before turning round and coming face to face with the man he is to fight against for the fifth time at Turmoil, Matt Jackson. There is an air of silence and tension which could be cut with a knife, the two wrestlers simply staring at each other neither wanting to make the first move, neither wanting to back down. Remember James, he’s just a man and you have something he has…you are the one with all the cards With that a small smile forms on the face of James as he simply nods at Jackson and proceeds to walk past him, his held high and his stride strong and confident. James: I’ll see you at Turmoil, Matt. After all, you’re just another man
