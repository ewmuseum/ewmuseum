Champions: Lost but not Forgotten [Part IX]

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 30, 2002 This morning the fear left them. Their conversation with the less-than-polite bell boy left them confident and ready to succeed. They felt great, from their look and manner to the way they walked through the lobby. This doubt grew less and less, and the hope was at an all time high. Not that the cocky pair needed any extra confidence, but it did help. It helped them to know that they felt good about themselves, and good about tonight. Jack Valentine: (Singing) “Tonight’s the night...we make hissstory...” Johnny Heart: “You really do sing better than the Pretty Cool Guys Jack.” Jack Valentine: “Doesn’t everyone?” Johnny Heart: “True.” Jack Valentine: “How many days have we waited for this moment Johnny...how many days?” Johnny Heart: “It’s taken 48 days for us to get a chance to regain our titles...48 days.” They can hardly realize their own happiness. They can hardly believe that the next 24 hours will be the most eventful 24 hours of their lives. It may complete the last day of separation between the PCW tag team titles, and the pretty boys in PINK! The belts have been locked up for the past 48 days, Jack and Johnny haven’t even got a chance to see them. Almost two months they have spent, lonely, put away in some cabinent at the home of Tyler Nelson or The Phantom Booker, not being able to see the light of day. The belts have been lost, remember? But not forgotten. This has been the theme of this entire series...lost but not forgotten. Tonight will be a reuniting of PINK! proportions. The Heartbreakers were preparing for this moment for 48 days. They will look their best tonight as the chance to be holding one of those belts over their shoulder again is great. After all, they can’t look filthy when their babies finally return home to their hot PINK! daddies. But few words sufficed to tell the public how their desperate venture had ended, and what the next event in their lives was likely to be. They left all details to be described later in the day, and at the Rupp Arena, The Heartbreakers’ fate would be decided at the hands of 17 other men. The Heartbreakers greeted another crude Bell Boy in the lobby again...a different one than last night. Damn rude bell boys in Kentucky...must be something in the water. Jack Valentine: (With an ear to ear smile) “Do you remember when we held the title belts on our shoulders young man?” Bell Boy: “No...I can’t remember you ever holding the tag team titles.” Johnny Heart: “Well we did...we were the last PCW Tag Team champions!” Bell Boy: “Oh that’s right...from what I heard, you guys cried when you had to give them up. What a bunch of losers!” Jack Valentine: (Patting the boy on the shoulder) “It’s okay to cry when you’re happy or sad young man...it’s natural.” Johnny Heart: “Yeah, it’s not gay, as the general public would like to describe it. It is perfectly fine.” Bell Boy: (Laughing Hysterically) “Gay? It’s not gay...it’s down right QUEER if you cry! Faggots!” Jack Valentine: “Now, now...vulgar language isn’t necessary...” Bell Boy: (Pulling Hair) “Ugh! I can’t stand you guys. I don’t see how any of those other PCW fans can cheer for you....you’re so stupid!” Johnny Heart: “Stupid is as stupid does.” The Bell Boy storms off into the distance as the Heartbreakers just grin at the rude boy. Tonight their confidence should be complete. Many have drawn their own conclusions about the outcome of this match, you can see it in their faces. However, The Heartbreakers want to know nothing, keep them out of your thoughts. Tonight they will sacrifice their bodies, for both their sake, and the sake of the fans...only to return to battle very soon after this excrutiating match tonight. Whatever comes of tonight, win or lose, The Heartbreakers will continue to give 100% in PCW. They will not back down, and they will not be plagued by injury as they were in the past. Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart are in perfect health, perfect mind and perfect form. They are prepared to capture what is rightfully theirs yet once again. Jack Valentine & Johnny Heart are members of a Brotherhood. Neither one of these men are false to one another, and will stay true to form in battle tonight. Akiyama has no brother, he has no mate, he is completely alone. Pretty Cool Guys have each other, but are lost in their music. Cowabunga Connection may be a threat, however, one too many rides on the surfboard will cause them to falter. Dark Messiahs, well, they are lost in the darkside, and their communication falters because of Mike Stone. Politically Incorrect, a team that is lost in the battle amongst themselves for power in the so-called “office.” Chef and Parshin are united through Allah, but three’s a crowd. Suicidal Tendencies are separated by, well, it doesn’t really matter what they are separated by now does it? And finally, Rent-A-Villian and Snow are a team separated because now they do not even know each other. Snow and Rent-A-HERO were a team...Snow and Rent-A-Villian are nothing more than future enemies. That leaves us with the ever so famous Heartbreakers. The team that many say are separated by their focus on PINK! instead of their match...however, that couldn’t be farther from the truth. We’ve all seen how close Jack and Johnny can be, and are not afraid to show their “tightness” in the ring. Jack and Johnny would like nothing more than to walk out of this match, and bring their missing children home. Their search for the gold is finally over, and the moment has come. If they do not capture their babies tonight, the status will go from lost but not forgotten, to stolen and deceived. The hours are going like molasses down a pancake...the time they waste before the match is not important, but the time is going by ever so slowly. 8 Teams and 1 Man will be in the ring together tonight, and the boys in PINK! will be in their as well. Lost but not Forgotten...Lost but not Forgotten...Lost but not Forgotten...the belts are no longer lost...they are being held hostage, and tonight the Heartbreakers will deliver the ransom, and take back what is rightfully theirs. -END-
