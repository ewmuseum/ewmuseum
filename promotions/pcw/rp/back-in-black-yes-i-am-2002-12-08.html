Back In Black: Yes I am

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Back In Black: Yes I am Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 12/8/02 -The camera opens up on a top-notch casino at 4am that is alive with gambling. Standing at a one-arm-bandit is a two-armed bad ass named Duke Williams. Duke is wearing a shirt with a small black child on it that reads “Kannon Touched My Privates”, black jean shorts, sandals, and a long bathrobe. His trademark, hat, shades, and beard also grace our presence.- Casino Pit Boss: Sir we do not allow your attire in this Casino. -The Malice Man swings around with a big fat Punch cigar in his mouth. The Churchill is smoking like Ellie’s thighs when she tries to run on a treadmill. The pit boss immediately realizes who the patron is and corrects himself.- Casino Pit Boss: Sorry Mr. Williams I didn’t know it was you. -The Malice Men lets out a warm Cajun smile.- Williams: Hey hoss you can call me Duke. I tell you what you get me a glass of ole number seven and one for yourself and all will be forgotten. Casino Pit Boss: Sure thing Duke!! -The pit boss flees the camera shot and we’re left with our lovable outlaw friend Duke Williams. Duke waves into the camera and you can tell that he’s been drinking all night.- Williams: Hi Kannon. How’s the blood pressure?? It must be stressful trying to come up with new and creative ways to get Ellie’s ass in and out of arena doors. Boy if we taxed ass size we’d all be rich!!! -Duke whistles as he shakes his head.- Williams: But buckaroo I’m not here to talk about her immense, cottage cheese filled, gluteus maximus. No, I’m here to talk about you her over rated, megalomaniac, small dicked, fetish with black boys, husband. That’s you Xavior Kannon! Oh wait I’m sorry I got the memo I’ve been saying your fucked up, third world country, name wrong; its XAVIER….big fucking deal huh hoss? Well Xavier lets get done to brass tacks shall we??? -A waitress comes with Duke’s whiskey he takes it from the lady and gives her ass a smack to her delight then takes a drink of the booze.- Williams: Now that’s what I call service with a smile hoss!!! Anywho back to the bloated head CEO. Hey Kannon when you go to the beautician do you ask for “Gay hair color number 5” or does it just come out that way?? I’m still trying to figure out how a grown man can get his hair to look so gay and still claim to be straight. Maybe Ellie’s ass isn’t so big, maybe she’s hiding something else? -Duke gives the camera an inquisitive glare.- Williams: You think I’m not worthy of a title shot? Well kid maybe your right but let me ask you something flock of seagulls. What are you going to do about it?? Huh hoss? You think you got the balls to take out the Malice Man. You think you can rid me of this company? I can assure you that its you or me. So step up to the plate bitch and take aim. This isn’t like last time this is personal. I’m a rageaholic, alcoholic, sexaholic, maliceaholic, redneck with a bad disposition and a target aimed at your head. So whatchagonnado brother when the Malice Man runs wild on you???? -Hulk Hogan is at the slot machine next to Duke and leans over into the frame.- Hogan: That’s my line hoss!!! Your supposed to send him WHISKEY BENT AND HELL BOUND!!! -Hogan leans back out of the frame.- Williams: Oh yea I’m sorry I’ve been drinking all night and have a bit of a booze problem. Hey well I’m here why don’t I pick on that bean picker know as El Diablo. -Duke drinks down his glass of booze like water.- Williams: You know why the Mexicans only brought five hundred to the Alamo?? -Duke laughs to himself at this really old joke.- Williams: yea they only had two cars. -The drummer at the piano bar lets off the snare drum.- Williams: Thank you folks two shows a night for the next week I’m Duke Williams. -Duke clears his throat and gets back to El Diablo.- Williams: El D I have to fight you again. Didn’t the know it all booker get the point your not main event material after you ruined the last PPV? Well I recon I’ll have to show dem huh? Weeeeeee dooogies!! -Duke tips his hat to a young lady walking by giving him the eye.- Williams: El D you are baby shit. Your funny for a second then the smell and the realization that the baby can’t change himself sets in. Then it all goes south like old people from the north in the winter time. Look kid why don’t you do us all a big fucking favor and deport you ass back to where you come from. -Duke eyes the women who makes a second round of him.- Williams: You’re a spaz man. Not even a cool spaz like Regis or the guy on the infomercials with the question marks on his suit. No your more like Carrot top or that creepy Richard Simmons guy. So please come back and bore me with a promo that may lend a sentence towards me. Kid I don’t believe you’re a devil. Hell I don’t believe you’re a Mexican. Last time you won gold you didn’t have it melted down into a necklace or new teeth. How am I supposed to belive you’re a Mexican. Plus last time I checked out your house the bushes were all messy and the grass was long. To quote the great Mike Ditka “who ya crap’n?” -Duke just laughs.- Williams: Hoss just put on your Green, Red, and White tights. Paint your face like the Ultimate Warrior and stroll down to the ring to get your ass handed to you Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound. -Duke laughs.- Williams: Oh and hoss, that’s the Honky Tonk Truth!!! -Duke goes back to pulling the arm of the bandit as the camera fades.- =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
