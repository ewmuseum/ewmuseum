Date: Saturday, December 28, 2002 Hello, and welcome to Ariz Ona¹s Birthday Bash. If you are unaware, this special event is a costume affair, with everyone attending wearing and bringing something different from everyone else. Who is going to show up to the party? What are they going to wear and bring? Who will win the prizes of Best Costume and Best Gift? It¹s about time to find out... (Marvin Harris) Businessman (Mordred) Normal Ring Attire (Violence Jack) Bobby Stormbringer (Magnum Wolf) Towlie (Shaun Styles) Double Trouble (Kohana) Michael Jackson¹s Baby (Steven Lee) Hugh Hefner & Playboy Bunny (Luis Sangre & Sister Ash) Hessian (Nerezza) Himself (Minion The Imp) School Marm (Tempest) Harry Potter (Jimmy Nguyen) Puppy & Puppy Owner (Chris Storm & Tessa Windsor) Vegetto (Vash) Marvin Harris (Deadhead) Pirate (Chris Collins) Jason McAllister (The Masked Mullet) MC Hammer (Vanilla Skillz) A Normal Person (Chris Matthews) Samurai (Radiant) Shin Yagyuu (Mani Shigeru) Mani Shigeru (Shin Yagyuu) Powdered Toast Man (Al Aska) Marilyn Monroe (Hoshi Akagawa) The scene opens in the kitchen of Ariz Ona¹s Skull Valley home. Currently Misery is sitting in one of the chairs at the table, wearing a sexy plaid skirt. She¹s also wearing a shirt that has the bottom part of the front tied so that it shows off her cute belly button. Meanwhile, Ariz is in the bathroom, putting on his costume. Misery (yelling): Ariz, are you dressed yet? It¹s almost time for people to start showing up. Ariz (also yelling): Yeah. Give me a second. A minute or so later, Ona comes out of the bathroom and into the kitchen. He is decked out in a black leather ninja suit with a mask covering his nose and mouth. He is also wearing a black wig that extends to his shoulders. They look each other up and then down. Ariz: Damn. You look good. What¹s my present from you? Misery: What do you think it is? Riz gets a smile on my face. Ariz: I¹ve got a pretty good idea. In any case, how do I look. Misery: You don¹t look too bad. Actually, you kind of look like The End. Ariz: I know. I decided that I should dress up like him for my birthday party, in honor of him retiring from PCW and all. DING DONG! Misery: There¹s the door bell. I guess I should answer it. I wonder who it is. She heads to the front door and opens it up. A person dressed as a small, funky robot is standing at the door, holding a box and a bottle in his hands. Robot (in a robot voice): Hello, Misery. Misery: Umm...who are you? Robot (yelling excitedly): It¹s me! Marvin Harris. Misery: Oh! Sorry, Marv. Couldn¹t tell that it was you under that outfit. Come on in. She lets Marvin into the house. They then begin to walk into the living room and stop in front of the couch. Misery: Here. Have a seat. I¹ll take your gifts and put them next to one of the walls in here. I have a feeling that there are going to be so many presents that they won¹t all fit on the table. Marvin hands Misery the gifts and sits down on the couch. Harris: Alright. Misery promptly puts them where she said she would put them. She then sits on the opposite side of the couch from Harris. Misery (yelling again): Riz, come in here! Marv is here. Ona walks into the living room from the kitchen. Ariz: Hey Marv. Marvin (shouts enthusiastically): HAPPY BIRTHDAY, ONA! The Rizmaster takes a quick look at Marv. Ariz: Umm...Marv, what is your costume supposed to be? A robot? Harris: I¹m G.I.R.! The Rizmaster gets a puzzled look on his face. Ariz: G.I.R.? Harris: Yeah! You know, the robot from Invader ZIM? Ariz: I don¹t know actually, but I¹ll take your word for it. DING DONG! Misery: There¹s the door again. I¹ll get it. Excuse me. She gets up from the couch. Ona sits down in her place. Misery then walks to the door and opens it up. Four people are standing at the door. They look similar to a Captain America Style Rent-A-Hero, Hugh Hefner and a Playboy bunny, and a Towlie towel respectively. All of them are also holding presents. She gives them an odd look. Misery: Alright. I¹m not going to be able to guess who you guys are so just tell me. Captain America Style Rent-A-Hero: I¹m Jason Marah. Hefner: I¹m Luis Sangre. Playboy Bunny: I¹m Sister Ash. Towlie: I¹m Shaun Styles. Misery: Ah. That solves things. Umm...you all can come on in. Please put your gifts next to one of the walls in the living room. The four of them enter the house and head straight for the living room. Before Misery can shut the door, six more people come up to it. People dressed up as a black pirate with a bird on his shoulder, Jason McAllister (with a mask on), MC Hammer (complete with the really baggy pants), Harry Potter, Hessian, and Michael Jackson¹s baby (with a baby blue sweatsuit and a white towel over his head with eye holes so that he can see) respectively. All of them are also carrying presents. Instead of giving them an odd look, like she did with the previous four guests, she just sighs. Misery: Guessing really isn¹t my thing. Just tell me who you are. It¹ll be much easier on me. After you tell me who you are, you can go in. Umm...please put your gifts next to one of the walls in the living room. I guess the pirate can go first, since he is the closest person to the door. Pirate: I¹m Chris Collins. Misery: Alright. Next. As Collins enters the house, the MC Hammer lookalike goes closer to the door. MC Hammer: I be Vanilla Skillz, Œfoo. Misery: Okay. Who¹s next? As Skillz goes into the house, the Harry Potter lookalike comes forward. Potter: Jimmy Nguyen. Misery: Three down, three to go. Who shall be next? The person dressed as Hessian comes forward as Nguyen goes into the house. Hessian: I am Nerezza. Misery: Only two more. Next. The person who appears to be dressed as Michael Jackson¹s baby steps forward as Rezz walks into the house. Jacko¹s Baby: I¹m Steven Lee. Misery: Alright. Last one. The guy who looks similar to Jason McAllister finally steps up to the door. McAllister: I¹m not going to tell you because you should know who I am. I mean, I am wearing my infamous mask. It should not be hard to guess. Misery: Alright. I¹ll guess. You are...El Spiffy!? The person dressed as Jason McAllister shakes his head. Misery: Umm...Stimpo? The McAllister lookalike shakes his head again. Misery thinks for a moment and finally a lightbulb goes off in his head. Misery: I¹ve got it! You¹re The Masked Mullet! Mullet: Correct. Misery: That¹s all of them. Maybe I¹ll be able to actually shut this door now. Just as she finishes his sentence and begins to once again shut the door, about five more people walk up to it. People dressed as Vegetto, a puppy and his master, a samurai, and an old fashioned school marm are all carrying gifts. Ariz: Alright. No more guessing. Just tell me who you all are as you walk in the door. Please put your presents next to one of the walls in the living room. I¹ll open them once everyone gets here. Each of the guests do as Ona asked them to do, telling him their real names as they walk into the house. Vegetto: I¹m Vash. Puppy: Chris Storm here! Puppy¹s master: It¹s me! Tessa Windsor! Samurai: I am Radiant. Marm: You should know me. Your boyfriend was in my class in PWA. Misery: Tempest, is that you? The marm smiles at her. Marm: It sure is. Misery: There. That¹s it for that group of people. Screw this. I¹ll just leave the door open. I¹m going inside. She leaves her post at the door and goes around the whole house before stopping in the living room. By the time she finishes doing this all of the rest of the guests have shown up to the party. There are also a couple of things going on. Chris Matthews, dressed as a normal person, is attempting to kill Vanilla Skillz who, as we already know, is dressed to look like MC Hammer. Kohana, who has two games of Trouble on his body, keeps getting asked who he is about every ten seconds. He responds by yelling, ³I am Double Trouble!² while popping himself on the head and chest, where the two games of Trouble are located. Magnum Wolf (Just guess what his costume is. Pointing it out really is not necessary.) keeps close tabs on Tessa Windsor, following her wherever she goes. Violence Jack, who is wearing his usual wrestling attire (Ceremonial black robes with various symbols and glyphs of the occult on his face) though everyone believes that he is costumed, preaches to anyone that will listen to him for two seconds about the gospel of Cthulu. Skitzo is talking with the other Skitzoes. Their clothes are all drawn on with a black marker in the style of a tuxedo for some odd reason. Marvin is also chasing around Deadhead, who is wearing a Marvin Harris costume with a chewed up mask and a face scarred as if it had teeth marks all over it. Currently, people appear to be scattered all over the living room. In addition, there are several people in the kitchen that are already getting some cake. Misery takes one look at the walls in the room, which have some presents next to them. Misery: Okay Ariz. Time to open up some presents. Ariz: Alright but before I start I just want to congratulate Vanilla Skillz and Skitzo for having the Best Costumes here! Yes, I said Best Costumes. It was a tie. Get over it. As the crowd applauds Skillz and Skitzo for their respective outfits, Ona gets up from his seat on the couch and walks over to the presents. He picks one of them up at random. Ariz: This one appears to be from Chris Matthews. He opens up the present, discovering nothing but a pile of ashes inside it. Ariz: Ashes? Why did you give me ashes, Matthews? Chris stands up from the place in which he was sitting. Matthews: It¹s a burnt up Arizona flag, you idiot. Man, does Arizona suck ass or what? Ona gets a shocked look on his face. Ariz: I cannot believe that you dare to insult my home state here in my own house! Get out! Matthews: Sounds like a good idea to me. This party stinks anyway. Matthews walks out of the living room and out of the house. The Rizmaster takes a few seconds to get over the shock of seeing the ashes of the Arizona flag. He then hands the ashes to Misery. Ariz: Please take this and throw it in the trash. Misery obliges and Riz picks up another present from the pile. Ariz: This gift is from The Masked Mullet. He opens it up to discover a wig. Ariz: A wig? Why in the world did you give me a wig, Mullet? Mullet: It¹s a mullet wig. That way you do not have to walk around in shame because you do not have a mullet. Ariz: Ah. I understand now. I¹ll have to put it on later. I already have a wig on at the moment. Thanks, Mullet. Let¹s see, what¹s next... Ona picks up another present as he hands the wig to Misery. Ariz: From Tessa Windsor and Chris Storm. He rips opens the box. He pulls out a cute little teddy bear and a collector¹s edition of autographed Chris Storm pornography. Ariz: Tessa, thanks for the bear. Storm, that¹s just plain wrong. I don¹t want to see you naked. I don¹t swing that way. Storm gets a little angry at Ona questioning his sexual orientation. Riz gives Misery the pornography collection and teddy bear and grabs another box from the stack. Ariz: This one is from...Marvin Harris. He opens it up to discover patented Marvin Harris merchandise, including his trademark mask, and a bottle of Jack Daniels. Ariz looks at Marv and smiles. Ariz: Thanks for the liquor, Marv. And for the merchandise of yours too. Alright. What¹s next? Ona hands the bottle and the merchandise to Misery and picks up yet another box from the pile, which keeps getting smaller. Ariz: Hmm...this one seems to be from Minion the Imp, Nerezza, and Skitzo. He opens up the box. He takes out a bottle full of the breastmilk of many virgins, one hundred cans of Rezz Bull, and donkey porn. At the sight of the breastmilk and the porn he gets an odd look on his face. However, that changes the second he sees the Rezz Bull. Ariz: Minion...Skitzo...your gifts are kind of nasty. Rezz, thanks for the beer. Minion and Skitzo laugh as The Rizmaster hands the Rezz Bull, breastmilk, and donkey porn to Misery and picks up yet another box. Ariz: This one appears to be from Magnum Wolf, Steven Lee, Shaun Styles, and Kohana. He tears the present open. He pulls out... Ariz: One dollar and twenty seven cents, Styles? Styles: And you¹re lucky to be getting that. The next thing he takes out is... Ariz: A coupon from Kohana for a night alone with Chief Squatting Dan? Kohana, I¹m going to tell you the same thing I told Storm. I don¹t swing that way. Next, he pulls out... Ariz: Chloroform, Magnum? Wolf: Hey, there¹s always some to spare. You never know when you might need it. The final thing he takes out is... Ariz: A 2003 XXX Tempest Calendar and Doll set from Steven Lee. Dude, that¹s kind of nasty. I don¹t like Tempest like that. Plus, I have a girlfriend. Luis gets up from where he is sitting and grabs the calendar. Sangre: In that case, I¹ll take it. He runs off. Lee gets up and runs after him. Lee: Hey! That¹s Ariz¹s present, not yours! Get back here! As they chase each other around, Ona hands the other gifts to Misery, who takes them to a safe place. The Rizmaster then takes another box from the very small pile, opens it up, and shows the presents to everyone. Ariz: This one¹s from Sangre and Sister Ash, Radiant, Jimmy Nguyen, and Tempest. Let¹s see here...there¹s a year¹s supply of Mexican jumping beans, a Speedy Gonzalez t-shirt, and a bible signed by Pope John Paul II. All of those are from Ash and Sangre. There¹s also a banzai tree, from Radiant, a Karate Kid DVD, from Nguyen, and my PWA diploma wrapped in a slinky, from Tempest. Thanks guys. I¹ll have to get a frame for the diploma so I can put it on the wall somewhere...probably next to the other one I have. Ona hands the presents to Misery, who puts them in a safe place, and picks up the last present. Ariz: This one¹s from Violence Jack, Jason Marah, Vash, Vanilla Skillz, Al Aska, Deadhead, Mani Shigeru, and Shin Yagyuu. The Riz tears open the box and begins to pull out the various gifts inside. Ariz: Let¹s see...A ³Da Skillz² CD featuring 15 cover songs from rap¹s greatest artists from Vanilla Skillz... I hate rap...with a passion. ³Holla Back² by Fabulous made sure that I would never like rap music in any shape or form in my lifetime. Ariz: ...Nude photos of Rei Ayanami from Vash... Those are some good pictures, even if they are from anime. Ariz: Log? Al Aska: LOG! Whatever. Works for me. Ariz: 12 empty cans of Guinness and a book on Grandism from Marah... Why are the cans empty? And what is Grandism? Ariz: A copy of Necronomicon from Violence Jack... I don¹t know what the hell Necronomicon is but oh well. Ariz: And finally a still beating cow heart from Deadhead, the heart of a politician from Mani, and a human head from Shin. Why all of the body parts? Ariz: Anyway, I guess that¹s it for the presents. Collins: Wait! There are some things that people brought which they didn¹t put into a box. Ariz: Well if anyone is one of those people now would be the time to give me my present. Collins goes over to Ariz and hands him the bird on his shoulder. Collins: Here is your present from me. I didn¹t want him to die in a box. Ariz: That¹s very considerate of you. Umm...I¹ll have to name him later. I can¹t think of one right now. Collins sits back down as Clyde comes up to Ona. Clyde: My present to you is Mike Kreller¹s will to live inside an empty bottle of Bourbon. Ariz: Well Congratulations Clyde. That present happens to be the Best Gift of the night! The crowd of people applauds at Clyde winning the title of Best Gift as he sits back down. Ariz: Okay. Anyone else? Hoshi Akagawa, who is wearinga white dress and blonde wig to look similar to Marilyn Monroe, gets up and walks over to Riz. Hoshi: I¹ve got something for you. Ariz: What is it? Hoshi: It¹s a song. Ariz: Alright. Go ahead. Hoshi starts to sing... Hoshi: Happy Birthday To You, Happy Birthday To You, Happy Birthday Dear Riz, Happy Birthday To You! At the end of the song, people clap once more. Hoshi goes and sits back down. Ariz: Well, I think that that¹s everyone... Mordred: Hold on! I¹ve got something for you, Riz. Mordred walks up to Ariz. Mordred: I brought you something special. Ona looks him up and done and discovers that he has no present of any kind in his hand. Ariz: Umm...where is it? Mordred takes a look around the room. Mordred: Avril! Avril, where are you? I sure hope he doesn¹t mean Advil Latrine. I hate her music. It¹s quite annoying. Avril Lavigne stands up from where she was sitting and walks over to Mordred. She¹s decked out in extreme bondage gear. Avril: Here I am! Wow. Her music might suck but she doesn¹t look too bad. Ariz: Avril Lavigne? Mordred: She¹s your present. Sweet! Ariz: Kickass. Thanks. Mordred: No problem. Ariz: Looks like that¹s all of the presents. You all can have some cake if you want. I¹m going to go outside for a bit for some fresh air. As the people begin to scatter, Ariz heads out of the house. He stands in front of the door for a few moments. He begins to go back into the house when all of a sudden an unknown person, who appears to be wearing some sort of costume that really cannot be seen, jumps off of the roof and nails Ona upside the head with a foot long object, knocking him out cold. As the scene fades, the unknown person runs off into the night...
