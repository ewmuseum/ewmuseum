A Silent Tension

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/03 Ding Dong Ding Dong The clock inside the house struck noon just as I began to stir from the bed I had made out of the couch. It wasn’t the clock that had woken me up though, it was the sound of children running around the living room having nothing to do because the cold outside was far too chilling. “Wake up Tessa!” Shouted the youngest one of the bunch, Evan. He jumped on the couch next to where I had fallen asleep once Allison had left. “Okay I’m getting up, hold on!” I responded as I sat up making room on the couch for Christa and Alex on the couch. While doing so I noticed Chris making his way towards the living room as well. The kids must have seen him as well because in just a matter of seconds they were off the couch and running towards the door like they had seen Santa himself. Chris didn’t seem to mind the show of affection from the kids, but he slowly walked over to the couch and took a seat on the other end looking over at me with a questioning look in his eyes. “Who was that at the door this morning?” Chris asked, looking at me with suspicion filled eyes as if I was guilty of trying to hide something from him. “Allison.” I replied to show him that I had nothing to hide from him. “Why was she here?” He questioned as both Christa and Alex both looked over at me at the mention of their mother’s name, both pondering the same question that Chris had asked. “We’ll talk about it later.” I said, trying to hint to him that I didn’t want to talk about it out in front of the kids quite yet. They would find out eventually, but I didn’t feel they were ready for news of this magnitude, especially when they were this cheerful. “Tell us now!” Alex pleaded with me, knowing very well that I couldn’t tell him no considering it was his mother so it was just as much his business as it was mine. “Fine…” I sighed, giving into the child’s demand knowing that if I didn’t tell him now he would find out anyways, so I might as well get it over before it became ever harder to say. “Tessa what’s wrong?” Young Christa asked as she walked over and took a seat beside me on the couch, wondering why I had such a long face. She could tell that something was on my mind, but she had no clue how much it would affect her as well and leave her with the same long face. “Allison got custody.” I answered in one simple line, making it clear that I didn’t like what had happened and that I had nothing to do with it. “WHAT?” Everyone but Evan and I shouted, just as surprised as me that someone like her could gain custody of children she had left for three long years without any sign of return. “She’s taking them on Monday.” I continued to inform them of the details knowing that it broke their hearts just as much as it broke mine, but there was nothing I could do to fix that. Instead of tears though there was a silent tension overwhelming us all. We knew that it was too late to do anything, and we knew that this was probably the last time we’d spend together in the family, the only thing left to do for us was to hope. Hope that things couldn’t get any worse and for me hope that everything would be fine again with Chris and I and that come time for Spring Fling I would no longer have to bear the name Tessa Green.
