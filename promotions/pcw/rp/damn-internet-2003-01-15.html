Damn internet

15 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Damn internet Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 01/15/03 “The Malice Man” Duke Williams sits at a desk in his Missoula, Montana home. Duke is dressed in all black with the top three buttons of his shirt undone exposing a thing gold chain sitting in his chest hair. On strange feature is Duke is not wearing chrome shades but rather reading glasses. Sitting next to Duke is his youngest daughter Springfield. She is wearing a pink sponge bob square pants nightgown. Springfield is trying to teach her father the wonders of the Internet. Springfield: Ok dad now you have to create a screen name. Williams: Ok, I just type it in? The young Malice girl shakes her head yes as Duke types in D-U-K-E-W-I-L-L-I-A-M-S painfully one finger at a time. An error pops up. Springfield: Somebody already has that name. Williams: Oh ball’s, that’s my name! Springfield: Somebody else got it first. Williams: How do I do that e-thingy where I can send a letter and demand my name back. Springfield: You can’t do that. Duke looks at the computer perplexed. Springfield: Pick a nickname. Duke again gets out his tardy fingers and starts to type in B-I-G-E-L-W-O-O-D-9 Williams: There my first wrestling name and my lucky number. Duke hits enter and gets the soothing tone of the “welcome” guy. Springfield: Your on the internet!!! Williams: One small step for Malice one giant leap for Malice kind. Springfield looks at her father like he’s nuts. Williams: Ok now what? Springfield: What to see a fantasy wrestling site? Williams: A what? Springfield: Fantasy wrestling it’s where people pretend to be wrestlers and right little stories. The Malice Man chuckles at the idea. Williams: Sounds like geek central to me!! I don’t have time for that. How do I mail a letter over the e-thingy? Springfield: E-mail? Williams: Sure, does it cost anything? Duke’s daughter gets a laugh out of her technical superiority to her father’s skills. Springfield: No you just type and send. Click the mail box at the top. Duke moves his mouse with caution over to the mail box and clicks the icon and nothing happens. Springfield: You have to click it twice. Duke clicks the icon once again. Williams: Nothing happened? Springfield: You have to click it twice in a row. Duke double clicks the icon and enters the e-mail. He studies it astutely through his reading glasses. He glares over to his PCW notebook and starts to enter in somebody’s address Pharaohatpcw.com. Springfield: No, you don’t type at you type this button @. Williams: That means at can’t I just type at. Springfield: Now it doesn’t work that way. Williams: Fine, this is a pain in the @ss. Duke enters in the address correctly. Williams: Ok honey I have to type a business letter go get ready for work. The young Williams girl runs up the stairs and out of the picture. The Malice man begins his letter. *Dear Pharaoh, Hi hoss this is your president and opponent for this Friday’s Fusion. A few notes before we engage in battle. First off I think that little skirt you wear is gay. No, maybe not gay just plain stupid, why don’t you buy some pants you cheap prick. Nobody wants to see you wrestle in a skirt you hairy-legged fuck. What are you anyway a Hawaiian or something. I don’t like Hawaiian’s and I don’t like you. Mr. Pharaoh I’ve watched promo’s with you and Tito and the rest of your merry men and they piss me off. If your one of them terrorist’s I’m really going to kick your ass hard. Something about you just irks me and makes me see red. Maybe its that stupid hair cut or the accent. Look amigo why don’t you go back to Mexico or where ever the hell your from. The brass tacks is this hoss, I’m an American and your not. I represent the hard working men and women of my great land. You are nothing more then a cave dwelling Hawaiian terrorist. Get bent hoss….that whiskey bent and hell bound!!! Disrespectfully yours, Duke Williams=w= * Duke hits send and mails the letter off. Getting into the swing of things Duke types out letter number two to Snow. Dear, Snow You know I have a lot of respect for you but the nice words end there. While you we’re taking it in the ass in prison I was battling Kannon and keeping this company a float. As a matter of fact our paths crossed a few times while you were let out to work. I don’t remember you impressing me in any of those matches. Now your free and the number on contender for the world title. I’ve been here almost two years and I’m yet to be number on contender for that title. Now I know you a former champion and deserve a title show. But the fact you got that shot over night makes me wonder whom you were blowing in the old stony. This Friday I have the opportunity to beat the living hell out of you and show the world it should have been me. I wish you the best of luck with Clyde. I hope you win. But come hell or high water that belt is my top priority and I will kill, slaughter, rip, bite, and tear to get what I want. I can care less who I have to go through to get what I want. I’ll send you and the world Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound….hoss. Duke Williams =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
