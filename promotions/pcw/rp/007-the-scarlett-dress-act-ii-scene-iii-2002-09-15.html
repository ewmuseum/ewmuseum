007 - The Scarlett Dress; Act II, Scene III

15 September 2002

Date: 09.15.02 Roland's Cafe`New Orleans, LA 10:15 amSunday 'What'll it be mack?' Peter Evans felt like having flapjacks and maple syrup, but he knew that his stomach couldn't take it. He considered waffles for a suggestion, but it was too rich. He would have to settle for coffee for now. Boring, bland, but a necessity. 'Just coffee,' Peter told the waitress, with a brief, forced smile. The waitress left the table taking down Evan's order, leaving the detective towards his lonesome once more. He opened up the newspaper that he had brought into the cafe` and the front page caught his eye originally. The headline screamed murder, 'Dante Jackson - Rap Star or Long Rap Sheet: But is He Guilty?' It was one large slap in the face for Peter, who had worked so hard since he got the case, only for the press to throw it all out the window. They disavowed his testimony and April Jackson's, calling it 'phony' and 'blasphemy.' They wondered, should they listen to the old man who is making a mountain out of an ant hill. Peter closed the paper and placed in the chair on side of him. He didn't want to further put guilt in his stomach. As he looked back up into the air, he happened to spot Kendra James walking into the cafe`, wearing an open red blouse and black skirt. Evans placed his right hand under his chin in pondering about what to do next? The two of them happened to make eye contact for a second, before she waved over to him. He didn't want to have any type of contact with Kendra, especially after the conference fiasco. The difference between the two of them was the ounce of care. Kendra happened to, she only wanted the best for people while Peter didn't. 'Hey Pete,' Kendra cheerfully told him as she took a seat next to him. 'Care if I join?' 'Nah, go ahead,' he told her as the waitress brought upon his coffee`. Peter began to slurp on it immediately. 'Vacancy's welcome.' 'You know all press isn't bad press, you know?' Kendra said with a whim look on her face. 'Don't let it get you down, I mean you had a pretty rough outing yesterday, all of us do.' 'That's not the point, Kendra.' Peter said, expressionless. It read out to him that Kendra was trying to rub the entire incident in his face, from point a of the beginning to point z of the end. But Peter was indomitable, and Kendra was no match for him to be knocked down. 'They're defiance towards the truth, it humors me.' Peter said with a chuckle, almost as defiant as his tone the day before. 'Those blind idiots, they don't know the truth, do they?' Kendra just shook her head, 'You're letting this guy get to you. Who is Dante Jackson before this? Nothing. You're blowing him up and its making him look better and you the fool.' 'We'll I'll just point the truth out to them. They'll listen sooner or later.' Kendra placed a hand on Peter's shoulder. 'Its not worth your blood pressure to rise man, its not.' 'This is not a crusade,' Peter snapped back at her, ravenous towards the point of failure. He pushed back away from her touch. 'This is about a dead man, who wants revenge from the grave.' 'Wake the hell up for a second,' Kendra began her macho idealism towards Peter. 'What is Jackson bringing to the table? The city, hell maybe even most of the government, and he's bringing in more money than you can get in a lifetime. What are you bringing? Nothing, except making this a huge mess. What do you think the city will side on after you tell the truth?' 'Him?This isn't about him, its about -' 'The Justice System?' 'Are you trying to tell me that the city will care about a man generating revenue, rather than the care of a case meaning to every man in this town?' Kendra placed her right index finger on his chin. 'Hmm, a celebrity using his swing to get off the hook? Wow, that's original!' She told him sarcastically. 'Get it through your head man, you think Jackson would make the mistake once more? You're dead wrong, dead wrong.' The waitress, scared to even speak a word happens to lay the bill on the table. Peter eyes it, before stepping up from the chair and tossing a ten dollar bill on the table. He sneered at his co-worker. 'I've had about enough of you.' Kendra grabbed onto his jacket, 'I'm not going to speak to you as a co-worker, nor as a consultant, but as a friend in this matter. You aren't going to win this. He has friends, power, hell even good looks! So you know what, back away and back away now.' 'Let's get this straight, Ms. James.' he shot back at her, brushing her away. 'We aren't friends, and I'm damn sure not moving away from this.' He stalked off, not even trying to look back as he pushed the cafe` doors open. Kendra tried to follow him, but stopped herself for reason. As Peter began to walk away, he could feel a lump building in his throat and tears streaming down his face. He wiped them away, before anyone could happen to see them and continued to walk, wondering to where go now. That's when Kendra's cellphone began to ring. It was probably another reporter working freelance for a tabloid hotsheet wanting answers from her co-worker's bogus story. She picked it up and answered it, 'Yeah?' 'Um, Ms. James, this is Dante Jackson.' he calmly said into the phone. 'I want to talk, for just a quick moment.' 'Sure, sure.' Kendra perked up a bit, 'But the question is, about what do you want to talk about?' * * * Waltz Hotel RoomNew Orleans, LA 11:00 amSunday The rain washed down on the hotel window, as I sat in one look of gloom and loneliness. Talking with Ms. James only about an hour ago happened to place me in a major check period. The mood in the room, with Desmond and my friend Aaron happened to be a silent once, each of them trying to cut the silence with a knife. Still, the rainy day kept me occupied for a minute as the time continued to pass. Looking at the passing cars from each person in town, enjoying their common Down South lifestyle. It was odd, because usually I would be excited for an upcoming match or not. 'Hey, what happened at your press conference?' Desmond spoke out, ending the silent treatment we had been sending one anohter. 'I hear it was something emotional.' 'I know, ' Aaron nodded. 'Something out of that nature, you just can't keep up with.' I sighed to the both of them, neither one of them looking towards the truth. They both believed I was innocence, sending their gestures of hope and understanding to my point of view. I thought to myself, before hearing the phone ring once again. 'Pick that up,' I sighed to myself, before laying my head on the pillow, in deep thought. Desmond raised and eyebrow, thinking should he or shouldn't he happen to prove something to me. I shot him an accusing stare, before he finally picked up the phone. 'Hello?' 'Hello? Is this Dante?' 'Dante?' Desmond questioned out loud enough for me to hear. I turned my head to look at him, while he raised the phone into the air, signalling that it was for me. He thrusted the phone into the air once more. 'Get the phone!' 'Fine.' I groaned to myself, before slugging over and picking up the phone. 'Yeah?' 'Well, nice to know you're still in this world, son.' the voice replied on the phone. I snuck in a slight chuckle. This man on the phone happened to teach me something since I was a child. Or you could say that he's my father. 'I know dad.' I told him, holding the phone handle with my shoulder. He stuck out a smile, I could tell by the warmness of his voice. His calls might have been come and gone with his calls, but I still welcomed his voice into my life. 'So how you holdin' up?' 'I'm doing fine, just great. I'm just wondering on your part.' 'My part ?' I asked him, blinking a few times before swinging the phone around. Eric Jackson was forty-nine, in his third year of retirement from Time Magazine and a wealthy man, thanks in large part towards my contributions. It didn't make much sense for a man of his age to be such a certain way, but it happens in the select few. Those select few happened to be on the top of their respective fields and left in the sunset. 'Yes, your part. What is this I hear about you killed somebody?' 'Dad, I'm not going to -' 'I don't want you to lie to me, Dante. You know liars aren't one of the bedfellows of a believer.' he came up with quotes to make only you listen, and then you'd come to terms with it. Lowering my head in shame, I could only wonder. With a bit of gloom in my voice, I told him. 'I did it.' 'So they're telling the truth?' he asked me with conscience. I nodded. 'Yeah, they're smart little boys and girls. I'm going to make a statement later about this to the press. This girl April Jackson, she pointed me out as the man she saw in the mayor's mansion, saving her.' 'April Jackson...' I stopped from continuing on, 'Wait, you know her?' 'Yeah, its a long story.' 'No...'I was stuck in that same disbelief mode that Desmond was in yesterday when he told me that I didn't do what I know I did. 'You're not saying...' 'Let me explain. Around 72' before I met your mom or anything, there was this co-worker. Well that night, we got real drunk and she happened to be real about our situation. I could only try and force her from stopping, but I continued. I didn't stop and it ended on a sour note. The proverbial one night stand if you will. A few months later, she calls me up and tells me that she has a baby on the way.' 'Like that Tom Lexian ordeal?' I subpoenaed, warranting him for question in this matter. 'Just like it, except no consipracy plots were brought up against me.' he tried to chuckle about it, but I wouldn't take it for a joke. It wasn't funny, none of it was. 'Continue on.' He sighed, 'After that, we became close, before she told me the baby had come and she named it April. She listed me as the father. Twenty four years old, I wasn't ready to be no father man!' 'So you bailed on her?' my blood was beginning to boil, wanting to erupt right then and there. 'You bailed on this woman, and then you met mom and had me?' 'Yeah.' I couldn't believe this, my own dad, the one person I had trusted since birth had lied straight in my face, shrouded it from me for 23 years. I had a sister, and that sister could have been anywhere in the United States of America. I could have been somebody's friend, but the press would get onto this fast, and if it came towards me, I'd lack credibility. I continued muttering. 'No..no...no....no!' I launched the phone into the air, letting it smash along the floor and have connecting wire strangled along the floor. I couldn't answer him to this point, but whatever was left of the phone, I could hear his voice still there, wanting me to answer him. What was I going to say? I couldn't give him an answer he'd regret, it'd be serve enough to severe the relationship we had created over the years. I fumed around the house, struggling to find an outlet for my rage and frustrations. Hearing Desmond speak out once more, 'What he say?' 'You don't want to know.' I roared towards him and slammed the door behind me, because I was too angry to yield the floor to someone else, no. My problems were the source, the main fuel in my fire at the moment and you couldn't bask that away from me. Plotting what I could say to the press, Aaron broke out of the room, leaving Desmond behind. He jerked me back by the shoulder and pinned me on the wall. That same hungry growl that was in my voice only a few minutes ago, had located into his. 'The hell is wrong with you man?' 'Get off me!' I strained to get off the wall, but the smaller, yet more built Aaron wouldn't budge. 'Stop, and breathe for a minute. What did your pops just tell you?' 'He - he said...'I choked up on the words, before tears started to stream down my face. It was all I could muster, no thrown fists, or screams and yells. My dad had broken my heart, like the first time that a girl breaks up with you. Or you lost a fight.It ate at me deeply. The brisk sudden feel of air conditioning against the nape of my neck sent chills down my spine. 'Let me down, you big ape!' I joked with him and he complied. Using my shirt tail, I wiped away the tears, quick enough not for him to see it. Aaron nodded, before pulling out his cellphone. 'What are you doing?' I asked him quietly. He shrugged his shoulders, 'Calling some people to get this all settled out.' I reached over and snatched the phone from his hands. He shot me an accusing stare, but I didn't pay no mind towards it. 'Call Kendra James.' 'Ain't that the chick trying to get you in jail?' 'No, her old friend Peter Evans is trying to, but I have to tell the truth towards the people, its the only way my friend.' I started to walk down the stairs, continuing my own trek to find an outlet to speak on to include my side of the story, many people had been wanting to hear it for days, and now had to be the time. 'The only way,' I repeated. * * * Louisiana SuperdomeNew Orleans, LA 6:30 pmDay Five of GTT The rushing sound of conversing reporters happened to surround the entire conference at hand, as Aaron stood on my left side, flanking me against the wall. Desmond happened to strictly protect ground control as he and most of the security guards happened to mush back reporters wanting a closer eye view on the action. Every eye inside of the large domed superstructure happened to be placed on me, as if I were the star player on the New Orleans Hornets. I finally answered Ms. James' phone call, because right now, I needed an alternative for people trying to believe me. The truth was out there, and with her doing her job and myself doing mine, I had let something off of my chest. Unlike the shocking news my dad had given me. I could even spot Ms. James and Mr. Evans standing in the background, a few inches away from the farthest reporter and both of them would either blow the roof off the Superdome with their allegations becoming more and more on the rise. They would do that, its their job to knock me off of my game right now, I couldn't deny that for long. Evans was perked up, once more from his usual tirade of solum silence and distinguished look of persona. I guess his own words were eaten up from the presses point of view as they didn't believe him. In a way, I felt sorry for him. 'Ladies and Gentlemen,' I broke the silence speaking into the microphone. 'I believe that we came here for the truth, not some gossip tale.' They laughed at the insight joke as Evans grumbled in the back. I chuckled myself, before continuing on. 'Anyway, I believe we need no introductions for you know who I am. Let's get down to business, shall we? As many of you know, I've been plastered onto every newspaper in the state and I'm cooperating with the local authorities about my case, working with them is probably the best for all of us right now.' 'Mr. Jackson -' 'Please,' I interrupted her collected. 'call me Dante.' She chuckled, before placing her pen near her lips. 'Well, Dante, an detective has been investigating your case, from the beginning towards the end. His name is Peter Evans, and is widly thought by the media to furthering her career by using you as her 'career-guinea pig.' Do you have any type of transgression against Mr. Evans?' 'You know, the New Orleans Police Department has been doing their job, right down to the book and you can't tell them that their job happens to be a used way of beating up on your celebrities. Mr. Evans is doing his job as well, just don't single him out if you don't believe him now.' Another interrupted. 'But what is the truth?' I scratched my head before placing my palms on each side of the podium. 'The truth is, that we will be conducting tests and procedures to make an accurate viewpoint of justice.' 'Will DNA be one of the test?' 'Of course it will, because if we didn't be might be like Texas. They execute people without finding the truth. Am I right? But yes, DNA will be the main test conducted.' 'If you are found the guilty party, Dante, will you truthfully want to do?' I chuckled. 'What do I want to do? I want to just prove something out to the people that my truth happens to be apart of that. I want the best for everyone, but I'm going to tell you for certain that when this is all over, justice will be served.' I walked away from the podium, proving something Evans should have done when he had his very own time to talk. The reporters continued to shout questions at me, but I didn't care for them. Whether or not they realized it, I was the guilty party, and now my sister is the person I want in question. Aaron rubbed his hand against my shoulder blade, 'You did good out there champ.' Champ, something I want to be when this is all over. There is a new number one contender for the tag team titles, and that's Playaz Inc. For a reason, or the shallow fact that Quo, Rush, and myself all have to deal with haters Friday, it does us no means. Its something we enjoy doing. I walked into the lockeroom, pulling a shower knob open and began to think what could I do, what is there to do now? 'Find April...' I whispered to myself. 'Find April.'
