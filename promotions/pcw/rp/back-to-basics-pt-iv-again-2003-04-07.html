Back to Basics Pt IV: Again?!

7 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th April 2003 Jade gasps to life as her head raises from the floor after her head smashing against the door. Her eyes slowly settled on her surroundings, it begins calming down. “Again?” Jade hardly sounded enthused as she pulled herself up from the floor, pulling a stick with her. Jade brushes her knees off and stands behind the large rusty door. The door rattles to life and Jade jams the stick under it. The man squeezes through the gap which was left by Jade and looks behind the door. Jade kicks the man in the face and pushes his back against the door. Before the man gets a chance to call out Jade covers his mouth with her hand and presses her fist against his spinal column allowing a crunch to ring through the air. “Night, night baby. Shhh.” Jade whispered as he drops to the floor. Jade chased into the room she previously left open and looked around it. This was previously what Jade would have referred to as her sitting room, with light allowed in all around the room. Now it was a pit, darkness ruled, challenged only by a miniscule candle sat on the table. There are paper profiles plastered all over the desk. They seem to belong to ladies; probably sluts knowing Mani’s taste in women. Jade looks around the room. There used to be a safe behind the Van Gogh painting which no longer remains on the dingy walls. Damp lifts from the bottom of the walls, slowly killing any nice effect this room once had. Jade traces her hand over the wall as she walks along the room. As she drags her feet along the wooden floor boards her foot kicks a metal tub tied to the floor. “Ow!” Jade quickly slams a hand over her mouth; she needs to shut up so she’s not to alert anyone. Jade falls to her knees and looks about. Her face is right next to the box yet she can not see a thing in the darkness of night. Jade stands back up and pulls down a candle, the candle flickers slightly in the soft breeze and she holds the light right next to box. “I wonder what delicious suprises Mani has left in the box for his favourite cousin Jade.” Jade fiddles through her hair and pulls out a hairpin. After fiddling and contouring the shape for a while Jade slams the fine piece of metal into the lock. Jade spits on her finger and forces it into the key hole before twisting the metal to the sound of a comforting click. Jades eyes almost light up as she sees just what suprises had been left in there by Mani. Jade’s hands quickly fiddle through the contents and yank out hands full of the content. There, when she holds her hands to the flame she sees a handful of cash we she manages to shove down the front of her cat suit in the plush cleavage. There are bags of white powder which Jade opens a bag of and dabs a finger into it, Jade is a little blown away when she tastes the contents. “Coke.” Jade sounded stimulated. “There must be at least Ten K’s worth in here! This shit will be nice for a good boost of the old money.” Jade thumbs right down to the bottom of the box and pulls out a sheet of papers with little A’s all over the paper. “Acid?” Jade looks a little worried. “What the hell is that fucker pulling here?” Jade questioned as she ripped the box off of the floor; the box is slammed onto the desk with a loud rattle. “Crap!” Jade looks at the door and is almost waiting for someone to come running through with their machine guns raring. A couple of minutes pass and still there is no one coming through the door. Jade lifts her eye brows and goes on her way. “He is running a ring! A prostitution ring from my home?” Jade sits down on the chair which sits next to the desk. A whole batch of emotions running through her head. A whole lot of emotions. And now this would happen. CLICK! “Oh you have got to be kidding me!” Jade hissed the shotgun ready to fire. [-TO BE CONTINUED-]
