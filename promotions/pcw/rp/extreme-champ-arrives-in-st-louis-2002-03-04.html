Extreme Champ Arrives in St. Louis

4 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/4/02 The helicopter carrying Warden Riley, and the Swamp Dawg arrives in St. Louis. The chopper is met by several armed prison guards as well as the mayor of the city. A TV reporter sticks a camera in Warden Riley's face as he steps out of the helicopter. Reporter #1: Welcome to St. Louis... can we talk to your wrestler? Warden: My wrestler? He's not a damn pet... he's the PCW Extreme Champ. Show some respect. Reporter #1: What's his name? Warden: Who the hell are you? Have you been living in a damn cave the past month? This is the most prized prospect in PCW for chrissakes! Reporter #1: Sorry, I don't really follow pro wrestling, I'm just interested in this story because I know this guy's a convicted murderer. I can't believe they let him wrestle in public. Warden: Well, under my watch, the only person in danger is the opponent in the ring. The fans have nothing to fear. But you do, if you don't get out of my way! Reporter: So what exactly are you all doing in St. Louis? Warden: We have a huge match scheduled for Monday night. LeBlanc makes his PPV debut and he'll defend his title for the first time. Reporter: Who's the unlucky opponent? Warden: Some woman named Jason Core. Reporter: A woman named Jason? Warden: That's what I said. And she's a dumb bitch too. I heard she went to some frat party to relax and take her mind of this title match with LeBlanc. Bad move. What do you think LeBlanc was doing while J-Core was out partying with kids half her age? He was training his ass off! He's primed for his first title defense and he's ready to end Jason Core's career! After the PPV, Jason Core can quit writing those dumbass letters to his dead wife Jenna, and when we're finished with him... he can join her face-to-face in hell! At this point in The Warden's tirade, the reporter has already turned off his camera and is slowly backing away. Warden: That's what I thought! Now get the hell out of our way! We have a title match to prepare for. Scene fades as Warden Riley and LeBlanc pile into the police car and drive off towards the police department where LeBlanc will be held until it's time to leave for the arena.
