Not Ya Average Bank Trip (Part 2)

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.10.02 20 Minutes after they sped away from the bank, Duzza and Pookie head onto the highway that leads out of NYC and into the outer city places. Pookie: “Yo dawg, how you think yo match gon’ go?” Duzza: “Wit Makonie?” Pookie: “Yeah, that nazi” Duzza: “I’ma kick his white, racist ass all over the mother fucking ring! Believe that” Pookie: “Shit, I ain’t believed anything you said since you told me that Micheal Jackson used to be black” Duzza: “What? He did used to be black!” Pookie: “Yeah… keep believing that…” Duzza: “You do my head in. I’ma turn on the radio” Radio: “And news just in from NYC. There was a bank robbery on 51st Street. Two African Americans were seen fleeing from the scene, arguing, carrying a black bag and they fled the scene in a black low rider type car, perhaps a Cadillac, heading north out of NYC… Police say they are on the tail of the robbers, but anyone with information about the robbery or the robbers would be appreciated. It appears the security camera’s in the bank were not working… and in other news…” Pookie: “Ha, a brother could get the impression they was talkin’ bout us!” Duzza: “Err dawg… they was” Just then a police helicopter flies over Duzza’s low rider. Duzza and Pookie both look at each other, with a mixture of shock and fear. Duzza then looks behind them and in the distance he can see a lot of flashing blue lights. Duzza: “Aww shit… exit, where’s the exit” Duzza quickly swerves the car down a tiny exit, while the helicopter hovers over the car. The exit leads to a tiny suburb with a small wooded section. Duzza drives the low rider into the woodland, driving over rough terrain and a few logs. After hitting a big rock, the low rider just dies Duzza: “Whyyyyyyyy” Duzza and Pookie get out of the car, while they can still hear the helicopter hovering just above the tree’s, but they cannot see it. Duzza: “Look at my car! Look at my rims! Awww man” Pookie: “Shit dawg, what we gon’ do. I’m too young to go to jail… again” Duzza: “We ain’t gonna go to jail! We ain’t done shit wrong!” Pookie: “So! You think they care! Awww we gon’ do time!” Duzza: “No we ain’t gonna go to jail… a’ight, let me think of a plan” Pookie: “We ain’t got that long!” Duzza: “Was that a dis!?” Pookie: “Who cares?? We about to catch a beatdown like Rodney King!” Duzza: “A’ight, I got a plan dawg, check this. You hide in the trunk and I’ll run and hide. Then I’ll come back in a hour or two and open the trunk, and we’ll drive home” Pookie looks at Duzza like he’s mad Pookie: “Did you honestly think I was going to go along with that plan?” Duzza: “Yes… that’s the sad part” Just then, a few squad cars can be seen pulling into the entrance of the wooded area Duzza: “You got any better plans?” Pookie: “Nope” Duzza: “Then get the fuck in the trunk!” Duzza then, using his strength picks up a protesting Pookie and pushes him to the trunk, he opens the trunk and throws Pookie in Duzza: Sorry dawg…” Duzza then slams the trunk down. Just then, he hears the police pull up behind him Police Officer: “Freeze!” Duzza: “Err… no!” Just then, Duzza sprints through the woods, ducking through the trees and swinging through branches as if he was the black Tarzan. With the police, getting further and further behind him, but still hollering at him. Suddenly Duzza runs through a big bush and finds himself out of the wood and into the suburb area… Duzza: “Errr… where to…errr” Just then he hears the police coming out of the bush, so he starts running again. He jumps over a chain link fence and through someone’s back yard, he then runs across the street, before seeing a wall about 6 feet high. Duzza see’s this as the perfect escape, thinking that he could hide on the other side of the wall. Duzza, using all his strength and natural ability, dives at the wall and using his arms, pushes himself over it. Unfortunately for Duzza, there is nothing on the other side of the wall apart from a 10 foot drop. Duzza crashes to the floor and lands awkwardly. Duzza: “ARGH! FUCK FUCK, ARGH” The police can no doubt hear Duzza, hollering away and after a few failed attempts they manage to get over the wall and to Duzza. Three police officers come over to Duzza. They roll him over and cuff him Duzza: “What! I didn’t do anything!” Officer: “Then why was you running from us?” Duzza: “I wasn’t! I was just going out for my daily jog” Officer: “Uh huh… so how do you explain, slamming that guy in the trunk, speeding away from the highway, and jumping over a wall with a 10 foot drop on the other side?” Duzza: “Errr… I’m high” Officer: “Get this thug out of my site” Two more police officers pick Duzza up and lead him away in cuffs as the scene fades to black. How will Duzza get out of this one? Will he get out of this one? Who knows…
