Ragamuffin's Revenge - Finale - Broken

22 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 22, 2002 [This is it. This is the night that 'Totally Tubular' Tommy finally gets exactly what he has coming to him. He tried to poison an innocent woman, take a sacred thing away from her just because he wanted to. Now he's trying to take another innocent woman from a good man, The End. He's trying to make Sandy Wong Harry's even though she doesn't want to be. These are people, human lives that Tommy is trying to play God with. Maybe that's what he wants, to be God. Because that's really the only thing he's never really truly experienced. It seems like it's the only thing in life that he hasn't truly conquered yet. Could it be true? That Tommy is somehow trying to make himself some sort of dumbass God? If it is true, somehow, then this world could really be in trouble. Ragamuffin is hiding in the darkness just feet away from Tommy's beach house. The police uniform a little tight in his crotch area, but that's nothing compared to the horrible manure smell that comes from him. He can see inside the side window and every single light in the beach house is on. The surfer wasting precious electricity all the while there are people who have to use candles as sources of light. Such a wasteful human being. Ragamuffin watches as Tommy crosses from the living room into the kitchen, obviously ready to fix some kind of snack before he goes to sleep. This is exactly when Ragamuffin will make his strike, send Tommy to the place where he belongs...HELL!] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: MAKIN' PEANUT BUTTER AND JELLY!!! [Tommy's odd voice carries through the home with a pretty good tone in his voice. If the angelic vocals of Tommy were accompanied by a nice guitar and good bass line, Tommy could make good money off a peant butter and jelly song. In fact, Tommy knows that he could do it if he put his mind to it, but music isn't his thing. That's what Harry does best with his Twisted Sister cover band, which still only consists of 'Heavy Metal' Harry. Harry sits in the living room watching Rumble In The Bronx, still trying to figure out if Jackie Chan is really a cyborg created by the Japanese] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Tommy... TTT: Yeah, Har? Harry: Do you think I could kick Jackie Chan's ass, man? TTT: Of course you could, dude. Harry: Far out, man. [Tommy goes back to spreading the Peter Pan Peanut Butter onto his bread, ready to throw on some grape Smuckers jelly on top, making it complete. His smile is so bright because he knows that in 24 hours he will destroy The End, take Sandy Wong, and hold the Continental Title across his totally bodacious waist. He got to tell off Shailyn and let her know the truth once and for all. He felt so bodacious to break the news to her and to see her face just buckle with sadness. If only he had a camera he could have made that a totally gnarly Kodak moment] TTT: Peanut butter and jelly, goin' to my tummy. Bodacious snack, going for the attack. I'm Tubular in a far out way, The End is so totally gay! [Tommy takes his sandwich over to the couch and sits down next to his brother to enjoy the bodaciousness of Rumble In The Bronx. Jackie Chan can do some gnarly stuff, he's probably a cyborg made by the Japanese though. The man hasn't aged in such a long time. It only makes sense, dudes and dudettes] [Outside Ragamuffin wipes the sweat from his brow. He has been outside for hours now, waiting for the perfect time to strike the two men. They were stupid enough to let down their police escorts because of the hot pursuit that Ragamuffin was under. Nobody ever thought he would be able to make it here, but he did. They underestimated him and that's going to be such a shame because Tommy is about to die. Tommy is about to face the Grim Reaper. A Grim Reaper named RAGAMUFFIN!] [The phone rings....] TTT: Hello? DUDE!! What's going on? Everything is going bodacious over here. You saw Fusion? Didn't I do a most stellar job on The End? I KNOW! Did you see her crying like a totallly heinous baby? Harry is going to be most pleased with her over Decimation. We already rented out the hotel room and everything. Hotel California? Big party? YOU BET, COMPADRE! After that bogus head on crash you suffered a few weeks back, your going to need an excellent party to bring your spirits up. Later, dude. [Ragamuffin rolls his eyes as he must have been on the phone with Greg, another surfer who thinks he's God. Just when he thought Tommy was drawing the line between intelligence and retardedness he met Greg...a primate if ever seen. The man thinks with his other head and thinks that steaks can turn back into cows. Ragamuffin watches, waiting for that moment. Tommy laughing and giggling like a little school girl at some stupid action movie. It's only making Randy's rage grow deep inside, making him want to leap through the window and end it right away. He needs to be stealth, to be careful about it. Shai confessed that she was in love with him, and it's the best news that he's ever heard in his entire life. Shailyn Matthews is ACTUALLY in love with Ragamuffin. He knows he can't go back to that mental institution now, and that what he does to Tommy must be done quietly. Then he and Shai can get out of the States and have a lovely life together down in Mexico. What a way to start off a new life, by murdering 'Totally Tubular' Tommy] [The phone rings once again...] TTT: Hey, dude! Yeah, everything is going bodacious then. No, I'm not worried about Ragamuffin. That dude is heinous and just plain out sucks. Come on, Brad, it's not even like he could harm me. I'd snap his bogus twig neck in two seconds flat and you know that. Harry could whip his ass with one hand tied behind his back, and Harry is recovering from a broken BACK! It's not even an issue. No, I'm not nervous about Sunday. Why would I be? The End is blind and bogus, he'll be absolutely no problem. The rest of those bogus compadres don't even size up to me. Rush? Chet King? Dante Jackson? Snow? They're all freakin' heinous in every sense. I know I could face fines for what I did, I have no problem with throwing around a little radical dough to hurt The End. What do you mean he didn't do anything wrong?! Don't tell me that your turning bogus on me now! ARGH! [Tommy turns off his cell phone and focuses back on to Jackie Chan whipping ass] [Ragamuffin has had enough. Tommy can not beat him! He crashes through the window getting shards of glass stabbed into his arms and sides, but he doesn't feel the pain. The rage carries him over to Tommy, who sits on the couch in total shock. Harry doesn't budge at all, just focusing his mind on Rumble In The Bronx, trying to figure out how the Japanese could make such a fine cyborg. That maybe HE TOO can have a cyborg of his own to do some ass kicking for him] Ragamuffin: I WILL KILL YOU!!! [Tommy jumps off the couch and trots into the kitchen. Randy grabs a butcher knife from the counter and stares into Tommy's fearful blue eyes. He can feel the kill coming already. The pure rush of adrenaline already coursing through his veins. He can't wait to feel the knife drive into Tommy's body and watch him squirm to death on the floor, his brother not even sure of what's going on...HE'S TOO STONED!] TTT: Please! DUDE!! Don't kill me. There is no need to kill me, Rand. Your a totally bodacious guy and I've always known that from the beginning. It was always a joke, and I never expected that NAME to carry as far as it did! I'm begging you totally righteous compadre, DON'T KILL ME! [Ragamuffin can only laugh to himself, loving that he's begging for his life. This is going to make the kill feel so much better now] Ragamuffin: Too late, Tom. It's time to die. [As Randy is about to slay his most hated enemy the sliding doors whip open, and there stands the woman that he loves, Shailyn Matthews] Ragamuffin: SHAI!! TTT: SHAI?! Shailyn: Randy.... [She approaches her true love, the man who is going to give her that happiness. She knees him in the crotch] Ragamuffin: OOOF!! Shailyn: Did you really think that a good Christian girl like me could ever love a sadistic person like you? [The police follow in and take Ragamuffin into custody, reading him his rights, and he will soon be returned to the mental institution to be placed under tighter lock and key. His face shows pure pain, but his eyes show hurt. Once again he was tricked and once again he was made a fool of. Just when he had a shred of hope it was ripped away from him in one fail swoop. Give the child a brand new toy and then take it from him after only an hour. That is what it feels like. Tears roll down his cheeks as Shailyn kisses Tommy on the lips. Both of them smiling in what they have done to him. Damn them...THEY WILL PAY!] TTT: You did a bodacious job, babe. Shailyn: Thanks, babe. I knew that staging that fight and staging my feelings to him that he'd be putty in our hands. Now that we have HIM out of the way...how are you going to handle The End? [Tommy shrugs] TTT: I believe that I already handled that totally heinous compadre already. All I have to do is by my bodacious self in the match and it'll be smooth sailing all the way to the COntinental title. Shai: I love you, Tommy. TTT: I know you do, babe. [They kiss as Ragamuffin is stuffed into the back of the police cruiser, watching the entire time. Adding to his madness, making him fall deeper into that dark place in the soul. Now...he truly has NOTHING left] The End
