Food TV Wants Me!?!?

28 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/28/02 The camera fades in… Slowly moving down a hallway backstage, a Food Network camera approaches the locker room of the recently victorious Culinary Conquistador. In front of the camera jumps the Food Network’s roving reporter, and host of “Unwrapped” Marc Summers. Summers viciously pounds at the locker room door, hoping to get an interview with The Sultan of Sautéing. Summers: Cheffy baby, are you in there!?!? The door slowly opens, and the Chef peers out of the opening, glancing at Summers, who is smiling like an idiot. The Chef: If you came for the dumplings, they’ll be done in twenty minutes… As the Chef begins to close the door, Summers pleads for his attention. Summers: No! I just wanted to interview you for a spot on my show! The Chef: Is this show on TV? Summer: Yeah, cable… The Chef: CABLE!?!? Why didn’t you say so!?!? I’ll be right out, just let me grab my pants!!! “F*cko!?!? Where the f*ck are you going!?!?” “Quiet B, there’s a man out there that WANTS to interview me! Do you know how many times I’ve been stood up for an interview by that Boobie Boy!?!? I need this!!!” “But f*cking what about me, f*ck head???” “Can’t you pleasure yourself for once!?!?” “I f*cking can’t anymore, holding up my f*cking countless c*ck-@$$ed rolls of f*cking flab takes f*cking two mother f*ckers!!!” Summers and his crew begin to hold their stomachs, the walk away and try to block out the disgusting comments being made inside the locker room. Suddenly the door opens, and the Chef comes out with a huge smile on his face. “F*cko!!!” SLAM!!! The Chef shuts the door behind him and walks over to Summers. The Chef is dressed in his normal attire, with bandages placed on his hands, and on one knee. The Chef: Sorry I look like a piece of day-old tenderized filet mignon. I was just in a gauntlet match, which I WON!!! Summers nods. Summer: That’s great Chef! The Food Network is proud that you are Pro-America again and Pro-@$$ sautéing! The Chef: Well I’ve always been pro-@$$ sautéing, it’s just what I do… Summers nod again, probably ignoring every word the Culinary Conquistador has to say. The Chef: Hey, weren’t you the guy that hosted “Double Dare”!?!? Summer smiles smugly. Summers: Why yes…and “What Would You Do?” as well… The Chef: Do you have that giant ear with you!?!? I’ve always wanted to crawl into that… Summer: Ummm, no… The Chef: Damn!!! Summers laughs, probably wondering why he chose this interview in the first place. Summers: Well Chef, I’ve been told by my superiors that you are being watched, you see one of our chef’s is going to retire…well he was forced to after an unfortunate accident at his home, a terrible explosion (see previous promo). The Chef: I see… Summers: If you keep impressing my superiors, a TV gig is definitely in the cards… The Chef: That’s great! I’ve always wanted to do a TV show, again… Do they want me to do anything specific!?!? Summers: They were very impressed with the way you handled the UAE… The Chef: HA HA!!! BUT OF COURSE!!! I’m still getting praises back here in the locker room for that…but the idea for the fondue pot was Bertha’s. Summers: (Ignoring the Chef’s confession) And that lobster in the pants was pure gold! The Chef: Actually I slipped a lobster in Al Gore’s pants during the 2000 Presidential Campaign; I formed a new party with Ross Perot, but only won fourteen states…I probably would have won if that idiot Perot hadn’t accused the Republicans of crashing his Super Bowl Party… If anyone crashed that party it would have been no good teens, or the Kennedy family… Summers nods. The Chef: You could say I’ve used my food in very peculiar ways, but I consider my keen culinary intellect a gift from Al…I mean God…hehe… Summers: Are there any challenges in the future that you are unsure about? The Chef: Unsure!?!? That’s poppycock!!! If there’s one thing about me anyone in PCW or on Food TV should know, I’m quite a confident fellow… I’ve predicted victory before every single one of my matches, because it’s no use being a pessimist. Pessimism is a creation of those culinary school drop out philosophers, I know of no such thing. When it comes right down to it, I sauté @$$, and then take names, unless if I’m already familiar with the person then it’s like, “Hey you, I just sautéed your @$$! Want to try out my newest soufflé?” I’m always a friendly winner, and a gracious loser. Summers just nods and pretends to acknowledge the Chef’s right to speak as the Culinary Conquistador rambles on. Summers thinks to himself, “You know, now I realize why he has Bertha in his promos. It’s because without her, they’d be a load of crap!” Summers: That’s great Chef, any new recipes to share with the home audience? The Chef: Ah yes! Since I’ve just done some major @$$ sautéing to the UAE I think it’s time to move on to a winter treat. You see, in two days I will be facing off against Snow, a multi-time world champion, and a self-proclaimed orgasm instigator. I think his sautéed rump would be fine by itself, not in a Jeremy Johnson way or anything like that, but for dessert how about a French SNOW Cone? Summers: A snow cone, isn’t that…amusement food? The Chef: What one must realize Mr. Summers, that when one cooks, he is catering to the masses, not some snot-nosed punk-@$$ed little Mexican-Asian kid that always wants to kick you in the shins and lives in a freaking gym bag!!! But domestic problems aside, Snow Cones can be quite the keen cuisine, if you know what I mean… Summers: Explain… The Chef: A French Snow Cone is a tasty treat loaded with calories, but also with… Summers: Love? The Chef: Nah, too expensive, actually it’s more calories… Summers: I see… The Chef: Take some crushed ice, and add a bunch of butter, real stuff now, not that half-@$$ed Parkay or anything like that… Then add some vanilla, along with some half and half, a few sprinkles of cinnamon, a cherry, chocolate syrup, cracked nuts, crumbled up cookies, strawberry syrup, more butter, even more butter, yet even more butter, some cream, more vanilla, yes even more butter, and…butter…and…nutmeg… Summer: That sounds quite complex for a Snow Cone… The Chef: Yes, but when Bertha works up a sweat, they really cool her down… Summer: I…see… Well Chef, thanks for the interview, we will probably post this on FoodTV.com and my superiors will be looking into adding you in the place of the departed… The Chef: Man in question, right? The Chef smile at Summers, who instantly realizes the Chef knows who is leaving. Summers walks away with the TV crew. Bertha peeks her head out of the door. Bertha: Did it f*cking go OK, Lord F*ckington? The Chef: Bertha, you may be looking at the next host of a live food related television program!!! Bertha gives the Chef an angry look. Bertha: You f*cking passed up d*ck to f*cking @$$ sex for that!!! Bertha then SLAMS the door shut. The Chef stands in the hallway shaking his head as the camera fades to black.
