This Week is the Week

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-7-02 [Fade In] [Light, bright light peering down from the heavens. The sun’s rays crashing into the glass roof above, causing a blinding sight if you glanced up towards it for very long. The glass roof is there, so that it will give the affect of being outside to the recreational area below. Nested in a very large area of linoleum tile below are a swimming pool, hot tub, shuffleboard platform, and a small playground type area for younger kids. It’s the middle of the afternoon, and the area is bustling with guests of the Holiday Inn.] [The shot focuses in on the hot tub area where Ryan “Streak” Stephens sits next to his wife Dana. Across from them is another couple, even younger than Stephens and his wife, and set up directly behind them is a tripod. Gathered around the tripod are three men all wearing khaki shorts and blue collared shirts, featuring PCW staff embroidered in white right above the right, and only pocket of the shirt. It seems as though Stephens is about to cut a promo for his upcoming matches against Stryfe and Anna Keyson.] [A closer look at Stephens reveals just his massive chest being attacked by thousands of bubbles caused by the hot-tub’s blow jets. His left arm is around his wife, who is wearing an orange bikini top. Her hair his carefully tossed back in a bun in a valiant attempt to keep it moderately dry. Stephens seems very relaxed, as does she. Stephens is zoned out, staring into an oblivion of nothing, while Dana watches the camera crew prepare all their gizmos and gadgets. Finally, she breaks Ryan's concentration, by speaking to him.] Dana: Are you feeling better? Streak: [He nods, and smiles] Yes baby, I’m feeling a lot better. I really am sorry about all that. You know I appreciate the fact that you put up with me so well baby. You should’ve have just left me there. Dana: It’s okay baby. We’ve gone over this a couple of times now. I’m not mad anymore. It just freaked me out, and frustrated me beyond no end. You never drink, and you never spout off like that. I didn’t know what to do. [Stephens runs a wet hand through his reasonably dry hair, and makes it stick up a little, before immersing his hand back into the warm water.] Streak: Hopefully this promo will get everything straight for anyone who saw that. Dana: Do you want me to get out of the shot, while you’re talking? Streak: Nah, just stay here for support baby. You support me every day, giving me advice, helping me prepare, so I don’t see any reason why you can’t sit here during my promo. [He hugs her a little tighter with his left arm, and smiles, before relaxing again. The cameramen have everything about ready to go, and Stephens and his wife advert their attention from one another back towards the camera perched atop the tripod.] Camera Man: Are you about ready Mr. Stephens? Streak: [Without hesitation] Ready when you are man. Camera Man: On three then. [One of the ‘main’ cameraman’s helpers adjusts the camera just right, and then the ‘main’ cameraman counts down from three with his fingers. THREE, TWO, ONE! The little red light comes on and Stephens begins talking.] Streak: Hello fans of PCW! I have decided to cut this promo hear today on account of my recent actions in a Canadian bar called Shenanigans. Needless to say, the shenanigans I displayed the other night were very disrespectful to both my opponents, my fans if I have any, PCW, and most important my wife. For those of you who have not heard or seen what went on, I’m going to go ahead and tell you on account of I’m am honest man, who can admit when he’s made an honest mistake. [Stephens pauses for a moment and then continues on.] Streak: Sunday night I was really down on myself, and was worrying once again about impressing all of you wonderful fans in my matches this week. I was worried about proving myself to the critics that doubt me, and most of all I was worried that if I didn’t get avidly prepared and prevail that it would crush my spirits and send me on another grueling duel with myself. I was scared that a loss might set the tone for another year plagued by inconsistency, and self-doubt, and I can’t afford that. Not during this challenge I’ve set for myself. Anyhow I drank away all those doubts and fears, and in turn, made a complete ass of myself, and embarrassed myself, my wife, and most likely PCW itself. Streak: The fact of the matter is, that’s what happened and I can’t turn back the hands of time to change my actions so they’ve become actions I am prepared to accept the consequences for. If you all want to boo me this week go right ahead, and if you don’t want your kids to watch me, I understand; I wouldn’t either, but for what it’s worth let me say this… It won’t happen again. I’ve had a few long discussions with my wife… [Stephens looks at her and smiles. She smiles back and whispers for him to keep talking.] Streak: Anyhow, I came to the conclusion that it doesn’t matter if I win or lose this week. Regardless of the outcome, I’m going to lay it all on the line, I’m going to unleash the fire from within, and I’m going to earn your respect back, I’m going to be the role model I’ve always been. You see I’ve always walked the path of role model, and it’s not always easy. Let’s be honest, it’s the role model who is often the victim of brutal sneak attacks, it’s the role model who is always picking their ass up off the ground. [He smiles, almost a sneer] It’s not a path for the weak of heart; however, it is THE path for Ryan “Streak” Stephens, and I’m ashamed I strayed from it Sunday night. [Stephens pauses to get a breath. He proceeds to quickly pop the knuckles on each hand before continuing yet again.] Streak: I’d like to issue an apology to my opponents this week, both Stryfe and Anna Keyson. Quite frankly some of the things I said Sunday night, under the influence mind you, were down right disrespectful and cruel, and while I don’t have to agree with either of you, or your lifestyles, some of the things I said, didn’t need to be said. That being said, there are something that I would like to say to the both of you, starting with my Turmoil opponent Stryfe. Streak: Stryfe, you’ve abused substances, you’ve abused your mind, and on Turmoil I’m going to abuse you. You took the road if you’ve taken, the lifestyle of drugs and what not, because that road was easier, that path didn’t involve heart, determination, and pride, things I understand and possess. And because you took that lifestyle, regardless of what you’ve accomplished in your career, you Stryfe will forever be a coward, someone who didn’t have the rocks to walk the straight path, to uphold justice, truth, and be a role model. You chose to be the way you are, because of fear, well fear me Stryfe, because the fire is burning within. It’s like hot lava just pulsating within my veins waiting, for the moment the bell dings, and it erupts all over you and your way of life. You sold your soul Stryfe, you bent over and grabbed your ankles, and come Turmoil, I’m going to dismantle you because of it. [Stephens has become tense, getting worked up over all his talk about his upcoming match. His piercing blue eyes seem as though they could cut a hole through iron right now, and his chiseled cheekbones flare in anticipation of a battle. He begins to address his other opponent this week, Anna Keyson.] Streak: Oh Anna, I feel sorry for you, for you are a poor soul. Unlike Stryfe maybe you didn’t choose to stray from the right path, perhaps you were thrown against your will from the right path, with all the awful things that have occurred to you in your lifetime, but it’s time to rejoin the few of us who still care Anna. And if you neglect to do so Anna, [he chuckles] well even if you do, I’m going to give you the battle of your life come Friday. You were given a gift Anna, and like me you have the fire that burns within, you know what it feels like. [Stephens pauses, as if recollecting his past.] Streak: I was once a young fiery rookie, who had an uncontrollable fire within me, as you do now Anna; however, within the course of a year I learned to tame that fire, and it was perhaps the greatest single thing I’ve ever done in my career as far as something that has helped me win matches. It’s all in here Anna. [Stephens taps his bare chest where his heart would lie, if it was exposed.] Streak: There is a journey Anna. A journey that bring about extraordinary change. Change that takes place within. A personal transformation that turns fear into courage. Doubt into confidence. A transformation that hones your mind to razor sharp perfection. This journey takes a long while, but the change is forever. Streak:I was born into an inferno that tests both mind and body. I am one of the few who has managed to complete the challenge. I have become a beacon of courage, honor and commitment. I believe that pain is weakness leaving the body. The question isn’t how much more I can take, but just how much more I can give. Just when I’m ready to quit my mind says push harder, I listen, sensing an inner strength that wasn’t there before, and suddenly I discover I no longer feel the pain. And THAT Anna, is when you know you’ve harnessed the fire! [Stephens sighs to catch his breath, and once again runs his hand through his hair, this time dampening it even more.] Streak: Harness you fire Anna, make the conversion back onto the path of the ‘good guy.’ Streak: This week is the week. It is the week that I prove to all of PCW that I am the real deal, and that I’m capable of running rampart through PCW, all the while standing for what is right. And if by some chance, I falter, and I lose, that’s okay too because unlike many of the PCW competitors I can still hold my head up with pride. I can hold up head up in knowing that this cold, hard, cruel world was unable to tempt me, it was unable to corrupt me, and I’m still, and always will be true! [And with that Stephens signals for the end of the promo. He hugs his wife tight, and kisses her gently on the cheek as the cameramen congratulate him on a nice promo. We fade to black.] [It seems as though Stephens is back to his old self. He’s erased all the doubt, and fear he let slip into his mind, and in cutting this promo he is sure to gain some respect for being an honest man, and a true competitor. Stephens is one of the few role models still out there in the world of corrupt, and greed that we call wrestling. His fire burns strong, and his heart is big, to prevail over Stephens when he’s not playing mind games with himself is truly an accomplishment.]
