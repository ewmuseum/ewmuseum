Frisco FEARS Sylo pt. 1

5 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-5-02 Frisco fears SYLO! pt. 1 The streets of an older German city were packed shoulder to shoulder it seemed. Buildings sat just as close; obviously these were buildings around the time of the first world war and managed to survive the second. Most looked poor with their rag clothing and old gray hats that the men wore and old nylon scarf's that the women wore tied securely under their chins. It felt as though he had stepped back through time. Of course standing at 7'2' he stood out like a bright red, soar thumb on a cold winter day. He moved through the crowed, some stared, others decided to look away. His forehead was covered with dry blood which plastered to him like some child's finger painting. He needed a place to clean, a place to change, but where? He had no money as of right now and surly no one would take in someone that looked, well had been hit by an airplane. So what now? Did he just wonder the streets until he found a stream or fountain? He had no clue. With no money nor any means of transportation home he was pretty much stuck here. Now the one known only now as Sylo strolled the crowded German streets until a voice echoed into his ear. 'Are you Sylo?' A voice so brittle it sounded like it would break flowed to him. He turned heaving his massive shoulders to the side and directing his icy blue stare down onto a smaller old woman. She smiled with her eyes half way shut, wrinkles covered her old face and she had seen better days you could tell. She seemed more like a marshmallow with toothpicks stuck in than a person. 'Yes,' He replied with his deep, strong voice. 'I owe you so much. You are the man who saved my grandson from death,' Her thick German accent made it hard for her to pronounce some of the words but all in all she did a good job. 'It was nothing....' He replied forgetting about his horrible appearance. 'Oh my, you are covered in blood. Please come with me and I will get you cleaned up,' 'I couldn't impose,' he said looking back. 'Oh please, it is the least I can do,' Reluctantly he agreed. The two walked, conversed, and made small talk until arriving at a smaller building. He had to duck to get in but was thankful to get a hot shower. It turned out her husband who had passed away wore the 'wife beater' type tank tops and they found one that would actually fit across the massive Sylo. The woman even went as far as to sew up patches on his pants making them like brand new. He didn't know how to thank her but yet she was thanking him for saving his grandson. That's when a knock came on the old wooden door of the cabin. The old lady quickly stood from her old wooden rockin' chair and went to the door turning her head to Sylo. 'I invited my grandson, he wanted to say thank you personally,' She smiled. 'That's fine....but I really must get back to the states....' He replied knowing he was running out of time. She slowly opened the door with a creak. A small boy stood with his father looming over head, hands on the boys shoulders. Both were thing and the father was balding as where the child had short but thick, brown hair. They both held a smile on their face as they stared into the cabin. 'Come in! Come in!' The old lady said in her highest voice which still wasn't high. The child slowly walked in followed by his father as Sylo stood. The two jumped back a small bit not realizing how tall Sylo really was. He held his emotionless look on his face as the two entered and the father extended his hand. Sylo looked down then back up and shook it; trying not to add to much pressure. 'Thank you Mr. Sylo! Thank you for saving my child!' The man cried out almost to tears. 'It was really nothing,' Sylo replied letting go of the man's cold hand. The small boy approached as Sylo looked down at him. 'Thank you Mr. Sylo,' The boy cried out hugging Sylo's leg. His instinct was to attack but something came over him; could it be he was actually gaining compassion? He moved a hand down onto the boys shoulder and simply patted it until the child let go of his grip. The boy smiled and Sylo let a small smile move across his lips. Martha, the old lady, announced that dinner was ready which broke the awkward feeling the room now held. The four sat down and ate a home cooked meal and afterwards the three adults sat at the table as the child went and watched the old t.v in the corner. 'Truth is I need to get back to America....well really Canada so I can wipe the floor with that piece of dictating crap Frisco,' Sylo's voice was low but still strong as he spoke about Frisco. 'Ah my friend, I have a friend in the aviation business. They are closed now but I can get you on a plane back first thing tomorrow I believe,' The father of the boy who's name is Vincent says in his German accent while patting his stomach after a great meal. 'It would be appreciated,' Sylo said staring down. 'It is the least I can do for you saving my child, but let me ask you my friend.....what is your burden?' 'Akira Akiyama. He took things to far, and now I must destroy him in his own playing field. The thoughts of his demise fill me and drive me making me want to destroy. He turned me into this monster and now his curse upon me will be inflicted upon him,' Sylo snarled as he finished. 'Be careful lad,' The old ladies soft, brittle voice echoed. 'I've seen the power that one has and let me tell you, it goes beyond reality. God or not he's dangerous,' From the background the sounds of the small boys voice could be heard mixed in with the intoxicating sound of television. He jumped from his seat and ran to the table with a flash of energy. 'Mr. Sylo! Mr. Sylo! Come quick that Frisco guy is on Television!' He spoke quickly, almost to hard to understand in his thick German accent. Sylo stood from the table and walked to the old Television set. He watched as Frisco's lackey spewed his drunken or stupid trash talk; whatever you wanted to blame it on. Sylo snarled and looked down at the small child then flipped the T.V set off. 'Don't listen to this stuff, it's just some washed up has-been who can't cut it any longer. I'll put him out of his misery soon enough,' Sylo snarled again. 'I know you will Mr. Sylo....and I know you will beat Akiyama as well!' The boy said with a smile. 'My only problem is getting home....hopefully that will be taken care of tomorrow....and remember Frisco fears Sylo.....always remember that kid,' Sylo said with a smirk. Time passed quickly and soon it was time for a peaceful night's sleep. Sylo was shown to an old room with a small bed and a chair in the corner with a lamp on the stand. Sylo thanked Martha again but she reminded him that she was always in his dept. And with that they parted ways letting Sylo quickly drift off into a sleep....but not before receiving a message from the Angel Tyros. 'Fear not for your path will be shown light. Your sight is blurry but soon it will be clear as the crystal waters of lord on high. God be with you always,' and with that Tyro's vanished.
