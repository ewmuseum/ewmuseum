The Right Path?

27 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-27-03 (OOC Note- Sorry this is short and pitiful. I haven’t had much computer time lately and I wanted to just throw something up. Sorry SyN for not giving you a better challenge.) The scene opens to Adam sitting in a small cramped hotel room. The TV is on but he isn’t really paying it any attention. He’s looking just past the TV and into the large mirror that is hanging behind it. He’s dressed in a gray Polo shirt and black pants. His hair has grown out a little and now hangs just slightly past his ears. It’s hanging loosely over his eyes. He runs his hands back through his hair and pulls it back out of his eyes. 'Where the fuck did I go wrong?' he says to himself, 'Everything seemed to be on the up and up. I was the Extreme Champion. But where the fuck did I go wrong?' Adam swipes the remote off of the bed and frustratingly clicks the TV off. He stands up and raises his arms over his head and stretches. Pulling his arms back to his side, he takes in a deep breath. 'I guess tomorrow will be my first step back on the right path.' Adam says exhaling. 'But what is that path? If I win tomorrow what does that get me? A shot at the Extreme Title? I don’t want that. I never really wanted that in the first place. It was basically given to me.' He starts to pace back and forth from one end of the small dresser to the other. His hands at his side. 'Maybe it would be better for me and my career to just do the job. Who the hell cares anyway? I’ve done it before and I will no doubt do it again.' He stops at the far end of the dresser and just stands there. 'I’m glad the Extreme Title is no longer around my waist, it was too much of a burden. But if I plan on moving up in the card…' Adam pulls his hand up to his chin. He strokes his chin slowly and softly. 'Then jobbing is the only way to go. I need to move on past the Extreme Division. It’s not good for me or my health. I can’t carry on the kinds of matches that are needed to be a good champion from that division. I need to look to the future.' Adam turns back and looks to the TV. Suddenly his face lights up. 'Of course! I need to make a play for the TV Title. I need to at least get myself into that division. I need to stop wasting my time in the Extreme division. I could be so much better utilized within that division.' Adam turns quickly and sits back down on the end of the bed. 'Of course Jaslene won’t like the idea of me just jobbing like this but I really have no choice. I don’t want the Extreme Title so there is no point in even trying in this match. Though I won’t let SyN off the hook that easily. I’ll still give him a fight but he doesn’t know that I’m going into this match to lose.' A big smile comes to Adam’s face as lays back on the bed.
