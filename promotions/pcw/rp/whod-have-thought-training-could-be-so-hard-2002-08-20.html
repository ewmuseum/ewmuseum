Who’d have thought training could be so hard?

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20TH of August 2002 DATE: 20TH of August 2002TIME: 8: 54 PMLOCATION: Norman, Oklahoma The Moon was already waxing by the time Pharaoh and the troops got to the training sight. It was more or less a large field, which, through many countless hours of strenuous hardship, had been converted into a sort of battleground. A thin stream cut the sight into cut halves, and on either side numerous obstacle courses and ditches had been situated, or in the case of the ditches, dug. Pharaoh led the men towards the stream and looked across the valley, an expression most fitting the description of ‘dreamy’ spread across his handsome mug. The Pharaoh: “Men, today we are gathered here in the noble art of learning. Now the Gods themselves know that I could have rented out something a bit better than THIS, but I aren’t spending THAT much on you guys! The field over in New Zealand that I checked out was almost six-hundr--… Anyway that isn’t relevant, what IS relevant is the fact that today you’re here to train!” The troops move uneasily, or at least the fatter, chunkier men of the 16-man strong battalion do. It’s mainly because most have already spotted the ropes meant for climbing that drape over a selection of tall logs. All in all it looks like the kind of exercise that grins smugly and says: “Hey, if you’re over 200 pounds of fat then you ain’t gonna be able to do this, bub.” And then, of course, there were the mats laid out near the shooting range…the mind of the chubby man could only wander when contemplating the possible acts that were meant to take place upon that certain spot… To their dread it was quite possible that Press-ups and Sit-ups were to be carried out. Even thinking about it made them shudder. The Pharaoh: “Now as you can see, it has taken much time and effort to set up this place for you, and so I’ll hope you all put as much effort into training as my clerks have into setting this all up. Now, however, I just want to see everyone trying their best and attempting to workout…Friday we’ll have a special assignment to carry out and it involves the enemy…” The men rumble at the mention of Frisco’s alias. The Pharaoh: “So get out there and show your commander your worth!!” At these words the battalion salute and rush off, many jumping the river and others spreading out to the other obstacles positioned about the same side of the field as Titu. Pharaoh smiles and nods as the men begin their training, but a gleam from bellow catches his eye. He looks down… The Pharaoh: “Oh my--…OH MY GODS! What the Duat happened here?!” A voice from between the river’s edges croaks: Troop: “Well…it’s dark, sir, and I didn’t quite manage the jump.” A nervous laugh follows. The Pharaoh: “Ah, geez. Look, can’t you wriggle free?” Troop: “I…I don’t think so, sir…” Even in the dark Pharaoh manages to make out the reddening of the fattish man’s little round face. The Pharaoh: “Ok, it’s going to take more than myself to heave you out. Are you sure you’re stuck tight?” Troop: “Quite so, sir.” Pharaoh raises his hands, cups his mouth and begins to shout. The Pharaoh: “SOMEONE GET OVER HERE, PRONTO!!” A young, lanky lad jogs over, salutes and straightens himself in the presence of his commander. Troop: “Yessir?” Pharaoh points to the plump solider stuck tightly between the tiny slopes of the river. The Pharaoh: “Get him out of there, will you? You may need some help, so just call over someone else to give you a hand or something.” The troop winces as he sees the struggling man sliding about between the two sides of the stream. With a look of concentration he stares at him and then at the river’s sides, he then gazes back up at Xansiv’s mug, a look of uncertainty flashed over his face. Troop: “Yes…sir…” The Pharaoh: “Good. You’ll find me over by the shooting range if you need anything.” The shooting range was nothing more than what it sounded, and it sure as hell sounded like a shooting range, too. Gunfire was all that could be heard as Pharaoh walked slowly towards the men - all of whom were testing their accuracy against a selection of large group of Frisco cardboard cut outs. As he grew closer he found a man lying on the floor, clenching at his chest. He’d been shot… Troop: “Sire, you…must…avenge my…death…” The Pharaoh: “Pull yourself together, troop! You’re not going to die!” Pharaoh kneels at the man’s side, the rest of the troops going about their business as though nothing out of place was happening. Troop: “You speak words that are…too…kind…but you can’t…deny the truth… You can’t beat the inevitable…” The man forces a weak smile. Troop: “I’m done…for…” Pharaoh sighs, shaking his head to and fro. The Pharaoh: “Don’t say such things! I know for a FACT that you aren’t going to pass away.” Troop: “Once again you speak…too…kindly, but your words are lies, sire. Defeat Frisco in…my…name, milord! I’m just sorry that I couldn’t be there at the big battle…” The man’s eyes close and his chest sags, arms sluggishly falling to his sides. Pharaoh rises to his feet and kicks him experimentally with the tip of his sandal. Troop: “Owww! What was that for?” The Pharaoh: “For faking your death, soldier! These bullets are blanks - every single one of them is a Godsdamn blank.” Hearing these words another member of the battalion walks towards the pair. Troop#2: “Sir, that’s Fake-It-Harry, you’ll just try to bear with him, I’m afraid. I don’t think it takes much to comprehend why he’s been dubbed with that nickname” Pharaoh’s brow furrows with realization. The Pharaoh: “Oh, right.” The second troop leans forward. Troop#2: “Just between you and I, sir, he’ll fake ANYTHING…just watch this…” The troop proceeds to kick Fake-It-Harry in the shin, the impact of the blow ringing out above the sounds of gunfire. Fake-It-Harry: “What the he--…WHAT THE HELL WAS THAT FOR?!” Ignoring F-I-H the troop leans back in Pharaoh’s direction. Troop#2: “See, sir! I told ya’ so…” Pharaoh nods slowly. The Pharaoh: “Yes I…see… Anyway, soldier, I must be going. Err… G-Good work, I…guess!” Pharaoh leaves the shooting range swiftly, the second troop waving him along with a loyal salute. Fake-It-Harry gets to his feet as another gunshot goes off. He hurls himself to the floor, gripping his face. Fake-It-Harry: “I’ve been bloody shot!! Man down, man down!” The troop rolls on the floor in ‘agony’ as his comrade sighs and looks down at him blankly. Troop#2: “Harry, if you’d been shot in the head you’d be dead, Ok?” F-I-H blushes through embarrassment. Fake-It-Harry: “I guess you’ve got a point.” Pharaoh quickly moves on past the shooting range and heads straight for one of the four obstacle courses. This one is surrounded by a wide selection of blue mats; the types one would be seen performing sit-ups and other light exercises upon. Currently, however, a bright glow fills the darkness ahead and a song in the middle of being sung echoes throughout the area. Pharaoh approaches, not sure whether to go cautiously or angrily. The Pharaoh: “What in the name of Horus is going on here?” Troop: “Err…well you’ve sort of caught us of guard, sir--…” The Pharaoh: “I can bloody well see that, solider! Now tell me what’s going on, immediately!” The man winces at the tone of his commander’s voice. The others surrounding the area begin to look at each other nervously. The fire still burns brightly, illuminating the obstacle course. Troop: “Well, s-sir, w-w-we’re having a… B-B-BQ…” He raises his hands to his face in preparation to be hit over the head by the enraged Pharaoh, but instead his boss simply sighs. Most of the men sat around the BBQ are the chubbier of the sixteen, and all seem to be in the middle of eating a burger. The troop sees the shock and disgust in Titu’s face and points to another of the men… Troop: “It was Chubby Charlie and Fat Fred’s idea, sir! They said, “Pete, don’t tell Pharaoh about this ‘cos he’ll go bleedin’ mad, he will!”!!” He salutes nervously as two other troops get to their feet. Troop#2: “What do you mean it was our idea?!” Troop: “Well it was!” Troop#3: “The hell it wa’! It was your idea, Pete!!” Pharaoh shakes his head in disgust. Why couldn’t they just do some press-ups?! I mean they weren’t that fat! He raised an eyebrow as a thought occurred. The Pharaoh: “Look, I don’t care whose idea it was, you’re all going to be punished as I see fit, anyway. All of you, every single person sat around this…this disgraceful display is going to do 50 press-ups, and nobody goes home ‘till they have!” The troops spring up to their feet in disapproval. The Pharaoh: “If you want to blame anyone then blame one of these three. One of them, at least, had something to do with this travesty! And it is because of this slack exhibit that you are being punished! So blame one of them!” The small crowd of men turn their attention to the ones referred to as ‘Pete’, ‘Fat Fred’ and ‘Chubby Charlie’. Pharaoh smiles as the men grumble with one another before they strenuously begin to perform the press-ups. The Pharaoh: “That’s only three, troop.” The man looks up wearily. Troop: “But…*Breathes*…Sir…*Breathes*…I don’t…*Breathes*…think I can…” The man’s arms finally give way and he falls to the mat, his face taking most of the impact. Moment pass before he says anything again, all the while his breathing rate grows heavy until eventually it begins to subside back to normal. The Pharaoh: “Troop, get back to work.” Troop: “But, sir--…” The Pharaoh: “There are no ‘buts’ in this battalion.” A giggle fills Titu’s ears. He looks around in a hawk-like fashion before matching it to a small, fat face; sweat dripping from every pour the rest of it’s body had to offer. The Pharaoh: “Do you find something funny, boy?” Troop: “No, sir. Why ever would I, sir?” Pharaoh watches him sternly for a second. The Pharaoh: “Well all right then.” He turns his back to the man and continues his speech to the soldier bellow. The Pharaoh: “As I was saying, troop: There are no ‘buts’ in this battalion--…” Once again the giggle erupts into the airwaves. Pharaoh spins on his heels and catches the same chubby man in mid-laugh. Slowly his face turns pale as he sees Xansiv walking steadily towards him. The Pharaoh: “Can I ask what’s so funny?” Troop: “Nothing, sir…” The Pharaoh: “So, what? You laugh for no reason, is that it?” The soldier’s face grows red. Troop: “N-N-No, sir--…” The Pharaoh: “Was it the fact that I said: There are no BUTS in this battalion?” The troop tries to hold back a laugh, but can’t help himself, letting out a hearty chuckle. Pharaoh glares, his eyes full of rage. The Pharaoh: “DON’T YOU EVER LAUGH AT ME AGAIN, OR EVEN LAUGH IN MY PRESENCE FOR NOW ON! IN FACT, IF I SEE YOU EVEN CHUCKLE WITH YOUR FRIENDS I’LL HAVE TO DEPORTED BACK TO EGYPT! GET THE HELL BACK TO YOUR PRESS-UPS, SOLDIER! IS THAT CLEAR?!” By this time the man’s face has reached a deep shade of crimson. Troop: “Yessir…” The Pharaoh: “Good! Brilliant!! Amazing! It certainly is to see we have an understanding!” Pharaoh lets out a huff of frustration and sets off towards the close combat section of the field, shaking his head. Once he reaches the sector he find two of the Egyptian troopers pushing each other back and forth. Troop#1: “Think you’re funny doing that? Huh?” The second troop pushes the man backward in return. Troop#2: “I dunno, do you think you’re funny saying that?” Pharaoh pinches the bridge of his nose, knowing already that some sort of disagreement is in progress. The Pharaoh: “Ok, Ok, Ok…what’s going on?” The two men turn to their commander. Troops in union: “Well, he started it!!” The second troop’s brow wrinkles. Troop#2: “What do you mean I started it?!” Troop#1: “I could ask the same thing about you, bitch!” Pharaoh sighs. The Pharaoh: “Ok… Will one of you start from the beginning, please?” The first soldier scowls at his comrade and then begins to fill Pharaoh in on the reason behind their dispute… Troop#1: “Well he said something about my mother, sir!” Troop#2: “And then you said something about my momma too, ya’ bastard!” The man throws a punch but his foe ducks it and uppercuts him for his efforts. Pharaoh watches, shaking his head, as the second troop tackles the other combatant to the ground and starts laying into him with sharp jabs. Troop#2: “Nobody says summat ‘bout my momma!” Pharaoh walks away from the two brawling soldiers, his head hung and his eyes weary. The Pharaoh: “Some of them aren’t even getting along, so how the hell are we suppose to fight Frisco’s battalion when we can’t even trust one another? I mean it’s not like I have anything else to worry about, what with my PPV match and my bout later on tonight. They just don’t seem to care…” Pharaoh looks around. Even though the moon has waxed he can just make out the two brawling troops, the fat soldiers trying their hardest to perform regulation press-ups and the men at the shooting range trying not to ‘kill’ Fake-It-Harry. He sighs. The Pharaoh: “Guess it’s just another of day at the office, huh?” He looks around, half expecting someone to reply. The Pharaoh: “Heh, who’d have ever thought training would be so hard?” And with that our picture fades.
