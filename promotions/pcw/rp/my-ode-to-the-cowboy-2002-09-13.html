My ode to the cowboy.

13 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/13/02 -The Malice man stands on a hill over looking a beautiful landscape straight out of a Louis L’Amour western story. Duke chews on some barley as he looks down the slope at miles of prairie land surrendered by forest. This is the virgin back drop of Hubbardville, Wisconsin also know as heaven on earth. Standing next to Duke is Bill Shaver the city planner for Hubbardville.- Williams: You see hoss this would be a perfect location for my event. Shaver: Explain your event again? Williams: Will have a rodeo and live stock show. Shaver: That’s with animals right? -Duke just shakes his head in frustration.- Williams: Yes animals of all kinds. Shaver: We have enough problems with out bringing carneys into God’s city. Williams: Its not like that hoss its good people, cowboys. Shaver: Mayor Kannon is already working on bringing the Cowboy’s in to play the Ellie~!’s. Williams: Not a football team but real cowboys. Shaver: Those guys with the yellow hats? Williams: Those are construction workers. Come on you know like John Wayne?? Shaver: John Wayne didn’t he dress up like a clown and kill little boys? -Duke is getting frustrated.- Williams: That’s John Wayne Gacey and he’s dead. Shaver: Well I’m glade. So John Wayne is his old man? Williams: What about Clint Eastwood? Shaver: The guy in that movie where he protects the president? That’s a cowboy? Williams: No that’s a secret service agent. Shaver: Then what in the name of Travolta did you bring that up for? Williams: Haven’t you seen pale rider? Shaver: No but I got pale riding a ride once at the Hubbardville fair grounds. -Duke just sighs.- Williams: Wait a minute what you said Travolta? Shaver: The legendary John Travolta. Williams: So you’ve seen Urban Cowboy? -Duke gets excited as they have connected- Williams: That’s a cowboy!! Shaver: So a guy who wears a funny hat and does a lot of dancing? -Duke just sighs.- Williams: Yea kinda like that. Shaver: I don’t see a problem with that but how do we get Rodeo drive style stores here. Williams: WHAT? Shaver: You said Rodeo? Like the rich strip in LA?? IS this like a wealthy flea market? Williams: For the love of Christ!! No its wear a cowboy rides a bull for eight seconds. Shaver: A bull? Like the basketball team. Williams: Like a steak. Shaver; So its an eating contest? Williams: NO!! The steaks are still alive. Shaver: That can’t be good!! Williams: They ride on top of the steak. Shaver: This sounds perverse we have morals here in Hubardville. Williams: Look hoss they ain’t fucking them they are riding them. -Bill Shaver is taken back.- Williams: It’s a contest. Shaver: And people enjoy watching grown men in silly hats riding steaks? Williams: People love it. They go nuts for the Rodeo. Shaver: Do they shoot the steaks and cook them up when its over with? Williams: No they move on to the next town. Shaver:: Would we be able to cook them up? Williams: Forget it. -Bill Shaver just shrugs his shoulders.- Shaver; What about this bowling thingy you were talking about? Williams: That’s for another time. I’m going to go for a job and get ready for my Turmoil Match. Shaver: Are you going to rip Miyawawa a new west end? Williams: I’m going to teach that kid a lesson…you don’t spit into the wind….you don’t tug on superman’s cape…..you don’t shoot John Wayne and you Don’t mess with MALICE or the FRONT OFFICE. -Duke jogs away.- =w=
