TPSC - Part II - Back To His Roots

28 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-28-02 OOC Note: Yeah. I'd have to say I'm definitely in a Limp Bizkit kind of mood. Hey. At least I match my character at the moment. .Song Listening Guide: 'My Way' by Limp Bizkit, from their second album. BOBBY S. CAMPBELL The Perfect Storm Chronicles - Part II - Back To His Roots Then.... Yet... Kira is left wondering exactly what Bobby has to talk about that could be so important. It seemed to be a lot more serious than just a botched wrestling move. He also wasn't the type to lecture. Not for long periods of time, anyway. She was worried about him. He has grown increasingly distant over these past few weeks, even stretching back into the end of their ten day vacation together. She was determined to help him fix anything and everything that was bothering him. After this was all over, she just wanted them both to be at peace. A couple of kids, a good relationship with his new sister and father, not to mention her own parents. If it meant walking right through hell itself instead of tip-toeing around it? So be it. And now.... * * * --5-21-02, 1:42 pm, last Tuesday afternoon-- About thirty minutes after our last scene began, we fade up onto the driveway of the Stormbringer family mansion in Orlando, Florida. Several other suburbian homes of varying sizes can be seen down the road in the distance. The view displays that each home has a cozy amount of room to themselves, but they are all still close enough to remain a tight knit community. The large yard is freshly mowed and most of the home has been painted over. Instead of it's usual brick red, it is now a soft baby blue with white trim. The only other time this 'smallish' manor has been seen is when Bobby 'The Showstopper' Stormbringer came home for a little visit (The Searching, Part Four, Home Bitter Home). On that day he found a simple little tape recorder under the welcome mat that has changed his life ever since. Sometimes for the better, sometimes not so much. Usually B.S. would just try to concentrate on the best thing, that he'd found a family member in the deal that was not only alive but actually didn't hate his guts. At least to the best of his knowledge. The much maligned and moody Stormbringer sits at the top of his driveway, his knees up and his arms propped up on top of them. The Showstopper changed into a pair of tight blue jeans, his blue and white T-Mac sneakers, and a very old black t-shirt that went back to his old indy fed days. 1998, 1999 or so. It simply reads in white lettering, 'Money talks. B.S. shuts you up.' Ah. Those were certainly the days. The days when he was quite evil and very nasty. When someone got in his face? He got right back in theirs. When someone punched him in the mouth? He proceeded to break their jaw. When someone stepped on his toes? He made sure they left with their foot broken. Bobby Stormbringer couldn't of possibly cared less about any wrestling fans back then. The faint memory of actually throwing a fan through a plate glass convienient store window floats through his mind. Why did he..? Oh, that's right. Because the punk had the audacity to ask him for some other meaningless wrestler's autograph. B.S. gently pushes his long red bangs out of his eyes at he chuckles all of the pure bullsh*t that he's had to deal with over the years... yep. He still has the broken remnants of that black bat he loved so much, framed and hung up on his bedroom wall. Right along with his three titles that mean next to nothing to anyone any more. They still mean just a little something to him. Only that he won them with no help from anyone whatsoever. Not friends, not management, not anyone. Besides for Jane Reagan... and recently, one other person... Bobby Stormbringer has never recieved any help. Then again? He'd never asked for any. Or wanted any. All that he's ever asked is for people to step aside and do what he's always promised he could do. Sometimes, in his mind, he would shake his fist at the heavens themselves. Challenging anything and anyone to bring their all at him. God, Allah, L. Ron Hubbard, pure fate and destiny, whatever was really in charge. It didn't really matter. Tragic knee injuries, love gone wrong, backstage politics, jealousy run rampant... 'Just bring it on,' Bobby mutters under his breath to himself. 'Bring everything.' He could act the simple-minded part of the hero with one arm tied behind his back. It was quite simple. Wrestling fans, not to mention people in general, try to stick you in this little box of morals. If you dare to step outside of it, if even just for a moment? 'Booooo, you suck,' they say. What-the-f*ck-ever. Perhaps now was the time. Now was the time to show all of them the real Bobby Stormbringer. The one that was more than willing to break your face as soon as he would shake your hand. The B.S. that so desperately wanted to separate from the pack and do his own thing. What a crime. Heaven forgive the poor fools who tried to get in his way. PIE Master. Maxx Fury. Bandit. Terror. J20. Shane Wethers. Drake Maxwell. The list grows... and it grows... and still they try to underestimate him. Still the list grows... With no End in sight. A soft hand on B.S.'s shoulder startles him away from his dark thoughts. With an annoyed click of the tongue, he looks up to see who could possibly be bothering him- -and lets his facial features soften when he sees it's his fiansce' Kira. Kira grins slightly down to Bobby, a red baby tee and denim shorts her choice for post-shower attire. She pushes her wet brunette hair out of the way while she leans down to give him a quick kiss. When Kira leans back, Bobby smiles weakly up to her. 'Sorry.. sorry I was so rough on you back in there.' Kira nods knowingly and gently pushes Bobby's knees down until he's sitting indian style. Then she hops into his lap with a giggle and wraps her arms around his neck. 'It's okay, babe,' Kira says calmly, 'I know you're just tryin' to make sure I can handle all of this. Besides, it's not like I wanna start wrestling in PCW tomorrow. We're just building for the future, right?' 'Right.' Bobby smirks at how easily Kira can always seem to lighten his mood. 'Although, it's not like I have much stroke anywhere in the PTC Complex... they'll prolly want you to go to the Academy like just about everyone else.' Kira nods, but Bobby knows his love well enough to see the disappointment that is clear in her eyes. 'That's fine with me,' the former Waldenbooks clerk says. 'I expected as much. You said you wanted to talk to me about something? Something important..?' B.S. finds himself taking a deep breath. Kira wasn't going to be all that thrilled about being kept out of the decision process, but it was the way things had to be. Mr. Prime Time reaches behind him and promptly produces a piece of paper, as well as a wrinkled and official-looking type of document from one of his back pockets. Kira wrinkles her nose at both papers in uncertainty. 'What's all this?' Without a word, B.S. hands her the less official looking of the two papers. Kira realizes with a start that it's an email. An email of the names that make up the new, post-Supremacy PCW Roster. The preliminary one, anyway. Kira's eyebrows narrow, this time anger rising up inside of her. '...the hell!? I didn't know-' '-Don't judge,' Bobby warns, 'Read.' Kira stifles her response and looks at the bottom. Tag teams. Then she looks towards the top to see that the email is dated May twentieth... just yesterday. She slowly starts down the list for single wrestlers, noting just a few people that her fiansce' raves about having much respect for... Violence Jack... Jason Snow... Chris Chase... this before reaching one name that sends a chill down her spine. She skips it quickly, reading until she reaches the very last name on the list. Bobby Stormbringer. Kira nearly drops the list and stutters up at The Showstopper. Her mouth moves but no discernible words come out. Bobby looks placidly to her and hands over the more official looking document. While she unwrinkles it, her lover unveils the details. 'My new PCW contract. Two hundred and fifty grand for one year, a restricted out clause after six months. Which basically means that if I take it, Nelson can match any offer given to me to keep me. Now-' 'Hey, hold on a second!' Kira says with an edge to her voice. 'Two hundred fifty!? That's all? There's some guy with the competition that's gettin' paid four million a f*ckin' year! Last time I checked? He wasn't even in the Global Tourney, let alone makin' the final eight of it!' 'Shhh, it's okay, it's okay. There's more.' Bobby runs his fingers through Kira's hair in a half-hearted attempt to calm her down. When she set her mind to things, it was usually difficult to get her to back down. 'We get total control over all 'B.S.' merchandise. All they have to do is distribute it. We design it, we make it. We get seventy-five percent of the proceeds.' Kira almost seems to shrink down to a smaller size, reading as much in the contract. 'Oh. That's not half-bad.' 'Not at all. It's sort of a bonus incentive. The better I do in the ring, the more t-shirts and hats and whatevah gets sold. What we don't get in contract money, we more than make up for with the merchandise and my request for this being short-term getting granted. ' Kira closes her eyes in sudden understanding. 'Just in case.' 'Just in case. We have plenty of money, babe. Plus, it's not like a quarter mil is anything to sneeze at! Trust me. I know what I'm doing.' Kira opens her eyes and sighs, still a little annoyed and feeling somewhat useless. She was his manager, after all. 'So... will I...' 'Of course you will! I don't know about you coming out to the ring, but you're definitely comin' on the road with me. That is... if you want to come after what-' Kira cuts Bobby off. There was no need to finish that sentence. '-I'm not scared of anything. I know it must of been hard to see what you saw, but I can take care of myself. I can take care of you too, if you'd just let me.' There was a definite bitterness in her voice. The kind of bitterness that just three months ago would never of existed. B.S. still struggles to get used to it, to this day. 'I know. I know... I don't think there's any one answer. We'll just have to play it by ear. Heh... I told you I had a feeling about Supremacy.' 'Pheh,' Kira rolls her eyes, 'I was tired. Sue me for sleeping. We can watch the tape, since I know you gotta have it.' Bobby chuckles. 'And the catch would be..?' 'Damn you... ya know me too well. The catch is we go out for lunch first.' 'Hmm. Sounds good. I better take that shower first, though. Unless we just get the rest of the restaurant patrons some clothesline pins for their noses.' Bobby grins at Kira while she shakes her head and stands up. 'Go on, get outta here. N' take your papers with ya. Silly boy.' The Showstopper snickers while he stands and stretches his legs out. Kira folds the papers and sticks them in his back jeans pocket herself. Bobby wraps an arm around her waist and kisses her gently before winking to her. 'I know you love ya some silly boys though.' 'Hehehe. Maybe.' They hug before Bobby walks up the stone set path and into the house. After dropping the papers onto the wood coffee table, Bobby Campbell settles himself onto the genuinely fake leather couch for just a minute. There was still one more important thing he had to tell her. He just couldn't do it right now. Yes... now is the time to go back to his roots, he reasoned. Despite what Kira said to him, right then and there he silently promised himself that from this day forward he would do absolutely anything to protect him and his own. Anything. TO BE CONTINUED.
