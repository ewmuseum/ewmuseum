Computer illiteracy at it’s finest! On with the show! ... ~!~!~!~! The Tank __13__ New Identities A king’s value in the desertis a dead president’s control or a chunk of goldin heaven. A chunk of coal in hell. ~Leigh Tuesday, July 30 - Day 26 “Smarter than I thought,” Doug Hattoni starts while peering through the open doorway of his cell. “The one-eyed bastard.” On the other side of the cell balcony, two skinheads stand guard outside Harvey Blake’s cell, keeping a watchful eye on everyone in the Unit - especially Italians and inmates they know are affiliated with Hattoni. “Why don’t you let me take him?” Snow asks with a shrug. “Because in the current state of things,” Doug starts to reply, “I can’t afford to have you sitting up in the hole for an extended period of time. Blake’s got every fucking Nazi in this prison waiting for me to let my guard down. With you gone, I’d be one step closer to a rough box. And considering all the Nazis out there watching his ass right now, I’d don’t imagine there’d be a way for you to walk through them all without drawing attention to yourself. I can’t have you in the hole, kid. And I especially can’t have something happen to you.” Snow sighs. He’s been itching to get a crack at Harvey Blake since his earliest days in the Tank, but he knows Hattoni’s right. As long as Snow, a well-oiled prize fighter, is keeping his protective eye on the old Italian, no one’s going to be stupid enough to try to make a hit. “So,” Snow says as he narrows his eyes, “is this a stalemate?” “You might say that,” Hattoni shrugs, finally stepping away from the open doorway. “But I don’t intend for things to stay this way for long.” Snow scratches the back of his blond head and steps over to the doorway of Hattoni’s cell - instantly, the two skinheads outside Harvey Blake’s cell direct their attention to him. “How’s your cellmate treating you?” Hattoni asks to change the subject. Snow shrugs, “He hasn’t tried to fuck me since I broke the fuckers cheekbone.” “These fucking faggots,” Hattoni says while shaking his head. “I’ll never understand them.” Snow peers over to his own cell, which he shares with a low ranking Thug, Darryl Jarvis. Jarvis is a pathetic man in Snow’s mind. A small time crook who wound up in jail for the rest of his life and now spends his time trying to ‘get some’ off his fellow inmates. And there, standing outside Snow’s cell is the same Darryl Jarvis, talking casually to the unofficial Thug leader, Jeremy Doris - J-Thug. “They’re replacing Bruno tomorrow,” Hattoni sighs, remembering his late friend. “New cellmate.” “They tell you who?” “They don’t tell me shit,” Doug chuckles. “I tried to get you transferred in here, but Rankin wouldn’t go for it. Says he wants you and Jarvis to ‘work through your problems.’ Christ - fucking hippie.” “When’s he supposed to be here?” asks Snow without taking his eyes away from the interaction between his cellmate and the ever-dangerous J-Thug. Since being incarcerated, Snow has noticed that he’s exhibiting a much greater attention to detail, especially in terms of potentially dangerous situations. And J-Thug, he’s learned, is always dangerous. Between the massive black, J-Thug, and the evil White Supremacist, Harvey Blake, Snow senses great danger in this Unit, for both himself and his boss, Doug Hattoni. *** From the Office of the Warden“Imagine, if you will, how hard it must be to go from being on top of the world to being in the world’s sewer,” the Warden says while sitting behind his desk. “One minute, you have everything you could ever imagine - fast cars, faster girls, and more money than you know what to do with. The next minute, your eating something that tastes like it was removed from a monkey’s digestive system, you’re locked in a five by nine cell ten hours a day, and you’re forced to shower with a bunch of large men - some of whom are probably fantasizing about you.” “It’s an adjustment that not many could handle.” *** Wednesday, July 31 - Day 27 Leigh Donovan stares down at the line of white powder he has spread out on a small piece of cardboard on his bunk. His massive cellmate, J-Thug, won’t be back from his nightly workout for at least another twenty minutes, and by that time, Leigh plans to be oblivious to his surroundings. He leans close to the cardboard, and with one quick snuff, takes the powder into his body. He waits. When the powerful drug finally starts to take effect, Leigh reaches down beneath his thin mattress and pulls out the pages that he keeps his poetry on. When he came to prison, the twenty-six year old Australian rockstar thought it would give him a great opportunity to write. But now, nearly a week into his sentence, he finds that he has been unable to write anything more than small bits of poetry - nothing near a song. He wishes Geoffery Rankin had allowed him to keep a guitar in his cell. That would have made things so much easier. Then again, a guitar wouldn’t last long with his cellmate, J-Thug. “Fucking hell!” he curses, tossing the pages aside as he realizes he can’t think of anything to write. In the past, drugs have acted a tool for him. But not today. Today, as he falls deeper into his stone, he can’t think of anything but how unfair the entire situation is. Leigh Donovan! The most famous rock star in the world! And he’s forced to share housing with some of the lowest criminals in the United States. The judge made an example of him. Things wouldn’t be so bad if he weren’t sharing a cell with arguably the most dangerous man in the Tank, J-Thug. J-Thug makes life hell in the Tank. Leigh can still lightly taste the semen on his tongue... he can’t wait until the drug numbs his mouth. It can’t go on this way, he thinks - there must be a way to change it. ...to end it... Maybe tonight, he’ll push J-Thug over the edge and provoke the monster to kill him. Or maybe, he can do something before J-Thug returns, so he’ll never have to face the beast’s wrath. Stoned and thinking through stained glass, Donovan rolls off his bunk and staggers out of his cell. With tearless bloodshot eyes, he leans over the railing at the edge of the cell balcony and looks down at the main floor, some twenty feet below. “Fucking look at me!” he screams loudly, gaining the attention of every inmate in the main room of Unit B. “I’m a fucking rock star, damn it!” His voice cracks and oddly, this experience makes him feel like he’s back on stage again - all eyes or on him. Without an ounce of caution, he climbs the four foot railing. When he gets to the top he has trouble balancing on the narrow rail, but is able to hold himself steady long enough to scream, “It... is... finished!!!!” With that, Leigh Donovan leaps from the railing. Every man in the Unit holds his breath as he watches the young rocker falling carelessly toward the hard, tile-over-concrete floor below. He lands, undramatically, with a sickening thud. “Jesus!” screams one of the C.O’s, grabbing for his radio to call for help. In the distance, even Doug Hattoni has ventured from his cell to find out what all the commotion is about. Within a minute, a medical team crashes into the main room of Unit B, pushing a stretcher. When they get to Leigh’s body, they are relieved to find that he’s still breathing, though the impact stole his consciousness. “How the hell did he fall?” One medic asks a C.O. “Crazy motherfucker jumped!” “Get him to the fucking infirmary!” *** Prisoner Profile File Photo: Coming Soon...Name: Timothy Gerald McDonaldPrisoner Number: 857395Nickname(s): Slick TimmyRace: White Born: 04-20-1974Height: 5'11Weight: 160Hair: Bleached Blond, spikedEyes: BlueFacial Hair: noneTattoo(s): Blue Tiger (right bicep), Blue Dragon (left bicep)Build: slenderFrame: mediumIdentifying Characteristics: Bleached blond spiked hairCurrent Sentence(s): 2002 - Distribution of Narcotics(Sentenced to seven years, up for parole in three.)Previous Convictions: 2000 - Assault (served three months)1994 - Possession of Narcotics (served two months)1993 - Possession of Narcotics (fined $1,000, sentenced to six months of community service, and one year of probation.)Family: Wife From the Office of the Warden “Timothy McDonald - Slick Timmy,”says the Warden looking through Tim McDonald’s bulky file. “Like nearly everyone in the Tank, he started out as a small timer. They say he was selling pot on street corners by the time he was thirteen. At nineteen, he got caught selling a much harder product. In the end, the D.A couldn’t prove he was selling it and the kid got away with a slap on the wrist.” “Same thing happened the next year,” sighs the Warden. “That time, the DA was at least able to put him in prison.” “After that, McDonald started running with some low ranking mobsters in NYC. He kept a pretty low profile. He got married. Things were looking up for him, especially since the talk around most mob circles was that he was an up-and-comer.” “So he started coming up through the ranks. He oversaw drug deals, made some fairly big decisions, and was even wining and dining with some pretty big names in the underground. Now, Tim McDonald is no Doug Hattoni - not by a long stretch... but with a chance to be back on the street in 2005, some people think he will be.” *** “Holy shit,” Tim McDonald says as he tosses the items he was allowed to bring into the Tank on the top bunk of Doug Hattoni’s cell. “You’re Doug fucking Hattoni!” It’s been about an hour since the medics scraped Leigh Donovan’s badly damaged body off the main floor, and almost time for the nightly lock down. “That’s me,” says Doug, sitting on the bottom bunk of the cell - he used to sleep on top, but in his advancing age, he’s lately found climbing to the top bunk more strenuous than it’s worth. “Now, why don’t you tell me who the fuck you are?” “Oh,” Tim says, extending his hand to Hattoni, “I’m Tim McDonald. I do some work for some of the guys under you on the outside.”“The mob?” “Yeah,” Tim says, obviously trying to impress Hattoni - Doug Hattoni has always been one of his heros. “The Italians, mostly.” “You’re Italian?” “No,” shrugs Tim, putting his pillow in the proper place and climbing up to his bunk, “but no one’s ever really made an issue of it - I’m very good at what I do.” “And just what is it that you do?” Doug asks, his curiosity finally evoked by his new cellmate “Well,” starts Tim, reaching into his pocket, “I’m an expert at getting...” he produces a tiny vile of cocaine from his pocket and holds it out to the side of his bunk so Hattoni can see it, “this.” “Jesus Christ, kid!” Hattoni whispers an exclamation, “Put that shit away before they cart both to the God damn hole! How the hell did you get that anyway? You’ve only been here for a couple of hours.” “I’ve got ways,” smiles Tim. “Actually, I’ve got a cousin who works in the mail department. He handles all my packages personally.” “You know something, kid,” Doug says with a smile forming on his aging face, “I think I can put your talents and connections to good use.”
