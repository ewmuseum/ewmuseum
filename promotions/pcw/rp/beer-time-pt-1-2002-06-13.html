Beer Time Pt 1

13 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13/06/02 An attractive young blonde bar attendant slowly pushes her way through the mass of people that have crammed into the up-market Manhattan bar.  She struggles to keep her tray upright as a drunken accountant type steps blindly into her, almost knocking her to the ground.  He growls something suggestive under his breath as she ignores him and continues on her way. I hate these sort of places. She finally makes her way to a table where four young women dressed in almost identical black dresses sit chatting aimlessly.  They ignore the bar attendant as she places their drinks down on the table in front of them and waits patiently for them to pay.  She stands there for a few seconds before politely asking for her money.  The young women stop their conversation and all look up annoyed. Ah, chill out man, relax.  One of the young women fumbles through a small purse and pulls out some money which she rudely shoves into the bar attendants hand and, without as much as a thank you, turns back to her friends who have resumed their conversation.  The bar attendant puts the money into a pocket in her apron and sighs to herself as she picks the empty glasses off the table and begins to work her way back through the jam packed crowd, towards the bar. I just don't like crowds. As she pushes her way through the noisy crowd, the drunk that had bumped into her earlier walks up behind her and knocks the tray out of her hands.  The empty glasses fall to the floor, shattering to pieces.  The drunken man then grabs her and tries to kiss her as she struggles to escape.  On the far side of the room, two security men see what is happening and come charging through the crowd to the bar attendant's assistance. What?  You don't like crowds?  Man, are you ever in the wrong business! The crowd around the bar attendant remain oblivious to the drunken man's attempts to grope her, or they just don't care, as the two doormen arrive.  The crowd suddenly goes quiet and all heads in the bar turn to look as the security men grab hold of the drunk and begin dragging him towards the exit on the other side of the crowded room.  He screams and tries to get away but the doormen ignore him and drag him away as the obviously distressed bar attendant begins to clean up the broken glass.  The drunken man's friends quickly gather up his belongings and hurry off after the doormen. I don't mind looking at crowds, I just hate being in them.  That's all. Within a matter of seconds, the crowd noise returns to the level it had been before the ejection of the drunk man as the incident is soon forgotten.  The bar attendant finishes cleaning up the broken glass and returns to pushing her way towards the bar.  The two security men return to their post against the far wall having finished getting rid of the troublemaker. Well I don't know about you, but I could murder another beer. Ricky Thunder raises his hand up in the air to catch the attractive bar attendant's eye.  She is busily loading up more orders onto her tray at the bar and gives him a quick nod as she notices him. Yeah, OK.  I'll have another one before heading home. That's the spirit! The Supreme Machine turns back and looks out at the crowd from the booth that he and Ricky Thunder occupy at the rear of the bar. What's so important that you have to rush off home anyway, man?  Don't you get bored being up there all on your lonesome all of the time? I get out.  I just don't frequent places like this. Well I think you need to get out more.  Hell, ever since we joined PCW you've become a damned hermit! Yeah, well....... The Supreme Machine watches as the bar attendant drops off her load of drinks at a nearby table and takes their money.  She looks up and sees him watching. So have you thought any more on the tag-team thing I was talking to you about. She smiles at the huge wrestler as she picks up some empty glasses from the nearby table. Dude? Sorry? The bar attendant finishes picking up the empties and walks over to the booth where Ricky Thunder and The Supreme Machine are sitting. Hello, what can I get you? The Supreme Machine smiles nervously at her. Can we get another couple of beers please. Sure.  I'll be back in a moment. As she turns to walk away, she glances down at The Supreme Machine and gives him a quick smile.  He watches her intently as she once again begins pushing her way through the crowd, towards the bar. What was I saying?  Yeah, that's right.  Have you given any thought to teaming up to face Crichton and Murray? The Supreme Machine slowly pulls his gaze away from the bar attendant and turns to look at Ricky. I don't know.  I'd rather focus on singles wrestling. But man, didn't you see what those bastards did? Yes, of course I saw it. The Supreme Machine quickly scans the crowd until he spots the bar attendant.  She is standing at the bar putting their two beers on her tray and looks up as if sensing his gaze.  Embarrassed, The Supreme Machine hurriedly looks away. Come on man.  All I'm asking is one match!  The Supreme Team back in action against Crichton and Murray. I don't know. Dude, I wanna kick their asses. And? We could kick their asses. And? SuMa, we can take these guys.  I know it. And? What's with this "and" shit?  AND you fucking agree.  OK?!? Both wrestlers stop talking and look up as the blond bar attendant arrives at their booth with the beers.  She doesn't take her eyes off The Supreme Machine as she places the two frosty glasses down in front of them.  Ricky Thunder pulls out his wallet and gives her twenty dollars. Thanks a lot luv.  Here you go.  Keep the change. Thanks. She puts the twenty into her apron pocket and pulls out a white business card that she hands to The Supreme Machine.  She flashes him a huge smile before turning and walking back into the noisy crowd. What's that then? As The Supreme Machine turns over the card, Ricky Thunder suddenly reaches across the booth and snatches it out of his hand. SuMa, you sly fucking dog!  You've got her name and number AND guess what else?!?! The Supreme Machine tries to snatch the business card back but Ricky holds it out of his reach. Give it back. She finishes at one o'clock dude. Ricky Thunder laughs loudly as he hands the business card back. I do believe you're in SuMa! Very funny. Ricky Thunder continues laughing as he takes a big gulp of beer and looks across the bar towards the dance floor where a group of amazingly attractive women are dancing.  As Ricky looks away, The Supreme Machine takes a quick glance down at his watch.  8:17.
