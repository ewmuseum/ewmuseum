A Walk in the Night

23 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/23/03 Tossing and turning in his sheets sweat dripping from every inch of his body. His legs rubbing up against each other as he fought to try and peacefully sleep. The thoughts and visions in his head were rattling his brain. He felt so dirty and shameful, but he couldn't help it. No matter what he tried he couldn't shake the thoughts from his head. Chris suddenly sits up in the bed, sweat covering his forehead. He looks over to his side toward Tessa. His hand reaches out to touch her but he pulls it back. The thoughts still refresh in his mind. Chris throws the covers off him and walks out of the room holding his head in his hands. 'Chris where are you going?' Tessa calls out from the bed as she sits up but keeps herself covered in the blanket, not wanting to be overcome by the cool air lingering outside the blanket. Rather then reply to her, Chris just kept walking. He pushes open the door to the kitchen and sits down at the table. His head falls onto the table and rest there. He just stares at the wall thinking. A sigh escapes his lips, but before he can be fully taken over by thoughts he is interrupted by the sound of footsteps making their way closer to the kitchen. Thump, thump, thump The sound of bare feet impacting with the kitchens tile floor breaks the silence. Chris turns to see who is making the sound and sure enough he finds Tessa standing behind him looking down with concern. 'Chris what is wrong?' She questions from behind him as she sits her hands on his shoulders and begins to massage his back just to keep her hands busy. Chris shrugs his shoulder and stands up, removing himself from Tessa’s embrace. Tessa looks at him with concerned eyes as Chris ways across the kitchen to the counter. His eyes never turn to her, his back always facing her. Moving toward him, Tessa steps carefully, not sure what is the matter with Chris at the moment. “Chris, if something is bothering you then please tell me.” She pleads with him. Her hand moves to his shoulder to give him a sign of comfort but Chris stands up straight. He moves his head a little to the side and looks at her out of the corner of his eye. His lips part but no words come out. Instead Chris just sighs and walks off. Feeling both helpless and frustration Tessa takes a seat at the kitchen table and lets her arms fall onto the table, burying her face inside of them. The overwhelming feeling of guilt falls upon her as she feels like she has become the weaker link of the two. Perhaps Chris wanted nothing to do with her anymore yet had no clue how to say it. It would make sense, they didn't seem to have much in common beside their love for wrestling and the kids they cared for. Chris pushes the door to the kitchen open and walks on out leaving Tessa alone. He knew the way he was acting was hurting her but right now he didn’t care. His own soul was going through a thunderstorm of emotions too. Chris just walked up the stairs and sat down on the top stair. His gaze was focused on the floor down below. Another sigh escapes his lips as he just shakes his head. “Everything is just falling to pieces. One by one they come crashing down. I’m helpless to stop any of it. I told myself I was finally going to strike back against our enemies and save Tessa and I. What do I do? I screw up again. Rather then look like a mighty warrior defending himself against his enemy I looked like an idiot that didn’t have any idea what he was doing.” He said with bitter and angry words. Chris’ fist hits the post beside him. The thud echoes throughout the silent home. He lays his head down as he continues to think about it. “Every event I walk into that PCW arena and I look up at the sign hanging above the locker rooms. ‘Primetime Championship Wrestling- Are you ready for Primetime?’ and I wonder to myself if I made the right decision stepping up from a small network company like the WIW: NG to a major company like PCW. Did I join too soon? Was I really ready? These have been haunting me everyday since I signed on the dotted line. Each week that idea is pressed in my mind more and more. Am I ready? When I have to bandage myself up after another beating I’ve suffered I look in the mirror and I ask myself, am I ready? Then after each week I think about it more and more…” Chris’ words start to trail off. “Am I ready for the PCW? If I had to answer that question right now I would have to say….no.” he says almost inaudible. Chris just shakes his head and rises up from his seat. Slowly he moves down the stairs, each step shows how he’s nearly given up on everything. His hand grabs the doorknob to his room; from the kitchen he can see Tessa is looking at him, watching him with tear stained eyes. Chris just shakes his head and looks away from her. He opens the door to their room and closes it behind him leaving her to her thoughts. Chris walks over to the bed and lies down. He just looks at the blank ceiling, the thought keep running though his head like a freight train. Chris just closes his eyes, he’ll think about it tomorrow. As for now, he needs to rest and let his wounds of another day heal.
