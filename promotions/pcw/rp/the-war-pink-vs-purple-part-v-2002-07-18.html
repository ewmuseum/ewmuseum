The War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part V]

18 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 18, 2002 With Valentine’s fingers poised over his eyelids and the cold blade nicking at his throat, even a question like who are you seems challenging. Valentine made the sort of stammer that is half way to being a choking noise. Jack Valentine: “I’m Valentine, Jack Valentine. The Rainbow Squad told me I would find you here.” The voice seemed to be coming from somewhere else, though that was clearly impossible Mystery Voice: “Liberace and Elton John? Those old rogues. Well if that’s the case, tell me what he is drinking these days?” Jack Valentine: “His liver is shot, it looks like his old vodka and PINK! lemonade but I didn’t get a chance to find out what it really was.” Mystery Voice: “Well any friend of the Rainbow Squad is all right in my book, a sound chap.” So saying a huge jovial giant of a man appeared and helped Valentine, and the others to their feet with a powerful helping hand. Valentine was gasping for air like a goldfish as the Village People dusted him off. Mystery Man: “Lovely coat, you’ll get it ruined in woods like these. Come on, I’ll stand you a glass of PURPLE~! punch back at the bunker.” The Mystery man led on. It was odd that someone from the purple squad was being so friendly to The Heartbreakers and the Rainbow Squad, who were fighting for the PINK! Valentine and the rest of the bunch did as they told holding their hands aloft and scurrying after the huge figure of the mystery man. Ten minutes later the mystery man, accompanied by the Village People, was leading them into a gully half filled with an insipid stream. A few yards down stream they came to a concrete drain shrouded with vegetation. The mystery man led the way, and in an instant he had vanished like some riverside creature, leaving just the Village People. The Rainbow Squad ducked down and just pushed on through the darkness and leaves. Up ahead a light clicked on and Valentine could see that it was a large concrete pipe that he was walking through. The pipe was about fifteen yards long and opened out into a largish room. The room was like a cellar or some other underground place. The walls were dark almost to the point of blackness and a jumble of what looked like rubbish covered the floor. The mystery man was back and was fiddling with a Tilley lamp which he had lit, and he was now adjusting the hissing butane to arrive at a stable flame. The light was sloshing across the room, catching objects and flinging shadows across the walls. The mystery man nodded distractedly at a deck chair next to a picnic table and invited the Rainbow Squad to sit down. Staten sat down, picking a load of magazines off the chair and putting them on the picnic table. Flicking through them they seemed to alternate between music magazines and money magazines. Mystery Man: “What do you want to drink, we have a choice of anything you like as long as it is PURPLE~!?” The mystery man had left the Tilley light and was in a corner of the room. Jack Valentine: “PINK! lemonade will be just fine.” Valentine smirked as he sat looking at the translucent glow of the lamp. He had never been anywhere like this before. His hands were still stinging with the nettle stings from the woods, and now that they were thawing the pain was getting more intense. His hands were covered in tiny swellings, he tried to count them but it was impossible. His outfits were ruined, as were his shoes. He sat picking the thorns out of his PINK! pants. Mystery Man: “What in the PURPLE~! hell do you think you're doing here Jack?” Teague handed over a glass of PURPLE~! punch with a return smile, as Valentine knocked it over on the ground. Jack Valentine: “I think you know why we're here.” Mystery Man: “Shouldn't you and Johnny be getting ready for your match tomorrow? You have a big record deal on the line and the higher ups are making you go on a mission?' Jack Valentine: (Pausing to think) 'Well...' Mystery Man: (Interrupting) 'There must be half of PINK! control out looking for me now, and a load of freelancers, why would they expect you to be able to find me?” The mystery man was sitting back tugging at his chin. Mystery Man: “Unless of course they wanted to get rid of you. You see Jack...Johnny...Rainbow Squad...” A smudge of gay faces looked up happily at the camera at the mentions of their names. Mystery Man: 'Elton John, Liberace...the heads of the PINK! organization were once fighting for a good cause...fighting for the PINK!, but now they are just greedy, just like Tyler Nelson...just like the Pretty Cool Guys, and soon you will be just like them.' Johnny Heart: 'It can't be true.' Mystery Man: 'Oh it's true...they have probably already sided with the Pretty Cool Guys. They are probably going to give them a corporate record deal...and more money than you could ever imagine. They are greedy, filthy scum Jack...that's why we turned our backs on the PINK! to fight with the Purple.' Jack Valentine: 'But we love the PINK!' Mystery Man: 'We did too at one point, but we realized what they were all about.' Jack Valentine: 'Maybe we could take it back from them, and make it right again...' Mystery Man: 'Highly unlikely, they are too powerful.' Jack Valentine: “You know diddly squat PINK!, you have all the control instincts of a girl.' Johnny Heart: 'Yeah, girls suck...you're lying! You just want us to work for the Purple.' Mystery Man: “Yes, more than anything. I’ve been in administration, personnel, finance, but I always wanted to you be in control of Purple. I thought that when I got promoted they would move me into control, but the director kept me in the same post but just had the post regraded! You guys and the Rainbow Squad must take over the Purple!” Johnny Heart: “Well this could be your big chance, Jack. I don’t know why the directors sent us out, but it seems to me that they are being greedy like the man says.' Jack Valentine: “Maybe, but what are they doing hiding in a concrete bunker? What kind of mission are you on?” Mystery Man: “Well you may have guessed this, I’m not on a mission. I’ve gone rogue, left the department, that’s why they are hunting me down, and that’s why the directors had to get you to find me.” Jack Valentine: “Why should I want to get involved, why shouldn’t I just go back to the directors and tell them where you are?” Valentine jabbed his finger at the magazines on the picnic table, reading about all of the greed in the world. Mystery Man: “You’re right of course Valentine. Nothing to stop you walking out of here, you go, and I can be out of here and set up in a safehouse tomorrow. You leave now and there’s going to be nothing worth telling the directors. You hang around, drop out whenever you like, its your best chance at control work. Pick up a few tips from the master. I am the best there is at this. And I could even give you some help at getting those tag team titles back.” The mystery man had made his pitch... Mystery Man: “There is only one way to find out if PURPLE~! is for you guys, you hang around, you are in for the trip of a lifetime, if you can take it ...” Will Jack and Johnny leave PINK! and become proud members of the PURPLE~! How could they leave something that has been so good to them for so long? And what effect would this have on their ladder match against PCG on Friday? Only time would tell, so stay tuned for Part VI of the War: PINK! vs. Purple.
