Call of the Mild Part #2

17 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/17/02 ***This Promo is dedicated to those people that force me to answer the door early in the morning right after taking a shower and relentlessly pursuing the chance to fill my happily uneventful Sunday afternoons with unhappiness. The camera fades in… A still scene shows Bertha hitting the trail awfully hard and going into a state of unconsciousness. The scene becomes paused as the Narrator speaks.Narrator: Welcome back to another ridiculous Chef Promo! Let’s show the last few seconds of the previous show just to make this roleplay look even longer than it actually is!!! ***As we last left the Chef and Bertha Clone #1… The Chef heard the violent thud on the trail and turns around. The Chef: Bertha!!! A thought then comes to his head. “Come on Chef, get out of there. She’s going to be bait! You can always make another clone, you’ll just have to unearth Bertha’s grave again!” The Chef ponders what to do… The Chef: I know, I’ll ask Allah! The Chef gets down on his knees and prostrates on the trail. The Chef: Gracious Allah, please hear my call for safety and…AAAHHHHHH!!! Suddenly, out of nowhere, a ravenous beast jumps out of the bushes and lands on the Chef! The creature violently tears at the Chef’s uniform and the Culinary Conquistador screams in agony. ***And now the mind-numbingly stupid conclusion!!! The beast angrily tears at the Chef’s Islamic Military garment, trying to rip open the fine quality suit and reveal some flesh to munch on. The Chef twists and turns, fights and screams, punches and kicks, and does everything else possible to free himself. The Chef: Back you vile spawn of Iblis! Smite him Allah! Smite him good for Ol’ Cheffy! The Chef is able to roll over on top of the wild animal and beats it senseless with a barrage of whaling right hands. He stands back up and checks his camouflage outfit. The Chef: Awww geez, I bet Sears won’t let me return this… The Chef looks back to the ground, and notices the creature is gone from his sight. The Chef: HUH? He can slightly hear some growling up the trail. The Chef: Bertha Clone! You’re in trouble! The Chef rushes back up the trail, but manages first to stop by the BMW SUV and pick up his trusty rolling pin. The scene is paused. Narrator: Now at this moment you are probably wondering why the Chef just doesn’t hop into his SUV and run the beast away. Well…you see the Chef, like many other Islamic Militants, is too “brave” to defeat a vile creature with the help of mechanized weapon from some 'Industrialized' Country that has forgotten Allah! The scene begins to play once again. The Chef recognizes the grotesque blob that is Bertha’s fallen body and rushes towards her aid. As he gets nearer and nearer, he notices that the growling sound he heard is actually that same beast gnawing at Bertha’s leg!?!? The Chef: Ha Ha Ha! I bet you think you can get quite a hunk of meat off that leg, don’t ya? Beast: GRRRRRRR!!! The Chef: Well the joke’s on you pal! Judging by the layers upon layers of fat packed together in that leg, you’ll probably have to gnaw on it for at least a week before you see any signs of cartable food! Now back away from the carrion! Beast: GRRRRRRR!!! The Chef begins taunting the fiend. The Chef: No hablas ingles? Well I guess there’s only one language to teach you…GASTRONOMICAL TERMS!!! The Chef whips a wide array of culinary utensils out of his pockets and holds them up in both hands. He starts flinging them at the beast like a bunch of Ninja Stars! The Chef: SPATULA! OYSTER MALLET! CITRUS ZESTER! TENDERIZER! BONK! CRACK! THUMP! SMACK! The four objects whack the animal over the forehead and drive it away from Bertha. The Chef: Scrap that, I’ll teach you a second language, the language of PAIN!!! The Chef pulls out his trusty rolling pin. The Chef: I learned much about pain after being imprisoned by this government for treason. That’s right, I spent a good month in a small building with a bunch of men! Beast: GRRRRRRR! The Chef: Hostile are we? Well the men at the prison camp were even more hostile! They all wanted a piece of my rump roast, and when I said, “Not even for Louis XIV”, they held me down and administered lots and lots of pain to my body! Beast: GRRRRRR? The Chef: If by that inquisitive growl you mean I was gang raped you are wrong! The pain did not come from a throbbing sphincter muscle but from many hard blows with a piece of wood to the head…LIKE THIS!!! The Chef lunges forward and swings his rolling pin, attempting to clock and KO the beast, but the creature dashes out of the way. The Chef jumps back and stands his ground, not budging and letting his Bertha become food for this ravenous monster. The Chef: Please Allah, guide this rolling pin to the area between the eyes of that hideous thing and help me smite it! I promise I will praise you over and over for at least an hour if you aid me! The Chef sees a mound of froth gather at the mouth of the beast as it prepares to come forward for a deadly strike, and quickly takes to action. He leaps up into the air, lands on Bertha’s stomach, bounces off the flab like a trampoline and flips into the air, screaming like the crazy Muslim he is. The Chef: SHALALALALALALALALALALA!!! The Chef SMASHES THE ROLLING PIN OVER THE HEAD OF THE BEAST!!! The vile creature falls onto the ground, and lays motionless on the trail of Cypress Mulch. The Chef: Thank you Allah! Praise be to you for helping me sauté some serious @$$! The Chef runs over and checks on Bertha. The Chef: B? B? B!?!? Are you ok!?!? Bertha finally stirs from her involuntary slumber, mumbling a few profanities… Bertha: F*ck…@$$…Sh*tty…Skank ho…B*tch @$$ F*ck…Queer…Pansy @$$… The Chef shakes Bertha almost violently. The Chef: Bertha! In the name of Allah wake up! Bertha: …………… The Chef: BERTHA!!! Bertha: I’M F*CKING AWAKE F*CKFACE!!! Bertha’s horrendous roar sends the Chef’s cap flying off his head onto the trail. Bertha: What the f*ck was tickling me!?!? The Chef: That! The Chef points at the creature laying on the trail. Bertha: What in Allah’s f*cking world is that f*cking piece of sh*tty as b*tch ho skank douche!?!? The Chef: That, my beloved, is one of the most feared scavengers on the face of this planet. It territory is boundless, it’s violence is unpredictable, and it’s body smells of rancid sausage…YUCK!!! It’s more of an endangered species than a Chet King Promo, YOWZA!!! Bertha: Quit f*cking stealing Yakov’s material f*ckdaddy! The Chef: Ahem, that was I own comedic material, thank you very much… Bertha: OOOOOOHHHH F*CKO… The Chef: Bah, save your compliments for our return home. Bertha: Ok, but what the f*ck is that f*cking animal!?!? The Chef: Yes…(pausing)…that animal…Latin name is BANGUS ONUS THEADOORUS…that my lemon meringue pie is…A JEHOVAH’S WITNESS!!! DUN! DUN! DUNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNN! Bertha: Well that explains the f*cking biting… The screen is paused for the Narrarator. Narrator: Now while most Jehovah’s Witnesses don’t gnaw on live flesh, there are some exceptions… The scene begins to play again… The Chef helps Bertha back up to her feet. Beast: Would you like a free booklet of our weekly gatherings? SMACK!!! The Chef kicks the unrestrainedly hostile evangelist in the face with his Military boot. The Jehovah’s Witness drops back onto the ground. The Chef: Adios Enemigo! Off to Fusion to fight Chet King! Bertha: But you didn’t f*cking talk about him in your queer as f*ck promo? The Chef: True, but I was busy thwarting an evil infidel! Come on; give this old gastronomer some credit! Bertha shyly and lovingly answers. Bertha: O-F*cking-K… The Chef and Bertha slowly make their way back to the BMW SUV as the camera mercifully fades to black…
