The Church?

18 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18th May, 2003 ‘I’ve got a great idea! Perhaps I can spread the idea of the Society of Order and Logic to normal folk!’ :: The scene opens in some downtown place, well, in Germany to be more precise. He has set up camp in this err…lovely country, while he sorts things out with his wife. He hasn’t seen or heard from his kids for 3 weeks now, and has only briefly spoken to his wife on the phone. Still, he would put finding the murderers of his family way ahead of getting some quick nookie from a beautiful blonded woman, since it hurts him so much. :: Chang: ‘Well let me think about it, after all, the Society is one leaned more towards perfectness, and having some random German person in, isn’t going to help at all, is it?’ Krikorian: ‘No sir, I guess not.’ :: Sivasian lies on a plush red sofa, with the phone attached to his ear. Well, hovering around his ear, for the Ukrainian has always liked technical improvements, and his phone today is non the different. A small ear piece with a microphone leading on the end of it makes him look quite the modern day agent. :: Krikorian: ‘But anyways sir, you’ve got to help me do something, the boredom is killing me. I can only train with my rifle and keep this superb body in shape for a certain amount of time before I get bored.’ Chang: ‘I understand Krikorian.’ Krikorian: ‘I knew you would sir, I know you would!’ :: Chang quickly skims through the ideas in his head. On one hand this man could go and hire a mister random, or, on the other hand, could prove to be a great leader, and get something great in, plus, it would hopefully stop him persistently calling Chang every day. In the end Chang gives in. Although there are some exceptions. :: Chang: ‘Here’s the deal Siv my man, you go out and get everyone to sign the possessions and belonging contract, as you did yourself, however, all those wanting to join must wait for me to meet with them.’ :: Sivasian strokes his chin for a second , deep in thought. :: Krikorian: ‘Sure then sir! We’ll meet at Tuesday Turmoil, when I need to speak to you again, or perhaps I’ll speak to you before?’ :: ‘Dear god! Stop calling me man!’ screams the leader of the Society of Order and Logic inside his head, before calmly coughing. :: Chang: ‘Well, you see err….of course, you can phone me some other time. If the lines busy, it’s because I’m on the Internet.’ Krikorian: ‘But sir, you have cable, you don’t need a phone line?’ :: Chang creases and slams his fist, before placing the telephone down, and disconnecting the phone line connection. :: Chang: ‘Excellent, no more calls from our man until I meet him on Tuesday!’ :: Sivasian took the head piece off and sighed. He felt that Chang was getting more and more annoyed with him in terms of his constant phoning. He felt left out though, Chang was ‘treating’ SOCIETY to an all expenses paid trip around the world, mainly with the money Sivasian had contributed. However, he didn’t know the extent of the boredom and jet lag that the continuing traveling had caused to SOCIETY, and the fact he probably wouldn’t want to put his own body through that. :: Krikorian: ‘Right, this Tuesday, it’s a 9 man elimination match. Matt Jackson? Oh sorry, ‘agent’ Matt Jackson. Been there, done that, brought the tee shirt, had my left ear slightly burnt by a grenade thrown, nothing special, he hasn’t even been a real agent in the REAL world, has he? But this Syn character, he interests me, and what interests him? The Church you say? Right, I’m off.’ :: Sivasian walked to his closet, he pulled his usual black trench coat from a hook, and placed it over his shoulders. He grabbed his wallet and his keys, and opened the door. :: :: The stroll to the church was an enjoyable one for him. The sun shone, the birds cheeped in their trees, and many beautiful young ladies said ‘hello’ and winked. Even though he was still married to his wife, he still felt great at his age, for his ability to get woman was still high and up their with the best of them. :: :: The church was old, and had a tombstone ground, a graveyard, to it’s right. The church was big too, with molded and old stone being the outlay for it’s fortifications. Several Japanese tourists are lurking outside, cameras at the ready. As Sivasian walks past, and hell, this guy doesn’t look normal, they start snapping pictures. How often do you have a guy wearing a long black trench coat, and who’s abnormally large in public. You don’t often find someone who’s 6’3 and 245 walking around, ya know. Sighing as he comes up to the church, Sivasian steps on in, and immediately feels the coldness hit his body. :: :: As he stands, he is reminded of the cold upbringing his childhood had, from it’s physical coldness of hunting in the snow, to the numbness and sudden burst of cold which swept over him when he heard about his parents deaths and the way in which, he believes, the Americans contributed when he put 2 and 2 together. He shudders for a second, and then blurts out. :: Krikorian: ‘Damn it! It’s so friggin cold!’ :: A majority of the people in the church turn to face him with frowns on their faces. Sivasian shrugs as a modern day priest, clad in a Bayern Munich soccer shirt, but with a elasticized white collar, comes over towards him. :: Priest: ‘Guten tag! Kann ich ihnen helfen?’ :: Struggling for a second, Sivasian tries to remember the most basic of German that he can remember from being stationed in the communist part of West Germany. He winches for a few seconds, before saying something. :: Krikorian: ‘Er…ja?’ Priest: ‘Super!’ :: Krikorian sieves through the information in his brain, before it finally clicks. :: Krikorian: ‘Sprechen sie English?’ Priest: ‘Ja! Hello, welcome to the Church of Wolsburg! How can I help you?’ :: Krikorian sighs a brief sigh of relief, before getting back to business.:: Krikorian: ‘It’s a great place, I’ve read about this place in books before. ‘ Priest: ‘Superd! What do you like about it!’ :: Krikorian suddenly starts surveying the large upper chapels of the church, and nods to himself. :: Krikorian: ‘It is true! It’s beautiful!’ Priest: ‘Aren’t the glass windows just excellent?’ Krikorian: ‘Not them you god shagging freak, the friggin upper wings are as good for sniping as I was told! I can imagine those hard fought German soldiers sitting up their, and sniping my Ukrainian comrades, but if you can pick out such a great sniping spot, you’re more than deserved of it!’ :: The priest is slightly confused, but shrugs and turns to walk away. :: Krikorian: ‘Oh, by the way, any of you religious guys, the ones with a bit of, ya know, money, want to join a weird but engrossed cult?’ Priest: ‘Er…no, sorry.’ Krikorian: ‘You’d be missing out, trust me!’Priest: ‘No, really, I don’t think so.’ :: Krikorian scratches his head and looks over towards some monks. Seeing gold chains around their necks he smiles. :: Krikorian: ‘Them?’ :: The priest growls and shouts back aggressively. :: Priest: ‘NO! NO ONE WANTS TO JOIN YOUR CULT! GOD FORBIDS IT!’ :: Krikorian flips his hand to push the priest away. :: Krikorian: ‘Phht, God says you can’t do anything anymore. Thank god I never became a Christian, I mean come on. I can’t kill anyone, yet I was paid to? Figure that out! Now, who has some money? Maybe a business meeting of some sort? Some large office…BILL GATES!’ :: Krikorian smirks as he walks out the church, muttering some evil, money swaggering dreams to himself. ::
