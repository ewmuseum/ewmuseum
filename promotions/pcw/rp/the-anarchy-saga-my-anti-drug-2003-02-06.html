The Anarchy Saga: My Anti-Drug...

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-6-03 The scene faded in, a battle was being fought. Bombs were being dropped, tanks rolling across a desert sand, crashing across the oasis in the center and destroying it for all eternity. Something that had stood there longer then any one person on the battle field. The footage was grainy, it seemed to be from some far off war. One long forgotten. American soldiers sprinted across the wasteland, slowing being picked off... they were all faceless, coated in nothing but numbers. Every bit of blood was considered only ink that painted the books of a statistical breakdown. Maybe if these fallen soldiers were lucky, their statistics would be deemed more important then other wars statistics, and maybe their statistics would get a wall somewhere where names would be put. This would be if they were lucky. If they were lucky, America would turn them into a tourist attraction where people can come and stare at the dead and watch the families cry. This was as close to war as alot of these pissant idiots would get. War is nothing to them except a prempted television show. Its not the intelligent ones, the intelligent ones debate, they complain, they organize, they march... most importantly though, they rebel, they take their rights and make new ones. They defend the civil liberties that were made a hundred years ago. Those aren't the ones I hate. Those are the people I side with, the intelligent, the strong, the brave. What I hate is the people sitting down in backwater hickville South Arkansas who wait for everything Bush will say about Iraq or about Korea or about our failing Economy and they take it for exactly what he said. They don't research, they just blindly follow the idiotic. When he says the economy is fine, they buy a new trailer. When he says the democrats are bad, they vote against them. When he says that we need to fight Iraq, these hill living fuckers paint 'Ready 4 War' on the back of their 88 Ford Broncos, load up a shotgun, and drive down to the Gas station and harrass the owners. The owners who aren't even from the Middle East. The owners that are Asian or Indian, they take it out on them because they are far too stupid to comprehend things like nationality. This is your fucking America. The ignorant and the proud. This is what you defend. This is what you say you will hold the flag up for. It's a disgrace. You see these grainy war photos, the death, the blood, the carnage. This is what we face, all because your president's dad got his dick shoved in his own mouth in 1991. 1991... Can you understand this? That was 12 years ago. The people that are going to make this war are barely old enough to REMEMBER that. They can't even comprehend what they are fighting for. And neither can you. So stop trying... raise your little skinny fists in the air and scream your convictions at the top of your lungs. I have the powers to give you those strengths... You know it. This isn't your war. But we can make it your country. Turn the camera off, I'm done. Truth
