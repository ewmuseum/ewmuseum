The Scottish Play: Act one, scene three

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/11/02 Act One, Scene Three. Three small figures can be seen sitting just outside the barriers of a construction site. The shadow of a huge crane swoops over them and drops it's load elsewhere, thundering down onto the ground. With the silence shattered, the first speaks up. He's clad in a two squares of wood painted white, with 'Sponsored By' on one side and 'Sponsored by Gatorade' painted on the other. 'So, what have you been up to?' he asks, toying with an object in his hand. The second, a lady with auburn hair looks down at a piece of paper in her hands. 'Making money.' she says, smirking at the paper. The word 'Divorce' can be read clearly. 'And you?' says the third. He's of dark skin tone and has a cocky smile. The man in the sandwich board grins. 'A women was drinking some of that filthy Snapple.I wanted to protect her from the poison and shouted GIMME at her. She gave me the bird, but her husband works right here, at Aleppo Construction on that crane with the tiger painted on it. And he's going to fuck him more than she'll ever have the opportunity... just a different way of doing it.' Natalia smiles and takes a wrench from beside her. 'A little present... should come in handy.' She smirks. Vilas bashes his fists together and flexes a bicep. 'I want to help out...' he grins, but Skitzo waves a hand in denial. 'No, I have everything I need... just a quick sneak in, a few screws undone here, a wrench in the works there and he'll have sleepless nights for the rest of his life. Besides, I'm in the mood. Look what else I did...' Skitzo. Natalia's interest perks, trying to see what was in Skitzo's hands. 'Hurry and let us see!' the woman hisses in delight. Skitzo opens his palms and the other two gather to see what looks like the brake pedal for a car, sawn off roughly. 'Let's just say that as for that guy that skipped the lights in his car, he'll have a hard time making it home in one piece.' the Mental One purrs. Suddenly, the sound of 'Where's Your Head At?' by Basement Jaxx comes from a car in the distance, gripping the attention of the trio of misfits. Skitzo's eyes open wide and his mouth follows suit. 'Heeeeeeeeeeeeeeere's Bastardos!!' The three speak at precisely the same time, closing their eyes as though in a trance. 'Now, the three of use, together as one, know what we must do. Prepare... the stage is set!' Stepping out of the car, Gaz and Nerezza take a look around. The Jester glances at a sheet of paper with directions crudely scrawled on them. Gaz takes a look at the dusty construction site and pushes his green sunglasses up to shield his eyes from the particles of crumpled building. 'Nice place, eh Squire? Shame about the decor.' he coughs. Nerezza checks the instructions once more and checks around for any landmarks. 'Guess they don't know the local area too well.' Suddenly, the trio of Skitzo, Natalia and Vilas catches his eye. 'What in Vorador's name? People say I have problem with the way I dress and act? Ugh, just when I thought I'd never see another vampire besides myself again, these freaks give me flashbacks. Seems they want me quiet too... don't know if I should listen to this bunch of sideshow rejects.' Gaz begins to grow impatient as they simply stare back, not saying a word. 'You've got tongues, use 'em or lose 'em!' The Jester frowns. Skitzo points directly at him and narrows his eyes. 'Congratulations, Tag Team Champion.' The Mental One proclaims. 'Congratulations, Cruiserweight Champion.' The russian beauty gently moans. 'Congratulations, PCW World Champion.' Vilas cackles. 'Hey, human-types! Could you act any more moronic than you already are? You're issuing all these predictions, tell ME what's in store... or is your usual human ignorance going to show through?' Nerezza spits with anger at the odd clairvoyants. 'Congratulations...' Skitzo murmurs. 'Congratulations.' Natalia winks. 'Congratulations.' Vilas whispers. 'You'll be the weak link of the team... yet carry it.' 'You'll be the gloomy one... but smile all the time.' 'You'll not be in the hall of fame... but be remembered forever.' the group of soothsayers giggle. Skitzo raises his arms into the air and begins laughing loudly. 'So congratulations, Gaz Green and Nascosto Nerezza!' The three then turn and make their way towards a shed. Gaz follows, confused and demanding more of an answer. 'Easy, Tiger, no need to disappear! I know we've survived Kannon's booking to try and shake the tag titles but how am I a Cruiserweight champion? Duke's holding the belt tight and the tournament's not finished! And PCW World champ? That's stretching things a little bit, I'm as much World Material as the Pimply Faced Intern Kid is! Where did you find this out? Why are you telling me this!?!' The Jester screams, opening the shed door to find no more than a few tools and spiders. Nerezza thuds behind him, staring into the empty shed. 'I'm a vampire. Don't question that they genuinely disappeared. Maybe every legend of monsters and the paranormal has some truth behind it...' he quietly groans. 'Yeah, they just... boom, like a magician. Wish they'd said more before leaving now...' Gaz sighs. 'Perhaps Duke slipped something bad into our drinks as revenge for the time we shoved laxative in his wine? I swear that orange juice wasn't one hundred percent pure.' Nerezza says with a look of worry on his face. Gaz simply stares into the shed, still captivated by the prophecy laid before him. 'Well... y-y-you're a living legend.' He stammers through a dry throat. 'And you're PCW World Champion...' Nerezza replies with a skeptic tone. A set of footsteps treads up behind the unwitting pair and the scene fades slowly to darkness, leaving them both mystified...
