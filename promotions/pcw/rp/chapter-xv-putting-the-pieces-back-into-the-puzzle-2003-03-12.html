Chapter XV: Putting The Pieces Back Into The Puzzle....

12 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-3-03 ::Introduction:: The thoughts that Jax have told Mark are taken to the heart by himself. Mark now knows what to do with the spics and how to get them to try to make then believe. He has been trying hard for these past couples of days down in Mexico, but as Jax said that is not going to work since the minorities down here are all the same. There is not any white supremacy down here and now its time to find some. Mark is going to another epic journey to find the right place. Now we continue back to Mark’s history and how he became to hate the spics. ::End Introduction:: Chapter XV: Putting The Pieces Back Into The Puzzle…. Mark dials the numbers to the taxi cab on his cell phone keypad. He walks out on the stones once again as the darkness is coming upon the whole country of Mexico. The light going through the trees is getting less and less, like it didn’t show much before. Mark puts the cell phone up to his ear as the phone starts to make a ring sound. The other phone on the other end starts to ring as a guy picks it up. “Hello.” The familiar Pedro voice says. “I am ready.” Mark says as he sounds like he is confident now. Mark hits the end button on the cell phone with his pointer finger. He flips the phone down and he stuffs it back into his front jean pocket. He starts to walk across the gravel more thinking about what Jax told him and thinking about a plan on how he can get to the spics. The thoughts are running around and he wonders why he hasn’t went back to see Gangra lately. He hasn’t seen him since he entered the PCW last year. Mark gets to the end of the drive and he doesn’t see the taxi waiting at all so he just takes a seat there in the gravel. “Jax really did give me a reality check in this past meeting.” Mark starts to say as he picks up a couple of rocks and starts to toss them into the woods. “When you are a racist then you have to do everything possible to make the minority feel like they are shit. They should feel like they shouldn’t belong in the world and I have not accomplished that yet.” Mark looks down at the rocks in his hands and he feels the power that they give him. He feels that they are special in someway and can use them against the spic minority. The yellow taxi cab of Pedro’s pulls up alongside of where Mark is sitting at. Mark does not notice the cab as he is in too deep of a thought with the rocks to look up. Pedro pushes the power window button on the passenger window so that it goes down. “Are you coming?” Pedro says as he sticks his head over to the passenger window. Mark doesn’t say anything. Instead he keeps on studying the rocks that he has in his hand. Pedro then goes to the steering wheel and he pushes down hard on the middle of it. The sound of the horn startles Mark as he jumps a little bit off of the ground. He looks up at the yellow cab and he sees Pedro inside waving his hand for him to get inside the cab. “I’m coming dammit.” Mark says as he gets up from the ground. “You spics can’t wait two damn minutes when you get here for me to get ready.” Mark puts the rocks in his other front pocket, not the one with the cell phone in it. He pats his front pocket so he makes sure that all the rocks are in there. He then walks towards the yellow cab as he opens up the back door and he looks back into the dark driveway. He thinks of his good friend Jax as he nods his head and he gets in the back seat of the cab. “Where are we heading?” Pedro asks as he looks at Mark like he usually does, in his rearview mirror. “Just drive you damn spic and I will fucking tell you when to stop!” Mark raises his voice at Pedro as he is trying to act like he doesn’t want to be around these people. “It was just a question.” Pedro says as he starts to drive. “Did I say you can ask a damn question?” Mark asks as he leans back in the back seat. “No.” Pedro says a little uncertain. “Then don’t talk to me, just do your damn job.” Mark says as he starts to close his eyes for another journey coming up his way. Once again Mark sits in the back of the taxi cab with no worries in his mind. He only has a plan that will get him as far as he wants it to. The darkness is calming Mark as he was getting heated up talking to the damn spic Pedro. This will be the last meeting that he will have with his drive as he is going to be getting rid of him real soon. Thoughts of the plan that Mark has is driving in his mind as his brain is not going to sleep, only his physical body. He is worn out from the day. He has had a lot taken out of him, with the café and then the meeting with Jax has really thrown his mind for a loop. Tomorrow is going to be just another day in the life of the racist Mark Makonie.
