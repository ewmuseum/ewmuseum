Cable Access Marking...Ugh...

undated

Date: meh...who knows... 'There is no greater way to defeat a man than to take away his complete means of existence. Then he is nothing...' ---Savage Tranquility, page undetermined The days dawn slowly upon each and every one of us. And with that new dawning, we start yet another process of toiling and grinding our bodies further and further forward towards a single common goal. What we may feel this goal may be differs entirely from what it truly is. Just as people say that their life goal is to become rich and famous...or to make a nice life for themselves. Well...they all have one thing that they need to realize this day. All of your goals...are nothing. Do you need to know the unified goal of everything that exists in this realm? 'Death.' Yes, no matter what happens, death is your final goal. You cannot stop it, no matter how hard you try. You cannot escape it...it is the collective finale for everything that exists from this realm. Animals, plants, humans...all doomed to die. But what of gods? Are they doomed to die as well? No. Gods do not die...they merely are...erased from existence. You see...that is exactly what is going to happen come this Sunday to The Pharaoh. He will not die...no, death is too good for him. There will be no escape such as death that will keep him from feeling the humiliation and disgrace that he will face when he is stripped of his godhood. His existence will be erased from the registrar of deities listed in the Deist's League...and then he will be nothing... Nothing more than a stupid...pathetic...worthless mortal. It doesn't even matter about the Television title...or about his skank-shark of a wife Isiy...being a god is everything that Pharaoh lives for. It is his embodiment...his heritage. Taking this away from him will be the greatest striking blow that the Neo Void God can deal towards his traitorous partner. The TV belt...it was leather and metal...worthless materialistic possessions. If he felt that taking an item away from the god was going to make him any more of a god, then he was more shallow a god than most of those on Mt Olympus. Pharaoh's wife...a piece of meat kept around for her face, her chest, and what resided in her pelvic area. As if there wasn't enough trash around earth... No...those two things were nothing...compared to taking away the livelihood of the man you hate. Once that godhood was taken from Pharaoh...there would be no doubt at all about who was beneath who's level. After all...what kind of war would this be if there weren't some casualties and sacrifices? But without further ado...let us begin. Akiyama wandered through the streets alone. What else could he do? He had no home in the United States, and he had been banished from Japan. But wandering proved to be a sort of meditation for him, allowing himself to wander aimlessly on through the city while letting his thoughts do the same. Training is his prime directive for the God Wars coming up at Spring Fling...but if you think about it, how many gyms are you going to find that prove to be sufficiently stocked to suit the god's training needs? Not a lot...honestly. This is why he walks now...since physical training cannot be dealt with at this moment...psychological means would have to suffice until otherwise. The streets were slightly crowded that afternoon with pedestrians and cars, both of them moving along back and forth towards their destinations unknown to us all except to themselves. But Akiyama had no destination...unless you counted the path to victory that he was going to trek through later on come this Sunday. But if you do not...then Akiyama had nowhere to go. He stuck out from the others like a pyramid in Nebraska; first of all his size causing him to be different. Second, his apparel of black multi-zippered vinyl, black silver buckled boots, and black mesh shirt causing him not to blend into the society that he would rather not be a part of. Third, his gray and black hair braided in a ponytail, the color on him looking quite unique on a near thirty-year old god. Stares and glances were a definite...no matter what...just as they would be definite after the beating that Akiyama would give Pharaoh...though people would be wise not to stare into the wreck that the NVG was going to mold the Egyptian into. The days approach, he thought to himself. The Sabbath...where two gods shall come to terms with each other to see who is the true god of PCW...to see who is man enough for Isiy...and to see who is worthy of being a televised god. Three falls...three chances to totally beat the complete crap out of this measly infidel...and we will totally enjoy it. And it was there that the sudden chime of the Divine Prophetess rang into his head, stirring up his thoughts within him. {~And you are worried about this match with Pharaoh why?~} 'We are not worried.' he muttered softly under his breath. 'It helps our focus to stay in constant thought on the battle at hand. What society thinks to be worry, we consider it thorough preparation.' {~I would call it worry, Akiyama. But what do I know?~} He sighed a bit. 'Worry involves doubt...which there is none of within us concerning our ability to entomb Pharaoh in a sarcophagus of pain on that fated night. If there is any doubt about the match...it is the doubt that he may even be able to retrieve his wife back from us. And that is something that we wouldn't mind too much...she could become quite useful.' {~Don't you mean what's between he legs could become useful?~} There was a slight bit of disgust within Cyclonica's voice when she asked that. Akiyama stretched his lips back in something that could have been considered a small smirk if you could notice it. 'Well...not only that, but there are other openings that could be put to work as well.' A sigh came from her voice hard. Even her sighs had an ethereal tone to them, sounding like a wisp of wind blowing across the mouth of a hollow tube. Akiyama took notice of how she took his statement and shook his head. 'If you're going to make such a big deal about it, you should know anyway that we're probably going to just keep her banished and be done with her. There's only so much we can take from her jackal-like face and her sandpaper-like skin. We have no need for her physical presence. She'll act as a trophy...always reminding us of the complete raping of Pharaoh's dignity, pride, and existence.' {~What's it matter to you anyway? You know you're totally better than Pharaoh, so why prove it?~} 'Geez, Cyclonica, for the Divine Prophetess, you sure ask some dumb@ss questions.' {~Humor me. I like to hear answers from people directly except for going on the hunches I get.~} And right on schedule, the stares were coming from the citizens around Akiyama who watched him talk to himself in public. He paid them no mind...it's not like they could hear the voice inside his head. He snarled a bit at a young couple staring, scattering them off down the street. Then he continued on his way. 'Well...we know we're better than Pharaoh Punk@ss...but he doesn't know it...and therefore we will have to enlighten him on just why we happen to be the Neo Void God...and just why the NVG is absolutely better than him and his Egyptian jackal gods.' 'Hey!' a voice called out. {~And so you're looking to prove your dominion over one man to that very same man, as well as the rest of PCW?~} 'In-frickin-deed. They'll learn to respect and fear the Neo Void God once this match is through.' 'Hey!' it called out again. {~Are you that self-conscious of your image that you need to prove anything to that lot of mortals in PCW?~} The god stopped dead in his tracks at those words. He folded his arms across his chest and closed his eyes, his brow wrinkled in aggravation. 'Cyclonica...this is by no means about our image. This is about taking it to the one person that we had thought to be our friend in this battleground. This is about completing the circle of revenge that we always have completed ever since Itagaki Freeze and those ingrates betrayed us at Alliances...and those International Imbeciles as well. Our allies stay true to the method of operation of backstabbing the Neo Void God...so therefore it is not just a matter of pride on the line. It's a matter of completing the circle of revenge that we are known for completing against those who decide that their egos are going to lead them into punishment at our hands.' {~Revenge? You're using revenge as a motive? I hope you know that he's using that same motive as well.~} 'Please,' Akiyama scoffed. 'Pharaoh didn't even have a decent reason why he's even defying us before we took his wife and banished her. There is yet another point where we had to act as his god and lead him towards the right direction. Ever since his start in PCW, we've been leading him into Divine greatness...but then he had to bugger off and grow an ego, making him nothing more than a self-loving, glory-grubbing moron.' {~Look, we can continue this later. I have some business to tend to.~} 'Fine. We'll talk again later.' {~Oh...by the way...there's some mortal clinging to your leg. I'd take care of that if I were you.~} Glaring down suddenly, the NVG became aware of the grungy man holding onto him by his ankle with both hands. Akiyama lifted his leg horizontally, raising the man into the air. Then with one hand, he pulled him off of him and held him up in the air by the nape of his neck. 'Who are you, who sent you, and why do you wish to scream in The Darkness?' The ratty haired man with his black-framed glasses and his tie-dyed shirt stammered on to respond to Akiyama. 'Well...I'm Scotty...and I-I was wondering if you would like to appear on my cable access TV show?' The brow of the god arched on his forehead. 'TV…show?' It was next that the god found himself sitting in the raunchy basement of someone's home out in the lower dwellings of the city. The room was dark, save for the poor lighting above and the weak spotlights standing in the back of the room. Behind the cheap camera, there was a few chairs set up for the audience; where it seemed like there were a few punk kids and rocker-chicks slouched back smoking something that most certainly wasn't tobacco. The strong aroma was like a kick in the face as he entered the room. 'What is that obscene smell?' he asked, his nose wrinkled at the smell of the smoke. 'Smells like someone's been mummified with manure for almost a couple millennia.' Scotty sniffed the air and looked at him innocently. 'I don't smell anything. Here, you come sit over here in the chair next to mines and get ready. We'll be rolling in about three minutes.' The god sat down in a chair that looked like it was whittled out cork and stared out at the youth in the audience. They looked like they desired to be there just as much as he did. One pair of kids were over in the corner going at it like the world was about to end and this was their last moment left with each other. Akiyama sighed as he went to break up the two longhaired lovers. 'Hey…filthy lust-ridden maggots…cease all of this meaningless libido acts right now! We're not here to sit here and watch you lick your gums. You're here to watch us speak to you about how much of a p^ssy Pharaoh is.' He got no response, forcing him to nudge them with his finger. 'Hey! Are you even listening to us, you crotch-grabbing mortals? Cut it out before we banish you both to the Void!!' The two teens stopped kissing and look up at the god. Akiyama gasped in surprise as he stared down at two long haired, fairy-faced boys, their hands groping each others' bodies like life preservers. The god backed away slowly, his hands up in defense. 'The horror…' he whispered in fear. 'The Ream Effect begins young…' behind him, Scotty looked around with a handful of index cards in his hands. 'Where's Akiyama?' as he turned to search more, he smacked face first into the retreating god's back. He clattered to the ground, looking back up to find Akiyama glaring down at him with a look of disgust. 'What kind of frickin' show do you have going on here? There's gays in the corner making out…the lighting here's horrible…and it smells funny in here! We are the Neo Void God and the god of television! We do not deserve this!' Scotty stands back up and shakes his head. 'Calm down, Akiyama sir. We're just reaching a different level of the demographic with this show. Here…let's begin the show.' With a mutter of disgust, the god sat back down in his seat, his large frame nearly shattering the seat from the weight. Scotty sat down next to him and gave a signal to the cameraman, who put down the porno magazine and started filming. The sound of applause rang out…not from the actual audience, but from a speaker above. Akiyama looked up at the speaker sharply. 'What the freak?' Scotty smiled as he began to talk. 'Welcome to another great episode of 'SCOTTY'S MARK-OUT!!' On this show, like always, we take the time out to talk about people about people that they totally love…or 'MARK' for.' Akiyama sighed, his head bowing in shame. 'Son of a frickin' b!tch.' 'This show's a special one, because we have a real life wrestler with us! Ladies and gentleman…let's give a warm welcome to the Neo Void God…Akiyama!' Scotty stood up and clapped as the applause rang out from the speakers again. The real audience stared drolly as Akiyama glared up at Scotty. 'Sit your hippie @ss down!!' he growled. Scotty complied immediately, the smile never leaving his face, though his eyes grew a bit flustered. 'So, Akiyama, tell us why you're here.' 'You know d@mn well why we're here, you monkey brained mutant. You nearly blew a blood vessel begging us to be on this craptasitc program, though you failed to mention that it was so poorly organized.' Scotty smiled on however. 'It's a funny thing. I was walking and I saw Akiyama walking along talking to himself. So I tried to get his attention… but he just walked right over me…literally. I think I bruised my spleen when he stepped on me.' 'Quit squawking, mortal. We never told you to stand in our way. If you're just as stupid as Pharaoh to stand in our way, you too will be squashed like a bug.' 'Speaking of Pharaoh, what is your take on the match at hand? Last Friday, you lost your TV title to Pharaoh in a strange match with Clyde and Badge as well. Has this lost affected you in any way whatsoever?' The god glared into him for a few seconds before replying. 'Shut your bong hole, you smelly hippie! If you think that Pharaoh's little miniscule win over us is going to phase us into screwing up at the PPV, you're CELESTIALLY wrong! The TV title's meaningless really, because even if he is champion right now, he wont be come Sunday. And even if he does manage to stay true to being the champ after the PPV…we all know who the true god of PCW will be no matter what. And with us being the Divine god of PCW…being the god of television will already fall into play.' 'But if Pharaoh retained the TV title, wouldn't that make him the god of television instead of you no matter what?' Scotty replied innocently, trying to strike up conversation for the already slipping audience. Akiyama gave him one of those looks that crossed confusion and aggravation. 'Are you retarded or just high right now? Look at the facts: who's on PCW televised programming more, Pharaoh, or the Neo Void God? Answer...the NVG! Next question: Who's always out there on camera about 93% of the time every show? Answer...the NVG! Final question: who's the god with the most devastating of techniques in all of PCW? Answer...the NVG! The Void Banishment is feared throughout the land! And it is we who are out there banishing the trash of PCW for the sake of better television! Banishing Isiy and Carmoon was just part of our job as television god. Let's see that selfish self-righteous bastard Pharaoh do anything to make TV better!' Scotty looked into the camera with an astonished face. “Did you all just hear that? Akiyama just cut a promo for all of us right here live!! Let’s clap that up for the Neo Void God!!” the room roared in applause...well, canned applause, actually. Half of the real audience was either gone, asleep, making out, or too high to know what was going on. Akiyama let out a growl, looking to begin rising from his seat. “STOP USING THAT FAKE-@SS APPLAUSE!!!” the audio man quickly shut it off, his frightened yelp crying from off camera. Scotty looked around nervously before moving on. “Well anyway...let’s move on to Point-Counterpoint. In this segment, we’ll have Akiyama face off against Pharaoh in a heated debate.” The god’s eyes whirl around, his fists clenching at the sound of his enemy’s name. “Pharaoh’s here? Bring him out so he can receive a CELESTIAL HAND OF FATE from the Neo Void God!!” From off camera stepped a small, scrawny tanned man with a shaved head, save the small braided ponytail at the base of his neck. He was dressed in a black towel...nothing more. Along his eyes was the black paint that lined his sockets. He looked out to the crowd and yelled before glaring up at Akiyama. The god took a glance at the smaller man, looked back at Scotty...and then turned back to the man again. “Pharaoh...you’ve gotten smaller...and thinner...and uglier...but on another note that three thousand year old morning breath you had ever since you awakened from your tomb is gone. Finally discovered the modern invention of Scope, eh?” “That’s not THE Pharaoh, Akiyama.” Scotty said with a nerdy snort in his chuckling voice. “That’s my producer, Ramsey. We call him Pharaoh because he’s a big Pharaoh mark!” Akiyama glared at the scruffy Scotty, cutting off his chuckling immediately. “Are you laughing at the N...V...G?” He stopped in mid laugh and began the segment, clearing his throat. “Now...we’ll get on towards our topics. Ramsey’s topic is...” he looked down at the card, checking the topic. “Akiyama, you suck, and Akiyama’s topic is...” checks the card again. “No, I don’t.” The eyebrow arched on the god’s face. “No...WE don’t.” “Sorry.” Scotty replied. “Akiyama, you go first.” “Very well. Now, we know that Ramsey here is a Pharaoh mark, and it is truly divine. It is the marks that truly make up the populous of the audience...therefore making the sport so profitable. But we hope Ramsey can see that we go out there and fight with everything we have in us, and we always do our best to dominate that opponent across from us.” Scotty nodded. “Ok...Ramsey...it’s your turn.” Ramsey nodded and drew in a deep breath to respond. “YOU SUCK!!!” Akiyama burned a hole into the little man after his outburst. Scotty stared at them as Ramsey leaned back in his chair feeling like he accomplished something. “Uh...Ramsey...you still have like thirty seconds left to respond” “Oh...ok. I’ll elaborate” he leaned forward again and prepared to talk again. “Akiyama...you suck on three very specific ways: YOU SUCK DIVINELY...CELESTIALLY...AND GODLY!!!” “Akiyama...you can respond.” The god stared down at Scotty, that aggravated/confused look returning. “Well...we’ve heard this argument before. As a matter of fact, Pharaoh marks have been so kind as to share this argument us that on the streets...along with Timo marks...Big Alan Steele marks...Boda marks...and it seems like everyone else’s marks as well. But if you look at our win/loss ratio, and the championships and accomplishments that we’ve earned...you’ll find us to be quite a force to be reckoned with...” “AAAAAAAAKKIIIIIII....AAAAAAAAAKKIIIIIII....AAAAAAAKKKIIIIII...” Ramses was sitting up close to Akiyama’s ear, jeering him as he talked. Akiyama whipped his head around suddenly, his ponytail smacking him in his face. “Hey! What the freak is wrong with you? You want a banishment or something??” Ramsey lowered his head defeated. “I’m sorry...Akiyama...I am, I really am. It’s just that you and people like you ruined my childhood, as well as my entire PCW viewing and marking experience. I swore that if I ever got within six feet of you, that I would kick your @ss for Pharaoh. But then... I saw you...and I knew that could never happen. But this can happen...YOU SUCK!!!” “Ramsey...we don’t suck. We’re the longest reigning Tag Team champion in PCW history, along with having three titles under our belt.” Ramsey thought about those facts. “Valid point...but if there was a championship of sucking...you’d have held that title for the longest time EVER!!” The god’s patience was wearing thin. “Look, Pharaoh Punk@ss is said to be totally skilled and talented and all that crap, but is it okay to say that he sucks?” Ramsey’s face sunk into sadness. “Whu...whu...whu...what did you say that for?” tears began welling up in his eyes. “Pharaoh’s been fired and he’s so nice and he’s like my best friend and everything...” he sniffed and stuff, preparing to cry his marking heart out. Akiyama looked away from the sobbing mark, a huge sigh breaking through his lips. “By the Void...we’re sorry, you sad sack of sh!t.” he muttered. “Quit your b!tching. What do we have to do to get you to stop your prissy Pharaoh-like sobbing?” His crying simmered down a bit as his eyes looked back up to the NVG. “Will you job to Pharaoh at Spring Fling?” “NO!!” the god bellowed. Ramsey shrugged, slinking back into his chair. “Well, will you sign my skull for me?” he asked, a pen in hand? The god took the pen with a big look of exasperation on his face. “Very frickin well...” “Will you sign it “Pharaoh”?” Ramsey snuck in quickly. “Yeah...sure...whatever.” he began signing away. {~Do you spell “Punk@ss with a hyphen or without?~} The NVG started snickering as he signed away. Then he sat back with a smile on his face from Cyclonica’s remark. “Thanks Akiyama, you don’t suck that much.” Ramsey admitted. “Whatever. You DO suck though.” “Yes, sir.” He said with defeat. Akiyama snarled as he stood up from his seat. “We’re though here. We hope your feeble mortal flesh rots in the abyss of flames.” with that said, the god stormed out of the studio back into the streets. Scotty stared into the camera blankly...before that stupid sheepish grin came back on his face. “Well...I wanna thank you all for tuning into SCOTTY’S MARK OUT, with special guest Akiyama! Tune in next time...when our special guest will be none other than our liege and universal mark-out professional, Havok! GOODNIGHT!!”
