Misplaced... eh, eh, eh?

June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 2002 Misplaced..What a bizarre name for a Tag Team... don't you think? I mean, come on... for all of a few seconds, could you really relate to being called that name? Hell, can you really even bring yourself to being called 'It'? 'Misplaced' is a term that one like myself would use when trying to explain why an item of clothing has magically gone missing... or why one of my favortie pens isn't in the case that I could have swore it had been left in. Why such a weird name for a weird team... its something that not even Indiana Jones would dare to find out... even with all of his items and what not, its nothing something our hero Harrisson would be willing to come back to collect. However, whilst many of you are looking for the second part of a documentary that should be been aired today... that should have been aired at this very moment in time... for reasons unknown to man, that segment has now been delayed until further notice... Why you wonder... well, for the time being, the fans who want to find out about the band... they can wait... because right now, we have a few more important agendas to attend to... namely, the matches against Misplaced this coming week on Turmoil... and the contest against the Cowabunga Connection following that on Friday Fusion. Now, you may be begging for the installment of whatever Pretty Cool show to take place... and in short terms, thats exactly where you will be going... next anyway!It was a warm summers day in the confinements of a town... so very far away! I was amazed at the fact that I couldn't remember the location that I was in... but just like everyone else before me, I blamed the fact that my sleeping hadn't been as comfortable as I would... and could have liked it to be. For instance, looking back at yesterday evening... it wasn't something that I would care to talk about... especially as I had this really bad nightmare... I was thinking that everything I placed down... was actually misplaced, when actually it hadn't been misplaced and then... well some Tag Team duo called Misplaced got told by some Phantom voice that they were going to do whatever... and well, that pretty much sums up the nightmare than I was forced to endure yesterday night.. However, stepping away from such crappyness, I bring forth the first sequence of events to happen on such a fine day. It all began in the third complex that belongs to the likes of Rich Sherwin (Thats Me) and Ryan Moore, we were sitting down at a futuristic looking table in the middle of a pretty good looking kitchen.... and yes, I know thats not really the kind of terminology that one should use when describing his kitchen, but if its the only stuff that comes to mind... well, can I help such a thing from taking place?? Regardless of how correct or incorrect my selection of words were, the two of us were sitting in the kitchen at the table with me being the one seated at the far end of the table while Ryan got to enjoy the life and times of being seated opposite... at the very spot that I had always dreamed of being at... about three feet away from the Fridge. I sat at the table, staring down into a bowl of Sporties... that had been amazingly infront of me since the beginning of this promo thing... its just that I never had the chance to realize they were infront of me at the time. I used my spoon to poke around at the cereal endorsed my England forward, Michael Owen and wondered why he had to ponce around with such a medicore cereal. Ryan could see that something was bothering me... and like the brotherly figure he was, the guy asked a question... and in terrible 'Parenthood' type too... 'Rich... you haven't touched an ounce of your breakfast... is something bothering you?', he asked... the reminder of that parenthood figure clinching on to every word that had flowed from his mouth. I glanced up from my cereal bowl, unable to bring myself to consume such horrific treats. Ryan looked into my eyes, a familiar trait of his when it came to attempting to faze people into telling them what was wrong. His eyes began to slowly move from side to side... I could sense what was going to come next and I looked away from him... the fact that he was going to mesmerize me into speaking clouding all logical thought... and then, in the truest form of the word, I had to counter with the ultimate countermeasure ever devised by the man... the animal... and... the Welsh... 'Yooooou know... this Booogie is for... Reeeeeeeaallll.... I used put my faith in worship... but then my chance to get to heav...', I belted out in all of its amazingly glory... taken from the track thats more commonly known as 'Virtual Insanity'.. something I would advise everyone and anyone to actually bother downloading at some point... however, with me belting out such a cracking song... Ryan Moore could do nothing but bow to the amazing power that was not PoP... you see, this was like Kryptonite to the boy... this was like a silver cross to a Vampire... or maybe even a Silver bullet to a Werewolf! Moore started to break out of his attacking 'Glare' and placed both hands over his ears... screaming out at the top of his voice... despite the fact that I'd finished my lyrics a few seconds ago... 'GEEEEEEEEEEEYYYYYAAAAAAAHHHHHHH', he shouted out in the most dramatic of fashions... ya know, if this was something that was being viewed by those people who deal with all of the Oscar Nominations... this certain segment is something that should be taken into consideration... viewed by a group of people that actually mean something... and then given a hint of glory that all the people of 'Primetime Championship Wrestling' know it deserves. I rolled my eyes.. trying to look intelligent... but ended up making myself look intelligently challenged, something that goes to show how retarded I generally am. Ryans voice continued to rip throughout the air waves like nothing that I'd ever been able to hear before. It was something that I didn't hold much happiness towards.. but it wasn't exactly something that I would go out to say that I hated. Ryan looked across the table at me and realized that nothing was coming from my mouth... he took another look to the side of the room to make sure that nobody else was saying anything either... before of course, stopping. I nodded my head to signify that the moment had well indeed passed... and that his decision to continue the mourning until he'd run out of breath... had effectivly killed ninty eight percent of our three people audience. At that point, I felt that was essential that we rewarded whatever two percent left with a show that was above all else... greater and larger than life itself... for our Patron Saints... 'The Backstreet Boys'... decided to proclaim...'... and THAT.. makes it larger... than LIFE!'... OK, ok, ok... so its not the greatest of comparision that I was looking for in this God Forsaken world... and so what if I just happened to possibly even get that line mixed up a little... I'm not even sure where, but I can promise you... somebody around the world will look upon that very line... laugh their tinky little ass off while proclaiming... 'Look at that chump... he couldn't even get a line from the Backstreet Boys right... God and he calls himself a PoP Star... who the hell gave him a career in this jig?!' 'Why didn't you tell me the moment had ended sooner?', he asked, looking a little worried about the way he laid himself out. I chuckled to myself a little, feeling somewhat happy at the retardness that my friend was once again in the middle of experiencing. He looked around the room again in a very self-concious manner and sighed loudly. I could not even begin to contain the emotion I was feeling at this point. 'Why should I have told you that the moment passed?', I shot back, expecting to hit him quickly and harshly with an 'Ultimate' rebound shot. He glanced over the table at me and shook his head softly from side to side... indicating that the comment I had just made was... so uncalled for. The patch of silence seemed to eventually pass as I realized how annoying it was to not have any verbal form of communication.. and decided to reopen the broadcast policy with the first 'installment'. 'Hmm... so, do you happen to know where the remote for the TV went... I mean, I'm pretty sure that I Misplaced it somewhere... unfortunently, I just can't figure out where the item was Misplaced..' The tone of my voice was very subtle at this point, trying to place extreme emphasis on 'Misplaced'... especially since they were going to be the very next Tag Team duo to face on our journey. Ryan Moores head shot up and he cringed at every mention of the word 'Misplaced'... just like the lyrics of the song before this, the mans sense was going crazy... his inability to even understand why such a perfect duo like ourselves had been placed into a pointless battle with... losers... had been planned to take place. In another moment of Oscar winning drama, Moore shot up from his seat, pushing the small chair that he had been sitting on across the room. Our hero arose highly and shot his arms out into the air... while managing to say something at the same time! 'WWWWWWHHHHHHYYYYYYY.... OH THE PAIN... OH THE TRAGEDY... just when I thought that we were about to get some of the exposure we deserve.... along comes another one of those..... whats the word?', he screamed out, taking a few seconds to look down at me and break away from the matter at hand. Flashing a sarcastic grin, I looked up and mouthed out the phrase, 'Jobber Team' before he nodded to indicate he'd recieved the responce. 'Now... where was I?... oh yes.... along comes another one of those... those... Tobber Jeams.... those freakin; Tobber Jeams... why.. oh why?!', he continued to scream before breaking down into a heap on the table, hitting the empty space beside his head. I looked over to my left, my eyes staring directly into the source that was taping this whole scene. I shook my head from side to side... pointing over to the guy in pain, mocking his little period of crybaby antics just seconds later. I stopped after a few moments and left the table, leaving my Tag Team partner to continue crying in his own way. Cautiously, I walked over to the screen... looking a little annoyed here and there about certain things... but otherwise generally OK. I tapped the window a few times before shifting myself so that I appeared to be in the middle of the screen. Casually, I began to talk in the tone that only many would like to think they could copy... you remember those episodes of 'Saved by the Bell' where Zack would talk to the viewers at home... well, bring forth the Richie moment.. 'You know..', I started to begin, looking back at Ryan Moore who was still hitting the table in tragic motion. The bowl of cereal that was just infront of him was now on the floor... my bowl was on the edge of the table, moving ever close to falling with every pound. I couldn't help but laugh at the fact he knew exactly where the bowl was... and in turn, knew exactly how much power to put into each one of those 'hits' so to speak. Anyway, returning my look to this little window of hope, I continued with, '... Ryan Moore is just having one of those episodes where you think you're on camera... but you're not... however, in this case, I think he found out a little bit of information from the thirteen year old girl that lives outside our front door. Regardless, the fact that I happened to talk about the Misplaced item was supposed to provoke my Tag Team partner into talking about them... thus leading to an uncanny plan... where I would think about stating that their team was ironically Misplaced... or something along those lines... Anywho... while in the Oscar mood... nothings going to happen... eh well, I'm sure something more interesting is happenning with Jordan Cr... I mean X Shabazz... and on that note, GOODNIGHT!!!... even though its the day still!'The scene faded out deep into black with Rich looking into the camera while his Tag Team partner hit the table with glee, rising when the other bowl had fallen from the high location and spilt on the floor. What was going to happen with their game plan moving into this weeks Turmoil show? Would they be able to do anything worthwhile in preperation for Misplaced or would they just have to dive in at the deep end and hope for the best? The answers to these questions... will make their happenning.... on PCW... TURMOIL!!!!
