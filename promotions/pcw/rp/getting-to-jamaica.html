Getting to Jamaica....

Date: 07/21/02 (PPV RP #1) (Anything in brackets is from Jordan X Shabazz’s point of view.) Last shot at a certain title. It's a stipulation you may have or may not have heard before. Jordan X Shabazz has had two previous shots at the gold. Both against his opponent for the Pay Per View. John Crichton, the current Television Champion, one of only two men who can say they've held the Television Title on more than one occasion. The first match that Jordan and John had, was a classic. A match that went the full fifteen minutes that the Television title allows. Shabazz had the victory in hand, when Jaden got on the apron and cost him the match. The second time around was much different. This time he lost, and it was his own fault. He had hurt his entire left shoulder, leaving him to be easy pickings for John Crichton, and his spinning pile driver. Now Shabazz has one last chance. One last chance to hold the title that he has been begging forever since his feud with John Crichton started, almost two months ago. Can he achieve his goal? Can he be the first African American (Timo is Samoan, not black. *Nod*) to ever hold the title? We'll have to wait until this Sunday to see... The scene opens up as we are boarding the ship that will take Jordan X Shabazz and the rest of the PCW superstars to Jamaica, the 'Wrestle Vessel.' The place is packed with tired and sweaty wrestlers who have just finished coming from a late PCW house show. We focus in on the threesome that is Thug roC, PCW Extreme Champion, Rush Mason, and the number one contender to the PCW Television Championship, Jordan X Shabazz. Rush is dressed for the occasion. Wearing baggy Caribbean looking shorts, and an open Hawaiian style t-shirt. In his right hand he clutches a book on Voodoo. Thug roC is also dressed for the occasion, dressed similar to Rush. In his right hand he clutches a camera. He plans on filming a few hours for the hit television series, roC TV. Jordan X Shabazz on the other hand, is NOT dressed for the occasion. He wears a long dark black suit. His hair is picked out into a small afro, and his thick horn rimmed glasses are on his face. In his right hand he clutches the skull of a dead white man… well… not really. Instead, he has a book that is really hot on the underground black scene. It’s entitled ’How to Kill White People and Get Away With It, In the Process Making Black People look bad all around the world’ by OJ Simpson. It has a foreword from Jonnie Cochran too! His shoes are freshly polished, and he wears a brand new Rolex watch, bought courtesy of N.I.G.G.A's earnings on the New York Stock Exchange. As they wait in line to get to the front, Jordan X Shabazz constantly begins checking his watch, trying to keep tabs on the time, and apparently waiting for somebody. Rush looks at Jordan, who is growing angrier and angrier by the second. Rush Mason: “What the hell is wrong with you, boy?” Jordan looks around, looking rather afraid. Jordan X Shabazz: “They’re not here man…” Jordan speaks eerily, as if he’s being watched. He looks Rush in the eyes, and we see fear in Jordan’s eyes for the first time since he heard that KFC was going out of business, a prank pulled by Rush. Rush Mason: “Who?” Jordan X Shabazz looks at Thug roC, and he speaks. Jordan X Shabazz: “You know who, right?” Thug roC shrugs his shoulders, obviously not knowing what the hell Jordan is talking about. Jordan X Shabazz: “Scott and Steve.” Scott and Steve were the two houseboys/butlers that Jordan X Shabazz owned… Erm… paid to be his servants. They did virtually everything you can think of for Jordan X Shabazz. From cooking, to cleaning, to walking Jordan’s dog, to lighting Jordan’s cigars, to washing Rush’s back… well… maybe not the last one. But it certainly makes Rush sound like a fruit. Thug roC: “You be trippin’ dog, straight up. I don’t even much know why you care so God damned much about two white boys.” Rush looks at Thug roC, knowing exactly why Jordan is ‘trippin.’’ Thug roC had yet to feel the good graces of Scott and Steve. He had not been treated to any of the luxurious things that Jordan and Rush had. Rush Mason: “RoC, you don’t know man. It’s hard for Jordan to survive without his white slaves. They do so much, and they give him the gratitude of knowing that he’s superior to crackers.” Thug roC sighs, not knowing what to think. He had changed Jordan and Rush permanently; they were now full-fledged evil, slave-trading bastards. Like the white man was to his people hundreds of years before. Thug roC: “…Uh… whatever. But if we brought them, there isn’t much we could do, due to the simple fact that we are going to be on a ship with maids and butlers.” Jordan X Shabazz grins ear to ear, his gruffy ass beard looking scrunched up. Jordan X Shabazz: “We could physically torture them in preparation for our matches at the Pay Per View.” Thug roC shrugs and looks to the front of the line. He sees some Indy wrestling stars getting on the boat, hoping to perhaps some how convince some PCW officials to sign them to contracts. Jordan X Shabazz has his back to the line up, and he sees his two slaves/butlers/houseboys running as fast as they can (which really isn’t that fast ‘cause we all know white people are slow as fuck) towards Jordan, Rush, and Thug roC. They carry all of Jordan and Rush’s bags. They appear to be tired, sweat on the face, and their legs almost buckling with every step. They get to the boarding area of the ship, and they drop Jordan’s bags at his feet. Rush’s bags fall to the ground too. Jordan X Shabazz, knowing how far they had to come to get to this boarding place, is angry. Jordan X Shabazz: “NO THE FUCK YOU DIDN’T!!!” Jordan’s trademark quote usually means that’s he very angry, and this time is no different (as was pointed out earlier.) Jordan X Shabazz: “Now, how in the fuck are you going to come here, onto the ship that I’M PAYING FOR YOU GUYS TO STAY ON (shiiit, I‘m a lying ass bitch. Heh, PCW pays for everything. You couldn‘t pay me to pay for white people to get on something…wait… you probably could pay me, but that‘s because we Civil Rights Activist are corrupt like that, but keep that on the DL.) , And drop my bags? You think I give a damn how far you had to run? Seriously, do you? Go ahead and ask me if I care.” Scott looks up, thirsty, tired, and pleading with Jordan not for any more physical or mental punishment, speaks. Scott: “Do… *gasp* you… *gasp* … care?” Jordan X Shabazz: “You can bet your white ass that I don’t. Now pick up my bags and get to the front, ‘fore you fuck around and get shot up in here.” Scott and Steve begrudgingly get to their feet as the line is now cleared. Thug roC is already at the gate, giving his ticket to the gatekeeper. Scott and Steve begin dragging Jordan and Rush’s bags up the ramp that leads them to the ship, and this earns them a smack from Rush. Rush Mason: “What in Voodoo’s name are you doing? These are expensive bags you know? Damn you ignorant white people. You’re the reason I’m going to kill Kramer Wild Wing. Those damn Native Americans are the reason that you white people run this place. If it was black people who were the aboriginals, you can bet your ass that white people wouldn’t run this world. We wouldn’t stand by and let them take over, shiit, white people would be dying all over the place.” Jordan X Shabazz nods and smiles as they left the bags on their shoulders and begin walking up the ramp to the entrance. Jordan and Rush follow behind them. At the gate, the men ponder putting the bags on the floor for a rest, but they realize that doing so would probably induce a violent ass kicking that they wouldn’t forget. As Jordan goes to the front and he begins fumbling around for his tickets. Shabazz pulls out two tickets, he hands one to Rush, and he keeps one for himself. He hands them his ticket, and Rush does the same. The man nods and takes their ticket stubs while giving them a short receipt. Scott and Steve try to get in, but they are stopped at the gate. Ticket Stub Man: “Where are your tickets?” They both look at Jordan, who stares back at them and begins going through his pockets. He obviously is faking the fact that he is looking for the tickets that belong to his slaves/butlers. He looks up at them apologetically. Jordan X Shabazz: “Rats. I can’t find them. This is such a tragedy.” Scott and Steve look shocked. They walked five miles with heavy baggage on their shoulders for this? Jordan X Shabazz: “Didn’t you guys recently get your checks?” They both nod quickly, not wanting to piss off their master. Jordan X Shabazz: “Well, the ticket’s cost around… two hundred each (I can’t believe they’re falling for this), and that’s only about… three months worth of work for me. If you pay this man here now, I’ll reimburse you when we get back… *Whispers* or when pigs fly.” Scott and Steve look skeptical. Jordan only pays them in groups, and they’re not sure if they can trust him. Reluctantly, they give some money to the ticket stub man and carry Jordan’s bags onto the boat. Jordan sticks around at the front, and checks to make sure they’re gone. Jordan X Shabazz: “Alright, they’re gone.” The ticket stub guy exchanges fifty dollars with Jordan X Shabazz, and he pockets the rest as Jordan gives the man the two ticket stubs that belonged to his slaves/butlers. Jordan X Shabazz walks onto the boat, smiling and looking at his money. Jordan X Shabazz: “I got gold on my wrist with my Rolex, I got money in my pocket, which is just like gold, but better. And soon, I’ll have gold around my waist; I’ll be Television Champion. TELEVISION CHAMPION OF THE WORLD!! THE WHITE MAN JOHN CRICHTON SHALL BOW DOWN TO HIS SUCCESSOR! BLACK POWER!! BLACK POWER I SAY!!” A few people eye Shabazz strangely, not knowing why in the fuck he is screaming. He laughs nervously and begins searching the boat for Thug roC and Rush Mason, where they will spend the day having some fun.
