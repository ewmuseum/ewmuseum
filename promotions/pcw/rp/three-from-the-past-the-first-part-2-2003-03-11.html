Three From the Past....The First.......PART 2

11 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-11-03 A lone horseman pounds across the green of the English countryside, and a small plume of dust rises behind him as the overcast sky looks down. Were it sunny, his red armor would glow the color of blood, and his black steed would soak up the light like a black hole. The plume on his helmet flies back from his helmet with the wind as he speeds across the grass. The man is Count Johansen. The first of three warriors the Druid Ancient has called forth from the past to deal with the hero Venneman Pendragon. Although he is not the best subject in terms of his will to do the deed, his skill with a sword was unmatched in his own time. If only his mission were not so grizzly…. *****FLASH****** The scene cuts back to the episode by the blood red pool. The ancient stands over the prone form of the Count, and looks directly upon him… Ancient: You are to lure the Knight from his castle, where he will return to train for his upcoming “matches”… Count: M..M..Matches, sire? Ancient: Aye…The fool engages in hand to hand comment for money. And the throngs love him…That matters not though. You are to ride to the village of Bannockuponshire, that lies due east of the fair castle Pendragon. There, you will commence to deliver unto Pendragon a blood bath of his peoples. They love him like a king, and he will come to their protection. The Count’s eyes widen in horror as in life he was not an evil man. Only now the Ancient holds his soul. And failure means an eternity of torment. Ancient: Now…You will ride east from here…On the double. Pendragon will have an excellent homecoming… ****FLASH**** And so one is able to see the horseman’s dilemma. He must become a murderer to save his own soul. The shot zooms in on the horseman’s eyes and they are filled with the glaze of un-cried tears. He speeds onward and in the distance, a small village begins to come into view on the horizon. As he nears the habitation, we see that the village is very primitive in design, like a village out of the medieval times. I guess that’s how some people choose to live; kinda like the Amish. That, though, is another story. As the horseman enters the town, a few children crowd around the man, staring in awe at the archaic armor that covers the pale form. Suddenly, with the ferocity of an animal, the Count lets out a war cry and draws a mace from his saddle. He crushes a child with the mace in one blow as the others scatter and let out cries of lamentation. The shot zooms in on his eyes once more, and we see that the tears flow freely now. At the sound of the cries, some of the men folk have emerged from their thatched roof houses carrying different implements like axes and pitchforks. A large dark haired man at their front carries a large battle axe as the group approaches the murdering horseman… Man: Aye, and what have we here, lads? A large man who makes targets of the young…..WHAT SAY YE, STRANGER???? The Count stares down with watery eyes and shakes his head… Count: ‘Tis for mine soul… With that, he draws sword and instantly beheads the large man in front of him. The others look at each other before they scatter like sands in the wind to their homes to fend for their families. One man walks slowly, eyes never leaving the crumpled form of the child on the ground… Man: Mine boy…… With a scream he runs toward a fence row where several horses stand tethered. He un-tethers the first he comes to, leaps deftly on and takes off towards the West, where the high towers of a grand castle can barely be made out in the distance. The Count watches him go with saddened eyes, before climbing from his horse and kneeling beside the fallen child and the fallen, now headless, man. He shakes his head as he bows it and utters a small prayer… Count: My Lord…One true god on high, I beseech ye. Mine actions have not been done with no remorse, and I will do what I can to make amends for it in mine next life…Now though, my fate rests in the hands of two mortals. The first, evil embodied. The Ancient Druid has seen more history than any being alive, and knows many powers. I am but a weakened soul, and he controls me…The second rides for the banner of purity and goodness. ‘Tis he I am to take down, and ‘tis he whom now my fate truly rests. A battle of the swords will soon occur, and mine fate will be decided. I only hope that whatever way it goes, I am not to be damned for eternity for mine transgressions due to the usurper of a higher power… The scene fades and cuts back to show the villager reaching the gate of mighty castle Pendragon. Spires rise high into the English sky, and the flowing Pendragon sigil tops each peak. The gate swings inward, as there is no moat nowadays, and the rider leaps from his horse and bounds through, running headlong into the strong, golden armored form of Venneman Pendragon. The man sags and sobs as Pendragon puts a comforting hand upon the man’s shoulder and looks down upon him with sympathetic eyes… Pendragon: What ails ye, William? What news from the village has ye brought to mine door? William wipes his eyes and looks up into the glorious blue of Pendragon’s own, and speaks, his words intermittent with his sobbing… William: My boy…..*sob*…..Lies dead….*Sob*…. Pendragon: Your boy, but how? William: A rider….*sob*….Clad in red….*Sob*…..Bloodbath…..*sob*….The man is not of this world!!! Pendragon listens to the sobbing man, his eyes growing darker with each word… ‘TIS THE DRUIDS. THEY WANT US NOT TO COMPLETE THIS UNION. THEY KNOW THE REPERCUSSIONS OF THE ACT WILL BE A BANE TO EVIL EVERYWHERE…WE MUST BE READY… Pendragon: Someone wishes to bring harm upon mine people. I am sworn to protect ye, William, and protect ye I shall…I swear it… Pendragon draws Excalibur from the scabbard at his side and slices the palm of his hand with the blade. He squeezes his hand into a fist and a drop falls to the ground… Pendragon: By mine own blood, and the blood of the ancients… He then rubs his palm over the flat side of the blade, smearing both sides with his blood… “Excalibur shall first taste the blood of a noble. We must be prepared for whatever foe we are to meet.” AYE, BUT IT SMARTS A TAD… Pendragon sheathes the sword and pulls a cloth from beneath his shirt of armor. He wraps his hand and runs through the corridor, taking the first left that he comes to. A large indoor stable spreads out and we see the massive mount Xerxes, looking at Pendragon in expectation of the battle to come. Once horse and rider are together so long, the horse can pick up on what the master feels, and vice versa. Horses are funny that way… Anyhow…Pendragon foregoes grabbing a saddle and leaps right on the back of the warhorse with only a long lash of leather. He hooks this into Xerxes mouth, gives him a nudge in the ribs and Xerxes takes off like a shot, out of a gate at the other end of the stable and into the light of day. The pair then speed off across the horizon, towards the desecrated village… *****FLASH***** The Count still kneels in prayer, babbling on and on about forgiveness… Count: And were it not the fate of mine soul, then the actions would not have…… SILENCE WHELP!!! The Count looks up and sees Pendragon sitting his mount bareback and holding a bloodied Excalibur in his right hand… Count: So thee has come. I knew thee would… Pendragon: What say ye for yourself? And where have ye come from? The Count draws his own sword as he recounts the tale of his resurrection and also the mission he has been sent on by the Ancient. He leaves out the part of the other two “Heroes” come from the past, but Pendragon frowns at the man with obvious disgust… Pendragon: Mine father taught me of thee, Johansen. A Germanic Count, skilled above many in the ways of the sword. He also taught me of thy noble heart and decency… Count: I must defend mine soul…. Pendragon: Then thou art a coward. I would sacrifice mine soul for the lives of just one innocent. Innocent thee are not, Count…And now I shall send thee to HELL!!! With the last word, Pendragon leaps from his horse and right into a fight with one of the greater swordsmen of history. With his fury and immense strength, Pendragon slashes and hacks his foe back, and it is all the Count can do to keep his balance with the larger fellow who has gone berserk. With a snarl, the Count launches back, but Pendragon holds his position, deflecting many a death blow with merely a twitch of his powerful arms… Count: Thou art good… Pendragon: And thou art a COWARD FOR ACCEPTING THIS TASK!!! Pendragon launches into another offensive. He suddenly drops back and slashes at the midsection of the count. Excalibur slices armor and opens up a would on the Count’s abdomen… Pendragon: ‘tis a good thing I whet the blade with the blood of a noble first…It might help to make the blade forget about the coward’s blood I feed it now… Count: Coward….GRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRR!!!! The Count springs forward with an assault and Pendragon blocks and parries, moving deftly on his feet for such a large man. A few faces peek from doors to watch the two masters go at it, and they stare in awe at a scene that should never happen in the modern world…The Count goes high with a thrust, and Pendragon spins, driving Excalibur backward before pulling the blade out and lopping off the Count’s head. The head rolls from the shoulders, but the body stands a moment longer, blood flowing from the gaping would. Finally, the body realizes what has happened and the form crumples to the ground. Once it hits, a smoke begins to rise from the body as it turns to dust, and a raspy voice can be heard laughing in the background. Pendragon stares in awe as the body disappears into the nothing from whence it was called forth… GOOD CHRIST!!! Pendragon: Aye…..What treachery is this? I AM SURE WE WILL FIND OUT THE CLOSER WE GET TO OUR DESTINY… A small crowd of villagers have crowded around the golden warrior, and they stretch out their hands to touch him as he stares towards the West… “More troubles…Back in PCW, I must listen to the maniacal bragging of one Tommy, claiming he defeated me in fair combat…BAH! I was defeated in a Syko sanctioned match and was only ousted due to interference from his brother, Harry. And then, Dante Jackson…Resident stable boy, sticks his nose into my match with Darkstar, and taints my glorious victory. Why must the fools try to steal mine thunder? Why? And why has a man from the past been sanctioned to kill me? Troubling.” TROUBLING INDEED… TO BE CONTINUED………………….
