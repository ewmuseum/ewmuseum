Cowabunga Campaign '02: Recruiting the Campaign Crew

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 5 COWABUNGA CAMPAIGN '02 - Recruiting the Campaign Crew Support the most tubular, gnarly, and righteous dudes, the Cowabunga Connection, become the next PCW Tag Team Champions! [The scene opens on the perfect, white smile of 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. His tan face stretches out the muscles in his cheeks, dropping into tiny little dimples. The Oakley sunglasses that are on his face dip downward a little so that you can still see his hypnotic baby blue eyes. Next to the shot of Tommy is a man who differs from the Tubular One, and he really doesn't seem to be all there. 'Heavy Metal' Harry's face is frozen in a stupid grin, with his bloodshot eyes squinted down. His long brown hair is tied in a pony tail, which from the shot you can only see his hair pulled back straight] [The scene pulls out showing that this is just a painted picture of the Cowabunga Connection, and it's painted on the side of a Chevorlet Van. The real life forms of Harry and Tommy stand just five feet away from the van, admiring the art work on the side. Tommy wears his PCW T-shirt that says 'I'm Tubular, Your Not' with his blue and yellow swim trunks. Harry wears his usual Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. Both men have very cheesy, proud smiles on their faces] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: That painting is MOST BODACIOUS! 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. [The scene zooms out more showing that the two men are inside a garage. Many tools like wrenches, screwdrivers, and hammers lie on the ground. A large black and red toolbox sits in the back corner, which is where many of these tools on the floor should be. The walls are decorated with posters of half naked women and large framed pictures of classic cars] [Tommy can't help but be bothered by a rotten smell that has suddenly been sent up his nostrils. What bogus thing died? It's like a mix of skunk and moose piss. Both of those smells alone are heinous and as a mixed formula it could be a dose of lethal poison. What the hell is he even thinking about? Skunk and moose piss? Tommy's mind can't work at the usual level, not that it was a high level to begin with, because of the upcoming Spring Fling Pay Per View. The tag titles will be the most gnarly addition to their tag team greatness. Out of the four matches they have wrestled they have done nothing but dominate the most righteous tag team division, and victory is a familiar thing] TTT: Since Politically Incorrect pinned us in that most brutal tag team gauntlet match, we have to go on a campaign, compadre. We have to spread the most bodacious word that the Cowabunga Connection are the right dudes to hold the tag team gold. We have to redeem ourselves from our one bogus mistake. [Harry's brow creases in confusion, not understanding the whole explanation of this far out campaign] Harry: I thought that everyone knew we were great already, man. [Tommy is silent for a few moments before answering] TTT: Not everyone does, dude. [Harry then smiles and nods, accepting that answer. As Tommy admires the far out painting on the van, Harry is thinking about the good times they can have INSIDE the van. Harry can get a hold of some good Mary Jane and pass Jerome Bongus around, spreading joy throughout the van. Because with the large amount of room this van holds in the back, they could pack in a lot of people to spread the weed...er...word to. Then after Jerome Bongus has worked his magic he can invite MARY JANE in the back corner for a little game of...ahem...Yahtzee...] TTT: COWABUNGA CONNECTION FOR PCW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS!! [Harry jumps backward, hitting the back of his head off of the air duct that hangs down from the ceiling of the garage. Tommy's voice came over boisterous and loud. How'd he do that?] TTT: This mega-phone system on the van works bodaciously! [Harry goes back to his stoned out smile as he now knows how Tommy's voice came across so loud. He used the mega-phone, almost making him poop himself...] Strange voice: I hope that you guys wreck and die. [That strange voice comes from professional skateboarder, Randy Raggagu. Actually this man is known as Raggamuffin, because thats what the Cowabunga Connection calls him. Obviously he doesn't like that very much. He wears a dark red Independent T-shirt with baggy cargo pants and dark blue Van's tennis shoes. The dark skinned, black haired young man is wiping his hands clean with a blue bandana. He looks hatefully at Harry and Tommy] Raggamuffin: That painting took me quite some time to do, jackasses. So, I've decided to raise my price. I want two grand for the job. [Harry begins laughing hysterically. Randy and Tommy look at Harry strangely, wondering why he'd laugh hysterically at something bogus like that] Harry: Did you say you want two grand for a blowjob? [Tommy begins laughing along with Harry. Raggamuffin's face just stays frozen and fixed, hatefully, on PCW's rising tag team. He should have predicted that these two morons would find some way to turn his words around into something else. That's why he can't get laid around this place anymore, all the ladies think he's gay] Raggamuffin: NO! I want two grand for painting your ugly faces on the side of the van! [Tommy quickly stops laughing, offended that Raggamuffin would dare say that he was ugly. Harry could pass for an attractive guy but ugly does almost hit the nail on the head. BUT if you call Tommy ugly its as if your committing a cardinal sin, and you will immediately go to hell for it] TTT: That was totally bogus. For that most heinous comment I'm only giving you one grand, dude. Raggamuffin: Two grand! TTT: ONE GRAND! Raggamuffin: TWO GRAND, MORON! TTT: ONE GRAND, RAGGAMUFFIN! Raggamuffin: Don't call me raggamuffin. TTT: Shut up, RAGGAMUFFIN! Raggamuffin: Tommy, your gay if you call me Raggamuffin! Harry: Raggamuffin, man. Raggamuffin: Shut up, Harry Bon Jovi. [Harry smiles brightly] Harry: That would be far out, man. [Raggamuffin sighs and pulls at his hair, ready to just yank it out of the roots. Tommy shrugs to himself going back to admiring the gnarly picture on the side of the van. Harry is smiling happily thinking about being Bon Jovi's little brother] Raggamuffin: I want my two grand, and I want it now. Because if you don't give it to me then I'll just toss paint thinner on the side of the car. [That last comment catches Tommy's attention] TTT: Don't do anything bogus like that, dude. You'll get your money. Raggamuffin: Of course I will. Otherwise your van will be covered in paint thinner. [Tommy goes into his pocket and yanks out his wallet, pulling out a wad of cash. Raggamuffin's eyes light up like the giant Christmas tree in New York City. Tommy slowly hands the money over with a stern look on his face] TTT: Here's a grand, dude. Once we finish the campaign and win the PCW tag titles we'll come back and give you the other half of it. [Raggamuffin snatches the money and smells it. He then begins rubbing it on his face and chest, trying to make himself smell just like the money] [Tommy smiles to himself as he throws a couple gym bags into the back of the van. He hops into the driver seat and then fires the van up. It roars just like he wanted it to. There is nothing better than a bad ass van to go across the country in, especially one with your own bodacious face on the side of it. Everything is going to fall bodaciously in place] [Harry follows along, a little ticked off as he enters the passenger seat. Raggamuffin shouldn't have gotten that much money to do the painting. It's a right on painting, but for two grand Tommy is really getting ripped off. Raggamuffin should do it for free, just for knowing Tommy. I guess some people have to have it all. Harry notices that Tommy has one of those 'I'm really slick and he won't notice until I'm gone' grins] Harry: What's the grin about, man? TTT: You know the money I gave him... Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: It's fake. [Harry smiles and nods with approval] Harry: Far out, man. [The van peels out in the garage, leaving tread marks behind. It's tires screech and spin down the small dip to the driveway and then the van disappears from view. Raggamuffin is still standing there admiring the money in his hand to get mad at them for doing that in his garage. His smile begins to fade away as he notices the faces on the bills. They aren't Benjamin Franklin, it isn't even Andrew Jackson, and it sure isn't George Washington...it's 'Totally Tubular' Tommy!!] Raggamuffin: SON OF A-- [He goes to jump up and down and cracks his head off of the air duct above. He knocks himself unconscious] [That scene fades, and opens up with Harry and Tommy cruising through a residential area in California. 'Bang Your Head' by Quiet Riot is playing in the background. The stereo system in the van isn't anything to go crazy over, but it does it's job which is exactly what the two men need] Harry: We should make a stop down in Florida, man. TTT: Bodacious Spring Break babes? Harry: Yeah, man. [The two men smile to each other, loving it when they think the same thoughts. Actually it happens all the time, considering that they are life long friends and have rarely ever been apart from each other. It's amazing all the times they have spent together, bad and good. They are more like brothers than friends really] Harry: Who are those guys, man? [On the side of the street two lone men walk on the sidewalk, seeming a bit down from their body motions. The young man on the left has long black hair, he wears a dyed T-shirt with a black vest over it, and red pants. The other young man, on the right, wears a gray T-shirt and blue jeans with a blue sweatshirt tired around his waist. Tommy pulls the van over next to them as Harry hangs out of the window] Harry: Hey, man. [The two men stop and look up at the van] Guy with black hair: Whoa, your 'Heavy Metal' Harry, dude. Guy with blonde hair: Gnarly. [Harry and Tommy both look at each other, seeming to think these guys are familiar from somewhere] Tommy: What are your names, dudes? Guy with black hair: I'm Ned Theodore Hogan!!! Guy with blonde hair: And I'm Will Preston Mesquire. Both men: AND TOGETHER...WE ARE MILD SCALLIONS!! [Harry looks absolutely confused and turns to Tommy] Harry: What the hell is a scallion, man? TTT: That's elementary, dear Harry. A scallion is a kind of onion; especially, a young green onion or a leek. [Harry looks at Tommy with even more confusion, but more confused as to why Tommy answered that so well. Harry then looks over at the Mild Scallions and they nod their heads with bright smiles on their faces. Harry then looks back over to Tommy] Harry: A scallion can be mild, man? TTT: I guess so, dude. [Harry then turns back to the Scallions. They shrug to Harry, not really knowing themselves but it has a very gnarly catch to it] TTT: We're the Cowabunga Connection from the PCW and we're going on a road trip to spread the word about us going for the tag team titles. It's going to be a most bodacious affair. Ned Hogan: EXCELLENT!! Bill Mesquire: TOTALLY!! [They play the air guitar] Both Men: MILD SCALLIONS RULE!!! TTT: Bodacious! [Harry seems to be a bit disturbed by all of this] Harry: This is freaky, man. TTT: It's because you had a couple hits of Jerome Bongus, dude. [Harry thinks about that for a few moments] Harry: No, it's not that. I'm more paranoid than usual, man. [Tommy totally ignores Harry and goes on about business] TTT: We should have these dudes be our campaign managers, dude. They seem like totally gnarly dudes, and they probably get the babes. Ned Hogan: Bodacious babes are our specialty, dude. TTT: GNARLY! Bill Mesquire: We would be MOST HONORED to be your most excellent campaign managers. TTT: ALL RIGHT!! We've got campaign managers, Harry. We're going to be on our way to being the most bodacious, popular tag team in PCW. We'll spread the word of how righteous we are and nobody will be able to stop us. With the tag titles we could take it to a most bodacious level in our careers. We've never held titles before, and this would just be a most tubular dream come true. Harry: Yeah, man. But I'm still freaked out. TTT: These dudes are bodacious. [The two men turn their heads to the back of the van and the newly acquired campaign managers are taking large whiffs of paint thinner] TTT: Well, not bodaciously smart. Harry: Yeah, man. [The scene then fades to black]
