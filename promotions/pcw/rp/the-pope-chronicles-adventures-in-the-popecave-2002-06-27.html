The Pope Chronicles: Adventures in the Popecave

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27th June 2002 We return to the duo of destruction as they exit the lift on what appears to be the basement. You can tell it’s the basement because they walk past the washing machine and the tumble dryer. So it must be the basement. And there’s a bicycle down here for not really any reason at all too. It’s a bit rusty, once again enforcing the fact that it’s the basement of the mansion. Miyagawa strides forth from the lift wearing his dapper black suit, with Den in tow wearing a frilly pink frock (read the last promo for reasons on the frock). He walks up to a door, and unsurprisingly, opens it, but surprisingly this reveals a massive cavern containing all manner of things. The duo make their way over to what appears to be a changing room, with two large glass cases with two leather and latex costumes in them. Miyagawa walks over to a control panel and pushes a button and the two open. Suddenly a tune from the 60’s Batman series plays in order to indicate a time warp... ... and the two are now changed into their costumes. Miyagawa is wearing an all white costume with a black catholic cross on his chest. He doesn’t appear to wear a mask, but instead wears one of those white bowls that clergy tend to wear. Den is wearing a red and black costume with a red bowl on her head. Den: Ok, I get that you are supposed to be “Popeman” but what on earth am I supposed to be? The devil? Popeman/Miyagawa shakes his head to indicate that is the wrong answer. Miyagawa: Nope, you are Cardinal. Den: Cardinal? Miyagawa: You know... a bird... like Robin... but it’s also a religious position. Humorous double meaning, no? Den: You said it yourself, I believe. **Den smirks** You know I’m sure I saw this outfit in an Ann Summers catalogue at one point... does it come with a matching whip? Miyagawa audibly sighs out loud. Miyagawa: You are Cardinal. Right hand man... erm... woman... of Popeman, defender of the free rights to religious persecution... erm... non-religious persecution and defender of the Catholic Faith. You are not Miss Whiplash. Den (quickly retorting): You say that, yet you have no idea what I do on my days off. Miyagawa: Mainly because I just don’t WANT to know what you do on your days off. Den reaches down and starts opening the pockets of her bright yellow utility belt. Den: What have we got in here? Miyagawa looks over to see what she is fidgeting with. Miyagawa: Just the usual... cross-a-rang, smoke bombs, lock pick, travellers edition of the bible. You know the usual. Den pulls out a small clear vial. Den: Holy Water? Miyagawa: I believe you are supposed to say “HOLY WATER POPEMAN!” Den shrugs. Den: Get a grip. What on earth use is a vial of Holy Water... **notices Miyagawa is staring at her to say it**... Popeman. Miyagawa: You’re getting there. Anyway, don’t open it. Den: Why not. Miyagawa: Let me demonstrate. **takes his own vial of holy water from his own utility belt and opens it very carefully... he flicks the contents onto the floor where smoke and a hissing noise is heard as acid melts through the floor** It’s Holy Sulphuric Acid. Den: HOLY SULPHURIC ACID? **realises she just said that**... Popeman. Miyagawa nods, happy she is getting the idea. Miyagawa: Quickly, to the Popemobile! The camera cuts as the two capped crusaders run through the night... well... through the cave... The Popeman and his eager assistant... Cardinal. Den/Cardinal: So what’s the Popemobile? A super charged hyper fast car? Miya/Popeman: Yep, of course it is. They stop at a control panel beside a large landing pad. Miyagawa hits a big red button and the floor retracts revealing... the Popemobile. Den looks at it with astonishment. Den: I don’t believe it. Miyagawa: Magnificent isn’t it? Den: It’s a white golf cart. Miyagawa: Ah yes, an armoured hype fast aquatic flying golf cart. It’s not just any golf cart. Den: And besides, if this all blows up in our faces, we can always just sell it to Tiger Woods. Miyagawa: Not funny. TO THE POPEMOBILE! The last we see of our Catholic Church Rights Duo is a white golf cart speeding out of the cave at a hundred and twenty miles per hour with its front two wheels in the air... but what does Scientology have in store for the Religious Rebels? Find out next time... Same Pope Time.... Same Pope Channel...
