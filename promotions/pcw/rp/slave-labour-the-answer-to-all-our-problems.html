Slave labour- the answer to all our problems!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered When we last left off, Flaymin was having trouble getting from the wharf and on to his rusty, tugboat necessary for his solo journey to Jamaica in time for the pay per view.Stranded and void of any creative ideas, Flaymin was saved by two little kids who came up with the ingenious idea to use a wooden plank to bridge the wharf to the boat. Finally aboard the boat, Flaymin now has one very big problem to answer…. Which way to Jamaica?? [Flaymin looks at the controls of the tugboat in total confusion. He picks up the nearby communication transmitter and speaks into it] Flaymin: Uh… hello, can you tell me where Jamaica is? [A crackling voice begins to speak back to Flaymin] Control Tower: State your vessel identification please. [Flaymin looks around the boat and notices nothing but broken equipment, a rusted deck and a broken mast] Flaymin: It’s a crappy boat… I repeat- ‘a crappy boat’. Control Tower: No, no, we need your ship’s identification number. Flaymin: How about I just give you my lottery numbers instead? Control Tower: Is this a prank call?I hope you realise that you’re committing a felony punishable in all states and territories. Flaymin: Just what I need… another notch on my police record! Control Tower: Excuse me? Flaymin: Never mind, I’ll figure this all out myself. Control Tower: Jackass. [Flaymin looks at the communication transmitter with his eyes steaming with rage] Flaymin: You got a problem, punk? Control Tower: Yeah, assholes like you who waste my time. Flaymin: That’s it. [Flaymin clenches his fist and with a swift motion drives it deep into the communication transmitter.The transmitter busts open and little bits and pieces scatter all over the floor. Having destroyed the transmitter, Flaymin looks at the broken pieces and grins] Flaymin: Yeah, you’re not saying anything now, are ya? [Little does he realise that he’s just destroyed all forms of communication with the Control Tower….] Flaymin: Now where was I? Oh yes… Jamaica. [Flaymin starts the boat and slowly begins to move the steering wheel. Little by little the boat begins to rumble forwards until it is finally out of the pier and in the open sea. However the boat comes to a sudden halt and Flaymin swivels his head to examine the left side of the ocean and then the right side. He then looks down at the Steering Wheel in bewilderment] Flaymin: Where the hell is the indicator on this thing? Maybe I could call the Control Tower for help…. [Flaymin peers over at the recently destroyed Communication Transmitter and shrugs his shoulders] Flaymin: Then again maybe not. [Flaymin turns on the motor once again and the boat jerks forward and makes its way deeper into ocean. Bit by bit, the wharf behind him grows further… and further… and further away, until Flaymin is finally out of sight and stuck in the middle of the sea] Flaymin: I really should have brought a map along. [Flaymin then walks over to the edge of the boat.He looks over the side of he tugboat and into the deep, blue water] Flaymin: Well this doesn’t look anything like the Black Sea… Or The Red Sea. Then again I don’t ever recall a Deep Blue Sea either… except for the movie. [Flaymin sits on the deck and places his hands behind his head] Flaymin: Jamaica….Jamaica….O’ where are thou? [With Flaymin daydreaming, slumped against the deck of the boat, little does he notice a nearby plastic cover begin to wriggle from side to side. Ever so slowly a head appears from beneath the covers.The head looks from around to check that the coast is clear before finally a little boy emerges from beneath the covers. Once again he looks around to make sure that no one is watching.Next, he pulls back the entire plastic cover revealing three other people who were hidden beneath the blanket. Speaking in a foreign language, the little boy speaks to the other people who appear to be his Father, Mother and younger brother. Meanwhile, Flaymin begins to hear the voices.He raises his head alertly but fails to see anything out of the ordinary. So he slumps his head back down on the boat’s deck and goes back to doing…. Whatever the hell he was doing in the first place a.k.a. nothing. On the other side of the boat, the little boy and his family look around at the surrounding sea. They then walk around the boat until they suddenly encounter Flaymin lazing around on the deck. Flaymin’s eyes pop open in shock as this family of refugees appear before him] Flaymin: I never knew I had some crew to steer the boat!Where have you been? Father: We have come fro… Flaymin: I don’t give two flying f*cks where you came from- just go steer my damn boat and tell me when we get to Jamaica. Father: I am sorry to be of err… how you say… ‘nuisance’, but we were trying to get into America.Is this boat going to America? [Flaymin looks back in the direction of the American port where his tugboat just departed from] Flaymin: Uh… yeah, this boat is going to America- but you’re looking in the wrong direction. [Flaymin reaches out his hand and points in the direction of Jamaica] Flaymin: Jamai… um I mean America is that way. Father: Are you sure? Flaymin: Of course I’m sure!How about you and your family steer the boat towards ‘America’ and then ill do you all a favour by smuggling you into the country. Father: But my children haven’t eaten in three days and my wife is suffering from several disea… Flaymin: Hey, hey!I don’t give a shit if your family is on the verge of dying- just get this boat to where I want to go and there will be no problems.Kapisch? [The father looks at his malnourished family and reluctantly nods his head] Father: Yes… boss. [Flaymin pulls out a chocolate bar from his pocket.He unwraps it and begins to eat it while the starving refugee family watch on] Flaymin: Whoops!Where are my manners? [Flaymin unwraps the chocolate bar and hands the wrapper to the little kid] Flaymin: Here kid, you can lick that. [The father looks at Flaymin with a mystified look on his face] Father: Your accent sounds familiar, is it Australian? Flaymin: Why do you ask? Father: My family were kicked out of Australia by the racist government over there. Flaymin: Yeah the Australian government is full of assholes. Father: Therefore I despise everything that is Australian. Are you Australian? [Flaymin’s eyes cautiously move from side to side] Flaymin: Uh… no.I’m Austrian. Father: Really? Flaymin: Yes, it’s a pleasure to meet you- my name is err… Sphen…Sphenson…. Father: Thankyou for helping my family get to America, Mr Sphenson.We are forever grateful! Flaymin: Yeah, yeah whatever.I’ll mention you in my United Nations Humanity Award Speech, but for now you’ve got to steer my freakin’ boat to Jam…err..ica Father: Jamerica? Flaymin: Silly me, I meant America. Now get crackin’ before I throw you all overboard! [The refugees happily oblige.They walk into the control room and begin to unknowingly steer the boat towards Jamaica. Meanwhile, Flaymin sits back and continues to eat his chocolate bar while soaking up the hot sun] Flaymin: Montego Bay, Jamaica…. Here I come!!! [Scene fades to black] Vote ‘Flaymin’ in the United Nations Humanitarian of the Year Poll!!
