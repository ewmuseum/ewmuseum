Jim the Truck Driver Part Dos

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-24-02 RP 3 Turmoil24 The interstate is no stranger to Teresa. The roads look the same. Medians look the same. Even the trees do after a few hours. It’s just the thrill of riding in the cab of a semi truck and looking down on everyone in their small cars and truck that Teresa is after. The driver, he seems nice. During the past 2-hour drive not really going anywhere, Jim has told Teresa where he’s from and a whole book on his children. After telling him she probably will never have any kids, he persisted a 10-minute speech on how wonderful children are and how a lot of wrestlers get by with kids. It took Teresa giving the half Mexican the evil eye before he got the hint she didn’t want to hear anymore. Stop here! Teresa calls out as they come to a liquor store. Knowing the dangers of drinking and driving a big truck, Jim looks to her cautiously. You know I can’t drink and drive Teresa. I’d hate to wreck and kill us both. Teresa laughs watching Jim pull the rig into the parking area. She runs her long fingers through her black streaked hair and grabs the door handle. Silly driver, Milk is for kids. I’m just getting a few bottles for after the show Tuesday. Since you’ll be my VIP guest, you’ll get to celebrate my win. The other bottle will be for Friday when I become number one contender. Jim shakes his head and waits in the truck while Teresa enters the liquor store. This day has gotten better by the minute. First he steps into a Waffle House for some grub, meets a professional wrestler, and to top things off, he’s escorting her around the city in his truck. It’s all happening so fast. Five minutes pass and Teresa finally makes it back inside the truck. She carries a paper bag consisting of two large bottles. The Southern Diva grins as she pulls out both bottles showing off her treasures. Southern Comfort is in one hand, and Jack Daniels is in the other. These are two of my favorite things She sings these words to the familiar tune before placing them gently back in the sack. The sack is then placed behind the seat. I’m looking at two of my favorite things. Jim smiles in Teresa’s direction. Naturally, when a man says this, he’s talking about T&A. Teresa been around men long enough, she can nearly read them like a book. Nearly. Teresa lifts an eyebrow questionably at Jim comment and he returns by pointing over her head. Teresa turns around and looks up to see a picture of his two children. What Jim didn’t see is Teresa’s ears turn red once she realizes whom he was referring to. Let’s not go back to that subject. I’d hate to have to shut you up, especially after you’ve been so nice to me. Jim nods understanding exactly what she is speaking of. After she gave him that eyes during their last conversation of children, he did not want to see what came after that. His dark brown eyes return to the never-ending interstate and he makes a right at the Davenport exit where Teresa’s hotel is. You actually think you will beat both Anarchy and Cyclonica? From what I’ve seen, they’re some bad bitches. I even wouldn’t want to mess with them. Teresa makes fists with both hands and squeezes tightly. She knows it’s gonna take probably more than what she has to beat these two women, but she’ll be damned if someone even implies she ain’t got what it takes to win. If Jim hadn’t been such a gentlemen thus far, his steering wheel would be wearing his face and they would be wrecked in the ditch line. Teresa would’ve just climbed out and walked away. But no, this truck driver had to be different. You see, truck drivers have a nasty rep for being drunks and whoremongers. They like to get any kitty that will give and they eat so much truck stop food and beer, they got huge bellies. Well, that is popular belief anyhow. Jim has to be exception. He loves his children and is loyal to his woman if he has one. Teresa hasn’t asked, but she’s sure he does. He hasn’t made the first pass at her because he still has his hands. Damn the endangered gentlemen. Gotta love them. Having sympathy for the poor feller, Teresa takes in a deep breath to loosen her nerves and her fists. She turns her head toward the passenger side window and releases her breath I can’t think Jim. Not with theses two women. I can’t just think I’ll beat them. I just have to do it.
