Love can get messy at times

2 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/02/02 Of all the nights to be stuck in traffic, all the nights to be late, it had to be tonight. There you sit, trapped in your car in the middle of highway 101. From the looks of it you won’t be going anywhere for a while. As you look farther down the long stretch of highway, you see hundreds of cars all resting motionless in front of you. You can‘t figure out what happened to the flow of traffic. As you merged onto the 101, traffic was good. Then all of the sudden it locked up, and you don‘t know why. You try turning on the radio to try and find out why the traffic is so harsh at this time of night. As you tune into the local traffic radio station, you catch the last bit of the explanation. “All motorists are advised to stay clear of highway 101 at this time. Again, an overturned semi is blocking all 4 lanes, and the highway patrol is working as quickly as possible to clear things up...” That’s all you needed to hear. You switch the radio off, and begin pounding your head into the steering wheel. As your head rests on the steering column, you begin to imagine your Emily waiting at the park, all alone. There she sits on a bench, waiting patiently for her man to arrive. As your mind continues to wander as you begin to imagine a tall dark and handsome man, approaching your woman, whispering something in her ear. She smiles as the man extends his hand for her to take. She leaves her seat and takes hold of the man’s arm and the two of them walk off. As rage begins to feel through your body, you reassure yourself that a scenario like that is impossible. Emily wouldn’t leave you for another man. You‘ve just met, things are fresh in the relationship, and the both of you are happy. As frustrated motorists begin to honk their horns in unison, you think back to last week when you first met her. At first you had thought it was a stupid idea to be set up for a blind date. One of your friends from work had set you up with a single girl from his church group. Though you felt like this was a ridiculous way to get a date, at the time you were pathetic. You hadn’t had a date in two months so you decided to go through with it. That night you remember a cold wind blowing as you rang her doorbell. As your palms began to sweat from anticipation you saw a figure appear on the other side of the door. As the door swung open you felt your heart fall down into your stomach. There standing in front of you was the most beautiful specimen you’ve ever seen in your life. As the angelic beauty began to spoke you gave her a quick glance up and down. She was about 5’9 long auburn hair, and hypnotizing deep brown eyes. “Hi, my name’s Emily. Chris right?” As you were instantly falling in love with this woman, you had forgotten to answer her. Your voice cracks as you reply. “Uh yeah, I’m Chris, nice to meet you.” Sheesh what a dork you are. Instead of saying something suave, you say that. Nice way to make a first impression you idiot. As you could feel yourself turning red, you see a small smile come across her beautiful face. Like everything else, even her smile was awesome. As she turned around to close her front door, you had thought to yourself that you would somehow have to pay your friend back for setting you up with such a beauty. The rest of the first date went about the same, Emily looking beautiful, and you making a fool out of yourself. Luckily for you, either she didn’t notice, or she didn’t care. As the date came to an end, and as you walked her to her door, thoughts of kissing her came through your mind. As you talked it became clear that she liked you almost as much as you liked her, and that she wouldn’t dispute a kiss if you tried. As you moved in for the kiss, you remember her closing her eyes and puckering her lips. That instant you kissed her you remember your knees almost buckling completely from just how amazing it felt. Not only was this girl the most beautiful woman you’ve ever met, but she was an amazing kisser as well. Emily gave you a smile as she turned and went into her house. How badly you wanted to follow her in and finish that kiss. Unfortunately you knew it would have been stupid to press your luck with her and risk ending a very good thing. The rest of the week, you talked frequently on the telephone. You both realized how much you had in common, and you noticed what a funny and interesting women she was. It seemed as if you had known her your whole life. Again you bang your head on the steering column. You still can’t believe it could be possible that you could ruin a chance like this. Hopefully Emily could understand, and forgive you. Just then an almost miracle happens. The cars in front of you actually began moving. You look upward and notice the traffic begin to move forward. The Highway Patrol must have somehow moved the overturned semi. What a bunch of heroes they are! What quick service too! You look at your wristwatch and notice that you’re only going to be 5 or 10 minutes late instead of 30 or 40. As you breathe a sigh or relief, the vehicles start to improve to decent highway speeds. As you finally arrive at the park, your mind begins to fill with different excuses you could give Emily for your tardiness, but by the time you park you car, you assure yourself that one look into her eyes and you wouldn’t be able to go through with a fib. You check your hair in the rear view mirror, then exit your vehicle, ready to find your new love interest. You look around for Emily, but find no sign of her. As you pan around the park, you see a tall dark haired man talking to your woman. Ironically this man looks similar to the man that was in your daydream while you were stuck in traffic. As you approach them, you notice Emily backing away from the man with a frightened look on her face. You begin to worry about what might be happening, but the worried feeling turns to panic when you see the man punch Emily across her beautiful face. You try to scream to Emily to run, but she seems too frightened to even listen. Was this man a mugger? Was he trying to rob her? As you begin to race towards the scene, you watch as the man grabs Emily by the throat and lift her into the air. As your love fights for her life, your courage kicks in as you jump on the man’s back. You try to put a chokehold on the man, but he grabs you off his back and throws you into a nearby tree as if you were a mere rag doll. The impact of your contact from the tree temporarily paralyzes you. You helplessly watch as the man continues to assault your love. As you struggle to get feeling back to your body, you watch as the man pulls out a .45 and aims it at your frightened girlfriend. That man mumbles something about redemption then pulls the trigger. You go in shock as you watch the bullet enter one side of her head, and leave on the other. You watch your love fall back to the floor, her brains splattered everywhere, some still coming out of her head. As your body feels queasy from the site, you still lay there motionless, as you look at your now dead girlfriend splattered across the grass. With your mind still not being able to take in what you’ve just witnessed, the man points the gun at you. Your body shivers with fright as the man stares you right in the eye and says: “You wouldn’t have liked her anyways. Her p*ssy smelled like the devil. Speaking of him, say hello for me.” You scream out as the man pulls the trigger, sending a bullet directly into your forehead. (The Scene Fades)
