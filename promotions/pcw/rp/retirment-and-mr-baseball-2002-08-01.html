Retirment and Mr. Baseball

1 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/1/02 -America’s hero Duke Williams sits at a bar stool in the dive of a city know as Milwaukee, Wisconsin. The depression is brought on by another PPV loss giving him one of the worst big show winning percentages in PCW. Duke downs glasses of booze with out flinching or making a face. Through his trademarked shades stare empty eyes of defeat.- Stranger: Why the long face pal? -A familiar looking man sits down next to the defeated “Malice Man”.- Williams: I’m not in the social mood hoss. Stranger: Things can’t be that bad. Come on let me buy you another. -The stranger is starting to annoy the once promising, now aged wrestler. Duke looks over at the man and recognizes him.- Williams: Do I know you from somewhere? Stranger: Well you may have know me from my years as Mr. Baseball!!! A true star of the outfield. Williams: No that’s not it. I hate baseball. Stranger: Humm? So what’s the beef pal? Williams: Every time I get up to bat I strike out. Stranger: I know how that feels. Williams: How can you? Stranger: I have the lowest batting average in baseball. Williams: Look hoss I told you I don’t know baseball. Stranger: That means I missed the ball a lot. -Now the stranger is also drinking down some booze in this second rate Wisconsin bar.- Williams: But I was once a great!! Do I just retire? Stranger: No, just move it into the broadcast booth and make a killing. Williams: I’ve already done that. Stranger: Hummm? So quite dropping the ball. Show up to play. Williams: That is an option. Stranger: Right on!! Picture yourself as a tight virgin pussy. Then go out and loosen up a bit!!! Go back to what worked for you. Williams: You know something mister I think your right. Stranger: The name is Bob. -Bob and Duke shake hands.- Bob: Or you could just hang it up and live off of your past and be a great announcer? Williams: That sounds simple enough. Bob: Sure it is but you’d miss the drive. Williams: Yea the drive to work is pleasant. I usually listen to CD’s. Bob: No I mean the drive….the excitement of work….the thrill of it all. Williams: Yea I would miss the fans. Bob: So quite crying in your beer and move on and kick some ass. Williams: You sure are a wise man. Bob; Thank you!! -Duke does a double take at the big nosed smiling Bob.- Williams: I do recognize you!! -Bob fixes his hair and smiles with pride.- Williams: Yea I saw you on TV!! Bob: I’ve been on many commercials, Johnny Carson over 30 times, and many other shows. Williams: No, no, no, that not it. You worked with Mr. Belvedere!! What was he like? -Bob’s smile wears away.- Williams: Yea your George Owens!! Why were you such a prick to Mr.B? Bob: No, I’m Bob Uecker!! George was a character I played that I would love to forget. Williams: Man your kid Wesley was a jerk. Bob: It was just a TV show. Williams: Yea but it was a reality based show. Bob: No it was a sitcom. Williams: Does Mr. B still work for you. Bob: Hey buddy have another!! -Bob Uecker gets up and storms out.- Williams: Don’t mind if I do!! -Duke has another drink as the camera fades.- =w=
