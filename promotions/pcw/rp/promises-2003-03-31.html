Promises

31 March 2003

Date: 3/31/03 The sky is gray in color as moisture comes pouring in buckets from the sky. I probably shouldn’t be out in this type of weather but right about now I could care less. Who could possibly care about their health when the one person they cared about most wanted nothing to do with them anymore? After all it was not my fault that Gaz and I were accidentally married that fateful Monday a week ago. I didn’t want it to happen anymore than Gaz or Chris did. Yet for some reason Chris believes it is justified that he limit his contact with me now that I bear the name Tessa Green. BOOM Thunder clapped and was followed by a bolt of lightning that lit up the dimmed sky above but it didn’t stop me from continuing my way towards my destination, the sight where my brother’s life had been taken away six months ago. If I was going to have to be alone because of an accident that was not my fault then I might as well turn to the one person I always relied on when I was younger, even if ninety percent of the time he only made things worse because of a little thing called sibling rivalry. The wind blew creating a shiver that crawled up my rain soaked back, but I didn’t let it bother me any. If I was going to have to suffer before things got better than so be it I would suffer, although I don’t think anything could possibly be worse than having to be referred to as Tessa Green for the time being. Why, damn it! WHY?! That one question had been stinging my mind every since the unfortunate uniting of Gaz and I had occurred. Why did I have to be the unfortunate one? Why did Chris have to be mad at me for what happened? And most of all, why did this have to happen to me? Yet the answer was one simple word, revenge. P.W.A. wanted revenge and they would get it at any cost. Though the bond that Gaz and I now shared through marriage was not intended to occur, it was the aftermath of making Chris and my lives a living hell in any way possible, and their job was well done despite the fact that a wedding between Gaz and I was probably the last thing on both of their minds. It wasn’t my fault, why can’t Chris understand that? I’m just as unhappy as he is. Cutting across the street I rushed over to a place where the remains of wilted flowers lay as did a card Christa and Alex had made for their father long ago right after they learned of his passing away. Had it not been laminated it might have had the same fate of both the flowers and Brendan. Hopefully the relationship between Chris and I would not have this same fate, if we had stuck together for this long there is no way we could let something this simple get to us, even if it prevented us from being married like we had wanted to be. Taking a seat beside the pile of wilted flowers and taking the card into my hand, I could not help but to remember the last moment spent with Brendan. It was in the hospital with both Christa and Alex where he had promise that he wouldn’t let go. It reminded me of the way Chris had promised me multiple times that he would never stop caring for me. Brendan couldn’t guarantee his life the way Chris couldn’t guarantee being there for me when the sun rose up the next morning and just like the way I couldn’t guarantee that just because I had defeated Teresa Tomas before I could defeat her once again. “Why Brendan? Why did you have to break your promise like that?” I shouted out in an anger that was filled with more sorrow than actual madness while looking up at the sky as if I were actually talking to his spirit in the so called heavens above. It made me think that if Brendan had kept his promise, perhaps Chris would have too and perhaps everything would have been fine, but then I realized that not even my deceased brother could have stopped those events from occurring, considering the masters behind this prank did not see this union coming themselves until the vicar announced the words, “I now pronounce you husband and wife.” Curling up like a child trapped in a dark corner I let tears fall from my eyes the same way the rain was falling from the sky, in plentiful amounts. While doing this I realized one thing to be true during this whole predicament. The only type of promises there are, are broken ones or ones that can be easily maneuvered around.
