Remembering the past by paying visit to a forgotten shrine

Date: Sometime this week (Minor Note: This RP contains some historical references that are copied and pasted. I could care less about RP size, so that's not why they're there. They're there for the sake of getting the point across. Since archives is a foreign word to most, it was the only way.) It doesn’t feel like it’s been that long. It feels like only yesterday since Kid Wonder was gracing the halls of Primetime Championship Wrestling. Walls that the young stud helped build. Kid Wonder helped build PCW as much as anybody else who was there in the early days. He was the heart and soul of the company. When others would come and go at will, Kid Wonder always stayed loyal to his PCW. K-Dub paved the way for a lot of people that came after him. As a cruiserweight, Wonder had to prove that smaller guys stood an equal chance. And Kid did just that. He set the stage for other cruiserweights. Kid Wonder’s success paved the way for a Miyagawa, or a White Mexican, or a Chet King, or a Rent-a-Hero. Kid Wonder’s work in the early days made it a little easier for the cruiserweight to get a fair shake. But, alas, all that seems to be ignored by the new school of PCW. Much like the New School of Old School Wrestling, the new breed in PCW has no appreciation for anything but themselves. From the Front Office to Playaz Inc. to the Mark Amulets and Michigans of the world. Not a single newbie appreciates what guys like Kid Wonder did for them. And not a single one of them care because their own selfishness won’t allow them to. It doesn’t matter though. While the current “stars” of PCW are overseas, their minds are ultimately set on San Antonio, Texas. And in San Antonio, the new breed will have to step in the ring with guys like Kid Wonder… guys who turned PCW from upstart indy promotion to mainstream success. At PCW Alliances, the biggest PCW show of the year, Kid Wonder, along with partners Ivan Stanislav, White Mexican, and Tempest, will represent Old School Wrestling in the annual event. The goal is simple: Destroy as many PCW “stars” as possible until only members of your team remain. Once done, take PCW title shots and capture their gold. Kid Wonder is no stranger to PCW gold. He’s a former 4-time PCW Cruiserweight champion and a former PCW Television champion. But one title that has eluded him is the PCW World Heavyweight championship. While many feel that the “Quintessential Teen Idol” deserved to hold the title at one point, his fellow wrestlers didn’t. Never did one of the champions of the past willingly defend the World title against K-Dub. Never! From Meanstreak and Reverend, to Jeremy Howard and Trashcan Man, to Ivan Stanislav and Snow. Not a single person would willingly defend their championship against a deserving Kid Wonder. The only time a World champion even willingly fought Kid Wonder was at Alliances 2000. Doug Troy was the champ, and he effectively lost to Kid Wonder in their ladder match. But it’s all good. That provides more motivation for the Alliances match. It provides more motivation to win Alliances. By winning Alliances, Kid Wonder can shove all of his anger, all of his frustration, right down PCW’s collective throat. And never will it feel any sweeter. We fade in to Atlantic City, New Jersey. We’re up high in the rafters of the PCW Pavilion. Up in the nosebleed seats we spot current OSW Cruiserweight champion, Kid Wonder. Next to Wonder, as always, is his lovely girlfriend, Amanda. We zoom in closer on the former first couple of PCW. The two of them are leaning back in their chairs. K-Dub has his feet kicked up on the chair in front of him. He’s wearing a Tampa Bay Buccaneers cap, a vintage “Kremmen Sux” T-shirt, and his customary genie sequin pants. Amanda, as usual, is wearing a pink halter top with “STAR” written in silver glitter across the chest, and he trademark black leather pants. Kid Wonder takes a deep breath and sighs. He turns his head to the right towards Amanda. “This place sure brings back memories,” Wonder says to her. “Mmmhmm,” Amanda nods in agreement. Kid Wonder pulls his feet from the chair in front of him and plants them on the ground. “I mean,” he starts as his whole body turns in Amanda’s direction, “there was a time when you’d never see this building empty like this.” Amanda turns her body in the direction of her boyfriend. Kid Wonder sighs. “Now look at it. This sight is commonplace,” he says somewhat dejectedly. “It’s amazing how PCW can forget where it came from when ignorant jackasses are in charge.” “I know what you’re saying,” Amanda says in agreement. “The people in PCW are more egotistic and self-centered than ever. They only care about themselves and nothing else.” “For real. I could never do that,” says the teen idol. “I can never forget where I came from. I can’t be like a Statuz Quo or Xavier Kannon. I can’t forget what PCW once stood for. And I could never forget the people who came before me.” “Like who?” asks Amanda. “I mean, you were there since damn near the beginning.” Kid Wonder just snickers. He looks at Amanda with a small grin. “Like Johnny Raindance,” he tells Amanda. “Because of him, I had absolutely no pressure. We debuted at the same time, and all the pressure was in him to perform well.” “What’s that have to do with your success?” Amanda asks. “Everything when you think about it,” Wonder responds. “If there’s no Johnny Raindance, I could’ve buckled under the pressure,” he claims. “I could’ve fizzled out time after time like he did. For that, I should be grateful towards him. Not Christmas Card grateful, but grateful none the less.” Amanda remains silent, not wanting to respond to Kiddie’s stupid assessment. “Hell,” Wonder continues on, “I can remember a lot of stuff from my PCW days.” “Like what?” Amanda asks her boyfriend. “Like what?” mockingly says Wonder. “Like my first title victory.
