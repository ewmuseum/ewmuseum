Thank you and good bye.

14 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Thank you and good bye. Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 3/14/03 Life is little more then a collection of souvenirs and memories. We live everyday in an effort to make it memorable or to gain a new item to your collection know as life. Eat, drink, crap, and it all starts again the next day with a little more wisdom and a little less energy. It’s funny how the wiser we get the weaker we feel. It’s like knowledge is the baggage that slowly drags you back to the earth. No matter how wise we get in age we still can’t stop our pending demise no matter what we do. Jog, pray, and eating right only prolongs the definite end. I’ve been a wrestler now longer then most of my colleagues have walked the earth. I’m now leavening behind a legacy while collecting more souvenirs in the process. When were young we dream and dreaming comes natural like the first breath from a baby. As we age our dreams fade like the sex life of a fat man till they are nothing more then a joke. Have you ever looked back and laughed at yourself for dreaming of one day becoming an astronaut? Why do we laugh and shake our heads at the failed dreams of our childhood? Its not funny but rather sad to ridicule that dreamer when you in fact are the failure. So why do we give up on our dreams? When and how does that desire melt away like a dropped snow cone on an L.A. sidewalk? At that point when the dreamer becomes the failed, is that when death begins? Is that the top of the hill with only one way down? No longer are we striving to achieve a dream but now we’re simply surviving. But if that theory holds true then I’m going to live forever. My dream was to become a wrestler and make an impact in peoples lives by entertaining them. I have fully succeeded in that dream and if I win the PCW world title come Sunday I will have done everything I have ever set out to do. I am a dreamer who achieved his dreams, but yet death is still coming. Perhaps the young are the wise and the old are the foolish. The young never stop dreaming even when their dreams are reached. I just told you my dreams have been achieved and I can honestly say that I have nothing else to reach for. The drive is now gone and today I start dying. I hope it’s a slow, steady, and painless death but I have no doubt its all downhill from here. It could strike me tomorrow or in 50 years from now but it’s going to happen. But if death is all I dwell on then I have no more time to learn and collect souvenirs. My feet our wet from thinking this thing over and its been a while since I’ve felt the warm sunshine. So too death I say kiss my ass cus this Malice Man is flying on hoss. Lots of folks are speaking out against the war. Seems like any asshole that’s ever stood in front of a camera or a microphone has an opinion. From Darkstar to Dixie Chicks nobody can keep there mouths shut. All I can tell you my friends is Duke Williams supports his president. I support our troops even more so but this isn’t a political rant it’s my good bye letter. As I sit here in mediation in a society I helped corrupt. Where they grind out sex, and they rate it with an 'X' just to make a young man's pants grow. I can’t help but ponder the future with out me. I hope a young buck will step up to champion the cause of the workingman. To take the role of the good guy and fight off the corruption of the bad and give the people something to cheer for. By the time you read this I will have one show left in my career. It’s kind of funny I haven’t felt butterflies in my tummy since I was a green back rookie. But yet tonight they flutter like an eyelid in the heat. I can’t tell you for sure that I will win the world title but I can guarantee you that I’m going to give more then I’ve ever given before. It’s strange to think that at this point right here my past is nothing more then a build up to this moment. All I’ve ever worked for comes to a boil my last night in the business. Most guys go out with a gold watch and a smile but me, no, I want to go out with a big, gold, belt! Call me selfish but that is a major element in surviving the business. So in a few days it’s all said and done and I want to thank those of you who have supported me. To those who haven’t then you’re probably not reading this right now so I’m not going to waste the space. Graceful going up; graceful coming down. That’s what Vince Gill told me in Nashville when we talked about his 20 plus year career fading out on the charts in recent years. Why get bitter at the young when you were once there your self? No point in it. To those who have read, watched, and followed my work and my career again I must say thank you. The kind words I’ve received over the years have meant so much to me I can’t really put it in words. My hosses and you know if your one or not, you guys have been nothing but the best. Good friends, good fun, and good whiskey summarizes my stay in PCW. My successes are the result of others had work and commitment to the sport. My failures are my soul responsibility and nobody else’s. To those who have carried me, worked with me, and chatted with me, I’m toasting you all right night with a shot of whiskey. To those who have feuded with me and I didn’t do my fair share or dropped the ball I apologizes to you. Sometimes I had to put other commitments first and that’s not your problem but you suffered from my greediness. We get involved in this and beg for responsibility and then when we get it sometimes we forget that when we slack the community suffers. I take my hat off and hang my head in shame to all those I have let down in the past. The staff of any federation puts there all into it everyday. These guys are the real stars of the show and sometimes I don’t agree with them but they know what’s right. They give more to this sport then anybody on the roster and to them I can only say thank you. God bless everybody and lets do it Malice Style one more time. Lets get whiskey bent and hell bound for old times sake. Let the madness begin and the malice fly away like an eagle in the wind. DW=w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
