They came like the black plague (Part 3 of 4)

9 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-9-02 Scorpion walks up the steps very slowly preparing himself to comfort him wife from the pain she felt from her parents telling her what to do. I hate doing this, they come here, get her mad and I pay for it. It must be a plan, a plan of attack. Scorpion walks into the room where is his wife is laying face down on a bed weeping. He walks over slowly, sitting down on the bed. Rubbing her back he begins to calm her down. “Steph, what’s wrong? Don’t let your parents get to you.” Stephanie: I can’t stand it anymore Jeff; I have wept a thousand tears. I have had to listen to them moan and groan of how I didn’t succeed there expectations. They wanted me to marry a lawyer or an accountant, but I married a wrestler and I proud of you. They just wanted me to live ravishing. “They need to realize that you aren’t going to live ravishing, you live with a professional wrestler. I don’t like that caviar shit anyway, if they want you to live like your sister then that’s to bad. At least you love your husband, she just wants his money.” Stephanie: (laughs) Your right Jeff I shouldn’t get worked up over this. Go down stairs and tell my parents I’m getting there room ready, ok? “No Problem.” Scorpion heads down stairs where his mother and father in laws are sitting on the couch watching a video of Scorpion wrestling that Toby is watching. Father in-law: How is Stephanie? “She’s fine, she’s just getting your room settled.” Mother in-law: Oh that is delightful. Jeff this is what you do for a living? “Yeah.” Father in-law: My wife and I never realize how intense this stuff really is. It’s truly amazing how grueling and how much pain you suffer throughout your matches. We have a newfound respect for you. “Well thank you very much. I really appreciate it.” Mother in-law: I don’t have a problem with what you do. I’m just wondering what will you do when you get severely hurt and you can’t hold up your own son or help out your own wife. Is it really worth it? “I respect what you say and believe me that always crosses my mind when I do some of these matches. But you can’t let that fear induce your whole entire body or you will get yourself hurt.” Mother in-law: But for the benefit of Toby and Stephanie you should think about leaving that business, I can get you a job as at a very high position job. “With all do respect, I will never leave this business until I know my body can no longer stay around long enough to be competitive with this sport. It’s a job it has its downfalls and it’s good points just like any other job. I could be walking to my job in the city and get hit by a car. There is always that chance you are taking by doing something, but that is in the back of my mind, I don’t think what could happen.” Mother in-law: Jeff you need to realize what you do is not safe for your family! I don’t think you should be showing your son this shit either! Father in-law: Don’t talk to Jeff like honey. You need to show him respect as well. Mother in-law: I don’t want to see my daughter marry a vegetable! “I think you need to calm down. To say something like that is way out of content and shouldn’t be talked about because I know people that are in a wheel chair for the rest of their life. I know people that don’t even know where they are! To say something like that was outrageous and out of line. You should think twice about saying stuff like that.” Suddenly Stephanie walks down the stairs to where the commotion is taking place. Stephanie: What’s going on? “Nothing.” Mother in-law: I’m expressing the way I feel about Jeff wrestling and possibly getting severely hurt. Making his family pay for what he has done to his body. Stephanie: Mother! What are you thinking? To say something like that is ridiculous! He loves his family and does his best to support his family! Mother in-law: Maybe the best to you isn’t really the best in essence. “In essence? Listen and respect your daughter’s opinion at least one time in your life Mrs. Waffle. All because she has down something the way you didn’t do doesn’t mean you have to try and make her feel bad about herself. I was just up stairs trying to calm Stephanie down because you made her feel shitty about herself.” Mother in-law: I don’t need help from someone like you to tell me how I should treat my daughter. “What’s that suppose to mean?” Mother in-law: It means what you say is bullshit to me. Father in-law: What is wrong with you Mary? Mother in-law: Frank this is outrageous you and I both know our daughter could have done better for herself. Father in-law: Do you realize what you are saying? You are disrespecting your own daughter and her husband in there own house I can’t believe you would do this in front of your own grandchild. Mother in-law: They need to realize that life is not a game. Life is a gift and they shit all over it. “I can’t stand this much longer. You got two choices either cool your jets or getting the hell out of my house cause you will not talk to my wife like that ever again I don’t care who you are.” Mother in-law: Pardon me? “I said I couldn’t stand what your saying and I’m going up stairs. You on the other hand have two choices, don’t talk to your daughter like that again or you can leave.” Mother in-law: This is how you treat a lady? “I’m treating you the way you have treated me for the five years I have been married to your daughter.” Scorpion walks up stairs leaving Stephanie and her parents in the room. Stephanie: I agree with what Jeff said. Either treat me like a normal daughter should be treated or leave. I can’t stand you trying to lower my self-esteem my whole life and I’m finally standing up for myself now. Mother in-law: Stephanie do you realize what you are doing by taking his side? Stephanie: Yes I am and at least he knows how I feel. He treats me with respect and love, unlike you has shown me today. Mother in-law: Stephanie? I cannot believe your decision. Stephanie: Believe it because it just happened. Stephanie walks up stairs leaving her parents in the room. Father in-law: You need to stop doing this, she is your own daughter, she is our daughter and you treat her like she is a piece of dust under the carpet. Mother in-law: No I don’t I tell her the truth and try to help her be successful in this world. Father in-law: No you don’t you try to make her do what you want her to do, live the way she lives and she nor I can not take it anymore. You need to stop this and wake up. Mother in-law: Frank what are you talking about… Father in-law: I’m going to walk outside and you think about what you have said what you have said here today. Mother in-law: I can’t believe this. Mr. Waffle walks outside as she is left inside the living room. Mother in-law: This is the thanks I get! My family turns there back on me because I have voiced my opinion?
