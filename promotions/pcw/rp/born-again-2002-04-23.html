Born Again

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd April 2002 14th of April, 2002. Well known PCW Superstar Charlie Loc had literally disappeared off of the face of the Earth after baring witness to a violent shoot-out in front of his apartment in the China Town of New York City. It was as though he just vanished into thin air….No one seemed to know, No one seemed to care, the only person who even thought about him was his mother. Her tears where the only thing that kept her son Charlie alive, No one else seemed to notice that he was even gone. People would constantly tell her not to worry that he had probably wound up in Juvenile Detention due to his life of crime, but through all the pain of his disappearance his mother kept her faith, and maybe, just maybe it paid off in the long run! It was like a scene from a movie. The grayish and white clouds from the darkened sky were casting their pale light through the kitchen window of a rather small apartment, past a vase of flowers and small three dishes with mirrors in them and on to a wooden table that had a clear plastic cloth covering it, where a frail old Oriental woman was sitting with her chin on her hand. As the wind picked up outside the window, chimes could be heard coming from somewhere in the distance as the petals on the white flowers that were sitting in the glass vase on the window sill began to flutter slightly due to the breeze. The Old Oriental lady strangely seemed to feel this as she shook her head and slowly closed her eyes looking as though it might be the last time in her life that she would. She seemed weary, tired and exhausted, as though she had been waiting for something oh so long, but for some reason it just never came….a faint smile crossed her wrinkly face as a tear drop slowly formed beneath her eye as she opened them again. The room seemed to reflect off of that one tear…it seemed very clean! As though the woman had been stressed and the only way that she could ease the tension was to make sure everything was neat and tidy over and over and over again. Suddenly the wind began to blow harsh and violently outside, the familiar howling that it ever so often gives off began to sound through the kitchen as the Old Lady sitting at the table wiped the tear from her dark cheek and slowly picked herself up from the black steel chair that she was sitting on. It scraped the tiles as she moved it back but she didn’t seem to care as she took her time to get up and walk behind it to push it back in. As she did, the howling of the wind picked up once more as she turned towards the window to see the dark rain clouds moving quickly by. Her feet made soft slapping sounds on the white and sea green patterned tiles as she moved towards the window sill. Above the stainless-steel sink the white curtains began to flutter and one of the three white flowers in the vase shed a petal as she reached up and then pushed out, slamming shut two windows that weren’t even visibly open. Just in time too…as soon as she shut them the thunder seemed to come right on cue, the banging sounds almost exactly on time! There was a rumble of thunder from outside the window as the sweet scent of rain began to fill the apartment as the rain began to fall from the clouds, thick, heavy and soft, hitting the pavement below the apartment. The Old Oriental Lady didn’t seem to phased by this, instead she slid open and reached into a drawer below the sink, producing a small, thin stick which seemed to be something like a sparkler. She slid the drawer back into place and then placed the stick in between the flowers in the vase before picking up two of the three small, mirrored dishes and placing them on top of the center one in a pile. With her other hand she reached into a pouch of the pink apron that she was wearing and produced a small lighter and sparked it up with a click, lighting the end of the small stick before quickly blowing it out, sending a thick trail of smoke which swayed in the air of the kitchen.As nothing had ever happened the Old Oriental Lady placed the lighter back into the pocket of her pink apron and fiddled around before producing a white square of cloth…only it wasn’t a cloth it was a white bandana, which she tied up backwards over her thin gray hair. Once she had finished tying the bandana around her head she then paced back over the tiled floor which began to shimmer in the dull light that was being cast out through the window. The thunder rumbled yet again as she reached behind an oven which was tucked away on the other side of the kitchen to produce a Mop and a bucket which was presumably filled with water. As she dragged the mop and the bucket out from behind the oven some of the water began to splash onto the floor and the familiar riddle began to rise. Is the bucket half-full? Or is the bucket half-empty? The riddle didn’t seem to puzzle the Old Oriental Lady much as she muttered away something in her own language before pulling the mop’s head out of the bucket of water and sending it flopping onto the ground with a SPLAT~! Sound…. As the mop fell to the floor the Old Lady became like a drone, she began to mop up and down, and up and down, in the same place over and over until finally, a reflection could be seen and she moved over to the next group of tiles to do her job. She was clearly depressed, something was eating away inside of her but there was nothing to give off any clues what was wrong! Her dog could’ve died, she could’ve lost something important, it may have been the anniversary of someone close to her….who knew? The only person who did was her, and the only way that she could help herself and find comfort was to mop the floor! The swishing sound of the mopping began to break the silence as the soft sound of the rain outside became heard only when she was dipping the mop back into the bucket to re-dampen it to continue cleaning the floor. The only time that she ever stopped was to wipe a bead of sweat away from above her eyebrow and to push her bandana back into place…. BOOM~! BOOM~! BOOM~! A loud knocking came from a door somewhere behind the Old Lady as she didn’t even seem to care, it was as though she was expecting it! The knocking continued as the Old Lady removed the bandana from her head and put it back into the pocket of her apron and the placed the mop back into the bucket before moving towards the door and turning it’s golden handle to slowly pull it open, taking a quick peak through the gap as it did. As the door slowly swings open the expression on the Old Lady’s face becomes quiet distraught as she steps back a little giving the full view of the person standing in the door way. The Old Lady covers her mouth before shaking her head and muttering something to herself and pulling out the chair from beneath the table and sitting back down. The Old Lady begins to sob as the man steps from out of the darkness of the doorway and into the light, revealing himself to be none other than Charlie Loc. He closes the door behind him and removes his black jacket and adjusts his turtleneck before walking over to the table. His feet clap on the tiles as he makes his way to the table, the chair scraping as he pulls it out from underneath it, as he slings his jacket over the back of chair and then sits down. He reaches out towards the Old Lady’s hand who is now in tears and grabs it, clenching it tight and shaking his head, pushing his tinted fringe out the way of his face. His eyes are filled with no emotion as he stares at the old lady, presumably his mother crying. 'I sorry mother….' The words escape Charlie’s mouth almost dry and rasping his mother continues to cry. She looks up at him with tears still staining her eyes. 'Where you been Charlie?! I been worried sick! Every day you turn out more and more like your brother! Every day you slip away from me further and further! Why you not listen to me?!' His mother replies in an almost young sounding voice amidst tears before letting go of her sons hand and wiping them away from her cheeks. Charlie’s mother begins to speak in her native tongue as Charlie hangs his head low listening to what seems to be a punishment lecture. She wipes away the tears from her eyes before the rain begins to pick up outside the apartment and the thunder rumbles once more. She sniffles before speaking to Charlie in English once more…. 'You still not answer my question Charlie….where you been? I think you should explain what happened before I tell you what you missed!' The stereotypically menacing voice of his mother sounds out over the rainfall. Charlie looks up and exhales heavily before replying. 'Something’s needed to be done…so I did them…' Charlie’s mother begins to shake her head and a look of anger crosses over her face as the tears begin to reform in her eyes. 'What needed to be done Charlie?! What so important that you not tell me and you disappear for a week?! You never think of anyone but yourself….do you?!' The expression still stays ice-cold on Charlie’s face as he hangs his head low in shame as his mothers tears patter as they hit the plastic covering of the table. 'I not want to lose you too Charlie! You everything to me! I losing everyone! Please Charlie….I not want to lose you too!' His mother begins to sob as she reaches out and grabs ahold of Charlie’s hand as he looks up with a confused look on his face. 'What you talking about?' A confused tone pairs up with the confused look on Charlie’s face which is now lit up in a strange light. At the sound of hearing it, the Old Lady begins to weep out loud and the veins on her frail hands protrude as she clutches onto Charlie’s hand for dear life. 'I got a phone call the other day….you’re aunty….Dessie…she die in a car accident the other day!' The words leave Charlie’s mother’s mouth like a whisper as Charlie gulps and his eyes widen. Charlie struggles to hold back tears as he clutches his mothers hands with his free hand as he hangs his head low once more and begins to whisper to her. 'I sorry Mamma…I so sorry!' 'Charlie, we not have much money! We not have much family left, but we need to stick together! I telling you now Charlie, I not bare the thought of losing you too!' Mamma Loc replies amidst tears and sobs. Charlie releases his grip on his mothers hands before leaning back in his chair and a weak smile crossing over his face. He turns around to his jacket and then fumbles around with something in his pocket, as he does a small silver object appears for a few moments before he pushes it back away and removes his hand with a wad of cash. The smile on his face changes and goes back to his usual ice-cold expression as he opens his mothers hand and places the money inside of her palm as she looks up at him with a smile crossing over her old face. 'It not much, but it all I have! You need it more than me Mamma….don’t worry I can survive on my own! All that important to me is that you ok…' Charlie extends his offering to his mother. 'You not need to do this Charlie….but thank you! All I ask is that you help more than you have ever helped me before right now….' Mamma Loc sniffs and wipes away a tear with her hand.Charlie lays back in his chair again, still expressionless as he tilts his head back waiting for his mother to tell him what she wants him to do. She stands up and places the money inside the pocket of her pants and then begins to undo the apron that she is wearing, talking a she takes it off. 'The funeral is tomorrow in Brooklyn, I going to need to leave tonight! While I gone I going to need you to look after someone for me…' Mamma Loc talks as she is hanging up her apron and pushing the mop and the bucket back behind the oven. 'I listening….' Charlie replies as though he’s talking about a business deal, all of a sudden forgetting about his aunts death. 'She waiting for you inside the living room Charlie….She going to be living with us since you’re aunty not going to be around!' Mamma Loc walks out of the room before returning back in with a black suitcase in her hand. She fiddles around with a set of keys and walks over to the door. As she begins to jingle the keys around in the lock she opens the door and leaves them hanging in there before yelling out a reply to Charlie. 'Look after her Charlie….I need counting on you!' The footsteps of Mamma Loc can be heard echoing outside the apartment as the sound and smell of rain begin to pick back up in the kitchen of the apartment. Charlie doesn’t seem to waste too much time as he gets up and places the jacket back on before sliding the chair underneath the table and reaching into the pocket of his jacket. He produces a packet of Wingfield Blue Cigarettes and takes one out of the packet before walking towards the window sill and seeing if the incense is still burning….which it doesn’t seem to be! He takes a lighter out of his pocket and re-lights the incense before sparking up his Cigarette and putting the packet and the lighter back into his jacket before taking a draw and walking through a door in the kitchen and into the living room…. As he enters the living room, a pair of blue eyes from the couch that is beneath him stare up with a shimmer of pride of them. Charlie doesn’t seem to care too much as he exhales the smoke from his cigarette and then takes another draw as the pink haired, blue eyed, Asian girl sitting on the black leather couch stands up. 'You ready to go out…..I got business to take care of!' The meeting seems informal as Charlie nods his head towards the door and the girl’s face changes into a look of confusion. 'I’m Tsuya' The girl’s look of confusion changes into a smile as she literally jumps on top of Charlie as he hugs embraces her and a smile crosses over his usual ice-cold face. She finally stops hugging him before adjusting her white jumper and taking the cigarette from out of the mouth of Charlie. He looks wide eyed at her as she takes a draw and blows a smoke ring into the air of the living room before handing it back to him. 'Last time I see you….you so little, now you like, grown up?' Charlie seems to be quiet impressed by his baby cousin’s maturity to smoke. 'Now you can teach me what you do, can’t you Charlie?' A seductive smile crosses over her innocent face as Charlie smiles back and places his hand around her shoulder as they walk out of the living room. 'You want to be a Gangster….I teach you how to be a Gangster….Tsuya' Charlie nods his head in approval before walking up to the front door and opening it for Tsuya to exit the apartment first. Charlie removes the keys from the door handle and places them in his pocket before clicking the lock from the inside of the door. 'First I told you before….we need to stop off somewhere, I need to pay some White Boy named Duzza a visit!' Tsuya can be seen nodding at Charlie as he doesn’t seem to show any emotion back except for pulling his jacket tight around him and slamming the door shut behind him, leaving the apartment empty. The sound of the rain falling onto the pavement below the apartment can still be heard as the smell of incense and rain wafts through the kitchen of the apartment which is now lonely and deserted. The pale light from the grayish, dark rain clouds begins to glisten off of the spotless tiles as the storm continues to rage on outside the streets of New York. There is no trace of sunlight what so ever as the Storm doesn’t seem to be over, it just seems to have begun.... Having dealt with the harsh reality of his Aunt's death, Charlie has somewhat spent a little time soul-searching and 'growing up' while he was Missing In Action. His mother has given him the responsibility of looking after his gorgeous 17 year old Cousin Tsuya but could this prove to be a burden or a blessing? Whatever the case, something says that Charlie's not getting himself out of trouble, but rather getting himself back in and pulling himself in deeper....maybe too deep?
