The Stupidest Wrestler Ever II

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-30-02 [Fade In] [The aroma of hotel room dances through the air, blown towards our noses by the heating/cooling system over near the window. The shot pans around the rather dark room. It’s the hotel room of Ryan “Streak” Stephens, and his wife Dana. They’ve been living in it since Stephens signed with PCW, but his wife is not here. She’s out searching for a new house for the happily married couple. Stephens is alone, but apparently he has forgotten as he rolls over in his bed, and then pats the empty place next to him twice.] [Stephens comes to full attention, raises his head from his pillow and looking at the empty place next to him before remembering that his wife is down in Texas seeking a new home. Stephens rolls over to stretch and lets out a massive and almost painful groan. It’s not a stretching groan, but rather and painful groan as his clutches his fractured ribs with his right hand. A wincing look is displayed on his face; nonetheless, he sits up on the edge of the bed. With broken ribs it hurts to yawn, and it hurts something awful to cough. In the back of his mind Stephens hopes he doesn’t catch a cold while his ribs are broke.] [He finally rises from the bed and makes his way over to the curtains. The morning light is beginning to seek through, but Stephens spreads them open, and allows numerous rays of the sunlight to beam into his room, causing us to change positions to better film the PCW low-carder (insert snicker here). Stephens then makes his way into the bathroom, where he turns on the water and splashes a little bit on his face to wake himself up. As he opens his eyes clear and wide for the first time this morning he stares at himself in the mirror, but all he can see is Makonie staring back at him rather than his own image.] [Stephens’ intense blue eyes flare up, his jaw clenches tight, and his breathing becomes less relaxed. He stares into the mirror, his mind taking over, envisioning Makonie there glaring back at him, much like the recent stare down on Fusion before Akiyama wiped the mat with Mark (And he did). Stephens begins shaking a little, his mind so anxious to take on Makonie, but instead of punching the mirror he begins speaking as if he’s talking to Makonie himself.] Stephens: Mark, what you don’t understand is that I have you precisely where I want you. Oh I know, it’s so cliché, but I really do. I mean I have you so damn flustered and insecure about yourself, that you actually went to an IQ testing place and had your IQ tested to prove me wrong. You probably wasted half your day going to that place, waiting to be tested, being tested, waiting for the results, and coming back. [Stephens laughs aloud to himself, still glaring at the mirror. His eyes move from straight in front of him down to his injured ribs. He gently rubs his right hand across the ribs, but doesn’t wince. He merely stands there, jaw clenched, like a statue, withstanding the pain. He looks back at his brain’s image of Mark on the mirror.] Stephens: You’re probably thinking you have the upper advantage because I have a couple of bum ribs. I warn you now, do not make that assumption Mark. I’m a man who wants this match so badly, that these ribs will probably not even play a part in the match, and besides that you and all your Nazi’s didn’t give me half the beating that Akiyama gave you on Fusion. Stephens: You said you weren’t looking past Akiyama in your last promo, but in the one before that where I made that conclusion it said [he smiles] and I quote: “Mark is thinking of Stephens right now and about the Pay Per View. Akiyama is not on his mind as that match is not that big, only a mood setter for Gold and Glory.” So you see Mark, you were looking past Akiyama to the big PPV match with me, and thus you received the ultimate beat down from the Neo Void God. Stephens: In fact he gave you a beat down that only a monster like himself is capable of. He bounced you a foot off the mat, then he nearly broke you in half, and finally he pinned you by merely putting his foot on your chest. Bare with me her Mark, but if someone beat you that bad, then you looked past them because after all, you’re a fairly talented wrestler right? If you didn’t look past Akiyama, and you got beat that bad, well then shit, you’re the fucking low-carder Mark, and we can stop this stupid argument. [Stephens laughs and almost evil and sinister laugh, one very uncharacteristic of himself before continuing to try and verbally dismantle Mark Makonie.] Stephens: You waste all your valuable Nazi breath in which you could be preaching to your followers on me Mark! Why? You spend countless minutes thinking of ways to prove me wrong, to retaliate against my statements! Why? Because I’m in your head Mark. I’m in your cranium, and I have you so fucking flustered that you don’t know what to do. Who cares what I say Mark? You yourself said that I’m nothing but a low-carder, so why would a superstar like you care about what I say? Why would a superstar such as yourself try to come back at me with an explanation? It just doesn’t make that much sense to me Mark. Stephens: Stop trying to turn my statements around on me and go train Mark, because I assure you that you will need all the training you can get when you step into the ring with a low-carder looking to ascend himself as I am Mark. You said you were going to bring everything you have at me, and I sincerely hope that you do, because I want to utterly dismantle everything you have Mark. I really do, but I just can’t help thinking that you’re spending too much time worrying about me instead of training so that you can bring everything you have at me! [Stephens has calmed down a little bit now. His eyes still hold their same intensity, but he’s stopped shaking, and his voice his much calmer as he speaks. He runs his hand, still a little damp from the water he splashed on his face through his hair before he continues.] Stephens: It’s not very often that I just stand here and speak to myself Mark. I mean usually I’m out training, writing a rumor so that I get paid extra money, or spending time with my wife, but today when I woke up something told me that I should address you, and therefore I am. The day that you blindsided me in that ring, is the day that you became the stupidest wrestler ever. All this other verbal banter that I spew forth from my mouth is merely further proof that you are the stupidest wrestler ever Mark. Stephens: You’ve worried about me long enough Mark, run along and train now. I want to have an epic battle with you at Gold and Glory. I want to show the world that I can beat a superstar like Mark Makonie on top of his game WITH fractured ribs, because Mark that’s what I do. Every time something tries to hold me back, I persevere, I fight through it and I prevail. Since I was a young rookie in ninety eight, I possessed what some call a heart of gold, because NEVER, EVER, do I give in or quit, it’s not in my makeup Mark. Stephens: While you’re out getting your IQ tested, I’m hitting the weights, I’m watching film, and I’m learning about you Mark. Your stupidity proceeds yourself Mark. You scored a seventy-five on your IQ test and you believed the lady when she told you that low was good in this case. Seventy-five is almost virtual retardation Mark. An average American scores at least a hundred. All you did by taking that test was prove to everyone who was still unsure of my statements that you are truly the stupidest wrestler ever. After all let’s look at question number two on your test Mark. [Stephens is chuckling to himself, and can’t seem to stop long enough to talk. Every time he tries to begin again, he just keeps laughing. Finally he gives in, and merely dabs his finger with water, and then with soap and then begins writing on his mirror. He writes Makonie’s problem number two and the answer in this fashion on the mirror in front of him.] Question 2: What is 49 + 35?Mark’s Answer: 85 [Stephens is now hunched over in pain from laughing so hard, with his injured ribs. Earlier he acted as though they weren’t affecting him, though we assume he was trying to act tough. Now he clenches his ribs in obvious pain as he makes himself stop laughing.] Stephens: Owww. This is one instance in which laughter is definitely not the best medicine. Now you probably were worried about the time, or hell you were probably smiling, thinking you were going to prove me wrong, but you fucking missed the second fucking question on the IQ test Mark. Forty-nine plus thirty-five is and always will be eighty-four, not eighty-five you stupid fucking moron! [Stephens sighs, he’s made his point, what else can he say?] Stephens: Tonight is the big night Mark. The night in which your personal pride will spiral lowly down to the depths of nothingness. Before I end this, let me leave you with this statement Mark… Stephens: There is a journey that brings about extraordinary change. Change that takes place within. A personal transformation that turns fear into courage. Doubt into confidence. A transformation that hones your mind to razor-sharp perfection. This journey takes a long while but the change is forever. I am Ryan Stephens! I was born in an inferno that tests both mind and body. I am one of the few who has managed to complete the challenge. I have become a beacon of courage, honor and commitment. I believe that pain is weakness leaving the body. The question isn’t how much more I can take, but just how much more I can give. Just when I’m ready to quit my mind says push harder, I listen, sensing an inner strength that wasn’t there before, and suddenly I discover I no longer feel the pain. It is then and only then that you are like me! [Stephens has a look of satisfaction on his face, a look of confidence. He’s prepared very well for this match, and he isn’t over confident. He expects an epic match in which both men batter and bruise one another. He yearns for a match that will last a long while, so that he can physically and mentally dismantle is stupider opponent. He knows Makonie is a solid wrestler who will put up a tough fight, and yet he also knows that it’s his to lose. The ribs won’t play a factor, he wants it to bad. His mind is in a state of utter preparation, and his heart has never failed him before. Tonight is most definitely Stephens’ night to shine, to prove the whole world, and all the critics wrong, and he will! HE WILL!]
