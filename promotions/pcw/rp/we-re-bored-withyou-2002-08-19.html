We're bored withyou...

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19 August 2002 “Bastards…” Humans are so predicable… “I’m tired of looking at them…” …Boring… “You would think that they’ve gotten a bad enough beating from you by now…but no!” …Repetitious… “Same old flat-scans…same old weaklings…” …Overall…a huge drag on the rapid progression of the Neo Void God. “Three faces against you? You’re supposedly outnumbered in this match up…but that’s nonsense.” Turmoil, Akiyama faces off against three men that have been thrashed by the nVg at one point or another. The Pharaoh…Duke Williams…and Chris Chase. A fatal four way match…the main event. “I know you’re tired of looking at them…going match after match against them.” With the status of being god, comes the reoccurring moments of sheer boredom. “I would hope by d@mn sure that they’d be bored of getting their @sses kicked by you by now. Even the dog knows when to heel when whacked enough times. Even the child knows not to touch the stove after being burned.” Gods have sat back and watched humanity repeat their flaws over and over…a result of their own DNA and species genetics. Be it in different form, the sin still remains the same. “This shows that these idiots are dumber than dogs and children…” Fornication…Murder…Theft…Adultery… Mankind giving into their greed and their lust, casting aside their morals as a snake would cast aside their skin. Speaking of snakes… “El Diablo still proves to be dumber than all of the above though.” Yeah…for some reason or another this guy must be getting hard or high from the pain Akiyama’s dishing out upon him. Either way, it doesn’t sit well with the god. And when something isn’t going the god’s way…he goes towards making it IN HIS WILL. “There’s only so much pain that one man can take…so we’ll have to push this threshold of his sooner or later. We’ll have to see if this Devil can die…because we’ll be damned if we’re gonna let some Movie Directing Devil-Wannabe walk away after @sskicking and @sskicking from your hands.” Things do get boring after a while…it’s so boring how El Diablo just wont die… his repetitious returns for more suffering only proves to be more droll entertainment for the nVg. That’s all El Diablo really was…Entertainment… A practice dummy for when Akiyama would really need to put forth effort. Xavier Kannon? Sounds like the right man the god’s been waiting for… “I’m tired of all their jackassery…this foolishness that Xavier Kannon keeps throwing in front of us to stall us from kicking his Hubbard humping @ss. And whoever gets booked in our way because of that Ela-Whore Hall-Kannon will just have to be the sacrifices for the god in his surging rampage for destruction of one Scientologist!” When man becomes too big for his britches…just because of a little power surging to his head…and just because everything is going his way because of the strings he can pull and the anuses he can lick… When a man thinks of himself as almost …godly… That’s when Akiyama will step up and set the mortal back in his place. It happened to Pharaoh…. he thought he was a god…and now look at him… a sandy-@ssed cheap Egyptian with a poor Frisco-army rip off. That had to be the most eye-gouging scene with the armies fighting backstage…like this was some sort of battlefield in Afghanistan. You want a battlefield…you step into the ring…and if you want to remind yourself of how pathetic you are in this world…you step into the ring against Akiyama… He’ll remind you right away that you’re nothing more than a flesh sack of bones, water, and organs. Kannon and Diablo…just like L U C I F E R …they think themselves to be better than the god…but in all honesty that they hide from themselves…they FEAR the god…and they CANNOT defeat the god…and they shall be cast down from their high posts in their own mental H e a v e n into the cold, yet fiery pits of the shame of losing Victory…being Overwhelmed… Insignificance… and Defeated. “All of these POSERS trying to be more than just human will pay for their crimes…and the humans will pay for their sins and their defiance! It’s pure blasphemy to stand against you again after having tasted the pain that the god dishes out in whopping super-sized servings! The Pharaoh…the Saint…and the Malice Man shall all receive an encore of anarchy from the Neo Void God!!” But that’s just fine…you humans shall always prove to be predictable…always letting your smaller attributes lead you around by the nose…where larger attributes land you in painful situations. You do the math… It’s that predictability that makes your repeated offense all the weaker… And the god yawns at the sight of your names… Please…do try and be a little more original about your attacks… “If we see Duke’s smutty comments, Chase’s asinine tomfoolery, or Pharaoh’s soap-operatic Frisco-copying tales of troops again…Banishment may just be on the menu for Turmoil. “ “If Kannon and Diablo consist with their blasphemy…they just might find their lives a living H E L L.” Heh…rest assured…when the god gets bored with you mortals… Your lives will suddenly turn out interesting… Though that turn…may just be… For the worse… A cold stare graced the face of the Divine Prophetess as she and the Neo Void God walked off into the night streets…the current trek to escape the boredom of the current challenges lying in their path…
