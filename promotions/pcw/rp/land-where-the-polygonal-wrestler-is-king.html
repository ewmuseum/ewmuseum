Land Where The Polygonal Wrestler Is King

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12.2.02(#1 FF, AD) “Welcome, Ray Ray, to the Conference of the Mathematical Peoples of The United States.” That is how we are greeted as the promo opens. Rush Mason’s voice, seemingly suspiciously calm, meaning that some sort of evil plan is probably in the works. “Tonight, we shall strike back at those who consider themselves the upper echelon of geeks, those who understand trigonometry and calculus.” The scene fades in, as Rush and Ray Ray stand before what seems to be a large convention center or building that might exhibit such a fine conference as the aforementioned conference of mathematical peoples. Rush: Yes, in the fine tradition of opponents finding new comical monikers for the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid and berating him in ways never before seen, we shall strike a blow to his people! The manager of Mason pays little attention as his employer continues to rant on about the evil of math. Perhaps this might have something to do with a certain 10th grade geometry class that Rush failed to understand, but let perpendicular bisectors be perpendicular bisectors and let’s forget that. Rush: First, Ray Ray, m’boy, it’s off to the land of the geekiest appliance on Earth….CALCULATORS! With a superhero-esque turn, Rush heads off for one of the stands, going off into nerd land, with Ray Ray following not so close in tow, devouring an enormous sub, filled with various meats that might’ve once been an animal product. Ray Ray: They got sammiches in the upper echolon? The slaughtering of the English language goes remarkably unnoticed, though, as Ray Ray heads off for where Rush now stands: in front of a stand holding thousands of scientific calculators. “How may I help you, sir?” The voice emanates from a small, spectacled, man standing behind a counter. He stands about a foot shorter than Rush, who is at least twice the size of the human toothpick, but still seems relatively cheerful. Rush: I’m looking for the most powerful thing here. None of this dinky crap you’ve got in the front of the stand. I want one of those ones that you have to have an IQ of, like, over five billion to get. Toothpick boy gives a short chuckle, although Rush fails to find anything humorous about his own words, and of course Ray Ray couldn’t really give less of a care in the world, for he has his lovely sandwich as company. “Sir, I do have something back here that may just blow your circuits, but keep this all on the down lo’, know what I’m talking about, homie g? The idiotic use of fake Ebonics almost elicits and instinctive response of “Nuthin. Just chillin” from Ray Ray, but Rush throws an arm out, trying to prevent both men from the public humiliation that often results when Ray Ray opens his mouth without food around. Rush: Uhh…yeah….whatever. Just give me the big one. While the pencil-thin avenger of mathematics searches in his small cabinet for the ultra-powerful calculator, Ray Ray makes a terrifying realization. It takes off all of his willpower to stop himself from shrieking as he whispers, fear apparent in his voice, to Rush. Ray Ray: Rush, dawg….my sandwich is gone! The Voodoo Villain rolls his eyes and then turns to the giant of girth. He is quite unsympathetic in his response. Rush: Didn’t you eat it? It takes a moment for the large semi-intelligent being to process this, but then he responds, in a way that would end this conversation for most people. However… Ray Ray: Yeah… If Rush had any hair, he’d be pulling it out right now at the sheer ignorance of his manager, yet somehow, he manages to keep relative composure as he hisses back to his manager as he turns around. Rush: Go find another, you big dummy! With that, Mason turns around just in time to see the lord of lean come up with what looks like something between an iPod and some sort of alien ray gun. “Here you go sir, our most powerful model: the Calculomaster 6700bx.” Rush gives a small roll of his eyes towards the grand introduction that the god of geeks gives to him. He looks down at the calculator, and seems remarkably unimpressed. “Would you like a demonstration of exactly how it works?” Giving the small electronic machine a once-over, Rush combines a shrug with a nod of his head, making for what translates into an affirmative answer. He continues to watch on while the skinny sheik begins to punch some buttons, seemingly in no set pattern. “Now, I’m just going to begin by taking the cosine of your height, which would normally be in millimeters, but just for show, I’ll go with hands, which are-” Of course, Rush, not being a total moron like a certain manager of his, interjects, throwing a hand into the emaciated officer’s face and cutting him off, quite rudely, in mid-sentence. Rush: I know what a hand is. Please do continue, Prince of Poindexters. Oh, we’re far from over with using the royalty metaphors, so I hope you’re not sick of them yet. Our glasses sporting host continues with his demonstration, continuing his random punch. “Now, we’ll take your shoe size. That looks to be about a 15.” Rush begins to show signs of boredom and irritation as he taps his foot impatiently. The exhibit attendant takes the cue, and does some quick math, finally grinning and coming up with the answer he was looking for from the mini super computer. “Your name, sir, is Rush Mason, and you’re 24 years old.” PCW’s next Continental Champion looks up at the small man and his jaw drops somewhat. However, it’s not exactly what it seems… Rush: It took all that work for you to figure out something that millions, if not billions, of dumbfucks worldwide know! Hell, there’s more than that, just in my bio…loser. The man becomes quickly defensive, showing off several of the calculator’s functions, while Rush doesn’t bother to feign paying attention any longer. “Well, it’s all about the fact that we solved it with a mathematical formula and that we’re able to-” However, Rush grabs the calculator, looking at it for a moment. He then looks back up at Starvin Marvin and gives a contrite, obnoxious retort. Rush: I’ve got a terrific formula. If I take the diameter of my foot… With that, he drops the calculator and stomps on it wildly, ending up with a shell of what might’ve been a calculator once long, long ago. He looks down and grins at his work. Rush: …and I can find the distance that it takes to go back to my car and leave Nerdmania. With a chuckle to himself at how brilliantly devious he can be, Rush walks off in the direction of the exit and presumably the parking lot, very impressed with himself for the day, while the scene fades to black, but not before we hear Ray Ray’s voice, harassing another mathematical fiend. “Dawg…any of those formulas make for a sammich?” “No, sorry sir, but if you’d like to try some isosceles ice cream or parallel puffs, they’re at the Sine Snack Bar!” “Isosceles? That some kind of STD?”
