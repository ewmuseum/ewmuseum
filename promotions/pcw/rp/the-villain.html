The Villain...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: (Turmoil #3, Fusion#1) How frayed are the lifelines of a man’s own heart… Is love that much a power of humanity? One could think so…love can alter lives, destroy them, and create them. Then sometimes, love can do all three, destroy, create, and alter…all within one person. “I’ll destroy him!!” Enter Franco Xavier… age 27. Born and raised in the aristocracy of Paris. He was a nice boy…with loving parents making them a wonderful family. The son of an American ambassador, he was sometimes ridiculed for not being like the other French children. But he showed them a quick lesson or two with his fists. Fighting was his answer…a loner in a different world that refused to accept him. Sound like anyone you know? “I swear on my life that I’ll make him pay!” One day, our little friend Franco ran across a very special young lady during his youthful and carefree days in high school. They hit it off right away, and they both fell in love. ..pledging each other’s lives to the other so that they would always be together in life and death. Everyone thought of them as a happy couple…and marriage was foreseen in their near future. Then one fine day…something happened that ruined his life forever. It happened at the University of Versailles, along the French Riviera. Graduate school, where our happy couple were attending. They had made some nice friends in that school, names you would know like Yuri “Quest” Takazawa, and supermodel Charlotte Virtuoso Alexandros. Here’s another famous name you might recognize: Akira Akiyama. Yeah, Akiyama went to graduate school in France. He sure as hell got around this planet. Anyway, Franco soon found his lady growing further from him…and closer to Akiyama. “Akiyama!” Then she left him altogether. “Akira Akiyama!!” The story was that Akiyama had proposed to her, and she accepted. Others said that she had grown so infatuated with the present nVg that she grew delirious with love. Then some say that Franco forced her into his arms because he was suffocating her. Either story you believe, there’s one ending to it. Akiyama left… And with him leaving, she fell deeper into the spiral of insanity. Franco was crushed that he had lost his one and only true love…but he was even more upset with the fact that Akiyama would disrespect her by just up and leaving without a word. Any person that would force that much pain upon any sweet woman like Willow Marquette should be made to pay, said Xavier. And so he plotted for his revenge for years…all under the cover of night as during the day he accumulated wealth to fund his vengeance. All so that one woman’s heart, mind, and soul could be saved. Hero? Villain? Lunatic? All one in the same, you lucky people. “Yes…soon I will make you pay for your crimes…” A yacht sped along the crystal waves towards the tropical islands ahead of them. At the head of the boat stood a man dressed in a silver trench coat, black slacks, and a white mock tee shirt. His neat black and purple highlighted hair was tied back in a short ponytail. A pair of black shades were upon his face, with his direction facing his destination. Determination plastered his face, right down to the grim mug he held. He whipped a fencing foil up to his face, the blade nearly grazing the skin. “I don’t care if you are some sort of god…you’re going to die here and now. Then I can take my Willow all for myself…BWAAHAHAHAHAA!!!” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “I have a bad feeling…” God and Prophetess…walking along the beaches of Haiti…decked out in vinyl and PVC. Yes, stares were imminent from the other swimsuit wearing pedestrians. The Neo Void God’s steps sunk deep into the sand as Cyclonica stepped lightly. “Yes…there’s something not right about booking you for two #1 contender’s matches for two different belts. PCW is not going to let you become eligible for both of those belts…they’re trying to set you up for a defeat. Those self-righteous bastards just cannot bear with the fact that you’re tearing a hole into the collective ass of the company, so they have to set you up!!” Akiyama clenched his fists a bit tighter at his sides. Cyclonica folded her arms, her brow wrinkled in thought. “Yeah, I disapprove of that as much as you. That’s why we’re going to show just why we’re the god of destruction around this realm. Turmoil…no one walks away! And Fusion…El Diablo gets sent off in a stretcher! SO SAYS THE DIVINE PROPHETESS!” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “Sir! Target has been spotted in our sights! What are your orders?” The ponytailed man’s brow wrinkled. “Open fire.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “By the Void, walking in heels on the beach is a b!tch!” Cyclonica stopped to pull the tall spike of her heel out from the sand. Just then, two huge BOOMS rock out from across the waters. Everyone looks out to the ocean to find a sleek yacht making a beeline for the shore with TWO HUGE GUNS ON THE SIDE! People scream and scatter in all directions as the beach heaves up in explosions of sand. Cyclonica looks on with an uninterested glare towards the speeding boat. “…Man never learns…” she hissed under her breath. Akiyama turned around and cracked the joints in his neck, staring dead on at the ramming boat with nothing but fire in his eyes. Explosions rocked all around the Celestial Duo, but they stood their ground where the mortals ran in fear. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The man glared ahead, a look of disappointment covering his face. “Why isn’t he dead yet, people?” he asked, more like a demand for an answer. A female voice rang out from the intercom above him. “The target seems to be standing his ground, looking us dead on, sir! The gunners are trying, but at our current velocity, they cannot really make direct contact with a target that size!” The foil at his side scratched at his boot. Then he nodded as he slipped his hands behind his back. “Miss Shido…I’m going overboard.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The yacht rocketed ahead, growing closer and closer to shore. The hull of the ship crashed into land, kicking up a wave of sand at the feet of the nVg. His eyes shot up as a battle cry came screaming from the boat. Then all of a sudden the man in the silver trench coat lunged off the boat into the air. “DIE!!!” Akiyama looked back at Cyclonica with a questioning look. Then he stepped to his left and stretched out his arm. The attacker looked on with a face of panic as he fell neck first into the god’s arm, flipping him back and sending his head crashing into the sand. Cyclonica walked over to the man stuck head down in the sand and rolled her eyes. “Only human…just like the fools that dare to challenge you.” Akiyama raised his foot to end the job, but the sound of gunfire rang out from above. They looked to their side to find an attractive Japanese woman in a black leather catsuit carrying a sniper rifle, with a number of other guns strapped to her shapely waist. The orange haired woman aimed the rifle at the two, locking her sight dead on the head of the god. “I wouldn’t do that, mister.” Cyclonica turned to face her. “You think you can stop us with ONE of your mortals weapons?? Akiyama…DESTROY HER!” She cocked her eyebrow up at her. “ONE?” Just then, an entire squadron of troopers donned in black combat gear filed out from the blind side of the boat. They all took up ready positions, their assault rifles cocked and aimed for the Celestials. Cyclonica inched her foot back slowly, her eyes trained on each muzzle of their guns. “I digress…” Just as they took off, their guns started blasting. The woman motioned for them to make pursuit, as she moved in to check on the man who was beheaded earlier by the arm of the Neo Void God. She pulled him up, lying him against the hull of the ship. Then she stood up and sighed, her hands going to her hips. “Will—.” “Where’s Willow??” The man jumped back to his feet, fresh as new. The woman next to him let out another exasperated sigh as he looked around the area. “Welcome back, sir.” She said. “I sent the squad to pursue and detain the target. You should go back on board and take a rest.” He scoffed at the Japanese orange-haired waif. “Nonsense, Miss Shido. I shall rid myself of this fool once and for all, proving that he’s no hero to these people! Then after Willow sees me standing in glory, I shall have her heart back right here…” he pats his chest with his hand lightly. “In your shirt pocket, sir?” Shido asked drolly. He looked at her with an annoyed look, then nodded. “Yes…in my pocket. Now…off we go!” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- “Why in the world do you have FRANCO XAVIER shooting at you?” Cyclonica asked as they ran off through the streets of Port-Au-Prince, past many of the locals, and many tourists as well. Fruit stands exploded as a hail of gunfire forced the people down to the ground. Akiyama gave a quick shrug as they turned a corner. Cyclonica rolled her eyes at the god as she clicked hard in her tall heels. “Yeah, you WOULD have no idea, wouldn’t you??” she snapped. “Either way, you’re going to have to take care of this before I get myself involved! And if I get involved, the body count’s going to RISE!” That sounded like an Akiyama match… if he gets involved, the body count rises. The gun squad came rounding the bend and let off another round of lead for our favorite Divine couple. They dashed past an alley and disappeared around the bend…but deep within the darkness of the alley stood the Neo Void God and the Divine Prophetess. “Now…Franco Xavier is trying to kill you…would this have anything to do with this whole Willow Marquette situation I had a prophecy about the Sunday night?” A short nod from the god is more than enough answer for her. She paced in the alley thinking over the situation quickly. Then she turned to the nVg and pointed her finger into his chest. “If they want to shoot off lead…we’ll bring out the hardware personally.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Franco Xavier and Miss Shido walked along the war-torn streets with their eyes peeled for Akiyama. Shido’s sniper rifle was strapped to her back, and looked imposing to the vacationers. Just then, her headset radio squawked to life. “We have lost sight of target, Miss Shido!” an officer reported. She nodded before looking to Xavier. “They lost sight, sir.” “By the beard of Lucifer himself! I’ll find him if it’s the last thing I ever do!!” Shido sighed as she held the microphone to her lips again. “Keep a search on him, and if you don’t find him in the next half hour, report back to the ship. Do you copy?” “WE SPOTTED HIM! REPEAT: WE HAVE SPOTTED HIM!!” “They spotted him, sir!” she exclaimed. Then she heard gunfire in her ear. “Do not terminate! I gave no order for gunfire!!” “HE’S ATTACKING! THE BULLETS AREN’T STOPPING HIM!! IT’S LIKE HE’S ENCASED IN SOME SORT OF EXOSKELETON ARMOR! HALF THE SQUAD IS DOWN!!” “Report back to base NOW!” she ordered with urgency. “HE’S CHASING ME!! I’M…ALMOST AWAY FROM HIM!! I…I think I lost him when I climbed into this tree. Oh God…I’ll never forget that in my life…he was like…inhuman.” F/X glared over to his assistant. “What’s going on, Shido?” “Akiyama took out most of the squad, sir!” “WHAT?” a surprised look fell over him. “How could he take them out??” “He had some armor around him that kept the bullets from making mark. It’s strange…we use nothing but armor-piercing bullets, and they still couldn’t make mark.” “OHMYGOD!!! HE’S BACK!!! HE’S PUSHING THE TREE OVER!!!” gunfire was heard…along with the sound of ricocheting metal. “I CANT STOP HIM!!” a huge roar erupted before the man screamed. Then there was DEAD…air. Shido looked up with a bit of a worried face at F/X. She cut her thumb across her throat slowly. F/X’s face grew angrier. “Grrrr….Akiyaamaaaa!!!” “Hey there, flat-scans!” Cyclonica stood behind the two of them with her arms folded across her chest. Shido whipped out a pistol and aimed straight for the head of the Divine Prophetess. Cyclonica wasn’t intimidated by the flashy weapons. “You want Akiyama? Be at the beach in an hour…” She disappeared into a crowd of people as F/X and Shido gave a nod of agreement. Then she looked at him with another worried look. “Trap?” she asked. “Maybe…” was all he got as a reply. “Do you really want to go through with this?” she asked him now, a small plea in her voice. “I mean, sometimes its best to leave the past in the past.” “Miss Shido…when you lose your love to a force who’s name is unspeakable, then you can know how I feel. Now, let’s go get Akiyama.” Franco walked off to battle, leaving Shido looking on worried. She lowered her head a bit as she followed. “I already have…sir.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- There stood Akiyama…minus the Void Amor. Alone on the beach…staring out at the waters. To the left of him, clear waters…to his front, clear waters…and to his left, a attack yacht owned by one Franco Xavier. All was calm…all was silent… Then the familiar battle cry of F/X rang out from above. It rang for a long…long time… Then Akiyama took two steps to his left and stuck out his arm like last time. And just like last time…F/X got his head taken off and knocked into the sand like an ostrich hiding. F/X pulled himself out and stood to his feet, facing the nVg with hand in coat. “Come on now, you love-stealing fiend! It’s time you got your desserts! En garde!!” He pulled out his rapier and held his ground against the god. Akiyama stood there silently, his arms still at his sides. Then F/X lunged at him, his foil making repeated jabs at the god at an amazing speed. He actually managed to make the nVg move from his post in the sand! Akiyama countered his thrusts with his always impervious defense. The biggest insult, however, was how he dodged the attacks with his arms folded on his chest. On board the yacht, Shido stood with her hands to her face, shaking her head in pity. “This is a shame to watch.” “Why’s that?” Cyclonica suddenly appeared next to on the boat. Shido motioned towards the “fight” going on down on the beach. “That! It’s clearly obvious that this Akiyama is levels above his fighting potential. I mean, nothing personal to Mr. Xavier, but he couldn’t fight a cold better yet Akiyama.” “I wish everyone that tried to prove that fact wrong were as smart as you.” The Divine Prophetess placed a hand on Shido’s shoulder. “Willow Marquette is in Japan. If you go there, you should be able to find her in Sendai. Deal with this crap and leave us be.” Shido gave her a quizzical look. “Why are you telling me this?” Cyclonica glared into her hard. “Don’t question me. I only wanted to sever our link with this Willow character, as well as the rest of the god’s past. That’s all. Now get the hell out of here before I decide to have him banish you all.” She looked down at the fight and raised an eyebrow. “As a matter of fact…AKIYAMA!” He glanced up and saw her put up her fist. He immediately responded by drilling a HAND OF FATE straight into the chest of Franco Xavier! F/X flew backwards and landed back first in the sand, his hands clutching his chest and his face twisted in pain. Shido leaped off the boat to aid her boss. “Let’s go, Mr. Xavier. We’re done here.” “We’re…NOT!” he strained out of his caved chest. “Akiyama still STANDS!!” He stood back up and ran for the nVg with his foil ready to pierce. Akiyama sidestepped and swatted at the sword with two fingers. F/X stopped in his tracks and held up a now broken rapier. “CURSE YOU, GOD MAN!” He exclaimed with his teeth gritted. “I, Franco Xavier, SHALL PUNISH YOU!! PREPARE YOURSELF!” “I know where Madam Marquette is, sir.” Shido announced, hoping to stop him in his tracks. She succeeded. F/X froze with a surprised look in her direction. “Let’s go, Miss Shido.” He and Shido climbed aboard the boat and the crew pulled out for the waters as fast as they could. F/X stood at the head of the ship glaring down at Akiyama with his fist shaking. “We’re not done here, Akira Akiyama! I will defeat you…in the name of Willow!!” And away they went…off into the waters towards their next destination… Cyclonica joined Akiyama’s side as they watched them leave. She clasped her hands together at her stomach and nodded in a relaxed fashion. “What some foolish mortals will do because of the love they claim...I tell you, Akiyama...Some days are just too easy…” she cooed with a slight smile. “Turmoil and Fusion are going to be some of those days. Let’s go.” He gave her a questioning look, which she responded with a shrug of her own. “What was I doing on the boat? Oh…looking at their lovely glass bottom viewing window.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Franco Xavier stood in the midst of the yacht’s cabin, which was flooding with water. Behind him, Shido and the crewmen were rushing for their lives trying to figure out some way to plug the C shaped cracks in the glass bottom. F/X grimaced off into the distance. “I’ll get you for this, Akiyama…mark my words…” “Sir! I don’t think he did this! I think it was that woman with him!” Shido yelled from across the room while trying to get something to plug the hole. F/X nodded slowly, his mind focused more on revenge than fact. “Yes…Akiyama will pay dearly…” ---Fin---
