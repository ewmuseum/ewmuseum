False Beliefs

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.5.02 6.5.025:26 pm.Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. The rain drips like a leaky faucet, hitting the ground with ease. The rainy day leaves everybody indoors today. Instead of the kids playing hide-and-seek on Gordon Street, they snuggle away in bed, tired from the rainy day. The elderly couple that was on the portico swinging and loving on each other stay inside, also. The woman is in the kitchen whistling the Andy Griffith tune, and the gentleman sits back in his recliner, “watching” Soap Operas, although his eyes appear not to be open. As usual, the local weather station was correct again this morning, “Goooooood morning, Pittsburgh! Today is going to be a wet one, with showers galore. All of Pennsylvania is to be expecting random rain showers and possible scattered thunderstorms. Don’t let it keep you down, though. Outside, the temperature is still warm, and everything will be back to normal tomorrow!” Yeah right. I don’t see how weather can make everything normal, again. I close the blinds in my dark hotel room and lay back on the bed, clasping my hands together behind my head, staring at the white ceiling. It seems that ever since I lost Erin, I haven’t really been able to sleep. I usually just stay up, either staring at the ceiling, or writing away on some pieces of paper, getting my feelings out. There’s not much to do with my life anymore…It’s mind-numbing, irritable, and above all else…self ruining. I sigh and look over to the alarm clock on the stand. The red numbers show that it’s a little bit after five thirty in the afternoon. Well, my options were pretty simple. I could either sit here or dwell on my past, or I could write. I chose to write. Reaching over to the table with the alarm clock, I pull out the small drawer. I seize a small pad of paper and a black pen, opening the first page. Instead of writing right away, I notice a post from a couple months ago… -- 2.14.0212:08 pm.Fuck Cupid. Fuck you. Fuck God. You took her from me. What am I to do without the love of my life? Instead of bringing love, you took it with harmful intentions. Just like you took my mother. Just like you took my father. Just like you took the next best thing to my mother! WHY!? Why do I do this to myself, you ask? I do nothing that has anything to do with you. Get out of my life. I sit here, and I write on this piece of paper, afraid that somebody may find it. Then, I sit and I wonder why I am so scared. I have nothing to be frightened of, for all that frightens is the frightener in my nightmares. A lurid nightmare is what I live…the blood stains the towels that I wash with. There is no washing away what I now have. I tell myself that I’m not crazy. I just want to destroy my self existence. There’s just no way of getting around this life, without falling back into the same fucking hole. Fuck Cupid. Fuck You. Fuck God. Happy Valentine’s Day. -- I close the notebook and heave it across the room at the television. Now, here I am back in the present. I really find no way to prove that my life has changed any, because it hasn’t. It hasn’t changed at all…in fact…it is ten times worse. Valentine’s Day. That used to be one of the best days of my life. It may have only happened once, but that day fulfilled a lifetime of experiences for me. -- 2.14.939:12 pm. “Don’t open your eyes. It’s a surprise,” I told her and smiled as I drove my blindfolded girlfriend down a lone country road.’ “Where are you taking me?” She giggled. “Tell me, Kris!” She pleaded with a huge smile as I drove forward, looking at her beautiful face about every tenth of a second. “It’s a surprise, Erin. If it wasn’t really a surprise, I would tell you!” I said and laughed as I came to a dead end. I knew exactly where I was, so it was no problem, as I made a right on Sunset Blvd. “I can’t wait. You’re always having these romantic tricks up your sleeve!” She smiled and rested her head back on the headrest, with her gorgeous sparkled red dress glimmering in the moonlight. “Don’t peek,” I warned her. I didn’t want her to see anything that was happening. It was all planned and I wanted her to know nothing of it. 15 minutes later, we had arrived at the chosen destination. Erin was beginning to get antsy and almost begun to itch at the dark coloured blindfold. I was actually more nervous than she could ever be. I opened the passenger door of the black Grand Dam and helped her out of it. The light of the moon seemed to shine down on us for the time being, as I aided her through the dark night. As I found the right spot, I got behind her and begun to untie the blindfold. “Ohh…I can’t wait,” I could. I was edgy as hell. I didn’t know what was going to happen. “Here we are…I call it Kris’s World of Luvvvvvv,” That’s the first time I’ve ever said anything with such…how do I say it…’skill.’ I pulled the blindfold off with one quick movement and watched. “Oh my God…,” I watched her reaction as her eyes widened with excitement. I guess that the picnic under a waterfall at night must be very romantic. As the night went on, we had a candlelight dinner, just talking about good times, before sitting on the blanket staring into each other’s eyes. It was the happiest moment of my life. But, things were just getting started. It was time. “Erin…,” I almost stuttered the words out. Here goes. “Yeah…?” It was like she didn’t see anything coming for miles. “Well…,” I began the most crucial speech of my life. “I know that we’ve only been together for a couple months…but I know that you feel the same way that I feel for you. Every chance that I get to be with you, I take advantage of. There’s no way to explain the emotions…Tonight was a great night. It was one of the best I’ve had in a while…and most of all, I got to spend it with you. What I’m trying to say is…well…I want to have days like this for the rest of my life.” Her eyes widened as she stared at me, her eyes beginning to puff up into tears, like she discerned what was about to ensue. I got up from my sitting position, and made the move to my right knee, bringing out a small black case from my back pocket. “Erin Saunters…will you marry me?” I said it…and damn, was it hard to say. She just smiled at me…Fuck; I knew it was the wrong thing to do. I knew I should have waited. I knew I should hav... “Kris...I love you...,' This was the way any rejection would start. I knew it was over.'Of Course I will.”What a turn around! I was bowled over. The woman of my dreams had just said yes to marry me. She was not only my Valentine, but now my future wife. Life was turning out for the best. Nothing could ever go wrong. -- 6.5.025:42 pm.Back to the Present I heaved another sigh and got up from my sitting position on the bed. I opened up the blinds again, peering into the now shady sky. The rain was letting down more than usual, as I gazed upon what would be another lively night. Silence was in the air, as I looked down from my hotel room. I could hear some sort of church bell in the distance… “And do you Kris Burden, take Erin Saunters to be your lawfully wedded wife…through richer and poorer, for better and worse…to have and to hold, till’ death do you part?” I closed my eyes. “I do.” My throat seemed to be blocked, as I found it hard to speak. “By the Power Invested in me…I now pronounce you husband…and wife. You may now kiss the bride.” I opened my eyes, and looked back out the window, praying that I would give up the ghost. “Fuck You.” End.
