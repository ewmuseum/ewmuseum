The Rise above mediocroty

19 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/06/03 In the course of a week, we are all involved in numerous situations that need to be dealt with through force; this occurs at work, at home, and plenty of other places. A conflict is one in which there is a conflict of interests or what one wants isn't necessarily what the other wants and where both sides prefer to search for solutions, rather than giving in or breaking-off contact. Few of us enjoy dealing with with conflicts -- either with bosses, peers, subordinates, friends, strangers, or opponents. This is particularly true when the conflict becomes hostile and when strong feelings become involved. Resolving conflict can be mentally exhausting and emotionally draining. But, it is important to realize that conflict that requires resolution is neither good nor bad. There can be positive and negative outcomes. It can be destructive but can also play a productive role for you personally and for your relationships -- both personal and professional. For instance, after I roll over Ganker, Jackson and Xenos , I'll be moving a step closer to supremacy in PCW. The important point is to manage the conflict, not to suppress conflict and not to let conflict escalate out of control. Many of us seek to avoid conflict when it arises but there are many times when we should use conflict as a critical aspect of creativity and motivation. In life, we'll always be negotiating and resolving conflict throughout all of our professional and personal life. Before a battle, it is helpful to plan; know whether you are in a win-win or win-lose situation. Be sure of the goals, positions, and underlying interests. What information do you have and what do you need? What are your competitive advantages and disadvantages? What is the other's advantages and disadvantages? My advantage: superiority. Ganker,Jackson,Xenos advantage: none. See what I mean? Give some thought to your strategy. It is very important to be clear on what is important in the battle. For instance, my goal is supremacy. To achieve that, I know where I stand; I'm clear about my real goals. People who lose track of their own goals break off even if they have achieved their needs because they become more concerned with whether the other side 'won.' Before going into war, it's important to know your competitive advantage -- your strongest points. Also, you need to know the advantages of your opponent. Similarly, know your weaknesses and the other's weaknesses. Throughout Manuel Labor's brief life, losing has not been in option. It's become accepted when it happens, but is never an option when doing something. Everything Manuel Labor does, he has to do just right. The Lucha Legend has to be the best at whatever he is doing at that particular moment. Half-assing, or mediocrity of any kind, is unacceptable. Lately, however, that's the highest Labor has been able to get in his career in the ring. Mediocrity. He's never been able to win a title that matters in a promotion that matters. He has been made to feel like an outcast in a group of Outcasts. He is unwanted in a DAMN promotion that wanted him. And, all in all, Manuel Labor has dropped below the competition that he is, on a talent standpoint, clearly above. Yep. Manuel Labor has been the picture of mediocrity for well over a couple of weeks now. But it's about time for all of that to change. It's about time for 'The Lucha Legend' to take his place among wrestling's elite. Sure, some do consider the young mexican one of wrestling's elite, but hardly has he ever lived up to those standards. But that is going to change and change now. Manuel Labor's mission, if he chooses to accept it, is to rise above the dreaded mediocrity stick. He is to rise above his weaker foes and insert himself into the elite where he has always belonged. Last Friday may have been a step in the wrong direction, but that is behind Manuel Labor. A young man with a lot of nerve, a man named Gaz Green, took it upon himself to cheat Labor out of the W, while one of the Jester's greatest friends/enemies in the business, Tessa Windsor also suffered at the hands of his prank. But Manuel has a chance to put the failures in the past and step out of the shadows. Manuel Labor's win in the number one contender match for the cruiserweight title will be the first step in the right direction for the Lucha Legend in a long time.. A few more of those against the mediocre mid-carders of PCW and the elite will have no choice but to take notice. Serious notice. Manuel Labor's mission to rise above mediocrity is far from complete, but he's getting there. Soon enough, he'll be where he should've been all along. And soon enough, all those tags and labels that have been given to him over time will be shed away for good. Jackson,Xenos,Ganker ... I am sorry ... Manuel 'Lucha Legend' Labor will NOT go down without a fight.
