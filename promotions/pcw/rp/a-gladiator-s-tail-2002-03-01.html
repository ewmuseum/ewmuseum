A Gladiator's Tail

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-1-02 A Barbaric meeting pt. 2: 'I have walked the paths; the shadowed roads that led to terror's breast. I have plumbed the depths of Hatred's womb and scaled Destruction's crest. For every secret left unveiled, for every power learned, I'd sell the remnants of my soul, regardless how it burned. And still i sought a higher wisdom few could have attained. 'Though i found it, it would leave me – broken, damned and drained. For now I find this power gained is more unto a curse. My spirit burns with every spell and each irreverent verse. Despite this strength and knowledge earned, I have paid a heavy toll, Never should've traded power for my own immortal soul.' - C. Vincent Metzen Mysteries can be as small as an ant or as big as a mountain it just depends on how the aspect is treated. Mysteries of the world becoming unlocked over centuries, some good some bad. Personal mysteries become unlocked and born every day. One can search for their past, their present, or even their future. The search for true knowledge will always be a mystery to some. To other, a select few, this mystery becomes unlocked and what they find is a library of vast knowledge handed down by some of the most brilliant minds to ever walk the face of the earth. But with these mysteries of life comes a great deal of responsibility. Only a few can handle them without going insane due to the fact the human mind isn't trained to hold enough information. If too much information is acquired the human mind shuts down and becomes useless. How does your mind work? Do you think your smart or are you just another wannabe? Think long and hard as you look into the mirror at the man standing before you, are you all that you think or are you just a shell of the person you want to be? Last time Jay Sylo was seen he finally met the Barbarian known as 'The Beast'. He learned of how the beast came to be and how he was handed down through generations of family members. Sylo felt he owed the world to this being but it seems the beast felt he owed Sylo. He called him the chosen one and informed Sylo that the family curse had skipped over him, he was truly the last of a dying breed. Sylo has decided to meet with the beast once more...to hear more of the past and get more insight into this being. So we join him now as he sits in his bed slowly slipping back to sleep.) (His eyes were so heavy that he could barley hold them open using all the muscles in his body. The transfer from the real world to a dream state quickly moved upon him, grabbing him and pulling down. Soon Sylo found himself back in the seat around the swirling vortexes once more. In front of him, wearing the same attire, was the 'beast'. A smile moved across his tan, bitter lips. His eyes seemed to glow a small bit as Sylo stared at the surrounding colors of Blue, Orange, and Red once more.) 'Welcome back Lad,' The Barbarian warrior let out a friendly greeting. 'Thanks, How have you been?' Sylo asked looking straight forward at the beast. 'Great, you should know I live inside of you, I feel what you feel,' The beast replied. 'Ah, yes....I was wondering do you know more of my family's past?' Sylo asked in an interested tone. 'Aye, I have helped your family much in the past,' The warrior replied. 'But I thought you said I was the one?' Sylo said confused. 'Aye ye are lad, I helped family members in the past but I stopped because none had the power it took to be the best until you. Yer different lad, you have something that no other Sylo possessed trust me on that,' The Barbarian replied. The Story (Flash back mode w/ voice over's of the Barbarian) (The sky was gray with dim grey clouds rolling through them making waves as they moved. Down below sat an arena, a stadium made of stone. People rushed into the entrances and inside many dressed in white robes sat eating grapes and cheering for not reason.) Voice Over: 'The Gladiators, a bunch of scavengers who fought to stay alive. This story applies to your upcoming match at March Madness. It was during the time of Maximus Sylo, he was another one of your great grandfathers. He was one hell of a fighter and ruled the coliseum. Roman's travled from miles just to catch a glimpse of this warrior in battle. But soon he would be faced with a bigger challenge. The emperor, Cesar commanded Maximus Sylo to fight against three challengers at once. This was the true test for Maximus Sylo,' (He sat in the chambers, awaiting the fight to come. Above he could hear the screams of the fans lusting for death. Maximus Sylo was handsome with black spiky hair with matching black goatee. He wore a black chest plate with a red undershirt and on his legs he wore a metal type kilt with a black type spandex on the bottom. In the blood soaked soil sat a sword with blood stains on the blade. Maximus Sylo let out a sigh as he stared up listening to the crowds roar for blood. It made him sick but he learned to please the crowed, that was his key to living. If the crowed didn't love him he would fade away and die.) 'Father I ask you to help me, save me, and lead me for I know not what awaits me once I step through those gates. I will either walk into the pits of hell or step into a sanctuary of peace, only the gods can help me now,' Maximus Sylo looked at the sky. (He felt something from within. Something growling within, it soon took on a figure in front of Maximus Sylo.) 'Do not fear me Maximus, I am only here to help...I am a power within and if you unleash me upon yer opponents then nothing will go wrong. Just trust me lad,' The figure spoke out. 'O...Ok but what am I in for?' Maximus Sylo asked confused. 'Yer about to face three men to the death. They are all gunning for ye so ye better be careful. Go for the throat and I'll guide ye to victory,' The figure replied. (Maximus Sylo had no chance to reply because the figure disappeared once more. Slowly Maximus Sylo sifted the soft bloody dirt through his fingers as the match in process ended feeding the crowds thirst for blood. He knew it was his time so slowly he stood and took a deep breath inhaling death into his lungs. The crowed began to chant his name as the old wooden planks raised up. Maximus Sylo dashed out hit by a wave of chants and cheers. He raises an arm in the air as they continued to cheer. The gates to the east, west, and north popped open. Three large men charged out. The one from the east gate was large and swung a mace , The north Gate held a smaller man who had a spear with a very sharp point, and the west gate had a medium sized man who had a bow and arrow.) Voice Over: 'Maximus Sylo was scared, yes but did he show it? No. He clinched the sword and let out a fierce growl. He could smell their blood and knew he had to destroy all three or he would be walking through the fields of death very soon,' (Maximus Sylo charged letting out a yell as he drove his sword down at the man with the mace. He blocked but Maximus Sylo followed up with a slice to the left arm cutting it plum off. Blood splattered out and the arm fell as the man let out a scream of pain. The fans cheered almost as they could taste the blood. Maximus Sylo turned as the man to the west gate shot an arrow. It landed into the leg of Maximus Sylo who let out a yell as he ripped it from his leg quickly. The man with the spear charged at Maximus Sylo but Sylo rolled out of the way and sliced the back of his leg. The man yelled out but was still able to stand. He turned and chunked the spear at Maximus Sylo as the one with the bow and arrow shot. Maximus Sylo dropped and the spear impaled the man with the bow and arrow. The arrow flew and hit the man with the spear in the shoulder.) Voice Over: 'There were two left alive. Maximus Sylo was getting tired so I began to take over,' (Maximus Sylo let our a mighty roar and snarled as he ran toward the one who had the spear. He shoved his sword into the chest of the man and rammed him up into a spike on the wall driving him in head first. He died on impact. The one armed man (no pun intended) charged at Maximus Sylo with his mace. Maximus Sylo ripped his sword from the chest of the dead man on the wall and charged. Soon the man with the mace swung quickly at Maximus Sylo's head but with quick thinking Maximus Sylo threw his sword up causing the Mace to wrap around the neck of the man with the mace. Slowly the man choked to death as Maximus Sylo snarled and grabbed him lifting him up and driving him down on his neck, snapping it in two. He picked the man up and threw him up where Cesar sat and snarled. The fans let out a loud chorus of cheers as Maximus Sylo only snorted and walked back through the gates.) End Flashback 'Ye see, even your ancestors were challenged. Maximus Sylo unleashed me upon those men that day and they found themselves in the fields of death,' The beast said staring to Sylo. 'What happened to Maximus Sylo?' Sylo asked. 'He eventually won his freedom and started a family in the hills. He died an old man, happy and free,' The beast replied. (Jay paused a moment rubbing his chin. He stared at The Beast a moment then began to speak once more.) 'I see how the battles are the same,' Sylo said still rubbing his chin. 'Yes, ye see you are faced with a match where you must fight three men. Yecould do it alone but it would be hard...that's why I am going to help ye,' The beast replied. 'You and I will make one hell of a team, that's for sure,' 'Yes, we will but never forget what yer purpose is,' The beast replied. 'Yes I know....but the title is in the light now and I can feel it in my grasp....it's not a want it's a need,' Sylo said with a determined look. (The beast slowly chuckled as he patted his own knee a bit. His massive laugh came from deep down inside.) 'Ye remind me of someone,' The beast said. 'Who?' Sylo asked. 'A young version of me,' The beast smirked. 'But now ye need to get some rest lad,' 'I guess your right, maybe we can talk more later...I have so much I want to learn,' Sylo said standing up. 'Don't worry lad, everything will come clear soon, ye can count on that...fer now rest and ye and I will talk more later....we still have some training to do fer the match at March Madness,' The beast replied. 'Yes, well thank you my friend,' 'Anytime Lad, anytime,' The beast replied as he faded away. (He woke up looking at the rising sun of Day. He knew even more about his ancestors, he knew even more about the beast but one question still burned inside his mind...what was this beast's name?) 'What a night,' Sylo said rubbing his head. 'But I sure as hell am ready for March Madness,' (Sylo smirked as he got out of bed to take his shower. More would be revealed later but for now time is up.)
