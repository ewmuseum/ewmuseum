The Early Christmas Present

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Early Christmas Present Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 20/11/02 A lone, dingy looking Christmas tree stands in the corner of the bare room. “Jingle Bells”, stripped of any grandeur in it’s jangley, tinny stripped down sound plays weakly. “Merry Christmas PCW, the Front Office has a special present for you.” Words are spoken in monotone, coming from the mouth of PCW CEO Xavier Kannon who appears from behind the tree which seems to look a little Greener now Kannon is beside it. The Brown, dry needles replaced with lush, shiny Green. He has said he has something to give to PCW at Alliances, something to show for all the money he has taken from fans and wrestler. A token gift to justify the millions, maybe? “I’m afraid this one didn’t come gift wrapped.” His bright Green eyes, beacons of attention for those face to face with him direct the gaze down to the bottom of the tree where the once dirty wooden floor has been covered in thick Red carpet, sandwiched between it and the lower foliage of the tree are gift wrapped presents of all shapes and sizes. The reflections of the bright Christmas lights than now adorn the tree reflect off the shiny Red, Green and Gold wrapping, the new life given to the tree showing clearly in the surprises that wait below it. “Want to open one? Go on, just one.” The camera zooms out to show that the corner of the once dingy room is now alive and vibrant. Lush Green tree in perfect health, dazzling lights wrapped around it and enticing gifts below. To it’s left a Red brick fireplace decorated with all kinds of brasses borders a roaring log fire. One of the boxes poised perilously atop the gift pile finally gives in to gravity and drops off, it’s delicate thud attracting the attention of Xavier who reaches down and grips one of the bow’s ends, pulling it to loosen the tie and the boxes sides fall down. “Sorry, no peeking.” A jovial chuckle resonates throughout the festive scene as the box is shown to house nothing but Holly leaves in the shape of the question mark, the Red berries painstakingly stuck onto every Green protrusion on every leaf. Will this be a present for PCW? Or will Xavier be a typical man and buy something for himself? “I’d hate to ruin this surprise.” Well, all we do know is that the CEO of Primetime isn’t doing this out of festive spirit... Usually he hates this time of the year, all that money being mis-spent on the Christian holiday, all the good cheer flying about grates his very being. Scientology makes it’s money though doubt, depression and misery. Happy Holidays are not their time. “Maybe you’d like some clues?” Maybe he’s changed? Maybe this is his apology to PCW and all it’s wrestlers? Could the Christmas season effect Xavier in such a positive way that he can’t even wait until December 25th to give his gift? Usually when he can’t wait to do something it’s because he can’t hold off seeing the looks of anguish, disgust and frustration on his victims face. For a man who’s time in PCW has ruined so many’s lives, don’t expect much. “Don’t expect much? Then this will surpass you’re expectations by far.” You have to give it to him, he has the resources to give PCW a very merry Christmas. The coffers of Primetime are brimming with money and assets, business is up and the promotion is vibrant. He could probably buy anything that PCW fans wanted. “I could, but I don’t like their tastes.” The tinny rendition of “Jingle Bells” has been replaced by “Silent Night”, no inferior sound here as the carol fills the whole room with it’s splendour, a perfect accompaniment to the now homely room and spectacular Christmas tree, housing the beautifully wrapped gifts. What PCW fans would probably ask for, is Kannon to go. Is he? “That’d be telling.” Maybe that’s it. There surely couldn’t be any bigger reason to celebrate for PCW fans other than the detested Scientologist walking out and never coming back. Seeing the back of Kannon would beat any sweater or pair of socks they may unwrap. And it’d be just like Kannon to hype up his own departure, never one to fade away he’d always choose to burn out. “What? You want me gone?” What would PCW be like with no Xavier? Would the Front Office hold together? Would things be better? Or does his unpopular style and personality make it all work? “Well you’ll see if I go.” If it isn’t him leaving, what else could he be hyping like this? So far in PCW he’s made all his moves seemingly out of nowhere, catching PCW and it’s stars off-guard, pulling something out of the Blue so there can be no quick counter. His turn on Jeremy Howard took one sucker punch. The Ultimate Authority managed to catch every other bidder unawares. Tyler never saw Kannon’s betrayal coming. The Front Office formed in the blink of an eye. Long planning, quick execution is his style. “Just for once, I want to tease this one.” Surely there has to be a reason why members from RUSH, Five Star and Old School Wrestling were invited to Alliances. He may hate the companies but he knows their members are capable of causing the upset and winning the event. He wants the world to watch. PCW fans. RUSH fans. FSW fans. OSW fans. A wide audience... But for what? “You know, thinking about it won’t do any good.” It must be to send a message to the whole wrestling community. Never one to do things by halves, the Scientologist would love to do nothing more than have his name on the lips of all the opposition fan’s lips. Or maybe come Monday they won’t be the opposition. Who’s to say that Xavier will be PCW come the beginning of next week... After all, if he leaves PCW he isn’t the kind to just walk away. “Hmmmmm, I do have options.” Could we see Xavier Kannon v Ivan Stanislav headlining OSW’s Slam!? The two ‘Alpha Males’ as they were once called locking horns for supremacy on the Russian Bear’s turf... Xavier has never been one to turn his back on a challenge. Then there is the ever-present lure of Nelson. Was taking PCW from him not enough? With Mob Rule seemingly running the show in FSW, can Xavier resist taking another stab at his mentor, just to show the first time wasn’t a fluke. Where would RUSH figure in this? He may have loose connections to it, but the are connections none the less. The company founded by Jeremy Howard after Kannon helped drive him from PCW, a company his ally Tim Walden used to have a stake in... A company that Xavier could walk into and demand anything he wants. “Well, you don’t have much longer to wait.” On Sunday the present will be unwrapped, but there is a definite feeling that if we don’t like it there won’t be any taking this one back to the shop... ...
