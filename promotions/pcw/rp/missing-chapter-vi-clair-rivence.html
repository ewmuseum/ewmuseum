Date: Chapter 6 No priest! I came all the way here to Germany to find Ivan Chistov or Fredrick Thomas and I found neither of them? Instead, I found this nice women who didn't have a clue at my intentions. I calmy kept my cool about the whole situation. SyN: Who used to be the priest here? Women: Father Thomas. He left on personal matters about a month ago. The replacement priest will be here later on this week if that is who you are looking for. None of this made sense. How could Father Thomas leave Germany a month ago and go to America, stealing money? How long was Chistov a part of the Krishna? The missionary back in America said he was a loyal member so he must have been in touch for a fairly long time. SyN: Okay, thank-you. Can I ask what your name is? The women smiled at me. Women: Clair Rivence I nodded and waved to the lady, walking over to my car. It was doomed. I would never find Ivan Chistov... I played over the seen one more time in my journal. Having a lot of time on my hands, I analyzed every part of the scene, trying to find something that would give me a clue on where my arch nemesis, Ivan Chistov was. I still couldn't believe that I ventured all the way to Germany to a local priest's house. I planned on finding Ivan Chistov right there I guess, ready to talk about information with me and give me the four million dollars that he stole from the Hare Krishna. It wasn't what I thought it would be. I circled the name on the journal I had. Clair Rivence? It was an ordinary name and she was a nice girl. Could her name be in the databse program I had? It was worth a shot. I mean, it was still a day until Turmoil and I had no erands to run. What was I so worried about? Clair was a regular old church person who was looking over the priest house. Yet still, I had some hope. What was I waiting for? I analyzed things to much. Just enter the name into the computer already! I entered it into the database. Name: Clair Rivence Age: 36 Occupation: Secretary, Undercover Spy Email Adress: [detail withheld] Other Information: Clair was more of a teacher's pet in school but when both of her parents died in a house fire, she found the wrong crowd. On a new path of hatred and evil, Clair found a spy, Henry Claval. Claval, who is considered armed and dangerous, brought Rivence under his wing when he was looking for some new workers. Clair and Henry's other top student, Ivan Chistov teamed up together on a Nazi mission but their plan was foiled when the CIA found out. Ever since then, Clair has resided in a small town in Germany, acting as an innocent, poor church women who takes care of the priest's house. Recently, when Ivan returned to Germany, the two were in suspicion of plotting another plan but since then, Ivan has disappeared. No other information available at this time. Wow, was I wrong. I thought that Clair was so nice, and yet, Ivan could've been in that house, listening from in the living room at my voice. Why didn't I ask more questions? I scolded myself for not getting the job done. Clair was evil, and dangerous. I read over the information database profile one more time and realized that unlike the other database profiles, this one had an email adress. Not only that, it had an AOL email adress. That means that if I picked the right time, I could be able to catch Clair on AIM. If I found her, I could talk to her and hopefully get some more information. I once again, had my lead. I had yet to find out Ivan's fifth alias, but things were starting to get interesting. The only thing was, could I get information from Clair without her blocking me without getting the chance. It was a risk I had to take. I put the computer screen of my labtop down and decided that I needed a break from the whole Ivan Chistov thing. I thought about my amtches in PCW ahead. Having a lot of time on my hands, I could sit here in this hotel room for hours. my first match was tomorrow on Turmoil. It was a tag match with me and partner, Dan Osbourne against Stone Burns and Sivikian Krikorian. To be honest, I wasn't to sure of Osbourne. He hasn't really surprised me yet and I fail to see any future in him. I would have to do most of the match myself and hope for the win. Stone Burns was a guy that off the start, I sort of admired. He was a stand up guy who I could see fighting down the future. Krikorian, I wasn't to worried about. Anybody who had a name like that, couldn't be harmless at all. He was foreign and only a few select people were strong foreign people, me being one of them. I could beat him. The match that I was more worried about though was a handi-capped match. I was with Mark Makonie. Makonie, I had faced in matches before and even was with him in a tag match one time. I don't know how reliable he is and he better not screw up the match. SOCIETY was the person I was scared against. SOCIETY was being called the next, 'Ed'. Do you know what kind of pressure I have against someone who is like that? We're both big man and I'll be sure to wrestle him, toe to toe. Only time would tell if I could win my matches or not. I would try my best and if things worked out, the Rising Star Title at the PPV, could be mine. COULD be mine...
