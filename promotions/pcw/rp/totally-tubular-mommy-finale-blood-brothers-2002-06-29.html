Totally Tubular Mommy - Finale - Blood Brothers

29 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 29 [Friendship is an ultimate bond between people that is just unexplainable. You meet a person, you do things with them, and share your life with them. A friend is someone that is there when life is at it's lowest, and a friend will be there when life is at it's highest. This person would surely die for you, and you would surely die for this person. It's like becoming an extended family in a way. Just because the person doesn't have the same gene code as you doesn't mean that this friend can be family. Ultimately friendship is a special feeling to share, and something that all experience in their lives no matter who you are. It's like being brothers....] ['Totally Tubular' Tommy and 'Heavy Metal' Harry have been friends since Harry was born. Tommy happens to be a year old than Harry, but the age difference doesn't matter one bit. They grew up on the beaches of California together, their friendship growing strong and strong every single day. When their father's died in the ring, at the same time, they really became closer. Both boys knew that they had to stick together to make sure things were done right about their father's legacy. Tommy became a professional surfer and Harry promoted him through every event. They were a great tag team in the surfing world and they were so strong that they decided to retire to let other surfers rise up the ladder of greatness. Wrestling was a love both men shared, and something both of them had put off for surfing. Tommy wanted to carry on his dad's legacy in the ring, and Harry wanted to carry it on as his manager. It came true. NTICW was good to both men and Tommy was successful there as a wrestler, and Harry as a manager. PCW would offer them contracts....as a tag team. Harry's father may have been a very small man, but Harry grew into a much larger offspring. Harry could wrestle and he could wrestle well and it spawned the beginning of THE COWABUNGA CONNECTION!!] [Brothers. Both men always thought of each other as brothers. When Tommy was 10 and harry was 9 they cut their hands open and signed it in blood that they were blood brothers. Ever since that day they never spent much time apart, truly becoming brothers. With the news from Tommy's mom it seems that everything has changed. With just one simple sentence the entire friendship has shifted in a major way. 'Heavy Metal' Harry is the half-brother to 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. Tommy's mom had an affair with Harry's father, 'Far Out' Frank, and conceived Harry on that night. Was it an error or was it planned? Destiny can be such a bitch sometimes] [The kitchen is in pure silence. The Tubular One, The Heavy Metal One, and THEIR mother sit around the blue and yellow kitchen table in their blue plastic chairs. The sky light above the table shines the sun down into the center causing a nice lighting effect on Harry's face, making him look kind of like Tom Selleck from certain angles. Everyone sits in silence as Harry looks to Tommy and then to HIS mother. Mom looks from Tommy to Harry, her sons. Tommy just stares down at the kitchen table in a sort of trance-like state. The news was just broken to him about the new family member and Tommy's mind is still trying to process everything] Tommy's mom: You do realize that you and Harry are brothers now, right? [The trance is broken as Tommy looks up to his mom with tears filling his eyes] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: How do you expect me to react to this, mom? You just pop in here, do a bunch of guys, and then tell me that my life long best friend has really been my life long brother the entire time? THAT IS SO BOGUS!!! [Tommy rises from his chair, making it shoot backwards and scratch against the floor. Harry nods is head as if agreeing with his BROTHER] Tommy's mom: It seems that way but that's not how it was happening. I came here to support you and to show you that I really care about you. I always have and I always will. BOTH OF YOU! TTT: Isn't that just freakin' special, mom!! You always cared! You certainly had a f**ked up way of showing it! You neglected US... [Tommy points to Harry and then to himself] TTT: You neglected us!! You went all those years with all those guys, in front of BOTH your children. Before it was just me who had to deal with all of that crap but now HARRY has to let all of that go through his head?!?! That isn't fair, mom, and your really a huge BOGUS BITCH for doing that!! [Tommy's mom looks absolutely shocked that Tommy would even dare to call her a bitch. After everything she has done for him, and the number one reason for him to respect her is that she bought him into this world. He should be down on his knees thanking her everyday for that. She stands up from her chair, slamming her hand down on the table] Tommy's mom: HOW DARE YOU!! You never appreciate a thing that I do for you. You were always the victim of every little thing that I did wrong. I'm sorry about the men, I truly am, but you just have no idea what that feeling is like. I'm going to change. I'm going to change for both of you. [Everyone is silent for a few moments] Tommy's mom: I would expect you to have been a little happy about this. Harry, your life long friend, is your brother now. I would have figured you two would be absolutely ecstatic but instead I get verbally abused!! [Tommy seems to not be phased by her words at all, just still pissed off] TTT: Like I'm supposed to just forgive you for every little thing you did now that I find out Harry is my biological brother. All the bogusness and all the heinousness is just going to flutter away like a bodacious bird flying south. BUT THAT'S NOT WHAT IS GOING TO HAPPEN! You had an affair outside of your marriage with DAD. It doesn't matter if it was Harry's dad because you still cheated on MY DAD! How could you go and do such a thing?! [Tommys mother now sits down, dropping into the seat as if her legs had just given out on her. Tommy's mom's face has a look of pure sadness] Tommy's mom: It wasn't something that we had planned or was doing behind your fathers back. It was after your father had a very big match for a number one contendership and he came SO CLOSE to winning that match. He was so let down and he went for a walk out on the beach. I had a lot of alcohol in me and Frank, Harry's dad, was just talking to me about how Black Sabbath was the greatest band alive and that Ozzy Osbourne was going to be one of the greatest rock singers of all time. Something inside of me just snapped, I had this burning desire in me to just rip his clothes off. So, that's what I did and sure enough I got pregnant. I didn't want an abortion because I wanted Frank to have his child. [Harry smiles, somewhat proud of the fact that his dad had sex with Tommy's mom] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Far out, man. TTT: A burning sensation?!?! That's how you explain that you cheated on my dad! That you were just drunk so that gives you an excuse to start becoming the biggest slut this side of California?! That's so f**king pathetic! Tommy's mom: GOD DAMNIT, TOMMY!! You just shut your mouth right now! I don't need you jumping down my back and making me realize things that I already know. This problem I have is hard to deal with and your not making it any better. I'm trying to tell you the truth and let everything out. Your just SO filled up that your father was this great do good hero that did EVERYTHING right. Well, I've got news for you, son...your father was'n't all perfect. In fact, your father had an affair before I did. He had many of them before I did!! [Tommy just sits in shock. His father had affairs with other women? It's so hard to believe? How could he have done something so horrible?] Tommy's mom: Yeah, I bet you feel a different opinion about your father now. Well, I forgave him of it because I understood what it was like being on the road and wrestling three or four nights a week. He gave into temptation just like everybody does once in their lives, at least. That's why I was able to have Harry without a problem from your father because he forgave me for my cheating with Frank. He wanted to see this child come onto the earth and he wanted HIS BEST FRIEND, FRANK, to have his own child. [Tommy just sits in extreme silence, staring at his mother with sorrwoful eyes. He was being such a bogus prick with his mother, treating her like she was some Sunset Strip Scumbag. That is his mother and what she does is her own business and he should love her no matter what. He just couldn't let go of the past and let things be the way they are. She's telling the truth and she's willing to change. Right now he should be extremely happy with what is going on, not angry] TTT: I'm sorry, compadres... [The kitchen is silent once again. Tommy stares at the table so deep in thought. The positive aspects are coming back to him now. There is one large positive to come out of this situation and it's the fact that Harry is his brother. All those years of pretending that were brothers wasn't pretending after all. It was all true and legal] [Tommy turns to Harry] TTT: Dude, I'm sorry. I totally bashed your most gnarly dad and that's something I shouldn't have done. We are best friends, compadre, and nobody can take that away from us. Even though we didn't know the truth all those years ago we still thought of each other as brothers... [Harry nods] Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Now that we ARE brothers I think it's time to show the world just how BODACIOUS these brothers are! Harry: YEAH, MAN!! [It is silent for a few seconds] Harry: How are we going to do that, man? [Tommy laughs to himself. Harry just doesn't get it sometimes and it's so hilarious] TTT: At Gold and Glory, dude! The big tag team battle royal. We're going to walk down to that ring as two new radical dudes. We now have something over all those compadres in the battle royal and that's the fact that we have been life long friends. Now we are BODACIOUS BROTHERS!!! So when Surfin' USA hits the speakers and we strut our way down ringside all of those bogus dudes are going to see something dangerous, and that's the fact that we share a brain. [Harry looks frightened] Harry: We share a brain, man?! [Tommy can do nothing but laugh] TTT: We have our own brains, but we think the same. Harry: Oh. TTT: We're going to win those tag team titles on Sunday, dude. We're going to do it for the first time as best friends, and we're going to do it for the first time as brothers. [Harry smiles] Harry: Yeah, man. [The two men stand up from their chairs and stand face to face. Tommy smiles and then extends his hand for a nice, warm handshake. Harry smiles and then shakes his head as Tommy looks at him strangely, wondering what is going through Harry's head] Harry: Brothers don't shake hands, man....BROTHERS GOTTA HUG!!!! [The two men embrace strongly, truly feeling now that they are brothers. Tommy's mom is smiling as tears flow down her face. She knew that it wasn't going to be an easy task letting them know that they are brothers, but it has been done and it's been done for good] [Two gnarly dudes have entered a new era of bodaciousness. The Cowabunga Connection was a dangerous unit as life long best friends, two guys who had conflicting styles of wrestling that made them such a dangerous force inside the tag team circle. Now...they are brothers. They have been brothers the whole time but now that each man knows this now, how much dangerous can these two be? Well, VERY DANGEROUS! Gold and Glory will mark the beginning of a Bodacious Era. A Gnarly Time. And on of TOTALLY TUBULAR proportions] Darkness
