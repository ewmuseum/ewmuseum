Date: Today PWA. And so it began. XWA. We still lacked experience but we knew our time would come. NTICW Three months of wrestling reality. PCW A new era begins. ---------------------------------------- Although The Refugee's haven't even wrestled for more than a year they are known throughout the wrestling world. Countless individuals have faced The Refugee's. Some win, others aren't as lucky. Still they remain longer than others before them. Ali who is just one half of the great team is the leader. The guy who does all the talking. He has seen it all and plays most things safe. You are about to take a step into his mind, his world, a world many cannot even begin to imagine.---------------------------------------- 2002,1,July My name is Ali. I am not sure if anyone else will read these word's but here is a bit about me. I am an Afghan warrior turned wrestler. I do what I have to in order to survive. That was the way I was brought up. I have no regrets about my life and I would do it all again the same way. The reason I have started to write this journal is simply because I have to many thoughts floating around in my head. I still cannot believe that I am in a stable with The Chef. He truly was sent from Allah and he will be the turning point in our career's. My wrestling life has not been easy. I came here with no friends. I still don't have any. I simply have allies, people who won't try to hurt me. It all started way back, even before PWA.I still remember the day we came here.... *Flashback* The night sky is lit up by falling bombs. We fade in on a large cave. Hundreds of people are all huddled together near the back. Bodies lay everywhere and some people are tossing them outside the caves. To the United States Army they are all targets even though there are US Soldiers in that cave. We see the two tribal leaders, Ali and Qaboos, in deep thought. Qaboos:We must leave here. Ali:But what about the people? Qaboos:We must have faith... Ali sighs and then grabs a radio. He puts his mouth close to microphone and speaks. Ali:Nuristani tribal leaders in need of assistance, requesting arial rescue unit. Voice:Please state your location. Ali:Shak-I-Kot Valley, the highest ridge. We'll be waving. Voice:Sir, your chopper is on the way. Ali:Thank you. Ali looks over at Qaboos and he looks back. They take a last glimpse of the crying and scared innocent tribe members. Then they scramble out the back of the cave and hide in some brush. Soldiers walk by slowly, but they are not caught. Finally the helicopter comes into view. A rope is lowered and Qaboos grabs on. He begins to climb but looks back at Ali who appears to be frozen in time. Qaboos:You coming? Ali:Oh. Right, the plane. Qaboos scurries to the top. As soon as he reaches they realize that they are being fired at. Pilot:Hurry up, okay? Ali:Sure thing. As Ali makes his way up the long rope he takes a last glance at the cave. Just as he makes it on board the plane a missile heads straight for the Nuristani cave. Ali and Qaboos watch horrified as the cave they lived in for twenty years is engulfed in flames. Screams of pain fill the air and yet they do nothing. Tears stream down their faces and not a word spoken for hours. Finally Ali speaks. Ali:The United States will pay for that mistake. Even if it means taking my own life. It means nothing to me now anyways. *End Flashback* Those few sentences started it all. This dream if mine to extinguish the rage burning inside me. Although I haven't fulfilled it yet be ready for anything. Our dream will be taken one step at a time starting with International Terrorists.
