Burning Hatred

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today RING…. RING…. RING…. RING… (The scene opens in Kid Krazie’s apartment in southern Houston, Texas where we find him sitting outside on the concrete stairs leading to his apartment along with three others across the hall. As Krazie hears the phone ring, he picks up his Nokia cell phone and pushes the TALK button.) Voice: “Hey K-Man, it’s Big J here…” (Krazie just sits there not answering and hoping that the fool will hang up thinking that no one is on the other line.) Big J: “I can here ya breathing K-Man…” (Krazie snaps his fingers and begins to cuss under his breath as he realizes he might as well talk and get the conversation over with as soon as possible.) Krazie: “Oh hey Big J! Didn’t hear it was you my friend! What’s up buddy?” Big J: “Hey Krazie, what are you doing right now?” (Krazie sits there and rolls his eyes as he makes a makeshift gun out of his finger and puts the “gun” to the side of his head and goes on to pull the trigger so to speak.) Krazie: “Well, Big J I’m sitting here wasting my minutes on my cell phone talking to you!” Big J: “Oh… So you aren’t busy???” (Krazie once again rolls his eyes and this time he puts his head back and then snaps it forward like he is hitting it against a brick wall.) Krazie: “Nah Big J, I’m not busy! Why what did you have in mind?” (Big J chuckles as Krazie juts sits there with a knowing look on his face as he knows just what Big J is going to say.) Big J: “Well, I am throwing a barbeque and I was wondering if you’d like to come?” (Krazie shakes his head side to side in frustration as he regrets that he didn’t hang the phone up five minutes ago.) Big J: “Four words… Free beer… Free food!” Krazie: “Free beer? Free food? Hell yeah dude, I’m there!” Big J: “Sweet…” (Big J proceeds to give Krazie directions to his house with Krazie trying to write the directions down in his mind. As Big J finishes with the directions he says good-bye and hangs up. Krazie hangs his cell phone up and gets up to his feet as he heads inside to get ready. As he enters the apartment he notices Lu-Lu sitting there watching a cardboard box shaped to look like a television.) Krazie: “Anything good on?” Lu-Lu: “Oh you know the same ole same ole.” (Krazie smiles as he walks over to the cushiness couch where she is sitting and gives her a kiss on her handle bar.) Krazie: “You feel like going to a barbeque?” Lu-Lu: “Not really… I don’t feel to good.” Krazie: “Is it that time of the month again?” (Krazie smiles but that smile vanishes as she answers.) Lu-Lu: “How’d you know!? Anyway, you gone on and have fun. I’ll be here watching the twenty-four hour Krazie network!” (They both look over at the makeshift television screen as all it is, is a cardboard box set up to look like a television with a large picture of Kid Krazie taped to it. Krazie smiles as he looks at it.) Krazie: “This is my favorite channel! Some people like the Hitler Channel, other people like the Woman Channel, well here we have the Krazie Channel!” (Krazie chuckles to himself, followed by kissing Lu-Lu on the handle bar. Krazie turns and leaves stopping by the front door only to pick up his wallet and set of keys to the supper up flaming ’32 Ford Coupe. He runs out and jumps in his ride and takes off speeding out of the parking lot and down the street heading for Big J’s house.) **** SCENE FADE**** Voice: “Hey look who made it!” (Krazie pulls up to a rather large three-story brick lake house sitting in a rather rich area where a lot of wealthy people reside. Krazie climbs out of the Coupe as he notices twenty some odd Import cars parked around the three-story house. Running up from the three-story house towards Krazie is none other than party host Big J.) Big J: “Hey buddy, welcome to my home…” (Big J proceeds to show Krazie around his home giving him the grand tour. The tour ends out back where Krazie’s eyes pop wide as he sees about thirty-to-fifty people running around. There is a pool with people swimming and floating around in it, people barbequing off to the side and other people playing volley ball. Past the backyard is Big J’s dock where about ten wave runners are parked along with a speed boat and large fishing boat tied up.) Big J: “Nice place…” (Big J smiles and claps Krazie on the back. As Krazie turns to him Big J hands Krazie a bottle of Smirnoff Ice not to be confused with Smirnoff the brand of vodka. Krazie takes the bottle and pops the top with a quick twist followed by a deep chug of the alcoholic beverage.) Krazie: “…BURP… Damn, that’s good stuff.” Big J: “Well I have to go mingle so make yourself at home, there are some suits up stairs in the bathroom so knock yourself out and have fun!” (Big J leaves Krazie standing alone with a half drunken bottle of Smirnoff Ice. Krazie finishes the rest of the drink and walks to the house stopping by the trash can to throw away the empty bottle.) Voice: “BEER!” (Krazie spins around and sees a toothless, fat, sloppy looking white guy dressed in torn blue jeans, a white stain covered shirt with flies swarming around him. He walks over past Krazie and takes a bottle of beer and chugs it down like water. As the strange guy drinks Big J walks in with a smug smile on his face.) Big J: “So I see you have met B-Boy.” Krazie: “B-Boy!?” (Big J walks over and pats B-Boy on the back followed by grabbing a bottle of Bacardi Silver. He then walks over next to Krazie.) Big J: “Yeah Beer Boy is his name… How’s it going B-Boy?” B-Boy: “BEER!” (Big J pats B-Boy on the back again and walks out towards the pool area with Krazie following right behind him.) Big J: “I saw your match with Murray last night, great job!” Krazie: “Thanks…” Big J: “Where is your title?” Krazie: “I left it with Lu-Lu back at the house.” Big J: “I see… So when’s your next match?” Krazie: “I got to go to Columbus, Ohio on Tuesday and fight in an Airport for the PCW Extreme Title. There’s going to be a little over 20 wrestlers, all fighting for that title.” Big J: “Is this a good thing?” Krazie: “I am not a greedy person! I don’t expect to win this match, cause let’s face it I am as hardcore as a sixty year old woman in porn!” (Both men cringe at the thought of a sixty-year-old woman doing porn.) Big J: “That sounds pretty hardcore to me!” Krazie: “Yeah it’s a bad analogy! Any who, the only thing I am concerned about in this match is getting my hands on that chump Duzza! As long as I see him getting eliminated then I’ll be a happy camper!” Big J: “What’s your beef with Duzza?” (Krazie looks at Big J with hatred burning in his brown eyes as he remembers why he hates Duzza so much.) Krazie: “Why do I hate Duzza!? When I was in RSE with Thee Obsession, I got no respect from Duzza! He was the World Champ and thought he was king shit and better than everyone in that place! Or at least better than the new guys!” (Krazie chugs the rest of his beer and then chunks it across the way as it crashes into the brick wall. Big J steps back a step or two leaving Krazie to himself.) Krazie: “Duzza, you are going down! You will not survive long enough to fight me at Supremacy!” (B-Boy walks out and comes up from behind with a bottle of beer in his hand. He hands the beer to Krazie who takes it with a nod of appreciation.) B-Boy: “BEER!” (The scene fades as B-Boy runs and does a cannon ball off the diving board into the pool with a beer in each hand.)
