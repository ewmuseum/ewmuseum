Debut of Kid Krazie

8 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/8 'How will it feel? To lace up these bad boys for the very first time in my career knowing it is for a different promotion? A new promotion I have never stepped foot in! How will it feel as I try to fit in? Will others respect me? Will others come to like me? How will the fans react? I guess only time will tell! Just got to hold my head high and put one foot in front of the other and hope I don't trip and make a fool of myself!' *********** The Quest *********** (The scene opens up in a San Jose air strip where a small million dollar jet comes screeching to a halt. There sitting in the jet with a smile on his face is none other than PCW Rookie Kid Krazie. He is dressed in khaki colored cargo shorts, black sandals, a white sleeveless t-shirt, and to top things off a straw cowboy hat.) Krazie: 'Hey Tiffany you ok?' (There in the back of the jet is the small uncomfortable bathroom where we can hear some coughing followed by a little bit of gagging noises. Krazie meanwhile begins to laugh as the door swings open.) Krazie: 'Babe?' Tiffany: 'Shut-up! I don't want to hear it! And wipe that smile from your face!' (Krazie stands up grabs his duffel bag and grabs Tiffany's purse and makes his way to the exit as Tiffany follows. Krazie hands Tiffany her purse as the door to the jet pops open. Krazie makes his way down the steps and to the concrete landing strip as Tiffany follows suit. Tiffany has a California tan. She is wearing some tight daisy duke shorts, a white spaghetti strap velvet top that is cut short showing off her firm stomach with belly button ring and her gorgeous breasts. She is wearing black sandals and a Texas bandanna that is holding her shoulder length hair back.) Tiffany: 'Finally!' (Tiffany gets the ground and kisses the concrete as Krazie just stands back laughing.) Krazie: 'It wasn't such a bad trip as you make it babe.' Tiffany: 'Yeah, well you weren't the one puking your guts out so you can just shut-up!' (The two make their way towards the terminal building as another small jet type plane lands.) Tiffany: 'Do we have a ride?' Krazie: 'Yeah, I called and rented a car for the week.' (The two make their way through the terminal building stopping to pick up their luggage, a quick bite to eat and the keys to the rental car. They then make their way to th parking lot where their blue Plymouth Prowler is sitting ready to go.) Krazie: 'Oh yeah.' (The two hop in and get ready to go as Krazie starts the engine and puts on his seat belt. He peels out causing the tires to screech as he makes his way out of the enormous parking lot. As he makes his way down the roads of San Jose, California, Tiffany and him begin to talk.) Tiffany: 'You sure you made the right decision?' Krazie: 'We'll soon find out won't we?' Tiffany: 'You don't regret your decision?' Krazie: 'Nope! I have never regretted any decision I have ever made! Millions of decisions are made day in and day out. From what toothpaste will I use this morning, or will I even brush my teeth this morning? What pant leg will I put on first right or left? What shirt will I wear? What will it be today boxers or briefs or none of the above? You see babe I don't regret any decision I make. If I make a bad decision I learn from it and try to prevent it from happening again.' Tiffany: 'What do you think will happen?' (Krazie smiles as he thinks to himself. Tiffany gets anxious wondering just what is going on through his head.) Krazie: 'Who knows? I'm not a fortune teller and well what ever happens, happens!' (Krazie pulls up to a red light. He stops and looks over at Tiffany as she just sits back looking back at Krazie.) Krazie: 'What ever happens, as long as you are here I'll be happy.' (Krazie goes to kiss here but bolts back at the last second. He wipes the air with his hand in front of his face.) Krazie: 'Dang girl, you need a Tic Tac or something!' (Tiffany swings and slaps Krazie in the arm. After a twenty minute drive Krazie pulls into a Holiday Inn where he parks the rental and makes his way to the lobby with Tiffany by is side. Krazie and Tiffany make their way to the front desk where three ugly women are standing post.) Clerk: 'Can I help you sir?' Krazie: 'Yeah, you got any reservations under the name Krazie?' Clerk: 'Yes we do. You Krazie?' Tiffany: 'I'd say so. More like insane!' ***Cheap pun there*** Krazie: 'Yeah I'm Kid Krazie.' (The Clerk tallies the total up and after Krazie pays she hands him an electronic key and a note.) Tiffany: 'What's that?' (Krazie looks at the small sticky note. He reads it as Tiffany reads it over his shoulder.) Krazie & Tiffany: [I]'Mr. Krazie, you have been booked for a match against The Multiple Man for Friday Fusion. Good Luck and Welcome to PCW'[/I] (Krazie smiles as he folds up the letter and makes his way to the room. The two stand in the elevator waiting as it ascends to the top floor.) Tiffany: 'Already?' Krazie: 'Apparently...' Tiffany: 'You going to be ready?' Krazie: 'We'll soon find out won't we?' (Krazie chuckles to himself as Tiffany looks on worried.) Krazie: 'Why do you look worried?' (The doors to the elevator open and the two make their way down the hall way to the room on the end marked 727. Krazie puts the key in, pops the door open and makes his way inside followed by a worried Tiffany.) Tiffany: 'You haven't had a match in two months.' Krazie: 'Don't worry about it babe. You can take me out of wrestling but you can't take the wrestling out of me... Or something like that!' (Tiffany shrugs and plops down on the bed as Krazie goes out to stand on the balcony over looking the algae green, scum filled pool. Krazie turns back to Tiffany and smiles.) Krazie: 'Want to go swimming?' (Tiffany looks up at him from the bed and shrugs her shoulders and nods her head. She gets up and goes out onto the balcony and sees what Krazie sees.) Krazie: 'How about a little skinny dip?' Tiffany: 'Knock your self out.' (Krazie stares off at the blue cloudless sky as Tiffany gives him a kiss on the side of the cheek and goes back inside. She calls the front desk requesting their luggage to be sent up to their room as Krazie begins to think aloud.) Krazie: 'The Multiple Man? My debut in Primetime and it's against the Multiple Man. Is this a good thing?' (Krazie scratches his left nut as he thinks further on the issue.) Krazie: 'I guess it's better than nothing at all. I wonder if Multiple Man is any good? I guess I got some work to do!' (Krazie stops scratching his left nut but realizes that now his right nut itches. He begins scratching his right nut with a look of concentration displayed on his face.) Krazie: 'Hey! I wonder if he knows he's got a match against me!? It'd be pretty funny if he didn't know and he didn't show! I'd get my first victory!' (Krazie stops scratching his nuts as the itching is satisfied. As he stands there thinking he begins to pick his nose.) Krazie: 'Dang that one is way back there!' (After a couple of minutes of digging for gold he finally got it. He looks at the green mass and smiles.) Krazie: 'So that's what my brain looks like!? Didn't realize it was so big!' (Krazie flicks the booger off his finger and watches it fly the seven stories to the ground. Tiffany comes from behind and wraps her arms around his waist. She gives a little purr as Krazie turns his attention to his girl. She is wearing a leopard print thong a leopard print see through bra. She has a mischievous smile on her face as she takes his hand in hers.) Tiffany: 'Now I forget... Isn't this one of the spots that gets you going?' (She smiles and she extends his index finger. She begins kissing the tip of it and then begins sucking it. Krazie smiles and closes his eyes as she continues to suck his finger. Little did she know that was the same finger that scratched his balls and picked his nose with!) 'Yummy! Tasty!'
