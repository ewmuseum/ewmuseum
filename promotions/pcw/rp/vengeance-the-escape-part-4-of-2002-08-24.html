Vengeance: The Escape? Part 4 of ?

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-24-2002 The shot opens into the Temple of the Night, and the dominating force of the scene is the statue that is now rising to its feet. It is a hideous statue that somehow resembles PCW Superstar, Syko. Behind the statue, we are able to discern a flash of gold and a flash of silver as Venneman Pendragon runs into the shot. Now the scene is equal...Good versus evil. The noble Knight facing off against the diabolical goblin god. Pendragon's eyes blaze with the lust for carnage and combat, and the statues eyes, now alive, convey nothing but evil. Pendragon stands his ground as the statue continues to find its feet. He holds Excalibur in one hand and the Goblin battle axe in his other. He smiles and tenses up, waiting for the monster to make a move. The other goblins in the great hall have made their way to the balcony. Nobody else wants to play a part in this episode of good and evil. Pendragon steps from side to side, and finally, the immense dark eyes of the statue come to rest upon him.... Statue: Who would dare disturb my slumber? It would be you, yes? Venneman: It was I...Lord King Venneman Pendragon...Knight of PCW, Crusader of Truth. My plan here was not to slay ye, but slay ye I will if you do not let me leave peaceably.... The statue cocks its head to the side and lets out a laugh. Soon the statue is bellowing as Venneman stands with an annoyed look etched upon his face... 'Not only was this completely a digression from my original plan, the thing was laughing at me! At ME!! I guess that someone needs to be taught a lesson, and I will be happy to be the teacher...' At last the thing quiets down. It looks at Venneman and shakes its immense head... Statue: You poor, pathetic human. DO you really think you have what it takes to stand toe to toe with a Goblin? And, more than that, the Goblin God, yes? You have come here not to steal my jewel, Crusader...you have come to meet your doom....MWAHAHHAHAHAHA!! Venneman: I have come to steal the jewel, and you must now realize that it will be you who leaves not this room in one piece. Excalibur, my blade, thirsts for your soul, hellspawn. And after I dispatch you, I plan on dispatching as many of your 'people' as I can before I leave this domain to destroy the most renown of your kind...Syko the Goblin falls by my hand at the Third Dawning... The statue stops laughing at the mention of Syko. It narrows its eyes and stares straight at the Knight... Statue: You would dare to utter the name of my favorite son? You shall pay for your transgression with your blood, Knight. Tonight, I feast upon your flesh! Venneman: Come get some... The statue lumbers forward and swings a giant arm at the Crusader. Venneman deftly dodges out of the way and hacks at the beast's midsection with the axe. Sparks fly and the axe clangs off. Pendragon shakes his head... Venneman: This might be tougher than I thought... He dives in and dodges a shot from the giants other arm and takes a whack at the beast's knee. The blade once more bounces off, doing no damage. The damned thing doesn't even chip. Pendragon looks at the blade, stunned, when the arm of the statue comes crashing down once more. This time, the blow finds its mark and catches Pendragon squarely across the shoulder blades. He is thrown across the hall and into a wall. He hits hard and falls to the floor... 'The beast was a formidable foe. Lucky for me though, I can deal with the mentality of Goblins...' Venneman races up the stairs, hacking at goggle eyed goblins the whole way, and soon the floor is littered with limbs and covered in blood. The screams of the wounded fill the air, and Venneman relishes in the carnage he has wrought upon the enemy. He finally reaches Yiko and gives her a wink before yanking the fake TV Title out of her hands. He whispers to her and she falls to the floor, acting as if she fainted. Venneman smiles as he stands and holds the belt high.... Venneman: Behold, ye vile creature! I have regained what is mine from your favorite son! He is a cursed disgrace to the name of Goblin, and your race shall fall with his passing! The statue, which ahs not really moved all that far at all looks up and instantly the eyes glaze over with the gold lust that goblins have recently become famous for. Instantly, the statue charges the stairwell and Venneman races down ahead and down the corridor towards where he entered the Temple. The statue busts through the doorway and follows as Venneman reaches the door and exits the structure. The shot switches to show Venneman running from the entrance, sword in one hand, fake title in the other. From behind him the temple seems to explode into stone pieces as the statue bursts through the door that used to be far too small for him. Venneman runs on and at last comes to the edge of the cliff that he crossed to get to the temple. He looks around and then turns to make his stand. The statue races closer, and Venneman tosses the fake title into the air over the cliff. The statue passes him, lunges out and catches the title in its stony grip. It totters unbalanced as Venneman smiles... Venneman: I don't always need to rely on brawn to win my battles. My superior intellect comes in handy as well. Have a nice trip, vermin! Venneman reaches out and gives the statue a poke with the tip of Excalibur. With a girlish squeal, the Goblin God falls from the cliff, screaming its terrified scream the whole way to the bottom until eventually it is cut off with a rather large thud... Venneman: Stupid Goblins....Now. I need to get back to the states. Third Dawning awaits and my gold needs me! Pendragon shimmies out onto the rope once more, and halfway out he hears a whistle. On the side of the temple stands a small goblin in a black mask. It waves as it pulls out a large pair of scissors... Venneman: Ah shit! The Goblin cuts the rope and Pendragon is hurtled towards the opposite cliff face. He bounces off with his own thud and hangs there, nestled against the unforgiving stone... Venneman: Damnit! What else could possibly go wrong? 'This was not what I was needing. I had to get back to Xerxes before he left, and I needed to be in the states soon. I had a date with a goblin and it would not be a fun affair for the vile fool! My only question was, how in the hell was I gonna climb with all of this damned armor?'
