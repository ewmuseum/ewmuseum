-Bondage-

1 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.01.02 OOC Note: I got to my sister’s house today...and managed to pull out a roleplay. Hopefully I’ll be able to tomorrow and so on for this match. -wishes self luck- Okay, anyways, it’s far from my best work, but enjoy. It’ll be a shame if I gotta lose this ‘cause of lack of time to roleplay... -growls- ~*~ Fade in... So I lie here, on my bed, haunted by recent events. The sounds of Bif Naked throb into my ears through the headphones in my ears. My debut in this promotion called PCW with Nerezza and Gaz, followed by a string of reactions. It’s like this little domino effect. When I signed with this company, I had no idea I’d be running with the big boys. That I’d get a chance, immediately, to be part of the legendary Asylum...and keep company with Alexander Miyagawa, Nerezza, and Gaz “the Jester” Green. Some call us the “best Asylum ever”. Can I take any credit for making them believe that? I doubt it, but in time, I will prove a thing or two to all of you. I laugh, just thinking of what people must think of me already. I’m working to give all of you my best impression in the ring, but outside of that—I will work to make you hate me. What have you seen of me as of yet? A few appearances with the Asylum...one match...an attack with my stable...In short, not much. Yet, still, I have been hearing a lot of judgments...and a lot of labels. So I’ve become this little “Goth” with my blue hair, and my strange ways...that are so foreign to you. And upon you seeing me keeping this female in my basement...watching her shower for fear she’ll kill herself or escape... I get referred to as a ‘lesbian’. Nice thought... I won’t even mention the others I’ve heard whispered about the PCW locker room. Let me tell you something. You can point your fingers, and put your little labels on me...but you don’t know a thing about me. So I don’t mind. Think those shallow little thoughts of yours. Put your little playground labels on me. If it makes you feel better, it makes me feel better. I laugh at such petty little mortal ideas. I’ve even heard comments on the noted differences between the other members of Asylum. How they just pose as jokers...pulling amusing little pranks on the members of the Asylum...While I sit here and abduct other women and starve them in my basement. Stark contrast there, eh? How I seem to have this dark, sadistic, cruel “Gothic” side to me...while they leave that to their performances in the ring. You know what I’ve heard on that?...That I won’t last. That I’m not going to make it in Asylum...That I don’t belong with them. After all—Miya, Nerezza, and Gaz are household names...and who’s Anarchy? I’m just this little no one...some cute little chick wrestler with blue hair. They’re the talent and muscle behind this stable. I’m just the looks...I’m all for show. I’m just riding their coattails to get some success and recognition, eh? A gold digger... Well, whether or not I “last”, you can bet your ass I’m not just a bit of eye candy that’ll be gone from PCW by next week. If I can’t get it into your head with my words, I will beat it into you in the ring...or in a nice little meeting backstage. So, go ‘head. Call me all the names that your little black hearts desire.“Lesbo.”“Freak.”“Sicko.”“B*tch.” “Jezebel.”“Goth.”“Gold digger.” “Bimbo.” Your words don’t hold any weight on me. They only show how shallow and judgmental you are. Forget about what I am. It all comes down to who I am. Anyhow, just a few days ago, on Friday Fusion, I was set to face Charlotte in my debut match. However, the pretty little housewife got injured in her own debut match, by Miss Tomas. So, my opponent was changed to the very woman who wounded Charlotte...Teresa ‘The Tease’ Tomas. Not that it made a difference... ever since I had signed with this company, a few weeks previously; I had been training my ass off. I was busting my ass off to make a good impression in PCW. Not that I wasn’t a capable, experienced wrestler before...I just never had imagined being in such a publicized, amazing promotion. PCW was way up their, with OSW and FSW...if not above them. It had been my dream and goal for a long while. I had been sending my wrestling tapes around, about a month back...when I was shocked to finally get a call back from a PCW agent. A call that made me sit up straight and spit out my Sprite...telling me I’d finally made it into PCW. So, I came into the match with a bit of extra muscle, training, and experience on my side. My advantage was not that I was as well-known as the former NTICW talent, Teresa. It was that I had seen her wrestle in NTICW and in her debut PCW match...While she knew nothing about me. I always take a good look at my opponents, sizing them up, and researching them a bit. Just so I can be prepared...to know what to expect of my opponent. And so far, I’ve seen a lot of impressive talent when I watched Teresa’s match with Charlotte...from both of the women. She was very tough to ever pull an advantage over...and I wouldn’t have been surprised or upset, no matter who won our match...but it didn’t end in a pinfall. But, I was a bit upset with the outcome of our match. We could’ve seen who the better competitor of the two of us was. In stead...we saw Romana run out and take down Teresa. Then we saw Cecelia come out and even the odds by going after me. Eventually, the Divine Prophetess, known to be with Akiyama—Cyclonica—came out and got a good hand in as well. At one point, I believe that I had the upper hand in the whole chaotic situation... but Cyclonica’s hands were free to take me apart, once she had disposed of Romana. Yet she chose not to. She even treated me with a measure of respect when I caught her backstage, later. This question haunts me. Why?! Why me? I could easily have become another victim of Cyclonica while she had the advantage over me. I had my back to her, taunting Cecelia and Teresa... and she seemed to be just waiting to nail me. Then, I sensed her presence behind me...and I turned around to acknowledge her. Yet I felt no need to strike out at her...I felt no threat. We just left it at that, with no mere, pointless physical attack. I await an explanation. I search for the answers...but I am a patient person when I chose to be. Now, I get a small measure of revenge. On this Turmoil, I am booked against Teresa Tomas once more...as well as Cecelia and Romana. A small chance to redeem myself...in a match whose winner is the #1 contender for the Rising Star title. An opening to earn myself some respect...pay some more dues... And to get my hands on the three women I previously have met on Fusion. It’s not gonna be pretty.And I’ll make sure of it. I will put my blood sweat and tears into that match...until I have earned a quantity of respect from the PCW male and female division...as well as the staff. The fans—I couldn’t care less about. They’ll just jump the bandwagon of whoever kisses their ass most... they wouldn’t know talent if it spat in their face. They’re just mere cattle...following whoever can suit their needs...whoever looks the prettiest and flatters them the most. I’m not here to impress them...I know they’ll never know me for what I am...never mind who I am. All the same, I will still give that match my all, just as if it were a World Title match...or anything of that caliber. So, am I to be insulted they booked me against other women in a contendership match for the Rising Star title? Far from it. I will work my way out of “Jobberville”—as I’m a female new comer—with whatever it takes. And I will watch the females of join me in doing so...such as Brandi, who pulled out a win on the Cruiserweight title. Anyhow, before I bore any of you further...I’m sure you’re interested in my relationship with Jenna Jones. Yea, that blonde chick you saw in my first promotion...the one who I locked in my basement...Jenna. Who? Yes, I said Jenna Jones, not Jameson...no, she’s not a porn star, either. Though I give her the same respect I’d give a porn star...none. I will reveal everything at a later time. Let’s just say for now that she was a former friend who wronged me...and now she suffers for it. So, to settle your stomachs...I’m not just some sicko who kidnaps pretty girls and hides them in her basement. Jenna and I have a long history, so it wasn’t without reason. She stands for everything I stand against. You will understand it further, and be filled in additionally when I feel you all are worthy of it—. The door bursts open, and Jenna walks in the Anarchy’s room, interrupting her flow of thoughts. She’s in a coral-colored t-shirt and a pair of jeans. Jenna’s hair is wet, from showering. Anarchy must’ve grown bored of watching her shower once more... And she had no fear of Jenna escaping, as she had locked and barred up the bathroom window, and there were no other exits besides the door that connects to the bedroom in which Anarchy is in. Jenna looks a bit better and less emaciated since last time. Her face is bare of makeup, but has more of a glow to it...and her hair lies in far more healthy strands of wet golden blonde. Anarchy’s lying on the bed, dressed in a dark blue velvet baby doll dress that hugs her curves and barely covers her long, lean caramel crème legs. Her indigo lipstick is smudged, and her navy hair lies in a silk ocean of curls across the bed. She watches Jenna with hazy violet orbs. Jenna’s own ice blue eyes open wide. Jenna- “Omigod...what the...” Anarchy looks casually to the bed. A guy with long, curly milk chocolate brown hair lies next to her. He looks like your average pretty boy...with his toned abs bare. His flawless peaches-n-cream complexion is adorned with Anarchy’s indigo lip prints. His eyes are closed...and he’s tied to the bed with silk undergarments. Jenna rushes to his side with big eyes, and puts a hand to his warm face. Jenna- “Is he...is he dead?!?” Anarchy laughs and takes her hand away from his face. Anarchy- “Good God, Jenna sweetie—no! See, his face is warm. He’s just sleeping. I—” She giggles softly. Anarchy- “I must’ve tired him out.” Jenna- “Oh!...Ohhh...” She blushes, and looks to Anarchy, who watches her with a soft smirk. Anarchy leans over, and kisses the guy’s cheek gently, to wake him up. His eyes flutter open, and he looks up at her, and then to Jenna. He starts to speak, but she puts a finger to his lips. Anarchy- “Shhhhh...” And then to Jenna, she turns with an arched eyebrow— Anarchy- “Sweetie, you wanna join in on the fun?” The camera pans to Jenna’s shocked expression... Fade to black... Fin~*~
