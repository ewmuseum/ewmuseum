Returning home...

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.29.02 [fade in] We pull up to the gate at our home in Houston, Texas just arriving from New Jersey where we made our debuts and a good one at that. I get out the car and walk to the mailbox at the bottom of the driveway. Of course we got alot of mail since we have been out of town for a bit. I grab the mail and run back to the car. I hop back into the drivers seat while Mike is crashed out in the passenger seat still. I throw the mail at him. 'Hey bro wake up? We are here.' 'What,' Mike replied. 'I said wake up we are finally home. Did we get any mail worth reading?' Mike begins thumbing through the mail. 'Let's see...bills..bills...ads...oh here we go, a wrestling magazine called The Insider,' Mike stated. He pulls the magazine out as we reach the driveway. We both get out and grab our bags. Mike snags the mail also. We reach the door of our house. I grab the key and open it. 'Home sweet motherfuckin home!, I said with pure enjoyment. I throw my bag on the floor as Mike throws his on the couch. He sits on the sofa while I go to the kitchen. 'Hey Mike you want something to drink.' 'Yeah bro, grab me a water or something.' 'Okay. Hey what's that magazine about, anything interesting in there,' I said from a distance. 'I don't see anything yet. It looks like this magazine is a review of what has been going on in the world of wrestling. It reviews PCW,OSW,NITC and RUSH and a bunch of others I don't want to mention.' I make my way back to the living room area and toss Mike his water. 'What do they say about PCW, that's all we care about.' 'Well some guy is giving his take on the new crop of tag teams in PCW.' 'And...',I said as I want Mike to go on. 'Okay now here we go. Suicidal Tendencies are a team with no gimmick what so ever, and are like any other tag team in this buisness...and so on it says.' 'Who in the hell wrote that! Wait I don't care who wrote that. All it is is a guy who has his opinions,a nd that's all fine and dandy. What did he say about the team we knocked off last Friday, Cowabunga Connection?' 'They didn't comment on them since they were and still are in PCW.' 'I'm pretty sure he was high on them since they were one, and possibly still are, one of the top tag teams in this federation.,' I said. 'Ron I wouldn't even worry about it bro. Like you said it's a guy giving his opinions and he has a right to do that.' 'Mike all I know is that we sure as hell didn't need a 'gimmick' to knock of one of the top tag teams in CC.' 'Your exactly right, but you know next week at Turmoil its going to be another obstacle for us to climb,' Mike said. 'I believe we face The Heartbreakers right?' 'Yes we do. Let's just keep doin what we have done since day one. It's going to be a tough one on tuesday, but it's something that we can get through,' Mike said. 'It's something that we WILL get through.' [fade out]
