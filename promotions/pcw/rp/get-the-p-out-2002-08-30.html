Get The 'P' Out

30 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/08/02 “Like... Like... Like... SO... Like.... EWWWWWWWWWWWWW!!!!!!!!!“ With the disgusted wailing of PCW Commissioner, Eleanor Hall, we open up on the growling face of Xavier Kannon, brimming over with anger after DarkStar’s “Treatment” of his wife. Xavier : “The Goth will die...” As he mutters under his breath, Ellie~! continues her high pitched whining from the shower beside him, the thick steam from the long, hot shower protecting her modesty and showing only her form. Eleanor “He... Like... He... PISSED ON ME!!!!!” The little prep has now been in the shower for 45 minutes and has only just been joined by her husband. On the tiled floor of the shower room there are two empty bottles of shower gel, laying disposed of on the wet floor. Xavier : “And because of that... The Goth will die...” Even being close to the steam filled room requires Kannon to wipe his clammy forehead as he paces back and forth, thinking over and over in his head the smell that wafted into his nose when he went to comfort his wife. Eleanor : “Won’t you come in?” His wife’s voice softens as she invites him to join her in the shower, but with the night’s actions he isn’t quite seeing her as desirable as he normally does, and starts to think of an excuse. Xavier : “Er... I just had a shower my little Fuck Ferret.” His face locks in a hopeful expression, hoping his naive wife will believe him as another empty bottle of shower gel is thrown from the shower and down onto the slippery floor. Eleanor : “ARGH!!!” The whiny, high pitched and tearful sounding wail echoes through the steam and causes Kannon to cringe due to it’s sheer volume and the sound of his wife slapping her hands against the shower wall is heard. Eleanor : “Eugh... He pissed on me and now not even my own husband will shower with me!!” The PCW Champion again winces at the squealing of his wife who then can be frantically seen squeezing another bottle of shower gel all over herself and throwing the bottle out again.. Her bare arm protruding through the thickest of the steam. Xavier : “And as I said... The Goth will die.” He starts to talk into the shower room to comfort his wife but the image of her, wet with DarkStar’s urine won’t leave his head and forms a mental restraint that keeps him away from his naked, wet, and sud covered wife. Eleanor : “I’m out of shower gel.. Get me.. Some, like.. Sulphuric Acid or something!” Without thinking he turns and sets off he grab what his wife desires before he double takes, looking back to the steam filled shower and wondering if he heard her right. Xavier : “Sulphuric Acid? My little Shag Squirrel... Th-” Before he can even question her the preps voice, more venomous than before, shrieks out through the steam and sound of running water. Eleanor : “Yes I said Sulphuric Acid and don’t call me your little Shag Squirrel because... Because... YOU WON’T EVEN TOUCH ME!!!” The Scientologist looks around his locker room for something to use. Xavier : “In Hubbard’s name they’re going to be cleaning Goth blood off the ring for weeks...” Throwing anything he can’t substitute for Acid out of the way, Kannon hunts for something to appease his wife with. Eleanor : “I mean, like, HELLO?!?! He could have pissed on Cyclonia, or someone else who’d enjoy it... BUT ELLIE~!S DON’T LIKE IT!!!” Finally Kannon finds a bottle of mineral water, left out too long so that bubbles form on the sides and he proceeds to rip off the Evian label and reach into the shower room where Ellie~! swipes it. Xavier : “Hurry and use it, no time to look at the bottle.. It’s eating it’s way through the plastic as we speak!” The placebo appears to be working as the sobbing subsides and the plastic bottle is thrown put onto the tiles wet from the condensation of the past 45 or so minutes, enough to cause a little splash while Kannon continues to play along, doing his best to sound convincing. Xavier : “Oh.. I hope there was no acid left in that... It’ll burn through the floor!” His eyes look hopeful but it appears Ellie~! has bought it hook, line and sinker. Eleanor : “Now I’ve, like, burnt it all away with Sulphuric Acid there’s no reason why you can’t join me!” The Champion turns pale, the whole incident running through his head again, fighting a desperate battle to expel the thoughts to the back of his mind. Xavier : “I’m sure that shower is only meant for o-” Eleanor : “XAVIER!” Seeing there is no way out, the Gold Patron Meritorious walks into the shower room and towards the naked silhouette of his wife.. His face screwed up before he headbutts the wall. Xavier : “DarkStar... You are one dead Goth...” Again his forehead pounds into the tiles to try and rid his brain of the thoughts before he pulls his t-shirt off and unbuckles his pants, sending them falling onto the wet floor before he steps into the shower to entwine with his wife. Eleanor : “See?” Kannon’s less that sure voice is heard replying as the shot starts to fade out. Xavier : “Er.. Yes.. My little Fuck Ferret... Nothing wrong at all...” Through the steam we see the PCW Commissioner raise her near leg up and wrap it around Xavier’s waist, just over the hip joint before he reaches around and lifts her off the floor and against the tiled wall that has been bathed with hot water for 45 minutes to take the usual chill off it, Ellie~! sighing softly as the shot hits Black.
