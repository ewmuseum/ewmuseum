Victory at Last

27 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/27/03 Victory at Last! I tried week after week after week. So many shots, so little luck. My mind would wonder, my focus would diminish, and I would end up on my knees screaming for another shot after each and every failed attempt. Then it finally came to me on Sunday Night. All the focus and determination had come back to me. Winning was the only option. I had began a new lead in life, this lead could be no sweeter than with my hands wrapped around this handsome and lugubrious title. I starved to win, and my hunger died when the three count echoed from the mouth of the referee. I’ve felt satisfaction in my life, but none to this affect. The lust, emotion, surprise, and cheers could all not match up to this one moment. I was on top. The old El Romantico wasn’t on top, but I the smarter and worldlier El Romantico was on top. My fingers tingle, my heart pounds, my face changes it’s form as a smile extends from cheek to cheek. I was happy, and I didn’t need to love the world and be a messenger of God, doing only things I thought were right in order to be happy. Maybe I wasn’t the greatest soul on earth, but I got what I wanted and it brings joy to me. Nothing will come in my path to success. The blood spilt on this prestigious title will not be mine, but rather the souls who get in my way. Romantico grasps the title holding it to his bare chest. The clock appears to hit midnight as the lights flicker on, and music begins to play. The once dark and mystic room is now a beautiful ballroom, with just Romantico and the title beside him. I dance with this belt like a lover. The passion is immense, my grip is tight, and I do what ever I can to please it as much as it pleases me. Romantico continues to dance with his eyes closed, dreaming of the future, and a long beautiful bright road. I never had this before, and now, well now everything is so clear. This empire is so grand, so ready to do nothing but good for the world. And this empire is all mine. This title, that’s right all mine. Everything is at my fingertips. My search for power is in the past. Power has already come to me. Romantico prances around crazily in excitement. He slides on the ground and lifts his head back up with his eyes as red as blood. He opens his mouth slightly exhaling over and over. He takes his open free hands and then makes a hard mean fist. He who comes between thy, will feel my wrath and the wrath will not be a lovely sight. The door slips open and Romantico draws his attention to the person behind the large silver chamber door. From behind it comes a figure with so much beauty and hope. A face like an angel, and eyes that could do nothing but make you smile. She comes up to me in her light blue nightgown, harnessing a pillow to her side. Romantico, I can’t get to sleep the furnish keeps waking me. Come to me Julia. I grabbed her hand and I held her in my arms smiling. The joy was overwhelming. She looked at me with worry, but she would never know how great I was really feeling. I spun around let her down low to the ground pulling her back up. She laughed and rejoiced by my wild and silly antics. I was opening up in a manner I had never done before. I grabbed the rose I once held with supreme caution and rubbed it on her sweet and tender face. Tell me you feel happy. I feel happy Romantico. Good, because I want you to take my hand and follow me. She did as instructed as we walked together to the balcony. It was cool outside and caused chills to roll up her arm. She held her arms across her body as I smiled into her long and tender eyes. Have you ever tried to stand above the world? Sometimes I come out here and stand above it all. I take in the air, the light, and the darkness. Everything I value. And one day it will all be mine. I’ll engrave my name in history Julia. One way or another it will happen. You already have marked your name Romantico. A title is not enough, it’s a start, but more will need to come. Follow me! I stepped up on the ledge on my balcony. It was slim so I needed to use good balance. Julia was skeptical at first to step up, but she trusted me and did it. It was a little windy so she showed a nervous side at points. Just let the earths handle you for a while? Trust it, and it will trust you. Romantico hops around in one foot with no worries. Follow me Julia, be one in the crowd. Julia hops and almost slips off of the balcony, but remains balance. She becomes shaken and immediately goes back down. Apparently your trust isn’t what I thought it was. Julia ran off weeping back to her room as I stayed and laughed to the highest of worlds. I leaned back and feared nothing but fear itself. My joy was present; my goal was only a footstep away.
