Bringing Gehenna: A Vow Affirmed

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 20 of 2003 A.D. Bruce Shanahan knelt amongst the swirling darkness in that center of nethermost chaos, head bowed in reverence amid the encompassing blackness. A familiar guttural chattering flooded the realm and a wavy, multi-chromatic being hovered above the ground before him. It took no defined shape, its form continually fluctuating with the moment and shifting hues of color with the passage of time. Shanahan was torn within; instilled with unholy veneration as usual, yet despondent over the events of the past Sunday. He had felt the cold brush of death when Skylar Green had plunged the blade into his flesh, and even now felt he was only spared because Nyarlathotep forbid his demise. His thirst for glory had consumed him in the days prior, but his ambition simply was not to be that night. He had made an error in dealing with his foe, thinking the Old Ones would aid in the Celestial’s final annihilation. Nyarlathotep had felt otherwise, however, and he was not pleased in his priest’s inability to fulfill his assignment unassisted. There are motives behind our actions. Had the result of your contest been disclosed to you prior, there is no telling the impact it could have had. The future is a predetermined entity. However, human will makes it possible to alter these events and doing such has detrimental effects on the fabric of this dimension. For that reason, mortals are never told of their fates beforehand. Your defeat was foretold, but not the specifics. To say we are dissatisfied with your performance….. “I was almost killed, m’lord. I would have liked to know this.” You were never in any danger. You rank amongst our more vital Earthly agents, and I would have never permitted your demise until your duties have been fulfilled. Not even the Celestial held the power to mortally wound you with the knife he wielded. He does not hold sway over the dominions of life and death. I do. The Bringer of the Dark Gospel stood, gazing off into the eddy of eternal darkness beyond the sphere. “My ambition has yet to come to fruit. Will there be a time, Nyarlathotep?” Your aspirations will one day bear you success. Your time simply has not yet come. “Duke Williams does not even bow before the right pulpit. Why did that degenerate rustic achieve his goal whereas I was condemned to bear the wrath of the Celestial?” His glory will be short-lived. Those he worships hold an influence over the great scheme of life, though in truth not enough to maintain his exultation for long. We humored them, and Williams’ time in the sun was short-lived. But please…… that is not important right now. Dante Jackson won a trophy coveted by most of your organization, and your opportunity to seize that honor comes this Friday. Shanahan cracked a grim smirk, goose-bumps rising on his flesh. That chromatic aura, a manifested avatar of Nyarlathotep, emitted a mental cackle. Its tone was maddening, but thankfully Bruce had already lost his sanity long ago. This pleases you I gather? “Indeed so, m’lord. And I thank you for bestowing your blessing upon me.” He bowed low before the wave of color, bathed in the lambent light radiated from its ever-altering form. This opportunity has been granted for your unfaltering faith in our eminence, but your duties are far from complete. Not only must your efforts to unify the sects of the Earthly dominion continue, but Skylar Green must still be destroyed. Or at least……his threat must be purged. The cryptic demeanor of the Crawling Chaos’ revelation perplexed the cultist, and he showed his confusion with the raising of an eyebrow. “Is there an alternative way?” he persisted. “A method to cleanse Green of the power within him?” Shanahan could feel the deity exert a mental nod. It is possible to forever nullify the capabilities of Skylar Green and not necessarily bring about his physical destruction. The Celestials’ motives were never fully concealed from our vigilance; our agents are many and thus we always had a grasp of their intentions, that being the desire to bestow existence with one of the Sleeping Dragon’s capacity. Never did we intend on allowing their endeavors to go unchecked without answering in kind. The head of the Sect of Black Wisdom peered into the wavering entity of shimmering lights, his mind flooded with the knowledge of the cosmos as he basked in the presence of the immortal’s avatar. At the same time, hope was instilled back into his consciousness that he hadn’t been merely dispatched as a proverbial kamikaze to absorb the brunt of his rival’s wrath; the latter notion had cast him into despair that Sunday night as he had nursed his wounds. Ah yes……you wish to know the solution to negating the blind one’s energies? “Yes, m’lord,” replied Shanahan with an exulting bow. When the time comes, the necessary wisdom will be infused within you. But I will offer you fair warning at this time…..the battle shall not be decided either this week nor the next. He is one that is to be dealt with over a more elongated period, but do rest assured that in due time your labors will consummate in final, glorious victory. And when the Celestials have been deprived their trump card, the Great Equilibrium shall shift yet again. The dark cultist nodded his understanding, taking in a deep breath of the air permeated with the nurturing energies of the outer nebulas. To merely breathe the air around the incarnation of the Black Pharaoh could impart vitality in the faithful, and Shanahan felt renewed vigor and potency of spirit as the air flowed through his lungs. For now, mortal retainer, go forth into the word of men and spread the new gospel. Invoke faith in the faithless, and eradicate all whom deny our virtues. Capture the prize from Dante Jackson to demonstrate the benefits which our blessings award unto the believers. He is an irreverent cancer, one who must be removed as soon as possible. Do not allow his impiety to spread among the other agnostics. Make an example of the one they call the “Lyrical Messiah”. “Jackson shall reap the just rewards of his insolence, m’lord. I shall not fail.” You will be a beacon for the heathens to follow; it is your destiny, Bruce Shanahan. And you will be justifiedly honored for your deeds. Green stripped you of your right last week, and this week we come the harvest. “The Heavyweight title?” The deity was silent, that mad symphony of the hunting horrors’ demonic tongue reverberating through the void. His silence served just as well as words would have to the one known as Violence Jack. His opportunity had arrived. Go now, bellowed the dark god in his voice of a thousand agonizing souls. Head forth and vanquish our enemies. By your hand, Earth shall be plunged into the chaos from which it was begotten. Open the gates, loyal servant….. The glimmering entity slowly dissipated from view, its voice continuing to echo diabolically in that cold, desolate void floating amidst primal chaos. Shanahan stood, the cacophonous chattering of the horrors’ fading with the departure of their master. His purpose rang as clear as ever, and he would fulfill his duties. No one would extinguish his ambitions. They would be his enemy’s burden to bear this night, and not even death was an obstacle any longer. Jackson would pay, and this whole corrupt civilization would come to be martyred. Mankind would come to learn the truth, one way or another, and there would be no denying the Apocalyptic cleansing that doomed their destiny. Mortality was fleeting. But the Old Ones are forever…….
