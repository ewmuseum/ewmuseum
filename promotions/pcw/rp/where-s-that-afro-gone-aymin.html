Where's that Afro Gone, Aymin?

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Friday Fusion, a crowning day in the life of Flaymin Aymin. As that would be the day that he would lose his beloved afro, and whose hand was it that controlled the fateful razor? El Diablo. Why would El Diablo do this to an ex friend? Why would he do this to an ex stable member? Many questions left unanswered, but over the coming weeks these questions may be answered by this Mexican Devil. A proud El Diablo stands outside of a barbers shop, on a dark and lonely night. The area is completely baron, except for a few parked cars outside of buildings, where they no doubt work the night hours. El Diablo is with his wife of two months outside of this barbers shop, located somewhere in Dallas. The sky and streetlights, leave large shadows of light in some places, coincidentally, El Diablo and Angel are stood directly under one. There is just silence in the perfect night, as no cars pass through the dismal and desolate area and no people wander the streets, El Diablo and Angel are the focus of the entire street. Angel shivers; obviously cold from the lack of clothes she chooses to wear. All she wears is a leather mini skirt, which leaves little to the imagination, some high-heeled boots and a low cut top, with no jacket on. 'Can we go yet? I'm freezing,' pleads Angel still shivering in the cold breeze that blows. An insistent El Diablo looks across at her, and eyes her up and down slowly. Angel notices this and looks back at El Diablo with an odd look on her face. 'What?' she asks him rudely. He looks at her with a look of disgust in his eyes. 'Maybe if you wore more clothes you wouldn't be so cold,' El Diablo almost shouts, but is careful to keep his voice quite low on this late night out. Angel gasps at the response he gave her and turns away from looking at him, like how a child does. 'Oh come on,' he pleads with her in a joking manner, 'you know I'm right,' he finishes as a few droplets of raindrop onto the now angry and pms-ing Angel. Angel looks up at the sky and lets out a sigh. El Diablo walks towards Angel, who is now getting very annoyed at El Diablo and the situation she is in. 'Can we please leave now,' she begs while placing her hands on El Diablo's chest. El Diablo lets out a sigh. 'We'll find some cover,' he says to the delight of Angel, which is clearly shown by the smile on her face. The rain starts to come down heavier as they look around for a bus shelter of some kind, but there is not a shelter in sight, and no shops round are open to just take cover in. 'Where will we go?' Angel asks in panic. El Diablo just looks at her with a look of shock on his face. 'We came in the van, we can just get in there,' he states in a bewildered tone at Angel's recent stupidity. Angel smiles, as they run towards the black van they own. They open it at the back and both climb in, slamming the doors shut behind them. El Diablo leans over into the drivers seat and pulls out a torch from nearby it. He then switches the electric torch on, so that himself and Angel can have some light as the storm outside gets heavier and heavier by the second. 'Thanks,' says Angel politely to her husband as she tries to rub her hair dry with her top. Angel and El Diablo are both seated near the back doors of the van with the torch at the front. The sounds of that rain hitting hard on the roof of the van is loud on the inside, much to the bitterness of El Diablo, as he seems to want to be outside rather than being stuck in a van. 'You see that barbers shop?' El Diablo asks his young wife. Angel still rubbing her hair looks up at El Diablo. 'Yes?' a bewildered Angel asks. 'That reminds me of Fusion,' he laughs. Of course Fusion was the day that he sheered off all of Flaymin Aymin's afro, and left him bald and bitter. 'And the best part is, Angel is that there is more to come,' he smiles sadistically, 'I am fighting him on Sabotage and who knows how he'll be emotionally and mentally,' he jokes with Angel. Angel smiles slightly, but is still pissed from being cold and wet, as the sound of rain hitting the roof grows louder. 'It reminds me of Kojack,' he continues to joke at Aymin's expense, 'We're all born bald, baby,' he quotes Telly Savalas in another joke aimed at Aymin's new found baldness caused by him. El Diablo laughs, as Angel just remains silent attempting to dry her hair. He does fight Aymin on Sunday Sabotage, and these continual jokes aimed at him won't be much of a help to Aymin's inevitable backlash at him, but Diablo just doesn't seem to care about that anyway. The rain continues to crash down on the vans roof as a loud bang of thunder is heard, making Angel jump a little bit. 'Are you even listening to me?' he asks Angel with a look of contempt in his eyes. She looks up slowly. 'What?' she asks nervously. El Diablo almost throws a fit. 'OK, that's enough, we're getting out of the van,' he shouts at her. Angel pouts and then complains 'But it's wet and cold and thundering,' El Diablo's eyes light up. 'We're getting out now,' he yells at his wife. He stands up and grabs Angel by her arms and literally drags her, as he kicks the van doors open and pulls her out into the rain and jumps out as he does so. 'I oughtta punish you like I did and will Aymin,' he says to her, as she stands up with a wet mini skirt due to El Diablo. El Diablo then slams the van doors shut as the scene ends.
