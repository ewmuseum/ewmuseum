Adventures with Pete [6]: Next stop, a very janky cafe

23 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 23, 2002 Who am I? Do you really want to know? Okay, so you’ve already heard that line. If you haven’t, maybe you need to go down to your local cinema and buy a ticket to Spiderman. You see, I’m a lot like spiderman, although without the ability to stick to walls and shoot webs out of my arms. That’s just a little too weird for me. But we both have problems, we both have unresolved issues, and we’re both looking for something. He’s looking for love and romance, and I’m looking for a damn amulet that continues to evade me. I need it, if I want to win some shiny title and some damn respect. I don’t like to be disrespected, but it’s pretty obvious when you are just a flea in gaggle of flies. Well, if I need to win this title to get some attention around here, that’s what I’m going to do. [Tom removes his Oakland A’s cap while he’s at the table and picks up the bottle of ketchup.] Tom Lexian: Now this is touching…. Ketchup in a bottle. You know, I’ve been to about a million ball games, and never did they have ketchup in a bottle. It’s always in one of those packs or in a dispenser. It’s just so damn annoying. [Pete Rose, or Pete, is sitting alongside of me. He’s chomping into a burger which is oozing mayo and other greasy side products.] Pete Rose: That’s because we live in a lame society nowadays. Everyone wants it easy. No one cares about the classics or how things used to be done. Like all these cars nowadays. Why the hell do you need it to be so far off the damn ground? WHY? Back in my day, we used to care about two things: Did it run and how much does it cost? That’s it! Tom Lexian: Yeah, but back in your day dinosaurs ruled the Earth. Pete Rose: Oh, shut up Tom. You’re just mad because we had to take Sleazer with us. Tom Lexian: [throws up his hands in frustration] Fine, I’ll admit it. I don’t know why in the hell he’s here. I mean, he’s so badly dressed, I’m embarrassed to be sitting next to the guy. Pete Rose: If he can help us find that damn amulet, rest assured you won’t be complaining. [Did I fail to mention that we’re all eating at a café called Laura’s? Well, after meeting with R.L. Stine about the amulet, we figured we’d just head out onto the road and find this magic dragon. But of course, even seasoned heroes like ourselves need fuel. Road side cuisine is always in high demand.] Tom Lexian: Well, that’s true. But first we have to find this dragon, and how the hell does anyone expect us to do that? I mean, it’s not like we’ll just find a dragon flying around. [Just as I finished speaking, old Sleazer sat down. He was wearing a sky blue t-shirt with the words “Loser” on it (fairly appropriate) and navy blue pants. The pants had pink hearts on them. Please don’t ask me why.] Sleazer: Hiya guys. I just got out of the bathroom and, WHOO, does it smell bad! Tom Lexian: [covering his nose] I think we noticed. Why don’t you try to air yourself out a bit? Grab a mustard bottle and shove it right up there. Sleazer: Shove it where? Pete Rose: Your anus. [Sleazer looked confused, but did as he was told. Just as he was ready to send that sucker up there, I stopped him. There was no reason to make a scene here. And it was just flat out disgusting. Why did I even suggest it?] Tom Lexian: That’s quite alright, Sleazer. Why don’t you just sit down and enjoy your…your…[looks at the plate]…fried kumquats? Sleazer: Oh golly gee, you guys finally noticed! Doesn’t it look great? [Sleazer took a large bite and smiled, allowing some kumquat to come oozing down his chin. Pete looked like he was ready to heave, but I had seen this disgusting manner before. I, after all, have worked with kids. There isn’t a damn thing you can do about a kid who just doesn’t want to eat his lima beans.] Pete Rose: How can you eat that stuff? I mean, it smells like feet. Sleazer: It may smell weird, but it tastes like chicken. Pete Rose: [flabbergasted] Oh for christ’s sake, everything tastes like chicken. This hamburger tastes like chicken! Tom’s fries taste like chicken. I TASTE LIKE CHICKEN! [The entire café turned around to look at us, and I just buried my head in my hands. Why didn’t I ever fall in with the cool guys from OSW?] Tom Lexian: [whispering] Let’s try to keep our identities hidden here. We don’t want to make a scene and tell everyone that we’re looking for this lost amulet, okay? Sleazer: [shouting] WHAT? I CAN’T HEAR YOU! SPEAK LOUDER! Tom Lexian: [throws the ketchup bottle at Sleazer’s head] Now you listen to me, you ill dressing lunatic, I will not have you jeopardize this mission for the sake of being an idiot, so you will behave yourself. It’s bad enough we don’t even know where to begin, but to have driving me fucking crazy will be the end of me. Now sit down and eat! [Sleazer just sighed and began to eat his kumquats. Pete put his hand on my shoulder and tried to console me. I brushed off his hand and continued to snip away at my fries. It’s oily goodness wasn’t enough to put me into a good mood, though. I was upset, and why not? Everything was going wrong, and not only in my life, but with everything.] Tom Lexian: [sympathetic] Look, Sleaze, can I call you that? Sleazer: I guess. Tom Lexian: Sleaze, I’m sorry I snapped at you. But you have to understand what we’re up against. This amulet could change all of our lives. Pete could go back and make sure he doesn’t bet on those damn games. Pete Rose: HEY! Tom Lexian: I could go back and make sure I don’t break my leg in that car accident, which would eventually cause me never to become the high flying wrestler that I always dreamed to be. And you…well, you could go back and change whatever fucked up your life. Sleazer: There have been so many events, I wouldn’t even know where to begin. Tom Lexian: Well at least you’d get the chance. That’s all we want. The chance, and if we don’t get that, then we’re all wasting our times here. Pete Rose: You know, if I didn’t know any better, I would say that you were scared Tom. You’re scared of those two fellows that you have to face in that match, aren’t you? Tom Lexian: [crosses his arms] Don’t be ridiculous. I’m not afraid of anything. In fact, I’m not afraid of any animals, aliens, or large wrestlers named RENT-A-HERO! And for your information, there are three other guys in that match. Pete Rose: Oh, I’m soooooo sorry! [sarcastic] Three big, bad wrestlers that want to kill you. Tom Lexian: Look, I sort of have a friend in there. I mean, I wouldn’t kill Duke Williams an enemy. But when there is a title on the line and that bell rings, there won’t be anything stopping us from firing a few punches. Pete Rose: Okay, maybe you have an ally or two. But you’re still scared. I can tell. I used to act like this before a big game. Shaking, jittering, and a few loose farts of gas here and there. Don’t think I can’t smell it. Tom Lexian: [sniffs] Sorry. But anyways, let’s get back to business. I mean, we’re talking about an amulet here. [I look over at Sleazer, who looks like he had been thinking about the whole situation.] Sleazer: I understand. Hell, if I could change one thing, it would be that prom night where I wore a green suit. [Pete and I looked at each other.] Sleazer: Yeah, I know what you guys are thinking. You’re thinking, what was that varmit thinking by wearing a green suit to his prom? Pete Rose: Well, actually, I used the word MOTHER FUCKING IDIOT instead of varmit, but yeah, that’s basically what I was thinking. [8==0! OBSCENITY!] Sleazer: It wasn’t really green. More of a lime color. Tom Lexian: I think that’s beside the point. I mean, green or lime, what in the world were you thinking? It wasn’t Gumby’s prom or anything… Sleazer: I know, I know. It was stupid, but I had to do it. I couldn’t wear a boring black suit just like everyone else. I needed to be different, you know? Be my own man. Pete Rose: So you sacrificed your own self-dignity for the sake of being a man? Boy, you have it all wrong. To be a man, you have to play a sport. [Sleazer looked at me for help, but I just nodded in agreement.] Tom Lexian: It’s true. You have to ball it up in some kind of competitive sport, or else you’re not really a man. Sleazer: Well, I used to bowl… Tom Lexian and Pete Rose: [shouting] THAT’S NOT A SPORT! [I leaned over to Sleazer and whispered in his ear. I didn’t want the others to hear this, because I know how popular bowling is, so let’s just keep this between you and me. You see, bowling is a game. A very entertaining game. But are people going to shell out money to watch professional bowlers go out and try to score 300? No, I doubt it. That being said, bowlers get very little respect. How many bowlers do you know get laid each and every night. Thank you.] Sleazer: So, I’m not a real man. Pete Rose: Nope. Tom Lexian: [slaps Pete] Don’t listen to him, his burger has rat poison in it. Now, I wouldn’t say you aren’t a man, but brother, you have to get it straight. I’m not a man because my name is Tom Lexian. I’m a man because I go into a ring and beat the living hell out of people. Sleazer: So do I need to become a wrestler? Pete Rose: You need to find the amulet. [So there it was: We all needed that damn amulet. How we were going to do this, I wasn’t sure, but this was certain – It wasn’t going to be easy. As we finished our meals, the floor began to shake. I looked around, thinking it was an earthquake, but suddenly the wall to the café blew open, causing a huge, gaping hole to appear. Everyone fell to the floor from the blow, and when I finally came to, I saw a very familiar person coming through.] DARTH VADER: WHOOOOOP EEEEERRRR….WHOOOOP EEEEERRRR….I have come for the amulet. Where is it? [Oh, goodness…]
