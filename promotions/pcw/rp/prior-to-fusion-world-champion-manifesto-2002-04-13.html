Prior to Fusion - World Champion Manifesto

13 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 13th April 2002 OOC Note: Jeez…what have I got to say but thanks? There’s a part inside me, smacking me around the back of my head, telling me to stop being such a moron, forget about being overworked and unleash your creative side and to thank those that have supported me in the past year or so – you know who you are *Crash of lightning*. A change of style in this one…more an evolution into a double-skit. One being an article written by this overworked wrestler here (Through no fault of my own or PCW for that matter, it’s A-Level exam time over here, and my local supermarket must be selling brainmatter at 3 quid per ounce with the amount of work I do these days.)…and the second being a more traditional view of the roleplay. Thanks again for your support, and enjoy this hastily written promo that was written before I could check out Fusion… The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book Two: Satisfying Demand…… RP#1 for Sabotage… Location: A secret location, an empty room…with a table…and Dustin…and Chris Chase…and walls…and the stench of papyrus…and the PCW World Title…and --- Chapter One: The World Champion Manifesto…from the New World Champion…you know, the one that DIDN’T blow it after three DAYS! – From Beyond the Mid-card Machine… The PCW World Champion Manifesto – First Edition… It’s fair to say that I was unfair in capturing this belt…at such an interval in the timescale of the PCW federation… Unfair? Of course, being brilliant is unfair! I mean, it’s unfair that I pretty much beat the competition hands down when it comes to brilliancy. Heck, I was bright enough to eliminate BOTH of my opponents in the Spring Fling Mainevent – Chris Sloboda and Alexander Miyagawa – instead of being the prototypical cocky “asshole” type of person who’d turn the turnbuckle into a temporary hammock. But that’s just Chris Chase…Chris Chase is a person that need not be applied to the typical clichés of the Professional world…a person that cannot be sufficiently stereotyped to a degree that is crystal clear to those cretins sitting at home… Of course, what is crystal clear to these trailer park citizens, other than a mildly stained wine glass? Sheesh…you’d think that they’d just hand out definitions these days, instead of leaving it to a person who isn’t even responsible for this fiasco to pick up the torch and run with it, wouldn’t you? Originality. I am THE first to conjure up a true to script manifesto…an obnoxious way of saying that “I’m Better than You” all over and over and over and over and over again – the first of its kind…a virgin territory for the PCW World Title. I popped my World Title Cherry on Sunday, thus it’s now my given right to pop the cherries of this and many other wonderous things – such as female teenage groupies for example – until the cows come home…or at least until this “Pinnacle of Profession Wrestling” finds it in the hands of some undeserving loser who’d basically just lucked it out. And there was I thinking that the Lucky Charms corporation had folded…well, I can dream, can’t I? Why, it were the dreams of millions that got me here. Call me conceited, but the dreams of millions of fans wished me on my way to becoming the PCW World Champion…the saviour…the king of the new era. How can you not LOVE me? How can you not SUPPORT me? How can you not WORSHIP me? The overwhelming influence of ‘The Messiah’ will continue to prosper in the wake of my coming of age… I’m twenty-three years old…I may not be the youngest World Champion, but I’ve already managed to accomplish what supposed “PCW Greats” Kid Wonder and Howard Porter couldn’t dream to achieve. I’m only the third former Cruiserweight Champion to hold this strap, I’m only the fourth former PCW2 wrestler to hold this belt…and I’m your only hope against the tedium of old. After all, who in their right friggin’ minds would want to watch ‘Grampa’ wrestle his good buddy ‘Sid’ from his old High School. These people don’t want some extra from Gladiator as their champion…they don’t want Big Alan Steele to be champion. After all…what warranted this shot? Did he singlehandedly defeat BOTH Boda and Miyagawa for this belt? Nope…did he make the downright spectacular leap from the Cruiserweight division to World Champion? I think not. Speaking in tongues and wielding such delightful catchphrases as “HI-KEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEBA~!” aren’t going to get you very far against Chris Chase…yes, the same Chris Chase, descended from a great line of Champions. Yes…I am better than you…you…you…you…and you of course…and don’t forget you…jeez, you’re particularly sucky…yep, you too… ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ “Chris, Chris, Chris, Chris…Baby, I’m gonna make you a star, a living legend, a king among jobbers. Mister Chase and the PCW World Heavyweight Title – THE Movie! I can see it now, your name in neon, a PCW World Title replica the size of a winnibago…and my name in the credits. Dustin Downey! – Director extraordinaire…defeater of Reeco Suave…king of the Cruiserweights, buddy to the CHAMPION! I tell you Chris, there is an immense amount of marketing potential leaking from you baby!” “DO YOU REALLY HAVE TO TALK LIKE THAT? I’M TRYING to WRITE HERE!! Sheesh, you could at least give me some rest after the week that I’ve had, all this media publicity, book signings, kissing babies. That and my title victory went off without a hitch. God, I love myself for that one…and no one saw it coming!” “I did…” “You were in on it for a start…that’s why you saw it coming Dustin…” “Actually I didn’t mean that…I was chatting to some obese fan before the match, and he just happened to let me in on it…and besides…you’ve loved yourself ever since I met you anyway, buddy. You frickin’ Canadians are so predictable…” “WHAT?” ::.~(Voice of Chris Chase)~’The Messiah’ Chris Chase – PCW World Champion. If that sounds so good, why do I feel like I don’t truly deserve it? What the hell are you thinking, Chase? You’re the most spectacular individual in the history of the planet, and --- Oh, I forgot about you there for a moment. This feeling’s been driving me nuts, of course, I deserve the title and all…but aren’t I still before my time? Confused? Thought that I’ve gone soft all the sudden? Like I’m some kind of insecure little kid, who whines at most things? Well…you’re 100%, completely…WRONG! Heck, even if I was before my time, I’d be able to brag about it – the immature saviour to a federation that had its fair share of ups and downs over the years. All this media hype has knocked me off my feet. I mean, heck, I’ve bragged about myself ever since I can remember, but now with EVERYONE doing so…it just doesn’t have the same edge anymore. Well…I tell you, this lil’ PCW World Champion isn’t going to rest on his laurels. Nah, far from it actually…let’s find more ways to brag, showoff, and generally berate the dozens of self-righteous jobbers trying to make their mark here… Returns. Usually considered to be nothing more than jokes, and excuses to make celebratory commendations of a returnee. Ever remember the Undertaker’s return to the WWF a couple of years back? Well…didn’t that lead to something sucky for the Under-seller and treated as a joke for the rest of his career? Sure, sure, sure…that isn’t to say that the recent return of New Breed – with Snow’s intervention at Spring Fling winning me the strap – wasn’t anything desirable. In fact, what could be more prosperous for a federation than a team combined of the greatest PCW talents that have ever stepped into the federation. Two PCW World Champions. This is what Big Alan Steele has to defeat if he is to become PCW World Champion, if he is to beat yours truly – which is a mammoth task for anybody…let alone Big Alan Steele. The dude wouldn’t know what a title win would be even if it slapped him in the face. By ZEUS…THOU IS A LOSER! And if you think that this Promo is going to end here…then you’re right. Nothing more can be said about this situation – where I sit in an empty room, accompanied by my former tag partner and now Stablemate…sitting at a table with a piece of paper, documenting the thoughts of a World Champion. As I said, this room is empty…far less cluttered than the outside world. Ah, the outside world, where my name is chanted as the great hero that I am…actually…come to think of it…this promotion will live on. It will live on because a great Champion can make even the simplest of things look semi-exciting…take Dustin Downey as a direct example. (Handler’s note –See Computer…See Window – if it crashes again, that’s where it’s going to exit my room.) My mind races, it’s close to my match with Big Alan Steele. What are my plans? What are my tactics? Why in the blue hell am I asking you for? What am I going to WEAR? Jeez…it’s typical…I’m due to make my first defence ever…and I’m at risk of screwing it up. Not this time, I can’t foul up tomorrow. It’s not physically possible for me to fail now…as long as I wear the World Title belt…I am all-powerful….:: Chris: I AM ALL POWERFUL!! ---- MUAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!! Dustin: Eh? Come again? I didn’t quite understand what you were saying just then…you, all powerful? Ha! There’s fat chance of that ever happening I tell you…Chris Chase being all powerful has just as much chance of being real as Dustin Downey being halfway intelligent. Besides, these ass spelunkers would try their hardest to dethrone you… ::.I smack Dustin around the back of the head…smirking arrogantly as I scribble down onto the paper…almost tearing a hole through the papyrus type material….:: Chris: Damnit Dustin…I was just finishing my manifesto with a particularly witty ending. Are you some kind of total moron or something? You think I’d just yell that like it for the hell of it? Do I look like Alan Kriegman or something? Dustin…say something that stupid again, and I will throw this table at you… Dustin: Oh no…you wouldn’t do that, would you? Come on, I’m your buddy…your pal…don’t pick up that table…don’t you even bother trying to throw it at me…YOW!!!!!!!!!!!!! ::.A table soars across the room, and smacks Dustin firmly in the forehead. He lands on the floor with an appropriate bump. The camera pans, and a seething, annoyed looking picture of an exasperated World Champion brightens up the dull picture. I look down on my buddy with a snort as he steadily brings himself around. He looks at me with a confused look, and frantically scans the room with his wide-open eyes. Swiping a hand around the immediate vicinity as if he were wielding a broadsword, he glares at me as if he hadn’t ever met me….:: Dustin: AHA! Taste cold steel…feeble Jerkacles!! Spawn of hades, you will perish! Oh…who in the seven seas of Poseidon might you be? And what is with that ridiculous belt?!? HI-KEEBA~! TO BE CONTINUED…………
