A Morning at the Salon

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/02 Losers. Mike Frampton and Big Daveylicious, the self proclaimed Sensations of Annihilation prove just why they are losers on Turmoil. They represent everything in the world that is imperfect and blemished. Not only did they lose, they lost to two wrestlers that are just as bad as they are, Suburban Uprising. If Jack Valentine had his way, he would join the tag team division all by himself, just to prove that he could take out the miserable peons that are involved with that waste of a division. But, PCW won’t allow that to happen, and Jack sure as hell will never take a partner…he doesn’t need one. So it looks like the tag team division is lucky…for now. We join our cocky, egotistical friend in a hair salon. The same hair salon he rejoices every morning for several hours to get that perfect hair for the day. The camera opens the door to the hair salon and pans around to see all of the hair-gripped customers receiving their hair treatment. The cameraman continues on to the back of the salon until he reaches a pink curtain with Jack Valentine’s picture on the side and a little biography of the mediocre wrestler. His hand reaches out in front of the camera to pull the curtain back and inside Jack Valentine is sitting in a huge pink chair, lined with pink fur and there are several large framed photographs of hearts surrounding the room. As the cameraman positions himself in front of Jack, he points the camera up at the hairdresser for a brief second. The hairdresser is dressed in all black, has his hair gelled back and a cross earring in his right ear. He carries himself in a very femme fashion as he clips Jack’s hair and styles it hair by hair by hair. The hairstylist begins striking up a conversation with Jack once the cameraman gets positioned. He speaks with a very gay, girly voice as he continues to trim away at Jack’s hair. Hairstylist: “So Jack…any big plans for the weekend? You and Christina going anywhere…exquisite?” Jack Valentine: “No, actually I’m going back to PCW Friday night.” Hairstylist: “That’s…Fabulous! I thought you were wounded pretty bad though the last time you were that PCW nonsense.” Jack Valentine: “Yeah, that unstylish piece of trash Hank hurt my knee pretty bad, but I’ve been training hard to get back in that ring.” Hairstylist: “And who may I ask will you be rolling around in the ring with Friday night?” Jack Valentine: “That would be none other than another tasteless piece of trash. Someone who calls themselves Punisher 2000, Mike Frampton.” Hairstylist: “Oh, I’ve seen those big, bulging muscles that he has. His style went out in about the year 2000 though…” Jack Valentine: “Along with his wrestling ability.” The two have a laugh together and the hairstylist throws up a hand in a very gay manner to Jack for him to high five him. As the hairstylist continues to laugh with Jack, he pretends to kiss Jack on each of his cheeks from behind the chair. The cameraman reaches his head out from the other side of the camera and questions Jack. Cameraman: “Did he just kiss you?” Jack Valentine: “He’s French, it’s ok…it’s what all French people do, so I’m told.” Cameraman: “Whatever floats your boat.” The cameraman goes back to refocusing the camera, and the hairstylist and Jack continue their…intriguing…conversation. Hairstylist: “How do you think you’ll fair in the ring against him…you haven’t wrestled another man in quite some time now.” Jack Valentine: “Are you kidding me? Jack Valentine doesn’t know the meaning of ring rust. It’s like riding a bicycle, you always want to know once you get in there.” Hairstylist: “Tell me about it…” The hairstylist let’s out a giant grin as he pauses snipping Jack’s hair and looks to the ceiling with his eyes closed. He shakes his head and let’s out another sigh before he continues cutting. Hairstylist: “Aren’t you afraid that his partner, Big Daveylicious, might be down at ringside to help me. I love Big D, and I can’t get enough Big D, but Big D might get in the way when you are fighting that other man.” The cameraman shakes his head in disbelief at the gay comments coming out of the hairstylists mouth, but doesn’t say anything. He just continues shooting this event…for whatever reason, he doesn’t know. Jack Valentine: “Big D and Mike Frampton are quite possibly the worst tag team in PCW history. They need to put two deficient wrestlers like that together, just to get their combined talent close to that of someone like Hank.” Hairstylist: “Ain’t that the truth. But, you know what they say, two Big D’s are better than one.” The hairstylist chuckles to the disgust of the cameraman. Hairstylist: “What else is new in the fashionably loud life of Jack Valentine?” Jack Valentine: “Well, since the heads of PCW know what a huge draw I am, they decided to throw me into the Rising Star battle royal, just to add some spice to that match.” Hairstylist: “And what beefy men will be competing in that packed ring?” The cameraman seems to choke on the words of the hairstylist, letting out a cough of abhorrence that is heard on air. Jack Valentine: “Jay Sylo, Mike Frampton, Ben J. Pailthorpe, John Crichton, Andy Murray, Shawn Mosley and Duzza.” The hairstylist let’s out a loud, semi-orgasmic noise after each name is mentioned from Jack Valentine. The cameraman almost drops the camera after Jack mentions the last name due to the sick, gay mind of the hairstylist. Hairstylist: “Aren’t you excited to step in the ring with all of those hunky pieces of meat?” Jack Valentine: “Actually I am…” The cameraman coughs, due to Jack responding to the gay comment, but then Jack finishes his statement. Jack Valentine: “…I can’t wait to kick all of their asses left and right. Obviously, I’ve had the most experience out of all of them…” The hairstylist interrupts. Hairstylist: “Yes, yes you have.” Jack Valentine: “…but I don’t know if I want to be known as the Rising Star champion. I’ve had so many chances to win that belt in the past, yet I have never wanted to keep that blue piece of trash, so I lost on purpose.” The cameraman coughs under his breath, “Bullsh*t.” Hairstylist: “You are a world class ‘champ’ in my mind Jack. You could go the distance every time if you wanted to.” Jack Valentine: “I know, I know. These damn injuries keep blocking my window to greatness. It’s not like I need a belt to prove I’m the best though. I’ve pinned Vampir Nosferatu, Ed and Trashcan Man…what more do I need to prove?” Hairstylist: “Not a thing to me honey…I’ve loved you ever since you won your first bodybuilding contest and started coming to me for hair advice.” Jack Valentine: “I’m getting sick of talking about how bad I’m going to beat all of these wrestlers…even though it’s almost a sure lock that I will win every single match that I compete in this time around. What else is on your mind?” Hairstylist: “How’s Christina been, I haven’t heard you talk about her in awhile…” Jack pauses and looks around for a little while, as if he is stalling to answer the question. Jack Valentine: “She’s been away for the past couple of weeks, out doing some modeling shoot or something like that, I haven’t really talked to her much since she’s been gone.” Hairstylist: “I know how that goes…whenever my partner goes away, I hardly ever talk to hi-” Jack interrupts before the hairstylist can say him, as if to keep his openly gay ways oblivious to him. Jack Valentine: “I’m not worried though…” Hairstylist: “About?” Jack Valentine: “I’m not worried about all of the guys that she’s with right now…all of those models. I’m better looking than all of them, and I’ll always be a better model. It’s not like she’s going to leave me for any of them…no way.” A somewhat worried Valentine seems to be a little worried about Christina and her happenings during the modeling job that she is on. Hairstylist: “I’m sure everything will be just fine with Chris…and if things don’t work out…I’ll always be by your side, you can count on me.” The cameraman lets go of another nauseating expression on his face after the hairstylist’s continuous gay references. Jack Valentine: “I know everything will work out…who would leave Jack Valentine? I know I wouldn’t. I’ve got nothing to fear…nothing at all.” Jack adjusts himself in the chair and takes a glance at his hair in the mirror. Hairstylist: “You are looking fine today Jacky…mighty fine.” Jack Valentine: “Why thank you, are you almost done?” The hairstylist makes one last dainty cut of Jack’s hair and throws his hands back as if he had just won a marathon. Hairstylist: “Done!” Jack moves side to side in the mirror, glancing at every single hair and making sure that each one of them is in place. Jack Valentine: “Great job, couldn’t of done it without you. Well, actually I could…because I look so damn good already, but you just add that extra pizzazz that gets me those extra looks on the street.” Hairstylist: “Always a pleasure rubbing your head.” As Jack hands the hairstylist a hundred dollar bill…the only tip that the pompous Valentine would give to anyone…the cameraman exit’s the room, mumbling to himself in disgust about the ambiguously gay hair dresser. The scene fades to pink as the Heartbroken Production text flashes across the screen.
