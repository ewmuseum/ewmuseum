-Conniptions-

8 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.08.02 ~*~ Fade in... The camera opens at the PCW locker room. The camera pans to the names of the roster on the locker room doors for a useless moment. It comes to the wing of the locker room that has the female division’s lockers after a while. BRANDI TERESA TOMAS CHARLOTTE ELLIE~! CECELIA VERUCANA ANARCHY The camera then stops in front of this locker room. The name of the female PCW wrestler is in a steel blue, and the ‘A’, in capital letters, with a circle around it...the symbol of anarchy. The door to this room is partially open. The cameraman nudges the door open, and walks in. From the back, a blue-haired vixen can be seen. Her hair cascades half-way down her back, in a mass of midnight azure curls. She’s sitting at the edge of a dark blue velvet stool. Her pants are sagged low at her hips, and a violet thong can be seen peeking out of the top of her jeans…As can a small tattoo of a snow leopard be seen on the lower right side of her back. This woman, presumed to be Anarchy—as that’s what it says on her locker room—is sitting in this small room that’s barely larger than a broom closet. No, in these little temporary stadiums that hosted PCW shows, she was lucky to even get her own locker room...never mind a large one. All the same, she didn’t hold an office position like one of those chicks in the “Front Office” had, like Ellie and Brandi did. The camera moves around to the front of the small, cramped-up room. He views the walls, which are a powder blue color, and have “ANARCHY” and “ASYLUM” spray-painted in navy on them...with, once again, the ‘A’ being the symbol of anarchy. There are sketches posted all over the walls...Sketches done in charcoal pencil, of various creatures...mythological ones...among them are some of the various wrestlers of PCW. This must be what she does on her spare time...the insanity of an artist... Then the camera finally pans back to Anarchy. Over her torso is a slate-blue scoop neck baby doll t-shirt. Her anarchy tattoo is peeking out, on her lower abdomen, and her sapphire navel piercing is prominent against her caramel crème tummy. The camera travels up to her elegantly sloping shoulders—that somehow had a surprisingly powerful look to them. Around her graceful neck, adorning her defined collarbone is a choker. At the end of the thin, black leather rope is a silver emblem. It has the appearance of a distorted crucifix, but is the Egyptian sign of life...The Ankh. Her large, almond-shaped eyes are lined in a smoldering charcoal grey pencil, painted in the typical Egyptian style. Her eyes are the usual bright, exotic violet of the contacts that she wears. Her natural eye color is a warm honey brown, but she chooses to wear contacts that bring out the blue of her hair...and of the clothes she wears of various blue shades. Her navy blue eyebrows are arced and furrowed—in concentration of whatever she’s doing—causing a small crease between her eyebrows. Her succulent lips are painted indigo, and she chews on the side of her lower one. She narrows her eyes, working the paint brush that’s gripped in her fingers onto the canvas propped up in front of her. Thoughts race through her head, as she paints the ivory surface with a furious pace. Overwhelmed by her thoughts...engulfed in her own little world... One by one, stings my eyeseach is gone, each a disguiseSmiling, so awarePrecious moments so rareBoiled thoughts... Fusion... The word itself refers to a combination, a mixture. Two or more elements basically come together. They blend, and become one...And fuse, so to speak. And so there happens to be a certain PCW wrestling show on Friday called “Fusion”. I found it a very fitting word for the show that went on this Friday. Another appropriate name could have been “Chaos”...or, hehe, “Anarchy”...for there was a lot of that. Confusion...disarray...pandemonium...might as well have been “Turmoil”. I was booked for no matches at that time, but I was most definitely involved throughout the whole show. Previously, on that weeks Turmoil, I had caught up with Cyclonica to talk with her...after saving her from a good beating from Akiyama. A bit after our talk, the rest of Asylum caught up with us... ...With an attack. Now that...was the farthest from my intentions... a set up. While that is my stable, and those are my boys... I wouldn’t save you from Akiyama only to give one of them a shot at the #1 Contendership... -A small, ironic laugh- Oh, no, no...If the contendership to the World Title was on my mind... I’d take it for myself... Evaporated – Gonecan’t bear the loss of never loving you so jaded, so wrongStripping my skin over again Oh, call me selfish, but I say to each his or her own. I don’t see it that way at all. I know very well that every member of Asylum is capable of getting that contendership on their own. As for me...as a new comer, and a female...who knows, I probably won’t get a shot like that often. So, if I wanted it enough...believe me, I would have that already. I would have tried to catch you by surprise, and I’d have it. But that wasn’t the case...Those were not my intentions, my dear Divine Prophetess...or else I ‘d make sure you would know it. To add...injury to insult, later, Asylum got into the limo—with one member discluded. That member was assumed to be me...as someone later ran Akiyama over, barely missing Cyclonica. Akiyama...even as a God...suffered fatal wounds from the hit-and-run. I can claim that it wasn’t me all I want...but that is meaningless to everyone. Who would believe me? So, on this Fusion, the Divine Prophetess herself confronted me in the Asylum locker room...questioning my whereabouts on Tuesday night. She brought with her, the wounded Akiyama. Her intentions were still unsure. Back when they were teaching me to color in between the linesin the cushion of my soul I found a remote and now it's changing timepeeling layers, layers, layers.Stripping my skin over againShivering pale, under my nails Later, she brought me to the parking lot. There I found her spray-painting Asylum’s limo silver. I couldn’t understand her intentions still... I had found the black shell of the limo bland and boring all the same. Silver would be a nice change of color...if not my favorite color, blue. Then she tricked me into getting into the limo...and locked, then chained me in. I managed to break out from the limo, before she could set it afire, I broke out through the other window. Asylum later came out to assist me, unknowing I had already escaped. They found that an I-beam had suddenly crushed the limo, courtesy of Cyclonica and her hooded companion. So what was this to anyone? A betrayal of me to Asylum? A betrayal and mistrusting of Cyclonica towards me? That is left to be judged by those involved and those viewing. It is nothing that concerns you... Now, onto this coming Turmoil. I discover that I’m booked against Seymour Smoke...as I won the #1 contendership for the Rising Star belt. I have intentions of winning it...of walking out of that building the Rising Star champ. But, win or lose...make no mistake...I will get you to notice me. I will put on a good show. I am also involved with an 8-Person, Intergender battle for the World Belt...Asylum vs. The Front Office. I have little doubt in my mind of who’s going to win. Unless...as usual, that the Front Office does a screw job to tip the scale in their favor. I will not bat an eyelash or arc a blue eyebrow if such an event occurs. But I will not job to anyone, no matter how powerful and important they are...take good note of that. All the same...my mind is in conniptions. What is Cyclonica’s connection to me? What is Akiyama’s link? Why, when given a chance, do I not feel like attacking Cyclonica? Why must I feel obligated to look after Cyclonica...as I did on Turmoil? These questions haunt my mind...they have been hampering me since my debut. A simple, close-minded person would label it as being some sort of sexual chemistry between us...and I laugh at such a suggestion. Until I decide again to grace you all with my presence, I am done... At that very moment, the door to the little locker room opens. Outside, stand three men—one with goggles, the other with green hair and a trademark jade suit...and the last, a towering behemoth. As can be assumed, it’s the other members of the Asylum...Alexander Miyagawa, Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green, and Nerezza. The voicing of the camera fades, as the four can be seen conversing. After a bit, Anarchy finally gets up from her painting and pondering. She pulls a black, full-length trench coat over her t-shirt and bondage pants, and follows them out the door. The camera finally pans to the easel that she was painting at. The image in the intricately detailed painting is that of a female...With ice blue and raven tresses...blue lips much like her own...lovely, slanted, almond-shaped eyes...high, defined cheek bones... Back when they were teaching me to color in between the linesin the cushion of my soul I found a remote and now it's changing timePeeling layers, layers, layers.Escape in a drug not lasting enoughnow I just use my fingers for loveRocked back, forth, cornered alonePurging mind feeds a starving soul Uncoil, unwrap, shed, scrape, peel, naked, alive, tear down – feel. ...Cyclonica... Fin ~*~
