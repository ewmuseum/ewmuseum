Good Vs. Evil

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered “It is a tale as old as time itself… the unending duel betwixt good… and not so good! Between that which is right, and those that would wrong others. There is no escaping it – in order to have righteousness, we must also forcibly accept the true nature of evil upon the Earth.” We rejoin our Big and Bold hero, Big Alan Steele, as he stands before a huge auditorium, anointed with a cap and gown, sheepskin clutched in his gesturing hand. The massive crowd here is dressed alike, as it is graduation day here at UCLA, and Pauley Pavilion to be exact. It has been our heroes great honor, to receive an honorary diploma from this distinguished hall of knowledge, and only fitting that he speak to this outgoing class of burgeoning youth about that which he knows best. Our need for heroes. “Our world is at a crossroads, as even my humble hometown of San Jose has been beset with the ever scrawling stench of evil, as it seeks to infiltrate each and every crevasse the world has to offer. Yes, I speak of the ongoing threats of world domination by super-villains! Who can save us of this pox on humanity?! Whereas I can safely say that I have begun to belabor that very task, I would also like to extend the challenge to each and every one of you youthful graduates, as inside each one of you, as superhero is waiting to emerge!!” A goateed student stands, raising his hand. Big Al graciously nods towards the student, granting him the floor. “Wait… are you suggesting that we all try and take out SUPER-villains? By definition, they are beyond ordinary human standards, and statistically, 50% of all of us here are mediocre and lower. How do you expect us to challenge a villain the likes of… Dr. Doom, for example?” Steele nods slowly, indicating a wealth of knowledge and experience on the subject. “Indeed! Tis quite the foamy dilemma. While I would be foolish to put forth the notion that you or any of your friends here today would be adequate combatants in the war against super-villainy, I merely suggest a metaphorical approach to the problem! Using your same statistics, possibly only one or two of your grand graduating class have the mettle to become a superhero… or a super-villain, a path I strongly dissuade! But the vast majority of you shall wallow in a series of mediocre careers and human relationships, causing you an untold of amount of grief as you long unendingly for the salad days of your college years! It is to that end that I say that let life become your super-villain, as you resist the urges to possibly take your own life through fatal, and often times messy, means!!” The student’s face blanches, as he nervously looks around and slowly sits back down. The audience is a bit squeamish, as Big Al beams a grin of satisfaction and a thumbs-up to his trusted manager, Sue Saksnussum, who sits in the wings of the stage. Sue breathes a deep sigh and hangs her head. Steele again faces the audience and booms aloud, his voice suggesting strength and virility. “As you can see, the battles we face are all relative. Whilst I shall wage wars against the evils of humans imbued with supernatural strength and minds bent purely on destruction, you shall battle against the phone company from taking your service away, or possibly fighting the uphill battle of publishing your weak attempts at a screenplay! Each person put on this planet by the gods serves a purpose, and their battle proportioned to the size of the shoulders that bear them! And as it happens, blessed by the gods, as it were, I find myself burdens with the yolk of goodness, forced to carry the banner of justice until my dying day! Hi-Keeba!!” “Tis a life of sacrifice and eternal vigilance, as the demonic hordes of Hades breach unto our world, which is why the role of the hero, big or small, must be played out continuously. As it is, our fight shall essentially be for naught, as the world struggles to maintain a balance between good and evil. Ours is not a fight to be won, but an everlasting war waged against the underworld that bubbles forth, threatening to overtake our society!! It is the bane of the superhero… for as one hero appears, the villainy rise up to take the hero down. Should good heroes ever cease to fight… our world is doomed as the murderous scoundrels that would take over have no plans on balancing the Earth justly, and only seek to feast on its resources, like parasites!!” Another student stands, his face curious, “You mean, you don’t fight to eliminate evil, but to only sorta counter-balance it?” “No, see how your comprehension fails you!” A grinning Big Al admonishes, “Truly I fight for good to overtake evil and replace it with the sweet, delicious nectar of heavenly edict, but alas, the role of the superhero is less proactive than you would imagine, as ALL people have a right to these freedoms… super-villains included!! It tis this very fact that forces my hand to be REACTIVE, and only battle the criminal element as it happens, thus allowing such nefarious scum the opportunity to change their ways, and become productive, functional members of a utopian society.” “But wouldn’t it just be easier to wipe’em out BEFORE they get on their killing sprees, and bank holdups and whatnot?” the curious student furthers. Big Al speaks in heavy tones, “But of course it would, and thus lies a hero’s problem: how doth one attack that which has not committed a crime? That is inherently wrong and goes against our code of conduct. Thusly, only a balance may be maintained, and never a true, crushing defeat of evil.” Another student rises, hand raised. Steele gestures grandly to her. “You said everything is relative, even as far as super-villains go. So, are you saying that super-villains are a natural reaction to the imbalance created by the presence of a superhero?” Steele’s eyes go wide and he jabs a meaty finger at her direction, startling the young girl. “HA!! Exactly! The gods above cannot abide by a world with the scales tipped too far in either direction, so when a hero comes, one can be sure to assume that villainy will soon follow. There are many, many theories on this phenomena summed up thusly… The criminal mind is ego-driven, and therefore the ego’s insatiable thirst must be slaked with conquest!! So when there is an emergence of a hero, the villains flock in desperate attempts to destroy the beacon of good, gaining recognition throughout the criminal underworld. To be the man, you have to beat the man, so to speak… Or the ancient Chinese theories of ying and yang. When the scales tilt towards good, evil MUST follow in balance, restoring the karma of the Earth… And lastly, The Legion of Doom’s notoriously efficient network of spies reports back of a hero’s arrival, thusly ensuring work for the super-villains who pay union dues. This, however is unconfirmed, as Lex Luthor’s Legion of Doom organization categorically denies its own existence.” Steele finishes with a flourish, looking down at the pondering female student. Slowly, she begins to speak. “So… basically… the reason for all super-villains is the appearance of the heroes.” Steele frowns, “Well, yes… technically…” “And the super-villains are only a natural reaction to the unchecked superhero, who begins to clean up a city of the lesser crime statistics, in order to keep this superhero occupied with a battle worthy of his time.” Big Alan Steele flounders, his face blushing crimson, as he tugs at the collar of his toga. “Er… hmmm… indeed… but… ha ha ha…” “And we wouldn’t HAVE to worry about super-villains trying to take over the world or bombing the downtowns to pieces, because it would only be the basic, HUMAN criminals running around if it weren’t for the heroes.” “Look! I, Big Alan Steele, did not create such a faulty system!” snarls our bohunkular demi-god, “Twas that damnable over-powered bastard of Krypton, Superman!! That omnipotent alien started it all! If it were not for his silly costumed person, the start and subsequent spread of super-villainy would have been stilted!! I am merely a Mount Olympus reaction to the crime and degradation of life within the California domain!” “But we wouldn’t have to worry about super-villains, right?” “But… but… I was sent to be an envoy of righteousness, cleaning up the streets of San Jose…” whimpers Big Al. “C’mon! San Jose was BEAUTIFUL before YOU showed up! You heroes have gone and ruined it for everyone else!!” Big Al stammers, “No! Tis… tis not that way!” “But you just said it!!” “But look at PCW since my arrival…” A young male stands up, pointing and shouting, “Yeah, let’s LOOK at PCW!! There are more heels than faces… heck, I can’t even NAME another face besides you! And since you became champion, there are only heel stables and heel challengers!! It’s all your fault!!” Sweat pours down our hero’s face as he tries in vain to wipe it away. He gulps and looks across the stage to Sue for help, who sits back and crosses he hands, a malicious grin on her face. She gestures with both hands, in essence saying, “Don’t look at me, joker.” The young man sneers, “I hope Chef kicks your ass Tuesday! Maybe then we’ll get some balance…” Big Al steadies himself on the podium, slamming a fist into the wood. “Allow me to re-explain…” FWA-BOOOOOOOMM!!! An explosion rocks the foundations of the arena, shaking the audience from their seats and causing Big Al to forcibly steady himself at the platform. A nervous riffle goes through the crowd as screaming can be heard outside. A woman, obviously in a state of panic, runs into the auditorium, screaming her face off. “Omigod!! Run for you lives!! A freak explosion in the science labs sent a whole ton of experimental solutions on Dr. Richard Stanisek!! He turned purple and grew HUGE!! Now he’s made himself a bunch of explosives from other lab research and he’s going across campus trying to gain revenge on the researcher, Dr. Igor Laganov, who originally sabotaged the experiment that resulted in his new form…” The woman faints from asphyxiation, given her long-winded scream. Steele laughs nervously into the mike, as the entire audience stares at him with cold, angry eyes. Big Al places his hand over the mic, and leans down to whisper to the dour-faced dean of the university. “Uhm… this wouldn’t be coming out of my honorarium, would it?” The dean frowns, “That depends on how much damage is done…” KWA-POOOOW!!! Another massive explosion shakes the arena. Steele sighs and begrudgingly hands over the sheepskin, which is quickly snatched away. Feeling the eyes upon him, he straightens up, giving a lackluster battle cry before running off to battle the evil. “Hi-Keeba… stupid Superman…”
