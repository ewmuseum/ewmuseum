Duke drinks some Coffee

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Duke drinks some Coffee Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 10/28/02 -It’s a truck stop in Billings, Montana where our Goth God sits drinking coffee on his way back to his mountain home in Missoula. Duke burnt his finger on the egg buffet only moments ago. The camera flashes on Duke who’s jet black hair is put back in pig tails and his all black clothing make him stand out in the conservative God’s country. He quickly pushes the camera to focus on the coffee.- Dukestar: Ahhh the warm black abyss that is coffee. -The camera stares at the motionless steam pool of black.- Dukestar: So scary looking yet so many addicted. -The coffee continues to be hot Java.- Dukestar: Why do you accept coffee in all its darkness and evil yet you shun your President God? I know I’m a hideous burnt Goth freak but on the inside I have feelings too. But none of that will matter cus when I win the world title you will be forced to love and respect me once again. Waitress: You need a warm up hun? Dukestar: I’m fine thank you. Waitress: Thank you dear, call me if you need anything. -Duke gets close to the camera and speaks quietly into the camera.- Dukestar: Did you hear the negative intonation in her voice? -Duke peeks to make sure she isn’t around or that nobody is listening.- Dukestar: You see the way she came to check on me last??? -Duke points the camera over to a black truck driver.- Dukestar: That African American hoss got service before I did. Now that may not be a big deal in Chicago, New York, or LA, but this is Montana!! Montana still thinks slavery is an active law!!! -Duke sips his coffee.- Dukestar: Little does she know that I am her God and can conjure up some evil spells to use against her in my lair. But this is the anti-Semitic/anti-Goth prejudice I have to put up with. It’s not easy being the leader of the Goth nation. -Duke just shakes has head as he stares at his burnt body. Or rather the tiny burn on his finger.- Dukestar: I must be strong to let all the other freaks and germs know they can stand up for themselves. I mean who needs showers, hair cuts, or Gap clothing. I’d rather be an individual like these guy and gals. -Duke places a copy of Goth Weekly Magazine on the table. As he flips through the pages you notice all the guy are dressed in the same black out fits and the girls have on pretty much the same stuff also.- Dukestar: That’s why I love being Goth. Look at all the individuality. Going against the grain as a single rebel. Not like them norms who dress all the same and act the same. Ha pity them. -Duke lets out a loud evil laugh but hushes it up when a big truck driver starts to stare at him.- Dukestar: Look at that man staring at me. He is either a homosexual attracted to my rugged good looks or he hates me because he knows I’m better then him cus I’m an Evil Goth God. -Duke stares back which is intimidating cus although he is dressed like a freak he still stands 6’3 and weighs close to 300pounds. Built like a Mack truck coming from an era when steroids were the rage and legal. Duke is a true monster even at 42 most kids in the back wish they had a body like his- Dukestar: I imagine it’s a mixture of the two. I think he is a jealous homosexual angered by my rugged good looks and power since he can have neither. -Duke points his arms at the gentleman and waves his fingers.- Dukestar: woop woop woop -The man gets up and approaches the Malice Goblin.- Truckdriver: I didn’t know it was Halloween today. Dukestar: Well duh that’s cus Halloween ain’t this Friday. Truckdriver: So you going to a party or something. Duketar: I just came back from a party with you wife. She sleeps with as many guys as Hugh Hefner does with ladies. -The tough looking yet over weight truck driver throws a punch at Duke which he catches in his had. Duke stands up.- Dukestar: Don’t you know I’m a Goth God? Please behavior yourself in my presence you over weight blue color sorta norm slob. -Duke lets the man go who quickly exits the truck stop.- Dukestar: You see my followers you need to stand up to the bullies. I didn’t instigate that. -The camera pans over his choice of apparel and loud voice.- Dukestar: I was just sitting here talking with you’ll. This is the crap that will end soon. Mexicans don’t like freaks. They make fun of them so I must make an example of El D. Snow is like a preppy prison jock he too must be taught to respect the Goths. Walden is sure to help his boss. That my friends is the Evil Goth God Honky Tonk Front Office truth. -The truck driver comes back with some friends carrying crowbars. Duke lays down some cash and high tails it out the back door….malice style.- =w= ---- View Duke Williams's Biography
