LOTA: The Old Forest

Date: ..... I still couldn’t believe it. I sat in my temporary bedroom, untucking my bed pillows to finally sleep in a soft bed after what seems like forever. I was still going through all of the events that had happened. During supper, I had finally worked up the courage to tell all my friends that I wouldn’t be staying in Brickethollow, but instead, going to find the amulet in Mordor. I had expected everyone to be shocked and kind of sad that I wouldn’t be staying to relax, but instead what they told me blew me away. They told me that they somehow knew all about my plans and wanted to come with me. They said that they had found out threw a bunch of small conversations, mainly the one I had with Gildor, the elf. At first, I didn’t know whether I should accept there gracious offer but I had no choice, AJ Pippin Palmer, Jesse Sam Ramey, and Merry Kaldorn were coming with me to help. When it was decided that they would come with me, a conversation came up on when to leave. Merry told us that the black riders that had crossed our path once already was in fact looking for us. It was planned that we would leave the next day. Some of us, specifically Sam didn’t want to leave so soon because his ‘back’ was aching but wanted to help out in any way he could. I rolled into my bed, ready to think more about the events. I hardly started when I fell asleep, dead into sleep. “Mark, wake up!” “Wha?” Who could be waking me up at this time of the morning. It was still early, wasn’t it? “Come on sleepy, it’s almost eleven o’clock and we have to be going!” Eleven o’clock, holy crap! To me, it was like eight o’clock but it was really eleven? We had said we would be leaving at eleven. I rolled around in my bed for a second. “I’m up.” I mumbled to Merry, who I could now see. Merry nodded and left the room for me to get dressed. I quickly got dressed, putting on an old shirt and a pair of pants. When I got into the kitchen, some toast and orange juice was laid out into the counter. I glanced out the window that I could see at the other end of the kitchen and saw Merry, Pippin, and Sam getting four horses ready to be used from a back shed. I picked up my pack that was in the corner and walked out of the door, chomping the last bit of toast and drinking the last of the orange juice. I met my friends. “Hey guys!” “It’s about time!” joked AJ ‘Pippin’ Palmer. He brought my pony over to me and we all got onto our ponies. “Where are we off too?” asked Sam. The truth was, nobody really knew where we were heading except too Mordor, but Mordor is way to far to get there in even a year by walking. The plan was to go to Rivendell to meet Elrond, a famous man of his times. Also, Rivendell was where I thought the missing Gandalf was. “Well, I want to go to Rivendell. It was far but the house of Elrond is there. “Ah.” said Sam. Sam knew who Elrond was so I didn’t have to explain all of Elrond’s story to him. We started trotting along, eventually down the road and out of Brickethollow. Soon thereafter, we cut off into the forest terrain and into the direction we would be heading for some time. The path we were taking was small but away from the road where the black riders might be lurking. We were following along the path when we all knew that we must turn north. Trying to turn north, it was like the trees were blocking our path. “What is happening?” asked Merry. Merry tried to turn northwards, off the path for a moment but in the right direction. The tree branches seemed to turn and block the path that Merry was taking. “We’ll just have to continue on this path.” I announced. The others all nodded in unison and continued onto the path. We had now been walking for about three hours. Every time we would try to cut north, the trees would block our ways. It was if the trees were listening in on us and our conversation. “I am getting worried sir. We are heading further and further east when we should be turning North!” said Sam. The trees seemed to growl at the words that Sam were saying. “I know but soon the path turns north and we will just follow the path, I’m sure of it.” The other three nodded like they had before but I could tell they weren’t sure. I told them that the path would bend north two hours ago and it still hadn’t. What if it didn’t the whole way through? “I need a break” Finally someone said it and it was good old Sam. “Me too. These horses looked tired.” Same old Pippin, not wanting to show his weakness so blaming it on the horse. I had to admit, I was pretty wiped myself, horse or not, I needed a break. We used the rope we had to tie the horses up to a nearby tree and stopped to sleep. A small river was ahead where we got some water to bathe in. Everybody had dozed off except me. I couldn’t get to sleep so I walked over to the river and bent over, splashing some water into my face. I soon layed back and fell asleep. I slept like a baby for who knows how long. I only woke up to a screaming noise and I felt wet....
