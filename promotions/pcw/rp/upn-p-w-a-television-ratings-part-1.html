UPN + P.W.A = Television Ratings Part 1

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26 - o9 - 02 The sun has gently set in the west and the night slowly draws in, watched closely by Nerezza who lounges about on a reclined chair out on the balcony of his partners pent house suite that overlooks the densely populated streets below. Sitting immersed, Nerezza sports his draping trench coat as he peers over the moveable snacks aimlessly going about their daily lives whilst his comrade his heard murmuring within. Capturing the Jester as he removes his emerald coloured suit jacket, he is watched closely by the camera as he slouches into the comfort of a feather white sofa. Almost falling asleep with flickers of his heavy eyes he is disturbed by the howling of the telephone prompting him to answer via the speakerphone. 'Speak to me....' gloated the Original Prankster. 'Gaz, I want you?' whispered the female voice that occupied the other end. Suddenly the Jester's hand swept over the dials and the line went dead. The once jovial face of our host turned immediately to a frown as he cowered away from the phone and with all the antics happening, it was merely a matter of time before Nerezza would be drawn with curiosity. 'Who was that?' hissed the vampire as he questioned his fellow tag champion. 'Err-... Hmm-... Well-... Her from the airport?' stuttered Gaz Green. 'Didn't I tell you to use precaution....' retorted the Angel Of Fear before an untimely interruption from his recipient. 'I DID, I MEAN I NEVER GAVE HER MY NUMBER!!!' 'Well, how do you suppose she got it?' roared the behemoth with a subtle showcase of teeth. As the jaw of Gaz Green drops ajar in preparation for his announcement the telephone sprung into life once more. With a monotone series of 'Ring - Ring' Gaz slipped back into hiding as Nerezza lunged forward pressing the speakerphone to the sweet innocent voice of a female. 'Gaz, I want you....' 'Hey lady... It was a once in a lifetime opportunity. I mean, it doesn't happen everyday and I can understand that you want a second helping but you won't be the first or last in the saga of conquests of the Jester!' groaned the goliath, Nerezza with a commanding tone to his harsh voice. 'I BEG YOUR PARDON! I was calling with concern to a television contract with regards to Pranks With Attitude?!?' replied the unknown female, yet to be unveiled or introduced. 'HUH!!!' was the response in unison from both P.W.A members. 'Well I represent the UPN network and since your arrival into Primetime Championship Wrestling the ratings have soared...' injected the woman as the eyes of the P.W.A gaped open like Animee characters. 'So has head of programming I have persuaded the board of directors to fund a television project based on PCW's newest additions and Tag Team Champions. Congratulations by the way - The show would be chat show based and during it's broadcasting you will basically have sole control, a free run!' continued the UPN spokes person increasingly interesting the devious duo. A moment of silence filtered the proceedings before Nerezza's star struck expression faded and he composed himself to answer. 'Hello - Hello - Hello....' murmured the voice once more. 'Where do we sign!!!' bellowed the Living Nightmare. 'Well we would have to iron out some of the details and there would be some paper work involved but we can cover everything tomorrow providing you haven't any current plans....' asked the inquisitive head of programming for the UPN network. 'Awesome! Nope my plans of bungee jumping can take a nose dive to the bottom of my 'To Do' list, so who should we ask for?' said the emerald menace, grinning like a Chestershire cat. 'Oh, sorry... We shall send you a limousine, say 1 o'clock and at reception you can ask for Ms. Fran Martin!' 'Well Ms. Martin we have a date... Oh, and a contract signing but one step at a time, hey tiger!' joked the Lord Of Laughter in a flirtatious manner. Gaz swept his nimble fingers over the dial again and the line faded allowing the trickster team to bask in their newfound career like children. Both individuals sprang up and down in glee, both adorning smiles and in Nerezza's case a slight tint in his pale complexion. Obviously overwhelmed by the prospect of taking their act globally they wore each other out with their child like antics, the next step in Pranks With Attitudes journey had finally been paved courtesy of the UPN network. 'I told you, first we become Champions then things will start to fall at your feet, what next movie contracts....' proclaimed Nerezza with a smug grin. 'JUST THINK OPEN HOUSE! PRANKAGE TO THE REST OF THE WRESTLE TATTLER WORLD~!' screamed the Prince Of Pranks in bated breath, his eyes still glazed over in emotion. 'For once you have a point....' yawned the Spawn Of Evil before a Gaz Green intrusion. 'Charming I'm sure?!?' 'I know you like the sound of your own voice but listen up - With this power we can entice the competition into appearing and in the words of our new boss 'A Free Run' all bets are off and Pranks With Attitude can't be held responsible, any shape or form' continued the titanic blood sucker, towering his colleague, bathing him in nothing but shadow. 'Well I'm exhausted squire, shall we call it a night?' questioned the Jester, his eyes flickering and his body limbering as he stands motionless. 'DOUBT IT. It's early and I would like to celebrate by washing down this unexpected good news with the juices of some virgin!' murmured the Asylum run-in buddy. 'There are virgins here in Las Vegas? Well I wouldn't bank on it and feeling as confident as I do, my money will be backing the lottery this week but good luck all the same, as you know, your last meal ticket required the animal magnetism of Ga-...' yelped the PCW comic as he gazed over his co-worker who stares cold-heartedly. 'YOU WERE NOTHING BUT A PAWN, A LURE, AN EXPENDIBLE PEON' replied Noscosta Ireul Nerezza. The huge frame of Nerezza concluded his speech and turned, fleeing the scene via the glass panel doors of the apartment that lead out onto the open top balcony. His 300+ mass disappeared into the night allowing the smaller comedian to capture forty winks in the comfort of his own chamber (room). - A number of hours have passed - The sinister Jester remains in the sanctuary of his bedroom asleep in his bed, murmuring under his breath, talking in his sleep, the words barely recognisable. The camera drawing closer with curiosity finally unveils the garbage spoken by the incoherent prankster. 'Err-... Let her go...' 'Hey, let... Anarchy go, huh!?!' Suddenly Nerezza breezes past the camera and strikes his comrade firmly with a clenched fist trying to wake his slumbering partner who continues to mutter under his breath, when without warning he sits erect, his eyes adjusting to the darkness. 'Wha-... Wha-... Err-... What happened?' 'YOU WERE DREAMING - MENTIONED ANARCHY... AGAIN, Is there something going on?' asked the solemn giant, licking away the excess saliva from his canines. Before any answer the image closed, leaving nothing but a black screen.
