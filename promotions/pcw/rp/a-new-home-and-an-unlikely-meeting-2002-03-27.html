A new home, and an Unlikely meeting…

27 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/03/02 A new home, and an Unlikely meeting… [[Recorded before PCW Tuesday Turmoil-27/03/02]] The scene opens up on the Face of Titu Xansiv. A confused and fairly worried expression paints itself upon our resident Egyptian Gods mug, yet the reason behind this expression is yet to be revealed…or is it? Next to him we find Horus, his mentor and his fellow Egyptian God, an immortal that as of late we have found to be quite senile and in his own right very confusing. Now that Pharaoh’s mentor is revealed to be with our hero it isn’t hard to see why Titu may look perplexed, in fact its not difficult at all! The surroundings that we find the two Gods in is that of the interior of a jumbo jet. Travelling at a great speed over open waters bellow, the two are heading towards a currently unknown destination, seemingly of great importance. Every now and again Xansiv turns to his left and looks out of the small port-window to observe the odd island or country pass bellow him. At the moment we have no idea where on earth Titu and Horus are travelling on a plane, but by the looks of things we could be about to find out. The Pharaoh: I cannot believe you convinced me to revisit this place! Horus: Titu when we get there, many old memories will be relived and old times and spirits will return to you… The Pharaoh: That may be so, mentor, but I think you’re keeping something from me! I can tell by that crazy look in your eyes. Horus grins broodingly. Horus: There may be more than one reason to why we are travelling there, but you will have to wait and see what that reason may be… Pharaoh sighs quietly. The Pharaoh: Well I am quite against leaving America this close to Friday Fusion, we have to be there in two days! Horus: That is true, but please Titu, do not worry. A silence falls over the two. Horus: I have noticed that you worry too much lately, my friend. The Pharaoh: Well when your wife has just been kidnapped then you can tell me how you feel! Snaps Pharaoh. Horus grunts in anger. Horus: She will be fine, trust me! The Pharaoh: It isn’t you that I cannot trust, master; it is the Neo Void God, Akira Akiyama. After the events that took place on Sunday Sabotage last week I simply cannot risk taking chances! Only the damned Egyptian Gods know what he’s doing to Isiy right now! And I swear down, if he hurts a simple strand of her delicate hair I’ll make sure he receives a punishment fit for such an asshole as he! Horus: Just calm yourself my friend. The sound of trolley wheels upon the Jets floor suddenly fills the sound waves. Pharaoh jumps to his feet to see an airhostess approaching with a trolley in her hands. Seemingly it is filled to its brim with liquor and alcohol. Xansiv licks his lips dreamily and before long a huge grin paints itself upon his handsome face. The Pharaoh: Over Here! He bellows. The airhostess looks up from controlling the fairly shaky contraption and smiles in Titu’s direction. Pharaoh sits back down and waits in anticipation for the woman and the alcohol-filled trolley to appear next to Horus and his seats. The Pharaoh: Fancy a drink, old friend? Horus contemplates the thought for a few seconds before reaching his answer. Horus: Ah maybe one, I’m not too accustomed to heavy drinking…but now seems a good time for a grand drink of a fine alcoholic beverage! Pharaoh grins in response to his mentor’s reaction and after a little while the female airhostess emerges next to the Gods. Without further waiting Pharaoh begins to place his order. The Pharaoh: Well I’ll have a whisky on the rocks… He pauses and turns to Horus. The Pharaoh: And what will you be having, master Horus? Horus: Erm, nothing too strong for me, I’ll just have a bacardi and coke… The woman smiles and begins to prepare the two Godly being’s drink while they talk. The Pharaoh: Alas, there is one being who seems to have been more sourly dismayed in my feud with Akiyama than any of us, Isiy included… Horus: Who? The Pharaoh: Carmoon… Horus sighs deeply and a deadly silence falls over the Gods. The Pharaoh: He truly was a loyal servant, you know. The God of the sky nods with a look of sadness about his aged brow. Horus: I had not even seen Carmoon before he was banished, I heard that he was a great immortal being of a respectable background…he will be sourly missed… that is for sure. The Pharaoh: That he will, I pray that his soul lives on and he manages to get to Duat unharmed and unburdened by your father, Osiris. Horus: I’m sure he will get in just fine. He was a good being of a good heart… The Pharaoh: And he served me, and Egypt for that matter, very well… Horus nods in agreement. Horus: His soul will live on in Duat and all the pains of his labours in the past life will be lifted, Titu; he is in a better place now… The Pharaoh: I can only hope that Akira does not perform some act of evil, as he did upon Carmoon, upon Isiy… Horus: Pharaoh, if you want to see Isiy again, then you must simply concentrate on your match at Spring Fling and your match for the TV title on Friday Fusion! Put all your anger and hate into your performance in the ring…do not let your feelings burden your training and your current endeavours… Pharaoh smiles. The Pharaoh: You are truly full of wisdom old friend, I am glad that I still have you to counsel me in times like these. The airhostess finally finishes preparing Horus and Titu’s drink and then hands the two beverages to them. Pharaoh reaches into his loincloth and produces a piece of strange gold, full of ancient Egyptian markings and letters. The Pharaoh: Thank you… The woman takes the coin reluctantly and stares down at it in wonder. Airhostess: What’s this? The Pharaoh: (Grinning) It is, my dear, a piece of gold from a time long ago; when I reigned over Ancient Egypt to be more precise. It is worth a hell of a lot of US dollars and you should feel lucky to be given it! Airhostess: I don’t know if I can take this as payment for your drinks gentlemen. Pharaoh looks up at the young woman in dismay. The Pharaoh: What ever do you mean, fair woman? Airhostess: I don’t know, sir, if I can take this as payment for those *points to the drinks in Pharaoh and Horus’ hands* drinks! The Pharaoh: But it is worth so much more than the American dollar! Surly you can take it! The airhostess frowns and then looks about for someone to give her counsel on the situation. Horus takes a sip of his bacardi and coke and then smiles. Horus: Come now…surly you can take it…it’s a precious piece of Egyptian coinage…why, you could take that to a museum and be handed a sack of American dollars if it suits your fancy…But apparently you are willing to throw away such a great opportunity for a set of worthless drinks? Lets not become too silly now, shall we? Horus’ words are amazingly convincing and soon enough the woman leaves the Gods alone again, the Egyptian coin still in her hand. Horus: Good, eh? The Pharaoh: You are a very manipulative God, Horus; I’ll give you that… Horus smirks. The two gods share a laugh and then almost simultaneously hey both raise their glasses and take a sip of their drinks. The Pharaoh: It just seems to me that Carmoon cannot be truly gone…even here on this plane, so high above the sea and the land, I seem to feel his immortal aura… Horus: Hmmm… perhaps Carmoons soul has not yet reached Duat, perhaps it never will… Pharaoh sighs as the scene begins to fade to black…Quickly we reopen up on a plane, ostensibly the one that Pharaoh and Horus are travelling on landing on a runway. The surrounding of this place are very familiar, it feels as though we have been here before, at another point in time. Rolling deserts, orange cliffs and the red-hot sun all seem too well known to us. The cameras fix now on the Jumbo jet, a set of metal steps are wheeled underneath the massive mechanical birds door and soon the planes entrance is opened to the hot climate of this unknown place. Pharaoh and Horus, along with many other people quickly pile out of the plane and are escorted off the landing site to the airport itself. The Pharaoh: Ah back home…Cairo, Egypt! Horus: (Taking a deep breath) It has been so, so long since I last came here, Titu, it certainly feels good to be back! The Pharaoh: The last time I was here, Carmoon, and I travelled into the deserts of Cairo in search of the two books… Horus: And did you find them? The Pharaoh: (Grinning) Oh yes…they’re at home in California, stored away in a safe place that nobody will ever be able to find… Hours suddenly grows quite white in colour. Noticing this odd fact the Pharaoh questions the old God about it. The Pharaoh: What in the name of Isis is wrong, Horus? Horus: I can’t believe it! It was meant to be a surprise, my friend- Horus pauses and shakes his head to and fro. Horus: (Continuing) -but I sold the mansion back in California and went about purchasing a real palace over herein Cairo, a real home fit for a true king!! A cross between anger and happiness grows on Titu’s face… The Pharaoh: Horus, that is a great feat to perform, but what of the two books! Pharaoh sighs worryingly. The Pharaoh: They are dangerous and potent weapons in the wrong hands! We must travel back to California at once! Horus: Titu, let us not go yet, you must first see the new and improved palace! It is a marvellous place I tell you! Pharaoh considers Horus’ word carefully. The Pharaoh: Do you not see the importance of getting the book of the dead and the book of life back? Horus: Of course I do, Titu, but the new house… Horus pauses… Horus: The new house!! Pharaoh sighs once again. The Pharaoh: Very well…we will go there first, but after that we must retrieve the two books! Horus: Yes, yes, come now, as you are so fond of saying ‘time is of the essence’! Pharaoh and Horus now enter into the jam packed Egyptian airport and after several strenuous minutes of manoeuvring between people the two gods manage to plough through, gaining access to the airports front entrance doors. Outside the airport a selection of small red taxis are gathered, they glimmer in the hot sunlight cast down by the radiant sun. Quickly Pharaoh approaches one and opens its door, he gets inside and Horus follows him. The Pharaoh: Now where? Horus: Leave this to me… The Pharaoh: Well I’m not going to leave it to anyone else am I? You’re the one who knows the directions to this new ‘home’! Horus gives the driver the directions to the new palace and at length the two Gods arrive outside a huge, monstrous building. It has a strange looks of a temple of some kind but its very design is complex and more homely than your normal place of worship. Pharaoh gets out of the taxi before paying the man his fee for driving the two here and then Titu gazes up at the immense structure in awe and marvel. The Pharaoh: You have done well, Horus! This place is amazing… Horus: Shall we take a look inside? The Pharaoh: I don’t know… Pharaoh looks down at the expensive looking watch strapped to his Egyptian wrist. The Pharaoh: We don’t have a lot of time to spare… Horus: Of course we do…the next plane back to Anaheim doesn’t arrive until the morning! The Pharaoh looks at the Egyptian God of the sky in puzzlement. The Pharaoh: How do you know that? Horus: Well I knew damn well that you weren’t going to check for yourself, so before we left the airport I took the liberty of asking for the next flight times to Anaheim, California, and apparently the next flight out of Cairo is tomorrow morning… Pharaoh frowns. The Pharaoh: Are you completely sure? Horus: Yes, as sure as the sky is blue… The Pharaoh: Very well…if you say so…let us go inside then… Horus leads Titu up to the massive places front door and without waning suddenly brings out a key from his long white robe. He puts it into the doors lock and twists it in an anticlockwise fashion, there is a click and then the door is effortlessly pushed open. Inside the lobby of the new palace we find sets of great cream pillars of massive size holding up the places roof. The floor is made from the finest green and blue marble, in the light from outdoors the place looks radiant and fit for a true King of Egypt. Horus: Marvellous, is it not? The Pharaoh: That it is… Suddenly Pharaoh’s moment is spoiled when a huge rat, the size of your average house-cat dives out from the dark shadows. It hisses and scowls up at the two Gods in anger and panic. The Pharaoh: You never said anything about sharing the palace! Horus is about to speak when abruptly a tall thin-necked cat of jet-black fur dives upon the rat. It toys with it a little and then kills it, snapping its germ-infested neck with ease. Pharaoh and Horus watch in awe as the cat picks up the rats dead carcass and brings it towards their Godly feet. It purrs and then drops the Rat on the ground in front of them. Horus: What on earth?! Horus is about to pick up the cat and toss it out of the palaces doors when Pharaoh stops him. The cat and Pharaoh make eye contact and at length the jewel smiles. The Pharaoh: This cat… Titu pauses. The Pharaoh: It just saved us from imminent death… Horus looks at Pharaoh sarcastically. Horus: Titu, we can only be killed if we are decapitated! This rat couldn’t have even killed a mere mortal, never mind us! Pharaoh frowns. The Pharaoh: That is not the point! This cat just tried to save us from what it saw as a possible threat…I agree this creature, the rat, could not have hurt us in any way, shape or form, but this cat, it was wise enough to show its true power and kill the vermin beast… Horus: What are you getting at? The Pharaoh: Take a good look at this feline…is it not simple to see? Horus: What? The Pharaoh grins… The Pharaoh: This cat, it is Carmoon! Horus is taken back by this strange rambling. Horus: Carmoon? How on the earth could this cat be Carmoon?! The Pharaoh: It has been gifted with the soul of the monstrous man! This cat is a strong creature, wouldn’t you agree? Horus: Well yes, I suppose it is…but that proves nothing… The Pharaoh: I have known Carmoon all my Godly and natural life, Horus, this cat is the man himself reincarnated! And I stand by that statement! Horus sighs. Horus: If you say so, my friend… Titu smirks. The Pharaoh: I do not wish to call this feline Carmoon though…what do you think a suitable name is for the little thing? Horus: I don’t know… The Egyptian God of the sky passes Pharaoh the cat in his hand and immediately the feline seems to take to the PCW superstar. Horus: I’ll allow you to decide, Titu… The Pharaoh: Very well… Horus: I still don’t think its Carmoon. The cat hisses and claws the air in the direction of Horus. The Pharaoh: But I do, and that’s all that counts! Horus: Very well! Horus begins to walk away from the Pharaoh… Horus:(Mumbling) I will not be led into madness, as you seem to have been! The Pharaoh: I heard that! Horus turns down a distant corridor as the scene begins to fade to black, the cameras concentrated on the smiling face of The Pharaoh, who is now standing next to the Carmoon the cat, apparently Carmoon reincarnated…in a cats body *sighs*…whatever will happen next in the life of Titu Xansiv?
