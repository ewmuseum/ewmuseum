Marriage

28 April 2003

Date: 04/28/03 Their antiquated marriage was seemingly perfect togetherness between the both, their love visibly like an unbreakable monolith, to display their fondness and amity they conceived a child, precisely ninth months later from being an active fetus an infant by the name of Emily was removed from her stomach. The years progressed as both her mother and father adorned her. But depression reigned them both (parents) as argumentsAnd quarrels plagued their love. Emily certainly glued it back together but it slowly deteriorated as 'she' was secretly having an affair with a wealthy neighbor by the name of David Rollins. They were in a financial depression, as he was not well educated to seek a well paying career. Their affair had expanded deeply as to driving her to force a divorce. He hadn't known about the affair and thought it was about monetary and financial problems, his mind was determined to go find a profession but nothing came in to his grip as he wasn't qualified for anything with his level of education wasn't good enough. Emily had now grown to the age of eight, and she tried to piece everything together but now it had fully collapsed as once known to be 'unbreakable'. Emily only at the age of eight was driven to a suicide attempt which left both together for a further stint at marriage but months of nursing and aiding her they returned over to the law, as they were working on being divorced. (Their financial status grew as she worked as a steady social worker.) But days, weeks, and months had gone by as their love had started to blossom once again but now he was turning in to a baleful, and abusive spouse now physically mutilating his wife after their usual debacles. She had secretly continued her affair with David Rollins but their meetings decreased because of her marriage beginning to grow once again. He now had been 'Arching an eyebrow' as she would start suddenly sneaking out to see David and not addressing it first to him. She had perjured towards him as he continuously asked on where she had traveled those past weeks she had been leaving her house unnoticed but really noticed. Emily had remained eight as the years had grown longer and longer. Their marriage had been going for eight years and towards a full decade, and a prediction from an anonymous being would've thought that their marital tenure would've ended in their adolescent years as both got married at an early age, her being 16 and him 18. One night David had called the house for her but he had nabbed the phone, as she was not present in their home. This gave him negative thoughts about her as this didn't seem to be a co-worker and even if it were to be it would be a TOTAL fib seeing that she had no partners in her profession. She came home one night being questioned by him with statements like: 'Where were you? You weren't at work were you?' And things continued but she denied all. Emily stayed out of their marriage as she grew more and more depressed but hadn't displayed it. Months passed and the affair continued now suddenly having him find out about it. HOW? She had been prolonging a phone call and had been shoving him away from any questions that he would ask. He found out about the affair and how long it had been going for. He had been ravaged by this endeavor, and didn't even bother threatening David but he just cowered through the weeks. Finally, Emily had her annual ninth birthday. All the gifts received from her relatives, having a moderately large uproarious yet jovial celebration. She had received her cake, with the nine candles and tenth for luck sticking vertically out, and for tradition she made a wish but from the usual mind thought wish she expressed it verbally and verbatim she said.. 'I wish that my mommy and daddy to get back together and stop fighting and just be happy like they used to be.' Although she tried to assuage her parents life they had made it worse as her mom had become reckless and started revealing her love affair with David. He just serenely conspired a plan and one-day making it lethal. He had obtained a knife; much less a long blade with a wooden made handle from his kitchen counter. She now had told Emily about David, which made her speak about her birthday wish. David was invited over for lunch but had been late because of job-related reasons. He serenely bantered with her knowing the invitation set out to David and knowing their divorce was now set to be final and official in another agonizing week but as he blandished her mind. He has slid the blade in to his tux pocket that he had been wearing ever since they had gotten married. He struck and impaled the blade straight in to her stomach, causing a struggle as she continued to motion with vigorous blows and movements but hurting from the stab. She had died in her room as he just left her there, decaying. David had came that day but wasn't allowed in and till' this day he has yet to appear in HIS life. He had told Emily that her mother committed suicide just like she tried but this time did it for love. He put it in a way that would make her less emotional still bringing out the reactions. They had moved away from where they had lived which was Bainbridge, Georgia and every house looked as it should've been coveted by many but they were just imaged like that. This was their marriage, which expanded for a rough decade. Who were they? ... ... Stone and Stephanie ... ... The Burns' And that was their marriage.
