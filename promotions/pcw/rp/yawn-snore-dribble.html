Yawn...Snore...Dribble

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/24th March The Chris Chase Continued Chronicles – Book One: In the Council of the Immortals… RP#1 for Sabotage… Location: Larry’s Discount Store, 13th Street, Albequrque, New Mexico. Time: Approximately 12:00pm on Saturday 23rd March 2002. Weather: Relatively Dry. Points worthy Note: Rampaging hordes of Discount shoppers and Edward the Strolling Minstrel. Musical Guidance: ‘Today’ by Smashing Pumpkins. Chapter Two: Yawn…Snore…Dribble… “Aha! I found you, you elusive little scamp. Excuse me, Excuse me…err…Mister…Pete. How much are you willing to take for this beautiful little piece of musical mastery?” “Duh, didn’t you read the sign, you moron? It’s 75% off…ergo, that means that the price is now one quarter that of the original. Hence, since that used to cost four dollars…it now costs just one dollar. What do they teach you kids in school nowadays?” “I wouldn’t know…my father used to say that school was for idiots, so I didn’t go…” “Rich kid huh? Meh, it’s obvious that you don’t really have much experience with the down and homely folk, like us discount salesmen.” “Oh come on…I’ve been to flea markets, I’ve wrestled midgets, I’ve been chased by hordes of mexicans…I’ve dragged cardboard cutouts to the least desirable of places. I’ve got a hell of a lot more experience with the dropouts in society than some Middle Class Canadian wrestler…” “*Yawn…Snore…Dribble*” ::.~(Voice of Chris Chase)~ For as long as I can remember…I’ve hated everything that bores me, I mean, who wouldn’t? Those daytime talk shows that discuss politics, the judicial system…Chris Sloboda. Boredom accounts for 75% of accidents, rendering the victim tired and fed up…causing them to lapse into a comatose state from which they’ll never return. That, saddeningly enough, doesn’t apply to the likes of yours truly. I have to take in the most tedious elements of society without a moment’s notice…due to the fact that either I’ve got a relatively long attention span, or I’m more of a gullible idiot than I first made out to be. To call it a virtue of mine is perhaps throwing myself at the mercy of nimrods like Boda, who’ll probably then go on to make Homo-erotic jibes, just because they were spurned by Tim Walden for crying out loud… ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase, Number One contender for the PCW World Heavyweight Title – It has a ring to it, don’t you think? I’ve waited months for the time when I’ll finally prove all those cretinous critics wrong, that I’ll prove that my victory over the jackass himself was not a fluke. My story is a long and involved one…I’ve seen off many a wrestler on my path to the ultimate heights of PCW fame. The likes of Xavier Kannon and Miyagawa have succumbed to the entire brilliance of ‘The Messiah’. I can brag, for once…about things that aren’t fictional…about things that cannot be proven outside of medical science. And now, the only thing that stands between me and the ultimate prize of the PCW World Championship is an old foe…Boda-Boy! Everything outside of this feud pales in significance to the future of this company, the future of professional wrestling as you know it. It all pales when compared to the likes of ‘The Messiah’ Chris Chase… It’s been a week since I last appeared on a promotion, typically enough I’ve been bogged down with enough work to basically rip the life out of me. And when you consider that I’ve been thrown through a table and have been bounced a couple of times in the last week, then it’s not surprising that I’ve been at a loss as to creating enough time to produce these friggin’ things. However…picture a discount store, a mecca for single mothers and for smelly hicks who can’t afford the higher-tier merchandise. Then picture Chris Chase, Dustin Downey, a Strolling Minstrel and about a thousand shoppers…and you have the scene presented in front of you. It’s a warm early Spring day, the birds are out in force…and I’m probably more fed up than usual. I sigh as Dustin bargains with a homely, bad-tempered old man over a Bob Dylan 8-Track….:: Old Man: Alright, so are you going to pay for this or what? A dollar…and that’s as far as I go before I ask anyone else if they want to buy this. Come on kid, I haven’t got ALL day. Dustin: ~(Searching through his pocket)~ Would you be prepared to take ninety-six cents instead? Chris: ~(Sighs)~ … ::. Dustin scrambles frantically through the pockets of his ripped combat jeans, the pair that were mutilated after the encounter with the giant Demon Chicken, as the elderly sales person stares down at him with an impatient frown. Again, I sigh to myself…in exasperation more than anything, sitting on a bench beside some lunatic dressed in middle age dress. Drifting away from this pedestrian scene in my thoughts…I reminisce at the time when I obliterated Boda that time when he managed to beat me for the first and ONLY time. Up until the very point when Dustin decided he’d make things difficult for me, I was pummeling Boda into the ground…wiping the ring clean with his own blonde crop. Then, Boda was nothing more than some angry former PCW2 wrestler who needed Tim Walden and Dustin Downey to get the cheapest of victories. And let’s face it…he hasn’t changed one bit since. He still makes the same idle threats that he did almost a year ago, he worries over the very fact that I’m here to pluck that title away from his Czech grasp. At the very end of the day, Boda versus Chris Chase is always going to be a predictable battle, with the merits going to Chris Chase almost every single time. It happened in PCW2, it almost happened in November…and it’s sure to happen now. Only time will tell as to when it’ll happen again. But it’ll be soon. Little more than five minutes have passed, and Dustin continues to barter with the old salesman, fumbling with his loose change as he argues and rants over the worth of the Bob Dylan 8-Track. Around him, there is much other arguing and ranting going on between the other salespersons and the customers in the store, the numbers of which appear to be in the near hundreds. Ah, the pedestrian life of the dropouts in society…so this is what it’s like to be a member of the Sloboda family, which probably numbers in the thousand region. There is a faint wiff of Nyquil as I sit…impatiently with my head propped up on my hand….:: Chris: Is this going to take much longer? Heck, I’ve yet to see a banter go on for over 10 hours, and with the rate that this is going…I’ll probably see it today. Why can’t you cretins just stick at a dollar and leave it be? ::. With a moronic grin, Dustin turns his attention away from the salesman…who sighs angrily as 82 cents is shoved in his face. Dustin glances down at me, and frowns…perhaps suspecting my cynicism…or perhaps he spotted some rare Bocephus 8-Track that he doesn’t yet own….:: Dustin: It’s the thrill of the hunt Chris, bantering isn’t all about chatting and distracting the salesmen. It’s about getting the merchandise at the lowest possible price. Now, I’m going to get this 8-Track at 82 cents, or else! Chris: Can’t you just find one of those in the nearest trash depository? I’m sure Boda would have plenty of those knocking around. Dustin: Again with the Boda thing? Chris, call me an idiot or whatnot, but aren’t you just getting a little obsessive with this whole beating Boda thing. Heck, if it wasn’t for me…you’d be well on your way to winning that argument with ease… ::.Sadly enough, I nod. For once, Dustin is more right than he could ever be in the space of a year. It WAS all his fault that my bragging rights against Boda are just a little impaired…after all, it was his doing that caused me to lose to Boda-Boy all those weeks ago, back when the Wild Deuces actually existed, and New Breed had double its members. Staggered by Dustin’s sudden tendency to come up with something correct for once in his life, I spring up from my near comatose position on the bench, smirking arrogantly as Dustin rounds off his purchase in front of yours truly….:: Old Man: Alright…85 cents, and that’s my final offer, kid. I hope you realise that all 36 of my children are going to go hungry tonight… ::. Dustin nods slowly…gradually taking in the information passed on by the older man….:: Dustin: Deal…now hand over that 8-Track before I have to resort to leaping over this sales desk and beat some home-tutored mathematics into you… Old Man: Oh sure…like that’s the most initimidating threat on the planet. Look…I’m Dustin Downey, and I’m Wrestling’s biggest idiot. Cruiserweights never were so freakin’ annoying. Now, take this and scram! ::. The Salesman hands Dustin the 8-Track reluctantly…before turning to deal with yet another customer. With a pleased, yet wholesomely irritating grin on his face. With an immense swarm of customers flocking around the old salesman, he looks back and chortles nonchalently…before slotting the 8-track into his jacket pocket. With a tweak of my leather jacket, I pull myself up from my seat…stretching with a loud yawn. I twinge at the hurt eminating from my back…after two painful moves in a week, it’s not surprising that I’m hurting, if just a little. No one is perfect you know, everyone gets hurt at one point or another, whether it’s mental or physical hurt. The piercing sunlight from the noon sun pierces through the top of the skylight, causing me to squint in its light, as I stagger past queues of people…making my own way towards the exit… Albequrque, New Mexico seems to be pretty empty at this time of day, what, with a large percentage of people enveloped in the interior of a huge discount store. The empty street ahead of us stretches out quite a distance…and being the arrogant toss that I usually am, I swing my arms out wide as I swagger my way out onto the street, spinning on the spot to the disgust of my former Tag Partner, and a strolling Minstrel that won’t quit following the two of us around the place. This place provides the backdrop to my first clear-cut shot at the PCW World Heavyweight Title, for which I can get a second shot at in the small chance that I might possibly fail on Sunday Sabotage…tomorrow night….:: Edward: Thou shalt really not embarrass yourself in public Chase, prancing around like some strolling minstrel isn’t going to get you approval ratings… Dustin: The Strolling Minstrel is right Chris, you can’t just strut around…looking like something out of the Early 1990s…you’ve got to push the letter on this one…you’ve got to beat Boda like you’ve only beat him in PCW2 up until now… ::. Edward nods to Dustin’s notion, which really ended up being a variation of that from the Strolling Minstrel himself. I look at the two with a piercing stare, a stare fit only for a pair of idiots that question the creative flair and brilliance of the Celestial Cowboy. Only Boda would be fool enough to do that, to underestimate his opponent by the mere sense of debasing everything that said opponent has under-achieved. If I’ll admit to one thing, it’s that I’m an under-achiever. However, with the titanic proportioned impact that I’ve made in the last month…that’s all to change. To cut to the Chase in every way possible, it’s simply a matter of mind over Boda…my superior intellect and technical knowhow is bound to put that over-sized behemoth back in his place. The Meanstreak of PCW2? Pah…he’s barely even worth giving a job to him at Chuck E. Cheese….:: Chris: ~(Stopping Almost Immediately)~ Oh please…like I’m really going to do a downright idiotic thing like that… Dustin: Well…you were just a minute ago. You spun around like a Shawn Michaels clone…possibly embarrassing yourself in front of Close Circuit Surveillance cameras. ARE YOU INSANE? ARE YOU ME? NOOOOOOOO! ::.Of course, I’m entitled to slap myself on the forehead at this point….:: Chris: For MY sake Dustin…will you be quiet whilst I think of a better way to mock Boda? ::. To be Continued…MWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA-HAHA-HA-HA! Fade to White!.::
