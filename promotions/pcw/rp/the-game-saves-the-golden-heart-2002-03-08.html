The Game Saves The Golden Heart?

8 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-8-02 {TRANSMISSION BEGIN} {The scene opens up with Mandy and Juliet inside Kevin’s blue Dodge truck driving down the express way. As Mandy turns on the radio, Juliet puts on some make up using her mirror. Gracefully, Juliet puts on her make up. Mandy checking her eyeliner cautiously looks over to the road.} Mandy: Juliet what’s wrong? Juliet: Well remember when we talked about me and Juan breaking up after this whole going to jail thing happened. Mandy: Yeah so were you two getting back together or what? Juliet: That’s the thing, we’re talking and sleeping with each other just like old times. But we aren’t back together. What should I do? {Before Mandy gets a chance to reply the scene shifts over to Juan and Kevin sitting inside the hotel talking.} {With little light emitting from the lamp, Juan is shown with a slight mid day’s shadow around his mouth. With Juan sitting on the bed and Kevin getting ready to leave for a night on the town with Juan, they begin to have a conversation.} Juan: So Kev, who you fightin’ Sunday? Kevin: Damian Xavier, what about you? Juan: Remember our days in CoJ? Kevin: Yeah what about them? Juan: Well remember Big Allan Steele? Kevin: The guy who thinks he’s a God who lives on Mt. Olympus? Juan: Yup that’s him. {Kevin answers in a shocked tone.} Kevin: You fightin that guy? Juan: Yeah that’s what it’s goin down as. Kevin: Well then who in the hell is getting our tag title shot? Juan: I think its Frisco and Legend Inc. {Kevin getting infuriated begins to count.} Kevin: Diez, nueve, ocho, siete, seis, cinco, cuatro, tres, dos, uno. All right, what do we have planned for The Divinity? ‘Cause everyone knows that they are no way near the status of The Golden Game. Juan: Exactly, but we have to take things one step at a time. And besides our plan will take a lot of time to master. {Kevin finally puts on a black silk shirt with yellow flames all around. Coming through the flames is a dragon that is lit up by the light of the fire. As he walks to the mirror and primps Juan just grabs a bottle of cologne and puts it on. Kevin noticing that it’s not that bad of an idea grabs a bottle of Avatar and sprays it on.} Kevin: So dude what’s goin on wit’chu and Jewls? Juan: Well we’re getting along great but things are still iffy between us. But one thing is for sure… {Just at that moment the screen splits as Juliet and Juan are both sitting there saying the same thing at the same time.} Juliet and Juan: I still love him/her and I don’t know what to do. {At that moment the scene shifts back over to Juliet and Mandy as they open the truck’s doors and go to Déjà Vu. Not being carded at the door the big bouncer let’s them get in free of charge just this once.} {They walk over and take a seat near the runway and table. As they wait for anything to happen they continue to talk.} Mandy: So why don’t you two get back together. I know that Juan is still really wild about you. When Juan and I have time to talk we do and all he talks about is you. He’s really in love Jewl; he’s in love with you. Juliet: So you’re saying we should make things official? Mandy: All I’m sayin is look back at why you two broke up and how you felt and look at what’s going on today. THEN YOU can make a decision Juliet: I know I love him and I want everything to be like it was back then. Mandy: Hey when Kevin proposed to me he wrote something for me. Later I found out he took it out of some book but it was so beautiful. Just listen to Loves Philosophy The fountains mingle with the riverAnd the rivers with the ocean;The winds of heaven mix forever,With a sweet emotion;Nothing in the world is single;All things by a law divineIn one another’s being mingle:-Why not I with thine?See! The mountains kiss high heavenAnd the waves clasp one another; Juliet: No sister flower would be forgivenIf it disdained its brother;And the sunlight clasps the earth,And the moon beams kiss the sea!-What are all these kissings worth,If thou kiss not me? It is a very lovely piece by Percy Bysshe Shelley that I’ve read many times. Another one that I absolutely love is Para Cada Vida by a young author Kevin Southern. Just listen: Para Cada Vida Para cada vidaHay muertePara cada corazonHay un amorPero cosasPuede cambiar.Si para un amor,Hay muerte?Si para cada corazon,Hay vida?Las estrellas en el cieloMe lleva a mi corazon.Donde mi vida estaEn el fin.Sin amor, sin cambioViviendo en miedoDe rechazo.En todo mi vidaYo nunca tuve un amor,Yo creo que yo tuve nada.AdiosTodos,Adios {Mandy looks a bit confused.} Juliet: It’s in Spanish I assumed that since Kevin is Spanish you might know some too. Mandy: Well I do know some, just enough to get around. Juliet: Well I’ll translate it for you. For Every Life For every lifeThere’s deathFor every heartThere’s a loveBut thingsAre able to changeIf for a love,There’s death?If for every heart,There’s life?The stars in the skyTake me to my heart.Where my life isIn the end.Without love, without changeLiving in fearOf rejection.In all my lifeI never had a love.I believe that I had nothingGood byeAll,Good bye {Juliet looks on at Mandy realizing what she must do. Mandy grabs the keys and motions for Juliet to follow.} Mandy: I understand Jewls let’s go so you can make everything right. Juliet: Thank you this really means a lot for me. {They leave the strip club and make their way to the car. The scene pans in on them getting into the truck. Zooming in on the truck leaving the parking lot the scene fades to black. It looks like The Golden Heart is getting his love back. Juan as they say in those beer commercials “This ones for you”. Good luck partner.} {END TRANSMISSION}
