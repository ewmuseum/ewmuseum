The Undiscovered Truth (Part 2)

12 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/12/02 The Undiscovered Truth Part 2 {Blowing wind sends whistles through the half open window until it’s slammed down with force.} The female doctor who x-rayed Romantico back at the hospital is now in the comfort on her own home. A blanket is wrapped tightly around her and she is sitting Indian styled in front of the fireplace. Everything would be nice other than the hundreds of questions she has inside her head. Then comes the scientific down low she’s recorded in the last few days from these x-rays taken. In front of her is Romantico’s x-ray’s, about thirty copies all in different sizes and lighting. She stares at them fixed on finding out what this could be. Was he human? Were his bones made of some type of alternate substance? Was he a product of a human clone? She couldn’t comprehend what these pictures meant. Romantico’s muscle tissues were made of vast thickening fluids. These fluids affected his emotional flow, not letting his mind decide on one thought at a time. These fluids acted as a depressant, or more of a drug within his body. He’s become so immune to it that it never bothered him or never realized it. This type of substance is unknown to man, but it is very fragile. The alternative would be to take Romantico in for testing, where it could cause to be fatal. Usually when people are tested on like this, it turns into a government issue, and the government may spare your body for the scientific cause. Isabella, who is the doctor that took these initial x-rays wants in no way to see the harm of Romantico, so she decides to keep it to herself eventhough it’s troubling her to what the problem with this man is. Her first instinct is get closer to him and learn more. Her second is that this man may be very dangerous. Either way, she doesn’t know if Romantico has realized this within himself yet. Chances are he goes on living with this without ever knowing he had it. It’s hard to trace and since Romantico never liked going to hospitals, it could have gone undiscovered his whole life. The Problem: This fluid in his body and muscles move at a very high speed and when irritated it could combust. This would make for such a surge on energy, that Romantico would die on the site. The Background: Isabella has been able to big up some interesting facts on Mr. Romantico and realizes that this suspicious lifestyle may have something to do with his physical disorder. El Romantico was a nickname given to who is legally listed as R. Borro. He was given this in the premiere underground fighting alliance in Spain. He’s kept with the name and doesn’t go by any other. Although when he makes purchases he is charged with the name R. Borro. His mother is not on any legal records, which is very odd seeing as she must have signed for something having to do with Romantico during her lifetime. Romantico’s father in listed as none. As she looks down the line she sees schools he’s attended and boarding houses he’s stayed in, but all have failed to keep him on record. This leaves Isabella with one way to gain the information she needs. By talking to El Romantico herself. The Records: The police records are clean. Romantico has never been put down for any heavy crimes in his lifetime. He has though been associated with several gang related crimes and assaults where he was never convicted or penalized. In all cases, there was not enough evidence to get him behind bars. His career: Romantico’s career is one that shows to be quite impressive. At one point Romantico was the only undefeated fighter in the Spanish fighting circuit. He gained a mass of fans, and woman from all around the world would be faint at his mere sight. Managers made millions of him, but he never seemed to get his fair share. He then went over to the United States to pursue wrestling, and has already grabbed the attention of many people. His style was adjusted, but he was back to the old flow of doing things. That was the information she was dealt, and now all she needed was some final testing, for the still missing factors she didn’t quite find information on. That night she put away her fire and went to sleep with paranoia over the issue. With little sleep and in a weak stage she woke up the next morning and got ready for work. She grabbed her things and was off. At work she used every free minute she had to run tests and study up on the substance found in El Romantico. She worked all into the night without a second to eat or rest, and eventually just collapsed from exhaustion and fell asleep. A few minutes later an associate of hers, walked in and saw her on the ground, in her hand he spotted the x-rays of El Romantico and was extremely surprised to see such odd photos. He knew these were abnormal, and in a cunning and tricky manner he grabbed the x-rays wondering what type of man would have such bones and tissue. And then he was off, with the x-rays that could make El Romantico’s life a living hell. TO BE CONTINUED………..
