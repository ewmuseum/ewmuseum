An Old Teammate & Superhero Blues

13 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.13.02 “Yo dawg….who you think the most tightest superhero is? We are greeted with those insightful words from Ray Ray, as the scene begins to fade in from black. His voice seems more thoughtful than usual, but we’re not talking about a man who rivals Einstein’s genius here. “Listen…I hate superheroes. Every single one of them. Ah yes, and that would be our lovely protagonist, Rush Mason. The scene finally fades in totally, and we can see Rush, sitting inside his own apartment on a large leather couch. Ray Ray is sitting across the room, mesmerized by the TV that’s on, showing some sort of terrible old Japanese cartoon with a cheesy looking hero. Rush: How the hell did you even get here? I closed the door before you could get in, and I’m pretty sure you’re not flexible enough to get through a window. Ray Ray looks up for a moment, clearly confused by the question. He responds, however, in a way that isn’t exactly suspected. Ray Ray: Yo, I always thought Mighty Mice was ill like whoa. Rush shakes his head as his large manager turns his head back to the screen. Rush slowly mutters to himself. Rush: Teaming with Kannon again…. Rush looks nervous as he fiddles with his hands, apparently deep in thought about yet again teaming with Kannon, though the roles are much reversed now. However, disturbing the thought process is a brilliant observation from Ray Ray. Ray Ray: Dawg…why you teamin’ with that hater? Yo….he’s crazy fo’ sho. Again, Rush sighs and shakes his head, turning to the uncomfortably stereotypical black man. He is met with a blank stare, as Rush attempts to point out something quite logical to the 300+ pound black man sitting across from him. Rush: Ray Ray….I’m not quite sure if your brain has the capacity to comprehend this, because obviously common sense didn’t make its way in there…. Ray Ray does nothing to disprove this, as he continues to stare blankly at Rush, who just continues to talk. Rush: but….whether I like Kannon or not….whether I can stand to hear the screeching voice of that blonde bimbo….whether or not I might have accused him and Nelson of being gay together, I’ve got to team with him. Ray Ray scratches his head in what a normal person would display with a sneer: disdain. The huge man speaks to himself softly for a moment, doubting the intentions of Rush’s newest and oldest tag partner. Ray Ray: I ain’t sure…that mofo might be lookin’ to….ya know….bust a cap, fo’ sho. Rush looks up, amazement in his eyes as he looks over to his quite large associate. Rush: You were thrown down stairs as a child, weren’t you? Ray Ray just responds with a shrug, but then continues to stare at Rush, who just looks at him as if to say, “What?” Rush: The hell do you want? Is there a Whopper on my forehead or something? Ray Ray, however misses the joke, but does reply with what is on his mind, maintaining a serious tone. Ray Ray: No seriously dawg….who you think the most tightest superhero is? You down with He-Man and that bullshit? Rush blinks several times, and smacks his forehead lightly, cursing himself for picking up such a buffoon as a manager. He then replies in a condescending tone as he sounds almost scolding to Ray Ray. Rush: If you insist on using the primitive form of speech known as Ebonics, then at least don’t use a double negative. It would be, “Who is the tightest superhero?”….if I were a Neanderthal like you. Never one to miss the point being made, Ray Ray quickly shoots back his own thoughts in an uproar, seeming more alive then he ever has in his usual near comatose state. Ray Ray: Double Nega….Neta….Fuck Double….Bullshit! Rush chuckles to himself under his breath at the huge man’s outrage. Then, Rush brings his head up, and finally responds to Ray Ray’s question. Rush: Heroes never mattered….they were made by the villains that they faced. For example, we made Drake Maxwell and Christopher Knight….now do you see who is in the upper echelon of PCW? Ray Ray turns back, and Rush nods to Ray Ray, who puts on a confused face, and scrunches his face up after, looking straight at the Voodoo Villain. Ray Ray: Dawg….that shit is crazy like….whoa.Rush sighs, and picks himself up, dusting his shoulders off, and calling off to Ray Ray as he begins to leave and the scene fades out. “C’mon Ray Ray, we’ve got a knight to obliterate. “Dawg…when you gonna get a cool sword like that Pendragon homie?” *sigh*
