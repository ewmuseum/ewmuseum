The Men In Very Very Dark Grey.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23/07/02 (#3 for PPV) Not a lot is known about the inner workings of Scientology... On the surface they have the recogni sable faces. The actors, the musicians, and in Xavier Kannon, a wrestler... But who rally knows how it works? What is it that the shredded documents and raids on Government offices hide? One person who knows most and is about to find out more is Kannon himself, now the mayor of Hubbardville after his rival Giovanni Ribisi fell out of the L. Ron Space Needle to his death... With this new found position of power will come new knowledge... It is just up to Kannon whether he can avoid being distracted so close to something he wants even more than Power and Knowledge... ... The PCW World Title. After his acts at Fusion, and in Hubbardville he is on a roll bioth in and out of PCW... But will it last through until this coming Sunday? “LET ME SEE YA NOD YA HEAD....” “THE VERY VERY DARK GREY SUITS ARE COMIN’!” Our shot opens up in a plain White room.. No furniture, just 2 White staircases leading onto another White balcony. Standing in this room, nodding their heads to the sounds of the funk-etched backing track are famous Scientologists Isaac Hayes and Jenna Elfman. Isaac : “LET ME SEE YA NOD YA HEAD....” Jenna : “THE VERY VERY DARK GREY SUITS ARE COMIN’!” Both of them are dressed in very, very dark Grey suits with White shirts underneath and matching very, very dark Grey ties.... Their shoes and sunglasses just not quite Black enough to be Black... ... They keep nodding to the catchy beat as a Hari Krishna dances onto the scene from each side of the screen, their heads nodding in the same beat and time. Isaac : “LET ME SEE YA NOD YA HEAD....” Jenna : “THE VERY VERY DARK GREY SUITS ARE COMIN’!” The Hari Krishnas can be seen mouthing the words behind them as 2 Rabbis appear on the balcony above, dancing away and nodding their heads... All 6 in perfect sync. Isaac & Jenna : “LET ME SEE YA NOD YA HEAD....” Hari Krishnas & Rabbis : “THE VERY VERY DARK GREY SUITS ARE COMIN’!” From the right hand side of the screen 2 Catholic priests come strutting into view as from the left, doing the moonwalk come 2 Muslim Clerics.... ... All 10 of them form 2 rows.. Isaac and Jenna flanks by the 2 Orange robe clad Hari Krishnas forming the front, and the Catholics, Jews and Muslims forming the back rank... They nod to the left.. They nod to front.... They nod to the right.... ... All nodding their heads and singing. “LET ME SEE YA NOD YA HEAD....” “THE VERY VERY DARK GREY SUITS ARE COMIN’!” “LET ME SEE YA NOD YA HEAD....” “THE VERY VERY DARK GREY SUITS ARE COMIN’!” Uppruptly the backing track stops just as they all nod centre and Isaac pulls his very, very dark Grey sunglasses down to show his eyes and whips what appears to be an E-Meter... A Scientology devised device used to measure the reactive mind, the tip of the E-Meter flashes a blinding White that drenches the entire scene in White. “Well, now you are Mayor of Hubbardville...” The White starts to fade out and we are shown a White room, similar to the one seen earlier only this one is abuzz with activity... A huge wall of video monitors, 3 dimensional holographic map grids and numerous computer terminals manned by workers all in White... Amidst the uniform colour scheme, only a few people stand out... Men and women in very, very dark Grey suits. Walking into the room we are shown PCW superstar Xavier Kannon, his wife Eleanor Hall, and Hollywood’s top spokesperson for Scientology John Travolta who had flew back to his spiritual home of Hubbardville for the swearing in ceremony of Xavier Kannon as Hubbardville’s Mayor after his rival Giovanni Ribisi had a nasty “Fall”. John : “.... It is only right you know what else goes on beneath it.” The 1st couple of Scientology need a moment to take it all in, looking around in a mixture of shock and awe at the surroundings.. Cameras showing every corner of the city above. “Good to see you are finally in on it...” To meet them, none other than the star of Vanilla Sky and Minority Report, Tom Cruise walks across to meet the group, like Travolta he is dressed in a very, very dark Grey suit. The short in stature actor then gestures to the room that hosts them. Tom : “This is the home of the “Men in Very, Very Dark Grey” you always hear about... They are very real and so is what they do... And as Mayor, they are now under your control.” Tom shakes Xavier’s hand in a gesture to symbolise the passing of command.. Ellie~!, as usual, looks a little confused. Eleanor : “Er... Like, WHAT is a man in very, very dark Grey? Is it like a MIB who’s suit faded?” All 3 look to the Prep who just shrugs their glances off, not knowing what was wrong with her question... Kannon puts his arm around her and points to the monitors around them. Xavier : “No my little Fuck Ferret... A MIVVDG is one of Hubbardville’s secret operatives. Their job is to keep outsiders out of our city....” She opens her eyes wide, gesturing that she understands with a nod. Eleanor : “Like aliens?” Xavier : “No.... Like Hari Krishnas... Jews.... Muslims.... Christians, all the unwelcome people in this city.” They all look to her again, seeing if she understands... An act that makes her rather nervous as she slowly nods her head and looks at them oddly. Eleanor : “Ahh.. O.......K.....” Then, suddenly, an alarm sound goes off all around the room, sending it’s inhabitants bursting into action, fulfilling their individual duties as Travolta and Cruise pull their very, very dark Grey sunglasses from their very, very Grey suit’s breast pocked and put them on. Travolta : “Agent L will look after you...” The 2 high profile Hollywood actors then run through the rounded door that slides open before them with a hiss as none other than Hollywood actress Juliet Lewis, star of From Dusk ‘Till Dawn and The Way Of The Gun walks over to them, wearing the standard MIVVDG attire. Juliet : “Hello Mayor, Ellie...” Eleanor : “I’m Mayor Ellie~!?” With a hiss and a whirring noise, a flat screen monitor lowers from the ceiling and stops right in front of the MIVVDG, the wrestler and the Prep which is showing the parking lot of the secret bunker where a very, very dark Grey sports car is shown starting up before something ignites behind it and in the blink of an eye it shoots off. Juliet : “We picked up an unwelcome in South East Hubbardville, using our secret network of underground roads Agents T and C will be there in about 26 seconds.... I suppose this has all come as a big shock to you both?” Xavier : “Not really, I once took a wrong turning on the way to the Biological Weapons of Doom~! labs and caught a glimpse of this place.” Eleanor : “And I just believe what I am told.” The actress and MIVVDG looks a little embarrassed as the camera now zooms in on the monitor to show the Black car speeding along the wall of one of the underground access roads below Hubbardville... A city with so much under it it’s a wonder how it doesn’t collapse. *SCREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEECH!!!!!!!!!!!!!* Suddenly the camera cuts to the surface accompanied by the sound of a speeding car taking a corner at high speed. We are in front of “FRANK’S JOSTICK EMPORIUM” in the South East of Hubbardville, just of Battlefieldeathdidnotsuck Street and Runningoutoffunnynames Boulevard. “This is the place...” Panning to our left we see the very, very dark Grey sports car, and departing it the very, very dark Grey suit wearing John Travolta and Tom Cruise. Tom : “Let’s do this...” As they once again make an exhibition of putting their not quite Black sunglasses on, the all familiar song can be heard playing in the back ground and the 2 A-List actors start to nod their heads rhythmically. “LET ME SEE YA NOD YA HEAD....” “THE VERY VERY DARK GREY SUITS ARE COMIN’!” As the funk beat continues we cut to the inside of the shop where a handful of customers are shown browsing he scented goods... *WHAM* ...They leap in alarm as the 2 MIVVDG burst in through the door and the owner, behind the counter makes a run for it... John : “Oh no you don’t...” The portly follower of Hubbard takes a few long strides forward and tackles the seemingly normal man to the floor, handcuffing him and yanking the man up to his feet... ...Nothing seems off about him. He has smartly cut Brown hair and wears a pinstripe suit... He almost looks TOO normal..... John then grabs his hair and pull it to show that it is merely a wig and that he has a bald head underneath. The customers look on in shock as Travolta then pulls the man’s suit of to show he is wearing Orange robes underneath it and the customers either scream or gasp in shock... The mumbling then starts. “My Hubbard.. It’s a Hari Krishna...” “I KNEW they were really here...” “These guys must be the MIVVDG!” John Travolta ushers the Hari Krishna out of the shop as the customers all look at Tom, disbelief in their eyes... Tom : “Wanna see my E-Meter that looks like Xenu?” From his pocket he pulls the strange Scientological devise and as 1, all the customers flock around looking closely at the device that then flashes and fills the screen with blinding White light. “Huh? What am I doing here?” “I don’t remember anything?” As the White dissipates out, we return to the secret underground home of the MIVVDG where Xavier and Ellie~! like what they have seen. Eleanor : “Like...... WAY COOL!!!” As the employees high 5 one another at a successful catch, Kannon stares at the sight around him.. The cogs in his brain almost being heard turning over as out shot starts to fade to Black on his calculating Green eyes. Xavier : “Think we can add Egyptians and Greeks to the unwelcome list?” As Kannon’s face breaks into it’s usual, sly grin we hit Black and the familiar funk-esque backing track kicks back in. “LET ME SEE YA NOD YA HEAD....” “THE VERY VERY DARK GREY SUITS ARE COMIN’!”
