The Saves the Day Saga: Sell My Old Clothes, I'm Off To Heaven

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 7th, 2002 Recap: The majority of the worlds Gods and Ideas have dissapeared due to Fate losing a game of chess to Death. Because of this whole in the Knowledge of the world, everythings gone wacky, people have stopped dieing, noone is worshiping, the world is falling apart. Of course, however, DarkStar has stayed, due to the fact he is mostly mortal. He has finally decided to go try and solve this problem. -------------- 'Talena, you coming?' DarkStar looked at the half assleep girl as she stood at the top of the stairs in their converted church of a house. She rubbed the sleep out of her eyes, clutching her doll to stomach with the other hand. DarkStar couldn't help but smile. Talena: Where are you going, lover? He sighed. He really hated her second sight and all, but she was always so much smarter when she used it. She was still a very intelligent woman... Just insane. DarkStar: To see if God is still around... this whole thing is really annoying me, I can barely sleep without having to go out and do some sort of celestial cleanup. She nodded and ran her hand through her hair to get it into place. Talena: I'll come. I cant stand being away from you any longer. She skipped down the stairs, and they were on their way. ------------------------- You see, you can't really take a car ride to heaven. Well, technically you can, but you would have to be crashing it into something unmoving to accomplish it. Its rather a difficult think to explain. Its basicly relying upon DarkStar's ability to enter his realm, then he just takes transit. What? Its not like he can fly or anything. Calm down. -------------------------- Heaven was a freaking wasteland. DarkStar just stood with his mouth open looking around him, there were hookers on every corner... the angels were hawking their wings for crap, Peter wasnt even at the pearly gates. Man. This is all going to hell... That was a horrible pun. DarkStar just pushed open the gates and walked in, he heard the sound of gun shots off in the distance. DarkStar: Everyone here is already dead, what would shooting them do? Talena scratched her head, as if contemplating the problem. Talena: It would look cool... DarkStar just kept walking, further into what was now nothing but a rundown ghetto. He saw, standing outside of a pawn shop, a man beating on a smallish prostitute, he slapped her and gently picked up the money. DarkStar just sighed. Talena: Isn't that.... DarkStar: Gabrielle. Yes. I realize. This is just getting disturbing. We need to find that information filled bastard Peter. Talena smiled, her eyes turned normal, and she ignored the doll. Talena: You know... just a hunch, but I think he could be over in that building over there. DarkStar: Why do you say that? She pointed at the neon light that said 'Peter's Angelic Strippers XXX' DarkStar: Thats disturbing. Talena: Don't read the caption below it... DarkStar looked at it and shuddered... ' 'Come give our dancers some 'pearly gates'' ----------------------------- The bad techno pounded loudly as DarkStar and Talena walked through the strip joint. Talena cringed as she stepped in something sticky, DarkStar had to surpress a laugh. They eventually (after a few stops to watch Serendipity) got to a VIP area, with a rather large man looking over it. Man: Name... DarkStar: I have many names. But I am the King of Dreams, of the Nightmare Realms... this is my love, Talena... if you want me to get complicated, she is the Angry Princess, and I... the Torn Prince. The man, obviously not one to pay attention to underground folklore, scanned the list with his index finger, he shook his head. Man: Not here... DarkStar: Look, I'm a friend of the boss... Talena: Friend? DarkStar: Shut up. The man squinted. Man: Boss gone... DarkStar: Why, arent you a wealth of polysyllabic words. Man: ...What? DarkStar sighed and looked down, when he looked up, a knife was embedded in the mans chest and he was quite stuck to the wall. Talena giggled. DarkStar: Talena! That wasnt nice! She frowned. DarkStar: But it was really cool. He kissed her forhead and dragged her into the VIP lounge, she smiled and apoligized to the (quite stabbed) guard. The angel Peter was a fairly imposing sight. The things you hear about his old age are greatly exagerated. He looks fairly young, muscular, and... right now, is wearing a white suit with a white hat and snorting a line of white powder into his white nose. Is nothing sacred. DarkStar: Ahem... Peter looked up and snorted the air, running his palm up his face and over his nose. Peter: D..D..D...ark... ar-... Gavyn! Plea- pLeasure to see you agaaain! DarkStar leaned over and spoke to Talena. DarkStar: You know, theres a line you just dont cross... and its in Peters nose now. Talena burst into laughter and fell to her knees, Peter seemed like he wanted to ask a question but couldn't quite get it out before DarkStar interupted. DarkStar: I just want to know whats going on here, how can I get all these blasted primadonna gods back on earth. Its getting annoying. Peter sniffed again. Peter: I-i-I dont know... its poppposible that yyyou just need to right Fates wrong... quipwtquite easy yes, no yes! DarkStar backed away, his hands in the air. DarkStar: Thanks... um... have fun... He left as quickly as he came. He couldn't hide the fact that he was so amused about Heavens falling apart into disaray. It reminded him so much of home.
