Singing, Running, Catchphrases

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-20-02 **Note: Two RP's in one day. Sick. They're short but good.** ----------------------------------------Dear Jenna, Ya gotta have heart...!Ya gotta have heart...! Love,Jason---------------------------------------- Jason: SUCK IT UP, CHILDREN! [We see eleven figures jogging down Pensacola Street, on a rainy, windy spring morning.] Sandra: My feet hurt! Jamie: My legs hurt! Kyle: Frickin'-A~! Pegleg: ARRRR~!!! Eddie: I'm wet! Jamie (as sexy as possible): So am I!! [And thusly, the platoon of CWS students, led by Jason run down the street. Jason starts running backwards to yell to the crowd. Violator, Nick, and Mark are all close by...with everyone else trailing, and Kyle and Sandra pulling up the rear.] Jason: Cardiovascular training is the most important physical skill in wrestling! [Joe Stephenson comments over his shoulder to Jamie.] Joe: I thought he said promos were the most important thing? [Jamie just shrugs.] Jason: No, Joe...PHYSICAL skill! Are you SURE you're in college? [Violator briefly passes J-Core as he's running backwards. He looks around, and sees no one in front of him.] Violator: I AM THE VIOLATOR!! [Jason kind of rolls his eyes.] Jason: You sure are, buddy... [Jason murmurs something to himself.] Jason: All these freakin' catchphrases rolling around, and I can't find a single person with charisma. Why me, god? [He turns around, and overtakes Violator for the lead. Violator gives him the preverbial 'Look of Death,' or in his case, the patented Violating Game Face.] Violator: PREPARE FOR TOTAL VIOLATION!!! [And with that, The Violator picks up speed and again overtakes J-Core. J-Core uses this as an excuse to turn and address the CWS students again.] Jason: See? That's what you should be doing! Pushing each other! One-upping one other! We're here to work together, but we also need to push against each other! You hear me? [There's a murmured chorus of 'yeah, whatevers' and the like.] Jason: When I say 'You hear me?' I want you all to yell 'YES, J-CORE!' Do it, you hear me?! [Eddie, Kyle, and Mark all give a full on...] Yes, J-Core! [But Jacques of Arc's a little slow.] Jacques: Oui, Monsieur J-Core! [Jason slaps his palm against his forehead.] Jason: For the rest of the day, NO MORE GIMMICKS AND CATCHPHRASES, YOU HEAR ME!? Yes, J-Core! [This time, only one person does not yell the proper words.] Violator: I AM THE VIOLATOR!!! [Jason releases a long, deep sigh.] Jason: It's gonna be a long, uh, rest of my life. [He adresses his students...even the lagging and overweight Kyle Andrews.] Jason: You kids are going to be lean, mean cardio machines! You will be able to have thirty minute matches with PCW superstars! You will be able to have your matches nominated for Best of the Month, Best of the Year, and frickin' Best Of All Time! YOU HEAR ME!?! Yes, J-Core! Violator: PREPARE FOR TOTAL VIOLATION!!! [Jason shrugs his shoulders with resignation.] Jason: You just can't teach some old dogs new tricks...like the Swamp Dawg. The animal may learn some new tricks from his 'handler' Riley and the thug...but like always, I'll end up with the pin. [Jason smiles a little to himself. From the back, a cry goes up.] Kyle: Are...are we done yet, sir!? [Jason smiles even wider.] Jason: HELL NO! You kids need musical accompaniment, don't you? Sing it with me... [Jason breaks into his rough, and warbling singing voice.] Jason: I DUNNO WHAT I'VE BEEN TOLD!! I DUNNO WHAT I'VE BEEN TOLD!! Jason: BIG D'S SH*T IS GETTING OLD!! BIG D'S SH*T IS GETTING OLD!! Jason: DON'T CARE THAT HE'S A MENTAL CASE!! DON'T CARE THAT HE'S A MENTAL CASE!! Jason: PUTTIN' THE WOMAN-BEATER IN HIS PLACE!! PUTTIN' THE WOMAN-BEATER IN HIS PLACE!! [Jason's grin is a mile wide.] Jason: SOUND OFF! ONE! TWO! Jason: SOUND OFF! Violator: I AM THE VIOLATOR!!! Pegleg: ARRRR~!!! [Jason can't help but shake his head. The eleven intrepid wrestlers run comfortably down the street, singing of Big D's tired schtick much to the delight of passersby.] Jason: ONE MORE TIME! I DUNNO WHAT I'VE BEEN TOLD... [As our scene, as strange as it is, fades to black...]
