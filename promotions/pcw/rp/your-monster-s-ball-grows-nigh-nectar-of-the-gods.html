Your Monster's Ball Grows Nigh: Nectar Of The Gods

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11.24.02 (#10 PPV) Finally, the time has come. The hour of Alliances draws near, and with it the fates of every wrestler in PCW grows uncertain. Every man. One match. How do you turn sixty into one winner? Use five rings, cages, and weapons of all sorts, that’s how. Let men like the Neo Void God, the Burned Man, Gold Patron Meritous, The Chillin’ Villain, and the Culinary Conquistador on the loose in the same arena in the same time period. But who can call himself the best man in PCW? Who can become king of the night, on this monster’s ball of sorts? The last day of the same, plain life. Tonight, a crown is placed upon he who can be a man amongst men. Who is that? “You kidding, ya nimrod?” Rush Mason. You hate him, and deservedly so. He doesn’t deserve your cheers, nor does he want them. He hates you in particular, for the simple reason that you stand for the good that he so vigorously opposes. Teamed up with three ‘Extreme Crackas’ in another little ploy by the Xavier Kannon regime. Keep him down, why don’t you? Give him the penis-obsessed guy, some Italian, and another guy who didn’t even show up. He’ll show exactly what he plans to do with that team. “I plan to win.” He would have it no other way. The last time there was a match of this magnitude was March Madness. The Brawl for It All. Who, pray tell, was that 6th to last man in the match? Rush Mason, perhaps? But come now, there must be others who were in that same match with him. The winner, Boda? Jobbing away in FSW, too embarrassed to come back. Mexi? Off in OSW, having to fight that whore Reagan for his job. Chase? Another loser in FSW, but then again, he probably deserves it for the pathetic title reigns that were New Breed. Miyagawa? Retired by vampo-freak. You know who else that leaves? “Jason Snow, or as we like to call him, Inmate 8793.” What a pathetic human being. Out of jail after five months. Ahh… that’s why PCW prospered so. And Rush Mason still stands, now elite amongst the elite. Ready to win Alliances for his joke of a team and wipe the smirks off the face of every PCW’er. Ready to take the throne. But, what does he have that separates him? What makes him better than your El Diablos or your Skitzos? Talent. Desire. A Deadly Intent To Win. The only fear that lives within him? The fear that there is only tonight. After tonight with all the big names lying, he will stand tall. “As I stand above your bodies on my night, you will know exactly who your king is.” A king amongst the upper echelon of a federation that he looks at, no longer from the outside, but from within, spying his compeitition with the deadly eye of an eagle. Who will the final pinfall be upon? On Akiyama, to give the nVg knowledge of his own mortality? On Chef, to silence the lovable cheers for Cheffy and his clone wife? On Darkstar, to prove the worthlessness of that whore of a man? On End, to pay back old blood debts? On Rent-A-Hero, to stifle the fans who still call his name? On Snow, to prove the coming of the new and the passing of the old? On Duke, to settle things once and for all? Or on Kannon, to kill his former comrade, and take back what he deserves? “Every single one goes down this night.” Every one goes down indeed, but he who goes down finally, who will it be? “Whoever keeps out of my way until the very last.” Indeed, that’s who. Lick thy lips, for the taste of revenge is sweet like the nectar of the God’s. Neo Void, Hubbard, all of them. You will taste Bon Dieu’s own nectar. How sweet it will be, indeed.
