Night of the Living Krishnas

28 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/28/02 Pain is a funny, funny thing. It can take many forms, so many varieties of suffering.......so much beautiful, beautiful torment. Sometimes, the cause of the pain is unknown.......were you infected by a virus, a life form mutating and adapting to your pitiful immune system? Bacteria perhaps, festering, growing, overrunning you.......or did you merely, as the quaint saying goes, 'wake up on the wrong side of the bed?' Whatever the case, once the affliction has taken root there is little you can do to be rid of it. It may go into slumber......you may even think you have overcome it. But it's there.......waiting, waiting eternally for you. And when the moment is right....... 'ARRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRGH!' A muffled scream fills the air......we race through the black, past the eternal void and finally emerge, to find a tall man before us. Agony and frustration both twist inside his expression.......he stomps his foot, turning away from us. Clyde: (Holding his chin) Mummmmph! A short distance away a small child prances, wielding a baseball bat. Boy: Ha ha! Clyde: Bahmamamadah! The killer attempts to chase the child, but only succeeds in tripping over his own two feet. The boy's laughter rings in his ears, as he lays face down on the pavement. Clyde: Ugggggggh. Up to the fallen demon strolls Bethany, her high heels entering his now limited plane of view. Bethany: Goddammit, I can't go two seconds without you being assaulted by baseball bat wielding children can I? Clyde: Meahama. Angrily, the little girl hauls Clyde to his feet......dusts him off, then looks at his mouth with an expression of disgust. Bethany: Dear God Clyde, you're swelling up like a puss ball inside Brandi's crotch. Clyde: Balama. Clyde holds his mouth in agony, stumbling away. He wanders down the street, falling to his knees before a mud puddle.......looks into it, to find indeed his lip is now swollen to roughly the size of a grapefruit. Clyde: Agggh. Bethany runs after him, stopping just in front......then breaks out laughing. Bethany: HA! God you suck! Clyde: Baaitch. The burned man takes an errant swing at Bethany, just barely missing her small head. She dances around him, chuckling meanly. Bethany: Ha. You are washed up......you think you're gonna kill Chris Chase, but you get your ass beaten by a six year old. Ha ha! Clyde: Shuuft mup. Clyde stands quickly, kicking out his foot and catching Bethany in the gut. She lets out all her air, doubling over......then laughs again, giving chase as her lover attempts to escape. Clyde: I'mma een paain. Bethany: Bwahaha! Clyde: Aaaaaagh. Once again the psychopath attempts to escape......blood drips from his mouth, forming a trail which his young bitch of a sex kitten follows while chuckling meanly. Bethany: Ah God, I never get tired of seeing you suffer. Clyde: Wha......why mmmfh yu say tha. Bethany: Because you just suck, that’s why! Same reason I love watching all the useless bitches like Brandi get maimed.......I don’t need a damn reason! Clyde gives a sarcastic grin, then howls in fresh pain and rounds a corner. Bethany follows......then gasps as she sees a peculiar sight. Bethany: Huh? The burned freak known as Clyde sits upon the pavement, looking up at a group of men. This platoon gazes down at him, the light shining from their balds heads. “Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare.” Clyde: (Wide eyed) Baacck......ack ah may. Bethany: RUN CLYDE!!!!! Bethany darts in, scooping up the fallen killer just in time to avoid his being encircled by the peace lovers. They move after him, slowly marching. Hare Krishnas: Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare. Bethany: Back, back I say! Hatred brimming in her eyes, Bethany assumes a fighting stance.......leaps forward, but falls out of midair as the lead Krishna raises his hand calmly. Hare Krishnas: Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare. Bethany: BAH~! Bethany scrambles to her feet again, backing off and trying to find a way to break through the Krishna’s force field of peace and tranquility. Bethany: What’ll we do Clyde? They’re getting closer! Clyde: Mmmmuhmph. Bethany: DAMMIT! Taking the demon’s hand, Bethany runs back around the corner......slowly the religious zombies follow. Hare Krishnas: Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare. Bethany: (Huffing and puffing) We’ve gotta find some way to stop them before they convert us all! Clyde: Harrr krisssa, haa krisssa, rrisha rishna, haae hare hhar raaa, hare rrma, rra rama, are mare. Bethany: No, not you too! Snap out of it! The little girl slaps Clyde in the face.....he shakes his head like a wet dog. Clyde: Whaaa? Mmmph. Bethany: What’ll we do? Once again the pair rounds a corner, the Krishnas close behind. Hare Krishnas: (Muffled) Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare. Bethany: Up there! The little girl and the serial killer dart to a nearby ladder, climbing up. The Krishas tail close behind, their dreaded mantra growing ever closer. Hare Krishnas: Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare. Bethany: Hurry! Clyde clambers over the side of the building, followed by Bethany. The zealots follow close behind, threatening forced conversion.......their leader reaches the top. Hare Krishna: Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna...... Suddenly the ladder holding the bald man tips, creaking dangerously. At the lip of the building Bethany squats, prying at it with a crowbar......the Krisha reaches for her. Hare Krishna: Hare Hare Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare Bethany: Come ON!!!!! The man’s arm reaches the little girl’s head......hairs begin to fall from her scalp. Bethany: NO! Clyde: Harrr krisssa, haa krisssa. Bethany: GRUAAAAGH!!!!!!!! Finally with a mighty effort Bethany succeeds in prying the ladder free......with a final and terrible squeal it falls back, taking the entire line of Krishnas with it. Hare Krishnas: (Fading) Hare Krishna........ Finally a crash sounds out far below.......Bethany slumps to the ground. Bethany: Well this is a fine mess you got us into. We were nearly converted! Clyde: Mmmmagham. Bethany: Yeah yeah. Come on, let’s go find the damn ship before it leaves. The little girl marches off......Clyde follows. But below, in a pile of rubble a small noise emerges......a hand raises, a tiny and threatening “hare krishna” sounding forth as it struggles for life, and we mercifully fade to black.........
