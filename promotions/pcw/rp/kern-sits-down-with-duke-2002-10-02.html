Kern sits down with Duke

Title: Kern sits down with Duke Featuring: Duke Williams Date: 10/2/02 -We join Duke Williams as he sits back in his office with his hands behind his head with a look of ecstasy on his face. All of a sudden a PCW camera crew busts thorough the door as Duke face turns from pleasure to shock. A moment later Duke’s secretaries head pops up from under the desk to see what’s going on.- Williams: Ahhhh Jen did you find that file? Jen: Ahhhh I think so. -Jen flings up the desk drawer and pulls out a random file and exits the room red faced. Duke would like to get up and yell at the camera crew but he can’t at this moment. So he sits and yells.- Williams: What the hell is a matter with you people? -PCW broadcast personality Dave Kern replies.- Kern: We couldn’t find your secretary so we just came in. Looks like you were about to do the same. -Kern and the camera crew laugh as Duke gets red in the face.- Williams: I do NOT find humor in this. Kern: Look I’m here to interview you. Williams: Then lets do it so I can get back to…… Kern: Filing? Williams:…yea…yea…filing. -Kern sits down in the chair in front of Duke’s desk. The camera crew sets up with a side profile of both men.- Kern: First off on Fusion you will team with Miyagawa in a match that pits all the member who could possible be in the final four for the gold. How do you see your teamwork with Miya happening. -Duke grabs a long Churchill Cohiba cigar and cuts the tip.- Williams: How do I see it happening? I don’t. I’m not teaming with that nut job. If he has the balls to come to the ring I’m going to choke his f*cking neck till he turns blue. Kern: So you have no interest in winning that match. Williams: F*ck no, what does it mean hoss? Kern: Pride, principle, respect. Williams: I’m happy for you if your into those things but I can give a sh*t. Ann Nicole Smith has more of a clue then you hoss. It doesn’t matter. Only one thing matters. Kern: And what’s that? -Duke sniffs the cigars length.- Williams: What do you think hoss? Kern: Red October? Williams: Bingo. Nothing matters to me more then beating Miyagawa and putting myself in a position to win the world title. Kern: You think you can accomplish that? Williams: Except for Kannon the rest of them shmucks in that match up are all pussies. I’ll say that again. They’re all vaginal. One more time so it processes the mind just right. They are all raged up bloody cunts. Kern: Why do you say that. -Duke lights up his cigar and takes a drag.- Williams: Because I live in the Untied States of America and can say that. But more so because its as true as Jessi Jackson is a criminal and Rosa Parks is black. I can call these cocksmokers out all night and I won’t get a respons,. why cus one is incarcerated and the rest live in fantasy lands. They live somewhere over the rainbow. I’m the only normal one in the lot of them and that kiddies is a sad statement. -Duke lets an ash fall into his ashtray shaped like a set of spread legs.- Kern: Putting their mental satiability to the side they have all functioned well under these circumstance for a long time. Williams: Be that as it may the fact is I have studied each and everyone of those vulva’s and know their strength and weaknesses. Kern: You have had losses to many of these guys in the past. Williams: And I have had wins. It works both ways Kern. Plus I’m hungry. Kern: I’ve heard you talk about the drive in the past. You feel its now or never? Williams: Your damn right I feel it’s now or never. I’m not getting any younger and my chances keep getting slimmer and slimmer; like an anorexic on slimfast. Kern: If you lose will the “r”word create? -Duke thinks it over as he smokes down his stogie.- Williams: With out a doubt. If I lost his match it’s the end. The end of who ever wins it. Revenge will be avenged. Kern: I was talking about retirement. Williams: I’ll retire when I drop dead. Wrestling is all I have and I’m not walking away from it. You may have to wheel me to the ring one day but hoss I’ll be there fighting till the end. Kern: What are your thoughts on what Randy Savage wrote about you. Williams: I’m going to have to send him a Christmas card this year. Savage is right though its my time. Its time for an old dog to be king. To hell with the rest of these shit rocks. -Duke offers Kern a cigar but he waves it off.- Kern: So you truly believe your time is now? Williams: It is Malice Time.
