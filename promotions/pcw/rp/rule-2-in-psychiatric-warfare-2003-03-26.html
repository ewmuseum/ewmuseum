Rule #2 in Psychiatric Warfare...

26 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 26, 2003 OOC: BTW, some of the war news that I talk about here is a bit false, British forces are engaged in the battle of Basra, but the 82nd Airborne is still stationary in Kuwait, awaiting to move. Also, both of these were gonna go up tomorrow, but I figured why not put them up tonight since I had spell checked them and everything already, but enjoy...-------------------{{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up back inside the limo where it had just left off in the last promo. The driver has begun driving again and the location that they’re headed toward is unknown. Smoke is watching the TV again on continuing coverage of the war in Iraq. He’s watching CBS news with Dan Rather and they’re showing live coverage of a battle in progress in southern Iraq near Basra. The 82nd Airborne has been sent out to the battle to try and help reinforce the British troops who are already fighting in the area and Smoke wants to see if he recognizes anyone that he knew when he was part of the Army. He smiles as he sees a US Army soldier walk into the battle scene on the TV... Semore Smoke: Ha! See that emblem on his arm, the two A’s, shows that he’s part of the 82nd Airborne, much like how the 101st Airborne has an eagle on the sleeve, the 82nd has that. {{{{The two guards in the limo nod their heads, but it’s obvious that they’re bored about talk about Semore Smoke and the war and they’re awaiting the second rule and the second installment of the mind games that Smoke is playing against El Diablo to try and psyche El Diablo out. The guard on Smoke’s right, or the guard that didn’t talk at all in the last promo speaks up with a curious question...}}}} Guard 2: Now, what of rule number two in the psychiatric warfare manual? {{{{Smoke turns to the guard and turns the TV off, he smiles at the guard and says in reply...}}}} Semore Smoke: Patience, we have to get there first. The element of surprise always come first... and it looks like we’ve arrived. {{{{The limo stops and Smoke motions for the guards to stay in the limo like before, he does turn the TV back on however and turns it to NBC news with Tom Brokaw. He opens the door and then as he’s getting out of the limo, he points to the TV and says...}}}} Semore Smoke: Watch this. Ha! {{{{Smoke steps out of the limo and walks towards the building that the limo stopped in front of. The building is that of a famous one located in New York City. It’s the NBC studios, or better known as the Rockafeller Center. Smoke walks up to the rotating door that enters the building and enters. He walks up to the increased security and steps through a metal detector. Nothing happens and they then scan him with a hand wand to make sure that it wasn’t anything wrong with the detector, and nothing comes up and then they let him through. Smoke walks up to the center lobby table and stares at the secretary behind the desk with a smile. The secretary smiles back, she’s got blonde hair and blue eyes, and looks to be about twenty-five or so years old...}}}} Secretary: Yes, may I help you? {{{{Smoke points to the monitor in the lobby that shows the current news from Tom Brokaw and asks...}}}} Semore Smoke: Yes you can. Can you tell me which studio the NBC News is in? {{{{The woman behind the desk laughs for a second and then stares at Smoke with the continuous smile on her face...}}}} Secretary: Oh, wait, you’re serious. Well, first I’ll need a name and check to see if you have an appointment to go up there. {{{{Smoke smiles and says as he points to himself...}}}} Semore Smoke: I’m Semore Smoke, and I’m here to go on the news and give a quick correspondent report about something {{{{The woman hears the name and then her smile fades from her face. She stares at Smoke with a frightened look on her face...}}}} Secretary: Are you Semore Smoke from PCW? {{{{Smoke nods his head...}}}} Secretary: The one who resides in Atlantic City? {{{{Smoke nods his head again and the secretary looks even more frightened...}}}} Secretary: Well, look! Your name is on this list! Just head to you’re left and take that first elevator and go to the second floor, then it’ll be studio A7. {{{{Smoke smiles as he walks away and he says to the secretary...}}}} Semore Smoke: Thanks. {{{{Smoke walks to the elevator that the secretary pointed to and Smoke enters it after hitting the button for the doors to open. He then hits the second floor button and the doors close and the elevator begins to climb up the elevator shaft. The elevator stops seconds later at the second floor and the door opens. Right in front of him are several cameras and Tom Brokaw sitting at his desk, talking about the on going war in Iraq. Smoke walks forward and walks in front of the main camera that is focused on Brokaw and stands there facing it as Brokaw asks what he’s doing here...}}}} Tom Brokaw: Hey! Who let you up here! Get out! We’re trying to do a live newscast here! {{{{Smoke turns around for a second to face Brokaw...}}}} Semore Smoke: I know... but this is Semore Smoke time on your show, it’s time to show rule number two in the warfare handbook. {{{{Smoke turns and faces the TV camera again and speaks...}}}} Semore Smoke: Some of you watching at home know me, I’m Semore Smoke of Primetime Championship Wrestling. Well, I’m here on live nationwide TV to tell you a story about a man in PCW, a man that goes by the name of El Diablo to the public. Well, this man you see, returned to a place called PCW just over a week ago, just like he and myself stole my spotlight and stole my heat upon my return. Well, El Diablo doesn’t realize this, but I’m gonna make his life a living hell for the next few days starting with today. You see, the easiest way to take down a person is to get into their head, and the way I’ll get into this guys head is by turning his people against him and humiliate him on live TV. El Diablo has a deep dark secret that he keeps from the public, that of late nights and Saturday night Pay Per Views. You see, he’s got what I like to call homosyndrone, or in normal terms, he’s gay. Plain and simple, he likes man meat in his ass, and I’m scared to be in the same ring with him with that fact being known. I’m scared he’ll try to butt fuck me. Wait... can I say fuck on live TV? Oh well, I just did. And guess what, it felt well. Now since I hear the pitter-patter of security coming up the steps to take me away, I’ll leave you with this. El Diablo is gay and that’s a fact. {{{{Smoke walks out from in front of the camera and then walks towards the elevator and walks onto as the doors stayed open the entire time. He hits the first floor button and the elevator goes back down to the lobby floor. The doors open and the two guards are standing there, waiting for him. They’re holding security at bay with two hand guns in their hands and they look at Smoke with a smile as Smoke comes off the elevator, the three then walk away from the security and the out the revolving door. As they walk up to the limo, the guard that talked earlier in this promo turns to Smoke and asks...}}}} Guard 2: Is El Diablo really gay? {{{{Smoke shakes his head as he sits down in the back of the limo and smiles as he answers...}}}} Semore Smoke: Nope, he’s not, but now the nation thinks that he is, so this Friday will be just a walk in a park as I see rule number three won’t need to be used, lets get back to New Jersey, to prepare for my departure on Thursday for Fusion. {{{{Both guards nod as they enter the limo after Smoke and close the door. The limo then pulls off into the distant sunset as Smoke has finished what he came to start in New York...}}}}
