PCW News: An El Diablo/End Production

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/08/02 End:“Are you sure about this?” El Diablo:“Positive, you’ll do fine. Sandy, are you ready?” Sandy:“Yes, Mr. Diablo, sir.” El Diablo:“ED is fine. We’re about to start now. I gave you all the cue lines, just jump in when you hear them.” Wong:“Okay.” The lights come up on the news platform, the PCW logo in the back on a blue background. The desk is about five feet high, brown oak. El Diablo, his long hair tied back behind his head in a ponytail, is dressed in a black Armani suit, but he still has thesymbol for the Mexican flag painted under each of his eyes. Sandy Wong, not doing much else at the moment, is wearing a gray women’s business suit, and her hair is black, worn bouncy and out down to the shoulders. The stage director calls for the generic PCW music from off camera. Director Juju:“Wonk.” El Diablo slaps his head with his palm. El Diablo:“I never should have agreed to let The End’s pet iguana be our stage director.” Juju throws a clipboard and some pens at El Diablo. Juju:“WONK!” El Diablo:“Sorry.” The music comes up and the cameras begin recording, showing El Diablo brushing pens off the desk, and Sandy Wong snickering at him. With the end of the music, the two quickly regain their composure. El Diablo starts. El Diablo:“Thank you for joining us, and welcome to PCW News. I’m running things on this time slot, so it might be a little different than what you’re normally used to. We’ll get to our headlines, but first, here’s pictures of Kremmen Von Smackdown raping a cow!” Blurry photos of a man raping a cow, a bull no less, with Kremmen Von Smackdown’s enlarged head cut & pasted on, are fully displayed on the screen. In the top right-hand corner, the rating of the show goes up from TV-PG to TV-XXXXXXXXX. After ten seconds of pure animated KVS bull-raping horror, the camera feed returns to El Diablo. Sandy Wong is off to his left side, trying to keep down her lunch. El Diablo:“On the other hand, the bull seemed to enjoy it. Okay, it wasn’t rape. Anyway, now it’s time for us to deal with the headlines. Sandy?” Wong is put on camera, coughing after swallowing back down her nearly expelledemesis. Sandy:“I don’t *cough* argh! Not right now, I *cough* need some water. Back *hack* to you, ED.” El Diablo was not expecting to be put on the spot; ED is silent for a moment as he turns back to the camera. But he regains himself and carries on with the headlines that Sandy Wong (who has run to get some water from the production desk) should be doing. El Diablo:“Our top story today is... ME! Well, I’m making a movie called, ‘The End.’ It’s about ruthless, murderous cyborgs that try to take over the world, and the one man who stands up to oppose them. I can’t give you many details right now, but expect for the bootlegsto be on the street within the month.” A photo of El Diablo is put up in the top-right corner of the screen, with ED taking up the left side of the screen. El Diablo:“I talked to PCW wrestler, The End, whom I hand-picked for the lead role, just as recently as now. This is what he had to say.” The camera pans to ED’s right, where The End is sitting in a black suit, with his mask over his head. El Diablo:“Thank you for joining us.” End:“It’s no problem. I’m free until Turmoil.” Diablo begins the interview. El Diablo:“So, can you tell us what attracted you to the script?” End:“Actually, you haven’t presented me with a script yet.” ED laughs nervously. El Diablo:“Ha ha. Such a kidder. *In a low voice* Just read the cue cards.” A shrug comes from The End, and he doesn’t bother speaking at a volume lower than normal. End:“I’m blind, remember?” El Diablo:“Oh, yeah. Anyway, let’s skip this part of the show and go straight to Cal Seaver, who is out in the field covering an AFL-CIO protest of the closing of several car manufacturing plants by GM in Michigan. Cal?” The camera switches to the satellite feed, with Seaver in a light-brown trenchcoat and matching hat, standing out in front of the GM building. He’s facing away from the camera; there are people running around frantically. Seaver turns around to the camera,and his panicked face is easily noticable. Cal:“ED, what were you thinking giving me Anna Keyson as an understudy? She keepsdestroying our equipment whenever I try to get her to do anything!” Smoke appears on-camera. The cameraman, Tom, shouts to Seaver from off-camera. Tom:“Cal, the van’s on fire.” Cal:“AGAIN?!” The smoke billows out from the van, moving in from the left of the screen. Seaver inhales some unintentionally and coughs harshly. Cal:“I’ll have to get back to you, ED. Seaver out.” The feed returns to El Diablo, sitting wide-eyed. El Diablo:“... Wow.” The since-returned Sandy Wong takes over. The studio cameraman goes to her and Juju’s instruction. Wong is recovered, washed up, with a fresh voice and a smile. Sandy:“Well, it’s time for the weather with our designated meteorologist, The End! What’s the weather forecast for this coming week?” The End is standing in front of randomly-placed weather graphics on the green-screen behind him. The End just points off in certain places and speaks. End:“You local forecast is as follows -- look out of your window, and dress accordingly. For your extended forecast, keep an umbrella and sunscreen ready.” The End stops speaking. Juju the Albino Iguana stage director prompts him to say more. Juju: *Hushed, but direct*“Wonk!” The End nods and turns back to the camera. End:“This has been your all-purpose weather forecast. Back to you, ED.” ED reappears on the screen. He’s holding his right hand to his ear, using his left to motion “hold up.” Of course, he doesn’t have a headset, he’s just pretending he does. El Diablo:“We have an incoming satellite message coming an local news station in the Detriot area. Let’s put that up.” The feed is scrambled, and snowy static comes up for a few seconds. When the satellite feed filters through with the tracking, a burned and black Cal Seaver is sitting with an ice pack over his head. Sandy:“Cal? What happened to you?” Cal:“Ah! Don’t talk so loud! And someone answer that phone!” The ringing, of course, is only in Seaver’s ears. Cal:“Long story short, after we put out the second fire and tried to get on with covering the protest, Keyson blew up our truck so we could salvage anything from it!” The camera returns to ED, looking over his shoulder as he writes a check to Anna Keyson for “services rendered.” ED notices the camera and rips up the check, swallowing the pieces. Sandy is confused. Sandy:“Um... listen, Cal, you just get some medical attention. Don’t worry about the story.” Back in Detroit, Cal Seaver is sleeping, snoring, on camera. Tom (bearing a remarkable resemblance to Magnus Ver Magnusson) slaps Cal in the face. Cal:“Leave me alone... I’m tired.” Tom:“You don’t sleep when you have a concussion. Get up.” The feed cuts out. ED is back on camera, acting normal. El Diablo:“Now we go back over to The End with the traffic report. By now, he should be in our PCW Chopper 6, over I-95. What’s the traffic look like out there?” The End, hunched into a chopper not designed for someone his height, shouts into the camera. End:“HOW THE HELL SHOULD I KNOW?! God, you’re a dumbass sometimes.” The camera returns to a wide view of ED and Sandy Wong. El Diablo:“Just read the--” Before ED can finish, Sandy Wong elbows him in the ribs. El Diablo:“What? Oh, right, the blindness.” Before ED can save himself, Tyler Nelson walks onto the screen behind Sandy Wong. He’s dressed in his trademark suit ensemble, his hair slicked back. He wears a cockysmile and his body language is closed. Nelson:“Okay, El Dildo, get off of my set.” Nelson points at ED, and then motions his thumb behind him to the door. ED contests. El Diablo:“I went through all the right channels to get this airtime--” Nelson cuts him off. Nelson:“I OWN this airtime, and frankly, I’m saving you from embarassing yourselves anymore than you already have. Now get out, or I’ll have you removed!” ED, not wanting to start anything with Nelson or the UA, gets out of his chair and walks out. Nelson sits down in the vacant seat and turns to the rest of the crew. Nelson:“What are you all still doing here? Get out! I have my own people coming in.” Sandy, Juju, and the stage crew leave everything and walk out. The cameras all drop to the floor as they are left unattended. The shuffling of feet and mumbling are heard for about a minute, before the cameras come back up again, and Tyler Nelson’s music plays in the background. The set is changed to a big picture of Tyler Nelson’s smiling face on a black wall, with a spotlight on Nelson standing besides the picture. Nelson:“Thank you for staying with us, loyal PCW fans. I apologize for what you just had to witness, but I promise that there is still hope for your entertainment on this particularbroadcast. And you will get it, as I will now be hosting ‘Tyler Nelson’s Greatest Hits’ for the next 76 hours! There’s that much memorable Tyler Nelson footage! Well, that’ssuitable for airing on a TV-14 rating, anyway. So let’s get to it!”
