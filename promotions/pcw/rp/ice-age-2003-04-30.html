Ice Age?

30 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-30-03 The scene opens to Jaslene walking around the firstfloor of Adam’s home. She’s got a cell phone in herhand. A cord can be seen coming from her ear, it’sclipped onto the strap of her white tank top. She’sgot dark blue mesh shorts on and white socks. She’spacing around, trying to be quiet. “So you’ve never noticed anything Gina?” Jaslene sayslooking up the stairs. “No? Shit…I know there issomething going on here but I don’t want to confronthim without some kind of proof. It could push himaway.” Jaslene continues to walk back forth in the fronthallway. “I know Gina, I should say something but I can’t. Ijust don’t know how to approach him on something likethis.” Jaslene says running her hand up and down thecord, “He doesn’t know I know and I don’t think hesuspects anything either.” Jaslene stops and nods a couple of times. “Yes Gina I was smart enough to wait until I knew hewas going to be gone long enough before lookingthrough anything.” Jaslene says in a very punctualtone, “I’m not stupid ya know!” Suddenly another phone starts to ring. The suddenshrill of the phone startles Jaslene. “Shit!” she exclaims heading for the kitchen, “That’sAdam’s phone!” She lunges across the kitchen table and grasps thephone in her hand. She flips it open. “Hello?” she answers gasping for breath, “No, noyou’ve got the right number. Adam’s still sleeping. Isthere something I can do for you?” Jaslene swings her legs around the middle of the tableand kicks her feet back and forth. “A match? For Fusion?” Jaslene asks moving her headaround, “I’ve got some shitty reception here, couldyou repeat that please?” Jaslene nods. “Ok, match on Friday against Shane Wethers.” She saysin confirmation, “It might help him get in gear forhis Extreme Title Match, speaking of which youwouldn’t be able to tell me anything about that wouldyou? Didn’t think so, thanks anyway.” Jaslene closes the phone and turns her attention backto her own cell phone. “Hm? Oh that was a PCW agent calling to let Adam knowabout his match.” Jaslene says, “Yeah he did say itwas a reminder, I don’t remember Adam telling meanything about a match on Fusion.” A large thump can be heard through the ceiling. “Heh, he must be ready to get up. Man I tell you thiswhole getting up at five to work out must really behard on him.” Jaslene states walking back through thehallway, “He always goes right back to bed after heshowers getting back from running. I don’t know Ginahe just does...” “JAS?!” a voice calls out from the top of the stairs,“You down there?” “YAH!” She replies, “I’m on the phone with Gina.” Adam comes down the stairs with a pair of black meshshorts on. He notices that Jaslene is on the phone butdoesn’t seem to care. “Was that my phone that was ringing just a little bitago?” he asks stratching his stomach. Jaslene nods. “Go make yourself something to eat and I’ll be therein a couple of minutes.” Jaslene says pointing towardthe kitchen. “Ok,” Adam says turning to walk away, “Tell Gina Isaid hi.” Adam walks off toward the kitchen. “Adam said hi Gina.” Jaslene says looking down thehall, “He seemed all right there. Didn’t complain ofanything. Well I need to get going lest I arouse hissuspicion. Talk to you later Gina, love ya babe,bai-bai.” Jaslene pulls the earpiece from her ear and shuts thephone off. She places it on a small table in thehallway before heading on down the hall toward thekitchen. She comes into the kitchen and sees Adamleaning up against the counter with a bowl in hishand. He looks up and sees her cross the room and pickup an orange from a bowl. “Well?” he asks raising his brow before taking aspoonful of cereal into his mouth. “Well what?” Jaslene responds beginning to peel theorange, “Oh, Gina said hi back.” Adam smirks. “Was it my phone ringing or not?” he asks. “Huh? Oh-oh! Yeah it was, it was a PCW rep remindingyou of your match on Fusion.” she replies pulling thepeeled orange in half. “With Brianna Washington? Should be interesting...”Adam trails off taking another spoonful of cereal intohis mouth. “Uh...” Jaslene looks down and trails off. “What?” Adam asks placing the bowl down on thecounter, “The match is against Brianna right Jas?” “Uh...well...no it’s not...” Jaslene says popping apiece of the orange into her mouth, “Dante Jackson hasthe honor of taking on Brianna for her Extreme TitleFriday.” Adam slams his fist down onto the coutner. The spoonrattles in the bowl. “WHAT!?” Adam yells, “Dante Jackson?!? What in thehell was the point of making my match against Hank~! aNumber One Contender’s Match if I’m not getting theshot!” “Calm down Adam!” Jaslene says, “I’m sure you will getyour shot soon. You just need to have some patience. Idon’t think it matters who has the title because whenyou step into the ring you will be the one walking outwith the title on your shoulder.” “That’s true but I should be the one in that match notDante Jackson.” Adam frustratingly says, “I meanseriously, just why is Dante getting his shot at theExtreme Title before me?” “I don’t know Adam, I asked the rep and he said thathe knew nothing of who booked the match or why it wasbooked. His only job was to remind those PCW wrestlerswho go home of their matches.” Jaslene says tossingAdam a couple of pieces of the orange, “You’ll getyour chance, just be patient about it.” “It doesn’t matter because the person I want to faceis going to be the person walking out of Fusion thechamp.” Adam says with a manaical grin coming to hisface. “What’s that supposed to mean?” Jaslene asks. Adam takes a sliver of the orange and holds it up forcloser inspection. “It’s like this Jaslene,” Adam says, “PCW has put meinto the position where I know that I have an ExtremeTitle shot coming to me. All I have to do to ensure avictory in that match is to make sure a person that Iknow I can beat has the title.” Adam places the sliver of orange into his mouth. “But enough of that, you said that I had a matchFriday?” Adam asks to which Jaslene just nods, “If notagainst Brianna then who?” “Shane Wethers? Who in the hell is Shane Wethers?”Adam asks eating the rest of the orange in one bite. “Adam he’s the guy that attacked you when you wouldn’tbreak the Restraining Order on Smoke...you don’tremember that?” Jaslene says in disbelief. “Must have just slipped my mind, I’m still a bitgrogging after all.” Adam says rubbing his eyes, “ThisWethers character, what is his story?” “Beats me, I just know he is some rock band named IceAge I think but I’m not sure of that.” Jaslene saysrubbing her chin. “Ice Age? What you mean like the kids movie?” Adamsays shaking his head, “Great a kiddie rocker, I’mexcited for this one. Though the son of a bitch didattack me on Turmoil so I suppose payback is inorder.” “I don’t think he’s a kiddie rocker Adam.” Jasleneresponds. “Ugh...fine teenie bopper, what’s the difference? It’sall crap in the end anyway. Worthless shite that noone wants to listen to. The thing that amazes me isthat people are just getting handed recordingcontracts these days.” Adam says placing the bowl intothe sink. “Do you even know what his group sounds like?” Jasleneasks. “Well I suppose I don’t but that doesn’t mean I can’tformulate an opinion on them basedly solely on thefact the little bitch attacked me from behind.” Adamsays with a chuckle. Jaslene just scoffs. “Adam you shouldn’t make light of this!” she saystaking the orange peel to the garbage can in thecorner of the counter behind Adam. “Every match youhave in PCW is a chance to move yourself up the card.” “Don’t worry about it Jas. It’ll be fine. Shane willget his and I will get mine.” Adam says with a smirk.“I’m going to take a shower.” Adam walks past Jaslene and heads back up the stairs. “He seemed ok but I don’t know. I guess we’ll have towait and see what happens this Friday.” she says toherself. The scene fades out as Jaslene walks out of thekitchen back to the front hallway.
