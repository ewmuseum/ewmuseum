Golden Heart Chronicles: Lucy In the Sky with Diamonds Chapter 2 Part 3 (The Finale)

30 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/30/02 Golden Heart Chronicles: The Golden Heart chronicles are an insight to the life of Juan Borro. Through these chronicles we get to experience what Juan went through to lead to how he turned out now. The Golden Heart Chronicles are split into different chapters where something sparks “flashbacks” during Juan’s present day life to take us back to his childhood. Read and Enjoy, hopefully this will fulfill you’re thoughts on how Juan Borro really is. Time: 11:15 A.M Location: MexicoScene: Spring Break “Lucy In the Sky with Diamonds.” Part 3 The scene opens with Juan Borro lying down in front of a beautiful green field where a soccer tournament between the young Spring Break college students is going on. Juan isn’t in a mood to compete, but decided to check out some of the action. The day is a little cooler than the others, but still very warm for climates Juan is used to. Juan is tired from all of the chaos going around him in the last few days. (Juan decides to lie back in his bleacher, when all of a sudden he sees this little girl with ice cream in her hand, standing right over him.) Little Girl: Excuse me mister, but can I sit over here. I want to see the boys play soccer up close. (Juan smirks and steps aside so the girl can sit down a lot. He tries to get comfortable, but the girl keeps slurping her ice cream very loudly.) Juan: Can you do me a favor and keep it down? Little Girl: Keep what down? (The girl takes another few loud slurps as Juan cringes and grinds his teeth together. He then grabs her hand and speaks.) Juan: That slurping noise you keep making is a little bit disturbing. Do you think you could possibly keep it down? (The little girl pulls her hand away from Juan and nods.) Little Girl: I’ll try mister, but if the ice cream is about to fall all over my dress, I’m gonna slurp it before it does. Juan: Okay, deal. (Juan lies his head back down, on the bench, as the girl just stares at him. She then pokes at his knee a few times until Juan slowly gets up right, with a not to pleasant look on his face.) Juan: What now? Little Girl: Are you a hobo? Juan: Excuse me. Little Girl: My mommy used to tell me to stay away from people who sleep on benches and don’t shave. (Juan touches his face, and notices he is a bit unshaved, but shakes it off and takes the comment as an insult.) Juan: No, I am not a hobo, but maybe you should take you’re mothers advice and stay away from people like me. Little Girl: I don’t listen to my mother anymore. Juan: Why not? Little Girl: She’d dead now, so I guess I don’t have to do what she says. I live with my brother in Florida. (Juan goes silent, for a few moments, until the girl starts to tie Juan’s shoelaces. Juan leans over and notices giving the girl a quiet odd look.) Juan: Any reason why you’re tying my shoes? Little Girl: You’re shoelaces were untied. It’s not good to walk around with untied shoelaces, you might trip on them and get a boo boo. Juan: Oh I see. (The little girl finishes and goes back to eating her ice cream when Juan pauses and decides to ask her a question of his own.) Juan: Why are you here? Little Girl: What do you mean, why am I here? Juan: Why are you in Mexico at Spring Break, surrounded by a bunch of college students, alcohol, parting, sexual activity, and a bunch of other things you shouldn’t be encountering at your age. Little Girl: It’s because my brother is here. Juan: So that still doesn’t make it right. Why would he come here if he knew he had to bring his little sister? Little Girl: Oh, he doesn’t know. I snuck out of the house and followed him all the way to here. Then once I confronted him, he was stuck with me. Juan: That’s not a good thing to do. Little Girl: Well the lady who is supposed to look after me is really mean and makes me go to bed before I can watch Six Feet Under. (Juan can’t help but laugh after hearing the previous comment.) Juan: Are you trying to tell me that you watch Six Feet Under? Little Girl: Yea, what’s so wrong about that? Juan: Nothing, but it seems a little weird that such a little thing like you watches such a big person show like that. Little Girl: Oh well Nate rules. (Juan nods his head, and just sits back down again. He close his eyes and till hears, the slurping of the girl.) (Juan is about to get up being fed up with the slurping sound, but when he does and looks around, he no longer sees the little girl. All he sees is a pink bow on the bench, with dust over it.) (Juan looks around for the girl and examines the bow for a while before he puts it into his pocket.) Juan wonders what just went on, but doesn’t think much of it. He glances at his watch and notices his watch just hit twelve, meaning he has to go packing for his three o’clock plane. (Juan gets in car and drives back to his hotel, on the way there he notices a familiar looking hospital, and has a sudden flashback.) (Flashback begins.) The flashback begins with Juan being rushed into a hospital. He is put on a bead, and sees people taking his blood pressure and heartbeat. Everyone seems worried, but Juan doesn’t know why, He tries to tell them he’s fine but then seem like they can’t hear him. Juan goes up to every one of them and screams, but nothing is heard. Juan becomes confused until he turns around and sees himself laid out on a stretched. If he’s in one stretcher and walking around at the same time something doesn’t make sense. Juan then notices he can’t feel himself, and comes to the conclusion that he has died. (Juan snaps out of flashback.) (Juan comes out of the flashback, right as he is about to hit another card in the road. He swerves out of the way in the nick of time, and avoids all cars. The car beeps at Juan, as Juan goes off to the site.) (Juan takes some time to regroup himself as he continues to drive until he reaches his hotel. Once at his hotel, Juan feels a little out of it, barely able to walk. He tries to make it back to his room, but collapses there in the ground.) (Flashback begins) The flashback occurs again with Juan realizing he is dead. Juan stands there crying and screaming, not wanting to be dead. He thinks back to the little drops of LSD that graced his skin and how they ruined his entire life. As Juan sobs by himself, he feels something tap him on the shoulder. When he turns around he sees his mother standing right behind him. Juan: MOM! You’re here. What are you doing over here, and how come I can actually see you? Mother: I’m dead dear Juan. That’s why you can see me. Juan: I almost forgot. Now that I’ dead I can be with you forever. That’s all I ever wanted, was to be with you. That’s all I really need. (Juan grabs on to his mothers leg crying, but his mother pry’s him off.) Juan: What was that for? Mother: Juan, you can’t be with me. Juan: Why not? Mother: It’s not you’re time yet you have to go back. Juan grabs back on to his mother and gets hysterical. Juan: No mom, I’m finally with you, I don’t want to go back and leave. Why do I have to do this why? Mother: It’s the way God intended. Promise me, you’ll never want to end your life or make nothing of it. Life is a wonderful thing I wish I still had. While still crying and sniffling Juan responds. Juan: All right mom, I will. With that said, Juan’s mother gives him a kiss on his head, ad the scene goes white. (Flashback ends) Juan wakes up on the alley of the hotel, with the janitor fanning him off. Juan slowly gets up as the Mexican janitor mumbles words in Spanish. Juan shakes off and goes back to his room. With that the Scene Fades to Black. The Following promo was paid for and publicized by the Golden Heart Chronicles.
