Date: (Turmoil #1) -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-From the pen of the Neo Void God-=-=- =26, October, 2002= It has been quite some time… Almost a year…since our voice has been heard in this realm. Have you missed us? Does reading our dialect make you nostalgic of the not too old days of PCW? She has done our speaking for us… Cyclonica…the Divine Prophetess… Our…daughter. It seems like a foreign concept to deal with…having offspring. Not just one at that, but also the one labeled Anarchy is our daughter as well. The thing about it…we cannot remember who her mother is at all. But…one step at a time…that has been the path of the righteous that we have taken ever since stepping foot into this place called PCW. We know how to bide our time…how to let smaller things pass, only to gain the bigger thing in the future. Because the best thing about being immortal…is that we have all the time in the world. We can wait… Patience is a virtue, as our Divine Prophetess says. Remember Titu Xansiv? The Pharaoh? Of course you do…it was only a week ago that he left this company. How convenient that he left the company after taking a final defeat at our hands in the Mall Brawl. It surprised a lot of you to see him go… But we were not surprised in the least. Ever since that Trinity Match we had, with the worthless Jay Sylo as a referee, everyone believed that Pharaoh had bested us by winning the Television title, and his wife. But they failed to realize the greater prize that we gained out of that match… His soul… Yes, his godhood we earned in that match long ago. And ever since he lost that…he has slowly been spiraling down into the final descent of defeat that was inevitable for him. He has done nothing but doubt his identity since we took his godhood…and if you do not know yourself, how can you hope to know anyone else great enough to defeat them? It was our doing…that in the end…banished Pharaoh from PCW. Not Diablo…he is not worthy of doing anything of the sort. U S At this point…there is no one for us to hate. Our greatest enemy was Pharaoh…and he is gone. There is nothing left for us at this point… But there are others in PCW…why can we not hate them? Because…there is no reason for us to do so… Neophytes are joining the ranks every week…and new people are trying to make a name for themselves in this realm where we have started from the bottom and came to the top on sheer talent alone. We’ve never bothered with people…rivalries outside of personal involvement, all of which were caused by the betrayal of an ally. While people kept themselves in the light by picking fights with other people…we kept ourselves in the spotlight from destroying everyone in our way. One step at a time…one stomp at a time… Now look at us…at the top, the one at the top of the PCW mountain worthy enough to face the champion…the demon, Clyde. The people’s demon…ironic that the people deal with their own demons daily, that one of them is actually their hero. But that is not a bother…Clyde and we know things…things you mortals could never hope to hold within your brains. With inhumanity comes intelligence…one not based on logic, or knowledge…but a chaotic insanity that does not make sense until WE make it make sense. It is our intelligence that keeps us on top of the slower ones in this battle…that is why we cause so much gore single handedly in one night to equal years of war-torn battles. Because…we are monsters… We are feared. Youth fear us…men fear us…women fear us… Kannon…fears us… The owner of this federation…the almighty scientologist… he must cower in our face so that he can stab us in the back. Weak… Nothing more than weak… It is dusk right now, the moon rising over the forested mountains in the horizon. Our table on the balcony overlooks the mountains to our right, and in the distance the sea to the left. We have been in Jahana Castle for about two or three weeks now, since our meeting with the Japanese government. It was quite quaint of them to give us a realm of our own…right next door to our eternal enemy. The only separation between us and Inphino Blitz is a one hundred foot gorge, with rocky waters below, and a rope bridge linking the two sides. But neither our people nor his people ever dare to cross… lest war comes forth from their foolish venture. We have been quite prosperous…gaining a family, a home, and a religion of our own. CODE…the Celestial Order of Divine Existence. Cyclonica is in the process of importing a few of the followers over to the castle as servants…a live in sect right on the grounds. She and Sandra Wong have been organizing the religion from our knowledge. That will build up soon…give it time. Anarchy rests in her room after her ankle injury this past Friday. Her wench…Jenna James…tends to her needs, from food to pleasure. Our daughters have unique tastes in what pleases them…it receives an approving nod of our head. The End…our faithful ally. He wanders the land daily…coming back when he fancies to check on Sandra Wong and make contact with us. His loyalty to the collective in its down time has been tremendous, and it will not go without reward. But End is not just an ally…he is family to us by the dark blood within him. Brother to our daughters…the son we never asked for… Shin Yagyuu…the Celestial Shogun…he was supposed to be our golden-boy. He was to follow in our footsteps of destruction straight to the top…but he was a failure. Failure that early in his existence will end in nothing but tragedy for both him and us…and we will not be brought down by anyone else. No one will keep us from finishing the path we are set to finish. Cyclonica…Anarchy…End…they know the risks of being Celestial…they know the risks of being members of this family. Glacia…Cyclonica’s mother…her presence is not felt often within the castle walls. She is often on her own business…and comes back on her own for short periods of time. Ever since she revealed herself…Cyclonica has been edgy, especially around her. Her breathing grows steady and her heart slows…as if she feels the urge to combat her when her mother is near. Our daughter does not trust her mother… And personally…we are not too comfortable with her as well. Not after the last confrontation we had with her in Haiti. That is the whole Celestial family…Matrix and Sonata have not been seen since we arrived in Japan. We can still feel their essences existing though…they are alive still. It would be wise to keep Sonata living…if the captors value their lives if anything. As for us… We train by day…in the old training room we used to have back when the Void Elements were occupying this castle. The weights are still here…the mirrors lining the stone walls of that circular room…the thick padded stone pillar we used to practice on. The Hand of Fate was perfected from relentless days of nothing more than repeated striking on this thinly padded pillar with our bare knuckled hand. Even gods can grow…train up themselves to be more powerful than before. No one is above advancement. During night…we sit on the balcony of our spacious bedroom and stare up at the moon. Sometimes we hear things…Anarchy and her woman pleasing each other…Wong speaking with End either on phone or in person. Cyclonica is silent as we are during the hours of the night…her voice most of the time in her mind like ours are. But we can still hear her… Sometimes we can hear her when she dreams…having visions of destruction or tranquility…they are never the same whenever they happen. Besides that…we can hear Inphino Blitz… Alone…meditating in his training hall…trying to keep his soul at balance with the Aura and the Void within him. He tries too much to be something he is not…which is in control. Finally, we hear pain and suffering rising off from the hearts of men in this world. It floats on the wind, with gusts of cries and wisps of sorrow. As much as people consider themselves content with this world…satisfied with what their lives are now…there will always be a voice inside of them screaming for release from this torturous realm. We…are the one to answer those prayers. Kannon had the audacity to book us in a tag match with him for Turmoil…against Rent A Hero and Darkstar. Like we would not remember his underhandedness at the mall…like all is forgiven. We are not a forgiving god…ask all of the people we have banished from PCW because they betrayed our trust. Go find Pharaoh and ask him personally. Does he think having the aid of the Neo Void God will aid him in his retribution against his enemies? …He will find out for himself how assisting we can be …
