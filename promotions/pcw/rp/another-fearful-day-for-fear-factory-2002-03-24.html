Another Fearful Day for Fear Factory

24 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/24/02 CLICK! CLICK! CLICK! (The sound of typing can be heard.) CLICK! (The scene slowly fades in to show the cluttered office of the local police chief, with the chief himself sitting in his black chair in front of his extremely messy desk. Sitting in wooden chairs in front of the desk are Mason Creeper and Rudolph von Hackenmann. The chief is looking at his computer, and after about ten seconds, he looks up at Rudolph and Mason.) Chief: That body you found in your house... It was identified as the body of Samuel Maresta. He was a worker at that shoe factory. He's been dead for eightteen years! Rudolph: Did you ever hear of him before his death? Say, he came in and told you something about the factory? (The chief looks confused.) Chief: How did you know about that? Rudolph: I read the diary of the owner. He said someone in the factory turned him in for some secret activities. Chief: Yeah. He was doing cruel scientific experiments on living people in the hidden basement of his factory. He tried to control them with weird shoes! Rudolph: Well, I know who killed him. (The chief chuckles a bit.) Chief: Oh, we've already captured him. He was put in jail for life, the sick bastard... (Rudolph smiles.) Rudolph: That's good. Thank you for your time, sir. Chief: Hey! No problem! (Rudolph and Mason get up out of their chairs and walk out of the room.) (The scene quickly jumps to a large prison, with tons of small doors, and the pale heads of high felons pressed against the glass. Into the large room walks an enormous black man, with very big muscles and a permanent frown wiped across his sweat-stained face. As he walks through, all the felons in their rooms open a six inch wide slot in their door, and the large man slips in a small carton of milk and a covered bowl of cereal. When the man gets to the last door, a large clunking sound is heard on the door. A pale man with sunken, bloodshot eyes then presses his face against the glass.) Guard: What the hell is this? (Through the glass, the pale face mouths something. He is saying he can't get his slot open. Thinking that, with handcuffs and shackles on, the man will do no harm, the guard slowly inserts his key into the door and opens the door. The pale man is dressed in an orange suit, and his hands are secured behind his back. The guard sits the food down on the floor.) Guard: There ya go. (A sinister grin comes across the face of the convicted felon. He headbutts the man in the throat and stumbles him. The guard grabs the pale man and slams him against the wall, pulling his gun to his head.) Guard: Don't move! You don't know how much I would love to kill you now, so don't tempt me! (Once again, a sinister grin comes across the face of the felon. He jerks his head forward, headbutting the man in the throat four more times. The guard drops to the floor, gasping for air. He sits down next to the man and fumbles his hands around behind his back, trying to grab the keys. Finally, he unlocks himself and puts his hands in front of him. He then grabs the gun from the guard and shoots him. He then laughs, and talks to himself in a gravelly voice.) Criminal: Well, that was easier than I thought... (The criminal gets up off the floor and opens the door to exit the cell chamber. After a few muffled gunshots are heard, the scene quickly jumps back to the office of the police chief. The white phone on his desk suddenly starts to ring. He picks it up after two rings and listens. His face obtains a look of utter horror as he listens.) Chief: What?! The shoe killer escaped?! Someone call the FBI! (The police chief runs out of his office and into the main room. There are tons of cluttered desks and many police officers. He starts to yell!) Chief: GET ALL OF THE MEN IN THIS DAMN STATION OUT THERE! THE SHOE KILLER HAS ESCAPED! I WANT HIM KILLED ON SIGHT! GRAB YOUR DAMN RIFLES! (All the officers in the room scramble for their jackets and they run to the weapon storage room. They come out, each with a rifle in hand, and run out the exit. The scene slowly fades away, and the hunt for the Shoe Killer begins...)
