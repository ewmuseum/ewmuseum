C I V I L..W A R(PPV #3-Enmity)

Date: ... The eyes of fifteen thousand dead men...marching against the gallops of ten thousand horses. Row after row...breath after breath...man after man. The marching echoed throughout the night sky as the armies stained the ground below, with sweat...tears...and blood. The overcast was dark and gloomy while the chill in the air was stinging and merciless. One army...defending the heritage and tradition of it’s land. The other... was fighting for the new era...the modern day. Conservatives... Against... The liberals... “This is so pointless...” I said aloud resting my forehead on the table. One day away from Red October and my heart is still not where it needs to be. My head...my mind...is still free and ever wandering. Both armies...of the same land...the same blood...but torn by politics and culture. Instead of joining together to fight the perils and anguish both face...they fight one another for a dominant grip on the people... “This whole PCW-PURE mess is nothing more than a mere civil war.” I’ve noticed that every time I’m confused or perplexed, I carry conversations with myself. One army will stand...while the other shall fall. One will stay atop the ground...the other shall be buried below its soil... “Hey, E!” He called storming into the room. He looked around and spotted me in the wooden chair and made his way over. He put is hand on my shoulder, “how you feeling?” “Oh hey, Jamar.” I said looking up at him. He was wearing black, baggy jeans and a PURE tee shirt on...not very presidential like. “You alright, man?” He asked. “Something bothering you?” “Um...well...” I was at a loss of words because I was really out of it. I had stayed up all night pondering about the pay-per-view that lies ahead. “This whole PCW versus PURE ordeal...” “Yeah?” He pulled a seat away from the bed and slid up next to me. He picked up the book I had been reading and nudged me. “This whole thing is bullshit. I mean, it’s pointless. Why are we facing Primetime anyway?” I took the book back and threw it under the bed. “I don’t feel like going into an explanation with you, E. You’re still going to show up and fight for PURE, though...right?” He was getting nervous, I could tell. “...” I didn’t answer him. “Don’t even fucking tell me you’re having second thoughts!” He shouted slamming his hand against the table. “I never said that. Calm down...” I didn’t want him to get upset, but I can’t say I blame him. “Even though I showed up at Fusion...my loyalty lies with PURE.” “I’m glad to hear that...” “You’ve been like a brother to me, Jamar. For that I thank you...and for that...my loyalty lies with you.” I told him, staring at the desk...not wanting to look him straight in the eyes. “Thanks, man. Sooo...we’re cool?” He asked removing himself from my side. “Yeah...we’re cool.” We shook hands and he left the room. When he closed the door I noticed he left a card on the table. Being the jackass that he is...it was a PURE business card. I picked it up, eyed it, tore it into two pieces, and chucked it into the wastebasket. “I...d...i...o...t...” The generals headed their respective packs and eyed one another. They raised their swords...pointed the shimmering blades at their opposition...and as it stands... The... Civil... War... Has... Begun...
