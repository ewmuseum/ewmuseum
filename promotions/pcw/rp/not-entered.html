Date: not entered [I] Scene opens at a hotel near the Saddledome, site of this week's Sabotage. The Warden and company are preparing for LeBlanc's Extreme title fight with Hank and Jason Core. [/I] Warden: I've never been more ready to get to ringside. This Sunday will be our night. Security Chief: You said it. LeBlanc is due. I think that week of training will really make a world of diffence. Warden: Yeah, can you believe that reporter? Questioning LeBlanc's ability? Hell, we're gonna turn this sonofabitch into a wrestler if it's the last thing we do. Security Chief: Hey, teach me that move you were showing LeBlanc. Did you use that when you were wrestling in college? Warden: Yep, that's the move that won me the NCAA title. LeBlanc hasn't perfected it quite yet, but don't worry, there's still time. Security Chief: Jason Core and Hank... man these guys are no match... Warden: Wait a minute now, those guys are trained wrestlers. Don't cross them off. LeBlanc's a bad ass, a killer... but his not polished. You've seen what been happening. They're outsmarting him. There's a huge differnce between street smarts and ring smarts. Security Cheif: Yeah, but he'll get it. There's no substitute for his strength or attitute. That stuff goes a long way. I have no doubt that it will be enough to destroy Hank and Jason Core. That stuff you taught him last week back at Amaranth will be more than enough to bring home the title. Warden: Well, that's all LeBlanc is focused on right now. He's determined to bring back the gold. Once the Extreme belt is around his waist, we crank up the training sessions about 10 notches. I'm telling you, you give me six months and I'll get this man a major title. Security Chief: Do you think he can make it to the top of PCW without talking? You know entertainment is huge part of wrestling success. Warden: Don't you worry about LeBlanc. I'm working on him. He'll come around when the time's right. For now, his actions speak louder than any words. Hell, I don't want him to talk. It's too distracting. All he needs to focus on is getting that Extreme title... and he'll do it Sunday night. [/I] Scene fades as The Warden slams his pen down and walks out of the room. [I]
