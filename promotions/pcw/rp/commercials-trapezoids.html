Title: Commercials + Trapezoids= ????? Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: not entered The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid is on television a lot. He battles in wrestling matches. He has EVERY WAKING HOUR of his life open to PCW cameras. Yet, he has one great weakness in front of the bright lights... He's not very good at selling a product. Yes, the HIT, master of like eight million moves and many a verbal jest isn't good at commercials. Still, Mountain Dew has brought him in to do a promotion for their drink, as he will represent the popular beverage in the Alamo soda contract match. Are the executives nervous? Absolutely they are. But, then again....there is no reward without risk.... ::A group of nervous advertising executives sit in a room, about to ask the new star of their commercial about what he can do in front of the camera. Right on time, The HIT walks in...as the group eyes him carefully. The HIT: What's wrong, guys? Executive #1: Sit down, Mr. Trapezoid....sit down.. ::The HIT follows instructions, looking over at the executives....:: Executive #2: Well, we've heard of your relative inability to shill things, so, do you have any suggestions as to how we can make a commercial with it not sucking? The HIT: Well, I'm good at teaching, wrestling, and rapping. As long as the commercial involves some mixture of those three things, I'm fine. Executive #3: Hmmm....we're not used to ads that involved. Couldn't we just have him stand in front of a tarp and talk? Executive #2: Err, no. Remember, we want this commercial to SELL SODA, not encourage people NOT TO BUY IT! No, our best bet is to go along with the Trapezoid's idea.. Executive #4: You mean? Executive #1: Yes. We have no choice.....go make us a classroom set! Executive #4: Aye, sir... ::The Executive quickly pulls out his cell phone, dialing a number.:: Executive #4: Yes, Creative Department? Make us a classroom set, pronto! ::Random unintelligble gibberish follows, as the Executive nods: Executive #4: Good. :The phone is turned off, as the Executive grins.:: Executive #4: The classroom set will be ready shortly. Any ideas, Mr. Trapezoid? The HIT: Actually, yes. I'll need a bunch of teenage extras.....a good sound system, and other fun stuff... Executive #1: Fine. Meet us down at the set in five, you have full.....:Sigh:.....creative control.. **FIVE MINUTES LATER** Executive #1: I can't believe we gave this idiot creative control. Executive #2: Neither can I, but he's popular, so we can probably get lots of stuff out of it. Executive #3: Indeed. Let's just see what idea he has. ALRIGHT EVERYONE! TRAPEZOID COMMERCIAL....TAKE ONE! ::A generic rap track begins playing in the background, as Trapezoid sits in the front of the classroom set, looking out at the teenage extras.:: Teenage Extra #1: Man, I'm thirsty! Factoring cubic equations ALWAYS gets me ready for a good drink! ::The other students nod concurringly, as The HIT begins to show off some rhythm, or at least, what he percieves to be rhythm. He performs a series of dance steps, and then breaks into song.:: The HIT: When you've got cubic equations gettin' you down... Turn your facial parabola away from a frown! No reason to feel sad or blue......all you need is to DO THE DEW! ::The horrible rapping done, the students rise, and proceed to put Trapezoid to shame with a well-choreographed dance number. Trapezoid busts out the Trapezoid dance on top of his desk, as Mountain Dew cans parachute down from the ceiling. Trapezoid grabs two cans of the Dew, and pops the top, somersaulting off the desk. He of course spills it everywhere, and, as he crashes the cans together, they explode further, covering The HIT into the carbonated beverage.:: The HIT: MAD PROPS TO MOUNTAIN DEW, YO!! The commercial ends, as the Executives look at each other, HIT smiling expectantly.:: The HIT: Well? How was it? Executive #1: It....well.......what did you guys think? Executive #3 looks on sadly, realizing he will have to tell HIT the sad news. Executive #3: It......well, sucked. The HIT: It did? Executive #3: Yeah...but don't worry man! The HIT: I HAVE to worry! MY COMMERCIAL SUCKED! HOW AM I GOING TO PROMOTE THE IMAGE OF MOUNTAIN DEW IF I CAN'T EVEN DO A GOOD COMMERCIAL! Executive #2: We can have other people do the commercial, or use clips of your matches! All you have to do is win that Soda Contract match, and we can plaster your image all over the soda cans! You have four matches at Alliances. Even if you don't win the contract, our product will be getting a hell of a lot of free exposure! Just do your best...it's all we can ask. The HIT: Thanks.....I'm going to go prepare. Executive #1: Take care, and good luck. ::Trapezoid walks out of the room, headed back outside to the waiting schoolbus. He pauses for a moment, just a moment, to sulk.:: The HIT: Well, I've finally found what I'm not good at. Commercials. Ah well, got lots of other things to worry about. Alliances Match. Tag Titles. Sodas. And Tim Walden, in that order. Walden's a joke that just keeps managing to get more and more comic. As he wallows in his own self-pity, he gets funnier.....it's like that one goofy kid in class. You KNOW its wrong to point and stare, but you just can't help it! Walden is that kid.....a joke his entire career. A joke he wants to end at Alliances.....to deliver the punchline by capturing the Cruiserweight Championship and moving on to better things, but, sadly for poor Tim, I'm there again. There to shatter his dreams of glory! As I did with the Television Title....Walden will fall before Parallel Bases II. And then, as he looks up, broken, he will see me ascending the ladder to the title he's wanted so long. Tim Walden......your reign of terror as PCW taxman will fall down yet another rung as I ascend the rungs in the middle of the ring. I'M GONNA BEAT YOU DOWN! RIGHT INTO THE GRASS! I'M GONNA SMACK YOU ROUND! I'M GONNA KICK YO' ASS! ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
