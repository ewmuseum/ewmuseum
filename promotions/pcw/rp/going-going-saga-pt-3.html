Going going... (Saga pt.3)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th December Deuce 00 Deuce § The three fighters where sitting around Ben's living room listening to his plan. 'As I was saying, after Kai and I left Master Cronin's dojo, we were always on the road much like Kai is doing to this day.' Ben explained. 'I want to be able to do that again for one last time, before I won't be able to do that anymore. My girlfriend Eliza is in Europe with her family for a year, and when she gets back I plan on asking her to marry me.' 'YOU’RE GOING TO GET MARRIED?' Dan exclaimed. 'Man, what a mistake! You have the car, the money, maybe not my good looks, but the car man! You can have any women you want anytime! Why settle for one? I'll show you how easy it is, can I borrow the car tonight?' 'Dan, it is something you probably will never understand, I love Eliza. That's whom I want to spend the rest of my life with.” Ben said. 'And NO! For the last time you can't take the Porsche out.' Dan was grumbling to himself when Kai spoke up. 'Yes those are fond memories Ben. I treasure them above all else. Where do you have in mind to start out at?' 'Well, I was hoping you would have a good idea of where to start from. I can fly us to anywhere you want.' 'Before I got your letter, I was thinking about going to Hong Kong, from some of the fighters I have run across, they heard of some pretty tough fighters there.' Kai said. 'OK! Then we have a destination. Dad wants me to go with him today to some function the Thompson Foundation is hosting, but after that we can leave whenever.' 'Cool! I always wanted to go to Hong Kong!' Dan said jumping up from the chair. 'They always make a lot of fighting movies there. They will obviously see my talent and beg me to do a movie.' He said with a big smile. 'Uh, yeah Dan. That COULD happen.' Ben said shaking his head. The phone rang just then and Ben answered it. 'Ok, on my way down. That's Dad, he is outside in the limo, and I must go I am late as it is. I'll see you guys tonight.' After Ben walked out the door, Dan looked at Kai, 'Well, what do you want to do? We can go cruise the town.' 'No thank you, the flight was long and I feel like going for a walk and maybe doing a little training before I get back on another long flight.' Kai explained. 'Okay, your loss then.' Dan said getting up going over to the counter and picking up a set of keys and heading out the door. Kai found his way to the beach; which was about a mile away. He walked along the beach, hardly even noticing the hot sand against his bare feet. The Shaolin Warrior was surprised at the number of over zealous women there that came up to him and asked if he would like to put lotion on there backs, while giving him a sly gaze. After quickly declining and moving away from the public part of the beach, Kai found the perfect quiet spot to meditate. It was in the middle of his meditation that he suddenly felt it. A presence of pure evil was near him. 'Let the power flow out!' someone said with a sinister voice. 'Claim your birthright! Others seek to harness your power for there own plans.' Kai's eyes shot open and he quickly scanned the area. No one was there. The presence was gone... On the other hand, maybe not even there to begin with. Kai held his head low trying to figure out what had just happened. 'Maybe it was the jet lag,' he thought to himself. 'I haven't had a good night's rest in a few days.' he tried to convince himself that it was just his imagination. It wasn't until late in the day that Kai finally made his way back to Ben's house. He was about to head up to the door when he saw it fly open and Ben came storming out. 'I can't believe he would be stupid enough to do this!' Ben was shouting. 'What's wrong Ben?' Kai asked. 'That idiot took my car!' Ben said as he was opening the garage door. 'Go on ahead inside Kai, I'm gonna take my motorcycle and go look for him. If he so much as puts a scratch on it....' Ben threatened. Across town... 'Come on baby! Wanna ride with a real man and pick up some chow?' Dan called to the girl who was walking down the street. 'Quit bugging me and get LOST!' she called out from the sidewalk. Dan pulled up the car to the curb. 'MMMM', he thought to himself 'Why don't any of these chicks want to take a ride with a guy like me?' 'Did I mention I own a condo...OWWWW?!' Dan screamed as his left ear was yanked rather hard. 'Shut the car off and get out so I don't have to clean any blood off of my dash board.' Ben said between clenched teeth. 'Ben! Hey, I was just on my way to wash your car. Honest! I didn't do anything to it. OWW...OWW.... OWWW!' Dan screamed as Ben led him out of the car by his ear. 'Give me one good reason not to kill you right here!' Ben growled as he threw Dan to the street. 'Because of all the witnesses!' Dan screamed as he cowered before Ben. 'They would consider it as self defence' Ben said as he was inspecting his car. 'Ok, I'll let you live awhile longer, only because I am such a nice guy. Now you WILL drive straight back to my place.... UNDERSTOOD?' Glared Ben. 'Yes' Dan mumbled. Dan walked back into the living room holding his head as Ben smacked him, ' Go sit down and say nothing.' Ben ordered. He looked over to Kai who was looking out of the window. 'You okay?' Ben asked as he walked up behind him. 'Yeah, it's nothing' Kai answered back. 'Okay, well let's get packed, our flight for Hong Kong leaves in 6 hours.' §
