The Sage of A Champion: The Interlude Part 4

15 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-15-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!The Interlude Part 4” Chronicles!Chronicles!Chronicles! Chapter 2: Metal Armor? :: The scene picks up after a lot of information was given to form up a lot of questions that need to be answered. Everything from questions of time travel to a person or persons that are way too advanced for his or her times was brought up last time. Maybe if we are lucky we are going to be able to get some of those answers as we speak, but only time can tell. We still don’t even know who wrote this letter or how much longer it is going to last, but the longer it lasts the better it gets and the more information that is revealed, so hopefully the letter won’t end until every single question we can think of is answered. :: It scares me to think that a person could be this smart because if there is a person alive today that is this smart just think of the possibly of this guy. I mean there is probably no end to the terror he could cause if he has the knowledge that can create a machine that can blow up a country the size of the U.S. By the way, just on a side note, nothing in this letter must be given out to the press. Nothing in this letter can be printed anywhere, so be careful when showing this letter to different people because we can’t be having the wrong people with this letter, but you probably knew that in the first place. Anyway, back to the story. Mr. Unknown had a couple of letters hidden somewhere. If you read the book there are sections of the book that hint places where he has hidden letters. There is no telling if the letters are still in place, but they basically contain secretes he never revealed in his “How to do” book. The “Book of Metal Armor” is 3,470 pages long. You would think that in a book that long everything from the basics to the machines secretes would be revealed, but he has a couple (which hopefully only means two) of letter that is hidden in a couple (once again hopefully only meaning two) places in the world. These letters are described to be about four pages long each with information that are just secretes, so without these two letters you can build and run Metal Armor, but with them you can make the machine two times stronger. I have no clue where to find these letters, but for almost a year now Russia has been on the search for these letters with big money rewards for anyone that finds the letters. And just to make sure that the letter isn’t fake, it has special coding inside of it that basically “marks” it is what Mr. Unknown wrote, and only Russia officers know of these “marks” to look for once someone gives them the letter. I am not sure what the money reward is, but I heard it is so high that you basically won’t ever have money issues again in your life with the money you receive, so Russia is even in worse shape than before right now because everyone and their dog is in search of these letters. The chances of a civilian finding the letters are slim to none because they are hidden that good, but civilians are still taking that slim to none chance to it fullest and I wouldn’t blame them because a chance is a chance and this chance is worth pretty much all the money in the world that they will ever need. I am not going to waste my time searching because as long as I have enough money to survive and a little left over for things here and there I am happy. I don’t like being a greedy person especially with something like this were chances of winning aren’t really even chances if you know what I mean. I wouldn’t suggest anyone go on this “letter hunt,” yet a lot of people have and a lot of people have been asking me why I am not on the hurt. Believe it or not, some business have shutdown for a while in hope that one of their employees can find the letter and get the big bucks for it. It is really the stupidest thing I have ever heard, but Russia doesn’t just want to get back at the U.S., they also want to just put the U.S. out of commission and that is what it looks like is going to happen unless someone stops Metal Armor. The worst thing about Metal Armor is that the thing is so huge and has so many pounds of steel built on it that it would probably take thousands of rockets from rocket launchers to just knock the thing down. I doubt that one nuclear bomb from the U.S. would be enough to destroy the thing. That is how huge and well built it is. Commander Fuller: MG! Why that is insane!! General Powers: That can’t be possible commander! It just can’t be possible! Commander Fuller: I wouldn’t take any chances, but it does sound impossible that Russia could make something that strong. I bet one nuke on top of it would at least knock the thing down and put it out, just not destroy it completely, but that would mean it could still be in use. General Powers: That is just… insane. Point blank. That is just ungodly. We are going to be putting a 14-year-old boy up against something that is so ungodly… I mean… I am not sure about all of this, commander. I am just not sure. It would think that because it is this strong that it would move slow, but it moves at a walking pace which is very surprising and shocking for a machine this heavy and how it is able to do so I am not sure, but in the facility it is built in I have seen it walk around. Commander Fuller: That isn’t good. That means that Russia is probably just waiting for secrete letters and Russia will be ready to attack. I am just going to take a wild guess and just say that they are waiting for the letters, but other than that the machine is ready to go. I am not exactly sure how they are going to get the thing out of the building though. I think that is one of the problems because I think they are going to have to knock out a whole a little bit over two stories high in order to get Metal Armor out of the building. No air plane or helicopter known to man could let Metal Armor out of the building, so taking out a walk and just letting it walk out of the building is their only choice of action for getting Metal Armor out in the open, so I am guessing that is why I am beginning to see the top of the building slowly being taken a part. This process is going to take a while, but before the year is over they will have Metal Armor out and running. They are getting the best guys money could buy to safely, without harming anyone, knocking out two stories worth of this building to get Metal Armor out. It is a fast, but safe job. Most people would take about six months to get this job down and these guys are talking about having the job done in three months. General Powers: So I guess that means, we have three months to stop Metal Armor. That is a good thing. Commander Fuller: Yea. It doesn’t give us too much time for any types of delays, but the time is a lot longer than I expected. I was thinking around six weeks time to get this all done with, but with about twelve weeks, seeing as we have double the time I expected I think we can get this mission accomplished. General Powers: But BladeD sir… Commander Fuller: He is the best man we have and unless you find someone better than BladeD, which I doubt you will, he is going to be the man… I mean boy… kid… whatever… to save the world. General Powers: :: grabs a list of the roster of people in the NMA. :: I could randomly, without looking, point my finger at a name and that person would be a lot better than BladeD. I know that for… :: Commander Fuller stops him. :: Commander Fuller: You know that for nothing. I have been watching these men since they entered and some just entered the NMA. After seeing what BladeD as done at only 14-years-old I know he is tons better than anyone we have right now because I am telling you that, that kid is a one in a million. He is going to be our hero whether you like it or not general. General Powers: If that is way it is going to be, then that is the way it is going to be and I am not going to argue anymore. Commander Fuller: Good. We don’t have time anyways to find anyone else for the job when we know BladeD will do it for sure. General Powers: How do you know that for sure? Commander Fuller: We can offer the kid tons of money or something I don’t know. He is only 14-years-old, so I know we can find a way to bribe him into doing this job for us. It isn’t going to be hard. Even when we tell him that he will be risking his life. We will be able to bribe him no problem. :: Scene cut and man that is wrong. They are going to take advantage of a 14-year-old boy like that, but I guess you have to do whatever it takes when you know the world is at risk, but still. I just don’t like that idea because it just seems wrong. Maybe you guys feel different, but maybe BladeD will want to do it just to become a hero. They may just use that to bribe him and he will want to do it even knowing his life is on the line, he will probably say he doesn’t care and that he just wants to do whatever it takes to be a hero. I guess you will just stay tuned and find out. ::
