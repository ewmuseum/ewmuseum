Preparing for War...

Date: Today...dumb mortal... Only one man could draw the entire hatred and spite of a single country in one night. Gaining the hatred of the entire world was a different thing…that at least took a couple weeks. But Japan… …All they needed was one night. That fateful night where Akiyama, their PCW golden boy…their country ambassador to Primetime Championship Wrestling…all they needed was for him to slip up just once in front of them… And he would be forever banished from the country. That night…Timo Bolamba managed to get the three seconds on the god…and all the hearts of the people that were in full support of the god dropped at once. The fans in the stadium at Tokyo…the fans at home across the country…and most importantly…Akiyama’s family. It was they who suffered the hardest of all. Mr. And Mrs. Akiyama…they managed to disappear into obscurity after that night…they never returned to their home, where a mob of unruly Japanese were waiting for them to shun their disgraced family name. And Ayumi Hamasaki…the cousin of the god…she paid a hefty price as a celebrity for being involved in the entire country’s televised dishonor. …Charity drives… -=-=-=-=- “Yes…thank you for calling the Nakamoto Foundation. Your donation is greatly appreciated…” Ayu sat there with a bored face, opposite to the cheerful one she kept there 24/7. She was dressed in a white tank top and blue jeans as she sat in that cubicle on the telephone. “What is this charity for?” she looked away and sighed heavily. “Children with chronic diarrhea.” Just then, a fleet of toilets flushed in the background. Ayu looked up and sighed again before going back to the phone. “How much to wish to donate, sir? …Hello?” Another drop out…she placed the phone down and placed her head in her hands. “Crap.” Just then, the toilets flushed again. She looked up slightly before sinking her head back into her hands in shame. -=-=-=-=- Akiyama was banished from his home country…the only place he had ever lived. Since then, he has wandered the world on the back of PCW, blazing a path of destruction in each city, country, and continent until the day came back when the Neo Void God would once again set his sight back on the land that abandoned him…his former home… But they were preparing for him…they knew he was coming on the day of the PCW PPV… and if he thought he could walk back into the country just like that…even just for a few nights…then he had another thing coming. -=-=-=-=- “Let’s go men!! We need to get these barricades set up as quickly as possible!! Make sure they are impregnable!!” In Narita airport, about 60 kilometers from downtown Tokyo, dozens of heavily armed soldiers from the Japanese National Defense Force marched briskly along the exterior of the structure. Roadblocks were formed in all possible outlets of the airport, and inside there were ten guards at every gate, equipped with assault rifles and vicious dogs snarling at the mouth. The commander stood amongst the scattering soldiers barking orders in all directions. A tourist walked up to him and politely asked him what was going on. “Martial law!” he snapped gruffly. “We have a national threat potentially trying to get into the country, specifically this city! If we catch sight of him…he’s either going to end up in jail…or dead.” He turned away to speak in his radio. “Are those checkpoints set up around the city limits?? I don’t want him sneaking in any other way! And get some men down to the docks in case he wants to come by boat!!” Clipping the radio back to his belt, he folded his arms across his broad chest and nodded. “That ruffian isn’t getting in on my watch…” -=-=-=-=- Akiyama was all over the Japanese news, with protestors lining up in almost every city and town complaining of the nVg’s return to the country. The bounty on his head has tripled…now if he’s even seen in the boundaries of Japan, dead or alive, he’s worth 9.9 million dollars. Yep…Japan despises the Neo Void God… Now they’re not so much outcasts anymore from the rest of the world. But do you think Akiyama is going to stand around and let some country stop him from his ultimate goal? No frickin way… And why is Akiyama set to fight Miyagawa? Because they’re of Japanese heritage? That’s false… The day his country turned his back on him…he turned his back on his heritage. So what if he looks the part…the god is less Japanese now than Rush Mason. You want to put a label on Akiyama… You call him… C E L E S T I A L Friday night… …Akiyama deals the first blow in his destruction of a nation…
