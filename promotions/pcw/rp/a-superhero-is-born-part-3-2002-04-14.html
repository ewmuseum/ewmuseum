A Superhero Is Born Part 3

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 14th, 2002 (Mike Frampton is seen sitting in a chair typing on a typewriter. He is wearing a pair of black glasses and a suit. He pulls out a sheet of paper that reads at the very top 'The Daily Planet'. Mike reads over his work and smiles. Suddenly he stands up. He checks to see if anyone is around. He removes his glasses and tears off his suit revealing his Superman costume. Mike then storms out of the room as the scene fades to black. A moment later the scene fades back in to Mike Frampton who is running down a street. He stops at a bank where four men are inside with automatic Shotguns trying to get money. The police are outside the bank trying to come up with a plan to rescue the hostages. Mike walks over to the Chief of Police, Mike Hawk.) Mike Hawk: Who the devil are you? Mike Frampton: The name's Superman. Mike Hawk: Superman? Mike Frampton: Yes Superman. What's the situation Chief? Mike Hawk: Superman? Mike Frampton: Damn it man what's the situation? (The police chief stares at Mike. He reaches down and pulls out his revolver. He points it at Mike. Mike looks at it with a smile.) Mike Hawk: Never say that to me again comprende? Mike Frampton: Hehe, a gun eh? Don't you know I am faster than a speeding bullet? (Mike Hawk stares blankly at Mike. He pulls back the hammer on the gun.) Mike Hawk: Get out of here or I shoot! Mike Frampton: Yoink. Mike Hawk: Yoink? (Mike grabs the gun and pulls it out of Mike Hawk's grip. Frampton tosses the gun into the air. He catches it and bends the barrel back. He hands it back to Mike Hawk.) Mike Frampton: Nice toy. (Mike Hawk's face turns red. He takes the gun and tosses it into his car. He reaches in and pulls out a shotgun. He points it at Frampton.) Mike Hawk: So you wanna be a hero eh? Mike Frampton: Wanna be? I already am. Mike Hawk: Why don't you go face those four armed men inside the bank. Mike Frampton: You mean they have four arms? OH MY GOD! Mike Hawk: No, no, they have shotguns. They are armed and dangerous. Mike Frampton: So am I. (Mike rubs his arms.) Mike Frampton: I am definitely armed. Mike Hawk: Damn it man go do something. (Mike looks inside the bank. Two men are watching the hostages while the other two keep their guns pointed at the police.) Mike Frampton: This looks like a job for SUPERMAN! (Mike charges towards the bank. The robbers fire. Mike drops down and continues with a commando crawl. He then hops back up and jumps through the hole in the bank window. One of the men charges at Mike and takes a swing with his gun. Mike ducks and points him in the eye. The other man shoots at Mike. Mike pulls one of the robbers infront of him and he is hit with the shotgun shell. Blood splatters up. Mike tosses the man down.) Mike Frampton: I never knew a holdup could get so holy. (Mike picks up the shotgun. He throws it out the window. The gun fires and hits one of the robbers.) Mike Frampton: That had to hurt. (One of the last two robbers fires at Mike. Mike leaps over the shot. Mike rolls and spears the man. He then pulls off the robbers belt and puts it around his neck. Mike then tugs on the belt and throws the man out the window.) Mike Frampton: Now it's time to go one on one with the great one. Now where have I heard that before? (The robber charges at Mike and tries to take him down with a right hand. Mike catches it and head butts the robber. Mike grabs the robber by the back of the neck and runs with him. Mike tosses him through a plate glass window. The police round up the robbers. The hostages exit the bank. Mike walks out to the crowd of police. Mike Hawk walks up to Frampton.) Mike Hawk: How in the hell did you do that? Mike Frampton: Let's just say I had a little help. From the big man. He was watching down on me tonight. I owe it all to him. Mike Hawk: God? Mike Frampton: Well him too, I was referring to Michael Graves! Mike Hawk: What? Mike Frampton: Now I must leave you. To infinity and beyond! Mike Hawk: Isn't that Buzz Lightyear? Mike Frampton: Meh who cares. (Mike runs off. Suddenly a police car runs him over as the scene fades out.)
