RAH In High School Part 3 of Many-Impressing Your Dream Girl

18 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: RAH In High School Part 3 of Many-Impressing Your Dream Girl Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 8-18-2002 'So there I was...confronted with the woman that made Lois Lane look like a low-budget Ursula. My past conversations had no effect of impressing her, I mean 'um, oh my god, yowza, and dead silence' hardly qualify for good conversation. This was my chance to actually impress the girl with my charm, physique, and UNCANNY charisma...I think I did a pretty good job myself, but don't take my word for it.'..::/::..We return in the middle of our PCW superhero's recollection of his high school days, smack dab in the middle of his first 'real' conversation with his high school crush...the beauteous Erica Angel. When we last left off, she was walking slowly towards our young hero then known as Jayson to his peers...now he is right before her, the siren cradling her books protectively to her chest as she looks at Jayson, her coffee brown eyes melting him where he stands as he taks in the sight of her....the perfect dream girl across the street. As the wind continues to breeze through her flowing locks of dark brown hair, she sweetly greets our hormone-powered hero, her voice soothing to the ears and pleasent to take in..::/::.. Erica:'Hi, Jayson.' ..::/::..Suddenly, Jayson sweeps her off her feet, cradling her in his strong arms as her books plummet to the lush grass below them. She is shocked by the sudden action, her hands gripping the ripped shoulders of our hero as he stares deep within her eyes, piercing them with his very own sea blue eyes as he says to her in the most debonair voice this side of a cheap romance novel..::/::.. Jayson:'Mi amor...how I have waited so long to hold you within my arms like this. Let us stop denying out burning passion for one another my sweet wild flower, hidden within the weeds of the world. Let me take you away to a world where we may make love as much as we please as I whisper sweet nothings in your ear and feed you the sweetest of fruit from my hands...'..::/::..Erica has completely given into this cheesy romanticism, sighing and not daring to fight the loving embrace of Jayson as she lets out a sigh of utter pleasure, looking deep into his eyes and trying to fill complete his sentence..::/::..Erica:'Is this strange land the land of 'ecstacy' my muscle bound hunk of heroic love?' Jayson:'Well...it actually was the backseat of my dad's station wagon...but just close your eyes and let me radiate you with my rays of loooooooooooove...and you shall see it..' Erica:'GOD....say 'love' again...say it my piece of Kryptonian man-beef!' Jayson:'Loooooooove...loooooooooove...loooooooooove...looooooove...'..::/::..Jayson repeats the exaggerated word as his lips grow closer and closer to Erica's, her eyes closing as she prepares to feel them press against hers...'WAIT WAIT WAIT...CUT!! I REFUSE TO TELL THIS CRAP!! Come on Rent....tell the truth!' 'AWWW COME ON...THIS IS MY STORY...JUST TELL IT THE WAY I TOLD YOU TO TELL IT NARRATOR-MAN OF NYOLYPHIC NARRATION!!!''You are destroying my crediblity as a narrator! I'd rather describe Karl LeBlanc prison raping Boca Del Inferno in a Viagra on a Pole match then lie this badly!''Oh fine fine fine...but my way was WAAAAAAAAAAY more entertaining Mr.Narrator man. Here...read this script then...'..::/::..Rent-A-Hero hands me a script as I rewind the scene all the way back to where Erica says high. I let out a sigh, wondering which is more silly...Rent-A-Hero ACTUALLY having game or the fact that I'm describing my own actions when I'm supposed to be describing everyone elses actions. With that, I unfurl the script, reading it as the story continues..::/::.. Erica:'Hi, Jayson.' ..::/::..Jayson has now gone from a silly, yet clean cut young man in a pair of baggy JnCo's, a Green Lantern emblem tanktop, and a thin Robin style mask to....A SUPERWIGGER IN A TOMMY HILFIGER BEANIE CAP, FUBU JEANS, AN '05' JERSEY AND A PAIR OF TIMBERLAND'S WITH SUPERMAN EMBLEMS ON THE SIDE?! He approaches Erica...ALLOWING HIS PANTS TO HANG OFF HIS WAIST SO HIS UNDERDGO BOXERS ARE CLEARLY VISIBLE AS HE SAY?!..::/::..Jayson AKA Rent-A-Wigger:'YO YO YO SUPER-SHWADY....YOUZ A REAL FINE BREEZY, FOR SHEEZY, I WISH I COULD THINK OF SOMETHIN THAT RHYMED WITH SHEEEEEZY! Look here gurlie...how bout you and me head on down to McDizzies and we can get a 'Number 11'...that's a numba 5 and a 6 shawdy. Let's stop playin deez games gurlie...you know you want a superhero in yo life who has less rhythm then Garth Brooks during the Electric Slyzide. Come on ova here gurlie and let's him dem superskizines in my pops Station Wagon, fo sho. Word to yo home planet.' Erica:'OH MY GOD...YOUR WIGGERNESS IS SO SEXY, I MUST HAVE YOU NOW!!' Jayson AKA Rent-A-Wigger:'Word..' ..::/::..ERICA SPEARS JAYSON DOWN TO THE GROUND AND PROCEEDS TO...'SLOB HIM DOWN'?!..::/::..'Excuse me for a minute ladies and gentlemen...' 'What's wrong narrator man...you didn't like the 'Ghetto Edit' of the script? Think it needed a few more 'fo' sho's' or a 'bling bling'?'..::/::..I leave my narration, stalking towards Rent-A-Hero with intent to kill. In little under a minute, I thrash our hero soundly for these dumb alterations to his memories...unearthing the real script from his back pocket as I crack my knuckles. I remind myself that after one more month, I'm applying for an Xavier Kannon narrator position as I read the ACTUAL script, rewinding the scene yet again back to where Erica first greeted our young hero as we continue..::/::..'Owwwie...mental note...never alter the script...end mental note...prepare for gurlie yelps of pain. 3..2..1...OWWWWWWIE!!' Erica:'Hi, Jayson.' ..::/::..Jayson looks lost for words, unable to even think of the proper greeting for a woman of her stature in his world...he wants his first line to be perfect...simple yet elegant...to the point, but leaving room for her to discover the further mystery that is Jayson. With that, he finally parts his lips to speak to her...expelling one single word followed by a cheesy smile..::/::.. Jayson:'Hi.' Erica:'Wow...that's new. Most guys I meet either start off a convo with 'Whoa momma', 'Are you a goddess', 'Is your father a burglar? Because how did he steal the light from the stars and put them in your eyes', and 'WOOOOOO NICE ASS!'..::/::..The two share in an innocent giggle, Jayson slowly building a means of confidence about her now...realizing that even though she may be beautiful and popular...that she has quite the personality as well. Jayson twiddles his thumbs, still quite nervous around her as she asks..::/::.. Erica:'I know we haven't had many opportunities to chat in the past, and I know it seems kind of selfish that I'm coming to you now about this, but...I need you...' ..::/::..Those three words...'I need you'...enrapture young Jayson, his youthful exuberance getting the best of him as he exclaims in dangerously excited gibberish..::/::.. Jayson:'GREAT GASKETS OF GILGAMESH!!! I KNEW IT...I KNEW YOU FELT THE SAME WAY I FELT FOR YOU!! I KNEW THAT DEEP DOWN, YOU WANTED ME TO BE THE SUPERMAN TO YOUR LOIS LANE!!! I KNEW YOU WERE JUST AS SHY AS ME AND DIDN'T KNOW HOW TO APPROACH ME!! YES, YES, LET US STOP PLAYING THESE GAMES AND UNITE LIKE THE WONDER TWINS...EXCEPT WE WILL UNITE IN THE NAME OF LOVE AND SOAR HIGHER THEN ANY STARSHIP!! I NEED YOU TOO ERICA...I NEED YOU TOOO!!!' ..::/::..Erica blankly stares at Jayson, confused about his sudden outburst of enraptured joy as she replies to him slowly..::/::.. Erica:'Ummm...your THAT excited about tutoring me in English?' ..::/::..Jayson's eyes widen with shock, his mouth wide open but no sound able to escape. He turns firetruck red, amazed he let those emotions escape him in such a dumb moment as he giggles nervously, trying to cover up as best as he can as he replies..::/::.. Jayson:'Umm umm...OF COURSE...of course I'm excited about tutoring you. Oh and nevermind that outburst...those were just lines for the school play...it's about this loser who's in love with the most perfect girl in the world and says something really dumb that makes her stare at her blankly and stuff....not to insinuate I'm a loser or anything or your a beautiful girl...I mean you are a beautiful girl, but I'M not the loser...okay maybe a mild loser, but not as big a loser as...ummm....do I shut up now?' Erica: *giggles* 'Your funny Jayson...I like that. Long time since a guy's made me laugh. They're usually so worried about impressing me that they just don't know how to let themselves go. But anyways, how about you come by after school and you can help me out? I'll have Gummi Bears!' Jayson:'Will you marry me?' ..::/::..Erica laughs playfully, batting her eyes sweetly at Jayson as she lets out the rest of her laugh....not realizing Jayson was DEAD serious about that remark. She just smiles, tapping him on the arm before turning to leave...but not before saying..::/::.. Erica:'I'll see you later Jayson.' ..::/::..She holds her books tight to her, walking away with that sweet saunter that makes the men of City High School drool at her feet, Jayson not even immune to it's effect as his eyes remain glued to her as she waves, wiggling her finger tips as she walks down the road on the way to school. When clear out of sight, Jayson remarks..::/::.. Jayson:'HOLY BOOTY CURVATURE!! SHE HAS THE BEAUTY OF ELEKTRA AND THE BOOTY OF J'LO!! Well....my mission is clear....I shall woo Erica to the best of my Kryptonian might and make her my prom date! This will require all my skill...all my strength...and plenty of deoderant, I get really sweaty when I use my superpowers. NO ONE SHALL STAND IN MY WAY.....NO ONE SHALL STOP ME FROM CLAIMING HER AS MY LOIS LANE!!! NO ONE CAN DAUNTER MY...'..::/::..Suddenly, a newspaper comes hurtling out the window, smacking our youthful hero in the side of the head, which breaks his heroic pose effectively. He looks in the direction of the throw to find it emitting from the house, our hero's father standing their and piercing Jayson with a stern look before screeching..::/::.. Jayson's Father:'GET TO SCHOOL AND STOP DOING THOSE DA** MONOLOUGES!! You'll NEVER GET ANYWHERE IN LIFE DOING THOSE!' Jayson:'Well...to the halls of City High School....TO THE GARAGE!!'..::/::..Jayson unearths a tiny, grey garage door opener from his pants, pointing it towards the metal garage door on the house. He presses the red button on the remote, the door sliding up slowly as Jayson smiles....the door finally reaching the very top and stopping as it unearths a 76' Caddillac El Dorado...painted red, yellow, and blue in respect to Superman. Approaching his car, Jayson exclaims..::/::.. Jayson:'STAND BACK CITY HIGH SCHOOL...THERE'S A TEENAGE SUPERHERO COMING THROUGH!! Hehe...I DARE someone to steal that line.' ..::/::..With that the scene fades....the beginning of young Rent-A-Hero's adventures not even TRULY beginning as he makes his trek to City High. With Erica in his sights and the prom only days away, it will take all his power to and incredibly luck for him to get her to walk the aisle with her on his arm. WILL JAYSON GET ERICA AS HIS PROM DATE?! WHAT ADVENTURES AWAIT OUR YOUNG HERO?! HAS MY APPLICATION FOR E-PORNO MOVIE NARRATION BEEN ACCEPTED YET?!
