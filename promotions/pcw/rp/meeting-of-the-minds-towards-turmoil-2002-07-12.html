Meeting of the minds towards Turmoil

12 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 12, 2002 The deepest enemies lie within us. Is it fame? No. Is it fortune? No. These are material items that do nothing but accentuate the pain we have within ourselves. It’s a shame, because those demons cannot be excised easily from any soul. No, it would undoubtedly be difficult. Too much for one man to bare alone? Perhaps. The scene opens up to the Denver airport. A busy skyway, maybe not as bustling as SF0 or LaX, but people could be seen hurrying to their destinations and scurrying about their lives. They wondered about who they would see, friends they would meet, and family they could argue with again. Yeah, the airport was a fun place. But for one man, it wasn’t quite the jovial atmosphere he had hoped. Although, he doubted a thirteen ring circus would be able to bring him back to earth. I sighed as I continued to wait in line for a ticket. I stamped his foot, I made bubble noises with my mouth…in essence, I tried to be as annoying as I humanly could. It worked to some degree. People became increasingly wary of my presence in the line. Maybe it was the black overcoat I was wearing with the black leather pants. I had to admit, I was looking awfully Matrix-like today. Apparently that’s what most of the people in line were thinking as well. As I blew another bubble with his mouth, an old, overweight woman standing in front of me turned around. Old Woman [looking annoyed] Excuse me, do you mind? That is quite annoying. Tom Lexian: [blew another bubble] Oh, does that bother you? I’m sorry…[blows a bigger bubble and it pops in her face] Old Woman: [rubbing away the fluid] You are the rudest man I’ve ever met. A man your age should be enjoying life, not sitting here wasting away in it. Tom Lexian: [feeling annoyed now] And what exactly do you know about me, madam? Do you know who I am? Or what I’ve been through? Old Woman: I know enough to know that you’re very rude. I mean, people have a right to some peace and quiet, especially here in this busy airport. Everyone’s tired…have some respect for god’s sake. Tom Lexian: [his eyes widened, rage ensuing] Tired? Do you know how it feels to be tired? I don’t think you do…I am TIRED. I’ve been through the depths of hell and come out shaking the devils hand. I’ve been through the hells of a firestorm and lived to tell the story. I have been burnt and come back to show the scars…I am tired. I’m tired of people who come into other people’s lives…I’m tired of back stabbing liars…madam, I know what being tired is like. Do you think this airport is the worst possible place to be? No, no it isn’t. Old Woman: Look, don’t try to scare me. I was a former police officer. I worked the beat on the streets of Denver for twenty-five years. Tom Lexian: Really? I’m glad. Then maybe you will understand…a man I respect very much was injured quite badly the other night. It was a vicious attack. The attacker has become one of my worst enemies. I have a chance to repay him this Tuesday. Hell, the guy had superhuman powers, yet was still injured by this lunatic. Old Woman: [looking worried] Listen, you cannot take the law into your own hands. It’s wrong. Tom Lexian: Can’t I? Madam, I am the law. I have seen injustice day in and day out, and I am tired of it. There’s only room for one man, and one man can only take so much. No, there is no other choice. Only vindication remains. Old Woman: [begins to look around begins shouting] Help, help! This man is unstable! Someone help! I stood there stunned. I couldn’t move. I thought this woman would understand, but she didn’t. Instead she mistook him for a terrorist with a license to die. Nice move, romeo…. I realized the danger he was in as I saw three security officers rushing over to the line. I bolted away and ran towards the terminal. I pushed my way from the many passengers making their ways towards their own planes… “Hey!” “Jerk!” “Moron, look where you’re going!” Couldn’t stop. Couldn’t slow down. I reached the terminal to find it locked. I banged on the door but to no avail. The plane was being delayed…big f*cking surprise. I made his way down the other side of the airport but began to slow down when I realized that no one was following me. I carefully scanned the airport, but realized my assumption was correct. They had lost me in the shuffle. I sighed and looked up to see myself in a small Starbucks coffee café located in the airport. “Where else am I going to go,” Lexian thought to himself. I made my way in and sat down at the nearest table. I slumped down and rested my head on the seat top. My head was a swirl of emotion. “Looks like you could use a friend…” Tom Lexian: [looking up] Captain? Captain America? Captain America: [sitting down with two cups of coffee] In the flesh, my friend. Tom Lexian: [at a loss for words] Wha…what….what are you doing here? Captain America: I heard you were heading out for your big match. I also heard you were looking for the Hulk. Thought I’d head out there to and give you a cheering crowd. Not like you need one now, though. You have legions of fans now, as I understand it. Tom Lexian: Yeah, well, I could use someone to talk to right about now. Captain America: [handing him a cup of coffee] I’m here. I got a mouth. I can talk. Tom Lexian: [accepting the cup] Thanks. Captain America: Why don’t you tell me what’s bothering you? Tom Lexian: Well f*ck, where would I begin? I assume you saw the Tuesday Turmoil match where they crucified Robert right? Captain America: I did. Tom Lexian: Then you saw what happened to us…the Crusaders, I mean. Captain America: [nodded sadly] I did, it was tragic. You Crusaders have been a breath of fresh air, both on the streets and in the ring. You’re a fine champion as well. You three share a lot of similar attributes – pride in what you do and immense amounts of confidence in your abilities in the ring. Two key ingredients in a champion. What the Dark Age did was a crime. A real crime. Tom Lexian: [angrily] What they did was a mistake – a big mistake. And they will pay the price for his disregard. Captain America: Look Tom, you can’t keep getting worked up about this. As I understand it, you guys have some sort of six man tag match coming up? Tom Lexian: You’re damn right. The only thing is I’m teamed up with Chris Chase and Duke Williams…two guys I can’t really trust at all. Captain America: You see, there you go again. You’re letting your emotions get ahead of what’s important, and that is defending that title. I know you want to get your hands on the Dark Age, but one way of hurting them is to keep that title away from their waists. Keep your head in the game. Make sure you don’t get pinned. Who cares if you lose the match or not? You’re still the champion. Tom Lexian: I appreciate you helping me back there, Cap. I need to repay you, and I’m going to do that when I beat down the Dark Age. Captain America: But you’re missing the point again. The worst thing you could do is to try to go out there and injure those washed up phonies. Just play your cards right and make sure they don’t get you. The worst thing you could do is get injured yourself. Tom Lexian: [thinking deeply] Alright, alright. I know you’re right, …it’s just…these past few days have gotten me so worked up… Captain America: You’re angry, and rightfully so. But Tom, you can’t let your emotions get the best of you. Your emotions can be a powerful tool, but they also can end up leaving you vulnerable. Darkstar pushed all the right buttons with you. Maybe he planned this from the beginning, who knows? But you have to play it smart, Tom. Don’t let him beat you. Tom Lexian: [giving in] Ok. Captain America: You’re still a young man. You still have championships to win and more wars to fight. This will be one of your first wars that you’ll have to win in the PCW. Tom Lexian: [leans his head back again] Everything is going so quickly. There’s a lot to worry about. Captain America: I noticed. But hell, it comes with the territory doesn’t it? I mean, you’re the Continental champion. You knew people would be out there gunning for you. Tom Lexian: Yeah, guns as big as town cars. Captain America: [starts to stand up] Well, then you have some things to worry about then. Tom Lexian: What do you mean? Captain America: The ones that don’t make themselves readily apparent, are usually the most dangerous ones. Tom Lexian: I guess you’re right. Captain America: [shakes Tom Lexian’s hand] Hey, good luck in your match. Not like you need it though. Get on that plane, and make sure you remember what I told you. Tom Lexian: [stands up and returns the handshake] Cap, I doubt there’s anything that you say that I won’t listen to. Captain America: [smiles] That’s why you’re a champion, Tom. I watched Captain America as he walks away and fades into the endless ocean of people. I looked towards the terminal which lead to the flight for Sacramento, CA. I picked up my bags and began walking towards it. As I did, I heard a huge roar coming out from my right. Screams and cries follow, and suddenly I saw crowds of people running in all sorts of directions. When the crowds began to thin out, I saw the Hulk standing over a desk, holding up a man by his throat. Hulk: YOU MAKE HULK ANGRY! Scared Man: [choking] Please! Please! Don’t hurt me! I grabbed the amulet and rubbed it, feeling the heat protruding from it as it felt my fear. Tom Lexian versus the Incredible Hulk? I don’t know about you, but I’ve already soiled my pants.
