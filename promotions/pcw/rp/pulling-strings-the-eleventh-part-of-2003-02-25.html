Pulling Strings, The Eleventh Part Of

25 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 25th, 2003 As the sweat glistens from everybody and the hots and hollars are heard, a voice cracks through the ever present mind of Dante Jackson, inside and outside as he secretly whispers to himself, Club date, love it now or hate it later Racks: 'DAMN RIGHT YO!' The incumbent voice of Racks blasts through the crew backstage as the scenery is just that of a bar, but just not your daily average bar of drunks and idiots playing pool. This is the very best bar that man has ever witnessed, well for black people anyway. The club. If you have ever been to a club in New York, Houston, California, or Miami, then you know just what goes down there and exactly what to expect. Vivrant bodies shaking, enough to make Sean Penn stiffer than a Iraqi missile, but for all we know right now - Dante Jackson and the rest of the crew have a show to do here. Promoting Tha 2nd Coming, which has already been certified 5x platinum and pushed out two singles played in regular rotation - 'Jump Up' and 'Me and My Pimp' Like almost every other rapper this year, Dante had to remix the already now infamous Tupac song, 'Me and My Girlfriend'. Only difference is that no female sang the hook, Dante did. Will: 'Jesus! What the fuck were you thinking singing that fucking song, what fucking bullshit!' Will screams as his black and white army bandana hangs around his head in classic Tupac form. A giant wifebeater and blue jeans hug to his supremely muscular frame, rivialing that of Bob Sapp, a 375 pound monster in PRIDE. Jason Cunningham, the only recognizable white person in the group murmrs to himself, Jason Cunningham: 'He wanted to be more popular.' Kenny: 'That dude already more popular, but I gotta find me some scratch!' Kenny darts off and away from the group, heading down to the dancefloor and getting his hustle on the only way he knows how - acting injured during a Ludacris song. As the club banger, 'Southern Hospitality' croons its way onto the DJ's turntable set, its purely evident that Kenny's plan has been set in fruition and placed on working mode. Kenny: 'OW!' He screams as he drops down to the floor, holding his nose as people all around him begin throwing their elbows in some sort of relative jungle dance. Crowd: 'DROP BOWS ON EM! DROP BOWS ON EM! DROP BOWS ON EM! WHEN WE...OH! DROP BOWS ON EM! DROP BOWS ON EM! WHEN WE THROW DEM BOWS!' The crowd screams and chants religiously as Kenny shakes like a seizure that's been set off from a firecracker. From views back in the booth where the crowd happens to be seperated from just about everybody else that has to deal with Dante at this current moment, Will staers at Kenny repeatedly getting stepped on and literally disposed of to make room. Will: He's getting himself killed! Dante shrugged off seeing his friend being ripped to pieces by the mob as Buddha can be seen hovering over him like a thought bubble, high out of his very loving mind and just having a good time. Dante: Buddha, get him will ya? Buddha: FOCK YOU! Dante: But Bud - Buddha: FOCK YOU! Dante: Why? Buddha: FOCK YOU! Dante: C'mon, I already asked why! Buddha: Cuz you don't pay me in weed, and you left Statuz, an' yo blackness! Dante sighed, but for some reason, yes Buddha was right. The roots of grimey, street prophets he had grown up with had left him ever since he becamed a hot rapper. He knew he couldn't go back there, people had it out for him as he turned to no one in particular and starred into nothing, one of those odd camera angles where it seems the character it is revolved around happens to be stuck in some odd thought period. Finally, Racks smacks him out of it. Racks: GET THE HELL OUT OF IT! YOU HAVE A SHOW TO DO! Dante: YES, BUT YOU DIDN'T HAVE TO SMACK ME DUDE! Racks eyebrows burrow a bit, shocked by the language Dante has gone. Racks: I am so damn offended of my skin thanks to you. Dante: Why? I didn't do anything! Racks: Actually, yes you did. You used 'dude', next you'll be saying 'tubular'? Dante: Stop quoting everything! Racks: No, 'homeslice' I won't. Its time you didn't even envoke the white man anyway. Need to go on some Shabazz shit, 'brotha'. Dante shudders. Dante: I can't, I can't be like the only non gangsta gimmick for a black mnan in PCW history now can I? Racks: Well, atleast he didn't try to make a word out of the Southern dictionary a damn house of God and then convert to Voodoo. Dante nods, reminiscing about The Rush and Jordan X Shabazz in their hey day of two thousand and two, the earliest known time of him even being in the wrestling circuit. He shakes for a second before walking down the stairs of the club, watching various men and women continue to stomp on his compadre as if he were road kill hopping up from the dead. His meek screams can be heard from the dance floor, but are drowned out thanks to none other than Ludacris. Kenny: HELP ME! Dante: Okay people, okay! You can stop massacring the black man on the floor right now, he really doesn't deserve what you're doing to him unless he pickpocketed all your money... Crowd: WHAT?! Dante shakes his head. Dante: No, he didn't take your money okay? Crowd: WHAT?! Dante (muttering): I now know how the Rock must feel at night... Will: Quit ya yappin man, just pick him up and get your ass on stage before the crowd turns into a bunch of crazy mother canuckers! Crowd: EAT US WITH THE ROCK QUOTES! Will takes a step back, obviously scared and almost scarred for life as he sits back down, whimpering into his meaty hands. Dante takes a gander at this before realizing that this is a hetic situation, and it might make the other club incidents of the month topped. Dante: Um....sorry? Crowd: SHUT UP! WE'D RATHER SIT THROUGH A VIOLENCE JACK EPILOGUE THAN HEAR YOU! Dante: What are you? Androids to think that I, the greatest rapper of all time will not speak in front of you? What nerve have you got?! Kenny: Dante I think - Dante: No you don't think pleb! These people will hear the voice of Dante Jackson, whether you'd love it or not! Dante pushes through the crowd, hopping upon the DJ vooth and snatching the Ludacris record out of its proper holding slot. The crowd groans in dissapproval as Dante takes one of his records already in the DJ stack and places it on the record player. 'GO! GO! GO! GO!' Crowd: GO SHAWTY! ITS YO BIRTHDAY! WE GO PARTY LIKE ITS YO BIRTHDAY! WE GON SIP BACARDI LIKES ITS YO BIRTHDAY! AND WE DON'T HAVE TO GIVE A FUCK ITS NOT YO BIRTHDAY! Dante: That's better. Kenny: Wait, you said you were going to perform! Dante smirks. Dante: Yes, but not towards these people! They deserve to listen to lesser rapperssuch as 50 Cent. He doesn't have my talent, but he can pack a crowd like me, so there's no beef. Besides, Violence Jack and Chet King can have all the testament of my voice Tuesday night, when I am named the number one contender for the PCW Continental championship! Kenny: I thought you had to win a match to do that! Dante looks around, petrified into a gaze as if he can't do anything about it. Dante: Oh crap...
