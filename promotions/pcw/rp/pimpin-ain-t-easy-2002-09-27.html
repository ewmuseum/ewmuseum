Pimpin Ain't Easy

27 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/27/02 Down a lonely street we wander. Dimly lit, only the shadows and the faint screams are with us....drifting like ghosts, eternally alone. Forever beyond the reach, the grasp of salvation. The usual nightlife for such a place, the dealers, the scum is strangely absent. In the simplest terms, this place is as quiet as a tomb. Perhaps a poor choice of words. A lone piece of paper drifts, discarded and dirty....it tumbles, unhindered by the tread of human feet. The whistling breeze aids it on its journey, as it blows to the left and into a darkened alley. The refuse tosses and turns, finally brushing by a leather boot. Up from it, stylish clothes greet our vision....a black trenchcoat, barely visible against the gloom. And its owner, a familiar man looking on. Ice Tea: What do you mean you ain't got the money? In Tea's grasp, firmly held off the ground by one hand is the jobber known as Scourge. He shakes his head, struggling for air in the other's deathgrip. Scourge: (Coughing) I toldja man....I was....ack.... Ice Tea: Man.... Behind the rapper/pimp, the behemoth named the Violator stands held back by several toughs. In spite of his struggles, the numbers prove too great....he is unable to move. Violator: (Frustrated) I am the Violator! Ice Tea: I don't care if you're mother fucking Don King. Your boy didn't pay up. Violator: Prepare for total violation! Violator rocks back and forth, to no avail as Scourge looks on with wide eyes....he remembers. Remembers.... Another street corner, though at least this was populated by a few assorted cars. Innocent conversation. Scourge chuckles to his long time companion. And an interloper doth appear. Ice Tea: Hey man, got something that might interest you. Forward a little, and Tea explains things to the long time jobbers. A peddler of lust. Violator: Prepare for total violation? Ice Tea: Well, if that floats your boat. But uh.... Paying no heed, the huge fellow bolted forward into the doorway. What he saw there froze him in his tracks. Violator: I am the Violator. Inside the makeshift brothel, all manner of fine specimens wait. Legs splayed, they call to the Violator. He however, merely turns to Ice Tea. Violator: (Hands on hips) I am the Violator. Tea walks in, past the monster and into the midst of a group of dogs. All manner of dogs. Shaggy dogs. Pretty dogs. Poodles. Retrievers. And in between them all, a shrugging Ice Tea. Ice Tea: Well, like I was trying to tell you. The old pimp's fallen on some hard times. Violator: (Shaking his head) Prepare for total violation! Ice Tea: They'll do that for you....just....close your eyes and pretend they're human. Violator: I am the Violator. Ice Tea: Come on dude, I haven't eaten in three weeks. Not caring, the Violator argues with the struggling pimp....then looks to his right. Barely paying attention, his glance falls to Tea again....then quickly to the right once more in a double take. Violator: I am the Violator! Over Ice's shoulder, Scourge lays in the midst of the mongrel brigade. A huge smile splays across his lips as he tilts his head to and fro. Scourge: There's plenty o' Scourge to go around, don't worry. Violator: I am the Violator. Ice Tea: (Beaming) We're in business again! Yessir. Back in the alley, Scourge kicks his feet in the grasp of the pimp. Scourge: Look man, I know I owe you for all that man....but man....uh.... The jobber's eyes shift to the Violator, still held fast by the thugs. Scourge: Violator stole my wallet! Violator: (Shaking his head) I am the Violator! Scourge: He....he also said on the actor scale you're buried by Snoop Dogg and Spongebob Squarepants. Yeah. Violator: (Furious) Prepare for total violation! Ice Tea lowers his head slowly. Ice Tea: That's low. But it doesn't change your situation. Scourge: Damn. Tea reaches into his pocket....producing a switchblade, which he shifts back in his hand. Ice Tea: Any last words? Scourge: (Sweating) Uh....uh....um...uh.... The knife glints off a faint beam of light....Scourge throws his eyes frantically to the right of Tea. Then....he grins. Scourge: Sayonara. Ice Tea: Huh? Ice glances to his side, just as a beat up automobile charges into the alley and smashes into him! His body sails through the air, crashing into the far wall and literally shattering....the driver's side door opens, revealing the demon known as Clyde. Clyde: The hell? You said to make a left at the damn intersection. A lighter voice screeches forth from the passenger side, clearly perturbed. Bethany: (Muffled) Not through a goddamn alley nitwit! We'll never find Kannon this way! Clyde: Bah! For a brief moment the evil one looks on....his gaze shifting from Violator to the remains of Ice Cube, to Scourge, to the remains of Ice Cube. Clyde: .....I won't ask. Violator: (Shrugging off the goons) I am the Violator! With a grunt of irritation, Clyde throws his car into reverse....backing out of the alley quickly. A short distance away Violator and Scourge give chase, attempting to pry the rear doors open as he speeds along and we mercifully fade to black......
