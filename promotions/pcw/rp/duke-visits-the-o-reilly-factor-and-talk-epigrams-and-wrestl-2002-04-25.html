Duke visits the O'Reilly Factor and talk epigrams and wrestling.

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/25/02 The following transcript was pulled off the Fox News Web Site and is for PCW Fans it is the soul property of the Fox News Organization. -From the Fox news studio’s in New York City it’s the O’Reilly Factor with Bill O’Reilly. The program is live in the primetime slot of 8EST and is the number on rated news show in America and it’s largely due to O’Reilly’s hard-hitting style of journalism. Entering the no spin zone tonight is PCW President Duke Williams and from The Parents Right To Censor Sandy Kay - O’Reilly: Right now on the factor we’re joined by twenty year wrestling legend, the current president of the most watched federation PCW, and the host of the extremely offensive yet popular PCW Malice. And Via satellite from Los Angles, California she is head of The Parents Right To Censor Sandy Kay. Sandy I’m going to start with you. You find this stuff they call entertainment offensive and detrimental to a young persons mind? Kay: Bill I think calling this stuff entertainment is a stretch. It holds no value to the person watching it except maybe an opportunity to find the new buzz word. If the PCW was a movie it would get an ‘R’ rating and you people would not be permitted to view it. Since it is on TV nobody stops the kids from watching this trash. O’Reilly: OK fair enough,. Duke how do you respond to that? Williams: What value does any show have…… O’Reilly(Interrupting): My show offers a fair and balanced show that informs the audience the issues that effect their lives. My show holds value Mr. Williams!! Williams: I’m talking in the entertainment sector Bill, there is no entertainment-based program that offers value. O’Reilly: I think my shows pretty entertaining and informative. Williams: That’s for you viewers to decide. What PCW offers in its programming is a legal escape. A world where good and evil collide but nobody dies and there is always resolution. It’s a place where the nine to fiver could tell his boss to flip off and not get fired. That’s every working stiff’s dream…… Kay: Bill let me interrupt here. That would be fine if your audience was grown men who use the show as an escape but that’s not the case here. Young children watch this program and absorb the offensive nature of its content. They see their hero Duke Williams flipping the bird to his boss and then they go to school on Monday and the same to their teachers. It breeds a negative attitude into the kids who become hell bent on turning into outlaws. Williams(Laughing): Jesus lady when’s the last time you got laid. O’Reilly: Cut his mic off!! Now that’s just not appropriate Mr. Williams. We’ll come back to you when your ready to act civilized. So Mrs. Kay what’s the answer here? Kay: When’s the last time I had sex??? -Bill throws his hands up in the air as if he’s giving up. Duke just laughs off to the side.- O’Reilly: No Mrs. Kay what the solution if wrestling is the problem. Kay: That’s easy Bill lets ban the show. O’Reilly: I with you on the surface of the issue. I feel that wrestling and shows like it are dangerous to our moral stability as a society. However I think banning something is crossing over on our first amendment rights. Put his mic back on. Duke what do you think? Williams: I think we should ban oxygen in that women’s surrounding area. It’s not the government’s responsibility to tell us what we can or can not watch. If parents don’t want their kids to watch my program then they should lay down some rules or get rid of the TV. O’Reilly: Aside from banning oxygen in Mrs., Kay’s area I agree with you. Now if parents are watching my program with their kids it’s time to put them aside I’m going to be reading some quote’s from Duke Williams’s promo’s. I agree it is the parents job to police their kids but how can you feel good about your self knowing that most kids aren’t supervised and watching you say this. Open Quote “Now I’m no doctor but I know the best cure for a headache is to come over to my place and take it Malice Style. So Ellie, if you want to feel good chicky, stop by the Malice Mansion, bend over the Malice mantle, and let my shove my Malice stick in an uncomfortable crevasse in your body. Honey when I’m done with you your headache will be all better and you’ll forget about ole what’s his name.” End Quote. Williams: Bill if your trying out for the Malice Crew you just failed. O’Reilly: That’s offensive!!! Your insinuating sodomy with a young lady half your age. That’s sick and a kid is hearing this and you don’t care or have any remorse?? Williams: Hell no, I said I wasn’t a doctor! It’s an old wives tale on how to get rid of a headache what’s the difference between that and feed a cold starve a fever? O’Reilly: Come on Mr. Williams you know darn well what the difference is!!! Here it gets worse. Open Quote. “I’ll track down your mother and “f” her in the ass with my big black bleep. Then I’ll invite over my posse from the projects in chi-town to bang her a few rounds with their African spears of pleasure.” End Quote. Williams: I don’t remember saying bleep? O’Reilly: I had to bring this program to an adult level so I edited out the real nasty words. Williams: Well bleep that! HA HA HA O’Reilly: I’m not finding this the least bit funny Mr. Williams. Your supporting rape!!!! Williams: Your taking it out of context the guy I’m fighting is a nazi and I’m just being crude to get a rise out of him. O’Reilly: So you have Nazi’s running around on this show also?? I’d love to get a transcript of one of his promo’s. Williams: That’s funny I was thinking the same thing. I reckon I scared him away with that foul line. O’Reilly: This stuff is too disgusting for even Hustler magazine!! It’s vulgar and it needs to be policed with in your own organization or the advertisers should pull out. Kay: We’ve tried to arrange boycotts with the advertisers and there customers but it fails. O’Reilly: Nobody cares about nothing if money is involved and as foul and grotesque this show is it makes money. Money talks in this country and that’s why the show goes on. That is why parents must take control of there TV’s. Kay: Lets talk about the radio show. Williams: Yes the radio show!! The number one rated internet radio program in the world you can catch it every Saturday Night at 11EST on Fancast.com. O’Reilly: Alright if you want to buy commercial time on this program I’ll give you a sales contact but frankly I think my audience is too intelligence to listen to you radio show. Last weekend after we booked you as a guest I listened to your radio show and I as an educated adult was offended. You get around the FCC decency laws by broadcasting on the Internet where you can use every word under the sun and you do. Williams: Bill I invite you to call in so I can put you on hold and shut your mic off. O’Reilly: It wouldn’t be worth my time enter that swear emporium. We’re not here to talk about me Mr. Williams we’re here to talk about your show PCW Malice. The show is pornographic in nature. Williams: If you think that’s pornographic then you and Mrs. K are really hard up and should consider hooking up if you get what I’m saying. O’Reilly: I heard more f words in a minute on your show then I do in a whole rap song. Williams: That must be a pretty lame rap song Bill!! O’Reilly: Look its offensive. Williams: In your eyes. O’Reilly: In the eyes of anybody with half a brain. We’re out of time I’ll give you the final word Mrs. K. Kay: Lets work together to ban this vulgarity before it goes to far. O’Reilly: Fair enough thank you both for joining me tonight. Up next on the Factor will look into Jessi Jackson finance with a former accountant of his. Interesting stuff join us after these. -Duke walks off the set where he’s greeted by a PCW staff member looking for him to cut a promo.- Staff: Come on Duke lets hear what you have to say about your tag match this Friday? Williams: What do I have to say?? What more can I say. Hey Kannon I bet you thought you were going to see a promo where I don’t call Ellie~! what she is and that’s a cum net. You hear me science boy, a cum net. I hope your enjoying your stay in Disney land because when you exit that little town and enter the real world you’ll realize what a joke you really are. Not even a good joke that’s repeated at bars across America, more like a joke you’d fine on the other end of a candy wrapper. Kannon you can dodge and duck me but when you get right down to it you fear me. I’ve now mailed a tape of last weeks show out to Ellie I hope she enjoys it as much as I did. -Duke motions like he’s using a chair to somebody’s skull.- Williams: Not only am I bigger, stronger, better looking, and smarter then Xavior Kannon but I also have a better partner. X I’m sorry I scared your little partner away before our match. Like he said, he’s sending an excellent message as to his staying power. Jordan is teaching my a new pimp limp so this Friday boys get ready for me to stroll down that aisle PIMP STYLE, get ready for me to beat you kids Malice Style, and most of all pack up for a trip that’s WHISKEY BENT AND HELL BOUND and killer that’s the honky tonk truth. -The camera fades.-
