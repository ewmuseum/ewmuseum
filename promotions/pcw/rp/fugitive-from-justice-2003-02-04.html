Fugitive from Justice

4 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/04/03 Footsteps clomp through damp underbrush. Labored, intense breathing pulses out, as shrubbery is torn aside. Onward a man runs. Further through he escapes, desperately attempting to flee his pursuer. His own body fights against him, as the air leaves his lungs....he hunches over, sucking wind. Voice: Hey! At the sound of the one behind him, the man quickly stands again. Grunting, he presses forward.....as he does, the second man gives close chase. Further, further he runs.....a gunshot rings out, narrowly avoiding contact with the back of his head. Cursing, our fugitive ducks and crawls, stumbling and falling with a yelp into a stream of running water. Man: Argh! The weak tide pushes against him as he struggles to find his footing. At last he does, looking all around.....the crack of another gunshot spurs him on quickly. With an exasperated shrug, he runs onward down the path of the water, into the darkness of a tunnel. Voice: Get back here! The eternal darkness gives way to a small light. He races towards it, not knowing where it may lead and not caring at all....faster, faster he runs, struggling to pull his feet in and out of the water. As he nears his goal, the cold and damp air strikes his face....closer, closer still it looms. At last with a roar he bursts through the opening....and stops cold, looking slowly down. Man: ...... The roar of the rushing water fills the ears of the escapee, as he beholds a manmade waterfall. Into an endless abyss it rushes, down, down, down.....feeling himself falling, the man flails his arms. Man: Uh, agh!!!!! Madly he tries to find something to grab on to, at last stumbling across a fitting of pipe. Grabbing hold, he clutches it for all he is worth....then his eyes sink, and he sighs as the voice booms out from behind him. Voice: Hands in the air! Man: I know you're kidding. Voice: You think so. From the darkness emerges the familiar, aged face of Tommy Lee Jones. With cold determination in his eyes, he keeps his pistol trained on his prey. Tommy Lee Jones: You couldn't make this easy, could you Clyde. The burned man grimaces as he fights to keep his footing on the water's edge. Clyde: Neither could you. You could have just let me escape. Or you could have died. Tommy Lee Jones: I'm not dying till you do, sunshine. Tommy takes a step forward, resulting in Clyde edging a step away. Tommy Lee Jones: I wouldn't do that. Clyde: Oh bugger off. Tommy Lee Jones: Well hey, your funeral. Go right ahead, just you're gonna make it a little messier for me when you go splat. Clyde: ...... Bone weary, the demon glances again down the roaring waterfall. Then he looks up again at his pursuer. Clyde: I didn't kill that puppy! Tommy Lee Jones: I don't care. Clyde: ......why don't you care? Tommy Lee Jones: Why should I care? Clyde: You're the goddamn marshall, you're supposed to care. Tommy Lee Jones: Who ever said I was supposed to care about anything? Clyde: Well you really should. Tommy Lee Jones: Why should I? Clyde: Because it's the right thing to do! Tommy Lee Jones: You're a serial killer, apparently some kind of hellspawn, you escape from prison, ransack a cattle farm, scare tbe bejesus out of poor old man Winsock, steal his TEETH for crying out loud, lead me on this wild goosechase and cause me to get my feet all wet.....and you're going to lecture me on what's the right thing to do? Clyde: Yes. Tommy Lee Jones: Alright do me a favor sport, run at me so I can just shoot you, okay? Clyde: Bah. Once again Clyde looks down.....resignation fills his eyes. Slowly he raises his hands up.....then leaps straight over the side of the falls. Tommy Lee Jones: Ah shit, I HATE when they do that. Jones runs after him, looking down.....below, Clyde speeds through the air. Faster, faster he falls, his tormenter's visage quickly becoming a distant speck. Clyde: (Falling) Baaaaaaaaaaaah! Clyde looks down again, then yelps in panic as the craggy rocks below race toward him. Clyde: Ah crap!!!!! The burned man begins flapping his arms like the world's largest demonic bird. Not that it helps, his descent doesn't slow a bit.....then, a smaller form floats next to him. Clyde: .....what the hell? Bethany: What the hell are you doing here? Clyde: I could ask you the same thing. Bethany: I asked you first. Clyde: I asked you second. Bethany: It's a long fucking story! Just tell me! Clyde: Bah! Well, mine is as well....suffice it to say I managed to escape from that hellhole of a prison. But now here I am falling to my horrible death. Bethany: Hmph. Clyde: Well, are you going to tell me why you're falling down this selfsame waterfall? Bethany: No. Clyde: ......oh come on, there must be SOME reason. Bethany: I'll tell you later. Clyde: We have no later, in about 10 seconds we're going to die! Bethany: Your tough luck. Clyde: BAH~! Tommy Lee Jones: Hey kids, simmer down. Both Bethany and Clyde look up in shock as Tommy Lee Jones floats between them, still brandishing his gun. Clyde: What in the HELL!? Tommy Lee Jones: Oh don't look so surprised. Now hold still. Tommy fires off a wild shot, that parts Clyde's hair right down the middle. Clyde: Hey watch it! Tommy Lee Jones: Only making this harder. Hold still! TLJ squeezes off another round, again just missing the demon as he throws himself backwards. Bethany: You're insane! Tommy Lee Jones: (Squinting) No maam, just doing my job. Jones fires again, nicking the side of Bethany's cheek. Bethany: Ow, you fucking COCKSUCKER!!!!! Furiously, Bethany flaps her arms and guides herself onto Tommy Lee Jones's back. She begins biting and scratching at him, as Clyde looks down tiredly. Clyde: Well, death to us all in t-minus five....four.....three..... Tommy Lee Jones: Quit your whining, cowboy. In spite of Bethany biting into his jugular, Tommy again manages to move himself towards Clyde and grab him in a chokehold. Clyde: (Coughing) You....don't.....know.....when....to.....quit..... Tommy Lee Jones: I'm funny that way. Bethany: ARRRRRRGH!!!!!! Screaming all the way, the threesome plummets into the icy waters, vanishing into murky blackness...... TO BE CONTINUED
