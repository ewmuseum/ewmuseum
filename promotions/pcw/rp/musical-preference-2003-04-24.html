Musical Preference

24 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 24, 2003 'Heavy Metal' Harry: This is not Twisted Sister, man. [Harry looks at the stereo with absolute anger filling his brown, stoned eyes] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Dude, it's totally better than Twisted Sister!! [Harry stands at the stereo system in the LA based Best Buy and seems to be contemplating. He listens to the crunching guitar riffs and pounding drums as if a taste tester tastes his or her entrees. His ears have become so accustomed to Twisted Sister for so long that many other bands quickly lose interest with him. Tommy, for the past few days, has been trying his damnedest to try and get Harry to listen to some different tunes] [A couple days ago Tommy tried passing some Beach Boys tunes by him...] Tommy: Listen to this, Har! It's totally bodacious in a far out way! [The soothing sounds of 'In My Room' by The Beach Boys fills the room of Tommy's living room. The unbelievable sounds make Tommy sway back and forth as if he's right there with the band in the studio. He has a babe on one arm and a marijuana joint in the other. Life is just simple at this point. Show up to the shows, defend your World Title, and slowly begin to cement yourself into the record books as the most bodacious compadre that the wrestling world has ever seen. When the events are over and the kids are asking for autographs, he'll just pass them by and get with the totally bodacious babes in the bikini's. Life doesn't get any better and that's what 'In My Room' does for The Tubular One. He stands in the living room bobbing from side to side hoping that it has the same effect on his most excellent bro] Harry: The Beach Boys suck, man. Tommy: How can you say that and call yourself a man?! The Beach Boys have written some of the most bodacious music EVER!! How dare you even insult them! You know how I get totally heinous when people bad mouth Da Boys! Harry: Twisted Sister is more far out, man. Tommy: Pft, keep telling yourself that bogus stuff. [Back in Best Buy, at present time, Tommy slightly bangs his head to the hard rock that blares through the speakers. Harry slowly begins to bob his head to the song as well. A smile begins crossing Tommy's face as he's finally found the juice to broaden Harry's horizons. Give him something different to check out and to maybe find some different inspriation. Tommy knows that when he's listening to some totally bodacious tunes he needs to vary some things up. When he's feeling on for a gnarly match he'll flip on something fast paced and heavy. If he's happy and content he'll toss on some oldies or pop music. If he's sad and angry he'll put on some depressing music to wallow in his self misery. Harry...well, he just uses Twisted Sister for every little emotion that he's feeling. He'll just change albums every once in a while, but mostly it's the Greatest Hits CD] Tommy: I know your totally into this, dude! Feel the bodacious rhythm of those guitars! This is some classic stuff, dude! Harry: I don't know, man. [Well, at least Harry didn't just immediately say that it sucks. Tommy decides to push it to the next track and hope that it starts to soak into him like one of those totally bodacious sponges] [Yesterday Tommy decided to give some rap music a shot. He knew that it was going to be a longshot but you never know because sometimes longshots pay off big time. Tommy only knew of one rapper that would maybe turn Harry on and that rapper would be Eminem. The skinny white boy with 'mad flowing skillz' according to this totally gnarly looking black dude at the record store. Tommy pumped the volume on the stereo in the living room and Harry immediately began throwing up] Tommy: TOTALLY HEINOUS! [Chunks of potato chips mixed with ranch dip, mixed with Coca Cola come spewing forth from Harry's mouth. It's almost projectile like that most odd babe in the Exorcist movie. The puke slides all over the hardwood floor covering Tommy's bear skin rugs with stomach acid. Normally Tommy would have leapt with great agility to save his rugs but he's just too shocked that a few seconds of the Eminem song would make Harry spew like that. Harry ended up slowly passing out into the puke as Tommy finally hit the stop button on the stereo] Tommy: This is bogus...so very bogus. [Back at Best Buy Tommy spies some stains on Harry's Twisted Sister T-shirt from yesterday. He can see the white stain fade from the top of his shoulder to the top of his pec. Tommy knows that that's the stain caused by the awful puke that Harry let up. It'll probably smell like spew for the next few days, but Tommy will try to keep his time in the bedroom. Hopefully the smell won't turn off the bodacious babes and hopefully this awesome guitar solo that Harry is listening to doesn't turn him off. In fact, this CD is totally bodacious and Tommy decides that whether Harry likes it or not he's going to buy it himself] Tommy: That solo was totally sick, dude!! [Harry slightly nods trying to hold back the utter joy that is flowing through his stoned veins. He so badly wants to just start banging his head all around, whipping his long brown locks into the faces of people passing by. He'll feel the adrenaline flowing through him and he'll probably break something expensive, but he can't let Tommy see him enjoying this. Harry honestly doesn't want to really listen to anything else since Twisted Sister is all the bodaciousness that he needs. But...this band is totally awesome too! It's going to be one of the hardest decisions of his life] Tommy: I should totally change this song to my new entrance theme! I'll totally walk down the aisle on Fusion with these guitars dueling and the drums bashing...my opponents will be MOST terrified! It just sucks that I'm teamed up with Skylar Green! How am I supposed to carry a totally bodacious blind dude to victory?! LUckily, Nezzera is on my side and totally ready to help me out if need be! Though I don't know if I can trust that compadre with his tag team partner on the other side. [Harry just stands there with his stoned eyes looking at the stereo as the next track comes onto the CD. The bassist of the band is absolutely far out and the singer sure knows how to hit the notes right on. He sounds better than Dee Snider. WAIT! Did Harry just think that this compadre sounds better than Dee Snider, leader singer of Twisted Sister? It can't be. Has there been a band that not only equals Twisted Sister but about buries them?] Tommy: If one of those heinous dudes pins me then that means that he gets my title. Now that just totally calls for bogusness! WHat if one of those dudes just happens to get luckily or something? They could totally gang up on yours truly and take the belt away from me. See, that's where you'll come in dude. Get a stick. No...a METAL PIPE! Metal pipes are most bodacious. You use that pipe to smack people with... [Harry has totally lost touch with what Tommy is saying to him. The eerie guitar and the blaring vocals rip through Harry's heart like an oncoming tractor trailer. He can feel his insides catching fire and his body is telling him to go crazy. His mind, however, is telling him to keep his cool and just hope that Tommy does buy the CD. See, that way he can take it, burn it, and then rock out to it when Tommy isn't home. It's be far out and it'll work out in the end for everyone. He just can't let Tommy see that he likes this because who knows what else he'll try to shove down his throat] Tommy: ...I don't know, dude. Maybe, just maybe, you should just use some brass knuckles or something. Those other dudes are pretty tough, especially that Violence Jack compadre. He is Continental Champion....hmmm. [Tommy scratches his chin, looking up to the ceiling with a revelation] Tommy: DUDE! If I pin Violence Jack I could ALSO be Continental Champion for a second time! Now tell me that wouldn't be bodacious! World Champion and Continental Champion...AT THE SAME TIME! DUDE! That is most comparable to having two babes at the same time!! [Harry immediately flails his body all around and begins bumping into people walking around him. He slam dances his way from the stereos to the televisions, playing air guitar as he does so. Tommy smiles with absolute joy as he sees that he's finally been able to turn Harry onto something new. Tommy then remembers what he last said when Harry broke into his slam dance] Tommy: Dude, are you that excited over two babes at once? [Harry doesn't answer and just shoulder blocks someone into a television display] Tommy: Dude...Iron Maiden TOTALLY OWNS!! I've NEVER seen Harry go this crazy. [At this point a manager at Best Buy comes up to The Tubular One. He has a mullet, a bushy mustache, and has pock marks all over his face. The dude must be into those heinous roleplaying games or something] Manager: Sir, can you please control him! Tommy: Dude, there is no containing the Heavy Metal One now. He's gone beyond the point of return. The only true way to stop him is to turn off Maiden. Once that happens, I feel sorry for the poor heinous dude who turns off The Number Of The Beast! [The manager looks at the stereo and then at the slam dancing Harry. He quickly presses the off button and then runs like hell toward the exit of the building. Harry stops slam dancing and then pukes all over a young couple shopping for a new television] Tommy: OHHH! Now that is totally nasty, dude!! Lets get out of here before you throw up on something else. Fade to black
