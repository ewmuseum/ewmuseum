Where are you Chris?

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/22/03 CRACK! The sickening sound of a human cranium being smashed into a brick wall causing of river of crimson fluid to come pouring out. It was a sight that not only shattered Chris Storm’s cranium, but shattered my heart into little pieces as I watched on, too far away to do anything to stop this inhumane act. “NO! DON’T DO IT!” my mind cried out as I watched on helplessly as the man who calls himself the living nightmare took grasp of Chris’ head of blonde hair and reared it back, knowing very well what would happen once he got his hand far enough back! “PLEASE, NO!” My plea fell on deaf ears as Nerezza’s arm lunged forward sending Chris’ skull colliding with the brick wall and creating the last moment I would have seen Chris that evening, lying in a puddle of his own blood just as helpless as I was as I watched on only wishing that there was someway I could have prevented this from happening. “It is all my fault!” I screamed out as I stumbled across the hospital parking lot in tears. I had tried once again to gain permission to visit Chris inside the home for the beaten up and sick, yet once again I had been denied for no apparent reasons. They simply told me each time that I was not allowed to visit at this time and sent off on my way. Climbing into the mini van that Chris and I had shared from time to time to transport the kids we once had custody of back and forth, I climbed over the obstacle of the drivers seat and made myself at home in the backseat of it, stretching my body across the length of it as I buried my face into the palms and continued to pout, “ It’s my fault for wanting those damn tag titles. If we didn’t hold them none of these problems that we have faced as of late would have fallen upon us.” RIIIIINNNNNGGGGGG My pouting was interrupted by the screaming ring of my cell phone, demanding to be picked up. RRRRRRINNNNNNGGGG It screamed out once more as I tucked the tears away and reached into my pocket, fishing out the cell phone in obvious hope that it would be Chris, or a doctor telling me that he was all right. “Hello?” I answered in a cheery tone, hiding any sign of obvious sorrow since I did not want to be consoled my anyone but myself or Chris at this point. “Tessa?” A familiar voice called over the other end in response, obviously recognizing my voice as well. “Jacko?” I questioned as I tied the voice to the name of my best friend as well as a fellow PCW member, Jackson Rust. I wondered in my mind why he was calling. Perhaps he wanted to hang out just to get my mind off shit and get me back in focus for my match against Chet King on Tuesday for the Cruiserweight Title, or perhaps it was just out of pure concern. “The one and only.” He chuckled in response in his usual joyfully teasing voice. I could tell just from knowing him as long as I had that he trying to lighten the weight of the situation for me, but it did little good. “What do you want?” I questioned, doing my best to hide my sorrow. Jackson had helped me get through many things before, but this time I wanted to tackle this situation on my own despite Jackson’s want to help me out just to be a good friend and watch out for someone he cared for like a sister. “Just calling to see if you got a hold of Chris yet.” He replied in a tone of voice that hinted to me to calm down and realize that he was only trying to be supportive like a good friend should be. “No I haven’t.” I sighed as the though of Chris was renewed in my mind. I was hoping to stay off the topic of Chris’ mysterious disappearance but it seemed that every time I dropped it in my mind someone was there to bring it back up, but then again who better to talk about this than to your best friend whom was one of the few you could depend on, and in this case for me it was Jackson Rust. “That sucks.” He said bluntly, sharing the same thought that continued to run through my mind in those two short words. “Why won’t they let you see him?” He questioned, knowing very well that I was the closest person to Chris and if there were anyone who should be allowed to visit him, it should be me. “I don’t know.” I answered quietly into the receiver, wishing inside my mind that I truly did know the answer to his question. “Aww darling, I’m sure they will eventually let you go in to visit him.” Jackson calmly replied to my answer, trying to establish the least bit of hope in my mind. “Not if Chris never wants to see me again.” I responded letting my true feeling be known, feeling that if there was anyone who would listen and understand where I was coming it would be Jackson. With that said I hung up the phone along with these false hopes. I would just have to accept the fact that I would never again see Chris, until he chose to show his face to me once again.
