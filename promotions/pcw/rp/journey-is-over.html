Journey.... Is Over.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January NOTE: If you already read the 'Yesteryear' series on the old 'PTC's Best RPs Board' then you're already a step ahead of the game. If not, you'll just have to wait for B.S.'s background history. Coming in June. Song listening guide: 'It's My Life' by Bon Jovi. Can be rarely found on VH1. BOBBY STORMBRINGER Journey... Is Over. 'There will come a time when you believe everything is finished. That'll be the beginning.' -- Louis La'Amour --1-10-02, Thursday, 1:43 pm EST-- DISTINGUISHED VOICE: So when will you be headed out, sir? YOUNGER VOICE: I don't know. I'm thinking Saturday morning. I have a schedule to keep to, you know. DISTINGUISHED VOICE: Indeed, sir. The scene fades up from black to show the back of a long-haired red-headed man speaking with a somewhat overweight and definitely balding man that looks to be in his mid-fourties. The red-head is wearing a pair of old, light blue, denim jeans and a plain black jacket. It also seems to be wearing a dark pair of sunglasses. The balding person wears a dirty white apron, a blue button down shirt with white pinstripes, and a pair of brown sweatpants. They are standing in a living room. What gives this away is the large entertainment center with the modest-sized television set in the middle. Not to mention the wooden coffee table and green leather couch and love seat set. The view pans around to the other side of the conversation, just over the shoulder of the overweight apron-bearer. This vantage point shows the other man in the conversation. That's man's name, as a matter of fact, is Bobby Stormbringer. Robert Maxwell Stormbringer the Second (he despises being called junior) to be precise. It wasn't his family's given name, but it belonged to his rich, drunk father on the day he was born, so that's all that truly matters on the subject. The constant mutterings that he was a raging alcoholic is only a rumor, but a rumor that takes flight when you change your family name from Sheridan to Stormbringer of all things. The family's Irish, not indian. When asked why he would do such a thing, which is exactly the sort of thing that was asked of him on multiple occassions after he signed the proper legal documentation, he went on to respond: BOBBY'S FATHER: Well.. because it sounded 'good' one day. Also, because I can. Why? It may have something to do with the fact that I can buy you and your hick town eight times over! Now get that camera out of my face, peasant!! Embarrassing, no doubt. Although the buying over and over comment, as rude as it was, probably was true. To some extent. At least at the time. Through sheer luck in the way of fate and chance, Stormbringer was born to two very wealthy parents. His mother, a doctor. No, not a nurse. A doctor. That correction had to be made on several occassions at snobby parties and the like. His father made the high majority of the money to pay for their vast holdings, by way of real estate, stocks, and other ventures that could easily be manipulated by 'illegal' methods. None of the family asked. No one cared for what the truth most likely was. These days, in Stormbringer's not-so-humble opinion, they were all quite simply... weak. STORMBRINGER: There are some things that need to be addressed and taken care of before I leave, though. The man he is speaking to is easily recognized by now. The guy is the Stormbringer family's long-time personal assistant, Carl Everstrong. After Bobby lost his parents in a tragic plane crash when he was only seventeen, Carl stayed on to look after Bobby and to some lesser degree, the black sheep of the Stormbringers and twin brother extraordinnare, Randy. The same Randy Stormbringer who was sentenced to a juvenile detention center as a result of assaulting a bully in high school with a deadly weapon. The same Randy Stormbringer who stole eighty percent of the family estate away from brother Bobby. It was jealousy, jealous that his doppleganger could recieve all the cash while Randy was locked away. In Randy's mind, he was the one that deserved all the money. In Randy's mind, Bobby stole it right out from under his nose. The same Randy Stormbringer that is owed. Owed something from that same estranged brother. Bobby's most recent rival Shane Wethers mistakenly broke into Randy's home on Halloween, thinking that the twin was really Bobby! Bobby bribed Randy for the security tapes, but could only banter with a rather dangerous prize: A favor. Any favor Randy wished. Bobby would have no choice, being a man of his word, but to come running. The worst part may be that Randy has not yet come to collect. Then again, the worst part may truly be that Bobby knows someday, sooner rather than later... he will. CARL: What needs to be taken care of, sir? The Showstopper takes a moment to sit on his sturdy coffee table before continuing. Perhaps a none-too-subtle hint that he won't be the one taking care of these 'things'? STORMBRINGER: Lots of things. For example. The laundry. You had that extra day off last week and when you came back.. dirty clothes? Everywhere. Carl expresses a look somewhere in between sheepish and embarrassed. CARL: Erm, my apologies, sir. STORMBRINGER: Ever noticed you say that a lot lately? B.S. puts his hands on his hips and leans with his right leg, his good leg, as he awaits his predictably unclever response. CARL: Oh. Now that you mention it- STORMBRINGER: Dude, is something on your mind? You've been all distracted for a few weeks. I wouldn't have even noticed except for the fact that it's affecting your work! CARL: No, no, sir, it's nothing. I suppose I am... getting forgetful in my old age? Carl says that in a nearly questioning way, almost as if he's seeking approval for his answer. Mr. Prime Time's (I'll tell you why he calls himself that later) immediate response is a full-out belly laugh that makes Carl almost redden in tension and nervousness. He knows that loyalty doesn't mean all that much to his employer. Outside of pro wrestling at least. One too many slip ups and a pink slip would be waiting for him. Just above the sink just to add insult to financial injury. There Carl would see when it was time to do the nightly dishes. Finally B.S. wipes a tear from his eye and quiets his laughter to the level of snickering. STORMBRINGER: Heh, heh! Ah man.. how weak was that? You gotta know that was weak, right? Right? Carl bows his head a little and nods sharply, his humiliation turning quickly towards anger. STORMBRINGER: You're not quite old enough to blame your piteous, pitiful mistakes on Alzheimer's Disease yet. That's your own doing. I mean you're old, don't get me wrong, just not ancient yet. You'll get there before you know it though, so don't worry. Your excuses will be legitimate soon enough. Carl Everstrong quite literally swallows his rage and his pride for a few moments longer and manages to say the right thing, the expected thing, without choking on the words. Spitting out the response in one muddled answer: CARL: Yessir. The normally even-tempered Carl then turns on his heel and strides back toward 'his' kitchen before he finally says something utterly stupid and costs himself his job. It takes years of training before everyday human beings that don't necessarily live a life of pure service can take that kind of abuse and smile back. Or, at the very least, walk away without smacking that sumbitch upside his empty head. By the way, Carl doesn't even make it three steps. Not even into the dining room in between the living room and the kitchen. STORMBRINGER: Where do you think you're going? That's not the only thing you're supposed to do. Carl finally gives up all civility and, in a move that is rather odd for him, rolls his eyes. While his back is to B.S. of course. Then he turns around with the most disingenuine smile you've seen this side of Mona. While sometimes in the past Stormbringer has recognized this 'smile' and called Carl on it, he's too preoccupied to realize it's plastered all over his housekeeper/accountant's face this time. CARL: Yes, sir? What else is there to do... STORMBRINGER: You know that PTC Promo Channel..? CARL: Ah naturally, sir. The one you've been watching constantly as of late. STORMBRINGER: I got to. Couldn't stand not knowing everything that needs to be known. Anyways, while I'm gone this weekend, you tape absolutely every promo that's from PCW. CARL: Isn't.. everything from PCW these days, sir? STORMBRINGER: Shaddup. CARL: Sorry, s- STORMBRINGER: What did I say before!? CARL: Eh? Oh. Right. Err.... it won't happen again. Sir. STORMBRINGER: Good. Actually, they play some PWA ones real late at night. Don't fret about those, PWA has already served it's purpose to me. CARL: Oh. May I ask what purpose that would be, sir? B.S. grabs the remote off the coffee table next to him and turns on the TV. Talking about the PTC Channel reminded him that he hadn't watched in a whole hour or three, so it was high time to get back to the scouting. He switches to the proper channel and discovers... commercials? Useless. He wouldn't buy any of this nonsense anyway. Although those new Tracy Mcgrady shoes look pretty cool- CARL: Sir..? Carl's sometimes annoying voice pops up against his eardrum, quite unexpectedly. It startles B.S. and turns his head to him with a click of the tongue. This is one of those annoying times. STORMBRINGER: What is it?? CARL: May I ask what purp- STORMBRINGER: Oh, that. I never know who may be crashing a chair in my back two weeks from today. Or who may try to run me over in a car at the next Pay-Per-View. If I get them in a match..or a fight.. then I better be prepared for what's coming my way. We clear on that? CARL: Understood, sir. STORMBRINGER: Hmm. Need every edge I can get, after these surgeries... B.S. instinctively rubs his left kneecap, all too aware of how many times he's gone under the knife and how long those various times have taken him 'out of the game'. A year. Twice. A few months out for another time and another month lost for this most recent operation. STORMBRINGER: Well, at least these dimwitted doctors say that this last operation finally cleared up all the problems. We'll see about that. No matter what, I'll never get that great speed I used to have when I was younger back again. Carl merely nods, showing that he's listening but also knowing through experience that now is not the time to interrupt. STORMBRINGER: As far as soreness... it's all gone. It's like a religious experience, can't remember the last time I didn't feel it. CARL: Well-timed, sir, I suppose. STORMBRINGER: Yeah, it would be. Ah, enough of that sob story or whatevah. If anyone from the PTC Building calls to confirm you just make sure you're here to give the right info. CARL: Understood, sir. STORMBRINGER: That should be all.. just make sure you get that stuff taped. Heaven knows I got more than enough blank videotapes. Oh, and don't tape over anything! That junk is irreplacable. CARL: No worries, sir, I have it covered. STORMBRINGER: Hmph. You better. I'm paying you good enough not to screw up. Which reminds me, I'll be cashin' my advance at the bank before I come back Sunday. CARL: Courtesy of one Mr. Cassidy, sir? STORMBRINGER: You got it. I also have this freggin' biochip in my wrist to thank him for.... At least through this last comeback I got all the money troubles settled. Now it's all about the fame. And I'll still get a little more 'fortune' for signing with PCW. CARL: Of course. So you think you have this contract in the bag, sir? B.S. stares incredulously at Carl with his dirty apron and his shiny, reflecting head. Not because of the apron, but because of what he just said. STORMBRINGER: Look at me! What do you think? I'm only the hottest free agent on the market. Besides, they asked me to come up there. That's a sure sign. CARL: Perhaps the main reason is because they found out that Mr. Cassidy was in dire financial straits? Sir, it was in all the newspapers- STORMBRINGER: Shaddup. CARL: Yes sir. STORMBRINGER: Either way, it's not like they asked, say... Pirana to join up with them. Hell, they should of taken me straight outta PWA. But that's water under the bridge and I won't disappoint anyone this time through. CARL: Wait a minute, sir.. if you're going to the PTC Building, then- STORMBRINGER: Yes, yes. I know! Before I sign that blasted piece of paper I might have to attend a meeting with one Tyler Nelson. CARL: Well, wouldn't that be a problem? STORMBRINGER: Problem?? Yeah, I think so! Besides for the fact that he's generally a cowardly, pompus ass who can't do anything alone. Gawd I can't stand run-ins. He's the king of 'em. On top of all that he consistently messes with K-Dub. CARL: 'K-Dub', sir? Oh yes, Kid Wonder. Won't you be in the same fed now as well? What about that? STORMBRINGER: Hmm. You usually don't remember those things. Sure, as a fan, I root for him. You gotta root for somebody, or how do you watch in the first place? Now, as a wrestler... I'll beat him down like any other person. Especially those that choose to stand and block where I'm pre-destined to be headed. No really. I mean it. CARL: ..I see.. B.S. decides to save the promo-watching for a few minutes and hits the mute button on his remote control. The Showstopper stands up and stretches for a short time before continuing. STORMBRINGER: So there's your mission list. Don't think about that stuff for a couple days- except for that freggin' laundry. Please! CARL: Yes sir, yes sir... STORMBRINGER: Can you go fix me a sandwich? I'm gonna go take care of something minor before I take a ride out to the store... CARL: Uh? Alright, sir. Carl heads off to the kitchen, unaware and curious as to what 'minor' thing B.S. felt he had to take care of. * * * About fifteen minutes later, Carl sits on the sofa to take a short break and watch some soap operas. ABC, if you must know. The Showstopper's turkey and cheese sandwich sits on the coffee table. B.S. walks out of the bathroom, rubbing his face with a white bath towel. As you would expect, doing such a thing conceals his face as well. Carl only looks up at him around ten seconds later, once the commercial break begins. CARL: Ah, hello sir. Your sandwich was ready a little while ago. Why were you in there so long? I heard the water running, but I thought you already took a shower.. this morning.. what did you do!? Carl Everstrong's reaction is to what he sees after B.S. drapes the semi-damp towel over his shoulders. What he sees is this. While B.S.'s trademark lightning-streak shades and long, blazing red hair remains, his trademark goatee with soul patch are completely.. well.. gone. The first thing someone familiar with Bobby Stormbringer would say is how much he looks like his 'evil' twin brother Randy all of a sudden, with the obvious exception of his long hair. Bobby seems happy and almost giddy with the change, while also not being bothered with Carl's reaction. As a matter of fact, he doesn't even seem to notice. STORMBRINGER: I tell ya, man. Between this knee and my face, I feel like I'm eighteen again. The truly twenty-five-year-old B.S. walks over to the leather love seat near the front door and drops his towel on it. He pats his butt to make sure his wallet is still there and he quickly walks into his nearby bedroom. Carl's shock that B.S. would change anything about his appearance is quickly overriden by the fact that the soaps are back on. B.S. comes back in the living room with his dark silver and Edge-like trenchcoat on that he wears while on his way to the ring for a wrestling match. B.S. takes the wallet out of his pocket and puts it in one of the trench's inside pockets so it can't be stolen. He heads straight for the door after grabbing the car keys and opens it wide, the sunlight spilling in past the trees in the front yard. B.S. turns back to his attendant while standing in the doorway. Carl is busy, intently watching just how in the world Todd will explain why Blair's baby is missing this time. STORMBRINGER: It looks like finally this journey... is over. And baby, that's no b.s.! On that note, B.S. shuts the door behind him without waiting from a response from his charge. Carl peers at the door out of the corner of his eye with a placid look about him. CARL: On the contrary. It appears things are just beginning for you, young master Stormbringer. Just beginning. Carl looks back at the TV just in time. To see the next commercial break start. CARL: Augh, come on! See, now how am I gonna find out if Christian told Viki that Ben knows Lyndsey's daughter isn't pregnant!? 'Devil Without A Cause' by Kid Rock fades in as soft background music while a close-up of the 'new' Bobby 'The Showstopper' Stormbringer driving his battered Camero to the corner store is shown. B.S. looks focused. Determined. Prepared. And above all else.... Dangerous. The shot stops while the car continues on down the narrow and otherwise empty road. A message is left in its place.
