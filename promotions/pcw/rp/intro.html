Date: now... The moment, quite exhilarating in the duration of it, was suddenly cut off by sudden realization that he had fallen asleep face down on the keyboard. Kicking himself with frustration, he can’t believe he was being sexually invigorated by aliens, meanwhile leaving the AOL connection un-signed off. Has he not enough bills to pay monthly? A tight budget like his has no place for such a form of gluttony, and… Follow the white rabbit, Lucifer. His spine tensing upon the most chilling sensation, and his skin rippling with goose bumps, Lucifer stares at a black screen. Reaching back into his memory, he wonders whether he shut down the computer before nodding off into galactic, anal probe land? The black screen seems to show evidence of something such as that, but the words in white text. “Follow the white rabbit, Lucifer.” Those are what unsettle him so… That’s all he saw inside his dreams in his unconscious state. “Hey, I think the little guys finally coming, too.” “(Vicious Snarling.)” Voices that rattle him out of his slumber come from all sides. Even with his eyes closed, he can sense the bright light overhead beaming down on him. He wants to visor eyelids that block his sight, but can he dare risk facing the burning light? “Where am I?” He voices his confusion, giving the whole sight thing a little time. “Oh, so you are awake. Well, I think you’ll need to see for yourself…” Doing as he is told, he breaks the crusty tear marks, and immediately with the separation of his eyelids he winces. A bold illumination above before him is a bit sudden, but blinking the pain away, the appearance of the room unfolds, adjusting accordingly. All he sees is a large light staring back at him. “Where… Where is everyone?” Someone whistles, catching his attention. To his right, causing his perspective of direction to twist drastically, he looks over to see a man on a table beside him. Already startled, he becomes even more surprised noticing that this man is strapped down at the wrists, and ankles. Man: “Sorry, bud. I know this isn’t the best way to wake up in the morning, even though we have no idea what time it is, seeing as we’ve been here for a while… (Chuckles.)” “We’ve?” Scanning in all the other possible directions, he notices that he too is strapped down, as well as three others: Another man, a woman, and a beastly looking… something. Man 1: “Hey, sport… what’s your name?” Lucifer: “Well, Lucifer… Lucifer Abromowitz Boradinsky. (Takes in a deep breath.)” The room itself, simply five metal medical tables in a circle, and hanging light over each. Strapped down to them, of course Lucifer, who is quite small, and wears a lab coat. The courteous one beside him has the attire well trained officer, all black. Across from his table, a woman’s physic totally concealed by a full body leotard. Next to her, a strange man with strange eyes, pale skin tone, and an ancient looking robe. And, finally there’s the massive purple man with shredded rags for covering his hinny. Lucifer: “Who… What… Where…?” Man 1: “Well, I’ll tell you what I know… We are all wrestlers, first of all. I’m John, the one over there is Thokor, make sure you don’t piss off the feminist bitch off, and the other guy… I think he’s a magician, but he doesn’t talk much.” In a heartbeat, everyone’s got something to say, just for the purpose of arguing. Magician: “WHAT? I AM NOT A MERE MAGICIAN, BUT A MASTER OF THE CELESTIAL ARTS! I AM A MASTER…” Thokor: “(Snarls, whilst in a sudden struggle to be free.)” Woman: “You will not address me as you have, for…” “SILENCE, YOU PATHETIC FOOLS!” Suddenly, a new echo fills the room, as the one and only door to the room opens. At first, he is only a silhouette. As this newcomer steps forward, he becomes the not spitting image, but the actual Syko the Goblin, himself. Wearing a cheap suit, and an even cheaper grin, he stands dead center in the middle the five tables, for all to see. There is silence. TBC
