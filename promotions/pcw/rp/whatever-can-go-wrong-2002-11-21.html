Whatever can go wrong...

21 November 2002

Title: Whatever can go wrong... Featuring: Tim Walden Date: November 21, 2002 The lingering doubts trace their elliptical motions back and forth in the mind, like a pendulum in that once one starts paying attention, it's hard to ignore them.. Tim Walden realized this, but it was rather late to do so, since once he realized it, he had obviously cast thought toward the doubts himself, and was thereby obligated to maintain as healthy a notice to them as he could keep...Funny enough, the doubts themselves were due to his obligation. This was no longer a matter of just him and Teresa, now that his opponents had been brought together by their intense hatred, every little thing the Trapezoid took from him could be thrown back in his face in the form of greatened punishment through his required actions..This was why he had been forced into the classroom, which may have turned out to have been his luckiest break of all..To be caught once again would mean that he would have to somehow escape both of their clutches to get away quickly. However, he knew the shape didn't seem to hold the concern about him that would allow him to be held on a consistent basis by both..As soon as Tomas slipped up, he could slip away, but there was risk that damage would still be done before, and this was why he had to be cautious...Maybe this was why he was walking to the rental Renault in the hotel parking lot, instead of the usual limousine. After all, goodness knows she expected arrogance on his part.. He wasn't just going to step blindly into the trap. 'Good evening, James.' ...Didn't necessarily mean he was going to drive the car himself, after all, it's easier to identify a driver in traffic and there's more room to work in with the pressure on like this..'Good afternoon, Mr. Walden..The arena, I presume?' 'Yep.' Tim pulled out a paper from the briefcase beside him. The arena's floor-plans, two paths marked on them converging at a single room...Usually used for the arena's management, it would be both easily convertible for another executive's usage and mostly secure from being accosted by others....The night's matches would provide his greatest challenge, but should he be compromised early, he would benefit by the opportunity to pummel his way to freedom once more. However, should this happen, he would be under pressure to fight once more, forcing him to remain in the building for longer than he had hoped to...The prize was worth the efforts. He could keep perfectly safe in the office, but would there be much of a point to it? Maybe being caught wouldn't be so bad, it was a risk he could take on occasion...Maybe, maybe he could afford to let the chance be taken if it meant going to the finals. Nobody wanted to be known as a washed-up loser who wouldn't fight a full-time tag wrestler, whether it be Gaz or Harry..Tim wasn't going to back down now. It wasn't worth backing down, he started to tell himself..Tell himself that there was nothing to worry about, so he shouldn't worry... He didn't have to worry, just as long as the Front Office was on his side... Just as long as he could stay away from Teresa outside of his opportunity to prove his dominance... Just as long as his self-assured superior technical skill could carry him over his relatively inexperienced opponents in the ring no matter what came about... Just as long as the Trapezoid didn't screw him over like so many times before when so many titles had slipped through his fingers... The car stopped a little earlier than usual. 'Sorry, sir, it appears we've hit a gridlock.' What was it about tournaments that always got the fans excited enough to come in greater droves than usual? It didn't matter, it was all good for the revenue of PCW anyway... 'Thanks, James.' The briefcase was shut, latched, pulled along by Tim as he shoved the door ajar and strolled away, brimming with confidence...Tonight would be as fine as the other nights should've been. He had no remaining reason to be self-doubting, everything goes according to plan... Of course, to think that would be defying the facts of life. Nothing ever goes just the way you planned it, Murphy would never stand for that... One would think that he might have known by now, but you could never be sure with the man whose career had gone from Canadian Jobber to 'Invincible' in a span of mere months and come right back down to earth as a lackey, just to snap and with it snap the stigma of untapped potential and fly into the stratosphere of fame.. Then, in an instant, it was nothing. He was gone, out of sight. Partly because of his own failures..He had the chance to be the #1 contender for the Continental Title, all he had to do was beat Kano. Something just went horribly wrong, maybe he choked, maybe Kano just had luck on his side and the all-important x-factors going his way..The fact remained that it was his opportunity, and he missed out...Perhaps that had something to do with what came in the weeks to come, Tim didn't know, but the fact remained that he wasn't responsible for his disappearance from the spotlight... That distinction belonged to one man, one man he had had little reason to trust in before and should have found absolutely no reason to trust the motives of then, for the 'Greediest Player in the Game' had never been one to forge alliances and keep his part before...But there was always some way to rationalize the actions for Tim, and this was one of those times where he missed out on the forest in favour of the trees. What had possessed him to hold any faith in Tyler Nelson was simple...The rise to the top he was in for if their takeover succeeded would be meteoric, gone from fighting Flaymin Aymin every damn week to main-events and first-class lifestyle, this was such a shock to the system that it almost acted as an anesthesia to the obvious: Tyler never kept his friendships..He held 50%, a controlling option of the stock under the deal...He always dumped his co-conspirators as soon as he got what he wanted, Tim would be no different...He hadn't cared. He wanted to get his piece of the pie, and he wasn't even taking into consideration how long it lasted... But when it was over, so suddenly after a decent run on top, the numbing effect was no longer there, having melted away from Tim to leave a burning hatred and a fire in his heart, the fire that did a lot for him...Without that fire, he might have held firm over his share of PCW and been happy to keep making money off of it instead of selling out in the hope that Xavier's plan might, could, would work out, and one day he would be back in the glory, one day he'd have the chance to get his revenge and show the world what he was capable of...He entered the halls, looking down at his map with a grin finally.. One day had come. He had survived his exile, and he wasn't going to let anything get in his way... ....Not the Trapezoid...Not Tyler Nelson...Certainly not Teresa Tomas. ....Or at least not if he'd realized his path was taking him past her locker room...'Hi Mr. Walden!' 'Heya, Pimply!' ....And for the first time in PCW history, Murphy's law took the form of an intern. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
