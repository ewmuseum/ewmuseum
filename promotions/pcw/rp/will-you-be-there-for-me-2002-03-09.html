Will you be there for me?

9 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-9-02 The end of Xavier Kannon is upon Scientology. When he chose to blast Alexander Miyagawa in the back of the head with a bottle of Snapple, the Krayzie One had no choice. Xavier Kannon is now going to have to face a pissed off former friend now when they meet for the Continental title on Turmoil. It's one thing to strike Jeremy out of anger at one's performance. It's another to strike one of Jeremy's friends to get back at the Krazed Lunatic. Xavier Kannon did just that..... And now he must meet his fate. But, that's still two days off. Kannon has two days to prepare for the psychotic madman that'll be entering the ring against him. Before that date with destiny though, Jeremy Howard has a few other things he needs to take care of on his priority list. One of which being, getting to know his son, Troy, better and spend time with his lovely and beautiful girlfriend, Jessica. And, with that, the scene fades into the bedroom of the Krazed Lunatic on the first floor of his luxurious home in Elkhart, Indiana. Jessica: Jeremy? The lovely blonde woman known to everyone as Jeremy's girlfriend leans up from her bed, looking around. There's no Jeremy around. She shoves her sheets and comforter off, stepping out of the bed, wearing a pair of blue and white plaid pajama bottoms, with red panties sticking out of the top, and a white shirt that bears alot of navel. Jessica: I thought he was supposed to be home last night..... Odd. Instead of sticking around her bedroom and wondering, the beautiful lady heads out of her bedroom and toward a set of stairs that sit ten feet in front of her. As she ascends the stairs, her bottom can be seen shaking from side to side, leaving quite the sight to see for all the perverts of PCW to see. Finally, she reaches the top and makes a left to Troy's room. Jessica: Troy? You okay baby? Jessica reaches out and grabs the knob on his door, turning it until the door opens. Inside the door, she can see her two year old son laying in his bed, sound asleep, next to his superstar father, Jeremy Howard. Jessica: Honey! There you are! Jeremy stirs with the tone of Jessica's voice. Jessica rushes over to Troy's bed, where her son and boyfriend lay. Jeremy sits up, staring at his girlfriend, while trying to adjust to his awakened state. Troy, was laying in his father's arms, continues to stay asleep as his parents talk. Jessica: I thought something might've happened to you. Jeremy shakes his head, trying to clear his head. Jeremy: No. I was extremely pissed when I got him, so I come and laid with Troy to calm down before I went and bothered you with my problems. Jessica: Things with Kannon not get better? Jeremy knods, 'no'. Jeremy: That whiny b!tch cracked Miya in the back of the head with a Snapple bottle, costing Miya his TV title. I'm pissed. Jessica puts her arms around Jeremy's neck, pulling his head in close to her ample breasts. Jessica: I don't blame you. All you do for some people and this is how they repay you. Jeremy: I know. I'm beginning to think that befriending people in the wrestling business is a bad idea. Miya, Dan and Duke are the only ones yet to stab me in the back. Jessica, while still holding Jeremy close to her chest, heart for that matter, lowers her head in a saddened state. Jeremy can't see it, but she becomes a bit misty in the eyes. Why though? Jessica: You forgot about me..... Jeremy pulls back from her, looking right into her face. Jessica: .....I helped you beat Drake Maxwell the first time and I'm still with you. Jeremy: I know. Jeremy wraps his arms around her, pulling her in closer to him. Jeremy: You're not someone I would ever suspect of turning your back on me. Jessica: Ah. It still hurts that you forget about me sometimes. Jeremy: I'm sorry. I've just had so much going on in my life recently that I forget things sometimes. This does little to ease Jessica's pain. She continues to find comfort in being in Jeremy's arms, but knows in her heart that she's not the first thing he thinks about anymore. Sadly, for her. Jeremy: How about, we leave Troy with Justin, so they can get to know each other, and you accompany me to the ring on Tuesday. Jessica sighs. Jessica: This doesn't have anything to do with the fact that you know Kannon is going to take Ellie out there with him? Jeremy: You take it for what you want. I'll take it as the person that's gotten me through so many obstacles in my recent life is by my side while I face a man I once trusted deeply. If Xavier Kannon, or the Gold Patron Meritorious as he sometimes calls himself, has any thoughts of having his precious Ellie help him to victory against the Krazed Lunatic, he's just had a wrench thrown in his plans. For, if his egotistical mind can remember back to the first day of January and the World title match Jeremy had with Drake Maxwell, then he'll remember how Drake Maxwell tried to cheat his way to a win, only to be turned back by the lovely Jessica and her quick thinking. Will history repeat itself? Jessica: Okay, I get your point. You don't have to rip me a new ass in the process. Jeremy: Well. Jeremy pauses, looking her directly into the eyes. Jeremy: Don't you ever doubt my love for you. I risked everything going to Terre Haute to get this wonderful little guy back for us. I think that should show you everything you need to know. Jessica: I'm sorry. I just get so scared while you're away that someone else will be come important in your life and not me. Jeremy gets to his feet, stepping out of the bed. Without so much as giving her another look, the Krayzie One storms out of Troy's room and down the steps. Jessica, beginning to cry at this point, chases after him. Suddenly, the scene cuts to the bottom of the stairs to find Jeremy making a right into the den of his home. Jessica: Jeremy! I'm sorry! Instead of stopping at the curio cabinet that prominently displays his championship belts of PCW and other various wrestling federations he's been in, Jeremy stops at a picture of him hitting the Krayzie Death Slam on Jacob Yoder right before he made the final and winning pinfall at Alliances 2001. Jeremy: You see that? Jessica knods her head, 'yes', while trying to control her tears. Jeremy: You have no idea what I went through to get that win. Two days before that match I was locked in an insane asylum by Kremmen. For two days, I had to continuously try to find ways to get out of there. Once again, he stops. Jeremy raises a finger, pointing toward the picture. Jeremy: Was it to get to Alliances and do that? Yes. I'll admit it. I wanted to get that pay per view so badly. I knew I had something to prove. But, thats not what got me out of that insane asylum. You were! Jessica: I'm sorry. Jeremy: There wasn't a waking moment in those two days that I didn't think about you. I thought about how much I wanted to get out of that place just so I could get to Alliances and show you that you weren't with a loser, but a winner. And I won! I won because of your love, your strength and your heart! Jessica: I'm so sorry. I should have never doubted you. Jessica, obviously having had enough, practically falls into Jeremy's arms. The Krayzie One catches her, sliding to the floor, while still holding her. Jeremy: In two days, when I fight Xavier Kannon, I would be the happiest man in the world if you'd accompany me to the ring. I love you. Jessica wipes her eyes, clearing her face of the tears as much as she can. Jessica: I love you too Jeremy. She bats her eyes, looking right at him. Jessica: And, I'd be honored to accompany you. Jeremy pulls her closer to him, holding her as tight as he can to show her just how much she's loved. Jeremy: I think it's time for us to show those two why I'm a former World and current Continental Champion and Xavier is nothing more than a former Cruiserweight Champion. If Xavier Kannon can think about something other than his ego, he should also remember than Jeremy Howard beat the man that he lost his NTICW World title too, being Drake Maxwell. Come Tuesday, at Turmoil, in the tables match for the Continental title, the Gold Patron Meritorious might get another memory to think about later..... Of how he went directly through a table at the hands of the Krazed Lunatic. With Jeremy continuing to hold his lovely girlfriend tightly, showing her all the love she deserves in her life, the scene fades out..... to black!
