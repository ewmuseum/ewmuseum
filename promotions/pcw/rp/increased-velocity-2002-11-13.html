Increased Velocity

13 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Increased Velocity Featuring: The H.I.T. Date: 11/13/02 The long and winding road begins... The pathway to San Antonio, Texas.. The pathway to Alliances. And, on that night, The HIT will take on one of the most strenous workloads in PCW history. The PCW Television Champion has three matches before him, none for his Television Title. Therefore, on this night for The HIT, there can only be gain. Gain of the Tag Team Titles, with his partner and friend, Teresa Tomas. Gain of the soda contract for PCW under the greatest drink ever, MOUNTAIN DEW! And, gain of a shot at some title, as a result of victory in Alliances. A busy night indeed for the Trapezoid, but his greatest enemy, Tim Walden, will also be in each and every one of these matches. Lots of chances for vengeance...and lots of possibilities for pain. Is HIT ready? You'd better believe he is.. :An annoying ringing noise makes its way over the landscape....vibrating relentlessly. The rumbling continues through the Belgian countryside, as its source is finally visible. A school bus is making its way down the dirt roads, driven by none other than the Sultan of Sides, the Bishop of Bisection, the Monarch of Midpoints.....THE HARDCORE ISOSCELES TRAPEZOID! The bus is pushing 90 miles an hour, as the HIT has turned the lonely road into his own personal Autobahn..: Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid: HARDCORE ISOSCELES TRAPE-ZOIIIIIIIID! ::The HIT is listening to his now-famous theme song. He smiles, bopping his head up and down to the music, turning to the seat next to him: The HIT: So, Jeff, you enjoying the ride? ::A 16 year old student sits in the seat behind him, desperately grabbing on to anything he can to keep from flying back on the seatbelt-less bus:: Jeff: I've been better! The HIT: Don't worry! Just hold on, you'll be fine! I need to crank this up FASTER!! Jeff: WHY? The HIT: Because, I've got to get in the right frame of mind! I've got three matches at Alliances, and a match against Syko the Goblin and his blasted duck, and I've got to be in top form! Jeff: And making a schoolbus go ONE-HUNDRED MILES AN HOUR ON AN UNPAVED ROAD HELPS YOU HOW?!?! The HIT: Jeff, it's simple. Wrestling's one gigantic game of CHICKEN! When you're fighting in an Alamo, in a ladder match, or with seven, or fifty-nine other guys, YOU DON'T WANT TO BE THE FIRST GUY TO FLINCH! FIRST ONE WHO FLINCHES LOSES! ::Trapezoid takes the school bus up five more to 105 miles per hour. Jeff is in near terror, as the speed now forces him against the back of the seat.:: The HIT: I MUST BE STRONG! All the moves in the world don't mean a damn if I can't take just about anything! Including physical punishment, and mental duress! Like this! 105 m.p.h.! You know of anything besides a vehicle that goes 105 mph? Jeff: NO! The HIT: A wrestling match! Especially with multiple men in it....guy sneak up behind you, making it seem as if they travel at the speed of light! The better your reaction time, the better chance you have! The fast reactions, quick decisions...all betters you as an athlete! Quick, Jeff, toss me the Mountain Dew! Jeff: Why? The HIT: Because, damnit, when you're going 105 miles an hour on a lonely Belgian street in the country, and you need something to drink, ONLY MOUNTAIN DEW WILL DO! NOW, GET ME THE DAMN DEW! ::Jeff obliges, tossing the drink up to the Trapezoid, who catches it, and opens it with his teeth. Meanwhile, he punches the bus up to 110 miles an hour, as he lets go of the controls for a moment, raises the can high, and chugs the Mountain Dew back! He tosses the can backwards, as it careens to the back of the bus!:: The HIT: NOW THIS IS ADRENALINE! Jeff: MR. TRAPEZOID! The HIT: THIS IS INVIGORATING! Jeff: MR. TRAPEZOID! The HIT: THIS IS-- Jeff: MR. TRAPEZOID, YOU'RE GOING TO HIT THE BARN! The HIT: OH SHIT! ::Trapezoid spots the red barn, and begins turning the wheel like hell, while simultaneously applying the brakes. The bus spins out, and finally stops, roughly three feet from the barn. He throws open the door, and Jeff runs out frantically, kissing the ground. HIT is nonplussed, taking another Mountain Dew, and steps outside the bus, drinking it.:: Jeff: How...How.....HOW THE HELL CAN YOU BE SO CALM AFTER WE NEARLY DIED! The HIT: Because, when you've just had a near death experience in a school bus and you're thirsty..... Jeff: AAAAAAAHHHHHHH!!! ::Jeff faints dead away, leaving a shrugging Trapezoid:: The HIT: Ah well...ALLIANCES! I and Mountain Dew shall triumph thrice! A tag title and a Soda Contract shall be mine! I'M GONNA BEAT YOU ALL DOWN! RIGHT INTO THE GRASS! I'M GONNA SMACK YOU ROUND! I'M GONNA KICK YO' ASS!!! ::Trapezoid grins, dragging Jeff's body back inside the bus, as we fade out to the MOUNTAIN DEW!:: ---- View The H.I.T.'s Biography
