Interview with the Devil: Satan Revealed (Part 2 of a series done in another fed, DO NOT count toward match)

28 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-28-03 The floating gray mass of a cloud continues to fill the room, as Shane has sat on his bed in fear. Holding his Bible near to him, a foot emerges from the dark cloud. Shane begins to slow press himself toward the opposite side of the bed; the fear growing in his eyes even more. Another foot emerges and the smoke begins to rise to the ceiling of the room and the figure of a man is left standing before Shane. Sheer terror captivates Shane overwhelming him to the point of having him gasping for air. The figure continues to proceed through the room and to the edge of Shane’s bed, where it flips on a light. Shane continues to move and comes tumbling off of the side of the bed, to hit the floor with a rather loud thud. With the light now able to help Shane focus in on the actual identity of the other being in the room, he cautiously brings his head up to the edge of the bed. The man, looking rather harmless, was dressed almost as casual as any other person you would see walking down the street, but this could obviously not be any ordinary person; or so Shane obviously thinks. Shane begins to make his way from the floor to his feet as he finally finds his words, “Who are you?” The man keeps himself quiet for some time, as he reaches into his jacket and pulls out a cigarette. Shane still befuddled at the sight of this ominous figure continues to examine him like he has never done another figure before in his life. The man takes the cigarette and places it into his mouth; with the flick of one two fingers a spark of fire begins to blaze, from which he lights the slender stick hanging from his lip. Shane’s eyes grow large once more at the sight of this as the figure begins to chuckle a little bit. “What are you laughing at you fool?” Shane says in a furious tone, his eyes piercing into the soul of the man, “That’s nothing but witchcraft and it does not impress me!” The man closes his eyes and begins to shake his head a bit in disbelief of Shane’s comments, “You could be no more wrong, boy. Do you really not understand who you are in the presence of?” Shane begins to think as he lowers his eyebrows, “No, it would probably be in your best interest to just come forward with your name.” “I’m sure you’ve heard of me,” The man says as he begins to pace slowly to one side of the room, where a chair is located, “You’ve heard tale of me all of your life I’m sure,” He then proceeds to sit down into the chair as he lets forth a sigh of relief. “I am that being that distills fear into every heart of man. I am the reason you are where you are in this world now. For if it wasn’t for me, then that apple would have never been eaten.” The man begins to chuckle even more, as his body begins to move slightly. “You call me a fool, but since the beginning the only fools that I have come to know are that of the human race!” Shane stays leveled off in his position beside the bed, “I’m getting ready to call security. I don’t know how you got into my room the way you did, but you are certainly not going to continue to harass me the way you are.” Shane reaches for the phone as the man begins to chuckle even more, “What are you laughing at?” “Do you really think that once you lift that phone you’ll have an actual dial tone?” The man asks leaving Shane with a very weird look on his face, “Take a look around you and tell me this is the same hotel room you were once in. You’ll be surprised to know that you are not in Kansas anymore, Dorothy.” The look of confusion grows even more heavily on Shane’s face, “Explain yourself to me. This room looks exactly the same as it did when I first entered it. Your words are a labyrinth of perplexity to me.” The man stands to his feet and walks gradually to the curtains next to him, “Come and open these blinds and see for yourself what it is that I speak of.” Shane finally budges himself from his positioning and begins to make his way over to the curtains. The man motions to the curtains and he offers the drawstrings to Shane. Continuing to watch the man with astute accuracy, Shane takes the drawstrings from the man and pulls the curtains open revealing a horrific scene of terror and anguish. Fire and brimstone come shooting up from the ground outside and Shane’s face grows even paler than it has ever been. Shane then comes stumbling backwards and crashing into the bed, Shane begins to grab at his chest as he continues to panic. “How much more does it take for you to realize just who I am, Shane?” The man closes the curtains quickly as Shane continues to look at him with open eyes and sweat pouring down from his head. “Was that not enough of a sign for you to fully understand that I am who I say I am?” The man thrust his arms into the air as he continues to empathize every word coming from his mouth. “No…” Shane declares as he continues to shake his head furiously, “You are not here… I vowed to never let you enter into my life!” Shane ends yelling out his finally words. The man raises one hand and all becomes silent and the room becomes pitch black once more, “You see, Shane, you are wrong. They are all wrong; they all proclaim that I shall never enter into their lives, but the truth is that I am always there.” The darkness begins to get even darker as Shane cannot even see the nose in front of his face, “People would always have you believe the things that they know are not true, Shane. Why else would they try to fight it so much?” “I’m really confused; why do I feel like this?” Shane asks as he covers his entire face with his hands and continues to shake his head in disbelief. “Yes, I can see it in your eyes and I can hear it in your voice, Shane. You are not devoted to your cause,” The man continues speaking, putting much more emphasis on his words, as he walks closer to Shane, “You wouldn’t realize how much this world tries to make you feel that you are nothing and you always stand around here praying that it will all get better, but does it? Tell me honestly, Shane, has there ever been a time in your life when what you have prayed for has ever come true?” Shane lifts his head up, and anger begins to spew from within him as he jumps to his feet and begins throwing fingers at the man, “Satan, you listen to me! The Lord is my Shepard and I will not let you drive me astray! I am a sheep for the Lord, not a goat walking this Earth doing your bidding!” Shane reaches down, pulls his Bible to his side and begins to push it out forward toward the man, “You’ve been here too long and you’ve tried to show me things to bring me into your womb! I say to you now! BE GONE!” With that said, the man begins to walk backwards slowly to the door, “Mark my words, Shane, your heart is not true to your cause and I will come for you once more. You will see when that time comes that you are weak, and then you will embrace me as your own. You are my spawn.” He pauses for a second as he opens the door and turns into it, “My son!” Suddenly the room goes dark again as Shane comes lifting out of his apartment bed. Sweat beading on his forehead and draining quickly from his face. The look of shock, bewilderment, and then a sudden relief strikes Shane’s face as he slowly lays himself back down into the bed, to try and gain a little more rest. Hoping nothing of the same happens to him again. The picture freezes on that and we’re left wondering, was it really a dream, or was this in all actuality the truth? ~Series to be Continued~
