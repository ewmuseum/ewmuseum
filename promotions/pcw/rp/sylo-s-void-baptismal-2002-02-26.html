Sylo's Void Baptismal...

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-26-02 And just as all things have a beginning and middle... ...the end has arrived. Sabotage left a marking upon the stable called the International Incident. It was official...the Divinity consisting of Akira Akiyama and Titu Xansiv were herby severed from the group. As Ty Nelson would say, that's a wise business move. Looks like El Diablo learned a little from the gods as well...he also departed from the stable. He talked about the stable not being unified because of the members...though he failed to notice that he was a part of the problem himself. But you cannot blame him...jobbers are known for making mistakes repetitiously...and we will see that soon. But with that problem out of the way, lets get to the other matter at hand. Sabotage...Jay Sylo came out to save the gods from the International tantrum those four had. It was all cool...Flaymin Aymin, Borro, and El Diablo were soon to find their reward. But Sylo...he came to the aid of the gods, and for that he will be gifted with those things that society tends to overlook. It seems impossible to obtain heavenly status when one is so soiled by mortals daily, but this one individual shall find his bearing. Turn back to the past... Jay Sylo sat in a dark room against a steel chair. There didn’t seem to be any light source in the room, but he was still able to see within the dark room. It was like the walls, ceiling, and floor were painted black. But all he knew was that his head hurt...HARD. Some would say that he is a big man, in the high two hundred pound class, and in the upper middle six-foot range of height. But size never had anything to do with things like this. Only skill and technique...as well as the abilities. It must have taken a greater person to take out someone the likes of him. He knew his body was quaking with the pain like a shivering child. But all around him there was black. He tired of sitting there with nothing or no one around him. He tried shouting, but there was no sound from him. It was like he was deaf...or he was mute. Then he tried standing, but he body wouldn’t respond. It was like he was petrified, in suspended animation and still conscious. In a natural state of humanity, he began to panic. Then a voice of clarity came through... It told him that panicking was normal for him...along with the fear...the uncertainty he felt. He had no idea what was going to be done with him, he had no idea what his life was in store for. Here he was, captive by some strong unknown force, and now he felt as if his existence was in store for a drastic impact. He was told that the pain racking through his body...it was normal of those not enlightened. There were so many mortals out there looking to be defeated, and because of those who wish to take their own pace towards the top, mortals were going to stay thinking that they were the top of the evolutionary cycle. Humanity knew of their limitations, yet they still tried to expand themselves to become more...and that was simply not possible. But things were going to change...soon... The voice told him not to stir...told him to sit still. It was more like a sarcastic suggestion rather than an order. He knew that Jay wasn’t going anywhere. Jay could hear his voice as clear and transparently like the silicon in Cindy Bi's chest. Yet his mind would not let him place the voice with any form of identification. But he knew that he was being spoken to...and he knew the words were important as well as correct. Could this be the voice of reason? The voice of judgment? No...neither one or the other... This was the voice of the one who was condemned to a fate he never asked for. Just like a child is pushed into doing something he wishes not to by his parents, he is so. This is the voice of the one who has been judged repeatedly because of his name, and because of what he represents. It's the status he was born into that drew him into this position. This was the voice of one who was Cursed...from birth...to be bound to the negativity of the universe. Because of his curse, those who wish to be skeptical of his threat doubt his existence, and his status is declared a lie, nothing more than a egotistical psychological tool. But they are all misguided... What do you see before you, the voice asked Jay Sylo. He had no ability to answer, but the voice knew what his reply was. The Darkness... You would know it as the Void... The owner of the voice came from behind Jay, and stared him right in his face. Sylo thought his image looked familiar, but like before, his mind was unable to generate any form of identification. But the man was large, quite larger than him actually. Asian...with black and silver hair in a ponytail atop of his head. Dressed in all black was he, like an executioner of more modern times. But his eyes...those almond orbs...they held more than the soul of a man. They seemed to embrace humanity entirely... But the man went on with the message to Sylo. He continued to speak his mind about the reason that Jay Sylo was abducted. He said that a time was approaching...and from that time there would approach a new enlightenment throughout existence. But first the existence had to know of their part in the grand scale...they had to know how things were going to be played out. So far, one man has continued the mission...but now there were more to aid him in his quest. More ethereal beings to assist...to help open the eyes of society to what already lies within them... But first Sylo was going to have to be brought about into cleansing. The man waved his hand before Sylo, and his body acted against his will. He stood up to his feet before the other man, as rigid and as stiff as Darkstar used to be. Then the man before him placed his hand atop of his head, palming his forehead like a pomegranate. Then with foreign words spewing from his lips, he proceeded to push Jay Sylo down...straight down. The floor underneath Sylo seemed to liquefy and take movement. Then Jay realized that the entire room was moving. The darkness that was around him swirled and flowed like it was living liquid, slight unnoticeable streaks of silver breaking through it altogether. His feet were sinking into the darkness as the man before him shoved him down like a nail. But as before when all he could feel was pain, now he felt a tingling chill sweep over him, starting from his feet and creeping all the way up into his body. He was pushed down slowly until he found himself nose deep within the dark substance. And just as his eyes gazed glassy upwards at he who baptized him, his mind opened wider than the space in between Boda's head. There was finally clarity...understanding...ENLIGHTENMENT... The present is now... There are three beings atop one of the buildings within the city. It is the start of a brand new day, the sun rising to their eastern side. Below them the cars bustled and hustled along towards their destinations, the mortals all oblivious to the fight that was soon to take place in the shadows away from their vision. You see...in their eyes, the evil existed in those individuals who bore oversized evil, those who abused the power they stole, those who hated a country because their own weren’t as powerful as the one they looked to terrorize. But when they closed their eyes to sleep at night, that's when the other side of evil wished to lurk. That is when those who sought themselves to be new messiahs came out...among the other deviants. Some of them would look to turn the tables on these three...but that is just how they wish to disavow their own wrong doings. Twisted thinking...as always... The first of the trio was the tanned, toned, and distinguished of the Pharaoh, Titu Xansiv. He looked down at the people below with a regal bearing about him, his royal blood showing through a thousand years past. He was the smallest of the trio, though that certainly did not make him the least threatening. It was he who shared ownership of the tag team titles...the Divinity bringing some godly bearing to the belts. He was the first recruited...and most certainly not the last. Next was the hulk of the alliance. Jay Sylo...now looming over them all like a divine golem. In his former self, he was not as large as now, hulking over at least three hundred and fifty pounds and ranging about seven foot three in height. His eyes were ghostly white, with dilated pupils of ice blue to match his hair and goatee. He was a monster of sorts in appearance, right down to the low growl coming from within his midsection. But unlike the monsters that society viewed, Jay Sylo was a divine monster...a Celestial Knight. And finally, the man who started it all...the one man everyone has grown to hate, yet recently tolerate. He was dressed in black vinyl and leather from neck to toe, silver buckles attached to their various places. His black and silver hair was shimmering like the stars, and his face, though it bore a darkness, was handsome and solemn. Those almonds stared out towards the sun with a determined look. He crouched on the edge of the rooftop and brooded with his palms pressed together in a prayer fashion. 'These people...they looked down upon us at one point. Some of them still do in a way...just for the mere fact that they envy our status. But forgiveness is not the tool we are able to wield at this point. No...the only tools that we can use now is violence and knowledge. So many of them wish to be more than they are truly destined to be, and we cannot blame them for it. But they have to come to the conclusion sometime amidst their constant failure that it is simply not their time...to be a champion...” Akiyama sighed heavily, shaking away the thoughts of how they once were treated. ‘But now they look to us for their heroism, which is the way things have always been. We are Celestials...heavenly beings...fighters looking to bring unity into the existence of the universe. This March Madness brawl will be so perfect to unite many skilled fighters in the harmony of battle, and with that, we will be one step closer towards the true enlightenment.”“Most people will never suspect us to win this battle...and we personally think that the odds are slim indeed. But that is not the issue. What is important is that we go into that battlefield and show as many of the participants that the Neo Void God is not going to walk in and walk out empty handed. When we get in there...we’re planning to take out as many of those misguided souls as we can...and through their pain, maybe they can see the truth...” He stood up and cracked his knuckles before looking to his other allies. “Pharaoh...Sylo...it’s time to get down with the Madness...”
