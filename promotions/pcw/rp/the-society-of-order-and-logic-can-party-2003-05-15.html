The society of order and logic can party.

15 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15th May 2003 Everyone loves to party, whether it be some B list celebrity or some wrestler in PCW, it’s all the same. The feeling of getting down and rather jiggy as someone once put it sends shivers down most people’s spines. Just because you’re a hugely suspicious cult who the FBI, M6 and many other major world secret agency’s are checking out carefully, doesn’t mean you don’t have the right nor the want to throw a hugely successful party. And so, because of a certain match on Tuesday Turmoil in which a certain Ukrainian sniper managed to pick up his first 1,2,3 win in the federation named as PCW, there’s nothing better than to throw a ‘shindig’. Of course, only members of the illustrious Society of Order and Logic were able, or rather allowed to attend. Those from far out American or Finland, who’d joined through the catalogue for a year or lifetime membership for just $19,995 finally got the chance to meet, and, if they were clean enough, touch the hand of Michael Chang, the ultimate leader of the Society. And so, in a farmyard in the deep south of England, somewhere unknown to most people, stood SOCIETY. Yes, but this time, the perfect human being stood, ready to use his huge frame if any trouble arose, and with his figure lurking, Chang was pretty sure no one would cause any trouble. Inside, balloons were attached to the ceiling via ropes, Chang stood at the far end by the snack table munching on a special ‘luxury’ Society of Order and Logic sausage roll, and Sivasian Krikorian shadily and sheepishly stood by the door. He took of his trenchcoat and placed it on the several hooks on the side. Knowing that it would be terrible punishment for anyone caught stealing around a SOL meeting , or in this case party, he simply left his belongings inside. He played with the wool of his jumper, it made him itch, but his wife said he looks ‘hot and cute’ in it. He watched the various nationalities meet, and of course, not understand, but nothing seemed to care tonight, for this party was for him. Chang slurped down some plain, fresh water, after having a fellow member test for chemicals, before walking over to Sivasian. Chang: Good times eh Siv? Sivasian: Yes, yes I do say so sir, great times to be me , anyway. Chang nodded, he tried to make conversation with the usually quiet Ukrainian, but apparently to little success. Chang: Oh, nice wedding ring, I take it your married? Sivasian flashes the silver diamond in the light of the party. Sivasian: Well, err….. He coughed slightly. Sivasian: Yes sir, yes I am. Chang: Then why didn’t your wife come with you to this marvellous social event, the event celebrating the fact YOU won a match at last. Sivasian: You, in fairness sir, told me she wasn’t part of the Society, and indeed she never signed the contract, for now, we are on telephoning grounds, I refuse to see her for her unfaithfulness. Chang smiles. Chang: Excellent then, we can’t have people back stabbing or trying to get around the Society without directly being in and giving their possessions in , in the hope that we can achieve something, is there old boy? Sivasian: We can’t sir, not at all. Chang takes another sip of the water, as he frowns at the Finnish people flashing their chests towards the American ‘hunks’. Chang: Oh god! Sivasian: May I ask why they are in here sir, are they part of the Society? Chang hangs his head slightly and nods slowly. Chang: They are as such, but are not. They are not in the exclusive range of you, SOCIETY or I, but they paid their money, and they were given a temporary contract to the Society. Of course, we’re milking them dry for their money, see the big busted woman on the right. Sivasian checks, and indeed a blond woman with a larger than normal chest winks back. Sivasian: Yes. Chang: She’s given more than $2,000,000 to this great Society of ours, that’s going to pay for stuff, for this party for instance. Krikorian: Of course sir, but, do I really, NEED this party? Chang slightly laughs a little, then slaps Krikorian on the middle of his back. Chang: Why, of course my fellow, we need you to feel wanted within the Order of Society and logic, and since you’re slightly more, shall we say, charismatic, than my dear friend SOCIETY, we know we appreciate these sorts of thing. Krikorian ,feeling slightly disgruntled about the whole not seeing his wife thing, and the attention, shys away towards the food stand. He takes a large beaker of punch before standing, and watching the different nationalities again dance. Krikorian: Ugh. A large, blonde woman, hailing from the states bundles over. She’s obviously had too much to drink. Krikorian: Jesus woman, what’s your problem? The woman smiles a dirty, grease and black toothed grin towards our favorite Ukrainian. She then winks and leans over, resting herself on the man. Woman: Hey baby, are you and your good looks and experience looking for some action. Krikorian blushes in the attention he is receiving and shudders. Krikorian: Please, leave me alone woman, go away, take your scanky, sexual transmitted infected vagina somewhere else! As the woman looks in disbelief, and proceeds to slap Krikorian HARD round the face, the crowd suddenly falls into silence and turn towards Krikorian and dirty woman. Michael Chang sees this and catches eye contact with Krikorian. Chang frowns slightly before Sivasian can say anything, then matches over. Chang: Well people! Nice to see we can get some Society of Order and Logic relationships going on! Round of applause for this lovely err…couple! There is a slight silence, before someone starts the golf type clapping, and everyone joins in. Chang: Excellent! As the crowd goes back into a mingling type of mode, Krikorian pulls Chang aside. Krikorian: Sir, she was not with me, nor have I ever seen her before. She started coming onto me. Chang: Well, she’s an outsider of the group usual, paid her way in, unlike you, who’s pure gold! Krikorian: Yes sir. Chang: Anyway boy, you need to put in a good showing on Fusion and Turmoil to really push through the Society of Order and Logic once more! Krikorian: Of course. Chang: Excellent….more punch? Looking into the punch bowl and seeing various pieces of filth floating about, Krikorian shakes his head. Krikorian: I think I’ll pass sir. Chang: Excellent.
