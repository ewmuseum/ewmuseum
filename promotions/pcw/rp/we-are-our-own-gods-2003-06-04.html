We are our own gods..

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/04/03 (OOC: Just disregarding my past 'PART' for the contagion and bacteria making.. Anyway, just a small portion of information I'm putting out for Stone being on his own, in attempt to go his own ways and put out more slaughters, now prohibiting god from his life.) Wandering in the vehement winds of the late evening like a knight-errant on task was Stone Burns, completely covered by his mackinaw coat impervious to the winds. He'd began to once again, like months ago start visiting the parts of Bainbridge that were held most sacred to him. Bainbridge had always been quite warm, yet still chilled by the late evening frigidness. Before him, visually touching the cloud-enveloped sky was the cross exuding the omni potency in just the masonry. Stone milled around the gate-swathed church, as it had been different then month’s ago.. There were people gathered around before the church, pagans specifically looking at it with disgust and transferring eerie incantations from their mouths. He ignored the insults as he'd walked through the team of disbelievers, hearing the surprising roar from a priest from inside, clad in a black, toga-like dress with a cross-coiled around his neck... 'GOD WILL PUNISH YOU ALL!' the priest routed returning in to the church with the protection of the doors from the pagans running up the stairs in beeline to capture him.. They returned back as Stone stared them down, equipping his blade to the side of him.. He walked up the church pavement stairs, looking back at the unit of people, and pulling back his blade. Stone opened the doors to the church, scaring the priest who'd been looking through the glass eyehole. 'STAY AWAY SATAN'S SON!' he shouted, resounding through the entire church. Stone shook his head, even though he did look like he was apart of the assemblage formed outside of the church. He laid his hands on the priest and continuously shook his head while retorting.. 'No.. I've come here to give a prayer.' From the serene onslaught in his mind, a prayer even though coming from the diabolical misogynist was not surprising. The priest nodded, and smiled as Stone advanced on by the priests hand signals and gestures. He walked in to the perfect; tandem of benches seemingly superfluous as groups wouldn't come in that large on specified days. Stone looked up in the heavens and to the side of the church top, viewing the perfect embroidery of the Jesus Christ pictures and crosses glowing beautifully. He strode forward down in accompany by the church red carpet. Making his way to the front, before a podium and platform overcastted by a large cross he knelt down on a single step leading to the higher platform. 'God (if there is one).. Give me the power like you did many, many months ago for me to make the brutes and harlots suffer painfully. Give me the power to dwindle the feminine gender, and give me the power to overcome anything, especially the brutes that comes in my way.' he averred evilly, waiting for a response. Unexpectedly the deafening roar of thunder from the heavens outside shook the church. Stone shaking his head in disapproval, stood up, looking in to the cross and up in to the church ceiling. 'I knew It.. you were nothing but a fake.. All along I gave myself the power.. I gave myself the valor and strength to endure the brutes.. not you.. I. And I thought you were supposed to be the omnipotent. Now you're making my begin to believe you're a woman.. A brute like the rest. But sooner or later you shall all decay, especially you.' he culled, insulting the prefect at the 'upper level'. He groaned inside, and spit so far onward his saliva sprayed over the cross in a way displaying his disgust. Walking back, forward out he didn't pinion his thoughts as he profusely began spitting over the house of worship benches and carpets. He shouted ever so audible obscenities towards the windows of the church and to whomever, in the church might've heard it. Stone made his way to the exit of the church as the priest confusingly looked on and asked. 'What was that?' Stone chuckled lightly and patted the priest on the back, disguising and blanketing his uproar. 'Oh nothing, must've been the thunder from the outside.' The priest nodded understandingly as Stone exited, and emerged down the stairs hearing the hampering yet amusing sounds of the incantations continuing on and on like a broken record. This time, instead of defending god, his house, and the priest he went on, screaming to the top of his lungs, literally swaying the sycamore trees and having it's leaves fall from the boisterous tone... 'THERE IS NO GOD!' Finishing the pagans commended the words and continued on, hoisting their hands in to the air and screaming even louder as Stone ended with a sickening look across his face, cosseting his diminutive, blood-caked blade. 'We are our own gods.'
