Date: When A Man Loves a Woman OOC Note: I didn’t particularly like this chapter of my writing. There wasn’t much dialogue that fit the Syko character, and I , not wanting to totally change the character, did as much as I could. That said, the next chapter should be a very good one. Enjoy this one though, it’s not too bad. Rushbo opened his eyes, and rubbed his eyes again. He saw nothing. For a moment, he wondered if he was blind, for he couldn’t remember what had happened. He felt around in the air with his hands, but there was nothing near him. He was frightened, to say the least. He was almost as frightened as when he was first kidnapped by a shit lot of white people. He slowly got up, groping around at the floor on all fours. He still found nothing. There were no walls, no doors. He continued to crawl around on the floor, still not making any noise, until his hand found a cold ring of steel. This would change his Crunkit life forever, but he didn’t know that yet. Without really thinking about what he was doing, Rushbo put the ring into his pocket, he would examine it later once he escaped from this horrid place. Dejected in every way, Rushbo just stopped and sat on the floor, feeling extremely sorry for himself, even though he knew he could kick John Crichton’s jobber ass. He couldn’t think of any reason of why he was where he was, why the goblins hadn’t caught him, whether John Crichton and his pathetic whining were somehow related to Drake Maxwell. After a while of feeling sorry for himself, Rushbo reached into his pocket for some CrunKola that he had stashed in his pocket before leaving the house and just felt the cold can. Rushbo: Boy, I sure love this delicious, refreshing, one of a kind drink, CrunKola. It not only refreshes your mind, but gives you amino acids or some shit like that. Well, we’re saying that it is, but it’s basically sugar, water, and some stuff that we can’t identify. That’s right kids, tell your mom you want some CrunKola! Somwhere inside the cave, Rush’s teeth gleamed as he smiled towards a camera that wasn’t there. He reaches back into his jacket pocket, searching for matches, but he came to the hilt of his short sword, CrunKiller. He had forgotten about it during the incident with the goblins, but apparently so had the goblins themselves, for it still sat in perfect condition at his hip. He pulled it out of its sheath and looked at the pale blue glow of the great weapon. Rushbo: So it is of Elven making. It seems I’m not out of the goblin’s reach, but those cracka goblins won’t find me anytime soon. Somehow, the glow of the blade brought comfort to the little Crunkit sitting in the dark. It was pretty crunk, at least he thought, to be holding a blade made in Gondolin to spill the blood of goblins. He knew that in his hand he held a weapon so great, that the sight of it scared goblins. Rushbo: Let’s see. Can I go back? Nope, probably white people there. Can I go to either side? I don’t know if there is an either side. That leaves moving forward. I guess I better get hoppin’ then. Up the small Crunkit got, no longer feeling downtrodden, and walked off down the tunnel, holding his sword in front of him, feeling like the most powerful king of all the lands in the world. Now, Rushbo was in a confined space here. However, this space wouldn’t be so very cramped for Rushbo as it would be for a person of normal size. Crunkits are also much more used to tunnels then us, and these tunnels were not all that much different that some Crunkit homes. Most of those houses were in the Crunkit hood, but that’s an entirely different story. So, having recovered from bumping his head, Rushbo had a fair idea of where he was going. That is another thing about Crunkits, they take injuries very well. It didn’t hurt him either that his feet could trot along quietly in the damp dark halls of the cave. The tunnel seemed to never end. Rushbo knew to keep going in the same general direction, despite some turns and twists in the path. There were side tunnels all over that he felt running his hand along the wall. He didn’t pay attention to these, except to scurry past them on his Crunkit legs, scared of the things that could lie ahead in them. On and on he went like this, down, down, further into the cave, not sure of his actual destination, only that it was away from the crazy white people so it couldn’t be all that bad. Suddenly, without the slightest warning or change of color from Tom Ridge’s little safety system, Rushbo stumbled right into a large gathering of icy cold water. Rushbo: Damn! Black people weren’t made to crawl around in skanky little holes like this. I need to be where I belong. There is a long silence. Rushbo: Of course I know where I belong, it’s uhh… oh shut up handler. Anyways, the shock of touching the water had made Rushbo pull up short and stop right there. He couldn’t tell if it was just a pool or the edge of a cave creek, or even the bank of a huge underground lake. Now, his sword barely had its blue faint glow to it. When he stopped, he could hear the plip-plop of water falling into the pool below, but there was no other sound, other than his mumbled cursing about damn white people and their caves. Rushbo: So, that means that it’s a lake or a pool, collecting over millions of years from those drops. Ugh, I feel like I’m on the fucking Discovery Channel. Can’t a brutha get some light down here? Not to Rush’s knowledge, there was a creature who lived down in these caves. This creature had lived here for a long time, perhaps before even the goblins lived there, but no one was sure exactly how long the creature had lived there. The creature was darker than dark, and just as mysterious as how Xavier Kannon stole Isaac Hayes for his own evil uses in Scientology. The creature had a little boat that he rowed around the lake, quiet as a mouse and never making a ripple. No he did not make a ripple. Syko the Gollum was his name, and this lake was his hunting domain. Tonight, he was out hunting for fish. Syko loved meat, but he was on a strict tofu diet at the moment, because *gay voice* if you get fat then you can’t be thuper like Thteven Thmith and Boca~! So, Syko was out, waiting for some tofu to fall onto his midgetty Gollum ass. Syko actually lived in Transylvania, but for the hell of this promo, he’s not technically Syko, so he lived on a rock covered in slime in the middle of the lake. Right now he was watching Rushbo with his eyes that were like, I don’t know, something that sees really good. Rushbo couldn’t see Syko the Gollum, but Syko was wondering about him, and wondering whether perhaps he was made of tofu… Rushbo: Hell no I’m not made of tofu. I’m 100% dark meat, and you can feel free to suck my meat if you disagree, biatch. Rushbo gets a little uppity when he talk about his skin color as you can see. He also likes to talk about having a large wang, but we all know the truth that short guys are erm… a bit smaller in certain areas than the rest of us, if you know what we mean. Rushbo: I’ll kill you, handler. I’ll cap your dumb black ass full of lead. Anyways, back to the story. Syko got into his boat and whispered to himself, wondering about the origin of the creature, which he expressed very eloquently. Syko: BNARGH!! SRAFFAGNAFFA!! Apparently that means “What is he, my precious” but Syko, not the best person in the world at keeping quiet while talking, was attracting the attention of Rushbo. Rushbo: Why is there a puny little white man out there screaming in a boat? Doesn’t he know there’s a black man on the shore who is about to punch his ass out if he doesn’t shut up? Syko sped off, going strangely fast for a little boat propelled only by oars. Rushbo watched him intently as he came closer, and he once again eloquently expressed his thoughts as he reached the shore. Syko: GNAARRR!! BLAAARGH GOLLLUM!! Rushbo: Dude, do you always have to scream like that, it gets really annoying and you could just talk like a regular person, every a regular white person. Syko: No I can’t talk like a regular person- I mean BARRGGG!!JANAAAA!! Rushbo: Okay then. I know you can understand me, but you have to show me the way out of here. I’ll wrestle you for the right to have a guide. Syko: A little pisser like you- I mean RABAGNABBA! JINNNNLLLEEEEEE!!! Rushbo: Okay great. Thus, they started. Actually, it went more like this. Rushbo kicked Syko the Gollum in his goblinoids, if you know what we mean, and pinned him 1…2…3 Rushbo: I pinned you, now you have to show me the way out of here. Syko: Fine I don’t wanna eat your ugly ass anyway. Rushbo: What about saying GNARR?? Syko: Oh right. RAKKAAAKKKKAAA!!! Syko got into his little boat. Syko the Gollum’s birthday present, which was the golden ring, that co-incidentally was in Rushbo’s pocket at the moment, was the present worry of Syko. He muttered about his birthday present as he paddled back towards his little island, where he hid the golden ring that he called his birthday present. Syko: What has it got in its pocketses? GLLAAARR!! Rushbo: What did you lose? Because there is not going to be any Rodney King’s goin’ down here and then you say that you were just looking for what was in my pocket. Now, the fire in Syko the Gollum’s eyes was more intense than ever. He was paddling back towards the shore very quickly, and he was mad with rage. Rushbo saw that he meant to murder him, so being the smart Crunkit that he was, he jetted up the slope. Syko: What has it got in its pocketses?? GNAARRGH!! Rushbo went quicker than ever, but he heard the hisses right behind him. Without even thinking, he had slipped the ring on his finger. He tripped though, catching his foot on a loose rock. And Syko was upon him!! Before Rushbo could even raise his sword, though, the strange creature passed by, sprinting up the slope. He knew that Syko the Gollum could see in the dark, but why had he just run by. Rushbo got up, shaking off the effects of the fall. He followed quietly behind Syko, perhaps he would lead Rushbo to a way out of the tunnel. Rushbo continued to follow Syko, who cursed at himself for losing the ring. Finally, Syko the Gollum arrived at the “back-door” to the cave. Gollum found no one, he hissed and shrieked and made the worst noise, one never even known to man. The new passage was low and primitively built. It was tough, even for Rushbo to go through. It climbed and climbed, and then sloped down steeply. Rushbo ran down the final slope towards an area that seemed to be filled with light. He burst into the area, and found that the sunshine was really just the sunshine coming through a slat in a great iron door. He blinked from the light, then he saw the goblins. They were all alert, looking in every way. In fact, the goblins saw him before he saw them. Yes, they did indeed see Rushbo, even with the ring. Whether it was a trick of light or a trick of the ring, his ring was not on his finger. The goblins rushed him madly. He quickly put his hands into his pockets, and luckily his ring was in his left coat pocket. He quickly slipped it on, confusing all the goblins who were near. All the goblins stopped short. They could no longer see him. Confsued, they went off in every direction to look for him. Relieved, Rushbo walked towards the giant door. However, it was like a terrible torture. He had to carefully step past all the goblins, and one wrong step would mean his death. He felt as though they could all see him and were playing with him in a big game of cat and mouse. Finally, he arrived at the door. It was not open, but a goblin had started to push it. He tried to move through, and got half his body through, but somehow he couldn’t get the rest through. He was stuck!! He could see outside, the mountains in the distance and the fresh air, but he could not move!! Goblin: Look, there’s something outside. Chaps, let’s go get that blood fiend!! Ignoring the blatant white people talk, Rushbo moved with more strength than he ever had in his life. Buttons burst off his coat everywhere, but he somehow squeezed through the doorway. He burst out. Soon the goblins came after him, but white people don’t like sun, it makes them more and more like black people. So they had to go back inside, but Rushbo was already far off, for he had escaped…
