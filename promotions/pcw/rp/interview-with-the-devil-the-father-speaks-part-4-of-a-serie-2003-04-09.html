Interview with the Devil: The Father Speaks (Part 4 of a series done in another fed, DO NOT count toward match)

9 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-9-03 The scene opens gradually inside a small hotel room. We find that the room isn’t in the best of shape and doesn’t look like it has ever been in too good of shape. The walls are bleak and uncolored, much like that of a prison cell and to one person that is exactly what this place is; a prison. The lights in the room so dim that you can barely see the nose in front of your face, but there is light never the less. Light that could only set the mood of anyone into that of being depressed. The obvious look of mess is all over the room considering all of the luggage scattered about, unlike anything you would ever see in your life unless you had a seven-year-old child and even then their room would be much cleaner than this. A person could never dwell in a place such as this, not even that of a pig would have this place. Then the unthinkable becomes the conscious and the sound of a person comes streaming throughout the room, bouncing from wall to wall and echoing the harsh sounds of a very dramatic conversation. “Does it really matter?” A voice comes shouting through the room, “I’m already sick of doing all of these things. I have no support whatsoever. You tell me week in and week out that I’m doing the right thing, but not once have I seen the fruits of my labor.” The sound pauses for a moment. “What do you mean I’m very much appreciated by the people back home and the Pope?” The voice comes echoing throughout the room once more, “I would up and quit this project right now if it wasn’t for the fact that you made me quit my job for you. I have to support a family and this is all I have to show for it!” Once more an almost eerie silence befalls the room only to be broken by the massive roaring of the mystery man’s voice, “Are you being serious? I can’t believe you would say something like that! God is important to me, but my family is as well and I don’t think God would make me choose this over my family. You are totally wrong on that account. If anything he’d tell me to be with my family and this wouldn’t be my calling, but I was so willing to do anything for the Lord and then this happened.” “I’m not even sure if this really is my calling,” The sound of uncertainty comes rolling off of the tip of his tongue as another silence befalls the room, “You say this is my calling, but the Lord hasn’t spoken to me on whether or not it is.” The sniffling sound of the man is heard, as he seems to have over active allergies. “Yes, Satan is battling me, but that doesn’t mean that this is my calling.” The man says this in a very positive tone; “Satan will battle all man whether they like it or not, so long as they are working anything for the Lord. Satan battles us all, because we are Christians,” The man puts empathies on his comments, “God is happy with me, as long as I continue to do a work for him. I would understand if the Lord wanted me away from my family, but he hasn’t spoken to me, so you can’t go around telling me that this is my calling.” Another silence befalls the room, “Listen, I know you are trying to help matters, but if I continue talking to you then I’m going to get myself in more over my head than I already am. I’m not quitting, but if I get a sign from the Lord that he doesn’t want me here then you can bet I’ll be on the first plane home to New Jersey and there isn’t a thing you can do about it and there is nothing the Vatican can do about it either.” Sniffling a bit more, “No, I’m not going rouge and I’m not turning my back on God. There are just some things in life that I as a walker in this faith, have to figure out on my own and I’m sure God will speak to me as soon as he sees it fit. Good bye.” The sound of a phone clicking is heard and then the sound of footsteps is heard as the man begins to walk from the back room where he was located. Shaking his head, we now see the man revealed as Shane Robertson. He runs his hand across the top of his head once and lets out a tremendous sigh, only to look up and realize he isn’t alone in the room. A shocked look comes across his face and then bewilderment as he reaches down for the nearest weapon and only comes up with a suitcase. Shaking the suitcase at his visitor and shouting, “What are you doing here? Who are you? How did you get in here?” The darkness of the room cloaks the man’s face revealing nothing of him other than the bottom portion of his pants and then he opens his mouth. The sound of his voice like that of demon’s crying, “You need not know my name.” Chuckling a bit, “You listen here buddy,” Shane then begins to yell at the top of his lungs as he shakes the suitcase, “I’ve been through this crap way too much! Show yourself or I will knock you down!” Shane begins to proceed towards the man. “BE STILL!” The man stands and stretches forth a hand in anger, stopping Shane in his place completely, “DARE YOU COME AT ME WITH AN OPEN FIST AND BELIEVE TO LIVE TO SEE ANOTHER DAY AFTER THAT?! YOU MY SON ARE TRULY IN DENIAL IF YOU THINK YOU ARE GOING TO TAKE ME! I’VE GIVEN YOU EVERYTHING AND WHEN ASKED TO BE SILENT YOU COME WITH AN OPEN FIST! WOAH UNTO YOU MY SON!” The man lowers his hand, “I’ve given you everything and yet this is how you repay me. Take a look around you in this place and tell me that you’re doing everything you can for me.” Shane’s expression goes from fright to breaking down on his knees and crying, he looks around the room and the tears begin to stream from him even more, “I’m sorry Father.” “Now the truth be known, you will come forth and you will speak your sorrow unto me at a time such as this, but when you scream out that you do not know if this is your calling or not, then you second guess me.” The man says as he seats himself back down in the chair, “I ask nothing of you, but to serve me and to love me, but you question whether or not to do so because of Satan.” Shane begins to weep even more, “I’m sorry Father, I don’t…” “Be silent my son,” The man continues on, “I created man in my image, but I knew this would happen and it pierces my heart to see the things that happen in this world. I have brought you here for a reason, but not that of the hate mission that you were sent here for.” Shane looks on not knowing what to say, “Father, if this is not my mission then what is?” “The time for you to know will come shortly, but right now, I want you to know that I am here and I hear all. Do not let Satan win a battle over you when he is already a defeated enemy.” The man stands, “Come my son and be delivered from all of that which hurts you, come in to me and all of your worries will be gone. Do my bidding and I will make your life plentiful my son, you are the worker of my word and I will come forth unto you once more, but not now. That day will come soon, I assure you of this.” Shane stands to his feet and nearly kills himself in a dead sprint toward the image with open arms only to come into contact with nothing. Turning and looking to the sky, a single droplet flows from the corner of his eye and not a tear, but a red dot. A trickle of blood, the anointing of Jesus Christ and the blood of the Lamb that flows within in Shane and cleanses him free of his past. ~Series to Continue~
