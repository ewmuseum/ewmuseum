Meeting with the Beast -- Barbaric past

28 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-28-02 The Barbarian History: 'It is said that when the world was very young the tribes of the Northern Steppes were given a sacred charge. Somewhere deep within the great mountain, Arreat, lies a source of great power, crucial to the well being of all humanity. The tribes act as guardians of this artifact, and through this sacred duty, have shaped their way of life in observance of this power. Steeped in mystery and tradition, these people refer to themselves as the 'Children of Bul-Kathos.' the great and ancient king. To better protect their lands from outside forces they adopted a nomadic lifestyle, frequently moving within the confines of the Steppes, and maintaining few permanent settlements. Isolating themselves from the world outside their territories, they eschew the use of magic's and complex machinery, as they believe these things can only weaken the resolve developed over so many years. The Children of Bul-Kathos have developed a kinship with the land and have learned to harness the primal energies within nature to enhance their own substantial physical prowess. It is because of this, together with their freedom from the trappings of the outside world, that the Western Kingdoms have historically refereed to these tribes as 'Barbarians,' and epithet that belies the rich cultural and spiritual history these people truly possess. Although some trade with these curious fold, they do so only along the outermost borders of their lands. All intrusions into the territory surrounding Mount Arreat are forbidden, and warriors from the northern tribes are quick to thwart any incursions. Every attempt at conquest has always been met with fierce and decisive resistance. An account of one skirmish recounts how hordes of Barbarian tribesmen silently appeared where none had been but moments before. Their bodies painted in mysterious designs and their faces howling as they charged like the fierce mountain winds, the tribesmen descended on the invaders. Fully half of the trespassing army immediately dropped their weapons and fled, the remainder were set upon by the Northmen with fervor that none of the battle-hardened invaders had ever witnessed. No quarter was asked and none was given, but in the end, when the outlanders sounded a full retreat, no chase was given either...at least none that could be observed.' A beastly Barbarian? Is that what dwelled inside of one Jay Sylo? Possible...but what was it's true purpose and how did it come to existence? Sylo is determined to find out, he knows there is more to this but the key is finding out what this 'beast' knows. Last time we left Jay Sylo he had finished training in the mystic void of Akiyama. A void in which few traveled and survived. He destroyed three beast which represented each opponent in the Rising Star title match coming up at March Madness. He now sits in a quiet room where he can think and slip into a place where he and the beast will be two different beings. We join him now as he begins to slowly slip into a trance. (His eyes became as heavy as sand bags. His thoughts rushed through his mind at a million miles per hour but suddenly came to a crashing halt. Time seemed to stand still as lights twirled around in a massive and spectacular dance. His eyes slowly opened, not covered by the shield of blue any longer. Before him sat a warrior of sorts. On top of his head he had a long blue pony-tail with blue war paint over his eyes in the shapes of jagged blades. He was very muscular and wore brown leather clothes which clung to his massive physique. In his hand he held a giant axe which he twirled around on it's base, his skin was a dull color and his feet were covered with leather boots which had fur around the top.) 'You are the one?' Sylo asked looking forward at the warrior. 'Aye,' The beastly man replied. 'You and I have come to know each other but who are you really?' Sylo asked curiously. 'It was a long time ago, so very long.....' The beastly man replied as he began to fade. Flash Back: (Mountains surrounded the north as men approached from the east. Each man wore armor which shined in the dull sunlight. The mountains blocked most light with their snow covered tops but you could make out an army of massive beings standing on the side.) Voice Over: 'The alliance of east villages got word that the Barbarians had killed one of theirs but it was all a nasty lie. They still decided to wage war though because in those times you either knew war or death,' (The next thing seen was men dressed just like the beastly warrior from before. They all let out blood curtailing war cries as they charged down the rocky mountains at the knights below.) Voice Over: 'My men were so young and so full of life. They would fight at any time, at any place, no matter the odds. On this day though I noticed a warrior in which I had heard a great deal about. His name was Sir Sylo, a great knight which lead the eastern alliances and had one many battles, he was your great great great great great grandfather. He was a man known for his mercy but on this day I had a feeling no mercy would be shown,' (Back to the plains below the mountains blood began to spill onto the lush, green ground. Axes and swords flew back and forth in a massive melee of metal. Knights fell dead in their path along with many of the Northern Barbarians. Cries of death filled the air along with the sound of flesh being torn apart. The one known as Sir Sylo held a massive claymore in his hand as he tore through the Barbarian's. Each man fighting to stay alive. It was hard for all, they had to step over their fallen brothers and friends to save their own life as each side continued to swing the axes and swords at one another.) Voice Over: 'The battle waged for hours on end. I even saw my own brother become decapitated by one of Sir Sylo's soldiers. I knew I had to put an end to this but the only way to was to take out Sir Sylo,' (The leader of the Barbarians lunged at Sir Sylo. The two met with quick lashes of metal. Their weapons clanged together as they both let out their cries of war. Sir Sylo kicked the Barbarian in the gut sending him sprawling to the ground over a few rocks. He raised his mighty claymore and struck down but the Barbarian rolled out of the way in time. The Barbarian leaped to his feet and snarled swinging his large axe down toward Sir Sylo. Sir Sylo blocked with his claymore as the two pushed back and forth, struggling to prove each mans strength. With a quick flash the Barbarian kicked Sir Sylo in the gut sending him sprawling to the ground.) Voice Over: 'I could smell it in the air, he was close to death but something in his eyes kept me from killing him. One of my warriors came from behind though and shoved a knife into his back. Sir Sylo dropped to his knees, and I knew not what to do,' (The Barbarian leaned down picking up Sir Sylo's body and staring down. He knew it was his foe but for some reason he was drawn to help him. He looked up and let out a yell which could scare the fiercest of animals. All troops stopped fighting as the Barbarian stood up with Sir Sylo, helping him stand.) 'DO YOU NOT SEE?!' The Barbarian warrior yelled. 'ALL THIS FIGHTING IS FOR NOTHING! A GREAT MAN MAY DIE BECAUSE OF OUR WARS! THIS MUST STOP AND IT MUST STOP NOW!' The warriors voice carried. (All of the fighters dropped their weapons and looked to one another as humans instead of enemies. Sir Sylo began to cough up blood as The Barbarian yelled out into the masses.) 'WHERE IS THE HEALER?!' The mighty warrior shouted. (The men pointed to the hills so that's where The Barbarian began to carry Sir Sylo. He trudged up the hill toward the healers home. The healer was an old woman who knew many arts of magic and healing, if anyone could save Sir Sylo it was the healer.) Voice Over: 'I knew he was dying and I felt it was my fault. Just because I am a Barbarian doesn't mean I do not have a heart, I bleed like any other man. So I carried him to the healers home...' (The warrior known as Sylo laid on an old wooden table surrounded by various herbs. A cauldron bubbled near by as a fire lunged up towards the black pot. An old lady who had Cleary seen better days was dressed in a purple dress with flowers dotted in various spots. She hummed out different ancient chants as the Barbarian looked on. Sir Sylo groaned in pain as death slowly clawed at him, wanting in, ready to take his soul to the after life. The old ladies chanting became louder and louder as the rhythm from Sylo's heart grew and grew.) 'He's close to dying,' The old lady said looking to the Barbarian. 'WELL DO SOMETHING WITCH!' The Barbarian yelled. 'The only thing now is for another life force to become one with him,' The old lady said looking straight into the Barbarians eyes. 'Then....Take me!' The Barbarian said looking away a small bit. 'Very well...' The old lady replied. (She placed her hand on the Barbarian's massive chest and began reciting ancient hymns. The life force from the Barbarian was beginning to be sucked away as his eyes rolled to white. A blue beam of light shined up through Sir Sylo's chest as the life force flowed into him. He rose from what would have been his death bed a new man. He looked around and saw the dead body of the Barbarian laying on the ground. He quickly turned his attention to the old woman.) 'What happened here?!' Sir Sylo yelled. 'The Barbarian gave his life for yours,' The old woman replied. 'What?! He died for me?!' 'No not died, but he did give his life. He lives inside of you now and will be handed down to each generation, he saved your life and for that you should be grateful,' The old women replied once more. (Sir Sylo bowed his head and everything seemed to go dark. A few days later a group of both Barbarians and Knights circled a grave, it was the grave of the Barbarian who gave his life force to Sir Sylo.) End Flashback 'So you gave your life to save my family?' Sylo asked looking to the Barbarian sitting before him. 'Aye that is correct,' The Barbarian replied. 'Why did you never show up in the patters of my ancestors?' 'Because Ye held something special lad. I knew you were the one who had the most potential out of all the family members. I travel from life to life like an enigma of sorts but since ye were young I knew ye were bound for great things,' The Barbarian said in his strong voice. 'My family owes you. I owe you,' Sylo looked into the Barbarian's eyes while saying this. 'No I owe ye, Your the one helping me finally get my revenge on the evil souls which brought about war on my land,' The Barbarian replied. (Sylo stared deep into the Barbarian warriors eyes, looking way into the past and seeing the generations flash in his ice blue eyes.) 'So you and I will work together to win the Rising Star title?' Sylo asked. 'AYE! AND WE WILL TEAR THROUGH ANYONE WHO STANDS IN OUR WAY LAD!' The Barbarian warrior shouted waving his Axe in the air. 'Remember were not out to hurt anyone....we work as one, we win as one, but we do not injure unless the person deserves it always remember that,' Sylo said staring back into the Barbarian Warriors eyes. 'Aye, I understand but don't let anyone walk over ye, like that twerp Lance Bell. We used to kick men like him around, they were known as the village idiots!' (Both the Barbarian and Sylo had a hearty laugh over this. But then the atmosphere changed, Sylo became serious once more.) 'What about Ripper and Johnson?' Sylo asked the Barbarian Warrior. 'Ripper is only a danger to himself Lad and Johnson is afraid, I can see it in his eyes. A Barbarian warrior always learns to study the eyes of his opponent and then attack,' (Sylo looked down into the swirling colors of the dream state of mind. Just then he felt the powerful hand of the Barbarian on his shoulder looking down.) 'It will be ok Lad, don't worry. Yer Family had a curse set upon them a long time ago but it passed you....if it hadn't ye would have been dead by now. Ye got me on yer side and you and I can destroy anyone who stands in our way....trust me,' The Barbarian said with a smile. 'What's your name?' Sylo asked. (The Barbarian removed his hand and smiled once more. He hoisted the giant axe with the razor sharp blade on his shoulder and looked down at Sylo.) 'Fer Now Lad, just refer to me as The Beast,' (Sylo nodded his head up and down. Soon the image vanished and everything went black. Sylo opened his eyes to reveal he was back in his bed room. He scanned the room making sure it was real and it was. A smile moved across his face as he laid back once more.) 'You were right, We will win and over come our opponents together....March Madness is only days away and once the time comes the world will see we do mean business....may god have mercy on their souls,' (Sylo smiled and closed his eyes once more. Slowly he slipped into a coma-type sleep. He needed his rest and that's what he got. More of the 'beast' would be revealed in time but for now....time was up.)
