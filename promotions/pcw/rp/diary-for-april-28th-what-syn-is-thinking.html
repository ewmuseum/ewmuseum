Diary for April 28th-- What SyN is Thinking

undated

Date: Today Diary for April 28th Dear Diary: It’s nearly been about three weeks since I started out in PCW. I’m glad the way things are moving along and I think I am impressing quite a few people backstage. My debut I think went off well and I’m getting used to everybody booing me and saying they hate me. I thought it would bug me but the Hare Krishna has taught me to not listen to the crowd. Overweighter is a ticking time bomb, gonna explode when I set it off. At first, I didn’t know how I would attack Overweighter but I realized, just do it. The people at the Hare Krishna, and more importantly, the missionary in booth number 17 is really nice. I never thought the Hare could be so relaxing. I have way more confidence for myself than I thought I would ever be. The Krishna is my life and a while ago, I thought about become a missionary. Instead of that, I took a good alternative. I could missionary outside of the community centre, in the PCW ring. My first job was to pick a person, any person at all. Teach them confidence like I was taught and ultimately, I can make them do whatever I want. It sounds crazy, but I believe in it and am going to try that. My first object is The Overweighter. That fat piece of mess is just a road block in my way and once I get a hold of him, good things will happen, I can feel it. I will keep attacking The Overweighter until it goes through his thick, fat skull that I mean business. He may feel sorrow for himself but forgive me, I AM SYN! On Fusion, my two opponents are Mark Makonie and Shane Robertson. I beat Makonie last week and I’ll pin him again. He is a fairly easy opponent and is a very confused mind, I can tell. I thought a bit of going after Makonie, teaching him a lesson but in the end, Overweighter was the better choice. If I get him to do what I want, it will truly prove that I am a focussed mind. Shane Robertson is a new face in my eyes. In the beginning of the week, I was actually scared of this new opponent, memorizing his move list and style of wrestling. I was putting to much stress on myself until I realized, Shane Robertson is like Mark Makonie, a confused soul is doesn’t know anything. I learned all this from the Krishna. Matt Jackson, my tag team partner. I figure that Matt is a good partner for me to work with. He’s ruthless, mean, and knows how to get the job done. Matt was an associate of Xavier Kannon, former PCW champion and legend of this world. If I can tag with someone who was trained by a master, I know that I am as good as Jackson, meaning I am just a step away from reaching the level of Xavier Kannon. As for Matt Jackson’s manager and protege, Haley Hilton will make sure that I win my second match in PCW. Haley is a real bitch and knows what it takes to succeed in this business. Her cocky attitude matches mine except for one difference. She is cocky, I am confident. It’s the same thing, only my mind is more set on what I do and what I am doing. Haley will keep interference down. This new Rising Star title division idea is something that is more of a thorn in the side of my shoulder. Doesn’t PCW see that I am too good for the division and that I can move up in no time if need be? On the other hand, the division is something good for me. It gives me a chance to prove that I mean business. Ten wins should be nothing for me, because I have confidence. I already earned an easy win over Makonie, I can defeat Shane Robertson and get a second win. Then a third, a fourth, and so on until the Rising Star title is within my reach. More importantly, the Extreme title, I can smell it. I can feel the gold around my waist, standing over The Overweighter. Whatever happens in PCW, I gotta remember to take it one step at a time and not get too cocky. The crowd can boo me all they want, it just motivates me more to win the match and hear their boos in my ear. When Fusion comes, I will prove that I am the better man. I WILL win that match and move on, one step closer. Forgive me, BUT I AM SYN!
