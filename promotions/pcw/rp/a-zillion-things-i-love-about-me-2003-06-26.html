A Zillion Things I Love About... Me!

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th June 2003 Cloud Nine and the probable bout featuring Dante Jackson against Gaz Green for the prestigious Continental Championship never once entered into the mind of the Jester for it will be a situation he has been in before. The egomaniacal Jester will highly likely cock his nose up into the air and proclaim that this bout is another opportunity to prove to the masses that he is indeed the greatest Champion the division has bare witness to, a mammoth task knowing the long list of accomplished predecessors but with a smug grin etched along his angelic face you can almost predict his retort to the critics, “I was the King Midas of Tag, fact, whatever division I touch I wear gold!” In terms of tag, who can argue but with Dante Jackson far from his mind what or who are in his thoughts? Well it’s been a few weeks into this airship tour and the one person who has came under emotional warfare from the Emerald Menace is his wife of all people, Tessa Windsor-Green. Like Dante, this ongoing feud has escaladed from the tag division and into their respective singles tenures but courtesy of the Higher Powers of Primetime Championship Wrestling then this matter has been forcefully moved from business to personal. With malice both husband and wife have been booked into the same room, the honeymoon suite as it’s been referred to and it is this backdrop where the scene opens, A large spacious room is laden before the camera, almost separated as two polar-opposites for the nearest side is the epitome of chaos with bed covers hanging loosely from where they should be and draping across the carpeted floor. Magazines litter the bedside cabinet and a Walkman sits nestled atop of the pile humming to itself whilst the batteries run down. The furthest side comes across picturesque with an undisturbed bed, the covers free from crease and cuddly toys sitting erect against the backboard cushions, sitting to the side in thought is the proud owner of this personification of perfection, Tessa Windsor-Green. The Television Champion’s trim figure sits on a wooden chair and brushing a hand through her brunette hair she returns to scribbling another entry into her diary, “How we looking sweet cheeks?” comes a distinctive voice and standing in the midst of the doorway stands a smug Jester with his trademark smile emitting towards the recipient, in this case his darling wife. Tessa tilts her head with a sarcastic smile of her own and with a clap effect the diary shuts, some frantic hand movements later and the leather embossed book vanishes within a draw and the diva puts in place her security measures of a brass lock and key. Raising her eyebrows and swinging her pupils full circle she stands to attention and idly strolls by her husband with tunnel vision and disappears out onto the metallic corridor. The tip tap sound of her feet against the glistening surface finally subsides and checking whether the coast is clear the Jester sneakily ventures inward, hell bent on unearthing the content of that diary. “And you think a simple drawer is going to stop me huh?” mutters the Emerald Menace flinching his lips and trying not to laugh, the whole assumption making his cheeks blossom in cherry red. The Lord of Laughter traipsed over to the bedside cabinet and puzzlingly inspected the depositary box with a look of intrigue, firstly toying with the brass handle before tapping at the wooden structure itself. Rummaging his head into the breast pocket of his green tailored business jacket he removes a thin piece of copper wiring and with a hand movement right, then left the murmur of the latch indicates entry. A smile generates across his rosy red face and he pulls the draw towards himself before taking the book firmly in his grasp, “What to do with this diary? Maybe I’ll tear a few pages and auction them off over the net, come on some adolescent teen would give his right arm to sniff pages that contain the scent of Tessa’s hand moisturizer or maybe I’ll just be me and burn the bloody thing!” grunts the Jester curling his face into a snarl. The Continental Champion opens the diary and flicks randomly through the pages, spending very little time on the content. Suddenly he stops, his mouth lowers ajar and his eyes erect themselves like protruding daggers and you can almost sense the rage building as his teeth grind down to the gum line, “1.) His pranks never end what ever the circumstance!” The Jester merely sighs towards the hearted comments of his wife. “2.) That damn hand buzzer of his and the fact that I’ve seemingly been shocked more than anyone by it!” A slight giggle materializes and our host retorts, “My heart bleeds for you!” “3.) He seems to enjoy this marriage for the simple fact that he can torment me with just reminding me that I am a Green now!” and, “4.) He ruined what was suppose to be the happiest day of my life, not once but twice!” The Jester waved his hand through his spiked mop of green head hair and as though a bulb had lit up an idea strikes. A sadistic grin later and the Jester sits at the cabinet with a biro at hand and his hands soon wave from left to right in a pendulum motion and the calligraphy of print emerged. Lines and lines of handwriting flowed in list form down the page overleaf. Standing in triumph the Prince of Pranks cleared his throat with a subtle cough and recited his own entry into a neighbouring mirror that hangs on the white-washed wall opposite, “I entitle this, A Zillion Things I Love About… Me!” Allowing for a slight pause the prankster begins again, “1.) Every man wants to be in my boots and every women wants me to be in, well them!” “2.) I’m a happily married man. I can’t vouch for the wife though…” “3.) I’m the GREATEST Continental Champion ever to wear the strap!” “4.) I’m a former FIVE time Tag Team Champion – That reminds me, note to self, must win the titles again to get in the record books!” “5.) I’m the only surviving member of the Front Office!” “6.) I hold the unofficial record for the fastest victory against The Mandrake, I mean Edwin Mandrake or is it Eddie, anyway point being I won…” “7.) I’ve beaten two people who’ve gone on to be World Champ…” continues the Jester but his self-promotion comes to an abrupt conclusion for his better half of Tessa Windsor-Green has entered. The PCW diva stands hands on hips and biting her tongue, she lunges forward like a lion striking but the evasive Jester sidesteps to safety but in the commotion Tessa manages to snatch back her property. A false look of solemn filters upon the face of the Continental Champion but Tessa pays it no mind and flicks a finger through the pages of her diary, finally coming to rest on her husbands entry and in a u-turn of roles, she recites, “2.) I’m a happily married man. I can’t vouch for the wife though…” says Tessa Windsor-Green and as the words materialize from her mouth the Jester is midway through the exit, “YOU BASTARD!!!!” bellows the Television Champion throwing the article at her husband but the Conniving Comic pulls the door shut and the book crashes against the wood with a thunderous thud. Out in the midst of the passageway/ corridor and standing on its metallic surface the Jester looks dead ahead quite shaken from his ordeal and having to have the last say he mutters to himself, “Did she just say BASTARD?”
