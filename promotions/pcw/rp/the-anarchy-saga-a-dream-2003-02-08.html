The Anarchy Saga: A Dream?

8 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-8-03 The heart beat... Once... Twice... Eyes were closed, dream was beginning. Three... Four... Something in the distance, near the trees. Its almost ephermereal, translucent. A blue haze, yet closer.. closer to it. Five... Six... There, there it is again. Running now, after it, going. Faster and faster, trying to catch another glympse. Whatever it is it must be important. Eyes closed. Seven... Eight... A graveyard? Why? Hurdling the gravestones. It's amazing, whatever it is. It is worth everything in the world... or is it? It seems different, the translusence, is it a spectar? Why here? Why now? The heart still beats. Nine... Ten... Tripping, falling, down the grass hill, stopping. Where is it. Where did it go? Damn it. Lost it. May never know what it is. Wait... A flash. Startled. Eyes open. Eleven... Awake. It was only a dream.
