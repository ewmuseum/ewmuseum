And So The Rage Is Directed At The Hero

8 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: And So The Rage Is Directed At The Hero Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 08/10/02 “So... You’re kinda, like, taking this losing thing badly, huh?” *SMASH* We see the face of PCW’s Commissioner, Eleanor Hall standing just inside a doorway as a vase flies across her, dropping it’s flowers across the floor and leaving a trail of it’s water before crashing into the wall... The prep just tilting her head to the side. “THAT.... THAT....“ The exclaiming voice of CEO Xavier Kannon is then heard, taking his loss hard as Ellie~! just looks upon him, wondering how long the tantrum will go on for. Eleanor : “.... Champion?” She shrugs at her unsympathetic words.. Because they are true. “DO NOT CALL HIM THE CHAMPION!!!“ The Scientologist’s voice booming out is heard followed by more smashing. Ellie~! just looks back at him, her vacuous eyes confused. Eleanor : “Oh, are we, like, playing a game where we lie?” She looks inquisitively off-camera to where her husband is apparently still on a rampage as the sound of plaster being kicked through is heard. Eleanor : “In that case, you’re hair, like, SO doesn’t look like it has a big hole burned in it!” She grins broadly, her words being ignored by her husband. Eleanor : “Oh, and tying it back like that TOTALLY helps!” The shot pulls out to show the furious Scientologist kick a chair across their locker room and into the far wall. His mane of singed Strawberry Blonde hair is tied back in a ponytail, charred and a lot thinner on one side. Eleanor : “Lying is, like, SO much more fun that the truth... YAY LYING!“ Her giggly words still go unheard as Xavier kicks the chair back against the wall it just rebounds off, a Black silk shirt covering some of the burns and cuts to his body. Ellie~! starts to get a little annoyed that he isn’t responding to her words and starts to whine. Eleanor : “Hey, I, like got hit with a Chair and stuff too... But you don’t see an Ellie~! PMS’ing all over the place!” Still, the Scientologist remains encased in his bubble of rage, not coping at all well with the loss of his beloved PCW World Title, taking another kick at the unfortunate chair. Xavier “GHAR!!!“ As his foot smashes into the metallic leg underside he lets out a roar of pain, not stopping his smashing spree as with his right arm he whips out at the items on the dresser, sending them either to their sides or onto the floor. Eleanor : “Did you see me... Like, SO get the belts of those funny two? Huh? Did ya? Xavi?” Her voice becomes a step more whiny with each word as Kannon takes a Bottle of Champagne he had on ice to celebrate a successful Title defence and pelts it against the far wall, sending it smashing in an explosion of bubbles and glass shards. Eleanor : “Was that, like, a yes? Or a no?” As the last shatterings of glass drop to the floor, the furious Scientologist dropkicks the ice bucket against the wall, making a point of picking it back up and throwing it with all his might down onto the floor where he takes a stomp at it. Eleanor : “You know.. You should TOTALLY book yourself on one of those Sciencey things you sell to people.. You seem, like, kinda stressed...” Her eyes are almost showing concern at the actions of her husband... Probably more concerned because he’s ignoring her. Eleanor : “Or giving me some more Credit Cards to go shopping with might relax you a bit, huh?” For a brief second, the Gold Patron Meritorious snaps out of his rage induced trance and turns with a softer look to Ellie~!, not having heard anything she said amidst his tunnel vision and smashings. Xavier : “What, my little Poon Puppy?” She looks back at him blankly, wondering what he heard before he just runs his hand through his hair and takes a deep breath. Xavier : “Just do what you want...
