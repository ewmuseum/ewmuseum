Eau De Toilette Revisited Part #2

Date: 13/14/02 The camera fades in… A still scene is shown. Both the Bertha Clone and the Chef are rushing across a bustling street avoiding oncoming traffic with no regard to the city’s policy on jaywalking. The Chef promo Narrator’s voice is heard. Narrator: On the last Chef promo, we left the Culinary Conquistador and his clone just as they were beginning to launch an offensive assault on Denny’s. Let us return to our story… The still scene quickly becomes a moving scene. The Chef and Bertha rush to the other side of the street. A car barely grazes the Chef’s ankle as he hops to the other side. The Chef: In the name of Allah, I declare a Jihad on Denny’s!!! Bertha: F*cking WAR! HUH! Good f*cking God, what’s it f*cking good for b*tch @$$? The Chef: It’s good for toppling poor quality, over priced sit-down restaurants that discriminate against minorities! Both the Chef and Bertha charge towards the eatery, both screaming at the top of their lungs as if they were alongside William Wallace. Bertha steps in front of the Chef and kicks the door open. The duo quickly enters and lets the patrons know their purpose. Bertha: This is f*cking hold up! Put your mother f*cking c*ck sucking hands in the air you bunch of rotten @$$ queer as f*ck skank @$$ sluts! Everyone in the restaurant including the Chef gasps. Bertha: F*ck yeah! The Chef promptly interrupts. The Chef: No! No! No! We just want to talk to the owner of the restaurant, and bash his head in with the rolling pin. The shock of the restaurant patrons quickly turns to a calm uneasiness. An old man sitting in a booth stands up and faces the Culinary Couple. Old Man: I’ve been sitting in this chair for pert near fifteen years, and I ain’t about to leave! The Chef: Our qualms are not with you infidels, and…how can you have been sitting in the chair for fifteen years? My restaurant closed down less than a year ago… Old Man: Um…Uh…El Nino? CRACK!!! Bertha cold-cocks the poor old feller in the back of the head with the rolling pin. The old man falls to the floor with a thud. His motionless body becomes a constant reminder not piss off clones or to contradict Chef promo storylines. Bertha: Now that’s what I call a f*cking Grand Slam!!! HeHeHeF*ckHeHeHe! Just then the kitchen doors swing open. An even older man dressed in a purple business suit and wearing an astonishingly expensive fur coat enters the dining room. He takes his monocle of out his pocket and holds it up to his left eye. The Chef: What’s with old people and Denny’s? Bertha: …(Continuing) And f*cking Big Boy, and that mother f*cking Cracker Barrel, and the f*cking IHOP, and the mother f*cking WaffleHouse!!! Older Man: Hush you fat piece of trash! I scoff at your attempts to take over my restaurant! The Chef, angered by the man’s introduction, replies. The Chef: Oh yeah, and who on Allah’s green Earth are you pops? Older Man: I…am…DENNY!!! The entire crowd in the restaurant gasps. The Chef: The…Denny? Denny: Well actually I’ve changed my name to “The Entrepreneur Formerly Known as Denny”. The Chef: Well…Denny! This is my restaurant that you’ve illegally occupied… Denny: No I haven’t, I got a court order for permission to build on this lot after the Eau De Toilette went bankrupt. The Chef pauses for a moment. The Chef: Well, he’s got us there, let’s go Bertha…As the Chef walks out Bertha steps forward, bravely confronting Denny. Bertha: So you can f*cking talk the f*cking talk, eh? Well f*cking walk now f*cko! Denny though has a trick up his sleeve. Denny: Oh cooks, prepare me a…Free Grand Slam combo for this fine woman here… Bertha stops dead in her tracks. Bertha: Grand…Slam? The Chef: No angel pie! It’s a trick! Bertha: Can I get a burger with bacon, and cheese, and barbecue sauce? Denny: Yes, it can all be yours… The Chef: Vile tempter! I shill show you the wrath of Allah! SHALALALALALALALA!!! The Chef charges at Denny, but slips on the freshly mopped floor and falls on his back. The patrons in the restaurant all laugh at the Culinary Conquistadors humorous antics. The Chef: Must…save…Bertha clone…burger…too…powerful… Just then a cook comes forth from the kitchen with the Grand Slam platter ready for it’s journey into Bertha’s stomach. Bertha receives the platter, tosses the garnish aside, picks up the burger and moves it closer and closer to her mouth. The Chef: Allah! I’ve fallen and I can’t get up! In a surprise to us all, Bertha rejects the burger and slams it in the face of Denny! Bertha then scoops up the store chain’s owner and spins him around in the air like he was weightless. The Chef finally stands back up after the nasty fall. The Chef: Praise Allah for Icy Hot! The Chef pulls some of the lotion endorsed by various football stars out of his pocket and applies it to his back.. The Chef: AHHHHH!!! COLD COLD COLD!!! Ahhhhh…hot as Mecca in the midsummer! Bertha finally sets the dizzy Denny on a stool and threatens him again. Bertha: Now f*cking give us back our restaurant or it’s f*cking @$$ hopping curtains for ya! Denny: Never… The Chef: How about if I give up my slave holdings in Haiti? Denny: DEAL! Bertha: F*cking Wha? The Chef: Well I know that the restaurant chain is one of the most discriminating places on Earth, so it’s owner must be the most narrow minded individual in the world. Bertha: I thought Haiti had a f*cking slave revolt! Toussaint L’Ouverture!?!? Did that f*cking happen!?!? Denny: Sure did… The Chef: I believe so… Bertha seems dumbfounded. Bertha: Then why are there f*cking slaves still in Haiti? The Chef: I dunno, El Nino? The Chef hands a few documents out of his pocket to Denny. The Chef: There you go, now you’ll never have to worry about harvesting sugar again! Denny hugs the Chef. Denny: Thank you military clad cooking person, you’ve made me the happiest racist in the world! Soon the Negro race will fall to the awesome power of DENNY!!! Bertha: That’s not very f*ckingly appropriate language, skank @$$! The Chef: Hey, this ain’t the Ritz…it’s Denny’s! As Denny leaves, the Chef walks from table to table rounding up old people and helping them find their way to the exit. The Chef pulls out his cell phone and presses a few buttons. The Chef: Hello? Ricardo? Yeah, I’ll need some new wallpaper; a nice 18th century French style fireplace, Le Cordon Bleu trained Chefs, fifty pounds of snails and caviar, and put the tab on Bin Laden, Osama Bin Laden…Ha Ha Ha! I’m back in business! The camera fades out as the Chef celebrates to Bertha’s dismay.
