Through The Eyes [5] Thanksgiving

22 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22.12.02 Date: November 22nd, 2002Place: New York, New York (Jackson Estate)Time: 8:00 PM (EST) Relaxing back in an easy chair, the elder Jackson would smile upon his family, with more of that same desire, or understanding back those years ago when he happened to be contracted to Northeast Entertainment Wrestling. From one year to another, he can briefly think back to where he was the champion, and that everybody from Paul Hanson to Clive Owens, to Ron Chambers loved him until he blew out his knee in the ring. That stopped him from wrestling, that forced him away from the sport. That made him change the outcome for his own future and family. Looking up, he can see those pictures, framed up for him and everyone else to see whenever they step into the mansion that he helped build, the empire he wished his son, Dante would carry over into. A smile crossed his face as his eyes met along side the Northeast Enetertainment Wrestling World Heavyweight title, the gleaming gold face plate with his name carved in the lower portion where whom ever happened to be champion had their named branded there as well. He shared the distinction with countless others, and they all happened to have the same feeling when they saw that title in their homes. The last he heard of Paul Hanson, he was a tag on of the fWo, a booking talent/scout for new talent. Hanson was probably sixty something at the moment, well into his dignified late twenties and early thirties when Jackson first became champion. 'Oh my god!' he could hear the screams of Mike Jones into his headset as he saw what had occurred in the ring. Ox, better known to Paul Hanson as Keith Swanson happened to have Eric stuck in a figure four leg lock. Only problem is, a 6'9', 315 pound man isn't supposed to apply such a move on a much smaller body. 'He just twisted the leg of Eric Jackson, its not supposed to even bend that way!' Jones' colleague Dennis Branch waved around his hands for a medical staff to come and look at Eric. He didn't even want to look at it, the ankle was twisted to the left while his entire body and the upper portion of his leg was shifted to the right. Paul Hanson was backstage, watching the action from a monitor not too far away from the entrance ramp. Horrified, he screamed. 'Dammit! DAMN YOU OX!!!' The Ox got up, looked at the wound on Eric's leg and didn't even flinch towards it. After driving Clive Owens out of the federation, he was the juggernaut that Hanson had created, the monster that the fans loathed until he became sick of them. Branch got up from his seat and climbed into the ring, sliding under the bottom rope. Ox leaned up against the ropes and snarled, 'Is he gonna finish this or what?' 'You broke his damn ankle, how the fuck is he supposed to finish?!' Ox smirked as he pushed Branch out of the way, Eric looked up and in a possum situation rolled up the big man. 1...2...3! 'Amazing! Even with a broken leg, Eric Jackson has done it!' Jackson leaned back against the ropes, sitting, and starring at his leg, one that would probably cause him to not even take the two year reign of World Heavyweight champ into the eighties. Tears began to form on his face as he sat there, looking at his leg until medical personnel arrived at the scene. 'Eric Jackson, the people's champion.' Mike Jones relayed that over his headseat, as Jackson was gurneyed out of the ring and towards the back. Parting the curtians, he could hear the fans chanting his name, and for five minutes they wouldn't let go of it. 'Somehow, I wish I could stay champion.' he told himself, as the memory died down. He could hear footsteps from the kitchen, as his wife Pamela entered the room. She set up everything nicely, giving everyone a chance to take a peek at barbeque, fried chicken, salad with french dressing, turkey, dirty rice, mustard greens, and drinks for everyone. She sighed and slumped down into a chair. 'God, Thanksgiving cooking is just hell isn't it?' 'Yeah, but still babe its good enough for the family.' 'Seriously, you're not even going to allow April's mother to come?' 'She's dead Pam, gone back in 92'.' 'I can't believe you did that though...' 'It was before I met you, it was a different thing actually. I couldn't seriously turn it down, when you're on the road, you're lonely as hell and when you're lonely - everybody wants to make sure that they get a piece of you.' 'So this woman, she got a piece of you didn't she?' He sighed, 'Yes, she did.' Pamela arched her eyebrows, but at forty nine she still looked the part, as if she were the Pam Grier of the family, old, but doesn't look it so. Wearing something hear mother wouldn't even allow, Pamela sighed, before returning to the kitchen. 'She's still here though,' Eric said to nobody in particular. Soon the doorbell rang, causing his attention to chance towards the door. 'Who in the hell?' Pulling the door open, he saw the face that almost looked just like his. 'Hi pop.' His son had just arrived, along with the entire partying guest.
