As The Snow Falls...

20 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/20/02 A lonely snowflake falls in Detroit Michigan, and Timo Bolamba curses. The air has grown wet with the little bastards, and he was none too pleased. Soon it would be time to put all his cars away in storage and break out the winter vehicles, but that wasn’t what bothered him tonight. No, this was an anger, an old heated fire, not unlike that which burned under his mantel tonight. He looked to that mantel and saw his replicas of the PCW Rising Star Championship Belt, and the PCW Television Title, then he reached out and grabbed the FSW World Championship. “Been a long time hasn’t it?” Timo turned and he saw Miyagawa standing inside his living room area, and he squinted. “Holy cripe, Miya, is that really you?” There in all his glory stood Miyagawa. He wore a long leather coat and a pair of glasses, but it was him. Certainly a surprise, Timo knew not what to make of it. Last time they were in the same ring, there was a bit of heated rivalry between themselves. “Yeah, I was in the neighborhood and, what the hell. How long can you stay mad at a guy?” “Oh, that.” “Yeah.” Timo turned to his belt and placed it on a chair. Miyagawa walked up and looked it over, grabbing it a little and holding it into the light, then he chuckled. Timo looked at him in an odd fashion and shook his head. “What’s so funny?” “You Timo. World Champion, damn it took a year dint it? Hell, you were so bound and determined to get it right there that you turned on us and left for those punks. What ever happened to them anyhow?” Timo sighed. “I was young and stupid Miya. This game has changed, and I’m not the same man I once was. You know, I heard about Ed.” “Yeah. Shame isn’t it?” Timo nodded. Ed was the only one who had trusted him after their falling out and that made a load of difference in the world. There was always this giant hole, and the hero of FSW was nothing more than a petty thug at one time in PCW. It was truly a shame. “Yeah. You heard from Jeremy?” “Not recently. I don’t suppose you have?” Timo laughed a bit. “I sent him a letter. Never got a reply.” “That’s him alright.” The air had grown thick, and Timo turned around, facing Miyagawa. He was wearing an Alliances 2001 Champions shirt, and for the first time, Miya could see the fire reflecting in his eyes, it was almost eerie. The man who stood before him was not the same greenhorn he had worked with no less than a year ago, and now, things were indeed different. Timo couldn’t really figure out why Miya had traveled to his domicile, but it was welcome. Honestly, things had slowed down a lit in the recent days. Jake had went home to celebrate Thanksgiving and take a break from his training. The boy was probably going to break his diet too. “Timo.” Afwa had went out with some of Timo’s buddies to a bar, and no doubt he had a few drinks by now. Mike was off playing miniature golf, and Azala was so busy shooting commercials for Asparagus Spears of Zameer~! That he hardly had time to train anymore. “Timo...” Gwen on the other hand, well she had taken the kids to the relatives, and you all know how relatives can get. Pinching cheeks and making food, they always want you to… “TIMO!” “Huh?” “I said your name three times man, what’s wrong with you?” “Uh, sorry Miya, inner monologue problems, you know how it is.” Miyagawa looks at him funny and shakes his head. “No, actually I don’t know how that is, I’m not insane.” Timo chuckles a little and takes a seat, offering one to Miya as well, and the good man takes it. He looks across to the Samoan and gauges several scars that he had never seen before. Timo also looked older, physically, maybe mentally, but he looked older. It was the same for Miyagawa though, they were both becoming old, slowly but surely. “Miya, you know I’ve often wanted to go back to that day and take back everything I said to you guys. I would take Snow out and show him a few lessons, once and for all.” Miya gauges a response. “Timo, you can’t take it back. Ed isn’t coming back, and Jeremy is nowhere to be found. Personally, I have no problem talking to you, but I’m not turning my back on you.” “I see.” The tide of the conversation had indeed became more serious, and Timo hung his head a little. Miya was right of course, what business did he have being trusted? None, and that’s the bone truth. “However…you can show Snow a few lessons for us. You know he is going to be there when you and Team FSW go to Alliances.” “I know. I’ve been thinking about it. I think about what I would do every god damned day Miya. Trust me, if I have the chance, I’m going to make sure he pays for what he did, and not just to me. This is going to be a great night Miya, all I need is to get my hands on that bastard one time.” Miya smiles a little, actually for the first time that night. Something had indeed changed in Timo, and he liked it. No more indecisiveness, pure unadulterated fire to take down the man who had once wronged him and his friends. This was the right way to live, not cowering like Snow had done. “Timo, you changed. I still don’t trust you, but I think I have to respect you a bit. I think that’s what I came to find out. I heard you have been doing good things in FSW, and I had to hear it myself.” “Well…some things time can’t heal. I’m glad you came out here. Maybe someday Jeremy will find his way to my house, but I’m not holding my breath.” Miya nods and stands up, he reaches for Timo’s hand, and for just a moment, they shake. Timo turns to the kitchen for a second. “Hey Miya, you want a dri…” He turns around and Miyagawa is gone. Odd, but not unlike him. Timo still smiled a bit though as he looked down and there was a picture of the Alliances version of Asylum laying on the chair. Fittingly, he reached above the mantle and placed it nestled in with his TV Title. “Good times my friend…good times.”
