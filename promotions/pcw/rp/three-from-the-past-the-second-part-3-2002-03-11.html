Three From the Past....The Second....PART 3

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-11-2002 The scene fades in to show Venneman Pendragon still standing amongst his people. He has a look that combines anger, shock and sadness writ upon his face. He raises his blade and pulls a cloth from a pocket in the back of his pants and proceeds to wipe the blood from Excalibur. He shakes his head as the shot becomes wavy and pulls back to show the blood red pool, with the Ancient Druid standing before it with rage in his eyes. Ancient: YEARRRGHHH!!!! What in the hell kind of showing was that? The Count cried!! He CRIED! Had I known he was such a pansy I would have called forth someone who could deal with the task I designed…Oh well. His soul is destined for eternal torment now…You two though. You will not fail me… The shot pulls back and we see that Richard and Arthur still stand side by side, eyes searching for a way out of the mess they have been resurrected into. The Ancient merely smiles at them and begins to speak, his voice raspy and low, hard to hear… Ancient: Richard. You will go next. Your prowess as a warrior king is unparalleled in English history. You will seek out the hero, before he has a chance to make it to Stonehenge. You shall ride to meet him before he has a chance to meld himself into one being, with the help of the ancient order of the Nobles. Bastards. Trying to ruin the coming darkness by tainting it with light. Richard: My soul is pure…Why would I taint it? Ancient: Because if you fail me, I shall destroy the last remnants of your lineage that still reside upon this hellish rock called earth. I will make them pay for your transgressions against me. Can you live with that because you sought to spare the life of one man? Richard: …. Ancient: Good. The Lionhearted knows when he is caught. Now…Beat him to Stonehenge, or watch thy progeny whither…. Richard sighs and exit’s the shot, heading for his horse. The ancient one turns to face Arthur, who stares at him coldly… Ancient: Worry not, Pendragon. Thou shalt have thy chance. You will be able to face your progeny for what is rightfully yours. Richard will fail. I have underestimated this Venneman Pendragon. Perhaps the old blood is still strong in at least one mortal on this rock… Arthur: I must reclaim that which I have lost. And with Excalibur, I shall reclaim mine place at the head of this world… Ancient: Aye…We will rule a thousand years, you and I… Pendragon grins as the Ancient moves close and pats him on the shoulder… Ancient: I have known thee. I have been around far longer than any mortal should be allowed to persevere, mainly because of the dark arts that I have employed for many years of mine existence. I was there to see your rise to power because you were the rightful holder of Excalibur, I was there when the Lady of the Lake returned the heaven sent blade to ye. I always thought of you as a son, and I did hate to see you perish. That is why I brought thee back. I missed you, Arthur…Stuff of legends, we made. And legends we have been for many a year… Arthur: You do seem vaguely familiar…Good CHRIST!! The Ancient smiles and cocks an eyebrow, feigning innocence… Ancient: That is right. You knew me as Merlin, but through the ages I have gone by many names. Arthur: You used evil to aid me in mine endeavors? Ancient: Aye, I did. But it was for good purposes, and I needed a person who wielded absolute power. I needed someone to guide… Arthur: To manipulate… Ancient: You went your own way. I only pushed and guided when thee needed it. I raised you, boy…Of all mine endeavors, you were my most precious…Many names, in many places…Most recently I tried to help a Russian as I went by the name Rasputin. That was a bad time, and only now have I ventured from mine hiding to once more lead my dark forces against the light. Only now, I have my most superior creation by my side…. Arthur: Aye….Merlin…. ********FLASH********* The scene cuts back to the village, as Pendragon offers a hand here and there to the gathered people as he strides towards his horse. He reaches Xerxes and hoists himself onto the beasts back before riding slowly away from the scene. He shakes his head in thought…. “How could this be? An ancient warrior brought from the past to face me in mortal combat? Who would wield such power to be able to pull of such a feat? And how is it I knew nothing………” BUT I DID KNOW. ON THE FLYING CONTRAPTION. I FELT SOMETHING WAS WRONG. KNEW IT TO BE SO, YET I FAILED TO PUT MINE FINGER UPON THE PROBLEM. IF ONLY I KNEW MORE OF WHAT I WAS TO DO…. “To be able to become whole once more, perhaps for the first time. That is key, and that must happen. It would…..” APPEAR THAT I COMBINE THE THOUGHTS OF TWO WITHIN ONE MIND. WE FINISH ONE ANOTHER’S THOUGTS NOW….. “And that has me a tad worried. I need full control in the battles to come…” As he rides from the village, hoof beats are heard fast approaching and in the distance a cloaked rider comes into view. Pendragon stops and looks at the slight built man who rides up and reigns in his horse next to the immense warhorse Xerxes. He pushes back his hood to reveal a delicate face, topped with a nose meant for the aristocracy. Pendragon’s hand falls to rest on the hilt of his sword as the man measures him… Man: Lord King Pendragon? Pendragon: Aye, ye’ve found me. Now…Speak of who thou art and what thy wants of me are? Man: Simple. I am Joseph, and I am a messenger of an ancient sect….The Nobles. Pendragon: Nobles, eh? Keep talking… Joseph: I have come to help thee on thy quest…I know all about you. My order has waited your coming for generations. And now, the time of light is at hand, though others would try to darken the day… Pendragon: Can thee tell me why I was forced into battle with a swordsman from another century? Can thee explain to me what I need to do to rid myself of mine second personality…. RID? COME NOW!!! Pendragon: Not rid….Complete, then… Joseph: It has already begun. Times will begin to move quickly now, my liege. Pendragon: What do you mean? Joseph: We must hasten to Stonehenge. There, my order will help thee with all you need to know… Pendragon: Why dost though speak half modern, half correctly? Art thou insane? Joseph: Habit I guess…Come now, quickly… Joseph pulls his hood up and takes off across the plain, with Pendragon riding close behind… Pendragon: Good Christ… AYE ******FLASH***** Richard waits in a small valley before Stonehenge that happens to lie directly in the path that the two horsemen will take. He has dismounted and now sits on a large stone, idly playing with his sword….(NOT A SEXUAL REFERENCE)…He looks bored, but his eyes are alert as ever, and he seems a tad disturbed. He sighs as he begins to speak…. Richard: Aye, born of the high line is he whom I must vanquish to protect mine progeny. The Ancient knew I would need more motivation; I would not risk damnation for less, it would appear. If only I knew more. This Venneman Pendragon sounds like a mighty foe, cut in the mold that I myself came from. ‘Tis a sad fact that the two of us will not know one another, other than for the death blow that is sure to come from the encounter that looms heavy upon the horizon. If only things were different. If only I knew the plan of the Ancient, what he sought to accomplish with the death of this so called hero. Things might go a little easier. I would be able to make a better informed decision. I guess it is too late for that. The die is cast, and mine own course is set. All I can do is wait…. TO BE CONTINUED
