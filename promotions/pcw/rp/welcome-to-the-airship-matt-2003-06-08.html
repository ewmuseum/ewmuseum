Welcome to the Airship...Matt

8 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/08/03 Matt Jackson’s PCW career has been rapidly changing over the past few weeks, going from the Raising Star Champion…to next to nothing. Looking worse then he did last month, just passing things off and taking everything with a grain of salt. Nothing about him is the same since he came into PCW…no longer the cold-hearted man that he once was, hoping that the PCW would close and that everyone would respect him. He was acting like Silvio Rochelli, currently is doing. Demanding things and going about everything in the wrong way, he is what Matt Jackson was. He even took the next step up, interfering in Jackson’s match at Fusion, believing that he should be allowed to cost Matt a loss…slowly becoming a burden. Still despite his best efforts, the passive Jackson didn’t get angry, only trying to prove to Silvio what he was doing, was wrong, but it resulted in Rochelli spitting in his face and running away, like a dog with his tail between his legs. Now they have a match, probably making Silvio water at the mouth, knowing he gets his chance to square off with one of the best in PCW…getting a chance to make a name for himself. Likely his experience isn’t going to be what he thinks it will. Well this is a nice room…this airship trip should be fun. I just hope they have lots of donuts, Matt thinks to himself, as a smile parts his angular face. Gently he pushes open the door to his quarters and looks in, not walking in right away, but taking in the small room he is going to call home for the next few weeks. Everything is just, as he expected it, the powder white walls, with the king-sized bed in the corner, draped with a white comforter, tucked in so tightly, making the bed look cozy. Off to the right of the bed, is a large oak dresser, each of the seven drawers having a rather dull brass handle, with some ornate design carved into the legs of it to make it look more costly then it really was, and against the wall to Matt’s right is a recliner, black leather, just like he requested. Ducking his head down a bit, he walks into the room; careful to make sure his tall spiky blonde hair, doesn’t collide with the frame. Slung over his shoulder, is a navel blue duffle bag and in his left hand is a brown canvas suitcase. Following behind Matt are two women, directly behind him is the first of his guests…Yulia Volkova. She stands about five foot eight inches; her short black hair hangs down over her round face a bit, but doesn’t take away from her beautiful face, which is outlined so perfectly by the red lipstick and pink blush on her cheeks. Her slender body is covered in a pair of tight low-cut dark denim jeans and a white tank top. Standing right behind her is Yulia’s musical partner…Lena Katina. She stands just a few feet taller then Yulia, but their differences are highly notable with Lena’s sandy blonde hair, which is pulled back behind her head. The lack of make-up also sets her apart, as she has nothing else on, but a bit of lip-gloss. Her body is intertwined with a black mini-skirt and a white blouse. She just like Yulia is carrying two black canvas suitcases, one in each of their hands, like the agent before them, they walk into the room, not having to duck, due to the fact their hair doesn’t add an extra foot like Matt’s. Tossing his duffle bag next to the bed and setting his suitcase down right along with it, Jackson plops down on the bed, letting his feet hang down, weighted down by the black combat boots on his feet. He places his hands behind his head, letting the black gloves atop of them, rest against his hair and form a type of pillow. The trip thus far, isn’t exactly what he thought it was going to be…he didn’t expect to end up with the pop group of T.A.T.u in his room, but Yulia’s face and sad powder blue eyes, got the better of him and he agreed to let them come with him. It will be making for an interesting ride, with Haley coming rather shortly; she wasn’t going to find this, as great as the Stampede currently thinks it is, but she will get over it and if not, this is going to be one long trip, with no exit. Unmoving from his position, his emerald irises start up at the ceiling, wondering what PCW holds for him in the future…knowing that everyone knows that he’s no long invulnerable, but just like them, biting a couple of losses. The only question on his mind now, is how badly this is going to spiral out of control and considering the way it’s been going, there’s a good chance it might, but Jackson’s not just going to take a back seat and watch it happen, he’s not just going to let Silvio come into PCW and knock him off the throne he made, the defenses of the perch might have been weakened, but they are far from broken. This week on Fusion is only the start…the rebirth and repair of what has been lost. “Mr. Jackson…we are so glad you let us come and stay with you…” Yulia’s soft voice, cuts through his thoughts…bringing Matt back to the reality, that he has a goddess and her friend in his room. In the confusion and tiredness, he had completely forgotten they were there. Sitting up, Jackson shoots a glance their way and smiles, at seeing Yulia. She gives him a little smile back, pressing her dark red lips together and blushing a bit, as Matt just stares at the raven-haired beauty. “It’s really no problem, but if you could please call me Matt…those formalities never did anything for me…” Matt says, feeling a little uncomfortable with the two girls staring at him, but he shrugs it off and continues to sit on the bed, watching his red trench coat dangle over the bright wooden floor beneath it. The coat is the one thing about him no one understands…just passing it off, as he is abnormal, but it’s in place, because it’s the colour of his mother’s favourite flower, which in flower talk means…determination, the thing he is most right now. Everything he worked for isn’t going to fall apart…not now. Even with Silvio costing him a win, it’s not anything he can’t move past and continue without. He isn’t angry about the whole situation, just frustrated that Silvio is going about things the way he is, that’s why he’s going to end it all on Fusion and put a stop to the disrespect that he’s been showing. The only problem is, it’s not just him and Rochelli, there’s two others…men who aren’t used to competition alone, they won’t be much of a problem. “I think I’m going to explore the ship a bit…want to come Yulia?” Lena asks, tugging on the arm of her friend, Yulia stands…unmoving staring at the troubled looking agent and gentle shakes her head, which throws her hair out of the way a bit, and reveals more of her face. Lena lets go and slowly glides across the room in a few swift steps and leaves, letting the door click shut behind her, as Matt and Yulia, sit and stare at each other, though his eyes aren’t directly on her and his mind is so far lost in his own world, that he barely notices she is there. “Is everything okay?” She asks with a friendly Russian accent, once again Matt startles out of his thoughts and smiles a bit at the young singer. She once again smiles; filling the room with a bright light…the light of an angel almost, the warmness behind the smile fills Jackson and makes him smile back at her, showing his perfect white teeth, as he drifts into her eyes, crossing his arms over his barrel of a chest. “I think everything is going to be just fine…” ~>… “You think I am going to consider you a threat Silvio? That’s a laughable matter…” Matt Jackson is sprawled out on the large king-sized bed, with the ornately carved out frame, covered up to his chest in a soft comforter. His eyes focus in on the young beauty who’s snuggled against his right arm, holding on tightly, letting Jackson feel some comfort in the young body next to him. His face is one of total passiveness and relaxation, as Yulia rolls over and looks up into his big eyes. He only smiles and continues to lie. You are in the PCW now, there’s no place to run anymore…no place to hide. Everything you have been trying to get and prove ends, his mind sneers. I don’t hate you for it, but I can’t stand for the fact…you spit in everyone’s faces with blatant disrespect. It makes me sick, that you bought and cheated your way into the PCW. You think I am going to stand by and watch you harm defenseless people…never. Letting his eyes wonder around the room, he sees their luggage all round the room, still canceled in their suitcases. With ease, he slides his arm from her grip and slides it behind her neck and ushers her in closer, letting her head, rest on his chest, hearing the crushing of the material of his trench coat. Both are still fully clothed under the billowy comforter, as Yulia slowly starts to shut her heavy eyelids, not caring as Matt pulls her closer to him and continues to match wits with the ceiling. What do you hope to accomplish Silvio? His mind bellows out, showing his dislike of the man. You think that you can just come into the PCW, after a stint in the PWA and try to take me out…try to topple my empire. Not likely that it’s going to happen…at least not on Fusion. Who’s going to stop me, from taking you out? Preston? I doubt the man even understands the set-up of a singles match…Warrior? You think he’s going to want to step toe to toe with the best new comer? I highly doubt it, but this match should be too your advantage. His face splits in two, as a smile over comes him, after his last thought. This match isn’t to Silvio’s advantage as much as he hopes it will be…this is just one of many four corners matches Jackson has taken part in. Before, he won the Raising Star Title, it seemed like there was one every week…this is his element, his plain of existence, which he can welcome Silvio to the PCW in his own way and show him it’s not exactly, what he believes it’s going to be. Yulia looks up at Matt and rubs her petite hand over his chest and lets it fall just past his waist…trying to figure out what exactly is running through the head of the man, she just met a few hours ago. The man that she is developing a crush on, even with his distance and bleakness. She can’t decide, but there’s something about this caring man, that draws her in…draws her closer. “Is something the matter Matt?” She questions, her Russian accent still prevalent and the warmness is still there. Matt looks down at the young face, trying to fake a nod, but she sees right through it and continues to probe his eyes with her own…seeing if she can’t reach into the depths of his soul and figure out the problem with him. “Yes actually there is,” he begins, knowing that he can’t lie to her. “I just have a match on Fusion and as much as I want to believe that it’s going to be okay and nothing is going to stop me…I don’t believe it to be true, ever since I lost to Edwards, I just haven’t been the same dominating person I was in the ring. I’m just scared is all and I don’t know anyway to convince myself other wise.” “Everyone is scared Matt,” she says in her thick Russian accent. “The difference is…people either deal with it or they fall apart. You are falling a part right now and that’s not something you can just let happen…you aren’t the kind of man to just give-up when the chips are down.” She quickly smiles to get her point across, moving closer to Matt face, keeping her eyes deadlocked with his. In almost one motion, she swings her leg over his whole waist and plants her hands on his chest, giggling the whole time. Jackson looks over to the comforter, that was just across him a few seconds ago, to see it in disarray at the end of the bed. Yulia leans her head down and brushes her soft lips against Matt’s…he pushes her raven coloured hair out of the way and their lips touch again, this time they kiss. Silvio…welcome to PCW, it’s going to be a bumpy ride.
