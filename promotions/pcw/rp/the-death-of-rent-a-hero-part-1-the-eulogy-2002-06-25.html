The Death Of Rent-A-Hero...Part 1-The Eulogy

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: The Death Of Rent-A-Hero...Part 1-The Eulogy Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 6-25-2002 OOC:You will see where this is all going in due time, just sit back, relax, and enjoy the unseen ride... ..::/::..The sounds of quiet wind howl throughout the background....the loud and vivacious openings we normally are used to...replaced by utter darkness as a solemn voice speaks aloud. A loud, commanding voice as if it were the speakings of a higher God or so. This voice's words not just sounding like the run of the mill scary movie intro...but as if it were a prophet to a certain future...a future none of us would want to be saddled with, yet were so responsible with branding upon the ever-loveable Rent-A-Hero...the voice speaks, uninterrupted and ominous of things to come..::/::.. Dead.... Dead from the sins of those he thought cared about him... Dead from the power of the ones he tried to protect.... Dead in all ways possible to man... His superpowers and heroic standing not even being enough to keep him alive... YOU did this to him....each and every one of you who claim to be his 'Rent-Heads'... You all lied....you lied through your teeth about your feelings for Rent-A-Hero... You ALL turned your backs on him when he needed you most...To think all the times he put himself on the line for YOU... The ladder matches... The countless beatings.... The constant NEED to show appreciation and love to YOU.... Neglecting his home life, sometimes his love life, and at times; his mental sanity for YOU...And you repay him by what.... Turning your back to him and lettting him drift into the wind... That's the problem with the world... You latch onto someone who will sacrifice his entire being to you....love you with all their might... Then when the times get hard and they ask for that SAME love, that same support....You turn your backs on them in favor of another, leaving them alone and cold in the world... YOU ALL DID THIS TO RENT-A-HERO... HE DIDN'T KILL HIMSELF....YOU KILLED RENT-A-HERO!!! EACH AND EVERY ONE OF YOU WITH YOUR SELFISH ACTS... From the sins of the one he fell madly in love with.... To the betrayal of a close friend.... Each and everybody had an intricate part in the death of a man who never even reached his full potential.... ..::/::..The darkness subsides for a few short moments....long enough for a picture of Rent-A-Hero, donned in a full body red and black Spider-Man costume with the mask cut off at the mouth, a huge smile tattooed on his face in the picture as he points out to a sea of PCW fans, their hands outstretched and wanting for a tag. Flashbulbs litter the sea of fans like a blankets of twinkling stars at night, our hero having that unmistakeable look of estatic joy in his face...that joy of entertaining and becoming one with the fans..::/::..Are you proud of yourselves? How does it feel to be baptized in the blood of an innocent man? An innocent man who just wanted love, acceptance, and a sense of pride in his life....all taken away from him with a mere short series of events, and all of you can thank yourselves for it. Clap hard, clap loud...you mercilessly, brutally, sadistically, CRUCIFIED one of the few saving graces of this world... What is it about the world and destruction of any good within it? When ONE ray of pure light tries to shine it's way through all the darkness you ingrats shine upon the earth, you flush it out, kill the source without a second thought....you all just enjoy wallowing int he sin you have created. Want examples? John Kennedy... Martin Luther King Jr. Abraham Lincoln King Arthur Princess DianaAll they wanted was to drive back the darkness in the world that keeps us from all living in a world where you don't have to squint your eyes to see the good....a world where safety is not something that has to be enforced, it just happens....a world where love is blind....where love has no color or conditions....WHY THE HELL CAN'T YOU PEOPLE ACCEPT A GOOD THING WHEN IT STARES YOU RIGHT IN THE FACE?! WHY DO YOU HAVE TO JUST KILL IT LIKE YOU DO EVERY OTHER BEAUTIFUL THING THAT TRIES TO THRIVE TO SURVIVE IN THIS WORLD OF OVERFLOWING EVIL! Just like a little child who crumples a golden sunflower between their hands in a wide open grassy field....you don't see what you have just destroyed as an object of grace and beauty, but as just another item yet to be destroyed. You used Rent-A-Hero....everyone used him... Amy... The Fans... His 'friends'... They all fed off the fact that he would lay down everything for them and asked for NOTHING in return....and that is the true mark of a gulliable, bendable fool...so of course, he was used... Whether it be his limitless talents... His power to make people smile... His selfless acts in the face of bad and good times... Or anything else you might be able to ponder....Rent-A-Hero was just a mere PAWN in everyone's entire chess game...a piece that could be moved and used at will and when sacrificed, easily replaced. For that, I hate you all....You are all UNWORTHY to have even been graced by the good soul that was Rent-A-Hero....From his golden sun blonde hair...To his ripped, Adonis structured body and good looks... To his endless talent and capacity for ever exceeding amounts of love... To his quirky nature that could never be matched or replaced... You all deserved NONE of it...All you people did was drain him for what he was wirth and destroy him slowly...it started from the very inside... And it ended with the destruction of the empty shell that was left... You ingrats... You peons... You leeches... Are you so soulless and lost on the inside that you had to feed off yet ANOTHER martyr, drain him, and let him die like the rest.... You people make me sick for that...And while the man that was once called PCW's superhero rots within a wooden coffin, buried deep under the earth, never to enjoy another rise of the sunlight.... Never to enjoy the feeling of love... Never to enjoy the roar of a hot crowd...Never to play a new game for one of his many gaming systems... Never to spout off a 'HOLY SHIZZNITTLEBAM' or a 'HOLY BOOTY SHORTS'... And while these are just a few tiny things he shall miss, what do you people do... You look for the next martyr to feed from, the next man or woman you can drain for all their love...And with that, you ALL deserve to be six feet deep. I pray for the next who wants to give their love to this cruel and selfish world...For they will drain you, discard you, and feed off yet another...the pattern never changes, the cycle is always the same.... From the fan in the first row.... To Amy Campbell... To Jason Snow... Jeremy Howard...To the damsel in distress... To the Dark Ages... To the 'Rent-Heads' across the country.... You are all LIARS.....you are all LEECHES... And I await your slow deaths.....Only then, when the world is cleaned of all of you sick and sadistic people.... Will our hero be truly avenged. Rest In Peace Rent-A-Hero....a rest way before your time....To think this slow death....all started over the words... Rent-A-Hero:'Will You Marry Me?' It's time you feel the worst pain possible...What's the only way you can TRULY suffer for the pain our hero felt in his final days.... LIVING ITYou owe this to him you bastards.... Live his pain... LAST-RENT-A-TIME....LAST RENT-A-CHANNEL.....He shall be avenged... ---- View Rent-a-Hero's Biography
