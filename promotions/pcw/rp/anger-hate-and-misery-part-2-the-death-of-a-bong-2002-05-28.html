Anger, Hate, and Misery - Part 2 - The Death Of A Bong

28 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 28 [The scene opens on a long, brown haired freak. Oh, that's just one half of The Cowabunga Connection, 'Heavy Metal' Harry. His brown goatee has spots of lint, chunks of food, and wads of spit hanging down. His dark brown eyes are immensely glazed over still from the day before, another straight day of tokin' the cheeba. His eyes, though glazed, seem unbelievably determined. They are fixed straight ahead on a goal, at least he's got one now. He takes in a deep breath and a football helment now covers his head. Some of his brown hair hangs out from the helmet as his eyes now begin to light up with a large amount of excitement and pride. The helmet is a Pittsburgh Steelers football helmete, with 'Bongus' painted across the top where Harry's forehead is at. He takes in a large mouth piece and holds a Twisted Sister zippo lighter up and now smiles with glee, as if a child who is about to play with his brand new Christmas present] -=- This is far out, man. Jerome Bongus is all ready to be fired up, man. Hmmm, I'm a little pissed off that Chewbacca wasn't in the new Star Wars movie, man. I mean he's the entire reason that stoners love Star Wars, man!! I know that I'm no marketing genius but they needed to put some kind of far out wookie in that movie, man. WOOKIES ARE FAR OUT, MAN! -=- [The scene zooms out showing Harry in his usual Twisted Sister T-shirt, black leather pants, and black cowboy boots. He has two large containers strapped to his back almost like a proton pack that the Ghostbusters wear. One side contains a large amount of water and the other side is completely empty and clear. He lights up the zippo and there is a small extended part of the contraption on his back with a container full of the 'leafy stuff'. Harry holds the flame over the 'leafy stuff' and begins inhaling with a vengence. The water in the backpack bubbles and fiercely moves around as the empty container now fills up with large amounts of smoke. Harry's eyes begin turning dark red, the veins in his head begin to bulge out, and his stocky chest blows up like a balloon. He then takes the mouthpiece out of his mouth and holds the smoke inside, letting the fumes decorate his brain with colorful thoughts. He then quickly exhales and a large amount of smoke exhales from his mouth and nose. He giggles madly and does a victory dance like a football player would scoring a touchdown] -=- KILLER, MAN!! This is the best stuff I've had since that trip to Jamaica...MON!! HAHAHA! Wait...I just laughed in my subconcsious, is that even possible, man? This is freaky because I can hear my own voice inside my head, man. Wow... -=- [Suddenly everything becomes a large haze for Harry. Reality soon leaves and a world of fantasy rears its head into his mind. He is still in 'Totally Tubular' Tommy's living room which is filled with surfing memorabilia, expensive leather furniture, and a large entertainment center that has far out electronic equipment like a stereo, television, X-Box, and DVD Player. Harry does notice that one thing is missing from these items in the living room, and that's all of Tommy's surfing memorabilia. His trophy case is empty, congratulation cards have been taken from the shelves, and complimentary surfboards are missing. Harry just shrugs his shoulders and begins laughing loudly to himself as the Steelers football helmet, also known as Jerome Bongus, still occupies his head] -=- I wonder if Tommy still has that far out movie on DVD...Mulholland Drive. That lesbian scene is killer, man! -=- [Harry goes over to the entertainment center and begins searching the drawers for the DVD. He is interrupted by a large hairy hand on his shoulder] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: What, man? [Harry turns around and sees the star of the first Star Wars trilogy, CHEWBACCA!!] Harry: FAR OUT, MAN!! [The large hairy wookie, from planet Endor, stands proudly] Chewbacca: ARRRR!!! Harry: I know, man. I'm glad to see you too. Chewbacca: ARRRR!! Harry: You love me, man? Chewbacca: ARRR...ARRR....ARRRRRRRR! [Harry begins breaking into tears of happiness] Harry: I love you too, man! [The two embrace and then the sound of a very eerie guitar comes chiming in. Harry looks to the sky, as does his newly found love, Chewbacca. 'Keep on Rockin' In The Free World' by Neil Young becomes playing out of absolutely no where. Suddenly Harry and Chewbacca begin line dancing, that's right, LINE DANCING, to Neil Young's kick ass rock classic] Harry: Keep On Rockin' In The Free World!!! Chewbacca: ARRRR!! Harry: Keep On Rockkn' In The Free World!!! Chewbacca: ARRRR!! [Then the sliding doors behind the two line dancing weirdo's opens and Pearl Jam's Eddie Vedder walks into the room with a microphone in hand. He looks at the wookie and the stoner with a timid look and then suddenly joins them in the line dancing to Neil Young's Rockin' In The Free World] Eddie Vedder and Harry: Keep On Rockin' In The Free World!!! Chewbacca: ARRRR!! Eddie Vedder: Keep On Rockin' In.... Harry: JEREMY SPOKE IN!! SPOKE IN!! CLASSSS TODAY...man.... Chewbacca: ARRRR ARRRR ARRRRRRRRRRRRR!! [Harry looks at Chewbacca weirdly and then sees the angry look on Eddie Vedder's face] Harry: Wrong song, man? OH yeah!!! [Suddenly the hazy images of the mighty wookie from planet Endor and the Grunge rock singer Eddie Vedder disappear as a large object comes crashing through the two sliding doors of 'Totally Tubular' Tommy's beach house. Harry leaps up to see one of Tommy's surfing trophies lying on the floor surrounded by a small pile of glass] Harry: Far out, man.... [A very muscular, tanned man comes running up the deck and leaps through the newly broken door like he's some sort of evil demon come to collect someones soul. Harry looks on in fright as he begins to cower in the corner] -=- It's Boba Fett, man!!! He's coming for that bounty on my head, man -=- [Luckily for Harry, it's not Boba Fett, but it's his best friend and tag team partner, 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. The professional surfer turned professional wrestler looks like a wreck. His blonde hair is all over the place, there are black rings around his blue eyes, and his designer Hawaiian button down shirt is covered in blood, dirt, and sand. Tommy then grabs the surfing trophy out of Harry's hand and begins bashing it off of the hardwood floor] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: DIE!!!! DIE TROPHY DIE!!! [Harry no longer feels the fear, but more of curiousity as he now wants to bash something into the ground] -=- Hmmm, I bet he wouldn't mind if I kicked in the television, man. Nah, that means I can't watch that killer lesbian scene in Mulholland Drive. I'll punch the walls in, man!! Yeah! -=- [Harry pulls back and throws a hard punch into the sky blue painted wall, but the only thing that happens is a jolt of pain up Harry's arm] Harry: ARGH, man! [Harry holds his arm in pain as Tommy throws the trophy back outside. Tommy then looks around the living but finds that all his trophies and memorabilia are all completely trashed. A bodacious anarchy job well done, his anger is sustained for now] TTT: Harry, I feel much better now. I really needed to just break some stuff and all my surfing trophies seemed totally radical to break. It felt so good, dude. I felt this rush of bodacious power running through my veins, and that I could accomplish anything. I hold the power, compadre!!! [Harry shrugs, stoned out of his mind] Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: I'm ready for this Extreme Title match at Fusion, dude. I'm going to contain my anger and hate for only so long and then let it all build up for Fusion. Once the anger is balled up like a big radical ball of Play Do, then I'll take it out on all of those bogus dudes in the match. I don't care if it's Xavier Kannon, Duke Williams, Miyagawa...I'm going to make those bogus dudes suffer what I have been suffering for the past two months. I'm tired of us being held down and treated like a couple heinous dudes. Just because we don't go around sucking top guys coc--- [Harry decides that this is a good time to think about the new Star Wars movie] -=- What the hell was up with Yoda in the movie, man? I thought that the little odd dude was crippled or something? Oh well, it was a killer scene to say the least. Personally, I feel that Natalie Portman could have gotten naked and sexed up Anakin all night long. They didn't show that though, which was really not far out, not far out at all, man. Actually Natalie Portman should have just been naked throughout the whole movie, man. I mean everyone knows what is going to happen, man!! Anakin gets evil, puts on a far out looking mask, and talks like that guy from the Bell Atlantic commercials, man. JUST SHOW HER NAKED, MAN!! -=- TTT: ---then eat it out, compadre!! Talk about a bunch of ass kissers, literally! [Harry seems confused] Harry: HUH, man? [Tommy looks at Harry with very cold, dark eyes] TTT: You mean that you weren't listening to my speech about winning the Extreme Title on Fusion?! Harry: I was listening, man. TTT: What did I say, dude? Harry: That you were going to win the extreme title, man. TTT: That's a given, but what did I say about Tyler Nelson's crooked co--- Harry: That it was crooked, man. [Tommy looks on, satisfied] TTT: You were listening. That's just too odd!!!!! [Tommy leaps over to Harry, rips the Steelers helmet from his head and then spikes it to the hardwood floor. The helmet shatters into pieces and slides all over the living room. Harry drops to his knees, with tears already forming up in his eyes. Tommy breathes in and breathes out feeling truly satisfied like a smoker who inhales the final drag on a cigarette] TTT: Sorry, dude, but everything that is slightly meaningless to me now is all broken. I'll buy you a new Jerome Bongus helmet. [Harry doesn't hear anything at all] -=- How could Tommy do this to you, man? Your dead. Your gone. You've given me so many nights of stonedness and now your taken away from me at such a young age. WHY DID YOU HAVE TO LEAVE ME, BONGUS?!?! WHY, MAN?!?! -=- [The scene then fades to black]
