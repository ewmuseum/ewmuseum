The first REAL promo(Part 2)

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 24, 2002 [Fast forward twenty minutes. Eddie is still trying to get used to the chair. After being introduced to the quasi-life of a wrestling star…well, at least making the promos, Eddie is rather comfortable at the moment. He doesn’t want to leave the chair to do anything at this point. However, James and Steve re-enter the trailer, Steve is drinking another Vanilla Coke. James has a tape with him labelled “Promos 101.”] James: Alright Eddie, it’s time to start learning about promos. Eddie: Later. James: Promos are what make the wrestling industry… Eddie: I SAID LATER! James: AND I SAY NOW! YOU’RE GOING TO LEARN THIS, BECAUSE IF YOU DON’T YOU’LL BE TERMINATED, AND ARRESTED, GOT IT?! [Eddie now, realizes the gravity of the situation, sits up right.] Eddie: Yes, Sir! James: Okay, now, here is a tape of some material that we picked up from the past. [James inserts a tape into the VCR. Steve grabs a stool, and a bag of popcorn, and he sits down. The tape starts playing automatically. Turns out the tape were a segment from Wrestlemania 4. We see “Mean” Gene Okerlund, decked out in a tuxedo and microphone, and Hulk Hogan standing to his right, in his in-ring attire, “Hulkamania” Headband, and gold chain with cross.] Gene: Alright, over the last couple of months, it has been very difficult to introduce this man without using World Wrestling Federation Champion…Hulk Hogan, here at Wrestlemania four this afternoon, you’ve got the opportunity to change that… Hulk: Oh yeah it’s been hard to live with man. FEE, FI, FO, FUMB, ANDRE, one long year and your time has come, man. No marks, no scars, no blemishes on the Hulkster brother. But inside, man, I’ve been scarred for one long year, everywhere I go, man…All the little Hulksters ask me, “Is there any truth to the fact that there was a controversial count?”, “Hulkster, did you really get him over your head? Did you really beat the Giant?” Well, today man, at Wrestlemania Four, we’re gonna wipe all the controversy out. Andre the Giant, in the second round, when you’re fresh as a daisy, with the whole world watching, I’m gonna prove, brother, that I can beatcha, anywhere, anytime, and all my Hulkamaniacs they’re gonna feel it too. Gene: Speaking of the Hulkamaniacs, Hulk Hogan, we have seen it here in Atlantic City, and I know millions others are watching, very intently, all around the world… Hulk: Yeah, but have you looked in their eyes, man. Have you seen the fear in all those little Hulksters? They realize when I get Andre the Giant cinched up in the launch position, when I slam him through the Trump Plaza, brother. From New York, down to Tampa, Florida, the fault line is gonna break off. And as Andre the Giant falls into the ocean, as my next two opponents fall to the ocean floor and I pin ‘em. So will Donald Trump and all the Hulkamaniacs, but as Donald Trump hangs onto the top of the Trump Plaza, with his family under his other arm, as they sink to the bottom of the sea, THANK GOD DONALD TRUMP’S A HULKAMANIAC! He’ll know enough to let go of his materialistic possessions, hang onto the wife and kids, dog paddle with his life, all the way to safety, but Donald if something happens and you run out of gas, and all those little Hulkamaniacs, they run out of gas, just hang onto the largest back in the world, and I’ll dog paddle us, backstroke all of us to safety! Gene: WHOA! I THANK YOU HULK HOGAN, LET”S GET BACK TO ACTION, WOW! [The tape flips back to snow. James is smiling, Steve is still eating popcorn.] Eddie: You have got to be [BEEP]ing me. You want me to look like that?! James: No, not at all…but we do want you to head to the trailer across the way. Eddie: For what purpose? James: You’ll see, just come with me. [Eddie, James, and Steve exit the trailer. They walk across the set which they are still constructing. They head to another trailer, Eddie enters it, and they see two people in there. One of them, male, is wearing all black. The other is female, also wearing all black. Eddie looks at them both briefly. Eddie Turns to James. He nods, and Eddie continues into the trailer. The male begins speaking first.] Male (With a slight lisp): HI! I’m Stuart, and I’m your stylist. Allow me the pleasure to introduce Kara, who is your make-up artist… [Eddie somewhat rudely turns his back and storms out of the trailer. His pace is incredible. James can’t catch up with him, until Eddie heads back into his trailer. James almost has the door slammed on his face. Steve who is following James, who is too slow, ends up getting his hand slammed into the Trailer door.] Steve: OWWW! YOU MUTHA[BEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEP!] Eddie: No way, NO [BEEP] ING WAY WILL I ALLOW THAT…FREAK TO TOUCH ME! James: YOU DARE CALL THEM FREAKS?! LOOK AT YOURSELF! Eddie: When I do this…I do it on my own terms, and not some [BEEEEP] in the office who thinks I’m a freaky looking Asshole, and you know what…I AM! LIVE WITH IT! James: Okay, Eddie, calm down. The only thing that they will do, is…slightly…alter you to make you a little more camera friendly. Eddie: Slightly? Define Slightly. James: Well, they will define your blade scars a bit better, cover up any entry wound scars, and try different styles of hair, without changing the color. How’s that? Eddie: Well…Since you put it that way… [Eddie exits his trailer, turns out that Steve was trying to get in at the time, and ends up getting a door in the face. Eddie cracks a smile and a slight chuckle as he returns to the other trailer. He sees the two “freaks,” and he well…] Eddie: Sorry about the little outburst. Stuart: Hey, we have our moments. Shall we start? Eddie: What the hell? What do I have to lose; no one’s going to see me in here. [Eddie takes a seat. James as well takes a seat. James picks up the latest copy of PCW Magazine, and begins to read it. Stuart, the stylist begins to work on the head of Eddie. As per James’ promise, Stuart does not use scissors at all, and works with what he has. After a few minutes, the first style is done. All that has been done, is the hair is slicked back.] Stuart: Well? Eddie: Meh…Try something else. [Stuart returns to work. James still reads the Magazine. Another few minutes pass…and Stuart completes his work again. This time, the hair looks like Kensuke Suzahara’s style from his PURE days]. Stuart: Well? Eddie: Ugh! Makes me look gay, do something else, quick! [Stuart returns to work again, and James still reads the Magazine. Stuart, now with beads of sweat forming on his forehead, goes all out. This time a good ten to fifteen minutes pass. This time, his hair looks like Jeffry McWild’s from Virtua Fighter] Stuart: Well? How’s that? Eddie: Hmm…not bad. Although I need to be about 200 pounds heavier, and be a black man in order for it to look very good, if you can try once more. Stuart: Okay. [Stuart tries one more time. James pulls out a huge centerfold of a female PCW Personality. It only takes a minute this time. Now his hair looks like Jacky Bryant’s from Virtua Fighter] Stuart: How’s this? [Eddie cracks the biggest smile of his life.] Eddie: GREAT!!! Ow… James: What is it? Eddie: I smiled too much. James: It was only for three seconds. Eddie: I know…I smile too much. Stuart: KARA! You’re up! [Now the Make-up artist begins her magic. It doesn’t take long, as she does a few things to Eddie. She backs away, and he notices that his scars are almost highlighted.] Kara: How is that Eddie? Eddie: Very good, your reputation is rather warranted. Kara: Why thank you. James, care to give your approval? [James stops looking at the centerfold for a brief moment, and Looks at Eddie.] James: Very nice, you are ready for the big time. [Eddie and Company stands up and exits the trailer and we fade to black…for now.]
