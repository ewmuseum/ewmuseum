That's the Way Things Have to Be.

3 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 3rd, 2003 As the sun rises the chill of the morning slowly decreases as the sun heats the atmosphere slightly. Inside of my Chatham home the heat had yet to come on. I shivered as the cold penetrated it’s way though the blankets as I pulled it over my head. I suddenly wished that I was by Chris’ side. I wished that I hadn’t gotten all upset regardless of the situation I had been put in, and I wished that I could just wake up and all my worries would fade away. No more Ashea, no more problems, and if need be no more life. Normally I would be happy to be back home, but without Chris there it just made me regret my actions more. If I thought things looked dark before, I had not seen anything yet. I pretty much buried myself in a cave six feet under now. Surely if Chris had been angry with me before, he’d be more than ready to rip my head off now and I was sure that was exactly what he would do once we stepped into the ring together as a team with Jackson on Friday should I make a wrong move.I sat up in bed as I heard young Christa’s feet drag across the carpet. I sat up in bed knowing that she would be making her way towards the room, and sure enough in a matter of seconds she walked through the door and found a seat on the edge of my bed. She stretched herself across the end horizontally looking at me with those big blue puppy eyes that she had obviously inherited from her father. “Tessa I thought you said I was going to get a surprise.” She said while looking down at the ground afraid that I might get mad at her for that sentence. Instead I felt my own head lower feeling guilty that I had lied to her and knew that if Chris continued to be as angry as he was, we might never go back to his apartment. “Soon Christa, soon.” I said, crawling from the head of the bed down beside her at the end, rubbing the back of her head in a sad attempt to make her feel better. I knew it wasn’t working though. If it was she would her lifted her head long and exchanged a smile with me. Instead her head remained sunk with her baby blue eyes watering up. “You and Chris don’t love each other anymore, do you?” She said, her words hitting me like a bullet. “That’s not true.” I fought back, not wanting to accept her words as a reality but knowing damn well that she could be right. “Then why can’t we go back to Chris’?” She questioned, turning her back to my face to show her anger. If she wanted something more than anything in the world I knew exactly what it was, and that was to be back with Alex and Chris at Chris’ apartment with all the joy that there once was. “Because, I don’t think it would be right after being hit in the head with a chair on Tuesday” Was the only response I could come up with. “He didn’t mean to. It was an accident!” She argued back in Chris’ defense. “He should have had a chair in the first place. Jackson is our friend!” Were the words that came from my mouth, filing the room with tension. Christa understood that you don’t beat up on friends. It was like a lesson learned in preschool but at the same time she understood that if Chris and I didn’t make up she might never be able to see Chris again and be left without a father figure once again. “Can you forgive him?” The words of Christa broke the silence with words that tugged at my heart. Forgiveness. How could I forgive someone who supposedly loved me yet brings another woman home? How could I forgive someone who nearly took my head off with an unjustified chair shot? Yet at the same time I wondered how I could stay mad at him after all he had helped me with. A father figure to the children that were not even mine that kept the tears from my eyes even in the hardest times. Yet now he was the one bringing the tears to my eyes. I was totally lost in what to do. I couldn’t stay mad at him, that would only make things worse but at the same time how could I forgive him. He had no reason to be mad at Jackson, yet for some reason he found it justified to attempt to take his head off with a chair, but instead missing and hitting me. Maybe there was something I missing. Maybe Jackson said something cruel to Chris that my ears were deaf to. But how does that make bringing another woman home right and then take up for her even after she had suggested such cruel words in my direction. It just was not right and in my eyes deserved no sympathy. I loved Chris, but if this was the way it had to be then so be it. Christa looked up into my eyes, knowing the answer. An answer she did not want to accept, so in response she jumped off the edge of the bed and headed back to her bedroom the tears in her eyes finally getting the best of her. I hated the truth just as much as she did but until Chris apologized that’s the way things would have to be. No Chris, no Alex, just Tessa and Christa alone in the world to fend for themselves.
