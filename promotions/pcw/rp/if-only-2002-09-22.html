If only...

22 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/09/02 It's 8:52 pm. I met them in a club. I noticed the big guy first, you couldn't help but see him. The way he dressed, the way he moved, he stood out. Then I saw his friend, smaller but he was really handsome. And boy, could he dance. The big one wouldn't do any more than a little wiggle every now and then but the little guy was really tearing it up. 'Hey, check out the - ' 'Seen her. She's been watching me, watching her watch you. Keep her amused, I'm going for a walk.' I couldn't help it. The green suit and hair, he looked so different that I had to at least ask his name. I got close to him and put my charms to work, even though I was already under his. We got a little closer on the dancefloor... maybe a little Dirty Dancing, you might say. He had his emerald tuxedo, I had my snakeskin catsuit. He pulled me away from the crowd and bought me a drink. I thought to myself that this was normal for him, so I played a little harder to get. 'You're looking rather well dressed' He said, using it as an excuse to look up and down me. Who could blame him? I was curvier than a circle. My blonde hair swept down my face and I'd even managed to work on my tan that day. I leaned back, pushing my shoulders backwards so my chest was thrust out. I'd used that pose a million times before. Of course, no man can resist it and if I don't want him, I can promise he'd ask his next date to do the same. 'You're a rather dapper man yourself. What's the name of your tailor?' He gave me a little smirk but before he could answer, a Flaming Zombie and a Manhattan came over the bar to us. Of course, just for more of a tease, I asked for a straw. Not really what you usually do, but you should have seen the look on his face. 'Speaking of names, how about you give me yours, sweet-cheeks?' He looked so cheeky, like there was a little boyish smile on his face and a mischievous gleam in his eyes. 'Sarah. And what do I call you?' He wasted no time in handing me a piece of paper with a phone number written on it. 'Call me on this and then call me Gaz.' I blushed as he gave me a wink. I retaliated with a quick sip of my cocktail. He must have adored watching me suck the straw. He was getting all flustered and cleared his throat. 'Well, excuse me, Sarah. I'll be with you in just a moment...' And with that, he darted off. He must have been fairly rich from the ring on his finger, it was the shiniest silver I ever saw. The disco lights reflected off it into a big rainbow. I like rainbows. So, he's not just a looker, but probably got deep pockets. Now my girlfriends call me a Gold Digger sometimes. I can't help it if the wealthy ones like me now, can I? And if I couldn't get him, then his friend must probably have a big wallet too. Mm, slow down Sarah, one at a time. Besides, you've got your hands full with ....what was his name? Hm, I'll just call him babe or something. 'I kinda like her!' 'But I haven't eaten since we got here. Here being PCW.' 'Can't you just take a sip and then wander off until we find something better?' 'If I did that, then she'd be like me. Only she'd be picked up by the council. If she didn't, I don't care to take an apprentice on. It's all or nothing. I drain her completely or she becomes just like me.' '...or I find myself one partner less against R. Kelly and Hitler.' 'Bingo.' '....fine. Wait out back.' He walked back up to me sporting that little grin again. It was beginning to grow on me, I have to admit. Total package here, he was cute, looked like his cash flow was a total river, dressed good, acted like a kid but looked all grown up in the right ways... 'Sorry to keep a lady waiting, Sarah. Some people I know have a twisted sense of humour. Shall we relocate to somewhere a little less crowded? He was right, the bar started to full up. I was never one to hang around when too many people got close, I like my space. 'Got anywhere in mind, babe?' He gave me that smile again. I think that's always been my weakness, when someone has that naughty little smirk. 'Sure I do... quite close, just a stone's throw away.' He offered his arm to me. I took it like a kid running to her mama. As I slid my hand to his, I noticed his ring seemed kinda... weird shaped. Like some bump in it. Best not let your hands get close to his jewelry yet, Sarah, restrain yourself. We both staggered a bit on the way out, I think that Manhattan must have been the icing on the cake after the guys from earlier tried to get me sloshed. I could hear the music fading slowly as the words became indistinguishable. I was just latched onto his arm and tripping over things. My fault for wearing these platform heels. I hardly even noticed which way we were going, it was hard for me to keep my eyes open. 'We're here, sweet-cheeks...' He had led me down a back alleyway. It was kinda dirty but I didn't care. I was past caring, my head was all electrified from the alcohol. My brain was practically dancing in my skull, but it wasn't out yet. I could make out ....that guy's outline, blurred against the wall. I think he knew this after he did what he did next. I felt that ring on his hand, the odd bump was gliding up and down my body. It felt electrical for a moment, his mere touch made me feel stimulated. Then the next thing I knew.... well I never knew. I just felt a huge rush of pain as he ran his fingers through my hair. I was half alive and half asleep, I simply couldn't stand or anything, not even put my hand to my heat. And then ... HE appeared. The big man from earlier. 'Snake. Hm, shouldn't we watch the poison?' 'I think your bite is deadlier. Well, she's all yours, Rezz. Thanks for ruining my night!' 'There'll be others like her. Now leave me.' It's now 8:53pm. It's true when they say your life flashes before your eyes. And that was less than a blink. Now I lay here, cold. He... he bit me and held me up by my neck. I watched as he held his mouth open. It was sickening looking down at that red gaping jaw, all those stained teeth... stained with what made my neck feel wet. And when I started to simply drip, he... UGH... those hands, he put them right over my face and spread those fingers across my eyes. I could feel his tongue licking those two holes he made. I knew he was doing it even when I lost all my feeling. Then he slung me to the floor like a spoiled brat slinging an unwanted doll down. The last thing I'll ever hear are those Police sirens and the screams around me. If only I was able to tell them my story... If only I could have warned them about the man in green, who I could spot through a tiny gap in the crowd, standing at the bar with another girl already... If only... If on...
