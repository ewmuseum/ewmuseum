Rumor Mill Reports

15 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-15-02 [Last time we saw Stephens he was at the dentist making sure all of his teeth were secure before his big clusterfuck match for the extreme title. Since then Stephens finished fourth in said match. Only his Phantom Phive teammate Ricky Thunder, a lucky old bastard Lovecraft, and a great wrestler Miyagawa, finished ahead of him. A solid showing for someone as new to the PCW as Stephens.] [Since we last saw Stephens he also managed to get a side job in which he writes rumors, stories regarding PCW and it’s wrestlers on his newly designed web page. He is sponsored by ESPN and the Sun tabloid magazine; therefore, he can be as scandalous as he wants. As of late the stable war has been heating up with the bad blood between The Political Machine and the Phantom Booker’s Group growing increasingly intense. On the next Turmoil Stephens, and his stable mates The Supreme Machine, and Ricky Thunder take on Politically Incorrect and Andy Murray.] [Fade In] [A large computer is perched atop the center of a massive mahogany desk. A closer shot to the computer screen reveals a large logo reading Stylin’ With Streak in massive lime green letters. Light blue, and lime green text, and menus cover the black screen. Down the middle of ‘Scandalous’ Ryan Stephens’ new site we see blocks of text, and though we can’t read them, we assume they’re rumor reports or stories on PCW and it’s wrestlers.] [The shot adverts itself from the screen to Ryan sitting in a large swivel chair with comfortable cushions and armrests. Stephens was always financially comfortable; however, everyone knows the wrestling business doesn’t pay all that much unless you’re truly on of the greats. Mid-carders riding around in limos just doesn’t happen. Now judging by the scenery surrounding him including the large desk, the nice swivel chair, and a massive fish tank just off to his left we assume that he’s making a solid sum of money with his new side job, (pause), or perhaps wrestling is his new side job.] [Stephens glances at the camera to acknowledge its’ presence and then back to the screen where he furiously types a new block of text. The shot looms closer to view the text. It reads…] Rumor Mill reports state that William Powell was seen rubbing a thick creamy substance labeled wrinkle remover onto the face of his tag partner and current extreme title holder, Reginald Lovecraft. The report went on to state that Lovecraft made the comment that if the wrinkle remover worked he would be sure and try black hair plugs the following day. The rumor mill also received report that because Powell though of Lovecraft as a father figure he was going to purchase him a cane for the upcoming Father’s Day so that he could maneuver better in the ring against the likes of the Phantom Phive members. [Stephens finishes typing and reads over the text he’s just typed, before nodding to himself a nod of complete satisfaction. Stephens turns to the camera in promo-like way and begins to speak.] Stephens: While this rumor may not be completely true, the fans will really get a kick out of it, and in turn the team of Politically Incorrect will get riled up and want a piece of me. There’s nothing I want more than to get two savvy veterans riled up so that they can’t outthink me in our upcoming match. I mean rather I want to admit it or not, both of them are very capable wrestlers. Even Lovecraft in his older age is a solid wrestler, but possibly, just maybe, if I can get inside their craniums, rustle the feather in their domes if you will, then I can outthink them and help my team prevail. [Stephens pauses to catch his breath and think of what he’ll say next.] Stephens: Lovecraft can make the argument that he is extreme, and despite being older, can still hit wacked out spots. Well good for him I say. I’m not like that, I intend on wrestling smart, slowly but surely dismantling my opponent, and then when he’s battered and bruised I’ll go for the big spot when I’m sure he won’t be able to counter. I don’t take unnecessary risks that could risk injury just because I want to labeled hardcore, screw that! [Stephens runs his hair through his thin brown hair and glares into the camera.] Stephens: And honestly, I mean how scared do you want me to be? I’m facing the likes of a tag team that lost to the Price Brothers for crying out loud. Here are the former tag champions losing to a team that probably wasn’t even ready for the big spotlight of PCW. And their partner for this little six-man tag, Andy “I speak jibberish” Murray. Now how on earth you expect me not to be confident in my teams’ abilities to win this match. I have the Supreme Team on my side. I have Ricky Thunder, the last man pinned by Miyagawa in the Extreme Title match, and I have one of the fiercest competitors on the PCW roster in The Supreme Machine on my side. [Stephens’ concentration is broke when he hears a voice behind him. It’s that of his wife, Dana.] Dana: Hey baby! I’m sorry to interrupt you, I just thought you might want a glass of lemonade. I wasn’t aware you were cutting a promo. [She holds a glass of iced down lemonade in her right hand and smiles at her husband. He smiles back at her and motions her over. He signals with his hand for the camera to stop shooting.] Stephens: That’s enough for now guys, you can print that, thanks. [He turns his attention to his wife who has made her way over to him and handed him the glass of lemonade in which he takes a large swallow.] Stephens: Thank you baby. And don’t worry about interrupting. I wasn’t intending on cutting a promo today, I was just going to let them get a glimpse of the new site, but I got all excited and decided to give’ em a few words. You know how it is! [The camera crew has backed up out of the way now. Ryan has set the glass down on top of an empty CD case, and seems intent on talking with his wife.] Stephens: We need to get some coasters for my new desk baby. Remind me to swing by the store later and snag some, will you? Dana: I’ll get them for you, while you update your site. Stephens: Nah, I’m going to take a break, we can go together. [Her facial expression shows that she likes this idea. Stephens picks up the glass of lemonade and stands up from his position in the swivel chair. He drapes his left arm, the one not holding lemonade, over his wife’s shoulder. They’re not really dressed to go to town, both wearing shorts, T-shirts, and athletic sandals, but they’ll just throw on a baseball cap and sunglasses so not to be noticed and go anyway.] [Stephens dismisses the camera crew with a wave of his hand and thanks them for their time with handshakes and a simple ‘thanks’. He and his wife walk arm in arm as we fade out.]
