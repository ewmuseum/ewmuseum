'Are we a swell god...or are we a monster?'

Date: not entered OOC>> This RP is accompanied by the musical lyrics of “Swell Guy” by Swift…Lyrics are in brackets, regular text outside. Thank you… [I’m sorry--that I killed you.] People never learn their lessons when dealing with certain matters. If you know not to touch a hot stove, don’t touch it. If you know that red is going to enrage a bull, don’t wear it. You know that goading a vicious dog will provoke it, prepare to be bitten. On another note…if you happen to do something that could be interpreted as a threat to the god…even accidentally…you had better run. Nothing wrong with running if it’s for the right reasons…sometimes it’s more intelligence than cowardice. But standing there…looking stupid and apologizing like saying sorry could take back everything that happened…that’s just plain ignorance. No one’s apologizing to Big D for the pain about to be brought to him…it’s all intentional… the god wants to see him scream in The Darkness for his inferiority. Akiyama wanted nothing more than to make sure that Arma-frickin’-ggeddon was unleashed on him…but why exactly? Has Big D done anything to him? Nothing in the least, to say… It was all about reminding him that he was inferior…reminding him that he was human. And being human instantly meant that you were Akiyama’s punching bag. [My bad mistake, now I’m forced to marinate,in my regrets--because I chose to make my life,so much easier, like some sort of cheater--I took away your life from the inside.] There were three of them left within the bar that late night. The first was the bartender, cleaning glasses and organizing things before he closed for the night. Like tradition, he was dressed in a white shirt and black slacks with a black apron over it all, with black bushy hair and eyebrows. The second was a customer, a rather plump fellow sitting there guzzling down some imported swill that he repeatedly told the bartender was “REAL BEER”. He was dressed in a gray striped shirt with a matching vest and slacks and leather loafers. He looked rich…right down to the fifty-dollar haircut and his one hundred dollar manicure that he held his Cuban cigar with. The third…was the Neo Void God. He had been walking around for a bit until he found a place that actually had Divine Blood in stock…this raunchy city was uneducated in their taste of liquor. He sat over in the corner of the room with his back towards the bartender, hunched over the table with his head lowered. By his right was a small crystal glass, half full of a rich red liquid. On his left was the bottle of Divine Blood. Now, there’s nothing wrong with a god drinking this piss you mortals called alcohol. It’s no Ambrosia, or wine from the Holy Grail…but it satisfies one at the time. Akiyama spent most of his twenties drinking Divine Blood while he was a Yakuza enforcer for Maxis Inc… so you could expect him to prefer the taste of it over anything else. Only the best…as they say… That phrase must not have applied to champions…at least most of the champions within PCW, by any chance. You need an example? Big D’s a frickin’ example…an example of a championship being wasted…and soon to be an example of what happens when you desire to face Akiyama in a match that has absolutely no rules whatsoever. The god just sat there…and stared down at the red drink in his glass. Behind him, the bartender could see the wide back of the rise and fall as he breathed. He could only shake his head at the size of the man. The robust cigar smoker coughed out a laugh as he looked over to Akiyama, turning back and taking a big swig of his drink. As the bartender stepped into the back room to clean up, the rich man slammed his beer down on the counter and stood up. He sat his cigar on the edge of the ashtray and stepped towards the bathroom. Only the god could hear everything else that happened next. He made no acknowledgement of what he heard…he just stared down. But in his ears he could hear the slow roll of the cigar leave the edge of the ashtray and fall to the counter. It rolled a bit more, slowing its pace before it reached the tip of the bartender’s cleaning cloth. Then Akiyama could hear the molecular reaction of when a lit flame meets alcoholic substances. Fire… ---*---*--- The rich guy was washing his hands when he sniffed. It was strong…smoky…he looked down and saw the gray smoke rolling in across the white porcelain. Pushing past the door, he drew back when he found the entire bar ablaze. The bartender came running out of the back and cursed at the sudden fire. The flames were skating across the floor now, snapping up at the tables and licking at the ceilings. They both wasted no time in finding the nearest exit, the rich man whipping out his phone and calling the fire department in mid-flee. A few moments later, the red and white trucks wailed towards the now blazing building. As the firemen rushed to hook up their materials…a sudden thought struck both the bartender and the rich patron. …inside…the god sat amongst the inferno…his head lowered. [Broken inside and I see the one I lied to, I’m so selfish, how could I forget you? because they told me everything would be ok, but I swear I think about you every fucking day.] Why was the god drinking anyway? Was it to forget about something? Someone? Maybe so… Maybe he was drinking to forget about how much of a loser Big D was…how much of a waste of his time fighting him would be…maybe he was thinking of something else… Or he could just like the taste of the drink… Whichever you decide…only he would know the exact answer. But around him there was an inferno, the flames dancing around his godly frame like worshippers praising him. But he paid them no mind at all. His thoughts were priority…he was priority. He could hear the crunching of the building going down behind him…the burning wood falling closer and closer towards the door where he sat. Then the door flew open…and a man in charred yellow ran into the room. He spotted the god sitting still at the table. Rushing over, he asked if he was crazy after he found out that he was still alive. Akiyama ignored him as he took another sip of his drink. He grabbed his arm in an effort to save the man from death…knowing not that he was above death. Akiyama most certainly didn’t appreciate being touched…he didn’t appreciate being interrupted either. His hand grabbed the underside of the man’s chin and flipped him into the table, grabbing his bottle of liquor before it spilled to the ground. What a waste of a life… Akiyama turned to leave the man unconscious in the flames, but something stopped him. No, it wasn’t mercy or grace…it wasn’t pity on the poor mortal’s soul… It was the huge fiery beam crossing his path. [I see your face, I can't erase--I can't erase the past for you.] He knew nothing of this man…why he risked his live to save others…why he wanted to be such a hero. Heroes were nothing but men who did what society considered to be the right thing. Heroes were decorated with honor… Akiyama was going to leave him decorated in burns…but he decided otherwise. He palmed the man’s skull and dragged him towards the beam. Then with his free hand he lifted the post upright. He could feel the heat tickle at his skin…Akiyama was beyond singeing. He threw the firefighter ahead of him before ducking underneath the pillar and shoving it away behind him. Then he grabbed the man’s head again and headed down the stairwell. Outside, all of them sprayed streams of pressurized water to douse the fire. One of them aimed for a window, shattering the glass and spraying inside. Akiyama looked up suddenly as a huge beam of liquid barreled for him. He dropped the man and raised his forearm to block the hose. Water splattered all around as he growled, getting irritated by their efforts of safety. Did they not know that all efforts would nevertheless prove ineffective? They did not…neither did Big D… [Same mistake, I remember like it's yesterday. what kind of person do I portray? am I a swell guy?--or am I a monster? and when I die, do I go to hell I wonder?] Hell surrounded them, and the cool refreshing water pumping in the NVG’s face didn’t help him enjoy himself. He pushed his way past the stream of water and leaped down the flight of steps to the next level. The force and the weight of the Neo Void God caused him to crash through the floor and fall into the lobby at least three stories below. He rolled around in his plummet and landed feet first, his thighs tensing and flexing in the crouched landing. He snarled a curse under his breath as he heard another movement over the crackle of the flames. Looking up, he found the fireman’s body falling right after him through the hole. The fall could have killed him…if he landed the right way. The god stepped aside and threw out his right arm as the man fell right in his hand. Looking to his left, he could see outside through the soot-covered windows. Outside, the crowd that had drawn near gasped as the body of the firefighter flew through the ground floor window. Paramedics rushed over to him as sounds began to overpower the sirens…overpower the hoses…overpower the flames… Footsteps…stomping…storming…approaching closely from one point ahead of them…from the building. Gasps and shocked expressions draped their faces as the double doors exploded open in a burst of fire…and kneeling in the doorway before the orange and red hues of the blaze…was Akiyama… [I see your face, I can't erase--I can't erase the past for you.] He arm was outstretched, having executed the HAND OF FATE© upon the gates. One of the firefighters was so surprised by his presence that he turned the hose on him. Akiyama’s face never changed as he stood up, the water blasting off his chest. Everyone watched in awestruck as the god walked slowly before the frozen firefighter. He grabbed the hose from him with one hand…his other setting the bottle of Divine Blood down and gripping him by his neck. The hand holding the hose blocked the water from shooting out as he raised it towards the man’s mouth. Screams of panic rang out…such sweet songs…his head would surely explode from the pressure pumping through that hose. But then he stopped… [And I see your face, I can't erase--I can't erase the past for you.I’m sorry.] Across from him stood the bartender and the rich man who were in the bar before. They watched just as flabbergasted as the rest of them. The firefighters had even stopped hosing down the burning building, too entranced by the man who walked out of the inferno without a scratch. Akiyama dropped the hose and picked up his bottle of Divine Blood. It snaked around like a serpent in pain, whipping the petrified fireman in the face and knocking him to the ground. The two men were still as the god approached them. As he towered over them, he drank the last bit of his drink and sat the bottle down on the pavement. Then he glared down at that inept duo…those who left him in the fire…forgetting of him… After he was done…they’d have no trouble remembering him…knowing who he was…recognizing his face… [Same mistake, I remember like it's yesterday. What kind of person do I portray? Am I a swell guy?--or am I a monster? And when I die, do I go to hell I wonder?] He saved that firefighter…but his life wouldn’t make up for the pain he was about to cause the others. The NVG’s hands wrapped around both of their throats and hoisted them into the air. Their eyes were coated in fear as the god stared back with blank…emotionless…eyes. Then he jumped up and fell face down on the ground, driving the back of both of their skulls cracking against the street. Red leaked into the crevices of the tar used to make the roads… Women screamed… Men heaved… Children cried… Nothing but memories for them to remember always…the god’s face would grace their dreams and the sound of dual skulls cracking against the ground would haunt them…consciously and subconsciously. [I see your face, I can't erase--I can't erase the past for you.] It was unholy…that roar bellowing from within the god…vibrations shook through their entire body as Akiyama laid atop of his chest, glaring into both of those men with his roar deafening the bystanders’ screams and cries. Why did people piss him off? It would only end in their destruction anyway… Big D… (I’m sorry.) If you don’t survive the altercation…whoops…you were only supposed to learn a lesson. You were just supposed to remember the face of god…and realize that you were nothing… Do you think of him to be right…or wrong about it? Your decision…either way, the god was going to remain the Neo Void God… But he still saved that man’s life… So was he a hero…or was he still the evil one? [Am I a swell guy??--or am I a monster?Am I a swell guy??--or am I a monster? Am I a swell guy?....] “…or are we a monster?”
