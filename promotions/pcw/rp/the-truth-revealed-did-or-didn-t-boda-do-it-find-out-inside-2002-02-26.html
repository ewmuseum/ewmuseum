The Truth Revealed. Did or Didn't Boda Do It? Find out inside.

26 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/26/2002 *Daybreak, and that only means one thing. Boda has to face the judge, in a pre-trial litigation. After 4 nights in the Boise Correctional Institute, Boda still can not fully recollect the events that led up to John Smith being hospitalized on Friday night. Was it indeed the doings of an outraged Boda? Or, is it simply a ploy, to keep Boda from going to March Madness, and becoming the World Champion. Let’s evaluate both sides. before Boda wakes up for his litigation meeting with the judge.* Boda Outraged *Sure, why not. He’s been screwed out of every World Title shot he has had since returning to PCW. First one against Trash Can Man last year, and Ivan got it turned into a three-way dance, and that not only cost Boda the title, but also it started the riff in the foundation of Red Army. Then came the World Title shot against Ivan. A well fought battle, with Boda and Ivan exchanging the advantage back and forth. Only to have it end with a shovel shot to the back of the head from that little weasel, Alexei. That was right before the elbow surgery that put Boda out for almost two months. Upon returning, with a fury no one has seen before in Chris, he is screwed at a PPV title shot against then Champ Jeremy Howard, by Howard himself. That set up a series of title shots at Jason Snow, the current Champ. And, what happens then? The same thing that has happened in the past to Boda.... he gets screwed. Both title shots, ending in DQ victories for Chris. But no belt. Two times, Boda beat Snow like no one has beaten him before, taking him to the shade tree and whipping his ass like a red headed step child. But no belt. Two times, New Breed had to save their leader. Two times, Boda had the gold snatched from him like a lollipop from a child. Sure, that would enrage Boda, hell, it’d enrage everyone of you.* *Sure, good theory. Hell, it might even make a man crazy enough to beat down some one else. But not Boda. He’s never been that way. Again, this isn’t right, jailed for something he knows he didn’t do. Jailed for something he can not prove he did not do. Of course, there is the possibility that Boda was set up. Jailed in an attempt to keep him from going to the PPV, and winning the title. Especially since he is the hands down favorite to win it now.* Boda Set Up?*Who would want to keep Boda from winning the title at the PPV? Who would want to keep Boda out of the PPV, out of the way? I can think of about 19 people that want to. All 19 of the wrestlers in the main event at the PPV, they all have the motivation to do this to Boda. A few of the guys in the match have no reason, so Boda thinks, of doing this. Why would Kano do this to his supposed best friend in the business? Maybe it’s the ‘Boda’s better than Kano’ factor. Sure, Kano Rocks... but Boda’s always been better, and Jon knows it, and probably deep down, he hates it. So, why not get Boda locked up, and keep him out of March Madness. And, what about Juan Borro, The Asylum members in the match. They have no reason to really keep Boda out of this Rumble, other than the mere fact that taking Boda out, gives them all that much more of a shot at winning the title. Let’s not forget Akiyama. With Boda out of the picture.... Akiyama has the ‘Big Man’ size advantage now. That’s almost a good enough reason to set some one up. Oh, and then there is Chase and Williams. Two New Breed members, both that stabbed Boda in the back, both that would like nothing more than to make Chris’ life a living hell. Plus, Chase has ‘Boda Envy’.....goes back to PCW2. Then, there is Legend Inc. The newcomer, on his second tour of duty with PCW. Why would he want Boda out of the picture? Because he wants to make a name for himself, and Boda is the biggest obstacle in his way. Of course, The Chef has more than enough reasons to incarcerate Boda, and keep him from competing. Boda has never lost to the Chef, and Boda did single handedly destroy The European Union, and that can’t sit well with the Allah praising Chef. Nah, he is to self-indulged in his new God to do something like that. Then, again.... Tyler is in the match, and Boda and Tyler go way back, and hate each other just as far back as that goes. Tyler fired Boda, sending him to PCW2. Of course, Boda returned, and made a major impact in PCW, and that can’t sit well in Tyler’s stomach. Miya on the other hand, has just as much reasoning to do this to Boda. Both have fallen short on many attempts to win the World Title. Both are on top of their games right now. So, why not eliminate Boda. Huh? Why wouldn’t the Jap-American do it. Good question. Sure, I know.... there are others in the match, and one not to be named yet, but none of them have as much motivation, as much reason to get Boda locked up as ..... JASON SNOW. Boda holds three victories in a row over him. It was a Jason Snow fan that is in the hospital. And, Snow knows, his luck is about to run out. Yeah, all fingers point at Snow. He’d be the one out of them all to want to eliminate Boda from the match. And so far.... it’s working.* Sarg: “You awake boy?” Boda: “Sure. Where’s my coffee?” Sarg: “Funny, funny. Get ready, you’ve got 10 minutes till you have to see the judge.” *Boda sits up from the prone position he is in on the uncomfortable cot. With a stretch, he views his not so friendly surroundings that he’s called ‘home’ for the past four nights. Chris steps on the cold floor, and walks to the toilet. He relieves himself, and washes up in the sink, brushing his teeth and combing his blonde locks. Chris puts on his standard issued Boise Correctional Institute Orange jumpsuit, and a pair of sneakers. He walks to the bars, and watches as they slowly open, letting him free from his 6 by 10 chamber. Boda walks out into the hall, and is led down the way by Sarg. This time, they head in the opposite direction from when Boda had to meet his lawyer Brad yesterday. This time, he is taken out the back doors, and led into a patrol car. Once inside, the Sarg shuts the back door, and climbs into the passenger front.* Sarg: “Comfy?” Boda: “Yeah, sure. It’s real comfy back here. I can barely sit and you ask if it’s comfy. Funny guy Sarg.” Sarg: “Let’s roll.” *The other officer in charge of driving Boda to see the judge backs up from the parking spot, and heads into the streets of Boise, Idaho. As he heads down the road, towards the courthouse, Sarg turns to face Boda, who looks to be in a not so comfortable position.* Sarg: “So, how’s the memory this morning?” Boda: “Just great. I remember waking up this morning, and yesterday. But, it’s funny I can’t remember that night in the bar. Guess, it won’t help my defense much now?” Sarg: “Nope. And, not to worry you, but you and your lawyer are facing the toughest judge in the state. And, I hear the people got Miles Graham to represent them. So, it won’t be looking to well for you. Too bad, because I had a bet on you becoming the Champion at the PPV. My kid really likes you.” Boda: “Well, I’d hate to disappoint your kid. So, I think I’ll still be showing up at the PPV, and winning that title. I’ve got a few guardian angels on my side.” Officer Driving: “You need a whole army of angels from what I hear.” Boda: “Whatever.” *Boda turns to face the outside. He watches as kids play on the sidewalks, and he thinks about his two little girls back home in Eustis. He thinks about his lovely wife, who had plans on flying to St. Louis to watch the PPV. The thought of never seeing them again saddens Boda. Even more... the thought of not ever having sex with his wife makes him cringe. Will Chris ever feel the loving caress of his wife again? Or, is it to be that he will be in jail with some one named Bubba, who Boda is going to have to kill if he tries to grab him a piece of Boda.* [Man, I need to get out of this somehow. Why would I risk everything to hurt some fan? It’s not possible. I know better. Some one has to be behind this all.] *The police car slows down, in front of a large white building. Media stands outside, looking in the squad car, and wanting to and trying to take pictures of PCW’s Superstar. The car stops, and Sarg gets out, and opens the door for Boda. Chris steps out, handcuffs in place, and is lead through the media, amid the hundreds of flying questions. Sarg takes Boda into the white building, which reads Boise Courthouse outside in big, marble letters. Up the stairs, Boda is led inside, and towards the courtroom. Inside, is some of the local residents, some from the bar that Friday night. Also, in attendance is PCW’s Cal Seaver, and The Pimply Faced Intern. They are seated behind Boda’s table, and are there for moral support. As Boda is lead to his seat, next to his awaiting Attorney Brad, Cal and PFI reach over the railing, and tap Boda on the shoulders. Brad asks Boda if he can yet remember anything. To which Boda responds, ‘NO, I damn well wish I did though.’ * ‘ALL RISE, THE HONORABLE JUDGE GILS LANE IS RESIDING AT THIS TIME’ *The courtroom rises, and in walks a short, balding judge. He looks like Mils Lane, who had that unsuccessful television Judge show. Only, from what Brad is telling Boda, this is Mils older brother, Gils. Judge Lane takes his seat at his desk, and the rest of the courtroom sits also. Judge Lane looks over the documents in front of him, and clears his throat.* Judge Lane: “Case 420, the People vs. Chris Sloboda, aka Boda, aka Big Bad Boda Daddy, aka The Ameri-Czech Bad Ass, aka the Next PCW World Champion.” *Judge Lane stops, and takes a sip of water, his throat apparently dry.* Judge Lane: “It says here, that Chris Sloboda, you are charged with one count of attempted manslaughter, with the possibility of being charged with manslaughter increasing if one John Smith does not recover from his coma, and passes away in the hospital. Now, this charge does of course stem from the witnesses at the bar, and from what I’ve read, you, Mr. Sloboda have no recollection of the night in question.” Brad: “That is correct your honor. My client has experienced temporary amnesia surrounding the hours in question.” Judge Lane: “Well, we are not here today to run you through the jury, to determine if you are indeed innocent or guilty. We are here to see if there is any concrete evidence in your defense that may help the case. Do you have any witnesses for the defense?” Brad: “No sir. None at this time.” Judge Lane: “Okay, well, according to eye witnesses, who are in this court room at this time, the following events happened on Friday, February 22nd, 2002......” *The judge goes over the eye witness statements, and reads them all. The stories all seem to be alike, too much alike at one point Boda tells Brad. Almost as if these were pre-fabricated. But, alas, no evidence in favor of Boda has surfaced. When the judge finishes reading the statements, he calls for Brad and Chris to stand up* Judge Lane: “At this time, I would like you to make a plea to the court. A plea of guilty will forgo the trial, and sentencing will take place one week from today. A plea of not guilty will force a trial, which you are entitled to. The trial could start in two weeks, and could last up to five months. During the trial, you will be staying at the Boise Correctional Institute. At this time, council and defendant, what plea would you like to enter.” Brad: “Sir, if I may, my client has an important event coming up on March 3rd. I would like to request my client be allowed out on bail, and allowed to wrestle in the event in St. Louis.” Judge Lane: “Let me see. You wish this court to allow your client the opportunity to wrestle, in a pay per view. Well, council. Let me remind you that your client is charged with attempted manslaughter, that charge carries a minimum sentence of 10-25 years. Now, you ask the court this request why? So he can skip bail, and leave the country? Or, because he is a famous athlete, and this entitles him to get let off. Well, had I tried O.J, or Ray Lewis.... they’d be sharing a cell with Mr. Sloboda, so the answer is no. Plead guilty, or innocent. NOW!!” Boda: “Yes your honor. I understand about the no bail request. So, I would like to enter my plea of not guilty, and hope for a speedy and timely trial.” Judge Lane: “Very well... this court recognizes the plead entered by........ WHAT THE HELL?” *Suddenly, from the back, rushes in a television on a cart, being pushed by some one. The unidentified person pushes the cart right to the front of the court, and Judge Lane gets aggravated.* Judge Lane: “WHAT IN THE HELL IS THIS ABOUT. THIS IS A COURT ROOM, NOT A CLASSROOM.” ‘I’ve got the proof that that man is innocent.’ *The whole courtroom looks to the back of the courtroom. There, in the doorway stands The White Mexican. In his right hand, a video tape. He approaches the bench, as he smiles at Boda. The other person plugs in the TV, and takes the tape from Mexi, then puts it in the vcr.* Judge Lane: “And who are you?” Mexi: “I am also council for the defendant. Sorry I am late, but, I needed to secure the evidence.” Miles Graham: ‘I object. I have no showing that Mr. Sloboda has two lawyers.’ Judge Lane: “Over ruled. Mr... what is your name?” Mexi: “Just call me Mexi.” Judge Lane: “Okay, Mr. Mexi.... I suppose your evidence is on that there tape?” Mexi: “Yes sir. Bailiff, if you please.” *The bailiff hits play on the tape, as the entire courtroom watches. Mexi narrates the viewing* Mexi: “What we have here, your honor, is the surveillance video of the Dirty Dick Bar on Feb, 22nd 2002. According to the tape, it is approximately 10:45 at night. All is quiet in the bar. We see the patrons, drinking, and shooting pool. But, look, in comes a woman, very scantily clad. And, jeez, she’s holding a briefcase, wonder what’s in it. Let’s listen” Woman on Video: “Hello guys. Listen, in this briefcase, I have about 10 thousand dollars for you to split among yourselves. Also, there is a drug in here, it’s a powerful elephant tranquilizer, and a few PCW t-shirts for some of you to wear. In a few minutes, in this bar will walk a big man, goes by the name of Boda in PCW. I want him to be drugged, and detained for beating one of you up. He’s a major player in PCW, and a threat to win the world title at March Madness, their upcoming PPV. But, it has to look like he got enraged, and beat one of you severely. No questions asked. Do what ever it takes. Just see that he doesn’t make it to the PPV.” Mexi: “And so, the mysterious lady starts to leave, but is asked why she wants this done to Boda.” Woman on Video: “Because.....Mona... CAN!!!!” Mexi: “And, thus, the mysterious lady, who is none other than my ex-wife, and a woman that Boda has scorned over the past few months, leaves, no briefcase in hand. And look, the patrons are putting on the t-shirts provided by Mona. And, one of them, John Smith, is pulling on the ‘Snow’ t-shirt. And look, here comes Boda, smiling, and happy. Celebrating his victory at Fusion. And, from there, it goes down hill. He orders a drink, and leaves for the bathroom. What... the bartender is pouring that elephant tranquilizer in the drink? Wow, who would of thought. Here comes Boda. And, there he goes, pounding it down, and look, that’s John Smith, wearing a Jason Snow PCW shirt , walking to Boda.” *The videotape plays on. Showing Boda getting wobbly from the elephant tranq in his drink. Suddenly, Boda passes out on the floor, and John Smith has the bartender call the police, and another man in the bar beats down John Smith. The cops arrive, and haul of Boda, after the bar keep tells them that he had to spray Boda with mace to get him to calm down. The judge tells Mexi to turn it off, that he’s seen enough. Mexi walks over, and sits next to Boda at the defense table.* Judge Lane: “Well, looks like the concrete evidence Chris Sloboda needs. This case is dismissed, and all charges are dropped against Mr. Sloboda. This court is adjourned.” *Boda stands up, and hugs Mexi. He shakes hands with Cal, and Pimply Faced Intern. Boda wipes the sweat from his brow.* Boda: “Mexi, thanks bro. I can’t believe Mona did this. She is a bitch. But why me?” Mexi: “Because of our friendship, she’s trying to ruin my life, so she started trying to ruin yours too. Oh well, we better roll, Turmoil is only a few hours away, and you, my friend, have a handicap match to wrestle in.” Boda: “Shit, let’s go.” *Mexi and Boda head out of the courtroom, but Boda stops to talk to Sarg* Boda: “Tell your son.... I’ll definitely be winning the title now.” *Boda walks out of the courtroom as the scene fades to black.*
