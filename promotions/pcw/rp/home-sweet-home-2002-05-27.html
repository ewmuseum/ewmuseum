Home Sweet Home

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27th May 2002 A black screen lingers as the sounds of a male voice whistling to ‘Home on the Reign’. The sounds of hissing and crackling linger as well as the sounds of birds tweeting. The screen begins to blur its way into view. Before we are fully focused all’s we can see is a blurred bright blue sky and blurred green grass, a blurred red border around the grass which appears to be the fence. To the far end of the now almost fully visible grass is a figure. We can make out much, but judging by the short brown hair that we can see, we are able to recognize it is a man. After a couple of more seconds, the whole screen becomes visible. The male figure we saw just seconds earlier is wearing a white polo T-shirt, a pair of blue jeans and a pair of brown shoes. He has a silver spatula in his right hand and is standing in front of a large red electric barbecue. On the barbecue are a couple of burgers; each topped with a slice of cheese. The man is facing the opposite direction so as of now we cannot see is face. Footsteps are heard, but from the man standing still we know these are not his. Every second or so there seem to be four foot steps giving the impression that there are two people. As the sounds draw louder, the shadows of two male figures spread across the green grass. As the seconds slowly pass by, one of the shadows reaches onto the back of Jack Price. Given more time, the taller shadow soon spreads across Jack and onto the face of the flamed barbecue. Jack quickly turns to see who is behind him. His face turns from determination to joy ads he produces a huge smile. Jack: “Boys!” The camera swings around to the right as the two males are now recognized. The man to the left is Chet Price. He is wearing a black tank top and a pair of black jeans. Just inches below his spiked blonde hair is a large white plaster. To the right of him is his brother, Michael. Michael is wearing a blue polo T-shirt and a pair of cream colored golfing pants. His short brown hair is, as always, neatly combed. Michael: “Hey dad, glad to see us?” Jack: “Sure I am, what are you guys doing here?” Chet: “We haven’t been booked this week so we thought we come home for a couple of days. Is that Ok?” Jack: “Sure it is, there’s no problem there. Here, grab a couple of plastic chairs and sit down. I’m doing a barbecue for your mother and sister when they get back.” Chet: “Where’ve they gone?” Jack: “Your mother went to pick your sister Maggie up from singing practice down at the old church.”Chet walks over to the patio at the far of the garden set outside the house. He grabs two white plastic chairs and brings them back. He places the two down beside each other and sits on one of them. Michael sits himself on the other. Jack: “It looks like a couple of these burgers are done. Michael, grab a couple of plates from the kitchen.” Obeying his father, Michael does so. Seeing that Michael is out of the clear Jack decides to have a quick talk with his other son, Chet. Jack: “I couldn’t help notice you had a plaster strapped to your head. You wanna tell me what happened?” Jack turns to face his son as Chet began to slowly touch the plaster. Chet: “What this? It’s nothing, I just got busted open in a match.” Jack: “When?” Chet: “Friday. Michael and I finally worked together.” Jack: “Oh that’s right, PCW. How did you get on?” Chet: “I got my ass busted. Michael kept trying to come in and help me but the referee kept stopping him. When the referee was distracted our opponents kept double-teaming me. One of them ripped the padding from the turnbuckle and beat my head into it. Hence the injury.” Jack: “Does it hurt?” Chet: “Not as much as hitting that open turnbuckle. Michael wasn’t able to get in until the last moments of the match.” Jack: “By the sounds of things I assume you lost?” Chet: “No dad, we won. As one guy held me from behind, another swung at me with one of those judge hammer things. I ducked leading the guy to knock out his own partner. Seeing the big opportunity, Michael dove in the ring and snatched the hammer from the standing guy. The guy spun around and WHAM! Goodnight New York. I rolled over and scored the pin.” Jack: “You try and be careful, you know Michael doesn’t like violence. Blood, gashes, slices, he hates everything. Quiet now, here he is.” Michael walks from the kitchen with a couple of white China plates spread across his hands and arms. Michael: “I see you moved everything around in there, I couldn’t find the plates right away.” Jack: “I did it as soon as your mother left. You know what she’s like. Always wanting everything in a set order, I can’t stand it.” Michael sets the plates down on a small wall beside his father barbecue, then sits down. Jack: “So Michael, Chet tells me you won your first match.” Michael: “Oh God, it was great. Chet was getting beat down the whole match and every time I tried to help him the referee stopped me. One of the guys cheated by stripping the turnbuckle. Chet was on the unfortunate end of the illegal moves. But that’s how he got the cool gash on his head, so it all even out.” Chet laughs hysterically as Jack turns back in astonishment. The scene comes to an end.
