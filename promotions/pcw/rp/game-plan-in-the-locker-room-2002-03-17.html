Game Plan in the Locker Room

17 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-17-02 {The scene opens up with Kevin and Juan sitting inside the locker room. Before their big match for the tag team titles they are both in gear with their lovely girlfriends and valets right beside them. As Juliet is massaging Juan’s shoulders Mandy is on top of Kevin kissing, and “loving” him hoping it’ll loosen him up. As Mandy gets up straightening her shirt Juliet continues to massage Juan’s shoulders in the dim lit locker room. As a knock on the door is made the camera turns toward it as a voice is heard.} Roadie: Golden Game you have ten minutes. {The camera then turn towards Kevin and Juan as they both just look on recollecting on their tag team championship reign.} Juan: Hey yo Kev I was just thinkin’ how we have to be the greatest like Fidel. Really man, Fidel is a leader and he totally rules. Kevin: Yeah man he does but we have to keep our mind set on the prize my friend. Keep the mind on the prize. Juan: Well yeah I agree wit’chya there but really man this time around we’re not gonna slip. Kevin: I’m totally with ya there man. This time ‘round we’re gonna do things our way. We are going to do everything to retain these titles that we are sooooo totally gonna win tonight man. After we smack that big doufus with the Golden Bomb much like you say Juan, instead of showing respect he’ll fall like the rest. Juliet: Like the Storms… Mandy: And FBC… Juan: Those dumb Japs Kensukie and Hirose… Kevin: And we can’t forget about those simple minded Elia brothers. By the way where is boobie boy at? {Mandy and Juliet look a bit confused.} Juan: It’s a long story we’ll tell you after we win back our titles that AFL-CIO so totally stole from us. Kevin: Well dude I so totally despise AFL whatchamacallit. Even more so the Blood Brothers. They were the ones who stole the titles from us. Then the two union jobbers beat them for the titles. Mandy: And Kevin talkin about not slippin I am putting the law down right now. No matter how bad you want to be there for Juan and not let him down. And I know everyone here knows what I’m talkin about. I remember when you passed out in the ring. NO MORE OF THAT. Juliet: What exactly happened, my little Golden Heart? Juan: Well when Kev and I were going back for our tag titles he had a huge shot for the world title. Juliet: Was that the fight against Ivan? Juan: Yeah and well Kevin got seriously hurt before the match and went out there to fight it out. He got hurt even more. And the next night we had a tag match to get back in that tag scene. Well even as sore as he was he still went out there. Juliet: And what happened? Juan: Well after I got my butt whooped and ready for a tag I seen Kev over there in pain. SO I decided to stick it out cause I didn’t want him to get hurt even more. And eventually he tagged himself in and went for a super kick. Then bam he just fell and was out of it for a while. Juliet: Oh my gosh was he all right? Kevin: Yeah I was all right but I was under too much stress and strenuous activity. So yeah… {Knock on the door again} {The roadie pokes his head through to inform them on their time left.} Roadie: GG you have two minutes… {The Roadie then exits the door and leaves.} Kevin: Juan man, it’s time to defy PCW and their American biased fight. Its time that The Golden Game take care of these two jokes once and for all. Juan: Man you are just too cocky! Kevin: Hey it’s not being cocky if you can back it up. Juan: Too true bro. Let’s get the GOLD back into THE GOLDEN GAME. {The four of them walk out the door as the camera zooms in on the opened door. The only writing on it is ‘GOLDEN GAME” written in gold around the star. The Golden Game is ready and willing to get back into the slot as “THE BEST”. We just hope that you are ready for the International Incident’s plan to dominate the title scenes.}
