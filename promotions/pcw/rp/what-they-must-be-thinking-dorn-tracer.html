Date: not entered I hate you. Linkin Park's new track. 'Somewhere I Belong.' I hate you all! It's off of their new CD, Meteora. I hate you as much as you hate me! It represents me on so many levels. The pain I feel for never being able to fit in. You are the bane of my existence. I tried it your way. Don't you think I tried? Listening to you inane pop groups full of 30-year-old men calling themselves boys, signing to 12-year-old girls about how they want to hold her hand and spend their lives with them. I watched your movies, which were big on explosions and low on plot. I refrained from reading books, because it's common knowledge that you're all idiots and don't read. I lived in an uptown apartment, went to the clubs, stayed into the hipster scene, dressed like I was supposed to... I was never good enough for you. I did everything right! Everything! Each and every part of your popular culture, I followed it to a tee! I even followed the stuff that contradicts the other stuff! I even followed the stuff that contradicts the stuff that contradicts the stuff that contradicts the other stuff! Everything I did, I did it because you told me to! I was a perfect little automaton. Your every whim, I performed it without fail. Your every desire it was my duty to fulfill, and you can't say that I didn't carry it out because I did! You always wanted more. But it was never enough. You'd bait me into thinking I was somebody. Trick me by pretending I was important to any of you, just to get more and more out of me. But when I finally reached for that full acceptance, you pushed me away like a disease-ridden hobo. Oh, how I hate you. I was never good enough for you. You always wanted more. I don't know if you were never going to accept me because you never intended to, or because I was just too flawed to associate with. I couldn't be your friend, I couldn't be your lover. I couldn't be anything more than 'That guy who does us favors.' You took advantage of me. Did you have fun? Was it good for you? I hope for your sakes that it has been enjoyable for you, because I'm not taking it anymore. It was never enough. I took the torture of embarassment, ridicule... Every day that I woke up, the only thing I could think about was receiving a satisfactorily favorable judgment from you. You judged me. Oh, everyday you judged me. Every hour, every minute, every second; every action and reaction, motion and movement, muscle contraction and blinking of my eyes was constantly scrutinized. My life was nothing more than a huge test that I could never pass, no matter how hard I tried. Because no matter what, you weren't going to pass me. No more. You hated me when I conformed. You hated me when I did everything you wanted me to do. You hated me when you could snap your fingers and I'd jump to attention. When I was doing things 'right' in your way, I was cast aside. I was a good little robot, but you didn't care. I wasn't someone you could ever be close to. I was just another face. You never cared. You pretended to, but you didn't. No more. Now, I can detect your bullshit. See through your empty smiles, to your cores. You decide from birth what people you want to be around, and everyone else is just a tool for you to manipulate as you see fit. I was one of them, I know. You can't deny it, it's in your psyches. We train people to think this way. 'Everyone's nice in their own special way.' Crap. You don't believe that, no matter what eye-opening afterschool specials that may be shown, you always form your elitist cliques, make everyone want to be like you and be friends with you, and reject them. Power... It's all about power. The power you can have over others by making them feel less of themselves. Yuo want to feel important, but you do it by maknig everyone small. All my life, you've made me feel small. Insignificant. I was never one of you, never could be. I was just another ego booster for you. And I hate it. Taking control... I hate that I used to be your tool for self-gratification. I hate that everytime I did what you said, it would give you a pleasure like hands-free masturbation. I hate you. Just as much as you hate me. You hated me, but I loved you. But you didn't care about my feelings, it was all about you. You just wanted to use me to your own ends. Congratulations, you got that far off of me. You trod on my back, and you feel pretty good about yourselves. You wake up in the morning with a smile on your face and a spring in your step. You stare into the mirror for an hour, repeating over and over 'Holy shit, I'm glad I'm me.' And it's all because you caused 100 other people to wake up depressed, slowly dragging their feet across the floor. They don't even look at themselves, they know they look bad. They hate the way they are. Because you don't approve of them. You didn't approve of me. Independence... Surprise, I break free of your grasp. I take control of my own life, I make my own decisions about my own self-worth. And you still hate me. You always will. I used to be hated because you needed it to make you feel good about yourselves. Now I'm hated because I'm not allowing you to use me to fel good about yourselves. No matter what I do, I'll never gain your favor. Not as things are... Religion... Astrology is the ticket. I'm going to use it to make you feel like you made me feel. I'm going to make you hate yourselves. And I'm going to make you love me. Astrology is the key. It is a religion based in all the things that you decry in other religions, yet secretly praise. The stars hold the answers, you just have to read them correctly. While you have a fate, there is no predestination. One can know their own future by reading the stars. Knowing one's fate, they may preserve it or change it. Changing the future... I saw my future. It was exactly the same as my past. I was going to continue on the same patht that I was always going to. I was going to pinver over you and hope that one day you would let me into your upper crust. But you never would. I would die alone and lonely, having spent an empty life trying to become one of you, never being more than the one that you step on to get a smile. That is not the life I want to lead. I saw the future, and I'm making a change. New destiny... I hate you for making me feel less of myself than I should have all these years. So I'm going to make you love me. Is it a contradiction? Yes. But what is life but one giant contradiction. What is the world you have created, but the saying of one thing and the doing of another. I've lived your lie long enough. Now the truth has set me free. And for you... the truth will most definitely HURT.
