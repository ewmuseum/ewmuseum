Chapter IX: The Landing In A New Country

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-3-03 ::Introduction:: Mark has not been wrestling in PCW lately since he hasn’t been booked in any cards. He has juts been sitting his lazy ass down since he doesn’t know where his life is taking him and where his wrestling career is going. Both are at a standstill as we speak and he needs something to spark his life. Now Mark is reliving his past from when he did get fired from PCW and what he did after that to change his life around. We continue that journey into the life of Mark Makonie. ::End Introduction:: Chapter IX: That Landing In A New Country… As Mark is sitting his the rattling three-seater plane he looks out at the countryside beneath him. He then looks at the pilot who looks as if he knows what he is doing and is happy with his job. Mark gets disgusted with the guy being so happy that all he can do is close his eyes, but all he can see is that damn happy face. “What in the hell are you so happy about?” Mark asks as he opens his eyes up. “Did you get laid last night by some old hag or something?” “Well actually I did get laid.” The guy starts to say being all happy. “By my own sister!” Mark gets out the puke bag and he blows it up with air and just lets lose with his dinner that he had at the old town he was planning to take over. He keeps on blowing chunks into the bag and the pilot is starting to hold his nose shut from the stench of the puke smell. Mark lifts his head up as his face is all pale from puking and he feels it and his forehead is all cold and shit and he starts to shake. “Dude that is some fucked up shit.” Mark says as he puts the smelling puke bag down on the floor. “That is incest and only if you are from Kentucky is that legal and I am sure you are not from around there because you sure don’t act or dress like they do.” “Well I was born in Cali and I moved out here to the Carolinas when I got out of college now I own that airport.” The guy starts to say as Mark is blowing off all the shit he is saying. “My mom and dad were close second cousins and I feel that it is right to do sexual actions with your family. Hell even my mom, my dad, and I have all had a threesome together. It was the best sexual experience I have ever been through.” Mark starts to just gag once again as he reaches for the puke bag. He opens it up and the stench that comes from it is just horrible. It fills the whole plane up with the smell as the pilot has to open up a little window to get some air flowing through the whole plane. Mark just dry heaves though in the bag since there is nothing left in his stomach to puke out except for bile. Mark puts the puke bag down under the seat once again as the pilot is looking all happy even from the smell he still looks happy. Mark looks at the man and realizes his minority. He is a spic as they call a damn fucking spic. The guy has a peach fuzz mustache and his hair is all colored orange and black and is spiked up. He is wearing a shirt that reads in Spanish, but Mark cannot read what it says. “Is this how every spic is like?” Mark asks as he looks at the man who takes his eyes off the course. “What did you just say?” The guy asks as he is starting to land the plane at the airport down below. “I asked you if every fucking spic believed in incest and was this gross and that they are just this fucked up.” Mark says as he is starting to turn back his normal skin color and is getting all hot. “I am a racist motherfucker and the African American community I have already taken over and now its my time to take over your damn community you stinky as bitch.” “Never talk about my country like that again to my face!” The guy screams as he is coming down in the plane to the runway at normal speed. “Fucking spic!” Mark says as the plane lands down on the runway with power. The pilot looks a little pissed off and doesn’t look happy with what Mark has said to him. Mark has a smile on his face now and it looks as if the expressions have changed on the face of both of these two guys. The pilot pulls up to his parking spot since they landed at a little airport, nothing fancy. “Get the fuck out of my plane!” The guy says as he seems mad and angry with Mark. “Welcome to Mexico!” “Thanks sir and you will be hearing from me soon enough on the news and all across the nation.” Mark says as he grabs his black duffel bag and he opens up the door to get out of the plane. The hot air of Mexico blows in Mark’s face as he opens up the door and then he hangs his feet over the edge. He gets down from the plane as he hits his legs on the ground and his right one is still injured badly. The bone is starting to stick out some from the skin as Mark is not hurting but needs to get to a doctor soon. “Hello Mexico.” Mark says to himself as he sniffs the warm fresh air of this new country. “My new home for a little while as I figure some life issues out here and plus to torment all the people I can and I am going to love it.” Mark starts to head to the lobby area of the small airport as he is surrounded by jungle and all the jungle animals. But also he is surrounded by more culture than ever. Many Indian civilizations have created this country and have brought their lifestyles to this right here. Mark can’t wait to explore what is new here and plus he needs to get his point across now about the spics and how they treat the white people and how they take all their jobs.
