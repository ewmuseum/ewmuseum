Operatation 'Get Duke Out Of Scientology' Begins (Feat. Kelly Preston)

5 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/05/02 Some say he has been doing everything he can to avoid it. Most say it is the thing he has feared ever since Duke Williams finally chose to make a stand against the Ultimate Authority and the Commissioner in particular. Despite Xavier’s best efforts, even making himself the referee to virtually insure that Frisco won the match....But things didn’t go his way. After a series of slow counts and blind eyes being turned, Xavier’s plan backfired and he caught a Steel Chair across the skull for his troubles that left him a crumpled heap on the mat....A perfect position for Duke do use the Commissioner’s head to count 3 and mean that the Malice Man will square off against the Gold Patron Meritorious at Supremacy in 8 days time. So now it is staring Kannon in the face, he no longer MAY face Duke...He WILL face Duke and his beloved Continental Title will be on the line as Commissioner faces President...And given the way Duke send Kannon Whiskey Bent and Hellbound at the end of the show, things don’t look to promising for the Gold Patron Meritorious. “DAMMIT! DAMMIT! DAMMIT! SOMEONE GET ME A MEDICAL NOTE OR SOMETHING...GET ME THE HELL OUT OF THAT MATCH AT SUPREMACY! DUKE DOESN’T DESERVE TO FIGHT ME!!!” Our shot opens up on Xavier Kannon, lying on his hotel bed the morning after Fusion as the events of Fusion flood back to him after an evening spent groggy from being knocked unconscious. He is wearing only his wrestling attire, a pair of worker-cut Black jeans with Purple and Green embroidery snaking down the left leg to his bare feet. Around his head he has a bandage that helps tie his long Auburn hair back, wrapped around his head after the Chair Shot he received. His face is etched in pain yet he continues to rant, screwing up his face when his voice gets to loud, the volume not doing much for his headache. Xavier : “There HAS to be rules about supposed Scientologists hitting other Scientologists....WE ARE A CIVILISED RELIGION! We do not turn on each other like the Christians! How DARE Duke attack me WHEN I WAS UNCONSCIOUS! I am a GOLD PATRON MERITORIOUS! He is NOTHING!!! He should be thrown out of Scientology for what he did to m-....” Suddenly his pained face relaxes and a sparkle comes to Kannon’s eyes. A grin starts to cross his face as it does whenever he has one of his ideas....Call it satisfaction. Xavier : “...Wait.....I WILL get him thrown out! Who is he? He is a nobody...I am SCIENTOLOGY’S GREATEST WRESTLER...Time to start Operation Get Duke The Hell Out Of Scientology!” “Out........Of........Scientology!” From the bathroom comes Eleanor, still looking her bright and breezy self despite the events the night before that ended up with Duke standing above the unconscious Scientologist as Fusion went off the air. Eleanor : “Then I can, like, take his spot....As long as I don’t have to actually do..Um..Er...Anything and stuff...Because let’s face it....Minority Religions are, like, SO late-90’s!” Lying flat on the bed the Scientologist rolls his eyes back into his head and sighs at his preppy valet. Eleanor, after escaping Fusion unscathed is wearing a White silk peasant top with a pair of dark Blue jeans, accessorised by a rainbow fabric belt. He long, wavy brown hair is pulled back and is under a linen head scarf. Xavier : “No my little Poon Puppy....You can’t. After I succeed in having Duke removed from Scientology the admissions system is going to change....I mean, HOW IN HUBBARD’S NAME DID DUKE WILLIAMS GET IN?!?!? Scientology is quickly becoming PCW during the Duke era! Hey, have a pulse? Okay, you can join! AND I WILL NOT HAV-” *KNOCK* *KNOCK* *KNOCK* He is cut off as there is a knock at the door. The prone Scientologist then looks first to the door then to Ellie~! who is just standing by it with her hands on her hips, almost ignoring it. Eleanor : “Like.....What?” He motions with his Green eyes to the door and his valets sighs heavily stomping her feet the few paces to the door and then abruptly pulls it open to show none other than Kelly Preston....Not that Eleanor can remember as she puts her hands on her hips again and tilts her head, not looking very forthcoming. Eleanor : “Um.......Like, YES?” Kelly, having spoken to Ellie~! before, many times, seems taken aback by the hostile reception she is getting from the valet who’s annoyed she had to walk the 4 steps to answer the door. Kelly : “Um...Eleanor? It’s me....Kelly Preston.” Miss Preston, one of the select celebrities to have a street named after her in Hubbardville gestures to herself, but the look of apathy and mild disdain does not go from Eleanor’s face. Eleanor : “Like.....Who?” Kelly : “Kelly Preston? I was in Twins....And Battlefield Earth...An-” Eleanor : “You mean that film that sucked?” As the valet makes her comment Kelly’s face squints, not at all happy with the preps critique of Scientology’s most successful foray into Hollywood. But, she shrugs the comment off and continues. Kelly : “I have been in 2 episodes of Chips.....I’m married to John Travolta....I live 2 doors down from you in Hubbardville!” Suddenly Ellie~!’s pale Blue eyes show signs of recognition an she opens the door more and appears less defensive, almost reaching a smile. Eleanor : “Ohh, you have the house with the Green door...Wait, if you are married to John Travolta how come your surname is Preston?” Kelly goes to answer but Kannon’s voice is heard shouting from the bed. “Ellie~! Who is it?” His valet turns and looks to the bed where the Scientologist is still laying on his back, cringing as he rolls slightly to one side to get a better look at the door. She then steps to the side of the door and gestures to Kelly standing there. Xavier : “Kelly, hey, good to see you....Come in, please...” Miss Preston smiles and flashes it at Eleanor before walking into the 1st couple of Scientology’s hotel room. As she heads toward the bed Ellie~! closes the door behind her and follows, mimicking Xavier under her breath. Eleanor : “Hey...Come in...Please....I insist....” Kelly turns around to Eleanor and looks at her with peculiarity, making the valet suddenly stop talking and looking her dumb innocent self even better than when she does it naturally. Kelly : “Hey, me and John were in the area and we saw what happened....He really socked it you huh?” Before Kannon can reply from the bed, Eleanor who is in the process of walking past Kelly and around to the other side weighs in with her opinion. Eleanor : “Yeah....Duke, like....SO kicked his ass!” The face of the Scientologist contorts with mild anger at the reminder of the fact he was knocked unconscious by a chair then send Whiskey Bent and Hellbound by Duke Williams. Ellie~!, as usual, acts as if she has said nothing out of the ordinary as she walks around the bed. Kannon then sighs and turns to Kelly, taking a break from the tiring force that is Eleanor Hall. Xavier : “Yes.....Duke did ATTACK ME WHILE I WAS DOWN! But I have a plan to get back at him...He WILL be kicked out of Scientology...All I need is to find out a way for the rest of them to see what kind of a low life he REALLY is...” As he says this he suddenly has that flash in his eyes we have grown used to as he looks Kelly up and down, almost un-nerving her with his thoughtful gaze. Xavier : “I HAVE IT!!!” Over in the corner Ellie~! is now pouring herself a bottle of Snapple~! Apple into a glass and then she holds the bottle up to the camera in a crude bit of product placement. Then seemingly time freezes as Kelly and Xavier suddenly stop moving and only Ellie~! is animated. Eleanor : “That’s right Snapple~! fans, the official Soft Drink of PCW is up for renewal so it’s time for the shilling....Mmmmm, Snapple~!...” She takes a sip and winks to the camera before Kelly and Kannon come back to life, and Eleanor looks over to them and continues from where time froze. Eleanor : “I.........Have........It! Er, wait......You.........Have.......It! There, that’s better...YAY ME!” Xavier ignores her much to Ellie~!’s annoyance and she once again mutters under her breath out of hearing range, jealous at the attention Xavier is giving Kelly. Eleanor : “Why don’t you, like, just do her Malice Style and like, SO be done with it!” Not hearing Ellie~!, Kannon continues on. Xavier : “If you went to Duke’s hotel room with a camera hidden in your bag, get him to ply you with a few drinks then say you want it Malice Style...When he obliges and you catch the entire deed on tape they’ll see exactly what kind of sick old man he is!” At hearing this Ellie~! speaks softly in a whiny voice, stamping on the spot. Eleanor : “That was, like, SO my idea...” Kelly appears to be confused by the Scientologist’s suggestion. Kelly : What is that?” The Gold Patron Meritorious seems stuck as to how to answer the question, struggling for the right words to use. However, before he has the chance, Ellie~!, drinking her Snapple~! while walking back towards the bed where Kannon is still lying flat, butts in. Eleanor : “He....Like, you know? Stick it in your ass...” Travolta’s wife’s eyes widen at the thought of this and her face screws up in disgust at the prospect. Kelly : “WHAT!?!?!? I COME HERE TO SEE WHO YOU ARE AND YOU ASK ME TO DO THAT...I...I...I’M GOING!” With this the wife of John Travolta turns on her heel and heads out of the room, slamming the door on her way out.... *SLAM* .....Sending vibrations that shudder Kannon’s back causing him to wince as the shot starts to fade out to Black. Eleanor : “Wow...I’m, like, SO surprised she didn’t go for that....That was sarcasm by the way!” We zoom in on Kannon’s face, contorted by rage as he wonders how on Hubbard’s earth he will get Duke out of Scientology, and we finally hit Black. “DAMMIT!!! I’LL GET YOU OUT OF MY RELIGION DUKE!!!! I WILL!!!” The camera then fades back in for a few seconds to show Ellie~! holding up her glass. Eleanor : “Oh, and like....SO remember to drink Snapple~!“. With this she takes a sip and winks again as we fade out for the last time.
