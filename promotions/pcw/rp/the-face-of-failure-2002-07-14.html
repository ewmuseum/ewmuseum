The Face...of Failure?

14 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/14/02 Well, it’s surely been an interesting ride for Alexis Parshin in the past few weeks. He won the tag team titles after a tough battle, and now he loses to a pathetic Japanese man who is dilliusional. After that, his only ally, other than the frequently incompent Raghab, Chef, turned his back on him, leaving him to fend for himself. Perhaps this mission was not as easy as he had earlier thought… “Raghab…bring me my boots…NOW!” Parshin’s booming voice doesn’t have the slightest tone of happiness in it, and for good reason. As it is known, Konstantin Aliyev Alexei Parshin is not a fan of losing, and in fact it is one of his most hated things in the world. Unfortunately, his first loss in PCW had to come. Parshin is wearing no covering over his enormous upper body, but is standing in a gym of some sort. As Raghab waddles onto the screen, we can see that he is wearing a sweat suit with Arabic characters adorning it. Of course, it barely fits his pudgy frame, but he struts/waddles around as if he were some sort of male model in it. “Raghab…quit your insolence. This is no time for any happiness. I have failed my home…I have failed Allah himself.” Alexis shakes his head indignantly, seemingly disgusted with himself as he sits at a dusty bench press machine. “Konstantin, you haven’t failed.” This causes Alexis to bring his head up, looking curiously at Raghab. “How have I not failed? I have lost the titles that I brought glory to so soon and that dillusional Jap! Then, an imposter infidel betrays me and makes me the face of failure in PCW! Raghab, how in the Iblis does that make me not a failure?!” It’s shocking to see Parshin so upset, the huge man seems possibly near tears. “Konstantin, you are too hard on yourself. You have been here for less than a month and you expect yourself to conquer the land where infidels rule? That is too much even for a man like yourself.” Alexis nods at this, and Raghab’s fat jiggles as he moves and talks at the same time. Raghab now stands in front of Alexis, speaking like some sort of army general to his troops. “Konstantin, I have never known a man with as much of Allah’s own blood in him.” As he says this, Parshin nods and grins slightly. “You have persevered through civil war and seen things that have made other grown men weep on the battlefield. You are the bravest man I know.” These words bring Parshin’s head up a bit more…though he still has a look of glumness on his face. “Raghab…perhaps you are right. We have just arrived and time is on our side. The longer we keep the morons waiting…the longer we have to strike.” This finally brings a full evil grin to the face of Alexis Parshin, and Raghab’s as well. “I now have things to take care of, go continue to work out.” Raghab’s face twists in dissapointment, but waddles back over to the machines, breaking a sweat just walking over. Alexis looks up at the sides of the walls around him, they say things in Arabic, most concerning Jihad and war against America. “Kareem…you broke the first rule of Jihad. You…NEVER…let your opponents know your weakness. I know your weaknesses, and I will attack them, one by one, breaking you down.” The Uzbek Annihilator cracks his knuckles as he whispers these words, speaking towards Mecca. “If you wish to be a cook of fun and games, Kareem, then we will play fun and games. I will fry you alive, and you will wish that you had never crossed Allah and his personal soldier. It’s time for Jihad against you, Kareem…”
