AIRPORT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!

30 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/30/2002 ~Do you remember when today's big names were just rookies? Do you remember when Xavier Kannon was some no name with a few wins who suddenly began to feud with Jeremy Howard? Do you remember when Clyde was just some burnt freak who no one knew and no one wanted to know? Do you remember when some tall, muscular man named Jon Kano took the President of PCW at the time, Professor Herbert Dorkiman, and threw him in a trunk of a Lincoln Towncar? Most of you probably don't. There are a few select people in the world who can remember all of that because two of the three aforementioned examples happened in the very infancy of PCW, just a few months after the initial show. Jon Kano has been a part of this juggernaut that we all love known as Primetime Championship Wrestling since October of 2000. He was the first truly great big man in PCW. Jon Kano wrote the book on how to live the PCW lifestyle. And he is still in PCW, rocking as hard as when he first got here. He is constantly defending PCW now. He has changed his tactics since his birth as the real legend, though. He used to listen to fans, he drew his power from his fans, now he has reverted back to the tactics that got him to PCW and to the titles he has won here in the first place.~ *The scene opens up in the Rome International Airport. This airport is huge, even by American standards. In the main terminal of the airport, Jon Kano, dressed in a red Armani suit with a black silk tie is seen walking briskly, obviously trying to catch a plane to get to the next stop on the PCW tour, Zurich, Switzerland.* Kano: Son of a bitch, I hate Rome. I love the pasta and everything, but there is entirely way too many people here, plus in the center of this city, is the center of the most corrupt institution in human history. Yes, that is right, I am talking about the Catholic Church, but to each his own, that is what I say. *Suddenly a bald man wearing a orange-red robe comes up to Jon Kano.* Man: Have you found Buddha? Kano: Shit. *Kano grabs the man by the robe and more or less reverse monkey flips him over and continues his trek to his gate.* Kano: God damn Buddhists. *Suddenly a man with long, flowing brown hair approaches Kano.* Man: Buy a flower. *Kano grabs the man and snap suplexes him as quick as greased lightning to the hard polished brick of the floor of the Rome International Airport.* Kano: I hate hippies. *Now a Jewish man, wearing the traditional hat and curls, comes to Kano.* Jewish man: Wait for the savior son. *Kano grabs the Jewish man by the curls on the side of his head and double handed curl (choke) slams him to the floor.* Kano: God hates me, that is all there is to it. *A man wearing a suit approaches Kano now.* Man: Oh no, son. God doesn't hate you, God loves you. Come talk to me and my friends at the local chapter of the Johovah's Witness and see the plague human race due to the temptations of the devil. *Kano simply punches this guy in the mouth as hard as he possible can, probably breaking the man's jaw, but the man does drop like a fly to the floor.* Kano: I happen to enjoy some of the devil's temptations. *Kano begins to walk very briskly to catch his flight once again.* Kano: You see all of those religious zealots who just approached me and spoke their venom of their particular religions had one thing in common. All of them had no respect. Here I am a man who is obviously in a hurry, I don't walk like this because it makes my ass feel tingly. They all took it upon themselves to interrupt me and ask me to join their cult. They remind me of my two opponents this Friday. Or is it the other way around? Either way this new wave of newbies are supposed to be the superstars of tommorrow? My ass. I watched these two wrestle, and I am not impressed. They might have some ability, but they lack in charisma or they can't live the PCW lifestyle. And the one thing that sticks in my craw, the one thing that really pisses me off, is their lack of respect for myself or for PCW. Just like those religious zealots that I just took out. The result will be the same. And that is what REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF!!!!!! Voice over PA: Attencion!!! Attencion!!! Flight 918 on Italian Air into Zurich has been delayed. *Kano reaches into his inside breast pocket and pulls out his ticket. He looks at it, folds it back up, and puts it back into his pocket. Now he really looks pissed off. The scene fades to the outside of the Rome International Airport.* Kano (distant, but clear): FFFFFFFFFFUUUUUUUUUUUUCCCCCCCCCCCCCCKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKK!!!!!!!!! *The scene fades to black as the pigeons fly away as the sound of Kano's voice scares them away.* ~Respect for PCW, at any price.*
