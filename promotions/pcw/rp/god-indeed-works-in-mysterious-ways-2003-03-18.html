God indeed works in mysterious ways.

18 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-18-03 The walls, painted an eggshell white reflect only the neon green light projected by an alarm clock. Much of the rest of the room is left a mystery due to the lack of light spent to describe it. Two forms lay beneath the blanketed surface of a king sized bed, just the outline of two people, sleeping. Suddenly the figure on the left begins thrashing about loudly. The small amount of light shows a tidbit of legs kicking out, arms swinging around, and suddenly a indeterminate voice can be heard. “I just want to go home….. Please let me go home……” The rooms suddenly fills with light as Amira springs up out of bed trying to gather herself after being awoken in a panic by A.J. laying next to her. Placing her hands upon the figure under the comforter she begins shaking wildly. “A.J.! Wake up!” Amira pleads. “You’re having a nightmare!” Sitting straight up, A.J. Trinidad breathes as if though he’s just finished sprinting a marathon. Beads of sweat roll of his brow and encompass his entire face at this point as he grasps the bed with both hands looking around until he realizes that he is indeed in his own bedroom. “My god Amira.” A.J. says through heavy breathing. “I just had the world’s worst nightmare.” “It’s all right now baby, you’re up,” she consoles, “everything is fine.” “This dream though, it was amazingly real.” A.J. says as he slowly begins to catch his breath and takes a drink from a glass of water sitting on an end table next to the bed. “You were there, so was Crespo, and Space Ghost.” “Space Ghost?” asks Amira trying not to laugh. “Yeah, it was the strangest thing. We were all trapped in a snow globe style container, but it wasn’t a snow globe, it was a bong.” A.J. continues. “A bong?” Amira questions again. “Yeah,” A.J. pauses. “There was a tornado, our house was lifted off the ground, and when we landed, we were surrounded by little people.” “You mean midgets?” She wonders aloud. “Yeah, except these weren’t any ordinary midgets, they were all from the “Little Peoples Acting Union”. You know, those little people on television?” “Yeah, I know,” Amira responds while taking a drink of her own glass of water. “We were trapped, and they said the only way I could get home for Turmoil was to travel along the red hair skunk road. Everyone was high as a kite! They were all giggling and eating leftover Domino’s pizza. We had no choice, so Crespo, Space Ghost, you and I started off down the pot laden road towards what we could only guess was our only way home.” “A.J., this is starting to sound a lot like the Wizard of Oz.” Amira states plainly while fluffing her pillow before laying her head back down. “The Pink Floyd movie?” A.J. asks “What the hell are you talking about?” Amira questions back wondering if A.J. has even truly woken up fully. “Never mind,” A.J. rebuttals, “And of all the people to run into, Jerry Garcia shows up out of the poppy fields smoking a joint. He tells us that we need to journey across the land to the Great Bong Palace in the sky, but to be wary of the Wicked Stoner with a multi personality disorder who also thinks he’s a gangster. Then, he simply blows out a cloud of smoke until we can no longer see him, and he disappears.” “This sounds a little disturbing A.J.” Amira admits. “That isn’t even the most bizarre part yet,” A.J. dredges on with his story. “In the horizon, we see a glass grafix palace, built by the great king of Rolladoobie, that is the name of the land we were in. So we walk for what seems like days, and every day, everything in the land stops at exactly 4:20 in the a.m. as well as in the p.m., and smoke fills the air, getting everyone high as hell! After what I think was our third day of walking we had appeared to get nowhere. It seemed that as everyday 4:20 hit, we all passed out and ended right back where we began. But anyway, I digress, it was the third day, when out of nowhere we hear a bellowing voice echo throughout the land.” “Fee, Fi, Fo, Fum” It went, “ I’m crazy jones’n for weed and gonna get me some!” “A.J., I’m really tired now, can this story wait until the morning?” she begs as she pulls the comforter back up over her body. “Amira, this dream may hold the secrets to winning my match on Tuesday! I must tell my story!” A.J. demands pulling the pillow out from underneath her head. “Well, if you must, you must,” Amira pouts as she sits up in bed. “Anyway, where was I?” A.J. asks. “The part where the voice is looking for weed” she responds, none to excited to be up at this hour. “Right, continuing,” He starts up again. “From over the trees, a long haired, smelly, hippy with a pot leaf on his shirt appears standing about 4 stories tall! We all dart for cover, but it was too late, he had spotted us all and before we knew it, he had snatched up Space Ghost, rolled him up in the biggest rolling paper I’ve ever seen, and began smoking him.” “This grass tastes like shit, said the giant pothead” “Space Ghost had died” A.J. states with a hint of sorrow in his voice. “We all ran, as the stoner chased us, this time actually moving closer to the castle, but the Stoner was gaining on us too quickly, so we darted into the woods, which were actually super sized marijuana plants. Low and behold we had managed to stumble upon the Giant’s cabin. We run inside, and begin looking for places to hide. You hid in the giant’s hitter box, I was attempting to get into the Stoner’s laundry basket, but it reeked to high heaven! Stoners should really wash up a little more often, cuz this guy Stu-unk! Anyway, I couldn’t bear the smell, so I darted out, but this happened just as the Hippie busted through the door. He snatched me up, pulled out his rolling papers, and began to lick the end with the sticky substance on it.” “How did you manage to get away?” Amira questioned trying to hurry the story along. “I’m getting to that.” A.J. shot back, not wanting to be hurried. “Just as he began wrapping me up, Crespo appeared on the Hippie’s table, standing next to a large glass bong. The hippie spotted Crespo and an immediate look of worry came to his face. Noting that the Bong held a special significance to the hippie, Crespo gave it a running jump and crashed into the bong sending it towards the wooden floor. The Hippie immediately dropped me and ran for the bong but it was too late, glass had shattered everywhere. As the Hippie knelt crying over his broken water bong, I grabbed a hose, turned it on and showered him down. The Hippie immediately started to shrink in size, continuing on until he vanished into a big pile of clothes.” “Most interesting” Amira says. “Well, even in my dreams I thought to myself. What is the one thing Hippies fear most?” A.J. asks of Amira. “Getting their bong broken” She replies. “No, absolutely not!” A.J. answers. “A good shower. Hippies hate to take showers. The water I sprayed on him was his ultimate undoing! Isn’t that great!” “So what happened next” Amira questions now getting into the story. “Everything in the land turned back the way it was. The trees went back to being trees, the little people got to eat something other than pizza for a change, the stench of dirty hippie had totally left the air! We were heroes” “And then?” Amira asks again. “What?” A.J. responds. “Oh, nothing, you woke me up” “That’s it, we never got out of the Bong?” She questions again. “I figure we must have somehow,” A.J. answers, “we probably just woke up and were back home, kind of like what happened in real life.” “Well that ending sucks! I’m going back to bed” Amira says as she rolls over, pulling the blanket up over her head. “Shower the Stoner” A.J. mumbles to himself as he drifts back to sleep. “It’s gotta work.” “God does indeed work in mysterious ways.”
