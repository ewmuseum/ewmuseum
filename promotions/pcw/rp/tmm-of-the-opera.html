Tmm of the Opera

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Done while dead tired, deal with it. “Get back here!!!” (The angry shout of Keira Marshall reverberates up and down one of the hallways in THE Multiple Man’s secret lair to no avail.) Keira: “Morons, I’m surrounded by morons!” (She stalks along, vainly searching. The passageway leads to the main living chamber. Steve and HV are there, watching ‘Beastmaster’ on TV.) Keira: (demanding) “Did the boss just come through here? He’s gotta sign this week’s paycheck!” Steve: “Top hat? Cape? Domino mask?” Keira: “Where?” (HV points over towards the kitchen. Keira nods sharply in thanks and heads in the indicated direction.) Tmm: “Hisss!” (A brief blur as he jerks the opera cloak up to cover his face before it can be seen. A quick exit stage left.) Keira: (growling) “Dammit!” --- Hours later --- (Keira, still chasing after Tmm, is now an interesting shade of purplish-red and just this close to exploding messily with frustration.) Keira: “I’M GONNA KILL HIM!!!” Val: (reasonably) “This place isn’t that large that he can evade you forever.” Keira: (seething) “You would think so, but that rat has more holes built into this place than your average chunk of Swiss cheese.” Val: “Have you checked the poolroom?” Keira: “That’d be crazy, Raul’s whole family is still living there.” (She shivers.) Keira: “They give me the creeps with their beady red eyes glinting out from the darkness… that and I’d welded the door shut months ago.” Val: “How about the roof?” Keira: “Checked and locked down.” Val: “Well, what about…” (She pauses.) Keira: ”We usually like to finish our sentences when speaking. Care to try again?” (Val waves her hand in irritation.) Val: “Shhh! I hear something.” (Keira tilts her head, ears straining.) Keira: ”I don’t hear anything.” Val: “It’s coming from downstairs… the conference room?” (Keira is in motion, already half out the door. Presently, they arrive at the outer door to the conference room. Haunting music echoes from within…) Keira: “Looks like somebody found the demo button on the Casio.” Val: “What is that, a rumba?” Keira: “My musical skill both begins and ends with the ‘on’ switch of my CD player, how should I know?” (WHOOM! She kicks open the door with a dull booming impact and steps inside. There, playing the small keyboard with exaggerated hand, arm and body motions is THE Multiple Man, still in the hat and opera cloak.) Keira: ” Hold it right there, buster!” (Tmm’s hands sweep down and sound a harsh discordant note.) Tmm: “So… you’ve found me at last.” (Another note, this one rather like the ones used for dramatic reveals in soap operas.) Keira: “Just sign the check! I gotta car payment due today!” (Another note, harsher than before!) Tmm: “I am beyond such things now! They have stolen my face from me!” (He half turns, eyes narrowing behind the white half mask.) Tmm: “I can never go back to the man I was!” (Dah-Daaaaa!) Val: “How do you get the mask to stay on like that?” Tmm: “Oh, I’ve got a whole buncha Oil of Olay underneath this thing. Stuff sticks better than glue… and it moisturizes too!” Val: “Doesn’t it sting?” Tmm: “That’s the beauty of a mask. Get horribly burned, put on the mask, leave it there… and poof! Rejuvenated face.” Val: “That works on third degree burns? Toast burnt to a cinder comes to mind.” Tmm: “Hence the Oil of Olay, I’ve got it all figured out.” Val: “Good deal.” Tmm: “It’s an alternative medicine I read about in Time Magazine. HISSS!!!” (Keira, sneaking up as stealthily as she can, has crept too close! Tmm jerks the opera cloak up over his face and dashes through the second door leading from the chamber! Keira follows in hot pursuit!) Keira: “SIGN THE DAMN CHECK, YOU DIMWIT!!!” (The camera fades out.)
