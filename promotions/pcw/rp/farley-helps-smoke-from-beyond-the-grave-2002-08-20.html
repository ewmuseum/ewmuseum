Farley helps Smoke from beyond the grave!?

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 20, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up with Semore Smoke sitting up on his sofa, which is seated in the middle of his vast living room. Across from the sofa is a huge set-up that he bought with his first PCW paycheck. The entertainment system consist of the latest video game systems and video games, such as a Playstation 2 and the latest World Wrestling Entertainment video game, which he go off the streets as a bootleg copy. It also consist of a twelve CD changer/burner, hooked to it are four 36x24 inch speakers with extra bass boost attachments hooked to it. In the middle of the system set-up is a TV with a DVD player and a VCR on top of it. The TV is a big screen TV made to resemble that of a Theater screen, but about a fourth of the size, yet that is still pretty big. On each side of the TV, underneath the PS2 on one side and the system on the other is a line up of DVD’s and CD’s that are all out of order, but Semore doesn’t care, he’s barely home to listen to them anyway. Off a pretty confusing night on Tuesday Turmoil Semore is just sitting contemplating how he’ll pull off the show that he has the next night for the Comedy Central Amateur Stand-Up comedian version of Premium Blend. Smoke looks over to his left at a clock sitting on a small table with a lamp seated on top of it, and sees that it’s currently eleven thirty at night. Smoke hadn’t realized that it was that late. Maybe if he lies down he’ll figure something out that will make the fans laugh. It’s hard coming off as a heel or bad guy in the wrestling world and then become a funny guy who everyone loves in the comedy world. Smoke takes off his white weight lifting T-shirt and then lies down in his long baggy black jeans. With his legs hanging over one side of the sofa and his head resting firmly on a small pillow on the other side. Smoke starts to think about his life and how comedy might effect, with these thoughts in his mind he slowly drifts off to sleep… {{{{A wavy sequence takes place as the dream begins. It shows a scene in what appears to be a hotel hallway. Semore is standing in the middle of the hallway looking on as several police officers file in and out of one room. There is police tape everywhere to mark off a crime scene or maybe just to keep people out. A woman is dressed as a hooker is talking to a police officer in a far corner of the taped off area and the officer is taking down notes of everything that she says. Smoke can’t hear what she’s talking about, but he’s just intrigued about the whole situation. He starts to walk forward and lifts the police tape over his head as he steps through into the crime scene. He turns and looks inside the hotel room and literally just stands there in awe as he sees the scene inside. Several police officers are standing around one body, which is right in front of the door. The man lying there is of a pudgy to fat build, he has blonde hair, which is semi-long and he’s lying face down in his underwear. Semore Smoke recognizes the man, even if he is face down, the man is none other then Chris Farley! Semore Smoke is dreaming about the death of Chris Farley and all the information about how he was found, and even the hooker that he was paying for “companionship” that night. All of a sudden, Semore finds himself walking into the room, which reeks of the smell of reefer and you can see Cocaine and other hard drugs strewn throughout the hotel room floor. As soon as Smoke walks in, an officer seems to walk right through him, as if he weren’t there. Smoke doesn’t pay attention though it, because he can’t take his eyes off of Farley, why was he dreaming this? All of a sudden, to Smoke’s surprise, Farley’s body moves, no, a cop wasn’t turning him over, nor were the EMT’s there to take him away, but the body was getting up on it’s on. But no wait! It wasn’t the body it’s his soul! Foley seems to climb out of his own body, but this person or thing isn’t human, it’s pale and you can seemingly see through it. As soon as the soul floats up onto it’s feet, or maybe it’s floating, Smoke is just too mesmerized to tell. It looks at Semore and smiles a famous Chris Farley smile and says…}}}} Chris Farley: Hello Semore. {{{{Smoke can’t believe it! Chris Farley knows his name… but then he realizes that this is his dream, and that there must be a meaning for this, so Smoke answers up…}}}} Semore Smoke: Hello Chris. Why am I here? {{{{Chris continues to smile as he walks around his body and puts an arm around Semore in a friendly kind of way and they both walk deeper into the room, towards the bedroom part where there are two queen sized beds. In the middle of the beds is a table that has several drugs across it, and a couple cans of Coke along with a few bottles of scotch. Semore and Chris both sit down on the first bed they get to, but on the side closer to the table. Chris sits closer to the table and he grabs two joints from the table and puts one in his mouth and lights it, and hands the other to Smoke as well as the lighter. Smoke doesn’t question him and he puts the joint in his mouth and lights it up. Smoke drags on it and he can tell that it must be laced with Acid or something immediately but this is a dream and nothing can happen to him, right? Chris turns to Smoke and pats him on the back…}}}} Chris Farley: I hear that you want to become a stand-up comedian, is that right? {{{{Semore drags on the joint and he can feel the weed starting to go to his head as he answers the question…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yes it is. But why am I here? Chris Farley: Well, I’ve got to be the most famous drug addict/stand-up comedian in all time, so of course I can give you a few pointers on how to be funny. That’s why you’re here talking to me. Semore Smoke: Wow! Advice from the great Chris Farley! Chris Farley: Yep. First things first, always pick on yourself in someway, I did that a lot and that’s what got me on Saturday Night Live for a couple of seasons. Of course my drug problem hurt my career up there, but it also helped my career at some times. Always do something original is another option. You remember the Matt Foley skits on SNL? {{{{Smoke drags on the joint and chuckles as he answers the question…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yeah, that’s got to be the funniest thing ever on SNL. The whole, “You’ll end up living in a van down by the river!” It’s hilarious! {{{{Chris seems to frown at it though when Smoke laughs and this upsets Smoke a little…}}}} Semore Smoke: What’s wrong big guy? Chris Farley: I never liked the sketch. Yeah it made me a famous person on the show, but, it picked on my weight problem. I always had to pull my pants up in the sketch and I was nothing but a sweaty fat guy with a catchphrase, that’s it. Semore Smoke: Oh so you were Duke Williams? {{{{This gets a chuckle out of Chris…}}}} Chris Farley: You see, you’re already funny, you just need material. If you want my advice, I’d say take a page out of Dennis Leary’s book and use your drug use in your shows. Yeah, you can’t smoke weed on stage, but you can smoke cigarettes and drink beer or whatever if you want. Dennis got a lot of laughs from one show when he talked about how much he loved both things. You have to do something like that. Maybe have a can of Coke up there with you and do this. {{{{Chris grabs a can of Coke from the table, but the can is empty. He sticks it up to his nose and acts as if he's sniffing at it he then pulls it down and sees Smoke laughing as Smoke catches the joke side to it…}}}} Chris Farley: See, I was acting like I was sniffing Coke, get it! Ha! That’s always a killer, and I bet you could pull something off with it Wednesday night if need be. When you pick on yourself, maybe add some of the wrestling stuff into it. Make it common knowledge that you’re using probably the gayest song in the world, “Why can’t we be friends” by War as your theme song for the Pay Per View match. By the way Semore, do you think that you’re going to win the Rising Star Championship at Third Dawning? {{{{Smoke drags on the joint and feels the lasting effects of the laced weed…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yes, I believe I can. Eddie Mandrake might have had an impact when he debuted by attacking Scorpion on a Tuesday Turmoil, but that just means that they’ll both be busy with each other, and Blayze? He’s a joke! I mean, I could probably pick him apart in one of my stand-up acts. Chris Farley: Why not do those? That would be great comedy, you picking apart your opponents for Sunday is hilarious! Semore Smoke: You know what, you’re right, in fact I could pick several people from the PCW roster to pick on, of course Mandrake, Scorpion, and Blayze will be three of my victims… but who else? {{{{Farley smiles and then drags on the joint again and seems to roll his eyes into the back of his head as he lets the effects settle in. He then rolls them back forward and looks Smoke straight into the face with a grin from ear to ear…}}}} Chris Farley: You could always pick on Jon Kano and Corey Williams, sure Corey might be like your pal right now, but hey, it’s comedy! Am I right? {{{{Smoke listens to the idea and speaks up with an enlightened voice…}}}} Semore Smoke: Yeah you’re right! I could also pick on Violence Jack and Jaden, in fact! Anyone I’ve faced in the past I could pick on… I might even pick on Xavier Kannon as well! Chris Farley: Yeah, we all know how he pulled out that victory in the first GTT back last December when you two faced each other in the fourth round, that was a huge drag. But you know, this could be like your semi revenge. Semore Smoke: Yeah! Thanks a lot Chris! You’ve helped me a lot! {{{{Chris pats Smoke on the back again as if they were life long pals…}}}} Chris Farley: Don’t mention it buddy. Oh, and before you leave, make sure you remember that whatever happens up on that stage, I’ll be standing there like your guardian angel, and you can always think of me for a joke to save yourself. {{{{Smoke nods as he listens and smiles. He then stands up and shakes hands with Farley, for some reason he can actually shake his hand, it’s as if they’re both in the same dimension…}}}} Semore Smoke: Thanks again. I’ll remember that. {{{{Foley takes another drag on his joint and then he seems to pass out onto the bed and then disappear with a smile on his face. Smoke smiles again and then turns around and walks out of the room. Several EMT’s file into the room running either through him or past him as they run to start performing CPR and other types of life changing techniques, in hopes to bring the dead man back to life. But Smoke knows that their attempts won’t heed any response from Farley. Smoke walks through the police tape as if it’s nothing this time, and he starts to walk towards the elevator at the end of the hallway. As he hits the button the dream ends…}}}} {{{{Smoke wakes up and sits up covered in sweat. Not from a nightmare of course, but because the room had gotten so hot for some reason. Might be the summer heat in North Carolina just getting to him. But as he sits up he smiles as he’s got his help and now he knows what he’s going to do for the Premium Blend show…}}}} TO BE CONTINUED!!!
