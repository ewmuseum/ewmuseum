The Jobfather - - Part I

5 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05 March 2003 The scene opens within the cramp conditions of a simplistic dwelling, an apartment, no more like a motel room usually occupied by passing motorist so it would seem strange that it’s present occupier is a person of importance. Deep in slumber, lying fully clothed over the dirty bed sheets was none other than Primetime Championship Wrestling’s green tailored Jester, Gareth Green. Usually a person of taste this present scene appears somewhat below his high standards, many a time have we been invited into his lavish apartment set in the back-drop of Las Vegas Nevada but this accuse he currently resides in would be deemed it’s polar opposite. - Ring, Ring - Sitting in the midst of darkness atop of a corner unit the telephone sounds. One ring after another as the occupier on the other line persists causing our host, the Emerald Menace to stir frantically in hope of drowning out the annoyance. The flicking of a red light accompanies the call when all of a sudden it changes colour like a traffic light going through it’s sequence of red, amber and green, in this case however it is the latter of the three. From no-where the Jester’s voice filters the room as the answering machine plays back his personal recording, “Hey, how you doing? I’m fine before you ask but… Oh, should’ve mentioned this earlier but this is actually a recording and anyone trying to pester the Jester will find that I am somewhat engaged right now so if you can leave your message like a good little squire then I’m sure to get back to you!” As the message concludes the distinctive voice of Nascosto Ireul Nerezza soon follows, “If you spent the same amount of time training as you did partying then the Blood Brother’s record would’ve been eclipsed weeks ago but aside from the banter of the tag division, I would like to speak with you in regards to March Madness. Somebody failed to mention they were going to be guest appearing at Tuesday Turmoil, as did they neglect to mention the two tones of damage they inflicted to the event…” The call drifts into a desolate silence as our host wriggles in a comedic fashion on the bed. Finally sitting erect, his eyelids twitching open to showcase their green insignia and blinking violently as he adjusts to the dim lighting, when without warning the vampire concludes his rant, “Speaking of neglect your appearances down in Primetime Wrestling Academy rival that of Rich Rollins stint with PCW, which was not a lot by all accounts. Anyway we’ll speak later…” Yawning and stretching his arms to the heavens, Gaz Green intently listened to the Living Nightmares conclusion even responding to the Rich Rollins statement in a witty manner, “WHO?!?” Later remembering the former superstars alias of ‘The Renegade’ but even in his short tenure with the company he has seen many come and go, try and fail, Primetime Championship Wrestling has a unique yet full proof strategy, ‘If you continually fall at the hurdle within the first two months we wont be expecting to see you in six!” A statement of considerable truth as the Jester murmurs under his breath tallying up the since departed deadweight, “Well there’s been Rich Rollins, Statuz Quo, Ali Khadafi, Jamie Celt, Misplaced, The Heartbreakers, Amber Token, Two Ton in which I played a considerable part, Rush Mason, as well as the odd few who have had the luxury of coming back, be it Violence Jack. How can I forget the entire RUSH roster?” For no apparent reason the Jester’s face cringed as he peered over to the window. Nothing appeared out of place, except that the window was slightly ajar allowing cold air to circulate and that the curtains were pulled apart. Seemingly uninteresting but not so for Gaz Green who distinctively remembered drawing the curtains before going to sleep, prompted by the street light outside flickering into the room like curled fingers crawling up his bed sheets. Waving the thought away he climbed from atop of the bed and down to the cold laminate flooring, bouncing about in the process as his bare feet looked to be treading over hot ashes. “How very odd…” Drawing the curtains, his slumbering state had subsided thus prompting him to venture over to the joke of a bathroom he homed. On route Gaz also noticed that his personal garnishes of a watch and gold bracelet had been moved from their resting place of his bedside cabinet and over to the corner unit, where the telephone resides. “What the… Is this somebody’s idea of a joke? Let’s redecorate while the Jester sleeps. I wonder if my redecorating elves found the time to hire Carol Smilie to add a lick of paint to the walls or that Craig from Big Brother to relay the laminate flooring?” continued the Front Office Jester waving his head in disbelief, trying desperately to fathom the events around him. Finally entering the not so lavish on suite bathroom Gaz ran the water of the sink and almost to the brim as steam perspired throughout. Firstly washing, Gaz then coated his face with shaving form and slowly pealed away the uncommon bristles of hair, restoring his face to its former glory. Well almost, mid session the annoyance of earlier, be it the telephone sounded once more. - Ring, Ring - De ja vu one might say but in the midst of shaving the Jester was rather reluctant to answer. Scraping his metallic blade along his cheekbones the phone typically persisted and Gaz could only retort by grinding his teeth and cringing his face, when suddenly the answering machine blared into action once more, “Hey, how you doing? I’m fine before you ask but… Oh, should’ve mentioned this earlier but this is actually a recording and anyone trying to pester the Jester…” Annoyed at his own voice, Gaz Green jogged into his living quarters and lifted the receiver, fighting with the wiring he finally managed to warp the instrument into position before ranting to the occupier of the other line.“I know vampires don’t require sleep but us humans do and as much as I’d like to talk about all those interesting conversations you can muster up, be it the tag division, March Madness, Primetime bloody Championship bastard Wrestling I’d appreciate it more tomorrow…” However the voice was not that of Nerezza and he/ she continually asked to be put on speakerphone. Obliging the Jester put the person on speakerphone and listened as a distinctive male voice took center stage, “Mr. Green, Primetime Championship Wrestling is exactly the topic of discussion we would like to address. It seems that your private family, more like your circle of friends, The Front Office have been swimming in wealth, money taxed from the workers…” “Is this some kinder Union squire?” retorted Gaz Green. “Union? Hmmm, more like a collective of unhappy persons who worked for peanuts while the Front Office lived in luxury. It’s not surprising that Gaz Green owns a beautiful pad in the heart of Las Vegas’s golden strip even if he is away, staying in some dank motel room with a window that doesn’t shut!” continued the perpetrator as Gaz gasped for breath, shocked by the revelations of some stranger via a telephone. “By the way this leather reclining chair does wonders to your back. Right to business – It’s quite simple; we want you to speak with your newfound friend Mr. Kannon and put forward our case. Our case it that of loss of earnings under his tyranny and now we want a slice of the pie, call this Operation Pay Day… Squire…” Suddenly the line went dead leaving a terrified Jester sitting on his bed trying to recompose. Was this a prank? Revenge from a previous victim? The list is endless; the pranks have been right across the spectrum, as high as Kannon’s beautiful wife Ellie and right down to the likes of Two Ton. There isn’t any knowing who the culprit could be. “Nah, this is somebody messing with me, playing the Jester at his own game!” subtly giggled the Prince Of Pranks before venturing over to the telephone. Dialing via the keypad Gaz tried tracing the call and to his amazement the number utilized by the fiend was none other than his very own line from his Las Vegas apartment, maybe it’s time to get back to the ‘City Of Sin?’ - To be continued -
