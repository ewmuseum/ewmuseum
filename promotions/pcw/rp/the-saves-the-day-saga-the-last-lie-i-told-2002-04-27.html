The Saves the Day Saga: The Last Lie I Told

27 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-27-02 Stepping out of a house is leaving your shell of safety. Even in this sick, pathetic version of the world, you feel safe in your shell. A turtle, although its slow and easily murdered if you kick it hard enough, feels safe in its shell. Thats how it works, it has always worked like that, you could be in a house exactly the same as yours, but its not the one you know. So you don't feel safe. You feel vulnerable. A car is a moving version of your house, your going 60 mph, enough to kill most people if you hit something, and yet you feel incredibly safe. Like nothing can go wrong, just like your house, no matter how dangerous. DarkStar never felt that way. Never actually having a home, he realized what a house is. Its a place that temporarily holds your body until you die, then it does the same for someone else... this is assuming your house outlives you, a number of things could happen to it. Fire, Floods, Wind, Rain, Explosions... anything. But that doesnt phase normal people, because its safety. DarkStar feels the same way about his car. Its a death trap, a vehicle that controls your 60mph movement as you hurtle down the street surronded by nothing but concrete, metal, and bloody death. DarkStar always feels safe however... You would too if you could murder a dozen people with the flick of your hand. Hell, that even makes you feel better when your hair wont turn out the color you want it, or when you get turned down for sex. Yes, sex. Many times, DarkStar would wake up, kiss Talena on the forhead, say goodbye, and drive away, for miles, today was one of those days. Stress causes things like that. Thousands of time a day, teenagers who have school, businessmen who have a big deal to close on, musicians who have a big show, all of them eventually just start driving to calm down. And a history test is alot less important then being the sole remaining Endless in the sky. Bastards. At times I just think that all these other gods took this as a oppurtunity to go to Florida and lounge around on a private beach somewhere, while I am forced to stay here the entire time and keep everything in line for these jackass gods. Bah. Too bad my rational mind (or whats left of it) kicks me in the ass and tells me that when Fate loses something, this happens. Its in the handbook you get when your promoted to god status. But now, its into the car, key to the ignition, foot to the pedal, and shoulder to the wheel. Leaning back and slowing down only to adjust the radio. Just Drive I just want to get away from it all. Its a bit much. This importance, having to keep the human race grounded... its rough. Especially considering the fact that I hate it, and wish that I could just eliminate the entire lot of them. Humans are pointless. Not one of them start off good, they have to force themselves to get that way. Trust me on this one. I remember sitting down with Christ and playing Gameboy Advanced (He loves that thing) and I told him that if I beat him at Street Fighter then he had to tell me a secret to life that I didn't know. And after Vega kicked his M. Bison ass, he told me that No human is born without sin, in fact, all humans are born into a world of dispair and ignorance. Its the exact opposite of what the catholics preach. You are driven to bad because bad is fun. The fact I'm apparently ranked with these selfish deitys just places me into a seperate realm of bad. Aside from Akiyama, noone really understands what its like. Not even my lover. So thats why I drive. My pedal to the wheel and my heart beating fast, I figure maybe if I keep driving eventually everything will right itself and noone will be forced into this lousy existence that the rest of the gods have seemed to drop on humans. As much as I hate them, noone deserves a lack of death. People aren't dieing anymore. Although noone notices yet, in a few days the world will be doubly as crowded as it was. All because Death is a selfish bastard who can't just let Fate win like the rest of us. Hell, Fate leaves its damn King open EVERY SINGLE TIME. Its quit easy to beat Fate. But who would want to. That selfish wangbandit Death obviously. Maybe I should just let the car keep going. Let it crash into a pole or something. This is too much. I can lie when I get up to heaven or down to hell or wherever those Soul Jockeys choose to send me... Wait. Son of a bitch. I couldn't even kill myself right now. Sigh. Its worth a shot though, people who can't die don't need dreams, people who can't worship don't need nightmares. This is hell. Talena Well... maybe there is hope after all... He stops... his car taking a hard right. Driving back home. Past the sign he knows so well. Welcome to Philadelphia!
