A dangerous encounter with The Grim Reaper!!

18 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/7/01 When we last off….Flaymin had uncovered the secret underworld of gambling in hospitals.Doctors betting on the mortality of patients and their chances of survival was a widespread epidemic sweeping through hospital wards, great and small. How will this immoral corruption affect Flaymin as he is wheeled into the operating theatre to have Red Rock’s Balls of Steel inserted into his nutsack? You’ll have to see for yourself…. [Scene opens where we last left off.The nurse slowly wheels the hospital bed of a nervous Flaymin from the waiting room and into the operating theatre where two surgeons are waiting. The first surgeon look at Flaymin and smile] Surgeon: Relax, Mr Flaymin everything will go fine. [While the surgeon reassures Flaymin, his assistant walks over and hands the surgeon Flaymin’s progress chart (a.k.a. the latest hospital betting odds on Flaymin’s chances of survival).The surgeon flicks through the ‘progress chart’ and begins to frown] Surgeon: Well, these odds certainly do suck. [The assistant looks up at the surgeon and smiles with a devilish grin on her face] Assistant: Maybe we can ‘fix’ the score? [Flaymin’s eyes suddenly boggle open as the two doctors openly consider killing off Flaymin] Flaymin: Whoah, what the f*ck are you planning?!?!? [The assistant looks at Flaymin in shock] Surgeon: The Gas! The Gas! Assistant: Dammit, that’s the second time this week I’ve forgotten to gas the patient! [Fearing for his life, Flaymin tries to jump off the operating table.However the doctor rushes over and struggles to strap Flaymin to the table] Surgeon: Forget the freakin’ gas and break out the tranquilliser!!! [The assistant reaches underneath the operating table and pulls out a tranquilliser gun.She quickly loads it up with a tranquilliser and then aims it from point blank range at Flaymin’s neck.A second later she fires the shot and instantly, the dart pokes deep into Flaymin’s skin. Ever so slowly Flaymin begins to lose his power…..His eyes begin to flutter…..Until suddenly his head hits the operating table. Sedation has been successful. The surgeon breathes a sigh of relief and then reaches for his scalpel] Assistant: Wow, that was a close call. Surgeon: Yeah, but why are his eyes still fluttering like that? Assistant: I loaded up the tranquilliser with 5 times the legal limit of chemicals. [Flaymin’s eyes continue to flutter rapidly and sweat begin to trickle down the side of his face. Suddenly we are drawn into the mind of Flaymin…. literally.Inside the mind, body and soul of Flaymin we are witnesses to the raw thoughts of this incredibly crazy human being. Flaymin’s spirit suddenly begins to rise and it floats above his lying body on the operating table. The spirit of Flaymin looks down at his body in amazement] Flaymin: Wow, I really need to snip those nose hairs. [Suddenly another figure appears in the distance.Floating towards the spirit of Flaymin is a dark figure wearing a black hooded coat and clutching a scythe. Flaymin stares at the figure as it comes to a halt only a few feet from his hovering soul] Flaymin: Oh my god, I can’t believe it’s you!!! Grim Reaper: I’ve come to take you.Your destiny awaits. Flaymin: This is like so cool!I used to watch you all the time!!! [The grim reaper raises an eyebrow and looks at Flaymin with a confused look on his face] Grim Reaper: You do know who I am…. Don’t you? Flaymin: I sure do… I mean, I used to watch Masters of the Universe all the time when I was a kid.Why is it your character always jobbed to He-Man? [Once again the Grim Reaper looks on with a perplexed look on his face] Grim Reaper: Not to burst your bubble or anything but…. I am not Skeletor. Flaymin: Say what? Grim Reaper: I am the Grim Reaper and I am here to take you to the afterlife. I am also here to show you what life would be like if you weren’t around. Flaymin: What’s the point of that?? [The Grim Reaper shrugs] Grim Reaper: I don’t know- its in my contract. Now take my hand and I will take you on the magical journey of a lifetime. [Flaymin looks at the Grim Reaper’s hand] Flaymin: I’d rather not, I mean who knows where that hand has been… if you catch my drift. [The Grim Reaper grabs Flaymin by the collar and jerks him forward] Grim Reaper: Jackass. [The Grim Reaper takes hold of Flaymin’s hand.Suddenly, they both begin to rise high into the sky, soaring above the earth] Grim Reaper: First stop- PCW. Flaymin: Can’t we go to a whorehouse instead? Grim Reaper: Nope. [The Grim Reaper and Flaymin float through the windows of PCW Towers in New Jersey. They float through the corridor until they finally come to the largest office in the building] Grim Reaper: Read the sign on the front of that door. Flaymin: ‘PCW President and Owner: Phantom Booker’. Grim Reaper: That’s right.If you never existed, The Phantom Booker would still have sole control of PCW, the lawsuits would have never existed and Tyler Nelson would still be a lowly jobber. Because of you- none of that ever occurred. Now lets move on to our next stop- that gloomy night in Portland almost two years ago when you ran over all those people with that tractor- the beginning of your journey through misery and torment. [Flaymin and The Grim Reaper glide into the arena in Portland where Flaymin ran over all those people on that cold Winter’s day in November 2000. The two of them float throughout the arena until they reach the exact spot where the incident occurred] Grim Reaper: Tell me what you see. [Flaymin looks at the Grim Reaper in frustration] Flaymin: Hey Stevie Wonder, why do I have to keep telling you everything??Can’t you see for yourself! Grim Reaper: Once again- its in the contract, basically it says that you have to tell me what you see. Flaymin: You need a new contract. Grim Reaper: Tell me about it.My union is still negotiating. But anyways, tell me what you see. Flaymin: I see people…. Not crushed.Not bleeding.Not on a one-way trip to the morgue. Grim Reaper: Exactly.There would never have been a tractor accident.These people would have all be healthy and not filing lawsuits. Flaymin: Damn, I’ve had a pretty destructive reign in PCW, haven’t I? Grim Reaper: You sure have. Flaymin: So like, when do we get to see the bad stuff. Grim Reaper: Bad stuff? Flaymin: You know all those things in the world that would be much worse off if I wasn’t alive. [The Grim Reaper reaches into his pocket and pulls out his schedule.He browses through it and shakes his head] Grim Reaper: Nope, there is nothing in here that says the world would have been worse without you.Matter o’fact the world would have been a much happier … and safer place without you.Lets face it- you’re a burden to society, which is precisely why I’m here to take you away. Flaymin: No thanks, I’d rather stay here. Grim Reaper: This is not an option I present before you.It is a demand. Flaymin: Look, not only did you rip-off Skeletor with that cheap costume you’re wearing but you’re totally out of character too.Didn’t you do any research on the greatest super-villain of all time? Grim Reaper: Your failed attempts at humour are ….um humourless.If you shall not come by request- I will have to take you by force. [The Grim Reaper raises his scythe and takes a wild swing at Flaymin.Flaymin quickly ducks out of the way] Flaymin: Well apparently the ‘force’ isn’t with you. [Incensed, the Grim Reaper glares at Flaymin before he once again tries to be-head the Flaymin one with his scythe.This time around, Flaymin ducks again but he also manages to grab a hold of the scythe and pull it out of the hands of The Grim Reaper. But before Flaymin can take a swing with the scythe, he fumbles it and it falls down… down…. Down a bottomless pit. The Grim Reaper slaps his forehead] Grim Reaper: Aw shit.Do you know how hard it is to get my scythe back from Satan?The ‘Lost and Found’ department in hell is pathetic. [The Grim Reaper breaks down and begins to cry] Grim Reaper: *Sob* I try to be intimidating.I’ve seen all of the movies in the Freddy Kruger series, but nothing seems to work.I’m hopeless at being a bad guy.I’m this close from being on the dole! [The Grim Reaper continues to cry and wipe the tears away from his eyes. Flaymin begins to feel uncomfortable.He reaches over and hesitantly pats the Grim Reaper on the back] Flaymin: There, there.Its not the end of the world.Life goes on…. No wait, you’re supposed to put a halt to that, too. Grim Reaper: And I can’t even do that right!!! [Flaymin strokes his chin in an attempt to come up with an idea] Flaymin: Hmmm…. I think I may be able to help you. [The Grim Reaper looks up at Flaymin with a glint of hope in his eyes] Grim Reaper: How? Flaymin: You’ll see…. [Scene fades to black]What happens next?What does Flaymin have in mind to resurrect the ailing career of The Grim Reaper? Most importantly, what ever happened to the Masters of the Universe??
