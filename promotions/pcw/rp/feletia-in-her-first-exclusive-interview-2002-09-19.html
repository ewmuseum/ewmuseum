Feletia in her first exclusive interview

19 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/19/02 (The scene opens inside the large estate of one Mike Harper. It’s been a few months since we’ve visited the house, as Badge has been out with several injuries. As we get a glace around the living room, we can tell that Badge hasn’t been there for a while. First, there’s no Playboy lying on the coffee table in front of the television. Instead rests a small arrangement of flowers in a red-transparent vase. Infact, looking at the furniture, and the television, there doesn’t seem to be any piece of furniture that was here the last time we visited. Just as we are beginning to get acquainted with the room, We see Feletia inside some sort of silly looking see through bubble. Feletia seems stressed and her hair is slightly messy, a big no-no for Feletia.) Feletia: Where are you Bethany? I think Barney and Friends is on! (There is nothing but silence as Feletia sits inside the protective bubble. Then out of nowhere, the demon child appears, thrusting herself at the bubble with all her might. Thankfully for Feletia, the bubble resists the attacks by the spawn of Satan.) Feletia: **Sighs in relief** Thank goodness for this anti-hobo protective bubble Mike got from the police! (After a few more tries at breaking the bubble, Bethany finally gives up and leaves. Feletia once again gets back to reading the latest issue of People magazine as the mood dies back down. After a few minutes, and as if strategically planned by the demonic child, the doorbell goes off. Feletia, not one to leave herself open to such an obvious attack, doesn’t move a muscle. Again the doorbell goes off. This time Feletia gets to her feet.) Feletia: Oh crap! That’s probably Crater Face here for the interview! Or is it? It could be Bethany trying to get me out of the bubble. Oh man, how am I going to answer the door? Wait a second why am I talking to myself? DAMN YOU BETHANY!!! (Feletia, not one to give in so easily to a measly child, regains her composer then leaves the protective bubble towards the door. As she gets closer to the door you can see the worry begin to come across her face. No sign of Bethany however. As Feletia reaches the door, she takes a deep breath before opening the door. As the door opens Feletia tackles the unknown person at the door, punching them as she begins to scream.) Feletia: **Swinging wildly at the person** Take that you little freak! I won’t have you terrorizing me anymore! (As Feletia continues to pummel the unknown person, we hear a high pitched squealing coming from under her. Then out of nowhere a second person pulls Feletia off of her poor victim. Was it Clyde rescuing Bethany? Or perhaps Badge has come back? Sadly it was none of the above. Instead it is only a cameraman from the PCW.) Cameraman: Hold it Feletia! That’s not Bethany you‘re beating on! That’s Melvin! Feletia: **Stops her attack and looks down** Melvin? (As Feletia gets off the victim of her fury, we see that it is just the Pimply Faced Intern Kid crying from the attack.) Feletia: His name is Melvin? Cameraman: It’s Melvin right? PFIK: Shut up. Dang does anyone know my name? Feletia: Look I’m sorry about beating you up Crater Face. PFIK: It’s ok. In fact it was fun. I was kinda turned on by it actually. Feletia: **shudders** I’m going to pretend I didn’t hear that. PFIK: **dusting himself off** Did I hear you correctly? Did you say Bethany was here? Feletia: Unfortunately yes. PFIK: Eeep! I am not going in there with that psycho child in there! Feletia: Relax Crater Face, I have a place she can’t get into. We’ll be safe there. PFIK: What is she doing here anyways? Feletia: Ugh. Clyde ran off to chase some girl named Leah. Said Bethany couldn’t come for heath issues. PFIK: Health Issues? Feletia: Yes. Probably his. Since he’s been talking with Mike, he is now chasing other women. Sometimes I tell yah, Mike is such a horrible influence. PFIK: Oh man. Bethany better not find out. She’d kill him. Feletia: She won’t find out as long as you don’t say anything. And if I find out you told her, god help me I’ll rip your balls off and mail them to your mother! **Looks at the cameraman** And that goes for you to @sshole! (PFIK and the cameraman begin to slowly step away.) PFIK: You got my word. So why were you stuck with her anyways? Feletia: You think a day care or a babysitter would live through a day with Bethany? Not Likely. Anyways, it was a stupid idea by Mike. He thought it would be fun if Bethany and I got to know each other better. PFIK: He’s gotta be joking right? You and Bethany? Feletia: No kidding. ** Looks around and begins to whisper** Ok I think the coast is clear. Maybe the little psycho is taking a nap. On the count of three follow me. And stay close. One, two, three! (Feletia opens the door and the begins to tip-toe into the house. PFIK and the cameraman are happy to follow Feletia, as you can see by the expression on PFIK’s face.) Feletia: **Whispering** Get your eyes off my @ss you perverts! (As the trio creeps into the hallway, there is no sign of Bethany. Then out of no where, the cameraman screams in horror.) Cameraman: **pointing outward** Look out! Here she comes! (As the trio looks down the large room, they get a glimpse of a young girl swinging on a rope, a large butcher knife in her right hand.) Bethany: Die whore! Die! (Before anyone can react, the evil child is upon them. As Bethany reaches back to swing, Feletia ducks in the nick of time causing Bethany to whiff. Again, Bethany attacks from the air, and again Feletia ducks the attack. Bethany swings back out of view, but a sound of metal clashing can be heard) Feletia: She coming back for a second attack! Run now! (As PFIK and the cameraman run to the protective bubble like cowards, Feletia keeps her eye glued towards the last place Bethany was seen. As Feletia begins to walk backwards towards the bubble, she spots the demonic 8 year old coming for a second wind. As Bethany charges as Feletia, we can see that she has a kitana sword in her hand. What a Kitana blade is doing in Badge’s house is beyond anyone, but there she is, Kitana in hand. As Bethany leaps in the air, ready to attack, Feletia has a stupid thought of come through her mind. All of the sudden she too leaps in Bethany’s direction, only she is unarmed. As Bethany is about to swing, Feletia pulls both her arms back, pushing her bosoms out towards Bethany. By some freak coincidence, Bethany somehow bounces off of Feletia’s breasts, tumbling backward and sliding across the smooth wood floor into one of the rooms across the hall. Feletia wastes no time, racing into the bubble and closing it, as the two males in the room stare at her in awe.) Feletia: **Looking at the gawk-eyed freaks** What? PFIK: You and your.. Uh breasts make a good team. **Blushes** Feletia: Oh that? I saw it on a cartoon once. That’ll show that little beast who’s boss. PFIK: That was awesome! Feletia: Can we get to the interview already? PFIK: Uh Yeah. Let’s get started. (As PFIK looks at his notes, and the cameraman readies the camera, Feletia takes the time to try and fix her hair. After a few quick brushes with her hand, she sits up, ready for her first exclusive interview for the PCW.) PFIK: **turning towards the camera** This is the Pimply Faced Intern Kid, reporting live from badge’s house. Tonight, I will be joined by Badge’s valet, and personal friend Feletia. **Turning back towards Feletia** In behalf of everyone in the PCW, I’d like to be the first to say Welcome back. Feletia: Well thank you. I know it’s been rough for Mike, but I know he’s dying to get back in the ring. PFIK: That brings me to my first question. Where is Badge anyways? Feletia: Who knows. You’re guess is as good as mine. For the last couple of weeks, he hasn’t been here. I’ve been here by myself. PFIK: You don’t know where he is? Feletia: Mike’s not himself right now. Infact I’m kind of glad he’s not here. The last time I saw him he scared the crap out of me! PFIK: I would like to ask you about that. It was noted that back in the PCW2 Badge was asked by management to tone down his aggressive and violent behavior. Badge, by request of management was sent to a well-respected psychologist, to help cure him of his aggressive behavior. As of late it was rumored that Clyde somehow triggered Badge’s aggressive behavior back. Is this true? Feletia: Sadly yes it is. When I used to watch Mike back in the PCW2 I remember when he was asked to tone it down. If I remember correctly, he was overly aggressive thanks to the torment Jacob Yoder was putting him through. Management wanted him to get help in fear that he would lash out at the fans, and other PCW2 employees. It worked, and Mike calmed down, though it cost him. He was no longer feared in the PCW2, but mocked. It continued to the PCW, as everyone knew that Mike was not the same guy he was in the PCW2. When Mike was injured, he was supposed to report to the doctor’s to rehab his shoulder, but he never showed up. Then we saw him in the grasps of Clyde. Somehow, that sickening man freed the violent side of Badge. When I went to see him after Turmoil, he looked like he wanted to kill me. PFIK: If you thought he wanted to kill you, then what are you doing here? Feletia: Unlike that cheap whore Mona, I stand behind my man. PFIK: Even though he won’t commit to a relationship? Feletia: I’m not going to push Mike on it. He doesn’t want a committed relationship, so that’s fine. Sooner or later I will wear him down. PFIK: Would you care to comment on that weird rumor about Badge‘s “wiener“ having the ability to instantly regrow if cut off? Feletia: **Sighs** I really wish that I would never have to say anything. When Mike was first born, he lacked a certain rare gene and he was dying. This doctor in in University of Stanford’s medical division created a replacement gene. What wasn’t known at the time was that it was taken from a lizard gene. Fortunately, it wouldn’t hurt him at all, and it saved his life. The one side affect was this little problem that no one likes to talk about. PFIK: So you’re telling me that if Lorena Bobbit chopped of Badge’s “wiener” 50 times it would still grow back? Feletia: How the devil should I know? You think I have experimented with it or something? What are you sick? PFIK: Erm, let’s change the subject... (Before he can do that, the lights in the house are shut off.) Feletia: Damn it! That little b*tch probably did this. I tell you I am ready to... PFIK: AAAGGGGHHHH! (As the lights turn on, Feletia turns around slowly to see Bethany with her face pressed against the bubble, breathing heavily.) Feletia: What is this Jurassic Park? Come back when you’re a big dinosaur honey. You aren't scaring me that easily. (Bethany mumbles something before dashing down the hall into one of the rooms. PFIK sits there obviously shaken by the demonic child.) Feletia: Come one Crater Face, she didn’t get in. (PFIK stares blankly, before shaking himself back to his normal, annoying manner.) PFIK: Ok, let’s talk about the alliance between Badge and Clyde. Is Badge hypnotized by Clyde? Feletia: I wish it were that easy. Then we could snap him out of it and he’d be normal again. Unfortunately, Mike wanted this to happen. He wanted to remember everything again. And now Mike will help Clyde because he thinks that he owes him. PFIK: Care to comment on the scandal involving Badge’s 27 bastard children? Did you know about them? Feletia: I wouldn’t call it a scandal. I’m sure anyone that knew Mike’s lifestyle could tell you that he’d probably have a few kids running around somewhere. I’m surprised that there are only 27. PFIK: Any of them yours? Feletia: God no! I happen to use protection every time I am with Mike. And it’s not a STD issue. He’s been tested and is clean. I just don’t want this body to be ruined because of some worthless child. PFIK: Let’s move on to Badge’s return match on Turmoil against Bobby Stormbringer. Can you tell us if Badge is physically ready? Feletia: Yes, I do know that Mike is ready. He’s been ready for a week now. Bobby Stormbringer is in trouble. I have no clue what Mike plans on doing to him, but it won’t be pretty. That is if Mike shows up. PFIK: What do you mean? Feletia: Like I said, Mike hasn’t been home in weeks. I don’t know where he is, or what he’s doing right now. PFIK: But he was at Fusion? Feletia: That doesn’t matter. He was just getting back at Red Rock for injuring him. He’s not himself right now. I don’t even know if he cares about the PCW. But I’ll be at Turmoil, and I’ll be in his corner if he shows up. I’ll just keep my distance. PFIK: **turning towards the camera** Well ladies and gentlemen you just got the scoop on PCW’s corrupt cop, thanks to his beautiful valet Feletia. Watch out for Badge if he is in your area. This is the Pimply Faced Intern Kid signing off. (As PFIK turns back to thank Feletia, he is surprised to see that she is already up at her feet, opening up the bubble.) PFIK: What are you doing? Bethany is out there! Feletia: I gave you the interview, and now you need to leave. You don’t gotta to go home, but you gotta get the hell out of here! (With that Feletia leads PFIK and the cameraman out of the bubble, and across the room. About halfway across Feletia is attacked from behind by Bethany, knife in hand. Feletia somehow manages to drop the knife from Bethany’s hands, but the psychotic child still clings to her back. Like cowards that they are, PFIK and the cameraman have already left the house. As Feletia is struggling to free herself from Bethany’s grasp, the evil child bites down hard onto Feletia’s shoulder.) Feletia: **Wincing in pain** Ah! You little whore! (As Bethany and Feletia fall to the ground, the door to Badge’s estate swings open and who else but Badge comes storming through the door. Both girls are shocked to see the ex- cop, and stop fighting. ) Bethany: Gah! It’s lizard dick! Badge: Hey Bethany I think I saw the mailman coming... Bethany: Goody! (Bethany races to the front yard, to ready herself for the attack on the postal worker. That leaves just Badge and Feletia all alone.) Badge: Baby get your @ss into the bedroom! It’s been 2 weeks since I’ve hit that, and killing people sure makes you horny! (Feletia looks confused, then let’s out a smile and races into the bedroom. As the scene fades, we see Badge pick up the knife off the floor and take it with him towards the bedroom.) (Scene fades)
