Licking the Wounds

15 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/15/04 Our scene fades into one commonly seen by wrestlers on a daily basis, a lonely hotel room. The room like any other hotel room, decorated with cheesy picture of ocean views and scenic location, a couple of double bed, a TV stand with a 25 inch color TV, a night stand with a lamp and an old rotary phone. Nothing really special. The only thing special about this room is the fact of who occupies it. Laying on one of the double beds, Luke Chadbourne is putting and ice pack on the back of his neck after the horrible attack from the Primetime Players on Saturday night. His muscular body is exposed since he isn't wearing a shirt, only a pair of old rustic looking blue jeans. He baby blue eyes are filled with pain when he moves his neck even the slightest bit. The room is quite, not much going on. But that all changes as the door to the hotel room whips open and Hawk, red mohawk standing straight up and all, casually strolls into the room. 'Hey there Luke, how you feeling?' Luke grimaces in pain has he turns his head to look over at Hawk, standing at the side of the bed wearing a pair of black jeans and a red shirt with the band name Rancid on it. Pain and agony fills Luke's usually happy go lucky voice. 'I have been better. You saw that shit the Primetime Players pulled on me the other night. My fucking head and neck are killing me.' Hawk shows no remorse as he rolls his eyes at Luke in his weakened state. 'Whatever man, just suck it up pussy. You knew what you were in for when you started this whole wrestling thing.' 'Fuck you, just do me a favor and grab me a Mountain Dew out of the mini frig.' 'Hell no, you legs aren't broken get it your damn self.' Hawk plops down on the other double bed and grabs the remote off the night stand. He presses the power button and the TV turns onto MTV. Just by chance Rancid is playing, the video for 'If I Fall back Down' shows with crystal clear reception. Hawk begins to sing along in perfect time with the video. 'If I fall back down... You're gonna help me back again...And if I fall back down... You gonna be my friend.' Meanwhile Luke is attempting to get off his bed. He turns and places his bare feet on the thin carpet the covers the floor, pain shooting through his neck as he sits on the edge of the bed. He turn his head right and a loud crack is heard, then left followed by another loud crack. Luke stands up and stretches his muscular arms back as another loud crack is heard coming from his back. Luke lets out a moan of pain as he now stands upright. 'Holy shit man, you're pretty beat up aren't you??' Luke walks around the foot end of the bed and then toward the huge window at the far end of the room. He walks over to the mini-frig that is sitting in front of the window and pulls the door open, his muscles aching from this everyday task. He pulls out a green and red can that says Mountain Dew and pops the top. He takes a few sips and then walks back between the beds and sits on his bed. 'I'm not exactly at 100%.' 'Not shit, I can see that. But I thought you were just trying to make it seem worse then it really was. Like you always do. Luke places the can of Dew on the night stand and looks over at Hawk. His expression is filled with pain as he speaks in a grizzly tone of voice. 'Shut up and just give me a square.' Hawk listens and pulls out a pack of Marlboro Red 100's out of his pocket. He opens the pack and pulls out a cigarette. He hands it to Luke. 'You got a light or you want me light it with my ass?' 'What you want me to smoke the mother fucker for you too?' 'Give me a lighter. Quit being such an ass.' Hawk throws his pack of smokes onto the night stand and then reaches back into the pocket of his black jeans. He pulls out a silver Zippo and sparks the flint. Luke leans over and places the non-filter end of the cigarette in the flame. He places the filter end in his mouth and inhales the smoke deep into his lungs. 'God, for some reason there is nothing better then cigarette when you're in an ass load pain.' Luke lies back on the bed and takes a few more drags of his cigarette as the video changes from Rancid to Chingy. The two men sit there in silence as the music plays. After a minute or two, Luke turns and places his feet back on the floor. He gets up and begins to walk away again. 'Where the hell are you going??' Luke looks back over at Hawk and a bit of humor shadows the pain that fills his voice. 'To the bathroom, why? You wanna help me hold it??' Hawk rolls his eyes and lifts his right arm off the bed, showing Luke the one gun salute so to speak. If you don't know what I am talking about, he flipped him the bird, gave him the finger. Anyways, Luke smiles and starts walking toward the bathroom. He walks past the foot end of Hawk's bed and a sudden dizzy spell comes over him and he almost falls to the ground. Luckily he gets his hand up and catches himself, using the wall to hold himself up. 'What the fuck is wrong with you? You drunk or something?' Confusion fills the mind of Luke, he has never felt dizzy like this before. He has been drunk and all but this is a whole different kind of dizziness. He can feel it welling up in his brain as the dizziness causes his to take a seat on the floor next to the wall. 'No man, I just got a sudden dizzy spell, I don't know what the fuck is going on. I think I took a few too many bumps on the head or something.' Hawk's usually sarcastic demeanor is now replaced with one of concern as Luke struggles to get back to his feet. 'Maybe you should get that shit checked out. You know that's why we have doctors and all?' The dizziness has passed now and Luke is standing up right. He walks toward the bathroom, not even looking at Hawk. 'I don't need a fucking doctor. I'll be fine. Just give me some time to lick my wounds, and I'll be ready to go.' Luke disappears into the bathroom and Hawk just shrugs his shoulders at Luke's 'tough guy' attitude. Then our scene slowly fades out.
