Jyu Ni Gatsu no Mokushiroku {9}

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December/March “What, are you kidding? I don’t think Meanstreak himself would want to be around when this big boy wakes up!” Matrix was confused with the sudden command by Sonata. Cyclonica on the other hand ignored her, closing her eyes tightly and trying to keep a mental lock on the god. Sonata didn’t budge from her spot, staring down at the Divine Prophetess with steady eyes. “You heard what I said, Cyclonica.” That voice…before it’s always been so sweet, so loveable…now it was cold, demanding, and forceful. “That wasn’t a request. That was a demand.” Cyclonica opened her eyes once more, but snapped them back tightly just as quickly. She gritted her teeth hard as she focused her mind. Matrix stood up, glancing down at the computer before approaching Sonata. “Hey come on! Don’t be so unreasonable!” he said, patting her on the shoulder calmly. “We’re all a bit nervous with this mission. But once we have the neural transmitter hooked up to his mind, we can control Akiyama like he was our own child. Then the entire country of Japan will be grateful for the service you did here. And you? You get to go free. All charges dropped.” “No. This is all wrong.” She swatted his hand away before looking back to the Divine Prophetess. “Aki is a human being. He is not just some weapon. He is not a piece of biotechnology for the Japanese government to toy around with! He has a mind of his own, and for the first time in his life he’s going to use it!” That got the icy girl’s attention. Cyclonica snapped her eyes back open, slowly rising to her feet and towering over Sonata. Matrix watched with wide eyes, not believing what was going on as the two women stared at each other. “What gives you the right to say that? Huh?” “How about because out of all the women that’s been in his life I’M the one he cares about the most! I grew up with him, I was his best friend. I’ve known him longer than anyone else in this world! I say when enough is enough!” Unimpressed eyes shone dark in the Divine Prophetess. “Please. Like I’m supposed to just stop everything because you have some irrelevant sentimental bond with him. Haven’t you realized by now that he cannot coexist with humanity?? His physical bearings are too much, and his unstable mind frame will surely lead to the annihilation of everything around him, not to mention himself! What we’re doing here is preserving his existence for the well behind of the world, not to mention his own well being!” “Oh, by taking away his freedom? By taking away his brain! His mind! His soul??” Sonata wasn’t buying any of her words like the god wouldn’t buy anyone stopping him from getting the world title after he returned. Determination was a grand thing…even when it came from the ones that had no hope of success. “He had no soul to begin with.” Cyclonica tossed her hair aside and stepped up to the woman, her fists clenching tightly. “And what soul he has we gave him. We’re finally taking back what is ours…me, the Department of Divine Examination…and the country of Japan.” “Don’t make this into a patriotic deal! This is because you can’t bear for him to exist unless you’re pulling his strings! Akiyama suddenly starts to think for himself and his Divine Prophetess can’t handle that, so she puts a sledgehammer in the back of his head and gets the entire Japan Special Forces to biologically make him your slave!! I’m not standing for it?” “OH?” Cyclonica looked around, an amused smile on her face. There was no way this woman could stop her…she was the Divine Prophetess after all. Twice her size, twice her skill, and she had the god’s blood running through her veins. Nothing could stop her. “Give me one reason why I should stop everything right now? Give me ONE!” “Because I LOVE HIM!!” silence filled the training hall underneath Jahana Castle. Sonata whipped a demanding glare at Matrix quickly, causing him to jump back. “Pull the plug, Matrix.” Cyclonica didn’t even look at him. “Don’t listen to her, Matrix. We’re almost done. Once we have him under our will, there’s no one that can stop us. Not her. not Japan, not even the cursed world. It’ll just be you…and me…” That seemed to sway some words with the computer genius. He stepped back from the computer, his arms folding at his chest. Sonata looked at him hard, her eyes cutting fiercely at him. “End this now, Matrix! You’re a good person, I know. You don’t want to hurt anyone, especially Akiyama. If you do this…you’re hurting him way more than he could hurt anyone.” True words…nothing hurt a man like taking away his soul. Some men sold their souls to money, to fame, to their egos…to their own idiotic ramblings and beliefs…and it may have looked like they were happy…but inside there’s nothing that can describe the heavy burden of being a sellout. Matrix began to reach for the computer. “Matrix, I swear here and now that if you touch a single key on that keyboard I will make you feel a thousand deaths in a matter of minutes.” Her hand stretched out briskly at him, ice blue finger pointed at point blank like a pistol. And she wasn’t afraid of pulling the trigger… He stepped back from the computer again. “Matrix…” Sonata’s eyes cast despair upon him. She was desperate, pleading with him. “Please…” Once again the techno-genius was swayed by humanity… “Get real! Matrix, if you fall for the oldest trick in the book then you’re a---!” She never got to finish. Matrix never pushed that button… Sonata didn’t stop him either. For during that entire time Cyclonica and Sonata was arguing…the Divine Prophetess’ hold on Akiyama’s mind was slipping inch by inch. Matrix was too distracted to read the warnings flashing across his computer…or he would have regained control. You see…as soon as that last slip of psychic control left the god’s mind…there was no stopping him. His body lurched up from behind both women, a mighty roar escaping from his lungs as he thrust his hands into the air. Both Sonata and Cyclonica were caught in his grasp, neither of them able to break free from the choking death soon to dominate them. Matrix was frozen in shock for a second, but then he looked up in the gasping face of Cyclonica and knew what he had to do. He reached down and grabbed the sandbag, slinging it back before looking back at the god. But he never had a chance to swing. Sonata flew at him with the force of a cannonball, sending them both flying back into the stone wall. Cyclonica was left in his clutches, daughter left to father for her punishment. Her hands were to her father’s hand, trying to pry the fingers clasped around her neck like a vice. Therefore there was no way she could block the fist the god was aiming slowly at her. She didn’t see it until the knuckles were down upon her…but when you think about it, there’s no hope of safety the second the Neo Void God locks violent means upon you at all. Her body flew across the room, flipping over and landing face down in the cold stone floor. Matrix got up with a groan in his throat, finding Cyclonica decked out on the ground. Before he could respond at all he was drilled into the wall by a tackle. His spine hit first, shocking his body before his head jerked back…knocking him unconscious… And Sonata….dear…sweet…innocent Sonata… She was left alone with the Neo Void God…locked up in that vault…no one left to consider for help… You see that’s what happens when you put your faith in others. It fades out just as quickly when a being crushes the connection you have. You think numbers will be able to stop a malevolent being driven to eliminate all opposition from his mission? Nonsense…just like the propaganda everyone puts out in his or her quest for dominion. Pure unadulterated nonsense… Where’s the help when you’re all alone? When there’s no one left to turn to…when it’s just you…and the god…and you’re left praying… He doesn’t hear though… He only wishes to hear your screams… … … But she let out none. Sonata fainted, right at the god’s feet. =-=-=-=-=-= There is nothing worse than living a lie. People do it all the time. Nothing left in their own lives that they have to take on a lie…just to make their lives interesting. It’s a sad sight indeed… But what if someone forced you into living a lie? Controlling your life in aspects where you have no say, only obedience. No freedom, no free will, no mind, no voice, no soul. All in all…it’s no life at all. It’s almost hilarious though when someone lives a lie unintentionally…especially a lie they don’t wish to live. For instance…say a man lives as an individual, his own beliefs and his own life. Then he comes to PCW, has disputes with powerful men in the corporate world, and next thing he knows…he’s just like them. A money savvy businessman. Did he intentionally want to become this? Maybe he did, and maybe he didn’t. The fact of the matter is…he became the essence of the men he hated… Such a man cannot exist, correct? Xavier Kannon does… He became almost carbon copies of the men he’s hated. Jeremy Howard…once ruthless owner of PCW, a feud with one Xavier Kannon sparked the change, though it was long after his corporate Krayzie days. Tyler Nelson, the epitome of corporate evil. And now look at Kannon’s position…looked on as nothing more than a corporate devil. Owned PCW…probably has plans in motion to own it again. March Madness is the grand stage he has to post up such an event. Kannon’s changed…a lot. Remember the Scientologist? Remember when all he was…WAS a scientologist? Hubbard this, Xenu that, schmoozing with celebrities in the church, Hubbardville… GOLD….PATRON…MERITORIOUS…,/I> Hubbard’s no longer his idol now…there’s new gods in his eyes… Benjamin…Franklin…Jefferson… Green gods of currency… Abandoned ideals… Abandoned hope… Abandoned individuality… And looking upon the god…is there anything abandoned from his life? Indeed… He’s abandoned the fantasy pulled over his eyes. The foolish supernatural hype that everyone has played over him. The Void, the Aura, the Celestial…everything… He’s abandoned all that’s kept him down, held him back from becoming what he knows he is in his dysfunctional mind. He knows what he is…and he knows what he will become soon… …THE BEST… Because in the end, Akiyama’s not becoming Kannon…he’s not becoming Big Alan Steele, he’s not becoming Pharaoh or even Inphino Blitz. He’s only going to be himself…the being labeled a god. And if that’s how his individuality is defined…then at least he can look himself in the mirror and not see someone else. How haunting it is for Kannon…looking in the mirror daily and not seeing the golden mane and rugged features he dons…but the cocky grin and centered glare of Jeremy Howard…and Tyler Nelson… Identity lost… Victory lost… And PCW…every last one of them will take a fall so that the god’s individuality…remains intact. -=-=--=-TBC-=-=-=-=-
