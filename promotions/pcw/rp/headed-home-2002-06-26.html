Headed Home

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-26-02 [Fade In] [After Turmoil new Malice Crew member, Ryan Stephens and his wife Dana hopped on a plane and flew into Houston, Texas. They had until Thursday evening to shop around for a house before they had to fly back into Knoxville, Tennessee so that Ryan could scout out Mark Makonie against Akiyama. They probably needed all of those two days and more, but Dana, and even Ryan missed their families and decided they would stop by their home town of Huntsville and spend some time with their families before they did any house shopping.] [Ryan proceeded to rent a rent-a-truck, a 2001 Toyota Tundra, and then he and his wife loaded up their suitcases in the bed of the truck. Dana looked relaxed, glad to be off the of the road, and near home for once. Ryan seemed anxious. He was seemingly worried about having enough time to visit his family, find a house and get back to Fusion in time to watch Makonie. Not only that he hadn’t come up with any good new rumors yet today. He pulled his laptop out of his suitcase and spoke to his wife.] Ryan: Baby you drive so that I can update my site with a couple of good new juicy rumors that will invariably piss off more wrestlers so that I will have some good feuds in the future. [he smiles to himself.] Dana: Ah, come on baby… this is like a mini-vacation, and besides that you know I don’t like driving big trucks. [Ryan sighs, but agrees and puts the laptop back in the suitcase. He then walks around and opens the passenger side-door for his wife. She than thanks him, and hops into the cab of the truck. He walks around in front of the truck and as he does she reaches over and honks the horn. He turns to her and laughs, but it didn’t startle him, it’s something she’s done before. He climbs in and starts the truck up.] Ryan: Do you really think we’ll have time to visit our parents, and find a house. I mean you’ll want to look in Dallas, and Austin, and The Woodlands. Dana: Honestly, I doubt it, but I haven’t seen my parents in a while and I really would like to see them, maybe stay the night tonight, and look around The Woodlands area for a house tomorrow. If we don’t see anything we can just fly back down another time. Ryan: No, I know you’re ready to go ahead and find us a house to settle in, so here’s what we can do. I’ll fly back for Fusion, and stick around for Gold and Glory, and by that time you will be able to find a house, and I’ll fly back down and look it over, and if it suits me we’ll buy it. Besides you know our mother’s are going to want to look over the house anyway. Hell they’ll both probably want to go with you in search of one. Of course then you won’t be alone, so that’s fine I guess. [A look of sadness is displayed on her face.] Dana: I don’t mind missing Fusion, but since we married I haven’t missed a minute on the road without you. What makes you think I want to now? Ryan: Baby, I’ll be fine by myself, and besides that after Gold and Glory we’ll have us a nice house to settle into courtesy of you, and our mom’s. [Ryan, who has been thinking this issue over, hasn’t even left the rent-a-car place yet. He now shifts the vehicle into drive and pulls out of the parking lot onto a road that will lead him to the interstate.] Dana: [In a meek and unsure tone] I guess the break could do me good. Ryan: [In a reassuring tone] Of course it will baby! Besides that as violently as I intend to take care of Mark Makonie, I’d just soon you not see that anyhow. Dana: Okay fine. I’ll stay down here and go house shopping with our mother’s until after Gold and Glory, but you know I’m going to miss you so much baby. Ryan: I’m going to miss you too baby, but once I return victorious I’ll be in a good mood, and we’ll have a new house that we’ll need to break in if you know what I mean. We’ll have to make up for the few days we’re apart. [She looks at him, smiling, with a little twinkle of excitement in her eye.] Dana: You’re right, this won’t be so bad. And I’m already looking forward to making up that lost time. [she laughs a cute little laugh] [Ryan now focussing on the road catches the right entrance ramp and gets up on the interstate. He quickly gets up to speed and the Tundra falls into the sea of traffic headed out of Houston.] Ryan: Well, how do you think my joining of the Malice Crew turned out? Dana: Well you haven’t really discussed it that much with me, but I guess it had to be done since the Phantom Phive was coming apart at the seams and you were the only one even trying to hold them together. Now you’re amongst some of the best wrestlers in PCW, Duke, and Shabazz, RAH, and Snow. Ryan: Yeah, and the big man Kano, and the Voodoo daddy himself, Rush Mason. Dana: A real different group of guys if you ask me. It’s like they’re all so different and yet they’re all fighting for the same cause. I guess that’s kind of cool. Ryan: I haven’t really got a chance to talk with any of them except the boss. I hear Snow isn’t really all that happy to have me aboard. He was out getting pizza instead of congratulating my joining of the Crew. I guess that can be understood though. [Dana gets a kind of agitated look on her face as she responds.] Dana: That’s crap! He should’ve been there to welcome you like the rest of the Crew. He’s just an egomaniacal bastard, and ooh, if I ever get my hands on him. Ryan: Baby, Baby, calm down. That’s my new stablemate. He’ll get used to the fact that I’m Malice material soon enough. [All of a sudden she bursts out laughing and Ryan just looks at her like she’s crazy, though trying to keep his eyes on the road.] Dana: I was just playing baby. He does seem kind of arrogant though. Ryan: [sighs] It’s just his persona. He comes off that way, that’s just Snow. When you’re as solid a wrestler as he is, sometimes you just can’t help it. Dana: I suppose, but it doesn’t have to be that way. Big Alan Steele doesn’t come off as arrogant and he’s probably the best wrestler in PCW, [Ryan cuts his eyes at her playfully] besides you of course! [They both laugh, and then for a moment there is nothing but silence. Ryan is obviously thinking about something as he drives, and Dana is trying to figure out what he’s thinking about.] Dana: Baby, are you thinking about Makonie again? Seriously, you have to get your mind off of him, at least for these next two days until you go up to watch Fusion. Mark Makonie is just a stupid Nazi who’ll get his come judgement day! [Ryan smiles at his wife, and then chuckles a bit before responding. He picks up his cell phone and hands it to her. She takes it, but looks confused.] Ryan: Actually, I was thinking that you should call your Dad on my cell phone, and ask him nicely if he would fry us some of his famous fish. [Dana busts out laughing, and reaches across the cab of the truck and wraps her arms around Ryan’s neck and kisses him on the cheek as he stays focussed on the road.] Dana: Didn’t see that one coming! [She scoots back over to her side of the truck, and begins dialing her parent’s phone number on Ryan’s cell phone as they get closer to home, and as we fade out.]
