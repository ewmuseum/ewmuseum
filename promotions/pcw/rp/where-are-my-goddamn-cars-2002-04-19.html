Where are my GODDAMN CARS?!

19 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/19/02 “This is TOTALLY UNACCEPTABLE!!” screams the CEO of PCW, Tyler Nelson, as we fade in to a view of his face. It is beat red with anger, with veins pulsing and protruding from his temples. Even a few beads of sweat have formed on his forehead from his boiling blood. A sick, angry, contorted scowl is formed on his mug as his teeth grit together nearly grinding them to dust. “I-I-I’m s-s-sorry, s-s-sir,” stammers a high pitched, scratchy voice from off camera. “I d-d-don’t know how th-th-this could have h-h-happened.” We pan back to see that Tyler has a pencil necked geek pinned up against a wall by his throat. The nerdy looking young man, dressed in a nice (but not as nice as Tyler’s) suit, is raised a good three to four inches off the ground. His feet shuffle about in the air, trying in vain to touch the ground below him. Tyler leans forward, getting nose to nose with the man. “Well, I think that you better fix this RIGHT FUCKING NOW!!” The man’s eyes squint shut in fear as the CEO screams right in his face. A shudder moves up and then back down the spine of the younger man as Nelson unceremoniously drops him back to the floor. The man’s legs buckle beneath him somewhat as he lands, causing him to stumble as he hastily walks off. The camera moves around to find that we are in a car dealership, Porsche to be exact. The showroom is exquisite in its décor, a modern look with all glass walls and bright white fixtures. The black marble floor shines like a black sun, reflecting the images of the exotic automobiles almost perfectly. Almost all of the new models on the showroom floor….the Boxter, 911 Carrera, 911 Carrera 4, 911 Carrera 4S, and the 911 Turbo. The colors range from the remarkable Arctic Silver Metallic to the racy Speed Yellow to the sleek Lapis Blue Metallic. There are several other prospective buyers inside the dealership, perusing the precision driving machines. There is one gentleman who sticks out to us, however. He is a massive black man, wearing a pair of black overalls with no shirt on underneath. His hair is tightly braided into cornrows and a platinum chain glistens around his neck. His tan Timberland boots don’t make much sound as he steps on the solid marble floor. Moving back to Nelson, he is stewing in his own juices and tapping his polished leather shoe on the floor. “I cannot BELIEVE this shit! Who do these fuckers think they are dealing with, George W.? I’m the CEO for Christ’s sake!” grumbles Tyler to no one in particular. As he continues to glare toward the cubicle that the pencil necked geek ran into, fire coming from eyes, Tyler notices that another salesman has walked up to the massive black man……”OG” Orpheus Grant. We move in on the action already in progress. “Excuthe me thir,” says the skinny salesman with a lisp from hell. “Ith there thomething I can help you with?” Orpheus ignores the salesman and continues to check out the Zanzibar Red Boxter. “Ummm…,” the salesman gently taps Orpheus on his broad shoulder. “Thir?” Grant slowly turns his head and looks at the salesman, who flashes an obviously fake and oddly feminine looking smile. Orpheus snorts and turns his attention back to the car. The smile fades from the salesman’s face and is replaced by a snooty look of disgust. “Do you plan on buying thomething or are you jutht dreaming before you go back to the ghetto?” blurts the salesman. Without looking over, Orhpeus’ right hand shoots out and grabs the salesman by the throat. He jerks him over and bends him backward over the side of the Boxter. “What tha FUCK did you just say ta me, ya scrawny muthafucka?” snarls Grant. “MOMMMMMMYYYYYY!!!!!” screams the salesman. Several other employees of the dealership come running out from their offices to see what the commotion is about. A few of the men start to head over to help their colleague. “I wouldn’t do that if I were you,” warns Tyler. The men stop and look at the massive form of Orpheus Grant, taking careful notice of his thick chest, python-like arms, and bulging neck. They shake their head and mumble to themselves as they turn back toward the offices. “HEEEEELLLLPPPPP MMMMEEEEEE!!” cries the salesman in a raspy voice as Orpheus squeezes tighter around the man’s neck. “I used ta have lil bitches like you doin my laundry and rubbin my feet back in tha pen,” says Grant with a curled lip. “How’s about if I make you my bitch on the outside and you wash my muthafuckin clothes and rub my muthafuckin feet?” The salesman convulses, then his eyes roll back in his head and he passes out. Orpheus sighs and tosses the unconscious body of the salesman off to the side. He shakes his head as he walks over to where Tyler is standing, a slight smirk now breaking across his lips to interrupt his anger. “That was….interesting,” chides Tyler. “That muthafuckin homo was tryin ta talk down ta me,” says Orpheus in disbelief. “Didn’t he see this big nigga standin in front of him? I could snapped him like a Slim Jim.” “Heh,” chuckles the CEO. “I’m going to have to do some of that on Tuesday I’m afraid. My good friend, and Commissioner of PCW, Gold Patron Meritorious Continental Champion Xavier Kannon was oh-so-kind as to book me in a number one contenders match for MY PCW World Championship. And to make it all the more easy on me, he puts three worthless pieces of shit in the ring with me. I’m thinking that if I just show up I’m a shoe in for the victory. Then it’s on to reclaim what was wrongfully taken from me, MY World Championship.” “Hell yeah, nigga,” agrees Orpheus. “Split them muthafucka’s wigs, Ty. You know how The Ultimate Authority gets down. Handle yo business, nigga.” “I tell you what,” adds Tyler. “The only thing that could make this better is if after I won I would fire all three of those worthless payroll leeches. Boda has more than overstayed his welcome in PCW. Same with Frisco. Both of them need to be gone and stay gone. I swear, that Frisco is like a Goddamn cockroach they way he keeps coming back. I thought I took care of that son of a bitch when I kicked his ass for the title the first time, but I guess I was too nice and didn’t cripple that alcoholic bastard. I won’t make the same mistake twice.” Tyler pops his arm out and checks his platinum Rolex. “Mother FUCKER!!” shouts the CEO. “If this scrawny little prick doesn’t hurry up I am REALLY going to get pissed!” “You want I should go find this chump?” offers OG. “Not just yet,” says Tyler. “I’ll give him about another sixty seconds. Besides, that’s about the time that Legend, INC has left in the limelight. His fifteen minutes should have been cut down to about three seeing as he has the talent and charisma of a fence post. Anyone that hangs out with Duke ‘I Vote for Cowboy as the Official Second Language of America’ Williams and thinks it’s fun is just a loser, plain and simple. The Malice Crew will go the way of the Dodo bird and become extinct faster than Boda’s wife drops to her knees for a peanut butter and jelly sandwich.” Just as Tyler finishes up his rant the first salesman, you know the one Tyler had stammering like a skipping record, comes darting out of his office. He has several pieces of paper in his hand as he strides over to the CEO and OG. “Okay,” starts the geeky salesman. “Your order was help up in customs but is released and on it’s way. Eight 2002 Porsche 911 GT2’s, fully loaded, at a total price of One Million Four Hundred Forty Thousand dollars….” The salesman stops and looks at the papers again. “….plus a ten percent destination fee…” The CEO interrupts. “Destination fee? Are you fist fucking me? You are going to charge me a Goddamn destination fee? My mother fucking cars aren’t even HERE yet and you’re charging me a destination fee?” Tyler reaches for the salesman again, but this time he is able to escape the CEO’s grasp. “Wait, wait…..,” stalls the salesman as he backpedals. “I’m sure I must have read this wrong.” He looks at the sheet again, then nervously nods his head. “Yeah, now that I look at it again it reads ten percent discount. Sorry for the confusion.” “That’s better,” says Tyler as he calms down again. He digs in the breast pocket of his black suit and retrieves a piece of paper. “Have them delivered to these addresses, in the quantities stated.” The salesman takes the paper and nods without saying a word. Tyler points a accusing finger at the salesman. “If you fuck this up,” starts Tyler with a cold, calculated tone. “I will come back here and buy this place, and you will be washing cars for five dollars an hour for the rest of you pathetic and miserable life. Is that understood?” “Yes sir!” exclaims the salesman. “Very well then. Let’s go.” Tyler starts to leave, with Orpheus following close behind. As they are walking past the Boxter OG was looking at, the other salesman has awoken from his fear induced slumber. He looks over at OG and is about to say something when he gets rocked by a short right cross to the jaw, returning him to the land of unconsciousness. Tyler ignores the situation as OG just laughs. Both men walk out the door as the scene fades to black.
