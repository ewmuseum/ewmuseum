Spiritual Stuff...or something - Part 1 - Reincarnation of Harry

29 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 29, 2003 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Dude, do you believe in reincarnation, man? ['Totally Tubular' Tommy looks up from his large Chicken sub, with a large mouthful moving up and down in his mouth. Soy sauce drips from the back of the sandwich onto the Subway wrapper which is being used as a place mat. Tommy's face looks tired, but certainly not depressed. After the most tiring match against Rent-A-Hero at Shanghaied, any man would feel the bogus after effects of it. With the great skills and ability that Tommy possesses, he was able to use his super villain ways to defeat PCW's resident superhero and to retain the title that is rightfully his. It feels good to be the one retaining the title for once, instead of being the totally heinous team that comes up short every Pay Per View. Those days are over because of Harry's back injury, and now new days are ahead! New days of totally bodacious villainy, championship winning, and GNARLY sandwiches from Subway] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: I suppose, dude. [Harry just nods looking down at his empty Subway wrapper. He already scarfed down his meatball sub with the greatest of ease. That's what happens when one has the munchies all the time. It would also explain Harry's large gut and flabby bottom. He thought that Jared guy got all skinny from eating Subway...well, the only thing that it's doing to Harry is making his ass jiggle a little longer] Harry: I think that I'm Richard Nixon, man. [Tommy stops eating his bodacious sub for a few moments to look up at his stoned brother. Tommy's baby blue eyes, which stun the babes, are fixed on Harry with a bit of surprise and yet not surprised. Harry has been known to just bring up very odd things at very odd times. Like right now, Tommy is enjoying the thrill of his radical victory and eating his Chicken sub with soy sauce poured all over it. Harry, as Tommy is scarfing a large portion, decides to say that Richard Nixon, a former president, was reincarnated into him. Tommy, who is not the brightest bulb in the line of Christmas lights, even knows that Harry was born long before Richard Nixon even died] TTT: Dude, your not Richard Nixon. Harry: Oh yeah, man? Well, how come when I do this-- [Harry throws his arms in the air with his index and middle finger up, like holding his fingers up for a two count. It's that famous thing Nixon did when he was getting off an airplane...and then he fell down the stairs. Or maybe that was Reagan. Nah, it was Washington. That dude was TOTALLY clumsy!] Harry: --it seems familiar, man. TTT: Har, you've seen those videos in high school when we took that bogus social studies class. That's why it's familiar. Now can I go back to my sub and enjoy a most excellent victory over a totally bogus dude? [Harry shrugs and then begins to stare at his meatball sub once again. He knows that there is a piece of Richard Nixon inside of him, there has to be. Well, maybe Tommy is right about that social studies thing, man. It could have just been one of those far out memory relapses that he gets every now and then. Those kind where sometimes he thinks he's back in the caveman days and he tries to beat a hot chick over the head with a piece of wood. Luckily, Tommy stops him everytime before he gets into some not so far out trouble with the police!] Harry: You know, you shouldn't take too long to celebrate, man. You've got that match on Fusion against Skylar Green and Dante Jackson. TTT: Skylar wha and Dante who? Harry: The End and that black guy, man. TTT: OH!! Those two heinous compadres! Didn't I waste them back at Decimation to win MY bodacious Continental Title? Harry: Yeah, man. TTT: Then why do I have to do it again?! Harry: Pft, beats me, man! I'm not the guy who signs these matches. [Tommy takes a bite into his sub as more soy sauce drips onto the Subway wrapper. Tommy's blue eyes begin to take him away into another place. Almost like one of those gnarly dimensions. Where Tommy sees that bogus blind dude The End, or Skylar Green now, and just wants to destroy that dude once and for all. Tommy can't forget how Blind Bogus Green humiliated him. How he used that bogus babe, Sandy Wong, to try and drive Harry absolutely crazy. It nearly worked, but Harry saw right through it and made sure Sandy would remember him forever. Unfortunately, The End didn't stay away after his loss against The Tubular One, but he should have stayed away. Because now he's entered himself into a most heinous world of trouble, one that is ruled by powerful super villains. A world that is run by PCW Continental Champion 'Totally Tubular' Tommy. The dude who is eating the Chicken sub with the soy sauce! The dude who defeated Rent-A-Hero in an epic battle. The dude who will finally end the career of SKYLAR GREEN!] Harry: I figured it out, man. TTT: Figured what out, dude? Harry: Who was reincarnated as me, man. TTT: Who? Harry: WILLY NELSON, MAN!!! [Harry's face is so lit up with realization. It's as if he's discovered the cure for cancer and is ready to introduce it to the world. That's why it's hard for Tommy to say what he's about to say...] TTT: DUDE!! WILLY NELSON ISN'T EVEN DEAD YET!! [Harry's smile fades as his mind begins to explore the the possibilities of life for Willy...and then he realizes it] Harry: Oh yeah...sorry, man. [As Tommy chomps on the last bite of his bodacious sub one of the fine employees of Subway approaches the two men. To Tommy's surprise, she is a most gnarly babe...well, in a sort of dirty Subway worker kind of way. Tommy sees himself taking an excellent shower with her. He's washing her long, straight red hair and with his other hand he's making sure those perky breasts are getting the soap that they deserve. You know, nice perky breasts can never be TOO clean, Tommy always makes sure to remember that. The girl's lovely green eyes are filled with excitement but a little bit of nervousness] Subway babe: Hi...ummm...I was wondering if I could, you know, get your autograph? [The girl smiles nervously, and it's always cute to see a babe intimidated by Tommy's most excellent looks] TTT: Well... Subway babe: Not you, Tommy. I was talking to Harry. [Tommy's spirit, which is extremely high at this point, drops about two levels. He can't believe that a babe would dare pass up the bodaciousness of Tommy!! Granted, she does have some taste when she goes for his brother, but why take McDonald's hamburger over a perfectly cooked Delmonico steak fresh from the slaughter?!] Harry: Why, of course, you can have my autograph, man. What's your name far out chick? Subway babe: Marissa. [Harry scribbles a bunch of nonsense on the Subway napkin and hands it back to the lovely Marissa. She looks like she has just hit the lottery as she holds the napkin to her chest and giggling like a mad woman. Tommy is feeling evil. How evil? Well, evil enough to use this babe to get what he wants. What does Tommy want? He wants a free meal, damnit. He was insulted by one of the workers here and he demands free service. How will Tommy get this free meal? Well, the giggling Marissa enjoys Harry...well, she's going to enjoy him enough to pay for their food] TTT: Har... [Tommy whispers] TTT: ...get the babe to get us free food. Harry: Why? [Harry does NOT whisper] TTT: Shhhh. Harry: WHY?! [Harry still doesn't whisper] TTT: Dude! Your bogus! Marissa: Harry, the meal is on the house. Harry: You are just too far out, babe. [Marissa giggles and then slowly begins making her way behind the counter. Tommy just stares at his brother with wide eyes, completely surprised that the stoner Harry is was able to pull off something so masterful like that. He didn't even have to ASK for the free meal, she just gave it to him. It takes a true pimp master to pull something like that off. Harry has just gone from Super Saiyan level 1, to level 2!] [The boys exit Subway. Tommy celebrating his victory over Rent-A-Hero, and Harry celebrating his pimp mastery] Harry: I'm lost, man. TTT: Dude, we're in Marquis Plaza. Harry: No, not that kind of lost, man. Like lost to why we got that meal for free. TTT: Dude... Harry: I mean...come on, man. We're millionaires right? TTT: Totally. Harry: Then why did we need to get all that food for free, man? TTT: We're super villains...HELLO!! Harry: Oh yeah, man!!! TTT: Dude...watch this. [An old man with a cane comes hobbling up] TTT: Hey, old dude. Old dude: Hello the-- [Before the old man can finish Tommy kicks the cane out from under the man and he falls flat on his face] Harry: We're evil, man. TTT: Totally. [Tommy walks out into the street without looking, and a green '75 Chevy Malibu comes barreling at Tommy....] TTT: BOGUSSSSSSSSS!!!!!! [Fade to black] To Be Continued...
