Scenes from a hat?

Date: not entered (We see Tmm's logo [copyright 1997.] on a blank screen. Cue voiceover…) Voiceover:' Previously on Tmm…' (The scene changes to a hospital room. Tmm is in the bed with his head bandaged. Steve and Val are nearby.) Steve:' Don't you remember the Chef? Anything at all?' Tmm: (rather confused) 'The Chef? What's that? Some kind Food Network program or something?' (Val burst into tears.) Val:' He's lost his memory!' (Cut to a gala celebration! Tmm is there, black coat, black shoes, black hat, Cadillac, oh yeah! Tmm seats himself next to a starlet.) Starlet: (Running an appreciative eye over Tmm.) 'The breadsticks are quite excellent…' (Tmm stylishly raises an eyebrow.) Tmm:' Indeed. Then I shall have some.' (The waiter brings it over. Tmm enjoys a bit.) Tmm: (To the starlet.) ' You are quite right. So… hot tub?' (Cut to an office. Tmm is behind the desk. Raul comes in with an envelope.) Raul: '[something in Spanish].' Tmm:' Secret documents, eh?' (Tmm opens the envelope and takes out a card, 'Big Alan Steele Fan Club Member # 0000013', on the bottom it reads 'Timmy.') Tmm:' Only this can bring a stop to the conspiracy.' (Men in suits burst through the door waving automatic weapons! Raul goes down in a hail of lead as Tmm makes a diving roll through the window! Cut to a secret laboratory. We see Tmm over the shoulder of some guy in a lab coat.) Tmm: 'So, all this super sciencey stuff, and you used it to clone a fat, ugly woman? GENIUS!' (Cut to the final scene of a house burning down. Tmm, sooty and scorched, is comforting a small child…) Tmm:' Don't worry, Johnny. I saved your favorite toy.' (Tmm produces a 'PCW Chef' doll, and the child's eyes light up. He pulls the string on the back of the doll… and the head promptly falls off with a soft plop! Timmy stares at the head with mounting horror.) Tmm: “Don’t thank me, thank the Moon’s gravitational pull!” (He turns and leaves the child vainly trying to re-connect the head as the camera fades out.)
