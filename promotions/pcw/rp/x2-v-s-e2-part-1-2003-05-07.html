X2 v.s. E2: Part 1

7 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5 - 7 - 03 Wolverine, Cyclops, Storm, Professor X, Magneto and Night crawler the list goes on and on throughout time. Have you ever heard of the Marvel comic X-Men? When you ask that question you are almost immediately answered with a “Are you stupid?” That is how big it has become, so big that X-Men has become a hit movie not once but now just recently twice. One of the most anticipated sequels of the year just hit theatres and already it has become the number one movie not just in America but in the whole World. The new title is not what you expect; it is called X2 instead of X-Men 2. The name X2 has taken some ridicule, not by the critics, not by the number of countless fans but by two men, an actor and a comedian. Could this ridicule come from actual name infringement policies or could it come from pure jealousy? The two men by name comprise the rookie tag team E2 who have just recently been taken under the wings of one Nerezza and one Gaz “The Jester” Green. Two men searching for there identity, for there place in this world. Could they be a new team claiming there spot in time or are there just another standard on the evolutionary chain of one P.W.A? 1 Year ago…… “Tyler have you seen this?!” A wrinkled newspaper is thrown on the ground as John Gargano begins to get up from his lazy boy recliner in a fit of helpless rage. “What is it now?” A voice is heard from behind a colorful book with the name “Jokes and You” on the cover. The book is lowered and the blonde hair of Tyler Preston comes into view. He races over to the recliner to see what has John in a fit. “This isn’t right! This just is not right!!” John runs at the wall and punches it like a straight bullet then he quickly turns around and tries to compose himself while looking at the mix of shock and confusion upon Tyler Preston’s face. A flash of black and white catches Tyler’s eye as he slowly lowers himself to the floor and stares at the newspaper John threw on the floor in his childish fit. Tyler reaches down and picks up the paper still with John in the background yelling and pushing things over. Tyler slowly begins to read the title. “X-Men new movie set to start filming next month, for more go to page 7” Tyler begins to chuckle lightly as he looks up at John with a look of humor and almost pity upon his face in thinking how could this make him turn into something like the “Incredible Hulk”? He seamlessly begins to flip the pages of the gazette with reckless abandon until he reaches the dreadful page 7; his eyes bulge out just like John’s did at this same horrifying discovery. In shock he reads the title aloud. “New X-Men movie to be entitled X2” The paper falls in what it seems as an eternity as Tyler loses his legs and falls back into the recliner still with the look of shock upon his long face. John rushes over, now losing steam as every second passes. “Did you see it?” The Golden boy looks up to see John standing above him studying his face for any sign of movement. Tyler slowly gets to his feet then goes to the wall to balance himself even with John still babbling on. “We are E2 and the movie is X2, I’m sure they copied us! Those dirty Sob’s, we we’re just starting to make it big and now they take our name! And the worst thing is they didn’t even cast me to play Wolverine! I would make a good one, better than that Hugh Jackman punk! I mean I’ve kicked more ass than that guy has in his whole life! And those claws are just so sweet, I want some! This isn’t fair” Tyler just turns around and stares at John this time with a certain look of pity upon his face, John just puts his head down like a small dog and walks away as Tyler regains himself and bends down to pick the paper back up and look more into the movie. “It says here that they are filming the movie right here in Los Angeles, Isn’t that where your movie is going to be filmed?!” John peers out from behind the bathroom door and still with a toothbrush in his mouth he shakes his head up and down signifying a yes. Tyler smiles and starts to scratch his chin. “Did Mick send over your pass yet to get onto the set?” John walks out of the bathroom and straight to the refrigerator and pulls out some day old pizza and slams it into his mouth like a man starving for a week. His speech is muffled as he tries to answer the question still with the pizza in his mouth. “Yeah……it’s on…….the……desk…….Why?” Tyler jumps up and runs over to the desk and searches for the badge, at finding it he grabs it and studies it then smiles and holds it to his chest, with John looking at him with a baffled look upon his face, Tyler replies…… “John we’re going to see an advanced screening of X2” - End Part 1 -
