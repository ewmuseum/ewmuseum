What If?

3 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/3/03 “What if” What if the life granted to me were given to someone else? What if I was given the all-normal household family? What if I hadn’t gone through all the downs before I had my ups? What if I never trusted and never fought to get where I am today? What if I never had of left the orphanage I was placed in? What if I was never born? What If I hadn’t taken chances? What if I was born rich? What if I had the power to love unconditionally? What if I didn’t break all the hearts I broke along my way to stardom? What if I thought about my actions before I actually did them? What is Juan Borro wasn’t my brother? What if I had never come to the United States? What if I was still living on the street? What if I didn’t have God to look too? What if I knew my name? What if I knew my father? What if I knew my mother? What if I didn’t always-rethinking things over and over? What if I wasn’t strong? What if I couldn’t write poetry? What if I never caught a break? What if time passed me by and I died tomorrow? What if I left the world without ever loving? What if I never find the one I was meant to be with? What if I become a has-been? What if I need more than I have now? What if I become depressed? What if I never have another friend in my life? What if my father called me? What if I was caught and used for scientific research? What if I killed myself? What if I found out what this thing inside of me was? What if I wasn’t so cunning? What if I wasn’t human? What if there was no heaven? What if I never have peace in my life? What if I change? What if I have a family? What if I find the right one for me? What if I make a difference? What if I become a World Champion? What if I change the way people live their lives? What if this is only the hard beginning in a miracle of a life? What if God could grant my prayers? What is there was no God? What if the world just ended? What if I was never different? What if I had people there for me in my time of need? What If? I ask these questions to myself everyday. I never know the answers, and sometimes I never think I will know the answers. The day I die is the day everything I ever wondered will happen.
