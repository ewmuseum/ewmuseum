At The End

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-20-02 In his sleep, Trigger tosses and turns. He dreams, but dreams of terror, and anxiety… he cannot rest. Something troubles him--but what? Into his mind for a closer look, we see the hell that consumes this warrior. In his nightmare, he sees a large crowd in front of him. No, now that he takes a moment to glance around, he sees that it is a line, a line of exuberant folk, frolicking towards a destination point. What is it they are lining up for? What is it they want? Now he spots the end of the line… far forward, almost blocked from view by the endless people scurrying about. But in his mind, it is clear to the Infinite Warrior what is at the end of the line: the meaning! The meaning that so desperately he has searched for all his life, the meaning that always seems in sight, but just out of grasp! Trigger: Not today… He juts forward, squeezing between the flocks of people. He pushes them aside, he hurls himself into the line--but he cannot get in! They keep pushing him out, not taking notice. Trigger: Let me in! Let me in! I must find the meaning! But they just ignore him. He yells and screams, but doesn't make a dent in the occasion. Suddenly, the line becomes enclosed in a tunnel. The warrior is physically blocked out, but he refuses to stop and give up. He pounds on the outside of the tunnel until his fists are bloody. He runs around to either side, but there seems to be no entrance. But he knows that inside is the prized possession that he thoroughly desires, and will not breathe another breath until he has it! A turn for the worst. He looks down, and under his feet, the earth is cracking. Cracking and cracking until it breaks off--the world breaks off before him! It breaks off, and slowly, it slides away, carrying the tunnel… carrying the line… carrying the meaning. Trigger: Noooo! He uses all his strength, and leaps off into the nothingness. Will he catch on to the tail end of his destiny? No. He comes so close, but so far at the same time, and as his aspirations float steadily away, the Infinite Warrior flies flailing into the darkness--into the doomed future…into eternity. He awakes in a cold sweat. Where is he? Home? Never--he has no home. Then where? It doesn't matter. Does it? No, while the dream may be done, Trigger knows that he will always be in the darkness, always in the shadows. He finds himself in the park, sitting on a bench. Trigger: Did I sleep here? Were my terrors only moments ago, or has my life drifted by a few hours? Hardly knowing, hardly caring, the Infinite Warrior stands, taking in the surroundings. The sun is cracking into the sky, displaying a beautiful sunrise. Trigger: Beautiful? Not to this heart of stone. And so he walks, thinking of self- hatred, loathing, and demise. Why was he put on this earth? A cruel fate? He wants to know, but is beginning to feel hopeless. He thought he was on the right path. His new journey had been rewarded with a chance at glory--yet if the unknown powers that controlled him so were truly satisfied, wouldn't he have been blessed with an even greater prize? The PCW World Championship. Such a grand prize… such a shock that a measly midcarder had been granted an opportunity to wear it's sparkling gold across his waist. Not a surprise though, not to Trigger. Either punishment or reward, that had been his understanding. There was no grayness, only black and white. Only night and day. Trigger: I'm lost. I'll always be. Searching for the meaning to life in no spiritual quest, but one of choice? One of curiosity? Lost. Somewhere between life and death. But not truly lost, not thrown into the Neo Void, banished from this realm. I am too strong, in my will and in my heart. Trigger: The Neo Void. How ridiculous. A figment of one's imagination, yet blown to such proportions that even I might believe in its existence--if I wasn't so tough. Now Akiyama, he not only believes in the Neo Void, but considers himself its god. In the mind of this disillusioned PCW star, he is the caster of judgement, he decides who is worthy, and who must be stricken from this world. He is not of concern. After all, what is a god compared to a warrior? The Infinite Warrior stops himself. Words of such impact were hardly those of a warrior. A warrior doesn't brag or boast… it was the bravery and courage of one's soul that led him to the place that Trigger based his life on. Trigger: I suppose in retrospect, Akiyama and I are not so different. We both believe ourselves to be higher, not in strength or intelligence, but in being. …The only difference is his booming success, and my humble defeats. Such will not be so on Friday, for that is the day when I prove to the world that the life of a warrior is much more fulfilling than that of a god--god of the Neo Void no less. This gets him thinking about PCW, how he let a great opportunity slip through his grasps, yet he is obviously being given a second chance. In his mind, he promises himself that he will succeed in this attempt. Akiyama may be a main eventer, but Trigger is a warrior… even with a full schedule this week, the Infinite one feels no panic. Trigger: Now Kramer Wild Wing… he is very different. Not a speck of his personality coincides with mine… not one of his morals is one I would contemplate… not one of his ideas is worth listening to. He is nothing. No, nothing is what I am right now. Kramer Wild Wing is less than nothing. For the second week in a row, I face him in some way, shape or form. For the second week in a row, I will defeat him… pounding him to a pulp. Trigger: Jaden? Brandi? It is clear that PCW underestimates me. Don't they see that I have what it takes? Don't they see I have the power to succeed? I'm a warrior. Jaden and Brandi… they are not as bad as Kramer, yet I see little talent in either. Not nothing… but they will succumb--oh yes. I have no doubts in my mind. A smile graces his lips. Trigger begins to realize that things might not be so bad after all. He knows he must travel great distances, and break through unbreakable barriers. He knows he must contain himself, to shove through the disbelievers. But he knows that eventually he'll reach the end of his journey. Eventually, he'll be at the end… and the meaning will be clear.
