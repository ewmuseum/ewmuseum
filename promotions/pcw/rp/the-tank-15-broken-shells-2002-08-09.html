The Tank 15 - Broken Shells

9 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 9, 2002 ﻿********* The Tank __15— ********* Broken Shells If I could hold you foreverYou would never get awayYou would never run wildAnd I would never worry We could be happy in my pictureIn my perfectionIn my head We could be happy if you keep me happyOr maybe unaware ~Leigh Donovan Saturday, August 3 - Day 30 It took three guards and seven strikes with a billy club to finally get Snow to the ground, wherehe was promptly cuffed and dragged, kicking and screaming, back to Unit B. Krista watchedthe whole scene, in her chemically numbed state, through the glass that had been widely crackedwhen Snow threw his chair at it/her. She was later escorted out of the prison and left to fend forherself, a twenty year old Canadian girl - stoned in LA. Now, Snow stands in the center of Geoffery Rankin’s office, glaring at the smaller man thoughhe’s able to keep his temper in check. Rankin, the manager of Unit B, runs his fingers throughhis thick brown hair and sighs, “I just don’t know what to do with you, Snow.” Jason Snow is silent. “You just keep losing control,” Rankin continues, standing up from behind his desk. “Well what the hell am I supposed to do!?” Snow fires back, startling Rankin with the stern toneof his voice. “My fucking sister-in-law, a person that I care about, is out there wandering thestreets of Los Angeles stoned out of her fucking mind! And I’m stuck in here so I can’t do adamn thing about it!” “Jason,” Rankin says calmly, “I know you’re angry.” “Angry?” Snow smirks sarcastically, his eyes still burning with rage. “You have no fuckingidea. You just sit in here and play with your God damn hair all day, Rankin! You don’t have afucking clue what it’s like to have people to protect!” “What the hell is all of this ‘protection’ shit, Snow!?” Rankin suddenly explodes, tossing Snow’sfile into the corner. “Has it ever occurred to you that maybe people aren’t always looking foryou to fly in and save the fucking day? That’s the same shit that landed you in here, Snow!” Snow turns his back to Rankin, still handcuffed and under the watchful eye of a team of C.O’s. He has no response. “Well, Snow, as much as I feel for your situation,” Rankin says while scratching the back of hishead, “I can’t go easy on you this time. You have to learn what types of behavior aren’tacceptable - under any circumstance.” Snow clenches his teeth. “Take him to the hole,” Rankin says to the team of C.O’s. “He can come out Tuesday morning. Maybe with a little time to think things over, he’ll understand how things work.” *** From the Desk of the Warden “If you were to chain a speeding car to a wall,” the Warden begins, holding a pencil in his righthand and looking up from a stack of documents. “The chain would obviously act as a leash. The wheels would turn faster and faster, but the chain would always keep it in its place. Well,some cars just don’t have a top speed - they just keep going faster and faster. And sooner orlater, the chain’s going to break...” “Then,” the Warden sighs, “there’s going to be hell to pay.” *** Monday, August 5 - Day 32 Snow doesn’t mind the hole sometimes. There are brief moments where he lies on the dirtyfloor, naked, staring up at the bare ceiling. The hole gives him time to think about things heusually doesn’t have time for. Thoughts of his daughter, Haley, always bring smiles to his face,even in situations such as these. The horrid part about the hole, as far as Snow is concerned, is how it also gives him time toworry about all the stressful parts of life. Right now, the most stressful thing to him is Krista’sstate. He hates the thought that she continues to wreck what he thinks of, in a roundabout way,as her ‘purity’. Trapped in this dirty room, there’s nothing he can do to protect her. Everyone told him a daywould come where he couldn’t protect her, and he would never believe them. In fact, he tried toprotect her in that time they all warned him about - and that’s why he ended up in the Tank. But now, not only are there moral and ethical codes that keep him from protecting her, but alsotons upon tons of concrete, steel bars, and specially trained teams of armed guards ready to shooton command. They all said the day would come - he just didn’t realize how literal it would be. The worst part about all of these issues with Krista is how they all remind him that one day he’llno longer be able to protect his own daughter, Haley. If Krista’s gradual loss of innocence iscausing him this much trouble, he can’t imagine what will happen when Haley begins toexplore the world with her own eyes. It’s time to focus the aggression, thinks Snow. Tomorrow night at Turmoil, he plans to get alittle revenge on Darkstar. But his real focus is Friday, when he gets a chance at Darkstar in thering. Slowly, the young lion stands up and brushes the dust from his broad chest. He steps over to thedirty concrete wall and pictures his new nemesis, Darkstar, staring back at him. Suddenly, hewinds back and cracks the wall with a hard right hand... then a left... then another right. It’s not long before his knuckles are skinned and bloody while his rage drives them on and intothe wall over and over. Darkstar and Clyde may be free men, but on Friday, Snow plans to showthem a hell much worse than prison. *** From the Office of the Warden “Human beings are wild by nature,” says the Warden. “The only difference between us and therest of the animals is the fact that we value our own safety and understand that some of ouractions may jeopardize it.” “But when our safety’s not at stake, human beings are, like I said - wild. We break, we destroy,we kill.” *** Tuesday, August 6 - Day 33 “For fuck’s sakes!” Harvey Blake booms as he drops the long steel bar, heavily weighted down,in its place on the weight bench. “You mean that Irish cocksucker has been in the hole for threedays and we haven’t made a move on Hattoni? Fuck! You pricks had better get your fuckingacts together when it comes to gathering information!” Blake scolds his troop of skinheads,pointing an angry finger at them. “We just found out,” one of the young skin heads starts, only to be angrily interrupted by hisleader. “I don’t give a fuck!” Blake bellows. “You get your information early, you hear me? That goesfor all of you. I’ve got no complaints with how you’re handling the security, but for fuck’s sakespeople, we had a perfect opportunity to take out Hattoni!” “Well, so what if they let him out? He’s off at one of his fucking wrestling shows now,” one ofthe skinheads shrugs, “why don’t we go get the job done?” “Oh, great idea,” Blake rolls his eyes. “Lets walk into Hattoni’s cell tonight and off the fatfucker in front of every C.O in the fucking Unit. Derek already got himself stuck in solitary forthe sake of this war, I’m not going to lose anyone else.” Blake nods to them all, taking a breakfrom his anger to show his appreciation for them. He then returns to the issue at hand, “Mypoint is, we had three days to get Hattoni out of his cell and get him out of our way. We couldhave all walked away clean.” One of the young skinheads shrugs and steps around the weight bench to look Harvey in his loneblue eye. “It’s not so bad,” the young man tells Harvey Blake with a sinister grin. “Who the fuck are you, kid?” Blake asks, not recognizing the new skinhead in his gang - anyonewith a shaved head and a swastika tattoo’ed over their heart can walk with Harvey and his gang. “Jake,” says the young man, holding his smile. “Jake Hooper.” “And how is this situation not so bad?” Jake shrugs again, still holding his smirk. “Jason Snow is a fucking headcase. A walking timbbomb.” He smiles as he leans in close to Harvey Blake, “How hard could it be to get him sentback to the hole?”
