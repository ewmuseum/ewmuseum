Where Are We Going?

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.26.03 After a good amount of time away from each other, Tyler Preston and John Gargano decided that it is not a good thing to be away from each other so long. After all, with a big tag team title match coming up at Fusion, they have to be at the top of their game, and wrestle in a cohesive fashion. This is the factor that will bring them together, and everyone knows that once brought together, some crazy things can happen. John Gargano approaches Tyler's room door with a bored look covering his face. He cheeks droop down as he gives a hard few of stacatto knocks on the cream wood of the door. Tyler swings the door open with a smile on his face. His smile is instantly contagious as John can't help but to fight back a grin of his own. John pears over Tyler's shoulder in to Tyler's room where he spots Tyler's most recent creation, 'Club Preston'. John gawks at the mini comedy club that Tyler has created. 'Hey man, where have you been wandering these days? It's been a few days since I've even seen your ugly mug walking around the ship,' Tyler says as he pats John on the back of his black, tight shirt. Tyler has grown accustomed to 'Club Preston' and doesn't think anything of it. John doesn't remove his eyes from the unbelievable scene. 'Tyler, what the hell did you do with your room. Is this even your room?' John asks with utter confusion in his voice. He pushes past Tyler slightly to enter the room amongst the various folding chairs that sit in front of the empty, make shift stage that is otherwise used as his bed. 'Yes, this is my room. Didn't you see the door on the way in. See, number 464,' Tyler says as he prances to the door and opens it slightly to reveal the gold plated numbers that are centered on the cream colored wood. John walks all the way in to the room now, ignoring Tyler's explanation. 'What is this all about?' John asks, wanting to get to the bottom of this. He is in wonder as to why Tyler didn't inform him of this prior to the construction. 'I thought I told you this, but it must have been someone else. I had to build me a small comedy club. The entertainment on this ship just isn't doing it for me. I figured that this is the only way. This my friend, is 'Club Preston',' Tyler says as he spreads his arms out as if he is revealing the room for the first time. John just laughs, thinking that this idea is to 'out there' to be true. 'You have got to be kidding me. I can't believe this,' John says in a tone of dissapproval. Tyler's face immediately drops with dissapointment. He thought that John would be all for his childish antics, but he unfortunately picks up the vibe that John thinks it's stupid. 'Well, nothing is really that permanent. If the idea doesn't work out I can just take all this stuff down. It will only take me a couple of hours,' Tyler shifts moods from being happy and excited, to be down and undefensive of his creation. John grabs his shoulder and shakes his head in dissapproval. 'Take it down? Are you nuts, this thing is awesome,' John screams right in to Tyler's face. A full-tooth smile spreads across Tyler's cleanly shaven face as he gains the approval of his companion. A sparkle of pride twinkles in his bright blue eyes as he puffs out his chest. 'I'm glad you like it Mr. Gargano. You just might be my first guest when we I take to the stage for the first time,' Tyler says as he begins to shove John out the door so they can continue their adventures. 'Make sure to let me know when that is. You know that I'm always up for a little 'Tyler Preston Stand Up' on the weekends. You put out a good product buddy,' John says, doing nothing but complimenting Tyler as he struts around with pride. 'Oh John you're to much. Now this is all in good fun, but right now, we have a lot better things to be doing. I hope you're ready to take a pretty healthy risk,' Tyler says as he slips on a pair of snow white sneakers. The shoes are already tied as he shoves his large foot in. He stomps it on for good measure, and begins walking out the door. 'Risk? Hey now, you know that I'm not one to get in trouble. I have a reputation to protect,' John says with nervousness as he follows Tyler out the door. Tyler slams the door with chuckle and tries to re-enter to make sure that it is locked. 'Reputation, bah, no one thinks highly of you,' Tyler says with a booming laugh as he looks down both ways of the narrow corridor of rooms. John sticks closely to Tyler to receive an explanation. 'If this has anything to do with playing a prank on The Society, then you can just forget it. The days of pranking have gone out the window the day that we showed the Pranksters who the real Tag Team in the PCW is,' John says with pride, but hidden nervousness. He hopes that Tyler doesn't have any plans to stir up hostile emotions between the two tag teams before they meet on Fusion. 'Are you kidding me. Take a good look at my John. I think I have better things to do then chase around The Society. Do I look like a big dumb green Jester to you?' Tyler asks with a snobby tone in his voice. He begins walking in the eastern direction of the corridor. His feet make a soft sound against the grey carpet. The 'pitter patter' of John's fast past also echoes through the empty halls. John glances over Tyler up and down. He pears at him from his ratty jeans to his white T-shirt. Tyler swats his face out of his own. 'Well, if you we aren't going to do anything to The Society, then what do you have in mind?' John inquires, thinking that he would have had this all figured out already. After knowing Tyler for such a long time, he figured that Tyler would do the obvious thing and stir trouble with the opposition. Tyler smiles, happy at the fact he knows something that his partner does not. 'I'll tell you what. This Cloud Nine Space Craft has quite the accommodations for super stars. After thinking over everything that this ship has to offer, I thought of one thing that could be the single most important element of the ship,' Tyler begins his long speech, not wanting to reveal their destination quite yet to the panting John who is almost bursting with curiosity. 'Yea? What's that?' John asks with eagerness in his voice as he tugs at Tyler's white T-shirt. The neck of the shirt stretches out until Tyler gives him another healthy shove. John grows irritated, but doesn't want to get in a fist-to-cuffs with his friend who holds back the secret. 'John, without this thing, the Cloud Nine air ship would not even float the majestic skies of this universe,' Tyler hints against as he scratches the smooth surface of his chin with a smile. John stops dead in his tracks as an idea pops in his head. Tyler examines a four way split in the corridor to determine where to venture. 'The celebrities! We are going to have us some good old fashion super star hunting aren't we? We can get the autographs of every one on this ship. Hell, we could sell those for thousands of dollars. That's a great idea Tyler,' John says, hopping up and down like a little boy. His bushy black hair bounces up and down with each bounce. Tyler stays calm and shakes his head, downing that great idea. 'That's a fair idea, but the last thing I want to do is deal with egotistical celebrities. They remind me so much of.. of.. Silvio Rochelli, but that is all water under the bridge at this point,' Tyler says with bitterness in his voice as he recalls the defeats at the hands of Rochelli and Matt Jackson. He grits his teeth. John prevents him from going in to a rant by standing in front of Tyler, not allowing him to move an inch forward. 'Then tell me right now, where are we going,' Tyler stares into John's brown eyes and smiles. He figures that it has been enough torture, so he finally reveals the answer. He puts his arm around his friend as they walk together. 'Buddy, put on your golden wings, because we are going to check out the heart of the Cloud Nine, the cock pit,' Tyler says with a twinkle in his eye as he shakes John in a variation of a choke hold. John shakes his head with a smile, knowing that this endeavor has disaster written all over it. The two of them opt to go into the dimly lit right corridor in attempts to locate the central command cock pit of the air craft. It is unknown to them if they are authorized to enter this area, but they will give it a shot anyway. As Tyler has proven many times, a person will never know what they can achieve unless they make an attempt at it. This is one attempt that Entertainment Extreme will make a good old college try at.
