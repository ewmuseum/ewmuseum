XK04 : “Chasing Rainbows/The Infinite Scientologist?”

9 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/08/02 XK04 : “Chasing Rainbows/The Infinite Scientologist?” Location : Clearwater Scientology Complex.Date : 8th August, 9pm. “Where do you see yourself in 10... 25.... Even 50 years Gold Patron Meritorious Kannon?” If anything was going to inspire such a thought provoking question to be levelled at the Scientologist it was the vision before him. The murky Peach of the sunset viewed from the majestic Clearwater buildings who’s glass exteriors seem to absorb the filtered sunlight as it slowly drowns in the ocean beyond. The view will be seen many times again, but for now the question is asking itself over and over again in the already intense mind of Xavier Kannon, the PCW World Champion. Xavier : “I try not to look past the present. Less nasty surprises that way.” Not an honest answer. All who know Xavier will testify that he is always thinking. Present, past and future... The cogs are always turning. Plotting. Planning. Then executing. But where does he see himself? Or more importantly, what does he see himself as? A Scientologist foremost and a wrestler too or a wrestler foremost and a Scientologist secondly? There is no doubt he is successful in both, but since the incident the night of March Madness faith and profession have intertwined so drastically it is now near impossible to separate them. The answer to the above question is either a wrestling Scientologist, or a Scientologist wrestler. Both are the same. “You don’t see yourself becoming an Operating Thetan? Or something higher?” What was there he hasn’t got or done before? While months ago he was driven by hunger, desire... What is his inspiration now? Consolidation? In Scientology he is already a Gold Patron Meritorious... A position of great power, respect and profit.. This alongside being Mayor of Hubbardville, the home of Scientology beside the complex in which he now stands... But does he hunger for anything higher knowing once he gets it that is it? In PCW he is at the top of the mountain. He has held the Cruiserweight, Extreme, Continental and World Titles... His faction owns 50% of the company and his wife is Commissioner... What next? CEO? President? 100% owner... Then what? Again. Anything more will leave nothing more to take. Where’s the fun in that? “After all... They do call you the true son of Hubbard.” Was this what he is destined to become? Thought of alongside the likes of L. Ron Hubbard, the founder of Scientology? His name forever linked to the minority religion. His biography on web pages alongside Hubbard’s... Chronicled for as long as Scientology lasts. A second. Infinity. However much longer the faith is around for Xavier Kannon will be praised. Is he the Infinite Scientologist? Xavier : “I am perfectly happy where I am right now...” The smug coldness that has taken over his voice in recent months makes it impossible to tell if he is lying or stating the solemn truth... A smoke-screen around his words of his own making, allowing his innermost thoughts to be kept secret. Xavier : “Anyway, Gold Patron Meritorious has a nice ring to it... And any upgrade in status will only mean I have to arrange new stationary.” Another small jest that could almost be true. On Tuesday Xavier Kannon will fight Trigger... 3 days after he steps into the ring with Clyde and Darkstar. The phrase “Bad Blood” has never been more apt. When Xavier says he isn’t looking past the present... Tyler Nelson and the Dark Age may by the things that make the statement ring true. He doesn’t even know if he will be able to see Saturday morning, or whether he will wake from a coma to see Christmas snow falling from his hospital room’s window. They are violent. They are possessed. They want to hurt Kannon.... And they make it fun to be Champion. “I suppose when you have it all that’s easy to say, Xavier.” Did he have it all? While there are still some things he hasn’t reached... There always will be. Surely there is no point at which you have everything.... The moon, the stars, the planets do not belong to him... But what can be said of more earthly possessions? He has a beautiful young wife. He has a bank account that most people would faint at seeing.... But there is one thing more important than all of those... One thing that keeps him sane. Keeps him wanting to live. Greed. Xavier : “There will always be some things just out of reach...” Tyler Nelson called himself the “Greediest Player in the Game”... And how it was true. But his greed was limited. It was with boundaries.... Xavier’s greed does not. He wanted to win the PCW Title.. He wanted to get to the top of the mountain. Many win the big one then lose focus.... If anything it made Kannon more determined. he had the money. Now he has the Gold. next he will want the power. Xavier : “But who says Chasing Rainbows isn’t fun?” “Chasing Rainbows” perfectly sums up anyone’s desire to take power away from Tyler Nelson... People have always chipped away at it... Managed to take a position... But the very name “Tyler Nelson” may as well be called a position of power. You can be CEO.. Commissioner... President... But you won’t be Tyler Nelson. Xavier : “One day... Just maybe... You’ll catch up. The fun is in the game, not the result.” And so he chose Trigger. When his wife Eleanor Kannon-Hall took to the ring on Tuesday everyone thought it would be to announce the stipulation for his defence against Akiyama... Wondering what stunt he would have pulled on his behalf to give him the advantage. Not even the harshest Kannon critic would have predicted it. Xavier : “And there’s always the things you’ll catch as you chase.” Imagine a hunt in full chase.... Riders guiding their horses at a full sprint through the wood, the thuds of the hooves on the floor channelling up the rider’s spine.. The sights the sounds and the feelings... The thrill of the hunt. In front of them are the hounds... Guiding them on their way and in front of them is the Fox.. Or the Stag.. “The Goal” of the hunt.... The entire showpiece will only end when the animal is dead. Whether it be shot or savaged by hounds or simply dying of shock. Trigger is a rogue track, a scent, that acts as a pleasant diversion for the hounds. “Sometimes the detours are more rewarding than the destination, Xavier.” Very true. With the Dark Age pursuing him, and Akiyama wanting his match.. Trigger is just that... A distraction. Not a threatening one that will cause the Scientologist to take his eyes off the big picture, but more something that will amuse him... A plaything. Sometimes even a hunting Hound likes to play with a squeaky toy or chew on a bone. Xavier : “Sometimes distractions are obliterated for one’s amusement.” Will Trigger, the Infinite Warrior amuse Xavier Kannon, the Infinite Scientologist? For while Trigger, who has sworn himself to fight on eternally sees this as the greatest opportunity of his life... Xavier, the man who is still accepting jjst how powerful he is sees it as a vacation. Whether or not he catches the rainbow is something to be followed. But you can be sure it won’t be anyone but himself who calls of the chase. After beating Trigger on Tuesday he will grow bored of him and move onto the next little distraction he throws himself. The Infinite Warrior.... The Infinite Scientologist.... Is he chasing an Infinite Rainbow?
