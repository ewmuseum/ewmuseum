Waiting For The Bus...Exhausted...

4 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6 - 04 - 03 Bitter, blustery wind accompanied by an already chilly temperature doesn’t exactly spell enjoyment. Especially if you have to wait on the #6 bus to arrive from whatever far corner of the sprawling metropolis it’s chugging along in. So why even take the bus? Perhaps it’d be logical to walk, or to rent a car, or take a taxi… “If only I had a dime to my name,” The Astronomer spits through his chattering teeth, struggling to compose himself. Though his black trench coat is toasty, the biting wind seeps through his sleeves and the bottom opening like a slithering snake, chilling him despite his leather protection. “Fuck me for stopping at that museum…” To be brutally honest, grapplers do not exactly make a lot of money unless they are top draws. Dorn is in the lower main event level, so he earns a hefty paycheck, but it goes out the door just as quick in the form of hotel reservations, overseas flights, food, clothes, and other essential things for his constant journeys of whoop ass. When all is said and done, he only has about a couple hundred dollars, which go into accommodations and vehicles. Well, usually, anyway. Dorn Tracer tightens the folding grip his arms have around his torso and curls his body forward, trying to converse the escaping heat of his body, “Why did I do that… now I’m stranded and waiting for this fucking bus! GRAH! CURSE THE WEATHER!” As if in response, a sudden gust of sharp wind breezes by and causes Dorn to nearly crawl into a fetal position on the bench. He turns his gaze upwards to the dismal gray of the sky and looks for any signs of sunlight, but alas, to no avail. Looks as though he’ll have to endure the weather for quite some time while he waits for the bus. Guess that’ll teach him not to spend so much on museum arcade games… “Mind if I sit?” The high, strangely cute voice causes Dorn to bring his eyes downward and look in the direction of his visitor: A tall, slim woman with blonde hair and pretty blue eyes wearing a heavy cloth jacket with a matching purse. Dorn shrugs, “I don’t care. I don’t know why ANYONE would want to sit out here unless they have to…” The woman giggles, “Well, I have to. I assume you do as well?” The Astronomer nods and begins to look about, feeling a sudden wave of uneasiness strike his body. The woman giggles again and takes a seat next to him, sitting her purse in her lap. She clasps her hands together and places them neatly on top of her purse, almost like she’s in a movie or something. It’s just TOO choreographed. Nevertheless, Dorn takes no notice and watches the afternoon traffic breeze by… breeze… poor choice of words. It’s colder than a nun’s tit out here… The Astronomer thinks to himself, unable to push the thought out of his mind. The woman must notice, because he can feel her eyes probing his being. He shifts his eyes in her direction and his lips curl into a smirk, “You’re being kind of obvious.” “Really?” She asks rather nonchalantly, as if she was HOPING he’d notice. “Wow…” The look Dorn gives her, one of annoyance and puzzlement, gets a rather hearty laugh from her and she shakes her head, “I’m sorry, but you just seem real nervous. Why is that?” Once again, he just gives her a funny look. This person doesn’t even know his name and she’s trying to judge his emotions! What a crock of shit! The Astronomer scowls and turns away, “I don’t know what you’re talking about.” “Of course I do! I’m a psychologist.” “Oh, a headshrinker…” Dorn mumbles, then picks it up some. “Fascinating, but I’m not some crazy from the loony bin who just killed his mother because he thought she was a pirate from Norway. So I doubt you can just read me like some book.” His snide, uncaring tone seems to draw a pearly white smile from the woman, which only annoys him further. “You’re so hostile,” She cracks with a giggle to her voice. “It’s kind of funny. You act like you’ve got a reason to be upset!” Well, that clinches it. “I DO!!” Dorn roars, bursting up from his seat and startling the poor woman. “I’M OUT HERE IN THE FUCKING COLD, WAITING ON THE FUCKING BUS TO TAKE ME TO THE FUCKING AIRPORT SO I CAN BE FLOWN TO FUCKING FRIDAY FUSION WHERE I’M SUPPOED TO FUCKING FUCK UP SOME FUCK BY THE FUCKING NAME OF FUCKING A.J. FUCKING TRINIDAD! FUCK!” And at that, his face goes pale and he slowly slides back down into his seat. The woman’s face is frozen in a contorted mixture of fear and shock that you’d have to see to believe. Finally, after an awkward moment of silence, she digs in her pocket and brings out a little card. “Here,” she blurts out, “This is my card. Call me, Graphic Violence. I’m sure we can work something out.” At that, she gets up and scurries away. Dorn just stares for a moment before shaking his head and flinging the card to the ground. “Bah… Graphic Violence…” At that, the bus suddenly roars up from over the horizon like a turbo-charged caterpillar. Dorn rises from the bench and removes his shades to clean them, revealing his heavily fatigued eyes. He wipes his shades off with his jacket, slips them back onto his eyes, and waits for the bus to come to a grinding halt. After letting out a disturbing cloud of exhaust, the bus stops and the door slides open. The Astronomer begins to step on, but stops at the sight of the driver: A giant puddle of purple Jello that seems to have eyes. Dorn shakes his head and looks again, and this time, it’s some old lady smoking a cigarette. “Are you getting’ on?” She croaks, then proceeds to nearly hack up a lung on Dorn.Dorn nods and steps up, “Yeah… fee?” “A dollar.” The Astronomer removes a crisp dollar bill from his pocket, nearly the last of his funds after the plane ticket, and slips it in the collection box. The door slams to a shut behind him, blocking off all wind, and the bus gives a mechanical, aching groan before setting off. Dorn slowly makes his way to the back, keeping his eyes off the people. Upon reaching the back of the big metal Twinkie, he sits down and casually glances over at his fellow passenger. He nearly faints. “Hi, how are ya?” President Bush says with a mock wave of his hooked hand. Dorn slaps himself in the forehead and stares at the floor. “This is going to be a LONG ride…” [End.]
