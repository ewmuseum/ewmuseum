He said what?

4 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/05/03 At times in life you are presented with opportunities, which you have to take. Occasionally they would work out for the best and occasionally they wouldn’t but at the end of the day you would be able to look back on life and say, I saw my chance and I reached out and took it. This had just happened to James Edwards, in one moment his new career had been turned upside down. The scene opens in the hustle and bustle of a suburban metropolis a location where people swarmed in order to gain sustenance to fuel their bodies through the laborious day...that’s right a McDonalds Sat down on one of the bright red mushrooms, with white spots on top in the children’s corner of the fast food restaurant was James Edwards who was staring down at his tray containing what looked like reconstituted, burger which had a high percentage of being road kill just a few hours previously. He was getting a few strange looks from the parents of the children who were running amuck around his feet clearly not understanding why a man would choose to sit on a mushroom. One thought kept running through my head…how was I supposed to be able to help a performer like Skylar Green? When he approached me I was more than a little nervous, that showed I turned into Chandler from FRIENDS, using humour as a defence mechanism, making fun of the man being blind could have been the end of my career yet he simply laughed as well, he probably knew that I was intimidated, not only was he taller and more built than myself but also…he was as I said a world level performer yet he approached me after just one bout. James took a bite out of his burger, a small amount of crumb and tomato ketchup dribbled down his clean shaven chin before dripping onto his FCUK white t-shirt leaving the inevitable red stain as he tried to rub it off but proceeded to just mush the sauce deeper into the fabric. ‘I’ve got something, I need some assistance with’ those were Skylar’s exact word I thought to myself trying to ignore the children pointing and laughing at the ketchup stain which was making it look as though I had been shot in the chest. ‘I think we can help each other’ yeah right. I would give my left arm just to have an hours training with someone the calibre of Sky…the learning experience would be phenomenal what could I offer him though. He was everything that I wasn’t tall, strong he was even quicker than me despite weighing more, he was blind but at the same time seemed to have more awareness than me proving the daredevil theory to not be a myth. “I’m telling you I’ve seen him somewhere before” screeched the voice of a child to the left of Edwards breaking him out of his deep thought glancing over he saw two ‘star’ eyed children staring at him. They could have been brothers, cousins or simply two little boys who happened to be best friends. The smaller of the two visibly shook his head before saying with a rather unfortunate lisp “youwe wong it’s not him!” before poking him in the arm rather violently I glanced at the taller of the boys who was now clutching his arm rubbing it tenderly; he stood proudly wearing a black t-shirt with PCW’s logo just visible above his forearm, which was moving backwards and forwards. It was amazing how something that we do for a living can touch someone’s life so much that they want to go around and parade how much they care for it. Deep down it was quite evil of the company knowing that loving parents would not want to hurt their child’s feeling by saying that they couldn’t have the t-shirt evil but a brilliant piece of merchandising. I continued to look at the children as their squabble continued until the taller of the children moved his arm to reveal the rest of the t-shirt and the large handsome face of the emerald menace Gaz Green his eyes just glaring out…maybe he wasn’t happy about the photo being taken. I suddenly realised that I had been staring at the children for quite some time, the parents around me were starting to get agitated and I could see them shuffling about and gathering their belongs as though at any second they would scoop up their child and take them away before I did. “I’m gowing to be there and Egyptian and the Wushsian are gowing to beat the blind man and the astwo man.” The smaller of the two children’s speech defect almost making the sentence not understandable. My ears perked up, once again taking notice in the children’s conversation. Taking a risk I beckoned them over, the horrible trust that children often show excelled as without a care in the world they strolled over to me standing in front of me rocking back and forward waiting for the silence to be broken. “Nice T-shirt kid” said James breaking the silence with an opener pointing at the large face of Gaz ‘the jester’ Green on the child’s t-shirt. His face beamed as he proudly went on to describe how great Gaz was his title reigns, his hair and his suits. It was as though the child was either Gaz’s illegitimate son or had been stalking him for the past year. James nodded politely trying not to show his annoyance at the complete lack of recognition that he was receiving but he needed to finalise the information that he had heard. “I can do Gaz’ finishing move” said the child barely taking a breath in-between his sentences of praise towards the prince of pranks. Rolling his eyes James nodded at the child who slipped on a small hand buzzer showing it proudly to James. “Can I do it on you?” Nodding slowly James let out a large sigh he knew he would regret what he was about to do but it was just a child after all he couldn’t crush his spirit. The child extended his hand and placed it tentatively on the temple of Edwards who felt a small tingle of a clockwork motor buzzing lightly on his head, suddenly snapping his own head back James let out a scream of pain, throwing himself to the floor and rolling around pretending to be in agony when in all truth he was simply feeling embarrassed. “AND THERE IT IS THE MOST ELECTRIFYING MOVE IN PRIMETIME AS THE CROWD GOES WILD GAZ NOW DROPS DOWN FOR THE PIN” shouts the kid a few of the parents who were previously agitated now watching with wonder. You’ve got to be kidding me I thought to myself there is no way that I’m going to let myself be pinned by the kid. The next thing I knew the kid had jumped off the top of one of the mushroom stools landed on my chest and screaming down my ear with as much enthusiasm as I had ever seen before it’s as though the child was on a sugar rush with someone slipping an ecstasy tablet into his Mcflurry. “ONE TWO THREE GAZ GREEN WINS GAZ GREEN WINS” screams the kid a few claps come from parents who had been watching whilst Edwards simply wanted the earth to open up and swallow him whole. “Gaz could beat you because he rocks but I think you’re going to do well against Pharaoh and Krik…krik…kri…the Russian Man” spoke the kid before bounding off towards his parents chanting about how he had won and was going for the world title next. I rose to my feet and dusted myself down; a small boot mark had been left on my chest to accompany the ketchup stain earlier. Regaining my composure I took a final bite of my road kill burger, which by now had turned clay cold, hardly shocking as it wasn’t exactly hot when I had received it. Skylar had said that he would like a third party involved and that third party would be me but I never expected to be involved in his match. That was of course if the kid was telling the truth. If it was I was about to be getting involved with men who were at the top of the game Skylar Green and Titu Xansiv. Then there was Titu’s partner a man who appeared to be following me around no matter what I did, a man who seemed to be everywhere I turned the Russian man. Krikorian.
