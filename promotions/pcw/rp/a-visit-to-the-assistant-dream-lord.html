A Visit To The Assistant Dream Lord

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/27/02 (#2 FF) “Okay people, we’ve got a Clyde-mare scheduled for one Timmy Johnson, 8 years old in Aurora, Illinois in about 5 minutes. Move it!” The voice of an authoritative sounding man comes across the screen as we fade in from a black screen, hoping to see what in the hell could be going on that could be making for such a strange audio representation. “Dammit people, I need a Neo Void Dream for a pothead passed out in an alley somewhere. The scene finally clears from black as we see a short man, sitting in a director’s chair and watching dozens of monitors, while barking orders at others around him, most of whom are scurrying around madly. The man scratches his chin as he looks at one of the monitors, with what appears to be two walruses mating. “Jesus, get the hell over here!” One of the employees scurries over, notepad in hand, as the man barking orders gives more orders. “I need this crap out of here, give him a short transition dream where he’s getting whipped by Talena, he’ll love it. But you’ve gotta cut it short, this idiot’s got a baby to deliver in 0100 hours.” With that, the camera pans out to show that there are literally hundreds of monitors that this man is sitting in front of, and even more strange is the fact that all the monitors, and the man’s own chair seem to be resting on a thin layer of clouds. “Jorgè, I need a moist towel pat-down, stat!” Out of seemingly nowhere comes running a twig look-alike Hispanic man with a pencil-thin moustache, bearing a damp towel. He rubs the towel on the director’s face, and speaks to him in a soft, strangely disturbing voice. “Sir, you are the most bestest Dream Assistant Lord…” He then pulls the towel away, and sprints back to nowhere land, where he continues to not exist. Then, seemingly out of nowhere again pops Rush Mason, who looks around confused, and then asks the director a question pertaining to his whereabouts. Rush: Hey buddy, you seen DarkStar? The director turns around, looking just as confused as Rush at seeing the large man up in his…well wherever this place is. “Hey, what’re you doing up here? This is my work place. I don’t just come down to the weed spot and barge in!” Rush shrugs slightly, walking towards the hundreds of monitors, despite the director’s efforts to tell him off. Rush: No seriously, is DarkStar around? Somebody told me to come here to find him. The director looks at one of the monitors, and begins to fidget with a dial on his right, turning it slightly to the left and making an affirmative noise. He then looks back at Rush, who is still awaiting an answer. “No, DarkStar’s not in right now. In fact, he’s barely ever in. What do you want to see him for?” Rush nods, continuing to admire the massive electronic set-up, then meeting the director’s gaze, and remembering the question that was asked of him. Rush: Actually, I was looking for the skinny clown so I could dig sharp objects into his skin and watch the faggot bleed to death, but I can wait ‘til Fusion. Killer set-up you’ve got here. But uhh….what in the depths of Loa is it? The director sighs, probably not wanting to divulge any more information, but then replies to Rush, although he doesn’t directly answer the question yet, avoiding Rush’s eyes as he continues to monitor the…uhh…monitors. “I’m DarkStar’s assistant, I suppose you could say. I’m not really his assistant, per se, more of an associate.” Rush raises his eyebrow, visibly confused by the director’s words. Although Rush hasn’t yet thought of it, it probably wasn’t that smart to reveal his plan to try and injure the Mourning Star, but alas, too late. Rush: Assistant? What’s he need one for? The only things he’s apparently good at are dressing like a mime in denial of his violence fetish and getting his ass obliterated. The director/assistant only continues to fidget with the dial, as he inspects multiple monitors at a time, while Rush taps his foot irritably behind. The director once again doesn’t directly address the Voodoo Villian, but gives somewhat of an answer. “As you know, DarkStar is the “Dream Lord.” Now normally, he would oversee the dreams of everyone in the world, as a good Dream Lord might do. However, due to his PCW work, he can’t always be here to supervise the goings on, so I’m what you might call the Assistant Lord of Dreams.” Rush looks on, blank-faced, blinking several times, as he stares directly at the director, who takes no notice of it and turns back to his monitors. Rush raises a finger and asks a question in a fairly passive tone. Rush: Now call me DarkStar, but could’ve sworn that we’re not in heaven, but a soundstage that is inside a building. For some reason, the director turns and replies quite sternly to Rush, who breaks out a large grin when he realizes that he has begun to get under the director’s skin. “Do you have any idea how hard it is to get a spot reserved in Heaven or Hell these days? I mean there’s the obvious thing with DarkStar being goth, but you’d be surprised how little that matters to the devil.” Rush’s grin just turns to shaking of his head, thinking how deranged this man must be. He then motions towards the monitors, asking more about how the ‘dream’ system works. Rush: Okay, if you control peoples’ dreams, then show me how. The director doesn’t even bother to turn to shoot down Rush’s ideas, simply giving a ‘Pfft’ noise that makes it very clear that the answer is no. Rush: Aw, c’mon, why not? It’s not like you’ve got a hell of a lot more to do here. The director turns around, and addresses Rush in a scolding tone that would remind you of your fourth grade teacher when you tried to get the girls to play shirts and skins basketball, you sick little fucker, you. “Fine, I’ll show you once, and then you really need to leave, because I’ve got several children whom I need to deliver piss-inducing dreams to.” Rush nods solemly, then rolls his eyes as the director turns back to the monitor, pointing to one that shows Santa Claus with a child on his lap. “See, this is what we call a fairly happy dream. Now if I just turn this dial all the way to the left…” Suddenly, the dream image fades quickly into a picture of a demonic Santa, fangs bared, about to drain the life from one of the reindeer. Rush: Bon Dieu….that’s some serious shit. The director nods, and then speaks again. “However, if I turn the dial up all the way…” Rush watches the screen change into an image of Santa Claus, pants down….anally penetrating the young blond-haired boy whose dream it is. Audible vomiting can be heard from Rush. Rush: Ughh….change the dial all the way to the good side. The director can’t quite turn around, and there is a look of some dissapointment on his face. “That….uhh….is the good image.” Rush shakes his head and smacks his forehead, then just turns and walks out the door, as the Assistant Dream Lord sighs, and Jorgè comes running again, as the scene fades out. “I am so sorry sir.” “It’s not your fault, Jorgè.” “Sir, may I continue to watch the Santa Claus making the love?”
