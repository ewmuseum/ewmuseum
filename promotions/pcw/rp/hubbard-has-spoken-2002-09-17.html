Hubbard Has Spoken

17 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/09/02 “Amongst and amidst the peons and false faiths of this decaying world... One can always find solace in the works of Hubbard...” The shot opens up on a burning candle, drips of wax absailing down the thick fuel base to a rustic, Bronze stand.. It’s transparency being lost on impact with the cold alloy to once again return to cloudy White. “For every problem a Scientologist has, the works of Hubbard have an answer.” Through the hazy glow of the candle we see the face of the PCW Champion Xavier Kannon.. The Orange light from the candle casting a shadow of every facial feature he has. Xavier : “Unlike the Bible we do not have to decipher meaningless STORIES.. Our faith relates to the lives of every follower. Everyone who’s mind is open enough to accept it.” The CEO’s hair is neatly tied back, reflecting the skipping of the flame on it’s shiny strands as he reads from an aged book, several other painstaking intricate leather bound volumes dotted about the desk he reads on. Xavier : “Hubbard talks of the “Clowns”.... “The Jokers”, those who laughed him away like a pack of Hyenas trying to drive him from the religious circles...” Behind Kannon, row upon row of book shelves can be seen in the faint light that is propelled out from the burning wick of the candle. Xavier : “These.... JESTERS would try to torment him, try to break him and cloud his mind.” This reference is not a coincidence... The Scientologist’s Green eyes might as well have “Gaz Green” written across them as they burn into the page. Xavier : “They try not only to humiliate him, but those close to him in their petty attempt at distracting his focused mind.” Resting on the corner of the page, Kannon’s right fist can be seen clenched... His thoughts resting on the Pranks’ treatment of his wife, humiliating her on television. Xavier : “The works talk of how the Jester and his accomplices will lurk in the shadows and whisper rather than shout.... Skulk around rather than charge. They will pick their moments, look for the right moments... They will try to humiliate with false representations and dredge through the past...” As he lowers his head to focus once again on the page, the light casts deep shadows where his usually bright eyes are, and the Black silk shirt that the Gold Patron Meritorious wears is alive with dancing Orange reflections. Xavier : “There is a saying, not from Hubbard, but spoken by the peons of this planet and it goes like this...” Looking up again from the literature before him, Kannon’s face is awashed with candle light, never the same shadows cast or areas illuminated as the flame flickers. Xavier : “He who laughs last laughs loudest.” As soon as he finished the sentence his head shakes in dismissal of it. Xavier : “But I won’t laugh... I probably won’t even celebrate. Just like Hubbard, I will know I was victorious, I will know I am right and I will know my soul has a place in the stars while your bodies are being eaten by the lower end of the food chain.” Without even looking at the book, the Scientologist turns the page and navigates his eyes down to exactly the spot he wants to begin reading from.. Knowing the volume by heart. Xavier : “Then there is the blood sucker... The spiritual and physical companion of the Jester.” The scowl on the Champion’s mouth makes it pretty clear that he has chosen a passage referring to Gaz Green’s partner, Nerezza. Xavier : “Behind false coloured eyes this creature will work in cahoots with the Jester, their parasitic ways trying to play with mind of their victim... But a man strong in both body and mind can easily cast aside these two creatures...” As he reads from the works, his finger follows the text. Xavier : “Then, once the mastering of Dianetics has cast the duo aside, back into the shadows of nothingness... There is one creature that will stand before the man on the true path...” Again, the page is turned without the Scientologist even having to look down. He knows the works, he has studied them and now, he draws strength from them as he relates the passages to the present. Xavier : “... The Burned Man.” The face of Kannon looks ready to growl at he mention of the creature. Xavier : “With his scared face and rags for clothes, the Burned Man shall come forth following the fall of his masters...” Kannon focuses his mind on the man he’s relating the words to... Clyde. The man who under the direction of Tyler Nelson helped Crucify him... The man who set about avenging the firing of DarkStar, saying it was he who was meant to rid PCW of the Mourning Star. Xavier : “A sick mind combined with the belief that his actions are right makes the Burned Man a most dangerous adversary.. The modern world having sculpted him into a Demon who will not rest until he destroys those society has sculpted into the rich and powerful.” In the Scientologist’s mind, he pictures the scowling face of the Demon known only as Clyde.. The scarring from his hideous burns taking the focus of every other facial features. Xavier : “Unlike the mind of a Scientologist which is open and free, the mind of the Burned Man is clouded and violent. In every cell of his brain, hate breeds hate.. One hate fuelled cell divides... The process continues until the entire creature is made up of loathing.” Kannon pictures the Demon in the cage that housed him on Fusion.... The dead eyes still seeming to calculate from behind the steel, always scouting for a weak point, his animal instincts coming to the forefront. Still, the Champion reads from the work of Hubbard. Xavier : “The untamed beast will hunt down those with evolved minds... In a mixture or instinct and jealousy he will try to rid them of their bodies in a feeble attempt to get to their souls.” With recent events, Kannon looks to be taking heart from the words he reads... The wise works of L. Ron Hubbard never having let him down in the past. Xavier : “But just as the Burned Man’s masters and allies were vanquished, a clear and open mind can triumph over this scarred creature. The following of Dianetics will give the scholar an insurmountable advantage over the warped Demon. He will be the Matador to the Burned Man’s Bull, a cool and enlightened head triumphing over animal aggression.” Before the CEO even gets to the end of the sentence he closes the book, knowing the words from the page by heart. Xavier : “Just as he had foreseen me overcoming the Oppression of Jeremy Howard, just as he had predicted the Gold Patron Meritorious bringing his faith to the millions... L. Ron Hubbard knows that I, the PCW Champion shall not be stopped by the obstacles before me.” Now the book is closed, the Scientologist’s eyes look right into the candle flame. Xavier : “The Jesters, Blood Suckers and Burned Men that stand before me shall fall just like the Krayzie Ones, the Saviours and the Mourning Stars...” He licks his right index finger, still looking right into the burning wick. Xavier : “Hubbard has spoken...” With a confident beginning of a smile on his face, Kannon presses down onto the flame with his moistened finger, immediately plunging the scene into darkness. “....And never has the word of Hubbard been shown false in PCW.”
