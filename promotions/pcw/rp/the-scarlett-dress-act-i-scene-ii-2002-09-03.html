The Scarlett Dress; Act I, Scene II

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-03-02 (- OOC Note: The reason why I'm posting the series here, is because I feel that its necessary character build-up for Dante. Thank you and enjoy. -) Magnolia Police Station; District 9New Orleans, Louisiana 7:31 PMEnd of Day One of GTT The small fourteen inch computer screen gave Peter Evans all of the information he wanted, about any criminal logged into a United States police book. Various names sprang up, from O.J. Simpson, to Charlie Manson. The internet age gave the word 'privacy' a second guess, because anyone could check upon anyone thanks to it. With a few more keystrokes, Evans had been at this for hours, until his information had come up. A place of criminal background, and residence. 'Hmm, seems like this guy has been all over the place.' he chomped down on a donut, the sugary morsels sliding into his mustache, but he twitched them out. Like everyone else, he might had envied various people and their professions, but by having an athletic build and a mind to boot, Evans stopped at only a short list of envy. His last girlfriend happened to be somewhat of a model, a lucky break coming off a bad marriage, and it didn't cut with him. He was forty-one, self centered, and wanting to get a break upon things. Footsteps clattered along the linoleum floor as Evans continued to focus upon the screen. Larry Donovan happened to be walking by, supervising every little action the homicide division did. Old with wrinkles and a bald spot circuling his head, Donovan had been on the force a good thirty two years, chief of police for twelve of those years. 'Evans!' Donovan snapped quickly, causing a cheap groan of frustration to Evans. He'd been on the detective's case for months on end, not stopping until Evans was gone from the force. Evans sighed, 'How I wished the old fart was dead.' he muttered under his breath. 'I heard that.' Larry told him, much to Evans chagrin. 'So, what do you have on this case?' Peter blinked a few times, 'Well, its kind of odd. I mean the man has a residence in New Orleans, and his fingerprints happened to turn up at the mansion, but no motive.' 'Most murders don't have motives. Especially contract killings.' Donovan shorted, fixing his cornflower blue tie and wafting through his hair. 'You need anything?' Evans nodded his head, 'For you to let me handle this case on my own.' His boss chuckled, laughing at the attempt of Evans trying to figure out the case on his very own. The last time Evans happened to say something like that, he blew it right on a bank robbery which kept him in from getting the job Donovan currently had. Evans had envy for his boss, thanks to his screw up. 'Fine,' Donovan began to start, before sipping on a brew of coffee and sugar. 'Just don't have anymore bank style trails left.' As he continued to scroll down, the screen lowered down to his occupation. Astonished, he only tapped the keyboard with an eyebrow raised. 'Musician and Wrestler? Ugh..' This guy does so many different things, no matter a murder might sway his mind, he thought. No wonder he moves back and forth from city to city, not even letting the authorities know about it. He went down to his 2001 tax returns, each of them like a normal income for a then twenty-two year old man. His eyes perked up a bit, '41,000 to 1.6 million in a six month difference? Hell I could string words together and make them rhyme.' All of that money, he could pay someone to take the fall for a murder. Evans thought. He wasn't the prime suspect, but was spotted in the same vicinity as Tyrell on the night of the murder. All he knew, the man had so many different ties with New Orleans gangs, and Houston gang wars, a murder is a possibility to string upon him. 'What do we have here?' he starred until the siblings page, highlighting the letters until he got one full name - April Jackson. Evans eyed the name, 'The girl...' He swiveled this chair around, flicking through the manila folder he uses for all evidence collected. Fliiping through some mug shot pictures of Dante, but finding not what he wanted to be looking for. He feels the pockets on his brown Dockers, rubbing against pocket lint, but finding nothing. 'Dammit, where did I put it?' Evans blurted out, continuing to look for the piece of paper he'd collected from the crime scene earlier. Feeling the breast pocket on this shirt, he felt the folded piece of paper. He smiled, 'Here it is.' Peter pulled out the piece of paper, before unfolding it and placing it on the screen, making sure the names matched. They did, and Evans smiled, pleased. 'What'd you find?' Larry stated looking over his shoulder. 'Better be something good.' 'It its, something Jackson himself doesn't even know.' Peter nodded. Donovan's eyes widened, 'You're going to break this open on him, aren't you?' Peter looked up at him, 'Sorry if its my job to hurt the man, I signed up to take these guys off the streets. Not keep them by giving them a slap on the wrist.' Donovan walked away, raising his hand in the air with his index finger extended, 'Let's hope so.' He clicked on the print option, and waited to stack all of the files into the manila folder located at the edge of the oak desk. He looked at the paper, the ink starting to dry on the heated paper. 'Let's scoop some more on this Mr. Jackson, shall we?' * * * Tulane Stadium - FieldhouseNew Orleans, LA 10:25 amDay Two of GTT 'You ugly piece of crap!' I muttered under my breath, punching the steering wheel with most of my might. This car wasn't the one I happened to ask for at the Rent-A-Car agency, it was the bane of everything a soon to be hip hop icon was wanting. Cheap? Yeah. Old, you guessed it, but the ad told it purrs. Liars. The small compact made it to the stadium, barely. I could risk it by trying to swerve all around the campus, but I'd have hefty fines on my case, so I did the old granny style today. The parking garage, dimly lit happened to be four stories tall, enough for me to fit the rusty car in. Switching on the headlights, I took the route towards the first space I saw open. 'Thank godness,' I said, switching the death cart off, slamming the door behind me. As I turned around, I nearly screamed like a teen in a horror film at the sight of the stranger. 'Holy crap...'I shouted. 'Desmond, what are you doing here?' 'You didn't check in at the house show, been busy?' I had a odd sigh on my face, choking from from either laughter or fear. 'So that gives you the automatic right to scare me in a parking lot? You know how many things go on out here?' 'I wasn't expecting a thing, wondering on would you duck me or not.' he started, snickering. 'Afterall, you don't want a bad pyrotechnic to go off in your face do you?' I managed a slight smile, 'You know, out of all the people, I trust you the most?' I nodded to him, before taking my Nike duffel bag out of the car. We started our way out of the car park, me and the pyro tech. 'The most, not one of those odd peeps.' 'So, these tournaments a full thing or what?' he started, looking to start up some decent conversation between one another. It was like him, since he was there from the beginning, the young wise man. He'd chastise me sooner or later, time to lay down protection for the storm upcoming. 'I'm all right okay?' I lied to him easily. 'I'm actually looking forward to winning it all.' He turned towards me, trying to find any evidence for me to lie straight in his face. It was like that moment out of Juice when Tupac was right next to Q's locker, and surprised him out of nowhere. I don't let my emotions hang out, not in front of guys anyway. I may joke, as I am a prankster, but if something is bothering me, you better expect for no one to hear a thing. 'Is that right?' Desmond asked with a cautious shrill in his voice. 'No shit on your mind?' 'Nada.' I told him, shrugging my shoulders with the weight of the duffel on them. A short moment of pause came, because there we were, two young men stuck in the middle of New Orleans without any protection to harm us from getting jumped. If there was an odd couple in the area of Magnolia, without colors then we'd be it - no questions asked. 'Who's Peter Evans?' he finally blurted out. Fuck, fuck, fuckity, fuck, fuck. Who told him? 'Just who are you talking about?' I asked him instantly, trying to play the dumb role that's common with my line of work. I don't think I was convincing enough to make you miss the truth starring you in the face. 'Don't try me,' Desmond said. 'Remember I know more about you than you yourself. I might know more about this situation than you do.' I stopped walking, turning to face him. 'What is there to know?' 'Tell me what you know, for starters. Then I'll fill in the blanks and you can go from there.' 'Here's how it goes. I was up with Tracy yesterday, checking over stats for Street Manifesto, still at #1 by the way and the phone rings. Out of the blue, and I have no clue in the world who's calling.' I started telling the story. 'I thought it was you for a second, but it turned out to be this detective named...' 'Peter Evans.' he finished my statement for me. 'Yep, him.' I told him despondently. 'Anyway, turns out this cats from Magnolia Station, about three blocks away from here and he starts asking me questions. As if I were in Louisiana in the past week or two. He said he'd come by and speak with me to see if I know anything.' Desmond sighed, wafting his hands through his curly brown hair. Normally, he'd leave his hair alone after finishing putting the touches on it during the morning. He had some fidgeting problems, but when he was silent and playing in his hair, he happened to be worried, his baby blues told me that. 'I got a call from some chick, nice, spoke to me from the District willing to take on this case. She doesn't have anything about black kids, or anything like that,' Desmond said. 'she's rather one of those 'work for everybody type of things. Apparently, they're saying you're being investigated for murder one.' 'Murder?' I choked once he finished saying that for a second. It was pure disbelief to me.'Don't they dust finger prints or anything like that?' 'They just want you for questioning, you aren't a serious afterthought towards them, but you're in their eyesight.''Right.' I replied to him sarcastically. 'I know the 'Nolia, and those cops are like watch dogs, they don't care who you are, they bust on you easily.' Desmond nodded. 'Well, since the man Peter Evans came into the division. He changed everything down for the detective role in Louisiana. For I think about 13 years, he's been on the force, taking names down and picking their asses off of the streets. Now, its all good for him, getting his work on, but it worries me that you happen to be apart of it.' 'I didn't kill anybody.' I responded flatly. Desmond snapped, giving me one of those, 'tell me the truth, or I'll beat it out of you' looks,' and I snapped back on him like a gaged Pit, ready to be unleashed. 'I don't kill anybody, nobody you got that?' 'Anymore.' he responded quickly, silencing me. He knew that I happened to snap on Frenchy Davis and it took me down to not getting a chance. 'I'm not charging you myself, but you don't think that you arrive a week before and things get shaky already?' 'I didn't make that mistake twice.' 'Fine.' Desmond said. 'But for you? This is bad publicity, you know? Say that your record falls short, no #1 for nine weeks and it burns out right here and right now. This is a huge tournament, and you are being investigated for a murder - initial bad rep right there. They're going to purge you until there isn't a thing left, and then what is there of you? Nothing.' I lifted my head a bit. This wasn't something I needed, not at a time like this I suppose. At first, my original goal for this tournament was erase those clouds left over me thanks to the Wrestling God and Last Man Standing tournaments. I mean, come in here, bust my ass and get the job done. After this, there was a hurricane to be thrown in my face. The press would hound me everyday of this tournament, and afterwards along with it. Some pushy reporter in the business, and then I'd probably have to bust a man in the nose. But now, with this, how can I concentrate? 'I do my best to keep everything in check.' I told Desmond, not being playful about it. 'I'm going in here, to do my thug thizzle, and then come out a winner.' 'I know you'll do well in the ring, I have to check the pyro and everything so for it thanks to the entire WT. Its not that I'm worried about. Outside of those ropes, I fear for.' 'Don't worry.' I told Desmond with a smile on my face. 'I'll handle that.' As I began to walk away from Desmond, I heard him shout over my shoulder. 'Dante, that Evans, one tough motha. Tougher than any opponent in this tournament.' Great, just exactly what I need, another person waiting to strike on me. * * * Tulane FieldhouseNew Orleans, Louisiana 1:34 pmDay Two of GTT The stench of my own sweat, mixed with the air conditioning inside of the Fieldhouse was enough for a man to gag upon. I didn't care, the sweat stinging my eyes everytime I lifted the weights off of my chest, I had to get used to it. Here I was, lifting about a good 325 bench pressing, which didn't seem real to me. These weights could come crashing down on my chest, ending my tournament hopes. While struggling on position, two light brown hands happened to save me. I could feel the weight pushed off me and I let my arms relax, by letting my shoulders rotate clockwise. It felt like I had been released from the shackles, so to speak. My eyes, barely open with a smile on my face, I shook my head with a laugh.'Thanks guy, I owe you big time for it.' 'No prob, just as long as your ass gets kicked later.' That voice shook my eyes open, it was all to familiar. I knew who it was almost instantaneous. 'Cordozer...Cordozer Stevenson.' I got up off of the bench and looked at my friend whom I hadn't seen in a few months. He was a bit leveled than me in height. He still looked the same to me, shaven bald head, a few tattoos on his biceps and one on his neck. I lifted more, but he had the bigger arms and the broader chest. He works out constantly, improving on something he failed to capitalize on. 'Its been awhile.' Cordozer replied, along the guitar strings of a Staind song that made me crack up. Cordozer Stevenson, better known as X in some places happened to be my protogee`, the man I would bring into the business. I hadn't heard from him since the 250cc competition ended in June. It was weird, because I first met him during the Making A Star series, regularly seen on MTV nowadays and he knew this part of Louisiana. It was his hometown afterall. 'Sure has,'I said. 'Just what are you doing back home anyway? I thought you were going to Philly for the X Games?' Cordozer laughed, his smile known throughout my days before getting into any serious wrestling thing. 'No, I've retired from the sport, its just not the adrenaline rush it used to be.' 'I hear ya.' The reason why I sounded down and despondent was because Cordozer wanted all of his secrets out in the open, not letting them slow him down. When something happened to be eating at him, you could tell by a flush look on his face. I thought I was close enough with him that we could share past and present secrets, I guess not. 'I actually thought you were done here for the tournament,' Cordozer said, eyeing me. 'Tracy said you'd probably give tournaments one last run, before you focus everything on a federation. I just wanted to wish you luck.' 'Thanks man, that means something from the bottom of my heart. So you came all the way home just to see me man?' 'Erm, not quite.' His body shifted as he took a seat against the brittle loganberry colored bleachers. 'I have some business to take care of.' Once again, I point out that ambiguity is Cordozer's middle name. Its cool with me. If he didn't want to tell me, then leave it at that, no questions asked. I don't push on things, I'm not that hard ass cop wanting to bring me down... 'Mr. Jackson?' I turn around to see a black female, with long raven hair , white blouse, black skirt. Attractive, hell yes, because I thought this woman at first was Tyra Banks. Okay, I'm a Victoria Secret follower, happy? Cordozer looked at me sheepishly. 'Is that your lover Dante? You cheating on Tracy already?' I shot him the middle finger, sending him into hysterics. 'Acutally, I'm not his new love, and I don't know him.' the man replied to Cordozer, and quite nostalgic I might add. 'Mr. Jackson and myself have some business together.' 'Let me introduce you to Ms. James.' I said pointing to her. 'Is that right?' 'Yes.' Ms. James said. 'Could we speak in more,' she looked at Cordozer, nodding her head in his direction. 'Private facilites?' Dozy, which is what I call Cordozer just to piss him off, got the picture and chunked the peace sign towards me, mentioning that we should get together later one. I nodded, as Ms. James moved in closer. I hoped he stayed, two on one would help against this woman, I thought. 'So, could we go to more private place?' she repeated to me once again. 'I know there's a dorm we can -' 'Sorry.' I interrupted to her. 'This is private enough. Nobody is hear to listen to either myself or you talk about personal business Kendall. May I call you Kendall?' 'No, Ms. James will be fine.' I whistled to myself, reeling from the heat and placing my arms in the air. 'Tough crowd. So what do you want to know, Ms. James?' 'Let me be a little short here,' she began. 'I'm here because I have the belief that you happened to be inside of the Mayoral Mansion about one week ago and either assisted or completed the deed in murdering him.' she took out a picture and handed it to me. 'Do you know who this is?' I looked at the picture, the pale man happened to have a slight smile, right during a parade. 'Don't know him. Nice guy, I suppose.''That's not what April Jackson told me,' Ms. James replied. 'Who?' She took out a folder, opening it up and handing me a picture of a light brown haired woman, colored like myself that happened to share that same kind of smile that I had as a kid. 'She said you were in Louisiana at the time of the murder.' she told me. 'Her name doesn't come up as anything, though.' 'That's impossible, I wasn't even near the place. I was stuck in my hotel room on account of the WT wanting everyone to be under a strict curfew. Their wrestlers aren't going to fluke up for this tournament.' I replied. 'But you do know her..' I shook my head. 'Of course not, that woman can be a complete stranger to me anyway. I haven't heard anybody by the name of April Jackson.' 'Are you sure it didn't become more...' Now hold on just a damn minute. Who is this woman to dive deep into my personal records? I wonder. 'I've been with the same woman for nine years now,' I said, not letting my anger slip through my voice. I could taste the coin like feel of blood on my lower lip as I bit my lower lip. 'Would that be Tracy Ellis? Let's be honest Mr. Jackson. You're on the road for the better part of 200 days. It must get lonely...' 'At times it does.' 'And you're telling me that you haven't been with anyone else since then?' I looked her dead set right in her eyes, stern and serious 'I'm telling the truth.' She stared back at me for a moment, before smiling 'Well thank you for your time, Mr. Jackson. I'll be in contact with you as soon as developments in the case begin to come...' 'Case?' I abruptly questioned. 'I'm in the middle of a very competitive tournament. Can't this wait until after its over?' 'I'm afraid not.' she stated. 'To be completely honest with you, I don't care what happens in-bewteen yourself and the profession you happen to work for. I'm going to make sure that the press, and every single falsified sheet in America gets a whiff of this.' 'Aren't you the ever loving bitch?' I said towards her, venomous as ever. 'I just can't wait to expose you. I love it when I expose trash like yourself.' she said. As she began to walk away, I fumed with anger. Already she pegged me and nominated me as the guilty party. She even had a sick smile towards bringing people like myself down into the wall. I only had one response towards her, 'We'll assume who happens to be true and fake, Ms. James.' She left, leaving me to despise her as she now probably despised myself. I didn't know I'd be having to face off against a double headed monster every single night in this tournament. Luck isn't on my side.
