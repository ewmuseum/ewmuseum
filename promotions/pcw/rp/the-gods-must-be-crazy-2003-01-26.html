The Gods Must Be Crazy

26 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1 - 26 - 03 Space: The final frontier. Mankind’s last great crusade is that of the stars. What secrets lie beyond the endless blackness? Is it just a desolate plane of revolving stars, burning nebulae, and wandering chunks of rock and ice? No ladies and gentlemen, it isn’t. In fact, it’s more of a kingdom spread out over millions and millions of light years. There’s no boundary, no border to worry about. The ‘land’, the endless blackness of space, contains the subjects of that kingdom. Different races of organism inhabit just about every planet known, but some are subtler than others. For instance, Jupiter contains a race of larvae-like creatures that feed off of other organisms that happen to find their way to the planet, or the indignant ones that live their already. Or did, anyway. They were extinguished long ago by order of At’Kellam, the lesser god under Sheor, the Lord of Chaos. And like every other kingdom known, it has its lord and kings. The Lesser Gods being the Lords that control the smaller aspects of the Universe, while the chief Gods, the Tiamats, rule over everything in existence. Even the subtle thoughts running through your mentality are influenced by their divine power. These beings are so incredibly powerful that if they were to go to war with each other, existence would cease to exist and would return to the one being that the Tiamats wrestled it from in the first place: Chaos. Chaos was a monster, to put it plainly. It’s very difficult to understand just what Chaos was, but it’s there. Imprisoned in some hidden location millions of light years from Earth, no one is to ever find Chaos. If Chaos was found… or worse, RELEASED… even the great Heroes of old could not stop him again. For as long as there is strife, hate, bloodlust, deceit, lying, and other forms of negative energy, his power grows. But it doesn’t matter at the moment, for the Tiamats have him safely locked away. They have other things to worry about. Like kings or rulers, the Tiamats must attend to the creatures, resources, and other things at their disposal. They cannot simply let things stay as they are, for if they did, evolution would be a concept spoken of in myths. They are to take care of their creations… when a planet has a hiccup in the form of a gigantic storm formed by the psionic waves emitting from Chaos’s location, the Tiamats must rush to solve it. That includes Earth. And actually, that’s exactly what they’re dealing with right now. The ‘palace’ for these Gods and Goddesses of Astrology is in a location tucked away in the winding tunnels of energy and time called ‘black holes’. The structure is so incredibly huge that only a plane without the concept of density and volume can hold it. Describing the structure would taken ages, so focusing on the most important part of it shall be our concern. We will journey into the Lunar Room. A meeting has commenced between the Tiamats on a subject of panic. A problem has arisen… and the meeting commences. The room itself is like a great black void with a large, beaming blue crystal in the middle. Its aura shines brighter than any star and stands as beacon of the Tiamats power. Contained within is a portion of their divine abilities, the ability to create Universes and entire races. Let us join these beings as they take the matters of Earth into their hands. “[b]DEATH TO YOU, DESIMA!!![/b]” “Calm yourself, Tartorus!” “Let the creature expel his anger, Zenander. It will only cause him grief.” “MAY WE GET ON WITH THIS?” Seems as if they’re having trouble. The one that cried out for the extermination of Desima, who Zenander called Tartorus, emerges from the black void with a bright silver light and seems to float in mid-air. Rather strangely, he appears to be a humanoid/bull creature that stands on two hoofed legs. Great, dark red hairs line his body and black armor covers his torso. His helmet is fearsome: It’s open in the front with cheek guards extending to a point at his chin. The helmet is made from some hard, black material with 6 great white horns extending from the sides, 3 on each. The bull’s face flares with anger and frustration, which is reflected in the blue crystal. Each time he says something, bits of flame spew from the depths of his throat. His mighty, snarling voice booms across the void like a thunderclap. “[b]SHE’LL NOT SPEAK TO ME AS IF I WERE A MERE ACOLYTE, ZENANDER![/b]” “Then do not act as if you were one. I ask only for your patience in this matter, Tartorus,” The Goddess known as Desima says softly. Her voice is light and sweet, floating through the air the song of birds. Despite her quarrel with Tartorus, Desima appears calm and collected. “While your temper disturbs me greatly, Vain One of the Stars, I suggest we settle this dispute for the betterment of the Tiamats.” Desima smiles warmly beneath the shining white veil upon her face, putting forth a friendly gesture. Upon her body is an endless dress of white, floating farther and farther off into the void. She seems to stand as if she’s on something, but no solid surface is visible. Being the Goddess of The Way, or Gravity, she’s able to control her position anywhere in the Universe. She’s also the calmest one of the ground, which helps in moments like this. “[b]BLAST YOU, DESIMA! AND BLAST THE TIAMATS! WE’RE JUST SITTING HERE WITH OUR THUMBS UP OUR-[b]“ “SILENCE!!!!!!!!!!!” Instantly, a harsh quiet falls over the void. The voice booms with the force of 1000 supernovas, silencing the mighty Tartorus before he can finish. The source of the voice then steps forth from the void: Zenander, Lord and God of Matter. Able to control and change any physical, chemical, or supernatural substance, Zenander forms the very planets that organisms live on. Mountain ranges are at his beck and call and the fires of the Great Star yield to his might. He cannot, however, CREATE matter… just merely change it. “I will have no more of this quarreling in my Council! While the great En’Flarim is away, I am in command. Now, you shall silence this bickering, Tartorus, and we shall discuss the matter of Earth when the others arrive,” Zenander announces, crossing his arms. Reluctantly, Tartorus obliges. “[b]Yes, Zenander…[b]” “Yes, my lord,” Desima smiles, having no problem with calming Tartorus and the fight down. “Good! Now, first of all, on the matter of En’Flarim…” Zenander approaches the great blue crystal and gazes into it softly. His cyan-colored skin shines in the presence of the crystal while his silver eyes glow with a majestic, peaceful aura. His face is almost human, with a sharp nose and long pointed ears. His hair is a beautiful, shining silver color and flows to the shoulders of his light blue and golden cloak. His voice is deep and firm, but peaceful. “We believe the Goddess has gone rogue on us. She has somehow managed to escape the Great Star and has made sure there are no means of contacting her. I assume she has gone to Earth…” “[b]So??[/b]” Tartorus blares, as he’s not a very patient God. “En’Flarim is no longer the Conqueror, but she may still have an agenda against the mortals of Earth.” Tartorus scratches his helmet with his great clawed hand and shakes his head, flaring his nostrils and sighing to himself. “[b]Zenander, spare me Earth’s troubles. I wasn’t brought before you today to help with Earth.[/b]” “I see his grudge against Orion continues to his very hour…” Desima chuckles beneath her breath. Tartorus snaps to her direction and growls angrily. “[b]WHAT DID YOU SAY?![/b]” “SILENCE!!!!!” Zenander roars once again, tiring of the bicker. Tartorus’ nostrils flare with angry flame and he crosses his huge, muscular arms. “I apologize, Zenander…” Desima proclaims softly, lowing her head. Zenander rolls his eyes and just nods in acknowledgement. “It’s fine, Desima. Now, may I continue?” Both Tartorus and Desima nod their heads and prepare to listen to Zenander as he continues to explain the situation of Mother Earth. “En’Flarim, our Goddess of All, is supposedly on Earth. That cannot be the best of things, for it was the great hero Lunus that vanquished the majority of her power to the core of the Great Star. He was Earth-born, one of At’Kellam’s creatures, so En’Flarim vowed revenge…” “[b]We all remember the story, Zenander! It was a time of turmoil, blah blah blah… I’ve done worse in my time. What’s the problem[/b]??” Desima just shakes her head, dismayed at Tartorus’ attitude. “Calm yourself, Tartorus! I’m getting there,” replies Zenander, with a hint of aggravation in his gentle voice. “En’Flarim could engulf the Earth in flames if she wanted, extinguishing all life!” “Which ISN’T a good thing, Tartorus,” Desima snaps, cutting the great bull God off before he can make a hostile comment. “Exactly, as Earth is our main source of faith and we’re drastically losing in. There are few who believe we even exist, let alone rule from on high!” Zenander exclaims, trying to compose himself. He’s right; the grim truth is that the Tiamats are falling out of favor, which will mean “death” for the Gods and Goddesses if they lose their followers. “[b]So what shall we do, Zenander?![/b]” “I can make no decision without the council of Sheor, as you know, Tartorus. I’m afraid he’ll not care one way or another, though, so it may come down to just us.” Desima nods her head reluctantly, understanding fully. The thought of vanishing into Nothingness because her followers deserted her isn’t a very pleasant idea, but she knows the Tiamats have overcome such things before… “Fear not, Zenander. We have means of contacting our disciples on Earth and regaining our favor.” “[b]Says who, Desima?! We have not had our presence felt on Earth since we had to destroy that race of insolent reptiles and their leaders![/b]” “Tartorus, just-“ “He’s right, Desima,” Zenander intervenes, almost reluctantly. Desima gives him a blank stare while Zenander himself merely frowns sadly. “It’s going to be difficult. We cannot just go there like En’Flarim. We would have to possess a body and that’s against Tiamat rules.” “[b]THEN WHY DOES EN’FLARIM GET TO DO IT?![/b]” “She makes the rules, Tartorus. She can defy any of them and there’s not much we can do about it… we’re at her mercy, after all.” Tartorus curls his lips in an angry smirk and draws what appears to be a large spear made of shining blue crystal. He raises it in the air and bellows loudly, making the very entity of the blackness shake. “[b]MERCY?! HA!!! THE TIAMATS ARE AT THE MERCY OF NO ONE!! I SAY WE GO TO EARTH AND CLAIM IT AS OUR OWN, THEN PIN EN’FLARIM DOWN IN THE CORE![/b]” Barely phased emotionally by this call to arms, Desima shakes her head and sighs sadly. “That’s crazy, Tartorus. There’d be no way to fight En’Flarim, especially if she has possessed the Lunis Superia.” “[b]The WHAT?[/b]” “The Lunis Superia,” Zenander replies, shaking his head. “The reincarnation of Lunus, the hero of old. How do you know of this, Desima?” “Merely logic, my lord. You’d think that would be her target,” Desima replies, having a great amount of faith in his theory. “The Lunis Superia is rumored to have great powers deep within his being.” “I know of the legend, Desima. It’s also said that the Lunis Superia will be born by lightning, so what is your point?” “I would think that En’Flarim has the means of finding out if the Lunis Superia exists, then go about the means of possessing it.” Zenander remains silent… what Desima says is true. En’Flarim is a force to be reckoned with on all fronts. And since the Lunis Superia is supposed to be the Astrology zealot, who rains down fire and brings one religion to the land, it makes things more difficult if she has possessed him. “I understand that, Desima. So we must find some way to contact our followers on Earth… any ideas? “[b]I’m telling you, let us rain death and plague upon the denizens and force En’Flarim out of hiding!![/b]” Tartorus bellows, obviously not thinking. “Quiet, Tartorus!! Desima, have you any ideas?” “I believe we should send a message explaining the situation and then letting THEM handle it until we could do something else.” Zenander looks up into the endless blackness and just kind of stares, thinking quietly to himself. He knows it’s risky… and he knows there’s a lot on the line. An entire sect of Gods and their creations could be virtually erased due to the stubborn human bastards… but then again, the Tiamats must RELY on the humans to help solve the problem. A choice has to be made, and Zenander knows that. Giving a long, heavy, reluctant sigh…Zenander agrees. “Alright, send the message. And have them return it to us via the Kaydin Crystals. I want to know everything that happens.” “Yes, my lord…” “[b]PFFT![/b]” Desima then raises her palm up and forms a strange, purple crystal the size of a baseball out of mid-air and holds it to her lips. She speaks softly into the surface, obviously recording the message she’s supposed to send. The crystal glows as the words are collected, and when she finishes, she holds it up for Zenander to see. “Prepared, Zenander.” “Good… send it.” The Goddess of The Way lets go of the crystal, but the thing doesn’t even budge from its position in the air. She pulls her hand back slowly, stares deeply into the crystallized message, then thrusts her hand forward. The crystal shoots through the void and disappears into the blackness… where will it land? == 5 Days Later == In the barren plains of the Africa, a lone tribal warrior stalks a zebra. His tribe hasn’t eaten for days and their children are dying. This zebra is a matter between life and death… so the warrior MUST succeed. He approaches the feasting zebra oh so quietly… not wanting to make a sound. Closer…closer… [b]EEEEEEEEEUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUU!!![/b] A sharp whistling noise pierces the ears of the warrior and suddenly, his head feels like it’s on fire and his vision blacks out. His body gives away and he falls to the grass. After about 10 minutes or so, he finally recovers and sits up, feeling the giant burning spot on his head. Something has left a HUGE bump and a very poor feeling… and that something happens to be a glowing crystal in front of him. He stares at it for a moment before jumping up to his feet and pointing his spear at it. He lets out a bunch of strange, hostile clicking noises but nothing happens… [b]Zuuurrr[/b]… The crystal begins to hum softly. The warrior approaches it and pokes it with his spear, which is a bad idea because it lets out a jolt of electricity and shocks the poor guy, sending him scurrying away back to his tribe. [End.]
