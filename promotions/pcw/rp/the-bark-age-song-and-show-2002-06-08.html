The Bark Age Song and Show

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 8, 2002 I had my directors cap on, along with my directing gloves and script, and suddenly I had been transformed. Yes, ladies and gentlemen, I was no longer Legend Inc., the hero that you knew and loved. I have been transformed into Tom Lexian, director. What was I directing? Well, you’ll just have to wait and see. The studio itself was huge, with large lights beaming down onto the stage like a beacon in the night, and workers constantly moving about. They all had headsets on, which was obviously ultra futuristic, and had serious looks on their face. I was usually serious enough for everyone, and I didn’t like this one bit. We were here to have fun, not to sit here and frown all the time. “Hey, hey, hey…” I said, clapping my hands to get everyone’s attention. “I’m looking around and I see a lot of sad faces and frowns around here. I want to know why. I want to know what we’re all afraid of.” Faces turned to one another, all afraid to say what was on their mind. I crossed my arms, making it painfully clear that I wanted an answer. A woman holding a clipboard stepped forward. “It’s because of our former director. He was a real dick. He would make us work long hours and never let up on us.” “Maybe he just wanted to make a quality product.” “I doubt it,” She replied. “I think she liked to see us suffer. He was evil.” I cracked a smile. “Is that so? Well, that’s a good thing then, because the picture we’re doing today deals with that very topic.” I began to pace around all of the workers as they whispered amongst themselves. “What do you mean exactly?” She asked. “The last picture we did had to do with gremlins and ogres, so we’re a little tired of doing stupid stuff.” All of the crew members nodded in agreement. “It’s stupid,” I replied with a smile. “But it’ll be fun. Very fun. Let me introduce myself. My name’s Tom Lexian.” A man steps forward nodding his head. “Yeah, I knew you looked familiar. Now I remember you. You’re that wrestler guy for PCW. I’ve seen you on TV.” “That’s right,” I said, pointing at him. “But now I’m behind the TV, and I’m looking to make one hell of a promo today. I really want to stick it to these two guys, Dark Star and Clyde, for all the hell they’ve shown me. I want them to know what I truly think of them.” “What are you going to do to them?” He asked me eagerly. “Well…” I began to say. I suddenly jump on a table and began to dance as my song begins… (Sing to the theme of “My Boyfriend’s Back” by the Angels.) Tom Lexian: The devils back and you're gonna be in trouble CHORUS OF STAGEHANDS: (Hey la, hey la, the devils are back) Tom Lexian: If you see them comin' better get your ugly glasses CHORUS OF STAGEHANDS: (Hey la, hey la, the devils are back) Tom Lexian: They've been spreadin' lies that I was untrue CHORUS OF STAGEHANDS: (Hey la, hey la, the devils are back) Tom Lexian: So look out now 'cause my Duke’s comin’ after ya! CHORUS OF STAGEHANDS: (Hey la, hey la, the devils are back) Tom Lexian: Hey, he knows that you've bee tryin' And he knows that you've been lyin' (The CHORUS OF STAGEHANDS begin doing the twist.) Tom Lexian: He's gonna beat ya for such a long time CHORUS OF STAGEHANDS: (Hey la, hey la, the devils are back) Tom Lexian: Now he's back and things'll be fine CHORUS OF STAGEHANDS: (Hey la, hey la, the devils are back) Tom Lexian: You're gonna be sorry you were ever born CHORUS OF STAGEHANDS: (Hey la, hey la, the devils are back) Tom Lexian: 'Cause he's kinda big and he's awful strong CHORUS OF STAGEHANDS: (Hey la, hey la, my hey the devils are back) Tom Lexian: Hey, he knows I don’t be cheatin', Now you're gonna get a beatin' (CHORUS OF STAGEHANDS begins to stomp on the floor with their feet like they’re beating up on Clyde and DarkStar.) Tom Lexian: What makes you think he’ll let you keep your eyes? (hmmm) (CHORUS OF STAGEHANDS begin to play peek-a-boo.) Tom Lexian: You're a big man now, but he'll cut you down to size (hmmm) Wait and see (Tom and the CHORUS OF STAGEHANDS begin to hop around the stage.) Tom Lexian: My Dukie's back he's gonna save my reputation CHORUS OF STAGEHANDS:: (Hey la, hey la, the devils are back) Tom Lexian: If I were you I'd take a permanent vacation CHORUS OF STAGEHANDS: (Hey la, hey la, the devils are back) Tom Lexian: Hey, he knows that you've bee tryin' And he knows that you've been lyin' Tom Lexian: He's gonna beat ya for such a long time CHORUS OF STAGEHANDS: (Hey la, hey la, the devils are back) Tom Lexian: Now he's back and things'll be fine Tom Lexian: Hey my Dukie's back , So look out now 'cause my Dukie’s back And I can see him comin' , So you better start a-runnin' , Yeah, yeah, yeah As my song ended, everyone erupted into cheers. I waved into the crowd and gave a little bow. Why, I knew I was a great singer, but I didn’t know they were going to adore me like this. Perhaps I was in the wrong business. “That was amazing!” The man exclaimed, starting to do some pelvic thrusts. I quickly stopped and him. “Calm down there bro. We aren’t here to start some ruckus, just to have some fun. Now, where are our actors? Are they here yet?” “They’re in the back…” I hear a loud crash behind me, and as I turn around, I see two slobbed out, overweight, large pieces of cow dung meandering towards me. Although I was first disgusted, my disgust gave way to a smile. These were my actors. “Dark Jar!” I exclaimed, giving the large, piece of cowdung with a DarkStar t-shirt on a high five. “How are ya man?” His eyes became wide in disbelief and he began to ramble. “And as ashes we came, to ashes we shall return! The death of the stars only bring the genesis of all that we know is good, but an evil tide will and we shall see nothing but blood…BLOOD…BLOOD!” “Uh-huh…” I walked away from him as he continued to shout away at someone else. “Slyde!” I gave the large, piece of cow dung with a “Clyde” t-shirt on a shot in the stomach with a punch. My hand became wedged in between layers of fat and I had use quite a bit of force to get it out. “That tickled…” He gave his belly another rub and had a dumb look on his face. I shook my head. “No, no, no!” “What?” He said with a sheepish look on his face. “Do I smell too bad?” “No…well, yes, that too. But that’s not what I was going to say.” I put my hand on his shoulder as we began to walk towards the stage. “You see, in order to play this part, you have to pretend that you are EVIL INCARNATE. Do you understand?” “Do you mean like the devil?” I snapped my fingers in unison. “YES! That’s exactly what I mean. Now, I don’t want you to be to serious up there, but you have to really tap into this evil character. I want you to really believe that you are a bad mama jamma. Can you do that for me?” He nodded his head like a little child would and gave me a thumbs up. As Slyde and Dark Jar ran up to the stage, they began to do their evil acts up there. You know, trashing furniture and spray painting the walls. I cued my special effects guy. “Hit the pyro spectacular!” Flames shot up from the stage, causing Dark Jar and Slyde to jump back terrified. As the entire stage caught on fire, I suddenly realized that I had hit upon a magnificent hit. This was it. This was the true Dark Age, the people that wreaked havoc and chaos everywhere. As everyone ran out of the studio, I stood there with my hands extended outwards. “The Dark Age is here, and I have captured it!” Fire and brimstone can’t stop Legend Inc. What makes you think you can?
