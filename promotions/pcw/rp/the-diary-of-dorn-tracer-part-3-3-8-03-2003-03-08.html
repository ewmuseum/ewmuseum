The Diary of Dorn Tracer - Part 3: 3-8-03

8 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3 - 8 - 03 Written: 3-8-03 Friday Fusion… what a piss poor night it was. On certain fronts, anyway. While they weren’t many, they were significant enough to leave quite a scar on me. But I suppose I should relate the good news first: I retained by Shooting Star Title and defeated Eric Wolfson personally. I shut that fucking green-tongue up and sent him packing, licking his wounds with a cowardly shadow in his eye. He stood up against the Ace of Astrology and fell victim to the celestial energy that courses through my veins. Through En’Flarim’s guidance and my own true will, I overcame three other people and was the one with my hand in the air. I suppose that’s all right, but the bad news certainty weighs more than the good news. As I sit here and write, my hand trembles with grief. I committed a grave sin against someone who has supported me through thick and thin. I practically failed them, and for that, they may never forgive. I don’t have many I can rely on, and I never have. It strikes me like a blazing long sword when I lose the trust or favor of someone for something I’ve done. I don’t know what I could do to make it up to them… it boggles my mind, breaks my heart, and tears my soul to shreds. I could feel tears welling up in my eyes as I thought more and more about it after my match. How could I have done such a stupid thing…? I let En’Flarim down. Why, journal? I made a fool of myself in front of millions and brought shame to En’Flarim and the entire Lunar faith. My match was lackluster against the three blasphemous cretins and I did nothing to cement myself as a formidable force. Yes, I am proud that I beat Jade, du Prez, and that bastard Wolfson… but what did I gain? I retained my title, but it is now stained through having to be drug through such a shit fest? That cannot please En’Flarim… not at all. I tried contacting her through various forms, but I could not reach her mentality. I felt her presence leave me after the match… I felt weak. I had to stumble to my locker room as the very essence of my being had been torn in half. May the stars and moon strike me down, journal. There’s only one chance to regain her favor. I perform the Blackest Ritual at March Madness, in front of everyone. The centuries-old ritual to the Moon will be the perfect remedy to his problem. I know she and the other Tiamats will appreciate it. It will cleanse myself and the air of impurities and imbue me with newfound strength. It’ll also be perfect in the sense that a full moon will be out that night. It was be the ultimate testament to the Tiamats. Though it will be draining and tedious, it must be done… I will cease to exist with En’Flarim. I must regain her favor and prove to her that I truly am the Lunis Superia, and her true warrior. I have vanquished blasphemous dogs left and right, but the river of blood can only run so far, journal. I must perform the Blackest Ritual, in front of everyone. It will be the first major Astrological ritual ever performed in front of non-believers, but I cannot worry about such things. I will be regaining En’Flarim favor AND imbuing myself with enough strength to overcome the 29 bastards that stand in my way of the ultimate prize. Shining like a thousand suns, it shines so beautifully, journal. But yet, I own one of its children in the Shooting Star Title. A truly wonderful prize in itself, the Heavyweight Title should complement my greatness along side it wonderfully. Therefore, these are my goals: 1. Perform the Blackest Ritual.2. Win the March Madness Battle Royal3. Obtain the PCW Heavyweight Title4. Regain En’Flarim’s favor Quite a journey that lies before me. But with faith and spirit, I will strive forward and meet my enemies head on, journal. I will make En’Flarim proud and my enemies’ knees buckle in fear when they know of my approach. I will enter the Battle Royal… and I will succeed. Other than that, journal, the night went rather well. Not to change the subject, or anything. There’s not much more to say; we’ll just have to wait and see how far I get. On another Fusion note, I accidentally broke Jessica’s deceased father’s necklace. That fucker Wolfson ran into me in the corridor and I dropped it. She was rather pissed. But oh well, I’m sure she’ll get over it. It’s just a fucking necklace, and there are other things that need to be done. Besides, I can always buy her a new one. Well journal, time to wrap this up and go train for March Madness. A fellow Astronomer contacted me, and he thinks he’s got a training regime ready for me. We’ll have to see. Write you later. And with that, Dorn closes his journal, lies the pen down, and walks away. [End.]
