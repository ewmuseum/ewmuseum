Who in Sam Hell are the Schmoopies?

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-5-03 Opening up to the living room area of Trinidad’s apartment the same way we left it but moments ago, in shambles. This time A.J. and Amira sit together on the black leather couch as Adam Stryker is seated opposite from them on the ottoman, still in shackles, but his gag has been removed. Reaching for the remote, A.J. mutes the television before speaking. “That was close dear,” A.J. begins, “I almost actually dropped Adam there, 34 floors to his death.” A.J. begins to laugh but any humor is all but severed when Amira pipes in. “Anyone care to fill me in on just what the fuck is going on here?” “That’s easy” A.J. interjects before Adam can open his mouth. “Adam is a dick licker who felt it necessary to come back after leaving PCW and attack me, teaming himself with Smoke. He’s all pissed cuz he could never beat me, and takes it personally that I am better than he.” “You make is sound so simple A.J.,” Adam replies. “I had no choice in the matter, I watched as you came in, dominating your first match, I knew you had what it took to be here. When I came to PCW I lost right off the bat, jobbed even. That was the straw that broke the camel’s back, it was a complete sum up of our whole relationship, everything just came to a head at that point. I realized I was at my best when I was against you, you brought that out in me. I could no longer fake that humor and tom-foolery was the life for me, that is your life, not mine!” “Well that makes sense,” Amira agrees not considering how A.J. would react. “You should have just been honest from the start Adam.” “REGARDLESS”, A.J. says obviously not caring about Adam’s purpose, “You know how I can get Crespo back! You hold the key to me finding Smoke and kicking him a new asshole right through his old one. You’re the bait now!” Adam shrugs in his corner giving in to his predicament, “You’re probably right A.J., but the thing is, I don’t know where Smoke is. We always met at an anonymous, predetermined site, he never took me in to his hide out. But you do have a match with him tomorrow, against the Schmoopies for the Continental titles right?” “A.J.?” Amira questions. “You have a match tomorrow? And Seymore Smoke is your partner?” “Well, what the hell was I supposed to do!” A.J. exclaims very defensively. “This was the only way I could figure to get Smoke into the same building as me, and who the fuck are the Schmoopies?” “Tessa Windsor and Chris Storm.” Adam replies. “Chris Storm!” A.J. yells out, “he’s actually a real person? I thought he was only used as a ghost story that straight heterosexual males made up to scare their kids.” A.J. alters his voice a bit so it’s a bit deeper before continuing with his impression, “ Keep playing with those Barbie dolls ya little bastard and you’ll turn out like Chris Storm. It would have been enough to scare me into playing with balls instead of dolls.” Amira -smirks and shakes her head. ”Well Adam, I'm sure that you'll get many chances to “be all that you can be” as A.J's adversary, but for now, we've got a missing marsupial that must be returned as soon as possible!” A.J. leers at Adam. . “Which is why I need to win this fight against the Schmoopies and kick Seymore's ass until he gives me the correct location.” Amira just shakes her head again and cracks up. A.J. blinks at her. “What so hilarious?” Amira looks up and wipes a tear from her eye. “Oh nothing...just....well....I thought Seymore Smoke was a retarded name, but I think even his cliched ass is beat.” Adam shrugs, “I don't think it's so bad for a pet name.” A.J. retorts. “Oh just schmut the hell up.” Amira chuckles, as she sinks more comfortably in her seat. Adam ha-ha's, “Yeah, creative, you just better hope Smoke doesn't get any creative ideas with your kangaroo.” A.J . grabs the remote once again, aims toward Adam and clicks “mute button” repeatedly. “I told you to shut the hell up, I'm gonna find Crespo, and Seymore better not have gotten ideas of any kind, or else I'll be creative with him too. Adam snickers and says beneath his breath, “Yea, make him swallow your jokes and hope he chokes on the cheese...” A.J. gives up using the “mute button” and flings the entire clicker at Adam, hitting him square in the head. Adam groans and shakes his head at A.J. “I'm gonna have so much fun kicking your ass over and over and over...I don't think it'll ever get old. “ A.J. looks around for something else to throw at him, and fails. Amira watches him as he abruptly gets out of his seat. Amira looks at him puzzled, “Where are you going?” A.J. replies “Nowhere hunny..what makes you think that?” and before she can reply, he removes the cushion he was sitting on and grabs hold of it, creeping toward the shackled Adam as if he's a bout to smother him. Amira shrieks “A.J.! Sit down! Goddammit. Don't suffocate him!” Adam shouts out almost tauntingly “He wouldn't do it! He doesn't have the balls to. Besides, why would he do it?” A.J. closes in, leering into him menacingly “Anything to shut that mouth of yours.” “A.J.!” Amira hollers out again, “this is neither the time nor the place to be messing around with such minor obstacles, you need to remained focused on this match with the Moonpies or whatever the hell they call themselves.” Amira looks at a picture on the PCW booking flyer for the Continental Title tournament and begins to laugh again, “these two were going to get married?” A.J. shares in her laughing, “Well, I think they still are, it’s just that PWA keeps fucking up their plans.” “Didn’t PWA come out and actually bless their wedding, saying they wouldn’t interfere any longer?” Stryker cues in with a question of his own. “Ya know, I’m not really sure.” A.J. responds. “One thing is for sure,” Amira states while still looking at the picture, “I wouldn’t be surprised to read that these two aren’t cousins, I mean look at the resemblence, it’s uncanny.” Amira shows off the picture as Adam and A.J. both finally agree on something. “God forbid they should ever procreate” Amira says after abruptly ceasing her laughing attack and taking a serious tone. “Think of the poor children.” Adam begins laughing hysterically out loud but soon stops when Amira swipes the couch cushion out of A.J.’s hands and smothers it across his face. “Ugly, disfigured children are nothing to laugh about ass face, head!” Amira scolds. “These two must be stopped, if even for a night, if even for a shot at any success, for the love of humanity, of all things that are just and right! Alright, I ran out of all the cliché lines I know, just go out and win baby, then we can get Crespo back, dump Adam over the balcony, and get back to normal life again.” Making it sound all so simple, Amira removes the cushion from Adam’s face, then abruptly punches him in the balls. “I’ve always wanted to do that!” The scene fades as Adam collapses on the floor and Amira jumps into A.J.’s lap on the couch.
