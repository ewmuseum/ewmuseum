Spendin Money (part 1)

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18.10.02 The scene opens up in a dark room in Duzza’s Hampton mansion. The room is full of smoke, from the blunts that Duzza and his homeboys, Playaz Inc members Statuz Quo and Dante Jackson and Duzza’s close friend D-Kay Calyx have been smoking. The four, African American men are sitting around a table, playing a game of poker. Duzza is dressed in a pair of baggy white pants and a black t-shirt, with a big, platinum chain around his neck. D-Kay is dressed in a pair of black jeans, a white tank top and has on a white nike air hat. Statuz is dressed full in red, red pants, t-shirt and bandanna, and Dante is dressed in a pair of baggy black pants and a black t-shirt. Stautz: “A yo Duzz, dawg, ‘sho was nice of yo’ to let us, use yo big ass house, fo’ dis game of p-p-p-poker” Duzza: “hahaha” Statuz: “What chu laughin’ at dawg, what chu laughin’ at?” Duzza: “I have no idea what you just said, hahaha” All four men burst out laughing, but then Duzza’s half black half Asian queen/wife Brittany hollers out at Duzza Brittany: “Malek! Get yo ass up here now!” D-Kay: “Oooh, Duzz’ in trouble, Duzz’ in trouble” Duzza: “A’ight, I’ma be right back, don’t be lookin’ at ma cards” Duzza, then gets up from the table and goes upstairs to his wife. Dante, also gets up, but just looks around the room. Statuz: “Dante dawg, don’t think Duzz wants you lookin’ through his stuff dawg” Dante: “Yo, peep dis! Duzz done left his check book here! And every single one of the checks has been signed! What the fuck?” D-Kay: “You know why he does that don’t cha?” Dante: “So he ain’t gotta write his signature in the shop?” D-Kay: “Got it in one my man” Dante: “Ha, I gots a idea. Let’s go shoppin! Wit’ Duzza’s check book!” Statuz: “Maaaan, don’t chu think ma nigga would notice!?” Dante: “Aww c’mon dawg, Duzza has got more money than all of us! Which one of us lives in a mansion? Duzza. Which one of us, has a recording deal? Duzza? Man, we’ll tell him!” Statuz: “A’ight, a’ight” Just then Duzza comes back into the room Duzza: “A’yo, what y’all doin’?” Dante: “Duzz, change of plan, we goin shoppin!” Duzza: “Fo real?” D-Kay: “Yeah… I got my eyes set on a Gold-plated, limited edition Technics SL-1210 MK III's, with a gold-plated Vestax Custom Mixer, and a chrome finish Kenwood amplifier” Dante: Yeah, and I got my eyes set on a solid gold microphone!” Statuz: “Yeah, and I got this urge to give a large cash donation to the African Apartheid” Duzza: “The what?” Statuz: “Nigga please! Don’t you watch the news? It’s that thing, to help the white farmers, who was thrown of they land by the blacks in Zimbabwe, man don’t chu watch the news!” Duzza: “I gotta stop smoking….swear I just heard you say you was gonna donate cash to a charity for white people!” Statuz: “Haha… let’s go” So, of go the four men, as high as kites, of to spend Duzza’s money, and Duzza doesn’t even know about it. (end of part one)
