Behind the Ego of Jason Snow: Family and Relationships, Chapter 5 - Final Decision

Date: not entered What color is this night? He's been sitting on the floor in the corner of the 'blue room' for hours now - since Krista left for God knows where with God knows who. His head rests against the wall and his eyes are sharply aimed at a shelf on the far wall that's filled with toys that he's sure will never be used. His eyes are glazed over. Nothing in this room will be used. It's all empty. He's leaning against the barred wall of an empty crib - he remembers being so happy when he bought the crib just a few months ago. Now it only serves as a dark reminder of a dead future, a dead dream, and a dead child. The crib will remain empty. Its blue blankets are left useless, sitting in the crib for the mere purpose of being touched from time to time by a mourning parent. ...so empty... His mouth lips are parted slightly - motionless. Jason Snow is broken. He knows that he'll never fully recover from this experience. How could he? With all the constant thoughts about how happy life could have been. He doesn't think that blue is a happy color anymore. Blue, like the crib, will always be a reminder. Blue may be the color of the ocean, but people drown in oceans. There's a teddy bear under his limp right arm. He wonders if these emotions are normal - maybe everyone who loses a child to miscarriage goes through this. Maybe he's being silly to think that there's no person on this planet going through the same pain that he is. Or maybe he's not being silly. Maybe it's not only the miscarriage that has brought him to this point, he realizes for the first time - Maybe it's the way he and his wife seem to grow distant every six months or so. Maybe it's the side of himself that he saw when he cruely clutched a fistful of Krista's hair and tossed her against the wall. Maybe it's the way he sees more and more of his monstrous father, David Snow, in himself every day. Maybe it's a collection of things. He's holding a gun in his left hand. What would God think? He's always had a loose following of Christianity. Is God really watching, he wonders? Does God see him sitting limply in the corner, holding a gun in one hand and a teddy bear that would be his son's under the opposite arm? Is God crying, unlike Snow who has refused to all this time? Does God care? The gun is the same antique pistol that Krista found in the chest in his trophy room. It looks ancient, but at the same time, all too dangerous. In Snow's mind, it has the power to end finally put an end to everything. It can finally get him out of this nightmare, even if it can't give him back the dream life that he was living only a few short months ago. Maybe the gun can take away his overwhelming guilt for what he did to Krista just a few days ago. He can vividly remember the scene, though he wishes he could just wipe it from his mind. He remembers her crying so hard, her face contorted and soaked with tears - trying so hard to defend her boyfriend, who she later claimed to love. Snow doesn't regret hurting the boyfriend, but he knows that no amount of time will take away the mental image that he has of Krista's face painted in such fear - fear of Jason Snow. He doesn't want to be a monster - not like his father. He remembers every line on the man's face, knowing that now - especially with the thick blond beard - he is a picture of the man. Jason Snow hates David Snow ... Jason Snow is David Snow... Tap... tap... tap... Snow's only motion is tapping the barrel of the ancient gun against the hardwood floor as he searches the corners of his mind for any doubt that he might have. So far, he's found none... For a brief moment, he considers the possibility that this action might sadden the people he cares most about. He's sure that there will be a period where they'll mourn, cry, and mabye even turn to his photo and remember happier times. Snow knows all too well, however, that times aren't always happy. He believes that if they'd only look at the big picture, they'd realize that they're all better off without him. What's he good for, anyway? Finances? They'll get all of his finances anyway, after the Will is read. In the end, he believes that the pain he will cause them - all of them - will far outweigh any joy he might be able to give them. He can't control the way he hurts people... ...The way he's hurt his wife... She longs for him while he's gone. But much like David Snow, Jason Snow is a chronic workaholic. Even in the face of his wife's recent miscarriage, Snow hasn't been able to pull himself from the ring. He's continued on in PCW and the Wrestling God 3 tournament as if nothing has happened - but something has happened... so much has happened... He hurts her every day, and she's been hurt so much now. He knew she was hurting when he was on the road too, but it didn't stop him. Every time that she couldn't get him on the phone - the life support of their marriage - because he was still at the arena, it hurt her. He's always so emotionally detached. If only she knew, Snow thinks to himself, how strong his emotions really are - despite being buried down in the pit of his stomach and only surfacing in the form of terrible acts. Suddenly, he wishes that she had never met him. He's never been there for her... ...The way he's hurt Krista... His young sister-in-law looks up to him so much, taking great pride in the simplest signs of appreciation from him. But his expectations of her are so high and every mistake she makes saddens him to the point of madness... to the point of monstrous actions... He still can't fully believe he assaulted her a few days ago. He doesn't understand why he did it - he just knows that he did it. That's the worst part, knowing his actions but having no explanation for them. It reminds him so much of David Snow... ...And what about Haley?... He cares so much for the tiny child. She's so small - so fragile. He would do anything for her. He's tried hard to be a model parent... but it's hard to be a model parent from thousands of miles away, and it's harder to be a model parent when there's a monster living deep inside. But would is it possible that Snow may one day turn on the child? He can't conceive it... but then again, he would never have been able to conceive getting violent with Krista until it actually happened. Would, one day, Haley get in the way of one of his violent rages? Snow lowers his head and taps the gun's barrel hard against the floor... Clack From the time Haley was born, he swore that he would do anything to protect her. From the time Krista was thirteen years old, he swore he would do anything to protect her. From the time he first laid eyes on his wife, he swore that he would do anything to protect her. And now, he must face the possibility that the biggest threat in any of their lives is himself. ...He takes a long hard look at the antique, dusty, gun in his left hand... He's been sitting in this tiny corner of this empty room, leaning against a crib that will never be used, evaluating his life and how it effects other people. There's no way around the facts - they're better off without him. All of them. But what about God? His mind reverts to thoughts of an all-powerful being whose looking down at this sad, yet motionless, situation. Does God agree? Does God understand that Snow is making this decision in the best interest of his family? Does God know that Snow is only trying to protect them, like he's always tried to do? And if God doesn't agree, does he care? He must not, Snow thinks, or He would have shown up by now to stop Snow from taking this ultimate, and final, action. Maybe, just maybe, God knows that everything is so wrong that this, indeed, is the single action that can clear the slate. Besides, if God had any objections, He would simply show up and take the gun out of Snow's hand, wouldn't He? So here it is - the moment of truth. What is Snow's decision, and will it be his final decision? His mind wonders to all of the pain he's brought the people he cares about. He's hurt them all so much... However, he also can't help but remember them smiling - enjoying good times. He can't help but think about the way he felt the first time he saw his daughter, Haley, smile. And of course, he was doing very well in the Wrestling God 3 tournament. Snow thinks these things over for a moment. 'Fuck it!' Suddenly, he picks up the gun, stuffs the rusted barrel in his mouth and pulls the trigger. Click
