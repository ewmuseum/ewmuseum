Hulking up, and I don't mean the Orange and Red

12 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 12, 2002 The amulet was warm in my hands, as it felt the sudden surge in adrenaline and emotion that I was feeling. It could feel my heart racing and my heart pounding, and it was trying to keep my calm. Your emotions can sometimes can the best of you. Tom Lexian: [looking back at Thinh and Robert] I’ll be right back. If I’m not back in ten minutes, come after me. Robert Yarney: [ding-dong in his mouth] Mfffffff, ve’ll che therrrr vor voo. Thinh Alexander: [shakes his head] I think he means we’ll be there for you, Tom. Go ahead. Save the girl. I didn’t anyone to tell me that a second time, so I immediately leapt off of the side of the tower, feeling the air beneath me. The amulet gave me the power to jump higher and farther than a regular human, but I could not fly. It was one of the powers I wish I had, yet I could survive a large jump of fall. As I saw the ground approaching, I braced myself – hell, even the Executioner gets a little jumpy. BOOM! CRASH! SMASH!? I tumbled head over heels, rolling on the ground with a trash bag stuck to my head. I tore it off, swearing under my breath. I had little control over what happened when I actually hit the ground. I’ve never seen guys like Spiderman or Superman make tumbles like that, but hell, I don’t exactly see Tom Lexian lunchboxes being toted by small school children either. As I brushed myself off, my heightened sense of hearing allowed me to hear the murmuring of voices in the distance. I looked about myself, adjusting my vision so that I could see clearly in the dark. It helped to zero in on vamps who loved to hide in unlit areas. The better to sneak up on you, I guess. The voices got closer and closer, until finally they were so close that I could hear their heart beats…. …two men, one smoking a cigarette. The other is humming a tune…”2000 and Gone” by Anathema. I guess he had taste. I leapt out from my hiding place and dropped kicked one of the men, sending him flying towards the wall. He bounced back with a vicious thud and fell to the ground unconscious. The other man was too shocked to do anything, and the cigarette that was in his mouth fell out as he gaped in shock. Tom Lexian: Who are you? What do you want? Where did you take the girl? The man just stared at me, and then wildly swung his arms. I suppose he was trying to hit me. Fancy that. I ducked his first blow, landing a knee into his gut. He doubled over in pain, now hunched over my knee. Tom Lexian: [screaming] HI-YAH! I landed my patented karate chop to the BACK OF YOUR MUTHA FUNKEN HEAD~! He collapsed to his stomach, no longer moving, but I could still hear his heartbeat. It was rapid – he was still conscious. Tom Lexian: [taking the man’s head in his hand] Not so tough anymore, are you? Why don’t you surrender now and I won’t have to show you the karate kick to the BACK OF YOUR MUTHA FUNKEN HEAD~! Feels like? Criminal: I..I...I give up. I do. I really do! Tom Lexian: Then cooperate with me. When I ask a question, you answer it. Do you understand the rules of this game? Criminal: [becoming more and more hysterical] Yes, yes, yes, yes, yes…. Tom Lexian: Ok, and stop saying the word yes. You’re starting to annoy me. Now, where’s the girl? Criminal: [confused] Which girl do you want? I got all sorts of them. Blonde girls, black girls, asian girls, big, busty girls… Tom Lexian: [agitated] The girl that was just screaming for her life down this alley. You know, the one you were chasing. Criminal: Look man, I have no idea what you are talking about. Me and my friend were just walking down this way when… I tugged at his hair a little harder, making him squeal a bit. Tom Lexian: Your friend? You mean your scum bag acquaintance? Criminal: [voice becomes a pitch higher from the hair pulling] Owwww, yes, yes, him. Tom Lexian: You mean to tell me that neither of you had anything to do with that little girl? You both are completely innocent? Criminal: [pauses] Well… Tom Lexian: That’s what I thought. Scumbags like yourselves don’t just walk around aimlessly because you want to. You’re obviously guilty of something. Just spit it out. He spits out a wad of gum. Gosh, that was fairly disgusting. Tom Lexian: [looks at the chewed up gum] What the fuck are you doing? Criminal: [sobbing] I stole that piece of gum from a store around the corner. I’m SO sorry. I’ll pay for it, I swear. Just don’t kill me. I let the man go, hanging my head in desperation. I had just caught two rogue guys, not the men that were capturing the little girl. How could I have been so stupid? So careless? God knows what those evil tyrants are doing to that poor, innocent little angel… OH DEAR GOD! KEEP HER AWAY FROM ME! KEEP HER AWAY! Suddenly a man’s body is launched out of nowhere and crashes against the man I just captured. I step back in shock and look towards the shadows were the body originated. Tom Lexian: [in a demanding voice] Who’s there? Answer me! A small girl stepped from out of the shadows. Wonder Girl: Duh, it’s me, Wondergirl. It sure as hell took you long enough to try to rescue me. Tom Lexian: [bewildered] Wha…who…Wondergirl? Wonder Girl: That’s right. What? You haven’t heard of me? Oh, that figures. All of you heroes are all alike. You have superhuman powers, you kick ass, and yet because you’re a girl, no one notices you. Not unless you’re Wonder Woman, and you’ve been blessed with the two breast of apocalypse. Tom Lexian: [embarrassed] I’m sorry, I didn’t mean to upset you… Wonder Girl: Upset? Do I look upset? We both look over at the man she just creamed. Do I really need to reemphasize the fact that she just kicked his ass with very little help needed? Wonder Girl: He didn’t really upset me. But it sure as hell did take you awhile to save me. Are you new at this? Tom Lexian: [shrugs] Well, sort of. Wonder Girl: That puts an interesting spin on this mission then. Tom Lexian: [looking confused] What mission? She grabbed my hand and sat me down, telling me that this was going to be one hell of a story. Now, I’m not a father, but it’s sort of weird seeing a little girl leading around like you are the…well, little girl. But she was nurturing, in a sort of mother type way. Too bad she could squash my hand into pieces if she wanted. Wonder Girl: What do you know about the Hulk? Tom Lexian: Hulk Hogan? [starts to pose] HULKAMANIA, BROTHER! Wonder Girl: No, not that Hulk. The other Hulk. The green guy with big muscles. Tom Lexian: Akiyama? She smacked me. Tom Lexian: Oh, you mean the Incredible Hulk. Ok, I got you. Gamma rays, right? Sure. Wonder Girl: He’s been running a muck lately. I mean, he’s usually a good guy, but he gets these rage attacks sometimes. Usually we have the avengers or someone stop him, but this time everyone’s out of town. Tom Lexian: Where are they? Wonder Girl: Big party at Spiderman’s house. Apparently he’s made some new drink called the “Webby.” Every superhero in the tri-state area was invited. Uh, I guess not everyone. Tom Lexian: Why aren’t you there? Wonder Girl: What are you stupid? I’m too young to drink. Besides, Spiderman pisses me off. He thinks he’s so smart and great just because he can stick to walls? I stood up from my seat, waving my arms around. Tom Lexian: Sorry Ms. Wonder, but I can’t help you. I’ve got a match on Tuesday, with my title on the line, and I can’t be banged up because the Hulk decided to take a bite out of my neck. Wonder Girl: But you’re our only hope. Otherwise this city is going to be one big smear on the map. Tom Lexian: What makes you think I could stop him? Wonder Girl: [shrugs] I don’t know. Some guy named DarkStar told me you were the man to call. Mother fuck, man. Mother fuck.
