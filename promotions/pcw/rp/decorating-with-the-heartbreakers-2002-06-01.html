Decorating With The Heartbreakers

1 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 1, 2002 The crystal blue pool water glistened in the backdrop of the large PINK! house. The house that has become ever so famous in shooting promos for The Heartbreakers in PCW. Two lawn chairs graced the backsides of Jack Valentine & Johnny Heart as the sweat dripped down their chins on this sweltering hot day. The sun bounced off of their heart-shaped sunglasses ever so brightly. Almost simultaneously, the pair reached to the small table separating their chairs. From it they pulled two very feminine glasses with girly straws. Inside? Well none other than the drink that they pleaded with PCW to make it the official drink...PINK! lemonade. Of course, we all know how that turned out. They sipped and sucked from their glasses...no pun intended...then carefully set them back down on the table before embarking on an intense decorating conversation. “There is just something that’s missing in our yard Johnny,” Jack promiscuously said. “Well, let’s see, we’ve got the PINK! umbrella, the PINK! pool, PINK! chairs...what else could we need. Our house is the ultimate PINK! festival,” Johnny said. “Flamingos,” Jack added. “Flamingos? What are you talking about Jack?” Johnny questioned. “We need some flamingos to spice up the backyard area. That is exactly what is missing and that is exactly what we need!” Jack enthusiastically said. Jack rose from his...seat...and ran inside. Johnny just tipped his heart-shaped glasses down ever so slightly to see what in God’s name Jack had up his sleeve. Seconds later Jack came sprinting out of the house with the Yellow Pages in hand. He hustled back to his chair, spun around and flopped his behind right back on the chair. “Can I ask what you’re doing Jack?” Johnny intrigued. “I’m calling Jean Pierre...I’ve needed a reason for him to come over here for weeks and now this is it!” Jack screamed. “Jean Pierre? Is that the guy with the...” Johnny was cut off by Jack. “Oh...yeah,” Jack responded. In a very quote on quote, “gay” fashion the Heartbreakers seemed very anxious about the arrival of Jean Pierre. Whoever he was, they both seemed to have a particular liking for this man. Jack and Johnny both ran inside and threw on some cologne and two PINK! robes. Several minutes later a small, cherry red, Mazda Miata pulled up. Both of The Heartbreakers were peeking out from behind the pulled open blinds in their front window. A small, French looking man steps out of the car, dressed in an Armani suit with black sunglasses. “Look at that...that is one fine piece of machinery,” Jack said. “I would love to ride in that over...and over...and over again,” Johnny said. “You know, we’re rich enough now, we could probably buy several of those,” Jack said. Almost simultaneously, both of the Heartbreakers look at the disgusted cameraman, who is almost ready to drop the camera out of sickness, and shrug their shoulders. “Alright, we know it doesn’t look as great as the other ones that we have rode, but looks aren’t everything,” Jack said. The cameraman’s face gets even more abhorrence to it. “Yeah, I mean look at how cute the little thing is,” Johnny continued. The cameraman sets the camera down on the chair and begins to walk out, waiving his arms frantically above his head. “What is it lowly cameraguy?” Johnny asked. “What is it?!? WHAT IS IT?!? I’ve been putting up with your gayness the entire day, and now I have to hear you gawk about another gay man. I can’t handle this anymore!” The cameraguy screamed. “Gay? What on earth are you speaking of?” Jack obliviously questioned. “Did you hear yourselves talk about that man that is walking up the driveway right now? That’s disgusting!” The cameraguy replied. Jack and Johnny both look at each other and smile. They each put an arm around the unsuspecting, and unwilling cameraman. “Man? We weren’t talking about any man...we were talking about that cute little Miata out on the street. You’ve got nothing to worry about,” Johnny said. “Miata?” The cameraman started. “Just the Miata,” Jack said. The cameraman nervously picked the camera back up and Jack and Johnny each slapped him on the butt just as the doorbell rang and Jean Pierre made a grand entrance inside The Heartbreaker’s home. “Hallo!” Jean Pierre screamed. Jack and Johnny went over and gave Jean Pierre kisses on each cheek. Jean Pierre was a small, well-dressed man who spoke with somewhat of a Swedish tone. He carried himself very much like Martin Short in Father of the Bride. “Oh my lord, what seems to be the problem...I came as soon as Jack called,” Jean Pierre said. “We’ve got an emergency Jean,” Johnny said. Jean puts a hand over his mouth and gasps for air. “I know, I know...come with us,” Jack said. Jack and Johnny took Jean Pierre by the hand into their backyard. As they swung open the two large doors that lead to the back, Jean put a hand on his head and fainted. Moments later, Jack and Johnny were splashing PINK! lemonade on Jean’s face to wake him up. “I’m sorry Jean, I’m so sorry...we should’ve warned you,” Johnny said. “It’s okay, I should’ve been expecting this,” Jean replied. “I should’ve known this was a bad case of flamingolessitis. Your yard needs flamingos!” Jack slapped Johnny on the back. “I told you so Johnny, I knew it all along Jean,” Jack said. Jean pulled out a clipboard and began frantically writing down ideas for rearranging the yard to fit the flamingos in. “This needs a lot of work boys,” Jean said. “Just like Suicidal Tendencies,” Johnny laughed. “Good one Johnny,” Jack said as he slapped Johnny on the behind. “Suicidal Tendencies might as well commit suicide...there is no room in this world for ugly people like them, let alone wrestlers like them!” Jean pulled out a cell phone and drifted away from the Heartbreakers, frantically talking to someone about getting a “crew” in to work on the house. The scene faded to “Stay tuned for Part II” as it went to the now infamous PINK! screen.
