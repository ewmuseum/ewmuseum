Duke shoots and quits?

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/2/02 (The following is an interview with Duke Williams for PCW’s Magazine.) **PCW’s wrestle vessel has come and gone and has left Duke Williams with another PPV loss. This has left Duke in a bit of a fowl mood as we sat down with the bulky brawler.*** PCW: Thank you for joining us tonight. Williams: Yea, thanks. PCW: Another PPV loss, how say you? Williams: I had some other things to attend too before the PPV. Personal things, I wasn’t able to give the match my all. The right man won and I give Darkstar all the credit he deserves. PCW: So your used to losing at PPV’s at this point. Williams: Are you a sick motherfucker looking for an ass kicking? I’ll jump down your throat and stump on your liver if you ask anymore ignorant questions like that again. You dig hoss? PCW: Let me rephrase that. Are these big show loses getting to you? Williams: During practice I hit them out of the park then when I get up to bat on game day I strike out. Your damn right it bugs me. Your damn right it makes me think. It does hurt me. But this ain’t Oprah and you ain’t Dr. Phil so I’m not going to start crying or bitching or work out my inner demons. PCW: Why do you think you’ve been less then luck at Pay-Per-Views? Williams: Who knows? Maybe I sleep in to late on game day. Maybe I drink to much. Maybe I f*ck to long. I really don’t know. I try to stay consistent and maybe I don’t raise the bar high enough for big shows. If I know don’t you think I would have changed what was wrong? PCW: Yea. Williams: So then why are you asking the stupid f*cking questions? Am I Albert Einstein? PCW: No? Williams: Then don’t expect insight where there is none hoss. PCW: So how are you going to stop the PPV bleeding? Williams: Get real f*cking mean or just stop trying. PCW: What do you mean by stop trying? Williams: Maybe I’m putting to much effort into things or maybe my efforts are just going to waste and I should stop trying. PCW: Retirement? Williams: Oh the “R” word? PCW: So? Williams: I’ll retire the day I no longer serve a purpose to the federation or an interest to the fans. PCW: What about right now? Williams: Are you saying I don’t serve an interest to the fans? PCW: No, I’m asking you where you think you sit. Williams: Do the fans still buy tickets to see me? You bet your ass hoss. Do I still have a role in PCW? That I don’t know. PCW: So maybe your time in PCW is over? Williams: Did I say that? PCW: If you don’t have a role in your own words then is that an option? Williams: If I was going to hop fences to OSW I would have done it when the money was offered to me a long time ago. PCW: So its not an option? Williams: I didn’t say that either. Anything at this point is possible from OSW to Retirement. PCW: Lets talk for a moment about OSW. Williams: Shoot trigger. PCW: What are your thoughts on Legend Inc leaving on the next thing smoking? Williams: Who called it? Who told the fans he was a flash in the pan. Who said history would repeat its self and he would disappear like a fart in the wind like his first PCW showing? HUH? So he’s off to OSW….BFD hoss….Big Fucking Deal killer. What was he? Over pushed? Yup. Over rated? Yup. You see PCW is like a nymphomaniac in a whore house after spending a year trapped on an empty island. There ready to blow their wad on their next young thing that comes by with talent. They did so and guess what?? They ended up with an STD!! PCW: That was a very colorful analogy. Williams: Who the f*ck are you Mr. Rogers? Lets talk wrestling hoss. PCW: So you think PCW is aiming for a younger product. Williams: How old is the world champion? Who are half the kids on the roster? It’s a whole new world but I’m the same old me. PCW: So that hurts you or does it make you more valuable? Williams: I think it does hurt me but f*ck them. PCW: Fuck them? Williams: F*ck them so hard it give’s them all vertigo!! PCW: Who is them? Williams: Those who are holding me back. The young ones who don’t respect me to the old ones who disregard me. PCW: What are you basing that on? Williams: You ever look at those polls? PCW: Sure. Williams: When am I in the top ten? PCW: Ahhhh Williams: Exactly. PCW: Do you think you deserve to be in the top ten? Williams: Are tits fun to play with? Your damn right. I have been the only consistent face in this company who offers in card action and witty promos. I don’t know why I don’t get any respect but I’m getting pretty f*cking sick of it and that will change. PCW: How so? Williams: You’ll know it when it happens. You know everybody put a lot of stock in Shabazz, Legend, that other black guy and where are they now? Gone. They f*cking left. I’m still here. Don’t you think its my time to shine? PCW: Don’t you feel like you are shining? Williams: How can I when I look at the in house polls like the new “talent in PCW” poll. A poll of my peers. My name in that f*cking poll has been mentioned maybe once. FUCK YOU. HUH. RIGHT HERE. PCW: That upsets you? Williams: Clyde gets mentioned. Rush is higher then me? Rush who left me stable to fall on his ass? The guy who loses more then me. He ranks higher then me? PCW: He is talented. Williams: Not the point skippy. Can somebody tell me who the fuck Charlie Loc is and why he ranks higher on somebody’s poll then me? HUH? Did you know psycho ranks higher then me and he can’t even spell his fucking name right!! BULLSHIT KID BULLSHIT PCW: But that’s just the opinion of a few. Williams: I take all criticism to heart. What do I have to do? I just finished to great programs with Kannon and Legend Inc. I’m out there putting the future over while I take it in the ass. Fuck those humps. PCW: So what’s your next move? Williams: Undecided as far as PCW goes. If I do stick around……you know what somebody backstage told me the other day? PCW: What? Williams: That they would love to see me with the TV title. PCW: Nothing wrong with a little gold. Williams: I should take a shit on the floor right here and rub your nose in it for that comment. PCW: I’m sorry I didn’t mean to offend. Williams: Then just shut up for a moment. If I stick around I’m not going to go after no TV title. I’ve been in the continental division for over a year. I’m done with that. I’m sick of watching everybody else elevate above me. Not this time. I’ve watched great champions like Howard go down to people like Boda hold the strap. Not this time. No more waiting for my turn. Action is now. PCW: You want the world title? Williams: No I was talking on behalf of El Diablo, yes I want the title. PCW: What are your thought on Kannon? Williams: A deserving champion who just happenes to be an asshole. PCW: Can you hang with him? He has beaten you in the past. Williams: The only reason he beat me is because of that walking, talking, yeast infection in Ellie. Can I hang with him? Are blow jobs great? Your damn right I can hang with him. Fuck it I can hang him. PCW: What about Nelson and his new outlook on life. Williams: I trust him about as much as I trust Jeff Dahmer to baby sit my son. Nelson I have beaten in the past. If I have to help Nelson win the title just to snag it from him….then good. Fuck I’ll do what ever it takes. PCW: Would you work with Nelson? Williams: I would work with anybody to further my career. I don’t give a shit at this point. What ever it takes. PCW: We wish you the best of luck in all you efforts. Williams: Whatever. Kid, just remember its Malice Time. =w=
