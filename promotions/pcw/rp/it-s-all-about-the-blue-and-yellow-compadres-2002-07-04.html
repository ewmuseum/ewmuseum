It's All About The Blue and Yellow, compadres

4 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 4 [The scene opens on a folding closet door that is painted blue on one side and yellow on the other. The doors magically open to show a vast array of high priced clothing all perfectly folded and hung up. This is all due to the great housekeeping skills of that gnarly Mexican lady, Rosalie Fernandez. In most closets you'd find such a wide variety of colors and styles for any kind of activity. Such as sweats for working out, business suits for that early morning meeting, or just some T-shirts for when your lounging around the house. You find that in the closet of 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, but there is a big difference from his taste and everybody elses taste. Tommy's taste is: BLUE AND YELLOW] [Across the hall, 'Heavy Metal' Harry stares into his closet, which doesn't have a door by the way. Inside is something not really of variety, but of the same nature. Everything is black, but different shades of black to the untrained eye. Most shirts have been worn so many times that under the California sun they have faded and worn down. Some of them have shades of gray in them, but if taking a glimpse into Harry's closet you see nothing but black. Bon Jovi, Quiet Riot, Poison, Stryker, Slaughter, Ratt, and the greatest of them all...Twisted Sister. Those are the bands that grace the many T-shirts in his closet. Shirts that he purchased at concerts and banged his head until things were spinning so fast he passed out in a pile of puke, produced by the drunk bald guy next to him] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Far out, man. [The memories of Twisted Sister's Reunion tour in the mid-ninties just brought back so many great memories. The smell of marijuana in the air. The splashing of beer throughout the crowd. The topless women that wanted to show their favorite band 'the goods'. It was an amazing sight for any man to witness] [Rosalie Fernandez, their middle aged Mexican housekeeper, walks into Harry's room with a hamper being held at her side. She seems a bit frightened walking into the room as there are posters of scary heavy metal bands and marijuana leaves all over. The stench of marijuana is just so strong that she is forced to cough a couple times as she is picking up clothes from Harry's floor] Harry: No, man!! [Harry stops her from taking the crumbled up, dirty clothes] Rosalie: Si, senor!! [Harry smiles to himself, remembering those six years he took Spanish] Harry: Ma Gusta Frijoles, man!! (English translation: I like beans, man.) [Rosalie looks at him with a half smile on her face and she nods. Just agree with the odd looking headbanging American. He'll leave you alone and then you can go and collect all that American money from the good looking, smarter one] Harry: Just leave those clothes there, man. I don't think I wore them yet. Rosalie: Then why are they on the floor, senor? [Harry shrugs] Harry: I don't know, man. Must be the clothes fairies that try to steal them, but can only get halfway across the room. [Rosalie seems extremely frightened now. She just turns and walks out of the room, heading over to Tommy's room. It's a very nice room, much better than the other ones. The bed is already neatly made, the dirty clothes are placed in the hamper that sits by his door, and the room has an odor of the beach. It's such a pleasant smell. Rosalie smiles brightly to herself as she likes this Tommy gringo, and would love for him to get fixed up with her daughter] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Most gnarly Rosalie!! How are we doing today my most bodacious babe? [Rosalie smiles, not really sure what the hell he said] Rosalie: Very good, senor. TTT: Gnarly. Rosalie: Are you going to be here long? [Tommy shakes his head as he still browses through his blue and yellow closet, trying to find something to wear] TTT: No way, Rosalie. We have to head out to make a most bodacious show at Fusion to take on those bogus dudes, Pretty Cool Guys and The Heartbreakers. Rosalie: That is nice, senor. [Tommy has a frown on his face as he looks at Rosalie. She seems a bit taken back, thinking that it was a good thing they were going to this so called 'Fusion'. Whatever the hell that is] TTT: This is going to be a most bogus match. Not only did Pretty Cool Guys cost us the tag team titles, but they are totally heinous N'Sync wanna be's!! Then The Heartbreakers are just totally...well, their totally...PINK!! That is just the most BOGUS!! And I really mean BOGUS thing ever!! Pink is a color that bodacious babes cherish and enjoy. You like pink don't you? [Rosalie smiles, still not understanding what the hell Tommy is talking about. She just nods her head with a very enthusiastic smile on her face] TTT: SEE!! Bodacious babes like pink, not two extremely heinous dudes that can't wrestle. They need to learn that it's not all about the pink... [Tommy pulls out a blue blazer with a yellow dress shirt hung neatly inside of it] TTT: They need to learn that it's all about the BLUE AND YELLOW!! Now that is a man's color, Rosalie. [Tommy places the yellow dress shirt neatly on his bed, which has a blue comforter and yellow sheets, and he shows Rosalie the beautiful hand tailored blue blazer. She seems quite impressed with it, considering it was her husband, Fernando, that did the tailoring on his suit] TTT: The blue is a most gnarly color if placed correctly on the body. When you surf the most gnarly waves your engulfed by the BLUE water that keeps your a float. The water is blue and the sky is blue. Do you think that is a coincidence, Rosalie? [SHe shakes her head] TTT: It's not, babe. God made the sky blue because blue is a manly color and God is a man. The water is blue because the water is just a most bodacious reflection of the sky, but in a most peculiar liquid form. God certainly is a crafty compadre when it comes to creating things. [At this point Rosalie has lost Tommy. God is a woman, and she's hispanic. Everybody knows that!! Tommy then places the blazer onto his bed and now holds the yellow dress shirt in his hand. His bright blue eyes are filled with such confidence and passion. He really loves the colors blue and yellow, there is no doubt about that] TTT: Yellow may not seem like it's a manly color when you take a look at it, but it truly is. The sun, that gives us our most bodacious lighting during day time hours, is yellow. Some see hints of orange into it, but you need a hint of yellow to make orange...right? [Rosalie just nods] TTT: Gnarly. The sun is yellow and powers the earth and gives me this most BODACIOUS tan that I have. [Tommy looks at himself in the mirror and smiles brightly. Knowing he looks totally tubular in most bodacious way this morning. Actually it's every morning] TTT: That's why blue and yellow is such a bodacious pair. What has pink ever done for the world, Rosalie? It's really done nothing but make bodacious babes look twice as bodacious. That is a most radical thing, but it's not really something the world needs. If God were a woman she would have made the sky pink and the water pink. To our most excellent luck God is a man and he knew what he was doing when he made colors. Pink is for the bogus. Blue and yelllow are for the TOTALLY TUBULAR!! [Tommy smiles to himself, feeling so proud that he was able to explain his gnarly hypothesis to Rosalie. The Mexican housekeeper just smiles brightly, hoping that she's doing a good job to show she's interested. After all, Tommy does sign the checks around here and she doesn't want to lose that. Plus she wants her daughter to get together with this hunk of beef. If it's going to be anytime, now is the time] Rosalie: Senor... TTT: Yeah? [Just as she is about to put in a good word for her daughter, Harry walks into Tommy's room with a picture in his hand] Harry: Check it out, man. [The three of them observe the picture and Rosalie smiles brightly, seeing that it's her daughters picture. Harry found it on the kitchen table, which was 'accidentally' left there for Tommy to see. Looks like that headbanging burn out can be good for something around here] TTT: She looks kind of...well... [Harry has a very sly smile on his face. Rosalie stares at Tommy to see what he is going to say about her daughter] [The picture is...well...not good at all. Rosalie's daughter is no Jennifer Lopez, not in the least. She's extremely heavy set, has very oddly placed moles on her face, and looks more like one of those hispanic women that will cook you all the tacos you want] TTT: Well, she's no Jennifer Lopez...that's all I have to say, dude. [Rosalie is completely let down. Her hopes for her daughter to find an attractive, rich American man have completely gone down the drain. This was the shot that she needed. She goes to take her picture back but Harry is already gone with the picture. Where in the hell did that stupid headbanger go to?] TTT: I wouldn't use the bathroom for a while Rosalie. [Her eyes grow wide in horror] Rosalie: NOOO!!! [The scene then fades to black]
