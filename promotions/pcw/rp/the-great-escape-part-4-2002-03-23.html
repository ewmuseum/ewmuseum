The Great Escape Part 4

23 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 23rd, 2002 (Mike Frampton is seen hanging upside down over a tank of boiling water. His hands are chained together as are his legs. However Mike's mouth is not covered. Doctor No and his three minions are near a switch. Sweat drips down the forehead of Mike and hits the rock floor of the damp, grim room. Doctor No has a sadistic, satisfying grin on his face. His hand dangles over the switch. The three men in white coats look on too with grins. Mike stares down at the water then looks at Doctor No upside down.) Mike Frampton: Why are you doing this No? What will it do you to kill me? Doctor No: For the good of science Mr. Frampton. You're death will truly help myself understand the amount of pain and torture the human body can withstand. Mike Frampton: So basically, you don't know that when you boil something it dies? Damn how did you ever get your degree? Doctor No: I got that degree at Clown U and I thank you not to distort it's reputation for turning out the best mad doctors in all the land. Mike Frampton: So let me get this straight. You are going to boil me alive, just so you can see that boiling a human will kill it? Doctor No: Correct. Mike Frampton: Damn it! (Bones enters the room through an iron door. His chest is sticking out with an odd rectangular shape showing through. Doctor No notices him.) Doctor No: Ah hello Bones, how great of you to be here for the defining moment of my career. We finally will get to see what happens when you boil a human. Bones: Doctor No, I have bad news. Doctor No: Bad news? What bad news? Bones: Well sir, we already know what happens when you boil a human. Doctor No: WHAT? Bones: They die sir. (Mike smiles.) Mike Frampton: I told ya. Doctor No: Shut up Mr. Frampton. When did this happen Bones? Bones: Oh back in the day of the Roman Empire or so. Doctor No: My life's work, ruined! Bones: I am sure you can begin work on something new. Mike Frampton: The guy is old, he's done. Doctor No: I can still kill you! (Doctor No hits the switch and Mike begins to be lowered slowly into the water.) Mike Frampton: Ah crap! Bones, help me! Doctor No: Now why on earth would Bones ever help you? (Bones is struck dumb. Without an excuse he is caught. Suddenly he is hit with an idea.) Bones: Doctor No, sir, he is my son! Mike Frampton: What? Bones: Sorry I never told you son. Doctor No: Say what? You’re his daddy? Bones, how? Bones: Let's just say his mom was a drunk. Doctor No: Oh, I see. Hillbilly? Bones: Definitely. Doctor No: Perhaps we should then spare his life. Bones: If you would. (Doctor No pulls the switch back up and Mike stops descending.) Mike Frampton: Jeez! You weren't actually going to do that were you? Doctor No: Well... (A grin crosses Doctor No's face.) Doctor No: I never liked you anyways. (Mike's jaw drops.) Doctor No: Franky get him down. (Franky steps out from where he was standing and climbs several steps. He pulls Mike over and undoes his hands. Mike then pulls himself up and undoes his feet. He then jumps onto the stairs and walks down. He walks up to Doctor No.) Mike Frampton: Did you get all that Bones? Bones: Yup. (Doctor No turns to Bones with a look of curiosity.) Doctor No: Get what? (Bones reaches into his shirt and pulls out a tape recorder. He presses the stop button. Doctor No's look of curiosity turns to rage.) Bones: We never liked you either. (Frampton grabs Doctor No by the throat. He raises him into the air and throws him into the boiling water. The screams of Doctor No are silenced as the police rush in.) Officer McDonald: You three in the corner. We have a warrant for your arrest. Someone get that guy out of the water and take him downtown too. (Officer McDonald turns to Mike Frampton.) Officer McDonald: You Mike Frampton? Mike Frampton: Ah yes, I think so. Officer McDonald: You no longer have to stay here. But you will have to come downtown with us. Mike Frampton: What about Davey? Officer McDonald: He is not under the care of Doctor No so he can not be released. But all of Doctor No's patients will be including all the mimes who were locked away for not speaking. Bones: Thanks Officer. Officer McDonald: No problem. Just doing my job. Mike Frampton: Well Bones I must thank you for all your help, I hope you have avenged your father. Bones: Oh I have Mike and could not have done it without you. Thank you. Mike Frampton: Don't worry about it, well I better get going. (Mike walks out with Officer McDonald finishing the Great Escape which he has only dreamed of for so long.)
