Finally... the DMV. NOW DON'T SCREW IT UP!

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/2/2 And so we open with Slade, Richard, Lamar, and Drek Redaxe standing in line at the DMV. Despite the early hour, they are still stuck at the end of a very long line of bored people that's not moving anywhere. In the back behind the counter, two single 40-somethings wearing respective purple and blue knee-length dresses and sporting puffy heads of gray hair smoke cigarettes while talking to their young niece for her 'Take Your Kids to Work' Day. Patti:'Some days, we don't let the line move at all.' Selma:'We call those days - Weekdays.' Taking a long drag of their Laramie cigarettes, they laugh mischievously. Just then, the Crazy White Police Officer runs in the door. Officer:'Everybody! Please, don't panic, but there is reason to be alarmed. A mad bomber has placed a bomb in the building.' Everyone is panicked, but stays in place. They don't want to lose their line spot, as precious as it is. Officer:'Now, once the building has accelerated to speeds of 70 miles per hour, the bomb will be armed. If the speed of the building then drops below 50 mph, the bomb will explode.' The officer just stands there for a moment, expecting something, anything. Finally, he gets a reaction, though not the one he would have hoped for. End:'Drek, attack.' Drek spears the officer to the ground and mounts his chest, punching him rapidly in the face. The officer covers up, but Drek's blows are too strong to really block. A few other people in the building at the end of their respective lines come to kick him and stomp him into the floor. 1 hour later, the officer, wearing a straight-jacket and one of those Hannibal Lechter mouth-muzzles, is carted off to the asylum, where he won't bother anyone else any more. Richard:'Okay, I'm sure we're in the right place now.' Drek:'Beh'er be. 'Spesh-ly with the big sign sayin DMV on i'.' Attendant:'Next.' Slade:'Fxpoq dhkas mklez, huisd oawnjuh ijwea?' End:'Richard.' Richard:'Again, we need licenses because it's part of the plan that The End has for his opponent.' Teller:'Next!' Slade:'Lkhjalsn liau haihlnads' Richard:'Because!' The attendant gets impatient and shouts. Attendant:'NEXT!' Richard turns around. He is next in line. Richard:'Hello, Richard would like to take the driving test.' Attendant:'Who's Richard?' Richard points to himself. Attendant:'Alright, pay the $20 fee and you can go take the written portion.' He shells out the money and walks into the next room. The next Misfit in line is Slade. Forgoing any speech, he just hands over $20 and walks away. Attendant:'Next.' Lamar comes up to the counter. Attendant:'You with them?' Lamar:'Uh-huh.' Attendant:'That'll be $20.' Lamar reaches down the back of his pants, digs in his ass, and PRODUCES A JAR OF 2000 PENNIES! The teller doesn't even touch the pennies; she just takes out a yardstick and whacks Lamar a few times with it. With the lashing complete, the attendant sends Lamar into the next room. She then calls for the next person while burning her yardstick. Attendant:'Next?' Drek comes up to the counter, but he sadly does not stand tall enough to clear it. Drek: 'I, too, am 'ere fer th' driving test.' The attendant looks up and doesn't see anyone. Attendant:'Who said that?' Drek has to jump to get her attention. Drek:'Here!' The attendant sees him. However, she won't let him take the test. Drek:'Whuh'dya mean t' say Ah'm too yoong? I'm hundreds of years old.' Attendant:'Do you have any identification?' Drek:'Nay.' Attendant:'Then what proof do I have that you are hundreds of years old?' Drek reaches over his shoulder behind his back. Drek:'Ye want proof, eh? How 'boot a look'it me axe?' Drek, of course, doesn't pull at anything except the pants of the man behind him. Drek:'Dahmmit! Fergot me fooking axe ag'gin.' Man:'Hey! Don't touch me there!' The attendant's voice chimes back in. Attendant:'I'm sorry, but based on your height and lack of maturity, I can only take it that you're an extremely hairy little boy. So just sit over in the waiting room and wait for the others to come out. Next?' Drek doesn't go to sit down; instead he just walks right over to the driving part of it. In the written testing room, the Misfits sit next to each other at the same table, ready to take the test. It's not really written, but on computer. Richard and Slade seem well enough, but Lamar's nervous. Richard:'What's wrong?' Lamar:'I'm gonna fail!' Richard:'Yeah, you'll be fine. Slade:'Paoinn ioaej oijsdfn aionuapo.' Richard:'Oh, yeah. The whole illiteracy thing. And you're afraid of computers.' Everyone's silent for a moment. Then, Richard speaks again. Richard: *Shrugs*'Well, do your best.' The testing proctor, Mark Curry in military drill-sergeant uniform (guess where this came from and get 50 points). Curry:'Welcome, maggots! I am Sergeant Bullock and I will be your testing proctor for today. DO I MAKE MYSELF CLEAR?!' Everyone's silent. Curry:'Well?' Everyone's still silent. Curry:'Screw it. Any questions?' Lamar raises his hand from his seat about ten feet away from the front of the room. Lamar:'I'm not good with computers.' Curry waves it off. Curry:'These computers guide you through everything. You'd have to be illiterate not to be able to use it. Anything else?' Lamar raises his hand again. Lamar:'I'm also illiterate.' Curry takes it as a joke that's not funny. Lamar:'Just take the test.' Lamar shrugs and just sits there until Slade reaches over and hits the 'Start' button for him, which by that point is already 15 minutes into the test. Test Questions: 1) What is your name? 2) What is your quest? 3) What is your favorite color? 4) Which sign color indicates road warnings? [A: Red B: Yellow C: Blue D: Green] 5) Before you can drive you must put the _____ in the ignition and turn the _____ over. 6) A STOP sign prompts you to do what? [A: Yield B: Drive like a bat out of hell C: Stop D: Play that funky music, white boy] 7) If a woman asks, 'does this dress make me look fat?' you would do what? [A: Run B: Run C: Run D: Run] 8) You're driving a bus with four men on it; each man has 4 day-planners to keep track of the 4 events he participates in with each one of his 4 wives. Each of the 4 wives have four bags containing 4 animals that have 4 offspring with 4 hairs each of 4 different sections to the strand of hair with 4 fleas on each hair that produces 4 eggs. What's the bus driver's name? 9) ARE YOU WITH THE GARDEN MASTER OR THE HEADMASTER?! 10) This is indeed a strange world. Muhahahahahaha!!! Meanwhile, in the driving portion of the test... Road Testing Proctor:'So you're saying you aced your written portion of the test, Mister?' Drek:'Woot ah, Drek Redaxe, 'm sayin' is th'aht ah did'na ge' a single question wroong on the written test.' Road Testing Proctor:'Excellent! Well, you're a little short, but we can help you with that. Just put on these shoes and take these books.' The shoes have metal rods attached to the soles to help Drek push the pedals. The stack of books shall act as a booster seat. Drek receives his 'driving aids' and follows the Road Testing Proctor to the car they shall be driving in. After the written test, the Misfits compare their scores. Richard holds up his printed score. Richard:'Richard passed, but missed questions 8 and 9.' Slade holds up his printed score. Slade:'Hujaufd.' Richard:'Perfect score? You weren't even awake to read the screen! Give that here!' Richard snatches the score sheet from Slade and takes a look at it. Sure enough, there's a perfect score recorded on it. Richard:'Luck.' Slade smiles and nods like, 'I'm bad.' Then they turn to Lamar. Slade:'Mngf wqeiob diug?' Lamar shrugs. Lamar:'I don't know how well I did.' Richard:'Let's check your score sheet.' They look at his sheet. It records -35. Everyone looks at each other. Slade:'Apsuhpnuifnuiba ieurnailsdufhl?' Lamar:'What's he talking about?' Richard:'Well, since the test is based of the percentage scale of 1 to 100, he's wondering how -35 is even possible. The sheet says that at first the grading computer developed a sense of pity and gave Lamar a perfect score, but then it melted down and sent Lamar's paper through with a grade of -35 it was so bad.' The End joins them. End:'And you only pushed the start button. Imagine how much worse you would have done you actually completed the test.' As the Misfits walk out for the Driving Test, one of the DMV practice cars flies through the air, crashing through the window and landing but a few feet from Slade. There's a lot of smoke coming from the engine that kicks up upon impact. The wreck just sits still for a moment in the cloud of its own exhaust. Suddenly though, something comes out of the car. Drek:'Did I pass?' Road Testing Proctor:'Please, for the love of God and all humanity, don't ever get into the driver's seat of a car again.' The Road Testing Proctor walks away, so shaken that he can't complete any tests for the day. Richard:'What the hell just happened?' Slade:'Dno.' Drek walks up to the window, ready to get his license from the silhouette he sees on the other side of the glass. Drek:'Give me a license.' Attendant:'I can't you failed all of your tests, and we're very picky about who we give licenses to.' A boy the age of 8, a homeless drunk, and others get licenses just being handed away by other man. Drek:'What the?' The End receives his driver's license from the DMV. Drek:'Oh, that's it. If a guy who can't even see can get a drivers-license, why can't I?' The attendant opens the glass and shows Drek a BLT, which she starts eating. Attendant:'Because you're cutting into my lunch hour. Go away.' Drek stamps off to the waiting room and finally just sits until the others finish their tests. The Misfits meet back together after they've taken their driving tests. Lamar is sitting in the corner with a dunce cap on. Slade:'Klugh nuzibskapi?' Lamar:'What happened? All I did was try to open the door, and the next thing I know the car shoots back into the wall, flips over, and bursts into flames!' Richard:'You're a jinx when it comes to technology, you know that, right?' Lamar:'Yeah...' Richard holds his head up high and pats his chest. Richard:'Richard passed. Missed a few things, but nothing major. What about you, Slade?' Slade flashes a set of keys and points to the blue Dodge Neon in the corner. Slade:'Whyelj doof mooslapf aiglbwher vbli ufhgwleir mn.' Richard:'WHAT DO YOU MEAN YOU DID SO WELL THEY LET YOU KEEP THE CAR?' Slade just gets into his new car and drives off. Richard:'Richard is shocked and confused.' Drek rejoins the group and they meet out front to go to their car. They are surprised, however, to see Juju sitting there at the wheel, sporting a brand new license. Everyone is happy and excited for Juju. Except for one... Drek:'Goot dahmmit!' Driving home, The End confers with his Misfits. End:'Good, at least some of you have licenses. My plan for Makonie will still go off without a hitch... We can get by with Richard and Slade.' Juju:'Wonk!' End:'Shut up and drive, Juju.'
