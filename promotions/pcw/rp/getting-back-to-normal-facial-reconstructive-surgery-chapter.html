Getting Back To Normal(Facial Reconstructive Surgery) Chapter 1 -

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/07/2002 Blinding lights shine bright through the arena in a mind numbing torturous haze as if the light its self could infect the very wounds inflicted by those who you would have found to be allies. A young man beaten and bloody lye face up threatened by the sting of the piercing lights. Crowds stand to their feet in praise of the event cheering with deafening yells of suspense, which could cause even the sanest man to go mad. The cold mat in which the young man lye lets off a stench of old sweat and blood as it is stained with both. His eyes roll into the back of his head still trying to catch his full range vision which he had lost in the barbaric beating he had received at the hands of his assailants. Finding it difficult to move his broken bones the young man manages to arch his neck so that he can see above him. But what he finds in his blurred vision is worse then any nightmare a man could have for he is not able to awake. Little rain drops of blood drip down upon the young mans face as for the tenth and final time the spearing end of a steel chair is driven maliciously down into his face shattering his very skull. It would seem that his once good friends had gotten the best of him by taking away what he loves most, that is to wrestle. 'Wake up wake up Lance' A soft soothing voice bellows into the ear of the Perfect One.'It's time to remove the bandages from your face but I can not if you are not awake.' The young injured man mumbles a few words from with in the wraps on his face; the nurse leans in closer to listen but finds it impossible to understand a word of his ramble. 'Try not to speak Lance, I'm going to remove these wraps from your face so hold really still.' The nurse begins to unwrap Lance Perfections face one bandage at a time carefully avoiding touching the newly built face of this poor horribly battered young man. After the bandages had been removed, the young nurse stands back frozen with amazement at the over all perfection of Lance's newly built face. 'Mirror!' Lance demands sitting up quickly in his hospital bed. The young nurse startled out of her trance like state grabs a mirror from a near by medical bed. 'You should find your face to be perfectly reconstructed back to normal' the nurse says in a prideful tone. Lance snatches the mirror from the young woman's hands causing her to jump back with fear of his possible dislike for his new face and the wrath he could bring down upon that hospital if that was the case. He stares into the little hand mirror admiring himself from the left and then to the right. A good ten minutes would have had to have gone by before Lance finally spoke. 'I'm beautiful all my scars that had plagued my face before from wrestling for these few years gone! I don't know how these doctors where able to reconstruct the damage that was inflicted on my face but they did. I look the same as I did when I was sixteen years of age having a three-some in the back seat of my new car my stepfather bought me on my birthday.' A hint of joy gleams over his ravishing new face. 'Yes the doctors did very well but as they say you get what you pay for.' The nurse says interrupting Lance's admiration with himself with a dry joke. The Perfect One turns to give her a putrid look but finds it hard to get clear vision from a distance. He blinks his eyes a few times but still the woman's face seems very blurred. Lance continues to stare until he thinks he has finally figured out just who this nurse could be. 'Multiple Man is that you' Lance says in an intrigued tone of voice. The nurse looks on upon Lance as if he has completely lost his mind. 'Have you gone bonkers?' The nurse replies. But Lance ignores the young woman still thinking that she could very well be The Multiple Man. 'Don't play games with me I know its you. You have that same blurred over face and.' Lance pauses, 'One hell of a nice set of tits you have there Multiple Man. Did they grow in themselves or have you been pumping the juice?' Now Lance has finally gone and went too far. The nurse looks on at him as if he was the devil himself. Obviously offended by his words she moves in closer with in his vision and replies. 'This may be cute in your mind but frankly sir I find you to be an obscene jackass and a rude simpleton. If I never see you again in my life it will be to soon.' The young woman's rage towards Lance causes him to chuckle as he realizes that it isn't Multiple Man at all but just some nurse who had been treating him. 'Calm down Miss' Lance replies. 'I'm just glad that your actually a woman and not Multiple Man, For a second there I thought I was going to have to Paint my dick bright neon pink, dress like Kid Wander and tattoo VER-GINA on your ass!' 'Why I never in all my life' The nurse yells as she storms out of Lance's hospital room slamming the large door behind her. 'Wait what did I say' Lance yells back. 'Oh well, woman are so damn touchy' He thinks to himself while climbing out of bed still admiring his new face in any thing that lets off a reflection.'I have no idea how long I have been in this hospital, but one thing is for sure I am starving and need to eat before I die and waste this perfectly beautiful new face of mine.' He gathers his apron that all patients must wear forgetting that there is no ass end covering his rear end nonchalantly walks out the huge heavy door that the nurse slammed in anger earlier. Wandering around the isles of the hospital Lance finds himself face to face with a vending machine. His mouth waters as he swiftly darts towards the machine. He studies it with sure genius intellect as if it had been the cure for cancer and one false move would botch the whole design. Suddenly it hits him,'I have no MONEY!' Tears almost appear in his eyes as his stomach rumbles like a bear in heat. Then all of a sudden Lance feels something tapping him on his shoulder. He promptly turns finding himself starring down at a young woman holding a pen and note pad in her hands. 'Stand back' Lance yells frightening the young girl. Almost petrified by the sudden out burst of Lance the young girl replies in a nervous tone' are you ok?' The look Lance gives the young girl in return to her question is that of a hysterical mad man. 'Of course I'm not all right. Your UGLY is melting my face!' 'What did you say to me?' The young girl yells. 'Let me see if I can spell it out for you because you must have a hearing problem as well as your unattractive handicap.' Lance begins constructing hand signs as if speaking to her in sign language while making obscene faces at the young confused girl. 'I said your UGLY is melting my face! I'm sorry if this offends you in any way but you look as if you fell out of an ugly tree and hit every branch on the way down. 'How dare you!' The girl yells! Then out of the blue a large hawk claw shaped hand takes flight. Lance's eyes widen as the claw like hand lands smack dab on his left cheekbone. The force behind the blow that the young lady applied on the Perfect One for his rude comments didn't fair well with the reconstruction of his brand new face. Cracks begin to form as if his face had just had an earthquake shatter its foundation. As if torn from the very bone it had been stapled upon the skin that had once made up Lance's brand new Perfect face drips onto the ground in a puddle of mush. The young angry girl screams in a dreadful panic terrified at what she had done. The anxiety in her feet over shadows her feelings in her heart of helping her fellow man. Fleet of foot the young girl removes her self from the scene. Meanwhile Lance falls to his knees in distress gathering the mush that once was his face. ' Someone help me!' He yells but there is no one in site to help him in his time of need. Swift thinking causes Lance to catch a tub of paste out of the corner of his eye. He grabs for it spreading the white sticky substance all over his face then smudging the clump of skin attempting to mold his face back as it had been before. 'You stupid little girl!' He yells 'I told you your UGLY would melt my face!'
