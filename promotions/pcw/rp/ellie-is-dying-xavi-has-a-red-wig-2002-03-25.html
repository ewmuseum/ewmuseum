Ellie~! Is Dying? Xavi Has a Red Wig?

25 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/03/02 The shot opens up at a Ward in the Albuquerque general hospital in New Mexico ahead of PCW’s Sunday Sabotage. Nothing much seems to be happening in the sterile White surroundings until a loud whiny voice, instantly recognisable is heard. “Wow.....I am like SO pregnant, and as well as that, I am like SO ill...OOP, lavish sympathy upon me for not taking proper birth control precautions and being from a stupid country with crappy blood types....” We move down the corridor to a private room where we see Xavier Kannon’s valet Eleanor Hall lying in bed with a Bambi style, doe eyed look on her face. She looks at the camera and frowns, talking in a whiny voice, something she is very good at. Eleanor : “My name is Ellia~! It’s like Ellie but seeing as we have to queue all the way down a street to buy a 2nd ‘E’ in the SO un-rulingful country I come from with our pathetic currency I have to use an ‘A’...Although that makes me sound like an Elia so I am just gonna call myself Eleanor from now on...Okay?” Ellie~! is wearing a pair of Purple pyjamas with Gold stars on and she quite clearly has a pillow shoved up her top to create the illusion of pregnancy. Eleanor : “Please...Donate blood to me as I am all ill....” She adds to this with another doe eyed frown which is obviously fake. Eleanor : “I have a rare blood type that not only makes it near impossible to find a blood match but also means I have to marry someone who suddenly changes their name from Isawa to Alexander....Maybe I could do that with my blood type...” She closes her eyes for a second and screws her face up before opening them in hope for a few seconds before the frown comes back. Eleanor : “Alas...I cannot change my blood type as easily as my husband can change his name from one stupid name to another stupid one just so he can have the same number of syllables in it as part of some romantic gesture....Hence I am still all ill....” The camera now zooms right in on her frowning face, showing her pale Blue eyes and Brown wavy hair with Blond highlights cascading down the side f her face. Eleanor : “So all my husbands effort, the beatings he took at the hands of Boda and the near slaughtering he will take at the hands of Xavier Kannon and Corey Williams will all be for nothing as I am just going to do the World a favour....Take one for the team.....Give the Nurses a rest....Save to Doctors a few hours and open up some appointments in the Midwife’s schedule....” The look of pity fades off to one of disdain as she snarls at the camera and whispers harshly in a venomous hiss. Eleanor : “....By........Just.......DYING!” With these words she laughs evil into the camera as a second, more deeper and masculine voice laughs along side her and the camera pans to show wrestling’s only Scientologist Xavier Kannon. Xavier : “Oh my darling mail-order bride...Please do not die as I didn’t keep your receipt!” Kannon walks in with a look matching that of Ellie~!’s earlier one, a deep frown crossing down his face but his eyes are obscured by a pair of goggles. Over his head where his long Auburn hair usually flows is a bright Red wig and he wears a Hawaiian shirt with a pair of White Karate pants. Xavier : “Also, if you die are Japo - American - Russian - Swiss - Austrian - French - Swahili - Australian - Brazilian - Mongolian and any other nationalities that want to be thrown into the mix baby that you are carrying...Although I can’t quite be sure it is mine as for all I know someone planted the seed then sent you back!” Ellie~! sniggers at Xavi’s line but then reverts to her ill frown and she lets out a quiet groan. Eleanor : “Ohhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh! Xavier : “What’s wrong?” Eleanor : “I remembered I married a man who used to wear goggles and a Red wig....” Xavier : “You poor thing...Want me to pull the plug now?” The Scientologist forms a wicked smile in the left corner of his mouth and as he pulls the goggles up onto his forehead his Green eyes turn back to the door as another bed is wheeled in, this one is empty. Eleanor : “Oh no...Someone else is going to be all poor and ill and poorly like me, maybe they married someone for American citizenship too!” Kannon looks at the bed and a look of sorrow crosses his face, as if someone he knows is soon to be injured. Xavier : “Oh no...You know what that means?” Eleanor : “My ass is too fat for the one bed?” Xavier : “I’m afraid not my funny-named, mail-order Bride...It means that Jessica Howard is already having her bed made ready for when the greatest Scientologist of all time and the man who has been teaching Jeremy Howard about wrestling all over again X-Bombs her bleach Blond head right through the mat!” Ellie~! looks over to the bed, her frown so intense that her bottom lip almost turns inside out and a single tear rolls down her cheek, this would be touching if it wasn’t caused by her dip[ping her finger in a glass of water and touching it to her face just under her eye. Eleanor : “But surely my multi-national, fruity haired husband...You and Jeremy will be able to stop it?” With this line Xavier bursts out laughing, so animated he has to grab the Red wig to stop it falling off. Xavier : “Oh my soon-to-die wife...It is good to see that so close to a much welcomed death that you can still joke! Me, Alexander, Isawa, Ian, Andreas, Indigo, Alaska, Osaka, Dave Miyagawa actually beat Xavier Kannon and Corey Williams? Jeremy Howard and his rather untalented and slightly...Easy...Girlfriend beating Xavier and the next big thing in wrestling...Eleanor Hall?....Why, if that happened I’ll keep the same name for all of March!” Ellie~! feigns more sadness at the news that her friend is hospital bound. Xavier : “I mean look what happened last time I was in the same ring as the great Xavier Kannon, he broke a Snapple bottle over my head and made me loose my TV Title to some guy who thinks he is a set of freaky, Anime obsessed twins!” The puppy dog eyes return to Ellie~! along with the frown. Eleanor : “Oh...That is soooooooooo sad!!! Your complete ineptness, which you have shown so well against Boda and Chris & Corey Chase-Williams will not only lead to little old me dying, but also your son, and maybe even the girlfriend of your best friend....Wow....Sucks to be you!” Xavier : “Sure does...And come Tuesday it will only get worse....” They both adopt their signature smug grins which they aim at each other as the shot fades to Black.
