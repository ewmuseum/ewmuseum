Date: 2-8-03 The picture opens to a wide view of a packedairport. It could be any large commercial airport onany given day, but this was in fact the KingsfordSmith Airport in Sydney Australia, on Tuesday, justbefore 4:00 PM. Panning in closer to the arrival deskpeople scurry around, coming or going, early or late,everyone always seems to be in such a hurry in anairport, as if through their life was dependant ongetting to their destination more quickly than needed.A very attractive woman with dark cherry hair and asleek, sexy build waits patiently for some attentionas the camera is now focused solely on here. Shewaits for no more than thirty seconds before a short,portly woman with an Air New Zealand tag on readingthe name Wanda pulls up in front of her. Wanda: Can I help you ma’am? Amira: Actually, yeah, I’m supposed to pick upa few friends arriving around now on flight number0962? Wanda (flipping through her flightplanner: Well, that is not one of ourflights, but it arrived about twenty minutes ago, butexit was delayed due to some unruly passengers. Amira: What gate did they come in on, andwhich way to get there? Wanda: Well, they came into to gate 7, and it’sright over the… A ruckus tears Amira’s attention away from thetable as a crowd of people sway, parting like the RedSea as a man yells out. Man: SECURITY!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! He then dives out of the way as A.J. Trinidadbarrels through, pushing Adam Stryker on luggage rackon wheels, paying no mind to any other people in theairport, managing to knock a few people to the groundas they go. Amira: Ummmm, never mind, I found them. Trinidad continues pushing Stryker along, all thewhile Adam making loud siren noises as Jaslene chasesafter the two juvenile men. Jaslene: God damn it you two! Knock it off! Looking up and seeing Amira making her way towardsthem, A.J. releases the luggage cart leaving Adamhurling towards an indoor fountain pond. Strykerturns back just in time to see that A.J. is no longerpropelling the make-shift wagon before crashing overthe barricade and into the water. ~SPLAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAASHHHHHHHH!~ Stryker: Mother F*cker! A.J. you A**hole! A.J. simply laughs as he turns to Amira who has arather peeved look on her face. His laugh soon turnsto a puppy dog face as Jaslene catches up to him andkicks him in the back side. Jaslene: We haven’t been inAustralia for ten minutes and you two are alreadygoing to get thrown in jail again! Amira: Again? As in, you’ve alreadybeen in jail once this week? A.J.: Well, that’s a long story, mostly Adam’sfault, but we’re here now Bebe! Amira: And, you’re drunk I see. Jaslene: Yeah, A.J. here snucka couple of bottles of whiskey into his carry-on. When the stewardess refused them a second drink, A.J.popped open a bottle and the two boneheads startedgoing shot for shot while I was passed out cold. Stupid flight sickness pills. Amira: Nice A.J., very nice. I assume you werethe two belligerent passengers who delayed the exit onthe plane. Jaslene: You’re damn straightthose two are the reason. They were playing leap frogdown the aisle. Amira: That doesn’t seem so bad. Jaslene: It was during thelanding! We had to circle the runway twice so thecaptain could get these two morons to sit down andbuckle up. Then, after we land, they lead the entireplane in a rather pathetic rendition of 'Leaving on aJet Plane' from that stupid movie where the guys haveto go to a comet and blow it up or it’ll destroy theearth. I figure that was the second on flightmovie. Amira: Armageddon? Jaslene: That’s theone. By this time, Adam has pulled his self out of thewater and made his way back over to A.J. and thegirls, drenched and looking rather worse for thewear. Stryker: Can we go see Hank now? Huh? Can we? A.J.: Yeah, it’s only another day drive to ourcabin in the Outback, and boy do I need a nap. Amira: I assume both of you boys need to catchup on some sleep, meanwhile, Jaslene and I have somecatching up to do. A.J. (whispering to Amira): You’re notmad at me are ya bebe? Amira (Leaning down to softly kiss A.J. on thecheek): I’m just glad you got back safely,and you can make it up to me later tonight. A.J.: Whoo~! Walking over to the luggage retrieval area A.J. andAdam manage to, albeit rather drunkenly, pick outtheir luggage and follow Amira and Jaslene out toAmira’s mid night blue colored Range Rover. Loadingup the bags in the back, all four climb into theautomobile, Jaslene in the front, A.J. and Adam in theback, and Amira in the driver’s seat. Amira: Get set for a beautiful 25 hour drivehour drive through Australia’s expansive outback. Youshould love the serenity Adam. Adam? Stryker: snore………… Jaslene: That didn’t take long,look at the two of them. In the back seat, Adam lays with his head on A.J.’sshoulder, snoring away, as A.J.’s head falls back, hismouth agape completely passed out. Amira: Oh well, we really didn’t need those twointerrupting the drive for us. We’ll get some goodtime to catch up on things. Jaslene: Heh, I needed a breakfrom them two boys anyway. Get em together and theirjust like young college guys. Amira: I can imagine. How do you do it Jas? Jaslene: I don’t know. I’veasked myself that many times now. But ever since Imoved in with Adam it ha- Amira: WHAT?!? You moved in with Adam? When?Why? Jaslene: Whoa, whoa, whoa calmdown and slow down Amira, one at a time. Yes I didmove in with Adam. It was about six or seven monthsago. Our relationship had progressed immensely and Ifigured that I might as well. Aimra: He is kind of cute, I’ll give him thatmuch but he just seemed like such a jackass in theDWO. I couldn’t stand him. I don’t know what AJ saw inhim. Jaslene: Saw in him? What do youmean? Amira: Well AJ used to tell me how much heenjoyed Adam’s company and how such good friends theyhad become outside of the DWO world. I was confusedbecause when they stood across each other in the ring,there was nothing but pure hatred. Jaslene: Ahhhh…that’s the markof a great actor. I never knew that he and AJ werefriends outside of the ring until your suddenlydeparture from the DWO. Amira: Yeah that was unfortunate but there wasnothing that could be done about that. AJ said he hadsome of the best times of his career in the DWO and tojust pick up and leave like that must have been tough. Jaslene: Heh, you should haveseen Adam when he heard the news. Amira: Pissed? Jaslene: You could say that. Hewas really upset because he told me that he and AJused to joke about what would happen if Adam came backfrom rehab and AJ was gone. At first Adam thought itwas a joke AJ was playing on him. But when he foundout it was for real…well let’s just say that pissedisn’t really the word I would use to describe hisdemeanor. Amira: Well it wasn’t a field day for AJeither. He was upset as hell. He thought that Adamwasn’t going to want to have anything to with himafter that. But much to his surprise Adam remainedAJ’s friend. Jaslene: Well Adam has too fewfriends right now that I think if he had lost AJ itwould have sent him over an edge. He just lostarguably his best friend period and that made him gonuts for a bit. Amira: AJ was telling me about that. I wasreally sorry to hear that. Jaslene: AJ really helped himout though. Amira: Oh? Jaslene: The night out they hadreally brought Adam back to life. I was really worrieduntil they came back that morning. Amira: Yeah speaking of that night out, howexactly did they end up in jail? Jaslene: Well from what I cantell, AJ got Adam really drunk and then used him toinstigate a fight. Adam was arrested for fighting andAJ for instigating but it helped Adam in the end so itdidn’t bother me one bit. AJ called from prison anywayto let me know what was going on. Amira: That’s one thing I have learned about AJin our time together, he’s extremely loyal to those hecares about. Their conversation is broken by AJ letting out aloud groan. He looks up for a bit, blinks and thenlooks down to see Adam’s head on his shoulder. AJshrugs his shoulder sending Adam’s up and out. Adamcomes to life as his head hits the window. Stryker: OWWWW! What the hell? The girls start to laugh as AJ falls back asleep.Adam looks around for a couple of moments beforeputting his head back and falling asleep again. Amira: Those two are just too much sometimes. Jaslene: But they arehilarious. Amira: That’s most certainly true. Suddenly a phone starts to beeping. Jaslenestartles as she fumbles through her purse and pullsout a phone. Amira: What is it? Jaslene: It’s a message forAdam, he’s got a match on Tuesday. Against…SkylarGreen? Who is Skylar Green? Amira: I guess we’ll find out Tuesday but westill have quite a ways to go. Jaslene: Yeah but that’s ok, aslong as those two stay out for most of the ride itshould be ok. The scene fades out as Amira laughs at Jaslene.
