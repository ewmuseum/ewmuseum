Snap, Crackle, Pop!!!

14 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/14/2002 ~What does it take to be a legend? Just ask Jon Kano. A legend is a man who has set the standard, and then redefined the standard after he set it. He is a man who has changed the face of PCW forever. On Friday, Kano takes on 'Controversial' Corey Williams. Corey Williams is the first man who called Jon Kano a legend. Kano liked him for that. Notice that the last sentence was in the past tense. Jon Kano doesn't like it when people talk about his friends or family. Corey Williams has managed to do both. He thinks making comments about sleeping with others' wife or mother is the cool or hip thing to do. This does not sit well with the 'Man Who Rocketh the Mosteth.' On Friday, Corey will pay for his crimes against Kano.~ *The scene opens up inside of a large chiropractic office in Las Vegas, Nevada. Jon Kano is seen sitting the waiting room on a large, white leather couch. He is reading a Forbes magazine and is dressed in yellow Armani suit with a black undershirt. He seems to be very involved in his reading as the receptionist opens the door from the back room and steps out with a chart in her hand.* Receptionist: Mr. Kano. *Kano doesn't react as he is oblivious to the outside world while reading his magazine.* Receptionist: MR. KANO!!! JONATHAN KANO!!!!! *Kano jolts to awareness and stands up. He throws the magazine onto the coffee table and walks through the door and into the nearest room. He removes his jacket and puts in on a hook. He lays down on the chiropractic table as the chiropractor walks in.* Chiropractor: Hello, Mr. Kano, I am Dr. Ruplinger. What seems to be the problem? Kano: Well, I try to get my back cracked as often as I can, but sometimes I just need a good pop or two out of my back. Dr. Ruplinger: Alright. Well, are you experiencing any pain or stiffness in your body. Kano: Well I have a pain in my ass. Dr. Ruplinger: Really? Well it could be a pinched nerve originating in your C-12 disk. Kano: I don't think so. Dr. Ruplinger: Really? Are you trained in the chriopractic arts? Kano: Well, I know what is causing the pain in my ass. Dr. Ruplinger: What is it? Kano: Not what, but who. Dr. Ruplinger: Who? Kano: Corey Williams. He is the pain in my ass. Dr. Ruplinger: Ahh, yes. I know you are a professional wrestler. You wrestle him on Fusion, right? Kano: Yes. I used to like him. I really used to. He was the one who came into PCW and called me a legend. I liked that. He seemed like he actually respected PCW and the elders of PCW who built this company from the ground up. I am one of those people. Dr. Ruplinger: Well has he disrespected you? Kano: Not me specifically, or atleast to the degree where I could care. Then he made a reference to sleeping with Stacey Sloboda. Dr. Ruplinger: That is Boda's wife, correct? Kano: Yes. Chris Sloboda, or Boda, is my best friend. Stacey is one of the greatest women I have ever met. She is a loving wife and a devoted wife. She supports Chris in whatever he does, personal or professional. She is probably the most loving, devoted mother in the world. I see the way she constantly nutures both of her children without reservation. Then Corey makes that stupid comment about something that would never happen for several reasons. Dr. Ruplinger: Like what? Kano: One, Stacey would never, ever sleep around on Chris. She loves him and her children too much to do something like that. Second, Corey is an extremely ugly man. He looks like a cross between Sloth from the Goonies and the ugliest people in the world, known simply as Idahoians. Third, I have heard from some of the groupies who follow PCW from town to town that Corey has a very small penis. Dr. Ruplinger: Is that all? Kano: Then he made a comment about sleeping with my mother. That royally pissed me off. I don't care if a person makes fun or makes a stupid comment about me, but when you bring my family into it, all rules are non-existent. I go postal. I completely lose my self-restaint. Look at Tim Walden, I made him into what he is today, whether he wants to admit it or not. I was booked in a match against him, he made comments about myself, not a problem, then he made a comment eluding to my family. In our match, I kicked the sh*t out of him for five and a half minutes until he decided to punk out and get counted out. I could go on and give more examples, but all that would accomplish is to get Corey Williams to piss himself. I am not a man who's bad side you want to be on. I can make you, or I can break you. I ended Johnny Raindance's career. I put him out for four months, he tried to make a comeback, but he couldn't. So, in effect, I ended his career. Now he is down in New Mexico in wide spot in the road pushing Indian bead necklaces and miniature totem poles while he drinks firewater. Corey Williams needs to recognize that I am the man who built PCW. I was here at the beginning, I was here at our low point, I was here at our high point, I am here now when the future of this company is in question, and I'll continue to be here until I can no longer wrestle and after I am done wrestling, I'll do something backstage. I have made a living off of surviving the waves of self-righteous, over-confident pricks who come into PCW thinking they are God's gift to us all. Then they step into the ring with me. They then look at the lights on the top of the arena ceiling as they lay with their backs on the mat wondering what the hell just happened. Then, it dawns on them that they have just been hit by the deadly duo of moves, the two most devastating moves in the wrestling industry, today, the Kanospear accompanied by the Kanoplex. And that, Doc, is what REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. Dr. Ruplinger: Alright, well lay down, face down and I'll crack ya. *The scene fades to black as Kano begins the chiropractic therapy after his short rant.* ~Jon Kano, the REAL LEGEND of PCW. This man may just be pissed off enough to do some serious damage to the so-called 'Controversial' one.~
