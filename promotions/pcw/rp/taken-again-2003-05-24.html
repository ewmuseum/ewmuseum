Taken Again?

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/24/03 Everything has been building to Supremacy…building towards Matt Jackson’s run at the PCW Raising Star Title. Something everyone believes will shortly be around his waist, everyone hailing him, as king of the new crop. Slowly waiting in the wings, for his chance to step up and claim everything that he’s worked for and take it…to take payment for his hard work. In under three days, he will get the chance to face three other men for the Raising Star Title, three men that he doesn’t fear…isn’t worried about, three men that he has beaten at one point or another over the past month. Showing that he is the one constant in the Division, proving that none of them are, as good as he is…falling short in every contest they face him in, failing to realize that it’s not because they aren’t good, but because they aren’t as good as Xavier Kannon’s boy. Matt Jackson is the man that Xavier Kannon brought into the PCW…brought him in, because he wanted the best, and that’s exactly what he’s getting. Of course, the mission objective is different, Kannon failed to realize something too…when you bring the CIA in on a mission, they don’t stop until the mission is completed. Matt doesn’t want to bring down the PCW anymore, in fact…he’s the one who’s keeping it around, keeping it from being closed by the government like Kannon wanted. A month ago, I wanted PCW gone, just straight off the map…then I noticed something, it had grown on me and I still wanted to keep it open, keep it operating. Odd response with everything that has happened over the past month…Makonie, but it’s one that I am pleased I made and one I am glad worked out. Pushing me closer to the Raising Star Title and pushing my closer to Haley. Matt Jackson is sprawled out on the king sided bed in his hotel room, white comforter pulled up to his barrel of a chest and silk sheets, touching his tanned 5’8” frame, as he lies silently on the soft feather pillows, careful not too hit the stitches in the back of his head and cause himself more pain then he’s all ready in, from the beating at the hands of the Russian Bear and the gauntlet match at Turmoil. Even with the just one match, with Edwards, his body just couldn’t hold up anymore. Sitting next to the bed in a wooden chair, is his girlfriend, wearing a pair of denim jeans and a pink mid-drift tee shirt, which matches her pink tennis shoes and the pink hair tie, that holds back her shoulder length blonde hair. Her head is resting in her petite hands and she fights off the sleep that is trying to overcome her. It’s been her choice to stay up with Matt, but it’s one she knows is right…watching him sleep peacefully getting some much-needed rest. Rest that he hasn’t gotten in over a month, with all the punishment the captors gave him, keeping him awake almost everyday for more then three weeks…on top of the punishment he received from PCW, as a whole. The beatings from Makonie, the matches and most recently the beating he took at the hands of Ivan…all of it, is going to be worth it though, because at Supremacy, after he holds that title up, he will see the payoff and know that it’s been worth it.Without a sound, Haley crosses her legs, letting her right foot act, as support and feeling it sink into the plush white carpet…not taking her bloodshot pale blue eyes off of the operative, glad that he’s finally getting rest. Hoping that this will become more of the common, after Supremacy, but with her knowledge of Matt, she knows this is only the beginning of it all…his quest is far from over and it will continue until he doesn’t have anything left at all and then, only then will it finally be all over. She is fine with it though, because it’s one of her favorite qualities in him. Her eyelids are heavy, as she looks at the digital clock on the metal nightstand next to Matt. It’s the only thing in the room, besides the bed and the chair she brought in. There aren’t any pictures on the power white walls and the only colour in the room, is the pink boarder by the ceiling, which is thinks is so cute, because it matches the pink sheets, that Jackson’s currently entangled in, like a dead animal in a net. She knows he is alive though, from the constant raising and lowering of the comforter on his chest…raising and lowering just like his opponents at Supremacy. Each one moving up and then down, only to raise up again, but just coming up short in the end…the only place that it really matters. Coming up short in the gauntlet, proving once again, why Jackson is the only person who is going to win the title at Supremacy. Slowly Matt rolls over, plopping his head back down on the pillows below, not noticing the cotton bandage that is wrapped around his brown hair, but Haley notices it and the look of pain on his angular face, as he grits his white teeth together, before falling totally back to sleep…the sleep that he’s been in for the past two days, only moving when he heard Haley’s voice and quickly falling back asleep once he knew she was safe. Sleeping right through his PWA Graduation announcement and probably sleeping until Supremacy. Drifting in and out of sleep, the curvy blonde is quickly snapped to full attention, by a knocking on the bedroom door, cocking her head to the side, like a confused puppy, she stares at the door for a few seconds, before finally walking across the tiny room and to the medium sized, power white door. Jerking it open, she curiously looks at the man, who is now standing in front of her…he is mid-aged and his head is completely shaved and he’s wearing black jeans and a white tee shirt…sleeves rolled up, to proudly display his swastika tattoo. Taken a back by the presence in front of her, Haley steps back, her slender body trembling. The man takes a step forward, letting a smile cut his wrinkled face in half, raveling his chipped yellow teeth and letting her get a whiff of his rancid breath, as she continues to back peddle, confused as to what this man is doing here and why he is continuing to walk into the room. Haley doesn’t stop backing up, until her calves touch the side of the bed, sending her toppling over it, crushing onto the sleeping Jackson, who quickly awakes, groggy eyed and in pain…without a word, he looks at Haley, who is almost shaking in fear, as the man keeps walking towards the bed. Looking up, Matt’s eyes light up like the forth of July, noticing Tom Manger…the recruiter he used to get into the skinheads. “Simon…or should I call you Matt Jackson?” Manger screams out. “You never thought I would tune into PCW, you piece of shit? Well, you were wrong…I watched Fusion where you mick ass got dropped by that Russian and then I watched Turmoil. I know everything and you know, I am not pleased at all. I would have come to kill you sooner, but it took a while to track you down.” With a look of shock on his face, the operative begins to speak. “Your right, I used you to get something I wanted…thing is, I didn’t even get what I want, but it doesn’t matter now, because your not going to stop me. I mean think about it Manger…professional wrestlers can’t over come me, you’re going to be able to?” Reaching into the back pocket of his jeans, Manger pulls out a knife…small in size, but deadly with a swipe. He takes a few more steps forward, waving the knife all around, trying to scare Matt, but the only person it scares is his girlfriend…he only lets a smile come over his lips, as he reaches into the top drawer of the metal night stand and pulls out his silenced 9MM Berretta pistol. “Just like a raciest dumbass, you don’t come prepared. You don’t know everything Manger and you never will, because you aren’t going to live that long unless you get out of here and that’s a promise,” Jackson tells him, letting the words roll of his tongue, like a man who has pulled the trigger of his gun countless times and of course he has. Manger doesn’t stop and only continues to walk at the couple, as two more skinheads walk through the door behind him. Before Jackson can get a clear look at them, all three men rush the bed and throw Haley off and knock the gun out of the all ready hurt Jackson’s hand…proving it not to be a hard task. Then without a sound…Manger cocks back and punches Matt, sending him into the land of the unconscious. “I always come prepared Jackson, but I am not going to kill you quite yet. I am going to take this pretty little bitch of yours and see what you won’t do to get her back…should be interesting, Mr. Professional Wrestler, you are going to wish Mark Makonie killed you, the failure of a skinhead,” Manger barks out, nodding his head at Haley, who screams and kicks as the skinheads grab her, Matt defenseless to do anything about it. Without another word, the door slams, letting the knocked out Jackson, hear the cries coming from his princess…able to do nothing to stop them, as he body falls totally limp and he lets out a deep breath and continues to lie motionless in the bed…despite the pain coming from his head…she is gone.
