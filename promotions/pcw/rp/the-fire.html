The Fire

Date: not entered Monday, April 8, 2002: 10:29 a.m. “Where’s Connors, I can’t find him.” “Forget it…. He’s gone” “We are now outside the hospital where the violent fire continues to burn. One can only wonder how many victims who were being treated have now perished as fire fighters continue their brave work.” “I don’t really know what happened. All I know is that the whole placed exploded and I was ejected from the window” “Needless to say, the whole town is in shock and grieving.” “God help us all.” *It was a hellacious day at what’s left of the Jack Kavorkian memorial hospital, and these were some of the sounds that rushed through the burning rubble. But hellacious could not possibly describe everything the man known to many simply as Stryfe went through. Injuries, constant pain, heartbreak and loss only began to paint the picture of the past several months, and that picture was quite a frightening sight to say the least. He stood alone in the raging fires of the hospital wreckage that day, yet he wouldn’t be singed one bit. No, he just stood there basking in its warm glow. He knew the inferno around him was not dissimilar to another fire: One internal, one within Jeremy. It’s the fires burning of those past months…* October, 2001 *It wasn’t long after a national tragedy struck a personal one would follow. Because of his ill consumer skills, Stryfe’s car had erupted into flames, shattering his spine. This didn’t surprise him though. He was convinced for many a time his so-called God had been hoping to deliver a crushing blow, leaving him for dead. However, he never would have thought though that the one person close to him, dare we say it, the one he loved would do the same. Doctor Niviera had walked in the room that day with a somber look on his face. He looked down at the stricken man. Such a sobering look on the face of one who has nearly seen it all could never amount to a good thing. The tears running from Sierra’s eyes confirmed. But the grim news had to be told. There was no other way.* Niviera: After viewing the x-rays we have been able to confirm the damage is irreversible…. Young man, you will never be able to walk again. *A cold silence filled the room, and why not? What was there to say? Apparently nothing at all. The doctor figured his business was finished and began to leave the room. The silence was soon put to a stop however before Niviera could step out the door.* Stryfe: That’s what you think! That’s what you want ME to think. That’s what you want this whole fucking world to think! Who are you to tell me my capabilities?! Niviera: Look, I’m sorry but you have to face the truth sometimes. Things don’t always turn out the way you want. There’s nothing we can do. Stryfe: Then there must be something I can do! I will walk again, I will compete again. I will prove… you… wrong. *How could Niviera respond? Give the man a glimmer of hope to find his way through a dark world of realism? No. He simply turned his shoulder. He left. He left him lying in his own self-pity with a girl who no longer seemed to matter when everything else was lost. What would she matter as she would soon within the blink of an eye connect with a punch Stryfe may never recover from.* Sierra: Listen, I hate to say this…. But I just can’t go through with this. I need to make something of my romantic life. If someone just lies in bed all day there’s no involvement with me and I personally can’t live like that. *Although she only paused a second to breathe, it seemed like by far the someone had stretched that second to infinite proportions as she delivered the knockout punch.* Sierra: I’m sorry Jeremy….. *… And with that she had walked away, not only out of the door, but Stryfe’s own life.* “And those three simple words still echo through my mind.” *A dark, enigmatic soul had exposed its heart to the girl for the first time since he was a child, and she simply stabbed it, embedding the knife there forever. So there it was; dangling and rusty. And there HE was, left to simply rot away for the rest of his days. What was there to do at a time like this? He couldn’t come to anything and nothing would come to him. By all means he seemed to be social vegetable. His only means of sanity was the one thing he feared to do the most: Reaching out to others. Hospital workers and volunteers were the only thing that could keep him from becoming completely muted from society, and would Stryfe really be willing to do his part? Not likely.* *A month had passed now since the horrific accident and Stryfe had not even attempted to pull the dagger from his heart. One fine day a nurse had come into the lonely man’s room to try to pry it out herself. She couldn’t have possibly known what she was in for, but it would have been in her best interest if she did though.* Nurse: Um… Do you mind if I come in? Stryfe: What do you possibly want? You want to rub and smear my newfound disability in my face? You want to join in and continue ruining my life with your words? You want to join in on the fun, hmm?! *Even though the she obviously didn’t know what to expect, the thought hadn’t crossed her mind of how cold he could be. She didn’t let that get to her though as she sat down on the bed next to him.* Stryfe: I’d advise you not to do that. Nurse: I-I’m sorry. I just thought you were lonely and maybe you just wanted someone to talk to. Stryfe: So in other words, you waltz in here and expect me to all of a sudden put my sacred trust in you? Nurse: That’s not what I meant. Stryfe: Please, just leave me be. Nurse: But you don’t understa- Stryfe: I’m asking you nicely… NOW GO! *The nurse was obviously stunned by the mysterious man’s unusual sense of apathy, but to him she at least had enough brains to know when to leave. ‘Slam’ went the door, just like before as young Jeremy was once again left in his isolation of the world, left in the cold. After all his days in PCW many seem to know him. Many would think a smile would have crossed his face in his ‘Perfect Isolation’, but no. Instead he simple buried his face in his hands.* “I always wanted to reach out to others, but I was never brave enough to. I guess I just didn’t know how.” *These are the confessional words of the man. Much like the way a blind follower confesses to his or her sins, this was his sin and he had bowed his head to pray forgiveness. When nothing in the world mattered anymore, he began to realize perhaps all these years he was indeed wrong. Was this world really all so cruel after all? Perhaps not. He was still pondering these things as another month had passed. This time it was Doctor Niviera himself that would try and yank the dagger out with all his might. Even if it killed him he was determined to show something for his efforts.* Niviera: Knock, knock. Stryfe: Yeah, what is it? Niviera: You can’t just keep living like this. Stryfe: And what do you propose I do?! Niviera: Everyone has feelings. You can’t just bottle them up inside of you. Stryfe: And what exactly do you want to do to me? You want to dig out my inner personal demons and expose them to the harsh conditions of this world? You want me to throw you a big pity party so everyone can gather around and laugh and point fingers? You want me to cry for you? *The grief stricken man let out a sigh to prepare himself for a simple yet powerful statement, which for the first time spoke the honest truth, not just another opinion from his corroded mind the powerful truth. With a frighteningly human and emotional tone that’s never came from him before, he says,* Stryfe: No one wants to hear it anyway… Niviera: That’s not true. Stryfe: Who would? You? Niviera: Absolutely. *There was nothing for Stryfe to say after that. All he could do was look on in disbelief that another person, a worthless human had shown him his own feelings.* Niviera: It just so happens I am a fan of professional wrestling. I’ve been following your career ever since it began. Stryfe: Yeah right, since PCW2? Niviera: Ever since the first show. *Amazing. How long has it been since the fateful evening where Jeremy was reborn as the unstoppable force that took the proverbial breath of PCW’s old sister fed away? Already it’s been a year. He rose to the top only to fall down again. Everything that happened, the entire pandemonium that started with his title win. It was time for the truth to be exposed.* Tuesday, June 12, 2001: 2:35 p.m. *It was two days after the biggest victory in his life. Not just triumphant in the ring over Alan Kriegman, but seemingly against his own personal demons that have haunted him for years. He had arrived home and decided to take a walk to get his mind off the anxiety of his title win. Everyone would think Connors was the happiest man in the world. But instead he began thinking to himself, “What does it truly mean”. He knew the weight of the world was now on his back, and there was only one way to relieve the pain. Those demons would not stay down for the count the way Alan did.* Paper Boy: Extra, extra, Kriegman is a bitch! Read all about it! *A young man had approached Stryfe that day in the park. The man knew him very well.* Young Man: Hey, you’re that ‘Stryfe’ guy, right? Stryfe: Maybe I am. Young Man: You must be under a lot of pressure Stryfe: Hmm… Well, I suppose you figured me out there. Young Man: I think I have just the remedy. *As the young back plunges his hand into his back pocket, Stryfe could only wonder… He pulls a small bag out from his jean pocket with a strange white substance inside. Could this really be what he thought it. The youngster’s expression seemed to turn from near expressionless to sickly, distorted smile.* “Heroin…. HAHAHAHAHAHA!!!” *Stryfe’s old vice. The small controlled substance that made him into the mess he was and still is. But what was he to do? He couldn’t even think straight with the title strapped over his shoulder and a cross nailed on his back. He figured there was no choice.* “Fix me…..” *The downward spiral had begun again for him once more. His world span around him, out of control. His performance had been affected and was no longer as many people may have put it, ‘The shit’, just a rotting pile of one.* Stryfe: … And that’s where it all began. *Compelling to say the least. Never had Stryfe any desire to even think about telling this personal tale to anyone, let alone a stranger. But never can anyone be to sure of many things in life. No one would have possibly expected the stricken man had even more to tell.* Stryfe: I guess I could understand why Sierra had left me after all. She deserved everything in this world for believing in me, and I couldn’t provide. Saturday, August 18, 2001: 7:59 p.m. *Night had fallen in the cold night sky above. It provided a chilly aura for the scenery. Maybe it was this aura, or perhaps not, but Sierra sitting alone in her hotel room had a premonition that night. Something would soon be amiss.* Sierra: Where is he? *The door creaked open. A dark figure had stumbled through the door. No surprises, apparently as the figure was simply Jeremy. His eyes told it: He had too much to drink again. Perhaps this was a surprise after all? No, this happened all the time. But once again it seemed to her something would go horribly wrong.* Sierra: Finally, you’re back. Stryfe: What’s yer fuckin’ problem?! Sierra: Jeremy, you can’t keep living like this. Stryfe: I do what the hell I want. Ya gotta fuckin’ problem? *With a nakedly horrific gleam in his eye, he slowly plods over towards the gothic girl with his temporarily blinded judgment. All she could do was simply back into the corner of the room as her boyfriend played his game of mental monopoly, which could have erupted into physicality at any given moment…* Stryfe: That’s all I can remember from there. But I have a question Rick. *A question for Rick?! Indeed it was. Never, NEVER before did Stryfe even have the heart to ask guidance from another human in his life. Just saying this alone was a huge leap forward for him.* Stryfe: Is it so wrong after she left me for dead to lie here in bed for what will probably be the rest of my life? Niviera: Don’t say that. Stryfe: Don’t say what? Niviera: That you’ll be lying in bed for the rest of- Stryfe: Well, well, well, weren’t you the one that told me that to begin with? Niviera: Well yes, I did doubt you at first, but now I truly think you can recover. Stryfe: What made you change your mind? Niviera: Medical conditions are not only a simple physical ailment. Emotions can relate just as much. I think since you have finally been able to talk with others, you’ve finally found a healthy release for your bottled up feelings. Without all the negativity, I honestly don’t think anything can stop you inside. Rehab begins tomorrow. Stryfe: Thanks doctor. *Success. A common, everyday man had now done the impossible and had awakened the hidden trust buried within Stryfe. As Rick turned out the light that night young Connors had dreams of once again walking, once again dominating in the ring, once again truly feeling…alive. Morning came and with that, a hope dawning on Stryfe’s life. He had tried his hardest to walk again, but to no avail.* Stryfe: I just can’t do it. Niviera: I told you, you can’t keep such a negative outlook on life. Now get up! *Doing all he possibly could, the paraplegic pushed and pushed with everything he had…………… he still didn’t budge though. Then a thought had crossed him. One not surprising and Stryfe-esque even if had had started to trust Doctor Rick.* “Remember everything you’ve been through, all the pain you’ve endured. Sierra and PCW. You can’t let them stop you, nothing can go unavenged.” *One more time he dug deep within himself, exerting his hatred as well as his newfound trust into the a concoction of strength on both sides of the yin and yang. He let out a mighty roar…………… Stryfe: I…. did it. *Indeed he had. With that one step, his life had fallen back into place. His insecurities had left him once and for all. He was born once again. Would his third birth be the charm? Within seconds it would be nearly impossible to tell.* Niviera: I knew you could. *Niviera though would be greeted not with enthusiasm, not gratitude, but more apathy. He snapped.* Stryfe: Yes….. What’s it to you? Niviera: What’s wrong? Stryfe: You. Niviera: I-I thought you trusted me. Stryfe: Never. I don’t need you anymore. *Although walking once again, yet still stricken, Jeremy had limped out of the room with the doctors following behind.* Niviera: You can’t just leave now! You have months of rehab ahead of you! Stryfe: Just you watch! I TOLD you I don’t need any of you anymore! Now you must pay for seeing the side of me that was never meant to be. Niviera: What are you doing?!?! *After the past several months of his life had finished flashing before his eyes, Stryfe still stood in the flames that day, immersing himself in his destructive creation. He loved it, but he knew it was time to move on.* Stryfe: So, the fire in me burns again. Many people must now pay as I unleash this power, and this pain I went through. It starts now with an old PCW2 pal: The one they call… Red Rock. *He finally stepped out of the inferno and into the cold gray world once again. It will be a long time before he can adapt to the extreme conditions, but either way, progress had indeed been made already.* Some things never change….
