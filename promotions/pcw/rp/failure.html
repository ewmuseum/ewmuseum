Failure!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: No Clue Failure! Losers! Scum! Trash! A Waste! These are a few words that come to mind when people think about the Black Dragons. Some would say they had the potential to win the OSW Most Extreme Team competition… But NO! they lose and are booted from OSW and find themselves unemployed yet again! They drift over to a second term in Japanese based PURE. In a land they despise they again are capable of going far but through some unknown reason PURE is gone and the Black Dragons are once again unemployed and back home in China… (The scene opens up outside a large mansion in Dalian, China that sits up on a high ridge overlooking the Yellow Sea. It is a quiet evening as the moon illuminates the sky above, but the silence is broken as a loud SMACK is heard followed by a low grunt and a primal growl. The scene shifts quickly to the back of the mansion and down a winding trail leading to a small opening on the shores of the Yellow Sea.) Voice: You are worthless! (There kneeling on in sand shirtless and bloody with their arms raised and tied in a precarious position over their heads to cause pain and to keep their arms from protecting themselves are none other than Chi’ing, Chen Lei-Chia, and Chiang Ho Nam. The three high standing members of the Black Dragons are being struck with bamboo to their backs by their leader Mountain Lord Pai Po, who is obviously angry beyond words.) Pai Po: You three have failed me on numerous occasions! You did not graduate PWA but rather you were just passed along because they could do nothing for your lack of talent! You were then given a great opportunity in PURE, TWICE, need I remind you! But NOOOOO you stunk up that place as well! I bet the real reason why they closed was because they were tired of seeing your worthless attempts at wrestling! (The Mountain Lord let’s his anger get the best of him yet again as he smacks the giant Chiang across his back tearing at the already raw flesh. Chiang grimaces with the pain but other than the smack of the bamboo connecting to the flesh no other sound is heard.) Pai Po: I won’t even mention the OSW fiasco! But I will say this… You three have had opportunity after opportunity after opportunity and you have NOTHING to show for it! (Mountain Lord Pai Po paces back and forth behind the three Dragons. He stops and smacks Chi’ing across the front of his chest causing Chi’ing to break down and whimper in pain. Chen just closes his eyes and tries to tune it all out, while Chiang raises his jaw a bit and takes it all in stride. Lord Pai Po stops pacing as he stands in front of the three tortured failures. He smiles as three sets of eyes stare up at him with disappointment.) Pai Po: Need I remind you why we are going into the wrestling industry? (With no forth-coming answers Pai Po just shakes his head in frustration.) Pai Po: With you three in the Wrestling industry, gaining contacts will be easier, spreading our territory and our influence will be larger and more powerful! But the most important reason is MONEY! With gold straps around your waists the more money you get! The more time you are one the television the more exposure we’ll get! You see, you three are not out there to look pretty! You three are there to dominate! You three aren’t there to make friends! You three are there to destroy your enemies! Now get out of my face! (Lord Pai Po stands there with his bamboo stick caked in blood embedded in the sand at his side and his arms crossed across his wrinkled old chest. He stands there with a scowl on his face as Chiang, Chi and Chen all stagger to their feet with their arms still tied up. As they get to their feet the three degraded men make their way back to the mansion leaving a frustrated Lord Pai Po standing alone on the shore of the Yellow Sea. He looks out at the crashing tide as a feint smile crosses his power hungry lips.) Pai Po: Their time will come… (Time passes by as the scene shifts to the inside of the mansion. Chen, Chi and Chiang are standing outside a closed wooden door with the word Office carved into the door itself. With a quick knock on the door by Chiang, it swings open and Mountain Lord Pai Po stands in the doorway with a flowing satin robe tied at his waist with matching satin pajama pants. The elderly Pai Po smiles and invites the three men into his office with a wave of his hand as he quickly returns back to behind his desk.) Pai Po: Are you three ready? (Chi, Chen and Chiang look to each other confusion displayed on their faces as Pai Po continues.) Pai Po: I just got you a deal with a federation that actually wants you! Why they want the three of you, I have no clue but they do… So I guess you three will be heading back to America! (The three men look to each other with frowns on their faces as Chi and Chen say simultaneously…) Chen and Chi: Ah shit! Not America! (Chiang raises his hand to shut the two up as he pulls a pen and piece of paper off Pai Po’s desk. He quickly scribbles something down and slides it to the Mountain Lord.) Pai Po: “Which organization?” (Pai Po smiles and looks up at the three warriors.) Pai Po: Are you ready for “Primetime”!!! (The scene ends with Chi and Chen looking at each other confused and with Chiang sitting in his chair with an ear to ear smile on his face.) The Return! Anticipation! Hope! Gold!? The time is now upon us… The Black Dragons are in PCW for the first time in their careers. What will be their goal? Who will be first the feel the wrath of the Dragon? Will anyone be safe? Only time will tell!
