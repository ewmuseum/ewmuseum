Chambers of the Soul

26 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-26-02 Introduction: Unconscious in the hospital after being struck by a car, our Infinite Warrior takes something he usually does not: a moment to think. Balancing his rising wrestling career with his relationship with Minion and the Warriors In Training, Trigger's problems are almost too much to handle. But then again… He is a warrior. Alone, confined to his own space, away from all humanity, a warrior thinks. He goes through information rapidly in his head, undetected and unknown by anyone. This is the life of a warrior, and secret as it may be, this particular warrior is content. How does he deal with the troubles that life involves? How does he maintain the power of the warrior without becoming a dictator? Who is this warrior? Pay close attention, as he unlocks the chambers of his soul… P-C-W. A name synonymous with greatness… a name filled with overwhelming excitement… a name I have yearned to hear for so long. Two months I spent, slaving away in PWA and XWA, with only one goal in my mind. PCW. This one goal fueled my ambition's greatest extents, allowing me to fight with such capability that I have not seen in quite some time. PCW. True it is that I would be fine just being a part of Primetime Championship Wrestling. I am a warrior after all, a natural-born fighter knowing only the acts of battle, nothing more. A warrior does not yearn to achieve greatness in the eyes of others; the sole job of a warrior is to be there when the violence starts, and to fight until the battle ends. I only wanted to be a part of PCW out of curiosity, to feel the magnanimity so many others have boasted. That is what I prepared to do, and would have been entirely satisfied with… but things change. Last Friday when I stepped into the arena to face Duzza, a rush came over me, causing a loss of words. I could not concentrate-- I could not even move; I was paralyzed by the prominence that is Primetime Championship Wrestling. For the first time in my life, I wasn't looking for respect. I was looking for fame; notability. That is what I achieved. Frisco… Snow… El Diablo… all famous faces that have once held the Rising Star Championship. And now I could be next. It is impossible to describe the feeling I get just thinking about what would happen if I win my match this Friday at Fusion. Unimaginable even is the glory that would come with holding a golden belt in such a prestigious federation. I feel I can attain this gold… but it will not be easy. Yes, as with everything else in this world, there are difficulties. After all, the road of life is always under construction. Heaven Rayne… Mark Mackonie… Kid Krayzie…these are my co-workers, these are my adversaries, and next Friday they are my opponents. Even in my noble soul, I get chills thinking about what it will be like to face these… legends. Heaven, a pupil of PWA, just like I, has been called the best out of the batch of new wrestlers. Directly compared with her, I have been counted as the underdog. Not that it is bad, being the underdog that is, but I will not be in a position to get cocky. Mark Mackonie, a veteran of NTICW and PCW, well surely give me a run for my money. His battles with Jordan X. Shabazz are infamous. Do I have what it takes to defeat him? On that I am not sure. I cannot answer. The moment of truth will come, but in the mean time I must remain in the shadows. Finally, the last opponent, Kid Krayzie. The name…I have heard it before many times. I have seen his face on television. I never imagined I would be in a ring with this seasoned expert. Of all four, he has the upper hand, seeing as the man has held a Rising Star Championship in the past. He knows what it takes to win… He obviously has what it takes to win. So what is his weakness? I draw a blank. The threats of loss do not end here. Vividly I remember last week as PWA star Goeren brutally attacked me after the match. I had no choice but to give in to his dastardly ways. How must I fend if this beast should attack during my Rising Star Title match? What can I do? Five on one is a doubtful task even for a warrior. So it seems, apparently, I am not expected to overcome these odds. The world does not think Trigger is ready for the Rising Star Title. That is fine. The world never wanted Trigger… that is why I have become an outcast and risen up to reincarnate as a warrior. Nobody thought I would defeat Duzza last week. He was much more experienced than I in the eyes of the fans and the other wrestlers. Yet I did defeat him. I took him down with a DDT and I pinned him, damnit! I can do this. No matter how hard it seems, I can do it, and I must do it! This is not just a matter of pride anymore… this is a matter of survival. And however presumptuous this may seem, when the match turns into a battle of heart, I am the only one who can win. Minion… the WITs… PCW… Rising Star… Gold…Gold… Gold! I've been given my chance, a thing that is rare and sweet, but if I do not take it, there is no hope for a future. I chose this life, now I must lead it. I am a warrior, and my chance is now.
