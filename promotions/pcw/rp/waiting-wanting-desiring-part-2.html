Date: Again, today :-p Beautiful, sunny Tokyo, the home for Sunday's big wrestling event PCW's Red October pay-per view. Last year Russia hosted this large extravaganza, but now it is Japan's turn and what's in store for them is a huge series of matches, which lead into a fatal four-way Main Event for the PCW World Title. The PCW World Title! A match that El Diablo has a possibility of getting to and the title El Diablo has a possibility of getting. But El Diablo wants more than a possibility, as that title is something he's been waiting for, something he's been wanting and something he's been desiring for a long time now and for the very first time he has a very real prospect of capturing that gold. But obstacles stand in his way, most notably the man called Jason Snow, a man who knows more than anyone else what that title means! He's held the PCW Title three times, and that is three more times than El Diablo has! Snow, however wants that title around his waist one more time. Of course Snow will have a passion to get that title back, and that will make him just as dangerous and ever, so that leaves the question as: Can El Diablo overcome the obstacle of Jason Snow at Red October? El Diablo and his wife Angel have just recently landed in Japan from a long flight, which they finally took after El Diablo tried to get them to Japan on a bus. It's only been one day since their arrival, which is quite a risk for El Diablo in arriving so late so close to match time, as it could really affect him when wrestling a possible two times that night.
