The Handler Strikes Back

12 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/12/02 Monologue Expectations. To anticipate or look forward to the coming or to await an occurrence or outcome. We all expected after Turmoil we’d have a new Extreme champion. Seriously, a 23 man elimination match featuring the likes of Clyde, Jay Sylo, Akiyama, and Chef you’d expect one of them coming out as the Extreme Champion dethroning Big Daveylicious. I bet some of you even thought he’d been eliminated before Frampton. But the outcome didn’t turn out as expected. There was no new champion at Luken Airport. In all his glory Big Daveylicious left a 20-man elimination match as the Extreme Champion. Which very few Extreme Champions had done before him. We all expected his title regain to last two weeks and he’d lose it. It’s gone longer than expected. He’s got the 2nd longest Extreme title reign. You might have expected the Sensation of Annihilation. To remain a tag team and go on the ranks of the floundering tag roster. But you didn’t expect Big D to win gold. You didn’t expect Mike Frampton to end his own career betraying him and destroying the tag team. Nobody expected much out of Big Daveylicious. Unexpected would be the appropriate word for him. People didn’t think anybody could ride to the top of the FSW as fast as he did. Nobody expected him to win the Intercontinental title in his second week in the company. After two months of holding the title making a record which is unbeaten in FSW history. He loses it and goes a step higher. Becoming the FSW World Heavyweight Champion. Beating the likes of Mike Marvelous, Jacob Jackson, Epitome of Evil, Creature and even the man who killed the FSW, Johnny Mayhem. Most memorable battles were his match’s against EOE, Creature, and once again that loser, Johnny Mayhem. FSW was the place were Big D met Mike Frampton than known as Punisher 2000. A new wrestler finally made his break into the business. The FSW legend would give Mike guidance and even promised him that he would make him into a champion. However, he left for the PCW. Departing from the company he once called home due to scandals backstage. Who could blame Big D for what he’s done to Mike since double crossed Big D in his first title defense? After all Big D made Frampton. He got him a job as his tag partner. But no it wasn’t good enough? Nothings ever good enough. We all want and want just like the struggling Frampton wants. Frampton expected our Extreme champion to just hand the title to him. Unfortunately, for his sake unexpectedly Big D will end a man’s career but future also. (The scene fades to April 30th, 2002 inside Lunken Airport in Cincinnati, Ohio. It appears we are in the terminal of the airport as some people are in the waiting seats of their flights. Some children look out the window noticing planes taking off and other people scattering around the large terminal. Suddenly, Big Daveylicious jumps into our view on his knees, holding the PCW Extreme title up smiling joyfully.) Big Daveylicious: Whoooooooooooooooooooooo! I’m the Longest Reigning Extreme Champion! (Sorry, Big D but it’s April 30th for this promo. You’re not the Longest Reigning Extreme Champion ‘till May 19th. Your not even the Second Longest Reigning Extreme champ ‘till May 9th. ) Big Daveylicious: What the hell? Why am I doing a promo set on April 30th when it is May 12th? (Uhh....) Big Daveylicious: On top of that how come I don’t have a girlfriend or wife? (You’ve had hookers and female fans to have sex with...) Big Daveylicious: Yeah, but I pay for the hookers and the female fans do it for autographs! (So...you got money.) Big Daveylicious: I get lonely. You know! I WANT A GIRLFRIEND NOW! (Well, I can stop my damn workrate and have you jobbing to the likes of Benny the Janitor Guy!) Big Daveylicious: ... Ok, April 30th sounds good. (Good! Sorry, about that folks. Now, let’s begin. Ahem! Big Daveylicious jumps into our view on his knees, holding the Extreme title up joyfully. He wears the same Armani suit he just fought in the elimination match.) Big Daveylicious:Uh....Whooo. I didn’t job. Yeah. Yeppie. Hooray. (Why are you scratching you head with that, Big D? Show some enthusiasm you just beat 20 men including Akiyama, Jay Sylo, Clyde, and even Chef!) Big Daveylicious: But that was April 30th. I mean it was last week. (So?) Big Daveylicious: Who gets excited about something they did a week ago? (Argh. What about my needs? Do you realize I can be making out with a chick or riding my bike in the sunset in my happy place but nooooooooooooo gotta do the promo. Gotta give you the spotlight!) Big Daveylicious: Can we not do this and let me at least train and get exercise in the gym instead of arguing with you? (No...) Big Daveylicious: But...I wannaaa (Do you honestly think training and exercising win you matchs?) Big Daveylicious: Yes... (Isn't it odd to you that you never did a training promo or weightlifting and you became the Extreme Champion?) Big Daveylicious: Listen, Mr. Monologue man I done it all by myself. (Bah! Lets just restart the scene again. Sorry, again folks. Suddenly, Big Daveylicious jumps into our view on his knees, holding the PCW Extreme title up smiling joyfully.) Big Daveylicious:Yo, Adrian! I DID IT! (Out of nowhere Sylvester Stallone appears in boxing trunks and gloves from the move Rocky and punches Big D in the mouth. Big D sits himself up from the sneak punch and looks on a a running away Stallone.) Big Daveylicious: Great, I beat a couple of main eventers and I job to a has-been actor all in one day. (I thought you said it was May 12.) Big Daveylicious: Stop talking to me! (Suddenly, we hear an intercom in the terminal echoing a flight.) Intercom: Flight 2906 to Florida. Please line up. Big Daveylicious My flight! I can’t miss it! (Big D rushes toward the line as passengers aboard the plane. He turns to hand his ticket to the attendant but she stops him.) Attendant: Sorry, sir but these tickets are scheduled to go to Florida the May 12th. Big Daveylicious: I know today is May 12th and the problem is? Attendant: It’s April 30th, sir. Big Daveylicious: Liar! You incompetent idiot. Don’t you realize what your doing? Your stopping me from seeing my Mommy! (Big D stomps his feet onto the ground in a tantrum as two security men come over behind Big D.) Security man: Is there a problem, Mister? Big Daveylicious: Yes, there is a fucking problem this witch won’t let me see my Mommy and your doing crap about it! Security Man: You’re coming with us. Big Daveylicious: I want my Mommy! (Both security men grabs both of his arms and drag him as the scene fades to poop.)
