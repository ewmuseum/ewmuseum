Is it anything?

Date: da fifth (This was sent out to all member of Duke Williams fan club via e-mail) So what is it all about? Welcome to the handbook of Malice. This newsletter will introduce you to the creed and reasoning behind Malice. Malice is not only a term but a way of life to live by. It encompasses everything the Malice Man fights for. Who is good and who is evil? Is violence the only answer? Traditionalism: excessive devotion to tradition. This is the foundation of what I stand for. Change is a scary thing but not as scary as forgetting the past. When I started in wrestling we didn’t tell elaborate stories or have big angled productions. What we did was hit the ring and tell the fans what it was they wanted or didn’t want to hear. WE didn’t cut promo’s from out homes, offices, parks, or anywhere else. It happened in the ring with an audience that offered a response. Now times have changed and technology and taste dictates us away from the ring. Is it wrong? I don’t know yet. What I do know is as a traditionalist I will keep it simple. I will always mention my opponent when I take up PCW television time. If not what’s the point? Do I really need to know every aspect of my opponents life? F*ck no!! I want to know why he has a beef with me and how I can solve it. To those who don’t like trash talk f*ck them…..f*ck’em twice on Sunday. Right in the ass!! Hey look I’m here to fight and feud not get to know you better. I will not over whelm the view with excessive details and elaborate scene changes. You want to see a movie go to the theater. You want a story read a book. You want pith tune into Duke Williams. No b*llshit here. Just the facts, Jack. Simple is what I do and what I do best. Follow the Tradition of. The Members: Any 9 to 5 worker who is American and civil. No freaks, f*gs, or MTV junkies. We fight for the hard working men and women of this great country of ours. We fight for those who are looking to better the world but are being held back by the upper one percent of the country. It is my sworn duty to uphold the voice of the blue collar American. We are the greatest country in the world and the rest out side of a few friendly countries like England, Canada, and to a much lesser extant Mexico can all f*ck off Malice Style. Red is for the blood that will be shed in this battle. White is for the fallen and blue is for what my opponents face will look like when the step in the ring with me. We will not accept those who do not want to be accepted by society. Men with painted finger nails. People with blue hair. Social outcasts destined to fail in life. Social losers is really what they are. They are criminals offering us nothing. My taxes pay for their drug habits and freak children they make out of wedlock. These monsters should be killed and put out of their misery. They are the cockroaches of our civilization and shall be stamped out. The Boss Man: The guy in charge suppressing the real workers. The suit. The corporate criminal. These monsters must be stopped at all costs. I’m all for a free market and raising ones riches however the common man has been sh*t on for too long. I’m not looking to eliminate the few fair and honest CEO’s out there. Just the tax cheating, back stabbing, inside trading, dirt balls running most of our top companies. These people our the vile in our society. Well the freaks are dangerous they are unorganized and voiceless. These men however are lobbyist, politicians, decision makers, who do have a say. They must be silenced. They must be our focus. They must be systematically eliminated and replaced by honest men. Tyler Nelson, The Phantom Booker all must be made an example of. They have ruined some many hard working wrestlers lives for their own gain. Pay back is a bitch. Violence: The aggressive action we must take to win. The crusaders of light are out there trying to fight for the good. However with their paltry army and floated egos, and immaculate beliefs they will never succeed. You need to be tough or die and they are weak.. They will fail. We will not. We will stop at nothing to defend our creed. Violence will be met with violence. Malice will come into play when it must. Nothing is too sick or twisted in making sure we succeed. Failure is not an option. Hostages, severed limbs, murder, torture are all means of accepted acts of war. That is what we are declaring, war. Blood must be shed for us to succeed. Revenge: the act of retaliating to acts against us. An eye for an eye and a tooth for a tooth. Any attack placed upon a member of the Malice Crew will be repaid ten fold. You cut off a finger I’ll cut off ten. You take a life I’ll take your whole family. You interfere in one my match I’ll screw ten of yours. You hit me with a chair I’ll throw a love seat at you. Noting is unfair or out of bounce. When dealing with sick pricks the only thing they understand is giving it right back. Respect: What others owe us. We will earn or take the respect of others in our actions. Those who do not follow are ways we can careless. We will however respect each other in this mission of excellence. WE WILL NOT BE DEFEATED DUKE WILLIAMS =w=
