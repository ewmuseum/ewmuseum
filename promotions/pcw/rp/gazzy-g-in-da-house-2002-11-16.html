GAZZY G - IN DA HOUSE!

16 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16 - 11 - 02 'Every woman wants to be in her shoes and every man wants to be in-... Her. Please give it up for Gail Porter!' The voice in question is incredibly distinctive, a renowned celebrity yet his name eludes the masses of America. The scene opens as a silhouette peers down at a cube shaped monitor that displays the said celebrity hosting his show. From the grim backdrop of England this rising star now faces the daunting task of taking on the home audiences of America, this person, Sacha Baron Cohen, better known as 'Ali G'. The luscious Gail Porter strides onto the set under a lighting spectacle, accompanied by bellowing music played by the disc scratching DJ merely feet away. Sitting opposite the notorious Ali G, the petite television host looked a bag of nerves, obviously well aware of the shenanigans that accompany this programme. Then-... Nothing. The image fizzled out and the camera panned out revealing the real reason for Primetime Championship Wrestling's film crew’s presence. 'What have I to fear from a white man portraying a black, an absurdly dressed black mind you. This Ali G makes people laugh, well I make people laugh and more importantly cry-...' said the Emerald Menace standing before the recent acquisition of Natalia Miyagawa who literally cut off her client mid-sentence. 'Hold your horses - This is an interview and nothing more, we have been asked to advertise Alliances2K2 by those above. A little publicity would benefit the company hugely and if all goes well maybe it'll push you closer to that Cruiserweight Championship, huh?!?' 'Talking about the Cruiserweight Tournament, it has been officially recorded that Gaz 'The Jester' Green has the fastest victory in history!!!' roared the Jester in triumph, a little glimpse at his expanding ego. Waiting for his entrance, his unveiling so too speak, Gaz Green paced back and forth, almost to the point of making his valet hypnotised. Left, then right, the Jester's pendulum motion was growing a little tedious as his impatience seeped through into his activity. 'STOP IT!!!' cried the Russian beauty with authority. Immediately the pacing stopped and Gaz stood motionless, approved by Natalia sighing of relief and again they wait. Again the minutes pass and as though on queue Gaz's impatience swamped his body, tingling from the brain like a locomotive. Suddenly the annoying tap of Gaz Green’s foot thwarted the buzz of activity as it crushed repeatedly on the bare concrete, it's annoyance personified through the rage-fuelled eyes of Natalia Miyagawa. 'SSSSSTTTTTOOOOOPPPPP IIIIITTTTT~!' blurted Natalia, fighting her grip on sanity. Grinding her teeth, eyes flamed over in crimson red, she undoubtedly lived up to the parable, 'Hell knows no fury like a woman scorned'. 'Would you rather I whistle?!?' retorted her client with puppy dog eyes, taking a page from the PCW Commissioner Eleanor Kannon-Hall. Dirty handed grant you but this manipulative counter is almost full-proof, certainly from an Ellie standpoint but Natalia knows only too well the depths Gaz is willing to sink in order to keep his peaceful existence. The Jester's comments however only spurn Natalia into attacking her client as she attempts to throttle the very life from his 226 lbs. mass of flesh. Lunging forward like a wild animal, her efforts flawed by the injection of a backstage employee who intervenes. The intervention nothing to do with the safety of Gaz 'The Jester' Green but his instructions are for the high-profiled wrestling star to make his way to the stage, as Ali G prepares to announce his next guest. 'Whoa-... Mr. Green your up next!' Cutting to the stage, the multi-coloured comic Ali G steps up to the plate and has the all clear, so with a gulp of air he announces PCW's Gaz Green to a thunderous pop from the live audience. 'My next guest is a wrestler and ting, so big it up for me main man Gazzy G-... No relation, he's not even a bruv'er!' With the flicker of strobe lighting and a remixed version of Offsprings 'Original Prankster' Gaz 'The Jester' Green emerged from behind a curtain, cockily strutting into full view of both the cameras and those live. The two celebrities shake hands and make their way over to the comfort of the sofa's, a place where many a celebrity has fallen foul to the wrath of Sacha Baron Cohen. 'So Gazzy tell me what's this Alliance ting, cause me personally have me own posse 'Da West Ends Massive' but dis event sounds like a bunch of butty boy groping one another?' 'Well it's a huge Pay-Per-View-....' begins the Lord Of Laughter, interrupted upon by the newcomer. 'A male orgy is on Pay-Per-View. Now personally me finks that's a little sick even for da white men who rule Channel 4 but you'se Americans watch some weird shit!' 'Channel 4? Anyway our team consists of myself, Gaz 'The Jester' Green-....' explains the Tag Champion, again an untimely interruption from the host and we find the conversation circling. 'You'se a Molester?' Gaz can only offer dumbfounded glares as every word murmured is twisted by the warped mind of Ali G, one tracked, a mind orbiting the topics of sex and ethnic origin. 'Arr-... No, I said Jester!' retorted Gaz Green solemnly. 'So you ain't a brother, nor molester but a Jester. Well me has never eva heard of a Jester in da U.S-....' said the comedian, stalling on the latter half of his sentence. 'Don't you mean U.S - A?!?' 'Aye - Anyway me wanna know whether you are boning dat fine Russian bitch 'cause me saw what dat aids victim Rezza did with her pimp and me finks he should go first and you'se get sloppy seconds!' added the masquerading white man still in full portrayal of his black counterpart, Ali G. 'Arr-... Err-... That wasn't a pimp. It was her husband Alexander Miyagawa!' replied Gaz 'The Jester' Green, beaten at his own game. 'Yeah for sure - So me has heard you is in a team called S.T.D with Rezza, me know he has aids and all but as you got a problem down below and if so don't you fink you'se should see dat clinic and ting!' questioned the native of Staines. Gaz could only rub his brow in shear disbelief. With every passing comment the hole grows a little deeper but through the Jester's despair the crowd lap up everything on offer and pop at every opportunity, jovial reactions of laughter and clapping roar in tandem. 'Actually the team is called Pranks With Attitude-...' 'Really.... Pricks With Attitude, me fought children go and watch dis shit!' said Ali G with yet another untimely intrusion. Gaz now begins to display similar symptoms to that of Natalia Miyagawa as he reaches the end of his tether, again a fight with sanity, a man on the brink of mental collapse. 'So Alliances is right around the corner, a huge event with loads of butty boyz in tights having a turf war but me wants to know who's you brothers in dis fing-... See me finks your the best tag team in NTICW-...' continued the shows party host as Gaz injected himself into the conversation pulling off a reversal of his own. 'Whoa-... Easy Tiger! I still have nightmares about NTICW so can you at-least get the right wrestling promotion, it's PCW, Primetime Champ-....' 'You'se right, Primetime Crappy Wrestling init? Anyways as I was saying me finks you and Rezza are da best tag team-... Oh! No there's Playaz Inc. who are a bunch of bruv'ers init, who are better!' The Jester biting his nails in frustration, followed by running his fingers through his slick hair and almost resembling Jack Nicholson's character from 'The Shining,' certainly this show had hit a downward spiral. Opening his mouth to respond, it merely served to catch flies as Ali G continued the charade, whilst drawing a negative reaction from the PCW employee, the crowd’s feedback was nothing short of electric. 'Ok, nuff of dis shit-... Me knows you beat some record against The Mandarin on Friday Night Fuck-up in some Cruiserweight Tourney-ment but now you'se facing some moshing poof called Heavy Metal Harold, like what’s up wiv dat?' asked the inquisitive comic, as he played with his gold laden fingers, his character possibly inspired by the A' Team's B A Baracus. 'Oh! Believe me I'm going to do exactly what I've done to him every other time we've competed and that's a nasty shock, a new look and three seconds of my valuable time-....' 'It only takes me free seconds of Me Julie playing with me beast and it's a white Christmas-... BOOYAH, anyways getting back to you'se team of white homey's-... Team Bastard, does hope you'se all find your fathers and peace out!' 'BIG UP YOURSELF!!!' concluded Ali G with a gesture of goodwill. Suddenly the 'Bastard' comment was the final straw and Gaz exploded, opening with a thunderous spear, taking the comic down in a heap and hooking a few wayward shots. Rolling about grappling and swinging violently the duo were dragged backstage by personnel as the set erupted into chaos, spurned on by the blood thirsty audience who cheer on the combatants. 'I'M GOING TO FUCKING KILL YOU~!' hissed the PCW Cruiserweight, struggling to free himself as the stage became desolate with all involved now far from the prying eyes of the public and cameras. Still a built up shambles the duo are released and square up once more as personnel gather round for what could quite possibly erupt into a second helping when without warning a grapple escaladed into nothing more than a friendly hug. No violence, only pleasantries and everything gently simmers down as Ali G returns back to his live set and Gaz Green joins the side of his faithful valet Natalia Miyagawa. 'HUH?!?' winced Natalia, star struck in her expression. 'It was a publicity act to get us noticed by the media. The guys in storylines finalised everything and we did our jobs, rather well I thought, what an eventful night?'
