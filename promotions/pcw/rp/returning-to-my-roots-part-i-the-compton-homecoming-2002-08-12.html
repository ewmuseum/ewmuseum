Returning To My Roots: Part I - The Compton Homecoming

12 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 12th, 2002 Jerome “Blayze” Jordan will narrate this story. [It was just something I need to do, I had held back for a long while but now I felt it was time. Returning to Compton sent a eerie sensation down my spinal cord, would the street habitants still remember this famous face? Man, growing up on those brutal streets with a dead mother and a lost father I barely knew, raised by my brother in the surroundings of gang warfare, still finished school but never bothered to pursue anything. Went from entering the professional wrestling in CCW and becoming a household name, to now battling for a rookie’s title in the number one fed in the nation, PCW. How can they not respect? I put this city on the map! No more stereotyping us Compton natives, saying we cannot be successful? Well damn them, I am 100% proof of all their queries. ]  Honk! Honk! * Voice: MOVE IT GODDAMN IT!! It’s a green fuckin’ light! Blayze: Alright! Jesus…. [I looked up and I saw the sign right away, “Welcome To Compton” and just by hearing the voice of the angry African-American driver honking his horn like a madman, I knew I was home. Though I did feel a bit weird, seeing as I was chillin’ in my Honda Prelude and the man behind me was cruising in his beat up 92 Ford Taurus. The guy was livid, so I let him pass me, also allowing him to flip me the never forgetting “bird” through his dusty passenger side window. Yeah, they all still have those “anger management” issues on the road as always. I was now..home. ] Blayze: Compton baby! Your hero and Icon is only a few minutes away from arriving back where he was raised. First things first, cruise around this mutha’ and check to see how they living their lives. Might as well hit me up my childhood bedren Saul, wondering if he still keeping it real as he always did. [As finally arrived on the main streets, I noticed the gigantic wooden donut over Compton’s famous “Downtown Donuts” shop were only the best hometown donuts and jelly pastries are made. The streets are still crowded as usual, but for the first time I see motorbikes and street scooters. This is mostly the busiest part of Compton, a lot of offices and small business work their ways to move up in the business world. Clear blue sky as usual, surprising it is not raining as it usually does. Kids are all out biking on the streets and playing dominoes, only 2 more weeks until they all go back to school. It seems so peaceful now, but as I drive down these streets, I began to notice something strange, it actually bugged me a bit.] Blayze: What is up with these peoples? Damn, looking at me like I am some poison to this community. [That was right, normal black citizen were stopping and eyeballing me. I know I did not sell out to my hometown, why would they just turn on me like this? I ignored it for a while and just continued down the streets. When I arrived at the corner of 130th, that is when I took a turn down my old road, my stomping grounds, my home lane..Warwick Avenue. Felt so good to be home, still same old place. Mr. Jenkins is still rocking on his lawn chair up on his balcony I see..] Blayze: JENKINS!! [I screamed out the window like a teen on his way to Spring Break. Jenkins opened his eyes, moved up from his chair and glared over his balcony. He seemed a bit confused, but nodded his head and just went back to rest. I was a bit dumbfounded, still I continued to cruise on. I passed by the former playground where I had my first fight. * Sigh *, good old ass kicking days, never will forget them. Finally, I had made my arrival to where I was born and raised in: 2200 Warwick Avenue. It has not changed] Blayze: Still, in the same condition it was almost 5 years ago. I wonder if the flooring and the backyard garden have been given a touch-up. [I did not get out of the car and walk up to the steps of my old house. Instead, I continued to travel down passed my road to meet up with Saul, I thought us two would go back and check it out together. I ended up down Northside Street, the new road where Saul lives, all on his own now, away from his parent’s house. He was a bit of a troubled child in high school, I really think he set a record for suspensions but due to him managing to keep his marks over the 60% mark, they could not expel him from school. He had numerous jobs, bus boy at a local pizza shop, movie theatre usher, co assistant at a breaking school and your every day weed dealer. He could not keep a steady job, now he is a radio DJ at Compton’s premier Underground Hip-Hop and Mainstream radio station. You can say he deserves it after going through a pretty harsh childhood, a bad parental divorce and a striking blow in a good relationship he had with this chick. ] Blayze: I wonder if Saul still can break-dance like he used to. Well, here I am, 1550 Northside Street, damn, this home is in amazing condition [I stepped out of my Prelude and made sure the security alarm is on, cannot trust anyone on these streets you know. Beginning to walk up his concrete steps, I was wondering if it was just my imagination or if there was really moaning coming from inside the house. Ah classic Saul, still up to his womanizing ways. I knew he kept a spare key inside his mailbox, it was taped to the bottom so you cannot hear it move if something rumbles within. Opening the door, I was shocked by the interior, shining wood flooring, a big screen TV with a PS2 attached to it, Surround Sound Stereo System, marble dining room table, a rich man’s life in his eyes. I suddenly realized I was not going crazy, there was moaning going on and that is when I began to walk up his wooden stairs. The higher I went, the louder the moaning got. I took a left, aiming right for what seemed to be Saul’s “Master Bedroom”, I must have hit the jackpot because the noise was surely coming from in there.] Blayze: Man, Saul still bumpin’ and grindin’ like he did back in the day. [I cracked the door open just enough so I can get a peeping tom’s eye view and I tried not to laugh. Saul, fashionably rockin’ it in his smiley face yellow boxers was working it with a fine young black female. Her legs were wrapped around his back, his right hand rapidly spanking her ass as she screamed in joy for more] Lady: Oh papi, smack big boy, smack it!! Saul: You like it don’t you bitch! Ughhhh, fuck yeah, shake it baby, shake it! Lady: Oooohhhhhhhhh [I shook my head with a smirk on my face, this was such a classic moment. About 5 minutes later, Saul “removed” himself from the young lady, allowing her to slip her panties and bra back on. He seemed really satisfied, locking lips with the beauty before putting his baby blue shorts and Karl Kani shirt back on. After getting dressed, she glided her hand across his face on her way out and blew him a kiss. Before she turned around to face the door, I opened the nearest door, which was the bathroom and jumped right. From the inside, I heard a “Bye baby” and footsteps down the stairs. That is when I felt it was time to make my surprise appearance..] Blayze: Classic Saul! [I really think I scared the hell out of him. He flew back and hit the door of his bedroom, holding his chest as if he was having a heart attack.] Saul: You sick Fuck!!! [I placed my hand on his shoulder, but shrugged it off, still pissed and freaked out. I had a hell of a good laugh out of it, too bad Saul didn’t.] Saul: How long were you here? How fuckin much did you see? Blayze: About 10 minutes or so, she can really get a grasp of your back with her legs, venomous lady you got there haha Saul: Fuck You [A smile finally came from Saul, he knew he could not flip off his old school homie.] Saul: So explain why you decided to make an impromptu return to Compton, Jerome? Blayze: Guess I missed how things were Saul: You fuckin with me right? Blayze: No seriously, I know I have not been the greatest role model for this town as a professional athlete but I do try my best at each time Saul: Entertainer, not athlete Blayze: You really want to debate that? Saul: No, since you always think you are right [I was a bit offended, like always, when he kept saying I was not an athlete. I felt like telling him to see how it feels to go through 4-hour surgeries or dislocate a kneecap but I guess I just had to respect his opinion.] Blayze: Anyways brother, I want to have a talk with you, have a little man on man conversation. Plus, I want you to come with me to my old house and shit., Saul: Aight fine..but one thing? Blayze: Shoot Saul: Scare the fuck out of me again, I will slit your balls Blayze: Haha, you wish kiddo. [He escorted me downstairs and we made our way out his home. I needed to speak to him, I needed to know, why everyone in Compton suddenly seems to have had a change of heart..] To Be Continued…
