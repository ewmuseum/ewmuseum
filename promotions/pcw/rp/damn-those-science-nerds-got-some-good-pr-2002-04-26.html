Damn those science nerds got some good pr

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/26/02 -Duke is on set at the Public Broadcasting Service building in St. Paul, Minnesota. He is in a suit and tie sitting at a desk with a city back drop behind it. He is talking wrestling with a producer before the production begins.- Williams: SO this Mark Mookie or what ever the hell his name is tells the world that I have no mic skill cus he heard me on the radio show. Basically this nazi nitwit has insulted all of the PTC. Of all the wrestlers out there they picked me as the host. So he in a sense, is saying that Tyler Nelson and the rest of the PCW/PTC organizations have no idea what they’re doing. If my show sucks, and I can’t work the mic, then it’s a wonder to me and the fancast people why my show ranks number one, week after week, after week. Why is it that in the half year we’ve been doing this NOBODY has torn me apart? The real question that must be asked is WHY if my show sucks so bad, and I can’t work the mic, how come this Mookie continues to listen?? Now I know he lives at home with mommy and really doesn’t have much of a life as a racist waste of space with an ugly chin beard. Here’s some advice for that rookie mookie if your going to criticize somebody else make sure you criticism is just and DO NOT STILL LIVE AT HOME WITH MOMMY you pathetic f*cking c*m stain. That tosses your creditability right out the f*cking door. -Duke makes a wistle noise as he mimics opening a door and kicking something out of it.- Producer: Your right Mr. Williams the only thing worse then a racist is one who lives at home with his mother. Your in a few seconds get ready. Williams: Ready. -The producer cues Duke with his pointer finger as the stage lights turn on and the camera’s start to roll.- Williams: Welcome to this PBS special the life and times of Scientology. In the next few minutes we will dive into and debunk this phony religion as nothing more then a scam. The word Scientology comes from the Latin word scio, meaning “know” and the Greek word logos, meaning “the word or outward form by which the inward thought is expressed and made known.” Thus, Scientology means knowing about knowing. I don’t know what all that means but I know, that I know, that these silly bastards are brainwashed and they don’t KNOW that they don’t know that they’re brain washed. Ya following along at home hoss? -Duke looks into the camera with a perplexed look.- Williams: When one dives into a scam it’s best to start with the creator of the money grabbing material. The con-artist and rumored homosexual is none other then author L. Ron Hubbard. A former police officer who’s penned such pieces of trash like Battlefield Earth. This literal masterpiece was turned into an almost Oscar winning movie and if you believe that I’ll sell you ocean front property in Arizona. E. Ron used his training as a police officer to manipulate followers into believing his rhetoric philosophies and convincing young boys to follow him into the bedroom for some L. Ron Love making. Also a proven factual note L. Ron Hubbarb liked to hit the acid before writing. -Duke sips down some booze from the cup on the table- Williams: Tonight we’re not going to examine the whole gimmick but rather one of its outspoken members know as Xavior Kannon. If he was hooked up to one of Hubbarb’s E-meters he would be discovered as nothing more the a fraud in a fraud religion. Of coarse the E-meter a scientology tool that can tell if one is lying is as phony as a Mr-T doll so maybe that’s a bad example. We wanted to ask Mary Sue Hubbarb about her book “The Book of E-Meter Drills” but she went to jail for a long list of crimes including bugging the IRS and stealing files from the FBI. Her husband L. Ron Hubbard was named as an un-indicted co-conspirator and now they’re both dead. Where’s your scientology now dead folk? -Duke fixes his tie.- Williams: We all know Kannon is a lieer but now we know why!!! Here is a direct qoute from Mr. Hubbarb himself. 'THE ONLY WAY YOU CAN CONTROL PEOPLE IS TO LIE TO THEM..., because the second you start telling anybody close to the truth, you start releasing him and he gets tougher and tougher to control.'. So now we know why Ellie~! stays with that ass muncher Kannon. He has her programmed with b*llshit and lies. Very nice Xavior too bad the rest of the PCW and the fans don’t fall for your TR tricks. You’re a pawn in your religion as you are a pawn in Nelson’s army….. -The producer hands Duke a slip of paper.- Williams(reading): This production is to cease immediately as it is direct slander against Scientology and all its good people. If stopped now and never aired we will drop the suit against PBS but will continue the charge against Duke Williams. Thank you from the Scientology PR department. -Williams takes off his tie mic and walks out the door.- Williams: Ahhh f*ck it!! You can’t hide the truth or manipulate it forever. The truth is Ellie is a licensed c*m net and Kannon is a pathetic pawn ready to be exposed by a middle aged and crazy president. Kannon you are nothing in my eyes. You’re a walking contradiction partly truth and partly fiction. You team up with a nazi who goes directly against your beliefs of finding good in everybody. You whine and bitch every chance you get, and you’re a lying sack of sh*t. I’m short on time and short on patience so I’ll see you in the ring till then keep it Malice Style hossssssssss. -The camera fades- (Sorry its short something came up and I had to wrap it up.)
