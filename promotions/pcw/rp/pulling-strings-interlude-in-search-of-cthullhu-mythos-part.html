Date: Last Friday Night I began to think while I was crawled up in a fetal position. All of this occurred in the ring mind you, the sweat, the cheers and the utter humility of the crowd's eyes upon me, that I had lost in six days the most prized posession of my career. To two people. Skylar Green, the man who was looking to become the simple thorn in my side that could not be stratched, that could not be picked up and tossed away like garbage or even a 'has-been'. No, ever since I became a singles wrestler, ever since Playaz Inc. DIED in my hands, this man has been right in the front of it - looking from the top, laughing at the bottom. For months, torment of my mind, my soul, the little things that reach out and santifiy the chances I've had. He's quaked under large heels and rose like a volcano to stop those heels. But now, he is my heel, the simple thing that I cannot let go for the life of me. Decimation - stops my chances of being Continental champion. The thought of second chances, they run through each and every person that you come in contact with, only to fade away once wasted. My second chance at the former End has come only in tag matches, a forte` of mine dating back to previous months when I teamed with Statuz Quo. I leave with those, the thoughts of whatever may come because the time for games, the time for jokes are completely over. Not once have I ever crawled into a fetal position and think and hope, and wish that things could be different. On one fatal Friday night, they didn't change. The referee slammed his hand against the mat three times, odd while I was on the mat, profusely in agony from an Anthem of Ry'leh and Skylar Green made it sure. While I was dominating Jack, his co-hort, he stepped in and dropped me like a pair of panties from a horny teenage female. That's when it was done, the shady figure of a title reign justified by one single loss, in one lone title defense. Then the Juan Borro reject came calling. Anger, hate. Anger, hate, they roll inside of my stomach quicker and quicker, tossing and turning, wondering which one is going to come out and show its face for the world to see. The nevrve of such a man to challenge me towards a style of mine is barbaric on his hands. I would love to see him try at freestyling, kicking a beat and trying to see whether or not his words will dethrone mine. There are judges for this type of contest, not the crowd... That sea of cheap dick riders. Oh how me and Xavier Kannon loathe them with more passion than anything. More than the hierarchy that is the PCW title system, more than the simple fact that we have been screwed over time and time again just to the benefit of the people, THOSE INGRATES! Oh, I've struck a little incentive with Mr. Romantico, and Friday, he shall find those wishes of battling me a fools tale, because I'm taking this tournament, and carrying it on my back. My staple. My form of victory. My avarice. And most importantly of all...my revenge on not only Jack but Green. So says the Curve of Life, so says Dante Jackson.
