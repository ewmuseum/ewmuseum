Date: Season I, Episode 6 ﻿************************************The Snow ShowSeason I, Episode 6************************************ This is the life I’ve chosen. It’s strange, how after twenty-four years of life, one can feel so damn old yet still be constantlyreminded that his life is only beginning. Most people my age are just starting out, you know... Me? I’ve got a wife, a daughter, and I’ve already managed to gain and lose a fortune. Well, the fortune will be lost soon enough. I guess some might say that I did what any man would do in my situation - I took on a secondjob. But I can’t help but think that the reality of the situation is that I didn’t only take on asecond job - I took on a second life... a second family, one might say. The Hattoni’s have been good to me so far. They offered me protection, and they offered toprotect my family, at all costs. “You’re on the fast track to the top,” Justin Hattoni told me. Ithink it was Justin. In all honesty, all of these Goombas start looking alike after a while. Andthose fucking New York accents... New Yorker’s are scum, I sometimes think. Even thesepeople who I’ve been calling my “friends” for the last little while. And if I have to serve one family to protect my real family, then so be it. It’s the path I’vechosen. ~ Jason Adam Snow *** Location: Marty Verrelli’s Car - New York CityDate: December 26, 2002Time: 8:40 pm Snow ran the palm of his left hand soothingly over the knuckles on his right. The trucker hadn’tbeen quite so submitting as they had expected, and Snow was the only one out of the five-mancrew willing to tangle with him without waving a gun around. “Hard fucking teeth,” he cursedas he looked down at his scraped knuckles. Wrestlers were never so hard to deal with. Things weren’t supposed to get violent with the trucker. It was supposed to be a hold up. Maybe they would shove him around a bit and scare him while two others searched the trailer ofhis 18-wheeler for the batch of, of all things, bananas that they were looking for. No one mentioned the trucker would be a six-foot-four redneck who suffered from flashbacks of“Nam”. In the end, however, all worked out well, and the heroin-laced bananas was safely in the trunk oftheir car as they drove North through the difficult New York City streets. To Snow’s left, in the driver’s seat, was Marty Verrelli - it was his car. If Snow was a baby inthe city’s underworld, then Marty Verrelli was a premature birth. He was only twenty-two yearsold, and came into the business by blood. His father was trusted with some of the toughest jobsin the family. In every-day terms, his father was a hitman. The great Jack Verrelli - the Dragon,as he was known inside the family and through the criminal circles of New York. Marty wasn’t nearly so large as his father was reputed to be, but he was every bit as snake-like. He drove confidently toward the North end, where they were to meet Johnny Silver and hand offthe heroin. “You’re driving too fast,” Snow said quite suddenly, without looking up from his sore hand. “Yeah,” Marty answered sarcastically, “pardon me if I want to get half a million dollars worth ofheroin out of my car as quick as fucking possible.” “Sure,” Snow said cooly, “you’re afraid of getting caught by the cops so you drive faster... makesperfect fucking sense if the cops didn’t pull motherfuckers over for speeding left and right.” Heturned toward Marty and eyed him. “Slow the fuck down.” Obediently, Marty eased up on the gas. Snow didn’t know the three men in the backseat, and he couldn’t remember their names. All ofthem were somewhere in their late twenties, he thought, and relatively new to the business. Everyone in the car was expendable to the Hattoni family, and that’s why they were sent to pickup enough heroin to land them in prison for the remainder of their lives, if they were to becaught. “That’s the left,” Snow said suddenly, pointing to a small general store built on a corner. “Marvin’s convenience. Johnny’s supposed to be at the end of that street, waiting in front of thewarehouse.” *** Location: Snow Residence - New York SuburbsDate: December 26, 2002Time: 8:45 pm “It’s getting colder,” Krista said as she shut the blinds and drew the curtains over them. Sheturned to see her sister, Raven, who was sitting at the table, cradling her sleeping daughter close. “I know,” Raven said with a sigh. Krista sat across from her sister and fiddled with the tea cup in front of her - they had eachdecided to make a cup of tea and wait for Snow. They were determined to get to the bottom ofhis odd behavior. Krista loved her brother-in-law like a father. He was there for her the exactsame way. When she looked at him, she didn’t see a man who was only a handful of years older,she saw the experience that his brief years carried. He was so much older than his years, to Krista, at least. She turned back to the window and nearly peaked out again. It was pointless, she knew. Shewould hear the car drive in anyway. Raven was sitting across from her, staring blankly at theclock above the fridge. Almost nine o’clock... “Do you really think he’s... you know...” Krista said in a half whisper. “I mean, this is Jasonwe’re talking about here. He gets a little violent with people, I know, but killing people? Anddoing all of the stuff that... those... people do? I don’t think he has it in him.” “Jason can be surprising,” Raven sighed. “For good or for bad. To be honest with you, Krista,sometimes I don’t know what he’s capable of.” Suddenly, Krista’s mind flashed, and she remembered the look in her brother-in-law’s eyes overfive months ago after he’d caught her in bed with her then-boyfriend. She had never seen suchrage. It was inhuman. She never told Raven about the incident, and it was just as well. Raven would try to put JasonSnow in some anger management class that would do nothing for him, and then he’d come backout and everything would be just the same as before. Snow was a dangerous man, and nothingwould ever change that. But he was a loving man, she knew, and he would protect his family with his life, if need be. They both knew this. They both believed it. Krista pushed her hair from her eyes with one hand and raised her cup with the other. The heatfrom the tea soothed her somewhat. She couldn’t help but thinking how cold it was outside. “The mafia,” Raven said, finally getting the word out in the open. It kind of stunned Krista for amoment. She hadn’t said the word yet. “Maybe we’re jumping to conclusions,” Krista said, though there was little hope left in her. “I hope so,” Raven said. “But so much has changed since Jason went to prison. He won’t talkabout it with me so I don’t know what to think. He’s so... distant.” Krista reached across the table and touched her sister’s hand. “I know,” Krista said softly. “I’ve talked to him about that before. But in his own silent way,he’s telling you everything, sis.” “Well,” Raven finally levelled her stare, “not everything. But I suppose if he’s... involved...we’ll find out soon enough.” She sighed and peaked through the side of the curtain. “Hopefullyit won’t be too late...” *** Location: Warehouse - North New York CityDate: December 26, 2002Time: 9:00 pm “He’ll fucking be hear!” Snow told Marty as the five men stood outside Marty’s car. Each ofthem held their hands in their pockets and blew fog for breath. “He ain’t here yet,” Marty said sharply, “and he was supposed to be here a half hour ago! I don’tfucking know about you, but that makes me nervous. That’s my fucking car, and that makes theheroin my God damn responsibility!” “Calm down!” Snow snapped back. “He’s running late! He’ll be hear!” “Don’t you tell me to calm down, motherfucker!” Marty said, and quite suddenly, he drew hisgun and held it steady a few inches from Snow’s face. “I’m the one looking at the rest of my lifein fucking prison over this shit, motherfucker! Not you, and not that bald fucking Johnny Silver! You wanna know what I think!? I think this might just be a fucking set up! Maybe they’ve gotthe God damn feds heading here right now!” Snow took a step back and raised his hands a little in submission. He hadn’t expected Marty topull a gun, but then again, he was new to these situations in general. The other three men eachstepped back and exchanged glances, but none of them spoke. “Put the fucking gun down, Marty, this isn’t helping anything,” Snow said evenly. “Sure is making me feel a hell of a lot better,” Marty said as he pressed the silver barrel againstthe skin beneath Snow’s chin. “Maybe you’re in on this with Johnny. Maybe you’re a fuckingfed. I ain’t never heard of no fucking Irish man in the Hattoni family, damn it!” “Put it down, Marty!” Snow persisted. “Put it down, now!” “Fuck you!” Marty said three inches from Snow’s face. “Alright,” Snow said softly. “Have it you’re way, you stupid prick. Keep waving a fucking gunaround - maybe that’ll keep you from getting caught with the heroin. Or better yet, blowmy fucking head off! I’m sure that won’t draw any attention!” Marty stood silent and stared into Snow’s cold blue eyes. Irish man or not, he knew Snow wasright. “You’re smarter than this, Marty,” said Snow. “Alright,” the young man finally answered as he slowly eased the gun away from Snow’s face. As soon as he was out of harm’s way, Snow struck. He grabbed Marty’s right hand, which heldthe gun, and pinned him mercilessly against the side of his black Benz. Marty’s eyes jumpedwith fear. Snow’s lurked menacingly. “You little prick,” Snow hissed an inch away from Marty’s face. “I should break your fuckingneck right here. You held a fucking gun on me, you cocksucker! I’ve got a wife and alittle girl at home, and you think I’m going to leave them alone at your grimy little bitchhands!?” Snow’s right hand gradually found its way to Marty’s throat and he applied a bit of pressure. Suddenly, he felt hands on his back. “Come on, Snow,” one of the other companions said. “We can’t do this. Not like this. Takeyour problems to Johnny and he’ll move them up.” Snow squeezed a little harder. “I could send you to hell,” he hissed at Marty. “Right fucking now! I could end your God damnlife, and I could do it easier than you could fucking imagine!” He let the young man stew on thatfor a moment, and then finally, released his grip. “Cocksucker,” he added as a parting shot. Marty immediately dropped to his knees and began coughing frantically in attempts to draw airinto his chest. “Forget about Johnny tonight,” Snow said finally to the others. “We’ll take the shipment tomy place since this fucker’s such a pussy. We’ll leave it in the trunk of my car, and I’llget in touch with Johnny in the morning. Lets get moving.” Reluctantly, the three men piled into the backseat of the Benz. Snow leaned down beside Marty and picked his gun up from the pavement. “Next time you point this at me,” Snow said as he handed the fire-arm to the young man, “you’dbetter fucking use it, because if you don’t, I will murder you. Don’t test me on that, oryou won’t live to know how I did.”
