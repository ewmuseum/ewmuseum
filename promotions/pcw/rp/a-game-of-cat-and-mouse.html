A Game of Cat and Mouse

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Decmeber 12, 2002 Standing at only four inches more than five feet, surely I was big enough now for people to stop telling me what to do and how to handle my life. I could take care of myself. Sure I was small but what people must remember is that good things come in small packages. I could easily be out muscled, but who could keep up with me? Absolutely no one. So why is it people worried about me so much? It was a simple game of cat and mouse. When the cat finally gained enough intelligence to outsmart that mouse, which was when I would worry. I was the mouse and my enemy was the cat, Shin Yagyuu. He might have gotten the first hit in, but this time his luck would not be the same. This time I would be quicker, smarter, and outsmart him no matter how much thinking it took. Under the snow white sheets I sat, trying my best to capture the heat emitting from my small figure and use it to keep me warm. It was three in the morning and there I sat not sure if I was awake or still dreaming, the only thought running through my mind, revenge, revenge in the greatest form. The cool breeze from outside hit me and then I knew I was awake. I curled up under the sheets Chris had lent me and tried my best to use my own heat to keep me warm. It was not working well against the strong winter breeze creeping in through the window. The breeze was much too intense though to only be seeping in through the slight cracks of a window, something had to be wide open but I tried my best to fight the cold and fall asleep. It didn’t work. I stood up from the couch I had crashed on inside of Chris Storm’s New York apartment and started to walk around looking for the source of this cold air. With my eyes half closed I walked around slowly and as quietly as I could to avoid waking up the children by clumsily bumping into something, whether it be a lamp or a chair. Reaching the front door I had noticed that it was wide open as another breeze blew, numbing my body completely. Someone had left the door open, but who? I was the last one up and I was no where near the front door before I went to sleep. While pondering in my mind whom it could have been I felt something furry creep between my legs and then run up against my leg. I looked down hearing a small cry of an animal and noticed that there was a cat the color of night looking for my attention through it cries and wide green eyes. The cat cried once more followed by the laughter of a child. I looked over in the direction of the opened front door and noticed that Christa was wide awake, her blue eyes filled joy. “Christa what on earth are you doing up at this hour of the night?” I said, walking towards Christa and away from the cat. The cat followed me following the same routine as before, only this time rubbing against Christa’s leg. “I heard something at the door so I went to see what it was.” She said, reaching out the cat and patting it as the cat stirred with excitement, letting its motor be heard loud and clear, causing a smile to emerge on Christa’s face. “Well it’s time to head on back to bed. Let’s get going.” I said, taking her by the hand and pulling her away from the cat. Christa looked at me with her big blue eyes looking more disappointed than a child who had received nothing but coal in their Christmas stocking. Instantly I knew what she was going to ask, but I tried my best to walk her away from the question and up into bed where she could dream of such things without having to turn them into a reality. “Can the cat come with me?” She said, pulling away from my grip while gravitating back towards the cat. The cat meowed once more, as if it too were pleading with me to let it stay. My heart melted but I could not give in. The cat had to go. This was Chris’ apartment, not my house, therefore nothing would stay without his permission and I was not about to wake him up over something of such small importance. He needed every bit of sleep that he could get just like me. “No.” I stated with one simple word which would transform me into the heel of the situation. I hated to say not, but who was I to answer for Chris? It was his apartment not mine and the last thing I wanted to do was to have him wake up to a zoo. Sure it was only one animal, but with that animal came a lot of responsibility, responsibility I had no time for. “Please Tessa! I’ll make sure it doesn’t make a mess of the house, I promise.” Her pleads turned more into whines, but I would not give in. I had to stand by my word no matter how difficult of a situation it became. “No means no, now get up to bed.” I stated my authority, leading her with a gentle grip towards the stairs as far away from the cat as I could get. She understood and walked up the stairs leaving me the winner, but not without the guilt sinking in first. I looked over at the cat and felt the guilt drown me, but I fought the guilt and somehow found the strength to open the front door once again and lead the cat out to its home, the streets. No creature should have to live on the streets in the cold during the dead of winter, but it had to be done. I did not want to wake up to Chris having a stroke because some weird animal was stomping around his apartment like he was the king of all just because he managed to win the affection of both Christa and I. Blocking the cat from my mind I retraced my steps back to the couch. I crawled under the sheets once more and closed my eyes, but the cold that had leaked in through the front door had now flushed out the warm air and left me shivering. I jumped up from the couch and brought my sheets along with me, wrapping them tightly around my body to keep as warm as possible. I started to head in the direction of the guest room but remembered that there was barely enough room for Christa and Alex as it was. Letting out a sigh I realized that Chris’ room was my last resort. I hated to do it, but I had no choice in the matter unless I wanted to live the life of an Eskimo for the remainder of the evening. I tip toed quietly into Chris’ room and stretched myself across the bed, lying my head on the other end, being careful not to poke Chris’ in the face with my feet and using the blanket that he had given me rather then climbing under the sheets with him. Sure I loved him with all my heart, but I still was not comfortable enough with getting that intimate with him, even if we were not doing anything under those sheets. I did not want to rush into this relationship even if we nearly spent every second of the day together. Using my arm as a pillow I shut my eyes once again more and began to drift off to sleep. Just a few more breaths and I would leave the real world behind for a few hours, and then return to it when the sun came up the next morning. I was so close to getting there before I felt something crawling up the sheets that were covering my legs. I giggled figuring it was Chris fooling around as always, but looking up at the other end of the bed I saw that Chris was fast asleep with her arms draped over the blankets. I looked down where my legs were and noticed the cat had snuck back into the house. He lay down on my legs and made himself at home. I wanted to kick my legs and scare the cat away, but if I did that I would more than likely end up kicking Chris in the process. So I laid back down figuring that if I left the cat alone and paid him no attention he would become bored with me and leave. I closed my eyes for what I hoped would be the last time in the evening. The room was overcome by silence. No heavy breathing, no cat purring, just pure silence. It did not last for long though. Out of no where Chris let out a capricious sneeze that broke the silence. He sat up in bed looking rather haggard, his hair pointing up in every direction. I quickly tossed the cat to the side suddenly remembering that Chris was allergic to cats. How could I forget something like that? I slapped my head feeling as a large dose of ignorance overcame me. How could I be so stupid? Chris started to stir and opened his eyes half way. I hoped he would not notice me at the end of the bed, but surely enough luck worked against me and Chris’ eyes opened obviously not sure if he could believe what he saw sitting before his eyes. My body froze and I looked back over at him as I felt my eyes lower. I couldn’t look him into the eyes because I felt as if I were trying to rush him into an even bigger commitment by sleeping in the same bed even if it were on opposite ends. He rubbed his eyes once more just to make sure that what he was seeing was real. It was indeed very real and it left him staring with the words being trapped in his throat. Finally realizing that this was indeed reality Chris crawled over to my side of the bed, wrapping his arm around my shoulder. He sneezed once more and I watched as his eyes watered up. Damn that cat. He brought out Chris’ weak side. I reached to dresser beside Chris’ bed and handed him a tissue. He wiped his nose and did his best to keep his eyes from watering anymore. I simply watched as he tossed the tissue to the side, making a basket as the tissue tumbled into the waste basket under the dresser. “Damn allergies.” Chris muttered to himself, pulling the blanket over both him and I. I laid my head on his shoulder as his head tilted to the side and locked with an object on the ground, widening at the sight. He saw the cat. Damn it, what was I going to do now? I looked down at my pink silk pajamas. They were covered in cat fur. I couldn’t deny having contact with the animal but at the same time I didn’t want it here in the first place. Chris simply sneezed once more and followed it with the words, “Tessa, what is that doing here?” “I don’t know.” I stated in brusque manner. It wasn’t a lie after all. I really did not know why the cat had come to haunt us. I only knew how it got in. “Then why are you covered in fur?” He said, brushing the fur off my pajamas and trying to get it as far away from him as possible. “I didn’t do it, it was Christa I swear!” I sounded like one of the children myself, but I had to let the truth be known. I didn’t want the cat, Christa did. I simply forgot Chris was allergic to cats, but maybe knowing that it was Christa who wanted the cat Chris would take this into consideration and let her keep the creature. “Just get it out of here.” He said, wiping his nose with a tissue looking about ready to cry. “Well I was going to ask you if…” I started up, getting ready to ask the killer question on Christa’s behalf. “No way Tessa Bear! I am not sharing a house with that thing! ” He cut in, clearly expressing his distaste for the animal. “Oh come on Chris, you aren’t afraid of sneezing occasionally just because of a little cat, are you?” “Then why won’t you let it stay?” I questioned, putting on pouting face while puffing out my cheeks to add tot eh effect. “ I am not afraid of that cat! Do you want me sneezing to death?” He said, wiping his nose once more. “Of course not.” I said feeling more than defeated. I gave into him, hugging him before standing up and picking up the cat. I felt horrible having to put the cat out in the dead of winter, but Chris meant much more to me than that cat whom I had never seen before. The cat had caused Chris pain; therefore I couldn’t not bother to have any sympathy with it. The same held true for Shin. He brought harm in Chris’ direction, therefore he must pay, and he would. He would pay more than anyone and that was more than a promise, it was a guarantee. (Sorry for the repost but I forgot to add what I was using this one for =()
