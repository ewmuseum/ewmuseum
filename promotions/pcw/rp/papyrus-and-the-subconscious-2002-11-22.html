Papyrus and the Subconscious

22 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: Papyrus and the Subconscious Featuring: Tim Walden Date: November 22, 2002 The papers strewn about the 'office' that was Tim Walden's hotel room made for a scene that was, to say the least, chaotic on this late afternoon..The stress had creeped in, and the once relatively pristine piles of papers had given way to messy stacks spilled left and right, Tim in the middle of them intently reading over a thick report on the desk. He reaches up, and grabs his forehead, throwing aside the pages for the moment...The strain on his eyes from the dim lighting having made its presence felt, Tim pushes himself away, pulling himself up to his feet with an audible groan before dragging himself the few feet to the bed, falling into the papers thereupon in a full bellyflop. He stays face-down, immersing his face in the pillow for a few seconds before rolling over onto his back, eyes still shut but opening slowly toward the ceiling.. 'Damned eyestrain, never had to deal with it beforeagain...Givin' me a headache just trying to get the forms done, need to cool down and relax....' Relaxes a little too deeply... Tim suddenly finds himself alone. This would probably not be much of a surprise, since he was alone in the room, but he has found himself alone in the center of a PCW ring.....'Hello?' The echoes return quickly and clearly, signs of the emptiness of the arena....Walden looks out, wide-eyed with surprise....'Is anybody there?' A faint response this time, perhaps a little muffled....Walden slowly turns to see the source of the noise, a look of irritation beginning to cross his face.....And is met with a vision of the Trapezoid's charge, diving forward to deliver the infamous low headbutt known as the Minus Sign. Tim winces, closing his eyes for the pain..But none comes. The ring is still there, but now he hears cheers as well, evidence of a crowd gathered....He opens his eyes to get a better idea of the situation, but the crowd isn't there...The cheers increase nonetheless, and Tim suddenly whirls around, leaping up to avoid another attack... Only to hear the clatter of boot...Against metal.....Against skull. He falls to the mat, surprisingly in fine condition. More than can be said for the HIT, tumbling to the mat, ladder laying across his upper body..Walden looks down in surprise as the boos break out from the crowd, then stares upward at the Cruiserweight Title dangling from the rafters... He has to do it now. The last few days must have been a blur, he doesn't even know if he succeeded before, but it's time that he ends this and gives himself some glory. Tim rushes forward, stomping down on the ladder and eliciting a yelp of pain from the downed opponent before grabbing the ladder, setting it up to ensure steadiness by pulling the legs apart. The ladder's, not the Trapezoid's, though that may have been a good idea at the time...He clambers up the steps, one by one, easily as he waltzed through the tournament..Boca, Tomas, Heavy Metal Harry? Tim pauses, shaking his head as the hisses persist..He pulls himself up to the second-highest step of the ladder, then makes a lunging grasp at the belt, brushing it with his fingertips before accepting its existance out of his current reach.. ...He has to go to the next step to win this one. Tim pulls his hands down, steadying himself against the ladder on the other side as he puts the first foot upon the top rung...Only to feel the ladder tilting, looking down to see the shape sending it tumbling forward, Walden's arms on the opposite side helping send him into a forward roll, he flinches once more, feeling the pressure of going end-over-end still as the crowd goes wild...And he hits with a resounding impact. The pain isn't nearly as bad as he imagined, Tim says to himself..Maybe he was numbed by the pain itself, maybe something'd happened, maybe he'd just managed to catch a lucky break when his back made contact...Perhaps today he was invincible once again. No, he still felt something..The pain began to reach his brain. He wasn't on the mat, though....He rolls off, and looks over to see the Trapezoid's body flat on the canvas, still writhing in pain, but not out of it...Glancing up, then across the ring to the side...Tomas was there, and he knew once again what was needed...He rolls toward the opposite corner, up to his hands and knees, looking out to the turnbuckles... Just as he'd hoped, Brandi was leaning over them, eager to get in and take the match home...He starts crawling, dragging himself toward the corner with all of the energy in his being..This would be the turning point, she was relatively fresh to the match and would be just enough to take the momentum back into their favour...He reaches out his hand once more, waving it in an effort to get the tag, just a few inches short, he had to push off with his leg... ...But the other one isn't going anywhere..Brandi's face goes wide with horror, as Tim is dragged back to the center of the ring, looking backward to see the Trapezoid holding him in an anklelock..He tries to roll forward and through, but the blasted shape rolls him back toward his own corner..Tim desperately rolls onto his back, trying to kick his way out of it, but he's brought right back to his stomach, no matter how he tries to roll, always brought back to the inevitable...Until suddenly, it ends... ...With a thud. Tim falls out of the bed, papers scattering over his body. The impact does the trick to shake him back into reality, as he slowly regains his bearings..It felt so real, the emotional pain was still lingering...He needed to forget it, just let it go, this was unrealistic..He made the Trapezoid tap before, he could systematically destroy him if need be.. The emotional pain had to leave, there was no room for this sort of underconfidence...It was nothing more than a dream, and not even a realistic one at that, he couldn't afford to make such severe errors as in the dream so consistently anyway and he knew it. There would be pain, no doubt about it..4 matches in one night tended to have that effect. However, that pain was not so bad, he knew that he could win if he gave it his all, it would be lessened by the gleam of victory... If only paper cuts had the same property. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
