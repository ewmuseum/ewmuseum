Hunting The Hunter

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01 December Walking down the street like a re-enactment of the famous Reservoir Dogs strut and every bit as devious, the dynamic duo of Pranks With Attitude were hot on the heels of a monster. Not some fictional creature, dreamt up in the minds of writers and story tellers who wish to entertain the masses, but a genuine entity that has been manipulated by a maniac, not just any maniac... Skitzo. The behemoth Nerezza camouflaged his hatred of the sun by shielding beneath dark sunglasses whilst Gaz 'The Jester' Green openly basked, inviting the warmth on his face. Almost in unison the duo stopped, pivoted and peered as a young mother struggled to the sidewalk, her child in tow. The scene almost amused the Jester as they retraced the steps of a new force to be reckoned with, a person yet to feel the Darker Side Of Comedy, a tie that had now be severed. 'This makes a welcome change from lounging about in my apartment, don't you think?' asked the Jester, grinning like a Chestershire cat and loving every glare his celebrity status draws from the natives of this dank town. 'This makes a welcome change from watching Natalia manipulating you, molding you, almost wiping your personality clean and-....' With an untimely interruption, Gaz instantly reacts to the comments of his partner. Even though it has been well documented that Nerezza saw ulterior motives in Natalia's assistance, Gaz finds her presence welcoming yet even he has niggling doubts that haven't yet surfaced. 'Easy Tiger! Need I remind you that if it wasn't for her assistance against Playaz Inc, then we may to this very day be challengers rather than Champions. Personally I have some unanswered questions I'd like to get off my chest but as it stands I see her glowing face a little more inviting than the snarls we receive from the roster!' The vampire merely shrugged off his partners retaliating comments and the Pranks With Attitude collective made their way down a lone street that sat ominously on the outskirts of the town. Almost a ghost town, with those out in the daylight looking wary and paranoid, intimidated by an unforeseen presence. 'Was this paranoia a side-effect of the monster Skitzo had aligned himself with?' 'Is this a typical suburban town, after all Gaz Green is from the bright lights of Vegas and rarely ventures from his beloved City Of Sin?' 'Or was this crowd reaction brought on by their very presence?' As it stood, there was more questions than answers, yet the gold laden Pranks With Attitude were prepared to go the distance in unearthing the essence of this mystery Skitzo touched upon. 'I wonder if they know you’re a vampire and that's what has got them spooked?' questioned the Emerald Menace in a moment of de ja vu, almost a copyright infringing moment. 'Well unless they have E.S.P (Extra Sensory Perception) I have no tell tale signs, my complexion could be deemed a human condition!' hissed the Angel Of Fear, the word 'Human' leaving a bitter taste that could only be disguised by the injection of fresh blood. Still hotfooted they storm towards the tree line, woodland, possibly a forest, which had a dusty footpath that was more of a warning than an invite. Ignoring the tell tale signs that this area was not so much forbidden but forgotten their curiosity pressed them further on in their quest to put a name to the monster. 'Stay well clear-... You don't want to go there-... Some stranger earlier ignored my pleads and has since perished!' said a voice from behind. The Conniving Collective turned to greet their assailant who was revealed as a hunched individual, his face partially masked by the hood of his tunic and the odor he adorned was almost tangible. With Gaz's face cringing, Noscosto Ireul Nerezza was forced to intervene. 'Perished? Where's your proof old man?' 'He vanished, like before, no body, nor signs of his return!' retorted the demented old fool as he squared up to the towering figure of Nerezza who stood firm, motionless the odds certainly favoured the Primetime Championship Wrestling superstars. With silence, Gaz twitched under his jacket, preparing to hit home 'The Most Electrifying Move In Primetime' when suddenly the unknown individual whaled like a banshee. 'You're one of them!!! GETOUTTAMAWAY!!!' The man brushed past the Gaz Green and Nerezza blockade, stumbling, falling to a heap he continued down the street screaming like a biblical fanatic, those old men who try to force their beliefs upon you, always crying Armageddon and Judgment Day. 'I'm glad he's gone, I almost painted the town red!' murmured the vampire licking the excess saliva from his now extended canines. Saliva swishing inside his mouth like a washing machine had now caused his chin to drown in body fluid, teasing the blood sucker, making Gaz a little on edge and wary of his partner. 'Well we have a fitting setting squire-... This town could have easily appeared in a George Romero flick, I'm getting a 'Texas Chainsaw Massacre' vibe!?!' yelped the Lord Of Laughter. 'Hmmm - MASSACRE!!!' continued the Creature Of The Night. With a whistling wind and the sway of branches, almost like fingers calling over the duo, both Gaz Green and Nerezza ushered forward. Stepping onto the dirt ridden road, which sank under their weight they pressed on, pushing aside the overgrown plant life and feeling wary themselves for whatever resides here has at-least left a spine chilling hallmark on the town folk. 'Even this place gives me the willy's!' whispered the Child Of Satan, Nerezza. 'Hey, I'd rather have this place give me the willy's than Steven Smith giving me the willy's, huh?!?' replied the Jester, his fear seeping through with his tone of voice. Both men looked at one another and nodded in unison as they trampled further into the unknown. With howling and the snapping of branches, the rustling of leaves, Gaz personified fear, a picture of trauma, only spurned on by the thought that this nightmare may end. 'I'll give credit to Skitzo, he had the balls to go through with this!' said Gaz Green, his mouth trembling and his words slightly stuttering with the chatter of his teeth. 'THAT'S WHY HE'S A PSYCHO~!' roared the attitude half of P.W.A with authority. Picking up pace and almost re-enacting a scene from the Blair Witch Project P.W.A stormed through the overhanging greenery of bushes and trees as though something had picked up on their scent and was stalking them in the darkness. Nerezza remained a pillar of strength but Gaz was becoming a liability when suddenly, as both men looked back a hand crashed down on the shoulder of one terrified Jester. - THUD - 'WHAT THE FU-....' Standing with a pistol at hand and guarding the infamous Shady Acres Asylum For The Mentally Disabled was the clerk of this institute. His black uniform ridden in grime, his face a dwelling for dirt, his eyes squint and watchful, a grotesque individual but a welcome sight though Gaz 'The Jester' Green. 'How can I help you'se guys?' choked the guard, spraying saliva over the PCW Tag Team Champions with one splutter after another. 'Arr-... Err-... Green. My name is Green, I phoned earlier about doing a promotional piece for Primetime Championship Wrestling!' retorted the Jester regaining his normal rhythmical pattern of his breathing. 'Right you are. Mr. Green we agreed that you could checkout the dwelling of Skitzo's mystery partner, but be warned that the inmates here are not to be toyed with-... These be some shady beings young sirs!' concluded the guard with a sinister hiss as he lead the pair of PCW employee's into the compound passing the complexes nametag. Shady Acres Asylum for the Mentally Disabled... Passing a metallic door and waltzing down a flight of stairs, they enter a concrete chamber/ corridor where the inmates reside, enclosed behind Perspex glass and cages, never to see the light of day-... Supposedly. Slowly creeping down this corridor, they pass a variety of monsters, well inmates, but monsters of society. 'Hannibal Lecter-... Charles Manson-... Fred and Rose West-... The Washington Bigfoot!' read out Nerezza with glee, as his associate Gaz Green peered into their holding cells. 'Ugh - We have quite the celebrity function here. Between Bigfoot, Dracula, The Krays we have enough people to make a tea party... Oh, I do hope the Tooth Fairy would make an appearance!?!' said the Prince Of Pranks as he peered into the cell of 'Bigfoot' which was occupied by a man dressed horribly in a fur coat, covering his face with a poor excuse for a Halloween mask and looking nothing short of ridiculous. Suddenly both members of P.W.A turned to the camera, both adorning sinister grins and looking somewhat devious, as though they knew something we didn't. With the camera turning we see that this charade had taken place in a movie studio, with this Asylum being nothing more than a film set built from cardboard, a hoax performed by the master of pranks and pulled off by his equally impressive accomplice Nerezza. 'You see Skitzo we could care less about the joke you have taken on board, after all it would require a true fool to align with such vermin-... Your old run-in buddy Syko knew you were deadweight and has since left you to yourself-... Well at Alliances saw twelve Skitzo's pinned, well we shall go one better and kill twelve Skitzo's!' proclaimed the King Of Komedy, looking stern and a vice versa of his recent attitude of fear. 'Happy hunting-....' bellowed Nerezza as the charade drew to a close. With the scene closing the sound of set workmen filtered with clangs and the banging of metal, eventually panning out to nothing more than silence and a snow filled picture. - The End -
