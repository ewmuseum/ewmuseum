The final score (Part 2)

11 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 11, 2002 [Fast forward a few minutes to a white hallway where The Large guy, dressed in black, who sticks out like a red guy in a blue man group concert in the white hall. Walking with the monster of the man is the young pro wrestler/bounty hunter, Eddie Mandrake. They make their way down the hallway and go into an open elevator. The monster places a key, which he wears around his neck under his clothes, into the keyhole. He turns it and the doors close and the elevator begins to move. Eddie looks at the monster, but not at his face. He stares at the gold lapel pin. It glistens in the light of the elevator. Eddie thinks for a second…Then finally speaks.] Eddie: Is that what I think it is? Large guy: Nope. It’s not. [He pulls the pin away from the light. Eddie gets a good look at the colored sections of the pin. He recognizes the pin now.] Eddie: It is what I think it is. Large guy: Just drop it, ok? Eddie: I know what it is, don’t deny it. Large guy: Just drop it…Now. Eddie: I know what it is…it a C… Large guy: [BEEP] off. We are here. Eddie: Alrighty, I’ll shut up now. [The doors slide open. It looks almost exactly like Carl Grissom’s/The Joker’s office from the Batman movie. A desk sitting at the far end of the room, with a large chair turned toward the window. The remaining effects are almost placed exactly like Carl Grissom’s/The Joker’s office. A living room ensemble to the right of the desk…after that, it’s mainly floor space. Most like for the boss to practice the golf shot. On the couches that are in the room, we see two other men. One of them is playing “The-knife-game-where-you-place-your-hand-on-the-table-and-stab-the-knife-in-the-table-trying-to-avoid-your-fingers-game.” The other is fooling around with an M-14 assault rifle. Mandrake walks onto the tile floor, and everyone gets ready to kill him, however the monster speaks up.] Large guy: Guys, he’s cool. He’s our help for this mission. [Everyone who matters goes back to what they were doing before Eddie walked in. Eddie sits in a chair in the living room set. The “chair”, reveals a hand to the large guy. The large guy kills all light in the room, and a projector comes on. The chair begins the lecture.] Chair: Mr. Edward Mandrake. I am extremely pleased that you accepted our invitation for this party. Eddie: I do have other engagements, so please, if you could make this brief. Chair: Very well, I shall try to make it as brief as possible. [A slide pops up onto the white screen that has a single title. Corporate Assassination: Plan A The chair raises his hand again for the large guy.] Chair: As you can see Mr. Mandrake, this whole plot has one massive bounty headed up by the Chief Executive Officers of Enron, K-Mart, WorldCom, Nortel Networks, and a few dozen other companies who have been exposed with accounting fraud. Your mission is somewhat simple. [The slide changes to two pictures with a crosshair overlay on each one.] Chair: Your mission, should you choose to accept it is the following. President Bush, Chief of Staff Colin Powell, and a few other government officials are coming to Vegas to break ground on a new IRS Tax return sorting facility here. Now, due to the recent corporate responsibility act that the President has just signed, makes my employees, rather uncomfortable, and can put my entire operation under a very large microscope. I, for one will not stand for this. Therefore, I sent out a general e-mail to all bounty hunters who wanted in on this big score. Your task, Mr. Mandrake, is to assassinate either President Bush, or Chief of Staff Powell during the ceremony. Eddie: Who are the other backers of this quote-unquote “operation”? Al-Qaeda? Maybe Hamas? How about just some redneck KKK chapter in Alabama? Chair: What if I told you…all were involved in this as well. Al-Qaeda put up a good half a million in the bounty in order to be involved, Hamas put in two hundred and fifty grand. Eddie: If I’m as famous as you say I am…you know my stance on working for terrorist outfits. Chair: True. The other CEO’s put up WAY more money than the terror groups did. They just wanted to be in on it. Eddie: Heh. Please. Anything that involves the death of a U.S. President, Most Arab Terror Groups are in on it. Chair: Anyway. If you choose no right now, I will have my assistant take you to the cashier downstairs; you can get your twenty-five grand, and leave in peace. Not one question asked. Choose yes, and you’ll be in for the long haul. The mission must be completed. Also, if you chicken out of this mission at any time, my boys, Jimmy and Carlo, will kill you without restraint. Eddie: I would like to see them try. [Carlo (The Knife Guy) took extreme offence to that.] Carlo: What the [BEEP] did you just call me? Eddie: I didn’t call you anything; I just said you couldn’t kill me even if you had a tank. Carlo: Oh. Okay. [Carlo takes a seat again. Not even realizing that he’d been insulted again.] Chair: I need a decision today however Mr. Mandrake. Right now preferred. Eddie: For $10,000,000. I’m not gonna turn down one final big score. After this though. I am out of the business…for good. Chair: Excellent. Boys, show Mr. Mandrake to the Cashier, and then to the bank. [Jimmy and Carlo get Eddie up, and escort him to the elevator. They board it and Jimmy turns his key. The doors close, leaving the Chair and the large man in the room.] Chair: He said he wanted out of the business, Eric. If, by chance he has second thoughts and fails the mission. I want you to bring him to me…alive. Eric: I shall do as you ask. You paid my bail bond, my legal fees, and got me reinstated in all the casinos that I have been banned from, and for that I am extremely grateful. Chair: You better be. Edward better be grateful that I do not give him the same fate that he gives his victims. The three D’s that Edward follows. Disemboweling, Dismemberment, and Decapitation. I will be the one to enjoy it if Mandrake fails to kill the President. Eric: I doubt he will fail, sir. Chair: Do I pay you to think? No. Now, go and help escort Mr. Mandrake to the back, you promised. Eric: Yeah, Yeah, I’m going. [The elevator returns as Eric heads for it. He walks into it, and turns his magical key which makes the doors close. The Chair is the only object remaining in the room.] Chair: Yes, Edward…You will complete your mission. Or I will expose you to the entire world, and you will be finished! [Now enter the Dr. Evil laugh, and fade to black.]
