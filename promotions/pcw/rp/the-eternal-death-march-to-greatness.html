The Eternal Death March to Greatness

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: not entered Enigma: Welcome…… {{The camera pans around a dark room looking for Enigma}} Enigma: Come a little closer. {{Still unsure of where Enigma is, the cameraman reaches for the flashlight mounted on top of the camera and flicks the switch to ‘on’. Suddenly Enigma appears in the shadow of the light. Seated on a chair a few yards away, Enigma glares at the camera and indicates for the camera to zoom in a little closer. Enigma runs his hands over the top of his mask, but all the while never takes his eyes off the camera}} Enigma: Over the past few months I have led a confused life. On one hand I joined forces with my good friend, Flaymin Aymin…..And yet on the other hand I found myself succumbing to his way of life….And losing focus of the goal I set myself when I entered PCW. Flaymin, you and I are like brothers.We squabble and bicker but deep down inside we have an undeniable bond that will never be broken. However this worries me….. {{Enigma momentarily adjusts his mask}} Enigma: Because what we are being asked to do by that psychotic nemesis of yours, El Diablo, goes beyond any brotherhood ties. We’re being asked to fight.Not only that, we’re being asked to destroy each other…And victory shall only come when one of us is alight and writhing in the torture of flames. And it sickens me to think that this match is nothing short of a ploy to get us to turn on one another and create ‘in-fighting’ within our relationship. Therefore, in advance I’m asking you not to take the nature of our match too personal.Because Flaymin, I’m not going to hold back out there at Spring Fling. As much as I’d like to go easy on you and as much as it hurts me to be forced to attack you in that ring- I can’t go back on my morals. {{Enigma stands up and begins to walk around the room, still speaking to the camera}} Enigma: You see, I have been in PCW now for approximately two months and what do I have to show for it? Not a single match for the Shooting Star title….Nor any opportunities to prove myself in the Cruiserweight Division. In other words….. I’m done nothing. And that fact alone is eating me up alive and corroding my soul as every day passes and someone new is given their golden opportunity whilst I…. Suffer. Flaymin, now you see why I have to do what I have to do.It’s not personal.It’s not prove myself against you- It’s to prove myself to PCW.It’s to prove myself to the world. Enigma has arrived and at Spring Fling our match will definitely open up some eyes.It will make people sit up and watch.They’ll take interest in our match, they’ll be over-joyed with the effort we’ll display…. But most importantly- they will recognise Enigma as an individual.As a serious wrestler who shall not be pitied- but feared. And Aymin, I know you’ll help me achieve this is. We’ve come a long way together and this is just another stop in our journey to greatness.While we are likely to take different paths in achieving this greatness I know that whatever the situation or scenario we’ll always be able to destroy that crazed madman, El Diablo. After everything that asshole has put us through not only over the last few months but over the last year or so- he deserves to be beaten to a pulp and you can always count on me to help you do so. So, the message to all my other competition in PCW is this…. The death march begins at Spring Fling and it won’t conclude until I have proven myelf…. And until I achieve the greatness I covert and desire so passionately. At Spring Fling you will see an Enigma, hardcore enough to match it with Flaymin and yet nimble enough to send shivers down the spine of every cruiserweight in PCW. You will all bear witness to a revolutionary wrestler whom can’t be beat and as the time wears on the PCW titles should fall within my grasp. First the Shooting Star Title….Then the Cruiserweight Title…. And this death march to greatness will not stop until I have won the most coveted title of them all- The PCW World Heavyweight Championship. Enjoy the journey….Experience the thrills….. Because…. MY TIME HAS COME! {{The camera fades out}}
