Origin - The Future

25 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th April 2003 The events of this role-play are complete fabrication and have no truth whatsoever so therefore should be taken by a pinch of salt. These events are actually set in a utopia future, somewhere imbedded in the mind of Primetime Championship Wrestling’s resident Jester The now defunct Dark Wrestling Alliance that birthed the Jester’s first wrestling incarnation of New Blood hasn’t been spoken of in years and remains isolated in the minds of those who once attended. New Blood’s Double Gold Championship reign means absolutely nothing, as does the company’s short tenure amongst other promotions working the very same circuit. “What does matter is the Phoenix scenario, where the gimmick of New Blood died and from its ashes rose its successor in the Jester” Common knowledge is that the Jester once adorned Wrestle Online’s Academy and his stint there was mentored by the Old School Wrestling goliath Hessian who has since hanged up his boots, his memory documented alongside further greats in the communities ‘Hall Of Fame’. Pleased was the Mercenary Of Satan when his student broke from the shackles of the Academy and into the long since forgotten New True Internet Championship Wrestling but that warm hearted smile would be nothing in comparison to that he now adorns when the name Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green is murmured in his direction. Long gone is the ‘Old Guard’ and with them the memory of Wrestle Tattler, RUSH, PURE and some of the fondest memories of past accomplishments like Alan Kriegman’s Universal Championship victory over the mobster and current Pro Wrestling Circuit Champion Angelo Deville. Long gone from the public eye, can people really forget epic encounters like Xavier Kannon against Jeremy Howard and across the waves Communism versus Capitalism in Ivan Stanislav tackling his long-time rival Tyler Nelson. “Can people forget the birth and run of the greatest tag team of the Third Age in Pranks With Attitude?” Arguably ‘No’ for their accomplishments still holds strong in Primetime Championship Wrestling, accomplishments like the longest title reign and number of individual reigns with the gold but time has past since the team once graced the community, a community that forever changes. No pun intended but the community now oozes with new blood, people ignorant to the past and more concerned with the present and it is THIS present that sets the stage. Pranks With Attitude the collective has since evaporated and its occupiers in Nascosto Ireul Nerezza and Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green have been hard at work establishing them selves as individual stars. Their tag team label, the oppression even has vanished and both members continue to defy the critics by continually climbing the corporate ladder. Some of the greatest angles and segments concerning the Conniving Collective have been lost in the sands of time, the days when people laughed at the expense of Byron Calver and their conceited orchestrating of the Tessa Windsor marriage, which coincidentally allowed the duo to worm their way into a PCW Tag Team Championship bout. The days when they were aligned as fan favourites, fighting against the wrath of the Front Office, ironically a stable they later joined but all the nostalgia has faded away with only the veterans able to appreciate these historical moments. When the Jester and Vampire first appeared on Primetime television the roster was to the brim in quality but over the months those household names vanished from public eye, names on the magnitude of Jason Snow, Duke Williams, Clyde and Xavier Kannon. Their successors still carrying the torch and trying desperately to keep the home fires burning but Primetime Championship Wrestling continues to change and who would’ve thought that the Front Office would be once again resurrected. This latest incarnations genesis courtesy of one of its only surviving members in the Emerald Menace, his flock consisting of the future of this industry, replacing Pranks With Attitude with Entertainment Extreme’s Tyler Preston and John Gargano. Then finally this quartet is concluded with the Jester’s very own wife who turned her back on both Chris Storm and Jackson Rust for Tessa Windsor-Green had finally turned her allegiances to the dark side. Before all is explained the separation between the Vampire and Jester was on good terms and occasionally the bond continues to resurface. The Living Nightmare strangled his authority over the Continental division while his former friend and partner fought with desperation down in the Cruiserweight but the argument over their singles success had finally been quenched. The Jester handing the torch to his wife meant he could take the next step in his quest for World accomplishment and Entertainment Extreme (E2) were filling the void left by their mentors absence in the tag division, so nothing was standing in his way. “Nothing but the current Champion in Violence Jack and the long fruitful list of contenders all nipping at his heels, established names like Pharaoh, Totally Tubular Tommy, El Diablo and Skylar Green!” Being associated with the Front Office means that there’s always a legal loophole in such matters and being the evil mastermind behind some dustily deeds such as live liposuction to Two Ton and the early retirement to Chris Storm then the Champion played this game on borrowed time. While the Jester prepares to add World to his list of accolades the Angel Of Fear would be making the same transition very soon but first he would be dealing with the infidel Dorn Tracer during the same card of Third Dawning. “The place where it all started” The stage had been set and the place that birthed the beginning, the first couple of cobbles aligning the yellow brick road to recognition were finally coming to an end as Third Dawning’s card homed the match the rookie Jester had craved ever since that debut years earlier. Beneath the opportunity to adorn World Heavyweight gold the Vampire would be pitted to defend against the astronomer Dorn Tracer with the winner automatically facing the Champion from the Main Event at the next Pay-Per-View thus meaning… Continental Champion versus World Heavyweight Champion Possibly… Nascosto Ireul Nerezza versus Gaz ‘The Jester’ Green After months of separation and pursing other avenues the deceased Pranks With Attitude would be resurrected but not as an entity for the card would once again read ‘Partner vs. Partner’ however with much greater stakes. This luscious bout had already played out on two separate occasions and both men hold a victory over one another but now the bar had finally rose to the point where history would be left exactly where it should be, The Past. “If this is to be the future then its time to make a name for oneself in the present by starting at Tuesday Turmoil” - The End -
