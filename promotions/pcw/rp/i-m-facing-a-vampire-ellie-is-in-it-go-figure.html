Title: I'm Facing A Vampire & Ellie~! Is In It... Go Figure Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: Jan 10-12... Yah, that burned “Do Scientologists dream of Dianetic Sheep?” Man, that was lame. Our shot fades in on the sleeping face of PCW CEO Xavier Kannon, his face having expelled the mask of arrogance and contempt he wears during the daytime hours. In his slumber, Xavier is just another man, he does no evil and he does no wrong, just sleeps and dreams. Xavier : “Hmmmmmmmm-uh...” Rolling onto his side, the Scientologist stretches out fully, like a cat basking in the high, hot summer sun on the patio, exhaling heavily in a content way before grasping the pillow to his head once more. “Is he, like, STILL asleep?” Walking through the door into the poorly lit room comes Ellie~!, hands on hips and muttering to herself. Unlike the sleeping CEO she’s perky and dressed, seeming impatient for her husband to do the same. Eleanor : “Er.... HELLO?!?!? Like, morning and stuff!” She flicks the light switch, drenching the room in electric light and causing the narrowly opening eyes of Xavier to retreat behind his hand to protect them from the sudden influx of harsh brightness his optic nerve carries to his brain. Xavier : “Ghar....” Whipping a pillow from under his head and over his face, Kannon waves his hand at the light switch, gesturing for Ellie~! to turn it off. Eleanor : “Like... Get up! I want some toast.” Grumbling through the pillow, the words of the angry and still dazed Xavier are just about audible as his wife waits impatiently still. Xavier : “Can’t you make it yourself... I’m tired.” Looking back in a mixture of shock and surprise, PCW’s preppy Commissioner exclaims. Eleanor : “Hello?!?! Since when did I become, like, MasterChef of the Universe and Stuff?” Her pale Blue eyes not removing their gaze from the pillow Xavier holds over his face, Ellie~! quickly adds something. Eleanor : “Oh, and before I forget, I’mnowthedarkandevilundead.” Removing the pillow from over his face, the tired eyes of Xavier look to Ellie~!, confused and thinking he misheard her. Xavier : “Huh?” With a perky smile crossing her mouth, Ellie~! shrugs her little shoulders. Eleanor : Nothing! Now... Ellie~! toast!” Groaning, Kannon collapses his head back onto the pillow and whispers just loud enough for his wife to hear. Xavier : “Just give me another 10 minutes...” Pulling the bedclothes over himself once more, Xavier tries to drift back off and maybe rejoin whatever dream was playing in his head where he left it. Ellie~!, on the other hand, has other ideas. Eleanor : “Er...... Do I know any Romanian? Hmm... Vnaglachiou sounds right....” She stalks around the bed, and slowly eases herself onto her side so as not to wake of her man. With her knees resting on the mattress she places her left hand on the headboard and leans over above Xavier, staring at her neck. Eleanor : “Wait, curtains... Don’t, like, wanna fry myself and stuff!” After quickly checking over her shoulder, Ellie~! leans down and softly kisses Xavier’s neck, bringing a soft smile to his face and nearly dragging him from his sleep. Again her Pink lips press against his neck, Kannon’s body reacting before Ellie~! rears her head up. Eleanor : “Like...... VNAGLACHIOU!!!!” Thrusting her neck down and forward, the little prep digs her teeth into her husband’s neck as hard as she can, causing his eye-lids to dart apart and a yelling to find it’s way from his mouth. Xavier : “OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOWWWWW!!!!” His hand reaches around and grabs his neck as Ellie~!’s teeth pull out from his neck, Xavier pushing her away with his other arm so that she falls off the bed with a thud. Eleanor : “Eeeeeeeeeeeeeek!” Cussing and grabbing his neck, Kannon’s screws his face up and scurries off the bed and up to his feet, his teeth grating against each other with the pressure as his body absorbs the pain. Eleanor : “Er... Like.. OWWWWWWZIES! What was that for!” Pulling herself up off the floor on the other side of the bed, dusting herself off and looking none too happy about her tumble, Ellie~! looks to him, annoyed. Xavier’s eyes widen in disbelief, showing the traces of blood on his palm. Xavier : “What in Hubbard’s name was THAT for?!?” Ellie~! looks to him as if he just asked the most obvious question ever, and obvious to Ellie~! is obvious indeed. Her eyebrows raise as se explains. Eleanor : “HELLO?!?!? Like, SO ‘Creature of the Night’ here!” Kannon’s eyes just roll back as he sighs. Xavier : “You’re what now? And how come you can’t ever just suddenly decide to be a Cheerleader who does Porn all of a sudden? Ellie~!?” She doesn’t reply to her name, rather looks at him blankly wondering who he is addressing. Eleanor : “Er... I’m not this Ellie~! you speak of... Although her name WAY rules!” Shaking his head, Kannon dares ask. Xavier : “So, who are you... Wait, don’t tell me...” Pondering over all the Vampiric possibilities, he settles on one. Xavier : “Nerezzellie?” Her pale Blue eyes just look back in confusion. Eleanor : “Isn’t Nerezzellie’s, like, the Pizza place around the corner... YAY PIZZA... With Blood! YAY BLOOD!” Kannon looks a bit queasy as he sees Ellie~! lick some of his own blood off her Pink lips, before tilting his head and raising his brow to prompt an answer. Eleanor : “Oh, yeah... I’m Vampir Ellieferatu!” With his head in his hands, Kannon mutters to himself. Xavier : “I should have guessed.” Overhearing him, Ellie~! replies inquisitively. Eleanor : “WOW! Does that mean, I’m like Telepathic and stuff to.... Okay, let me try....” Holding a finger to each of her temples, Ellie~! stares right at him with her vacuous eyes. Eleanor : “Like, make me some toast! And blood... YAY BLOOD!” Staring back blankly, Xavier can only think of one thing to say. Xavier : “It’s telepathy.... You’re not meant to say it too.” Ellie~! just stands there, fingers on temples staring at Xavier who’s still slightly grimacing from his neck wound. He tries waving but Ellie~!’s deep in telepathic focus, waiting for Kannon to carry out his command. A minute passes and still she doesn’t move, Kannon stepping from side to side without a reaction from Ellie~! before he just shrugs his shoulders. Xavier : “Oh well.” Seeing Ellie~!’s doing something Ellie~!ish and will probably be at it a few more hours, Kannon drops back down onto the bed and pulls the covers over him, leaving Ellie to continue her trance looking at the wall as the shot fades to Black. “If she wasn’t so ditzy... Wait, I don’t really want to think about what might happen then...” ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
