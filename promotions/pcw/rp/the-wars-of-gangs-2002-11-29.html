The Wars Of Gangs

29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29-11-02 What traitorous raison d'être would an individual have, for attacking one that had caused no harm or mishap to him? Well, you may think no harm or misfortune had been caused, but your ignorance shall soon evolve to knowledge, for the one known as Jamie Celt always has a morale for his doings, and this is one where justice has not nearly be served. Yes, the vindictive ways of the Chinese Gang, the Triads, had left many innocent members of the sophisticated Celtic Organization wounded; no morale was given for this show of aggression, so why would such an act be committed. What shallow drive would one have to commit an operation such as the one, which was vilely performed? When no reason is giving for such a crime, then revenge, vengeance and retribution much be won! Yes, and it was, for the Celtic Organization retaliated for these acts of disrespect and humiliation. They stopped the heist that the Triads intended to take place at Boston harbour, and took the goods for themselves; this attack was lead by the son of the Celtic Organization, one Jamie Celt. He loved every second of it; the adrenaline rushing through his body like a speeding car driving to is peril. He felt so alive and invigorated; something that wrestling was taking away from him. Yes, he still saw wrestling as a big part of his life. Yes, he was already rich and prosperous, but he felt something was missing, and wrestling was the missing peace to the though-as unconquerable puzzle. Though wrestling had not been all 'roses and chocolates.' PCW was going badly, to put it lightly; he had been on a losing streak for quite some time. Why? He did not know and feared if he found out it could ruin him. Injury? A possibility, depression? Maybe, lose of love for Wrestling? Never. His knee had indeed been giving him quite a lot of despair over the previous weeks; he just hoped it would hold up for him. He did not want to go to a Doctor, but he promised himself that if it still impaired him after his match on Fusion, he would have it checked out. His match on Fusion was a big one, for him anyway. He went up against PCW's most prosperous newcomer, Pete~! Though the name makes him sound like a mediocre, everyday man, he is far from it. His skill in the ring is quite profound. Then, there was Duzza, and member of Playaz Inc. who would surely play a role in the match. Duzza had experience and three guys to back him up. Jamie expected a hectic battler, though he prayed he could sustain the pressure and somehow come away with his first victory in a month. 'Give us our god damn stuff back!' Comes the word's of an Asian man dressed in all blue attire. 'Or what?' Replies Jamie Celt, dressed in a Boston Celtics basketball top and blue, baggy Jeans. His hair was wet due to the heavy rain pouring down from the heavens above. His eyes were narrowed and he looks straight ahead of him. A handgun was clenched tightly in his right hand, his muscles were alert, and ready to bring his right arm to horizontal level is order to fire his weapons at that opponent. In the middle of Boston City stood thirteen men. They were in the one of the most vile places you could be. Boston City had taken and turns for the worst and it was infested with crime and murderous ways. In Lake Isle, on the east coast of Boston City, the Triads and the Celtics stood eye to eye. Tension grew stronger throughout the area as no man dared take their eyes of the man in front of them, rule thirty-six of war, never take your eye of your opponent, for you plans may be similar to his. The eight Triads who had stopped the five Celtic gang members on their way to a party had no intention on stepping aside and letting the Celtics move along. 'Look Celt! You and your guys stole our goddamn ammo, and we want it back! We planned that heist weeks ago and you had no god damn right to interfere.' Said the leader of the Triads, Johnny Kawasaki. Jamie did not flinch; he stood strong with his eyes relentlessly remaining of Kawasaki. 'Just like you didn't have the right to come onto our turf and nearly kill five of our men?' Jamie responds emotionlessly. 'You killed three of our men! That's us even, now give us our god damn stuff.' Johnny replies, getting impatient. Jamie smirks almost devilishly at these comments. 'So, you don't care that three of your men died? That's just like you damn triads, and human sacrifice is no real loss to you is it, tell me, do you Asians feel any emotion at all?' Jamie asks obviously dismayed. Johnny seems to contemplate on this for a second, his entire fellow Triads look at him with a sense of ignorance in their eyes. 'Those men were sacrificed in war, the same as many that have passed. Why do we fight if we are afraid of sacrifice?' He responds, seeming pleased and proud of his words. Jamie does not even need to think about his response; it seems to come out automatically. 'I was their that night, I saw you're men, it was every man for themselves, so where wounded and the rest just drove off letting us have our way with them. You are not friends, you are 'associates, you everything a gang shouldn't be! The Celtics look out and care for each other, we are like a family!' Jamie responds nearly shouting. 'Who are you to judge us?' Johnny replies in a very loud tone. 'I am Jamie Celt, son of Anthony Celt that's who I am, and I judge you because you are wrong!' Jamie shouts. 'We are not wrong, we treat each other with enough respect to let us take care of ourselves!' Jonny screams at the top of his voice. The atmosphere gets soon becomes extremely apprehensive and irritating. 'To treat men the respect you must feel there life is worth living! Yet you left men to die at our hands!' Jamie shouts back. 'You didn't have to kill them!' Johnny bawls. 'Rule fifty-four, kill or be killed!' Jamie screams. 'Rule sixty-one, respect your enemy!' Johnny responds hollering at the top of his voice. Rage and wrath enters both men's eyes. Both their right arms come to a horizontal weapons. Jamie Celt's M-54 handgun and Johnny Kawasaki's U-32 Uzi face each other's faces. All the men, Triads and Celt's around them do not dare move as their leaders hold guns to each others face. Fear is what enters the eyes of the other men. They do not know what to do as the two men do not back down. 'Hey, what's going on down there?' Come the words of a low voiced man in uniform. Two police officers start to run down the street looking at the men holding guns vindictively at each other. The Celtic members look at Jamie. 'Jamie, come on! It's the cops, we have to go man!' Says Peter, a friend of Jamie's. The two men's eyes don't leave each other as they put down their weapons and slowly walk in the opposite direction. Jamie then begins to run away with his friends. A thought then entered his mind. If he hated murder so much, why did he commit it? This was an answer he did not know. And he hoped that one day, some how it could be answered, but adrenaline rushes were what he lived for, and he would never let them go.
