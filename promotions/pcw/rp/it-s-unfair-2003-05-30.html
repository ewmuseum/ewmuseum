It's unfair!

30 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30th May 2003 [ ] – Sivasian speaking directly to the reader, his thoughts. :: :: - Action, and reflection on other stuff from a narrator Krikorian: ‘’ – Someone speaking :: Sivasian stood at the international airport waiting lounge. He was anxious, for it was the first time he would, and had seen Chang in a long time. He stood with a large coffee in his hand. Ah, coffee, the thing which had kept him going through all his career, and yet, he didn’t drink it, he was far too nervous to bother with drinking coffee. Then, he saw them, both SOCIETY and Chang walked through the airport doors, shades on, and looking all of the millions of pounds the Society of Order and Logic had to back them up. Sivasian swiftly went to shake Chang’s hand, and they both walked towards the terminal exit. :: Krikorian: ‘I must admit, my friend, it is nice seeing you for once, for once in a long time.’ :: Chang nodded, he wanted to make Krikorian feel wanted, but indeed, felt nothing as strongly as he did for SOCIETY. In some ways, SOCIETY was family, and Krikorian was really just a business acquisition. :: Chang: ‘And too it is nice to see you. I’ve seen you’ve been spreading the word of the Society of Order and Logic around the world my friend?’ :: Krikorian nodded in agreeing , and felt slightly proud that the leader of his ‘cult’, and the man he looked up to had freely and shown appreciation for his work. However, he didn’t wish to push his luck, and didn’t force it on. They both walked towards his car and slid in. Krikorian had some up building pain and anger which he wanted to unleash. :: Chang: ‘Well done on Tuesday Sivasian, even though you didn’t win a match, the odds were truly stacked against you, and you did defeat two men.’ Sivasian: ‘Indeed I did, it is a shame neither SOCIETY or I won that match, but still on Sunday I have a chance to do some good, to win something for this Society of Order and Logic, and to spread the word.’ Chang: ‘Agreed Sivasian. SOCIETY and myself have been promoting internally, getting money in, and spreading the word throughout the order itself.’ :: Sivasian looked suspicious, from what he had seen and heard, they had merely traveled the world selfishly, and excluded their newest and newest highest member of the Order. :: Chang ‘But anyways, you have done a good job. You’re ideas about way to improve the Society are truly, truly superb Krikorian. :: Sivasian put his foot down, as they drove away. He turned and looked at Chang again. :: Krikorian: ‘I feel I let you down at the pay per view Mr.Chang. Indeed Sir, I feel I let everyone down. I didn’t even reach the match finale for the Rising Star Title. Something which would have made the Society as a whole a lot more well known, and the bonuses for title winnings in PCW could have bolstered the coffers. Chang: ‘You needn’t worry, you have another chance this Friday against that bitch Tessa Windsor, win, and you could be in with a shout at the credited Television Title, more money and fame for the Society my son.’ :: Krikorian nodded. He understood what Chang was saying, but still wanted to get something out. :: Krikorian: ‘You know sir…’ :: Chang looked over, almost egging Krikorian to finish what he was saying.:: Chang: ‘Yes?’ Krikorian: ‘I was screwed at the PPV?’ Chang: ‘Oh? How so my child?’ Krikorian: ‘I had a 4 and 4 record, not a 3 and 4. I should have been placed straight into the final match!’ :: Chang nods for a second, but doesn’t seem to want to take any action. :: Chang: ‘Ok, but you understand, this is because you’re in the Order, they do not want to see someone from a cult succeed, our ideas will be cherished and wanted, they don’t want their viewers seeing the light and buying our tee shirts, rather than the PCW ones, do you understand? ‘ :: Krikorian hangs his head slightly and looks down. :: Krikorian: ‘Of course I do. But it seems unfair.’ Chang: ‘Well my child, you’ll have to come to accept this through your life.’ :: Krikorian thinks for a second, before bursting out. :: Krikorian: ‘If I beat that slut Tessa though, it’ll give me a great chance at the title, something they can’t screw or take away from me, and this unfair and injustice against our Order will be undone!’ :: Chang smirks. :: Chang: ‘I guess you’re right, I guess you’re right.’
