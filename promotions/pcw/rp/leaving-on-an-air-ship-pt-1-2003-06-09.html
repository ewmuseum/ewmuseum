Leaving, On An Air Ship (Pt. 1)

9 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 9, 2003 TAKES PLACE DURING BOARDING OF THE CLOUD NINE... The PCW’s Cloud Nine air ship readies to leave. Excitement builds in the hearts of all the passengers as they wait to take to the majestic decks of this one of a kind air ship. There is one heart, however, that does not beat at an amazing rate out of excitement. Tyler Preston, although included for admission on the air ship, grieves over his lost love. It has only been a month, but now Tyler is starting to feel as though things are permanent. Now that he is taking to the sky for a month or so, he will not be able to make any more attempts to be reunited with his former girlfriend. He really thought that she was the one, but now, things have fallen apart. The scene fades in to the port where the large, majestic, Cloud Nine air ship is sitting. People and luggage scatter the asphalt of the boarding docks. The asphalt is also home to tourists that stare up at the mammoth air ship in wonder. They hold their hands flat over their eyes so that the sun is shielded from their vision. A select few people board the huge door on the side of the ship. In front of the door, on the asphalt, stands John Gargano, who is prancing around the luggage in a circle, with excitement. His black hair bobs around as he circles around a sitting Tyler Preston. Tyler grabs a chunk of his blonde hair to try to keep sane. It seems as though he has come from a comedy gig, because he is decked out in his black dress pants with a white shirt. John is a little more casual with his T-shirt and jeans. “I can’t believe that in only a matter of minutes, we are going to be lounging with the hottest celebrities on this planet,” John says as he allow his black duffel back to fall from his shoulder and land with a thud on the ash gray pavement below. John looks, wide eyed at the Cloud Nine air ship, and he can’t help but to grin. He looks over at Tyler who is starring straight down at the ground, still holding tightly to his luggage. “Yea, I can’t wait,” Tyler forces out. He didn’t want to leave John hanging in his own excitement, but he definitely doesn’t have any excitement of his own to offer at this time. “Not to mention, a few PCW wrestlers. I’m sure we’ll all get along. I mean, it’s an airship for goodness sakes. If that doesn’t bring people together, then I don’t know what will,” John continues, ignoring Tyler’s lack of excitement. He’s hoping that his own will be contagious and soon Tyler will be ready to go. Tyler continues to sulk as John rants on. “Yea, the celebrities will get along with the wrestlers I’m sure. The air ship is a happy place,” Tyler mumbles, attempting to inject something of substance into the conversation, but much to his dismay, it doesn’t make any sense at all. John looks confused and bumps Tyler on the shoulder with his clenched fist. “Tyler, what the hell are you saying? You’re just sitting there letting your tongue to bounce around,” John says as a joke, but doesn’t score so much as a smile out of his good friend. Tyler rests his chin on his clenched knuckles and tries to ignore John who hovers him. “Get off my back man; I was just answering your question,” Tyler says as he pushes John off of him. John just smiles, knowing that the only reason he is being treated this way is because of Tyler’s present love situation. “I’m not really one to pry, well, actually yes I am, but I know your problem. You are still bummed out because Kalli hasn’t taken you back, and now you’re going to be thousands of feet from the ground for a good month,” John says in a tone that almost suggests that he is happy at Tyler’s misfortune. Tyler sighs, not caring what John actually thinks, but just nods his head in agreement. “I wasn’t even given time to try to get her back. She breaks things up with me, then bam, I’m going on the air ship. No wonder she hates me, it’s true, I am never there for anyone but myself,” Tyler admits. It is now where Tyler realizes that Kalli did have a good reason to give him up. “Come on Tyler, you have to buck up. You can’t board the happiest ship on earth looking like you lost your favorite puppy,” John attempts to buck him up. His words fall upon deaf ears as Tyler sits as still as a statue. “You can say that, because you aren’t leaving anything behind. You blow where the wind takes you,” Tyler convicts, pointing a stubby finger right at John’s face. John shrugs with a smile, proud of these recent allegations. “What are you talking about? I’m leaving my whole career on hold just to go on this air ship. You don’t see me moping around about it,” John says with a smile on his face. He knows losing a girl friend is probably a lot worse than taking a few months off of work. “Wow, what a sacrifice. You’re going on vacation,” Tyler says with sarcasm in his voice. John just laughs, still keeping his happy-go-lucky mood intact. He is growing a little restless however. He wants to begin his adventures as soon as he can. “What time are we boarding. I thought they said two o’clock and it’s almost three. I bet they’re holding everyone up because Justin Timberlake has to get on the air ship. Everyone, move back for the pop star,” John says in a goofy voice. These are more simple attempts to cheer Tyler up. John does harbor spite for the main stream celebrities that he attempts to model his career after. “Kalli loves Justine Timberlake. We were singing one of his songs together for karaoke at about this time, two months ago,” Tyler says in a pathetic voice. John rolls his eyes and sighs. He thinks that Tyler sounds pathetic, and quite frankly, he does. “Oh my, this is getting a little out of control,” chuckles John, who just stares at the sulking Tyler Preston and shakes his head. “Can we please have all PCW Wrestling Community Members over here? The celebrities are on board and it is not your turn to board,” a small man with a clipboard yells out in a high tone of voice. He isn’t the biggest of men, but it is obvious that he is the mastermind of how all the passengers are getting on the ship. “Yes, that’s us finally. Of course we had to wait for the celebrities,” John yelps as he quickly gathers his things. Tyler moves at a slightly slower pace. He slowly gets up from his seated position on his cream suitcase and straightens out his shirt. By time he looks to the side of him, John has sprinted to the door way to be the first PCW wrestler to get on board. Tyler shakes his head with disapproval and straps the black duffel bag on to his shoulder. He slowly starts to strut to the doorway. This was it. This is the time that he was dreading. He would now have to board the luxurious air ship, and fly thousands of feet above Kalli Stearns, the one that he loved. He sighs once more and continues his trek. His duffel bag slips off of his shoulder. He cusses softly under his breathe and goes to pick it up. Just as he picks the duffel bag up, he hears something that intrigues him. “Tyler, please wait,” says a soft, female voice from behind Tyler. It raises above the rest of the business noise that fills the air as the air ship is being packed and getting ready for sail. Tyler turns around, with his eyebrows arching up over his eyes in anticipation of who the source of this voice may be. His hopes are fulfilled when he lays his eyes on Kalli, who is running to him at a frantic pace. She wears a white sundress with huge hoop earrings that jiggle up and down when she runs. Her dark brown hair is crimped and looks like several springs protruding from her head. Tyler drops all the things that he is holding to welcome in a much-wanted hug from Kalli. A tear wells up in his eye, but he fights it, judging by the company that he is keeping at this time. She showed up for him. Is this one last goodbye or will there be future days in store for the couple? The unanswered questions stay that way for a moment as Tyler holds Kalli close.
