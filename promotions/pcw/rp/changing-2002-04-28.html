Changing?

28 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/28/02 The scene opens up inside of a room. Its almost pitch black except for a tiny stream of light that fights its way through the silver colored curtain that is trying to keep it out. From that little illumination all we can make out is a broad desk, with a chair positioned right behind it, as well as bookshelves and such off to the sides. The turning of a knob can be heard as a figure briskly walks into the room instantly flipping the light switch up to bring some much needed light to the room. The camera turns to the man who turned it on, and there is Kramer, in a blue buttoned down shirt and black suit pants. After making a quick pause to stretch a little he walks over to the desk and sits down in the huge black chair and kicks his feet up. He pulls up his right hand and we see a KFC bucket grasped in his fingers. He peers into the bucket several times before reaching in and yanking out a Wild Wing. He stares at it looking it over before taking a big bite out of it. Some of the barbaque from the Wild Wings stain his lips, but he realizes this and takes it away by licking his lips. Kramer: Man these are awesome!! That bold and savory taste gets me every fricken time!! After he picks every last bit of meat from the bone, he tosses the remains into the bucket and wipes his hands on a white hankerchief that he pulls from his pocket. Before he puts it away he dabs at his mouth to get any leftover residue that he missed before. He pulls his feet from the top of the desk and sits up straight as he starts rummaging through the mass of papers all over his desk. This seems quite unusual because Kramer normally isn't the businessy type as he is portraying himself right now. Instead of finding him at a desk, you would be more likely to find him in a video game chair playing the newest game with the coolest graphics. To raise even more suspicion he reaches into his drawer on his right side, and pulls out a pair of black rimmed glasses which he slides on. Kramer: A piece of fan mail? I haven't seen one of these for a while. I wonder what awesome things they wrote about me? Feeling very excited he rips open the white envelope from a Timmy Charters which he found jumbled into his mass of papers. He pulls the lined paper up and unravels it as he begins to read aloud. Kramer: Dear Kramer, I have been a fan of yours for a very long time. You were never my favorite wrestler, but I always thought that you were cool. ((Kramer pauses.)) Not your favorite? What's wrong with you kid? ((Continues to read.)) Over the past few weeks I have noticed that you have been paying of members off the Ultimate Authority in return for protection. ((Takes another pause.)) Lets give a hand of applause for Mr. Obvious here. ((Continues.)) I was wondering if you are involved with the Ultimate Authority? If so why? They are the bad guys in PCW for crying out loud, and that would make you a total sell out! The only things that those sissies know how to do is screw people over, and piss people off. Kramer stops reading right there and quickly crumples the paper into a tiny ball which he tosses into the small metal trashcan located on the left side of his desk. Kramer: Is this all people care about? All I get now is 'Oh Kramer you're not with UA are you?' Either I am or I'm not, but I'm not going to respond to it. And what’s up with all of that sell out crap? My decisions are my decisions, and I can do whatever I want! If I were to be with Ultimate Authority I wouldn't be a sell out. I mean how could I be? They are still part of PCW no matter how you slice it right? He places his head onto his arm showing a sign of weariness. His head props right back up though as the door opens. In walks his trusty manager Jason Alexander who is wearing a nice gray Armani suit. Jason looks around for something as he steps in closer towards Kramer's desk. Jason: Why do you only have one chair in here? Kramer: There's only a need for one, because I am the only one who should be in here. This is my new sanctuary to get away from things in case you forgot. ((Did he just say sanctuary??)) Besides why don't you ever knock? When I forget to you always harp on me. Jason: Sorry....I just had something very important to tell you. Kramer: I'm listening.... Jason rushes out of the room real quick and comes back in with a folding chair that he unfolds and sits down in. Jason: Actually there is two things. One is that you start filming for the new KFC commercial on Wednesday. The other thing is that you have a match on Turmoil. Kramer: I already knew that. Jason: Oh....how? Kramer: Well after realizing how often you forget to tell me things I got in contact with the front office and I was informed. And from what I am to understand, this is my last match for the week thus far. Jason: Yes that’s pretty true. I have been trying to get in contact with the committee in charge with the big pay per view coming up, but it doesn't seem like you will be wrestling. Kramer: That's a slight bummer, but oh well. I can still be involved with the PPV one way or another anyway. Jason, having finished releasing his news, stands up to leave. Kramer: Hold on a second, I want to talk to you about something before you go. Jason sits back down on the cold metal chair staring attentively at Kramer. Kramer: I have been looking over some merchandise reports, and from what I have gathered, I have made a good five thousand dollar profit in the past month. Yet I only received two grand of it. At first I figured that I just wasn't doing well, so I decided to look into it. And this is what I found. So I want to knowif you have any idea on where my money is magically disappearing off to? The camera pans over to Jason who looks a bit shocked as well as guilty as he swipes a few strands of sweat from his brow before responding. Jason: I....I was the one who took the other three thousand dollars, but it was only used for an investment that I was almost a hundred percent sure was going to make you a richer man. Kramer: Let me guess, the investment fell through and I am in the whole a good three thousand dollars? Jason slowly shakes his head in compliance waiting for Kramer to throw something at him in a fit of rage. Kramer: You know if you would have asked I would have happily given it to you. You didn't have to steal it. Now I know you were only trying to help me out in the long run but please promise never to do that again without permission from me first. Jason: Oh I promise I won't do it anymore. Kramer: Besides you are going to have a wad of your own money to blow once this commercial goes on air. Jason: That’s a good point. Well I have to go.....I have other things to do. By the way have you heard anything from Grant or LeBlanc about your match on Tuesday. Kramer: Unfortunately I haven't, but I doubt those guys will let me down. Besides there is no doubt in my mind that we won't win. Jason exits the room, with the steel chair in hand, and closes the door leaving Kramer to himself once again. Kramer continues to look through more papers on his desk as the scene slowly fades to black.
