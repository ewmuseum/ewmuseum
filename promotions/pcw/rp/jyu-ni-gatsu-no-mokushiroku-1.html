Jyu Ni Gatsu no Mokushiroku {1)

Date: December/March [Thursday, December 6, 2002] She was dead… We don’t care about the reasons she had…all we cared about was punishment. Our own daughter…blindsiding us from behind with a sledgehammer. We saw her reflection in the tinted window of that limo Kannon jumped in right before we were going to flip it over. We saw her face…we saw the cold intent she had in her eyes when she swung the huge hammer into the back of our skull. After that things got fuzzy. After that we woke up in the darkness…we were in the basement of the castle, shackled, muzzled, and left alone in the Hall of Jahana. The Hall was the biggest room in the basement, and named for the previous owner, the great Lord Tatewaki Jahana of the ancient times, the most lavish political rogue exiled Sado Island. Funny how they put the biggest threat to their country in the best accommodation for in-country banishment. We knew we were in the Hall; our arms were chained down to the arms of the iron throne, legs against legs, and chains around our throat and abdomen keeping us against the near ton throne. Cyclonica made sure that we weren’t breaking free and coming for her in the dead of night for some parental discipline. We could bide our time…daddy’s little girl was getting grounded on sight…six feet deep. Then there’d be a CEO that would feel the wrath of the god in a bloody destruction. Lots of things made you think while you were exiled away from humanity…exiled from the wretched simpletons that plagued our life day in and day out. We had to admit…it was actually soothing for once not to deal with those idiots. No Diablo b-tching about not being the world champ…no Syko and his stupid immature antics…no Darkstar and his annoying laziness and bizarre ideals no one really gives a sh-t about…no Kannon ranting on about how f-cking great of a mastermind he thinks he is. Best of all…no Cyclonica knocking the audience into seizures with her voice. Nothing but silence…silence and the voices within us. They say the wrong things…but one voice stands out and says punishment is inevitable. Something within us says we should forgive Xavier Kannon for what he did to us at Alliances. We know what kind of man Kannon is…he’s a man that cannot even the odds on talent alone, so he has to use trickery to be anything more than a high-paid chicken. Deceit is his muscle…but the scrawny bastard’s gonna pay for crossing us…screwing us from something he didn’t deserve to win, CEO or not. That voice said to just let go of his pettiness and move on to better deals… Something within us says we should forgive Cyclonica for her sins. Our daughter…betraying her father, the one who gave her life for her own reason. Just forgive her and return to the happy family life that awaits us. Do that, and it’ll be “Daddy” and his “little girl” just like it used to be. No offense to us, but that voice is a dumbass. A more reasonable voice rose out over that one, and it sounded pissed. Everyone should pay, it said…the names don’t matter. They’re just labels to corpses. Their status doesn’t matter…it’ll fade like the scent of rose from a dead bulb. If you had a pair of spheres in your tights that made you think you could approach us with anything, from a challenge to a conversation…you’d be coughing up blood and spitting out crimson coated molars. Do not f-ck with us…because the god will make sure to test your mortality and remind you just how weak you are. We were beginning to like this guy. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- A long day’s ride had passed, and finally the older couple was at home. Yoshihiko and Yukina Akiyama climbed out from their Nissan and walked towards their front door. They had just come from visiting their son…the nVg on Sado Island. For once in their lives, they were proud of their child not because of his abilities alone…but because of his character. The two slipped off their shoes in the genkan and Yoshi reached for the light. There’s something about flipping that light switch…you do it in expecting illumination, but when darkness beckons to your summon, it brings dread into your heart. Yuki shot a sharp look at her husband, her brow wrinkled. But a faint sound from deep within the house stole both of their attentions like Kannon stole wins…quick, surprising, and of the utmost displeasure. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- We guess PCW can rest easily now that we’re stuck here in our own basement. We can only imagine the crap that Kannon’s pulling around now that he’s crowded the joint up with those RUSH rejects. On return we’ll have to clear out the ranks so that we can have a little elbowroom to do our work. But right about now, we didn’t care in the least. The federation didn’t matter to us. We wanted the owner. We wanted the owner in the hospital, to say the least…mummified tighter than Pharaoh’s Jew-enslaving ancestors and snacking on the best IV his body will accept after we crush his frail frame. Yeah, we wanted this man broken…he deserved no less from the god of PCW. But then again…we had a bad feeling about events if we were to ever break free from this place. Nothing dealing with those peons in PC-dub…but more our personal life. We felt that if we ever saw daylight again, it would never be something that we could enjoy during our stay on this wretched coil. We lowered our head, our chin resting against the cold touch of the choking chain as we thought on about our fate… -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Every room looked like beasts have been through it. Walls scratched on, sheets ripped, tables broken, chairs smashed, glass shattered along the floor…one would think vandals actually adopted a sense of work ethic and updated their activities. Now Mr. and Mrs. Akiyama stood in front of the last room they have yet to check…the basement…the training hall for the Akiyama household. Yuki had a staff gripped tightly in her hands as her husband held onto a pair of clubs, looking more like a broken bo than a pair of clubs. Yoshi opened the door and walked slowly down the stairs, taking the lead through the darkness with his wife behind him. They both reached the bottom, and finally they were able to see with the aid of light… At the end of the training hall…a figure wearing all black with a black facemask and bandana knelt in waiting, his eyes closed as he looked down. By his side two lights hung from the ceiling, and before him a black bowl sat, burning incense. Yuki and Yoshi approached, glaring hard at the ninja-like man as the incense wisp floated above his face. Then the eyes opened…and an icy blast shot out from his pupils… -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- End’s been absent since our departure from PCW. What, now that the Celestials team is done, he thinks he doesn’t have time to pay a visit to his dear old friend, the Neo Void God? We’ve been there for him no matter what, and this is how he repays us. That’s just fine… we’re sure he’s doing what he can to represent the heritage that we possess. He’ll do fine…if not, we’ll show him the path as we’ve always done. Yeah, there’s no remorse for having to strike down a close being. Think back to that incident in the ring a while ago…Darkstar had Cyclonica backed into a corner, and there was no other alternative but the one given. Cyclonica knew it…we knew it…but Darkstar didn’t expect it. Celestial Spear…right through the both of them… If we had knew this was going to happen to us, we would have spiked her harder…taken her out of commission way before things came to this. If she has some stupid dumbass notion that this is the only alternative for being backed into a tough situation, then she’s about to get spiked again by another Celestial Spear. This time into a stone wall. Yeah, we knew retribution was our motive of violence for now…but Cyclonica wasn’t the focus of things. Thinking this much about destroying one person made them seem like they were of high importance. That’s why we have never placed complete focus on one being alone, lest they think they’re a huge f-ckin threat and get full of themselves. When the real truth is they’re full of nothing but hot air and sh-t. Kannon was on the list, yeah. He was getting a foot in his ass harder than the Ream Team on foot fetish night, except there’d be no lube on this boot when it made contact. Then there was RUSH…the whole lot of them bastards. They stuck their noses in our business, and now I’d shove their teeth down their throats. We’d have an asskicking labeled for them by name personally…if we could bear to remember any of their worthless little Indy names. Flat-scans never could make enough of a name for themselves to get remembered 10 minutes past the match. Front Office…Darkstar…Clyde…El Diablo… Come to think of it….every-f-cking-body was due for a thrashing when we got ourselves out of this sh-t. …some more extreme than others… -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The staff twirled in the air from being kicked out of Yuki’s hands. The ninja leaped off the walls and snatched it out of the air, landing in a crouched position ready to strike with it. Yoshihiko slid in front of his wife, who held her wrists from the previous kicks to them. The ninja rolled forward, spinning and thrusting with the staff for the father of Akiyama. Yoshi avoided a large swing and came back on the offensive, swinging his two clubs back and forth at the dark figure. It was like a dance, the two of them going back and forth, each end of the staff in the ninja’s hands deflected a strike from Yoshi. Then the figure planted the staff in the floor and lunged forward, the staff between his legs as he kicked Yoshi back with both feet. Then the ninja slid down the staff and spun around it, nailing a swinging dropkick straight into Yoshi’s face. Yoshi stumbled back, but Yuki came back to keep the pressure on the assailant. The ninja landed with the staff in his hand, his back turned towards the oncoming Yuki. With one motion, the staff came swinging upwards, connecting with Yuki’s jaw. She flew back and hit the ground hard, dazed completely. Yoshihiko watched in shock as the ninja spun the bo over his shoulder and held it across his shoulders. This was a cocky ninja to strikes poses on the Akiyama family. Yoshi ran in next, a roar in his lips to strike out revenge for the attack on his wife. The clubs were out of his hand, weapons nothing but limiting agents in the methods of battle. But the next attack…he didn’t see coming at all. It was totally Kamikaze…the ninja slid the staff from his shoulders and snapped it up in between both of their legs, nailing them both in the crotch. Yoshi stood frozen in pain, a gasping look on his face. The ninja surprisingly stepped over the staff and quickly snapped it straight into the forehead of Mr. Akiyama. He was down…next to his wife…dazed and defeated. The ninja stood there, glaring down at the couple with cold eyes. Then he took the staff in both hands and snapped it in half with his head, leaving two halves in hand. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Heh…complete destruction has always been our goal. It’s destiny for the Neo Void God to destroy all things and bring about universal harmony. The ultimate banishment…brining about a single collective of darkness…final fulfillment… How delightful… It would finally bring peace within our mind, that’s for sure. Was it selfish of us to destroy everything just so that we can have peace of mind? Not really…it’s not like most of these people didn’t ask to be destroyed anyway. It’s subconscious…the actions they do, the words they say, the expressions on their faces…it all begs for death. We’re the one that grants that wish…they pray to be the prey…and we can do nothing but hunt them down and do as they command. Strangers…they who’ve done wrong against our divine will…friends…even family. They all will be dead as they ultimately wish. Family… That’s something that’s just become a factor in our lives…stronger than it used to be. We are a father…two daughters, Anarchy and Cyclonica, even though one of them is an ingrate. A cousin, Ayumi Hamasaki, who’s been like a sister to us in most. Parents…Yoshihiko and Yukina Akiyama…the reason for why we’re here. The End, who’s played dual roles as a brother, a son, and a friend… But it is not our will anymore…to do the things we have to do… If there cannot be complete control…there must be complete chaos. If everyone will not submit…then everyone will have to die… Peace will be achieved through their obedience…or through their destruction. Sanity…through insanity… Construction…through destruction… Life…through…death… Everyone in PCW is dead…carve the tombstones now, have them ready for when we return… -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- A leg was stretched over the throats of both of Akiyama’s parents. The ninja sat poised in a split, cutting off their air as he twirled the broken staffs in his hands. Most of this seemed like torture, as if he were enjoying every second of it. Just then, Yukina glared up, struggling for air as she reached up and grabbed the hood of the ninja. When she snatched it off…a surprised look now hooded Mother Akiyama’s face. Half of the staff smacked her in her face, knocking her back out. Then he grabbed his hood and covered his face back up. Standing back up, he stood over both bodies with the staff halves over his shoulders. He did a handstand…and the speared ends of the broken staff speared into Yoshihiko and Yukina Akiyama’s hearts. The ninja flipped off, his back on the two staked martial artists as he walked towards the exiting stairs. The candle lit at the end of the dojo…it slowly flickered…and died… -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Hmmm…we know the sound of those heels… Click, click, click, click… It was silver heels…stiletto…. Our precious daughter was coming for a visit. The iron door creaked open, light flooding in from the lit hallway into the darkness of the hall. Then lights flooded the Hall of Jahana, brining our eyes to shy away from it. A growl escaped as we closed our eyes, trying to make the darkness linger just a little more before visibility was clear. Then as we looked back up, we saw her… She was dressed always in her vinyl pants, heels, and ice blue halter-top. Her hair, which was usually neat, was messy, and scattered over her face. She stood on the steps of the throne, approaching slowly. That was smart of her, she knew we were going to make her pay for her actions. Our little girl was always smart… Lock after lock opened…chain after chain fell loose from our body. Then finally she unshackled the chain from around our neck. Then our hand clamped around her neck. We grew to full height, brining her into the air with one arm and holding her at full extension. Our roar echoed through the hall like a thousand beasts. What a wonderful way to begin a killing spree…our own daughter…the one who started the rampage of death upon the world…how glorious… Something was wrong here… There was no effort of resistance from her…no cry or plea of forgiveness…no sound of pain from the vice around her throat…no strikes against us to break the grasp. She was like a rag doll, limp in our hand, accepting the fate dealt to her. Normally this would be no problem, as it makes our task easier…but we expected more from our seed. Then we saw her face. Her cheeks were moist; streaks of fluid stained her face. The cold look of superiority that we had grown accustomed to…was replaced by one of sadness. We sat her down…sympathy taking over for the one who betrayed us. A sniff came from her body as she brushed past her hair and looked up at us, her ice blue eyes wide and glassy. “Your parents are dead.”
