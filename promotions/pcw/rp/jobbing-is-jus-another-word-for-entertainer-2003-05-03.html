Jobbing is jus' another word for entertainer

3 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-3-03 ~ The scene opens up to Hank back in his Seattle, Washington apartment with Jenn there by his side. Both sitting on Hank’s black leather sofa watching some boring news station, Jenn has her head lying on Hank’s shoulder, and her feet on the coffee table in front of their couch. Hank is just slouched on the couch with his feet on the floor. Hank: “So what’s on the agenda for this week?” Jenn: “You have a tag match with some guy who calls himself Manuel Labor.” Hank: “Hm, so they’re jus’ simply using me as a guy to break in the new talent, or team up wit’ the new talent eh?” (Jenn shrugs) “Okay cool I guess I could give some rookie a break and be his tag team partner for a night.” Jenn: “You haven’t been doing too well at winning in the PCW babe.” Hank: “It’s not all bout winning, it’s about entertainment. Besides who cares bout winning when I get paid the same amount either way?” Jenn: (smiles) “That’s true, but you used to be all about the win, all about entertainment. You used not to care if you got paid at all.” Hank: “Meh, shit changes and shit happens. However, I still put entertainment of the fans at the top of my list. Money second, winning distant third. Hell I don’t even care if I get a heavyweight title shot and lose. I’m so used to it; it doesn’t even both me anymore.” Jenn: “So about Manuel Labor as your partner?” Hank: “What about him? He’s my partner and he will learn how to be an icon in the PCW, like I have become.” Jenn: “The only reason you’d be an icon is because you beat Doug Troy and won the extreme championship.” Hank: “Yeah and those two events have stuck in my mind. They made me the household PCW name I am today Jenn. It was my two biggest wins in PCW history.” Jenn: “Your opponents are The Significant Others, Flaymin Aymin and AJ Trinidad, Dante Jackson and Nerrezza.” Hank: “Flaymin Aymin, what a familiar sounding name. He’s had his fair share of PCW victories and probably a little less of a household name than I am, but still he will lose and will not be the more entertaining superstar! I am the most entertaining superstar! As for the rest, well they can all kiss my ass.” Jenn: “The Significant Others is a female tag team.” Hank: “So? I’ll beat their ass the same way I beat any opponent in the ring? Gender doesn’t matter a damn when it comes to entertainment of fans. You can be transsexual for all I care, I’ll still lay you down wit’ the ‘Off-Beat’ and out entertain you! Anyway I need to get to the arena before Tuesday, so what day should we leave?” Jenn: “Tomorrow?” Hank: “Meh, works for me. That gives me a good two whole days to workout in the gym and look jus’ too damn good for my third match, probably my third loss as well.” (laughs) “Either way I’m here for my fans and if PCW went down the gutter and lost all of it’s millions and billions of fans I’d go like the damn wind and find somewhere else to perform for my millions and billions of fans. Hank hath spoken!.” ~ The scene cuts off to black. ~ OOC: Sorry my promos have been so short and crappy.
