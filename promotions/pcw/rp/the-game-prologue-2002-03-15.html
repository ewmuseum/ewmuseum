Now that's how ya do it!) * * * --3-15-02, Friday morning, 10:43 am, day of the big GAME!-- Ladies and gentlemen. Boys and girls. Peasants of all ages! Welcome to the B.S. Invitational Primetime Central Street Basketball GAME at 'K-Dub So Wonderful Center'. Welcome one and all to the city of Orlando in the state of Florida. Yes this may be only third to New York and L.A. when it comes to the TV and movie industries. Yes... Orlando may just rank second in the country to Las Vegas when it comes to being a hot tourist spot to go to. But does any city have both in the same amount as O-Town? I don't think so. Not to mention everything else that the real City of Angels has to offer! Jimmy Spinks, internationally known as the phenom himself, Kid Wonder. Disney World. The Orlando Magic, Tracy McGrady in particular. Universal Studios. Two time. TWO TIME! Two time Primetime Championship Wrestling's World Heavyweight Champion, Chris Sloboda. Downtown curfews that are made to be ignored. The fact that it isn't half as dirty and humiliating as Tampa or Miami. Orlando Predators (Arena Football). Matchbox 20. Backstreet Boys. N'Sync. Hate the music or not... they make lots of cash, which must be admired. Just like O-Town, the band. Carrot Top. Jerry Flynn. Wesley Snipes. The greatest talk radio station on the east coast or the west siiide, Real Radio 104.1. As far away from Canada as possible while you still have your freedoms in the superior U.S.-of-A. Fourty-five minutes from Kennedy Space Center while everyone else in North America has to watch shuttle launches and landings from their filthy couches. Not Texas. No Earthquakes. No mudslides. Only a few months of forest fires each summer! Bobby 'The Showstopper' Stormbringer. A revolutionary force in the world of Professional Wrestling. Only because he did not choose his second love in this life. Basketball. Before starting the long and arguous (that means stressful n' tough, kiddos. Stay in school!) road to superstardom in Pro Wrestling, B.S. was a star in High School basketball on a national level. In fact Mr. Prime Time just barely missed the cut of making the high school McDonald's All-American all-star game. Making the NBA was far from definite but B.S. could of chosen a path towards being very successful in college basketball for either UNLV, UConn, or one of many Florida universities. But instead B.S. has chosen to relive his brief former glory in a mixture of both the possible life and the one he chose. To also remember fondly when his knees were new, young, and healthy. When he had as much speed as any non-Olympic track star., just about. His team will represent their grand city of Orlando. The fastest rising 'future legend' in PCW history, Bobby Stormbringer. PTC Free Agent Kid Wonder. PCW World Champion Boda. They will face off against world class athlete Juan Borro, the returning Badge, and NTICW snake in the grass Mark Makonie. Ironically enough DA COP~! and B.S. will square off on PCW Turmoil this upcoming Tuesday because the fromer leader of Thugs 4 Hire screwed B.S. out of what would of been yet another 'biggest win ever', this time against Clyde. Both Bobby Stormbringer and K-Dub have declared that any physical fighting off or on the court that has nothing to do with the fine and honorable sport of StreetBall will be ejected from the building. Probably in a cannon Alan Kriegman style as well. Stormbringer is the host of the event while it is K-Dub's arena after all. Which means they are promoting and encouraging verbal thrashings and beatdowns, you see. The 'K-Dub So Wonderful Center' security force, namely PCW Celestial Star Champ Jay Sylo, Young Tiger, and their many faceless cronies have been instructed that if any former member of Asylum decides to show their face? That they are to be brought onto the court for a public mob beatdown. The warning on the wall outside the enclosed gym states it plainly: SIGN: Jeremy Howard. Darkstar. Alex Miyagawa. Xavier 'three minutes and eight seconds' Kannon. Even that f*ckin' mime Gaijin! We have the horses ready for the drawing and quartering out back if you would like to join us. That goes for you too, Dwayne Johnson. Inside Kid Wonder and Bobby argue about the first round of the NCAA Men's Basketball Tournament. Namely Wonder's piss poor advice to The Showstopper about Gonzaga going to the Final Four. Badge continues to tell his teammates horribly insensitive jokes about Mo and his alleged virginity, nonstop. Extreme Champ Jason Core has fellow referee Tim Walden fitted for glasses. Civil Disobedience plays hide and go seek around the court, which makes for pretty quick games. Also makes for lots of running around on the court while the players try to practice. Munkey and Boolie share their 'best' ideas for new sets of cheers. The part owner of the Orlando Magic in the form of Boda tolerates Coach Green's unnecessary advice. William S. Powell hides out of sight from Bobby Stormbringer for the moment. Robbie Decker and Stormbringer's fave commentator, Asa Fountain, both sit through their sound checks for the PA system. The Rush experiments with newish DJing and scratching techniques while Duzza wanders around. Zero is one of the very first audience members to arrive and he prepares his cardboard and shredded paper for his spitballs early. Amanda and Kira York tolerate eachother's existence. B.S.'s personal assistant Carl Everstrong gives young Chris Matthews of CHZ some professional advice on the subject of 'The Art Of Camerawork' while Sylo shows Eddy Ramirez, the Young Tiger, how to properly handle a TAZER~! A tazer, YEAH~! B.S., 'The End' Skylar Green, Young Tiger, and Duzza all end up gathering in the middle of the court a few minutes later for a quick Eternal Wrestling reunion that mostly entails of making fun of those sorry sumb!tches Jax and Shane Wethers. Things begin to settle down as more PTCers and more members of the general public file in to this event for charity and take their seats over the next half hour. Ten bucks for the general public, five to those that don't participate in the entertainment. So if a wrestler buys a ticket and then participates? They get a refund. What great motivation! We can fade to black safely for now. Because The Phantom Booker himself repells from the ceiling and his connected cables into his pre-designated spot on top of the rafters. TPB continues to do his best Sting impression, panty hose-on-da-head and all. Now all is right with the world. Until Syko the Goblin decides to show up. Chris Matthews suddenly cups his hands around his mouth and screams for no apparent reason: MATTHEWS: And baby.. that's no b.s.!! B.S. responds by cupping his hands together as well while he chats with Duzza about who the finest half-white / half-black people in the world really are. STORMBRINGER: Heh. Thanks, man! No really- bah~! You know the rest! The final whistles from the flute in 'Sure Shot' play and then fade off like everything else does for now. If Bobby Stormbringer can defeat Ben J. Pailthorpe on Saturday afternoon, then you'll see this strange tale play out in the bracket finals of the Global Title Tourney. If B.S. can't quite defeat the former World Champion then you'll just see this during PCW hours of the PTC Promo Channel like normal. Stay tuned and don't be bogus to each other. TO BE CONTINUED.
