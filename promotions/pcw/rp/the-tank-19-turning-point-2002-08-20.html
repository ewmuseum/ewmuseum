The Tank 19 - Turning Point

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 20 ﻿*********The Tank __19—********* Turning Point Is it ever too late to see that the road you travel ismade of gravel? ~Leigh Donovan Friday, August 16 - Day 42 Leigh Donovan hasn’t been seen in the main room of the Tank since he leapt from the cellbalcony and nearly broke his entire body. “Welcome home,” snickers his escorting C.O as heremoves the handcuffs. “Nice to be back,” Leigh says in his thick Australian accent as he looks up at the C.O with asmile. “Have a nice fucking day, mate.” “Get inside,” the C.O says with a forceful shove. “Fucking prag.” When Leigh enters the main floor of Unit B, it takes some time for the other prisoner’s torecognize him - his appearance has drastically changed. While in the hole, after the sedationtreatment stopped, Leigh one day began cracking his head against the wall, splitting it wide open- his head was completely shaved in order to get the stitches in. Now, a week later, some brownstubble has shown up on the top of his head. His wrist is still in a cast since the fracture from the fall was quite bad. His ribs, however, seemto be nearly as good as new. The best part, to Leigh, is the fact that the craving for heroin hasfinally begun to subside. “Hey!” Darryl Jarvis, a low ranking member of the Thugs and Jason Snow’s fearful cellmatechuckles. “Look who it is! Jesus, I thought you’d be broken into a million pieces by now.” “Fuck off,” snaps Leigh while passing the slinky young black man. Darryl Jarvis doesn’t like taking insults - he gets enough of them from the higher-ups of theThugs. When insulted by someone he feels he is physically superior to, he doesn’t react well. “What the fuck you say to me, prag?” Jarvis fires back, standing up from his seat at thecheckerboard. Leigh stops in his tracks and turns to Darryl Jarvis. “I said,” Leigh begins, “Fuck... ... Off.” Leigh doesn’t move an inch. He simply glares a hole through Darryl Jarvis. “You looking for an ass-kicking, boy?” Jarvis says, looking down on Leigh Donovan, the smallerAustralian rockstar. “I’ll have you sucking my dick by nightfall if you keep that attitude up,prag.” “That so?” Leigh smirks, never taking his eyes from Darryl Jarvis’ cruel face. Suddenly, LeighDonovan snaps. In one quick motion, he drops down to his knees and nails Darryl Jarvis in thegroin with a headbutt - a sickening hollow crunch is heard through the Unit, prompting everyprisoner who’s been watching to groan. “Jesus!” Darryl Jarvis manages to squeak as he drops to his back and clutches his aching groin. Breathing is quite troublesome at this moment, but with Leigh Donovan climbing on top of him,he realizes that his troubles are just beginning. As if trying to keep a beat, Leigh raises his armand begins cracking Darryl Jarvis in the face with his cast. “You sick fuck!” screams a C.O as he buries his billy club into Leigh’s still-recovering ribs. Within seconds, two more guards are on the scene and pulling Leigh away from his bloodied andunconscious victim. “Take him back to the hole!” orders one of the C.O’s as he looks over Darryl Jarvis’ condition -his nose is obviously broken, and there are a few teeth lying around on the floor beside him. Both of his eyes are already beginning to swell. “And Jesus Christ, someone get a fuckingstretcher in here, now!” At the top of the cell balcony, watching like a patient hunter, J-Thug looks down on the scene. Itseems his cellmate will be spending a few more nights in the hole. *** From the Office of the Warden “People like Leigh Donovan spend life thinking that in the end, your physical ability doesn’tmatter,” the Warden begins. “However, when a person like Leigh Donovan comes to the Tank,he soon realizes that what he used to think was the biggest crock of bull shit ever passed off asthe truth.” “You don’t have to look far to see that violence, indeed, is the answer. Look at the president. He spends his days at schools teaching children not to fight and to use words over fists. Then atnight, he has a man in uniform tell him how the nukes are coming along.” *** Saturday, August 17 - Day 43 “What’s he doing?” Harvey Blake asks his young companion, Jake Hooper as they take a seat atthe Aryan table in the lunch room. “I think...” Jake hesitates as he squints. “I think he’s giving us the finger.” “Christ,” Harvey sighs. Two tables down, Jason Snow sits at the right hand of Doug Hattoni, smiling while he holds hismiddle finger up for the Nazis to see. It feels good to Snow to not side with scum like HarveyBlake and the Nazis. Doug Hattoni will never know the option was even considered. “I guess that’s our answer,” says Jake Hooper, sitting back from his food. “I lost my fuckingappetite.” “So Snow’s going to stick by that fat fucker’s side,” sighs Harvey Blake. “What are we going to do about it?” Jake asks. “Don’t do anything stupid,” Harvey says, adding an element of seriousness by turning to Jakeand looking him directly in the eyes. “If you set up a hit on him, we’re going to lose a couple ofgood men. That’s not an option. Not yet, anyway.” “Then what’s the plan?” Jake asks looking back across at Snow. “I’m going to talk to him,” Harvey says with a nod. “I’m going to let him know that we don’trespond well to little pricks that string us along.” *** From the Office of the Warden “If we could all write the movie of our lives, none of us would be a bad guy,” says the Warden. “So, before judging the people in the Tank, maybe we should each take a look at ourselves. Sure, these are bad men. But in their own movie, their the hero, just like us. What if each of ourmovies are as fucked up as theirs?” *** Sunday, August 18 - Day 44 Tim McDonald, Doug Hattoni’s young cellmate and protege, runs his fingers along the back of arow of hardbound books in the small library that Unit B prisoners are allowed to use during thelate hours before lockdown. Since Miggy died, more and more prisoners have been coming tothe library to get their reading material. Suddenly, a faint voice breaks the usual silence of the library. “Jason,” one low gruff voice says, “I’m not one to be jerked around. You know that.” “I’m not jerking you around you fucking Nazi,” says a voice that Tim McDonald recognizes asJason Snow’s. “I told you I’d think about it. I thought about it, and my answer is no.” “I already explained to you what kind of man you’re protecting,” says the gruff voice. TimMcDonald pulls a book carefully out of place and finds himself with a great view of a meeting inthe next aisle. He’s surprised to find Jason Snow speaking to the arch enemy of Doug Hattoni -Harvey Blake. “And you’re so much better?” Snow fires, finding it hard to sustain a whisper under these heatedcircumstances. “You’re the man who murdered Miggy. You’re lucky I’m not stompingholes in your neck right now, you sick fuck!” Harvey grimaces. “You don’t understand shit about this place, son!” Harvey nearly grabs Snowby the front of his white tank top. “I do what I have to do to survive. Killing that little spick wasjust fucking business - nothing more. But you defending that God damn drug dealing bastard -that’s not business. That’s fucking gluttony. You could survive just fine without Hattoni. Hell,you could even run with my boys.” “We both know that’s not going to happen.” “Hey, whatever you fucking want,” Harvey says, glaring at Snow through his lone eye. “Butremember this you God damn guinea-fucker hippy, you’re going to regret this decision. Youmark my fucking words.”
