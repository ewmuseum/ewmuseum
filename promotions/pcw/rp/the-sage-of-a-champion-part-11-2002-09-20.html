The Sage Of A Champion: Part 11

20 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-20-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!Part 11” Again Same - Chapter 4: A Sidetracking Sent Straight From HellAgain Same - Day 6: The Insane One Has Been Chosen :: Well, the time keeps going by and we are still in the same scene pretty much… All four men: BladeD, Mir, Kirk, and General Powers are all discussing the events that took place back in a war which we still have no idea about and they are also discussing something called “Violent Ray” and “Metal Armour/Armor (same thing)” which we know little information about… when we left off BladeD and Mir were in an argue over the fact that Mir had a plan A and that was it, which was a stupid idea because if that failed there would be nothing to back up on. :: Athens Mir: Ok, I understand now! Stop yelling and clam down! BladeD: I am just doing this to save your life that is all. General Powers: Ok you two. It is time to stop this arguing and decide exactly what we are going to do about all this… The only problem is you BladeD. BladeD: Me? …. What about me? General Powers: Well, it looks like to me that if you decide to be apart of this journey you are going to have to forget everything going on in BattlePowers because you will not be able to attend anything that deals with BattlePowers for the next week or so. BladeD: And why is that? General Powers: For two reasons: (1) You will need the time to prepare for this journey and make sure that all the back up and radio communication is properly dealt with. (2) If you tried finishing off BattlePowers it would take a lot more than just a week and you know that, so you better decide right not or before Monday, what it is that you want to do. BladeD: Well, I guess I have till Monday to decide then. Kirk: BladeD… You really want BattlePowers shutdown so bad don’t you? Just give it up man! BladeD: Kirk, that thinking right there is exactly why you haven’t moved up a rank in almost a year… so stop your talking.:: Kirk begins to get infuriated with BladeD and at this point wants to just deck BladeD in the face, but at the same times knows that there is no chance on God’s green Earth that he could hang two seconds with BladeD. :: Kirk: You better watch the way you talk to me or I will :: General Powers stops Kirk in his tracks :: General Powers: Kirk, sit down and be quiet right this instant. Kirk: But BladeD General Powers: I don’t care. Just sit down and be quiet before I have to threaten you with demotions. :: Kirk doesn’t say a word and sits down. :: BladeD: I really hate to say this because it is just going to make you more mad at me Kirk, but check! :: well saying check BladeD also using his first finger to make a check sign in the air. :: :: Kirk bursts right out of his chair and steps forward to punch BladeD, but Athens Mir catches Kirk in the middle of the punch and BladeD is on the ground laughing at this point seeing as Kirk just got checked hard. It may have been an immature thing on BladeD part, but at the same time it was for sure a funny thing, in fact, even the general himself has a little smirk on his face. Kirk leans over a little bit to hit BladeD, but Athens Mir is too much for Kirk to get through. :: General Powers: OK KIRK! WE DON’T NEED YOU ANYMORE AND ALL YOU ARE TRYING TO DO RIGHT NOW IS CAUSE TROUBLE, SO I WANT YOU OUT OF MY OFFICE THIS INSTANT. YOU CAN BACK TO DOING WHATEVER AND IF WE NEED YOU WE WILL CALL FOR YOU AGAIN!!!! :: That is one angry general. Athens Mir forces Kirk out of the room, but has a lot of trouble doing so. Kirk is captain for a reason, so he is a tough man. It isn’t anything compared to BladeD, but he is a tough man to handle and Athens Mir can’t handle much, so I bet he was having a harder time, then what we can see. Which that is kind of funny in it’s own view because Athens Mir is probably going to be involved with saving the country and really he isn’t near the rank you would have to be in order to perform a task like this. The only reason why he is getting the chance to perform a task like this is because he was supposedly going to be performing this task in the first place. Anyway, back to the scene. :: BladeD: ::still laughing on the floor, but gets up and in his chair. :: Athens Mir: :: sits in his chair laughing as well. ::General Powers: Ok you two. You can stop laughing now because you are going to make me laugh. BladeD: Come on general. I haven’t ever heard you laugh before. You can laugh just this one time. General Powers: No. I really should be having you suspended or something of that sort for making Kirk mad at you like that, but the only reason why I am holding it back is because you are about to save the world if you choose to do so. BladeD: Well, I guess I am a lucky person then. Athens Mir: What about me? I am going to be involved right? General Powers: Well, that is what we are going to have to decide because really I would say no, but if you are truly on our side on not going to backstab us then I guess we might be able to find a way to fit you in all of this, but at the same time I am not sure because this is mainly a one person save the world type of deal. BladeD: And like I said before. I don’t even think you are ready for something like this. Athens Mir: Well, I know I am, so you should let me. General Powers: Ok, this is what we are going to do. First we are going to make up three plans… Or is that too many BladeD? BladeD: You can never have too many plans. General Powers: All right then. We are going to have Main Plan A, Main Back Up Plan B, and Back Up Plan C. BladeD: And Mir is going to be involved? Athens Mir: Yes! General Powers: Yes, BladeD. He is. BladeD: I don’t really like the sound of all of this, but you are the general I can’t disobey your orders. Athens Mir: Trust me BladeD! I am on your side; I am going to backstab you or anything of that sort. I am here to help and I have been here to help since day one and I wish I could get that through your thick head. BladeD: Well, you know. If you would have told everyone that you were going to be a spy and everything before hand, then it would have been easier to trust you, but you wanted to be some big hero and not tell anyone until you were all ready going into the war. Athens Mir: Yes, I wanted to surprise everyone. That was part of the plan. BladeD: Then you tell the general. I mean that is one reason why he doesn’t really want you involved right now because of trust…. Which could have been kept if you would have told him all of this before hand. :: Well, just like it always been lately, we leave you in the middle of a conversation, but at least now we know we can except three things coming up. Main Plan A, Main Back Up Plan B, and Back Up Plan C…. So we have a little knowledge of what is ahead of us and I bet that because they are starting to get the plans together it means it is almost time for BladeD and/or Athens Mir to take action and retrieve this book that is going to make the difference in whether the USA stays the USA or not, so stay tuned!
