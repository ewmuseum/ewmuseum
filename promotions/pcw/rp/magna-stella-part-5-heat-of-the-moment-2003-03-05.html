Magna Stella - Part 5: Heat Of The Moment

5 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3 - 5 - 03 Merrily bouncing up and down the edge of the queen size hotel bed, Jessica Carter holds a cordless Southwestern Bell phone to her right ear and steadies herself with the edge of the bed with her other hand. She seems to be all smiles as she listens to the feed from the other end of the call, wearing a bright, toothy grin on her face and a shining sparkle in her beautiful hazel eyes. Wearing a neon green halter-top and tight denim blue jeans, she looks as though she’s getting ready to go shopping with her girlfriends in some elaborate journey through the local mall. Though it’s nothing of the sort, she still seems to be excited. “Well that’s great, Dorn!” Her voice rings with giddy, girlish excitement. “I’m glad you agree! It’s going to be great to see you perform, too! Should I bring anything?” By her comments and questions, it seems as if the Shooting Star Champion is going to be flying Jessica in again to witness him during the fatal four way on Fusion. An audible noise that resembles words comes from the other end of the call, but it’s hard to make out what they are. Jessica nods her head and suddenly takes on a focused, determined look, even resorting to biting her lip. “Alright, I understand, but why did she say that?” More murmurs come from the receiver, and Jessica’s pretty face suddenly takes on a grim mask of realization. She begins to nibble on the meat of her lip even more, almost inadvertently. The muffled sounds of Dorn’s words seem to be disturbing her more and more as the mere seconds go by, drawing an ever more worried look from her. She nods her head to acknowledge something, but we know not what. Finally, she speaks, her voice trembling with hints of terror, “Alright, Dorn… I’ll try and be careful. Any idea why she was so angry?” She listens to the other end, and sighs gravely, “I see… well, thanks for the info, Dorn. I’ll try to be careful. Talk to you later.” A muffled response from the other end, then a mousy click. Jessica pulls the small, gray cordless telephone from under her dark brown hair and presses the large green Talk button, causing the phone to turn off with a loud BEEP! She lifts herself from the edge of the hotel bed and walks over to the wooden dresser before her, carefully replacing the phone back on its charger. She runs her thin, trembling fingers through the rows of her straight, soft hair and begins to slowly pace back and forth in front of the bed, letting her morbid thoughts run rampant through her head. Why would she be mad at me? I’m just trying to help Dorn. There’s nothing wrong with that, Or at least she doesn’t think so. After all, isn’t that what En’Flarim would want? Her warrior in tip-top shape? That is what all I’m doing, right? That’s what she wants to believe. She wants to believe she’s helping her best friend, but alas, that’s not what En’Flarim… or Dorn seems to think for that matter. Jessica has a feeling he’s just playing along to make her happy, which in turn greatly upsets her. In fact, as Jessica pays to and fro across the hotel room, she can feel a ball of angry slowly starting to rise from the pits of his stomach. I understand him telling her, but why would her just totally blow off my efforts? Doesn’t he understand what I’m trying to do? She’s got to have him brainwashed… he totally ignores what I’m doing… The beauty stomps her foot on the hard carpet floor and lets go of her body, plummeting back first onto the bed. Her chest rises as the young woman takes in a breath of air, and slowly exhales with a hint of sorrow buried deep within the wind. She drapes a forearm across her forehead and sighs once again, just trying to relax her mind and body. “I’m sure it will all work out…” She states dully, slowly drifting off. Jessica kicks the blanket away and turns her head to the left, a blank look in her eye. “I’m sure it could be much worse. That Dragon Goddess could just confront me personally…” Jessica then begins to fidget uncomfortably, feeling the heat in the room rise. Her body rolls from side to side, as if trying to ward the waves of warmth away from her. Beads of sweat slowly begin to roll down her forehead and into her long eyelashes. “Geez, it’s kind of hot in here…” The subtle wave of heat slowly asserts itself more, pounding Jessica’s delicate body like a torched sledgehammer. She begins to gulp rapidly, trying not to sweat so hard. A feeling of horror begins to build inside of her, Why is it so fucking hot? God, I’m going to die if I don’t get out of here! Jessica climbs off the bed weakly and starts to stumble towards the door, reaching towards it. But her vision has since blurred since the heat rise, and she can’t get a lock on the knob. Her breathing becomes rapid and labored as she can feel herself suffocating under the heat. She wants to cry for help, but the words come out but mere hoarse whisper… I’m dying… God save me…, and with that, she collapses. Her body plummets to the floor and her head crashes off the carpet, bouncing off before settling against it. Her breathing slows… the sweat drips down her face… then she blacks out. Almost immediately after Jessica falls into a coma, the heat in the room begins to dissipate. Little good it does now, for she’s already out. In fact, the temperature drops back down to normal, in a strange turn of events. Everything appears to be normal… …except that image of En’Flarim in the mirror, grinning with fire blazing in her eyes. “Sleep well, mortal. Interfere no further, for your time has ended.” And with that, the image bursts into flames and dies away. Padded thumps in rapid succession suddenly break into the air and sound like they’re getting closer and closer. Soon, the door bursts open and the hotel manager comes charging in. “Is everything alright? The room scanner said the temperature in high got up to 170…WOAH!” He stops short and gasps at the sight of Jessica Carter, out cold on the floor. The manager charges right back out of the room and down the hallway, to go get the authorities. Jessica never stirs… …and En’Flarim looks on with glee. [End.]
