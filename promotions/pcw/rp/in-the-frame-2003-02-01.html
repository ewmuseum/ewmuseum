In the frame

1 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/02/03 A small while ago, I wondered to myself about the mysteries of this chemical reaction in the brain that they call love. Teresa Tomas helped me to understand at least a few of it’s mysteries. Now, I have been signed... no, forced into a match this friday with a man I loathe - except at my side. To ensure no tricks are pulled, especially with a man who has a reputation such as Gaz Green, I’ve taken steps to make sure someone is always watching my back. My faithful pet, Fluffy the chupacabra rests on my right shoulder. Marching behind me with a heavy plodding is the man that I made in my image - my metallic representative, Nerezzatron. A robot built merely for my own amusement, now serving as my bodyguard. And where are we marching in this chilling February night? We're following that trickster in front. That backstabbing, plotting little runt. Gaz Green. And why? Because he has a great idea involving the people that I handed the Tag Team titles to. Since Pranks With Attitude is now Front Office property and I have the threat of being fired hanging over my head like the Sword Of Damocles, I have to march to the beat of this lunatic. Tonight, his depraved little song has lyrics that suggest we’re going to do something and blame Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor. “Quit muttering to yourself back there, Squire. Pick up the pace, the three of you! Just because one of you is made of metal doesn’t mean the other two can act like they have lead in their feet!” The Jester barked with a snotty air of superiority. We’d entered a park in the dead of night and while nobody was around, the plan was to destroy as much public property as we could and mark the area incriminatingly. What makes me sick is that I had suggested it to him. I guess the old days of pranking had never left my body. “HISSSSSSSSSS!” Fluffy's spines perked up as we stopped in our tracks. “What's wrong with the Kangaroo?” Gaz said, suddenly spooked. Maybe it hadn't occured to him that he was alone in a deserted area with a vampire, a chupacabra and a robot, all of which didn't really like him. “It's a Chupacabra...” I grunted in a low voice. “And he's spotted something.” “Spotted what?” The jester quivered. The fear in his voice was obvious. I could feel it in the air, after all, it's what a vampire like me feeds off. His heartbeat was quickening and I could feel another heartbeat nearby. It wasn’t human, but Fluffy’s hissing and spitting had driven Gaz into a petrified stop. Suddenly, Fluffy leaped forwards like a cat with his huge, muscular hind legs and came down with a thump. I tiptoed forwards to see what was happening while Gaz let out a shriek. Peering down, I saw Fluffy holding a squirrel to the ground with his front paws. Fluffy’s little jaw popped open and like a flash of lighting, his tongue shot out, driving itself deep into the creature’s neck. Blood flowed up the hollow tongue like a straw until no more than a few bubbles left. He then withdrew the tube-like length of dark pink flesh and squeaked, looking from left to right. Like a litle puppy, he then bounded back over to me and sat on his haunches, awaiting my arm. I obliged and held out one hand, where he jumped up onto it and ran up my shoulder, even going upside down at one point like the squirrel he had just consumed. He then sat back down on my shoulder pad, which had become his favourite little perch. “Just a squirrel, oh fearless one.” I smirked. Gaz frowned and reached inside the bottomless pit that was his pine green jacket. His gloved hand pulled out a cyclinder, which he threw at me and aimed for my head on purpose. Luckily, Fluffy had seen it coming and jumped up to snatch it out of the air. He was about attempt to bite it, but I quickly took it off him and held my hand out for him to climb up again. He peered at the object as I examined it. “Pink spray paint? We're going for petty vandalism?” “Not WE, Rezz. Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor. Just smash a few things up, uproot the swings, spray something like Chris Loves Tessa wherever you feel appropriate. I'll take care of the miniscule details. Now let's get to work. Start with that climbing frame over there.” Gaz ordered, indicating to a set of red, blue, yellow and green rungs shaped like a tank. This wasn't going to be easy. “You know, Chris and Tessa wouldn't be strong enough to move that out of place... hell, they wouldn't even be able to dent something like that. Shouldn't we at least do things in their strength limit?” I objected. Gaz was preoccupied with covering a nearby tree in soppy song lyrics and adding love hearts. Sighing to myself, pointed towards a park bench and looked towards the Nerezzatron. “Sit on that... HARD.” I commanded. Lumbering up to the bench like some great steel caveman, the robot turned around and pulled it's ankles out from under itself, driving his posterior into the wood. The splinters must have jumped ten feet into the air as the mechanical monster crushed the wood beneath his weight. Fluffy had decided to try it out himself, but only suceeded in bouncing up and down as though the bench were a trampoline. Frustrated, the creature floated over to an inner tube swing and began to tear it up with his sharp claws and teeth. “REZZ!” Gaz barked. “Don’t just give orders, even I’m doing something!” he said, laying down a lacy black stocking over one of the smashed benches. “Gaz, what the HELL is that doing there?” I squawked, searching for something to do. “Quite simple: Chris and Tessa, well known for their activities, had a little too much to drink and then they started to cuddle in a park. And then the cuddling got a little heavier and then...” Oopsie Tessa Bear, I think we broke something! No matter Chrissie-poohs! Hey, let’s declare out love by writing on this tree! In my haste, I’d not thought about it. Of course, this was perfect. Frame the happy couple and give them a vandalism charge to worry about during the tag match. For a moment, it seemed as though the old times were coming back. I marched up to the metal, hollow tank and spread my fingers apart as they began to lengthen. Turning into dark grey knives, each digit helped me to scratch crude graffiti into the paintwork on the sides of the centerpiece of the park. Scratching my claws on the frame made a horrid screeching noise that caused Gaz to cover his ears. Now written on the largest panel of metal were the incriminating words “Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor were here at 3am 01/02/03” “Rezz! I believe you’re coming back to us! Perhaps the Inhuman League here has a place in the Front Office after all.” The Jester mused to me with a surprised and proud grin. I was looking cheerful myself until the mention of Kannon’s corrupt stable was made. I brushed his hand from my shoulder and snorted, looking away from him. “I’m only helping you out of the fact that I haven’t pulled a good prank in ages. Besides, this was my idea. I had to personally see it through.” I growled. My lips pulled back as I turned, showing him each an every one of my teeth. “And you can forget any thoughts of friendship. I’m in this purely for the sport, just like the rematch this Friday. Chris and Tessa are good competition.” “Whatever, Squire. Just make sure you have my back, or you can kiss your PCW spot goodbye.” Gaz purred with confidence. “Now let’s get out of here. Bring the InHuman League too. It’s time to plan for phase two.” Gaz had a point. There was nothing I could do but to obey what he said. My career was in his hands like a Slinky. As much as I wanted to turn and strangle him, things were clear. My name is Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. And I am the property of PCW’s Front Office.
