The Black Suits [3]

7 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 7, 2002 I walked into the wrestling locker room, more joyous than ever. Not only did I feel energetic and exited to be working out, but I had an incredible looking crystal to give to my sugar lips, aka, Nat. I was sure the second that she saw it, that she would gush all over me and love me to death. Was I do anything to get laid? Who me? As I walked through the metallic doors, I noticed a man standing near my locker. His name was Bryan Topper, a dark match wrestler. He was about five feet four, maybe a tad over one hundred and eighty pounds, and very well built. His muscles didn't seem to fit the body though, and it was unfortunate that he had stopped growing. He had a strong chin, something that made him stand out from others. I always joked around with him that if he didn't make it as a wrestler, a chin like that could land him a seat in the senate. He wasn't very amused. I worked out with Bryan occassionally, since he was so knowledgeable about weights and such. He lifted quite a bit, hoping that big muscles would make up for his lack of height. I knew better. He would probably never make it past a midcarding position. I just didn't have the heart to tell him. Bryan was dressed in his regular work out clothes which consisted of a black Metallica t-shirt and blue shorts. It was the same outfit that he had worn to all of their workouts so far. I didn't mind it at all, but I'd wish he'd at least take off the sun glasses. That just made everything seem so unprofessional. How many people do you know who work out in sun glasses? 'Hey Tom,' Bryan said, lacing up his shoes. He had nice Nike work out shoes, which probably cost him a nice bundle. Good for him. Jealous? Who me? 'What's up, Topper?' I replied, mocking his name. I did that often, and I knew it bugged him. Leave it to me to find your weakness and then exploit it. I set my bag next to him, and sat on the locker shelf. 'So, you ready for the hardest work out ever?' Bryan laughed, shaking his head confidently. 'I was about to ask you the same thing. You're the one that can't bench your own weight.' 'Look, I never claimed to be the world's strongest man. Just because you're Mr. Muscle doesn't mean you have the right to bag on poor weaklings like myself.' 'Hey, look what I have,' I said, taking out the emerald crystal. I couldn't help myself but brag, although I knew that I shouldn't have brought it down to the lockerroom with me. Bryan stared at the crystal, not taking his eyes off of it. 'Whoa...where did you get that nice piece of rock?' 'You wouldn't believe me if I told you,' I replied, still admiring the crystal as well. 'You think Nat will like it?' 'Like it? Dude, I'd bang you if you gave me something like that...' Bryan said, nodding his head. 'As would we....' A strange voice said behind us. I looked behind myself, expecting to see some big bad wrestler guys trying to hoard in on my crystal. Wrestlers I can handle - shit, I am a wrestler myself. I'm not undefeated for nothing, you know? But my surprise could be seen from a mile away, as I looked up and found myself face to face with an old, middle aged man, and a younger, asian man. They were both dressed in black suits, with black ties, and very bad hair cuts. I laughed, not really knowing what to make of the situation. 'What's so funny?' Chopsticks said, getting annoyed. Oops, I had annoyed the asian dude. 'I was just thinking how the three of you could fight over who is going to bang me.' I replied, sarcastically and stupidly. I always made dumb ass comments - forgive my ignorance. 'Could you do me a favor, Tom?' The older man said. He had a serious voice, although I could tell he's had his share of adventures as well. No one that old couldn't have. 'What's that, old man?' 'Could you please hand me the crystal?' I nearly wet my pants at his request. He actually thought that I would give him the crystal willingly, without a fight. Who did this guy think he was? Rambo? He obviously had no experience with dealing with hostages, because if he did, he would known that there is some give and take involved with anything. What he had told me was a demand, nothing more. I didn't like being demanded of - actually, I hated it. There were only two people that were able to order me around at all, and they were my mother and Nat. Both of them were women, and this old geezer standing in front of me was a man - the odds were already against him. 'Why should I?' I said, feeling a bit rude and like an ass hole, but then again, sue me. This foreign guy was asking for my girlfriend's gift...wrong move. I shifted the crystal into my back hand, slipping it into my back pocket. I always hoping that even if they did jump me, they wouldn't be able to find the crystal. Then again, there were two of them. If they were desperate enough to jump me to get for it, they would most definitely attempt to pat me down. I looked at the two men with a weird look. I couldn't tell if these guys were the physical type or not. The old white guy seemed a bit out of shape, but I couldn't tell anything about the asian guy. He was asian, just like myself, so there was no telling if he had any martial arts background or what not. Sure, it was a stereotype that every asian person knew martial arts, but then again, maybe I'm just jealous. I never had the great agility that guys like Bruce Lee or Jet Li had - I just had their hereditary background. That wouldn't be enough against a third degree black belt, however. 'Because if you don't, your life will be in danger, as well as the lives of many other people.' The old white guy was giving me thoughts of world domination, appealing to my sense of morality and duty. This was already sounding like a very bad episode of Miami Vice, or maybe a James Bond flick. I wasn't white, I didn't own spiffy outfits, and I didn't have ray guns in my jacket pockets. I guess all I could do is use what I used best - my wonderful charm. 'Fuck you.' I replied. Oooh, nice reply. I'm sure that's going to go over well. I surprise myself, sometimes. 'Don't be a fool,' Chopsticks said, getting angrier. He had to be restrained by the old white guy, but I could that he wanted to rush me, proving to me who the better asian was. We were competitive like that. 'You would risk the lives of people, just for the sake of keeping a shiny piece of crystal?' I shrugged. These guys were strangers, and I didn't know who they were. Besides, I wasn't about to give up the crystals to two guys I didn't even trust. Albeit, I didn't trust too many people anyways. For all I knew, these guys were the baddies that I should be protecting the crystal from. Bah! Who am I kidding? I'm no super hero; I'm not the military officer who protects the president...I'm just a wrestler. A wrestler with a very nice piece of crystal in my back pocket. It began slidding around in my pants, and it was only now that I began to appreciate the slick and cold feel of crystal. I nearly yelped out, and started to adjust the crystal back there. If I didn't know better, it looked like I was scratching my ass. Oh god, how embarrassing. Anyways, if the baddies didn't know where the crystal was before, they knew now. I was fiddling around with my pants so much, it must have been obvious. As I looked back up, the white man was still trying to calm the asian guy down. I heard him address the asian guy as 'chopsticks.' My, if that wasn't a racially prejudice name, I was a main event wrestler. But the asian guy didn't look too insulted, and actually was listening to him. He crossed his arms, as if he had lost the argument, and let the older guy take control of the situation. I knew who wore the pants in this black suit couple now. 'We don't want any trouble, ok?' The old man said, trying to calm the situation down. 'I will tell you, there's a very dangerous man out there. His name is Sporty. He wants that crystal you're holding, and if he gets his hand on it...' 'What? It'll be the end of the world?' I asked incredulously. I tried to sound as sarcastic as I could, with trying to sound as serious as possible. It worked well in hiding the fear in my voice. What if questions kept popping in my head. Damn my analytical nature. 'Yes it will be,' The old man replied. 'And if you are not cooperative, then we'll have to take the crystal by force. I hope it doesn't come to that, but you're leaving us no choice... The two men pulled out guns from their jackets, and pointed them directly at me. I started to back up slowly, making my way towards the door, but I suddenly backed into Bryan. I had forgotten he was behind me. He had a scared look on his face, and I honestly didn't blame him. If there was shooting involved, I would just give up the crystal. I didn't want Bryan to become just another innocent bystander. But as I looked at him, he nodded at me, and we both knew what was going to happen. I looked forward, to see that they were both advancing on me. The guns were not fake, as far as I could tell, but then again, I'm not too smart. That's probably why I was about to do what I was about to do. 'Hey guys! What's that behind you?' I shouted, pointing directly behind them. 'Oh my god! It's Sporty!' Yes, it was a lie, but I had no choice. As expected, they both turned around, afraid of whatever a Sporty was. That was my chance...as they were turned around, I rushed them both, tackling them to the ground. They collapsed to the floor, and hit head first. It was almost like a double bulldog, only without the right impact. I hurriedly got to my feet, grabbing a gun that had fallen from Chopsticks. As I took it and tucked it under my arm, I believe Chopsticks yelled at me to bring my asian butt back. I didn't reply back, since I was too busy trying to save my backstabbing behind. Sure, it wasn't all that brave, but I got away didn't I? I ran and ran...never looking back....I had exited the arena, and now I was just running in the streets... Beep! Honk! Get outta the damn street you loony! Beep! Beep! I hurriedly stepped out of the busy street, and breathed a sigh of relief that I was still alive. One sigh was for surviving the two armed men in the locker room, while the other sigh of relief was for not being run over. I was foolish to just run into the busy San Francisco streets, but then again, I wasn't exactly in the right frame of mind at the moment. That's what adrenaline could do to you - you often had no idea what you were doing. I looked around suddenly, noticing that some passerbys were eyeing me suspiciously. I realized that I was still holding a gun under my arm. I couldn't blame them, I supposed. I would stare at someone who had a gun in their hand, although I'd be more worried about a bazooka. I tucked the gun away into my pants, and saw a local McDonalds a block away. I was oddly thirsty and hungry, one from the running, and two...well, you saw what I had for breakfast. I didn't know if it was safe to head in to a public place after I had just been handed my life a second ago, but my stomach had made up its mind. I had to oblige. I walked into the fast food restaurant, which was unusually filled for some reason. I checked my watch, to see that it was only 11:00 o'clock a.m. It had just turned the hour for lunch, and I guess a lot of people had shitty lunches like myself. I stepped in line, where I was in back of at least eight other people. There was an assortment of families, public workers, and even a couple of suits waiting for their early lunches. I was a bit wary of the guys in suits, as I know held no love for them. For all I knew, they carried guns under their own coats. Paranoid? Who me? I was standing behind an arguing couple. They were arguing over what they were going to eat, which I thought was a rather stupid topic of discussion. Then again, myself and Nat had argued over more than that before. I was always embarrassed to be around people who were arguing - it always seemed like I was eavesdropping. Which I was, let's not make a mistake there. But I still didn't like it. I looked up at the menu, only to see that there were no specials today, except on soups. I don't know about you, but I didn't come to McDonalds for a soup. I wanted burgers and grease - two very bad things for your body, yet tasted oddly good. Oh, they were devils food. I looked into the back store window, making sure those two suited goons weren't still coming for me. As I did, I noticed something flying in the distance. I thought it was a bird or something, but as it flew in closer, I suddenly realized that it was no bird. No, it was much too large to be a bird. And it was coming straight for me... SLAM! CRASH! BOOM! Screams echoed all around me as the window burst into shards of glass. There was a man...uhm, I think it was a man, who had just flown into the McDonalds through the window. He had crashed straight through a poster of Ronald McDonald. How disrepectful. His face was like a man's, yet his eyes were completely a dark yellow color, and of course, he had those awfully cool wings. He came in hissing and shouting, and began grabbing people, flinging them aside. I suddenly realized who it was. Hello Sporty. 'Fuck,' I whispered. I then stamped my foot and threw my hands in the air. I'm never going to get a decent meal today...
