Guess Who is Back!!!

9 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th June 2002 Losing....... No one likes doing it, but everyone has got to go through it. Everyone has got to go through that sense of failure, that sense of humiliation, looking up as your opponent is claimed the victor. Losing makes you feel like there is no point to the world, that you can't carry on with your life. It makes you come to terms with the fact that someone else is better than you are, at something you thought you were unbeatable at............. Now, yes The Dark Messiahs did lose their debut match at Fusion, and yes they got injured in their first match and disappeared of the face of the earth. However they are back and for some strange reason Daniel and Mike don't feel that drained, they don't feel that sense of failure. They knew going into the Fusion match that they were going up against former Tag Team Champions, but that didn't bother them. As they entered that ring they had a sense of purpose, they were there to win. But that was not the case, as after a hard battle, the Dark Messiahs were tripped at the finishing line and The Heartbreakers took the victory. Now you would have thought that Daniel and Mike's moral at this point would be at and all time low, after losing and getting injured by the Heartbreakers but that is not the case, as we are about to find out........ The scene opens up in a house's hallway. The hallway is well lit with the lamp on the side table showing us the chaos that has consumed the hallway. Pictures and their frames have been smashed and broken with sharp shards of glass glinting in the light. Flowers lie on the oak floor surrounded by a puddle of water and the remains of a blue vase. However the most disturbing thing in this scene of chaos is the blood smeared walls. From all these clues it is quite obvious that The Dark Messiahs have struck again. Suddenly our attention is changed from the scene of chaos to the sound of the evil laughter of The Dark Messiahs from the room to our right. The door quickly swings open and we step into what was the lounge, the reason I use the word 'was' is quite simply because yet again we are confronted by another scene of chaos. Chairs have been upturned and yet again pictures smashed. Wooden coffees table broken in half and stained with blood. In the middle of this chaos we see Stone and Steel standing over a bloody and battered man. The man's face is nothing but a crimson mask, from what we can see of his facial features he is in his late 20s. His shirt and jeans are stained with blood. The pain on his face soon disappears as he passes into unconsciousness. Steel and Stone's faces are nothing but anger!!! Steel looks down at the man............ Daniel Steel: (Angry) Next time when ask you for some milk, you give us some f**king milk. Mike Stone: (Sighs and turns to face Steel) Some people today are just so anti-social, it is unbelievable. Daniel Steel: (Calmer) I know, if people are going to be idiots, then they have to be willing to pay the price. It makes me sick how today's society is turning out. I mean just look at the so-called United States Government with a damn chimpanzee at the helm it is enough to make you sick. Anyway we are not here to talk about politics, we need to discuss our plan for PCW. Mike Stone: (Agreeing) Indeed, we have been away a few weeks, it is time for us to get back on track, and achieve our dream, becoming the PCW Tag Team Champions. Daniel Steel: 3 weeks is a long time in the wrestling business, anything and everything can happen. I mean just a few days ago I heard Xavier Kannon's Valet, Eleanor Hall became Extreme Champion at that School Out Match. Also I learnt that the Price Brothers have gone on leave. Mike Stone: (Holding hands up) Woho, back up, you mean out of all those guys who reckoned themselves as hardcore to the bone, a woman won the title?!?! Daniel Steel: (Smirking) Yep. Mike Stone: (Smirking) Well that's just......just.....hilarious!!! Both men burst out laughing at the visual image of all those 'hardcore' guys getting their ass kicked by sweet innocent Eleanor Hall. After a few seconds of laughter both men start to get control of themselves. Steel takes a step back and seems to stumble on something. As he does the TV which seems to be untouched comes to life. Steel looks down at what he has tripped over and picks up a remote. Stone shrugs and both look at the TV screen. On TV at the moment (amazingly) is the PCW News Hour. The Newsreader looks up from a few papers, and smiles......... Newsreader: (Cheerful) Good evening, and welcome to PCW News. Tonight we discuss the shocking news of Kannon's and Ellie's wedding. But first the current situation of the PCW Tag Team division. Now over the past few weeks the tag team division has lost 2 teams. The first was The Dark Messiahs, who are rumoured to be making their return in the near future. Also The Price Brothers, who have been told if they are to return then they will have to start all over again in the PWA. When the PCW re-opened everyone had so much hope for the Tag Team division, but now it seems that it is following the same path it did before. With Politically Incorrect concentrating on single careers, and other teams seemingly to have disappeared, the division's hopes now lay in the hands off two teams, The Heartbreakers and the Pretty Cool Guys........... Both Steel and Stone's faces grow in anger as the Newsreader continues........... Newsreader: The Heartbreakers former PCW Tag Team Champions and former NTICW Tag Team Champions, Pretty Cool Guys, both look to be the favourites to be going to the next PPV to face each other for the vacant Tag Team titles. There is no doubt in this newsreader’s mind that both these teams are the PCW's Tag Team division's last hope. Now onto.................... Before the Newsreader can go onto the next segment, Stone's boot has smashed through the TV Screen. His face is of pure anger as well as Steel's.......... Mike Stone: (Pure Anger) Last Hope!!!! Last f**king hope!!!! How dare they call those two worthless teams, PCW's Tag Team division's last hope!!!! If they are the PCW's last hope, then god help the PCW!!! (Turns to Steel) It's time Daniel, it's time for the Dark Messiahs do take what is rightfully theirs, the PCW Tag Team Championships!!!!! Daniel Steel: (Shouting) The Heartbreakers and Pretty Cool Guys, maybe the favourites now, but not for long!!!! This is our time, this is our place!!!! The Heartbreakers and Pretty Cool Guys, better watch out because they have been targeted, and as they say revenge is a dish best served cold!!!!! With that Steel and Stone stand over the smashed TV, as the scene fades to black.........
