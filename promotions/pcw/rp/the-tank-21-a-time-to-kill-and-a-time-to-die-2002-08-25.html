The Tank 21 - A Time to Kill, and a Time to Die

25 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 25, 2002 ﻿*********The Tank __21—********* A Time to Kill, and a Time to Die Pinpoint the destinationAnd rip yourself apart along the way ~Leigh Donovan Saturday, August 24 - Day 50 A few minutes ago, Tim McDonald spotted Snow stomping out of Geoffery Rankin’s office,looking pale and disturbed. He knows, Tim thought with a smile when he saw the fiery lion. In fact, Tim McDonald has known for quite some time that the man who Doug Hattoni wassupposed to hire to keep Snow’s young sister-in-law away from drugs has actually been dealingto her. Now, Tim McDonald makes haste as he approaches the head C.O of Unit B, Benji Graham. “What the fuck do you want, McDonald?” frowns Graham, the man the prisoner’s refer to as‘Ahab’. “I’m a gambling man, Ahab,” Tim says with a sly smile. For the last few days, he’s been puttingall of the various information he’s been getting from various sources into order and now he’sready to bet on the outcome - “I’m about to make a gamble.” “What the fuck are you talking about?” Graham asks - just because he’s been in Doug Hattoni’spocket for years doesn’t mean he’ll treat Hattoni’s highest-ranking gang member with respect. Slickly, Tim McDonald pulls a wad of cash out of his denim pocket and slides it onto the controldesk, shocking Graham. “Jesus!” Graham exclaims before quickly lowering his voice. “What the fuck is going on here,McDonald?” “I need a favor.” “I gathered that,” says McDonald smugly. “Listen,” McDonald says, finally getting down to business. “Something’s going to go down inUnit E in a little while.” “And?” “And I want you to take all your buddies up there to get things back under control,” McDonaldsays with a sly smile. “You want me to lead all the C.O’s out of Unit B?” the large man smirks. “Fuck that, Timmy - Iain’t losing my fucking job over one of your scams.” “You won’t lose anything, trust me,” McDonald says. “The Warden’s going to call you guys upthere anyway - I just want you to make sure that you’re on your toes when it happens, and bringa couple of extra boys up there when it goes down. Trust me, you’re going to need them.” Withthat, Tim reaches into his pocket once more and adds another pile of bills to the one alreadyresting on the conrtol desk. “What the fuck’s going on anyway?” Graham asks, slipping the cash off the table and into thebreast pocket of his black uniform. “A good gambler never tips his hand,” McDonald smiles. “I learned that from Harvey Blake.” *** From the Office of the Warden “The climax,” the Warden begins. “That’s what it’s all about, isn’t it? Everything we do in life,we do for one single moment where all of our work pays off. That moment, is theclimax.” *** Harvey Blake bursts into Jake Hooper’s cell while the completely hairless Nazi lies silently onhis book. “Jesus, Harv,” exclaims Jake. “What the fuck’s the matter?” Just outside his cell, hesees Jason Snow stalk by. “Tell the guards your fucking sick and get to the infirmary!” Blake says quickly andpassionately. “Tell the boys in Unit E that the time to strike is now! I want all hell tobreak lose in Unit E! Move motherfucker!” With that, Jake gets up, picks a knife and quickly makes a slash across his forearm. Blood spurtswildly from the gash and Jake quickly rushes out of the cell. *** From the Office of the Warden “The climax is that one perfect moment where everything comes together. Like during sex - theclimax, if done properly, occurs at that final moment where neither lover can take the pleasureany more.” “But sometimes, climaxes arises out of pain. Imagine if missiles were flying toward the sametarget from every conceivable angle, and then at the last moment - all hell breakslose.” *** As soon as he sees Jake Hooper running with a bloody arm toward the C.O at the gate, TimMcDonald knows his gamble paid off. Out of the corner of his eye, he spots Snow stalkingslowly and menacingly toward Doug Hattoni’s cell. “Hey,” McDonald says to the pair of Italians who positioned at the other end of the cell balconyto keep an eye on Hattoni while Snow’s gone. “Snow’s back from his visit. You guys can getback to whacking off or whatever the fuck you do in your spare time.” “Fuck you McDonald,” says one of Hattoni’s body guards, half joking. Along with hiscompanion, the Italian eagerly leaves his post. In their absence, Tim McDonald smiles evilly -his plan is working perfectly. ***From the Office of the Warden “Or maybe the climax can be described as a car, speeding out of control toward a brick wall. Despite all efforts to stop the car, we all know that in the end, we’re going to be looking at acrash...” *** Moments before Snow arrives, Doug Hattoni reaches beneath his bunk and pulls out the cigarbox where he keeps his stash of cash. When he opens it, to his surprise, it’s empty. “....the hell?” Hattoni quietly mumbles. To the best of Hattoni’s knowledge, the only personwho knows where he hides his money is his cellmate, Tim McDonald. “That littlecocksucker...” *** From the Office of the Warden “Whatever the climax is or however it can be described, one things for sure... when it finallyarrives, all hell breaks lose.” *** “Christ, Snow!” exclaims Hattoni when he turns toward his cell gate. “You scared the fuck outof me! Listen, that little punk cellmate of mine just ripped me off for a couple of grand. I wantto go have a little talk with that fucking prick.” Snow silently stares at Hattoni and the old man notices the rage consuming Snow’s vibrant blueeyes. “Kid?” Hattoni says. “What’s wrong?” “You don’t know?” Snow says quietly, sure to block Hattoni’s escape path in case the fat oldman decides to use it. Suddenly, a commotion breaks out on the main floor of the Unit. Benji Graham’s boomingvoice bursts through the air from below - “I want as many people as possible to follow me up toUnit E! We’ve got a fucking riot on our hands up there!” No sooner than the words leave hislips, the majority of Unit B’s C.O’s rush to the main gate where they’re lead from to Unit E,where Harvey Blake’s Nazi’s have finally started a full-scale riot. The few C.O’s that are left soon find their own jobs increasingly difficult as many of theprisoners decide to start trouble due to the lack of supervision. “What the fuck’s going on out there?” Hattoni asks Snow. “Doesn’t matter to you,” Snow answers coldly. “What’s going on in here then?” Hattoni asks, finally cutting through the bush he’s alwaysbeating around. “You know what this is about,” Snow says, trying to control his anger. He takes a step towardHattoni, burning a hole through him with his raging eyes. “I trusted you to protect her youcocksucker - I trusted you!” “Is this about your sister-in-law?” Hattoni gulps, hoping it’s not. He knows how protectiveSnow is of her. “How’d you fucking guess!?” Snow explodes, raising his hand to Hattoni without striking him. “Snow,” Hattoni says in a pleading voice, “I don’t know what this is about, but -” Hattoni’sinterrupted when Snow’s strong thick open palm comes slapping loudly across his fat face. Thesting of the slap prompts Doug to bury his fat face in his hands, terrified of the young lion infront of him now. “Don’t you fucking lie to me!” screams Snow as he grabs Hattoni by the collar and slams his fatback against the conctrete wall behind him. “Don’t you fucking insult me like that!” “Snow,” Hattoni tries to sound stern - it’s useless. “I swear, I don’t know what-” A second time,Snow slaps Hattoni hard across the face. Now, the left side of Doug’s fat face is stinging red. “I should choke the fucking soul out of your body!” Snow says quietly, yet with so muchcontempt that it may as well have been screamed through a megaphone. “I should rip the heartfrom your fucking chest!” Hattoni knows better than to speak. He realizes that when it comes to his sister-in-law, Krista,Snow knows no authority. “I fucking trusted you,” Snow fires pushing Hattoni’s chest harder while holding his collar. “Allthis time, I thought you had your man out there protecting my sister-in-law. Instead, you sent aGod damn drug dealer! I told you to keep her off the drugs, instead you upgraded herfrom LSD to Smack!” Hattoni tries desperately to protest, “Snow, you’re making a huge mistake!” Snow doesn’t even hear the fat mobster. “And all the while, the money the drug dealer’s gettingfrom her is coming back here - to you! Money that she’s been withdrawing from a bankaccount that I set up for her! You had her buying your drugs with mymoney!” Hattoni’s eyes fill completely with terror and he begins to cry - there’s no way out of this now. Snow knows everything. “But you fucked up, Hattoni,” Snow leans in close and whispers. “You know why? Because hedidn’t just sell her Smack...” Hattoni’s eyes grow wide in horror as Snow finishes the story. “He raped her,” the raging beast whispers. A moment later, he puts his face and inch fromHattoni’s and screams, “HE FUCKING RAPED HER!” Hattoni’s face flushes white - he knows now that this is likely his last day on Earth. He begins towhimper like a tortured animal, “You’re going to kill me, aren’t you?” His lips shake and Snowis now supporting his entire weight against the wall - his legs are too shaky to stand on. “Nothing... Nothing... would please me more,” Snow hisses, letting the words sink deepinto Hattoni’s brain and soul. For a long moment, he tortures the fat man with his blazing blueeyes. “But I’m not going to kill you.” Hattoni’s eyes fill with hope and he looks gratefully up at his attacker as the tears flow freelydown his fat face. “Oh Jesus, Snow - thank you!” he exclaims. “You won’t regret this! I swearyou won’t regret this.” Slowly, Snow releases his grip on Hattoni’s collar and says calmly. “I’m not going to kill you,you fat bastard,” he says. “They are.” As soon as the words pass Snow’s lips, Harvey Blake and a pair of his larger Nazi comrades stepinto the cell behind Snow. Immediately, Doug Hattoni’s crying eyes grow wide with terror. “You traitor!” he shrieks at Snow, though the commotion on the main floor leaves his words foronly Snow and the three Nazi’s to hear. “You betrayed me, Doug,” Snow hisses, glaring coldly at the soon-to-die oldman. “And your betrayal left a fucking nineteen year old girl - my fucking sister-in-law -raped. That makes you my enemy, you fat fat fuck!” Snow steps chest to chest with Hattoni. “And you’re the man who taught me, after all...” Snow smiles sadistically. He’senjoying the way Hattoni’s reliving every decision that lead to this point in his life - the finalpoint, and ultimate conclusion. “Keep your friends close, and your enemies...” Snow leans close and whispers... “...In body bags.” With that, Snow turns his back on Hattoni - the man responsible for his sister-in-law’s rape. Heknows that Harvey Blake and his men will not let Hattoni see another day, and maybe notanother minute. The thought of the death of the man responsible for all of Krista’s pain brings aslight bit of happiness into the sickening pit of Snow’s stomach. As he leaves the cell, Snow feels no remorse for what is about to happen. To him, it’s justice. Justice in this place may not be as technical and supervised as justice on the outside, but it’sswift, harsh, and final. “Hiya, Doug,” Harvey Blake says with a smile as he steps toward the old mobster, grabbing apillow off the lower bunk of the cell. “Were you expecting our company?” At the opposite end of the cell balcony, staring across the large gap that exposes the main floorbelow, Tim McDonald watches all of this - an evil smile painted to his young face all the while.
