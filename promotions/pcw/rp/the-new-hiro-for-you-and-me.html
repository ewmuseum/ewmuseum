The New Hiro For You And Me

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Hiro Saotome Land “C’mon roo big roser!” The stereotypical asian sounding voice is heard as our scene begins to fade in from black, although why there would be an asian sounding voice in a Rush Mason promo is really beyond us. “Roo need to get in shape to be a gweat fightah!” The same voice can be heard again, except louder this time, sounding like something out of a terrible, dubbed kung fu movie. Rush: I didn’t come here to train, I came here to find someone, Bruce Lee! Now our scene finally fades in from black, where we can see Rush standing inside what seems to be some sort of gym, although it also seems to be made of bamboo. The asian man standing next to Rush has a name tag on his shirt that reads “Hello my name is,” followed by a bunch of scribbles that are supposed to read ‘Qian.’ Rush: I’m just looking for- Qian: I know who roo rookin’ fah! Went A Hewo is in Pee Cee Duberoo! Rush shakes his head, and tries to speak slowly, so the Asian man can understand him. Rush: Sir, I usually don’t interact with filthy little rat people, so I’m new to this to, but you need to listen to me, I’m not looking for Rent A- Qian: WENT A HEWO IN PEE CEE DUBEROO!! Rush screams back at Qian, shocking the short Asian man. Rush: I know that Went A…I mean Rent A Hero’s in PCW! I’m not asking about a spandex clad loser! Qian is puzzled by the word spandex, but finally he listens to Rush speaking through two whole sentences. Rush: I’m looking for Hiro Saotome. Qian’s eyes light up, and the small man begins jumping up and down, and dashing around, near hyperventilating. He yells back at Rush with glee. Qian: ROO ROOKIN’ FOR HIWO! Rush nods, trying to keep a smile for this, to be nice about it, utter moron. This is an amazing feat for Rush, who often has to push himself not to push old ladies into traffic, or to mug Girl Scouts. Rush: Yes…I am looking for Hiro…Hiro Saotome. There is perhaps no two words that strike more fear into a PCW fan’s heart than those two words. Perhaps the biggest push for the smallest amount of talent ever in PCW was received by that man, and now Rush Mason, a PCW star in his own right (and in his own mind as well) wants to see him. Either there must be something fishy, or this RP is taking a turn into Clyde-land. Qian: I bwing you to Hiwo quickwy! The jumpy man leads Rush through a door, which he passes through, and then as he walks into the room, looking all around, he throws a salutation. Rush: Hiro, you lazy piece of lo mein, where the fuck are yOHMYGOD! Rush looks up to see none other than former PCW Wrestler, Hiro Saotome, wearing a pink satin gown, sitting on a bed, eating chocolates, his hair in pigtails, watching Enough with Jennifer Lopez. Rush: You’re wearing a dress? Hiro looks up finally from his chocolates and peers up, trying to see who has walked into his small bungalow of a room. Smacking his lips, he questions Rush. Hiro: You are…? Rush stomps his foot angrily, while Qian jumps up and down excitedly, shouting at Hiro, and near pissing his pants with excitement. Qian: It Wush Mason! FROM PEE CEE DUBEROO!! Rush nods, trying not to strangle the annoying hyper Asian man, who is bouncing off the walls like a Mexican jumping bean, or Skitzo after a double Moccacino shot. Hiro: Rush Mason? Who’s that? Almost before Hiro finishes speaking, Qian responds in his high shrieking voice, sending Rush to cover his ears. Qian: FROM PEE CEE DUBEROO! He come to ask roo about Maww Braww! Rush interrupts for a moment, trying to keep Qian from talking.Rush: Yeah, I came to see you, since you strike fear into everyone’s heart with your jobberness, it was gonna be a spoof promo, but is just too sad for words. Hiro blinks once, then hits a button, and the lights go dim as “Hero” by Enrique Inglesias begins to play. Hiro leaps up and grabs Rush, twirling him about, despite Rush’s efforts to squirm away, and shouting over the music. Hiro: I don’t wrestle anymore, but I’ll still be your Hiro, Rush Mason… Rush somehow wrestles out of Hiro’s grasp, and darts out of the room, yelling back as Qian and Hiro dance the night away. However, in one of the strangest promo endings ever, we can hear Enrique Inglesias’ voice as the promo fades out…
