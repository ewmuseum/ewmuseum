Poker at the cabin

25 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-25-03 RP 2 PPV 26th A cabin sits comfortably in the woods. A steady stream of smoke puffs out of the chimney like the smoke out of great-granddaddy’s pipe. Patches of snow is left here and there where the afternoon’s sun couldn’t reach. Inside this cabin, a fire blazes in the fireplace and two best friends sit around a card table enjoying Bud Ice, Wild Turkey and each other’s company. The event of the night is poker. “Considering I gave you my last dollar over that pool game, this is my last chance to get my money back. C’mon, show me what you got.” Duke says this as he lays down his hand of cards on the table. It is three 10’s, a 3 and a 5. He sits back wearing his black Stetson, no shirt, and black wranglers, confident of his winning hand. “It’ll take more than three 10’s to win this hand Malice. With you lack of luck, you’ll loose your pants too.” Teresa lays down three Jacks and a 2. She is in her usual Levi’s and Titan’s sweatshirt. Her sneakers rest under the table with a pair socks stuffed inside. She grabs the bottle of Wild Turkey and takes a good swig while Duke sighs and stands. After he tosses his Wrangler’s aside, he sits and grabs the deck of cards. “I’m dealing this hand Tomas. Ya know I won a game of poker with George Strait.” “You’re shitting me.” Teresa stands and walks to her stereo. She takes the Brooks and Dunn CD that Duke brought over and replaces it Charlie Daniels Band. The system is a Kenwood brand with radio, a record player on top, duo cassette player and 6-disk changer. A set of 15-inch speakers compliments it with one on each side. The song, ‘Long Haired Country Boy’ begins and to Duke’s surprise, Teresa begins to sing. She’s off key by a hair and slightly intoxicated, but so is Duke. “PEOPLE SAY I’M NO GOOD. I’M CRAZY AS A LOON. ‘CAUSE I GET STONED IN THE MORNING. I GET DRUNK IN THE AFTERNOON. JUST LIKE MY OLD BLUE TICK HOUND, I LIKE TO LAY AROUND IN THE SHADE. I AIN’T GOT NO MONEY, BUT I DAMN SURE GOT IT MADE..” Taking this as a possible distraction from the poker game his is obviously loosing, Duke starts the chorus with Teresa. “AND I AIN’T ASKING NO BODY FOR NOTHIN’ IF I CAN’T GET IT ON MY OWN. YOU DON’T LIKE THE WAY I’M LIVING, JUST LEAVE THIS LONG HAIRED COUNTRY BOY ‘LONE.” Unlucky for Duke, Teresa returns to her seat and grabs her cards. She also grabs a bottle of Bud Ice and opens it. In his smiley face boxers, Duke returns to his seat also and they continue several more hands of poker. This time Teresa begins to loose till finally, she is down to her camouflage bra and panties and Duke still in his smiley boxers. The Wild Turkey his half gone and the case of Bud Ice is empty. “Hope you haven’t spent my 20 bucks Tomas. Cause looks like you’ll be needing it.” Duke once again lays down his cards with confidence. He takes a puff of his cigar and blows the smoke in Teresa’s face. Now anyone that knows Teresa knows she isn’t the type to flaunt what she’s got either in front of the camera or behind it, so what Duke is anticipating on seeing from Teresa could be priceless, only if he had a camera. To his dismay however, Teresa lays down the winning hand and while Duke stares at her in disbelief, Teresa easily slips his cigar out of his fingers and takes a puff. She then blows smoke in his face and returns the cigar. She sits back in her chair crossing her arms over her chest. “Well, I’m waiting.” Duke stands slipping his thumbs in the waistline of his boxers. “You asked for it. Just don’t faint.” When the Malice Man is completely nude, Teresa simply stares wide eyed. “Ya sure you didn't steel that thing off a horse? Damn.” This makes Duke blush a bit but he smirks and naturally makes a witty comeback to cover it up. “It was off of a pony but that's a tale for another time. Just let me know if you ever want a ride.” He winks at her and causes her face to turn red. She grabs the bottle of Wild Turkey and leaves the table and the Malice Man with nothing but his Malice stick.
