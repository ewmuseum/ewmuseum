Insomnia Part 2 (RP#2 for SF)

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-20-03 Another starry night, the moon is still in the full stage but tonight it is partially covered by clouds. A thunderclap murmurs as lightning can be seen flashing in the distance. The lights of Los Angeles suddenly become dimmer as rain begins on the city. Adam Stryker is sitting in the same chair from before. The only difference is that tonight he’s got a CD player in his lap and headphones wrapped around the back of his neck. “when you cried i'd wipe away all of your tearswhen you'd scream i'd fight away all of your fearsand i've held your hand through all of these yearsbut you still have all of me” “Such a beautiful night…” His mind races and fills with thoughts and memories. Ranging from the PCW to the DWO and even the AWR. But those are quickly subdued by memories from his life outside the ring. He closes his eyes and puts his arms down on the armrests at his side. “It’s been such a roller coaster that sometimes I can’t believe it. And even other times I can’t believe I’ve survived this long.” “these wounds won't seem to healthis pain is just too realthere's just too much that time cannot erase” “Heh these wounds of mine won’t even heal and I’m grateful for that, lest I forget. And it’s not just the ones that are physical, I’m so fucked…” He turns his arms over so they are facing palms up. Staring at his forearms and then his wrists, his eyes make motions from side to side. He’s seemingly staring at the scars of his attempted suicides. The scars, while glaring obvious to him, are not so noticeable since he has gotten tattoos done over them to try and hide them as best he can. “Christ…Spring Fling is just days away and I can’t stop dwelling on the past. What in the hell is wrong with me?” He continues to stare at his wrists before quickly turning his arms back over and slamming them down into the armrest. Adam frustratingly bounces his head off of the back of the chair. “Stupid…stupid…stupid…I need to take my mind off of things. I can’t help it but this is going to ruin me sooner or later. My past will catch up with me and it’s not going to be a pretty sight.” The song comes to an end. Adam takes the CD player in his hand and turns it off. A tear rolls down his cheek as he pulls the headphones from his ears. “Damn, that song always gets to me.” He drops the CD player to the deck. It hits with a light thud. The rain is getting closer as the moon becomes visible again, it’s clear to see that the storm is headed toward the house. “Just as well…I needed something to drink anyway.” Adam gets up off of the chair and takes a couple of steps toward the door that leads back into the bedroom. The wood creaks under his weight as he stops not wanting to wake Jaslene again. “Well I guess I should go the other way, I don’t want to wake her again. She’s worried enough as it is, I shouldn’t be adding to it tonight. She needs to sleep.” Adam turns to his left and quickly walks to a different sliding door. The wood creaks a bit but not enough to wake Jaslene. He carefully opens the door and steps into the house. The rain begins to fall slowly on the deck as he slides the door shut behind him. Adam turns to watch the rain fall for a moment. “How about that? I’ve got perfect timing.” The rain falls softly against the door in a rhythmic, soothing fashion. Adam looks into the bedroom to make sure that Jaslene is still in bed. He begins to move as he notices a figure still in the bed. Adam mindful of Jaslene walks down the stairs. “Why is all of this shit coming up now? A couple of nights before the biggest event of my career in years and I can’t let this shit go. I can’t go into Spring Fling with a clouded mind.” Adam stops at the base of the stairs and just stares at his front door. The dark wood seems more intimidating at night than during the day. “I can still remember the first day that Jacob came through that door. That son of a bitch interrupted my breakfast, and for what? An AWR interview. I’ll give it to him though, he always had business on his mind. It wasn’t until he met me that he began to change…” Adam stops his thoughts. “Does that mean? Oh my God, had I not changed him…he’d still be alive now. I never thought of it like that.” Adam’s fists ball up at his side. They begin to shake with anger and turn white under the pressure. He stands there, lost in his thought…his rage coming to the front. “That maybe true but then again if I hadn’t changed him, people would still be walking all over him like they were before. I gave him backbone. I made him into a real man and for that I can always be proud of him.” He finally blinks breaking his trance. Adam heads through a small hallway and a couple of rooms before coming out in the kitchen. He flips a switch and flinches as the lights come on. “Christ that is brighter than I thought.” He walks over to a couple of cabinets and opens it to reveal a number of glasses. After looking through the cabinet, he finally pulls one out and closes the door. With the glass in hand Adam steps in front of the refrigerator. He opens the door. “Hm…milk? No. what about some Orange Juice? No. How about Apple Juice? No. Vodka? Hell no, way too late to be drinking.” Adam sighs. “Looks like I’ll settle for some water.” Adam closes the door and fills the glass with crushed ice before filling it with water from the fridge. He takes a long drink from the glass as he crosses the kitchen and steps into the hard wood dinning room. “Ah…water has never tasted better to me. Cool and refreshing, it’s a change from my usual drink. Change is good. Change is coming and this Sunday, change will be made apparent.” Adam finishes the rest of the water and places the empty glass down onto the dinning room table. He walks over to a double door and opens it. The doors open to his backyard, under the deck. The rain is coming down harder, almost in torrents by now. Adam steps out into the night. “This is good, we haven’t had a good bout of rain here in a couple of weeks. Maybe the grass will be green this year.” Adam chuckles to himself as he leans up against the side of the house. A breeze whips through the area around him, it’s drawn in by the open doors. “I don’t know what Smoke has planned for this Sunday but I know one thing, AJ loves that Kangaroo. If Smoke isn’t careful, he could wind up seriously hurt. I’ve got to be smart at Spring Fling. AJ is going to be dying to get his hands on Smoke, that’s going to be key. I don’t really want to get between those two.” Adam rubs his chin and closes his eyes. “Jackson is going to be rusty, I’ll target him. He should be an easy victim. Someone to pummel until Trinidad is done with Smoke that is. This is going to be a dicey situation. Trinidad and I are friends but I can’t let that stand between me and total victory.” Adam slams his fist into the house. “I have to do something drastic this Sunday. I need a win. I need a good show to prove to myself that I deserve to be in the PCW. I don’t know what’s going on with me but I need to do a one eighty and start winning sooner rather than later.” “Of course you deserve to be in the PCW…” a groggy voice calls out from behind him. Adam jumps forward and turns quickly. “Christ Jas! You scared the shit out of me!” Adam exclaims. “What are you doing up anyway?” “Come on Adam,” she says yawning, “You need some sleep. Maybe that’s why you aren’t doing as well as you would like. You aren’t sleeping enough.” “Yeah I’m sure that’s it…” Adam smirks as Jaslene smiles at him. “Don’t worry about it Adam.” Jaslene says wrapping her arms around his waist. “Just go out there Sunday and do your thing, let the chips fall where they may. Remember, it’s about fun. Not winning or losing. When you start to bring that into the mix, that’s when the fun just shoots right out.” “Good point.” Adam says kissing her on the forehead. “Now can we please go back to bed?” Jaslene asks. Adam laughs and nods. Jaslene turns and heads back into the house. Adam stops for a moment and watches as lightning flashes in the sky. “Heh…fun huh? I haven’t had fun in a long time.” Adam mumbles turning back and heading back into the house. He closes the doors behind him. Adam picks up the glass off of the dinning room table. He walks into the kitchen and dumps the ice into the sink placing the glass down as well. He turns the lights off as he heads back toward the bedroom. Song Lyrics: ‘My Immortal’ by Evanescence, from the album Fallen
