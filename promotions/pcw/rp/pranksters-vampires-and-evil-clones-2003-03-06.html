Pranksters, Vampires, and Evil Clones

6 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/6/03 The Minneapolis, Minnesota local library; not the biggest or the best; but it currently is occupied by Chris Storm and his beautiful fiancée Tessa Windsor. In a back table of the library, Chris has different books shattered out, each one opened to a different page. He has a pair of dark sunglasses on despite being inside. Tessa is busy browsing around the library looked at the different books; seeing nothing that strikes her interest, Tessa bounces over to Chris playfully. She looks over his shoulder to see what he is reading. The characters are odd and look to be very old. 'What are you reading?' she asks him as she puts her chin on his shoulder. Chris turns his head and gives her a kiss on the cheek. 'I'm reading about vampires Tessa Bear. This is an ancient book of vampire records. It has everything I need to be the best vampire hunter I can be.' He replies. Chris goes back to studying his book. Tessa looks at the book funny and turns her head. She makes funny faces behind Chris' back. 'You mean you can read that?' she questions. Chris lowers his sunglasses. He sort of tilts his head to the side. 'Well….' He starts off. Chris' head falls. 'No.' he sadly replies. Tessa giggles behind him. 'And what's with the shades inside? It's not that bright.' She tells him. Chris pushes up the sunglasses again. 'Tessa, these are my special vampire hunter shades. Every great vampire hunter needs a cool pair of shades. I read it in one of these books.' He tells her, pointing out a book on the desk. Tessa looks at the book and laughs. Chris gets a curious look on his face. 'What's so funny?' he finally asks. 'Chris, this is a catalog from the Sun Glasses Hut, not a vampire book.' Tessa informs him. “Well…uhhh…here.” Chris says as he places the sunglasses on Tesssa, letting her sport the look. “They look better on you anyways.” “Thanks.” Tessa smirks before running off with Chris’ sunglasses in search of anything to keep her occupied in a place that she would have never visited without her companion Chris. “Hey come back with those, I didn’t say you could keep them!” Chris shouted in Tessa’s direction as he jumped out of his seat and dashed off in pursuit of Tessa and his beloved pair of sunglasses. “Well they are mine now!” Tessa chuckled as she dashed through the library looking for the perfect place to hide somewhere amidst the hundreds of shelves stacked to the ceiling with books containing information on just about any imaginable topic. Chris stops and looks around, trying to figure out where Tessa could have went too. His ears perk up when the light sounds of giggling dance in his ears. A smile comes to his face as Chris puts his hands behind his back and walk slowly in the direction from which the giggling came from. 'Oh where, oh where, could my little Tessa Bear have gone?' Chris asks. Tessa hides behind one of the shelves, trying to cover her mouth so Chris wouldn't hear her uncontrollable giggling. She looks between two books at Chris casually walked between the shelves. Chris turns down the wrong direction. Tessa looks up from her hiding spot trying to figure out where Chris went too. She slowly steps out from her hiding place, trying to locate where Chris has walked off too. She leans against the book shelve and slowly sticks her head out to look around for him, yet Chris is nowhere to be found. A slight frown comes to her face. Almost out of nowhere, Chris comes sneaking up behind Tessa and starts to tickle her. She tries to tell him to stop between fits of laughter. 'Chrissss, no fair! That's cheating and you know it.' Chris merely keeps tickling her as he presses his body against hers. Chris nudges his nose up against the back of her ear, kissing her neck softly. His tickling turns to a hug as his arms wrap around her stomach. He whispers in her ear, 'I love you.' Tessa blushes when she hears the words escape his lips. Sure he said it thousands of times before, but still, each time he said it to her it brought goose bumps to her skin. Tessa manages to turn around in his embrace and ducks through his arms managing to escape his embrace before running off once again to find yet another hiding space. Chris merely sighs and began to walk off again in search of Tessa. “You can’t catch me!” Tessa’s voice teased from across the library. “Oh really? We will see about that!” Chris shouted in response to Tessa’s teasing before speeding up his pace of walking. Ignoring Chris’ words Tessa continued her hike around the library like a child on a playground, never growing tired of fun. She simply kept a smile on her face with no thoughts of Friday, P.W.A., or the brand new match that would stand before her at the end of this week. Instead she kept her mind on one thing, getting away from Chris and winning this little game that she had started. Tessa looks back behind her to see if Chris is gaining on her. She turns a corner to cut between two book shelves and bumps right into someone. The person must be a large size as Tessa falls straight onto her backside. She looks up to see how she bumped into. 'Ohh, I'm sorry. I wasn't watching there I was going…' her voice stops in midair as her eyes lay upon who it is. 'Chris! How did you get there? I thought you were behind me.' She says as she gets up from the floor. Chris has a stone grim expression on his face. 'What the hell?' comes the scream of a familiar voice from behind. Tessa looks behind her confused as to what was going on. 'Chris?' she wondered. Indeed, the voice came from Chris Storm, who was standing in shock as he stared forward. Tessa turned her attention to this other person that looked very similar to Chris. On closer inspection you could see the differences. This person had on a black camouflage t-shirt where as Chris was wearing his simple gray workout shirt. The look-alike also had a scar on the side of his face and a buzz cut of blond hair. This person was almost like a military version of Chris Storm. Chris soon recognizes who this person is. It's the same person he's seen so many times before in his head. Yet this time he was real. That cold voice of the look-alike rings out in the library. 'Chris Storm and his blushing bride to be I see. What brings you two to the library? Come to try and read on how to be a successful wrestler?' The voice sent a chill down Chris' spine. He was immobilized by the sound of this haunting presence's voice. “You have a twin?” Tessa questioned as she looked over at the man that stood before her and then looked back at Chris who still stood behind her just as confused as her. “Twin?” The man in the camouflage questioned. “Don’t make me laugh. I’d never be the brother of such a weakling.” The arrogant laugh of the stranger fell on Chris’ ears as Chris stood there unsure of what to say. Instead Tessa cut in for him doing what she did best, trying her best to be as intimidating as she possibly could standing just several inches taller than five feet. “Then who are you mister I’m better than everyone.” Tessa barked out placing her hand on her hip like a young thirteen year old girl trying to raise a hype with her body language instead of her actions. The look-alike merely laughs in her face. 'Oh are we a tough little girl? Come now, could you really hit someone who looks so much like your beloved? Come on, I dare you. Hit me.' He taps his chin with his index finger, daring her to punch him. Tessa looks back at Chris then at this person who looked so much like him. He was right; she simply couldn't make herself strike someone who looked so much like the man she loved. Tessa steps back away from him slowly, the man laughing in approval. 'What a sad sight you two are. I look forward to this Friday when I may watch P.W.A. teach you a lesson. Those little belt buckles of yours is going back to where they belong. Then you two can go back and play house instead of trying to pretend either of you know how to wrestle.' He says. Chris' blood was starting to boil. 'What happened? What is going on?' all these questions flooding in his mind, nearly drowning him with thought. His body was still useless to him. Chris could only look down at the ground and clinch his teeth and ball up his fist. His body felt like it weighed a ton and a half. His muscles were shaking as the cold words fell onto his ears. Tessa looks back at Chris for support but sees he is still motionless. She looks back at the person who could easily be mistaken for his twin. The man stalks forward slowly, gaining on Tessa as she continued to back up. 'Come on Tessa, do you not find me attractive? Am I not the image of beauty that you lay down each night with? Don't be afraid of me, Tessa, I won't hurt you.' He tells her. His words are like ice as they stab at her emotions. Finally it was enough. Tessa stops in her tracks, causing this person to stop also. She closes her eyes and yells at him, 'Who are you!' The man looks at her with a smirk. He puts his hand on his chin and strokes it the way Chris often strokes his chin when trying to devise a plan. 'Who am I? I am perfection. I am what Chris should be, who Chris should be, but you can simply call me Ein.' As these words drop from the mans lips Tessa turns around and looks back at Chris who seems to be thinking about where he has heard this name before. Tessa looks back at the man who calls himself Ein and looks him up and down trying to figure who this guy was and why his presence was upsetting Chris such a great deal. “Ein you say?” She questions as she stands beside Chris and runs her hands up and down his arm in her best attempts to calm him. “You heard me right. Glad to see at least one of you has brains.” The man teases as he stares over at the two before walking over to them and looking Chris square in the eye almost as if he is trying to hint to Chris that he has met his match. “Well Ein maybe you should leave and go find some other mischief to get yourself into. I think we have seen enough of you for one day.” Tessa advises Ein as she stands behind Chris, not wanting to get any closer to the look alike. Ein taps his chin as he walks around them both in a circle, studying them. 'You know, I do have other business I must take care of. But...' he starts as he stares at Tessa's figure. '...I think I deserve a little something before a go, so I can remember this encounter better.' Tessa and Chris keep their eyes glued on Ein, watching his every move carefully. Ein goes to pinch Tessa's backside but she jumps away from his hand just before he can succeed in his intended actions. Chris stands there watching as still as a rock. By the look on his face you can tell he is becoming angrier by the second. Ein simply laughs as he looks at the motionless Chris. 'Well aren't you going to do anything to defend your woman? Or will you simply let me have my way with her? After all, we both know I am everything you are and better.' Ein mocks Chris. He puts his index finger under Chris' chin and points his face to where the two are looking eye to eye. Tessa stands back and watches on in amazement, it was just like looking into a mirror and watching a fight between the man inside the mirror and the person staring into it. 'So sad, I can not believe she stood by you all this time. You are pathetic, both of you.' Ein tells them. He pulls his index finger away and swings at Chris. Chris pulls his face back just missing the punch. Ein laughs as he walks away from them. 'Almost got you Chris and I was not even trying. You might want to think about yourself and your career if you think you can step in the ring Friday. I don't want P.W.A. too have too easy of a time winning their titles back.' he laughs as he talks to them before walking away and disappearing from Chris and Tessa’s sight. Chris stands there with his fist balled up and his face all red while Tessa stands by him wondering why he is not fighting back. 'Chris, what's the matter? Who is that guy?' she asks him, trying to get Chris to look in her direction. Finally regaining his strength in his legs Chris walks away from her not listening to a word she has to say. He walks back to the desk in the back of the library where the books he was reading earlier sit. He sits down in his seat and begins to flip through the book, not saying a word. Tessa makes her way slowly back towards the desk. She stand behind Chris and looks over his shoulders down at the book wondering think in her mind what Chris is feeling. The air is heavier and darker now as the mood has lost all it's playfulness it once had. Chris' eyes scan the material presented too him, looking for any sort of way to defeat P.W.A. and Nerezza. Tessa stands by his side loyally with the memory of Ein still wrapped fresh in her memory as she looks over her shoulder and into the distance wondering what could possibly happen from here.
