Revenge Saga Part 11: Featuring, Jaden, Shabazz and KIMLAR! The Master of Time!

20 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/6/02 Revenge Saga Part 11: Featuring, Jaden, Shabazz and KIMLAR! The Master of Time! From Behind the Scenes of Tuesday Turmoil… The camera opens up inside John Crichton’s locker room. It has his bags, and some of his clothes scattered around the place, and sat upon the pine bench is none other than Jaden. She has a very angry look upon her face and has a leather wallet in her hand. A closed door can be heard, as Crichton walks into shot, with a white towel wrapped around his waist, using another towel to dry his hair. He withdraws the towel from his hair and spots Jaden sat upon the pine bench. More importantly he notices the angry look carved upon her face and is consequently worried. Crichton: Erm…hey Jaden…what’s…wrong? Jaden rises from the pine bench and forces the leather wallet into his hands. Crichton looks at it carefully, then checks the insides to see if anything is missing. Crichton develops a puzzled look. Crichton: Yeah it’s my wallet! What’s wrong? Crichton throws the wallet back into his bag, then sits on the pine bench, continuing drying his hair. Jaden: So nothing seemed wrong with the wallet? Nothing seemed out of place? Crichton: No. I had $65 dollars in there and there is still $65. Jaden: I’m not talking about the money! Crichton stops drying his hair once more. Crichton: Then what in the hell are you talking about? Jaden’s head lowers and a tear runs down her left cheek. She walks back over to Crichton’s bag and retrieves the wallet. She opens the wallet and holds it up in front of Crichton’s face. The picture of his dead manager, Aeryn Sun, stares back at him. Jaden: Why have you still got a picture of Aeryn Sun in your wallet and not a picture of me? Crichton (sighing): Aeryn Sun was the woman I loved! I can’t just turn off those feelings because of your jealousy! Plus I haven’t taken or in fact ever seen any pictures of you to put in my wallet! The angry expression upon Jaden’s face transforms into a simple smile as she draws closer to him. Crichton: Glad you’ve calmed down a little! Now go get me… Jaden pulls back her arm and WHACK! slaps Crichton across the face, before storming out of the locker room and into the corridors. Crichton: BUGGER! Crichton quickly grabs his pants from the floor and attempts to get dressed in a hurry, as the camera fades to black. Later… The camera opens up in the corridors of the PCW arena, as Jaden makes her way into the scene. She has her head lowered and is still sobbing as she stops and leans against the wall. Seconds later, Jordan Shabazz cockily strolls into the scene, spotting a woman weeping. A concerned look develops on his face as he draws closer. Shabazz: Hey Honey, you okay? Jaden looks up, the tears still running down her cheeks and Shabazz backs off a little. Shabazz: Oh it’s you! Jaden slowly makes her way over to Shabazz, who is visibly uncomfortable, and places her head upon his chest. Shabazz: What kind game is this? Shabazz looks around warily looks around, trying to see if Crichton was lurking in the shadows some where. Shabazz: What the hell, are you playin’ at girl? Jaden remains silent and continues to cry. Shabazz: (Sighing) Why does this shit always happen to me? Shabazz then places his arms around her and begins to sympathetically pat her back. At that moment Crichton rounds the corner and witnesses the whole thing. Crichton angrily storms over to Jaden and grabs her by the arm and pulls her out of Shabazz’ grasp. Shabazz: Hey! There’s no call for that I was only… Crichton: Don’t make me bitchslap you like I did last week Shabazz! Crichton simply turns away, shoving Jaden back along the corridor in the direction of his locker room. The camera fades to black, as Shabazz stands there with a puzzled look upon his face. ************ Crichton stood on the roof of an old, abandoned Warehouse, on 3rd and 42nd street and watched the people below, as they scurried around beneath him oblivious of certain atrocities that plagued their world. He turns away from the view of the city below and walks to the center of the warehouse roof, hands in pockets and head down. His life had been so good until last night on Tuesday Turmoil. Not for the first time in his life he released that women had the ability to make a man feel guilty, for a solely innocent action or occurrence. One simple photograph had put him the shit and upon extension the wrong side of Jaden. That was the main reason for being up on the roof of a warehouse – keeping out of Jaden’s warpath. Essentially she was the one in the wrong, crying in the arms of his enemy Jordan X Shabazz, but he was afraid. Not of Jaden, but of something else. He could not confront her about this because he was afraid, afraid of losing control. He knew full well, the further he went into trying to reap revenge for Aeryn’s death, the further he went from being his true self. The further the dark impulses went to gain control of his consciousness. The dark impulses became abundant in his psyche as a direct resultant of the incident out in the desert. The incident that led to the death of two innocent women the wife and daughter of Dan Lyons. In retrospect, his mission of revenge had slowly began to turn him into the exact kind of person that Aeryn hated. He tried to stop this transformation in various ways, but he refused to give up the mission. After his last confrontation with Fleming he had gained a little revenge, he made Fleming feel what he felt when Aeryn died. Even though Crichton hadn’t been the one personally responsible for her death, he was the one Fleming would blame. In an odd fashion he was kind of responsible. He beat Fleming’s ass and so Fleming was forced to call in the heavy artillery, he was forced to call the Thunderbolts. As soon as Crichton found the body of Ms. Zemoff it was blatantly obvious that she was killed via the heavy bombardment from Jolt and Mach III of the Thunderbolts. He knew Fleming well enough to know that he would care or even acknowledge how Ms. Zemoff was killed, Fleming would just blame him more and simple. Looking back at that fateful day he assumed that the death of Ms. Zemoff and the pain this would cause Fleming, would be enough to quell the impulses that led him to revenge. He turned out to be wrong, very wrong. He still wanted to beat and torture Fleming, but he knew full well that he wouldn’t even get close to Fleming again, without him wanting it. If that were the case it would almost definitely be a trap. Could he take on the entire roster of the Thunderbolts on his own? He doubted it greatly. The only chance he would have was to take out each of the Thunderbolts individually, in a one on one fight. That was the only chance he had of getting his hands on Fleming. The death of Ms. Zemoff would no doubt enrage Fleming and would hopefully make him careless; it would make him come out of his hiding place more and more, allowing Crichton to know his whereabouts. On the other hand the death of Zemoff could affect Fleming the same way that Aeryn’s death affected Crichton. Could make him more determined, more focused, overall making Fleming a much more dangerous enemy. Crichton also questioned Fleming’s intention, after Zemoff’s death. He doubted that Fleming would even want to take him alive anymore, he figured that Fleming would want to kill him plain and simple, despite his orders to take Crichton in alive. This would undoubtedly jeopardise his career in the army, but Fleming wouldn’t care about such trivialities. Jaden. She was the person who could save his soul, the person who could make him quell the dark impulses. She could stop him from becoming a monster, or more accurately more of a monster. Last night on Tuesday Turmoil, his redemption had stumbled a little. He didn’t know whether she would or could forgive him, but Crichton hoped that she could. Getting away from the problem hadn’t helped him decided on an appropriate course of action; it had probably worsened the situation. He had no idea why he always ran away from his problem, he had much less of an idea why he always ended up in the same place. On top of the roof of the long abandoned warehouse, in the middle of Chicago, Illinois. He never flinched when confronting some of his worse fears and some of his most powerful enemies, but when it came to facing Jaden, he metaphorically ran away. Crichton shook his head in both shock and disgust, and absently strolled over to the fire exit steps, his only exit from his refuge. “Turn around, Villain! The voice demands. “How come I attract all of the nutcases in this city?” Crichton muttered to himself. Crichton instantly turns around, to confront the person responsible for the voice. Upon seeing the person at the other side of the warehouse roof, he really didn’t know whether to laugh or cry. Stood there, was a slightly over-weight man to say the least, wearing a totally unflattering white, Lycra costume. ‘Villains’, who didn’t have Crichton’s resolve, would have, undoubtedly, been defeated just by the sight of the Elvis impersonator. “And…er… who might you be?” Crichton questioned, trying his hardest to keep a straight face. The big guy in the white Lycra suit, closes his legs, stands up straight and thrusts one hand into the air. “I AM KIMLAR! THE MASTER OF TIME!” He declares in the stereotypical super hero voice. Crichton couldn’t help himself, and burst into tears of laughter. “Kimlar?…The Master of Time?…Priceless!” He blurted out, now finding it hard to breathe. “I also have the power of mind control over various furry animals.” Kimlar pauses a second. “…Especially squirrels.” Crichton nods in understanding. “I see…So what do you want Kimlar?” He questions, walking a little closer to Kimlar. “I recognised you from the news reports, you’re a wanted felon my friend and I am here to take your ass to the cleaners! My army of squirrels, assemble!” Kimlar states. Crichton stands there and watches, while absolutely nothing happens, no squirrels, no rabbits, no badgers, no nothing. Kimlar keeps in the same stance, for about a minute, before he realises nothing is happening. Crichton sighs a little. “Kimlar, nothing is happening!” Crichton declared bluntly. “ Do not doubt my powers evil one! Or I will go back in time and prevent you from ever being born!” Kimlar states, getting very frustrated indeed. “Yeah Right! Sure you will” Crichton muttered aloud, almost taunting Kimlar. Kimlar closes his eyes and starts to shake. “There! Mission done!” Kimlar declares valiantly. Crichton just stands there staring at Kimlar, with a blank expression upon his face. “Erm…Kimlar…I’m still alive!” Crichton states, sarcastically. Kimlar opens his eyes and realises that Crichton is in fact still standing right in front of him. “It should take a few minutes, hold on!” Kimlar states. Crichton sighs. “Listen buddy, I gotta be somewhere. I’m not going to die today…” Crichton trails off. “I’d like to debate that actually!” The voice declares. Both Crichton and Kimlar stared at the man, as he stepped off the fire stairs, guns pointing at both men. Crichton visibly sighs. “Why does this shit always happen to me!” He curses. To Be Continued…
