Reward!

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-22-02 'Ah,' thought WIT 3, 'there is nothing like a well-deserved payoff.' It had been just as the Warriors In Training were heading to sleep that the Infinite Warrior, Trigger, let them know of his plans for the next day. Miraculously, they were included! This was not a common occurrence, but the WITs were not thinking of its abstractness. Only one word jetted through their heads that night… Reward! Trigger had told the WITs at the beginning of their training that things would not be easy. Hard times would fall, and the Infinite Warrior would be relentless in pushing these students to become the best that they possibly could. He also told them that there would be almost no rewards throughout this rigorous set of lessons. Note the key word, however: almost. The WITs had been promised that if they behaved well, did what they were told, and blindly followed the commands of their leaders, they would be rewarded with a small treat at the end of every month. Last month's dinner at a fast-food restaurant had been a joy to the WITs' eager stomachs, and however small the prize would be the next day, they were determined to make the best of it. Another wonderful surprise to the reward last month was Trigger's openness. He told each WIT to ask him one question, which he would answer in full honesty. This bonus played a key role in the anxiousness of WIT 3 for the night to fall and the day to rise again… WIT 3 had much to ask his leader now. In some odd way, he had always felt connected to the Infinite Warrior, perhaps after Trigger compared himself to the third WIT's own defiance. Whatever the reason, WIT 3 hungered to know more about this mysterious man that was preparing them all to become warriors. Most of all, WIT 3 dared to wonder about Trigger's impending criminal case… would he be convicted? The answer was hazy, and WIT 3 dreaded that the Infinite Warrior might ask him to testify. He was after all, the supposed victim in the case… WIT 3 buried his head into his pillow, not allowing himself to think any more of this stressful subject. There were more important things to think about. Like what he and the others would receive as gratification for their month of acceptance to Trigger's training plans. What would it be…what would it be? This was in the minds of all three Warriors In Training as they went to sleep that night, and you can imagine their surprise when they awoke the next morning to the screeching of tires in front of the abandoned shop. This was quite curious, seeing as Trigger never drove to see them. And the only other one that knew they were there was… 'Minion?' questioned WIT 1 as he sat up. 'What is he doing here?' Within moments, their other trainer, The Insane One Lance Minion was inside their room, clapping his hands together. 'You WITs have worked hard this month, and now it is time to reek the benefits of my kindness! Prepare for your reward, students!' WIT 3 was stunned. Minion was giving them the reward? That was Trigger's job! There was something wrong, and everyone in the room felt it. For some reason, it seemed the usually sadistic Minion was acting…friendly! Unlike Trigger, whose silent ways made him invincible to emotional identification, Minion was open with his personality. He was evil, and everyone that had ever met him knew this fact. There are good people in the world, but there are terrible ones as well, and the latter was a perfect description of the Insane One. The WITs truly did not feel right about this, but as long as Minion was acting nice, they were willing to give him a try. After all, a reward is a reward. 'Umm… Mr. Minion, sir…' WIT 1 started and The Insane One snapped towards him, fiery hatred in his eyes. The WIT sunk back, and it was a moment before Minion realized how fearful his trainee was. A forced smile came across his face as he backed away and signaled for WIT 1 to complete his question. The frightened student remained silent. 'We shall begin now!' exclaimed The Insane One in an excited voice that was obviously faked. Nevertheless, WIT 2 spoke up. 'So who gets to pick our reward this time?' Last month, Trigger had set up an intricate system in which the highest-ranking WIT would choose from three categories, and once he did so the Infinite Warrior would announce the surprise reward. Minion seemed to want things differently. 'I will be picking, WIT. While when you were with Trigger, you may have had only a small prize, with me you shall be delighted with an entire day of festivities!' Did the WITs suspect a hint of spite in The Insane One's voice? Was he planning something they did not want to join in? Minion rolled his hands through his hair, revealing more of his Halloween-like painted face. He seemed eager to leave. A bit reluctantly, the WIT got out of their beds, quickly changed, and followed Minion to his bright red Convertible. Jumping in, The Insane One slammed on the gas pedal, and the car zoomed away into the morning.
