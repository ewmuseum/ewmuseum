Nerezzident Evil (Part 1)

undated

Date: 27th, April 2003 Raccoon CityJuly 24, 1998 Reported by a spokesperson connected with the Raccoon City Police Department was that an investigation helicopter operated by the special task unit S.T.A.R.S (Special Tactics and Rescue Service) Bravo team was missing. The announcement was made after contact was lost with the operation as they investigated reports of missing people who were last seen in the Arclay Mountains, the forest area of Raccoon City. The Raccoon Police Department suspected foul play and believed that the Bravo team had been involved with some trouble and so the order was made for the Alpha team to intervene. Stumbling on a secluded mansion, the property of Umbrella Corporation the events that followed were cloudy at best. The supposed survivors in Chris Redfield and Jill Valentine claim that the pharmaceutical giant Umbrella Corporation were using the mansion as a cover as they worked in the depths developing biological weapons, a story so far-fetched that they even claimed zombies inhabited the manor house. As you can imagine the story was laughed off for having no substance and five years have since passed. Raccoon Town, AlabamaApril 28, 2003 The Umbrella Corporation have since left the abode of Raccoon City and unfazed by the falsification of certain persons they have recently been injecting their wealth into the state of Alabama. Pleased by the good nature of the Umbrella Corporation the Senator of Alabama then with the powers invested in him renamed a small suburbia ‘Raccoon Town’. The stars litter the night sky and an eerie yet bitter cold wind circulates between the buildings that make up Raccoon Town. The streetlights radiate either side of the centre road and parked cars sit cluttered against the sidewalk, there appear no signs of life and give the impression of a ghost town. The cold wind ushers along the obstacles and tumbleweed scrapes along the concrete landscape when suddenly a ray of light crawls along the face of the buildings like a pair of witches fingers. The source of this light ventures forward unveiling a hearse coated in metallic purple and it continues to creep up the cobbled road. The vehicle in question presses on and finally comes to a halt in front of the mammoth building of a Victorian Manor House that has been converted as a hotel for travellers. A neon sign flickers with a welcoming banner and the engine that churns out clouds of smoke finally settled as the passenger and driver side doors sprung ajar. “This looks like a place we can lie low for awhile?” murmurs the distinctive voice of Primetime Championship Wrestling’s resident Jester as he steps out of the vehicle. Tailored in his pea green business suit, the Jester smugly smiles and tilts his head over to his passenger. Stepping from the vessel with his heavy boots crashing down onto the sidewalk, the leather trench coat that follows could belong to but one man, Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. Swiftly emerging like the Angel of Death the Vampire appears pale complexioned and looking slightly hungered for his isolating ordeal had lasted seven days and the perpetrators in ‘Entertainment Extreme’ would now suffer for each. Entombed in a metallic casket can have major psychological effects on a person and these appeared apparent as the Vampire toyed menacingly with his talons, muttering under his breath like a madman and forever suspiciously looking into the darkness with paranoia. “Cold… So cold… Wet… So wet…” whispered the Living Nightmare as he frantically peered about, his eyes squinting as he strained to see into the darkness. The Jester took lead with the Vampire languishing a few feet behind as the Conniving Collective entered into the rugged garden of the hotel, creaking open the metal gate that fortifies the entrance the sound of dogs filtered worryingly over the silence. Vicious and bloodthirsty the dogs barked aggressively towards one another yet their presence was in the distance, that fact alone slightly heartening thus the duo pressed on. The building was Victorian style with slight discoloration to the brickwork and large beautiful stain-glassed windows that would invite the daylight, however this was nighttime and the only light penetrating at this hour was that of the crescent moon. The current Tag Team Champions strutted up to the large oak door uneasy as they looked around, the Jester cringing his face towards the goblin statuettes that furnish the upper walls, his partner in crime stood reeling from his watery grave. “Why me? So, so cold…” the Angel of Fear continued to mutter. - Bang – The Emerald Menaces clenched fist thumping against the wooden door echoed from within and the thunderous sound either ignored or unheard by the occupiers as the duo continued to stand in the midst of the elements. Standing foolishly in the open the pair waited with patience as the heavens opened above and suddenly the rain came crashing down with the clouds clashing to form thunder and lightening. The blanket of light bathing the night sky lit up the features of the hotel and noticeably the assortment of demons and ghouls that eerily felt as though they had come to life. “This feels like a new chapter in the Resident Evil series!” whimpered the once jovial Jester whose face blanketed with white from the nights proceedings. Suddenly the door sprung open and the candlelight from within was visible to see and so was the beautiful decoration as everything on show was well crafted in stained ash. The dynamic duo moved in from the outside, never once questioning ‘How the door came to open?’ and standing in a large reception area they looked about for signs of life. The Jester cockily strutted over to the reception desk and with an open palm he rattled the brass bell to no avail however as both members of Pranks With Attitude stood dumbfounded, the room keys were missing except for a sole key baring the mark of a shield. Grasping said key, the Lord of Laughter spoke out, “Well I guess we take this and explain to the proprietor in the morning!” The entire scene gave a creepy vibe and even the sternest upper-lipped Britain would succumb to fear in such circumstances. The Vampire sat silently on the winding staircase clutching at his netted hair, still muttering under his breath the words a mixture of gibberish sentences. The saner of the two in Gaz Green scoped about with curiosity as he tried desperately to answer some of the niggling questions surfacing in his mind. Walking over to a sideboard he squinted his eyes in amazement at the glass-coated container with the warning label, “In case of Emergency break glass!” Worrying more so was the content, expecting to find a fire extinguisher or possibly an alarm the Jester grinned at the collection of items, be it a banana, grenade, a plastic water pistol and an oddly looking knife. Cringing his face in thought the Jester waltzed over to his fallen comrade who remained rooted to the bottom stair muttering to him self. “Maybe we should take a look around squire?” questioned the inquisitive comic ushering his partner back to his vertical base and the duo ventured into the belly of the beast. Sliding open two large wooden doors the brass rail used as the hinge mechanism creaked from poor maintenance. Walking into a mammoth room you could see the second floor safety railings above and taking centre stage at ground level was the BIGGEST dining table you could lay your eyes on. Polished and glistening from the candlelight the Jester stood in awe, his mouth ajar in amazement as he admired he splendour before him. Suddenly the silence quenched with the sound of a faint voice coming from the other end of the room. “What was that?” choked the PCW comedian a little startled. There appeared no signs of life but unless the voice was a trick of the imagination then somebody was in the room with them. “Death… Blood… Misery…” murmured the behemoth as his canines bathed in saliva, his trauma arguably subsiding. Clutching a brass candelabra in his right hand the Jester pursued the source of the sound and as he peered over to a fire place with a shield etched above it he could clearly see a rugged fellow with a bristled face kneeling in a knighthood position looking as though he were inspecting the floor. Nerezza trailing slightly behind falls almost into a trance like state as they come within feet of the stranger who stands clad in a camouflage uniform. “What, what is this?” says the gentleman as he dampens two fingers into the pool of red solution that smears the tiled floor. “How we looking squire? Could you tell me if you’re the proprietor of this humble abode? My colleague and me would like a room for the night…” asks the green tailored Jester, his comments falling on deafs ear. The man pays the superstars no mind and re-enacts his actions once more, like a ghost acting out a sequence, yet unlike his un-dead counterpart he appears to have density, density without a brain mind you but density all the same. “What, what is this?” the fellow murmurs again. The Jester prodding the gentleman with his finger gets no response, only for the event to unfold for a third time. “What, what is this?” he continues. The Vampires eyes widened with excitement and his tongue washes the underside of his mouth as his comedic partner helps in the investigation. Dabbing two fingers into the gooey substance it becomes obvious to what the fluid is and the Tag Champion speaks up, “It’s blood, hello, anyone in there?” bellows the prankster into the ear of his recipient who doesn’t even flinch from the cry. “Oh, stupid, it’s blood, no big deal…” the trickster concludes as he admits defeat. Suddenly the animalistic tendencies of the Vampire unleash and the smell of blood appears too enticing as he lunges forward and attacks the fellow who again doesn’t flinch, not even when the Living Nightmares fangs imbed into his luscious throat. A crack and the neck breaks flimsily easy and the Vampire continues to feed as the Jester watches on with horror, blood seeps onto the floor and the scene looks like a blood bath when to add salt to the wounds the grandfather clock chimes almost startling the spectator Jester into his own casket. “What the fu…” the prankster mutters, holding his chest cavity as he tries to recompose his breathing. Finished the blood stained face of Nerezza gazes up at the once jovial Jester with the look of hunger tainted in his eyes and curling his talons he springs forward like a wild animal… - Then silence -
