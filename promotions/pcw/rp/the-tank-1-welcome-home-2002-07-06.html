The Tank 1 - Welcome Home

6 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 6 Day One 'You ain't at home any more ladies!' says the grinning young Correctional Officer as he taps his baton on the palm of his left hand. Intimidation is always of the utmost importance in the Tank. If you don't intimidate these scumbags, thinks the young Correctional Officer, they'll eat you alive. 'Follow me!' The bars quickly, and noisily, slide across, opening the holding cell to the rest of the prison. A line of new prisoners, and a few returning ones - eleven in all - follow the young Officer out of the holding cell and into what will become their new home for whatever length of time their respective judges sentenced them to. Graham Johnson, and handsome and smallish young white man in his late twenties, is at the head of the line wearing a cocky grin on his face. This is his third, and final, trip to the Tank. In 1994, he was twenty years old and sentenced to a year for stealing a car and leading the LAPD on a chase that ultimately ended when he crashed into an old lady's back lawn. In 1998, he was sentenced to three more years for raping a woman who he claimed was a prostitute. Now, in 2002, he will begin serving a sentence that will see him one day die in the Tank. Three months ago, he thought it would be fun to find the 'prostitute' who had him charged with rape - he butchered her on a busy street corner. Next in line is Perry Gillman - also white. He walks slowly, his bottom lip trembling from nervousness. He's not the type of man to survive a place like this and he knows it. Even in his twenties and thirties, no one would ever think of him as a threatening type of individual. Now, at the age of fifty-three, he enters the Tank with stories of the animals that it encases running rampant through his head. He looks at the cocky young Johnson in front of him, and then back to the lion-like monster behind him - these are the type of men who can survive life in the Tank, he thinks. He wonders if he'll live to see the end of his own eight month sentence, which he recieved after being caught molesting the eight year old girl living in the apartment across the hall. Behind Graham Johnson yet another white man - lion-like Jason Snow, standing tall despite being chained at both wrists and ankles. He carries a bag of permitted personal belongings - two changes of clothes along with a pillow and a toothbrush that were issued to him after being marched into what will surely be a haven of hell. Snow was convicted of assault in 1998 and served six months in a Canadian prison. That prison was famous for tough love and tougher criminals, but this is the Tank. Everyone has heard of the Tank. Unlike any of the other ten inmates being marched into hell on this Saturday morning, Jason Snow is an extremely wealthy man. At just twenty-four years of age, he's already considered one of the greatest wrestlers of all time. He's been wrestling for years... he's defeated all of the biggest and the baddest men in the industry... he's won the PCW Heavyweight Championship of the World three times... but now, as he looks around his surroundings - the concrete, the bars, the confinement - a trace of fear shows up in his hard blue eyes. It's not until now that Jason Snow realizes these walls truly will confine him for the next eleven months of his life, minus the two days per week that he is permitted to appear on PCW Television - even those days will be made up later. 'Inmate 837462!' bellows the Correctional Officer as he stops the line's march in front of a barred gate. Jason Snow looks down at his chest and finds the number '837462' sewn onto his blue prison uniform. As he steps forward, the C.O grins, 'Unit B. Welcome home.' Snow turns toward the gate as it suddenly slides open. A moment later, a pair of new Correctional Officer's - both middle aged and full of battle scars - step out, grab Snow by either arm, and escort him into Unit B... A home of bars, steel, and horror... Tankerton, 'I run things around here - everything. For the past twenty-five years, the Tank has been my baby - been my family.' The Warden is a slender man - black - in his mid-fifties. He stands in his office, leaning back on his desk wearing a sharp black suit. Completely bald, and with his share of battle scars visibly lining his face, it's not hard to tell that the Warden isn't a man to hide behind his desk. 'I was a little surprised when Charles Jefferson, a good friend of mine, told me that I would be housing Jason Snow,' he chuckles, 'Jason Snow? Jason Snow is a household name. Everyone who knows what wrestling IS knows Jason Snow and what he's meant to the business. And now, the very same man is going to be sitting in a cell next to some of the baddest men in the country.' 'Snow is in here for assault,' sighs the Warden, 'his third conviction and second prison sentence. The first time he was convicted, it was for driving a truck into a man's living room and then giving him the beating of a lifetime. His second, which thankfully was much more low key, was for kicking a man at a nightclub back in 2000. Well, this one is kind of in the middle ground. Seems Snow got a tad upset after he got word that his sister-in-law's boyfriend broke things off with her. They tell me Snow has certain hang ups about sex and things of that nature, and genuinely thought the ex-boyfriend deserved to be beat into unconciousness for deflowering his sister-in-law and then taking off. To be honest, I don't know if he's nuts or just old fashioned.' The Warden turns around and straightens a few photo's that clutter his desk while speaking, 'Charlie told me not to show the famous inmate any favoritism. Of course, I would never dream of doing that anyway. However, I can't say I wasn't surprised when he told me to make an example of him. Well, I'm not one to argue with a man like Charlie, so I placed Snow in Unit B - the most rat infested place in the Tank... maybe the country. But these rats, aren't exactly the kind the local exterminator can take care of...' 'You should see Miss Vegas,' says the old man, nodding at the new inmate, 'She works wonders with people, you know. I hear she can make it so you don't get so mad.' 'Miss Vegas?' Snow perks an eyebrow to the old inmate. 'Yep,' the old Latino, Miguel Sanchez - Miggy for short - nods, 'You can get into one of her classes - she runs them all day, but I think they might all be full.' 'I'll look into it,' says Snow quietly without any real intentions of doing so. 'Well anyway,' Miggy sighs, 'recreation is kept down here on the floor while the cells are upstairs. As you can see, the TV's over in the corner there, and there's a pool table in behind you there, but those damn Nazi's have pretty much taken that over... you're not ... one of them, are you?' Miggy asks cautiously, raising an eyebrow. 'Nazi?' Snow repeats with surprise, 'No...' Miggy nods and goes back to giving Snow the grand tour of Unit B. It's not large, but it's much bigger than Snow had anticipated. He had assumed prison would consist of a five by nine cell and a term to serve in it. Even though he realizes life in Unit B will be harsh, the freedom it offers does exceed his expectations. 'What cell are you in?' asks Miggy, turning to Snow. The young lion-like inmate points to a cell off to his right. Miggy's voice cracks, 'You're in with Harding?' 'Harding?' Snow perks an eyebrow. 'You mean you haven't met your cellmate yet?' Miggy asks. Snow responds by shaking his head. 'Well,' Miggy shrugs, 'I suppose it's not so bad - not for a man like you anyway.' Snow wonders what Miggy means by those words, but his attention is soon diverted when the smallish old inmate changes the subject, 'Would you like to see the gym? It's three o'clock, so we still have plenty of time to go and take a look at it.' Snow nods. In a strange why, he likes Miguel, the talkative old Latino. He's glad Miguel approached him when he first walked in, escorted by the two middle-aged guards. Miguel will serve nicely as a friend in this place, he thinks - and besides, he looks safe. Meanwhile... 'Who the hell is that?' Harvey Blake leans back on the old and weathered pool table, staring across the Unit at the young lion, Jason Snow, who's being led out of the Unit and toward the gym by Miggy. 'Who?' asks a much younger man beside him whose only visible hair is a thick black goatee. The young man, Jared Thompson, catches a glimpse of Snow a moment before he steps out of the Unit. 'Oh,' he nods, 'Jason Snow - The Tank's resident celebrity.' 'Celebrity?' Harvey Blake asks, looking over to his young companion. 'Some hot shot wrestler,' says Jared, 'From PCW.' 'Why wasn't I informed of this?' asks Blake, glaring at his younger companion through his only eye. 'I thought every one knew, Harv,' says Jared, shrugging his shoulders with a voice that can't hide a trace of fear. 'I want to talk to him,' Harvey says to his companion, staring at the door where Snow has recently exited through his single eye - the other is covered by a patch with a bright red swastika sewn into the center of it. Harvey Blake is a hero to half the men in the Tank. To the other half - there's no bigger fucking menace confined in these walls. Harvey Blake came to the Tank in 1984 after he through his wife through a plate glass window. The police caught up with Blake right before he lit his wife's dress on fire - his three children, ages two to seven, were crying inside the house.' Miller sighs and rubs his aging bald head, 'Times were different back in those days. Almost eighty percent of the Tank was black. Partially because white criminals were being sent to prisons without such a reputation, and partially because most white inmates that came to the Tank didn't last a month. It was slavery all over again, only this time, it was the brothers cracking the whip.' The Warden stands up and takes a drag from his cigarette before continuing. 'Harvey Blake changed all that. Rumour has it, he was a active White Supremacy bastard long before he came to the Tank. Seeing what was going on in here pissed him off. He was the first white man in years to have the balls to walk around out of his cell with his shirt off - exposing all those fucking tattoos.' The Warden seems to shudder at the thought of Harvey Blake's tattoos. 'He started getting the whites organized. When they came into the Tank, he knew about it, and he recruited them. Men who didn't have a prejuidice bone in their body, when they came in to the Tank, were getting swastikas tattoo'ed all over their asses. It was survival. Show loyalty to Harvey Blake and be protected - that's the way things worked.' The Warden puts out his cigarette, 'Blake's done a lot of good and bad things for this prison. He made it a place where whites didn't have to be afraid to leave their cells. But now...' the Warden hesitates, 'now he's simply a fucking monster.' Miggy escorts Snow out of the gym and back into the main room of Unit B, closing the heavy iron door behind them. Miggy isn't an attractive man - short, old, bald, and with teeth that seem closer to yellow than white. 'Well, that's it.' 'Damn,' Snow sighs, looking at his surroundings, 'if only the boys back at PCW could see this.' 'Oh they will,' says Miggy with a serious expression on his face. Snow looks confused, 'What are you talking about?' 'Everyone sees everything in the Tank,' says Miggy, 'Everything. You can't eat, sleep, shit, piss, or whack off without someone seeing ... well, that's the way Lenny used to put it.' 'Lenny?' 'Nevermind,' Miggy says with a wave of his hand, 'The fact is, there are cameras all over this place - at least, we think there are. Some say the Warden is making a documentary to make himself rich when he retires.' 'Isn't that illegal?' Snow asks, raising an eyebrow. Miggy shrugs. Later 'So you gonna be living with me now, huh?' asks Darryl Jarvis, a tall, slender black man a few years older than Snow, 'We gonna be roomies?' 'Guess so,' says Snow, climbing up onto the top bunk, which is the one he noticed earlier was unoccupied. Darryl Jarvis stands shirtless by the tiny sink of the cell, looking into the mirror. A moment later, he approaches and puts his elbows on Snow's bunk, staring intimidatingly at his new cellmate. 'This gonna be your first time sleeping in the same room with a criminal?' Snow chuckles, 'Guess so,' he repeats. 'Ah,' says Jarvis with a sly grin, 'then I suppose you don't know... once you go to sleep, they ain't nothing stopping me from getting up and cutting your fucking throat.' Snow, lying on his back, turns his head to his cellmate who's smiling devilishly back. 'Or maybe,' Jarvis continues, 'Maybe I don't want you dead. Maybe I just wanna come on up there and fuck that sweet ass of yours. Maybe I do that, huh? Prag?' Prag? Snow's sure he's heard the word before but can't quite recall its meaning. 'I don't think you'd want to do that,' says the young lion, his eyes turning hard - Snow is an expert in the art of intimidation. 'No?' Jarvis says, his eyes lighting up. He slumps down and slides into his own lower bunk, 'Well, if I did, they wouldn't be a fucking thing you could do about it, you know. I mean, you may look like a mean and nasty motherfucker, but that don't mean shit up in here. Y'see Prag, I'm up in this bitch for the rest of my damn days. You're doin' - what? - six months? A year? If you don't behave your ass, you'll end up spending more time here than you want to. Me? It don't fucking matter what I do.' With that, Jarvis rolls over and closes his eyes, still smiling. In the bunk above, Snow is not smiling. His eyes are pasted wide open. A Correctional Officer bellows, 'LIGHTS OUT!'
