The Weekend - Part 1 - Mistified

2 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 2 [There comes a time in a dudes life when he has to do some pretty bogus things. At the time they may not seem gnarly in the eyes of the beholder, but there is always meaning behind actions. Take for instance the latest actions of the Cowabunga Connection. They were heinously duped by Redemption Song with the whole Tubular Tykes incident a few weeks back on Turmoil. It was a single most bogus act anyone could do to our two excellent heroes. In the end, Tommy ended up with a most brutal concussion to the head from the dudes of Redemption. What's a dude to do when he's hurt physically and emotionally? He has to get some totally gnarly revenge on the dudes who did this to him. That's what Tommy set out to do, and that came in the form of taking Redemption Song's valet, Misty, from them. A totally bogus act to steal a babe from another dude, but it had to be done in the name of bodaciousness. Now she is a personal valet to the Tubular One as Harry has some great footage for his E-Bay chronicles] Misty: Your going to win at Red October. ['Totally Tubular' Tommy and 'Heavy Metal' Harry sit next to each other at TGI Friday's looking across at the very beautiful Misty. There is something very bodacious about this woman. It's a combination of her petite and classic features and the way that she can throw around authority. Most guys dig a babe who takes charge every once in a while. Tommy certainly knows all about that now] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Babe, this is something that we know already. 'Heavy Metal' Harry: Yeah, man. [It's a crowded evening at TGI Friday's as people pass by the booth looking in at the two PCW superstars. Tommy keeps giving thumbs up and a hang ten sign to various people. Harry just shrugs at them because that's pretty much all he's capable of in his stoned state. Tommy is feeling bodacious because of GTT and the upcoming Pay Per View. Harry is just happy that he's making money on E-Bay by selling Tommy's sexual encounters. It's a far out way to make money] Misty: Yes, but you need to understand that your going to win because I'm in your corner now. Redemption Song WAS winning until I did left them. Now they are just a couple of losers who will fade out just like all the others. Your my boys. I love you. You love me. We're going to take the tag titles at Red October and there are no if's, and's, or but's about it. [The Cowabunga's just nod their heads, really not having to add anything to everything that they already know, except for that whole loving part. What in the name of bogusness is up with that? She's talking about love when they just met and had a *ahem* totally gnarly weekend together. In speaking of the weekend...Tommy's mind begins to remember exactly what happened. The big steal on Fusion, and then the lovely 72 hours that they spent together. Bodacious...simply bodacious!] [The hotel room was cozy, decked out in blue and yellow just like the Tubular One wanted it. Blue bedspread with yellow curtains on the bed to give it that exotic feel in the bedroom. The view was totally gnarly, overlooking the city skyline of New York City. Misty said that she loved New York and wanted to party hard, just like the Andrew WK song. Tommy was into that, and needed to let some, much needed, steam off after all the bogus stuff that has been happening to him. The setting was there, the music of Aerosmith was filling the room, and candles come in handy for an occassion like this] Misty: This room is great, Tommy. You sure know how to treat a woman. TTT: Babe, I've had practice. Misty: Yes, you have. [The luggage is light with the couple...they won't really be needing much clothes for a trip like this. Tommy can't wait to unwind with this bodacious babe, let out some of that revenge from what Surge did] Misty: Tommy, what's the agenda before your big Pay Per View encounter with my Ex and his stupid partner? [Tommy smiles, wrapping his arms around his new valet] TTT: Well, I know that this weekend is going to be totally bodacious in a far out kind of way...if you know what I mean. [Misty forces a quick smile] Misty: I know exactly what you mean, but do you have any big matches beforehand? [Tommy thinks and shakes his head...not really thinking about the ring at this moment and time. He just wants to pull back the totally gnarly blue sheets and drop the yellow curtains] TTT: Nothing comes to mind, babe. [Misty sighs and shakes her head] Misty: Tommy, you go against Jim Surge the Fusion right before the Pay Per View. How could you forget such an important match like that? [Tommy shrugs] TTT: I don't know, babe. I'm trying not to think about that totally bogus stuff right now. Misty: Well, you should start thinking about it. You have a chance to do some damage to Surge on Friday, soften him up for the big match...to make it easier for you. Then you can concentrate on the large agenda at the end...the tag team titles against PWA or Playaz Inc. [Tommy shrugs once again, not really enjoying where this thing is going] TTT: You make a most excellent point, and that's a while away yet. I'll make sure that I prepare for it, and let Harry in on the totally gnarly plan. [Misty begins spraying some perfume on, and Tommy suddenly gets excited] TTT: Babe, you don't have to get too dolled up for this. We're going to be getting pretty sweaty. [Misty laughs] Misty: Well, you'll be getting sweaty, I need to run some errands before I hit the club scene. [Tommy looks absolutely confused] TTT: I'm not understanding you, babe. Misty: I knew you wouldn't, that's all right. I'm going out to the club to have some fun, enjoy New York City. You need to go hit that gym downstairs and get in better shape. Your going to need it against my former team. I know them like the back of my hand and I can give you great insight on how to beat them. First step is to go downstairs and workout as much as possible. [Misty goes to the door and looks back at Tommy with a sexy smile] Misty: Don't wait up, Tommy. [Totally bogus....]
