The luck with Chuck

10 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-10-02 RP 1 Fusion 13th The time is 8pm. Teresa sits at an empty table in a steakhouse called Outback. She sips on a margarita and looks down at her watch once more. Chuck is an hour late and she’s getting impatient, thus the reason for the margarita. She ordered a large one and boy is that baby huge. If she finishes it and is able to walk to the bar without stumbling, her whole meal is free. Teresa stares to the empty chair sitting in front of her and she wonders how she got herself in such messes. Chuck is an all right guy to have a few drinks with or just hang out. The second he even attempts to make a move, she’ll put him into his place quick and he knows it. After hearing about her former temper problem and what she done to both Jack and Scott not to mention what she can do in the ring, one would wonder where the poor guy got the balls to stand up to her. Fifteen minutes and a quarter of the margarita pass and the average sized guy known as Chuck occupies the empty chair. He rests his elbows on the table and his chin in his hands. He watches Teresa stare off into space as if in a trance. What on earth are you thinking about Tomas? Teresa blinks and notices Chuck watching her with a goofy smile on his face. Obviously not you. You’re late as usual. As soon as I finish this drink, I’m gone. Chuck looks disappointed and orders a beer from the waitress. He makes sure he watches her small rear as she walks away. Teresa clears her throat and kicks his leg from under the table. Chuck groans. You can’t leave Tomas. We had a deal remember? You’re MY date for the night. Teresa rolls her eyes and takes a long drink. Tequila does either of two things, makes you in a very good mood or makes you want to fight. The more Teresa sits here watching Chuck acting like an asshole, the more she wants to knock him right out of that chair. She begins to think of Adrenaline. Her one night back only to find some strange RUSH guy sharing HER locker room and later finding herself almost Halmarked by Kannon. That whole night happened so fast, she mostly remembers standing on the entrance ramp in the center of Duke and Abner after an unofficial victory over Front Office. At that moment, she could sense the beginning of something huge in her career. Teresa, Teresa, are you alive over there? Man, you not acting like yourself. You sure you’re ok? Chuck is reaching over the table shaking her arm. Teresa seems to wake up from yet another trance and sighs deeply. Got a lot on my mind Chuck. I know about our bet. Next thing Teresa knows, Chuck moves his hand up her arm and into her own hands. It’s ok Tomas. Don’t think about work right now and I’ll make sure you’ll have a good time. His words are blown off like a feather. Teresa jerks her hand away from his. She sits back in her chair and begins to laugh. Stop while you’re ahead little man. Those smooth moves may work on your women, but that shit doesn’t fly with me and you sure in the hell not getting any from me. Chuck begins to laugh nervously while adjusting in his seat. Their plates of food are set on the table. Both are T-bone steak and a large baked potato. By now half of Teresa’s margarita is gone. The waitresses begin to make bets on whether she will finish it and her food or not. Come on Tomas, just one night. You know as well I that we have that certain chemistry. We wouldn’t be able to get enough of each other. The only reaction Chuck got was an airborne potato hitting him right between the eyes. Teresa cuts into her steak satisfied and potatoless as if nothing happened. Angered, Chuck rises from his seat. Realizing the woman sitting in front of him isn’t the same tomboy she secretly had a crush on in highschool. She grew up to be an egotistical bitch with an attitude. He walks to her side and pokes her on the shoulder. Before Teresa can turn her head, Chuck grabs the neck of her shirt. Drunken rage in his eyes. It is obvious by the smell of his breath; he has had a few before he arrived. You Bitch. Give me one good reason why I shouldn’t knock you flat the f—out. With a straight face, Teresa reaches for her margarita and with one huge gulp, she finishes it. In the back, the waitresses exchange money to those who won the bets that she couldn’t finish it. ‘Cause you won’t. Teresa throws a hard punch to Chuck’s gut and he slumps over releasing her shirt. He hardly works out so any simple punch could put him down. When he hunches forward, Teresa wraps her arm around the guy’s neck and drops him to the floor. Upon impact, his is out like a light. Teresa stands over his limp body and shakes her head. Sorry Bud, A girl has to do what a girl has to do. The whole staff of Outback begins to applaud as Teresa strolls out of the steakhouse
