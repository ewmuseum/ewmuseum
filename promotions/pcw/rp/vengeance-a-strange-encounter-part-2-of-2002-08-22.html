Vengeance: A Strange Encounter....Part 2 of ?

22 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-22-2002 When we last left our hero, Venneman Pendragon had infiltrated a Goblin temple in the deepest regions of the African jungle. Having already dispatched two of the filthy creatures, he delved even deeper into the temple. Facing daunting odds, he needed a plan if he was to gain the Goblin Jewel of the Night… “After the spectacle with Fryko and his buddy, I needed to find some sort of diversion to get the goblins’ attention away from the hideous statue. That, or I needed a damned good disguise…” Pendragon flies down the corridor, axe and sword in hand, until he comes to a left turn and a door to his right. He sidles up to the door and fins it unlocked, so he goes in. Looks to be some kind of Goblin bed chamber. There is a closet and a mirror, as well as a very uncomfortable looking bed. Upon the bed lies what would appear to be a Goblin babe; if you can call the repulsive creature a “babe”…. Venneman: My, my, my….I bet Syko would LOVE to meet you, lass… Startled, the Goblin sits up, awake, on the bed. She looks around excitedly and then twiddles her thumbs nervously… Goblin babe: My…And who might you be? Venneman: I am the Lord King Venneman Pendragon. I have come to fulfill a prophecy and wrest the Jewel of the Night from this goblin stronghold… Goblin Babe: But you uttered the revered name, yes? Syko is a hero to our people. The only one to gain notoriety, yes? Are you his friend? Pendragon stares wide eyed at the Goblin. He shakes his head and chuckles. His laughter builds until he is bellowing and the Goblin looks even more nervous. Eventually he calms and goes o as if nothing out of the ordinary happened… Venneman: If friends steal gold from their friends then aye, ye might say that we are friends. I however, look upon him as a treacherous worm who must be eradicated. If ye would wish to join his fate, deny me aid. If ye would live, do as I say. Goblin Babe: It would seem you leave little choice, yes? Do what you would, outlander. You may take this body, but it will not be me…It will not…Be…ME!!! Pendragon looks aghast at the Goblin. Immediately he leans to his side as he tries to hold back his dinner. He convulses but keeps it down. He quickly composes himself and addresses the Goblin once more… Venneman: I come here not to rape and pillage. I come here for the Jewel of the Night. That is all. Goblin Babe: So you would not have me? Nothing fulfills better than a real man does…. Venneman: Do not get me wrong, lass…I am sure that you have the goblins lining up, but I…I am too focused on my goals of glory to have myself entangled in an affair…. The Goblin blushes at Venneman’s near compliment, and stands up. Half as tall as the Knight, she looks up at him, beaming… Goblin Babe: I shall help you good sir Knight! You may call me Yiko… Venneman: Now…I need a suitable way to get into the main hall unnoticed. I need a… He is cut off by a pounding on the door. He freezes and stands with weapons at the ready. Yiko Pushes him to the bed and instructs him to sit. She then goes into the closet and comes back with some colorful cloths and what appears to be a hat…. Yiko: JUST A MINUTE! Now, Sir Knight; if you would stay hidden, you may keep the element of surprise… She then wraps some of the cloth around him and places the hat upon his head. Wrapped in the cloth and the hat, he still looks manly, but Yiko smiles… Yiko: You may pass as one of us yet. Pendragon snarls as Yiko goes to open the door. In the hallway stand fifteen members of the Goblin Temple Guard. Each carries a pike and the leader pushes his way into the room… Leader: Lady Yiko. I am sorry for the intrusion…Yes…but have you seen anything out of the ordinary? Yes, suspicious? Yiko: Nope. Nothing at all on this night sir. Leader: And who is that upon your bed? Yes, I wish to see! Yiko: Umm….That is my cousin, the Lady Nyko… The Leader nods and walks over to the bed. He looks at Venneman and smiles, a tad too appreciatively…. Leader: Yes…Cousin indeed. What a fine beard you have there, Nyko. If there were more time, I might like to gnaw upon it, yes? Though your skin is a tad fair, you are a site for Goblin eyes… Pendragon jumps a little, and blurts out, in his best falsetto voice… Venneman: Why captain…So nice of you to notice. I do take pride in my appearance, yes. The Goblin stands there a moment longer, mesmerized. At last, he shakes out of his revelry as he remembers the job he has to do. Leader: Though I would love to stay here all day, I have serious business. We may have an intruder. A nasty human type, yes. We cannot be too careful of their dastardly kind in these times, no? Venneman: Indeed. Good luck in your search, Captain… Venneman gives a slight wink and the Leader moves into the hallway with a grin and moves his troops on down the hall. Yiko starts to giggle and Venneman glares at her… Yiko: You do make quite a lovely Goblin, Sir Pendragon. Venneman: Enough of that…. Yiko: truly stunning…. Pendragon blushes but says nothing. Damnit, he is a warrior! How could she say these things and make Goblin eyes at him? No matter. He has a quest to fulfill, and she was going to help him… Venneman: No Yiko…If you helped me thus far, perhaps you would help a Knight some more. I need to come up with a diversion. I cannot just walk right into the main hall here in the temple. I have a plan, but I do need your help. Yiko: Anything you would ask of me… “Yiko seemed like a decent Goblin, if there is such a thing. Even if she were making fun of my attractiveness to the males of her people, er, species...She also seemed to be developing some kind of infatuation or puppy love for me. I would have to use that to my advantage. And if she was hurt along the path to my goal? Who was I to shed tears over a Goblin. I had already planned to shed the blood of as many as my blade could find. But, alas, it would be a shame to destroy the only decent being in a species of cowardly monsters. Only fate would tell the future. I only hoped that these Goblins would not take too kindly to my appearance. I was here to bring death and carnage; to steal their most cherished possession. I was not here to bring happiness. All of this was merely a prelude to 'Third Dawning', where I planned to inflict pain upon a particularly annoying Goblin, and where I planned to lay hands finally upon MY TV Title. And what was with the guardsman hitting on me? If I were a Goblin, I would have higher standards than he! CHRIST!' >>>>>TO BE CONNTINUED
