Date: What brought them back together? At a recent Friday Fusion we saw the return of The Duck. But, as we all had seen, Syko banished that malicious mallard from his sight… What brought them back together? This is their story. The Final Chapter of ‘The Duck’: Intro/Pt 1 --- The usual depressing tunes pour from the jukebox tonight. Some country song, a nasal vocalist whining about love lost and a broken heart, fills the bar with a mood as inescapable as the wafts of smoke in the air. For one soul, its melody struck a nerve. It also had lost something dear. Not wanting to think about the pains, or revisit the loss, it simply dipped its bill into the shot glass before it… …and slurped back another one. Shaking his head, the bartender couldn’t believe the Mallard perched upon his bar top was nudging the glass forward, yet again. Hell, he couldn’t believe there was a Mallard Duck perched on his bar top, period! “Listen,” started the bartender, “I think you’ve had enough.” Shuttup. Snapping its beady eyes onto the bartender, the Duck ordered, “Quack!” All eyes had been on the Duck since he came in. Now they were becoming quite attentive. Reaching for the empty glass the bartender repeated, “No, I think you’ve had enough…” You really don’t want to do this. Give the bird what it wants… It doesn’t matter. Look behind the bar. Bottle of brown stuff. Clear stuff. Green stuff. Pink. Black. Blue. A snarling bill snaps at the bartender’s hand, the intoxicated Duck quite intent on protecting its glass. “Jesus!” cried the bartender, taking a sudden step back. Luckily for him, he retracted quickly enough to save his hand. Going for the closest bottle of liquor, he silently complies with a nod. You just saved your ass. A foursome push back from their table, rising. Unaware, the Duck allows some Southern Comfort to be poured into its glass. Satisfied by the brown nectar filling the glass to the… It begins to overflow… Wide eyed, the bartender looks past the Duck… “I don’t want any trouble out of you boys,” he says to the four hunters lined up behind the Duck, the tipped bottle spilling all over the bar top as he draws back. “What are you doing here, Duck?” One of them questions. Watch it. Ignoring the spilled liquor, and the shadows looming over it, the Duck huddled quietly over its Southern Comfort. Presently, it gave them attention from the corner of its eye, if even. Someone decides to tempt fate. Prods the duck with the barrel of a rifle. It’s just a Duck. What can it do? “Ducks don’t belong here.” Snorts one of the hunters, meeting the Duck’s glare as it turns around. “He don’t serve your type…” Locking its narrowed orbs on the four, the Duck sizes up its adversaries. Prestigious NRA members no doubt, middle aged, clad in camo, each ranging from 180-230lbs. Like lambs to slaughter, they initiate with a threatening step forward… Hmmm, 4 on 1? Piece of cake. But, remember boys… You asked for it. “Sorry, but I have to ask you to leave…” The Duck snapped its vision back, where the source of the statement, the Bartender, steadily held a cocked shotgun. “You’re just causing too much trouble.” 5 now then? You’d better have some pinpoint aim. What you are about to deal with is no ordinary Duck. It’s a mistake made by the very hand of God. Or was it? That may be answered with time, but for now, we must look into the Duck’s past, so as to fully understand it… Born of a black egg, it grew up just like the Goblin had. Alone. But, unlike Syko, the Duck remembers its parents, and the look of horror upon their faces as their little hatchling devoured them alive. Memories… It would soon set off on its own, after eating all of its siblings naturally, venturing out into the world, and hoping to one-day find itself. That took about two seconds when the Duck realized it hated the world with a passion. Desire to obliterate it all, a burning lust, set the Duck apart. It was a monster, a vicious beast, an abomination…All trapped within the body of a normal, everyday Duck. Somehow it was genetically altered. The natural DNA of the Duck changed its body… Made it stronger, faster, and more efficient. …the Duck understood its power… It hated the world, and wanted nothing to do with it. So it wandered… A vagabond, psycho killer. No one would dare tempt fate in the path of this creature. This… thing… Fear… Well, fear after laughter, because it was a Duck… anyway, FEAR would drive all who came close away. But… Of all things… Love mocked it. It was so hateful. So alone. It HATED love. It LOVED hate. And for a long time, it was alone with that one truth. That is, until it met came across something just as hateful on one fateful evening. http://pcw.ewmuseum.com/roleplays/messages/1962.shtml The hate CONTROLLED the Goblin. It was like a mirror image of the Duck. It had to follow! And so it did! And by god, it even learned what love was. It loved the hateful Goblin for his hate! HATE FOR THE PLANET BROUGHT THEM TOGETHER!!! However, Syko didn’t show the same fondness for the Mallard. Shunned by the same hate the Goblin showed for everyone, the Duck only became more attracted to its newfound companion. The Goblin truly had no heart for anything but himself. Until that belt showed up… And then HER… THE DUCK WAS BEING ROBBED OF THE MOST IMPORTANT THING IT EVER HAD! …Thus, mistakes were made. The Duck would lose it all. Just as quickly as it had found it. Since, the Duck just hasn’t had the drive to kill, or destroy, or be evil. It misses Syko, the only friend it had. Or… the only friend it thought it had. Putting out a cigar in a half empty shot of Southern Comfort, the Duck finally hopped down from the bar top. Blood stained walls marked that the Duck had been there. The only survivor, the Bartender, reported what he saw. “A Duck?!?” was the only appropriate response from the authorities. --- Well… Now to see how they came together again. COMING SOON
