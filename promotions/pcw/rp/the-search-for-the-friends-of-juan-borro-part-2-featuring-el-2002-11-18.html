The Search for the Friends of Juan Borro (Part 2) featuring El Diablo and Kevin Klaymore

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/18/02 Swoosh! The wind blows as yellow and orange leaves fly into the air. With every leaf, flies a memory of the past. Yet another segment of the past will be discovered today, when a man who served great importance in the life of Juan Borro, will be found, Kevin Klaymore. “Still thinking about what happened last night?” El Diablo asks in a concerned tone. “He didn’t have to go out that way. I just don’t understand.” El Romantico says with a faded tone, grasping the hair on his head. “Everyone is there own person, and even though you may try and change them and think you know who they are, they know best. That’s why you should just not get involved.” Diablo says as he rolls down his window to get some air. “I guess your right.” As Romantico drove on in the deserted highway he looked over at all the simple things he had taken for granted. He noticed the grass, and how every single strand seems to be perfect in it’s own way. He noticed the sun and the array of colors it produces. He notices the wildlife, all alone and miles away from any other living thing. They just stood there and dealt with what life brought them. “There has to be more for me in this world.” Romantico mutters to himself. “Did you say something?” Diablo replies as he hears the slight mutter from Romantico. “No. Must have been the wind.” Romantico replied as his head lowered in sadness. Later on in the day, the two men arrived at the Academy of Wrestling in Alabama where Kevin Klaymore has been working the last few months. The two men gather their things and enter the building. “Is Kevin Klaymore here?” Romantico asks as a young and beautiful woman in the front calls on him. “Yes, he is, May I ask what you need him for?” The woman asks as she takes off her glasses to get a better look at whom she’s talking to. “Were old friends, just stopping by to talk to him?” Romantico adds as she smiles back at him. “Oh, well let me welcome myself, I’m Mandy, Klaymore’s wife.” Mandy says as she shakes El Romantico’s hand. “So the big fella is straight after all.” El Diablo says under her breath. “Excuse me?” She asks. “Nothing, just a little cough.” Diablo says as he breaks out in a small laugh. “Mandy where does that name sound familiar?” El Romantico asks himself. {Flashback} “I wish I could have had something like Kevin and Mandy have, but I didn’t I blew it. No one could love me, no one understands where I come from.” {Flashback ends.) A shivers rolls down the spine of Romantico as he remembers the words of his brother just days before he died. “Are you all right you look a little sick.” Mandy says as she notices the sweat pouring from the forehead of Romantico. “I’m fine, thank you.” Romantico politely responds. “Let me go get Kevin, he’ll be thrilled to see you.” Mandy says as she goes into the ring area to get Kevin. Romantico goes over and peaks through the window as he sees Klaymore just the way he pictured him in front of groups of young inspired wrestlers giving them different strategies and techniques. “Still as goofy looking as always.” Diablo says as he watches Klaymore almost lose his balance after performing a move to the crowd. “As much as I hate to say it, that buffoon was a good guy. A stupid guy and one who didn’t know when to shut his mouth, but a good guy.” Diablo says showing an almost sensitive side. “That has the be the first time I almost heard you say something nice.” Romantico replies. “Yea, and it’s the only time, so get the hell out of my way. I want to see what’s taking this douche so long.” Diablo shouts as he boulders through the door. Right as it opens Kevin and Mandy appear walking through. “EL DIABLO! Long time no see.” Klaymore shouts as he goes to give Diablo a big hug, but Diablo backs away . “Still the same old stiff as always.” Kevin says with a big laugh on his face. “Yea, same old stiff.” Romantico comments as he laughs along to the displeasure of El Diablo. “And you are?” Kevin asks. At first Romantico was hesitant to say whom he was, knowing that various questions would arise, but he knew he had to get through the hard things, before he could go into the things he wanted to find out. “I’m El Romantico, brother of Juan Borro.” Romantico replies in a dignified tone. “Holy shit, are you serious? Where is the big guy, we haven’t spoken in ages. I almost thought he forgot about me.” Kevin says in a rage of excitement. “Well I’m sure he always thinks of you, but unfortunately he’s no longer here.” Romantico says as he drops his head low. “What do you mean?” Kevin says with a confused look on his face. “Juan died, about four months ago, after a PPV.” Romantico spits out as quickly and as not painful as he can. Kevin suddenly freezes like he was shot with a bullet and he sits in the nearest seat. Tears begin to roll from the eyes of Mandy who knew him very well. “I know this is a hard time for you, but I need to ask you something very important.” Romantico says showing the little emotion he has. Kevin takes a deep gulp and then regains his composure. “What’s your question?” “I need to know where Juan’s father Manuel lives. I know its somewhere in Portugal, but I don’t know the location.” Romantico asks as he stares at Kevin for an answer. “I’m sorry but I don’t know.” Romantico quickly shows signs of displeasure as he went all this way to learn nothing. “But I will tell you where you can find someone who does know?” Kevin says lifting the spirit of Romantico a little. Kevin writes down a name and address on a sheet of paper and hands it to Romantico. As Romantico grabs the paper he notices the name on it is Juliet Svetlana. A woman Romantico had heard so much of, but never been able to meet. “Thank you so much.” Romantico says in joy as both him and Diablo rush out of the building and set out for their new destination. “You know Romantico, your pretty set on finding Juan’s father, does it really matter that much?” Diablo asks, as he doesn’t see the point of everything that’s going on. “If you ever knew what it was like to live without even knowing your own name, you’d feel the way I do. Juan’s father is the one man who can give me answers, and I’m determined in finding him” With those words the scene fades into a twinkling star in a planet up above, where all of us are just dots and nothing more.
