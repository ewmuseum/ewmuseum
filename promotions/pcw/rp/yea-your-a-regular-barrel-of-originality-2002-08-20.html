Yea your a regular barrel of originality

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/20/02 He claims to be a god but what proof does he supply?? Is it his slightly higher then average win/loss record? Or maybe simple the idea that as a God he chooses the ever-popular career of a professional wrestler. To me it’s all a load of bullshit but what I am I besides a predictable human mortal?? Repetition is what drives each and every one of us. Think about it. What is life besides a cycle of tedious activities? To some this may seem boring and to those who hate it this Buds for you. -We join the Malice Man as he walks through the midway of the county fair in Missoula, Montana. Duke is wearing all black with an Archie Bunker T-shirt adding its only color. He is eating a bush of cotton candy as he walks hand and hand with his wife Jessi.- Jessi: So Akiyama thinks he’s a God and finds us boring? Williams: Yea cus God knows his promos that all send the same message of his superiority and excellence are riveting piece of intelligence and originality. -The sarcasm is heard loud and clear in Duke’s voice.- Williams: For a guy who always claims to be better then everybody he sure has to reiterate that point time and time again. If he was a God you would think he would realize we give less then a damn. Jessi: Sounds insecure. Williams: Sounds like he’s half way between here and Timbuktu. He’s also a f*cking mute. Jessi: A mute? Williams: Yea he doesn’t have the balls to talk so his bitch speaks for him. Jessi: He sounds like a real fruitcake. Williams: Your not lying. I can’ trust, understand, or respect a man who can’t look me in the eye and say a word. A real chicken shit. He got the boot from Japan you know. Jessi: I had no idea. Williams: I did some checking on the internet thingy and it turns out he was fondling young boys and then claiming to be a God to get out of it. So they tossed his ass out of the country. Now I know history repeats its self so I’m sure he’s petting boy ass here in the state and using that God excuse too. Jessi: He sounds catholic!! Williams: Yea, Cardinal Law has nothing on the hi priest of boy sex Akiyama!! Jessi: You better teach this pedophile a lesson!! Williams: Oh I will!! He’s no God but rather a walking contradiction. He’s half-truth; half-fiction. Everything he speaks badly on he is. Its just the rest of us accept what we our while he lives in Disney Land baby. Jessi: His mind is Space Mountain. Williams: Akiyama will be administered the truth Malice Style. Jessi: So is the guy who smells like urine going to shower? Williams: I’m not sure if they have showers in the terrorist training camp he lives in. You know them middle eastern folk are stuck in like the 1800’s hossette. Jessi: No wonder he smells. Is it worse then that taxi driver we had last time you wrestled in Madison Square Garden? Williams: About the same I recon. They say they smell that way cus of the food they eat?? But I don’t understand what they eat that smells like urine!! Jessi: Who know honey! Williams: Plus he dresses like he’s at a toga party. Jessi: Like in college? Williams: Yes but nobody ever gives me an invite to the party. I love toga parties! TOGA TOGA TOGA!!!!!!!! -Some fair goes stare at the large screaming wrestler.- Jessi: Chris Chase is in the match too ain’t he? Williams: Yes and the kid is as dry as the dessert. When Akiyama was talking boring I think he was talking Chris Chase. This kid is the definition of dull. Jessi: Worse then a PBS medical special? Williams: He make PBS and NPR look and sound like a God damn parade. Jessi: Jesus! Williams: Yea so I have to do this for all the fans out there who have lives, personality, and aren’t fucking insane. Jessi: I’m sure you’ll do a good job baby. How about winning me something? -The Malice couple head over to the game stands as the camera fades.- ==w==
