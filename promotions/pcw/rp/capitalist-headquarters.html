Capitalist Headquarters

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: #1 FF This past Turmoil was yet another success for the Ultimate Authority, especially for the Uzbek Annihilator. No, it’s not because Corey Williams actually remembered his name once. It was because, yet again, Alexis Parshin proved his dominance over everyone who stood in his path. First, he aided Corey Williams in taking out that moronic Malice hero, until Snow cheated to get his own way. Then, in the main event, Alexis Parshin showed more of his raw strength in dealing with Rush Mason. Now, though, Raghab and Alexis have stayed behind in Washington in regards to two men, William S Powell and Reginald P Lovecraft, capitalists of the worst kind… “Yuck! Look at this filth, Raghab!” As usual, the booming voice of Alexis Parshin opens the promo as the scene fades in. The two men are outside on the streets of a bustling city, presumably Washington D.C. Cars whiz by with every passing second while they stand outside. Alexis is wearing a leather jacket, with the Afghan flag on the back, with his ring attire underneath. Raghab is wearing-well it goes without saying that the man’s chubby body is covered by vibrantly colored clothing-a yellow sweater with a PCW Chef t-shirt with khaki pants that are barely staying buttoned. At the moment, Alexis is looking down at the street, as he stands near the edge of the curb. He looks down at the garbage under the curb’s shadow and spits down at it. “Indeed, Konstantin, it is a land where filth is yet another of the growing problems.” Alexis nods and smiles, “Yes it is, Raghab. The list of problems includes Alexis Parshin and Chef…how lovely.” Both men snicker at this, the mention of the people’s hatred for Alexis Parshin and the Chef. “This Friday will be perhaps the most marvelous of days in the Ultimate Authority yet. First, Chef and I will take out those two sad capitalists who call themselves Politically Incorrect. Then, Corey Williams will make waste of Jason Snow, the bourgeois fool. Finally, Frisco and Xavier Kannon will destroy Big Alan Steele and Miyagawa, making it the perfect night.” After Alexis’ mini-speech, Raghab must of course, reply with an ignorant comment. “Konstantin, why are we still in Washington if you must fight on Friday?” Raghab asks the question wonderingly, to which Alexis replies with a sigh and a shake of his large head. “Raghab, we are here to prove a point to Politically Incorrect. In this vast capitol of the United States, we will show them that their capitalism and their American government is all a sham, although I don’t they do not know that with their moronic President.” Raghab shows rare intelligence and takes a pretzel from the bag in his pocket and begins to pretend to choke on the pretzel, causing Alexis to erupt with laughter, as Raghab smiles to himself. “Now, we must find ourselves a taxi, Raghab. How might we go about to do that?” Unfortunately, Raghab is not able to help out this time, shrugging his shoulders unknowingly, not knowing how to hail any of the many yellow taxicabs that whiz by. Alexis, not really thinking, comes up with an idea, but obviously doesn’t actually think out the entire idea. As Raghab watches on in confusion, Alexis steps out in the moving traffic, narrowly missing being hit by one of the taxis that he’s trying to call for. Having no where to swerve to, one of the yellow cabs is forced to stop in traffic, yelling curse words towards the large man, which of course Alexis and Raghab can’t hear, so they smile towards the man and slide into the taxi. “Take us to the White House.” Alexis booms up to the driver, a short Italian man with a balding spot on the top of his head. “The White House? Whachu gonna do? Blow da whole thing up?” The Italian man chuckles to himself at his own joke, while Alexis feigns a laugh, then his face goes stone cold. Raghab chuckles a bit, but at one glance from Alexis goes silent. Most of the drive is silent, but as the two men near the White House, a gasp from Raghab can be heard loudly. Alexis glances over and gives him a look of scorn, but Raghab cannot hold in his excitement. “Look at the size of it, Konstantin. They may be capitalist scum, but the architecture is incredible!” Alexis doesn’t bother to reply, sighing loudly in an exasperated tone, meaning to tell Raghab that he doesn’t care. The taxi reaches the end of the street and Alexis hands the man a twenty-dollar bill, which includes a miniscule tip for the man. He shakes his fist at Alexis as he drives off. “The peasants deserve no extra money for a service that takes no skills at all.” Raghab usually would nod at something like this, but is still amazed by the White House, which is testing Alexis’ patience with every passing minute. “Raghab, it is not that great. It is a house where capitalist scum attend to themselves and do not think of anything else. The decent exterior doesn’t make up at all for the vicious crimes and selfish deeds that go on inside.” Raghab’s eyes are still the size of dinner plates, and the man runs up towards the gates of the White House, trying to stick his fat head through the bars to get a better view. Alexis walks behind the man, bored with the current surroundings. “Raghab, the only reason we came here was to show the stupid capitalists that their government is nothing but capitalist lies. If you don’t hurry up, then we will never be able to get to everything today.” Raghab doesn’t even look back, just holds up one pudgy finger to single for more time. This is the last straw, as Alexis explodes on the chunky short man. “RAGHAB! GET OVER HERE NOW!!” Normally, this would send the man running, but at this point Raghab just waddles back over, sighing. “What in Allah’s name are you doing?” Raghab scratches his chin for a moment, thinking of a way to disguise his interest. “Well Konstantin, I was actually looking for key structural points by which the White House could be taken out, but if you want to stop our quest for some silly governmental plans, so be it.” Raghab’s voice even has a little bit of snap to it, which just irritates Alexis more. “Look at this, the meeting of Congress is over now. We have lost our chance because of you.” Raghab smiles, shaking his head. “That’s not true, Konstantin. They meet once again tomorrow to discuss nuclear waste.” A smile comes across Alexis’ face at this as well and he pats Raghab on the back. “You have done well, Raghab. Perhaps it was not a mistake to bring you here after all.” Raghab smiles like a child who has been praised as Alexis turns and speaks to no one in particular, “The United States is but a pond of scum. Once the pond has a small amount of poison within it, everything in the pond changes. Alexis Parshin is the poison to the ecosystem…” Alexis laughs with his booming laugh as the scene fades out…
