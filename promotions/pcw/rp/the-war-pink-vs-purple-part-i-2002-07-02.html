The War: PINK! vs. Purple [Part I]

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 2, 2002 OOC: I came up with this very corny idea for a series of roleplays, and I decided I would try it out. It’s a little far fetched, but hey, that’s what e-wrestling is all about...right? Well, anyways, here it is! The killers had awoken early that morning. Although Jack had already prepared thoroughly, he cleaned and checked his murder weapon one last time before loading it. He left the weapon in his sleeping bag and went to shave. The air was still cold on the skin, and the nearby trees were faint in the morning mist. With luck the mist would not lift until much later in the day. He would have liked his normal weapon carrying bag but with his battered PINK! jacket and pullover it would have been impractical and conspicuous. The PINK! Range Rover was the second car in the convoy. At the head of the convoy was the PINK! Cadillac driven by Jack Valentine, and containing the beaters for the day. The Range Rover had no radio, and the beaters were being bounced about on the wooden benches in the back. They were all cold and uncomfortable, their clothes still damp and clinging from the day before. The Caddy kangarooed through a particularly large gully in the road. The tallest of the beaters crashed his head against one of the struts for the canvas awning. He cursed loudly, the other beaters smiled through their hangovers. Yes, Jack Valentine and Johnny Heart had a posse of people. Why? Nobody knew. But they were all dressed conspicuously as if they were up to some master plan. Jack and Johnny looked like the head guys, dressed in PINK!, while the others were dressed in Rainbow colored jumpsuits, all with nametags on their right breast. The Range Rover was driven by of course, Johnny Heart, the passenger seat was empty, and in the back were a few more homosexually clad men. The Range Rover swooped and rose, passing the gully. Gay Man #1: (Pouting like a little girl) 'Ooh...easy Johnny...I can't have my rear end bounce that hard, especially after last night.' Johnny Heart: (Oblivious to the man's 'gayness') 'Why's that?' Gay Man #1: (Crossing his legs and putting a hand to his chin) 'Oh, nevermind.' The final car was a VW Beetle, PINK! in color of course. There were six men in it. Oddly enough, the men in the car were dressed like members of the Village People. There was a cop, a cowboy, an Indian, a G.I., a Biker and a Construction man. This obviously was the strangest group of the three, but they all seemed to be working together. The car effortlessly climbed out of the gully. They watched the surrounding woods, half expecting one of the looming shapes to turn and take a pot shot at them. None of them liked this trip. Even with the six men in their team, it would be impossible to provide adequate cover. Something was about to go down...just what, was unknown to the viewers at this time. The trees were mostly birch, and a few conifers, it was light relatively open woodland. In the mist it felt like a white walled room. The noise of the cars seemed suddenly very close, and in a few minutes the convoy reached the destination. The Cadillac drove to the far side of the clearing and parked close to the trees. The Range Rover parked in the middle of the clearing. Johnny Heart got out of the car, and went round to the back, opening the hatch, and unwrapping the shotguns. Guns? Johnny Heart? Normally these two things wouldn't go together...what was going on? The final car parked askew blocking off the entrance to the clearing. The Village People quickly got out and pranced over to the Range Rover, it was only then that the passengers got out. It was perhaps their rainbow colored outfits that made the Village People feel that the open space was more dangerous than the woods themselves. In the mist the clearing was probably a less threatening environment. Twenty minutes later the shoot was ready to start. The beaters had fanned out across the far side of the woods, and Jack Valentine & Johnny Heart were in position. Occasionally the air was punctuated by the stutter of walkie talkies, or the crackle of twigs broken under foot. Although the ground was normally sodden and marshy, the cool, crisp air of the night was severe enough to leave it crisp and hard. Jack Valentine: 'Alright boys, they should be setting off now.' Valentine glanced down at his watch, and loaded his gun. He sat his gun down at his side, as there would likely be little to aim at for awhile. Jack Valentine: 'You take the flank Johnny, and I'll take the right...and will you god damn Village People keep quiet, you'll wake up everything on this side of San Francisco.” Johnny Heart: “I can’t wait to take out those evil bastards. I hope I get to shoot as many as you promised Jack.” Jack Valentine: “I was told there would be hundreds here today...hundreds!” Jack and Johnny both tiptoed around the area, in their conspicuous PINK! clothing. The Village People and the beaters just pranced around as the Village People attempted to show the gay men how the YMCA was actually done. For Johnny the whole expedition was a bloody pantomime, the shooting in this glorified wooded area was indifferent at the best of times, and with the Village People here, a herd of elephants would have been spooked by now. It promised to be a long day. There was doubtless an ulterior motive for this invitation. He would be buttered up all day (No, not like that perverts) before some words to slip in the right ear about the match on Friday. He could care less that his idle band, the Village People were there. This was a time of action, and a time for destruction. If the gay men thought that the goons from the Village People were impressing anyone, they were sadly mistaken. Back at the cars, the Village People and the gay men sat listening to “Macho Man” on a CD the Village People were playing out of their VW Bug. All of the Village People were there except for one, The Cop. The Cop officer was wandering the perimeter of the clearing. The day was gradually warming. Jack Valentine had got ahead of the others in his search for whatever he was looking for. He had just shot his first shot of the day, and was hoping for some more. His gun, whatever it was shooting, went off in the air out of the excitement from his trigger finger. A twig snapped. He started and turned. The Village People Cop was standing behind him. A broken twig in his hands. Jack Valentine: “What the hell are you doing here?” Village People Cop: “Don’t you recognize me Jack?” Jack Valentine: “Of course I do, you’re the Village Person Cop. But you’re interrupting me...we brought you here for one reason, and one reason only...to destroy the Purple!” A small pause came about the scene, as we finally realize what Valentine’s intent was. His intent was to destroy the color Purple. But how would he do that? How would he destroy all that is purple...you cannot destroy a color. Jack Valentine: (Continuing) “Not the color purple, but all designer clothing that comes in the color purple...or any other color for that matter. It seems as if purple is taking the lead in designer clothing, when it should be PINK! that is bought by all models, actresses and pretty people like ourselves. I see you chose not to dress in PINK! today Mr. Cop.” Village People Cop: “Purple is my friend...it is our friend. Just like you are mistaken about the Pretty Cool Guys and Cowabunga Connection, you are mistaken about the Purple...Purple is better than PINK! And just like PCG and CC will destroy The Heartbreakers, Purple will destroy the PINK!” Distant gunfire broke off in a distance. Jack Valentine: “Why are you doing this? We were supposed to work together in this battle today. We were supposed to spray paint all of the purple designer clothing coming from this warehouse PINK! today, and now you want to change the plan? I don’t think so.” Well, the plan was revealed to the viewers of this ongoing drama. Valentine wanted to rid the world of purple. And rightfully so...after all PINK! is better than Purple...and Purple should not steal PINK!’s spotlight. As Valentine ended his sentence he pulled out some kind of cartridge and loaded it into his gun. He then unloaded a mist of PINK! spray all over the Village People Cop, sending him to the ground. Jack Valentine: “The world must be rid of purple lovers like you, and Cowabunga Connection, and those damn Pretty Cool Guys. Purple Sucks! I’ve got better things to do than waste my time on you...I’ve got to save the world!” Village People Cop: (Stunned by the PINK! mist) “I’ve got something to say to you, and I wish your partner was here to hear it!” The Village People Cop makes an evil grin across his ugly face and pulls a small gun out of his back pocket. He nails Valentine in the face with 3 blasts of his purple spray, knocking him down. The Village People Cop then dumps loads of purple paint all over Valentine, causing the Heartbreaker to burst out in tears. Village People Cop: “We aren’t one of you...despite what everyone thinks! And now, the Village People, along with the color purple will destroy you PINK! loving fairies.” The Village People Cop kicks Valentine in the gut and disappears into the mist. Valentine struggled on the ground, still crying after what was done to him by one of his idols. Soon, the rainbow colored squad and Johnny Heart made their way to check up on Valentine after hearing the shots of paint fired upon Jack. They showed up, themselves will bits of purple on them, seeing Jack Valentine covered in purple ooze. Johnny Heart hits the ground and looks up at the sky. Johnny Heart: “Damn dirty purple lovers! Damn you all to hell!” As the gay men and Johnny Heart tend to Valentine, the scene fades. -TO BE CONTINUED-
