Because... EVERYBODY LOVES S&M!!!!

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th April 2002 We arrive here at last, back in the Asylum Gym. Although it’s now called the Anaheim Dungeon, but let’s not get into that since everyone still calls the place the Asylum Gym. Miyagawa is walking up his little garden path... well the pavement path actually since the place doesn’t actually have a front garden as such... unless you count that small pile of vomit that Syko left six months ago and Miyagawa still hasn’t cleaned. It’s growing so much moss on it now that he’s even watered it a few times. He turns the key in the lock and opens the door quickly, he is once again on his own, alone... as it is what it means, on his own. As he is about to close the door, suddenly a foot is jammed in it. Miyagawa turns about and re-opens the door, and standing there is none other than... Miyagawa: SYKO!!! Syko: MIYA!!!! Oh god, not him again. Syko: Same narrator? Miyagawa: As always. I heard you were back in PCW, how’s it going? Syko: Aren’t you going to invite me in? Miyagawa realises that he’s left the young goblin standing on his doorstep, and invites him in. Syko: You really need to wash that mould off; it’ll learn to play the banjo eventually. Miyagawa: I’m grooming it for in ring talent for PURE. Syko: You are kidding, right? Miyagawa grins at the goblinoid. Miyagawa: Of course I am. Even a Goblin is a better gimmick then “Hey there, I’m mould!”. Syko: Hey! Miyagawa leads Syko through to the Television room/kitchen and begins to brew the coffee machine. Syko: Can I get a cappuccino? Miyagawa: **grins at Syko**Yep. Starbucks is three hundred yards down the road on the left. Syko: *shrugs** Whatever you make is fine then. Miyagawa fills the little pot with water and sticks it on the hotplate and starts the process going. Syko: So how’s Natalia doing now? Miyagawa: Ah, she’s good at last now that the Blood transfusion has finally been made. Everything is good, and life is sweet at the moment. Syko: Well there is a reason why I came here... Stryfe... well... Stryfe and Boda. Miyagawa: Yeah, I saw the booking. I face Stryfe and you face Boda.... and a week later it’s reversed at Supremacy. It’s all about the Benjamin’s. Syko raises an eyebrow. Syko: Eh? Miyagawa: Sorry, I’ve been hanging around the hospital too long... I’m starting to pick up jive speak. I mean... look at my hands... Miyagawa sticks out his arms straight and his hands seem to automatically shake and flop to the side out open. Miyagawa: I’ve got Jazz hands and I can’t get rid of them. Syko: Ye gods, Jazz hands. Miyagawa: Damn these here foul Jazz hands. The Jazz Hands of death will reap supreme vengeance on Stryfe and Boda twice in eight days. Syko: Hanging around Clyde? Miyagawa: **shakes his head** No, I was being serious there. Syko stares at the coffee machine and frowns at it. Syko: How long is this coffee going to take? Miyagawa: At least until the next promo I’d imagine. Syko: Darn. Caffeine always makes me more alert in the ring... also makes me kinda hyper as well which is really never much of a good thing. Miyagawa: So are we reforming S&M then? Syko: Sure... if you want to. Miyagawa: Of course I want to. Incidentally, I have got us a catchphrase because I am the master of all things phrase and catch... Syko: And it is? Miyagawa: Well... Everybody LOVES S&M!!!! Syko: Me likes. Do you think I could get a muffin with my coffee. Miyagawa: Three hundred yards down the road and on your left. Syko: Damn. And with that not so important announcement, the camera slowly fades out on this extremely rushed promo.
