To The Homeland [Finale]

24 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/24/02 From Jordan X Shabazz’s computer diary. I had arrived in Africa. The homeland. The place where I, and all African Americans, wait, check that, the place where the whole world originated from. Even the redneck racist hicks like John Crichton, who gets off on using nice, beautiful, wholesome Hispanic woman for his own, sick, disgusting sexual pleasure. I only had a few days to spend here in Africa, before I went to America for Turmoil, and then to see my organization, the Negroes International Growing Group Association, hit the New York Stock Exchange. I’m just sitting here, in my hotel room, fantasying about all the stupid ass white people that are going to buy stock in my company. Back home, my two butlers, Steve Armstrong and Chris Bird, are relaxing, thinking that I’ll forget about their stupid white asses. How wrong are they, they’ll see when I get back, they’ll see. Right now I’m at home, on my laptop computer. I’m bored as fuck, and angry too. At the airport I was totally violated by some white security guards, who wouldn’t allow me to bring my televisions into this country. They just don’t seem to understand. I wanted to show the African children that when I am in America, slapping the hell out of white devils and representing the Malice Crew, that I do care about them. That I would not forget my roots. Being Television Champion is a responsibility. When you are Television Champion you have to be responsible. You are a role model. I want to be a role model for Black youth in America, and in Africa, and in Japan for PURE…. Wait… there are no black people in Japan… Hmmmm… another example of racism. But I’ll leave it at that, for now. I’m off to downtown Johannesburg, South Africa, so I can go to some shops and buy some gifts for the children. I’m a modern day Princess Diana. The scene opens up in a crowded South African street with stores every few feet. It’s very packed with people trying to get to wherever they need to be. Jordan X Shabazz is seen walking into the market with a white man. This man is his tour guide. Jordan and the man duck into a tiny little novelty shop where they sell used electronics. Old radios, beaten up and broken down televisions, even a few broken down computers are in this store. Normally, most of these prices would be too much for most of the African population, but since Jordan X Shabazz is raking in the big dollars from PCW and N.I.G.G.A, he’s going to do what any Civil Rights Activist looking for publicity, and to help the children of the country where his history lies would do… he went into the store and bought as many appliances as he could. **************************** It’s three hours later now. Jordan X Shabazz spent a total of $523 in that store, almost buying the small place out. Now he was going to a small village in South Africa. He was going to distribute all the goods that he bought to the South African children. The scene opens with Jordan inside of a small jeep driven by the tour guide that was with him we saw earlier. They are nearing the small South African village of Ramas. There are kids of all races, shapes and sizes. Well, not all races, since this IS South Africa is mainly just blacks and whites. Though there are a few Middle Eastern people in this village. As soon as Jordan gets out of the jeep, it’s like a scene from Ali. The children crowd around the jeep, full of appliances, as Jordan reaches into them and hands them to as many children as he can. Jordan smiles, he’s doing something for the good of his kind…. And this better be on some damn tape! Give a brotha some credit for his hard work. ************************ The next morning. Jordan Shabazz was treated like a God in this village. They offered for him to stay in their village and spend the night at one of their small houses, but Jordan declined. He was dead tired, and was expecting a call from somebody back in America. The call came that morning. The call came from HBO. They heard that Jordan had gone to Africa. And after seeing Jordan X Shabazz on 20/20, they decided that they’d much rather see Jordan X Shabazz, uncensored, on HBO. They convinced Jordan into doing a 60 Minutes like television show, except it was just a segment of a show, and he would just rant about things that bothered him about the world. When Jordan told them his schedule for the week, HBO officials decided to send a man to Africa to film Jordan in a small local community center. Appearing at Turmoil, going to Fusion, and then going to the Pay Per View, left no time for extra-curricular activities. It was morning now, Jordan had gotten out of his stiff hotel bed. He quickly put on the gray jumpsuit that he was going to do the interview in, and he went outside to start his morning jog to the community center where the segment/interview would take place. As soon as Jordan opened the door downstairs and stepped into the humid and hot African night, he knew something was up. There were children, everywhere. Jordan began jogging slowly through the gravelly and broken down road (If you could call it that). Every few minutes, Jordan would be joined by another few young children and teenagers. After about ten minutes, when Jordan was almost at the community center, there were at least 40 people jogging with Shabazz. And they were screaming “Shabazz Bobiyay”, meaning “Shabazz, kill him!” The “him” they were talking about was John Crichton. Not too many Africans can afford televisions, let alone Pay Per View. But the few that can afford both of this luxuries, usually invite as many people as their house can hold to watch it. The PCW was being shown in Africa, albeit a week behind, it was being shown. The African people see Jordan as a Freedom Fighter. So when they saw John Crichton smash Jordan X Shabazz’s face into a wall, they were outraged. Now they wanted revenge, and they were telling Shabazz to extract revenge the only way possible… by death. When Shabazz reached the community center, he was alone. The African People respected his request to go and do the interview by himself. When Jordan entered, the cameras were already set up. Jordan wiped some sweat off of his forehead with a towel that was tossed to him by one of the cameramen. Jordan took a swig out of a water bottle, which was handed to him by one of the cameramen. Jordan stands in front of the camera, he already had all of his material ready. The night before he sat and starting thinking to himself. Thinking about all the things he could say, and now was his chance. He was going to be heard. He could use profanity if he wanted. He watched as one of the directors counted down until he went to taping. Jordan smiled, just like when he was on 20/20. The counting finished, and he was taping. Jordan X Shabazz: “Television and mass exposure, I like it. But Mike Wallace, eat shit, 60 AIN’T GOT SHIT ON THIS BLACK MAN!” Jordan chuckles at his own violent screaming outburst. Jordan X Shabazz: “White people own television now a days…” Jordan sighs. Jordan X Shabazz: “Damn whitey tryin' to always make us hate the Middle Easterners...we didn't hate the Arabians, the Arabians hated white people!” Jordan takes a deep breath and nods. Jordan X Shabazz: “So, Taliban, Mr. Bin Laden, FUCK UP WHITE PEOPLE!” Jordan is now going on pure adrenaline. Little beads of sweat are on the side of Jordan’s head now as his screaming fit has worked him up quite a bit. Jordan X Shabazz: “I don’t support terrorism, but when white people get what they deserve for holding black people and other people of other races down for so many years, I can’t help but think about the way fate plays into this world.” Shabazz takes a breather, and he leans on the wall that is behind him. He looks calmed now. Jordan X Shabazz: “Before I came here to Africa, I was stopped at the airport, they thought I had drugs. That’s what White America wants everybody to think. That every black man is a drug smuggling, Ebonics speaking, unintelligent piece of trash, but that’s farther from the truth than anything I’ve ever heard in my life.” Jordan shakes his head to put emphasis on what he’s saying. Jordan X Shabazz: “I’m an intelligent, good looking, proper English and grammar using, African American. And I do not use drugs, and I do not advocate the use of drugs. So all of White America, and the whole white world, can kiss my big black ass.” Jordan is now livid again, he doesn’t stop. Jordan X Shabazz: “They security everywhere in America is beefed up, is that so? THEN WHY THE FUCK WAS I ALLOWED TO BE ATTACKED AT A FUCKING WRESTLING SHOW?! HUH?! ANSWER THAT, WHITE AMERICA!” Jordan’s rant has broken down. He’s referring to the John Crichton attack that happened to him a few weeks before on PCW Friday Fusion. Jordan X Shabazz: “I just hope that John Crichton, his entire family, his bitch Aeryn, and all members of the KKK, and anybody to do with them, BURN IN HELL!!” Jordan X Shabazz stops screaming, and is now calm again. Jordan X Shabazz: “Thank you, I am Jordan X Shabazz, and those were my five minutes.” Jordan’s small segment, which will be shown in-between programs on HBO, is now over, and Jordan’s time in Africa was over. He was going back to America now, back to the white man’s world…
