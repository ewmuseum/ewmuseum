Where Are You Going? (Featuring Chris Storm)

20 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/20/03 Outside the moisture falling from the sky hits the pavement and freezes with contact. The mercury in the thermometer goes up to the ten that is printed onto the side of the thermometer in bold black lettering. As my eyes come in contact with the number it runs through my mind that maybe today isn’t the best of days to go out with the children and go build a snowman or perhaps go sledding. Instead maybe I should use some of my time to spend on my own. I needed a break to just relax and think about everything that was piling around me. A losing streak that just continued to grow each time I stepped into the ring, the potential of marrying someone who might not really love me at all, and a tag team title shot that I did not want to ruin with my losing streak. For awhile now I had began to feel like the weaker link between Chris and I. Perhaps it was because I was the smaller one and tended to get tossed around like a rag doll, or perhaps it was because I was the one with the softer heart. The one who could be bent by simple words such as the ones that had been coming El Romantico’s lips as of late that were often just ones of lust twisted around to get into my mind and weaken me not in a physical manner, but in an emotional manner, and the worst part of it all is that it was working. I was beginning to get the feeling that there was something serious bothering Tessa. Yet she wouldn't tell me what it was. I hated her hiding things from me but what could I do about it? If she didn't want to tell me then she won't. I wasn't going to force her to do anything she didn't want to. I had my own things on my mind that I wasn't telling her though. So I guess that made us even. I was still debating with myself on how much I loved her and why? I never really could see myself settling down with anyone just a few months ago. Yet here I was with a woman with my band on her finger. I needed some fresh air to think but the winter cool was not the type of weather needed to go out for a walk. Thinking about it, I started to feel a little suffocated by the walls. Ever since the weather dropped down to the tens and lower we have been saying cooed up in the house. I could tell it was getting to the others also. Going from staying in the house to cramming onto a small plane to jamming into a locker room the size of a closet isn't anyone's idea of fun. I looked over toward Tessa, she was deep in thought. About what I didn't know though. I stood there at the window inside the kitchen and stared there for awhile as my mind drifted off into twenty different directions. One part floating towards Chris, another towards the match I had on Tuesday, and then another about the winter and how much I could not wait for it to be over with. I was sick of being inside, I needed some fresh air. I needed my space and the only way I could get it would be on my air far away from the children and Chris, even if it were only for five minutes and then it hit me. Perhaps I didn’t need to be outside to get my space; all I needed was a distance as small as a mile and within that mile stood the mall. Every girl loves to shop and with me that convince was only a mile away so it was then I decided that in order to get my mind to rest and get back on track all I needed was some time and a little bit of cash and I’d be just fine. I paced back and forth in my room. This was making me a wreck. The wedding was getting closer and closer and I was having cold feet. Was Tessa having second thoughts also? What if she realizes she doesn't love me? She might even fall for El Romantico's tricks and think she has a crush on him. 'Oh that bastard...' I muttered to myself. Just the thought of El Romantico made me sick. It hurt me even more because I use to be just like him. I can remember the first time I was in the ring with Tessa. I planted a kiss on her then drove her down with a Powerbomb through a table. I admit I regret doing that but I don't regret the kiss. It was amazing. Yet on Turmoil....El Romantico seemed to have had the same ideas that I had as he planted a big kiss on Tessa during the match. Something I plan to make him pay for. But still, Tessa had fallen in love with me after all I did to her. Who is to say she won't do the same with El Romantico? I didn't want to think about it. I needed to get out of here. Staying in one spot was driving me crazy with thoughts. Finally coming out of my daze of thoughts I made my way up the stairs towards the bedroom. Chris must have heard the muffled sound of my steps across the carpet in the hallway because there he stood in the bedroom doorway waiting for me with a hug, and then followed behind me into the bedroom where I began to shuffle through my drawers for some extra cash. “Tessa Bear how about me and you spend some time together today. You look like you need to be cheered up some.” He flashed me one of those smiles that no matter how hard you tried you could not resist, but I had to do my best to avoid it. I needed my time alone. I needed to say no, no matter how much it ached my heart to say it. There was still no reply from Tessa. She doesn't want to spend time with me or something? Maybe I am pushing her too much. Being too over protective of her. After all, I have jumped on every guy's case that even looked at her funny. Maybe I should back off some. I noticed Tessa was going through the drawers. I wanted to walk up behind her and give her a hug but right now that might not be the best thing. So I merely try and look over her shoulder to see what she is digging for. 'Tessa Bear, what are you looking for?' I asked her, trying to sound as sincere as I could. “Money.” I answered honestly as I finally found some money buried under a sweater of mine. I smiled and yanked it out stuffing in neatly into the front pocket of my jeans and then shutting the drawer before looking up at Chris who was curious as to just why I needed this money. “Money? Why do you need money?” He scrunched his face slightly as his eyes narrowed in confusion, figuring that he would be the one that treated me if we went out anywhere. “I’m going shopping.” I answered in a simple tone as I headed back into the hall with Chris following close behind like a lost puppy. “Shopping? For what? You already have enough clothes.” He followed me down the stairs to the coat closet standing as close as he could without actually having contact with me still trying to figure out just why I was leaving him behind. “For whatever. I’ll be back.” I answered as I rushed out the front door shutting the door behind me. I looked behind and felt like a child who was leaving its prized teddy beat at home. I could not help but feel guilty even if I wanted to be alone, but I turned my head and continued walking to my car doing my best to block out the feelings of guilt that I was having. The door slammed in my face. Well maybe that was a little harsh. It actually just closed in my face. But it still sent a message I didn't like. I opened the door and walked out to see Tessa already getting in the car. I walked up to the window and tapped on it. Tessa rolled it down slightly for me. 'Can I at least drive you there? I don't like the idea of you driving out on the icy roads by yourself. It's dangerous.' I told her. I was worried about her driving, but I was more worried about what ever was on her mind then the icy on the road. Tessa sighed and opened the door. She walked around the van and got into the passenger side. I hopped into the driver's side. 'What about the kids though?' she asked me. I knew if I couldn't think of a good excuse then I would be staying put. I thought quickly of something to say. 'I'll just drop you off and pick you up later. It won't take long to get there since it's so close to the mall as it is. So there is no need to worry about it.' Unable to resist Chris’ pleas I finally gave in and strapped myself into the passenger side seat. I let my head rest up against the cold window as my mind went to work. There I go giving into him again; I don’t think I’ll ever learn until it’s too late. But maybe everything will be fine. Maybe he does really care, maybe I’m just overreacting. I let out a sigh as Chris pulled out of the driveway and began his drive towards the mall, but not before beginning further investigation as to where I was going and why I was going there. “So what stores are you going to?” He asked as casually as he possibly good to keep from sounding suspiciously overprotective or just plain nosy. “No where real important. Maybe the GAP or Old Navy. No where in particular honestly.” I answered without any sign of aggravation although I was getting rather annoyed with all the questions that were being asked. Couldn’t he see that I just wanted to be alone and that I needed some time to myself? She was annoyed with me and I could tell. Today was already starting to be a bad day. I could just tell. I just wanted it all to end and for things to go back to normal. But maybe things never will be normal. Perhaps my proposal to marriage was a little too extreme, but I can't take it back now can I? I just watched the road ahead of me, writing down where she said she would be so I could remember it later. I could see the mall up ahead. The entire car ride had been silent. I didn't want to press any further into her business and she just stared out of the window at the free world like a bird trapped in a cage. With everything seeming for the worst, I had to ask myself something. Could this be the death of the Schmoopies? Friday when Tessa and I have to step into the ring to battle for the Tag Team titles, everything will come out. Our partnership will either break under the stress or blossom with gold. Suddenly Friday seemed a long ways away.
