A Date With A Very Bald Destiny (2/2)

25 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/06/02 Despite all his dastardly actions, Kannon has never really paid for his actions.... Against Jeremy Howard he won the Title from Howard, and although was knocked out in revenge he ultimately won when the Krayzie One was fired.... Against Duke he may have taken the beating of his life but managed to pull out the win... And against Miya, he has so far suffered severe annoyance and frustration.... But there has been no major loss. But eventually his actions must catch up with him, and it wasn’t by a wrestler.... But rather a religion. The Armies of the Hari Krishnas are looking down upon Hubbardville and are ready to advance, with Kannon, and at her own naive insistence, Ellie~! in tow, heading to meet them. Has the Scientologists charmed existence run out? And will the man who has vowed to take Miyagawa out before Sunday even make it to Turmoil to try? Or will he once again find a back door to weasel out through? “ALL RIGHT YOU LITTLE BALD MIDGETS, WHAT DO YOU WANT?!?!?!” Our shot opens up on a furious Xavier Kannon as he marches, his valet in tow, towards the commander of the Hari Krishna army. Xavier : “The LAST thing I need are a Million little Buddha Gnomes running around MY City.... So what do you want? Wigs to cover your stupid bald heads.... Some decent clothes so you don’t have top wear that fruity Orange? Booster shoes so you are over 4 foot?” The face of the Commander shows disbelief, thinking he finally had Kannon surrendering to him only to be once again insulted by the temperamental Scientologist himself. Hari : “WHAT?!?!? We have you defeated! We have your City in our grasp.... STOP MAKING FUN OF US!!!” Eleanor, standing just off Kannon’s shoulder steps up to his defence. Eleanor : “But.... It’s.... Just so EASY!!! I mean you’re all little, and bald.... Can I keep that one on the end?” She points to the man on the far left of the row of officers behind the Commander, infuriating the leader further. Hari : “NO YOU CAN NOT KEEP ONE OF US!!!“ Ellie~!’s eyes roll up as the little man jumps enraged on the spot. Eleanor : “Touch... EY!!! ” The 2 lovers turn to each other and nod while the Commander of the Hari Krishna Army composes himself under the watchful eyes of his troops. Hari : “Look... There are lots of us.... We are surrounding your City.... We have you out numbered and we have already beaten one of you.... KIND.... To death!” He gestures toward two of his troops who are dragging the bloodied and bruised corpse of Giovanni Ribisi to him before dumping it on the floor in front of the couple, Ellie~!’s eyes lighting up. Eleanor : “Like... WAY COOL!!! Is ALL of that blood HIS!?!?!?” Kannon prods the lifeless body with his foot. Xavier : “Wow...... I appreciate this gesture of friendliness. There I was thinking you wanted us all dead and then you go and kill that retard for us.... On behalf of the citizens of Hubbardville I accept your gift. Again the short little bald man jumps up on the spot, the first couple of Scientology covering their eyes as his Orange robe flies up. Xavier : “That’s one more memory to repress...” Eleanor : “And the mouse has left the house...” The bald leader of the Krishnas quickly grabs his robe and pulls it tight to him, deciding the time to be jested is over. Hari : “ENOUGH!!! It was not a GIFT! We killed him because he is a SCIENTOLOGIST!!! And we HATE him because he is one... We KILLED him because he is one.... That and he sucked in “Gone in 60 Seconds”!” The couple nod along as they continue to look down on the dead eyes of Giovanni Ribisi who looks like he just gatecrashed a Dark Age meeting dressed as Bobby Stormbringer. Xavier : “Yeah, that’s what pisses us off about him too.... If he was any other faith we’d tolerate him but as a Scientologist, he had to die.... Looks like we both won!” Hari : “NO!!! WE will WIN!!! YOU will LOSE!!! You will lose your CITY, your LIVES.... And THAT BIG HUBBARD STATUE!!!” Kannon turns to look down upon the City of Hubbardville, the glittering representation of Hubbard standing out in the skyline. Xavier : “Surely that would come under the section “City”?” Behind their Commander, the Hari Krishna Army grow restless and mumblings if discontent can be heard throughout the ranks... Something that their leader picks up on. Hari : “ENOUGH!!! WE WILL TAKE YOUR CITY NOW! IT WILL BELONG TO US..... YOUR TIME IS UP SCIENTOLOGIST AND YOU WILL PAY DEARLY FOR YOUR SMITES AGAINST US!!!” The Gold Patron Meritorious and his Preppy fiancé look to one another then back to the Hari Krishna. Xavier : “Smites? You’re shot and I call you short.... You’re bald and I call you bald.... You wear Orange and so do many Homosexuals.... Miyagawa is going to die and a Doctor will have to call that for the Death Certificate but I’m drifting off topic...” Hari : “I AM NOT SHORT!!! I’m only an inch or so shorter that you!” He points to Ellie~! who looks at him with sympathetic eyes, leaning towards him. Eleanor : “But I’m a GIRL!” His face now matches the colour of his robe, the words coming from Kannon and Ellie~! enraging him further in what is meant to be his moment of victory. Hari : “SILENCE.... THE DECLARATION OF WAR HAS BEEN MADE AND MY ARMY’S WILL NOW INVADE HUBBARDVILLE!!! ORANGE WILL BE FLYING FROM EVERY FLAGPOLE WITHIN THE HOUR!!!” The look on Kannon’s face is of defeat as he accepts the inevitable. Xavier : “Fine.... I’ll go and call the Lawyers so we can get this over with, I have to fly to Turmoil and my Flight leaves Hubbardville International in a few hours...” Hari : “LAWYERS!?!?!?” A message then flashes up on the screen that reads *30 MINUTES LATER* and we are shown the same hilltop view over Hubbardville, the bright Sun illuminating the towers of the City and the grass of the countryside. “So you would have this section of the Financial district marked here in Orange but with a corridor being allowed for Scientologists to get to the Kelly Preston Court development on the upper East side via Wearenotacult Avenue...” Between Kannon and Ellie~!, and the Hari Krishna’s stand a team of Lawyers who are looking over a map of the City. “But then that opens up the possibility of Ribisi Road becoming part of the Hari Krishna zone in exchange for Isaac Hayes Square which will lead to one less checkpoint thus making it easier for motorists.” “No, we won’t give up Isaac Hayes Square BUT we are willing to give up Jenna Elfman Gardens...” Xavier leans over one of the Lawyers shoulders and points to one of the places on the map. Xavier : “Um... My House is in their zone...” “Ah, okay.... So we move their East border back to here and compensate by giving them the West half of Kirstey Alley Boulevard...” “Yeah, that could wo-” “ENOUGH!!!! YOU HAVE WON THIS TIME KANNON! YOU MAY HAVE BROUGHT IN YOUR CRACK TEAM OF LAWYERS TO THWART US BUT WE WILL FIND ANOTHER WAY TO GET OUR REVENGE!!!” The angry little man waves his fist at Kannon, deterred by the legal jargon and the contract that is growing at a rate of 5 pages a minute... Giving in and giving his troops the order to retread. Eleanor : “If you REALLY want to get him we’re getting married in Lexington on Sunday, tha-” Xavier nudges her on the shoulder much to her annoyance. Eleanor : “Like.... OWWWZIES!!!!” The Hari Krishna just sneers as the Lawyers start to fold their map up and calculate their bill. Hari : “We would..... But we spent all out money booking the Coaches to get us here.... BUT DO NOT RELAX KANNON! THE HARI KRISHNA FAITH WILL NOT REST UNTIL YOU PAY FOR ALL THE THINGS YOU HAVE DONE TO US!!!” He turns and marches along behind the rest of his Army away from the City of Hubbardville as our shot starts to fade to Black. Eleanor : “What a nice guy.... He head was all shiny!” Kannon raises the eyebrows above his bright Green eyes and turn to his fiancé, shaking his head as our shot hits Black. “Krishnas down..... Japo-American to go....”
