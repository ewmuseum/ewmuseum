All Apologies

4 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: All Apologies Featuring: Tim Walden Date: November 4, 2002 'I'm sorry. I'm really, really sor--' 'I'm sorry, but Miss Wilson can't come to the phone right now. If you are calling to get an interview time for a position with Primetime Championship Wrestling, leave your name, phone number, and the number assigned to the resume you faxed in, if there is one. If you are not called back within one week, assume that you suck beyond the limits the job allows. If you are not attempting to obtain a position, please give your name, number and reason for calling, and she will try to reach you if she deems it worth her time. Have a nice day.' It beeps..'Hey Brandi...Have you talked to Nathan lately?'..I couldn't talk to her straight now, after what happened last night..It was really still a bit of a blur thanks to that accursed shape and his attack putting me in no condition to count a pin for the time being...But still my focus of the time was 'count the pin for Brandi, get out of here, maybe get some vengeance on that damn Trapezoid'...That was my one major error, and several mistakes piled into it to get me where I am now. I could have counted that three instead of letting myself get distracted by the damn Tron in the first place. I could have called a disqualification against Tomas, but I figured if Brandi was still winning she wouldn't want that..I could have at the least made sure that he hadn't cheated to place Tomas in a pinning position, but I just didn't think..This led to now, sitting in my hotel room and trying to figure out how to get back on her good side. I'd tried leaving an apology with Nathan...At least he'd try to talk to her, and maybe listen more than if I tried to go about it myself..But for now I had to get to work, it was all I really could do..Reaching for my coat, I slipped it on and walked over to the door, picking up the briefcase as I left. I continued down the hall, a little walk to the lobby as I started to think over lunch, or at least a snack at the office..I reached the front desk quickly, just a little anxious as to my day's hopes..'Hi, I'm Mr. Walden. Is my limo here yet?' The man at the desk quickly grabbed a little note-paper, looking it over before wandering over to the window and opening it....'Well, I don't think we have any other limousines scheduled, so that's probably yours.' I should've hoped so, since if the limo hadn't showed up, I'd have had to have taken one of those horrid pedestrian transports..God only knew where those taxis had been, even if they sounded nice from the name. I hurriedly ran out the front doors, rushing to the limo and hopping into the back. 'Good morning Jasper, same destination as always.' I may have had trouble on the horizon, but I didn't want to miss a moment of my working day at the offices... Jasper turned around to acknowledge the response. 'All right, sir.' And with that, we started to move forward, and I suddenly realized how alone I was, nothing really to do in the back of a limousine than just sit and think...Which wouldn't be such a bad thing if there were something nice to think about, but today I had something to worry about..Tuesday night, I had a match that could end either of two ways.....Success, and I would hold a decided mental advantage over the Trapezoid with the fact of his second defeat at my hands, an opportunity to gain tag team gold, and additionally increase my power and the Front Office's domination by taking away Teresa's freedom..But it was the possibilities of defeat that held my attention now, for while my confidence was still in that we could win the match if we worked together as well as I had with Xavier, I realized that this possibility was more distant than before as well..My victory now depended on my partner, and my partner was still angry over helping cost her the match, and though it probably wasn't good for the Front Office's stability to hold a grudge, she was the sort who would turn on you if she felt she had something to gain from it... She couldn't stay THAT mad though, could she? To abandon me for the match would be ludicrous...We had a tag title shot coming to us should we win, and the HIT was to blame for my disorientation that cost her the match, not to mention the injuries she and Nathan had suffered before...Wouldn't she rather get her revenge on them with a win to prove that we were better than they were? But the fact remained that I'd made the count, and I couldn't help but worry that the Front Office's stability was threatened by the temptation to just leave me alone to lose, lose the match and control over my career, allow my own opponents in the TV Title match Friday to hold power over me..I had to be sure I could win. I had to make it up somehow, to be certain that I still had the support of my teammate...The offices were in sight now, and it was then I realized.. Maybe I was getting ahead of myself. Maybe she had already talked to Nathan about it and realized I was really, truly sorry, maybe she'd forgiven me already and there was nothing to worry about now, just getting too paranoid over the repercussions, she was just angry then and it had passed...It was only an undeserved loss, not like it was the end of the world for her..In any case, I had business to attend to, and it was only the start of my day anyway...There were more important things to be concerned about, taxes to enforce and institute, suitable valet wardrobes to select...'We're here, sir.' 'Thanks, Jasper.'..The walk to the PCW building was short, as I checked in at reception and walked to my office..Then suddenly, as I was pulling out my office key, I heard a clopping sound of footsteps from behind me, hurriedly from the frequency of the noise. I put the key in the door and turned to see what was of such great importance, surprised to find Nathan jogging toward me, an envelope tucked in his hand.. 'Mr. Walden!' The athletic young man hurried up, stopping before me and holding out the envelope before I could get a word out. 'Miss Wilson told me I should give this to you. She said it's about last Friday, didn't say anything else though..' 'Oh, thanks Nathan..' I took the envelope, a degree of uncertainty evident in my speech...And Nathan just stood there. 'Er, I think it'd be best if I opened this in my office, you can go tell Brandi I got her message now.' He looked rather disappointed, but still turned and started to walk away, as I opened the office door, and the first thing that happened afterward was my placing the letter on the desk and dropping the briefcase to my side, as I hurriedly gripped the letter opener and unfolded the paper within, heart racing faster than my mind could comprehend the words..I stopped, breathing in and out slowly to calm my emotions, before looking back to the paper. [I]Tim: Nathan told me about how sorry you are about counting the pin on Fusion, and I really just didn't give a damn about it to be honest. You had all the time in the world to count that 3 the first time, and then you didn't even pay attention to who was on top in the end. I thought you'd have at least enough sense to make sure you knew who was getting pinned before you made a count like that, but you managed to do the impossible and screw up something that easy. I know that it'd be best for us as a team to work together, since we both still hate those two and it would only hurt the Front Office's power to have this friction, but though Nathan was really persistent about your honest interest in making it up to me and winning the match, I was honestly a little tempted to not show up and let you end up Tomas's bitch for the month. However, I know it's best that we stick together, since if we don't work together here, it'll create an uncomfortable working situation and the last thing we need is more enemies. I accept your apology, but just don't mess up again. --Brandi.[/I] ...The message was blunt to the point at which I was struck by at least a little fear for my actions, to the point in which relief was almost overwhelmed by my insecurities..However, one thing was important above all else. She might be mad, but she was still on my side. ---- View Tim Walden's Biography
