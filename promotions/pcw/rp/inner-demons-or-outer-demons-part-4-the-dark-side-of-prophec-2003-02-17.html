Inner Demons, or Outer Demons...Part 4...The Dark Side of Prophecy

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-17-2003 “So many enemies, so little time. Goodness is a quality that so many men lack in the world that is PCW. Aye, and that be the least of mine worries. It would appear that I am not who I believe I am as of late. My mind is no longer totally mine own to command. Strange happenings as of late. The dreams of mine sleep turning up on the front page of the morning news. Troubling times indeed…What if ‘tis I who am the Night King? What if I am fighting the wrong thing, for the wrong reason? BAH! Goodness shall overcome…I always do!” And the shot opens to show a vibrant sunset, against the backdrop of a rolling green field. The shot pans down and we see the recognizable stones of Stonehenge. A fire burns in the center of the stones, and a group of hooded, black cloaked men circle the campfire. A pale bald headed man stands at the head of the circle. He speaks, and is the only one who does not wear his hood and cowl. His eyes are dark, and his tone somber as he speaks to the others who have gathered…. Man: Long have we waited my brothers…Long have we waited for the coming of the one who would bring glory back to our hallowed bloodlines. Time passes, and ages come and go, but we Druids have stood strong together for millennia. We are the wisdom, and we are the way. Long have the masses questioned our sacred arts, our ritualistic ideals…No more. With the coming of the king, we shall be redeemed… A slight man in a golden cloak seems to appear behind the bald headed man. This man seems aged beyond anything that should still draw breath. As he appears, the druids, including the one at the head of the circle, kneel and prostrate themselves before the aged one. They keep this position as he begins to speak, his voice like paper being rubbed together…. Aged One: Our time is at hand. Long have we waited in the shadows, lived in the night. People have misunderstood us…For good reason have they feared us. But now; it is of little meaning. The prophecy is in motion. The Warrior of Light is already playing into our hands. He will fail, and the dark prophecy…The prophecy that the foolish Christians have dared to question will come into play. And darkness will ravage the world. The Christians are want to believe that they have the true prophecy. That the Golden Warrior will appear and vanquish evil whenever he comes into contact with it. Our prophecy reads a tad different…. The aged one raises his arms and the Druids raise their faces to the sky, and an ominous chant fills the air… AND THE ONE OF LEGEND WILL APPEAR….ALL GOOD WILL FADE….AND FOR ONE THOUSAND YEARS….DARKNESS WILL REIGN….AND THE ONE OF LEGEND WILL APPEAR….ALL GOOD WILL FADE….AND FOR ONE THOUSAND YEARS….DARKNESS WILL REIGN….AND THE ONE OF LEGEND WILL APPEAR….ALL GOOD WILL FADE….AND FOR ONE THOUSAND YEARS….DARKNESS WILL REIGN…. Finally, the chant fades and the Aged One lowers his arms, and the Druids once more sink into a position of prostration… Aged One: Already the chosen figure fights himself. He knows not what reality is any longer. Questions shall be his downfall, and thus the downfall of the world…And we will rejoice with the coming of the darkness…. With a wave of his arm, the fire rises higher and the image of a man slowly comes into focus. A sword wielder, we instantly recognize the hulking form of Venneman Pendragon. Next to this figure rises another, darker form. Same shape, same man, but different. The two stare at one another before engaging in combat. Eventually, the darker Pendragon succumbs to the skill of the original, and the two figures become smoke. The image that now arises is one of chaos…Armageddon. A world devoid of light, and hope. The Aged One allows a bit of drool to escape his mouth as he looks upon this sight, and the other Druids emit sounds of awe. The bald man sits with a look of complete rapture upon the scene in the flames. Aged One: He will destroy his lesser self to preserve what he believes right. And then, we shall have him. Darkness reigns…. ***************************************************************************** The scene cuts to a moonlit courtyard. Hedges enclose the grass, and a shirtless man goes through the forms with his sword. Sweat glistens off of his chest, although by the steam that rises from his body, we get the feeling that it is quite cold outside. Back and forth he slashes and dashes, fighting enemies that we cannot see, nor would we care to. In times past, the man would be king of a country. In the here and now, he settles for being the only Knight in a federation filled with evil….. “So many worries. So much on my plate. Dante Jackson sniffing around my knightly ways…..” SLASH! A hard thrust and then a mighty swing at nothing…. “The foulest owner of the company that one would imagine….Syko. My old nemesis come back to haunt me…” Pendragon kicks and then punches at the air with the hilt of Excalibur. “And couple this with a newfound respect for the Lord King from the company I have done so much for. Tuesday I team with the self proclaimed Black Knight…BLAH! He lacks the knight’s heart, the knight’s courage. He is unfit to feed grain to Xerxes.” A spinning roundhouse kick and then a low thrust and Pendragon drops into a defensive stance…. “Xerxes. Hope he is able to keep himself….Contained at the next show. Hate to see mine horse impale another fool. Couple all this with mine title shot on Fusion, and I have a lot to worry about….” AND WHAT OF ME, PENDRAGON? HAVE THEE COME TO TERMS WITH THE FACT THAT I AM THEE, THO ART ME? Pendragon: I hear nothing…I focus completely on retaining King’s Gold on Friday. Tommy has wronged me, and he shall feel the punishment of Pendragon…. THOU ART CORRECT. THE MAN WHO CALLS HIMSELF TUBULAR HAS WRONGED ME. ‘TIS HIGH TIME HE HAD HIS COME UPPANCE. THEE AND I, I AND THEE. WE SHALL SHOW TO THE WORLD THE METAL THAT PENDRAGON STANDS FOR. WE WILL DELIEVER DEVINE PUNISHMENT TO THE USURPER AND TAKE WHAT IS RIGHTFULLY OURS…. Pendragon: Sense? How is it that thee speaketh words of truth now, spirit? What treachery has thee in mind now? Thy words are a mirror of mine own thoughts? How can this be? LORD OF THE NIGHT, KING OF DARKNESS….LORD OF LIGHT…. Pendragon: And the winds blow cold…a change rides upon the gusts…Good Christ protect me… The scene fades as Pendragon lets his sword arm lower and shakes his head in bewilderment at the voice of the apparition….
