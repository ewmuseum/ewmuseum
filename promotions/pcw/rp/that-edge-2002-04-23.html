That Edge!

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/23/02 (The scene fades to black then fades inside an old boxing gym. Inside the gym the lights on the ceiling are on dimly, duck taped up punching bags are in place, the wallpaper on the walls are showing the brick wall. Little midgets are all punching the punching bag their hardest. Big Daveylicious enters wearing black Addidas pants, a PCW shirt, Oakley sunglasses, and the Extreme title around his waist. He is followed by his lackey, Michael Graves. Big D’s agent, Tommy Stillinger directs them towards a African American man in red boxing trunks and a tank top. He greets them.) Tommy Stillinger: Allow me to introduce to you the man who will get you ready for Chef, Apollo Creed. Apollo: Glad, you boys can make it. Big Daveylicious: Isn’t Apollo Creed just a movie character from Rocky? Apollo: Listen, man! You had this when you won the FSW World Heavyweight title. You had this when you won the PCW Extreme title. The eye of the tiger! It’s time to rise up! Big Daveylicious: But don’t I need a woman for that? (Apollo looks over to the midgets in the gym.) Apollo: Hey, guys! (The little midgets turn around and crowd Apollo with Big D.) Apollo: See these guys have the eye of the tiger. Big Daveylicious: They don’t have the eye of the tiger. They got the teeth of the tiger. You ever see how these midgets bite people in the balls? Michael Graves: I know from experience. It’s very exciting. Apollo: They prefer to be called “little” people. Big Daveylicious: Why do you try and call themselves something they are not? Hell, their midgets. They might be small but they bite you in the balls. (Suddenly, one of the midgets break free from the crowd to face Big D. Big D doesn’t notice him until the little person tugs on his shirt.) Big Daveylicious: What?!? Midget: Excuse me sir, but just because a little person bite Mike Myers tally whacker in Austin Powers 2 doesn’t mean we bite balls.Big Daveylicious: I’ve seen it with my two eyes! Don’t you try and lie to me about that. Because when I am not looking at you, you will bite me in my balls! (The midget grows a tear in his eye.) Midget: Will you stop harassing me? I have feelings too. Just because I am small doesn’t mean I bite balls. (Apollo pats the midget on the shoulder.) Apollo: Their there my small friend. Midget: Shut up. Apollo: Just let your feelings flow. Burst ‘em out. Come on let the emotion run out. Midget: Shut the hell up! Apollo: Don’t you hold those tears; come on let ’em out. (The midget uppercuts Apollo in the balls. Causing Apollo to fall on his knees in pain.) Big Daveylicious: That goes to show don’t mess with a pissed off midget. Apollo:(In Pain.) See that! That’s the eye of the tiger! Big Daveylicious: No, that’s a pissed off midget. (The midget stomps on the ground with rage.) Midget: It’s little person, damn it! Big Daveylicious: I don’t give a damn, midget! Midget: That’s it if you call me midget one more time I’m gonna bust your balls like I did to Apollo! (Quickly Michael Graves stands in front of Big D and blocks the midget from harming Big D in any way he can.) Michael Graves: Your not gonna bust his balls! You have to get through me first! I can see your lies. I know you guys bite people in the balls! I have proof! Big Daveylicious: Proof? (Michael Graves unzips his pants as Big D looks away and revels his bites on his balls.) Michael Graves: See look. Here are bites from my several encounters with midgets biting me in the nads. (Big D shakes his head in disgust. The midgets eyes get really wide, we even notice some drool fall on the floor dripping from the midgets. The midgets look like they have that edge. They have the “Eye Of The Tiger”! The midget who uppercut Apollo opens his mouth and runs towards Graves, jumps, and bites him in the balls with a really tight grip. Michael Graves is in pain.) Michael Graves: Ahhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh, the pain! Apollo: See that Big D, the Eye of the Tiger! Big Daveylicious: If biting somebody's balls gives me the Eye of the Tiger I don’t want any part of it. Apollo: You don’t have to bite people in the balls to have the eye of the tiger you can do several different things to obtain the eye of the tiger. (Apollo gets up but is still holding his crotch and leads Big D to the boxing ring. As they slide their way into the boxing ring they both each get a pair of boxing gloves off of the ropes which they were hanging from. Michael Graves somehow escapes the grasps of the midgets, and pull his pants up, and runs out of the Gym.) Michael Graves:( Running Out) Screw you guys I am getting the real eye of the tiger by myself! (We lose our view of Graves, and concentrate on Apollo and Big D.) Apollo: Now, boy your gonna see if you got the eye of the tiger! (They both get into their boxing stance, Creed starts with the first couple of punches at Big D but Big D blocks them. Big D kicks Creed in the balls causing Creed to stop his punching so he can hold onto his balls in pain. Big D throws the gloves off his hands. At this moment we can tell Big Daveylicious has the Eye Of The Tiger!) Apollo: Stop! Big D you got it my man! That edge! The Eye of the Tiger! (Big D pauses for a second, nods his head, and delivers a Daveylicious Bomb onto Apollo Creed! Suddenly, the midgets look at Big D with that edge, ya know the eye of the tiger! Big D also has the eye of tiger look on them. The midgets slide into the ring. One of the midgets scream something inside the ring.) Midget: Battle Royal! (All the midgets are battling it out as Big D stands in one corner having his devilish smile on his face, and notices Apollo is grabbing the ropes to pull himself up for leverage.) Apollo: Can’t we all just get along? Why can’t we all just live peacefully like little trolls and princesses? (Big D levels Apollo with a kick to the face causing him to fall down on the mat.) Big Daveylicious: That’s the last thing I want. (Unbelievably, the man we know as Michael Graves is running halfway towards the ring, stops, he holds up a eyeball, and points to it.) Big Daveylicious: What the... Michael Graves: Look Big D! I did it! I got the Eye Of The Tiger! (We see a tiger with an missing eye running behind Graves and bites his pants off, knocking Graves over on his back, Graves is in a panic as the tiger spots Graves sac, and we then face Big D faces as we hear a crunch.) Michael Graves: My balls!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Ohhhhhhhhhhhhhhh! (We see a twitch in Big D’s face as the scene fades to black.)
