Shopping for the Baby Shower

2 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/2/02 End:'Hurry up, guys. We don't have all day.' As The Misfits walk around the store, looking for stuff to buy, The End urges them to make final choices. Standing beside The End at the cereal section are Lexicon and Kay Fabe (Yes, all you longtime readers and fans - Lexicon and Kay Fabe are back!). End:'How was the vacation?' Kay Fabe:'It was good. Nothing happened to me since Lexicon couldn't break kayfabe.' End:'Of course, he can't do anything until it's written in to happen to you, and nothing can happen to you if the handler didn't write anything about your whereabouts until now,' Kay Fabe:'Dammit! Why'd you have to break kayfabe!' Lexicon:'Exactly! Destroying the character of this hobby is my job.' Meanwhile, the long heavy cereal shelf falls on Kay Fabe. End:'Ouch...' Lexicon:'He'll be down for quite a while. Looks like we'll be preserving kayfabe from this moment forward.' End:'Yeah... You go on ahead, I have to do something first...' Up in the Realm of the Celestials, the 9 foot tall Golden Kayfabe Ape (I claim one-time use of it since Akiyama hasn't used it in months) is resting comfortably in his pristine garden chambers, enjoying a peaceful nap. Suddenly, a flock of doves scatters. 'Hey, I didn't know John Woo was directing this RP!' The End drops down from the ceiling and locks a sleeper choke on it. The ape fights and struggles to get The End off its back, but its arms were never meant to bend back that way. Falling back, it crashes to the ground, smothering The End. He still does not let go of the choke. The ape rolls back and forth trying to get back up again, but The End locks his legs around the ape and won't let it move. Twenty seconds later, it's over and the ape is knocked out. The End uses all his strength to push the once-mighty Golden Kayfabe Ape to the side so that he may breathe. Exhausted, The End stands up and walks back to Wal-Mart. Lexicon:'And to what location had you made your excursion during our shopping venture?' End:'What? I just went to take care of something.' Lexicon:'Privileged information?' End:'A privilege you shall never receive.' Lexicon:'Oh.' The End and Lexicon walk around the store to the various departments where The Misfits are picking up what they wanted to buy. The first section is the electronics section, where Richard is waiting for them. On their way over, Lexicon asks The End a question that had been on his mind all the time The End was away pummeling the Golden Kayfabe Ape. Lexicon:'Do you and Miyagawa have any close friendship? Anything that would make you favor him,, and he you?' End:'Not that I know of.' Lexicon:'I see.' They continue walking past the bloody linen section. I say this because a 70% off sale on all linens, pillows, and facecloths and towels has turned into a free-for-all of housewives exchanging blows in order to get their hands on the last few pieces of merchandise. Lexicon paused a moment as he took notice of the melee, but now he finishes his question. Lexicon:'So the question I must ask is why are you having the Misfits buy presents Miyagawa?'' End:'Not sure. Even though I don't like him, but I don't hate him; and besides, they're expecting a child soon enough, so we might as well send them some presents since I either missed the baby shower or there just hasn't been one yet.' Lexicon:'Quite a polite and noble deed for you to charge yourself to undertake.' End:'A rare, awkward moment for me, as well. I'm sure I'll get over this soon. In the meantime, let's see what the Misfits picked out for the young expecting couple.' Finally, The End and Lexicon reach the electronics department. Richard is standing in front of a flat-screen 60-inch television/DVD player. Lexicon assesses the product for Richard. Lexicon:'I believe that Miyagawa may have already purchased something like this.' Richard shakes his head. Richard:'No, no, no. Richard is purchasing this television specifically for the child. It can watch The End on the TV, and use the DVD to watch The End's greatest moments.' Richard pats the TV with his right hand and leans on it. It falls to the floor. Quickly, Richard moves over two TVs down, and when someone comes to investigate, Richard points to a random passerby and points, shouting, 'It was him!' The innocent shopper is dragged away and charged with destroying merchandise. Lexicon:'What about Miyagawa, the child's father?' Richard shrugs his shoulders with the blink of an eye. Richard:'So what about Miyagawa? He sucks.' Lexicon folds his arms in a disappointed manner, frowning at Richard. Lexicon:'You can't be serious.' Richard:'You're right. Oh! This TV has a 3-inch picture-in-picture option. The kid can have the small picture for Miya, and the bigger picture for The End since he's more deserving to be watched in 60-inch wide screen.' Lexicon places his hands on his hips and turns his back to Richard to go to the next section. The End follows, speaking back to Richard. End:'I'm not going to argue about your intended purpose for use of this TV. However, I would highly recommend that should you send along a card that you keep what you just said to yourself.' Richard nods and calls someone to retrieve the TV for him. Richard:'Okay.' Over in the toddler's section, Drek Redaxe has picked out a number of clothing ensembles that would make any parent jump to have their kids wear them. Once again, The End is at a loss, and Lexicon must critique the intended purchase. In this case, he doesn't have much to say. Lexicon:'Osh-Kosh overalls with white shoes and a red shirt. Orange shirt, blue pants combination. A black-and-white striped little piece... and yellow booties! Drek, you did a great job! The matching outfits and colors, the baby will look great in these clothes!' Drek furrows his brow, looking at Lexicon as if he just spoke in tongues. Drek Redaxe:'Beh-bee? Wha' beh-bee?' The End, Lexicon, the other Misfits, the Golden Kayfabe Ape, Kay Fabe, and the reader all slap their heads in unison. At the mention of a 'reader,' the ceiling above Kay Fabe (still trapped beneath the cereal shelf) collapses and falls on top of the hapless Misfit. The ceiling itself isn't that heavy, he just gets a little dirty and water from the overhead pipes starts falling on him. Noticing the Golden Kayfabe Ape is back up, The End knocks him in the head with a lunging superkick, knocking him out again. End:'Stay down!' Lexicon taps The End on the shoulder. Lexicon:'Why did you knock out the employee?' End:'It's not the Golden Kayfabe Ape?' Lexicon:'What in the name of Duke Williams are you talking about?' The End shuts in on himself and rejects any outside statements or comments, brushing them off. End:'Nothing. Nothing at all.' Drek will buy the clothes, but once The End informs him that the purpose of the shopping trip was to get presents for Miyagawa's child (via his wife in a long-distance anonymous baby shower), Drek decides to get the child a toolset. Drek:'You 'ave to be good with yoor ha'nds when you're little. It builds muscle and character.' Lexicon:'I assure you, a newborn child wouldn't even be capable of using a hacksaw, let alone would the parents allow it.' Drek:'Yoor're just a jealoos girly mahn.' Drek uses The End's Capital One Visa No Hassle Card to pay for the items (and the legions of aliens ignore him and instead attack the man with a regular credit card, all the while the richest chimps in the world watch the action from their diamond-encrusted CCTV monitors). Walking to the next department, The End turns to Lexicon. End:'What did you get the kid?' Lexicon:'I have already ordered Miyagawa's child the Lexicon comic book series, complete with Lexicon trading card game. Also on the list of items I have sent to Lexicon's child are a life-size poster of myself and Lexic-O's, the delicious breakfsat cereal that's as verbose as it is nutriotious.' The End walks ahead, not wanting to be associated with Lexicon after such a shameless promotion of his own products Lexicon:'Well, what did you get?' End:'I'm not good at this sort of thing. I just leave it to you guys.' Lexicon:'Very well, then. We're coming up on Slade and Lamar, let's see what they've found for Natalia Miyagawa.' In the sporting goods section, Slade catches up with The End and Lexicon. He has someone with him. Lexicon:'What did you get the baby?' Slade:'Jyk lhadmenaf. Lexicon:'This guy? Who's this?' Slade:'Pfdn sadoppjwjk.' Lexicon:'Hoobastank? Who or what is Hoobastank?' The End forms his hand into a tiger claw before breaking Hoobastank's nose with a palm thrust. Hoobastank falls to the ground shaking and twitching. Slade:'Enr oiasdjf ekljhnru iodn nd.' End:'What do you mean what did I do that for? You know I hate Hoobastank.' Lexicon:'But the present wasn't for you.' End:'And so we're going to give Hoobastank to Natalia Miyagawa?' Slade:'Iasd uiashefp iausnpjk sdopiasdh joiasdnf poahja sdoa pd phm awmioasd fso pi em rpowir zmxv nzxcliv elf awlf alifjk oz mfaso iedf a soimo sdi fao skdfma sodim knfasemnah.'{Translated - Well, she seems to like Asian-Americans, and Miya's not much of a catch. He was just her ticket out of Russia. So, I saw Hoobastank, Chad from the Neptunes, and Chow Yun Fat. The others kept insisting that they were spoken for, so I took this guy.} Slade and Lexicon look down at Hoobastank's prone body. Lexicon:'Well, it's the thought that counts...' End:'If that's the best you can do, pick him up, clean up the blood, and throw him in the car. I don't think we'll have to pay for him.' Slade drags Hoobastank away. Lexicon turns around and sees a double-barreled shotgun staring him in the face. Lexicon:'Cripes!' Lexicon ducks down to the floor. Lamar pulls the gun away.' Lamar:'I wanna get this for what's-her-name.' End:'Natalia.' Lamar:'Yeah. Wait, no, that's not it.' Lexicon stands back up and smacks Lamar on the arm. Lexicon:'Careful with that thing!' Lamar:'It's okay-' Before Lamar can finish his thought, the gun goes off. It blasts away bits of ceiling, where it was aimed. Lexicon:'They keep loaded shotguns on the shelves?' End:'Thanks to Charleton Heston and the IRA, yes.' Lexicon:'Don't you mean NRA?' End:'Nope. IRA -- Ignorant Rifle-Owners Association.' Lexicon:'And exactly why would a shotgun be a good choice of present for Natalia Miyagawa?' Lexicon takes the rifle away from Lamar and puts it back on the counter. Lamar:'Well, I remember my mom saying that when she was in labor with me that she wanted to shoot my father in the head with a shotgun, and I figured that Natalie-' End:'Natalia.' Lamar:'Natalia might want to do the same thing. So I thought let's get her a shotgun and if she wants to shoot Miya in the head with it she'll be able to.' Lexicon stands with his mouth wide open. The End shakes his head. End:'Now you see why I don't take you anywhere?' Lamar grabs the gun off of the counter and puts it in the shopping cart, along with Hoobastank. Meanwhile, over at another register in the same department, a robber in blue shirt, jeans, and a ski mask is holding up the cashier with one of the department's loaded guns (thanks a lot Charleton Freaking Heston). The cashier is a carbon copy of the Pimply-Faced Intern Kid. Robber:'Give me all your money!' Cashier:'Okay, just please don't hurt me.' Sensing trouble, Juju leaps into action from the basketball hoop. He jumps down on the robber's head and claws out his eyes. Blinded, the robber cannot defend himself. He shoots wildly, hitting a bunch of sporting equipment but no people. Juju's thoughts run a mile a minute. Juju:'Wonk.'{Translated - I've got to take this guy out before he hurts anyone.} Juju moves down on the robber's shoulders and the 1-foot, 19-pound iguana with scales as white as snow executes a frankensteiner on the robber, flipping him over and spiking him on his head. The robber is alright down and out, but just to make sure, Juju climbs up onto the cashier's counter and jumps off with a shooting star press. Staying for the cover, Referee Bob Charlie runs out of nowhere and counts for Juju. Charlie:'1... 2... 3!!!' Juju stands upright on the robber's chest, looking down at his face. Juju:'Bitch. I mean, WONK!' The Misfits and everyone in the store cheers and applauds Juju for his heroism. Meanwhile, The End is voicing his disapproval. End:'No! This whole thing was messed up! It wasn't supposed to be like this! This was supposed to be a serious RP showing my good side!' Lexicon claps and answers The End in an aside. Lexicon:'Then you're SOL with the handler you've got.' Kay Fabe pulls himself out from underneath the shelf, but can't get good friction grip with the ground because of the water that keeps pouring on him. Then, he hears Lexicon and The End break kayfabe. Kay Fabe can only frown and cry as an electrical cord falls into the puddle of water formed beneath him.
