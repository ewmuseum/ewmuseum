Date: 12/11/02 |--Scene--|Opens up outside of a large futuristic city. You could see that the skies was a beautiful shade of light blue, which consist of a small amount of white fluffy clouds. Up in the skies, you were able to see many rail tracks, that belonged to Japanese-style bullet trains, above the many skyscapers that mostly made up the futuristic city. Above the tracks you were able to see futuristic flying cars, trucks, and motorcycles soaring across the blue heavens of this futuristic city. Soon able to see random shots of people on the streets. Some of the shots had people walking sidewalk. Could see that some people that were walking on the streets came in different sizes, colors, and races that you would see only in sci-fi books. |--Starwind narrates--|Welcome to Paradigm City.... No this isn't dream... And no this isn't a real place in the US. This is a place that was in another dimension. That's right I said, 'Another dimension.' This is one of the main dimension that made up the other 20 dimension. |--Scene--|Continuing to see random shots of people walking the streets, living their orignal lives in Paradigm City. |--Starwind narrates--|I moved to this dimension right after Raven died. Basically the Mortal Realm, which is the place that you called Earth, wasn't a place for me anymore. I mean I lost Rain and Raven there, so I decided to start a new life in a new world. |--Scene--|Showing a large futuristic building, that had a large fountain infront of the building. Could see the emblem belonging to the NDCP. Saw police cars leaving or entering inside of the NDCP main headquarters. |--Starwind narrates--| I also moved here, because this is also the main dimension where the main headquarters of the NDCP is located at. |--Scene--|Switches to the inside of the building. Able to see many people sitting at their computer desks, typing something on the keyboard. Soon you were able to see a familiar face at one of the desk, as it was no other than Rei. Rei was wearing leather onyx colored everything which consist of: a short tanktop that could see within a short jean jacket, tight pants, high heel combat boots. Finishing the attire off with her short white bandana tied around her forehead. She was looking down at the computer screen with a bored look on her eyes. |Rei|'This is so frickening boring!' |--Scene--|Everything froze as you were seeing a close-up of Rei's face. |--Starwind narrates--|This is my partner, Rei Moon. She's 19 year's old. A Scorpio. Blood Type... Well unknown, since she isn't human. She is a demon.... I know... I know... I should hate Rei, because of her race. But she is a pretty kool person once you get to know her. She use to be this major space pirate, but she saw the error of her ways. Who I'm kidding. The main reason she's in the line of this work is because she was forced by the Head Council on Eden. Basically she could either be sentence to an enteral sleep or become a NDCP and become a free demon. And well... The rest is history.... |--Scene--|Soon notice a man approaching her desk, which he was looking down at Rei. The man looked like a nerdy guy, as he was holding many folders in his hands. |Nerdy Guy|'Rei.' |Rei saying in a dull tone|'What is it?' |Nerdy Guy saying in an amusing tone|'The boss wants to see you and Starwind in his office.' |--Scene--|She sighed softly in a dull way. |Rei saying in a dull tone|'Perfect...' |Nerdy Guy saying in an amusing tone while leaving|'You two are in trouble...' |--Scene--|Noticing Rei lifting a finger up, spun it slightly. With the spin of that finger, that caused the nerd to lose his balance and fall flat on his face. Heard a sound of glasses breaking. Rei stood up from her seat, having an innocent look on her face. |Rei saying in a playful tone|'Are you ok?' |--Scene--|She snickered as she started to walk away from the scene. She had a curious look on his face. |Rei says in mind|'Hmm? Speaking of my partner... Where the hell is he?' |--Scene--|Saw Rei blinking slightly as she had formed a curious look on his face. --|>Soon seeing Josh Starwind's opening video playing with his opening theme song, 'I Stand Alone by Godsmack,' playing in the background.
