I'll Be Watching

29 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/05/02 Yes that's right, I used your services a few times back in '99........... Sorry?............... Yes, well, I've been out of the country for a while......... really? The Supreme Machine laughs heartily as he listens to the person on the other end of the line.  He sits an the end of a neatly made bed listening intently to the red telephone hand set that is held to his right ear. He stretches his back and wipes his mane of jet black hair out of his face as he sits looking out the hotel window at the New Brunswick skyline.  He is dressed in a loose fitting black shirt and a pair of black jeans.  The telephone looks tiny in his massive hand. Yes it's great to be back.......... Well tonight I'd like two if that's possible........ He laughs again as he stands and walks to the window.  He continues to listen as he looks down at the traffic eight floors below. Exactly, you read my mind........... Great........... I'm at the Hyatt, do you need the address?......... No, I didn't think so.......... Room 802.......... How long will they take to get here?................ Great, I've got to go out later so that will be fine....... Do you remember where to charge this to?.............. Sure He continues to watch the pedestrians and cars through the window as he waits patiently for the person on the other end of the line.  Yes I'm still here......... Yes.......... Yes.......... That's the one......... Sure......... Yes, just charge it straight to them and they'll take care of it........... Great........ Thanks a lot..........You too.......Yes.......... Bye. He places the hand set down on the wooden bedside table and sits back down on the bed.  He lays down across the end of it and stretches his back for a few moments before standing back up and drawing the curtains across the one large window.  He switches on the bedside lamp and sits back down.  On the bed beside him, a magazine lays face down on the blue bedspread.  He reaches over and picks it up. The Supreme Machine sighs quietly as he reads the cover of the latest copy of Wrestling World magazine, with it's headline about him and Ricky Thunder coming to PCW.   He flicks through the pages until he finds the story and briefly scans over it.  He stops as he comes to the section that mentions his former tag team partner, Shane Thunder.  His face hardens as he recalls his friend's death from cancer not that long ago.  He had been at his side when he had finally succumbed to the disease that had eaten away at his lungs.  It was just before Thunder died that he had made The Supreme Machine promise to look after his younger brother Ricky.  It was this promise that had bought him back to the world of professional wrestling and into PCW. He shakes his head as he looks at a photo showing Shane Thunder at his peek, holding the CCW Heavyweight Title triumphantly in the air.  The tanned, muscular athlete with the mop of blonde hair looking happily out from the pages of the magazine is a far cry from the emaciated figure that The Supreme Machine had watched die in a sterile hospital bed.  He had first heard about his friend's illness while he had been holidaying in Bangkok, Thailand, and immediately hired a private jet so he could rush back to Australia to be by his side.  When he arrived, he found Thunder to be in the advanced stages of the disease and close to death.  Two days later, he was gone. After reading through the rest of the article, The Supreme Machine closes the magazine and tosses it onto the bedside table.  The glossy cover causes it to slide across the table and fall onto the floor.  He watches it fall but leaves it there.  The Supreme Machine's massive shoulders heave with another sigh as he stands and crosses the medium sized hotel room.  He stops at the minibar and makes himself a strong Scotch and Dry that he downs in one big mouthful.  He pauses for a moment, savoring the flavor. That one was for you Shane. The Supreme Machine raises the empty glass in a salute to his dead friend. To the good times. He goes to make another drink but stops himself. One's enough. He looks at his watch before turning and walking back to the bed.   Well, in a few hours it'll be time for Tuesday Turmoil.  I may not be on the card but that doesn't mean I won't be there.  Why else would I come to a place like New Brunswick?  It's not really high up there on my list of places to visit. No, I'll be in the arena alright.  The question is, what will I be doing there? The Supreme Machine smiles to himself as he sits down on the bed and begins to unbutton his shirt. Perhaps I'll just be a spectator, scouting the opposition, so to speak.  Or maybe I'll be making a personal appearance.  Who knows?  After all, it could only help the ratings having a legend such as myself on the program.  I guess you'll all just have to wait and see. He takes off the unbuttoned shirt and tosses it across the room.  It lands in a crumpled heap in an open suitcase sitting on the floor next to the door to the bathroom. Then next on the agenda we have Friday Fusion over in Teaneck, New Jersey.  Never been to Teaneck before but I can't say I'm looking forward to it.  Some of the places you have to go to when you're on the road are, well let's just say, they're not the end of the world but you can see it from there. The Supreme Machine slips his gold Rolex off his left wrist and puts it in the top drawer of the bedside table before standing and walking into the bathroom.  His voice echoes out of the white tiled room as he continues. Again, I have no match at Friday Fusion.  Sure I could have entered myself into the School's Out match but I find those matches to be...... well, beneath me, really.  I mean, brawling in a school yard?  No, I think I'll leave that to those less fortunate than myself.  I've never really been one for those big....what do they call them.... clusterfuck?  Yes that's it.  Anyway, I've never been one for those clusterfuck matches.  Three way matches, tag team, even four way, that's fine.  I just prefer to keep my hands clean and not get involved in dirty, pointless brawling.  After all, it's only for the PCW Extreme Title.  Not interested so sorry, you'll have to wait a little longer to see me in the ring. He reappears from the bathroom wearing only in a fluffy white towel tied around his waist that highlights his tanned torso.  He walks over and checks the time on his watch before going back into the bathroom.  The sound of running water is heard briefly before he re-emerges, wiping his mouth dry with the back of his hand. I do have an interest in the School's Out match though.  Seems that a friend of mine has decided to enter so I'll be there to check out how he goes. The Supreme Machine reaches down and picks up the crumpled magazine from where it was laying on the floor.  He flicks to the picture of his former tag team partner. A friend made me promise that I'd look out for his little brother.  That's a promise I aim to keep.  So I will be there on Friday night. He drops the magazine onto the bedside table.  This time it doesn't fall onto the floor. But I won't actually be taking part in the match.  Like I said, those "group" things are just so.... messy.  He looks at the time on his watch again as there is a soft knock on the door. Ah, that will be my special delivery. The Supreme Machine laughs softly to himself as he walks towards the door.  He pauses before opening it. So you're all going to have to wait a little bit longer until you have the privilege of seeing me in a PCW ring.  Don't worry, it'll happen soon enough.  I'm just sitting back and picking my mark.  It won't be long, trust me. He reaches out and opens the door.  In the hallway stand two beautiful women, one blond, one brunette.  Both are dressed in tight black dresses that accentuate their curvaceous bodies and large, sumptuous breasts. Good evening ladies. The Supreme Machine moves out of the doorway so that the two women can enter the room.  Before they do, the blond smiles at him and gives him a business card carrying the Escort Agencies logo and phone number. I'm Stephanie.  This is Brooke. Pleased to meet you, why don't you come in. The two ladies pause as they see the camera crew in the hallway.  The brunette, Brooke, protests. Hey, no one said anything about filming.  That's going to cost extra! The Supreme Machine turns and gestures angrily at the camera crew to get out. Don't you worry about them, they're just leaving. The camera crew squeeze past The Supreme Machine and continue filming from the hallway as the two women walk into his room.  He turns to face the camera as he starts to close the door. Like I said, three ways are OK.  Just don't like too many people at once, that's all.  But I will be there on Friday.  Now get out of here!  I've got a few hours before I have to be at Turmoil so I'm going to get some relaxation, if you know what I mean. He closes the door, leaving the camera crew out in the hotel hallway.
