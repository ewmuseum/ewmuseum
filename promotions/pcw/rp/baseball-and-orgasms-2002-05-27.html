Baseball and Orgasms

27 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 27 Black Screen... Snow’s Voice: “How dare that masked bastard interrupt me on Fusion!? He has absolutely NO respect for Greatness! For TRUE Greatness! Why, I should have broken his costumed neck on the spot for such deviance! I’m the Killin’ Villain, damn it! He can’t treat me like some peasant without an ounce of Greatness running through his whole damn blood stream!” Child’s Voice: “That sure is harsh, mister.” The scene opens in the half empty stands of a baseball game between the Chicago Cubs and Pittsburgh Pirates. The former Champion sits sourly with his resting in his hands which are planted on his leather-clad knees. Next to him sits a small child, a boy, somewhere around five or six years of age, fully decked out in Pittsburgh Pirates gear. There is no sign of the child’s parents... Snow: “Harsh doesn’t even cover it. Who in the hell does he think he is!?” Boy: “I don’t know mister...” The small boy has bushy blond hair and mirrors Jason Snow with his face resting on his hands which are firmly planted on his knees. We notice that he’s wearing an oversized baseball glove on his left hand. Curiously, he looks over at the Killin’ Villain... Boy: “Er... who IS he anyway?” Snow scowls... Snow: “How in the hinges of hell should I know who he is!? Some blasted inferior freak flipping around in pantyhose as far as I can tell!” Boy: “Sure sounds strange, mister.” Snow leans back in the bleachers, resting his elbows on the seats behind him. We can’t help but wonder what Snow is doing way back in the cheap seats... or what business he has at a baseball game whatsoever... Snow: “Strange doesn’t cover it. ‘INFERIOR’ is a more fitting term. He’s a joke, and nothing more. I’ll prove that to the world at Fusion!” Boy: “I sure hope so, mister.” Snow: “Hoping has nothing to do with it! By Greatness, just LOOK at me!” Boy: “I am, mister.” Snow: “How can you be looking at ME when you’re watching the inferior happenings down on the baseball playground!?!” The young boy points to the jumbo-tron where the former three time PCW Champion is being shown. What audience is in the stadium begins cheering as they see Snow being featured on the large screen. Beneath the giant picture are the words, “PCW Star, Jason Snow, in attendance.” Our camera man aims back at Jason Snow, who exclaims... Snow: “OH MY GREATNESS!” The boy, startled by Snow’s sudden surprise, looks up through wide eyes and an open mouth. Snow, still standing, begins raving... Snow: “They can’t do this! They just don’t understand! Have they no idea what disaster this may cause! By Greatness, the mere SIGHT of me has been KNOWN to cause ORGASMS!” Snow’s words come right in time to catch the boy’s returning parents, who are shocked by Snow’s choice of words. The father, a rather young looking man who is tall with dark hair, quickly grabs his son by the hand and leads him away from the fowl-mouthed PCW star. Snow doesn’t even seem to notice. Quickly, he stomps off toward the exit... Snow: “THIS IS AN OUTRAGE! By Greatness, by this time tomorrow, every wench in this building and all across Pittsburgh will be overpopulating hospitals, foaming at their mouths with their skirts ablaze! I WILL find out who’s responsible for this!” As Snow nears the exit, he’s cut off by a pair of beautiful women in their early twenties. One, a beautiful young blond speaks up... Woman 1: “Oh my God! Y-you’re Jason Snow! You are like SO cute! I would like, die, if I could get your autograph!” Snow scowls, but before he can say anything, the second woman - a young brunette - begins to speak... Woman 2: “Yeah, we would like, die!” Snow takes a step back... Snow: “And that’s EXACTLY what you’re at risk of doing right now! Do you not know that the mere SIGHT of me has been KNOWN to cause ORGASMS!!?!” A devilish grin comes over both of the young women’s beautiful faces. The blond, with seductive eyes, voices her opinion... Woman 1: “Oh believe me, we do know that.” The two young women look at each other and giggle loudly, covering their lipstick painted mouths with their hands. Snow, who’s standing a few feet back from them now, is not amused... Snow: “You... you think it is a JOKE? Oh, sure, for you it IS a joke! You’ve never had to see an innocent young wench flopping around on the floor with flames shooting from her groin! Until that day comes, you can laugh all you want! Now, for your own safety, I demand you take your eyes from my Greatness! I’ll not have a casualty made of you!” Just as Snow is about to move past them, we hear the crowd begin to cheer. Before we can see what the commotion is about, a wayward fly ball screams into the picture and catches the unsuspecting Killin’ Villain directly in the back of the head. Snow staggers backward... Snow: “Oh...” Snow spins around, barely holding consciousness... Snow: “My...” Snow stops for a moment and his cold blue eyes roll upward as he spits the final word... Snow: “...Greatness...” With that, Snow collapses in a heap on the stairway of the baseball stadium. The surprised women do nothing but stare at the former champion’s fallen body for a long moment. Finally, the brunette speaks... Woman 2: “Oh my God! I think he’s really hurt!” Woman 1: “Well, what was your first clue? When his eyes rolled back or that gurgling sound that was coming from his throat?” Woman 2: “But he has to be in New Jersey for Turmoil tomorrow night!” The blond’s eyes narrow... Woman 1: “Are you thinking what I’m thinking?” The women raise their heads from the fallen body of the PCW superstar and meet eachother with a grin... Both Women: “ROADTRIP!” The women quickly bend down to pick up the fallen body of the blond-headed champion. As they begin to drag him outside, before the paramedics can get to the scene, our screen fades to black...
