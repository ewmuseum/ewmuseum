Date: (Turmoil #4, Fusion #2) The devil… We all know the standards that this image represents…evil, murder, mayhem, and destruction. He comes to steal, kill, and destroy… since the dawn of time, he’s been set out to ruin all that is good for humanity. Numerous events have lead to this man…wars, pestilence, famine, death… How’s that for a resume? But in this day and age, many people fail to recognize the influence that the devil has upon us. He has his hand in everything…in all religions…even within the atheists. Whenever those bad things happen…you should stop and think…who’s behind this? It’s usually because of him… …or her… When it’s not because of the devil…and something just isn’t going your way… then the nVg probably has a hand in it. Turns out you’re just one unlucky individual that just so happened to have been in his way at the wrong time. Therefore…you must pay for your sins. This week…three people step into the wrong place at the wrong time…one even with the audacity to try twice to topple the god. They’re in the way…because he has plans to go straight for the top…straight to Xavier Kannon. And then…oh yes…then so much blood will be spilled with that divine placement. The saint…the villain…and the devil… They’re all going to stand judgement before the god…and as judge, jury, and executioner, he would find them all guilty of crimes against god. The bellboy stood nervously at the hotel door with a paper in hand. He tugged at the folds of his jacket, looking around down the halls. Then with a shaky fist, he slowly reached for the door to knock. Akiyama swung the door opened and glared into the youth’s face. The boy let out a cry of fear and fell back on his bottom. He looked up with mouth gaped open at the massive frame of the god, clad in black vinyl, leather, and mesh. His body jumped at the questioning grunt of Akiyama. Then he slowly held up the document that he was set out to deliver. After it was snatched from his hand, the Haitian boy ran off down the hall, giving tale of his encounter with the god to his peers. Akiyama sighed as he scanned over the document…and then slammed the door suddenly. “What did he want?” Cyclonica called out from the bedroom. Akiyama said nothing in reply. In the room, Cyclonica stood at the balcony looking up at the sky. She looked back towards where the god could be heard having not received any sort of answer. “Must be serious…” she muttered. Walking into the living room, she found Akiyama reading the letter with an intense look on his face. The Divine Prophetess stood up on tip toe and glanced over at the paper. “What is it?” she asked. With no much as a growl in respond, she took the letter from him and read it herself: “Dear Akira Akiyama: I request your services as soon as possible, seeing how it is a matter of life and death. In a basic summary of events…Fantasy has returned to kill me. Please be out front of your hotel in an hour, where I will arrange transportation for us to meet. Sincerely… Glacianna Frostine Iceberg“Glacia”” Cyclonica, for the first time in her life, had a surprised look in her eyes. For those of you who don’t know the past between Glacia and Akiyama, (read Friends and Sex: Past Remix promo) these two had a not so prideful past together for a memory-making one day. It was in that one day that Akiyama was forced to kill his former partner Fantasy after the present day god had slept with his supposed wife, Glacia. Fantasy was a bit of delusional…actually more than a bit, his entire life was delusion. Either way, after Akiyama threw him from a nine story balcony, he and Glacia split paths…she went and did her own thing…and Akiyama became the Neo Void God…cased closed…right? Well now the case was open again. This all happened back when Akiyama was going as human, which was the first twenty-something years of his life. Now he was above humanity…surely this past event had nothing to do with him now… Cyclonica slipped on her sunglasses, handing Akiyama his own. “Come along, Akiyama. We have a car to meet.” -=-==-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The rain drizzled down in sheets at that point. Like someone had just broke the sky’s heart and made it cry. It was most certainly atmosphere for suicide…or genocide…one or the other. It was one of those rare rainy days in Haiti, and everyone runs in when it pours. Akiyama and Cyclonica stood outside in the downpour, not affected by the wetness. Most of the water beaded against their vinyl attire, while the rest soaked into their hair. Both of them stood tall, with their hands at their sides waiting patiently like convicts sent to death. Their eyes whipped to attention at the same time as a black sedan pulled up to meet them. A man stepped out…muscular in size dressed in a red mesh tank top and black leather pants. His black hair was pulled back into a slithering ponytail trailing down the middle of his back to his elbows. On his face, just like the god and the prophetess, was a pair of black sunglasses. “Hop in.” was all the speech the deep voiced man said. Unfortunately, you’re going to need more than words to budge the god and the prophetess that easily. “Who are you, who sent you, and how do you want your remains presente--!” Akiyama nudged Cyclonica, interrupting her. She sighed, having realized her slip of words. “Who are you and who sent you?” The man didn’t take kindly to being questioned…much like the god didn’t take kindly to men questioning his might. “Name’s Equinox…and I believe you know very well that Glacia sent for you.” The two of them gave each other a wary look before climbing into the backseat of the vehicle. -=-==-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Minutes later, they stopped. Akiyama looked ahead and saw their point of destination… a small, run down hut of a building out near the sea. It sat on the peak of a cliff, overlooking a small island a few miles out. Equinox nodded towards it, and motioned for Akiyama to step forward. “She’s in there.” Cyclonica and Akiyama walked by him, but he grabbed the wrist of the Divine Prophetess quickly. “She only asked for Akiyama. You were not invited.” She looked at her wrist…then to the man. “1…2…3.” On three, the hand of the god swooped up, locking around the man’s neck and holding him in the air with a roar. His feet dangled in the air as he tried prying the grasp of the nVg from around his throat. Cyclonica walked around so that the man could look down in her own eyes. “I’m afraid that you don’t have a choice in this matter. Akiyama doesn’t speak to just any human flat-scan. I’m the ambassador here…I will speak for him. And if you have a problem with it, your name goes down with the rest of those who take punishment for their crimes.” No argumentative words from our new friend…so down he falls to the ground. He glared back up at the backs of the Celestials as they approached the door of the dwelling. Cyclonica opened it for the god, letting him step in first. Then she stood behind him as he stared on at a figure sitting alone in the darkness. There were holes in the ceiling, where some of the rain was pouring through. One hole was positioned at the feet of the woman, her black strapped heels tied around her calves. The ice blue leather pants were not wet, but looked shiny and moist enough you could make mud if you threw dirt at them. The rest of her body was hidden back in the shadows, leaned back in the chair she sat in silently. A white cloud came from out of the darkness, a cloud of cigarette smoke no doubt. But there was no smell of tobacco…and stranger that that…the room was freezing. “It’s nice to see you after all these years…Akiyama san.” The voice was soft, smooth…seductive. Like every word flowed slick as ice from her lips to the air. Akiyama didn’t budge at the sound of her voice, his eyes trained only on her image within the darkness. “How have you been?” “We’re not the type to trade cordialities, mortal. We only want to know what you want with us.” Cyclonica stepped out from behind the god, glaring off at the person in the shadows. It sounded like the woman was at a loss of words for the moment, probably because she didn’t notice the Divine Prophetess standing there. Then she continued. “I…had no idea you were here.” She admitted a bit surprised. But even in her surprise she held a cold demeanor about her…it was quite familiar. “You wouldn’t know if I were here…being that you’re not enlightened like the god and I.” There was silence from her…a few seconds. “I’ll cut to the chase, then, young lady.” “You may refer to me as the Divine Prophetess Cyclonica.” Yep, right on cue from Cyclonica. “That won’t be necessary. I won’t refer to you much in this conversation.” Ooh, a comeback worthy of the god. Cyclonica cut her eyes at the hidden woman as she continued. “Akiyama, Fantasy is not dead. As a matter of fact, he’s more than alive. I encountered him…and after my husband threw him from the boat we traveled her on, we found out that he’s still alive, and out for revenge.” Equinox appeared from behind them. His arm pointed out towards the window, where Cyclonica could spot the small island they saw outside. “He’s been there ever since,” he informed the two. “Watching…waiting…screaming your name…her name.” Cyclonica didn’t seem to be fazed by this tale. “Okay…so what’s this have to do with us? Akiyama’s through with his past. We’re above humanity now, and therefore we don’t have to deal with this crap. You call the police or whatever and get yourself a lawyer.” “I think that this is something Akiyama would rather finish off himself.” Glacia interjected, her voice becoming a bit sterner, yet flowed like silk. “Do you know anything about honor among compatriots, young lady?” “I thought I told you not to call me young lady!” The Divine Prophetess whipped around with eyes of flame into the mysterious Glacia. Then she stepped back herself as the woman leaned forward to look glare her back down. Her face was emotionless, and still beautiful like heaven. Glacia’s eyes were pure ice blue…and within those eyes laid a coldness that existed beyond reality…and inside the reality of another. “It would be the best interest for you to eliminate Fantasy…that is if you wish to rid Akiyama of his past…young lady.” For the first time…the Divine Prophetess was at a standstill. She looked between Glacia and the tiny island outside the window of the small shack. Then she peered up into the eyes of the god…holding anger and a torn look. A sigh hissed out of her like a flat tire. “After tonight…we cut off all ties to this human waste.” ---TO BE CONTINUED---
