Title: Memo To Self Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 25/01/03 (XK/Brandi Joint RP) “Memo to self, hate more people.” With the mumbling moanings of PCW’s bitchy Personnel Director, Brandi Wilson we open up on a placid street in Shanghai, host as always of PCW’s 1st PPV of the year, Shanghaied. Brandi looks all around her, at the buildings, the people, their daily lives and sees nothing she likes, or tolerates. Brandi : “It brings a tear to my eye when we have Nuclear Weapons yet countries like this still exist.” As she walks, Brandi looks down to her feet, watching every stop to ensure she steps in nothing ‘offending’. Brandi stops, talking to herself. Brandi : “Wait, if I stop and walk back I’ll still be in Shanghai, if I carry on I’ll be going further into Shanghai. Why the hell are we in Shanghai? We’re wrestlers not sweatshop workers making Nike trainers for 3 cents an hour.” Shaking her head and sighing loudly, Brandi keeps walking through the city, still not enjoying any of the sights around her. Across the street, some local PCW fans see her, immediately rushing over to her cheering. Brandi : “Kill me now...” Brandi sees them coming and drops her head, realising she can’t escape them. As they run towards her, Brandi tries to shoo them away as if they were houseflies. Brandi : “Shoo, shoo!” The fans are not un-deterred and hound the Personnel Director, waving little pads of paper and pens, wanting her to sign an autograph. Brandi, of course, doesn’t. Fan : “We larve Peeshee Dubyaw!” Brandi looks back at the fan blankly, hoping he’ll go away but he just looks back at her, smiling a goofy grin and waving his autograph pad. Brandi : “That’s nice, but you must have the wrong person because I’m in PCW, not whatever Fed you just said while spraying my shoes with spit... Memo to self, buy new shoes.” Going to take a step forward, Brandi sees she’s boxed in by the PCW fans and moans to herself. Brandi just puts her hands on her hips as the fans continue to pester her. Fan : “We li Teweeza Tomarse!!!” Brandi looks back to the fan as if he has just stepped on her parent’s grave, a look of total disgust. Brandi scowls at him, the fan not understanding her body language and just grinning. Brandi : “If I had Diplomatic Immunity, I’d kill you dead. Memo to self, get Diplomatic Immunity.” She tries to barge her way through the group of Shanghai fans who still hold out their pads and pens. Realising they’re all around her still, she reluctantly takes one of the pads and swipes the pen from the girl’s grasp. Brandi : “Fine, if I do this you can leave me alone.” Scribbling carelessly on the pad, Brandi writes ‘I hate you’. Brandi : “There, who’s next.” Not even looking at what Miss Wilson wrote, the fan holds the scribble up to everyone and yells something cheerily in Cantonese as Brandi smirks. Brandi then takes another pad and pen, scribbling again. Brandi : “I.. Should... Be.... Cleaning.... Brandi’s... Shoes...” She mumbles to herself while scribbling then handing it to the fan who just sees ‘Brandi’ written amongst other words and runs off to show it to her friends. Despite the insulting she gets to do, Brandi still doesn’t look totally happy. Brandi : “Memo to self, learn their language so my insults insult them.... Wait, hire someone to insult them for me.” With the crowd thinning around her, Brandi finally sees daylight ahead of her, albeit Shanghai daylight and sees her escape route. Brandi barges through but another fan jumps in front of her, waving an autograph book. Brandi : “I thought they only let them have one kid, where they hell are all these brats coming from?” Sighing and swiping the book, Brandi waits for a pen. Brandi : “Don’t think I’m wasting my own ink on you.” The girl hunts around in her pocket and then pulls out a beautiful, intricately decorated pen and hands it to Brandi. Seeing the girl has now moved from her path, Brandi drops the pen on the sidewalk and stamps in it much to the girl’s distress. Brandi : “Your county is poor, shame on you for having such luxuries.” Brandi walks off as the girl drops to the floor, crying as she picks up the pieces. “You hate this place as much as I do?” Finally hearing a familiar voice, Brandi turns to see Xavier Kannon heading down the steps onto the street, chuckling to himself after seeing Brandi’s actions. Brandi : “We should of just bought some backwards farming town in Oklahoma, named it Shanghai and fought in a barn... Anything would be better than this.” Leaping the last 5 steps, Xavier lands next to Brandi. Xavier : “It’s only for 2 days, just imagine this is a nightmare and you’ll wake up on Monday hopefully not having caught anything.” The Front Office pair walks along, looking down their noses at the city’s occupants. Brandi : “Had your shots yet, Xavier?” Rolling the sleeve of his black sweater up, Xavier shows Brandi the needle battery marks on his arm. Xavier : “I never go into a match against a Redneck before getting properly immunised, never know what I might pick up being that close to Duke... Ringworm, idiocy, his accent...” Brandi shudders, thankful that she has had the shots too. Brandi : “So, where’s Ellie?” Kannon looks around for a second, realising his wife isn’t with them before remembering. Xavier : “Oh, she took my Gold Card and went Kimono shopping.” Brandi looks confused. Brandi : “Aren’t the Japanese?” Nodding, Xavier just raises his eyebrows and shrugs. Xavier : “Try telling her that.” Brandi chuckles and the Front Office pair head off down the street, the crying girl still shown trying to put the pieces of her pen back together. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
