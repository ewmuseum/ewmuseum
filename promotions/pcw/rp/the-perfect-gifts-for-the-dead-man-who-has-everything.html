Date: not entered Last Tuesday, Flaymin committed the ultimate disrespect to the deceased- He obliterated the grave of El Diablo’s brother with his tractor. Leaving behind the smashed and trashed grave, Flaymin laughed hysterically at his latest ploy to destroy El Diablo’s life. But today, Flaymin has returned to the grave.But why? The damage has been done… but the mental torturing of El Diablo is only beginning. [Scene opens at the graveyard where El Diablo’s brother is buried. Flaymin and his Pornstar assistant- Jenna Jameson, walk through the graveyard towards the grave of the El Diablo’s deceased brother. In his hand, Flaymin carries a large box, while Jenna carries a bag in her hand] Flaymin: Wow.Look at all these dead people. Jenna: Yeah, they’re almost as quiet as the crowd during an El Diablo match. Flaymin: Eeeek!Death has to be better then an El Diablo match. Jenna: Are you sure we should be doing this? Flaymin: Why not.Dead people have feelings too, ya know. [Flaymin and Jenna stand near the trashed grave. The dirt around the grave is scattered everywhere and the tombstone is still shattered into a thousand pieces. Flaymin looks at the trashed grave and smiles contently] Flaymin: I did a pretty good job. Jenna: Yep.You can almost see the coffin from here!! Flaymin: Maybe we should pull it out…. I need to take a leak. Jenna: And dirty this suede halter-top?I don’t think so!! Flaymin:Pfff.What is it about women and clothes?You look better with out them. Anyways, we should do what we came here for. [Jenna sticks her hand into her bag and pulls out a G-String. She sweeps away some of the rubble on the grave with her hands and then places the G String on top of the grave] Flaymin: Nice Touch.I’m sure he’s smiling at you from down there.Either that…. Or he’s looking up your skirt. Jenna: He probably would be considering I just took of that G-String in the car. Flaymin: I can just envision his hand breaking through the dirt and….. putting on the G- String. Jenna: While he’s at it, he might like my latest porno on DVD- ‘Jenna licks her Gigantic Jumblies’. [Once again, Jenna reaches into her bag and pulls out another item.This time it’s her latest porno on DVD. She puts the DVD on the grave and then steps back] Jenna: Do you know if they have DVD Players in hell? Flaymin: Satan might have one.The rest are probably still using Beta VCR players. [Flaymin and Jenna laugh for a moment. Jenna then reaches back into her bag and pulls out the final item in the bag- An Official ‘Flaymin Aymin Remote Control Tractor’ She then leans over and puts the toy tractor on the grave] Flaymin: That’s a nice gesture.Now he has something to play with besides his donger. Jenna: Yeah but what about when he runs out of batteries? Flaymin: I’m sure they have a 7-11 down there. Jenna: I should have brought him a solar vibrator. Flaymin: They probably would have confiscated it off him at customs. Jenna: There’s a Customs in hell? Flaymin: Sure.It’s right next to the Car Wash. Jenna: Hold on a sec.If you don’t qualify to make it into Heaven and you don’t quite make it into hell- where do you wind up? Flaymin: In a place worse then hell. It’s a big dark steel vault with no way out.Inside the vault you watch never ending El Diablo promo’s for eternity. Jenna: Ouch!!That’s the worst possible torture known to mankind… Besides bondage. [Flaymin looks at his watch and indicates to Jenna that it’s time to go] Flaymin: We’ve got to leave soon.Our Gang Bang is scheduled in half an hour. Jenna: But you didn’t pull out the surprise you brought with you in that box. Flaymin: Oh right!I almost forgot. [Flaymin looks at the grave and begins to talk to the corpse of El Diablo’s brother] Flaymin: Well.Um, how’s it going? Now I’m not going to beat around the bush or give a lovey-dovey speech. All I’ll say is that I’m sorry for running over your grave with my tractor. Actually I’m sorry for causing your death to begin with. To make it up to you I’ve brought you a new tombstone to replace the one I smashed. [Jenna warmly rubs Flaymin’s shoulder] Jenna: Oh that’s so sweet! [Flaymin reaches into the cardboard box and pulls out a case of Beer. He places the case of beer in an upright position at the top of the grave so that it resembles a tombstone] Flaymin: There, much better. Jenna: A case of beer? Flaymin: Yep, and I didn’t even have to engrave it- it was already done when I bought it. Here lies Fosters Draught.He was 24 bottles of beer years of age.And he weighed 375 millilitres per bone.He was brewed….err….I mean born in Australia. What more does a tombstone need? Jenna: How about one of those titles that says something about the person? Flaymin: Good idea! [Flaymin pulls out a pen from his pocket.He reaches over to the tombstone and at the top he writes ‘Flaymin Woz Here’] Jenna: ‘Flaymin Woz Here’?? Flaymin: Yeah I know, that sucks.I’ll change it. [Flaymin crosses out the ‘Flaymin Woz Here’ slogan and replaces it with another phrase. He then steps back and crosses his arms] Flaymin: Well what do you think? Jenna: ‘Wish You Were Here’.Haha!I like that one! Flaymin: Come on lets get out of here. [Scene ends as Flaymin and Jenna walk away from the grave of El Diablo’s brother, leaving behind a G-String, a pornographic DVD and a case of beer a.k.a a Tombstone, with the phrase ‘Wish You Were Here’ written at the top of it.]
