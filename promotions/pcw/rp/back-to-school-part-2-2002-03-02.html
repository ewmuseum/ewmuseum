Back to School Part #2

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/02/02 The camera fades in… Jack Perkins, a narrator for the A&E television network that always seems to be a tad tipsy appears in front of a desk that certainly isn’t his. He holds a glass with a dark red colored liquid, with a few ice cubes dangling about. Jack looks towards the camera and begins. Perkins: Well, now since we last left the Chef, a lot of chicanery and culinary shenanigans have been committed. Now I’m drunk off my ass, but I’m still sober enough to see the message that the Chef puts in every single one of his promos, and that message is, “Jack, you’re a loser, you don’t pay child support, and you drink way too much” but I don’t! So don’t you tell me what to do ok fella? I’m the big narrating guy here at A&E, and you can’t take that from me. I’ve got witnesses, I need a lawyer, I’ve got dysentery from eating at Jack-In-The-Box, and I need a bath… As the A&E crew comes out to drag the overly drunk Perkins away he manages to shout out a little statement… Perkins: Viva la revolution! The crewmembers quickly stick Perkins with a few syringes full of tranquilizer and drag the geezer off. The camera then fades out, and into the spellbinding conclusion of the last Chef promo. Homeslice: Yeah, I think I need a little work on the “Islamic” in my Islamic Militancy… Suddenly the door opens… “Here’s your dinner!” The Chef sees the guard coming in with bowl of corn mush. The Chef: I am a culinary genius; you can’t expect me to eat mush! The Chef quickly charges at the guard and SPEARS HIM DOWN TO THE GROUND!!! The Chef: Praise Allah for Reeboks, I got good traction off that slick floor… The Chef and Homeslice run out of the vault and slam the door shut, trapping the guard inside, they take off down the hall. The Chef: We are almost out of this cesspool of corruption, and bratty kids! Suddenly the Principal steps in front of the two escapees as they run towards the only unguarded door in the school, the women’s locker room… Principal: Where do you two think you are going? Narrator: Will the Chef survive this escape from school?!?!? The Narrator pauses for a moment… Narrator: Of course he does, because he’s telling the freaking story! Well…I guess you’ll find out how the Chef escapes without perishing in our next promo! Continuing with the story… The principal stands in front of the women’s locker room holing both men’s permanent records in his hand. Principal: One false move and your chances of ever graduating with a degree in Islamic Militancy will be gone! The Chef thinks to himself. “Ok, I can ruin my chances of graduating, or go into the woman’s locker room…choices…” The Chef looks back at the principal, and then looks at the locker room. Homeslice: I…I…I…I submit to your authority oh great principal! Hashish prostrates himself on the dirty hard school floor and submits himself to the might of the principal. Principal: Ha! Ha! Ha! Now, what say you Chef? The Chef looks again at the principal, and then at the locker room. He hears a giggle from inside the locker room, and quickly bolts towards the door. Principal: Hey, come back! The Chef flies past the principal, and opens the door, seeing many half naked girls running around the locker room area, snapping each other with towels. He leaves the door open, and the principal and Hashish peer in. Homeslice: Dear sweet Allah! Principal: Momma of Muhammad, I’m in Heaven! The Principal walks up to the Chef and puts his arm around his shoulder. Principal: I cannot thank you enough for disobeying my oh so ignorant orders. The Chef can but merely drool a reply. The three men then quickly and quietly sneak over to a closet and all three hide inside, opening the door just a crack. Principal: Look at that one… One of the girls starts to the remove that covers the lower area of her body. As she slowly unveils her birthday suit, Homeslice’s anxiety takes over. The Chef: Why is the closet door slowly moving more and more open? Principal: I…don’t’… Both the Principal and the Chef look at Homeslice, who can hardly contain himself. Principal: Don’t you wear briefs boy? Homeslice: Can’t…stop…boner…must…relax…sexual…tensions…girl…too…hot… Hashish’s woody gets the best of him and it pushes the closet door open enough so that all three men are exposed before the females, yet none turn around at the doors squeak. Principal: Hurry; let’s close the door again… The Chef reaches out to close the door, but as he does the conniving principal kicks him in the rump and the Chef falls face first onto the floor. The principal and Homeslice then shut the closet door. The girls, all startled by the loud thumping noise turn around. “AAAHHHHHH!!!” Both the girls and the Chef shriek upon viewing each other, The Chef quickly gets to his feet and high tails it to the exit of the locker room. The principal though, opens up the closet door, walks out, and slams it shut in front of Hashish. He then walks over to the locker room entrance, pretending to come through the doors. Principal: What’s going on here!?!? The girls quickly turn around and point to the Chef, who is trying to open the locker room exit door. Principal: Don’t worry girls, you’re principal will not let you down. The principal walks over to the girls. Principal: Listen to me, this terrorist in training has unleashes a noxious gas into the locker room. The girls begin to shriek. Principal: Don’t worry, this isn’t a life threatening gas, it just reduces your bust size. The girls shriek even louder. Girl: How do we cure it?!?!? Another Girl: Yeah, how!?!? Principal: Easy, now just expose your breasts to the clean air and jump up and down as fast as you can to waft the poisonous air away from your otherwise sumptuous boobs! The girls begin to frantically jump up and down, the principal looks and salivates. The Chef jimmies the lock on the exit door with a couple spare kabobs out of his pocket and slams the door behind him, unaware of the tantalizing sight in the locker room. The Chef: Gotta run, gotta hide, break on through to the other side! The Chef runs through the school parking lot and hops into the first school bus he can find, trying to hide from the principal. He hops into the first seat behind the bus driver. “It’s you!!!” The Chef mutters an almost silent, “Me?” “You two timing piece of camel manure!” The Chef looks up and sees the bus driver that beat him up during his first day of school. The Chef: No!!!! Bus Driver: I saved an aluminum one for you this time!!! The Chef sees a sparkle of light and then CRACK!!! Nothing… The camera fades out and then fades back into the Chef and Chester Bin Laden watching the Bush attack on tape over and over again from the Chef’s hotel room. Chester: So what were you expelled for? The Chef: Terrorism…Chester: Terrorism in an Islamic Militancy school!?!? Isn’t that what you are expected to do? The Chef: I don’t know, there’s a lot of red tape attached to it… Chester sighs. Chester: And what about the principal, Hashish!?!? The Chef: The principal was cited for mass perversion, and for all I know, Homeslice is still trapped in that closet. Chester: How was the principal punished? The Chef: He had to apologize to each of the girls. Rumor has it that he told them to accept his apology by jumping up and down really fast instead of nodding. Chester: Wow, that man is a genius! Chester gets out a notebook and starts writing some information down. The Chef: But I’m expelled, and I’ll never get back in… It looks like I’ll never become an Islamic Militant Chef… Chester: Now wait a second Chef… Chester closes his notebook. Chester: Allah is a very gracious and fair god, and I think Allah’s will will permit my father to use his awesome monitorial power to buy you back into admission! The Chef: Praise be to Allah! Chester: No, praise be to mullah! The camera mercifully fades out…
