Finally...on the way

26 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/26/02 Night has fallen as Ricky has finally gathered his senses and made his way to his feet. The knot on his head has started to fade back away, but the vision still remains a bit blurred. He makes his way stumbling and bumbling out of the alley and hails down a taxi. The cab driver pulls up to the curb as Ricky slides in the back seat. He falls over pretty much collapsing across the seat as he pulls the door shut. Driver: You ok bud? Ricky: Airport please. Driver: You sure you wouldn't prefer the hospital? You don't look to hot. Ricky: No, I need to get to the airport. Time is running low. Driver: You're the boss. The driver pulls away from the curb as he peels down 134th avenue. Ricky sits in the back seat fading in and out of reality as the time is pretty well stopped....or running together. It is hard to tell reality from the dream world right now. His consciousness is far from his reality now. The driver pulls into the airport, as Ricky stumbles from the cab. He digs through his pockets for the cab fare but the driver pulls away. He reaches back for his wallet but he doesn't have it. It was taken along with the rest of his clothing. Upon entering the airport he steps directly into the gift shop and puts on some new clothes. When he heads to the registers he realizes he don't have any money. He picks up the phone and makes a quick phone call. Ricky: Hey I need a favor. I got mugged up here in New York. They took my wallet, my money, my everything. I just got some new clothes, can you charge it for me, and I will repay you on payday? Yah? Thanks. Oh, one more favor, I need to get me an airline ticket to Florida. I gotta catch that boat ride to Montego Bay for the Pay Per View. I really appreciate this, and I will hit you back on payday. Thanks again. Here ya go. They need to talk to you to confirm payment. Clerk: Ok. PCW offices? As in Primetime Championship Wrestling? Wow. Ok. Thank you. The clerk blushes a bit as Ricky stumbles from the gift shop. He goes to the counter to pick up his flight ticket, but encounters another small setback. Ricky: I need to pick up my flight ticket. Teller: Name? Ricky: Ricky Thunder. Teller: I need to see some identification. Ricky: I'm sorry, I don't have any. I got mugged today, they stole my wallet. They stole everything. Teller: I am sorry sir, but I will need some type of identification before you can pick up this ticket. Ricky: It was JUST bought and paid for by my offices like 15 minutes ago. I have to get this ticket or I am going to miss a very important endeavor this weekend. I have to get to Florida to catch this cruise liner to Montego Bay. It is a serious matter. I must have this ticket. Teller: I understand sir. However, I must have some type of identification. Ricky: You're not understanding me. I have no identification. All you can do to get ID for me is to contact Perth, Western Australia. You can reach them and them fax my ID to you. Would that work? Teller: Yes. However, we can't do that. You will have to contact them and we can go from there. Ricky: You mean to tell me.... Teller: Sorry sir. Next please. Ricky: SHIT! Ricky stomps away from the counter as he heads towards the payphone. He picks up the receiver and dials out. After a few rings a familiar voice is heard on the other end. They exchange greetings for a minute then Ricky explains his situation and what had happened Voice: Damn bro...that sucks. You all right though? Ricky: Yah I will be fine as long as I can get to Florida on time. All this bullshit goin on and now they won’t give me my plane ticket, because I don’t have my ID on me. Voice: Well man, I can have a car dropped off to you within an hour. Ricky: Thanks SuMa. I will head out front. Can they meet me there? SuMa: Sure thing. Take it easy bro. And avoid those damn alleys. Ricky: Definately. Ricky hangs up the phone the heads back to the front of the airport. He exits out and takes a seat on the bench, awaiting his ride.... Voice: Hey...buddy....excuse me...wake up man! Ricky groggily opens his eyes and focuses in on the guy lingering over him. He sits up, and notices he is on the ground. He gets to his feet, still a bit shaky, as the guy reaches out to steady him. He grabs his arm as Ricky jerks away, and staggers backwards a few steps. Ricky: Get off of me! I don’t need anymore fucking help today. Man: Hey, don’t get all pissy with me bud. SuMa sent me down here to scoop you up. I show up and you are taking a goddamn nap on the sidewalk. I am just doing what I am paid to do. Ricky: Oh...you’re...yah...sorry. Man: Names Bob Roberts. Anyhow, come on bud, I will help you to the car. Bob helps Ricky to where the car is parked but as he staggers to the drivers side, it is obvious he isn’t going to be able to drive. He directs Ricky to the passenger side, and hops back in behind the wheel. Bob picks up his cell phone and phones SuMa back up. Bob: Yo, SuMa. This kid looks like shit. He can’t drive. Hell he is barely able to stand. Apparently he blacked out while I was on my way. When I showed up he was knocked, straight on the sidewalk. SuMa: Damn him. Let me talk to him. Bob hands Ricky the phone, as he rolls his eyes a bit as they pull away from the terminal. Ricky: Yo. SuMa: Ricky, what the hell is going on? Bob says you can barely walk, let alone drive. Ricky: I got to get to Florida. The Pay per view. Sunday. Rising Star title shot. SuMa: DAMMIT!! Rcky, you are speaking in broken sentences, and you’re speech is slurred. There is no way you are going to be in any condition to wrestle this Sunday. I promised your brother I would look after you. REMEMBER? How can I, in good consciousness, allow you to compete this Sunday, knowing damn good and well what you are in for. Ricky: SuMa. One thing...I don’t need your permission to wrestle. All I got to do is show up. You see... SuMa: MOTHER FUCKER! Ricky, let me talk to Bob again. Ricky tosses the phone haphazardly to Bob, who fishes around for it, while trying to focus on the busy New York streets. He finally finds the phone, and glances at Ricky who has again blacked out. Bob: Sup bro? SuMa: Bob, what the fuck is going on? Bob: I don’t know man, but he is fucked up. He keeps fading in and out. Should I take him to the hospital? SuMa: Won’t do any good. He don’t have anything identifying him or anything. They will just send him over to county and he would die before they tended to him there. FUCK! All right, here’s the deal. I want you to drive him to Florida. Call me AS SOON as you hit the state line. I want an update on his condition as of right then. Bob: I can’t drive him to .... SuMa: Bob, for $10,000, I think you can. Bob: For $10,000 not only will I drive him to Florida, but I will carry him on my back and swim his ass to Montego Bay. SuMa: As I thought. Now, call me, as soon as you hit Florida. SOONER, if ANYTHING happens to worsen his condition, or if you feel something odd is happening. Bob: Will do boss. I will have him there by Sunday Morning. SuMa: Good deal. I will talk to you in a few hours then. I will be calling back to check up on him throughout your little journey. Bob: Aight bud. Later for now. Bob hangs the phone up and hits the highway bound for Florida. He turns the radio up, and Ricky doesn’t even flinch. Bob: Man...this is bad.........
