...But What Does It Mean?

3 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-03-02 We fade in to a hotel room. An anonymous one at that. From the view of the window, it is dark out; late in the night, and closer to morning, but our subject is not asleep. Instead he sits scrawled up on the edge of the bed, a notepad in one hand and a pen in the other, with the dim bed-side lamp giving light to his writing. Upon a closer examination, we see it is Trigger, scribbling down thoughts in his journal. What does he write? What does he think? What does he feel? 'Demoted. That is what happened, is it not? One day I'm a 'legend' in the division of Rising Star Title, and the next I am opening the card for a newcomer. I won't get too worked up. I shouldn't… a warrior wouldn't. But I can't help feeling a little depressed. It's starting to seem that whenever I put my mind to something recently, only backfires against me. At first I wanted to train three WITs. To let my legacy live on. They turned their back on me, destroying my confidence and for a short while my conscience. How foolish I was to think I could instill myself into others. How utterly stupid. Now my legacy will never live on. One more time I lusted. One more time I trusted. And now I can never trust again. When stopped listening to the Voice of Reason, and concentrated on the evil within, I thought I had stumbled upon something great. Warrior? So old fashioned, I had thought. I let the evil guide me, hopefully take me to places of glory… in the end it brought me agony and defeat. Another wishful dream down the drain. And now my latest venture… I guess you could call it that, although it was only for a day. To think I could change the world… convert it to my warrior ways. To think I could really get to the top--of PCW at least. Well… maybe I can get to the top. But not of the world. Seems like in PCW I am back to square one. Down to the first rung of the ladder. The first step towards success. I won't abandon it. I can't. If square one is where I am, then it is at square one that I shall begin.' With nothing left to say, or perhaps no will with which to say it, the Infinite Warrior tucks his pen into the journal, closes it, and flips off the lamp. In the near-pitch dark, he lays restless, unable to drift into a silent slumber. Something is missing… but what is it? He sits up, and flips on the lamp once more, to high power. In the brightness we can see cold beads of sweat dripping down his pale face. Something is missing… from his thoughts? Or from his life? He slides off the bed, and stands motionless for a moment. He is wearing his usual attire, a white tank top and black sweats. Brushing hair back off his face, Trigger walks slowly towards the bathroom, where he stands solid in front of the mirror. He speaks to his reflection. Trigger: I am a warrior. I am a warrior! I am a warrior, damnit! …but why? What is my role? What is my meaning? Am I simply here on this earth to fail? To lose miserably in the game of life? Tears swell up in his eyes. He stares into the mirror, unable to speak, for several minutes. When he does talk again, he turns away from the mirror, wiping the tears from his face. Trigger: …a warrior. What does it all mean? The meaning of life. What is mine? They say that the meaning of life is to be born, breed, and die. Why then does that not seem like my path? I am a father of knowledge; my children are my thoughts and ideas. So what greater meaning is there for me? His sadness now turns to fury. In a meaningless rage, he tears the sheets from his bed, and gets atop it, kneeling on the soft cushion. Head bowed, towards, the wall, he speaks again-- perhaps our warrior's version of a prayer. Trigger: Life… World… You have thrown me many curves. If I just knew the path I was supposed to take, I would follow it with all my heart. Yet whenever I think I have found my path, I find myself at a dead end. From this day forth, I will no longer jump to any conclusions. I will do the only thing that I can-- I will search. I will search long and hard, day and night, until I find my calling in this world. If I stumble, fall, crash, and die, it will not be for shame… because I will know that I have died in search and not in failure. Trigger: From my years of work, I have learned something very important. It doesn't matter what you are, or what you have achieved, unless it has a meaning that will last longer than it's glory. He cannot take it anymore, and paws his hand towards the lamp. It falls off it's stand, crashing to the ground into a million pieces, so like the fragments of the Infinite Warrior's life. No longer can we see in the darkness, but the sounds of sobbing against a pillow are clear. Trigger: I am a warrior… but what does it mean?
