Bum Fight

26 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/09/02 “Eugh.. You never said that there’d, like, be homeless people here...” Our shot opens up on the disgusted face of PCW’s preppy Commissioner, Eleanor Kannon-Hall as her Bambi-esque pale Blue eyes look all around her, only adding to her look of disgust. Eleanor : “I mean, when you said “Homeless Shelter”... I SOdidn’t, like, think you meant THIS!“ Unsurprisingly, as the camera zooms out from the little prep we see that she’s standing just inside the entrance of a Homeless Shelter. The interior looks cold and sterile while those moving about inside it are dressed tattily, relishing the warmth inside. Eleanor : “Because, like, HELLO!?!?! SO not a mind reader here!” Amidst the scrufilly dressed inhabitants and the uniformly dressed staff, the little prep, wearing a Pink skinny fit sleeveless t-shirt, faded Denim mini skirt and high Brown leather boots looks decidedly out of place. Eleanor : “Wait... Now I’m here, does that make me homeless? because I, like, have a house!” Her worried look is eased as we then hear the familiar voice of PCW Champion Xavier Kannon who comes walking into shot wearing a perfectly tailored dark Grey suit. Xavier : “Fear not my little Poon Puppy, we’re here for a very different reason... One I’m sure you’ll like!” Letting out a deep sigh of relief, Ellie~! trundles after her husband, drawing looks of lust from the homeless that frequent the shelter, something she seems none too happy about. Eleanor : “Ewwwwwwwzies, like, cut your eyes our or something.... Only people with JOBS can stare at my rack like that....” Her whiny words of disgust are met with deaf ears so in her own little way, she takes some “Affirmative Action”. Eleanor : “Fine, I’ll like SO stop you myself...” Reaching into her bag she pulls out a small spray can and thrusts it out into one of the homeless men’s faces. “ARGHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!” With a scream the unfortunate, in more ways than this, man holds his eyes after Ellie~! sends a fine mist of Pepper Spray into his old, blood shot eyes. The prep giggles. Eleanor : “Wow, now you’re, like, SO blind.... YAY ME!!!” Hearing what his preppy little wife is doing behind him, Xavier turns around and grabs her hand, pulling her to him and they walk arm in arm through the dull corridors. Xavier : “Try not to anger the natives, my little Fuck Ferret” As they stroll through the Shelter, those sleeping there give the well... And slutty.... Dressed people odd and un-trusting looks, something Kannon ignores and just mutters “Peons” under his breath. Eleanor : “Sorry, somethings are just TOO fun.” After walking deeper into the building, Kannon finally locks his bright Green eyes onto one of the workers, their body language oozing the authority of the Shelter Manager... Kannon’s own natural sense of authority drawn to the man. Xavier : “You, Peon...” The man’s attention is drawn from his business rather abruptly and focused on the couple who stand out like a very, very, very sore thumb. Xavier : “... I want to give one of these men $1000.” This rather uncharacteristic offer from the Scientologist takes the Shelter Manager slightly aback before he immediately warms to the couple and their act of charity. “Wow, thank you is all I can say....” Kannon looks back at him, annoyed at the man speaking to him. Xavier : “... I haven’t finished....” Ellie~! sneers at the man. Eleanor : “He....... Hasn’t....... Finished!” The warmth the Manager felt towards the PCW Champion quickly fades away and is replaced with a suspicious glare while Xavier looks at some of the men passing. Xavier : “... I want to offer $1000 as the prize money for a “Bum Fight”. The look of suspicion then morphs into a puzzled expression as Ellie~!’s enthusiasm rises. Eleanor : “A.... Bum...... Fight!” As the Manager gives him an odd look, Kannon is quick to act. Xavier: “And I DON’T mean in a Chris and Corey Chase-Williams way!” Ellie~! nods at Kannon’s covering of his tracks. “What’s one of those?” Xavier sighs and shakes his head. Xavier : “Do you peons know nothing? You get two homeless people, wave money in their faces and get them to fight each other for it.” The Manager looks back wide eyed and mouthed at the grinning Scientologist. Xavier : “You know, your average ‘Rich people gaining entertainment from the bottom of the food chain’ thing... I’d have thought running a place like this you’d have had your own fights.” Shock. Horror. Bewilderment.... And a little bit of Intrigue can be seen in the Manager’s look. “No way.... It goes against every moral code of myself and my job to allow anything like this to happen.” Ellie~! puts her hands on her hips and sighs at the man’s morals as Kannon just smirks and looks him in the eye. Xavier : “I’ll throw in $500 for you and 100% of any betting profits.” As is his usual way, the Scientologist manages to convince the man to go against his better judgement and Kannon’s smug grin is mirrored in the face of the Shelter Manager. “I’ll get the pair with the violent histories... And see about grabbing a Mental Illness too!” He gives a symbolic thumbs up to the Scientologist and heads off to find the combatants as the shot fades out on the smugness and anticipation laden face of Xavier Kannon. *ROOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRR!!!!!!* We fade back in on what was once the makeshift canteen at the Homeless Shelter but what is now the fighting arena as a circle of homeless bums sit or stand, shaking their fists and cheering. In the middle of the circle stand two scowling men dressed in their rags... Hair unkempt and faces aged by their years on the streets.... They circle each other under the watchful eye of Xavier Kannon. Xavier : “Smelly, diseased Peon One... READY?” The dishevelled man nods. Xavier : “Smelly, diseased Peon Two... READY?” While the second nods, the Homeless Shelter roars as he is knocked down by the first Bum... His scowling face contorting with anger as he lands blows down on his opponent. Eleanor : “Cool.. They’re, like, SO hitting each other!” As Ellie~! wraps herself around her man, enjoying the fight, the Shelter Manager walks over to join the couple with a large handful of notes on his hand. “Thank you so much, Gold Patron Meritorious. With fights like this twice a week, this Shelter is saved!” Another huge roar goes up as one of the Bums is thrown to the floor and his ribs kicked in. Xavier : “As long as I’m entertained by those less fortunate than me, I’m happy to help!” Ellie~! bounces on the spot cheering on the two fighters as one straddles the other and starts to smash the back of his skull into the cold, hard floor. Eleanor : “Happy..... To...... Help!” A hearty cheer resounds as the Bum finally passes out from having his cranium bashed against the floor and the Homeless victor rises with blood on his hands and soaks up the applause. As Kannon tosses the bundle of cash to the winner, the Shelter Manager turns to him “Don’t you ever feel guilty?” The Scientologist bursts out into laughter at both the question and the scene before him where the entire Shelter is now fighting for the $1000 the winner was thrown. Xavier : “Guilt? I don’t know the meaning of the word...” With the smirking face of the Champion shown, the shot starts to fade out and as the cheering and sounds of visceral violence die down we hit Black. “Of..... The..... Word!”
