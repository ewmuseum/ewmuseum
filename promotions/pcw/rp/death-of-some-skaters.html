Death of some skaters

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday, January 5, 2003 We find ourselves on the streets of Punxsutawney, Pennsylvania. More like a side road to be exact. But, in any event, we spot two young boys with skateboards. Both are dressed in XL t-shirts and baggy jeans that are three sizes too big for their small bodies. Boy #1: Yo, Joe, check this out. The red-headed, freckle-faced boy does a cheap ollie. The other boy, whose name we have found out is Joe, is a short, dark-haired boy who couldn't be no older than 12. For some reason, he looks impressed by the weak move. Joe: That was hot, Steve. But, yo, check this... Joe skates to the curb. The kid then grinds the curb. he only grinds about four inches, but the boys act as if it's the greatest thing ever. Steve: Dude, that was frickin' awesome! The two skaters slap hands in celebration over a pussy grind. Steve: But, hey, watch what I've been workin' on. Steve does a half-ass manual, but falls flat on his face. Joe busts out laughing. Joe: Dude, you just crashed and burned! Bwahahahaha! Voice: Let... me help you. A dark, boney, skeletal hand reaches into the picture and digs right into the back of the downed kid. The hands jerks around a couple of times. Then, in one giant tug, the spine of the kid is pulled out of his body. Joe: AHHHHHHHHH!!! As Joe screams, the shot pans over to show that the hand belong to none other than PCW referee/wrestler, The Dark Gimmick. Joe: Wha... What did you do to Stevie? The Dark Gimmick tilts his head to the right. Gimmick: Give me... the board. Joe: But... Not waiting, Dark Gimmick uses whatever mysterious power he may have this time to pry the board away from Joe and float it into his hands. Gimmick drops the board down to the pavement. The board lands on its wheels. Gimmick levitates onto the board. Joe, who stands there, looks baffled by the evil man. Joe: What are you doing, freak? Gimmick: You... are a pussy. Joe: Pussy? Yeah right! I'm a skater boy. Again, Gimmick tilts his head to the right. A blank stare lies in his eyes. Joe: You know... like that song from that punk chick. 'He is a skater boy. Something, something, skater boy.' You know the song! Gimmick: Watch... and learn. Joe looks at DG with a straight face for a second, but can't help but burst into laughter. Joe: Yeah right! Like you can skate, freak! Gimmick: I... will grind off the corpse. Then... a 360 dropping this piece of wood down your throat. Joe slowly stops laughing. he just shakes his head. Joe: I'd like to see you try, freak. Dark Gimmick stands on the board. He just stares a blank stare at the punk kid. Joe is visibly starting to grow impatient. Joe: Skate or die, freak. With Joe's comment, Gimmick's eyes glow. Gimmick: Die! Gimmick skates about five feet before grinding on the corpse of the punk Steve. After he does the first bit, Gimmick hoves himself and the board in the air. DG doesn't hold the board with his hand. The board just... floats. Gimmick slowly executes a 360 degree spin in the air. Joe's jaw drops in shock and amazement at what he's seeing. With the kid's mouth open, the board drops from under Dark Gimmick. Dark Gimmick stays afloat as the skateboard falls down and finds its way right down the throat of Joe. The kid attempts to scream, but the skateboard is choking him making Joe unable to be heard. Gimmick looks down at Joe. He nods his head as the choking kid falls face first down to the pavement. Gimmick: Freak. Dark Gimmick hovers away as two dead skaters lie on the ground. Fade to black
