Celebratory Events

17 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/17/02 It was a happy day, finally, for Alexis Parshin. He was rarely in a good enough mood to smile or laugh for long. But past events had concluded into a rather good day for the huge man. However, what makes a terrorist happy? “BAHAAHAHAA!!” A loud booming laugh, almost reminiscent of Ivan Stanislav comes bellowing onto the screen as the current scene fades in. Alexis Parshin is wearing what seems to be his ring attire, a beige shirt featuring Afghan and Uzbek half-flags with military style camouflage pants. Raghab is sitting to his left, laughing loudly as well, with a camouflage long sleeve t-shirt to cover his round belly. The two men are sitting in opposite leather chairs in a hotel room in Washington D.C. Their current amusement is coming from whatever is playing on the TV, although the camera is not an angle to see the TV at the moment. How strange, though, that two men who despise capitalism and everything that the United States stand for, are laughing it up while in the heart of the very country that they resent. “And I though that these Americans had no sense of humor!?” Alexis bellows out between long stretches of laughing. “Raghab, what is the channel that this comedy is on? I would like to have more comedy like this back home.” Raghab shrugs, unknowingly, and then picks up the hotel’s room guide, going through it for the answer to his boss’s question. After a few more minutes, Raghab throws the book to the ground. “I do not know, but of course leave it to moronic Americans to provide terrible service!” Alexis nods once again and suddenly a loud crash can be heard on the TV as both men turn and begin to laugh heartily once again. “BAAHAHAHAH!! There it is again!” After another long series of laughs, the camera pans sideways and shows the sickening truth, the TV is showing footage of the US consulate car bombing in Pakistan and bus bombings in Jerusalem. “Oh, Allah does certainly bless us on this day for this joyous occasion.” Raghab nods once again. “Who do myself and Chef have the pleasure of smiting today?” Raghab scratches his head, never annoyed at being the bringer of news to his superior. “I believe it was a blind man and the other one…the annoying one.” Alexis thinks for a moment and then replies smartly, “I take it he is American then?” Raghab chuckles to himself and thinks of the face…then snaps his fingers, realizing who it is. “El Diablo! That’s it!” “They will fall just like all the others have.” Raghab looks nervous once again, and Alexis can always tell once Raghab becomes nervous. “What is wrong Raghab?” Raghab takes a deep breath and then replies, his voice quivering, “Well…I saw one of these recordings made by the two men…and they mocked Allah, the Koran, and even the most great of the martyrs…Osama bin Laden.” “WHAT?!” Alexis’ rage is making yet another appearance now. He stands up from his chair, his face going red with anger. “Those two fools dare to insult not only the fight of myself and Chef, but Allah and Mohammed, the most holy and the prophet?! They shall meet a bitter end tonight then.” With this, Alexis storms out of the room, slamming the door on the way out. Raghab looks confused and calls out to Alexis, “Konstantin, where are you going?” There is no answer, so with a sigh, Raghab waddles out towards the hallway, and the two men head downstairs. 5 Minutes Later… The two men stand outside in the fresh city air, but Alexis has a wide sneer on his face for everything and everyone that passes him. “Look at this place Raghab, does it not absolutely reek of failure and inferiority?” Raghab unfortunately replies without any thought, “Well, actually that building looks very nice, Konstantin. Umm…I mean…” It’s too late for Raghab to take the words back though and Alexis fumes at the man, “Raghab, do you wish for us to fail?! Do you wish to ever see power restored to the Middle East?! If you do, then you will shut your mouth, and if not then…you will be considered an opponent of the Middle East…like one of those filthy Jews and filthy Americans! Is that what you want Raghab, to be remembered as a traitor?” Raghab shakes his head emphatically and says nothing for a long while as the two men walk down the streets of Washington. “Konstantin…I apologize, I was for a moment thinking of home, yearning for Urganch.” The sappy apology rolls off of Raghab’s tongue in a familiar manner. Alexis just looks down a the small chubby man and snorts, but Raghab knows that the man has only the Ultimate Authority as his other allies, and none of them, besides Chef, can truly understand his struggle as well as Raghab can. Alexis Parshin needs Raghab Karszat…yes that sounded good didn’t? Raghab thought to himself often about things like this. “You will learn respect for your superiors Raghab. That is the first thing that a good worker learns.” Raghab nods, pretending to listen closely, while mostly zoning out at the moment. “But tonight, you will learn what happens to those who don’t show respect. El Diablo and The End have chosen a path of mockery and comedy…but that will only lead them to a crimson path. Yes, their path is chosen…now let it be stained…”
