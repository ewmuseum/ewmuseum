: It was just a nut shot between friends is all.

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-13-03 Jaslene picks up a remote and begins to flip throughthe channels. She is lying back on a black leathercouch. A small table sits in front of the couch, aglass sits atop a coaster next to Jaslene’s perchedfeet. She sits up and reaches down for the glass. Adoor opens behind her, she doesn’t even notice. Adamappears behind her, his clothes ragged and dirty. Hesteps into the room behind her. He’s huffing andpuffing. 'J…J…J…' Adam bends over and tries to catch hisbreath. Jaslene still hasn’t noticed Adam. 'Jas?' She turns around and sees Adam standing there. Herface lights up as she jumps up off of the couch. Shethrows her arms around him and gives him a big hug. 'Where have you been?!' she asks pulling back andlooking him over. 'It’s been almost a week! You son ofa bitch! You could have at least called me and let meknow that you weren’t going to be home for a week!Jesus you look like shit Adam, what happened?' Adam makes a motion for something to drink. Jaslenehops back over the couch and picks up the glass thatwas sitting on the small coffee table. She hands it toAdam. He takes a big swig. After mere seconds, heturns and spits the liquid to the floor. 'Jas! This is Vodka!' Adam exclaims looking at theglass and then to Jaslene. 'It’s only…' Adam pulls hisleft wrist to his face but then slaps himself in theforehead, 'I forgot, I gave my watch away. Ok so Idon’t know what time it is but it’s too early to bedrinking.' 'Oh you’re silly!' Jaslene says reaching for theglass. 'It’s never too early to drink!' Adam pulls the glass up above his head, 'Hell no!You’re done.' Jaslene pouts crossing her arms in front of her chest,'I’m a big girl! I know when I’ve had too much todrink and I know I haven’t had too much.' Adam waves his hand in front of his nose, 'Tell thatto your breath, whew!' 'Just give me the drink before I have to get violent…'Jaslene mutters pointing her finger up into his face. 'Come on,' Adam takes her by the hand and leads herinto the kitchen, 'Here sit down.' Adam props Jaslene up onto a stool. She places herelbows on the table and drops her head into her hands.Adam pours the remaining alcohol into the sink beforerunning water into the glass and placing it into thesink. 'No! My drink!' Jaslene cries as she notices himpouring the drink out, 'Why did you do that?' Adam pulls a coffeepot from an instant coffee andfills it with water. After placing the pot back intothe machine, he pulls a can of Folgers from a cabinet. 'Because Jas,' he says scooping some of the groundsinto the top of the coffee maker, 'You are drunk andyou need to drink something that doesn’t have alcoholin it.' 'Oh…' Jaslene says pulling her head back up, 'Well Iguess that makes sense when you think about it.' Adam pulls a couple of coffee mugs from anothercabinet and places them down in front of Jaslene. Shelooks him over. 'You really do look like shit Adam.' She says leaningforward over the counter top, 'What happened?' 'That son of a bitch AJ kidnapped me!' Adam saysrubbing his eyes. 'I had to hitchhike all the way fromChicago to LA, it took me so god damn long to do it.' 'Hitchhike?' Jaslene says, 'Why did you hitchhike? Youcouldn’t have gotten a plane ticket or something?' 'Oh I would have given anything for a plane ticket butTrinidad stole my wallet.' Adam says looking back tothe coffeepot. 'It had my Driver’s License, my creditcards and all of my cash so I couldn’t do anythingother than hitchhike.' 'How did you manage to make it all the way fromChicago to here?' Jaslene asks, 'I would think that itwould take more than a week.' 'I would have thought so too but it appears I’m muchmore popular with truckers that I thought.' Adam sayswith a smile as the coffeepot behind him fills up. 'So then what happened to your watch?' Jaslene asks,'I know you had it when you went to Tempe.' Adam turns around and takes the handle of thecoffeepot in hand. He slowly pulls it from the baseand walks back over to where Jaslene is sitting. Shesets a mug under the pot so Adam can pour the coffeein. When the one mug is filled, she switches them.Jaslene picks the coffee mug up and takes a sip. 'Well truckers can only take you so far.' Adam says ashe places the coffeepot back into the base of themachine, 'I mean, people don’t usually see big Semitrucks come barreling into our neck of the woods youknow? So I used the watch as a trade with a cab tobring me here.' Adam takes a drink from the cup before walking aroundthe counter and taking a seat next to Jaslene. 'Oh…' Jaslene says rubbing her temples, 'At least yougot back ok.' 'Yeah which reminds me, what have you been doing thispast week?' Adam asks before taking another sip,'Hopefully not just sitting around drinking?' Jaslene shoots him a look, 'Of course not. In fact Iwent to a police station to file a missing personsreport but the flatfoot cop wouldn’t help me.' 'Well I’m going to say that calling him ‘flatfoot’probably was going to help your chances.' Adam sayswith a chuckle. 'Why didn’t you just go to anotherstation?' 'I tried but it was like that Seinfeld episode withthe doctor’s office.' Jaslene responds, 'No cop wouldhelp me. I went to at least seven or eight differentplaces but no one would help. So finally I gave up andfigured you’d come back when you could.' 'And that’s when you took to the bottle?' Adaminquires. 'Nooooooooooo.' Jaslene says as she slaps him in theshoulder, 'I ‘took’ to the bottle this morning. I wokeup and I wasn’t feeling well.' 'And drinking straight Vodka was supposed to helphow?' Adam says with a smirk. 'I never said it was a well thought out idea now didI?' she answers him. They both just laugh. Adam stops abruptly as henotices a note scribbled onto a Post-It note. Hereaches out and takes it into his hand. Adam pulls itoff and brings it closer to his face. It reads,‘Remind Adam about Tuesday.’ He looks over to Jaslene. 'Hey Jas? What is this note about?' Adam asks holdingthe note up, 'What about Tuesday?' 'Oh! Oh! I know what that is!' Jaslene says as herface lights up. 'You have a match this Tuesday.' 'Great. Super. Fun!' Adam says in a sarcastic tone,'So who is it against?' 'It’s…uh…it’s a…' Jaslene scratches the side of herhead trying to think, 'Oh yeah! It’s a Four WayTornado Tag Match.' 'Lovely not just any match but a Tornado Tag Match.This day just keeps getting better and better.' Adamsays as he crumples the note up, 'Who is in the matchand who is my partner?' 'You’re partner is the Pharaoh, he’s the CruiserweightChamp!' Jaslene says but Adam just smirks, 'I don’tknow who is with who but I know AJ, Smoke, Rust,Clyde, Darkstar and Chet King are all involved.' 'Trinidad huh?' Adam says rubbing his chin, 'Good,I’ll get a chance to repay him for all of thathospitality he showed me those few days I was in hiscaptivity.' 'What did he do to you?' Jaslene asks getting up andmaking her way around to the coffeepot. 'Oh you know, the usual things you would do to someonethat you’re holding hostage.' Adam replies. Jaslene pulls the half-empty coffeepot out and refillsher mug. She looks back to Adam as she slides thecoffeepot back into the base. 'Such as?' Jaslene asks, 'Try to remember Adam thatnot all of us have lived the colorful life style youhave. I don’t think I have had the pleasure of everkidnapping someone.' 'Oh well there were beatings…um…Amira punches me inthe balls a couple of times…AJ subjected me to hishorrible musical collection on more than oneoccasion…oh and lets not forget the attempted murderthat was a good time for all.' Adam says with a smile. 'Hold the phone! Amira punched you in the balls? Thebitch…' Jaslene says taking a drink from her mug. Adam just looks at her, 'Um…did you hear the attemptedmurder part?' 'Yeah I did.' Jaslene quickly replies. 'And?' Adam asks finishing his cup of coffee. 'And what? I don’t care about that because I know AJwasn’t going to kill you.' Jaslene says as she placeshis cup into the sink. 'What makes you so sure of that?' Adam asks standingup. 'Because no matter how much shit goes on between thetwo of you inside of the wrestling world, you’llalways be friends in the end. You’re too much a likeit’s sickening.' Jaslene says, 'But Amira punching youin the balls crosses a line.' 'O…k,' Adam says walking around the small counter,'It’s no big deal Jas. Just a nut shot between friendsis all.' 'If you say so…' Jaslene trails off. 'Now I just need to find out more about this match onTuesday.' Adam says walking out of the kitchen andinto the living room, 'But at least I have a championon my side. Even if he is a crazy Egyptian character.' Jaslene is quick to follow him as he heads up thestairs.
