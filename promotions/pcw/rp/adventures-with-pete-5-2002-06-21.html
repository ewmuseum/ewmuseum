Adventures with Pete [5]

21 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 21, 2002 The lights only seemed to get brighter as they neared the palace of R.L. Stine. Suddenly, the bare sidewalks of Reno turned into sparkling walkways, with trees lining the edges of the walkways and expensive cars parked on its side. Tom began to feel like he was in Hollywood, and Pete was his pimp…or was it the other way around? Who knows. Anyways, they reached the outside of the palace within a few minutes. It was enormous, reaching up to forty stories high. Tom wasn’t even sure buildings were allowed to be that tall. The very tip of the building seemed to disappear into the clouds themselves. Guards lined the entrance as if there was something very important inside…well, there was. A man with a ton of money, and a ton of answers…Mr. R.L. Stine. Tom was about to head into the palace, when he was stopped by a sleazy looking man. He wore a bright green blazer, with pink pants, and a yellow shirt underneath the blazer. He had black hair, but it looked odd on his pale, white face. Are we sure he wasn’t related to Dark Star? Tom Lexian: [trying to walk past the man] Uhm, can I help you with something? Who are you? Sleazer: [shakes Pete’s and Tom’s hands] Sleazer’s the name, sleazing’s my game. Now, I bet you’re wondering why I’m even talking to you gentlemen. I’ll tell you why. Because I know why you’re here. I know exactly why. You’re here to speak with Mr. R.L. Stine. Pete Rose: [impressed] He’s right. I have got to say, he’s pretty good. Are you psychic or something? Tom Lexian: Pete, you need to be a better judge of character. This guy isn’t a psychic. As I understand it, hundreds of people see Mr. Stine in hope of fame, money, power, or a combination of the three. What’s your point…Beazer? Is that your name? Sleazer: Gosh no, it’s Sleazer. With an “S” and an “L.” Now, you’re here to see Mr. Stine, but what you haven’t figured out yet is that you can’t. Tom Lexian: Oh? I can’t see Mr. Stine? Please Sleazoid, give me one good, god damn reason why I can’t speak with him? Sleazer: That’s Sleazer, and it’s because he never sees wrestlers. Pete Rose: How’d he know you’re a wrestler? [Tom shrugged, reaching for his can of pepper spray.] Sleazer: Of course I knew. How could I not recognize the ultimate good guy, Kid Wonder? You’re just oozing goodness and fair play. Tom Lexian: You idiot. I’m Tom Lexian, not Kid Wonder. Just for that… [Tries to spray the pepper spray into Sleazer’s eyes. Sleazer covers his face, but nothing comes out of the can.] Tom Lexian: [looks at the can angrily] Dammit, I must have used the remaining pepper spray on my soup. Shit. Sleazer: Look, I know you’re a busy man. Hell, I know you probably have better things to do then to be speaking to a sleazy guy like me. But I have information that you can use. I know about the PCW PPV coming up. I know that you’re up against some steep odds… Tom Lexian: How could you possibly know anything? Sleazer: I know, it puzzles me too. But I really do. You see, everyone knows that you’re on some secret mission, and that you’ve stopped by R.L. Stine’s joint to get some information. That being the case, you’ve probably hadn’t had the chance to get a viewing of the promos being cut by Miyagawa and Kannon. Tom Lexian: Psssh. They’re nothing. They think they’re big shots because they’ve been here for a long time. I’ve been in wrestling longer than both of them and I have better hair. How could I lose? Sleazer: Well, they’ve been cutting promos all day. They seem to obsessed with the fact that Kannon is Miyagawa’s best friend though. Not too much talk about you. Tom Lexian: I wouldn’t doubt it. Something tells me there is more than meet’s the eye going on between them. Maybe some hanky panky. [Pete begins to spin the bat with a smile on his face. Sleazer gets a weird look on his face.] Sleazer: Okay, okay…well, did you know that mysterious TILDE is here too. Tom Lexian: [answers simply] Yes. Sleazer: I thought you wouldn’t. Now…[pauses]…Wait, what the fuck?! How did you already know? Tom Lexian: We had an unfortunate run in the Puzzled Bird Guy earlier. Any time something as weird as that happens, the mysterious TILDE is usually involved too. He had crashed his Camry into our Volkswagon Bug…erm, our car….not that it was a Bug, because god knows I wouldn’t rent a piece of crap car like that…. Sleazer: That was you two in that accident? So, you were in the Volkswagon Bug? You guys do realize that you left the car in the middle of the road and it’s been causing one hell of a traffic nightmare today. Pete Rose: [shrugs] Eh, what are you going to do? Sleazer: Anyways, rumor has it that the Puzzled Bird Guy had fled the scene of the crime, and flew all the way to the palace to meet with R.L. Stine. He said something that Stine didn’t like, so he had him caged. Tom Lexian: [confused] Caged? What do you mean he was caged? Sleazer: Exactly what it sounded like. According to my sources, he said something to the effect of, “Quack!” and Stine thought he was cursing at him in some foreign language. It was pretty funny, until Stine ordered him into a cage. Tom Lexian: So what am I supposed to do about that? Save him? He’s not exactly a damsel in distress. More like a pheasant under glass. Pete Rose: That wasn’t funny Tom. Sleazer: Well, I’m just giving you some advance warning. I know you’re here to ask Stine about the amulet of orion, and if you talk to him without knowing how to, you could wind up like the Bird guy. Tom Lexian: [places his hands on his hip] How do you know about our mission? Who told you? Sleazer: I told you…I have my sources…[points to a camera crew standing behind Tom.] Tom Lexian: [starts throwing his can of pepper spray at the camera crew] What did I tell you guys at the airport and at the train? I told you to stay the fuck away from me! Shooo! [Pete suddenly chases them all away with his bat. It’s good to have a hall of fame slugger at your disposal sometimes.] Sleazer: I’m also here to tell you that I want in on the mission. I want to use the amulet as well. Tom Lexian: What could you possibly want with the amulet of orion? Sleazer: Look at me. Look at these clothes. Do you think I was born this way? Don’t answer that. I need to change, Tom. I need to find the turning point in my life when I was changed from the handsome young man that I was into the slob you see before you. I need the amulet, and I’m willing to risk my life for it. Are you? Tom Lexian: [shrugs] Fine. I just hope I don’t have to be the one to bury you if you can’t keep up man. [So the party grew to three. We had Tom Lexian, famed wrestler and a lamer at heart…we had Pete Rose, a major league baseball slugger and an immoral man…and finally Sleazer, a bad dresser, a poor judge of colors, and a mismanaged man. Shit, there just isn’t anything good to say about Sleazer. Ah well. The three walked into the palace, being escorted by men wearing black jump suits. On their uniforms reads, “Goosebumps,” each designated by a number. They were being escorted by number 10 and 12. Where was 11? Maybe in the can or something. The palace was incredible…gold plated walls, silver floors, and large breasted women roaming the halls at every turn. One word comes to mind…HOOTERS! No, wait…WOW! Yes, that’s it. Much more respectable. They were escorted into a room that had fire burning on all sides. It looked like something from an Indiana Jones movie, and that they had just entered the Temple of Doom. No such luck here. Finally, a man appeared in the center of the room. He was short, around 5’1’’, with short, brown hair and a squared face. He wore a red suit, with a black shirt underneath, and no shoes. Next to him sat a green blob. That’s it. It was just a blob. Cool, huh? Yup, I was impressed too.] Sleazer: [amazed] Dammmmmmn, look at that huge booger sitting next to Stine! That’s fucking awesome! [Tom elbows Sleazer in the ribs, signaling him to shut the hell up. He didn’t want to end up in cage today.] R.L. Stine: [laughs] This is not a booger, as you so eloquently put it. This is Monster Blood. Tom Lexian: [confused] Monster blood? What’s that? R.L. Stine: [insulted] Oh please…tell me you’ve heard of Monster Blood before. You have read my books, haven’t you? Tom Lexian: You write books? I thought you were just a rich bastard with answers. [The green blob near Stine suddenly moved towards Tom.] R.L. Stine: [holding back the blob] No, no Monster Blood. You can’t eat him. I promised you that I would get you something to eat, but not Mr. Lexian. Now, behave yourself. Tom Lexian: How do you know my name? R.L. Stine: I know everything, Mr. Lexian. I’m very close to Lara and the powers that be. They’ve informed me of the situation. Of your intentions, as well as Mr. Rose’s. By the way, how’s the bid to get back into baseball going, Pete? Pete Rose: Just great, R.L. Thanks. They still won’t let me in, but I’ll keep forging letters pretending to be little kids so they’ll feel sorry for me and cave in. R.L. Stine: [smiles] Good for you. Now, I’m not entirely surely what the badly dressed man’s intentions are, but I’m sure it’s something stupid, so I won’t entertain it, nor will I kill him for being here. Shall we get down to business? Now, the amulet of Orion has been in existence for as long as human civilizations were born. We aren’t sure who built it, or what it’s original intent was, but we do know that it’s keeper is a dragon named Melvin. Tom Lexian: Melvin? Melvin the dragon is holding the most important artifact in existence today? R.L. Stine: We aren’t even sure why he has it. Hell, we aren’t sure if Melvin is a “he,” actually, but who cares? The point is that he’s grumpy, irritable, and he’ll eat you if you try to get the amulet. He patrols the borders in Canada. That’s where you’ll probably find him, although he’s known to just fly off and go on sporadic vacations. I hear he loves Hawaii at this time of the year. Pete Rose: But what exactly do we do when we find him? How do we kill him? R.L. Stine: I don’t know. Conventional weapons won’t work, that’s for sure. Appeal to him, be his friend. Anything. Rest assured, this will be the toughest battle you fight in your lives. He will not back down from you. He’ll only try to murder you as quickly as possible, so he can get back to doing nothing. That’s what he does during the day, you know? Nothing. He’s the laziest dragon I’ve ever had the mispleasure of meeting. Tom Lexian: [speaking up suddenly] What are our chances of succeeding? R.L. Stine: There have been two hundred pursuers of the amulet. They have all died. No trace of their bodies. You will be number two hundred and one. Maybe that’s the lucky number. I highly doubt you’ll succeed, but if you do, you’ll most likely die fighting over the amulet itself. Tom Lexian: I see… R.L. Stine: I believe you have all of the information you need, gentlemen. The rest is up to you. [turns to walk away] Oh, and I released that bird fellow friend of yours. I’ll spare him for now. Just tell him not to show his beak around here anymore, and “Quack!” right back to him. Now go… [So the party is complete, and now the true journey begins. Are you ready? I hope so, because we sure as hell aren’t.]
