A Time in the Life of... Rich Sherwin

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ... Pink...What is it that those freaks... 'The Heartbreakers' see in that damn color? What do those idiots aim to achieve by placing their entire careers on the basis of a single color? Do you think that Ryan Moore and myself make our careers out of singing for various different colors of the Rainbow? Do you see the two straightest guys in all of 'Primetime Championship Wrestling' singing about a color that has about as much 'Mano Appeal' than a transexual? One day, I'm going to find out what the problem is with all the pink... but meh, thats one day... for now, there is a bigger problem on hand and its really beginning to shake the world by its very roots!This past week on Fusion, we saw Jonny and Jack visit a nice little recording studio... grr, they damn as hell got offered a big money deal... and for what? Being able to replace the letters of Y.M.C.A with... P.I.N.K? Who cares about what those losers have to say about the color 'Pink' and what it can do for 'Gay America'... I sure as hell don't care... Ryan Moore sure as hell doesn't care... and wherever he may have wondered too, Sean Smith doesn't care about it all. Next week, on the last Fusion appearence that PCG will be making for the month of June... next week, you're not just going to see a Ladder Match that has their contract on the line... you're not just going to see, a piece of paper worth millions of dollars... handed from one team to the other, no, you're going to see something thats more real than ever... you're going to see the real Artists walk out of the contest with the RECORD DEAL that should alway be theirs! Rich Sherwin... Ryan Moore... and the 'Pretty Cool Guys'... will damn as hell... be leaving Friday Fusion on July 19th with the exclusive... conclusive... elusive... recording deal! However, for now... I'm sure that many of you will be wanting to see us in our natural habitat before the big showdown against 'The Heartbreakers'... but, what on earth can we do in this day and age? The idea of making a mockery of everything around us... thats a suggestion that went out of date a very long time ago... and you know, it really didn't win over alot of fans for both the band and the labels that want to offer us really big money deals. However, while I was seated in the confinements of the 'Official' 2002 'Pretty Cool Tour Bus'... I had a vision, I had... something of a dream if you will... there I was, standing in the middle of a cold field... wearing nothing but my 'Pretty Cool' Red Underpants. Covering myself up in the dream, a giant... no, make that MASSIFFO!... Platinum Record floated down infront of me... for some reason, it looked like Elvis on a stick, but I wasn't here to talk small with the Record, I was going to be given some very important information about where to go next... 'Rich... Rich...', he beckoned forth as I stood alone, watching the bright light engulf me. My eyes strained, I tried to get a decent look at the face of the record... but unfortunently, I was unable to make a positive ID on its identity. After a period of twenty seconds, the bright lights around me began to tone down... they began to hold back and my vision started to return. I tried to make another attempt at the ID of the record, but unknown to me at the time... was the fact that the light only shone so that the Record could turn away from me and hide its face ever more. 'Excuse me, Oh Masterful Platinum Record of Virgin Records... why have you beckoned me forth at such a time in my life? You and the world know and understand... that in exactly one weeks time, I will do battle with those raging homosexuals... the 'Heartbreakers'....', I asked, questionning the reason why I had been selected to have this very dream. The Record tried to frown at me... but soon realized that its 'behind' was looking upon me instead of its unknown face. I hesistated in my dream for all but a few moments.. trying to understand what kind of actions were going to come through next. Time passed us by and I started to get a little edgy... taking a risk, I tried to step around the Record to take a glance at its face, but by the time I had walked around... the large fellow was wearing a Tribal mask that covered his face completely... and at a perfect size too. The record peered down at me with its dark eyes... dark because I never quite knew where they ventured off into... coulda been anywhere, but in either case... somewhere. '... Rich Sherwin... you and Ryan Moore have your toughest ever challenge ahead of you in one weeks time on Friday Fusion... you have the damnest challenge of your lifes as you attempt to steal your own glory away from the hard working team of the 'Heartbreakers'... I am not appreciative of your homosexual comment towards the tandum... and shall now refuse to wish thee...', the record spoke before I was rude enough to cut through like a hot knife through butter. 'How can you not be appreciative of myself stating the whole truth... the damn truth... and nothing but the truth?! Did you just fail to see the way those two... batty boys walked along and kissed both myself and Ryan Moore?! Thats not the kind of stuff that people do to us... the chicks, well... the underage chicks all buy us... chocolate and other cool candy... and any male thats loser enough to love our tunes, they give us their demo tapes... which we shoot down with rocket launchers because lets be honest... NOBODY in this freakin' business is going to come anywhere near us... NOBODY MESSES WITH THE 'Pretty dang Cool Guys' and gets away with it!', I shouted... starting to get a little aggitated at the way that my newly found annoyance in this Record was throwing away the way I had used the term 'Homosexual' with regards to those very, very, very dang weirdos 'The Heartbreakers'. 'Judge not your opponents Sherwin... for they already have the skills to defeat you... and make you look like little fools... after all, just imagine how pathetic thou shall feel... when Jonny and Jack are able to parade around the Streets of London.. celebrating the release of a single... the release of an Album even... that YOU... and your partner, Ryan Moore... should have released yourselves...', the voice continued to groan on in its wavey pattern... I wasn't bothered anymore about what he had to say about the contest at hand, The Heartbreakers were nothing... and by God, they were going to stay that way for the rest of all eternity. At that point, I awoke from the dream, back in the bus where everything had begun... although, by this very moment in time, the movement had stopped.. and we were ahead of the whole company in Oxford, Mississippi... I guess thats the advantage of running to the next point on the map instead of just walking. I arose from my seat, and walked down the gangway of the 'Tour Bus', everything around me seemed to be so plain... so damn two-dimensional.. but it wasn't anything of a hassle at this moment, I had days to think about what would be happenning in that Ladder Match... days to be thinking about the kinds of stupid things that 'The Heartbreakers' were going to attempt to do. Maybe I should give Elton and George a call... tell them that some 'friends' are going to be at the next showing of Fusion, make them come along... take 'The Heartbreakers' away and allow me to get the recording contract that... by all means, should already be in my possession. I stepped out of the bus and looked around at my current surroundings, it looked like the norm for a city... a little busy, a little dirty... all in all, a relativly 'OK' place to be. I looked from left to right before gazing into the air, inhaling deeply, before exhaling and turning to face my left. I could feel the urge to do something worthwhile... something to show the world that I was still as lean... still as mean as anything that I'd ever been in my career. My eyes darted up the street for somewhere I could play out my trade... somewhere that I'd be able to make people 'RESPECT~!' me for what I could do in this business... it took a few moments to fish it out, but about twenty paces away, I saw a big Neon sign, switched off of course... it would be a waste of money to have such lights glaring in the middle of the day. 'Bill and Bobs Singin' Song Shack' is what the sign read... I had my doubts about the authenticity of the location, especially since it was the only one of its kind within its immediate area. Curious however, I began to wander over to it... looking a little excited, a little worried... and a little of all those other emotions that run rampant in your head. Each step seemed to take forever... like my legs were made of solid Gold or something... it really was a strange feeling that went throughout my body... either way, I soon made it to the front door of the location. Still as curious as ever, I glanced through the window of the location and surely enough, I saw some guys at the end of the room, holding microphones in the air... belting out whatever track they were singing... I wasn't sure on what it was, namely because I couldn't actually hear at of the music that was being played. I pushed open the door and stepped into the domain, looking around at the crowds of people who were seated at tables, drinking their drinks... and eating up whatever food they may have ordered. I could hear the music as it blared over their cheap dollar speakers... it was... so corny... so lame... but, totally familiar for some reason. The fat people on stage were about to sing some of the track... when I heard something familiar... thankfully, those fat fools had been too slow to begin singing... but I heard a voice that was going to be ringing in my ears for a long time... 'P... I... N... K!... Its all about the P... I... N... K', the voice shouted along to the tune of the backing music... at first, I thought that it couldn't be... that it just had to be somebody playing a trick on me.. but then, the voice settled in and I came to realize exactly who was behind such trash... who was behind such pathetic music... WHO WAS BEHIND IT ALL! Eager to cease transmission of such garbage... I rushed down to the back of the complex, pushing past whoever was stupid enough to get in my way. The fat people looked over to me in a homoerotic manner as I pressed the 'STOP' button the the Karaoke Player. 'What in Gods name do you think you're doing... listenning to trash like this from those... WANNABE Band members? Huh, Huh, Huh? You all sit here... and you're listenning to people who can't even sing... who don't even know the first thing about being in this business?!... I am sick and tired of having to deal with you... homo-freakin'-sexual people... who weigh ten times myself... and slobber over me whenever I appear on TV... and then totally lose it when I appear around this here location.', I shouted... angry and disgusted at the way that such music had even been allowed to be heard by these people... even if they were the skank of skank... the very bottom of the barrel, because, lets be honest for a few moments... everyone needs to be able to listen to decent music... and if they're not listenning to the music of myself and Ryan Moore, then they are SO not listenning to decent music! Everyone in the bar looked at me with the hint of disgust lingering infront of their eyes. It was a glare that I was all too used to... and after something like this was about to go down... something alot worse was going to follow. Looking to the corner of the complex, I saw a couple of guys begin to excuse themselves from their positions at tables, I wondered what I should do... stay and fight like a mad-man... or run like the wind and take the 'Good Fight' somewhere else... or, simply run back to the bus and drive to a safer part of the city. As soon as another chair made that scraping sound... where is rubs across the floor... I jumped down from the stage and sprinted to the main entrance, pulling the door open and hopping out into the street. I turned left and ran back toward the Tour Bus, banging on the doors for a few moments before hopping in and driving away... the people whom had hated my decision to tell them what music they should be listenning to, had tried to catch up with me... but failed none-the-less... the picture fades... the picture ends... and this chapter of Sherwin... is forever closed...
