The Tank 32 - Shadows of Dead Men

17 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 17, 2002 ﻿*********THE TANK __32—********* Shadows of Dead Men Things draw closer to an endyet things stay so much like the beginningI can’t tell if I’m hearing whispers or silent screams ~Leigh Donovan Friday, October 11 - Day 99 He dreamt last night that he was tossed off a bridge by a pair of Italian thugs, both lookingremarkably like his dangerous hitman cellmate, Vincent Giovanni. If not for the telephonechord wrapped around his thin neck, Tim McDonald would have fallen a hundred feet to hisslumbering death. Instead, he found himself dangling by the snaky phone chord, fighting for airand numb through the body from the damage in his neck. That’s when the buzzer went off and another day in the Tank began. Vincent Giovanni was quiet this morning. That’s not surprising to Tim, as he knows Giovanniwill be doing plenty of talking later today when he gets his first phone call since becoming aprisoner in the Tank. And then, Tim’s family, Jason Snow’s family, Harvey Blake’s family, butmost importantly Tim’s future as a high ranking member of the Hattoni family, will all be dead. Doug Hattoni died because of Tim, Snow, and Blake, and Tim knows that everyone close tothem will pay for it now. If there’s one thing Hattoni and his crew did well, Tim knows, it wasdealing out maximum suffering. No one lives without regret for their actions, or the actions oftheir loved one, because no one lives. Tim never thought he’d feel an ounce of compassion for the imminent deaths of Jason Snow’sfamily. Now, on the day that their execution notice will be electronically passed from VincentGiovanni’s lips to Justin (son of Doug) Hattoni’s ear, Tim can’t help but wish he could schemeup some way to get out of this - or at least, by some time. In truth, Tim knows that he cares more about his own future than Snow’s, or even his own,family. That’s the thing that he knows he must save - the chance to sit along-side, or above,Justin Hattoni on the food chain. Families are a secondary concern, though he’s surprised toadmit that they’re a concern at all. Having a muscle-bound hot head like Jason Snow would come in handy right now. Too bad forTim McDonald, the fiery young lion has been in and out of Asian hospitals suffering frominternal injuries since his match at Red October, where he was blindsided by a brilliant schemeof El Diablo’s. No one knows when Snow will be back as of yet and chances of him re-injuringhimself tonight on Turmoil are good. “Fuck!” he curses at himself for not being able to think of something as he paces the vacated cell- Vincent Giovanni is in the shower. “One fucking hour,” Tim says under his breath, referring tothe amount of time until the phone lines open. He knows Giovanni will be first in line. Soonafter the phone call is made, the high priced mafia lawyers will find a loop hole in Giovanni’scase and get him out of the Tank. All of these things are so obvious to Tim McDonald - so why can’t he figure out a way to beatGiovanni? Sure, he could pay someone to kill the bullish Italian hitman, but even if that worked,it would only launch further revenge from the outside mafia. “It’s like playing poker against a fucking full house,” Tim says to himself in frustration. At thatmoment, he glances outside his cell and catches the sight of the man known as ‘Ahab’, the headC.O of Unit B - Benjamin Graham. “What I need,” Tim says to himself slyly, “is an Ace up mysleeve.” Friday, October 11 - Day 100 As soon as phone lines open, Vincent Giovanni is in the crowded telephone room where fortyprisoners are waiting to make calls on two phones. Leigh Donovan is among the prisoners andstands next to Giovanni in the crowd. Like most prisoners, he’s angered at the length of timebeing used by the two prisoners currently occupying the phones. “For fuck sakes, mates! Can yaplease hurry the fuck up? I’ve got a parole hearing in a couple of months!” “Fuck you!” comes the response from the larger of the men on the telephones. “Uncivilized niggers,” spits Leigh under his breath as Vincent notices the swastika tattoo’ed onthe young Australian’s chest. “Hey,” Vincent says to the young rocker. “Watch your fucking mouth.” “What the fuck do you care?” asks Leigh, turning a harsh look at the bullish Italian. The leftside of Leigh’s face is still showing the large purple bruise given to him by Tim McDonald. “Just keep that shit to yourself, alight?” Vincent says. “I don’t want to listen to it.” Leigh smirks, but obliges, secretly wanting to stick a blade into the fattest part of the bullishItalians body. As time moves along, Vincent and Leigh finally find themselves at the front of the line as asmallish Latino man finishes up his telephone conversation. “It’s about fucking time,” Vincentsays, tired from standing in the line up for nearly two hours. “What the fuck are you talking about, fat ass?” Leigh asks, slapping Giovanni roughly on theshoulder. “I’m going next. I’ve got to call my fucking lawyer. I got parole in a coming soon.” “Fuck you and your lawyer,” Giovanni says harshly. “I’ve been waiting two God Damn weeks tomake a phone call, and I’m going to fucking do that. You can wait a few minutes longer.” “You God damn guinnea!” snaps Leigh. “Always thinking you can push people around. All thefucking same, you motherfuckers! Well, not this time! As soon as that spic gets off the phone,I’m getting on it. You got that?” “We’ll see,” says Vincent, turning back to the phones ahead of him. “Skinny disrespecful fuck,”he mutters. “Time’s up!” Benji Graham, who’s standing at the side of the telephones with four other C.O’s,barks, and immediately the small Latino man finishes up his conversation. Graham steps up tothe crowded line up where Leigh and Vincent are waiting. “Which one of you ladies is next?” “I’m next!” barks Leigh. “Like fuck!” Vincent says, grabbing Leigh by his open flannel shirt and tugging him back. Seeing Graham is holding a clip board of names, Vincent immediately spits his. “VincentGiovanni, Unit B.” “Giovanni, eh?” sighs the Benji who’s known as Ahab because a scar that runs from his facedown beneath his collar. “Wait a minute, bud. Says here you’re on the restricted list.” “Restricted list?” Giovanni gasps. “What the fuck are you talking about? What the fuck is arestricted list?” “Means you can’t make a call until further notice, partner,” Benji smiles, looking up from hisclipboard. “Now, unless you’ve got some other business here, I suggest you get your ass back toUnit B before I throw you in the hole for loitering.” Enraged, Giovanni is barely able to hold down his emotions long enough to stomp out throughthe crowd, and back to Unit B. Watching all the while is a very amused Timmy McDonald. Saturday, October 12 - Day 101 A cat will wait for the most opportune moment to pounce... When noon rolls around on Saturday, the buzzer blasts loudly through Unit B and the prisonersline up like sheep to head to the cafeteria. Few shouts of rebelliousness and anger are heard, asusual, but for the most part, things are kept orderly. Tim McDonald takes his time in his cell, pulling his green shirt over his head and tossing it asidebefore looking for something warmer - even in California prisons, winter has its affects. Justthen, he finds himself fighting for breath with a strong arm that has wrapped around his throat. “I know it was you, motherfucker!” Vincent Giovanni’s voice whispers into Tim’s ear as hechokes the life from his body. “I fucking know it! You God damn weasel! You think I haven’tseen the way you run your shit around here!? With the fucking guards in your pocket!? Youthink I don’t know that? You little prick!” Tim realizes quickly that it’s a losing struggle - there’s no telling how many men died in thisvery position. “I know who you are!” Tim is able to say with what breath he’s allowed. “You’rehere to kill me!” “Not just you,” Vincent, known on the outside as Vinny G, says. “You fucking bastard! Youslept in this very cell, while Doug Hattoni slept below you. He gave you every opportunity in theworld! But that wasn’t enough for you! You wanted more! You didn’t just want to learn fromthe man, you wanted to be him! So you killed him, you little fuck! And now you’re going to pay- everyone’s going to pay!” Suddenly, a strange voice enters from behind - “Not today.” Vinny G is surprised by the amount of strength possessed by the man who pulls him away fromTim McDonald. He turns around to find himself staring into the fiery eyes of Jason Snow - justreturning from Fusion. Instinctively, the bullish Vinny fires a roundhouse right at Snow, butSnow easily blocks it and fires back with a right of his own that connects cleanly on Vincent’sjaw. Vincent stumbles back until he finds himself against the wall and realizes that Snow is morethan a formidable opponent. With deceptive quickness, Vincent fakes a blow to Snow’s ribs andthen strikes with a vicious elbow to the face. At this moment, Snow also realizes that Vincent isa formidable opponent. Youth and training are on Snow’s side, however, and his next attack, a devastating superkick,floors the middle aged Italian as it has so many professional wrestlers before. “I owe you,” Tim says after realizing Vincent has no intentions of getting up for another shot. “Fucking right you do,” Snow answers, glaring deeply into Tim’s eyes. “But if we can get out ofthis thing with my family alive, we’ll call it even.”
