The Sage Of A Champion: Part 12

6 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-6-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!Part 12” Again Same - Chapter 4: A Sidetracking Sent Straight From HellAgain Same - Day 6: The Insane One Has Been Chosen :: The scene is picked up right where it left off with General Powers and BladeD (along with Athens Mir) getting ready to discuss Main Plan A, Main Back-Up Plan B, and Back-Up Plan C. But before they get to that there is still information that needs to be attended to, then the plans can begin to unfold. :: General Powers: Before we get on with making out the plans… BladeD. Explain to me your views on Metal Armor… And you know what I mean by that. What its strong points were? What its weak points were? And all of that other good stuff like that. BladeD: All right, then. Metal Armor like you know was a diabolical machine known to man at its time. It had weapons that we didn’t even think were possible. The machine itself was made to destroy other countries and enemies. That was basically its only purpose and that is why it was so powerful. Its strongest points were actually down on the bottom of it’s feet. Because if you remember Metal Armor, was four stores high from head to toe, so it had to have missiles on its feet to make it easier to attack ground enemies. The missiles that came out of the feet were the only heat seeking missiles Metal Armor had. Now you would think that because Metal Armor was, four stores high that the metal it does take to build the thing up is so thick that our normal guns wouldn’t hurt Metal Armor at all and you are right. Athens Mir: So the missiles were mainly used for destroying ground building, but couldn’t Metal Armor just step on or over the building itself? BladeD: Well, see. That is thing. It could if it wanted too, but the only problem is that the building explode when they get stepped on like that, so it does more harm to Metal Armor that way… Athens Mir: Then why heat-seeking missiles? BladeD: Well Metal Armor is a machine to destroy… When Russia wanted to destroy us they didn’t want to just destroy the people that rule this country, they also wanted to destroy just regular civilians. Also, if we had some of our troops using rocket launchers or something of that sort that could actually do damage to Metal Armor, Metal Armor has the heat-seeking missiles to find them, because basically all Metal Armor had to do was shoot out a couple of the missiles and it would keep going until it found a person or in some cases, something with a lot of heat, before it would explode. It was designed to be able to maneuver around trees and things of that sort. General Powers: But didn’t also tell me once that Metal Armor had missiles on the back of its feet also, or was that just a rumor I heard? BladeD: I am not exactly sure if you heard that from me, but Metal Armor did, in fact, have missiles on the back of his feet, but I am not exactly sure if they were heat seeking missiles or not. I read some information on it that it did have heat-seeking missiles and I read some information on it that it didn’t. General Powers: But, I thought it was like Violent Ray, in the sense, where there is only one book. BladeD: Well General, what you have to do understand is that there is only one book on how to build to machines themselves, but that one book only tells how to build it. There are other books that till how to use it. I think for Metal Armor there were five books. “The Book of Metal Armor”, was the main one that told how to build it. After that there was four other books. One book told how to use it/operate it, one book told how to use it/what it could and couldn’t too/anything in that category, one book told what kinds of weapons to use on it, and finally the last book was just a book that told all the other miscellaneous information about Metal Armor. General Powers: And you read all those books from start to finish while you were in Japan? BladeD: Well, I tried to read up on it has much as I could, but I didn’t get to read all the books. I read “The Book of Metal Armor” from start to finish, but the rest I just got to read however much I could in the time I had to read it in, so no I didn’t get to read it all. I basically read enough to build up a strategy for me to destroy the thing when I had to destroy back then. General Powers: Anything else in particular about the feet of Metal Armor. BladeD: Metal Armor was flat-footed… that is about it and because the machine is so heavy Metal Armor didn’t have the ability to ever run, so that is my theory to why Metal Armor was flat-footed. General Powers: That is a good observation there BladeD. Was Metal Armor very detailed body wise or did it just look like a very powerful machine… Meaning did it have color or something of that sort that you would see in like a…. Power Rangers or ::pause:: Transforms show? BladeD: Well actually, Metal Armor was pretty detailed, but it was only three colors. Black, brown, and white, so you didn’t much color, but they didn’t fool around like that… I mean this thing wasn’t meant to be used all the time and be on air, crap like that, so really it had no reason to be “colorful.”General Powers: You are right there. I wouldn’t expect it to be colorful, but it must have been very detailed shape wise. BladeD: Yea… I mean Metal Armor almost looked like a ::pause:: Robocop in a sense. The machine also kind of… this is hard to say with a straight face, but in a sense Metal Armor kind of looked “buff” in a way. Athens Mir: Ha! Ha! Buff Machine. BladeD: Well, I am not sure how else to explain it. ::laughs:: Basically, the machine had definition. That is really the only way I can explain it. General Powers: I understand now, but do you have any idea of what Violent Ray will look like or might be capable of doing? BladeD: Well, Violent Ray may be “colorful” and it will be able to do a whole lot more then Metal Armor from what I have been told, so I am almost scared to know what Violent Ray will have and be able to do. General Powers: I guess that is understandable, and that is why two men should go in. BladeD: Well, we aren’t about to get into this fight ago that is certain. Athens Mir: Good, because I don’t want to get into either. General Powers: But were you ever able to retrieve AND keep any of the books that you read? BladeD: No. ::pauses:: Why do you ask? General Powers: Because I was going to have Mir here read the books and get a better idea of what to expect, so he wouldn’t be totally be caught off guard. Athens Mir: I am getting a good enough idea of what expect just listening to you guys, so there is no way I could be caught off guard. BladeD: ::sarcastic laugh:: I wish! Athens Mir: What is that supposed to mean? BladeD: I read all those books. I saw all the pictures and diagrams of Metal Armor. I will still caught off guard. Not too much because I had ideas of what to expect, but I was still caught off guard and I would have been totally caught off guard if I would have just listened to people describe Metal Armor, so you may not know it now, but you are really in for it now buddy. Athens Mir: Well, I doubt it because I am not caught off guard easy. BladeD: I have no clue why you choose to try and be better than me because that is the stupidest thing you could do in this kind of situation and that,:looks over to the general:, General Powers is why I am so uncomfortable with Athens here being the second person in this mission. Athens Mir: Well, maybe… just maybe… I am better than you BladeD…. And you just don’t know it yet! (The scene is going to cut here. Don’t you just love it when the scene gets cut in the middle of heat like this? It keeps you in all and wonder, so that way you will want to read the next part and find out what happens in the middle of all this heat. In fact, I am not going to hint one word of what is coming up next because I want you to read the next part, so you can just find out for yourself instead of you trying to guess your way into the next chapter, so get ready because I don’t think you are going to want to miss the next part of this amazing sage! Part 13 is next!)
