002 - Total Chaos, Mass Confusion

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-28-02 Reminisce Over the Guitar {:: Time was it a while ago, back way when, musical legends standing above or seated, smelling the air as thick and gassy as it was. A movement of deep incense to get the mind going, the fingers twitching in anticipation waiting for the feeling of the correct time to start. Yet right here and right now, the voice breaks through the empty silence, cutting through deep and solid to make the sonnet even that more beautiful. ::} {:: It was like this, people like Robert Plant, Kurt Cobain, and Jim Morrison. They all did this, light a cigarette and let the mind ease when it was necessary. I don't actually pin point what happened in their careers that looks like they were martyred towards the religion of music. ::} {:: In essence, its is the same mentality a person who grows up with the religion thrown onto them as a simple question of belief. There is nothing sent towards the mind to sway it from the originality of which dreams were conceived and eventually reached. ::} In my time, I prepare the rhyme as a weaponUsing as a drug it keeps me hype and all collecting.The narcoleptic silence all of the warriors before me,trying to attain.For there is nothing to gain when I'm on the top.The bullets rain down like a copycat storm,you cannot stop the yearned from earning and not having mourn.Its not hologram, its up life and personal eyes glued to the man standing in front of youBeocoming your stars right towards hitting forty-eight bars while people listening in cars and then you nod your head, the shrinking white to the color blue.I never sleep because sleep is the cousin of deathAs this message makes me lose my breathBecause we know I'm too tight to lose by nines and glocksClicking by and by thanks to a tick tockThe night howls a crescendo behind methe wails of a manic-depressive all for me to see.I got my mind right... {:: The words, 'I got my mind right' repeat over three times, as Dante nods his head, feeling in a tight warp of labyrinth towards success. Through the future, it was told in dreams and flashbacks in which were used before him and will be after him. ::} Nothing shall take me backFor its time for my to use this knowledge given to take actYou keep silence in the hollow tips of a nineI've got a plan for your ass and it suits just fine.Don't blink for a second; you may miss the action of strifeI guess why we call this existence life; you don't understand it enough to be under the knifeSurgeons taking the scalpels and digging deep for your brainpower.Taking what I've already given to myself, leaving the taste in the mouth sour.For if you only had the correct powerYou wouldn't be lying in the dirt like a coward.I got my mind right, {:: If life had been between a rock and a hard place, he continually found the rock starring him in the face. He felt his body, standing under a purple hue. The drum set right behind him, the sticks laying on the cylindrical shaped stand. The microphone lies in his right hand, tightly clenched as if it were his tool of trade. ::} {:: His lips part at every lyric coming out of his voice, the dubbed 'Lyrical Messiah' continues as he lays back in a chair. The band members right behind him banging on the drums without abandonment, the guitar playing around like silly putty in containers with the label Play Do on them. No silence could cut him from the experience as he felt like he had done something important to the people as the song died down and finally faded. ::} .:[Dante]:. Amazing, I know it was. {:: The light around him changes from the passive darkness that only three minutes ago dropped down onto the body lavish with foreboding. He sees himself, right inside of Music Television Studios and the Unplugged set. He hears the clapping of the audience as he bows telling them mouthed pleases. ::} {:: From his point of view, there were no sounds, just the repeating volumes of music, each changing, each a different movement of what was his thoughts and what were his closest chances of adaptation. In anticipation and intervening fantasy, he could have become whatever he liked, a mystical animal, a warrior unmatched by any on the planet of Earth, clicking of the teeth and the laughing continued playing. ::} {:: And then, the eyes changed from what he once felt on a stage, to a much larger one. ::} ~>The Other Side of The Hourglass
