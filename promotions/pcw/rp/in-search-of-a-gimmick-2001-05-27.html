In Search Of A Gimmick

27 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-27-01 [Fade In] [The familiar smell of a hotel; a smell wrestlers become very acquainted with during their countless road trips from venue to venue. PCW Wrestler Ryan Stephens lounges in a hotel chair featuring a thick navy cushion, and a hard wooden frame. The chair is pushed back away from a round wooden table in which Stephens’ feet are perched atop. He’s wearing sky blue mesh shorts and a white cut-off T-shirt. He’s relaxing and reading a New Jersey newspaper.] [The door to the hotel is opened all the way letting all the summer air into the hotel room. It’s open because his wife Dana has just stepped out onto the balcony of their 3rd story room to scout out a possible restaurant for dinner. We are unable to see her, merely her shadow cast on the open door by the summer sunlight.] [All of a sudden, Stephens drops his feet from the edge of the table, and leans forward placing the paper on the table, before scooting the chair up closer to the table. He stares intently at the paper for a moment, and then calls out to his wife.] Streak: Baby! Baby, come in here and check this out. [His wife emerges in the doorway and then enters the hotel room, gracefully pulling a piece of dangling hair behind her ear. Her curly blonde hair is down, and wet with mousse. She shuts the door gently behind her and looks at her husband and then at his finger pointing to somewhere particular on the newspaper.] Dana: Don’t tell me you’ve found a house for us to buy way up here in New Jersey. You know I want to build a house down in Texas Ryan. Streak: No, no, that’s not it. I was just thinking, that here are all these classified ads right? Dana: [small laugh] Well yes baby, there has always been classifieds in the paper to my knowledge. [she smiles again] Streak: Well I was just reading through some of these and they have classified ads for virtually everything Dana. Dana: [smiling gently at Ryan] Where are you going with this baby? Streak: I thought maybe I could put an ad in here saying that I was seeking a good gimmick and someone would sell me one. [Dana starts laughing at her husband, now getting the point of his joke. Stephens laughs along with her for a moment before pushing the paper aside and patting his right thigh in an effort to get his wife to come sit in his lap.] Dana: You think you’re pretty funny don’t you? [She says as she makes her way over to him, and sits down in his lap wrapping her arms around his neck so that they’re quite close.] Streak: I know I’m pretty funny baby; however, I also know that I really do need to establish some sort of gimmick for myself. It has become apparent to me that being a babyface who doesn’t break any rules, stands up for what is right, is a good role model, and has a heart of gold isn’t a gimmick. Dana: Well I don’t see why not. Streak: [sighs] That’s beside the point. I need one. Do you have any ideas? [She reaches up and kisses him on the cheek, and bats her little green eyes at him.] Dana: Your gimmick can be that of a white supremacist and you can run around yelling racial slurs so that Rush gets so mad at you he can’t see straight, and you can beat him. Yeah, that will work, because then after you beat Rush you could fight that other guy who just created the N.I.G.G.A.’s, Jordan X Shabazz, and beat him too. Streak: [laughs for just a second] I’m not racist baby, besides I don’t know if that will work against Rush or Shabazz, they’re both pretty talented wrestlers. What if I just stay the Streak and claim I’m the fastest white boy ever and run circles around the ring until my opponents get tired of looking at me, and I can nail them with a missile dropkick? [Now both Dana and Ryan are laughing, but a tiny look of concern actually does appear on Ryan’s face. He seems to really be worried about getting himself a gimmick of some sorts.] Dana: What if you called that really old manager of yours? He could probably help you think of something. Streak: Raymond “Fingers” Douglas? [Her eyes light up now recognizing the name and putting a picture together of him in her head.] Dana: Yeah, that’s the one! Streak: Ewww, no baby, he’s a psycho. Last I heard he got ten years for trying to make an infant gnaw on his decapitated finger necklace. Dana: Ewww, yeah don’t call him, but maybe that’s what you need, a gimmick that’s totally insane and gross. Everyone would wonder what the heck you were thinking and you could generate a lot of heat. [Stephens is smiling at his wife as she speaks. She looks up at him and smiles on account of she doesn’t know what he’s smiling about.] Dana: What are you smiling at? Streak: Just the fact that you’ve been hanging around me so much that you’ve picked up all this wrestling lingo like gimmick and heat and stuff. Dana: Well you’re making all the money so I figured if I learn more about the sport, then I can help you to make more money, maybe. [she smiles] [Stephens hugs his wife tightly and then lifts her off his lap and stands up. He lifts his hands high above his head, stretching and yawning at the same time. You don’t get a whole lot of sleep in the wrestling world. He stares down at his wife who is wearing a spaghetti strap shirt and tiny white cotton shorts.] Streak: Well on account of all your help, I’m going to take you out to eat to whichever restaurant you picked out down there, and we’ll just enjoy our meal and worry about figuring out a gimmick for me later. [She nods as if to approve of the idea and then she takes his big hand in hers. She opens the door with the other hand and pulls him outside. All of a sudden we see his hand reach back inside the hotel and feel around on the table for his wallet, as he finds it and snags it from the counter he pulls the door shut and we fade out.]
