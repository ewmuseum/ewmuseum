Ebonics Erased (In da hood)

19 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19 - 11 - 02 It's been very well documented that the collective known as Pranks With Attitude have an interesting week set before them. Tuesday Turmoil will see the jovial Jester Gaz Green run the gauntlet as he battles in the Cruiserweight Championship Tournament. The P.W.A behemoth Nerezza will aid his partner in this quest for singles success but will also be hoping for a similar success story when the bell sounds for the Alamo match set for the Pay-Per-View. However walking from Alliances still Tag Team Champions remains the dynamic duo's main priority, a task made harder by the booking system of one Commissioner, Eleanor Kannon-Hall. THE FATAL FOUR-WAY ELIMINATION MATCH 8 Combatants 4 Teams 1 Championship The scene opened on yet another caper in the long running saga of PCW's Pranks With Attitude with only a few days too spare, the duo have left the bright lights and adrenalin charged city of Las Vegas. From the electrical awe that is the City Of Sin, we open on the grim backdrop of Harlem New York, a polar opposite to that of the Jester's hometown. 'Bring the merchandise?' hissed the satanic goliath Nerezza. Through the decay of run-down buildings and graffiti ridden streets a large black van sat stationary on the side-walk, churning out smoke and doing it's bit for the ozone layer it was the mobile command post for Pranks With Attitude. Usually the pair would mastermind the masses misery from Gaz Green's Las Vegas apartment but on this particular mission they have decided to broaden their arisen, baring the question, 'What has P.W.A installed for the people of Harlem?' 'Merchandise? This isn't the 'Cubs' on a mission to sell cookies, or in our case t-shirts and memorabilia-....' retorted the distinctive voice of the Emerald Menace. Gaz remained hidden from sight, obscured from view and guarded by his gladiator as he rummaged in the storage hold of the van. Walking in the background, playing a cameo role were natives of the area who peered over to that of Nerezza with questioning glares. Harlem is renowned for its population of blacks and seeing a white man is a real rarity, a rarity on the scale of re-discovering the Dodo but idly they stroll by. 'Hurry up back there!' murmured the Living Nightmare, sticking his head through the gaping doors of the van and returning with a twitch in his mouth, as though he were about to laugh. 'MWA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA~!' Finally the silence was broken as Gaz 'The Jester' Green emerged, baring-... Nothing, well nothing but a pair of boxer shorts with the insignia 'Get your coat, you've pulled'. 'They were a Christmas present!' snarled the Prince Of Pranks, looking somewhat embarrassed, his face beaming red. Lucky for the PCW comic, the natives have long since gone and the area returns to its desolate backdrop and the devious duo continues to hatch this charade with no interference. The Jester's comrade continued to erupt with hysterics as Gaz removed a sandwich board from the storage hold and with little aid strapped the article across his shoulders. 'I HATE NIGGAZ - I hope you know what your doing!?!' said Noscosto Ireul Nerezza, questioning his partners sanity as he recites the wording on the board. You could believe this board is going to get a negative reaction but to what extent thought the vampire as he shook his head frantically before entering the driver’s side of the vehicle. Gaz gripped a simple dai-cap and placed the article over his head, making him look like a crazed Welshman and descended into the bowels of the city, raising a thumb as he happily strode along the sidewalk. Seeing the thumb held in the air, Nerezza hit the accelerator and the van jerked forward through it's own created mist of exhaust fumes. 'What am I doing?' gulped the Lord Of Laughter, taking a page from Skitzo's book he spoke to himself openly, only unlike his Team Bastardos counterpart he did so worryingly. Walking down a long stretch passing further decay and misery, something that spurned a smile, almost undetected by the camera, Gaz continued a charade that could be deemed racist by that of the public and more importantly his peers. Such a prank may have serious repercussions for the team of Pranks With Attitude, certainly from a Playaz Inc standpoint but could also see them getting on the right side of one Xavier Kannon after some of the comments he has recently said. 'What the fuck?!?' said an elderly black gentleman as Gaz waltzed past. 'How we diddling squire?' responded the Jester, using his infamous greeting line, usually found offensive by those adorning the Northern parts of America. 'Have ya lost your bloody mind, son?' Gaz simply kept smiling, but the man's words were true enough, 'Has he finally lost his mind?' Dwelling on such a question, the PCW comic strolled further into the heart of Harlem, again drawing attention to himself but his mission was far from concluded. 'Talk about backstabbing - I'd rather take a knife here in the midst of these infidels than one from any of Team Bastardos!' groaned Gaz, his mind ticking over the parties involved and two of those have never associated with the Jester before hand. Skitzo-... Fair play to the demented one he had at-least made the effort in trying to get to know Gaz 'The Jester' Green. They spent a day together where tension mounted and still the bonding continued, he may not associate with Pranks With Attitude exclusively but has shown no ulterior motive. While-... Vilas Lavagna hasn't made any effort. Not really a team player we shall find out where his loyalties lie when Alliances backs him into a corner like a wild animal, will he come out guns blazing, defending the good name of Team Bastardos or will he buckle under pressure and show his true colours. Questions looming in the mind of Gaz Green with no immediate answers, these answers to materialise in five days time but until then P.W.A must keep the faith. 'WHA DAT FUCK IZ GOIN ON IN YOUR SICK HEAD BRUV'ER?!?' Standing before the incoherent comedian were a bunch of black youths, typically clad in baggy cargo pants, draping tops and laden in gold jewellery they tapped what appeared to be a basketball against the concrete before launching the item at the cranium of one Gaz Green. 'YOU'SE LISTENIN DUDE!!! I SAID WHAT DA FUCK IS GOIN ON?' 'WAIT, YOUR LIKE THAT PC-DUBYA DUDE WHO'S BEEN BITCHING WITH DOSE BRUV'ERS PLAYAZ INC!!!' continued one youth in particular, brushing his hand through his dread locked hair and smiling with a sinister grin. Another of this five-man strong party stepped forward and picked up where his colleague left off. 'So you be hating niggaz!' Suddenly a knife shone in the radiant sun, glistening and almost blinding the Jester it lunges forward piercing the sandwich board and remaining imbedded in the wood. Missing Gaz literally by inches, the group giggle in unison, even taking the time to mock his choice of underwear. 'I'm lost-.... Someone stole my clothes and left this board in its place, so I decided to give my compadres, Playaz Inc a tinkle in the hope of a lift or maybe a change of clothes, see where I'm going?' replied the King Of Komedy. 'YOU'SE THINK WE WERE LIKE BORN YESTERDAY MOTHAFUCKA?!?' roared the leader once more, snarling and fuelled by his increasing rage he removes another blade. 'YOU WONT BE SOOOOOO LUCKY WHITE BOY DIS TIME, YOU HEAR ME, OH, YOU CAN BELIEVE IN DAT!!!' As the black youths hand draws back, almost sealing Gaz Green's fate, a thunderous screech echoes followed by the arrival of the van, drove by the Jester's guardian angel, Noscosto Ireul Nerezza. The Playaz Inc substitutes looked in disbelief as the side door sprung open, unveiling the Russian beauty Natalia Miyagawa brandishing a nasty piece of equipment, namely the 'Ebonics-Eradicator'. Trigger-happy and gleaming like a child at Christmas, Natalia opened fired, leaving each member dazed and confused, coincidentally allowing Gaz Green to make his escape. ZZZZZZZZZZZZZAAAAAAAAPPPPPPPP~! ZZZZZZZZZZZZZAAAAAAAAPPPPPPPP~! Again the weapon sounds. In due course the van again shot off like a rocket, as the Trickster Team made their getaway. High-tailing from the scene under a fog of smoke and almost choking the poor souls to death, the camera panned once more on the youthful blacks as they try to fathom the latter half of events. 'How very strange!' spoke the authority figure with an English accent, a complete u-turn to they Ebonics speech minutes earlier. 'Well let’s get down the boozer and sink a couple, pet!' 'AYE CHAMPION!!!!' As the group got acquainted with their new speech pattern, the scene slowly faded, closing another conniving caper by PCW's Pranks With Attitude, begging the question, 'What have they installed for Alliances2K2?' - THE END -
