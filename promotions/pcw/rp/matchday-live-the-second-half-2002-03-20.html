Matchday Live: The Second Half

20 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/3/02 One-Nil, One-Nothing, the most commonly boring score in football...apart from a no score draw. In this particular football match the score is one-nil and clock says 45 minutes played. The referee, a blind swordsman, stands in the center circle and signals the start of the match. The two teams dressed in their respective kits, Allstar Team wearing red and blue shirts and shorts, Team PTC wearing black and red shirts and shorts, begin the second half of football. Duke Williams: Its 1-0 to Team PTC folks! Ben J. Pailthorpe looks a little confused, slightly out of it, Juan Borro skips confidently past the right back and sends over a looping cross, Ronaldo the brilliant Brazillian center forward leaps up and connects the ball with his forehead, Scottish goalkeeper Robert Renfrew leaps to his side and tips the shot around the post. Duke Williams: What the hell is wrong with Pailthorpe? Ben wanders aimlessly into the penatly area as the cross is taken by Roberto Carlos, DarkStar rises above the attackers to head the ball away and as the players surge forward with the ball Ben lags behind. Duke Williams: That Pailthorpe better get his act together. Andy Murray recieves the ball and he tries to cross it into the opponents penalty area, however he trips over his shoelaces and lands arse over tit on the ground much to the mirth of the Allstar team. Duke Williams: Murray has got all the heart in the world...just none of the talent. Robert Pires picks up on the ball and plays it to Zidane, the French World Cup winning star slots the ball through the defense to the waiting Juan Borro who elbows BJP in the face as he goes past him and calmly slots it past the keeper. Duke Williams: REF! He hit him in the face. The End however consults his linesman and gives the goal. Duke Williams: You must be joking. 1-1. Team PTC 1-1 Allstar TeamChase 20' Borro 59' Ben stays down and the replacement physio comes out onto the field, Ben props himself up on his elbows while the physio examines his nose. Physio: Looks ok, whats wrong with you? Ben J. Pailthorpe: I got elbowed in the face. Physio: No, I mean why are you acting so dazed. Ben J. Pailthorpe: Something the CEO of Fifpro said to me. $500,000 if Team PTC lose, I could use that kind of money. Physio: If they win? Ben J. Pailthorpe: $500,000. Physio: Then win, dont let the thousands of people here to cheer you guys on down, dont let your friends down, dont let your career down. Ben stands up and the physio leaves the field. Duke Williams: Game on. Team PTC kick off and Aimz plays it back to Chase, the tricky midfielder runs forward and plays a neat one two with Jay Sylo, Chase continues his surging run and plays a ball through the Mr. UK who blasts his shot over. Duke Williams: Close one there for Team PTC. Schmeichel restarts by throwing the ball out to Neville, Neville passes t to Vieira who dodges Sylo's tackle and passes it to Borro. The PCW star takes the ball foward and has a crack at goal which is easily saved by Renfrew. Duke Williams: That son of a bitch didnt trouble Renfrew there. Renfrew throws the ball to Jayson Frost who in turn plays it to Pailthorpe, BJP runs with the ball before sending a long ball to Chase who traps it nicely and plays a return ball to Pailthorpe who surges towards the penalty area, dodging two defenders Pailthorpe is about to unleash a shot when the goalkeeper tackles him recklessly, he spins through the air and lands on his back. Duke Williams: PENALTY! The End points calmly to wear he believes the spot to be and the crowd of wrestling fans cheer. Duke Williams: Who is gonna take it? Ben gets up and picks up the bal, he calmly walks towards Aimz and hands her the ball, she nods and places it on the penalty spot. Duke Williams: Aimz to take the penalty for Team PTC! The jeers of the crowd dont seem to phase the fiery red head as she calmly runs up and slots the ball towards the left hand corner. Schmeichel dives to his right and watches helplessly as the ball rolls into the bottom corner. Duke Williams: YESSSSS!! Aimz wheels away from goal only to be mobbed by her fellow team mates. Duke Williams: 2-1!!! Team PTC infront again! Team PTC 2-1 Allstar TeamChase 20' Borro 59'Aimz PEN 67' The match restarts with the Allstar team piling on the pressure. Duke Williams: If Team PTC can hold on for 20 minutes they will be victorius. Juan Borro is the maestro in all of their attacks. Robert Pires takes the ball from midfield and passes it accross field to Vieira who passes it to Ronaldo, Ronaldo turns past DarkStar and flicks the ball past Frost into the path of Borro who launches a left footed strike past Renfrew. Duke Williams: Not again! Borro leaps into the air and celebrates with his team mates as Team PTC trudge back to the center circle. Duke Williams: Cant these guys keep a lead? Team PTC 2-2 Allstar TeamChase 20' Borro 59', 73'Aimz PEN 67' Duke Williams: Its all square again. Aimz and Mr. UK kick off and play the ball back to Sylo he passes it out to Murray, Murray trips AGAIN and looks like a total poser in his number seven shirt with imitation Beckham hair. Pires takes control of the ball but he runs into Kay-Nyne who disposesses him. Duke Williams: Nice tackle by Kay-Nyne. Kay-Nyne passes to Sylo as the clock ticks to 80 minutes. Duke Williams: Times running out. Sylo skips past two players and plays a long ball which just goes past Aimz and into the arms of Peter Schmeichel. The Danish goalkeeper punts it upfield, Pires chests down the ball and starts on a run up the wing, the clock ticks up to 83 minutes before the French winger gets stopped by Pailthorpe. BJP passes it to Chase who passes it to Mr. UK, Mr. UK turns and gets tackled by Vieira who plays a one two with Zidane. The midfielder plays the ball to Borro who surges forward to the pentaly area, Sylo makes a tackle but the ball falls to Vieira who plays it out wide to Pires. Duke Williams: Team PTC need to clear it. Pires runs to by the corner flag as the clock reachers 85, he launches a cross into the area which DarkStar heads off for a corner. Duke Williams: Corner to the Allstar Team as time ticks away. Pires steps up and looks into the box the players are all jostling for space, Ben J. Pailthorpe is standing on the edge of the area, the ball is lofted in and the players in the box rise up, DarkStar connects to it with his head and sends it flying away, BJP begins chasing after hit, he flicks it over the heads of Vieira and Zdiane and chests it down to his feet, still running he spins past Adams and looks up at the rapidly approaching penalty area, Schmeichel leaves his goal line and runs towards him, he reaches the edge of the penalty area.... Duke Williams: 89 minutes! You can do it! ...Schmeichel tries to tackle but Ben calmly flicks it over him and taps it into an empty net! Duke Williams: YESSSSSSS!!! The fans erupt as The End siganls the end of the game. Duke Williams: Its all over, 3-2 to Team PTC, the wrestlers have done it, Juventus, Manchester United, Boca Juniors, Porto, every soccer player in the world, today your boys took a hell of a beating. Team PTC celebrates in the center of the field as the Allstar team trudge slowly off of the pitch. Kid Wonder runs onto the pitch and joins in with the celebrations. Team PTC 3-2 Allstar TeamChase 20' Borro 59', 73'Aimz PEN 67'Pailthorpe 90' Alex Ferguson goes ballistic on the touchline and begins throwing things at his players as they get booed off by their own fans. Duke Williams: Wrestling saves its pride by beating soccer at its own game! Ben jogs to the side of the pith and up to the CEO of Fifpro. Ben J. Pailthorpe: I beleieve you have something that belongs to me. The CEO mutters and hands Ben a check for $500,000. CEO: You were lucky... Ben J. Pailthorpe: No WE were better. The scene fades as the celebrations continue and rioting begins in the streets outide the Silverdome.
