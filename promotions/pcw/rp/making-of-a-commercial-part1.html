Making of a commercial...(Part1)

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-7-02 With Supremency coming up in five days Mark is thinking about that number one contendership to the Rising Star title, but that does not even measure up to what the feeling Mark will have when he gets his hands on Jordan X Shabazz. It is not even about the Rising Star contendership now, but about pride for each others race. It is a black versus white match and that is a match that will be talked about for a while along with their feud that is already heating up. But even before Supremency Mark has a tag match who he will team with Politically Incorrect to take on the flamers in pink, The Heartbreakers along with Jordan X Shabazz. With that match before Supremency it should do a lot to heat up their feud with each other for the pay per view. The scene opens up to a recording studio. Now when you think of a recording studio you think about music and an artist recording a song. But that is not the kind of record studio it is. It is a television recording studio where inside they have a set made up for a commercial for an important drink. The director is pacing back and forth between a black curtain with the backdrop behind that curtain, but will reveal what it is when that certain somebody arrives. 'Where in the hell is Mark Makonie?' screams the director to his camera crew. 'Sir I am sure that he will be here to do this commericial if he signed that contract to be our spokesperson.' says a tall man from one of the camera crew. 'Well he better be here or he can kiss that five hundred thousand dollars away and down the drain!' screams the director once again. 'Thousand dollars away and down the drain, that rhymes.' The camera crew chuckle a little bit, but when they see the director turn around they stop and give him a straight face as if they were acting normal but they know that that comment was funny. The back door of the studio makes a lound bang sound as the metal door closes behind Mark Makonie who comes in dressed in baggy blue jeans, white Converse shoes, and a red with an African American person on the front with a X through the person. The director turns around and sees who has just entered the building and is relieved when he sees that it is Mark Makonie. 'Where have you been for the past hour?' asks the director trying to keep his cool, but is in Mark's face. 'First off you don't have to get a milimeter away from my face.' says Mark looking into the brown eyes of the director, Mike. 'What's second off?' asks Mike to Mark's face still real close to it. Mark laughs a bit and then says 'I have been in my apartment catching up on the religion of Scientology, the next religion of PCW.' 'Sceintology? What does that have to do with our commercial?' asks Mike ponndering why he was learing about Scientology. 'Nothing at all I just figured that since I am a rising star that it did not matter what time I got here because I know that if you want your product sold then you would wait for me no matter how late I am.' says Mark managing to get all of that out in one breath. 'We do not have a lot of time so lets get into make up and then we will shoot the commercial.' says Mike backing up away from Mark and making his way down the hallway to the make up room. Mark shakes his head and laughs a bit to see the reaction of Mike's face to find out what he was doing. He goes down the hallway the Mike went down and he sees all of the blue doors on the right hand side with a bunch of writing on them that does not mean anything to Mark. He goes into the room that says make up. He enters the room to see Mike put a packet of papers on the counter right in front of the chair. A very large lady looking about a size of four hundred pounds and about six foot tall comes out of the restroom and the room starts to stink like the McDonald's bathrooms or just those African Americans. Anyways Mark gets in to the chair and he leaves the door open for Mike who goes out of the room with his head set on along with some papers on a clipboard. 'Well how are we going to buff this cute little boy's face up today?' asks the lady trying to be all nice and comforting to Mark. 'First off try not to get all lovey dovey because I am not interested of hooking up with anyone here, especially someone like you.' says Mark looking at the lady in the mirror. 'What is second off?' asks the lady getting close to Mark's ear. 'Second off you are only here to do my make up, so that is what I suggest you do right now so I can get out of here.' says Mark looking at the stack of papers stapled together on the counter. The lady goes over to the counter as Mark picks up the packet of papers. The lady gets a make up bag with all of her supplies inside that certain bag. She drops the bag right in front of Mark and she has to bend down to get it. Mark looks up and and he can see some of the lady's leopard thong sticking out of her size 34 pants. (Women's sizes not men's.) Mark looks at the first page of the packet and reads what that exacltly says. 'The Kahlua Iced Coffee commercial with Mark Makonie as the spokesperson.' Mark reads to himself real quick as he turns to the next page. 'Hunny you need to put that down so that I can do your make up on your little rosy cheeks there.' says the lady looking down at Mark as he is reading the script for the upcoming commercial. Mark shakes his head as he puts the script up on the counter and closes his eyes so that the make up lady can touch up on Mark's pale color. While she is doing his make up Mark is trying to think about Jordan X Shabazz and his whining voice. Mark thinks about Jordan complaining about not making it into People magazine top 50 beautiful people. He says that it was because he was black but as he read off Denzel Washington and Halle Berry who when I last checked were African American people. Then is trying to sue People magazine for racism but although they had African American in their magazine. Mark just laughs as the make up lady is putting some skin toner on his cheeks. 'Hey can you lighten up on the toner because I do not want to look like a damn n***er in this commercial!' says Mark looking at the make up lady sternly. 'If people only knew how much I hate that race because of what they do. I am not in the mood to go there right now so do not even ask.' 'Well did someone wake up on the wrong side of the bed this morning?' asks the lady looking at Mark, putting away the make up in the bag. Mark just shrugs his shoulders as the lady puts back her make up bag down in a cupboard under the sink that is in the room. Mark grabs the script and he heads out of the room, slamming the blue door behind him as the lady comes running towards him from inside the room. Mark hurries up and quickly gets a two by four board and he puts it between the two sides of the door so that the lady cannot get out of the room. Mark makes his way back to the set and he goes over to Mike as he checks out what Mark is wearing. 'You need a shirt that expresses what you are selling to the people out there watching this commercial.' says Mike as he looks at Mark's racist shirt. 'What is wrong with my shirt?' asks Mark looking at Mike and the shirt that he has in his hand. 'It does not sell the product that we want it to sell. Here put this on for the shoot.' says Mike throwing the black shirt at Mark, hitting him in the left arm with the shirt. Mark unfolds the shirt and he looks at it closely. He turns it around and he sees that the shirt has something on the back. He throws off the red shirt that he has on and he puts it on the ground next to him. He revelas his tattoo of the Nazi symbol that is on his inner left bicep. He puts on the shirt and it has the Kahlua Iced Coffee logo on the front and on the back it says the next official drink of PCW. 'Looks great because now you look like you are a real spokesperson.' says Mike checking out Mark in the shirt. 'See do you not get what I am trying to get here? Did you ever see the 7-Up commercials where it had the up yours shirt. That is what we want to get across, the product that we are selling.' 'Well I did see the 7-Up commercial for about two seconds then I turned it because it had an African American guy selling the product for them, which he did a horrible job in doing.' says Mark looking at his new and improved shirt that the Kahlua company gave him. 'You are just saying that because you have to because of your gimmick in the wrestling business.' says Mike looking at Mark and his skinhead. 'I know that you can be nice and happy for once in your life instead of just saying that you hate Africa Americans twenty-four seven.' 'Whatever.' says Mark as he shrugs his shoulders as he is heading to the set with the black cloth over the backdrop. Mike the director goes into his black director chair that has the legs made out of cheap wood. He sits down as Mark makes his way over to the set. He is reading the script as he heads over and he almost trips on the set that is up two feet that that ground. Mark sees that right before he trips and he uses the one and only step that is needed to get up on set. There are cameras everywhere, up high, rolling cameras and up close cameras to catch the image of Mark and the commercial. Mark is reading over to lines one more time as Mike is waiting for Mark to get finished. 'Come on Mark I do not have all day.' says Mike as he sits in his chair anxious. Mark throws the script over to the side as Mike nods over to a man that has an action slapper in his hands. He moves over towards the one standing still camera that is up on a tripod. 'Kahlua Iced Coffee commercial with Mark Makonie take one in 3...2...1.' says the guy with the slapper as he is counting down Mark with his fingers. Mark is standing on top of the set, nervous with his palms sweating and his face getting some sweat on it. He has a lot of thoughts going through his mind right now as he is thinking about getting all of the lines right for this commercial. To Be Continued...
