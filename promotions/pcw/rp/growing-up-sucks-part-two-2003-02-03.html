Growing Up Sucks; Part Two.

3 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 3rd, 2003. “Run, run, as fast as you can…you can’t catch me, I’m the—“ You get the point. What that represents is sort of…no matter how hard you try to you can’t succeed. True; Jackson has secretly been thinking that for close to one year now. He never would have imagined being anywhere near where he is today. He banished himself into thinking he was going to be a lowlife jobber wrestling, that eventually got promoted to swabbing the Dominant Wrestling Organization’s arena floors with a toothbrush; if he was that lucky. But he was luckier. He broke through somehow. People took notice of his unique style and somehow fell in love with the character. And then what happened? Confidence started to build up, and he eventually got better at what he was doing. It is sort of the ‘believe in yourself and you’ll become’ better type of story, but it is actually quite miraculous for Jackson. What were the odds that he was to come from jobber, to DWO World Champion…to the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament bracket seven finalist, to PCW, one of the best leagues today, to holding a piece of PCW Gold. The Rising Star title. Somehow, the title that Rust carried around was looked down upon in PCW, as it was thought just to be a low carder championship. But it’s more than that, really it is. Just the name says it all: Rising Star championship. It was a signal that Jackson could only go one way, and that was up the rankings of PCW. He may not be the best at what he does, but Jackson Rust certainly doesn’t care. All he needs is some time, and sooner or later, he’ll make a name for himself. Like…Flash Magic. The scene reopens from the last Jackson Rust promo, to Rust’s Louisiana home. Sat upon his black Italian leather sofa, Jackson relaxes in a comfortable position, before taking the remote control that was conveniently place upon the backrest of the sofa (which he isn’t using), Jackson flips the large television set on in front of him. Jackson Rust: Dude, where’s everyone? Narrator: They’re coming…they just went to the bingo hall so Al could drop off his so-called ‘hot date’… Jackson Rust: Al had a date? Is Judgment Day coming or something? Hell possibly froze over? Jackson arches an eyebrow, flipping through the channels… Narrator: I’d have a word with Satan, but she’s Courtney Love. Jackson Rust: I know what you mean…Naked News Live…. Narrator: What was that? Jackson Rust: Just skimming through some channels. Narrator: Ah… Rust presses the ‘channel up’ button on the small remote, as the television screen flips through random channels. Narrator: What the hell? You have auto skim? Jackson Rust: I suppose…I know the TV well, and vice versa. It sort of reads my mind ever since my late night TV shadow stained the walls. Narrator: Wha--? Jackson simply uses his arm closer to the free space, and points over his body to a dark gray shadow of Rust against the wall. Jackson Rust: Yeah…seems I ‘rotted my brain’ by watching it so much… Narrator: Well, if you liked watching TV so much, why didn’t you just try to be in a show, or movie? Jackson sighs in a depressed tone. Jackson Rust: I did…once. Narrator: You did? What movie…did you get rejected from? Jackson Rust: Lighten up, dude! That’s my childhood you just insulted… Narrator: Sorry…anyway, what movie? Jackson Rust: I was Rocky in the special test screening. But then they made Sylvester Stallone star in the real version because he had “sex appeal,” “superior acting skills,” and “made it more believable!” You know, all of that high tech showbiz mumbo jumbo! Narrator: Yeah…they’re always tryin’ to screw ya outta something. Jackson Rust: Amen. Narrator: *sighs* Jackson Rust: So…this is how boring a promo is without Al…or Kim…or Steve, huh? Narrator: Yep… Jackson Rust: Can’t you make them appear or some—hey! The Playboy Channel! CHA-CHING! Narrator: You are so child—hey! Turn it up, wouldja! Jackson smirks as he moves his fingers down the remote to press the ‘volume up’ button, and does so successfully…albeit, it doesn’t exactly take a rocket scientist to do that, just as long as you’re not a preppy blonde with big “hoo-hoos”, and you’re fine. Female Voice #1: Do me harder! Female Voice #2: Okay baby…hold still…Ashea! C’m’here and we can get a daisy chain started! Female Voice #3: Okay, sugar. Jackson Rust: Wasn’t Chris’s porn-star friend named Ashea? Narrator: Yeah…but who cares! Look at the stack on her! Jackson Rust: You’re right… Narrator: Of course I am! I’m the Booming Heavenly Voice of Knowledge, Wisdom, and Truth. Jackson Rust: So I’ve heard. From out of shot, a voice is heard… Al’s Voice: We’re back! Jackson quickly looks to the sky, where the Narrator would be. Narrator: Fuck! Turn it to Animal Planet or something! Jackson Rust: And look like a nerd? Hell no! Narrator: Do something! The sound of a door opening is heard and footsteps follow. Kimberly’s Voice: Yeah, what Al said! Steve’s Voice: Y-yeah… Jackson quickly presses two random buttons, landing the television on MTV’s new show, 3 South, just as Kimberly peers into the living room. Kimberly: Whatcha watching? Jackson Rust: Err…uh… Narrator: 3 South…it’s…cool. Al: I love that show! Can I watch? Rust slides up his leather couch, and shrugs, before turning the volume up a bit. Jackson Rust: I don’t see why not. It ain’t doin’ no harm… The scene cuts back to a news desk, with a serious looking newscaster, whom is adorning a wig, trying to believe he is still young, while suffering a major case of denial. Newscaster: This just in! By Al, of infamous JacksonCO Jackson Rust promos, watching television with both Jackson and his Narrator, could in fact cause biological harm, extending to endangering the o-zone layer! Women scream…gasps are heard…R. Kelly walks into the building. R. Kelly: Who wants me to play “Bump and Grind” for y’all?! The news station immediately gets cancelled, and fined, before cutting back to the Jackson promo. Jackson Rust: Hmm…Al watching TV just killed ABC news. Al: I’m just Godlike. Kimberly: No you’re not. Steve: I a-am. Jackson Rust: Yeah, Steve’s a frickin’ candidate for God. Steve: Y-yep. Narrator: When did this happen? Steve: Ju-just n-now. Narrator: Ah…I see. Al: Aye. Kimberly: Bugger off. Jackson Rust: What’s with the British terms? Narrator: Seemed like the thing to do. Jackson Rust: Oh…well, can we continue some other time? Al: Why’s that? Jackson Rust: The writer of the promo has to get off to watch RAW… Narrator: Oh, very well…g’night. [name withheld]: Yeah…later. The scene abruptly fades out. Watching pornography…kids never learn.
