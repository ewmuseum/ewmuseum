A Loss Of Sanity...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday evening... “Oh my time will come this Sunday, you can bet your bottom dollar on that, my love!” “What are you rambling about? And have you seen the STATE of these workers? What the HELL did you do to them?!” (The scene begins to fade in from black on the interconnecting hallway system of The Palace. Bodies line the floor here, and around six or seven feet away from the cameras two figures stand, deep in conversation. The male of the pair wears a long, white loincloth and countless amounts of golden jewellery. The female is a plainly clothed woman of great beauty. The skin of each is tanned heavily, and their hair, both lengthy, is jet black, shimmering in the artificial lighting.) The Pharaoh: “I keep telling you, my jewel of the Nile, they tried to disarm me when I walked into our bedroom! T’was not my fault that they were so aggressive. No! NOT OUR FAULTS! I swear, and so does thy that we shan’t see the black moon of the valley again!” (Isiy looks at him blankly.) Isiy: “Huh? …What the heck are you talking about, Titu? Is something WRONG with you? Up there?” (Pharaoh’s dark eyes flicker wildly as Isiy attempts to make some sense out of the situation.) Isiy: “Hello?! ANYONE there at all?” (With a scowl Pharaoh unexpectedly spits upon the ground, the phlegm splashing on the rug near a fallen guard.) The Pharaoh: “Are YOU accusing me of being unable to fight off this demonic creature? Ay? Who are YOU to tell me what I can DO, and how I SHOULD do it! If WE wish to take our time then so be it, you shan’t get involved, WOMAN!” Isiy: “How DARE you talk to me like that! How bloody dare you!” (Isiy scolds Pharaoh further more by paying him a hateful glare. Titu looks on unaffected for a minute but quickly grows saddened, his eyes widening and his bottom lip trembling.) The Pharaoh: “He’s sorry! He’s SORRY, all right? I’m sorry too. Even if it wasn’t ME who said it, my love! Oh, no…the Gods won’t like this. They won’t LIKE THIS AT ALL!” (Isiy tilts her head to one side, cocking an eyebrow. After a moment or two of surveying her now trembling husband’s behaviour she takes a step back, her eyes wary.) Isiy: “What’s happened to you, Titu? What the HELL has happened to you?” (As though ashamed, Titu looks at her shyly, his voice nothing more than a plea.) The Pharaoh: “It had to happen one day! They always SAID I didn’t know what I was saying and doing, didn’t they? ALWAYS! They never believed what I said about the Gods, they NEVER believed a single word of it! And then HE came along…” (Titu pauses for a second, clenching his fists tight.) The Pharaoh: “Akiyama came along and pretended to give me the POWER of the Gods THEMSELVES! But it was all just a cheap FUCKING lie! A LIE! Then there was Kannon…he couldn’t leave me alone, either…just like THEM! He tortured me physically AND mentally but I never broke, I NEVER cracked…I never let in!” (Isiy looks dumbfounded. She watches vainly as her husband fidgets frightfully and stares down at the men lying near unconscious along the lobby. His eyes shoot up sharply again, and they meet her own, locking with unnatural authority.) The Pharaoh: “And NOW… now there is the demon, Clyde. Do you KNOW what he’s doing to ME?! DO YOU KNOW WHAT’S HAPPENING INSIDE MY HEAD? I feel frustrated and agonizingly hurt at the SAME Godsdamned time! And it is only they, the Gods that keep me going, Isiy! The Gods alone!” (Isiy takes another step back, her face twisted with shock.) Isiy: “Titu, I think you need to see someone. Someone who can HELP you.” (Her expression falls into sympathy as she moves closer to Pharaoh, reaching out to him with a warm smile. However, Xansiv pushes her arm away from him, cowering against the wall for support, scowling insanely.) The Pharaoh: “No! BACK!” (Isiy moves away in surprise as the mentally broken Pharaoh continues ranting…) The Pharaoh: “You can’t help ME! And neither can ANYONE else! I’m the ONLY one capable, Isiy. I am the only one CAPABLE! ARGHHH! Why did the demon bring these curses down upon me?! Why has he weighed my shoulders down SO much?!” (Sighing, Isiy shakes her head, frowning.) Isiy: “Just take a moment to listen to yourself, will you?! You’ve turned into a rambling fool, Titu! You can’t let Clyde get to you like THIS! You have to TRY and surpass what he’s doing to you! This is just what he wants! Can’t you SEE that?!” (Pharaoh squeezes his eyelids shut and forces his palms against his temples, Isiy’s words clearly sparking some sort of pain inside of him.) The Pharaoh: “It’s easier SAID than DONE—” Isiy: “No, it’s not! You have to TRY for the love of God! If you continue to let him get to you like this you’re going to hurt YOURSELF! Look at what you did to these guards today! And the bedroom, Titu; look at our BEDROOM!” (The cameras move away from the couple for a brief interval to demonstrate the wrecked bedroom of the Egyptians. Returning to the passage again, we see Titu curled up against the wall, his arms wrapped around his thick, tanned legs.) The Pharaoh: “You don’t…understand. No one does! NO FUCKING ONE!” Isiy: “Just calm down for a second! You’re beginning to SCARE me, Titu! The guards I can bear with, but this sudden lack of mentality…I can’t! Just think for one damned second about what you’re saying!” (Pharaoh begins to shake his head to and fro.) The Pharaoh: “Oh, I know EXACTLY what I’m saying! And I know EXACTLY what I’m going to do to those who DARE to oppose me this Sunday at March Madness.” (With a worried tone about her voice, the Egyptian beauty dares to ponder her husband’s plans for the up-and-coming PPV and quickly voices them.) Isiy: “What do you mean? …You sounded a little craz—” (She pauses, actually thinking over her words.) Isiy: “…You sounded a little DANGEROUS.” (Xansiv grins wildly.) The Pharaoh: “That’s because I AM dangerous! Look at what I did to Clyde on Fusion! I caved his face in with a sledgehammer, my precious little jewel of the Nile, and LOOK who’s laughing now! ...ME! HAHAHAHA!” Isiy: “What’s happened to you?” (Titu’s eyes grow stern and curious.) The Pharaoh: “What do you mean?” (Isiy gazes down at her husband pitifully and begins to walk away back down the hall.) Isiy: “You’ve changed, Titu. You’ve CHANGED.” (The scene fades.)
