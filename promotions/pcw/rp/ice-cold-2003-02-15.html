Ice Cold

15 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/15/03 Ice Cold Weather changes emotions. It’ll affect the way you feel, think, and act. It’ll make your or break you, irritate you or comfort you, but most importantly it will tell you how to live. The seasons go by and we adapt to live with them. We sometimes change the people we grow close to, we change the habits we grow used to, and we change the ideas our minds rose to. So what happens when it snows? Is it just beauty and peace like it appears or does everything in the past just freeze away never to be found again. We reevaluate the way we are. So what’s so special about the sun not shining? What’s so different about the rain pouring? And what’s so different about the wind swooshing? Is it all in our minds? Or is it more. The scene opens in an almost empty park in the center of Barcelona. The city that is never at rest is finally silent. The wind is blowing hard and the sky is pitch dark, with just the reflection of the moon allowing man to see one another. One man stands alone in the frigid frostbitten air. His lips blue and chapped, with his finger nails short and thin. Other than a few sleeping homeless citizens with just the feeling of rum keeping them warm, Romantico stands alone. On a bench with the letter H.T engraved in the third wooden palette, El Romantico lays back smoking away the empty emotions in his hardened body. The sky never sets, for it always in motion. Making sure everybody could see its beauty. Like a model in a pageant it reveals itself to us. It lets up look deep within it and takes everything we desire from it. Some nights we look at it in awe, and the sky is suddenly a popular celebrity within the mind, and then some days when the weather is too harsh to bear, we let its beauty go unseen. So beautiful… The breath from the mouth of Romantico stays within the view of the eye as the light from his cigarette goes out in a sudden flash. I wonder why the weather changes? It’s one of those things in this world, I may never understand. Does the god up there that looks over us, want to see our reactions, or does he want to make us aware. Aware that something of greater power is out there. That greater power is not the one who controls the seasons, but yet the one who understands everything about the seasons. The one that understands the color, the heat, the drop, the rise, the flavor, the touch the feel…. god you’d have to be a genius to know it all. So I guess you’d have to me… I did not create, I just took that creation and made it so much better. I took from the creation an used it to add to the power I had obtained. You may be confused, but until you understand the moves and steps of my soul, you’ll never understand the words I speak. So I ask the world, how can you defeat a man who understands the biggest mystery in the world? I know the earth, better than I know myself. The mind is a valuable weapon. The body is strong, but the mind is a million times stronger. A fighter doesn’t win with his muscle he wins with his mind. I’ve used it in the past; I’ll use it in the future. So does the weather affect out mind? Sure it does. It all links together. Romantico lies back, as he takes a deep breath in. He smells the aroma of a bottle of Scotch. He looks to his right and sees a dirty bum with crust all around his fingernails, holding a sticky glass bottle, completely empty. He holds on to the thing he loves. And why wouldn’t he? It gets him past the night. It’s there when he needs it. It’ll never leave him nor thing of leaving him. The weather doesn’t affect that bond. Whether cold or hot, that bottle and him go through it all. That bottle changes the way he sees, feels, and thinks. So what does this bottle have to do with anything? Well the bottle is like a person and the bond it has with another person. Could two people be so close that even through the seasons they stay together and depend on one another? Does one feed off the other and take in whatever it can from the other? The weather doesn’t mean anything to a bond this tight, but a bond that tight is bound to break. Everything breaks. Whether it takes weeks, months, or years, it will soon break. Love is no stronger than anything else. The seasons are the strongest force on the earth. And like anything else it will break down, it can break love as well. So why do I speak of love? Of course I am not one that would be in love. Hell, I wouldn’t know the first step of love. I guess I’m the person who likes to be loved, but I won’t shed energy to give it. You see the couple I am talking about is the one of Tessa Windsor and Chris Storm. Both so close. Knowing everything about the other, and not allowing anything to get in the way. They fight together, love together, and live together. So what could disturb such a strong bond? Well the seasons of course. Many say that I am the one that is trying to break up the happy duo, but that isn’t true. I have power, but I don’t have enough power to break a bond such as love. I do know what can break it though, yes, you know it, the seasons. Ice cold will turn to cool warm. When the Madness of March approaches the dynamic duo will fall steep and hard in a pit that they can’t find there way out of. I will not be the one to defeat Chris Storm and Tessa Windsor. I’ll leave it to the seasons. The cold air sweeps from under the feet of Romantico, as the scene disappears.
