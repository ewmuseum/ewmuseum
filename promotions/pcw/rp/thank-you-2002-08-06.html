Thank You...

6 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6 August 2002 Put yourself in the following scenario: You are walking along the street, minding your own business. Then all of a sudden you find an elderly lady trying to cross the street. In an act of kindness, you help her cross the sea of traffic, and once you get over there, you smile at her. Do you expect a thank you? A reward of gratitude? Compensation? Hell no… She hits you with her purse and calls rape on you. That’s not appreciation. Here’s another situation: You are walking out of your apartment, and your neighbor is coming in with a set of groceries in hand. Being the Good Samaritan you are, you offer to help him with his bags. Do you get thanked for your assistance? Do you get a “Thanks come on in and have a beer.”?” No…no you don’t. Instead you get cursed at to “Mind your own f^ckin business” and the door slammed in your face. That’s gratitude for ya… Try this role on for size: You’re walking into the bank, and before you is a man looking to be in a hurry. So in order to keep from getting in the man’s way, you hold the door open for him. He grins a toothless grin and the tattooed man dashes past you and around the corner. Oh, and here comes the security guard, maybe he’ll thank you for assisting that bank patron like a good citizen. “I commend you on your niceness, so here, help yourself to a sack of money…on the house.” Nope…he doesn’t say that. “You just let that f^cking bank robber run right past you ya goddamn asshole!!! “ Now that’s what he says…and for thanks, he gives you a nightstick to the ribs. Guess you’re not looking for that kind of thanks. Well then, how about this: You’re in your home with your lady, and you’re working it out. You know…in the process of the sexual activities. You’re feeling it, getting with the rhythm. Then you feel yourself hitting that spot, and you just go overboard. So what happens? You blow it all out…and collapse next to her. Catching your breath, you look over to her and smile. She looks over to you as well… “Is that it?” Now, that’s sure as hell not being grateful. All of you mortals deal with this sort of thing…you should expect it by now. Ungrateful Unappreciative. Unthankful. All in all…you’re a spoiled species. You take without compensating, be it verbal or material. You take from the planet, and in the end, it’ll end up a wasteland because your greed raped it of resources. You people see it as survival…and maybe in a sense it is. But if you creatures were as intelligent as you claim to be, you would realize that nobody wants to not be appreciated and not be thanked. It’s just plain rude. All humans want to be appreciated for the good things they reach down inside of themselves to do. It makes them feel better about their own inadequacies. If someone can admit that another person has been a help to them...that really shows the humility of a person. That’s just for humans… Gods think differently about thanks. When a god does something for you, or to you…you should thank him no matter what was done. It’s not a matter about boosting his self esteem…it’s a matter of respect. The god didn’t have to let you win that match…the god didn’t have to step aside so you can move up and out of his way…the god didn’t have to leave you barely breathing after the savage beating he gave you. Not thanking the god for what he has done to, for , or against you is just as good as meaning you don’t appreciate your own life. And when you don’t appreciate life, it’s taken from you….like a thief in the night. Remember almost a month ago, when the Neo Void God was dominating the tag team division? Remember when he single-handedly destroyed the United Allah Enthusiasts, starting with the Refugees, later ending with Alexis Parshin for the tag team titles…but before that wasting The Chef? That night was the trigger in what became the Absolute Destiny Apocalypse of the UAE. Yet there was no gratification made to the god… If not for Akiyama, Chef would still be stuck with a bunch of low level flat-scans who, night after night, get their Allah loving asses handed to them by the Neo Void God. Parshin took so many ADAs that the man’s damn neck should be broken. Chef would still be pushing the “current news trend” gimmick, and would get stale and bland like an old baguette. He’s not the only one who’s ungrateful. El Diablo should be thanking the god that he didn’t find himself banished long before he started being a nuisance in the god’s eye now. He’s also lucky that he’s only beaten up and bruised…and not completely broken and crippled. The Mexican Devil…just another name for a broke down Satanist. He’ll have to pay the ultimate price for his defiance, his blasphemy, and his sheer stupidity. But for now… …There’s Chef to deal with… Decorated PCW champ, Grand Slam and all…that’s nothing major. Akiyama’a on his way to becoming one himself…all he needs is that Akinental and the World. Hall of Fame? Just a category to place the old whispers of PCW’s yesteryear. Akiyama has no distinction between you mortals…be you Chef, Hall of Fame, Mexican, or Scientologist. You’re all one and the same to him…and unless you prove to him that you possess worthy qualities of being Celestial or Divine…you’re just another pile of crap waiting to be scraped off the face of the earth. Frisco is Hall of Fame…and he got his ass BANISHED…. And Akiyama never got a word of thanks from him either… Fusion, Akiyama takes on Chef...since Chef is unappreciative of the gift that the nVg has bestowed upon him by setting the actions forward in the destruction of UAE, it's time to show him how things COULD have been if he persisted being Allah's helper... ...Could...more like how things WILL be... So remember, you peons… next time you’re driving along the highway, and you need to cut in front of someone in their lane…Remember… Thank you… Otherwise… F^ck You.
