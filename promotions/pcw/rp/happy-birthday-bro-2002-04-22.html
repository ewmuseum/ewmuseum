Happy Birthday, Bro

22 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/22/2002 ~Rememberance. One of the truly amazing things about humans is the fact of the remarkable memory that humans posess. Some say it is a wonderful thing, others say it is a curse. Jon Kano sees both of the points of view. He has great memories of wrestling in front of thousands of people and statesmen. He has great memories of capturing various titles in various wrestling organizations. But he also has one memory that he wishes everyday that he could lose. That memory is the death of his brother, Ken. Jon and Ken were as close as two siblings can be. Ken was Jon's idol. Jon followed in every step of Ken's. Ken set records in high school for basketball and baseball, only for Jon to break them two years later. Ken enlisted in the army after graduation from high school and Jon followed after his graduation two years later. Ken was a demolition specialist and Jon was also. Ken loved professional wrestling and planned one day to become a professional wrestler. Jon fulfilled Ken's dream. Jon is eternally grateful to Ken and will never forget him.~ *The scene opens up in a cemetary in Las Vegas, Nevada. It is an overcast day with light rain falling. As the scene goes over the names on different gravestones, it stops at the one marked, 'KANO.' Underneath the name of, 'Kano,' is 'Kenneth,' and underneath that is, 'Beloved son and brother.' Suddenly, Jon Kano, still bandaged up from the Ultimate Authority's brutal attack on him on last Tuesday's Turmoil comes into the picture and kneels down to his brother's grave. Jon is dressed in an all black Armani suit and his usually well-groomed long blond hair is soaking wet and messy as it dangles in his face. Kano puts his hand at the top of the gravestone as he begins to speak.* Kano: Happy birthday, Ken. I can't believe you would be 29 today. I guess if you would still be alive, you and me would be revolutionizing the wrestling industry together instead of me doing it alone. Ken, I would give up everything I have now or in the future if you would come back right now. Jesus, I miss you. *Kano's eyes appear to be forming tears, but Kano chokes them back.* Kano: I am sure you know that I married Kristin a couple weeks ago. You sure called that one. I remember a couple months before you died you told me that I would marry her. She is the most amazing woman I have ever met. She put up with a lot of sh*t from me. She supports me in whatever I do, and that one fact is why I love her with all of my heart. So Ken, wondering why I look like I just got ran over with an eighteen wheel semi? The son of a bitch, Nelson, is back in power. I don't know how he did it, but he is back and wants not to just get me out of PCW, but to end my career, period. Ken, I have worked too damn hard to let him beat me now. It is quite funny, actually. He had something in MEDIA. He had the greatest collection of talent in the wrestling industry; past, present, or future. He had Chet King, myself, Boda, and other people who supported the objectives of MEDIA. We had the sole objective of taking PCW to the next level and making it the greatest wrestling organization in the world, by any means necessary. We achieved the goal, but Tyler wasn't satisified, he wanted to be the man in PCW. He changed. He started to use us for his own personal gain. And ever since MEDIA imploded from within because of Nelson, he has tried to resurrect MEDIA into his different stables. Each time he does this, he fails. MEDIA will never be topped. For some reason, Tyler holds me responsible for his problems, namely his erectile dysfunction. But it is his own fault, the result of his own ego running wild. He and I went one on one last Tuesday and he could not pin my massive, powerful shoulders to the mat. I had him, Ken, I had him in the Kanoplex and then all hell broke loose. His goons, his latest reincarnation of MEDIA, the Ultimate Authority, came and it took all of them to kick my ass. It took all of them to take me on. It took all of them to make me bleed. And when I tasted my own blood, I knew one thing to do, the one thing I had to do. I knew that that beating wasn't the end, nor the beginning of the end, but it was simply the beginning. The war is back, Ken. My personal war on Nelson and his goons. Tuesday I have his tag team champions, Politically Incorrect. That name in itself is another contradiction in Nelson's twisted world. Wouldn't it be politically CORRECT to be in cahoots with the boss? Either way, it doesn't matter. I remember another tag team of goons for Nelson. The AFL-CIO tried their damnedest to take me out. They even enlisted the help of Bubba McCoy and the three of them couldn't do the job. They hit me with everything they had. And when all was said and done, all three of them were on their backs, victims of the runaway freight train that is your baby brother, Ken. Ken, these two are very talented, but they are down with Nelson, so I know that these two won't even fight by themselves, they will get help from their running buddies in UA. And all I have is me. I am one of the most talented wrestlers to ever grace a PCW ring, but Ken, I need your help on this one. *Kano reaches into his pocket and lights a cigarette.* Kano: Ken, I am not asking you to tip the scales, but to just even them. I can't do this alone with all of the goons, I need you. Ken, with you and me rolling together down the aisle and rocking the PCW ring like only the Kano brothers can. And that Ken, is what makes me and you what REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *The scene fades to black as Kano walks away and the sights of lightning in the distance and the crack of thunder is heard in the distance.* ~Rememberance. Kano shall never forget.~
