Date: not entered Returning to Japan has been a trip to remember. Falling from the heavens…landing upon the earth…destroying all in our paths…finding family amongst ourselves…and discovering a cult dedicated to the Neo Void God. Interesting material…indeed it is. But if there was a cult that humanity belonged to…it was the cult of lust. Men fed their libidos on flesh of the opposite sex…flesh in tantalizing movements, in erotic fixations, in beauty and proportion of the human anatomy…it tickled at their fancy…and brought the mind to perversion. Fantasize…dream of sexual desires while their hands mimicking the illusionary actions… Idle hands spend time at the genitals…and you know how much THAT’S looked down upon. PCW has been handed a new dream girl…a new woman for the male fans…and yes, even some of the female fans…to star in their mental porno films. A real American woman…a real American idol. Teresa Tomas. She’s all there is to America… carefree, happy go lucky optimistic girl with that CAN DO attitude…and when I mean Can Do…she CAN and WILL DO it. Tomas isn’t ready for this honor yet. She’s not ready to step into the ring with the Divine Prophetess. She hasn’t prepared enough to face the amazing daughter of the Neo Void God. Tomas needs a little more time in the bush leagues before she can hope of facing me…Cyclonica. Reopen WCWF…reopen NTICW… anyplace where she can learn a little more skill before she can take a stab at becoming a PCW fighting diva. Because this is the big leagues, small town country girl…this isn’t Nashville, where pig wrestling is considered worthy of donning a belt. Monster truck rallies aren’t worthy of the prestige they get in the good ole south…not compared to the prestige merely seeing me in the squared circle is valued. Step back into the mud, and get ready to lay with the swine like you always do, in and out of the bedroom. Of course… if anyone’s about to wrestle a hog…I’m the one. Tomas…you’re not ready for this…keep dreaming…keep wishing…keep hoping… Speaking of not being ready… Is Clyde ready yet to come out from behind his mask and be the killer he used to be? Not at all… Clyde loves the weak mask he lives behind now…he loves how the people chant his name….DA MAN…DA MAN…DA MAN… It’s a new thing…something he’s never heard before. But on Red October…the people who hated the god…the people who shunned him from their country…they had something to chant as well as he was beating the life out of Duke, Diablo, and Clyde… NVG! …NVG! …NVG! Why were the Japanese cheering for the god? Because they were finally beginning to understand the struggle he was going through…because they were beginning to appreciate him as a god…because they had forgiven him for destroying half of downtown Tokyo and killing numerous Nihonjin? No…they were chanting his name… for the V I O L E N C E You DO remember what Violence is, do you Clyde? You used to be a pro at it…you used to make people sick with your actions…you used to bring tears to men’s eyes just by watching, women to faint from sight, and children to live forever scarred with the mental images of the nightmares you created just for them. Now what are you…? Jester? Fool? Clown? Comedian? You’re a person to laugh at…not a person to fear. Men break out toothy grins at you…why aren’t you pulling their teeth out one by one? Women smile at you… why aren’t they covering their groins from fright of Bethany and her butcher knife? Children wave at you… why aren’t they fleeing with screams escaping their lungs? Things have changed…. times have changed…. YOU have changed… And come Fusion…the owner of that PCW World Championship Belt…WILL…CHANGE… But on Turmoil…the Cruiserweight title will not CHANGE…hands… So says…so it will be… =©= “Is this the place?” Father and I stood in front of a large rundown warehouse near the docks of Tokyo Bay. Behind us, my…mother…stood dressed in black leather pants and a blue sweater, showing her figure for a person…probably about not so much older than me. Her age bothered me…because she looked really young…about maybe in her mid or late twenties. If that was true…how can I be nineteen? Glacia nodded her head as she looked at the GPS device in her hands. “This is what the coordinates read, daughter. From the material I gathered from my sources…this is the location.” I looked back to the god, my eyes growing narrow. “You think we’re going to find anyone in here?” He stared at me for a second, where I responded by folding my arms across my chest. “I can hear their minds…just as you can hear their hearts…and they’re both in unison… saying the same things over and over in my mind.” The voices of the people inside the building began to sing in my head, like a unholy choir warbling of Armageddon. My hands rose as if I were conducting them in the Celestial Hymn of Doom. “Spill our blood…crush our bones…dance to our screams…it’s all so divine.” I said in a whimsical tone. When the psychic juices were flowing…I could really get caught in the motion. Akiyama clenched his fists as he stared on into the building. Glacia walked up in between us with her arms folded. Was she getting impatient with the god and goddess offspring? I had hoped not. Yes, she may have been my biological mother…but even she was not above blasphemy. “What better time than now to check this place out, hmm?” Father and I nodded at her, both of us looking at each other with a knowing look. Inside of this place could be a denizen for trouble…then again, this place could be the birth of the Celestial Army…the silver horde of the Divine Void bent on bringing the ADA to this world. “What better place than here…” I muttered, the singsong tone still in my ethereal voice. “…What better time than NOW.” =©= Sonata and Matrix sat handcuffed in two black leather desk chairs in a oak paneled office. Behind them was an oak desk, cluttered with papers and folders, along with a computer monitor. They were in the center of the room, where their eyes glared up at they who were before them. Illusion looked down with a smile. “I’m gonna enjoy this…” Next to him, the red minidress wearing Spice nodded, a sick smile on her face. “You thought this was over…but we wont be finished with you until you’re all gone.” Knight towered over the both of them from behind, his large hands rubbing together. “Tis shall be mine greatest triumph! I cannot waiteth until their insides are spilteth out before our eyes!” Sonata and Matrix would have responded by now…to halt their taunting, but the bandana gags in their mouth kept them from adding their two cents. Sonata’s eyes watched as the shadow of a pair of hands loomed over her. Illusion came back with red hands… Spice smacked her lips. “You spilled it all over the place! Now her shirt’s stained with it!” On Sonata’s yellow shirt…a wet red spot was bleeding into the fabric. Bleeding…Clyde didn’t know how to make people do that anymore… “Mmmm…” Illusion sniffed his fingertips, inhaling the aroma of the red substance on his hands. “Cherry filling.” The scene panned down, revealing a box full of doughnuts spread out before them on a table. Sonata glared down at her shirt as the cherry red filling left a stain, her voice spitting curses from behind her gag. Knight smiled as he patted her on her black and yellow head “Do not freteth, fair maiden,” he said, his speech of yore both booming and unusual. He was like a modern Venneman Pendragon, dressed in that silver vest, slacks, and white dress shirt with silver tie. “We shall saveth thee some of the pastry. We cannot haveth our hostages famished, now can we?” Matrix rolled his eyes Angelica and Reality walked into the room. Reality glared at the backs of Illusion and Spice hunched over the table. “Chotto!” he snapped in Japanese. “What is all this??” Slowly Spice and Illusion turned around, their mouths stuffed with doughnuts, their lips coated in powdered sugar, and their hands full of bear claws. Reality sighed, as he looked on at the supermodel like woman and the yellow haired man in the yellow zoot suit. “I swear sometimes I can’t deal with the jackassery of you two.” He held his hand to his temple as Angelica opened the folder in her hands. “I just got word that Japan Special Forces released Akiyama our of their custody on bail.” She said, sounding both annoyed and disappointed. Spice and Illusion looked on with shocked eyes. “MM MHMhmhhmm mmmhhh mhhhmmm!” another reason why you shouldn’t talk with your mouth full. Reality approached Illusion and smacked him in the back of his head, sending the chewed up doughnut flying out from his mouth to the floor. “Don’t f*ckin speak with food in your damn mouth!!” then he turned towards the wad that came flying out of his mouth. “And clean that sh*t up!” Illusion grinned as he grabbed a napkin, picking the waste from his mouth up. “I said why in the world would JSF even allow bail to be posted?” Angelica shrugged her shoulders as she looked to the corner of the room. “Why wonder why they do any of the asinine stuff they do. They’re a bunch of inept bozos…isn’t that right, Shift?” In the corner of the office, the marksman Shift hung upside down from a rope attached to a hook on the ceiling. His arms were tied behind his back, but his mouth was not gagged. Guess he was smart enough not to speak unless spoken to. “I’m not too sure about the ability of the JSF, but--.” “Shut up.” Reality snapped suddenly, his eyes turning away from him. “The fact of the matter is this: we have an Apocalypse bringing demon god and his insane oracle goddess daughter on our hands! Where’s the first place we’re going to find them?” Sonata and Matrix’s eyes widened when Reality referred to Cyclonica as Akiyama’s “insane oracle goddess daughter”. That’ right…when the big news was revealed…they were being shot at by old Shift playing the batman role in the corner there. So much for keeping the big news secret, eh? Angelica smiled slightly as she closed her folder. “My fellow Executives…let us not forget about…CODE.” Reality, Illusion, Spice, and Knight looked at her with enlightened eyes, realizing something they had lost from memory…like a demon losing the lust to kill suddenly remembering that it’s there all along…right Clyde? Angelica smiled at her comrades before walking past them and standing before Sonata and Matrix. The two of them looked up from their bound seats at the tanned Japanese woman with her shiny jet black hair pinned in a neat bun at the base of her neck. She peered down at them with dark eyes through her wire-framed specs, her smiling widening more and more until it became… … devious… =©= Akiyama, Glacia, and I stood in the center of the dark, dingy, and well enough deserted warehouse. There was absolutely no one in the entire place…on the surface, anyway. “Well…where are they?” Glacia asked us. Boy, she was REALLY new to the whole Celestial business. She asked too many questions, in my opinion…sign of either a narc or a newbie. Both, in the game me and the god play called survival…were not tolerated for long. “I can still hear them…” I said, my voice almost at a whisper. Akiyama turned to me as I kneeled down and pressed my ear to the ground. “The earth is speaking to me…it wants you to kill it, daddy.” Akiyama and Glacia walked over to where I had my ear pressed to the ground. He stomped his foot a few times against the ground, and I heard hollow emptiness…reminding me of the sound Duke “Duke Star the Goblin” Williams’s head makes now that my father knocked his brains out after the tremendous ADA he gave him at Red October. I’d hear that hollow sound again should the newly formed Goth crossed the god’s path. Glacia looked down at the ground, searching for traces for an opening. “Are you sure there’s an opening here?” We were all sure…as the floor gave out beneath us. We fell… But it was NOTHING…compared to the fall Clyde would take at Akiyama’s hands on Fusion… So says the Divine Prophetess…
