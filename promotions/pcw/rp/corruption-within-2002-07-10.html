Corruption Within

10 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-10-02 You didn't have to break out in hysteria. 'I did. I was angry. Now the world knows my fury, and I am content.' You aren't content. You'll never be. The voice inside my head tormented me. It agitated my every bone. I had become a new man, a new warrior… and yet a part of me had clung on. A part of me refused to go. I let out a shutter, just at the short reminiscence of my past. 'Forget it. I am who I am now, there is nothing that can change me.' If that is so, then why to you hear my voice in the back of your head? Why do I gnaw at you every waking moment? There was a long silence in my mind. This warrior would not be corrupted within. 'I am who I am. You are only momentary, and soon I shall settle into this new lifestyle. Soon, my past shall be… history.' You know that's not true. Another silence. 'What could change me back? There is nothing for me in my past, only golden opportunities for the future. That is where I must aim. That is what I must achieve!' Nothing for you? What about the WITs? What about the Warriors In Training whom you so selflessly strengthened into mighty soldiers? 'I direct the question to its sender. What about the WITs? Where are they now? My conscience didn't have an answer. Old wounds began to hurt, exposed to the treachery of my own soul. Wounds that I would do anything to heal. 'The WITs are gone. They abandoned me, just like everyone else in my life. Just like everything else in my life. I had nothing. I have nothing. And I cannot be blamed for having some amount of desire to change that.' Nothing? Are you forgetting your wrestling career? The career you've put so much effort into? You started at the bottom of the ladder, and you climbed. Rung after rung, you ascended higher and higher until-- 'I got to PCW? I won the Rising Star Championship? I accomplished the feat that only… dozens before me have?' You have a match this Tuesday. You can prove yourself once again. It will do good for your self-esteem. 'Self-esteem? Self-fucking-esteem? Is that the best you've got?' Jaden, Heaven Rayne, Kris Burden, Venneman Pendragon, Scropion-- 'Any of which would be gladly accepted by the fans! Any one of them could attain the standing that I have, and any one of them could take away the only pride I have. Perhaps it is for the best. Perhaps I am not deserving of the championship. I've let everyone down, afterall.' You have. 'And I should care? I'm a warrior!' Exactly. That is why you must listen to my voice, let me bring out the Trigger that has been thriving for so very long. 'I wouldn't exactly say thriving.' I wouldn't exactly call you a warrior. 'What does being a warrior have to do with the change in my lifestyle?' Everything. What is being a warrior all about? What was it you once said? Decency, peacefulness, fighting for the right cause. Stabbing your supporters in the back and attempting to seize the spotlight is not the right cause. It never was. It never will be. This isn't what being a warrior is about. 'I'm the Infinite Warrior. I decide what being a warrior is all about. I make the rules--I break them.' You certainly do, and that is why it is only you who can save yourself from what you have become. Forget your hurt and your pain. Forget your greed and ambition. Focus on the lessons you have taught, focus on the training you have directed, focus on staying true to the ways of the warrior, because that is the only pure thing in your life. Listen to me, Trigger. I am your guidence. I am your voice of reason. I am your past… the past that so many adored. Now unlock me from my cage. 'You will not be returning, Voice of Reason. For now, upon my completion of this statement, your destruction is imminent. I will do all in my power to crush you, and I will succeed. I will always succeed. There is no Trigger of the past, and Trigger of the future. There is only I, Infinite Warrior, the one destined to climb the ladder to the top, and spit down on all of the creations. No one will stop me. No one can stop me… because they are weak. Because they are frightened. And because… I am a warrior.'
