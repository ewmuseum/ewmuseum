Conspiracy Theory

11 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11 December Opening in the humble abode that belongs to PCW franchise unit Pranks With Attitude and now their base of operation, the camera panned the living quarters looking for the mischievous duo in question. No trace of the behemoth Noscosto Ireul Nerezza but with the buzz of the television the apartment set in the heart of Las Vegas Nevada certainly homed somebody. A jovial whistle went head to head with the television set and the source of this contest soon followed as Gaz 'The Jester' Green emerged. 'Life is sweet-....' sighed the Emerald Menace, reflecting over his short but prosperous Primetime Championship Wrestling career, a career that has seen their stock sky rocket alongside some of the companies leading names, Xavier Kannon, Jason Snow, Akiyama and current World Heavyweight Champion Clyde. Maybe the above was a little over-evaluated, but in just five months P.W.A have secured two Tag Team Title runs, one that may even eclipse the benchmark set by Politically Incorrect and they have accomplished this in the face of stern criticism. 'Two-time Tag Team Champions and soon to be PCW record-....' continued the Conniving Comic, interrupted upon by the crashing of the apartment door and the unveiling of Natalia Miyagawa. Fixed in a catalogue pose, hair the picture of perfect and physically looking level par to any other diva PCW could throw at her, Eleanor Kannon-Hall included. A now rejuvenated women since giving birth to her first child and kicking the no-good father to the curb, the former valet and wife of Alexander Miyagawa continues to extract her revenge with her aligning of Pranks With Attitude. Brushing her hands over her pin-stripe business suit, panting and wheezing heavily she questioned her client. 'Were you just talking to yourself?' 'Err-... Arr-... No, it was the television set!' retorted the devious Jester. 'Oh! I have good news-....' informed the Soviet Scarlet before Gaz hit home an untimely interruption taking the conversation into a different direction. 'Alex has agreed a divorce! So does this mean we'll be adding the wealth of your marriage to the wealth of talent Pranks With Attitude already holds-... To accompany our Championships we shall be limousine driving, styling and most of all, profiling-...' Again another P.W.A copyright infringing moment yet the retorted answer Gaz Green longed for was no-where in sight as Natalia Miyagawa put the conversation back on the straight and narrow. Her solemn frown was the first real indication that she were about to dash the hopes of one Gaz 'The Jester' Green, but Natalia's response soon lifted his spirit. 'The divorce has been left in the capable hands of attorneys and lawyers but what I do know does however concern your Championships-....' The Jester's eyes widened like an anime character, intrigued, almost begging for Natalia to relieve him of the suspense and cure his twitching curiosity. 'Well it was announced today that you will be competing at Friday Night Fusion-....' Gaz Green's eyes widened further, praying that the Tag Team Championships will see the daylight of Saturday still around his slender waist, yet knowing the unscrupulous booking of the Front Office may jeopardise their chances of going into the record books and onto Wrestle Tattlers 'A' list. 'WWWWWWWWEEEEEEEELLLLLLLLLLLLLLLL~!' 'Well it was announced today that you will be competing at Friday Night Fusion in a-.... House On Haunted Hill Match for the PCW Extreme Title! Congratulations you will be inducted into the PCW record books for having the longest Tag Team title reign, eclipsing Politically Incorrect's forty-five day run!' The sound of applause and the popping of champagne corks failed to materialise as Gaz merely smiled, a wayward reaction to the one Natalia expected, no jumping for joy, no mass celebration plans, just a warm hearted smile. 'What's wrong?' injected the Russian beauty a little concerned at the Jesters reaction. Peering up at his colleague, still wearing his half-hearted smile Gaz sighed before informing his valet of his disappointment. Soaking in one last gasp of air, Gaz Green settled the score with regards to his shallow reaction. 'It has nothing to do with the Tag Team Championships - Nerezza and I discussed a swerve we were about to put into effect concerning the Tag Team titles and now miraculously we're booked in a situation I had dreamt up!' Gaz Green waltzed over to a small oak desk, covered in writing utensils and paper, a cluttered place of work from first inspection, tightly gripping a piece of paper that fluttered in the breeze caused from an open window he struts back handing the article over to Natalia. 'Oh, I see what you mean-....' said Natalia inspecting the document thoroughly, her head swaying like a pendulum as she followed the lines of writing, eyes fixated she read up to the last full stop and sympathised with her client who has been lured to the television set, the flashing images that of OSW's Slam! 'There were only two people who knew about my 'House On Haunted Hill' plan, a master plan that ensured the safety of the gold. I was going to exercise this plan and possibly with it, eradicate the competition but now, somehow, The Front Office have came into knowing of this and to rub my nose in it they have me to compete!' roared the Emerald Menace shaking his head in disapprovement. Suddenly the green tailored Jester bounced to his feet, standing erect he ventured over to a lamp that occupied a sideboard a few feet away. Inspecting the furnishing with a touch of Dave Dickinson, usually as he relentlessly searches for that elusive antique, Gaz Green soon found himself screaming at the inanimate object. 'YOU IDEA STEALING BASTA-.... FRONT OFFICERS-....' 'Front Officers?' murmured Natalia under her breath. 'KANNON THIS IS COPYRIGHT!!! WELL NOT REALLY BUT I DID COME-UP WITH THE CONCEPT FIRST AND BUGGING MY APARTMENT IS SO DIRTY AND UNDERHANDED-....' Through the verbal onslaught of one PCW Tag Team Champion, Natalia tried to inject herself into this charade, unfortunately to no avail, and then moving from a verbal injection to a physical, Gaz cried in pain as Natalia planted her foot into exposed shin. 'AAAAAAAARRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHH~!' 'Why are you shouting at a table lamp?' Looking relaxed and more in check with his common sense, Gaz Green took control of his breathing that looked to have won over Natalia when, with out warning the Jester raced over his phone and the charade played over once more. 'YOU IDEA STEALING FRONT OFFICE BASTA-....' As the sentence was about to be completed a distinctive voice filtered over, interrupting the proceedings. The person in question was unveiled as fellow P.W.A member Nerezza who stood, leaning against the door, clad in his Matrix-esque trench coat and black t-shirt with a splashed Gothic insignia running along its front. 'Why are you speaking to the telephone without even lifting the receiver?' hissed the man labeled the Living Nightmare. Like a man who had reached the end of his tether, Gaz Green stormed towards his partner, gripping hold of his coat firmly, almost worryingly he voiced his suspicions with a few choice whispers that were merely shrugged off by the behemoth with a few cackles of laughter. 'Rezz-... They know about-... About the House On Haunted Hill angle-... I reckon they've bugged my apartment-... Stealing my ideas-... Making money off the back of my ideas-...' 'Coincidence - Forget what they hold in the basement of Studio 54 and the night club front of Area 51, one damn conspiracy is Guatemalans With Attitude (G.W.A)' said the vampire as he glared cold heartedly at the television set, which happens to be rolling with footage of Slam! Notably G.W.A's attempts at cracking the grueling obstacle course, master-minded by that of Jane Reagan. Both members of Pranks With Attitude stared at the flickering images with evil glints, sinister grins and a rage fuelled by facts. Fact: G.W.A were OSW's attempts at running the Conniving Collectives names through the mud. 'Bryce Dolan and Orlando Fernandez-....' 'Who are they, they're nothing, nobodies-....' 'They're pointless-....' 'And anyway!' said the duo in unison, utilising the teams unofficial catchphrase. Hell knows, no fury like P.W.A scorned and we have seen feelings from all over the emotion spectrum, from the jovial reception as the team eclipse a long-standing PCW record to that of pure hatred aimed towards the copyright infringing G.W.A. The scene eventually fizzled out as Gaz and Nerezza watched over their OSW counterparts, minds twitching with evil motives, finally closing with a snowy picture taking their places. - The End -
