The Long Talk With the King

16 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 16 *****Last time, Mark was called to visit an English throne who he was related to in a long line of his family. The king told Mark that he had a task for Mark to do but didn’t tell Mark what the plan was. We last left Mark when he was being taken to his small bedroom by James, his personal trainer. ***** James was nice. He was a hard worker and liked teaching me, though he didn’t tell me very much more at my “task” that I had to do. Whenever I had asked him, he had replied to me, “in time, in time”. Also, he had given me archery and fencing lessons and I now knew how to do both, fairly well as he had said once. The king that I had met before had sometimes came to watch my lessons and every so often he would nod, saying, “Very good”. The king came more in the beginning to watch but recently, he hadn’t showed up. Otherwise, I hadn’t talked to the king much. Months passed and before I knew it, years passed and for four years, I did a daily routine of eating, sleeping, and working out, learning old style techniques of fighting and how to sword fight. Now, tonight was a rather special tonight, I could tell. It had been exactly four years since my first talk with the king and he wanted to talk to me again. It must’ve been something important because guards, men in the castle and especially James had joked around with me more. I was told to put on some clothing and meet the king in the private, King’s room. The King’s room was special because you didn’t get to go into it unless it was important. The room was the same room as the first night I had talked to the king but since than, I had forgotten what it had looked like, except the red carpet leading up to the king’s throne. Maybe I would be told more about the task that I had to do. I slipped on some black dress pants and a blue dress shirt from my closet in my room. The room was small, but manageable. It had white wallpaper and blue carpet. Besides that, it had a small bed to sleep in, a closet in the corner, and a window that overlooked some grass-land. Also, beside my bed was a phone which I used to phone my parents every so often. I stepped out from my room into the familiar, long hallway that had doors to almost every room in the castle as I passed, walking towards the end of the castle where two wooden doors were, the king’s room. I reached the wooden doors and opened them, walking in. As I stepped into the room, I remembered what the room had looked like, almost as if I had just been in the room. The roof was thirty feet above me and the lights that hung from the roof, instead of shining brightly, were now dimmed. The red carpet was normal and expensive and beside the carpet on both sides, the white pillars that led up to the king’s throne were sparkling like they had recently been shined and spiffed up. I walked up he short, five stairs that led up onto a platform, about 15 feet by 15 feet wide. The king sat there, sitting in his king’s chair, smiling at me. Facing him was another chair, not as elegant but that wasn’t the point. He motioned me to sit in the chair which I politely did. When I was in the chair, I looked at the king, taking a good look at him. The king had creases in his forehead. Four years ago, the king was forty-two James said but now, he must’ve been forty-six or forty-seven and had aged considerably. He still wore his gold robe like a priest would wear but sat like he had a hunched-back. “Well hello Mark, long time since we’ve talked, hasn’t it?” His voice had even gotten older and more shaky. “Yeah, it has. Good to see you again, sir.” The King’s attitude seemed to change then. When he first spoke, it was a happy voice like he was happy to see me again but he was now focussed and there was something else about him that I couldn’t put into words. “Mark, I’m sure you have been wondering what task I want you to do.” “Yeah, I am.” I knew this was it. I was finally gonna get told the plan that I had waited four years to here. I had done vigorous training in Fencing, Sword Fighting, Archery, Martial Arts, and had learned sixteen different languages but wasn’t told anything! I deserved to be told this! “Well, you see, a long time ago,” started the king, continuing on, “a mysterious source had made seven amulets. It was said that the amulets had various powers and only special people could have the amulets. The people who had the amulets had to be trustworthy or else the world could become in peril. One amulet that was made was a special amulet and it had the power of all six amulets combined and more! The only way to identify this one, special amulet was to look on the amulet for a small jewel in the centre on it that was red, no other colour. When in the power of the beholder, the red jewel in the middle would shine so powerful, it could blind! You got me so far?” What the king said was definitely a mouthful to say, that was for sure. It was also hard to listen to, not knowing any other info but I think that I had gotten the most of what he said and could hopefully remember it. “Yeah, I got you.” The king looked at me and knew that I was anxious to here what he had to say so he continued on, trying to get every detail and not leave anything out. “The six, less powerful amulets were given to six men who were worthy of the job and could keep the secret of the amulets with their life. The seventh amulet was given to my father, who was king at this time. The father had trusted the amulet with his life and tried to learn as much as he could about the amulet. Then, one day, he was getting old and decided to pass the king-hood onto me, planning also to give me the one powerful amulet. On the day that I was going to become king, a great crusade from the Muslim world had happened and the six beholders of the amulets had went to war, five of them being killed and there amulets taken but one man, my fathers best friend had survived, bringing back the six amulets with him to me, the new king.” “Wow.” I didn’t know what else to say. Being trained for fours years, I of course has heard numerous times where James had been talking to various people and I gotten information from those small conversations but the only words that I remember getting were, “powerful”, “stolen”, “glow”, and “Muslim”, meaning I didn’t get too much! “It’s a mouthful, that’s for sure,” started up the king again, “I now had the seven amulets in my power. When all seven were together, they suddenly started glowing without warning, causing great controversy from unknown places. Then, one ordinary day with the seven amulets still glowing and I not knowing what to do with them, an attack happened on my kingdom. I tried to save the amulets but one amulet was stolen. That amulet was the most powerful one. The next day, the amulets stopped glowing.” This was crazy! What are the odds of the most powerful one being stolen? I kind of saw how I fit into this whole plan but I wasn’t for sure. “What happened next?” I was hoping that the story wouldn’t be over yet, when I kind of new that it kind of was. “Well, I wondered why the amulets only glowed when they were all together. After years of research, I found out that the amulets could either be used for good or for bad and whoever had taken the amulet knew this two and was planning to use them for evil! That’s where you come into play. I need you to find the amulet and destroy it!” It was what my hunch was, what I had to do. I had to destroy the amulet, but why would I learn archery and sword fighting and not how to use a gun? “Why was I never learned how to use a gun then?” “Because, my child, the amulet can only be destroyed using the old style fighting methods and not using a gun.” That was interesting to know in case I found a gun and stupidly tried to kill it with that. “So, where is the amulet?” “I don’t know. Some say Spain, some say France, some say America so here is what I have planned for you.” “Yes?” I had no idea what the king had for me to make my way around Spain, France, and America. There was no job that I was aware of that did those things. “I want you to goto America and join a professional wrestling promotion. Learn the fine art of wrestling and learn there, but never remember what your true goal it. To KILL THE AMULET! ”
