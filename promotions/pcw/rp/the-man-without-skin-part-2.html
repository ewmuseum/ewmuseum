Date: The...uh, I dunno You will never be the same again These words were an obstruction in Eddy's mind today: A usual aspect of today's very confusing world. Eddy felt so hopeless, staring at the TV at the current war over in Iraq. Eddy didn't know exactly how to feel, anyway. He usually just tried to push it into the back of his mind and look forward to other things. However, his overwhelming pessimism was not only annoying, but a true factor that always came into his mind on day by day activities. He could barely concentrate on things. With an upcoming match with Skitzo, he didn't know he could actually make it to the climax to fight him. He also didn't want a 20 inch dildo up his ass, but he looked forward to pair with Skitzo. It was for the Extreme Title: A title Eddy usually didn't have any true pleasure of fighting for, but it was a title and he would try his best to get it. Why? Because that's what Eddy tried to do every match. Eddy was currently in no important spot in the federation. He was a low to mid carder without much else. He did get the mid card match against Mark Makonie, in which Eddy felt a bit embarassed over. Not because mid card, not at all, but because in the end, he ended up attacking the ref. Eddy did feel he deserved it though. Eddy was fighting Mark Makonie, and the ref happened to be a good friend of Makonie's. So, in the end, the ref began to cheat Eddy from the win, and Eddy let his anger out by attacking the ref. Eddy looked back, embarassed he did so yet he felt that the referee, Mr. Liebig, deserved it for his biased view on the match. Eddy didn't like Makonie either for attacking his father backstage either. Eddy was casually showing his parents the backstage area when Makonie came along and scared his parents, and eventually attacked Eddy's dad. Eddy began to stop Makonie, but Makonie grabbed a chair and tried to attack Eddy. If it wasn't for Brianna Washing to come in and stop the scuffle, Eddy could've been hurt. Eddy's father was in the hospital, where Eddy was now. He looked down at his dad solemnly on the patient's bed as he lay eyes closed and with tape around him. He felt sorry for his father. He was innocent and did nothing to harm Makonie, but Makonie began to hurt him regardless. Eddy got back the results for Makonie's attack: 3 cracked ribs, a broken nose, a spinal bruise and a fractured left wrist. Although not to critical, it was enough for Eddy to despise Makonie for his nerve to do such a thing. 'Dad, are you okay?', Eddy said, looking down on his father. He was in blue hospital patient clothing. Eddy's dad peered his tired eyes open and stared up at Eddy. 'Yeah', his father said in a croaky, froggy voice. It was midly eerie to hear his father like that. It sounded like a mixture of a bad sinuses and the kind of voice when you first wake up. His dad's eyes looked a bit unassuming too. Eddy didn't know how to feel, but took his father's response like it. 'I still can't believe Makonie did this to you...' Eddy's father nodded, looking as if he didn't care to talk. 'I fucking hate that bastard.' Eddy was a bit shocked at his father's use of profanity in that statement. He only rarely heard his dad say 'bastard', and even less 'fucking'. 'I hope you get that bastard to pay for the shit he did. That fucking racist asshole...He judges everybody on the skin that protects them from pain. Without skin, everything we'd touch would hurt like hell. It's just fucking bullshit we have to be judged by looking at what protects us from being hurt', his father said in a more stronger voice, almost raising to a bigger climax with each word. 'I will dad. I agree with you', said Eddy, almost baffled and confused. His dad then reached up and tugged on Eddy's shirt hard, almost choking Eddy. His father had a fracture left wrist, and he was using his right. But his dad held with so much agression is was shocking. Eddy could feel his green sweater stretching and his father tugged. 'I mean it son. Make him pay for what he did. Make him feel miserable for what he did to me...actually, fuck what he did to me...Make him pay for what he does to everyone else who isn't white. I'm an iota of importance, so forget I said anything about me. But make him pay. Do it son, please...For the sake of everyone.' 'Okay Dad...', said Eddy really confused at this point. His father didn't let go, but loosened his grip. 'But dad, I'm fighting Skitzo this week for the Extreme title...Makonie isn't of importance.' His dad's eyes bulged. Eddy was startled. 'Eddy...Prevail over Skitzo, and I have to tell you something...Makonie will come. He's gonna run in and attack you like the asshole he is. He's going to fight you and try to make you lose. Know that. But Eddy, if he does this, take all that Extreme weaponry use and use it. Lift up your fucking chair and clock him over the head with it as hard as you can hit.' 'What if Skitzo blitzed me with his 20 dildo?'. Eddy felt stupid asking that. 'Look out for him too. Beat Skitzo, but beat Makonie too.' His father let go of Eddy's shirt and laid to rest. 'But dad...I don't want to attack Makonie if I don't need to...' 'Don't NEED TO?!!! You think that Makonie curbs all these African Americans, insults Latinos, tease Asians and doesn't DESERVE to get his ass kicked?' 'Well...That's true.' 'Once you hold that Extreme title, Makonie is going to attack you. But don't let this stop you. Fight. Kick his ass...Kick that bastard Skitzo's ass and win that God damned title. Holding that title is like telling Makonie 'Haha, I'm Hispanic and I hold a title unlike your sorry ass'. That's all the proof you need to tell him he's a bastard for being to bigot he is...His bigotry is not the nicest thing, and prove to him any race can do just as well as any other race', his father said. 'Okay Dad...' 'Hold that title forever son. Don't let it go. It's yours. You earned it. Beat that sorry ass guy named Skitzo, and kick his ass, and kick whatever 10 alter ego's he has.' Eddy nodded. 'I'll try.' 'That's what I like to hear, son...You can leave now. Train hard and long', said his father before shutting his eyes. Eddy nodded and left the hospital room, his feet squeaking on the freshly aqua and white checkboard tiled floor. Eddy opened the door, and walked down the hall, his father's words ringing. Eddy never heard his father curse that much. Eddy felt so heavy on pressure. He knew if he lost, he would put his father down...He didn't want that to happen. He wanted to win. Eddy felt his confidence level go up as he knew he had to win to prove to Makonie white wasn't the only race who could fight and win. Beating Skitzo on Fusion could prove that. Like his father said, without skin, we'd touch things and it would hurt, wouldn't it? The skin is protection from pain: A thing Eddy was grateful for. Skin was of great importance. Skin was just protection, nothing more. The color of the skin wasn't of any importance, because that skin is there for the reason to protect, not to give off impressions. Eddy walked out of hospital and into fresh air. The hospital had a garden-ish area as you left, giving off the scent of flowers as Eddy walked across the brick ground. It was a plaza like area, with a fountain to his left and an area of grass and trees to the right. Straight ahead was his truck as Eddy walked toward it. Eddy reached it, put in the keys into the key slot and opened the door. He opened the door, stepped in, and drove off out of the parking lot, his father's word still in his mind. Eddy had a sudden burst of energy as he drove to his local gym to train. He wasn't going to lose. He couldn't lose. That's what Eddy told himself as 'Eye Of The Tiger' played. It was an esteeming rising song Eddy he liked. He strummed his fingers on the steering wheel to the catchy beat as he drove off to train and beat Skitzo. It had to happen.
