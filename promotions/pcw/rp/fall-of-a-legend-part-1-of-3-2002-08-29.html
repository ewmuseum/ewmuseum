Fall of a Legend....Part 1 of 3....

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-29-2002 Darkness envelops the screen, and the sound of rain can be heard falling hard in the background. Lightning flashes and illumines, briefly, a lone figure trudging along, head down. The lightning flashes again, and we see that his clothing appears to be made of a gold that flashes in the lightning. A huge bolt crashes in the darkness and we see Venneman Pendragon, heading somewhere in a hurry. Well, in a hurry for someone who walks around in the rain in golden armor. Suddenly, a spotlight flashes on the warrior, and a voice booms over the rain.... HALT! THIS IS A RESTRICTED AREA! Pendragon stops walking, and looks at the camera, which seems to be situated where the voice originated. He looks to be quite a mess. His blonde hair is plastered wetly to his head, and his eyes are blue, tinged with insanity. He stares in the direction of the voice and smiles.... Venneman: I am no dog for you to bark orders to. I am the Lord King Venneman Pendragon. I authorize myself. Go back to your television program, rent a cop, before I smite thee with Excalibur... The shot switches to show an overweight fellow leaning out of a security booth. He smiles wanly and waves Pendragon on, forgetting his duties as a guard. A guard for PCW if the sign we see on the booth is correct. PCW Headquarters. Where else would Pendragon be heading in the pouring rain? 'Pay a fellow ten bucks an hour and expect him to do a good job keeping people out of PCW Headquarters. Just like management. Always selling themselves short. That fat bastard's one duty is to protect the gate here at PCW and all he can do is smile and wave me on. No sense him losing his job because of mismanagement. I wasn't planning on going in through the gate anyhow.' The shot switches back and follows Pendragon as he walks around the fenced perimeter of the huge building. When he is out of site from the guard shack, he draws his sword and walks up to the fence. He rears back and slices through the fence a slot large enough for him to slide through. Once on the other side, he cautiously approaches the building. He stops and looks to his left and his right. The camera follows his gaze and he heads off for whatever he was looking for. He stops in front of a door and tries the handle. It is locked, of course, so he gives the door a jerk and rips it from its hinges.... Venneman: I guess that will come from my pay. Oh well. I think I can afford it.... He walks through the now doorless doorway and starts up a stairway illumined with fluorescent lights. He comes to a doorway after a couple of flights and pushes through. He steps into a carpeted hallway on the second floor of PCW headquarters. He looks around and sheathes his sword as he encounters nothing out of the ordinary... 'Why was I here? Because I should have had a real match against Syko the Goblin. The powers that be let him steal my belt, and then give him a shot at it when he had done nothing but lose to me since my arrival...The filthy dogs! And then the match is a Ladder Match. A match that plays into his hands perfectly. In one on one combat he loses. In a trumped up gimmick match he has a chance. But, instead of giving me what I deserve, Syko in the ring, one on one, I get Jon Kano. Somebody needed to be brought up to par on the booker's oversight, and it was high time I brought out the truth...' Pendragon storms through the empty hallways and sees nobody. He passes locked offices and dark break rooms, but no people. Finally, he hears a voice from behind... God Damnit. Look at you, drippin' all over my clean floor. Who the hell do you think you are? Pendragon slowly turns and we see the voice belongs to a janitor. He holds a broom in one hand and trembles a bit as he looks into Pendragon's insane eyes... Venneman: I think I am the Lord King Venneman Pendragon. I think that I shall do as I please. If I want to urinate on your nice clean floor I believe I would probably do just that. Janitor: Hey! No problem! Do what ya like, man! The janitor starts to back away as Pendragon starts in on one of his crazy laughing fits. Abruptly he stops and stares at the man.... Venneman: Before you go, you'll answer a few questions for me... The janitor swallows hard and shrugs. Pendragon smiles his best smile to put the man at ease... Venneman: Ok...Where might I find the boss's office? Janitor: Kannon's office? Umm...You don't want to go there. I would say he doesn't want to see you... Venneman: Did I ask your opinion, you sniveling worm? No. I asked where the office is located. Surely even you can understand that. Now...Where is it? The Janitor starts shaking again as Pendragon's tone had turned a bit nasty. Janitor: Top floor...Elevator at the end of the hall, then all the way to your left when you get to the top floor...You can't miss it! Venneman: Thank you....Next. Is the boss here? Janitor: I don't know. I know they've been moving Nelson's stuff out and his in, but I don't know if he is there now or not...Why? Venneman: I have a bone to pick. I guess you could say I am tired of the lack of respect the power-mongers of this company are displaying towards me. Xavier Kannon took on Tyler Nelson and the Corporate Dark Age and now it would appear he is protecting the vermin known as Syko.... Janitor: Oh yeah, the TV Champ.... Venneman: WHAT IS IT YE UTTER, YE MONGREL DOG??!! Janitor: SHIT! The Janitor runs off in terror of the insane Pendragon, leaving his broom behind. Pendragon snarls as the janitor goes, his words grating on his conscience. Finally, he regains some composure and takes off down the hall in the direction of the elevator. He reaches the door and presses the 'up' button. The doors slide open with a 'BING' and Pendragon steps inside as a voiceover starts... 'I did not understand why Syko seems to have the protection of the powers that be. I could understand because of the alliance he had with previous management, but why would Kannon condone him? After all, one would figure that he would be out to eradicate those who would lie down with the vanquished enemy. I would have my answers, but I also wondered why PCW would see fit to toss me Jon Kano. The man had just won a retirement match. How could he expect to stand in the ring with the Lord King? Unfortunately, I have listened to his troublesome words. On one hand, he commends my ability. On the other, he insults my attitude and my motivation. Says I remind him of the way he used to be...I guess that is true inasmuch as he used to be a winner. Now though....Now, he is washed up at the tender age of twenty six years old. Same age as I, and yet his body is battered and broken; his will, weak. I am in my prime. Perfection both physically and mentally. And this man would make the boast that he would give me a heads up about what PCW is about. What did he have planned? Would he throw himself upon the sacrificial altar for Pendragon to gain glory? Because it seems to me that THAT is what PCW is about. In one on one combat, I am unmatched in PCW. Was that a boast? Neigh, 'tis truth. Kano considers himself a Legend. A true Legend who cares about bringing PCW back to the top. Harsh realization will set in on Friday, I fear. What will bring PCW to the top once more is the young and hungry talent...Namely, Lord Pendragon. With the glory that I gain and elevate myself with, so to shall PCW be elevated. As I trod the path to greatness, so to shall PCW become even greater. The people do not want to see has beens and never was wrestlers like my opponent. They want to see Pendragon with all the pomp and circumstance that comes with the King. Kano thinks he is a Legend, huh? I have some news then....' The camera zooms in on Pendragon's face and he smiles.... Venneman: Legends Fall... The doors close as the elevator makes its ascent to the 'boss's office'... >>>TO BE CONTINUED
