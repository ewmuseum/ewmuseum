You've Finally Made It

19 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-19-02 “You’ve done it Gene,” the great doctor yells out. “You’ve done it!” Gene stands smiling as he holds the notice. A small sheet of paper. It was a fax sent to him by the RUSH front offices. It’s the card for Alliances 2002. Sure G.O.D. had known about his placement on the card, everyone did, but there was something about this. There was just something about truly seeing it on paper that brought tears to his eyes. There’s just something about this sheet of a tree that made his lips quiver and curl. It’s been awhile. It’s been a few months since G.O.D. set out in the wrestling tattler battlefield. He’s been in New True Internet Championship Wrestling and then moved onto RUSH Wrestling when that folded, but it wasn’t these feds that he was striving for. It was always OSW and PCW. It was always either Old School or Primetime that he wanted. Now, right now, he can step into one of those rings. Now he can put down on all the accomplishments he’s had, ‘Wrestled In PCW Alliance Main Event.’ Truly something to be proud of. And now, as he sat there, tears of joy pouring into the crevices of his face, he loved it. He loved being who he was. He loved doing what he does. He just loved it all. Even thoughts of his career landing on past vermin, Wes Outlett, Markus Sinnianti, Rich Cannon, Scott Cage, and the ever present Matt Hunt, didn’t stop his love for everything. Dr. Jenkins smiles and laughs. All his hard work, all their hard work. It’s all coming together. It’s all now what they’ve been doing. Jenkins flops in a chair, laughing and smiling. Gene still stands, crying, holding the paper, smiling. He’s just so happy. They’re both just so happy. “I know you can do it Gene. You know you can do it.” He laughs heartilly at this thought. “They all know you will do it! That’s why you are where you are! You’re going there to do it! You’re going to do it Gene, you will.” He leans back in the chair more. The cushions fade into the great doctor’s lab coat. He pushes up his glasses and lets out a long chuckle that seems like he’s losing his breath. It’s just a little more abnormal than it should be. This kills G.O.D.’s crying joy and he wipes at his tears. He sniffles. “Doctor?” “Yes Gene?” he asks curiously. “What will I do? What will I do?” The doctor swings his legs and flips himself forward. He puts his hands together and lets out a sigh, still smiling. “Gene, you’ll win. It’s that simple. You’re in a good team. You’re an amazing asset to them as well. By damn, you four boys’ll win.” “But doctor,” G.O.D. stammers, “It’s an odd match. I’ve never heard of such a thing.” “You’ll be fine. We’ll begin training immediately.” “Thank you.” The doctor sighs again, his look satisfied. “It’s nothing Gene. You’ve done so much for me, all I can do for you is nothing compared to that.” And so they begin their preparations for an event that will make or break them. For an event that will have them laughing and reeling. For an event that will change the course of the Genetic Oriented Drone’s history forever. Prepare to meet your alliances G.O.D. What will be of all of you?
