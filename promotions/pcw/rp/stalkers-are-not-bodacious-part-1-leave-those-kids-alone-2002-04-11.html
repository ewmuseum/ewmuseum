Stalkers are not Bodacious Part 1: Leave those kids alone

11 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 11 [The scene opens on a mysterious man wearing a trenchcoat and matching top hat. The brim of the hat casts a shadow which hides the man's face from view. You can tell that it's a man by the light stubble you can make out in the shadows, and his build through the trenchcoat makes it obvious as well. The scene zooms out a bit showing the man walking outside of the lovely beach house of the Cowabunga Connection. The house is an odd combination of blue and yellow and stretches out almost one hundred feet long, which is the largest house on the beaches of Los Angeles] Man in trenchcoat: These cretins have been a thorn in my side since Day One. They pretended to be something their not, only to use me to get attention from the rest of their peers. I'm sick and tired of being some comedy tool for their pleasure. It is now time for a little payback, redemption is a much better word to use in this situation. [The man takes a few more steps, his breathing becoming a little more heavier] Man in trenchcoat: 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, how dare you! Your stupid surfing awards mean nothing! Any idiot can just stand on a board and let the waves carry him. There is nothing special about you, never has and never will. Your father was a putz and he was used by your slutty mother, whom I've had the pleasure... [You can see a shadowed smile grow] Man in trenchcoat: I think I'll leave it at that. Your weed smoking partner is just a drain on society. Seriously, what special quality can he offer anyone? Besides coming up with great ideas to make bongs and pipes, he's utterly useless. [The man in the tan trenchcoat looks all around him as if being absolutely careful on his mission. He walks up the small, wooden boardwalk toward the 'Totally Tubular' House. His walk is extremely slow and almost stalker-like. Through the shadow you can see a smirk crossing the man's face, as if something like this turns him on. He stops as a man comes out on the patio that attaches to the side of the blue and yellow beach house. That man is none other than 'Totally Tubular' Tommy, one half of the Cowabunga Connection. Tommy wears only his blue boxer shorts with little yellow surfboards scattered all over them. The bright, blonde haired man stretches and yawns loudly. The man in the trenchcoat whispers to himself, his voice is quite raspy] Man in trenchcoat: I'm tired of the abuse, of your useless jokes. I'm tired of that smile that attracts women in flocks. IT MAKES ME SICK!! Everything about you is sickening!! [The man seems to settle down, and keeps his concentration on the house] Man in trenchcoat: Your going down, punks. The Cowabunga Connection will die, and it begins today. Because I am THE CANCER!! [As the man in the trenchcoat goes to turn around and leave, he's confronted by two small children. A young boy, about eight years old, and a little girl, just a bit younger and obviously the boys sister. The children are soaking wet and covered in sand. They both hold plastic pails and shovels in their hands, obviously failing their sandcastle skills] Little Boy: Why are you wearing that? [The man in the trenchcoat towers over the children, standing motionless as they look up at him curiously] Little Girl: Your WEIRD! [The man in the trenchcoat seems offended by this] Man in trenchcoat: I'm not weird, I'm just misunderstood. Little Boy: What's that? Man in trenchcoat: Misunderstood? Little Boy and Girl: Yeah... [The man in the trenchcoat just shakes his head, as if these little children should know the meaning of the word. Talk about a very arrogant, mean man!] Man in trenchcoat: You'll find out soon enough, cretins. Now if you'll just run along and play I'll just be leaving now. Little Girl: Your mean! Man in trenchcoat: Am not! Little Girl: Are too! Man in trenchcoat: Am not! Little Girl: Am not! Man in trenchcoat: Are too! [The two children laugh loudly] Little boy: She got you, dude. [The trenchcoated man stomps his feet on the boardwalk and points his finger in the little girls face] Man in trenchcoat: That wasn't fair. Little Boy: It's hot out. How come your all covered up? Man in trenchcoat: Don't try and change the subject, cretin. I was cheated into saying I was mean!! Little Girl: You've got problems, mister. Man in trenchcoat: Well, your mother has problems...and IT'S YOU!! [The little girl begins to break into tears as her big brother goes to console her from this evil and mean man] Little Boy: That wasn't cool at all, mister! Now look what you did! [The man in the trenchcoat smiles to himself, feeling a large sense of victory. I guess one can feel that way making little children cry] Man in trenchcoat: Life is tough, kid. You've got to learn to deal with things and stop being a FREAKIN' CRYBABY!! [The man begins to walk away, absolutely pleased with himself. His shadowed smile quickly turns into a shadowed grimace of pain. The little boy took acception to the insults and cracks the man in the back of the thigh with his plastic shovel. The trenchcoated man turns around, holding the back of his thigh] Man in trenchcoat: That HURT!! Give me that shovel! [The little boy runs in a circle around his little sister, and stupidly, the man in the trenchcoat follows him every step of the way] Little Boy: You can't catch me, na na na na!!! Little Girl: YAY! Man in trenchcoat: Give me the shovel you dirty little bastard!! [In the distance the familiar dumbfounded, easy going voice of 'Totally Tubular' Tommy rings throughout the beach] 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: What's going on over there, dude?! Little Girl: This man is being mean to us!!! TTT: Is it your dad, little dudette? Little Girl: EWWWW!! That's gross. He's trying to grab my brother and play with his shovel! [Tommy just smirks to himself on the patio, thinking about the first time a bodacious babe played with his shovel. WAIT A MINUTE! That's a guy, in fact, that's a grown man! He wants to play with a little boy's shovel?! That's totally HEINOUS!] TTT: Hold on, little dude! The Tubular One will save you!! [Tommy disappears back into his beach house and then quickly returns wearing a blue robe, with a yellow belt. He kind of looks like a superhero with that robe on. As Tommy reaches the boardwalk the man in the trenchcoat grows in fright and takes off down the boardwalk to get away from his hated enemy] TTT: Get back here you totally bogus, dude!! [Another figure comes out onto the patio and that figure is 'Heavy Metal' Harry, the second half of the Cowabunga Connection. Harry wears a Twisted Sister T-shirt, leather boxer shorts, and black socks. His brown hair is all over the place and his eyes are extremely bloodshot, showing signs that he has just awakened. Another odd thing about Harry's appearance, as he begins staggering his way toward the boardwalk, is that he has two joints stuck up his nostrils. Tommy's voice can be heard in the distance as Harry staggers closer] TTT: I'm gonna get you, dude. Nobody tries to play with little boy shovels and get away with it!! [The man in the trenchcoat runs around a small shack just adjacent to a set of men's and women's bathrooms. Tommy runs around after him and only discovers the trenchcoat, and his ridiculous looking top hat, lying in the sand. Tommy picks them up and looks around with very angry, focused eyes] TTT: I'm going to find that bogus dude. [Harry then strolls into the scene, looking at the trenchcoat and hat with curiousity] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: What's going on, man? [Tommy keeps looking out to the beach, hoping to find the man as he answers his best friend and tag team partner] TTT: I think we have a most heinous stalker on our hands, compadre. [Harry looks a bit frightened, and then his looks suddenly changes to a little child wanting to ask a question] Harry: Is it a chick, man? TTT: Not even close, dude. It's some dude that is into playing with little boy's shovels. Harry: That's not far out, not far out at all, man. [Tommy then hands the coat and hat over to Harry. Tommy looks out to the ocean, his eyes completely focused and filled with a mix of anger and fear] TTT: Harry, I think we are going to have a most bogus battle on our hands. We lost our match at Spring Fling because of that heinous compadre, Xavier Kannon. Then after wrestling one of the most grueling matches of our bodacious careers, Suburban Uprising attacks us from behind. Talk about being HEINOUS. Every since that attack weird things have been happening, dude. Very strange things. [As Tommy was rambling on about serious things, Harry is sniffing the coat and hat for some sort of scent] Harry: I think I've got something, man. [Tommy turns to Harry with hope in his eyes] Harry: The coat smells like weed, man! [Tommy lowers his head and points at Harry's nose] TTT: That's because you have joints stuffed up your nose, dude. [Harry smiles brightly] Harry: Oh yeah, man. [He then pulls one out and sticks it in his mouth] TTT: BOGUS, DUDE!! Ugh! Harry: What, man? TTT: You had that thing up your nose and then you put it in your mouth! That's most gross. [Harry shrugs] Harry: They're my boogers, man. [Harry goes to light up when the two children approach him] Little Boy: Whatcha doin'? [Harry pulls down his lighter and takes the joint from his mouth] Harry: Nothing, man. Little Girl: You were gonna get high!! Harry: Was not, man! Little Girl: Were to! Harry: Was not, man! Little girl: Were to! Harry: Was not, man! Little Girl: Was not, man! Harry: Were to, man! [The little girl smiles] Little Girl: Told ya. Harry: Wait...man. TTT: You totally fell for the Bugs Bunny Trick, dude. [Harry is mad at himself for falling for the Bugs Bunny Trick. Then he gets an idea] Harry: ICE CREAM, MAN! [The children celebrate loudly and the three run off the scene. Tommy then walks toward the ocean, as the sun rises into the afternoon hours] TTT: I'm prepared for all bogusness. [The scene then fades to black]
