The Origin Of....The Hero & Howard...How It All Began

31 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-31-2002 OOC:'[detail withheld] so I'm desperately trying to catch up in RP's today. I needed something to RP about today besides the party finale, so since I have none of my other RP's saved from the pre-unemployed RAH era, I thought I'd do a nice lil re-write(with a few present day additions) of the first meeting between Jeremy Krayzie Howard And Rent-A-Hero...ENJOY!' ..::/::..Mere Weeks Before Red October...Ahhh yes, before our hero knew anything of the physical temptation known as Amy 'Aimz' Campbell, when the PCW Rising Star title was highly sought after and held by none other than Rent-A-Hero himself, and just before he came to be the hero the PCW Nation knew and loved, our hero was thrown into a drawing and given a tag partner for the Lethal Lottery, a huge random pairing tourney where the final 4 teams would square of, each match determining what shots were given. Now it is well known in the history books the results and what not, but what many are unaware of is the strange bond formed shortly thereafter between a young spandex enwrapped superhero and a a hot PCW mega-star, who at that time was in the huge heel stable of Greed Inc. The team was supposed to be temporary...but the bond and memories are ones for the ages...now let us turn back the hands of time and show the HISTORIC, first meeting of Rent-A-Hero and 'The Krayzie One' Jeremy Howard...the beginning of what turned out to be a dysfunctional friendship..::/::..
