XK01 : “Let The Games Begin.”

31 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31/07/02 XK01 : “LET THE GAMES BEGIN.” Location : Hubbardville, Wisconsin.Date : Monday 29th July, 5pm. *BANG* *BANG* *BANG* *BANG* *BANG* Looking up at a clear Blue sky our shot suddenly becomes alive... *BANG* *BANG* *BANG* *BANG* *BANG* Against the bright, monotone sky Purple and Green fireworks explode in clusters that pepper the heavens above the sleepy city in Wisconsin. *BANG* *BANG* *BANG* *BANG* *BANG* They intensify.... 10’s of explosions every second dotting the scene with bursts of colour and falling, glowing sparks that leave a trail all the way back down to earth. A loud shout then bellows out from what sounds like a Public Announce System.. An authoritative, clear yet joy-filled bellowing that sends the four words echoing out around Hubbardville. “LET THE GAMES BEGIN!” The announcement is met by a huge resounding cheer. 1000’s of celebratory voices all resonating as one in both approval and happiness. The day they hoped would come has finally arrived, and there will be much good cheer and amusement to accompany it. “Welcome back Gold Patron Meritorious... You truly are crafted in Hubbard’s image...” Moving our view from the sky which has become hazy with smoke we see ground level, and more importantly a group of 3 very different looking people who however have the same emotions coursing through their bodies. Pleasure. Contentment. Satisfaction. Jubilation. Pride. However, accompanying these are their less desirable attributes that led them to this day. A day of celebration that only occurred by the unjustifiable actions of the three human beings we see. Their hearts cold to anything that does not include or benefit them. Xavier Kannon. Eleanor Hall. The Phantom Booker. “KAN-NON! KAN-NON! KAN-NON!!!“ The reception they receive in this city of Scientologists does nothing to make them doubt their actions, or question the deeds they have done... But at the back of their minds, the prospect of what awaits them haunts even their jaded being... The prospect of paying for what they have done at the hands of Tyler Nelson and the Dark Age. Xavier : “As great as Hubbard was, he never had one of these...” The arrogant, smug and smirking Scientologist looks as self assured as one man ca be as he gestures his PCW World Title to the organiser of these events. Beside Xavier, to his right, the Phantom Booker just watches Kannon, his project, the Champion, while on Xavier’s left Eleanor does not shy away like the facially obscured one. Kannon’s young wife relishes this. Her husbands fame rubs off on her. His achievements are passed on to her. She is the wife of a Champion... And she’s loving it. Eleanor : “One... Of... Those!” The young and preppy Ellie~! points with both hands to the plate of Gold that Xavier is carrying on a new Purple strap. Personalising it as hi. It is not the Title that Kremmen first held. Not the Title his rival Tyler Nelson has held twice... It isn’t even the same belt that he won last night. No, the change in colour is Kannon’s way of cutting history off. Done for his own benefit. No other Champion walked to the ring with the Purple leather belt around their waist. He will be the first. The only. Xavier : “What do you have planned for me fellow Scientologist?” Hubbardville’s own Mayor seems keen to see what his people have in store for his triumphant return to the religion’s new spiritual home. He will take each celebration, each jubilant action. Each firework exploding and each clap of the hand as justification of what he has done. He tormented the Pharaoh for weeks and his people love him. He cost Big Alan Steele the World heavyweight Title and his people celebrate. He tossed the big bold hero off the side of the Wrestle Vessel and they mark his return with fireworks. They care only for their fellow Scientologist. He does what he does for Hubbard. They do what they do because he does it for Hubbard. “We have a week of games planned for you Gold Patron Meritorious... The whole city is alive in jubilant celebration, tonight they will feed Christians to the Lions in your name and in a few moments the Hari Krishna Aeroplane lawn Darts shall commence.” The organiser is in awe of Kannon as much as he is trying to please him. While previous Champions like Big Alan Steele and Snow would be congratulated, they’d get pats on the back as they walked along the street... Kannon’s victory was treated like Ivan Stanislav’s. Only the celebrations for the Russian Bear were held in a land far away from America where his victory was greeted by many as a triumph against Capitalism. In Hubbardville they applaud Kannon because they don’t know any different. He is one of them. The pack mentality. The familiarisation... They cheer him because they know him, because he is one of them. Xavier : “Sounds good... I hope you have us good seats young Hubbard follower...” Despite the smug grins and the smirking, Kannon never lets his emotions show. His voice is usually monotone, completely flat other than the hint of a British accent. Not even this seems to excite him on the outside. One day he may slip. Let a word escape his lips. Nobody could know what it would be, but maybe in his deepest thoughts he may think. Wonder. Ponder. How did I get it so good? “You have the sat we would have reserved for Xenu himself, had he have not been a transitory, formless stellar being... I am sure it will be to your satisfaction.” The way the organiser speaks shows how in awe of the wrestler he is. This young man who has organised days of events to be viewed by 1000’s of Scientologists. Acts that have kept him without sleep. Events which have his blood pressure dangerously high. Events that HE alone has organised is Brown-nosing a man who has gotten to where he is by cheating, complaining, and buying his way to the top. The man doesn’t even see it. But you can be sure Kannon knows it. You can be sure the Phantom Booker, looking at his young charge, the man he will very soon manage, knows it. But there will be no end to the facade. As long at there are celebrities, there will be fans. As long as there are wrestlers there will be fans. As long as there is religion there will be followers. The PCW Champion has all 3 rolled into 1 and he knows it. Eleanor : “Will there be Popcorn?” If anyone is proof however that things will change... It is Kannon’s 20 year old bride. 6 years his junior in age and a gulf apart in everything else. When she first came to be Kannon’s side she did not love him. She wanted to be on TV and she made no attempt to say anything different. Some would call her a groupie. Some would have called her a whore. But now they call her Mrs Kannon... And those who serve her every whim call her Ma’am. She now loves both Xavier and what he does. They are a perfect match. Sick soulmates who take pleasure in what their partner does to others. Ellie watches in awe as her husband makes men bleed and their bones break. Xavier chuckles in jest at the way his wife puts down those she sees “Lower” than her. Eleanor : “Hey, look... That is, like, SO COOL!!!” The trio of wrestler, wife and manager all look skywards as a plane traverses the Wisconsin sky. Behind it trails a banner, another mark of celebration for the victorious Scientologist. Around them, the 1000’s of fellow Hubbard followers who are frequenting the wide open spaces of north west Hubbardville for “The Games” all look skywards too and try to make out the banner the vehicle is trailing behind it in the Hubbardville sky. XAVIER KANNON - GOLD PATRON VICTORIOUS The briefest hint of a chuckle can be seen under the Phantom Booker’s shrouded head as Kannon’s Green eyes fill with pride and Ellie~!’s mouth hangs open, loving the sites and the sounds around them. “This way, Gold Patron Meritorious...” The man behind all we are seeing ushers Xavier up into the stand that over looks Travolta Field. Nothing grand, just a set of stands erected overnight with a section at the top, in the centre standing out... This will be the place for our trio. As every head in the stand turns to look at their idol, Xavier reaches their makeshift private box. Grand not in appearance but in symbolism. Xavier : “A good choice of location young Scientologist, I approve of the view.” Such few words from the thin lips of the Gold Patron Meritorious mean so muck to the star-struck Scientologist. No matter what else he does after these Games. Whether he lives to 100 or dies next Thursday.. He will remember that he pleased Xavier Kannon. So many are pleased by so little. So may of them no nothing else. Eleanor : “Yeah, these are like, killer seats.” In the sky above, the sounds of more aircraft flying above filters down through the air to the ground below and stirs some enthusiasm into the crowd. The excitement immediately infects Ellie~! and she springs up to her feet, happily clapping along in her White vest top and short Burberry skirt. It is now, finally seeing the crowd before him cheer. Cheering for something celebrating Kannon. That Xavier realises who he really is. Eleanor : “WHOOOOOOOHOOOOOO!!! YEAH!!! COME ON!!!” He flashes his Green eyes left to his wife, he skirt flapping up as she bounces in jubilation. They then look right to the Phantom Booker, now both literally and metaphorically his right hand man. Then eyes centre to all that is being done in his name. Muttering comes from his mouth. His voice content. His being loving every second of it. Xavier : “Let the games begin.” He is Caesar. Hubbardville is Ancient Rome. The stands in which he sits are the finest and largest Coliseum. The declare games in his name. They have no God to worship so they worship Kannon and his fellow celebrities. They are not his fellow Scientologists as they thing, they are his loyal subjects. Just as these games are beginning, so will more on Friday. These games are to commemorate what he has done, Friday’s will be about solidifying it. The game will shortly begin, and Tyler Nelson is the Greediest player in it. “YEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAHHHHHHH!!!!!” The stand erupts as a plane flies overhead and something Orange drops from it and hurtles towards Hubbardville’s fertile earth and more importantly, the giant White rings that have been painted onto the field before them. The crowd buzz, watching every metre of it’s rapid descend, wondering where the Orange clad dummy meant to represent a Hari Krishna will land. Eleanor : “That is, like, SO FIFTY!!!” In front of, and to his left, Ellie~! looks enthusiastically at the Orange form that is plunging to earth, clapping and cheering it on as she bounces excitedly, getting into the games and still the Burberry skirt flashes up to show the pale, bare cheeks of her derrière. “FIFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFTYYYYYYYYYYYY!!!!!!!!” The sack of Orange impacts with the ground a few feet inside the 50 ring and the crowd stand as one... Apart from 2 men, Kannon and the Booker. While the Scientologists around them see the acts before them as the game, the 2 calculating men see the crowd as not even a game, but more a piece of it. Eventually Kannon gets to his feet, the Phantom Booker following shortly after and they join in the applause. But his mind is not on points, or plummeting faux Hari Krishnas.. It isn’t even on Hubbard and Scientology. For this week, Kannon’s mind will be on Tyler Nelson. The games will act as both a distraction and an ego boost ahead of his duel with the CEO. But in his Scientological mind, the actual match is towards the middle of his mind, the front as usual is reserved for the events before the match... The time where Kannon’s acts have gained him most notoriety. Eleanor : “That game, like, SO RULES!!!” Ellie~!’s child-like enthusiasm melts even Kannon and he subtly shakes his head and chuckle, putting his arm around her shoulder as she reaches up to hold his hand. As one hey collapse back into their seats, entwined with one another, in a split second going from thinking about the games and PCW to thinking only of each other. Kannon’s Green eyes reflecting in Eleanor’s pale Blue pair. Love. Lust. Desire. Mutual disgust of the majority of humanity. Have such an odd couple ever been so suited. Beside them the Phantom Booker slowly takes his seat again, noticing how easy his young charge can be distracted. Under the pantyhose his brain is ticking over. He has the PCW Champion by his side. He has the man Tyler Nelson hates most at his side. But he is flawed. Xavier is both ruthlessly effective and ultimately flawed. When he is focused he is deadly. When unfocussed he weak. Eleanor may inspire him, but she cannot manage him. Kannon needs direction. Kannon needs him. That split second, seeing the two so in love, the Phantom Booker decided he would become Xavier’s manager. He put Xavier in that World Title match. He will make sure Xavier keeps the belt in his next one. Eleanor : “So, are you like, gonna kick Tyler Nelson’s ass on Friday. because, like, NOBODY does that to MY husband... You should, like, throw him off a boat or something like you did on Sunday!” Quietly the Booker looks on. Presently those words from his wife inspire the Scientologist. But they are naive. Xavier Kannon at present has more raw, natural talent that anyone else in PCW... But with the Phantom Booker guiding him.... He can accomplish even more. But he must work fast. He must work in the shadows. The Scientologist’s mind id clear of any such thoughts. He doesn’t like direction, he doesn’t like being boxed in. He does what he likes. He always has. And he has always been able too. Still he looks deeply into his wife’s loving eyes. Xavier : “Yes my little Poon Puppy. As you so eloquently put it... I’ll kick his ass.” She snuggles up tighter to him, almost sitting on his lap as the Scientologist continues, speaking softly as he looks out on the entertained crowd who are watching another Orange mass fall to the ground. Xavier : “For Tyler Nelson.. The games have yet to begin.” As he speaks we see only the Phantom Booker, the sun dipping just down low enough to cast a shadow over him. A shadow that he has no desire to move from. In this shadow he will remain. Watching. Whispering. Moulding. Kannon may be the Champion. But in weeks to come he will not be the man who holds the Title. A new Xavier Kannon is being groomed. Being directed. Being designed in the mind of the Phantom Booker. As has been said at the start of the evening’s events. Let the games begin.
