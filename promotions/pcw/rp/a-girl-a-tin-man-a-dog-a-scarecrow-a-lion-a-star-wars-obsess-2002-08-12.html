A Girl, A Tin Man, A Dog, A Scarecrow, A Lion & A Star Wars Obsessed Cat!

12 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12th August “We’re off to see the wizard! The wonderful wizard of Oz! Because, because, because, because … “ [With the all-to-famous Wizard of Oz theme tune playing in the background, our scene slowly opens up in a lush, green meadow, green grass and blue skies stretching on for as far as the eye can see. A yellow brick road (Figured it out yet?) winds through the grass, many varieties of beautiful wild flowers, bushes & trees can be found. The birds are tweeting, the sun is shining, and it truly is a beautiful day.] “AHHHHHHHHHHHH!” [The voice of a falling man draws closer and closer, until suddenly … THUMP! We home in on a large bush, where the fallen body of none other than Andy Murray lays. Andy is today wearing a black Nike track suit, with white trimming, stripes and logos. His short, black hair is supplemented with two scoops of hair gel. Andy slowly rises, tweety birds flying around his head, just like a cartoon. Sitting up, Andy glances up, noticing the tweety birds, and smacks his head, trying to get them away, clearly realising that this is out of the ordinary.] Andy Murray: Haw! What the hell min!? [Andy rises as the birds flutter off. Brushing himself down, Andy steps out of the bush, and onto the yellow brick road. In the distance, he hears the singing … ] “We’re off to see the wizard! The wonderful wizard of Oz! Because, because, because, because … “ [Shrugging, the look on Andy’s face says ‘What the f^ck?’. Andy looks around for the source of the singing, but is unable to find anything.] Andy Murray: Hmmm … Where’s that pish comin’ from!? [Andy scratches his head, as unknown to him, the Tin Man, the Scarecrow, The Lion, & Dorothy and Toto step up behind him, ceasing their signing. Noticing that the singing has come to a halt, Andy turns around, and nearly jumps when he sees the characters before him.] Andy Murray: Pure pish min! Wizard ‘o Oz!? [Dorothy is wearing a blue & white spotted dress, her brown hair tied back into ponytails. The Tin Man looks just like you’d expect The Tin Man to look, all silver and box-like, with an incredibly obvious sign hanging from his neck, which reads “I R Da Tin Man!”. The lion is just some big dude in a really bad Lion costume, that looks like it could have been made in the 70’s, and The Scarecrow looks like … well, a Scarecrow, with authentic straw hair and all. The Lion smiles, before speaking.] Lion: Golly Dorothy, look! Is that the Wizard? [Dorothy turns, and shakes her head at the Lion, Andy looks on in total confusion.] Dorothy: No Mr. Lion, that isn’t the Wizard, in fact, I’m not quite sure who it is … Tin Man: Gee, I hope we find the Wizard soon, I cant wait until I get my new finger! Scarecrow: Yeah! And I really need the toenail. Toto: WOOF! [Andy holds out his hands, as if to say “What are you talking about.” The Group suddenly turns their attentions to Murray.] Dorothy: Oh, hello there. Tin Man: Hi! Scarecrow: Yo. Lion: Who are you? Toto: Grrrrr? Andy Murray: WHO ARE YE!?! … I was aboot tae ask ye … [The group pause for a second to think, before finally catching on.] Dorothy: Oh … I am Dorothy, this is Toto, The Tin Man, The Lion and The Scarecrow. We’re on our way to visit The Wizard of Oz. Andy Murray: Oh, ye dobbers. Dorothy: Yes, you see, we all seek something from the wonderful Wizard himself. Tin Man here, well, he needs a new finger, The Scarecrow needs a new toenail, seeing as the last one fell off, The Lion needs the new ‘Hooked On Phonics’ Christmas Special, and I just want to get back home … [Andy scratches his head, trying to comprehend what has just been said.] Andy Murray: Yer a chube min! The Wizard of Oz is’nae real! It’s a kiddie story min! Dorothy: Oh no, Oz is as real as it gets … Care to join us? [Dorothy points to the yellow brick road.] Andy Murray: Naw! Dorothy: Oh go on! We’d be awfully upset if you didn’t! Lion: Yeah, this trip will be mighty swell with ya! Tin Man: Be a sport! Scarecrow: Go on! Toto: RUFF! [Pausing to think, Andy makes up his mind.] Andy Murray: Meh … Awlright … But Ah’m warnin’ ye, if ye piss me off, Ah’m gonnae chib ye, awlright? Dorothy: Alright … Lets go gang! “We’re off to see the wizard! The wonderful wizard of Oz! Because, because, because, because … “ [Breaking in to song, The Tin Man, Dorothy, The Scarecrow and The Lion all link arms, and begin skipping along the yellow brick road. For a few moments, Andy stands still, staring at the motley crew, before shaking his head in disbelief, and walking at a pace behind them. After about 20 seconds, they come to the end of the song, and all break out in laughter.] Lion: Gee, that was a jolly swell song that was! Tin Man: Yeah! Lets do it again! Dorothy: Alright … One, two thr- [Before Dorothy can finish her sentence, another person comes tumbling down from the sky, much like Andy did, and lands in a bush. The group quickly rush over to see what just fell to the ground.] Scarecrow: It’s another one of them! [A head and shoulders rise up from the middle of the bush, making it instantly obvious who fell from the sky, it’s Ross, one of Andy’s Ned Posse.] Ross: What’s this min!? … Andy! [Andy steps up towards the bush, with the rest of the group.] Andy Murray: Haw! [Ross gets up, and brushes leaves and pieces of bush from his clothing, which is now revealed as a blue & white Reebok track suit. Ross steps out of the bush, as the group start their generic rants.] Lion: Golly Dorothy, is that the Wizard!? [Dorothy turns, and shakes her head at the Lion, this time Ross looks on in total confusion.] Dorothy: No Mr. Lion, that isn’t the Wi- [Andy cuts off Dorothy before she can finish, obviously he doesn’t want to go through the same gibberish as before.] Andy Murray: Eh, no … Shut up ye bawbag … Dorothy: Bu- Andy Murray: Nae buts. Dorothy: Alrighty then. Lets go gang! “We’re off to see the wizard! The wonderful wizard of Oz! Because, because, because, because … “ [And with that said, the group burst in to song, skipping along the yellow brick road once more.] Ross: Andy … Haw min … What the f^ck? Andy Murray: Ah’m nae sure, they think this is that Wizard ‘O Oz pish …. Ross: If this is the Wizard ‘O Oz, how come it’s a pure children’s story pish min? Andy Murray: I dinna ken … Ross: Hmmmm … How … Pish. Andy Murray: Aye. [Keeping their distance, Andy and Ross start to walk behind the pack, watching them carefully, yet speaking to each other as they go.] Ross: This is pure pish! Ah need a chippae! Andy Murray: I dinna think they have chippaes here min. Ross: Aye … This is pure borin’. Andy Murray: Nae Buckie either … Ross: NAE BUCKIE!? Andy Murray: Aye min, Nae Buckie. Ross: PISH! [Andy and Ross continue to walk along, before Ross speaks up again.] Ross: Haw! Andy! Don’t ye huv a match on Tuesday min!? Andy Murray: Aye min! Ross: Against they Mandark fellow? Andy Murray: Aye, Mandrake or somethin’ … He’s a pure dobber min … Ross: How? Andy Murray: Because he’s one ‘O they new chubes from CCW I think, Chube Championship Wrestlin’ min! Pure pish an’ awl, so of course, he’s gonnae be a chube, and Ah’m gonnae huv tae pure hammer him, by the way. An’ it’s fer the Risin’ Ned Title tournament, which is a pure briwliant title by the way, ‘cos Ah’ve held it before. So, once I win this tournament pish, Ahm gonnae pure be a two time champion min! An everybody back in Govan will be pure totally jealous min! Ross: Briwliant! [By this time, Andy and Ross are at least 10 metres from the group, who are still singing that damn song.] “We’re off to see the wizard! The wonderful wizard of Oz! Because, because, because, because … “ [Ross nudges Andy on the arm, and says something.] Ross: Haw, can we no leave these bawbags? Andy Murray: Aye min, lets gie it a shot … Ross: Pure briwliant. Andy Murray: Look fer places tae hide … Ross: Awlright. [1/2 of PCW’s resident Ned population start walking at a slower pace, trying to find a way to branch off from the group. Eventually, Andy comes across an opening in the trees and bushes, he grabs Ross by the shoulder.] Andy Murray: Haw! [Ross and Andy step through into a large clearing in the trees, nothing but grass for about 20 metres. Getting their bearings, Andy and Ross take a look around.] Ross: Eh? [As the continue to look around, suddenly, something swings out of a tree.] Thing: OOO-EEE-OOO-EEE-OOO! [Making a noise reminiscent of Tarzan, it swings down from a rope, and lands right in front of Andy. What is it? The Cheshire Cat, of all the things … The Cheshire Cat in the Wizard Of Oz!? The cat makes a soft landing, as cats do and then looks up at Andy, who looks bamboozled.] Cheshire Cat: Luuuke … I am your faaaatheeeeeeer! Andy Murray: Wut!? Cheshire Cat: Meeesa Jar Jar Binks! Pinch yerself! Ross: What’s he tryin’ tae say? Cheshire Cat: Pinch yerself! Or I’ll get the Emperor on you! [Andy shrugs.] Andy Murray: Ye waint us tae pich ourselves min? Cheshire Cat: Yessssssssss … [Andy shrugs, holds out his arm, and pinches himself … ] [Suddenly, inside the bedroom of Andy Murray’s country residence, Andy sits bolt upright, a cold sweat dripping down his brow. In the dead of night, Andy wipes the sweat from his brow, and starts to breathe slowly. Looking around, he suddenly realises something … ] Andy Murray: Haw! Pish dream min! [And with that, the scene fades out, once and for all.]
