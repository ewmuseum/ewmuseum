The dream to be a champion

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-20-02 Ever since I put my name on the dotted line on my contract this has been my goal, my chance to break through, to be a champion in the PCW. Now that opportunity is a reality, as I stand one win away from getting my shot at that PCW Rising Star Championship at the PCW PPV, “Third Dawning.” The road to where I am today hasn’t been always easy, in the early days I slept in a car to save money, I ate at fast food restaurant on what I called the “Wrestling budget” so I could keep some of the money I earned. I made twenty-five dollars a night with a independent federation that headlined in a beat down warehouse in front of eighty to a hundred people a night in New York City mostly every night so we could stay in business. That’s where I began to learn the ropes of the ring trying to get better and better as time persisted, climbing my way to the top of the wrestling field. I remember sitting on my beat up couch in my low rent apartment in the city watching PCW Tuesday Turmoil on my little fourteen-inch television and watching legends like The Trashcan man and Kremmen. My mouth jaw dropped at what the PCW was producing and I immediately wanted to be there more then anything else. So I started to practice and practice training my body to get ready for the PCW. Hopefully someday being able to join the PCW as one of their superstars and to be crowned as a PCW champion a dream I have always wanted to have ever since I saw people like Kremmen holding the title, with all it’s jewels and gold, like a crown every man wanted, but I felt I wanted it more and needed to continue pushing myself to get that opportunity. I bounced around for a while in small independent federations waiting for a big name company to see my style and hopefully take me in, perfecting my ability. I couldn’t believe that Cyberspace Championship Wrestling, the CCW wanted me to join their talent roster, and soon contacted me. My jaw dropped and I felt like I was on cloud nine. I was so excited that I would soon be gracing the rings that legends like The Supreme Machine and Sylkk were apart of. It was a dream come true and I began to soon flourish there as a top young superstar that was climbing the rankings very fast by winning the CCW Hardcore and Pacific championships in one night in my second week with the company. I was shocked and glorified for my accomplishments. Towards the end of the CCW run I began to finally get my spot in CCW as a top guy in the company. I was heading of the most successful and talented stables in CCW history, the New Era Alliance and coming off huge matches perfecting my ability in CCW to become a better wrestler, learning the ways of the ring by the legends of the CCW like The Supreme Machine, Sylkk and Ace Hawk. I couldn’t believe that they took there time out of their busy schedule to help me out to become a better wrestler, they showed me to always pass down my knowledge to the younger superstars so that when you are leaving your wrestling federation you can always be rest assured that it is in good hands as it continues to flourish to a great extent. CCW closed at an awkward time for my character with me to begin a feud with now PCW superstar and a close friend of mine, Vennemen Pendragon. I was deeply saddened by the way CCW went out and thought that we should have stayed around kicking ass and taking names along the way but what you want always doesn’t happen. Sadly the CCW closed its doors. That left me without a job and I need a job fast. I even began leaving the wrestling world for good and moving on with a normal life but the wrestling bug was still with me not allowing me to leave quite yet. Two days after the CCW closed I was contacted by the PCW to join them and sign a contract right away. I was shocked that they wanted me and I couldn’t believe that my dream came true; I finally reached the big time, the PCW. I boy’s dream became a man’s livelihood and all I worked for leading up to this point meant something that the hard work, money and time in my wrestling ability was worth it because I had reached my goal, something that I set since I joined the wrestling scene and now I knew I need to set new goals to push me even further to being one of the best superstars that has ever lived. Setting out new goals was easy for me because I wanted to succeed at the PCW level more then anyone so I set out some goals, to win a match, be a PCW Champion, have wins in triple digits and to be a part of the PCW Hall of Fame when my career is over in the wrestling world. I completed my first goal and won a match. Now I need to go for my next goal, the chance of being a PCW champion and I have had two other opportunities to complete that chance all were shots at the PCW Rising Star championship. The first chance was my first match and was a six-man match that my friend Vennemen Pendragon won. I was disappointed but I was so happy for Ven cause he earned it more then anyone. The second chance was a five man match at the Wrestle Vessel where Ven retained his championship and I left with a mouthful of sand as a gigantic sandcastle landed on me, not the way I wanted to start off. But then I got a third chance, in a Rising Star Championship tournament as the title was vacated. I got passed the first round and I’m currently stuck in the semi-finals facing my archenemy in recent weeks, Eddie Mandrake. Whatever happens in that match happens, I just hope I can come out with the big win and move onto the next round because the Rising Star Championship is taking over my body pushing being points I never thought I would succeed. The PCW Rising Star Championship signifies someone that “next big thing” in PCW. Someone that will be a star soon but as it stands right now is someone that is climbing the rankings to become a star. That is a championship I would like to start off in my career in the PCW and my chance is now. I can’t blow it, I need to prove to everyone on the roster that I’m not another passerby that I really am someone that should be taken seriously and is soon to be a threat to every competitor in the PCW when my time comes. But as it stands right now I just setting the path that I will take in PCW. I’m sort of beginning the pouring of cement in my stone on the imprint of PCW. Hopefully I can full fill my dream, something that runs through my veins, and something that I eat, drink and sleep. I desire winning a championship and now this is my chance. I can’t blow my chance and I won’t allow myself to blow my opportunity. I need to prove myself and now is the time to do so…
