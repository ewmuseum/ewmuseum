We Dub Thee.

1 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-06-03 Sergio Bullent- They don’t want none of this. Voice- Don’t you think it would be the healthy way to channel your negative energy? Sergio Bullent- Yeah if I was Greg Louganis. Voice- Ahahah, okay but seriously…. You’re going to have an emotional breakdown. Sergio Bullent- Breakdown? @#%$ if I Menolly Xenos I would be preparing for a cognitive shakedown. Voice- Say what? Sergio Bullent- I said Ill put Menolly out like a cigarette butt. Pimp smack her like a slut… then when I’m feeling generous, crack her like a nut. Voice- Damn you’re driven Sergio Bullent- Oh yeah, one more thing… “I Dub Menolly Xenos Unforgiven…..” ---New blood joins this earthAnd quickly he's subdued Through constant pain disgraceThe young boy learns their rules --- [[Brought up believing the world is handed to you and its there for the taking. The belief anything you want to accomplish in your life can very well be conquered. The idea that we can do what we want, when we choose. But is this really the impression we come to place faith in? It sure seems like it. Although in many situations this isn’t the case. More than we ourselves could imagine. We have been shaped at one point in time by someone else. Our very desire to be someone different. Someone original…. No one likes a rebel. Especially not the society we have come to grow into. This disillusionment has cost us many lives. Many more young breathes. People look upon suicide and other escapes as sin. But its more along the lives of a broken promise in which we become devastated. Promise given to us by our parents, teachers, and the rest of societies puppets. Escapes from this society in which we are EXPECTED to be successful and worthy of other peoples expectations. People who don’t abide by these regulations set by our environment are targets of criticism and neglect. In this culture those who want to try something different are labeled radicals. Or how about freak? Weirdo? Lunatic? Idiot? They do not enjoy the tranquil waters of which they settle disturbed. So when someone gets out of line a mask is placed upon their face. A mask more literally in the form of lies and dissension which only makes you feel like @#%$. Just because you step away from the crowd you are blanketed with deceit. Then everything you have come to trust explodes in your face. Why can’t they understand? Why can’t they accept us? Why can’t we set our own goals but live up to theirs? How can we take this pressure? We didn’t ruin our futures this culture did us in! Everything that was promised……. No more. I am but a “Harvester of Sorrow”. And now I must pay for their flaws…]] ---With time the child draws inThis whipping boy done wrongDeprived of all his thoughtsThe young man struggles on and on he's knownAvow unto his own That never from this dayHis will they'll take away --- [[Deprived of all hopes and wishes we have yearned for after they take them away. As a child its no secret I grew up in a wealthy upbringing. But it was apparent the rich hold their children in high regard and will not tolerate failure in their expectations. And for this I as well as my siblings suffered. It is but a lie if I were to say my life has been an enchanted dream. Far from it. You see I have this PH D in clinical psychiatry because of societies will. I have this MD in Medicine because of societies edict. And did I ask for it? No. But I guess once your born that’s your signature on the dotted line. Being punished simply because you want to take your own path in life…. ]] ---What I've feltWhat I've knownNever shined through in what I've shownNever be Never seeWon't see what might have been --- [[If you are a PCW superstar then you know what Im talking about. Many people didn’t make it here until they changed their attitudes and methods. And I am a clear example. Its play nice in the locker room but kill your opponent in the ring. This never sat well with me. Its either one or the other. But did I have a say in it? No. Its what people like Menolly Xenos wanted for me. At first I didn’t accept. Then I gave in. I gave in and look at me now. If I hadn’t had given in its obvious I would never be in this position. All the work habits in the world wouldn’t get you where you are unless you live up to someone else’s prospects. Im sure they anticipated I would but I didn’t. And so now in this position I am in…. After I have done it all. I now will take great pleasure not in paving a legacy in which they hoped for but starting a legacy in which none will soon forget. But now I hope to make it okay to be yourself. Make it possible for us to do what we want when we want. And I am proud to see that this federation is taking the steps in the right position. After all not too long ago it was them again hoping to compel everyone to go “old school” again. But there were a select few that didn’t give in. And for that I noticed that the Duke Williams and Pharoes are all about yourself and acting upon your own directions. Which brought more satisfaction in being a little Spaniard. To stand for sovereignty and choice from the control the tried to inflict upon us. And for the first time in my life and can say I am being my own man and choosing my own conduit.]] ---What I've feltWhat I've knownNever shined through in what I've shownNever freeNever meSo I dub thee unforgiven --- [[Turmoil has now been deemed Judgment Day for the mouthpiece of this communal society. Menolly Xenos as a woman has been stuffed with all this propaganda. And as a harvester of this attitude she must go down. shes never been more week and easy to take down. After all I have yet defeated her which is astonishing. And he was the one man to do away with one of my links. But this is now life or death. Natural Selection. I don’t lose in my own diversion. And he will understand just as everyone else that I am a man on a mission and I am not about to pause it because of Menolly Xenos' unwillingness to accept facts. To fail to this woman in a match as important as this one is not on my agenda. Menolly don’t get too excited about your past victories over me. What seemed to surprise me about your latest promo is you tried to avoid me at all costs. Again your ignorance is profound and will ultimately lead to your great fall. If I can defeat you then there is no reason to believe you have the upper hand in this match. I am all the more superstar than you can handle. The only person that ruined this place was Menolly Xenos herself. Why did I ruin this place? Is it because I don’t fall in line like the rest of the pawns? Menolly YOU ruined this federation with your kiss ass ideologies and intense oblivious attitude. You make a mockery of everything men like the undisputed LEGENDS worked to build the road for superstars to follow. Xenos as long as I have a breath in these lungs you will never touch my incumbent. This Tuesday you will experience my fury as I attempt to overhaul these ideologies which have consumed Menolly Xenos' very acumen. Tuesday Menolly becomes reintroduced to reality…. Prepare for the Tango…]] ---They dedicate their livesTo running all of hisHe tries to please them allThis bitter man he isThroughout his life the sameHe's battled constantlyThis fight he cannot winA tired man they see no longer caresThe old man then preparesTo die regretfullyThat old man here is me --- [[Their scar I will fight to vanquish from my body. Their word I fight to free from my ear. Their form I fight to rid from my vision. The resentful taste of their presence I liberate from my tongue. And their putrid odor of disdain I release. Menolly Xenos is but a product of this corrupt society in which the innocent are deceived. And as a product she fights for the factory which produced her. She is Public Enemy Number One and will be eliminated. As so many in the past failed I will succeed and I will overcome their influential presence. It is now time to build the bridge to deliverance from this attitude. You all lost your faith but I have not and am strong in mind. Xenos is not. So now the uprising begins. Can Menolly Xenos discontinue it? No. Autonomy from these products shall be realized this Tuesdat. My own personal mission is now Menolly Xenos’ problem. Cupcake…. Prepare to be Victimized by The Little Spaniard, Sergio Bullent and the Rising Star Champion, Matt Jackson.]] ---You labeled meI'll label you So I dub the unforgiven --- Sergio Bullent- This war is deeper than they could had ever imagined. Voice- Nothing I envisioned. Sergio Bullent- They now will pay for their broken promises. Voice- Those who failed to realize are but Doubting Thomas’s. Sergio Bullent- Never again will the children pay for their misguidance. Voice- Just wait for the subsidence. Sergio Bullent- Ignorance won’t be accepted Voice- Their careers soon to be intercepted. Sergio Bullent- These tears my reason. Voice- So we “Dub Menolly Xenos Unforgiven….”
