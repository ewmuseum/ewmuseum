Blitzo 2: Bloodhunt - Finding Astler

27 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27-10-02 In a quaint little run-down factory house, a table surrounded by slick looking laconic vampires is covered in all manner of illegal materials, from the blood red drugs being test-snorted by one vampire in a red suede jacket to the deck of nude poker cards. The drug tester snorts a line of whatever drug has been apprehended by the vampires and nods to the apparent “lead” vampire, who shuns away from the drugs as the tester offers him some. Another vampire with dark sunglasses and a homosexually inclined goatee checks over the deck, grinning at the voluptuous beauties on each card, until a thunderous knock sounds, originating from the large steel door serving as entry to the factory house. The lead vampire cocks his eyebrow as the rest of the vampires load their weapons, ever suspicious of a drug bust or worse…a hunter. Nodding to the goateed vampire, the lead vamp watches as goatee vamp rises up out of his seat cautiously, his pistol aimed at the door…ready to shoot until he hears a familiar voice. Voice: HELLO?! It is me, Fat Fanpire #1! Let me in! Let me in! The goateed vampire turns to the lead vampire in confusion, receiving a nod from the leader, the goateed vamp proceeds towards the door, sliding the lock out, he grabs the handle to open the door, only to gasp and suddenly burst into flame and ash as silver-tipped bullets from the Mach pistol blast through the steel into the factory house! The door slides open to reveal Blitzo holding Fat Fanpire #1 as a shield before him, and starts blasting the entire gang of vampires, reducing most all of them to ash. In the crossfire, Fat Fanpire #1 is repeatedly shot with the regular bullets, and screams out in horror. As the last of the vampires in this from room disintegrate, Blitzo pushes forward as Fat Fanpire #1 checks his wounds… Fat Fanpire #1: Oh FUCK! It isn’t silver but fuck me it hurts like a bitch! Blitzo: Y’know, I’m due to fight another hundred vampires in this scene, but to be honest I could just do this… Blitzo pulls back his cape and removes a big old “Ultra Violent” U.V. torch. Turning it on to full beam, Blitzo walks through the corridor whistling to himself, as tens upon tens of vampires scream from every crevice and nook in the walls, turning to ashes as Blitzo turns the corridor into a massive epileptic vampire’s nightmare. Fat Fanpire #1 ducks and covers as Blitzo reaches the near-end of the hall, turning to find five vampires running at him. As he turns the torch towards them, the batteries suddenly go dead…opening it up at the back and hissing, Blitzo throws the torch away… Blitzo: DAMN YOU EVERREADY!!! DAMN YOOOOOOUUUUUUU!!!!!! Blitzo lands a roundhouse kick to FF#1, sending him crashing against the wall. Turning to the five vampires still running at him, Blitzo yawns, then goes crazy, throwing punches and throws to the vampires, tripping them up and beating them around. The vampires roar in horror as the corridor fills with the sound of bones cracking and weapons firing. Five Mach shots later and the vampires are reduced to dust. Blitzo makes himself look cool again before walking past Fat Fanpire #1 to a series of canvas tarps at the end of the corridor. Blitzo turns back to Fat Fanpire #1, shivering and shaking in his boots…and points a threatening finger at him… Blitzo: Catch you later… Blitzo pushes past one of the tarps as Fat Fanpire #1 takes off, and walks into a room filled with dull red light. Before him there are three large tanks, humming ominously. Each one is filled with some kind of liquid, except for the middle one, a large one, which has in it suspended a man…Blitzo walks up to the tank and holds up a little torch to it, looking it over until the light passes over an old face…aged with wrinkles and topped with long grey hair and a thick beard. Blitzo lowers the torch and peers deep into the tank. Blitzo: Old man………what have they done to you… Blitzo: Fucked him up and kept him inside a big tank by the looks of it… Blitzo: Quiet you heartless bastard… Blitzo tries to find a means of opening the door, and instead leans against the tank to think, but swerves to the right as the door suddenly opens, a large red button reading “Open” pressed in where Blitzo was once standing… Blitzo: That explains a lot… Blitzo: For a vampire that can kick ass like a motherfucker and walk in the daylight…you really ain’t too bright are ya? Blitzo: Yo’ momma, fuckface… Blitzo: My momma is your momma… Blitzo: Silence… Blitzo watches as the fluids spill out onto the floor, and the body of Blitzo’s mentor falls out, suspended still by large tubes attached to his body, feeding him blood. Blitzo removes his trusty sword from its sheath, flashes it around, and with one swift slice cuts the tubes from his mentor’s back. The body falls to the ground, motionless still. With no signs of movement coming from the body, Blitzo kneels down next to his mentor’s head, and reaches for a stake on his thigh. As he grabs hold of it, his mentor’s eyes suddenly open, looking blankly at Blitzo…who quietly puts the stake back into its holster. Blitzo takes a few moments to decide what to do, before hoisting his mentor up around his shoulders and taking him from this place. Blitzo: Let’s go home………Astler… Blitzo: Like I said at the start…that whole introduction thing led up to this…NOW FOR THE MAIN PLOT!!! To Be Continued
