Juan Recieves an Identity Change (Part 2)

2 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/2/02 Time: 6: 04 P.MLocation: Lisbon, PortugalScene: On The Road Part 2 “ Juan is going to get a new look, huh?” The scene opens with Juan Borro, with the two secret officers Manuel and Pedro with him. Manuel is driving, so he can take Juan to the place where he will be getting a type of makeover. Juan is completely not ready for this, but knows it’s coming. He’s a little nervous about how he is going to turn out, but he’s hoping everything is for the best. Juan- I hope you guys know what your doing over here, because I don’t want to end up looking like some type of freak. You know I have a girlfriend back home, so you can’t have me look too bad ya know. Pedro- Don’t worry about a think, the people who will be handling you are skilled professionals, they do this type of stuff as a living. The worst thing they’ve ever done is implant silicon in someone’s cheeks once. Juan- Are you kidding me. Open the door right now, I want to get out of this car, right now. Pedro- Hold on to your seat man, it was just a little bit of humor, it’s like you’ve never heard a joke before. Juan- I know what a sense of humor is, but everyone knows you can never have one in front of cops, because it’s just not something you do. Manuel- Hey, get it right over there. Neither of us are cops, we are secret officers, we work for the government, and we are a much higher statues than all cops here in Portugal. So watch what you say? Juan- What ever, if you ask me, they’re all the same. Or at least, I can’t think of a major difference between all of you. Manuel- Well ya know, we don’t really care what you think Mr. Borro. All that matters right now is that we have to make sure you’re safe. Pedro- Yea, we have to keep you safe, even though after what you just said, we’d like to see you rot in a garbage can. Juan- Wow….you cops are really positive. I swear I don’t care who you guys are, but if you screw up my looks, every women in Portugal will have your heads on a stick for it. Just wanted you to know that. Pedro- Sure whatever you say. If you ask me you kind of look like a mouse. But then again that’s just me. Juan-What is up with everybody thinking I look like a mouse? I do not look like a mouse, and that’s final. Manuel-All right why don’t you two stop whining? Were at the place where we have to be so let’s go. The guys arrive, as they open the car doors and get out. Everyone approaches the place, but Juan stands back and looks at the place in awe. Juan- Why does this place look like a dirty garage, where they’d have a tattoo parlor or something? Oh wait, I know why, it’s because this place is a dirty garage where you get tattoos. Pedro- hey, before you start whining like a little old lady, why don’t you get the facts down? The truth is this place is a tattoo parlor, but there are skilled professionals in the back who have nothing to do with tattoos. We needed a place where you can do this all in secret and there wouldn’t be much people who noticed you. Juan- All I’m saying is this better turn out good, or else there is going to me some major problems around here. And when I say major, I’m not kidding I can throw a really mean fit if I have to. I can also get violent. Pedro- We know, we’ve seen video tapes of when your soccer team has lost matches, you sure are a little cry baby. Juan- Hey, being a cry baby and throwing fits, are two completely different things, so you better get it right. Manuel- And you better realize that cops and secret officers like ourselves are two completely different things. Juan- Whatever. The three men make their way inside, as they go through a bunch of different doors. Juan hides his face, but notices people around his with tongue rings, and all other types of body piercing and tattoos. Juan gets sickened, and quickly moves on. They finally reach the back and it’s a big and dark room. Juan- Is this where they are supposed to be? It’s so dark in here I can barely see a thing, can you? Pedro- Not really, but I see a light up ahead, so maybe we should go around there. Follow me, and I’ll show you. The other two men follow Pedro, until they finally arrive near a lighted area with a chair, and a man in a dark suit and glasses near it. Pedro- Hello, I’m guessing you are Mr. Vantello, the man who is going to be working on Juan over here. Mr. Vantello- Well you’ve guessed right. Juan, you can call me Victor. If you have any questions, just ask me? Juan replies slyly under his breath. Juan- Maybe I should begin with what you a freak in a black suit and glasses, can possibly do with my style. Victor- You know I did hear that. Juan- You did? How come everybody can hear, what I say under my breath, it must be some kind of curse against me. Victor- Not really, because I didn’t hear what you said in the beginning anyway. So let’s stop chatting and get down to business. Juan- All right. Victor- Follow me, Juan. Juan along with Pedro and Manuel, begin to follow Victor who goes to the back to pick out Juan’s wardrobe. Victor notices how Pedro and Manuel are following him, and he quickly stops. Victor- Sorry guys, but you’re going to have to wait for Juan outside. I get distracted when to many people are around. Pedro and Manuel cautiously leave. Juan- Don’t worry guys, I am a wrestler. If he tries anything, I’ll just break his neck, and throw his body away. Pedro- That’s what were afraid of. Pedro and Manuel leave as Juan is taken into the back. Pedro and Manuel wait for about two hours, as they don’t know about anything that is going on. They stand around and wait in their car, until Juan finally comes walking out. Pedro- Oh MY GOD, is that supposed to be Juan. Juan comes out totally different looking, as he is wearing big black boots, with black slacks that have a gold belt attacked. He has a white under shirt on, with loads of golden chains. Over it he’s wearing a big fur coat. He also has darker brown eyes, with longer hair, and a grown out beard. Not to mention the cigar in his mouth. Pedro- Juan, you look like Fidel Castro. Juan- I don’t know I kind of like it. I feel all-powerful and rich. I’ve never felt powerful and rich. I just hope Juliet likes it. Pedro- Well you sure do look different. Manuel- And old also, very old. Juan- You make it sound like I look like an old bag. Yes, I do look older, that’s what Victor told me was our goal. Pedro- Yea, well let’s forget about the whole clothing thing for now. Were running late for you’re meeting with the soccer team. We have to quickly get out of here, and put you in some soccer gear before we get there, so let’s go. The three men head back to the car, and are off to meet the soccer team, where Juan should be in for a big surprise. Scene Fades to Black. TO BE CONTINUED…………………………………………..
