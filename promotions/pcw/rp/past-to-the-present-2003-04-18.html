Past to the Present

18 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18/04/03 'Another day another dime I suppose' The camera cuts in on the smiling face of Manuel Labor, PCW's very own janitor. The reason he is smiling is cause it is 5 o'clock, knocking off time, and he final gets to leave the mops and buckets behind and do what he loves to do, dream about becoming a pro wrestler. As the camera follows Labor out of the Turmoil arena we see him pause as he passes the entrance to the ring area. A strange look crosses over his face and the screen begins to blur as we experience a typical Hollywood flashback. Mexico City- 10 years ago We see a young Manuel standing next to his mother and about 1000 other people in a small converted warehouse. Some cheer, some boo, but all have their attention focused on one thing, the ring. Inside the sqaure circle two men go at it, countering move after move, eliciting cheers and groans from the enraptured audience. Both men are sweating copiusly, in fact one even begins to bleed as we watch him take an amazing spin kick direct to the forehead. As the man drops the screen splits and we see the young Manuels face light up, obviously the man who just completed this brilliant move is his father. 'C'mon dad' the young man utters under his breath. He understands the importance of this one match, its winner will become Junior Heavyweight champion of all Mexico and go onto the finals on the American TV syndicated federation PURE, to wrestle alongside the likes of ALexander Miyagwa and Mang a Hero. As the camera swings back round to ringside we see Manuels father perched atop the turnbuckle and as he takes off in a Shooting Star Press, the whole crowd seems to freeze. Time slows down, second by second his fathers body slowly spins through the 180 degrees as the fans rise from their seats to applaud him. Suddenly thouygh the clock snaps back into regular time and with a sickening crash Manuels father is down... but not onto his opponent who moved at the last minute but right onto his neck. His opportunistic opponent quickly exploits this and picks up the three but when the bell rings it is the only sound that can be heard. DING DING DING For all eyes are on the prone body in the centre of the ring, Manuels father never got up from the SSP, the pressure ran from the top of his skull straight down his spine killing him instantly. As we cut away and begin to return to present time all we see are the medics rushing to the ring and a scared confused little boy clinging to his mother as tears stream down her face. Present Day Manuel wipes a solitary tear away from his eye and looks for the final time at the ring before walking out the door to his car. Climbing Manuel puts on the stereo and flicks through before finally settling on a classic rock Station playing 'Living on a Prayer' by Bon Jovi. Driving down the highway Manuel speaks to himself 'Oh well another day in the life of Manuel Labor. Every day I wonder how many people go home and think, oh that janitor in PCW is a nice man. He must have great job satisfaction. No, these fools they do not know the real me; the inner wants and needs that Manuel desires. Have they felt my pain and loss? No! Do they know that there is only one way to clear my conscience, to honour my father? No!' Manuel slams his hand off the dash before calming down. 'But why should they. Why would a Mexican like me matter to the great American following of PCW. I am just another river jumper with a slightly more exciting story than the last one.' Shaking his head Manuel drives in silence for a while before drawing the car into a car park where we see the sign for Golds Gym. Exiting the car we see the sign above the Gym entrance saying 'Wrestling Tryouts'. Obviously Manuel has got sick of waiting for fate to bring him what he wants so through Hardk work and manual labour he will bring fate to him.
