RAH in High School-Part 4-The Hallway Incident That Changed EVERYTHING...

Title: RAH in High School-Part 4-The Hallway Incident That Changed EVERYTHING... Featuring: Rent-a-Hero Date: 8-18-2002 'Ah yes...that faithful Wednesday at City High School....that morning started off beautifully, I had not only impressed my dream girl, Erica Angel...but I also landed a study date with her later that day. Things couldn't have been better. But as in all graphical literature, just when things seem to be looking up for the hero...everything gets a heckuva a lot worse.' ..::/::..We find ourselves in the halls of City High School, cluttered with students of all sizes, races, creeds, and styles as they all populate the halls in mass. Inaudible voices fill the halls, everyone in their own particular cliques as expected in high school ranging from the ever present jocks in their letter jackets and boasting their strength to the lowest common denominator, the nerds, their shirt pockets laden with pocket protectors and their pants suffocatingly tight upon their waists. We close in on one 'clique' in particular...that clique consisting of a group of four girls...a beautiful Latina with long flowing hair and in a light green dress, a gorgeous blonde chewing gum and in a pair of hip-hugging Guess jeans and a 'Delicious' belly shirt, another blonde with short, spiked hair and in a pair of baggy pants and a pink Hello Kitty t-shirt, and of course...the ever sweet Erica Angel, adorned in her split jean skirt and her low neckline white shirt as they all stand in a circle, the male passersby unable to keep themselves from stealing a glance at the lovely quartet of City High School's beauties as they gossp and talk amongst themselves...the topic of conversation actually quite different from their usual topics of 'hair', 'nails', and 'trash talk'. Their topic....Jayson..::/::.. Maria:'I heard from Lucy Hughes mother' cousin's babysitter's baby momma's best friend of Josh Harle's littler sister that SOMEONE in this circle is going on a DATE with...JAYSON --' Lisa: *cutting off Maria as she fixes her spiked blonde hair* 'NOT JAYSON --'Candy: *cuts off Lisa as she tries her hardest to think* 'You mean Jayson Summers, the guy who fingered me? Or Jayson Nickols, the guy who I gave head to at the party? Or Jayson Patrick, the guy me and Lisa had that threesome with...' Lisa:'WE DID NOT HAVE A THREESOME!!! How dare you even say I got involved in something like that...no we DID have that foursome with Jayson Priest AND Jayson Kyles.' Candy:'Yup yup....I've had my legs wide open all night like drive thru's for Jayson's...unless your talking....JAYSON --'..::/::..Erica cuts Candy off, the angelic beauty defending the rumor with anymore hesitation as she says..::/::.. Erica:'LOOK, it's not a DATE...we're just studying together.' Maria:'I bet. You know good and well he's coming to study 'anatomy'...and I'm not talking about no book either girl.'Erica: *giggles* 'Oh stop, we're just friends and he's helping me wiht my English, that's it. I mean...he is kinda cute....but I don't think we see each other as anything more than friends you know?' Lisa: Only figures...especially with BRUCE ROBERTS after you!' ..::/::..At the very mention of the name 'Bruce Roberts' every girl in every hallway lets out a loud orgasmic sigh, the female population practically head over heels for the handsome football star of City High and resident jock, all he City High girls except for Erica exclaming..::/::.. All The Girls In The School:'BRUCE ROBERTS........AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHH!!!' ..::/::..Erica sighs, seemingly not THAT impressed by Bruce as she answers her friends respectively..::/::.. Erica:'Look girls...he's cute, but he's just not my type. He treats his hair better then he treats most of his 'ladies' anyways.'Candy:'Speak of the devil...' ..::/::..The hallway begins to clear as a group of muscle bound jocks have a scrawny young man in dingy clothes by his arms and feet, his face bloodies from what appear to be hard shots to the face by the army of jocks as they carry him over towards the lockers behind the group of girls, the fate for the young man not looking too good..::/::.. 'Dewey Travis...it used to EAT ME UP to see how the jocks treated him. He was the exemplar of someone who was a completely nice guy...all of that being overlooked for his poor conditions of living and for being scrawny. For four years, Bruce and the 'Jock Squad' made his life living crap and it wasn't fair....and a guy like Dewey can only take so much...mark my Rent-A-Words' ..::/::..One of the jocks rushes over to a locker, popping it open quickly without hinderance as they force of five jocks stuffs Dewey unremorsefully within the steel box, his body contorting violently within it the harder they stuff him before finally slamming it shut as the school boy laughs at the act...all but Erica who stands appaled by the act. The jocks congratulate one another, slapping fives and butting heads, pleased at their one-sided victory yet again over Dewey. After the celebration, a handsome young jock with short black hair and wearing a black and red 'CH' letter jacket approaches the circle of beauties. Maria, Lisa, and Candy swoon at the mere sight of the man who must obviously be Bruce Roberts...his eyes only focused on Erica however as he strikes a flashy smile, leaning against the locker that Dewey is stuffed in as he bangs the door for his release, Bruce showing no concern for him as he says to Erica..::/::.. 'Bruce Roberts...Mr.Jock...he thought a letter jacket and good looks meant he OWNED City High. That may have flew with everyone there, but it never flew with me. He was my foil....the ultimate villain at the time in City High...he and the 'Jock Squad' were unstoppable and anybody who dared to stand up to them was in for the worst...I learned from experience boys and girls.' Bruce:'So...Erica...your looking quite lovely today. Any chance you thought about that date tonight?'Erica:'I've got other plans Bruce, I'm studying with Jayson.' ..::/::..Bruce looks appaled at her decision, offended even. His face contorts into a horried look as he approaches Erica closer, lightly caressing her chin with his thumb as he looks her in the eyes and asks..::/::.. Bruce:'Now come on Erica, lets be real. Why would you wanna hang with some comicbook freak and study when you can go cruising downtown with number 13 himself....Bruce Roberts. Come on, I know you wanna give into temptation here.'Erica:'I set a date and I'm going to keep it Bruce, sorry. And could you PLEASE let Dewey out of there?' Bruce:'As much as I'd love to do that for ya sweety. He deserves it, can you BELIEVE this guy stepped on my new kicks. This piece of trailer trash is learning respect right now.'Voice:'Somebody needs to teach YOU some respect, Roberts.' ..::/::..The school body grows silent, NEVER witness to anyone standing up against Bruce Roberts before. Bruce looks behind Erica, who is also turned until the eyes of the entire school body have their focus on one person....Jayson. He stands tall and undaunted in the middle of the hallway, slowly approaching Bruce as the students keep their eyes fixated on him....their eyes fixated on someone daring enough to speak out against Bruce, even the jocks shocked at his actions as they stand guard behind Bruce, who is flaming with annoyance at the sight of Jayson..::/::.. Jayson: *mumbles under his breath* 'If I knew I was gonna get THIS much attention...I shoulda said something like 'Holy Shizznittlebam' or somethin...'..::/::..He continues his stride towards Bruce until finally, he is face to face with his arch-nemisis...the babyface of the school, but the thorn in our hero's heel as they stare at one another disdainfully, all eyes on them. Bruce sighs, forcing a smile before saying to Jayson..::/::.. Bruce:'Hehe.....if it isn't SUPERLOSER, the only guy in this town who think's he some kind of superhero. Well news flash...there's only one Kryptonite rod around here and it's in my pants. So screw off while I'm talking, prick.' Jayson:'HOLY SENTENCE STRUCTURE BRUCE, you actually formed THREE FULL SENTENCES! My Jay-Son-Senses tell me that if you could have done that last semester, you wouldn't have to be taking English over again now!'..::/::..The crowd of their peers 'oooooo's' at Jayson's remark, Bruce flaming in anger that someone lower on the 'hiearchy' is standing up to him publicaly. Jayson continues, growing a bit more sterner in speech..::/::.. Jayson:'Now look here mister...Dewey doesn't deserve this. You open that locker NOW and let him out or I'll be forced to thrash you with some of the STRONGEST SUPER SAIEN STRIKES O' DOOM EVER!'Bruce:'Ooooo...I'm so scared comic boy. Why not give it your best shot?'Jayson:'YOU ASKED FOR IT!!' ..::/::..Jayson swings his arms about, the crowd confused at his display as he swings his arms about ever more wildly...in a circular rotation as his hands begin slapping the chest of Bruce with all his might...his might not being much as they seem to tickle the ever-popular jock. Continuing this sad display that makes the entire school body snicker, Jayson finally tires himself out, falling slowly down to his knees as he loses his breath, sweat beading down his Robin style mask until finally....slapping at the ankles of Bruce as the school body is either laughing or confused by this act..::/::.. Jayson:'Had enough?' Bruce:'My turn....guys, let's stuff him in their with 'Dorky Dewey'.' ..::/::..The students watch on, Bruce and two of his jock friends snatching Jayson up off the ground, Jayson trying his hardest to fight their fell clutch as one of them opens up the locker Dewey had just been stuffed in. The locker pops open, Dewey breathing for air, but the jocks givng him no chance to escape as they attempt to cram Jayson within....of all the students, Erica watches on with the most concern, in silent worry for our hormone driven hero as they push with all their might to cram Jayson within the locker. As they continue to push, Jayson thinks fast and exclaims..::/::..Jayson:'LOOK.....SOMEONE'S WEARING TOMMY HILFIGER...WITH FUBU!!' Random Voices:'NO THEY DIDN'T!! WHO DOES SOMETHING THAT DUMB!! HANG HIM!!'..::/::..The entire school body turns their heads in the direction Jayson is pointing, even the jocks as he takes this advantage to retaliate. Jayson smites two of the jocks with hard fists to the face, then breaking the grasp of Bruce as he takes the locker door and slams it into his beloved face which is accompanied by a brutal clanking sound, the concieted jock stumbling back holding his face as Jayson pulls Dewey from the locker, the victimized 'dork' barely able to stand as he gasps for air. The student body turns to Bruce, who finally uncovers his face to reveal a bloody nose...the jock star filled with indescribable fury as he races towards Jayson with a fist raised high in air...but his jock friends clutch him by the waist and arm, pulling him back and away from the high school hero as Bruce exclaims furiously..::/::.. Bruce:'THAT'S YOUR A**!! THAT'S YOUR A**!!! YOUR GONNA PAY FOR THIS JERK!! JUST WAIT!! THAT'S YOUR A**!!'Random Voices:'Whoa he stood up to Bruce! He hit Bruce! Jayson stood up to Bruce!'..::/::..The quartet of girls, including Erica, stand shocked at Jayson's heroic efforts as he tends to Dewey, who is down on one knee still trying to recapture air..::/::.. Jayson:'Are you alright my jock entortured friend?' Dewey:'LEAVE ME ALONE!!'..::/::..Dewey pushes Jayson away, running away and through the crowd of students as Jayson stand baffled by Dewey's reaction. He simply shrugs it off, turning his head to either side as he notices the looks of amazement from his peers...but only one look means anything to him...Erica's...her endearing look of adoration for his efforts. She softly smils towards him, her reaction more then words can say as Jayson just smils back the moment being broken by the bell as the students scramble off to class....Erica and Jayson heading their seperate ways as the school body continues to gossip amongst themselves about the evnts just seen..::/::.. Jayson:'Impressing girls is getting waaaaaaay too hard these days....whatever happened to pushing them in the dirt back in Pre-K, that always got thier attention.' ..::/::..As Jayson walks away, Erica and the girls stand still, watching him walk away for a few second quietly before Candy breaks the ice by saying..::/::.. Candy:'Somebody wants to cut on his Bat Signal....' Erica:'OH STOP...'..::/::..The hallways clear, the students off to their respective classes....but for four people....the events in this hallway will forever effect the course of their high school year. WILL BRUCE GET HIS REVENGE ON OUR YOUNG HERO?! WHAT WILL BECOME OF JAYSON AND ERICA'S STUDY DATE?! WHAT ELSE COULD HAPPEN JUST DAYS BEFORE PROM?! WHERE IS A PRINCIPAL WHEN YOU NEED IT!!
