Reflection

12 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12 January 03 REFLECTION A reflection would usually follow in a sentence containing the word 'Mirror' unless the statement were referring to that of Nascosto Ireul Nerezza who will never adorn such an overlooked luxury. However reflection in this particular form is how Gaz 'The Jester' Green has perceived his short but fulfilling tenure with Primetime Championship Wrestling. It has only been approximately six months since the former NTICW performer debuted during 'Third Dawning' yet the Emerald Menace has seen his career rise and fall like a heart monitor over the course of that time. The tangible smell of polished oak filters as we open on the furbished dwelling of the Front Office Jester. Such variation, Gaz Green is renowned for living like a celebrity, the new and improved, the younger yet equally talented second incarnation of Tyler Nelson so to speak whilst his former Tag Team partner Nerezza continues oppress himself by living in squalor. It is these differences that our host wishes to 'Reflect' on and as the camera sways basking on the up-market furniture showcased we capture the man in question sitting in complete concentration. Frantically scribbling over a piece of plain white paper the camera dropped over Gaz Green's broad shoulder as he continues to input his thoughts into his diary. 'What are you writing!?!' said the sweet and innocent voice of a female, without having to fight the curiosity we already know that the assailant is none-other than the Soviet Scarlet Natalia Miyagawa. Defensively Gaz Green closed the hard cover book with a shallow thud, pivoting on his chair he curled the corners of his mouth to that of a smile as he greeted his loyal valet who bared a smoking cup of coffee. 'Nothing....' retorted the Front Office Entertainment Manager. The prying eyes of Natalia breezed past her client as they ushered curiously towards the journal, it's leather red cover almost inviting yet Gaz Green kept up his defensive position, defending his most inner thoughts, even from his own personal circle. 'Anything else, Natalia?' questioned the inquisitive Gaz 'The Jester' Green. Natalia simply brushed off the question with a cold shoulder, her eyes fixated on that red leather book that sits ominously on the desk. The Russian beauty continues to stare wide eyed forcing Gaz to retort by concealing the article within a draw, sliding it away its mystique awe immediately vanishes and Natalia waltzes off. 'PHEW~!' sighed the present PCW Tag Team Champion as he dipped his hand back into the desk draw, removing the journal and placing it back in position his hand levitates brandishing a pen. In a downward motion the pen finally comes to rest on the paper surface, but before words and sentences grew in tandem Gaz Green peered around the room checking the perimeter for Natalia activity. Satisfied with his valet’s disappearance the Jester finally began and the word 'Reflection' took shape, the previous six months would be inducted from Gaz Green's own perception. REFLECTION Far from a seasoned veteran I'm neither a rookie and after being a shadow for so long I have been given my very own spot-light by the one man who has done so much to oppress me yet in this last month has done so much to benefit me.... Six months ago I would've laughed at the prospect of fighting alongside The Front Office and would've cringed at the thought of sharing a wrestling ring with CEO Xavier Kannon but the oppression has finally ceased and once again the King Midas of the PCW Tag Team division has been re-installed. Not even a year and the Jester is a three time PCW Tag Team Champion but all this wealth has came at a cost, a cost of friendships and arguably the greatest team to grace the division in a long time, it begs the question, 'Was all this sacrifice worth it?' It depends on how you look at it... Offering Nerezza as the sacrificial lamb to the Front Office God meant that the oppression was finally over, no longer would people deem me as the supporting player in Pranks With Attitude and I, in reward would have a singles career. Granted that Pranks With Attitude as an entity was successful but success in a depleted, non-existent Tag Team division meant that Main Event status was slipping from our strangle hold. What I am trying to say, and politely is that 'PCW's Most Hated Man' Gaz Green actually did the public a favour. In six months the hiring and firing toll has mounted to the point where it rivals the combined death count of Clyde, Dark Gimmick and DarkStar.... Gone have talents like.... Violence Jack... Charlie Loc... Frisco... Syko The Goblin... Chef... And the more expendable.... Kai... Amber Token... Romana Dumitrescu... Rich Rollins... I mean it's easy to accommodate the Lower Card but when Mid/ Higher Card talents vanish then you have a problem, with so many people already under contract to other promotions you need to look at those presently employed to fill the void. Nerezza may brandish Gaz Green as a traitor but that is a charade for the television, after all my bargaining prowess has opened further opportunities he is failing to capitalise on, I have saved his undead ass from, to quote the masses, 'A Dead Division!' People like Statuz Quo and Dante Jackson have made the transition and just look at them, I'm branded a traitor and sell-out but all I've done is exactly the same thing, stopped backing a dead horse. I should be looked upon as a Saint, I've handed Xavier Kannon the only missing piece of silverware he craved and my joining of the Front Office meant someone had to be ushered through the back door... I've filled the void left by the 'Has-Been Man' Duke Williams Maybe six months ago Duke Williams could still hold his own but times have changed, he can barely hold his trousers up, he can barely control his bladder and there's the niggling fact that his pension looms. The only reason he got the job done at Decimation was that he tarnished Xavier Kannon's name backstage and as he stirred the pot he knew that his band of Rednecks would intervene, 'CHEATER!' and with the numbers in favour of Duke Williams you can always expect the vultures to make an appearance, 'The RUSH Resistance!' Decimation Need I be reminded? After giving Nerezza the one thing he has craved since waltzing in through the PCW office doors, a singles career, I'm automatically loathed by every other circle other than the Front Office and victimised. Nerezza is portrayed as the face, the man waving a 'HELP' sign while I have to contend with his rage and poor officiating by Skitzo, it all boils down to the fact that Xavier Kannon took aboard the marketable asset of Pranks With Attitude. After I single-handily gave the fans what they wanted it is no way I will allow them to dictate Gaz 'The Jester' Green again, They wanted Gaz Green and Nerezza in a singles light? I gave them what they had beckoned for and while Nerezza soaks in the positive fan reaction Gaz Green is jeered one week to the next, shunned by the people who once laughed alongside him and all because of his P.W.A 'Get-Out-Clause'. They wanted heat for the Partner versus Partner match? I gave them heat, I threw away my tag team ties to put over a friend and how was I repaid, Gaz Green was beaten from pillar to post at Decimation. I never received a title shot for my trouble, nor an improved contract, can you blame Xavier Kannon, not at all, I came off the back of that Pay-Per-View with a loss and losers don't deserve reward, even if the contest was officiated dubiously. Shanghaied? A chance to redeem myself, another opportunity to rise above the personal oppression of Nascosto Ireul Nerezza but to date nothing has been signed, sealed nor delivered so is Gaz Green interested in a return bout? Why give the fans what they want, why humble to Nerezza but in saying that.... 'Do let me see!!!' murmured a voice from behind the trance-like Jester as he continued to scribble the events of six months. The book once again slammed shut, its closure accompanied by another thud and Gaz Green quickly placed his prized possession, no not the PCW Tag Team Champion, his personal journal back into the draw of the desk, keeping it from the prying eyes of one Natalia Miyagawa. Her miniscule hands clawed over his back and around his shoulders as she fought her clients resistance, she desperately wanted to see the inducted words of the Corporate Jester but with a turn followed by a click the draws compartment was sealed shut courtesy of a brass key. 'So Natalia are we going to wine, dine and.....' joked the Emerald Menace before the Russian beauty intervened with a timely interruption. 'I'm still a married woman, but... It's the nineties, the era of the open relationship, huh?!?' She concluded her sentence with a cheeky yet warm smile as she brandished her Wedding ring, swaying her finger in front of her client she toyed with the prankster as his head followed in a pendulum motion, allowing the scene to slowly close, ending yet another chapter featuring the 'Conniving Collective'. - The End -
