Metal Gear Spoofage 2 (part 6)

5 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/5/02 Atop the Big Shell, Raiden runs around like a chicken with his head cut off. Having disposed of the gelatinous pile of goo known as Bobby....Randy....Bobby.....whatever the hell his name is this week Stormbringer, he was informed that the most powerful bomb in the place was still active and ready to explode. Raiden: (Sucking on his thumb and rocking) I want my mommy. However, he cannot locate it. Colonel: Raiden, get a hold of yourself! You have to find that bomb. Raiden: (Whining) But I don't know where it iiiiiiiiis..... Colonel: Try to think. There are only so many places he could hide it. Raiden: Well he ate the last one. Colonel: That's it! He must have hidden it on himself! Raiden: But I checked his pockets, and.....oh no. Jack rolls over, shaking his head. Colonel: Yes, that must be it! Raiden: Hell no! I'd rather be blown up. Colonel: Raiden, Bobby has hidden the last bomb up his own ass! It's up to you to get it out. Raiden: No! Colonel: Yes! Raiden: No! Colonel: Yes! Raiden: No! Colonel: No! Raiden: Yes! Colonel: Then get to it! Raiden: Bastard!!!!! Muttering and whimpering, Raiden drops to his knees behind the protruding gelatinous ass of the fatso known as Bobby Stormbringer. Raiden: .....I don't want to do this. Colonel: Leah, motivate him. Leah: Hi Jack, do you know what day it is tomorrow? Raiden: (Holding his ears) Okay okay, I'll do it! Closing his eyes, Raiden yanks down the pants of Bobby's corpse. Raiden: God, did you ever wipe? Colonel: Do it! Whining all the way, our hero plunges his hand where the sun don't shine. Raiden: Ewwwwwwww! Feeling around, Jack grows desperate as he cannot find anything. Raiden: Where IS it!? Finally his grasp falls on something solid....he yanks with all his might, pulling a small bomb from Stormbringer's rectum. Raiden: (Muttering) My arm is tainted by evil, it must be removed. Reaching out for a nearby chainsaw, Raiden prepares to sever his own feces covered arm.....the Colonel speaks. Colonel: Raiden, the bomb's still ticking you know. Raiden: Oh yeah. Dropping the cutting tool, Raiden pulls out his voodoo doll....shaking his head once again at the utter absurdity of using a voodoo doll to diffuse a bomb, he pricks it with his crap caked hand.Raiden: (Muttering) Now this appendage which has been defiled may be exorcized. Again Jack grabs the chainsaw, revs it up. As he walks along bringing the blade to his flesh a figure jumps out of nowhere and lands before him. Raiden: Who the. Ninja: I am like you....I have no name. Raiden: I have a name, it's Jack. Ninja: ......quiet you. Raiden: Sorry. Ninja: I am simply a messenger from the La-le-lu-le-lo. The mysterious ninja taps his ear....Raiden looks on. Raiden: ? Again the cyborg taps. Raiden: Charades? Uh, okay. Jack holds up two hands, making a bird out of them as the ninja sighs. Ninja: Use your codec you moron. Raiden: Oh, sorry. Gripping his earpiece, Raiden speaks to his mysterious benefactor. Raiden: Why are we using codec? We're two feet away from each other. Ninja: It prevents eavesdropping. Raiden: ....eavesdropping from who? We're alone up here. Ninja: (Ignoring him) I have come to give you information. The location of the president. Raiden: Really? Where is he? Ninja: Or rather, the location of the person who KNOWS the location of the president. Raiden: Would it have been that hard to find out the info yourself? Ninja: Do you want my help or not? Raiden: Yes. Ninja: Then shut up. Raiden: ..... Ninja: The president's assistant, agent Aimz is locked up somewhere on the Big Shell. Raiden: Okay, what does this Aimz look like? Ninja: I don't know. Raiden: .....how am I supposed to then? Ninja: You'll be able to identify this person. Raiden: How? Ninja: She....how to put this delicately. She stinks. Raiden: Huh? Ninja: Just follow your nose. And put this uniform on, that way you will be able to sneak in undetected. The robotic warrior produces some sort of clothing and tosses it to Raiden. He holds it in front of himself, somewhat in disbelief. Raiden: It's a sailor suit. Ninja: Quiet. Raiden: I'm not wearing a sailor suit. Ninja: I could have gotten you a g-string. Raiden: Uh....guess you're right.... Ninja: But no one wants to see your pasty white flesh. Raiden: Hey! Raiden shakes his head, looking at the gaudy outfit. Raiden: But what do I.... The hero looks up to find he is once more alone. Raiden: Crap. And so a short time later Raiden marches on, looking about angrily from a white sailor suit. Guards stop to laugh at him, pointing and giggling. Guard A: Dyahaha! Guard B: You look like Village Person! Raiden: Shut up. Continuing on and blushing horribly, Raiden arrives at an elevator....down he goes to a chamber where several hostages line the floors, wearing identical clothing. Raiden: I smell fish. Sniffing the air, our sailor suit adorned hero walks slowly along....nearby, a guard watches. Guard: Hey you, wait a minute. Raiden: Uh oh. The sole watcher of the hostages marches up, eyeing Raiden warily. Guard: .....nice pants. Raiden: Thanks. Guard: Where can I pick up suit like this? Raiden: Try San Francisco. Guard: Dah, have summer home there. Looking about nervously, Raiden tries to find a way to escape the talkative man. Finally..... Raiden: Look, a half off sale on loinclothes! Guard: (Turning) Where? With the man distracted Jack leaps upon him and crashes his gun into his skull. Down goes the guard, fumbling for his radio. Guard: Need backup..... Raiden: Gimmie that! Guard: Get your own! Raiden: Argh! Raiden finally resorts to punching the man repeatedly in the face. Finally after an eternity he lays still. Raiden: (Breathes deeply) Then the guard's fallen radio blares. Voice: Why are you late with your status report? Raiden: Ack! Voice: What's going on, respond! Jack looks about frantically, then picks up the device and tosses it between both of his hands. Voice: We've lost contact with the main chamber. Send in the death squad, tell them to bring extra sharp..... Raiden: (Speaking into the radio) Da....all is....good, yes! Da. Da. Voice: .....is something the matter? You sound odd. Raiden: No, all is well. Yes. Das vidna. Gutentag. Parle vous francais. Voice: What? Raiden: Uh, nothing. All is good. Da. Voice: I think we should come down and assist you. Raiden: No, no, not needed! Good! Good Russian guard! Da! Voice: .....very well. Breathing out deeply, Jack places the radio back on the guard.....then once again turns, following his nose. Raiden: (Sniffs) After a brief second our hero looks down to find a bound and gagged figure, eminating a very not pleasant odor. Raiden: (Holding his nose) You're Aimz aren't you? Aimz: Mmmmph. Raiden: Hang on. Raiden kneels and rips the tape covering agent Aimz's mouth. He then looks at it for a second, staring at the large clump of hair stuck to the sticky end. Aimz: That hurt! Raiden: Sorry. Aimz: (Rubbing her lip) Who told you about me? Raiden: Some guy dressed like a ninja. Aimz: (Looks blankly) Raiden: What? Aimz: You're sure you haven't been dipping into the shrooms? Raiden: No. Aimz: Nothing wrong with it, I have some in my pocket actually..... Raiden: No. Aimz: Oh well. Let's talk on codec, it's much safer. You never know who may be watching you. In a room high above, Revolver Hosselot sits picking his nose and watching the pair talk on a monitor. As they go to codec he slams his free fist off the desk. Hosselot: Damn! Hosselot then looks at himself crosseyed for a second, reaches up and yanks his arm, pulling Liquid Clyde's finger out of his nostril and holding it down as it squirms. Hosselot: Stop that! Below, Aimz continues her convo with Raiden in private. Raiden: Do you know where they're keeping the president? Aimz: Well, he was in here but for some reason he started complaining about the smell. He wouldn't shut up, eventually they took him up to the first floor. Raiden: It is pretty ripe. Aimz: ...... Raiden: Anyway, you don't think they'll hurt him do you? Aimz: Probably not, considering he's working with them. Raiden: Working with them? Aimz: Is there an echo in here? Raiden: ..... Aimz: So yeah. They input the launch code with his help. Raiden: You mean they can nuke us at any time!? Aimz: Basically. And.... The smelly captive stops short. Aimz: Wait, here comes Snake. Raiden: (Whining) But I'M Snake! Raiden looks on as through a nearby window a man in a black trenchcoat walks up to Revolver Hosselot. Hosselot: Kriegidus, Stormbringer is dead. Kriegidus: Saves us the trouble of killing him ourselves. Hosselot: Why would he betray us? Kriegidus: How should I know? The man ate a live bomb and shoved another up his own ass for the love of evil. He obviously was a nimrod. Hosselot: True. Hosselot: I heard a man who looks strangely feminine has been wandering around too. Raiden: Hey, I'm not feminine. Hosselot: What was that? Aimz: Get down you idiot! Kriegidus: (Shaking his head) Anyway, it doesn't matter. The president is needed for another hour, then we'll use his code, then we'll get rid of him. From behind the pair walks another form, up to Hosselot. Brandi: What about the ninja thing? Kriegidus: I'm looking into it. Hosselot: Don't cast suspicion where it isn't due. Brandi: Where it isn't due? You watched my father die! Hosselot: That was an accident. He was climbing behind me on a ladder while we were trying to escape, my foot accidentally hit his face. Brandi: 27 times!? Hosselot: Yes. Brandi: Argh! The Russian soldier draws her gun and points it at Hosselot, who does the same in half the time. Hosselot: I realize you're angry, but if you ever do that again I'll make the far wall into a Picasso rendition with your brains. Brandi: Feh. Fine, I'll shoot you in the back instead! Kriegidus: Stop this bickering! Hosselot: She started it. With a disgusted sigh Brandi leaves the room. For a moment all is silence....then Hosselot screams in pain. Hosselot: (Holding his arm) Not again! Kriegidus: (Sighs) The old man's arm suddenly whips out, slapping him across the face. He tries to push it down, but it overpowers him. Kriegidus: (Shaking his head) How am I supposed to conduct evil when I have help like this? Hosselot: Argh! Hosselot's Clyde arm moves south, digging into the seat of his jeans. He clutches his ass, scooting in a circle around Kriegidus. Hosselot: Ow....that...ow...isn't....funny! Looking away, Raiden again confers with Aimz. Raiden: So that's Snake.....but he died. Aimz: Either that or it's someone totally different just calling himself Snake too. Raiden: Why didn't I think of that. Aimz: Anyway.....oh no, he's coming! Raiden: What? Jack looks up to see the in pain Hosselot jogging into the room. With a mighty effort he pulls his arm out of his nether regions and looks across the room at Raiden. Hosselot: What IS that smell? Raiden: (Gulps) To Be Continued
