Back in the Saddle

23 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-23-2003 We fade in to find our resident Adonis, Dan “Big O” Osbourne sitting patiently to be interviewed. For once, he has clothes on, a navy blue Armani to be precise. Draped over his left shoulder appears to be a Championship belt. Unfortunately the camera is not close enough to see what belt it is, exactly. The room has a very soft appearance, with plush red carpeting and red throne type chairs. Behind the cherry oak desk sits a small man, somewhat shady in appearance. His suit, although nice, is obviously a J.C. Penny special as it fails to drape him as well as Dan’s suit does him. His handlebar moustache looks completely out of place. With the addition of the bad perm, he’s almost a spitting image of Gene Shallot. Truly a high class show. Interviewer: Greetings, Ladies and gentlemen. Welcome to the show. Tonight we have a special treat for you. With me tonight is Dan “Big O” Osbourne who at one time was a promising young talent in the PWA, PCW2, and then mysteriously after moving up into PCW, he disappeared. Tonight we begin to delve into the mystery. The interviewer turns to Dan “Dan. Welcome to the show.” “Thank you. It’s good to be here.” “Let’s just cut to the chase. What happened to you?” “Well, it’s a long story, full of mystery, intrigue, and sex. Luckily though, I have some great flashbacks for you.” “Flashbacks? And just how are our viewers supposed to see these? I don’t get it.” “If you’ll shut up, I’ll tell you. It’s like this: I was on my way to the arena to compete in my match, but my limousine got hijacked.” “Hijacked? By who?” “I’m getting to that.” “So you didn’t make it to your match?” “No.” “But the world watched as you jobbed… hard.” “Look, do you want to hear the story or not?” “Sorry, Dan.” “Why don’t we just jump right into the flashback?” Fade in We find ourselves in the back of the limousine belonging to our resident Adonis, Dan “Big O” Osbourne. We see Dan and his valet, Molly drinking champagne while his manager, Mikey, slams back what appears to be another Guinness. Dan is dressed casually in a dark blue jumpsuit which everyone knows is his usual garb for the days he has a match. Molly, as always, is dressed to the nines in a sensual black sequined gown. Mikey looks like crap in his ripped jeans and ugly green t-shirt with the front pocket torn. Mikey wipes the foam from his beard and speaks… Mikey, in his raspy voice, “Dan, I tell ya, you’ve made it to the big time.” Molly speaks much softer that the grizzly old man, holds Dan’s hand sweetly. “He’s right, Baby. Now that you’re in the PCW, the sky’s the limit. You’ll be at the top in no time.” “Yeah, I know. Especially after they see my match tonight, the bookers will have no choice but to move me straight to the top. It’s what the fans want I assure you.” Suddenly, the car veers of the road and violently slams into a telephone pole. Like a tsunami crashing into the shore, the limo is overwhelmed by hundreds of persons dressed from head to toe in black jumpsuits. They appear to be… ninja. Dan steps calmly from the limo, pops his neck, and eyes the interlopers. “Something I can help you with?” A slender figure steps forward, clad from head to toe in what looks to be black spandex. “Our master would like to speak with you.” “I don’t suppose you’re going to tell me who your master is are you?” The ninja says nothing. “Didn’t think so. You’ll have to excuse me, but I have a matter of a bit more importance to attend to. Have your master’s people get in touch with my people.” Again, nothing but silence. Dan grows obviously more impatient. “No? Well I hope you don’t mind if I try to prevent this meeting?” Silence. “Don’t everyone speak at once.” With a gesture, the lead ninja orders the others to attack. Dan ducks behind the first attacker and delivers a powerful German suplex, rolls through and Snap Mares the ninja coming from behind him. “You know, you’re a lot taller than most of the ninjas I’ve run into.” Bodyslam! “And softer.” Dan jumps up to the roof of the dilapidated limo and begins throwing lefts and rights, kicks, suplexes, clotheslines, and everything else he can muster. He soon discovers a submission master is not equipped to deal with an onslaught such as the one before him, and he is eventually drowned in a sea of black, as the mass of ninja swarm on top of him. Just before he loses consciousness, Dan sees something truly disturbing… himself. “GASP! Yourself?!” The interviewer, along with the studio audience, is aghast. “What are you getting so worked up about? I told you it wasn’t me. C’mon, man, you should’ve seen this one coming.” Calming himself, the interviewer replies, “It’s showmanship.” “If that’s what you have to tell yourself to get you through the night.” “So what did you mean about the ninja? Taller? Softer?” “It was the strangest thing. They were all women. Tall, skinny, well-built women.” “That’s really odd. So what was the significance of that?” “That will become apparent as the story unfolds.” “Oh, Okay. So what happened next? Who was the master? How did you escape?” “I’m getting to that. So anyways….” An obviously high priced office is our current setting. Dan is tied to a red velvet chair. He begins to awaken… Obviously waking from unconsciousness, Dan is quite confused. “Uhhh… Where am I?” A soft, yet professional woman speaks, “You are in my office, Mr. Osbourne.” The view pans around to see an attractive woman dressed in a power suit sitting behind a large oak desk. Strewn about the desk are numerous files and miscellaneous paperwork. She has one file in her hand. “My name is Elizabeth Montoya. You have been brought here at my request.” “Request? More like orders. A request would be a phone call. You sent ninja! Now granted with as many of them as there were they could’ve lain on the ground to spell the word ‘please’ but that’s just not the same.” “Are you done, Mr. Osbourne?” “Not by a long shot, sweet cheeks. Do you have any idea how expensive this suit is? Or that limo? And what about my companions? They might be dead.” “I assure you they’re not. They’re safely tucked away in a different room.” “I’m not done yet! Don’t interrupt me! Let me tell you something about manners…” Dan is suddenly shot in the chest by a dart. “Don’t you ever shut up?” Dan awakens again in the same plush office, but notices something he failed to the last time. Lingerie is scattered all over the room, but not as though thrown from a body in the heat of passion, but rather tossed aside as though rejected. Dan, now in a familiar state of confusion begins to stir. “Wha? Where am I?” “Still in my office, Mr. Osbourne” “I suppose you want to tell me something now?” “If you’re finished complaining.” “I’m not, but I’ll put it on hold… for now.” Rolling her eyes and speaking with unadulterated sarcasm, “I count the minutes until you really let loose.” “Can we get on with this?” “By all means. Now as I was saying, I requested… excuse me, ordered to have you brought here because I have a job for you and you alone.” “Why me?” “Why you? Wasn’t it you that infiltrated the fortress of the terrorists that kidnapped your precious Molly?” “How do you know about that?” “I know many things about you, Mr. Osbourne. More importantly, I know you’ll do this job for me, because I have Molly and your manager, Mikey, as well.” “How do you know that I won’t just bust us all out of here and leave you lyin’ in the process?” “I don’t. But I have faith.” “Like from Buffy? I love that show. Where is she? I’d love to get her autograph. I’m sure she’d like mine as well. Did I ever tell you about the time that I met Sarah Michelle Gellar? I was in Vegas… “No, you twit, not Faith from the TV. Faith, as in belief without proof.” “Oh. So. What’s the job?” “You are to use any means you have to infiltrate and sabatoge…” Back in the studio, the audience sits on the edge of their seats. ”What?! What was it?” Dan chuckles at the interviewer’s impatience. “I’m gonna hold on to that bit of info for a while.” “Jeebus why?” “Suspense. Heh” Back in the office of Lady Montoya… “So will you accept?” “Well, it doesn’t appear that I have much of a choice. So I guess the answer is ‘yes.’” “Excellent.” “When did you turn into Mr. Burns?” “You watch too much television.” Elizabeth tosses a manila folder in Dan’s lap. Dan eyes the folder for a few minutes, peers over the top and looks at Lady Montoya. “Too easy.” “Glad to hear it. You better get started.” Fade to Black.
