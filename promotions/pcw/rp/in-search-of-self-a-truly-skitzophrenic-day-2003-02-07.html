In Search Of Self: A Truly Skitzophrenic Day

7 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-02-03 The king-sized four-poster bed, a 30” flat-screen TV, a mini bar, Jacuzzi, balcony…Skitzo was living the life in the master suite of this hotel! He had ordered champagne and strawberries which arrived approximately 45 seconds after he ordered them, whereas in his old room it took 6 hours to have one towel delivered…albeit it was for cleaning up the sheep blood, but it was 6 hours all the same. This room was especially groovy for the simple fact the bed was bouncy and reinforced so it wouldn’t break! Skitzo: Fuckin’ killer! Skitzo leaped and jumped all over the king-sized bed, even flipping and slamming down, practicing his elbow drops, frog splashes and Nestea Plunges. While other PCW wrestlers were training in the official gymnasium in the Lloyd Center, toning their muscles and preparing for one night of brutal combat, readying themselves for putting on a show and staying safe, healthy and fit doing it…Skitzo was doing jumping jacks on his bed. Grabbing a pillow, Skitzo threw it down and jumped up especially high, flipping in mid-air and coming back down to earth with a belly flop. Skitzo: I’M THE KING OF THE WOOOOOOORLD!!! Knock…Knock…Knock… Voice: Room service! Skitzo jumped into the air again just as the knocks sounded, and froze…falling back to the bed with a crash. As the bellboy outside waited, rumbling inside the room brought a look of concern across his face. He contemplated opening the doors but as he reached for the exquisite golden handles he was almost thrown back as something large hit the doors with force. Looking at the door with horror, he gasped as a loud screech echoed through the master suite bedroom, and took off down the hall and into the elevator, the doors opening suddenly and Skitzo falling out, tangled in blankets and hitting the floor in time to see the bellboy screaming as the elevator began to descend to the ground floor. Skitzo: Fucking kids… Looking at the cart the bellboy was accompanying, Skitzo’s eyes light up as he peruses the selection of goods. Skitzo: Complementary towels…perfumes and aftershaves…Lady of the Night recommended service cards…wow, they really think I’m this Highlander guy! Taking the cart back into his room, Skitzo pondered. It wasn’t often he pondered, but this was the perfect opportunity. He had tripped over the blankets on the way to answering the door for room service. That was fine, but the bellboy misinterpreted it for some kind of commotion. Now he had to put his thinking hat on. He assumed the bellboy had brought the cart up because, for the fact Skitzo hadn’t asked for anything else, the bellboy must’ve known Skitzo was booked in as “Adrian Paul” a.k.a. William Williams. Therefore, the receptionist was a liar, but the main point was that now the hotel staff were going to be informed of a commotion in Adrian Paul’s suite…which could mean… Skitzo: MORE DEMANDS!!! Taking no time to think about it, Skitzo suddenly began twitching and convulsing, his body becoming fuzzy until the next thing he knew he was standing next to his 12 split personalities. Skitzo: What the hell am I doing here? Skitzo: Hey, nice digs…did I hit the main event? Skitzo: Guys… Skitzo: Maybe we got fired and we’re staying in a hotel! Skitzo: No guys, listen up… Skitzo: I think I’m seeing double… Skitzo: Where are all the titties at?! Skitzo: SILENCE! The room is filled with silence as the 11 other Skitzos looked at the original. Gathering the split personalities around him in a huddle, the original Skitzo divulged his plan. Skitzo: Now look; we’re all on the same team here, and from what’s just happened you guys know the deal. Now I’ve got a plan to score us some demands and shit, y’know like on Worlds Worst Hostage Situations… Skitzo: This sounds promising… Skitzo: Anyway, I need you guys to get some matching masks and suits, I don’t know if you just tear up some towels or something, and then tie me to a chair. They all think I’m this fucking Highlander guy, so if we act like you guys are terrorists we can score some cash or something, maybe even fuck up Tommy Gordo a little, you in? The 11 Skitzos nod slowly, then dash off to find matching suits. As Skitzo sets up a chair in the middle of the room and sits down comfortably, he watches as his split personalities rush into the bathroom, and after simultaneous “Wows” emerge carrying two towels each. Setting them on the king-size bed, they take the first towel, tearing it in half and wrapping one part around their forehead, and tying the other around their faces in a bandit-style mask. Taking the second towel, they wrap it around their bodies and tuck it into their pants, to give the illusion of terrorists. Sitting in the chair, Skitzo looks thoroughly impressed. Skitzo: Ok guys, great! Now just tie me up and we can get this to goin’! With that, the 11 terrorist Skitzos tied Skitzo up using the ropes lining the bed, and some curtains. As soon as the entire ensemble looked convincing, they gathered around Skitzo, one of them holding a telephone to his head. Pushing the necessary buttons to dial room service, one of the terrorist Skitzos pressed the receiver against the hostage Skitzos head, and the dial tone signified the count down to fuck-up central… Room Service: Hello? Hostage Skitzo: Hello. My name is William Williams. I am being held against my own will in the master suite bedroom #45B. It is my captors’ demands that the owner be present at this room within five minutes. If this or any of these demands are not met, I, William Williams…will be executed. On the other end of the line, room service is quiet…until one of the terrorist Skitzos hangs up the phone. Skitzo: And now…we wait! In 3 and a half minutes, the door was knocked upon, opened by one of the terrorist Skitzos to find the hotel manager standing there. Terrorist Skitzo: HAYABUSA MINKY PINKY DOSS KAMATE KAMATE KO!!! The Hotel Manager still stood there, accompanied by two security guards, clasping his hands nervously and looking at the wild terrorist with a kind of reserved fear. Hotel Manager: Please, we don’t want any trouble here. Take what you want and go, please…we won’t call the police, just don’t do anything too drastic! A few of the terrorist Skitzos leans across and whispers into the chair-bound Skitzo’s ear. Skitzo: They say OK. They’re only demand is a helicopter and…SAMUEL L. JACKSON! The hotel manager cocks his head in confusion. Hotel Manager: A helicopter…and Samuel L. Jackson? Through the magic of boredom, we can skip the following ten minutes whereby the hotel manager argues with Skitzo, three of the Skitzos make glorious love to one of the receptionists, and a brass monkey knocks out the cab driver as Samuel L. Jackson makes his way into the hotel. Samuel L. Jackson: Yo muthafuckaz! Wut up!?! Random Person: Some terrorists are holding the Highlander hostage! They asked for you specially! Samuel L. Jackson: Yo muthafucka betta make it quick! I gotsta deny Statuz Quo bein’ my baby boy! AIIIIGHT! Making his way to the top floor, Samuel is welcomed by hotel staff and rushed into the room with the Skitzos. Slamming the door shut behind him, one of the Skitzos makes sure everyone has been herded away as Samuel sits down next to the chair-bound Skitzo. Skitzo: Look I ain’t got time to waste, these guys demand that you go postal on someone for us… Samuel L. Jackson: Got an issue? Playa I’ll help out anyone from Scotland! Dem funny skirts are da shiznit! LOL! LOL! LOL! Skitzo: Ok…one of these men has in his hand an address, and waiting outside is the helicopter. You know what you have to do? Samuel L. Jackson: Psht…ain’t no thing but a chickenwing, I’m back and ready to play, muthafucka! Skitzo: THEN AWAY! Fleeing like a flailing madman, Samuel L. Jackson boards the demanded helicopter, as it races off into the horizon towards a Los Angeles beach house nestled away on the coast before a golden sunset, sending rays of light shimmering across the blue calm waters, the diamonds dancing along the surface as the wonder of a new film era arrives on a front porch… Samuel L. Jackson: HEY YO!!! In the shadows beyond the door, a figure slowly approaches; opening the door the figure is suddenly planted with a hard bitchslap. SHABLAMMO-SMACKATAZZ!!! Samuel L. Jackson: DAT’S FO’ DA SCHIZOPHRENIC MUTHAFUCKA, MUTHAFUCKA!!! Recoiling in horror, the figure looks back up, as Samuel L. Jackson runs away, tripping over a dog shit and hitting his head on an old salami as he falls, while the figure in the doorway rubs his sore cheek. TTT: BOGUS!!! Back in the hotel, Skitzo suddenly recombines into one, as the door bursts open and hotel staff return to find Skitzo lying in his bed napping. Hotel Manager: HEY! Where’d all the terrorists go?! Skitzo: …Eh…what terrorists? Hotel Manager: …………………………Ok, sorry to trouble you……… Lying in his bed, Skitzo was sure to go to sleep quickly and wash away this nightmare of a day…forever!
