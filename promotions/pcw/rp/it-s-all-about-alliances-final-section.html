It's All About Alliances, Final? Section

Date: not entered Nobody questioned the rich and powerful. They were the top of the food chain…the E L I T E …if you wanted to label them. That was a lie. “We’re just going to check on some things. We’ll be back to pick you up in a few hours.” Xavier Kannon was one of those rich and powerful. CEO of PCW, and high-ranking official in Scientology, everything seemed like it was on top of the world with the Gold Patron Meritorious. But there was one thing keeping him from completing the final wrung in the global ladder. A god who’s drew in more fear alone than Kannon’s entire West Beverly Hills religion could ever hope to achieve. “Okay. Take care! I’ll just be training with Inphino until you get back!” Of course how much fear could you inflict when your faith’s idea of Hell is having to wait at all the best restaurants, having only TWO cleaning staff, and NOT being invited to the surprise party Jenna Elfman threw for herself? His kingdom was elegant on the outside…but the inside of it was just as shallow and empty as Eleanor’s skull. “I will have lunch prepared for her once we are finished with her lesson, Maxis.” Fame, status, and wealth…that’s the life for you, is it not? Well it must be…otherwise you would be better at the whole fighting aspect of this business. You see…there are two paths to success in this business. There is the path of the weak…and then there is the path of the strong. The weak take the path that is easiest…of course they must give up certain things to make this path much easier. Trust… Conscience… Ethics… A sense of honor… Sell your soul to your monetary master, children…because you are weak. The money makes your lives easier…it makes things much smoother than having to work for it. Once your soul is gone…it will be so much easier to lie, cheat, and steal your way to the high point where you think you belong. “See you later, sweetheart.” Yes…say goodbye to what makes you a man… Just like Kannon did… Because the second path is the true path…not the path that so many of the weak have taken. You see…the weak hate work. The sweat, the overexertion of energy…it is sickening to them. Why do so much when you can place words in a convincing order for your own personal gain? It’s that simple…right? Yes…it is that simple… But so are each and every one of you who thinks you can be invincible with words alone. Alexander J Maxis and Ryoko Godai climbed into the back seat of their black Mercedes. Mano and Utsure, looked back at them, their gaze asking the silent question the Maxis couple already knew the answer to. “Jahana Castle.” Words are fun, no doubt about that. I’ve used them on many occasions…to foresee things of great importance…never to hurt or cause harm though. It’s just not much of a challenge to do so. But I can see how the rich and powerful get their kicks on outthinking the less intelligent man. It’s like a superior being playing with inferior beings… Like a G O D playing with T O Y S. . . get the picture? Xavier Kannon thrives on his superiority. He must be the top of the game, or his life has no meaning. It’s shallow, but it’s filled with purpose in his eyes. Because you see…Kannon has a sickness that thrives within him. No…it wasn’t contracted through Eleanor, though that could very well be another topic. See, Xavier Kannon has come down with a chronic case of Egoism. The world must revolve around him…everything must go his way…everyone must follow his word. In other words, he wants to be a god. Imitation…is often said…the best flattery. Akiyama didn’t care that much about what his parents were saying to him. His mind was focused on their movements. He knew what they were capable of…he’d had seen them in action many times during his childhood. But now they were challenging him despite his superior god status… Senility was ever so grand…but old men like Duke and Kano would never remember just how grand it was. Let’s get one thing straight first and foremost…Kannon was indeed a god in his own retrospect. His realm…Hollywood and Vine… His subjects…the superstars, musicians, actors, actresses, directors…Tom Cruise, Kristie Alley, John Travolta… His rule…enforced by the will of a parent who doesn’t exist. Old…Mother…Hubbard… But now that he had control of PCW, he wants to make his PCW rule just as concrete as his scientologistic rule. That’s where we have a problem. You see…Kannon will try to control you with one of two things. Money… Taxes…wages…fines…anything he can come up with to deduct funds from a person. Money makes the world go round. And that is how Tim Walden has come about becoming such an influential person upon return. Kannon may have that power over most people in PCW…because most people are foolish to hold this position in PCW as a job alone. Being in PCW is about more than getting a paycheck for beating on people…no matter how much any street thug or ex-con wanted to admit that. Being in PCW…was also about bringing the pain. The god slid into a position, preparing for attack from the front. His parents stood nonchalantly as they always did, but that was nothing to keep your guard down. They could snap at you while sitting in a chair if you proved to be a threat. Yoshihiro had his arms folded…and his wife Yukina’s arms were behind her back. Akiyama knew though…that an attack can come from all sides…even the side of the man you were supposed to be in alliance with. The second way Kannon tries to control you is through trust. Yeah…like any of us can actually trust Xavier Kannon…. prince of lies. Satan stole the name from him, and got taxed for it later on. Kannon’s entire career has been based on his trust…and how he can steal others’ trust and twist it around for his own will. Because once you have someone believing that they’re on your side…nothing hits them harder than being hit from behind. But unfortunately for Xavier…he didn’t hit the god hard enough. Akiyama puts his trust only in family. Cyclonica, Anarchy, End (who was practically family anyway), Ayumi, his parents… them and them only. Because blood is thicker than water…and nothing comes between family. “This is the day, boy…the day you see if you’re good enough to topple your old man…or your mother.” Kannon was most certainly…NOT family…so the bonds of trust were similar to Kannon’s body… Frail…weak… Of course that’s both before…AND after the Neo Void God is done with him. Kannon wanted to be a god, remember? What better way to achieve this than by leaving the Neo Void God beaten in his the game of deception? Akiyama was everything…that Kannon hoped he could be. They exploded from their stance…way quicker than most of the spectators’ eyes could catch. One point they were standing there feet away from the god, and the next they were inches before making contact. Fearsome… Akiyama’s eyes were as busy…just as busy as if he were playing a game of poker with Kannon and there were a few cards “missing” from the deck. Everything before him was noted, and taken care of. The blazing punches were blocked by his palms alone, each of his hand assigned to an oncoming pair…the lightning quick feet aimed at his sides, buffered by his massive arms flexing and shoving them away. Kannon wanted to be strong… Kannon wanted to be powerful… Kannon wanted to be feared by his mere presence…and not just by words alone… Kannon…could never have any of those things… Just like he could never have a victory over Akiyama. Akiyama was a c a n c e r in the body of supremacy that Kannon controlled…each day that it existed… It ate away at his authority… Decayed at his dominance… Rotted at his rule… A Neo Void Tumor…no method of removal was effective on it. And until Akiyama just decided to stop killing Kannon slowly in his ego and physical torture…Kannon would forever remain the god of washed up movie stars and whiny crooners. Akiyama let out a growl as he snatched both of his parents up into the air by their throats, their feet dangling as he crushed hard onto their necks. He was showing them right here and now how things were going to be…and that’s just the way it will be. A D A You hear those initials… you go into hiding. No matter what… Even if it’s the American Dental Association…you better defend your incisors… Akiyama’s knocking them clear out of your mouth with his Hand of Fate. ADA was commonly known to be the Absolute Destiny Apocalypse…the ill fated maneuver that brought about the apocalypse of many a men’s chances for victory. But when it dealt with Kannon…it had a special meaning just for him. AUTHORITY DESTROYING ANIMAL The Akiyama couple knew that a few mores minutes of this wouldn’t toll well on their bodies…hard kicks to the head of the god showed their urgency to live. When they both landed a double thrust side kick into the god’s windpipe, he released them, his own hands rubbing the pain away from his thick throat. Yoshi and Yuki landed on their feet and quickly reacted, the both of them dropping down to one knee and snapping sweeping kicks into the back of Akiyama’s knees. The god dropped down on both knees, and watched as his parents came back around with swinging roundhouse kicks aimed at his face. Kannon knew of the huge threat that he possessed in his plan for dominance. Rent A Hero could be beaten. Darkstar could be fired. Clyde could be…dealt with…in the most efficient manner possible. But there was nothing that Kannon could pull on Akiyama. His sleeves were only so big, and he’s already pulled out all his aces dealing with lesser men…Jeremy Howard, Tyler Nelson, Asylum…all the men he’s swerved or backstabbed or pulled an angle upon. Kannon couldn’t fight Akiyama the same way he could the others…based on the fact alone that Akiyama was like no one Kannon would ever come across in his weak rich boy life ever again. You cannot beat him… You cannot buy him… You cannot threaten him by cutting off his pay… Firing or suspending him wont keep the beating from coming…most likely it will both intensify and hasten your destruction. There is NOTHING…that Kannon can even dream of…that can hope to defeat Akiyama. He’s better off pushing his Snapple while he has the chance…before his neck goes “Snapple” after he gets driven with the intense ADA Akiyama has in store for him. Two huge palms shot out, knocking both Mr and Mrs Akiyama back a few feet. Akiyama hopped back to his feet, anger in his eyes as his parents came running at him again. Their oncoming kicks had arcs to them, which was more than enough for Akiyama to duck underneath. As they turned back around, both of them were gored into the ground by the Celestial, Akiyama jumping back to his feet and glaring as they both rolled around and picked themselves up. Yoshihiro’s arm was draped across his midsection, taking most of the brute force of the Celestial Spear. Yukina winched a bit as she stood up straight, her body taking most of the impact into the ground. They both kept their eyes on their son, who stood with his fists before his face and a will in his eyes… No one was going to stop him… Assemble your alliances, Kannon… Front Office is nothing more than a glorified group of self-righteous money hungry punks in the spotlight. Numbers wont matter much at all. No matter how much you offer, the amount of money you use to hire your thugs wont cover their funerals. In the end, it would always be just you…and the God… Kick. Crunch. Hallmark… Funny how you don’t label where that kick is going… your best bet will be aiming for the Neo Void Nuts…best chance you have of even setting him up for your weak hammer and nail move. Lift. Awe. Absolute Destiny Apocalypse. You only wish there was an awe period in between surprise and unconsciousness. Yoshi was the first, having his head clutched. His body flew up into the air, and his kicks couldn’t keep him from crashing headfirst into the hard ground under him. Akiyama shook his arm off from the sting of his father’s kick as he heard oncoming feet from behind. They stopped…and Akiyama spun around just in time to see his mother rotating in the air, facing him with a kick aimed at his face. A step to the side was all it took to catch her on his shoulder, the package signed, sealed, and ready for the Absolute Destiny Apocalypse. He grabbed her waist and heaved her headfirst off his right shoulder, a cry escaping her mouth as she crashed back into the Koi pond in the center of the courtyard. She…was done. Yoshihiro stood back up holding his head, but had no time to shake the pain as Akiyama clamped his hand around his neck. He swung him overhead with Uranage, but stepped back and caught him face up on his shoulders. He too…met his Absolute Destiny Apocalypse. When it comes down to a real battle…one with everything on the line…there is no time to showboat for the people in their seats. Yeah, they paid for a show…but they were getting a slaughter. Alliances was a big deal for a lot of people… But not for Akiyama… Everyone wanted those title shots, but for the nVg, it was nothing more inflicting pain into a huge load of men who thought themselves foolishly to be better than the god. Their arrogance would be their downfall… Good luck to Kannon’s team…it really inflicts team spirit when you have to be constantly cautious of if your leader will betray you or not. Akiyama didn’t have to worry about that, because past Boca…the Celestial team was a family affair. And family didn’t go behind each other’s backs…because family knew where each other sleep at night. The god stood there silently…staring at the two stunned bodies floating face up in the Koi pond. Above in the alcove, Cyclonica, Anarchy, Boca, Jenna, and Glacia watched on, most of them surprised at the outcome…except one that is. And at a higher altitude…Ryoko Godai and Alexander J Maxis watched through high powered binoculars. “A specimen…he is.” “Can you do something with him, Ryoko?” “Dear husband…don’t you know I can do anything with the right pair of genes?” Kannon believes he played the god… Only at a game he was playing by himself… Kannon believes he suckered the god… Suckered him? More like Kannon further aggravated the god to make his Alliances beating just more violent. Kannon believes he betrayed the god… I think trust must be instilled before you can betray someone. Kannon believes he eliminated the god… One match…and the god punished you for your sin immediately. What makes you think you can get away with anything else… …What makes you think you can beat him? How do I know these things? I was watching everything…from the moment Kannon and Akiyama destroyed Rent a Hero and Darkstar…right up to today’s events… As my name indicates…I have a bit of omni within me…here and there… Right from my perch overlooking the world…watching the deceit that men think they can get away with…when there is always someone waiting right there to make them pay. Xavier Kannon…it’s now time for YOU…to pay full price. ---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===---===--- Akiyama stood in front of his parents in the courtyard, his cousin Ayumi by their sides asking them repeatedly if they were okay from the fight. Yukina shooed her away as she looked on at her son. “Surprise, surprise…” she began, a little spite in her proud voice. “Looks like our son finally leaned something we didn’t teach him.” Yoshihiro shook his head, his eyes straining through the pain in his neck. “Your Karate still needs work, boy. I advise you come see us after your big match for some refresher courses.” “Or you could always go next door and spar with that Inphino boy.” The god could get in your face within a blink of an eye… “Just kidding!” Yuki said with a smile, the first one sincere one since she arrived. Cyclonica sighed as Akiyama backed out of her face and folded his arms. “So now what, granny? You two heading back to the home now?” Cyclonica jeered from her father’s side. Yuki shot her cold glare as Yoshihiro shook his head. “You still need some spiritual training, young lady. If you think you can become half the woman your grandmother is, then you have a long way to go.” Cyclonica scoffed, waving away at them. “Screw that. I’m just happy being me…C…Y…C.” Anarchy and Glacia both sighed, their heads shaking at Cyclonica. The Divine Prophetess glared back at them with icy eyes. “You two got a problem?” Yoshihiro chuckled a bit. “That’s some wife you have there, boy.” Glacia showed the first sign of emotion EVER…an insulted look washing over her face. “No, no, no, no, no……we’re NOT married!” Yukina heaved out a huge sigh, her shoulders slumping. “Great” she muttered, glancing at her husband. “My grandchildren are motherless bastards.” “Hey!” Anarchy interjected at that comment. “We have a father!” Yukina shook her head at the injured girl. “I said MOTHERLESS BASTARDS…not your standard run of the mill bastards.” Much like half the men in PCW though…lord knows that Kannon’s dad wouldn’t stick around long after his son started talking, the nonsense flowing out of his mouth more than unbearable. Weakness would run in the family in that case. Yoshihiro gave a shake of disapproval as well. “If she’s not your wife, then are you seeing anyone?” Great, they were in parent mode again. “What about that nice Kojima girl you used to hang out with? What was her name?” Ayumi knew this one. “SONATA!” “That’s right!” Yukina agreed. “Whatever happened to her?” Ayu tapped Yuki on her shoulder. “Um…she’s right here!” That brought everyone’s attention towards the main walkway that lead towards the front gates. Standing there, dressed in her yellow stretch jeans, yellow jacket, black tank top, and yellow heels was the girl they spoke of…the black and blonde haired Sonata Kojima. By her side, was the also well missing master of machines…dressed in all black, his short spiked hair reaching for the stars. He slipped off his black sunglasses and nodded at them all. “Hey, “ he said…as if it was just normal to appear a month later after infiltrating Japan. Sonata smiled at them all as she bowed, fatigue thicker on her face than Ellie’s makeup. “It’s good to see you all again.” -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-TO BE CONTINUED…?-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-
