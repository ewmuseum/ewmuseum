Lickin' The C.L.I.T. Rent-A-Hero Style

Date: 4-19-2002 ..::/::..Days have past....things have changed....things are anew in the world of the PTC's resident superhero. Coming off of PTC Intervention....Rent-A-Hero suffered a hard fought loss to PCW's Jay Sylo, the new PTC Extreme Icon via a devestating moonsault by the aforementioned opponent while our hero haplessly was laid upon a car. But that loss would be made up for in dividends for the events of later that day...for Rent-A-Hero would finally give into his primal want...a deep-rooted desire he never knew he had...he gave into the beautiful Amy 'Aimz' Campbell and in his 'Rent-A-Car' was the site of which he lost his sexual innocence to the popular OSW vixen. With so much changing in that span, you'd expect a lot more excitement....but that is not the case as we find ourselves in the apartment of Rent-A-Hero. Usually you'd expect me to say something to the affect that he's 'sleeping' or up to his usual 'lolly-gagging'....but this is not the case. The case I DO speak of, is our hero laying on his black leather couch with the addition of Aimz with him, his strong arms around her tight waist as she snuggles into his chest, his chin resting on her head as she lays with him in a position we usually never see Aimz within....defenseless. Both look to be insanely happy in each other's company, Aimz needing attention, love, and protection, and Rent-A-Hero supplying it in hordes to his red-headed angel. What plays in front of them on Rent-A-Hero's wide screen Sony TV seems to be of no importance as they lay in each other's comfort, Aimz now moving her hands to meet Rent-A-Hero's clasped hands which rest at her waist and she rests them upon his, getting more comfortable as she says..::/::..Amy:'Are you happy Rentie?' Rent-A-Hero:'Indubitably crimson-haired vixen of my heart. I never knew you could have so much fun just laying limply on a couch with a person of the opposite gender....this is WIZZER!! This dosen't even require batteries. By the way Amy, what seems to be bothering you. You've had a burden on your mind the size of Rosie O' Donald's undergarments my sweet.'..::/::..Aimz rolls her eyes at the thought of her problem as she now partakes a slightly annoyed look on her face, burying the back of her head in the chest of Rent-A-Hero's Spiderman 2099 costume as she begins to explain her problem as calmly as possible...which means a profanity laced monologue by Rent-A-Hero's Lois Lane..::/::.. Aimz:'Well basically it's this group of church chicks who have cobwebs all over their ancient pu**ies.. getting...' Rent-A-Hero: *GASP* 'Amy, I'm appaled by you...EVERYBODY can't have a KITTY CAT of their own! They're very nice pets, and it's not fun to make fun of people just because they want them and can't have them.'Aimz:'Rentie...your so cute when your clueless as to what sh** means...anyways it's this group of church chicks who seem to have a problem with the 'Cool Book' I published and they've been harassing me ever since. That explains the bible incident the last time I came over...my forehead's still stinging from that one....dumb bi**hes...'Rent-A-Hero:'I'm quite sorry Amy, but maybe everybody needs to just sit down and talk about this whole confusing mess over a glass of tall milk and some cookies...that's a problem solver right there!' *smiles innocently*Aimz:'Once again...your cute when your clueless. All I know is, this mess has got to stop or they're gonna drive me crazy Rentie.' Rent-A-Hero:'Don't you even worry about...'!!!!CRASH!!!! !!!SMASH!!! !!!!TINKLE TINKLE!!!!..::/::..That sound alarms our hero, who immediately gets up from the couch, Amy sitting on the edge of the couch curious about what just happened as Rent-A-Hero approaches the window, raising the blinds as the rays of the sun shower the apartment now, the shadows backed away by the light as our hero looks down below....he then turns back around...a shocked look on his face and looking as if he has no clue what to say as he looks to Aimz. She sees the expression on our hero's face, elicting a response..::/::.. Aimz:'WHAT THE HE** JUST HAPPENED?' Rent-A-Hero:'Ummm well....you know that Human Torch hot Mistubishi Eclipse you drive in my love?'..::/::..Aimz nods, her arms folded as she expects an answer from our hero, obviously lost for the correct thing to say to his love..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Well...ummm....that church group your mad about made a few alterations to it...' Aimz:'WHAT THE FU** DO YOU MEAN ALTERATIONS?!'Rent-A-Hero: *Gulp* 'Ummm well...first off....it's now a low-rider....second off....it's now a covertible....third off....you got some cool new racing stripes...' *Gulps once again*..::/::..Aimz dashes over to the window, bogarding Rent-A-Hero out of her way as she looks down at her Mitsubishi Eclipse to find her lovely car completely vandilised by the Christian Ladies International Tribunal, or C.L.I.T. for short..::/::...*insert your own little snicker here about aforementioned acronym,don't wortry I'll wait...*..::/::..Her tires have been slashed viciously, no signs of air in any of them as her car is slumped on the ground. Her roof is non-existant, ripped from the car as jagged remains of what held it are the only tell tale sign that their was one. And her candy black paint job has been ruined by excessive keying of her car, leaving raw strips of the car's metallic inards upon it, the scratches adding insult to the destruction of her car. Aimz turns back around, her fists clenched and her face construed into a ball of anger as Rent-A-Hero takes a step back, Aimz taking in a deep breath..::/::..Aimz:'Must...kill....churchie.....f**king....bio**hes....must....destroy...strangle...them...mercilessly....' Rent-A-Hero:'If it's any consolation, they could have always put sugar in your tank my sweet...'!!!!POP!!! ..::/::..The gas tank of Aimz's already wrecked car bursts open, sugar flowing out from within it as Rent-A-Hero just shakes his head in shame for even mentioning it, Aimz letting out a primal scream as she runs into our hero's bedroom, slams the door, and continues the screaming that could make a banshee cover her ears and say 'Shut the f**k up.'..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero:'HEY NO CURSING ON MY WATCH MR.NARRATOR MAN!!'..::/::..Sorry Rent...just thought it'd add effect..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Little kids might be reading these RP's!! Jeebus, have some shame man. Now lead me into a heroic monologue, STAT!'..::/::..Alrighty. Our hero stands in the middle of his apartment, completely shocked by the radical actions of C.L.I.T. and the toll it's taken on Aimz as he strikes a pose, his hands firmly upon his waist as he looks out to the sky, his eyes squinting in a serious manner as he says aloud, hand outstretched in the air..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'HOLY PEP BOYS, Cher's plastic surgeon couldn't even get that vehicle looking good again. But the actions of this church group greatly upset Rent-A-Hero....and they shall END!! Speaking of End's...that relates back to my opponent on Fusion...the in..'Aimz:*From other room* 'GOD F**KING DA** THOSE BIT**ES!!!'Rent-A-Hero: *to Aimz* 'HOLD IT DOWN IN THERE AIMZ...CAN'T YOU SEE I'M IN THE MIDDLE OF A HEROIC MONOLUGE...JEEBUS WOMAN!!!' *Ahem* 'As I was saying...the infamous End. He seems to be next in line to try to take none other then my very own...' *reaches on the yin-yang coffee table and upsurps the PCW Crusierweight title into his hands* '...PCW Crusierweight Championship. I don't know if he realizes this, but NOBODY is gonna rip this title from my grasp for QUITE a long time and I'm sure even he is not blind enough to realize that. He will a be a formidable foe to face this upcoming Fusion, but he is just a roadblock of all things and I will KAMEHAMEHA BOMB my way through him so I can reach my true goal...battling Juan Borro and FINALLY beating him like my destiny calls upon me to do. The End will be a great challenge, he is no paper wrestler and I'm sure the match we will put on will be a memorable one...but the FINAL memory will be of me with my hand raised high in the air, title in hand, and him laid out on the mat after I defied his gravity! I just ask him to bring all his Energon and give it all he's got...cause when I beat him, he will have no excuse as to why he lost...he can only simply say he lost to the better man of the match and he must step aside so I can bring down Juan Borro once and for all...my most bitter rival. It won't be THE END for everyone's favorite son of Krypton, and that other guy with the 'S' on his chest too, when I beat THE END...but it will be THE END of his thoughts of EVER gaining my title. But I have no more time to ponder my battle with him....I must race to make end of this evil church group before they push my Amy off the cliff of sanity...although sometimes you gotta wonder if she hasn't been thrown off of it at times already. NO MORE TIME FOR GAMES...IT'S TIME TO LICK THAT C.L.I.T.!!' ........'TO THE RENT-CA....*sigh*....My room. WHEN AM I GONNA GET A RENT-CAVE?!' ..::/::..Our hero spins about in place and dashes over to his bedroom door, opening it up and entering the room where Aimz is also presiding in at the moment, closing the door behind him before a hair-raising scream overtakes the apartment, our hero then yelling out..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'I COULD UNDERSTAND YOU ANGER....BUT DID YOU HAVE TO BUST MY YODA CLOCK!! YODA....I HARDLY KNEW YEE!!!' *Crying ensues*Later On.... ..::/::..A while alter after that incident, we now find our hero racing through the streets of the city in the infamous Rent-A-Car, a 68 Chevy Sprint in the poorest of condition and with decals on each side reading 'Rent-A-Car', sputtering smoke from the tail pipe as he rips through the streets at a BLISTERING 45 miles per hour..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'MY RENT-A-SENSES DETECT SARCASM MR.NARRATOR MAN!!..::/::..Hey, I couldn't resist. Anyways, he controls the wheel with one hand while holding a scrap of paper in the other, with directions to none other then the main C.L.I.T. mission in the area. He tosses the paper aside and looks about, passing numerous restaurants and other establishments before finally seeing a giant sign reading 'C.L.I.T. Headquarters'. Our hero immediately puts on the signal for his car...or in Rent-A-Hero's case..::/::.. Rent-A-Hero: *Sticks his head out the window and yells at the car behind him* 'GOOD SAMARITAN DRIVER BEHIND ME, I'M TURNING RIGHT!!' *Driver behind him flips him a bird* 'YES....I KNOW I'M NUMBER ONE...THANK YOU!!!'..::/::..He waves before easing into the right lane and making a turn into the parking lot of the headquarters of the Christian Ladies International Tribunal. He brings the car to a halt within the parking lot and smacks the dashboard, the car shutting off immediately and proving it's overall bootlegness. He then removes the keys and exits the car, probing the area once out of it before walking towards the grand church building, with a giant a cross built on the very top. His power walk continues, shortly leading him to the large milk white wooden doors of the building as he opens it up and walks within. He is shortly met by lavish paintings and depictions of Jesus and other holy scenes of the Bible, walking along a cushy red carpet as he slowly strolls down the hallway. After aimlessly walking a few feet, our hero is met by a attractive young lady in a long black dress and with a Bible in hand. She smiles at our hero and fixes her short honey blonde hair as she looks to him and asks..::/::..Woman:'Hello sir, what brings you to our headquarters this blessed day.'Rent-A-Hero:'Well it's high time I told ya lady...your group has been harassing my Lois Lane as of late and it's starting to really....forgive me Lord....PEEV ME OFF!! Now she's already hadone of those Bible type thingees tossed at her head, you harass her through the mail of e's, and you severely crippled her car. Now I DEMAND answers from you missies IMMEDIATELY or I'm gonna get reeeeally angry and you don't want that, cause then I'm gonna have to thrash you within this holy place.'Woman:'Sir....this is news to me. We at C.L.I.T. pride ourselves on being peaceful and holy women...these acts of vandalism MUST be some kind of mistake.' Rent-A-Hero:'Beating around the bush are we? Maybe THIS will make you loosen your tongue!!' ..::/::..Our hero drops down to her ankles and begins slapping away at her open-toed high heeled shoes, slapping in a dog paddle type motion as the lovely blonde C.L.I.T. member just stands there looking incredibly confused as he continues the so called 'thrashing'. FINALLY, Rent-A-Hero grows tired, sweat beading down the openings of his mask as he gasps for air. He slowly rises back up to his feet, still breathing hard before saying..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'Now...have THOSE tactics refreshened your memory?'Woman: *In awe of his stupidity* 'God...must have made you VERY special...my child. Seriously, what you speak of makes no real sense to me. Maybe because I'm new here, my name is Clarise Honeypanties.' *holds out hands*Rent-A-Hero:'Honeypanties...an interesting name indeed. Are you by chance in relation to the 'Biotchee's'?'Clarise:'You are too good sir, they my sister married into their family! How do you know them?'Rent-A-Hero:'I used to play at Dumb Biotchee's arcade all the time when I was just a tiny crimefighter, protecting the sandbox. I'm Rent-A-Hero ma'am, champion of the innocent and Virtua Fighter Grand Master in 25 states and 2 galaxies.' *shakes her hand* Clarise:'Quite nice to meet you Rent-A-Hero. Now about all this business with your significant other...it's quite strange. C.L.I.T. just isn't that kind of...'..::/::..Suddenly, Clarise is cut off by an intercom that overtakes the hallway her and Rent-A-Hero are chatting in, a almost seductive voice ordering over the intercom..::/::..Voice:'Clarise...please bring Mr.Hero to my office my child.' Clarise:'Yes ma'am, I will lead him to it.' ..::/::..The intercom abruptly cuts off, our hero in disarray as to what's going on as Clarise looks to him, clutching her Bible in hand and pointing down the hallway as she says..::/::..Clarise:'It seems the mistress wants to see you Mr.Hero...this way if you could please.'..::/::..Clarise begins walking down the hall, her hips having a beautiful sway to them as the tight black dress adds so much for her Coke bottle figure. Rent-A-Hero takes a look at her ample backside, looks to the camera and says..::/::..Rent-A-Hero:'HOLY GLUTEUS MAXIMUS CITIZENS!! She's got more booty then a pirate's treasure chest!'..::/::..Our hero begins to follow the lovely Clarise down the hall, going further and further as the scene starts to fade, our hero about to have a chat with this mysterious woman on the intercom and hoping to solve the problems C.L.I.T. psoes for Aimz on a daily basis.
