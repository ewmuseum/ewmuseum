Family (A Heartwarming Story)

19 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/19/02 The camera fades in… The camera beholds an exquisitely decorated mosque full of prostrating and praying Muslims. The intricate geometric shapes tantalize the eyes as the camera scales up towards the rotund ceiling and descends to the enormous carpeted floor. The chants echo throughout the mosque and flutter up into the minarets above like beautiful nightingales. (From the Holy Koran) In the name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful! All praise belongs to Allah, the Lord of all the worlds! The Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful; Sovereign of the Day of Judgment! You alone do we worship, and from you we seek help! Show us the straight path, the path of those who fear you and on whom you hast bestowed your blessings! Not of those committing wrongs deliberately on whom you are angry, nor of those who having wrong opinions go astray! Amen!!! Allah is Greatest! Allah is Greatest! I worship no God but Allah! I worship no God but Allah! I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah! I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah! Come to prayer! Come to prayer! Come to Success! Come to Success! Allah is Greatest! Allah is Greatest. There is no God but Allah! The mosque is now silent, its members begin to stand back up and walk away from the prayer center. They slowly leave the mosque and go back to their lives in corporate America. They walk down the street, hearing the same taunts, shouts, and curses they do each and everyday after worship. “Terrorist bastard!” “F*cking Muslim!” “Coward!” “Killer!” With each taunt, each curse, the men become stronger. They are unified by a strong faith that can never be broken, never be silenced, and never be taken away. “It’s because of f*ckfaces like you that those mother f*ckers get called those c*ck sucking name all d*ck @$$ day, anus breath bastard!” The camera peers back into the mosque, where we see Bertha and the Chef looking through a window. The Chef: That is a bunch of rubbish Bertha. It is REAL Militant Muslims like me that have kept the faith alive for Lukewarm Muslims like them! Bertha sighs, shaking her head in disbelief. Bertha: I f*cking don’t like America, but that’s because it was f*cking programmed into me. I really want to f*cking know the b*tch @$$ reason you hate this mother f*cking @$$ fairy country so much, @$$ butt d*ck head… The Chef: Where should I start? The values of America, the culture, the gluttony, the greed, the anger, the frenzied behavior, the corrupt government, the imbalance of power, the horrible distribution of wealth and income, the…CHRISTIANIY (The Chef struggles to say the word, almost as if it physical hurt him to mention it), I hate everything about this country! Bertha: So much f*cking anger I sense… The Chef: Yeah, well it’s that same anger that transforms itself into sexual energy that gives one wild night of passion after another! Bertha: I’ve f*cking had better… The Chef: You’ve had better!?!? You’re a clone! Bertha: No, I’m f*cking talking about this sandwich! Bertha holds up a meatball sub. Bertha: F*cking c*ck sucking Fazolis! She throws the sub onto the carpet. The Chef: Bertha… Bertha: What f*ckdaddy? The Chef: I…I…I… Bertha: Say it f*cko! The Chef: I have a surprise for you! The Chef runs over to his prayer post and grabs a Nike gym bag, he runs over to Bertha and holds it in front of her. The Chef: Guess what I have in here? Bertha: F*cking Manuel Hung? The Chef: No… I don’t know where you got that name from… Bertha: But that’s your f*cking adopted son! The Chef: Oh yeah, maybe I should call him, or at least pay those bills I get every month. Bertha: F*cking Child Support? The Chef: Yeah, those. Bertha is fuming. Bertha: You could be f*cking sent to jail for that f*ckface! The Chef holds the bag up to Bertha and smiles. Bertha, a sucker for surprises, slowly turns her face from an expression of anger to an expression of ambiance. Bertha: Ok… what’s f*cking in the bag? The Chef unzips the gym bag, out pops Elian Gonzalez! Bertha: Oh, f*cking Elian! Elian: Hola madre! Bertha embraces the youngster, practically squeezing the life out of him. The Chef: This used to be Manuel’s gym bag, but now it’s Elian’s! Bertha: Did you adopt the mother f*cking b*tch @$$ queer? The Chef: (Rolling his eyes) Nooooo, but I released him from his debt servitude at our restaurant. He should prove to be a valuable ally in our fight against America! Elian: Madre, Besame! Besame mucho! Bertha: I am not a fat f*cker you piece of cow sh*t! The Chef: No! He’s asking for a kiss… Bertha: OHHHHH!!! Well I shouldn’t f*cking deny the queer as f*ck tyke that! Bertha puckers up, and her chap, dry, peeling lips move closer and closer to Elian’s cheek. Just as soon as Bertha’s lip graze the skin of Elian Gonzalez, the little punk KICKS BIG B in the shin! Bertha: F*cking OUCH!!! Elian: Mama es muy gorda! Bertha: Did that f*cker say I was fat!?!? The Chef: Nooooo, he said you…your name was “Gordy”! Bertha: Awwww, ain’t that f*cking sweet, come here you little raga-f*cking-muffin! Bertha goes to hug the Cuban boy but he dashes away from her. Bertha runs after the boy, calling out to him. Bertha: Come back you little f*cker! The Chef chuckles watching the clone chase Elian Gonzalez out of the mosque and into the street. The Chef: This is my family…I couldn’t be happi… The Chef is interrupted by a familiar voice. “YOWZA!!!” The Chef: Yakov? The Chef turns around and sees Russian comedian extraordinaire Yakov Jakov with his two kids, Sherry Jubilee and Manuel Hung. Manuel: I dis many years old! He holds up three fingers. The Chef: That’s great… What do you want Yakov? Yakov: I’ve been trying to track you down Chef! It’s hard to find people when there’s no Secret Police, HEY NOW!!! The Chef: Haven’t had a lot of time to work on your routine? Yakov: You could say that, YOWZA!!! The Chef: Was that a joke or something? Yakov: Not really, I herniated a disc a couple weeks ago and it hurts like a Siberian Huskie bite to the crotch every time I aggravate it, WHOAH!!! HEY!!! YOWZA!!! The Chef: Well Yakov, why have you take it upon yourself to find me out and make me relive my oh so horrible past. Yakov: How could you say it was horrible Chef!?!? You had two wonderful children, a happy marriage, and best of all everyone loved you! The Chef: Well Yakov, I’ve discovered my true self, and Allah has shown me the light. I am now at peace. Yakov: Well when you went of to be cook in the army you signed your parental guardian rights over to me, and these kids have become my life. When you came back to the United States and didn’t try to get in touch and correspond with me I got really worried. Don’t you want to see your kids? Don’t you want to hold them, love them, and be their father!?!? HI-OH!!! The Chef pauses for a moment. The Chef: ……….. Yakov: Well? The Chef: ………. Yakov: You’ve got two wonderful kids Chef. Manuel is still kicking people in the shin and telling his age. Sherry has learned her first word, “Cook”, and we visit Bertha’s grave every weekend and bring her flowers. When have you visited Bertha?!?! When have you visited your past, and taken responsibility for what you did!?!? The Chef: ENOUGH!!! I’m done with my past; I don’t care to see it! The Chef pulls his wallet out of his back pocket, grabs a few Benjamins in his wallet, and tosses them at Yakov. The Chef: Here, take a few hundreds out of Osama Bin Laden’s treasury. You can expect the same amount coming every month. NOW LEAVE, GET OUT OF MY SIGHT! I DON’T WANT TO SEE THEM ANYMORE!!! The Chef turns away from the three. Sherry begins to cry, Manuel hugs her and slowly begins to frown and whimper. Yakov picks up the money and tosses it back at the Chef. Yakov: Is that what you think parenting is? SUPPLYING MONEY!?!? It’s far, far more Chef… I hope you have fun now with your artificial wife, and your ARTIFICIAL LIFE!!! YOWZA!!! Yakov takes the kids away and heads out of the Mosque. He carries Sherry and holds Manuel’s hand, slowly walking to their White BMW SUV when all of a sudden… “STOP!” Yakov turns around and sees the Chef running towards them. The Chef: I do want to see you! I do want to relive my past! I do want to be a father! The Chef gets down to his knees and offers himself to his children. Manuel cheers with an excited glee and run towards the Chef. Sherry screams “DaDa!” and points towards the Culinary Conquistador. Yakov slowly walks over to the Chef. Manuel runs up to his father and hugs him; the Chef warmly accepts Manuel and hugs back, patting his son on the back. Yakov hands Sherry to the Chef, who holds her in his arms. The Chef: I may be an Islamic Militant, but I’m also a father. Thank you Yakov, thank you for teaching me that family always comes first… The camera fades out as we see Bertha approaching the group and holding Elian. It looks like this new family is about to unite when the camera totally fades to black.
