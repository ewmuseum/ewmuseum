-Crimson River-

16 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09.16.02 ~*~ Fade in... The scene opens outside of a dark violet house, with turrets at the bedroom windows, and columns holding up the porch of the house. It does not quite possess the usual picturesque look that the neighboring houses have. It lies in a descent neighborhood, with cookie-cutter houses, yet this house doesn’t exactly fit in. Around the modest residence, a tall black picket fence lines from the front lawn...till it reaches the area in which the driveway is, until it forms a rectangular shape, connecting around the small backyard. A statue of a gargoyle; carved out in ebony granite, is perched at the side of the steps of the house. The grass is an exotic, dark jade color, and—unlike the other lawns in this area—it is not well-manicured. Bushes of mauve and raven colored roses pepper the lawn. Along the winding pathway, a small smear of a dark crimson stains the pebble stones. In the shadows of the dusk, it is concealed quite well, unless one would look closely enough. A midnight blue Ferrari is parked, most likely in a haste, diagonally on the driveway. The headlights are still on, but at least the doors are shut...and apparently locked. The black-painted gate lies slightly ajar. After the creak of the gate opening sounds, we travel further up the pathway. Much like the gate, the door is partially open. The house is entered, and along the welcome mat, a dark red stain is noticed. The carpet on the inside is rumpled up a bit. The sounds of a television can be heard. Cautious footsteps enter the hallway. Still, the trail of darkened crimson is followed, where it is pinpointed to continue up the long, winding staircase. There it is followed up, noting now a disturbing print on the banister of the staircase. ...A bloody handprint... The footsteps hasten their pace, curious and sickened by this display...Curious to find the source of this trail; which further identifies the crimson stains along the pathway, hallway and stairs. The sounds of quickening breathes is heard, and finally, the smudged blood trail is followed until it comes to a stop. So does the footsteps of this person. The view finally travels to a figure curled up in the corner. With hesitation now, the person approaches the figure carefully. They stop two feet from the figure, which is coiled up in a fetal position. This figure—with her long masses of curly hair and a petite, feminine figure—can be identified to be female. Her feet are bare and covered in small cuts. She is dressed in a powder blue cut-off t-shirt, and short faded denim shorts that just reach her lean thighs... Yet, without close examination, those colors cannot be recognized......For they are drenched in blood... Her clothes are soaked in a dark purple, and much of her skin is stained with dried blood. A shocked gasp escapes the female who stands over this bloodied figure. The camera pans out, to reveal it is a young woman with honey blonde tresses. She has a very slight figure, and is quite pale. Her soft curves are covered in a pale pink dress that hangs just above her knees. Her bright baby blues are opened wide in horror, as she eyes this young woman. It is Jenna, the female whom Anarchy has been holding captive for these past few months. Yet she is out of her usual prison of the basement. This could just easily be her escape...whatever she was doing out on her own, in the first place...? She kneels down carefully, besides this other female. Her eyes travel to the tangled mass of curls that hangs from her head. Blood seeps from a small wound on her head. Yet her hair can be clearly labeled as a dark blue. This causes a small whimper to escape those soft lips of Jenna. It confirmed her fears...it was Anarchy. Despite their distorted, violent relationship—with them being best friends in a far away past—she still felt sympathetic for Anarchy, if not empathetic. Sure, Anarchy had tortured her and imprisoned Jenna in her basement...yet, in her eyes, it was justified. It had all been done as a small scale of revenge...for Jenna’s betrayal. She knew, with the sickening amount of blood still seeping from Anarchy’s wrists, that this was no set-up. The girl had obviously driven herself And, if one was not some sick, twisted individual...it was impossible not to feel sorry for Anarchy now. Here she lay, helpless, wounded... And for once, so vunerable... In fact, Jenna could go as far as to think Anarchy was finally at her mercy. Wasn’t she just in Anarchy’s shoes, not too long ago? ...Well, to a small, small degree. Maybe she wasn’t bleeding all over the floor previously, but she was definitely more of the underdog...while Anarchy had more of the upper hand. And now, the blue-haired goddess lay...The victim... Trusted youwith my lifeShattered dreamsbroken glass A small, sad smile crossed Jenna’s lips. “You’re right where I want you, girl...” She shook her head, that almost sadistic smile leaving her lips. What had she been thinking? She bit her lip, and her expression softened. “Oh God... Who did this to you?! I need to get you to a hospital...” I hope there is a closuredown your pathfor I have yet to findthe means To forgive Jenna slowly leans over once more, gently shaking Anarchy, in hopes of her being conscious...or at least in hopes of waking her. Anarchy finally stirs, and murmurs drowsily, apparently still in her unconscious state... “My blood, your Betrayal...” Jenna looks to Anarchy in confusion, a line between her eyes forming. “What? Oh, Jesus...Don’t talk in your sleep like that—you’re scaring me!” Anarchy rolls over with a soft whimper, never directly responding. Finally, she slips her arms under Anarchy’s, gingerly, and attempts to haul her. Anarchy flops on her back, and her eyes flutter wide open. A command escapes her bruised lips with a gasp; and she gripped Jenna with a blood-covered hand, as she looks into Jenna’s eyes painstakingly. “No...Hospital...” Seemingly, that had drained all of the energy out of Anarchy...for her eyes closed, and she released her grip, as she passed out from the loss of blood once more. Jenna winces, knowing not to argue or go against her wishes...even while Anarchy was unconscious...because, with all hopes, she wouldn’t be unconscious forever... The blonde-haired female carefully wipes the blood-matted hair from the face of her blue-haired companion. She notices how pale Anarchy’s normally soft caramel complexion has become... So what if this Was all I had?Is it over?Washing awayand forget to seeGo choose your way Jenna finally manages to drag Anarchy’s unconscious body over to the master bathroom. She pulls her to the foot of the bathtub. Anarchy lies there, shivering. Jenna shuts the window quickly, and starts to run the water of the bathtub. She smiles softly, remembering Anarchy doing that for her a while ago...yet she wasn’t dieing. She winces, noticing the trail of blood that follows them. The source of it could be from her clothes and body...but then Jenna picks up Anarchy’s wrist. A bloody wound has been punctured into it. That seems to be where it’s all coming from. With a soft gasp, she comes to a realization. “Wow, you dragged yourself all the way here? Girl, you’re crazy...that would explain the blood stains all up the driveway and hallway and all...and you drove yourself here? Oh, man...I need to get you bandaged up...” Jenna heads downstairs. She looks at the TV, which is already on and blaring. She rolls her eyes as a certain commercial comes on...Wrestling. She never understood why Anarchy got into the sport. She’s distracted from getting the bandages from the kitchen cabinet when she realizes the federation the commercial is for. PCW...wasn’t that the fed Anarchy was in? The commercial was advertising for some show, ‘Tuesday Turmoil’...on which they eventually announced a Cruiserweight belt match-up. Brandi was booked against Anarchy. After mentioning the questionable help Brandi had been giving the blue-haired new comer in matches, clips from ‘Friday Fusion’ are then shown. Leaving everythingyou walked away from thisdid it make it easier on you?So now what? ...Graphic images of Anarchy hanging from a meat hook in a large refrigerator are shown... “Oh...my...God...” A small cry of dismay escapes Jenna’s lips. A female, another with blue and black tresses, is shown. Oh...so she did this! ...Then, the name. Cyclonica...As this exotic Japanese female wrestler dug her nails into Anarchy’s wrist, puncturing it...Another quick flash of her blood being squeezed out into a container, which elicited a whimper from Jenna...Then, the question of if Anarchy would be in the condition to face Brandi? Then, that was it. The commercial had ended... Meaning...No more clues? Hell—that was all Jenna needed to know. She headed into the kitchen, and grabbed those bandages. She ran up the stairs, heading to the bathroom. There, of course, she found Anarchy lying on the floor. Yet somehow, she had propped herself up on the ledge of the bathtub. She was hunched up, shivering. Finally conscious...her wrists were propped up on the bathtub ledge, her hands dangling into the hot water. Her wrists left small blood stains on the ledge. Anarchy looked up at Jenna finally, with pleading, pained eyes...with chattering teeth, she spoke. “It...Hurts...shit, help...” Life must go on Still hauntedit’s so hardTo face the dayI hope it is good for you Oh, she could leave Anarchy now...after all she had done to her...but would it make her a better person? Would there be a weight lifted from her shoulders? She had done this girl just as wrong...and it would eat a hole in her stomach every day now if she had decided to walk away from a vulnerable Anarchy. That is, if Anarchy didn’t gain her health back, hunt her down, and disembowel her. Without hesitation, Jenna begins stripping her blood-soiled clothes from Anarchy’s body. When finally done, she helps hoist Anarchy into the hot water. Quickly, as Anarchy settles, Jenna washes her petite wrists. Drying them with a towel, staining it with blood, she begins wrapping Anarchy’s dried skin with bandages. Anarchy sinks into the warmth of the bathtub. Her eyebrows furrow, causing a crease on her forehead. She looks very much in pain, and faint with loss of blood. At least her shivering has calmed with the warmness of the hot water. Jenna watches her with concern, as the blue-haired nymph sinks into the bubbles. Jenna takes a sponge, pouring some body wash over it, and carefully scrubs the caked blood off of Anarchy’s arms, legs, and torso. She smirks softly, thinking that if Anarchy was in her right mind, she would good-naturedly curse Jenna out. Like, “Girl, do I look like a nursing home patient to you? Did I say it was time for a ‘sponge bath’? Shit, get away from me, you pervert!!” Yet maybe the girl was just too worn out to bother. Jenna sure couldn’t blame her for that... She feels the indigo-haired chick grabs a pretty strong hold of Jenna’s wrist. The blonde young woman stiffens up, perhaps out of fear. Finally, Anarchy looks up at Jenna with a smile. She cocks a blue eyebrow, and speaks with a weakened voice, yet almost sounds like the Anarchy she’s known for years. “Thanks, girl. But don’t you go too nice on me, you hear?” This elicits a laugh from Jenna, as Anarchy watches her with wide violet eyes, and that smirk of hers... I tried, Oh how I tried, But its brokenLet me go, I could have died. Fade out...~*~
