The Revenge Saga: Red Roses for a Blue Lady (Repost)

Date: 2-??-02 Before SabotageThe living room was barren again tonight. All the lights off, save the Televisions company. Blood, sweat, water, and tears rolled down her face, forming streaks in her make up. Her left hand had the remote in it. Flipping through the channels. Trying to forget. She pushed her hood back and layed her head onto the couch. Day dreaming and gazing off into the ceiling. Her thoughts all melded into one. The TV was no companion. Miss Emily did nothing for her. Nothing at all did anything. Except for what was on her TV tray. She glanced over to it, the bottles of pills. Anti Depressents, uppers, some she found in the medicine cabinet that had nothing on them. It didnt matter. It all made her forget.She flicked through the TV for a few more minutes, basicly going through the rotations. Nothing. Until she reached channel 6, ABC news in the Philadelphia region. She raised a eyebrow at the screen and the caption.The news anchor flickered in, a bit of static after a large burst of lightning.'In other news, a tragic turn of events has happened just outside of Philadelphia in the Southern New Jersey suburbs'A graphic comes up in the top corner, next to the anchors head. It is a crime scene outline and a crashed car.Talena: Oh fuck.'A young couple was dealt a tragic but poetic blow. In the late hours of last night, 21 year old Courtney Prescott was stabbed to death in her home, the police report states that it was a random attack most likely, based on the stab pattern it was perpetrated with a curved knife. The PYPD are looking for leads on the case...However, the tragedy does not end there. Her boyfriend, who left moments before, ran a red light, and was killed after being thrown from his vehicle. The time's of death were almost exactly at the same time. The couple didnt have to be apart for ve-...'Talena growls and stands, raising her right hand at the screen and firing.Now we see what she was holding aside from the remote, a small pistol. The TV shattered, electricity bursting outwards. She stood, the gun in her hand still and dropped the remote.Talena: And they  make them out to be some sort of romantic coincidence??? I FUCKING KILLED THEM BOTH!She sends her foot into the speaker of the TV, breaking that inwards.Talena: YOU DIDNT MAKE GAVYN A MARTYR, YOU JUST LET HIM GO!!!Her stability has finally fallen over, a earthquake rocking her mind like a chair. Things began to snap as she lost it, her small frame seemed to grow to Taxi Driver proportions as she swung the gun around, yelling violently to herself.Talena: EVERYTHING I FEEL HURTS!!! WHY!!!She puts the gun to her head.Talena: WHY! WHY! WHY!She feels her grip tightening on the trigger, her eyes close but suddenly shoot open again, she kicks over a end table, her dress rides up, revealing her knee high platform boots and purple striped stockings, the end table falls, sending the lamp that was on it to the ground, it shatters loudly. She brandishes the weapon again, firing at the wall and splintering a clock on the mantel piece. Signifigantly, she has lost it.She crumples to the ground again, burying her head into her hands, the gun pointing out above and away from her, she rocks back and forth like a little girl throwing a tantrum. She looks up, her eyes filled with more tears then ever before, she knows that this is it.Life or Death...And slowly... she walks towards the right... taking the path that leads to her brains spilt onto the floor.Her hand lifts the pistol...I can see it now... 'In a tragic turn of events, the girlfriend of local hero Gavyn Drazail kills herself'She looks at it for a second.I wonder if it will be poetic when my blood is staining these dark carpetsRaising it and placing it next to her temple...Head or Mouth. Head or Mouth. That sounds like sexual innuendoShe keeps it on her temple, deciding.It will be the quickest... Her eyes close tight as the barrell begins to click.Ding... Dong...She flinches, thinking the noise was the gun, it drops. And she just stares at it. Looking at the piece of metal laying on the floor. The one that could end her life. She never takes her eyes off of it as she stands. Walking to the door. She looks through the peephole and see's noone.Great, I stop my self-murder for a game of doorbell di-...Something catches her eye, the fluttering of a black bird flying away from the doorstep. Curiosity gets to her as she opens the door and looks onto the rain drenched steps.Roses?She looks around, confused, and picks them up, the red roses are wrapped up tightly, a small white note card on the paper. She plucks it off with her trembling fingers, looking around again. She opens it and reads...'But where shall wisdom be found? And where is the place of understanding? Man knoweth not the price thereof; neither is it found in the land of the living... for the price of wisdom is above rubies.'Stay Strong,Love,      GavynOur view lingers, the pale light of the moon gives her face a blue illumination...And she dreams...
