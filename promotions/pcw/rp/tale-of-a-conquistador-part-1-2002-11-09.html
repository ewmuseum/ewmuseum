Tale of a Conquistador (Part 1)

9 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/9/02 A Tale of a Conquistador Decades ago, fierce Spanish explorers roamed the world. They found new places and showed no fear. These men were the best minds of our times and till this day they are looked up upon as kings. There was once a Conquistador who went by the name of Julio De Rojas. Julio was a young man sent on his way in his father’s footsteps. We were young and had so much to learn about life. He took part in the crusade to Valencia. Tales back in the day said how Valencia was ruled with witchcraft and evil spirits. These conquistadors were sent there to rid these evil spirits and secure the land in the name of Spain. Julio was scared and prayed every day for safety. Weeks passed before the mission aroused and Julio kissed his family and life behind. He never really wanted to go, but when he saw the luck on his fathers face with joy, he knew he had to. By horseback the men arrived on the second day. They searched the area but didn’t find anything suspicious. The castles in the city were completely empty. The gothic feel and sudden emptiness worried Julio, but everyone else seemed to be set. They had marked the area under the name of Spain and decided to relax and party. One night when all of the other Conquistadors were having a good time and not having a care in the world, the citizens of Valenica arrived. A large group of barbarians and mystical beings surrounded the conquistadors. Witches on horseback and zoodoo masters with flaming torches made there way toward the men. “Leave us now” They said with a deep and sadistic tone. One brave conquistador stood up in front of them all and screamed out to all of the lands. “This land is property of SPAIN!” “Is that so, SPANIARD, well I don’t think it is.” The conquistador tried to flee the mob, but they just became more rowdy and began throwing objects at him. The conquistador then drew his sword and made it completely clear that he was ready to fight. The other conquistadors just watched and backed off as the mob was a good ten times larger then there fleet. They urged the other conquistador to do the same but he didn’t budge. “Bravery may prove to be your stupidity.” The sorcerer said. “But my stupidity may prove to be your death.” With those words the conquistador charged at the sorcerer slaying his head and then slicing away at the next five men near him. Within seconds the mob surrounded him, but he took out one by one without a second to breath. It was almost unreal how he dealt with it. Everyone looked out in amazement, but right when we thought we had hope it ended. A barbarian came charging from behind and jabbed two razor blades into the neck of the conquistador killing him. The mob rejoiced and screamed in joy, as they believed they had won the battle. Everyone was willing to give up, but Julio didn’t. At the tender age of 16, Julio charged through all, not thinking about his own life one bit. He stabbed the man who killed the conquistador right in the heart. As the mob came after him he screamed: “IN THE NAME OF SPAIN” He then continued by killing three more in a trance of violence. The other conquistadors saw his drive and followed him into battle. They all put their lives on the line for the land they loved. On that night the sixty conquistadors defeated the people of Valencia. Julio had been injured and his last words were: “I died a warrior, and there would have been no better way to go out.” Julio showed that even though he was a young and scared boy all of his life, that in the time where he needed to step up he was able to. That one boy led to a revolution and whether you like it or not he shaped history. That my friends is part 1 in The Tale of the Conquistador. And this is my selection. Twas a young boy so filled with lifeWilling to die by the knifeSo quiet he’d seemTo fill a fathers dreamThe day he rode away with the men His fright got the bestTill he was put to the testThe boy had become a man He fought so hardHe gave it his allThe last thing he had heardWas the Spanish were going to fall He wouldn’t believe That the conquistadors would give inSo he ran out in a blaze of glorySo here a lie, full of sorrowTelling you the conquistador’s story. The morale of it allIs to always stand tallIn the face of hate and injusticeBecause when you succeed and do something right By God you know you’ll love it So let us all pray on this very daythat the conquistador in us will always remain. *The crowd claps as El Romantico gets up from his seat on the stage and bows to everyone in the coffee bar* That night El Romantico went home from the coffee club thinking of the story he told everyone. To him every story he ever told related him to some kind of way. In this story Romantico thought of himself as Julio. When Romantico was first faced with fighting for something he believed in he was very young as well. He put it all on the line and didn’t even think of the consequences. This Tuesday I shall do the same when I give it my all to accomplish stardom. My first step will be a hard one against a former World Champion. It may be my hardest fight till this day. As for Vilas Lavagna, he seems to be a talented fighter, but one who dos not know what he’d getting involved in. Just ask Sir Rollins who has been the first to feel my wrath. I shall continue my fight, my fight in the name of Spain and in Christianity. FOR I AM THE CONQUISTADOR EL ROMANTICO! And I shall NEVER quit.
