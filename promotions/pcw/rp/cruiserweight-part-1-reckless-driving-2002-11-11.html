Cruiserweight?! - Part 1 - Reckless Driving

11 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 11, 2002 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: Cruiserweight?! [The surfer turned professional wrestler looks at the sheet of paper with his bright blue eyes as wide as half dollars. He can't believe what he's seeing. It doesn't make any sense, none at all] TTT: Cruiserweight?! [For a second time the tanned man exclaims the word. The wind blows through his blond hair with much force. The piece of paper flaps in the perfectly manicured hands. Our fathful surfing hero doesn't really take notice that he's in the passenger seat of his blue and yellow 2002 Mustang. Who's driving the expensive, gnarly vehicle? Well, if you know who the Cowabunga Connection is...then you'd know that Twisted Sister loving, Marijuana Smoking, Seashell collecting, 'Heavy Metal' Harry is behind the wheel of the car. His bloodshot eyes are filled with ecstasy as the powerful Mustang whips through the California freeway. His shoulder length brown hair flows behind him, making him look like some sort of strange looking model in those stupid Calvin Klein commercials] TTT: CRUISERWEIGHT?!?!? [Tommy's voice cracks loudly as Harry looks over with a half smile. He quickly realizes that this car needs the right tunes for the high rate of speed that they're going at. Harry pushes the eject button on the CD Player and notices that 'The Beach Boys Greatest Hits' slides out into his hand. He opens the glove compartment to search for the CD case, but cannot find it. He looks all around him to a place to put the CD. He can't find one. He looks over at Tommy who is still in absolute disbelief and Harry tosses the CD into the air, letting the air take Tommy's favorite CD of all time. Harry pulls a CD out from his pocket, almost side swiping a Chevorlet Monte Carlo in the process. It's Twisted Sister's Greatest Hits. Harry smiles devilishly as he slowly places the CD into the player, watching it suck it into the system. In only a few short moments the car's far out speakers will be pumping some of the greatest music that has ever been created. The most bitchin' musicians to ever grace the hard rock scene will take us all on a journey through greatness. The bass drum and raspy snare romp through the car's system as Dee Snider screams in rebellion] Dee Snider: We're Not Gonna Take It!! Oh NO!! We're Not Gonna Take It! We're Not Gonna Take It...ANYMOREEEEE!!! [Harry begins banging his head to the hard rocking guitars that have joined in with the pounding drums. The Mustang swerves from lane to lane of the freeway. Cars honking relentlessly at this maniacal headbanger taking up the entire road. Tommy doesn't notice, he's in too much disbelief to notice that his gnarly automobile is in most bogus danger] TTT: How can Harry be a cruiserweight?! [Tommy's laid back voice comes over the loud music. His eyes examaning the paper for the tenth time. 'Heavy Metal' Harry versus Vilas Lavigna for a first round tournament cruiserweight title match. Tommy knew that the title was bogusly up in the air, but he never would have imagined The Heavy Metal One to be participating in the match up. Harry as a cruiserweight champion?! Lets face it, Harry does have the gnarly skills to pull it off, dudes. It would be a most weird thing to see Harry holding a title that all the little compadres of PCW have held. Maybe the most excellent bookers have lost their minds when they booked Harry into the tournament. That could be it. Well, it'll be their best choice since signing the Cowabunga Connection, because Harry is going to win the tournament. Come on...who's going to stop him? Tim Walden? Gaz 'The Jester' Green? Those dudes are totally heinous!!! They could never hang with Harry's expertise in power moves. He'd kill the compadres] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: RAAAAAAAA!!!!! [Harry holds his fist in the air, the universal signal of rebellion. His brown hair hangs all down his face from the headbanging. Honestly, Harry isn't even paying attention to the road at all. In fact, Harry doesn't even have his license. The only thing Harry is concentrating on is the bad ass guitar solo that is about to be laid down by J. J. French. Here it comes...FAR OUT, MAN!!! That guitar solo is freakin' bitchin', man!] TTT: Harry, this could be your chance, compadre. [Tommy has finally accepted the fact that Harry is going to be in the Cruiserweight tournament. He's no longer trapped in the trance, he now is fully aware of his surroundings. THe surroundings are TOTALLY BOGUS!] TTT: DUDE!!!!!! [Tommy leans over to the driver's side and swerves the wheel to the right, barely avoiding a car that was going much slower than they were. Tommy swerves them into the middle lane, but there is another car there. Tommy swerves to the right again, then to the left, then to the right. The Mustang's great handling is making it possible for The Tubular One to avoid these cars. Two hundred yards ahead there seems to be a most bogus traffic jam. Tommy's blue eyes grow wide with horror as they close in at One hundred yards now. The Mustang swerves to the right, getting onto the shoulder of the freeway. The blue and yellow car is nothing but a totally gnarly blur to the people waiting in the traffic jam. Harry just bangs his head and throws his fist in the air, not even realizing his near death experience. At the beginning of the jam there looks to be a line of cop cars] TTT: Move over, dude!! [Harry doesn't hear his brother, even with Tommy leaned into his ear] TTT: Dude! Get out of the drivers seat, compadre! [The soothing sounds of 'I Wanna Rock' have entered the stereo system. Harry is completely gone from this world. The future cruiserweight champion has the pedal to the metal, his ears tuned to the rockin' guitars of Twisted Sister, and his mind on getting some more far out sea shells when they get home. Harry doesn't care what happens to the car, or what happens to anybody else's car. Seashells, Twisted Sister, and Marijuana is all the plagues his mind. It used to be Hustler, Twisted Sister, and Marijuana. Then that far out meeting changed his life forever. He's no longer one of those stupid, unbitchin' sexual addicts. He's actually never been happier. Kind of far out if you think about it] TTT: HARRY!! Now you better listen to me! We're about to go crashing into a totally bogus line of cop cars. YOU NEED TO MOVE, DUDE!!! [Do you really think Harry is going to hear that? Did it have to do with Seashells? No. Did it have to do with Twisted Sister? No. Did it have to do anything with Mary Jane? No way, man. Harry won't hear that] TTT: I've got an idea. [Tommy's mind moves quickly, like a bunny. He pressed the stop button on the stereo and the heavy sounds of Twisted Sister are gone. Now Harry will listen, because his favorite band of all time has faded away] Harry: Why did you turn off Twisted Sister, man. TTT: GET OUT OF THE DRIVER SEAT, CRUISERWEIGHT!!! [Tommy grabs Harry by his tattered Twisted Sister T-shirt and gets him out of the way. Tommy leaps into the driver seat and slams on the brakes. The Mustang just comes within two inches of one of the cop cars. A police woman begins approaching the out of control Mustang. Her man-like walk shows exactly why she's a police officer and not a totally bodacious model] Police Woman: Do you realize that you could have killed someone going out of control like that? [Tommy looks up at the woman still a bit shaken from the scare of almost dying. Her face is rather round, but she is very pretty. Her brown eyes go well with her tan complexion. Tommy begins having thoughts of being arrested, taken to her bedroom, and handcuffed to her bed. She reads him his most radical rights, then has her way with him. WAIT!! He's a recovering sexual addict!! That can't happen. He cannot have random sex with random babes anymore. It's all over, done with. No more, dude] TTT: I apologize for the bogusness, officer. Nobody got hurt, and we stopped before we clobbered your gnarly police cruiser. [Harry begins recovering from being tossed into the backseat. His bloodshot eyes are no longer filled with joy and happiness, but confusion and anger] Harry: Why did you throw me like that, man? [The police woman looks confused now. What was going on in this car?] TTT: DUde...NOT NOW! [Tommy turns back to the police woman] TTT: Babe, the car just went out of control. It was totally heinous. I really thought that it was the end for yours truly... Harry: And me, man. TTT: ...and Harry. Luckily the brakes kicked back in just at the right moment. It was a most bogus scare. [The police woman stares at Tommy with very hard eyes. It's almost like those gnarly brown eyes are trying to break his mind, make him admit what really was going on in the car] Police Woman: Sir, I think that your friend in the backseat was behind this wheel. I believe that he was driving recklessly across the highway with music blasting. You were too into something else to notice his recklessness, and when you realized what was going on it was almost too late. TTT: Ummm.... Police Woman: That justifies my hypothesis. TTT: Babe, you have to know a few things first. [The police woman looks up, deciding that she'll listen to what he has to say] TTT: We're professional wrestlers for the most bodacious federation in the world, Primetime Championship Wrestling. We are the greatest tag team that has ever graced the squared circle, The Cowabunga Connection. We just recently lost a most bogus 'No Ladder' Match on Friday and I was feeling most bummed about it, so I let Harry drive from the gym. I happened to look at the upcoming matches memo and, to my totally gnarly surprise, I see that Harry is entered in the Cruiserweight Title Tournament. Harry: Really, man?! TTT: Yeah, dude. Harry: FAR OUT, MAN! Police Woman: What does that have to do with the reckless driving? [Tommy lifts up Harry's Twisted Sister T-shirt to reveal Harry's large gut. The pale, hairy dome hands over his leather pants. The police woman sighs and turns her eyes away from the hideous sight] TTT: Does that look like a cruiserweight to you, babe? Police Woman: I guess not. TTT: I was totally shocked by it and didn't realize that Harry COULDN'T DRIVE. Harry: Sorry, man. TTT: I was able to take over at the last minute. [The police woman shrugs and she smiles at Tommy. HE GOT AWAY WITH IT!! THE TRUTH SET HIM FREE. TOTALLY BODACIOUS!] Police Woman: Just be sure to pay your ticket. [Tommy takes the piece of paper and his bright blue eyes fall to sadness] Harry: I'm going to be the cruiserweight champion, man. TTT: 300 dollars?!?! TOTALLY BOGUS!!! Harry: Yeah, man. [Fade to black]
