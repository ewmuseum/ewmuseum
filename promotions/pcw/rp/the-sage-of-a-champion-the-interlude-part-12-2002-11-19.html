The Sage of A Champion: The Interlude Part 12

19 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-19-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!The Interlude Part 12” Chronicles!Chronicles!Chronicles! Chapter 3: The Mission Begins :: Last time we were in the middle of a conversation between BladeD and Commander Fuller. For some reason just out of no where the cameras turned off, so hoping when we pick back up we haven’t missed that much information. Anyway, the scene is back on BladeD and Commander Fuller. :: BladeD: I realize all these guards are going to be around. I am just saying that this place looks so easy to get around. It is almost like they want people infiltrating the place because of all the good places located inside the building for good infiltrating. Commander Fuller: That is nonsense talk BladeD. Besides even if you do see it that way I bet you anything those areas are so heavily guarded that you won’t even be able to get near them without being caught. BladeD: If there’s a will there’s a way! Commander Fuller: You are full of yourself! BladeD: I am just saying… Commander Fuller: You are saying a lot of nonsense that is what you are saying! BladeD: But commander… Commander Fuller: Listen to me BladeD! This place may be easy to dig into on a map, but trust me it isn’t going to be a joy ride. You are going to have to take a lot of time and effort to pull this off. Even if it is as easy as it is inside of there, then somewhere along the lines of getting there you are going to run into an area so heavily guarded it will seem impossible to get past. This mission is no joy ride BladeD and you are going to have to understand that. BladeD: All right Commander! All right! Anyway, that is all I wanted to talk about, so I will go ahead and let you go. I know you are a busy man yourself, so I will call you later if I need something again. Commander Fuller: All right BladeD. Just remember that if there is anything concerning this mission that you need to talk about don’t hesitate to call me because you are going to be leaving in a couple of days. You need to make sure you are 100% prepared when you get out there because even though you are going to have radio contact through the whole mission I am not going to be at the radio the whole time. It will probably take you one or two days just to get inside the building, then there is no telling how long you will spend in that building itself with all the guards it has, so I am not going to be there all the time. We have people already ready to do the shifts that they are going to be doing. BladeD: All right Commander! I will I will! :: BladeD hangs the phone up and gets back to the map he was looking at. :: BladeD: Well. I am almost got this whole place figured out. I just wish I had pictures of insides. :: short pause :: Wait a second! :: BladeD goes into a backpack and digs around a little bit, then he finds some pictures of the place he got when he got the files and folders on the building. :: :: BladeD begins to examine the pictures and in one of the pictures in the far corner on a wall you can just barely see a bloodstain on the wall, but it is hard to tell because the picture cuts off. :: BladeD: I wonder what that bloodstain is from. I don’t see why there would be any fighting going on. The only fighting I could think of that would be going on is internal fighting, but that doesn’t make any sense. Why would they be fighting themselves? That is just stupid. :: BladeD starts looking at other pictures. He seems similar things, but just barely. :: :: BladeD decides to call the commander again. :: ::The phone rings and the commander picks it up. :: Commander Fuller: Commander Fuller here. BladeD: Hey commander. I am sorry to call you again so quick, but I found something very disturbing. Commander Fuller: What is it BladeD? BladeD: Well. I looked over everything in the blueprints, so I started looking at the pictures of the inside of the building. Now the thing is that…. Well it is hard to see… but in these pictures I am saying bloodstains on the walls. They are really, really hard to see, but they are visible. I just don’t understand why there would be bloodstains on the wall. Commander Fuller: That is weird BladeD. Are you sure that it is just you and you aren’t seeing things? BladeD: I am not commander. These are bloodstains for sure! But it shocked me at first also. I don’t understand why they would have internal fighting going on. I can’t think of any other reason why I would see bloodstains. Commander Fuller: It really doesn’t make any sense BladeD. I will try to find as much information on it has possible. The pictures we gave you were copies, so I will have our lab guys do some work with the pictures and see how far they can zoom in, so we can confirm that these really are bloodstains like you said they are, and then we will go from there. As of right now, I really don’t understand it. It is a sickening thought. BladeD: Yes it is. It is also scary. Commander Fuller: Yea, but let me let you go, so I can go down to the lab guys and see what they can do. I will call you as soon as I get any information BladeD. BladeD: Thank you sir. :: This may sound a little off subject, but it isn’t. Right now BladeD is just examining everything in the building. From the blueprints to the pictures everything is being examined. Why you ask? He needs to know about his surrounding. The reason why this isn’t off subject is because the bloodstains are a part of the surrounds. Also the bloodstains could give clues about the guys in the building. :: :: BladeD continues to look at the pictures. :: BladeD: This is really strange. I just don’t understand it. First the place looks easy to infiltrate and now it looks like there is internal fighting going on in the building. This place is just not making any sense. I just quite don’t understand it all. There is something missing that I am not catching onto. Hopefully the commander will find something out when he does those lab tests and gets research done because this place is really being to scary me. :: BladeD pauses for a couple of second. :: BladeD: ::shrugs:: I don’t know man. Gosh, what else is wrong with this place? It is insane! :: The scene is going to start here. The commander needs to find some stuff out soon because this is really becoming weird and it is probably freaking out the commander himself. Anyway…. :: :: Fade that screen thingy or whatever to blackness dark of stuff…. Something of that sort yea! ::
