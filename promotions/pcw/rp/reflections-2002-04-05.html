Reflections

5 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 5, 2002 A candle burns so softly that not even an angel could extinguish its flame. I've never known myself to be a poet, but even now I cannot help but swim in my own rhymes and riddles. A chorus of cheers can be heard in the back of my head, though I drown it out every chance I get. I see the ring - the squared circle - and I long to slid underneath the ring ropes and throw my arms up in ultimate victory. It is greatest feeling in the world, yet here I am, not partaking in my own joy. I fall awayso softly at firstnever losing a beatjust watching it work Forever it seemsbut never I straythe path that was laid outwas never as gray So many wonder who I am, or even what I truly am. I am a man, plain and simple. Anything more would assume that I am a god, and truly I am not. I don't claim to be more than who I can be, yet untold expectations are placed on my back as if I were to carry them to Olympus and set them at Zeus' feet. This is no fairy tale, and I am no prophet. I can only tell you what I know and what I feel. You can take away money, prized possessions, and other world objects, but never the fire that fuels a man who never relinquish what he feels inside. The burning desire to win, the insatiable hunger to be the best - that is what is needed. Not the glitz and glamour. Fuck the fireworks and the pretty cinematography. I am Legend Inc. I was Legend Inc. I will always be Legend Inc. Never dictate to me who you think I am, or who I should be. In your eyes, I am just another figure that you want to be. Live vicariously through me, for nothing else matters but your own selfish wants. I will continue to be Tom Lexian, and you can perceive me however you wish. Anger filled, yet never uncontrolledI see myself standing tall Picked up from such a long fallBut I return ever so strong Who believes in one true soulThat beats in harmony with the heartA relationship so deep and trueI find myself wishing I had found it sooner And so here I stand at a crossroads. Who am I? I will answer this. Not you. Not him. Not her. When you come to grips with that, perhaps you'll realize that I cannot be swayed. If you are confused - good. If you think you know what I'm trying to say - think again. I am an enigma, a puzzle waiting to be solved, but not so quickly. Nothing comes that easily, and with that, my disappearing act is now over. See me now, see me later, see me again. Don't blink, you might have missed me already. And so the candle burns out, but not for long. A flame can never truly be extinguished.
