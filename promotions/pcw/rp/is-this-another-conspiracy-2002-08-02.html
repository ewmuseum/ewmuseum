Is this another conspiracy?

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-2-02 ::Introduction:: After the results of Turmoil Mark seems as if he is in a happier mood now. He finally gets a win from a five match losing streak. Mark started to not feel how it was like to win not in him anymore. After winning against The Refugees in Turmoil in about ten seconds Mark has confidence in the team going into Fusion. He feels that he and Duzza might actually pull a victory here. That is if Duzza can act properly in the match. We didn’t get to see how the two worked as a team during the match on Turmoil since the match was so short. But now on Fusion, I guarantee that the match will not be a go in the ring and one two three. Mark is actually going to have to work for this win on his own as usual. Plus who is this new Blayze guy in PCW? Who knows all that Mark knows is that he is an African American and everyone knows how much Mark loves African Americans. But will the team of Mark and Duzza cooperate on Fusion? ::End Introduction:: As Mark sits on the bus by guess who? Duzza. He is thinking about Fusion and how Rush Mason’s injury is going to come into affect for the match. Will Duzza turn on him to save his old friend or will Duzza beat him down and injure him some more? Who knows, only Duzza really knows what he is going to do. Duzza has his head phones on as he is listening to some R. Kelly crap of music. R. Kelly is Duzza’s idol, although he molests little kids. “Get that crap off of your walkman.” Mark says as he turns to Duzza who has his eyes closed and his banging his head. “That is way too loud for the bus if I can hear that bunch of crap.” Duzza obviously cannot hear what Mark is saying as he is making a fool of himself. People are looking at Mark and giving him funny looks as he is still looking over at Duzza. Mark then nudges Duzza as he puts his walkman on pause. “What do you want cracka?” Duzza says as he has his headphones off. “Can you not listen to that crap while you are around me?” Mark asks politely as Duzza gives him a funny look. “Now if before we go to wrestle at Fusion and I don’t listen to R. Kelly. How am I not supposed to be pumped up for the match?” Duzza asks. “Well you have to get a little bit of Rammstein on the headphones to get pumped up.” Mark says as he goes into his book bag that he took on the plane. “This is the real reason that we win. This is the source of our winning right here. I have in my hand the best cd in the world that is a lot better than R. Kelly.” “Rammstein?” Duzza asks. “They sound like some black persons haters just like you.” “Nah they are just a German groups that gets me pumped up for the shows. I mean this group is the best thing that has happened to rock music since Metallica.” “Wait a minute.” Duzza says as he cuts Mark off of what he is saying. “Before you fill my head wit all of dis no sense about rock, let me tell you about the real music.” “Wait a minute yourself.” Mark says as he cuts Duzza off of what he is about to say. “There is no real music unless you listen to this here rock.” Mark says as he is putting the cd in Duzza’s face. “Cracka, do you know what you are doin by putting that shit in my face?” Duzza asks as he is pushing the cd away. “No what am I doing to you? Annoying you with the best music ever?” Mark asks as he puts the cd back in Duzza’s face. “You are making me get more annoyed by your little music liking.” Duzza says as he starts to put the headphones back on. “If you put those headphones on I will walk right off this bus and make you lose at Fusion on a two on one attack.” Mark says as he is threatening Duzza with the Fusion match. “And you know how much we both need to win a lot more, so if you lose me you lose the match yourself.” Duzza just looks at Mark as he starts to put the headphones on. Then Duzza takes the cd from Mark and he replaces his R. Kelly cd with the Rammstein cd. “I knew that you would see the light.” Mark says as he has the R. Kelly cd in his hands. “You see now that we are going to probably be a team for sometime, since people enjoy seeing me kick your ass, we have to compromise on each other.” “You kicking my ass?” Duzza asks. “If I remember you gave me a cheap shot at the end of the match.” “No, lets not start this again.” Mark says as he waves his finger at Duzza. Duzza just shakes his head as he puts the headphones on and he turns the walkman on play. Duzza’s eyes gets real wide as the first song blares through the headphones and to the ear drums of Duzza. Duzza flips the headphones off and the walkman goes flying on the phone. “What in the hell was that?” Duzza asks as he looks surprised. “That was the worst thing I have ever heard.” Mark stands up from the seat and he looks as if he is in a fighting position. Duzza starts to stand up as the whole bus is looking in amazement at the two men who are both of different race. “What are you going to do now you motherfucker that don’t even belong in this country?” Mark asks as he starts to get in the face of Duzza. “I am going to kick your ass right now to prove that I am the best partner that we have.” Duzza says as he is in the face of Mark’s right now as well. “You couldn’t kick an ass on a little kid.” Mark says as he is about ready to punch Duzza in the face. “A little kid?” Duzza asks. “Well I don’t know how good you are goin to be with three African Americans in the ring at the same time.” Duzza says as he starts to head back to the window. “I have done it before and I will do it again.” Mark says as he starts to put motion forward into his punch. Right before Mark hits Duzza, the bus driver hits his breaks and sends Mark flying to the front of the bus. Duzza sits back down in his seat as he puts R. Kelly back into his walkman. Mark gets up from the ground and he heads back to the aisle where his seat is. Duzza throws the Rammstein cd into the aisle and where Mark can’t see it as he is so mad right now. He then steps right on his own cd that Duzza put out there and Duzza starts to laugh. Mark sits down in his seat and he puts his seat back. ”I don’t want to talk to you or see your face until we get to the arena.” Mark says as he closes his eyes. Mark is thinking about the match and how Duzza is going to affect how the match will come out. He hopes that the team can get another win, but saying he wants to get a win with Duzza means that he likes him. Mark will never like African Americans in his life unless something real good comes in him, but no one sees that coming soon at all.
