Desperation and Confusion.

30 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30th June El Diablo the wrestler, El Diablo the director- What could he have done wrong? What could he have done wrong to make The End attack him during their tag match with The Chef and Alexis Parshin? Those are the questions that the Mexican Devil wants answering, yet cannot find one. So what is next for this Mexican luchadore? Again, Diablo has returned to his home in Chicago, Illinois. The home where El Diablo has retreated to many times after many troubles. It is the place he would hide when Flaymin Aymin would torment his already deceased dead brother, and where he would run to when his wife Angel left him. The house has many memories for El Diablo, mostly bad ones, but perhaps that is what raises his spirits- Knowing that he can overcome all set backs. Can't he? El Diablo lies lonesome on his living room carpet, with all the furniture parted for some reason. The three seat, leather sofa is pushed right to the side and the matching leather chair right against the window, where the curtains are drawn. The living room lights are switched off and over a section of the carpet is a rugged green mat, that has bits of dirt scattered all over it. 'This will show him,' says Diablo with malice in his voice, 'This will show everyone that has doubted me,' finishes the Mexican as he finally rises to his feet. Also pushed aside is the television and coffee table, so that the whole center of the living room is empty for some purpose. Empty, except for the mat and dirt, and for some reason a digital video camera that is on the floor by El Diablo's right foot. 'Soon we'll see the main attraction, soon we'll see what film making is all about,' adds El Diablo, sounding kind of bitter. Maybe he is bitter at the loss of his first attempted film 'The End' in which his star walked out on him and assaulted him or perhaps just at all his doubters over the last few weeks. Whichever it is, it has really affected El Diablo, and he has taken it all to heart. Weeks and months of planning 'The End' all over, or at least put on the shelf anyway, and Diablo knows that, he knows that oh too well, and that must be why he is on film project version two. 'And we're almost ready to begin filming,' states Diablo as he walks over to behind the sofa and pulls out an old ice cream tub, only ice cream it isn't, as that can be told by the rattling in the tub as he walks back over to the muddy green mat. Diablo smirks and pulls the lid off, throwing it towards the windows in some enthusiasm. 'The Battle of the Mini Giants,' beams El Diablo with the title of this featured film, 'Nothing beats a completely improvised film,' comments the Mexican. He soon empties the tub onto the dirt filled green mat, and what spills out, but the old gray and green miniature toy soldiers that children play with, and some miniature women too. You just have to wonder what El Diablo is up to. In there too, are plastic mini tanks and plastic mines as well as gray plastic 'walls' Diablo begins positioning the plastic men and women around, and all the weapons. 'Ah perfect!' comments the Mexican Devil, after getting all the plastic pieces into position. He takes a moment to look through the lens of the camera and looks back up. Everything is in position, including the distance of the camera, to catch everything in one shot. 'I'll show you who can make a film,' says Diablo to himself, 'Who's unprofessional now?' asks Diablo in response to what The End said in a segment on Fusion. El Diablo looks up to make sure the lighting is correct and again at his soldier mat and tanks to double check that everything is fine. Diablo clicks the camera on. 'Fight scene, Take 1, ACTION!' he says professionally. At that point he moves one hand onto a toy soldier and starts to move it. 'Prepare to die,' El Diablo speaks as the soldier, in the best German accent he can give. 'Nuh-uh-uh-uh-uh-uh-uh.....' he says repeatedly to mimic gun sound. He takes his right hand and starts to flick a few of the soldiers down. 'Noooooooooooooooo, you got me,' he screams as a rival soldier that he just knocked down. El Diablo at that point then realises he forgot the tomato ketchup to use as fake blood, but is too far into the scene to take it back now. 'No, you killed him,' Diablo says in a high-pitched voice, pretending to be a woman in the scene. He turns the 'shooting' soldier around to face the plastic woman. 'Hasta la vista, baby!' he says again in a bad German accent. 'Nuh-uh-uh-uh-uh-uh-uh.....' he sounds again as gun sound, and knocks the woman down with his right hand. El Diablo looks around where he is for a few moments and lets go of the soldiers. 'CUT!' he yells to no one but himself, as he switches off the camera. He stands up and looks down at the set, and just kicks it all out of the way in frustration. 'That's no Hollywood classic,' he says disappointed with himself and efforts. Diablo walks away from what he just recorded and seats himself down on one of the chairs he pushed aside earlier that day. 'It's just not the same,' he mutters in disappointment, 'I need a cast again, dammit,' Diablo finishes in anger. He stands back up and shakes his head, he then looks down and sees one of the toy soldiers that he kicked away, with it's head nearly off. He gasps in horror. 'Stuart, I didn't mean it,' he shouts in fear. El Diablo drops onto his knees and picks up the toy soldier in his hands. 'Stuart? Can you hear me? Stuart, don't go! You can be my star!' he shouts trying to revive the broken toy. He has issues, right? Diablo drops the toy on the floor in disappointment and looks up at the ceiling of his house. 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!' Diablo looks back down. With issues like these, it makes you wonder how on earth, he plans to defeat The End this coming Sunday at Gold Glory.
