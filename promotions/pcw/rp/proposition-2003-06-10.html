Proposition

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6 - 10 - 03 “More and more, I’m beginning to think that under these clouds, evil will never surrender…” We rejoin our badass and superior hero, Dante Jackson, as he treks amongst the gods and demigods upon Mount Olympus in the Greek mountains. A rust begins to form under the pedestal that The Messiah stands upon as he quavers his body around, rotating as charcoal beings of gray clouded smoke and dense precipitation appear above him, drizzling downward as a sneer of loneliness appears on the bold hero’s face. It had been some days since the battle with Totally Tubular Tommy at Fusion, which led to the subscription of Violence Jack’s dubious return to ringside, gesturing for yet another match with current PCW Heavyweight champion Skylar Green, but a masked man snatched Jack’s hopes away, for the moment it. Earlier on in the night, the spite of tongue that Jackson had been using had warded him away from sinister ghouls Avalanche and Hiro Saotome, but the failure of his wife in trying to capture the metallic device that keeps his neck shocked with pain sent him back upon a trial of misguidance, and devious plot thinking once more. The Messiah sulks down as he grasps a felt pin, jotting down information upon a slab of rock, once more in which the Greeks used back during the times of the Trojan Wars for information and reconnaissance. Dante: “For those who cannot understand that even my own tongue can belittle the opposition faced in front of thee, thy still do not feel as great.” Away from the sitting Messiah, our hero’s father, or God in both ways continues to sit down with his hand tightly imbedded into the stone chair he sits in at the moment, quickly shifting through television stations with his free hand. Zeus: (sighing) “Six billion lives and not a single decent one of them is interesting!” He groans to himself, obvious in more pain that his son is at the current moment as he gets up from his heavy chair, clutching onto a set of arrows leaning upon the steel chair as golf clubs would to a caddie. He slugs the bag along his beef shoulder, smirking to himself. Zeus: (enthralled) “HI-KEEBA!” Dante: “Immortal Dad, you can stop with the traits you’ve given along to Alan Steele. Ever since he was thrown off of the port during the Wrestle Vessel presentation in July of the last moon revolution, it seems PCW has been coming down with those heroes who ‘fit only towards their own greed’. Zeus: “I suppose your right, but you have to understand son, that is the way life is.” Dante: “I know, but I cannot kept save haven above here while evil reigns from below!” Zeus sighs still, bored with the Prodigal Son’s continuing mannerisms of infidelity and question of his given abilities. More and more the father of life continues to stalk among the clouds as he can see Hermes, the fastest among all immortals roaming around with a quick and almost inhumane speed. Zeus: “Oh Hermes!” Hermes: (under his breath) “Hell ought to be better than this…” Zeus: (shouting) “HERMES!” Hermes: “Oh yes Lord?” Zeus: “God fetch my son two wenches.” Hermes: “He’s ‘The Messiah’! He should be able to get…” Zeus: (raging) “NOW HERMES!” The man enthralled with the Nike insignia upon his feet, Hermes bolts out of the Greek God’s sight as Dante looks on with peculiar eyes. Zeus turns around with a cheesy smile upon his face, lending Dante the benefit of the doubt as he sits back down, scribing once more. Dante: (thinking) “So it is with great pleasure that I continue my duties upon the earth and once more Primetime Championship Wrestling to battle those of conformity, those of idiocy and those of just downright evilness.” Zeus: “Hear hear!” Dante: (thinking) “I also wish the old man would stop trying to read my mind in order to figure out his own good will.” Zeus: “No, its too fun.” Dante: “But-” Zeus: “Yes I know you’re old enough to think on your own without me judging it but this is Olympus! We hear and see everything!” Dante: “Even when Sanders is in the bathroom pleasuring himself to a copy of O Magazine?” The God shakes his head in shame. Zeus: (sniffing) “Yes, sadly.” Dante: “Oh. Great, just great. The powers of the immortal even include perverts.” Zeus: “Sorry son. It’s been that way ever since Kennedy happened to get up here.” As Dante nods in complete understanding of the manner, storm clouds begin to rise below the chateau of which happens to be the manor of Stately State, but including Houston as well. Zeus turns towards his son in a fitting moment. Dante: “Time to go?” Zeus: “Yes. And for my sakes you better duck the next time that ditz of a blonde throws a weed eater at you. I can’t have you back here all the time now.” The Prodigal Son nods. Dante: “Hi - no, that wouldn’t work. I have to be original…” Zeus: “You’re still trying to find a decent closing aren’t you. Just say what Stuart Scott says and then get back down there and move!” Dante: (boldly) “BOOYAH!” Looking downward below, moving away from the heavens and glitz that happens to be modest décor for the home of Zeus, we position ourselves upon the bustling city of Houston, site of many landmarks and skyscrapers as we join the heroine of our hero, his alpha to her omega, Tracy Jackson as she cradles her bundle of joy Simmone upon her chest, clasping her hands around the small girl’s back. Joining them for the ride however in the simple lavender and crème` Winnebago, dynamite and ace reporter to the stars, Elliot Sanders as he clutches a microphone in-between his tiny right palm. Elliot: “And with us today happens to be Tracy Jackson, wife of Dante Jackson as she takes her toddler, the beautiful ten month old Simmone Jackson for her first attempt at proper school.” Elliot booms into the microphone, smiling as Elise, the henchwoman for our lady happens to careen the vehicle into the east lane, sending the group upon embarking at the School of the Gifted and Young. Tracy: “Elise!” Tracy barks out as the female driver tries to make certain that this road trip does not end in crash or even worse, death. Tracy: “Hurry this thing up!” The driver looks into the rear view mirror, checking in on Ms. Tracy and Sanders who sits alongside with the champion of feminine rights. Elise: (weary) “I’m trying my best to keep up with the speed limit. Not to mention the multiple lights or stopping octagon signs that keep halting us from getting towards the school faster.” Tracy: (scowls) “School is nothing!” The words send the driver to slam her foot upon the acceleration pedal, speeding the car up even further as the friction between both wheel and street begin to quicken. Elliot snuggly leans his baldhead back along the nape of the passenger seat. Elliot: “Such a beauty of a chair.” Tracy: (scornful) “Keep telling yourself that in such a deathtrap.” The reporter’s eyes shift east towards the mother of the child. Elliot: “Excuse me?” Tracy clicks her teeth along her tongue. Tracy: “If I’m not mistaken, I said that this poor excuse for a land vehicle is sublimely a throwback to the days of when comic book heroes drove these crappy machines. Thank God for the eighties and the ways of excess, otherwise Superman would have just SKIPPED to the crime scene!” Elliot bites his tongue in short protest, but Ms. Jackson keeps him at bay. Turning the car west, hugging alongside the sidewalk, Elise stops the car in front of a large building, shapely in various corners and degrees as the sign teetering on the ground a few feet away from it reads out clearly for the group in the car. “Welcome, to the University of Gifted and Young Babies.” A smirk arises on the face of our heroine as she can only bask in the glory of what the name Jackson can do upon this great institution as she begins moving out of the vehicle. With Elliot slamming the passenger side door behind him, the man smirks alongside Tracy as they begin walking towards the front doors of the institution. CRRREAAAK! SLAAM! The passenger side door falls off of the hinges, as the horrible sound of metal being bent rushes through the ears of the group as they step back in looking behind them, taking in the open compact as a sign of somewhat before moving forward inside of the building. Looking around, a sea of children surround the man and two women, all sitting obediently in an Indian style manor as the dean of instruction, dressed sharply in a dress coat with the school emblem stitched into the right breast pocket and a white dress shirt with red tie instructs the children on the occurrences of the day. Tracy can only hold her mouth gaped open at the discipline of the children. Tracy: (smiling) “There is a God.” Elise: “And his name is Zeus.” Tracy nudges Elise in the shoulder, sending her shifted a few inches over and colliding into Elliot. POWWWW! A reciting pin of bowling balls ring through the ears of the children as well as the dean of instruction as he stops his lecture, looking dead ahead at the three bystanders causing the interruption. The children turn their heads, looking at Tracy, Elliot and Elise before the sharp cackling of the dean’s voice stops them dead in their movement. Dean: (grunts) “CHILDREN!” The children quickly shift back towards the dean’s full attention as he smirks with a common smile on his face. He steps away from the oak podium as he comes to greet our heroine as well as the henchwoman and the reporter assigned to cover the story. He looks over at Sanders first, leaning in over him while standing at an imposing frame, causing Elliot to begin labored breathing in fear. The dean steps backwards, moving away from the reporter as he sighs relieved before coming near Elise who only smirks with a loving smile. Elise: (naive) “Um, hi!” The dean looks over Elise, picking up a small strand of her golden hair before setting it back down to rest upon her matted head once more. He sniffs in compressed and conditioned air before moving towards our heroine who stands stern faced as if nothing could faze her. The dean raises and eyebrow towards her as she clutches onto Simmone tightly, the principal’s eyes focused upon the daughter more and more. Finally he steps back into a center position of the room. Dean: (squeaky) “Why hello!! And welcome to the School for the Young And Gifted! I’m Professor Nightrodder, head master and basically all fun and dandy man. We teach various things and in subjected curriculum which makes this one of the finest programs in all of the country! Our only drawback is that there are not many children whose parents are strong towards the option of private school.” Tracy: “Yes, my husband knows all about school. He says that school is the only time people will pay close attention towards you. It is when you get out of school is that when you become overlooked and more and more sought after by your length, not the quality of your work.” Dean: “Your husband is a most noted man if he thinks that way.” Tracy: “No, not really. My husband is a faux. He believes he was sent by God to handle things of good and evil on the plane in which he was subjected to work. When he was ‘normal’, he was always in good spirits. But now, ever since we were kidnapped he’s become this odd renegade hero biased upon evil and more working for good. I haven’t been able to even take a good glass of red wine since he’s taken up this crusade!” The dean nods in agreed valor. Elise: (interrupting) “But Ms. Tracy, Mr. Dante is only doing his - “ Tracy: (scowling) “Interrupt and you’ll see that conformity is only a way for you to be broken down into being a subjected IMBECILE!” Nightrodder looks on rather pleased by the objection as Tracy fixes her black “Sexy” T-shirt as Simmone is dropped back down upon the ground, crawling towards other children as they begin to make their dissention away from the sect of discipline Nightrodder has set. Professor Nightrodder: “Well Ms. Jackson, we have the traits of a genuine four star school in the Houston area, it wouldn’t be bad if - ” Tracy: (questioning) “If what?” Professor Nightrodder: “If your daughter was at the top of our class. Give or take a few decades, I’m sure she can be of a better society!” Tracy: (outraged) “A FEW DECADES? She is of nine months! Old enough to send your blithering behind out upon your ass for festering!” Professor Nightrodder: “Well Ms. Jackson…” Elise: “I think you should just stop trying to reason with her.” Tracy: (pissed) “Elise, say one more word…” Tracy gives Nightrodder a cold gaze into his eyes as he begins to tremble for a second. Planting his feet firmly, he stares back into the eyes of the woman, as both of them release of quasi energy in a firm battle of trying to upend one another in this starring contest as Elliot creeps behind the Professor and taps him on the shoulder, sending him tumbling downward onto the floor, the loser. Tracy: (boasting) “I WIN! Now, is she accepted or not? Before I have to conform your ass you my foot giving you a royal face beating?” The Professor pops back up. Professor Nightrodder: “Okay, okay, she’s accepted. Will you just take the tour already?” Tracy: “Fine, now which hallway to go? I hope there aren’t any molesters in these halls unlike the last school we went to.” Professor Nightrodder: “And which one was that?” Tracy: “The Society of Spelling Society in All Capitals?” Professor Nightrodder: “Oh, well thank GOD you stayed away from that place.”
