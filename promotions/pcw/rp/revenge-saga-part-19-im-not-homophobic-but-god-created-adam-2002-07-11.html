Revenge Saga Part 19: I’m not homophobic, but God created Adam and Eve…not Adam and Steve.

11 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/7/02 Revenge Saga Part 19: I’m not homophobic, but God created Adam and Eve…not Adam and Steve. From Gold and Glory… Shabazz rolls Crichton right into the pin with 25 seconds remaining. Crowd & Duke: 1-2-3! Jeff: Nope, Jaden’s still on the apron. Shabazz does his best to quickly get to his feet and get Jaden off the apron sohe can pin Crichton with the 20 seconds left in the match. Both Shabazz andMeacham are standing in Jaden’s face, telling her to get off the apron. Crichton rolls over. Jaden sees him and reaches her hand around Meacham’s side, tossing two brass knuckles into the ring. Dave: What the? Crichton slips the knuckles onto his hand and stands behind Shabazz. Duke: Look behind you, hoss! Holding his arms wide apart, Crichton sandwich punches Shabazz with the brassknuckles and quickly tosses them out of the ring. Jaden jumps down off the apron, and Meacham turns back to see Crichton pinning Shabazz with four seconds left. He drops to count the pin. Timekeeper: 3... Referee: 1... Timekeeper: 2... Referee: 2... Timekeeper: 1... Referee: 3! The referee quickly calls for the bell and the timekeeper records it just in time. Clay: Ladies and gentleman, your winner by pinfall at a time of 14 minutes 59seconds, and the NEW PCW TELEVISION CHAMPION... JOOOOOOHHHNNNCRIIIIIIIIIIIIICHTOOOOOOOOOON! Crichton is joined by Jaden, and they celebrate in the ring with a kiss as Crichton’s music plays on the speakers. Dave: It was getting to be a good match. Damn, I can usually see swerves coming. Jeff: Just don’t act like it, huh? Duke: I can tell ya, Shabazz ain’t gonna be too please with the way this matchwas taken from him. Jeff: Look, The End said something good in his column. Don’t complain aboutanything in your match. That also counts for stablemates trying to cover up their partners’ failures. Shabazz can’t wrestle, and he got beat. Get over yourself, Williams! SMACK! Dave: Quite a shock, we thought for sure that Crichton and Jaden had split up. But you never can trust politicians, or anyone associated with them. Duke: I smell a rematch It had been more than a week since the PCW PPV Gold and Glory, it had been more than a week since his titanic struggle with the challenger Jordan X Shabazz. For Crichton the PPV had brought him only one of the things the title promised…he had gold, but he did not have the glory. Part of him didn’t care…after the way the title was stolen from him on the night of the mass firings it was just…it didn’t matter how he got the title back, not to him. The other part of him was almost ashamed at the result, but what could he do? He couldn’t give the title back. This perspective changed somewhat directly after the PPV. Crichton was only guilty that he won that way because he thought he was fighting a decent man…a man Crichton respected and almost wanted to be like and he was…at the PPV at least. As soon as Shabazz, Rush and Thug Roc started attacking people…starting with him…he lost respect for Shabazz and upon extension the rest of them. He was now glad that he cheated to get that damn title, because he now knew that Shabazz wouldn’t think twice about cheating for a normal match, never mind a match in which a major title was on the line. From the Last Edition of Friday Fusion… Jordan X Shabazz tips John Crichton’s table over, causing glass to crash, and furniture to fly, again. John Crichton has had enough, he stands up and looks Jordan in the eyes. John Crichton: I pinned you in the middle of the ring, boy, so I suggest you leave with your friends and go out somewhere to steal something. Jordan X Shabazz looks back at Thug roC, and Rush Mason, laughing. Then he suddenly gets serious using a catchphrase that he coined on EW 24/7 Malice with the Phantom Booker. Jordan X Shabazz: NO THE FUCK YOU DIDN’T, BIATCH! YOU BEST SIT THE FUCK DOWN, ‘FORE YOU GET SLAPPED THE FUCK UP! John Crichton looks in Jordan’s face, not moving, but smiling. Jordan rears back and smacks the taste out of John Crichton’s mouth. Crichton touches his mouth, checking for blood. When he sees some, he runs and tackles Jordan. People in the restaurant begin to scatter, heading towards the door to head for cover and await the arrival of the cops. With Jordan and John Crichton brawling on the floor, Rush Mason and Thug roC decide to come and interfere. RoC smashes a glass bottle over the head of John Crichton, knocking him clean out. Rush Mason goes into his pocket and begins reading from a book. He’s chanting some saying, in a non-recognizable language. Suddenly, blood starts to pour from John Crichton’s mouth. The police bust in the door and move in. A staff worker is screaming. Staff Worker: THOSE THREE OFFICERS!! A swat team of police officers enter the room screaming at Rush, Jordan, and Thug roC to get cuffed as we fade back to the supreme announce team of Duke Williams, Dead Hiro Saotome and Dead Bobby Stormbringer. Crichton suspected that his next two matches involving Shabazz would prove his theory right, Shabazz would cheat to get a win over him. That is the only way Jordan X Shabazz would ever beat John Crichton…that he was damned sure of! Shabazz had two other guys waiting in the back, just itchin’ to cause havoc, but so had Crichton. He knew that if he was ever to make anything in PCW he would need people to watch his back. Upon entering PCW he was involved with the stable known as the Forgotten Ones, he, Andy Murray and Ben Pailthorpe kept each other’s backs, and then it was the Political Machine. Crichton didn’t care much for politics, he just needed some extremely competent guys watching his back. No one came more competent than Politically Incorrect. Crichton lay on his bed, in the motel room he had come accustomed to over the last couple of weeks, with his hands behind his head and his feet crossed. Still wearing his familiar knee-long leather jacket. It was funny…before hooking up with the beautiful Jaden, he had never stayed in a motel room this long before. Even when Aeryn was with him, he never stayed in a motel more than a couple of days, but since there was an apparent lull in his life, he didn’t think it would do any harm for him to stay in the same place for too long. After all he figured out that, the only reason people always managed to find him was the secret tracking device that Mr. Fleming planted on his car. Now that the device was gone, people hardly bothered him anymore. Plus it was more convenient to see Jaden on a regular basis if he stayed in one place. On the Friday Fusion before the PPV Gold and Glory, one of his worst nightmares had come to pass. He struck a woman. Ever since he was a teenager, and he realised he had an anger problem this had been his worst fear. He believed that striking a woman was the lowest thing that a man could do, but he also knew that when he lost his temper not a lot could stop him from becoming violent. This was but another reason to join the army, he was taught discipline and was able to partially control his anger. With the things in his life currently, the past anger that haunted his was rampant, and his greatest fear had come true. He decided that if he and Jaden didn’t talk about it, the whole incident would go away and he would forget about it. He was wrong. Two Weeks Ago On Friday Fusion… [Crichton looks down at the gold bracelet and notices the name Aeryn eloquently engraved in fancy lettering. The sudden realisation dawns on from the night before, when he was drunk.] Crichton: Goddamn it! Jaden: Is this some sort of way for revenge or something? Just because I burnt a few pictures of Aeryn you think… [Crichton extends his arm lightening quick and roughly places his hand around her delicate neck, making it very difficult for her to breath.] Crichton: This was an accident! I got drunk and put the wrong name on the bracelet! Jaden: Crichton…grhhh… you’re hurting me!… Crichton: And for your information they weren’t just pictures you burnt, it was the only thing left I had of her! The pictures are the only things I had left to respect her memory! You’d better deal with it too, because I ain’t never…ever gonna forget her! [Crichton lets go of her throat and storms out of the locker room, as Jaden is left to slump down to the floor. She rolls herself up in a ball against the lockers, tears flowing from her eyes as the camera fades to black] The memory haunted him, and he didn’t doubt that wouldn’t stop haunting him until the day he died. A perfect punishment for the cruellest of acts…having to live with the atrocity on his mind for the rest of his life. Crichton sat up on his bed and rested his head in the palms of his hands. How could he ever face her again? Perhaps if he thought about it, an answer would come. He never got the chance. Just as the thought entered his head the door of his motel burst open, in spectacular fashion. Crichton stood and backed away from the door the best he could, expecting some really big guy, or yet another bastard robot, but it was none of those things. There before him, stood a man wearing a bubble-gum pink spandex suit, with a Hand Bag in his left hand, and the letters HBW eloquently sewn into the front of his suit. Crichton ran his hand over his face. Crichton: I really don’t believe this! Guy: Believe it villain, for on this day, you will surely fall at the hands of THE HAND BAG WARRIOR! Crichton: The what now? HBW: The Handbag Warrior! Crichton: Your gay, aren’t you? HBW: I am, but I can kick your ass nonetheless! Crichton: With what? Your Handbag o’ Doom? HBW: I see your familiar with it’s power, villain! Crchton: [Holding his forehead in his hands] This is going to be one of those days! TO BE CONTINUED…
