Sore...hurt...pain...(TT#1)

15 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2-15-03 Ice falls into a plastic bag. Little by little the bag fills up and is twisted shut by the slender hands of Jaslene Myst. She weighs it in her hand and tosses it up into the air. Jaslene steps away from a refrigerator and closes the door to the freezer portion. Looking around for something she reaches out and opens a cabinet. ”Ah! Here we are…” she thinks to herself reaching into the cabinet and pulling out a couple of twist ties. She takes a couple of the ties and uses them to close the bags of ice she has with her. Tossing the remaining ties into the cabinet she closes it and takes the bags with her into the living room. Adam is sitting on a black leather couch watching TV. A bag of ice sits on his knee and the other on his shoulder. Jaslene walks in with a smile and hands him the new bags. ”Hey thanks babe,” he says, ”You have excellent timing.” He hands her the bags that were on him which are now mostly water. He places the newer bags back in on his knee and shoulder. ”I’m just good like that, you should know that by now Adam.” she says with a smile. ”Yeah that’s true, silly me.” he replies sticking his tongue out at her. ”That’s real charming,” she says, ”So how are feeling today after getting your first of many PCW wins? She sits down as Adam sinks down into the couch, ”Sore…hurt…pain…” is his response. ”Oh come on now, it can’t be that bad!” she exclaims smacking him in the shoulder where the ice bag was sitting. The bag falls down into his lap as he reaches up and starts to rub his shoulder. Jaslene smirks as he looks over to her. ”Ouch! That hurts!” he says still rubbing his shoulder. ”Seriously Adam get over it. I don’t get it, you were a machine in the DWO and I didn’t see you complain then so what makes now any different?” she asks. Adam sits back up and scoops the bag of ice out of his lap. As he places it back on his shoulder he answers, ”Well in the DWO I had hit a rhythm plus I wasn’t doing two shows a week. It takes a lot out of you. But as soon as I hit that rhythm I’ll be fine. It’s just a matter of waiting.” ”Oh that reminds me, you have another match lined up for Tuesday.” she remarks. Adam looks over to her with a look of pity on his face. ”You can’t be serious, another one already?” he sighs, ”Good lord these people are running me into the ground, well whatever it’s my job anyway. Who is it against?” ”Chris Storm but-“ she manages to get out before Adam cuts her off. ”Chris Storm? The so called Perfect One?” Adam says as he sits back into the couch, ”This couldn’t be more perfect if I had planned it for myself. I thought it would take me a couple of more weeks before I got to prove to the world what a loser Chris Storm really is…he deserves none of the praise he gets…” ”Before you get too far ahead of yourself there’s a catch,” ”Goddamit there is always a catch! I can never catch a break these days it seems.” he lets out a sigh, ”Whats the catch?” ”It’s not a normal one on one match, it’s a handicap match with Erik Erratic.” she says. This brings a smile to Adam’s face, which is quickly followed by a chuckle. He sits up and takes the ice off of his shoulder and his knee before standing up. ”This is great now Storm will be forced to take on two men he can’t handle one on one at the same time. The Perfect one is doomed to failure and Erratic and I will bring it to him.” he lets out a loud laugh before heading off toward the kitchen. Jaslene follows him into the kitchen and watches him dump the ice into the sink. She looks at him with a new curiosity. ”And you’re ok with this? This doesn’t upset you at all?” she hesitantly asks him. ”Yeah.” he says looking back to her with a smile. ”Ok, who are you and what have you done with the real Adam Stryker?” Jaslene asks raising her eyebrow. He laughs as he wipes his hands dry on a towel. ”Heh, come on Jas. Of course it’s me.” he answers with a laugh walking over to her, ”Who else would it be?” ”Oh I don’t know some alien perhaps!?” she thrusts her finger into his face and looks stone cold serious. Adam doesn’t quite seem to understand what’s going on as he playfully slaps Jaslene’s hand out of his face. ”Come on Jas, aliens? Don’t tell me you believe in that crap.” he says. She shakes her head from side to side, ”Of course not. Don’t be silly. It’s just that you usually hate being involved in something that isn’t a one on one match.” ”Oh yeah well this is different,” Adam replies, ”But this time with Erratic in my corner we can accomplish something amazing.” ”Oh yeah? And what’s that?” Jaslene questions him. Adam laughs before walking off back into the living room from where they came. Jaslene follows in anticipation but Adam doesn’t give her answer. Instead he walks over to a bar and walking behind it he takes a bottle of water from small fridge. ”Well?” Jaslene asks, Aren’t you going to answer my question?” Adam opens the bottle of water and takes a drink. He slowly pulls it down and twists the cap on. Adam places it on the bar as Jaslene sits down on a stool across from him. ”It’s real simple,” Adam starts, ”With Erratic and myself teaming up to take Storm down we will be able to totally destroy him.” A maniacal smile comes to Adam’s face as he twists the cap off of the bottle. He takes one more drink before putting the bottle down on the ground. He squeezes the bottle in frustration until he crushes it. The remaining water comes out the top and spills over onto the counter. Jaslene leans back in the seat as a look of concern comes to her face. ”Is there something I should know about here Adam? I mean seriously, totally destroy him? What’s up?” ”Do you know what it’s like to be in someone’s shadow Jaslene? Especially someone whom you know is beneath you?” he says concentrating on the bottle as he still squeezes it. Jaslene shakes her head from side to side again. ”It is the worst feeling in the world. Not being able to get the recognition, the respect you deserve all because someone else has taken it from you. Storm has been that shadowing figure, the one holding me down.” he says through his teeth finally letting go of the bottle. ”Wha? Storm? But you two were never even in the same league. How does this work?” she says with a hint of confusion coming through in her voice. Adam looks up and into Jaslene’s confused eyes. ”It’s real simple Jas, P…T…C…” he says, ”I was the champion of the most dominate fed in that organization and what’s the result? All anyone can talk about is Chris Storm. His named was popping up all over the place where mine should have been!” ”Yeah but Adam that is no reason to really hurt someone,” she says, ”So what if he was consider to be more popular than you in the PTC? All of that is in the past now, it means nothing now!” ”Jas all I do is hurt people,” he answers, ”It’s how I’ve made my living for the past four or five years. And I’ll tell you just who cares…ME! I do!” Adam throws the crushed bottle to the floor before walking around the end of the bar. He walks out into the middle of the room, Jaslene spins around in the stool and listens to him. ”Chris Storm was nothing but a punk, is nothing but a punk. Tuesday Erratic and I are going to prove that. We are going to beat him so badly that he’ll wish he’d retired. I’ve already pinned one half of the former tag team champions, the only thing left is to pin the other half. Jaslene gets up off of the stool and throws her arms around Adam. She squeezes him. ”Adam honey you’ll always be one of the best, there’s no doubt of that.” she says adding, ”There is no need to try and live up to something that isn’t worth living up to. Besides what do you think of your partner Erik? He is kind of young you know.” Adam looks down into her eyes. ”So he’s kind of young? He has beaten Storm before and now we will beat him. Come Tuesday Storm is going to learn a whole new meaning to the word pain courtesy of myself and Erratic. It’s going to be a night to remember that’s for damn sure. ”That’s all well and good but let’s watch some TV now, there’s bound to be something on.” she says. Adam reaches down and kisses Jaslene on the forehead before letting go of her. Jaslene sits down on the couch and turns on the TV as Adam walks into the kitchen.
