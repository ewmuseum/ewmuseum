The Return of the Russian Bear

17 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-17-02 It was raining outside of the PCW Headquarters when the red, nondescript van with SOVIET for a license plate roared up to the entrance and stopped suddenly, knocking over the ‘no parking’ sign outside of the brightly lit skyscraper. For John Gould, security guard of the grounds outside of the building itself it merely meant waving a flashlight and asking the driver to park elsewhere… …or so he thought. “Excuse me!” Gould yelled over the splattering rain that drummed on the roof of the van. “You’ve gotta move somewhere else, Sir!” Gould raised the flashlight to peer into the drivers side window of the van, but he didn’t need to. Instantly the door flew open and the large, pale hand of the OSW World Champion, Ivan Stanislav, gripped him by the front of his blue rain jacket. Gould yelped as Stanislav exited the van and stood before him, towering high above the man, and raised him to eye level. “What?” Stanislav growled. “Uh… y.. you can park here.. actually.. I… I’ll watch the car for ya!” Gould yelped and kicked his legs weakly. “Da… far better.” Ivan grumbled as he dropped Gould to the ground. Ivan slammed his fist on the side of the van three times and rounded the van as the rain slowly drenched him. The side door of the van swung open while Loyal Communist Party Member Wang, The Two Round Faced Women, and Speedy Riggs climbed out of the side of the van. The passenger side door flew open quickly and Tempest bounced out to the concrete sidewalk to join the congregation of communists. “Whoa!! Big place!” Tempest said as she stared up at the huge building. “Yes… the Evil Empire…” Ivan growled as he stared up with her for a moment and then trudged towards the entrance. “Don’t we need an appointment?” Tempest asked, somewhat confused. “Oh no Tempest!” Wang piped in, “Ivan has his own appointment… his fist!” Tempest gasped and looked up at Ivan, who merely winked in response, and pushed open the glass doors to the lobby of the building. The secretary in the lobby glanced up as she had been through the Nelsonian era of the PCW and continued to keep her job through the Scientological era. “Oh Jesus…” she gasped as she saw a face that she hadn’t seen in months, “Uh… w…welcome to the PCW…” she stammered while Ivan approached. Ivan, Wang, Riggs, and the Women walked in lock-step with one another in a military fashion while Tempest skipped along beside Ivan. She looked somewhat perplexed as the demeanor of Stanislav had starkly changed upon nearing the HQ. Stanislav reached the desk and slammed both of his hands onto the surface. The secretary jumped and looked up at Ivan, shivering. “Actually, we’re looking for…” Tempest began until Ivan’s voice boomed over her own. “Xavier Kannon…” Ivan growled, “where is he?” “Uh… Mr. Stanislav…. CEO Kannon is not present at the moment, he is in Europe with the rest of the PCW roster.” She replied in a desperate attempt to stave off Stanislav. “Actuawwy,” Wang interjected as he produced a large paper, “this wog here states that Kannon is here today on business….” The secretary went pale, “Well uh…. AHH!!” She screamed as Stanislav grabbed her desk and ripped it from the wall before promptly shoving it across the linoleum floor. “I think I know where he resides…” Ivan chuckled while Tempest gazed at him. “Ivan,” she questioned, batting her eyes, “is that necessary?” “Completely necessary, Tempest!” Ivan bellowed while the secretary huddled in the corner. The Russian Bear stood in the center of the lobby with his arms outstretched, the logo of the PCW built into the floor itself below his military boots, “Behold the PCW! My first federation in America! The capitalist juggernaut that tried to destroy all that is good! The bulwark of evil that has bred tyrant after tyrant!” Ivan slammed his fist into his open palm, “Yes, this is necessary… it is all necessary!” Flashes of lightning from outside shot into the lobby as the dramatic moment was broken by Tempests little voice. “Well… she doesn’t seem like much of a tyrant….” There was a pause. “Oh…” Ivan finally answered, looking at her, “but she is!!” Ivan whirled and snapped his fingers while the Communists stood at attention. He turned and trudged down the far hallway towards a stairwell. “Come Comrades! The schematics of the building has not changed!” The group followed Ivan as they walked into the large stairwell and began to ascend the steps. “But Ivan,” Tempest asked as their footsteps echoed up the shaft, “why not take the elevator?” Ivan nodded in a very militaristic fashion, “This is good question, Tempest. You must understand that if you take elevator, they can stop them! We are invading, my dear… invading into dangerous territory!” Tempest bit a nail and looked back to Wang, who nodded gravely. “Wow, this is very serious… isn’t it?” “Extremely.” Ivan answered while he continued to climb. Eventually, the group would make it to the top of the stairwell and entered into another area. This one was actually the newsroom of the complex which consisted of a long hallway in which doors lined, mainly for reporters, commentators, and crew of the PCW itself. Stanislav walked down the hallway quickly, speaking to Tempest as he did, “I have spent many times charging through this area, Tempest…” “Ahhh…” Wang continued dreamily, “Wememba when you charged thwew the walls, Ivan?” Ivan nodded, remembering when the hallways were barricaded and he had to charge through the plaster walls instead. Back in the Nelson era. “Da, Wang, I do remember vividly.” As the group walked, headed by Ivan, a door flew open and Jeff Marx himself, color commentator for the PCW and loyal communist to Ivan Stanislav, walked into the hallway and stood dead in his tracks. “Holy SHIT!!!” He yelped and stood stock still, “Ivan Stanislav?! What are you doing here?!” Ivan stopped and stared at him, “Comrade Marx, is it not?” Jeff blinked, not knowing what to do. “H… hold on a second!” He ran into his office and fumbled around before quickly returning with a tiny, tattered communist flag. He waved it feebly. “Ha ha! I still have it!! Please don’t kill me!” “At ease Comrade.” Ivan growled, “I have Chief Executive Officer to fry, not you.” Marx nodded quickly, still waving his flag, “Just so long as you don’t kill me Ivan!! Y’know what, I’m gonna… I’m gonna go hide… er… stay in here…” He slinked back into his office, slammed the door, and locked it. “Who was that?” Tempest asked. “Jeff Marx, communist commentator of the PCW… and turncoat to boot… but he is funny to scare.” He winked to Tempest and continued on his way. “The last time I was here, I was hated! Now I return under the banner of goodness!!” He roared down the hallway as various people poked out and looked at him before gasping and hiding in their offices, door locks latching shortly thereafter. The group walked to the end of the hallway in which two doors, one to the left and one to the right, were to be chosen. Ivan grinned and grabbed the knob to the door on the right and walked through which led to another stairwell. After ascending the stairwell and exiting, the group marched down a long, ornate hallway complete with chandeliers, paintings of Xavier Kannon, Ellie, Brandi, and Duke Williams. Ivan scoffed as noticed a large door at the end of the hallway. “Sickening… the corruption of capitalism at it’s best!” The far door swung open as two security guards entered and slammed it shut. They froze at the entrance of Ivan and looked at one another and then at Ivan again. “Uh… freeze?” One asked. Ivan did not stop. “If you do not get out of my way,” Ivan growled, walking faster, “I will be forced to go through you,” he quickened his pace, “which will not be quite hard!” Ivan’s feet thundered on the floor as he started to jog toward them. The two men cowered to the left and the right, hiding themselves from Ivan. Tempest giggled, “This is fun!” Ivan made it to the door and glanced at the two men, “Good choice.” He grinned and grabbed the doorknob. “Behold Tempest… the nucleus of all that is evil in the PCW…” Ivan threw the door open and walked into the lobby of the CEO of the PCW, which happened to be Xavier Kannon at the time. But instead of the typical secretary post it was gone, replaced hastily by ten security guards. Behind them, Xavier Kannon stood. “FREEZE!!” The guards yelled as Ivan and his group filtered in. “….please?” On one end of the spectrum, Xavier Kannon and his ten guards stood, on the other, Ivan Stanislav, Tempest, Loyal Communist Party Member Wang, The Two Round Faced Women, and Speedy Riggs stood. Eleven vs. Six. “Stay where you are, Stanislav!!” Kannon screamed loudly, his voice cracked. “You can’t get any further!” “DYAAHAAHAA!” The Russian Bear roared, “Xavier Kannon, the ‘Alpha Male’!! The Russian Bear has returned to the PCW to dispose of you once and for all!” “Yeah!” Tempest yelped beside Ivan. “I’m no Tyler Nelson, Stanislav!” Kannon hollered back, “You’ll never touch me in this building!!” Kannon backed up slowly and grabbed the doorknob to his office. “There are Eleven of you.” Ivan growled as he took a step forward, “There are six of us! Those odds are in our favor!” “What?” quipped Kannon, “Aren’t you supposed to shoot opium into the building and kill us all first!?” Ivan growled, “I am OSW World Champion, Xavier Kannon….” “Get out of my office, Peon!!” Kannon yelled in response. “GRAAAHH!!!” Stanislav roared and bolted at Kannon. Kannon jumped and opened the door while yelping like a woman. The guards rushed forward to grab Ivan, four of them reached him first and each of them were flung to the ground. The other six leaped and shoved into Stanislav, stopping him in his tracks. Wang and the others, save for Tempest, moved forward. “Nyet my Comrades!” Ivan roared, “Allow me to handle this!” Kannon opened his door and walked into his office, but remained in view for Ivan to holler, “Xavier Kannon, you have toppled Tyler Nelson, but you are no better! Come Alliances, the OSW will show the PCW what real wrestling is made of! The PCW will once again remember who Ivan Stanislav is, and who is the most dominant force in wrestling world today!” “GET OUT!!” Was all that Kannon could muster before slamming his door shut. Stanislav roared and surged forward once again, pushing through the guards to the door itself! He bellowed and slammed his fist on the heavy wooden door, “KANNON!! The Red Scare has returned to the OSW!! You are finished Kannon!! FINISHED!!!” The guard grabbed Ivan and pulled at him while his comrades stared in disbelief. Tempest watched on, suddenly quite frightened. “I will crush you Kannon!! BURY YOU!! THE OSW WILL SUCCEED!!!” He roars and stops his fight, backing off from the guards who are now quite exhausted. “You are finished, Xavier Kannon. DYAAHAAHAA!!” “Uh… Ivan? Lets go.” Tempest said and tugged his suspender. “Da…” Ivan growled before glaring at the guards, “our work is done here… the fear will only grow throughout the week. Come my Comrades, let us return to OSW and prepare for the war ahead!!” As the Bear and his comrades walked away, Tempest skirted over to Kannon’s door. She giggled to herself and reached into her pocket, grabbing a hefty handful of glitter, and tossed it all over the door and floor before joining them once again. With that, Stanislav and his comrades along with Tempest exited the PCW Headquarters. Of course, every couch, table, or object that wasn’t nailed down was flipped, smashed, destroyed, or glittered in one way or another. The security let it slide, far too many times had they had to cope with the terror of Ivan Stanislav and his rampages. And the PCW could only hold its breath until Alliances…
