Go Away!

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/5/03 Knock, Knock, Knock Had I not fallen asleep downstairs on the couch I would have never heard the knock of the person on the other side of the door. But it was only six o’ clock in the morning. What the hell would someone want at six in the morning? It couldn’t be Chris; he was asleep upstairs in his bed. It could be one of those religious freaks trying to catch me and brainwash me into following and believing their religion because it was the only way to be happy and go to heaven considering I was half asleep and I’d believe just about anything I was told because my brains wouldn’t be awake enough to deny it. Then again it could always be Gaz, trying to further agitate me and rub salt in the wound that I was bonded to him because of a prank gone wrong. Even if he did not want the marriage himself I’m sure he wouldn’t rule out coming to the door at six AM with something repulsive planned just because he was a prankster and well that’s what pranksters do best. In a semi awake trance I made my way to the door, ready for just about anything. Reaching out to the knob I turned it and slowly pulled the front door open slightly incase someone was waiting for the second I made a false move and was ready to beat me down to the ground with fists or weapons. “Who is it?” I whispered through the crack in the door before looking hoping that perhaps I would get an answer without having to look outside where it was much colder than it was inside of the well heated house. “You know who it is.” A cheery female voice rang out in the morning air. The voice then identified itself with a body as a head peeked through the crack in the door. It was much worse than imaginable, it wasn’t Gaz or some religious maniac trying to sell me their religion, it was Allison, the blood mother of two of the children I was raising. “My god, why are you here?” I said, as she walked through the front door inviting herself inside the house. If there was one person I didn’t want to see at this time of the morning, it was her. Quite frankly with Chris still somewhat upset about the occurrences of the wedding and then a match against three others on Turmoil, the last thing I wanted was to have to worry about what she might say or do, but it looked like I might have to do that now anyways. “Why else would I be here Tessa?” She replied, knowing very well that she would get on my nerves with an answer like that. But I had to try to calm myself; I couldn’t let her get to me no matter how much I hated her and wanted her to crawl back to the hole she emerged from. “You can’t see the kids, they’re sleeping right now.” I said as calmly as I could, shutting the front door which she left wide open. “Oh but Tessa, I don’t need to see them right now.” She smirked as she walked towards the living room, taking a seat on the couch. I flopped down on the smaller couch beside it hoping that this visit wouldn’t last too much longer. She had only been here for three minutes now and already I was sick of her presence. “Then why are you here!” I shouted out in aggravation, making it obvious to her that her visit was unappreciated. “Well Tessa, I’d just to inform you that better cherish your final weekend with Alex and Christa, because after then they’ll be coming with me.” An evil grin surged across her face, knowing that if I hadn’t already been lowered down ten feet into hell with all that has happened as of late, then this would do it. “WHAT!” I shouted out, unable to believe what I was hearing from this woman who left my belated brother on his own several years back. “You heard me.” She smirks as she begins to fish through her pocketbook for something. “The court granted me custody.” As she said this, out of the pocketbook came a piece of paper which had granted her full custody of both Christa and Alex, therefore taking my custody away. “What the…how did you manage to get this?” I questioned as I yanked the piece of paper out of her grasp and scanned it myself. Surely enough, it was an actual legal document which stated that the children were now hers, but what I couldn’t figure out is how she managed to attain one after being the one to walk out on the kids when her and my brother divorced several years back.“It’s quite simple, blood mothers can do pretty much anything they want as long as they don’t break the law, and well there is nothing against the law about walking out as long as you pay child support.” Her grin only grew larger as she said this and tore the piece of paper out of my grasp and back into hers. “You little…” I started off, but cut myself off realizing that my cursing her out would only make matters worse. She was now the legal guardian of Christa and Alex and there was nothing I could do about it but bite my tongue and accept it the way I had to accept the name Tessa Green as my new official name. “Now now Tessa. No need to let tempers flare this early in the morning, wouldn’t want to wake the kids up with all this arguing, would you?” She teased as she stuffed the papers back in her pocket and got up from the couch heading towards the front door knowing that her mission of making my life a living hell had been accomplished. “Get out already!” I shouted towards her as she made her way towards the front door, silently snickering to herself. “See you later, Tessa Win- oh wait I’m sorry I forgot! I mean Tessa GREEN!” She shouted back, rubbing salt in the wound that only continued to get deeper. I stood there silently defeated as she walked out the front door, closing it behind her this time. I closed my eyes letting out a sigh knowing that tings couldn’t possibly get any worse than this, and hoping that perhaps Tuesday would bring a change of luck with me defeating Jade, El Diablo, and Makonie and becoming the number one contender for a title that would suit me well, the cruiserweight championship.
