Turmoil Wars - James Edwards (Part I)

25 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/25/03 Just days after the mischievous Misogynist made his debut in to the PCW cluster, which he more importantly lost at the hands of another debutant by the name of Shane Whethers, although ending in an unjust low-blow, a win is a win. Stone rested home, carefully aiding his jewels and recharging his physique in the aftermath of this past Turmoil, although it seemed to be some time since the event Stone was far from insensitive when it came to corporal punishment. He held his crotch firmly keeping his eyes closely shut, and as always sporting a once fine, now ancient black suit. Near him was the sheath sheltering his blade, placed upon the side table. Thoughts sparked afloat within the accursed mind of Stone Burns but running aimlessly in his mind like a broken record were men.. Not homosexually speaking but generally. Being the baleful Misogynist who had slain two of many more to come women, his plans became more and more dubious and actively augmented when he quarreled with PCW's other chosen talent, Shane Whethers. As his mind went in to the occasional daze, he snatched the sheath off his table while the chimera occupied his brain. The scenes surroundings were at utter darkness, the only source and spot of faint light coming from the semi-draped and semi-opened window. No ventilation whatsoever produced from the room, giving off unbearable heat from the unknown size structure of the pad. All but silence reigned over the room, only to be broken by audible footsteps pounding profusely as the auburn haired; blade wielding, misogynist, Stone Burns came forward, still clad in an ancient black suit. To the front of him came his primo opposition, of two others to come and he was... James Edwards. Almost like an apparition with faint vision of his visage and body but the leisurely gait as his ceased to a reasonable battle position. Stone firmly held his blade, and brought it forward in a quarrelsome matter as James brought his fist in the air, but as a dream comes with fantasies a sudden appearance of a similar blade came in to his possession. They slowly came forward, their blades beside them and a dull face expression. Just meters away they began readying themselves, as Stone had once failed a battle, he was confident he wouldn't fail this one. James came forward, lunging his blade recklessly and missing by a sidestepping Stone, who winded his blade and swung it to the side of his head but James brought his hands in the way, blocking any fatal damage. The blade sunk deep in to his hand, through his palm, laving the outside of his epidermis in blood but continuing without show of any agony. Stone took a few paces back, as James swung once again, this time skimming his skin and cutting him, also bringing out a moderate amount of blood to the middle of his forehead. Stone staggered back, James advanced onward once again cutting Stone's skin, gaining the upper hand as Stone continued to stagger. Making matters worse Stone fell over himself, and landed flat on his back on the hard pavement. James emerged over him, evilly laughing, while joining both hands in holding the blade, bringing it to the back of his head and aiming down in the midst of blurting... 'You've reached your Turmoil!' The same thing Shane Whethers had claimed but this time Stone drove his knee right in to his stomach and he winced... Stone shook his head and returned... 'I think you have.' He stuck the blade deep in to his flesh at the top of his abdomen, rendering a loud cry from James and a final fall to the ground, with the blade protruding from it. He came atop Stone who shoved him off to the side, and picked himself up on his pair of feet. He grinned but something didn't seem right... A multiple pounding of footsteps came after and Stone confusingly waited. The footsteps of Matt Jackson... His next opposition in the series of Turmoil Wars. TO BE CONTINUED
