The Sage Of A Champion: Part 9

17 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-17-02 BladeD: The Sage Of A Champion“The Return Of A Hero! Friend! And Amazing Wrestler!Part 9” Again Same - Chapter 4: A Sidetracking Sent Straight From HellAgain Same - Day 6: The Insane One Has Been Chosen :: The scene picks up from where we left off last time. More and more information about Violent Ray is spilling out and people with information as well :: Athens Mir: Well, I am not sure if this is completely correct, but my best guess would be that Kirk got the information from a man we like to call “The Bullet.” General P: What do you mean when you say we? Athens Mir: Well, The Bullet’s real name is Allen Ransford, but we all call him Bullet and only best friends of his like myself know his real name… so when I say the word we I mean the people that associate with him. General P: So since he is your best friend he knew that you were supposedly on our side the whole time, but how would a guy like Kirk know someone of the likes of this “Bullet” character. Athens Mir: Well, I wouldn’t personally know because a lot of people he associates with he never tells me about because most of the people he just sees that one time and that is it…. So you would have to ask Kirk about that. General P: Hold on for a second. :: General Powers phones the secretary and tells her to get on the phone with Captain Kirk, asking him to come to General P’s office right this moment and also told the secretary to let Captain Kirk know that if he doesn’t show up immediately he would be demoted two ranks and making those two ranks harder to get then the first time around. :: Secretary: Hello. Captain Kirk? Captain Kirk: Yes? Secretary: General Powers’ needs you in his office right this moment for something very important. Captain Kirk: Umm… What would that be? Secretary: That information was not supplied to me Captain. Captain Kirk: Well can’t it wait? Because I am sort of busy at the moment. Secretary: Well Captain. Let me put it to you this way. The General said if you don’t get here immediately he is going to automatically demote you two ranks and make those two ranks harder to get then the first time around. Captain Kirk: WHAT!!!!!!!!! All right then, I will be there! :: Kirk wasn’t that busy… He shouldn’t have had any trouble coming in the first place. All he is doing is working out. It is nothing special, but lucky he wasn’t really busy with something. :: General P: While we wait for Kirk to get here. Explain to me who this “Bullet” person is Mir. Athens Mir: Well, like I said. His real name is Allen Ransford and only his best friends know that. He is pretty much a gangster only he is one of the topnotch gangsters around the world. BladeD: I think I have heard of the guy, but only under a different name… Like Super Shot or something. Athens Mir: Some people call him Sniper Shot, some call him Gun Shot, but the name he uses and the most commonly know name is Bullet. General P: Interesting. This guy most be pretty popular… Yet I have never heard of him before. Which is kind of strange because when it comes to combat, he is there is anyone that popular I would usually of at least heard of them and most likely know them personally. Athens Mir: Well see. The thing about Bullet is that he isn’t known in the eye of the public. Insiders and underground people of that sort only know him… He is actually kind of hard to get a hold of because there are only groups of people in different parts of the world that know about him. He doesn’t like to be in the eye of the public because one he would think of himself as preppy then and he doesn’t like that thought. Also, he is almost scared you could say of being in the eye of the public because he is scared if he got too caught up in having fans and “loving” them that it would eventually destroy him in the end. BladeD: I remember when I use to have that problem. I use to fear that problem all the time, only I got to the point where it wasn’t going to be avoided, so when it did happen I was ready for it and I tried not to let it get to me. In the end I have turned out all right. I mean, it isn’t that big of deal. I have found that even if you fail your fans will still love you for who you are. That is basically what happens… You are scared of failure really…Athens Mir: Yea, I think I know where you are coming from. I remember when I started having that problem in Egypt and places in the Middle East. Finally, I just got to the point where I didn’t care what anyone thought and I went on with life, so what had happen was all that fear that was in my mind just slowly disappeared and I turned out good, but the only problem is that you can’t teach something like that to a guy like Bullet. He is kind of guy that once he gets a thought stuck in his head he isn’t going to change that thought until he thinks he should change that thought and not when someone tells him it is time to change that thought. BladeD: I never really understood how a person could live like that. It makes no sense because they will be proven wrong of something and still wants to believe it when the proof is there that they are wrong. It is almost being stupid on purpose. Athens Mir: Yea, but what happens is that person doesn’t want to be wrong and when proven they still don’t want to be, so they act like they are still right. BladeD: Yea, but it stupid. Period. General P: Ok, you two. You are getting off subject. You two need to get back on subject. :: Suddenly there is a knock at the door :: General P: Come in! :: Captain Kirk walks through the door and shuts it behind him :: Captain Kirk: You wanted to see me General? :: Captain Kirk looks over and sees Athens Mir :: Captain Kirk: Why…. Hello… Athens? And BladeD. What are you doing here Athens? :: Captain Kirk just realizes that Athens is supposed to be dead. :: Captain Kirk: Wait a second…. Your…. Your…. Your supposed to be… be… Athens: Dead? Captain Kirk: Yea. General P: Don’t worry about that right now Kirk. By the way, why are sweating so hard? Don’t you have better manners then that? Captain Kirk: Well I was in the gym working out when I was told that I had to be here immediately or you would demote me by two ranks.General P: Well, That is true, so I guess I can forgive you this time. :: Captain Kirk as just made it on the scene and now we have even a lot more questions then last time. Now we have Violent Ray, “The Bullet,” and how Captain Kirk is in the mix of all of this. This is definitely a lot of information that needs to be figured out, so that means you are going to have to keep saying tuned in to figure out what is going to happen next in this amazing sage of an amazing champion. Part 10 is next!
