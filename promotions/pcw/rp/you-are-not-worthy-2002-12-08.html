You Are Not Worthy

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Title: You Are Not Worthy Featuring: Xavier Kannon Date: 08/12/02 As the lights above flicker on to illuminate it’s interior, we see the CEO’s room at the PCW offices in Altantic City, New Jersey, the unfortunate home of PCW from the view of Xavier Kannon. Not a second passes before the sound of the door reaching it’s critical point and slamming into the door-stop is heard. “He’s not... Not.... WORTHY!” comes the bellowing and frustrated tone of PCW’s leader, Xavier Kannon. The man who ushered in the Third Age’s face is Red and is showing uncharacteristic signs of stress. “A man like Duke Williams barely deserves to be putting the ring together, let alone be fighting for Titles.” his harsh and bitter tone continues while he strides across the blood Red carpet. Now the Front Office has split, it seems hard to believe how Duke and Xavier ever co-existed. Diametric opposites once again, their battles from April and May continuing after a brief honeymoon in the Front Office. Disgusted is the word that best describes Kannon’s face, lines never seen before appearing as a queasy distortion maps over his usually youthful and cocky expression. “His voice....”, muttering and thinking out loud, Xavier pauses trying to find the right words, “His accent....” continuation, still no closing, “Dammit! I want to squeeze someone’s throat as a stress ball every time he opens his mouth!”. Not often is Xavier this rattled, but Duke has a way of getting under his skin like only Duke can. “This is meant to be the Third Age!” the Scientologist exclaims, falling back into his leather seat that has created his perfect body mould in the months he’s occupied it, his safety blanket that reminds him who he is. “We can’t have an ageing drunkard like Williams be our company’s representative... Our pinnacle... Not in MY Fed, and not on MY watch!” he rages on, sounding more like he’s trying to convince himself while hammering each point him with a thud on the Mahogany of his desk. The camera is now zoomed in on his face, showing the Red criss-cross of his blood-shot eyes, and the slight reflection of perspiration on his forehead that darkens the tops of his eyebrows from it’s decent. Moving out, the camera shows the floor to ceiling windows behind him that reflect the harsh artificial light from the ceiling, just shining through the White film are the bright coloured lights of Atlantic City below. “He’s probably out there now,” Xavier scowls, thinking of the other half of PCW’s odd couple. “Breaking the dress code of some tacky Casino, drinking that Hubbard-awful Bourbon that makes his breath smell like a decaying tramp, smoking those Cigars like a cat pissing up a tree to leave it’s scent.” continues PCW’s owner, any sense of mutual respect between the pair well and truly gone. Again both the men will do battle with the PCW Title on the line, the serious and stern Scientologist who’s always impeccably dressed for his role outside the ring who speaks the language without the smallest of slips, who keeps his lungs clean and trains everyday to keep every muscle in his body at their optimum ready to do combat again with Duke, PCW’s Bourbon drinking, Cigar smoking, fast-food munching red-neck President who’d much rather be knocking drinks back with the fans in the back alley bar than in a meeting. The tapping of keys at Xavier’s desk is then placed in the background by more words against Duke. “I built this company back up, made us number one and put us on the map again!” the ever modest Gold Patron Meritorious claims while continuing to think aloud, his thoughts too vital to him to keep inside. “What did Duke do for PCW other than run it into the ground? Can you imagine him as Champion?”. Bright Green eyes roll up under his eyelids, no matter how hard he tries not to the Scientologist imagines Duke with the World Title, his grubby hands leaving smears on the Gold, and drink spills fading and tarnishing the leather strap that will eventually smell of stale smoke. “After the disgrace that is Clyde, PCW needs a Champion to bring same class and dignity back to the Title,” Xavier claims, his eyes now looking longingly on the framed Black and White prints on the wall, focusing on the image of himself holding the PCW belt aloft after defeating Tyler Nelson. “The Champion shouldn’t be some vagrant with a burned face who dresses in rags, or a drunkard in need of subtitles, or some Mexican immigrant who should be working in an LA hotel for less than minimum wage.” He makes it sound like some kind of valiant quest, that he wants to win the PCW Title for the belt’s own good... But as convincing a liar as the Scientologist is, he isn’t convincing anyone but himself. Xavier wants the World Title because he doesn’t have it. The smirk slowly returns. “Well, on Adrenaline, the show I made, the show I gave PCW... I’ll strap that belt back around by waist.” he speaks through his smug visage, “The only worthy man in the match will get his rightful win.” With a click, the dull whirr of his PC’s cooling fan that you only notice once it stops dies down and the leather of Kannon’s chair creaks as he rises to his feet... Each determined footstep echoing around the office. One by one, he flicks the lights off until only those from the hallway give up his position in the dull light. “I’m worthy.” he mutters in a decisive tone, “I’m worthy.” Through the darkness the squeaking of the hinges slices before the thud of wood against wood and the clicking of the lock lead us into quiet. ---- View Xavier Kannon's Biography
