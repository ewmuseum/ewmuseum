The Training - Chapter 5 - Dodgy beginnings

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th August 2002 ***OOC Note: The following RPs are from a series I started while I was back in PWA. I shall be carrying on the series here in PCW so I figured it might help to provide you with the beginning segments of the series. Thanks :-)*** Chapter 5 – Dodgy beginnings RECAP: In her quest to achieve fulfilment, the illiterate Charlotte Lawrence left her prim lifestyle in Oregon for one weekend. For three days she was based in New York hotel ‘The Epitome’, the location of a PWA weekend training camp for athletes with no training in the art of professional wrestling. How did she fare in this rough and tumble world of pranksters and assholes? Charlotte did the zip up on the back of her beige cotton skirt, which extended down to her knees in length. She was also donning small black heeled shoes, and a matching round neck jumper, with the collar wrapping around her neck warmly. Her blonde hair was tied back in a bunch with a flowery metal clip, and as normal a few loose hairs dangled loosely over her face. This was by no means attire you would turn up for a wrestling training session in, but the evening was to consist of just a welcoming meeting. May as well create a good impression she thought. It was now two hours since Joe had dropped her off at the hotel, and he was currently in the middle of a long journey back to Oregon. They had decided democratically before they made the trek that her return leg would be made via airplane. Two cross country journeys by car in four days would be too much even for Joe to navigate. Travelling was the last thing on Charlotte’s mind though as she looked around at the wonderful apartment allocated to her. Everything was in one room, but what a spacious room it was! As you walked in the door, a gigantic window offered a fabulous view of the hectic streets below, and if you didn’t wish to take advantage of this you could merely shut the royal-like curtains and block it all out. To the left was the double bed, complete with clean bed linen and real feather pillows. Of course, on this particular ‘vacation’ Charlotte could stretch out, for she wasn’t sharing with anyone. In the middle of the room was a wonderful fluffy mat, which would be more than welcoming to any kitten which was allowed access to the facilities. On the right stood a wide-screen TV, placed carefully on top of a glass cabinet, in which a collection of videos and DVDs were graciously provided by the hotel management. In the far right corner were the kitchen facilities, including a futuristic looking fridge-freezer, an impeccably well kept white oven, and a grey microwave. In that area the floor was tiled with symmetrical blue & green tiles. Also, just inside the door, located next to the bed, was an on suite bathroom. The metallic taps sparkled the artificial light radiantly, and the mirror looked like it had just been unwrapped, although windows were noticeable in their absence. Charlotte had the bronze apartment key in her mouth as she stared into the mirror, fiddling with the hair clip behind her head. Once she was satisfied that her look was worthy of creating decent first impressions, she removed the key from her teeth’s grip, and slung her handbag over her right shoulder, before unlocking the front door and shutting it behind her. The walk through the hotel, whilst hardly riveting, was far from dull. Along the endless maze of corridors and hallways, one could have been forgiven for checking the details of this place out, for on the walls there was a staggering amount of picturesque art. Monet, Picasso, Nelson, they were all there in brilliantly colourful glory. If the meet and greet wasn’t just about to start then Charlotte might well have consumed more time staring in appreciation of the artwork. However, a rumbling of human voices could be heard in the great hall a few corridors away, so Charlotte carried on, until she came to the open doors. The doors themselves set the tone for the hall. They went at least 20 feet high, and the handles were a polished gold. Stepping through the arch and into the gigantic hall, Charlotte suddenly felt insignificant and humbled. There were about 50 chairs laid out in the middle, and eighty percent were already occupied by fellow students. With elegance, Charlotte sat down carefully on a chair at the end of the back row. The seat was made of plastic and most certainly not up to the standard found elsewhere in the hotel, but Charlotte wasn’t about to complain. She smoothed her skirt out underneath her legs as the 22 year old sat down gingerly, placing her handbag across her lap. Tight and closed, her body language didn’t exactly ooze confidence. “Great, here comes my depression again,” mumbled Charlotte, recollecting her miserable years at school, where her illiteracy took centre stage. Looking around her surroundings, Charlotte couldn’t see any other females, and a quick glance at the list of potential graduates confirmed her suspicions. All around her men were being boisterous, loud, cocky and conceited, yet here was this quiet, pretty, innocent girl, sitting on her own. Funnily enough, she felt out of place. For the next few minutes a few more people drifted into the hall, sitting down. A man shuffled along the back row and sat down next to Charlotte, and they acknowledged each other with a brief smile before returning their attention to the stage, where a podium was set up. Upon the wooden structure was a microphone and a few sheets of paper, stapled together. Once everyone had taken their seats, a man dressed smartly in a suit marched up the steps. He straightened his tie and coughed once, as a signal for everyone to be quiet. A few rowdy teenagers didn’t take the hint, but a series of cat calls shut them up. The instructor on the stage, Lee Harper, is a PWA trainer, and the man who passed Charlotte’s application video. “Hello everyone,” started Lee, “I am going to be your trainer for the following weekend.” A few boys who had consumed a little too much alcohol started cheering, but a quick glare from Lee once again kept them quiet. “I’m not going to say too much because in my experience, it would take away from the friendly atmosphere we have going, but I will say this. Over the weekend, you will learn a lot of stuff. A lot of NEW stuff. If you’ve done backyard wrestling, forget all you know, it’s a load of rubbish. If you come in here thinking you are as big as Ivan Stanislav or Alan Steele, then go home right now, as I have no time for big shots. Just remember, when you are in that ring, your opponent’s body is your responsibility, as your body is their’s.” Charlotte shuffled slightly in her seat, knowing that she would be trusting her fragile body with other men. Sure, she knew this was going to be physically demanding, but did she trust her body with these yobos? The man sitting next to her turned his head and whispered. “Like heck I’m trusting my body with these drunk up children!” Charlotte gave him a smile. “Weird, I was thinking exactly the same thing,” she hissed back. Meanwhile, back on the stage, Lee had calmed down a little. “That is all I want to say for now. Glasses of champagne are at the back, although don’t drink too much as you have a vigorous day of training tomorrow. Mingle with each other, make friends. I’ll meet you all back in here tomorrow at nine. Wear clothes suitable for training, and be prepared.” Lee jogged down the stairs and across the wooden floor of the hall, eager to get at the alcohol before everyone else. Charlotte didn’t react with such enthusiasm though, as she rose from her seat and stacked it neatly along the back wall. No one had instructed this, but others seemed to be doing it, so in order not to look out of place, she followed suit. Resigned to an evening of drunk male chauvinists, Charlotte slowly made her way over to the drinks table, passing by a gang of ‘tipsy’ males. Walking past, the inevitable cat calls and chat up lines were dragged out the closet for her. After all, she was an attractive woman, dressed up and on her own. “Hey babe, I have a double bed in my room!” called one of the men. Charlotte weakly smiled back, before turning towards the table, hoping they would go away. No such luck though, and as she raised the ice cold champagne to her lips, the pushy man continued his advances. “I’m sure we could do some wrestling of our own,” he whispered in her ear, resting his dirt ridden hand on her left shoulder. “Um, no thanks,” squeaked Charlotte, her voice going all high, as she was slightly scared and unsure what to do. She could hardly slap the guy, because he would find a way to get his own back during the course of the weekend, but there was no way she was packing up with him, especially not as she had been going out with Joe for two years. The guy didn’t get the message, as he reached over her shoulder for her chest, so Charlotte backed up some more. Couldn’t this guy take a hike? “Back off!” A man shoved his way between Charlotte and this unwanted male. Charlotte caught a brief sight of his face, and it was the same man who had sat next to her earlier. He had a stare down with the gang for a few seconds, but he stood firm. Eventually the gang retreated, slinging a few insults to try and restore what in their eyes is pride. Smiling, the man turned back around to Charlotte, who seemed somewhat relieved. “Thank you so much for that, I need to learn how to handle myself,” laughed Charlotte hysterically. “Anyway, what’s your name? I forgot to ask you earlier.” “Beppe, Beppe Langsome. And you are?” “Charlotte Lawrence, the only female on this course,” giggled Charlotte, apparently still nervous. With feigned shock, she glanced at her watch, and immediately put her glass of champagne back down on the silver tray. “Look at the time, I’ve got to go! Thanks Beppe, I’ll see you tomorrow.” Charlotte turned to make her exit, but Beppe grabbed her hand, before raising and kissing it. Beppe noticed the precision with which her pink nail varnish had been painted, and also the perfection that her hand held. No creases, and her skin tone was perfect. He thought he was falling in love. Charlotte didn’t know how to react. She flashed a smile, before walking hastily out the hall without saying a word. Her high heels clunked on the wooden floor, and her cotton skirt flapped around in the air. After what seemed like an eternity, Charlotte reached the big doors once more, turned a corner and leant against the wall, sighing. Adjusting her hair clip, and gazing at the Mona Lisa hanging opposite, she pondered over what had just happened. Beppe had seemed very interested in her, and more than once she thought he had been looking deep into her blue eyes. And then the kiss on the hand, a sign of affection. He seemed like a nice man, and in his smart suit he was exceedingly dashing, but wasn’t she taken? Hadn’t she been with Joe for the past two years? As she retreated into her bedroom and collapsed on the bed, stretching out her legs, one question stood higher than the rest. What did this mean?
