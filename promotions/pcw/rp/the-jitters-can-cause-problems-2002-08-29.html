The Jitters Can Cause Problems

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/29/02 The scene opens up on a set of steps which lead up to a small quaint house. Sitting at the very bottom step is a suave looking Kramer who is dressed in a dazzling white button down silk shirt, and black dress pants. In his right hand, held firmly by the stem, is a red rose. He twirls it a bit as his mind kicks into deep thought. What if she was expecting more than one rose? Am I the type of guy she's into? Maybe she really does like my dad, and me going to ask her out will only end in my humiliation? Is that dog crap I smell on my shoe? Kramer pushes all of the worries to the back of his mind as he sits up with a new look of confidence on his face. He knows that if he leaves he will never find out the truth which will eventually eat him alive inside. So what if Nancy doesn't see him as her type, he has faced humiliation before and this will only result in one person witnessing it. He ventures up the steps thinking about what he will say and how exactly to say them. Kramer pulls out his index finger and pressed the doorbell once. A few second pass and he gets no response, so he turns half way around whistling. As he is doing Nancy arrives at the door and pulls it open. Nancy: May I help you? Kramer is a bit startled as he didn't hear her open the door, but he quickly turns around. Nancy: Your Walter's son aren't you? Kramer: Yes I am, although I really don't like to claim him as my father. They both chuckle a bit at Kramer's lousy attempt at a joke. Remembering the flower clutched in his right hand, he pulls it out in front to present it to Nancy, but she looks a bit dissapointed as the rose looks a little wilted with barely any petals left on it. Kramer sees this and quickly tosses it out of his hand looking at Nancy with a beat red face. Kramer: So how are you? [he asks as though nothing has happened] Nancy: I'm fine. Kramer: That's good! Nancy: If you don't mind my asking....is there a reason your here? Got a message from your father I need to get or something? Kramer: No. I just came over here to..... At that moment his mind just goes completely blank. His heart starts rapidly thumping as all Kramer can seem to do is stare into Nancy's eyes. He tries desperately to say something but only a few mumbles come out. Nancy: Are you alright? Kramer manages to gather a thought and quickly spurts it without much thought. Kramer: I just wanted to see if you needed any toilet paper? Toilet Paper!!?? What in the hell is Kramer thinking? Nancy has a shocked and disturbed look on her face as Kramer grows a bright shade of red as he realizes how stupid he must have sounded. Nancy: Uh I have enough. There is a pause as the two just stand there. Nancy: Is there something else? Kramer: No I just wanted to ask if you had toilet paper. [he says with very little enthusiasm as he can't dig himself out of this hole] Fearing that he might embarass himself further, Kramer starts to walk away. Nancy looks on and follows him with her eyes as he jumps into his truck and shuts the door. She heads back inside and as she does, Kramer bangs his head several times on the steering wheel in frustration. Kramer: Toilet paper!!! God I am pathetic!! He shoves the keys into the ignition and guns the engine, taking off at a high speed. Kramer sees the small chihuahua run out into the street but he doesn't stop in time. The collision causes a small shake in the car, and Kramer quickly leaps out of the truck to check on the injured or by now dead animal. The dog lays on it's side twitching up a storm, but fortunately it's still breathing. Kramer pulls it up and cradles it in his arms. He takes a quick glance at the collar so he can contact the owner, and his eyes widen. The address on the tag is where Nancy lives! Just then Nancy screams from her doorway. Nancy: Oh my god!! What happened? The scene slowly fades to black as Kramer continues to mumble, 'This can't be happening.....' Not only does Kramer make a fool of himself, but also runs over Nancy's dog! How does he expect to get her attention when these things continue to happen? And why doesn't he ever worry more about his career as a wrestler? I mean he has to face Rush Mason on Fusion which won't be a picnic, as the two probably still have hostility towards one another. And on Turmoil he gets a chance to face Skyo The Goblin for the TV Title! If ever there was an oppurtunity, this is it. Sure it's not easy fighting a spitting goblin, but hey someone's gotta do it.
