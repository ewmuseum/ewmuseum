PCW Wars: New Hope 'Finale'

28 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28 - 10 - 02 Gold Leader: 'It'll be a fruit picker-... I mean fruit cake like yourself that will win this war for the Rebellion!' The scene opens on the final preparations for the approaching battle. The hangar is buzzing with last minute activity as the pilots and crewmen alike make their concluding adjustments. The hum of activity is occasionally trespassed by the distorted voice of the loudspeaker that issues commands and Alex breaks his personal silence to respond to his superior. A. Skywalker: 'Fruit picker? Are you implying I'm gay-... As in homosexual?' The squadron leader powers up his engines, as do the remaining X-Wing Fighters and one by one they sail through the hangar doors allowing the scene to change to a more appropriate exterior view. All that can be seen of the fortress is a lone guard standing on a small pedestal jutting out above the dense jungle. The muted gruesome crying sounds that naturally permeate this eerie purgatory are overwhelmed by the thundering din of ion rockets as four silver star ships catapult from the foliage in a tight formation and disappears into the morning cloud cover. As we witness the Death Star moving into the foreground of the yellow planet of Yavin, we can make out the miniscule swarm of rebel fighters preparing to intercept via their attack formation. Red Leader: 'ALL WINGS REPORT IN!!!' The formation remains tight and looking in on the cockpit of Red Leader we see him adjusting his collar, looking rather sheepish. Red Leader: 'Gulp - Gulp - Gulp!' Again silence, his fear filtering over the com-link, his complexion fading fast and his wingmen snickering under their breaths. A few wayward jerks indicating Red Leaders reluctance prompts his cheeky wing men to call in. Red Ten: 'Red Ten standing by!' Red Seven: 'Red Seven reporting in!' Voice: 'Red Sixty-Nine-... The Princess's favourite number, or was it position, uh!' Suddenly the tacky Millennium Eagle pulls up alongside, roaring into battle with the Rebellion's best pilots as the face the indestructible force of the Empire, their Death Star battle station. The mechanical monstrosity looms over the minute crafts, bathing the entire Rebellion fleet in its shadow and magnetic field, the latter buffeting the ships slightly. Red Leader: 'All wings hold tight and switch on your deflector shields-... Arr-... That include you number Sixty-Nine!' Gaz Solo: 'Tell the Wookie. NOW LET'S KICK SOME EMPIRE BUTT~!' The fighters, now X-shaped darts, move in formation. The Death Star now appears to be a small moon growing rapidly in size as the Rebel fighters approach. Complex patterns on the metallic surface begin to become visible. A large dish antenna is built into the surface on one side. Looking in on the saga's star of Alexander Skywalker we see him amazed, yet frightened at the spectacle before them. A. Skywalker: 'Err-... Arr-... Maybe we should negotiate a truce, huh?!?' Red Leader: 'Shut it farmers boy!!!' A. Skywalker: 'Farmers boy, huh, well I'd have you know I'm a Jedi in the mak-....' Red Leader: 'Jedi? Really and who do you suppose is going to teach you since the cult is extinct - I know some green midget with bad acne, sagging ears and hunched back-...' A. Skywalker: 'NO, NOT MINION THE IMP!!!' Red Seven: 'Hmm-... He's purple-...' The group go silent as the fighters move closer to the Death Star, the awesome size of the gargantuan Imperial fortress is revealed. Half of the deadly space station is in shadow and this area sparkles with thousands of small lights running in thin lines and occasionally grouped in large clusters; somewhat like a city at night as seen from a weather satellite. Red Leader takes centre stage as they make their decent and individually the wingmen pealed off, each X-Wing firing their array of weapons, while a lighting spectacle to the eye the attempt remains nothing but futile. Red Ten: 'FFFFRRRRREEEEEDDDDOOOOOMMMMMM~!' Suddenly Red Ten became engulfed in flames as the turbo-powered laser guns mounted either side of a trench swat the bug like craft, leaving the vehicle to spiral into space debris. Another shot from the same emplacement laser gun cuts up the attack formation making the team flank and scramble randomly. Red Leader: 'Hey guys remember meeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee!' Targeting the main reactor Red Leader looks upon the option of a suicide mission, similar to that of 'Independence Day' but as his ship angles towards the gaping hole his engine is shot upon and he crashes into the structure of the Death Star. Bursting into blue flame the team watch on, mouths ajar. A. Skywalker: 'JESUS CHRIST! Gaz Solo: 'Who? Was that the fella's name-... Sounds like a Messiah but I guess our prayers were ignored, what’s left now-... Believe in the force?!?' The team give off a blare of half-hearted giggles and Alexander reacts, grasping the throttle and looking composed. A. Skywalker: 'Let the force be with-... Me I guess!!!' Laser bolts streak through the star-filled night. The Rebel X-wing fighters move in toward the Imperial base, as the Death Star aims its massive laser guns at the Rebel forces and fires. From an illuminated hole, swarms of TIE Fighters emerge, all of which containing trigger-happy pilots. Flooding the darkness with both TIE Fighters and X-Wings, we see a gunfight as ships fall from the heavens. Both sides take casualties as the Rebel's lose Red Seven and Eight whilst the Empire's smaller black ships also join the mounting space debris. Alex's swaying avoids a blast, causing the X-Wing to pull some fancy manoeuvres, which fail to shift the threat but as death looms the TIE Fighter vanishes from radar. A. Skywalker: 'Wha-...' Gaz Solo: 'Force? Yeah right - I rely on my personal sniper Cousin IT here!' A. Skywalker: 'Cheers, Cherezza!!!' Cherezza: 'No problem Western Consumer~!' A. Skywalker: 'Wha-... Again?!?' Cherezza retorts with a howl as the Millennium Eagle showcases some evasive moves, whilst Alex changes his star struck expression and makes his final approach. The young Jedi apprentice's destination the main reactor and core of this superstructure. Walls buckle and cave in. Troops and equipment are blown in all directions. Stormbringers stagger out of the rubble. Standing in the middle of the chaos, a vision of calm and foreboding, is Darth Pharaoh as one of his Astro-Officers run up to him. Astro-Officer: 'Whoa-... There's about thirty of them evasive little things Lord Pharaoh, best you get your ass up there and put a stop to this charade!' Darth Pharaoh: 'Err-... Arr-... OK-... Wait I am the authority figure here mate, but your right, if you want anything done then do it yourself!' Smoke belches from the giant laser guns as they wind up their turbine generators to create sufficient power. The crew rushes about preparing for another blast. Even the trooper’s headgear is not adequate to protect them from the overwhelming noise of the monstrous weapon. One trooper bangs his helmet with his hand in an attempt to stop the ringing. Trooper: 'Imperial fuckers could at-least give us ear-muffs!' Alex continues along the trench-like canyon, avoiding various laser blasts gleaming in the process, show-boating so too speak. A. Skywalker: 'YOU AIN'T SO BAD, YOU AIN'T SO BAD!!!' Gaz Solo: 'Sad really - From quoting the lame Star Wars to copying the equally over-hyped Independence Day to friggin Rocky 3-...' As the smuggler mutters over his inter-com, Alexander Skywalker interrupts quickly quenching any copyright infringement. A. Skywalker: 'No comment-... FREEDOM!' Gaz Solo: 'There you go again-...' A. Skywalker: 'Arr-... RUNAWAY!' Gaz Solo: 'And again - Monty Python And The Holy Grail, I dare you to try another?' Gold Leader: 'Quit it already!!!' The thunder and smoke of the big guns reverberate throughout the massive structure. Many soldiers rush about in the smoke and chaos, silhouetted by the almost continual flash of explosions. The war fails to cease as countless victims mount up, the Empire flooding the perimeter of the Death Star with their TIE Fighters whilst the Rebellion fight on with their bare numbers, yet Gaz Solo and Alexander Skywalker remain active and very much the threat. Gold Leader: 'WOOOOOOO THAT'LL TEACH THEM!!!' Gold Leader took out a lonesome ship when suddenly the Syth Lord himself raises his craft, instantly destroying the Rebellion's commanding officer before being fired upon by the conniving collective P.W-... Gaz Solo and Cherezza. Darth Pharaoh: 'Solo, I saw your momma kicking a can down the street and I said, what you doing and she said MOVING!!!' Gaz Solo: 'Argh-... White Men Can't Jump quotes, that and mocking my mother-... Err-... You don't know my mother, but neither do I-... Err-... Confused!!!' Darth Pharaoh: 'Hmm, old Jedi mind trick!' As Darth Pharaoh chuckled to himself, the Millennium Eagle took aim and fired a shot, piercing the ion engine of the TIE Fighter sending him adrift in space. Without the menacing presence of Darth Pharaoh lurking in the wings, Alex Skywalker made his decent, a collision course with the main reactor. Gaz Solo: 'ALEX PULL UP!!!' The X-Wing Fighter rose yet managing to fire its rockets through the small hole and into the core of the superstructure. A few stalling seconds and the mass of metal exploded in a neon blue light that spread through the far reaches of the universe, almost wiping out the entire air-born fleet. A. Skywalker: 'BAM - BITCH GOES DOWN!!!' Gaz Solo: 'Uhhuh-...' A. Skywalker: 'I know Scream, but it's ideal for this situation!' The remaining crew head for home, turning right into the illuminating yellow planet of Yavin, penetrating the atmosphere like falling stars. Returning to the temple a handful of crafts zoom over the towering tree's that sway from the passing engines almost igniting as they approach the eye-catching ruins. Flying through the hangar doors with a roar the scene fades only to return to the docked X-Wing's that align the bay, accompanied by the supposed scrap metal 'The Millennium Eagle'. Anarchy: 'I knew you would be back-... If not then I would've cancelled that cheque I wrote, why, cause I'm so clever and you are so-... Err-... Not clever!' A. Skywalker: 'What about me!' Anarchy: 'Oh! I knew you'd be back, after all you have another three movies to do, like hello, I mean isn't it obvious-...' Gaz Solo: 'It's obvious why she is so glad to see moi?' A. Skywalker: 'And that is?' Gaz Solo: 'Well for the majority of the second and third movie I'll be mugged and placed in cement courtesy of the mafia, I mean the Princess likes me to be stiff but that’s just ridiculous!' Alex, Gaz and Cherezza enter the huge ruins of the main temple. Hundreds of troops are lined up in neat rows. Banners are flying and at the far end stands a vision in white, the beautiful young Senator Anarchy. From one side of the temple marches a shined and fully repaired R2 D2. He waddles up to the group and stands next to the equally pristine Rush-3PO, who is rather awestruck by the whole event. Cherezza looks confused. Dodonna and several other dignitaries sit on the left of the Princess Anarchy Oragana. Anarchy is dressed to impress with a white flowing gown and appeared staggeringly beautiful. She rose and placed a gold medal over the necks of her comrades, Gaz even winking at the royal. Repeating the process ceremony with Alexander Skywalker she turned and faced her assembled troops who show their respect with a bow. Cherezza growls whilst R2 D2 signals his happiness via a series of beeps. - The End - (How I have longed to say those two words. This saga of role-plays has been wrote by Geor-... Gaz Lucas, a man capable of milking a single franchise)
