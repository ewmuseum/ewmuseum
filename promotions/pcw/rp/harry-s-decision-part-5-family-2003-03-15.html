Harry's Decision - Part 5 - Family

15 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 15, 2003 'Totally Tubular' Tommy: BODACIOUS!!! [Tommy leaps off of the bookshelf onto the hardwood floor] 'Heavy Metal' Harry: RADICAL! [Harry also leaps from the bookshelf and onto the floor] Tommy: GNARLY!! [Tommy front flips onto the leather couch, making it tip over] Harry: AWESOME! [Harry front flips onto the fallen couch next to his brother. The two men laugh loudly like two girls at a slumber party. Both have their suits undone and have cut loose, considering the empty Crown Royal bottle on the coffee table] Tommy: Har, I'm kind of scared, dude. [Harry looks over at his brother with some concern in his eyes] Harry: About March Madness, huh? [Tommy's eyes light up with anger a little bit] Tommy: NO! I'm TOTALLY gonna rip through those heinous compadres! I'm talking about you leaving, dude. Harry: You don't have to be scared about that. I'll call you and everything, it's not like I'm going to disappear off of the face of the earth. Tommy: Well, what if you do? Harry: Tommy... Tommy: What happens if you just disappear and I can't get a hold of you or something. What if some totally bogus serial killer grabs you out there in New York and your all hacked up in the Hudson River somewhere. Then after I have one of those heinous nervous breakdowns they find your body with maggots all in it. Then like this gnarly black dude, who sort of resembles Morgan Freeman, will investigate the crimes and it will just be like in that movie SEVEN! Kevin Spacey was most bodacious in that flick...but...what if something like THAT happens? [Harry pauses sort of just looks up at the skylight in the living room. He can see the clear California night sky and the stars shining above. A smile crosses his face slowly and then he begins to bust out in a roar of laughter] Harry: Tommy! NOTHING, and I mean NOTHING like that is going to happen to me! I know your going to be worried, but your going to be so busy defending your World-O-nental championships that you probably won't be thinking about me too much. And if you do, then STOP IT! You need to stay focused on the ring, man... [Tommy holds up his hand toward his brother, the universal signal for, 'Dude, stop talking'.] Tommy: What did you just say? Harry: That you shouldn't worry about me so much and stay focused on your match... Tommy: You said something at the end there. I think you said...man. [Harry thinks about it in his buzzed state and then giggles a bit to himself] Harry: I did say that... [Tommy smiles brightly and begins nodding his head] Tommy: Your returning. Harry: I am NOT returning! I'm just a bit tipsy that's all. Tommy: Tipsy or not your totally coming back to the surface!! Harry: Tommy...STOP IT! [Tommy just keeps smiling and then gets up from the fallen couch. He dances toward the telephone, not really knowing why he should be dancing, but he does anyway. His mother rejected them today and both agreed that a bottle of Crown would help soothe the pain that they feel, which it is] Tommy: I'm calling some babes to get over here, dude. You need some lovin' and with your new look the babes will be all OVER YOU, COMPADRE! You need a must GNARLY send off, dude. You know, and I know, that I'm the only bodacious dude to get the job done! [Harry just laughs on the tipped over couch as Tommy begins dialing the numbers of every babe he has in his little black book. Well, not EVERY number...that could take an eternity...but the numbers that he remembers off the top of his head] Harry: This is gonna rock, bro! A party tonight to have an early celebration for your title defense AND your huge World Title win! Not to mention a goodbye party for me?! This bash is going to be pretty far out, man. [Tommy sort of pauses dialing another number as he looks down to his brother who is giggling like a madman. Tommy heard the words correctly...he heard Harry say, far out, man. He hasn't heard that in almost a month and suddenly after a few drinks Harry is sort of coming into his old self. Tommy always thought that weed was the source of it, but maybe it's not the only thing...that Harry is not immune to other stimulants. It may not bring the old Harry out totally but it's quite a bodacious mix. There is a knock at the door...] Harry: Holy flaming poop! That was fast, man! [Tommy looks up at the door and then he drops the phone. His eyes can't really believe what he is seeing, or maybe it's the alcohol in his bloodstream that is doing the talking. But what he's seeing is his mother. She stands at an intimidating six feet tall and the sexy, black high heeled boots she wears only add to her height. Her tanned skin without a blemish and with little make up, because she DOESN'T need make up for her beauty. She's slender, but not like a stick figure. She has just enough meat on her bones. Her bright, fully white smile melts hearts...something Tommy inherited from his mother, and unforunately, Harry did not. She stands at the door with an emotionless look on her face as her first born son just stares at her as if she had morphed into some kind of evil monster. Harry turns his head with a drunken smile on his face and then it slowly begins to fade as he sees who it is. The woman, that earlier in the day, did not want to see them...rejected them because she was still angry with how Tommy handled a serious conversation with her. Even with her eyes puffed up from crying she still looks as beautiful as ever, even in his mid-forties] Harry: Ummm...get the door. [TOmmy slowly walks toward the sliding glass door with so many thoughts pulsing through his head. Did she come here to chew them out some more? Were their apologies just not enough to please her? As Tommy opens the door he is prepared for a tongue lashing like no other. Except the second his mother has room to fit through the door she collapses onto Tommy and hugs him tightly] Tommy: Whoa... [He embraces her back as she cries loudly into his shoulder, muttering as well] Mom: I'm sorry...oh god, I'm sorry. [Harry gets to his feet as the drunken feeling begins to fade from his mind. He walks toward her and realizes that maybe he ISN'T sober as he almost trips over the coffee table. He stands to the side of Tommy as the two let up from their hug. The woman that gave birth to them looks at them with a sad smile] Mom: I just want you boys to be safe, to not have any harm come to you. Tommy, I just wanted to see you be the person that you are, that's all I was trying to tell you. But I've realized that if you WANT to be a super villain, that if you don't want to change then I am going to support that decision. [Tommy smiles and gives her a thumbs up] Tommy: Thanks, mom! [She then looks over to Harry and her smile becomes brighter. Harry sort of holds his head down, letting out a sigh] Mom: Harry...oh Harry. I never knew that you had these feelings in you for so long. I know that I haven't treated you the way I treated TOmmy and it was NEVER because you were a mistake, honey. I'm not just saying that to you. You were not planned with your father and I, but you WEREN'T a mistake. In fact, you were an unbelievable blessing that brought the family closer together then every before. Tommy's father, your father, and me...we were so close and we loved each other so much. You two boys were the beautiful lives that needed to hold what we all had together. I am sorry if I treated you like I never loved you and I'm sorry if I've hurt you... [She looks over Harry and smiles, proud of what he's done] Mom: You look great! [Harry smiles and looks up at his mom with tears in his eyes] Mom: I'm so proud of you that you were able to stop smoking weed. I'm proud of you that your ready to move on with your OWN life instead of trying to live through Tommy's. You never gave yourself enough credit, whether you were high or your were sober. Maybe I was so tough with you because I wanted you to be better than you were, because I knew that you were just self destructing. You were just a boy who was lost...with no answers. You didn't have your father, and at the time, you didn't have a mother. Well, you did ALL ALONG and you know that now... Harry: I love you, mom. Mom: I love you too, son! [The three share a hug and they can feel the energy between them all exploding into one giant happy feeling. All of the past is being laid to rest once and for all. Tommy putting to rest the way his mother would ignore him so that she could go be with buff beach boys and how she would sometimes belittle his intelligence. Harry finally putting to rest whether he was loved or not, and why his mother always kept him silent all those years. For the first time in their lives all of it makes sense again and it's the perfect time and the perfect place for it to happen] Tommy: You guys are TOTALLY RAD! I love you guys! This is freakin'....ARGH...This is just so bodacious!! Harry: Does this mean that your super villain ways are over? Tommy: Are you kidding me, dude?! I'm TOTALLY a super villain! I'm going to mess Rent-A-Jerkface up in the name of all the past villains of the world! Mom: And we're going to be with you every step of the way when you do just that. Tommy: Mom, you should invite Fernando over. We're going to have a most gnarly bash for Harry's farewell since we won't get a chance after Madness. Mom: I'm with Enriquez, remember? [Tommy looks confused] Tommy: What's with you and the Italian compadres, Mom? Harry: They're Latin, bro. Tommy: Either way she's obsessed. Mom; I am NOT obsessed! Tommy: Mom, you are most obsessed with Latin penis. Mom: TOMMY! [She slaps his shoulder and he winces] Tommy: Watch the shoulder, bodacious Mommy, I've got important matches coming up. Harry: Yeah, man. [Tommy and Mom both look at Harry with their eyes wide] Harry: What? Tommy: Nothing, dude....nothing at all. [Fade to black]
