To Win or Not to Win

27 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/27/02 The scene opens up inside of Kramer's house. He is staring straight down at a book while manuevering around all of the furniture. He obviously knows his way around as he breezes past everything without touching them the slightest bit. He takes a few steps into the kitchen and the house is suddenly plunged in blackness. This change causes Kramer to collide with the corner of the kitchen table. Kramer: Ahhhhh my nuts!!!! He staggers back holding on tight to his family jewels as they throb in pain. We can see all of this as there is still a little bit of light outside illuminating through the windows. The lights come back on after a few seconds and Kramer immediatly rushes outside. As he had suspected, a squirrel had chewed through the wires and now layed on the ground black and crispy as hundreds of volts of electricity surged through it's tiny little body. Kramer: Poor little guy. So stupid and so young. Just then, a man dressed in ragged clothes, carrying a shotgun, rushes over to the squirrel and scoops it up in his hands. Man: You's is gonna be our dindin tonight....yeehaw!! Kramer: Hey Hillbilly Joe! Man: Hey Wild Wing. Joe runs back towards his house and Kramer steps back inside. Kramer: God bless that Hillbilly Joe. Without him there would be roadkill and burnt squirrel all over the place. He walks back over to pick up his book. Before he does though, he looks up to read the time on the VCR. Kramer: Oh crap I gotta get packed for Wrestle Vessel!! He throws the book to the coffee table and runs upstairs. Twenty minutes later he is back downstairs wearing a hawaiian style shirt, black baggy shorts, and sharp looking sunglasses. In his right hand he clutches his wrestling bag. He takes a couple of steps to the door and suddenly it swings open. In walks Jason who is wearing the same outfit as Kramer!! Jason hasn't looked up as he is busy pressing away at his palm pilot. Jason: Kramer you better be ready this time. I am tired of getting to the airport late and in return having to ride in your damn black truck all of the time. Kramer: I am all together this time. Jason looks up for a brief second and instantly notices that him and Kramer are wearing almost the exact same outfit. Jason: You know, we do hang out way too much! Kramer: Tell me about it. They both step outside and Kramer locks the door behind him. They then jump into Kramer's formentioned black truck and take off down the road to the local airport. Jason: You know I was looking up odds on you winning your match tonight and you have a one in a hundred million chance of regaining the Extreme Title. Kramer: Gee that's refreshing. Jason: Hey you have pulled off big wins before. Wins that even shocked the hell out of you. Kramer: It isn't as much about winning this time as it is about getting payback. I mean for about a month now I have been screwed over and over again, by Rush Mason in particular. Jason looks over at Kramer with a raised eyebrow. Kramer: Not like that you idiot! There is a few moments of dead silence. Kramer: Last week I defeated roC to become the number one contendor. That was definately a blow to the gut of Mason as he probably wasn't expecting that. But now I really want to drive it in. I am going to kick the living crap out of him and I promise that he will not walk out with the title! Jason: Who do you hope wins it then? I mean wouldn't it be more to your advantage if Rush did walk out with the title? Then you could call for a match and would have a much better chance of winning as it would be just the two of you. Kramer: Look at it this way. If Rush loses the title some of his steam will be gone. So if I call for a match I can kick his ass even more than if he were to retain the title. I would love to win it to rub it in his face, but like I said it's doubtful. Jason: Then who do you want to get the win? Kramer: The Flaymin one man! Jason: Why not Violence Jack? Kramer laughs a bit. Kramer: I just don't like the man. I mean he has the nerves to say that I am not at all going to be a problem and how I basically don't deserve to be in PCW. Sure I have had some rough times, but hasn't everyone? Honestly I think he is still ticked that he lost at the last PPV. I mean is it my fault I am just so damn good. Both Kramer and Jason laugh at the last sentence. Kramer: But yeah, I have held three different titles in my tenure at PCW and what has he got to show? Nothing! He has been in PCW for quite sometime and to my knowledge has yet to win a title. Jason: I guess you have somewhat of a point. What will you do if he wins though? Kramer: Well I can't really do anything can I? If he wins he wins, if he doesn't he doesn't. Kramer takes a sharp right turn into the parking lot of the airport. They get out of the truck and walk to the building. As they step inside the scene fades to black.
