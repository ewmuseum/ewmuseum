Date: Whenever *We start off with The End, giving an audio promo.* Woman: No no no... *The camera comes up... well, the camera was already on. The lights are turned on inside the empty black-walled studio. Actually, it's End's garage, reoutfitted into a studio for promos, researching tapes of his opponents, and watching DVDs on the 40-inch flatscreen mounted on the wall behind where the camera is facing, so it can't be seen on-screen. End sits in front of the camera on a chair in his full ring outfit of a black leather jumpsuit and mask with white trenchcoat. A few of his locks cover his eyes; he brushes them out of the way. The other voice came from Sandy, wearing a blue short-hemmed dress. She steps forward with her hands on her hips, the muscles in her thin arms flexing at the motions. The camera records them.* Sandy: First things first, you need to stop with the 'audio only' promos. End: Why? Sandy: Because, you need to establish more of a connection with the people. It's bad enough you wear a mask but you don't even make a visual appearance in your promos? You can do better than that! End: Sandy, you're not helping. *Sandy shakes her head.* Sandy: I've been doing this for years. I've worked behind the scenes managing the best independent talent in the business. Messiah, Christopher Daniels, Donovan Morgan, American Dragon, Michael Modest... End: And where do they work? Sandy: Over in Japan, Ring of Honor shows but also All-Japan and New Japan... occasionally some Zero-ONE or NOAH shows. You might catch them in APW or UPW or one of the higher end indy shows in the states. They sometimes work WWE and NWA/TNA dark matches. End: You realize I've no idea who or what you're talking about? *There are some snickers from the cameramen and promo crew out of view. Sandy shoots them a glare to shut them up, then turns back to End.* Sandy: Quiet you. Listen, if you want to get back to your winning ways, you need to first do your promos where the people and your opponents, can see you. End: *Shrugs* What good will that do? Sandy: Just helps to establish your presence. Otherwise you're seen but once, maybe twice a week on television, not enough to make a real 'connection' with anyone. End: I'm not looking to make a Love Connection video. Sandy: Get serious! You've done things your way and look where it's got you. The End points to the replicas of the PCW Cruiserweight and Extreme Titles he has hanging on his wall. Sandy: Two low-level titles. Nothing major. You got those on wrestling, but at the top it's not about wrestling, it's about promos. THAT'S the how you win matches. You think it's about the wrestling? Ha! The better promos determine the better wrestlers, because the better wrestlers can back themselves up in words. *End shakes his head and walks over to the wall.* End: Ow. Sandy: As you're blind you really need to stop walking around without your cane. End: Whatever. Anyway, you need to work on making your wording more clear and concise. And you're talking to me about how I cut promos? Sandy: I'm not the one who's wrestling for the PCW, you are. You're lucky I'm not charging you anything for these services rendered. Actually, you keep questioning me I can't render any services. End: *Shrugs* I told you when you signed on that I would be a difficult guy to work with. Sandy: And you've been wasting all this tape arguing with me! *Sandy rubs her temples with the pads of her index and middle fingers on each hand.* Sandy: Let's just cut our losses and start over with a new promo. You can still somewhat salvage this if you just look at the camera and say that this entire thing has been directed at your opponents for this upcoming pay-per-view. End: Why? Sandy: Just say that this whole thing has been against your opponents for Decimation and you'll be a little better off. *End turns to the camera as instructed.* End: This entire... promo has been done against 'my opponent for this upcoming Sunday.' As you can tell, there were many parallels between this and 'my opponents for this upcoming Liberty.' I just have trouble figuring out what they are. But they're there. They're most definitely there. *Off-camera, Sandy makes cutting sounds to tell End to finish the promo. End shrugs at Sandy.* End: What? Sandy: Stop talking and end it! End: *Sighs* Ugh... Promo over. *End*
