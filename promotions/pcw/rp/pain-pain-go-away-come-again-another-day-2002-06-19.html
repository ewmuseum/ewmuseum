Pain Pain Go Away, Come Again Another Day

19 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/19/02 “Bright Light Sunshine” It was the day Portugal lost, tears rolled down my eyes. Never again did I think I’d feel such humiliation. My heart was on the floor, and my eyes were speaking rapidly with word after word. Depression comes in awkward ways, but this time it came right down the middle. Who would of thought the urge to kill ones self would come from a measly game with a measly team. Well when that measly team and that measly game is your whole life things fall in place. So that brings me to this point in my life, where living no longer becomes fun, but instead more time to be ridiculed and insulted. *Sniff* *Snort* What’s going on in there! *Tiptoeing up steps* Who the hell is that? Sorry dude it’s my mom. Just keep the hits coming, I paid for more and I need more. Okay. One-minute mom I’m just putting a few things away, I’ll be down in just a few minutes. *Tiptoeing ends, sigh* What’s a man like you doing selling drugs in his mother’s house? Well what’s a former soccer star like you doing buying drugs from a man who still lives in his mothers house? The key word is former, it seems everything in my life is former, at least I still have a way to escape it all. And ya know what, I guess some questions are just better left not being answer, eh? You say you want to escape life, then why don’t you just kill yourself. If things ever got too bad for me I’d escape that way. *sniff* *shaking* I thought…I…I…was going…to, BUT I COULDN’T. It….it was against my beliefs, my goal is heaven. Heaven, oh. Everyone needs a heaven. I don’t need any heaven. That’s what your saying now, wait about ten more years. You’ll wish you can get to a place that may be different then this one. Why do you seem so down, I wish I had your life. The fame, the fortune, the name, the icon, what else could you possibly need? *Bang* *Fists Stomp* The friends, the family, the love, the companionship, that’s what I needed. Spotlight just helps you see yourself better, but that didn’t even help. I don’t know who I am, I bet others know who I am better then I do. *shaking out of control* What IS this? Don’t worry it’ll pass in just a few seconds. *Biting lip* Errrrrrrrrrrr! The pain, the light, the peace *Faint sounds* It’s over now… Dark hall. Walking down closer and closer to door. Open door, LIGHT! Enter light dark bad. Stay away, light = death. Life or death, decision decision, over it’s too late, no more light. Clown appears. What does he want? Where am I is this heaven? Follow me, SUNNY! SUNSHINE! NO! The pain is coming, memories of hate, can’t control, run far, and run away. *Running* It keeps following me won’t it leave, LET ME BE IN PEACE! CAN’T YOU LET ME BE IN PEACE! A spirit appears across the shadows. WOOOH! The breeze keeps blowing against my back, goose bumps appear. Is it heaven is it hell, neither my child. Who are you? Show yourself? *spirit appears* *so soft, so precious* MOTHER! Yes, son I’m here to deliver you away from the pain. Grab my hand and don’t look back. We’ll prosper together forever. *grabs hand* *so soft, so precious* Shaking thinking this can’t be real. My lips graze her face, but everything changes. HATE! Clown appears, morbid feel, blood draws, THAT ISN’T MY MOTHER. Nothing is clear, so confusing take me away I must leave. *Faded Voice* you did it t yourself! Colors change light to dark, sun hits, sunlight, and sunshine, blast. Why does the pain keep coming, CAN I EVER ESCAPE? AWAKEN! WHAT WAS THAT PLACE? *nobody their, just a bag* Blasphemy, your no help. *Running….running* Sun goes down, and dark hits, pain relieved. FREEDOM, it’s over. IT’S JUST BEGUN.
