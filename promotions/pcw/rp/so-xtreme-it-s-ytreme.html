So Xtreme it's Ytreme!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Slightly before I posted it (We see THE Multiple Man in a stark white room. Off to one side Val, HV and Raul wait politely.) Tmm: “Hiya folks out there in the so-called ‘real world’! THE Multiple Man here, and boy do we have a treat for YOU!” (A title, dripping blood, rises from the bottom of the screen: The most X-treme Roleplay EVER!!! Pause a few moments, and the title descends and vanishes.) Tmm: “That’s right, this latest installment of THE Multiple Man’s long-running story will be the MOST X-treme chapter ANYONE’s ever seen! Guaranteed!” (Pause as he lets this sink in.) Tmm: “But without the money back portion part, of course.” (He claps his hands together and rubs them gleefully.) Tmm: “Shall we begin then?” (He yips and leaps into the air, clutching his backside. Behind him, Val is tossing a used syringe into the designated BIOHAZARD container.) Val: “You have now been injected with a chemical cocktail that even Elvis would hesitate to shoot up. Synthetic hormones, Adtesine and its XD35 activator, concentrated Ethanol, and the whole second and third shelf of the medicine cabinet upstairs.” Tmm: “Cut the technobabble! Give it to me straight!” Val: ”Well, it’ll either kill you of give you super powers… probably kill you, but that’s a risk I’m willing to take.” (Photogenic grin for the camera.) Tmm: “Xtreme ADDICTION! Well fellas, better get me dressed before the drugs kick in!” (With an almost cruel efficiency, Raul and HV twist, fold, and generally stuff Tmm into a variety of Xtremely trendy clothes! A pair of Doc Healey Double-Cross Traitors shoes, a pair of ‘Crevice’ jeans with the really big legs, [Tmm fits just fine in the one], a real ‘Polio’ shirt, necklace, watch, sunglasses, rings, etc., etc. [best if I do this, ‘twould read like a catalogue.], etc., etc. Quick freeze frame as the prices of the clothing is displayed on screen… and resume.) Tmm: “Xtreme PRICING!” (He staggers a bit as a backpack is placed on him.) Tmm: “Whoa! What is IN this thing? Bricks?” Val: “Not just any bricks! ‘El Bildon’ bricks used in the houses of the rich and famous! Five hundred dollars a pound street value give or take.” Tmm: “Xtreme WEIGHT! C’mon, let’s get this going, my vision is already starting to gray out from the chemicals…” (Unceremoniously, Tmm is picked up on the shoulders of the other men and chucked through a small door mounted in the wall… into a giant DANCE PARTY!!! Shadowed bodies twist and thrash in the strobing lights completely at odds to the beats of Fish Picker’s ‘Hideous Control Now Remix’ as laid down by the heavy handed musical [?] stylings of the resident DJ Uncool. THE Multiple Man writhes and jerks as arhythmically as the rest of them as he takes the stage.) DJ Uncool: “And now for your party pleasure, the man who’s more man than ANY man is gonna lay down some PHAT rhymes to get this party really jumpin’! THE MULTEPOLE NAM!!!” (Lusty cheers from the sozzled crowd, who obviously have no idea who the hell he is and could care less as long as they can still get their dance on.) Tmm: (lifting microphone to lips) “I came tonight, my clothes are tight, and I’m really white. When I come through the door, people on the floor, start shouting for more, like I’z a cheap whore…Whoa! Whoa! Whoa!” (Cut to another room where Keira and Steve are watching all this on a monitor with the sort of sick interest that a trapped mouse regards a hungry snake.) Keira: “If this doesn’t kill the rap genre, nothing will. It’s like watching a trainload of nuns slam into an orphanage… my mind is horrified beyond measure, yet… I can’t look away.” Steve: “What the hell’s a swarnge, anyway?” (Keira, eyes still locked on the monitor, sadly shakes her head.) Keira: “Best to file that in the ‘Things Man was Not Meant to Know’ folder.” (Even though their eyes ache from the strain of not blinking, they stare on…) Tmm: “…can’t be no sin, I’m Invada Zim, a playa playin’ to win …” Steve: “You have to admit, though, it is actually better than that album he released last year…” Keira: “You mean ’Tmm Lays You Down By the Fire’? The only album reputed to ever undersell William Shatner’s ‘The Transformed Man’?” Steve: “That’d be the one.” (Keira merely shivers in reply.) Steve: “Bear down, it’s almost over. Just think, later on we’ll look back on all of this and have a good laugh.” Keira: “Of the hysterical variety, I’d imagine.” (WHOOM! Tmm piles through the door into the room. Behind him, the dope beats of DJ Uncool almost drown out the pathetic weeping of the horde of freshly soul-dead fans of rap.) Tmm: (croaking) “Xtreme… dehydration!” Steve: (eyes bloodshot) “That’s our cue.” (Keira picks up a can of soda as Steve inserts an IV of the same into the desiccated Tmm.) Keira: (woodenly to camera) “When facing extreme dehydration…” Tmm: (croaking) “Xtreme!” Keira: “…THE Multiple Man enjoys the extreme taste…” Tmm: (still croaking, but less so) “Xtreme!” Keira: “… of that most bipolar of beverages, ‘Dr. Pibbs and Mr. Pepper’. Good, bad, cool cucumber or party animal, ‘Dr. Pibbs and Mr. Pepper’ will kick you in the groin and leave you howling for more.” (The little dial on the IV machine reads ‘empty’ as Steve removes the needle. A revitalized Tmm stands tall.) Tmm: “Is that all? I thought this was going to be Xtreeeeeeeemmmmmeeee!!!” (His last word is stretched out due his dropping from sight because of a strategically placed trap door. He falls into open space as the shaft leads outside of the jet [didn’t know they were in one didja?] and into a high altitude plummet! Tmm whips out a cellular phone… Voxboss brand… and speed dials!) Tmm: (screaming) “Xtreme TERMINAL VELOCITY!” Phone: “A suicidal terrorist strapped with explosives is falling towards that Acid factory beneath you! One has already hit, and the building is aflame! Subdue and disarm the second one before he can destroy the factory and the Broken Glass Boutique next to it!” (Angling himself like a human bullet, Tmm steaks down through the stratosphere towards the little black splotch of the falling terrorist! Unslinging the backpack full of ‘El Bildon’ bricks, [used in the houses of the rich and famous!] Tmm slams it hard into the head of the terrorist, knocking him out! No longer steering himself in freefall, the terrorist quickly drops off course… away from the factory!) Tmm: (speed dialing again) “Xtreme DISARMAMENT!” Phone: “Use your ‘Boredyuppieboard’ brand skateboard to hit the half pipe on the roof of the burning factory to land safety!” Tmm: “Skateboard?” (Clunk! It takes him cleanly in the noggin! More by dumb reflex than by design, he grabs ahold of the plank and wrestles it into proper position with only instants to spare!) Tmm: “Xtreme FINAL APPROACH!!” (The little wheels smoking with the sheer speed of the landing, Tmm cranks out a 2160 degree aerial, then a another move of some sort that I don’t know the name of, then a handplant that seems to last forever! Tumbling upright, he gives the camera a thumbs up.) Tmm: “Xtreme FINISH!” (Boom! The flaming Acid Factory collapses beneath him as the camera fades out!)
