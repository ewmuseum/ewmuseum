Mr. Psycho Goes To Washington

24 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24-11-02 Skitzo: Y’know it’s times like these I wish I was a big famous celebrity! Skitzo: Celebrities all have defects, like Cher’s botox op, or Verne Troyer’s size…or Michael Jackson’s…………erm…or Michael Jackson! Walking along a nice city street in Washington D.C. on a fine Autumn’s eve, Mr. Psycho himself and his good friend the Living Nightmare, Nerezza. As they march along the streets, kicking at the dust and watching another day pass by. Nerezza looks at Skitzo and thinks, then speaks… Nerezza: You know something man… Skitzo: I know a lot… Nerezza: No I mean seriously, in this environment, in this company and atmosphere I have only one question on my mind… Skitzo: Shoot… Nerezza: WHY THE SHYTE AREN’T WE IN TEXAS GETTING READY FOR THE PAY-PER-VIEW?!?!?!??!?!?!?!?!!? Skitzo: I thought Vampires can fly? Nerezza: I CAN’T FLY US BACK TO TEXAS YOU MOOK!!! Skitzo: Oh well…you do know why we’re here anyway, don’t you? Walking on, Nerezza becomes quite enthusiastic as to what Skitzo’s plan was… Skitzo: We’re going to see the President! Stopping in his tracks, Nerezza’s face becomes livid and he simply turns and walks away. Skitzo: OH COME THE FUCK ON!!! What the shit have you got against Dubya???? Nerezza turns back and looks at Skitzo, the familiar look that says, “What the fuck do you think?” emblazoning itself across the Living Nightmare’s visage. Creeping back up to Skitzo, the 2-time PCW tag champion responds as only he can… Nerezza: A knife, preferably… Skitzo begins to laugh, while Nerezza keeps his stern gargoyle-like expression across his face. The two members of TEAM BASTARDOS plod along, the breeze and crisp air invigorating to them, until Skitzo stops and looks to the left… Skitzo: Erm…Rezz? Nerezza: Yeth? Skitzo: The President lives in a White House, yes? Nerezza: A very big White House… Skitzo: Well, not to sound like Blair With fodder but…WHAT THE FUCK IS THAT?!?!? Skitzo points at what can only be described as………well, as a really, really, really, really big…Bouncy Castle! The once great White House has been transformed into a big, beefy, boisterous, colourful and comforting BOUNCY CASTLE! Nerezza’s jaw drops, while Skitzo’s eyes are the size of dinner plates…staring in horror at the H.Q. of the most powerful man in America, conspiracy pending… Nerezza: WHAT IN VORADOR'S NAME!? Skitzo: I feel weak…we’re being governed by a man who lives in a fucking BOUNCY CASTLE?!?!?!? Nerezza: Well, at least he doesn't wear a towel on his head… Skitzo: It’s times like this I wish the plotline of Metal Gear Solid 2 was true… Skitzo: You want to fight a man with testicles-… Skitzo: -…tentacles-… Skitzo: -…erm…tentacles, and kill the president and find out the true leaders of the country were killed 100 years ago after you spent 48hrs trying to find out more about them? Nerezza: Soul Reaver had a better plot. Ahhh, a world where humans are merely cattle and vampires roam underneath an eternal fog with no sun...anyway, to our original point... why DID you bring me here? Skitzo: We need exposure for Team Bastardos, right? Nerezza: Right... Skitzo: So who better to get us on worldwide news than the man that everyone wishes they didn't know??? Nerezza: But Stephen Hawking's attending a seminar in Switzerland... Skitzo: Ok look anyway, basically today Dubya is going to be talking in Congress about this and that, and we need to be there to promote ourselves, so let’s go to it! Nerezza: Politics isn't my strong point... but all right. Just keep yourself... selves... under control in there. At that, Skitzo and Nerezza take off down a newly paved road, and stop before a pair of ominous steel gates, as part of a network of barbed wire, razor-wired, electric fences with signs posted on them such as “Knock b3fore entry” and “This is not the President’s house”. A guard tower has posted in it a number of soldiers with sentry guns, eyeing up the Living Nightmare and Mr. Psycho. Soldier #1: HALT! WHO GOES THERE!!! Skitzo: WE’RE REPRESENTATIVES FROM TEAM BASTARDOS OF PRIMETIME CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Soldier #2: STATE YOUR BUSINESS!!! Nerezza: Watashi wa Nascosto Nerezza desu. Sore wa William Williams-san desu. Anata wa nihongo dewa arimasen, ne? Soldier #1: He speaks Japanese…what do we do? Soldier #2: Well they ain’t riding camels or driving a Boeing 747, let ‘em through! From their tower, the soldiers open up the gates and watch as Skitzo and Nerezza merrily skip down the long road paved with gravel on the way to the President’s house! As they draw nearer and nearer, Skitzo and Nerezza realise just what madness has been going on in the former White House as large balloon clowns and animals float around the entrance, while there are big pink furry turrets on each corner of the building, mounted by five soldiers, each with a large mini-gun in hand. Nerezza shakes his head in disgust while Skitzo clears his throat and approaches the large, overbearing door…complete with a big funky ring bell, underneath a panel of numbers. Pushing it, a machine-like voice sounds out, crackling through a speaker above the bell. Voice: Welcome to the White House, home of George Dubya Bush…If you are an American, Press 1…If you are an Iraqi, Press 2…if you’re from Toys ‘R’ Us, come on in! HA! HA! HA! HA! HA! Skitzo: Oh my god this is horrendous… Skitzo: Let’s have a bit of fun with it… Skitzo pushes #1 on the panel while Nerezza’s eyes open in horror. Mr. Psycho and the Living Nightmare wait in silence, as nothing happens…until the front door bursts open and there stands the President…weighing a pink pinny with a picture of him in a chef’s hat on it and “Kiss The Chef”, holding what appears to be a humungous cannon, mounted on his shoulder. George Dubya: DIE YOU EYE-RAQI SCUM!!!!!!!!!!! George promptly fires the cannon, and the two PCWers duck as…confetti flies out of it…George throws the cannon to the ground and stomps around, weeping. George Dubya: DAMN! DAMN! DAMN! DAMN! DAAAAAAAMN!!!! Nerezza: Y’know if we really were Iraqis, I doubt we’d walk right up and knock at the door of our nemesis… Skitzo: And why are you attacking us when you got a shitload of military personnel less than a few metres away??? Skitzo: You daft bastert… George Dubya: I AM THE PRESIDENT!!! Skitzo: Good for you, see George, did you get our phone call earlier? We’re from Primetime Championship Wrestling; we asked to see you about something important? George Dubya: Pull ma finger! Nerezza: I think we ought to let ourselves in on this one Skitz… The Living Nightmare and Mr. Psycho walk in past George, who still has his finger out, waiting for it to be pulled. A look of sadness adorns his old face, and to satisfy himself he pulls his own finger and laughs out loud as a fart booms out from his rear. Then he suddenly stops and cringes. George Dubya: HA! HA! HA! HA! HA!………….oooooh I pooed a little… George follows Nerezza and Skitzo into his house, and closes the door behind them. Inside, the two members of Team Bastardos look in horror at the walls, the big yellow walls, the stick figures of George killing Saddam and Osama and Ulrika…the pink furry carpet, the elevator in place of the stairs… Skitzo: May god have mercy on our souls? George Dubya: Come on in to the den, I already got some visitors! Walking on into the den, Skitzo and Nerezza’s eyes open wide as the horror further unfolds…and they find themselves in the presence of George’s visitors… THE OSBOURNES!!! Skitzo: OH SHIT!!! Nerezza: OH FUCK!!! Ozzy: OOOOOOOOOOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHOOOOOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHOOOOOHHHHHHHHHHH JOO FUCKIN’ LET A ‘UCKING HUGE BAT INTO THE FUCKING HOUSE!!!! George Dubya: These are my friends boys, Ozzy, Sharon, Jack and Kelly… Nerezza: Erm…not to be a bother but I think I’m going to wait outside…I ain’t no appetiser for a walking Bumble Ball… Sharon: What did you call my husband??? Skitzo: Y’know what I never got, while Ozzy has done all that work getting to where he is, his wife is mooching off the money and his kids are two little fat spoiled brats… Skitzo: True… Kelly Osbourne: What the FUCK did you just FUCKING say to me??? Skitzo: And another thing! How come is it that Ozzy can swear and sound normal, but you three sound like you’re putting it on for the cameras??? Jack: FUCK off you silly little CUNT! Skitzo: Why don’t you? Suddenly, Sharon, Jack and Kelly disappear in a cloud of pink smoke, to the delight of Dubya who stands clapping his hands like a madman. George Dubya: HA! HA! HA! HA! HA! THAT WAS GREAT!!! Are you two magicians as well??? Nerezza: No but we can make your heartbeat disappear… George Dubya: ……………………Save that one for later… Nerezza sits down next to Ozzy and clears his throat, while the aging rocker looks at the Living Nightmare licking his lips. Ozzy: Y’y’ykno’ I ain’t no fuckin’ r…rump rocket scientist maaan, bu’ you tryin’ to take the piss? Nerezza: ‘Scuse me? Skitzo: He thinks you look like a bat version of him… Nerezza: Oh… Ozzy begins looking at Nerezza with a hunger in his eyes, and the PCW Tag Champ becomes a little nervous as the Prince of Darkness suddenly bares his old nashers. Ozzy: Mmmm…fuckin’ d…d-d-d-delicious…man… Nerezza: Ozzy…Ozzy I wouldn’t…Ozzy stop it…Ozzy…OZZYYYYY!!! Skitzo: HE’S COMIN’ RIGHT FOR YA!!! Ozzy suddenly lunges out of his chair, his mouth gaping and teeth aiming for the head of Nerezza! Leaping out of his chair, the Living Nightmare speeds past Skitzo and the President as Ozzy hobbles after him at a speed of 0.5mph. Giggling at the old man, Skitzo watches as Nerezza throws chairs and tables and ornaments at Ozzy to no avail, trying desperately to avoid getting his head bitten off. George Dubya: He must like fast food…HA! HA! HA! HA! HA! Skitzo: LOL LOL LOL tru dat playa! George Dubya: Huh? Skitzo: Anyway…George we need some kind of promotional material for our Team Bastardos campaign and indeed our Alamo Match at the upcoming Pay-Per-View today… George Dubya: Who are you? Skitzo: We’re from PCW, we told you… George Dubya: What’s that? Skitzo: The biggest wrestling promotion in America… George Dubya: Is it an Eye-raqi base camp? Skitzo: *Sigh*…Yes…yes it I-…I mean…YES!! PCW IS FULL OF IRAQIS!!! You should address the problem immediately!!! George Dubya: QUICK MAN!!! TO THE DUBYA CAVE!!!! Skitzo: You have a cave? George Dubya: I mean…Congress… 20 Minutes Later Ozzy: C--c------COME 'ERE you fffffffffookin' Flyin'....... thing! Nerezza: YOU’LL NEVER TAKE ME UNDEAD!!!!!!! The scene opens out on the front of Congress, where Skitzo and George Dubya Bush are entering, watching as Ozzy hobbles after Nerezza across the paths. Entering the building, the benches are already filled with people and government officials, awaiting George Bush’s arrival. Walking to the podium, Skitzo stands alongside his new buddy as Bush makes an announcement. George Dubya: It has come to my attention, that as the President of the United States of America, we are living in a world of deception and lies. An acquaintance has brought to my attention the knowledge that Iraqis are operating under our very noses in an organisation known as the PCW…Primetime Championship Wrestling. Furthermore, it has also been unearthed that on this day in San Antonio, Texas, an event will go underway known as “Alliances 2K2”, the main event of which has been found to be full of these Iraqi bastards. The only course of action is my colleague and his team’s conquering of this evil foe, indeed in this ‘main event’ and an Alamo match to go underway previously. Standing by George’s side, Skitzo begins whispering to himself. Skitzo: This kinda material belongs in PWA… Skitzo: Hey the President is backing Team Bastardos for Alliances, who gives a flying fuck? George: Therefore as the President of the United States of America, I ask for the people’s support, and the nation’s support of Team Bastardos to conquer at Alliances and defeat this Iraqi threat! Skitzo: Maybe we should tell him they ain’t Iraqis… Skitzo: …………………………………naaaaaa… George: I would now like to welcome the man who has divulged this knowledge, to give a further analysis of the situation…Skitzo? Skitzo: He wants us to talk??? Skitzo: Oh shit! Skitzo: Shut up and get up there! Skitzo walks up to the podium and clears his throat…looking across the sea of people, he takes slow, deep breaths and pulls the microphones to his face. Skitzo: We need the support of everyone in this great nation to back Team Bastardos and Gatorade to conquer at Alliances! Skitzo: Except Florida they can go fuck themselves… George steps up surprised and whispers in Skitzo’s ear… Skitzo: Oops, ok sorry………Florida AND Canada can go fuck themselves!!! Skitzo: Now with everyone’s support for Team Bastardos we know we can win at Alliances, there’s not much to it other than an ass whipping or two but we need your support, now…does anyone have any questions? A man in a far row to the left dressed to kill raises his hand, and clears his throat as Skitzo points towards him. Man: Good Day, my name is-… Skitzo: -…Unimportant, get to the question… Man: Ok…well why are you basically urinating on the good name of the President with this charade? Wrestlers have no place here, why are you wasting our good time when you could be making $25 in some sleazy bar somewhere? Skitzo: Good question………………any more? No one else raises their hand, but before Skitzo can step down from the podium, the same man can be heard shouting, trying to mask his voice as he ducks. Man: *COUGH* JACKASS! *COUGH* Skitzo: ………………………………… 5 Minutes Later Being dragged kicking and screaming out of congress, Skitzo thrusts around furiously as security, followed by George Bush, throw Mr. Psycho out onto the dirt, followed by Nerezza who seems to have lost Ozzy Osbourne. Skitzo: WELL HOW THE FUCK WAS I SUPPOSED TO KNOW YOU DON’T SCREAM “IM GONNA POP A CAP IN YO’ BITCH ASS” IN CONGRESS MAKES YOU THINK I HAVE A GUN ON ME??!?!?!?!?!?!?!?! George Dubya: Skitzo I am so sorry, that was my fault I shouldn’t have let you stand up there by yourself… Skitzo: You should have held my hand… George Dubya: Look the least I can do is give you a lift to Texas in Air Force One along with my apology, will you accept? Skitzo: Air Force One, eh? 35 Minutes Later Sitting in a nice comfortable chair, sipping on gin and juice on Air Force One, Skitzo and Nerezza and George W. Bush converse a little more on Alliances, while Skitzo tries to explain there really are no Iraqis, but support is welcome… George Dubya: Sounds good to me, maybe I can watch? Skitzo: Sorry, it’s PG-13, you couldn’t handle it… George Dubya: Oh damn… Skitzo: So Rezz, are you ready to win us the main event and watch me take Gatorade to new levels of excellence… Skitzo: Nerezza? Skitzo: OH MY GOD!!! Ozzy Osbourne: I-I-I-I…I’M NOT F-F-F-F…………FOOKIN’ NEZERRA J-J-J-J-J…OOO FUCKING SILY MM-M-M-M-M-M………MOTHERF-F-F-FUCKER!!!!!!!!! Skitzo: So wait…if you’re here…where’s Nerezza? Meanwhile, hanging onto the tail of Air Force One… Nerezza: GET ME FUCKING OUT OF HEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEERE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The End
