Smokie Tang seeks help...(Part V)

19 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 19, 2002 {{{{Scene}}}}The scene opens up with Smokie Tang being escorted back out of the Calyx Inc. building. Smokie isn’t struggling or anything as the 3 guards and the Dark Messiahs escort Smokie by holding onto his arms. They have gone on the elevator ride and are now in the lobby at the scene. They walk up to the door and one of the guards opens the door, then the other four shove Smokie out of the door and he just brushes himself off as he lands on his feet on the sidewalk. He flips the bird to the five escorts and then walks away. Smokie looks up at the cloudless sky wishing it were filled with clouds and storming such as he feels. He walks past several billboards, which have already been painted and redone with his picture on them endorsing such products as Calyx Fruit Juice and Calyx Baby Powder. Smokie just yawns and starts to talk to himself as he walks along the sidewalk with a sad look on his face and his head down… Smokie Tang: I can’t believe it, after these past seven or so years of running my dads business, it comes down to a Virgin and a contract signing. I can’t believe it I’m dead when the rest of the pimps hear about this. I bet I’ve already lost my whores and my territories. What am I going to tell Dante? {{{{The scene of Smokie walking down the street on the sidewalk washes away and another scene of Smokie Tang stepping up the steps to his apartment washes in. Smokie opens the door and still has his head hung down low. He walks a little ways down the small corridor until he gets to the elevator in the run down building. He pushes for the doors to open and they do, he steps onto the elevator and pushes the button that is marked, “Smokie”. The elevator goes up at an alarming speed then, but it doesn’t seem to alarm Smokie. The elevator is at the top before you know it, and the doors open up to a luxury corridor, with a door right at the end of it. Smokie takes a few steps into the corridor and reaches out and opens the door. The door swings open without a hitch, and the Smokie looks up to see the lights out. That’s odd to him, considering when he left Dante was still there, and Dante doesn’t go anywhere. Smokie steps into the room and flips on the lights, as soon as he flips on the lights he’s knocked over by something hard and as he falls he looks up to see Dante Jackson with a hammer in his hand. Dante has a look of sorrow on his face as he realizes that he just hit his friend, Smokie Tang…}}}} Dante Jackson: I’m sorry I didn’t know it was you. People have been coming by here throwing stuff at the door and knocking at it all day, I thought you were one of them getting into the room. I’m sorry man. {{{{Smokie holds the back of his head as he feels what he feared, he can feel the hot liquid gushing out of his head, it’s none other then blood coming from the back of his head. He stands up with the help of Dante and the two of them walk into Smokie’s bedroom. Smokie falls over and lands on his bed, and Dante walks over to the closet and opens it. He rummages around through it for a little bit and finally finds the kit he was looking for. He pulls out the First Aid kit and puts it on the bed beside Smokie. He opens it up and inside is a needle, stitches, ace bandages and a few other Emergency items he pulls some of them out, including a sewing needle and the lining for the stitches. He pulls out some alcohol pads after that to sanitize the area around the wound. Last but not least he pulls out a joint and hands it to Smokie, Smokie puts it in his mouth and he lights it with a lighter from his pants pocket. He starts to drag on the joint and all the pain starts to go away. Dante starts to touch up the spot on the back of Smokie’s head as Smokie starts to talk about his day…}}}} Smokie Tang: You know, my day has been hell all day long. First off I’m walking down the street, minding my own business, and I’m dragged into that damned Calyx Incorporated building. It seems that I signed a contract, I must have forgotten about, and now I’m working for D-Kay Calyx and now endorsing his products that I don’t like! {{{{Dante continues to work on Smokie’s wound as he says…}}}} Dante Jackson: Damn, what are you going to do? You know that this gives you a bad reputation around here. You’ll be killed for doing such things. {{{{Smokie sits there and thinks for a little bit as Dante finishes up the sewing of the wound…}}}} Smokie Tang: I don’t know what I’ll do. But I think I need help from someone else. Has Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey left with the rest of the whores to go to other pimps? {{{{Dante bites the thread and finishes his half ass job of sewing up Smokie’s wound. He just smiles at Smokie as he sits back and says…}}}} Dante Jackson: She hasn’t left she’s the only one who is still loyal to you no matter what. She’s at her house. If you want me to I can take you over there. {{{{Smokie shakes his head as he stands up looking a little woozy, but a drag on the joint fixed that. He then turns around and says to Dante…}}}} Smokie Tang: No, I can go there by myself. You stay here and watch the place like you were, Smallie Tallie Jesse Ramey is the only person who can help me now with this problem. She’ll probably have a place for me to hide out. {{{{Dante stands up and puts all the things that he took out of the First Aid Kit back into it and he walks over to the closet to put it back. He then turns around and says…}}}} Dante Jackson: All right then, good luck, I hope you find a place to hide. {{{{Smokie walks out of the room and through the living room door, then the narrator voice of Dante Jackson comes across as the scene starts to fade out into Smokie tang leaving the building and walking down the sidewalk…}}}} Narrator: But if I had known what was going to happen that day, with Smokie and all, then I would have gone with him. Oh how I wish I could have stopped what happened… {{{{The scene then fades on Smokie Tang as he walks down the sidewalk in the sunny late afternoon, but what will happen when nightfall comes upon him? Who knows?..}}}} TO BE CONTINUED
