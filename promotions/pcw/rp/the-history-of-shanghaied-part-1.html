The History of Shanghaied - Part 1

undated

Date: 26th Jan, &#039;03 Voice Over Man: Tonight’s special presentation ‘The history of Shanghaied’ has been produced by the producers of The Chat King Wrestling Show, and will be presented by the host of The Chat King Wrestling Show, Chet King. The voice over man speaks as onto the screen in golden hieroglyphic letters comes the words ‘The History of Shanghaied’. This then fades out and onto the screen comes Chet King ‘The Chat King’ standing in front of the Shanghaied Gymnasium where Shanghai-ed is coming from this Sunday. Chet King: The third Shanghaied is coming to us live this Sunday from the arena directly behind me, so I thought that this would be the most appropriate time to look at the history of one of the premier events in the wrestling world. Back in January of 2001 The Phantom Booker was in control of PCW, despite the fact that he was suffering some financial problems due to a lawsuit resulting from a tractor accident involving Flaymin Aymin and some fans. But he was still in control and he decided that the January pay per view would take place in Shanghaied, China. This was considered a risky move by many people in the wrestling industry. After all why should PCW superstars, crew and equipment all have to be flown out to Shanghai and it wasn’t a sure thing that there would be a big interest in PCW in China. The Phantom Booker proved his critics wrong though, as tickets to the Shanghai Gymnasium ended up being sold out. This however didn’t solve The Phantom Booker’s previously mentioned financial problems, and despite what looked to be a successful upcoming pay per view, the booker was still in trouble. The main event for Shanghaied was slated to be a five way world title contest featuring the champion at the time The Reverend, former champion Doug Troy, and three other up and coming talents in Kid Wonder, Trashcan Man and Lack’o’Stress. In the days prior to the pay per view we’d seen many interesting developments such as a Times Square Brawl between Doug Troy and The Reverend, and the return of Frisco to PCW to side with Doug Troy. But the biggest developments regarding the main event were still to come. When we come back from this break we’ll find out more about the very first Shanghaied. Commercial: The History of Shanghaied is proudly brought to you by The Chat King Wrestling Show. Watch The Chat King Wrestling Show at whatever time it’s on, usually whenever Chet can be actually arsed to do an episode. We return to outside the Shanghai Gymnasium where Chet King looks at it, presumably remembering the first Shanghaied. Chet King: Ah, the very first Shanghaied, what a magnificent day it was. But it looked at first like the pay per view could be lacking what it needed to be successful. As in the previous few days some other developments took place. The first was an injury to Trashcan Man that took him out of the main event. With the main event now down to four men it still looked to be a great match, but not as great as it once was to be. But then another development took place. Lack’O’Stress had an unfortunate accident in his family and understandably had to pull out of the main event. So the main event was now down to three men. Although this didn’t look as special a main event as five man contest, PCW was lucky enough to have it’s top three guys competing in it and there was no doubt that it would still be a great match. Other matches scheduled for the pay per view included a Chinese Fire Drill match for the Extreme Title, a tractor match between Bang Davenport and Flaymin Aymin, a tag title match involving Chef and Frankie the Mook, Chris Allen aka Boda and Anthony Cay, and the Blood Brothers. Also on the card was a cruiserweight title match between Miyagawa, Clyde and Bad Battio, Vegita taking on six other men in a handicap match, the finals of the second new blood tournament, a rising star title match between Tyler Nelson and Jason Wulf, Chef against Bertha, Kano defending his Continental title in a four way match involving Kriegman, Stephen Blood and the Ultimate Warlord. There was also one other match that caused a lot of controversy. That was a Euthanasia match between Doug Troy’s Koala Nibbles and Lighting Kid’s dog Sparky. No one wanted this match to happen, except for The Phantom Booker that is. To find out how this match turned out, and what else went down at Shanghaied stay tuned. Commercial: Order Shanghaied 3 on pay per view this Sunday. There isn’t any Euthanasia matches, but it will still be a killer of a pay per view. Okay, that was a lame joke, but you should still order Shanghaied 3. We come back with Chet King still outside the arena. Chet King: This is what I’m sure you’ve all been waiting for. You’ve been waiting to find out why Shanghaied is such a spectacular pay per view. Well now I’m about to tell you what went down at Shanghaied. We saw the pay per view open up with a shock, Steve Smith wasn’t allowed into Shanghai. This meant that we had to have Major Wang become the third commentator. This wasn’t a bad thing however, as the commentary still ran smoothly, or as smoothly as possible when Dave Kern and Jeff Marx are in there together. We saw victories to Macaber Payne in the Newblood tournament, Dark Lotus in the Chinese Fire drill match, HAC jumbled in the tag battle, Aymin defeated Bang, Kano retained in the Continental Title match, Vegita defeated six jobbers, Miyagawa held onto the cruiser weight title following the return of Jake Keeton and Chef took out Bertha. The two matches I haven’t mentioned yet are the rising star title match and the world title contest. In the rising start match, we saw Tyler Nelson regain the title thanks to the help of three masked men dressed in Armani. Immediately following this match, Tyler vacated the title saying he was too good for it. Later on in the show we saw Nelson meeting with the financially troubled Phantom Booker. This got many people wondering what they were meeting about. Then came the world championship match. Even though it was now just a three man match it was still a great one thanks to the efforts of Kid Wonder, Doug Troy and The Reverend Jon Hull. However the match did deteriorate as we saw interference from people such as Frisco, Macaber Payne and the ‘infamous’ Scott Kilborne. This caused the match to be thrown out, but just because the main event was over, it didn’t mean the night was over, because there was still a lot to happen. After the match The Phantom Booker came down to the ring followed by Tyler Nelson, Dan Hollywood, Mr Showtime, myself, The Executive Producer and Key Grip. What happened next was one of the most famous moments in wrestling history, and I’m sure everyone has heard the story thousands of times and don’t need to hear it again. Tyler had solved the booker of his financial troubles and bought PCW, and was going to run it along with men who had joined him in the ring. Tyler gave me the position of President, and immediately that day went down as one of the greatest in my career. It was an infamous night and it changed the landscape of PCW forever as Nelson turned PCW into the giant it is today. I hope you’ll all join me again for the second part of this intriguing retrospect at the history of Shanghaied in which I’ll discuss Shanghaied 2 as well as having another quick talk about the inaugural Shanghaied. [We then leave the show as clips are shown of Shanghaied 1, including the infamous clips of the Media takeover. Commercial: Order Shanghaied 3 today, and if we’re to believe Chet King, you’ll see Chet defeat Dante Jackson because as Chet has claimed it is his day, and good things always happen to him at Shanghaied.
