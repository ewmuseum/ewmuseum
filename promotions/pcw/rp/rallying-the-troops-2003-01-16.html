Rallying the Troops

16 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16th January A black picture appears on your screen. For a few seconds, you believe that there’s an untimely fault with your television but seeing as you’ve yet to replace the dead batteries in the remote and the fact that you’re too lazy to get up and manually change the channel…you decide to stare at the darkness. Moments later, you spot a light in the distance. As you squint your eyes to make out the small spot, the light draws nearer and it appears that there’s a figure underneath the light. Gradually, the point of interest pulls nearer and on closer inspection, you notice that the light is actually five individual spotlights and the figure underneath this light are in fact five men, stood under each white glaze. The men are stood to attention, perfectly paused with their heads raised upwards and a stone-faced expression on each facial feature and their arms as straight as an arrow. They are all dressed in the identical dark green outfit, seemingly an uniform of some type and their bulky, muscled body size shows that they are not exactly the average male. Along with the shaven heads, rough demeanour and dark surroundings; this is quite an intimidating sight The camera angle displays the quintet in a diagonal fashion, with the closest man being the larger object and the person further away is the smallest. Moments pass of pure silence. These five giants continue to stand, almost in robotic fashion, as the single spots proceed to shine down on their tightly shaved heads Within seconds, slow echoic footsteps are heard; the kind of noise that gives the impression that the person behind the footsteps is taking a leisurely stroll The camera shot changes to a more straight on approach, with the five men and their respective spotlights spreading straight across the screen. Enter Matthias Kuntz-Hunt, the current RUSH Network Champion and the Head of PCW Security. He walks past each chiselled male with a proud expression as he glances at the five focused features. “Gentlemen…” he starts in his near-perfect English accent “Here is your first target…” Hunt halts and turns to face the camera, he seemingly points right at you…but as the shot itself slowly glides to the left, he is in fact pointing to whatever was behind the camera… “This is the man who threatens the very existence of my most valued and treasured possession…” he barks as he points in an aggressive manner. The German stares at the wall with disgust as the camera slowly spins around to reveal a large image of Stimpo, the challenger to Hunt’s Network Title. “This is the man who threatens the very existence of this fine collection of the male species, who threatens you five man beasts…who threatens your monthly paychecks!!” he shouts as his voice reverberates around the darken scene. “Because if this man gets his own way…and believe me, he’ll stop at nothing until he does. If he gets his own way, then it will signal the demise of you fine athletes in this organisation.” He pauses before continuing “If he gets this cherished item of mine, then it opens the door to some more riff-raff and not to mention that the Network Title will not be on my shoulder and it won’t be in the Front Office camp!” Hunt has now turned to face his crew and looks each of them square in the eye as he moves down the line, despite the solider continuing to stare straight ahead “Now, I am more than capable of taking care of this punk inside the ring…no doubt about it. But as the saying goes…strength in numbers and there’s nothing better than having five genetic freaks in my work force than some gas masked freak, an idiot with a teenage obsession with the female chest and a moron with a mullet problem watching my back.” He stops at the third solider before continuing “Like I said, if Stimpo gets his grubby mits on my title, it may give the incentive to others rise to DDT and show Das Bundeswehr that they are not afraid!” “Do you want that? Do you want people to point and stare at you in hysterics like some sort of freak circus outfit?” “NEIN!” shout the Bundeswehr “You are five perfect physical male specimens who are going to be intimidating, fearful men who will squash an average sized head like a damn grape on my order and use an enemy’s human body like a ragdoll!” “I will not let you be laughed at! I will not let any of you be ridiculed!” Hunt is now at the first and largest Bundeswehr member and he talks in a more composed manner. “Otto…your raw brutal power will be an asset to this group. You are a monster who is waiting to be unleashed and this man…” Hunt points at the image of Stimpo while still staring into Otto’s eyes. “…is the one to provoke you! He is laughing at you…WILL YOU TAKE THAT? ARE YOU GONNA LET THIS FOOL LAUGH IN YOUR FACE?!” “NEIN, MEIN FURHER!” Otto replies “WAS?” Hunt barks back “NEIN, MEIN FURHER!” Otto responds again in a stern manner as Hunt grins. “Schon, du bist readlich Otto...deine zeit ist jetzt” DDT then moves on the next individual, who has a gruff complexion and a stare that would burn in the walls of hell. “Jurgen. You are one mean, tough son of a bitch who will let nobody take you for a fool and this idiot…” Hunt points at Stimpo again… “…thinks you’re 300lbs of pure waste! He isn’t scared of you…WILL YOU TAKE THAT? ARE YOU GONNA LET THIS MAN WALK THE STREETS AFTER MOCKING YOU?” “NEIN, MEIN FURHER!” Jurgen replies “WAS?” Hunt snaps back “NEIN, MEIN FURHER!” Hunt nods his head in approval and moves on to the third member, who looks older than his peers. “Wilheim, you are the more experienced in this group. You are a former drill instructor in the German Marines! This man…” Once again, he points at Stimpo “…is showing you disrespect! He’s not concerned with discipline, Wilheim…ARE YOU GONNA TAKE THAT? ARE YOU GONNA LET THIS MAN DISRESPECT YOU, SOLDIER?” “NEIN, MEIN FURHER!” the veteran retorts “WAS?” Hunt yells back “NEIN, MEIN FURHER!” “Just as I thought…” Hunt nods his head again and moves on to the fourth bearded man. “Karl…you are just pure evil, aren’t you? You hate everybody…with the exception of your peers and your leader. Furthermore, you hate people additionally if they dislike anything about you and guess what…this punk…” Stimpo’s image is pointed out again “…doesn’t trust bearded men! Now he’s got good reason not to trust you, but it also shows that he doesn’t like your taste in facial hair and that displays victimisation…ARE YOU GONNA TAKE THAT, KARL? ARE YOU GONNA LET THIS ARSCH DISCRIMINIATE YOU JUST BECAUSE YOU’VE GOT A BEARD?!” “NEIN, MEIN FURHER!” the bearded one immediately responds “WAS?” Hunt gets in his face “NEIN, MEIN FURHER!” Hunt flicks something off the beard and grins as he moves on to the final member, who has a snarled expression. “Finally, Stefan…despite your rough presence and mean exterior, you are one funny guy! I’m cracking up right now! You’re a hoot…you’re hilarious…you’re a comedian! However…” Hunt rolls his eyes and slightly nods towards the image of Stimpo to his left while humming “He doesn’t think you’re funny at all, in fact…if you were doing your routine in the Gelbe Dusche back in Stuttgart, he’d be the inconsiderate little shit who would heckle you all night long! He’d embarrass the hell outta you…ARE YOU GONNA TAKE THAT? WILL YOU LET THIS MAN HUMILIATE YOU IN PUBLIC?!” “NEIN, MEIN FURHER!” The buffed “comedian” snarls “WAS?” Hunt stamps his foot “NEIN, MEIN FURHER!” Hunt nods in approval while taking a few steps back. “That is what I need from you five caged animals. I want to see you frothing at the mouth, I wanna hear you snarl and growl and if this nothing…this nobody was ever to threaten the existence of Das Bundeswehr…you’d show the little runt the consequences of stepping on the wrong side of Matthias Kuntz-Hunt and his army…” DDT pauses while scanning the Bundeswehr “BIN ICH RICTHIG?!?” All five respond immediately in a deafening roar which reverberates around the surround. “JA, MEIN FURHER!!!!” “You’re damn right…as the Americans say!” He states as he looks upon his army with the utmost pride. Within seconds he jerks around and stares into the camera… “Let…the games…begin!” The shot of Hunt staring into the camera with his soldiers stood at attention in the background lasts for a few moments, before the darkness fades in and the feed is gone off your television set.
